Live & Interactive V.2

4 January 1999

[Fade up from black to a black and white image of Alex Kidd and Youth Gone Wild, standing together in a wrestling ring. The picture is from early 1996 as the picture gives way to the footage, it is the birth of Legion. Tom Landis walks to the ring, and Timothy Vexton gets up from his position as guest commentator at ringside to join Wild and Kidd in the ring. Freeze again, on a shot of all four. As "Ava Adore" by the Smashing Pumpkins slowly fades in, we can also hear the voice of David Rogers narrating.] Voice-Over: The year was 1996, as the Universal Wrestling Federation had just opened it's doors. Among the crop of new, young talent were two men, two men destined to change the face of wrestling forever... [The image flashes to white, and now we see old footage of Alex Kidd walking to the ring, in one of his first matches in the UWF. Likewise we see Youth Gone Wild, in the ring fighting with Rock Ramon. Each man looks considerably younger than they do now.] Youth Gone Wild went on to capture the UWF Heavyweight Championship of the World from Serge Annis, a feat that many still talk about today. He proudly wore the heavyweight championship title for two months, while the newly formed group Legion rose to the challenge of defending the UWF against all forces. [Cut to footage of a stable war, a chaotic brawl between all members of Legion, the Asylum, Empire and the Street Gang. Freeze on a shot of Alex Kidd and Youth Gone Wild fighting back to back, and the image turns to black and white again.] On the last day of 1996, Alex Kidd was the man who went on to win the UWF Championship. It was to be the first of three championship reigns, and once again, Legion was on top. [The image shatters now, and the anti-Legion group known as the Pride is seen. Brett Greene and Scott "Hotspot" Daniels argue with Wild and Kidd.] 1997 brought a new threat, the supergroup known as the Pride. Formed by two men who hated Legion, the Legionnaires banded together to brave the forces of the Pride. Amidst controversy that one of their own was going to stab them in the back... [Footage of Brett Greene and Scott Daniels standing at ringside, rooting for "Heartbreaker" Tom Landis to take it to Dan Kidd, then Kidd, Greene and Daniels beating on the members of Legion.] The friendship grew, and when Alex Kidd was set to marry Alicia Powers, it was Youth Gone Wild who was named as Alex's best man. [Footage from the ill-fated wedding ceremony on Rampage is shown.] Much of 1997 was spent battling the Pride, a job that neither Alex nor Wild took lightly. 1998 brought a year of turbulence to both men, as a threat from the past, Randolph Chalmers the IIIrd, bought the contracts of both Alex and Youth and forced him to do his evil bidding. [Alicia Powers throws the Miss Uni crown at Randolph Chalmers the IIIrd, and Chalmers fires her. As Alex's wife walks off, Alex looks on in sadness at not being able to stop it from happening.] Alex was separated from his wife, and as a final indignity, his contract as well as his friends' contracts were given to their hated rivals, the Pride. The strain became too much for Alex, forced week in, week out to do the dirty work of the Pride. [A clip of Brett Greene and Tim Vexton defeating the Pride to win back the contracts appears. Freeze on a shot of Alex Kidd watching the ensuing celebration by Legion, with a frown on his face.] When Legion was finally freed, by of all people one of the original Pridesmen, Alex had enough. Youth Gone Wild and the rest of Legion celebrated their victory and a return to freedom, while the hatred inside Alex grew... until June 5th, 1998. [Cut to footage from Friday Night Lightning. Alex watches from inside the ring as Chris Scottsdale attacks Steve Ryott and causes Alex to lose by disqualification in his main event match. Alex lashes out at Tom Landis as well as the crowd, then storms off.] For the first time since almost the beginning of his career, Alex Kidd was out of Legion. As Youth Gone Wild and the rest of Legion tried to cope with Alex's change of heart, it was the Wonderkid who became hated by the fans, especially after he ended the career of Sebastian Jericho en route to becoming the first three time UWF Champion in history. [Alex hits Jericho with the Somersault Bulldog, coming down wrong on him and giving him the career ending injury to his neck.] Since that day, Alex Kidd has defended the title against all comers, and in the process his former best friend, Youth Gone Wild, became the number one contendor to his title. Harsh words have been exchanged between the two, each promising victory. [Clips of Youth Gone Wild against Steve Ryott, "Badboy" Randy Acorn and in the battle royal are shown, intermixed with clips of Alex defending the title against Kensei Tokugawa, Robbie Greer and Scott Daniels.] Two of the hardest working athletes in the history of professional wrestling collide, January 3rd in Seattle's Kingdome. Former colleagues, former allies, former best friends... This time it's personal. Only one will win, only one will wear the heavyweight championship of the world. Will Youth Gone Wild recapture the title he helped make famous? [A slow-motion clip of Wild executing a Wildflyer moonsault.] Or will Alex Kidd prove all along he was justified? [A slow-motion clip of Alex Kidd executing a Full-Nelson Bulldog.] Tonight, the world finds out as two Legionnaires collide for the Heavyweight Championship. But are we ready for the outcome? He leaves Wild on his knees, and steps through the ropes to the apron, pausing for a moment to catch his breath.] DR: I think Alex may be going for the knockout blow! He hits the Somersault Bulldog and this one will be over, bar none. Youth Gone Wild put up a tremendous fight tonight, but not even he could kick out of a somersault bulldog if it's hit right. TV: It is the most dangerous move in wrestling. It's knocked countless opponents out, it's ended careers, and it was the 1996 RSWPF best finishing move. [Alex slowly climbs the turnbuckles, while at the same time Youth Gone Wild pushes up from his knees, to a crouched position and finally to an upright standing position. Alex targets his opponent, and then takes flight. He leaps up, somersaults in the air and comes down straight at Wild, his arm crooked to grab him around the head with a bulldog... but at the same time Youth Gone Wild reaches up and hooks Alex's fast approaching downward arm and slides out from under Alex, driving his face into the mat and then wrenching on his arm with his head locked, a crossface submission tightly applied!] AM: WILD COUNTERS! WILD COUNTERS! DR: What a tremendous counter to the Somersault Bulldog! Youth Gone Wild sensed that Alex was going for it, and shocked the daylights out of his former best friend with it! TV: I can't see any way for Alex to escape that. Wild has it locked in too tight, and he's dead center in the ring. [Youth Gone Wild sits on the canvas, one hand around the neck and head of Alex Kidd, the other stretching the left arm of Alex's out to the side. Wild leans back into the hold as the pain on Alex's face intensifies. The Wonderkid grimaces with the pain, and Wild rears back again with the hold. The crowd watches on the edge of their seats, ready to explode should the bell ring anytime soon. The champion is firmly in the grip of the challenger, but Alex Kidd refuses to tap out or submit to the pain. Two and a half minutes goes by and Alex still refuses to say the words to end the match, as Wild's expression changes from exasperation to desperation, trying with all his might to force Alex to give up.] AM: How could Alex Kidd possibly be resisting the temptation to tap out? There certainly won't be any shame in submitting after such a classic match... but he's flat out refusing. DR: If Alex passes out, referee Junas will have to declare Youth Gone Wild the winner. TV: He'd be that stubborn too. His arm could be in danger of being ripped from it's socket and he'd never give up. Wild better force him to pass out, because submission is the one way you'll never get Alex to end the match. SS: Rip it out! Rip it out! [Youth Gone Wild digs his heels into the mat and leans back as far as he can, almost laying on the back of his opponent. The pain in his shoulder is evident as Alex Kidd squeezes his eyes shut as Youth Gone Wild did much earlier in the match, but every time Junas asks him if he wants to submit, Alex chokes out the words: "No!" Wild pleads with Kidd himself, asking him to submit and not be so pigheaded as to cause himself early retirement, but Kidd steadfastly refutes his attempts. Alex kicks the mat with his foot, but every attempt at inching closer to the ropes is in vain. Wild keeps the pressure up still, as we pass the four minute mark.] DR: This isn't good, fans. It's one thing to have the courage to fight the pain, but it's another to ignore the advice of everyone and simply refuse because of your pride. Alex Kidd could be doing major damage to his shoulder, damage that could be proven irreversible. AM: We might be witnessing Alex Kidd's last match. [Alex bites his lip and opens his eyes, which by now are full of tears from the pain. Wild doesn't release any of the pressure from the hold, but Wild himself looks upset at what's transpiring. Alex's breathing becomes labored as he tries to conserve what little energy he has left now, and it appears as though he's starting to fade out. Junas takes hold of Alex's free wrist and holds it up, but Alex jerks it out of his grasp on the first try, a rare sight in wrestling. He makes a fist with his free hand and grinds it into the mat, but still refuses to tap out. Well past the six minute mark in the crossface submission hold, Alex's hair and face become drenched as the sweat and perspiration roll down his forehead. And as the brave champion makes his last stand in the ring, the entire building goes deathly silent for a moment, as a figure stands in the entranceway, watching the ring from afar. Realizing who it is making their entrance, the Kingdome crowd lets loose with easily one of the loudest cheers of the night.] DR: Oh my god... SS: What the?!? Who let her in?!? TV: Ahem. AM: THAT'S ALICIA POWERS! DR: This place has erupted! Alicia Powers has made her way into the aisle, and the roof has literally been ripped off this place, for the wife of Alex Kidd! TV: We haven't seen her in months! [Alex Kidd's own wife, Alicia Powers quickly makes her way down to the ring, wearing an extreme look of concern. She's not dressed to kill like in the past. She's wearing a grey wool sweater and blue jeans that fit her figure to a T. Despite the casual look, the crowd are still on their feet as Mrs. Kidd makes it to ringside.] DR: What is she doing out here? Is she here to cheer on her husband, or is she concerned with his health and future, after being in this painful hold for over seven minutes and counting? SS: Maybe she's going to bitch that Wild made a pass at her? TV: Shut up Sam. This is neither the time nor the place for smut like that. [The ref asks Alex once again if he'd like to quit. Alex is slower to answer, but resoundingly shouts out "Never". Wild looks just as worn as Alex does, but refuses to break the hold. Wild spots Alicia as she rounds the ring, so she is in front of both Kidd and Wild. Wild's eyes light up in shock, just like the crowd when they see her... But even Wild's look of surprise can not outmatch Alex's. Alex forgets about the pain as he stares at his very own wife for perhaps the first time in months. Alex's eyes open wide... gasping for breath and energy. On the outside, Alicia leans into the ring, looking her husband straight in the eye, and mouthes words... "I still love you..."] SS: What'd she say? I still hate you? AM: No you twit! She said she still loves him! DR: We were aware that Alex and Alicia haven't seen each other in quite a long time... but I had no idea that their marriage was having troubles. AM: I don't think it ever was Dave. She said she still loves him. Doesn't sound like trouble to me. SS: Oh god, I'm going to be sick. This is a UWF World Title match, not the Love Boat. There's no room here in the UWF for the "L" word. AM: Sam, grow a heart why don't you? [For a moment, it seems as if time stands still in the ring. Alex and Alicia's eyes locked on each other, forgetting about the painful submission that his former best friend, Youth Gone Wild has locked on him in the center of the ring... Wild, just like the thousands watching in attendence, and the millions at home on pay per view, is a witness to this. But as is the nature of time, the moment soon passes as Kidd squints his eyes shut and screams out in pain! The crowd are all on their feet as Jim Junas gets to his knees and asks the champ one more time...] AM: I can't watch... SS: Neither can I... I think I'm going to be sick. TV: This is amazing. I never thought Kidd would have this in him. DR: Wild with the hold still locked on... Kidd still fighting, with Alicia Powers now at ringside... Wait a minute! He's... he's... Alex Kidd is fighting back! [Bigger pop, forgetting about Kidd's "allegiance to the fans". These people just love a great match!] DR: Alex Kidd is fighting up! In front of this capacity crowd in the Seattle Kingdome. Alex Kidd is mustering the strength to fight up, after eight minutes in the crossface submission hold from Youth Gone Wild! [Pulling strength from the lowest regions of his body, Alex Kidd begins to power up. He waves his free arm around, trying to help gain some kind of adrenalin, or energy. The crowd continue to explode as Wild can't believe that Kidd is fighting back like this. Alicia continues to stare at her husband, with concern-filled eyes, and a worried expression on her face. Ignoring the incredible pain in his possibly broken shoulder, Alex is able to fight up to one knee... As the sweat rolls down the face of the British Wonderkid, he screams out, trying to fight with everything he has.] DR: I can't believe it! Alex Kidd has fought up to one knee! This is amazing! AM: Only in the UWF will you find heart like this! SS: Amy, weren't you against Alex Kidd at the start of this? AM: Like him or not Sam, this is a true test of... TV: [Interrupting Amy] He fell! He fell! Alex Kidd fell back down! Dammit Kidd, don't be stupid! [Alicia's expression of concern doubles as Kidd, whom one second a ago was fighting up to his knees, collapses forward, landing face first on the mat! The crowd goes silent, as they all stare and watch... Kidd is quiet... Wild is quiet... Everyone in the arena is quiet, excpet Jim Junas who asks Alex if he wants to submit. The challenger continues to lock on the hold, but his expression is one of dire concern... Even he, not wanting to take it this far.] DR: Alex Kidd... is hurt ladies and gentlemen. SS: [In an abnormal, concerned tone] Junas is raising his hand... [Several members of the crowd count with Junas as he raises Kidd's hand. One.... Two... thre----] AM: NO! Alex Kidd holds it up! DR: How did he do it? AM: Kidd appeared unconcious, after falling face first to the mat, but he held it up! After all this... he's still being stubborn. He refuses to give in to Youth Gone Wild. [With his nose buried into the mat... a small trickle of blood streaming down from his lip... and the sweat pouring down his face, and after fighting the cross face so long... Kidd leans his neck back, applying even more pressure to Wild's death grip... but Alex now is able to look across the ring through fog glazed eyes, at his loving wife, whom has yet to tear her tear-filled eyes away from the submission hold. Once again, time goes still... The crowd is deafly silent... as Junas watches Alex, Alex's worn eyes fixated on Alicia... His brown eyes giving way to emotion... as he speaks the words... "I give up..." Silence. Not a sound is heard... Until the first sound breaks the silence. DING! DING! DING! The Kingdome explodes... As every fan that was not already standing up roars to their feet, and produce the loudest pop ever heard for a UWF World Title match. The weary Youth Gone Wild instantly releases the hold, falling back onto the mat, looking up at the lights. Kidd slumps to the mat, face down as Alicia Powers scrambles into the ring. The pop from the audience is awe-inspiring. Dave Rogers tries to speak... but can faintly be heard.] DR: ...historical night in the... ...ling Federation.... ...Gone Wild has captured the... ...-ship belt fr...-derkid, Alex Kidd... [Tom Davis' voice booms over the loudspeakers...] TD: Ladies and gentlemen... the winner of the match via submission... and _NEEEEEEEEEEEEW_ UWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION... YOOOOOOOOOOOOOOUTH.... GOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOONE.... WIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIILLLLLLDDDDD! [Junas is handed the World title belt from an attendent, and brings it over to Wild. The twenty year old superstar continues to rest on the mat... looking up at the ceiling... soaking in what just happened. Wild is fully awake, and Junas places the World Title on the mat beside him. Wild turns his head and looks at his new title... and then over to his opponent, who is still laying out on the mat with his loving wife on her knees beside him, gently rubbing him on the back with one hand as she leans over and whispers something into his ear. The Wild Pack continue to roar, as the opening guitar sequence for Skid Row's "Youth Gone Wild" starts up again. Hearing his music, Wild is rejuvenated with energy, and leaps up to his feet with a kip-up. He looks down at the World title at his feet in disbelief... He looks out to his Wild Pack, and his eyes light up like a child at Christmas. He drops to his knees in front of the title belt, and slowly bends down and picks it up. Wild stares at it... as his close friend and fellow Legionnaire, Tom Landis races down the aisle with a huge smile on his face. Without saying a word, Tim Vexton gets up and rips his headset off and rolls into the ring right when Tom enters it. They both approach their friend. Wild looks up at Tom and Tim... he smiles, and holds up two fingers... They both extend their hands and pull Wild to his feet.] AM: Amazing. Youth Gone Wild has become the UWF World Champion for the second time in his story book career that started, and always will, symbolize the UWF. [Wild shakes ands with Tim and Tom, and the spin him around and raise him up onto their shoulders... Fireworks go off from the lighting rig assembled high above the ring. Wild smiles, and holds up the UWF World title after a history making victory. On the mat, Kidd still does not move, the exhaustion proving too much for him. Alicia continues to rub her hand over his back, trying to comfort him. She looks up and sees Legion parading the new World champion around the ring. She wipes away a tear, and begins to applaud the new champion. She wears a weak, but wholehearted smile, looking up at Wild, before she returns his attention back to Alex once a paramedic team arrives at ringside with a stretcher.] DR: Alex Kidd is going to have be removed from here by stretcher... He is that hurt. [Two paramedics climb into the ring and check out Alex. Talking to him... Although he does not move, Alex talks back with them... The camera's unable to pick up what they are saying, so instead, does a close-up of Alex's weak and dis-jointed shoulder. Wild continues to smile, holding the belt out to the crowd until he sees his one-time best friend, with two paramedics kneeling beside him, and his loving wife by his side. Wild's happy expression is dropped, and asks to be put down. Tom and Tim oblige, and the world watches as Wild aproaches Alicia Powers from behind. He bends down and kneels beside her... She looks startled, as he asks the paramedics... "Will he be all right?" [They reply with an answer that not's easily understandable, so Wild looks over his shoulder to Alicia. Alicia wipes away a tear from her eye and looks back at Wild. She smiles faintly, and nods her head. Wild nods his head as well in approval, and pats Alicia on her shoulder. She brings her hand up and pats Wild's hand as she watches the paramedics roll her husband onto a stretcher, paying careful attention to his left shoulder. Once Alex is strapped onto the stretcher, Alicia looks back at Legion as Wild returns to his feet. She says the words, "Thank you." and quickly exits the ring, taking her husband's side as they roll the British Wonderkid, and former World champion, out into the aisle, making their way to the locker rooms, and an ambulance. Wild leaps up onto the turnbuckles facing the aisle, and surveys from the ring. More fireworks go off overhead, as Tom Landis grabs the UWF World title, and wraps it around the waist of the brand new World champion. Wild smiles... and once Kidd, Alicia and the medical team dissapear behind the curtain, he climbs down and points out to his Wild Pack. All of which are roaring with cheers and applause. In an era of grey areas and lost heroes... where bad is good and evil is cool... the entire capacity crowd are cheering on one of wrestling biggest, and best good guys... As the music kicks in a second time, Wild moshes for a moment, in appreciation of his Wild Pack. Without a word from our broadcast team... And as the camera focus on Youth Gone Wild with the world title... Fade to black.]
