[Focusing on Richards entirely now, Tasker picks him up and executes a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker and then tries for the pinfall. Richards kicks out again, so Chris executes the Rocker Dropper through the makeshift table, the pieces collapsing under the weight. Tasker goes for the pin, but again it's interrupted as Chris Nelson breaks the count, dropping an elbow across Tasker's back. Nelson pulls Tasker off Richards and drops him with a DDT facefirst into one of the surfboards(boy, are they ever being used today), and then covers him, trying to get the other pin. Tasker kicks out at the last second, and takes a piledriver from Nelson, then Matt Richards crawls over to make the pin. Bryan Sanders pulls him by the feet off of Tasker, and Nelson tries to kick him in the head for the interruption. Sanders ducks the kick and grabs a hold of his foot now, pulling him out of the ring. Nelson is pulled to ringside and tries to surprise Sanders with an enzuigiri, only to miss when Sanders ducks. He hits his leg on the ringpost and drops to the concrete, while Sanders re-enters the ring and climbs to the top rope, coming down onto Richards with a flying elbowdrop. Chris Tasker gets up on his feet again, pulls Richards up, and executes his gutwrench suplex on him, then covers him, getting the final three count.] AM: BAY BREAKER! BAY BREAKER! IT'S OVER! TD: Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners... THE MASTERS OF MOTION! SS: I can't believe they won, that referee is blind! DR: Make all the excuses you want Sam, the Masters of Motion have defeated the Twin Ninjas. [As the Masters of Motion stand on the turnbuckles, celebrating the hard-earned win, Nelson and Richards collect themselves, and Cameo tells them to attack. They do, grabbing each of the MoM and executing a back superplex to the mat. They start to kick both men, and Shinobi gets up on the ring apron and pleads with them to stop. He is given a microphone.] Shinobi: "Matt! Chris! Please... you do not have to do this! You two know better than that! These men you beat down, are your friends! There is no need for this attack! Please, stop it!" [Cameo is thrown a mic by Tom, upon requesting one.] Cameo: "Shut up old man. Frankly, I'm sick of hearing you speak to my men, and tell them what to do. Only I do that! I am their manager, not you! Shinobi: "Please... Miss Cameo, I meant no disrespect by it! I didn't mean to cause you to be upset. I am here to help." [Scott Daniels, Dan Kidd and Joey Valachi storm down the aisle, and head to the ring. Chris and Matt stands in a corner, as Tasker and Sanders are still down. Cameo is in the center of the ring looking very ticked off. Shinobi now steps through the ropes, and is in the ring looking very apologetic.] Cameo: "They don't need your help old man. They are Pride now!" [Scott and the rest now roll into the ring.] "They don't need you anymore." [Scott walks right up to Shinobi, and has a mic with him too.] Hotspot: "You got a lot of nerve old man, getting into this ring and telling us what to do! Who the hell are you to tell anyone in the Pride what to do? Huh?!? You're nobody! YOU ARE NOBODY!" DR: Oh come on, that's enough. Stop that, Scott Daniels. Shinobi doesn't deserve this, he is a legend of the ring. [Scott shoves at Shinobi's chest lightly when he talks, as if to bully him around. Chris Nelson watches on with concern, as Matt and Dan whisper something to each other.] Hotspot: "You have no business being out here geezer!" Shinobi: "Mr. Hotspot, I am very sorry! I meant no offense to occur." Hotspot: "Oh... wait a minute guys..." [Scott turns his back on Shinobi, and smiles as he talks to his fellow Pride mates.] Hotspot: "He meant nothing by it... boy, were we ever wrong. He's sorry. We should forgive him." [Scott whirls around right into Shinobi's face and yells at him.] Hotspot: "Forgiveness is something we've forgotten geezer because the Pride are sorry for nothing they've ever done! You are right though, because you are one sorry excuse of an old man I have ever seen! You are washed up! Now get the hell out of the ring and go back to Shady Pines or something! Just get the hell out, and don't bother coming back, because there's no place for your ninja crap here!" [The crowd boos massively as Tasker and Sanders slowly start to stir, but are then quickly levelled with kicks by Valachi and Kidd. Hotspot looks irrate, and Shinobi cowers against the ropes.] Shinobi: "I am sorry! Very sorry! I will leave. Good luck Matt and Chris. I'm going now... very sorry." Hotspot: "No... wait..." [Hotspot motions for Shinobi to stay.] Hotspot: "You know what?" [Hotspot takes a few steps back.] Hotspot: "It's too late now." DR: What?!? No! [Scott steps back more, until he stands with Joey, Matt, Chris and Dan.] Hotspot: "It's too late now. You're gonna pay... GET HIM GUYS!!!!" DR: Oh no! That's an old man, come on! This is utterly revolting. AM: I swear if they touch him I'll... SS: Shut up Red! I want to see this. [Dan and Joey slowly begin to stalk towards Shinobi, who cowers against the ropes. Nelson and Richards follow, and look at each other.] Hotspot: "Now you'll see why the Twinja's are Pride! Because the Pride is always right!!!" [Kidd shoves Shinobi down and Vexton laughs as they are about to attack. Nelson and Richards look at each other again and nod their heads in agreement... and then blast each Vexton and Kidd with a superkick out of nowhere. Kidd and Vexton go down hard and then the Twinja's lunge at Daniels and begin peppering him with right hands!] AM: Look at this, the Twin Ninjas have come to Shinobi's aid! Alright, this is more like it! SS: Those idiots, what the hell are they doing? [Matt and Chris lift Hotspot back to his feet, and give him the Clothesline Cradle to the ever-increasing pops of the crowd. Shinobi immediately embraces the Twinjas, who hug their former manager back. They put their heads together for a few minutes, and then extend their hands to the Masters of Motion, who are once again starting to stir. This time the Twin Ninjas help them up, and after a few more minutes of trying to convince their former friends, the MoM and Twinjas actually walk up the aisle to the loudest pop of the night. After waking up, the Pride leaves to a chorus of boos, and they are spitting mad.] AM: What a great sight that is. The Twin Ninjas are back with Shinobi, which is exactly where they belong. DR: This is truly a shocking turn of events we've seen, and for once it's for the better. SS: The Twin Ninjas are losers! They turned on the Pride, and for that they will pay. You'll see. DR: One more match fans, that's all that remains. Let's go to comments from the current World Tag Team Champions, Anarchy, and their challengers from the Pride. [Brett Greene and Shawn Vexton, both wearing the official Pride Wrestling t shirt, stand side by side in the hall outside a locker room door that reads "Pride" on a big brass plaque. Shawn seems to be explaining some strategy as the camera turns on, and the two talk quietly for a moment before noticing they're on.] BG: I have no idea who asked you back here, but as long as you are here why not make yourself useful ad film some comments for those idiots, Anarchy. SV: MadDog, Zombie, you may pride yourselves on being tag specialists, but remember that I was a former holder of those belts you prize so much, and Brett here has carried a stiff like Mark Durman to be a decent team. BG: The Pride's had lots of enemies in the past, and lots of problems to deal with now... Ninjas, you will regret your actions... but tonight, Anarchy, tonight you have my total focus and Shawn's total focus. We've got our eyes on some shiny gold to add to our resumes, not that they need the padding, and you guys are the ideal candidates. Remember, pain is temporary... SV: ...but Pride is forever. [Fade out as the two flex, holding their impressive biceps side by side. After a couple of seconds of blackness, a new image is seen. In the dim light, the audience sees the Pride; Brett Greene and Shawn Vexton talking next to each other, Daniel Kidd polishing his belt, Scott Daniels holding up a toaster. They aren't moving. A light switch is turned on, and the room suddenly gets brighter. Under the new light, the "Pride" are shown to be nothing but mannequins, dressed up like the Pride. Over in the corner, MadDog and Zombie look over the mannequins.] Zombie: Excellent work, MadDog. You really bring out the arrogance of the group, especially Greene. [MadDog grins and nods his head. He opens up a duffel bag.] Zombie: No, not yet. [Faces the camera] Hmm. The Pride. Four men who have dedicated their lives to dominating the UWF. The very mention of their name strikes anger and fear and respect into the hearts of every fan in the UWF. We admire that. So now they are targeting us. Perhaps they think that we will fall for the same ploys as all of the other wrestlers in this league. [MadDog starts giggling uncontrollably] Zombie: Pride. We do respect you. We respect you so much that it will be an honor to destroy you. An honor... and an outstanding performance. As for anger... we don't get angry. As for fear... there are things that scare me, Pride. There are things that even scare MadDog here. You're just not capable of causing those fears. [MadDog reaches into the duffel bags and pulls out a welding torch. Still giggling, he lights the torch, and points to the mannequins.] MadDog: Now?!?! Zombie: All right, MadDog. Now. [He reaches into the bag, and grabs another torch. As MadDog skips over to the mannequins, Zombie methodically lights his torch.] Zombie: They say that pride is the ultimate sin. Perhaps; perhaps not. But this Pride will be very repentant after our performance. Pain does that to people. [He also heads towards the mannequins. Fade.] AM: I really don't know which side to cheer for. I hope both come up broken and battered. ========================================== World Tag Team Championship Match SHAWN VEXTON/BRETT GREENE versus ANARCHY ========================================== [The crowd, upon seeing Tom Davis get back in the ring one final time tonight, rises to it's feet in huge pops.] TD: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is tonight's main event, and is for the UWF World Tag Team Championship! [Huge pop] Introducing first, the challengers... First, from Seattle, Washington, and a member of the Pride, weighing in at 255 pounds... SHAWN VEXTON! And his partner, from Baton Rouge, Louisiana, weighing in at 265 pounds... BRETT GREENE! [As the PA system gives the fans what they don't want, "Lakini's Juice" one more time, Brett Greene walks down the ramp towards the ring, and slaps the fans' hands away from him. Shawn Vexton walks out right after Brett, a p.o.'ed look on his face, and an equally disdainful attitude towards the fans. They slide into the ring, and Brett grabs the microphone from Davis.] BG: Alright, let's make this short and sweet. Twin Ninjas, your asses will belong to the Pride soon enough, and you've signed your careers away with what you did earlier. But right now there's two other freaks to deal with, the members of Anarchy. Zombie, MadDog, get your asses out here right now so we can take those gold belts of yours and shove them up your- [Brett's microphone is cut off by the signature "theme music" of Anarchy. The Vogon Poet's Guild is alive and well in San Diego, and seem to have bought up all the front row seats. MadDog and Zombie emerge from the locker rooms with the tag titles over their shoulders, and MadDog is also carrying a big box with a ribbon tied around it. Tom Davis ducks out of the ring, the smart thing to do, as Anarchy makes it's way down to the ring. The VPG at once start waving shiny objects around, and as Brett and Shawn realize what the objects are, they can't help but laugh.] AM: Aren't those- SS: Yup. Pride toasters. DR: How ironic. Wait a minute, here comes MadDog right towards our table. He's- offering you the box Amy. AM: I'm not taking that. [MadDig insists, but Amy still won't accept the gift. Finally Sam takes the box and tells MadDog that he'll open it for Amy, and MadDog grins and nods.] SS: Alright, let's see here. Hey Marshall, mind if I have these chocolates? AM: Knock yourself out Sam. And that goes double for you, MadDog. SS: [opening the box] I- Oh my god! Ack! [Sam throws the box down, and a nearby camera reveals the contents- pounds and pounds of big, thick, crunchy, cockroaches. Also inside is a note to Amy from MadDog, asking her how she likes the gift.] AM: That's precisely why I don't accept gifts from them. [Amy shudders, while the challengers finally get fed up and attack the champions from behind. Brett tosses MadDog back into the ring, while Shawn continues to kick Zombie in the ribs and drops elbows on him.] I wish the main event was better tonight. [Brett Greene puts MadDog in a chinlock while placing his knee in the back of his neck, and then changes up, into a sleeperhold. MadDog tries to break the hold, and manages to get to the ropes but Greene takes him down with a fireman's carry. Shawn Vexton, finished with Zombie at ringside, enters the ring to make it two-on-one, and the two Pridesmen hit MadDog with a double elbowdrop. Shawn leaves as Brett covers, but a kickout from the champion prolongs the match. Greene uses a front slam on MadDog, then presses him over his head and slams him to the mat again. To his credit, MadDog keeps getting back to his feet, but now Brett Greene executes the Pride Bomb on him. Another count by the referee, and the crowd senses new champions. MadDog kicks out, and Greene tries to hit MadDog with a legdrop but a roll away saves the championship. MadDog crawls over to the Anarchy corner, and tags out to Zombie while the VPG cheer like crazy(redundant description, no?).] SS: Those people are nuts for Anarchy. AM: You said it Sam. [Brett Greene whips Zombie into the ropes, but the former World Champion takes a kick to the midsection, and then a short lariat. Zombie then walks back to the corner and brings MadDog back in, for no reason whatsoever.] AM: Doesn't that defeat the purpose of tagging out? How could he have recuperated enough in that little time? [MadDog hits a flying spinning leg lariat on Brett Greene to answer any questions of how he is, and the VPG is behind MadDog 150%. He runs off the ropes and executes a somersault legdrop on Brett Greene, and pulls him right back up for an irish whip. A double clothesline sends members of each time sprawling, and Brett rolls to the floor. MadDog climbs out to the apron and dives at Brett, both being knocked over the guardrail into the sea of maniacal Anarchy fans. MadDog gets up, seemingly not feeling the pain, and whips Brett into the rail, then runs to clothesline him over it backwards. Brett sends him over in a backdrop first, and then climbs over, using an axehandle blow to keep MadDog down. He sends him back into the ring and then climbs in himself, but Zombie runs along the apron and stomps on his head, before allowing Brett to climb back in.] DR: This is a back and forth contest, either team could come out of this with the belts. MadDog with the cover, but Brett kicks out. MadDog hits Brett Greene with a spin kick, and then uses a Soviet suplex to put him down in a pin position. He covers Brett again, and Brett Greene kicks out to a loud pop. Another irish whip, and MadDog comes running at Brett only to take a backdrop right out of the ring. Greene crawls to the Pride corner and tags in a fresh Shawn Vexton, who's first move is to level Zombie off the apron with a running shoulderblock. When the other champion comes back into the ring, Shawn takes his knee out with a clip, and then locks MadDog in a bow-and-arrow submission. MadDog is able to escape it, but not a scorpion deathlock which is next from the younger Vexton. Zombie enters the ring with the referee distracted, and DDT's Shawn out of the hold, bringing Brett Greene in with a running lariat.] SS: Chaos! I love it! [Shawn Vexton works to his feet, and grabs an equally dazed MadDog, throws him into the ropes and connects with a spinebuster slam on him. Brett Greene meanwhile climbs to the top rope and nails Zombie with a flying bulldog, as Shawn rolls MadDog over.] SS: It's over! 1, 2, 3! New champions! DR: No! Kickout at the very last second! Anarchy is still alive! [Brett Greene tries to use an enzuigiri on MadDog, but being bigger than a cruiserweight he found it tough to do, and MadDog ducking did nothing to help. Shawn meanwhile is pushed out of the ring by Zombie and then takes a baseball slide kick to the jaw, right into a toastershot from an overexcited VPG member. All alone, Brett Greene is victimized by the doubleteam, a double kick to the midsection followed by a double forearm smash and then double vertical suplex. The referee gets Zombie to leave the ring, but an Asai moonsault by MadDog finishes Brett off. The count, and Anarchy remain champions.] TD: Here are your winners, and STILL UWF WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS... ANARCHY! [The champions roll out of the ring, not wanting to be the latest Pride target, and grab the championship belts before jumping out into the midst of their fans, the VPG. They dissapear into the crowd as Brett and Shawn leave via the aisleway, dissapointed and hearing the heel pops for them.] DR: What a main event that was, and Anarchy manage to retain the titles. You can't call them paper champions. SS: Well, I'll hand it to them, they were the better team tonight. But Brett and Shawn showed great cohesiveness tonight, and I hope they remain a tag team for at least a little while. They could be tag champions. AM: I hate to agree with you, but you may be right. Even still, I think the Twin Ninjas showed tonight their true colours when they defended Shinobi from those monsters. DR: Fans, we're out of time tonight, please tune in tomorrow for Sunday Morning Slam 'n' Eggs! Goodnight everybody. [Fade down to black on the stadium, the crowd still cheering loudly.]
