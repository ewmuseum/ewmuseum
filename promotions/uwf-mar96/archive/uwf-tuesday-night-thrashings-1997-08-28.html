UWF Tuesday Night Thrashings, 28 August 1997

28 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Part 1] [Fade up from black to the UWF logo, and then fade into clips from the battle royal from last Saturday. Members of various tag teams throw out other wrestlers, as a voice-over by Ashley Wilder starts.] AW: Last weekend the world got it’s first glimpse of a ‘Road to the Gold’ battle royal, as Travis Conley of Twister managed to pull off the upset, last eliminating Greg ‘Iceman’ Smith of Black Ice. In a few short days another battle royal will be held, this time to crown a new UWF World Heavyweight Champion. [Cut to a clip of Tom Landis rushing out and dropping Smith with the Chicago Thunderbomb, then Travis Conley rolls on top and makes the pin while Landis rushes away to huge cheers.] AW: 40 men will enter, but only one will stand as the champion of the league. Tonight, we get a preview of some of the frontrunners. Alex and Daniel Kidd fight once more over Daniel’s Cruiserweight Championship, which has yet to be declared official by the UWF. ‘Superman’ Mike Stewart faces Unified Champion Mike Stewart, and Bishop faces the rookie sensation Kyle Hatcher, a true PTP’er. And Paul Reed will be here live to bring the entire card for Summer Heat Wave to our attention. Tonight, on Tuesday... Night... Thrashings! [Cut to the opening of TNT. 'Hard To Make A Stand' by Sheryl Crow starts to play as fast clips of the UWF's roster fly by, three at a time. Marc Stevens catching someone in the DOA full nelson, Shawn Vexton spinebustering a jobber to the mat, and Ricky Johnson executing the second rope powerslam. Then the screen peels away to reveal more images, of the Balanced Budget from the Grapplers of Politics, the Clothesline Cradle from the Twin Ninjas, and a Cathedral DDT by Bishop. More images replace the old ones, and eventually we are left with images of the Riot Bomb, Tornado Drop, and Kalamazoo Clutch all at once. Another short fade to the Cobo Arena in Detroit, Michigan brings a view of a nearly empty arena, except for the usual small sized crowd hanging around to watch Ashley in action. Some carry signs from earlier in the show, and messages such as “Jericho is God!”, “Bring back Apocalypse!” and “Jen Shards is trash!”. Miss Wilder soon comes into view wearing a “Big Leagues? Big Deal.” t-shirt, knotted at the waist and exposing her midriff. She’s got her hair styled down, simply letting the long locks fall where they may, and has a small pair of jean shorts on too. She sweeps the hair out of her eyes as she opens the show with a giggle.] AW: Travis Conley? TRAVIS CONLEY?!? Ha ha ha ha! He won the battle royal? Good god, I hope a fluke of that type doesn’t happen on Saturday, or we could have one laughingstock of a world champion. Good, nice to get the jokes out of the way at the top of the show. But big news, like I promised during the opening. Ladies and gentlemen, the Atlantic Coast Wrestling Federation is no more. [A shocked murmur passes through the small crowd, almost all seeming unhappy with the news.] Just yesterday, a memo came down from the head of the ACWF, announcing the collapse of the league. This means that unfortunately, men like Joey Valachi, ‘Slick’ Ricky Reno, and the Chalmers Corporation have nowhere to go. Or at least, that is what we thought until earlier this afternoon when the UWF received a call for an interview request. Don’t ask me how, but Amy Marshall of all people was granted this special exclusive interview, and let’s go to her live. Take it, Red. [Cut to a live feed. The scene features a very large office, exquisitely furnished and damn near palatial in both it's size and decor. This office is probably worth more than most of our viewers. You can see through the windows the beautiful Toronto skyline, meaning this is probably UWF Towers. However, the office is much too ornate to be Paul Reed's. In one corner, Amy Marshall is seated in a patent leather chair across from a woman who's face can't be seen. The camera shifts to another angle, showing an immaculately dressed woman in her late thirties, who despite wearing one of those stupid power suits seems to be friendly judging from her facial expression. In fact, her entire appearance, like so many businesswomen, is designed to give off an air of harshness that may or may not be present. This is not to say, however, that she's totally unattractive. Quite the opposite, really. Marshall begins the interview as if hearing some far off cue. She seems to turn her nose up at the cue(wonder who from?) before beginning.] AM: I'm here in UWF Towers, the place where the UWF administration keep things running as smoothly as they do. With me now is the newest UWF Vice-President, Elizabeth Wilson. EW: Please, call me Lisa. I try not to be too formal unless I have to be. AM: [Amy smiles a bit, and goes on.] Well, Lisa, you are the first woman ever hired to the UWF Board of Directors, which I'd like to touch on in a few moments. But first, could you please tell us what exactly your duties consist of? EW: Certainly, I'd be happy to Amy. The UWF has decided to create a new division to be known as the Skyweight division, which is open to all wrestlers weighing 215 pounds or less. This division will be presided over by myself. AM: Certainly I and most people involved with the wrestling business know your credentials, but for the benefit of the viewers at home could you tell us a bit about your background? EW: Well, I've been involved in the wrestling business for almost 20 years now. I started out way back as a valet, which was a fun experience but not what I wanted to do all my life. A lot of women in the wrestling business rely on their looks and don't do anything else, but I was busy learning the business. I wanted to call the shots someday, and eventually the fed I was with, the Mid-West Championship Wrestling Federation, promoted me to a junior executive position. After that, hard work and dedication brought me up progressively through the ranks, and in the past six years I've handled the Light Heavyweight divisions of two different federations quite successfully. AM: I'm sure this division is an exciting prospect to a lot of lighter wrestlers, but I have to ask just who'll be competing in it. Will you be looking to sign in new talent among your duties? EW: At this time, the UWF is always looking for new talent. Due to the unfortunate collapse of the ACWF, we've picked up a few of those contracts, and I'm happy to announce the first two now. At Heat Wave '97's Free-For-All, former ACWF champion Joey Valachi will face an exciting Mexican superstar, Cosmos 3000. Now, we'll be looking to use some of the UWF stars who qualify in this division as much as they'd like to wrestle in it, as well as some of the ACWF wrestlers who are looking for work, but I would also like to hire some wrestlers from outside, as I'd like this to be a full-time division and not just a diversion for someone who's out of the title hunt elsewhere. AM: It's great to hear we have someone so compentent running this division, if I may say so... [Wilson blushes slightly] One thing springs to mind, however, seeing as you are the first high-ranking female executive the UWF has ever hired. Do you expect any troubles from the Skyweight wrestlers? By that, I mean do you think they'll try to test you because they don't think a woman can run the division as well as a man? EW: Anyone is welcome to try, Amy. My duties including scouting talent, booking matches, determining a fair way to award the Skyweight Title when it is commissioned, and if need be laying down the law. If anyone thinks I can't do that... well, I've been doing it longer then most of our roster's been wrestling. AM: That's about all the time we have right now, but I'd like to thank you for your time, Lisa, and I hope to hear from you soon with updates on how this Skyweight Divison is shaping up. EW: It's been a pleasure, Amy. Mind if I plug my division's debut? AM: [laughing] Not at all! EW: The Cardiac Kid vs Cosmos, only at Heat Wave '97. Catch it live on the Free For All! [The image of the plush office fades, and we return to the shot of Ashley in the arena, sitting at her desk.] Well, it looks like Joey Valachi will finally get to wrestle in the UWF. Not a moment too soon I might add. He and ‘Slick’ Ricky are definitely going to breathe some new life into this place. I just hope those idiots David Graves and ‘Caffeine-Seeker’ Tommy Elliott don’t show up. Seven matches were on the card tonight, and if nothing else the crowd was treated to see great talents like Daniel Kidd, Kyle Hatcher, and Black Ice. Of course, they also had to suffer through sights like Alex ‘Whiny Brat’ Kidd, Chris ‘Paper Champion’ Scottsdale, and the most gruesome sight of all, Sebastian ‘Screaming for a facelift’ Jericho. I say we dive right into the mat action, with Underground against Blaze to start the night. I know, not exactly spectacular, but the UWF does have them under contract, so they might as well dust them off once in a while. Poor Detroit. ========================== Blaze versus Underground ========================== The crowd seemed into the first match of the night, and both competitors had cornerpeople. For Underground, Marc Stevens and Crystal Cutglass accompanied him to the ring, while Ace Malone as always stood next Blaze(or in his corner, as it may be.) Underground seemed to be the lesser of two evils, and dominated much of the contest to the fans delight. He was able to overtake Blaze at many points in the match with submission reversals, blocks and counters, and a few high risk manoevers. A flying bodypress without using the ropes as a springboard or a slingshot brought the house down, but later on Blaze replied with a kamikazie dive over the top rope, flattening Underground on the concrete floor. Finally as the match seemed to be over with a bow-and-arrow submission hold, outside influences showed up. From the locker rooms stormed Robbie Greer, Jen Shards, and Blaze was jumped by Greer in the ring, causing him to win by DQ. Ace Malone was of little help as Lydia Anderson showed up in the crowd, standing in the front row and confronting Ace with the question “Why did you leave me to think you were dead?!?”. Stephan LeRoc emerged from the crowd after a few minutes of Lydia’s pleading, and pulled her away from Malone, looking at his cousin with nothing but pure hatred. Inside the ring meanwhile, Marc Stevens joined in on the attack on Blaze, without realizing that Robbie Greer was the other attacker. Suddenly he figured it out, motioned to Underground, and Greer was blasted with the Magma Wave reverse DDT. The Asylum left the ring, while shortly after Blaze and Robbie Greer left seperately. WINNERS: By disqualification, Blaze AW: What a scene. And to think, in that group three of them have held the Cruiserweight Championship. Stevens twice, Blaze, and LeRoc. And don’t forget the UWF still recognizes the Asylum’s Chronosphere as champion, even though everybody knows Daniel Kidd now posesses it. And speaking of champions, I had a chance to interview the brand new United States Tag Team Champions as they arrived here at the arena, a chance I didn’t really want. Hah, if anybody in the league doesn’t deserve gold, it’s the Gruesome Old Potheads. And I found out something, those three have about as much class as Ross Perot. [Scene: Outside the arena, Ashley Wilder is standing in front of a large group of UWF fans who are watching as various UWF stars pull into the back entrance leading to the locker room. She does not look pleased by this assignment.] AW: And here I am, sentenced to wait outside standing next to fans who didn't think they got to look at Sebastian Jericho enough in the ring, they have to come out here and try to see him going in as well. Why the UWF is interested in people this stupid is beyond me... [Behind Wilder, a fan yells out "We love you, Ashley!"] AW: OK, maybe they're not ALL stupid. [A rented Lincoln pulls into the entrance, and from it emerge the Executive Aide, Kenny Carroll, and Reggie Martin, aka the Grapplers of Politics. They are dressed in street clothes, and carrying the U.S. Title belts. The GOP are the type of team most people either really love or really hate, so they provoke a strong reaction in the fans outside. The GOP, however, seem distracted, as they only produce faint smiles, and a few waves to the fans.] AW: They're not my favorite people in the world, but given the alternative, they'll do. Hey guys! How does it feel to know you owe your U.S. Title victory to Sanchez and Karensky? EA: We owe nothing to those two. Anarchy has never been able to stand up to men like these. Karensky was just searching for another avenue to cause trouble, that's all. AW: So are you looking forward to the title match at Heat Wave? KC: As champions, we are obligated to face whoever the UWF deems to be the top challengers. If those challengers are Working Class, so be it. Even fans who don't like us know that we never give anything less then our best, and that's what Working Class will have to face. [Not the most enthusiastic response. Wilder begins to get a little irked.] AW: Come on, that can't be the best you can do. Sanchez sold you out! Repudiated everything you stand for! Spit on you! And all you can say is "We'll do our best to win?" RM: Julio made a mistake, but the decision was his to make. Wrestling spends too much time being driven by hate. We can't pretend we don't feel betrayed, but life's too short to waste time trying to destroy people like Karensky and Sanchez. We will win because we're the better team, and then Julio will realize what a horrible mistake he's made. Hopefully it won't be too late. [The GOP begins to walk away, but Wilder, determined to get a rise out of them, isn't done yet.] AW: [Yelling] Isn't it true that nobody in your party knows anything about satisfying a woman? [The GOP pause, and turn around. Wilder has a smug look on her face, obviously very pleased with herself.] EA: Very droll. RM: [With a small smile] Actually Ashley, my wife and I have been married for six years. Kenny's been happily married for nine years, and the EA's has been married for over twelve years. What's the longest amount of time you've ever persuaded a guy to hang around? [With that parting shot, the GOP enter the arena as Wilder fumes. The shot fades, and Ashley looks no more happy now than she did on the tape.] AW: Boy, I can't wait until Heat Wave. Karensky and Sanchez are going to take those two apart. And as for 2000? Well, to that I can only say “No More With Clinton OR Gore!”. Next up this evening was a battle between pugface and Los Muertos. Imagine, one guy who wears a mask against another guy who should wear one. As a matter of fact, the hometown beagle had some words, which the crowd just ate up. It figures, travel to a sewer of human life, expect to be surrounded by waste. [Fade in on a somewhat urban looking blacktop, tattered and beaten... it's obvious that this place isn't in good condition, nor is it a place where a lot of people seem to frequent. The exercise equipment consists of a tilted over basketball goal, a pull-up bar, and a weight bench, all of which don't look too sturdy. On the far side of the screen, Sebastian Jericho can be seen working out on the beaten old weight bench. The camera zooms in, so that every heavy breath the pugnacious superstar takes can be made out, each one like a battle cry, stating that he won't give up, and must press on. His meaty, thickly muscled arms pump and bulge as he pushes and pulls on the bar, which looks to hold a weight of more than three hundred and fifty pounds. After what was probably a final set of ten, Sebastian rests the heavy barbell on its pedestal, and sits up slowly, beads of sweat trickling down his shoe-leather forehead. He is almost out of breath as he speaks.] Sebastian Jericho: Oh, good, you're here. [He pauses, trying to get a bit of his wind back, and wipes his face with a nearby towel in the process.] Sebastian Jericho: I'm sure some of you are wondering why I'm at an old rundown place like this working out for my match with Los Muertos. If you're not, bear with me, you're gonna hear about it anyway. [He chuckles slightly, his last comments back in his normally rich and baritone-like tone.] Sebastian Jericho: It gives me a sense of being alone... being away from everything, all the distractions, all the noise. This is a place where I can clear my head and just think about my next match... that happens to be the match with Los Muertos this Tuesday. [He wipes some of the sweat from his face with the black t-shirt he wears, which reads "Jericho: All Business" in white bold-face type.] Sebastian Jericho: Muertos... a couple words for you. Spare the rod you spoil the child...your attitude is pretty bad, I think you need a lesson in humility. I'm gonna give you the beating your daddy shoulda given you. [Fade to black. Fade up on the wrestling ring in the arena.] ====================================== Los Muertos versus Sebastian Jericho ====================================== For the first time in his (UWF at least) career, Seb seemed to be amused with the crowd response, which came in the form of tremendous cheers. They cheered on the Michigan native, and Jericho entered the ring feeling the rush of adrenaline from the audience. Joe Reed also made his presence known, simply by sitting in the stands a few rows up to scout the match. Los Muertos was clearly the heavy in the match, and Seb dominated much of it with a power based attack, easily able to overmatch the masked man. Muertos got in a few good shots with his fists, and even took Jericho down with a vertical suplex and nearly scored the pin. But Jericho kept at it and fought bravely, continuing even after damaging his leg from a leg whip to the mat. He stood on the one good leg and executed the raptureplex, but it was only after he caught Muertos during a flying cross bodyblock and slammed him down to the mat with a front slam that he gained the pinfall. Muertos argued that his feet were outside the ring, but clearly on the replay they were not(not that it’d matter in the decision anyways.). Joe Reed shook his head and exited the arena, obviously not impressed with Muertos tonight. WINNER: Sebastian Jericho AW: He won again. Is there any justice? Wait, yes there is. LCA gets demolished by Black Ice. ========================================== Black Ice versus Lights, Cameras, Action ========================================== There was hardly any match to this, as Greg Smith was bent on getting a little revenge, wanting to erase the end of the battle royal on Saturday night. Black Diamond was mean as ever, and the dynamic duo didn’t stand a chance. Devon Taylor matched up well at first and was able to avoid Diamond’s devastating moves with simple escapes and counters. But after getting kicked in the back by ‘Iceman’, Taylor was put on the mat and left there. Both Diamond and Smith wiped the floor with him, and when he finally did tag out to Frankie Hollywood, the much smaller man was pinned directly after a vicious boot to the face as he came off the top rope. A Snakebite still followed after the match, and Black Ice continued to steamroll the competition. WINNER: Black Ice AW: The single greatest tag team in the UWF wins another. And unlike Sam Steeley, I don’t flip flop to whoever I’m speaking to. Twin Ninjas, you two are near the top of the list, but nobody has the sheer power of Greg and Big D. Another man who has the tools to be one of the greatest is Kyle Hatcher. And he had some words for his opponent, Bishop, before their match. [Camera cuts to the locker room from earlier in the day. Kyle Hatcher stands next to Moe Owens wearing blue jeans, black boots, and a white t-shirt.] MO: Fans, I'm standing here with "Primetime" Kyle Hatcher, the man who will later today met the legendary Bishop one-on-one in the ring. Kyle, when you first came to the UWF, you made a challenge to Bishop. Now, it seems like your challenge has been answered. KH: Yeah, my challenge has been answered. It seems the preacher man got somehow forced into signing a contract with the hottest young prospect on the UWF scene. The man with the golden touch, "Primetime" Kyle Hatcher. Now, Bishop, I know I'm supposed to be in awe of all your talents and of your career record. Former IEWF World champion and such, but that doesn't matter to me. What matters to me is kicking your teeth and walking all the way to the top of the UWF. MO: Speaking of the top of the UWF, the man who presently is actually the number one man in the federation is the United States heavyweight champion, Scott "Hotspot" Daniels. We all know that you've made it clear of your intentions to join the Pride, but Daniels seems to be especially vocal about not letting you in the group. KH: You know, when I came into the UWF, I threw down the title that I won in that punk federation called the AEWA. I proved to everyone that I am loyal to the UWF, and since I've come into this federation, I've beaten everyone I've gone up against. They keep coming at me and I keep knocking them down. That's how I do it. But now Scott Daniels has taken if upon himself to keep me from joining the most elite group in all of the UWF, the Pride. He's taken it upon himself to be the leader of the Pride and attack me. I haven't heard a thing from Brett Greene or the other members of Pride about me, and yet Daniels has been doing all the talking for them. Well, Daniels, I think it's time you saw what I'm really all about face-to-face. I think that once you see me in the ring, you'll see me for what I really am: Pride material. MO: So what are you saying? KH: I'm saying that I want a match with Scott Daniels. I want to show him how good I am and how I deserve to be in the Pride. I don't care if it's after the pay-per-view... where I will be kicking Youth Gone Wild's ass by the way... or before. I want him in the ring, with or without the U.S. title on the line, and I'm gonna show him what I'm all about. [The interview fades down, and fade up to Bishop standing in the ring with Hatcher.] ======================================== Bishop versus ‘Primetime’ Kyle Hatcher ======================================== Bishop, being the bigger man, was able to take over the match quickly with a few clotheslines, knocking Hatcher down. For the next few minutes Bishop dominated with punches, bearhugs, legdrops, and piledrivers. And just when Hatcher seemed to be down and out, Bishop whipped him into the ropes and was felled with a frankensteiner into a cradle. Hatcher held on for a count of two, before Bishop kicked out. Hatcher went on the offensive and was doing well, until Bishop caught him in a gorilla press. Still he struggled to break out, and dropped to his feet behind Bishop, then rolled him up from behind and pulled the tights to gain the win. The crowd gasped in disbelief, and Kyle took the wise move and left before Bishop got angry at the loss. WINNER: Kyle Hatcher AW: Yes, you saw it here fans. Kyle Hatcher defeats Bishop! As a matter of fact, right after the match it seems that Bishop was very upset after losing to Hatcher, and threatened to quit the UWF. What a crybaby. Boo-hoo. The Twin Ninjas and Twister were on deck next, but both teams spoke before the match. I love the Twinjas, but Twister have real attitude problems. [Part 2] [Cut to an interview recorded before the program. The scene opens up to the locker room in Cobb Arena, as Travis Conley and Paul Langford are sitting on a bench in front of the lockers, with Kelly Perich standing behind them.] PAUL LANGFORD: Travis, I just want you to know how proud Kelly and I are of you with your great victory last Saturday in the battle royal. TRAVIS CONLEY: Thanks, Paul. Without the support of you and Kelly, and of course, the great support of the fans, I never would have made it out of there on top. I can't say that I'm thrilled with the way it ended, but I understand Landis had a score to settle with Black Ice. I would have loved to won it cleanly, but after that wild match, I'm just glad to have won for us. KELLY PERICH: But, now we have put that behind us, and concentrate on our opponents for tonight. We know them well, as we beat them last week. But instead of just moving on up the ranks, we are being pulled into a feud with these Twin Ninjas. I can't say I'm not happy with that fact, as now we have a chance to put these two into their proper place. TC: Richards, Nelson, we heard what you said last week, about us saying rude and vulgar things to you in the hallway before the match. So, as for you two, all I can say is... [silence, as Conley looks into the camera and smiles.] PL: [Breaking the silence with a small chuckle.] I know what you mean Travis. Twinjas, all I can say is this. Our goal here is to become champions, whether we as a team win the tag straps, or we as individuals win singles championships. Travis took everything you two could give last week, and we came out on top. Then, he takes everything 12 other wrestlers could give this past Saturday, and he came out on top. Travis and I had talked, and if it happened that one of us drew the other's name, then the one who drew the name would let himself be tossed out. You see, although we both wanted to win it, our friendship and our tag team mean more. Richards, Nelson, after what happened last Saturday, can you say the same? [The scene is backstage in front of the UWF interview area, logo on the wall. Peter Phillips stands with Matt Richards and Chris Nelson, the Twin Ninja's.] PP: Guys, tonight you grapple with Twister, a team that you have a 'beef' with, but I must say... your anger seems to be much more of a farce than it is fact. CN: Of course you would, wouldn't you Peter? You go out and have the Twinja's all figured out and judged, just because we choose not to cater to the fans any more. We haven't changed much Peter. Let's see the footage one more time. [The screen splits to show the black and white security camera's shot of a hallway, somewhere backstage from last Saturday Night's card. We see the backs of Travis Conley and Paul Langford walking down the hallway with Kelly Perich. They are discussing last minute strategy for the battle royal. The fronts of Chris Nelson and Matt Richards are now shown walking down the opposite way of the hallway, towards Twister and the camera. Twister ignore the Twinja's presence, as they are still discussing the match. Nelson walks by just fine, without making a scene, but as Matt passes, he turns around and in a very rude and sarcastic tone of voice speaks to Twister.] Matt Richards: Hey losers, good luck in the battle royal. You idiots are going to need it! [Travis, Paul and Kelly just keep on walking, not falling for Richards' tricks, as Travis just says...] TC: Yes, good luck to you too. [...loud enough for The Twinja's to hear. Nelson disappears from the camera's sight as does Twister. Richards stops with his hands on his hips, as if he were taking offense to Travis' comments. The screen jumps back to Peter with The Twinja's.] CN: See that!!! Did you see that utter lack of respect for my partner??? [Matt paces around once more, with his hands on his head looking more and more frustrated.] PP: But Travis didn't say anything wr-- MR: Shut up!!! I've had it! I've had enough of Twister's sarcastic comments backstage! I've had enough of UWF politics! I've had enough of everyone not believing us! This kind of stuff goes on every night in the locker rooms! Twister's sense of higher being! I went out of my way to extend my well wishes to Twister, and they shoot it back in my face with the most sarcastic [bleep] I have ever seen!!! Well no more! You two hicks are in for it now! I've got a savate kick that will twist your frekaing heads off! You've beaten us once, and we can take that... BUT I CAN'T TAKE ANY MORE OF YOUR ATTITUDES!!! [Matt storms off, out of sight. Chris looks at Peter angered.] CN: Now look what you did Peter! You ticked him off even worse! Is that everyone in UWF's job? TO make the Twin Ninja's feel worse than they already do! We can't take this much more Phillips. We just can't do it... we have rights too. If you'll excuse me, I have a partner to attend to. [Chris runs off camera yelling 'Matt!'] PP: What sarcasm??? I think that The Twinja's are just bitter about their clean loss to Twister, and are using this as a cover-up to their unprofessional bitterness. Back to Ashley and the rest of Thrashings. ================================ The Twin Ninjas versus Twister ================================ Twister came out to a huge pop, as their popularity these days keeps growing and growing. Kelly Perich got as loud a pop as any of the other valets do, and Conley and Langford walked to the ring wearing the CWAA Tag Team Championships, having reclaimed them on the weekend at a special event. Nelson and Richards came out with Cameo to balance the numbers at ringside out. The contest itself went back and forth several times, and each team had the chance to put the other away with one of their special finishes, Nelson blasting Langford with a superkick at one point and Conley just about to hit Nelson with the Husker Slam. A turning point came in the match when Matt Richards leapt off the top rope for a moonsault legdrop, nailed it, and then dragged Conley into the center of the ring, and then taunted the fans by asking them “This is who you cheer?” That comment lit a fire in Conley, who pulled his knees up in time to stop a splash from Richards, and lunged to make the tag. Paul Langford entered, and it became a huge pier six brawl, with all four men battling it out. Matt Richards was knocked out of the ring with a clothesline from Langford, and he landed at Cameo’s feet. She helped him up and slipped him a small object, which he then held in his fist as he re-entered the ring, and spun Conley around and nailed him between the eyes with it. The Twister’ite went down like a ton of bricks, and Richards pulled the referee over to make the count, and the Ninjas got the pin. Before the referee could see the object Richards rolled out of the ring, and Chris Nelson joined him and Cameo in walking out, arms raised. The members of Twister regrouped, still not knowing what had happened(wait till THEY see the replay). WINNERS: The Twin Ninjas AW: I love to see a great team win a match with finesse. And the Twin Ninjas did just that. They’re going to be World Champions someday. And once again speaking of champions, Daniel Kidd looked to put his Cruiserweight Title up against his cousin, the scene stealer Alex Kidd earlier. And then he got smart. [Daniel Kidd is standing in a darkened studio with a large "Big Leagues? Big Deal." graphic floating on the back wall. We can assume that's a camera trick. He smirks at the camera, patting his title belt which is draped over a shoulder.] DK: Seeing as I'm the UWF's _real_ Cruiserweight Champion, I thought it'd only be fair to seek out the best wrestlers in the weight class to defend against. Someone has to look out for the fans and make sure they get to see some great Cruiserweight action.
