[Fade up from black into a hospital setting, subtitled "After the last SNR". The hospital hallway is deadly quiet as "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme paces back and forth like a cat on the prowl. Both brothers seem very calm and at ease while engrossed in their current activities. Alex Martinez is strangely calm. The tall man is sitting in a chair, his feet propped up on the chair across from him. He's quiet, and his face is unreadable. But his demeanor is completely the opposite of the manic Extreme.
