A camera placed far away starts to zoom in, and we see the blue and red truck bearing the UWF logo on the side, as well as the words "Tuesday Night Thrashings". Fade into another camera shot, inside the truck. Ashley Wilder, host of TNT, sits by an editing bay, behind her the various VTR's, edit controllers and monitors blink and flash. Ashley wears a bright blue t-shirt with the words "Championship Challenge? More like a Lesson in Pride.", and also a pair of tight fitting bluejeans, topped off with her blonde hair in a ponytail. The music fades out, and Ashley looks at the camera.] AW: Welcome once again, all you lonely out-of-shape couch potatoes who want a glimpse of the perfect woman. I'm Ashley Wilder, but I'm sure you all knew that. Tonight the UWF travelled to Oakland, California, continuing the Sun, Sand and Slams Tour. Okay, maybe it's not an official tour, but we've been baking out here for a couple of weeks now. President Reed, this has been great and all, but enough is enough, time to leave this state. Enough about the setting, we have eight great matches to bring to you tonight, including one debut that has broken with tradition. Ladies and gentlemen, hot on the heels of the Lady in Red's interview on Slam 'n' Eggs, Paul Reed signed her to her first match. Not much of a challenge with JBK, the JobBreak Kid, but it's a start. I don't like her attitude, I don't like her man, but I'll give her credit for coming out and getting a contract to wrestle here in the UWF. You all know I'm petitioning for a match against little Stevie Armstrong, but I'm not looking for a full time career in the ring. Girl Power. Let's go to the film after we get the LiR's thoughts. [The Lady in Red, wearing a one-piece workout suit (red, of course), stretches her legs using the bench. Wearing a grey suit and a red tie, 'Crimson' Joe Reed holds a notebook in his hands.] CJR: The camera crew's finally ready. You want me to take this? LiR: [looking up] I can handle it, Joe. [CJR leaves the room, while the LiR faces the camera] Someone in the UWF finally grew a spine and took a chance. I have to admit that I'm pleasantly surprised. I finally get a real match, not under any mask, or against any some fluff chick. A real match, against some real competition. [She smiles] It's about damn time. Alex Kidd says he won't wrestle a woman. Well, Kidd, I am going to prove to you that this woman deserves a match for a title, even your title. I don't give a damn about the cheers from anyone out here in the UWF. But I demand their respect, and I'm going to earn it, even if it means that I have to beat it out of each and every wrestler in the UWF. Even you, Alex. ========================================= [DEBUT] THE LADY IN RED versus THE JOBBREAK KID ========================================= When the crowd heard the announcement that the Lady in Red was making her debut tonight, half decided to boo her for being anti-UWF, the other half cheered her on for the groundbreaking match. One thing was clear though, nobody really cared about JBK, nor did they like his self-sung theme music: I think I'm cute, But I'm really ugly, I've got the looks, That makes the girls sick, I've got the moves, That really bore 'em, I get a chill, When I'm slammed on my spine, I'm no sexy boy, I ain't no boy toy, I'm no sexy boy, I ain't no boy toy, I make them puke, I make them quiver, I'll make 'em sick, Whenever I'm around, They see me job, They hear me cry, I make them feel, Like they wish they were high, I'm no sexy boy, I ain't no boy toy, I'm no sexy boy, I ain't no boy toy, I'm no sexy boy I ain't no boy toy... As the music trailed off, the Lady in Red immediately ran at JBK and floored him with a clothesline, and the crowd cheered her for it. JBK proceeded to get up and take a series of punches on the chin, and Joe Reed encouraged the LiR to keep it up. She grabbed the legs of JBK and turned him over into a half boston, working on his left leg until he was forced to struggle for the ropes. The only woman standing 6'6 in the UWF pulled JBK back to his feet and bodyslammed him, then dropped an elbow across his chest. The LiR bounces off the ropes for a kneedrop that caught JBK in the stomach, and tried to pin him. Miracurously JBK kicked out(for the first time in his career that we can think of), and when the LiR irish whipped him to the ropes a second time, he ducked her swinging punch and knocked her backwards into the corner with a dropkick. She saw JBK running towards her and avoided a running elbowsmash, then kicked him in the stomach, set up for and delivered a DDT to the mat. JBK was knocked clean out, and the LiR hooks the leg to gain her first win. On her way out she actually heard a chant of "L - I - R!" and seemed to like it, but when Reed caught up and walked next to her, the boos for him outweighed the cheers for her. WINNER: The Lady In Red AW: Like I said, JBK didn't put up much of a fight, but then again neither did Alex Kidd at War in the Desert. The Lady in Red may not be in favour of the UWF, but at least she has to agree that this is the only league to let her wrestle as she is. Not even the AEWA does that. The very next contest was between the rookie sensation Jamal Shabazz and KO Corral member Sterling Justice. A word to Brewster Cogburn and Running Eagle Feather though, you dumped Garavello out of the Corral because he wasn't tough enough. What about Justice? What about Durman? The last time either of them won, Bret Hart heard cheers in America and Shawn Michaels didn't sicken anybody. ==================================================== JAMAL 'BLACKFISTS' SHABAZZ versus STERLING JUSTICE ==================================================== As the theme from "Shaft: The Motion Picture" started up, the crowd gave Jamal Shabazz a pretty decent pop upon his entrance. He didn't seem to be too interested in the fans, only getting to the ring and proving his superiority over Sterling Justice. Justice, a member of the Corral, was not backed up, not even Jack Moran was out at ringside. Shabazz made short work of him, blitzing him with clotheslines at first, and then rocking him with elbow after elbow. Sterling fought back with raw power, picking Shabazz up and gorilla pressing him to the mat, then picking him up and catching him in the Unconditional Surrendor, a torture rack-like move. Shabazz managed to struggle out of it after breaking Sterling's grip, then slid down his back, pulled Sterling into position for a reverse DDT, and instead lifted him straight up and then straight down into a backbreaker across his knee. Justice rolled to the ropes, gasping for air, and Shabazz threw him into the opposite ropes and executed the retooled Shockazulu, an old-style spinebuster. He covered, got the win, and rolled under the ropes, beginning to walk up the ramp. The crowd burst into a heel pop as Brewster Cogburn, Mark Durman, and Running Eagle Feather raced down the ramp and into the ring, and they proceeded to thrash Sterling Justice within an inch of his life, and effectively kicking him out of the Corral. Meanwhile, Moe Owens was sitting at ringside and ran up beside the 2 and 0 newcomer, trying to get a word with him. WINNER: JAMAL 'BLACKFISTS' SHABAZZ MO: Mr. Shabazz... Mr. Shabazz... May I ask you a few questions? Shabazz: [turns and glares at Moe and slowly strokes his face with heavily taped fingers] "You can ask little man, but that don't mean Blackfists will answer." MO: Your recent debut in the UWF began alot of chatter the competition and fans alike. It's even been rumored that your being scouted by a couple of the major stables, any comment? Shabazz: [touching his afro] "I hear alot of talk but see no action. Within a couple of weeks I'll be busting heads just like I did with that poor white trash Sterling Justice. If members of the Pride or the Legion want to add me to their rosters then they'd better show some respect. Where I'm from gangs are backstabbers and your better watchin' your own back." MO: Why would you turn down an offer from a group who could help you gain a title shot? Shabazz: "Like I said, they tell me everything and show me nothing. Alex Kidd and his crew are the most talented among the UWF ranks but would they want someone of my profile in their ranks? I get the job done and that's what it comes down to. The Pride has a lot of talent and attitudes to match, but would they consider a black powerhouse as a threat? I've spoken with members of all the stables in the UWF and I'll make a decision when you least expect it." MO: How did you become associated with this federation? Why did you come here? Shabazz: [shaking his head "no"] "No more questions little man, go back to the broadcast booth before I get angry. Many are called but few are chosen, the UWF will become a mainstay for "Blackfists" Shabazz. Men of the UWF, watch your back... 'cause black is back again and I'm gonna win!!! BOOOYAH!!!!" [Shabazz begins a walk to the dressing room as a very suprising pop from the crowd arises. Could this be the UWF's newest superstar? Cut back to Ashley in the truck.] AW: I told you Justice couldn't win, and now he can't walk either. Ha ha! Meanwhile Jamal Shabazz is on a roll, and that superfly cat is headed for greatness. I just hope he doesn't hook up with Legion, that'd be bad news. As for Luis Garavello, he's bad news anytime he shows up. Luckily for the crowd here in Oakland, he didn't. That attack by the Pride last Saturday aggrevated his injuries stemming from the original Corral attack, and he's in the hospital trying to recover. Like I always say, good things happen to good people, and vice versa. Stay away as long as you need, Kid, the ratings are starting to soar again. Jimmy Dangerous was without a match tonight, at least until somebody else stepped up to the challenge. Take a look. ===================================== JIMMY DANGEROUS versus ____________ ===================================== Jimmy Dangerous came out, flanked by Ms. Miki and 'The Samurai' Kensei Tokugawa, each on one side. Jimmy is dressed in a totally different outfit then last we saw him, a black leather jacket, buzzed haircut, and full length black tights with the Daimyo logo on the left leg. Tom Davis announced that Luis Garavello wouldn't be out tonight, and the match was to be cancelled. Jimmy grabbed the microphone from Tom. JD: Alright, listen up people. Jimmy D. is here, and ready for a match. I don't give a damn who I face, whether that Kid drags himself out of the hospital room, Alex Kidd gets down off his pedestal, Brett Greene puts on that mask of his, or JBK gets back out here. JD is gonna show his loyal fans once again why he is OK. Jimmy dropped the mic and stood in the corner waiting for somebody to appear, and the crowd popped heavily as the theme song from "Twister" began. Both Paul Langford and Travis Conley came out, pointing at the ring and at Dangerous, who wasn't ready for them. Kelli Perich led them to ringside and then the two jumped into the ring, going after Dangerous. Kensei Tokugawa tried to enter the ring and use the kendo stick on Twister, but Conley grabbed the stick from the 'Samurai' and cracked him over the head with it, and then as Langford caught Jimmy in a drop step toehold, Conley dropped it across his chest. Dangerous and the other members of the Daimyo scurried out of the ring and up the ramp, leaving Twister holding the kendo stick, and hearing the crowd cheer them on. WINNER: MATCH CANCELLED AW: Hey, you weren't expected to see those two gutless wonders come out, were you? They specialize in taking advantage of surprising people, and that Kelli Perich is as nasty as you can get. Jimmy and the Samurai will get even with them, you can bank on it. The next match between Frank White and Bishop took a little bit of time to get started, because White decided to run his mouth off first. Here's what happened. ==================================== FABULOUS FRANK WHITE versus BISHOP ==================================== 'Fabulous' Frank White made his way out to the ring accompanied by his bodyguard Nailz when he was announced, before Bishop. Most of the fans booed and threw garbage, save for a section of female fans. As soon as he got into the ring, he started posing off and exposing his ravishing body. Before Davis could announce his opponent, White grabbed the mic(boy Tom had trouble with keeping the mic in hand tonight). White: Ahhh... Fabulous as ever... At least the ladies seem to think so judging by the whistles and comments being made to me. Don't worry men, I'll leave all of you a handful of women to fight over[laughs; some men grunt]. Come on guys, what do you expect when you're competing with a man like me, the most fabulous one of all, surely you must understand if I take your ladies away from you. Some men that were with their wives try to climb over the barrier and into the ring but security managed to hold them back while White further taunted them. White: Anyways, enough of that talk, time to get to the issue at hand. Tonight, one on one, me and Bishop. Bishop... [chuckles] ...that's exactly what you are....a piece on the checkerboard, a pawn in the game, a game that I'm playing with my object being to be the best in the UWF. [Pauses] Wait, aren't I already the best? [laughs] Well I guess tonight will just be a little demonstration of what I plan to do to everyone in the UWF. [Bishop started making his way to the ring, or a look - alike Bishop at best.] White: Well well, look who decides to show up for his match. ['Bishop' walked over to the announcers table and grabbed an auxiliary microphone.] 'Bishop': Mr... Mr... White... uh... White: That is Mr. Fabulous Frank White, get it right! 'Bishop': Mr. Frank White... 'Fabulous' Frank White... I was wondering if I could get out of our match because... well... you are too good of a wrestler for me to wrestle... you'll tear me to pieces and I'd be quite embarassed. White: What? You want out of our match? Hmmm... get in the ring boy! Climb through those ropes and come here. ['Bishop' climbed into the ring.] White: You're right, I will kick your sorry ass. Second of all, no one gets out of a match with me. The only option you have is whether you want the match now or later. [Bishop started to tremble, so White went on.] White: You know what? Since I feel kind of sorry for you, I'll delay your ass kicking for another time, is that ok? [White patted 'Bishop' on the shoulder.] 'Bishop': Oh, thanks Mr. Fabulous... White: No problem. Now get out of my face. 'Bishop' started to leave but Nailz grabbed him and drove him down with a chokeslam. White then proceeded to apply the figure four on 'Bishop, snapping his his leg, then he posed over top of the fallen 'Bishop'. White: I guess there won't be no match afterall, oh well. White and Nailz left the ringside area with boos and garbage thrown at them. However, as they started walking up the ramp, the lights dropped down to blackness. The Cathedral Remix was heard as a single red spotlight hit the wrestler entrance, and the real Bishop appeared. The crowd went wild when they saw his visage, and as the lights return to normal he charged at White and Nailz, knocking both down from a clothesline. Bishop picked the smaller White up and threw him into the ring while Nailz was left to himself on the ramp, and Bishop began to kick White, and then rolled the unfortunate Bishop clone out of the ring. White got back on his feet and tried for a clip to Bishop's knee, but Bishop spun around in time, and caught him with a knee to the face. Meanwhile up on the ramp, Serge Annis skulked out, hearing a tremendous boo, and the Epitome helped Nailz to his feet, and then handed him some sort of foreign object. By the time Nailz regained his senses Serge was gone, and he found the object in his hand. White meanwhile was being set up for the Cathedral DDT from the top rope, so Nailz rushed down to the ring to save White. He leaps up on the apron and jammed the foreign object into Bishop's forehead, knocking the Avatar of Extreme to the mat. The referee called for the DQ, and White flew down onto him with a flying kneedrop before rolling to the floor and escaping with Nailz right behind him. Bishop sat up, wiped the stream of blood from his face, and left in search of Annis once more, the crowd totally behind him. WINNER: BISHOP BY DQ AW: As if that wasn't bad enough, when the idiot couldn't find Serge, he came back out a little later. [The lights dim down to a dusky tone. The crowd falls to a hush as the first three tones of a Deaths bell ring out.] Voice: Lights. [Suddenly, a large crimson cross is superimposed over the crowd from the spotlights above. Coloring the arena the color of blood.] Voice: And now the sermon. [The PA system starts to pump out the sounds of the Cathedral Remix, the crowd as one rushes to their feet, throwing their hands in the air and screaming like mad as the familiar theme of the one and only 'Avatar of Extreme' fills the arena. The camera focuses on the curtained entranceway as the music goes on, but no one comes out... Suddenly, drawn by the screams of excitement, the camera whips closer to the ringside area where the scarred and bruised form of the Avatar of Extreme has made his way over the guardrail, steel chair in tow. Facing each corner of the guarded area, he throws his arms out to the sides, absorbing the cheers from his fans. Finishing with the last side, Bishop throws the chair into the middle of the ring and sets it up, before taking a microphone from a ringside attendent. The arena falls into a silence] Bishop: Redeemer, Reformer... Two of the greatest men I have known. Brothers, who stood by me side by side. I did not speak after last weeks spectacle, I could not. The actions made me sick to my heart. My words choked, head heavy with guilt. Why hadn't I helped, why hadn't I been there for a man who has saved my life more than once. Why, why , why... [Bishop sits in the chair, his heavily tatooed arms resting on his legs.] Bishop: I have come to a decision... The Fraternity, is no more! But now, Serge... Don't smile, don't be happy for what you have done. Say all you want about making me alone. Yes, you have done that. But Serge, poor, poor serge. [Bishop actually smiles, scar on his cheek pulling his lips into a sneer.] Bishop: To kill a psycho... Takes a psycho... [Bishop stands in one fluid motion eyes ablaze.] Bishop: The Epitome... I have sat and watched what you have done to this federation for almost a year. I sat and watched what you did to men in the World Cup of E-wrestling. I watched all this. I watched your hate and your anger boil up. I saw your envy of me drive you over the edge time and time again. You could never handle what happened could you Serge? That you lost. It was to be your big moment, the moment of your life that you made up for everything. And you failed. It is time to face facts Serge, you... couldn't... beat... ME! [The crowd errupts as Bishop takes a few minutes to think, the crowd chanting Bishop's name in the humid arena air.] Bishop: And now, all your insanity. All your anger... Against me... [Bishop thinks for a few minutes, eyes closed.] Bishop: You wish to be my father Serge? [Bishop laughs.] Bishop: No, for this time, the final time we meet. The moment of truth when you look into my face and peer into the depths of my soul and see an insanity, see a pain that matches yours then Serge... Then you will realize what I have always been talking about. At one time Serge, I WAS you. But I pulled myself from the Styugiuan darkness and I looked out into the light and I smiled... And since that day these people, these people that stand by me night after night. Through surgery after surgery, they know what I talk of. You and I Serge... Lifes eternal riddle. Eternity's favorite joke... Two men, so similiar, and destined to destroy each other... This time Serge... I don't pull any punches, by everything that I am... by every bone in my body... I will beat upon you until the life leaves your bones and the light from your eyes dim... You and I are now truly one and the same... And tonight, when I step in this ring with my opponent. He will be taught, of what I speak... Hells fire Serge, remember the flames... They will be keeping you warm soon enough! [Bishop throws down the mic and slides under the bottom rope as his music kicks in again, the crowd cheering his name as he makes his way to the back.] AW: Ding dong, the witch is dead, the Frat's no more and Bishop sees red. Haven't we heard all that before? I'm sure Serge was watching, and those two will meet in what should be a brutal contest at Fright Night 2.
