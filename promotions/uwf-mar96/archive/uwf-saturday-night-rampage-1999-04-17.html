Saturday Night Rampage

17 April 1999

[The scene fades in an outside shot of a fairly large summer home. We can see, on a mailbox to the side, the words "J. Valachi" in large letters. Obviously, the J stands for Joey. The moon shines down the fairly large house, giving it a soothing evening glow. There is a large pair of golden gates that guard the inner grounds. From what the scene is showing, the entire grounds are surrounded by a tall, brick wall. There is a lone camera on top of the brick wall. The incredibly scrawny figure in the dark is wearing a black sweat pants and sweater, along with a black ski mask... and white sneakers. The decisively larger and bulkier figure, seems to be wearing just a jean vest, black jeans, and incredibly large army boots. The husky figure bends down and cups both hands in front of him. The scrawny figure takes a step into his cohorts hands and is boosted up. The scrawny one scurries up and over the gate, finally making a thump on the ground on the other side. The scene fades out as the husky figure on the other side, with his back to the camera, lights up a cigarette. We can see the scrawny figure scooting across Valachi's front lawn... The scene fades back in as we see the scrawny figure rappelling against the wall of Valachi's house. From the angle the cameraman is on, it brings back memories of the old live action Batman TV show. The figure finally makes it to the open window. The camera zooms up for a moment, so we can see the grappling hook nicely fitted on the edge of a small ledge above. When the camera pans back down, the window has been opened, and the climbing rope is swinging in the wind. The scene fades out yet again... ...and fades right back in again. Now, the scrawny figure is somewhere in the house, and standing at a pair of double doors. The glass windows that make up most of the doors are fogged up severely. The figure wipes some of the window fog away with his fist, and peers in. He then looks to the camera, holds his index finger to his lips and, "Shhhh!" as he slowly creaks open both doors at the same time, in a dramatic fashion. The figure feels a rush of hot air as soon as he opens the doors. He stops, and with a "Whew!" he waves his hand in front of his masked face.] Voice: No way in heck I'm going in there with THIS on! There is so much plant life, that you cant even see 10 feet ahead. The camera pans up for a moment to take in the fine construction work on the greenhouse. Unfortunately, when he pans back down, the cameraman has lost El Gee, again. We spend the next minute or so, as the cameraman fights his way through all the plant life and comes across... a fairly decent sized swimming pool! And laying in the imported mud at the side of the obviously well heated pool is... SNAPPY! Deciding to keep his distance, the cameraman manages to see Lenny... who is now taking slow steps towards SNAPPY. Go on, feeeeeeel its energy just soaking into your scaly self. Be the Gatorade! Become _ONE_ with the Gatorade! [Once again, his attempts to entice SNAPPY go unimpressed. The scaly one... SNAPPY, that is... moves it's front legs a bit, sifting the mud a little bit between its toes.] LG: All right... we'll do this _YOUR_ way... HA! [Lenny dives at SNAPPY, grabbing it around the snout to keep its jaws tight. SNAPPY finally comes to life as it rolls around in the mud, frantically trying to get the human gnat off of it. Lenny looks over at SNAPPY...] LG: Call it even? [SNAPPY gives the only response it can... nothing] Yeah... [puff puff] me too. [Lenny reaches into his belt pouch and pulls out a neatly folded white cloth. The camera picks up a small, sly smile on Lenny... as he shouts a battle cry... and pounces, one last time, on SNAPPY!
