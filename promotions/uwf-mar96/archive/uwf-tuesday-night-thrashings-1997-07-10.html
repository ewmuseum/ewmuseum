UWF Tuesday Night Thrashings, 10 July 1997

10 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Part 1] [Voiceover by Ashley Wilder] Joe Reed has always considered himself to be among the top wrestlers in the game... [Clips show Joe Reed competing in the WCeW last year.] AW: Many critics agreed, and few were surprised when he became the AEWA's World Heavyweight Champion earlier this year... [Shows Reed with the cover to win the belt, courtesy of the AEWA.] AW: What shocked people was his about turn of face in the UWF, and the swelled head that would follow... The old Joe Reed, kind to everyone, was gone, at least in the Universal Wrestling Federation... Although in his own mind within his rights, he degraded the UWF at every turn, labelling it a simple regional promotion to the AEWA... [Shows Reed insulting the UWF, and a face to face verbal battle with Brett Greene.] AW: He lost the AEWA title yet still spoke out on behalf of it, or so he'd like you to think... [Shows Reed's leg being held down outside the ring as Heavy Metal defeats him for the title.] AW: And a week ago tonight he shocked the world once more by claiming a prize he didn't really want but felt he had to win... The UWF World title now belongs to 'Crimson' Joe Reed, but now the league has no idea what's going to be in store with him as champion... [Cut to the final seconds of the match with Black Diamond, and then a freeze frame of Reed holding up the UWF World Title. Fade to the opening of Tuesday Night Thrashings, 'Slang' by Def Leppard plays while the logo of the UWF bursts onto the screen. The logo splits into numerous sections, and on each piece we see clips of UWF wrestlers. As pictures of Serge Annis, Joe Reed, Sebastian Jericho, Marc Stevens, Daniel Kidd, and Black Diamond freeze at the same time, the screen shatters away to reveal the Aston Villa Football Club in Villa Park, Birmingham, England. The crowd errupts once it realizes the show has started, and fireworks begin to thunder around the stadium, down the ramp and aisle to the ringside area. More English fans scream and jump around trying to get on camera, holding up signs that bear messages like "Come on UWF- Sign the Thrillseeker!", "Ashley can't get any Wilder!", and "AEWA Rocks!"(yes, even over here the league wars are present). Finally the camera fades to the broadcast table where David Rogers and Ashley Wilder sit. Dave's wearing his UWF-ACWF t-shirt and an AOPW baseball cap, but Ashley's doing a better job of modelling the league apparel with the new 'Badboy to the Max' t-shirt knotted at her midriff and a slick pair of 'Iceman' shades.] David Rogers: Hello everybody, welcome to TNT once more. We're coming at you from Birmingham, Englad, in the midst of the UWF European tour. I'm David Rogers, and with me is a person whom you've all had a lot to say about since her debut on the European Explosion Free For All, Ms. Ashley Wilder. Ashley Wilder: Well Dave, what can I say? I bring something to the UWF that nobody else can. Sex appeal for the males out there. As for the ladies, you're on your own all you overweight housewives. Although I'm sure Jobrooq and the Nation of Stagnation probably floats your boats' out there. DR: And once again the executive of the UWF are having fits. AW: Let them, it's about time they exerted themselves physically. They're all so overweight. DR: I'd love to get into that- AW: I'll bet you'd love to get into something. DR: -but with a heavy heart I open the show with the following. [The camera shows a closeup of Dave's face, with a very depressed look on it.] Ladies and gentlemen, [sighs in resignation] Here is the UWF World Champion... 'Crimson' Joe Reed. ['Bad Company' starts up, and Reed and his manager enter to a huge chorus of boos. The Lady in Red is wearing the UWF Title over her shoulder, and both are explaining to the english fans that a REAL wrestler now holds the belt. The fans are unimpressed, to put it mildly. Both enter the ring to join Dave Rogers, who climbed into it himself when the other two came out.] LiR: Geez, Rogers, is that the best introduction you can do? No wonder you won't make it to the big leagues. [Dave rolls his eyes in disgust, but being a good soldier, plows ahead.] DR: Well, you beat Black Diamond for the belt, but not without controversy... CJR: Hold up, Davey. Diamond won the belt with controversy, so why should he complain? He who lives by the sword yadda yadda yadda... DR: But... you were the one who cheated for him to win the belt? CJR: You see? All the more reason I should have the title. I made Black Diamond the champ, and I took it away. DR: [Giving up, and changing the subject] Well, several wrestlers have stepped forward to challenge you. Alex Kidd, Scott Daniels... CJR: Hold up again, Dave. You see, THIS is what's wrong with the UWF. Actually, it's just one of many things, but it's a major one. Everything is Legion and Pride, Pride and Legion. Meanwhile, the few wrestlers who are actually worth a damn around here end up sitting around wasting away their talents. I'm the head honcho around here, and I'm going to change this. [The Lady in Red pulls out some papers from a folder, and grabs the microphone.] AW: I like her style. You go girl! LiR: Right here are three contracts for title matches against Joe. These contracts are going out to the three best wrestlers in the UWF: Blaze, 'Iceman' Greg Smith, and 'Badboy' Randy Acorn. Guys, the top brass can't screw around with you any more. We all stuck together to beat the bastards who run the UWF, and here's your well-earned rewards. DR: Hold on, what about the other contenders? Brett Greene, Bishop, a rematch with Diamond.... LiR: Here you go again. Legion, Pride, again and again, no matter how badly Joe beats them. And as for Bishop... someone really needs to let him know that he's been beaten. CJR: Hell, I'll even give Bishop that title shot just to beat him again. Lord knows that with me facing Blaze, Smith, and Acorn I'll need an easy win. BUT... let's get one thing straight. I like all three of you, and we all worked great together to beat the others at European Explosion. But when I face each of you for the belt, I'm going to do whatever it takes to keep the title. Because while it is only the UWF Title, I intend to try and keep it for a while. [Reed and the Lady in Red leave to a huge chorus of boos as 'Bad Company' plays again. As the LiR and a particularly vocal female Alex Kidd fan exchange some heated words, Dave Rogers returns to his seat at ringside. Reed and the Lady in Red return to the locker room without further incident.] AW: Those two are really something, aren't they Dave? DR: [Thinking slowly, trying to remain impartial] How so? AW: They know exactly what the score is, so they aren't afraid to tell the truth. Although there's nothing wrong with overexposure of the Pride. Talk about a boys club that I'd like to infiltrate. DR: [Shudders] Please, can we just change the subject altogether? AW: Fine, let's talk about Blaze. DR: Okay. AW: There's an appetizer I'd like to nibble on. DR: Let's go to the ring, right now! [Tom Davis enters the squared circle with a wireless microphone, and tries his hardest not to look at Ashley this time, remembering his incident last week. Finally he can't keep away and glances at her just at the moment she licks her lips devilishly at him. Without a cord to trip over, he keeps walking... right into the corner and slams into the turnbuckles. Ashley giggles with delight at making a fool of him again, while Tom picks himself up and presses forward redfaced.] TD: Ladies and gentlemen, the first contest this evening is set for one fall. First, making his way to the ring and from Atlanta, Georgia, weighing in at 263 pounds... RICKY JOHNSON! [As 'The Distance' by Cake starts to rumble out of the speakers, Ricky Johnson emerges from the shadows and walks down the ramp to a good sized pop. He high fives a group of fans wearing Kelly Maloney t-shirts, then bursts into a run and slides into the ring.] AW: Atlanta eh? He should go back to Georgia and get a job as the newest Guerrero or maybe the third member of High Voltage. TD: And his opponent... ['Come Out And Play' is heard] The man with the mask, former Cruiserweight Champion... BLAZE! [Blaze runs into the stadium to a mammoth heel pop, and to heighten the dislike from the crowd he shows off with a backflip in the middle of his run. Ricky seemed out cold and Blaze tried to pin him then and there, but at the referee's count of two Ricky Johnson came to life and bucked Blaze off him and through the ropes to the floor. Blaze got up and stood in the way of a baseball slide under the bottom rope, sending him into the guardrail. Ricky exited the ring briefly to return the masked superstar inside, and picked Blaze up with a front slam, taking him down with a superkick next. AW: Wait, where was the foot stomping first? Or the slapping of his thigh to signal for it? DR: Stop it, nobody could be that obvious. Ricky covered Blaze but failed hook his legs and got a count of two before the cradle was reversed and Johnson found himself with his shoulders down. A kickout stopped it altogether, and Johnson worked on Blaze with a European uppercut and a spinning elbowdrop. For this the crowd got into it, chanting Ricky's name and leading him to go again for the pin. Once more Blaze kicked out and Ricky Johnson tried for a belly-to-belly suplex, but Blaze countered it with a facerake and then a belly-to-belly full nelson. The novel move caused Ricky to quickly shout out in pain, but Blaze couldn't hold it too long and whipped him into the ropes to catch Johnson with a kneelift. He next used a hiptoss on Ricky Johnson and then ducked a swinging punch, dropkicked Ricky and then ran at him with a high knee to the face. He grabbed Ricky and tried to deliver a german suplex to the fan favorite, but Ricky landed on his feet. Blaze popped up and executed a monkey flip on the charging Ricky Johnson, again causing him to land on his feet. Blaze got up again and tried to stun him with punches, then forced him into the corner and decimated him with kicks to the thighs and then groin. A handspring elbow to the face of Ricky allowed Blaze to set him up on the top rope and then go for a top rope frankensteiner, but Ricky used his strength to push Blaze off by himself. Blaze flipped and landed on his feet, then ran at Ricky and tried to jump back into position, but Ricky grabbed him first and brought him down hard with the Gutbreaker. Blaze had the wind knocked out of him and laid in pain while Ricky got back to his feet with the help of the ropes, and pointed out at the crowd. They cheered for him as he picked Blaze up and mounted the second rop for the Flying Powerslam, but at the last second before the move was hit Blaze hooked his feet under the top rope. Ricky went down with Blaze now on top of him, and Blaze's feet landed on the middle ropes for added leverage to get the pin: 1, 2, 3. TD: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match as a result of a pinfall... BLAZE! [As his music came on again, Blaze took very little time getting out of the ring with an angry Ricky Johnson on his tail. The crowd cheered Ricky on still after the contest, which made Ashley wonder.] AW: Why are they still cheering that loser? He's not a good wrestler, he's not even good looking. I don't like the guy, why should they? DR: Heaven forbid they should have a difference of opinion with you. AW: It's just not right. And speaking of things that aren't right, here's that pugfaced Sebastian Jericho. ``````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````` [The camera fades in on the dressing room of Sebastian Jericho, specifically the interior. Damien, I will be the man to do the same to you, except I won't be using a sling, and I won't be using rocks, I'll be using my bare hands. Too strong, in fact, for any of your devious evil to break, too strong for her to leave me, and too strong to succumb to any of your trickery. [He pauses, and points at the camera.] Sebastian Jericho: When we meet, I'm going to show you something about courage, skill, and integrity. I'm going to show you that I can overcome all odds, and I'm going to show you that I can beat you within an inch of your life... and then do it some more. Prepare yourself well, Nightfall, because you won't be beat into a bloody pulp by a baseball bat this time... I'll do it with my bare hands, and it'll be infinitely worse. [He snarls a bit, and the screen fades.] ``````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````````` AW: The only thing infinitely worse than pounding his face with a baseball bat is leaving it the way it is. He wastes no time and runs right at the ring and grabs Jericho's foot, tripping him.] ======================================= SEBASTIAN JERICHO versus MARC STEVENS ======================================= Sebastian was infuriated by Stevens' cheap shot and got back to his feet, then buried a fist to the midsection on Marc. He tried to kneelift Stevens in the groin and ended up on the receiving end of a dragon suplex, with both men's shoulders on the mat. The referee counted, but each got up at two, and Stevens managed to execute a faceslam on Sebastian Jericho before whipping him to the ropes and slamming him to the mat. He attempted a crossface chicken wing but was hiptossed and then irish whipped into the corner, where Sebastian Jericho used an avalanche on Marc Stevens and then delivered a short lariat in the corner. Stevens saw the lariat coming and countered it with an armbar submission, holding it on and using the ropes for added leverage. Sebastian fought with all his might to get out of the hold, but it did him no good as the former 2 time Cruiserweight Champion executed a snap powerbomb in the center of the ring. He held on for the pin attempt, only to have Jericho kick out from it with enough power to send him across the ring. Before Sebastian could mount a comeback Stevens shot across the ring at him with a back heel kick and followed with a faceslam and a fistdrop. Then he rolled Marc Stevens through the ropes and went after him, but Jericho played the cat and mouse game and got back in first. When Stevens again followed, Jericho was waiting with a forearm to the back of Stevens' skull. He hammered him further with lefts and rights, then atomic dropped him and blasted him with a kick to the stomach. A DDT from Sebastian might have put Stevens away, but Marc rolled out to the floor and took a timeout. Jericho waited patiently for Stevens to return, and when he did he tried a clothesline but had it ducked and then was rocked with a Thesz press to the mat. Stevens nailed Jericho with a flurry of shots to the face, then tried to choke him out. The referee broke the hold after a four count, and Stevens then tried to pin Sebastian, who kicked out and hit Marc with an uppercut to the jaw to slow Stevens, then got to his feet and kicked him in the head. Jericho climbed to the top rope and came down with a flying axehandle, then grabbed Stevens' legs and hooked in a pin that was kicked out of just in the nick of time. Jericho grabbed him and clamped a sleeperhold around Marc's neck, but he worked his way to his feet and then brought Jericho down with a jawbreaker. Having escaped the hold, Marc bounced off the ropes and came straight at him with a headbutt. It caught Sebastian in the head and knocked him senseless, then the former champion exited the ring and grabbed a chair. He slid it under the ropes and positioned it for something, then returned to the match and worked Jericho over with a reverse chinlock, then a front facelock on the mat. He drained Sebastian of his energy and then rolled him up, but Sebastian again kicked out. Stevens stood up and went to set his opponent up for a double arm DDT when Jericho from out of nowhere grabbed Stevens and applied the Kalamazoo Clutch. With nowhere to go in the middle of the ring, Stevens lasted a full two minutes before finally passing out from the pain and the referee had no choice but to stop the match. TD: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match as a result of the referee's decision... SEBASTIAN JERICHO!
