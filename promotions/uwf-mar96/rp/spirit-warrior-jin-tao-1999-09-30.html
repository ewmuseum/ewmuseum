"Spirit Warrior" Jin Tao — Up for Criticism

30 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
------- [Scene: A lazy Tuesday evening, in a distant and tranquil patch of land somewhere in the area of Portland, Oregon. The skies are an ebony black, with white stars twinkling within the weave of clouds. The whitish moon in the sky shines, shadowed behind one of the many clouds in the midnight atmosphere.
Among the patch of green grass and few trees on the open-land, sits a small and vague figure. The figure, who's surrounded by a deep kindled fire, sits there in a very subsided manner. He is attired with a black cotton robe, and a pair of matching pants. An obscure facial mask covers the mans face, as well.]
[He sits there, with his legs crossed in a butterfly like manner, peering up into the night skies.]
M: So beautiful. Yet, so mysterious. The beautiful moon light, withered behind the woven clouds. I sit here, in 'awe,' as I think of the similarities that myself, the "Spirit Warrior" Jin Tao, and the bright moon that is hidden behind the clouds, have in common.
[The man, now seen as the UWF newcomer, Jin Tao, continues to glare his piercing eyes into the night sky.]
For many months now, have I been shadowed by my mistakes. By my misinterpretation to this sport we entitle, "wrestling." My formal arts to which I have relied on for these months, have failed me, and have proved me nothing but defense worthy and foolish.
The time now that I have spent, readapting my formal and honorable arts to were they can be respected. To where I can be successful, and prove my master the worthiness that is, the "Spirit Warrior." The time is no longer within the future, the time is now, within the present day. The place has been determined, and a home which I feel comfortable with. Where I shall re-console the difficulties I have faced. Yes, the Universal Wrestling Federation. My preferable selection to which I have noticed, respects my beliefs and what I have to prove.
[Just as those last few words are spoken, Tao calmly turns his point of view from the atmospheric evening to the kindled fire which he now sits in front of.
He stares down into the fire sharply, looking as to gather thoughts, as suddenly he raises his head quietly.]
I have come upon the UWF for many noticeable reasons, obviously the most significant, is respect. One virtue I have lived and stood by, as I shall always until death. Though, in the present moment of time, respect is not something any woman `nor man can obtain easily.
Many battles, many struggles, and many hardships may pass us by in one lifetime. I myself have delt with many in the past. The UWF by itself is much to take on for one man alone. Much contribution and time must be set away, and
I myself, am not even sure that I can sustain the disastrous era which is occurring. `'Tis why I have bided to take place in the most recent of era's.
. The "Era of Defiance," A home which I trust in and which I believe in. A home which respects me and what I believe in.
[The flames from the kindled fire, spout in front of the face of Tao's mask, giving his appearance a mystifying effect.]
I now walk into the UWF with a reason, and with a belief. I walk in with trust in my fellow partaker, Gabriel Whitecross. As well as a fire, a fire which burns within me, to push the limit breaks of success. To urge myself to the peak of inevitability. I bring this all with me, to my 'formal induction into the UWF' this Tuesday. I make my induction with a respectful battle, between two spirits who inevitably collide. Myself and my advisory. Donnie Spader.
This man, I know little about. His past I know nothing of, his thoughts have not been looked upon. Though, Donnie Spader. This is the beginning of a
'Spirited' era. A new era all at once, for the UWF. My new home. I shall be ready for anything you may have planned, I shall be prepared and I shall be prompt to battle. Though, do not think for one-second we shall lose respect for one another. `Tis this is what I am bringing into the UWF, a new sense of competition. This Tuesday shall be the beginning.
[The flames continues to kindle as Tao lifts his attention from the fire, and straight into perspective.]
JT: The beginning of an "Era of Defiance." [The camera fades from a final shot of Jin Tao in front of the kindling fire, to a freeze framed picture of last weeks Saturday Night Rampage. Jin Tao holding up the hand of the new Rampage Champion, Gabriel Whitecross.]
