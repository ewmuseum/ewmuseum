Closing Statements I. About the League <--What is LEGENDS--> LEGENDS is one of the many E-Wrestling organizations. It is stationed in Southborough, Massachusetts and mainly tours New England. It has been created by Carlos Rivera and [name withheld], with plans to open sometime in the early spring of 1998.
<--Who runs LEGENDS--> Fictionaly: Carlos Rivera -- Carlos is a very successful lawyer who has been the lawyer to many wrestling stars such as Jeff "Madfox" Matthews, "Real Deal" Shawn
Harrison, Enigma and many more. With his fabulous fortune, Rivera decided to open LEGENDS. He and his second cousin, Jack Firestone, provide LEGENDS with its exposure. Rivera has placed all duties of running LEGENDS on his president. He just provides that capital needed to run it.
Kenneth Raymond -- Kenneth is 28 years old. He was in heavy training to become a wrestler, but a serious injury in a motorcycle accident prevented his debut on the proffessional scene. He was said to have promise, and now it is unknown if he will ever get to wrestle.
Kenneth is the President of LEGENDS, appointed by Mr. Rivera, he enforces the rules and oversees the happenings of LEGENDS. He has the power to fine and suspend any wrestler who breaks the rules. Standing 6' 5", weighing
287 pounds, not many dare question his decisions, and usually pay their fines.
Troup Mitchell -- The number one play by play man of LEGENDS. He's on just about every live show and is always doing some sort of work behind the scenes. He's close friends with Rivera and comes to us on loan from the
MWF. Leroy Jones -- The sassy, color commentator of LEGENDS who never seems to want to shut-up when it comes to insulting just about everybody in
LEGENDS. He's been in the MLWO,SOW,UEW and MWF. This man knows the wrestling business inside-out.
<--The Titles--> LEGENDS has two titles because as of now, unless I get interest in tag teams it will be strictly a league for singles wrestlers. Here are the titles.
LEGENDS Heavyweight Title: The holder of this title is the big dog of the organization. The one that everybody is buying for. It must be defended at least twice a month. Only defended against those in the top 10 in the rankings unless the champion challenges someone outside of the top ten. This is _the_ belt in all of wrestling. The cream of the crop are the ones who win this one.
LEGENDS Title: This is your basic secondary title, the holder of this is usually the number one contender for the
Heavyweight Title. The title is usually contended to by those outside of the top ten. It is defended on every edition of
LEGENDS. II. Rules and Regulations <--How are matches decided?-> Matches are decided on the basis of roleplaying, angles, and strats. RPing is the majority fo what decides the matches, plus, I always incorporate angle developement into deciding matches. If I am having a tough time deciding on the winner I usually look at the strats to pick the winner.
<--What are the Roleplay rules--> LEGENDS is a closed flashing fed, so all RPs are sent to me and I send them out daily in packets. You are allowed _1_ per week as a maximum so that the matches will be decided on quality not quantity of RPs. Plus you can do one for the card that will not count for the match but will be, cool. :) These on-card roleplays are used to break ties.
III. Events and Shows <--What Shows are there--> Tuesday -- LEGENDS -- This is LEGEND's number one show as we come to you live each and every week with Troup Mitchell and Leroy Jones calling the action. It contains 5-7 live matches each and week and sometimes even a dark match or two. Plus, it's seen every Tuesday night, live at 8 pm ET. The reason for Tuesday? Sources say that Mr. Rivera has no Tuesday classes and it's no other leagues usually go on Tuesdays. :)
<--Supercards--> Supercards will usually happen every other moth. Thus giving time for heat and also space them out. Each supercard will have some sort of theme, and of course, it will rock.
<--Where are the shows held--> LEGENDS -- The name of the league, is the name of the show. It's held live each week from the LEGENDS Arena which lies on the campus of St. Marks High
School in Southborough, MA, the alma matter of the owner. It's a very comfortable arena that houses around 6,000. The show sometimes goes on tour depending on if the owner feels LEGENDS needs to go on tour.
The supercards are usually held in large venues around the Atlantic coast. IV. Match Info <--What are the rules of an average match--> An average match in LEGENDS ends when a man has either been pinned, made to submit, counted out, or DQed. A wrestler is DQed for in-ring interference, use of foreign objects in the ring, and striking a official.
