Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

LEGENDS opened its first card on 10 March 1998 at its own arena on the St. Mark's School campus in Southborough, Massachusetts, in front of a crowd its broadcast puts at 6,000. In its own fiction it was bankrolled by Carlos Rivera, billed as a wealthy lawyer to wrestling stars who put up the capital and left the day-to-day running to his appointed president, Kenneth Raymond. Commentator Troup Mitchell opened the show promising “tonight we start a legacy”; his broadcast partner Leroy Jones answered the crowd with “WASUP SOUTHBOROUGH!”

That first card crowned both of the promotion's own titles in one night: an eight-man single-elimination tournament for the LEGENDS Heavyweight Championship, run alongside a separate three-way match for a new Worcester County Championship. The three-way ended ambiguously — a near-simultaneous double-down after a double cross-body left the ring announcer to credit Carlos Manticore as champion over Setzer van Strife and Magus, and the broadcast shows Manticore handing the belt to Magus afterward, only for Magus to shake his head and give it back. The tournament final matched “All Business” Zack Sharp against Terry “Shotgun” Storm; Sharp won it with a dragon suplex bridge at 32:45 to become the promotion's first Heavyweight Champion, receiving the belt from Raymond himself as the crowd, per the broadcast, “lets out a humongous pop.”

Its own handbook, reposted 26 March 1998, describes a closed, flashing federation touring New England out of that same Southborough arena on Tuesday nights, with supercards planned every other month: matches were “decided on the basis of roleplaying, angles, and strats,” each writer limited to one roleplay a week “so that the matches will be decided on quality not quantity,” with an optional on-card roleplay held in reserve to break ties. The same handbook still carried an open application for new wrestlers, asking for a name, a look, a moveset and a written profile.

No closure notice survives. The 26 March 1998 handbook repost, still recruiting, is the promotion's own last dated document in the surviving record; whether LEGENDS ran a second card isn't known from what remains.
