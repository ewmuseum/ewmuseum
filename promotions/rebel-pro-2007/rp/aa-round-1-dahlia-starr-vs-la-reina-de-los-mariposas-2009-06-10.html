Casanova — 19 June 2009 Fade in on a cloud of butterflies against a blue sky, Angle Shades, Common Swifts, Ghost Moths and Monarchs among others. As we watch them settle toward a field of brilliant flowers, we notice a figure standing in front of us. It is La Reina de los Mariposas, The Queen of the Butterflies.
Mariposas: Beautiful, are they not? Prized for their stunning, multicolored blossoms and grown for centuries not only for decorative but also ceremonial and cullinary purposes by the Aztecs, the Dahlia is the national flower of Mexico. But then ... you probably already knew that, didn't you Miss Starr? After all, you took their name as your own and you are at least marginally Luche Libre trained.
Mari smiles and then sighs. Mariposas: Too bad they also seem to have been given such a Black reputation by the media. There is even a segment of our society that would take the dark events of the Black Dahlia murder and glorify it. Can you imagine? It's a real shame.but I suppose if that's the sort of thing that puts the Glasgow Smile back into someone's heart then who am I to judge.
We expand our view as La Reina shrugs and starts walking along a footpath. Mariposas: I don't know how closely you have been paying attention to things around here but I find it rather ... odd that in this whole tournament, among the 20 participants, we seem to be the only two females and yet, somehow we have been scheduled to fight one another in round one. Now, while a 1 in 10 shot of such an occurance is not wildly horrible odds I do not ... cannot ... see this as simple coincidence. I don't believe in coincidence.
Mariposas continues walking, occasionally turning her head to look at the camera. As she passes into another area we note a terraced hillside dotted with topiary sculpture.
Mariposas: The first and most likely scenario is that some desk jockey with low self esteem decided it would be fun to get their jollies watching some T&A and HLA. If such is the case I am afraid he ... or she ... is in for a major disapointment. I'm not in this tournament to indulge the public's primal slap and tickle fantasies. I'm here to kick ass regardless of that particular ass's chromosonal makeup.
She passes a couple walking the other way and smiles. The young man's eyes follow her, as does the woman's. Then the woman notices her boyfriend and slaps him on the back of the head.
Mariposas: Another possability is that some Devious SoB, knowing our respective backgrounds, influenced the card to further a long standing, if underrated rivalry. You held the PWA Tag Team title as a member of the stable Self Inflicted Drama. My "Little Brother" El Rey de Corazones, held that same title as a member of the stable, Sex & Violence. SID and S&V have had issues with each other for near half a decade.
As we approach a gazebo, La Reina de los Mariposas turns and shrugs. Mariposas: Not that it truely maters WHY we were booked the way we were. Just that we were. It matters because with the two of us on the card the Rebel-Pro fans are going to be getting one hell of a match for their money. It matters because as two highly professional and skilled fighters no matter which of us wins, round two is guaranteed to be a success as well.
Sitting down on one of the gazebo benches, Mari leans back and crosses her legs. Mariposas: It's a real shame though that only one of us will be moving on to round two. Despite the equal opportunities supposedly allowed us in these changing times We both know that how hard it can be to overcome the odds against us as female atheletes in a "Man's Industry". If the fools on the booking committee had arranged things right then they would have made sure that their ratings were at an all time high for the entire duration of this shindig. But instead they allowed their stereotypical ideas of a Diva match interfere with their common sense. And their underestimation of our skills will be their undoing. Either of us alone could beat any of the men in this tournament and we both know it. But unfortunate as it is, only one of us will be advancing to round two.
A butterfly flutters into the scene and lands on La Reina's shoulder. Mariposas: I guess then, that it is all going to be on my shoulders when I beat you. I respect you and your abilities Dahlia, realy I do. I respect you as much as any member of Sex and Violence respects any member of Self Inflicted Drama. And because of that respect I will not be pulling any punches just because you are a fellow member of the girl's club. And when in the first round I am forced to knock out the second best Aerial Artist in this dance the bookers will realize the mistake they made.
We focus in on a close-up as she gives us a sad smile. Mariposas: Too late for you of course, and too late for the fans ... but such is life, eh? And the picture fades to black. Alexagogo — 20 June 2009 The Black Dahlia Murder [http://www.aowfmedia.com/selfinflicteddrama/Dahlia/interviews/AA.htm]
Sometimes you just want to be grand. "So I guess as the outsider here, I should really introduce myself." In confidence I grace the stage, an easy smile playing quiet on my lips. My time in the ring, however little, has been good to me.
It shows in how certain I hold the screen, and lift your ears with chill, perfect notes. "I'm Dahlia Starr. Affiliated with neither Rebel nor the NAPW. A former PWA Champion who left with her belt. Self~Inflicted Drama star. 2009 Aerial Assault winner. The Black Dahlia herself.
"The pleasures all yours." I extend a hand flat toward the camera. It all sounds quite hackney but then I know my audience.
"Beyond that, I'm a realist. Well aware that our paint by the numbers booking team decided this pairing would be neat; squaring off the only two girls in a tidy package because that's just how it's done when you think you're being clever.
"Still, I'm hardly just a name at this point, and so I wont pretend to get offended. I'll work it out in the ring, and two bells from now you can tell me where I deserve to stand."
A quick wink to round out my last point, a shrug to break in the next. "Besides, I'm young, I'll be content. One face in the crowd is as good as another, and pulling them apart is still so new and fresh that I'll be happy all the same.
"I admit the face sort of lacks this time out, but I suppose it's only 'round one. Take out all the talent now and there'd be nothing left to see, and I'd really like a crowd when I take this thing home.
"I'm an entertainer after all." And a smile once more as I turn my eyes left, casting off the thought. And a pause to let it sink and float by.
It's a slow focus back on the camera then as we finally get down to it. "You then, round one, since you seem content to educate, perhaps you can explain just how someone with so little as to be nothing worthwhile to say can do so with such confidence?
"Not that listening to your little suffragette speech wasn't moving at all. It was somewhat lost on me. Personally, I don't understand the difficulties in overcoming the odds in a male dominated industry because I've always been a winner, and so it's never really been a problem."
Once more to shrug, reeking in whatever. I lift out a hand as if to throw the thought a side and quickly move on.
"And we can pass by any Self~Inflicted Drama, Sex and Violence issues because from what I can tell that's all on you.
"Safe to say, when you're spread out, blinking in the lights, that'll be all on me; never a Seldon in sight."
Another drop just as quick as I tilt my head to one side. Still the pace never settles. "And we could go on from there picking at the inconsistencies. But really, that was it. From there you say you'll win, while reminding me just how great you think I am.
"Little girl, you don't know the half of it." I gently shake my head and bite my lip through a smile. It's a look that cries pity. It comes off rather well.
"There's only one possible end here: one fleeting touch and a splinter down your skull, and in that last moment before it snaps black, you'll know just how capable I am. You'll know just how little you ever could have done.
"Yet more than anything, you'll know just how sad a display this really all was. More than anything, I hope you'll release that, when faced with your eventual champion... just how really (BLEEP) stupid it was to waste my time chatting about just how we got paired.
"I bet that'll sting." I draw breath, soak in the thought before turning to my next verse. Words leave quietly then, slow to burn.
"I only prey you awake to see me pass you by, because I'd hate to be the reason you missed a chance to throw all your hope, dreams and "girl power" behind a real winner for a change.
"Can't imagine you'll be alone, because after our match - hell, after this video - I wont just be advancing, I'll be the one to beat."
I consider going smug but it's not really my look. Quiet confidence overflows. "After all, above everything else, I'm a winner, and I don't need the booking or some bullshit back story to reflect that.
"In the end of it all, there will just be me, one way or another." And I reward myself a foul grin, and leave the picture start to fade. "Congratulations little girl, your heroine has arrived. Here's looking at her." It falls. Casanova — 24 June 2009 We find La Rein de los Mariposas and El Rey de Corazones sitting in front of a TV moniter. There is a DVD case in Mari's hand which reads "Epic Wrestling".
Mariposas: I could only find her in two matches at Epic Wrestling after the Razordolls were stripped of the PWA Tag Titles.
Corazones: "El no es realmente todo el que asombrosamente. Tan cerca como recuerdo, crea que su tiempo en Pioneer Wrestling Association era su saqueo inicial en el mundo de la lucha profesional." (That is not realy all that surprising. As near as I recall, I believe her time in Pionere Wrestling Association was her initial foray into the world of professional wrestling.)
On screen, Lisa Seldon looks on intently as her freshly bandaged protégé stepped gingerly into lock and her and Eden Turner slipped through a few transitions. It all came to a close soon after as Dahlia took Eden down into a Side-Headlock, got backed to the ropes and then sent running a couple steps before her knee blew out and she crashed to the mat.
Corazones: "¡Ouch! Bien, supongo que usted tiene que dar a la muchacha los puntos por lo menos que intentan." (Ouch! Well, I suppose you have to give the girl points for at least trying.)
Mariiposas: "No ... no I don't, because what she did was stupid, not courageous." As Mari's eyes narrow The King of Hearts sighs. Corazones: "No diría eso. Cómo está Srta. Starr que va a descubrir sus límites si a le no se permite ponerlos a la prueba. Al lado de cuáles, yo saben un seguro alguien que tendría e HIZO la misma cosa cuando ella era el primer comenzar en el negocio." (I would not say that. How else is Miss Starr going to discover her limits if she is not allowed to put them to the test. Beside which, I know a certain someone who would have and DID do the same thing when she was first starting in the business.)
Mariposas: "Exactly, And the simple fact that Lisa let her do it shows just how little Dahlia means to her ... sensei."
El Rey tilts his head and stares at his "Little Sister" as the DVD continues unheeded. Corazones: "No hablaba del luchador conocido antes como 'Loco' Lorenzo." (I was not talking about the wrestler formerly known as 'Loco' Lorenzo.)
Mariposas: "So ... what you are saying is you feel I am being hypocritical? Corazones: "En una palabra… sí. Hagamos frente a los hechos aquí. Srta. Starr tiene razón sobre la carencia de cualquier tensión verdadera entre Self Inflicted Drama y Sex & Violencia. Ninguno del Rebel-Pro o los ventiladores de NAPW tiene cualquier conocimiento de la historia de las dos facciones y para los seguidores de PWA no es nada sino una memoria de descoloramiento." (In a word ... yes. Let us face the facts here. Miss Starr is right about the lack of any real tensions between Self Inflicted Drama and Sex & Violence. None of the Rebel-Pro or NAPW Fans have any knowledge of the history of the two factions and for the PWA followers it is nothing but a fading memory.)
El Rey leans forward and turns off the player before he continues. Corazones: "Verazmente, la única cosa que es demostración es cuánto usted todavía está obsesionando sobre Lisa. No necesitamos al Padre alrededor pronunciar eso como malsano. Más no pienso que Dominique aprobaría. Usted necesita cambiar su dirección antes de que usted golpee esa pared de ladrillo." (Truthfully, the only thing it is showing is how much you are still obsessing over Lisa. We do not need Father around to pronounce that as unhealthy. Plus I do not think Dominique would approve. You need to change your direction before you hit that brick wall.)
Mariposas: "Oh realy now? Do tell, Gran Profesor de Táctica. What would you suggest? Since you are obviously, oh so experienced, in dealing with obsession."
We fade out on Mariposas poised sarchastically as El Rey sighs and shakes his head. ~~~ We return, this time on La Reina deo los Mariposas alone. She is wearing her ring gear and it looks as if she has just finished a practice session.
Mariposas: "I have to admit, Dahlia, that you and my brother were both right. At least as far as my focus was concerned. As hard as it is for me to admit, this match has NOTHING at all to do with any old fueds between our respective stables. This is just about you and me. Which of us has the perfect combination of skill, attitude and experience to win."
Mari picks up a towel and whipes her neck and shoulders down for a moment before she sits down on a nearby weight bench.
Mariposas: "You definitly have the attitude down pat and have shown the potential for skills in the squared circle. Problem is ... you are not going to win the 2009 Aerial Assault through Spunk and Potential. You also need some serious experience Maybe if we were playing tag you would have a chance, but as a singles competitor you are sorely outclassed and unprepared."
Taking a pull from a botle of water, she allows some of it to run down her neck, washing off the sheen of surface sweat.
Mariposas: "Sorry if you find my support of your effort to be inconsistant, but I truely believe you have the skills necessary to have beaten anyone in this tournament except me. And I find it a shame that because of our matchup you will be denied the opportunity to proove yourself. Not that I'm all that broken up about it, you are young and your career is still just beginning. You'll have pleanty of time to get it right later."
Grinning, Mari nods her head and laughs. Mariposas: "And when this match is over and you find yourself being passed by I hope you will not consider it a defeat so much as a learning opportunity."
And with that The Queen of the Butterflies stands up and walks out of camera view. We fade to gray.

"That's it then. Everything I've said, everything I've done. Years of training, weeks of tape watching and a life time of waiting for a moment like this. All wasted because I'm not prepared.
"Well damn, I've wasted my life. If only I'd known." A click of the fingers, my eyes turn down and I sigh. A playful smile still sits in place. We roll quickly today.
"Here's an idea kid - Chylde, whatever works for you - how about you stop wasting my time. I'm trying to build a star here and stepping over you as you lay in contorted, weeping convulsing, secreting heap thinking "well damn. totally thought she wasn't ready" isn't going to fly.
"Sorry if that sounds a little graphic, but come on girl, you get one match out of this. Might as well look good on the way out. I know, I'd want something more to show for this match than a cracked skull and a lot of wasted breath."
I lift out my hands to wonder beneath a cocked head and furrowed brow. "Of course I'm wasting my time, I'm sure you'll disagree. Point blank, blindly, with little actual thought or reasoned logic - that seems to be your thing after all.
"However, I'm aware that, when this is over, you're going to have a lot of time to consider - sat as you will be with someone pouring soup passed your wired jaw - just where exactly it all went wrong. And in that I'd like to help you.
"I am here to make a good impression after all." A bright smile. The pace runs steady. "If I had to put it all down to one thing then that really (BLEEP) you over. I'd have to say that; turning up your nose at someone you know so little about was a most grave and unfortunate error.
"I mean really little girl, when you were sitting there with maybe a dozen matches of mine in your hands, how exactly did you fall on the conclusion that I was the one who was underprepared?"
The mind boggles. I blink in an out of focus to the make point. "I mean, did you really think that was it? That I turned up with Lisa to the PWA and walked right into a fat pay check because of who I know? That we were signed to the eWo with the intention of refreshing and running their tag division because of a few nothing wins as PWA Champions?
"Apparently you're not one for doing your research but the companies I work for are, and I was signed based on much more than word of mouth.
"They had shows to sell for one." I lift out a hand, shrug and let it fall. Nonchalant seems to be becoming my thing. "Myself on the other hand, I put in the work, enough to know that I've seen these same awfully named moves and, hell, even that tacky little tattoo of yours somewhere before, and that's given me plenty to go on.
"Essentially, I know exactly what I'm in for when that bell rings. While you probably haven't seen me in a singles match.
"To think I'm the one whose unprepared." I bite my lip and shake my head. A sad and sorrowful stare plays havoc with my grin. "Of course I might be getting a little ahead of myself here, and perhaps what you actually meant to say was that I wasn't yet skilled enough for a competitor like you. Though again, how you would hope to know is beyond me.
"To that end, I pray you're at least more capable in the ring than you are on camera, because this has been trite and lacklustre thus far."
A sigh again. Finally we slow. "For what it's worth, I'm not mad. You've been around God knows how long and seem just so set in your ways that the very idea that someone so fresh could contend with you at all is laughable. And with that in mind I suppose your playing this accordingly.
"Problem is, you don't seem to know a thing about me, because what little respect you've paid my way doesn't even begin to cover the scope of just what I am.
"The result is that, from bell to bell, you're going to be overwhelmed. You're never going to have a chance."
The words trail off gently into the night. The next falls blunt and unwelcome. "However, I'm a considerate kind of girl. and I'm aware that you've been given very little to go on as far as what I can do.
"So I'll make you an offer. When this is all over, once I step so convincingly right through you. I'll be waiting for a rematch, right here in your home. Maybe then, once you witness first hand, you'll be ready to pay me my due.
"Until then, guess I'll content myself with a pass to the second 'round. Oh woe is me." It falls. Casanova — 26 June 2009 A peal of laughter meets our ears. This week you will find just how wrong you are, just how ... well I won't be too graphic here, there are children listening.
