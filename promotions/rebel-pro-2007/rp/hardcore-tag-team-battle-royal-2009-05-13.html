TheFreakShow — 13 May 2009 The Freak is running up the stairs to a cheap motel room holding a envalope. Stopping at the door he begins to search his pockets. "F#@k!" The Freak starts pounding on the door. "Hey Shut Down open the door".
"Where's your key you dumbsh%t?" We hear Shut Down through the door, laughing as he walks to let Freak in.
"Hurry up, I have the 411 on our first match." The door quickly opens and the Freak runs in.
"well let me see midget." Shut Down grabs the envalope from Freak. "I've told you not to call me that you hippopotamic land mass." Freak gives Shut Down a friendly shot to the thigh. "Well open it up. What's it say?"
"It's a battle royal." Shut Down quickly holds it over his head as the Freak tries to grab it.
"A what, let me see that" He jumps against the wall, does a flip over Shut Down and grabs the letter. "A hardcore tag battle royal, huh? well then we just have to figure out what the hell that means."
"How dumb are you? It means it's an all in, every team for themselves, throw'em over the top rope, ass kicking extravaganza!" Shut Downs face lights up as he re-reads the letter. "And the winner gets a title shot."
"Holy Sh@t! A title shot already. Damn buddy what did I tell you. We are going to reign supreme baby! yeah!" Freak is jumping around like a kid on Christmas.
"Hey now, I hate to rain on your little one man pride parade, but are'nt we forgetting something?" Shut Down is now looking serious.
"What's that exactly Mr. Killjoy?" "Oh I don't know, our opponents maybe, you think." "Well it's not like I'm worried about them or anything. Who are they anyway?" Freak now looks somewhat abated but still smiling.
"Um lets see here, we have the Bluecheese Maifia, the Calzones Why Marrigolds, Dazed and Confused, The Retards, and Didja-poo." Shut Downs face remains completely stocic for a moment before he and Freak breakdown laughing. After a minute or two they straighten themselves out, the Freak holding his sides.
"Ok seriously, we need to find out what the elimination rules are." The Freak says after he catches his breath.
"I told you, toss'em over the top rope. F%ck you never listen." He give the Freak a quick shot to the shoulder.
"I know that," the Freak rubs where he was punched. "I mean if I toss out one guy, does his partner go too or what?"
"True that would suck if i got eliminated because your ass gets tossed." Shut Down looks down at The Freak, shaking his head. "Ah well. I'll just have to start tossing'em out early, I mean I doubt there's anyone I can't toss single handedly."
"Hey now, I hear that the "Bluecheese Fatass" is a pretty big guy." "Ok so I might not be able to just toss him, but I'll still send him packing all the same."
"Well lets ditch the crew and start working out our battle plans." He and Shut Down start shooing off the camera crew. As the door shuts we hear The Freak "A title shot after our first match. Damn"
