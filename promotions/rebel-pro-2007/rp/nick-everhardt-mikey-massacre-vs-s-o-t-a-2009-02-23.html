Nick Everhardt/Mikey Massacre vs S.O.T.A

23 February 2009

Bean — 23 February 2009 Tag Team Attraction Nick Everhardt/Mikey Massacre vs S.O.T.A 3 RP limit per team RP Period Begins: NOW! RP Period Ends: Sunday March 1st 10 Eastern/8 PM Mountain Strat Submission Deadline: Sunday March 1st 10 Eastern/8 PM Mountain Signature: First Blind Fed Head! Massacre — 24 February 2009 [FADEIN: Mikey sitting on the stairs outside of his apartment building. It's night. His head is cocked to the side, as if listening to someone. His eyes are staring at the ground and never move.]
MIKEY: Mark Kingston, we're not strangers. When I came back to REBEL after my neck injury, you were the first bump in the road. Remember that? You had your eyes on the Carolina title, were doing whatever you could to get your hands on it, even if it meant attacking the champ, Kurtis, and all possible challengers . You were well on your way to cheating and manipulating your way to the strap. If it weren't for me, you probably would have got your wish. You attacked me, tried poisoning me... But I came along, pinned you and took the title for myself. Then, you know what happened? You had a change of heart. Stopped chasing the title. You know what I think? I think you were scared. You showed your true colors then. You were scared that I had revealed to you THE TRUTH. That you were nothing. A fluke champion. A REBEL stepping stone. WEAK!
MIKEY: So then you return alongside Chris Sparx and together the two of you think that armageddon is coming. You know what, Mark? It doesn't surprise me in the least that a mental midget such as yourself would believe that. The weakness that made you stop chasing the Carolina title as soon as I had my hands on it is the same weakness that allowed you and Spark to believe that armageddon was near.
MIKEY: And it was that same weakness that led you and Sparx to attacking Nick after the Underground. You guys think you're clever? Attacking a man from behind, the man you just learned you'd be facing the next week... then leaving 'THE END IS NEAR' all over the lockerroom? Well, at least you admitted it was you who did it. So, I guess I have to respect that. But why not make it a fair fight? Why not just wait until the Madness? Why not just wait until we're out there in front the fans. My hypothesis? You're scared. Scared of what Nick can and WILL do to you guys. And me? Well, boys, I tapped out the last two weeks. I'm sure you saw it. Is there anyone who DIDN'T see it? It gives me something to prove. Prove what? That I'm still RELEVANT! So, Soldiers, bring your guns, your grenades, bring the whole (BLEEP) army because at March Madness, like you predicted...
...THE END IS NEAR! [FADEOUT.] Signature: ... also starring Mike as Mikey Massacre.
