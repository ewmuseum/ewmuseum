REBEL Pro — Roleplay

Vincent Black(C) vs Sean Robinson

13 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bean — 13 May 2009 Rebel Pro World Title Match Vincent Black(C) vs Sean Robinson 3 RP limit per wrestler RP period begins: May 13, 2009 RP period ends: may 21, 2009 10:00pm Eastern 8:00pm Mountain Strat Deadline: may 21, 2009 10:00pm Eastern 8:00pm Mountain Signature: First Blind Fed Head! Kronske — 18 May 2009 Vincent Black is a firm believer in the philosophy "You have to spend money to make money." So, most of the money he makes, whether it be from the bar, or wrestling, usually goes back into the bar. Since Vincent isn't exactly "in the money", he lives in a little apartment in downtown Durham, next to an elementary school.
Every day, Vincent Black goes for a jog before he starts his afternoon of training. On this particular morning, Vincent is getting ready for his jog, wearing a pair of black basketball shorts and a red t-shirt. As he steps outside of his apartment, he notices tons of children on the playground, probably during their recess period. He smiles, but it isn't a very heartwarming one. Even while all the kids are screaming, running, and playing, Vincent Black is scheming, plotting, and being bitter.
"STOP HAVING FUN!" he yells at the tops of his lungs, to which the entire playground goes silent. Even the teachers who are looking over their students just stare in disbelief as Vincent smiles to himself. He then turns left, and jogs out of the driveway to begin his morning routine, leaving the children in their silence.
As he turns onto the main road, he begins to jog towards the bar. Usually, he jogs the five miles their, has a beer, then jogs back. Today would be no different, if he hadn't noticed the white sedan slowly following him.
He stops and spins quickly, which in itself is weird for a big guy lke that, and the sedan comes to a screeching halt. Vincent's anger begins to show, and only gets worse as he notices that Chet Whettleson is driving the car.
Yet, Vincent sighs and walks towards the car. He notices the cameraman next to Chet, and admits defeat. Chet wants an interview, he'll get one. Even if he is a slimy little bastard about it.
As Chet steps out of the car, Vincent puts his hand up. "Don't ask, let's just get this over with." Chet begins to smile, and Vincent says "And don't smile. You look ridiculous."
Chet's smile leaves instantly, and the cameraman begins to film the interview. "Why are you always in a bad mood lately, anyway? You always used to be so nice."
Vincent stares at Chet in disbelief. "You serious? Man, let's be real here. You were Rex Caliber's little messenger when he was the M.I.C of REBEL. You were the one who told Bubba he was out of a job. You stole my camera for a bullshit promo. You put a negative spin on everything I do! Chet, why should I like you? Just do your damned job, before I break your jaw! I don't care what my contract says I can and can't do."
Chet inches away from Vincent a little bit, then begins his introduction. "Well, hello REBEL-" Vincent steps forward and grabs the microphone. "Cut the shit, Chet. Listen here, Sean Robinson. This just ain't you're month. First, you get the hell beat out of you by Rex Caliber, a man you looked up to to help lead you to greatness as a member of NAPW's CRIMES. But you didn't make the cut, because you weren't worth what you thought you were. Now, you somehow get a world title shot against me, another man who you looked up to to get you into REBEL's limelight. But you weren't worth what you thought you were. And since the Extreme Elite were better than CRIMES ever could have DREAMED to be, it's no big surprise you were cut out and shut down. And the result will be the same: Sean Robinson will be a motionless body in the middle of the ring, wondering where he is and how he got so god damned beat up. Our last match, you won by a fluke- I bounced my head off the steel steps, I think. It's not even worth my time remembering. But this is a new, improved Vincent Black. I am a man on a mission. I have a championship to protect, and a status to uphold."
Finally, Vincent let's Chet get a word in. "What status is that, Vincent" Vincent smiles and replies "I am not going to take away from the accomplishments of Chad Kurtis and Rex Caliber. I may hate them both, but I will not discredit them. They are both amazing REBEL wrestlers, and very deserving of their status as ICONS."
Vincent looks at Chet, and says nonchalantly "I'm just better than them both. I have accomplished a lot in a little amount of time. I've already read the list a few times, so why do it again? Sean Robinson knows what's going on, as does the rest of the REBEL world. The fact of the matter is REBEL wrestlers have something to fear, and it is me. I'm a powerhouse. I'm technically sound, as I showed Cuzin' Zeke. And most importantly, I'm Vincent F*ckin' Black, and I am REBEL's TRUE Icon. Rex doesn't lead this federation, I do. Chad Kurtis isn't the World Champion, he's just holding a belt I set the standard on. 91 days Carolina's Champion? No one else can say that, or can claim better. And Sean Robinson will not come CLOSE to beating me, so help me God."
Chet begins to ask another question, but Vincent just begins to jog away. Chet sighs heavily as the camera fades to black.. on Black.
Signature: Former REBEL Carolinas Title (91 days) Current REBEL World Champion (04/07/09-) Sean Robinson — 19 May 2009 [Fade in on a stereotypical "archives room". Stacks of metal film canisters dominate the scene, and sitting on a small table in the center of the room is an old reel-to-reel projector. The decades-old machine is still operational, and it's currently shining on the one wall that isn't covered in shelves for film. The blank surface is a perfect projection screen. Sean Robinson enters from the left, through a door that's nearly invisible amidst the hundreds of canisters, containing god only knows how many movies. He's holding a few of his own, and sits them down on the meager space left over on the tabletop. The projector has the large footprint expected of such an old piece of equipment, but there's enough empty table left for one stack of film. Sean sits in the chair next to the table, and speaks without looking at the camera. His gaze is fixed on the blank wall, the target of the projector's harsh light.]
"I've had a few nicknames over the course of my career in professional wrestling. When I was just starting out, I wrestled in Seattle's independent circuit. Not much a circuit, really, just a couple of companies who shared talent and sometimes co-hosted big events. I was using my real name back then, before I had come up with a ring name. My dad's coffee shop in Montlake was getting pretty well known, so people started calling me The Caffeinated Kid. Stupid, I know."
[He opens up the topmost canister of the stack, and starts feeding the reel into the projector.]
"Then when I 'hit the big time', I picked up the Ca$h moniker. That one was actually a promoter's idea, he thought it would be good to play off of the fact that my dad was filthy rich, and therefore, so was I. It worked out well enough for me. I got the big gigs I had always wanted. London first, then some podunk regional federation down in Arkansas and Georgia. Then my Canadian tour. Ontario and Alberta."
[The reel is fully spooled up now, and he stands to flick the switch by the door he came in. The lights go down, and a few seconds later, he's back in his chair, where he flips the projector's switch, bathing the wall in front of him with a moving image. It's a highlight reel from early in his career, in a large federation in Toronto. Sean, as Ca$h, fights his way through five other men, winning the company's World Title in an epic six-man battle royale. He stands at the turnbuckle, holding the belt aloft, as the boos rain down on him from all corners of the Air Canada Centre.]
"Top of the world, ma." [The reel cuts quickly to the very next pay per view, in Ottawa's Scotiabank Place, then known as the Corel Centre. Ca$h wrestles a brutal title versus title match against the company's other singles champion, losing with the help of a masked man from outside the ring. His opponent walks out with his world title, while Ca$h is relegated to number two champion, their equivalent of the Carolinas title.]
"Still the champ. No biggie. Not the highlight of my time there, but it's not the bottom. Not yet..."
[Another cut, and this one is the very next week. Same place, different circumstances. Ca$h is defending his newly-won title against an up-and-comer in a ladder match. Both men are at the top of the ladder, within arm's reach of the gold, when Ca$h takes a brutal right hand to the chin, which causes him to lose his grip on the ladder and plummet to the ring. He lands awkwardly on his shoulder, and screams in pain as his opponent climbs down, belt in hand.]
"That's the bottom. Destroyed my shoulder. Spent more time than I'd like to remember in rehab. I thought I was done for good. Of course, this little series of matches got me another nickname. They started calling me Anti-Clutch. Not to my face, of course. I still had one good arm. But I heard. They would laugh about me when they thought I wasn't listening. They'd crack jokes about how I could win when there was something to gain, but when I had what I wanted, I lost all the drive to defend it. One guy actually said that he wouldn't be surprised if I won the lottery, then sat around all day doing nothing, surrounded by money. The general consensus was that I had all the talent I needed to win titles, and none of the determination needed to keep them. I didn't do much to prove them wrong."
[He swaps out the current reel for the next one in the stack. As it spools to the correct place, the previous reel gets dumped into the nearby trashcan. The canister the film came in is discarded into a corner of the room with a crash. The new film starts up, and we see Sean wrestling in a new set of tights, but still green with gold trim. Ca$h hasn't left yet.]
"The next stopping point was Alberta. After I healed up, I went back to the indies. The best place to start again is back at the start, right? Not really."
[Ca$h's highlight reel rolls on, this time in an NAPW ring. The Heritage Championship is on the line this time, though it was known as the Provincial Championship at the time. Jeff James tries, unsuccessfully, to defend his belt, after beating Lloyd Rees, who had held the title for three months prior. Ca$h delivers a Midas Touch from the top rope, and gets the pinfall victory in the center of the ring.]
"Looks like I was back in my old form. Unfortunately, that was true on all accounts." [Skip ahead to one month later, Ca$h tries to defend the title himself against Stone Zellor. Zellor manages to reverse Ca$h's signature suplex variation into a cradle pin, and gets the three count, despite protests from the now former champion.]
"That's about the extent of my Canadian highlight reel. A reinjured shoulder took me out again, and I spent another spell on the disabled list. But old habits die hard, don't they? Soon enough, I was on the road again. North Carolina this time, my current home away from home..."
[Another reel change, and this film goes into the trash as well. The final canister from the stack opens to reveal the smallest of all the reels, but it goes onto the projector in turn, like the others.]
"REBEL Pro. Just last month, I won the Carolinas Championship away from Bird of Prey. Jacob gave me the best fight I've had in almost a year."
[REBEL Pro's Birthday Bash II. Raleigh, North Carolina. A threeway match between Sean Robinson, now free of the Ca$h name, Derek Stephen Taylor, currently free of a REBEL paycheck, and Jacob "Bird of Prey" Venar, soon-to-be free of the Carolinas title. In the main event of the night, Sean and Jacob team up to take Taylor out of the match, and focus on each other, giving the fans their money's worth in one grueling brawl. It ends with Sean reversing Jacob's high-flying finishing move into a vicious DDT, and the two men collapse into a heap, with Sean on top. Jacob can't even get a shoulder up on instincts, and Sean comes out with the Carolinas Championship over his shoulder.]
"I thought I was finally breaking old habits. I managed to defend that title the very next week against Cousin Zeke. I went into Darlington with high hopes. I wanted to make it two defenses, something I had never done before. I walked right into Chad Kurtis, the man of a million nicknames. He whipped my ass all the way back to Boston, and had enough respect for me to give me a pat on the back afterwards. That made one person in that ring who had respect for me at that moment. I hated myself. I lost another title, I blew a shot at turning my career around."
[This film goes into the trash as well, and Sean stands to turn the lights back on as the projector dims.]
"And now I'm going up against Vincent Black. The longest-running Carolinas Champion, the current World Champion, a man who has kicked my ass before. He's gone through the two men I beat to hold the Carolinas title myself, and now it comes down to me or him. And to be completely honest, I'm not at all sure of what's going to happen. I've been known to come up huge in big matches, and I've been known to blow some major chances. But I guess what this is really about is the past, after all. I've got one more shot to turn things around for myself. Something I couldn't do against Chad, something I've never been able to do."
[He reaches into a pocket, and pulls out a book of matches. On the cover is the logo and phone number for Hardcore Drinking. He tears one off, and strikes it on the pack. The flame glows bright despite the overhead lighting. Sean looks at it briefly, then uses it to light the rest of the matches, along with the cover. The whole thing goes up in a flash of smoke, and Sean drops the fireball into the trash can. The film inside shrivels and melts, then burns, turning the trashcan into an inferno. Sean turns, and leaves the room, hitting the lights on the way out. The only light now comes from the trashcan, where his past burns bright enough to light up the room. The camera lingers for a few long seconds, then fades to black.]
Kronske — 19 May 2009 The scene opens in a dark room, where the only light comes from a trash can in which a fire is burning. A scene similar to the ending of Sean Robinson's promo, except something a little different is happening. In the light thrown by the barrel of fire, we see the big man himself, Vincent Black. We can't make out what he is wearing, since the light from the flames is dancing unpredictably, but we see he has serious look on his face. The light also bounces off of the gold faceplate of the REBEL World Heavyweight Championship, which is draped over his shoulder. Vincent begins to speak. His voice carries a tone that is not as sarcastic and generally negative as his last few promos, but is instead focused.
"Sean, today I watched you try to put the past behind you. I watched you try to take all of your past 'accomplishments' and, more importantly, failures, and prove that they are just that, the past. You threw them all in a garbage barrel, much like this one, and set fire to them with a book of matches I paid for. It was quite a scene, to be honest. An emotional one. One where I actually felt the intensity of the heat radiating through my computer as watched the promo on REBEL's Youtube channel. I saw the intensity in your actions. I caught the symbolism of you using the Hardcore Drinking matches to burn away your past, blazing a trail into your future by burning away your past. It was like you were saying 'the future is mine." And I can't help but sympathize, in a way."
Vincent claps his hands, and the lights come on in the room. The room itself is actually not just any room. It is the main hall at the REBEL Underground training facility. The main hall is empty of all the folding chairs, but the ring is still set up in the middle of everything. In the middle of that ring is Vincent Black, wearing a black "Embrace Today" t-shirt and jeans, and the barrel of flaming... whatever it is that's on fire.
"But enough with the Roger Ebert praise talk, Sean. Now, we're going to talk about cold, hard facts. Most of your career has been spent in the midst of failure and relative uselessness. You can win big matches, sure. I witnessed it when you beat the No-name and The Bop in a match to win the Title I revolutionized. I mean, you lost it later to Chad Kurtis because you can't hold on to your hat long enough for it to actually be yours, but that's neither here nor there. Sean, I'm going to be blatantly honest with you, man. You've accomplished a lot throughout what some might consider a roller coaster career. Hell, I'd be lucky to have a wrestling history such as yours. You've been all over the world, and had an opportunity to, well, piss off so many fans. That's what I live for!! To piss off the general wrestling fan by outwrestling their childhood hero. By proving that the man they look up to is nothing compared to the man I am. Sean, you have done that countless times, and just proven how weak you are."
Vincent begins to pace back and forth in front of the garbage can as the flames are slowly dying out.
"I mean, look at you! Just with the experience you had with the Extreme Elite, you were a constant failure. Winning matches was few and far between for you. And then I crushed your hopes and dreams with a New Hope Powerbomb that damn near killed you, and we walked away. And you decided to 'change your ways', and go to the REBEL fans, who are too dumb to remember, but always quick to forgive. Imbeciles, really. But, the past is the past for a reason, Sean. It has already happened. Now, you and I are both looking to the future, and that future is Sean Robinson vs. Vincent Black. And this isn't for retribution, like our last meeting was. It's for the strap to prove you are the best. The REBEL World Championship."
Vincent takes the title off of his shoulder, and holds it high in the air with his left hand. The camera focuses on it for a second, before focusing back on a now smiling Vincent Black.
"Sean, you set your past ablaze with matches from Hardcore Drinking. It's a little ironic, because it's ringing oh so true. You will try your best to beat me, obviously. I will probably even get a good challenge from it. But just like your little demonstration: I will be the flame that burns you to the ground. Your future is going to go up in smoke, much like your past did."
Vincent slides out of the ring, and reaches under it. He pulls out a garbage can, and sets it on the floor. From off screen, we hear a cart being pushed across the floor. The camera pans out to see an unknown man pushing a laundry cart. He stops the laundry cart next to Vincent, and walks away.
"So, let's see here. You've already burned your past away-" Vincent says as he points to the burnng garbage can in the ring. The flames have died down quite a bit, but the intense heat has burned a hole in the side of the can. Vincent doesn't really seem too concerned about the fire as he continues his talking.
" So I'll take the liberty of burning your future. Is the REBEL Championship in your future?" Vincent holds the title towards the camera, before laughing. "No, of course not." he says as he drops the title back on his shoulder.
"But what IS in your future? I see.... blood." Vincent reaches into the laundry cart and produces a bucket. He tips the bucket upside down, pouring a deep red liquid into the can. He tosses the bucket aside.
"I see, pain. But how do I symbolize pain? I see you lying in pain due to a splintered tale."
Vincent produces a small portion of a broken table from the cart, and tosses it into the barrel.
"I see, thumb tacks." Vincent produces a bag of tacks from the laundry cart, and tips the bag upside down, pouring the tacks into the barrel.
"I see a disappointed crowd, for when you come up short. Again." Vincent produces a REEL banner, and unrolls it to show the camera what it is. He then tosses it into the barrel, with some of the banner hanging out the sides.
"And see a broken, bruised, and yet again defeated Sean Robinson." And from the laundry cart, Vincent produces a framed picture of Ca$h, now known as Sean Robinson, as he lies in the middle of the ring, motionless, bleeding, and laying a a pile of broken glass at the hands of the Extreme Elite on the 2/21/09 edition of REBEL Underground. Vincent places that picture in the trash as well.
"Now, your expecting me to light this on fire, but I'm not going to. I'm going to save it, and bring it with me to the underground. I'm gong to set it in front of your broken body after I beat you, and set it on fire then. So that way, you can see that I was right. Your future will burn in front of your eyes, Robinson. And I will pull an Inferno, and be the flamethrower. See you on the 23rd, and then see what's left o you after that."
Vincent picks up the garbage can, and puts it in the laundry cart. He pushes the cart towards the back as the camera focuses on the burning trash can in the ring. Well, it was burning but the flames have gone out mostly. We hear a voiceover from Vincent Black as the scene slowly begins to fade.
"And must like this barrel, you will burn out, Sean. Your past, your future. No one will remember you as anything more than a man with potential, but no follow through."
And... black. Signature: Former REBEL Carolinas Title (91 days) Current REBEL World Champion (04/07/09-)
