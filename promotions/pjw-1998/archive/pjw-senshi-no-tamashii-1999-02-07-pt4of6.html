Senshi no Tamashii

7 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Senshi no Tamashii continues...] AO: Welcome back, fans, and up next is a double debut match between two men who have made quite an impact on the independant circuit here in Japan as well as being outstanding athletes in other sports, Super Bakemono Mask Jr. and Agasha Gennai! Eddie, what can you tell us about them? ER: Super Bakemono Mask Jr. is a second generation athlete from a kickboxing and Tai Chi Chu'an background who has decided to step up the ladder and is moments before his first SJPW match, but Agasha Gennai really is a man to watch. A four hundred pound sumoka? Boy, there are a few bakas here in SJPW I'd pay money to watch him squash! AO: ...such as? ER: No comment. AO: At four hundred plus pounds, maybe Gennai is just the man we need to defeat that traitor Ed Dean. Let's hear the introductions! ___ ___ _______________________________________________ / _ \__ / / | /| / / / / ___/ // /| |/ |/ /Super Bakemono Mask Jr. vs Agasha Gennai /_/ \___/ |__/|__/____________________________________________/ (Camera fades to Nichi Toju, standing in the ring with mike in hand) Nishi: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, from Sapporo, Japan and weighing in at 196 pounds... SU-PER BA-KE-MO-NO MASK JU-NI-OR!! (The crowd applauds as Cibo Matto's "Apple" blares over the PA system. Through the curtains steps a slender yet tall and athletic man in a dark green bodysuit. His face mask is in a similar color, with red blood markings around his mouth and along both legs are various manga images. Bakemono raises his fists to the crowd, then sprints down the aisle and slaps hands with the fans, who welcome him with polite applause. Bakemono then enters the ring, launching himself over the top rope into the squared circle. "Apple" then stops and is being replaced with "Shinjo no Osano Wo") NT: And his opponent hails from Osaka, Japan, and weighs in at 432 pounds... A-GA-SHA GE-NNAI! (The fans go "Oooh" and "Aaah" as the curtains part, and a huge behemoth of a sumo wrestler steps down the aisle. He has considerable body fat, yet his huge limbs are hulking muscle. His head is shaved but a long streak in the middle, and he is wearing only red shorts with the Japanese flag on the right side. Gennai bows to the fans, then slowly stalks towards the ring as Bakemono eyes him carefully) ER: Boohay, he's huge! *DING*DING*DING* (Gennai bows to Bakemono, then takes throws some salt over his shoulder in the traditional sumo pre-match ritual. Bakemono steps back and waits for his opponent to finish, then starts to circle the big man) AO: Any predictions for this match, Eddie? ER: Bakemono's just gonna be too fast. He's a former kickboxing champion around the globe, and I don't think Gennai can handle that speed... Gennai charges... ooh! He's quick for such a huge man, but Bakemono dodges! And Bakemono with a swift kick against Gennai's knee (Crowd goes "Oooh") And another one! And Gennai is staggering back! AO: Bakemono pressing the advantage, and he is ramming his knee into Gennai's big stomach, but I think the big man completely absorbs it! Bakemono grabs Gennai's arm and tries to throw him into the ropes - no! ER: Reversal by Gennai, and Bakemono with a rookie mistake... he is now being thrown into the ropes and comes back... Gennai tries a clothesline but Bakemono ducks it! (Crowd ooooh's and aaaah's) Gennai turns around to slow... Savat Kick by Bakemono! (The Crowd applauds but lets out a shocked "Oooh" as Gennai doesn't go down!) ER: Bakemono now with a hard chop against Gennai's chest, and another one! But I don't think the little man can penetrate Gennai's thick hide... Bakemono goes into the ropes and now with a charge... Gennai catches him with a short rushing clothesline! (Crowd goes "Ooooh" and applauds) AO: I respect Bakemono's skill, but I fear Gennai may just be enough for him! Gennai slowly pulling Bakemono up, and he lifts him up with ease and drops him to the mat... hard bodyslam! (Crowd applauds) And a first cover! ..1.. ..2.. ER: No, kickout! Bakemono'd better get off of the mat though, with his weight and size Gennai can do considerable damage to him... Gennai slowly goes into the ropes... Huge Legdr... Bakemono rolls clear! (The fans give a loud pop as the big Gennai crashlands into the mat!) AO: Bakemono knowing his only chance is his speed, and he is using it to his full advantage! Gennai slowly shaking his head to clear the cobwebs while Bakemono gets to his feet... Hard Stomp against Gennai's ribcage! (crowd: "Ooooh!") and another one! And the big man is hurting! ER: Gennai fighting himself back up despite the barrage of assaults by the fast and agile Bakemono... and it seems as if the big sumoka is completely oblivious to those strikes by Bakemono! Bakemono rolling out of the ring and climbing to the turnbuckle! (The crowd pops in anticipation!) ER: What will she show us... he swoops down... Double Axe Handle Blow!! (Crowd goes "Ooooh! Aaaah!" AO: But he can't knock the big man down! Bakemono somewhat baffled, and he relentlessly goes into the ropes again and charges... Gennai catches him... One Armed Chokeslam!! (The crowd gives a big pop, which suddenly turns into boos as the curtains part. Chandrasekhar Yoshida steps through, flanked by his new servant, Susan K'uang-yin) AO: Gennai now with the upper hand, and... oh no! Please! (Yoshida is clad in a long purple robe with black Japanese symbols and a large golden orchid on the back. Around his waist is the Tsuihoonin championship. His face is painted white, with a golden crescent moon on the forehead, and he is wearing sheated katana and wakizashi on his back. Susan next to him is pushing a wooden cart, similar to those used by the Ashigaru farmers in feudal Japan. The cart is covered with a huge black blanket, bearing the Golden Orchid society symbol. She is wearing the same robe Chandy is wearing, but with lesser ornaments. The crowd boos, and Gennai and Bakemono spot the intruders and stare towards the aisle) AO: Dear Buddha, please! I thought we'd have seen the last of him... ER: What the heck is up with that cart? (Yoshida slowly produces a mike from his robe and shakes his head, his face grimacing in anger. Despite the referees' protests, he enters the ring and stares at the two men. The crowd boos relentlessly. Yoshida points at the two men, then raises his mike to his lips) Yoshida: "You... you are disgraces!" (Loud boos! Gennai folds his arms across his chest, and Bakemono puts his fists on his hips) Yoshida: "I have seen enough of this! This is not a match worthy being held under the banner of bushido, this is... this is beyond my words! You two are not bushi, you are merely clowns!" (The fans continue to boo. Susan K'uang-yin, usually close to the fans, seems emotionless) AO: What in the nine hells is up with this guy? Yoshida: "You - " (He points at Gennai) Yoshida: "Does not Bushido tell us that there is no combat without movement? Look at yourself, you fat oni! You are but an immobile disgrace for my eyes!" (Gennai's face turns red in anger, and he immediately rushes towards Yoshida. Yoshida spins around with one swift movement, drawing the wakizashi from his back and nailing the hilt into Gennai's forehead as he charges. Gennai immediately drops to the canvas, motionless. The fans give a shocked groan!) *DING*DING*DING*DING*DING*DING*DING* AO: Dear Buddha! Dear Buddha! He didn't just... (Bakemono tries to rush in and save his fallen opponent, but is too slow for the precise strike of Yoshida, his foot connecting with one swift movement into Bakemono's solar plexus. Bakemono groans slightly, then collapses) AO: This is a carnage! Someone's got to... (Yoshida angrily throws his sword aside, then drops on top of the fallen Gennai and throws his fists into the unconscious big man. His face a mask of anger, he is tearing relentlessly into the big man. He finally seems to calm down and gets back up, stomping once more against the chest of the fallen sumoka before addressing the booing crowd again) Yoshida: "Doesn't bushido, the Way of the Warrior, give us precise instructions? Isn't it said that the staple of diet shall be unhulled rice? Look at yourself! Unhulled rice, not whole cows! Gennai, you... you are not worthy of the air you breathe!" (Loud boos!) ER: This is uncalled for! These two men were looking forward to a match between two great competitors, and... and now this!" Yoshida: "And you. Bakemono Mask, is it? How pathetic!" (The crowd continues to boo loudly as Yoshida goes into the ropes and nails his boot against Bakemono's temple) Yoshida: "Your name is not Super Bakemono Mask. Your name is Hoshi Otaku. You tried to hide behind a mask, but now the whole world will know about the coward knows as Hoshi Otaku who denied his heritage. Look at him! Everyone look at justice fate holds for those who leave the path of Bushido and take the path of cowardice!" (Yoshida drops next to Bakemono and with one swift move pulls the golbin mask off of his face. The crowd pops with a loud shock witnessing this sacrilege. Yoshida pulls the head of Bakemono up, exposing the pain-stricken face of the young Japanese athlete to the camera before unceremoniously dropping him to the canvas. The boos and yells are becoming louder and louder) Yoshida: "Yes, bushido is very strict, but failure is not being tolerated. To guide you back onto the virtuous path, I - the preserver of the true path - have brought you all a little reminder." (Yoshida exits the ring and pulls the black blanket off of the cart Susan pushed to the ring. It seems to be filled with books. Yoshida grabs one) Yoshida: "This is a true piece of literature on how to follow the enlightened way of bushido. The essentials of Sun Tzu's teachings and Zen Buddhism, with a foreword from your Golden Orchid Tsuihoonin champion. I understand SJPW has to take a commercial break now and tries to cut me off as usual since they fear the truth in my words, but while we are off the air, my servant will hand out free copies of this book to each and everyone of you so maybe one day you will find the true path to enlightenment and bushido..." (The boos become even louder as Susan starts to hand the booklets to the fans) ER: Come on, this is sickening... let's take our break! AO: Wait! Here comes Jiraiya! [The camera shows Jiraiya running down the runway to the ring. He slides into the ring, and as Susan yells a warning, Jiraiya nails Yoshida from behind with a running dropkick, sending him into the ropes. He bounces back, and Jiraiya catches him in a waistlock, then hits a release German Suplex...] ER: It looks like Jiraiya's seen enough! I guess our next match is underway! AO: I'll say! Get him Jiraiya! ___ ___ _______________________________________________ / _ \__ / / | /| / / / / ___/ // /| |/ |/ / Chandrasekhar Yoshida vs Jiraiya /_/ \___/ |__/|__/____________________________________________/ [Yoshida sits up, holding the back of his head, and Jiraiya lands a dropkick to his face, sending him back to the mat. He sits Yoshida up, then attempts La Magistral Cradle, but Yoshida kicks out at one. Jiraiya drags Yoshida to his feet, and attempts an irish whip, but Yoshida reverses it, and back bodydrops Jiraiya to the mat. He runs to the ropes, and as Jiraiya sits up, lands a vicious Axe Kick to his shoulder. Jiraiya holds his shoulder in pain, and Yoshida grabs him, and stands him up. he lands a knife edge chop to Jiraiya's throat, and Jiraiya recoils from the blow, clutching his throat. Yoshida lands a standing dropkick to Jiraiya's face, and he hits the mat hard. Yoshida makes a quick cover, but Jiraiya kicks out at one and a half...] ER: Jiraiya had things fairly in hand for a few moments, but lost it in short order. Like him or not, Yoshida is a ring technician! AO: But his attitude is all wrong! He perverts the concept of Bushido! [Yoshida stands Jiraiya up, and lands a spinning heel kick to his gut, Jiraiya staggers back, and Yoshida charges for a clothesline, but Jiraiya ducks under, and rolls, coming to his feet. He continues to the ropes near him, springboards off, and lands a back elbow to Yoshida, who was coming in behind him. Yoshida staggers back, and Jiraiya lands a spinwheel kick that doubles Yoshida over. Jiriaya places his leg over the back of Yoshida's neck, then drives him to the mat with a rocker dropper. Jiraiya gets to his feet, turns, and hits a standing moonsault. He hooks the leg for a cover, but can only get a two count...] ER: Jiraiya pulling out all the stops, but Yoshida is too tough to beat this early in the match. Can he rebound from his recent loss to The Banshee? AO: As much as I want Jiraiya to win this match, I don't think he can. Yoshida has too much experience... [Jiriaya stands Yoshida up, and attempts another spinwheel kick, but Yoshida catches the leg, and hits a dragonscrew legwhip. He stands Jiraiya up, grabs his leg, and hits a second legwhip. Jiraiya lays on the mat, holding his leg in pain. Yoshida goes to the ropes, and comes off, landing a corkscrew elbowdrop onto Jiraiya's leg. He gets up, grabs the leg, and turns Jiraiay over into a single leg crab. Jiraiya cries out in pain, but won't agree to submit. Yoshida pulls back on the leg, then releases the hold when Jiraiya makes it to the ropes. Yoshida backs off, allowing Jiraiya to make it to his feet. The referee checks to see if he can continue, and when Jiraiya shakes his head that he can. the referee signals for the match to continue. Yoshida moves towards Jiraiya, who lashes out suddenly with a kick to the gut. Yoshida doubles over, and Jiraiya leaps over him, landing in a sunset flip. Yoshida tries to fight the move, but ends up being pulled down. The referee drops to count, but Yoshida kicks out at one. Both men get to their feet, and Jiraiya charges, only to catch a superkick on the chin...] ER: Yoshida just keeps countering any offence Jiraiya can mount! He's like a man possessed! AO: He takes his loss to Banshee very personally Eddie! The Grand Prix Senshuken needs to pay close attention to this match! [Yoshida looks down at his opponent with a look of disgust in his eyes. He stomps on Jiraiya's knee, then grabs his leg, and applies a spinning toehold. He applies the hold a second time, and Jiraiya struggles on the mat. Yoshida releases the hold, grabs Jiraiya by his mask, and drags him to his feet. He bends him over, double underhooks his arms, and takes him over into a butterfly suplex. He stands back up, and looks out at the crowd, who are booing loudly. He grabs Jiraiya again, and hits a gutwrench powerbomb. Yoshida once more gets to his feet, and looks down at the stunned Jiraiya with total contempt on his face...] ER: Yoshida is just punishing Jiraiya! Will this never end? AO: I don't know Eddie. Pray he stops this slaughter soon! [Yoshida picks up the dazed Jiraiya, hoists him over his shoulder, then slams him down with incredible force. Without releasing his legs, Yoshida immediately turns him over into The Genyosha Deathlock. Jiraiya, who is still out of it, is unable to cry out in pain. The referee checks on Jiraiya, then calls for the bell...] Nishi: The winner of this match, as a result of a submission... CHANDRASEKHAR YOSHIDA!!! [crowd booos] ER: Thank god it's over! AO: No it's not! Yoshida hasn't broken the hold yet! [The camera shows Yoshida with the deathlock still on Jiraiya. The referee calls for the bell again, but Yoshida still won't break the hold. He starts to threaten Yoshida, when Susan K'uang-yin enters the ring, and gets in the referee's face...] ER: Someone needs to stop Yoshida before he seriously hurts this young man! AO: I agree! I may have to do something myself. [The crowd suddenly cheers as El Gato Diablo comes out from the back, and runs to the ring. As he slides in, Yoshida breaks the hold. Diablo gets into Yoshida's face, who simply smiles at him in return...] ER: El Gato Diablo might have saved Jiraiya's career! Why did he come to his aid? AO: He was friends with Yaz, the original Jiraiya. He helped train this young man! [Diablo & Yoshida are still in each other's faces, then Diablo turns toward Nishi, and speaks to him for a moment. The referee comes over, and after speaking to the two, looks at Yoshida, who nods. The referees, and calls for the bell...] Nishi: Ladies & Gentlemen, Chandrasekhar Yoshida & El Gato Diablo will no face... [Diablo flies at Yoshida, nailing him with an elbow smash to the side of his face. He staggers back into the corner...] ER: It looks like we have an impromptu matchup! AO: Maybe Diablo can avenge Jiraiya... ___ ___ _______________________________________________ / _ \__ / / | /| / / / / ___/ // /| |/ |/ / Chandrasekhar Yoshida vs El Gato Diablo /_/ \___/ |__/|__/____________________________________________/ [Diablo charges Yoshida in the corner, who flies out with a lariat that sends Diablo spinning in the air before he crashes to the mat. Yoshida drags Diablo to his feet, then hits a snap suplex. He holds onto Diablo, drags him back up, and hits a second snap suplex. Still holding on, he drags him up a third time, and hits a third snap suplex!] ER: Yoshida is tearing Diablo apart! How can he do it after wrestling another match a few minutes ago? AO: Yoshida's mind is so focused, he isn't suffering from fatigue. Some say he has a will of iron... [Yoshida drags Diablo to his feet, and whips him to the ropes. He attempts a high knee lift, but Diablo rolls to the side to avoid it. Yoshida turns towards him, and Diablo rolls forward, springing up into a clothesline that sends Yoshida staggering back to the ropes. Yoshida bounces off the ropes, and Diablo catches him, taking him over into a spinning backbreaker! The crowd "ooohs" as Yoshida lays on the mat, holding his back in pain. Diablo runs to the ropes, and executes a beautiful quebrada, and hooks the leg. The referee drops to count, but Yoshida kicks out at one and three quarters. Diablo drags Yoshida to his feet, sets him up, then lifts him up into the air. He holds him for a few moments, then drops backward into a suplex. Yoshida sits up, holding his back in pain. Diablo applies La Magistral Cradle, and the referee counts to two before Yoshida kicks out...] ER: Yoshida definitely taking a pounding now! AO: And he deserves every moment of it! [Yoshida rises to his feet slowly, sucking in wind. He motions for Diablo to come at him, and Diablo charges, attempting a spinwheel kick. Yoshida ducks under it, and comes to his feet, nailing Diablo with a spinwheel kick of his own. He goes to the corner, and goes to the second rope. He stands there, catching his breath, while Diablo starts to get to his feet. When he makes it to his feet, Yoshida leaps, catching Diablo, and driving him down to the mat with a bulldog. He rolls him over, and starts to choke him on the mat, Diablo's legs kicking as he attempts to struggle. The referee puts a count on Yoshida, and he breaks at four. He stands Diablo up, and lands a knife edge chop to his throat. Diablo staggers back in pain, and Yoshida leaps up, and takes Diablo down with a rana takedown. Diablo sits up in pain, and Yoshida slaps on a rear chinlock, twisting Diablo's neck to the side. Diablo tries to struggle, but is unable to get free. Yoshida loosens the hold, then lands a swift elbowsmash to the back of Diablo's neck. He backs off, leaving Diablo to hold the back of his head in pain...] ER: Yoshida is a ruthless opponent. He could have beaten Diablo there, but he's dragging this out. AO: I told you, he's making a point. The Banshee needs to pay close attention... [Yoshida allows Diablo to get to his feet, then catches him with a high kneelift that sends him back to the mat. He drags Diablo up, bends him over, then drives his head to the mat with a piledriver. Diablo bounces hard off the mat, then rolls on the mat holding his neck. Yoshida grabs Diablo, pulling him up, then spiking him to the mat with a second piledriver! Yoshida looks down at his fallen opponet with derision on his face, then sits him up. He draws his thumb across his throat, then applies a Dragon Sleeper. Diablo tries to struggle, but is obviously too weak from the two piledrivers he just suffered. The referee checks with him, and he motions with his hands that he submits...] ER: Diablo tapped out! AO: He had no choice! He took alot of punishment from those two piledrivers! Nishi: The winner of this match, as a result of a submission... CHANDRASEKHAR YOSHIDA!!! [crowd booos] ER: So Yoshida wins two matches in a row by submission. You're right: The Banshee needs to look out! AO: Yoshida is driven by his personal honor. He won't rest until he avenges his loss at the hands of The Banshee. Banshee's title reign could prove to be a short one if he's not careful...
