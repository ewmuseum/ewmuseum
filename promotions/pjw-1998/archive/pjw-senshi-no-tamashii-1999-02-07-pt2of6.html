[Senshi no Tamashii continues...] ER: We continue the Frank A. Marano Tag-Team Tournament next as we go into round number two. AO: Ah, yes the art of tag-team wrestling is very near and dear to me. ER: The team of Heaven and Hell battle The Outlaws... AO: Baka! Why must we cater to these disrespectful gaijin? The Outlaws have done nothing but trample on the traditions of Puroresu. It is disgusting. ER: Well, while that is true, they are a very talented veteran team Akira. In fact they were recently listed as the number 1 tag-team in a wildly respected poll. AO: I know I didn't vote for them. No better than the Yakuza.... ER: Let's get to the ring. ___ ___ _______________________________________________ / _ \__ / / | /| / / Frank A. Marano Jr. Tag Tournament Rd Two:/ / ___/ // /| |/ |/ / The Outlaws vs. Heaven and Hell /_/ \___/ |__/|__/____________________________________________/ [Camera cuts to a shot of Nishi Toju standing in center ring, microphone at the ready.] Nishi: The following contest is a second round match in the Frank A. Marano Tag-Team Tournament![crowd cheers] Introducing first...Hailing from Osaka and Nagasaki respectively and weighing in at a combined 467 pounds...WHITE AN-GEL AND SOUL TA-KER...HEAVEN AND HELL!!! [The theme from "Mortal Kombat: The Movie" plays and the fans give polite if muted applause as White Angel and Soul Taker walk to the ring. White Angel wears a white robe with a white headwrap while his partner contrasts him with a black robe and headwrap. They both enter the ring. White Angel takes off his robe and headwrap to reveal white tights with "Heaven" written down the left leg and "Angel" down the right. Angel also bears a star tattoo on his right shoulder. Soul Taker wears black tights with "Hell" written down the left leg and "Taker" down the right. He has a cross tattooed on his right shoulder.] Nishi: Their opponents...Accompanied to the ring by Tex James...Hailing from Tombstone, Arizona USA and weighing in at a combined 470 pounds...DOC HOL-LI-DAY AND BRENT MAV-ER-ICK...THE OUTLAWS!! ["This is the Life" by "Weird" Al Yankovic plays and the fans give some low applause with some boos mixed in. Tex James walks out first, wearing a grey business suit, white shirt, and black suspenders. He grips them with pride and smiles. After a few moments Holliday and Maverick come out behind him. Both men wear similar black dusters, red cowboy boots, black hats, black leather gunbelts with six-shooters, and red tights designed decorated with the Four Aces and the Twenty-One of Spades. Maverick carries his trademark Haliburton as well, while Holliday carries a mahogony cane to help him to the ring as he noticably limps.] ER: The Outlaws coming in with a lot of confidence and many are picking them to do very well in the tournament. AO: Stop singing their praises Eddie-san, it is sickening. [Referee Owada Botan checks all four men for foreign objects. Mavrick seems to hesitate, and then breaks into a broad smile before patting Botan on the head and allowing him to search him. This draws some boos and some laughs from the fans.] AO: Look, he is already showing disrespect. ER: Easy Akira. [Maverick and Holliday both stay in, eyeing Heaven and Hell. When Soul Taker exits the ring, Maverick nods to Holliday and Holliday exits. as Owada signals for the bell.] ER: We are underway as Maverick faces off against the high-flying member of Heaven and Hell, White Angel. Interesting strategy early on as one would expect The Outlaws to counter with Holliday considering he and White Angel are similar. AO: One does not always want to match same against same Eddie-san. Even these ruffians realize that it seems. Sometimes you can counter speed with power. [Maverick eyes White Angel cautiously as the two men circle each other. White Angel attempts a kick, but Maverick avoids it, and quickly attempts to lock-up. White Angel spins out of his grasp, and dances away. Maverick claps his hands together, and rubs them before moving back after his opponent.] ER: Collar and elbow tie-up, and again Angel escapes, but Maverick is quick to persue him and kick to the midsection and now a knee and a double-axhandle sends White Angel down to the mat. AO: Maverick looks to try and ground the smaller man, but White Angel shows his quickness by rolling away and getting to his feet. [Maverick stays right on White Angel however with quick kneelifts that drive him back to the ropes and an irish whip. Maverick sets as White Angel comes off the ropes, but Angel deftly slides between the legs of Maverick. As Maverick turns White Angel connects with a spinning heel kick that drives Maverick to the mat and draws some light applause.] ER: White Angel up and he snaps down with a quick leg drop. Maverick rolls to his knees, and now to his feet and White Angel connects with backhand chops to the chest. Maverick responds with stiff punches of his own. AO: Illegal closed fists. Typical barbaric tactics from this one. [White Angel loses the toe-to-toe battle, and Maverick pounds him back into a neutral corner with repeated punches and now a wicked forearm shot sends White Angels head flying back. Maverick calls out to the fans to little response as he whips White Angel to the opposite corner. As Angel gets to the corner he leaps up, running up the turnbuckles and then executing a back-flip over the charging figure of Maverick. Maverick stops himself before he hits the turnbuckles and then spins around with a lariat, but White Angel ducks it and connects with a spinning Enziguri. The fans applaud as Maverick goes down. White Angel tags in Soul Taker and he quickly pulls Maverick up and takes him back down with a Vertical Suplex.] ER: Heaven and Hell now showing the first real momentum of the match, and Maverick is looking to tag out, but Soul Taker is on him and it looks like he is going for an Indian Deathlock. AO: Hai. Maverick trying to block the hold, and now edging toward the ropes. I would expect him to go for an eye gouge instead...baka. [Soul Taker locks in the Deathlock, but Maverick manages to make the ropes. A spattering of applause is heard as Soul Taker releases the hold and Maverick gets to his feet. Soul Taker quickly moves in and lands a quick chop across the chest and then sends Maverick into the ropes. Soul Taker catches Maverick on the rebound with an inverted Atomic Drop that stuns him. Soul Taker follows up with a short lariat that floors Maverick.] ER: Soul Taker tags in White Angel and Heaven and Hell suprisingly showing better continuity that The Outlaws. AO: I imagine the Outlaws came in overconfident, and now that is coming back to haunt them, and I for one could not be happier. [Maverick rolls to his feet as White Angel springs to the top rope and connects with a Springboard Missle Dropkick. The fans applaud as White Angel rolls over for a cover, but Maverick kicks out easily at two. White Angel stands and motions out to the fans and recieves a few cheers. He goes back up to the top rope as Maverick attempts to rise, but fails, appearing dazed. White Angel leaps off.] ER: OH! Maverick rolls clear of the attempted Splash from White Angel. Angel quick to roll over for the tag, and then he drops out to the floor, attempting to get his wind back. AO: Soul Taker must prevent the tag...No! [Maverick makes the tag, and Holliday steps in, right into a chop from Soul Taker. Soul Taker irish whips Holliday and catches him on the rebound for a Sideslam, but Holliday counters with a spinning headscissors that garners some reluctant applause. Both men get to their feet, and Soul Taker attempts a lariat, but Holliday ducks under it and runs to the ropes.] ER: Holliday off the ropes...High Knee Smash puts Soul Taker down. Holliday off the ropes...Powerdrive Elbow![Fans applaud] [Holliday jumps up and nods out to the fans as if to say "See, we ain't all bad" and then proceeds to stomp down on his opponent. Soul Taker rolls to his feet and Holliday fires in with a kneelift that doubles Soul Taker over and Holliday follows it up quickly with a Gut Wrench Suplex. Holliday stands, pulls Soul Taker to his feet, and tags in Maverick.] ER: Holliday with an irish whip...Drop Toehold as Soul Taker comes off..OH! Right into a Uppercut from Maverick. Great teamwork by these veterans. AO: It was...adaquet and that is all. [Maverick pulls Soul Taker up. Soul Taker attempts a few weak punches to the midsection, but Maverick quickly cuts him off with a double-axhandle and then tags Holliday back in. Maverick whips Soul Taker to the ropes and catches him as he comes off with a Gorilla Press. Holliday drops to one knee and Maverick drops Soul Taker gut first across the outstretched knee of Holliday. The fans "ohhhh" and applaud the move.] ER: More solid teamwork as the Outlaws have taken control of this one. AO: Referee Botan is being far to lenient with them. He needs to keep one men out at all times. [Holliday pulls Soul Taker to his feet and rams his head into the top turnbuckle in the neutral corner. He then lifts Soul Taker up and sits him on the top. The fans begin to "ohhh" and "ahhh" in anticipation as Holliday walks away from the corner. He quickly turns and runs to the corner, up the ropes and then takes Soul Taker off the top with a Frankensteiner as the fans let out a loud "ohhhhh".] ER: Holliday with a beautiful manuver showcasing his agility and now he has the cover. ...1... ...2... AO: Excellant save by White Angel. [Fans applaud] ER: I think it might have been over there but for that intervention by Angel Akira. AO: Perhaps, but it was a good move by Angel and now Soul Taker must get the advantage back, give him time to tag out at the very least. [Holliday pulls Soul Taker up and quickly puts him back down with a Belly-To-Back Suplex. Holliday then slides out to the apron and waits for Soul Taker to rise, testing the tension in the top rope as he does. Soul Taker gets to one knee, shaking his head, trying to clear the cobwebs, and then staggers up. Holliday leaps up.] ER: Springboard...Frontflip...[fans "ohhhhh" and then applaud] Powerbomb by Soul Taker. AO: Hai! Excellant counter to the bodyblock by Holliday. [Soul Taker folds Holliday up like an accordian, dropping him back of the neck first to the mat as he catches him out of the bodyblock. Holliday stomps the mat in pain as he clutches the back of his head and neck. Maverick has a worried look on his face as he reaches out for the tag. Soul Taker crawls toward his corner as White Angel stands ready for a tag.] ER: Holliday seems to be hurt and Soul Taker is close to his corner. AO: White Angel needs to get in the ring and capitalize on the weakended state of Holliday. [Soul Taker tags out and Holliday suddenly comes to life and rolls to his corner making the tag. Maverick comes in right as White Angel executes a handspring and then connects with a elbow to the chest of Maverick. Holliday drops down to the floor, on one knee. Tex James checks in with him as White Angel snaps Maverick over with a quick DDT.] ER: White Angel has Maverick down and now he climbs to the top...Moonsault...No! Maverick rolled clear. AO: But White Angel landed on his feet! Maverick is up and Angel leaps up...Hurricarana! ER: He reaches back and hooks the legs. ...1... ...2... [fans "ohhhhh"] ER: So close, but Maverick squirms free! AO: He almsot had him. [White Angel checks with Botan to make sure it was a two count and not three. Botan holds up two fingers and White Angel nods and then leaps up and comes crashing down on Maverick as he was attempting to sit up with a Senton Splash. Maverick clutches his arm in pain as White Angel landed awkwardly on it. White Angel rolls to his feet and runs to the ropes as Maverick quickly gets up, grimacing in pain and anger.] ER: White Angel leaps up...OH! It looked like he was going for a Quebrada, but slipped and now Maverick moves in and catches him with a forearm to the back of the head. AO: One misstep can be his undoing. [Maverick hammers away on White Angel against the ropes and then whips him to the farside. Maverick attempts a wild Lariat as White Angel comes back, but White Angel ducks under it and then leaps to the second rope and springs off as Maverick turns and hits a Quebrada. Botan drops to count.] ...1... ...2... ER: Kickout by Maverick![fans "ohhhh" and then applaud] AO: I have to give him credit for his tenacity, I suppose. [White Angel again questions the count and again Botan holds up two fingers. White Angel pulls Maverick up and sends him crashing into his corner. White Angel then runs forward and and attempts a Handspring Elbow, but Maverick catches him in a waistlock. The fans "ohhh" and "ahhh" ready for Release German Suplex from Maverick, but White Angel fends it off with quick back elbows that cause Maverick to release the hold and stumble back into the corner. Holliday scrambles back up to the apron and tags in, unbeknownst to White Angel.] ER: Tag made, but White Angel with his back turned and he goes after Maverick. He leaps up for another Hurricarana, but Maverick holds him up and walks out from the corner as the legal man Holliday climbs to the top. AO: White Angel in a very bad spot. [Holliday leaps off with a Somaursault Neckbreaker on White Angel as Maverick simultaneously connects with a Sit-down Powerbomb prompting a loud "ohhhhh" from the fans and applause as Holliday scrambles over for the cover.] ...1... ...2... ER: Diving save from Soul Taker![fans applaud] AO: Maverick now in and he and Soul Taker trading blows. [Holliday rises and pulls a limp White Angel to his feet. Holliday pauses to rotate his neck, whincing as he does so, and then heads to the top rope.] ER: Holliday looking to end this one, perhaps with his Aces High Reverse 450... AO: Maverick has Soul Taker across his shoulders for the Death Valley Bomb. ER: Or Acey Duecy as he calls it. [The fans cheer as Soul Taker spins free of the Death Valley Bomb and drives Maverick down with a Reverse DDT. Soul Taker quickly gets to his feet and charges the corner catching a surprised Holliday with a punch to the midsection. Soul Taker climbs up to the top rope, looking for a Superplex, but Holliday fights back with punches. Both men trade shots as Maverick gets to his feet.] ER: Maverick from behind and he nails Soul Taker with a double-axhandle to the back and now mounts him on his shoulders and walks away from the corner. White Angel rises and Maverick runs off one side of the ropes as Holliday rushes off the other.] ER: OH! Combination Spear tackle and Enzui-Lariat by The Outlaws and White Angel goes down in a heap. Holliday climbs up to the top rope as Maverick pulls up White Angel.] ER: Maverick lifts White Angel up in a bearhug and now bends him over, they are setting for the Twenty-One of Spades. [The fans "ohhhh" as Holliday comes off with a Guillotine Legdrop across White Angel as Maverick holds him out completing the move. Holliday goes for the cover.] ...1... ...2.... [Soul Taker comes in, but Maverick meets him with a Legsweep Faceslam] ...3... **ding**ding**ding** Nishi: Your winners advancing in the tournament...THE OUTLAWS!!! ["This is the Life" by "Weird" Al Yankovic plays and the fans give the Outlaws their due with applause, although a few boos sneak in as well. Tex James comes in and raises his boys arms in the air. Holliday seems to still be favoring his neck, but he smiles in victory.] ER: The Outlaws showing why they are one of the best in SJPW and in the world with a solid victory over Heaven and Hell. AO: Though I loath their manner, I must admit their talent. I fear they could indeed win this tournament, but I have faith that a more honorable team from SJPW will be able to best them in the later rounds. ER: Time will tell Akira, time will tell. [The camera cuts backstage, showing Takizawa talking to Hori Yatoro. Yatoro shakes his head "no", then walks away. Takizawa turns towards the camera, a look of dissapointment on his face. Tokugawa: Takizawa-sama, I know that we have been adversaries of late. Chiimu Tora & The Daiymo have been at odds since the return of SJPW. I am also insulted that an honorless cur like Yamashita can be named acting CEO. Therefore, I wish to offer myself as one of your partners. [Takizawa looks at Tokugawa warily] Tokugawa: Takizawa-sama, I know you have reason to trust me. Then again, you have no reason to trust Kilcannon, yet he too has offered to be your partner. I am loyal to SJPW, and I only wish to prove it by fighting at your side. [Takizawa looks skeptical, but he nods, and offers his hand to Tokugawa. Tokugawa takes the offered hand, and shakes it. The camera cuts back to Rose & Ono..] ER: Kensei Tokugawa is partner number two! Now if Takizawa can find three more men... AO: I'm proud of Tokugawa. His recent time spent training with me has done wonders for his sense of honor! ER: Our next match continues the second round of the Marano tournament, as Hiryuu Batsu faces The Suicide Machines... AO: Both teams had impressive first round victories. Now they face off to see who goes to round three... ___ ___ _______________________________________________ / _ \__ / / | /| / / Frank A. Introducing first.... at a combined weight of 482 pounds.... KIIINNNG... "Nemesis" STEVEN CONNNNNNERRRR.... THE SUICIDE MACHINES!" ["Sweet Dreams" by the Eurythmics starts up on the PA system as The Suicide Machines head down the aisle. King is a masked dark-skinned black male with a Brian Pillman build. His mask is black with two large white mesh eyeholes (think Spider-Man or Venom). He wears long black tights with white boots and the word "SUICIDE" airbrushed in red on the side. Conner is a caucasian with long curly dirty blonde hair, and a Chris Benoit physique with MASSIVE arms. He wears long black and blue tights with white boots and "SUICIDAL" on the side. His hair appears a bit dirty at times and his face is often unshaven. ] Nishi: "And their opponents... weighing in at a cominbed 409 pounds.... they represent Chiimu Tora.... THE BANSHEE.... JET JAGUAR II.... they are... HIRYUUUUUUUU BBAAATTSSUUU!!!!" [The arena lights dim as "Fly" by SUGAR RAY begins playing over the P.A. system. Dressed in a green-and-white wrestling mask and a green-and-white singlet, The Banshee tilts his head back, cups his hands over his mouth and issues his ululating warcry.
