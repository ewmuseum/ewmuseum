Senshi no Tamashii

7 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens on the runway entrance. Mr. Yamashita and the entire roster of SJPW wrestlers stand at the top of the runway. Mr. Yamashita has a microphone in his hands...] Shinsaku Yamashita: Last week, the world of Puroresu lost a legend. Giant Baba was truly a legend, not only in Nippon, but all over the world. His contributions to this sport were numerous, and at this time, All of Super Japan, friend and foe alike, stand here in honor of this great man. We ask all of you to please stand, and remain silent while a ceremonial bell rings ten times in his memory.... [The crowd stands up, and everyone bows their heads as the bell starts to sound. The camera pans to the wrestlers, showing Kenichi Kubota standing next to Tiger Extreme, The Outlaws standing next to The Young Warriors, and Ed Dean stands next to Mitsunori Takizawa. While the bell sounds, all of SJPW stands in silence, honoring the memory of one of the greatest legends of puroresu. As the bell sounds for the tenth time, an image of Giant Baba comes over the jumbotron....] ____ _____ __ __ /\ _`\ /\___ \/\ \ __/\ \ \ \ \L\ \/__/\ \ \ \/\ \ \ \ \ \ ,__/ _\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \/ /\ \_\ \ \ \_/ \_\ \ \ \_\ \ \____/\ `\___x___/ \/_/ \/___/ '\/__//__/ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= PRO JAPAN WRESTLING PRESENTS: __ _ _ / _\ ___ _ __ ___| |__ (_) \ \ / _ \ '_ \/ __| '_ \| | _\ \ __/ | | \__ \ | | | | \__/\___|_| |_|___/_| |_|_| _ __ ___ | '_ \ / _ \ | | | | (_) | |_| |_|\___/ _____ _ _ _ /__ \__ _ _ __ ___ __ _ ___| |__ (_|_) / /\/ _` | '_ ` _ \ / _` / __| '_ \| | | / / | (_| | | | | | | (_| \__ \ | | | | | \/ \__,_|_| |_| |_|\__,_|___/_| |_|_|_| =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Sunday, February 7th, 1999 Live from the Sapporo Nakajima Sports Complex Sapporo, Japan =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [The camera pands to Eddie Rose & Akira Ono at the broadcast position. Both men have solemn looks on their faces...] Eddie Rose: Hello Everyone. I'm Eddie Rose, and this is Akira Ono. As you saw moments ago, all of SJPW stood together in memory of Giant Baba, a legend in Pro Wrestling. His death has sent shockwaves throught Japan. SJPW is planing to further honor Baba, and Mr. Yamashita will make a special announcement on that later in our program. Akira Ono: Besides the loss of Giant Baba, we may have lost another legend in Tiger Kikuchi. After what Dean did to him last week, his career may finally be over. I truly hope Takizawa can pin him tonight, then take the title from him at Wrestlefest! ER: I understand how you feel Akira; Dean has been nigh unstoppable since he won the belt over Shinji Hamada. Can Takizawa prevail? Many say he must, or SJPW will never recover. And who will be his partner? AO: I don't know. I just hope he can find some reliable people! ER: I understand the camera is following Takizawa backstage, and he's approaching someone right now... [The camera cuts to backstage. Takizawa is seen approaching Masashi "Lightning" Lanther...] Takizawa: Lanther-sama! I've been looking for you. Lanther: I know Takizawa-sama. However, I can't help you tonight. Takizawa: Why not? Dean injured Kikuchi-sama, you're friend! Lanther: I know. However, Yamashita expected you'd recruit me first. So he is sending me to America. Takizawa: America? Why? Lanther: He is sending me to challenge for the NCWF cruiserweight title. He has some sort of arrangement with them. I have to leave tonight! Takizawa: Tonight? Lanther: Yes. My flight will be leaving twenty minutes before your match. I'm sorry Takizawa. I only found out a few minutes ago. I told him I wouldn't do it, but if I don't, he will terminate my contract with SJPW. My contract states that I am one of the tour representatives with American promotions, and if I don't go, I can be terminated. Takizawa: It's all right Lanther-sama. Yamashita is obviously plotting against me. Good luck in America. May Buddha smile on you during your title match. Lanther: May Buddha grant you the strength to put Dean in his place! [Takizawa and Lanther shake hands, and the camera cuts back to the broadcast table...] AO: Damn Yamashita! He's interfering in Takizawa's attempts at recruiting team mates! ER: It was obvious that Takizawa would recruit Lanther. Still, Lanther is at least getting a title shot at the NCWF cruiserweight title! AO: So? Avenging Kikuchi is more important! ER: I agree, but Lanther still has to be glad to be getting a title shot after his recent losses. AO: Maybe, but his heart will be with Takizawa... ER: Coming up, two men who are hungry for victory as they've both been frustrated in their ambitions recently. AO: Translation - this one is going to get ugly! ER: Colorfully put. Let's go to the ring. ___ ___ _______________________________________________ / _ \__ / / | /| / / / / ___/ // /| |/ |/ / Onslaught vs. Ace Kilcannon /_/ \___/ |__/|__/____________________________________________/ [The scene switches to the ring where Nishi Toju is standing holding his microphone.] Nishi: This contest is scheduled for one fall with a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first... from parts unknown... weighing in at 350 pounds... ON-SLAUGHT! [A loud explosion rocks the arena as "Natural Born Killaz" by Dr. Dre and Ice Cube begins pounding over the P.A. system. A huge behemoth of a man stalks through the curtain, Onslaught pausing at the top of the aisle to look slowly at the crowd before making his way down to the ring in a methodical manner. The fans respond with a polite cheer as Onslaught enters the ring and stands in the corner, waiting for the match to begin.] AO: I still have to wonder how much of a member of Chiimu Tora Onslaught can be. His nature doesn't really suit him to their ideals. ER: But he has allied himself with Chiimu Tora so we'll see if it's had any impact on his performance in the ring. Nishi: And his opponent... from Columbus, Ohio... weighing in at 245 pounds... ACE KIL-CAN-NON! ["Judgement Night" by Onyx and Biohazard begins thundering over the P.A. system as the streamlined figure of Ace Kilcannon steps out from behind the curtain. Kilcannon is dressed in his usual black combat boots, blue jeans, thick black belt, black ESWO T-shirt, black leather duster, black Oakley's with a red tint, and a black skull cap. He arrogantly stares out into the crowd for a moment as he saunters down the aisle with a cocky swagger. The fans again cheer politely to which Kilcannon responds with angry yelling and unheard obscenities. Hopping up onto the apron, he climbs to the top turnbuckle and moonsaults into the middle of the ring as Onslaught looks on. Staring at the bigger man, Kilcannon yells something unintelligible as he takes his gear off.] AO: Kilcannon as cocky as ever and you have to wonder if his attitude is a strength or a weakness? ER: I'd say both as it takes him places but sometimes into situations he can't handle. AO: He'd likely claim there's nothing he can't handle but there goes the bell. And this might be a mistake by Kilcannon as he goes for a lock up with the bigger man. Onslaught goes for a bearhug and slaps it on as the referee moves around to check it... OUCH! ER: Kilcannon counters with a facerake and the big man is temporarily blinded. And Kilcannon was clever enough to know not to do that when the referee could see or he'd have risked disqualification. AO: Kilcannon now grabs a beefy arm and attempts to whip Onslaught into the ropes but it's reversed by the masked giant. Kilcannon rebounds back and dodges, causing Onslaught to miss with a shoulderblock. ER: And here's some more of that attitude as Kilcannon takes a moment to showboat to the fans and tap the side of his head, indicating how smart he is. AO: He has to be careful... LOOK OUT! OH!! Onslaught just charged forward and connected with a running forearm smash that knocks Kilcannon flying! [the crowd applauds] ER: This is where the power of Onslaught comes into play as he pulls Kilcannon to his feet and sends him into the ropes with an Irish whip. Onslaught bends over as Kilcannon bounces back and he elevates the smaller man high into the air with a back bodydrop. AO: Kilcannon came crashing down on his spine as Onslaught lifted him up almost ten feet into the air. Kilcannon is in trouble as the masked giant lines him up while the smaller man struggles to his feet... AND ONSLAUGHT NAILS HIM WITH A LARIAT! [the crowd "ooohhs" and applauds] ER: He's really taking it to Kilcannon and you can't let a man the size of Onslaught throw you around like this. AO: Kilcannon is tough as nails, though, and can absorb a lot of punishment. Onslaught scoops him up and drapes Kilcannon over one shoulder. He lifts him into the air... and buries him with an incredible powerslam! [the crowd "ooohhs"] ER: And the referee is right there for the count: ...1... ...2... kickout!! [crowd applauds] AO: A lot of resilience shown by Kilcannon as Onslaught has really been taking it to him. Onslaught drags the smaller man to his feet once more and goes for a lariat... no! Kilcannon ducks out of the way! ER: Kilcannon with a punch... AND ONSLAUGHT DOESN'T EVEN MOVE! AO: Onslaught with a kick to the belly and Kilcannon doubles over as the wind is driven from his lungs. He's having some real problems against this powerful monster as Onslaught nails him with a double-axehandle across the back. AO: You wonder if Onslaught is targeting Kilcannon's back for special attention? Is he trying to soften him up for the Onslaught Bomb? Or maybe the Hell Buster? ER: Could be. Right now, Onslaught is dominating this match as he whips Kilcannon into the ropes, catches him on the way back... GORILLA PRESS SLAM! [the crowd "ooohhs"] AO: Amazing strength as Onslaught hoisted Kilcannon high up into the air over his head and then slammed him down into the canvas. ER: A devastating maneuver and now Onslaught makes another pin attempt: ...1... ...2... ...3-no! ...shoulder up! [crowd "ooohhs" and applauds] AO: Love him or hate him, you have to give credit to Kilcannon's toughness. ER: But how much more of this can he take? Onslaught is starting to look a little frustrated as he backs up while Kilcannon uses the ropes to pull himself upright. Kilcannon's looking very shaky as he leans on the ropes for support. AO: Here comes Onslaught with another running forearm smash but Kilcannon ducks out of the way... AND BACKDROPS ONSLAUGHT OUT OF THE RING! [the crowd applauds] ER: Quick thinking on the part of Ace Kilcannon as he needed to do something to defuse Onslaught's momentum. And the big man came down hard onto his side as he landed on the floor as he's still lying there while Kilcannon gets a chance to recover. AO: Kilcannon now sliding out of the ring and he's looking to dish out some punishment of his own. He comes up behind Onslaught who is struggling to his knees... OOH! ER: Kilcannon just rammed into Onslaught from behind, slamming him forward where his head smashed into the ringsteps! That hurt the big man a lot as Kilcannon grabs his head and pulls him over backward... INVERTED DDT RIGHT ONTO THE FLOOR! [the crowd "ooohhs"] AO: Onslaught had the early advantage but now it's Kilcannon who is in control. He pulls the masked giant to his feet and rolls him back into the ring, sliding back in behind him to pull him upright. ER: What's Kilcannon setting him up for? OH! Kilcannon takes Onslaught down with a Northern Lights suplex! AO: He's going for a pin and here's the referee to count: ...1... ...2... kickout!! [crowd "ooohhhs" then applauds] ER: Onslaught's taken a beating but he's far from finished. And Kilcannon is still looking a little battered himself although he's recovered nicely and is still in control of this match. AO: Onslaught's back up on his feet and Kilcannon is waiting for him... and he knocks him down with a superkick! [the crowd "ooohhs"] ER: Kilcannon with another pin attempt and here's the count: ...1... ...2... kickout!! [crowd applauds] AO: And again Onslaught is just too tough to be pinned at this point. Kilcannon is going to have to inflict a lot more punishment if he wants to keep Onslaught down for the three count. ER: That seems to be what Kilcannon thinks as well as he's climbing up onto the middle turnbuckle behind Onslaught as the big man climbs slowly to his feet. Kilcannon is lining himself up... he leaps off... and hits a flying bulldog onto the unsuspecting Onslaught! AO: Onslaught has taken a lot of hard shots to the head as Kilcannon does a little strutting, showing off to the crowd. ER: Look at this! Onslaught just shot up straight to his feet as if nothing happened!! And Kilcannon doesn't see him! AO: Now he does! Kilcannon turned around and froze in shock at the sight of the masked giant on his feet! ER: Onslaught grabs him by the throat... CHOKE SLAM! [the crowd "ooohhs" and applauds] AO: And just like that, the tide is reversed. But Onslaught is wobbling a little as he's still feeling the effects of all those moves that Kilcannon scored with. The masked giant bends down to pick Kilcannon up... OW! ER: Oh come on! The referee didn't see it but Kilcannon hit Onslaught with a low blow and big man staggers backward, clutching his groin! AO: You'd think it was obvious what just happened but the referee can't call an infraction he didn't see. Kilcannon now rises to his feet and bounces off the ropes to gain some momentum. He comes racing back... and executes a picture perfect flying dropkick on Onslaught! ER: But the big man doesn't go down as he staggers back into the ropes. Kilcannon charges forward and connects with a spinning leg lariat that rocks Onslaught back onto his heels. AO: Kilcannon looking to put Onslaught away as he whips the bigger man into the ropes and catches him on the way back in a waistlock... lifting him up... Hotshot! [the crowd "ooohhs"] ER: Impressive display of strength on the part of Kilcannon as he hoisted all three hundred and fifty pounds of Onslaught up into the air and dropped him throat first across the top rope. AO: Onslaught finally goes down and Kilcannon hits him with a kick to the back of the head to keep him there. Kilcannon now climbing up onto the top turnbuckle... leaping off... FLYING HEADBUTT! [the crowd applauds] ER: Kilcannon hooks the leg and he may have done it this time: ...1... ...2... ...3!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** Nishi: Here is your winner... ACE KILCANNON!! [The crowd applauds as a weary Kilcannon rolls off of Onslaught and out of the ring.] AO: I didn't think he'd pull it off but Kilcannon came back from a bad deficit to put Onslaught down for the count. ER: The masked monster was dominant at the beginning of this match but Kilcannon managed to overcome the size difference to come out ahead. [Kilcannon slides back into the ring, walks over to Nishi, and grabs the microphone from his hands. He motions for the crowd to quiet down, then starts to speak...] Ace Kilcannon: Last week, Mitsunori Takizawa challenged Ed Dean to a title shot. The bum was gonna accept, but instead, Yamashita forced Takizawa into some tag team match against Dean, his cronies, and Yamashita's bullsh*t corporate team. Well, I got news for Yamashita: I know you're trying to railroad Takizawa, so I'm gonna f*ck with you! Takizawa, you needed five guys for this match; well, now you just need four! [the crowd "oooohs"] I don't like you, and I don't give a damn if you get your title shot or not. However, I do know that Dean doesn't deserve the title, and if I gotta team with your dinosaur ass to get my hands on him, then so be it! [crowd cheers]. So get ready Dean, cause you're gonna be ACED! [The crowd cheers as Ace throws the mike down, and exits the ring] ER: WOW! Ace Kilcannon has agreed to be Takizawa's first partner! AO: I hate to say it, but Kilcannon has finally done something I approve of. I may not like his reasons for doing so, but it's refreshing that Kilcannon would side with SJPW in this situation. He's always been a loner; maybe he does have some loyalty to the promotion that's made him a star in Nippon!
