Senshi no Tamashii

7 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Senshi no Tamashii concludes...] [Usami stands Ace up, then nails him with a standing dropkick. Usami runs to the ropes, and springboards off into rana that sends Ace flying across the ring. Usami kip-ups to his feet, then runs towards Kilcannon, landing on him with a senton. He hooks the leg, but Loco breaks up the count. Loco grabs Usami, and nails him with a fisherman's buster before heading back to the ring apron. Ace sits up, and starts to get to his feet, when Ishida enters the ring, and nails him with a running boot to his face! The crowd "ooohs" as Ace collapses on the mat. Ishida helps his partner to his feet, and drags him back to their corner. Usami tags in Kubota, then the Dragons drag Ace to his feet. Ishida sets Ace up for a powerbomb, while Usami gabs his neck. They hit a combination powerbomb/neckbreaker before exiting the ring. Kubota chokes Ace on the mat, while Extreme & Tokugawa finally get Matsumoto & Takizawa seperated, and back on the ring apron...] ER: Ace is getting clobbered out there! AO: I never thought I would ever cheer Kilcannon on, but I am now! GET UP ACE!! [The crowd starts to chant "ACE! ACE!" as Kubota drags Ace to his feet, and rams his head into the turnbuckle. Kubota rams his head into the corner three more times, spins him around, then starts to lay into him with kicks to his gut. Ace sags in the corner, dazed. Takizawa, finally seeing enough, enters the ring, only to be cut off by Goto. While Goto forces Takizawa back to his corner. Douglas chokes Ace from behind, while Kubota knees him in the groin. Matsumoto sneaks into the ring while Goto is dealing with Takizawa, and charges Kubota from behind. Douglas calls out a warning, and Kubota turns around, only to be nailed by a jumping thrust kick by Matsumoto! Kubota falls through the rope to the floor, and the crowd cheers. Matsumoto grabs Douglas, and rams his head into the ring post, and he falls to the floor. Matsumoto grabs Ace, and in a move that shocks the fans, IRISH WHIPS him into his own corner! Dean enters the ring, and grabs Matsumoto from behind, then sends him to the mat with a vicious lariat! He goes back to the apron as Kubota re-enters the ring, and elbowdrops the back of Matsumoto's head. Ace, finally recovered in his corner, tags in Extreme. Extreme enters the ring, and lets loose a blood-curdling cry that silences the entire crowd. Kubota stops punishing Matsumoto, and looks at Extreme with a touch of fear in his eyes....] ER: Matsumoto saved Ace with that irish whip, but got nailed for his trouble! AO: And now the lunatic is in the ring! No one is safe from him! [Extreme lets loose a second cry, then charges Kubota. Kubota tries to get out of the ring, but Extreme catches him, and rakes his back. Kubota cringes in pain, and Extreme grabs the back of his head, and starts to lay into him with headbutts! The crowd cheers, then Extreme applies a reverse facelock, and inverted suplexes Kubota to the mat! Extreme goes to a neutral corner, and ascends to the top rope. He lets loose another of his screams, then comes off the top rope, legdropping Kubota across the back of his head! The crowd "oooohs" loudly, and Extreme rolls Kubota over, and makes a cover. Goto reaches two before Dean breaks it up. Dean sits Extreme up, slaps on a full nelson, then drags him to his feet with the nelson still on, before hitting a release Dragon Suplex. Extreme lands hard on his head, and the crowd "oooohs" as Dean raises his fist in the air. Takizawa slips into the ring, spins Dean around, and kicks him low in the gut. Dean doubles over, and Takizawa piledrives him to the mat! The crowd roars it's approval, and Takizawa starts to drag Dean to his feet, but Douglas saves Dean by nailing Takizawa from behind with a double axe handle. He grabs Takizawa with a waistlock, then falls back into a suplex. Douglas helps Dean to his feet, and they go back to their corner. Kubota makes it to his feet, and he tags in Usami. Usami goes to the top rope, and perches. Extreme gets to his feet, and helps Takizawa up. While he checks on Takizawa, Usami leaps off the top rope, catching both men with a dropkick. Takizawa flies forward, and once more slams into Matsumoto, who was standing on the ring apron. Matsumoto flies off the apron, and crashes onto the railing!] ER: Takizawa inadvertantly nailed Matsumoto again! AO: This doesn't look good, does it Eddie? [Takizawa recovers, and slides outside the ring. He helps Matsumoto to his feet, and Matsumoto shoves him off! Ace & Rupan Loco go to the floor, and seperate the two partners. In the ring, Usami nails Extreme with a series of three snap legdrops across the back of his head. He rolls Extreme over, and covers, but Tokugawa breaks the pinfall up. Tokugawa hits a gutwrench suplex on Usami, while Dean enters the ring. Tokugawa gets to his feet, and Dean spears Tokugawa through the ropes to the floor! Tokugawa lands at the feet of Takizawa & Matsumoto, who look up as Dean mocks them. Matsumoto slides into the ring, but Dean catches him with an elbowdrop. Dean slaps on a Fujiwara armbar, cranking back on the arm. Extreme gets to his feet, and moves to intercede, but Takizawa stops him! He holds out his hand for a tag, and Extreme tags him in! Takizawa, now the legal man, enters the ring, and breaks up the armbar. He drags Dean to his feet, and shoves him back into the corner. He then puts his boot on Dean's throat, and chokes him in the corner! The crowd "ooohs" as The Maulers enter the ring, and nail Takizawa from behind! Matsumoto makes it to his feet, and he grabs Douglas from behind, and hits a release german suplex. He then grabs Thomas, locking his arms behind him, then hits a release tiger suplex! Matsumoto helps Takizawa to his feet, but Takizawa shoves him off! The crowd "ooohs" as Matsumoto gets in Takizawa's face, and Tokugawa enters the ring to break it up. Dean recovers, and he nails Takizawa from behind with a lariat, and he again crashes into Matsumoto! Both men fall to the mat, and Dean alternates putting the boots to both men. Tokugawa nails Dean from behind with a forearm shot, spins him around, and nails him with The Shinzo Mahi! The crowd "ooohs" as Dean goes down hard, holding both the back of his neck and his chest. Usami springs to the top rope, and comes off with a missle dropkick that sends Tokugawa falling through the ropes to the floor. Usami waits, and as Tokugawa gets to his feet, Usami charges, and executes a somersault crokscrew plancha to the floor! The crowd "ooohs" loudly, and Ishida & Kubota attempt to enter the ring, only to be intercepted by Ace & Rupan Loco. Ace nails Ishida with a flying forearm, goes to the ropes, and takes both of them over the top rope to the floor with a cross bodyblock, while Loco catches Kubota with kneelift. He rolls Kubota outside the ring, and slides out after him...] ER: This match keeps breaking down! And we're almost out of time! AO: Takizawa can still do it Eddie! I'm confident he will prevail! [The Maulers make it to their feet, and they drags Matsumoto & Takizawa to their feet. They attempt a train wreck, but Matsumoto & Takizawa reverse it, and Douglas & Thomas collide in the center of the ring. Tiger Extreme goes to the top rope, then comes off with an elbowsmash to the top of Douglas's head. Douglas staggers forward, and Matsumoto catches him, and sends him over the top rope to the floor with an EXPLODER SUPLEX! The crowd rises to it's feet at this, and Extreme grabs Thomas, and throws him to the floor, then slides out after him. Takizawa grabs Dean, and hits his patented Cradle DDT. He makes a cover, but Dean kicks out at two and a half! The crowd "ooooohs", shocked that the champion has survived two devestating holds. Takizawa heads to the top rope, but Matsumoto follows him! The crowd "ooohs" in surprise as Takizawa sees Matsumoto coming up to the top with him...] ER: What is Tetsuo doing? AO: Buddha no! He's going to betray Takizawa!!!! [The two men make it to the top rope, barely keeping their balance. Suddenly, Matsumoto grabs Takizawa, and sticks his head between his legs. The crowd "oooohs" as Matsumoto spins on the top rope, lifting Takizawa up over his head, then flipping him over in mid-air, before driving both of them off the top rope, Takizawa slamming on top of Dean! Takizawa hooks the leg, and Goto drops to count. Matsumoto applies pressure to Takizawa's back, making it harder for Dean to kick out. He tries, but Goto makes the three count! The crowd rises to it's feet in applause...] Nishi: The winners of this match....TEAM SJPW!!! [crowd cheers louder] ER: Takizawa did it! He pinned Ed Dean! AO: With a little help from Tetsuo Matsumoto! What an incredible move! He inverted powerbombed Takizawa off the top rope onto Dean! [The rest of Team SJPW enters the ring, and slap Takizawa on the back. Takizawa, still a bit shocked that he won, walks over to Matsumoto. The two men staredown for a moment, then they both smile at each other, and embrace. Team SJPW lift Takizawa up, and he soaks in the cheers of the crowd. A horde of streamers fly into the ring...] ER: It was a swerve! Takizawa & Matsumoto wanted everyone to think they were ready to turn on each other! AO: They had me fooled! I was truly concerned that Tetsuo would turn on his uncle! [The crowd cheers even louder as the rest of Chiimu Tora come out from the back, and head to the ring. They all shake hands with Takizawa. Meanwhile. The Maulers help Dean to his feet, and assist him out of the ring. Along with Kubota & The Corporate Dragons, they head to the back. Tiger Kikuchi, wearing a neckbrace, comes out from the back, and the crowd starts chanting his name. He waves to the crowd, and enters the ring. He takes the microphone from Nishi, and motions for the crowd to quiet down. Team SJPW lets Takizawa down, and he faces Kikuchi...] Tiger Kikuchi: Takizawa-sama, you have done one thing I, and the rest of Chiimu Tora failed to do. You pinned Ed Dean. Now, you go to Wrestlefest, and challenge Dean for the All-Asian title. If you defeat him, you will restore Chiimu Tora to all it's former glory. Not only that, but you will restore SJPW's lost honor. However, win or lose, I can say this: Tonight, you have won the heart and souls of these fans here tonight. Forever more Mitsunori Takizawa, you will be their hero! [the crowd cheers wildly, and Takizawa's face is overcome with emotion. Kikuchi and Takizawa embrace, and the members of Chiimu Tora hoist both men onto their shoulders, and parade them around the ring. The crowd starts a Takizawa chant, and a second wave of streamers hits the ring...] ER: What a tremendous night! In two weeks, Ed Dean must face his former tag team partner, Mitsunori Takizawa for the All-Asian title. The one man Dean thought he'd never have to face could be the man to finally wrest the All-Asian title from his waist. AO: Takizawa can do it! I Know he can! He will restore honor to both Chiimu Tora AND SJPW! [The credits start to roll over the image of Kikuchi & Takizawa waving to the fans. The camera pans to the runway entrance, where Ed Dean stands, gazing out at his next opponent. He stares grimly, then walks to the back as the camera fades....] ____ _____ ____ __ __ /\ _`\ /\___ \/\ _`\ /\ \ __/\ \ \ \,\L\_\\/__/\ \ \ \L\ \ \ \/\ \ \ \ \/_\__ \ _\ \ \ \ ,__/\ \ \ \ \ \ \ /\ \L\ \/\ \_\ \ \ \/ \ \ \_/ \_\ \ \ `\____\ \____/\ \_\ \ `\___x___/ \/_____/\/___/ \/_/ '\/__//__/ © 1999 http://www.[name withheld].com/sjpw/
