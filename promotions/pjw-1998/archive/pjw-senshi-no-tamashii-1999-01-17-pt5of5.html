Senshi no Tamashii

17 January 1999

[Senshi no Tamashii concludes...] ER: Banshee wrestling a more submission style tonight. It's not like his normal high-flying attack. AO: He's wrestling smart! Kagemusha was caught totally off guard by this gameplan! ER: Banshee drags Kagemusha to his feet, and executes a kneebreaker. He drags Kagemusha back to his feet, and executes a second kneebreaker! [crowd "oooohs"] AO: Banshee held onto the leg, and he starts to apply the figure four leglock...Kagemusha counters it into a cradle... ...1... ...2... ER: Kickout! [crowd applauds] Banshee rolls to his feet, and dropkicks Kagemusha in the knee again as he tries to get up! Banshee falls on top of him, grapevining the leg... ...1... ...2... AO: Kickout! [crowd applauds] Both men get to their feet, and Banshee goes for the knee again, but Kagemusha side-steps the dropkick! Banshee falls to the mat, and Kagemusha drags him to his feet...irish whip to the ropes...kick to the gut doubles Banshee over...GUTWRENCH POWERBOMB!!! [crowd "oooohs"] Goto drops to count... ...1... ...2... ER: KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] I thought he had him there! Both men showing fatigue all ready, as both of them had hard fought matches earlier this evening! AO: Kagemusha gets to his feet, and hobbles over to the corner. He goes out on the apron, then starts to climb to the top rope. Banshee sitting up now, and he starts to get to his feet! Kagemusha on top now, and he tries to steady himself on that weakened leg...Banshee charges the corner...DROPKICK to the knee causes Kagemusha to crotch himself! [crowd "oooohs"] ER: Banshee now climbing up in the corner, and he starts to pummel Kagemusha's face with his right hand. Kagemusha reels under the blows, unable to defend himself! AO: Banshee now standing Kagemusha up on the top rope...he applies a front facelock, then hooks his leg...FISHERMAN'S SUPERPLEX!!! [crowd "ooohs"] Goto drops to count... ...1... ...2... ...3... **ding**ding**ding** Nishi: The winner, and NEW PJW Grand Prix Senshuken....THE BANSHEE!!! [crowd applauds] ER: Wow! That came out of nowhere! I didn't think the match would end so quickly! AO: I must admit surprise myself. Kagemusha's knee took some nasty damage, and when Banshee caught him on the top rope, it must have been too much for his knee to take. After the Fisherman's Superplex, his leg just wasn't strong enough to kick out of the pinning position! [The rest of Chiimu Tora heads to the ring as streamers fly into the ring. Strangely enough, Shishi Fujiwara is the only one missing. Shinsaku Yamashita heads to the ring, followed by The Corporate Dragons. They enter the ring, and Shinji Usami hands Yamashita the Grand Prix title. Banshee holds the title over his head. He goes to the corner, and stands on the second rope, then lets out his patented war cry. He raises the title over his head, then jumps down, and is congratulated by his fellow Chiimu Tora members. Kagemusha sags in the corner, breathing heavily, and rubbing his knee. Banshee walks over to him, and extends his hand. Kagemusha looks up at him, his face emotionless. Banshee again offers him his hand, and Kagemusha stands up, and looks at him. The crowd starts to chant his name, and he turns to look at the crowd, then turns to Banshee, and shakes his hand! The crowd applauds as Banshee raises Kagemusha's hand in the air, and more streamers hit the ring...] ER: What a momentous victory for Chiimu Tora! After all the recent setbacks at the hands of Ed Dean, Banshee has brought some gold to Chiimu Tora! AO: This is a great moment for Chiimu Tora! Now if Jet Jaguar II can defeat Ed Dean, it will be even greater! [Chiimu Tora exit the ring, and slap hands with the fans in the back. The ring attendants start to clear the streamers out of the ring...] ER: I'm afraid I don't know what will happen in our next match. Has Ed Dean become unstoppable? AO: It would seem so! After knocking out The Banshee last week, Dean has completely dominated every member of Chiimu Tora that he's faced!! ER: I only hope that Jet Jaguar II can put an end to this. AO: But he's the smallest man in Chiimu Tora! Dean will tear him limb from limb! ___ ___ _______________________________________________ / _ \__ / / | /| / / All-Asian Juuryou Shimeijiai: / / ___/ // /| |/ |/ / Jet Jaguar II vs Ed Dean /_/ \___/ |__/|__/____________________________________________/ [The camera switches from ringside to the ring itself where Nishi Toju is standing with his microphone in hand.] Nishi: This contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the All-Asian Juuryou Championship! Introducing first, the challenger, accompanied by his tag team partner, PJW Grand Prix Senshuken, The Banshee... AO: The Banshee is coming back for more? Is he mad?? ER: Hush, Akira-san! Nishi: ...hailing from Kobe, Japan... weighing in at 190 pounds... JET JAGUAR II! [The crowd gives a loud cheer as "Jet Jaguar" by Neon Bushido begins playing over the P.A. system. From the arena entrance, a cloud of dry ice fog forms as two slim figures come running out. The first is Jet Jaguar II, his hands raised in the air as the crowd cheers and throws paper streamers into the aisle. The Banshee, his expression grim, stays a few feet behind him as Jet jaguar II jogs quickly to the ring, slapping hands with fans along the way.] AO: Do you think Banshee might be contemplating attacking Dean? It would be suicide considering what happened to him last week! ER: He cannot be happy with the result of that match as Dean knocked him out cold with a cradle DDT. But The Banshee may also be here to watch out for his friend. [Once Jet Jaguar II gets to the ring, he hops up onto the railing and slingshots over the top and into the ring. The Banshee walks over to his corner and cheers his partner on as the masked youngster climbs up onto the second turnbuckle, raising his fist in front of his chest to give the "Team Tiger" gesture. The fans respond with another loud cheer.] AO: It's easy to tell who the fan favorite is in this match! Nishi: And his opponent... accompanied to the ring by Hadi Moditian and Doug Thomas... ER: It's going to get crowded at ringside. Nishi: ...fighting out of New York, New York... weighing in at 355 pounds... the undisputed All-Asian Juuryou Champion... ED DEAN! [A polite cheer is heard from the crowd as three figures appear in the entranceway without any accompanying music. The huge figure of Ed Dean is in the forefront, wearing his usual red singlet, red kneepads and shin guards over blue Asics, white wrist tape and a black T-shirt that reads "G-MAX DOJO" on the front in red. Hadi Moditian and Doug Thomas walk immediately behind him, Thomas wearing a black, red and white SHVF jumpsuit with a "G-MAX DOJO" T-shirt underneath. Moditian is wearing a well cut black suit, white shirt, and red tie, carrying the All-Asian title belt which he brandishes proudly over his head.] ER: The day that man gets his come-uppance cannot come too soon! AO: It might be a long time coming yet. Dean is like an avalanche, unstoppable! [Ignoring the cheering crowd, Dean stalks down to the ring, staring at Jet Jaguar II intently. At ringside, Moditian and Thomas step over to his corner as Dean climbs in, performing some last minute stretching in preparation for the match.] ER: Jet Jaguar must be careful in this match as he is giving up an enormous amount of weight to Dean. There's the bell and Dean charges forward but misses with an attempted forearm smash. Jaguar ducks out of the way and runs into the ropes... DROPKICK! [crowd applauds] AO: Smart move by the youngster as he uses his mobility to his advantage. But Dean barely staggered backward as a result of that blow as he lunges forward with executes a lariat... and again Jaguar ducks out of the way! ER: This is what he must do if he is to succeed. He must avoid Dean's punishing blows and adopt a hit-and-run strategy. A snap kick catches Dean in the ribs and Jaguar immediately follows that up with a spinning back fist to the shoulder. AO: But his blows don't have the raw power that Dean can muster... oh! And Jaguar connects with a Shotay palm strike that catches Dean right under his nose! The champion felt that! ER: He certainly did as he backs away while Jaguar continues after him with a roundhouse kick and then a thrust kick. It's rare to see Dean give ground to anyone but he's adopted a defensive posture in the face of Jet Jaguar II's superior speed. AO: This is where Dean's experience is coming into play as he's using his strong style technique, holding his arms against his sides to ward off those blows. But he manages to snag Jaguar's arm, sending him into the ropes with an Irish whip. ER: The youngster doesn't want to let that happen too often as he comes rebounding back... AND CONNECTS WITH A FLYING SPINNING LEG LARIAT! [crowd "oooohhs"] AO: That one caught Dean flush on the jaw and he staggers back into the corner, using the ropes to keep himself upright. The crowd is on its feet, cheering on Jet Jaguar II who climbs up onto the ropes to give the Team Tiger salute. ER: He better be careful about turning his back on Dean but fortunately he doesn't waste too much time as he turns around while still on the middle turnbuckle. Dean staggers out of the opposite corner... and Jaguar hits a dropkick from the second rope! [crowd cheers and applauds] AO: Dean goes down! The champ is down!! ER: Jaguar right on top of him, going for the pin. The referee is there to count: ...1... ...2... ...kickout! [crowd "ooohhs"] AO: And Dean does it with authority as he presses Jaguar up off his chest, nearly throwing the youngster halfway across the ring. ER: Dean now taking a moment to collect himself, rolling to the outside to confer with Moditian and Thomas. Back in the ring, Jaguar runs into the ropes on the opposite side, rebounds back... WHOAH! AO: Jaguar is really pulling out the stops! He jumped up onto the top rope, bouncing off to fly right into Dean with a springboard plancha! ER: And again he takes the bigger man off his feet! The match has been all Jaguar so far as Dean has remained largely on the offensive. AO: This is the type of match that Jaguar wants to wrestle, fast-paced and high-flying. Dean needs to slow things down and... what's this? ER: The Banshee didn't like Doug Thomas and Hadi Moditian being so close to the fallen wrestlers and he's right over there, arguing with both of them. AO: Definitely some bad blood felt there as Moditian restrains Thomas from attacking Banshee. Meanwhile, Dean and Jet Jaguar II are slowly regaining their feet with the masked youngster looking to be in better shape. He shoves Dean toward the ring, pushing him under the bottom rope as he climbs up onto the ring apron. ER: I'm surprised by how dominant Jaguar is being as he flings himself up and over with the top rope, hitting Dean with a slingshot legdrop. Now he's pulling Dean upright... oooh! AO: Dean finally draws the smaller man in as he buries his knee into Jaguar's stomach! Scoop and a bodyslam sends Jaguar down to the canvas and all of a sudden, the advantage has turned to Ed Dean! ER: Jaguar finally let himself get too close and Dean is taking full advantage of the opening. Jaguar struggles to rise and Dean drives him down on one knee with a powerful forearm smash across the back. AO: And that's Jaguar's weak spot as Dean grabs him by the shoulders from behind and nails a headbutt right between the shoulder blades! ER: Jaguar cries out in anguish as Dean picks him up with an inverted atomic drop, dropping him across his knee and compacting his tail bone. Jaguar slumps down to a seated position and Dean immediately drives his knee into his back, wrapping his big hands around Jaguar's face and applying a reverse chinlock. AO: Now Jaguar is in trouble as Dean pulls back on his chin and presses his knee into Jaguar's injured back. A slow match down on the mat definitely favors Dean as he's derailed the high-flying youngster quite successfully. ER: Dean releases the hold, pausing for a moment to yell something at The Banshee on the outside as he grabs Jaguar's mask and drags him to his feet. Dean now steps up beside him... GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! AO: And that move definitely hurt as again, Jaguar goes crashing down onto his weakened spine. Dean stands over the youngster, taunting him as he slaps him in the face. ER: That's despicable! Again Dean is showing no respect for SJPW competitors as he's looking to embarrass Jet Jaguar II. The champ pulls Jaguar to his feet... only to take him right back down again with a sidewalk slam. AO: Jaguar was doing so well but now Dean is showing why he's one of the toughest men to ever step into the ring. Jaguar rolls over onto his stomach and... what's this? ER: Dean is kneeling on Jaguar's lower back as he grabs both of the smaller man's wrists, pulling back on both arms in what looks something like a crucifixion submission move! AO: Dean's using his size advantage to apply yet more pressure onto Jet Jaguar II's back with this unconventional hold. The crowd is cheering Jaguar on, trying to encourage him. ER: But he's unable to break loose from Dean's grip and finally the champ lets go, rising to stomp on the back of Jaguar's head and sneering at the crowd. He grabs an arm and jerks Jaguar to his feet, sending him crashing into the corner turnbuckle with an Irish whip. AO: How much more pounding can Jaguar's vertebrae withstand? He's slumped in the corner, unable to resist as Dean charges forward with a shoulderblock... NO! ER: Jaguar stepped out of the way and Dean crashed into the turnbuckle at full speed! And Jaguar's caught his second wind as he backs up, charges forward... SPINNING LEG LARIAT SMASHES RIGHT INTO DEAN'S FACE! [crowd cheers] AO: Amazing! The kid is still in this match as he lands out on the ring apron. Dean is dazed as he stumbles out of the corner... AND JAGUAR HITS HIM IN THE BACK OF THE HEAD WITH A SPRINGBOARD DROPKICK! [crowd applauds] ER: Dean is down on the canvas and Jaguar is on fire! He's roaring his defiance at the crowd as he turns back to Dean... and rams into his skull with a diving headbutt! AO: Jaguar not holding anything back as he's once again regained control of this match. It looked like Dean was going to put him away but the youngster's speed certainly helped him out of a dangerous situation. ER: Dean is thoroughly bewildered as he's taking some stiff shots from Jet Jaguar II. He struggles to his feet as Jaguar climbs up in one corner... and a missile dropkick sends Dean stumbling backward into the ropes... he's caught up in them! AO: This is not good! The champ is entangled in the ropes as his arms have been twisted between them and he's trapped. The crowd is cheering on Jet Jaguar II who points at his helpless opponent and then runs into the ropes on the opposite side. ER: Jaguar rebounds back, building up speed... AND HE SLAMS INTO DEAN WITH A FLYING CROSS BODYBLOCK! [crowd "ooohhs" and applauds] AO: Dean was powerless to avoid that maneuver but the force of the blow finally untangled him from the ropes. Jaguar is really pouring it on as he circles around behind Dean as the champ uses the ropes to pull himself up. ER: But what's he doing? He's going for a German suplex on a man almost twice his size?? AO: He's grabbed Dean and is trying to fling him over... no! Jaguar cries out and clutches his back, releasing Dean before he can hit a German suplex! ER: A bad mistake on the youngster's part as he's aggravated the injuries Dean has inflicted on his spine. Dean looks a little confused as he recovers from the punishment he's endured as Jaguar has collapsed to his knees. AO: This looks serious as Jaguar has had major problems with back injuries in the past. And here comes Dean to take advantage of the situation as he hammers Jaguar with a forearm strike across the shoulders. ER: Jaguar has lost his momentum as Dean pulls him up from behind... ROLLING GERMAN SUPLEX INTO A RELEASE DRAGON SUPLEX! [crowd "ooohhs"] AO: An incredible move as Jaguar is slammed into the mat with tremendous force. Dean now grabbing a leg as he rolls Jaguar onto his stomach... AND HE'S LOCKED ON A SINGLE-LEG BOSTON CRAB! ER: Jaguar is screaming in pain as Dean bears down on his damaged spine. He's summoning one last burst of energy, trying to break free but... he can't do it. He's submitting! Jaguar gives up! **ding** **ding** **ding** Nishi: Here is your winner and *still* All-Asian Juuryou Senshuken... ED DEAN!!! [a polite cheer rises from the crowd as they applaud Dean's ability but the cheering quickly turns into boos] AO: He's not releasing the hold! The referee is yelling at Dean to let go of Jet Jaguar II but the champion is refusing, inflicting more pain on the youngster! ER: Here comes The Banshee! And a jumping sidekick to the face knocks Dean onto his back! AO: But Doug Thomas is right there as well, hammering into The Banshee's back with a double axehandle smash. Now both members of Hiryuu Batsu are down as Dean gets back to his feet and he and Thomas begin stomping away on the two masked men. ER: This is awful! Somebody stop these men!! [Suddenly, a uncharacteristically loud cheer fills the arena.] AO: Look! It's Kikuchi-san! ER: Tiger Kikuchi is running down the aisle! And the security team is right behind him as they swarm into the ring... AO: Look at Kikuchi-san! He's going right for Ed Dean! ER: But security is keeping the two apart as Dean laughs in Kikuchi's face, pointing at the two fallen members of Chiimu Tora behind him. AO: And yet another member of Chiimu Tora falls before the juggernaut that is Ed Dean. Will Tiger Kikuchi coming out of retirement be the one to stop him? [The referee consults with Nishi Toju for a moment, and he motions for the crowd to quiet down. The crowd settles down, and Moditan stops Dean & Thomas on the runway to see what Nishi has to say...] Nishi: Referee Goto Kyoji informs me that he has DISQUALIFIED Ed Dean for refusing to release his submission hold after Goto ordered him to do so. Therefore, the winner, via Disqualification...JET JAGUAR II!!! [crowd cheers loudly, while Dean, Thomas and Moditan are irate on the runway ramp.] ER: I can't believe it! Dean lost! AO: Yes! Nishi: However, the title cannot change hands on a disqualification, so Ed Dean remains All-Asian Juuryou Senshuken... [Kikuchi grabs the microphone from Nishi...] Tiger Kikuchi: Ed Dean, you have finally broken your winning ways over Chiimu Tora! [crowd cheers]. Your streak of good karma is at an end! On the next Zenmen Sensou, you and I will face for the title. On that night, your reign as Senshuken comes to an end! [crowd cheers!] ER: Tiger Kikuchi serving Dean notice that his title reign will soon be over. AO: I can't wait! [Dean, Thomas & Moditan head to the back, Dean totally irate over Goto's decision. Kikuchi helps Banshee & Jaguar II to their feet, and checks to make sure they are all right. He then raises Jaguar II's hands over his head. Streamers start to fly into the ring.
