Senshi no Tamashii

17 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Senshi no Tamashii continues...] ER: Both men move to lock up, and Yoshida drives a knee into Banshee's gut. Knife-edge chop by Yoshida [crowd "Whooos"] staggers Banshee back...Standing Dropkick sends Banshee into the corner! AO: Yoshida charges in...SUPERKICK to the jaw!!! [crowd "oooohs"] Banshee sags in the corner, and Yoshida jumps up on the second rope...springboard enzugiri! [crowd "oooohs"] [Banshee staggers out of the corner, and Yoshida gets to his feet, hops up on the second rope, then leaps, bulldogging Banshee to the mat. Yoshida rolls him over, and makes a cover. Goto drops to count, and gets to two before Banshee kicks out. Yoshida gets up, dragging Banshee with him. Banshee suddenly connects with an enzuigiri to the face that sends Yoshida stumbling backward into the ropes. Bouncing off the ropes on the opposite side, Banshee charges forward. Yoshida attempts a knifehand chop, but Banshee ducks underneath it with a baseball slide that puts him out on the ring apron. As Yoshida turns around, Banshee executes a handstand, trapping Yoshida's head between his ankles with a headscissors and flipping him over the top rope and out of the ring. While Yoshida is on the arena floor, Banshee rolls back under the bottom rope, sprinting across the ring to bounce off the ropes on the opposite side. As Yoshida stands up, Banshee races toward him, vaulting up onto the top rope to launch himself into a springboard corkscrew plancha right onto Yoshida down on the floor!] ER: What a move by Banshee! Yoshida went down hard! AO: Banshee showing why he was chosen for membership in Chiimu Tora! He's showing resilience; few would expect him to be so aggressive after losing to Ed Dean last week! [Goto starts to count both men out...] ER: Banshee on his feet now, and he drags Yoshida to his feet. He grabs Yoshida by the arm, and whips him into the railing hard! [crowd "oooohs"]. Banshee charges Yoshida, who ducks, causing Banshee to slam onto the railing! [crowd "oooohs"] AO: Yoshida lifts Banshee up, and drops him throat first across the railing! [crowd "oooohs"] Yoshida now choking Banshee on the floor with his boot! [Goto's count reaches eight, and Yoshida breaks the choke. He climbs up onto the apron, and moves away from Banshee. Banshee gets to his feet, and Yoshida runs across the apron, somersaulting forward into a thesz press that drives Banshee back to the mat! The crowd "ooohs" as Yoshida straddles Banshee, and lands three stiff forearms to the side of his head. Goto's count reaches thirteen, and Yoshida stands up, and grabs Banshee by the mask. He drags him to his feet, and slams his head on the ring apron. Goto's count hits sixteen, and Yoshida rolls Banshee into the ring. Yoshida climbs up on the apron, then enters the ring. He drags Banshee to his feet again, double underhooks his arms, and executes a picture perfect butterfly suplex. Yoshida stands Banshee up again, grabs his waist, and falls backward, rolling over him for a cradle. Goto drops to count, and makes it to two and a half before Banshee is able to kick out. The crowd applauds, while Yoshida complains to the referee of a slow count...] ER: This match is so even. Yoshida has really come into his own as a singles competitor! AO: I agree. Few people thought he could come out of the shadow of his cousin Shigeru! ER: Yoshida with another standing dropkick that sends Banshee staggering back to the ropes. He is so agile! AO: He is! Yoshida runs forward, and hits another high kneelift! [crowd "oooohs"] [Yoshida sends Banshee into the ropes with an Irish whip and then drops down to his stomach as Banshee rebounds back and hops over him. Yoshida stands up and drops his head for a backdrop but as Banshee rebounds back once more, he leapfrogs over Yoshida. Bouncing off the ropes for a third time, Banshee counters a hiptoss attempt by Yoshida to nail a hiptoss of his own. While Yoshida is down, Banshee steps out between the ropes onto the ring apron. Grabbing the top rope with both hands, he turns back to face into the ring and use the rope to fling himself up and over for a slingshot bodyblock as Yoshida stands up. Goto drops to count, but Yoshida kicks out at one and a half. Both men get to their feet, and Banshee kicks Yoshida low in the gut, then snaps him down with a DDT! Banshee again covers, but Yoshida kicks out at two. Banshee goes to the corner, and climbs to the second rope. As Yoshida gets to his feet, Banshee hits a dropkick to the back of Yoshida's head, sending him flying forward. Yoshida's head hits the top turnbuckle in the corner, and he sags there. Banshee sets himself in the opposite corner, runs forward and leaps, landing an avalanche to Yoshida's back. Yoshida continues to sag in the corner, and Banshee grabs him in a waistlock. He starts to lift Yoshida up for a German Suplex, but Yoshida lands a palm strike to Banshee's face, shifts his weight, and crashes down on top of him! Goto drops to count, but Banshee kicks out at one...] ER: I thought Banshee had him there, but Yoshida again shows he's a ring techinician. AO: Banshee was lucky that palm strike didn't stun him at all. ER: Yoshida drags Banshee to his feet...Yoshida goes for a spinwheel kick...Banshee catches the leg...Banshee spins Yoshida around...BULLDOG!! [crowd "ooohs"] AO: Good comeback by Banshee. He stole Yoshida's momentum from him! [Banshee drags Yoshida to his feet, then hits an inverted DDT. Yoshida lays on the mat, holding the back of his head, while Banshee goes to the top rope. Banshee comes off, landing a vicious flying legdrop across Yoshida's throat! The crowd "oooohs" as Yoshida rolls around the mat, clutching his throat. Banshee drags Yoshida to his feet, and attempts an irish whip, but Yoshida reverses. Banshee ducks and rolls under the knife edge chop attempt, and goes to the opposite ropes. Yoshida turns around, and Banshee nails him with a lariat across the throat. Yoshida goes down hard, again clutching his throat. Banshee drags Yoshida to his feet, scoops him up, turns him over, and drops down into a tombstone piledriver. He makes a cover, and Goto drops to count. Yoshida somehow kicks out at two and three quarters! The crowd "ooohs", then applauds Yoshida's fortitude. Banshee sits up in disbelief, and questions the count. Banshee again drags Yoshida to his feet, and chops him across his chest. The crowd "whooos" as Yoshida fires back with a kick to the gut, causing Banshee to double over. Yoshida drives an elbow to the back of Banshee's head, and he drops to one knee. Yoshida applies a front facelock, and drags Banshee to his feet. He starts to lift Banshee up for a suplex, but Banshee shakes his legs, and drops back down. He shifts his weight, and lifts Yoshida up in the air...] ER: Great reversal by Banshee. What is going to do? AO: Right now, he's letting all the blood rush to Yoshida's head! ER: Banshee turns to face us.....SCREWDRIVER!!! [crowd "oooohs"] That has to be it! Banshee rolls on top of Yoshida, and grapevine's the leg... ...1... ...2... ...3... **ding**ding**ding** Nishi: The winner of this match, and advancing to the finals... THE BANSHEE!!! [crowd applauds] AO: The Banshee has restored some of his honor tonight! Now if he can defeat Kagemusha, he will have turned some momentum back towards Chiimu Tora! ER: I think he can do it. Even though Kagemusha defeated Fujiwara, his heart doesn't look fully into wrestling tonight. Something is wrong with him, but what could it be? [Banshee climb up onto a corner turnbuckle and issue his trademark warcry, celebrating the win with the fans, while Susan K'uang-yin helps Yoshida out of the ring. Yoshida is holding the back of his head, and allows Susan to lead him to the back.] ER: Next up Akira, we feature a first round match in the Frank A. Mareno Memorial Tag-team tournament. AO: Hai. I greatly enjoy the art of team wrestling. As a former champion I know what it is to rely and count on a partner, a partner who is counting on you as well. ER: Tonight we shall see the Heavenly Express taking on the Suicide Machines. AO: Heavenly Express? Suicide Machines? I don't see any need to bring in even more outsiders to SJPW. We have a wealth of suitable tag-team talent. This must be that baka Yamashita's doing. ER: Come now Akira, the Marano tournament has always featured talent from the SJPW and other federations. It provides a stage for some of the world's best teams to prove their skills. This isn't some conspriacy by Yamashita. AO:..... ___ ___ _______________________________________________ / _ \__ / / | /| / /Frank A. Marano Jr. Memorial Tournament, RD1/ / ___/ // /| |/ |/ / The Heavenly Express vs. The Suicide Machines /_/ \___/ |__/|__/____________________________________________/ [The camera cuts to a shot of Nishi Toju standing in the ring with his microphone in hand.] Nishi: This contest is a first round match of the Frank A. Marano Memorial Tag Team Tournament and is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Vero Beach, Florida... weighing in at a combined 480 pounds..."BEAUTIFUL" BRIAN FALCONE AND "SENSATIONAL" STEVE BRANDI...THE HEAVENLY EXPRESS!! ["Limelight" by Rush plays and the fans give polite applause as Brandi and Falcone walk through the entrance. Falcone has dark blond hair, short on top and the sides, but shoulder length in back. He wears long blue tights with a red star on the rear with a red, yellow and black rainbow extending from it down the leg, a tight fitting black ring jacket with silver sleeves and "Beautiful Brian" on the back in gold letters. Brandi sports short brown hair and blue trunks with a similar star and rainbow design as his partner as well as matching jacket although his naturally has "Sensational Steve" on the back. Falcone looks to be all business, but as Brandi gets on the apron, he smiles and does a hip swivel that draws a few isolated cheers and general laughter from the fans.] ER: The Heavenly Express, one of the most enduring tag-teams around. They have teamed since '91 and they will hold the clear experience advantage over there opponents. AO: Experience is very important. Even against greater skill, greater wisdom will often win out in the end. Nishi: Their opponents... from Brussels, Belgium and Palmetto Beach, Florida USA repectively ... weighing in at a combined 482 pounds... KING AND "NEMESIS" STEVE CONNER...THE SUICIDE MACHINES!! [The fans once again give polite applause as "Sweet Dreams" by Eurythmics plays. King emerges first, followed closely by Conner. King is a well built black man wearing a black mask with white mesh eye holes, white boots, and long black tights with "SUICIDE" airbrushed in red on the right leg. Conner has long curly dirty blonde hair with a very muscular build. He wears long black and blue tights with "SUICIDAL" airbrushed in red on the side and white boots. His eyes burn with intensity as he quickly stalks down to the ring. King walks behind him in slow measured steps.] ER: The Suicide Machines are, as their name would apply, a team willing to take risks and do damage to themselves as long as they dole out even more punishment to their opponents. AO: Such a foolish strategy. One should deal all the damage to one's opponent while protecting yourself. ER: I guess that is true, but sometimes the best defense is a good offense. [Referee Owada Botan gives some last minute instructions and then orders one man from each team out of the ring. King exits for the Machines while Brandi steps out for the Express. Owada calls for the bell, and Conner comes charging across the ring and catches a slightly suprised Falcone with a wicked lariat. Falcone rolls to his feet, and Conner is quick to catch him with a elbow to the head, and then three lightning quick chops that send Falcone sprawling back to the corner.] ER: Conner wasting no time in this one, and he is really taking the fight to Falcone. AO: I like his aggressive nature, but he must not be blinded by it. [Conner fires in with a European Uppercut that rocks Falcone's head back, and then Conner quickly drives in rapid fire kicks to the midsection that send Falcone down to mat in the corner. Owada backs Conner off as Falcone struggles to his feet, gasping for breath. Conner doesn't oblige him and rushes in, but gets caught with a quick and devastating uppercut that rocks him back a few steps.] ER: Falcone rushes out and takes Conner down with a clothesline. Conner is back to his feet quickly however. AO: Falcone should manuver Conner to his corner and utilize his partner. [Falcone lands a couple of punches and then drives a knee to the midsection before pushing Conner back to the ropes and then whipping him to the farside. Conner comes off and Falcone catches him with a knee to the midsection that sends him flipping over to the mat. Falcone follows up with a kneedrop across the throat.] ER: Falcone now in control and he tags in Brandi. Conner up to his feet and the Express with a double whip...Conner comes off...Double-Team Flapjack. They send Conner high into the air, and then crashing down face first to the mat. AO: An effective double-team manuver. They should string together several of those, and then go for the kill. [Conner gets to his feet, and Brandi measures him and then fires in with snap kicks to the body and then a quick backfist that staggers Conner to the ropes. Brandi with an irish whip and as Conner comes off, he attempts a hiptoss, but the powerful Conner blocks it.] ER: Crushing clothesline by Conner sends both men to the mat. Conner now up, and he stomps Brandi across the face, and tags in King. AO: Now we shall see the team work of these two. [Conner pulls Brandi up and he and King double-whip him to the ropes. As Brandi hits the farside ropes and comes off, Conner whips King towards him and King virtually explodes into Brandi with a dropkick to the face. The fans applaud as King rolls to his feet and runs off the ropes.] ER: Rolling Splash by King and he has a cover. ...1... AO: Brandi easily able to kick-out. Some inexperience showing there perhaps. It is too early to attempt the pin. ER: King now pulls Brandi up...Brandi an knee to the midsection and a quick snapmare..OH! Stiff kick to King's back.[fans "ohhhh"] [King quickly rolls to his feet, however, totally seemingly oblivious to the pain and fires in with a barrage of kicks, elbows and European Uppercuts that has Brandi whirling away, and into his corner. King stands back, waiting as Brandi tags in Falcone.] AO: He let Brandi into his corner, another mistake by King. [Falcone comes in, and he and King circle each other for a moment, and then lock-up. Falcone quickly controls with a side-headlock, but King immediately pushes Falcone back to the ropes and then shoots him off. Falcone comes bouncing back, and King catches him with a spinning heel kick. King quickly stands and executes a Standing Moonsault that earns applause from the fans, but only a two count on Falcone.] ER: King looking over to his partner, and Conner nods. King pulls Falcone up...Small Package! ...1... ...2... ER: Kickout by King. [crowd applauds] AO: Ah, but look, when Falcone took him in the small package, he rolled him to his corner, and now he is able to make the tag. Impressive. [Brandi fires in and connects with stomps to the body and a kick to the head, but King quickly rises. Brandi catches King with a knee to the midsection, and then hits a Snap Suplex. Brandi rises, and sets himself between King and his corner, cutting off the ring. He pulls King up and tags in Falcone.] ER: Brandi with an irish whip as Falcone comes in. King comes off the ropes and Brandi catches him with dropped toe-hold and Falcone follows it up with a elbowdrop to the back of the head. More solid team work from the Heavenly Express. AO: Hai. They have isolated King, and now they are picking him apart. [Falcone pulls King up and scoops him up and slams him to the mat. Falcone quickly climbs to the second rope, and leaps off connneting with a fist drop on the downed King. Falcone covers, but Conner is in to break things up before a one count is even registered.] ER: Conner showing some frustration and now Falcone motioning for Conner to come into the ring, and Conner charges in, only to be intercepted by Owada. AO: Conner falling for the taunt, and now he leaves his partner open to the double-team. [Brandi in, and Falcone pulls King up and the Express hit a Double-team Hotshot on King as Owada attempts to get Conner out of the ring. Brandi then lifts Falcone up, as if to Atomic Drop him, but instead drives him down with an assited legdrop onto King. Brandi then slaps his hands together to imitate the sounds of a tag as Falcone exits. Owada turns around, and asks about the tag, and Brandi smiles and nods as he pulls King up. Brandi scoops King up and drives him down across his knee with a backbreaker, and then tags in Falcone.] ER: Falcone climbing to the top, and now Brandi over to the corner, and he throws Falcone off...Rocket Launcher!! AO: Falcone has the cover... ...1... ...2... ER: DIVING HEADBUTT BY CONNER!![fans "ohhh"] AO: Conner making the save with a diving headbutt, and now Brandi in the ring, but Conner levels him with a lariat. Falcone up, and Conner kicks him in the midsection, and shoots him into the ropes. ER: Military Press by Conner showing his power. [Brandi gets to his feet, and Conner throws Falcone into him flattening both members of the Express and the fans cheer a bit. King gets to his feet and Conner calls out to him. King nods and he grabs Falcone's legs and as Conner comes off the ropes, slingshots Falcone up and into a Conner lariat. Conner continues and runs off the opposite side as King leverages Falcone back to his feet, and Conner catches the dazed Falcone with a bulldog and the fans applaud the innovative move.] ER: The Suicide Slingshot performed to perfection, and Owada has lost control of this one. AO: Hai. Conner and King now have Brandi up and Conner hits a bodyslam on Brandi. ER: King out to the apron, as Conner runs off the ropes...Rolling Splash by Conner...Slingshot Legdrop by King...The Suicide Lightning! [The fans cheer the exicting moves off the Suicide Machines and Conner rolls to his feet, and calls for King to head to the top rope.] ER: King up to the top, and Conner climbs up, it looks like they are setting for the the Suicidal Superplex, where Conner Superplexes King onto their opponent. [Falcone gets to his feet, and seeing the perdicament his partner is in, rushes to the corner and catches Conner with a double-axhandle. Brandi rolls to his feet, and joins Falcone and they hook up and send him over with a Double-Belly to Back Suplex.] ER: The Express back to their feet, but King is still on the top...Missle Dropkick to both Express members!![fans applaud] [King rises, and pulls Brandi up and nails him with an elbow smash that sends him back to the ropes. King quickly follows with a spinning heel kick that takes both men over the top to the floor, earning "ohhhs" and "ahhhs" from the fans. In the ring Falcone gets up slowly, and Conner catches him from behind with a clubbing Enzui-Lariato. Conner pulls Falcone up and sends him across the ring crashing hard into the corner buckles. Conner charges in and catches Falcone with a crushing lariat, and quickly spins around, and grabbing a handful of Falcone's hair, runs out and plants Falcone with a face crusher.] ER: Conner in control now, and he climbs up to the top...Moonsault..Oh! Falcone able to get his knees up and Conner is down, grasping his midsection. AO: On the outside, King is up on the apron as Brandi stands...King with an Asai Moonsault![fans applaud] [Falcone gets up slowly, and backs to the ropes. Conner stands, but is stooped over holding his midsection, and Falcone quickly rushes forward and hits a swinging Neckbreaker that puts Conner down.] ER: Falcone now up and he is heading to the top rope...This could be the Ginsu. [Falcone leaps off and connects with a crushing Flying Legdrop and the fans applaud. Falcone makes the cover.] ...1... ...2... [King suddenly jumps to the apron and comes in with a springboard splash that breaks up the pin.] ER: Great last second save by King! King now pulls Falcone up, and he hooks him...Suicide Buster?! AO: Brandi is in...Thrust kick to the back of King's head! ER: King turns around, and Falcone drops to his hands and knees behind him...Clothesline by Brandi and King falls over Falcone..Double Goozle by the Express. Falcone covers. ...1... ...2... [Conner attempts the save, but Brandi holds him.] ...3... NO!! AO: King able to kick-out. ER: Brandi now stomping away on Conner, and calls for Falcone to head up to the top rope. [Falcone climbs up and Brandi pulls King up. He sets for the Backbreaker to set up their tag-team Ginsu move. He is cut off however by a Conner dropkick to the back of the head. Conner gets to his feet, and charges the corner nailing Falcone with an elbow, and nearly knocking him from his perch, but Falcone manages to hold on. Conner follows up with a couple of quick punches, and then calls out to King who rises to his feet.] ER: King up and he climbs up to the second rope and hooks Falcone. AO: Brandi is up, but Conner catches him with a chop, and now hooks him. ER: King pulls Falcome up and into the air...OH!!! [The fans cheer as King spins around and drops Falcone face-first to the mat with a varation of his Suicide Buster. At the same moment Connner hits his Suicide Driver, a slingshot Jackhammer Suplex, on Brandi.] ER: Huge moves by the Suicide Machines, and they aren't done! [Conner kicks Brandi under the bottom rope, where he lands in a heap. King heads to the top rope yet again as Conner pulls Falcone up. Falcone looks to be in tremendous pain, and is unable to defend himself as Conner mounts him on his shoulders.] ER: Conner has Falcone up...King leaps off and catches Falcone with a Ace Crusher as Conner drives him down...1-900-SUICIDE!! AO: Why must they name every move? It is pointless. ER: Regardless of what it is called it is devastating, and King has the pin. ...1... ...2... ...3.... **ding**ding**ding** Nishi: Your winners advancing in the tournament...THE SUICIDE MACHINES!! ["Sweet Dreams" by Eurythmics plays and the fans cheer having been won over by the Suicide Machine's exciting offense. Neither Conner nor King celebrate the victory and just walk to the back as ring attendants see to Brandi and Falcone in the ring and at ringside.] ER: A very impressive win by the Suicide Machines over the more experienced Heavenly Express. I would have to call that an upset. AO: Hai. The Suicide Machines show promise, but they still have much to learn. It will be interesting to see how they will do in the later rounds of the tournament. ER: Indeed Akira, indeed. [The Suicide Machines head towards the back, celebrating with the fans. Brandi helps Falcone to his feet, then helps him out of the ring. They head to the back as well, with polite applause from the crowd. Suddenly, Tetsuo Matsumoto appears at the runway entrance, still street clothes and storms to the ring] Akira Ono: [suprised] Tetsuo? He's not even supposed to wrestle tonight... [Tetsuo grabs a mic as he climbs in the ring. His face i livid] Tetsuo: Keeping me off TV again Yamashita? Afraid of what I have to say? YOU COWARD!! [pauses for a moment] You want to strand me in Koubousen Division? FINE!! But at least give me someone to wrestle! Don't make me sit on the sidelines like some second string quarterback... I'LL WRESTLE WHOEVER YOU WANT!! I'll do it to prove a point... I'll do it if it will get me an All Asian titleshot! Give me that piece of trash Takada.. I'll dismantle him and show him REAL puroresu!! Just give me someone and your gaurantee that if I beat him that I'll get my shot!! Akira Ono: [stunned] N-nani? Eddie did you here that? Eddie Rose: Tetsuo Matsumoto is obviously upset!! He wants anyone he can get his hands just so he can get a shot at the All Asian title? This makes no sene!! Tetsuo HATES the Kouboutsen Geijutsu Division and probably hates the champ Toshiaki "The Pyro" Takada even more!! I mean this is the type of wrestling Tetsuo Matsumoto detests yet he's demanding matches... I don't get it!! Tetsuo: I'll give you 1 week Yamashita... 1 week or I'm coming after you!! No threats in the ring, no officials, just me letting my frustrations out on you when you least expect it!! I AM NOT A DOG SO STOP TREATING ME LIKE ONE!! [Matsumoto drops the microphone, and walks backstage to the cheers of the fans]
