[The crowd starts to suprisingly cheer. Ace turns to the them with a look of pure disgust.] Ace Kilcannon: "SHUT THE F<beep>K UP!! I don't need your damn applause or cheers! I'm not doing it for your stupid nips, I'm doing it for myself and this sport! Not only am I the epitome of what a champion is, but Dean is a disgrace! Ed, you know what...I'm not just out for one thing anymore, mother f<beep>er. Not only am I going to take that belt from you...but now...now I'm going to make you my bitch. You better realize, too, that when I set out for something...I sure as f<beep>k do it, bitch-boy. Heh...just ask Rupan Loco." [Ace reaches into his back pocket and takes out the mask he pulled off of Rupan Loco.] Ace Kilcannon: "Dean, this is the trophy of my first victim. The trophy I take from your hide is going to be the SJPW All-Asian title. Prepare to be AAACCEEDDD!!!" He puts on his headset, and smiles at the camera...] Eddie Rose: Hello Everyone. I'm Eddie Rose, and this is Akira Ono. As you saw at the top of the program, Ace Kilcannon is on a mission, and that mission is to topple Ed Dean. Akira Ono: He needs to get in line! Yamashita has a full plate of opponents for Dean all ready. Kilcannon isn't one of them. Besides, he will have his hands full dealing with Rupan Loco! ER: Very true. Rupan Loco wants to avenge his countout loss at Kilcannon's hands. Still, give Ace credit; he's going after the man everyone wants to see take a fall. AO: If he didn't disrespect the fans so much, he could be the main man around here. His loss! ER: We have a great show for you tonight folks. We have the last three first round matches in the Frank A. Marano tag tournament, which will crown the next PJW tag team champions. We also have the PJW Grand Prix mini-tournament, which should be very exciting! AO: My yen is on the Banshee to win this tournament. ER: Why is that? AO: Becuase he is Chiimu Tora. ER: Speaking of Chiimu Tora, young Jet Jaguar II has a chance to restore honor to Chiimu Tora, and defeat Dean for the title. AO: As much as I want Dean to lose, I feel Jaguar II is going in this match injured, and that gives Dean the edge. I hate to say it, but Dean will prevail. ER: That's a shock coming from you Akira. AO: I know, but Jaguar II was hurt last week. He was out with a back injury because of what The Ronin, and later Tasogare no Prince, did to him. Dean may have aggravated the injury, so Jet is not 100%. ER: I'd have to agree. Well, we're ready to start the mini-tournament, so let's go to ringside... ___ ___ _______________________________________________ / _ \__ / / | /| / /PJW Grand Prix Title Mini-tournament, RD 1: / / ___/ // /| |/ |/ / Kagemusha vs Shishi "Lion" Fujiwara /_/ \___/ |__/|__/____________________________________________/ [The camera cuts to Nishi Toju standing in the ring with Goto Kyoji. He waits patiently for the crowd to die down, then raises his microphone to speak...] Nishi: This first contest is a first round match in the PJW Grand Prix championship! [crowd cheers] Introducing first.... ["Seasons In The Abyss" by Slayer blares over the PA] Nishi: Hailing from Osaka, and weighing in at 229 pounds, representing THE DAIMYO, he is.... KA-GE-MU-SHA!!!! [The Osaka crowd gives Kagemusha a standing ovation as the lights go out, and a red spotlight focuses on the runway entrance. Kagemusha steps out, his face painted red with the Kanji symbols for "Akuma" written in White on his face. He looks out at the crowd, his face covered by his hair. He pushes his hair back, revealing sadness in his eyes. He sighs for a moment, then slowly walks to the ring. The crowd's applause gets surprisingly louder, and Kagemusha stops, and looks around. He continues to the ring, and the spotlight goes out, and the house lights come back on. Kagemusha stands up in each corner, and spits green mist into the air, the crowd applauding him each time he does so. He goes to his corner, and closes his eyes in silent meditation...] ER: Kagemusha being well recieved by the fans in Osaka tonight! AO: This is where he is from. They welcome their neighbor. Nishi: His opponent.... ["Even Flow" by Pearl Jam blares over the PA] Nish: Also hailing from Osaka, representing CHIIMU TORA, and weighing in at 202 pounds, he is SHI-SHI "LION" FU-JI-WA-RA!!! [The lights go out again, and a red spotlight focuses on the runway entrance. Fujiwara steps out, wearing the exact same outfit as Kagemusha! The crowd "ooohs" as they realize Fujiwara is also wearing red facepaint with the Kanji symbols for "Akuma" on his face. Fujiwara takes in the crowd for a moment, then heads to the ring. As he enters the ring, the spotlight goes off, and the house lights return. Fujiwara then goes to each corner, and spits green mist into the air. Kagemusha looks at Fujiwara with disgust on his face, insulted by this imitation of his ritual...] ER: Fujiwara looks exactly like Kagemusha? What is going on? AO: He's insulting Kagemusha by wearing his ring attire and facepaint! This is a serious loss of face! Kagemusha must win now, or he will be dishonored! [Goto calls for the bell, and Kagemusha charges Fujiwara, who catches him with a knee to the gut. Kagemusha doubles over, and Fujiwara hits a gutwrench suplex. He gets to his feet, and snaps an elbowdrop across Kage's chest. Kage sits up in pain, and Fujiwara lands a stiff kick to his back! The crowd "oooohs" as Fujiwara drives his knee into the back of Kage's neck, then applies a surfboard. Kage's face contorts with pain, but he shakes his head when Goto asks if he wants to submit...] ER: Fujiwara starting off with a strong offense. He seems to be angry at Kagemusha! AO: Fujiwara is mad at the world right now! He lost to Ed Dean a few weeks ago, so he is angry! He's going to take it out on Kagemusha! ER: Fujiwara now putting his foot on the small of Kagemusha's back as he pulls back on the surfboard. Kagemusha is in serious pain! AO: And he has nowhere to go! [Fujiwara continues to pull back on the surfboard, but Kage refuses to submit. Fujiwara breaks the hold, and with a look of disgust on his face, drags Kage to his feet. He applies a pumphandle, then hits a fallaway slam. The crowd "ooohs" as Kage hits the mat hard. Fujiwara gets to his feet, and runs towards Kage. He leaps for an elbowdrop, but Kage rolls out of the way! The crowd applauds as Kage rolls to his feet, comes off the ropes, and catches Fujiwara with a spinwheel kick. Fujiwara staggers back, and Kage grabs him, and whips him to the ropes. Fujiwara bounces off, and Kage catches him with a kick to the midsection. Fujiwara doubles over, and Kage grabs him, and hits a gutwrench powerbomb. Kage drags Fujiwara back to his feet, slaps on a full nelson, then hits a release Dragon Suplex. Fujiwara lands face first on the mat, and starts to rise, while Kage runs to the ropes. As Fujiwara makes it to his feet, Kage springboards off the ropes into a rana, sending Fujiwara sailing across the ring. Fujiwara rolls to his feet, and as Kage charges him, Fujiwara catches him, and takes him over with an overhead belly to belly suplex! The crowd applauds, while Fujiwara leans on the ropes, catching his breath...] ER: Fast paced action from both men! This is an intense matchup, moreso than their past encounters! AO: Fujiwara is fighting like a man possessed! Has his hatred of Kagemusha become so consuming that he has lost his way? ER: Kagemusha starts to rise, and Fujiwara comes off the ropes, and catches him with a clothesline. He drags Kagemusha up, then takes him over with a fireman's carry. AO: Fujiwara drags Kagemusha back to his feet. He grabs him...Exploder Suplex! [crowd "oooohs"] [Kage lands hard on his head, and lays on the mat. Fujiwara runs to the ropes, and hits a quebrada on Kage's back. He drags Kage to his feet, locks his arms behind him, and hits a release tiger suplex. Fujiwara rolls to his feet, and raises his arm over his head. The crowd gives him a mixed pop, and he gets angry at their lack of support. He runs over to Kage, and starts to put the boots to him, causing more fans to boo him. Fujiwara drags Kage to his feet, and whips him to the ropes. He bounces off the opposite side, and catches Kage with a cross bodyblock. He hooks the leg, and Goto drops to count. He reaches two before Kage kicks out. The crowd applauds, and a "Kagemusha" chant starts up in the audience. Fujiwara gets angry at this, and drags Kagmusha up, then spiking his head with a DDT! The crowd "ooohs" as Fujiwara covers once more, but Kage kicks out at two and a half! The crowd applauds again, and the "Kagemusha" chants get louder....] ER: The crowd is starting to get behind Kagemusha! This is weird! AO: I agree! Fujiwara is Chiimu Tora; why are they rooting for a Daimyo member? ER: Fujiwara is really ticked off now. He drags Kagmusha to his feet, and starts to lay into him with his fist! Kagemusha staggers back from this assualt, until he's back into a corner. Fujiwara continues to pummel him, ignoring Goto's warning to stop! [crowd "ooohs"] AO: Fujiwara now backing off, but he charges in...Knee to the face by Kagemusha! [crowd cheers] Kagemusha sits up on the top rope, and applies a front facelock...TORNADO DDT!!! [crowd "oooohs", then applauds] [Kagemusha wearily makes it to his feet, his facepaint now mixed with blood from a cut on his forehead. He looks down at Fujiwara with his sad eyes, then drags him to his feet. He hits another DDT, then runs to the ropes, and hits a snap legdrop to the back of Fujiwara's head. He rolls Fujiwara over, and covers. Goto drops to count, and Fujiwara kicks out at one and a quarter. The crowd applauds, and Kage drags Fujiwara to his feet. He sticks Fujiwara's head between his legs, then lifts him up in the air, and runs across the ring. He drops down, spiking Fujiwara to the mat with a running Liger Bomb! Goto drops to count, but Fujiwara kicks out at two. Kage gets to his feet, and runs to the corner. He stepladders to the top rope, then backflips off into a moonsault. He impacts hard on Fujiwara, then grapevines the leg. Goto drops to count, but Fujiwara kicks out at two and a half! The crowd applauds, and Kage slaps his hand on the mat in frustration....] ER: Kagemusha trying hard to put Fujiwara away, but he's too hard to defeat! AO: He needs to wear him down more! ER: Kagemusha now dragging Fujiwara to his feet...thumb to the eye by Fujiwara! [crowd booos] Goto warning him as Kagemusha staggers around blinded...Fujiwara scoops Kagemusha up, then hits a Michinoku Driver! [crowd "oooohs"] AO: Fujiwara back up, and he rolls Kagemusha over...LIONPLEX!!! [The crowd "ooohs" loudly as Fujiwara hits his version of the wheelbarrow suplex. Kage's head hits the mat hard. Fujiwara rolls Kage over, and hits a second Lionplex, grinning malevolently as he does so. He drags Kage to his feet, and starts to apply a front facelock, but Kage drives his knee into Fujiwara's gut. He drives his knee in a second time, and Fujiwara sags. Kage applies a front facelock of his own, grabs Fujiwara's leg, and hits a Fisherman's buster! The crowd "ooohs", then applauds loudly. Both men lay on the mat, unmoving, and Goto starts his count on both men...] ER: Quick thinking by Kagemusha saved him from defeat! AO: He can still lose if he doesn't make the count! [Goto continues to count, while both men struggle to sit up. Fujiwara sits up first, while Kagemusha rolls to the ropes. Fujiwara turns over, using his hands to help push himself up, while Kagemusha uses the ropes to pull himself up. Both men make it to their feet by 12, and Goto signals for the match to continue. Fujiwara charges Kagemusha, who catches him with a superkick to the jaw! Fujwiara staggers back, and Kagemusha bolts from the ropes, and lands a second superkick! Fujiwara goes down hard, and Kagemusha spits mist into the air, illiciting cheers from the crowd. He drags Fujiwara to his feet, then hits a swinging neckbreaker. He makes a cover, but Fujiwara kicks out at two and a half. Kagemusha looks dissapointed, then gets to his feet, dragging Fujiwara with him. Fujiwara drives his head into Kage's midsection, causing him to double over. Fujiwara drives his head into his midsection once more, gets to his feet, hoists Kage up onto his shoulders, then hits a Death Valley Driver! The crowd "oooohs" loudly as Kage's head spikes into the mat. Fujiwara wearily makes a cover, but Kage somehow kicks out at two and three quarters! The crowd applauds, and Fujiwara asks the referee if he got him or not. Fujiwara shakes his head in disbelief, then starts to get to his feet...] ER: Both men are tired now. This match has been very physical! AO: The next few moves will decide this match; I can feel it in my gut. ER: Fujiwara drags Kagemusha to his feet, and whips him to the ropes. He hoists Kage up on his shoulders again...Eye rake by Kagemusha! [crowd "oooohs"] That was a desperation move! AO: Kagemusha now dropping behind Fujiwara...he grabs him in a waistlock...release German Suplex! [crowd "oooohs"] ER: Kagemusha goes to the corner, and goes to the top rope. He steadies himself....Guillotine Legdrop! [crowd "ooooohs"] AO: Kagemusha now sitting Fujiwara up. He grabs the arm...La Magistral Cradle! Goto drops to count... ...1... ...2... ...3... **ding**ding**ding** Nishi: The winner of this match, and advancing to the finals... KAGEMUSHA!!! [crowd applauds] ER: Kagemusha pulling off an upset of Shishi Fujiwara tonight! This rivalry keeps getting more intense as time goes by! AO: Kagemusha survived, and now must face the winner of our next match. [Kagemusha helps Fujiwara to his feet, and checks to see if he is ok. Fujiwara shoves him off, and raises his fists, expecting treachery. Kagemusha's eyes take on their sad look again, and he holds his head down to the mat. He then raises his head, his eyes unemotional. He extends his hand to Fujiwara, who looks at him suspiciously. Fujiwara then looks at the crowd, who urge him to shake hands. Fujiwara looks down at Kagemusha's hand, then turns around, and exits the ring. The crowd gives him a mixed pop as he heads towards the back...] ER: Kagemusha offering his hand in friendship, and Fujiwara refuses to shake it! AO: I can't blame him. Remember what Kagemusha did to him the last time they faced off! ER: Our next match should be a doozie, as Banshee, still reeling from his loss to Ed Dean last week, takes on Chandrasekhar Yoshida, a man with a VERY big chip on his shoulder! AO: Yoshida was once a great talent, when he and his cousin were the Genoysha. Now, he has become some honorless cur. I hope Banshee defeats him, so that some honor is brought back to Chiimu Tora! ___ ___ _______________________________________________ / _ \__ / / | /| / /PJW Grand Prix Title Mini-tournament, RD 1: / / ___/ // /| |/ |/ / The Banshee vs Chandrasekhar Yoshida /_/ \___/ |__/|__/____________________________________________/ [The camera cuts to Nishi Toju standing in the ring with Goto Kyoji. He waits patiently for the crowd to die down, then raises his microphone to speak...] Nishi: This first contest is a first round match in the PJW Grand Prix championship! [crowd cheers] Introducing first.... [Traditional Japanese folk music pipes over the pa...] Nishi: Making his home in San Francisco, California, accompained to the ring by Susan K'uang-yin, and weighing in at 209 pounds... CHAN-DRASE-KHAR YO-SHI-DA!!!! [Yoshida steps out from the back, Susan K'uang-yin behind him. Yoshida is dressed in his blue jeans and black t-shirt, with his camouflage vest with the green Katakana "5" (Japanese for Warlord), and his samurai swords over his back. Susan is wearing a black kimono, with the Golden Orchid Society insignia on the back. Yoshida strides to the ring, Susan trying her best to keep up. Yoshida enters the ring, and hands his swords to the ring attendant, then holds the ropes for Susan to exit the ring.] Nishi: His opponent... ["Fly" by Sugar Ray comes over the PA] Nishi: Representing Chiimu Tora, and weighing 219 pounds....THE BAN-SHEE!!!! [The arena lights dim and the crowd cheers as a pair of green-tinted spotlights begin waving back and forth across the audience. Suddenly, small green fountains of sparks detonate from each corner ringpost with a loud *BANG* and a thick cloud of green smoke billows out from underneath the ring to engulf it completely.] AO: What's going on? I can't see anything! ER: Look!! [As the thick cloud of smoke enveloping the ring begins to dissipate, a slender figure can be seen standing on top of one of the corner ringposts. The fans cheer loudly as he tilts his head back and cups his hands around his mouth, issuing an ululating warcry before performing a front somersault off the turnbuckle to land in the middle of the ring.] ER: Impressive entrance! AO: I'll say! [Goto signals for the bell, and both men move to lock up in the center of the ring. Yoshida maneuvers behind Banshee, but Banshee reverses. Yoshida hits a back elbow, then snap mare's Banshee to the mat. Yoshida sits Banshee up, then drives a knee to the back of his neck. Yoshida stands Banshee up, and lands a knife-edge chop to his throat. Banshee staggers back, clutching his throat, and Yoshida charges, landing a high knee lift that sends Banshee to the mat. Yoshida stands Banshee up, and drives a knee into his gut. Yoshida grabs The Banshee's arm and attempts to send him into the corner with an Irish whip, but The Banshee reverses it! Yoshida goes crashing into the turnbuckle, and Banshee charges in, leaps up and catapults Yoshida out of the corner with a monkey flip. As Yoshida climbs to his feet, Banshee charges him once more, leaping up, and nailing him with a hurricanrana that flips him over onto the top of his head!] ER: Another fast-paced match! These two men have a past in PJW, don't they? AO: Not really. However, Banshee is allied with Shoji Suzuki in G-Pro, and Yoshida has berated Suzuki often in his recent tirades. ER: Banshee stands Yoshida, scoops him up, then slams him to the mat. He snaps an elbowdrop onto his chest, then makes a cover... ...1... ...2... AO: Kickout! [crowd applauds] Banshee kip-ups to his feet, and sets himself. Yoshida staggers to his feet...JUMPING SIDE KICK! [crowd "ooohs"] Yoshida might all ready be beaten! ER: Banshee stands Yoshida up...SLEEPER!!! [crowd "ooohs"] Yoshida tries to grab the ropes, but he's too far from them....JAWBREAKER by Yoshida! [crowd "oooohs"] [Yoshida sits up, and shakes his head. He stands up, then leans on the ropes. Banshee sits up, and Yoshida flips over him, snapping his neck forward! Banshee falls back to the mat, while Yoshida rolls to his feet, and smiles. Yoshida walks over to Banshee, and drags him to his feet. He sets him up, then takes him over with a swinging neckbreaker. Yoshida sits Banshee up, and applies a headscissors submission. He drops back to the mat, and Banshee tries to wiggle out of the hold. Goto asks him if he wants to submit, but Banshee says 'no'. Yoshida clamps down on the hold, and Banshee flops on the mat in pain. His arms flail about wildly, and somehow grab the ropes. Goto orders the break, but Yoshida keeps the hold on. Goto puts a count on him, and Yoshida breaks at four. Goto makes Yoshida back off, while Banshee sits up, holding his neck. Banshee grabs the ropes, and uses them to get to his feet. Goto asks him if he can continue, and he says 'yes'. Goto tells them to continue, and both men start to circle each other...] ER: Banshee took some punishment on his neck, but Goto's actions broke the momentum! AO: Goto did the right thing though. However, I must admit, Yoshida is wrestling fairer than I expected.
