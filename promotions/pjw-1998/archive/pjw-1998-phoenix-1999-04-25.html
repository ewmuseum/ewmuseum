PJW — Card

Phoenix

25 April 1999

[The show opens with a shot from floor level from a camera looking straight back at the entranceway which is a door covered by a black curtain bearing the PJW logo which has a split in the middle. "The Boys are Back in Town" begins to play over the PA. The Tokyo crowd seems somewhat confused, but are so enthusiastic that they loudly clap in anyway. After a few seconds PJW broadcasters Christopher Reece & Riki Togo emerge from the entranceway to heavy cheers and applause. Togo wears a lime green suit while Reece wears black slacks and a white dress shirt with a colorful tie and black suspenders. Togo is an older Japanese man with a slight beard and thick black hair which is combed to the side. Reece is a young American man with a healthy tan and black spiked hair. As Reece and Togo make there way to the ring greeting fans the shot fades back to black.] [Slowly the Pro Japan Wrestling logo fades onto the screen. The screen then returns to black as the words "The Rebirth" appear on the screen in whites letters. The shot then cuts to footage from a press conference. Hadi Moditian is standing behind a podium, infront of the PJW banner. Seated in front of Moditian are several rows of reporters and photographers, as he speaks flashbulbs go off...] Hadi Moditian: I've called you all here today to announce that I will be re-forming the Pro Japan Wrestling office. Since the closure of PJW I've tried my hand at several other aspects of pro wrestling, from managing to promoting the strong style, but today I am proud to announce that I am returning to my roots. Pro Japan Wrestling is where my heart lies. [The shot fades to black and the words "The Card" then appear on the screen as the shot cuts to footage from a Samurai TV Newscast. A reporter sits behind a plain black desk bearing the Samurai TV logo on the front...] Reporter: Pro Japan Wrestling promoter Hadi Moditian announced the lineup for PJW's return card, Phoenix, today outside of Tokyo's Korkuen Hall. [Silent footage of Moditian meeting with the press outside of Korkuen Hall as the reporter continues speaking...] Reporter: The card features a 4 team tournament for the PJW All-Asian 6-Man tag titles. First round matches will see Zokugun Sangai face El Tigre Guerrera, Super Dragon, & Tommy Kelekona, and Shoji Suzuki, Toru Nakano, & Mascara del Muerte take on John St. Vincent, the Golden Chimera, & Wildstar. [The screen returns to black as an abbreviated remix of "If I Ruled the World" by Nas featuring Lauryn Hill plays in the background. Slowly group photos of each trio team slide into view from each of the 4 corners of the screen: TORA Wanizame, Takuya Nobu, & Queero Bakemono from the upper-right corner; El Tigre Guerrera, Super Dragon, & Tommy Kelekona from the bottom-right corner; Shoji Suzuki, Mascara del Muerte, & Toru Nakano from the bottom-left corner; and John St. Vincent, The Golden Chimera, & Wildstar from the upper-left corner. Kanji characters appear in white letters with deep blue outlines at the bottom of the drawing reading "Experience Excellence... AGAIN!". As the Phoenix logo is completed on the screen. The ropes are joined in each corner by white turnbuckles which are fastened to red ring posts by black steel brackets. A black apron surrounds the sides of the ring. In the center of each side are the letters PJW in white letters with red trim. The ring is surrounded by black guardrail to separate the fans from the ringside area. The floor in the ringside area is covered in thin black mats. Three tables are located just behind the guard railing. One table is recognizable as the announcer's position. RT: My favorites to take the tournament are already off to a strong start! Their is no doubt in my mind that the team-work and showmanship of Zokugun Sangai will prevail tonight. They're the team with the most experince tagging together. Hell they've proven themselves to be an institution in purorseu and they've already began there legacy in PJW with there first round win. CR: In our other first round match-up Shoji Suzuki made a successful return to PJW leading his teammates Toru Nakano and Mascara del Muerte to victory over John St. Vincent, Wildstar, & the Golden Chimera in a highly contested and heated encounter. RT: That team relly impressed me. And you forgot the most important part! That really hammered Suzuki's neck. Theres no way hes anywhere near 100% after the beating he recieved at their hands! Hes hurting I'm sure, but so is every other man in the finals, they've all took their share of lumps in the first round. Look at Suzuki's teammates.... Their's Muerte, the american journyman, has been waiting 23 years for the break hes always deserved but never gotten. Hes came here tonight in search of the fame hes earned. And then their's the youngster, Toru Nakano. Hes dreamed his entire life of being a purorseu star, and tonight hes taking the first steps towards fufilling his dream. CR: Fans will be back with the match following this commercial break!
