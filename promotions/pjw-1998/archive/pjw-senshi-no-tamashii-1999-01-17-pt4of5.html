[Senshi no Tamashii continues...] ER: We're back everyone, and we've got three matches to go! Concluding the first round of the Marano tournament, we have The Dynamite Express taking on The Professionals. AO: The Professionals are former Wild West Wrestling tag team champions, and an excellant team. Introducing first... ["Dynamite" by Jermain Jackson pipes over the PA] Nishi: At a combined weight of 475 pounds...RANDY FULLER...RICKY JACKSON....THE DYNAMITE EXPRESS!!! [The lights go out in the arena. A loud hissing sound is heard for about five seconds. Then, a loud explosion rocks the arena as fireworks go off above the ring. The lights come back on and Fuller and Jackso walk down the aisle. They are wearing red and gold ring jackets over their ring attire. they slap hands with the fans in the aisleway. They hit the ring and vault over the top rope. They then remove their jackets and prepare for the match] Nishi: Their opponents... ["Bad To The Bone" by George Thorogood & The Destroyers pipes over the PA] Nishi: At a combined weight of 533 pounds..."POWER" PETE JACKSON..."DANGEROUS" DANNY JACKSON...THE PROFESSIONALS!!! [Spotlights hit the ring and the aisle as the music starts up and the Professionals appear from the curtains, then walk to the ring. While Danny looks all business, Pete occasionally stops to pose and toss insults at the fans, calling them fat, out of shape slobs and the like. Once they get to the ring they remove their vest or jacket. Pete will then often do another series of poses while Danny just waits patiently in their corner with that intense look on his face] ER: These two teams have a history together, don't they? AO: My sources tell me that is so. These two teams have an intense rivalry! [Owada Botan gives both teams their instructions, then calls for the bell. Danny Jackson & Randy Fuller start off for their teams. Both men circle each other, then lock up. and Jackson rakes Fuller's face. Fuller staggers back, and Jackson kicks him in the gut, then hits a gutwrench suplex. His sits Fuller up, and applies a rear chinlock, sticking his knee into the small of Fuller's back. Ricky Jackson enters the ring, and kicks Danny in the back of the head, breaking the hold. Pete Jackson enters the ring, and lands a forearm smash on Ricky, who retaliates with a knee to the gut. Pete lands another forearm shot, then grabs Randy by the back of the head, and throws him out of the ring. Botan gets him to go back to his corner, while his partner & Randy Fuller get to their feet. Danny charges for a clothesline, but Fuller ducks under it, and heads to the ropes on the opposite side. Both men come off the ropes, and Fuller catches Danny with a flying forearm smash! The crowd "oooohs" as Danny staggers back, and Fuller rolls forward, and sends Danny to the mat with a clothesline! The crowd applauds as Fuller drags Danny to his feet, then scoops him, spins around, and slams him to the mat. Fuller snaps an elbowdrop onto Danny's chest, then pulls his opponent up. He heads to his corner, and tags in Ricky, who enters the ring as Fuller whips Danny to the ropes. Fuller drops down as Danny comes off the ropes, Ricky catches him, lifts him off the mat, spins around, then slams him to the mat with a spinebuster! The crowd "ooohs" at the sound of the impact...] ER: Some good double teaming from The Dynamite Express there. AO: They are a talented team, are they not? Still, The Professionals are more experienced. ER: Ricky now with a Russian legsweep on Danny. He tags in Fuller, then whips Danny to the ropes...kick to the gut by Ricky...Swinging Neckbreaker by Fuller! [crowd "ooohs"] Cover by Fuller... ...1... ...2... AO: Kickout! [crowd applauds] Fuller wastes no time, dragging Danny to his feet...he hooks him for a suplex...block by Danny... REVERSAL! Danny rolls on top of Fuller, and starts to pummel him! [crowd "ooohs"] [Botan makes Danny get off of Fuller, and he stomps Fuller in the gut as he gets up. He drags Fuller to his feet, calls to his partner, then grabs Fuller by the back of his head. Pete sticks his boot out, and Danny rams Fuller's head into the sole. He tags Pete, who comes into the ring while Danny holds Fuller's arms behind him. Pete starts to lay into him with punches to the gut., and Ricky enters the ring to help his parner. Botan intercepts him, and orders him back to his corner. Meanwhile, The Professionals double team Fuller. Danny Atomic Drops Fuller into a belly to belly suplex by Pete. Ricky argues with Botan about the double teaming, allowing The Professionals time to Spike Piledrive Fuller to the mat! The crowd starts to boo, and Danny gives the crowd the finger. Botan turns around as Pete drags Fuller to his feet, then sends him to the mat with a short-arm clothesline!] ER: Pete Jackson falling on top of Fuller for a cover... ...1... ...2... ...3 AO: Randy Jackson with the save! [crowd applauds] He drags Pete Jackson to his feet, and hits a standing dropkick! Danny Jackson enters the ring, and Randy catches him with a standing dropkick that sends him over the ropes to the floor! [crowd applauds] ER: Ricky has Pete up...kick to the gut doubles Pete over...ROCKER DROPPER by Ricky Jackson! [crowd applauds] AO: Botan makes Ricky head back to his corner, while Fuller gets to his feet. He shakes his head for a moment, then heads over to Pete Jackson. He drags him to his feet, then executes a belly to back suplex. [Fuller drags Pete up, and attempts an irish whip, but Pete reverses. Fuller makes the tag to Ricky, and asPete bends over for a backdrop, but Fuller flips over him. Pete stands up as Ricky heads to the top rope, and Fuller dropkicks him in the back of the head, and as Pete staggers forward, Ricky comes off the top rope with a cross bodyblock! The crowd applauds as Ricky rolls off Pete, and heads to the ropes. Pete sits up, and Ricky dropkicks him in the back of the head! The crowd applauds again, and Ricky legdrops Pete across the chest. He drags Pete to his feet, then sends him to the mat with a russian legsweep...] ER: Ricky Jackson is a very agile man! That cross bodyblock was perfect! AO: I like the dropkicks he executes better. They were flawless! ER: Ricky drags Pete to his feet, and whips him to the ropes... hurri-POWERBOMB BY PETE JACKSON!! [crowd "ooohs"] Pete Jackson is such a powerful man! AO: Pete makes a cover... ...1... ...2... ER: KICKOUT! [crowd applauds] Both men struggle to rise to their feet. Pete's taken alot of punishment, but Ricky had the wind taken out of him by that powerbomb! AO: Both men make it to their feet, and Pete lands a forearm smash to Ricky's back. He starts to go for a scoop slam, but Ricky counters with a small package... ...1... ...2... ...3 ER: Danny Jackson makes the save! [crowd applauds] That was a close call! Danny now putting the boots to Ricky, and Randy Fuller comes in! He nails Danny from behind with a double axe handle! Fuller now grabbing Danny by the back of the head, and throws him out of the ring! Fuller runs to the ropes on the opposite side of the ring...TOPE on Danny Jackson! [crowd "oooohs", then applauds] AO: What a move! That was totally suicidal! [Pete & Ricky get to their feet, and Pete rakes Ricky's face. Ricky staggers back, blinded, and Pete grabs him, and whips him to the ropes. Pete catches Ricky coming of the ropes, scoops him up, spins around, then drops down into a vicious backbreaker! The crowd "ooohs" as Ricky writhes in pain on the mat. Pete stands up, then drops his knee across Ricky's gut. Ricky rolls around, clutching his gut, and Pete stands up, and flexes for the crowd. He then runs over to Ricky, and kicks him in the ribs. He drags Ricky to his feet, while Danny & Fuller head back to their corners. Pete sticks Ricky's head between his legs, and signals its over. He starts to lift Ricky up for a powerbomb, but Ricky shifts his weight mid-air, and drops down, DDT'ing Pete to the mat! The crowd applauds the desperation move, while Botan puts the count to both men...] ER: Ricky pulled that move out of nowhere! I thought he was finished there! AO: That was quick thinking, desperation move or not. Now if he can get to Fuller, they could win this match! [Botan continues to count, and both men sit up, breathing heavily. They both start to rise to their feet, and make it by thirteen. Pete charges for a clothesline, but Ricky catches him with a spinwheel kick. Pete staggers back, and Ricky runs to the ropes, tags Fuller, Ricky attempts a cross bodyblock, but Pete catches him. Pete smiles, but Fuller springs to the top rope, and comes off with a dropkick to Ricky's back, sending Pete to the mat. Pete frantically kicks out, and rolls to his corner, and tags in Danny Jackson. Danny enters the ring, but Fuller catches him with a flying forearm shot! Danny gets caught in the ropes, and Fuller spins him around, grabs him in a reverse facelock, lifts him up, slingshots him off the ropes, then takes him over with an inverted suplex! Fuller drags Danny to his feet, and brings him over to his corner. He tags in Ricky, then grabs Danny, and holds him in a form of bearhug, with Danny hoisted up off the mat. Ricky goes to the top rope, and comes off with a flying clothesline that nearly decapitates Danny Jackson! The crowd "oooohs" loudly, and Pete Jackson comes into the ring. The Dynamite Express catch Pete with a double dropkick that sends Pete into the ropes. They double dropkick Pete again, and he goes over the top rope to the floor. The Express drag Danny to his feet, then take him down with a double russian legsweep. Fuller goes to his corner, and Ricky stands Danny up. He hits a standing dropkick that sends Danny back into the Express's corner. Ricky tags in Fuller, and Danny explodes on Ricky with a knee to his gut. Ricky doubles over, and Danny hammers his back with a double axe handle. Fuller grabs Danny by the head, spins him around, and snaps his neck on the top rope!] ER: Danny Jackson just getting mauled in the ring! He almost broke free, but the Express took him down again! AO: The Express have been more impressive that I was expecting. They may just have this match won! ER: Fuller back in the ring now, and he heads to the top rope. Ricky Jackson drags Danny to his feet, and hoists him up to the top with Fuller. He heads out to the apron, then runs across it, and dives onto Pete Jackson, who is still on the floor! [crowd "ooohs"] AO: Fuller sets Danny's head between his legs....TOP ROPE POWERBOMB!!! He hooks the leg... ...1... ...2... ...3... **ding**ding**ding** Nishi: The winners of this match...THE DYNAMITE EXPRESS!!! [crowd applauds] ER: What a match! The Dynamite Express really shined tonight! AO: I'll say. I was wrong about them. I really thought The Professionals would prevail. [The camera shows the Dynamite Express heading to the back, while Pete Jackson helps Danny Jackson from the ring. The camera cuts to Jonnie Adonis stands by the side of the entrance area with mic in hand.] Adonis: "Fans at this time it is my pleasure to bring out a man who will face All-Asian Juuryou Champion Ed Dean in the main event tonight. Representing Chiimu Tora, Jet Jaguar II!" [The fans applaud and cheer as Jet Jaguar II walks through the curtains. He is wearing his signature black and silver mask, long black and silver tights, black shingaurds over black boots, and a white Hirryu Batsu t-shirt. He looks out to the fans and gives the Chiimu Tora sign raising his fist out in front of his chest and the fans cheer once more. Jaguar then walks over to Adonis.] Adonis: "Jet, tonight you face a huge challange in Ed Dean. Many feel you are getting in over your head. I even understand some of your Chiimu Tora teammates told you not to accept the match. Is that true?" Jaguar II: "Yes Jonnie. It is true. Banshee-san especially didn't think I was ready." Adonis: "Well, you haven't been in the ring since your injury at the hands of current welterweight champion Tasogare no Prince during the tournament." Jaguar II: "Hai, that is true, but I could not stand on the sidelines and use excuses as I watch this disgrace, this abomination that is Ed Dean dishonor the All-Asian title. He is an outsider, a traitor and he deserves nothing from the fans or the wrestlers of SJPW. It is the very nature of Chiimu Tora to stand and fight against such a man." Adonis: "True, but Dean has already beaten Onslaught, Hamada, Fujiwara, Nishida, and Banshee...Hasn't Chiimu Tora failed enough?" [Jaguar's head snaps around at Adonis's question, and the camera zooms in and captures the young mans eyes as they flash with intensity. He lets out a long breath as if calming himself and then speaks.] Jaguar II: "It is true that we have not succeeded against Dean yet..." Adonis: "It has gotten to the point that Tiger Kikuchi has come out of retirement just to face Dean." [Jaguar once again becomes angered, but he calms down, and then shakes his head. He speaks now, and his voice is low, saddended and shamed.] Jaguar II: "It should not have come to this. Kikuchi-san feels he must set the example for us all. Not only for Chiimu Tora, but for SJPW. To expel this invader once and for all." Adonis: "Do you think he can do this. Coming back from retirement and with the past injuries inflicted upon him by Hamada during his dark days?" Jaguar II: "I have faith that Kikuchi can defeat this monster, but first I will face this challange. I cannot back down from it. As a member of Chiimu Tora, it is my duty to fight Dean, and regardless of the outcome, I will fight him. Perhaps Fujiwara was right. Maybe it is time to fight fire with fire. To do to those as they would do to use. Dean deserves no mercy." Adonis: "Very strong words were spoken by Fujiwara and now by you. Do you feel that Chiimu Tora's sensibilites have no place in the year 1999?" Jaguar II: "Honor, discipline, and tradition will always have a place in puroresu, as they should in life as well. However there comes a time in battle when you must do whatever you can to succeed. These are sad times for SJPW. Kobayashi suspended. Dean as champion. Chiimu Tora must lead us into a better future and I hope to start that journey tonight be defeating that piece of haikibutsu in the ring!" [The fans cheer] Adonis: "Moving away from tonights match for a moment, I would like to get your comments on the actions of Chanraskhar Yoshida. He has some pointed words for your mentor Shoji Suzuki and Shoji's sister Misae." Jaguar II: "Yoshida has become poisoned. Poisoned in the mind. Poisoned by his own weaknesses, fears, and failures. He blames everything and everyone but himself for his troubles. He blames his cousin for casting too great a shadow when they tagged together. He blames entire federations for not giving him oppurtunities. He sees corruption and deceptions everywhere, but it is he who is corrupt and decieved. He can mock the traditions and values of SJPW and of this mask. I wear it proudly because to me it stands for the virtues of Shoji Suzuki. A fine warrior and a man who has given me ever oppurtunity I have had and asked for nothing from me be hard work and dedication. For Yoshida to make the claims he did. For further blaming anyone but himself and now for poisoning the mind of Susan K'uang-yin against Misae...He is below contempt." Adonis: "I suspect you would like to meet Yoshida one on one in the ring?" Jagaur II: "My total focus is on my match tonight, but who can say what the future holds. Since Yoshida could not best the original Jet Jaguar, perhaps he would find an easier time with his 'imitator'. That is for tommorow however, I must concentrate on today." Adonis: "I for one wish you the best of luck tonight Jet." Jaguar II: "Thank you Jonnie. I will fight my best fight tonight." [Jaguar bows to Adonis, and then out to the fans before flashing the Chiimu Tora sign once again and the fans erupt with applause.] ER: Strong words from the challenger tonight, not only for Ed Dean, but for Yoshida! AO: Jet Jaguar II shouldn't let Yoshida's comments distract him. It could cost him in the end! ER: Two matches left to go, and both are title matches! Up next: Banshee and Kagemusha face to decide who will be the PJW Grand Prix Champion. AO: Both men had impressive victories earlier tonight, so it's too hard to tell who will win! ER: I'll say. ["Fly" by Sugar Ray blares over the PA] Nishi: Representing Chiimu Tora, and weighing 219 pounds....THE BAN-SHEE!!!! [The lights dim once more and the crowd cheers loudly as a pair of green spotlights shine down onto the arena entrance. Climbing up on the outside, he will perch himself on top of a corner turnbuckle, taking a moment to tilt back his head and issue his piercing warcry. Then, pointing toward the sky, he will vault off the top rope and perform a twisting somersault into the middle of the ring.] ER: Banshee's very motivated for this match... AO: And well he should be. He is one step away from becoming a champion once more! [Goto calls for the bell, and both men circle each other, seeking an opening.
