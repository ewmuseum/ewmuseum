Senshi no Tamashii

7 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Senshi no Tamashii continues...] ER: We're back, and it's main event time! Has Mitsunori Takizawa found the last two allies he needs to battle the Ed Dean, The Maulers, Kenichi Kubota & The Corporate Dragons? AO: I hope so! Even if he doesn't, give credit to Ace Kilcannon, Kensei Tokugawa & Rupan Loco for siding with Takizawa against these men. If these diverse men can join forces to stand up for SJPW, then maybe Takizawa can prevail after all... ___ ___ _______________________________________________ / _ \__ / / | /| / / / / ___/ // /| |/ |/ /Team Corporate/Team SHVF vs Team SJPW /_/ \___/ |__/|__/____________________________________________/ [The camera opens on the ring. Nishi Toju, Goto Kyoji, Ed Dean, Kenichi Kubota, The Maulers & Corporate Dragons are standing in the ring...] Nishi: This is our main event! It is one fall, tv time limit remaining. The special stipulation for this match is that Mitsunori Takizawa *MUST* pin Ed Dean, make him submit, or earn a knockout decision to receive a title shot against him at Wrestlefest! [Dean yells out "It'll never happen] Nishi: In the ring at this time, representing Team Corporate: KEN-I-CHI KU-BO-TA & TEAM COR-POR-ATE!!! [crowd boos]. Also in the ring at this time, representing TEAM SHVF, THE MAUL-ERS & All-Asian Senshuken....ED DEAN!!!! [crowd boos again] ER: Dean and his allies look ready for battle. AO: And now we find out who else will side with Takizawa... Nishi: At the request of Mitsunori Takizawa, their opponents, calling themselves TEAM SJPW [crowd cheers], ACE KIL-CAN-NON, KEN-SEI TO-KU-GA-WA & RU-PAN LO-CO, and team captain MIT-SU-NOR-I TAK-I-ZA-WA!!!! [Takiawa, Kilcannon, Tokugawa & Loco come out to a thunderous ovation from the crowd. As they head to the ring, the crowd throws streamers onto the runway. Takizawa and the rest of Team SJPW keep their eyes focused on the ring. They enter warily, fully realizing the 6-4 odds...] ER: Takizawa is still short two men! AO: This doesn't bode well. [Dean starts trash talking Team SJPW, when suddenly, the crowd erupts into cheers again as "Jigoku" Tiger Extreme, bone in hand, comes charging down the runway, and slides into the ring. Both sides back away from him, not sure what he will do. Extreme turns to Takizawa, and gets on his knees. He looks up at Takizawa, and bows. Takizawa nods in assent, and Tiger Extreme gets to his feet, and SPITS on Dean! Dean tries to lunge at Extreme, but The Maulers hold him back. Kubota grabs the microphone from Nishi...] Kenichi Kubota: Takizawa, where is your last partner? Is there no one else in SJPW with the courage to face us? [The house lights suddenly go out leaving the arena in complete unified darkness. The crowd keeps its cheers lingering until the last sound dies out. Then the music begins. The arena remains dark even with the passing of the first few notes until finally...] [Song: Heart of Sword Artist: TM Revolution] Hitori de wa, tooi ashita wo Yoake no mama de, koesou de [A lone white spotlight hits the runway revealing Tetsuo Matsumoto,standing with arms folded over his chest. His eyes looking straight to the ring] Trans: When I'm alone, tomorrow feels far away. And I must go over still into the darkness of dawn. Butsukatteikya kokeru omoi yo Konya mo mata, sure chigai [Tetsuo remains standing at the foot of the rampway unmoving, save for his head that glances over the crowd and nodding slowly... approvingly almost] Trans: If I try to play it straight, it will no doubt fail, And tonight it won't go well between us again. Sanzan sugite doryoku no ato mo Naku naru kekka, only no tsuna watari Trans: You can't see all of my hard efforts, because it's only result Is that it makes no sense. It really is a "tightrope". Yaru dake son suru yona, mainichi wa Sha ni kamaeteta hou koso, raku ni naru [Finally Tetsuo moves, his hands clenching in fists as he spreads his arms wide, timed perectly with the pyrotechincs that go off at both sides. This draws a loud cheer from the crowd as Tetsuo finally starts walking o the ring] Trans: More effort, more damage--this is my daily life. Taking a cynical attitude may give me some comfort... Atsukute, tsurai jibun wo kakushite, mijikai toki wo ikiteru Trans: Hiding myself, heated and irritated; living only a short time. Hitori de wa, tooi ashita wo Yoake no mama de, koesou de Trans: When I'm alone, tomorrow feels far away. And I must go over still into the darkness of dawn. Hottokeba, hashiru omoi yo Yume mo mata, sure chigai Trans: If I let my emotions free, My dreams will once again not go well. [Tetsuo makes his way down the walkway, fans mobbing him, hugging him, others just reaching out a hand to touch hands or even his arm. Still his goal is forward, constanly looking ahead to the only thing that is in his mind... the ring] Kanpeki to chau, jinsei no shuushi Puramai zero da nanteba honto ka na? Trans: I think the balance sheet of my life is imperfect. If I add up the plusses and minuses, will it really equal zero? Shinu made ni tsukaikiru, un no kazu Semete, jibun de dashiire wo sasete Trans: I want to control all my luck That may be used up before my life is ended. Wakacchainai, kimi nara dou ni demo, rikutsu wo kaete ii noni Trans: You don't know--you can change logic at your will. Nando kimi ni, ketsu mazuitemo Modottekichau, aijou ni [As Tetsuo moves forward through the throngs fans pressing to him the Jumbotron plays images of Tetsuo. The image of him injuring his neck as Super Tengu. The image of him blowing an attempted Rider Kick and crashing into the security rail instead of his targeted opponent. A close-up of his face, under the mask of Tengu, blood pouring over his eyes with a pained, almost grimace, on his face. This is followed by the shot fading into a shot as how he is now, maskless, stoic... looking only forward] Trans: I hurt myself because of you, over and over; But my love didn't go away--it kept coming back. Shinjikaneru, utaretsuyosa yo Konya mo, soutou nemurenai Trans: The toughness gained from my damage is unbelievable. I won't be able to sleep at all tonight either. Nando nankai, kurikaeshitemo Modottekichau, ai dakara Trans: However many times it's repeated,. It revives again and again--because it's love. Butsukatteiku, kesunu omoi wo Semeru hou ga, suji chigai [As Tetsuo walks on the images overlap, a shot of Tetsuo as a child standing with his father Masami. A shot of Tetsuo and his sister Kasumi playing together, a shot of Tetsuo mourning at his father's funeral and finally a shot of Tetsuo standing with his family... his entire family, smiling peacably atthe annual Sakura festival] Trans: You can't blame my emotion, Because you should know it will never fade away. Hitori de wa, tooi ashita wo Yoake no mama de koeteyuku [Tetsuo finally is at ringside looking up into the ring, hands placed on his hips as looks over the surrounding ringside. He nods slowly. Suddenly a female fan jumps the railing and wraps her arms around him, hugging him tight. Security rushes to try and get her off the wrestler but Tetsuo raises his hands and shakes his head, instead opting ti gently cradle the girl in his arms before telling her to go with the men in uniform.] Trans: When I'm alone, tomorrow feels far away. And even though I'm in the darkness of dawn, I have to go. Aishou yori mo, fukai futari wa Sure chigatte kamawanai Trans: I don't care about "bad affinity". Even if our love is not doing well, Nevertheless we have deep ties. [Finally Tetsuo is in the ring raisning both fists in thair to a thunderous cheer. his hands fall to his hips as he waits center ring or the start of the match. The cheers are deafening and say just one thing... TET-SU-O!! TET-SU-O!!] ER: I can't believe it! Tetsuo Matsumoto is Takizawa's fifth partner! Dean is livid! [Dean charges Takizawa, and levels him with a lariat! Kilcannon & Rupan Loco attack Ishida & Usami, and all four men go to the floor. Kubota attacks Matsumoto, and the two go toe to toe in the corner, exchanging chops. Tiger Extreme nails Dean with his bone, then gets nailed by Doug Thomas from behind. Douglas charges Tokugawa, who ducks the lariat attempt, then nails Douglas with his kendo stick! The crowd roars its approval as Tokugawa nails Douglas again, then turns his attention to Dean. Dean is busy putting the boots to Takizawa, then collapses in a heap as Tokugawa nails him with his kendo stick. Tokugawa drops the stick, and helps Takizawa to his feet. He checks to make sure he is all right, then drags Dean to his feet. He holds Dean's arms behind him, and Takizawa lays into him with punches to his gut. Thomas throws Extreme over the top rope, then nails Tokugawa from behind with a double axe handle. He spins Tokugawa around, bends him over, then grabs his head. He drives his knee into Tokugawa's face, sending him to the mat. Thomas turns his attention to Takizawa, and aids Dean in pummeling him to the mat...] ER: This isn't a match; it's a free for all! AO: As if you didn't expect it to break down like this? With the men involved, you knew this had to happen! [Outside the ring, Kilcannon & Rupan Loco double suplex Usami on the floor, then Loco grabs Ishida, ad whips him towards Kilcannon, who catches him, lifting him up, then dropping him face first onto the ring apron! In the corner, Matsumoto has finally gotten the upperhand on Kubota, trapping him in the corner, and laying into the side of his head with stiff elbow shots. Goto makes Thomas head to his corner, and Thomas drags Douglas with him. Goto then makes Tokugawa go to his corner, and he keeps Extreme from entering the ring. Goto exits the ring to break up the fighting outside, leaving Dean in the ring with Takizawa, while at the other end of the ring, Matsumoto lays into Kubota. Dean whips Takizawa towards Matsumoto, and he slams into his partner, sending Matsumoto over the top rope to the floor! Takizawa staggers back, and Kubota lunges out of the corner, sending Takizawa to the mat with a lariat! Dean & Kubota put the boots to Takizawa, until Tiger Extreme enters the ring, and nails Kubota from behind with a forearm shot. He grabs Kubota by the head, and rams him into the corner turnbuckle. He then throws him over the top rope to the floor. He ascends to the top rope, and waits for Kubota to get to his feet. Meanwhile, Dean presses Takizawa over his head, then drops him face first to the mat! Dean tags in Douglas, and drags Takizawa to his feet. Kubota rises, only to have Extreme come off the top rope with a lariat that sends him over the security railing into the crowd! Dean whips Takizawa to the ropes, and he and Douglas double kick Takizawa in the gut, then double suplex him. Dean goes to his corner, while Douglas legdrops Takizawa across the chest...] ER: I see Dean's strategy now! They're going to pummel Takizawa so he can't possibly beat Dean! AO: It makes total sense! Team SJPW needs to get itselt together! [Outside the ring, Goto has gotten Ishida & Usami to head to their corner, and Kilcannon & Loco to theirs. Douglas stands Takizawa up, then levels him with a clothesline. He makes a cover, but Takizawa kicks out at one and a half. Douglas kneedrops his forehead, and Takizawa sits up in pain. Douglas applies a rear chinlcok, but Kilcannon springs over the top rope, and nails Douglas with a stomp to the head. Douglas falls back, and Kilcannon drags Takizawa towards his corner until Goto forces him to go back to the aprong. Douglas tags in Ishida, who enters the ring, and charges. Takizawa just tags Loco when Ishida nails him with a boot to the back of his head. Takizawa goes over the top rope to the floor, crashing on top of Matsumoto, who was just getting to his feet! The crowd "ooohs" as both men go down in a heap. Ishida tries to Yakuza kick Loco, who catches the leg. He keeps Ishida off balance, move in to lock his right arm under Ishida's left arm, then hits a modified Head and Arm suplex! Loco drags Ishida to his feet, and hits a belly to belly suplex. He starts to apply the Wolfe Bite Sleeper, but Doug Thomas runs in, and kicks him in the head. Thomas straddles Rupan on the mat, and starts to pummel him. Kilcannon springboards into the ring again, nailing Ishida with a dropkick, then he rolls to his feet, and kicks Thomas from behind...] ER: Kilcannon & Loco showing surprising teamwork, considering their current rivalry. AO: There is too much at stake here to allow them to be distracted by their dislike of each other! [Goto forces Kilcannon to the apron again, and Ishida & Thomas take the opportunity to double flapjack Loco. Thomas goes back to his corner, and Ishida tags in Dean. Dean drags Loco to his feet, and hits a belly to belly suplex. He makes a cover, but Tiger Extreme breaks the count. Extreme starts to bite Dean's forehead, then Kubota runs in, and kicks Extreme. He drags Extreme to his feet, and he lunges for a lariat, sending both men over the top rope to the floor! Dean sits up on the mat, shaking his head, while Loco rolls to his corner. He tags in Tokugawa, who comes in, and catches Dean with a running elbowsmash as he gets to his feet. Dean staggers back, and Tokugawa grabs him, then whips him to the ropes. Tokugawa drops down as Dean bounces off the ropes, gets to his feet, and catches him with a vicious powerslam as he bounces off the ropes again. Tokugawa tags in Kilcannon, and drags Dean to his feet. Kilcannon goes to the top rope, while Tokugawa hoists Dean up on his shoulders. Dean starts to pepper Tokugawa's forehead with punches, until Kilcannon comes off the top, catching Dean with a missle dropkick! The crowd roars as Dean does a 360 in the air, before crashing face first to the mat. Ace makes a cover, but Douglas breaks it up. Douglas presses Ace over his head, the drops him throat first onto the top rope! As the crowd "oooohs", Matsumoto & Takizawa get into a shoving match outside the ring! Tokugawa & Tiger Extreme try and calm their partners down, while Dean makes the tag to Usami...] ER: Team SJPW is starting to fall apart! Matsumoto & Takizawa are close to fighting each other! AO: Matsumoto is upset because twice Takizawa was thrown into him. His temper is getting the best of him!
