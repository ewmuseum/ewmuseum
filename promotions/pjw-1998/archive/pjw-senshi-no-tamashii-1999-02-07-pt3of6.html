Senshi no Tamashii

7 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Senshi no Tamashii continues...] [The camera opens on Jonnie Adonis, who is standing at the interview stage to the side of the runway entrance...] Jonnie Adonis: Please welcome my guest at this time, acting CEO of Super Japan...SHINSAKU YAMASHITA!!!! [a chorus of boos erupt as Yamashita, flanked by Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami, comes out. He smiles at the crowd, and waves. Ishida & Usami stand impassively behind him. He walks over to Adonis, still smiling...] JA: Mr. Yamashita, I understand there are a few issues you wish to address. SY: That is correct Adonis-san. Recently, "Iron Man" Tetsuo Matsumoto [crowd cheers] made a challenge to these two fine men behind me [crowd boos]. He wanted to face them in a tag team match. After serious discussions with both men, they have agreed to face Matsumoto and a partner of his choosing at Wrestlefest. [crowd cheers] JA: Interesting. What about his bid for a shot at Takada if his team wins. SY: If Matsumoto and his partner win, he will receive a *non-title* match with Toshiaki Takada. He is not ranked high enough to warrant a title shot. However, if he defeats Takada in this non-title match, he will then become a ranked contendor, and will receive a title shot shortly thereafter. JA: Amazing! No one expected you to do this Mr. Yamashita. SY: Of course not! These fans think I am against everyone. I'm not. I simply believe in doing business. The fans want these matches, so they will make SJPW money. As long as they do that, then I will see that they happen. JA: What about Takada's request that if and when he and Matsumoto face for the Koubousen Geijutsu title, that the winner be granted a title shot for the All-Asian title? SY: While that would be a major draw, I have to decline such a match. The Koubousen Geijutsu, while falling under the Juuryou & Welterweight divisions, is still very much seperate in terms of rules. I also will not have someone challenge for a title while holding another one, because there are other competitors ranked for both titles. To do such a match would be unfair to them, and after all, I am a fair man! [crowd boos] JA: All right. What about you sending Masashi Lanther to America so he wouldn't be able to team with Takizawa tonight? SY: The NCWF, who we recently signed an agreement with, asked us for a cruiserweight to challenge for their crusierweight title. All of our major competitors are currently engaged, so I decided to give Lanther one more chance at glory. Let's face it; he hasn't been the same since he won, then lost, the CWO lightheavyweight title last year. JA: I can see your point, but the timing is still suspicious. SY: I really don't care what you think! I did a business decision, and you need to remember who signs your paychecks! [Adonis glares at Yamashita] JA: All right Mr. Yamashita. You have some major announcement concerning how SJPW will honor Giant Baba. Are you ready to reveal this announcement? SY: Yes, I am. As you all know, we will have our annual Super J Crown tournament starting in March. After consulting with the board of directors, we have decided, to honor Giant Baba, that the Super J Crown tournament will be renamed the Giant Baba Cup! [crowd applauds] I felt this was the best way to remember this legendary hero of our sport, don't you? JA: I'd have to agree! This is a stunning announcement! SY: It is, isn't it? JA: Ladies & Gentlemen, SHINSAKU YAMASHITA!!! [The crowd actually applauds Yamashita for his announcement, and he bows to the crowd, the smile never leaving his face. He waves at the crowd once more, then heads to the back, flanked by Ishida & Usami. The crowd cuts back to the broadcast position...] ER: Wow! What a major announcement by Yamashita! AO: It is a great way to honor Giant Baba. As for his other comments, I hate to say it, but Yamashita has covered himself well. He certainly covered his interference in Takizawa's upcoming match well. ER: He did, didn't he? What about his comments concerning Matsumoto? AO: I'd have to say that he did the smart thing. These matches will draw crowds to the arena, and we can make money off of it. SJPW is a corporating after all, and it's job is to make money by providing sports entertainment! ___ ___ _______________________________________________ / _ \__ / / | /| / / / / ___/ // /| |/ |/ / Metamorphico vs Rupan Loco /_/ \___/ |__/|__/____________________________________________/ Nishi Toju: This next match is one fall with a fifteen minute time limit. [Spanish Mariachi music begins to fill the arena, the fans respond with a great pop] Nishi Toju: Coming to the ring, at 239 pounds and from Pico de Orizaba, Mexico.... MET-A-MOR-PHI-CO! [The great pop continues as the dark skinned, well muscled Metamorphico jogs down the aisle, slapping the hands of the fans. He is wearing a "AAA" tshirt, has black knee pads, magenta and black trunks and wears a reflective pattern on his mask with tassles. He vaults to the top rope and tears off his "AAA" shirt and tosses it into the crowd, which responds nicely.] Nishi Toju: And his opponent... [The Mariachi music dies and the fans begin to give many wolf "HOWLS!"] Nishi Toju: At 261 pounds and from Parts Unknown... RU-PAN LO-CO! [The fans continue to scream out "HOWL!" as the walf masked warrior emerges from the back to a warm reception. He has no armor on tonight, as the arena is a bit warm. He has a wolf mask, furry boots and dark blue tights on. As he gets into the ring, he lets out a huge "HOWL!" and the fans respond nicely] ER: This should be a great contest Akira. AO: Hai. Rupan has a strong amateur background but Metamorphico is just so daring in the ring and also possess a good counterwrestling ability. [The bell rings and both men approach each other. They are feeling each other out and finally get hooked up in a collar and elbow tie up. Metamorphico sends Rupan Loco into the ropes but Rupan recovers quickly and comes flying, looking to hit a clothesline. Metamorphico counters with a drop toe hold that sends Crazy Wolf down and then hits a nice Sentan Splash onto the back of Rupan. The crowd responds nicely. Rupan makes his way back to his feet and is caught in an arm wringer by Metamorphico, who twists the arm some more. Rupan goes down to one knee, looking to grab the mask of his foe to pull him over buut is unsucessful. Metamorphico, steps over the arm and vaults up onto the sholders of Rupan, looking for a Victory Roll. Rupan stands up quickly and pushes the smaller Metamorphico off of his shoulders, catches him in mid air and hits an awesome Release German Suplex. The crowd gives a large pop.] ER: Good solid moves from men at this point. AO: True. We still have yet to see Metamorphico try a high risk move though. [Metamorphico gets up and is trying to shake the cobwebs out from the suplex and is taken over by Rupan Loco with a solid, textbook Fireman's Carry Takedown. Rupan follows that up immediately with a cross armbreaker. The hold never gets hooked all the way as Metamorphico has joined hands, not letting Rupan have his arm fully. Rupan attempts to get the arm and finally does. The referee asks Metamorphico screams "NO!" as he is asked if he wants to submit. Metamorphico finally gets to the ropes with his feet and Rupan is forced to break the hold. Metamorphico is up quickly and Rupan tries to shoot in with a single leg takedown with is thwarted by a quick Metamorphico. With Rupan still on one knee from the attempted takedown, Metamorphico hits a nice dropkick right to Rupan's Wolf Mask which sends him through the ropes and to the outside.] AO: Nice strategy by Crazy Wolf as he tried to keep Metamorphico on the mat. ER: You are right Akira, but Metamorphico was just too quick that time and has sent Rupan out to the floor, where he wants him to be. [Rupan rises on the floor and is met with a nice Baseball Slide Kick to the face which sends him back first into the guard rail. Metamorphico follows that up by vaulting to the middle of the top rope, turning around and hitting a nice Springboard Moonsault onto Rupan. Metamorphico lands half way on Rupan and half on the guard rail and looks a little but injured. The fans give a warm ovation. Rupan is the first up and and Hammerlock Slam's the smaller Metamorphico to the arena floor and follows that up quickly with a big leg drop. Rupan picks his foe up by the tassles hanging off of his mask and sends him running into the guard rail. Metamorphico recovers, vaults to the top of the rail and hits a nice Asai Moonsaultfully onto Rupan and the crowd responds nicely. Metamorphico is back up and vaults to the ring apron. He then jumps to the top turnbuckle and puts his back to the crowd. He does not see Rupan already rising to his feet. Metamorphico leaps backwards and looks to hit a dazzling Moonsault. The lightbulbs go off and the crowd "Oohs" but Metamorphico is caught somehow by Rupan Loco who hoists him up and hits a powerful Shoulder Breaker. Rupan picks Metamorphico up by the mask and rolls him into the ring.] ER: What a great display by both me! AO: Hai, Metamorphico might have the advantage if he was not so daring. ER: Maybe, maybe not. That is most of Metamorphico's offense! [Rupan Loco has climed back into the ring and pulls up a winded Metamorphico. Rupan locks on a headlock and squeezes it a bit. Metamorphico sends Crazy Wolf into the ropes, leapfrogs up but is caught by a nice Spinebuster by Rupan who picks him up again and hits a quick Snap Suplex. Rupan goes for the pin. 1 - 2 - KICKOUT! Metamorphico rolls away from his foe and rises. Rupan comes in and is met by some kicks to the stomach which double him over. Metamorphico tries and hits a great looking Rocker Dropper. Metamorphico follows the up quickly with a Somersault Legdrop to the back of Rupan's head! Metamorphico quickly goes to the ring apron and waits for Rupan to rise. When he does, Metamorphico jumps to the top rope and attempts a Springboard DDT. Rupan counters nicely with a Spinning Northern Lights Suplex! 1 - 2 - 3 NO! KICKOUT! AT 2! Both men rise at the same time and begin to trade punches. Rupan Loco gets the upperhand and doubles Metamorphico over with a stiff knee to the gut. He then puts Metamorphico's head between his legs and hoists him up in a Power Bomb position. Rupan does not drop the hold, instead he runs with across the ring. As he is running, Metamorphico somehow counters and spins out with a Devastaing Hurricarana, using Rupan's speed running against him and flipping him right onto his Wolf Mask! Huge Crowd Pop! Metamorphico does hold the move for a pin but instead runs up to the top rope and hits a beautiful Moonsault onto Rupan Loco. Loco gets up slowly and once again both men begin to trade punches. Rupan somehow gets the upper hand and sends Metamorphico into the ropes. Metamorphico counters by Handspringing into the ropes and Rebounding with a huge handspring Elbow that stund Rupan and the crowd is going nuts!] ER: Great wrestling here tonight! AO: Agreed. I wish those Team Corporate no talent's would watch this and takes soem wrestling lessons. [Metamorphico picks up Rupan and punishes him with a quick Michinoku Driver. Metamorphico jumps up to the to the top rope and singals for a Shooting Star Press. He leaps high into the air and comes down hard... hitting nothing but mat! Rupan Loco moved out of the way just in time! Rupan picks uup his stunned opponent and drags him to the center of the ring. He gets behind him and hooks on the WOLF BITE SLEEPER, a version of the Dragon Sleeper. Metamorphico struggles to get free and the ref checks the hold. Metamorphico refuses to submit and is still trying to get to hsi feet. Suddenly, Rupan Loco, with the Dragon Sleeper still applied, hits a wicked Inverted Suplex on his struggling foe. Rupan refuses to to give up the hold and rolls back, onto Metamorphico's back. He still has the Dragon Sleeper on but looks to also have on a Variation Camel Clutch! The crowd responds loudly with deafening applause.] ER: WHAT A MOVE! AO: GOOD GRIEF! [The ref checks Metamorphico who looks to be in severe pain. He screams out "NO!" just as the bell rings] DING * DING * DING! ER: Did he give up? I thought he said "No!" AO: He might have given up. I have _never_ seen a move like that before. It is very effective! No matter the outcome, both men have fought bravely here tonight! ER: Without question Akira! Here is the decision... Nishi Toju: Ladies and Gentlemen, the time limit has expired here. This match is a DRAW! ER: MAN! [The crowd gives a huge "OOH" as both men, fatigued, rise. The crowd gives them both a huge ovation. Metamorphico rubs his neck from the variation Dragon Sleeper/Camel Clutch and is patted on the back by Rupan Loco. They both shakes hands and the crowd pop gets even louder!] ER: What a tremendous effort by both men! AO: I'll say! These two luchadors actually impressed me! ER: Now that is a surprise! AO: Tell me about it! [The camera cuts to backstage, near the runway entrance. Takizawa is talking to El Gato Diablo, who shakes his head, and walks away from Takizawa. Rupan Loco walks backstage, and approaches Takizawa...] RL: Takizawa, are you still seeking partners? MT: Yes, I am. RL: Then it would be an honor to team with you. MT: You just had a match! RL: So? Kilcannon wrestled once tonight, didn't he? If he can, so can I. Besides, I think you can trust me a bit more than your other two partners, don't you think? MT: You have a point. I thank you for your assistance Loco-sama. [both men shake hands, then the camera cuts back to the broadcast position...] ER: Wow! Rupan Loco makes partner number 3! AO: But he still needs two more people! Can he find them in time?
