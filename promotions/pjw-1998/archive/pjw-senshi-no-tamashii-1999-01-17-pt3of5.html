Senshi no Tamashii

17 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Senshi no Tamashii continues...] ER: Strong words from Tetsuo Matsumoto towards acting CEO Shinsaku Yamashita! AO: And deservedly so! Yamashita is a fool to treat one of his top draws like this. And now Matsumoto wants to take it out on Toshiaki Takada. ER: This is going to be an interesting situation! Up next ladies and gentlemen will be a special tag team match!! AO: What's so special about it? ER: Well, it'll feature two very promising young teams, Ono-san. The Notorious B.I.G. and the Hardcore Foundation! It should be very good. AO: Hardcore Foundation! Hmph!! You mean Garbage Foundation!! Why must we always be forced to watch these matches? ER: Many of our fans appreciate the Koubousen Geijutsu matches and division Ono-san. Would you deprive them of seeing the kind of matches they wish to see? AO: Just because someone likes something, does not mean it is worth viewing, Rose! Perhaps when Kobayashi-san comes back, we won't have to watch this "Hardcore" Wrestling every week!! ER: I, for one, am also looking forward to Jumbo Kobayashi's return Ono-san, but I wouldn't expect him to do away with the Hardcore style matches here in SJPW! Anyway, this match should be a very entertaining one, so let's go on up to Nishi Toju for the introductions of the competitors... ___ ___ _______________________________________________ / _ \__ / / | /| / /Frank A. Marano Jr. Memorial Tournament, RD1/ / ___/ // /| |/ |/ /Notorious B.I.G. vs The Hardcore Foundation /_/ \___/ |__/|__/____________________________________________/ Nishi: Minna-san, this next match is scheduled for one fall with a 15 minute time limit. Introducing first... ["Breathe" by Prodigy blares through the arena's loudspeakers...] Nishi: From Redondo Beach and Compton, California, USA... Weighing in at a combined weight of 500 pounds... The team of "Dangerous" Danny Dynamite and "Original Gangster" Gary Trent... THE HARDCORE FOUNDATION!!!!! [The lights dim and to a dark blueish tint, and out walks Dynamite from the back. He walks to the entrance of the aisleway and extends his arms to his side (Ala Bret Hart), drops them and five whistling flares shoot up from behind him, from left to right, one after the other, and numerous fireworks go off behind him, then the lights come on as OG emerges from the back throwing up his gang symbols to the crowd. The two men walk to the ring together and await their opponents...] AO: Just what is an "Original Gangster?" ER: Why don't you ask Gary Trent? AO: I'd rather not... Nishi: And their opponents... ["Big Poppa" by Notorious B.I.G. plays loudly over the arena's PA...] Nishi: From the Bronx, New York, and South Central Los Angeles, USA... Weighing in at a combined weight of 603 pounds... Phillip "Big Poppa" Murray, and "Big Daddy" Vice Lord... THE NOTORIOUS B.I.G.!!!!!!!!!!! [Murray emerges from the back, smiling and slapping hands with the fans. He is a tall, muscular black man with a shaved head. He wears an orange bandana, black leather vest and orange tights with the with the words "Big Poppa" on the legs in black Vicelord emerges from the back staring coldly at the ring in front of him. He throws up his gang signs as he stalks to the ring. Vicelord is 7 feet of African-American muscle. He wears a blue bandana on his shaved head. He wears a navy blue Nash style outfit to the ring with the letters "B.I.G." on the stomach in white] ER: As Nishi Toju leaves the ring it looks like it will be "Big Poppa" Murray starting against "OG" Gary Trent... AO: Hmph! Do you really expect me to call these men by those ridiculous names!! Bakarashiyo!! ER: The bell has rung and the match is underway!! "Big Poppa" goes right after Trent with a flurry of RIGHTS AND LEFTS!!! But "OG" seems to be giving as good as he gets!! Both men are known as brawlers, Ono-san! AO: It's a shame that they aren't wrestlers!! ER: Now Murray with an EUROPEAN UPPERCUT catches Trent off guard! AO: Imagine, an actual wrestling move surprising him! ER: Now "Big Poppa" with an IRISH WHIP... Trent off the ropes... Murray catches him with a BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX!!! That was an impressive move, eh, Ono-san? AO: I'm impressed that he actually knows _any_ wrestling moves, Rose! Now Murray gets Trent up to his feet and is DRAGGING him over to his corner... ER: And Big Poppa TAGS IN Big Daddy Vice Lord! Murray has Trent in a FULL NELSON... AO: KICK to the midsection of Trent by... ER: Big Daddy Vice Lord? AO: BAH! Whatever his name is! ER: Vice Lord with a series of FOREARM BLOWS staggers Trent into the center of the ring... Vice Lord GRABS him... SIDEWALK SLAM!!! AO: Now this... Vice Lord... goes for a cover... ...1... ...2... KICKOUT!! ER: Dynamite started to come in to break up the count, but it was unnecessary as Trent kicked out from under the 7 foot Vice Lord with little trouble!! But "Big Poppa" Murray, seeing Dynamite coming in has also entered the ring... AO: And now the referee is making Murray leave, giving the Hardcore Foundation a chance to double team on their opponent!!! ER: Dynamite and Trent have Vice Lord now... they WHIP him into the ropes... Vice Lord off the far rope... DROP TOE HOLD by Danny Dynamite sends the Vice Lord face-first into the mat!!! AO: And now Trent off the ropes with a LEG DROP to the back of the Vice Lord's head!!! ER: Excellent teamwork there by the Hardcore foundation!!! AO: Hmph! I suppose so... ER: The referee has finally gotten Murray back outside the ring as "OG" Gary Trent goes to the corner leaving "Dangerous" Danny Dynamite in the ring! But there was no tag!! AO: I don't suppose it matters, anyway. How can you expect a team with a name like the "Hardcore Foundation" to respect the rules?? ER: Vice Lord getting back to his feet, Dynamite waiting for him... DROPKICK by Danny Dynamite staggers the big Vice Lord back into the ropes!!! AO: And from the ring apron, Trent DRIVES THE KNEE into the back of Vice Lord! ER: That seems to have made the big man angry!! He turns to face Gary Trent now... He has him by the throat!!! OH! But a RAKE to the eyes by Trent forces Vice Lord to let go!! AO: And now Dynamite from behind ROLLS UP the Vice Lord... ...1... ...2... ER: Here comes "Big Poppa" Murray to break up the pin!! Murray is just POUNDING on Dynamite!!! AO: Now here comes Trent in as well!!! ER: All four men going at it in the ring now, as the referee is trying to regain some order!!! AO: Now Trent has rolled back outside the ring! ER: The referee trying to get "Big Poppa" Murray back over to the corner... AO: Meanwhile, in the ring, as Vice Lord gets back to his feet, it looks like Dynamite is settting up for his "Attitude Adjuster" Superkick! Vice Lord turns around... Dynamite with the SUPERKICK... NO!!!! The 7 foot Vice Lord somehow managed to duck!!! ER: Now, while Dynamite is still off balance, the Vice Lord GRABS him... LIFTS him up... VICE BOMB!!! That Powerbomb was from a long way up!!! AO: Vice Lord with the cover, but the referee is still trying to get Murray out of the ring!! ER: WAIT!!! Here comes "OG" Gary Trent from outside the ring with a metal chair!!!! *THWACK!!* AO: Disgraceful!! He just hit Vice Lord in the back of the head with that metal chair!! Now he's ROLLING his partner over on top of the the other man!!! ER: Trent slides the chair out of the ring as the ref turns around and sees the cover... The ref starts to count... ...1... AO: Here comes Murray... INTERCEPTED by Trent!!! ...2... ...3.... **ding**ding**ding** Nishi: Your winners... THE HARDCORE FOUNDATION!!!!! ER: Well, Ono-san, it looks like the Hardcore Foundation stole this one from the team of Notorious B.I.G.!! AO: Hai. But it's not like it was a real wrestling match!!! ER: One of these days, Ono-san, someone may take offense to your comments about Strong Style wrestling... AO: You mean _Garbage_ style! [The crowd starts to boo as Shinsaku Yamashita, flanked by Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami, the Corporate Dragons, steps out from the back. He smiles out at the crowd, then takes a microphone that Ishida hands him. He raises his arms to motion for the crowd to quiet down, then starts to speak...] Shinsaku Yamashita: I was in the back watching Tetsuo Matsumoto's little tirade earlier. Tetsuo, why must you persist in acting like a spoiled child? You *deserve* a title shot? last week, you LOST to Jensei Tenru after beating the other Tenru, and thus lost the MLWO title. You lost the last three way dance with the two Tenru's. You fought Oleg Vladikoff to a draw. On top of that, you BROKE your word to Jumbo Kobayashi, and continue to masquerade as Super Tengu! [Some of the crowd starts to chant "Tengu! Tengu!", while others chant "Tetsuo! Tetsuo!"] Shinsaku Yamashita: Oh yes, cheer for Tetsuo, or cheer for Tengu. How quickly you forget that it was Super Tengu who BLACKMAILED me, and FRAMED your HERO, Shinji Hamada? He also blackmailed Ace Kilcannon. Did you all forget that? [The crowd stops chanting] Shinsaku Yamashita: Oh yes, Tetsuo Matsumoto is such an honorable man. He seduced Sharon Akiyama, then discarded her like some common whore. He destroyed a marriage. He almost cost Shoji Suzuki, the original Jet Jaguar, his career, yet you CHEER him? You are the ones who should be ashamed, not me! [Stares into the camera, his eyes full of fury] Sinsaku Yamashita: Tetsuo, you DARE dictate terms to ME? You will wrestle WHEN I say, and you will wrestle WHO I say. You will not wrestle Toshiaki Takada for the Koubousen Geijutsu. Kenichi Kubota is the current number one contender, as he won the mini-tournament last week over Tiger Extreme. However, I will let you wrestle on the next Zenmen Sensou. You will wrestle Korean martial arts master Se-hong Kwan. If you make any attempt to interfere or prevent the title match between Kubota & Takada, I will personally see that you spend the remainder of your contract wrestling meaningless tag team matches, and you will always open the show. Make no mistake Tetsuo: I RUN SJPW, NOT YOU! I don't care if the fans like me, or if the staff like me. All that matters is that I get the job done, and as long as the board of directors are satisfied with my work, you can all go to HELL! [The crowd still sits in stunned silence as Yamashita turns around and storms to the back] ER: Strong words from Yamashita concerning Tetsuo Matsumoto's comments earlier. AO: What's worse is that he spoke the truth. ER: What? How can you defend him? AO: I'm not defending Yamashita Rose! However, he spoke the truth. He did frame Hamada & Kilcannon. He did blackmail Yamashita into covering it up. He did destroy a marriage. He did defile Sharon Akiyama. He did almost end Shoji Suzuki's career. He did lose to Tenru last week. The truth is the truth, even if it comes from the mouth of someone as vile as Yamashita. We have no right to forget Matsumoto's past. He has much to atone for before he deserves a title shot. ER: I never thought of it like that before. AO: None of us did, and Yamashita just shamed all of us into realizing the truth. ER: Yes, he did. We'll be right back folks.
