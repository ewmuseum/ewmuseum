SCCW Debut Card — Part One

18 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey everyone, The following is my first attempt at writing a card, and it's the first card ever for Saint Cloud Championship Wrestling, charter member of the
GWC. Check it out in all its glory at:
[name withheld] We're still looking for more heavyweights, cruiserweights, and (hopefully) tag teams! If you like what you see, please sign up! -- SCCW in St. Joseph, MN 06/18/99 @ the Del-Win Ballroom The following card was taped for release on home video to be sold at upcoming shows (for about $10 a pop). Highlights of the matches can be seen on "SCCW Weekly Television", airing Wednesdays at 7:00PM on Cable
Access Channel 10, St. Cloud.
[A shoddy looking SCCW Graphic is shown on the screen for a good 5 or 6 seconds before the show starts. Some eighties-style generic music plays as the graphic fades and a shot of the Del-Win Ballroom is shown. As the cameraman (on his first wrestling job, mind you) pans the audience of about 700, one or two unreadable signs are shown. Some terrible editing takes us abruptly to tonight's commentators, who are sitting at a table on a raised platform, a fair distance from, but facing, the ring. This segment was taped about an hour before people started arriving to the venue.]
Rick Karch: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the first-ever Saint Cloud
Championship Wrestling card! Those of you familiar with wrestling here in the Upper Midwest may recognize the venue here tonight. We're at the
Del-Win Ballroom, minutes away from Saint Cloud itself, and John Floyd,
I can't wait to see what this muchly hyped promotion has to offer.
John Floyd: I couldn't agree more, Rick, and I know you've been hearing a lot of rumors, as have I, about a mammoth 7'5" 500+ pound behemoth known only as JACK...and we've been instructed to let you all know that that name is spelled in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS.
Rick Karch: And who would argue with the guy?!?! I've been assured he'll be here, indeed, along with his manager KID. Also here tonight, a veteran of many Independent scenes all over the continent, the "Primal"
one, Thomas Clash. Those two will be facing off in the first half of our
Main Event, which will be followed by a 10-Man $1000 Over-the-Top Battle
Royale...and this thing is sure to get wild.
John Floyd: What's more, the masked man, Superunkown will be making his debut tonight, as will the controversial Chris "The Preacher" Craven, and the wild man, Cactus Jon. A lot of new guys here tonight, Rick, so you can expect the unexpected.
Rick Karch: Indeed. All that and a lot more...so what are we waiting for? Let's go to the ring!
[Another bad cut to the middle of the ring, where John Floyd (who doubles as ring announcer) stands.]
John Floyd: Ladies and gentlemen, Saint Cloud Championship Wrestling would like to thank you for your attendance here tonight for our innagural event. Tonight's proceedings are sanctioned by the Minnesota
State Boxing & Wrestling Commision, being represented by Jay Cordova.
Official time form the evening will be kept by the one and only Rick
Karch, and referee for all of tonight's bouts is the legend, Teddy
Sharkey!
[As their names are read, Cordova and Karch give half-assed smiles and waves, met by one or two cheers and jeers from the audience. As
Sharkey's name is read, he slowly makes his way to the ring to a loud pop. Sharkey's been wrestling, promoting, refereeing, and training wrestlers in the area for quite some time.]
John Floyd: All right ladies and gentlemen, now who's ready to see some wrestling?!?! [The crowd enthusiastically responds, bringing about the first match of the evening. The beginnings of "My Name Is" by Eminem are heard blasted throughout the ballroom, and John Floyd begins the wrestler introductions.]
John Floyd: Well then, our first matchup is scheduled for one fall, with a ten-minute time limit. Introducing first, hailing from "Happyland", weighing 187 pounds, Happy Helper!
[A rather pathetic looking weakling of a man, with a dyed orange goatee, and flaming red hair makes his way to the ring. He's dressed in orange pants, an orange tank top, black combat boots, and a gawdy looking orange robe. He walks out to a chorus of "boos." While being checked over by Sharkey, his music cuts off, and is replaced by "Baby, One More
Time..." by Brittney Spears.]
John Floyd: And his opponent, being accompanied to the ring by Brittney
Spears, from "The Bad Streets" of Chicago, Illinois, weighing 341 pounds, "The Heart Breaker" John Murphy!
[Murphy walks out to a good size pop (mostly for his valet, presumed by a slim few to actually be Brittney Spears, but hot-looking nonetheless).
He's dressed in a neon green wrestling singlet, she in a tight white blouse and short pink skirt. They step in the ring and Spears proudly shows off her man, as he cockily flexes his muscles for the crowd, who enthusiastically cheers. After much showboating, Spears leaves the ring,
Murphy is checked, the bell rings, and the match begins.]
***Happy Helper vs. "The Heart Breaker" John Murphy*** Rick Karch: This looks like quite a mismatch, John. John Floyd: And would you look at the broad John Murphy has brought with him to ringside?!?! I can't believe it! What a way to kick off SCCW's debut! The Brittney Spears, in all her youthful, surgically enhanced glory!
Rick Karch: Would you knock it off already?! Who is this Brittney Spears anyway? John Floyd: Are you kidding me? You don't get out much other than for these things, do you Rick? She's the hottest singing sensation in the world right now!
Rick Karch: Really? For some reason then, I've got a hard time believing that that's her...it doesn't matter though, as it looks like our competitors are about to lock up!
[Murphy and the Helper lock up. Murphy pushes Helper with such overpowering strength that it sends him flying into the turnbuckle!
Helper recovers quickly, and when up, charges at Murphy, who meets him with a devastating lariat. Happy Helper writhes on the ground in pain while Murphy mockingly stands over him, flexing his muscles much to
Brittney Spears' approval.]
Rick Karch: Well if this preliminary stuff is any indication, we're in for a short opener. John Murphy is simply overpowering...uh... Happy
Helper here...what are you laughing at?!?!
John Floyd: C'mon Rick, Happy Helper? Who came up with this crap? He looks like a Hamburglar reject from MacDonald's!
Rick Karch: Would you just call the match?! [Murphy picks up Happy Helper and slowly brings him to his feet. He puts his head between his massive thighs to set up for a powerbomb, but
Helper fights it by punching and squirming his way out. Murphy shoves his head in again, and this time acts more swiftly, delivering an excessively loud powerbomb, which the crowd pops heavily for. He goes for the pin! Sharkey counts 1...2...kick out!]
Rick Karch: Man, oh man! I can't believe Helper's got anything left after that! Give him credit for persisting to stay in the match anyway.
[A frustrated Murphy gets up and gets in Sharkey's face, allowing Helper time to get up, sneak behind him, and start biting his arm! Murphy grimaces in almost comical pain, much to the crowd's delight. Helper releases the "hold," and nails Murphy with a standing dropkick...Murphy's up...and another dropkick...Murphy's up again...and this time deflects Helper away after an attempted third dropkick.
Deciding he's had enough, Murphy picks up Happy Helper in a bear hug, almost throws him into the air, and nails what looks like the famed diamond cutter. He covers. Sharkey counts 1...2...3!]
Rick Karch: Well there you have it, a quick opener ladies and gentlemen! John Floyd: Did you see that? That was the Heart Stopper! Incredible move by John Murphy! And look at Brittney Spears! She's all over him in the ring! This is the kind of action I could live with, Rick.
Rick Karch: All right. Now how 'bout you get on the mic there and let the people know who won this thing you clown!
John Floyd: Time of the match 2 minutes, 11 seconds. Your winner, "The
Heartbreaker" John Murphy!
[The tape cuts to the start of the next match.] ***Superunknown vs. "Mr. X-plicit" Joseph Bauer*** John Floyd: This next match is scheduled for one fall, with a twenty-minute time limit. Introducing first, from Parts Unkown...
[Soundgarden's "Superunkown" begins to blast throughout the ballroom. Out walks a man very small in stature, but still in good shape. He wears classic black trunks, black boots, and a black mask, all cristened with a white question mark.]
John Floyd: ...weighing 177 pounds...Superunkown! And his oppponent... [The music gets even louder, as "Turn the Page" by Metallica is played. Quickly following is the entrance of Mr. X-plicit, with his manager
Adrenalynn by his side. He's dressed in black tights, a black t-shirt, boots, and sunglasses, all embroidered with silver "X's," or something similar. She's dressed in a white blouse and black mini-skirt, and black boots which rise nearlym to her knees. Her pettite figure is accentuated by her girlish-looking brown hair, which is in pigtails.]
John Floyd: ...being accompanied to the ring by Adrenalynn, from London,
England, weighing 225 pounds..."Mr. X-plicit" Joseph Bauer!
[The crowd pops largely for Bauer, whereas they were just confused for
Superunkown. The music stops, the wrestlers are checked, the bell sounds, and the match begins!]
Rick Karch: Well welcome back to the announce position Mr. Floyd, and
I'm curious to see if you know anything about this Superunkown character...'cause I haven't been told anything!
John Floyd: I'm as clueless as you are Rick...but get a load of the rack on-- Rick Karch: Cut it out! [As Bauer attempts to lock up in the ring, Superunkown swiftly nails him with a legsweep, taking his feet out from under him. He follows quickly on the attack, jumping on top of his opponent in the mount position, and nailing him with open handed strikes. Bauer blocks as much as he can before squirming away. Superunkown then quickly sends the stunned Bauer into the ropes, meeting him with a spinning heel kick on his way back.
The crowd appreciates the early action. Bauer makes his way to his feet still stunned, and out of instinct nails Superunkown with a chop as he goes in for another attack. The chop sends Superunknown the mat, and
Bauer proceeds to stomp on him viciously.]
John Floyd: A lot of action early on in this one, Rick! Rick Karch: Boy, you said it, John. These guys are a little more evenly matched, and this Superunknown is lightning quick, which makes up for his size disadvantage.
[Back in the ring, Superunkown is slowly recovering from the stomps while Bauer looks over at his valet for approval. When he finally turns around, Superunkown nails him with a hurricanrana! Bauer back to his feet... another hurricanrana from Superunkown! A stunned Bauer must once again react quickly by running forward with reckless abandon (like a bulldozer), picking up Superunkown, bouncing off the ropes still attached, and sort of oddly rolling backwards to throw him away. It's an odd tactic, but effective, as Superunkown bumps on his arm heavily enough to slow him. The crowd is loving every minute of the action.]
John Floyd: Man, they are going nuts in here! Rick Karch: Who says fans in the Upper Midwest don't appreciate good wrestling, huh? [Both men are slowed now, but Superunkown is the first to his feet, getting over to Bauer as quickly as possible and hitting him with an armdrag, which he holds onto in an armbar. Referee Sharkey asks if Bauer wants to submit...he says "no" emphatically. Superunkown, a careful wrestler, does not break the hold, but rather forces Bauer to find a way out. He does so by getting to the ropes. As soon as the hold is broken,
Superunknown goes back to work, sending Bauer into the ropes once more and nailing him with a flying head scissors! A dazed Joseph Bauer makes his way out of the ring, which isn't quite safe, as seconds later (while
Adrenalynn is about to check on him), Superunkown comes flying over the top rope with a summersault! The crowd goes wild again!]
Rick Karch: Holy Cow! Superunknown with a death-defying move! John Floyd: That's a tope-con-hilo for all you as unfamiliar with the
Mexican style as Rick over there, but it was awesome! I've got to give credit to this Superunknown...he knows what he's doing in there.
[Both wrestlers eventually climb back into the ring, where Bauer starts to take some control. He backs Superunkown into a corner with a series of chops, then hip tosses him out. He picks up Superunknown, locks him into a grip a-la-Taz, and nails him with an awesome belly-to-belly suplex! He goes for the cover...1...2...kickout! Bauer quickly locks on a rear chinlock. He holds for what seems to the crowd like ever, so they start to rhythmically clap. Superunknown feeds off their cheers and makes his way to his feet via-some back elbows. Once released from the hold, he runs against the ropes, ducking under a lariat attempt by
Bauer, bouncing again, and hitting a neckbreaker. That takes a lot of steam out of Bauer, and Superunknown drags him over to the corner. He perches himself on the top turnbuckle still holding onto his fallen opponent, looks up, snaps his fingers, and... the lights go out! The fans go nuts...]
Rick Karch: What was that?!?! John Floyd: The Del-Win was never known for its technical superiority, Rick. Rick Karch: Fans, we apologi-- [The lights come back on and Bauer is laid out on the ground.] Rick Karch: -- I guess not...ladies and gentlemen, something strange has just occured here, and Joseph Bauer is laid out on the ground!
Unbelievable! There goes Superunknown for the pin! [Sharkey counts 1...2...3!] Rick Karch: Wow!! John Floyd: Time of the match, 11 minutes, 24 seconds, your winner...Superunknown! Rick Karch: Well fans, there you have it. You heard John Floyd announce to the crowd of nearly 700 here tonight, Superunknown is your winner, but God help me, I don't know how he pulled it off!
[Cut to the next match.] ***Travis "Scared Stiff" Harrisburg vs. Chris "The Preacher" Craven*** [An obscure song..."Electric Haunted House of Love" by Green Jelly begins to play. We go to John Floyd for the introductions.]
John Floyd: Our next match this evening is set for one fall, with a
20-minute time limit. First, being accompanied to the ring by Miss
Jennifer Heath, hailing from Pheonix, Arizona, and weighing in at
186 pounds...Travis "Scared Stiff" Harrisburg! [The pair make their way to the ring, Travis dressed in an Arizona
Diamondbacks #44 jersey and jean shorts, as well as blue sun glasses.
Jennifer, the "prettiest girl around," comes to the ring in a stunning yellow flower-printed sun dress, showing off her very tan, and very sexy legs. Once in the ring, she too shows off her man, and the music eventually changes...to of all things, DC Talk, and their "hit,"
"Supernatural."] John Floyd: And his opponent, being accompanied to the ring by Rebecca
St. James, from Omaha, Nebraska, weighing in at 219 pounds...Chris "The
Preacher" Craven!
[The crowd immediately begins to boo as the pair walks out, "The Preacher" and his companion dressed pretty moderately, he wearing "Thy
Word" on his chest, as well as she. They both look surprised at the cold reaction they receive. The wrestlers quickly get ready for combat.]
Rick Karch: Is that the Rebecca St. James?!?! John Floyd: Okay, now I know you're kidding me. Rebecca St. Who? Rick Karch: It appears, John, that SCCW has decided to actually bring in a starlet from the music world to help with its first card, Christian artist extrordinaire, Rebecca St. James!
John Floyd: Christian music sucks...and quite frankly...so does she. Can't she show a little skin or something? Now on the other hand, Miss
Jennifer is looking mighty nice indeed!
Rick Karch: Unbeleivable...let's go the ring. [The two quickly lock up, with Harrisburg getting the early advantage with a fireman's carry, followed by an attempt at an armbar submission...Craven gets to the ropes and both go to their feet. The lock up again, and again Harrisburg gains the advantage, this time with a simple takedown, followed by a leg drop, he goes for the cover...1...2...kickout! Both wrestlers, again, go to their feet, and the crowd makes a little noise.]
Rick Karch: Well once again we've got some action early on here. Travis
Harrisburg is looking to put this one away quickly!
John Floyd: And hopefully he succeeds. Relgion has no place in wrestling! [Another lockup sees an advantage for Craven, who takes Harrisburg down with a fireman's carry of his own. He then mounts Harrisburg and begins to pummel him with what appear to be closed fists! After letting up for a moment, Harrisburg somehow manages to reverse positions, and returns the pounding! He lets up for some odd reason, and the two slowly return to their feet, with an immediate attack following by Craven. He grabs on to Harrisburg and sends him to the mat with snap suplex. He then climbs to the top rope and waits for Harrisburg to rise...he does...and hits him with a drop kick from the top rope! Cover...1...2...kickout!]
Rick Karch: And it's the Preacher with some offense of his own! These guys are really technically sound, wouldn't you agree, John?
John Floyd: They seem to be utilizing a lot of the newer submission style, popularized in the UFC...it's not as effective as good ol'
rasslin,' but it's damn entertaining to see two guys beat each other silly! [The Preacher sets Harrisburg up for a standing suplex, but before he can hit it, Harrisburg reverses into a DDT! The crowd goes nuts!
Harrisburg kicks his opponent gently until he rolls out of the ring. After a few moments, and after the Preacher rises, Harrisburg sends himself over the top rope with a plancha! Both men go tumbling down to the delight of the crowd! As they slowly make their way into the ring,
Harrisburg punches the Preacher to keep the advantage. Once in the middle of the ring, he locks on a front chancery to try to keep him grounded, but the Preacher, even though booed heavily, as if by some act of God, powers his way out! Harrisburg is stunned. Preacher rises to his feet, as does Harrisburg, and he begins kicking his legs stiffly and loudly. The crowd boos. The Preacher continues kicking away, and once Harrisburg is down, grasps onto his shoulder with some sort of a nerve pinch!]
John Floyd: That's gotta be illegal! Rick Karch: It appears as if Sharkey's gonna let it go...this Preacher sure knows a lot of worldly tactics, John.
John Floyd: He's a cheater. [He lets up the hold and sends Harrisburg into the ropes, Harrisburg ducks a lariat attempt, and another, but gets caught a third time with a loud superkick! Preacher signals up to the heavens with his finger then looks at Rebecca St. James who does the same...he locks on what looks to be the Rings of Saturn submission on Harrisburg! Harrisburg won't submit...he fights it...and finally gives up. The bell is rung.]
John Floyd: NO! Rick Karch: I believe I know the name of that one, John. It's The Revelation, and it's just won the match for Chris Craven! Tell the people! John Floyd: Time of the match 13 minutes, 11 seconds, your winner...Chris "The Preacher" Craven!
Rick Karch: An impressive debut here for The Preacher as he exits the ring area-- [Just then, "golden fireworks" (actually a couple of guys with sparklers) burst out of the ring, and a guy with long black hair and beard and "THE RAGE" written across the back of his jacket in gold lettering comes down to the ring. He climbs in the ring and raises his hand for a mic. Someone unknown to the promoter tosses him one.]
John Floyd: Looks like you were interrupted... Jimmy "The Rage" Martinez: WHAT'S UP BIOTCHES!!!!! I'm Jimmy "the Rage" Martinez, and it's time for all of you to learn what "the Rage" is all about! I may be small, but that don't mean a thing...Any of you want to take me on, I accept! Any size, any time, any match!
[He backflips from the turnbuckle and lands on the floor.] Jimmy "The Rage" Martinez: Everyone get ready to feel the Rage! [He exits to a smattering of boos and some laughter.] Rick Karch: What the hell was that all about?!?! John Floyd: I don't know, but did you see those cheesy-ass fireworks? SCCW ain't shelling out too much money for the fans here in St. Joe tonight! Rick Karch: Aren't fireworks against the fire code? Aw, who cares, let's get to the next match!
[Unfortunately, there's about a half hour intermission before the next match begins, but the tape makes the cut right away.]
