Oh wow! Another new Fed.. :>

4 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just opened.. International Extreme Wrestling Alliance! [Don't confuse it with any of the other 100 Feds that have the word "Extreme" in their titles.. :> ] Just opened on December 1st, we're a totally new Fed. It's a simulation Fed, running with the most recent version of the excellent TNM Wrestling simulator. [However -- don't be fooled.. some rolepalying is still required inbetween matches. See the IEWA Homepage at the address below for more specific info.] I'm working on making the IEWA at least average in terms of popularity [Of course, I consider 30 singles wrestlers & 8-10 Tag Teams average popularity. I dunno how that comapres to "general standards" of popularity. I'd actually appreciate it if someone would let me know about that :> ] I created the IEWA because all the good Simulator-Feds I found always had full rosters, and the best I managed to do was get on a "reserve" list for a few of them. IEWA right now doesn't have any limits to the number of wrestlers I'll accept. I'll close it off to new wrestlers when I get overloaded. :> [I imagine that'll be somewhere around the 150 wrestler mark.] Also, I only accept original Wrestlers. Please don't submit any Real-Life Wrestlers. Anyway, if the IEWA sounds good to you, check out the homepage at: http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3842/index.html Thanks.. Steve
