IEWA Card-Writer Recruitment (Follow-Up), 5 October 1997

5 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The IEWA is still looking for someone to write one card per week. I posted here about a week ago, and have filled some of the positions for a writer, but we still need one more. You'd be writing the Thursday night IEWA card, and occasionally a PPV. (IEWA doesn't hold a PPV every month, btw... October happens to have one scheduled, however.) Anyway, you'd be given a TNM raw log to write the matches up from, but I _don't_ want to get a TNM log back with a few comments stuck in it. I want something that doesn't resemble a TNM log. You'd have some degree of freedom in writing the matches.. shorten them, lengthen them.. the only requirement is that you don't change the winner, and leave the way they won alone. (ie, if it's from a pinfall, leave them as winning by pinfall, if it's a submission, leave it as a submission, if it's a DQ leave it as a DQ, etc.) The only other thing is that you'd haveta write in any other events I tell you to write in.. (ie, after someone in a stable loses, the rest of the stable members run into the ring and beat on the winner, then the winner has everyone he's ever known since 3rd grade come in and help him out.. or whatever..) I'm looking only for experienced card writers. If you're interested in writing for IEWA, please e-mail me at [detail withheld] and include a small sample of other matches you've written up. (2 or 3 matches would be good.. I don't need an entire card.) If you'd like to visit the IEWA homepage before deciding, it's located at: http://iewa.home.ml.org ... or it can be reached at http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3842 if ml.org happens to be down. Thanks... Steve
