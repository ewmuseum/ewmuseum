IEWA Recruitment Post, 8 July 1997

8 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3842/iewa.htm The International Extreme Wrestling Alliance is currently looking for new wrestlers. We've been around since December '96 .. we just got done a month and a half hiatus.. only to discover most our handlers are home for the summer and unable to compete until September. So, the IEWA is searching for new wrestlers to fill out our ranks. The IEWA offers the following belts: IEWA World Heavyweight Championship IEWA World Tag Team Championships IEWA World Television Championship IEWA Hardcore Title IEWA North American Championship the IEWA holds two cards per week, Monday Night Blood Bath and Extreme Competiton on Thursday. We also have one PPV every month, except for May. (Well, you can't have a PPV _every_ month, now can you? :> ) Despite the name, the IEWA is not one of those Feds that has constant hardcore action.. we mix the two, with probably 80% of the Hardcore matches being a Hardcore Title defense. IEWA is run on TNM 6.2 .. match results are never pre-determined. However, you won't be allowed to wrestle if you don't flash at least once per week or if your flashes are sub-par. A lot of current participants in the IEWA have called it the best fed they're in, and most of them compete in multiple feds. (Please note, I am not calling IEWA the best fed and trying to start a flame war.. I'm merely saying that some of the current handlers have called it that.) Unfourtunately, the IEWA Homepage is a little broken now, but it'll be fixed shortly. So, goto http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3842/iewa.htm and take a look. If yer interested in joining, e-mail me at [detail withheld] and I'll hook you up with an application to join. Also, the IEWA _DOES NOT_ accept real Wrestlers, or obvious rip offs of real wrestlers. (ie, a wrestler like Brad "The Best Ever" Heart, who is an obvious ripoff of Brett Hart, would _not_ be accepted.) Again, the IEWA's homepage is: http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3842/iewa.htm Thanks for your time... Steve -- Fed Head, President and Founder of the IEWA. http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3842/iewa.htm
