[IEWA] Pre-Tournament Predictions..

10 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Even tho the IEWA is very new [been around since December 1st] I thought I'd post the pre-tournament predictions for tomorrows tourney to decide the International Extreme Wrestling Alliance North American Champion. IEWA's homepage is at www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3842/index.html [Heavyweight & Television titles are still vacant if you wanna get in on the tourneys for them!] :> [The IEWA Logo appears on the screen, with heavy Industrial music by Front Line Assembly playing in the background] [Camera cuts to two men, sitting in a studio of sorts] Hill: Welcome to the Extreme Competition pre-show Predictions! I'm Jerry Hill, and this is my broadcast associate Frank Davis. We'll also be doing the color commentary for tomorrow night's Tournament to determine the first official International Extreme Wrestling Alliance North American Champion. Davis: That's right! And boy, what a competition that'll be. Let me say right now that I think The Mega Giant is going all the way and win the North American title. Hill: Yes, well Frank, we'll get to that in a minute. I'd like to discuss Round one, however. Who are your picks, Frank? Davis: In ECW vs. U.F.O. I haveta go with UFO! He's half of one of the most dominant Tag Teams in e-Wrestling, The New Destructors, and has been trained by some of the best! There's no way this guy can lose against.. yuck.. ECW. ECW is entirely too much of a wimp. Hill: I wouldn't count out ECW. He's been dominating in other leagues before. And, just because he has a good attitude, it doesn't mean he's a wimp. Davis: Sure it does! Hill: Yes, well, in the Big Poppa vs. Sean DiMarini match, they're both relatively new and inexperienced wrestlers. I think this one is too close to call. Davis: I have to totally disagree! Sean DiMarini is a shoe-in to win! Big Poppa is just a brawler.. nothing more! Dimarini, tho, has the technical skills to back up his brawling. And his finish manuever, the Belly-to-Back Superplex is just DEVASTATING with all his weight put behind it! The guy is almost 300 pounds! And he's BUILT! Hill: Yes, but Big Poppa is bigger and heavier than DiMarini. Plus, he has his own version of the Tombstone.. the Poppa Plunge! It's 10 times more deadly than a regular Tombstone. Davis: Oh, that doesn't matter. If Poppa can't get his hands on DiMarini, and he won't because Poppa is so slow, he ain't gonna win! Hill: How about our next match? Kevin Nash vs. The Mega Giant? Davis: The Mega Giant! No contest! It should be obvious even to YOU, Jim, why he's gonna win. Hill: Jerry, not Jim. Davis: Whatever! Hill: I think Kevin Nash has an excellent chance of winning.. he's certainly more experienced than The Mega Giant, and has his own unique style. Davis: Yeah.. a crappy style! The Mega Giant is a pure Brawler. You can't beat that! Hill: Wait.. but earlier you said a pure Brawler wasn't good enough to beat a Brawler with Technical skills. You're contradicting yourself. Davis: Don't twist my words like that! You know what I meant. Hill: Um, sure. The final match of the first round is Scott Hall vs. D-Kid .. I think D-Kid will inch out a victory over Scott Hall. They're both very experienced, but D-Kid has a unique Ariel style that I think will allow him to win, but you can't discount Hall's style, which is also very unique, but more ground based that D-Kid's. Davis: For once, we agree on something. I think D-Kid will also win, in under 4 minutes. There's been a lot of arguing between The New Destructors and The Outsiders. D-Kid is ticked off at Hall. Hill: There also have been rumours that Federation Head [name withheld] will be adding an extra first round match last second, and shuffling around a few of the first round matches. Davis: Bull Honky! Me & Steve are like this! [*crosses fingers*] I'd know about it if it were going to happen. Hill: Umm.. sure, Frank.. whatever you say. However, let's assume the rumours are true. Do you think that'll change anything significantly? Davis: Of course not! The Mega Giant will still wipe up the competiton. I predict the match for the title will be Mega Giant versus Sean DiMarini, with the Giant winning in under seven minutes. What do you think? Hill: ECW vs. D-Kid .. with ECW winning. Davis: What are you, crazy? It's obvious DiMarini will eliminate D-Kid in the Second Round! Hill: If you say so, Frank. Well, folks, that's all for pre-Tournament predictions! Join us again tomorrow night at 9pm/8pm Central when the tournament for the IEWA North American Title will be underway on IEWA's Extreme Competition! [Camera fades out]
