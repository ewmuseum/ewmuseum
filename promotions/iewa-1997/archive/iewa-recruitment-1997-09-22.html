IEWA Recruitment Post, 22 September 1997

22 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3842/iewa.htm The IEWA is looking for new wrestlers. Right now our active roster is around 10-15 wrestlers, and very few tag teams (around 3-4). The IEWA is looking to expand to about 50 wrestlers and between 8 and 12 tag teams. The address to goto if interested in joing is http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3842/iewa.htm (Please excuse the page, it's somewhat outdated. IEWA has been starting and going into haitus repeately for about 2 months. We're firmly back on track and running now, and the page should be updated soon.) You can find an application on the page. The IEWA offers the following: Titles: Singles Titles: IEWA World Heavyweight Championship IEWA World TV Championship IEWA Hardcore Title (This title can only change hands in a Hardcore match of some type... and we have rules to prevent a wrestler from getting the title and then refusing to wrestle Hardcore matches.) IEWA North American Title Tag Team Titles: IEWA World Tag Team Championship Cards: Two cards per week. Monday Night Bloodbath and Extreme Competition on Thursday nights, and PPV's in every month except May. (Due to the recent inactivity and somewhat small roster, September's PPV will most likely be cancelled.) Matches: While most matches are pretty standard, we offer almost any type of Hardcore/Extreme match. (I'm limited by what my simulator can do, however. I can't do a WCW style War Games, for instance.) In the past, we've had Strap Matches, First Blood matches, Music City Street Fights, Chicago Street Fights, and the IEWA exclusive "Extreme Death Match". All matches on the Extreme Competition card are No DQ/No Countout/No Time Limit. (They can also be Hardcore as well.) Technicals: Run on the most recent TNM. I simulate matches as promptly as possible, then e-mail the short results, and will write out match commentary if I have the free time. I do most the work in the IEWA, only recently turning page maintenance to someone else. What we're looking for in Wrestlers: First, I DO NOT accept real wrestlers. Only fictional ones. I'll consider any application submitted since I want to increase the roster size by well over 100%, I'm looking for the following types of Wrestlers specifically: Since this is _International_ Extreme Wrestling Alliance, any wrestlers that are from somewhere other than American or Canada (that's where all the current active wrestlers are from) I'd particularly like some Luchadors and Japanese wrestlers, although European wrestlers are good too. Of course, I'll also accept American and Canadian wrestlers. I'm also looking to start up a Light Heavyweight division, with 8 to 10 wrestlers (out of my goal for 50 active wrestlers) eligible for the Light Heavyweight class. (Light Heavyweight would be 205 pounds and under. Almost every wrestler in IEWA is currently above that limit.) I'm also always looking for any former or current champions in other Feds who'd like a challenge in the IEWA. (If you claim to be a former title holder in a fed, just for verification, please send a homepage address or e-mail address where I can verify the info. I just don't wanna be proclaiming wrestler to be ex-champions or current champions when they aren't.) Other: The IEWA has some great wrestlers currently active. Among them are Fast Eddie, who got his start in IEWA.. and has won all 5 offered titles, and is the only wrestler to ever do so. (He hasn't branched out to any other Feds as far as I know, but would definately be Title contender in any other Fed.) On his heels is Big Poppa, who has won 4 of the 5 offered titles. (Only title he hasn't won is World Heavyweight. He held the Tag Team titles with Fast Eddie.) Big Poppa also got his start in IEWA, and has stayed totally loyal to IEWA. The IEWA recently acquired the current (current at time of application submission, anyway) FWA California Tag Team Champions, the Smooth Operators. (Who defeated IEWA's current World Tag Team Champions in a non-title match last Thursday.) (As a side note, I've heard FWA actually closed down a bit back.. oh well. I never heard of FWA before the application submissions.) The IEWA has been around since December, 1996. Unfourtunately, between then and September '97, we've had a total of about 4 months worth of inactivity. These stoppages in action should be over for good. Anyway, if all this sounds good, please check out http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3842/iewa.htm and fill in an application if you'd like. Thanks... Steve -- Fed Head & President http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3842/iewa.htm
