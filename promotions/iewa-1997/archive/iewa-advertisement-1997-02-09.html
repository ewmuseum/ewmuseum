IEWA Advertisement, 9 February 1997

9 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--==>> International Extreme Wrestling Alliance <<==-- The IEWA is looking for new Wrestlers and new Staff members. We're mainly after Face wrestlers and new Tag Teams. We're also after Card Writers. The IEWA offers: 4 Singles Titles... World Heavyweight Title World Television Title North American Title Hardcore Title 1 Tag Team Title... World Tag Team Title We currently have about 25 active wrestlers, and are looking for more. We have 3 cards each week.. Sunday Slaughter Extreme Competition (Tuesdays) Friday Night Fright We also have about one PPV per month. (Although, it looks like we're gonna be having _three_ in February.. we've already had 2 so far... but this was a special case. In December & January we only had 1 per month.) The IEWA Homepage: http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3842/index.html If you've submitted a wrestler there in the past 3 weeks and didn't get a response.. please re-submit.. I was having Mail Server problems. Also.. if you'd be interested in writing up Cards.. let me know.. we need Card Writers badly. Steve -- IEWA Federation Head
