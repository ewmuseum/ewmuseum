GDWA Roleplays

The woman who knocked down Ma Porter

July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up with the camera posed in on Lady tiger, doing a double armed pose, muscles rippling from every possible orifice. A lady more muscular than the toughest superstar in the GDWA!! Well, it takes only a moment to notice something wrong with the picture, and sure enough, it's only the *face* of Lady tiger, who is standing behind one of those picture cut-outs you'd find at the fairs. A group of children, supervised by Charlotte LaMancha, laugh it up as the more slender Tiger now steps out from behind the cut-out.] Kids: Tiger's so strong! Tiger (bringing an arm up for a pose): Bwah ha ha! Tiger is all powerful! And do you know why?? Kids: Why?? Tiger: Because . . . . . *I* knocked down, the MIGHTY Ma Porter!! [Tiger does a "big" double-arm flex as the kids cheer her on] Kids: YAAAAAYYYYYY!! Tiger: Hraaaahhh!! [Tiger nearly topples over trying to do a side pose] Charlotte: Take it easy there, kid! You're "recuperating", remember? Tiger: Oh yeah, hehe! Sorry. (One of the kids tugs on Charlotte's shoulder): Auntie Charlotte! Thanks for taking us with you to the circus!! Charlotte: Well, I figured you'd all like to come, seeing who we're celebrating for. (Beaming with pride) Right, Tiger? (Slaps her on the back) Tiger: OWW-IEEE! Charlotte: Oops! Sorry, kiddo. Tiger: Awwwww, Charrrrrrlotte!! Winning hurts a lot MORE than losing!! Charlotte (snickering): True . . . but it feels better, doesn't it?? Tiger: Well . . . . (chuckling) yeah, maybe. Charlotte: Besides, we've got to get you all healed up! Because, come Founder's Day Tradition, you get to face . . . Tiger: SALLY!! YAAAYYY!! But Charrrrrlotte . . . I don't *want* to wait until FDT! I want to face her *NOW*! Pleeeeeeeeease?? Charlotte: Well, kitten, I guess that's up to Sally. So, Sal, if you're through playing around with the kids in Tiger costumes, then sign on the dotted line to face . . . um . . . the *OTHER* kid in the tiger costume!! Tiger: GRRRR!!! Charlotte: Next week, Sally! We're going to give the fans a little taste of what they can expect come Founder's Day Tradition! Sal, the question is . . . can you hang, with the woman who --- >KNOCKED DOWN MA PORTER
