Tiger/Grier/Weathers/Slade Classroom flash

August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in a kindergarten classroom. Pictures cover the wall of alphabet pictures, animals, and graphs. The team of Lady Tiger, Clarissa Slade, Jennifer Grier and Stephani Weathers are pacing the room, as Charlotte LaMancha walks in, yardstick in hand.] Charlotte: Could you gals take your seats? [Clarissa and Stephani look at each other, as they make their way to the 'seats', which are obviously WAY too small for them. Carefully, they attempt to sit down. Jennifer crosses her arms as she leans up against the wall, while Tiger continues to draw stick figures on the chalkboard.] Charlotte: Tiger? Could you join us? [Tiger continues to draw, and the chalkboard has five stick figures on it, each with a name of each wrestler, and manager above it. Tiger spins around, with a HUGE smile on her face, as she points to the one with Charlotte's likeness.] Tiger: LOOK Charlotte! It's YOU!!! [Charlotte shakes her head, smiling.] Charlotte: That's great, Tiger. Could you please sit down for me? Tiger: ANYTHING for YOU, Charlotte! [Tiger quickly sits down in one of the chairs, as Jennifer makes her way towards the back of the class.] Charlotte: OK, class, now today's the day we go over . . . [Tiger starts shaking her hand in the air frantically] Charlotte: . . . . and I see we have a question already! Tiger? Tiger (reaching under her desk and holding up a red, shiny apple): I've got an apple for the teacher!! Charlotte: Um . . . great. Toss it up here, thanks. [Tiger tosses the apple to Charlotte, and beams with pride as it is placed on her desk] Charlotte: Anyway, now today, we'll be going over . . . Slade: (Who is fiddling with something under her table, then looks up) It's over? Cool, see you guys later... Charlotte: Um . . . Clarissa, we've just started, this is important. Slade: (sighs and sits back down) This is ridiculous, wasting a perfectly good Saturday. Charlotte: Well, I'm sorry you think it's a dumb idea, but we have to do this eventually. Now, as I was saying, when it comes to Keiko Mita... [Tiger starts waving her hand in the air again] Charlotte: (sigh) What is it *now*, Tiger? Tiger: Teacher, can I sit next to Jenny? Pleeeeeeeeeeeeeease? Charlotte: Sit wherever you want. I don't care. Tiger: Is it ok with you, Jenny? Huh, is it? Jen: (shrugging her shoulders, face in hands) Whatever you'd like. Tiger: YAAAAAAAYYYYYYY!! (Tiger practically leaps out of her seat to rush over to where "Jenny" is) THANKS, TEACHER! Charlotte: Fine, whatever. Now, as I was saying, in relation to Keiko Mita, I think the best option would be . . . Stephani: (raising her hand and shaking it) Hey teacher person I have a question!! Charlotte: Um, yes? Stephani: First of all, will there be any Hollywood Producers in the audience? Charlotte: I . . . . . doubt it. Staphani: Oh. Say, why are we starting with Keiko again? Charlotte: Well, I just thought Keiko Mita would be a good place to start at. She is a former Western Heritage champion, after all .. Stephani: Wouldn't that make her hard to eliminate? Charlotte: Well . . . well, perhaps you're right. We'll start elsewhere OK, now the person we should probably focus on in this match is . . . Jen: I'll be back. Charlotte: Jen? You're going to miss this. Don't you think it's important? Jen: (turning around to look at Charlotte) What's it matter? I'm just going to blow it again anyway. Charlotte: What are you talking about? Tiger: Jenny! I know what you need.. you need a little TIIIIIIGER POWEEER! [Jen looks to Tiger, barely cracking a smile. The smile disappears quickly.] Jen: Aw, what do you know? Yeah, I had my streak, but I'm done. I don't even know why I'm here. [Jen turns to stand up.] Charlotte (grating her nails on the board): Jen, you're not washed up. You're a top-caliber athlete. Everyone has a cold streak every now and then. Jen: Yeah, and this 'cold streak' is my end. I'm washed up, Charlotte. You've seen me lately. I'm done. Charlotte: Jennifer, sit down. You aren't the weak link. There isn't a weak link. It's all about teamwork. Tiger: Yeah! Come on, Jenny!! Come back!! Please? Prrretty plllllllllease? Jen: I'm just wasting your time.. I'll do this for you, Tiger (sits back down). Charlotte: Ok, great! Now we're ready to start, right? (Looks around the class) No more distractions, right? [A paper airplane goes sailing over Charlotte's shoulder. Everyone turns to look at Clarissa] Slade: What? Charlotte: (sigh) Are we *finally* ready??? [silence from the class] Charlotte: Great! Now, I think the best method of attack would be . . . [Tiger raises her hand, Charlotte ignores] Charlotte: The best thing we can do in this match is . . . [Tiger is now hopping up and down in her seat, waving her arm up] Charlotte (trying to ignore the distraction, rubbing her temples): Isolation is the key to . . . Tiger (hopping): Charrrrrrlo . . . Charlotte (snapping): WHAT?!?! Tiger (a bit embarrassed): Um . . . . I gotta go to the little girl's room . . . Charlotte (stupefied): You . . . what? Tiger: Charlotte!! It's a number twosey!! Charlotte: (sigh) Fine, go. Stephani: Not be rude or anything, but I got an audition in about 45 minutes, so can... Slade (interrupting): Oh be quiet so we can get this over with! Stephani: )after a moment of silence and an evil look Clarissa's way) Can I move Charlotte, Clarissa sort of smells bad. [Charlotte surveys the room. Jen Grier is sulking over in the corner, Stephani Weathers and Clarissa Slade are in a heated dispute, and Lady Tiger has already left the room for the washroom.] Charlotte (holding her head in her hands): It's going to be a looooong month... [Charlotte puts the apple up to her mouth and takes a bite out of it. Fade to black]
