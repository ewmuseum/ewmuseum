GDWA Roleplays

Barber Shop flash

August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up inside a parlor, located in one of those barber college. A full class is each standing in front of a mannequin head with overgrown hair. One person, obviously the instructor, is standing in the center of a room, with a lab coat on. Also standing out from the crowd, the GDWA's "French Duo" of Charlotte LaMancha and Lady Tiger, standing next to one of the heads.] Instructor: OK, class, I'd like to introduce a few new people today. First, my good friend Charlotte LaMancha, who will be supervising with me today. And, I'd like to introduce a new student . . . . um, "Lady Tiger". Tiger (waving to the rest of the class): HIIIIIIIIII!!!! (A few snickers from the class, the instructor looks to Charlotte as if to say, "Are you sure?") Instructor: Ok class, it's time to put those lessons from class to good use. Don't worry, this isn't a test; just an informal quiz to see where your problems are. So, whenever you're ready . . . (Tiger dives into the head, clipping away with her scissors as Charlotte looks on and chuckles. The rest of the class follows suit, as everyone starts cutting away at their mannequin heads) Tiger: Oops! Hehe, well maybe I can fix it by . . . snip snip snip Tiger: Um, Charlotte? Was I supposed to do that . . . snip snip snip (Tiger makes a cut, and holds a way-to-big clump of hair in her hands. She looks around to make certain nobody is looking, and then stashes it in her pocket) snip snip snip snip snip snip snip snip snip [Time passes. All the students are standing in front of their finished heads, while Tiger is still snipping away at hers.] Instructor: Um . . . ok, let's take a look at some of them. Marcy! [Marcy steps aside from her head embarrassedly] Instructor: Hmm . . . you need to practice getting it all the same thickness, look at this . . . [She grabs a patch of hair, which is a little bit thicker then the rest] Instructor: Ok, Cindy, let's see how yours turned out! [cindy steps aside from her head] Instructor (looking it over): well done! Well done! You need a little bit of work in evening out the back, but all around, excellent job!! Good work Cindy . . . snip snip snip Instructor: Um . . . you almost finished, Tiger? [tiger looks the head over, muddles something over, and makes one last snip.] Tiger: All done! Instructor: OK, now let's see yours, Tiger. [Lady tiger spins her mannequin head around and strikes a pose with the scissors] Tiger: TA DA!!!! (The hair on the head of the mannequin is a *TOTAL* mess!! Big patches are missing from some parts, nothing is evenly cut off, parts are tangled up, etc. The instructor looks on at the head in utter horror.) Instructor: I . . . I . . . I don't believe it! This is, hands down, the WORST haircut I've EVER seen, and I've been doing this for 25 YEARS!!! Nothing has been measured off, you've over-cut some places . . . why, whoever gave you a pair of scissors is a BLOODY IDIOT!!! (Tiger starts sniffing, as if she's about to cry. Charlotte pats her on the back as she speaks to the instructor.) Charlotte: It's that bad, huh? Instructor: Absolutely. Charlotte: The worst haircut you've ever seen? Instructor: And I assure you, I've seen a lot! Charlotte, I don't know what you were thinking, bringing in . . . Charlotte: IT'S PERFECT!! (Tiger looks up, a hopeful look on her face) Tiger: It . . . it is? Charlotte: Absolutely!! Tiger, this is the PERFECT haircut to give to Sally at Founder's Day Tradition!! She'll LOVE it!! Tiger: She . . . she will?? Charlotte: I guarantee it! I don't want you to change a THING come FDT!! It's *just* what I was looking for, kitten!! (Motioning to the door) Come on, I'll treat you to some ice cream! Tiger: YAAAAAAYYYYY!!! (Tiger makes a beeline for the door.) Charlotte (as tiger's almost through the door): TIGER!! Tiger (turning around suddenly): WHAA!!! Huh, what . . . oh, yeah!! "Don't run with scissors in you hands!" Hehe, sorry Charlotte. (Placing them down on the table) Ok . . . LET'S GO!!! [The rest of the class looks on in confusion and awe as the pair speed out of the classroom. The instructor watches them leave, looks back at the mannequin, and shakes her head. Fade to black]
