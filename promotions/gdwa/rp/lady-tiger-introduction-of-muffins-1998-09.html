Introduction of Muffins!

September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[scene opens up inside a double hotel room. Laying on top of the bed, dressed in casual clothes with mask still on (does she ever take it off??), lies the heroine of this flash, Lady Tiger. She just seems to be relaxing as the door to the room opens, and in comes Charlotte LaMancha, also dressed casually, with a small, wrapped up box in her arms.] Charlotte: How goes the recuperation, kitty? Feeling any better? Tiger: A little bit . . . say, what's in the box? Charlotte: Oh, this? (Holding the box in front of her) Why, it's a present . . . for you, Tiger!! Tiger: Aaaaah! (Tiger leaps up to her feet) A present? For me?!? Charlotte: Well, you did *SO* well at Founder's Day Tradition 2, that I figured you deserved a little extra reward!! Tiger: Yaaaaay!! (Tiger rushes over and gives Charlotte a big hug) Thanks, Charlotte!! Charlotte: (laughing) Ok! All right!! You can stop thanking me, and start OPENING IT!!! [The words no sooner leave her mouth than Tiger is ripping wrapping paper and bows from the box] Tiger: (as she's opening the gift) Ooh! What could it be?!? Ice cream . . . no, you can't give ice cream as a gift, it melts. Hmmm, new mask . . . no, Charlotte knows I like my old one . . . [Tiger finally rips all the paper off and opens up the box. She slowly pulls off the lid and peers inside.] Tiger: AAAAAAHHHHH!!! How CUTE!! [Tiger reaches into the box, and lifts out into her arms a small, orange and black plush cat] Tiger: OOH!! It's so pretty and cute!! Thank you Charlotte! (Hugging the plush cat) Thank you- thank you - thank you - thank you - THANK YOU!! Charlotte: No need to thank me, kitten! You deserve it! Now . . . what are you going to call it? [Tiger looks up, perplexed] Tiger: Call it? Charlotte: Yeah, it needs a name, doesn't it? Tiger: Hmm . . . (Tiger bows her head in thought, her mask tips over and covers her eyes) "Charlie"? No, too much like "Charlotte", it'd get confusing . . . "Garfield"? . . . no, taken . . . "Rex"? . . . wrong species . . . (Tiger puts her ear towards the cat, as if listening) What's that? Muffins? OK, then, we'll call you . . . MUFFINS!! Charlotte: (raising an eyebrow) It speaks, does it? Tiger: Sure she does! Muffins has lots to say, don't you?? (Picking up the toy, and looking at it, as if asking, "Isn't that right?") Charlotte: (chuckling) Fine. There, don't you feel a little better now? Tiger: (a bit of a sad look comes over her face) A little, but I still feel kind of . . . what's that Muffins? I'm feeling bad over Aunt Jenny? Why, you're right!! What a smart little kitten you are! (Pats it on the head) Charlotte: (rolling her eyes) You're still caught up over that? I told you, Tiger, sometimes adrenaline takes over. Jenny understands that. Tiger: But what I did was wrong! What can I do to . . . hmm? (Looking over at "Muffins") A match? But I don't want to fight Aunt Jenny! She's my friend!! . . . hmmm, I guess you're right. I owe it to her. Jenny probably feels pretty bad about what happened. . . offer a challenge? Next week? And put my #1 contender's title shot on the line? But I don't want to . . . (sighing) you're right. I've got to show Jenny how much her friendship means to me. I've got to give her the chance she didn't get at FDT2. Charlotte: (hand in heads) You know, I'm starting to regret this gift . . . Tiger: (not noticing Charlotte): So it's settled! Next week, we'll fight Aunt Jenny, and the winner will get the title shot! Now, Muffins, while we're waiting, what should I . . . what's that? Ice cream? (Laughing as she walks towards the door, cat in arms) You know, I think I'm going to like you, Muffins!! Charlotte: (shaking her head as Tiger leaves) An imaginary friend, huh? (Chuckling) Sometimes I wonder about you, girl . . . [Fade out as Charlotte follows after Tiger.]
