GDWA Roleplays

Pre-Sierra title match "bread" flash

July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Charlotte (blowing into a whistle): BREAK! Tiger: How did I do, Charlotte?? (A look of worry passes over her face) Charlotte? What's the matter? Did I do anything wrong? Charlotte: We got a problem kid. Tiger: A problem? What? Charlotte: Well . . . you forgot to brag about beating Sierra last week, and some people are a bit upset. Tiger: Forgot? (Sticks her tongue out) No I didn't! "It's not polite to brag", guess who told me that! Charlotte (embarrassed): Well, those Tease people wanted you to brag, so maybe you could just brag a little . . . Tiger: But Charrrrrrlotte!! You told me, "It's not polite to rub in a win. How would you like it if other people did that to you?" Remember?? REMEMBER!?!??! Charlotte: Well . . . it's still not right. But just pretend for a second, ok? Tiger: But . . . Charlotte: Please? Tiger: Well . . . ok. (Tiger starts hopping around with her fingers in a "V") I BEAT SIERRA!! I BEAT SIERRA!! NYAH-NYAH NYAH-NYAH-NYAH!!! NYAH-NYAH NYAH-NYAH- NYAH!!! . . . Charlotte: Ok, that's enough! Tiger: Good! Now can I talk *to* Sierra? Charlotte: Well . . . ok. Tiger: Thaaaaanks! (Giving a big "tiger wave" to the camera) Hiiiiiiiiiiiiiii, Sierra! Hehe, I'm sorry if I hurt your feelings, not making you the center of attention last week. Maybe I should have been saying . . . "I knocked down Sierra Browne"!!! (Bows her head) But it doesn't have the same ring to it. Charlotte: You see, Sierra, you have to realize how this kitten's mind works. (Tiger raises her index finger on her right hand) Charlotte: See, a-way back then, when she was "Rookie Extra-ordinaire" Lady Tiger, you got a tainted DQ win with the help of Miko Azai . . . (Tiger now raises her index finger on her left hand) Charlotte: And last week, at the last possible minute, tiger pulled off the "Hail Mary" pass with a quick pin. So as far as she was concerned, everything was even. But now . . . (Tiger brings her fingers together to touch at the tips, making an upside-down "V" similar to the two sides of a step ladder) Charlotte: We've got the tie-breaker coming up this week. Don't worry, Sierra. We'll give you your wish. My little cat warrior isn't going to be thinking of anybody else. Not Sally, not FDT2, not the 8-woman cruiserweight match. Just you, her, 15 feet of tin, and that little piece of gold you carry around with you . . . Tiger: Gold? Charlotte: Yup, gold. Tiger: Awwww, Charrrrrlotte! I want it! I WANT IT - I WANT IT - I WANT IT!! Charlotte: TIGER!!! Tiger: Eeep!! (Bashfully lowers her head) I know, I'm sorry, I . . . Charlotte: If you *really* want it . . . you'll have to prove it this Tuesday. Tiger (perks up): Really?? Charlotte: Now let's see you're game face! Tiger (excitedly): GRRRR!!!! Charlotte (striking a "heroic" pose and pointing to the camera): Sierra, you may be the best thing in the GDWA since sliced bread. But . . . BUT!!! (Putting her hand on Tiger's shoulder) My champion here . . . EATS SLICED BREAD FOR BREAKFAST!!! Tiger: GRRRR!!!!! Charlotte: So, Golden Girl, Golden Toast, I don't care! You bring your best game this Tuesday, and we'll bring ours! And may the best lady win . . . Tiger: GRRRR!!!! Charlotte: Don't forget that catchy tag line . . . Tiger: Oh, right! Hehe . . . END!
