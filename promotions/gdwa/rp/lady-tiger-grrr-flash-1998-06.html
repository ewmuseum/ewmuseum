Lady Tiger "Grrr!" flash

June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The place, a dressing room located in the Meadowlands Arena. The time, just after the Tuesday Night Cat Fight. The people, GDWA's French duo, Lady Tiger and Charlotte LaMancha. Tiger appears to have a swollen lip, and Charlotte is gently touching it with a cloth dabbed in some kind of hygienic chemical.] Tiger (as the cloth touches her lip): Ow, ow, ow, ow, OWWWWW!! Charlotte (impatiently): Hold still! Tiger: But Charlotte!! It HURTS!! Charlotte: Did it hurt as much as much as "the Crippler Crossface"?? [Tiger becomes silent and lowers her head. A look of regret passes over Charlotte's face] Charlotte (sheepishly): . . . I didn't mean it like that, kitten. Look, sometimes you win, and sometimes you lose. It's OK! Even "The Great Charlotte LaMancha" lost! (Winks) But I think I turned out OK. Tiger: But Charrrrrrrrrlotte! I wanted to win! It's not fair. Charlotte: TIGER!! What have I told you about winning and losing? Tiger: But Charlotte, I . . . Charlotte: WHAT have I told you about winning and losing?? Tiger (sulking): "You learn as much from losing as you do from winning." Charlotte: Right. You've just got to take it a little more seriously, _inside_ the ring. And there's nothing wrong with that, right? Tiger: . . . . . right. Charlotte: Maybe cut down on the ice cream a bit, too? Tiger: . . . . . . . . ok. Charlotte: Good! Now, let me here a tiger growl! Tiger: grr. Charlotte: Come on! You can do better than that!! Tiger: Grrrrrr!! Charlotte: COME ON!! Show them that you're fierce!! Tiger (finally getting into it): GRRRRRRRRRRR!!!!!! Charlotte: YEAH!! That's the stuff!! Tiger: GRRRRRRRRR!!!!! (Charlotte picks up their stuff, as the two leave the dressing room and start walking out of the arena. As Tiger walks down the hall, she raises her arms periodically, shouting "GRRRRRRRRR!!" Employees give her weird looks, as Charlotte just shakes her head and laughs) Charlotte: Sometimes I wonder about you, kid. Tiger: GRRRRRRRRRRR!!! [Fade to black]
