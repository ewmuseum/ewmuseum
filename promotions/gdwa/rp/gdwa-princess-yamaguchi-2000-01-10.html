Princess Yamaguchi, 10 January 2000

10 January 2000 (critiqued 12 January 2000)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Part 1

Yeah, I know. Asking for someone to actually critique something on RSPWF is just asking for trouble. But I was hoping that some kind individual would take the time to read and critique this recent role play for the re-opened Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance. I was happy, with the role play, when I first wrote it but, once I actually read it, it left a bad taste in my mouth. I don't expect any actual critiques, just useless flames. But, what the hell!?! I'm a real glutton for punishment:-)) Anyway, feel free to critique, flame, demolish, yadda, yadda, yadda. Who knows? Maybe I'll actually get some useful criticism! =================================== (We once more find ourselves at the newly furnished, Malibu, beach house of Princess Yamaguchi, on yet another bright, breezy, California afternoon. The camera pans the small, cozy dwelling, catching flashes of Yamaguchi's recent purchases. A large couch and matching love seat, a television set, and a Victorian dining set are just a few of the items that stand out. The house is quiet, save for the sounds of birds calling and water washing upon the shore, the fragrance of the sea lingering heavily in the air. Suddenly, the door bell chimes and all hell breaks loose! Princess Yamaguchi comes racing from her bed room to answer the door, a broad smile across her radiant features. The sleek beauty wears a lavender bikini and a matching saroong, tied around her trim waist. Her long, silky, black hair is tied in two ponytails, draped over her shoulders, bangs brushing her sparkling, brown eyes. With a flourish, she opens the door to reveal her best friend and tag team partner, "Beauty Star" Angel Saito. Angel wears a black, halter top and matching, skin tight, leather jeans. Her black hair falls past her shoulders in a cascade of curls, framing her lovely face. In a swift motion, the two models squeal and immediately embrace, once more reuniting The Goddesses of Beauty!) Princess: Angel! Angel: Mina! (A few more minutes of girlish happiness pass before the two women finally part, each getting the chance to get a good look at the other.) Princess: (eyeing Angel's hair with a grin) You cut your hair! It looks nice. Angel: You think so? I was really skeptical about it but my stylist is always urging me to (in a fake and very bad, English accent) "live in the moment, darling". (Yamaguchi giggles as Angel resumes speaking in her natural voice) Besides, the men love it. Wow! Did you lose weight? You look awesome! (Yamaguchi does a slight twirl, showing off her curvaceous figure.) Princess: I've increased my work outs and added new routines to my training regimen. The GDWA is a tough place and I want to make sure that I am up to the challenge. (Angel nods) Oh gosh! Where are my manners? Come in. (Yamaguchi steps aside as Angel enters the house, closing the door behind her. The two old friends plop down on Yamaguchi's couch, the Princess folding her legs Indian-style.) Princess: It is so great seeing you again! Angel: Same here. With our various schedules, it gets hard for us to get the chance to talk much less actually see each other. So, when I got this shoot in California, I knew that I had to come give my best friend a call! So, how is everything? How is Tony? Princess: (blushes a bit at the mention of her boyfriend) He's fine....we're both fine actually. (beams with happiness) Everything has been great. You know how much I love California. Plus, Arielle stops by all of the time to check up on me and see that I'm doing okay. You know that she's always seen me as the little sister that she never had. I've been working steadily as well. I have a contract in the works with Leo Cosmetics and I'll be doing Alexandria Parker's Paris show. What about you? Any special man in your life yet? Angel: (with a devilish grin) More like men. Princess: (laughs) Same old, Angel! Tell me all about BLAZE, Miss 3WU champion! When I heard that you had won BLAZE's world title, I was so proud. I always knew that you could do it. (Angel begins to tell the Princess about her recent successes in Akira Khan's BLAZE promotion.) Angel: And the next thing I knew, I was being crowned the 3WU champion. It was almost like a dream and so overwhelming! I almost thought that I would faint! But enough about me. I didn't come here to monopolize the conversation. I want to hear all about you. Tell me about the GDWA. Princess: (wide eyed and excited) Wow! There's so much talent in this federation. I mean, I've been in some great wrestling organizations before but none with such a great assemblage of talented wrestlers. I guess I can understand how you felt about winning the 3WU title because this feels almost intimidating and just like a dream. It's hard to believe that little Minako Yamaguchi, a mere rookie in this sport, is sharing the ring with some of the greatest women wrestlers in the entire world! Sometimes I have to pinch myself to make sure this is actually reality! (Angel laughs) In fact, my first match is in a few days. It's a first round match to determine a GDWA cruiserweight champion. Angel: (squeezing Yamaguchi's hand reassuringly) You'll do fine, Mina. I have complete and utter faith in you. (Yamaguchi sighs nervously) Besides, if you weren't talented, the GDWA wouldn't have signed you in the first place, right? Princess: I suppose... Angel: (grins) So, who's the unlucky opponent? Princess: Her name is Lida Yanigasawa. I've never heard of her before but, from what I've seen, she's rather arrogant with a disregard for the rules. And, if her interviews are any indication, she's also pretty mean and ruthless! Angel: (concern flashing in her brown eyes) You just be careful, okay? Someone like that can be a very dangerous opponent, especially for someone like you. It's no secret that you are all about honor and fair play. But, facing someone like that, maybe you need to change your ideals a bit. Princess: (shakes her head) No way! That is completely out of the question! I have too much respect and pride in myself and my abilities to resort to cheap tricks and dirty tactics! Besides, I would never lower myself to the standards of any rule breaker. Angel: I know but sometimes it helps to fight fire with fire. Princess: I have never taken short cuts in my career and I won't start now! There are always other ways to combat such tactics without taking that route as well. I just think too highly of myself to do something so....cheap. Besides, what would the fans think? When I beat Lida it will be with my natural skill and ability. Angel: Fine. I can see that you aren't going to change your mind. I'm just telling you, Mina, that it's getting rough out there and you're dealing with people that don't just want to win a match. Some of them are out to end your career. Things aren't just black and white any more. Sometimes you have to beat them at their own game. Princess: I appreciate the advice but it just isn't for me. Angel: (sighs) I understand. Good luck with this Yanigasawa chick although I'm sure that you can take her! (Angel winks as Yamaguchi grins) I'm pretty sure that you can take everybody in the whole damn tournament! Princess: Well, I am confident in my abilities.... Angel: As you should be! Besides, you're my tag partner and I don't tag with scrubs. (she smirks) Even if I did have to carry your butt in a few of our matches.... Princess: (with a playful smile) You wish! (With that, Yamaguchi grabs a pillow and bops Angel in the head with it.) Angel: Not the hair! Anything but the hair! (The two young women giggle as the scene fades to black.) =================================== Dante' Handles more women wrestlers than you can shake a stick at. "Alluring" Arielle Starr (UWF, WWO, IWWA), "Beauty Star" Angel Saito (BLAZE), Princess Yamaguchi (GDWA), The Babe Squad (BRA), and a few others that escape me.....

Part 2

 Thanks alot for the critique and comments, ellie:-) Maybe that's what bothered me. Yamaguchi is such a pure face and still a rookie that I tend to write her way too nice and friendly. Which really stands out with all of the tough women in the GDWA. Sometimes it's hard to believe that she's actually a wrestler. Her personality doesn't seem right for the sport. Thanks again for the criticisms. They gave me something to think about and were a great help. Dante'

Part 3

What bothered you about this RP? It seems okay although a bit girlish. These women are in a rough business/sport and need to show it. This makes it sound like it's all peaches and cream. It probably isn't in their character, but they need to be a bit rougher and more cocky towards their opponents. I used to write matches for the GDWA about three years ago. I'm sorry I stopped, but I had other commitments that took up my time. I hope this helps, ellie
