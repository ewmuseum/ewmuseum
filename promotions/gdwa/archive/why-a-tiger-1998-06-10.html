Why a tiger?

10 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Why a Tiger? Informal article by Curt Crane Hey, y'all!! CC here! I've always wondered how other handlers chose to create their particular wrestlers. For example, look at Officer Order. Why a face? Why a cop? Was their some person he modeled his character after? It interests me enough, that I've approached Lawrence with the idea of starting articles about that very subject. So, since I've run out of steam for the night on part II of the "Hardcore Tiger" article, I figured I'd share with . . . anyone who's listening :) why I decided to create, Lady Tiger. First off, the story goes back a little ways to the DPW/ICW days. I had my Phoenix Squad (a male stable) in full swing at the time :) Just getting into e-wrestling, really. Hey, do you want some irony? :) My first ever e-wrestling match was "The Fox" Curt Crane (named him after myself, what a rookie I was! :) vs. El Bandito, which was the first ever e-wrestling match of GDWA handler [name withheld]!! (Ma Porter, Dementia Praedox). For the record books, I kicked his ass! ;) But anyway, I was in full newbie mode, reading every inch of the cards. And the one thing that impressed me the most, though, was that female division. It well out-shined the rest of the card! Ma Porter ruled! :) but, this was back in my early days, so I didn't enter a character for it. When I finally decided to get my stuff together and enter something (I forget what, but it was just a horrible character :) . . . . the fed folded! D'oh!! I finally caught a hold of the [name withheld] man (ICW fed head) many months later, and asked him what became of the female division? They went off on their own, apparently, and he didn't have a homepage for me; D'OH!! Well, fux! :) After a little investigative reporting, I *finally* tracked down Lawrence, and begged to be put on the mailing list! :) Finally, GDWA in my mailing box, each week! :) My favorite wrestler quickly became the GDWA prankster, Charlotte LaMancha (so Charlotte! :) I thought it was just some sensational roleplaying!! Anyway, after a month or two, it's not enough for me to be sitting on the sidelines; I gotta join!! :) S I ask Lawrence, but there's no room on the roster, AW MAN! He gives me the addy to another female fed, but I decide to wait until someday where the GDWA might need a new wrestler. Another few weeks, and that day comes! *Yes*!! You see, I still had my Phoenix Squad going full swing, and I'd alluded many times that the mastermind of the Squad, Fara Phoenix, had some past wrestling experience. I was *dying* to have her in the GDWA!! The consummate heel who never actually cheats!! (In hindsight, I'm glad it didn't turn out that way; she woulda been too much like Micki Duran) So, Lawrence gives me the OK and tells me that he has a spot open for . . . . . . a face. Great. Not only does that screw Fara from joining, but I've never roleplayed a face, *ever*!! Why bother, heels were so much more fun and easier? No limits to what heels could do, and you don't have to worry about keeping an image like you do a face. But, I sure the hell wasn't gonna pass the opportunity up, waiting just under a year to get it! So I told Lawrence to hold that spot, and I'd have something by the end of the week. Great, a face. Now what? Well, I took a quick inventory of what the GDWA had and had not. I noticed that there were no masked wrestlers in the circuit, which intrigued me a bit. Might get a bit of attention. So what could I use for my gimmic? I don't know how many of you guys follow Japanese feds; it's hard as *hell* for me to in Canada, but I do a passable job through the net and magazines. Anyway, there was this cartoon Strip in Japan, which went by the name of "Tiger Mask". A cartoon about wrestling, basicallike :) And at sometime early 80's-ish I believe, somebody actually debuted under the name "Tiger Mask", defeating the Dynamite Kid in his first match. He's since retired and in the HOF right now (his real name was "Saturu Sayama"), but others later went on and carried on the gimmic; there was a second Tiger Mask, then he unmasked, and later a third, etc. Tiger Mask; well, I had my inspiration! :) So this character would have a tiger mask during her matches. OK, now what style should I use? I've had heavyweights, technicians, martial artists, bralwers, everything created during my e- wrestling tenure, but never a high flier. OK, that's a start! :) A high-flying, tiger masked lady. Lady, huh? How about "Lady Tiger"? Hey, it's got a nice ring to it! :) She didn't seem to fit as an American, and Tiger Mask was a foreigner as well, so might as well do the same with her. But not Japan, that's too similar. Hmm, I do speak French, and I do like Charlotte's character . . why not France? I could say she came over idolizing Charlotte!! I didn't know how well this would work out eventually; when Charlotte retired, I was quick to ask if he'd mind me having her as a manager! The French duo, what a perfect combo! :) As I developed her, I tried to mold her into Charlotte's successor. The pranks on Sally, stuff like that, and it was all *fun* to rp!! :) I couldn't believe it, but I was rping a face, and I *loved* it!! I've still got the Phoenix Squad kicking around, but Tiger is my new #1 favorite in town :) She's the only one of my creations (only 2 active anymore) I actually "mark out" for during matches :) And that, as they say, is history. Anyone who reads this and feels like sharing your story, send it in! I'm always interested! :) So till next time, you ain't got the power, unless you got some **TIGER POWER!!** :)
