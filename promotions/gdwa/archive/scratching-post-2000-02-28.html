GDWA Scratching Post: a workrate report on the Tuesday Night Catfight of 18 January

dated 28 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Scratching Post So, a friend of mine has decided to get me hooked up with GDWA tapes, since he knows I am a mark for good women's wrestling, which remains an utter rarity on this continent. So without any further ado: GDWA Tuesday Night Catfight - January 18 (or : Hey, we don't need no backstage skits.) We are at the Key Arena in Seattle, Washington, and the place is jam- packed. They get cute on us by starting off with "Puff the Magic Dragon" and then go into what is essentially the GDWA theme song, "Tom Sawyer" by Rush. (Can't be too mad at that, Chris Page had a version of it as his entrance music once.) We re-introduce the announcers, and I know there is a tie to the Dawg Pound thing, but honestly, "Spud McKenzie"? I remember those commercials, this guy needs to change his name, not steal Buffer's act, and move on. And "Sam Mutt"? Oh well, at least Allen Bishop has a normal name, even if the chyron tells me he spells it weird. They run down the card a bit, and we are off to ***"Rich Bitch" Andrea Chandler & Black Lotus vs. Rekka Sakura & Marissa Monet*** They hype that Chandler and Lotus are former champs. Monet and Sakura enter as "The Grand Dragons". Monet enters on piggyback on Sakura, drops off and jokes that Rekka can't "carry" her. Wow. Way to alienate people by playing to the smarts. We've got two heels, basically a 'tweener, and a face. Should be interesting. Monet picks up a chair someone conveniently forgot in the ring and pastes McKenzie as a message to Dunbar. Bishop gets bonus points from me by noting that the crowd cheered the pointless shot to McKenzie. VP Leufroy comes out and threatens Monet. Damn, she's a big woman. Lotus and Rekka start to actually give us a match up. Mutt brings up that Lotus never really lost the belt. Lotus starts strong with basic, but sharp, takedowns. Rekka gets beat on a bit, tags in Monet. Cute double team bit where Monet does the military press on Lotus and Rekka follows with the frog splash. I lose all respect for Matsuda as an official as he counts the pin, which gets two, and then realizes Rekka isn't the legal woman, and ushers her out. Andrea and Rekka show us a bit, both teams with a bunch of tags, and eventually Rekka becomes face in Peril, even though she isn't legal. Lotus and Chandler look good dismantling her. Sakura kicks out of a neat pin attempt and hits the Liger bomb to set up the race for tag. Leufroy cuts off Heidi Leick as she comes down to interfere. I like how the fans pop for moves regardless of face/heel status. Lotus cinches in the Indian Death Lock on Monet and it gets broken up by Sakura. Pier 4 breaks out, Monet hits a gourdbuster on Lotus and goes to the top. The crowd shows it is American by yelling Mon-E! instead of Mon-Ay. Six-foot, five-inch moonsault off the top and Monet gets the pin on Lotus. Good match. Brawl breaks out after the bell. Leufroy tries to break it up and Sakura nails him by accident (?) but nothing comes of it. We move along quickly to ***Lida Yanagisawa against Princess Yamaguchi*** They hype Yanagisawa having ties to the Yakuza when she enters the ring. Yamaguchi is TALL for a cruiserweight woman. Thin, too. She looks too much like a model, not enough like a wrestler for my tastes. Yanagisawa starts quick, dropkicking Yamaguchi before the bell. Fast paced first 5 minutes as Yanagisawa slowly takes control with a dropkick to the knee. Both of these women have some stiff-ass kicks and they just abuse each other with them. Yanagisawa gets busted open on a kick, then clips the leg of Yamaguchi as the Princess tries for an enzuigiri. Yanagisawa flashes a bloody-lipped smile to the crowd. Nice touch. Back to the knee. Nice psychology in this match. SWANK suicide tope from Yanagisawa takes them both out, and I am reminded that the 20-count rule is in effect for cruiserweight matches. Yamaguchi catches a moonsault press, spins it into a tilt-a-whirl death-valley driver. (Isn't that a burning hammer?) Gorgeous move, full props. Yanagisawa gets a foot in the ropes on 2, and the crowd agrees with me in not believing it. Michinoku driver to set up… a moonsault stomp? What a stupid move. Especially if your opponent worked the knee all match. Apparently, psychology doesn't matter though as Yanagisawa goes up top and misses a 450 splash. Yamaguchi hits the cradle suplex, but only for two. An axe kick gets 2. They announce 30 seconds and Yamaguchi goes for a front facelock. (why?) Nice counter, reverse sequence sets Yamaguchi up to hit the Dragon Suplex and my watch says time already expired. Yanagisawa slips out, and handstands into a victory roll and gets the pin. They announce the win as 14:23, so Spud must've been wrong and meant to yell out "one minute, 30 seconds." Good match except for the time screw up, and that they built the attack on the knee the whole time and then ignored it in the finish. Yamaguchi bows to Yanagisawa, who returns the bow, and then slaps her in the face! HA! I like Yanagisawa. We move right to *** Bloody Mary & "The Showgirl" Anais Exotica vs. "The Machine" Amanda Reese and Rachael "Ice" Edwards*** All 4 women enter separately, playing up on the whole "do any of them trust each other" angle. Reese is in full face mode here, I think she's just happy to be in front of fans who understand wrestling. Vonya joins the announce team for this match, picks on Reese and harasses Exotica for the over-the top entrance. Edwards and Mary start off, and Mary uses the power game. Mary hits the big move, Exotica comes in and stomps. Then proceeds to be completely taken apart by Edwards. Mary comes back in and Edwards takes her apart, too. The fans chant "boring" at all of Edwards' rest holds, and then Reese tags in and comes off the TOP?? Wow, she's getting daring late in her career, she almost never comes off the top. Reese and Mary trade some high impact, neckbreaker by Mary and Exotica wants in. She comes in… and punches. What the hell happened to Exotica? One year ago she's trading moves with Reese in a 30-minute classic and now she is doing 1 minute jobs in the WWO and has an offensive repertoire of kicks and punches. How sad. Reese takes Exotica's head off with a clothesline and proceeds to take "The Showgirl" apart. Eventually Reese and Edwards beat on Mary. Exotica refuses to tag until Mary hits a big running powerslam on Reese. Mary has had enough of that and throws Exotica into the ring where -- guess what? -- she gets killed by Reese and Edwards. At least she can still sell like a champ. Mary won't tag in. Exotica flips out of a backdrop and blind tags her in. Edwards keeps releasing pins and showing off when she could probably put Mary away. Now Exotica won't tag in. Edwards lets a figure four go and Reese has had enough, so SHE blind tags in on Edwards. Reese sets for the Dragon Suplex but Edwards kicks her in the back of the head. Reese falls forward, Mary rolls through and gets the 3! Ugly match, never seemed to hit a flow. It was like an indy fed version of a Japanese storyline match. Mary's a better brawler than that, and Reese did almost nothing. Edwards came out looking really good, but at the expense of the others. Post match, both Mary and Edwards stomp away on Reese. Set up, or an alliance of convenience? Exotica powders out, since she apparently doesn't merit an angle. Lady Tiger hits the ring for the save and she and Reese do the "face bump/hand shake" thing that went out in the 80's but still is kinda cool. ***"Jumping" Jen Grier vs Lady Tiger*** Best friends forced to compete! As old-school as you get. How old *IS* Tiger anyway? Sonya Blade comes to ringside as another guest commentator now that Vonya left. This is all Lady Tiger, who pretty much humiliates Grier by completely wrestling circles around her. Eventually Grier breaks the momentum and asks for a handshake. Anyone who doesn't see what coming gets sent to their room without supper. A slap in the face to Tiger when she goes for the shake and ladies and gentlemen, we have a heel turn. Doesn't help, as Tiger goes back to kicking her ass. (Or rather, just out wrestling her, since Tiger isn't exactly the ass-kicking type. Blade screws up and calls Grier Charlotte. Grier gets a bit of a comeback, but it is still pretty much all Tiger. Tiger in control, goes for a bodyslam, and in a tribute to the old school, Grier pulls an inside cradle out of nowhere on it for the 3. The tights look hooked, but Grier denies it after the match. La Mancha takes exception, and Tiger tries to stop the inevitable fight by taking out La Mancha! Doesn't help as Grier cements her heel status by attacking the downed manager. Blade confuses the hell out of me by saying that this means Grier faces Rachel Edwards in the next round. What, did Edwards get a bye? Which reminds me, are we going to see brackets for this tournament? After all, there are 9 cruisers, which means one must not be in the tourney, unless a bunch got byes (like Edwards apparently) and no one told us. ***Zaranna vs. Originality.*** Zaranna then struts to the ring to deliver one of the worst interviews ever, where she proceeds to demonstrate she has no originality at all as she lifts other wrestler's schtick verbatim. The GDWA crowd proves it isn't as smart as it thinks it is, and sings along anyway. The gist is that she thinks Andrea Chandler is dateless and a lesbian, which Zaranna mispronounces as Thesbian. Zaranna also apparently doesn't know what a vibrator is, calling it a vibration. which is pretty pathetic. So after doing an interminable interview running down a major heel in the fed, she then says that Andrea sucks, Lanny Manson sucks, and that she will be the champ. The crowd then gives her hell heat, because apparently they are morons. ***Officer Order vs. "Diamond Girl" Heidi Leick*** Order comes in first, and the commentators note she doesn't get the pop she once did. Leick comes down with a Singapore cane, a 7-inch height advantage, and a bad attitude. She starts right off on Order and the match is underway. The fans (outside of the hardcore Dawg Pound) chant for puppies, further proving they are morons with no respect for wrestling talent. The fight spills to the outside, and down come Andrea Chandler and Lady Tiger. (Wow, Tiger's active tonight.) Heidi hits a gourdbuster on the outside, past the mats. Andrea follows with a piledriver. Why the match didn't end on a DQ right there I don't know. Tiger comes in to neutralize Chandler. The refs check Order and then stupidly allow the match to continue. Order gets stomped on and her neck is obviously in bad shape. Heidi abandons the neck and works the leg, laughing like a maniac. Charlotte La Mancha comes down as Chandler and Tiger fight on the floor. She eats fist, and then Tiger eats a wicked-looking jackhammer onto the ring steps. I love Chandler when she is in the zone. Order keeps coming back, as time starts to run out. Again, they misquote the time, because too much happens. Good psychology as Order can't hold a Northern Lights Suplex bridge at announced 30 seconds to go. Or she's legit after a neckbreaker hurt. Order goes up for the swandive splash and hits for 2. Leick fights back and we're well past 30 seconds. Leick gets the advantage but hits the powerbomb after the bell. 15-minute draw. As if enough other people weren't here, Marissa Monet comes down and helps Order up. And then, of course, slaps a Kataha-jime on her. She screams at Dunbar, continuing her obsession with his comments about her. Leick comes back after they escort Marissa out and hits a Fisherman's Buster onto the chair in case you thought Order wasn't hurt on the neck enough already. Order does the stretcher job, and the refs overturn the decision and give Order the win by DQ. We got to the LEGEND'S MATCH ***Zaranna vs. "Fear Factory" Lanny Manson*** Mutt immediately shows he is an idiot by calling this feud the greatest feud in women's wrestling *today*, even though it is billed as a Legend's match, as in *yesterday*. Zaranna owns it early, slowing it to a crawl with technical stuff. Manson comes back. This is VERY old school, but mostly in a good way. Then, too annoy me, Andrea Chandler comes down to ringside. It's a LEGEND'S MATCH! Another blow off of an old, classic feud, GO AWAY CHANDLER! Crescent kick from Manson, and you know this match is going to hell when Chandler pulls the ref out on 2. Manson goes out to fight with Chandler, Zaranna pulls out a foreign object from her boot. Who booked this crap? Zaranna hits with it, but doesn't go for the cover right away, and then loses the chance. Why give these two 30 minutes? They can't fill it up anymore. End comes as Manson gets the momentum, goes to the ropes and eats a chair in the back from Chandler. One DDT from Zaranna and she gets the three. WHY SCREW AROUND WITH A LEGEND'S MATCH?? So annoying. Andrea hits a Diamond Drill on Manson, and then beats on Zaranna and nails her with one, too. So, I guess this is to establish that the new GDWA top heel is Chandler? Put her over two legends? She was already over, didn't need to do it. A fan storms the barricade and nails Chandler with a chair, and then some moves. She pulls back her hood to reveal *Daisy Butterfly*! She hugs Manson as Chandler runs off. Everything old is new again. Show ends. FINAL WORD: So nice to see nothing but wrestling. Only one big interview, from Zaranna, and it sucked horribly, but she and Manson are hopefully only here for one night, so we don't need to worry about it. And, at least it made some attempt to justify the Chandler run on later, even if Chandler mostly attacked Manson. It will take some time to establish new feuds and such, but some seem to be coming out. Obviously they're building around Chandler and Tiger, since they seemed to be out in several matches. It looks like an Edwards/Mary alliance and a Tiger/Reese one is forming, but how that fits with the Tiger/Grier storyline is anyone's guess. Chandler and Leick seem allied, and Monet is going heel as well. Order and Sakura seem to be the top babyfaces right under Tiger. They really need to put up a bracket for the Cruiserweight tournament, since Edwards getting a bye seems completely screwy. Until the next time, Cal Go Back To Handler's Page
