Lady Tiger merchandise

9 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AWWWWWWWWWWWWWW!!!! Come on! Do you really want to go around wearing the merchandise of 'Sexy' Sal, or Medusa rage? Or . . . OR!! Would you rather be wearing the duds, of the craziest cat ever to grace a GDWA ring . . . LADY TIGER!! Yeah, I thought so! So show the world what a cool cat you are, and wear Lady tiger merchandise!! Plus, a portion of all profits goes to helping orphanages around the world! Sugoi!! T-Shirts Lady Tiger "Tiger 3:16" T-shirt: A specially made t-shirt to celebrate the masked one knocking down GDWA Mafioso Ma Porter. On the front, "Tiger 3:16", and on the back, "I just knocked you down!" Lady Tiger "Cat Warrior" T-shirt: Front, picture of Lady Tiger posing with a "V" pose. back, "She's a super-duper cat warrior!" Lady Tiger/ Charlotte "Grrr-eat!" T-shirt: Front, photo of Lady tiger and Charlotte, arms over each others backs, smiling at the camera. back, "They're grrrr-eat!" Accessories & Toys Lady Tiger "Tiger Mask": A near-replica of the furry Tiger Mask worn by everyone's favorite high flyer, Lady Tiger! (kid sizes only) "Tiger Tail": Join the legions of fans who show their support to Lady Tiger by waving your very own "Tiger Tail" at the next live GDWA event! (value priced) Plush Tiger: Nothing says "cute" like this adorable little plush tiger! A copy of the tiger given to Curtis Revolution by Lady Tiger Lady Tiger action figure: Chosen from the line of GDWA poseable action figures. Moving arms and legs. Collect them all, and pit your favorite GDWA superstars against each other!! Also available are: Medusa Rage Officer May Order Daisy Burtterfly Keiko Mita Andrea Chandler "The Legend" Micki Duran 'Golden Girl' Sierra Browne Lady Tiger bumper stickers: "ICE CREAM!!" (w/ picture of a Tiger mask) "Proud member of the Tiger Pound!" "Super-Duper Cat Warrior!"
