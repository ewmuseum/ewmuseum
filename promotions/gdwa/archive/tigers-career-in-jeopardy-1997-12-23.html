Tiger's career in jeopardy

23 December 1997

TIGER'S CAREER IN JEOPARDY!! Doctors suggest that Lady Tiger retire from professional wrestling After several months of rehabilitation efforts with Lady Tiger, doctors in Bordeaux, France suggested that Lady Tiger announce her official retirement. "Any other solution would endanger the life and future of Lady Tiger," Dr. Malcolm Munroe stated this past Monday in a press release. "We're only looking out for the future of Lady Tiger." Tiger's problems occurred at the GDWA's recent Fall Moonsault PPV. The headliner of this card pitted four separate woman in an "Ironwoman" contest; in which they would have to wrestle three separate matches that night. "The very nature of the tournament itself exerts a great amount of fatigue on all participants," states Jane Wattinson, a woman who is appealing the GDWA to ban the annual tournament. "This makes for a working environment even less safe than regular bouts, and jeopardizes the careers of each woman involved." Ms. Wattinson uses this past card as her evidence. Lady Tiger, in a match-up with Medusa Rage, was Rage-Slammed off the top rope, a move which broke several disks in her back upon impact. The contest quickly ended as security ad medical personnel rushed down the aisle to tend to the fallen Tiger. She was moved immediately to a local hospital and, two days later, sent to Bordeaux, France where she could be monitored by back specialists and recuperate in her hometown. Wattinson also questions Medusa's motives for attempting a move so dangerous. "If you ask me," she commented, "Medusa was trying to end that girl's career, just to solidify her reputation in the wrestling community." Others don't see it that way. Dr. Munroe noted, "I'm an avid fan of both the GDWA and Lady Tiger, and was watching the tape of the Fall Moonsault. Medusa was obviously distraught after the contest, and seemed to want to help in any way she could." But, as Dr. Munroe reminded us, tiger may be beyond help. "We've gotten her walking again, but a return to wrestling is out of the question. All it would take is one missed move for Tiger to be sitting in a wheelchair for the rest of her life." However, Munroe said he is heartened by the resolve and concern of Tiger's fans. "Fans from all over the world are sending letters and get well cards. They aren't pushing her to get back in the ring, they just want to see her pull through it all right." Dr. Munroe also stated that a few GDWA superstars have tried to contact Tiger as well. "Charlotte LaMancha has been constantly be her side during Tiger's rehabilitation. Jennifer Grier calls here every few days to check up on how Tiger is progressing, and several other superstars have voiced their concerns as well." GDWA president Denmark Vessey hinted that should Tiger be unable to compete in the GDWA, a career as a broadcaster or journalist in the GDWA would be virtually guaranteed. Mr Vessey also sent his best wishes for a speedy recovery. Charlotte LaMancha and Lady Tiger are choosing not to speak to the press at the moment.
