World Heavyweight Championship Medusa Rage defeated Officer Order via pinfall on May 19, 1998 in Oakland, California at the Oakland Colisseum. Black Lotus defeated Medusa Rage via submission in a Scientific Submission rules match on August 30, 1998 at the Municipal Stadium in Cleveland, Ohio during the Founder's Day Tradition II pay per view. 1997 World Heavyweight champions X. Western Heritage Championship 'Jungle' Radhi Ananda over 'Kyoto Crippler' Keiko Mita via pinfall on June 16, 1998 in Seattle, Washington at the Key Arena. 'Golden Girl' Sierra Browne over 'Jungle' Radhi Ananda via pinfall on July 28, 1998 in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania at the Pittsburgh Civic Arena. 'La Femme Nikita' Nikita Marx over 'Golden Girl' Sierra Browne in a "Throw in the Towel" match on July 28, 1998 in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania at the Pittsburgh Civic Arena. 'Ultima' Sarah Summers over 'La Femme Nikita' Nikita Marx via submission on August 04, 1998 at the Palace in Auburn Hills, Michigan. Daisy Butterfly defeated 'Ultima' Sarah Summers via submission to unify the Western Heritage and Intercontinental championships. She defeated Summers on August 30, 1998 in Cleveland, Ohio at the Municipal Stadium during Founder's Day Tradition II. 1997 Western Heritage champions III. Ironwoman Square Dance Tournament 'The Franchise' Daisy Butterfly crowned on September 1, 1996 in Charlotte, North Carolina at the Fall Moonsault pay per view at the Carolinas Stadium. Andrea Chandler was crowned on November 10, 1997 in Toronto, Canada at the Sky Dome at the Fall Moonsault 1997 pay per view. Awards, Tournaments, and other Decorations IV. Wrestler of the Month Award 1998 Award Recipient For the Months of March and April "Kyoto Crippler" Keiko Mita was awarded for defending her Western Heritage title and an incredible string of victories, including one over World Champion Officer Order. 1998 Award Recipient For the Months of May and June Medusa Rage was awarded for winning the World title from Officer May Order. 1997 Wrestler of the Month Award Recipients V. Lanny Manson Fan Favorite Award Lanny Manson and Daisy Butterfly voted by GDWA fans on June 16, 1996. Daisy Butterfly and Officer Order voted by GDWA fans on January 1, 1997. Daisy Butterfly and Officer Order voted by GDWA fans on May 28, 1997. Officer Order was voted by GDWA fans on March 22, 1998. VI. Wrestler of the Year Award Award Recipient For the Year of 1996-97 Andrea Chandler was awarded for her 6 month title reign and her two Wrestler of the Month awards. Award Recipient For the Year of 1997-98 'Franchise' Daisy Butterfly was awarded for rejuvenating her career and her incredible 7 month Intercontinental title reign. VII. Concurrent Championships Bi-titled champions Cruiserweight champion 'Golden Girl' Sierra Browne became the first multi-titled champion in GDWA history. She defeated Officer Order via pinfall on May 5, 1997 in Indianapolis, Indiana at Market Square Arena to win the Western Heritage championship. Cruiserweight champion 'Golden Girl' Sierra Browne became a two time multi-titled champion on July 28, 1998. She defeated 'Jungle' Radhi Ananda via pinfall in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania at the Pittsburgh Civic Arena to win the Western Heritage championship. Tri-titled champions Cruiserweight and Western Heritage champion 'Golden Girl' Sierra Browne became the first tri-titled champion in GDWA history. She defeated Daisy Butterfly via pinfall on September 10, 1997 in Los Angelos, California at the Great Western Forum to win the Intercontinental championship. Intercontinental champion Daisy Butterfly became the second tri-titled champion in GDWA history. She defeated 'Ultima' Sarah Summers via submission, to unify the Western Heritage and Intercontinental titles, inorder to be crowned the innaugural AMERICAN HERITAGE CHAMPION. She defeated Summers on August 30, 1998 in Cleveland, Ohio at the Municipal Stadium during Founder's Day Tradition II. Retired Heavyweight Championships VIII. American Heritage Championship Daisy Butterfly defeated 'Ultima' Sarah Summers via submission to become the innaugural champion. She defeated Summers on August 30, 1998 in Cleveland, Ohio at the Municipal Stadium during Founder's Day Tradition II. II. Transnational Cruiserweight Championship 'Golden Girl' Sierra Browne defeated 'Kyoto Crippler' Keiko Mita via pinfall to become our innaugural Transnational Cruiserweight Champion. She defeated Keiko Mita in the Cruiserweight Championship Tournament Finals on July 22, 1997 in Baltimore, Maryland at the US Air Arena. The Cruiserweight title is vacated on August 30, 1998 at the Municipal Stadium in Cleveland, Ohio during the Founder's Day Tradition II pay per view. IX. INTERCONTINENTAL Championship Daisy Butterfly innaugurated as GDWA Intercontinental champion on January 18, 1998 at the Miyagi-ken Sports Center in Sendai, Japan. The title is formally changed from the Internet Heavyweight Championship to the Intercontinental Championship. Daisy Butterfly defeated 'Ultima' Sarah Summers via submission to unify the Western Heritage and Intercontinental championships. She defeated Summers on August 30, 1998 in Cleveland, Ohio at the Municipal Stadium during Founder's Day Tradition II. 1997 Intercontinental champions Back to Main Page
