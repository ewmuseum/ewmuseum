Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance 1997 Lineages Titles World Heavyweight Championship Intercontinental Championship Western Heritage Championship Cruiserweight Championship World Tag Team Championship Awards, Tournaments, and other Decorations Wrestler of the Month Award Lanny Manson Fan Favorite Award Square Dance Tournament: Ironwoman Crowning Wrestler of the Year Award Concurrent Championships I.World Heavyweight Championship 'Fear Factory' Lanny Manson defeated Ma Porter via submission to become our first World Champion. She defeated Ma Porter in the DPW/ICW Women's World Championship Tournament Finals on April 5, 1996 in Oakland, California at the Oakland Coliseum. 'Fear Factory' Lanny Manson innaugurated as GDWA World Champion on April 21, 1996 in Oakland, California at the Oakland Coliseum. Zaranna defeated 'Fear Factory' Lanny Manson via pinfall on May 25, 1996 in Tacoma, Washington at the Summer Supercard pay per view held in the Tacoma Dome. Zaranna stripped of the World Title due to contract breachment on July 20, 1996 in the GDWA front offices in Walnut Creek, California. Andrea Chandler defeated 'Fear Factory' Lanny Manson via pinfall in the finals of our World Championship Tournament held on October 12, 1996 in Fukuoka, Japan at the Fukuoka Dome. Dementia Praecox defeated Andrea Chandler via pinfall on March 17, 1997 in Cleveland, Ohio at the Founder's Day Tradition pay per view at the Municipal Stadium. Officer Order defeated Dementia Praecox and Sierra Browne in a Three Way Dance via pinfall and disqualification (respectively) on July 29, 1997 in Oakland, California at the Oakland Coliseum Arena. II.INTERCONTINENTAL Championship Demonica over Valkyrie via pinfall during the GDWA Internet Championship Tournament Finals held on May 25, 1996 in Tacoma, Washington during the Summer Supercard pay per view in the Tacoma Dome. Charlotte La Mancha over Demonica via submission on September 28, 1996 in Portland, Oregon at the Rose Garden. Charlotte La Mancha stripped due to contract breachment on February 4, 1997 in the GDWA front offices in Walnut Creek, California. Staci X eliminates Medusa Rage during the Internet Heavyweight Championship Royal Rumble on February 15, 1997 in Seattle, Washington at the Key Arena. Dementia Praecox over Staci X via submission on February 22, 1997 in San Francisco, California at the Cow Palace. 'Legend' Micki Duran over Dementia Praecox via pinfall on March 17, 1997 in Cleveland, Ohio at the Founder's Day Tradition pay per view at the Municipal Stadium. Daisy Butterfly over Micki Duran via submission on August 26, 1997 in San Francisco, California at the Cow Palace. Sierra Browne over Daisy Butterfly via pinfall on September 10, 1997 in Los Angelos, California at the Great Western Forum. Daisy Butterfly defeated Micki Duran and Sierra Brown in a Three Way Dance to become champion on November 10, 1997 in Toronto, Canada at the Sky Dome at the Fall Moonsault 1997. III.Western Heritage Championship 'Dangerous Queen' Sachie Yokoyama eliminates 'Jungle' Radhi Ananda during the Western Heritage Championship Royal Rumble on May 25, 1996 in Tacoma, Washington at Summer Supercard pay per view in the Tacoma Dome. Officer Order over 'The Dangerous Queen' Sachie Yokoyama via pinfall on June 29, 1996 in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania at The Spectrum. 'Dangerous Queen' Sachie Yokoyama KO's Officer Order on September 1, 1996 in Charlotte, North Carolina at the Fall Moonsault pay per view at the Carolinas Stadium. 'Jungle' Radhi Ananda over 'The Dangerous Queen' Sachie Yokoyama via pinfall on September 14, 1996 in San Francisco, California at the Cow Palace. Officer Order over 'Jungle' Radhi Ananda via pinfall on March 21, 1997 in Cleveland, Ohio at the Founder's Day Tradition pay per view at the Municipal Stadium. 'Golden Girl' Sierra Browne over Officer Order via pinfall on May 5, 1997 in Indianapolis, Indiana at Market Square Arena. 'Golden Girl' forced to vacate the title on October 1, 1997 after winning the Internet title. Keiko Mita eliminates 'Yukon' Jane during the Western Heritage Royal Rumble to become Champion on November 10, 1997 in Toronto, Canada at the Sky Dome at the Fall Moonsault 1997. V.World Tag Team Titles Misfits defeated Lady Luck via pinfall to become innaugural Champions on October 26, 1996 in Osaka Castle Hall in Osaka, Japan. The Hyena Queens defeated the MISFITS via exiting the steel cage on June 7, 1997 in Madison Square Garden in New York City, New York. The Hyena Queens stripped of the World Tag Team titles after the division is held up on September 26th, 1997 in GDWA offices in Walnut Creek, California. VI.Wrestler of the Month Award 1996 Award Recipiencts for the Months of April & May Zaranna was awarded for capturing the World Heavyweight title and her incredible winning streak up to her match against Lanny Manson. Sachie Yokoyama for her incredible improvement as a wrestler & for winning the WESTERN HERITAGE Royal Rumble to become the inaugural champion. 1996 Award Recipient For the Months of June and July Charlotte La Mancha was awarded for her undefeated streak since May 25, 1996 and for two big wins in a row over Ma Porter. 1996 Award Recipient For the Months of October and November Andrea Chandler was awarded for going on to the finals of the WORLD TITLE tournament and defeating Lanny Manson to become the new World Champion. 1997 Award Recipient For the Months of January and February Andrea Chandler was awarded for Retaining the World title for 5 months and going undefeated for the months of January and February. 1997 Award Recipients For the Months of March and April Dementia Praecox was awarded for capturing the World title 'Golden Girl' Sierra Browne for defeating Officer Order twice in a row and for winning the Western Heritage Title. 1997 Award Recipient For the Months of June and July 'Kyoto Crippler' Keiko Mita was awarded for her success in the Transfederational Cruiserweight Tournament despite a nagging knee injury. 1997 Award Recipient For the Months of August and September 'Double O' Officer Order was awarded for winning the World title in a Triple Threat match. 1997 Award Recipients For the Months of November and December Daisy Butterfly was awarded for successfully regaining the Intercontinental Championsip. Andrea Chandler was awarded for winning the Ironwoman tournament at Fall Moonsault '97. I.Concurrent Championships Bi-titled champions Cruiserweight champion 'Golden Girl' Sierra Browne became the first multi-titled champion in GDWA history. She defeated Officer Order via pinfall on May 5, 1997 in Indianapolis, Indiana at Market Square Arena to win the Western Heritage championship. Cruiserweight champion 'Golden Girl' Sierra Browne became a two time multi-titled champion on July 28, 1998. She defeated 'Jungle' Radhi Ananda via pinfall in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania at the Pittsburgh Civic Arena to win the Western Heritage championship. Tri-titled champions Cruiserweight and Western Heritage champion 'Golden Girl' Sierra Browne became the first tri-titled champion in GDWA history. She defeated Daisy Butterfly via pinfall on September 10, 1997 in Los Angelos, California at the Great Western Forum to win the Intercontinental championship.
