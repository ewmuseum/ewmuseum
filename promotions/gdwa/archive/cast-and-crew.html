Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance non player characters... This is the cast of our various GDWA productions. Get to know them, as they shall be helpful for your flashes and angles in your wrestling career. Alison De La Cruz Alison De La Cruz is the President and Chair of the GDWA Championship Committee. She is head of general operations and is the woman that keeps the various regional promoters under the GDWA umbrella. She tends to be a favored official amongst GDWA competitors who need some leverage in the front office. Kirby Leufroy Leufroy is the Vice President and head of International affairs; he is also a member of the Championship commitee. As the GDWA tours throughout the world, Leufroy's expertise with various cultures makes him perfect at this position. He tends to be a hardline conservative in the promotion and if you get his attention, he'll fine you! Mike Whalen Whalen is the behind the scenes guy. He does interviews and the dirty work the other suit and ties won't. Paul Laurence Dunbar Paul Laurence is the director of each official GDWA flash show. He oft times serves as moderator for his co-hosts as well as offering unbiased analysis on GDWA superstars. On special occasions, Paul Laurence conducts special interviews for pay per views, the GDWA magazine, or other such rare occassions. Allen Bishop Allen is your standard babyface announcer. He does play by play for the cards as well as commentary on the flash shows. Sam Mutt Sam Mutt is your standard heel announcer. He does color commentary for the cards as well as commentary on the flash shows. Sonya Blade Sonya is a former GDWA competitor turned announcer. She does analysis on pay per views while doubling as the Host of the GDWA Hotline. She is a babyface announcer that is less biased than Allen Bishop.
