GDWA sawn song . . .

26 September 1998 the post on Google Groups
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Grand Dragon family: The day has come. The Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance must now officially fold after 3 years of active service to the e-wrestling world.
I could never picture myself sending this message. You know, I pictured us going on for at least another 5 years before I'd have to hang up the "ropes".
My reasons are mathematical. While my desire is still the same-- especially after that great %%% Founder's Day Tradition II %%% pay per view where you guys busted out some great angles--my workload has increased. As a Phd student, I have nearly 2ce as much reading as
I did last year on top of the papers I grade every 2 weeks. I haven't even been on e-mail in the last few days. My apologies to all the newcomers who didn't get a chance to debut. My apologies also go out to the new/old champions who didn't get a chance to defend their belts...And to you old timers--especially the old school who've been with us for years--I can honestly say this has been one of the funnest experiences I've had in a long time. There's a lot of history between us and, after a while, the GDWA felt like a "real"
fed. We had title histories, feud histories, and in the realm of character development, you guys are the best. The quality of the fed was truly peaking.
What saddens me most is that I have a lot of ideas I was kicking around. My interest has not abated in the least, nor my energy, unfortunately. Politics hasn't ruined us either. (In some ways I think it'd be easier if we could blame "something" or "someone" for
Grand Dragon's demise.) I simply no longer possess the time.
Barring a miracle, like some handlers wanting to jump in as the new fed head, this is the swan song of old Grand Dragon.
Take care and stay in touch, Lawrence Davis former fed head Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance
