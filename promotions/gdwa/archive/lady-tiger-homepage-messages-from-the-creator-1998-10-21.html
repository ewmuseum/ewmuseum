Messages Wednesday, October 21st, 1998 Hey, a full month since my last update, that's kinda funny. Anyway, I chucked a picture of Lady Tiger up on the main page; yes, that is an actual picture of Lady Tiger, and not a picture of Candy Okutsu! Honest! Um, anyway . . . thanks go out to Michiko, who's page I (ahem) stole this from :) Oddly enough, Michiko is also the name of my Japanese teacher . . . um, sensei. Yeah, anyway, enjoy the pic! :) Monday, September 21st, 1998 Added a picture of Lady Tiger to her stats page. HUGE props go out to [name withheld], who also runs New Classic Championship Wrestling (props :) for taking the time to make it for me. Thanks [name withheld]! Wednesday, September 9th, 1998 Just moved up to my res in university, so haven't been too active lately. Still thinking of some good features; if anyone out there in tiger land has any ideas, lemme know! :) Small point, when you enter the page, try to come through tiger.html instead of tigermain. That way, not only will you update my counter, but you'll see a cute little animated gif of Muffins :) Speaking of that, added the Muffins flash to the archive. I'm gonna have lots of fun with this one :) Thursday, September 3rd, 1998 FDT2 comes and goes, and Lawrence continues to amaze with arguably his best PPV yet. God I love this fed! :) Updated Tiger's record, adding the two wins from FDT2. Updated the highlight reel, adding clips from her two FDT2 matches. Check out that Ice Cream plancha :) Sunday, August 30th, 1998 Added the "Barber: and "Classroom" flashes to the list, check em out. See you at FDT2! Monday, August 24th, 1998 Well, anyone who even "kinda" likes tiger (and I'm hoping if you're here, you fit the bill :) has to agree it's her flashes that make her distinct (well, I don't know if anyone else get's a kick outta them like I do :) So, why the hell don't I have past flashes up on my page?? I've finally smartened up, and put some past Tiger flashes up, accessible from the main page. I even rated then due to personal preference (they can't all be jems :) Enjoy! Well ain't I just Mr. Updates today? Anyway, check out the Lady Tiger highlight reel off the main page. A collection of the highest of Tiger's high spots (shame I don't have Tiger-Mary on file anymore, grrr . . . ) Later! Updated Tiger's record page. Looks a little cleaner now, and it's now up-to-date. Also added some stuff to the Lady Tiger merchandise catalogue. Monday, August 16th, 1998 There! Finally got up the "Warzone" article and codes for Lady Tiger! One of my friends bought the game, and once I found out there was a custom wrestler option . . . well, making a *real* Lady Tiger became my new priority :) god I market out playing that game the first time; you didn't even wanna SEE me when I finally managed to pull off the Longbow Frankensteiner ;) Anyway, enjoy! Thursday, July 30th, 1998 The auto-load music thing took too much load time, so I took it down. Added a tiger merchandise catalog, but it's not much to look at now. Actually, I'm planning on giving this page a bit of a fcae-lift, pretty-ize it et al :) Plus, a neat little surprise for video game fans, but I don't wanna spoil it juts yet ;) Sunday, June 10th, 1998 Well, FUX are frames a pain to implement!! Well, I'm gonna work on that for the side now (cause I need more cute icons for my menu right now :) For now, I got the music playing in a second frame, so it plays on every page you go to! :) Kawaii!! and, if you get sick of it, there's that very convenient "stop" button there, so you can, in your little way say "Shut up, Curt! Your theme music sucks!" :) Well, anyway, back to the articles! :) Well, the Hardoce II article is coming along, but needs touching. For now, I posted an article about how Lady tiger came to be (created, that is), entitled "Why a tiger?" What, you're still reading this?!?! Go find it!! ;) Sunday, June 7th, 1998 Damnit, been 2000000000 years since I updated this thang (well, not that long :) but nothing interesting has happened to poor LT as of late :( Well, new music for tiger anyway, "The William Tell Overture"; I like it! :) Writing up an article as we speak, may be up tonight. contemplating giving the page a new look, it's kinda stale. Ah well, I'll see what magic I can whip up! :) Tuesday, April 27th, 1998 I'm baaaaaack! :) Well, now that exams are done, expect to see a few articles pop up here and there. For now, all I did was add a counter, add a few links and drop some dead ones. Mostly cosmetics, for now. In the works are the end to that damn "Hardcore Tiger" article :) and something on the return of the tiger. That's all for now, seeya! Tuesday, December 23rd, 1997 Added an article which updates the world on Lady Tiger's recent injury and rehabilitation. Tuesday, December 9th, 1997 DAMN!! It's been awhile! Well, since none of the other homepages (ahem! :) seem to be updating, maybe this will enspire y'all! ;) Updated Tiger's record (finally!) with that pinfall loss to Andrea Chandler. I'll chuck an article up about tiger's career threatening injury here as soon as my exams finish (1.5 to 2 weeks). Maybe I'll get a chance to do some baby updates before then. Oh well, time to check over my stuff for that Stats final tomorrow, later! Wednesday, July 2nd, 1997 I'm beginning to wonder if that Netmind button works. Anyway . . . updated Tiger's record with a count-out win over Bloody Mary. Good, close match. Added "Jumping" Jennifer Grier as an ally to the "Friends and Foes of Lady Tiger" page. *Finally* uploaded my Ironwoman article!! It's been in the works for quite some time, and I was just waiting for comments from all parties. Since I was updating my page, I figured I'd chuck that on as well. Expect to see it on a GDWA show (Sat. Night Special?, Cat Fight?) soon. But all you tiger fans get the jump on everyone else, and get to see it first!! ;) Monday, June 23rd, 1997 Added a tiger pic to the index page; "tyger.jpg". Uploaded a poem; "The Tyger" by William Blake. So it has, like, absolutely nothing to do with Lady Tiger OR the GDWA?!?! So what?? AT least now you can say, "I went to the Lady Tiger homepage, and learned something!" :) Created part one of a two part article, "The Hardcore Tiger". I'm not sure how well it cam eout; I was going for the "written by a 12 year old" style, but I may have overdone it! :) Still, it came off pretty nicely, and should make a good addition to the articles section. Friday, June 20th, 1997 Shown above is the Netmind registration button. Simply click it and fill out the form, and you'll be informed whenever the Lady Tiger Homepage is updated!! Now, is that just too kawai or what?! ;) Wednesday, June 18th, 1997 I edited the page to that the "Eye of the Tiger" midi file plays automatically when the tiger.html page starts up. So now, you can get that perfect, tiger-iffic experience every time you come here!! :-) Sunday, June 15th, 1997 Added links to the newly formed homepages of Officer Order, Daisy Butterfly, "Jungle" Radhi Ananda and "Sexy" Sally McClane. If anyone else has a wrestler homepage, let me know and I'll link you up. Hell, I'll do one better. If there's anyone out there who *wants* to make a wrestler web page, but is intimidated by lack of HTML knowledge or anything like that, drop me a line and I'll see if I can help! Oh, and more updates to come later today. Saturday, June 14th, 1997 Well, I'm *FINALLY* back on line!! So, without further adieu . . . Edited the "tiger.html" page. Changed the groupings and added a few extra details. Check it out. Added "Charlotte's Corner"; a section conserning Tiger's Hall-of-Fame manager. Uploaded the Charlotte LaMancha profile and interview from the GDWA Mag, written by yours truly! ;) Uploaded two tiger-articles; "The Rookie Report" from back in January, and the House of Styles interview from this past week. Working on an Ironwoman article. I've got a LOT more stuff to upload between today and Sunday!! The Tiger 10, images, pictures, links, etc. So, this ain't it!! :) Sunday, June 8th, 1997 Sorry for the lack of updates, but I've been having *MAJOR* server problems!! (And Tiger hasn't rped much as of late either . . hmmmm). Should be back on line for good either Monday or Tuesday with auracom. I've got about 3 or 4 completed articles just waiting to be uploaded as well!! :) [I'm doing this update at a friend's house] So, anyone who cruises by here every now and then, there should be **lots** of new stuff here on Tuesday. Oh, and a big *THANKS* to everyone who signed the guestbook!! Later!! Friday, May 30th, 1997 OK, I cheated! ;) It hasn't been updated *yet*! I'm currently working on the "Tiger Ten", a top ten list posing various questions about GDWA, Tiger, you get the drift. Right now, I'm on the last 4 (writer's block). So expect it up tonight. And I just got a whole buncha new ideas for this page from Jon!! Woo Hoo!! Once I finish the CCW PPV, I'll get working on them. Oh, and thanks for the hype Lawrence! :-) And, once again, if you're coming here from GDWA, *PLEASE* sign the guestbook!! It doesn't have to be long; or nice, for that matter! :-) Just let me know you stopped by anyway, so I know that more than, what, 4 people have come here? ;-) Tuesday, May 27th, 1997 Updated Lady Tiger's record with . . . a DQ loss :-( Well, it had to happen eventually, although it occored under some strange circumstances. Oh well. Check out the page with Tiger's record for more details. Now available to download/play from the main page is Lady Tiger's theme music, "Eye of the Tiger", in midi!! Now, isn't that just way too kawai or what?? :-) Have it playing in the background as you check out the homepage for the ultimate, tiger-iffic experience!! Miko Azai . . . come on down!! :-) After careful consideration, and reviewing her history with Tiger, I decided to add "The Tokyo Thriller" as a foe to the "Friends and Foes of Lady Tiger" page. That makes 2 foes and 1 friend. D'oh!! Monday, May 26th, 1997 We're up and running!! Woo Hoo!! I just gave the address to Joe (Charlotte), Lawrence (GDWA fed head), and Jon (Sexy Sal) to get some opinions. And L-dog, if you're reading this . . . why not link me up?? :-) Someone actually tried to steal the CCW homepage last night!!! Thankfully, with the help of the CCW handlers, this perpetrator was brought to justice!! >:-) We flamed him into prehistoria, until he finally succumbed, admitted he stole it, and made the changes. In the end, he was pretty good about it, so I'm not going to mention his fed's name or anything. Saturday, May 24th, 1997 Got a Lady Tiger guestbook from Lpage .
