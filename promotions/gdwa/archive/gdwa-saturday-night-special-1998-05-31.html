Saturday Night Special, 31 May 1998 (Part 2)

[The lights dim to almost black, an eerie moment of silence passing before 'Devil Inside' by INXS begins to play. The spotlights focus on the center,then break into millions of pin points spiraling over the crowd before reforming in the center, focusing on the darkly smiling form of Black Lotus. She's clad, as always, in black leather, silver buckled straps making it even tighter. The mic is cradled in both her hands suggestively she surveys over the crowd.]
Lotus: Hello pets. A very special treat for you tonight. A pair of stellar talents who have truly brought a new edge to the GDWA. Good girls gone bad. The squeaky clean girls turned into a Dirty Pair.
They've been breaking bones and exposing the seemy underbelly of the
GDWA. Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome Sachie Yokoyama and Daisy
Butterfly!
("Around the World" by Daft Punk plays for a second over the PA, before it is bombastically cut short by Prodigy's "Breathe." Sachie Yokoyama and Daisy Butterfly walk out to a loud objection by the audience, with a small minority cheering for them. The duo are both dressed in tight-fitting "Dirty Pair" t-shirts and hot pants. Daisy wears the
GDWA IC title around her waist and flicks her tongue at the camera as
Sachie just smirks and motions to her crotch. Lotus blows a kiss to the two as they enter.)
Lotus: You two have been creating quite a stir lately. Sachie, what brought you back? Not only from the dead, but back to the GDWA? It was a truly dramatic reappearance, dear girl.
Sachie: "One word, REVENGE!!!!! REVENGE!!!!! REVENGE!!!!! REVENGE is what brought Sachie Yokoyama back to the GDWA!!! REVENGE is what brought Sachie Yokoyama back from the dead!!! I want REVENGE against
Officer May Order, she ruined my *ucking life!!! Because I wouldn't spread my legs for Lawrence Davis, and that two dollar ho would, I was forced to job to everyone and their sister while she became GDWA World
Champion!!! I m not going to rest until I have rid the GDWA of that no talent, me love you long time, Isuckie and fuckie you real good if you give me GDWA World title slut Officer May Order."
(Daisy smiles and claps as the arena boos wildly, with few exceptions) Sachie: "I want REVENGE against Lawrence Davis, for forcing me to job to the likes of no-talent bums such Rekka Sakura, Keiko Mita, Bloody
Mary, and Jungle Radhi! You attempted to KILL MY HEAT because I knew that you were *UCKING Order just so she could be GDWA World Champion!
You attempted to KILL MY HEAT because you knew that I was going to tell the world your little secret! HELL, you even tried to KILL ME because
I was going to tell the world your secret! Despite you marks read in your little mark magazines or on the internet, the airplane that I was riding did not accidentally crash! The airplane that I was riding was
CRASHED ON PURPOSE! And who do you think SET ME UP, well it was no other than LAWRENCE DAVIS!!! You see, not only does Lawrence Davis attempt to *UCK his employees he also attempts to KILL THEM if they
'get out of line'. Despite what he claims, Lawrence Davis is the HEAD
AND SOLE OWNER of the GDWA!!! He said that he is only a regional promoter and what Daisy and I said about him were nothing but a bunch
of lies. Who's lying to who Davis, its time to cut the shit!" Daisy: Careful, girl... (Daisy points up to the nosebleed section) Daisy: I think we might have a few snipers up there gunnin' for us! Sachie: "YOU know and I know that you have a thing for Asian Girls ! YOU know and I know that you have a thing for Officer Order! YOU know and I know that you HAD, and STILL have, a think for Daisy and I!!!
Hell, Daisy and I have PROOF that Lawrence STILL has a thing for Daisy, but we ll let Daisy tell you a little more about that."
Daisy: There's a matter of this little "bootycall" Mr. Davis made to me while I was in Japan...
(Whistles from the crowd) Daisy: You all may have heard it...I made sure that it got the exposure it needed. Not only is Lawrence Davis a reckless philanderer, but he has made *no bones* about blatantly putting my job on the line for some action. The tape has been sent out to my lawyer, among others, and they're currently assembling a case. I can't talk about anything more to do with that.
(Daisy cocks her head at Lotus and smiles) Daisy: Next question? Lotus: Now, darling, what brought you two together for this? You've both gone through attitude adjustments over the past couple years, but had not seemed all that close Sachie disappearance and then this sudden alliance?
Sachie: "Daisy was the first person that I met in GDWA! Daisy was the one who convinced me not to leave when Lawrence Davis FORCED me to job to my first FOUR opponents! Daisy called ME FIRST when she came up with idea of creating the DRAGON DUO, oops, Dragon Trio! Daisy and I have a bond that can not be described! We have a bond that is STRONGER than friendship! Daisy and I have a bond that is STRONGER than family!
Daisy and I have a bond that a 16 year old virgin has with the FIRST
GUY WHO *UCKED THE SHIT OUT OF HER! No matter what he does or says, she still loves him! You can t describe the bond that Daisy and I have! You can t even attempt to understand it!"
(LOUD catcalls from the men in the audience) Daisy: (smiling) Awww...that's so sweet! And for the record, Lotus,
I don't recall *EVER* being distant from Sachie. We just had to drop some fat...
(Sachie coughs "Charlotte") Daisy: ...and get ourselves back in shape. Lotus: You had some rather interesting things to say about the GDWA. Not just the usual suspects like Vessey, but the powers behind the scenes like Lawrence Davis. What brought this on? Darlings, is there anything you will not dare?
Sachie: "Daisy and I will do any, and everything, within our power to bring disgrace and dishonor to the GDWA!!! If we have to say *HIT,
GOD*AMN, MOTHER*UCKER, *SSHOLE, SON-OF-A-*ITCH, on TV, SO BE IT! If we have to show our TITS...SO BE IT!
(Sachie and Daisy start to pull up each other's t-shirts as the crowd
ROARS. They stop and start to laugh)
Daisy: YOU STUPID MARKS!! Did you *really* think you'd get one for free? Wrong place, wrong time...
Sachie: If we have to break the neck s of EVERY MOTHER*UCKING wrestler in GDWA, SO BE IT! Lawrence Davis, you and the GDWA, are GOING DOWN!!!
So it is written, so it will come to pass!" Daisy: Lotus...I *KNOW* you'll wanna keep your eyes open. You really just never know what we'll do next.
Lotus: I suppose the obvious question now is...what do you WANT? What is your goal here in the GDWA? Where does the Dirty Pair go from here?
Daisy: Sum it up, dear Sachie! Sachie: "TOTAL DOMINATION OF THE GDWA! Hey Lawrence, LICK IT!!!!!" (Both Daisy and Sachie point toward their crotches) Daisy: That, in a nutshell, Miss Lotus... (Voice over the PA): Excellent performances, ladies! Bravo! (Emerging from the back are the Kingpin, Andrea Chandler, and Micki
Duran, striding toward the Darkside set purposefully. All hold mics.
They stop before Sachie and Daisy. Sachie and Daisy look at each other quizzically) Andrea: I'm glad you've found purpose here in Grand Dragon, and I'm glad that you've given the GDWA censors a little extra work. But there are more pressing issues here, and you know what they are.
Daisy: (huffs, sounding almost sarcastic) And what's that, 'Dre? Kingpin: Don't play dumb, Daisy. Last time I checked, you were Syndicate, and we didn't let you in on our action only to turn tail and start playing in a different sandbox. It's all well and good that the reports of Sachie's death were greatly exaggerated, but now it's time to put up or shut up.
Daisy: "Put up...or shut up." (Daisy looks blankly at Sachie) Daisy: What do you mean by that? Micki: Meaning, sister, that you need to tell us whether you're in, or out. This ain't no Rage family reunion where you can just come crawling out of the woodwork whenever you feel like it. We haven't heard from your ass in a couple of months now, and it's pissing us off.
It's pissing ME off... Kingpin: It's real simple, ladies. Daisy, as far as we're concerned, you're still a part of the team. And Sachie, we know your rep, and you're welcome aboard, too. But we're tired of playing guessing games.
So the question is: are you Syndicate, or are you against us? Daisy: (smiling and purposely playing dumb) Yeah. I guess I'm either with you or against you.
Andrea: Daisy...that really doesn't tell us much, you know? It's a
"yes" or "no" question.
Daisy: Look; Kingpin, 'Dre, Micki...you know I got love for you all. But, see, this is *MY TIME*... (Daisy looks at Sachie, then puts her arm around her) Daisy: *OUR* time. Micki, Kingpin...real good to see you back. But you just can't walk out of here, then walk back in and expect everything to be just how you left it. You want an answer from me?
(Daisy looks at every member of the Syndicate, who all nod with a cold look in their eye) Daisy: You'll get one. Some day. Cue the music. ("Around The World" starts up again, Daisy and Sachie push past the
Syndicate and leave. Micki Duran stares down Daisy obviously as she walks past.)
Lotus: Hmmm...pets, it appears that not everything is sunshine and smiles in Syndicate-land. We'll have to see how this play unfolds over time. Now back to our esteemed announcing team.
Bishop: Woah! What the hell just happened there? Mutt: Looks like th rulebreaker locker room just turned into even more factions. Bishop: This is just...odd. Let's just move on. Fans, up next we have our Cruiserweight Division match up tonight...
Mutt: Speaking of Cruiserweights, did Candi Bratton put the boots to Stephani Weathers or what?
Bishop: Sam is eluding to the GDWA rookie battle at Super Japan's pay per view last weekend. We'll see footage of it later on in the show. But....Hold on is that Baby Hotta? International GREAT
Baby Hotta here at a GDWA show? Mutt: Who? (Camera cuts over to a spot in the front row and focuses on JIWWA Champion Baby Hotta, seated in the audience. Hotta is a cute, young-looking Japanese woman, her long unnaturally dyed yellow hair is kept in two ponytails on the sides of her head. On her lap sits a
3-year-old girl wearing an ill-fitting Lady Tiger mask. Hotta holds the girl Bishop: This has been an interesting last few weeks. Mutt: No kiddin'. Spud: Wrestling fans, our next contest is a Cruiserweight Division match up. It will be refereed by officials Dawn Le and Aileen
Balahadia, and it is scheduled for one fall with a 15 minute time limit. Bishop: Laura Cameron is backstage with comments. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- [GDWA Reporter Lisa Panoli catches "Sweet" Laura Cameron before the match. Cameron, dressed to the 10's in her red and white bodysuit, with her dark brown/reddish tinted hair around her shoulders, gives
Kudron a grimace as she catches her.]
Panoli: Laura, tonight you're facing a woman, still looking for her first win in the GDWA. What do you plan to do against Hannah Blue?
Cameron: [A slight smile on the corner of her lips.] Hannah Blue, such a lovely name. Both you and I are going for a win tonight, dear. But one of us isn't going to win. You see, sweetness comes in two different flavors. Bitter and suckulant. After this match, you're going to have such a bittersweet smile on your face... ohhh. It's going to be
HORRIBLE! Hannah Blue, you'd better bring everything you got to the ring tonight, because it's going to take a lot to catch me and then pin me. Oh, such a bittersweet tragedy.
[She puts on her sweetest smiles and then waves at Panoli before walking off.] ----------------------------------------------------------------------- (Fans boo as they hear Sweet Emotion by Aerosmith.) Spud: Hailing from San Jose, California. She is 5 feet 7 inches, 128 pounds, here is "Sweet" Laura Cameron! When Steven Tyler starts singing "Sweeeet Emoootion"
then Laura comes out, wearing a red and white bodysuit with her dark brown/reddish tinted hair up in a scrunchie. She looks at each man and smiles. They watch Laura's back on the way to the ring. When she climbs into the ring, she puts her hands on either side of her head and smile at the crowd. They boo like crazy as she pulls the scrunchie out of her hair and tosses it into the crowd. Unfortuneatly, the lucky person who catches it throws it back to the cheers of the fans.)
Bishop: Over the course of 2 weeks, we've seen Laura Cameron's attitude really change. She went from yearning fan favroite to a true heel, and I don't understand it.
Mutt: Yeah, and considering who her husband is, it's even more strange. Don "The Dominator" Cameron is one of the biggest fan favorites on the planet. He also just won the NCCW World Heavyweight Title, defeating
"The Fox" Curt Crane at the NCCW's inagural PPV, Ignition.
Bishop: Cameron leaning against the near ropes, facing the ring curtain as Spud begins again.
Spud: And her opponent... (Minor cheers as fans hear "We Got the Night" by Coney Hatch.) Spud: Hailing from Eastbourne, United Kingdom. SHe is 5 feet 2 inches, 117 pounds...Hannah 'Midnight' Blue!
(Hannah heads down the aisle wearing a blue and black two piece with black elbow and knee pads. She slaps hands with fans as she heads down ringside.)
Bishop: Blue climbing up the ring steps, and Cameron watching on, giving her a wide smile. I think you're right Sam. She and 'Tattle

Mutt: Laura calling over the referee, and she wants him to check Blue over to make sure she isn't concealing a weapon.
(Fans boo as Hannah protests.) Bishop: Hannah has the gesture reuturned, and ref Dawn Le checks Laura's boots. Mutt: The Cruiserweight division is really hot, Bishop! I must say that I'm really impressed. Sierra Browne will be hard pressed to defend that belt against these hungry newcomers.
Bishop: Referee Dawn Le nodding to Spud, and there's the bell. *Ding*Ding*Ding*Ding* Bishop: Both women encircling one another, and Cameron offering her hand to Blue. Blue turning to the crowd.....
(Fans chant: NO!) Bishop: And Hannah she slaps Cameron's hand away. Collar and Elbow tie up and Cameron with the weight advantage....Side Headlock takedown.
(Fans boo as Laura Cameron blows a kiss to ringside fans.) Mutt: Both women rather small in size. Laura is tall and quick with very little strength. Hannah is similar, with less height. A pretty good physical match up as far as they come.
Bishop: Hannah Blue moving in, and another Collar and Elbow tie up. Laura backing her into the near corner, and the ref wants a break... Mutt: Laura Cameron with a thumb to the eye. onw hard right hand jabs to the head and Hannah is dazed. An Irish Whip to the far corner as Laura picks up a head of steam.

(Fans all on their feet as she climbs the turnbuckles.) Mutt: Hannah Blue up t oher feet as she charges the near corner and... (Fans all on their feet, amazed, as Hannah Blue taps out!) *Ding*Ding*Ding*Ding* Bishop: WHAT?! (Fans boo as they hear "Sweet Emotion" by Aerosmith.) Mutt: Damn, that's unbelievable. She dominated from start to finish. Spud: Wrestling fans, at 4 minutes and 07 seconds, your winner via submission, "Sweet" Laura Cameron!
(Fans all booing as the referee raises her hand in victory.) Bishop: Hannah can do much better than that. She must have been sick. Mutt: She got dominated from bell to bell.
