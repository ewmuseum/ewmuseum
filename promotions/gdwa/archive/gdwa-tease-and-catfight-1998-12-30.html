Tease and Catfight

Wednesday, 30 December 1998; San Francisco, California (Cow Palace)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
----------------------TEASE------------------------- Shumira gets into the elevator and pushes the button, giggling to herself as the *ding* sounds and the doors close. Slowly she turns to the man in a business suit behind her and chirps "Hi! Goin' up? I know I am!" The man edges away from her, backing slowly into the corner. He smiles nervously. "Uh.. yeah.. up." "Heyyyy. You know what?" Shumira says to the man as she reaches into her small black purse. "There's somebody who wants to say hi to youuuuu." Suddenly she whips out the head of Muffins (which is wearing little black bows tied around it's stuffed ears) and brandishes it at arms-length right in the man's face. "Hi you! I'm Muffins the cat! Gosh that's a nice [censored]ing tie you have on today!" "Waah!" the man screams and hits the button for the next floor, and dashes out of the elevator post haste. The receptionist sneers at Shumira as she staggers toward the desk (Shumira has never worn heels before. She prefers combat boots, ect.) "Hi. I'm Shumira Kishiro. I'm here to see MISTER Rob Tucker." Shumira grins big. Too big. The receptionist sets down her Cosmopolitan magazine and flips her hair. "Do you have an appointment?" "I'm Shumira!" she replies simply. As if that explains everything. "...and I wrapped it around my waist. She was always good like that. Always had a plan." Shumira grabs the receptionist by the throat and yanks her in close, her grin suddenly gone. "I have a plan too. And I'm here to see Tucker. So get your gum-snapping, hair-twirling, bimbo-blonde ass out of that chair, and tell him that his icon of virtue, his paragon of morality, his soon to be corporate champion is here to see him!" she growls. "There are a few things we need to discuss." Shumira grins wolfishly. She gathers up the brick chunks and places them back in her purse. Stepping on the unconscious bodies of the security staff, she stalks toward the office doors. "Hey, my Prince Charming. We need to talk." She peeks into the office. "Hellooo... huh.. he's not here." She turns on the receptionist, grinning darkly. "Ok love, so where is he?" The receptionist staggers back. "I.. don't know. I think he left for a meeting. Yeah.. a meeting!" I have some urgent business to discuss with him, and it really can't wait." "I...I don't know." "You.." Shumira sighs and calmly punches her in the stomach, "...don't..." punches her again, "...KNOW?!" Shumira finishes with a headbutt straight into the girl's face. The receptionist screams and clutches at her nose as she sits crumpled against the desk, her eyes wild with fear and pain. Shumira looks around slowly. "Well my little blonde angel...he has to be around here somewhere. Don't bother getting up, I'll show myself around the place." Shumira stalks away, then stops, turning back to the girl with sultry grin "And don't you go anywhere." she says sweetly. "I'll be back for you later."
