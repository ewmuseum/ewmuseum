Tuesday Night Catfight, 22 May 1998 (Part 1)

(The camera comes up on a destroyed limousine pulling into the parking lot. It's windshield has been smashed in, the driver's side window is gone, the headlights are destroyed, and there is a huge dent in the hood. It rattles in, then pulls to a stop. The rear door opens, and
Micki Duran steps out. She is dressed in street clothes, wearing her "THE LEGEND'S BACK" t-shirt. She pulls out a bag and walks towards the arena. Suddenly, she sees the cameraman and marches towards him.)
Micki: Wow. This must be my lucky day. A cameraman all to myself. Good. Could you stand right here, sweetheart? I have a couple of things to say. (She drops her bag and takes off her sunglasses.) Micki: Sierra, you can play games all you want. (Her voice gets deadly calm.) But the bottom line is this. You can pretend this is about my "hunger for titles" if you want. You can tell yourself I'm
"over the hill" and "washed up" and "irrelevant" if it helps you sleep at night. But you and I both know this ain't about any titles.
It ain't about reputation. This ain't even about who was "embarassed" and who wasn't. This is about me correcting a problem I overlooked a long time ago. Wanna see something?
(She pulls out a stack of photos from her bag and holds them up, one by one, to the camera.)
Micki: This is "Playmate" Dixie Foxy. GDWA superstar. I broke her knee in four places. She barely walks now, and she damn sure doesn't wrestle in the GDWA any more.
(She holds up another.) Micki: This is former GDWA World Champion Dementia Praecox. I put The End on her three times, and broke her vertabrae. She doesn't wrestle in the GDWA, or anywhere else, any more.
(She holds up another.) Micki: This should look familiar. This is the night I took your sister Indigo and tore all of the cartilige in her knee. Doesn't she STILL walk with a limp? I thought so. She's never been the same since.
(She puts the photos back in the bag.) Micki: What do they have in common? All put on the shelf by Micki Duran. So go ahead and tell yourself that I just want another title around my "jungle-fever-havin' ass." Once the racial slurs are over, once your little game is played out, once the fans are sick of your triflin' lies, when it all comes down to it, I'm not looking to beat you. I'm looking to cripple you. For life. I should have put the figure four on you instead of Indigo. My mistake. I intend to correct it.
(She picks up her bag.) Micki: You can go back to kidding yourself, Sierra. I'm done with you. For now. See you later tonight. (She storms past the camera into the arena.) ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ Tuesday, May 19, 1998 Scene opens up on a jammed packed Oakland Colisseum in Oakland, California. There are 22,744 screaming fans in attendance. "Atomic Dog" by Parliament starts blaring through the speakers. The fans erupt with a standing ovation, the camera zooms in on the east side of the ring, where a whole section of fans are wearing dog masks and barking at the camera as it passes them by. This section is known as "THE DAWG
POUND" and this is........ GRAND DRAGON WRESTLING ALLIANCE: Tuesday Night Catfight (The camera shows signs of ringside fans barking and wearing police officer uniforms. Some fans show signs that say "THis is the house
Order built!", "Daisy fear Mita!", "Rage world Champion" and "O.C. 4 life!". The camera pans down to two men who sit at ringside.)
Bishop: Wrestling fans, we are looking at one of the biggest Catfights in GDWA history. Allen Bishop with you again for 3 hours of GDWA's finest! This _is_ the Tuesday Night Catfight.
Mutt: All I can say is that the stuff is about to hit the fan. We are one week away from the first match in the Demonica Endurance
Challenge tournament, and two of its participants will get it on prematurely in a best of 3 falls match up for the Cruiserweight titles
#1 contedership. Bishop: Tonight fans, so many matches and stories behind the matches. We have a HOUSE of STYLES with MISTER Furious Styles as he interviews GDWA promoter Lawrence Davis. Davis will answer some charges that the IC Champ and Sachie Yokoyama made last week.
Mutt: Davis is not one to get in front of a camera...this interview will be interesting to say the least. Now, let's get to the matches at hand. Patra Marchand and Bloody Mary get it on in a match that was pre-empted from last week. In tag action, the Cruel Angels face GDWA
Team Dojo, and newcomers the Amazons face the High Flying Dolls. Bishop: And if you want to talk action, let's talk about Micki Duran and Rekka Sakura! Their athletic rivalry has turned another corner as they vie for the #1 contendership in the Cruiserweight ranks. ANd in the Main Event...signed, sealed, delivered...finally! The
Western Heritage Champion "Kyoto Crippler" Keiko Mita faces the "Franchise" Daisy Butterfly for the Intercontinental championship! Mutt: And it will be a defining moment for each woman. Mita and Daisy have faced once before with Mita gaining the pinfall...now, former friends come bitter rivals get it on for the IC title!
Bishop: And will Sachie Yokoyama play a factor in this? All of this and more part of a red hot Tuesday Night Catfight!
(Fans all cheering as Spud Mckenzie enters the ring. He's wearing a black blazer and slacks with a red striped tie. He stands in the middle of the ring as the fans settle down.)
Spud: Wrestling fans, first, I must say that IT IS GOOD TO BE HOME! (fANS ALL CHEER AND CHANT: MITA! MITA! MITA! MITA! MITA!) Spud: Tonight the best superstars in the sport will showcase their talents for all that their worth...for all of our fans right here at the Oakland Colisseum, the Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance has but one question to ask you....
(Fans P O P : A R E Y O U R E A D Y !!) Spud: That's right......Are you ready!? (Fans scream: YES!!!!!) Spud: I can't hear you!.....I said ARE YOU READY?! (Fans scream: YES!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!) Spud: Then Let's Get Rrrrrrready to Rrrrrrrumble!!! (Fans cheer as horns go off & Spud McKenzie stands poised in the ring.) Spud: Our first contest is scheduled for one fall with a 15 minute time limit! (Minor cheers as fans hear "Queen of the Pack" by Patra.) Spud: First, hailing from Kingston, Jamaica. She is 5 feet 10 inches, 146 pounds, here is Patra Marchand! (Patra heads down the aisle wearing a blue bottomed and white topped 2 piece. She enters the ring as Spud McKenzie continues. ) Spud: And her opponent.... (Hardcores cheer as they hear "Midnight Rider" by the Allman Brothers.) Spud: Hailing from Davenport, Iowa. She is 5 feet 9 inches, 145 pounds, here is Blooooooooooooooooody Marrrrrrrrrrrrry!
(Mary walks through the curtain to a chorus of cheers from the Dawg Pound. Most fans boo as Mary struts down to ringside.) Bishop: The Blonde-Bombshell is back! Her first match back since Dawg Pound Nights 1998 where she destroyed Jenny Tyler. Mary is trying to return to title contention...
Mutt: And she's got a tough customer in Patra. Both ladies are in need of a win here. Patra defeated Nikita Marx, but hasn't had much luck since then.
(Mary stops at ringside wearing a red and white tie-dyed 2 piece with red boots after removing black trench coat and hat.)
Bishop: Mary in the ring, and Patra Marchand just looking on calmly. Mutt: Patra better be on her p's and q's....cause I don't see Mary losing any matches any time soon. She's had enough of wrestling on the midcard and lower card of House Show and pay per views. Her career needs to meet the promise of her potential.
Bishop: The referee going over the rules...and here we go. *Ding*Ding*Ding*Ding* Bishop: Patra wants a Greco Roman Knuckle....no! Mary with a charge and a Double Leg takedown!
(Fans pop as Mary pounds away on Patra Marchand's face.) Bishop: Mary with a pick up and nailing away with Iowa Heaven Punches to the jaw! Mary now, with a handful of hair and ramming Patra's head into the near buckle, and now a Waistlock!
Mutt: BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX! (Dawg Pound pops as Patra clutches her lower back.) Bishop: Mary got the jump on her, and now a Double Leg pick up. Mary stomping away on the hamstrings and Patra is in pain. Mary with a stepover toe hold, and Patra squirming right into the ropes.
Mutt: The referee calling for a break, and Mary stomoping away on the left knee of Patra. Ref: 1.....................2................3................4...... Bishop: Mary breaking, and now a pick up! Mary with a frontface lock going for a Vertical Sulpex...leg block by Patra. And now forearm shots to the head of Mary.
Mutt: AND BLOODY MARY SHOVES HER INTO THE CORNER! Bishop: What power! (Fans pop!) Bishop: Mary with a head of steam and a running Clothesline! (Fans all groan as Mary nails her with a Lariat.) Bishop: Mary with a handful of hair as she drags her out of the corner and she twirls around.... T O R N A D O P U N C H !
(Dawg Pound pops as Patra Marchand twists through the air.) Ref: 1.............................2...................Kick Out. Bishop: Patra rolling out of the ring, and Bloody Mary just too quick, too strong for Patra.
Mutt: ANd that may be the Achilles heel of Patra Marchand. We've seen in her prior matches that she doesn't have much problem dominating the tone and pace of a contest. But her lack of lateral and linear movement is being exposed here.
Spud: 5 minutes have gone by in the 15 minute time limit. Ref: 1........................................2...................... Bishop: Patra walking around on the outside, shaking the cobwebs loose, and Mary just waiting. Patra having a hard time with this tough gal. Mary may be the most physically fit wrestler in the GDWA.
She only has 3% body fat. Mutt: Patra back in the ring, and she's walking along the ropes. She's calling in Mary who obliges willingly. Patra dancing about a bit. Keeping on her toes as Mary zooms in. Collar and Elbow tie up and Patra with an Armdrag. And now snapping that left arm!
Bishop: Mary with a reversal, but Patra with a Droptoe hold! Mary twirling back up to her feet, and Patra clipping the leg!
Mutt: Patra the technical wrestler with a great cheap shot! And now a Single Arm pick up and Patra dropping an elbow to the bicep. Patra back up and dropping another Hammer to the bicep.
Bishop: Now Patra down to the canvas, and an Overhand Wristlock by Patra. Mary slapping the canvas in pain. Mutt: Great strategy by Patra. She's got the gal down on the mat, and is taking away the primary weapon, Mary's arm.
Bishop: Mary flailing her legs, and Patra really bearing down on the hold. Mary can't reach the ropes, and so she's fighting up to her feet. Patra with a Fireman's Carry pick up into a Fall away slam!
Ref: 1........................................2.............kick out! Bishop: Patra with a pick up and another Armdrag. Mary backing into the ropes, and now Patra with an Armdrag Takedown! Mary back up and
Patra with a dropkick to the knee! Mary is down. Mutt: Patra with another Single Arm Pick up...INSIDE CRADLE BY MARY! Ref: 1................................2..................kick out! Bishop: Both women back up and BLOODY MARY WITH A BOOT TO THE MIDDLE AND SLAPS ON THE CLAW!!! (Fans all screaming as Patra backs into the ropes.) Spud: 10 minutes have gone by in the 15 minute time limit. Mutt: The ref wants a break, and Mary bearing down on the hold. Ref: 1............................2............3............4.....5... Bishop: The ref pulls her off, and Mary screaming at the referee. The ref holding back her arm, and Patra with a running Forearm shot! Mutt: Mary still on her feet? (Fans all pop as Patra looks around, amazed.) Bishop: Patra backs into the ropes, and with a head of steam..... Mutt: FOREARM SHOT! (Dawg Pound barks as Mary plummets to the canvas...then gets back up to her feet.) Bishop: Patra with a Waistlock pick up, but Mary slipping behind her back and slapping on a Waistlock pick up...RUNNING SHOULDER BREAKER!
(Dawg Pound barks: WooF! WooF! WooF! WooF! WooF! WooF! WooF! ) Mutt: Patra is down, and Mary twirling up to her feet. Patra getting up, and Mary charges...
Bishop: But Patra rolls out of the ring. Ref: 1..........................................2................... Bishop: Bloody Mary climbing through the ropes and on the outside. Patra trying to get back in, but Mary with a handful of tights and tosses her down to the floor!
Mutt: Mary crouching over her, and pounding away on her! (Mixed cheers as the ref counts away.) Mutt: Mary with a pick up and a boot to the midsection. Mary now, Irish whipping her right into the gaudrdrailing! Mary with a head of steam and.... Bishop: Patra dives out of the way! Ref: 7..................................8......................9... Bishop: Mary hit gut first into the guard railing. Patra slow to her feet, clutching her back as she grabs Mary by the back of her hair. Patra with a boot to the midsection and...
Mutt: S T O N E C O L D S T U N N E R ! (Fans go: Ooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooh!) *Ding*Ding*Ding*Ding* (Fans all booing as the ref calls over Spud McKenzie.) Bishop: Mary clutching the back of her head as Patra hobbles toward the ring. This thing is over.
Mutt: Patra rolling into the ring, and talking with the referee. Spud: Wrestling fans, at 11 minutes and 16 seconds, due to a Double Count OUt, this match is ruled a draw! (Fans chant: L-E-T T-H-E-M F-I-G-H-T ! L-E-T T-H-E-M F-I-G-H-T !) Bishop: Patra not to happy, and Mary slowly getting to her feet. These two will have to face another day...
Mutt: Well Patra had this match going her way, she just wasn't doing enough damage to Mary. Mary is just too fast and strong for Patra.
And on the other end? Mary didn't account for that great technial ability of Patra. You don't attempt to go hold-4-hold against a technician!
Bishop: Fans, that one is in the books. The rookie held her own, but not enough to score the win against a hungry Bloody Mary.
Mutt: Well, that's over simplifying it a tad bit. Bishop: We now take you to one of the most eagerly acticipated HOUSE of STYLES _ever_! Lawrence Davis will be here to answer charges made by that Dirty Pair of rulebreakers, Daisy Butterfly and Sachie
Yokoyama. (The arena grows dark and white paper falls from the ceiling. Purple lights flash across the arena as the opening chords of "Dove's Cry" by
Prince blast through the pa. MISTER Furious Styles struts down the aisle wearing a purple and black striped zoot suit. Fans POP as Styles climbs into the ring and twirls around for all to see.)
Styles: Oakland, California....who loves ya baby?!?!?! (Fans all cheer as MISTER Furious Styles struts across the floor.) Styles: Now, before we get started, I want to address an issue. As you all know, I've been taking this little survey from coast to coast.
I just wanted to get some things straight before we proceed. (Fans cheer in expectation as STYLES profiles for the fans.) Styles: Now, are you all here to see black-lotus-and-the-darkside?! (Crowd screams: O-H H-E-L-L N-O ! ! !) Styles: Or....or.....are you all here to see me and the..... ( ****THE__ HOUSE__ OF__ FUX'N__STYLES !**** ) Styles: Yeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeah, boy! Chalk another one up for the BLACK guy! (Fans whistle and cheer as MISTER Styles chuckles and leans against the ropes.) Styles: My guest this week is at the heart of contraversy. 2 very reliable sources have accused Denmarke Vessey of...and Lawrence Davis of....well, let's get to the fat and skinny of it. Oh, Lawrence....
(A medium sized African American heads down to ringside with GDWA Vice President Alison De La Cruz. Lawrence wears a cardigan sweater and black slacks as he climbs through the ropes and into the ring. He holds the ropes open for Alison who enters after.)
Styles: Alrightee, then! Hey Davis, let's get this party started right. Alison, glad you're here. Now, first question my friend...
ARE YOU OR ARE YOU NOT THE HEAD OF THE GDWA! (Fans look expectantly in the ring as Mr. Davis looks stoicly.) Davis: I.... Styles: Is Denmark Vessey simply a figure head...a puppet of yours to put up a facade? What's up?!
Davis: Look, I _am_ not the GDWA president or commissioner. I don't run this company. I am a regional promoter! What Sachie and Daisy said about me was a bunch of lies...
Styles: Well hold on. You're telling me that you don't own the GDWA? You've got no dibs on the company? You got some kind of clout. (Davis shakes his head, frustrated, as Alison De La Cruz grabs the microphone.) De La Cruz: Mr. Styles, Lawrence is not accustomed to the spotlight. So let me speak to this issue. Mr. Davis owns stock in the company, that is true. And, he owns more stock than any other SINGLE stockholder, this is also true...but he owns a plurality of the votes and *not* a majority. What that means is he owns less than 50% of
GDWA stock. So he owns more than any single other stock holder, but he lacks the potential to control the GDWA since he owns less than
50% Styles: Okay, but surely that 49%...or whatever percent Mr. Davis has, is significant. Significant enough to support the claims made by my girl Sachie Yokoyama. What about some "FAVORS" she alledges. She named Officer Order, Andrea Chandler and others as dippin' in the cookie jar to get a push....
Davis: That's bullshit! I... De La Cruz: Hold on, Mr. Davis. I'll handle this. You know, Lawrence is not on any booking committee or championship committee. He is a promoter. He advertises matches and advises us on what will get fans to a given set of wrestlers in his region. Mr. Davis promotes the lucrative West Coast where the GDWA makes its home.
Styles: Alright, Mr. Davis. What about you and Asian girls. I was slipped on the sly some information regarding....
(Minor cheer as Lawrence Davis shoves Alison aside and grabs the mic.) Davis: YOU WANNA SHOOT?! YOU WANNA SHOOT?! First, Daisy Butterfly! You G*ddamn backstabber.... (Fans cheer as Lawrence points at MISTER Furious Styles.) Davis: Does [name withheld] ring a bell? I helped him get a job about a year ago as tag team booker. And what happened? Where is he now? You know where? Working at some pizza joint kneeding dough and making deliveries! That S.O.B. ruined the tag division! Hell, it's taken us a year just to get it back to normal....
(Minor cheers as Styles rubs his hands together.) Davis: And Sachie, you are a disgrace. I trusted you! I trusted Daisy! I have a *thing* for Asian girls? Did I have a thing for Y O U when we went to that little gym you call a college? You know what I'm talking about!! What does "B.N.C for life stand for?" You know what it really stands for...we wrestled in some dank little gym in North
Carolina to make YOU happy! Why? Because your little roadie marks at that college were there. "BECAUSE you NEEDED CASH" ! Because your merchandise sales were going down since you started jobbing to Radhi
Ananda and Bloody Mary... (Fans go: Ooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooh!) Davis: AND FOR ANOTHER REASON TOO! But I'll get to that in a minute. Daisy, you girls really pissed me off...we were friends. You talk about no longer "sticking to the script". I never asked ANYTHING of you except for you to be a woman...and not on your own terms! You know, I would find your insinuations mildly insulting if you weren't so mixed-up about your own identity. Let's just say, Daisy Butterfly is not only Bi-racial but Bi.....
(Fans whistle as Alison repromands Mr. Davis and yanks the mic away. He retains the mic as he screams at Alison De La Cruz.) Davis: And you let them come out here on national televison and lie on me to get ratings. You act like the GDWA didn't know she was alive.
Sachie Yokoyama was NOT dead!...and YOU and Denmark Vessey knew it! De La Cruz: No....I... Davis: Yes you did! You had to know, because Sachie was a star. Ha! Sachie Yokoyama the great Japanese superstar. Her body was never found and they didn't even make a big stank about it on tv. Sachie, you are such a hypocrite! Do you fans want to know _WHY_ she was gone for so long?! ALl you stinkin' marks!? All you Japanese fans?!
(Minor boos as the Dawg Pound chants: YES!) Davis: Just like a lot of superstars, Sachie took some time off. You took a lot of time off, didn't ya! We at the front offices kept it all quiet and hush so you could keep your reputation & sort out your life.
But let me ask the fans...why else would a MAJOR wrestling superstar take a year off from action to "disappear" from the headlines? You only have to ask Scoot Hall...He ain't the only one that's been sniffing glue around here!
(Fans cheer as De La Cruz grabs the mic.) De La Cruz: That's enough! MISTER Styles, this interview has been completed. You got your answers, and as of right now, the GDWA is placing a gag order on Mr. Davis.
(Fans boo as Ms. De La Cruz grabs Lawrence Davis and leads him out of the ring.) Styles: Well, well, well! This ain't over yet! Fans, mabye there's more to this than meets the eye. Let's go back to action with Allen and Sam!
Bishop: Woah! Vessey knew Sachie was alive?! That can't be true. Mutt: WoW! Some serious...allegations made right back by Davis. But you know, he was showing his stroke in that rant. I think he divulged some stuff to the fans.
Bishop: This is just...odd. Let's just move on.
