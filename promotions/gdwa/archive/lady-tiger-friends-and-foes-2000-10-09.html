Friends and Foes of Lady Tiger

9 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friends & Foes of Lady Tiger Friends: Charlotte LaMancha: When Lady Tiger entered the GDWA, her idol was then-Internet Champion Charlotte LaMancha. All her life, Lady Tiger had dreamed for a chance to fight side-by-side with her hero! When Charlotte announced her retirement following a brutal attack by Ma Porter, Lady Tiger blamed herself for not being there to save her idol. In a hospital room located in Nice, France, Charlotte helped the distraught Lady Tiger through her emotional turmoil, and the two formed a friendship in the following months. Touched by the amount of fan mail asking her to come back, Charlotte decided to return to GDWA: as Lady Tiger's manager!! "Jumping" Jennifer Grier: Originally, Tiger and Grier were bonded together by an unlikely source; "Sexy" Sally McClane. Tiger had just been hospitalized following a brutal attack in the locker room, following her "Where have all the heroes gone?" speech on the House of Styles (also accessible off the main page). Grier, touched by the interview, lambasted Sally for her cowardly actions. She went further to say Sally couldn't get a clean win if her life depended on it! Recently, Grier and Tiger have taking to training together, preparing for a confrontation with their arch-rival, Sally. Tiger has found an ally in the up and coming GDWA star, and Sally's troubles have just doubled! Foes: "Sexy" Sally McClane: Since the day she arrived in Grand Dragon, Sally McClane has always been at odds with Lady Tiger. Sally's comments and insults towards the French Duo have enticed them to play a series of practical jokes on the Sexy one. Sally, of course, was enraged that anyone would try to embarrass her in front of the public, and vowed that she'd be the one to strike Tiger down!! Arguably the top two rookies in Grand Dragon, a match between McClane and Tiger would pit Sally's technical expertise against Tiger's daredevil tactics. But the pride at stake of being Grand Dragon's top rookie competitor, and the animosity between these two competitors, would make this match so important in the standings, these two would probably pull everything they could out of their arsenals in an all-out war!! "The Tokyo Thriller" Miko Azai: When Lady Tiger entered GDWA, there was another lady in the federation by the name of Lady Starr. Starr has since returned to her real name of Miko Azai. Lady Tiger sought a match with Azai, to prove who "The *real* Lady of Grand Dragon" was!! Tiger send a barrage of challenges to Azai for a match at Founder's Day tradition; all falling on deaf ears. Two-three months later, Azai offered up an open contract to the GDWA, and tiger immediately snatched it up!! When the match finally occurred, Tiger turned out to be too much for Azai, and finished her off in relatively short order. Angered by the loss to the then-rookie, and Tiger's lack of interest in signing a rematch, Azai took it upon herself to interfere in Tiger's next match; a title match with Sierra Browne!! Tiger lost the match by DQ thanks to Azai, and received a beating afterwards. It looks like Miko may get her wish for a rematch after all!!
