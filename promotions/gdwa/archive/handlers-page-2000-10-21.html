Welcome to the Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance Handler's Page October 21st, 2000. 72 Hours away! That's right, we are 3 days away from crowning our brand new G.D.W.A. Western Heritage Champion! Our first card out of Tacoma ought to be an explosive one! For those of you 'new schoolers' interested in the promotion who'd like to review GDWA title histories or fill out an application, enter the Dragon's Lair... Articles, Talk Shows and Things.... Join in on discussions regarding GDWA current events: The Talk of the Town Message Board <http://www.InsideTheWeb.com/mbs.cgi/mb971551>! Between the Ropes Monday 27 March For the latest on GDWA superstars, listen to Paul Laurence Dunbar's "Between the Ropes" program. In the latest installment he talks about the upcoming pay per view, Glory Days as well as the latest news on recent Grand Dragon signings! You will need the REAL AUDIO PLAYER <http://www.realaudio.com/> in order to listen. Scratching Post Monday 28 February Cal, a Grand Dragon wrestling fan, sent in a workrate report on our very first house show back in January. Very interesting read! Pay particular attention to his section on Officer May Order. 2 Pieces of Monkey Crap Tuesday 25 January The Sports Fan" Melanie Gilbert's column she discusses the upcoming pay per view GLORY DAYS and how Grand Dragon athletes can get over with the fans. Links and Thangs!
