The Hardcore Tiger (part 1)

23 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(ed note: this is part one of a two part article, written by one of tiger's more . . . blooodthirsty fans ;) The Hardcore Tiger by Richard Bromfield OK, here's the deal. I'm Richie Bromfield, and I'm 14 years old. And I'm about as hardcore as you can get! You know, one of those guys you "always" see in the Dawg Pound at GDWA events? Yeah, whatever. Anyways, I guess a lot of the suits and reporters are always wondering why the hell we always cheer for Lady Tiger. Yeah, she's one of those gals who always kisses up to the common fans. Wrestles "by the book". Goody-goody type. We HATE girls like that!! Usually. Yeah, Officer Order, Daisy Butterfly. Stick 'em up against 'Dusa and Andrea, and they get the shit kicked out of them. Look where all that kissing up gets them in the end. But, whenever I see Lady Tiger in the ring, I'm always cheering. I usually start those damn "Tiger" chants. she kisses up to those front row brats, just like OO and Daisy. She's got that relic Charlotte [LaMancha] in her corner. Hell, she even acts a bit like OO and Daisy!! So why the hell should I cheer for her? Well, I'll explain it then. Now, I'm from Indy [Indianapolis, Illinois], which means I don't get to see the GDWA live that much. But I'm at EVERY Pay-Per-View!! That's me, sittin' in the Pound, every [ed. note: insert Azai-style word here] card, with my older brother. And I was there at Dawg Pound Nights. I wasn't gonna miss a chance to see Andrea snap OO in two!! So, first match, Nomad takin' on this chick who calls herself "Lady Tiger". Tiger, right on, should be some 140, 150 pound monster. Instead, we get this 120 lb. shrimp who needs to hit the gym. BAD. So, she's taking on Nomad, and when you take on Nomad, you know it's gonna be BLOODY!! I couldn't wait!! So, out she comes, and all she did the whole damn match was throw drop-kicks. A few kicks, a few moves here and there. I was bored to tears!! And what's worse, that slut Wendy Marshall, who couldn't cut it in Grand Dragon, comes out. 2 on 2? Yeah, sure, why not. So Tiger goes back and starts throwing those drop-kicks again. I was half asleep. A kid a few rows down starts a "Tiger sucks!" chant. I started laughing, then everybody picks it up. Damn right!! This was OUR PPV!! And they stick some under nourished tramp in the first match!?!? I was so PISSED!! And the slut actually won!! Charlotte had to come down and stop the poor girl from crying when she couldn't beat Nomad with a spinning DDT. It took TWO wrestlers for her to even stand a CHANCE against Nomad!! Bottom Line: Tiger sucked!! Thankfully, Tiger kinda disappeared after DPN. She was all teary that Charlotte got her ass kicked by Ma. Whatever. So, anyway, Founder's Day Tradition comes up next. Tiger gets in a match with Vonya. Let's see, Vonya's wrested, what, 6 or 7 times? Tiger . . once? I didn't give a damn if we didn't hear nothing from Vonya in a month. Tiger was going DOWN!! Hell, she could bring Wendy and Charlotte. The entire US Navy wasn't gonna save her ass!! The night before the card, we all piled in the car to take the drive up to San Antonio (my brother managed to convince mom to let us go). I was prepared this time. I had a "Tiger Sucks!" sign with me!! Haha, the boys loved that one!! So, anyway, we get there (early, of course), and guess who's signing autographs?? Yup, Lady Tiger!! We got in line, went up to her, and asked her to sign our sign we'd made up just for her!! The boys in back were cracking up!! She got this big smile on her face, and said "Certainement, mes amis!" OH MAN!!! You shoulda SEEN the look on her face when we showed it to her!! We were cracking up laughing; I thought she was gonna start balling right there!! Some guards are getting on our case, whatever. Tiger runs off, man, it was priceless!! But man, I never DREAMED what was gonna happen later that night . . . and how I'd be eating my words!! So, FINALLY, things get started. And in the first match; you guessed it, Lady Tiger!! Haha, still stuck in opening match status?? she finally comes down the ring, and who's she got for a manager?? Ug, Charlotte LaMancha!! Just when I thought we got rid of that bitch!! Man, was I ever pissed!! I held that sign up as high as I could! So, the match starts. Tiger wants a shake. Vonya hands her her ass. She was getting her ass whooped, big time!! Then, Vonya makes a mistake, and tiger gets her with a bulldog of the top ropes. Yeah, big deal. Valkyrie's done better. Then, tiger starts throwing those damn dropkicks again, and I sat down. But this time, she starts going up top more. Man, she can really fly!! But . . . who cares, Vonya's gonna start kickin' her ass any second now. Right? But it didn't happen. Tiger kept hitting her. And hitting her. And hitting her. Up, down, over, this girl was coming from everywhere!! Still, she can dropkcik Vonya 100 times, and she still won't be able to pin her. Not enough weight behind the attacks. Eventually, she grabs her by the hair, points outside, and throws Vonya out. Man, I remember Vonya looking really old. Why the hell isn't she fighting back?? Then, Tiger brought me to my feet. She hit the ropes, jumped over the ropes, and hit Vonya with . . . something!! Whatever it was, it kicked ass. Vonya went flying back and over the railing. A few guys next to me start cheering. Big deal. So she learned a new move. Vonya's over in 3rd row somewhere, and Tiger climbs up to the top rope for cheers. Damn prima dona. I hold my sign up for her to see. But she never looked at me. Or anybody. Except Vonya. Then, I saw the move that changed my opinion of Tiger. Forever . . . . . More to come in Part 2!!
