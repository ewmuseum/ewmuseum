Andrea Chandler 36% Medusa Rage 36% The former World Heavyweight Champion is *still* one of the most hated in the sport...is anyone surprised. Fans are just waiting for her ultimate demise. Dementia Praecox 30% Fans and wrestlers alike may hate her guts, but they pay attention when she opens her mouth. The current World's Champ has taken over the spot traditionally held by Nikita Marx with her lunacy and general anecdotal style. Though others were mentioned, the rest of the votes generally went to Andrea Chandler and Medusa Rage. Andrea is a rather poignantely spoken woman who can make a wrestler feel small with her great articulation. Medusa on the other hand speaks strongly, more often and with GREAT authority! MISFITS 50% Suicide Blondes 50% The MISFITS wil have to share this one. The Age of Rage is never short on words, and the same must be said for the World Tag Champs. Their pernicious comments regarding their opponents need to be censored. And more often than not, they stir hatred where mere loathing existed!! Oh boy...what can I say? These girls are ridiculous, but even I was taken aback by their slander against Medusa Rages' father. The Blondes have the eyes AS WELL as the ears of the wrestling public. My question to the GDWA is: where are the fan favorite tag teams? These two teams garnered 100% of the vote by themselves. 8) Intimidation is a factor that is very difficult to measure. Some might look at win loss records, others at interviews and what wrestlers have to say. But the true meaning of intimidation is in the air a woman gives off. We asked our wrestlers who most exemplifies the 'don't mess with me' attitude most successfully. Andrea Chandler 60% The former World's Champ is the SINGLE most intimidating wrestler in Grand Dragon. Wrestlers realize that they are in for a battle every time they enter the ring with her...fan favorite OR rule breaker. More than any other wrestler, she comes across as having an undefiable will and a back that won't bend. Never having a loss for words regarding herself, Andrea Chandler stated,"You didn't see anybody rushing to sign the dotted line after [Founder's Day Tradition], now did ya? That's right, because I'm going to leave a trail of bodies behind until I get some key people in the ring, and everybody knows that." Other wrestler like Bloody Mary, Radhi Ananda and Ma Porter were mentioned as well. But coming in at a close second was Medusa Rage. Wrestlers are leary of her underhanded gang style and realize it is going to be a fight to the finish against the matriarch of the Age of Rage. 9) A few of us broadcasters sat around one night and wondered which of the wrestlers on our roster just doesn't seem that menacing. Here are the results: Wrestler's vote: "Fear Factory" Lanny Manson vs. Andrea Chandler Fan's vote: "Dangerous Queen" Sachie Yokoyama vs Officer Order Honorable mention goes to the Ma Porter vs. Officer Order #1 contender's Lumberjack match at the Summer Supercard last year. We asked both fans and wrestlers what the hottest feud was in the Grand Dragon. And the rist of Bloody Mary's career leaves many quetioning when she'll lay claim to a GDWA title. The war may run well into the summer, and fans hope even unto the Fall Moonsault pay per view. Wrestler on the other hand see a trend in the GDWA. A trend in which 'old schoolers'--those aligned with Grand Dragon's ICW & DPW roots--are up against the new schoolers. This has crossed the divison of fan favorite and rule breaker and the tensions have been rising thick in the locker rooms! 13) As this survey is partially an analysis of 'the year in review' we must take a look at prior feuds. Andrea Chandler 80% Medusa Rage No fan favorite even came close in this category which is telling of the state of Grand Dragon.
