I. History The Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance was created by the inspirational leader ship of territorial promoter [name withheld]. The GDWA traces its history to [name withheld]'s Dawg Pound Wrestling. Dawg Pound was a rough and ready federation of new and old stars who were ready for global prominence. The first Women's Division match up was in front of 50,000 screaming fans which pit Ma Porter against 'Fear Factory' Lanny Manson--followed by 'Luscious' Lisa Thomas taking on Sonya Blade--on February 24, 1996 at the Municipal Stadium in Cleveland, Ohio. The Women's Division featured such stars as 'Luscious' Lisa Thomas, Sonya Blade, Ma Porter, 'Fear Factory' Lanny Manson, Officer Order and Zaranna. Wrestler of the year ANDREA CHANDLER Due to expansion and the departure of several promoters, Dawg Pound Wrestling was reborn as Internet Championship Wrestling on April 4th, 1996 in Tacoma, Washington. The ICW under President [name withheld] hadn't the hard edge of DPW, and featured more traditional mat wrestling. Hardcore DPW loyalists retained a section in the crowd known as the DAWG POUND and supported the less EXTREME Internet Championship Wrestling. On that card, Officer Order took on Demonica, Valkyrie wrestled Vonya, and 'La Femme Nikita' Nikita Marx wrestled Dementia Praecox. This was a time when new talent was slowly entering the ICW. With that, the Women's Division blossomed as other divisions in the promotion seemed to be in turmoil. Because of conflict with the various promoters and higher ups in the ICW, it quickly folded. But within all of that chaos, renowned territorial promoter [name withheld] was able to maintain the Women's Division and drew thousands of fans under the defunct ICW name. Still employing the traditional mat wrestling that gave them prominence, as a Maverick Independent promotion, it was also able to be a little more Hardcore than before. On April 21st of 1996, with attendance at an all time high, the Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance was born. It quickly blossomed as the premier Women's federation around and one of the best in ALL of professional wrestling. It has since had its first pay per view, The Summer Supercard, and prepares trans federational action with other promotions. Back to Main Page
