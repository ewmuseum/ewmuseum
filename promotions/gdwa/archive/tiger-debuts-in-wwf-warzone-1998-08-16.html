Tiger Debuts in WWF Warzone!!

16 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This past week, Acclaim's new "WWF Warzone" game hit store shelves nationwide. Gaming fans everywhere snatched up a copy, relishing the opportunity to compete using their favorite WWF personas. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin!!! The Undertaker!!! Triple H!!! Lady Tiger!!! Huh? Well folks, believe it! Making her video game debut appearance, in the white-and-black striped singlet, the masked sensation from Bordeaux, France . . . Lady Tiger! Gregory Fischbach, president of Acclaim Entertainment, summed it up: "Acclaim Entertainment is all about giving the people what they want. So when we got this tremendous outcry from wrestling fans on the internet, requesting to see Lady Tiger make an appearance in our Warzone game . . . well, we didn't just ignore them. We decided to do something about it." The reason Tiger made the final cut can be thanked, in part, to Scoops Central. Al Issacs comments on the turn of events which led to Tiger being integrated into the game: "During the programming stages of the game, Scoops Central offered up a question to our loyal readers. We asked which wrestlers not presently on the WWF Warzone' roster, would you most like to see? Well, the general outcry from the fans was that they wanted to see Kane and the Headshrinkers make the final cut. However, we also received a lot of requests to see a non-WWF wrestler, Lady Tiger, make an appearance in the game." Also of assistance was a petition circulated over the net, by long-time tiger fan Richie Bromfield. In the period of just two weeks, thousands of names were added to a list, demanding that Acclaim add Lady Tiger to the Warzone line-up. "We were a bit worried at first of putting Tiger in the game", comments Steven Lux, the marketing whiz of Acclaim, "for the reason that this game was produced under a WWF license. If would be a bit like seeing Stone Cold in a WCW game. However, since three of the competitors in Warzone (British Bulldog, Bret Hart, Ahmed Johnson) are no longer affiliated with the WWF, and two (Bret, Bulldog) work for (WCW) . . . we decided that if we were ever going to do it, now would be the best time." Steve continues, "We couldn't really make her one of the default characters, as we didn't want to incur the wrath of the World Wrestling Federation. The WWF and Acclaim Sports have a very good relationship, and we didn't want to do anything to jeopardize that. After extended talks with their legal and PR department, they finally gave us the go-ahead to add Lady Tiger as a secret character to the game." GDWA announcer Allen Bishop wasn't surprised something like this happened. "The recent popularity of Lady Tiger has been absolutely phenomenal! While she has always been popular with the fans in general, her recent immature antics have garnered her a real cult' following on the internet . . . the likes of which I've never seen!" To put a punctuation mark on that last statement, we here at tiger.com received an e-mail from'#1TigerFan', who claimed, "The first time I pulled off the Longbow Frankensteiner, I was flipping out so much I forgot to actually *pin* the guy!!!" WWF Warzone is available for the Sony PlayStation and Nintendo 64. At press time, Acclaim had no plans to release a GDWA game for either system. Lady Tiger and Charlotte LaMancha could not be reached for comment. Coming soon! Moves and combos for Lady tiger!
