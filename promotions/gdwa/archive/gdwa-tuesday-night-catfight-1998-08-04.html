Tuesday Night Catfight, 4 August 1998 (Part 3)

Bishop: Fans, up next we have the "Loser Leaves" the GDWA match. Cameron and Kishiro going at it. But before that, I've just been notified that Medusa Rage is in the building.
Mutt: Well, she was supposed to defend her title against Andrea Chandler this week, but Denmark Vessey cancelled the contract. After that fiasco with Ma Porter on Saturday Night, Medusa is in the Dawg
House. Bishop: But Andrea Chandler gets screwed out of a world title shot. One would think Andrea may take her anger out on Dianna Prescott. Mutt: Yeah, well we'll see. Prescott is nobody's punching bag. Bishop: Speaking of punching bags, tonight, Laura vs. Shumira... Mutt: I don't understand the logic of this match. Laura forsakes the
Steel Cage match at FDT II in order to wrestle a "Loser Leaves" town match on our final house show. Where's the logic? WE all know that
Sweeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeet is gonna win this! Bishop: Come off it Sam! Mutt: I'm serious. Laura Cameron has slapped the taste outta Shumira's mouth each and every time they've met. Curtain call on a career.
Bishop: Fans, MIke Whalen is backstage with Laura Cameron, let's go to this exclusive interview.
----------------------------------------------------------------------- [GDWA Interviewer outside "Sweet" Laura Cameron's dressing room.] Whalen: We're here ready for.... [Suddenly "The Dominator" Don Cameron comes out of the dressing room, dressed in a pair of jeans and a "SWEETNESS" t-shirt.]
Cameron: Sorry man, but right now Laura's still preparing herself for this big match. So you got me this time.
Whalen: This is great! I actually get to interview the former NCCW Heavyweight Champion. But since this isn't the NCCW, I'm gonna ask you about Laura. First off, what do you think of her chances in this match against the
'Avengar' Shumira Kishiro?
Cameron: I've been following the GDWA since it opened, and ever since
Sierra was in that three-way dance for the GDWA World Title where
Officer Order won. Winning the Cruiserweight Title was a blessing for her, but Laura has the tenacity and the brains to be an even greater champion. After Laura sends Shumira out of the GDWA, the
Cruiserweight title is next on our agenda. Whalen: Since Laura's joined the GDWA she's been in a lot of turmoil. First the injury caused by Sally McLane, then the reappearance as Sweet
Emotion, and then coming back as a heel, and now she wants to be a fan favorite? What's going on inside her head? What makes Laura Cameron tick?
Cameron: Laura, like any other person feels like she wants to be accepted. When she first came here she was accepted by the fans. Then they forgot who she was after her return and she didn't feel accepted anymore. So she changed, and it helped her out a lot. Pretty soon she had a major feud going on with The Avenger and the crowd didn't like her attitude at all. So I sat down with her and listened to what she had to say. I realized that she needed help from me, so here I am.
Whalen: Will you be at ringside tonight? Cameron: Since nobody is allowed at ringside during this match, I'm going to be watching on the short-circuit television, cheering on my wife.
Whalen: Thanks for the little talk, Don. Cameron: Anytime, man. Take care. ----------------------------------------------------------------------- Spud: Wrestling fans, this Cruiserweight bout is a "Loser Leaves" the
GDWA Submission match. It is scheduled for one fall with a 15 minute time limit. It will be officiated by referees Aileen Balahadia and
William Andrews... (Fans all booing as they hear "Sweet Emotion" by Aerosmith.) Spud: Hailing from San Jose, California. She is 5 feet 7 inches, 128 pounds, here is "Sweet" Laura Cameron! ["Sweet Emotion" plays to a mixed reaction from the crowd. Huge cheers as "The Dominator" Don Cameron will come out from behind the curtains, wearing a pair of jeans and a "SWEETNESS" t-shirt. He will give high fives to as many fans as he can. His light brown moppy hair will be pulled back in a ponytail, he will have a black armband on his left arm, and a LMS necklace, a tribute to his wife. He will make his way to the ring and pull off his t-shirt, giving it to a fan in the front row.
Then he will climb into the ring and ask for the mic.] Cameron: Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce to you my wife.... "Sweet" Laura Cameron!!! [There is a smaller cheer for Laura as she comes out from behind the curtains. Wearing a red and white bodysuit, it looks like she has bulked up some. SWEETNESS is written on the front of her bodysuit, with
R-E-S-P-E-C-T written on the back. Her reddish-brown hair is pulled back in a scrunchee. She offers to give high fives to the fans who will allow her, and then throws her scrunchee into the crowd. For a first, it isn't thrown back. She climbs into the ring and gives Don a hug, whereas he leaves ringside and gives as many high fives as he can.]
Bishop: Laura in the biggest match of her career. She's met adversity since day one in Grand Dragon. Sally McClane put her out of the sport for half a year, and then she lose her fan base.
Mutt: But she has such an edge now. Laura Cameron, I salute you. Bishop: Oh please. Spud: And her opponent... (Minor cheers as fans hear 'From Her to Eternity' by Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds.) Spud: Hailing from Southern Arizona, she is 5 feet 3 inches, 125 pounds, here is 'the Avengar' Shumira Kishiro!
(Fans cheer as Shumira heads down the aisle. She enters the ring wearing a greay and black tiger striped bodysuit with black combat boots and fingerless gloves.)
Bishop: One thing you can say about Shumira Kishiro, she is all biz. She totally focussed on 'Sweet' Laura Cameron. Mutt: Shumira heading down the aisle as Laura dips through the ropes and flips her off. These two can't wait to face off in the ring.
Bishop: NO love lost at all. Mutt: Shumira in the ring, and Laura smacking gum and mocking the Avengar. Bishop: Let's not waste any time. The ref going over the rules, and here we go! *Ding*Ding*Ding*Ding* (Fans continue to chant: Laura Sucks! Laura Sucks! as Cameron dips through the ropes and curses out ringsiders.)
Bishop: Collar and Elbow tie up, and SHumira tosses her to the canvas! (Fans pop!) Mutt: Laura Cameron rolling to the outside, and as you know Bishop, this is the pivotal difference between these two. Laura is quick and nimble, sorta like Lady Tiger...but she's just as weak. Shumira is tough and sturdy like a rock.
Ref: 2...........................3......................4........... Bishop: Laura back into the ring, and now both ladies encircling each other. Collar and Elbow tie up, and Laura with a Droptoe hold! Laura with an Armbar, and Shumira getting beted with Laura's technical acumen.
Mutt: Shumira reaching for the ropes, and Laura holding her own. A pick up now and right back into an Armdrag. Look at the conservatism of Cameron. SHe knows a minor mistake in this one will send her packing.
Bishop: Shumira shoving Laura into the ropes, and the ref wants a break. Shumira with an Irish Whip and Laura is sent to the far side.
Mutt: Laura bouncing off the far side and Shumira with a Harlem Side Kick! (Fans all pop!) Bishop: A pick up by Shumira and she nails away with "Cat Fury" punches to the head. Laura is dazed, and now a Thrust Kick to the head! (Fans al pop as Shumira heads for the near buckles.) Mutt: Shumira climbing up top, and measuring Laura. Shumira flying off the top and...._misses_ the Senton Splash!
Bishop: Laura slow t oher feet, and now a pick up. Frontface lock and she executes a Vertical Suplex! Laura now, with an Arm Scissors and Shumira slapping the canvas in pain.
Mutt: Shumira into the ropes, and Laura won't let go. Balahadia: 1..............2..............3...........4.............! Bishop: Laura releasing and now a pick up. An Armdrag and Laura snapping down on the Armbar. Shumira gritting her teeth but she nails a hard right hand!
Mutt: And another! And Another! Shumira with an Irish Whip to the far ropes. Laura bouncing off and Shumira with a Military Press....
(Fans all pop as Shumira walks around with her.) Spud: 5 minutes have gone by in the 15 minute time limit. Bishop: and drops her down across her lap for a Gutbuster! (Fans all pop: "oooooooooooooooh" as Shumira clutches her midsection.) Bishop: Shumira with a pick up and a Double Arm Underhook...but Laura reversing out with an Armdrag and now into Fireman's Carry Takeover.
Mutt: And she falls back for a Cross Armbar! (Fans chant: Laura Sucks! Laura Sucks! Laura Sucks! Laura Sucks! ) Mutt: Shumira slapping the canvas in pain, and Laura has been wearing down the arm all night. One of these ladies is gonna have to sumbit their career away.
(Ringsiders all on their feet as the ref slides into position.) Bishop: Ref Aileen Balahadia asking the question. And Shumira shaking her head no. Laura smiling as she rocks into the hold!
(Fans chant: Laura Sucks! Laura Sucks! Laura Sucks! Laura Sucks! ) Bishop: Shumira into the ropes, and the ref forcing the break. Laura laughing heartily as she picks up Shumira and applies an Armdrag.
Mutt: SHumira hooking the ropes, and the ref wants a break. An Irish Whip by Laura and Shumira is sent to the farside. Shumira bouncing off as Laura sprints across the ring...
Bishop: And Shumira baseball slides through her legs. Laura twirling around as Shumira gets to her feet and DIVES into Laura with a
Forearm Shot to the middle. Mutt: Laura doubling over and Shumira slaps her head between her legs. Shumira with a Waistlock pick up and HOISTS her up into the air.
Bishop: A Powerbomb?...no! Shumira with a head of steam and NAILS the Ligerbomb!!! (Fans all pop!) Mutt: Shumira with a pickup....STANDING DROPKICK! (Fans pop!) Mutt: Laura is dazed, and Shumira with another pick up. Shumira nailing away with those "Cat Fury" punches to the head. Shumira with an Irish Whip to the far ropes...
Bishop: Reversal! Shumira into the far ropes as Laura runs to the middle of the ring. Shumi bouncing off and Laura dropping down to the canvas.
Bishop: Shumira bouncing off the ropes as Laura slowly gets to her feet and Laura with a Monkey Flip takeover.
Mutt: Both women back up and Shumira with a Clothesline...but Laura ducks and EXECUTES a Belly to Belly Suplex!
(Fans all booing as both ladies lay on the canvas.) Spud: 10 minutes have gone by in the 15 minute time limit. Mutt: Laura showing signs of movement, and SHumira is dazed. Laura slow to her feet, and a pick up...but Shumira with a Forearm shot to the middle.
Bishop: And now a High Back Body Drop.... (Dawg Pound cheers as Laura Cameron lands on her feet.) Mutt: Shumira turning around and Laura Cameron cutting into her with a Lariat... Bishop: But Shumira sidesteps and grabs the arm out of nowhere tackling
Laura Cameron to the canvas...
(Fans all pop as Shumira traps the left arm with a Scissors.) Bishop: AND SHUMIRA SLAPS ON THE "Avenger Crossface"! (Fans all screaming as Shumira pulls back on the hold.) Mutt: THAT CAME FROM NOWHERE!!! (Fans all on their feet as the ref gets in Cameron's face.) Bishop: Cameron in the middle of the ring, and Shumira rocking into it! My lord, Aileen Balahadia asking the question and tears running down Laura's face...
((FANS ALL ~~SCREAMING~~)) Bishop: She's asking the Question! She's asking the Question! Mutt: S H E 'S T A P P I N G O U T ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! *Ding*Ding*Ding*Ding* (FANS ALL ~~SCREAMING~~ as fans hear "From Her to Eternity" by Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds.) Bishop: Laura is out of here! Mutt: Shumira has done it! Spud: Wrestling fans, at 12 minutes and 23 seconds, your winner via submission....'the Avengar' Shumira Kishiro!
(Fans all pop as Laura Cameron rolls out of the ring and flips off Shumira.) Spud: As a result, 'Sweet' Laura Cameron must leave the GRAND DRAGON WRESTLING ALLIANCE! (Fans all pop as Shumira's hand is raised in victory.) Mutt: 'Luscious' Lisa Thomas lost a "Loser Leaves" the GDWA match to
'Dangerous Queen" Sachie Yokoyama 2 years ago. SHe's just now been re-instated to the singles division. And now we can add Laura to the list.
Bishop: Hard fought win by Shumira. Laura dominated this bout, but
Shumira took it up a notch in the closing minutes to secure the win.
Mutt: Shumira with a must win. And you know, Bishop, after %%% Founder's
Day Tradition II %%% Shumira could garner the next shot at the Cruiserweight title. Bishop: Well fans, as we... hey ... wait just a minute. What are these guys doing here? (Scott Spade, Andrew "Flash" Tucker and Mr. Odd are all seen sitting in the front row. Spade has blue eyes. His hair is a dirty blonde color and is a little past his shoulders. He's got a tattoo on his left arm, going downwards at an angle it says "DaNGeRouS" in red blood-like lettering.
He's got a medium build. He's wearing a pair of black sunglasses, a plain black T-shirt that has "Caution" in yellow and black & underneath it says "Contents may be harmful to health. Keep out of the reach from small children", a long black leather trench coat, his tights (pants style) which are just plain black, and a pair of black boots. Andrew
"Flash" Tucker is a small man, about the size of a Rey Mysterio or
Ultimo Dragon. He has a bronzed body, steely blue eyes and long, wavy blond hair. He's wearing long black tights that say "Flash" on one leg.
Mr. Odd has a hat covering his hair and is wearing a pair of dark black sunglasses, with a black T-shirt with a "GDWA" logo on the sleeve, and a pair of black khakis. Andrew "Flash" Tucker has a notepad and pen in his hands and is obviously taking notes on the competition.)
Mutt: ... What? Who is it?! Bishop: If I'm not mistaken, that's Scott Spade, his tag partner Andrew
"Flash" Tucker, and Mr. Odd. Okay folks, we're gonna try to get someone in the GDWA crew to go and see what's going on with them and why they're here.
Whalen: Yes, I'm here. Well, ladies and gentlemen I'm standing here with Scott Spade. This man I'm standing beside is also the International
Hardcore Wrestling Vice President and has been most known for beating up fans. He's been arrested on a few occasions. Well, Mr. Spade, I guess the big question on everyone's mind is just what _ARE_ you doing here?!
(Spade looks calmly into the camera as he begins to speak.) Scott Spade: ... What am I doing here? Sarah Summers ... you called for assistance. Your savior .... (Tucker and Mr. Odd both rise from their seats as they stand beside Spade) ... has come to your aid. Make of it what you will ... but now you got all the help you're gonna need.
(Tucker pushes the interviewer out of their face as they all sit back down.) Spud: Wrestling fans, our next contest is a Semifinal match up in the
Demonica Endurance Challenge. It is scheduled for one fall with a 15 minute time limit...and it is for the Western Heritage
Championship! (The crowd pops as Big Wreck's 'That Song' starts up.) Spud: Accompanied to the ringside by 'Jumping' Jennifer Grier! Hailing from Tampa, Florida. She is 6 feet 2 inches, 201 pounds, here is 'Ultima' Sarah Summers!!
(The crowd cheers on as Sarah Summers strides through the entrance. Sarah talks into a camera as follows her down the asile. Sarah is dressed in her traditional sleeveless red bodysuit with a black tiger-stripe print, matching striped wristbands; black padded boots and kneepads complete the garb, and her shoulder length auburn hair is tied back in a ponytail.)
Bishop: Sarah SUmmers has risen through the ranks at a near unprecedented speed. Andrea Chandler and Demonica are the only other women in GDWA history to boast similar dominance.
Mutt: Summers is big and strong. Okay, that's all nice and dandy. But she's got incredible reserve. Just when ya think she's beat, she's pulls this LannY Manson crap on ya and hulks-up.
(Ultima enters the ring and walks to the middle where she turns to the crowd and offers a quick muscle pose, slapping her biceptes and yelling out to the crowd.)
Bishop: Fans, Nikita Marx may be in the biggest match of her career. No doubt, she faces a dirty Russian that has no plans of losing her title. Let's hear comments from Marx who's already looking forward to %%% Founder's Day Tradition II %%%
----------------------------------------------------------------------- (Shot opens on dark laboratory, as a flashlight illuminates a large heavily locked door we can see a sign which reads " FBI Evidence Lock
Up- DNA Testing Lab 23". As the light gets closer we see the flashlight is held by none other than 'le Femme Nikita' Nikita Marx. Marx is dressed in a black jumpsuit and black knit cap in a dark and black back pack. After she deftly unlocks the safe, she turns and smiles at the camera. )
Nikita: (in hushed whisper) Good Evening Darlings ... sorry but I had to do a quick favor for an old friend.
( Nikita steps into the evidence lock up, she appears to be looking for something. As the camera follows her, she turns to face it. )
Nikita: (Still in a hushed whisper) I hope you all enjoyed the Primetime Theater of Pain as much as I did. I am so excited to have won my very first title Darlings ... now if only they would rename it to the Eastern Heritage title everything would be perfect.
( Nikita finds the locked steel box she was looking for and sets it gingerly on a near by table. After looking at the large lock on the box, she takes off her backpack and begins to open it.)
Nikita: (Still in a hushed whisper) I must say that I am very sorry about what happened to Sierra. While we have had our problems in the past, Ms. Browne is truly a force to be reckoned with. But she has been some what confused of late, like I said before Sierra you should learn from Muhammad Ali...
( Pulling out a lock pick set, Nikita begins to work on the lock while never taking her eyes off the camera. )
Nikita: (Still in a hushed whisper) You remember when they took his title away because he would not fight in Viet Nam ? He said he had nothing against the Viet Cong, he knew who his real enemy was. Do You
Sierra ? I would hate to see you end up Driving for Ms. Daisy.
( The lock pops open with little trouble and Nikita quickly puts away her picks. After a second she takes a plastic bag containing a small blue cocktail dress out of the evidence box. )
Nikita: ( her voice now getting a little louder) Ah yes, The Franchise, to be sure one of the most annoying wrestlers in the GDWA, but Wrestler of the year ? Darling Please !
( Putting the bag with the dress into her backpack, she quickly pulls out a replacement and slips it back in the box. )
Nikita: ( now talking at her regular volume ) Ms. Buzzingfly you will finally be forced to face me. I warned you that you would have to deal with me, and now you see the truth, yes ?
( After locking the box, she places it back where it was, and then sits on the table top.) Nikita: Your lucky that I waited until could get you in the ring. I've had so many opportunities to take you out. Putting a hit on you would have been easy, but it would not have ... how do you say it ?
Satisfied me, Yes ? ( She smiles like a tiger eyeing her next meal, seeming to forget where she is. ) Nikita: I don't just want to hurt you or take your title. I want to break you ! I Want To Crush You ! I WANT TO BURY YOU ! ( Catching herself getting very loud, Nikita begins to move out of the Evidence lockup. Closing the door and re-locking it behind her.)
Nikita: (back to a hushed whisper) And I will ...Oh Yes, I will Bury
You Ms Buzzingfly and none of your so called friends can help you. Just remember, Capitalism is a wonderful thing. Anything can be had for the
Right Price. Are you willing to pay to win Daisy ? Are you willing to do what it takes ?
( Walking over to an open window, Nikita climbs out and attaches a repelling rig to a section of black rope which hangs down from the rooftop.)
Nikita: You forget Daisy darling, I am a professional. I'm willing to take any chances, in Fact I'm willing to pay the ultimate price, My life in exchange for yours. Are you willing to do the same ?
( and with that Nikita drops out of sight and the camera goes dark.) ----------------------------------------------------------------------- Spud: And her opponent, representing Organized Crime! ...'La Femme Nikita' Nikita Marx! (As the lights dim, the Theme from the Godfather comes over the sound system.
