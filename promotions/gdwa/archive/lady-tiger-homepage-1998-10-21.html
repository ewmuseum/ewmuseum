" . . . making up for whatever lack of size with *incredible* daring!" - " . . . and Charlotte LaMancha directing traffic on the outside . . . " Bonjour, mes amis! Welcome to the homepage of one of the hottest cruiserweights in the GDWA, Lady Tiger!!! Last updated October 21st, 1998 Messages First off, a little about her stomping grounds. GDWA stands for "Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance"; the premiere female wrestling fed on the net, and one of the hottest e-feds of all time! The competition is fierce and hungry. But the handlers are mature, the cards unparalleled, and the flash shows well organized. There's a link at the bottom of the page, if you want to find more information on the GDWA. Lady Tiger burst onto the scene on November 24th, at the Dawg Pound Nights PPV. Her opponent in her debut match was the powerful Nomad. The match went back and forth, Tiger relying on her speed and quickness to combat the toughness of the Nomad. Eventually, Tiger managed to get the pin after her idol, Charlotte LaMancha, came down to offer encouragement. However, Tiger was less than sharp in her debut. Chants of "Tiger Sucks!" were heard from the dawg pound. Grappling over whether she'd made the right decision coming to America, she decided to return to Europe, to further train, and try to conquer some personal problems. The deciding factor which helped Tiger make her decision about returning to Grand Dragon was the retirement of her idol, Charlotte LaMancha. Charlotte had been the victim of a brutal attack later at Dawg Pound Nights, and Tiger felt guilty for not being there to save her. After a long talk with her idol, set in a hospital room in France, Charlotte convinced Tiger to return to the GDWA. Her next match was at the Founder's Day Tradition PPV. Lady Tiger had promised everybody a huge surprise, and she delivered! The GDWA Hall-of-Famer herself, charlotte LaMancha, came down as the manager of Lady Tiger! With the voice of experience residing in her corner, Tiger turned out to be too much to handle for her adversary, Vonya. During the match, Tiger's daredevil style had the entire arena behind her. Even the dawg pound was behind Tiger as she defied gravity like only she can, and managed to get the pin using Charlotte's old finisher, "The Longbow Frankensteiner"! Today, Lady Tiger has distinguished herself as one of the hottest new rookies to Grand Dragon. Only time will tell how far her career will take her, as she finally gets a chance to challenge the upper echelon of Grand Dragon's roster!
