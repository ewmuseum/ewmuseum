31 October – 19 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As for having separate womens' divisions in existing e-feds: it might not be a bad idea to encourage the development of this oft-neglected aspect of wrestling. It could serve to broaden e-wrestling's appeal, which is currently dominated by adolescent males with a smattering from other perspectives. What could be wrong with having a few ladies around here? What say you, fed-heads? Robert Robert and everyone else ;) There are quite a few e-wrestling feds that push WOmen's Wrestlers. The MWA is a new fed that is ONLY for WOMen's Wrestling. The IEWA is also a new one that has a STRONG WOmen's Division. (Not to mention Super Japan Pro Wrestling!!!) ANd of course, you have the GDWA, Grand Dragon Wrestling ALliance, that is 2 years STRONG and is ONLY for WOmen's Wrestlers. I find the handlers to be more mature and better role players in Women's Feds. 1) NO copycat wrestlers. 2) It takes a certain maturity to 'get in the mind' of a Woman athlete and handle her well. 3) It's not the first type of wrestler that comes to mind. These creations must be culled forth from our creative unconscious! Since I started this string, I figure I'd jump in again! I think it is pitiful that she is the fIRST and ONLY woman to hit the Top 200....but Women's Wreslting isn't prolific enough to warrant their own list. What, you have a handful of feds that treat their Women's division with any sort of respect. The ESWP, the GDWA, SJPW, the IEWA and a few others that escape me at the moment. But it is not enough to have 5 wrestlers in a women's division and consider it on par with let's say the GDWA or the IEWA. Heck, you can't even call that a title woth wearing. Now, if you are building up your women's promotion that's a whole different issue. Women's wrestling with probably need a few more YEARS before the wrestlers themselves become popular enough to get recognition in the awards. With quality promotions like my buds over at Super Japan putting out Women's matches, who also have a men's division, that's a start. But take a peek at the e-wrestling Hall of Fame, and ironically you'll find Womens e-wrestlers there. We must continue to plod along and do our duty as pioneers. :) Comments anyone?
