GDWA 2 Pieces of Monkey Crap: Melanie Gilbert on getting over

dated 25 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
2 Pieces of Monkey Crap by "the Sports Fan" Melanie Gilbert That's right, the _only_ Internet sheet reporter who's worth her weight in salt..."the Sports Fan" Melanie Gilbert telling it like it is! This week I will be addressing the problem facing every wrestler in the GDWA . . . The NeW Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance is going through my favorite period of an organization's life: the beginning. That is because "in the beginning" none of the wrestlers have political clout backstage with the regional promoters nor do they have an easy rise to the top. In essence, they have to GET OVER on their own. They have to get over with the fans. So the perennial question asked is, 'just how does one do that?' The "Sports Fan" Melanie Gilbert has often thought about havin' a little job on the side. I would open a camp for athletes soley focussed on trying to do 2 things: develop their wrestling skills and helping them to develop an "image". I say develop an "image" because one can not create an image. One creates a "gimmick". Developing an "image" is not the same thing as creating a "gimmick". A "gimmick" is corporate and involves marketing. An "image" is the persona that a wrestler has; part of her personality really contains that "image" that she is projecting. For example, the "Sports Fan" would be my gimmick. I'd come to the ring dressed in the latest jersey of some kind of football/basketball/baseball team. Whatever team or person is hot at the time, I'd be sportin' it! And I would talk that talk that all jocks talk. But my the attitude and the way I carry myself--my actions and such--would convey an "image" of an old cranky bitch who really doesn't give a damn about anyone but myself...and that's pretty much true to who I am! Still confused? Let's evaluate the top 3 Babyfaces of the OLD School G.D.W.A . . . 1. Daisy Butterfly (before she turned into a rulebreaker) 2. Officer May Order 3. Keiko Mita Now, the "Kyoto Crippler" Keiko Mita was the badass of the three. The "gimmick" was that she was a modern day Samurai; think about it, all of the foam katanas (translation: swords) that she sold through concession stands coulda bought my parents that summer home in Connecticut they have always wanted. But that is all marketing. Sure, the fans actually saw a woman who stood or honor, but she executed her brand of honor brutally. She had a persona, an image, of being this unstoppable, dangerous, and crippling woman. (She went out and crippled a whole lot of wrestlers. And there weren't too many Cruiserweights, or Heavyweights for that matter, who wanted a piece of the "Kyoto Crippler".) She became, arguably, the greatest Western Heritage champion of all times. Keiko Mita was the "Mean" Joe Greene, Dick Butkus, and Mike Singletary of Women's wrestling. Now those men had images...and nice butts too! When we think about Officer Order and Daisy Butterfly, they could be compared to Hulk Hogan and Sting in Mens' wrestling. Order was the wily veteran who believed in that UNIVERSAL platitude called justice. Hence, her "gimmick". Her "gimmick" was of a police officer, but her "image" was all about fair play and good sportsmanship. She was of the old gaurd that did not use chairs or any kicks to the groin to get over in this era of ASS. . . did I mention Mike Singletary had a nice butt? Daisy on the other hand was losing her persona as the years went on. Sure, she still had her 'gimmick". She was the Japanese-American Superfighter who took down all of the heel stables and an evil Russian counterpart. But Daisy turned heel in the "Sports Fan's" opinion because she was losing her "image". Her persona was that of a hotshot: a got get 'em, flashy and elecrifying Superstar...but a lot of other Babyfaces had this "image", this "persona" like Rekka Sakura, Radhi Ananda and Medusa Rage. In essence, in order to get over you need to corner the market on who you are. Find your niche! What makes you different than everybody else? Now before, while, and after you kick but in the ring, project that persona in everything that you do. Don't get me wrong, it is important for "Stone Cold" to be thought of as "The Toughest S.O.B in the WWF" and the ROCK to be the "Best Sports Entertainer in the WWF today", but those are "gimmicks" that followed their success in the ring. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin had to win the KING of the RING (tm) in order to evolve as a wrestler. And he had to come up with his 3:16 code of ethics in order for us to really follow his career. Hey, let the marketing executives worry about packaging your "image" into a "gimmick". You gotta figure out who the hell you are first. As far as the new G.D.W.A goes, it will be interesting to discover who the top Babyfaces will be. They will need to persevere against the Heels and wrestle their asses off...but just as importantly, they need to convey to the fans why we should care about them. Let me close by saying this: Rumor has it that the top 8 wrestlers in the Heavyweight Division will be in that Women's World Title tournament in a few months at the GDWA's GLORY DAYS pay per view. Half of them dames are gonna have to be heels, and the other half are gonna have to be Faces. The fans are gonna have a lot to say about who gets selected. Now, not later, is the time for these athletes to pick up their game and Wrestle their asses off. And more importantly, now is the time to get noticed by the fans. Now is the time to find or who you are and what makes you different from other wrestlers. "To Thine Own Self Be True" or something like that. That's 2 PIECES OF MONKEY CRAP this week. Agree or disagree with my opinion, everyone's got one, but the only one that matters is mine! Go Back To Main Page
