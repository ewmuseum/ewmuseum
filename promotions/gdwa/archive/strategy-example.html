GDWA strategy example: The Masked Superstar

Subject: [GDWA] Strat - The Masked Superstar Date: Fri, 04 Sep 1998 13:29:40 PDT From: "[name withheld]" <[detail withheld]> To: [detail withheld] The BIG Return!! The Masked Superstar is well rested and her game plan will emphasize quickness and suplexes. The opening: Bertha X might expect The Masked Superstar to come in all full of anger and fury but that is just the opposite. The Masked Superstar will wrestle slow, working Bertha X's left arm with armbar's, cross armbreakers and wristlocks. The Masked Superstar will also incorporate strong kicks to the body and to the. This should lead to a more ground oriented gameplan, still working the left arm. Submission moves like the fujiwara arm bar and flying hammerlock also coming in to play. The power attack will include body slams and variety of suplexes from behind (dragon ,tiger and release geramn) Middle: The Masked Superstar will stick with the left arm and also start to work her speed in to the match. Lots of off the ropes moves (running clothesline, dropkick) as well as still using the armbars and hammerlock. Also winging the neckbreaker and tornado DDT may come in handy as countermoves. The Close: The Masked Superstar will no doubt be behind at least some point in the match, a way to rally i the classic bulldog. From there it will be a full blown assault on Bertha X's left arm!! Shoulder drivers, armbar hammerlockas again. For power the classic Bodyslam and Legdrop combo! If it becomes apparent that Bertha X will notgive then The Masked Superstar will use a german suplex as the precusor to the powerbomb and a pin.
