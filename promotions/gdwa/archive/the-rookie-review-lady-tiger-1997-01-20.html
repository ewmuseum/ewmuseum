The Rookie Review: Lady Tiger

20 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[January 20th, 1997] The Rookie Review By Charlie "Flamin'" Amon Fact Sheet ---------- Wrestler's name: Lady Tiger (La Belle Tigre in France) Place of Birth: Bordeaux, France Theme Music: "Eye of the Tiger" Height: 5'6" Weight: 127 lb. Manager: None The GDWA has it's share of animals, in and outside the ring. Newcomer Lady Tiger plans to be another, though she also intends to add some honor to the term. "I love each and every one of my fans,"she says. "Unlike some of the other wrestlers here in America, I realize that, without their support and their encouragement, I wouldn't be where I am today." Lady Tiger, while a newcomer here in the GDWA, already has a solid base with the fans she loves. She's been well received here, due to her successful career in France. However, La Belle Tigre has left her homeland for the competition and excitement of North American wrestling. "It was a difficult change in my career." This newcomer brings more than wrestling excitement with her, though. She also brings a moderate share of controversy. It all revolves around two wrestlers, Francine DeLaville and Cristina Leroux. Francine was a hot young wrestler, Christina was a rich man's daughter who'd become a wrestling champion, not unlike Andrea Chandler. The two of them had been feuding all over the country, and Christina challenged the younger wrestler to a loser-leaves-wrestling match. A signed contract, a fast count and a retiring referee later, Francine was out of wrestling. Not long after, La Belle Tigre appeared on the scene. The Tiger shared a wrestling style and appearance with the young Francine, and many people, including Christina, believed they were one and the same. Christina put her career on the line against Lady Tiger's mask. Christina lost. Lady Tiger is rather tight-lipped when it comes to the resemblance between her and Francine. However, she does have a few words about her fellow Frenchwoman, Charlotte LaMancha. "Charlotte was the main reason I chose to wrestle in GDWA. I looked up to her a lot back in France, and I consider myself one of her biggest fans. It gives me much hope to see what someone from my own country could accomplish here in America." This rookie will be debuting here in Grand Dragon in very grand circumstances, she'll be wrestling her first match at the Dawg Pound Nights against the Nomad. When asked about her plans for that match, Tiger gives a smile that is equally friendly and mischievous. "Sorry! I'm not going to give away all my secrets just yet! Let's just say, ...you can expect a few more surprises from Lady Tiger; that I can guarantee!" Obviously, the Tiger is reluctant to talk about her life outside the ring. However, one well know fact is that she volunteers much of her time to organizations that deal with abused children. She explains this with her usual guarded honesty, only mentioning that she has had more experience with family violence than she would have liked. As the interview came to a close, I asked Lady Tiger what her plans in the GDWA were. She came right to the point. "[The GDWA] is full of tradition and history, and the best in the world come here to wrestle. I just hope that, someday, I'll be part of that great history too."
