BCW Card I

4 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
. . . . . 3 . . . . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . . . . 1 . . . . . . . . . . Roll the tape . . . . . x-x-x-x-x . . . WARNING . . . The following contains violence, adult themes and strong language. It may not be suited for all members of the viewing audience. Parental discretion is advised. x-x-x-x-x (A solitary black cat jumps onto a fence as "Stray Cat Strut" by the Stray Cats begins to play softly in the background. After turning his head to the left, then back to the right, the cat jumps down to begin his nocturnal prowl down the darkened alleyway. Gliding along the litter strewn alley, the cat is startled by a sound . . . causing it to spit and hiss as it arches it's back. Walking nearly sideways, the cat turns towards the direction of the sounds off to it's right. As the camera turns towards the direction of the sound, the beautiful raven haired Mean Darlene, dressed in a short black leather skirt and bustier, is seen walking closer. Over her shoulder is a heavy chain attached to a collar around the neck of the very large, 300 pound, masked Mangler, dressed completely in black, from his singlet to his matching gloves, knee pads and boots. With a quick hiss, the black cat continues on it's journey. Moving along the alley, the cat is once again startled . . . this time, a door from what appears to be an abandoned building opens. As the cat again arches it's back and starts hissing, a tall, well built man with shoulder length brown hair, full length black tights and green boots . . . his face covered by a green gas mask, Genocide steps out into the poor lit alley. The cat darts off as it continues on it's journey through the alleyways. Nearing a chainlink fence, the cat slips past a tear in the links as it makes it's way around a large brick building. Rounding the corner, the cat emerges from the rear of the building where we find several dozen people waiting in line by the front door as a good looking platinum blonde haired man with a matching goatee makes an entrance. As the fan-favorite, the Southern Gentleman, Ron Niles nears the door, he is quickly surrounded by a dozen fans all seeking his autograph. As Niles signs autographs and makes his way towards the door, the black cat can be seen deftly twisting and turning through the "Sea of Feet". Niles turns and waves to his fans has he opens the door. The black cat clears the last few sets of legs without being trampled and disappears into the building as the last few notes of "Stray Cat Strut" dies away. Chants of . . . BCW . . . BCW . . . BCW . . . are heard as the camera slowly fades out into a brilliant flash of light . . .) x-x-x-x-x (the chanting continues as we fade out of the bright light and into the arena. Panning quickly, the camera shows the small but vocal crowd of perhaps 100 or so people before it settles in on the two men sitting at a table ringside. Both men dressed the same with matching black blazers. The smaller of the two with glasses and long dark brown hair and a neatly trimmed goatee turns towards the camera.) <Perris> Welcome back to Black Cat Wrestling coming to you live this evening from the De Land National Guard Armory in beautiful De Land, Florida. On my right is my partner, Adrian Mueller . . . (Perris turns towards the vice president who almost went at it with Hollywood Johnny Rocker last week.) <Perris> Glad you could make it, Adrian. What can you tell us about tonight's card? <Mueller> Always a pleasure to be working with you, B.J. . . . especially with the caliber of talent we have been able to amass here in the BCW. <Perris> You are correct, Adrian . . . three men from different parts of the country have invaded Florida and more importantly, Black Cat Wrestling . . . <Mueller> Three men . . . all looking to make an impact here in central Florida. Two of these men are polished wrestlers who have been around the block so to speak . . . In Brian Hines, we have an Indy Wrestling Legend . . . a man who has won 17 titles in his career. That in itself is a feat! Coming to us from Seattle, Washington is the very talented Genocide . . . a former World, U.S. and Tag Team Champion. Size, speed and a background in striking and submission fighting. <Perris> And let's not forget the young Chris Ultra . . . <Mueller> Chris Ultra is a guy that I think the fans are really going to love. This high flying extremist along with Ryan Miller and Andrew Gannon is going to make noise in the BCW. <Perris> Alright Adrian . . . let's go up to the ring and the very lovely Vanessa Benitez for our first match of the evening . . . (the camera moves from the ringside table and focusses in on a lovely hispanic woman in the center of the ring. With long black hair flowing down her back, the beautiful woman is wearing a long black dress with matching high heels.) <Benitez> Ladies and gentleman . . . our first bout of the evening . . . set for one fall with a 10 minute time limit . . . introducing first . . . from Orlando, Florida . . . at 6'2" and weighing in at an even 230 pounds . . . Nate Hopkins . . . (Hopkins entered the arena from the back and got a less than warm welcome . . . no boos but not much cheering either . . . the exceptionally ordinary looking wrestler in his red trunks with black boots makes his way to the ring slapping a few hands. Entering the ring, he pumps his fist into the air while up on the middle ropes and recieves a less than enthusiastic cheer. <Benitez> and his opponent . . . making his Black Cat Wrestling debut . . . standing 6'1" . . . weighing 236 pounds . . . Chris Ultra . . . (Long seconds passed as no one emerged from the back. As the seconds ticked by and became minutes, the restless fans began to let everyone know of their displeasure as the boobirds echoed throughout the nearly empty venue. Referee Joey Dayton came over to where B.J. Perris and Adrian Mueller were seated and a discussion ensued between the three men. After a couple of minutes, BCW President B.J. Perris walked into the ring, taking the mic from Vanessa Benitez.) <Perris> Ladies and gentleman . . . it would appear that Chris Ultra is not in the building thus making him unable to fight. With no other recourse, this match has been declared a No Contest . . . I am sorry . . . (The fans were fairly upset with the decision preferring to see the two men go at it in the ring and rained down boos upon those in the ring. From the back came Mondo Creed . . . a very solid 252 pounds . . . with muscles showing from underneath his white tanktop. His sweats and boots matching the black bandana on his head. The no nonsense guy was coming to the ring clutching a kendo stick. Creed walked into the ring and asked for the mic from Perris.) <Mondo Creed> Hey . . . these people deserve to see a match. I see two wrestlers in the ring . . . both ready to fight . . . ring the damn bell and give the people what they want to see . . . (The fans went crazy ready to see the two Florida residents kick off the action. Perris shrugged his shoulders and nodded at Joey Dayton to start the match. As they both turned towards Nate Hopkins, Creed launched himself into action nailing his opponent across the head with the kendo stick. It cracked loudly as Hopkins crumpled to the canvas. Perris and Vanessa Benitez hurredily left the ring as the bell sounded.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** Hopkins was still down from the shot with Creed's kendo stick as referee Joey Dayton intervened. To his credit, he was in Creed's face forcing the big man back away from the downed wrestler. Creed pushed past Dayton and started to beat Hopkins across the back with the kendo stick. Once . . . twice . . . three times it whacked loudly across Hopkins' back. Mondo Creed dropped his weapon as he pulled Hopkins to his feet. Standing, it was obvious that Nate Hopkins had been busted open . . . badly as blood was now flowing down his face. Creed locked a Side Headlock on Hopkins before hooking a leg with one of his own and throwing his body backwards with a Russian Legsweep. Creed was up quickly and bouncing off of the nearby ropes and dropping a Corkscrew Elbow to Hopkins' head. A Knee Lift to the face sent Hopkins back to the canvas. The Tampa native then began stomping on the downed man. Creed then dropped to the mat alongside Hopkins and used a blatant Choke which had the bleeding man in pain and fighting as best he could. Dayton was calling for a break and when Creed ignored him, the referee grabbed Creed's arm and pulled him off. Of course, this angered Creed and he was all too happy to let Dayton have a piece of his mind but the ref did not back down. While Creed and Dayton were verbally going at it, Nate Hopkins was getting to his feet . . . his face a mask of blood red. Hopkins then struck from behind with a Double Axe Handle across the back of Mondo Creed. Hopkins then reached between Creed's legs and pulled him to the mat with an Inside Cradle. Dayton dropped to the mat with Creed's shoulders down and slapped the canvas for a quick one count before a kickout. Creed was back to his feet grabbing an arm and lifting it over the head of Hopkins. With an Arm Wringer, Creed again was punishing Hopkins. With a twist, Creed worked his opponent to his knees with an Arm Bar. Behind Hopkins, Creed grabbed the other arm and had the man in a Standing Surfboard. Creed was yelling at Dayton for a submission but Hopkins was shaking his head no. This caused Creed to drop a knee into Hopkins' back and pulled harder on the arms. Tiring of this, Creed pulled Hopkins to his feet and Kicked him in the back. A Hip Toss sent him to the mat as Creed followed with an Elbow Drop. Grabbing Hopkins by the hair, Creed tossed him through the ropes and out onto the floor. Following closely behind, Creed picked up his kendo stick and smacked Hopkins across the back . . . a new large red welt was already rising on his back. As Hopkins turned to face Creed, he recieved another shot across the skull with the kendo stick. The blood was now freely flowing from a gash on his forehead. Creed grabbed a handful of hair and smashed Hopkins' head into the table . . . blood going everywhere. Creed pushed Hopkins up onto the table as Perris and Mueller vacated their seats. The kendo stick raised and fell across Hopkins' stomach several times as Creed continued his relentless assault. Creed dropped his kendo stick and pulled a dazed Hopkins to his feet where he picked him up by the legs and dropped him through the table with a Double Leg Slam. Joey Dayton had seen enough and was again in Creed's face. Creed ignored the referee and went back to work. Grabbing a leg, Creed dragged Hopkins completely around the ring leaving a bloody trail getting a mixed reaction from the fans . . . the hardcore people loved the action while the wrestling traditionalists wanted an end to this fight. Once Creed went entirely around the ring, he rolled Hopkins back inside. A beaten and very bloody Nate Hopkins fought back with a couple of Rights to Creed's face and a Standing Drop Kick sent Creed down. Hopkins went to the ropes and jumped off of the bottom one and landed a Knee Drop to Creed's gut. Standing quickly, Hopkins landed a Leg Drop across the chest of his opponent. Hopkins grabbed up the kendo stick and straddled Creed's chest before dropping the weapon across his neck and strangling him with it. Dayton was over warning Nate Hopkins as Creed struggled to break the grip. Finally, Hopkins broke the hold and stepped off of Creed. Joey Dayton followed Hopkins wagging his finger in the wrestler's face for not breaking the choke sooner. Mondo Creed was up and pushed Dayton out of the way as he smashed Hopkins with a Roundhouse Kick. The blow sent Hopkins back into the ropes and bouncing off . . . Creed dropped him with a Drop Toe Hold. Working his way up the downed Hopkin's body, Creed grabbed an arm and rolled him to his back. With a Scissorlock around the arm, Creed dropped to the mat and started pulling. The Crucifix Armbar was just too much for Nate Hopkins who quickly tapped out . . . the first match of the evening was over. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Creed let go of the arm as Vanessa Beintez entered the ring.) <Vanessa Benitez> The winner of the first match of the evening by submission . . . at 4:48 . . . Mondo Creed . . . (Creed grabbed up his kendo stick and lifted both arms into the air as he recieved a mixed reaction from the crowd. Leaving the ring, Creed shook a fist at several fans booing and flipping him off but the BCW security intervened before it went any further. Back in the ring, Nate Hopkins was helped to his feet by referee Joey Dayton and made his way to the back with a small cheer following. The camera swung through the small audience as it made it's way back ringside where B.J. Perris and Adrian Mueller were standing near the broken remains of their table.) <Perris> Our first match of the evening, though not what we expected to see with a No-Show by Chris Ultra, still turned out to be very entertaining . . . <Mueller> Yes it was B.J. . . . Mondo Creed and Nate Hopkins put out a tremendous effort in this one . . . and Creed with a little help from his "partner" walked all over Nate Hopkins . . . <Perris> A little help? He used it fromthe beginning to end of the match . . . <Mueller> True . . . but here in the BCW . . . just about anything is legal . . . <Perris> Too true, Mr. Mueller . . . too true . . . and we'll be right back before we get on to our second match of the evening . . . (the camera fades out as it goes to a commercial.) x-x-x-x-x (The camera fades in on a white wall bearing a BCW banner . . . a black banner with a white circle sitting in the center. The white letters forming the words Black Cat Wrestling running around the outside of the circle in the center of which sits the silhouette of a black cat with red eyes. White tassles hanging from the bottom of the banner. As the camera pulls back, a large breasted slender woman with jet black hair walks in front of the banner. Wearing a black leather bustier and skirt . . . her legs covered in fishnet stockings and ending in black combat boots . . . the pretty young woman is followed by a rather large man wearing a black singlet and matching boots. Around his neck is a collar attached to a heavy chain being held by the young woman. The man, wringing his gloved hands, easily over 300 pounds towers over the woman.) <Mean Darlene> I am Mean Darlene . . . join me and the Mangler each week right here in Florida's best Independent Wrestling Promotion . . . Black Cat Wrestling . . . coming to you live each Sunday from the De Land National Guard Armory and arenas in the central Florida area . . . See the best on the Independent Wrestling Circuit . . . "Regul8er" Brian Hines . . . "Hollywood" Johnny Rocker . . . Genocide . . . Young Justice - Andrew Gannon and Ryan Miller . . . the Southern Gentleman, Ron Niles . . . and the 6'5", 315 pound machine known as the Mangler! Black Cat Wrestling . . . Wrestling with Attitude! (the young woman turns and walks off with the Mangler in tow. As they leave, the camera fades out on the BCW banner.) x-x-x-x-x (with a smooth transition, the camera fades in on Perris and Mueller sitting ringside.) <Perris> We are back . . . and at a new table. We are getting ready for our second match which looks to be a great one . . . <Mueller> Brian Hines brings to the ring a "Devil-May-Care" attitude which has helped him to turn that into 17 different titles around the country. <Perris> An attitude and one Hell of a wrestling talent . . . a man who specializes in Hardcore action . . . according to him . . . <Mueller> That's right B.J., according to him . . . he will fight anyone . . . anywhere . . . in any type of match . . . and he has come to Florida and Black Cat Wrestling in search of another title to add to his already impressive collection of belts. <Perris> And tonight is his first step in that direction . . . <Mueller> Tonight . . . the BCW gets it's first look at Hines but I would like to add that he is jumping into a snakepit when he signed on to fight Young Justice Member, Ryan Miller . . . <Perris> They are both about the same size . . . with a slight edge in weight for Hines . . . as well as age . . . <Mueller> Throw experience into the equation and it looks like Hines has this one sewn up before we start . . . but of course, that is all on paper . . . Ryan Miller is one talented individual . . . he has size, strength and speed . . . all the makings of a fine professional wrestler . . . the only thing he is lacking is true ring experience . . . All of the hours in the gym with the weights and sparring with Andrew Gannon will only get you so far . . . the true test comes when you are standing across from a guy who wants to hand you your head. I think we will all see what Ryan Miller is made of as Brian Hines is not one to stay back and let anyone have time to think . . . <Perris> And your thoughts on the outcome of this one? <Mueller> I will take the experience over the raw talent but I think this one will be a good one! <Perris> Alright then . . . let's go to the ring and Vanessa Benitez for the start of our next match . . . (The camera swings through the crowd holding up a few homemade signs each bearing some witty saying or mentioning the name of their favorite BCW wrestler. As the camera swings past, the fans cheer as they try their best to be noticed. Continuing on it's journey, the camera finally moves into the ring where it finds the lovely Vanessa Benitez talking with referee Joey Dayton. Seeing the camera enter the ring, she moves to the center ready to call the next two wrestlers for their match.) <Vanessa Benitez> This match . . . set for one fall with a 15 minute time limit . . . marks the BCW debut of both men . . . Making his way to the ring first . . . accompanied by the other half of Young Justice, Andrew Gannon . . . from Tampa, Florida . . . weighing 230 pounds . . . standing 6'2" . . . Ryan Miller . . . (As both members of Young Justice stepped out of the back, there was a loud cheer that filled the arena . . . everyone present made it known, that here was their favorite wrestlers . . . Miller and Gannon made their way to the ring slapping hands and talking with fans. They finally entered the ring and climbed the ropes, standing on opposite turnbuckles as the audience continued cheering. Vanessa Benitez waited until the cheering subsided before announcing Miller's opponent.) <Vanessa Benitez> From Topeka, Kansas . . . at 6'2" . . . weighing 235 pounds . . . the "Regul8er" . . . Brian Hines . . . ("Bulls On Parade" by Rage Against the Machine blared over the PA system as a rugged looking man with a neatly cropped blone beard stepped out. Raising his arm into the air, clad in a B>O>P t-shirt and cargo shorts with black boots and a blue bandana on his head, Brian Hines was met with a mixed reception as he made his way to the ring. Here was a man who looked serious and was focussed on the matters at hand . . . namely, introducing one Ryan Miller to the sport of professional wrestling . . . "Brian Hines Style". Entering the ring, Hines moved towards both Miller and Gannon but was intercepted by referee Joey Dayton. Hines was pointing at Gannon who reluctantly left the ring under the direction of Dayton. With Gannon out of the ring and pacing the floor, Dayton returned to the corner of Hines who seemed to be upset by Gannon's presence. Dayton did his best to reassure Hines before moving back to the center of the ring. With a nod towards each wrestler, Dayton called for the bell.) *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** The bell sent both men, the more experienced Hines and the still learning the ropes but learning them well youth known as Ryan Miller, into each other. Miller took a pass, so to speak, glancing a swift kick off the shin of the Regul8er, which had about the effect of a pebble being tossed into the Atlantic. Miller, undaunted, rushed into the ropes, coming off with a forearm smash, which sent Hines back a few steps. Hines reacted by reaching forward and locking onto Miller, delivering a belly to belly suplex. Coming back to his feet, Hines' eyes locked on Miller's Young Justice partner, Andrew Gannon, who stood at ringside watching the match intently. As Hines bickered a bit, Miller rolled to his feet and came off the ropes fast, delivering a short drop kick to Hines' backside, sending the Regul8er out of the ring. Hines fell hard into Andrew Gannon, who merely sidestepped, allowing Hines to slide down his body and to the floor. Miller came soaring behind seconds later, his head blasting into the prone, and floored, Hines. As Miller rolled to his feet, Gannon moved an open chair into position next to the fallen opposition, which Miller launched onto, leaped off of, and landed a high leg drop from, driving air from Hines' chest. As Gannon stood, taunting down but not intervening, Ryan Miller rolled under the ropes and raised his arms, walking around so as to see the entire crowd. On the floor, Hines was regaining his footing, and slowly but without hesitation pulling himself back onto the apron and rolling into the ring. Miller turned back to face Hines just as Brian was back on his feet, charging at the youth. The results were a double leg takedown followed by a quick, violent flurry of ten punches to the face. Hines then launched himself into the ropes, coming off with a rope momentum aided elbow, then following that with a standing knee drop. Hines then stood over his opponent, huffing a bit from the explosion of action. Seconds later Hines was pulling Miller to his feet, executing a quick snap suplex, then pounding a series of less than scientific boots to the back of the fallen member of Young Justice's skull. Hines then gave a growl to the crowd, and reached down to pull Miller to his feet. At the same time, as if by chance or perhaps brilliant coincidence, Andrew Gannon strolled to a position outside the ring before Hines, inciting another outbreak of insults from the angry "Bull On Parade". As Gannon looked up, expressionless and non-responsive, Miller rolled to the side and pulled himself up with some help from the ropes. The referee looked on at Hines and Gannon in confusion, wondering why Brian Hines would risk a match to mock a man at ringside. Five minutes had come and gone just like that as the two men continued to eyeball each other. On his feet again, Miller rushed at Hines, who had just turned back but had time to put his arms out, flipping Miller into his partner at ringside. Hines glared out at the duo, as Gannon helped his partner to his feet, patting him on the back and whispering small nuggets of strategy into the young man's ear. Miller was in the ring again driving forward with a punch which missed the mark by a matter of inches. He follow up, a quick gut kick, didn't miss, but rather sent Hines reeling into the ropes. Miller leaped, literally, into his opportunity, delivering a lariat, then pulling Hines back for a DDT in the center of the ring. Rolling onto the stunned BOPer, Miller hooked a leg, drawing a quick two count before Hines rolled the pin over, taking a two himself. The two men both came to their feet at the same time, Hines rushing into Miller with a shoulder block. Hines then rushed into Miller as he started to stand, nailing a bulldog, driving the young man's head to the mat. Hines then pulled Miller off the mat, lifting him for a fall away slam. Miller, however, used the move to his own advantage, swung with the momentum into a crucifix, getting a two and a half count before the raging Regul8er kicked free. Miller raised his hands, thinking he'd won the match. Hines was up behind Miller, who was being informed by the referee that there was no actual pin. Hines drilled a standing kick to Miller's back, then spun him around for the Bulldozer. Hooking a leg on the shocked youth, Hines got the three count and his first victory in the BCW. *** Ding *** Ding *** Ding *** (Hines quickly vacated the ring as a hail of debris showered down on the ring from a very upset crowd of people. Following the garbage, a chant of "Bull . . . Shit" echoed throughout the De Land National Guard Armory. Hines was surrounded by the BCW security as he made his way to the back. Several boisterious and demonstrative people were pushed back by the security forces. Meanwhile back in the ring, both Ryan Miller and Andrew Gannon were arguing with referee Joey Dayton over the ending of the match. Both men were complaining about Dayton's count but to no avail . . . this match was over . . . They both left the ring to a big cheer as they headed to the back of the arena. A second barrage of garbage found it's way into the ring and directed at referee Joey Dayton. The camera made a sweep of the litter strewn ring before heading back to the announcer's table with B.J. Perris and Adrian Mueller . . . and a third person, the manager of the Mangler, Mean Darlene . . .) <Perris> Wow! <Mueller> Wow is right . . . a very fast and furious match . . . <Mean Darlene> And total Bull Shit! <Perris> We have been joined by Mean Darlene, manager of the Mangler . . . <Mean Darlene> They know who I am . . . and that match was total Bull Shit! As much as I dislike those two "Pretty Boy Pansies" . . . I like Brian Hines even less . . . there is no way that loser is going to come in here and take home any titles . . . not today . . . not tomorrow . . . And as for knowing anything about hardcore fighting . . . (Darlene laughs before continuing.) Baby . . . you don't know nothin' . . . (The fans start cheering at Darlene's comments . . . right now, they would cheer Hitler if he had something nasty to say about the "Regul8er" Brian Hines.) <Perris> Ok . . . it seems that there needs to be a bit of cleaning up in the ring before we go on to our next bout . . . Adrian, I understand we have a clip to show? <Mueller> That is right, B.J. . . . we have a man who has come to Florida all the way from Tokyo, Japan and is looking to make a name for himself in professional wrestling. Dragon Akai is an exciting young wrestler with a ton of talent . . . speed and high flying tactics . . . <Mean Darlene> Is going to get him sent home in a body bag if he ever gets into the ring with the Mangler . . . <Perris> Ok . . . let's roll the tape . . . (The camera fades out on all three and fades in on the taped clip.) x-x-x-x-x (The camera is traveling across a hilly barren field where there is a gray overcast to the sky while the song Tubular Bells plays. A man in the distance starts to come into focus as the camera starts to move faster and stops short. The man can be seen from the lower chest up, his head is bowed. He wears a green satin robe with a black and red ying/yang decorating the left breast. He lifts his head slowly to show his face which is covered by a green mask with black and red highlights. Jet black hair hangs down the sides of the mask sprouting from the open topped mask. As the music stops, the man with an Asian accent starts to speak.) I am Dragon Akai. I have come to the BCW with the intention of making my first mark on the world of professional wrestling. I do not come to make friends or fans but to prove that I am a supreme fighter. My intentions are simple and that is to make the Akai name ring through the ages. I bring with me the spirits of my ancestors some of the greatest warriors of many centuries. I now extend the challenge to any man in BCW who is willing to engage in the art of battle. I forewarn you that the Dragon's fire is lethal be sure of your decision before you except, prepare well after you have. The Dragon excepts only the truly best as a competitor. (He places his right fist in his left upright open palm and bows his head back down the camera speeds backwards as we see the man disappear into the distance.) x-x-x-x-x
