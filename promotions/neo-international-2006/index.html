A multi-federation tournament hub whose sixteen-man World Championship tournament drew wrestlers from at least five other named federations to a two-night supercard. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NEO International Wrestling opened its own record with a newsletter, posted 4 February 2006, for a sixteen-man World Championship Tournament billed as bringing e-wrestling's scattered email, board and angle communities together in one event; a mailing list and a dedicated website carried updates, submissions and brackets for the two-night card at the Knoxville Auditorium.

Sixteen wrestlers, drawn from at least five other named promotions plus several outsiders invited individually, were seeded into the bracket for night one — among the announced pairings, junior heavyweight Griffin James against the larger Justin Harper, and Independent Super Lightweight Champion Usagi Yoshida against the freelance Alex Extreme.

A second document, posted 13 April 2006, announced the tournament's own pay-per-view, “Dawn of a New Age,” as complete across three released nights, capped, in its own words, by “a spectacular World Title Main Event.”

No result for that main event, or any further document, survives; how the tournament was ultimately decided isn't in the surviving record.
