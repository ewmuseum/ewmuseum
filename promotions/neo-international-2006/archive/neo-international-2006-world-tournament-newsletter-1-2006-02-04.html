NEO I World Tournament Newsletter #1

4 February 2006

NEO INTERNATIONAL WRESTLING PRESENTS... The NEO I World Championship Tournament! NEWSLETTER #1 1. The Board of Directors speaks! 2. Mailing List 3. Deadlines and Judging 4. Lineup 1. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS SPEAKS! =*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*= Thank you everyone for your interest. This tournament has generated a lot of buzz across the entire spectrum of e-wrestling as you will soon be able to see by the breadth of it's competitors. Hobbyists from the email, board and angle worlds have all talked about it. We at NEO International Wrestling are so proud that we can bring the e-wrestling world together like this considering just how scattered and sheltered it has been in the past. We do truly hope and believe that this can be the most watched (read) show in many years. The buzz is great, we hope to do everyone proud. In this first, albeit short, newsletter we will have deadlines, the mailing list ALL will need to join and of course, the lineup. As more newsletters come out, more news will. Showcase matches for the two day event, odds for the tournament, news and rumours, a lot of fun I think. And of course, stay tuned to the NEO International web site, which will provide all breaking news and features. Thank you once again for your interest and congratulations to all involved and chosen for this tournament! 2. THE MAILING LIST =*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*= **VERY IMPORTANT!!!!** http://groups.yahoo.com/group/neointernational/ That is the mailing list you will _all need_ to join. We will not be sending the show and later newsletters out through the federations individual mailing lists as they share so many readers. This would simply be chaos. It is a quick and easy matter of joining, you all know how. I CANNOT stress how IMPORTANT this is to join. http://www.geocities.com/neointernationalwrestling This is _THE_ web site for the tournament. News and updates will come through here. Expect a subsection for the tournament itself to be put up in the coming days with info on the competitors, bios, brackets and all news. RP, Strat, Oncard email address: [detail withheld] _ALL_ NEO I World Championship tournament material and emails go there! **VERY IMPORTANT!!!!** 3. DEADLINES and JUDGING =*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*= Again... VERY IMPORTANT! Please read this thoroughly! The tournament will consist of two roleplay packets. These rps may be in any format you wish: Traditional promo, a setting style rp, press conference, anything your mind can conjure and none shall be discriminated against. However, no ON LOCATION rps please as these will not be right before your match. You COULD do them on location, but remember it'd be weeks before the show itself. Let's see your very best. Everyone here roleplays in a different manner. Show that difference, really stand out. ROLEPLAY PACKET: DEADLINES are February 18th and February 25th. Two roleplay packets and we certainly hope you do something for both packets. We expect the first to be a general introduction packet, the next to be reactionary... though we leave that up to your discretion. That is just how we, the BoD, see the packets progressing. STRATS: These are due February 20th. We want these earlier so we can get on writing matches earlier. ONCARDS: These are due February 25th as well. A LITTLE bit more leeway might be given on these, but very little at that. These _must_ be on location at the event or will not be used. EMAIL: [detail withheld] 4. LINEUP =*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*=*= NIGHT ONE! It is finally upon us, the biggest tournament in 2006 is about to hit the airwaves as NEO International Wrestling holds a tournament to crown it's first ever World Champion! Sixteen of the organization's best as well as several hand picked 'outsiders' will come together in Tennessee in the spirit of competition and greatness. The Knoxville Auditorium shall be absolutely rocking with an after noon meet and greet. That will be NOTHING compared to what they have in store that night... The NEO International World Championship Tournament! GRIFFIN JAMES (Dream Fujin) is arguably the most popular wrestler in Japan at this very moment and possessed of spectacular skill and charisma. A junior heavyweight by trade he will be outsized by JUSTIN HARPER (NEXT) but don't think this will stop him! Styles clash or not, expect this one to open up the tournament with a bang! Neither ALEX EXTREME (free agent) and USAGI YOSHIDA (Dream Fujin) are no strangers to gold. Yoshida is the current Independent Super Lightweight Champion. Extreme has held the RCW title and excelled in several major wrestling companies. This could be Yoshida's toughest challenge to date... or it could be the re-emergence of one of the top wrestlers of the past half decade! Most will immediately write off DEMONIO DE LA SANGRE II (NeWA), unfamiliar with his abilities. JOHNNY F'N MALIBU (NEXT) will certainly be one of those people but he,. along with everyone, would be smart to not judge a book by it's cover. Sangre is a top tier talent, waiting to break out on a world wide level and could very well do it on this, the biggest stage in the world of wrestling. This has all the potential for show stealer. Bet on it! SHOJI TAMURA (Dream Fujin) had to beat almost twenty other men to earn his spot in this tournament and will not give it up easily, even to the veteran SKYE ASHNER (DCWL)! This is another exciting, cruiserweight oriented bout sure to bring the fans to their feet! Both have the break out material... but who will put it to best use? There is hard hitting and then there is the fifth match in the NEO title tournament. ERIC JAMES (FSW) is big, ornery, tough and mean but none of that will scare _the_ breakout star of late 2005 and of 2006 in ERIC DANE (NEXT). Dane has stood toe to toe with some of the toughest. He is no stranger to championships and no stranger to competition. This competition will be fierce! LARK FENRIZ (free agent/PWW) has always been a journeyman, however successful. He has had stints in nearly every wrestling company at one time or another. He will need all that experience and his bloodthirsty means against BILLY IAKIKA (FSW), perhaps the biggest man in the tournament! Don't be surprised if this one makes it's way a little closer to you then you'd like! And of course there are the characters, this match the boldest indication of it. PARIS LECLAIR (FSW) loves nothing more then his home country of Canada. However, an evil, heartless cat? DARKNESS KITTY! (Dream Fujin) might just be more then he can take. Or... vice versa! Both men are on the cusp of stardom. All they need is that one performance to get them there! And the money match. Either of these men could headline anywhere else in the world and it's only fitting they end the first round of the tournament. MESSIAH (NEXT) has held NEO gold before, when the company was in it's singular infancy. BILLY SWEETS (FSW) has tasted gold in several smaller organizations but is touted as the world's best wrestler no one knows. This one could very well leave the biggest mark in the first round with wide implications in the wrestling world and in this tournament as it progresses! NIGHT TWO! The QUARTERFINALS of the tournament shall be held! The SEMI FINALS! THE FINALS! Plus... --Expect showcase matches from wrestling companies around the world! --Could there be a Night 3, putting this tournament and supercard beyond epic?! --Wallace Storm shall speak! --And... of course... the best damn wrestling in the world! NEO INTERNATIONAL WRESTLING 2006
