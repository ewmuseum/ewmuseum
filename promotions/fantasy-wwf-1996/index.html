Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Fantasy WWF surfaces on 14 February 1996 with an address from its own newly installed president, Darrell Sykes, “to let you know what is going on in the federation.” He announced that every title had been stripped and would be settled in four parallel tournaments — World, Intercontinental, Tag Team, and a brand-new Television title, “the first ever TV champ crowned” — on a rebuilt In Your House card, and promised to drop “almost all the cartoon gimmicks that Mr. McMahon has given the wrestlers” along with “the kiddie stuff,” declaring the promotion out to prove itself “the number one wrestling organization in the world.” Its schedule ran four shows a week, each with new matches: Raw on Mondays, Superstars on Fridays, Mania on Saturdays, and Action Zone on Sundays.

The president's own address laid out all sixteen first-round pairings across the four brackets in full — Mark Calloway against Sean Waltman and Bret Hart against Vader opening the World tournament, Diesel against Davey Boy Smith opening the Intercontinental, the Godwins against the Spiders opening the Tag Team bracket, and Duke Droese against Unibomb opening the Television.

Results followed on 20 April across three of its own broadcasts. On Mania, Diesel beat Jake Roberts with a Jackknife and Hunter Hearst-Helmsley beat Bart Gunn with the Pedigree. On Superstars, Mark Calloway pinned a bloodied British Bulldog with a Tombstone Piledriver and Yokozuna pinned Jeff Jarrett with a legdrop after Lawler and Landell's interference backfired. On Action Zone and elsewhere the same day, Bret Hart beat Al Snow off a roll-up and Ahmed Johnson won by disqualification after Tom Prichard struck the referee.

Its last surviving document, dated the same 20 April, is a further sixteen-match card spanning all four shows in results shorthand, with no confirmation the World Title tournament ever reached a final. Whether the promotion continued past that date, or how it ended, is not in the surviving record.

This federation’s fiction portrays the WWF.
