Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A wrestler in this CWF posted a short plug on 30 January 2001 calling it simply “a good fed... worth joining, you owe it to yourself,” and pointing to the promotion’s own site hosted on Fortune City.

Two months later the same site drew its own community’s attention, when a member posted an open invitation for “old-timers of e-wrestling” to critique the promotion’s own talent and cards; the review that came back a day later named its roster in its own words — “Next Generation” Bryan Blayze, Tommy D, Rage, Nick Cage, and Grant Ralton, whom the reviewer called “a blatant HHH clone.”

The review’s real interest was the promotion’s own championship history: it named two wrestlers, Jim Mason of a group it called the Elite, and a character billed as Violent J, as both former and current world champions despite what the reviewer rated as poor roleplaying, while praising a group it called the NEF as the promotion’s own most consistently strong writers. Its author, writing while “on a sabbatical” from the promotion, placed himself in the same roster, handling a character named Andrew Saunders and describing him as one “with little to no direction.”

No founding date or closing notice survives; the promotion’s own championship lineages, title histories, and the outcome of the roster this review describes are not otherwise recorded.
