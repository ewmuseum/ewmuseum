Interviews, 20-31 December 1996

1 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI newcomers The American Rockers (Scott Windhiam & Chris Gunn). Taped Friday 12/20/96: [We fade in on Scott Windhiam and Chris Gunn standing in front of the FWLI logo. They are in their wrestling gear.] SCOTT: The American Rockers have arrived here in the FWLI. And we are here for one reason. We want to test our skills aganist the top competition this fed has to offer. The FWLI has some of the top tag teams in the world today. Damage Inc., The Universial Powers, our good buddies Sudden Impact... the list goes on. Well, Chris and myself have signed an open contract. That means that we'll take on anyone... anyplace... anytime. You want a shot at us? Sign on the dotted line... and LET'S GET IN ON! CHRIS: That's right. Hey, we may be newcomers, but we ain't rookies. We've wrestled all around the world and taken on some darn good tag teams. We know how to hurt you. We know how to make you suffer. WE KNOW WHAT IT TAKES TO GET THE JOB DONE! And when you step in the ring with the American Rockers... YOU'LL FIND OUT EXACTLY WHAT I'M TAKING ABOUT! SCOTT: Tag Teams of the FWLI get ready. Cause the American Rockers are coming to rock and roll all night long and do a little dance ALL OVER YOUR FACE! [Scott and Chris raise their fists like the Rockers used to at the end of their interviews.] SCOTT AND CHRIS: LET'S ROCK! [They give intense looks at the camera, and we fade out.] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with The Devious Dangers (FWLI World Six-Man Tag-Team Champions Greg "The Barber" Roberts & "Modern-Day Romeo" Ryan Rankin & The Storm, & "Fabulous" Freddy Blair), along with manager "Devious" Dan Davis. Taped Monday 12/23/96: [The setting is the den in the home of "Fabulous" Freddy Blair in Charlotte, North Carolina. Fab Fred and a *very* attractive short-haired brunette are sitting on a leather sofa, watching the Steelers/Panthers football game on TV. Fab Fred is wearing a smoking jacket and a red Santa Claus hat. They are both drinking eggnog.] BRUNETTE: It was nice of you to let my sisters use your luxury box so they could see the Panthers game in person, Freddy. FAB FRED: No problem. [the doorbell rings] That's probably the guys. Could you get that for me? BRUNETTE: Sure. More eggnog? FAB FRED: Please. [The brunette leaves the room. Fab Fred focuses on the game. The Panthers score a touchdown.] FAB FRED : Whoo-hoo!! [makes the "touchdown" signal] Hey, Steelers! Season's BEATINGS!! Hahahahaha!!! [The brunette returns, along with the rest of the Devious Dangers: Greg "The Barber" Roberts, The Storm, and "Modern Day Romeo" Ryan Rankin.] FAB FRED: Glad you could make it, guys. Where's the Devious One? [The doorbell rings again, and the brunette leaves to go answer it.] RANKIN: He left a little early... said he had to pick up some holiday surpri... [Rankin stops in mid-word as the brunette returns with Devious Dan and three other women - a blonde, a redhead, and another brunette, who was the fan singled out in the Dangers/Dawgs match.] RANKIN: ... surprises. Oh, boss-man, you done good! Jenni, how are you?? [Rankin goes to embrace her as the other two women join Barber and Storm, and Davis stands alone] Hey, Danny... what about you? DEVIOUS DAN: Don't worry about me, Ry-guy. You guys have had a busy last couple months, so you need to enjoy this time. It's enough for me to see you all relaxed and having fun. That reminds me- I have to run back out to the car and get the presents I got YOU guys! [Devious Dan heads back out the door] RANKIN: [standing arm-in-arm with Jenni, calling out to Devious Dan] Thanks, Dan. You're the greatest. [he addresses everyone else] Guys, that's the holiday spirit right there, thinking of others first. And I think we can follow up on that... we should think of our fellow wrestlers. BARBER: Aw, man, this is our time off, remember? STORM: Besides, we've thought of our opponents enough lately. RANKIN: Now you know that's not what I meant. What I'm saying is, we should pass along our own holiday wishes to some of the other wrestlers. FAB FRED: Hey, I think I get it. Holiday wishes, Danger-style. Storm, you got one in mind? [Storm takes his eyes off the redhead for a moment and thinks.] STORM: Well, the first thing that comes to mind [Storm nudges Rankin] Sir Xavior Morgan. My wish is for him to some how fix that hair do he has. Oh wait, I think Greg took care of that. [Everyone laughs] One other wish that I have is that no one beats the Universal Powers so that MDR and myself can have the opportunity to take them and add it to our collection of tag team belts. [Devious Dan comes back into the house with an armful of gifts.] BARBER: Hey, let me help you with those! [Roberts goes over and takes a couple of the gifts out of Devious Dan's arms and sets them down on the floor] We'll just set them here for now. The guys and I are just starting our holiday wishes for the other competitors. DEVIOUS DAN: [He is setting down the remaining gifts] Did I miss much? STORM: Nah, I just wished Morgan would get a new hair style, but that was already taken care of. [Storm chuckles once again] Hey Ryan, you got any wishes for the FWLI competitors? [Storm sits down on a chair and the redhead sits on his lap.] RANKIN: Well, Storm, you already took two of mine. So I guess I'll wish that General Kane can just get his men thinking about Hardcore... thinking about what may be their last shot at our belts. Twice now, they couldn't stop us, and when they realized that, they got themselves DQ'ed. Well, we've already been in the giving mood by letting them have the shot at Hardcore. Sure, they drew up the contract, but because they've tried and failed twice, we could've blown them off. I sure hope they'll be appreciative of what we give 'em at Hardcore. DEVIOUS DAN: Very touching, Ryan. [Everyone chuckles] And speaking of giving... [Devious Dan grabs a gift and hands it to the Barber] this is for you, Greg. FAB FRED: [feigning the excitement of a small child] Open it! Open it! BARBER: [as he's opening the present] I wonder what... gee, an ordinary box. Thanks. You shouldn't have. DEVIOUS DAN: [smiling] Oh, just open the damn thing. [Barber obliges, and his eyes light up] There you are, Greg. A contract to face the Bandwidth Mountie. BARBER: Cool, just what I always wanted! FAB FRED: I hope there's one of those in my stocking... BARBER: This is great! Devious, you have a knack for giving great gifts. Why, two years ago, you gave MDR and me a shot at the World Six-Man Belts. RANKIN: Definitely the best present we ever got. [Jenni elbows him in the ribs] Ow! I mean, the SECOND-best present we ever got. Heh heh. BARBER: Nice save, Ryan. Anyway, it was history from there. To pardon the cliche, Mountie, you too will be history soon enough. I mean, let's assume you make it through the Crossroads match. What's at the other end of the tunnel? WE ARE! So I guess we'll be getting you a present, too, Mountie - peace on earth. After you step into the ring with the Dangers again, you won't have to worry about wrestling anymore. Anybody need more eggnog? DEVIOUS DAN: Just as long as it's not like the kind you made last year, Greg... RANKIN: Oh, the horror... BARBER: Small wonder I never went into cooking. STORM: I think I'll have a little more myself. Thanks again for hosting the party, Fab Fred. FAB FRED: Well, since you guys hand-picked me to be a member of the Dangers, I figured it was the least I could do. DEVIOUS DAN: Didn't I tell you guys he'd make a great addition to the stable? FAB FRED: You know guys, it's been a helluva great time since I joined up with the Dangers. But I'm not the kind of guy to sit back and linger on the past. I'm already thinking about the challenges that 1997 has to offer, and I have two wishes. First, I wish for a tie-breaker match with the Mountie, although by the sounds of it there won't be anything left of him except that stupid Stetson [everyone laughs]. But I wish for it all the same. Mountie, you can run your mouth all you want, you can try to hide, but sometime down the road our paths will cross again, and when they do I'll show you without a shadow of a doubt just who is the better man. BARBER: Spoken like a true Danger. But that's only one wish. STORM: Yeah, what's the second one? FAB FRED: [chugs some eggnog] The second wish? That's easy. Everyone, come over to the fireplace for a minute. [They all walk over to the fireplace. On the mantle are numerous wrestling trophies, and a few championship belts from various other wrestling federations. The centre of the mantle is conspicuously empty.] FAB FRED: As you can see, there is a vacancy right in the middle here. My second wish is to fill that vacancy with a FWLI championship belt. Everyone knows that the FWLI is the best fed around, and to win an FWLI title is like winning the Super Bowl, the Stanley Cup, and the World Series all rolled into one. Now wouldn't that be FABULOUS?! RANKIN: I had a feeling you were going to say that... [everyone laughs] [Everyone heads back to the places that they were sitting and the women go back to their respective men.] FAB FRED: [Looks around at everyone] Um, guys, didn't you forget something? [Storm, Rankin, Dan, and Roberts all look at each other with a puzzled look.] BARBER: Ah, I don't think so. FAB FRED: You sure did. Where's your holiday spirit? [Fab Fred gets up from his spot and heads into a different room. He comes back with a handful of Santa hats] Here you go, guys! [Tosses a hat to each one of the Dangers and to the women present] I picked up extras just in case you guys forgot! STORM: Thanks Fred! [He is putting on his Santa hat on his mask.] Hey Dan, you still haven't told us what your holiday wishes are. DEVIOUS DAN: Well guys [Dan looks at everyone] I wish all of the competitors in the FWLI the best of luck because by the time the Devious Dangers face them in the ring they're going to need it!!! Second, I want each of you to bring home some more gold to the stable. Storm and Rankin, the NA tag belts are yours... you just have to go claim them. Fab Fred, if and when you get that tie breaking match with The Mountie you'll get the win and the title all in one night. Barber, you will finally get your hands on the NA title too! [Everyone nods and smiles in agreement] Now I may not be Santa Claus, but I have a feeling we may get our holiday wishes answered this year. Because we've been VERY, VERY good in 1996. Of course, anyone who wants to find out JUST how good we are can step into the ring and find out! RANKIN: Wow, if all of our wishes come true... 1997 will be the year of the Devious Dangers!!! BARBER: "The year of the Danger"... I like the sound of that! DEVIOUS DAN: If you like the sound of it, just wait till it happens! 1997 will mark the beginning of the Golden Age of FWLI! And we're going to be leading the way! Which reminds me... here's one MORE present. [He tosses each person a t-shirt, reading "FWLI 1997: The Year of the DANGER"] RANKIN: I swear I didn't look in your bag! STORM: Well, I guess it's settled. We're just going to have to take FWLI by STORM! FAB FRED: Ooh... good one, I think. DEVIOUS DAN: [raising his glass] Ladies, gentlemen, let's drink a toast. To all the competitors of the FWLI, may you get to experience first-hand... all the joy and love we've expressed here today. It will be a joy to rule to roost in the coming year, and we're going to love finally getting the recognition and accolades we deserve. ALL: [raising their glasses and drinking] Here here! DEVIOUS DAN: Now who's up for some carols? [He sits down at a grand piano in the corner of the room and begins playing "Oh Tannenbaum," which turns into "Oh MDR". [Fade to black.] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with "Quickstrike" Chris Quigley. [HUGE crowd pop as "For Those About to Rock" starts piping through the arena, and Quigley emerges through the curtain, wearing dark blue jeans with a rip in the knee, a white New Orleans "Hard Rock Cafe" shirt, a black Quickstrike leather jacket, and silver wire rimmed shades. QUIGLEY: That's right. If you remember correctly, West, I requested this match a long time ago, and it's finally gonna happen! I don't need to repeat myself, everyone heard me in my last interview! I'm sick and tired of seeing my name at #27 in the rankings! I think it's pretty obvious that I'm better than each and every one of those so called superstars in front of me. In fact, there are only four men rated above me that I respect at all. They being Dan Kauffman, Chris Thomas, Mark Engel, and maybe most of all, Chris Taylor! But now it comes down to Vic Black and me! The FWLI North American title on the line. Black, you've had a nice little reign as champion, but it's time to wave goodbye to your belt, because you have NEVER faced anyone who can tie you into so many different knots like I can! Trust me, when all is said and done, you WILL feel the effects of the Quickstriker, and I will be the FWLI North American champion! The "Space Age Playboy" as he likes to call himself, is right there challenging me. And yeah, I want to fight Brian Knight. But that can wait. First, as far as the Universal Powers go, if you want to make your precense felt during my match? I'm confident that Mark Engel will be right behind you, making sure you don't wander too close to ringside. And the "Space Age Playboy" challenged me? Screw him! I've got more important things to bother myself with, you little punk. But let me make one thing clear, once I dispose of "The Rebel" at Hardcore, you are NUMBER ONE on my list! I am NOT a #27 ranked wrestler! I am the man who is gonna STRIKE YOU DOWN! Taped Wednesday 12/25/96: [FADE IN to the dining room. Six people are enjoying Christmas dinner, including JULIET VACHON (in a nice dress) and JEAN-PIERRE VACHON (in a regular sweater). CLOSE UP on JPV and JV.] JEAN-PIERRE: Christmas dinner. In households all across this continent, the stars of FWLI are celebrating with their families. "Peace on earth, good will to all men"- JULIET: Milord Jean-Pierre, though we do earnestly with for the first part - in FWLI the latter is most difficult to come by. JEAN-PIERRE: True enough. [puts a forkful of potato in his mouth, chews, swallows] But I think we must wish some of our FWLI competitors well. Take Jean-Luc Nancett, for example. Right now he's celebrating with the Players' Club. I certainly hope the Club is throwing him the best Christmas party he's ever seen - because, come HARDCORE, it will be his last. JULIET: My love, think not that thy victory over the Gambit is a foregone conclusion. 'Tis always a mistake to underestimate thy opponent. [puts a forkful of green beans into her mouth, chews and swallows] JEAN-PIERRE: Quite right, my dear. And it also seems that Haron Atkinson plans to intrude upon this match. A word of advice, Mr. Legend-In-Your-Own-Mind: don't. Come HARDCORE, both Gambit and I will be out to end each other's career - and in the mood we'll be in, it won't matter if we end YOURS, as well. And it seems obvious, from your last interview, that you might make a perfect Devious Danger. You've got that same chicken-counting attitude that Flaboulous Freddy has. Main difference is, you haven't got his talent. Come our upcoming match, I think you'll soon learn your abilities aren't enough to handle Jean-Pierre Vachon. Once I have you screaming in the grip of the Keyboard Lockup - you'll understand - and accept - that of all the people who can call themselves "living legends" in FWLI, YOU are NOT among them. [JPV picks up a bottle of wine and fills JV's glass, then his own.] JULIET: Jean-Pierre - after HARDCORE, there is the Canadian Invitational. Art thou not upset that thou were not among the invited? JEAN-PIERRE: [sips wine] Not at all, Juliet. Yes, it promises to be an exciting event - and I wouldn't be surprised if I showed up in a different match at that event. Maybe the one with Atkinson. Or perhaps Brian Knight would truly drop out and the FWLI would slot me in as a replacement. But I'm not at all hurt that I wouldn't be invited to the Invitational - even though it takes place on my home turf. You see, I've noticed a common thread among the invitees - they've all got something to prove here in FWLI. But with me, the point is already proven. Right now I'm the longest-reigning champion of ANY title in FWLI. I've already fought and defeated some of the best athletes in this federation. This isn't egotism, but pure fact, as reflected in the record. Which brings up one final point I want to make tonight... [JPV pulls out a contract from his dinner jacket.] JEAN-PIERRE: Greg Roberts, have a closer look at your contract. Not only is it a match with me, it's a CHALLENGE match. Two on one, you and any other current member of the Dangers you care to pick - Ryan Rankin, Freddy Blair OR Storm. Win is by pin or submission, no DQ, no countout. Which ever wrestler manages to defeat me wins the Television Championship. I wonder which partner you'll go for? Rankin, who's suffered one of the most humiliating moments in FWLI history because of me? Blair, to get that "tie-breaker" match he's so often demanded? Storm, who's the ONLY Danger I've never faced in the ring? Pick any Danger you want - not that it'll make much of a difference, because I've adjusted my training regimen so that I can take on any or all of 'em. Just remember, Greg Roberts - you're taking on one of the best. So get ready to fall, just like the rest. Now some of you out there might think this is career suicide, taking on two Dangers. But you gotta understand. Just because I've already proven myself doesn't mean I want to rest on my laurels. The Bandwidth Mountie ALWAYS likes a challenge, when the odds seem to be against him. In the upcoming year, I'm planning on defending this belt under some of the toughest conditions ever imposed on a match in FWLI. Because I know, and you know, and the fans know, that victory in the ring should NEVER come cheap. In 1997, the toughest, hardest, meanest, and BEST matches in FWLI are gonna be fought by Jean-Pierre Vachon. That's MY New Year's Resolution. Gambit - Player's Club - Atkinson - Dangers - Christmas 1996, and 1997, it's the period when you're gonna be in for some HARD TIME!
