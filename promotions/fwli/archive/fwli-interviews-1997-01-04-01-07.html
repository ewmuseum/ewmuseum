[FWLI] Interviews 01/04-01/07

13 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI European Heavyweight Champion and Awesome Alliance leader "Iceman" Brian Knight. Taped Saturday 01/04/97: [Greyscale shot... the look is bleak over an ice rink. There is a steady amount of steam coming off the ice. Standing in the center of the rink, wearing the same wrestling tights and boots he wore during the Canadian Invitational stands "Iceman" Brian Knight. Dispite the cold temperature, Knight stands topless, and is apparantly covered in sweat.] Knight: The Tournament is over... and look who won. Imagine what I could have done if I'd thought about trying. Imagine what I could have done if I'd considered this tournament important. Imagine... imagine. [Cut to a side shot of Knight's face.] Knight: I haven't slept one night since that day. Once again, I proved myself to this piece of sh*t federation. Once again, I proved I derserve to be on top of the world. Once again... once again. [Cut to the opposite side of Knight's face.] Knight: This world is a cold, cold place. The colder you are, the more you're able to survive. And I am a survivor. [Cut to a straight shot of Knight. There is a fire in his eyes, his fists are clentched, and he appears to be ready to explode.] Knight: Mark Engel, once again, you and the people in charge of the FWLI have put it upon yourselves to try to make my life miserable. You lost your first round match at the tournament, and to reward you for that, they gave you a World title match. This is was a BIG mistake. My lawyers were ready to leap on this federation, and slap them with so many lawsuits, it would destroy this federation, and shake it to it's very foundation. But I said no. [Cut to a slow pan up Knight's body. The sweat is covering his body now.] Knight: Mark, I'm sending this out to you. I am going to do whatever it takes.. I'm selling my soul to the devil if I have to... Mark, I'm going to make it my life to destroy you and that FWLI World Title you wear. Hardcore is RIGHT AROUND THE CORNER... it's time to see if you're man enough to wear the that title belt. Do you think you can stand face to face with The Awesome Alliance? I don't think you can. You beat Todd Newkirk with no one in his corner. Now, you're going to find out about the destructive force that comes with Brian Knight in an opponents corner. [Cut to a behind the head shot, which pans around to the front.] Knight: I've got other things on my mind... Otto Verhoeven... you've got a great valet. Let me know when you want a manager... Kauffman, you haven't got the talent it takes to stand in the ring with me. You have a fluke victory for the IIWF title, but you're riding on coattails that belong to better men. Dynamite is the best in that unit, and I beat him without training. No slight on 3-D. Just a COLD HARD FACT. Kauffman, you had respect from me. Now, you have my anger... and my sympathy for your lack of forethought. Black... thanks for the congrats... we'll go riding some time, just to make some noise again. [Cut to a far shot, slowly closing in on Knight, now drenched.] Knight: It starts with you, Engel... strangely enough, it ends with you, too. I'm going to destroy you Engel. Quigley won't be able to help you... Melanie won't be able to help you... there isn't a person on God's Green Earth that will be able to help you. You may wear the FWLI World Heavyweight Championship, but your day of "Execution" is coming, and it'll be at the hands of "The Iceman". The "ICE AGE" is here, Mark. It's time you realized that. [Camera continues to move forward towards Knight's face. It then focuses on his eye, and the greyscale picture becomes alive with flame. Taped Sunday 01/05/97: [Camera cuts to an open road, with a small black dot heading towards the camera, raising a cloud of dust behind it. The dot gets closer and closer and the rumblings of a motor are heard. Finally, an image can be made out. It's clearly a motorcycle, testing the limits of it's speedometer. The bike draws closer and closer until it looks like a collision with the camera is inevitable. Then quickly, the brakes are slammed and the bike screeches to a halt, directly in front of the camera. The man behind the bars steps off the bike, pushes down the kickstand, and removes a helmet, as long front bangs fall in his face. He hangs the helmet on a handle bar and hauls off a pair of leather gloves, and shoves them in the pockets of his leather jacket. He brushes his hands through his hair, revealing his identity, and then wipes his hands in his torn jeans. Then Chris Quigley begins to speak.] QUIGLEY: Exactly what is it that makes you a "Rebel", Vic Black? Is it because you can ride your big bad motorcycle? Is it because you can slick your hair back and wear shades and stay out late smoking cigarettes and swearing? I'm just a little curious, because I really haven't seen you do anything to deserve the title "Rebel". Tell me, do you break any laws? Well... I know that the fact you're wearing the FWLI North American Championship breaks all laws of Physics, but BESIDES that. [Quigley spits on the ground and wipes his mouth with his arm.] QUIGLEY: Suppose I decided to call myself a "Rebel". Would that bug you, Black? Would that get on your nerves? What can I say? You want your leather clad, shade wearing, motorcycle riding rebel, you got it right here. But who really gives a damn about all that? Let's put your little wanna be bad a*s attitude on the shelf and talk wrestling for a second or two. Vic Black... the North American Champion. That title makes it sound like you're a good wrestler, Black. A damn good wrestler. But you know how the old saying goes, "You don't judge a book by it's cover." so I can't judge a wrestler by his title. From what I've seen of you and from what I've heard of you, you are NOTHING. You want to wrestle a technical match against me? Mistake #1. You want to wrap me in some kinda submission move? Make me give up? Mistake #2. You want to go outside the ring and try to knock me in the head with a chair? Mistake #3. No matter what you try to do... no matter what style you want to bring with you for our match at Hardcore, you're going to be in for a shock! There isn't a style you can master, a plan you can develop, or a trick you can attempt that'll be good enough to get you past me! I think you'd better take a good long look at yourself! Take a good long look at the title! And then take a good long look at me! Then you might want to think to yourself, "This is going to be the fight of my life! This is quite possibly the end of my title reign! This could be the end of my career!" [Quigley turns around and gives the bike behind him a hard kick, sending it crashing to the ground in a cloud of dust and rattle of metal.] QUIGLEY: Leave all the gimmicks, and all the props, and all the distractions at home, Black! You can talk yourself into believing you've got my number, but you know just as everyone else knows, I was the #3 ranked wrestler in the entire world, and if you ask me, that was two spots too low! Come Hardcore, I'm going to live up to the hype! I'm going to achieve what I've been waiting to achieve in the FWLI! You can forget about going home with that North American title, Black! MAKE OTHER PLANS! When the final bell sounds, and you're nothing more than a grease stain on the mat, there will be a new FWLI North American champion! There's a new gun in town, and you'd better get used to it... FAST!
