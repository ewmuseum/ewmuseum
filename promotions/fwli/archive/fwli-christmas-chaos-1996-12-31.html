FWLI Fantasy Wrestling League International ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- FWLI Christmas Chaos December 25, 1996 LIVE FROM ASPEN, COLORADO Charity Card for Brian Davis Foundation ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Report by Daniel West: Seasons Greetings! This Year the wrestlers of FWLI spent their Christmas in Aspen, Colorado. At a charity card being held at the Apsen Extreme Lodge to benifit the Brian Davis Foundation, a charity organization providing help for underpriviledged kids. Here's a run-down of the night's incredible action. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- MATCH #1: Scott Rogers vs. Samson Stargill Here we had quite possibly the two top power wrestlers in FWLI finally going at it 1 on 1. The action was fierce from the opening bell with each man hitting with some incredible moves showcasing their strength and talent. In the end, though, Stargill hit Rogers with the Star Press and got the 1-2-3. I doubt this is the last time we'll see these two square off though as Rogers scored more than one near-fall. Winner: Samson Stargill by pinfall Time: 8:27 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- MATCH #2: G.W.R. (Loco & Spoiler) vs. The Devious Dangers ("Modern Day Romeo" Ryan Rankin & Greg "The Barber" Roberts) This one started out as a fast paced encounter with constant tags and excellent team work. However as time went on Rankin & Roberts began to take hold of the match and after about 8 minutes were pummelling GWR with vastly superior team work. Roberts hits Spoiler with Sedative and pick up the pinfall. Winner: The Devious Dangers by pinfall Time: 12:08 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- MATCH #3: Carnage vs. The Storm This one just wasn't even close. Carnage dominated from the time the bell rung to time he finally decided to pin Storm. He hit with the Maximum Carnage twice before going for the pinfall and picking up the win. Winner: Carnage by pinfall Time: 4:58 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- MATCH #4: Twisted Steel & Sex Appeal (Johnny "Hotbody" Nicholls & "Krushing" Kurt Kissinger) vs. The Lords of the Ring (Flex Jupiter & Electron) These two went after each other viciously. This match was even until just after the 10 minute mark. Jupiter hit Nichols with a tremendous powerslam, and Nichols looked to be out cold, but instead of going for the pin Jupiter began to taunt Kissenger, allowing Nichols to recover enough to roll him up for the 1,2,3 in a tremdous upset. TS&SA picked a big win here over the former World Tag Team Champions and look to have a bright future in FWLI. Winner: Twisted Steel & Sex Appeal by pinfall Time: 14:36 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- MATCH #5: FWLI European Heavyweight Champion "Iceman" Brian Knight vs. "Southern Shooter" Doug Fury This was quite possibly one of the best matches I've seen in years as Knight & Fury went after each other in a purely technical encounter. Fury managed to recover the Pyro-Plex twice, and Knight managed to break the Southern Cross. And after 45 minutes of some of the best tecnical wrestling you could ever hope to see, the bell rang and the match was declared a draw. Winner: Time-Limit Draw Time: 45:00 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- MATCH #6: 2 OF 3 FALLS: Otto "The Butcher" Verhoeven vs. "Ugly American" Ed Dean There had been some complaints that this match hadn't shown up on the card for Hardcore, but the fans here Colorado got to see Verhoeven and Dean go one on one. All-in-all it wasn't pretty. Dean got some good moves in, but Verhoeven clearly outmatched him here. Verhoeven picked two straight falls and another big victory as he works his way up the FWLI ladder. Winner: Otto "The Butcher" Verhoeven 2 pinfalls to 0 Time: 1st Fall 6:28, 2nd Fall 10:29 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- MATCH #7: The Misfits (Godiva & Dalbello Rage) & Pizzazz vs. Crystal Nova ("Black Lotus" Patricia Sabal & "Airborne" Angela Klein) & "Maniac" Melanie Engel Another thing some felt was missing from Hardcore was women's wrestling. This match was fast paced and showcased some great wrestling, but there was little crowd heat for it. Klein pinned Pizzazz to win the match, and I must say it is my opinion that Pizzazz should stick to managing and leave the wrestling to the Misfits. She was by far the weakest member of the team. Winner: Crystal Nova & "Maniac" Melanie Engel by pinfall Time: 12:51 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- MATCH #8: FWLI TV TITLE VS. NAME MATCH: FWLI World TV Champion "Bandwidth Mountie" Jean-Pierre Vachon vs. "Living Legend" Henry Atkinson In yet another TV title match with odd stipulations, Vachon shows us just how he's held that title for over a year. In less than a minute, he got Atkinson to the mat and applies the Keyboard Lock-Up. From there it's all academic. Let's all congratulate "The Living Legend" Jean-Pierre Vachon, on yet another defense. Winner: "Living Legend Bandwidth Mountie " Jean-Pierre Vachon by submission Time: 0:54 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- MATCH #9: FWLI WORLD TAG-TEAM TITLE MATCH: FWLI World Tag-Team Champions Damage, Inc. ("Mad Dog" Eddy Ramos & "Ace" Alex Porteau) vs. FWLI European Tag-Team Champions The Prophets of Rage (Derek & Shadoe Rage) Now with just three matches to go on the card, we got to see DI take The Prophets of Rage. These two teams just tore each other apart, before all was said and done all four men were bleeding like faucets and were pounding the hell out of each other. The results... a double count-out. I have to admit I look forward to seeing these two teams face off again. Winner: Double Count-Out Time: 18:42 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- MATCH #10: FWLI NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH TEXAS - TORNADO RULES - NO DQ - NO COUNTOUT: FWLI North American Tag-Team Champions The Universal Powers ("Blitz Lightning" Bobby Lincoln & "Thunderbolt Flash" Todd Franklin) vs. The Untouchables (Mark "The Executioner" Engel & "Quickstrike" Chris Quigley) All I can say is that Newkirk better get ready to hand that World Title over to Engel and Vic Black will be lucky to fend off Quigley and hold onto his title. That's right, folks, we've got NEW North American Tag Team Champions as Quigley forces Franklin to submit with a Boston Crab. This was a tremendous upset folks, and I'm impressed with how well Engel & Quigley worked as a team. Winner: The Untouchables by submission - NEW FWLI North American Tag-Team Champions Time: 14:09 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- *****MAIN EVENT***** $10,000 WINNER-TAKE-ALL 27-MAN OVER-THE-TOP-ROPE BATTLE ROYAL: ORDER OF ELIMINATION: 1. The Genius 2. Ricky Rice 3. The Perfect One 4. Mane 5. Mr. Arrogant 6. "Outlaw" Joe Wayans 7. Vic "The Rebel" Black 8. "Fabulous" Freddy Blair 9. "Cruiser" Eddie Carpenter 10. "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk 11. Dokuhebi 12. "Better Than You" Matt Foxx 13. T'Challa 14. Dan "Flash" Kauffman 15. Michael "The Maverick Reyna 16. Scott Taylor 17. "Heartbreak King" Shawn Masters 18. "Gambit" Jean-Luc Nancett 19. The Sandman 20. "Flagman" Joe Melillo 21. Sir Xavier Morgan 22. Chris "Hit-Man" Thomas 23. Brody Thunder 24. "Space-Age Playboy" Space O'Ryan 25. American Enforcer 26. "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor WINNER: "Desirable" Danny Dynamite Without going into too much detail, here are some high-lights from the match. Dokuhebi applied the Venom-Lock on a worn down T'Challa early in the match. Matt Foxx tried to flip them both over the top rope, and did so, but sent himself flying out in the process. Dokuehebi was sure to share his thanks by applying the lock onto Foxx until security got him off. Taylor and Dynamite brawled for over 10 minutes before Dynamite finally managed to flip him over the top rope and win the battle royal and the $10,000. After the match Dynamite announced that he was giving the money to the Foundation, recieving a huge pop from the fans. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- The event was a huge sucess and the Davis Foundation is more than pleased with the money that was raised. I have to say it's great to see all the stars of FWLI pitching in to help a good cause. This is Daniel West for Fantasy Wrestling League International hope all of you have a Happy New Year and tune in for our next even The Canadian Invitational Tournament. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
