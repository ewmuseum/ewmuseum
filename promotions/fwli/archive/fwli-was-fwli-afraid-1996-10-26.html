Was FWLI Afraid?

26 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I recently filled out an application for a wrestling fed called the FWLI. I'm sure some of the people on RSPWF have heard of it. I joined because it had a lot of the people listed in various top 10 lists on this newsgroup. A few other friends from the EWA signed up as well, so we could see how we compared to the FWLI. We sent the apps in with our debut roleplays... But they never replied to us. They completely ignored us. I sent an e-mail asking if I was in or not and it was never returned. I know I was good enough to get in, because I looked at other flashes posted on the webpage and my flashes were a hell of a lot better than most on that page. We then quit after we realized we were being ignored. I went back to the page today to look at some RPs and they had a new guy on there, so I know they didn't turn us down because they were full. Here's the RP that was on there... ------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI newcomer Mane. Taped Friday 10/25/96: FWLI, get ready, 'cause the world's greatest high flyer is here. You can call me Mane, and I'm here to prove myself to all the wrestlers and fans in North America. In my years wrestling Lucha style, I've become a favorite in my home country. But I've always found that the competition is at FWLI. I still must get used to the North American style of wrestling, but my goal is to become the greatest wrestler in the world eventually. What better place to start than the greatest federation in the world!!! My open challenge is out. If you wish to take the risk, face me. I know I can beat anyone here, and I just have to prove it to all the great fans here at FWLI! See you in the ring! ------------------------------------------------------------------------ That's it... With my application, I sent in two debut roleplays, I have pasted them below... ------------------------------------------------------------------------ [Christian Kingsworth sits in his home office. He's at his desk, sitting in a black leather chair. He's twenty four years old and a good looking man. He's dressed in a white t-shirt and black pants with black dress shoes. His blonde hair is slightly long, just long enough to put back into a pony tail. His fiancee, Ambition, sits on top of the desk. She's very attractive and the same age as him. She has long blonde hair and is wearing a tight white dress, accenting every curve on her body. Kingsworth looks rather humored as he begins speaking to the FWLI camera.] CHRISTIAN KINGSWORTH: I was looking around and I found a league called FWLI. It sparked my interest so I decided to check it out. I reached an agreement with them and they began their tests... And what interesting tests they were. [smirks] I get in there and what's the first thing they ask me? Name. They actually had to ask ME what my name was. ME! They didn't know the name of Christian Kingsworth, what kind of moronic fool doesn't know my name? I'm the greatest wrestler ever in the history oof anything, so help me God. That's no big deal though, soon the FWLI will know who I am, then they'll wish they never asked in the first place. Then they asked me where I'm from. Well people, I come from the one and only Santa Monica, California. It's no Beverly Hills, but I'm true to my place of birth. In fact... I'm probably the biggest celebrity in Santa Monica, and quit possibly everywhere else as well. I mean... Just look at me! It's obvious how great I am, isn't it? I have the looks, the skills, the money, the woman, what more could a guy ask for? They had to record my height, weight, and all that other good stuff that's useful. But then... Out of ALL the things they could ask... The ask for my EYE COLOR! My eye color! They actually had someone check to see what my eye color was. I hope this isn't one of those freaky places where they implant a computer chip deep back in my eye to keep track of where I'm going. After that hell I went through signing up, I finally got in. I mean, who WOULDN'T sign me? You'd have to be mentally ill not to want the greatest wrestler in the world in your league if you were the President, owner, or whatever. I was shown as list of FWLI wrestlers and I laughed my head off! Sorry about that guys, but that was just my reaction. Ambition, did you check that list out? AMBITION: Yeah, pretty comical stuff. I never thought I'd see the day when they let someone named "Greased Lightning" wrestle. And the worst part is, he actually has a title. [shakes head] It's just sad. KINGSWORTH: I see where you're comin' from here, they have some weird guys on this list, and it's amazing who actually does good here. They have all these guys... All these comical names... But the one that I thought topped them all was "Bandwidth Mountie" Jean-Pierre Vachon. "The Bandwith Mountie?!" What kind of name is that?! And he has the World TV Championship. Hey Pierre... Lets see you hold on to that thing against someone like me. I really find the competition her quite dismal at best. FWLI... Show me what you got, I'm ready for whatever it is and a whiole lot more. [Fade to Black] ------------------------------------------------------------------------ [Christian Kingsworth sits in his home office. He's at his desk, sitting in a black leather chair. He's dressed in a white t-shirt and black pants with black dress shoes. His blonde hair is slightly long, just long enough to put back into a pony tail. Ambition sits on top of the desk. She's wearing a tight white dress, accenting every curve on her body. Her blonde hair is tied back in a pony tail. Kingsworth looks into the camera begins speaking.] CHRISTIAN KINGSWORTH: I saw some of the big names in the FWLI, but never really said anything about them. Mainly because I haven't heard of most of these losers. I was looking at it before... Now where did I put it... Ambition? You know where the list is? AMBITION: Uh huh... [Ambition reaches behind the desk, opens a drawer, and pulls out a piece of paper. She reads looks at it and reads.] AMBITION: Okay, here we go, try to contain yourself. [smiles] FWLI World Heavyweight Champion... "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk. KINGSWORTH: Wow! "Nature Boy," I think I've heard that name before... I can't quite place it though... Sounds like he's a naturally born loser that's undeserving of that title to me. I'll fight Newkirk sometime down the road, but I think I'll let a friend of mine take that World Title from him first. [smiles] Trust me on this one, Todd, you can't beat me or any of my friends. Don't get pissed off at me either, worry about my friend, I'm letting him take you out first. It wouldn't be a good idea to get pre-occupied with me. [smiles] Who's next? AMBITION: FWLI World Lightheavyweight Champion... [small laugh] Greased Lightning. KINGSWORTH: Straight out of "Grease." Tell me, Greaser, do you dress up like John Travolta and sing songs every five minutes for no apparent reason what so ever? That's the image I get just from your lame name. Next! AMBITION: Wow! It's our friend The Bandwidth Mountie and he's actually the FWLI Television Champion. Jean-Pierre Vachon is doing quite well, if I must say so myself. KINGSWORTH: It's a sad sad day when someone called "The Bandwidth Mountie" wins a title in ANY place. I don't even know who this guy is and I already hate him, just for having a stupid name. Hey Jean-Pierre, you must be somewhat decent, correct? You must have at least some bravery in that Badwidth Mountie head of yours, right? Well, I'm challenging you to a match for taht title of yours. I don't reallt expect you to give it to me now, but perhaps after I beat a FWLI "great" you'll change your mind. Give it to me or not, but I'll insult you until you do. Who's next, baby? AMBITION: Ummmmmmmmmmmmm... Some guy named Vic "The Rebel" Black who just happens to be the FWLI North American Heavyweight Champion. KINGSWORTH: "The Rebel?" How exactly are you a "rebel" Vic? Explain that for me. My guess is your some little geek trying to be cool so you walk around using the "rebel" name. Try showing me how to be a rebel sometime and I'll make you look like Joe Do Gooder. Who's next on this list of comedic performers? AMBITION: "Iceman" Brian Knight, the FWLI European Heavyweight Champion. KINGSWORTH: European Champion? Doesn't sound too Eauropean to me, but whatever. Believe it or not, I've heard of this guy before. Only because I saw a poster of him when I walked into FWLI offices, but that's not the point. He seems like another undeserving champion if you ask me, but I don't wanna take his belt from him. Don't even THINK that you can beat me if you want to hold onto that belt, Iceman. I don't even want your European Championship, I want the Televsion Championship. So be greatful that you don't have to face me and move on. Who next? AMBITION: That's it for the singles champions, but there's a ton of other wrestlers on here. Here... Look at it. [Ambition hands Kingsworth the list of FWLI wrestlers. He skims it over and comments.] KINGSWORTH: I have never in my life seen a larger group of overrated wrestlers in my life. [shakes head] This is pathetic. Look who's here, "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor. I've heard a lot about this guy, but I've never actually seen him in action or do anything even remotely important. So, naturally, I assume he's overrated, which he probably is. Some people think he's good for some reason and I'm just the guy to end that rumor once and for all. Chris Taylor may be good, but he's no Christian Kingsworth. Show me your stuff "Hot Stuff" and we'll see who's the better of us. I already know the answer and any educated person knows the answer as well. That answer is me. I'm better than ANYONE in the FWLI and anyone who disagrees can take tha up with me in the ring. [Kingsworth looks down at the list again, looking for another name. He finds one, then looks back up and speaks again.] KINGSWORTH: Dan Kauffman! That just made my day! People think this guy's great too, I don't really see why. I'd kick his ass in a matter of a few short minutes. I think I just found my first FWLI match... Dan Kauffman... [laughs] I can't wait for this. Come on Danny boy, fight me! I BEG you, step into the ring with Christian Kingsworth and show me what you're made of. I've always wanted to fight you, just because people give you respect that you don't deserve. I should be the one getting that respect, not a bum like you. You hear that FWLI officials? Sign the match! Christian Kingsworth versus Dan Kauffman, A-S-A-P. I want to destroy Kauffman just to prove my greatness to you all. What a better way to start than with a bang? And what a better way to start with a bang than by beating a big name in the wrestling world? Kauffman... I really pity you. The rest of this list is one-hundred percent garbage and I could really care less about any of the guys on here. [Kingsworth crumples it up and tosses the list in the trash. Then he begins speaking again.] KINGSWORTH: Christian Kingsworth is here FWLI, so go warn all your champions, so called "legends" and especially Dan Kauffman, Chris Taylor, and "The Bandwidth Mountie" Jean-Pierre Vachon. I'll be there VERY soon to collect what's rightfully mine. The FWLI Television Championship and a hell of a lot of respect. Don't deny me. [Fade to Black] ------------------------------------------------------------------------ There... You can't honestly tell me that my roleplays weren't good enough to make it into the FWLI. I sent them to the addresses listed on the webpage and they were never returned. The page is updated frequently, so I know the fed is still around. The only conclusion I have is that they were afraid of us proving we could beat their feds best. If anyone in the FWLI reads RSPWF, which I'm sure they do, tell me how I didn't get into the fed while "Mane" did. We didn't try any NWO takeover angles, we just wanted to see how we compared to some other guys that aren't in "our" feds of the EWA, DPW, and PCW. And Janis... Lets see you try to explain this one to me. -Eric
