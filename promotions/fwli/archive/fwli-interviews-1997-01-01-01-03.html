--------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with the *NEW* FWLI North American Tag-Team Champions The Untouchables ("Quickstrike" Chris Quigley & the *NEW* FWLI World Heavyweight Champion Mark "The Executioner" Engel), along with "Maniac" Melanie Engel & Stephanie. Taped Wednesday 01/01/97: [The setting is a New Year's Eve party in "The Chamber" (the Engels' home gym), which has been decorated with streamers and banners reading "HAPPY NEW YEAR", "UNTOUCHABLES RULE", and "MARK: THE CHAMP SO NICE, HE'S CHAMPION TWICE". Other FWLI wrestlers and their guests are there too, as is FWLI Interviewer Daniel West and a VERY attractive blonde on his arm. A giant-screen TV shows the ball dropping in Times Square.] ALL: [counting down] 5... 4... 3... 2... 1... HAPPY NEW YEARRRRR!!! [Mark & Melanie share a passionate kiss, as do Chris & girlfriend Stephanie and the other couples. Everyone hugs everyone else. Champagne glasses are emptied.] WEST: Happy New Year, Mark, Melanie, Chris, and Stephanie! MARK: Happy New Year!!! And what a way to end 1996 and ring in 1997! [he holds up both the North American Tag-Team belt and the World Heavyweight belt] WEST: First off, congratulations to both you and Chris. You got an excellent Christmas present in Aspen, Colorado, winning the North American Tag-Team Championship from The Universal Powers. CHRIS: You got that right. And now I hear they've left FWLI altogether in shame. [kisses Stephanie, then slaps Mark on the back] Redemption is sweet, ain't it fellow Untouchable? MARK: Ain't that the truth! Our first time as partners, and we walk away with the North American Tag-Team Championship. And the fun's just beginning. We get to face some tag-teams who CAN wrestle as well as they talk. CHRIS: And for all those tag-teams out there who want to take us on, just remember that when you face The Untouchables, you have a choice of being quickly struck down, or slowly executed. Like Ness & his people in Chicago, we WILL take you down! WEST: It looks like we've got North American Champs that are in it for the distance. Now, Mark, let's move on to that piece of gold on your other shoulder. You reached the top of the mountain at the Canadian Invitational Tournament. You got your shot against The Nature Boy a little earlier than you thought, but you made good on it. MARK: Just like I said I would, West! There's a lot I could say, but this piece of gold on my shoulder says it all. Nature Boy, you and your pathetic Awesome Alliance tried to pull a fast one on me, taking me out of the Canadian Invitational Tournament. You thought I'd just roll over and die after that, didn't you? You thought you'd have the advantage going into HARDCORE, didn't you? Iceman, Nature Boy, you guys thought you were SOOOO smart, didn't you? Hot Stuff wouldn't even stick up for you. Well, to quote your favorite phrase... [holds up the World Title belt] ... WHOSE GOT THE BELT NOW???!!! [Melanie kisses him as the other guests cheer] WEST: But what about HARDCORE? You've got the Chicago Street Fight against The Nature Boy, and now he's coming after you and YOUR title. Now he's the one with nothing to lose. MARK: Don't get me wrong, West. I didn't beat Nature Boy by underestimating him. He's an excellent wrestler. Do you know why you lost, Nature Boy? It's simple. When you first came in the fed, you were hungry, hungry like I was. You even beat up your best friend to show how hungry you were. Well, you won the title, then you joined the Awesome Alliance. Suddenly, they were protecting your title, not you. You lost your hunger, and it was only a matter of time until you lost your title. Can you be hungry again? At HARDCORE we'll see who's more hungry. CHRIS: And speaking of hungry people, I'm hungry for that North American Heavyweight Title. I'm gonna rule North America, while my partner here rules the rest of the world. Vic Black, you may be The Rebel, but at HARDCORE, your title is MY cause. STEPHANIE: Enough of this interviewing. LET'S PARTY!!!!! If he can just keep improving from week to week he could have title gold around his waist within months. All in all, they are an excellent batch of wrestlers. West: So is there anything about tonights action that dissapointed you? Pierce: Well, first of all, where was Lord Byron? Additionally, it's nice to see someone from Britian in the federation with a brain. He almost makes up for the stupidity of the Royal Crown Champions. Morgan, simply put, is a moron. Would you please tell why a man who stand 6 foot 8 inches tall needs a bodyguard?!?! Not only should he be able to defend himself, but why would he worry about anyone attacking him anyway? I mean, what's to proven by jumping from behind a man whom one could easily beat face to face? I consider the entire RCC to be an insult to the intelligence of the wrestling fans of the world, and do be assured that I've got someone who's more than willing to take all three of those idiots. And speaking of idiots, who exactly let Matt Foxx into the federation? Foxx belongs in some sort of "Home for the Permanently Bewildered". Why should people be forced to listen to rambling of man who has now decided that American Enforcer is someone named Jorge Ramirez? Foxx is yet another example of stupidity running rampant in this sport. And since I'm speaking on stupidity, how can I help but mention G.W.R.? Apparently here we have a team that feels the best way to get matches is to whine and moan. When every other word of a team's interview has to be censored they lose a lot of credibility, but then again it's not as if GWR had any to begin with. To be quite honest, I'm not really sure who the Party Patrol are, but I'm quite sure they could pin GWR for the three count, and I'm even more confident they could do it with me in their corner. The reason for it is quite simple, Loco and Spoiler are losers, period. If Kane knew what has good for him he'd dump them immediately and stick to managing Carnage and Blitzkrieg, two wrestlers to actually have some potential, but I doubt that will happen in that Kane strikes me as the type of manager who some sort of sick masochistic pleasure from repeatedly slamming his head up against a brick wall.
