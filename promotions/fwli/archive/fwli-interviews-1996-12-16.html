[FWLI] Interviews 12/10-12/12

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI Six-Man Tag-Team Champions The Devious Dangers member "Modern-Day Romeo" Ryan Rankin, along with manager "Devious" Dan Davis. Taped Tuesday 12/10/96: [The camera fades in to a basketball court, where Ryan Rankin and Devious Dan Davis are shooting some hoops. Davis is wearing a t-shirt and sweats, and Rankin has on a Four Horsemen t-shirt (not the one w/ the pictures) that he turned into a tank top, and Devious Danger shorts.] DAVIS: Now Ryan, can you stay on topic this time? You know how important this situation is. RANKIN: [after sinking an 18-foot jumper] No prob, boss. The six-man situation, right? DAVIS: Yep. Forget about Morgan, Vachon, and the Powers for a while. RANKIN: Easy. They're not really worth thinking about anyway. But our upcoming six-man opponents are definitely worth thinking about - and talking about. Seems we've got a few teams lining up for shots, but one in particular comes first. G.W.R. and Carnage. The guys that have themselves so concerned with a team that probably won't ever show their faces in FWLI again. Well, let's talk about you, us, and the Dawgs of War. And I'm going to show you that the Dangers understand your problem better than you do. [Rankin takes a second to hit another 18-foot jumper] RANKIN: This explanation will be as smooth as my jump shot. Several months ago, gentlemen, Storm and I were discussing the possibility of teaming up. And we decided to give it a shot. We went out, and defeated Dynamite and Reyna in our first effort. Pretty impressive for a debut, we figured. So we decided to keep going. But for our second match, we wanted to make a huge impact. I mean, something FWLI would never forget. And we found the perfect target... the Dawgs of War. We noticed, back then, the same thing you claimed was a bombshell a couple months ago. That they hadn't defended the belts, and that there had to be a reason for it. So we got in touch with Jason Dean, wondering what was up. I'll say this much... he wanted the Dawgs to get involved again, just as much as we did. But he also said that if it came down to it, he'd work something out to force the Dawgs into the ring with us. And it came down to it. We got the match, and everybody knows that Storm and I have every right to lay claim to the North American belts, because of how we demolished the so-called champs. And that's the difference between us... and you. I'm going to lay this out for you in plain English, so even the "great mind" of General Kane can finally understand it. We saw a potential problem before you did, but we handled it differently. We didn't hire camera crews to follow our every movement, and to tape every little conversation we had. We sure as hell didn't send such tapes in for broadcast. And we didn't rely on words only to make a point. I already told you what we did. We went about it the way things are SUPPOSED to be done in FWLI... we got them in the ring, and wrestled them. We took matters into our own hands, and showed that maybe... just MAYBE... it was the Dawgs that were the problem. They were given a ton of chances to redeem themselves, and they chose not to take advantage of it. So when they stepped in against the Devious Dangers, they got their butts whipped, and when they stepped in against the Universal Powers, they couldn't escape. And now... they're old news. So do you get it now? The way to go isn't talking up a storm. It's getting a match signed, getting in the ring, and taking advantage of the opportunity you're given. There's a LOT of people in this league who could learn from that. [Rankin turns and sinks yet another 18-footer, then quickly moves to the free-throw line.] DAVIS: Ryan, what did I tell you? Stick to the topic. [Davis gives Rankin a bounce-pass, and Rankin prepares to shoot a free throw] Besides, what about that "loyal company men" comment? [Rankin's free throw hits the front rim. MDR gets a bothered look on his face.] RANKIN: What do I think of it? Just the mere mention rattles my concentration. Hint away all you want, boys, at the reasons for it. Consider yourselves clever, sneaky, whatever wets your whistle. But I'll tell ya why we're sticking up for FWLI. Because this can be the best damn organization on the planet, and because we're going to be a part of making it so! We're on a roll! We're in contention for just about every FWLI championship, while you can't get your mind off the Dawgs of War! We're moving ahead, and we're making a difference! [Rankin's anger begins to grow] We don't focus on the past, because it can't be changed! We learn from the past, and from the lessons it teaches us! And we move on! You want to call us loyal company men?? Well, maybe the Devious Dangers oughta do what's best for FWLI, and KNOCK YOU OUT OF THE COMPANY!!! As far as I'm concerned, G.W.R. and Carnage, you can all go to hell. And when you step in the ring with the Devious Dangers, we'll help you get there. [Rankin turns to the hoop and heaves a basketball at the backboard. The ball catches a bit of the front rim on the rebound, and drops through the hoop. Rankin storms off the court, and Davis hurries after him.
