[FWLI] BATTLECRY Control Center - 06/19/96

20 June 1996

I'm Daniel West and, Before we start, I have to extend a personal heart-felt SORROW at a GREAT disappointment to me. You never met a bigger fan of "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor than yours truly over the years. Now, with his recent actions, I can effectively say that the man I once knew and admired is gone. In his memory, I'd like to air the last segment of the Hot Seat that I will ever watch with the same enthusiasm. This, of course, was filmed prior to FWLI Blitz, and features the Player's Club as guests. ******************************************************************************* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * ******************************************************************************* [The arena goes dark, and the crowd grows silent in anticipation. Soon, the speakers play "Hot Stuff" by Donna Summer, and a spotlight follows "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor as he makes his entrance. As soon as "Hot Stuff" enters the interview area, he raises his arms, and pyrotechnic flames ignite all around the area, illuminating a backdrop of flame designs which read, "THE HOT SEAT". In front of the backdrop are two red chairs. The crowd applauds as "Hot Stuff" acknowledges the fans before picking up a microphone and sitting down.] CHRIS: Welcome back to THE HOT SEAT, wrestling fans! My guests tonight are no strangers to the FWLI fans, or to the HOT SEAT for that matter! Please welcome one of wrestlings most notorious duos......Former World Light-Heavyweight Champion and World Six-Man Tag Team Champion....... "DESIRABLE" DANNY DYNAMITE.......and his partner.....World Six-Man Tag Team Champion.......MICHAEL "THE MAVERICK" REYNA....... ["Love and Happiness" by Ron Isley blares over the PA as Reyna and Dynamite make their entrance. Sarah Simpson walks between the two men.] CHRIS: Welcome to the Hot Seat! So, I guess my first question tonight will be "what's going on back stage with The Player's Club?" DDD: Well, as the mafia would say, "If I told ya that, I'd have to kill ya!"...Its top secret Chris. Some teams here in the FWLI would LOVE to know that kinda info. MR: That's right. What happens behind the "closed doors" is vital information. But, soon, everyone will find out. With the help of this lady right here (points to Sarah Simpson) we are going to make waves.. SSS: *inturupting* We've ALWAYS made waves in FWLI.. MR: That's right, I should say BIGGER waves.. CHRIS: I see....well, there's something else I wanted to ask you about, and that's the fact that you were one of the people who offered to tag with me at Autumn Vengeance when my partner walked out on me. I appreciate the offer, but I was a little shocked. We've never really gotten along too well here in FWLI, or in NCW! DDD: Ahh yeah. I wanted that match for a couple reasons. Taylor, we've been on the opposite side of the fence for a while. RSPCW, FWLI, and the NCW have all felt the effects of when we are opposing each other. There was a certian person who couldn't live up to the challenge of being your partner. I guess you could say the "staked were too high". As I recall, the winners got a shot at the world belt. That had some influence, but remember what my teammates in NCW did to your wife? Adam and the gang? I was in that gang, and I felt the wrath of what they did. I was blind-sided by MY own team. I wanted to be your partner to show you that I had NO part in that beating, and if I was able to, I would have stepped in. CHRIS: Okay, I'll buy that! I appreciate the offer, but Blaze and I worked well together as a team that night, and got the victory with an assist from NUKE! If the occasion comes up again, though, who knows? So....what does the future hold for The Player's Club? MR: The future holds nothing but gold. U.S., Euro, and World tag belts will be crossing OUR paths VERY SOON! SSS: That's right. Contracts are drawn up, and waitng to be signed. My men are uncrowned champions, and very, very soon, they will be CROWNED! DDD: That's right, Soviet Front, Porteaux and Ramos, whoever it may be, watch out. And that goes for you too, Devious Dangers. These 6-man belts are OURS, and there is NOTHING you can, or will, do about it! Chris, who knows, maybe, just maybe, WE'LL meet for a championship, and if we do, may the best man win. [Danny extends his hand for a shake, which Chris accepts. The crowd pops.] CHRIS: Fans, you heard it here first! THIS IS THE PLAYER'S CLUB, I'M "HOT STUFF" CHRIS TAYLOR....AND *YOU'VE* JUST BEEN IN "THE HOT SEAT"!!!! ["Hot Stuff" makes a horizontal slashing motion with his arms, and the pyrotechnic flames die out, leaving the arena in darkness.] ******************************************************************************* WEST: As you can see, the attitude of the NCW Champion has changed radically since this segment was filmed. This has given the Lion Pride more to think about than ever in accepting the War Games challenge. We have received a videotape here of a recent meeting of the Lion Pride in the Lion's Den. What you see here will reveal the long-awaitted results of the most anticipated match at BattleCry this year. Let's not waste time. Let's roll the tape. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- HITMAN: OK, guys, this is it. You know what we're here to decide. We've got the odds against us like never before. You've pretty much got the nastiest and toughest wrestlers in the entire FWLI united in the Awesome Alliance. And they all want a piece of us. Seems lately like EVERYONE wants a piece of us, but I don't have to tell you guys that, right?? In fact, that might be the problem. We've all got our individual things going lately, and we REALLY have to be united for this. Hell, it might be cool to see me and Shark go in their alone against four guys, but, if we're gonna go into this match, we're going in as a team. And THAT'S why I wanna hear from each of you. T'Challa, looks like you made your choice..... T'CHALLA: Chris, you know I'd follow you from here to eternity and back again, but if I don't take care of Dokuhebi now, he's gonna be bouncing around in my mind like a superball forever. I haven't felt like this since the days of Chad Sun, and I think we all remember what I was like then. I want to be by your side, Hitman, but I've already got a match against someone who put me out of action for half a year, and if I'm gonna put him and Worthington in their place, I've got to stay focused on him and him alone. Sorry, man, but at War Games, I'm out. HITMAN: I can see you point, T'. Doug, how do you feel?? PATRIOT: Otto Verhoeven's been beating up on anyone and everyone who isn't in line with putting Germany ahead of everything. He cost me my shot at the TV title, but I paid him back in Montreal. Now we've got other guys like "Cruiser" Eddie Carpenter who want a shot at him right now. As for me, my place is by the Pride. Count me in the War Games!!! SHARK: YEAH!!!!!! We can go three on four, right, Chris?? HITMAN: Remember what I said. If we're gonna do this, we're gonna do it united!! Joe?? FLAGMAN: *sigh*....... I would love for nothing more than to shut the mouth of A.J. Charisma once and for all. I'm tired of him thinking I'm his stepping stone. I'm tired of brawling with him up and down the country... it's just not my style. But I've got a bigger problem. I've had my manager taken from me. Blaze has decided her place is by her husband, who's decided HIS place is next to that traitor Newkirk. If I go into this match, I have to face her on the OPPOSITE side for the fisrt time. I don't know if I can handle it. But there's one thing I know for sure. Even though my main inspiration has left me at this tough point in my career, my brothers haven't. I owe the Pride big time..... and I WILL be by their side!! I'M IN!!! SHARK: ALL RIGHT!!!!!! LET'S GO TO WAR!!!!!! T'CHALLA: HHHHHIIIISSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (The five Pride members clasp hands as the camera fades.) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- WEST: So now IT IS OFFICIAL!!!! The Awesome Alliance WILL meet the Lion Pride in a four-on-four War Games match!!! On one side: "The Southern Shooter" Doug Fury, "Iron Man" Chris Andrews, NCW World Champion "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor, the NEWEST member of the Alliance, and captained by FWLI World Champion "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk. On the other side: "Flagman" Joe Melillo, "Canadian Patriot" Douglas George, Mark "The Shark" Zagoudis, and their team captain, Chris "Hitman" Thomas. Now let's review the rules for a War Games match. There will be TWO rings next to each other, surrounded by a large cage on all sides AND on top. The match will start out with a coin toss between the two team captains. The team winning the coin toss will have the advantage throughout the match. The War Games begins with one man from each team entering the cage and battling for five minutes. After that, the team with the advantage will send another man into the cage for a two-on-one advantage for two more minutes. The team without the advantage will send in another man for a two-on-two battle for an additional two minutes. The participants will continue to enter the cage in this manner until all eight participants are inside the cage. It is ONLY after all eight men have entered that the match can end. And there are only TWO ways it can end: submission or loss of consciousness. There is NO disqualification!! Now, as you know, two other matches were dependent on the War Games match being signed. Now that "Flagman" Joe Melillo has made the decision to enter the War Games, his arch-nemesis, A.J. Charisma, will be taking on "Intensity" Craig Williams. Meanwhile, the recent rival of the Canadian Patriot, Otto "The Butcher" Verhoeven, will be taking on "Cruiser" Eddie Carpenter. And, as if this wasn't enough, there have been MORE matches signed!! Twisted Steel and Sex Appeal, a team who recently earned a win over the Player's Club, will face off against the rookie team of the Dynamite Express. Also, in what they're calling "The Battle of Regular Guys", it will be Phil "The Grease Monkey" Strong going up against Joe Bonanno. This makes a total of TWENTY-EIGHT matches!!! But, before we give you the run-down, we have ANOTHER unusual development in one of the matches already signed: the North American title defense by Vic "The Rebel" Black against the former champion, Samson Stargill. Recently, The Rebel was interviewed by Mike Bowie, and here's what happened.... ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Scene: A stage with the FWLI logo at the Continental Airlines Arena soon after the 06/10/96 Blitz taping. Mike Bowie is on the stage with a microphone. BOWIE: Ladies and Gentlemen........ (J.R. interrupts grabs the mic from Mike Bowie.) J.R.: LADIEEEEEEEEEEEESSSSSSSSS AAAAAAAAAAAANNNNNNNNNDDDDDD GGGEEEEENNNTTTTTTLLLLLEEEEEEMMMMMMMMEEEEEEENNNNNNNNNN!!!!!!!! CHIIIIIIILDREN OF AAAAAAAALLLLLLLLLLL AAAAAGGGEEEEEEESSSSSSSS!!!!!!!!!! IT IS AN HONOR AND A PRIVILEGE...... FOR YOU TO BEAR WITNESS TO YOUR NORTH AMERICAN HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION....... THE MAN WHO MAKES HIS OWN RULES AND HIS OWN OPPORTUNITIES........ FROM BROOKLYN, NEW YORK..... VIC........ "TTTTTHHHHHHEEEEEE RRRRRREEEEBBBBBBBEEEEEELLLLLLL"..... BBBBBBLLLLLAAAAAAACCCCCCKKKKKKKKK!!!!!!! (Rebel strides out wearing the North American belt around his waist. He is wearing his leather jacket and a pair of leather pants. He hugs J.R. Worthington and grabs the microphone) REBEL: I have spoken, Jersey Jerks!!!!(Crowd boos loudly....) Look at the gold, I have busted my ass for! I am the TRUE representative of the people, the FWLI fans! I am the champion! Besides myself, I owe this belt to one man, the man who saw me as a loner from the streets of Brooklyn, and guided me to the belt....J. R. Worthington!!!!!! (Audience boos as J.R. Worthington bows from Black's praise.) REBEL: And I am going to keep my promise...wherever I go, my belt goes with me! It will make this year Olympic Torch Relay look like a 100 yard sprint! Because I am the baddest man in the FWLI and I'm going to show the belt of. Even Giant BOB will get a look at it! BOWIE: You now know you are a target for the many challengers to the North American Belt? REBEL: (laughs) You don't think I'm that stupid, do you? I get a woodie thinking about all the competition I get to beat the crap out of! Now that I have the belt---the big guns respect me! BOWIE: What about Samson Stargill? You have heard that a rematch has been signed at Battlecry, AND THE BELT WILL BE ON THE LINE!!!!! (Crowd cheers loudly) REBEL: The only reason why I granted him the rematch was that I felt like it! Jason Dean, if your listening, you can grandstand all you want about the "questionable" way I won the belt....but I pinned him 1,2,3! J.R. and I have allowed Stargill to have another shot... I beat him once and I will beat him again! Oh, and by the way....I have one more surprise! J.R. and I have procured the services of your "friend" Death Warrior Meltdown to be at my corner at Battlecry!! And he will make sure that..... (The Rebel is interrupted by wild cheers as Samson Stargill comes out with Delilah--he has a portable mike in his hand) SAMSON: REBEL!! You think you can scare ME with your tactics?! This time, I will prove that good will triumph over evil!! You having Death Warrior Meltdown is no surprise to me, but let me tell you.. that I have a BIG surprise for you! REBEL: (sarcastically) OOOOOOHHHHH....I'm Scared.... SAMSON: I will also have someone in my corner at BattleCry.. Someone who, much like myself, epitomizes strength and power.. REBEL: Who the hell is that! That "RR" guy? Let him come here.....I'll kick his ass....The Warrior....I'll take his destrucity and shove it....WHO? WHO? BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMM!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!! (The sirens and horns of NUKE's theme play as the crowd goes crazy) REBEL: You got that FREAK? J.R.: BOWIE!!! You must have known about this!!! This is OBVIOUSLY a conspiracy by the FWLI Booking Committee!!! We're marching off to Dean's office RIGHT NOW to get this straightened out!!! (Stargill is posing and flexing to the crowd, who are going nuts!) BOWIE: Get a camera on them! (Rebel and Worthington are seen on Rebel's Harley leaving the parking lot.) J.R.: (off-camera) Hurry, before that ogre finds us!! BOWIE: WOW! What a blockbuster announcement! And NUKE wasn't even in the arena...What will happen at Battlecry....Follow the FWLI on the web and on TV. For the FWLI, I am Mike Bowie! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- WEST: So now Vic Black will have Death Warrior Meltdown in his corner for his match at BattleCry, while Samson Stargill has obtained the services of the legendary NUKE. To recap, here's the FINAL run-down of the matches at BattleCry: - War Games match: Awesome Alliance ("Southern Shooter" Doug Fury, "Iron Man" Chris Andrews, NCW World Champion "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor, captained by FWLI World Champion "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk) vs. Lion Pride ("Flagman" Joe Melillo, "Canadian Patriot" Douglas George, Mark "The Shark" Zagoudis, captained by Chris "Hitman" Thomas) - FWLI European Heavyweight Title match: "Iceman" Brian Knight (champion) vs. "Executioner" Mark Engel - FWLI North American Title match: Vic "The Rebel" Black (champion) w/ Death Warrior Maltdown vs. Samson Stargill w/ NUKE - FWLI Lightheavyweight Title unification match: Greased Lightning (World champion) vs. "Heartbreak King" Shawn Masters (European champion) - FWLI World Tag Team Title match, lucha libre rules: Damage Incorporated ("Ace" Alex Porteaux & "Mad Dog" Eddy Ramos, champions) vs. Lords of the Ring (Flex Jupiter & Electron) - FWLI Television Title match: "Bandwidth Mountie" Jean-Pierre Vachon (champion) vs. "Fabulous" Freddy Blair - FWLI European Tag Team Title match: Soviet Front (Soviet Hit-Man & Soviet Syko, champions) vs. Prophets of Rage (Shadoe & Derek Rage) - FWLI Six-Man Tag Team Title match: Players Club ("Desirable" Danny Dynamite, "Maverick" Michael Reyna, & "Gambit" Jean-Luc Nancett, champions) vs. Devious Dangers (Greg "The Barber" Roberts, "Modern-Day Romeo" Ryan Rankin, & Storm) - FWLI Women's Title match: "Daredoll" Lucy Kincaid (champion) w/ Jangles the Jester vs. "Maniac" Melanie w/ "Small Package" Tony Apollo - Straightjacket match: T'Challa vs. Dokuhebi - A.J. Charisma vs. "Intensity" Craig Williams - Otto "The Butcher" Verhoeven vs. "Cruiser" Eddie Carpenter - Slammasters vs. Sudden Impact - Dan "Flash" Kauffman vs. Sandman - Dr. Feelgood vs. "Z-Man" Brian Zordich - "Q-Man" Chris Quigley vs. Colonel Thunder - American Enforcer vs. Lord Byron - "Blond Bomber" Chris Cavallin vs. "Cavalier" Francois Leblanc - "Duke of Hazard" Winston Major vs. "Outlaw" Joe Wayans - "Prodigy" Avery Prosser vs. "Classic" Andrew Archer - Carnage vs. Sir Xavier Morgan - ABRAXAS vs. "House of Pain" Brian Balboa - Gladiator vs. Spartan - Genius vs. Scott Rogers - Punishers ("Grim Reaper" Jack Williams & "Texas Hangman" Mason Frost) vs. Horrors (Mr. Voorhees & Ned Krueger) - G.W.R. (Loco & Spoiler) vs. Total Package (Mr. Arrogant & Perfect One) - Twisted Steel and Sex Appeal vs. Dynamite Express - Joe Bonanno vs. Phil "The Grease Monkey" Strong Fans, this is sure to be one of the greatest events in wrestling history. All coming soon from the Market Square Arena in Indiannapolis, Indiana. For the BattleCry Control Center, I'm Daniel West.
