FWLI Card Archive

Interviews, 28-31 January 1997

3 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with Lord Byron. Taped Tuesday 01/28/97: [The scene opens at an FWLI housecard. FWLI senior interviewer Daniel West is stood at a raised interview area, waiting for the arrival of one man.] West: Everyone knows of the incredible record set by Jean-Pierre Vachon. [big crowd pop!] He has held the World Television Title for well over a year now, and has defended it against all comers, and silenced many a would be opponent. Will his newest adversary finally be the man to end Vachon's title reign? Or will he, like the others, fall by the wayside? My next guest thinks he can, but then again he is speaking from a personal viewpoint. He is the self- styled 'Greatest technical wrestler in the world' LORD BYYYYYYYYRROOOOON!! ['Intermezzo' from Karelia Suite starts up over the p.a. system, to boos and jeers from the audience, which increase as Byron appears at the head of the aisle, dressed immaculately in a designer suit and carrying of course, his customary brass-topped cane. Byron ignores the jeering crowd completely, and walks up to the interview area.] West: Byron. You have no doubt heard by now the most recent comments from Jean-Pierre Vachon... [The crowd explodes with cheers. Byron ignores them as West waits for the noise to subside.] West: ... Byron, he claimed that you rejected his offer of a 'No Excuses' match because you WANTED excuses! [cheers from the crowd] He claimed that you care more for your own ego than you do for winning the title! [More cheers! Byron glances around at the crowd with disdain] He claimed, Byron, that you are AFRAID! [The crowd explodes with cheers again! Byron snatches the mic off West, and turns to address the crowd.] Byron: Mr. West.... [The crowd roars with boos and jeers as Byron speaks. Byron pauses, and glares around at the crowd, waiting for them to quiet down.] Byron: Mr. West. I am well aware of what our friend [Byron sneers] The Living Legend's views are. [He takes on a mocking tone] If I'd known that Vachon would get this upset, I would have willingly signed the 'No Excuses' match. But as for the assumption that I refused it out of fear, the man is way off target. And as for the [Byron spits the word out] assumption that I care more about my own ego than I do about winning the TV title, I can only say this: The man is a fool. [The crowd erupts with rage. Byron again waits for the noise to subside, sneering with contempt at the crowd's response.] Byron: If he thinks that I'm interested in anything else but taking the Television title, then he's a fool. If he thinks that I'm going to go into our match looking for a count out or disqualification, then he is a fool. [The crowd roars in anger] And most importantly, if he thinks I turned down his contract because I am afraid of him, or anyone else in the FWLI, THEN HE IS A FOOL! [The crowd is now up on it's feet yelling abuse at Byron. Byron snorts in derision, and almost shouts to be heard above the noise.] Byron: Vachon, I back down from no one! You can list all the so called big names you want to, because I know that when I'm at my best I can give any one of them the wrestling lesson of their lives and come out on top! This isn't an ego defect, Vachon, this is confidence! I have complete faith in my own wrestling ability, and I know that it's more than enough to beat you, Dynamite, Engel and half a dozen other wrestlers whose names I don't care to mention! In time, I'll get the chance to prove it, but right now, I'm going to content myself with becoming the man to finally strip you of the title you should have lost a LONG time ago! [The crowd continues to react angrily. West waits for them to settle down again.] West: If you think you've got the talent to beat Vachon through pure wrestling, why didn't you accept the 'No Excuses' match in the first place? Byron: Two reasons, Mr. West. One: Because I don't intend to be just another chapter in the [Byron sneers] 'Living Legend'. If I had accepted the 'No Excuses' match, and won the title, people would remember not me beating Vachon, but him having practically handed it to me on a silver platter. Reason two: Because I simply don't trust the man as far as I could throw him. No, Vachon, I'm not going to let you play this your way, too many have tried and failed. I don't intend to join their number. When we step into the ring, the FWLI will see a match of a quality the likes of which are rarely seen. And when the dust settles, I thoroughly intend to be the next reigning FWLI Television Champion. [Byron turns to leave, then pauses and turns back.] Byron: One last thing Vachon. You claim you can defeat the Aristoclutch? That may be the most dangerous assumption you've made yet. To do that, Vachon, you'd have to break it, and I'll be damned if I let that happen. Maybe, Vachon, once you find yourself face down, writhing in agony and your consciousness slowly begins to fade away, maybe then you'll think differently. Maybe you'll begin to value your career more than your pride. [Byron sneers] But by that time, maybe I just won't care anymore whether you submit or not. Think about it, Vachon. Ciao. [Byron hands the mic back to West, bows mockingly to the jeering crowd and leaves the interview area.] West: Byron vs. Vachon. Whichever way this feud ends, it could well be the match of the century. --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with "Vivacious" Veronica Macpherson. Taped Tuesday 01/28/97: [The picture fades into a smoky, dungy bar, which is filled with unruly drunk men. The camera cuts to the door of the bar, which opens all of the sudden. Enter Veronica Macpherson, wearing jogging shorts and a tank top. She seems to be distracted, looking at a beeper in her hand. The men take notice of her immediately.] MACPHERSON: Excuse me, but where is the phone around... [notices the gang of men surrounding her] Umm, I think I'm in the wrong place. [Macpherson goes to leave when a thug grabs her in a reverse bearhug.] THUG #1: No baby, I think you're in the right place. [The camera cuts to a wide shot. Macpherson struggles for a second, trying to break free, and then she elbows the thug in the stomach. She then kicks the thug in the head, causing him to fly back and go through a nearby table. Two other thugs get thrown judo-style, and another is the receipiant of a stiff arm. One final brave man comes at Macpherson, only to have Macpherson grab his left arm and snap it over her shoulder. The camera shifts to a close up of Macpherson's face, which is wearing a scowl.] MACPHERSON: Who's next? [The rest of the gang move away from Macpherson, when all of the sudden a bearded man wearing a baseball cap and glasses breaks into the scene.] DIRECTOR: CUT AND PRINT!! Veronica, baby, that was beautiful! Very realistic! With your looks and talent, you're going to be the biggest star in the world! Take 5, everybody! THUG #2: I THINK SHE BROKE MY ARM!!! [Macpherson moves toward a set of director chairs as a set of EMTs come to the aid of the second thug. Macpherson notices the FWLI camera that's waiting for her.] MACPHERSON: Oh, I didn't see you there. I trust that you got that scene on tape? [The camera moves up and down] Good. Maybe I should explain why you're here. You see, I've taken a few months off and have been shooting a film. I star, along with Stephen Baldwin and Dana Carvey, in a film about a female attorney who's searching for the truth about her fiance's death. While she's searching, she meets up with a smart-cracking sidekick and a straight-laced tough cop who falls for her. We think that it's going to be a big hit! [Macpherson's expression becomes more serious, along with her inflection.] MACPHERSON: But this has taught me one major lesson: Acting is fun, but it isn't really work. I have no real desire to keep this up full time. All that it's done is intensify my desire to be a wrestler, and become the World Women's Champion! I've noticed that we have a new woman's champion. Well, congrats to you, Ms. Perfect, but you'll excuse me if I'm not impressed. You see, I know that I could defeat Melanie without any help, let alone with the inept aid of an over the hill wench like Blaze. You see, that scene back there should show you not only how serious I am about getting that belt, but what I'm capable of doing given the chance. I don't really care who it is that I have to defeat to get what would make my waist look even better than humanly possible. I've been with GL for his full tenure as Lightheavyweight champion of the World. I've seen how much that belt means to him, and I now have that desire, that passion, for your belt Diabello Rage! DIRECTOR: Veronica, we need you now! MACPHERSON: Just remember Diabello, The Queen of the Night always gets what she wants, and I want that belt! [to the Director] So who do I get to beat up next? [Macpherson heads back to the set as the picture fades to black.] --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI World Light-Heavyweight Champion Greased Lightning. Taped Tuesday 01/28/97: [Daniel West stands on top of the interview podium, ready to introduce his next guest.] WEST: Ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce my next guest. HE IS THE FWLI UNIFIED WORLD LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, HERE IS GGGGRRRRRREEEEEAAAAAASSSSSSSEEEEEDD LLLLIIIIGGGGGGHHHHHTTTTNNNNNIIINNNNNNGGGG! ["21st Century (Digital Boy)" by Bad Religion starts up over PA system as Lightning emerges from the dressing room area. The crowd's reaction is mixed, with most fans booing, and some cheering. Lightning is wearing is silver and teal tunic, and has the FWLI Unified Light Heavyweight belt over his shoulder. He climbs on top of the podium and shakes West's hand, much to the shock of West.] WEST: Well, that was a surprise. LIGHTNING: You were expecting me to hug you? WEST: NO! I mean, I was expecting to get blown off like all of the other interviewers that you've been with. But were have you been? You haven't graced us with your presence in a while. LIGHTNING: Let me assure you that my inactivity was not self-imposed. WEST: So you were suspended then. LIGHTNING: No, I've been able to wrestle if I was given the opportunity. I was at the Canadian Invitational, for example, and something hit me while I was there. WEST: [mumbling] Great, another wrestler gets in a fight with a fan situation. LIGHTNING: [waving hand in front of West's face] Danny Boy, Earth to Danny Boy! You're supposed to be listening to me, not having thoughts of your own. Anyway, as I was saying, I started reflecting on who was invited to this tournament. Chris Thomas was there, right? WEST: Yeah, but that's not really surprising... LIGHTNING: [cutting West off] Yep, pinned him for this belt. Danny Dynamite was also there. WEST: Well, he is a very popular... LIGHTNING: [cutting West off again] Pinned him as well. Chris Andrews is yet another one of the elite eight that participated in the tournament. WEST: He is a Canadian. LIGHTNING: Odd. So am I. I've also beaten him on many, many occasions. But hey, that's the past, right? There's still Hardcore to talk about. A fine, fine event. Many world titles on the line as well. WEST: You're right there. Hardcore will be remembered as one of the best events in the history of the FWLI. LIGHTNING: Yeah, and all of the male World titles were on the line as well. Except for one. Can you guess which one West? WEST: Yes, you were conspicuous by your absence. LIGHTNING: You see, there is a point to my rantings. Basically, the executive here in the FWLI HAS ABSOLUTELY NO RESPECT FOR WHAT I HAVE ACCOMPLISHED AND WHAT I HAVE BROUGHT TO THIS FEDERATION, AND THEY HAVE DONE EVERYTHING IN THEIR POWER TO KEEP ME DOWN! WEST: Oh great, yet another genius. LIGHTNING: [oblivious to West] And their disrespect for me has fed into the competitors in the FWLI as well! I beat Chris Quigley from here to Newfoundland, and he has no clue as to who I am? Mark Bagwell joins the federation and calls me a nobody? [Lightning takes the belt from his shoulder] WELL GENTLEMEN, THIS BELT RIGHT HERE SAYS THAT, POUND FOR POUND, I AM THE BEST WRESTLER IN THE FWLI, AND THIS ALONE SHOULD COMMAND YOUR RESPECT! [The fans boo] Problem is, if you aren't going to give it to me right now, you aren't going to until I prove that I deserve it. So, what this means is that I'm going to have to wrestle each and every FWLI competitor, and put your shoulders to the mat to get a three count. And once I've beaten each and every one of you, everybody in the world, whether it be Chris Quigley, Mark Bagwell, Chris Thomas, Chris Taylor, Todd Newkirk, Brian Knight, Mark Engel or even Mister D. Alexander Autrey himself, will realize that there is only one LEADER OF THE NEW GENERATION, AND THAT IS THE BEST DAMN WRESTLER IN THE WORLD, GREASED LIGHTNING. Now if you'll excuse me, I have some training to do! [The fans boo louder as Lightning leaves the podium and the picture fades to black.] --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with Mane. Taped Tuesday 01/28/97: [The interview opens up with Mane's theme song playing "The Great Southern Trendkill" by Pantera. There are clips of Mane doing some of his famous moves, the Main Attraction, a Somersault Splash from the middle of the top rope, and the Shooting Star Press, as well as other death defying moves. It then clips to Mane.] Mane: I just wanted to say that it's been a long wait, but when it's finally over, it'll be worth it. Brody Thunder, I'll be taking you on at Showcase in February. You can think beyond me, you can underestimate me right now, but at Showcase, you'll learn that Mane is no pushover. I underestimated you, and I think that you're underestimating me. I didn't know that you were near the top of the IIWF, or the other federations that you're in. It's nice to know that, now I'll treat you like a real threat. But one thing that you'll have to earn from me is my respect. And that's where Showcase comes in. I know how it'll work. You'll be the bull, chasing after me, and I'll hold the blue cape out for you. You know I'm quicker than you, I just have to stay one step ahead of you. I'll tire you out, I'll make you look like a fool. I just can't stop. And Brody, that's something I don't intend on doing. I was watching when you lost to the Living Legend at the house show tour. You're pushing too far too fast, and it's backfiring on you. I'm aiming for you, and you only. And that's how my career in the FWLI is going to go. One step at a time, moving forward. It's a long buildup to a match that essentially means nothing to the league at this time, Brody. But they'll be looking back on this match a year from now, they'll be saying "Wow, that was the match that started it all, the match that started the redefinition of the FWLI". Who's gonna be the leader of this movement, Brody? I think we're gonna find out in a few weeks. We can look back on all of this: the words, the money, the cigars, the employment lines, but we both know that it'll come down to the heart. Can you give what it takes to make it in the FWLI? Can you give what it takes to beat me? We'll find out at Showcase. --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with Scott Rogers. Taped Wednesday 01/29/97: [Scott Rogers is in the center of the ring after demolishing Perry Gordon in a couple of minutes. As the ring announcer announces who has won, Rogers hops out of the ring and grabs the mic off him. He stands in front of the guard rails and begins to shout of the huge heel pop which is mounting.] Rogers: Shut it. [pause] I said SHUT IT! [the crowd noise simmers down] Thank you. [Rogers pushes his hair back] So, Samson's finally answered my call. What a man. I ask him to name me an opponent... any opponent, and what does he say? Yeah, he tells me he ain't bothered. Well, Samson, I'll get back to you in a couple of weeks, or months, when I've beaten a few of your 'friends' up. I've been looking at the rankings here. I see there are a couple of guys who are taking my position in the top five for this North American Title, 'cause let's face it if anyone deserves to be there it's me! [big heel pop]. Shut your stinkin' mouths. [the pop gets louder and then dies down] Listen up. [sarcastically] Chris Quigley [big face pop]. Yeah, he could beat me. Chris Thomas. There's another, of course. Joe Melillo. Great athlete, but the guy I'm really worried about is this AJ Charisma. [Rogers' voice takes on a sinister tone] Charisma, when I first heard your name, I thought you had to be a jobber. And guess what? I was right. [the fans go bezerk] But then again, I always am. [A cola can hits Rogers on the shoulder. Rogers looks round at the guy who threw it and stares at him. The fans quieten down a bit as the guy, with a handlebar moustache sinks into his seat. Rogers pauses as the crowd go silent.] Rogers: Charisma... Quigley... Thomas... Melillo, I'm not difficult to find. I know the men in suits here have been keeping you away from me, but that's gonna end. I'm gonna find every one of you starting with Melillo and work through the lot of you you until I get to Stargill again. [Rogers throws the mic back at the ring announcer and walks back slowly to the locker rooms.] --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with Twisted Steel & Sex Appeal (Johnny "Hotbody" Nicholls & "Krushing" Kurt Kissinger), along with Vixen. NICHOLLS: Just like when we kick GWR's a*ses... and look so damn good while we do it!!! --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI European Heavyweight Champion and Awesome Alliance leader "Iceman" Brian Knight. Taped Wednesday 01/29/97: [Scene: The FWLI Arena, in Eustis, Florida. Daniel West is standing at the broadcasters position, microphone in hand.] West: Ladies and gentlemen, I present to you, the FWLI European Heavyweight Champion, "Iceman" Brian Knight!!!!!! [The fans give a mostly negative reaction, as "Little Things" strikes up. Knight heads to the broadcasters area in Cool Blue brand jeans, a "Hot Stuff" t-shirt, and the European Title over his shoulder. Knight takes off his sunglasses, and flicks them casually to the crowd. His ever present smirk still adorns his lips.] West: Brian, first of all welcome to the FWLI Arena. I know you are quite familiar with it. Knight: Quite familiar? I've trained many a time in this hall. It's almost a second home. West: Brian, before Hardcore, you were sure of yourself. At Hardcore, you went off the deep end, bringing in the long departed Mark Destructo, and having him try to defeat Mark Engel [crowd pop]. On that same night, you attempted to destroy American Enforcer with a baseball bat! What is going on in the mind of Brian Knight? Knight: Dan, let me tell ya. Watching Mark pick "The Executioner" up in that backbreaker made me smile. It reminded me of something that I'd forgotten. You see, I've worn World Titles in three separate federations. In NFW, I might just make it four. I've held wrestling's triple crown, the NCW, FoPEW, and CDWF World Championships. Nobody can say the same thing. But in the process of doing all that, I forgot one little thing. I'd become obsessed with winning World Titles. But then, I got to bring big Mark back around. And suddenly, it was all clear to me. Mark Engel, I don't want your title. West: Excuse me? Knight: I said I don't want Mark Engel's title belt. I don't even want his tag team title. West: Well, then... Knight: I want his soul. West: What?! Knight: You heard me, Dan. I want his soul. In my quest to protect the World title in my stable, as well as capture belts on my own, I forgot how much fun it was to destroy somone's mind. Oh sure, I was playing mindgames with Dynamite, but not to the degree I'd done it in the past, or the degree I should have been doing it. When I told everyone that the PC only had four members, it was because I had a plan. Everyone wanted to know what the hell I could have possibly been talking about, and then when it happened, everyone was in shock. But that's nothing compared to what I'm going to do to you, Mark. Just wait. And if your tag-along partner wants a piece of me, he's more than willing to fail just like the rest of them. West: So, the World Title no longer matters to you? Knight: Oh, it matters. Only now, it's part of the bargain, not the only thing on the table. Engel, I surprised you when Big Mark showed up. You weren't prepared for him. You might have won the match, but you'll have scars for the rest of your life. And I don't mean physical ones, either. Remember what I went through with The Black Scorpion, Mark? You took a bribe and wore a mask for one night. Well, you proved that anyone could be bought. Look at your friends, Mark. Chris Taylor? Stood with me when he had to. Amazin Jason? Took the cheap route out of town, and turned against the fans in the process. Quigley? The man's a self-professed loner. He'd turn on you if he thought it would get him somewhere. Chris Thomas? If Bret Hart could turn, then so could he. There's a reason why they call him a "Hitman". Dynamite? He might respect, but that doesn't mean you can trust him. Take a good close look at your friends, Mark. I could have any last one of them attack you... in a locker room, in the ring... at your home... at your wife's family reunion... there are no limits, Mark. Feeling comfortable, Mark? No. Probably not. Get used to it. I learned a long time ago that there is no honor amongst thieves. I know that guys like Verhoeven, or Black, or even Byron could come right after me. They're welcome to try. But you, Mark, you're different. You only expect guys like Black or Verhoeven to come after you. Only the biggest and the best. Foolish mistake. I'm ready to show you all about your "friends". I may not like them, but I know for a fact that they see you as a target. You're the World Champion of FWLI, Mark. It puts you at a very interesting position. Defend against those the federation deems worthy, or sign an open contract, and expect to see one of your best "friends" standing across the ring from you. Engel, it begins now. It ends with your soul. --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI World Heavyweight Champion Mark "The Executioner" Engel, along with "Maniac" Melanie Engel. Taped Thursday 01/30/97: [The scene is the ocean along the Jersey Shore. The camera shows a close up of waves crashing on the shore. The camera zooms out, and laughing starts to be heard over the sound of the waves. As the camera pans out, the laughter gets louder. Then the camera pans out to show the boardwalk in Long Branch. Mark "The Executioner" Engel is standing along the railing watching the ocean, laughing out loud. Melanie is beside him.] MARK: [laughing] So, Knight wants my soul. And he's gonna start playing mind games in order to get it. Mind games... [Mark suddenly stops laughing, and turns toward the camera, a sinister smile on his face.] MARK: This whole business has been MY mind game all along! Ever since the World Championship Tournament last April, Knight, I'm the one who's been playing with YOUR mind! I've been calling the shots all along. You think I didn't know you'd ally with Nature Boy to keep me from winning the title? I was counting on it. I wanted to show the whole wrestling world that you needed buddies to protect you. Our match at Battlecry... you wouldn't have wanted a match with me. I MADE YOU want that match, and I pretended to be such a babyface that I got you to not take it seriously. I WANTED you to show your true colors by getting Nature Boy to save your a*s when you were about to get pinned. I didn't care about your belt; I wanted to expose your soul. The Canadian Invitational... I set you and the whole Awesome Alliance up. Newkirk came down and put me out of the tournament... played right into my hands. I knew Todd would get so frustrated with you that he'd leave you behind in that match with me. I knew that Chris would get so frustrated with you that he wouldn't interfere with me. HARDCORE... the crowning jewel. I knew you'd dump Todd and hire a killer-type... I was counting on it. I even had an inkling you'd bring back Big Mark D., although I have to wonder how much money you offered him to come back and face the guy he ran from the first time. I managed to get you to alienate half the Awesome Allliance, and I STILL ended up with the World Heavyweight Championship. How does it feel, Knight? Knowing that for nearly a year you'd thought you were playing games with me, and now you realize you were the object of the biggest mind game of all. You're in the minor leagues of mind games next to The Executioner. It ends when I end it, not you. You want to scare me... can't be done. If Big Mark D and NUKE couldn't scare me, you can't. You want to try and intimidate me... can't be done. Your whole Awesome Alliance couldn't do it, DCI couldn't do it. I'll defend this belt against you or anyone else who signs a match, friend or enemy, and never think twice. I AM the World Heavyweight Champion. And may the Lord have mercy on the souls of ALL my future opponents... because The Executioner WON'T!!! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
