Taped Wednesday 01/15/97: [FADE IN to the FWLI studio. JEAN-PIERRE VACHON is seated behind a desk. There are two pieces of paper in front of him.] VACHON: My, my, my. The FWLI Television Championship is fast becoming the hottest singles title race in FWLI. Not only Lord Byron, but American Enforcer, some guy calling himself a Genius and young Mister Atkinson are making noises about going after my belt. Well, let's start from the lowest to the highest. I've already proven to the world that you're no "living legend," Atkinson. You and your lady ain't destined for gold just yet, my friend. First you're going to have to wipe out the shame of losing by submission in the fastest time ever recorded in FWLI for a submission. I think it was "Flamboyant" Tom Taylor who lost a title match even faster than you - and he was bribed to take a pin. You, Atkinson? You thought you were the baddest man in FWLI, but Christmas Chaos proved you differently. But look on the bright side, Atkinson. You shouldn't lack for matches anymore. Every newcomer in FWLI, every wrestler on a losing streak, every man in a slump - they're gonna be banging on your door now, in search of their first recorded WIN in the league. And judging from the way you handled your first - and ONLY - Television Title shot, you're just the man to give it to 'em. In fact, I expect one of'em to be this Genius guy. Him and his Dangerous Alliance - you ever get the feeling that they just don't get it? Yelling and screaming doesn't get you matches. Acts of senseless violence doesn't get you matches. Heck, not even death threats get you matches. No - it's respect, and reputation, and sheer force of personality that gets people to the top in FWLI. Genius, you and your Alliance right now have the reputation of being whiners. And what you seem to forget is that title opportunities are privileges, not rights. Better smarten up your act, boys, if you want a chance at this belt. Now American Enforcer, he ain't no whiner, but I wonder if he's really thinking. You've been going after Brian Knight, and now all of a sudden you think you want to take ME on? Enforcer, I didn't get to be the longest running champion in FWLI because I was a pushover. I didn't win five matches in a row because I was easier to handle than the Iceman. You're a legit contender for a belt, so if you want a shot, of course you got it, but I'd suggest you'd take a little more time to think about just who it is you're planning on fighting. And now, Lord Byron. The number 4 contender for the TV belt, now that T'Challa's gone. [points to the two pieces of paper] You see these? They're contracts for the FWLI Television Championship. And one of them will have your name on it. Now this one here [holds up one] is your standard contract, just like the one you asked for. No clauses or stipulations or other gimmicks, just your standard title defence. And this one [holds up the other] is just filled with gimmicks and stipulations - but before you make your choice, here's the stipulations on this one: If I get disqualified, you get the belt. If I get counted out, you get the belt. If I'm so badly injured that I can't continue, you get the belt. No one else at ringside - if there's outside interference, match is declared a draw, with an automatic rematch at another FWLI show. Standard time limit, time limit draw is an automatic rematch. Naturally, I pin you, or make you submit, or knock you out, I stay the Champion and keep the belt - but if you lose by disqualification or countout, you don't get another shot at the TV title. This is what I call the "NO EXCUSES" match - because neither side will have an excuse for losing. No "I was the winner, but the champ got counted out so I don't get the belt." No "if the champ hadn't been disqualified I woulda taken it." No "the only reason I didn't get the belt was because I beat him so bad we couldn't finish the match." And no "oh well, there's always next time." Now, why do I think that Lord Byron will prefer the "No Excuses" match? Because this one takes out all the loopholes an FWLI champion has at his disposal for keeping the belt. Which is fine by me; I don't plan on using 'em - and if Byron's the ring technician he says he is, he won't miss 'em. Nowhere to hide, no place to back up - just like the way I plan to fight. And one thing I want to point out to all of you here - I have never fought a match where my opponent didn't think he could win. The "five-minute" match, the "submissions-only" match, the "crossroads/retirement" match - all of my opponents thought they could win. The rules never stacked the odds in my favor, which is why the FWLI Championship Committee approved them. It's only after the bell rings that they all realize that they had underestimated Jean-Pierre Vachon. Just like what Lord Byron's doing right now. Well, Byron, the ball's in your court now. [holds out the two pieces of paper] Choose your match - and get ready for a fight that'll be the stuff - of LEGEND.
