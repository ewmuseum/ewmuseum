The camera zooms in on him, and we can see that it's Mike Bowie] BOWIE: Welcome fans to the Halifax Forum, in Halifax. Nova Scotia, Canada. In just a few days, you'll be seeing the greatest event ever to hit the Forum, the first ever FWLI Canadian Invitational Tournament! Here, one man will have the glory of having his hand raised in victory, while seven others walk away in defeat. Let's take a look at the brackets for this tournament, because this thing promises to be HOT! Chris "Hitman" Thomas ------------------------------\ "Iron Man" Chris Andrews ----------\ ------------------------------/ \ "Iceman" Brian Knight ----------\ ------------------------------\ / \ "Desirable" Danny Dynamite ----------/ \ ------------------------------/ \ WINNER "Flagman" Joe Melillo ---------- ------------------------------\ / Vic "The Rebel" Black ----------\ / ------------------------------/ \ / Mark "The Executioner" Engel ----------/ ------------------------------\ / "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor ----------/ ------------------------------/ BOWIE: As you can see, in the first round, Chris "Hit Man" Thomas will square off against his own brother in law, Chris "Iron Man" Andrews!! Let's take you to the Hit Man, to get his thoughts on the match. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- [Cut to a studio. Hit Man is wearing his ring attire.] So...a Canadian Invitational Tournament, eh? Well, everybody knows the Hit Man loves Canada, and Canada loves the Hit Man. I'd be honoured to compete in this tournament, and I WILL win it. My first round opponent is Chris Andrews. My brother in law. Finally, I have a chance to get my revenge on him for what he has done. Turning my own sister against me wasn't enough for him, he had to stick his nose in my match against Greased Lightning, costing me the FWLI Lightheavyweight Title. Well, "Iron Man", what goes around comes around, and tonight, you will get yours. And who will be the next to fall? Danny Dynamite or Brian Knight? It doesnt matter either way, whoever it is will go down. And then it's on to the finals, where I may have to go up against Mark Engel, the Rebel, Chris Taylor, or even my own Lion Pride teammate, Joe Melillo. Well, whoever it is, and this goes for you too, Joe, I'm going to do whatever it takes to win!! And when it all clears, only one man will be standing in the ring, and he will be the first ever winner of the Canadian Invitational Tournament. And he will be Chris "Hit Man" Thomas. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- BOWIE: Wow!! Chris Thomas really seems focused on this tournament!! I don't know if I'd consider him the favourite though, with so many great competitors, it's hard to pick a favourite!! But if you had to, it would almost certainly be one of these two men: ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- [Daniel West is interviewing Mark "The Executioner" Engel. FWLI Women's Champion "Maniac" Melanie Engel is by his side.] WEST: Mark, you're going to be participating in the first ever 8-Man FWLI Canadian Invitational Tournament. Your thoughts? MARK: West, the Executioner ALWAYS enjoys a challenge! I proved at the Heavyweight Championship Tournament that I can wrestle three matches in one night and still give my best all the way through. The Executioner is on top of his game right now, and at the Canadian Invitational, I'm gonna prove it by going all the way. MELANIE: And I'll be right by his side. Mark will whip the competition in the ring, and I'll whip anyone who tries outside interference. [pulls her whip off her belt and coils it to show the camera] WEST: In looking at the tournament bracket, you've got none other than "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor in the first round. No doubt he'll be looking for revenge from that loss to you from the last time you two met. MARK: No doubt, as well as try and intercept me on my path to the "Nature Boy". Taylor, you made the biggest mistake of your career by signing on as an enforcer stooge for the "Awful" Alliance, when you could easily rise to the top standing on your own two feet. And now you say that anyone who wants a shot at Newkirk has to go through you first... no problem. I went through Knight already, and I've pinned you once before. You want to bring the rest of your new buddies to ringside, go ahead. Remember, a DQ puts you out of the tournament and gives me a win just like a regular victory. WEST: Well, it looks like the Executioner is ready to kick butt in Canada. MARK: Taylor, and the rest of you in the tournament... prepare to meet your Executioner. And may the Lord have mercy on your souls... [draws his hand across his throat] ... because the Executioner WON'T!!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ["Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor is practicing in his private gym in Starville, Mississippi. He is working over a heavybag with various martial arts strikes and kicks.] HSCT: *whew* So, I've been invited to compete in an 8-Man Invitational Elimination Tournament with Engel, Black, Melillo, Dynamite, Andrews, Knight, and Thomas. The FWLI Canandian Invitational Tournament, huh? Guess that makes Thomas the hometown favorite. Okay, fine. I've never turned down a challenge from anyone, and I'm not about to start now, but I don't particularly care about what the fans think. I don't have any grief with any of these guys unless they start messing with me or the "Nature Boy"! If I wind up facing him, fine. Me and Todd wrestle all the time, but if anyone else does, they'd better keep it clean, or their gonna get BURNED! Engel, it looks like you and I drew each other first. Get ready! It's gonna be a HOT time in Canada! WWWWHHHHOOOOO!!!! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- BOWIE: Well, I'd say both of these guys are ready, wouldn't you? They're going to have to be ready because they will face each other in the first round. What an epic matchup that's going to be!!! We also had the chance to catch up with two other men who will be facing each other in the first round, so let's here what "Flagman" Joe Melillo, and the Rebel have to say. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- (From the Lion's Den in Calgary, "Flagman" Joe Melillo reflects on his upcoming participation in the Canadian Invitational Tournament. I'll tell the world when I go on the HotSeat with one Mr. Taylor. Then, the world will see who I've selected to join the elite. West:Very well, and speaking of Taylor, that brings up the AA, and well one Mr. Brian Knight. (crowd boos) DDD: Ice Cream Man.. But it you want to talk intense, the first name that comes to most people's minds is Brian Knight. Earlier on, I said he was controversial, and he proved it by holding this Press Conference, recently after the anouncement of the Tournament. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Scene: The Aspen Extreme Lodge in Aspen Colorado. A large room is filled with press people, as the front has a large podium in the center.) HEART: In a strange case of deja vu, the press corp has been called to the home of FWLI European Champion, "Iceman" Brian Knight. We have no idea what this is about, but anything is possible with the leader of The Awesome Alliance. Here he comes now. (Knight makes his way to the podium wearing a black suit, white shirt, and black tie. His hair is pulled back in a ponytail.) KNIGHT:I'd like to thank you for coming here today. I'm going to make this as brief as I can. When I first came to the FWLI, then RSPCW, I was brought in to be a loner fan favorite. The company decided to stick me with INTENSITY, and call us Fire and Ice. I didn't like it, and I told people about it. When I was put into that stupid Black Scorpion angle, I told people how much I hated it. When they decided that I needed a new look, and decided that "Hot Shot" was a good one, I told everyone how much I hated the gimmick. It was simply out of contract that I did what I was told. When my one year exclusive contract ended, I was offered a deal, which I re-signed with. Now, the FWLI is going against it's word. Despite my having control about what contracts I sign, I have been placed in a tournament, allegedly calling itself an "invitational". Despite not being told about it prior, I was not only placed into this tournament, but also bracketed in it. Regardless of who my opponent was in this match, I have a contract. And it says that I don't have to compete unless I want to. This tournament I feel is a farce, and I'm sticking to my contract, which says that I DO NOT have to compete. There will be no questions answered. Good day. (Knight walks off the stage.) HEART: Oh, Christ! Fisher got fined for being involved in one of these damn things. Now I'm in shit. Cut that camera off! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- BOWIE: Can you believe the things Knight said in that interview?? An unpopular decision made by Knight, but not one the fans would have to live with. FWLI Commisioner Jason Dean later made an anouncement where he gave Knight a choice: Compete in the tournament, or leave the FWLI. Well, I think we all can guess what Knight chose. Fans, this tournament is going to be amazing, and it happens in just a few days. I'm Mike Bowie, and I'll see YOU in Canada!!!!
