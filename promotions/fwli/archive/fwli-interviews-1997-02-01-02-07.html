Interviews, 1-7 February 1997

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI European Tag-Team Champions The Prophets of Rage (Derek & Shadoe Rage), along with Pizzazz. Taped Monday 02/03/97: [Fade in: The Prophets of Rage are seated around their lush dining table. The European championship belts sit on the table with them. Derek puts his feet up on his belt, grinding his heel into it.] DEREK: How damn long do we got to wait? That's all I'm asking you suits up in the FWLI towers. What's the problem, boys? You can't find anybody bad enough to get in the ring and fight with us? I'm not surprised. Look, we've taken out Heavy Metal, The Soviet Front, the Universal Powers, Total Package, the Horrors, the Lords of the Ring, GWR and beat up Damage Inc. so bad they lost their titles to the Players Club. And how do you reward a team that's never been pinned? You keep them out of the real matches, protecting your real big money earning teams. What's the matter? We don't sell enough toys? We don't sell enough crap to the little kids at ringside? What? Nobody wants to face us? That's all well and good. We've always been outsiders, but I guarantee you I'm going to do something about this that nobody will like. SHADOE: Freak out! Freak out! This is what it's all about, isn't it? You've got the greatest performers. The greatest competitors ever in your ranks and you're trying to suffocate us, aren't you? Autrey, Dean, I assure you it ain't gonna work. There's only so much you can do to ignore us and then we get vexed. And when we get vexed bad things happen. Very bad things. See, somebody's going to get hurt. And somebody's going to get hurt bad. So, this is your one final warning... get us some competition... OR ELSE! [Fade out.] --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI World TV Champion "Living Legend" Jean-Pierre Vachon. Taped Monday 02/03/97: [FADE IN to a training gym. JEAN-PIERRE VACHON is doing leg-lifts on a weight machine; the weights being moved are VERY heavy.] VACHON: [occasional puffing] Y'know, it can be pretty funny sometimes, watching someone make a fool of himself on national TV. Like Lord Byron the other day. Who'd'a thought with one interview he'd prove each and every point I've been trying to make? [He sits up. PAN with JPV as he moves to another part of the machine, this time working on bench presses.] VACHON: First, he's already started making excuses. "If I won under those rules people would say I had the belt handed to me on a silver platter." [snorts] They don't have silver platters in FWLI, Byron. Never did. Never will. They never gave me the Television Belt on a silver platter, and I'm sure not gonna give it away. Oh, and an interesting crack you made about my title reign, Byron. You say I should've lost this belt a long time ago? Tell me something, mister, who should've won it? Still, though, your little crack tells me you've made a mistake. The same mistake Dan Kaufman made, the same one Haron Atkinson made, the same one all my opponents so far have made about me. They've never been able to believe I've held the belt for so long by sheer wrestling skill. They think instead, "he cheated," or "he bent the rules in his favor." And once they start believing that, they do either one of two things. They start to underestimate me, or they start to overestimate themselves. Like you, Byron. Just like you. [He lets go of the bar and sits up, looking at the camera.] VACHON: They call it confidence. Some fans call it arrogance. I call it an delusion, and one I have no compunctions about shattering. [He stands up, picks up a towel, and begins to rub himself down. PAN with JPV as he walks along the gym floor.] VACHON: And I can tell it's a delusion, man. I mean, you seem to think that my opponents' big mistake was wrestling on MY turf, by MY rules. Fine. Ignore the fact that Dan Kauffman, Ryan Rankin and Brody Thunder challenged me for the gold under normal house rules, just like what you're planning. Ignore the fact that I beat all of them cleanly, no DQs, no countouts. You can even ignore the fact that Jean-Luc Nancett was the one who challenged me with the Crossroads Match, not the other way around, or that Haron Atkinson made the claim that he could beat me in five minutes. Ignore all that, well, you're going to anyway. One thing I've noticed is that you don't seem to have much respect for everyone else in FWLI. I'll have to rectify that. But one thing you can't ignore, and in a sense, you're right on the money. All of those opponents were wrestling on MY turf-... [He pauses by a regulation-size wrestling ring.] ... because THIS is my turf. THIS is my home. THIS is where Jean-Pierre Vachon became "The Living Legend." And THIS is the reality that you're going to face. You see, Byron, you don't gain an advantage over me by insisting on "standard rules." What you've done is pick a battleground that I know like nowhere else on Earth. You may think you're on your home ground, but you're treading a path that I've already blazed and never forgotten. And that means you've got a lot more in common with my previous opponents than you'd care to admit. But I guess that's how fear can blind you. [Slowly move to CLOSE-UP on JPV's face.] VACHON: That's right, Byron. Fear. A fear you can't admit to. A fear so subtle and insidious you won't even recognize it until it glares at you eye-to-eye. A fear that everyone who saw your last interview can see, but you can't, because you're too close to it. A fear of me? [smiles] Hardly. I'm not a Halloween goblin. No, Byron. Your fear is one you share with Haron Atkinson, and every other wrestler in FWLI who claims to be the best at what they do. The fear... is the fear of losing. Y'see, in a sense, you've got more riding on this match than I do. If I lose, all that happens is that I'm not a champion anymore. I just end a winning streak. I never claimed to be unbeatable, and I'm not claiming it now. But you, Byron? Your claim to be the best technician in FWLI. Your perfect record. Your reputation. This "aura of invincibility" that you seem to want to build up. And yes... your ego. All of that goes down the tubes if you don't win the gold. [Slowly ZOOM OUT to full body shot.] VACHON: That's the fear. You can ignore it, Byron, pretend it's not there, just like you're doing right now. Do like a lot of wrestlers and say, "That fear is nothing, because I never lose." But one thing I've learned in my career... people who believe they're invincible have problems winning the game, because they don't understand their opponents. Just like what you're doing now. Think you can't underestimate me? You already have, Byron. You already have. Better prepare yourself, man. 'Cause when we meet in the ring, you'll be in for the fight of your life. I know someday I may lose the TV belt, but it won't be to YOU, man. When you leave the match, you won't have the championship. You won't have the perfect record. The only thing you'll have is respect... respect for the abilities of "The Living Legend." Get ready, Byron. You're gonna be doin' some hard time. [FADE OUT.] --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI World Heavyweight Champion Mark "The Executioner" Engel, along with "Maniac" Melanie Engel. Taped Thursday 02/06/97: [The interview takes place in the FWLI Studios. Daniel West is sitting opposite Mark and Melanie. Mark and Melanie are dressed in casual clothes, and Mark has the FWLI World Heavyweight belt slung over his shoulder.] WEST: We're here with the FWLI World Heavyweight Champion Mark "The Executioner" Engel and his wife "Maniac" Melanie, a former Women's Champion. Mark, FWLI has been in transition since HARDCORE, with new front office leadership. So things have actually been a little quieter since after you won the title. Your thoughts on that. MARK: Well, things haven't been totally quiet. I'm still defending the belt and making promotional appearances. As for the new leadership, Mr. Autrey has been in charge of FWLI operations before, and I never had a quarrel with him or his staff. I look forward to the direction FWLI will go, and especially being in the forefront of it all. WEST: On the subject of title defenses... do you feel that you still haved unfinished business with the Awesome Alliance, or do you have bigger and better worlds to conquer? MARK: West, in my opinion the Awesome Alliance is finished. Over. I beat their leader at Battlecry, and I beat their champion at the Canadian Invitational. At HARDCORE, Brian Knight admitted that no one in the Awesome Alliance could beat me by hiring Big Mark D to try and take me out, and I beat him too. Knight started the Awesome Alliance out of a motivation to control the World Championship and beat me down. Well, I've got the belt and I'm still standing tall, while Knight and his friends have fallen. My determination overcame his mind games, and that's the way it will be with all my opponents. WEST: So you're looking to new opposition. Anyone in particular? MARK: Well, as champion, I have to be ready for any opposition. I learned that at HARDCORE. [smiles] I've already got a match signed up for FWLI Showcase against Samson Stargill. He's the one carrying the "Samson" legacy now, and we all know how dangerous he can be in a title match. I was impressed by Otto Verhoeven defeating NUKE, and I'm sure that puts him in the list of contenders. Knowing my history with butchers, I'm sure that will be an interesting encounter. Also, Vic Black and Brian Knight, being continental champions, are top contenders for the World Title, as is TV champ Jean-Pierre Vachon. I welcome the challenge to lock up with any of them... well, even Knight, if he somehow still thinks he can beat me. I'm here to wrestle; always have been and always will be. WEST: Now, Melanie, how do you feel about your husband being on top? MELANIE: Well, that's kind of personal... OH, you mean the FWLI Championship! [giggles] It's great. I've been Women's Champion before, so I know how it feels. I'm proud of him, and I knew he could do it all along if he was just given the opportunity. WEST: Do you have plans to take back the Women's Championship? MELANIE: Absolutely. That Dumbbell Rage is gonna have to give me a rematch, and this time no one will interfere. I will get my belt back, and we will be the undisputed First Family of Wrestling. WEST: Speaking of family, any next-generation wrestlers on the way? MELANIE: [blushing and giggling] No, nothing now. But as a well-known broadcaster is fond of saying, anything can happen... WEST: Well, Mark and Melanie, we look forward to seeing both of you in and out of the ring. Thanks for your time. For FWLI, this is Daniel West. --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI European Tag-Team Champions The Prophets of Rage (Shadoe & Derek Rage), along with Pizzazz.
