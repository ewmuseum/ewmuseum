[FWLI?] The Banned North American Tag Title Match

27 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As many fans of the FWLI know, GWR was awarded the FWLI North American Tag Team Titles over the team of the Untouchables. However, a hastily thrown together voiceover was shown in its place, stating that for various reasons, the match could not be aired. I have learned that the match was, in fact, written. However, this writer apparently tried to pull a fast one over on the organization, and wrote a match that was clearly inappropriate. Rather than be bullied into accepting this match, or holding back on what was already a late card, Mr. Autrey decided to acknowledge the title change, and simply not allow the match to be aired. So that the nature of this controversy can be understood, I am posting the final few minutes of this match. This may well get me kicked out of the FWLI, but I think that the fans deserve to have the chance to judge for themselves whether or not they should be subjected to this: --------------- BOWIE: GWR looking to finish things off here. HEART: I would hope so! They've been beating on Quigley for over a half an hour now! BOWIE: Spoiler winds up for the Frankensteiner! Quigley off the ropes, and...OH NO! HEART: QUIGLEY PUNCHED SPOILER RIGHT IN THE NUTS! THINGS ARE GETTING A LITTLE OUT OF HAND! BOWIE: A desperation move, but it seems to have worked! Both men trying to make the tag...Spoiler tags in Loco...AND QUIGLEY MAKES THE HOT TAG TO MARK ENGEL! HEART: WHOA DOGGIE, THEY BE CLUBBERIN NOW! BOWIE: Engel all over Loco like a house on fire! Engel whips Loco, but Loco blocks, reversed again by Engel, AND LOCO PLOWS INTO THE REFEREE! HEART: DOWN! REFEREE DOWN! BOWIE: ENGEL IN FROM BEHIND...FULL NELSON...WHIPPER SNAPPER! BUT NO REFEREE TO COUNT! GWR IS...WAIT A MINUTE! WHAT IS THIS!? HEART: THAT'S...THAT'S...IT CAN'T BE!? BOWIE: MARK DESTRUCTO, BRODY THUNDER, AND SOME NATIVE AMERICAN INDIAN HAVE HIT THE RINGSIDE AREA, AND THEY ARE DESTROYING EVERYONE IN SIGHT! GENERAL KANE, CARNAGE, EVEN MANIAC MELANIE HAVE BEEN AMBUSHED! HEART: GWR GOING OUT TO TRY AND HELP...BUT THEY GO DOWN AS WELL! BOWIE: ENGEL COMING OUT AS WELL...BUT HERE COMES SOMEONE ELSE...SWEET JESUS' MOTHER IN LAW, IT'S THE HEARTBREAK KID! HEART: JOE PETROW IS HERE TOO!? HE JUST LAID OUT ENGEL WITH A BULLET TRAIN TO HELL ON THE FLOOR! THAT MOVE SHOULDN'T HAVE BEEN TRIED! BOWIE: EVERYBODY IS OUT! I HAVE NEVER SEEN ANYTHING LIKE THIS! HEART: THESE THUGS HAVE JUST THROWN ENGEL AND LOCO BACK INTO THE RING! PETROW COMES IN...AND PUTS LOCO ON TOP OF ENGEL! BOWIE: And the referee is starting to come to! HEART: Now? Where's Quigley? BOWIE: Yeah, where is he? HEART: Nowhere to be found, as the referee covers...1...2...3! NEW FWLI NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS! AND HERE COMES THE CAVALRY AGAIN! BOWIE: HAVEN'T THEY DONE ENOUGH!? NOW LOCO, ENGEL, AND THE REFEREE GET SENT OVER THE TOP ROPE! THIS IS TERRIBLE! HEART: BRODY THUNDER IS HANDING PETROW SOME BLACK CLOTH...IT'S A SHIRT! BOWIE: AND NOW...WHAT THE!?!? [The camera pans back to the broadcast table...to show the announcers being attacked!...by former broadcasters Scoop and Ediie "The Fisher" Gibbons. They take the headsets and take over commentary duties.] FISHER:LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, YOU NOW HAVE A NEW BROADCASTING TEAM! WHAT A GLORIOUS SIGHT WE ARE WITNESSING TONIGHT! SCOOP: FOR ONCE EDDIE, I'M INCLINED TO AGREE WITH YOU! PETROW STANDING IN THE MIDDLE OF THE RING WITH THAT SHIRT, AND DESTRUCTO JUST HANDED PETROW THE HOUSE MIC! FISHER: LET'S HEAR WHAT THE WHAT THE _MAN_ HAS TO SAY! PETROW: Ladies and gentlemen, you've just been treated to an incredible display by four of the greatest athletes in our sport today! The crap out on the floor there [points to all of the wrestlers] represents the FWLI, the kind of wrestlers that the management types like, while the real talent gets passed up and screwed over! Well, we ain't gonna take it lying down anymore! [Petrow puts on the shirt, displaying the letters sWo] The Screwed World Order, the real talent of the wrestling world, is BACK to STAY! And Autrey...if you don't like it...you KNOW where to stick it, brother! [Fans pelt the ring with tons of debris, as the sWo flexes in the ring.] SCOOP: Well fans, I hope you set the VCRs for this one, because this is a moment unlike any other in the history of wrestling, and something we may not see again for a long time to come! FISHER:It brings a tear to my eye. This is incredible! SCOOP: Well, that's all the time we...wait just a minute! [The crowd pops, as Chris Quigley walks down the aisle, menacingly waving a bowling pin. He stops halfway down and stares at the ring.] FISHER:IT'S CHRIS QUIGLEY! IS HE SWO TOO? SCOOP: I DON'T KNOW, BUT WE ARE OUT OF TIME! TUNE IN NEXT WEEK TO SEE HOW THIS TURNS OUT! --------------- The preceding piece of fiction was written entirely by Joe Petrow, without the permission of the FWLI, or the handlers of wrestlers Mark Engel, Chris Quigley, G.W.R., Mark Destructo, Brody Thunder, any native Americans, or any other person portrayed in this work of fiction, and has no bearing on any current FWLI scenario whatsoever.
