FANTASY WRESLING LEAGUE INTERNATIONAL PRESENTS F W L I T U R N B U C K L E T A L K ******************************************************************************* J A N U A R Y 1 8 , 1 9 9 6 ******************************************************************************* Good evening, FWLI fans, and welcome to another edition of TURNBUCKLE TALK where you get to hear what's on the minds (or lack thereof) of the wrestlers in FWLI. TURNBUCKLE TALK is broadcast periodically with interviews collected since the last edition. Interviews can be read as soon as they arrive by going to FWLI's Home Page on the World Wide Web at: http://www.exit109.com/~marke/FWLI.html At our Home Page, you can also view: - The latest rankings - The current roll-call of champions - Past FWLI Pay-Per-Views and TV Broadcasts - "The Dean's List": The latest "inside scoops" on FWLI - The names and e-mail addresses of the demented maniacs (including myself) who run FWLI - How to join FWLI, including a "bio" form also usable for WHO'S WHO ******************************************************************************* WINTER WAR: NEW YEAR'S REVOLUTION is coming January 24. The Main Event: A 60-man, two-ring, Royal Street Fight, featuring wrestlers from the FWLI and NCWF. One pair of wrestlers starts off in each ring, then every two minutes a new wrestler enters each ring. Anything goes. The winner will be crowned as the 1996 Winter War Champion. Also on the card: - Mark Destructo vs. "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk for the FWLI World Heavyweight Championship. - The Slammasters vs. The Lords of the Ring for the FWLI World Tag-Team Championship. - "Black Scorpion" Alexander Webb vs. Samson Stargill for the FWLI U.S. Heavyweight Championship - CDWF World Light Heavyweight Champion Shawn Masters will defend his title against a yet-unnamed opponent - "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor vs. The Rebel in a "Rebel Rage Street Fight See the FWLI Home Page for more details. ******************************************************************************* Interview with The Rebel. Taped Wednesday 01/10/96: [Scene: A cemetery in Memphis, Tennessee. It is a cemetery well known to wrestling fans. It is the cemetery where Eddie Gilbert was laid to rest last year. The Rebel, looking very serious in a black leather suit, turns to the camera. He is holding an unopened bottle of Jack Daniels in his hand.] REBEL: Well, the Rebel is here in Memphis, Tennessee on my way to the first Winter War, where as always, the world will see that I am the Baddest Man in the FWLI! I nearly didn't make it out of Brooklyn, but J.R. strapped Giant BOB to the 4 X 4 and we made the last flight out! BOB makes a damn good snowplow! Memphis, is the home of the second baddest dead man in the USA, Elvis! Oh, and to all you psycho K-Mart shoppers out there watching...he's DEAD! And here is the first... [points to Gilbert's gravesite] "Hot Stuff" Eddie Gilbert... Hmmmm, you know, there's someone in the FWLI who actually thinks he can compare himself to Eddie over here. CHRIS TAYLOR, let's do a little side by side comparison, shall we? 1. Chick Magnet... Eddie was the man... he had all the women, and when he realized how stupid he was to marry both Missy Hyatt and Madusa Miceli, HE DITCHED THEM!!! Taylor, you're stuck with Blaze... and we all know her and what she has "done" for your career. Nuff said. Eddie 1/Taylor 0. 2. Party Animal... Eddie Gilbert took more illegal substances then all of WCW combined, and he lived life in the fast lane. Taylor... your idea of a party is milk and cookies and bed by 9. Score 2-0. 3. Finally as a wrestler... I have to give SOME credit, Taylor. You're good. But one thing Gilbert had that your sure as hell don't is courage. Gilbert NEVER backed down from a challenge. I'm still waiting to beat you up since July 1994. And at Winter War, you've got nowhere to hide!! You'll be in MY type of fight, Taylor!! There's a REASON they call it a REBEL Rage Street-Fight Match, pal!! Eddie Gilbert would've had a shot at beating me, but you?? Gilbert wins, hands down!!! [Rebel puts his ear to the ground.] REBEL: What, Eddie? Oh yeah, that's spinning I hear. I'll think you'll be needing this... [Rebel opens the bottle and pours it on the ground.] REBEL: Cheers.... TAYLOR, I'M COMING FOR YOU!!!!! [Fade Out] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with Chris "Hit-Man" Thomas. Taped Wednesday, January 10, 1996: [The scene is the Lion's Den, where a big Lion Pride logo is painted on the wall in the background. There are also several logos, and the camera zooms in on the one that reads...] ___|___ _ _ _ ___ / __|__ \ ___ ___ ____ ___ ___ | |_| | | | |_ _| / / | \ \ | \/ | / \ | \| | CHRIS | _ | | | | | -+--|--+--|--+- | | | () | | | THOMAS |_| |_| |_| |_| \ \__|__/ / |_|\/|_| |_/\_| |_|\__| \___|___/ | [Camera zooms in on the Hit Man's face] I just got a notice from the top office of the FWLI, with some updates on the Winter War. I took a look at the 60 competitors that are going to be involved in the 60 Man Battle Royal, and nowhere on the list did I see the Hit Man's name!!! Well, I'm going up to the front offices, and I'm looking to get into this match, to prove to everyone that I am what I say, the best there is, the best there was, and the best there ever will be!!! The FWLI seems to be taking a new, more agressive style lately, with Jason Dean being named the new Head Commisioner. Well, the Hit Man has also started down a new, more agressive path as of late. I'm going to do everything in my power to rise up the rankings, and prove I deserve a shot at the Lightheavyweight Title which I never lost fairly!! And speaking of Lightweight Titles, the CDWF Lightweight Title is also going to be defended at Winter War. Well, I haven't heard the specifics yet, but I do know that the CDWF will be naming the number one contender to that title in some kind of match. Rest assured, I intend to win that match, and leave Winter War the NEW CDWF Lightweight Champion. I'll become the first 3 Time FWLI Lightheavyweight Champion, and the first man to hold not only the FWLI Lightweight Title, but also the CDWF one. I've said it before, and I'll say it again: 1996 is going to be the year of the Hit Man!!!! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with The Prophets of Rage (Derek Rage & Shadoe Rage) and manager Pizzazz. The camera pans up to reveal Pizzazz standing on their backs, puffing on her long slim cigarette.] PIZZAZZ [as the Rages grind out a repetition]: Sept. Huit. Neuf. Et dix! The Prophets of Rage are comin'. SHADOE [off camera]: Yeah, all you tag teams out there brace for sudden impact. [Rage sticks his face into the camera.] Because we're comin', we're comin' rugged and raw. We're angry. We're furious. The Prophets of Rage are gonna push ourselves over the top. Sit up. Take notice. DEREK: We start with the first step. Universal Powers, we take you out. Then you'll know our names. Total Package, you're step two. We'll show you why you never did and never will win a title. SHADOE: And then the Texas Rangers. See, once we take you two cowboys out the promoters will have to recognise us as a force. And then, Sudden Impact, we take them out, too. Then we should get a title shot. The promoters won't have a choice. They'll have to recognise our painful progression to the top. DEREK: It'll be perfectly painful, all right. Because we'll be perfect and you'll be in pain. Remember, this is the age of the Prophets of Rage. SHADOE: Rough, rugged and raw. We're gonna murder everybody! Taped Saturday 01/13/96: [Setting: An introduction screen appears with a music background showing "Superstar" Richard Dean easily performing moves in a gym against a typical jobber. Then, the screen switches to a background with a star logo and Dean begins to speak.] What you people have just witnessed is just some of the many talents of myself, "Superstar" Richard Dean, the one TRUE superstar (laughs with a confident smile). I am coming to the FWLI for two simple reasons. One is to show off my vast array of talent and the second is to become the HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION. Why did I pick the FWLI? Simple, it is the best showcase of talent in the world. I am extending an open contract out at Winter War to anybody worthy enough to face me there. You see there is one simple fact that everyone in the FWLI will soon learn, nobody has the talent, the looks, or the ring smarts that I posess. Soon, the men, and I use that term loosely, in the FWLI will learn a lesson from the TRUE superstar. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with "Iron Man" Chris Andrews. Taped Saturday 01/13/96: [Iron Man is just finishing a 5-mile jog around his neighborhood in Dartmouth, Nova Scotia. He's wearing a DCI t-shirt, and black jogging pants.] Iron Man: I guess its time I faced the fact that the holidays are over, and answer a few of the people who've decided to make themselves quite a nuisance to me. First of all, Chris Thomas. You want me in a submission match? Well, I'll have to see what Tom has to say that. If he approves, then consider it a loss for you. Simple as that. Also, you don't think that Lisa's on my side? Well, who's the guy that she's with now? She may not be in the room with me right now, but she's on my side. Now for the so-called Z-Man Brian Zordich. I guess I got a little distracted after my loss to The HBKing, but like I said before, I'M BACK! And after a look at the last rankings, I know who I want to go after right now. The number one contender to the CDWF LHW belt, and that is none other than YOU, Z-MAN! You better watch your back, cause if that numbskull of a new President in the CDWF or that moron President Autrey don't get their acts together and give me a match with you, I'm coming after you myself! [Fade out] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with The Players Club ("Desirable" Danny Dynamite & Michael "The Maverick" Reyna). Taped Sunday 01/14/96: [In a weight room, Danny Dynamite is squatting and Micheal Reyna is jumping rope.] MR: [Putting down his rope] FWLI, listen up! The Players Club is fed up with all this crap we are being served. We were the strongest group of wrestlers in FWLI. We took people on that were unbeaten and we beat them, we were number one contenders to the tag titles, 6-man champs, and the list goes on and on. Now, we were down and out, but we are back for the new year. Thomas, your lucky your noth the L-H champ anymore. Lightning, watch out, the Maverick has been where you are, and believe you me, I know how to get back there, and I will. DD: That's right, both of us are former L-H champs, and like Mike said, we are the best. Come 96, the P.C. is going under construction. We will trim the fat, and make the P.C. stronger and better than ever. Matt Foxx, I need to know where you stand. Where does your loyalties fall? Give me a call Matt. As for the rest of FWLI, watch out. P.C. is coming full steam ahead, and we will take no sh!t from ANYONE, including you Rebel. If you wanna talk the talk, well Rebel, you dirty bastard, I will come at you first and make your life hell. even if it drives me out of action and out of FWLI. MR: Bottom line, champions, be prepared to give your titles to the best going today, the P.C.!!!! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI World TV Champion "The Bandwidth Mountie" Jean-Pierre Vachon. Taped Sunday 01/14/96: [FADE IN to BANDWIDTH MOUNTIE, standing on standard TV soundstage.] BM: You know, Winter War's coming up, and I just had a look at what's been announced. And you know what? Not only am I NOT on the list of competitors for that 60-man rumble, but apparently the FWLI commissioners think I shouldn't even bother coming down to defend this (holds up belt) the FWLI TV title! What, do they think I need a vacation or something? Come on now! I'm pumped, primed and ready to tackle anything -- even 59 other FWLI superstars for the title of Winter Warrior! I've even got enough energy left to do a title shot -- whether it be the Ragman, the Missman, or even that little boy Doug George! I dunno what the Commissioners are thinking, or if they're having trouble making up their minds, but one thing's for certain -- at Winter War, "law and order" are gonna be there! Whether it's defending the TV title, or battling in that maximum rumble, or even both -- the Bandwidth Mountie is gonna make sure -- Justice will win! [FADE OUT.] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with Samson Stargill, accompanied by Delila Williams. He wears a blue muscle shirt with FWLI scripted on the front in gold, jeans and cowboy boots. His long blonde hair is pulled back into a pony tail. The big battle royal street fight! DW: My man right here will take that match by storm! He is the most powerful man around, and this is his kind of match! Heaven is a playground. Come Winter War.. The wrestling ring will be Samson's playground.. And we'll see you.. in Heaven!
