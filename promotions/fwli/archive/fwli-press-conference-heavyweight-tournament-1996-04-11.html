[FWLI] Press Conference - Heavyweight Championship Tournament - 04/10/96

11 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: the FWLI Press Room. FWLI Commissioner Jason Dean is standing at the podium, addressing the crowd of reporters. Flash bulbs are going off at an alarming rate, and reporters are anticipating news on the FWLI title situation.] DEAN: I want to thank you all for coming here this afternoon. Let me get right to the point and say that the FWLI World Champion will be determined in a one-night, eight-man tournament at the OMNI in Atlanta, GA. The participants have been selected from the top eight male contenders, and randomly seeded thusly... [Dean turns on an overhead projector, and the following chart is projected on to the screen.] NUKE --------------------------\ Greg "The Barber" Roberts >---------\ --------------------------/ \ Mark "Executioner" Engel >-------\ --------------------------\ / \ "Desirable" Danny Dynamite >---------/ \ --------------------------/ \ Chris "Hit-Man" Thomas >------------------------- --------------------------\ / FWLI WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor >---------\ / CHAMPION --------------------------/ \ / "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk >-------/ --------------------------\ / A.J. Charisma >---------/ --------------------------/ I guess that's all I really need to say. I'll be more than happy to take some questions. VOICE: I've got a big question, Mr. Wrestling Dean, sir!!!!! [Camera pans back to focus on "Iceman" Brian Knight approaching the podium. He is wearing a Four Horsemen T-shirt, jeans, and a leather jacket. He is carrying the FWLI European Heavyweight Championship belt with him. Cameras turn to take pictures of what appears to be a confrontation.] KNIGHT: Well, Commish, I'm scanning this chart here, and I don't quite get it. See, this belt right here says I'm the number one guy here in this fed!! And you stick in losers like Mark Engel, like Todd Newkirk, hell, I've been beating these guys left and right!!! Now, SURELY you can make some room for the Iceman here, right?? DEAN: Brian, you are certainly one of the top ranked wrestlers here in the FWLI, there is no question of that. But, as the European Heavyweight Champion, you wouldn't be able to hold both belts simultaneously. We need a man who can be dedicated to the belt, because I don't want to be forced into another tournament after this one. KNIGHT: Nope... not buying it, Commissioner! Sounds like you're afraid to have the Iceman in your little tourney here!! But you should be MORE afraid of NOT having him in there!!!!!!!!! [Knight storms off, with photographers snapping pictures left and right.] DEAN: I apologize for that little interruption. Are there any other questions??? [Several reporters raise their hands. Dean points to one.] REPORTER #1: Have you taken into account that, with the possible exception of A.J. Charisma, all these men in the tournament are friends outside the ring??? DEAN: When it comes to the FWLI World Heavyweight Championship, you're talking about the most prestigious title in all of professional wrestling. All these men are CONSUMMATE professionals, and they will give their all to wear the FWLI World Championship belt. In fact, I'm rather glad it worked out this way, because I think the fans will be treated to a fine series of matches that night. Any other questions?? [Several reporters raise their hands. Dean points to one.] REPORTER #2: Have you responded to the claim by the "Duke of Hazard" Winston Major that he is now the FWLI World Champion??? DEAN: [rolling his eyes] All I will say is that Mr. Major obviously needs some more time to learn about the FWLI, in more ways than one. Any other questions?? [Several reporters raise their hands. Dean points to one.] REPORTER #3: Care to make any predictions?? [Dean and the audience laugh.] DEAN: Ha ha. I guess I'll close by saying that any of these men will be fine representatives of the FWLI. Thank you very much for your time. [Dean leaves the podium, as cameras continue to snap his picture, and reporters continue to ask questions. Camera fades.]
