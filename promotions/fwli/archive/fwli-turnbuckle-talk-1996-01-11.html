FANTASY WRESLING LEAGUE INTERNATIONAL PRESENTS F W L I T U R N B U C K L E T A L K ******************************************************************************* J A N U A R Y 1 0 , 1 9 9 6 ******************************************************************************* Good evening, FWLI fans, and welcome to another edition of TURNBUCKLE TALK where you get to hear what's on the minds (or lack thereof) of the wrestlers in FWLI. TURNBUCKLE TALK is broadcast periodically with interviews collected since the last edition. Interviews can be read as soon as they arrive by going to FWLI's Home Page on the World Wide Web at: http://www.exit109.com/~marke/FWLI.html At our Home Page, you can also view: - The latest rankings - The current roll-call of champions - Past FWLI Pay-Per-Views and TV Broadcasts - "The Dean's List": The latest "inside scoops" on FWLI - The names and e-mail addresses of the demented maniacs (including myself) who run FWLI - How to join FWLI, including a "bio" form also usable for WHO'S WHO ******************************************************************************* Interview with FWLI newcomer The Outlaw. Taped Saturday 01/06/96: So... I see i've entered a new league. Well that's fine. I like my opponents not knowing what i've done in the past. Everything is going to be brand new here. So lets get to it! Winter War, is right around the corner. I want it to be my first par per view event and my debut into the FWLI! So here's what I have in mind. I want to be the first particapant in the 60 man battle royal! If that's not possible then at winter war I would like to post and open contract to face me in a Steel Cage! I wan't the members of the FWLI to realize just how tuff I really am! This Winter, will NEVER be the Same! Nothing beats a street fight... and no one beats... THE OUTLAW! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with Samson Stargill. Taped Sunday 01/07/96: [The scene.. A building with romanesque pillars is shown.. The camera gets closer, and finally, A giant of a man walks out from behind the pillars. He has long, dark brown hair and wears cut off jeans and no shirt. A beautiful brunette has her arm around his muscular waist. She wears a pair of bright blue spandex pants and a cut off shirt.] Samson: Hello Wrestling fans! Thanks for joining Delila and I today. I have a few things to say about the FWLI's upcoming events. First of all, if you caught my last interview, you'll know that Delila was out signing contracts for me. Well, she did a great job! Delila: It wasn't easy, but we got you three big matches coming up! [Delila leans towards Samson, who leans down (he's about a foot taller than her) and they share a kiss.] Samson: That's right! You see, Winter War: New Year's Resolution is just around the corner, and I am now signed as one of the MANY participants in the main event! You see, we can come into the ring with anything we want in that one. But, all I need are these two fists and these guns. [Samson does a double biscep pose.] Samson: Oh, but don't be surprised if Samson Stargill has a little extra going for him in this one. I might just surprise you all with a little extra power in the ring! The 59 other men in this one are going to fall at the hands of the most powerful man around. It doesn't matter if I'm the first to enter the ring, or the last. Samson Stargill will come out as the Winter War Champion!!! Delila: But don't forget your other matches coming up, my dear. Samson: Oh yes, how could I forget? The Ket! I have a match signed with you, and it's going to be the toughest of your life! You see, Samson Stargill isn't here to give less than all he's got. And when I put you in the Rack.. or Take you off the top with a Superplex from my 6'8" frame. It's going to be all she wrote, and Samson will be moving up the rankings even further! Then I finally get my shot! Another DCI member to face, and a United States Title to win! "Black Scorpion"? Death From Above will not phase me... The Scorpion Sting will feel like a mosquito bite... and Your "Venom" will not put me to sleep. You see, you're up against a 6'8", 325 mountain of muscle. And you can't hope to win. Your 268 pound frame can NOT stand up to the power of Samson Stargill! The Walls Will come crashing down.. DOWN UPON YOUR HEAD! And Samson Stargill will be the NEW FLWI UNITED STATES CHAMPION!!! Delila: Get ready boys.. The MAN is coming after YOU! [The Camera fades out.] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with General Kane. Taped Monday 01/08/96: [The scene is a study. Most of the room is dark, but there is a small pool of light illuminating a table. On the table are several books pens and so on, and a carved chess set. A hand reaches down and moves one of the black peices, capturing a white pawn. Kane leans forward, resting on the table.] Kane: Chess. A game of strategy, a game of the mind. And an apt metaphor for what I am going to say today. [He smiles.] You see in chess the object of the game is to try and capture your opponents king. And how do you do that? You start by defeating his other peices. One by One. [He captures another white pawn] Unfortunetly, as in many other things, you more often that not have to start by dealing with the pawns. Dealing with the lesser pieces that are the first lines of defense. And that is what we shall do. We shall deal with the pawns. One by One they will be swept aside. [He sweeps his hand across the board clearing all the white peices] Until only the King is left. And then he too shall fall. [He knocks the White king over.] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with G.W.R. (Loco & Spoiler). Taped Monday 01/08/96: [The front office of FLWI. Spoiler walks in, carrying a piece of paper. He is clearly mad. He puts the piece of paper down in front of the startled secretary.] Spoiler: This had better be a mistake. Secretary: Sir? What had better be a mistake? Spoiler: Read that. [The secretary looks at the piece of paper, and scans it.] Secretary: It's the tag team rankings of FWLI sir. Spoiler: I know what it is. But do you notice something about it? Secretary: Sir? Spoiler: An ommision? Something not there that should be? Secretary: [Reading the paper] G.W.R. isn't mentioned. Spoiler: We aren't mentioned. Exactly. Why aren't we mentioned? [He starts to calm down a bit.] Look, I know we haven't wrestled much recently, I know that we haven't won many of the matches where we have wrestled, but we still should be on there somewhere. Even if it is on the bottom. We don't mind working our way up the rankings. But we should be on there. Secretary: Yes sir. I see your point. Spoiler: Good. So it'll be sorted out? Secretary: We'll see what we can do. [The Secretary turns around and walks back into the office. Spoiler turns around, and seemingly sees the camera for the first time.] Spoiler: Listen, and Listen good. We are still here. We are still training and we are still looking for competition. We still have that contract on the table for the Total Package, if their not too yellow to accept it, and we are still intent on proving ourselves to everyone. Now, we're hearing some strange things, Eurasian Tag Titles? We'd be interested in them. We'd be interested in any competition at all. But just remember one thing. WE DON'T LIKE BEING OVERLOOKED!!!!!!!!!! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI newcomers The Royal Knights (The Cincinnati Knight & The Royal Guard & Duchess Dana with manager The Duke David Jones). But the Fans you deserve the best and we is the best! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with "Iceman" Brian Knight. Taped Monday 01/08/96: [Scene: Aspen, Colorado, up on a high mountain top stands a lone figure, wearing a long black leather duster. His blond hair blows wildly in the wind. The camera continues to pull forward, until "Iceman" Brian Knight's face comes into full view. He now sports facial hair, and a serious look in his eye.] The winter winds are blowing hard, and the entire east coast can feel the biting cold. The ice age reborn. When I came into FWLI, I came in with one mission. To end the chaos. Then, I became the very chaos I set out to destroy. Then, I saw the light, and my mission became one of revenge. I lost my very focus. I lost the one thing that made me the best wrestler in the sport today. I lost my will of the cold. Now, I return to my roots, The Rockies of Colorado. It is here that my quest began. And it is here that I will again restart my quest. And my quest ends at The World Heavyweight Championship. From here on, Brian Knight will strive forward in his quest. The Ice Age is back in the FWLI, and there are a lot of people who aren't going to like it. People like Mark Engel, who continues to think that he's better than I am. Engel, you've been a thorn in my side for too long now. I respect you as an athlete. But you've said some things that make the ice water in my veins boil hot. You've never stated that you respect me, or my family. In fact, you've gone the opposite way. You and your wife have made no bones about your feelings about my family. Well, that's fine. One day, you and I will enter the ring again, but this time, there won't be a time limit. There won't be a count out. Next time, you won't have any excuses. And neither will I. The Ice Age has returned to the FWLI. And things will never be the same. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Interview with FWLI newcomers The Circle of Champions (Dan Kauffman, Matt Kauffman, Shao Lin, Essence Cahn & Christopher Drury). Taped Tuesday 01/09/96: [Outside their home on the Jersey Shore, snow-covered after the Blizzard of '96. Melanie is attacking (literally) a huge snowdrift, breaking it up with a snow shovel. The Executioner is using his be-heading axe to chop at the largest pieces of ice.] Executioner: It seems that we're not too popular among certain wrestlers these days. [Brings his axe down hard on a giant piece of ice, smashing it to pieces.] Well, that's no surprise. Throughout history, Executioners have always been feared and hated, because they have no regrets about what they do, because they show no mercy, because they take the lives of condemned scum and nobody can do anything about it. [Smashes another chunk of ice.] Just like no one's been able to stop this Executioner. I've been pounded, bruised, and had bones broken by the best in RSPCW and FWLI, yet I'm still here. Maniac: And people also seem afraid of me 'cause I may be a LITTLE unstable... [Slams her snow shovel blade hard into a large snowdrift, causing it to crumble to pieces of snow in front of her. She gives an irrepressible smile.] So what, nobody's perfect. But you should see the things people do when they think they have to protect themselves from a maniac. Daredoll, you actually thought that training in a lion's cage would protect you from me. [Laughs as she shovels the snow away, pitching it high into the air.] You escaped by the skin of your daring a*s only because of J.R. Worthless. Executioner: With the new year, I've got just one resolution - CHAMPIONSHIP. FWLI World Heavyweight Championship. Destructo, if you have enough dumb luck to survive The Nature Boy, I challenge you, no, I CONDEMN YOU to face me in the ring. You couldn't beat me in our tag-team matches, you sure as hell won't do it one-on-one. Trust me, if Nature Boy doesn't smash you... [Smashes another chunk of ice with his axe.] .. I will!!! Maniac: And Daredoll, I'm still ranked in the top five, which means that sooner or later, you're going to have to enter MY asylum again, and this time you'll be locked down, restrained, and there'll be no escape. Girl, you and I sooner or later are gonna have some fun, Maniac-style! Executioner: Winter War is coming up, and the sixty-man Royal Street Fight. I'm gonna be in it, and I'm gonna be winnin' it. No rules, no DQ, bring anything you want. It might as well have been WRITTEN for The Executioner. You know what kind of tools I use in MY trade. [Looks at his axe for a second.] I'm gonna CHOP right through the competition, and the FWLI Championship Committee will HAVE to make me the number-one contender, and the FWLI champion will have to meet his Executioner. Brian Knight, I see you're in the match too. Do you see what I do when there's too much ice in my way? [Smashes an especially large chunk of ice into tiny pieces.] How are the ribs, by the way? [Evil smile] You think we've got a problem with your family? Man, YOU'VE got a problem with your family. Your sister is running around with Thomas F**king Jones! THOMAS JONES! The man who would put you out of wrestling at the drop of a hat! As for you, you never did learn to leave well enough alone, did you? We had our matches, they were grueling and you DID earn my respect. But that's not enough, huh? The next time, I'll give you more than enough to handle. Maniac: And as for you, "Ice Queen", the next time we meet, you're gonna WISH you were in a strait jacket as opposed to what I'll do to you. [Attacks another piece of a snowdrift with her snow shovel, causing it to crumble.] Executioner: I'll see all you other 59 condemned souls at Winter War. And may the Lord have mercy on your souls, because I won't! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- THE CENTER RING.
