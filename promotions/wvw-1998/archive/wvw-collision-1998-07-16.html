The camera then shows two cars heading toward each other in slow motion. A heart is heard beating in the background.
Finally, the two cars crash and then the camera fades to black again. A giant stamp comes out of nowhere and stamps "The Show" on the screen in big red lettering. The shot then dissolves and shows Lance Peterson in a dim light, standing in the middle of the W.V.W. studio. He is wearing a black suit, white shirt, black tie, and black shoes. He looks up at the camera as it slowly descends from the ceiling. The lights brighten and "Collision" is on the air.]
LP: Hello, everyone. Welcome to Collision. I am Lance Peterson, you host for tonight. We have had a great show tonight. Let's take you to some of the action from earlier.
[The camera switches to a shot of Rob Stryker walking to the ring to a cheering crowd. The camera then shows the Mischief Makers standing in the ring. The camera then shows Rob Stryker standing in the middle of the ring with a microphone.]
RS: Everyone, I have some bad news. Buff Miller won't be able to make it due to a severe back injury and will not be back for some time . . .
[The audio slowly cuts back to Lance Peterson as the camera shows him in the studio.] LP: As you can see and as you have heard, Buff Miller has been injured. The sources tell us that it was in a car accident. Anyway, Rob Stryker was prepared to take on both men, but the ref said otherwise. Scotty
Tallion and Bobby Borden had a coin toss to see who would face Rob
Stryker and Tallion won it. He went off to face Rob Stryker. Let's take you to the closing moments of the match.
[The camera cuts to the action and Lance Peterson calls the action.] [Stryker takes Tallion over with a hip toss and applies an arm bar. Stryker brings him up and Tallion reverses into an arm bar of his own.]
LP: This match went back and forth for the first few minutes. [The shot cuts and shows Tallion as he catches Stryker with a power slam. He covers, but he only gets a two count. Tallion applies a rear chinlock.]
LP: Scotty Tallion got the upper hand after a while and kept the advantage. [The shot cuts to Tallion as he whips Stryker into the turnbuckles. Tallion charges in after him, but Stryker catches him with a boot. Tallion stumbles through the ropes and falls to the outside.]
LP: Stryker eventually stopped Tallion's momentum and Tallion went to the outside. Stryker then followed and they started to brawl. This led to a double countout. Therefore, the match ended in a draw.
[The camera cuts to a shot of Tallion and Stryker battling on the outside of the ring. After a moment of footage, the camera cuts back to Lance Peterson in the studio.]
LP: That was a hell of a way to start things off for the night. However, we went right to the next match with "High Impact" Danny Wilson facing
Jack Vars. Let's take you to the action.
[The camera cuts to footage of the Jack Vars/Danny Wilson match. The shot shows Vars stomping away at a fallen Danny Wilson.]
LP: Jack Vars took this match from the start. Wilson was just too small for the larger Vars and Vars took that for all it was worth as he leveled Wilson with a tremendous clothesline at the start of the match.
[The camera cuts to a shot of Vars clotheslining Wilson as Wilson turns around. The camera then shows Vars body slamming Wilson hard to the mat.] LP: Vars just kept taking it to the smaller man and eventually ended the match with his finisher, the "Vars Drop."
[The camera shows Jack Vars doing an inverted piledriver to Wilson from the top turnbuckle. After that, Vars goes for the cover and the shot switches back to
Lance Peterson in the studio.]
LP: From there, we went to Tony Diego and HeadHunter as they faced off. Man, I am telling you that this was one hell of a match. [The camera shows Diego doing a moonsault onto HeadHunter. The camera then shows HeadHunter land a shooting star press onto Diego.]
LP: These two did all that they could. [The camera shows Diego hitting a Super Kick on HeadHunter. Then, the camera shows HeadHunter nail Diego with a DDT.]
LP: Both men showed just how much they had and just how much they could take. [The camera shows HeadHunter nailing a superplex. Then, the camera shows Diego on a table on the outside of the ring. HeadHunter leaps from the top rope with a 450 splash, but Diego moves out of the way. Diego then takes HeadHunter and throws him into the ring.]
LP: HeadHunter took a nasty spill and that looked like it would be all. However, Diego was slow to the draw and only got a two count when he covered him. [The camera shows Diego pounding the mat in frustration. The camera then shows
Diego executing a brainbuster on HeadHunter.]
LP: Diego went for a cover after that devestating brainbuster, but the count was interrupted by the bell. They fought the full twenty minutes. Both men were tired and saddened when they were forced to end the match right there.
[The camera shows both men laying in the ring. Diego, realizing that it's a draw, shakes his head in disgust. The shot then switches to Lance Peterson in the studio.]
LP: Then, about five minutes ago, Brutal Brian faced "The Psychotic One" in what we thought would be a great match. This was considered the sleeper of the night. It started off with "The Psychotic One" coming into the ring with a microphone.
[The camera shows "The Psychotic One" standing in the ring with a mic. Audio cuts to him.]
TPO: . . . and if I do not beat this freak, I will leave World Vintage Wrestling . . . forever! [Audio cuts back to Lance Peterson.] LP: Psychotic One will live to regret those words as Brutal Brian snuck up from behind and tackled "The Psychotic One."
[The camera shows Brutal Brian tackling TPO and punching away at the back of his head.] LP: "The Psychotic One" tried to mount an offense, but Brutal Brian was too quick for him.
[The camera shows Brian ducking a clothesline and then following up with a boot to the stomach. Brian then applied the double arm DDT that he calls "Payback is hell." The camera then cuts back to Lance Peterson.]
LP: Brutal Brian applied his "Payback is hell" and pinned "The Psychotic
One." That leads us up to now. We still have two feature matches for you. We have Brass Knuckles facing GlacierIce and we also have our main event with "Flashy" Mike Robinson facing "Dangerous" Darren Shaw.
This will go down after we come back from out commercial break. Stay tuned. [Camera fades to a commercial.] ================================================================= A commercial is shown for Ball Park Franks. A commercial is shown for Baseketball. A commercial is shown for Burger King. A commercial is shown for Pringles. A commercial is shown for 10-10-321. ================================================================= [The camera fades back in from the commercial and shows Lance Peterson in the studio.] LP: Welcome back, everyone. At this time, I would like to take you to my colligues, Ed Crivits and Mike Dunn. They will be calling tonights action. I understand that Brass Knuckles and GlacierIce are in the ring at this moment and this match is just about under way. Take it away, Mike and Ed.
[The shot cuts to Brass Knuckles and GlacierIce in the ring. The bell rings to start the match and the audio cuts to Ed Crivits and Mike Dunn.]
[Ding Ding Ding] _ _ _ _ _ _ _________________________________ | | | || | | || | | | Collision | | | || | | || | | | | |/\| || | | || |/\| | Brass Knuckles | V V || | | || V V | vs. | __ |\ V /| __ | GlacierIce |_/ \_| \___/ |_/ \_|_________________________________ EC: Thank you, Lance. This match is under way. I am Ed Crivits and my partner in crime is Mike Dunn.
MD: Hello, everyone. [Ice and Knuckles circle around each other. Ice sees that Knuckles is the bigger man. They lock up and Knuckles uses his size to bring Ice into the corner. The ref calls for the break and Knuckles reluctantly obliges.
Knuckles attempts a sucker punch to the face, but Ice ducks out of the way and punches Knuckles in the back of the head. The ref goes to warn Ice, but
Knuckles angrily pushes him out of the way. Ice brings Knuckles down with a drop toehold and then slides into a side headlock.]
EC: GlacierIce is showing us some good technical wrestling. I wonder if he can keep this up.
MD: I have done some homework on this guy. I think he can take the big man out. [Ice releases the headlock and quickly rolls over and applies a hammerlock. Knuckles shows that he is in pain. Ice brings Knuckles to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Knuckles comes back and knocks Ice down with a shoulder block. Knuckles hits the ropes to his right and comes back with an elbow.
Knuckles misses as Ice slides out of the way and gets to his feet. Ice tries his own elobow and he gets the same result. Both men quickly get to their feet and into each other's face.]
EC: It looks like we may have a show down here. Things look as though they are ready to explode.
MD: Indeed they do, Ed. Oh my God! [Knuckles swings at Ice and Ice catches his arm. He locks the arm and then palms Knuckles hard in the face, staggering him. Ice then knocks Knuckles to the mat with a short arm clothesline. Ice then drops an elbow and goes for the cover. . . . One . . . Two-Kickout. Knuckles kicks out with force.]
EC: GlacierIce had the right idea there. You want to get rid of Knuckles early on instead of having to deal with him later.
MD: How right you are, Ed. Brass Knuckles is one tough customer and I wouldn't want him able to use that size after ten minutes of fighting.
EC: True . . . true . . . [Ice brings Knuckles to his feet once again and applies an arm ringer. Knuckles reaches for the ropes and grabs a hold of them. The ref calls for the break. Ice holds on for the first four seconds and then releases. Knuckles shakes his arm around, hoping to make it feel better. Meanwhile, Ice goes right back to the attack with a kick to Knuckles' ribs. Knuckles hinches over in pain and Ice takes him down with a Fujiwara arm bar.]
MD: GlacierIce takes the big man down with a Fujiwara arm bar. I guess the man spent some time in Japan to perfect that maneuver because that did not look like a sloppy one.
EC: He could have also had the right spot and applied it correctly. MD: Well, that happens . . . [Knuckles reaches for the ropes again and gets them. Ice releases the hold and gets to his feet. He stomps away at Knuckles and then backs away. He takes a second to think and walks back toward Knuckles. Knuckles takes Ice down with a double-leg takedown and punches him in the face. Knuckles brings Ice to his feet and whips him hard into the corner. Knuckles follows up with a clothesline and Ice slumps down.]
EC: It looks like the tables have turned as Knuckles has the upper hand in this match. MD: However, you cannot forget that GlacierIce has had the lead in this match for quite a while. Knuckles just saw a loop hole and . . .
EC: And took the advantage. MD: Right. [Knuckles starts to punch away at the slumped body of GlacierIce. The ref tells Knuckles to back away. Knuckles ignores the ref and continues his flurry of punches. The ref starts the count and Knuckles finally steps back. He grabs Ice and whips him across the ring. He follows up with a shoulder ram to the gut and Ice folds up. Knuckles backs away and lets GlacierIce's body fall to the mat. Knuckles goes for the cover. . . . One . . . Two . . . GlacierIce gets his foot on the ropes and that stops the count.]
EC: Knuckles had him! MD: Maybe you didn't see that GlacierIce had his foot on the ropes. EC: Shut up, man. GlacierIce didn't have his foot on the ropes. The ref should have counted to three.
MD: No, I think you are wrong . . . as always. [Ed snarls.] [Knuckles argues with the ref about the count. Ice slowly gets to his feet and
Knuckles still argues with the ref. Knuckles turns around and Ice pokes him in the eyes. Ice follows up with a kick to the stomach and then hits the ropes.
Ice leaps up and catches Knuckles in the face with a jump kick. Knuckles lands in the center of the ring.]
EC: Oh my God! He nailed him with the Flying Chin Music! MD: It's all over now. [Ice covers Knuckles. . . . One . . . Two . . . Three! The bell rings to end the match.] [DING DING DING] RM: Here is your winner . . . GLACIERICE!!! ["Walk This Way" starts to play over the loud speaker as GlacierIce leaves the ring. His music fades out soon after.]
EC: What a match that was between Brass Knuckles and GlacierIce. MD: GlacierIce had most of the match, but Knuckles also had his moments. I hope to see more of these two in the future.
EC: We should, they are under contract here. [Both men laugh.] MD: Well, I guess you can say that. Anyway, they had a good match and that is what we like to see here.
EC: Yes, we do. Anyway, we have to go to a commercial break. We'll be back with more action right after this time out.
[Camera fades to a commercial.] ================================================================= A commercial is shown for Burger King. A commercial is shown for Exxon Gasoline. A commercial is shown for The Weather Channel. A local commercial is shown. A commercial is shown for America Online. ================================================================= [Camera fades back in and shows Ed Crivits and Mike Dunn sitting at the broadcast table. Both men are wearing black suits, white shirts, and black ties. Ed Crivits is on the left and Mike Dunn is sitting to his right. The crowd is heard cheering in the background.]
EC: Welcome back, everyone. We are happy to say that this has been another great night of action. We still have more action to come as our main event is next, but first, let's show you the line-up for this coming
Monday on Distress.

EC: I am sure that many people are excited, Mike. However, one match that

MD: Yes, that will be a very interesting match. Consider what happened last week and add that to the competitiveness of both teams. You have the Crack Connection, who were disqualified last week due to the use of a chair. I am sure that the Mischief Makers will be looking for revenge. Tickets are still on sale for the MSG event. Get them now while you still can.
EC: Besides that tag match, we also have the debuts of such people like Hardcore, Steve Milla, Craig Haynes, and the television debut of Brutal
Brian. All of this and more happens this Monday Night on Distress.
Now, let's get to Ron Moore, who is standing in the ring with the pre- match introductions.
[The shot cuts to Ron Moore in the ring.] RM: Ladies and gentlemen . . . the following contest is our main event of the evening. It is scheduled for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. Introducing first . . .
[Lights go out, as "HellrAiser" by Dr. Draven starts to play in the arena. As "Flashy" Mike Robinson emerges from the entrance way. "Flashy" Mike
Robinson is wearing a a red colored tights, and black leather wrestling boots.
On the left side of red tights, it has "FLASHY" written in blue bubble letters, and on the right, side, it has, "MIKE ROBINSON" written in blue block letters. He also wears one pair of black colored elbow pad, which he wears on his right elbow, and two pairs of red colored knee pads. His brown golden hair is seen hanging down his back, and his blue hazel eyes is gazing all around the arena . . .]
Ron Moore: He hails from Miami, Florida. He weighs in at 250 pounds and stands at 6 feet, 5 inches tall. Here is . . .
. . . Miiiiiichaaaaael ROOOOOOOBIIIINSOOOOON!!! [Michael Robinson walks down the aisle way. A loud chorous of boos can be heard in the arena. We can see Robinson acting cocky and very obnoxious, as he talks trash to a few fans nearby the ring . . . He then approaches the ring, hops up the apron, and jumps inside the ring. Right after that, "HellrAiser"
cuts. The normal arena lights cuts on again.] RM: And his opponent . . . ["High" by Jimmie's Chicken Shack starts to play in the arena. "Die Hard" Darren Shaw emerges from the entrance way. Darren is wearing black tights with
"live easy...DIE HARD" written in dark red across his tights, black knee pads, black elbow pads, and dark red wrist bands as well as a black denim jacket with the sleeves cut off.]
RM: He hails from Los Angeles, California. He weighs in at 232 pounds and stands at 6 feet 4 inches tall. Here is . . .
. . . "DIE HARD" DARREN SHAAAAAAAAAAAAAAW!!! ["Die Hard" Darren Shaw walks down the aisle way. He slaps a few fans' hands along the way. He enters the ring and "High" fades out.]
[Ron Moore leaves the ring and the bell rings to start the match.] [DING DING DING] _ _ _ _ _ _ _________________________________ | | | || | | || | | | Collision | | | || | | || | | | | |/\| || | | || |/\| | "Flashy" Mike Robinson | V V || | | || V V | vs. | __ |\ V /| __ | "Die Hard" Darren Shaw |_/ \_| \___/ |_/ \_|_________________________________ EC: And we are under way. [After the bell rings, both men stare each other down from across the ring. Shaw looks a little less determined than Robinson. Shaw leans against the corner and the ref glances at both men. He shakes his head and tells them to get it on. Both men slowly walk to the center of the ring while neither man bothers to take their eyes off of the other. Shaw then puts his game face back on and they continue to stare each other down. They exchange some words and then lock up.]
EC: Here we go! The anticipation has stopped. MD: It has for now. Let's see who can emerge victorious. [Both men put up a struggle as they both try to get an advantage. Robinson starts pushing Shaw back, but Shaw's footing quickly stops that action.
Robinson tries it agian. Shaw ducks down and takes Robinson over with an arm drag. Both men quickly get to their feet. They quickly engage in a lock up once again and this time, Robinson gets the advantage. He brings Shaw into the corner and slowly breaks.]
EC: Both men are being very cautious as they do not want to take any chances and make a mistake.
MD: You are right on that one. Neither man wants to lose this match on account of a mistake. If they lose, they want to go out like men and be beaten.
[Robinson lays in a chop and stuns Shaw. He whips Shaw out of the corner and
Shaw hits the opposite corner just as quickly. Robinson charges in and nails
Shaw with a clothesline. Shaw slumps down and Robinson brings him right back up. Robinson lays in another chop and whips Shaw out of the corner again.
Shaw reverses it and Robinson hits the corner. Shawfollows up with a shoulder ram and then backs away. Robinson staggers out and Shaw knocks him down with a standing dropkick. The crowd mildly applauds.]
EC: The crowd likes what they are seeing here as Shaw has taken the upper hand. MD: I have seen both of these men in action before and let me just tell you that they both have great atheleticism.
EC: I must agree. They are both agile and they both know what they are doing in that ring. We are definitely in for a treat here.
MD: If I were to pick a winner here, Ed, I would shrug my shoulders and let you decide. [Ed chuckles.] [Shaw brings Robinson back to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Robinson comes back and Shaw takes him down with a power slam. Shaw covers. . . . One
. . . Two-Kickout. Robinson kicks out with force and Shaw doesn't hesitate to go right back to work on him. Shaw pulls Robinson to his feet and throws him through the ropes and to the outside. Shaw follows him and quickly hops over the ropes and to the outside. The ref slides out of the ring and tells both men to get back inside. Shaw takes Robinson and whips him into the guard rail.]
EC: This match has taken to the outside. Shaw knows his way around the ring . . . inside and out.
MD: Mike Robinson is also known for his brawling technique, so you can't discredit him for it.
EC: I wasn't. I was merely mentioning that Shaw knows his way around the outside of the ring. Not once did I mention Robinson.
MD: Oh . . . Well thanks for saying that in front of a nation wide audience. EC: No problem. [Ed chuckles again and Mike Dunn just sighs in disgust.] [As Ed and Mike were talking, The ref initiated the count. At this time, the ref is up to three and Shaw is stomping on a fallen Mike Robinson. Shaw takes
Robinson and rolls him into the ring. Shaw slides into the ring and the ref stops the count at four. Shaw starts to bring Robinson to his feet but stops as Robinson catches him with a low blow. Robinson then brings himself to his feet and quickly snaps Shaw with a DDT. Robinson covers. . . . One . . . Two
. . . Kickout. Shaw kicks out.]
EC: It looks like Robinson is ahead now. Look at Shaw as he lays there in the ring. MD: Robinson had to cheat to get ahead, though. EC: Hey . . . y'gotta do what y'gotta do . . . MD: I guess so. [Robinson takes Shaw and brings him to his feet. Robinson nails Shaw in the face with a hard right. Shaw retaliates as he nails Robinson with a right of his own. Robinson nails Shaw again and Shaw retaliates once more. Robinson throws another right, but Shaw ducks this one and catches Robinson. He picks him up and drops him with an atomic drop. Robinson doubles over in pain and
Shaw snaps him in a DDT.]
EC: An eye for an eye? MD: Oh yeah . . . Shaw just paid Robinson back for the DDT earlier in the match. Fight fire with fire and somebody, if not everybody, will get burned.
EC: Yes . . . You kids at home should listen to that. Don't play with matches. You will be burned. Your houses will burn down. Your skin will be chared. Your pets will be cooked and ready for the eating.
MD: What in the hell are you talking about? EC: Fire with fire . . . MD: Oh? Well you should be talking about this match. EC: Hmm . . . I may start doing that. MD: You better. Ian Walsh is paying you a lot of money to do this. EC: Who cares about Ian Walsh? MD: He's the boss, man. EC: The hell with Ian. [Audio gets fuzzy and a voice interrupts.] VO: Oh, Ed . . . EC: What? VO: Call the damn match or I will take the liberties of firing you and throwing your worthless hide out in the streets.
EC: Oh yeah? And who the hell are you? VO: I'm Ian Walsh. Now if you don't want me to come out there and fire you, you better do your damn job!
[Ed loses the cockiness in his voice.] EC: Oh, alright. [Audio returns to normal.] [During all of this, Robinson, once again, gained the advantage. Right now,
Robinson has Shaw in a surfboard maneuver.]
MD: Ed got his butt handed to him. Ha! Ha! EC: Shut up! [Robinson brings Shaw to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Shaw comes back and ducks Robinson's clothesline attempt. Robinson turns around and Shaw hits the opposite ropes. Shaw comes back and misses with his clothesline attempt. Shaw turns around and walks right into a super kick.]
EC: Holy mother of God! Shaw's head snapped back with force! MD: That's got to be all! [Robinson rolls Shaw up and grabs a hold of the tights. The ref doesn't see the pulling of the tights and counts. . . . One . . . Two . . . Three!]

EC: Man, what a night this has been. MD: Yeah, you almost got fired. [Mike laughs. Ed looks at him with a slightly angered look.] EC: Yeah, sympathize, why don't ya. MD: Oh, don't look for me to give any. I did my job, you, however . . . EC: Let it rest, man. It's done and over with. MD: Whatever. EC: Anyway, we are out of time.
