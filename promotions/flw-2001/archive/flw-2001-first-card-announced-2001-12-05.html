FLW: First Card Announced

5 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Folks, welcome John ("Boom Boom" Burgel Booger) and [name withheld] (Edgar the Ettin) to Fairy Land Wrestling! You know what that means... we have enough people to book the first ever Mystical Mayhem! So... without further adieu... --------------------------------- FLW Presents... MYSTICAL MAYHEM --------------------------------- The first ever full-length Mystical Mayhem has been booked, and it's looking like a doozy and a half! In the opener, Omar the Orc will go one on one with "Way Cool" Hepizah Stool. Omar is looking for his parents, while Hepizah is just looking good... well, as good as an Orc _can_ look. This huge Orc clash will set the tone for the rest of the night, and being the first match in FLW history, a lot is expected of it. Let's hope these boys can come through. Next up is a three-way battle of the heaviest hitters in the FLW, as the disgusting "Boom Boom" Burgel Booger, the disillusioned Zudgrut Gutbusta, and the absolutely mammoth (and very odd) Edgar the Ettin. There will be close to eight hundred pounds in the ring... and that's just Edgar! Needless to say, we're going to need some extra wood supports for this conflict, and probably a few barf bags if "Boom Boom" hasn't taken up hygiene by then. Finally, in our first main event in FLW history, Princess Sugar Fairy will team up with Galadriel Moonwhisper to take on the dark elf duo of "Dark Winter" Gallain Gloam and Xvair. Whereas the bouts before this promise ass-whoopery in massive quantities, this match promises speed, skill, and intrigue. Will the diminutive team (weighing all of 250 pounds combined) of Princess Sugar Fairy and Galadriel be able to overcome the odds? Will Gallain and Xvair be able to work together without stabbing each other in the back? And will Gallain's bloodlust towards Fairies interfere in the overall goal of defeating his opponents? These questions _will_ be answered on the first ever edition of MYSTICAL MAYHEM! ---------------- Due Dates ---------------- Roleplays: Thursday, December 13th, by 11:59 PM. Strats: Wednesday, December 12th, by 11:59PM ------------------------ [name withheld]
