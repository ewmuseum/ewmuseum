Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Fairy Land Wrestling was a promotion set entirely inside a fantasy Otherworld, where orcs, goblins, ettins, dark elves, gnomes and fairies wrestled in a ring built in a forest clearing, called by two fairy commentators, Binky and Winky, and announced by a pixie named Dipsy-Doo.

It came together in December 2001. Its operator waited until enough handlers had joined to book a full card, then announced the first ever Mystical Mayhem: an orc grudge match, a three-way battle of heavyweights, and a main event pitting Princess Sugar Fairy and Galadriel Moonwhisper against the dark elf pair of "Dark Winter" Gallain Gloam and Xvair.

The card itself, posted on 17 December 2001, framed the whole night as a dream the reader falls into and wakes from, and played its fantasy premise for comedy: a two-headed ettin arguing with itself, a goblin convinced he is a fairy, a shape-shifting doppelganger impersonating a dwarven referee.

Mystical Mayhem is the only card that survives, and how long the Otherworld kept running after it is not in the surviving record.
