New Millennium Wrestling

NEW MILLENNIUM WRESTLING Rules & Guidelines Introduction Welcome to NMW - New Millennium Wrestling. The NMW is an E-Wrestling Leageu solely created for your reading pleasure. We don't claim to be the best leageu on the net, but we will try to do our best to make the leageu enjoyable for everyone involved in it. To learn more about NMW please read the following Rules and
Guidelines. If you are interested in joining just fill out the Application Form (scroll down), include a Sample
Roleplay (more about this later on) and send the stuff to [detail withheld] . Roleplaying The NMW is a Roleplaying-based E-Fed. The outcoming of the matches won't be decided by a Simulator - It depends on your Roleplaying. Roleplaying means, that you'll have to write Interviews, Promo Flashes etc. in order to get your Wrestler "over". If you write good flashes you'll do well in here, if you write bad flashes you won't have any success. When applying please include a Sample Roleplay with your Application. Once you are in you'll have to send in one (1) Roleplay every week - no more, no less. Your Roleplaying (and the Roleplaying of your opponents, of course) will decide about the outcoming of your matches.
Characters Since I don't want the NMW Roster to become too big every Player can have a maximum of two Wrestlers. This may be two (2) Single Wrestlers or one (1) Tag Team. There will be no exceptions. Only orginal Wrestlers will be accepted, which means that you can't enter Bret Hart, The Undertaker or Steve Austin - you'll have to create your own Wrestler(s). Please note that I will not accept any stupid rip-offs like"Lionheart" Shawn Austin or "The Total
Package" Owen Jericho. I also won't accept Wrestlers who hail from "Parts Unknown" and wear a "3:16" T-Shirt.
Be creative. There are so many possibilities in E-Wrestling. Shows Currently we have one show every week, entitled "NMW X-Rated". All Matches will take place on this show.
Since the cards got bigger and bigger every week when NMW was around for the first time I have decided that no match will be bigger than 5 KB this time (most of the matches will be 3 - 4 KB). Exceptions will be Title matches, where the Write-Ups will be around 10 KB. Most of the Players are more interested in the Angles anyway, so I think this won't do any harm to the cards. If you are interested in helping out and doing a match (or two) just let me know. Help is always appreciated.
Championships There are three Titles in NMW. They are: the NMW World Heavyweight Title, the NMW World Tag Team Titles and the NMW Regional Title. While the World Titles must be defended at least once every month the Regional
Title must be defended on EVERY card, if not otherwise noted. No other Titles will be recognized by NMW. That means that you won't be allowed to create your own titles and that you can't bring in Titles from another leageu
(except if I give you the permission for it).
Profanity To quote another E-Fed: A lot of e-mail organizations accept it, some don't. This one does ... To a certain extent.
What I run here is an organization, and I always like to keep a business-like image when I run an organization.
This may not be real life, but some people often tend to be offended by such language. I will allow a such word as
"ass", a term which is usually very freely in the Wrestling World these days. I understand that at times a wrestler might become frustrated. But please, try and censor the profanity with "@$#%" or something like ::bleep:: or whatever. But anything other than that, might be punishable by suspension or expulsion.
Conclusion Well, thats basically all. If you have any more Questions feel free to e-mail me. I hope your experience in NMW will be a great one and do always remember ... this is just a GAME - not the real life. So don't take it too seriously and just try to have fun, because that's what E-Wrestling is all about - FUN.
