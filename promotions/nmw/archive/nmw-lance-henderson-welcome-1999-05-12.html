New Millennium Wrestling

12 May 1999

[[The scene opens to a shot of a clear night sky. The stars are twinkling, and there is a general feeling of peacefulness in the air. Slowly, the camera pans down, and we are treated to a majestic shot of New York City.
The camera begins to close in on one of the less impressive looking buildings, and comes to a halt outside one of the windows. Through the grimy glass, we can see a sparsely furnished apartment, with a bed, a wardrobe, and a small bedside table being the only items of furniture on display. We can also see a figure lying sprawled out on the bed. However, due to the opacity of the glass, it is difficult for us to identify the figure clearly.
Before we can even try, however, the scene slowly fades to black...]] [[The shot fades back up inside the apartment. We can now see it's contents a little more clearly There are several empty takeaway containers and pizza boxes scattered about the floor, as well as what appears to be a kit bag and some dirty clothes lying the corner. We can also see the figure on the bed more clearly. He is a fairly well built male with long blonde hair. His chin is covered in stubble, and he is clearly fast asleep. He is wearing a pair of grey sweatpants and a white t-shirt with several food stains down the front. His powerful chest rises and falls with each deep breath he takes.
The camera begins to search around the room again, revealing several newspaper clippings lying on the bedside table. Some of the more notable ones are "Electrifying Youngster turning a few heads", "Cowboy takes New
York State Title", "NMW Forced to close due to management difficulties", and
"WCWF newcomer struggles to find niche". There is also a framed photograph on the wall, which shows the man on the bed (definitely looking a bit cleaner<g>) posing alongside another individual. On closer inspection, the second individual bears a striking resemblance to Ricky "Power" Plant, although the photograph looks to have been taken when both men were in their late teens so it is difficult to be certain. We can also see a pair of slightly bloodstained jeans lying crumpled up at the foot of the bed.
Suddenly, the silence in the room is shattered as the phone on the bedside table rings. The man on the bed grunts to himself, and rolls over onto his side, turning away from the phone. The phone continues to ring a few times, before the answering machine kicks in.]]
[Answering Machine]: You have reached Lance Henderson. Unfortunately, I can't come to the phone right now, but please leave a message after the beep, and I'll get back to you [There then follows a faint clattering sound, and a mumbled "how do you turn this thing o...", which is followed a few seconds later by the beep.]
[Voice]: Hey Lance, it's Robert Blackjacket. I'm just getting back to you about your offering your services again to NMW. Well, I have a contract prepared for you, so as soon as you can make it, come over to my office and we'll sort it out. Thanks a lot.
[We hear the sound of the phone being put down, which is followed by silence. Suddenly, the man on the bed (who we can only assume to be Lance
Henderson) leaps to his feet, and dives for the phone.]
[Lance]: [Grabbing the phone] Hello? HELLO? [Slight pause] ...dammit. [He puts the phone down]
[The man pauses for a moment, before beginning to search the apartment frantically. Searching for what, we cannot be sure. He kicks over a half- full chinese meal container, spilling it's contents all over the floor.
However, it barely registers as he continues his almost frenzied search. Finally he finds what he is looking for. A slightly faded black suit, neatly folded up in the wardrobe. As he takes it out, we catch a brief glimpse of what appears to be a title belt of some kind lying on the floor of the wardrobe. Lance mutters "It's about time" to himself, and begins to get changed into the suit, as the scene fades to black...]]
