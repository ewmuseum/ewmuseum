SCW/NEO Affiliation Official (Staff/Handlers Needed)

2 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Let me introduce myself; my name is [name withheld]. I have worked in several feds over the past few years. The WWO, WWA, CWC, and
GCW are probably the only ones that truly mattered (or that I can still recall today). :-) I've participated in just about every possible position within those feds, from handler to headwriter to general manager. And...
um... enough introduction. Few of you may know this as of yet, but the up-and-coming indy efed known as NEO has recently dove into the turf of old-school NWA. NEO is looking forward to setting up an umbrella-style federation with several regional feds involved. The first of those feds to be participating in this venture will be Southern Cross Wrestling, a Mississippi-based regional fed.
And at that point we run into a few problems. Actually one big problem. We need fricken' handlers and a staff. I'm currently waiting to enlist atleast fifteen handlers and two fedheads in order to get the fed started. If you are interested at all in either of these positions, please take a trip to
SCW's temporary site. Quite soon the site will be hosted on neo-wrestling.com , but for now you can access it through: http://www.angelfire.com/games3/southerncross/index.html Don't make fun of the site or I'll threaten you over the internet! All the information you will need can be found in the rather lengthy FAQ. Yes, I admit that it's a little long and a possibly a bit too detailed, but what can I say... I was on a roll. Just go look-it! :-P If you have any questions, you can contact me at
[detail withheld] . And even if you decide not to try us out, please leave your condolences and IOU's in the discussion board, visit us on mIRC, and/or sign up for our super-special mailing list. One last thing: if the site screws up you can refresh each individual frame that's been screwed over to fix the problems. Don't refresh the whole page though. Hopefully everything will work fine for you... hopefully...
-=[name withheld]=-
