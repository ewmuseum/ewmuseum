Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Southern Cross Wrestling was announced on 2 March 2001 as the first regional federation to join an umbrella project run by the indy e-fed NEO, which its own announcement described as having “recently dove into the turf of old-school NWA” by setting up “an umbrella-style federation with several regional feds involved.” SCW, based in Mississippi, was named as that project's first member.

The announcement was as much a recruitment drive as an introduction: SCW said it needed to enlist at least fifteen handlers and two fedheads before it could actually start, and pointed applicants to a temporary Angelfire site pending its planned move to a permanent NEO-hosted address, with a FAQ its own poster called “a little long and a possibly a bit too detailed.”

That announcement is the only document SCW's own record carries. Whether the fifteen handlers and two fedheads it asked for ever signed on, whether it staged a single card under NEO's umbrella, and how or when it ended aren't in the surviving record.
