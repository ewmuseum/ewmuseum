A biweekly Florida-based promotion whose weekly Thursday Night Fights broadcasts and periodic supercards ran from a relaunch under the FSW acronym in September 2005 through at least October 2007, crowning its own Television and Sunshine State Champions along the way. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Florida State Wrestling relaunched in September 2005, its own newsletter of 12 September announcing a debut card through a newly created Yahoo Group after what it called a two-year gap under the same acronym. Running biweekly out of a home base it called The Basement, its flagship broadcast, Thursday Night Fights, later renamed In Your Face!!, ran alongside periodic supercards — Magic City Mayhem and Romp in the Swamp among them — and it billed itself, by January 2006, as a proud member of the umbrella organization NEO International.

Its own two championships both trace to tournaments run across its weekly broadcasts. The Television Championship was inaugurated by an eight-man tournament in October and November 2005, its final scheduled for the promotion's own debut supercard; no result for that match survives, though a reigning champion is confirmed defending it in a three-way match at the second supercard the following May. A second title, the Sunshine State Championship, was introduced by early 2006 and made official as a supercard match by an on-air contract signing, again with no surviving result.

Its own last surviving broadcast, “In Your Face!! — The Re-Evolution Tour,” aired 2 October 2007 from The Basement, opening on FSW Commissioner Gerry Richardson's office being burgled by a visiting AWA star, Adrien Cochrane, of a jar of candy on his way out. A recruitment letter two months earlier had described the promotion, in its own words, as running old-school style e-wrestling behind such characters as Billy Sweets, Larry Mulberry, Eric James, Paris LeClair, Domanic McLeary, Elijah and Hantu Penyardin.

No closing notice survives; how the Re-Evolution Tour, or the promotion itself, ended isn't in the surviving record.
