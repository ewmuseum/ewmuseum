Thursday Night Fights Lineup 6. Magic City Mayhem Lineup 7. Deadlines --------------------- A Note from the Boss --------------------- Hey guys, I know it's been awhile since the last card came out but shit happens. Like a hurricane that knocks the power out in 2 million homes for over two weeks. These things happen, but I'm a warrior ninja, and I'll conquer it like every other obstacle in life. So anyway, this is the last card before our HUGE, MONSTROUS, INCREDIBLE SUPERCARD, Magic City Mayhem! I'm so pumped for this card that I can feel it in my bone marrow. Which is why I want to get these cards out as quickly as possible... which is why I'm calling forth all match writers who don't suck at match writing. Your country and e-fed needs you. It's your duty. But that's only if you don't suck at it. There's some great stuff going on right now in the FSW and people are firing on all cylinders, one of the reasons I feel this is a very special fed. But if you want to stop by #FSW on StarChat to get to know your fellow FSW comrades better, feel free to do so. Some of you already frequent the chatroom, but I want ALL of you to frequent it! I don't know why, but after writing three matches and doing spell check and lots of formatting in a day and a half, I have a ton of energy. I think it's best to end this rant on a positive note: this is a great roster and I love working with all of you. I seriously feel like this is probably the best roster in all of e-w right now. So don't flake on me, or I'll have to kill off your characters in the worst way possible. Only kidding, only kidding.
