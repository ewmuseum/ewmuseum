Hello dear sirs and womens. My name is Steven. I run the future Hall of Fame e-wrestlers, Stevie Erickson and Freddy Lopez. I am running my own e-wrestling federation called Florida State Wrestling. I am looking for four more brilliant characters to get this wrestling train moving. If you join the FSW, you can expect: -to e-wrestle for the more prolific e-wrestling federation of all time. -to e-wrestle for the coolest promoter of all time. -to have your character shown in a positive light. -to participate in meaningful feuds and storylines. -to read some of the best summarized matches anywhere. -to become a famous e-wrestler simply for having a match in my fed. -to be ostracized by 75% of the e-w community simply for joining my fed. -to sing lyrics to many Marvin Gaye classics such as "Ain't No Mountain High Enough" by Marvin Gaye and Tammi Terrel. -to really enjoy yourself doing some old-school style e-wrestling.
