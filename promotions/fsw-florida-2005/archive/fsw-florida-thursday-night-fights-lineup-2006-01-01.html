Thursday Night Fights Lineup, 29 Jan 2006

Jan 1, 2006, 12:00:00 AM

----------------------- THURSDAY NIGHT FIGHTS Official Line-Up ----------------------- Florida State Wrestling is coming off the heels of one of the most controversial episodes of TNF in its short history. Richardson hopes to restore order, and has ordered both Elijah and Hantu Penyardin to be banned from The Basement! Will TNF play host to any unwelcome guests? Tune in, January 20th! -Tim Rivers vs. "Big Nasty" Eric James ERIC JAMES has been nothing short of unstoppable in his short tenure in the FSW. Standing in his way? That loveable scamp, TIM RIVERS! Will Rivers be the one to stop this machine... or will he be another brick in the wall? -"Corrupted Morals" Domanick McLeary vs. Billy Iakika BILLY IAKIKA is coming off a huge win at Magic City Mayhem, though he faces another challenge in DOMANIC MCLEARY, a foul mouthed drunkard who's wounds are still healing after a fireball to the face courtesy of Hantu Penyardin. Will Iakika's momentum propel him to victory or will McLeary score the upset? -"American Jesus" David Randall vs. Vincent Ivey A matchup of two very solid members of the roster. DAVID RANDALL, though hampered by a loss to Billy Iakika, will do everything in his power to take out outspoken newcomer VINCENT IVEY. -"Lemon and Lime" Larry Mulberry vs. Paris LeClair This match is taking place on orders of Commissioner Gerry Richardson, we'll see the huge rematch from Magic City Mayhem. Instead of the TV title being on the line, it'll be the rights to fight for the Sunshine State title at Romp in the Swamp. In one corner, the Canadian sensation sweeping the nation, PARIS LECLAIR. In the other, the webmaster of cool.com and current Television champion, LARRY MULBERRY! Expect one helluva match. -An update on the #1 contendership for the Television title.
