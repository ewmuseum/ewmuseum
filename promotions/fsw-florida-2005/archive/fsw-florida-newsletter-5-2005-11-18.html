------------------------------- FSW NEWSLETTER Issue #5 ------------------------------- In this update... 1. A Note from the Boss 2. Roster 3. Tournament Brackets 4. Rankings 5. Magic City Mayhem Lineup 6. Deadlines --------------------- A NOTE FROM ME --------------------- I know the card was short, but there was a ton of squash matches on it. In fact, every match was a squash except the main event... maybe I shouldn't book those types of matches in one lump together like that, but what's done is done. From here on out, everything will be much more quality. No more bullshit, throwaway matches. Alright, time to look past what I thought was a rather mediocre card (feel free to disagree with me, it'll help my ego) and onto our first supercard. We're going to do things differently for MCM. There'll be a pre-show which is where all the roleplays will go. So that means no oncards/in rings unless you ask me and I okay it. The card itself will be broken into two hours, the first hour being the first three matches and the second hour being the rumble and the main event. We've got three weeks before this card so I have plenty of time to write and prepare things. A note on the first cycle. I feel I should say this, and I'm not making excuses for myself either, but I know this cycle might have been pretty short and maybe I did things in an odd way that made the cards look awkward or something.. but please, let me know. I'm new to this fedheading thing and I could use the feedback. I love all the hard work you guys are doing, and that's great - but for my sake and FSW's sake, I need ideas on how things can be done differently, or more importantly... better. As far as match writers go, I'm not too in need of them. I've got three guys writing for this card, and if you'd like to contribute - by all means, do so. But realize, this is the first supercard for FSW, so these matches will be much longer and much more epic then your normal TNF match. Keep that in mind if you feel like you want to write a match. I think that's it for now. Keep up the good work and I apologize for this last card... I know it was a stinker. ---------------------------- ROSTER ---------------------------- -"Big Nasty" Eric James: 6'3" 278 lbs. from Kansas City, Missouri -"Lemon and Lime" Larry Mulberry: 5'10" 225 lbs. from Swingerville, USA -"Straight Shooter" Tommy Spurlock: 6'4" 252 lbs. from Plano, Texas -"The Almighty" Ozymandius Jones: 6'7" 285 lbs. from Veidt, Maryland w/ Kristoff St. Livingstone: 6'0" 206 lbs. from San Diego, California -Billy Iakika: 6'2" 300 lbs. from Pensacola, Florida -Billy Sweets: 6'2" 259 lbs. from Gun Barrel City, Texas -Cabana Boy X: 5'9 201 lbs. from Hanapepe, Hawaii -Christopher Street: 6'1" 249 lbs. from New York, New York -David Randall: 6'4" 246 lbs. from Greenwich, Connecticut -Hernan Iglesias: 6'0" 225 lbs. from Barcelona, Spain (inactive: out with multiple injuries) -Jack Erickson: 6'6" 295 lbs. from Miami, Florida. -Julian Powell: 6'1" 212 lbs. from Seattle, Washington -Manaloa Fatu: 6'1" 314 lbs. from The Isle of Samoa -Matt Marvel: 5'9" 208 lbs. from Tallahassee, Florida -Paris LeClair: 5'11" 215 lbs. from Canada -Tim Rivers: 5'11" 197 lbs. from Boston, Massachusetts -Vincent Ivey: 6'1" 256 lbs. from Auburn, Alabama ----------------------- TOURNAMENT BRACKET ----------------------- Paris LeClair vs. Jack Erickson --------- Paris LeClair ------------------------------------------------------------- Paris LeClair Dan Judas vs. Hernan Iglesias ----------- Dan Judas --------------------------------------------------------------------- FIRST EVER TV CHAMPION Chris Vangar vs. Tim Rivers ------------- Tim Rivers ------------------------------------------------------------- Larry Mulberry Larry Mulberry vs. Billy Iakika ---------- Larry Mulberry ----------------------- RANKINGS ----------------------- OVERALL RANKINGS: 1. Matt Marvel ----------------------- MAGIC CITY MAYHEM Official Line-Up ----------------------- Florida State Wrestling presents its first ever supercard extravaganza, Magic City Mayhem! Coming to you live from the Sunshine Network, the FSW will crown its first ever Television champion while a number of rivalries come to a head. You don't wanna miss the Florida wrestling at its finest! Tune in, December 3rd! -Larry Mulberry vs. Paris LeClair It's the finals of the TV title tournament and the main event of our first ever supercard! LARRY MULBERRY does battle with PARIS LECLAIR, both wrestlers widely hated by men throughout the roster, but to the winner of this, that won't matter. They'll be the FSW's first ever Television champion! -11 Man Battle Royale VINCENT IVEY, TOMMY SPURLOCK, ERIC JAMES, OZYMANDIUS JONES, BILLY IAKIKA, DAVID RANDALL, MATT MARVEL, CHRIS STREETS, TIM RIVERS, loser of BILLY SWEETS/CABANA BOY X match and MANALOA FATU will all be entered into an eleven man battle royale. Their numbers in this rumble will be drawn randomly, and a new participant will be entered every two minutes. The spoils for the winner? A number one contender's spot for the Sunshine State title. -David Randall vs. Billy Iakika Not only is this a no-hold's barred match, but the winner fills the coveted vacant spot for the FSW in the NEO I tournament as BILLY IAKIKA will finally have DAVID RANDALL one-on-one, man-to-man inside the squared circle with absolutely no rules! Expect nothing but blood and hate in this one, folks! -Billy Sweets vs. Cabana Boy X BILLY SWEETS, still under the impression that CABANA BOY X was the one who insulted him at last week's TNF, will be out for blood and a future TV title shot! Two fan favorites collide in this one! -Matt Marvel/Christopher Street vs. Ozymandius Jones/Manalao Fatu Questions of CHRISTOPHER STREETS sexual orientation have arisen lately, but that has to be the last thing on his partner MATT MARVEL'S mind as the two rookies take on hard hitter MANALAO FATU and newcomer "THE ALMIGHTY" OZYMANDIUS JONES! ------------- DEADLINES ------------- First and foremost, all roleplays for this card should take place in front on an FSW banner, or somewhere relevant like that.
