The camera zooms in: Rich waves to the fans at home and Mr. Ryan tips his straw sombrero hat.] JR: Welcome, ladies and gentlemen, to another edition of In Your Face! As always, I'm Jake Ryan.. RB: And I'm Mr T! -- JR: --And the man to sitting to my right is Rich Black! RB: Right. I'm Rich Black. JR: Anyway, tonight should officially be called Quarter-Finals Bonanza. Every match taking place has some kind of implication on whether or not the men competing in them will move on in the Middle- Way brackets. RB: I gotta say Jake, this by far is the most competitive show I've seen yet for our small little-engine-that-could company. The first match of the night? JR: I know, I know. You've been screaming about it the whole time we were in rehearsal backstage. RB: It's true. For the fans at home watching, what I was saying was... The Michael Hurley versus Elvis Sunday match is opening our show-- but it's a match that could main event any other promotion. We look at it more as a highlight on an already stacked show. JR: Very true. Over the past few weeks, we've seen competitors come and go, while mainstays have been here the whole time. Brandon Marks and The Shootfighter are on their way out, and replacing them is a rising star by the name of Seth Kinsey. While mainstays like Duke Thompson, Michael Hurley, and David Lee continue heating things up.. like they have been from the very beginning. RB: Yeah, and guys like Elvis Sunday, Erik Ledger, and Seth Kinsey are definitely making their presence felt in this corner of the wrestling world. JR: Yes they are. Once things get settled down here, and some story's start developing, I have no doubt in my mind that those six men will be at the center of all the drama. But right now folks, we've got our first match to tune in for. And I'll tell ya right now, this one should be a doozy. RB: Take it away, Todd!! TB: The following match is scheduled for one fall. The winner of this match will advance to the Semi Finals of the FSW Middle-Way Tournament! In other words, they get a real shot at the Goblet of Fire! [Pizzzop!] TB: Introducing first... _____ ______ __ _____________________________________________ | ___/ ___\ \ / / Middle-Way Quarterfinals Match | |_ \___ \\ \ /\ / / Michael Hurley vs Elvis Sunday | _| ___) |\ V V / Summarized by: [name withheld] |_| |____/ \_/\_/ _______________________________________________________________________ Sunday and Hurley, both being good sportsman, shook hands right before the bell ring. After that, it was all business. They locked up, and the orange-haired munchkin shot around Hurley's waist, cinching in a rear waistlock.. he flung Hurley into the air, showing impressive strength, and took him to the mat. Still holding onto the rear waistlock that kind of looked like he was trying to bang him doggystyle, Hurley rolled onto his stomach, but Sunday kept a firm grip, and had Hurley in a front headlock. Trying to fight his way out of the hold, Hurley took several hard forearm shots to the back, that took him down to one knee. Seeing an opportunity, Elvis was like a pinball shooting into the ropes. He bounced back, cartwheeled into the air for no reason, and caught a clothesline so packed with power that the force of the blow sent him backflipping the way he came. On the offensive now, Hurley went on attack-mode. Elvis Sunday was getting up to one knee, and Hurley send him back to the mat with a drop kick. Elvis on the mat, Hurley put a few feet of distance between him and his opponent, ran forward and baseball slide-kicked him... Sunday went skittering out the ring so far the back of his body crashed into the steel guardrail. Not stopping the flow of staggering offensive moves, Hurley spring boarded onto the top rope, leapt off, and was flying into the air like one of those jumping green frogs.. Sunday moved at the last second...and Hurley missed his lily pad. The steel bar struck Hurley in the mouth, and on instinct, a spray of blood shot out of his mouth and onto some little kids in the front row who screamed "AWESOME!!" Rich Black mentioned they might want to be tested for AIDS after that. Hurley was stumbling around the outside of the ring, showing possible signs of suffering a concussion. Sunday, making it easy on the veteran, rolled him into the ring. Slowing the match down, Sunday worked Hurley's wrist over using the hammerlock. The crowd was buzzing at the possibility of Hurley being upset, and some started cheering his name... but most of the fans were torn. Should they cheer for the orange-haired afro Short Stop or the seriously serious wrestling veteran? Either way, Michael Hurley drew on the support, and fought his way back to a vertical base, his red-washed teeth and mouth dripping blood down his chin like a leaky faucet the whole time. Hurley caught Sunday with one elbow to the ribs, two elbows, THREE elbows... he attempted a front kick, but Elvis caught his leg... WHAP!! Enzuguri takes the little man down! Slightly drained, Michael Hurley was turning very pale. The pasty color of his skin was offset by the crimson tide washing over his face and chest. He leaned against the ropes for support, and the referee asked if he wanted to continue fighting. Hurley concurred. He slowly staggered over to where Sunday was.. and pulling his opponent to his feet, he grabbed him by the arm and sent him into the ropes..except Elvis Sunday held on to his wrist and reversed the momentum, sending Hurley flying into the other direction... Right where the referee was standing. PLOW! Michael Hurley bulldozed into the referee like an All-Pro middle linebacker would to a 3rd down running back. Sunday, looking confused, didn't have long to think..because a man wearing a gray hoodie and black sweat pants, conspiciously holding what looked like a noose, slid into the ring. Elvis had no time to react, the man grabbed him by the tights, lifted him into the air, and dropped him throat first across the top rope. Holding his throat, he didn't have time to catch his breath, the man bounced off the other side of the ring and came back around-- sending Sunday flying into the air with a tough looking lariat. The man didn't stop there. He grabbed Hurley, who was still extremely dazed, and set him on the top turnbuckle (with Hurley's back facing the ring), and then climbed to the second rope, with his back to Hurley. The man wrapped his right arm around Hurley's neck, and then executed a vicious reverse neckbreaker off the top rope, leaping out into the ring and landing with Hurley's neck across his right shoulder. The man, waving the noose in the air, pulled Hurley to his feet and wrapped the noose around his neck before flinging him over the top rope, using the ropes and the noose as an effective noose. Hurley, gagging, choking and fighting for air, was only let go when the man saw the referee start to stir. He let Hurley drop to the outside of the ring, before going out himself and rolling him back into the ring. He rolled Sunday on top of the Hurley, and hopped out of the ring just as the referee saw Sunday covering Hurley, and slowly made the three count. Winner: Elvis Sunday via pinfall! JR: Wait a minute. I recognize that guy! That's Caleb Walters, otherwise known as The Hangman, of THW fame! What's he doing here? And what's his beef with Michael Hurley? RB: I think we're about to find out. [Walters paces around the ring, as the fans continue to boo. Several referees and a paramedic have rushed to the scene, attending to the now unconscious Michael Hurley. As the chorus of negative reaction continues to rain down, Caleb Walters steps outside of the ring and forcefully grabs the microphone from the announcer. He slowly strides back into the ring, and a slight grin appears on his face.] Walters: Well...first things first...it seems as though an introduction is in order. For those of you who don't know me, and you all soon will, my name is Caleb Walters, and they call me "The Hangman." That name is well earned, as you've all just witnessed here tonight! [Someone yells "You suck!" from the crowd, and Walters continues..] Walters: Now...I've been in professional wrestling for only a little over a year, and I've been bouncing around from territory to territory, trying to make a name for myself. I've decided I'm sick of all the two bit promoters in Texas and the Southwest, who can't stay open for more than a few months, before the Hangman's attention makes them close their doors! So, I heard there's this nice little promotion running shows here in Florida. Well, some sunshine and sand sounds pretty good about now. That's why I'm here in FSW...to bring the sun and the sky down! I'm here to be the darkness in the sunlight! I'm here to bring my own brand of justice to Florida State Wrestling! [He paces over towards the now limp noose, hanging over the top rope, and clutches it in his left hand.] Walters: Now, everyone's asking, "Why did you attack Mr. Hurley for no reason?" The fact is, to answer that question, there was plenty of reason! I chose my first victim here in FSW to make an impact, and it's evident that I already have! Now, let this example serve as a warning to others! If you get in the Hangman's way, your fate is going to lie at the end of this rope! [Walters grins sadistically, as the fans continue to react.] Walters: Now...what you'll see me doing next, I don't know yet. I seem to have interrupted this little "Middle Way Tournament" that's been going on! That's fine...I'm issuing an open challenge, right here, right now! If anyone thinks they have the sand to step into the ring with the Hangman, I'm more than willing to lock horns, any place, any time! Congratulations, Florida, you've just met the biggest gun in the business! *PLUNK!!!* [With that, Walters throws down the microphone and raises his arm in the air, holding his signature noose over his head.] JR: Wow. Just wow.. RB: Well, well, well. Caleb Walters here in Florida State Wrestling. If Hurley thought the only thing he was fighting was old age and hair loss, he's got one of the toughest Texans this sport has ever seen to think about! JR: Just think what this will do to bolster our position as the finest wrestling federation to ever come out of Florida. Granted, wrestling isn't a sport that Florida can much brag about.. RB: No. Football is. JR: Right, but what I'm saying is, a guy like Caleb Walters really brings a lot to the table. I'm sure with that noose of his, and that giant sized chip on his shoulders, Michael Hurley won't be the only guy looking to take his head off. RB: One man who's been taking heads off since he arrived here is up next. I'm talking about Duke Thompson. Tore a man's entire leg apart, destroyed Adrien Cochrane and Magnus Noble at the same time, and has won every match he's been in since he came here... he's up against a man who may be stupid, may be kind of Down Syndrome- y, but he's no joke in the ring. Yes, I'm talking about Frank Dylan James. And that's next! The bout opened with Duke Thompson telling FDJ to take his best shot. FDJ smacked him across the face with a walloping closed-fist that the referee didn't even bother warning him about. "The Shit" just laughed him off. FDJ, surprised a bit, took another shot..and another..and ANOTHER...but his attempts were futile. Duke Thompson didn't even stagger back enough to stop laughing. Almost completely dumbfounded, FDJ cocked his arm back to unload a cannon sized fist into Duke's face.. But "The Shit" caught the fist mid-strike. And head-butted the bejeezus out of the slightly moronic newcomer. Duke Thompson, laughing like some giant lumberjack, scooped FDJ into his arms like he were a bundle of logs and brought his baack crashing against his knee, successfully executing a disgustingly potent backbreaker. Frank Dylan James was rolling on the mat in pain, but Thompson told him it wasn't over. He pulled him into a standing headscissors, lifted him off the mat, and damn near powerbombed him through the canvas! Casually dropping to one knee, "The Shit" made the cover. One... Two... Three... You know the rest. Winner: Duke Thompson via pinfall! [Cut back to Rich Black and Jake Ryan.] JR: Wow... That man is scary good. [Suddenly holding his earpiece.] Folks, I'm getting word that the semi-finals is about to be processed backstage..and we're to take you back there. [Cut backstage. The Nuclear Madman, flat-footedly marches into Gerry Richardson's office-- he manuevers over to where the final lineups have been written out for the Middle-Way Tournament. Looking over the lineup, he scratches his mask. The camera switches vantage points so the viewer can see what he sees: Duke Thompson vs Elvis Sunday vs. [hastily scribbled on] Adrien Cochrane. If he wanted to make some noise, he should have went to the WFWA Council and talked to them about the way he was treated. Sneaking in to an FSW and actually forging his name on to official documents is bad business for everyone. RB: I couldn't care less about any of that, Jake. What's got me super stoked is the thought that Adrien Cochrane is going to get his opportunity to face the bear that maimed him, Duke Thompson. Throw Elvis Sunday into the equation, and that match will own my home base, officially. JR: Well, I just hope the kid knows what he's doing. I heard he's pretty big down in one of those other WFWA places, but he's got the prospect of facing one of the most dominant men to come into this business in the last couple of years. He could seriously get hurt. RB: We know that, Jake. But that's why you fight the battles...because nobody knows what can happen. JR: Yeah, I guess you're right. Anyway, it's time for the next match of the night.. [Cut to Todd Beckenstein, cue card and microphone in hand, about to introduce the next two combatants..] _____ ______ __ _____________________________________________ | ___/ ___\ \ / / Middle-Way Quarterfinals Match | |_ \___ \\ \ /\ / / Erik Ledger vs David Lee | _| ___) |\ V V / Summarized by: [name withheld] |_| |____/ \_/\_/ _______________________________________________________________________ David Lee and Erik Ledger started off the match by circling around each other, just trying to develop a feel for one another. just sensing where they're skills are at. Lee struck first. He powered up like some Super Saiyan and blasted Ledger's ass with a leaping sidekick that sent him sprawling into the corner. It didn't even look like a wrestling move, more like something out of a Bruce Lee movie. Lee didn't waste time, he moved right in on Ledger, but caught a few striking chops to the chest. Staggering backwards, Lee was the victim of a running clothesline that sent him hard to the mat. Showing his mean streak, Ledger dug the heel of his boot into Lee's forehead, opening up a small wound; that blood trickled out of. The referee warned Erik about that, but his focus was completely on the downed David Lee. Applying a reverse hammerlock, Lee showed his alert technical prowess by somersaulting forward, reversing the hammerlock with an armbar, kicking Ledger's legs out from under him, and allowing the armbar to flow into an STF submission. In an awesome amount of pain, Ledger _refused_ to tap out. The referee asked him over and over again if he submits, but Ledger told him verbatim: "I'm not crying uncle, so get the _fuck_ out of my face, you rat bastard!" Taken aback, the referee gave Erik Ledger plenty of space. Crawling.. inch by inch, foot by foot, Ledger reached out..mere fingertip away from those precious strands of safety..from freedom from the STF...but that didn't mean a thing to David Lee. Using his lower body as a propeller, he wrenched back and pulled Ledger into the danger zone of the middle of the ring..pulling back harder than ever on the STF. Ledger had no choice. He was out of oxygen, out of energy, and out of luck. His hands tapped the canvas signifying victory for David Lee. Winner: David Lee via submission! [Immediately, the camera cuts backstage. Gerry Richardson is strolling backstage, and he sees The Nuclear Madman waddling his massive frame from the other side. Gerry, smiling serenely, calls out to The Madman.] GR: Oi! Madman! Ya take care of that for me? TNM: Yeah, boss. I was meaning to talk to ya.. GR: What's up, big guy? [The Madman trots over to Gerry, holding the lineup that has the word "PROCESSED" stamped across it in red ink. Coming in close, The Madman holds the paper up and speaks.] TNM: Why'd ya rebook the Adrien Cochrane kid? I thought you said good- riddance to him.. GR: What are you talking about? TNM: Right here.. [The Madman holds the paper up to Gerry's face, showing him the name Adrien Cochrane hastily scribbled on.] TNM: It says right there you added Cochrane to the list. GR: WHAT?! TNM: Uh.. it says you added Adrien Cochrane to the list. GR: I know that. Who..what..how..THAT'S NOT EVEN MY HAND WRITING!! TNM: [gulp.] GR: Oh man.. [shaking his head.] Somebody is going to DIE!!! Madman, let's roll! Whealdon was backed against the ropes, but Kinsey showed no mercy. He used his legs to propel his shoulder into Petey's abdomen, and did so over and over again... until Petey was gasping for air. Doubled over in pain, Whealdon was the victim of a tornado DDT. Kinsey though, didn't go for the cover, he pulled Whealdon to the center of the ring and climbed the ropes himself -- going for The Spotlight 450. He leapt into the air, but as he went for the landing, Petey Whealdon rolled out of the way... Luckily, Seth Kinsey has amazing agility and landed on his feet. With Whealdon on the ground and Kinsey standing up, Kinsey smashed his boot into Whealdon's face: a bruise on his cheekbone immediately forming. Whealdon dazed and only partially conscious, was propped to his feet by Seth Kinsey -- who put him in a standing headscissors, lifted him up, and hit him with a spiked piledriver, aka The Widowmaker 360! The match at this point, was textbook. Seth went for the cover, and easily got the three count. [Ding! Ding! Ding!] Winner: Seth Kinsey by pinfall [Seth calls for a mic.] SK: So.. [Seth clears his throat.] SK: This is the best you can throw at me? [Seth feigns boredom.] SK: I'm bored. [Seth shrugs.] SK: Normally, I'd accept that big redneck oaf's challenge, what's-his- name... Caleb Waffles.. whatever. But I'm busy doing things that actually matter in this business - like winning matches and becoming glorious. Not blindsiding old washed up hogs like Michael Hurley. I know you got your little thing going with Hurley now Hang Ten Man, and no disrespect meant to ya, 'cuz I honestly don't give a lick of dog shit about you enough to insult you.. buuut.. the handsome guy with the microphone in his hand? Moi? Hehhehehehehehehheh... The most electrifying man in this sport today. Not you. Me.
