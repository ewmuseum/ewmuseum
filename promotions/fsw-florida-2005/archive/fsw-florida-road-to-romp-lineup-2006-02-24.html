Thursday Night Fights Line-up: Road to Romp in the Swamp (24 Feb 2006)

Feb 24, 2006, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Florida State Wrestling presents the Road to Romp in the Swamp! Possibly the only NEO I federation to bring you intermittent wrestling so close to the DNA tournament! See your favorite wrestlers live! Tune in, March 9th! -Mr. Robinson vs. Xanadu Xerxes Luxe The debut of two literal giants sees the very mysterious MR. ROBINSON take on the very, very large XXL! There's more then 650 lbs between the two. -Jack Erickson vs. Dazz In one of the most shocking moments to happen to date, JACK ERICKSON returned to the FSW at the side of Elijah. But right now, he'll be taking on the street smart, and very intense DAZZ! Expect sparks to fly! -Billy Iakika vs. Mojo Rizen The usually fun loving BILLY IAKIKA snapped last week, bashing Billy Sweets repeatedly with a steel chair, and inciting the fury of the fans- though Iakika couldn't care less. He's completely focused on taking his rightful spot in the FSW, and he'll have a tough task of doing that against the rookie sensation MOJO RIZEN, who's hot off two victories already. -Brett Hunter vs. Tim Rivers The "Ultimate Insurance Policy" BRETT HUNTER takes on "Possible Rookie of the Year" TIM RIVERS in a true David vs. Goliath match. Rivers has already bested men like Domanic McLeary and Eric James, though not so cleanly, will he be able to add this monster of a man to that lofty list? Plus! Eric James has demanded his comeuppance! Will he finally get it? Who will replace Paris LeClair in the Romp in the Swamp main event? What will Billy Sweets have to say about being attacked the way he was by Billy Iakika? And what will Billy Iakika have to say about his actions? All this, and more!
