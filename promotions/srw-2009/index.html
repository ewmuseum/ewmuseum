Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

SRW's own founding post, 13 July 2009, frames the promotion as the work of two classmates, Hugo and Pedro, billed as “the toughest in the class” at a school called Soares dos Reis — when the two of them decided to start a wrestling federation there, in the post's own words, “the whole class showed up.” Seventeen wrestlers filled the founding roster, each listed with a height and weight; one, Gustavo, is marked injured before ever competing. Three championships already had holders before any show existed: the SRW Championship (Cristiano), the SRW Heavyweight Championship (Carona), and the SRW Tag Team Championship (Miguel and Zé Miguel). Eight pay-per-views were named for the future, among them “Ginásio Show,” “Extreme Árvore,” and “Balneário to the Max.”

A same-day news update names Luís Rasga as the promotion's general manager.

No card, match or title change is on record. The founding post's own closing line calls the whole project “just for fun,” and nothing further survives to say whether it ever ran a show.
