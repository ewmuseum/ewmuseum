Allstar Wrestling of Motor City (AWMC) was founded in December 1997 by [name withheld] (known for his characters "The Legend" Sean Darring and
"Fantasy" Brett Young) and has been a well-regarded fed (#4 top fed in
1998 RSPWF Source Awards, various RSPWF 200 nominees, etc.) during his tenure. The fed, so the storyline goes, was formed when TDW (Top Dog
Wrestling) ran out of money. So they moved to Detroit and renamed the fed Allstar Wrestling of Motor City. The AWMC is based out of Detroit,
Michigan. In June 1999, [name withheld] stepped down from running the fed as well as all administration, and new bookers took over the fed. Several new policies came in. Handlers are limited to one wrestler each, and all members have a handler list to refer to. You're encouraged to work with other handlers to develop angles, alliances and feuds. If you want to form a tag team, you'll need to work with another handler to do that. We took this policy to encourage more community among handlers and to take e-w back to the old school. We realize some people are more comfortable forming teams with friends they've made in other feds, and you're welcome to bring in people to help.
2.Who runs the AWMC? *On-Camera Staff* Announcers/Administrators: John Stargel - He has a huge background in Broadcasting. Starting out in the World League Football announcing. The old EUWF signed Stargel to there broadcasting team. He quickly adjusted and made a name for himself there. After it closed though he joined several indy feds broadcasting for them. Finally the TDW signed him and he stayed with the AWMC. He is the face of the two Explosion broadcasters. He was recently appointed
AWMC interim president. Vince "The Prince" Richardson - Used to work old AWMC Friday show with Daniel Wright, but has moved to the Explosion show. Generally favors heels, but can call straight commentating.
Daniel Wright - This former National Hockey League commentator made the switch to commentating wrestling matches in the late 80s. A behind the scenes man for one of the largest wrestling federations, he recently re-signed with AWMC to announce the Motor City Madness show.
Marc Mayhem - Marc Mayhem was formerly a face roving reporter and fill-in commentator for the closed Ultimate Championship Wrestling and
Championship Wrestling of Los Angeles. Marc has a good knowledge of wrestling and has been a long time fan of the sport. He was born in
Philadelphia and is the brother of Jeremy Mayhem, who he worked with in CWLA. However, Jeremy landed a lucrative job with Great Lakes Championship Wrestling and ditched his older sibling. Marc's since become critical of fan favorites and gotten a little bitter. He works with Wright on Motor City Madness.
Henry Paxson - Paxson was hired by the TDW, he stayed on when we moved to Detroit. He is on the board of Executives and is the spokesman.
Referees: Mark Barnett - Barnett is a ref whose experience goes over eight organizations. He was recently hired as the AWMC's head official.
Barnett takes nothing from any wrestler and does his job well. He prefers the traditional wrestling as opposed the latest string of hardcore.
Robby Rocker - He wrestled in alot of indy feds but never made it to the big leagues. He then tried Broadcasting but it never suited him quite well. Rocker served shortly as AWMC commissioner, but was moved to refereeing big matches. He tends to be hard-nosed about the rules.
Bill Cartwright - Cartwright is a new official to AWMC. His specialty is hardcore matches. He's a huge fan of the new wave of wrestling and likes to see a good, bloody brawl. Cartwright allows the use of foreign objects to a certain extent and isn't one to disqualify wrestler unless in extreme circumstances, like interference.
Bingo Horner - Horner was one of the first AWMC referees. He's somewhat of a pushover to the wrestlers and won't call for a disqualification often out of fear. Horner doesn't care what type of match he calls, but he is the gimmick match specialist.
Claudia Stevenson - A former commentator, Stevenson is AWMC's first female referee. She's a no-nonsense ref who won't take slack from any wrestler and will call for disqualification if she's touched or pushed around. Good textbook knowledge of the sport.
3.What titles are there in AWMC? -AWMC Title (This is THE title of the federation. The best overall) -AWMC Great Lakes Heritage Title (This is the equivalent to a United States or IC Title, and it is the second-best title in the Heavyweight division) -AWMC Motor City Title (This is the title less important than US or IC, but more than TV or European.) -AWMC Tag Team Titles (Name says it all no?) 4.Why no National or World Title? There is no National or World title because we are a REGIONAL federation. ################################## II. Rules and Regulations ################################## 1.How are the Matches determined? Roleplaying Heat/Angles (You do not have to be Heel to get heat) Strats Effort (If you put forth effort, you'll eventually get a push.) 2.What are the roleplay rules? Two flashes and one in-ring per week is the limit on roleplaying, though one great roleplay can sometimes beat out two average ones. Numerous RPs sent in for one match will be frowned upon. Several RPs, whether weak or strong in quality, inconvenience bookers and break the rules. Focus instead on producing the best roleplays you can within the limit.
Descriptive, consistent (with the character), creative roleplaying ("RPs", "flashes") helps bring your character wins. We used to have a mega "RP Guide" to close every single loophole some of our more disruptive members would squirrel out like porch rats to get away with. For now, we'll go with some ground rules. Credit these to
GLCW (thanks Andj): a. The occasional curse word is ok, but try and censor it out using a <bleep> or <censor> or whatever. b. The Happy Clause - I can make some of the people happy all of the time, but I cannot make everyone happy all of the time. Keep that in mind at all times, please.
c. The Spunky Clause - From here on out, we're shooting for realism. If I don't think something you write would get aired on national TV, then don't send it to me.
d. The Egomaniacal Tyrant Clause - The basic premise of this rule is "No Bragging Allowed". Anyone who develops a serious chip on their shoulder will go through what's known as a "humbling process". I don't care how many titles you have won outside of AWMC, or how many people you have defeated outside of the federation -- if you're coming into AWMC, don't expect an insta-push.
e. Character Assassination - The basic premise of this rule is that a person is not allowed to attack another character in a way that the other character has potential to be 'ruined', without getting the permission of that character's handler first. This includes any and all attacks that would leave a character injured, overly humiliated, or permanently scarred.

The Diggs Clause - AWMC administration reserves the right to refuse flashes of inappropriate material (i.e. racism, personal shots, various retardedness) as well as the right to refuse to count the same inappropriate material towards a match. If it's refused, that's the end of it. Anyone who tries to talk me into running an inappropriate roleplay will be canned REAL quick-like. This is just a game. It's not life. If you lost, you lost because I thought you should've. If you wonder if you were out-rp'ed, you were. If you think you need some more heat, you do. If you take it out on me, don't expect to get any reaction, except reference to this rule. If you're seriously ticked, then you're seriously fired. If you've ever been a fedhead, you know that it can be a hard job, and the less aggravation I have to suffer, the better.
3.How do I get matches? a.You get signed randomly to a match b.You challenge someone in a roleplay and send a 'formal' request c.You send in the 'formal' request for the match
It is the bookers' discretion on whether that match is signed or not. 4.Are sneak attacks allowed? Attacks and interference are allowed in moderation and administration has the right to refuse a sneak attack. Try to keep it somewhat realistic in the context of wrestling. All members are expected to be professionals and 'sell' attacks and incorporate them into flashes, strats, etc. However, if you do have problems with the way your character was portrayed in an attack (used in a flash without your permission; portrayed in a way inconsistent with his character, etc.), e-mail the President and AWMC administration can decide if it's worth acting upon. Attacks and interference will never be used to affect the outcome of a match, unless (among other reasons) the party being attacked is already booked to lose, one of the parties in the match has something developed, or (extremely rarely) if an angle might benefit from the attack/interference. Don't ask to cost someone a match; if a member has worked on a match, they'll get consideration before attack/interference requests. You can ask for your attack to be considered if the person is booked to lose. However, be aware it might not get used.
################################## III. Events and Shows. ################################## 1.What shows are there? AWMC Motor City Madness Usually: Wednesdays Written: Summary Matches: Dark, with an occasional live bout Announcers: Daniel Wright and Marc Mayhem AWMC Explosion Usually: Sundays Written: Play-by-play Matches: Live Announcers: John Stargel and Vince "The Prince" Richardson Updates are out usually after cards Cheap Shots (RP Packets) come out every few days 2.Are there PPV's? We've done 'em. Cruise Control is our signature PPV, in April. 3.Where are the shows held? Explosion is held in Joe Louis Arena and various Michigan arenas. Motor City Madness is held around the state in smaller (1-3,000 seat) venues. 4.What's available at shows? While AWMC is a regional, it recently signed a deal with Fox Sports and has some new production equipment, including a video wall monitor, handcams and other stuff.
################################## IV. Titles ################################## 1.How will the titles be decided? Usually tournaments. 2.How often will the titles be defended? As often as we book'em. 3.How can I get a title shot? -If the champion challenges you and I see fit. -If you're one of the top three active contenders for the belt. -If I see fit you deserve one. ################################## V. Match Info. ################################## 1.How often can my guy wrestle? There is no limit. Sometimes he'll wrestle twice on the same card. Sometimes twice a week. 2.Where are the matches held? In a regular match, they're held in an 18'x18' square ring. 3.Can I add stipulations to my matches? Yes, within reason. Remember we don't have very much money, so take it easy, or it's coming out of your paycheck. ;) Please, no flaming cage death matches in the third match of a feud.
4.What are the regular rules to an average match? Standard wrestling rules. ################################## VII.
