He's one of the founders of Yakuza, archrival of Rob Cole, and undeniably one of the hottest stars in this promotion. Like him or not, he is Kuwasi Shakur and he's made a major impact on the AWMC.
[Shakur enters the trash-littered ring amid heavy boos.] Shakur: I gotta a helluva lot o' people to cap tonight, so I'm gonna get this started fast.
[Cameras show various anti-Shakur signs in the crowd going up: "Bomb Shakur." "End the Discussion" etc.] Shakur: Seems like a buncha people have been wringing your hands about the
Don without coming to my face about it. You ask 'how come Shakur is in another title match?' You ask 'what gives Shakur the right to be there?'
You say 'all Shakur does it bust heads, and he has no right to be fighting for the strap.' I'll make it simple and use small words for you.
[Shots of signs everywhere.] Shakur: This [holding up the bat] doesn't make the man. This [pointing the bat's business end to his chest] is why I'm here. This is why I'm the top-rated dog around here while a buncha y'all hatin' little baboons are having to do cleanup and teardown of this place for an extra dollar. I end the unbeaten streaks. I destroy the heroes. And I've been at war with every man in this organization since day one. You need a reason for me facing
Cole tonight? Look at the Don, recognize the mob, and you got your answer.
[Cameras show contingent of Yakuza fans cheering. They're drowned out by the many boos.] Shakur: Most weakly -- because they're too inept to be much more -- are a bunch of these Excess clowns. Top of that list are the Action Packed of
Excess -- Eclipse, who's done nothing but doesn't think the Don's earned his match with Cole… like anyone cares, and Cruise, who still wants respect for me knocking him out. Neither of you have a leg to stand on because you're no-talent bums but ya keep running your mouths like you can back anything up. Keep ya day jobs. And last but not least, Jared Crush.
[Big pop at Crush's mention.] Shakur: A few days ago, I'm watching AWMC TV and I hear Crush badmouthing
Trey Lawrenze as dead weight. You don't even want me to go there about your milk carton boy Damion Rage, do you?
[Many boos.] Shakur: Y'know, Crush -- and I'mma call you Crush because Silk's gotta be the most ridiculous thing I ever heard -- you can call my man a lot of things. Let me let you in on one thing he's not. He's not the one who was
Mr. Hardcore ten days ago and became Mr. Sellout ten days later.
[Crowd heat picks up.] Shakur: It's funky to hear you talk about how you needed this change of yours. You had titles. You had money. You had respect. Tagging with you,
Crush, I came to respect you as a man who could put 'em up and get down and dirty as only a few men can. Now I look at you -- new look, new style, pretty new image -- and I say that's a damn shame. It was a shame enough to watch you get dragged down by an group of inferior soliders and real dead weight. Now you up and pull a Freaky Ti -- this is what's best for my image and what people cheer for, I'll do it even if it looks retarded. Again, a damn shame. But that's your problem -- just don't go insulting a crew like
Yakuza until you look in your own backyard and the ever-dangerous Rage -- a hazard to himself and soundproof rooms everywhere.
[Boos.] Shakur: Now the man of the evening, Bob Cole. [laughs] I don't need to be let into your head, Cole. I'm already there and been squattin' for weeks. If that ain't bought and sold, Bobby, nothing is.

Behind the Mic Award winner for all it matters, boy. Fact of the matter is

Maybe you won the AWC strap and now big, bad Bobby Cole's back to the pile of jelly the Underground punked regularly. So's seeing Yakuza killed the Underground, I guess it's my job to take over where daddies of your past left off. Reality is that's been going on for months. Conclusion comes tonight.
[Boos pick up.] Shakur: Who else we got? Justin B.? [Cheers from the crowd] Shakur: Yeah. Saint Humble gives me a lecture. I don't sound grateful enough to you, Mr. Right? Tough luck. You can play wistful all you want. I harbor no illusions about this place. I get no teary-eyed idealism about what I come to do. I kick people in the teeth -- that's what they pay me for. You want a big smile? Kiss my ass and grin, boy, then go play the misty morning and apple pie somewhere else. For me, this is business,
Cole  is mine and you stand in the way. The people want blood, and the Don provides. That ain't gratitude. That's reality.
[Camera shots of some Yakuza fans.] Shakur: Like it or not, Justin, you *are* beneath me. Hell, you're not fit to carry the Don's bags to the hotel. But if you get in my face, son, I'm gonna kick your head in, no questions asked. Now take that to the glee club and smoke it, kid.
[More boos.] Shakur: And, finally, the man who's gonna bear Yakuza's love children, Paul
Styles. [Surprising cheers for Styles.] Shakur: It's sad to listen to you claim my injuring Cole as your own, Pauly. You laid on the mat, a broken man, as Cole climbed that ladder. *I* took out Cole. *I* put him AND YOU in the hospital. *I* did Cole, not you.
And no matter how often you repeat a lie, the Don's still got the skin in his living room, not you.
[Camera shots of signs.] Shakur: One battle does not the war make, Styles. We haven't even started. Night two, I want you so bad I can already smell your stench on the canvas. The Don speaks, Pauly, and we're hanging the king. End of discussion. [Small hardcore pop amid boos as Shakur exits.] Stargel: Well, this sure starts up the evening.  Lets get to our first match of the night.  A Barbwire match between Mr. Excitement Alex Extreme, and Asylum member Twizted.
