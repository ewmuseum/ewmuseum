The war between Yakuza and Underground continues to grow, and AWMC Hevayweight Champion Takada also has his problems with Top Contender Sinsiter. Will there be more casualites in this war? Sinsiter versus Takada and Cruise Control III could be one of the most emotional confrontations in AWMC History. Explosion: Noble Returns [New York City, NY] - True to the rumours of several net reports, former AWMC tag Champion "Losse Cannon" Priest Noble returned to AWMC on Epxlosion when he made his presence felt following the Great Lakes Heritage Tag Team Title match between Doom Machines and Southern Posse. Noble was released from AWMC last Fall after suffering a knee injury during a texas Tornado Match between Kindred Blood and Malicious Intent. Noble has yet to make his intentions clear. Explosion: Doom Machines Capture Gold; Southern Posse, SeX & Guns To Settle Score at Cruise Control III [New York City, NY] - AWMC Newcomers Doom Machines scored gold in their first match, defeating Great Lakes Heritage Tag Team Champions Southern Posse. SeX & Guns, who made earlier comments in the show towards their longtime rivals Southern Posse vowed to amke their rpesence, and did...by garnering front-row seats. Doom machines go on to face Assault & Battery, a team they defeated last week on Explosion, at Cruise Control III for the Great Lakes Heritage Tag Team Titles, and Southern Posse will collide with SeX & Guns in a Last Dance, Loser-Leaves-Town Rumble. Stipulations: Neither of these two teams will EVER meet again in AWMC, and the losers will leave AWMC. Motor City Madness: Threat Score Big, More Tag Team Competition Forms [Uniondale, NY] - The Threat scored a huge Non-Title pinfall victory over Ujimoto Mizukawa and Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur, as Wright slipped Sebastion a a pair of brass knuckles to score the pin over Mizukawa in the middle of a Tag Team melee. And a new alliance formed as "Top Gun" ben Tuckwell and "Iron Will" Neal Jennings combined forces to dispose of Jessie Muldoon and Tony Virtue. Motor City Madness: Contagion & Chrono Synthesis In Cahoots? [Uniondale, NY] - A possible payoff occured on Motor City Madness when Contagion seemingly hired the services of Chrono Synthesis to attack The Threat and Ujimoto Mizukawa and Juwasi Shakur. Reports are the teams shook hands backstage. Could a new alliance be forming? Motor City Madness: Virtue Injured, Pulled From Cruise Control III [Uniondale, NY] - After absorbing a Plancha from Ben Tuckwell, "Pure Athelete" Tony Virtue suffered a shattered ankle, requiring surgery. Virtue requested and was granted his release from AWMC, and has been pulled from Cruise Control III.
