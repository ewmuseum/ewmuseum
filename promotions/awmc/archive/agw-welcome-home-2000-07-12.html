[The flowing strands of "Perfect Stranger" by Deep Purple rips over the arena speakers. The arena plunges into darkness, but a yellow haze fills the entrance area. Gold stars line the aisle to the ring, along with these letters.]
_S_I_M_P_L_Y_ [Then a gold laser and a spinning circular light illuminates the area. From the back, two men walk out into the sea of anticipation. The two men stand at the edge of the entrance area soaking up the crowd reaction that the crowd is giving. The letters reading _SIMPLY_ now switch over.]
_F_L_A_W_L_E_S_S_ [The beat of the music picks up as the arena lights slowly brighten, revealing the familiar faces of "Mr. Attitude" Kenny Kopecky and Hunter
Douglas. Kopecky, wearing his usual black long tights with barbwire design, red knee pads, with red talons bounces with every step. His brown hair is soaked as he walks to the ring with determination.
Hunter Douglas, wearing his black short tights with a large gold triangle on the back with the word "DOUGLAS" in black and white letters. His black talons bounce with every step as does Kopecky's talons. His knees are heavily protected as thick knee pads cover them. His short blond hair reflects the flashing lightbulbs of the cameras from people in the crowd.]
[*FLASH* *FLASH* *FLASH* *FLASH*] [The two reach the ring at nearly the same moment. Kopecky springboards into the ring, while Douglas takes his entry under the bottom rope as he slides in, hopping to his feet. The two walk over to opposite turnbuckles and stare out into the crowd, listening to the pop that the crowd is giving. Hunter
Douglas then grabs the microphone from the ring announcer as he exits the ring.] HUNTER DOUGLAS - We are just _ONE_ more away! We are just one more match away from being the _VICTORS_ of the AGW Double Elimination Tournament. And hey, in the end...we'll be one hundred and fifty thousands dollars richer!
But with that one more match, comes two amigos. [Douglas looks about the crowd as some boo, while even more cheer as Douglas continues to speak.]
HUNTER DOUGLAS - Yo..._S.A!_ Yeah I'm talking to you two chumps in the back, Jose Calvera and Otis Lonnegan. We've been watching your work...not too shabby boys. But one problem..._IT ISN'T GOOD ENOUGH!_ That is...if you plan on beating us.
[Douglas points to himself and Kopecky who leans against the turnbuckle to the right of him.]
HUNTER DOUGLAS - You see Mr. Lonnegan and you, Little Boy Calvera, people like us..._COME ONCE A LIFETIME!_ But uh, people like you..._COME A DIME A
DOZEN!_ [The crowd pops from Douglas' comment, as Douglas smiles from the reaction. He raises his hand and continues.] HUNTER DOUGLAS - You see, you haven't quite experienced pain, nor a challenge. But with us, you will. And a whole lot of it as well. We fully expect to see how good you two chumps are, and I am one hundred percent sure that you'll _EXPERIENCE_ how good we are. And once it is all over, and that final bell as rung, while you are carrying your asses to the back, you will realize what it takes to defeat us. And you didn't have one bit of what it takes to accomplish that.
[Douglas looks over at Kopecky, how stares back with determination. Douglas tosses the microphone to Kopecky who outstretches his right hand and catches it. He lifts the microphone to his mouth as he walks out of the corner.]
KENNY KOPECKY - Otis Lonnegan...Jose Calvera...you have made it to the end of the road, the road. As much as I know you needed that $150 grand for clothes, food, and whatever else you Hispanics use your money for, well...how can I say this, Hunter. _TOO BAD!_
[Kopecky laughs smugly as the crowd gives no reaction.] KENNY KOPECKY - So listen up _IMMIGRATION SENSATION_, your little luck streak has come to a screeching halt, as you finally meet some _REAL
COMPETITION_ here in the AGW. When you boys ride that train back past the border to tell your family about your experiences, you will tell them that you met the _ULTIMATE_ opponent. You will tell them that we were _SIMPLY
PERFECT!_ [Kopecky pauses and chuckles a bit.] KENNY KOPECKY - But just like you have always been, you'll be _WRONG!_ Because you two and _EVERYONE_ else out there know...that we are _ABSOLUTELY_..._SIMPLY FLAWLESS!_ [Kopecky drops the microphone as Hunter Douglas climbs out of the ring. Kopecky shoots over the top rope to the floor as Douglas walks emotionless up the aisle. While Kopecky continues to taunt the fans, with most of them cheering them on. Both teams getting fired up for their match at Sandblast and wanting to enter with momentum tore into each other from the opening bell. After eliminating each other from their previous tournament matches it was all on the line here.After an earlier advantage by The Highflyers the SWA team took control. Ken Stanley was on the offensive end as Billy Higgins took the deffensive. Higgins connected with several high impact power moves including a Jumping Piledriver which was followed by a Falling Powerbomb. Stanley clinched his neck in a great deal of pain but Higgins and Slayer would not let up. After several tags in and out Higgins hit a Swinging Neckbreaker which he tried to follow with a
Missle Drop Kick off top rope. Stanley had the chance and made goos on it with a tag to his partner.Tommy Kain, now the fresher man in the ring, took over on Higgins and Slayer with several slams and clotheslines. Kain then kept his opposion off guard with several highflying moves which rocked not only Stylin With Attitudes but the crowd as well. Kain Mounted the rope and connected with a moonsault which got a two count on Slayer. Stanley having a chance to get a breath joined his partner and, went to work on Higgins as
Stanley continued to try to put away Slayer. The ref lost control of the match at this point as Stanley hit a crossbody which sent Slayer and himself over the tope rope. Higgins and Kain traded blows as they tumbled outside the ring. All four men started to battle up the isle as the ref began to start a count. The hatred between two teams coming to a boil the refs count was up to 8 and still nobody was paying attention. As the brawl continued the ref reached 10 and had no choice but to rule the match a dounle countout. This didn't seem to matter to either team as their battle went to up the entrance ramp and to the back stage.

They attacked Tuckwell and Denton before the bell was sounded landing in stiff blows with chairs as their manager Brittney had the ref distracted. When the ref turned his attention to the match he was able to get the chair away from Christopher and send Manning back to his corner.The damage was already done as
Christopher wouldn't let up on Tuckwell who was banged up from the chair shots. Christopher would focus the attacke on Tuckwells neck and back area.
With a combo off hard suplexes and bodyslams..Christopher would then sench in a boston crab and apply the pressure. Tuckwell held on though and after several minutes was able to get to the ropes. Christopher however wouldn't brake the hold until the ref reached the five count. Tuckwell favored his back the rest of the match as The Threat gave no room to breath.Christopher tagged in Manning and Manning applied a inverted back breaker and went to the top rope and landed a elbow to the small of the back. Mannin would try for another but would come up short as Tuckwell rolled out of the way. That was all Tuckwell needed to make the tag to his partner Denton. Denton the fresh man in the ring and full of rage after seeing his partner brutalized took control. Sending Christopher and Manning flying around the ring with slams, clotheslines, and armdrags. Brittney seeing her team lose control of the match poped up on the apron and tried to distract the ref. Denton had none of it though as he whipped Sebastion into Brittney sending her flying off the apron. Sebastion was then caught with a double under hook suplex by
Denton.Favoring his back Tuckwell gained some energy and sent Christopher over the top rope with a clothesline then connected with a swanton bomb from the inside of the ring to the outside ontop of Shane. Denton took control of
Manning with a sleeper hold. Manning would try to brake the hold by ramming Denton into the corner post. The ref losing contol of the match got caught in the middle and was snadwiched by Manning. With Tuckwell and Christopher fighting outside the ring The Threats brother in arms Sean Christopher rushed to the ring and smacked Tuckwell with the singapore cane. Brittney regained composure from her earlier fall once again got on the apron. Denton laid out by the shot was then crushed with a Shooting Star Press by
Sebastion. Brittney woke the ref up who turned to see Sebastion covering Denton. Tuckwell unable to make the save as he was being double teamed by Shane and Sean...the ref counted Denton out. Otis Lonnegan and Jose Calvera, who have been the Cinderella story of this tournament,taking on red hot Simply
Flawless! <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> OTIS LONNEGAN/JOSE CALVERA VS. SIMPLY FLAWLESS <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> The next match of the night was a tag team match. The first wrestlers introduced were "Simply Flawless" Kenny Kopecky and Hunter Douglas. Kopecky weighed in at 238lbs, hailing from Leavenworth, Kansas. Douglas rang in at
235lbs, hailing from Austin, Texas. An awe inspiring entrance caused the crowd to give off a moderate pop.
"Perfect Stranger" by Deep Purple added to the excitement of the laser light show. Kopecky was dressed in his usual black long tights with barbwire design, red knee pads, with red talons emblazoned on the pads. Douglas was garbed in black short tights with a large gold triangle on the back with the word "DOUGLAS" in black and white letters with black talons.
Next, the tag team of Otis Lonnegan and Jose Calvera were introduced. Calvera weighed in at 211lbs, hailing from Mexico City, Mexico. Lonngegan weighed in at 235lbs, hailing from Chicago, Illinois. These two received the larger pop of the night.
"Gone Shootin" by AC/DC accompanied their entrance. They were escorted to the ring by their manager J.J. Rosch, and his escort. Calvera wore his standard attire of black tights, black kneedpads, black boots, and his black vest. Lonnegan wore red and black tights, black wrestling boots, and red kneepads.
Buster McCracken, the ref for the match, rang the bell. The first two wrestlers to go at it were Lonnegan and Kopecky. Both were hesitant to lock up, but Calvera took the quick and advantage and threw out a front kick.
Kopecky backed up and went into a collar and elbow tie-up. The fought back and forth for a few seconds before Kopecky locked in a side headlock.
Calvera executed a throw off sending Kopecky into the ropes. He executed aclothesline, but Kopecky ducked under it, bouncing off the ropes once again.
Off the ropes, Kopecky executed a picture perfect baseball slide into the legs of Calvera, knocking him down to the mat. Capitalizing on his situation, Kopecky applied a reverse single leg lock to Calvera. McCracken checked on Calvera, but he broke out of the hold within a few seconds.
Kopecky brought Calvera back to his feet, and gave a swift chop to the chest, causing him to stumble backwards. He executed two more chops backing him into the turnbuckle, where Kopecky began pounding on him with quick jabs to the face.
The crowd gave off a few "ooohs" to the visciousness of the punches, but broke into a cheer when Calvera blocked a punch and gave a swift boot to the midsection of Kopecky. He sent Kopecky flying into the ropes, and on the return, executed a flying headscissors taking Kopecky down to the mat. The crowd gave off a clearly audible pop as Calvera went for the quick pin. He only got a one count but brought Kopecky back up. Taking Kopecky with him,
Calvera tagged in the big man Otis Lonnegan. Going to the top rope, Lonnegan executed a flying axehandle smash, causing Kopecky to eat mat. After letting go of Kopecky, Calvera got out of the ring and onto the other side of the ropes.
Taking control of the match, a fresh Lonnegan reigned havoc on Kopecky. He sent his opponent into the ropes and hit him with a nasty back elbow. He followed by applying a fujiwara armbar, further weaking Kopecky. He held it for atleast ten seconds before Kopecky broke the hold. Lonnegan nailed
Kopecky with a soccer ball kick, drawing an "ooh" from the crowd. He brought Kopecky back up, and went for a suplex. However, Kopecky blocked it. He went for it again, and blocked once more. Kopecky set himself and took Lonnegan down with a nasty snap suplex. Kopecky was up before Lonnegan and tagged in the even bigger man, Hunter Douglas.
Douglas got a moderate pop as he entered the ring. Using his superior size, Douglas took Lonnegan down with a double arm DDT. He followed by connecting with two leg drops. Kopecky entered the ring. Both men were in the ring, instantly bringing Calvera out of his corner. Calvera flew across the ring, executing a flying spin wheel kick, taking the distracted Douglas out.
Instantly McCracken "took control" forcing Calvera back to his corner. Rosch and Calvera started arguing with the ref while Kopecky began choking
Lonnegan against the ring ropes bringing boos from the crowd. Douglas got up from the kick, and went to the outside of the ring, onto the floor. He walked over to where Kopecky was choking Lonnegan, went up onto the apron, and attempted to execute a suplex on Lonnegan. However, Douglas felt a fist in his solar plexus as Lonnegan blocked Douglas's attempt. He quickly fought back and caught Kopecky with a super kick. Both men of Simply
Flawless were down, drawing a huge pop from the crowd. Lonnegan tagged in Calvera, drawing another pop. With Douglas the legal man, Calvera rolled him back into the ring, and wasted no time applying a figure four leglock to him. He hold it for a good twenty seconds, sufficiently wearing down Douglas. He then brought his dazed opponent, and worked him over to the turnbuckle. Waving his hand in the air in a circular motion, he sat on the top rope with Douglas trapped in a front face lock. What followed was a huge tornado DDT. The ring "boomed" audibly and Calvera went for the pin. He got a quick two count out of it. Before
Calvera could stop him, Douglas lunged for Kopecky's hand and found it. Kopecky quickly entered the ring. Calvera caught Kopecky, and executed a european uppercut, staggering his opponent. He followed with a quick right cross, and then a huge elbow smash.
Calvera sent Kopecky into the opposite corner, ran in, and executed a huge running splash. With Kopecky staggered, a jumping back kick was all it took to send him to the mat. Calvera went to the top rope, bringing the adulation of the crowd once again. Turning backwards, he poised himself on the turnbuckle, crouched down like a cat ready to strike. The luchadore followed by launching himself into a beautiful moonsault press, and came crashing down...
On Kopecky's _knee!_ Clutching his stomach, he rolled over as Kopecky got to his feet and began visciously attacking Calvera's backs with repeated stomps. Obviously enraged, Kopecky hauled Calvera to his feet and lifted him skyward, and taught Calvera the meaning of pain with a huge sheer-drop style brainbuster. The crowd began to chant "LONN-E-GAN, LONN-E-GAN!" over and over. Lonnegan was visciously shaking the ropes. Kopecky followed by bringing Calvera to his feet, and moving him over to his friendly corner.
Kopecky slapped in Douglas. Douglas hit the ring and forced Calvera up against the turnbuckles. Kopecky set himself up, and stepped in. His foot flew skyward as he inverted his body, torquing it sideways. His right leg, chambered near his upper torso, lashed out in a viscious high impact super kick, catching Calvera in the jaw. Calvera's head snapped backwards, letting out a sickening sound. Unable to hold his only body weight up, Calvera stumbled forward and felt himself caught in Douglas's kahatajime. The deadly jujitsu submission maneuver.
With every bone in his body feeling like it was going to fall to pieces, the crowd roared to life chanting "CAL-VER-A, CAL-VER-A." Through absolute willpower, he fought out of the hold. The surprised Douglas had no clue what to do, and within seconds, the fresh Lonnegan had been tagged in and hit the ring. Almost as if in a form of animalistic honor, the two men stood toe to toe, Douglas the obviously larger man. The crowd's pure decibel level was growing to an insane volume.
Lonnegan made the first move. He hit an insanely fast DDT and went for the cover. McCracken was there for the two count, but Douglas kicked out. Both men got up at the same time, with Douglas hitting Lonnegan with a DDT of his own. He went for a quick inside cradle, but NO!, Lonnegan reversed it after a two count and rolled over, executing his own inside cradle. But Douglas reversed that one after a two count, applying another inside cradle. 1...
2... Thr - NO! Both men threw eachother off with the crowd going nuts at this point. Both men got up, slightly winded but hardly exhausted. Lonnegan threw a quick right which was ducked by Douglas, who followed with a kick. Lonnegan caught the kick, and executed an enzuigiri. He missed with the enzuigiri and
Douglas slapped on a single prawn hold, which Lonnegan kicked out of after a few seconds, and both men were up again!
Toe to toe, another staredown ensued. The match intensity was escallating, and their breathing was becoming heavier and more rapid at the same time.
Douglas went to a quick collar and elbow tie up. Douglas took the advantage in a big way, drawing Lonnegan skyward into a vertical suplex position. He held him there for a few seconds, letting all the blood rush to his head, but instead of falling backwards, he started to bring his weight forward.
The Flawless Fulfillment. A vertical suplex into powerbomb. It was all over. Lonnegan's body neared the ground..... .... the seconds seemed to turn to minutes.... .... the minutes seemed to turn to hours.... .... every person in that audience, their mouth agape.... .... flashbulbs went off everywhere as these two men engaged in a brutal mortal combat.... .... the resulting injury from the maneuver has ended many wrestler's careers.... .... today, this is where Otis Lonnegan, the "Last Dance,"career could have ended.... .... did it? Lonnegan planted his hands, cinched his legs, and while blocking the powerbomb, hit a huge frankensteiner. The resulting impact from this maneuver caused a huge boom. Whether that was from the ring or from the crowd, no one could tell. The legs were caught, McCracken dropped to the mat. Kopecky came flying out of the corner. Calvera came flying out of his corner. McCracken's hand hit the mat once, as the crowd chanted "1." Calvera neared Kopecky as Kopecky neared Lonnegan. McCracken's hand hit the mat twice, the crowd chanted "2." Kopecky was now a foot away from Lonnegan.
McCracken's hand hit the mat three times, and the crowd erupted into a massive frenzy. Applause, cheering, and a huge pop were all mixed mixed together as Kopecky was interrupted by the clothesline of Calvera. McCracken wasted no time in raising Lonnegan and Calvera's hands up to the massive pop of the crowd.
"Gone Shootin" by AC/DC roared up as Lonnegan and Calvera were announced the winners. The quickly left the ring with Rosch and his escorts, Simply
Flawless still in shock. They eventually left the ring, causing the crowd to die down, and allowing the AGW to prepare for its next match.
