Retribution defeated Sinister after seeing interfence from Trey Lawrenze and The Organ Grinder, who were both serving as officials for the match. But the biggest surprise came post-match. Retribution's most deranged, most insane, most mentally unstable nemesis returnes as Twizted, former AWMC Extremist Champion, returned to his old stomping grounds, leaving a cross made from Retribution's own blood on the AGW World Title. The AGW history between this two men are unkown, but the history was well documented in other places..will we learn more? SHAKUR WINS WAR OF THE FOUR Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur joins the rank of "Midnight" Darren Skiles by becomes the AGW 2000 War of the Four Champion. Shakur defeated Masked Good Guy via TKO in Round One, and went to war with The Organ Grinder in The Prison Match Final. Shakur now moves on to face Obsessive Compulsive Disorder, who too was victorious in his No Apologies IV match, at Sandblast: All Fired Up. EARLY SANDBLAST CARD ANNOUNCED Trey Lawrenze will battle Retribution at Sandblast: All Fired Up as the Main Event for the card. Other matches signed: The Organ Grinder will do battle with Sinister in an Exploding Turnbuckles Match; President Musashi signed at No Apologies IV for Stylin' With Attitudes to defend the North American Tag Team Titles against The Suicidal High Flyers; A Hawaiian hardcore Burning Tables macth will occur between Obsessive Compilsive Disorder and Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur; Masked Plague will battle for Pooh in a "Pooh On A Pole" match against Suicide, and the Masked Good Guy will but his identity on the line as he takes on Jean Paul Valley in a Mask vs. Hair match.
