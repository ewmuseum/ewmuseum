"Blackfist" Shakur and "WildSide" Craig Miller stood tall together following the main event at the inaugural Global Warfare show. In a bloody main event,
Shakur battled Trey Lawrenze until AGW officials and Hiroyoshi Takada got involved, prompting a series of events culminating into Cole, Tracker,
Shakur and Miller clearing the ring together. Tracker and Cole battled in a memorable feud last year, which ended with a epic fight at the Never Say Die pay-per-view, while Cole and Shakur captured an award for 1999’s Best Feud.
Miller and Shakur clashed on several occasions in the early days of the
AWMC, when Miller held the Great Lakes Heritage strap and later when Shakur was AWMC champion. The quartet have made no further statement nor have they even given a name to this blockbuster crew, though its certain all four will continue to have a major impact.
CONTAGION CAPTURE TAG TITLES "Devil’s Advocate" Johnny Bagwell and "The Revelation" Ryan Violent, collectively known as Contagion, scored the pinfall many thought was long due, when they captured the AGW World Tag Team titles facing the Threat at the inaugural Global Warfare show. Pitted against their rivals in a cage match dubbed The Killing Floor, Contagion took the belts vacated at Cruise
Control III. Many insiders expect Contagion to be one of the toughest teams to beat may solidify the division.
HATE, NOBLE UNITE TO WREAK HAVOC "Loose Cannon" Priest Noble, and Dr. Hate united once again to attack "WildSide" Craig Miller before his match with Suicide, and also interjected during the Main Event. Could The duo formely known as The Fallen have more malicious intentions?
STYLIN' WITH ATTITUDES MAKE PRESENCE FELT Former-AWMC Tag team Champions Stylin' With Attitudes mad ethie rpresence felt through out the vening. First confronting Akira Musashi, then attacking both Assault & Battery AND Doom Machines, and finally adding an electrifying factor to The Killing Floor match.
SUICIDAL HIGH FLYERS DEBUT The Suicidal High Flyers debut in AGW last night, putting a quick end to the
North American tag Team Title match, and challenging Doom Machines. Could the High Flyers clash with The North American tag team Champion? Several Tag
Teams may have soemthing to say about that.
HATE RETURNS, COSTS TAKADA BIG One of the game's most dangerous stars returned to action at Cruise Control, costing champion Hiroyoshi Takada big. Dr. Hate, one of the sport's most recognized athletes and a former AWMC star, appeared at Cruise Control and helped end the reign of Takada and hasten a victory by Sinister. Hate, whose past alliances then battles with the Underground are legendary, is expected to make waves in the company.
LAWRENZE WINS TITLE SHOT AT SUMMER EVENT Trey Lawrenze, entering late in the Cruise Control battle royal, captured rights to summer's AGW PPV title shot with a win at the May show.
Retribution, Brian Storm, Ethan Love and Ryan Violent also made impressive appearances, with Retribution and Love entering first and second, respectively, and landing in the final four. Lawrenze tentatively faces
Sinister for the AGW strap.
NOBLE RETURNS TO DECLARE WAR ON YAKUZA Former AWMC and Global Pro Wrestling champion Priest Noble shocked the company with his return at Cruise Control. Noble, who allied himself with
Retribution, Dr. Hate, Cyric and Tank last year in a group called the
Fallen, announced he would go to battle against old foe Trey Lawrenze and
Yakuza, and punctuated his return by trashing the AWMC Videowall live at the
PPV. What could be the dangerous Noble's next move?
THE DON RAMPAGES THROUGH CC3 Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur tore into the company's biggest event of the year to "issue correction" to a number of stars, including National Champion
"FlyKing" Will Geddings and Masked Plague. Shakur, the self-proclaimed Don, also entered as a last-minute contestant in the battle royal, eliminating himself and Sean Christopher in the process. His appearance later in that event with former enemy Craig Miller continued to shock those in attendance.
What is the ruthless one do next? THREAT SIDELINE MIZUKAWA AFTER ATTACK The Threat, composed of Shane Christopher and Sebastion Manning, shocked fans at Cruise Control by dealing the death blow to the tag team champions
Kuwasi Shakur and Ujimoto Mizukawa. Manning and Christopher attacked
Mizukawa early in the program, resulting in an injury that forced AWMC
President Akira Musashi to strip the champions and allow Contagion and The
Threat to fight for the vacated belts. The wild title bout ended in a no-contest, when guest official Sean Christopher reunited with his brother and turned on Contagion. An angry Musashi declared Contagion would get their chance at revenge against The Threat next week in the Killing Floor, a top-sealed steel cage, with guest official Shakur serving as ring enforcer and the Threat's entourage banned from the arena. The winner becomes the first AGW World Tag Team champions.
