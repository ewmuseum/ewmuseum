[AGW] Cheap Shots (10/07/00)

7 October 2000

Cameras zoom around and show the luxurious look that it has to it. Complete with bar, black leather sofa and television. Cameras then show Hunter Douglas who sits on the farside of the plane. Douglas is wearing a pair of tan Docker shorts and a navy blue cotton t-shirt. His hair is combed forward, slightly messed up slightly near the bangs. He also has on a pair of black shoes with black socks.
>From the otherside of the room walks Kenny Kopecky in nothing but a white cotton towel wrapped around his waist. Water droplets run down his chest, soaking into the towel. Kopecky walks over and sits down across the table from Douglas. Kopecky reaches down under the bar and pulls a bottle of Jack
Daniel's Tennessee Whiskey, and slides it across the table to Douglas, and then reaches back under and pulls out a bottle of Sour Apple Pucker. He unscrews the bottle top and takes a refreshing gulp and clears his throat.]
KENNY KOPECKY - It's good to be back on the road, or in the air again. In more than a mere few months, we have been sitting on the sidelines, waiting and training for the moment, when we would be recalled upon. And since the layoff, we've stayed in tip-top condition. Or atleast one of us has been...
[Kopecky grins and looks toward Douglas as he is about to take a drink from his bottle of Jack Daniel's.]
HUNTER DOUGLAS - A little drink to kick back and relax won't hurt anyone every now and then. Besides, when you are as damn near flawless as I am, a good Jack Daniel's is my reward.
KENNY KOPECKY - Hunter, _OUR_ reward is only a couple days away, when we finally get our shot at the AGW World Tag Team Titles. Our reward will be the
AGW World Tag Team Titles in which we have to defeat not only Jose Calvera and Otis Lonnegan, but The Threat has also been thrown into the mix. But no problem there, the more, the merrier like they say. But the way the AGW is going, this title shot that we are receiving, could possibly not only be our first shot at the titles...but also our last.
[Kopecky pauses and shifts his weight to his right left. He stares at the countertop as if pondering something.]
HUNTER DOUGLAS - Fact is...opportunity has come and gone with us, and we are just gonna look back at our mistakes, and try and improve on them. And our biggest mistake we've made upon arrival here into the AGW is underestimating
Otis Lonnegan and Jose Calvera. We destroyed every other tag team before we ran into them, and we went in there a little too cocky. Although our match was the _SHOWSTOPPER_ of the evening, the men that you wanted to see walk out of their with the victory, wasn't us. [Douglas grins warmly.] HUNTER DOUGLAS - But...this is where we get our second chance, and this time around boys, there is absolutely _NO ROOM_ for mistakes. Whether you boys care to admit it or not, we _ARE_ the top dogs here in the AGW. We are the team that everyone wants to capture a victory over. Hell, we single-handedly revamped the AGW tag team division. As soon as everyone saw how flawless
Simply Flawless was, the hottest commodity is finding a buddy and
tag-teaming with him. [Kopecky chuckles at the comment and begins to interrupt.] KENNY KOPECKY - But see, that is where Simply Flawless, well...is simply flawless! If there is no chemistry between your team, you got a firecracker that won't light. You see, Hunter and I, we are _FIRE AND SMOKE!_ Where you find one of us, the other is sure to show up. And when you have such a deadly combination like that, that is the fire that you need to light the _BOMB!_
Actually, I could be extremely bold and use the word "nuclear warhead" to describe out impact, but we're calm..._CALM LIKE A BOMB!_
[Kopecky's trademark smirk rolls over his face as Douglas nods his head.] HUNTER DOUGLAS - Jose Calvera, Otis Lonnegan, Threat...maybe you don't know it yet, or you do, but you refuse to admit it, but your backs are against the wall this time. I mean, you look at us, we're under some pressure. I mean being beat by either of you would be a complete embarrassment. The Threat, I would hate to guess how many times you had your shots, but you blew them all, just like your career. How you are still in the game...only god knows why? But you two seem to be that spark of fire that just won't die out. There is nothing around to make yourself grow larger, but you just set there in a dormant state. Then, along comes Simply Flawless to _PERMANENTLY_ put you out!
KENNY KOPECKY - It's no secret that both the Threat and the team of Lonnegan and Calvera wish they were like us. I know they wish they had the bright future and dominating present that we behold. You know you envy us. But frankly, I don't blame you. Because I know I would. If I wasn't who I was,
I'd do anything I could to be what I am. And be a part of Simply Flawless. I mean look at you Hunter, 6'5"...235 pounds...the best mat technician in the
AGW. [Douglas quickly cuts Kopecky off before he can continue his words.] HUNTER DOUGLAS - My credentials are quite nice Kenny, but look at yourself. Held countless titles, beat countless opponents, and possess that deadly superkick of yours. 6'1"...238 pounds...with a wide background of technical wrestling as well. There is no doubt that without one or the other, Simply
Flawless wouldn't be what we are today.
[Kopecky shakes his head knowingly.] KENNY KOPECKY - You couldn't have stated that any better, Hunter. And with those great attributes that we possess, we will bring them with us when we win the AGW tag team titles. Otis Lonnegan, Jose Calvera, your little luck streak has went on pretty far, but October 11th, is the end of the line.
Common, like you haven't heard the old saying before? Every good thing must come to an end.
[Kopecky scratches his head with a puzzled look over his face. He gathers his words and continues.] KENNY KOPECKY - Except with you guys, you really weren't anything good or special. So when we beat you, it won't surprise anyone, and it really won't be anything more than just another win under our belts. We're just tearing on through the AGW collecting wins as we go, and lucky enough for you, you're next! And as each week rolls by, we will continue to keep our namesake alive as well...we will maintain being _SIMPLY FLAWLESS!_ The thing is, people look at the name of Simply Flawless, and think we are two cocky hotshots that are looking to make a name out of ourselves. Well folks, you are correct, except for one half of that. Yes, we are two cocky hotshots, but we have already made a name for ourselves in our short tenure here in the AGW.
[Kopecky laughs smugly, his light blue eyes burning with fury. Douglas rotates his eyes directly toward the camera.]
HUNTER DOUGLAS - Calvera, Lonnegan, Threat...take notice, because that's just how it goes on a day to day basis. Our name isn't a burden...its a way of life. We live our lives to be flawless. And that's what we strive for when we are in that ring. You can do whatever, say whatever outside of that ring. Because frankly, your words mean nothing to us, and when you cross through those ropes, and enter our _TERRITORY,_ you will realize first hand what it takes to be flawless, and that you are nowhere near that level of greatness.
But when you leave that ring, the two of you will know that we aren't quite perfect, no, no...we are...
[Douglas stares intently as he says the trademark catchphrase.] HUNTER DOUGLAS - _SIMPLY FLAWLESS!!!_ [Kenny Kopecky stands up, grasping his towel, walks back to the farside of the plane which he came from. He disappears down the short corridor he entered. Douglas sits alone as he stares deeply into the camera. Shortly there after, the scene slowly fades to black.]

The camera fades out slowly and then back in somewhere within the building moving over high tech exercise equipment and free weights. Twisting around slowly the vacant room begins to show the lone form of Jean Paul Valley on the treadmill clad in just a pair of running shorts sweat dripping from his face and streaking down his body. Arms pumping as his feet move hard and faster over the track for a few more moments before his hands drop down to the display pannel and he jogs to slow hault. JPV stumbles off the treadmil grabbing a towel by his feet and raises it to his fest padding down the sweat as he doubles over a bit catching his breath. He looks up to the camera and walks over to the work out bench taking a bottle water off it and pouring it in his mouth. He spits it out and rests his fist clenching the towel against his hip letting his other arm holding the bottle rest at his side. JPV raises his eyes to the camera and smirks a bit]
JPV: I know it had to be hard for all of you, to go so long, without hearing from me again. This whole AGW hiatus has had to leave everyone from NY to LA fiending for solid wrestling action, and on October 11th that void in all of your lives will be filled once again. "The Innovator of Extreme" Tracker, your cute little nickname really doesn't mean anything to me, you can act as
Extreme as you want to, but word is the most Extreme thing you've done involves a cardboard tube and a gerbile, so if I'm not exactly shaking in my boots, you know why.
[JPV tilts his head back raising the bottle over his head and letting the water drissle down over his face and into his mouth. He tilts his head to the side spitting it out once again as the water drips from his hair. He looks back to the camera staring into it.]
JPV: Tracker, you've paid your dues, I'll admit that, in the same token, so have I. I've worked the shows that Takada, Cole, Geddings and all the other coporate puppets wouldn't even think about showing up in, and where's my title shot? I've been here 6 months, 6 frickin months and never got a title shot. So Tracker, if beating you gets me one step closer to the AGW title, then I'm gonna beat you like a red headed step child in the center of the ring, it's as simple as that. Whatever it takes to get the job done...
[JPV tosses the bottle aside and begins to pace around the gym moving away from the camera before turning back and pointing at the camera]
JPV: And the next time, some masked fruit cake trys to make a name for themself by jumping me from behind every chance they get, they're gonna end up in the hospital, it's as simple as that, and if I'm not put in the ring with quality compition, I'm gonna start making my own matches, weather they like it, or the fans like it, or the guys in the back like it or not, it doesn't matter to me at this point. [motions around the gym] Look around,
I'm
not sitting back, kicking my feet up with a bimbo on each arm and a manhattan in my hand. For the 1st time in months I'm completly focused on my goal at hand, and come this time next month, you better believe I'll be inches away from reaching it.
[JPV glances to the enterence of the gym as the receptionist walks in with a phone in hand. She walks over to Jean who takes it from her and holds it to his ear raising his brow a bit]
JPV: Yeah?...What is it this time...That's right, Tracker...what do you mean a twist? WHAT!? They can't do that!?...That's not fair!...Jarred Bombay too!
What the hell! Yeah you laugh it up! Ya know what that's fine...we can put 5 more guys in the match and it really won't matter!
[JPV turns off the phone and hurls it against the wall of the gym shaking his head and packing back and forth more rapidly now. He slides his hand through the hair and looks up to the camera]
JPV: This just goes to show you that it's a conspiracy against Jean Paul
Valley! First tracker and now...."Sweet" Jarred Bombay. But that's alright...you see Jarred Bombay is nothing in my book, and he will forever be just someone who carries my bag from the car to the locker room so he can atleast basked in the presence of a real superstar. Tracker, Bombay it really doesn't matter to me if you team up against me, beat the life out of each other or don't even show up for the match, because when the final bell is rung, I'll be the one standing in the cener of the ring with my arm raised,
I'll be the one who walks down the asile on my feet instead of limping to the back like I was just in a car wreck. It's all about me, I've said it before and I'll say it again. Tracker, Bombay...because it is...all about me, don't hate me because you're not...me...
[JPV waves the camera off and heads towards the door of the gym walking out as the camera follows his path before fading to black]
<><><><><><><><><> DOUGLAS <><><><><><><><><> (The Scene opens in an empty arena, a few hours before the show. Mark
Douglas is seen pacing the inside of the ring, the ropes not even put up quite yet.... he strolls towards the camera, and smirks as he hops down to the floor. He quickly motions the camera to come closer, lowering his gaze towards the floor as the camera man approaches. The camera stops a few feet away and Douglas turns a cold smile to the camera. There is no warmth whatsoever in his eyes, absolutely no sincerity.)
DOUGLAS: In a couple of hours, this arena is going to be filled with screaming fans craving the sight of blood. They're going to be looking for the next big move, or the next catch phrase.... whatever. A couple hundred... hell, maybe a couple of thousand seats will be filled, with people who will be cheering till their damn throats are sore. They have alot of expectations... but the one thing none of them are expecting to see is me. None of them are expecting to see what it is I plan on delivering.
Not a single one of them expects Mark Douglas to step into this ring the underdog, and walk out of this arena the Star of the damn show. But you see, I've never been one to give the "people" whatever it is they expect.
Pegasus... they say you and I have to go at it tonight. Personally, I have nothing against you. You do your thing, you play your own tune and walk your own walk and that's just fine by me. I'm not out here to tell you that I'm going to tear you apart, or that I'm a better wrestler than you...
see, I let my actions in the ring do the talking for me. Trust me, you'll hear the message then soon enough. Instead, I want to talk to you about...
expectations. You see, if you're expecting me to just lie about and be a sandbag body dummy for your little display of wrestling technique... think again. What you should be expecting is a guy that has an awful lot to prove to the world.
(Douglas smirks and rolls his neck... he glances around the arena, and then back towards the camera.)
Mark Douglas: The problem is, Pegasus... we're alot alike. We're similiar, after a fashion I suppose. Neither of us gives a damn about the other, and we sure as hell aren't looking to impress the hundred mooks sitting in the bleachers. Me? I just want to get past you, and on to the next victim.
<><><><><><><><><> SINISTER <><><><><><><><><> * The scene fades in to a still shot of a beautiful waterfront located no where specific. Off in the distance is a picturesque view of a large city, the twinkling lights appearing like firefly's in the distance, casting their reflections across the water. There are numerous boats travelling slowly on the water, the lights emanating from them revealing the people aboard, some partying and others just relaxing by themselves or with loved ones. The camera zooms out from the waterfront to reveal numerous restaurants aligned in the proximity of the waterfront, an impressive array of international foods and cultures. The multitude of restaurants represent a good portion of the numerous ethnic groups that represent the United States. As the camera pans along the strip of restaurants, it is obvious to the viewer that the restaurants are very nice, as the patrons as well as the employees are dressed very nicely. There are the obvious arrogant individuals as well as the not so arrogant amongst the crowd, eating or socializing. Amongst the buzz of chatter and business, there is a gradually increasing booming noise and the camera focuses upon the main street. Off in the distance, a pair of approaching headlights are seen, and most of the people that the car passes by appear rather insulted, while a few smile and bob their heads to the bump of the loud system. The camera pans out and to the side of the approaching vehicle, and it is a Lincoln Towncar with Dayton rims, small fat tires and tinted windows. All of the windows of the car are down and "Shake It Fast"
by Mystikal booms from within. The car slows by the valet station and
Sinister steps out of the car, smiling widely and moving his body to the rhythm of the music. The valets laugh and shake hands with Sinister, and
Sinister slips each of them a $20. A few passers by, specifically three attractive young ladies, begin to dance slightly and Sinister winks at them.
Sinister is wearing a dark red suit with a black dress shirt, a maroon colored tie, and black dress shoes. He strides towards the restaurants, turning numerous heads as his mountainous presence is felt by all who see him. He strides into a seafood restaurant and is immediately seated by a window overlooking the water. A glass of cranberry juice is brought to him and he takes a few sips, thanking the waitress who giggles *
"Well, well, well. Surprised to see me acting civilized for once?" [He chuckles slightly] "Yeeeeeeeah, me too. All of you out there in AGW land know that I don't like to wear these clown suits and sit in a snobbish environment, but tonight I felt a little frisky, so hell, I figured why not go and traumatize some of the 'upper class' folks? So, here I am, just chiiiiiiillliiiiiiin'
like a villian." [He chuckles once again, taking a drink of his cranberry juice. The same waitress approaches, a striking young caucasian woman with long blonde hair and large green eyes. Sinister nods his head slowly as she asks what he would like to order]
Waitress: "Good evening Mr. Sinister, my name is Colleen and I'll be your waitress this evening, as if you hadn't figured that out."
[Both laugh gently and Sinister grins sheepishly] "Good evening Colleen, how are you doing?" [Colleen smiles widely] Colleen: "I'm doing well Mr. Sinister, thank you for asking." [Sinister nods his head respectfully while smiling widely also] Colleen: "What would you like to eat this evening?" [Sinister rolls his eyes as an obvious thought rolls through his mind. However, he quickly hands Colleen the menu] "May I please have the steak and lobster with a side order of shrimp?" [Colleen takes the menu and nods, scribbling his order on her pad of paper] Colleen: "Fine choice. Will there be anything else?" "No thank you." Colleen: "Very well. I will return with your order when it's finished." [She smiles widely and walks away slowly, glancing back from time to time. Sinister watches her walk away and laughs at himself] "I need to chill the hell out. Teresa will be here..." [He glances at his watch] "In about 20 minutes. I'm not rude folks, trust me, I would wait for her before I ate, but she told me to go right ahead, so I shall. Now, seeing that I have some time on my hands, let me speak about various business.
First and foremost, I'm damned happy that the AGW is back in business. Let me tell you, ever since the hiatus, I've been damn bored training all of the time but not being able to put the stuff to work, ya' know? And, much to my pleasure, well, mostly, I'm scheduled to step into the confines and unrelenting pain of a steel cage against one of my most prominent nemesis'...Takada."
[He takes a big drink of the cranberry juice and sets it down] "Takada...the name carries so much with it. Focus, intensity, outstanding ability, hellacious competitor, and a man possessed. Takada and I know one another damn well in the ring, but outside of it, we live very separate lives and there remains that mysterious aspect about him. The last I saw of
Takada, he was undergoing quite a bit of personal change, in fact, taking on an entirely new persona...one that is extremely vicious in nature. This is both a benefit and a hindrance. It is beneficial for Takada because his focus reaches new levels and he can withstand an enormous amount of punishment. The hindrance is it leads him into numerous mistakes that can be taken advantage of, if the opponent knows how to exploit them."
[He raises and lowers his shoulders, cracking them loudly and takes a deep breath, looking around the restaurant momentarily. His focus then returns to the camera]
"Takada, the reason you despise me so is due to the fact that I am one of a tiny collection of men who can exploit your openings...when you present them. There is no doubt that your wrestling ability far exceeds mine, I never have nor ever will deny that. I do not have the mat technique nor the high-flying ability that you posses, so I do not pretend to do so. I continue with my abilities, and that is pummeling the opponent into a state of near unconsciousness, and that works well for me. In terms of techniques, well, I posses enough to get the job done, and you know this
Takada. You also know that I too can withstand a great deal of punishment and pain as all have witnessed, most recently the horrendous battles against
Retribution where we put one another in the hospital for days at a time."
[He takes another deep breath and rubs his forehead in thought] "You and I have been down the path quite a bit lately Takada. We have watched one another go through a tremendous amount of hell in and out of the ring. You have defeated me, humiliated me, and hurt me. I have done the same to you, the most recent time costing you the AGW Heavyweight Title that now adorns the waist of Retribution and deservingly so. He was the better man on that fateful night of battle and I have no problem conceding that at all. The AGW Heavyweight Title will come into play in due time, but for now, my focus is upon you Takada. You have been the proverbial thorn in my side for quite some time, and I tire of it. Everywhere I turn, there you are, stickin' your nose into my business, distracting me and preventing me from achieving some goals while delaying the accomplishment of others. I had turned the tide slightly before the hiatus, and now that the AGW is up and running once again, I plan on continuing that pattern. How ironic that we are to battle in a steel cage, one of the most unforgiving of structures in the wrestling realm."
[Colleen stops by the table with a basket of bread and butter] Colleen: "Here you go Mr. Sinister." [Sinister's face changes to a look of pleasure and he smiles while nodding his head towards Colleen]
"Thank you very much." [Colleen looks at the camera, then looks at Sinister] Colleen: "Is everything okay? You looked a little miffed a second ago." [Sinister chuckles loudly, causing some heads to turn] "Please, don't feel pretentious. The camera is here for business, that's all. You are a very pleasant young lady and shall be treated as such by me."
[He smiles again and Colleen smiles as she continues her rounds. Sinister watches her walk away once more then turns to the camera, his smile turning into a scowl]
"The steel cage presents an opportunity to do some serious damage to an individual, and believe you me Takada, I know how to damage people. Despite what you say, despite what you deny, and despite what you think, you know deep down inside that I have had you in very bad predicaments before, and it was ONLY MY allowance of you to come back in a few battles that you are able to function normally. In front of my hometown of Chicago, I will show them and the AGW that I can be a man who has no remorse, no letup, and no mercy.
It is time that you and I begin to end this everlasting battle, and in that steel cage, I plan to take the first steps in achieving that. Chicago will bear witness to their 'adopted son' delivering pain, humiliation, and disgrace unto you Takada. I'll see you soon."
[Just as Sinister prepares to yell his coined phrase, a very stunning young
Latina woman sits across from him, stepping in front of the camera as she sits down. Sinister's scowl turns to a look of fascination]
"Daaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaamn!! Teresa, you are fine as May wine!" [Teresa laughs and Sinister kisses her hands. He then turns his attention towards the camera]
"I'll see you folks later. Teresa is just too damned fine to ignore.
