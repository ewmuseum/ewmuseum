[JPV screams into the phone as he paces around the private office quickly] Jean: No! No! CJ I'm not taking on Lawrenze a cage! You're out of your damn mind! Get me out of it! Jean: Ya know what! Forget it! The AWMC wants to screw me over by throwing me in a cage with Trey! Fine! We'll see who leaves the cage and who's left inside a bloody heap. I'm gonna make them and everyone else regret it, you think because you got in the ring and gang banged Rob Cole's ankle into splinters that scares me! I'm gonna do that to your leg inside that cage and I don't need anyone else to help me. [Jean paces around the room again still obviously nerbous but his bravado demeanor comming out as he continues to run his mouth]
Jean: Further more...Falco Inc is a corporation, we work togeather we don't roam around with this pack mentality like the yakuza does, not the real challenge in this match isn't weather you win or lose, it's weather you can keep your buddies away from the ring long enough to have a legit match. Jean: Right now...the AWMC is in a state of emergency as Falco International is declaring a state of war...there are no innocents, there are so civilians....there's us...and them...and we're gonna be the one sitting ontop of the world by any means nessicary...Eddie Rabid, Trey...don't hate me because you're not me! Hate me because I'm the one that put you on the selves for 6 months. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> COLE <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> (Scene opens with Rob Cole standing alone in front of an AWMC banner, wearing a leather jacket and a black AWMC Teeshirt with red writing. His tangled hair falls in loose strands about his face... the light catches off the bacitration smeared over the stitches along his forehead, and a microphone is held in his hand.)
RC: You know, I'd like to apologize in advance for my behaviour... both to the fans, and to the family and friends of Trey Lawrenze. I can't help but feel as though maybe I'm reacting a bit emotionally to my condition. But you see, I tend to be emotional when it comes to wrestling. You see, I've made my living off of competing in this sport with some of the best and greatest names throughout history.... And I would be lying if I didn't say that this has always been my dream. And, I guess I felt a bit as though Trey Lawrenze were personally responsible for nearly costing me the career that I've worked so hard at. Being hurt comes with the business, and I guess my aggravation with Trey was maybe a bit unfounded...
See, I also know that I'm partly to blame for what has happened to me. Because that dream of mine, that I just mentioned, also involved hurting myself and others. And I swore, when I was getting trained in the Hudson
Valley, that I would sacrafice everything to get to where I was. And that's what I did, Trey... I sacraficed my wife, my family, and my body... time and time again, I've sacraficed my body for this great sport. I've left better parts of my body on dirty and filthy concrete floors throughout this and several other countries. BUT NEVER IN MY SICKEST IMMAGINATION WAS THAT A ONE
WAY STREET!!!! (Cole snaps... his eyes growing hateful suddenly, and his lips curling in a snarl of rage as he speaks loudly and animatedly.)
RC: The thing that will be embedded in my skull to my dying day was the vision of /you/ laughing at my battered and bloody form! And, Trey Lawrenze, you alone are held responsible for the nightmares that have crept into my head... NIGHT AFTER GODDAMN NIGHT!!! And you may not be the old uncle that beat me with a belt night in night out, but you most certainly would laugh about it! So, take a moment and consider this moment right now, Lawrenze...
as you sit there at home, feeling the loving and tender fingers of your latest fling massage the stiffness out of your shoulder. I will be thinking about how you flushed my heart... how you flushed my soul... RIGHT DOWN THE
SHITSTAINED GODDAMNED TOILET!!!! And now it sickens me to look in the mirror and know that I'm going to do such terrible terrible things to you. Do you realize that? Do you even know about Guilt??!??!!
(Cole looks plaintively to the camera, clasping his hands in prayer.. .) RC: I have to ask for absolution, Trey... but not from you, because you will never be able to forgive me for what I am about to do. I have to ask for absolution from -your- family... from your -friends-.... from those who will be affected by the horrifying things that will be done to you. And, I beg of them, on my knees.... to Mrs. Lawrenze, who watches her son every monday night with pride and satisfaction... I beg her to forgive me... and maybe, if she does... I can maybe forgive myself.

Lawrenze as he pounds on a heavy bag with deadly precision. The "SLAM" of flesh meeting bag reverberates throughout the gym, and the camera observes in awe as Lawrenze's beating the bag picks up steam. By now, his fists have become a blur, as he ignores the camera and beats the bag mercilessly>
<<<<<<<<<SSSSSSSSSLLLLLLLLLAAAAAAAAAMMMMMMMMM>>>>>>>>> <The bag explodes and sand flies into the wall, and piles on the floor, as Lawrenze puts his ham-sized fist through the bag. The camera is startled by the loud, guttural scream from Lawrenze as he rips the bag from its concrete housing in the ceiling, and tosses it across the gym. He turns and the light behind him leaves him as unreadable as a shadow, but his tone of voice is.... frightening... This Lawrenze, is like a Lawrenze the AWMC has never seen. His voice creeps from the shadows, low, and deliberate>
Lawrenze: I thought when I adressed my "friend" Kuwasi's comments about myself last week, that I was past it. Fuck Kuwasi I said. I talked to Yosh and we both agreed to let him go fuck himself. Problem was, as the days went by my anger has done nothing but build and I would like nothing more than to get Shakur in the ring and prove to him I'm no longer the kid I was in the
CWLA. So here I am, ready to rip someones fucking head off, with no one to rip apart. <Lawrenze's looming form heaves with each breathe as he pauses> Lawrenze: You've got it all wrong you stupid son of a bitch. You came to me Kuwasi. You came to me and Takada, in hopes that you'd find some bodyguards for your fucking heavyweight title. And thats exactly what we did. Saved that title for you each and everytime you stepped in that ring with what you claimed was inferior competition. And if my memory serves Kuwasi, the only time you faced worthy competition --in myself and Takada --, you dropped the title. Coincidence?
<Lawrenze again pauses, and sighs, a deep, a pained sigh> Lawrenze: So you ask me to come to the AWMC, to once again play bodyguard to you Kuwasi. I was always there when you needed someone to watch your back. I languished in your shadow. Takada joined us shortly but abruptly left, at which point *WE* agreed to add the Headhunter, because you knew that the only real threat to you in the AWMC *ME* needed another direction. So I dominate the tag ranks. You have the nerve to question mine and Takada's judgement on Plague, when it was you that endorsed Tuulenux? You're a piece of shit Shakur. I should have seen it then, but I'm a lot wiser now. When the chips where down and the AWMC was faltering, you jumped ship. You left, and the two guys you claim you carried brought it back to the top.
<Lawrenze shakes his head in disgust> Lawrenze: Takada took the man that you couldn't beat without us and broke him, taking the title from him, not once, but twice. No Shakur, the AWMC does not need the "Don" like you would like to believe.
<Pause> Lawrenze: And if you need further prove, meet me in the ring Shakur. I see the hatred in your eyes for me. I'm where you where, five years ago. The top of my profession, and it kills you that the man you helped mold has moved beyond realms you ever reached. I did to Rick Showboat what you couldn't do to Rob Cole, and its eaten at you to the point that you came out of retirement to see if you could still hang with the best wrestlers in the
AWMC. <Lawrenze chuckles> Lawrenze: You claimed to have left because you where worried about your wellfare. You said something along the lines of "still being able to walk"
when you retire. For some reason, you've come back, but if next time you retire, you still wanna be wheel-chair free, get off my jock!
<Lawrenze shoves the cameraman to the floor as he heads for the exit> <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> RETRIBUTION & SUICIDE <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [Retribution and Suicide are sitting around what appears to be a hotel room. Suicide is sitting at the little table, reading his "1001 Ways To Commit Suicide" book. Suicide has his mask on, with the lower half of it tucked under his nose to reveal the lower half of his face. He has on normal clothes. He seems content for the time being. Retribution is smoking a cigar and laying on one of the beds, flipping through the channels. The remote is screwed into the end table between the beds. Retribution is apparently getting annoyed at that fact. He is wearing jeans, a plain black shirt, and his cowboy boots with silver plated tip. His face is still bruised in a few places.]
Retribution: Damn people. They think yer gonna still a damned remote. What kinda crap is that. But anyways, you see that I still have the war wounds from the last time we met Sin, but you now know of what I spoke about. You, me....Nuclear Bomb Match number two for the AWMC and the third for my career. To my knowledge, the previous two are the only Nuke matches in existenca, and you know what happened in them. Hell, there is not gonna be any difference in the ending to this one either. I WILL win the damned thing, becuase I'm the only man to have been in the previous two and I won them. I know what needs to be done. I know how to get myself physically and mentally ready. You don't. You have something that is mine and I'm damn sure going to get it. You are walking into my world, Sin....and to your Hell.
[Retribution flips the channels a few more times before getting pissed about the remote being stuck down. So he fixes that by pulling it loose from the end table, breaking the remote. He tosses it against the television near his feet.]
Retribution: Sinister, I am going to be....for thethird time....the Extremist champion. And I am and always will be YOUR and everyone else in AWMC's Franchise. Now turn that camera off. [The camera does nothing.] Retribution: Aight....Hey Suicide....I think the cameraman plans to steal yer book. [Without hesitation, Suicide springs to his feet and lunges at the camera. He begins sending lefts and rights into the lens of the camera till it shatters and cuts out.]
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> SHAKUR <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> (Shrouded in black, Kuwasi Shakur stands……shirtless, pensive and exuding a manner of menace. His left hand gestures as he talks -- swift, pointed moves that punctuate his reasoned tone. Somewhere John Coltrane's "My
Favorite Things" disrupts the oppressive heat and tenseness in the area. Shakur seems to ignore the interruption, and addresses the camera, his face hard and his words deliberate.)
Shakur: It’s time to take on some assumptions, not to mention the people who make them. I'll be the first to tell you I'm not a man who likes to bother with sniping and innuendo. I like to put words into action. The new
Yakuza’s been into sniping for some time, and dreaming of silencing that is foolish. Some people need something to talk about to validate themselves.
They’d never dream of putting those words into action, even if they look like fools in the process. I’m not gonna be the one to continue the back-and-forth. That’s for schoolkids. But I’m gonna speak on it and leave it there until one of these buffoons makes the move they’re afraid of.
[The single light dims, then flickers back.] Shakur: Of all people, Trey, you’re probably the guy I’m _least_ disappointed in -- least because I expected this of you. Nah, I don’t hate you or have something to prove with you. I don’t need to resort to your usual ‘beep me please’ to show I’m something. What I feel is more pity than anything. You know as well as me I’ve always been the better man, the champion, the legend. I was the infamous long before you copped my words to give yourself some fame, and I’d sooner start the Trey Lawrenze fan club then let you rewrite history to fit your little world. I was the guy who spoonfed you to what you are, embarrassing though that may be. And it’s sad to say you’re still the same cheap, egotistical baboon I tried to break you of. But I won’t bemoan it. It’s your responsibility to become a man, not mine. I can’t change you from the gossip you are. You need to want that. I can’t make you something. You have to. It’s clear you ain’t reached that yet, but that’s not my problem. I did my part. It’s your time to do yours.
[More dimness, then the light is renewed.] Shakur: Too busy kicking ass? Legend killer? Please. Your claim to fame has been being part of the worst tag team in AWMC history with Head Hunter -- a decision _you_, not me, made, and a guy _you_, not me, wanted in Yakuza.
Oh, and lest we forget the immortal Lawrenze appearances. How many times I sat in the back and watched you go break up matches among people you had nothing to do with for no reason but to stroke your ego and damage others’ credibility. Don’t blame the Don for your own faults or the fact you’re an egomaniac, boy. Be a man, accept growing up and move on. And you wonder why you've never been a world champion? You’ve always been an opportunist who’s all talk until it benefits you, Trey -- and it’s been obvious since your first day you’d make up a story if it benefited Trey Lawrenze. Again, I can’t change that. It’s on you to make something of yourself. I gave you the tools, but you choose to shove them up your ass. Personal problem. Not mine.
[Shakur quiets for a second, looks down a bit, then looks up, devilish smirk on his face.] Shakur: Do you really want to challenge me, pup? Don’t talk the noise to a camera. Call for a contract and I’ll be happy to put you in your place one more time. Until then, may as well keep my name off the little list written on your sleeve. You’re just talking trash about me, Cole or anybody else you think can raise your profile. Problem is, you’re not Cole’s level, never will be and never could be. Take it from a man who’s actually beaten
Cole. You’re nothing special, and I’m a man who’s on the level you’d slit your own mother’s throat to be on. But in Trey Lawrenze’s world, everyone fears the man who wouldn’t have an identity had I not put it on his sorry ass, ‘enforcer.’ When you get a clue or something bright to say besides
‘fuck’ or ‘shit,’ gimme a call. Otherwise, you’re just wasting my time. [The smirk turns to an almost-placid smile. His words are now reflective as Shakur continues.] Shakur: And you, Takada. It’s a real shame to watch you unravel. The strong relegated to weak pot shots and posturing rather than face facts. Look in the mirror and see the past, boy. Look at me and see what is timeless as combat itself. Loyalty? Don’t bother questioning it. You knew as well as little Jethro over there I was out of AWMC on business. You talk about me skipping out on you. Lawrenze talks about Cole running me out. Maybe you should try getting your lies straight. Not that either of you ever got what coherent meant anyway.
[Shakur clasps his hands -- an almost patronizing gesture -- as he glares, still smiling.] Shakur: Keep this in mind, m’man. No one keeps me on any ‘side’ of anything. I’ll be happy to kick your teeth down your throat again anytime.
Same with Lawrenze. I’ve beaten him before, it’s not like stealing some phrases and being your stooge has made him any better. He’s still the insecure kid who lives more off idle threats and swearing. You’re still the guy who talks a lot of smack about tradition, but needs to security in muscle to make it so. Oh yeah, let's not forget I beat your ass too. Hear what I said about Lawrenze? You got the mouth to talk that nonsense to the camera when you oughta be talking it into a contract and putting words to action. Isn’t that the ‘traditional’ way to handle business anyway? In the ring? Don’t talk it, grasshopper. Start walking it. Otherwise you oughta just take my name out of your mouth and go complaining about Ethan Love or whatever. You always seem to find something to whine about anyway. Sad.
[A final dimness. Shakur speaks in a relaxed tone.] Shakur: I see a lot of familiar faces here. Sinister, who’s been a tough customer his whole career. Cole. Plenty more. I’ll leave that for another day. For now, Monday night, The Don and Mizukawa fight for the tag team titles. Our opponents are skilled wrestlers, and it’s our goal to outlast them in a no-holds-barred battle. At No Apologies III, we make our move.
The Don speaks, you listen. End of discussion. [Fade to black.] <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> HEADHUNTER <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> (Fade In.) (Screen opens to a black and white shot of an AWMC ring, set in the middle of an empty arena. Standing in the ring, is Double H The HeadHunter in training gear, as he shadowboxes during this action shot. The camera then cuts to a shot of Double H during various training exercises before finally fading to a still shot, with Double H sitting on a chair in the middle of the ring. He has a towel draped over his head, and resting on his lap is the belt that formerly represented him as one half of Death Row, the AWMC tag team champions. The belt sticks out particularly in the shot, because it's the only object in the shot that has color, it's gold is shining as it illuminates the scenery.)
Double H(Looking down at the belt): It meant something. (Double H contnues to stare at the belt on his lap.) Double H: I had Yakuza, but Death Row was more than just Yakuza brothers, it was best friends growing up in the business. The fact that we went through any and everyone the AWMC brass thrown at us, proved the fact that we were unstoppable. Nothing could hold us back, or so I thought.
(Pause.) Double H(Looking up at the camera.): And of course you all know the rest of the story don't you. You know about me outshining Trey as the time went along. You all know about 'ow I was becoming THE rising star 'ere in the
AWMC. 'ow I was on the verge of becoming the first man to ever 'old two AWMC titles at the same time. I was the number one contnder for the Extremist
Title, and Trey and I were Death Row, the AWMC Tag Team Champions. (Pause.) Double H: I couldn't see through the lines. I didn't see it coming. In fact now that I tihnk about it, me leaving the AWMC a while back was the perfect thing for Trey. No longer would 'e be back in my shadow, no longer would 'e be thought of as the weak link in Yakuza, with me leaving, no longer would
Trey 'ave tag team obligations, it would be perfect, perfect for Trey, 'is time, 'is time to shine. So I walked away.
(Pause.) Double H: With no regrets. (Double H hops up from his seat. He places the belt on the chair, and turns back to face the camera.)
Double H: I 'ad no regrets until my return to the AWMC. (Double H starts walking around the ring.) Double H: When I returned I saw that while my "buddy" Trey Lawrenze was at the top of 'is game, and better than ever, something wasn't the same. I couldn't immediately put my finger on what it was. I saw a more ambitious
Trey, a more conniving Trey. Some would say a better Trey Lawrenze. Maybe so, maybe not. The truth is, in returning to the AWMC, immediately, I felt like an outsider.
(Pause.) Double H: The Don, Kuwasi Shakur wasn't there to provide the leadership that the young guns like Trey and 'Yoshi needed. They were left to lead their own paths without any guidance. nd they've done well. They've done well in the ring, in the AWMC overall, but most importantly, they've done well to dismiss anything Yakuza ever was, and form what they now 'ave, a weaker
Yakuza, a Yakuza that is no longer a brotherhood, a Yakuza that's now all about young guns with terrific talent, but who by their own words and deeds are like thorns in their paths
(Pause.) Double H: A Yakuza that isn't even the best stable in the AWMC. A yakuza that no longer is feared. A Yakuza that no longer owns this the AWMC. Yes
'Yoshi is the AWMC Champion, then again 'Yoshi is a 'ell of a wrestler. Trey apparently 'as been mowing down the competition, which I can't really fathom as being true because Trey will always be an average wrestler at best. And this Masked Plague, the man who took my place, 'e's nothing but a gimmick, a joke, a man who fits in perfectly with this new Yakuza because all 'e 'as is a reputation, a front to the outside. And like Yakuza, you Masked Plague, as time goes on, you will be seen as you truly are, weak, vulnerable, and not worth my time of day.
(Double H walks over to a turnbuckle and sits atop it.) Double H: This game is all about winning. To be the best, you've got to beat the best. To become a legend, you've got to be the best for a while. You don't survive this game, you don't become a legend, you're nothing in this game, if you don't win.
(Pause.) Double H: To win, you've got to strike at every good opportunity, and you've got to be sucessful. To win you've got to outsmart your foes, you've got to be quicker, you 'ave to think faster, you always 'ave to be ahead, one step ahead of your foes.
(Pause.) Double H: That answers the question about why I took out Trey. You see I could've attacked 'im during the match, but that's not my style. Besides I wanted to wait until everyone 'ad seen that the supposedly awesome Trey
Lawrenze couldn't even defeat Retribution, a man I've once defeated in an AWMC ring. (Pause.) Double H: So I waited until after the match. I waited until everything was set. And then standing face to face with Trey in the middle of the ring, I took 'im out, you all saw it. I laid out that worthless mass of sh(beep) and beat 'im like 'e should've been beaten a long time ago. I struck, at the perfect opportunity. I was successful. I had thought quicker, I was faster in my actions, and I outsmarted my ol' buddy Trey Lawrenze and the rest of
Yakuza. I accepted the war that Yakuza wanted, and I won the first battle. (Pause.) Double H: But the war continues. Yakuza is going to come after me, I know it. They're as bloodthirsty as ever, and they want revenge. One man destroyed one of their own. One man, took out one of their "shinign stars".
No I'll correct that, one man who was one of their own, me, I took out the supposed shinign star, I took out Trey Lawrenze. So now, now Yakuza is going to come after me, I say....bring it on. We're in a war, I don't expect mercy, I don't expect you to 'old anything back....all I expect is for
Yakuza to know that whatever they do....I'll come back, and I'll do it tenfold. (Double H hops down from the turnbuckle.) Double H: As for this Boa, you 'appen to be the first man I wrestle on my return to the AWMC. I'm looking forward to the challenge. I look forward to going one on one with you. This is going to be great because you're getting the lesson for of a lifetime, free of charge. Well....if you don't mind me taking you out after I teach you why I am the best young talent in the agme today.
(Pause.) Double H: Boa, I don't know a lot about you,a nd quite frankly, i don't care to. Every match I wrestle, every time I step into the ring, I know that I
'ave a job to do. And after outsmarting my opponents, outwrestling my opponents, and after I walk out with the win, then my job is done. It doesn't matter who the opponent is, I get the job done. Boa this time, it just 'appens to be you, oh well atleats you can tell your grandkids someday that you once wrestled the great Double H....The HeadHunter.
(Pause.) Double H: My first match back. 'ard to believe it's been so damn long!! I now know that I took too damn long in returning to the AWMC. A lot 'as been
'appening 'ere, and in the coming months, even more will 'appen. A lot just isn't right, and since no one else seems to 'as the balls, or maybe the talent and skill to make the necessary changes, I guess it's up to me.
Yakuza, you've met your match in the past, but now that we've been reintroduced in different roles, now you'll see that you've met who will be superior to you. Boa, and Yakuza, in different times and places, you'll see why they call me "The New Franchise"......of the AWMC, and you'll learn indeed why they call me.....ol' Double H....The HEADHUNTER!!
(Double H walks over to the chair where his Death Row Tag Team belt is lying. He turns to the camera before kicking over the chair, and sending the belt flying. The camera follows the belt as it falls to the floor. Double H leaves the ring making sure to step on the belt, before he walks away....as the screen fades to black.)
