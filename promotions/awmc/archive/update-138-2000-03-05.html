In a shocking turn of events, AWMC Big Man and Yakuza Enforcer Trey Lawrenze made his presence felt, kaboshing both Retributiona nd 'Flyking' Will Geddings following the Main Event. Lawrenze then fired back agaisnt the fans and the AWMC Front Office, and threatened physical violence if he doesn't receives his long-overdue Title Shot. Motor City Madness: Fisticuffs Out, Sterling In [Indianapolis, IN] - Motor City Madness proved to be chaotic as ever. We witnessed a great Double Debut by both Xavier Sterling and 'Top Gun' Ben Tuckwell, with Sterling snatching the win. Hiroyoshi Takada sidelined Father Fisticuffs from his Motor City Title Match at Sin City Showdown following a pulled deltoid from the Takadalock. By virtue of Sterling's win, X-Man will replace The Punchin' Padre at Sin City Showdown's Motor City Title Match, now facing off against Champion 100 Percent. AWMC: Tag Team Tournament Announced [Detroit, MI] - Executive Director Henry Paxson announced a second annual Double-Elimination Tag Team Tournament, to be held in AWMC. The winners of the Double-Elimination Tournament will go onto face the Tag Team Champions at Cruise Control III in May. The winners of last years Tournament, Platinum Strapped, ventured on to face Death Row at Cruise Control III. Who will win it all this year? No Apoligies II: Anarky Reigns Supreme [Washington, DC] - We saw the return of "champions", a brutal injury, and pure Anarky at No Apologies II : The Battle of Georgetown. First, "Alaskan Assasin" Kyle Jones was injured in the 6 Man No DQ Tag Match. Jones is expected to be out at least 1 month. We also witnessed the return of AWMC Tag Team of '99 when SeX & Guns returned, injuring Hex. SeX & Guns have replaced Hex for the PPV. And a chaotic Main Event saw Sinister retain his Extreme Title. Rue: Interview Is Imminent [Saginaw, MI] - The Fox Network, the company that owns the rights to show Explosion and Motor City Madness, has filmed a shoot interview with AWMC's favorite jerk, Eddie Rue. The interview comes live from the Saginaw City Jail, where Rue has spent the past three weeks for the damage he caused on an AWMC Explosion in which he blew up Ethan Love's dressing room and the ring. The segment will air on this upcoming Motor City Madness, and is a can't miss interview! Rue tells everyone when he'll get out of his cell, thoughts on various superstars, and MORE!
