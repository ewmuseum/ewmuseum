Masked Plague, who was on the receiving end of Four Castout Powerbombs, was unconcious in the ring for almost 5 minutes after the show went off the air. Plague is in OK condition and will continue to wrestle. Cole and Yazkuza Enforcer Trey Lawrenze are still slated to clash at Cruise Control III: Revelations. Motor City Madness: Assault & Battery Score Upset [Boston, MA] - Rookie AWMC Tag Team Assault & Battery scored an upset pin over AWMC Tag Team of the Year SeX & Guns on Motor City madness in the finals of the War of Attrition Tag Team battle. Assault & Battery now move on to face Southern Posse fo rthe Great Lakes Heirtage Tag Team Titles at Cruise Control III: Revelations Explosion: Musashi Fires Back Against Yakuza; Takada To Face Sinister at Cruise Control III [Charlotte, NC] - When AWMC President Akira Musashi made a statement at the beginning of Epxlosion concerning Yakuza, he was not kidding. He was not to be taken lightly. After Hiroyoshi Takada interjected into the Extreme Title contest to end the best-of-five series for Retribution and Sinsiter, he called down the Thunder from the AWMC Executive Board. Musashi proved his critics who called him weak and frightened wrong, as he ordered AWMC Hiroyoshi Takada to defend his Heavwyweight Title at Cruise Control III against Number One Contender Sinister! Explosion: The Threat Scores Big; Tides Change In Their Favor For Cruise Control III [Charlotte, NC] - Daniel Wright pulled the wool over the eyes of all, as he tipped the Tag Team Title Match at Cruise Control III in the favor of the The Threat following a controversial Main Event, which saw Wright make a fast 3 Count on Johnny Bagwell, also saw Wright join the forces of Shane Christopher and Sebastion Manning, who reunited wiht Brittany Christopher. Neither Contagion or Shakur have commented as of yet, but don't expect the two teams to remain silent. Explosion: Sinister & Retribution: A Matter Of Respect [Charlotte, NC] - I what could be one of the most shocking developments in AWMC History, we saw Retribution and Sinister engage in a handshake and declare a mutual respect for one another following an extraordinary Extreme Title defense that saw Retirbution retain the title following a No Contest. Retirbution goes on to Cruise Control II to defend his Title against Obsessive Compulsive Disorder, and Sinister faces off against Takada. Retribution stated he will be there for Sinister..but can the self-proclaimed Franchise be lying once more? Explosion: Storm, Rhodes To Settle Score At Cruise Control III [Charlotte, NC] - For the second time, AWMC Motor City CHampion "Wild Child" Brian Storm and "Supreme Being" jeremy Rhodes pushed it to the limits, and for the second time the match ended with controversy. Following a Double Knockout, Akira Musashi amde his rpesence felt for the first time by stating that the two men will face off at Cruise Control III in a No-Holds-Barred Ladder Match for the Motor City Title!
