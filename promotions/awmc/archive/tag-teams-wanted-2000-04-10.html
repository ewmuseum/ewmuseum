 Allstar Wrestling of the Motor City (AWMC) is currently looking for Tag Teams who could pose compeition to AWMC's Two Tag Team Titles: AWMC Tag Team Championship Titles (Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur/Ujimoto Mizukawa) Titles awarded to those wrestlers who team for action. These titles are on the same tier as the top singles belt.
AWMC Great Lakes Heritage Tag Team Titles (Southern Posse) Tag Titles awarded for those who team together. Second tier to Tag Team Championship Titles. Attached below is the AWMC Tag Team Application Allstar Wrestling of Motor City (AWMC) is an e-mail-based fantasy wrestling federation, which is booked and utilizes a closed-flash (RPs sent in flash packets) system. AWMC was founded in December 1997 by [name withheld] (known for his characters "The Legend" Sean Darring and "Fantasy" Brett Young) and has been a well-regarded fed (#4 top fed in 1998 RSPWF Source Awards, various
RSPWF 200 nominees, etc.) during his tenure. The fed, so the storyline goes, was formed when TDW (Top Dog Wrestling) ran out of money. So they moved to
Detroit and renamed the fed Allstar Wrestling of Motor City. The AWMC is based out of Detroit, Michigan.
In June 1999, [name withheld] stepped down from running the fed as well as all administration, and new bookers took over the fed. Several new policies came in All members have a handler list to refer to and you're encouraged to work with other handlers to develop angles, alliances and feuds. We took this policy to encourage more community among handlers and to take e-w back to the old school. In July, AWMC bought out the ADEF promotion and merged operations. In August, AWMC signed agreements with [name withheld]'s Ultra Japan Pro and [name withheld]'s Cross Continental Wrestling for future talent exchanges. Also, AWMC bought out Carl Blair's Unlawful Wrestling Council in
January.
