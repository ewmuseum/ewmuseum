Only one thing has been confirmed: The 30-Man Royal Rumble for a Heavyweight Title shot at Summer Warfare 2000. "Flyking" Will Geddings vs. "Hate Child" Ethan Love is one. Jean Paul Valley is one name being tosses around for the Main Event, as is "Heartless" Eddie Rue, Sinister, and others. Will Retribution step up? Could Trey Lawrenze receive his shot? Wil Rob Cole re-challenge Takada? Dave Meltzer has reported that a Rob Cole-Trey Lawrenze showdown is inevitable, while others say Cole will battle Masked Plague. Ryan Violent was recently heard speaking out about how he has "paid his dues" and "deserves a shot at Ethan Love". Jeremy Rhodes has fired shots at Brian Storm, and SeX and Guns have challenged Will Geddings for a Tag Title match. Sin City Showdown II: Takada Scores, Showboat Loses, GLH Title In The Air? [Las Vegas, NV] - AWMC's Sin City Showdown Pay Per View proved to be be pure Anarchy. In what soem would call and upset, Hiroyoshi Takada, a former AWMC Champion, regained his title, defeatign Rob Cole in the final match fo their Best Of Three Series. Takada won with assistance of Trey Lawrenze. Trey lawrenze, earlier in the evening, sent Rick Showbaot packing out of AWMC in The Ultimate Gamble match. Also: Eddie Rue and Ethan Love fought to a No Contest with Extreme Circumstances. Rumours through AWMC Offices are starting to become evident: Could the GLH Title become vacant? SInister recaptured the Extreme Title.
