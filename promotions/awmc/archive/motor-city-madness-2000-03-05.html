Oh, but we were saved by Xavier Sterling, who looks to be a true talent in the AWMC. Sterling, or X-Man as I shall call him, stole the show and pretty much dominated this match. Early on,
Tuckwell had him reeling, but X-Man fought back and landed a beautiful superkick followed by a springboard crossbody for two. He then threw
Tuckwell out side, and hit a plancha, followed by an insane Asai moonsault into the crowd on Tuckwell. I think Tuckwell just wants to be like Andrew
Tucker ... sorry :) Sterling hit a nice baseball slide and suicide dive that pretty much had Tuckwell done, but X-Man wanted more. He hit a perfect spingboard senton for the pin.

The match begins to pick up when Virtue goes for a piledriver, but Violent uses his momentum to spin it over and nail Virtue with a vicious piledriver. While this is happening, the crowd pops for someone in the crowd. Its SEAN CHRISTOPHER! After weeks of absence,
Sean is seen again, standing in the shadows of the crowd. Back to the action where Violent has a anklelock on Virtue, but Virtue reaches the ropes.
Violent tries a jumping leg lariat, but Virtue catches him and tosses over his head to the arena floor. Huge pop for the sickening thud. Virtue follows
Violent out and whips him into the ringsteps. Virtue brings Violent back in and nails a big boot for two. Virtue then stupidly goes to the top rope.
Violent has enough left in him to follow him up and nail the signature
Violentplex. Virtue back to his feet quickly and misses with a lariat ...
superkick by Violent followed by a new finisher ... a cradle piledriver he calls the Highlight Reel. He got the win.
