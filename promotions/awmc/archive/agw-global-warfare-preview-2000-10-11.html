[AGW] Global Warfare Preview

11 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[DETROIT, MI] - Detroit was proud to announce that on Wednesday, October 18 in Chicago, Illinois, Allstar Global Wrestling GLOBAL WARFARE will return, on a new day of the week! The show looks packed:
BILLY HIGGINS, former World and North American Tag Team Champion will step into the singles division, battling the bizarre, the one and only, "THE
DREAM" MICHAEL BAILEY. Expect fists and skirts to be flyin' in this one.
Falco International's own JEAN PAUL VALLEY will hope to climb the AGW plateau as he takes on "INNOVATOR OF EXTREME" TRACKER and former AWMC
Extremist champion "SWEET" JARRED BOMBAY in a THREE WAY DANCE! With Contagion departing from AGW, the AGW World Tag Team Titles will be up for grabs. THE THREAT will once again look to vie for the straps, but they'll have to take on SIMPLY FLAWLESS and the underdog duo of OTIS
LONNEGAN and JOSE CALVERA, who turned alot of heads by making short work of teams through the Double Elimination Tag Team Tournament! First pinfall will capture the belts in what should be a war!
The arena will rock when AGW Fan Favorite "HATE CHILD" ETHAN LOVE will step into the ring to battle AGW's Number 1 Contender, the mammoth TREY LAWRENZE!
With Yakuza looking on at ringside, will Love be able to pull it off? Or will Lawrenze's reign of terror continue?
And in our Main Event.... They blew the roof off the Silverdome in the Main Event at Cruise Control
III: Revelations. And on Global Warfare, they'll do it one more time. 2-time former AGW/AWMC World Heavyweight Champion SINISTER will battle his longtime rival, and former Great Lakes Heritage and Two Time AWMC Heavyweight
Champion HIROYOSHI TAKADA, IN A STEEL CAGE! Will Yakuza make it's presence felt? Will Retribution be there to help Sinister out of respect? It's gonna be insane, and a memorable one for some time!
----- [name withheld] President, Allstar Global Wrestling [detail withheld]
