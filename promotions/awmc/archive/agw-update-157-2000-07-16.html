The match had swicthed, going from Lawrenze-Retirbution versus Sinister-Organ Grinder, to ISnister and Retirbution teaming up to battle The Organ Grinder and Lawrenze. Following the match, Rob Cole appeared with former Referee mark barnett. Barnett finally got his revenge for the years of torment Retribution put him through. STORM RETURNS, THROW DOWN CHALLENGE Following an insane Triple Threat Tag Team match between ChronoSynthesis, The Sensational Dreams, and Nemesis, "Wild Child" Brian Storm returned, making his first TV appearance since his injury he suffered at the hands of Dr. Hate. Storm dealt punishemnt to Hate and Pegasus, before chellnging Dr. Hate to a Ladder Match for the National Title at Sandblast: All Fired Up. Will Dr. Hate accept? GPW INVASION "Midnight" Darren Skiles returned to his old stomping grounds on Monday, along with Global Pro Wrestling's World Champion, Tommy gunn. The two riled the AGW Home crowd, before eventually calling out AGW CEO Henry Paxson. Claiming to have a "peace agreement", then two atheletes attacked Paxson, leaving him left on a broken table, with "GPW" spraypainted on him. HOODED FIGURE RETURNS The Hooded Figure returned once again this week, catch Jean Paul Valley with a fireball. The previos week it was trey Lawrenze being ignited. When will he trike again? What are his intentions? SHAKUR CAPTURES EXTREMIST TITLE, NOSMO KING RETURNS Living up to his Challenge, Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur defeated Obssesive Compulsive Disorder on Global Warfare, capturing the AGW Extremist Title. A Post match brawl followed, and we saw the reappearance of Dr. Nosmo King. King brought along a monstros man, named Cravin Canity. Quinn Samuels interjected himself into the rbawl, and is set to face Sanity in a Falls Count Anywhere Match at Sandblast, while Shakur will be defending his title now against OCD.
