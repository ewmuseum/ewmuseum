The scene zooms back out and the silhouette of a man sitting in a beachchair with his arms crossed behind his head can be seen. The camera then moves to the right in a circular motion until it is directly in front of the man.]
[The camera then zooms in and the striking good looks of Kenny Kopecky can be seen. Kopecky is smiling as he wears his black Oakley shades and a blue and white hawaiian shirt with tan cargo shorts and a gold chain around his neck.
He uncrosses his arms and sets them to his side as he lifts his shades just above his forehead. He looks around the scene as if preparing to speak, and then does...]
KK : You know...the AWMC is beginning to become nothing more than a bad joke. Why? Because look around people. Half of them couldn't even find their ass with both of their hands. And somehow, we have a guy by the name of 100
Percent as our AWMC Motor City Champion. Now how the hell did that happen? This guy is a damn joke. And somehow, I look over my shoulder and I have the damn referees slipping in a fast count on me. Well thats fine, it really is, because I am so much more, and so _MUCH BETTER_ than _ANYONE_ in the AWMC!
[Kopecky smiles confidently.] KK : Yeah sure we may have some guys in the back that may _THINK_ otherwise, but it is _COMPLETELY_ true! Hell your champion of the AWMC, is
Rob Cole. Now where the hell has the AWMC gone? I mean common, the "Outcast" Rob Cole? Yeah I wonder how he got that name? Oh let me think, he was outcast out of all of the other prosperous promotions because he _ABSOLUTELY SUCKED!!!_ And please if your AWMC Champion or _ANYONE_ else has a problem with that, I am ready and waiting for your suggestions or comments!
[Kopecky laughs smugly in an arrogant manner.] KK : Shall I continue on in my tirade? Why not? Next we have the "Wild Child" Brian Storm, that continues to come out week after week and cry his little eyes about how "Mr. Attitude" is all of this...and he is _NOTHING!_
Well "Step Child" Brian Rainstorm, anytime you want me one on one, you just let me know. Because I would take a few minutes out of my day to smack you around the ring like the little red-headed stepchild that you are! And to come out and tell me that you actually of all people, pinned these shoulders to that mat? _ARE YOU A COMPLETE MORON?_ There isn't anyone in the AWMC that could pin these shoulders to the mat. And that is a proven fact!
[He swivels in his beachchair and positions himself directly towards the camera.] KK : Hell, when I watch our so-called "main-eventers" wrestle, it looks like a damn three ring circus, and one baboon is spanking the other chimpanzee. As funny and messed up as it may sound, its _TRUE!_ Sometimes I begin to even wonder who the hell _BOOKS_ this crap! _PAT?_ Is that a girl's name or a freaking guy's name? Or maybe you are a relative of the gameshow host? If you wanna book something "entertaining" for the AWMC, Takada versus
Rob Cole ain't gonna get that. Hell I remember seeing them as jobbers, _FOR THE JOBBERS!_ It is just like a snowball rolling down a steep hill, it just keeps getting bigger and bigger until it rolls right of the edge of a cliff!
And at the head of that cliff..._IS PAT!_ [Kopecky raises a confident smile as the hot glowing sun glares off of his forehead.] KK : I have _NOTHING_ to prove to the AWMC. Because I am just _TOO DAMN GOOD_ for your simple-minded brains to comprehend. My skills are far more superior than what you are accustomed to. Hell, I am a _CHAMPION_ right now,
I play with the big dogs elsewhere. But would you like to know why? Because the AWMC _ABSOLUTELY SUCKS!_ The only reason I am here is so that when your champion makes a mistake, I will be there to laugh directly into his face!
And _PAT_ can crawl under his little plastic playschool desk and hide from me when I come and ask for my release. I came to the AWMC because of all the crap I heard about it. But in reality, it is all a bunch of hype! Like
Retribution for instance...this guy was nothing _BUT_ hype. I come here for a challenge, a chance to wrestle with a big dog, but when showtime comes, all that is there is a little puppy to pat on its head. So what the hell is that?
[Kopecky grins arrogantly, almost daring someone to speak a word to him.] KK : But now, I have more pressing issues to attend to right now. And those pressing issues are Ryan Violent. _KISS MY ASS!_ [Kopecky flashes his trademark smirk as you know he is about to say something interesting.] KK : Ok back to Mr. Ryan Violent. This is the AWMC, the federation of losers, that under real competition, I'd beat your asses in a flat second. But lets say I defend my title victoriously one night in another promotion, and some bitch like Ryan Violent actually pins my
shoulders to the mat, like that will mean anything to me? Hell no. But then, the room just stops dead in my tracks when a man by the name of "Mr. Attitude" Kenny Kopecky enters. I put those flabby chested champions to shame with my muscular physique.
