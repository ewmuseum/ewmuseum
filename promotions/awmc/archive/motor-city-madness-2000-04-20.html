CB: Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to Motor City Madness! Tonight , one of
Four Teams Will Advance on to face Southern Posse at Cruise Control Three for the Great Lakes Heirtage Tag Team Titles! Who's it gonna be?
VR: No question Colin! SeX & Guns! CB: Well Vince, let's see! It's time for SeX and Guns to battle one8seven! <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> TAG TEAM WAR OF ATTRITION one8seven vs. SeX & Guns <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Danyon Tripp of one8seven and SeX of SeX and Guns started this tag-team contest off, and it didn’t take long for the much larger SeX to gain the advantage. Straight off, he backed
Tripp into the corner and begin laying in some heavy fists. He then whipped
Tripp to the
opposite turnbuckle, and followed up straight away with a heavy running clothesline that sent
Danyon Tripp straight to the ground. As Tripp recovered from the brief onslaught, SeX taunted the fans a bit before tagging in his even larger partner, Guns. Guns stopped Tripp from making the tag, and put Tripp down with a bodyslam, followed by a big elbow drop.
Continuing with his usual slow pace, Guns locked on a bulldog headlock in
SeX and Guns
corner to wear Danyon Tripp down. After a little while with that hold, SeX tagged himself back in and raised a knee into Tripp’s gut, sending him down again. SeX wasted a little time playing off to the fans, before uncharacteristically going to the top rope and attempting a shoulder tackle on Tripp, who was just rising up. Tripp saw it coming however, and stepped to the side, hitting a DDT-like maneuver on the airborne SeX. SeX wasn’t getting up from that anytime soon, and this gave Tripp the time to recover. He tagged in the fresh Michael
McKnight, who charged into the ring and planted a big dropkick to the rising
SeX’s face,
sending him right back down again. He followed that with a knee drop, and then went to the top rope, managing to hit a moonsault on SeX. He made the cover, but it was quickly interrupted by Guns, who charged into the ring and planted a nice big stomp to McKnight’s head. Tripp charged in to help his partner, but could only get a couple of hits in on the huge
Guns before being grabbed around the neck and tossed across the ring. McKnight tried to fight back, but nothing phased the much fresher Guns, and as SeX got to his feet, they whipped McKnight into the ropes and performed a huge double flapjack, a move they call the
“Deathtrip.” SeX made the cover as Guns saw to it that Tripp had no ideas of interfering, planting a few stomps to him as the referee slapped the mat for the three count.
WINNERS: SEX & GUNS VIA PINFALL AT 8:12 VR: Woo hoo! One down, one to go for the UNDEFEATED TAG TEAM-- CB: Let's see who they're gonna face! Stylin' With Attitudes or the hot newcomers, Assault & Battery!
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> TAG TEAM WAR OF ATTRITION Assault & Battery vs. Stylin' With Attitudes <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> The match began with Ken Slayer in the ring against Simon Quinley. The size difference was noticeable, but Quinley still managed to gain the upperhand in the early stages by immediately applying a drop toe hold and then laying some stomps into Ken
Slayer’s legs.
Quinley continued to work on the grounded Slayer, dropping a knee to the back of his legs, and then lifting his right leg and slamming Slayer’s knee into the ground.
After this, Quinley immediately tagged in Timothy Clemens, and they hit a double dropkick onto the kneeling Ken Slayer. Clemens applied a rear chinlock on the big Slayer, wearing the big man down even more, but after applying the hold for a little while, Slayer managed to grab onto the ropes, prompting the referee to break the hold. Clemens tagged in Quinley, and bounced off the ropes, then applied a big flying bulldog onto Slayer. Billy Higgins was getting mighty anxious to get into the ring, and as Quinley walked past Stylin’ with
Attitudes corner, Higgins spun him around and dropped Quinley’s throat across the ropes as the referee attended to Slayer. Clemens saw this and charged into the ring to attack Higgins, but the referee held him back, and as the ref was occupied, Higgins charged into the ring and planted Quinley with a DDT, then slid out of the ring, making sure the referee saw nothing.
Ken Slayer took advantage, placing Simon Quinley on his shoulder and delivering a big running powerslam, but instead of making the cover, he tagged in his fresh partner. Higgins came in and immediately began to stomp away on Quinley, before picking him up and delivering a body slam. He then hit one of his signature moves, a stalling suplex into a front neckbreaker, which got a two count before Quinley performed an unexpected kickout, showing just why his nickname is “Tenacious”. Higgins tagged in Slayer, and Slayer whipped
Quinley to the ropes. Although Slayer, Higgins and most of the crowd in attendance didn’t realize it, Clemens made the blind tag to Quinley as he bounced off the ropes. Slayer planted a boot to Quinley’s stomach, and lifted him up for his finisher “The Attitude Adjuster”, but
Clemens delivered a
dropkick to Slayers legs which brought the big man down and Quinley right on top of him.
Higgins charged in at Clemens, only to be back body dropped over the top rope. Clemens then placed Slayer on the top turnbuckle and delivered his finisher “The
Clemensizer” to
perfection. Clemens made the cover as Quinley dived over the top rope and hit a cross body block on Billy Higgins, and Assault and Battery got the three count.
WINNERS: ASSAULT & BATTERY VIA PINFALL AT 7:26 CB: Assault and Battery move on to face SeX & Guns in the main Event! VR: SeX & Guns are facing THEM! HA! Easy win for the TAG TEAM CHAMPION-
CB: Fans, hot newcomer Quinn Samuels looks ot make a big impact here tonight!
VR: He takes on Boa! <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Boa vs. Quinn Samuels <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> “Nitro (Youth Energy)” by The Offspring blares out of the speakers as Quinn
Samuels makes his way out of the entrance in a slow jog. As the music kicks into gear, Quinn begins his walk down the ramp and to the ring, slapping hands with the fans on the way. He arrives at the ring and stops for a couple of seconds, before leaping between the canvas and bottom rope, rolling into the center of the ring and standing quickly rising up, all in one fluid motion. He does a couple of warm-up jogging laps around the ring, signaling to the fans on the way around, before he stops and backs into his corner, waiting for the match to get under way. Boa came out next, drawing a mixed reaction. Match started off slow at first. Collar-elbow tie up led to
Quinn taking advantage and driving Boa down to the mat Quinn continued to lay into Boa, and connected with a vicious Sydney Slam, drawing a great crowd reaction. Boa mounted a comeback and even draw a close three-count following a bulldog form the second rope. Boa whipped Quinn to the ropes, then telegraphed a back body drop. But Quinn leaped up and springboarded off of Boa’s bent over back, bounded off the other ropes then, like lightning, performs his “Thrill 2000” maneuver, which draw a great crowd reaction. Quinn whipped Boa into the corner, then climbed to the second rope. Quinn placed his knee on boa's neck and then fell forward, driving
Boa's face into the ground. Three count later and Quinn picked up another victory.
WINNER: QUINN SAMUELS VIA PINFALL AT 4:56 CB: Samuels continues to make waves! VR: But now it's time to watch the only team that counts kick some ass! <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> MAIN EVENT - Great Lakes Heirtage Tag Team Title Shots Assault & Battery vs. SeX & Guns <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Timothy Clemens and Guns squared off in the opening stages of this rematch from last week, and Guns, who put on a powerful display earlier in the night, took early control. Guns backed
Timothy Clemens back into the corner and began to lay some heavy lefts and rights to the stomach and chest area of the much smaller Clemens, and it didn’t take long for Clemens to hit the ground. Guns tagged in his partner SeX, and as SeX made his way into the ring, Guns hit a textbook vertical suplex on Clemens, followed quickly by a kneedrop from SeX. Guns left the ring as SeX whipped Clemens into the ropes, dropping Clemens’ throat across the ropes with a hotshot on the way back. Unlike earlier in the night, SeX did not decide to play to the fans after hitting his move, but instead went straight back on top of
Clemens, albeit
choking him across the ropes with his knee. The referee broke it up and SeX backed off to the other side, spinning around and planting Simon Quinley with a hard fist that sent the
Assault and Battery member to the ground as the referee was attending to
Clemens. As SeX
moved back on top of Timothy Clemens, Quinley charged into the ring to attack SeX, but the referee forced him back into his corner as Guns, on the outside of the ring, planted an uppercut into Clemens’ jaw. SeX and Guns were by far dominating the match by now as in the ring SeX continued to lay the hurt on the worn out Timothy Clemens, hitting a side
Russian Legsweep that put the other half of Assault and Battery down once more. At this point, the Carolina Kidd and the Texas Playboy of the Southern Posse made their way down to ringside, much to the annoyance of SeX. He moved over to the side of the ring that they were on to mouth off at them, only to receive a swift kick to the head by
Quinley from his
respective corner, which sent SeX stumbling backwards and into a schoolboy pin from
Timothy Clemens, which got a nearfall. SeX immediately stood up and tagged in Guns as Clemens crawled to the corner to tag in Simon Quinley. Guns charged in and planted a boot into Clemens back, sending him face first onto the mat and stopping any chance of Simon
Quinley legally getting into the ring. Guns hit a couple of forearm smashes to Timothy Clemens’ forehead before grabbing him around the waist and hitting a devastating belly to belly suplex. Guns made the cover at that point, but Clemens just managed to kick out before the three. A little frustrated, Guns gave the Southern Posse some unkind words before picking Clemens up over his shoulder for a running powerslam. However as
Guns was about
to drop Clemens, Clemens slipped behind him and pushed him into the turnbuckle, and as
Guns stumbled backwards he showed amazing strength for his size by hitting a release german suplex on the much larger Guns. Clemens crawled to his corner, but just as he made the tag, SeX got the referee’s attention by charging into the ring, and
Simon Quinley ran in
and began to stomp away on the downed Guns. When the referee turned around, he had not seen the tag and therefore forced Simon Quinley out of the ring. Infuriated,
Quinley fought
back wildly as the referee backed him into his corner, and while the referee was distracted,
Texas Playboy joined Quinley and the referee up on the apron as the Carolina
Kidd tossed SeX into the guardrail, rolled into the ring, hit Guns with a DDT and then dragged Timothy Clemens over with authority to make the cover, and then rolled back out again. Texas Playboy dropped down from the apron, and Quinley calmed down a little too as the referee turned around and made the three count, awarding the victory to Assault &
Battery.
WINNERS: ASSAULT & BATTERY VIA PINFALL AT 10:01 VR: NOOOOO!!! THEY WERE ROBBED! CB: Assault & Battery go on to face Southern Posse at Cruise Control Three! We are out of time! Copyright 1999 Allstar Wrestling of the Motor City
