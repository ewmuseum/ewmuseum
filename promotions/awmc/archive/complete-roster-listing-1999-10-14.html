Folks, No word on the card's status yet. In the meantime, attached is a singles and tag roster. Two things you should note: 1.) Some people on this list are inactive and I'll be cutting soon. 2.) If you're interested in forming a tag team, your chances for a push increase exponentially. I've always been a tag wrestling mark and would love to grow the division more. If I accidentally left you off the list, let me know. Also, if you're interested in being in a particular division (i.e. Great Lakes, Extremist, Motor City) tell me. Lastly, if you know of handlers who might be active and interested in being in the fed, have'em send a blank email to [detail withheld] or visit http://awmc.8m.com .
