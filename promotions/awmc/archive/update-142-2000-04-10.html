Wright promptly came on and told the former Threat manager that he was not interested and didn't approve of her behavior at Explosion. Wright then announced a challenge to Contagion and The Threat for a three-way dance for the tag team titles at Cruise Control III. Unfazed by Wright's apparent rejection of Britney Christopher, The Threat came out to accept the challenge and attacked Daniel Wright. Not to be outdone, the tag team champions came out and Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur challenged Shane Christopher to a Texas Death Match that very night. The brutal showdown saw The Threat again double-team Shakur and escape as Mizukawa appeared. MCM: New Champs And More This Week [San Antonio, TX] AWMC came to Texas Thursday with surprises and hot action. Southern Posse, which has fought a bitter war against rivals SeX & Guns, beat their rivals for the Great Lakes Heritage tag team straps in the main event. Meanwhile, Ben Tuckwell and Tony Virtue stunned everyone with a brutal feud brewing to the top this week. Jessie Muldoon, who raised eyebrows last week by declaring his intention to become the company's first gay champion, rolled to victory over Kenny Kopecky and called for all challengers to come at the star. The show also saw Sean Christopher return to action. No Apologies III: Underground Reforms [Detroit, MI] - They're known as the most ruthless, blooddrawing gang of villains and thugs ever assembled. They've decimated and injured countless numbers of opponents. And at No Apologiess III, we saw the return. With the help of AWMC Newcomer Pegasus, Suicide, and Retribution reformed the Underground in AWMC. Could the reformation of this group pose a threat to the two Superpowers Yakuza and Falco International? Will another group step up to face these Megaforces? No Apologies III: Retribution Regains Strap In Nuke III [Detroit, MI] - AWMC fans at the Motor Dome were treated to the possibly the most spectacular in AWMC's History, as Retribution kept his Nuclear Bomb Match winning streak in tact and regaining his Extremist Title by defeating Sinister in the grueling, nearly 30 Minute contest. Boht men are Hospitalized and look to be realesed in the near future. Cruise Control III: Cole Throws Down Challenge To Lawrenze [Dallas, TX] - "Outcast" Rob Cole throw down a challenge that only a man of his past and mindframe could issue. Exploding barbwire boards, thumbtacks, weapons on a Steel Cage that is in the confines of a Cell is what Cole threw down for Lawrenze. Will the big man accept? Mizukawa/Shakur: New Champs, Controversey [Dallas, TX] - Controversey is already surrounding the championship reign of the New AWMC Tag Team Champions, Kuwasi "Blackfists" Shakur and Ujimoto Mizukawa. The match saw interference from jean Paul Valley, Stylin' With Attitudes, and The Threat. Musashi is reportedly very dissapointed over the final outcome, and has been rumoured to readying fines for The Threat and Britteny Christopher.
