AWMC — News

Press Conference

9 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Fade In. ] [ The Oak Room of the AWMC Executive Building in Detroit, Michigan fills with reporters, all anxiously awaiting the arrival of Executive Director of
AWMC Henry Paxson. Paxson has summoned the media here today for an important announcement concerning the future of AWMC. ]
[ Camera flashes erupt as Henry Paxson begins to make his way to the podium in the room. He stands, tall and dignified, looking out to the room full of reporters. Behind Paxson hangs a traditional "Allstar Wrestling of the Motor
City" banner. ]
Henry Paxson: Ladies and gentlemen of the press, I welcome you to Detroit and the AWMC Executive Building. It bring me great honor to announce the dawning of a new era in Professional Wrestling.
[ Paxson pauses. ] Henry Paxson: Through Brian Bell, John Stargel, Jimmy Cage, and Akira Musashi, AWMC has become one of the most renowned organizations in North
America. Since December 1997, Allstar Wrestling of the Motor City has become the cutting edge of professional wrestling. With an outstanding following and fans throughout the world, AWMC has drawn praise from everywhere.
[ Pause. ] Henry Paxson: Recently, Fox Sports, the company who brings you Explosion and
Motor City Madness, has decided to move Explosion from DetroitNET Sports to
Fox Sports on Monday Nights, 8 PM Eastern Standard Time, now giving AWMC
Global and International Coverage.
[ Cheers fill the room as Paxson grins. ] Henry Paxson: With Allstar Wrestling of the Motor City now receiving International Coverage, we can no longer be recognized as a regional fed. No longer will AWMC be considered "small" or "second tier". Today, is the dawning of a new era..
[ The "AWMC" Banner behind Paxson drops and now a new Banner hangs: A G W ALLSTAR GLOBAL WRESTLING The room of reporters erupt with noise and camera flashes as Paxson stands beside the banner with a wide smile. ]
Henry Paxson: As Chairman of the Executive Committee, it gives me great honor to announce that Allstar Wrestling of the Motor City will know be known as Allstar Global League Wrestling!
[ Cheers. ] Henry Paxson: Several changes will come with this. The AWMC Motor City
Title, which was introduced into the league last year and is currently around the waist of "Wild Child" Brian Storm, following Cruise Control Three will then be recognized as the AGW National Title!
[ Commotion begins to stir amongst the reporters. ] Henry Paxson: The Extremist Title will be recognized as the AGW Extremist
Title following the Cruise Control Three battle between Current Extremist
Champion Retribution and Top Contender Obsessive Compulsive Disorder!
[ More commotion. ] Henry Paxson: The AWMC Great Lakes Heritage Championship has proved to be a career maker indeed. Held by such legends as Jared "JC $ilk" Crush,
"Rolemodel" Paul Styles, and currently "Flyking" Will Geddings, following
Cruise Control Three, the Great Lakes Heritage Championship will be known as the AGW North American Title!
[ Several cheers fill the room. ] Henry Paxson: The Great Lakes Heritage Tag Team Titles will also follow the same path as their sister singles belt. The winner of Doom Machines-Assault
& Battery Great Lakes Heritage Tag team Title match at Cruise Control Three will be recognized as the North American Tag Team Champions!
[ More commotion. ] Henry Paxson: A Three Way Dance decides the fates of AWMC's Top Tag Teams at
Cruise Control Three. Top Contenders Contagion, consisting of "The
Revelation" Ryan Violent and "Devil's Advocate" Johnny Bagwell, as well as
Sebastion Manning and Shane Christopher of The Threat, will tackle reigning
AWMC Tag Team Champions Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur and Ujimoto Mizukawa, where the winner is declared AGW World Tag Team Champions!
[ Much talk and chatter arises. ] Henry Paxson: And finally, no longer will the AWMC Heavyweight Title, which currently sits around the waist of Hiroyoshi Takada be called a Heavyweight
Title. At Cruise Control Three, the Heavyweight Title Match between Takada and Sinister will be for the AGW WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP!
[ Much noise and applause for the announcement. ] Henry Paxson: Also, coming with the new international coverage will be a new
AWMC Anchor show. The Monday, May Eighth edition of AWMC Explosion will be it's last.
[ A depressing "awwww..." fills the room. ] Henry Paxson: On Monday nights, at Eight PM Eastern Standard Time, being brought to you by FOX Sports, will be Allstar Global Wrestling's GLOBAL
WARFARE! [ More commotion and cheers for the announcement. ] Henry Paxson: The future of Professional Wrestling is now. The Millenium
Force of Sports Entertainment is upon us. Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for joining me today, and God Bless.
