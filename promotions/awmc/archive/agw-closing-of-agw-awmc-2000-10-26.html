[AGW] Closing of AGW/AWMC

26 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To AGW & Non AGW Handlers: Unfortunately, as you can tell by the Subject, I've decided to quit beating the dying, and face the inevitable. I'm shutting down AGW for good.
This may come as a surprise, maybe not to some of you. I thought I'd be able to keep the league going, I even tried to convince myself by switching bi-weekly Global Warfare. But not even getting on the computer most nights
(this is the first time I've been on in a couple days for more then 15 minutes) till 11, then having to get up at 6 leaves me very little energy to do _anything_ with e-wrestling. So after my six month tenure as AGW Head
Booker, I've decided that it's best to put AGW/AWMC to rest. I have no idea how [name withheld] could run AWMC for as long as he did (December '97 to May '99). Just like [name withheld], 8 months was all I had in me untill real life caught up with me.
First off, I need to give a couple of Thank You's to some people who helped me all along..My Staff. [name withheld], [name withheld], and [name withheld]. You guys helped me keep my shit in gear, and you guys are ALL a big part of AGW, and I credit you guys with everything. I know we had our differences at times, but you guys helped out with _so_ much.
Extra big thanks to [name withheld], whose basically been my mentor for fedheading. He's hooked us up with the website, and was a big inspiration for my to even step up and take over AWMC when it was dying.
Big props to [name withheld]. If it wasn't for him, this fun ride called Allstar Global Wrestling for almost 3 years would've never started. I'm proud to say I followed in your footsteps, and cemented my spot as Disgruntled Asshole ex-AWMC/AGW booker ;) To all you handlers who stuck with AGW through everything...thank you. Y'all are the reason that makes closing AGW down such a hard decision. Good luck to y'all wherever you venture too. Hopefully down the road I'll get to work with all you guys in some leagues and bring fun back to the game. To you crybabies that complained about late cards and never offered any help...I found out the best way to handle cards..don't have 'em. Happy now? You guys know who you are..let the past be the past and move...I could make a list a mile long of things that are more important then Global Warfare being out on time. Grow up, and move on with your lives.
We had our fun (Poking fun at Diggs), we had our mistakes ("Yeah! 8 matches on Global Warfare! We can get it out on time!"), and we had our...uhm...errors (I think leagues we're opening and closing between Global
Warfare). But I've done my 8 months as Booker, and now it's time for me to bow out, and put to rest AGW.
As for me personally, I'll still be lingering around in [name withheld]'s KCW ( http://www.efeduniverse.com/kcw ) as "Midnight" Darren Skiles, and occasionally dipping into Starchat. I'm gonna hang on the DL for awhile and ride out the bulk of the 9 weeks period in school, and put in my godforsaken time at Arby's. This 8 months was a learning experience nonetheless. I took over back in February only with hopes of salvaging AWMC, which seemed on the brink of closing once before. I cranked out one of the worst PPV known to man (Sin City Showdown II), and IMHO, one of the best in AWMC/AGW's history in Cruise Control III. Some of you guys have probably come to hate me for giving you the shaft, some of y'all (Good Ol' Boys) will wonder what to do next since AGW was the only league you'll ever succeed in, since we're all out to push each other and keep the little guy down (kidding ;)). So if you held a grudge against me or anyone else, now would be better then ever to drop it...I mean, since AGW's done with, shouldn't the dumb arguing over stupid things be done with as well?
Here's a list of leagues for y'all to chew on if you're looking to venture into other leagues:
[name withheld]'s Knoxville City Wrestling: http://www.efeduniverse.com/kcw [name withheld]'s Western Shores Wrestling: http://www.efeduniverse.com/wsw [name withheld]'s American Wrestling Promotions: Contact [detail withheld] for info I'm only in KCW for now, but you never know where RON VAN CLAM MAY POP UP NEXT!! I'll still be around at [detail withheld] and I'm leaving the Junta list open as well ( [detail withheld] ), Feel free to drop a line whenever y'all wanna chit-chat. Sorry to anyone I forgot to thank. If I missed you, just say you were secretly telling me how to run AGW and I'll play along with it down the road ;)
So long guys. Thanks for everything. [name withheld] [detail withheld]
