Stan Hansen, Abdullah the Butcher. They would run thru the crowd on the way to the ring. Swinging chairs, chains, bullropes, whatever. NOT STAND FOR. But all the hatred, all the drive, the thirst for vengeance was gone. Kataki took it away.
