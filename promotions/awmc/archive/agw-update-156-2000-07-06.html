It's unclear if Love is formally aligned with the loose grouping, which includes Tracker, Shakur, Miller and "Outcast" Rob Cole, or of Miller's status. Miller appeared at the end of the match to ignite Trey Lawrenze on fire, though he didn't participate in the rest of the match. OCD-SHAKUR CONFRONTATION LEADS TO MATCH Earning significant crowd heat for criticizing the Extremist champion, Sandblast challenger Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur got a real shock at Global Warfare when champ Obsessive Compulsive Disorder appeared and faced off with Shakur over his taunts. A slap and belt shot later, and the men meeting at the Sandblast PPV agreed to battle at the coming week's Global Warfare in a match with stipulations to be named by the challenger. By program's end, a taped fists match was announced for next Global Warfare, with the Extremist title on the line. HEATED ACTION IN MAIN EVENT Trey lawrenze got burned by a mysterious figure who ignited the ring on Global Warfare's Main Event. Not much more is known about this incident. lawrenze managed to escape with only a minor, yet large burn on his left leg. The Masked Man also returned to attack Tracker once more, this time using the AGW North American TItle, leading many to think it was Will Geddings. And lastly, Twizted appeared once more, shattering a Cinder Block over the head of Retribution. BOMBAY RETURNS, CONFRONTS 100 PERCENT Former AWMC Extremist Champion and Impact Player "Sweet" Jarred Bombay returned to AGW, berating former foes, all leading to a confrontation with 100 Percent. Bombay challenged 100 Percent to a macth the next week if Bombay could get medical clearance.
