AWMC — Title Histories

Updated Title Histories

19 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AWMC Title 1. 04/25/98 - Retribution outlasted 29 other wrestlers in a Royal Rumble to become the first ever AWMC Champion. 2. 10/18/98 - "Rolemodel" Paul Styles won the vacant title in the Hell in the Cell 4 way dance at Shattered Dreams. 3. 01/ - Rob Cole was rewarded the AWMC title. Then vacated after his injury. 4. 01/31/99 - Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur won the vacated title vs Paul Styles in the Hell in the Cell at Sin City Showdown. 5. 04/13/99 - "Studmuffin" Kris Stone awarded the Heavyweight Title when Shakur stripped. 6. 04/13/99 - Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur beat Stone. 7. 05/02/99 - "Outcast" Rob Cole defeated Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur at Cruise Control . 8. 07/01/99 - Hiroyoshi Takada defeated "Outcast" Rob Cole. 9. 08/15/99 - Dr. Hate defeated Hiroyoshi Takada. 10. 09/19/99 - "Ultimate Male" Chris Del Rio pins Dr. Hate at Revenge, Redemption, Resurrection Great Lakes Heritage Title 1. 03/5/98 - "Spectre" Chris Lee won it in a Battle Royal. 2. 03/12/98 - "Rolemodel" Paul Styles defeated Lee and Justin B in a Triagle match. 3. 06/27/98 - "Playboy" Craig Miller defeated "Rolemodel" Paul Styles. 4. 10/18/98 - "Studmuffin" Kris Stone defeated "Playboy" Craig Miller. 5. 02/28/99 - J.C. Silk (Jarred Crush) beat Kris Stone on Heart Attack. 6. 06/27/99 - Alex Adams captured the vacated title. 7. 08/08/99 - Trey Lawrenze defeated Alex Adams. AWMC Extremist Title 1. 03/30/98 - "Mr. Hardcore" Jarred Crush defeated Venom in the final match of the Extremist Tourney. In a Lights on and off, Table Death match. 2. 06/27/98 - Chris Gotham beat Jarred Crush in a Lumber Jack Match. 3. 07/27/98 - Freaky Ti beat Chris Gotham in a Ladder match. 4. 11/09/98 - Fang defeated Freaky Ti 5. 12/27/98 - "Playboy" Craig Miller beat Fang in a Flaming Cage/Snakepit match. 6. 01/18/98 - Chris Gotham beat Miller on Monday Night Motor Madness. 7. 01/31/99 - Craig Miller beat Chris Gotham at Sin City Showdown. 8. 02/22/99 - Tuulunex beat Craig Miller on Monday Night Motor Madness. 9. 02/28/99 - Craig Miller beat Tuulunex in the Free Fall Match. 10. 02/28/99 - Rob Cole beat Craig Miller at Heart Attack. 11. 03/19/99 - Tuulenux defeated Rob Cole and Organ Grinder in a triple threat match. 12. 03/19/99 - Brandon Baxter defeated Tuulenux in a Ladder, No Holds Barred match. 13. 05/24/99 - Twizted defeated Brandon Baxter in an Inferno match. 14. 06/01/99 - Title retired. 15. 10/03/99 - New champion to be crowned. Motor City Title 1. 08/01/99 - Chase Firebird beat "Ladies' Man" Vince Taylor on AWMC Explosion. 2. 08/15/99 - "Superstar" Carl Blair defeated Chase Firebird on AWMC Explosion. 3. 09/15/99 - "Perfect Ten" Patrick Derek Jefferson pins "Superstar" Carl Blair AWMC Tag Team titles 1. 03/23/98 - Cobra/Viper won the 8 team Bunkhouse Battle Royal. 2. 04/25/98 - Royal King, Royal BallPlayer (VIP) beat Cobra/Viper and Darksiders in a Cage match. 3. 11/29/98 - Urban Legend beat VIP. 4. 01/31/99 - Crazed Animals beat Urban Legend at Sin City Showdown. 5. 03/08/99 - Urban Legend beat Crazed Animals. 6. 03/29/99 - Death Row beat Urban Legend. 7. 07/01/99 - "Midnight" Darren Skiles and "Studmuffin" Kris Stone won vacated titles. 8. 08/01/99 - Kindred Blood defeated "Midnight" Darren Skiles and "Studmuffin" Kris Stone. 9. 09/19/99 - Southern Posse won the tag titles in a triple threat match Special Events 1. 4/25/98 - Retribution won the Royal Rumble at Cruise Control. 2. 11/29/98 - Freaky Ti won Blood Bowl at Blood Bowl . 3. 12/27/98 - Eclipse won the King of the Cruisers battle. 4. 05/24/99 - Retribution captured the Motor City Cup. RETIRED TITLES AWMC Ironman Title 1. 05/01/98 - "Kewl" Kevin Paris brought the title into AWMC. 2. 06/15/98 - Cobra defeated Paris. 3. 07/27/98 - Fang beat Cobra in a Ironman/Snakepit match. 4. 10/20/98 - "The Blade" Justin Cruise won the 6 man Iron man tourney by beating Eclipse in the championship. 5. 02/01/99 - Title changed from Ironman to Middleweight. Cruise remains champion. AWMC Middleweight title 1. 02/01/99 - Title changed from Ironman to Middleweight. Cruise remains champion. 2. 03/08/99 - Brian Black defeated "The Blade" Justin Cruise 3. 02/05/99 - "Rolemodel" Paul Styles won the vacated Middleweight title in a three-way dance against Hiroyoshi Takada and Swingin' Johnny Styles, at Cruise Control. 4. Title retired. AWMC TV Title 1. 05/30/98 - Madcap won the title in a match against Alex Adams 2. Title retired.
