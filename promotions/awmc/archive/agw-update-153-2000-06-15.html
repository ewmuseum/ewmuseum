During the World Championship match between then-champion Sinister and challenger Retribution on Global Warfare, The
Organ Grinder made his presence felt. He aided Retribution in defeating
Sinister, and immediately challenged Retribution for the title. In what will surely go down as one of the most memorable matches in AGW history, The
Organ Grinder defeated Retribution to become World Champion in his first match back. In a truly bizarre twist of fate, it was outside interference from Sinister that sealed Grinder's victory. However, Grinder's celebration was short-lived when AGW President Musashi stripped the belt from Grinder because the returning wrestler did not have a valid AGW contract.
AGW WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP DECLARED VACANT The AGW World Championship was declared vacant following Retribution's loss this week to The Organ Grinder. As announced by President Musashi, the title will be decided at No Apologies IV in a barbed wire rope match between former champions Sinister and Retribution. Returning superstar The Organ
Grinder will act as the special guest referee. With everything that went down between Retribution, Sinister and The Organ Grinder on Global Warfare, this rematch is shaping up to be a bloodbath.
THREE SENIOR REFS RESIGN, WRESTLERS FACE SANCTIONS IN SHAKEUP An emotional Mark Barnett, accompanied by fellow senior officials Bill
Cartwright and Claudia Stevenson resigned as AGW referees on the air Monday at this week's Global Warfare. Citing a litany of complaints, Barnett told the crowd he would pursue other opportunities before facing injury from an increasingly lawless attitude among wrestlers. Cartwright and Stevenson, who came to the company with Barnett from a closed Los Angeles promotion, followed suit and walked out before the live audience. Hoping to quell disenchantment of the remaining three referees, AGW President Akira Musashi forced Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur, "Innovator of Extreme" Tracker and
"Wildside" Craig Miller into stints as guest referees for the night. Shakur was particularly called out by Barnett for his ring behavior. Following the conclusion of the show, Musashi announced Robbie Rocker's promotion as head official, the acquisition of three new referees to replace the departing staff, and the immediate eight-week suspension of "Loose Cannon" Priest
Noble for the lawsuit following the Flic Rogers attack and later assault on
AGW announcers. Is the shakeup over?
GPW RETALIATES; NOBLE RELEASED OVER ROGERS INCIDENT "Loose Cannon" Priest Noble was released by AGW on Sunday following a lawsuit being filed against Noble and AGW concerning Noble's attack on GPW wrestler Flic Rogers. No officials inclined to comment.
FANS RIOT, AGW PAYS DAMAGES Following the decision made by President Musashi to strip The Organ Grinder of the World Championship, a large number of fans in Minneapolis took out their frustration in an unprecedented show of anger. Upset with what they had witnessed, fans expressed their displeasure by committing acts ranging from tossing garbage at the President and his security to the shattering of large windows and damage to washrooms. It was a dangerous scene, as the commentators had to be escorted through the masses. Several AGW security personnel suffered minor injuries during the riot. Local police made 43 arrests in the incident. The AGW has agreed to pay for repairs to the arena, estimated to be somewhere around the $35,000 mark.
MAXIMUM RISK 2000 FORM AND CHALLENGE CHAMPIONS During the presentation of AGW Global Warfare, a new young exciting tag team was formed. Maximum Risk 2000 consisting of established wrestler Ben
Tuckwell and rookie Curt Denton united following the Tuckwell/Keeblor match after Denton assisted the Top Gun with the victory. Maximum Risk 2000 look like they have a lot of potential after their first showing, with a lot of high flying talent in the blood of both men. Following Tuckwell's victory, the new tag team put down a challenge to the AGW North American Champions in the Doom Machines for No Apologies IV. No word yet from the title holders, but word around the company says that it will be booked and should be a match to look forward to.
GEDDINGS MAKES IMPACT IN GPW Will Geddings made some semi-waves on the last GPW card, placing GPW Regional Champion Derek Sweger into an AGW T-Shirt after the two's match. No retaliation yet by GPW offices, but rumor has it they won't be putting out any GPW merchandise for one FlyKing Will Geddings. Geddings, also, kept his incredible AGW Win/Loss Record in tact after defeating Jesse Muldoon in what he called a match of 'nonexistent' proportions, stating backstage he could defeat Muldoon in, amongst other things, a wheelchair with the emergency brakes on.
