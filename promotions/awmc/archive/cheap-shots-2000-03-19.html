The tall buildings dominate the city view like giants among midgets, their lights shining up to seemingly the heavens, igniting the night sky with their radiance. Various flashing lights bring attention to the buildings that have them, serving various purposes. The noises of the city can be heard, honking horns, various laughter, some yelling, sirens belonging to police, fire, and ambulances, while life continues to move forward, marching along with time, some slowly, some very quickly. The picture zooms out to a small cliff where there is a black car with a large figure sitting atop the hood of the car. The figure is large and unmoving, and as the camera zooms in, the figure becomes the familiar shape of one Sinister, who is relaxing on the hood of his custom Mustang GT and taking in the scenario. The camera focuses upon his eyes, and they seem to be distant in some way, indicating that the man from Chicago is thinking of something. He seems rather focused but tranquil as he surveys the hustle and bustle of the life below. Taking a deep breath, he slowly peers into the camera, taps his chin in thought a few times, and speaks*
“Well, well, well. Sin City, how befitting a name for the upcoming PPV huh? Sin City…” [He smiles slowly, a crooked smile that is not all that pleasant] “What better a name for what’s going to happen at that PPV. There will be numerous sins committed that evening, no doubt, and one of the biggest ones will be the war that Retro and I wage in the streets of L.V. If the Mayor of Las Vegas is a smart man, he’ll close down the casino’s that are going to be in the vicinity of the battle because Retro and I are going to use anything and everything that we can get our hands on, and that includes slot machines, crap tables, bowling balls, poker tables, stuff from the buffets, restaurant equipment, and hell, maybe even use part of cars. Las Vegas is known for its shows, and the PPV is going to have one hell of a brutal, bloody, and breath taking event, the explosion that Retro and I are going to produce against one another.”
[He nods his head slowly, his eyes focusing upon the ground for a few moments] “Retro, you bested me with that last move of yours, having Suicide do a pin-strip number on me, stepping in as a referee and blasting me so Retro can have an easy pin. That’s cool though. I’m not going to cry about it or anything like that. I’ll allow my actions to do my talking, and I’ve got plenty to say, believe you me Retro! Right now, you’ve won back the title that has gained you some notoriety, and on that same token, it gained some notoriety for me as well. People stood up and noticed me just that much more after I won the hardcore title and defended it successfully against very good challengers including yourself Retro. For years you’ve mocked me, you’ve insulted me, and you’ve attempted to end my career through both humiliation and injury. Instead of just allowing myself to be bested, I challenged myself to step up to the next level, and the next, overcoming the odds stacked against me, and at the PPV, the level that both of us take this battle is one that very few people will ever reach.”
[He slowly runs his fingers over his cheeks, once again in deep thought. He quickly turns his head at the sound of sirens off in the distance and watches flashing lights speed through the city streets to its destination.
He then continues] “Who knows, maybe Retro and I will both be rushed to a nearby Las Vegas hospital after our battle. Now Retro, everyone knows that you’re one hell of an athlete and a dangerous individual at all times, but you’re stepping into a situation that even you will have a difficult time with. Street fighting is second nature to me, and though you may view that as humorous, degrading, or however you see it, it’s the environment that I had to survive in, and I know how to do that quite well. This match isn’t so much a matter of who’s better, it’s a matter of who wants to survive more, who wants to beat the other man into possible retirement, and who wants to beat respect out of the other man. I personally believe that a man is not a man unless he has a certain amount of respect, and I will earn your respect Retro, though you may not want to give it to me. Through trying to literally maim an individual repeatedly, a certain level of respect is earned if the man refuses to stay beaten down. For example, Rob Cole is one of your most hated enemies, and you his, but the two of you have a mutual respect because after every battle, each one of you kept pressing on despite your physical or mental turmoil.”
[He cracks his knuckles loudly] “If I have to beat it out of you, then so be it Retro, but respect will be had by you for me. You know that I’m a very good wrestler, and this battle of the two big men will be a telling of where each of us stands in the AWMC.
You are well known throughout the realm of wrestling, and I am still trying to make my name known. You have been through numerous historical battles, and I am just beginning to. This is my opportunity to show the AWMC and others that I am just as good a wrestler, just as fierce a competitor, and just as dangerous a man as Retro is, and by whatever means necessary, I will do so! Mr. Greene has taught me a great many things to be a better wrestler as well as a better man, but I can not promise him that I’ll integrate some of his teachings into this match. There are too many angry feelings that I harbor for Retro for his actions of the past and present, and if I withhold anything in this battle, then I have already lost. Retro, I do not plan on losing, and it’s going to take everything that you can muster to defeat me, and I know that I must do the same. You’re going to see me in ways that you had never hoped to see me in Retro, and it can only mean that this battle is going to be one that you’ll never forget for the remainder of your days.”
[He stands slowly and paces back and forth, overlooking the city in-between turning to pace]
“Not enough people in the AWMC take me seriously enough, at least that’s my opinion of the matter. When I defeated Tracker, people said that I was just lucky. When I defeated you Retro for the title and one of the battles to defend the title, people said that I was pretty good but just mainly lucky.
One man, Takada, thinks that all of my winning is due to steroids, which is absolutely ludicrous. In fact, the results of that extensive testing should be concluded pretty soon Takada, and when all of that bull is said and done,
I’m going to get your bitchin’ and moanin’ ass into the ring and break it with my foot! I tire of hearing you whine and groan about my size and power because you’re not genetically gifted, or not in the same ways that I am.
Is it my fault that I am naturally thick boned and very tall? Is it my fault that you’re not quite a big or tall? How can you blame DNA man? Come on, get with the program. I made it to this point because of hard damned work, sacrifice, and guidance from Mr. Greene and others, and I’ve been able to combine all of these teachings along with my will to succeed, and it’s created a pretty good formula of success. I’ve fought hard to wear various titles and gain some prestige, and this battle will allow me to gain that much more, pending a victory of course. I’ll either overcome the odds and
Retro, or I’ll end up laid up in a hospital somewhere, pondering whether or not I can go on. Well, I’ll probably end up in the hospital anyway, but if
I win this battle, it won’t hurt as much.” [He chuckles slightly] “Hell, maybe Retro and I can get a hospital room together so neither one of us sleeps because we don’t trust the other man. I bet that would be just comical. We’d slip different chemicals into the other man’s IV when he’s asleep or mix some laxative into the other man’s drink.”
[He shakes his head at his own sarcasm] “Well folks, I’ve got a lot of physical and mental preparation to do, so I’m going to shut up now, but I want all of you in the AWMC, fans and wrestlers alike, to watch what transpires between Retro and I because it will be one for the ages. For those who are rooting for me to win, I thank you for your support and positive thinking. For those who want to see me lose, you know what I say to that so I won’t even go there. Retribution, bring your ‘A’ game, Revenge 1, 2, 3…however many you have, Suicide, and whoever and whatever else you can, because I’m going to bring my unleashed rage and burning intensity. At the PPV, in the streets of Las Vegas, prepare to be…DECIMATED!! SINISTER STYLE!! See you soon.”

We see the next AWMC Champion working out on his treadmill. His eyes remain rivitted on a Rob Cole match that is playing on a big screen television. Jennifer: I told Yoshi that I wanted to do this today because there were some things I needed to clear up. Outcast Rob Cole, a few months back you called my man a-
[She holds her thumb and index finger an inch apart] Jennifer: Cruiserweight [She shakes her head and smirks] Jennifer: Well Mr Cole if Yoshi is a cruiserweight, I would gladly take that over a supposed heavyweight like yourself. Where you rely on violence and pounding,
pounding, pounding- Takada has techniwue. He has skills. He gets in there and gives it his all to get the job done. And believe me Robbie, he does it better than most. Look at him-
[The camera swings and Takada has moved to a stationary bike, it slowly comes back to her] Jennifer: He has incredible stamina. [She takes a towel and wraps it around Takada's neck, giving his temple a light kiss] Jennifer: Sin CIty Showdown is comming up very shortly. This man is set, and he's more than ready. The last time he had the strap it was a little regional title. But now, now AWMC is growing back to what it once was.

But Robbie, he also has one thing you don't have- me. You see Robbie, unlike you- Yoshi isn't a master in self-manipulation. People are cheering all around. In the middle of the arena is a boxing ring and two men are going at it. It is clearly an undercard bout, as the crowd (although cheering) is not really into it, and the competitors are unrecognizable to the casual fan. As the camera pans around it closes in near the front few rows and hits on a few celebrities. Charles Barkley is seen, as well as George Foreman. The camera
moves around a little longer until it closes in on Ryan Violent, who appears to be enjoying himself at the event. He's wearing a flashy gray suit, and spectators are constantly coming and shaking his hand.}
{The crowd roars as one of the boxers gets knocked out. They aren't necessarily cheering for the victor, but are more likely cheering for the main event which will shortly be underway.}
Violent: Welcome to one of my favorite pastimes. Boxing. To the casual spectator, it is just two guys beating each other up. Much like what I do, but when you look a little closer you find it is so much more than that. So much psychology is involved, and it takes so much than physical toughness to succeed. Much like what I do...
{Violent pauses as he looks into the ring and sees the champion Prince Naseem Hamed and the challenger Vuyani Bungu ready to go at it.} ::DING DING:: Violent: I love coming to prize fights...not exactly to see the outcome, because in most cases you know that ahead of time. No, I come to see the fans. It's amazing how they can start out in one man's corner, and quickly turn on them once his opponent gains some momentum. Boxing isn't a battle of
good guys and bad guys. The fans switch favorites for the guy that's working the hardest. The world of wrestling could learn from that. {The crowd cheers as Bungu hits a few flurries in the 2nd round, and they quickly begin chanting "BUNGU,BUNGU,BUNGU"}
Violent: Now I'm not saying that you should cheer on A[bleep]holes like Jean Paul Valley, CJ Hunter, and Johnny Bagwell, because since they started working under Valley's Falco International, they've gotten soft, and Bagwell hasn't had the edge he needed to really compete in a long time. He didnt have it when I brought him to California towards the end of last year, and he
hasn't had it since I met him in Washington D.C. ::DING DING:: Violent: John, you can act the role of a badass all you want. You can pretend to be something you're not, and you can even pretend that you want to
be working for Hunter again...but the fact of the matter is, come Sunday, you will see why I am still looked at as "The Self Made Legend." Johnny, I told you when you went to Vegas last year instead of AWMC, that you better not come back...you didnt listen, and here you are. I'm going to close my comments on The Devil's Advocate by saying, I brought you into this world, and I'll be damned if Sunday I don't take you out.
::DING DING:: {The crowd has dramatically shifted in the favor of Naseem Hamed who has taken over in the 3rd round and now in the 4th has totally taken control of the bout. Chants of "NAZ" AND "PRINCE" echo throughout the arena.}
Violent: A lot of people have come up to me wondering why I'm not training intensely for this match, and whether or not I'd be ready. The fact of the matter is that I've been ready for this match for three months. Whether
McGriff is on my side, or the rumors going around regarding his standing with Falco International are true...I'm going to show up with my best game ready to be played. The crowd won't shift momentum like they are here, because quite frankly, they dont like you or your team Hunter, and I have no intention of them cheering you for your team performing well either, because at Sin City Showdown, I'm going to leave it all on the table and light you up.
{Hamed knocks Bungu down and its apparent the ref will call the match.} Violent: Smoke you... {The ref waves wildly in the air to stop the fight. The crowd erupts as a victorious Hamed celebrates in the ring.}
Violent: And put your asses out!!! ::DING DING:: {Fade to black.} <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> HEADHUNTER <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> (Fade in.) (Screen opens inside the Conference room of a Days Inn hotel in a small town just outside of Detroit. The room is crammed with reporters who are all sitting in front of a podium with an "AWMC" logo imprinted on it's front. Behind the podium, is Bret Benoit. A former wrestler who in his time, was the toughest in the business. Benoit, who now works as a talent scout for federations throughout the country, is casually dressed in a white Polo shirt that has the "AWMC" logo on it, a pair of khakis, and casual shoes. Next to him is a roadie he's conversing with. The camera stays on Benoit as he finishes up his conversation mwith the roadie, gets up from his chair, and walks up
to the podium. The room, which had been somewhat noisy goes quiet with anticipation as Benoit walks up to the stage.)
Bret Benoit: Well folks, I just wanted to invite you here to this press conference today, that Mr. Micheal Hunters, known to all of you as Double
H, The HeadHunter, has called. As far as I'm concerned he's to be here right now, but as you can see, he's not, so if you have any questions, or concerns, you can just direct them to me, if not we'll just wait upon ol'
Double H's arrival. (The camera pans off of Bret Benoit and into the audience, where all the reporters remain silent. The camera then pans back to Bret Benoit.)
Bret Benoit: Since you all have no questions as of yet, we'll just wait upon the arrival of The HeadHunter.
(A voice is heard from the back saying.... Double H: It won't be necessary to wait any longer. (The camera pans off of Bret Benoit and over to the main door entrance of the conference room. Standing in the doorway is none other than Double H,
The HeadHunter. He's casually dressed in a pair of black Guess stonewahsed jeans, black Timberland boots, and in a brand new black t-shirt that has a picture of him on the front, holding an AWMC Title belt high above his head, and with the enscription "YAKUZA FOREVER!!" under the picture. He turns around to display the back of the shirt, the camera zooms in and we can read the enscription "The Brotherhood is still alive!!". Double H turns back around, and looks at the room as a wry smile comes on his face, the camera zooms in on the platinum and gold necklace around his neck, the camera keeps zooming in and stays fixed on the dangling "HH" platinum and gold pendant on the chain. Double H finally walks into the room, and up to
Bret Benoit. The two shake hands before Double H walks up to the podium. He takes a deep breath, and then with a smile on his face, he begins.)
Double H: First of all, I just wanted to thank my good friend Bret Benoit, for working this all out for me today. For getting all of you to come 'ere today, not knowing exactly what to expect, and not knowing why I've called you all 'ere. So Bret Benoit, "The Enforcer", I thank you.
(Double H looks over at Bret Benoit, who's now seated in his seat behind the podium, and he nods. Double H then turns back around to face the room.)
Double H: A lot of you 'ere today, don't even know you're 'ere. You're just 'ere because your superiors sent you to cover a press conference.
My first one since I've officially been back. Well first of all while I do 'ave your attention, I thought to just spent a little time thanking each of you for your support while I was in the 'ospital, I thank you the printed up stories about me, I thank you for the calls to find out 'ow I was doing, and I even thank you, those of you who were asking for the first interview when I got better. Well, that's because those of you who were asking were intelligent enough to know that I would pull through, so I just thank you for that.
(Double H nods to the crowd, the room stays still and silent, as he continues on.) Double H: So what's next?? I'm glad you were wondering that, because I'm not anymore. I 'ave been doing well since my car accident. I recovered quite nicely, and some of you even know of my success in Japan, and then briefly 'ere in the States. I wrestled in other organizations until I felt
I was ready to walk back into the AWMC. I faced some tough competition, but definately not the toughest. I beat some really good opponents, but definately, not the best. But for the first time since my accident, I feel as if I'm back to the level I was at before. And now, I'm ready to excel than my previous 'igh level, I want to be better, I want to defeat the best, and I want to be the best, that's why you see me 'ere today, and that's why I 'ave come back 'ere to the AWMC!!
(Pause.) Double H: Since my accident, I've 'ad to train 'arder, but I've seen the fruits of my labor in the many successes I've 'ad over the past months. But all that training, and all that 'ardwork?? Now is when I see, if it's really going to payt off. Don't ask because I don't know when my first AWMC match will be. All I know is this, you'll be seeing a newer, more fresh, and definately better Double H The HeadHunter, every time you see me walk into an AWMC ring. Get ready everyone, because you're about to witness the beginning of the best from yours truly!!
(Pause.) Double H: Before I open up the floor for questions, I want to address just a few more things. The first of all is Yakuza. When I left the AWMC, Yakuza was on top. We 'ad some of the biggest names in the business today, and also surprisingly, we had youth in our ranks. Youth, the foundation needed for future sucess in this business. And while Yakuza was the best back then, the youth we 'ad on our side, almost insured that we were going to be the best in this business, for years to come.

We go way back. And when we finally teamed up in Yakuza, we were unstoppable. Our metoric rise to the top is still unparalled. No one beat us, no one could, we were the best tag team going, 'ands down!! More than tag team partners, Trey and I were brothers. That's another thing everyone couldn't share with Yakuza, we were all brothers, and everyone could only envy that about us, Yakuza was a brotherhood.
(Pause.) Double H: So Trey L., to you I want to say this much. The rest of the crew is gone. It's only you and me now. Now I don't know what all you're involved with in the AWMC right now. I don't know if you 'ave a new crew, of if you stand alone. I don't know if you still 'ave the same old enemies, or if you've made new ones. 'ell, I'm not even asking for a reunion. What I am going to say is this, you can do your thing and go your way, that's cool because I'm going to do my thing and go my own way. But if you ever need a brother's 'elp, you ever need a brother's advice, you ever need a brother to watch your back....you know where to find me.
(Pause.) Double H: Now, you may all be wondering what my immediate role in the AWMC?? (The camera pans over to the crowd who seemingly agree with Double H's question.) Double H: Well, I don't know either. But what I do know, is that I will be 'ere, and right now, that's all that really matters. So that's why I've called you here today, to once again announce that I am back in the AWMC, and that I'm ready to wrestle with the best. I'm goign to be even better than I was before. Just 'aving a little fun, don't look so pale Jones.

There are a few guys 'ere who are legendary from what they've done in the wrestling for a good number of years now. Then there are another group of guys who are the best of the best right now in the business. The last group is the upcomers, the guys just starting out, and just starting to make names for themselves. So up and down, the AWMC's roster looks tough up and do, everyone's got something to prove, but no one's as good as Double H The HeadHunter, and that's why I'm 'ere, and that's what I'm going to prove.
