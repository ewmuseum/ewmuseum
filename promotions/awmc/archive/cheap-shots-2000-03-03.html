Cheap Shots (3 March)

3 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Papers are scattered about a large oak desk. Books of all kinds adorn the back wall, and the other walls are displaying varoius championship belts, tropies, and plaques. The camera then focuses on a shadow, which has been created by The sun shining in a large window. The camera slowly moves up the shadow and focuses on the figure.} {"The Natural" Ryan Violent is seen staring blankly out the window. He looks to be in a way unlike he is usually seen. Violent is wearing khakis and a black t-shirt. His hair looks as if he just rolled out of bed as it is going every which way and his face is ragged looking and unshaven. Violent turns around and fully faces the camera.}
Violent: You know, one of the first things you learn about this business is that the only person that you can trust all of the time is yourself, and your closest allies can turn into your greatest enemies on a whim. {Violent shakes his head in disgust.} Violent: You would think that I would have learned that by now and taken appropriate measures to see that it doesn't happen again, but the only way that is possible in this business is to totally shut yourself out from everybody and isolate yourself so that nobody can turn on you, but if I do that, I won't earn anybodys' respect nor will I advance in the world.
{Violent slowly rubs his hand across his unshaven face as he ponders his next thought.} Violent: In the last 4 months I have essentially lost all of the people that I consider allies in this business. CJ Hunter sold myself and all of his employees out. Johnny Bagwell left myself and Jack McGriff hanging in the dust and now it appears that Jack McGriff might be selling himself out in order to take the "road more travelled" I guess it would be called. I'll be the first to admit that CJ helped mold me into what I am today, and I wouldn't be as successful without my past with Bagwell and McGriff. So where does that take me now? Do I look to trust anybody now? {Violent once again stares blankly out the window at a lake. It is a very uncharacteristic February day in Violent's home of Minnesota. Sailboats are on the lake and people are seen swimming in the lake.}
Violent: A lot of people are telling me that McGriff has nothing to do with JPV and Hunter's plan. Whether that is the case or not, I really don't know, but I haven't heard a thing from Jack trying to explain himself, so I expect the worst. Because of that, I have changed my outlook on my upcoming 8 man tag match. Whether my team shows up or not is entirely up to them, but irregardless, Ryan Violent will be there and I will give it my all to earn a victory for ME. Not for my TEAM.
{Violent's demeanor changes from a concerned and almost skeptical one, to a much more intense one.}
Violent: Now I want to address just a couple more things before we part. Kyle Jones, "The Alaskan Assassin" [sarcastically], you have been really running your mouth off quite a bit over the past few days. You claim that you have made numerous calls trying to setup a match with "The Natural."
{Violent pauses and shakes his head slowly.} Violent: Bull[bleep]!!! I don't back down from challenges or make myself scarce. If you want a piece of me, you come to me, and we can get it on in the ring or I can drop your ass to the ground right then and there. Your a big talker, but I showed you exactly where you and the rest of your team stand at The Battle of Georgetown. If you really think you can go toe to toe with "The Natural," you are sadly mistaken, but I will be more than happy to show you that if you really want it.
{Violent laughs as he thinks more and more about the comments Jones has made as of late.} Violent: Now onto my upcoming match...Tony Virtue. You have something of an advantage over me this week, being that I really know nothing about you, don't let that whole mystery aspect of our match give you a false sense of security. I have a lot of friends checking up on you and finding out as much as possible. I can assure you by the time that bell rings that I will know more about you that you yourself does. Until then though, I'm preparing for what I feel is every possible approach you could take towards me, so on paper you may have the upper hand, but when you look at me in the ring you know it will only be a matter of time before "The Natural" lights you up...
{Violent lights up a cigarette} Violent: Smokes you. {Violent takes a couple of quick puffs of the cigarette and flicks it at the camera.} Violent: And puts your ass out! {Fade to black} <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> STERLING <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Open on what could be any dressing room in any arena in any city of The United States of America. The cinderblock walls are painted a cold, dismal grey. Hooks line the wall, some hung with clothing, others are bare. A wooden bench stretches along the entire length of the wall, and sitting in the middle of the bench is a man, head down so low it's almost between his knees. Despite the fact that his long hair is still soaked from a shower, one can tell that it is the lightest shade of blonde. He appears to be roughly six feet tall, weighing about 230 pounds. He is muscular without being bulky. The man looks up at the camera, his face handsome and young.
This is Xavier Sterling. "This all still seems like it's still just some kind of crazy dream." Xavier Sterling says as runs a set of fingers through his dripping hair. "All my life I've known that this is what I wanted to do. Ever since the first day
I saw Brett Hart excellently executing, I knew that I wanted to step into that squared circle and be a hero to millions. I knew that I wanted to be that hero. I knew I wanted to be that competitor. I knew that I wanted to be that superstar. I knew that I wanted to be that showman thrilling the masses with my skill. But now that my dream has come true... now that I've actually wrestled my first professional match here in Tri-State Grappling
Association... now that I've also got a contract with AWMC... now that I am on the very verge of being what I set out to be... it just doesn't seem real."
Sterling looks around him, taking in the drab surroundings, he is still smiling, excited to be competing.
"I mean... really, me, Xavier Sterling, stepping into the ring against some of the greatest names in the sport. Can you imagine it? Hell,
I hadn't even met a real wrestling superstar face-to-face two weeks ago and now... well... I am one." Sterling stands, drying his upper body with a towel. "I remember not so long ago making the drive from Toronto down to
Detroit just to catch a glimpse of men like The Organ Grinder, Retribution and Chris Del Rio. I remember the way my hair stood on end, goose-bumps running up and down my arms as I felt the electricity in the crowd when Rob
Cole made his way down the aisle." Sterling pulls a shiny green shirt off a hook and slips it on. "Now, I'm actually going to be in the same ring as these men." He says, buttoning up the shirt slowly. "Me, Xavier Sterling, facing men I've looked up to for so long. And who knows, maybe someday someone will travel a couple hundred miles just to see me, just to feel his skin come alive with
goose-bumps when I make my way to the ring." Sterling picks up his duffle bag and shuffles to the door. "Yeah, I know. Nobody's gonna pay attention to this no-name rookie. Nobody's gonna put any stock in what I say. But I kinda like it that way." Sterling smiles warmly. "See, I know everything there is to know about the superstars here in AWMC. I know their styles, I know their moves, I know their strengths, and I know their weaknesses. And what do they know about me? Nothing. See, put me in a ring with Hiroyoshi Takada, I'll know to be wary of the Takada Lock. I know counters. I know ways of avoiding it. But what would Takada know about me? Nothing. And that's the way I like it."
Sterling surveys the dressing room to be sure he hasn't forgotten anything. "This Monday, I make my AWMC debut. This Monday, Allstar Wrestling starts Livin' The Life Of XS... and trust me, that'll make life on the rest of you a little harder."
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> RETRIBUTION <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [Retribution is standing in the hallway of some undetermined location. He has on his regualr street clothes, a cigar in his mouth, and a pair of sunglasses on. He has a slight smirk across his face as he begins to speak.]
Retribution: Geddings, seems that I get what I want sooner than I expected. Good. You'll wish that the comments you have made in the recent past here as well as other places had never passed through your lips. I said that I owed you for that little trick months back when you put me against Sinister so that you would hope to keep the strap. I did make my intentions known when you defended that strap....and you lost as I expected you would. Now you and
I go one on one in the center of the ring. No hardcore rules, just two men fighting it out. Well, one man and the half of a man that you barely are.
[Retribution blows smoke out in rings.] Retribution: You will see what happens first hand when someone crosses me in a bad manner. But actually, you know what that feels like. I've whipped yer sorry hide from pillar to post on more than a handful of occasions. Yer probably still waking up sore in the morning from the beatings I've given you. Now this match really has no bearing on anything, but we are in the
Main Event. But that is nothing unusual for me. I've been in a lot of high profile matches throughout my career. This is nothing new to me and losing this match will be nothing new to you. So just accept thefact yer nothing around here when all is said and done.
[Retribution reaches into a bag just at his feet, from it he reveals a book. The cover is leather bound and the wording is unable to be identified due to the angle he is holding it.]
Retribution: Sinister, you have something of mine. I will get it back and I'll do it the next time you and I face off. Matter of fact, I want my title back before the Pay Per View. Will you have the guts to accept my challenge?
I doubt it, but you could suprise me and accept. If you do, then you'll need to look over this book I have. It belongs to a good friend of mine. It's a book about different ways to commit suicide. The reason you'll need this book is simple. When we meet again, I am going to put you through a hell that you have never been through before. Setting a man on fire? You'll wish you could get off that easily at the pain I am going to inflict upon that waste of space you call a body. You'll want to end yer life as quickly as you can and if you wanna get inventive in how to do that, well this book will definately be something you'll wanna borrow from me. Be ready Sinister, yer time is soon coming. And once again Geddings, you will fall to me like you always have. I AM The Franchise of the AWMC, no matter what anyone tries to say otherwise. If they feel I'm not and wanna call me out. By all means, do so. Just remember that what follows is your own damn fault.
[Retribution grabs the bag, stuffs the book into it, and then walks off.] <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> STORM <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [Fade in. The camera comes into focus in a dimmly lit stairwell. As the camera pansup the steps it focuses onto a pair of black boots. The boots flow into faded black jeans. The camera continues to pan up and reveals a white shirt that has an image of Jesus carring a cross upon his back and reads The Lord's Gym in red letters. Finally the face of Brian Storm is shown. He is unshaven and has a look of disappointment upon his face.]
Brian Storm: "I'm a little upset with myself... I mean I said I was going to become the shining star of the AWMC at NA2. Yet, what happens?
I slacked off! I let the lack of talent across the ring from me bring me down a few dozen levels."
[Brian slowly shakes his head from side to side and gently strokes his unshaven face. As a disgusted look falls across his face he looks away from the camera and stares into the blackness of the stairway.]
Brian Storm: "I mean look at me ... level upon level of untapped potential and I waste my first opportunity to make a name for myself.
Sure, McGriff, Violent and myself got the win ... but what did I do to further my claim? What did I do to prove to the nation that I am next rising star of the AWMC? From what I could tell not a damn thing."
[Brian looks towards the camera once again and a look of determination falls upon his young face. And amongst his green eyes a gleam of joy can almost be seen.]
Brian Storm: "Yet, on March 9th on Motor City Madness that's all going to change as I step into the ring with Xavier Sterling. Ah yes, another
Motor City Madness, and another chance to carry someone else to a memorable match. If I recall the critics gave me rave reviews for carring Walcott to his best match in ages. And one more time the brass here in the AWMC is giving me the chance to do it again, and do it again
I shall." "This time I'm not out to prove it to the thousands of fans in attendence. Nope. This time I'm out to prove it to [points to his chest]
MYSELF. I want to know that Explosion was a fluke ... that I just coasted to let McGriff and Violent shine. I want to prove I took one for the team."
[Brian stands up and descends a few steps before leaning against the stone wall.] Brian Storm: "Don't get me wrong the members of the Foundation have some talent ... there just not in my class. The three of us are three different styles ... there just isn't a way to compare us. Yet, the three of us have some similarities. The first is we're not like that
Kyle Jones ... on the injuried reserve list. I mean Kyle ... I'm sorry to hear of the injury and all, but maybe it's for the better. I mean maybe you'll learn to respect your opponent's ability, instead of not giving two cents about them."
"Also Jack, Ryan and myself aren't like that braggart Kenny Kopecky. I mean I was going into Explosion making a big claim. Yet, Kenny here was going yadda yadda, blah blah blah and the only thing I heard him say was how impressive he was. The only impressive thing I saw him do was ...
[taps on the wall with his right hand] was _TAP OUT_. Geez Kenny, how impressive was that?"
[Brian looks up towards the ceiling and gently laugh at himself.] Brian Storm: "I said I was going to be the next rising star and here I am admitting I dropped the ball. Are you a big enough man to admit that you are not nearly as impressive as your ego wants you to be Kopecky?
Personally I don't think you are. In fact I can hear it already [imitating Kopecky] Did everyone see how Storm ran from me at Explosion [mocking laugh] Well just you wait *child* I'll get my hands on you at the PPV [laughs]" "Kopecky ... I'm shaking. [rolls eyes] Just so you know Kenny-boy I'm not a child. Growing up though I was a little wild and that's just what everyone called me... a little *Wild Child*. Now that I'm all grown up though perhaps your right. Perhaps it's time for a change."
[After a few moments of silence Brian descends further into the darkness and disappears from sight as all fades to black.]
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> SINISTER <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> * The scene fades in to the large back of Sinister, the area where he was recently stabbed by a screwdriver, compliments of Tracker, is covered with a large bandage and is stained. The light in the room is dim as Sinister stands alone in the center of the room, his back to the camera and his head tilted slightly to the right. He is almost in a trance-like state as his voice is very calm, a true irony for what he endured the other day against
Tracker. As the camera pans out to take in the whole of the room, there is a gas can seen on the floor fairly close to Sinister's right foot. Laying next to the gas can are the remnants of a towel, most of it burned and charred. Sinister stands near motionless as he speaks * "Well, well, well. I see that some of the AWMC folks are trippin' off of the fact that I can get down and dirty just like the next man. It seems that since this war has been waged between Retribution and I, the both of us have gotten that much more sadistic…that much more deadly. Tracker, like many in the AWMC, doubted my abilities…both as a wrestler, and as a street fighter.
Just because a man doesn't choose to fight a match using hardcore rules all of the time doesn't mean that he can't make the transition, as you have found out the hard way Tracker. The other night Tracker, you were the unfortunate victim of the 'Black Beast from the East', the angry side of me, the cold, calculating, and twisted side of me. I do enjoy making people yell in pain
Tracker. I enjoy having someone look me in the eye and their face is full of fear, their eyes begging for mercy. Tonight, however, I had a lot to prove Tracker, not only to you, but to Retro and the AWMC as an entity." [He takes a long, deep breath, then continues] "Retribution…the things that you saw me do in the ring tonight are just additional methods of pain that I've conjured up. Our first battle with hardcore rules was an awakening of sorts of the troubled childhood that I lived. Growing up in the streets of Chicago taught me to survive, hurt the other man or men bad enough to where they wouldn't want to nor be able to bother or hurt you again, and to also make people respect you…out of fear.
I know that you have no respect for me Retro, despite the epic battle that we had involving the flaming barbed-wire sledgehammer which I dented your pectorals with. I'm sure your ribs haven't forgiven or forgotten about that
Retro, and we still have a long way to go. Trust me, I'll never forget you shooting that staple gun into my left temple, but that is part of what will cause me to seek my revenge on you…my form of…Retribution…if you will.
Whether I have your respect or not is a different subject in itself, but I know that I have your attention."
[He is heard chuckling slightly, his broad shoulders bouncing up and down ever so slightly]
"I know you, like the rest of the AWMC, was seriously trippin' off of what I did to Tracker in that ring…especially Tracker. You brag and boast of innovation Tracker? How did you like my creativity in that battle? Now, I always give credit where it's due. The screwdriver in the back, the vicious piledriver onto the chair, the Springboard Facebuster with the chair, and the vicious shot on the dome with another chair. You definitely nailed me with some big time moves, no doubt, but my moves were just a little bigger, a little more damaging, and in the end, a little better, and I walked out the victor. You went to the well one too many times Tracker, and once you missed that attempted Running Facebuster, that was my opportunity to take advantage, and take advantage I did. You're not the only one who can use variations of the facebuster, now are you Tracker? How'd you like my running staircase
Facebuster…with a chair on top of that? Pretty good for a 6'10", 330 lbs. man, is it not? How about my breaking your little nose with that 180 degree Guillotine Leg Drop?" [He looks skyward for a brief moment, seemingly reliving the moment] "Then, the fireworks really came out, no pun intended. What made me think to grab a gas can, grab a towel, wrap a chair with the towel, light it, and plant it into the side of your head? I couldn't tell you…let's call it the heat of the moment, no pun intended again. Did you think that you'd have your flesh burnt by me Tracker? How did it feel to smell your own skin cooking? Though the smell was putrid to me, I liked it, because I knew the pain I had caused. However, that wasn't enough for me. Setting up those two tables, tossing tacks onto them, pouring gas over them, and lighting them on fire was the catalyst of your excruciating pain Tracker. Powerbombing you onto through invention of destruction was the icing on the cake…and the end of the battle for you. Isn't it ironic how the fans tossed in numerous lighters, inspiring me to take the battle to new heights? And, to show my gratitude to the hardcore fans who indeed tossed in those lighters…I stood atop the top rope and executed a swandive onto your prone, burnt, beaten, bloodied, and bruised carcass for the three count."
[He slowly turns his body around, the look in his eyes one of pure intensity and determination. The satisfaction of his actions can be read easily by his body language. He flashes his infamous evil grin momentarily, then continues] "How often do you see a man my size doing that? Not too often, but I figured, hell, if I miss, at least Tracker is still hurt, so why not? You see, the state of mind that I'm in because of these hardcore battles is not a safe one, for me nor any of my opponents…and Retribution, I know that you're as twisted as anyone can be, so needless to say, our battles from here on out are going to be life threatening, but that's the only way that our struggles are going to be resolved. If one of us suffers permanent damage, then so be it. We have another round of utter brutality scheduled before our PPV war, and to be honest, one of us, or both of us, may not even make it to the PPV.
A Las Vegas Street Fight is right up my alley Retro, no pun intended. Geez, I should be a comedian. Be it the streets of Las Vegas, Chicago, Detroit, Philly, New York…wherever, believe you me, I know how to handle my business. You know it, and I know it. The match that we have before the PPV is for the hardcore title, a title that no one thought I'd ever hold, especially me, but that just goes to show you how life can be funny like that." [He turns his head slowly and eyes the gas can and remains of the burnt towel] "Utilizing those is just the beginning of much greater things, believe you me. On a side note, Mr. Matt Tatano, when I ask you for a chair, please give it to me a little bit faster, okay? Tracker dented my skull with the one that I wanted to use on him and it almost cost me the match. I also again want to thank the hardcore fans of the AWMC who were ever so gracious in tossing me so many lighters to finish what I set out to do. 'Flyking' Will
Geddings, I feel the sorriest for you, because when you face Retro, you face a man that is just as possessed as I in hurting people, making people suffer and cry for mercy, which he will not show you, nor me in that instance.
Retro, I will be keeping a close eye on your battle against Geddings, for I've had the displeasure of battling him as well, but the stakes were different then. Make sure that you emerge in once piece and able to fight
Retro, because you will be…DECIMATED!! SINISTER STYLE!! See you soon." * Picture fades as Sinister slowly makes his way towards the gas can and towel *
