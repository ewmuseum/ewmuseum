The self-described "Don" retired last year at the Golden Turnbuckle Awards, but is apparently back in the squared circle with former AWMC announcer Daniel Wright at his side. Shakur and Mizukawa defeated Too Damn Sexy for rights to a tag title shot that evening, then went on to attack Will Geddings, resulting in a disqualification. Before the main event, Shakur addressed the crowd to take former Yakuza associates to task on their recent efforts and pledge to bring the company back to its glory days. No Apologies II : Ground Zero: Nuke III [Detroit, MI] - Following the injuries both men suffered at Sin City Showdown II, Extreme Champion Sinister and former CHampion Retribution were sent to St. Joseph's hospital in Las Vegas Neveade. Little did they know that both men were in the same recovery room, as seen on a recent edition of AWMC Cheap Shots. As part of the Best-Of-Five deal, Sinister agreed to defend his Title one more time. But the stakes have been raised. This time, AWMC official and executives have asked for the deadliest and most violent match in AWMC history. The Nuke Match. Only one other time in AWMC's history has a Nuke match taken place. The Main Event of Summer Warfare '98 saw Retribution retain his Heavyweight Title by defeating Justin B in Nuke II. Nuke I saw Retribution retain his GEWA Hardcore Title by defeaing "Outcast" Rob Cole. Will history repeat itself? Can Retribution Three-peat on the Nuke match? Extra precautions are being taken for the delicacy of this match. It will telecast live, from the AWMC Motor Dome in Detroit, Michigan infront of AWMC's most Hardcore fans. The Motor Dome will have limited seating, and the tickets for the one-match event Sold Out in a record 27 minutes. Explosion: Obsessive Compulsive Disorder Scores Huge Win [Los Angeles, CA] - In what could go down as one of the goriest Televised matches in AWMC history, Obsessive Compulsive Disorder defeated Tracker in AWMC's first ever Extreme Measures match. Both men were treated and realeased from Cedar Sinai Hospital and aren't expected to miss anytime. OCD suffered multiple burns and Tracker suffered a bruised shoulder. Explosion: Storm Scores Gold [Los Angeles, CA] - "Wild Child" Brian Storm defeated 100 Percent to gain his first gold in AWMC. Storm faces stiff competition form the likes of "Top Gun" Ben Tuckwell and "Supreme being" Jeremy Rhodes. Could a rematch with 100 Percent be far down the road? Sin City Showdown II: Takada Scores, Showboat Loses, GLH Title In The Air? [Las Vegas, NV] - AWMC's Sin City Showdown Pay Per View proved to be be pure Anarchy. In what soem would call and upset, Hiroyoshi Takada, a former AWMC Champion, regained his title, defeating Rob Cole in the final match fo their Best Of Three Series. Takada won with assistance of Trey Lawrenze. Trey lawrenze, earlier in the evening, sent Rick Showbaot packing out of AWMC in The Ultimate Gamble match. Also: Eddie Rue and Ethan Love fought to a No Contest with Extreme Circumstances. Rumours through AWMC Offices are starting to become evident: Could the GLH Title become vacant? - An issue has arisen over the content of roleplays. The number one complaint seems to be the breaking of Kayfabe in roleplays. AWMC and its staff works very hard to bring you the most fun and realistic e-federation on the net. We work to maintain our own originality, but still abide the rules of kayfabe. In recent roleplay packets, several people have broken the kayfabe rules. Most using "smart" comments as to who is being pushed and how outcomes of determined. Breaking the rules of kayfabe can really damage your push as well as the beautiful realism that we shoot for in AWMC. If you have beef with the how the fed is ran, how matches are determined, or who is being pushed, please take up these problems with the AWMC staff at [detail withheld] . We're _always_ up for your thoughts and constructive [ or not-so-constructive ] criticism. But please, do not use "smart" remarks in roleplays. Effective immediately, any roleplays breaking the kayfabe rule will be denied and sent back to the handler for fix-up. We're shooting for realism in AWMC.
