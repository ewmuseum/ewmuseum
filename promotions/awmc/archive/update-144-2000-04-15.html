Several announcements are to be made concerning the Heavyweight Title as well as Cruise Control III: Revelations. Motor City Madness: Contagion, Threat, Mizukawa/Shakur To Battle at Cruise Control III [Miami, FL] - The Main event on motor City madness seemed to ahve taken a turn tipped the momentum in favor of Contagion, as "The Revelation" Ryan Violent outlasted both Ujimoto Mizukawa and Sebastion Manning. Before the match, Bagwell and Violent accepted Shakur's challenge for a Three Way Dance at Cruise Control III. Motor City Madness: Ogama, Tracker Battle Intensifies [Miami, FL] - "Innovator of Extreme" Tracker and new Japanese hardcore star Takisuma Ogama ante'd up on Motor City Madness, and decided to amke their upcoming battle on Explosion a Barbwire Chain match. Tracker has proven himself in the past, and this could be a great chance for Ogama and "Beautiful" Bobby Blair. Explosion: Long Live The King [Atlanta, GA] - Explosion saw the return of a man who calimed he was already here. In a stunning Main Event, "Flyking" Will Geddings returned to AWMC, proving it wasn't him wrestling a "Flykane", but a hired primary wrestler. The new Great Lakes Heritage Champion fired back against the fans and showed a new face. What type of resposne will this draw from Ethan Love? Explosion: Shakur confronts Bagwell for Tag Match [Atlanta, GA] - AWMC World Tag Team Co-Champion Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur surprised many people on hand for Explosion when the wrestler showed up during Johnny Bagwell's match against Sean Christopher, did some guest commentating, then confronted Bagwell and tossed a contract at him. Last week, Daniel Wright challenged The Threat and Bagwell and teammate Ryan Violent to a showdown at Cruise Control III. Shakur and partner Ujimoto Mizukawa won the titles last Explosion. Explosion: Rue Levied Fine [Atlanta, GA] - Keeping to his true anarchist roots, "Heartles" Eddie Rue was levied a $45,000 fine for his failure to comply and referee the Main Event on ppxlosion. How will Rue react?
