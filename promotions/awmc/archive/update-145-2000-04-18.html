He was not to be taken lightly. After Hiroyoshi Takada interjected into the Extreme Title contest to end the best-of-five series for Retribution and Sinsiter, he called down the Thunder from the AWMC Executive Board. Musashi proved his critics who called him weak and frightened wrong, as he ordered AWMC Hiroyoshi Takada to defend his Heavwyweight Title at Cruise Control III against Number One Contender Sinister! Explosion: The Threat Scores Big; Tides Change In Their Favor For Cruise Control III [Charlotte, NC] - Daniel Wright pulled the wool over the eyes of all, as he tipped the Tag Team Title Match at Cruise Control III in the favor of the The Threat following a controversial Main Event, which saw Wright make a fast 3 Count on Johnny Bagwell, also saw Wright join the forces of Shane Christopher and Sebastion Manning, who reunited wiht Brittany Christopher. Neither Contagion or Shakur have commented as of yet, but don't expect the two teams to remain silent. Explosion: Sinister & Retribution: A Matter Of Respect [Charlotte, NC] - I what could be one of the most shocking developments in AWMC History, we saw Retribution and Sinister engage in a handshake and declare a mutual respect for one another following an extraordinary Extreme Title defense that saw Retirbution retain the title following a No Contest. Retirbution goes on to Cruise Control II to defend his Title against Obsessive Compulsive Disorder, and Sinister faces off against Takada. Retribution stated he will be there for Sinister..but can the self-proclaimed Franchise be lying once more? Explosion: Storm, Rhodes To Settle Score At Cruise Control III [Charlotte, NC] - For the second time, AWMC Motor City CHampion "Wild Child" Brian Storm and "Supreme Being" jeremy Rhodes pushed it to the limits, and for the second time the match ended with controversy. Following a Double Knockout, Akira Musashi amde his rpesence felt for the first time by stating that the two men will face off at Cruise Control III in a No-Holds-Barred Ladder Match for the Motor City Title! AWMC: Musashi, Board Of Directors To Make Statement [Detroit, MI] - It was announced on Thursday that AWMC President Akira Musashi and the AWMC Board of Directors will be at the Charlotte Coliseum on Monday for Explosion. Several announcements are to be made concerning the Heavyweight Title as well as Cruise Control III: Revelations. Motor City Madness: Contagion, Threat, Mizukawa/Shakur To Battle at Cruise Control III [Miami, FL] - The Main event on motor City madness seemed to ahve taken a turn tipped the momentum in favor of Contagion, as "The Revelation" Ryan Violent outlasted both Ujimoto Mizukawa and Sebastion Manning. Before the match, Bagwell and Violent accepted Shakur's challenge for a Three Way Dance at Cruise Control III. Motor City Madness: Ogama, Tracker Battle Intensifies [Miami, FL] - "Innovator of Extreme" Tracker and new Japanese hardcore star Takisuma Ogama ante'd up on Motor City Madness, and decided to amke their upcoming battle on Explosion a Barbwire Chain match. Tracker has proven himself in the past, and this could be a great chance for Ogama and "Beautiful" Bobby Blair.
