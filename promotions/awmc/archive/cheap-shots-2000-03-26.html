{Violent slowly looks around his bleak surroundings as if legitimately interested in the concept of being surrounded by nothing.}
Violent: Look at this, [Violent motions around the area] I'm standing in the middle of nowhere. This means so many things…so many different things.
It's representative of how I look at almost everybody in my life. The fans, the executives, and the competition…they are of little meaning or consequence to me. It is also indicative of what stands between Bagwell and myself, and our
much deserved tag team prowess and eventual gold…Nothing. {Violent laughs to himself, knowing what his future holds and why so many people can only sit in bewilderment until he reaches that point.}
Violent: Before I came here, to AWMC, I knew I was going to kill off "The Natural," and bring in something so new, so fresh, and so startling, that I would once again rock the Foundations that wrestling holds itself on. I needed something that would prove to the world why I am the "F'n standard"
that those below me must adhere to in order to succeed. Why many people know me as "The Self Made Legend", and why, sooner than later, I will be known as the most influential force this organization has ever seen.
{Violent's previous look of laugher is now replaced by one of an intense demeanor as his eyes seemingly burn holes in the camera.}
Violent: Take this as a warning. If anyone…and I repeat ANYONE, even dreams of stepping in the way, or on the toes of Jean Paul Valley, Johnny Bagwell, or myself…you had better wake up and apologize. If you don't we will surely break you down mentally and physically, and by the time we are done with you,
nobody will be able to pick up the pieces. So take heed, and don't get in our way. Violent: Ask yourselves this, is it possible for one man to be your greatest fear, yet your only hope?
{Violent gets half of a smirk on his face.} Violent: Soon you'll find out...Don't take it personally though, its just business. {Violent belts out a sinister laugh as the lights go away. As time passes by the laughter echoes, as if coming from everywhere. Until dead silence is reached, at which time "Carmina Burana" begins playing again and graphics begin flashing on the screen.}
