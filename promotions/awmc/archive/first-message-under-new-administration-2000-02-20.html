First Message Under New Administration

20 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello All. To be honest with you..I don't know where to start. My name is [name withheld], and I've been involved in e-wrestling for Twenty months, give or take a week. :) I've been in AWMC since December-January of 98-99. As [name withheld] said, I've gotten many different perspectives of AWMC from a handlers and Administrative standpoint.
For those of you who know my work as roleplayer: I was "Midnight" Darren Skiles in AWMC since Summer of '99. I was also "War Machine" Damien Churchill in Carl Blair's UWC. I've been part of the AWMC booking committee since last November as well. Effective immediately, "Midnight" Darren Skiles and Trixie Monroe are gone from AWMC. A few may have heard of my work in Great Lakes Heritage Wrestling, which was an experiment of mine over last summer before hitting my stride in AWMC. I booked and wrote matches for over Five months, untill endless turmoil ruined the fun for all.
But...much like the vast majority of other handlers, have a life outside of AWMC and e-wrestling. I go to school, as well as balance a social life. I do not have the time or the tolerance for unnecessary complaining. I do, however, appreciate constructive criticism.
Without knowing what I'm doing wrong, AWMC will get no where. I consider myself, as well as being told by others, extremely diplomatic and easy to get along with. I always listen to ideas and tell you what I think. For now, I can be reached at
[detail withheld] . On Instant Messenger, my name is organized [detail withheld] . ICQ is [detail withheld] . Feel free to contact whenever. For now, it is most important that I hear from you as to weather you or aren’t in AWMC. If anyone feels that they don’t not like their position in AWMC and are thinking about departing, or have already decided to depart, then I must know.
I will say this, however. If you are struggling with a character..staying with AWMC through this time could be the first step in improving your future with
AWMC. After the last time a headbooker stepped down [[name withheld] in June of last year], several people stepped up to become key players. Chris Del Rio, Hiroyoshi Takada, Carl
Blair, Priest Noble, Darren Skiles, and Trey Lawrenze are a few which come to mind. I do wish that you all will think before deciding weather to leave or stay. New Booker,
Same Fun :) When I heard two weeks ago of [name withheld] stepping down as head booker, it struck me very hard. [name withheld] has been an extremely vital part of AWMC since its earliest incarnation.
Taking over after [name withheld], [name withheld] had huge steps to fill, as now, I have to fill those same boots. I plan on doing my best to keep AWMC on the same high-caliber level it has always been on.
Enough about me...onto federation matters :) : Sin City Showdown has been pushed back to an undetermined date in March. Most likely the last week of March. SCS is subject to heavy or little change. Below is the card as of today.
To speak on about more of what [name withheld] mentioned, Cruise Control 2000 will be happening in May. The 30 Man Royal Rumble, which has been a tradition for AWMC’s signature
PPV, will still occur this year, with Number One contendership on the line. Once things are straightened out, we’ll stick with two cards a week. Hopefully, I’ll have a week’s schedule out to you by next Tuesday. Here is your Sin City Showdown Card: *AWMC Title - Final Best-of-Three Match [if necessary]* "Outcast" Rob Cole (c) vs. Hiroyoshi Takada *The Ultimate Gamble - Loser Leaves AWMC - No DQ* Rick Showboat vs. Trey Lawrenze *AWMC Tag Team Titles* Stylin' With Attitudes (c) vs. Precious Moments *Great Lakes Heritage Title* "Hate Child" Ethan Love (c) vs. "Heartless" Eddie Rue *Great Lakes Heritage Tag Team Titles* The Threat (c) vs. Hex *Extremist Title - Vegas Street Fight* Sinister (c) vs. Retribution *Motor City Title* 100 Percent (c) vs. "The Punchin' Padre" Father Fisticuffs *Eight Man Tag - Strap-or-Can* Jack McGriff, "The Natural" Ryan Violent, Sean Christopher and "Wild Child" Brian Storm vs. "Mr. Attitude" Kenny Kopecky, Ric Beauty, "Pretty Boy" Troy Walcott, Jean Paul Valley I hope to see you all stay on board, and stay with AWMC. Pat
