The AWMC Vacant Heavyweight Title finally found a home. When Retribution finally returned to the AWMC, He made a huge impact and assisted Shakur in grabbing his 1st AWMC title. Congrats to all four men who participated for the title, you all worked your ass off.        Also, Craig Miller regained the AWMC Extremist title when AWMC found out it was a Hoax the whole time. He was Sanchez under a mask. Crazed Animals also captured the AWMC tag titles thanks to Death Row.        The battle between Excess and Yakuza continues..... Cruise was beaten after his sucsessful title defense on Night One. $ilk also recieved a small beating. How will Excess retaliate.        Fang joined his long time rival, The Royal Family. Along with Joey came crawling back to his idol Paul Styles.        The Ironman Title has recieved a NEW name, the AWMC Middleweight Title, only 240 or below can wrestle this title. Since Justin Cruise is 240 lbs he will keep the title.        Mr. Bell is looking into ways to cure all the interfearence in matches as of late.        As we all witnessed, Freaky Ti started a one man war on Yakuza.. Striking Head Hunter first. He came down on his Jet black Kawasoki Ninja and dragged the Yakuza member with a chain wrapped around his throat. Shakur and Ti's history goes back a long while. They started as allies fighting the Royal Family and the Underground together, One of the best tag teams the AWMC has yet to see. Then they went there own ways. As they incorporated Fame on there own, the two started to fall further apart. Then at Blood Bowl it began. Ti and Shakur were the final two in the ring, Ti ended up getting the win. That hit a sore spot with Shakur.... What will happen now?
