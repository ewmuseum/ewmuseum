First, "Alaskan Assasin" Kyle Jones was injured in the 6 Man No DQ Tag Match. Jones is expected to be out at least 1 month. We also witnessed the return of AWMC Tag Team of '99 when SeX & Guns returned, injuring Hex. SeX & Guns have replaced Hex for the PPV. And a chaotic Main Event saw Sinister retain his Extreme Title. Rue: Interview Is Imminent [Saginaw, MI] - The Fox Network, the company that owns the rights to show Explosion and Motor City Madness, has filmed a shoot interview with AWMC's favorite jerk, Eddie Rue. The interview comes live from the Saginaw City Jail, where Rue has spent the past three weeks for the damage he caused on an AWMC Explosion in which he blew up Ethan Love's dressing room and the ring. The segment will air on this upcoming Motor City Madness, and is a can't miss interview! Rue tells everyone when he'll get out of his cell, thoughts on various superstars, and MORE!
