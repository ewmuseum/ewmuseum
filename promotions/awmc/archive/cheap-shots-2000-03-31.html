[Breath in, breath out.] [Breath in, breath Out.] [Dark flickering light from a fire filters into your screen. We are in a sort of castle like room. The wall is of brick and the floor is hardwood. It is dark here, in the west wing, no lights but for the roaring flames in the fireplace. On the walls we see various paintings, they are clearly
Renaissance paintings, depicting mostly religious events. They are also beautiful, though not particularly note worthy after that. Because of the darkness it is difficult to make out our surroundings. Although it is easy to tell the room we are in is very bland. It was, perhaps, nice, even luxurious, but that would have been many years ago. From the small, length wise rectangular window we see the full moon, half hidden behind a small cluster of black clouds. As the wind moves the light, black clouds the full moon is able to been seen in its fullest. The new light is poured in and our visibility is increased somewhat.]
[The man breathing and grunting that we heard was none other than "The Devils Advocate", newly recruited Johnny Bagwell. He is shirtless, wearing black sweatpants and no socks or shoes. He is doing push ups at a pretty impressive clip, his olive skin glistening from sweat, reflecting the flame's light. His face is outward, looking at the camera, his brow arched with effort. Sweat is dripping off of his face, he grunts and does the last one, he collapses onto his chest and sits there breathing heavily for a moment.]
[Presently he pushes himself back up. He sits up and grabs a towel, wiping his soaked face. He stares at the camera as he towels the rest of himself.
Bagwell stands now, and drops his rag. He stands in front of the fire, blocking some of the light, making his form especially dark over the portrait of Jesus Christ on the cross that hangs above the fireplace. Facing away from us, he begins.]
"I have a University degree. I am intelligent, skilled and good looking. My parents were wealthy, I was denied nothing as a child. My current financial status is that I could purchase my own island off the shore of South Africa and live a quiet and comfortable life in retirement. I am, by the worlds standards, a very successful person. I have everything those slobs at our shows dream about every night after having one too many cans of cheap, tasteless beer.
So why do I put up with the constant criticism I receive every appearance from the fans at any AWMC event? Why do I wrestle, when even after I win I get booed? I have already answered this. But judging from what I've seen in the AWMC thus far, I think I should have to repeat it, very slowly I might add. You see I wrestle for spite. I wrestle because I enjoy the hatred I receive from both fans and wrestlers. I like knowing, some where out there a mother has forbid her child to watch wrestling in fear he might turn out a monster like myself. I enjoy it, I also enjoy punishing others for untold sins. Jack McGriff is a perfect example of someone I despised enough to cripple without just cause."
[He pauses.] "No Apologies" [Bagwell turns from the fire, not looking directly at the camera, but instead seems to be looking beyond it. As if he is searching for the answer to an unknown question deep within his own dark mind.]
"Considering what I have just told you, do you think it is a good idea to unleash me into a match with four, un talented cretins, which has no rules for their protection? To put myself and the most talented wrestler on the
AWMC roster as a team against the useless plankton that makes up two other tag teams? I assure you if your president enjoys watching his wrestlers suffer like "The Stalker" did, then this match was well booked. Otherwise it means that he has underestimated "The Revelation" and myself. Perhaps he is trying to punish us for crippling Jack. Perhaps he is as retarded as the wrestlers we must face. Either way, on April 3rd an event will take place that is un fathomable. It will be revolution. Right now, I am just another
AWMC wrestler who's taking up space on AWMC television time. But after "No Apologies" after you witness the methodical destruction of your beloved Tag Team Champions your malodorous minds will finally comprehend the fact that I am not just another face on the roster. I am "The Devils Advocate" and it isn't feasible for me to be a part of a federation and not make a difference."
[Still looking beyond the camera Bagwell sits down, not changing his facial expression] "Most of you inferior maggots don't understand my speech because you have fifth grade reading levels. That doesn't just apply to the fans at home, but to most of the wrestlers on our roster. As well as the inutile presidents and such we have running this circus.
However, I am still going to refer to my worthless opponents whether they understand me or not."
[Finally "The Devils Advocate" looks into the camera instead of indirectly towards it. It is a rare occasion to see Bagwell's eyes without his dark shades. And for that we are thankful. His eyes pierce through us like lasers, and that devilish grin appears ever so slowly across his otherwise handsome face.]
Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur and Ujimoto Mizukawa, what makes you think you are worthy to step into the ring with superior athletes, and humans such as Ryan and myself? I wouldn't allow either of you to scrub the dirt off the bottom of my thousand dollar shoe. You two worthless, feebleminded, imbeciles aren't worth talking about because you don't deserve to walk on the same streets as I do let alone enter the same ring. You two will learn as McGriff did that Falco International isn't your average group of bullies. I surpassed the 'bully' stage long ago and instead am referred to as a devil, a heartless bastard, a soulless monster. There are things I can say to you that may hurt your pride, but I would much rather wait and hurt your bodies on Monday.

Although this seems like something a young and curious child would do experimenting unwisely. Bagwell doesn't appear so. It looks as though being able to withstand the heat is important to him and he is testing himself. His hand swiftly glides across the top of the falme and then a second time slower. On the third attempt Johnny stops his hand with the small flame licking up the side of his palm. The sight looks painful but Bagwells facial expression doesn't change. He slowly moves his hand away and looks into the camera once more.]
"Will Geddings, another unavailing scrub who doesn't deserve my talking about him. However I was moderately interested to see his partner has chosen to stay unnamed. A mystery entrant so to say. The second is more likely. This person believes that by remaining anonymous we, his opponents, will be scared of the unknown. He thinks that by going unsaid it will give him and his partner an advantage. It is true that man has always feared what he does not know or understand. However, in this case

[Bagwell stands up and once again casts a dark shadow over most of the room. His eyes are penetrating and that grin make you feel inferior. His fists are clenched tightly and his chest and arms flexed. Every muscle and vein can be easily seen on his well defined torso.]
"I am the person little children picture when their parents warn them of the boogie man. I am the reason kids have their parents check under the bed and in the closet twice before turning out all the lights. I am the reason AWMC will fall to its knees!"
