Chaos — January 5, 2005

Chaos 1/5/2005 Show: Chaos Date: Wednesday January 5th, 2005 Place: Wings Stadium, Kalamazoo Michigan Time: 7:30pm As the New Year unfolds, the Chaos division grows in popularity. The superstars are anxious to get back into action. The division has been over in Iraq for the past 2 months performing nightly for troops all over the Middle East. After rave reviews, the Chaos superstars return and were back in action tonight! MATCH #1 Josh Render vs. Brandon Walsh (1/2 of the Chaos Tag Team Champions) ACTION: This was the first singles match for Render and he was without his partner Greg Martus. Brandon, who is accustomed to being isolated, took the early advantage over Render. Render looked over for help a few times and even walked over to make a tag to the chagrin of the crowd. Render finally realized that he was alone and it was up to him to win this match. Render showed that he could wrestle and after reversing spine buster into a huge DDT, Render set Brandon Walsh up for his finisher, The Dominator and Render got his first singles victory. RESULTS: Render wins by pin fall at 9:08 MATCH #2 Greg Martus vs. Dave “The Wasp” Wassic ACTION: Much like Render, Martus has yet to fight in a singles match. Fortunately for him, Render had already wrestled to victory and Render was present at ringside. The Wasp was not fazed by the presences of the 3 time Chaos Tag Team Champions and took it to Martus. Martus gained some leverage with a quick set of reversals, and after a dropkick, distracted the ref. Render entered and clotheslined The Wasp and slid out unseen. Martus covered for the victory and The DS Boys celebrated to the chagrin of the crowd. Bassel Noori entered the ring next and spoke to the encouraging crowd. “It is great to be back in the U, S of A. It was a true honor to perform for the troops in the mid-east and I will go back time and time again if the troops are entertained. Back in the USA however, there is also some unfinished business. I just arrived here because I had a meeting with the WWE board of directors last night. In a matter of hours, we have accomplished the unimaginable! Next weekend, January 15th, live in Grand Rapid Michigan, at Van Andel Arena, WWE and The Chaos Division present a live PPV!” The crowd roared! B continued, “The name of the PPV will be entitled, Chaos: Dissension!” The crowd liked the name. “We will feature 3 major matches. Now many of you are asking yourself, why only 3 matches. Well let me explain. Our first match will be a number 1 contenders match for the tag-team-titles. The winner between 703 and The DS Boys will face the Redneck for the titles on Chaos, Wednesday January 19th! The second match will be a No DQ last man standing match between Dave “The Wasp” Wassic and Ricky O’Ban! And finally we come to the former GM Eli Jimenez. For Chaos: Dissension, we will have a 6-man battle for the WWE Chaos Championship! Eli Jimenez will face DJ Finch (decent amount of crowd reaction in the form of boos), Brandon Walsh (little crowd reaction except for Walsh’s Army in the upper deck), The Man Beast Tony Lozon (good amount of face support), and Jay Taylor (crowd roars). Jay Taylor will be making his in-ring return at the PPV in Grand Rapids. But you are now asking me, that’s only 5, who is the 6th? Well folks I just signed a new talent that will add even more life to this division but you will have to turn in for the PPV on the 15th to find out who is that man! I want to thank you for coming tonight and have a safe trip ho…” B was cut off as the champion Eli Jimenez’s music hit and the crowd erupted in anger, but also was happy that the show wasn’t over. Hell they had paid $6 a piece for the floor seats. “Are you (Beeping) kidding me? What the hell. I have to defend my title against 5 other ass clowns! “ The DS Boys ran out and joined their leader. “You know something B, I am about tired of your decision making around here and I am sure that these fans are tired of it too. That’s why things are going to change after I leave Dissension the champ.” DS Inc slid into the ring and immediately pounced on the GM! B was defenseless and he curled up to protect himself. DS Inc took turns firing away. B made his comeback however and laid out both Render and Martus. Eli and B exchanged punches and B backed Eli into the corner. B fired away for a few seconds to the happiness of the crowd but The DS Boys gathered back and B was taken back to the middle of the ring for a beating. A huge Heavy Metal Scream blared over the loud speakers and Tony Lozon’s crazy fast Twizted music came on and the crowd roared. Tony slid into the ring and fired away at both The DS Boys. Eli and B continued slugging it out. The Rednecks music hit and DJ Finch and Brandon Walsh ran to the ring and pounded on Tony and B. It was mayhem! Everyone was kicking each other around the ring and DS Inc and The Redneck, who have no love for each other but a hatred for B and everyone else, began attacking Tony and B. The tides were turned on B and Tony and the two were in trouble. It was 5 on 2 and then Jay Taylor hit the ring to great surprise. He began dishing out punishment. There was 3 months of eagerness in his punches and he quickly gave Render and Walsh Franchise Stunners. Martus was then strapped in the Man Beast bear hug and was thrown from the ring. Finch was double clotheslined over the top rope by Taylor and B, just like old times. Eli stumbled into the middle of the ring in a daze. B, Tony and Jay surrounded him. Tony grabbed Eli and put his arms around his back. B fired away and slapped Eli like a bitch. Tony released Eli and Jay hit his 3rd Stunner and the crowd erupted at the 3 men in the ring. Business had just picked up in the DS Inc game plan and things began to look very bleak for a title retention.
