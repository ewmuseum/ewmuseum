AWR Live, 3/25/00 (Part 2 of 4)

2000-03-25
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AG: Up next, we have the in-ring debut of "Pure Athlete" Tony Virtue! We first got a taste of Tony Virtue on the last edition of AWR Live where he was the towel man for Devon Case! Virtue wasn't too happy with the actions of one Devon Case and he tossed the towel in causing
Case to lose to AWR Champion Mark McMahon!
JM: Yo, we should make a tag team! "Pure Athlete" Tony Virtue and
"Pure Mack" James Mack! Dat be fly!
AG: I don't know about that! Anyway, let's go to Andy! AB: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, from Brooklyn, New York, weighing in at 498 pounds...
"Sensational" FAT FRANKIE! ["Caught In The Act" by Earlene Bentley begins to play as the huge
"Sensational" Fat Frankie makes his way down the aisle, receiving a small negative reaction from the crowd. Frankie climbs into the ring, rubs his belly and begins to warm up in the corner.]
AB: And now, his opponent... [Rather prematurely, stepping out from the locker room area and into the Gay & Lesbian Community Center steps "Pure Athlete" Tony Virtue to a respectable pop from the fans. A few, especially further back in the stand jeer.. The ring boy tries to find some music to play as Tony walks to the ring but before he can, Virtue walks up the ring steps and
steps into the ring. He has a white towel, reminiscent of the one he threw into the ring last week during the main event around his waist. Andy, the ring announcer then announces Virtue] AB: Currently in the ring, hailing from Victoria Texas... [Tony prevents him from continuing as he takes the microphone from
Andy's hand. He then signals for him to exit the ring. In the corner,
Fat Frankie watches on as Virtue speaks out]
TV: Andy, I'm sure these spectactors know who I am after my performance last week on AWR Live, so there is no real need to announce my name, you got that?
[Now on the outside, Andy nods his head] TV: The Gay & Lesbian Community Center. [Fans cheer] What is the world coming to? [The fans turn their cheers into boo's as Tony shakes his head disgustingly] TV: You're parents must be ashamed, I know I would be, knowing my son or daughter are mentally and physically attracted to the same sex, that is just un-natural.
JM: Word. There ain't no fags in da hood! [Tony takes a gentle stroll around the ring looking at the fans who are not too pleased with his comments thus far]
TV: But you people are not the only people whose parents are ashamed of, there's another person's parents who are ashamed of him. That disgrace to his mother and father is standing opposite me in this very ring... Yes, I'm talking about Fat Frankie!
[Fat Frankie raises his arms in the air rather happily to a surprisingly good pop. Tony grins before going on]
TV: Frankie, when was the last time you ever looked at yourself in the mirror? You're a mess. You don't deserve to be here in this very ring with a "pure athlete". Instead, you should be down the local
McDonalds. Atleast that way you'll feel more at home, unless you are often on the receiving end of a massacre!
[Tony turns his back on Fat Frankie and drops the microphone. Frankie looks angry as he charges towards Virtue who spins around and sends him down hard with a drop toe hold takedown.]
[DING DING DING!] AG: Wow, and Tony Vritue wasting no time as he catches Frankie off guard with a drop toe hold takedown! Virtue grabbing the legs of
Frankie and he slaps on an indian deathlock! Virtue a master of that submission and mat style wrestling, and he's probably the best technical wrestler in Amazing Wrestling Rampage!
JM: Just like I'm da best "technical lover" in Amazing Wrestling
Rampage! AG: Frankie screaming out in pain, but Virtue releases the hold before
Frankie can submit! Virtue now going for that leg of Frankie! Virtue going for the Figure-Four, but Frankie kicks Virtue in the butt, causing Virtue to fly into the turnbuckle!!! Frankie getting to his feet! Here comes Virtue...baseball slide by Virtue right under the big man! Virtue from behind and he CATCHES FRANKIE OFF GUARD WITH A
CHICKENWING!!!! Frankie in serious trouble here as Virtue tightens that chickenwing submission hold and we could see a submission right here!
JM: I makes all the shorties submit! AG: Frankie looks like he's all but out, and Virtue releases the hold! Irish Whip by Virtue sends the big man to the ropes! Virtue charages in and DROPKICK BY VIRTUE causes Frankie to stumble aright into the turnbuckle!!!! Frankie stumbling back and Virtue hooks him! Waistlock by Virtue, grabs the leg annnnnnd OH MY GOODNESS!!!!! SALTO SUPLEX BY
VIRTUE!!! I can't believe he actually got the big man up in the air like that!!! Virtue calls that move the "Pure Genius" and I think the
Sensational One has been schooled tonight! Roma with the count...
One....... Two....... Three!!!!!!! [DING DING DING] AB: The winner of this contest..."Pure Athlete" TONY VIRTUE!!! AG: Tony Virtue picking up his first win here in the AWR and he did it in impressive fashion!
JM: Yo!!!! Somebody is in da ring! AG: A fan has just climbed into the ring and Virtue doesn't see him! The fan from behind and he begins RUBBING THE CHEST OF TONY VIRTUE!!! Virtue just SHOVES the fan down to the mat in disgust and a kick to the face for good measure! Fans, I understand that you are very excited about the AWR, but you should never climb into the ring!
JM: Best ta recognize! [The cameras get a shot at Danny Moore in the crowd once again holding up a pink GWO tee shirt, waving it wildly in the air as the fans in the audience cheer him on.]
AG: Danny Moore seems to be enjoying himself tonight! JM: I think he's gay yo! AG: Up next we've got two of the top young stars in the AWR! Devon
Case to take on Byron Hawke! Remember that on our inaugural card,
Byron Hawke interfered in the match between Devon Case and Mark
McMahon, attacking BOTH men! Neither Case nor Hawke was happy about that, and though we haven't heard much from Hawke lately, Devon Case will get his chance for revenge here tonight! Without anything further, let's take it to Andy Burton!
JM: Yo, hold up. Why you ain't neva aks me for MY opinion? AG: Well...James...what are your thoughts on this contest? JM: Dat isn't whut I axed you! I axed WHY you ain't neva be aks a brotha for his opinion? I thought we wuz down.
AG: I'm sorry, James! I didn't mean to rush and forget about you, okay?! I didn't mean to keep you in the dark!
JM: Oh, so now it's a black versus white thang? AG: JAMES! I didn't say ANYTHING about that!! Now, give me your damn opinion! JM: A'ight! I think dis match iz okay. It's a'ight. AG: That's it? That's what you think of this match? JM: Match? Whut match? AG: Do you ever think much at all? [DING DING DING] AB: Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, weighing in at 258 pounds, from Washington, DC, here is...BYRON HAWKE! ["Turn On The Night" by Lois & Lane begins to play from the COLLECTION
OF GAY CLASSICS CD. Byron Hawke storms down to the ring, shooting looks of hatred at the members of the audience, who rain down a storm of boos.]
AB: And his opponent, weighing in at 225 pounds, from Las Vegas, Nevada, here is...DEVON CASE! ["Eat You Up (Original Mix)" by Angie Gold begins to play. Devon Case looks over at Entrance Music Boy in disgust, and continues on his way to the ring.]
AG: Both competitors very known for being intense individuals. Case and Hawke now sizing each other up in the ring.
JM: Yo, Byron Hawke was in da military, so dat makes him the more dangerous one. AG: Very true, James. [DING DING DING] AG: This match is underway, and Byron Hawke shoving Devon Case back to the corner, holding him by the throat! Hawke reminds me of a
high-school bully! JM: True dat! I wuz a bully in my own right, yo! AG: Hawke pulled away by referee James Roma. But as Roma reprimands
Hawke, Case takes advantage and blindsides him! These two men will stoop to any length to win! Case with a series of right hands, followed by a boot to the midsection to double Hawke over. Rocker dropper by Devon Case, takes Byron down! Devon Case, the Anti-Hero, cockily walking around the ring. Case now pulling Hawke to his feet, and he applies a full nelson and turns it into a dragon suplex!
JM: Yo, any second now Hawke is gonna snap on his ass! AG: Case sets up Hawke for a side Russian legsweep, but Hawke blocks it and turns around to clothesline Devon Case! Case back on his feet, and
Hawke lays him out with a haymaker right hand! Hawke with a jumping kneedrop followed by a cover! ONE!
TWO! NO! Case kicks out! Hawke up in James Roma's face like a drill sergeant! Hawke back on Case, and he executes a swinging neckbreaker!
Byron Hawke looking like he wants to set up Case for the Gallows! Boot to the midsection by Hawke! He's got Case up for the Gallows! WAIT A
MINUTE! HURRICANRANA by Case into a pinning combination! ONE!!!
TWO! THREE! CASE WINS IT! [DING DING DING] AB: Here is your winner, DEVON CASE! AG: Devon Case gets his first win here in Amazing Wrestling Rampage, and winning it in impressive fashion, handing Byron Hawke his first loss!
JM: He be da Anti Hero Son! Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
