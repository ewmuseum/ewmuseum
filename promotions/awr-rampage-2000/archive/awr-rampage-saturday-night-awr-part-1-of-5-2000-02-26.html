Saturday Night AWR (Part 1 of 5)

2000-02-26
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VOICE: Public Access 63 presents.... SATURDAY NIGHT AWR!!! [A muddled up, fuzzy mix of "Enjoy The Silence" by Depeche Mode begins to play in the background as we appear to open up in the designated "backstage" area of the 2nd floor of the East River Rail
Shipping Warehouse. Backstage we see AWR Promoter, Anthony Gallo, wearing a decent looking sport coat and holding a few index cards containing what is presumably his speech. Gallo is visibly sweating and is looking tense. Andy Burton, AWR commentator, walks into the frame with a big smile on his face.]
ANTHONY GALLO (AG): Andy, how do you do it? You remain so calm. [sighs] I guess its because you've been to the big show already, huh? ANDY BURTON (AB): Don't sweat it. Everything's gonna go GREAT! It's about showtime, so get ready to make your BIG ENTRANCE!!!
AG: Will do, Andy. I haven't been out there all day. I'm sure there's a big crowd waiting for the show! And thanks!
[Andy Burton walks down the corridor, followed out by the sole AWR cameraman. The picture is extremely fuzzy and the sound is muddled, as one would expect from any decent Public Access program. Andy stops in front of a door on the left and opens it up, jumping out to meet a pile of scrap wood in a supply closet.]
AB: Hehe.... Wrong room, I guess. [Andy walks down a couple more doors and finds one with a yellow post- it note on it, saying, "AWR".]
AB: Hey! This must be it! [Andy opens the door, and is greeted with the cheers of exactly SIX AWR devotees. Andy is initially taken aback by the lack of bodies in the room, but being an experienced professional, puts on his best smile and walks down the aisle, shaking hands with each fan in attendance. At this point, the young man controlling the entrance music over in the corner notices Andy's appearance and skips to the next track on the
CD, "Policy Of Truth", which is hardly audible from the little radio on the
young man's lap. The warehouse space contains a ring, cheap string "barricade", and several scattered folding chairs. Andy steps up into the ring and picks up the house microphone, which is hooked up to a tiny, battery-operated speaker.]
AB: Ladies and gentlemen, WELCOME to the first ever AMAZING WRESTLING
RAMPAGE!!! [generates enthusiastic cheers from the few fans in attendance] I am your ring announcer, ANDY BURTON! And now...[waits for Entrance Music Boy to cue up the next CD]...here is one half of the
AWR broadcast team, the one, the only...."THUGGISH RUGGISH" JAMES
MACK!!! ["Return of the Mack" by Mark Morrison begins to play from Entrance
Music Boy's mini boombox as James Mack comes out to a relatively wild crowd reaction. James struts his way to the broadcast table and puts on his headset.]
JAMES MACK (JM): Yo, this headset reminds me of the ones the school nurse uses to test your ears, real proper lot! But where all da honeys at?
AB: AND NOW, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce the MAN WHO PUT IT ALL TOGETHER.....ANTHONY GALLO!!! ["Sad But True" by Metallica is barely audible from the back of the room as the fans give their best welcome to Gallo who opens the door and looks at the sea of six fans and a slew of lonely folding chairs.
Gallo is stunned, and his index cards flutter to the ground as Gallo begins shaking his head in disbelief. He falls to his knees and puts his hands on his head and begins to sob violently. The camera gets in close to Gallo and he promptly looks up and shoves the camera away.]
AG: GET AWAY FROM ME!!! [sniffs] I'm ruined. I knew I never should have trusted that homeless bum to post up my flyers... [sobs continuously] WHAT ABOUT THE BILLS?!?!! WHO'S GOING TO PAY THE
BILLS?!?! THESE GUYS WILL NEVER WORK FOR ME AGAIN!!!! ["Sad But True" continues to pump out of the boombox as Gallo begins crawling down the aisle. The fans are silent, continuing to stare at
Gallo, who appears to be a shell of his former self. Gallo approaches the broadcast table and wipes the tears from his eyes with his necktie. "Thuggish Ruggish" James Mack pulls Gallo to his feet and shoves him down in the seat. Gallo struggles to get his headset on we begin hearing his sobbing and whimpering over the broadcast.]
JM: Yo, you gots to stand up and be a man! You gots to be professional, know what I'm sayin'? Back in the hood you be robbed the second you comin around da way cryin'! But because you my boy, I gots to represent.
AG: James...you do the broadcast. I'm in no shape.... [James slaps Gallo in the head.] JM: You best be recognizin' the situation, son! You on TV now! They ain't no cryin' on TV!!!
AG: You're right! James, I'm gonna do this broadcast! And BY GOD...we are gonna put on the BEST DAMN WRESTLING SHOW IN HISTORY!!!!
[Fans clap and cheer as they gather around the broadcast position, presumably to be captured by the camera.]
JM: YO, BACK UP OFF ME!!! [fans scatter around to the other side of the ring] A brotha needs his space, know what I'm sayin'?
AG: James, why don't we give a brief rundown of tonights card? JM: Card? What card? AG: JAMES!!!!! JM: Aight...tonight, we gonna see da Secaucus Ninja against "Retard" Ron Robinson. Yo, I used to play ninja back in the day. WA-TA! AG: And we'll also see the massive Byron Hawke take on Yankee Doodle. I met Mr. Hawke back in the dressing room area and he is ANYTHING but a gentleman. JM: My boy is HARDCORE! I'm talkin' 'bout "Hardcore Hero" Tommy Payne taking on Sgt. Buck Smasher! Yo, ain't that the guy who beat down
Rodney King?
AG: I think you may have him confused with someone else. What about "Over-Hype" Dr. Barkley taking on "Ghetto Legend" Austin Taylor?
JM: Yo, it's a classic East Coast/West Coast confrontation! I'm down wit da East Coast so I say its gonna be ODB trashin' Austin Taylor's
FUNK and puttin' it in his TRUNK! WORD to Big Bird!
AG: And how about Sleepwalker against "Corporate" Alex Zagenski? Sleepwalker one of the most mysterious and unpredictable individuals in the AWR; Zagenski will certainly have his hands full!
JM: Don't be forgettin' 'bout the TAG TEAM action. Frenzy gonna put the beatin' down on some poor midgets. What up? Couldn't get any real people to face Frenzy?
AG: Everybody loves the Midgets. And don't forget about our MAIN EVENT! Former AICW LEGEND, Mark McMahon up against one of the brightest young stars in the AWR, "Dangerous" Devon Case!
JM: Yeah, they cool, but y'all know that seeing two midgets gettin' they ass kicked by Frenzy is gonna steal the show, real proper-lot! AG: It looks as though Andy Burton is ready for our first contest, as is referee James Roma!
[DING DING DING] AB: The following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 10 minute time limit. Introducing first, from Secaucus, NJ weighing in at
214lbs...THE SECAUCUS NINJA! ["World In My Eyes" by Depeche Mode begins to play. Very small, yet respectful applause for The Secaucus Ninja from the very small crowd.
The Ninja makes some wild karate motions with his hands, then jumps in the air to do a karate kick. Unfortunately, his balance is not as effective as his amount of energy and he falls on his backside, evoking some laughter from the crowd!]
AG: The Secaucus Ninja showing us some of his "martial arts skills" JM: Yo kid, he gots skillz like dat Mr. Miyagi kat in da Karate kid! AB: And his opponent, coming down the aisle, from the corner of Deficiency Street and Mental Disorder Drive, weighing in at 339 pounds..."RETARD" RON ROBINSON!
["Personal Jesus" by Depeche Mode begins to play, but the CD seems to be skipping quite frequently. Entrance Music Boy slaps the top of the boombox and seemingly straightens out the music. Almost no reaction from the crowd at the announcement of "Retard" Ron Robinson. The big man raises his hand in the air, then points threateningly towards The
Secaucus Ninja.]
"RETARD" RON ROBINSON: [stuttering]I...I...I...gonna...KILLLLLLLLLLL YOU! AG: Ron Robinson with some threatening words for The Ninja and he looks ready! JM: Yo kid, dat fool is MADD BIG YO! He look like he could smack dat ass real proper-lot and make change!
[DING DING DING] AG: The bell has sounded and here we go! The Secaucus Ninja making some threatening karate motions at Ron Robinson. The big man with a confusing look on his face!
JM: He scared yo! What a herb! AG: The Ninja CHARGING towards "Retard" Ron Robinson and OH BOY! This does NOT look good! Ron Robinson is CHOKING The Secaucus Ninja with one hand! The Ninja gasping for air and this big man has quite a grip on the Ninja! And HERE WE GO! "Retard" lifts him up in the air! The
Secaucus Ninja DANGLING his feet as this big man holds him high in the air!
JM: [singing from Onyx album] #THERE'S NOWHERE TO RUN TO BABY# #THERE'S NOWHERE TO HIDE!# AG: CHOKESLAM!!! CHOKESLAM BY RON ROBINSON! And Ron trips as he slams Ninja down! Ron falls and the Ninja falls on top of him! Referee
James Roma with the count....ONE...
TWO... THREE!!!! [DING DING DING] AB: Here is your winner, in the time of 45 seconds...THE SECAUCUS NINJA!!! AG: The Secaucus Ninja picking up the victory in very unconventional fashion in our opening contest, getting the pinfall, after "Retard" Ron
Robinson lost his balance executing a chokeslam!
JM: Yo dat Ninja kid gots madd skills! He did his thang yo! AG: At this time fans, let's take a look at a very special video package! VOICE: "Former AICW International Champion..." [We are shown video clips of THE hottest superstar in this biz today...that's right peons..."The Ultimate Male" Chris Del Rio. First you are shown clips of Del Rio and Gino Valentini from their classic
AICW International title match. You then watch as Del Rio hits The
Quickie (inverted DDT) on an overmatched Mista Smith.]
"Former NJPW J1 Middleweight Champion..." [Clips of the RAPTOR and Del Rio match from the NJPW are shown. Del Rio landing a spinning heel kick, and a top rope legdrop on RAPTOR.]
"Former AWMC World Champion..." [Clips are shown of Del Rio dropping DR. Hate with The Quickie from the
AWMC. Then you marvel as Del Rio lands a frog splash on "Midnight"
Darren Skiles.] "And the number three ranked wrestler in the world voted by the fans..." [Del Rio with a clothesline of Brody Thunder from the SCW. Del Rio with a savate kick on Jesse Muldoon from the UEW. Del Rio piledriving
Gabriel Whitecross from the CIWF.] "And the future of the AWR." [A still picture of "The Ultimate Male" Chris Del Rio is shown. Del Rio has a smug, $#!t eaters grin on his face...and gold on his shoulder.]
Woman: "Oh god! He's....HE'S.....HE'S COMING!!!" [Sloppy fade to a quick shot of Yankee Doodle making his way down to the ring dressed in a red, white, and blue outfit. The camera then moves over to the announcers table where Anthony Gallo is sitting with "Thuggish Ruggish" James Mack. "Enter Sandman" by Metallica plays as Yankee Doodle enters the ring.]
AG: Our next contest features the first of our talent outside of the
AWR. Byron Hawke is a former navy seal and from the little that I've seen of him, this man has a MAJOR chip on his shoulder.
JM: I got's all da shorties on my shoulder yo! Dey be tying a brother down baby! Tying a brother down!
AG: Let's go down to Andy who's in the ring! [The camera gets a shot at the small crowd of 6 people as we get a shot of a large, obese man holding two cans of beer in his hand, drinking both cans at the same time. The camera then turns back towards the ring where Andy Burton is standing inside of the ring with Yankee
Doodle in the left corner.] AB: The following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 10 minute time limit! In the ring to my left, from Washington D.C., weighing in at 235lbs...YANKEE DOODLE!
[A couple of cheers from two kids in the audience, otherwise, no reaction for Yankee Doodle. Doodle waves to the two kids, then continues to warm up in the corner.]
AB: And his opponent, also hailing from Washington D.C., weighing in at 258lbs...BYRON HAWKE!
["Return of The Mack" by Mark Morrison begins to play] JM: WHAT THE [CENSORED]??? What dat kid doing yo??? [Mild boos from the fans in the audience as Byron Hawke steps out. Upon hearing the entrance music, Byron Hawke's bloodshot eyes bulge out of his head. Beads of sweat trickle down his forehead, and his face turns bright red with anger. His cheeks start shaking, as he becomes more intense than we have ever seen him. He steps into the ring and begins to warm up in his corner, never taking his eye off his opponent.]
AG: Byron Hawke looks very intense tonight! I wouldn't want to be in
Yankee Doodle's shoes!
JM: Yo I ought to go over there and punch dat kid in his face! Messing wit my song yo! Don’t dey tell you never to mess wit a black man’s music?? [DING DING DING!] AG: Here we go! Hawke all over Doodle just as the bell sounded and he's hammering away on the patriotic Yankee Doodle! Doodle on the mat in the corner, and look at those vicious stomps by Hawke! Yankee
Doodle covering up and Hawke very aggressive in the early going of this contest. JM: He don't look like no Yankee Doodle dandy right about now yo! AG: Hawke just DRAGGING Yankee Doodle right out the corner and on his feet! And now he's just ramming him with some stiff forearms to the face, knocking Yankee Doodle back down to the mat! Hawke to the ropes and he NAILS him with a jumping kneedrop right in the throat area and look at Yankee Doodle clutch his throat in pain!
JM: He's crying yo! What a little BE-YATCH!!!! AG: Hawke with the cover....One... Two... And he pulls him up at two and a half! Hawke not finished yet! Hawke pulling Doodle to his feet! And Doodle fires back with a punch! And another one! And...no! He's quickly slowed down by a headbutt from
Hawke! Irish whip now sends Doodle to the ropes! Comes back and TILT-
A-WHIRL SIDESLAM BY HAWKE!!! [The crowd "ohhhs" impressed at the devastating move by Hawke. Doodle lays motionlessly on the mat.]
JM: I remember back in da days, my momz use to make me go to da store and get deez Yankee Doodle cakes. And I remember she got mad cause I squashed one before. Well dats what Hawke just did to dat Yankee
Doodle in da ring right there! AG: Byron Hawke now holding up Yankee Doodle just TAUNTING him as the crowd begins to let him have it.
[The crowd begins to boo the actions of Byron Hawke as Hawke continues to verbally abuse Yankee Doodle.]
AG: Byron Hawke showing NO mercy here tonight! Hawke now pulling
Doodle to his feet again! Doodle can barely move, end this already!
Doodle just standing dazed in the middle of the ring and he's MET with a stiff kick to the stomach at the hands of Hawke! Doodle doubling over in pain! Hawke now wrapping his arms around the waist of Doodle!
Lifts him up in the air and OH MY GOODNESS!!! He just BRUTALLY dropped
Yankee Doodle right on his head! He calls that move "The Gallows" and
I think that's going to spell the end for Yankee Doodle! One.....
Two..... THREE!!!! [DING DING DING] AB: The winner of this match, in a time of 2 minutes and 37 seconds...BYRON HAWKE!!! [The crowd boos the decision as the angry Hawke refuses to have his hand raised by referee James Roma. Hawke pulls his hand away and simply begins to stomp on the fallen Yankee Doodle once again!]
AG: Now, this isn't called for! Hawke continuing to stomp away on
Yankee Doodle! And finally, Yankee Doodle is able to roll out of harms way to the outside of the ring. Byron Hawke nevertheless with a very impressive debut. He's going to be quite a force to reckoned with here in the AWR!
