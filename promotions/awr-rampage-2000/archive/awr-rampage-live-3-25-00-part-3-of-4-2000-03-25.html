AWR Live, 3/25/00 (Part 3 of 4)

2000-03-25
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AG: Our next match features two men who have been really going at it lately here in Amazing Wrestling Rampage. Sleepwalker and "Hardcore
Hero" Tommy Payne have made it VERY CLEAR that they do not like each other one bit! This feud all started when Sleepwalker unexplainably attacked "Hardcore Hero" Tommy Payne after his debut match! Payne responded by attacking Sleepwalker the following week and the feud was on! Tonight, they settle it in the ring!
JM: And you know my boy Payne gonna represent son! He can't mess wit da Big Daddy Payne son!
AG: This is going to be a great match! Let's go to Andy Burton in the ring! AB: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first... AB: From Parts Unknown, weighing in at 255 pounds... SLEEPWALKER! [Sleepwalker steps out into the rec center receiving boos from the crowd. He is dressed in his usual black tank top with black camouflage pants and black boots. His gas mask covers his face. He ignores the jeers from the fans and stalks slowly to the ring.]
AG: Sleepwalker looking ready for war! JM: He gonna get it too! Payne don't play! AG: Sleepwalker pausing momentarily before taking off his gas-mask and he's just staring into space. He's quite a character.
JM: He be bugging! AB: His opponent... AB: From the "40 Projects" of South Jamaica, NY, weighing in at 235 pounds... "Hardcore Hero" TOMMY PAYNE! [The crowd cheers as "Hardcore Hero" Tommy Payne steps out into the aisle. He quickly makes his way down the aisle, ignoring the cheering fans. The camera gets a shot at Sleepwalker who finally awakes from his trance. He turns around and looks out at his surroundings as Payne climbs into the ring. Payne immediately attacks Sleepwalker as Burton scrambles to get out of the ring.]
[DING DING DING!] AG: And this match already beginning as Payne hammering away on Sleepwalker! Walker just SCREAMING at Payne to hit him more and Payne abliges with a big knee to the stomach, bending Sleepwalker over in pain! Payne to the ropes and ELBOW right to the face of Sleepwalker.
Sleepwalker just SHAKING the blows off! And now he's hammering Payne with a furious assult! Fists, kicks, elbows, knees, he's hitting Payne with everything except the kitchen sink!
JM: Yo dat's a DQ! AG: Referee admonishing Sleepwalker, but he doesn't care the least bit! Now he's CHOKING him! Referee with the count and Sleepwalker breaks the count at four! Sleepwalker backing up. He charges into the corner and PAYNE lifts his knee up just in the nick of time, driving that knee right into the stomach of Sleepwalker!
JM: Dats what I'm talkin bout! Show him what ya got Payne! AG: Sleepwalker backing up...HERE COMES PAYNE and he takes him down with a BULLDOG!!! Payne with the cover....One...Two....kickout by
Sleepwalker! Payne on his feet and he's stomping away on Sleepwalker!
Now Payne's returning the favor with a chokehold of his own! Referee with the count and Payne breaks at four!
JM: Yeah son! Told you my boy is hardcore! AG: Payne bringing Sleepwalker to his feet. Headbutt by Payne and
Sleepwalker shakes it off again! Sleepwalker responds with a headbutt of his own, knocking Payne down to the mat! Walker falls on top of
Payne for the cover...ONE....TWO....and Payne kicks out! So far this match has been going back and forth, neither man able to obtain a clear advantage!
JM: It's all good though. Payne's just playing with him! AG: Boy James, you're really providing objective commentary tonight! Anyway, Sleepwalker in control now as he pulls Payne to his feet. Sleepwalker steps behind him...setting him up for a reverse DDT here and NO! Midway through that move he turned it into a backbreaker variation!
JM: Backbreaker what? Why don't you say he drop him on the knee? AG: Because it's called a backbreaker variation James! Payne, clutching his back in pain!
JM: [laughing loudly] Payne in pain! Dat was type funny! AG: Anyway, Payne hurting from blow from Sleepwalker and Sleepwalker with another cover here...
One... Two... Three! No! Payne kicking out! Sleepwalker spreading the legs of
Tommy Payne open and he drops a knee right into the kidneys! Payne screaming out in pain and my goodness, I know that must hurt!
JM: Guess my boy ain't going wit me to meet dese two freaks I met down at da Q Club yo! AG: Sleepwalker now grabbing Payne and pulling him to his feet. Irish whip sends Payne to the ropes...Payne coming back and TILT-A-WHIRL
BACKBREAKER by Sleepwalker! Payne in serious trouble here as
Sleepwalker concentrating solely on the back of Tommy Payne! Payne trying to get up! Sleepwalker bounces off the ropes and DROPKICKS
Payne right into the back! Payne goes flying through the ropes onto the outside!
JM: Oh SNAP! Dat was PHAT! AG: Wait a minute! Sleepwalker off the ropes and SPRINGBOARD SOMERSAULT PLANCHA!!!! [Nice applause from the crowd at that incredible high risk move!] AG: High risk move by Sleepwalker as both men lay flat on their backs on the outside! JM: That was ILL son! AG: Referee Ramon De La Cerveza putting the count on both men and now both men are trying to get to their feet! Tommy Payne pulling himself up on the ring apron and Sleepwalker using the guardrail to get to his feet! Payne up and on his feet! Payne charging towards Sleepwalker and BACK BODY DROP over the rope seperator and Tommy Payne hits his back hard right into one of those chairs in the crowd! And I think
Payne is bleeding from the back of the head!
JM: Yo, check out the blood son! Dis match is OFF DA HOOK! AG: Sleepwalker jumping over the rope and he helps Payne to his feet and now HE'S CHOKING PAYNE WITH THE ROPES!!! Sleepwalker is choking
Payne with those ropes that are seperating the crowd from ringside and
THUMB to the eye by Tommy Payne stops the assault! Payne now ducking under the ropes and he rolls back into the ring! Payne looks hurt though.
JM: Naw kid! You can't hurt my boy Tommy Payne baby! You can't FADE HIM SON! AG: Sleepwalker following behind and Payne introduces him to some boots to the back of the head for his effort! Payne helping
Sleepwalker to his feet. Kick to the chest by Payne and HE PLANTS HIM with a DDT to the mat! Payne makes the cover...
One... Two... Three!!! No!!! Sleepwalker gets the shoulder up! Payne bringing
Sleepwalker to his feet once again. Hooks him for the suplex. Lifts him up...NO! Blocked by Sleepwalker! Sleepwalker hooking Payne now and NOTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX INTO A PINNING COMBINATION!!!!!!
ONE.......... TWO.......... THREE!!!!!!!!!!! NO!!!!!!! Tommy Payne kicks out of it! Wow! What an exciting contest! JM: Word son! Dey putting da hurt on! AG: Sleepwalker now going up! He's going for the kill here! High risk move by Sleepwalker!!!! Leaps off the top ropes and....BIOHAZARD
BOMB BY SLEEPWALKER!!!! This one's got to be over!!!! Sleepwalker with the cover...
One........ Two........ Three!!!!! No!!! No!!! No!!!! Payne kicks out again!!!! Payne kicks out again! What a warrior this Tommy Payne is!
JM: Told you don't sleep on my boy son! AG: Sleepwalker up on his feet and he pulls Payne up! Sleepwalker going for the armbar...NO!!!! Low Blow by Tommy Payne!!!! I don't think Cerveza saw it! Payne lifting Sleepwalker up on his back and
DEATH VALLEY DRIVER BY PAYNE!!!!!!! The cover...
One........ Two........ Three!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [DING DING DING] AB: Here is your winner... "Hardcore Hero" TOMMY PAYNE!!!! AG: Tommy Payne picking up the big win tonight over Sleepwalker and what a hard-fought battle by both competitiors!
JM: Told you son!!! Payne is DA MAN!!! AG: Wait a minute!!! Sleepwalker is up!!!! Sleepwalker is up!!! Sleepwalker spins the celebrating Payne around and... He's just staring at him! Both men staring each other down! Sleepwalker now grinning at Tommy Payne and he slides out of the ring! Ladies and Gentlemen, this feud is far from over! If you thought that match was crazy, you haven't seen ANYTHING yet! Next week, in the Main
Event, we are going to have Sleepwalker and Tommy Payne go at it ONCE
AGAIN!!!! However, this time, more is at stake! This match will be for the #1 contendership for the AWR Regional Title! It will also be a
HARDCORE MATCH!!!!! I for one can't wait for that one!
JM: No doubt! No doubt!!! Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
