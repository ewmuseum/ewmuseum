AG: Alright fans, we have had one exciting show this evening, and now we come to the Main Event. For the AWR Tag Team Championship, the current AWR Tag champs, Members Only will go one on one against the crowd favorite, the Gay World Order!
JM: Da GWO is wack! But I'd bang dat Coach Dike REAL PROPER LOT!!! AG: The crowd is pumped for this one, so let's go to the ring where
Andy Burton is standing inside, ready to introduce the participants!
AB: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and it is for the
AWR Tag Team Championship!!!!
[Big applause from the crowd!] AB: Introducing first, from Private Parts, weighing in at a total combined weight of 645 pounds, they are the AWR reigning Tag Team
Champions, "Big" Dick Phalus & "Little" Jimmy Johnson...
MEMBERS ONLY! ["Be Mine Tonight" by Promise Circle begins to play as the crowd erupts in the biggest boos of the night for the AWR Tag Team Champions.
Phalus leads the way greeting the crowd with an assortment of "crotch chops" Johnson walks slowly behind, scratching his "midsection" The
AWR Tag Team Titles are around the waist of both men as they continue their way down the aisle.]
AG: Members Only, clearly despised by the crowd as they will be taking on the beloved Gay World Order!
JM: Beloved? Yo, you turning fag on me Anthony? AG: No! AB: And their opponents, from San Francisco, California, weighing in at a total combined weight of 440 pounds, here are the challengers of the evening, Juicy Fruit & Butt-A-Cup...
THE GAY WORLD ORDER! ["I' Gonna Get Your Love" by Jade begins to play as the crowd ERUPTS in the loudest applause, cheers, and ovation of the night. The Jolly Gay
Giant leads the way tossing flowers into the audience. He is followed by Juicy Fruit and Butt-A-Cup. Both men make their way down the aisle dressed in matching pink binkini's with the letters "GWO" stitched to the butt of the bathing suits. Bringing up the rear is the very beautiful Coach Dike. The members of the GWO clap the hands of the fans as they make their way down the aisle. Juicy Fruit grabs one man, and lays a huge wet kiss on his lips as the rest of the Gay World Order climb into the ring. Members Only look on from the ring, a look of pure disgust on their face.]
JM: YUCK!!! AG: The Gay World Order here tonight and this crowd is GWO 4 Life!!! JM: I can't believe you made us come up here yo! On the real, I think you GWO on the D.L. son!
AG: Whatever! [DING DING DING!] AG: The bell has sounded and here we go! It's going to be Juicy Fruit kicking things off against "Big" Dick Phalus!
JM: I bet dat fag likes dat name! AG: Here we go! Both men standing in the center of the ring. Juicy
Fruit looking VERY anxious to lock up with Phalus. Dick, on the other hand, looks very reluctant. And here we go and.... Phalus backs away from the lockup!
[The crowd boos Phalus for his actions, as Phalus tries to complain to referee James Roma. Roma isn't buying it, and quickly admonishes
Phalus as Juicy Fruit waits patiently.]
JM: You besta not front on the ends for dis one Gallo, else I'm gonna make sure Juicy gets all up in dat behind son!
AG: Both men going back to the center of the ring, ready to lock up again! Here they go and... Phalus backs away again and the crowd really lets him have it!
[Chants of "Little Dick" fill the air as Phalus begins to get agitated by the crowd! He responds by "crotch-chopping" several members of the audience.]
AG: Phalus not taking too kindly to this crowd here tonight! JM: Dey probably want to see his "member" yo! AG: Phalus still trying to stall as we await for these men to lock up again! Phalus stretching in his corner and loosening up! Now he looks ready to finally lock up against Juicy Fruit! Here we go! Both men returning to the center of the ring! Both men in position for the lockup! They lock up...NO!!!! Phalus chickens out again, and this is getting....WAIT A MINUTE!!! Juicy Fruit from behind and he GRABS THE
BUTT OF PHALUS!!!! And Phalus is INCENSED!!!!! Phalus turning around and Juicy Fruit blows kisses at him!
JM: I'm gonna throw up yo! Dis is NASTIER den Big Momma's draws!!! AG: Phalus charging towards Juicy Fruit and hip-toss by the GWO member! Phalus back on his feet! Charges Juicy Fruit again and hip-toss again by Juicy Fruit! And Fruit with the armbar on Phalus!
With the other hand, Fruit reaches over and makes the tag to Butt-A-Cup! Butt-A-Cup skipping into the ring. Phalus still caught in that armbar and Butt-A-Cup puts his butt in the face of Phalus and is
RUBBING HIS BUTT IN THE FACE OF DICK PHALUS!!! And look at the look of disgust on the face of Phalus as the crowd cheers and laughs at the antics of the Gay World Order!
JM: Dis is OFF DA HOOK!!!! AG: Juicy Fruit letting go of the hold as Butt-A-Cup skips around the disgusted Dick Phalus! Butt-A-Cup now going over to Phalus and helping him up to his feet! And now he slaps on a bearhug right in the center of the ring! Phalus trying to get out of the hold, but Butt-A-Cup has that bear hug on pretty tight!
JM: I hope Phalus gots a tight behind tonight! AG: And Butt-A-Cup GRABS THE BUTT OF PHALUS AND IS HOLDING ON TO HIS
BUTT!!!! And Phalus BITES the face of Butt-A-Cup forcing him to release the hold! Referee James Roma with the warning, but Phalus with a drop kick right to the face of Butt-A-Cup, completely ignoring the orders of James Roma! Phalus now stomping away on Gay World Order member Butt-A-Cup as the crowd showers him with boos!
"Big" DICK PHALUS: [to crowd] SHUT THE HELL UP!!!! AG: Phalus starting to let this crowd get to him, and it can be pretty distracting wrestling against the Gay World Order!
JM: I bet you "wrestle" wit dem ALL NIGHT LONG! AG: Don't impose your homosexual fantasies on me James! Phalus grabbing Butt-A-Cup by the legs! Slingshot by Phalus sends Butt-A-Cup crashing into the turnbuckle! Butt-A-Cup stumbling back, Phalus hooks him, and BEAUTIFUL BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX RIGHT INTO A PINNING POSITION
BY PHALUS!!! ONE...... TWO...... THREE!!!! NO!!!! Butt-A-Cup able to kick out after two! JM: Yo man, I think I'm gonna go kick game to those two straight shorties after the show! Show dem how a brother gets down!
AG: Phalus over to the corner and he makes the tag to "Little" Jimmy
Johnson! Johnson anything but little standing in at a massive 7 feet and weighing 425 pounds! Johnson in the ring now and he pulls
Butt-A-Cup to his feet. Now he wraps those big hands right around the throat of Butt-A-Cup! James Roma with the count, and Johnson releases the choke at four! Johnson once again with a chokehold on Butt-A-Cup and he releases at four once again! Butt-A-Cup stunned and MY
GOODNESS!!!! He just HAMMERS him with a clothesline with authority and Butt-A-Cup is DOWN! JM: Da only time I's bee's down is when I take a fly looking shorty up to my crib, put on some nice musick, let her get under my covers, while
I goes to da other side and goes do...
AG: JAMES!!! JM: ANTHONY!!! AG: Why do I even bother? Johnson to the ropes and he DROPS a thunderous elbow drop right across the chest of Butt-A-Cup! Johnson makes the cover...
One.... Two.... Three...No! Butt-A-Cup able to get the right foot on the ropes! JM: Man, Coach Dike got's a BANGIE BOOTIE SON! I wonder if she can make her bootie go "da da da?"
AG: I'm not even going to dignify that with a response! Butt-A-Cup is in serious trouble here. He needs to make a tag otherwise Members Only are going to remain the AWR Tag Team Champions! "Little" Jimmy Johnson making the tag to "Big" Dick Phalus and Phalus is climbing the ropes!
It looks like they are setting up for their finisher, The Missle! If they hit this, it's all over!
JM: I got yo missle right here Gallo! AG: Wait a minute!!! Coach Dike shakes the ropes and OH MY!!!! Phalus is hurt!!!!! Phalus is SERIOUSLY HURT!!!! Butt-A-Cup crawling over to the corner and HE MAKES THE TAG TO JUICY FRUIT!!!! Juicy Fruit in the ring!!!! Right over to Johnson. Bends down and NAILS Johnson with a low blow!!! Roma giving the warning to Juicy Fruit! Fruit now setting up Johnson for the Irish Whip...NO! Reversal by Johnson and
Fruit goes CRASHING into James Roma!!!! Fruit coming back...runs right into the hand of Johnson! Lifts him up and CHOKESLAM BY "Little" JIMMY
JOHNSON!!! Juicy Fruit is OUT!!!!
JM: Yo, forget dem two shorites! I'm gonna bag Coach Dike and tell her to bring some of her friends over son!!! Get dis party started right baby pah!
AG: Speaking of such, The Jolly Gay Giant is in the ring! Johnson doesn't even see him! The Jolly Gay Giant grabs Johnson from behind!
Lifts him up and CHOKESLAM BY THE JOLLY GAY GIANT!!!! Phalus leaps off the top ropes...RIGHT INTO THE ARMS OF THE JOLLY GAY GIANT!!!!
CHOKESLAM FOR PHALUS!!!! And BOTH members of Members Only are OUT!!!! JM: So's da ref! AG: The Jolly Gay Giant now leaving the ring and Butt-A-Cup standing over Phalus! And Butt-A-Cup KISSES Phalus right smack on the lips as he rolls him up for a very "erotic" cover! Here comes Referee Ramon Da
La Cerveza running down the aisle! Cerveza in the ring to make the count....
ONE...... TWO....... THREE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [DING DING DING] AB: The winners of this match, and NEW AWR Tag Team Champions... JUICY FRUIT & BUTT-A-CUP THE GAY WORLD ORDER!!!!!! AG: I don't believe it! I can't believe it! The Gay World Order has defeated Members Only tonight, and they are the NEW AWR Tag Team
Champions! What a upset tonight on AWR Live!!! JM: Yo, hold up! How dey win! Cerveza wasn't da ref! Da white James was da ref! AG: James Roma was originally the referee of this match, but he was knocked out. Cerveza picked up the action where it lefted off and now
The Gay World Order is celebrating in the ring, while the fans celebrate on the outside!!!! The Gay World Order are the new AWR Tag
Team Champions!!!!
[The members of the GWO celebrate in the ring as Ramon De La Cerveza lifts their hands victoriously in the air. Roma finally comes through and when he sees what is taken place, he begins to wave off Cerveza.
Cerveza and Roma stand face to face with each other and they begin to argue over the results of the match.]
AG: Wait a minute! Roma has come to and he is arguing with referee
Ramon Da La Cerveza! Roma saying that the GWO is NOT the AWR Tag Team
Champions!
JM: So who's right? AG: Well, since Roma was the official assigned to this contest, I guess it's his call, although I don't know what can he possibibly call since he was knocked out! Wait! Roma whispering something to Andy
Burton and I think we are going to get the official results of this contest.
AB: The referee has ruled that since he was out of position, this match is a NO-CONTEST! Since the tag titles can only change hands on a pinfall or submission, Members Only REMAINS Tag Team Champions!
AG: Well, I guess we have it. Members Only are STILL the AWR Tag Team
Champions and this crowd not happy one bit, and neither is the Gay
World Order!
JM: I'd be happy if I can give Coach Dike my member! AG: Members Only grabbing the titles away from the GWO and, THE GWO
CLEANS HOUSE!!!! Clothesline for Phalus!!!! Clothesline for
Johnson!!!! The Gay World Order clearing the ring and Members Only running away to the back! Now the fans coming into the ring to celebrate with the Gay World Order! Folks, this has been one unique evening for Amazing Wrestling Rampage!!!! Be sure to join us next week for that explosive hardcore Main Event! Good night!
