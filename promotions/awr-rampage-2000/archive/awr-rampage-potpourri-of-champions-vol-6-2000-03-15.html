Potpourri of Champions, Vol. 6

15 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
---------- "Pure Athlete" Tony Virtue ---------- [The scene fades inside an empty gymnasium/fitness centre. Directly in front of the camera is a long line of running machines. The camera takes a slow pan around from left to right, taking a look at all the different weight machines as well as the rowing, stepping and cycling machines. Once we have stopped looking around we start to hear footsteps. They begin to come more frequent and louder and louder as if they are approaching. The camera turns around and standing there, wearing white sneekers, white shorts and work-out vest is none other than wrestling veteran "Pure Athlete" Tony Virtue with towel in hand.
After taking a few glances around at the surroundings, Virtue looks straight in front of him at the camera and then speaks]
TV: You know this is the home of _real_ athletes. The gymnasium or as many call it, the fitness centre is where all the world-class top athletes train including myself. You know, in recent years wrestling has gone one way and that's downhill. Nowadays, wrestlers dress up in colorful and glitzy attire. They are accompanied by several gimmicks such as 'hoes' and 'bodyguards'. Sports entertainment is ruining the great sport that is wrestling but soon, it will not. Now Tony Virtue has returned to the squared circle, wrestling will return back to it's very competitive and technical old self.
[Tony takes his time by taking a gentle stroll towards the running machine. He steps onto the platform, tapsin some digits and before you know it he's jogging. The camera moves in front of him and he talks whilst jogging gently]
TV: Now as you have probably already discovered, I am a very down to earth, calm and relaxed human being but there's a few things that I really cannot stand and that's all those internet jerks and nerds who write articles about how 'Tony Virtue is too old' and how 'Tony Virtue is a has-been'. That right there is absolute ludicrous. Those nerds who sit in front of their computers all day every day are just jealous. They are jealous of me because they know they I may be thirty seven years old but I'm still as quick, I'm still as talented and I'm still the greatest technicalspecimen in pro-wrestling and soon, the whole world will realise that again.
[Virtue begins to sweat slightly at the top of his fore-head. He takes a few breaths to cool him and calm him down before speaking once again]
TV: The reason why the whole world will realise that I'm still as good as I've always been even when I made my wrestling debut in the early eighties is because I have signed a contract to compete in an organisation, known as "Amazing Wrestling Rampage". Unlike most other federations that brag about being popular and having lots of pyro's and nonsense, the AWR is different. It is an indy fed and is based in one region, that region being the Northwest of the United States.
[The running machine starts to slow down making Tony stop jogging and instead turning into a fast walking pace]
TV: Now for everyone that doesn't know me, I don't wrestle for the almighty dollar. I fight to compete and for my job and that's another reason I decided to sign for the AWR. At the current moment, the AWR is a small, delicate organisation. You only have to watch their first show and see that only six spectactors came to view and most of the wrestlers only recieved a maximum of $5 for their contributions. I returned to professional wrestling after my almost career ending absence because I wanted to help pure, technical wrestling to return and to replace all this "sports entertainment" nonsense and that's exactly what I plan on doing.
[The platform gets even slower until it comes to a complete halt. Tony steps off the platform and wipes his head with the towel before talking again]
TV: I've looked at the current roster of the AWR and I see it consists of the past, the present and the future of pro-wrestling. Guys such as
Devon Case represents the future, whilst guys like Mark McMahon represent the past. But that's where I am different. Sure, I may be thirty-seven years old but I'm still as fit and as quick as I was eighteen years ago. I represent the past, the present and for damn sure, the future of the great sport, that is.. Wrestling.
[Tony walks over towards the cycling machine. He makes himself comfortable on the seat and then begins rotating his legs on the pedals, taking too]
TV: When I make my debut match in AWR, make sure that you _all_ have a pen and some paper handy so you can make notes and maybe one day, you too will have the privalege of calling yourself a "Pure Athlete".
[Tony focuses on the cycling machine and starts realling pedalling as the scene fades out to darkness]
---------- "Gemini" Todd Phelps ---------- [A dark room, lit only by a single candle, outlines the silhouette of an unknown person. He begins to speak.]
VOICE: The AWR: In a time of darkness, in a time of turmoil, in a time of desperation. Things have just begun, and already they seem bleak. Men like Devon Case come and think that they can just take what they want. They seem to think that they own the place, and unfortunately, there is little to stop them.
Until now... A ray of hope, a beacon of inspiraton, I come to the AWR. [The scene lights up and we can see "Gemini" Todd Phelps. He is wearing black sneakers, blue jeans, and a black t-shirt with a yin-yang printed on the front. He smiles at the camera and speaks again.]
TP: Yes, I have come to the AWR, and for men like Mr. Devon Case, it is only the beginning of the end. What they have started, I’m going to finish. Devon, you think that you can decide how the AWR should look?
Do you think that you should be the champion just because your giant ego dictates it? Well, you can forget about it. Everything is about to change for the better, or in your case, the worst, and there is absolutely nothing that you can do to stop it. So just get used to it.
[Todd pauses for a moment before speaking again.] TP: I know most people have heard of me, and most people have seen me in action. For those who have, this isn’t for you. You know what I’m about, but for those who might not have, I am "Gemini." And what I bring to the table consists of many things: youth, talent, ability, charisma, the respect of the fans, and just a little bit of attitude.
But the two things that "Gemini" has always stood for, and what stands out above the rest are my knowledge and my wit of the wrestling world.
The human brain has always been considered the primary part of the human anatomy, even dating back to primitive times, and today, it’s still no different. Survival of the fittest is out, and now it’s survival of the smartest that’s in. And I "Gemini" have got all the brainpower that I need to do what I do best, and that’s winning wrestling matches and giving the fans what they paid to see.
[Todd turns to one side, stretching his hands out like a tv monitor, as if he can see the scene before him.]
TP: This week, I step into the ring against "Beautiful" Billy Long. Now Billy, nothing against you, as we’ve never met, but I’ll tell you right now that looks alone will not do it against me. I have a lot riding of my side, including our great fans, and you had better be at over 120% if you are even to scrape the surface with me. Not bragging
Billy, but I’m just telling you how it is. Don’t come into this match with a big ego and a bunch of overconfidence. That kind of attitude will have you staring up at the lights for a 3 count faster than
Richard Simmons can do jumping jacks. What I want from you is only your best, and I promise you I will give no less. I look forward to our matchup.
[Todd begins to turn away, but then turns back to the camera at the last second.] TP: Oh yeah, and on a personal note to you, Mr. Case... if you know how the song goes: "It’s only just begun..." [Scene fades out.] Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
