AWR Behind the Scenes (2/26/00)

2000-02-26
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OTE: The Pre-show/Post-show segments are done to serve the following purpose. Since, the AWR is a small, independent and struggling federation, we want to use these segments as a way of furthering the general storyline of the AWR, which is its struggle to gain popularity and financial stability. This type of idea is totally different, so we're hoping that we can make it work, because it just adds another dimension to the whole thing. So, we're going to require a few things of you.
DO NOT use these segments as a basis for ANY angle or ANY roleplay!! Doing so will be counted as a "shoot" from your wrestler and we will flat out delete what you send us. Don't even think about making veiled references. Most of these segments take place backstage, where all the real problems happen. None of these air on television. The fans have no knowledge of this. This is between the wrestlers and Anthony
Gallo. When we run shows, roleplays, angles, etc..everyone is to remain in character and focus on whatever storyline you are in. For now, the most we will allow anyone to talk about is the horrible conditions of the venues, and the bad pay. Nobody is to talk about booking decisions, or things of that nature. And DO NOT refer to
Anthony Gallo as a suit. He's not a suit. He's a normal, average Joe who loves wrestling. There will come a time when all of these things can be brought up, but that time is not here yet.
Each week we will feature different wrestlers in these segments. Since the nature of these is very conversational, we suggest that you download AOL Instant Messenger and tell us your screen names so you have a better chance of getting in on one of these segments. Please go to
www.aol.com , and download the program. Then e-mail us your screen name at [detail withheld] so we will be able to contact you. Now, enjoy the pre and post show segments. ---------------------- WHAT YOU DIDN'T SEE!!! BEFORE THE SHOW! Before the show, AWR wrestlers took the time for a meet and greet session. Some friends of Anthony Gallo, as well as some AWR Academy students took the time to do the fed a favor and help out with the ring setup, as well as the merchandise and refreshment stand. Over 100 AWR
T-shirts were on sale, as well as a program, and souvenir baseball caps. Some stars, such as Mark McMahon, had their own T-shirts on sale.
At the refreshment stand, the same ring-setup crew, as well as [name withheld] took care of selling soda, hot dogs, and chips.
After everything was in place, Anthony Gallo disappeared backstage to take care of the wrestler as they arrived. Everybody showed up on time, with the exception of "Over-Hype" Dr. Barkley, and things appeared to be running smoothly, but as the wrestlers emerged for the meet-and-greet autograph session, it became apparent that the night was set to be a disappointment, as only 6, count 'em, SIX, paying fans showed up.
Among the fans, a teenage punk kid who told Frenzy that he had nothing better to do tonight, and got their autographs on a used napkin, which was found later in the stairwell, crumpled up with a big snot in it.
A housewife brought her two sons and a friend of theirs to the show. Throughout the night, the mother would be disgusted by the event, and was outraged that it would take place in such a location. But she thought The Midgets were kind of a cute and positive influence.
The last fan to show up was a big man with a beard and a Lynyrd Skynyrd T-shirt. He was the only one who purchased a program and a hot dog. However, he wanted beer and was informed that the AWR has no liquor license and he promptly left the venue only to show up later with his own case of Schlitz.
Man holding a can of Schlitz: Hey, McMahon! Where's Byron Brady?! HAHAHAHAHA! MARK MCMAHON (MMc): Haven't seen him for a long time. Man holding a can of Schlitz: Yeah, I hear ya! *burps* I got me a ringside seat! MMc: Sooo...how many of those have you downed so far? Man holding a can of Schlitz: This a reeeeal $#i@hole, know what I mean? [looks around and pauses]
MMc: And wh.. Man holding a can of Schlitz: [interrupting] FIVE I think....but I could be mistaken. MMc: Do you wa.. Man holding a can of Schlitz: [obviously already blasted] YOU INTO SKYNYRD AT ALL? MMc: Yeah, I'm into some Skynyrd. You know...."Free Bird." Man holding a can of Schlitz: That's a kick ass song! Classic! [singing] FLYYYYYYYYYYYYY HIGHHHHHHHHHHHHH FREEEEEEEE BIRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRD!!! *burps* HA HA!!! Yeah! That's the ticket!! Oooh man! Speaking of Skynyrd....what's that smell?! [Mark McMahon holds his nose as something is obviously wrong in the air.] Man holding a can of Schlitz: Damn! Must've been that chili dog! MMc: So, you want an autograph or what? Man holding a can of Schlitz: Is it free? MMc: Just for you. Man holding a can of Schlitz: Awright! [unrolls the program from his back pocket] MMc: There you go! Hey, what do you do for a living? Man holding a can of Schlitz: Me?! Why I'm a truck driver! MMc: Great! Glad you could make it! [McMahon walks away to greet another fan.] Man holding a can of Schlitz: [shouting towards McMahon] I DRIVE TRUCKS!!!! *burp* [stumbles backwards over some chairs and lands with a thud, but is able to save his beer] SWEET WILMA MAY!!! I HELDS ON
TO M'BEER!! AFTER THE SHOW!!!! [Anthony Gallo reaches into an envelope and hands Mark McMahon a wad of cash. McMahon promptly leaves with his gym bag and a bandage on his head, as Gallo turns to the group of wrestlers.]
AG: Okay, I'd like to thank each and everyone of you for putting on a terrific show tonight. Now, I know tonight wasn't the best of nights, but it can only get better, right?
[The wrestlers make some enthusiastic noise as Gallo comes around handing out their pay for the night.]
AG: [reaching down into the envelope of money] Okay, Devon. Here you go. Great work tonight, my man. [Hands Case a wad of bills, which
Case opens and realizes only translates into about 5 dollars.] DC: [sighs with a short smile] You're pulling my crank here right? Come on Gallo, fork up the cash that you've been giving to everybody else. AG: We agreed on a certain percentage for you, Devon. Me and you, together....before the show. That's your fair cut. Everyone has been given what they've agreed to, and while its not a lot, you're the only one complaining.
[Case seems to get a bit more serious now as confronted with a challenge.] DC: I'm the ONLY one who put on a good show, that's why I'm complaining. I had to carry that old boned fool McMahon for a good thirty minutes and I get gas money, and hardly any at that?! How much did McMahon get, it better be five bucks.
AG: [sighs heavily and nervously. The room of wrestlers begins to stare at the confrontation between Case and Gallo] Mark McMahon is.....um....a "special case." There are certain other, how do you say, "requirements", that one needs to take to book a superstar of
McMahon's caliber. I'm sure you understand, Devon. [begins, packing up some of things, acting as if the conversation is over.]
[Case hovers over Gallo, not giving him room to pack much less think. He whispers so nobody can hear him] DC: The only thing special about him is his past ... you better start playing the right field Gallo, or I'll turn this goddamn shithole you are runnin' upside down. You hear me?
AG: [looks up at Case, agitated] Look. Mark McMahon was part of AICW. THAT still means something. It means publicity, and it means butts in the seats. You may know a lot about wrestling, but you have got a lot to learn about the BUSINESS side of things.
[Rebuting quickly] DC: Business side my ass Gallo, that is what YOU deal with, I deal with putting fans in those seats and making this promotion the best in
WRESTLING and ENTERTAINMENT. Did you hear the boos Gallo? Huh? You already have a goddamn superstar and he's not running on fumes of yesteryear. Why don't you do what everybody in this arena did tonight, jump on the bandwagon, I'm
that good, you know it and I know it, so soon, you better be forking up that cash. AG: Dammit, Case. If it's one thing I hate about the wrestling business, it's the egos, and YOU, my friend, have one of the biggest egos I've ever come across! Who are you? Really, who are you? You're a nobody compared to McMahon, you know that? Mark McMahon was a near household name, and has been on the cover of every major wrestling magazine in the country. You're
lucky if anyone outside of the Northeast knows the name of Devon Case! I know people in the business, Case. And word can spread.... [smirks and smiles very cockily] DC: Uh-uh Gallo ... you see, I'm not an automaton like you want all these guys to be in your promotion. I speak what I want, when I want, if you don't like it, you know where to take it. As for McMahon, did you see him in the ring, did you even watch the goddamn match?! Time and time again he was slow and off mark, he's useless, I am your franchise Gallo, you know that whether you wanna say it or not, and you shall pay me as so in time. Ask anyone in Vegas ... outside this decrepit Northeast ... if they know me, they will say I am the future of wrestling entertainment ... look it up, then pay it up.
AG: Need I remind you that, Case, that it was ME who decided to give you a chance tonight? It was ME who decided to book YOU up against
Mark McMahon! It was ME who persuaded McMahon to let you work this match to a time-limit draw! Why do you think I put you on this card?
It's because I saw your talent, so don't ever doubt me like that again, Case! Show some appreciation for what you have. You took Mark McMahon to a time-limit draw, and get to put THAT on your resume, when I could've taken the easy way out and jobbed you! So watch the ego, pal!
[Case starts to chuckle and walk away. He lets these words go like shots in the dark.] DC: He was so lucky wasn't he. Maybe he can find a real job now that he's got a name like Case on his resume. Don't cha think?
[Case grabs his bags and starts to whistle with the five bucks waving high in his left hand.]
AG: Wait. Look, I know tonight wasn't the best night. Only six people showed up, I know that. But it's gonna get better. Trust me, it is.
And if you'll just stick with me, I can guarantee it's gonna be a great run. You're gonna be a future star, I know that. I know when I see talent. That's why I pushed for the time limit draw. I just want you to pay your dues,
that's all. Let's not ruin our partnership over this, deal? [Case turns around with more of a satisfied look upon his face.] DC: That's all I'm looking for, the fair shake. Don't worry, I'm not leaving, I'm gonna do what I said, make this the best goddamn place for wrestling ... my word IS my word. You got yourself a deal Gallo.
