AWR Live! 4/16/2000 (Part 2 of 4)

2000-04-16
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The crowd begins to boo as the AWR Regional Champion, "Corporate" Alex
Zagenski makes his way out. He is dressed in a very sharp black Aramni suit and is holding a championship belt of some sort.]
AG: Here comes the AWR Regional Champion, "Corporate" Alex Zagenski and interesting enough, he has some type of championship belt.
Interesting because if you remember, he was not awarded an actual title belt due to financial constraints.
JM: In other words, you was too cheap to buy da man a belt! [Alex Zagenski steps into the ring and takes the microphone away from ring announcer Andy Burton. He shoves Burton out the way and addresses the crowd]
"Corporate" Alex Zagenski (CAG): Ladies and Lowlifes, your REGIONAL champion has arrived! [Loud boos and catcalls from the crowd!] CAG: Thank you very much! I just want to let you know that your opinions mean as much as your bank accounts! ABSOLUTELY NOTHING!!!
[More boos from the crowd! An "Alex Sucks" chant begins to start.] CAG: Yes, yes, I know you are all jealous because my net worth is more than all of you Bronx lowlife's assets combined, but that is understandable. Just like all of the jocks in the back are jealous because I am you Regional Champion! Since Gallo's too damn cheap to purchase a man of my stature a belt, I had to use my own financial portfolio to acquire a belt of my own. Here it is!
[Alex Zagenski lifts the belt that he was holding high in the air for all the crowd to see. It is a very beautifully designed championship belt, embetted with diamonds and made out of pure solid gold. The belt is curved in a circular shape and the words "Regional Champion" are imprinted in pure diamonds. Zagenski smiles as he puts the belt around his waist.]
CAG: Now THAT is a belt worthy of a champion! Eat your heart out Mark
McMahon, because this belt is worth WAY more then that hunk of tin you proudly parade around your waist! NOW, tonight, YOUR Regional Champion will be in action against this "Derek Diamond" Well when I take a look at his wrestling skills, he looks more like Cubic Zirconium to me!
Diamond, prepare to get outclassed and outpurchased in true Corporate fashion! And unfortunately for you, since you don't have the SKILLS worthy of a title contender, the REGIONAL BELT will NOT be defended tonight!
[The crowd boos at the obvious cowardance of Alex Zagenski. A cocky smirk crosses the face of Zagenski.]
CAG: Maybe when you get some QUALITY wins you can earn a title shot! But until then, stick to the UNDERCARD where ya belong pal! Get ready to take the loss tonight Derek! CORPORATE STYLE!
[Alex Zagenski raises his hands in the air, then steps out of the ring and makes his way to the back, the crowd booing him all the long.]
AG: These fans certainly not behind that man! JM: Dats because they ain't gettin' hooked up with da phat quotes! AG: Our next contest is all about revenge. On our last show, Tommy
Payne was brutally attacked by Tony Virtue after suffering a tough and grueling defeat at the hands of Sleepwalker. Virtue slapped Payne in a figure four leglock and had him in that particular hold for 10 MINUTES after we went off the air. I don't know how Tommy Payne is going to even walk down the aisle, much less climb into the ring with an athlete on the calibur of Tony Virtue. But knowing Tommy Payne, I know this young man has a tremendous amount of heart, so if there is anybody that will do it, it's him.
JM: No doubt son! Payne takes care of his son! He knows how to handle his business! AG: Let's go to Andy! AB: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Victoria, Texas, weighing in at 256 pounds, here is...
"Pure Athlete" TONY VIRTUE!!! ["Sleep Now In The Fire" by Rage Against The Machine plays as Tony
Virtue steps out onto the entranceway, receiving loud boos from the crowd. Entrance Music Boy bobs his head to the song, really digging the music. Virtue gives an evil scrowl at Entrance Music Boy and the boy immediately shuts the music off. Virtue makes his way down the aisle, ignoring the jeering fans. He rolls into the ring, heads over to his corner and begins to warm up.]
AG: When it comes down to pure, straight up, technical wrestling, I don't think there is an athlete here in the AWR that can equal the technical ability of Tony Virtue.
JM: When it comes down to KNOCKING DA BOOTS, I KNOW there ain't no athlete in da AWR dat can equal da technical ability of JAMES MACK!
AB: And his opponent, from the 40 projects in South Jamaica, NY! Weighing in at 235 pounds... "Hardcore Hero" TOMMY PAYNE!!! ["Party Up (Up In Here)" by DMX plays as Tommy Payne slowly makes his way out, limping down the aisle. It is obvious that Payne is still suffering from the attack of Tony Virtue. However, Payne, being the warrior that he is, sucks the pain up. He slaps the hands of the fans as he slowly limps his way down the aisle.]
AG: Tommy Payne certainly not 100% for this contest, but he's going to give it a go tonight!
JM: D...M...X... all my ni@@as! AG: Payne in the ring now and Virtue looking at him. Here we go! [DING DING DING!] AG: And here we go! Payne still clutching that leg of his. Virtue slowly moving in towards Payne! Virtue grabs Payne by the leg and takes him off his feet! Payne grabbing the ropes, trying to pull himself back up. Payne on his feet, hobbling towards Virtue. Virtue grabs the leg of Payne again, takes him off his feet! Payne grabbing the ropes again, pulling himself up! Virtue moves in and takes him down with a Russian Leg Sweep! And Virtue immediately going to work on that leg, bending it back out of position!
JM: Yo man, dat Virtue think he all dat wit da fancy moves! Let him fight my boy Payne in a hardcore match and we'll see how it go down!
AG: Payne screaming out in pain as Virtue twists that leg! Referee
Ramon De La Cerveza asks for a submission, but Payne shakes his head no in refusal. Virtue releases the hold and now he brings Payne to his feet. Irish Whip sends Payne to the ropes! Payne coming back...ducks the clothesline attempt by Virtue, comes back and NAILS Tony Virtue with a clothesline from behind! Payne now stomping away on the fallen
Virtue and now he backs away and clutches that hurt leg of his! Virtue really did a number on him last week!
JM: Yeah Payne, show him how we do son! AG: Payne now falls on top of Virtue for the cover.....one....two..... NO! Virtue gets the shoulder up! Payne now bringing Virtue to his feet. And elbow to the chest of Virtue! Irish Whip sends Virtue to the ropes! Virtue coming back....kick to the chest by Payne! Virtue bends down...Payne hooks him by the head and DDT TO THE MAT! What a move by Tommy Payne!!! Payne with the cover again....
ONE........... TWO........... THREE!!!!!! NO!!!!!! Tony Virtue able to kick out at the very last minute! Payne really holding his own tonight, despite being hurt!
JM: You can't feel no pain in da ghetto, else you be soft! AG: Tommy Payne grabbing Virtue and he sends him flying through the ropes onto the arena floor! Payne slides to the outside and he's GOT A
CHAIR!!!! Virtue slowly getting up to his feet! Payne from behind and
HE SLAMS THE CHAIR RIGHT INTO THE BACK OF THE HEAD OF TONY VIRTUE!!!!
Virtue is down and this capacity crowd is on its feet cheering for
Tommy Payne! [Crowd: PAYNE!!!! PAYNE!!!! PAYNE!!!! PAYNE!!!] JM: What dat fool Virtue be talking bout? Da fans wanna see hardcore, not some wack ol' "technical wrestling!"
AG: I think you have a valid point there as Tommy Payne throws Virtue back into the ring! Payne slides back in as well! Payne now bringing
Virtue to his feet! He hooks him! Here we go! Suplex attempt by
Tommy Payne! Payne lifts him up and....
HIS LEG GIVES OUT ON HIM! Virtue falls on top of him for the cover.... ONE...... TWO...... THREE!!!!! NO!!!!! Payne gets that right shoulder up just in the nick of time! Tommy Payne tried to hit that suplex on Tony Virtue, but it was too much pressure for his leg and it gave out on him!
JM: Dat's wack yo! AG: Both men now struggling to get to their feet. Virtue still dazed after that brutal chairshot! Payne hurt from that extended figure four last week! Both men on their feet now! Payne with a kick to the chest! Virtue grabs the boot of Payne! Payne goes for the enzuguri...Virtue ducks the blow! Payne spins around....Virtue wraps his arms around Payne...lifts him up and nails a belly to back suplex!!! Virtue still holding on to Payne! Brings him to his feet!
Lifts him up again and another belly to back suplex by Tony Virtue! What a great move by Tony Virtue! JM: Naw, dat was wack! He should had hit him with some brass knuckles baby! AG: Virtue with the cover.... ONE.......... TWO.......... THREE!!!! NO!!!! Payne kicks out! Both men now on their feet once again! Payne catches Virtue off guard with a punch! And another one!
And another one! Virtue backed up against the corner and here we go! Payne backing up! He charges into the corner and Virtue moves out of the way! Payne goes crashing into the turnbuckle! Virtue steps to the side! Payne stumbling backwards. Virtue wraps his arms around Payne's waist and leg! Here we go! Virtue lifts him up in the air and......
PURE GENIUS!!!!!!!! SAITO SUPLEX BY TONY VIRTUE!!!!!! THE COVER..... ONE........... TWO........... THREE!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [DING DING DING!] AB: The winner of this contest.... "Pure Athlete" TONY VIRTUE!!!! [The crowd boos loudly as Andy Burton makes the announcement. Referee
Ramon De La Cerveza raises the hands of Tony Virtue.]
AG: Tony Virtue victorius over Tommy Payne and a big win for Virtue tonight! But you have to give a lot of credit for the kid, Tommy
Payne! He really fought his hard out tonight, despite being injured.
Nevertheless Tony Virtue victorious tonight! Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
