AWR Live, 3/14/00 (Part 4 of 5)

2000-03-14
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AG: Fans, I am pumped to see this next contest! They put on quite a show last time they met, but went to a 30 minute time-limit. This time, there is no time-limit, no disqualifications, and no count-outs!
The only way to win the match is when your opponent's cornerman throws in the towel! The towel match is one of the most grueling contests you can imagine!
JM: Tell me about it, yo! Even up in da ghetto dey don't be havin' no towel matches! AG: Andy Burton is just about ready for the ring announcements for what is sure to be quite a slobberknocker!
[DING DING DING] AB: Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is a towel match for the AWR CHAMPIONSHIP!!! AG: These fans certainly psyched for this one! AB: Introducing first, the challenger! Accompanied by his towelman, "PURE ATHLETE" TONY VIRTUE... AG: WHOA!! TONY VIRTUE!!! AB: ...from Las Vegas, Nevada, he weighs in at 225 pounds, he is represented by the GREEN TOWEL, here is...DEVON CASE!
["Strength Beyond Strength" by Pantera begins to play as Devon Case comes down the aisle to a chorus of boos. "Pure Athlete" Tony Virtue follows closely behind, holding a green towel. Case looks focused on the task at hand, ready for perhaps the biggest match of his career. He steps into the ring and bounces against the ropes, staying loose as he awaits the arrival of the champion.]
AG: The challenger is here and BOY does he look ready to go! [The 60+ fans boo the Las Vegas native as Case ascends the top turnbuckle, raising his arms to the air, mic in one hand, closed fist, the other. He hops down and raises the mic to his cocky smile.]
DC - From the looks of the handicap bus outside the building, we must have ourselves quite a number of Pace University students here tonight!
[Crazed boos from the crowd as many ARE from Pace University.] DC - And with all these special fans here with us tonight, odds are that most of you are spoiling your drawers waiting for the crippled 65 year old Mark McMahon to wheel his ass out here ... too bad for ya, seeing as how he is gonna get the worst ass-kickin of his god forsaken life. Mark, in a few moments you are gonna come down to this ring with that belt draped over your aged shoulder ... and that'll be the last time you'll ever carry that gold ... you will truly be nothing more than dead weight, just like all these students here tonight.
[Another wicked mad heel pop as a wild soda pop is thrown at the ring, missing completely. Case looks and laughs]
DC - Wow ... just like the sexual frustrations of all these fans, that soda MISSED THE MARK! and PREMATURELY EXPLODED! Just like Mark McMahon
... Mark you will miss the mark, and this time your purist attitude and lifestyle to get over with these fans ... well, it won't help you, the time keeper and his watch ... they won't help you ... nobody will help you ... and not because they won't want to, no, because they can't.
They fear death, and I AM death, as you shall feel once more. [DC eases up a bit, and starts to pace the ring.] DC - I mean, let's think about this Mark ... I would ask the fans to think along, but learning to tie their shoes this morning had to be mindblowing [BOO!]. Let's think about this ... who can save you when that bell sounds? Huh? Todd Phelps? Hardly, I've never seen so many wanna-be purists in my lifetime. Wanna-be because it doesn't take a rocket scientist to realize Phelps is here for that belt and he knows after tonight, I will have it ... Phelps isn't playing the good guy
Mark, he's playing the wise guy ... and wise guys don't hurt their cause, and helping you is hurting him ... simple and plain.
[DC contemplates and struts some more as the fans start getting really agitated.] DC - Then there is Byron Hawke ... who I fear not an ounce. Byron, you must feel like a real man huh? Big guy coming out here a few weeks ago and laying me out from behind? Well, I hope you learned from that experience, I hope you took notes, cause after you left me there for dead ... I still finished that little bitch McMahon off ... just like I would finish you if we were to ever meet. You won't help nor hinder, since this is a win-win situation for you Byron, stay the fuck outta my way.
Sleepwalker ... Alex Zagenski ... Tommy Payne ... Barkley ... I'm sure you boys are anxious to see just what unfolds here tonight, who the top dog and the number one target will be ... who the real franchise of the
AWR truly is. But let me forewarn you boys ... don't get tough man syndrome, don't get ahead of yourselves. I will win tonight and become the new AWR champ ... and you boys'll still be the dirt under my feet, nothing more nothing less. I'll give the title shots to those who show courage and determination [chuckles] or maybe I'll just sit around and do jack like McMahon, only time'll tell.
[DC stops his speech and looks at the entrance area.] DC - Markie Mark ... it's showtime. Time to dance with the devil in the pale moonlight as some wiseman once said.
[DC drops the mic.] AG: Right now fans, lets take a look at this pre-recorded interview with Mark McMahon! [Cut to the locker room area, in a pre-recorded segment.] AG: Fans, I'm standing here with AWR Champion, Mark McMahon and his cornerman for this towel match, "Gemini" Todd Phelps. Mark, what are your last minute thoughts going into this title defense?
MMc: Thoughts? Tony there are no more "thoughts." From this point forward there is only GUTS, DETERMINATION, INSTINCT, and most importantly, ACTIONS! I have worked harder than I ever have in my life for this contest, Tony. I am going to give Devon Case a crash course in PAIN! There will be no choice, but for Devon's cornerman to throw in the towel after he sees the horror I will put him through tonight.
AG: But what of your relationship with Todd? What limit, if any, have you set on Todd before he can throw in the towel?
MMc: Tony, I have instructed Todd not to throw in the towel....EVER! If I want the towel thrown in, I'll say so, but I'd pass out unconscious sooner than I would submit to a lowlife clown like Devon
Case! In other words, Tony, there is NOTHING that Devon Case can do to defeat me here tonight.
AG: Well, there you have it, Mark McMahon EXTREMELY determined to dominate this contest! [Cut back live to the ring.] AB: And his opponent, accompanied by his cornerman, "GEMINI" TODD
PHELPS, from Providence, Rhode Island, weighing in at 253 pounds, he is represented by the RED TOWEL, here is the AWR CHAMPION, MARK MCMAHON!
["Becoming" by Pantera kicks in as Mark McMahon storms down the aisle to a welcoming ovation. Phelps and McMahon rush the ring at full speed, taking Case and Virtue by surprise.]
AG: HERE THEY COME!! McMahon and Phelps charge the ring! Case and
Virtue never knew what him 'em!
[DING DING DING] AG: McMahon laying into Case with all he's got! And a standing dropkick sends Case through the ropes and to the outside! Phelps, meanwhile, going toe-to-toe with Virtue! McMahon now picking up Case's towel and he's stuffing it in Virtue's face! McMahon and Phelps whip
"Pure Athlete" to the ropes...DOUBLE BACK BODYDROP!! Virtue quickly back on his feet and McMahon and Phelps with a double clothesline to send Virtue over the top rope and to the floor! Listen to this crowd,
James!
JM: Yo, dey best stop yelling in a brotha's ears. AG: I for one can't blame 'em! Tony Virtue recuperating on the outside with Devon Case, trying to settle down the challenger. Virtue now taking his place behind Case's corner, and Case gets up on the apron, waiting as Todd Phelps jumps to the outside as well. Mark McMahon removes the AWR Championship belt from his waist and tries to pull Case into the ring. Case, however, with a rake of the eyes. Referee James
Roma warning Case, who nails McMahon with a right hand. McMahon staggers backwards and Case springboards into the ring, and takes Mark down with a flying headscissors! What a move by Devon Case! McMahon quickly to his feet, and Case charging in! But McMahon is ready and he surprises Case with a superkick to the chest! Case is stunned and Mark nails him with an enzuigiri! A series of stomps to the midsection of
Case, followed by a legdrop across the chest! Mark McMahon firmly in control here in the opening minutes!
JM: Case better get his thang together, yo. Or else I'm gonna have to step in and throw in da towel!
AG: Well, James, it only counts if the cornerman throws it in. McMahon now applying a front facelock! Expect to see a whole lot of submission holds in this one. McMahon starting to wear Devon Case down, which is a very smart move. McMahon doesn't appear have his hands locked in, and will have to release the hold. You can see that Tony Virtue never thought once about throwing in the towel.
JM: Yo, if he throws in da towel just for dat, he a punk! AG: Virtue and Phelps following the action closely, while also paying attention to each other. McMahon picks up Case and slams him to the canvas! Mark is going up to the top rope already! He may be trying to nail the flying headbutt! He goes for it!!! CASE MOVES!!! Mark
McMahon attempted that maneuver WAY too early, and he paid for it!
McMahon is now down, clutching his forehead. Case gets to his feet quickly, and pulls McMahon to his feet as well. Case clotheslines Mark out of the ring, and goes to the ropes. Case comes back and goes OVER the ropes and nails McMahon with a running no-hand plancha dive! Case, the consummate high-flyer!
JM: Yeah, son! And I'm the consummate playa, true dat! Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
