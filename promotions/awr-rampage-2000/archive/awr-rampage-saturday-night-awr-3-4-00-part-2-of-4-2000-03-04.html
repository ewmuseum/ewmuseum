THE WILD DIXIE BOYS! ["Ball Tongue" by Korn begins to play as the Dancer and the Strangler come out to mixed applause. The Charleston City Dancer immediately begins doing a nasty Charleston dance the second he hits the ring, which illicits quite a response from the fans.]

[crotch chops] Because when you play with tools [crotch chops] that have this much power, [crotch chops] you might get hurt! [crotch chops]

JM: Yo, what's wrong wit dat kid? Where'd you dig him up, yo? I still can't believe he forgot my CD, yo!

JM: Yo, I'm telling you...if it ain't one thang, it's another, know what I'm sayin'? [DING DING DING] AG: This one underway, and it'll be the Charleston City Dancer against "Big" Dick Phallus. The Dancer continuing to do his
Charleston, and "Big" Dick crotch chopping away. The Dancer, arms flailing, charges Phallus, and goes running right off the ring apron and he lands flat on his back on the floor! The Virginia Strangler now enters the action! Referee Ramon De La
Cerveza certainly going to have trouble with officiating a match where there are no ring ropes! The Strangler clotheslines Phallus, and now
Jimmy Johnson trading blows with him! Action coming fast and furious, and the referee has completely lost control of this one!
JM: Yo this match is tight, feel me bro? AG: The Charleston City Dancer now taking it to the outside with Dick
Phallus. Remember there are no count-outs within the ringside area!
The Dancer whips Phallus into the ringsteps. Phallus takes the full brunt of the blow on the shoulder! The Dancer now doing a Charleston in front of everyone, but from behind, Jimmy Johnson pulls the Dancer back into the ring by the hair! Jimmy Johnson picks up the Dancer, and he CHOKESLAMS him from the apron down to the concrete below! Jimmy
Johnson turns around to meet an uppercut from the Virginia Strangler.
Strangler now with an Irish whip and Johnson with nowhere to go but out of the ring and into the first row of fans! Much better crowd here at
Angelo's Gym than we had at the Warehouse last week! Looks like a paid crowd of about 75 or 80!
JM: Don' lie, yo. There only 38 peeps up in here. I checked da box office numbers an' I counted too.
AG: Either way, James, these fans into the action, 100 percent! Dick
Phallus now setting up the Charleston City Dancer on the outside for what looks like a piledriver! He nailed it! He calls that the John
Holmes Special! Phallus makes the cover, but there's no pinfalls on the outside! Meanwhile, on the other end of the ringside area, the
Virginia Strangler in control of Jimmy Johnson. Strangler sets up
Johnson up against the ringpost and charges in full speed ahead! OH
NO! The Strangler runs right into the ring post, as Johnson moves away! Johnson now rolls the Strangler into the ring, and he's calling to Dick Phallus to get in the ring! Perhaps they are going for their finisher, the Magnum Missile! Yes! Phallus climbing up on the ringpost, trying to maintain his balance. Strangler out cold in the center of the ring. Johnson launches Phallus onto the Virginia
Strangler! It's a direct hit! Here's the cover! ONE!
TWO! THREE!!! Members Only advances to the finals! [DING DING DING] AB: Here are your winners, MEMBERS ONLY! AG: That was certainly a wild match, and we've got several wild matches still to come! JM: What you trippin foo'? I still gots a wild night ahead o' me. Bitches, hoes, an' colt 45! AG: Our next contest features the always dangerous Byron Hawke taking on our AWR graduate from Hawaii, Prince Rapa Nui!
JM: I hope he brings some of dem hoola-hoola girls so a brother can get his mack on, knowhaimean?
AG: Anything you say James! Let’s go to the ring! [The camera gets a shot of the small crowd of cheering AWR fans, then swings over to the rope-less ring where Andy Burton is standing, mic in hand.]
AB: The following contest is scheduled for one fall, introducing first, from Honolulu, HA, weighing in at 254 lbs...PRINCE RAPA NUI!
["Helmet In The Bush" by Korn begins to play as Prince Rapa Nui makes his way towards the ring, receiving a very small positive reaction from the crowd. The Prince steps into the ring, heads over to the left corner, and begins to warm up in the corner.]
AG: Prince Rapa Nui shows a lot of promise. However, he’s really going to be in for a tough night when he takes on the always angry
Byron Hawke!
JM: Dat Hawke cat needs to get some bootie yo! Maybe dat might make him happy! AB: And his opponent, from Washington D.C., weighing in at 258 pounds...BYRON HAWKE! ["Divine" begins to play as Byron Hawke steps out, receiving loud boos from the crowd. Hawke completely ignores the crowd and keeps his concentration on Prince Rapa Nui who is standing in the ring. Hawke steps into the ring, heads right over to Prince Rapa Nui and NAILS him with a huge stiff kick to the stomach before the match even starts!]
[DING DING DING!] AG: And Hawke just NAILS him with a brutal kick to the stomach! Rapa
Nui, bending over in pain! Lifts him up and THE GALLOWS!!!! He just
DROPPED him already and Hawke just made real short work of Rapa Nui!
Hawke with the cover! ONE! TWO! THREE!!! [DING DING DING!] AB: Here is your winner...BYRON HAWKE!!! AG: Hawke with the victory, but HE’S NOT FINISHED YET! Hawke jumps to the outside and HE’S GOT A CHAIR!!!! Hawke climbing into the ring,
Rapi Nui still down!
JM: Ahhhhh YEAH!!! It’s ON NOW KID!!!! AG: Hawke placing Rapa Nui’s head inside the chair and HE FOLDS THE
CHAIR RIGHT INTO THE HEAD OF RAPA NUI! Hawke now stomping repeatedly onto the back of Rapa Nui! He’s not out for the win! He’s trying to end his career! Hawke now setting that chair up! Pulls Rapa Nui to his feet! I think Rapa Nui may have passed out from the pain! He grabs Rapa Nui by the head and DDT TO THE CHAIR!!!! DDT INTO THE
CHAIR!!!!! What carnage!!!
JM: Byron Hawke is OFF DA HOOK son! He could roll wit me anytime and get my back! AG: And Hawke gets a few last stomps onto Rapa Nui before he finally leaves the ring. Get some help up here for Rapi Nui!
JM: Nah, leave dat sucka out to dry! AG: James, I think it's about time for my big announcement. The AWR has just had the local trophy shop down the road produce both it's Tag
Team titles as well as the AWR Title. And did you know, James, that tonight, I am going to crown the AWR Champion!!!
JM: Yeah, who dat! It's ME! [Anthony Gallo gets up from the broadcast position and steps into the ring, holding the AWR Title belt on his shoulder.]
AG: Fans, it's a great pleasure for me to make a historic annoucement here tonight, and that is that right here tonight, I am going to crown the very first AWR Champion! It wasn't an easy decision to make, with so many great talents in the AWR. But in the end, I had to choose the one man I felt could uphold the values of the AWR and truly represent us not only in the
AWR's home territory, but around the world. Allow me to introduce to you, the first ever AWR Champion...MARK MCMAHON!
["Shoots And Ladders" by Korn begins to play as the fans applaud the entrance of Mark McMahon. McMahon emerges from the back with a big smile on his face. We can see that he is very surprised by this announcement. McMahon steps up into the ring and Gallo promptly places the belt around McMahon's waist. McMahon walks around the ring and poses for pictures as the fans begin to settle back down to hear him speak.]
MMc: Thank you, Anthony. It's such a great honor and a privilege to... [McMahon continues to speak not really understanding that the mic is now unplugged as the 36 fans in the audience boo and hiss as they see the man responsible, Devon Case. Case has the unplugged mic cord in his one hand, another mic and cord in the other. Case plugs his mic in, turns it on, and looks at the two men in the ring]
DC: [cockily] Finally, my chance to speak on this situation at hand. Where to start, where to start. Oh, how about with this ... Anthony
Gallo ... you are the lowest piece of $#!t I have ever laid my eyes across. What the [bleep] are you thinking ... don't you see? DC: But hell ... I guess when you get everything handed to you, you can probably fix the time limits as well. Remember Gallo ... remember
McMahon, last week, I took your ass from pillar to post and back again,
I weathered the storm of that fruitless b-rate amateur Byron Hawke and still had enough in the gastank to polish your ass off bell or no goddamn bell. And now
... what do I see, the man who I carried and destroyed is HANDED the most prestigeous title in this rat infested $#!thole?! No, that's not gonna happen, not while I am around, not while the real champion is in this god forsaken building. And there is nothing you boys can do about that, now is there.
[Case just smiles as he looks at the two slightly perturbed men in the ring. McMahon takes a few steps forward as Case disappears into the dressing room. McMahon is not happy at all. "Faget" by Korn begins to play as McMahon and Gallo make their way out of the ring. McMahon heads back to the dressing room, while Gallo takes his spot at the broadcast position again.]
AG: WHERE DOES HE GET OFF?!?! JM: What? What happened? All I know is dat I wants to get off on dat sweet package of brown suga in da front row!
