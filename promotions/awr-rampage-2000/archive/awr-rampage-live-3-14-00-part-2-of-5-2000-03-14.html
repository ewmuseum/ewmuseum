AWR Live, 3/14/00 (Part 2 of 5)

2000-03-14
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AG: We're back and... ["Deuces Are Wild" by Aerosmith begins to play and the fans begin to cheer. "Gemini" Todd Phelps steps out to a nice round of applause, and he makes his way to the ring. He steps in and grabs a microphone.]
TP: HELLO, AWR!!!! [The fans cheer.] TP: I know that all of you know me, but nobody really knows why I’m here. Everyone has been asking. Why has "Gemini" come to wrestle for the AWR? Well, I’m here to tell you, and it’s for a couple of reasons.
First, to restore a little law and order here in the AWR. All I’ve seen from this place since it opened is a bunch of crap spewing out of the mouth of one Devon Case. Case has tried and tried to be the leader of the AWR, but has yet to do so. This makes him a desperate and dangerous man, as you all saw last week when he attacked Mark McMahon and tried to end his career.
[The fans boo the actions of Devon Case.] TP: When I saw this going down, I knew that I could wait no longer. I had to act, and so I made my move. Now, nothing against Mark, he’s a great guy, and has for a long time been a world class competitor, but when you have the youth and talent of a man like Devon Case, you have to have another with that same level of talent and youth to equalize it. And so I’ve entered the picture. Good luck tonight on your title defense Mark. You know we’re all rooting for you.
[The fans cheer again.] TP: But the second reason that I’ve come to the AWR, and the biggest reason, is for you, the fans. You fans that sit here tonight are the true wrestling fans. You don’t go a wrestling event simply because it’s popular or the "in-thing" to do. You don’t go to these events because multimillion dollar celebrities wrestle there. You come because you want to see guys like me give it our all in great wrestling matches.
[The fans cheer once again.] TP: So that’s what I’m here for. I’m here for you. You’re the ones who pay for the tickets, the merchandise, etc., and I for one am going to give you everything that I can in return. And I really believe I have more to give than some, and that’s because I am "Gemini," and like
I’ve done for so many times, I will prove to the scum of the AWR, men like Devon Case, that TWO IS...
that TWO IS... [A voice-over is heard] VO: TWO is a crock of shit! [The crowd, if you want to call it so, rises and boo to the best of their ability as Devon Case, no music, no wrestling apparel, regularly clothed walks out into the ringside area, microphone in hand. Yes, two microphones. Case smiles at Phelps and speaks]
DC - Todd Phelps ... [sighs] what brings you here? Oh.. the fans? The rantings of 'scum' like myself? [bleep] you! Let's get some things straight ... let's restore some 'law and order' as you would say.
First of all ... take those illusions of ability and talent that you so seemingly believe you have and bring them back into check. Todd ... I
AM NOT some pushover ICW cruiserweight half-wit who couldn't tell an armdrag takedown from a flying plancha ... I AM NOT some sickly female competitor or dim-witted idiot like SOME of your previous opponents!
[DC paces the ringside area.] DC - You may BELIEVE that because you beat people such as that ... that you are up to MY level of ability and intelligence. Well Todd ...
you're not. And I know it burns a hole in you Todd ... and I know that is the REAL reason you came over to a place like this, a place where money IS AN object which you hate so, but where the man with the target lies. So don't go filling these peoples' heads with the notion that you are doing this all for the fans ... admit it Todd, you are jealous of me, jealous of the potential I bring ... whether negative or positive to this sport ...jealous that you know you will NEVER be like me ... so you want me gone.
[Case smiles at Phelps] DC - And don't string McMahon along in beliefs that he is helping the older and less talented man. We all know you came for ME and more importantly my title ... which will be properly placed across my waist tonight. Admit it Todd, just admit it and you won't get the beating of your goddamn life, I promise.
[Phelps simply looks back at Case.] TP: Jealous? Jealous of what? Oh yeah, I remember. I’m jealous of a man who spent over twice the time in another federation that I did, yet
NEVER held a title. I’m jealous of a man who is so personally insecure that he has to insult all of our great fans just to gain some attention. I’m jealous of a man who is afraid of a 40 plus year old man because he knows that he’s going to get twisted into a pretzel later on tonight.
[The fans cheer. Some jeer directly at Devon Case.] TP: So don’t go telling me that I’m jealous of you, Case. There is nothing, and I mean nothing to be jealous about. In fact, it’s the other way around, and that’s why you are really afraid. You know that
I’ve been in the business for less time than you have, yet I have achieved more. You know that we’ve been around the country doing the same thing time and time again, yet I have earned the respect of the fans while all that you’ve earned is gas money. And yes, Case, you knew the instant that I came into the AWR that your career was in jeopardy. No easy road, no fast life, no guarantees, and that’s simply the truth.
[The fans cheer in response. A couple hold up "Gemini" signs.] TP: But the question I want to know, like many people, is "why",
Devon. Why are you here? You and I have been in grander places. You and I have wrestled in much larger venues for many more people. As
I’ve said, I came back to perform for the greatest wrestling fans in the world. And since you’ve obviously not come back for that reason,
Case, tell me, what’s your excuse?
[Case starts to pace the ring, his face gaining anger as he eyes down this new foe.] DC - You're so full of shit Phelps ... it makes me ill. The greatest wrestling fans in the world?! The whole handful of drunken illegitimates ... my ass. [BOO!] Unlike you Todd... I'll admit why I am here... because I don't fight for 'wanted' fame ... and 'needed'
glory ... and 'feeble' fan favoritism, I don't have to lie to the masses to get it done. I came here for that belt that McMahon has in his possession ... I know that I can make something of this rathole once guiding the way ... whether through ring savvy or brute force.
[Case laughs at Phelps.] DC - A man as feeble-minded as yourself wouldn't understand true motive
... nor would these inbred fans. And it's funny ... you and Mark have something quite familiar ... the same flaw. You boys both think just because success has come early and often for you, you think experience will save you from the big bad wolf such as myself. It doesn't, nothing can save you Todd, not your fans, not your ability, not your delusion, nothing, nilch, nada. You CANNOT defeat true motive, true desire ... not you, not McMahon, not even that scumbag of a president,
Anthony Gallo. You better start to understand that.
[Phelps simply looks at Case as Case smiles in amusement. Todd raises the microphone back up.]
TP: I was hoping this wasn’t going to have to turn this place into a school, but I guess I have no other choice. So listen up to the teacher, dunce, and I’ll give you another lesson that you should have had when you learned your ABC’s, provided that you actually passed that test. I, Case, am a realist. Everything is perfectly clear to me. I have complete understanding of the situation. If you think, even for a second, that I’m living in some fantasy land where all I do is think of victory and title belts, then YOU are the one that is in trouble, for
YOU are the one who will be underestimating his opponent. I know you have talent. I know you have ability. I also know that you will use any dirty trick in the book to get what you want, so I will be extra careful with every step I take towards you, and when I get you to you,
I will be ready.
[The fans begin to cheer, but Todd holds up his hand for silence.] TP: And I’m going to tell you that I’m ready as of right now. So
Case, I hope you really aren’t underestimating me, because I’m going to accept McMahon’s proposition, and I’m going to be in his corner tonight. And I’m going to personally enjoy seeing him contort your body in various painful positions until you call out for your mommy. I just hope for your sake that you found someone to throw the towel in for you, or else this could be the last night of your career as far as
Mark and I care.
[Case chuckles.] DC - You see Todd, you may not like how he contorts my body in painful positions because it’s not what you are used to, this is wrestling, not you and Mark's hotel room. [BOO!!] But don't you worry ... I'll find myself a towel man, although highly doubtful he will be used for anything ... but I will provide one, you shall see later ... but for now, you bore me.
[Case turns and walks out of the main room as the fans boo wildly. Todd watches him go, and then speaks again.] TP: Crack all of the jokes that you want Case, because when the time comes, you’ll see that I am right. You’ll see that I speak the truth, and you’ll see that TWO IS BETTER THAN ONE!!
[Todd lays down the mic. He exits the ring and slaps a few of the fans’ hands as he leaves.]
