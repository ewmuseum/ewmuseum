AWR Live, 3/14/00 (Part 1 of 5)

2000-03-14
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens up with a shot at the 60 or so fans that have arrived for tonight's festivities. We see mostly college students standing and cheering loudly anxiously awaiting the AWR show. The camera then gets a shot at AWR Promoter Anthony Gallo making his way to the broadcast booth as "Use My Third Arm" by Pantera begins to play from the boombox.
Gallo waves at the fans, then sits down at the broadcast booth, next to the AWR's very own James Mack!]
ANTHONY GALLO (AG): Good evening ladies and gentlemen and welcome to
AWR LIVE!!!! This is our new public access show format and we are here tonight at The Pace University Gym! I'm Anthony Gallo and sitting right here beside me is James Mack!
JAMES MACK (JM): What's up yo? James Mack in DA HIZZOUSE!!!!! AG: We got quite a lineup here tonight. In our Main Event, we have a rematch of sorts, when AWR Champion Mark McMahon takes on Devon Case in what has been changed to A TOWEL MATCH!!!!
JM: Is dat da type of match where some fly shorties come down da ring wit towels on, and the winner gets to take da towel off them???
AG: Not exactly James! We have a great card for you tonight, so lets get this thing started!
JM: No doubt! AG: Our opening contest of the evening will feature none other than
"Corporate" Alex Zagenski going one one one with AWR Trainee Skinhead
O' Connor! What are your thoughts on this one James?
JM: On da real, I gots ta go wit my boy Corporate yo! AG: I'm surprised that's your choice. I figured somebody like you would be against Zagenski.
JM: Naw kid! He gave me da phat hook up on da stock tips yo! Brothas gots ta get paid baby! Gots ta get paid!!!
AG: Anyway, let's go down to Andy Burton! [The camera scans around the crowd of 60, mostly rowdy Pace University students cheering and yelling loudly. The camera then scans over to
Andy Burton who is standing in the center of the ring, microphone in hand. Andy Burton smiles at the camera, then gets into his
"professional" stance, standing up straight and tall and holding the microphone up in the air.]
ANDY BURTON (AB): Our OPENING contest is scheduled for one fall with a
10 minute time limit. Introducing first...
[The Pace University radio station begins to play from the radio, as obviously Entrance music boy mixed up the cd/radio buttons.]
AB: From Boston Massachusetts, weighing in at 212 pounds... SKINHEAD O' CONNER! [The camera shows the bald O'Conner make his way down the aisle towards the ring. Mild boos from the fans as Skinhead ignores the crowd. He steps into the ring, bounces off the ropes and begins to warm up in the center of the ring.]
AG: Skinhead O' Conner in the ring now, and I'm certainly glad we have ropes this week! JM: But you forgot da 40's kid! What up wit dat??? AB: And his opponent... [Andy Burton is cut off as we see none other than "Corporate" Alex
Zagenski holding the tail end of Andy's microphone. He also has a mic of his own, which he immediately plugs in as the crowd boos him on.]
"Corporate" Alex Zagenski: Aw Andy why don't you just SHUT THE HELL UP!!!!! [Loud boos from the fans as chants of "Alex Sucks" fills the gym] "Corporate" Alex Zagenski: And you fans can SHUT THE HELL UP TOO!!!! [More boos and chants from the crowd.] "Corporate" Alex Zagenski: The AWR just doesn't get it. They just don't get it. Why in the hell am I opening the show? I thought I made it PERFECTLY understandable that a man of my high class, and financial status deserves to be Main Eventing this little dump of a wrestling federation! I mean look at me! I'm worth more money than the ENTIRE
Fortune 500 COMBINED!!! I am the epitome of class and high standards.
I am the greatest athlete and man that has ever graced this ring here. This situation is totally unacceptable and I want it rectified in the very future, otherwise Alex Zagenski will take his high valued services
ELSEWHERE!!!! [Loud cheers from the fans at the thought of Alex Zagenski leaving the AWR!] "Corporate" Alex Zagenski: But of course, this federation would be finished without my financial guidance and ring showmanship, so I'm sure that the situation will be taken care of in the IMMEDIATE future!
Now, on to this Neo-Nazi trash that I'm being forced to dispose of! Skinhead, WHO THE HELL ARE YOU??? I'm Alex Zagenski, Chief Financial
Figure in the wrestling industry. You are beneath me! You don't deserve to step into the same ring as me! You don't even deserve to live on the same planet as me! So I tell you what SKINHEAD!!! Why don't you take your Neo-Nazi loving, Hitler-Worshipping, Low Class SKUM
SUCKING self out of my ring and out of my federation before I beat you so bad the entire Stock Market will crash!!! You're in the big leagues now chump, and to be quite honest, I don't think you can MAKE THE
CUT!!! AG: Zagenski throws down the microphone and attacks O'Connor from behind! [DING DING DING] AG: I can't believe the comments that Zagenski just made! This guy has got his nose way in the air, as well as up certain body cavities belonging to his superiors!
JM: I don't care where he puts his nose as long as I gets my phat stock quotes! AG: Zagenski pulls O'Connor up by his ear! An Irish whip to the ropes, and Zagenski with a nasty clothesline, taking Skinhead down! Zagenski walking around the ring, taunting the fans. However, Zagenski leaving his back turned, and O'Connor nails him from behind!
JM: See, people always stabbin' my man in da back! In da office and here too! AG: O'Connor with a couple of European uppercuts, and Zagenski is backed against the ropes. Skinhead now whips the "Corporate" one to the ropes. O'Connor sets up for a spinebuster, but Zagenski turns it into a DDT! Don't underestimate the ring smarts of "Corporate" Alex!
O'Connor looks to be out cold, and may indeed be finished! JM: He's been bought out, yo! AG: Zagenski now giving the signal for the face first pumphandle suplex, a move that he has named the Corporate Ladder! He's got
O'Connor locked in! He nailed it! ONE! TWO! THREE!!! AB: Here is your winner, "CORPORATE" ALEX ZAGENSKI!!! AG: Alex Zagenski wins easily over Skinhead O'Connor! What is this now?! Zagenski measuring O'Connor, and he nails him with his briefcase! That was totally uncalled for!
JM: It's just business, know what I'm sayin? AG: Coming up next, we have "Hardcore Hero" Tommy Payne taking on
"Big" Mark Brickowski in singles competition!
JM: Tommy Payne baby! Tommy Payne! AG: Tommy Payne has been looking good so far! However, there's always the constant threat of Sleepwalker making an appearance, who might be
looking for revenge after Pain's vicious attack! Let's go to Andy! AB: The following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 10 minute time limit. Introducing first, from Teaneck, NJ, he's Built like a brick s#!thouse!!! Here is...
"Big" MARK BRICKOWSKI!!! ["Hard Lines, Sunken Cheeks" by Pantera plays out of the radio as "Big" Mark Brickowski steps out from the back. He slowly makes his way down the aisle receiving mild boos from the crowd. Brickowski taunts one fan, then continues his way towards the ring.]
AG: This Mark Brickowski is a BIG man! He should be quite an opponent for "Hardcore Hero"
JM: Man PLEASE! My boy Payne will WRECK dis fools' MO FO SUCKA! AB: And his opponent, from the "40 Projects" of South Jamaica, NY, weighing in at 235 pounds...
"Hardcore Hero" TOMMY PAYNE!!! ["Use My Third Arm" by Pantera begins to play as "Hardcore Hero" Tommy
Payne makes his way out, receiving a nice ovation from the fans! Payne begins to slap the hands of the fans as he continues his way down the aisle. He steps into the ring and raises his hands in the air receiving more applause from the crowd. He then begins to warm up in his corner.]
AG: Tommy Payne looking very impressive as well, and OH BOY! [The crowd begins to boo as none other than Sleepwalker begins to make his way down the aisle. Payne spots him and immediately goes out of the ring to meet him in the aisle!]
AG: Payne going right after Sleepwalker and HERE WE GO!!!! We got a brawl in the aisle between Tommy Payne and Sleepwalker!!! Both men trading blows with each other and PAYNE RAMS SLEEPWALKER'S HEAD AGAINST
THE WALL! Kick to the stomach by Payne! Tries to lift him up for the suplex, but Sleepwalker blocks it! And Sleepwalker with a suplex of his own and both men are down in the aisle!
JM: Yeah son! Wreck him Son! Wreck him! AG: Sleepwalker on his feet and he helps Payne up! And Payne FIRES a left and a right!!! Some of the AWR Trainees coming to try and break this up and CANADA DRY takes a stiff kick to the throat by Sleepwalker!
Hopefully this situation can be taken care of, but this match is going to be a no-contest! These two men need to really settle this score in the ring, so next week you will see "Hardcore Hero" Tommy Payne take on
Sleepwalker in one on one competition NEXT WEEK on AWR Live! You really don't want to miss that show!
JM: True dat! True dat! Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
