AWR Behind the Scenes (4/18/2000)

2000-04-18
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OTE: The Pre-show/Post-show segments are done to serve the following purpose. Since, the AWR is a small, independent and struggling federation, we want to use these segments as a way of furthering the general storyline of the AWR, which is its struggle to gain popularity and financial stability. This type of idea is totally different, so we're hoping that we can make it work, because it just adds another dimension to the whole thing. So, we're going to require a few thingsof you.
DO NOT use these segments as a basis for ANY angle or ANY roleplay!! Doing so will be counted as a "shoot" from your wrestler and we will flat out delete what you send us. Don't even think about making veiled references. Most of these segments take place backstage, where all the real problems happen. None of these air on television. The fans have no knowledge of this. This is between the wrestlers and Anthony Gallo.
When we run shows, roleplays, angles, etc..everyone is to remain in character and focus on whatever storyline you are in. For now, the most we will allow anyone to talk about is the horrible conditions of the venues, and the bad pay. Nobody is to talk about booking decisions, or things of that nature. And DO NOT refer to Anthony Gallo as a suit. He's not a suit. He's a normal, average Joe who loves wrestling. There will come a time when all of these things can be brought up, but that time is not here yet.
Each week we will feature different wrestlers in these segments. Since the nature of these is very conversational, we suggest that you download AOL Instant Messenger and tell us your screen names so you have a better chance of getting in on one of these segments. Please go to
www.aol.com , and download the program. Then e-mail us your screen name at [detail withheld] so we will be able to contact you. Now, enjoy the pre and post show segments. ---------------------- WHAT YOU DIDN'T SEE!!! ["Pure Athlete" Tony Virtue storms into a section of the locker room and shoves Anthony Gallo against the wall.]
TV: Hey Gallo, why did you give me Payne? I wanted Case or Phelps. AG: What are you talking about? You said you wanted a "newbie." I gave you a newbie. TV: Yes you're right, but I expected one of the big name newbies. You promised me man. AG: Look, Virtue. I don't beat around the bush. I promised you a newbie, in exactly those words. If you wanted Phelps or Case, you should've been more specific!
TV: [Trying to calm down] Ok... Ok.... Next week Gallo I want either
Case or Phelps, you got that?
AG: Look, man. Don't try to take advantage of me. I run this fed, NOT you! You'll get Case or Phelps in a match when and where I want it.
And it'll be ME and ME ALONE who will decide the outcome of that match! TV: Listen Gallo... [He moves closer, almost nose to nose] Give me the match or else... AG: Or else what? TV: You know what. We got a deal?
AG: No, and I'm not going to give into you! TV: Oh yeah? Well then you can just forget about ever having me on one of your pathetic cards ever again, GALLO! I QUIT!!!
AG: [trying to stop Tony from leaving.] Hey, Tony!! No..we can work this out! [Tony grabs his bag and exits the building quickly] $#!T!
