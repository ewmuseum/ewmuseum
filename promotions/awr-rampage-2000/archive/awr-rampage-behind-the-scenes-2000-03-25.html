AWR Behind the Scenes (3/25/00)

2000-03-25
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OTE: The Pre-show/Post-show segments are done to serve the following purpose. Since, the AWR is a small, independent and struggling federation, we want to use these segments as a way of furthering the general storyline of the AWR, which is its struggle to gain popularity and financial stability. This type of idea is totally different, so we're hoping that we can make it work, because it just adds another dimension to the whole thing. So, we're going to require a few things of you.
DO NOT use these segments as a basis for ANY angle or ANY roleplay!! Doing so will be counted as a "shoot" from your wrestler and we will flat out delete what you send us. Don't even think about making veiled references. Most of these segments take place backstage, where all the real problems happen. None of these air on television. The fans have no knowledge of this. This is between the wrestlers and Anthony Gallo.
When we run shows, roleplays, angles, etc..everyone is to remain in character and focus on whatever storyline you are in. For now, the most we will allow anyone to talk about is the horrible conditions of the venues, and the bad pay. Nobody is to talk about booking decisions, or things of that nature. And DO NOT refer to Anthony Gallo as a suit. He's not a suit. He's a normal, average Joe who loves wrestling. There will come a time when all of these things can be brought up, but that time is not here yet.
Each week we will feature different wrestlers in these segments. Since the nature of these is very conversational, we suggest that you download AOL Instant Messenger and tell us your screen names so you have a better chance of getting in on one of these segments. Please go to
www.aol.com , and download the program. Then e-mail us your screen name at [detail withheld] so we will be able to contact you. Now, enjoy the pre and post show segments. ---------------------- WHAT YOU DIDN'T SEE!!! BEFORE THE SHOW! AWR Live was held at the Gay and Lesbian Community Center in lower
Manhattan. "Gemini" Todd Phelps was very unhappy with the location of the card. He was scheduled to make an appearance to give his comments about the towel match, but refused in protest of the card location.
Danny Moore, on the other hand, enjoyed himself fully getting his first taste of the AWR. Before the show, Moore was found maning the merchandise stand selling AWR tee shirts as well as the GWO tee shirts we saw many of the fans wearing in support of the Main Event. Moore helped set up the ring, entertained the crowd before the show, and was found mingiling with the crowd during the show.
AFTER THE SHOW!!!! AWR Promoter Anthony Gallo and new AWR wrestler "Pure Athlete" Tony
Virtue got into a discussion about Virtue's current status in Amazing
Wrestling Rampage:
TV: [Signalling over to Gallo] Anthony... Anthony.... May I have a word? AG: Sure, Tony. What's on your mind? TV: Ok ... Now I have very pleased with the way you have pushed me here in the AWR, but I am a little disappointed with my current
"opposition". Let's be honest, these guys I must face are not exactly
"pure athlete's" are they?
AG: Well, I beg to differ. I think Devon Case is an outstanding athlete, as is Todd Phelps. And Mark McMahon is one of the most respected in our sport, as one of the old-school guys.
TV: Anthony sir, you cannot get anymore 'old-school' than me. I, as you know very well, am here in the AWR to help bring back the days when wrestling was actually considered a "sport" but I feel that task is impossible with several guys, specifically Todd Phelps, Devon Case and
Tommy Payne who continue to drag the AWR down into this "sports entertainment" nonsense. It's ridiculous!
AG: Hey, give these guys a chance. Besides, the "old-school" thing doesn't really sell that many tickets that much, honestly. I mean,
I've already had people telling me that its time to take the belt off
McMahon.
TV: How _dare_ you say that. Old-school wrestling is what the fans are missing out on and what they WANT to see. They are sick and tired of seeing no talent "wrestling" wannabe's, swinging chairs week in week out. I don't want to argue with you sir but.... It's about time wrestling returned to it's _real_ self, and that's as a sport.
AG: Everyone's entitled to their opinions, Tony. Yeah, I remember the good-old days, as well. But keep in mind that its the fans that pay for the chairshots, and if they keep paying to see chairshots, they'll be getting more chairshots.
TV: So, where does that lead me exactly? Are you telling me to start packing and to not let the door hit me on the way out?
AG: Tony, I'm by no means gonna drop you from my roster. If I didn't think you were such a great talent, I never would've asked you to work for me
TV: Well if you think I'm such a "great talent" why don't you take my advice and turn the AWR into a _wrestling_ federation and NOT another
"sports entertainment" show?
AG: Need I remind you that we are in a BUSINESS?! You could've been stuck "competing" for "honor" in some backlot alley brawl every week, but you're here in the AWR now, where I am in charge!
TV: OH, so now we see your TRUE colors. Well that's just great... You've got a choice Anthony..... Me or the "Sports Entertainment" nonsense. It's your choice! AG: Alright, alright. DAMN! Okay, Tony...here's what I'm going to do for you. I'll put you in there with one of the young guys, and put you over him, and we work this thing out...together. Do we have a deal?
[Tony pauses for a moment] TV: I suppose. [He puts his hand out for Anthony to shake] Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
