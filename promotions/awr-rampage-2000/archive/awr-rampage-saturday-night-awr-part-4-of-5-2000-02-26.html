[Referee Ramon De La Cerveza signals to the timekeeper to ring the bell. After Andy Burton steps into the ring, the referee whispers his instructions to Burton.]
AB: I’ve just been informed that the referee has stopped the match and has awarded this contest to FRENZY!!!
AG: [sniffs] Frenzy getting the win here. [sniffles] JM: AW SNAP KID! Dat was DOPE YO! Frenzy is HARDCORE KID!!!! DEY HARDCORE!!! AG: Frenzy celebrating and now they are making their way to the back. AG: Wait a minute! Frenzy is stopping. JC and Den stop right before the door to the dressing area. JC and Den looking back at the wreck.
They seem to be having second thoughts about their actions. The two remaining fans are all over Frenzy, getting right up in their faces.
JM: AW SNAP! I knows whut deez boys be doin'. Dey gonna lay another beatdown on the Midgets!
AG: Perhaps not! Frenzy making their way back to the ring, and they appear to be showing some real remorse. JC very concerned as he tries to see if Mongo Midget is okay. Den takes one look down at Fat Man In
A Little Coat and puts his hands on his head! Den was the one who put them through that table in the first place, and he really must be regretting that action! Den puts his forehead against the ring apron, as the fans have now gone silent, seeing that Frenzy's concern is indeed sincere.
JM: F#@$ dat yo! Drop a ILL bionic-elbow on deez fools! AG: JC now picking up the still unconscious Mongo Midget, and he's got him up on his shoulder. The fans give some applause back to Frenzy, who thank God, have shown their true colors and true compassion here tonight. Den now going to pick up Fat Man In A Little Coat, but he pauses now. Den looking around the ringside area, and he's picking up the pieces of Fat Man's little coat! Den looking very remorseful as he picks up each piece. Den now scooping up Fat Man, and Frenzy is taking the Midgets back to the dressing room, and presumably to find some medical attention. I, for one, am glad that Frenzy has come back to help the little guys out!
JM: Oh please yo! You knew full well what da consequences were when you booked dat dere match!
AG: Well, it's been quite a first night, I'll tell you that. We've seen perhaps the smallest crowd in professional wrestling history, now cut down to one-third its original size, four wrestlers taken out with possible injuries, including two midgets, plus our first no-contest, and now we're down to our main event!

Northeast, "Dangerous" Devon Case! So, without further ado, let's get this one underway! [DING DING DING] AB: Ladies and gentlemen, this is the MAIN EVENT of the evening, scheduled for one fall and a thirty minute time-limit! Introducing first, from Las Vegas, Nevada, weighing in at 225 pounds, here is...."DANGEROUS" DEVON CASE!
["Enter Sandman" by Metallica begins to play on Entrance Music Boy's boombox. The two fans boo horrendously as Case steps up on the near top turnbuckle, mic in hand, looking out towards the fans nearby.]
DEVON CASE (DC): You know ... the inaugural first card and I already have about an hours worth of constructive criticism for this damned upstart of a wrestling promotion and its personnel. Firstly, the next time I step out from behind those cheap ass curtains we call an entranceway, I BETTER hear MY music or hell will be paid in extra damage fees and replacement fees for Gallo. How in the hell can you mess up two Metallica songs? [Case pauses in contemplation] I'm not sure, but, let it happen again you pieces of $#!t personnel, and I will personally see to it you pay, and pay heartily.
AG: I thought the entrance music we choose was pretty cool, don't you think? [The two fans boo wildly for the man from Vegas as he hops off that turnbuckle and starts to do his little pace around the ring, mic up to his lips the whole time.]
DC: And then, a few weeks ago I was sitting at home, just woke up from a great night of rest when the newspaper hit the outside panelling of the door. Morning coffee and cigarette in hand, I took that local paper and opened it to the inside sports section, and to my chagrin, a full page was taken out, with our line-up for tonight tucked away,

Gallo. So, what I EXPECT to be done is when you go to your cronies and decide on some cutesy nicknames and tags for your brightest stars, you better ask for approval, cause if I see my name destroyed by such a weak and overused tag name again, ever again, Gallo and everybody in the back is gonna
find out what happen when you push a crazed sonofabitch too far too quickly. Just like McMahon'll find out tonight
