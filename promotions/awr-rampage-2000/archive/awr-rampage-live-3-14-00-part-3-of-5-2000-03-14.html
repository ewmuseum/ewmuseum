AWR Live, 3/14/00 (Part 3 of 5)

2000-03-14
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AG: Up next, we have the highly anticipated debut of the tag team
Illegal Tactics! As we all know, Phil Blunt & LSD made their impact in a BIG way on Saturday Night AWR by attacking Den & JC Frenzy costing them the tag titles in the process!
JM: Aw don't front kid! Frenzy is just wack, dat's why dey lost! AG: I don't know about that. But what I do know is that Illegal
Tactics should make quite an impact in the tag team division here in the AWR. Let's go down to Andy!
AB: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Uncle Bens farm, weighing at a total combined weight of 245 pounds, here are The Local Yokel and The Slack-Jawed Yokel...
THE YOKEL BOYS!!! ["Becoming" By Pantera plays as The Yokel Boys come running down the aisle, almost receiving NO reaction from the crowd whatsoever. They both climb into the ring and begin to jump up and down, obviously very excited to be here.]
AG: The Yokel Boys making their debut here in Amazing Wrestling Rampage and they are looking very happy to be here! JM: Not after my boys handle them a little sumthin sumthin! AB: And their opponents, from Philadelphia PA, and Los Angeles CA! At a total combined weight of 540 pounds, here are Phil Blunt & LSD...
ILLEGAL TACTICS!!! ["Good Friends And A Bottle Of Pills" By Pantera plays as Phil Blunt &
LSD step out, receiving loud boos from the Pace University fans! Phil sticks his middle finger up at Entrance Music Boy for screwing up their music, then begins to make his way down the aisle. LSD stops in the middle of the aisle and stares at one fan in particular. He does a fake lunge at the fan, a short male with glasses, causing the fan to jump back, then begins to laugh at the fan along with Phil Blunt! The fan begins to yell at them, but Illegal Tactics completely ignore him and steps into the ring. Phil Blunt stands in front of LSD and does a
"smoking" gesture to the crowd while LSD stands tall, raising one fist defiantly in the air.]
AG: Here are Illegal Tactics ladies and gentlemen, and you better believe they are ready for action!
JM: Word up kid! Dese cats can't see my boys son! [DING DING DING!] AG: Here we go! It looks like Phil Blunt is going to start this one off with The Local Yokel. They lock up! Armdrag takedown by Blunt, and he slaps on the Armbar on Local Yokel! Referee James Roma checking for a submission, but Yokel shakes his head in refusal. Blunt releases the hold, and now a STOMP to the chest for good measure. Blunt now
CHOKING The Local Yokel! Referee James Roma admonishing him for it and now he's putting a count on him. Blunt releases at four! Blunt over to the other side and HE NAILS THE UNSUSPECTING SLACK-JAWED YOKEL WITH
A DROPKICK SENDING HIM FLYING OFF THE APRON!!! JM: What I tell you kid? You can't mess wit da Tactics! AG: Blunt now making the tag into the big man, LSD and LSD is the largest man in the tag team division! The big man grabbing The Local
Yokel and he pulls him off the mat! Irish Whip sends Yokel to the ropes and OH MY!!!! What a brutal powerful clothesline by LSD almost decapitating Yokel with that move!
JM: PROTECT YA NECK! AG: LSD tagging in Phil Blunt once again and here we go! LSD putting
Yokel on his knee for the cross knee backbreaker and Phil Blunt climbing up to the second ropes! Blunt leaping off the second ropes and HE CONNECTS with the elbow drop! Blunt with the cover...
One.... Two.... And he pulls him up before the three count! [The crowd boos at this as Phil Blunt looks out at them with a cocky smile on his face. He makes a "smoking gesture" at the crowd, then slaps on a chinlock on Yokel.]
AG: Illegal Tactics continuing this beating on The Yokel Boys, but The
Local Yokel hanging in there. He really needs to make a tag though.
JM: He really needs to go get a 40, smoke a blunt, and get the hell out of dodge before he gets hurt up real proper lot!
AG: Blunt releasing the chinlock and now he tags in LSD once again. Here comes the big man and he brings Local Yokel to his feet! A punch at the hands of Local Yokel as he tries to fight back and...
[SMACK!] AG: WOW! What a chop to the chest by LSD! LSD now grabbing him by the neck! Oh boy, this does not look good for The Local Yokel! LSD lifts him up in the air annnnnnnnnd CHOKESLAM!!!!!! CHOKESLAM BY
LSD!!!! JM: Hell yeah kid!!!! He just got knocked da [CENSORED] out kid!!!!! AG: I think that's about it for The Local Yokel. LSD now tagging in
Phil Blunt and Blunt going up to the top ropes! LSD lifting Yokel and placing him on top of his shoulders! Blunt leaps off the top ropes and he hits him with a FLYING CLOTHESLINE!!! They call that move "High
Times!" Blunt with the cover...
One...... Two...... Three!!!!!! [DING DING DING!] AB: Here is your winner...ILLEGAL TACTICS!!!! AG: Illegal Tactics looking very impressive in their debut tonight and
I for one can't wait to see when they go one on one with Frenzy!!!
JM: Yeah, so dey can show dem what time it is punk! AG: Up next, we have the in-ring debut of "Gemini" Todd Phelps, as he takes on AWR Academy Graduate "Beautiful" Billy Long in singles competition. On our last broadcast, Devon Case executed a vicious attack on our current AWR champion, Mark McMahon! However, Todd Phelps made his entrance to Amazing Wrestling Rampage by coming to the aid of
Mark McMahon!
JM: Phelps is gonna lose! AG: Why do you say that James? JM: Well, like my boy Chris Rock says, Scorpion...you're gonna die! Capricorn...you're gonna die! Gemini...you're gonna die TWICE!!! AG: That remains to be seen! Let's go to the ring! AB: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, from Deer Park, NY, weighing in at 218 pounds...
"Beautiful" BILLY LONG!!! ["Planet Caravan" by Pantera begins to play from the radio as "Beautiful" Billy Long steps out! He holds a mirror in his right hand and he gazes at himself, completely ignoring the jeers from the fans.
Long climbs into the ring and takes the mic from Andy.] "Beautiful" BILLY LONG: [still looking at the mirror as he speaks] The bible says that we are made in the image of God. Well God must have been looking at a mirror when he created me, because I AM SO
BEAUTIFUL!!! I mean, my goodness, it must be ILLEGAL to look as good as I do! Todd Phelps better thank the good Lord that this is a wrestling match, because if this was a contest of sheer looks, I'd be armed with a planetary nuclear destructor while he would be UNARMED!
[Boos from the crowd] "Beautiful" BILLY L0NG: Now let's get this match over with as I must get my BEAUTY SLEEP! JM: What a [CENSORED]!!! AG: Watch your mouth James! AB: And his opponent, making his debut here in Amazing Wrestling
Rampage, from Dalton, GA, weighing in at an even 200 pounds...
"Gemini" TODD PHELPS!!! ["5 Minutes Alone" by Pantera begins to play as "Gemini" Todd Phelps comes out receiving a big ovation from the crowd! Phelps looks up at
Entrance Music Boy with a confused look on his face, before continuing to make his way down the aisle. He is wearing black pants with the word "Gemini" written in bold, white lettering down the right leg. He steps into the ring, glances over at Billy Long, nods his head in a gesture of approval, and then prepares himself to fight.]
AG: "Gemini" Todd Phelps with this crowd solidly behind him! I am very impressed with what this young man has had to show me thus far!
JM: Aw stop frontin! He's a HERB yo! Word! [DING DING DING!] AG: Here we go! Both of these competitors are light-heavyweights so this should be a fast paced match! They lock up! Long getting the upper hand, backing Phelps into the corner. Referee James Roma forcing a break and Long breaks cleanly...on second thought, no he does not!
Phelps with a kick to the gut of Phelps and look at him pose in the ring! What an arrogant man!
JM: Don't hate! You just mad case he be bagging shorties and you don't! AG: Phelps from behind and HE TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A ARMDRAG TAKEDOWN! Phelps with an armbar applied and Long screaming in pain! Phelps releasing the hold. Both men on their feet! Long charges in, hip-toss by Phelps! Long back on his feet. Charges in and gets hit with a hip-toss by Phelps again! Long back up! Comes in again and PHELPS
WITH A DROPKICK right to the face of the rookie! Billy Long is down!
Here we go! Phelps ALREADY going airborne! Phelps climbing up to the top ropes! High risk move right here! Phelps LEAPS off the top ropes and HE NAILS HIM WITH A FROG SPLASH!!!!! The cover...
One.... Two.... Three!!! No!!!! So close! Long getting the right shoulder up just in the nick of time! JM: See! Told you Gemini can't win! Cause it's all about the Leo's baby! AG: I take it you must be a Leo! JM: Sure ya right! AG: Anyway, Phelps in control as he brings Long to his feet. A RAKE to the eyes by Long, stunning Phelps! Long to the ropes...NO kick to the guts by Phelps! And WOW!!! Swinging Neckbreaker DRILLS Billy
Long right into the center of the ring!!!! What a brutal offensive move by Todd Phelps! Phelps with another cover...
One.... Two.... Three!!! NO!!!! Long kicks out again! Phelps bringing Long to his feet again! He grabs him by the head. Lifts him up and BRINGS HIM
DOWN THE HARD WAY WITH AN IMPLANT DDT!!!! And this crowd is on their feet!!!! They are solidly behind Todd Phelps!
JM: Boooo!!!!! He's wack yo! AG: Phelps going aerial once again! I think he's going to finish this! Phelps climbing up to the top ropes! Phelps raising his hands in the air! Phelps LEAPS off the ropes for the Shooting Star
Press...AND HE TURNS IT INTO A GUILLOTINE LEG DROP!!!!! He calls that
DOUBLE TROUBLE and I call that the end of this match!
One.... Two.... Three!!!!!!! [DING DING DING] AB: The winner of this contest..."Gemini" TODD PHELPS!!!! AB: Todd Phelps with a very impressive debut here tonight, and I can't wait to see more from this young man! What a contest!
JM: Dat was luck kid! Luck! AB: Phelps now helping up Long in an excellent display of sportsmanship! Long just shoving Phelps away, very unhappy with the assistance. Nevertheless Phelps picks up the win!
