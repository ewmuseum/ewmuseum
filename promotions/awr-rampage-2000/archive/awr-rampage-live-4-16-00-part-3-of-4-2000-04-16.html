AWR Live! 4/16/2000 (Part 3 of 4)

2000-04-16
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AG: Ladies and gentlemen, lets go down to the ring for the DEBUT of
"Disgusting" Richard Dorkus!
[DING DING DING] AB: This contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, from
Boston, Massachusetts, weighing in at 168 pounds, here is "GLASS BONES"
JONES! ["Heart of Glass" by Blondie begins to play as the extremely thin "Glass Bones" Jones makes his way out to the ring to a mixed reaction consisting solely of boos and laughter.]
AG: "Glass Bones" certainly looks like a fragile individual. He'll be facing an almost 300 pound weight disadvantage.
JM: Yo, dis boy better run for his life and hope dat Dorkus passes out. AB: And his opponent...from Parts Unknown, weighing in at 450 pounds, here is "DISGUSTING" RICHARD DORKUS!
["Food, Glorious Food" from the musical Oliver begins to play as the camera focuses on the entrance way, awaiting the arrival of Dorkus.]
AG: "Disgusting" Richard Dorkus on his way to the ring! JM: He must be stuck in a doorway someplace. AG: Dorkus yet to emerge from the back. JM: He'll be waddling or floating out from the back any decade now. AG: Did anyone see him backstage earlier? JM: He probably ain't here! How can you miss dat guy?! AG: We have no idea of the whereabouts of Richard Dorkus! [All of a sudden the attention of the fans focuses on the entrance way as a man in a chef's hat and apron reading "Sal's Catering" runs through the door frantically. He trips halfway up the aisle and begins crawling around the floor aimlessly.]
Chef: OH THE HORROR!!!!! WON'T THE LORD SPARE ME!!!! STRIKE ME DOWN NOW FOR WHAT I HAVE JUST SEEN!!!!! AG: Hey! He's from the caterer! We better send someone outside to check this out!! [The cameraman charges down the aisle, but the chef grabs his pants, trying to prevent him from going.]
Chef: No! You mustn't! Don't go!!! No! NO!!! YOU'LL NEVER MAKE IT BACK ALIVE!!!! [The cameraman breaks free of the chef's grip and continues through the locker room area and out a service entrance to the street outside.
About 50 yards down the street, parked halfway up the curb is a van labeled "Sal's Catering". The cameraman opens the back doors of the van to reveal an absolute mess within the van. Cartons are opened and scattered about. Cans of soda are slewn about the interior of the van, some of them only half finished, sticky cola pouring all over the floor of the van. Among the candy bar wrappers, the half eaten hot dogs, the bags of potato chips is "Disgusting" Richard Dorkus, laying spread eagled on his back in the center of the mess. He wears a white T-shirt with chocolate stains all over it. He is out cold, chocolate ice cream spread all over his gaping maw. A strange gurgling noise is coming from his mouth.]
AG: UGH!!!!!!!!!!! JM: YO!!!!! We are not seein' dis yo! "Disgusting" RICHARD DORKUS: ......uhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh.....mmmmmph.......*burp*..........ukhhhhhhhhhhhhh hhhhhhhhhhh...aqqq....
*burp* wohhhhhkkkkkcccccccc........ [The camera man quickly returns back inside the venue, obviously disgusted by the site. As he enters the door, the chef pops out, and shivers up against the brick wall.]
Chef: No....please...I beg you....don't leave me...... [The camera man enters the venue, slamming the door in the chef's face.] Chef: (through the door) NO!!!!!!!!!!! SPARE ME!!!!!!! FOR GOD'S SAKE!!!!!!!! [The cameraman returns inside the ring area. The fans are going off the deep end.] CROWD: DORKUS! DORKUS! DORKUS! DORKUS! DORKUS! DORKUS! AG: Apparently this one is going to be a NO-CONTEST!! Richard Dorkus can't stop eating!!! JM: Yo, dat boy makes Fat Albert look like Steve Urkel. AG: Well, let's get on with our next contest then, pitting "Corporate" Alex Zagenski against Derek Diamond! AB: Introducing first and weighing in at two hundred and twenty-eight pounds. From Washington, DC Derek DIAMOND!
[Diamond steps into the aisle way holding a CD. He hands it to Entrance Music Boy and EMB begins to play Stan Bush’s "Dare." Diamond, dressed in a pair of black tights with blood red barbed wire around the legs, black boots with Diamond in red print down the outside, a pair of blood-red wrist bands covered in black fishnet that extend to just below his elbows, and a black t-shirt without sleeves and cut from the hem to the armpit, smiles at the choice of song and continues to make his way to the ring. He slaps hands with the fans at ring side and climbs onto the ring apron. He raises his arms slowly above his head and clenches his fists. A small smile crosses his face and he climbs under the top rope and into the ring. He begins to clench his fists rapidly as he waits for Zagenski in his corner.]
AG: Derek Diamond looks ready tonight, James. This should be a good match-up. JM: Yeah, and here comes da man, da Corporate man, Zagenski!! Andy Burton: And now introducing, formerly from St. Petersburg, Russia; now residing in Brighton Beach, NY, standing at 6' 1" and weighing in at 234 lbs, he is the current AWR Regional Champion, "CORPORATE" ALEX
ZAGENSKI!!!!!! [Zagenski comes down the aisle holding his regional title belt in total silence. He looks over at the Entrance Music Boy, who has disappeared from his post.]
AG: Where’s the entrance music? JM: I think Music boy had to visit John, man. [Gallo just shakes his head.] [Zagenski continues down to the ring. He climbs in and begins to parade his belt around in front of all the fans, who berate his little tirade with a chorus of boos. Zagenski simply brushes them off as he readies for the match.]
AG: The bell rings and we are ready to go with this matchup! [Zagenski puts down his belt. He turns around and is immediately met with a dropkick from Diamond. Zagenski staggers back and recovers. He runs back at Diamond with a clothesline, but Diamond slides under
Zagenski’s legs and when he turns around, Diamond nails him with another dropkick to the chin.]
JM: Hey, that ain’t right! [Zagenski rolls out of the ring and acts as if he’s going to walk away with his title. The ref begins to count. When the ref reaches 8,
Zagenski rolls back in. He points his finger at Diamond, warning him.
Diamond simply tells him to "bring it on." Zagenski responds with a slap to Diamond’s face. He then sends Diamond to the ropes. Derek rebounds off and then makes a swinging leap onto Zagenski’s back. He wraps his legs around
Alex and pulls him over for a crucifix pinning attempt. ONE! TWO!! AG: NOOOO!! A good attempt by Diamond. What an upset that would have been! JM: Zagenski’s tough. Dat’s why he’s known as "Corporate." Dem high boys in dem offices is always tough to take down. I know dis one boy who washes windows on a skyscraper, and he don’t come down for nuttin til da job is done!
[Zagenski gets up stumbling around. Diamond quickly takes the opportunity and picks him up. Diamond turns him over and positions him.]
AG: DIAMOND DRIVER!! DIAMOND DRIVER!! ONE!! TWO!! THREE!!!!!!!!!!!! AG: YES! Derek Diamond has pulled the upset! JM: Yo! He pimped ma boy Zagenski. That was totally wack! AG: Derek Diamond is ecstatic tonight! [Diamond, soaked in sweat, climbs onto the second turnbuckle and faces a raucous crowd. He raises his arms slowly and clenches his fists and the crowd pops loudly. Diamond climbs down from the turnbuckle and begins to makes his way up the aisle, slapping hands with fans sitting at ringside]
AG: Diamond certainly has a bright future here in the AWR. With this win, you’ve got to think a title shot of some kind can’t be too far off.
