Saturday Night AWR, 3/4/00 (Part 3 of 4)

2000-03-04
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AG: Our next contest should be a good one folks. The psychotic Sleepwalker will be in action against our newest graduate from the AWR
Academy, "Knick-Knack" Paddy MacDurin!
JM: I be knick-knacking all da shorties yo! AG: That’s more information then I needed to know. Let’s go to the rope-less ring where our ring announcer, Andy Burton is standing inside ready to introduce the competitors!
[The camera turns over to the ring without ropes as Andy Burton stands inside. He holds the mic up and begins.]
ANDY BURTON: Our next contest is scheduled for one fall with a 10 minute time limit. Introducing first, from Boston, Massachusetts, weighing in at 229lbs..."Knick-Knack" PADDY MACDURIN!
["Need To" by Korn begins to play as "Knick-Knack" Paddy MacDurin comes running down the aisle receiving almost no reaction from the crowd. He jumps up into the ring and begins to jump up and down excitedly.]
AG: "Knick-Knack" seems very excited about his debut match tonight! JM: For now. But when Sleepwalker got him cryin like a little bee- yatch, den we’ll see if he be jumping!
AB: And his opponent, from Parts Unknown, weighing in at 255lbs...SLEEPWALKER! ["Clown" by Korn begins to play as the crowd boos the arrival of Sleepwalker. Sleepwalker steps out and heads angrily towards the direction of Entrance Music Boy. He grabs Entrance Music boy by the collar and screams at him, apparently upset by the choice of music by
Entrance Music Boy. Sleepwalker then heads back towards the ring, climbs up and takes off his mask. His eyes are half closed and he seems to be in a trance-like state.]
AG: Sleepwalker taking exception to the music by Entrance Music Boy! JM: I can’t believe dat kid forgot my Cd yo! Whats wrong wit dat kid yo? [DING DING DING!] AG: The bell has sounded and here we go! Sleepwalker and "Knick-
Knack" Paddy MacDurin stepping into the center of the ringless ring.
They lock up...no! Sleepwalker ramming his elbow right in the head of
MacDurin! And he’s just pounding him in the head with that elbow and
MacDurin covering up, trying to protect himself from the blows!
Referee James Roma trying to separate Sleepwalker and Sleepwalker breaks cleanly! Sleepwalker stepping to the middle of the ring. He charges towards MacDurin and he DRILLS him with a driving boot to the chest, knocking MacDurin to the mat!
JM: He got PIMPED! AG: Sleepwalker going over to MacDurin and he pulls him to his feet...ELBOW to the ribs by MacDurin! And again! And another one!
And Sleepwalker is just laughing! MacDurin with a HUGE haymaker, and it’s not even phasing Sleepwalker! He’s just laughing! This guy is sick! And Sleepwalker responds with a knee to the hip of MacDurin!
MacDurin, clutching his hip in pain, and Sleepwalker responds with another knee to the hip! And a third one! Sleepwalker steps back and
OH MY GOODNESS!!! BRUTAL roundhouse superkick right into the head of MacDurin and he goes flying out of the ring to the outside! HERE COMES
SLEEPWALKER AND SPINGBOARD SOMERSAULT PLANCHA by Sleepwalker! Wow!
What a move by Sleepwalker! JM: Damn yo! He put da hurt on him! AG: Both men down on the ring floor, and Sleepwalker clutching his knee! I think he may have hurt his knee in the process of executing that move! Sleepwalker headed over to a nearby chair and I think he’s going to use that chair against MacDurin. What is he doing???
Sleepwalker is BANGING his knee against the chair repeatedly! JM: Yo, he be bugging! AG: Sleepwalker banging his knee against the chair, and apparently it worked! Sleepwalker no longer limping! Sleepwalker now grabbing
MacDurin and he throws him into the ring! Sleepwalker climbing back into the ring, and here we go! He hoists MacDurin up and BRINGS HIM
DOWN HARD WITH A PILEDRIVER!!! And Sleepwalker is laughing! What a wacko!
[Loud cheers as "Hardcore Hero" Tommy Payne comes running down the aisle. He is armed with a metal chair and he is headed towards the ring where Sleepwalker sits in the center laughing repeatedly]
JM: YEAH BOY!!!! CHECK IT OUT!!!! AG: Here comes "Hardcore Hero" Tommy Payne and HE’S GOT A CHAIR! Payne in the ring and referee James Roma telling him not to do it and
OH MY!!!!! Payne just PLASTERS Sleepwalker with a wicked chair shot right to the forehead! Sleepwalker is down and referee James Roma signaling to the timekeeper to end this match! Sleepwalker’s going to win this one by DQ folks!
JM: Dag yo, my boy Payne don’t play! AG: Tommy Payne grabbing Sleepwalker and he TOSSES him out of the ring into that rope separating the fans from the action! Payne going back to the outside and Sleepwalker is on his feet. Payne going after him and Sleepwalker headed towards the nearby table! Sleepwalker now lying on the table! Why would he do that?
JM: I don’t know yo! He crazy!!! AG: Payne with some more shots to the head of Sleepwalker with his fist and now he climbs up to the ring! Payne now charging towards
Sleepwalker and SUICIDE DIVE INTO THE TABLE!!!! SUCIDE DIVE INTO THE
TABLE!!!!! Payne just dived right into Sleepwalker breaking that table in half!
[Loud cheers from the fans as both men lay motionlessly outside of the ring.] AG: And for the second straight week, we need medical attention for both Sleepwalker and Tommy Payne! If these guys aren’t careful, they are going to KILL each other! Nevertheless, lets get the official result from Andy Burton!
AB: The winner of this match, as a result of a disqualification...SLEEPWALKER!!! AG: Sleepwalker getting the DQ win here tonight, and here comes the
EM’s to help out Sleepwalker and Tommy Payne! The feud is really heating up between these two competitors!
AG: Fans, our next contest will pit the newly crowned AWR Champion,
Mark McMahon, against the fearsome and fearless Crow! The Crow one of the most dangerous and unpredictable superstars here in the AWR, let's go to these pre-recorded comments by the Crow!
[Cut to a scene backstage where the Crow stands in front of a row of lockers.] THE CROW (TC): [monotone] Finally the Corw will see action in the AWR, hahahahahahaha. The Crow has now arrived from the depths of despair and eternal hell and burning fire to step back into the ring. Mark
McMahon, you are a fool, hahahaha. You have chosen to do evil against the Crow by signing your name on the contract, hahahahahaha. And now I am going to win your AWR World Title around my waist. I dare you to put your title up for grabs. I am not afraid to go into battle against you McMahon. I challenge you to a hell in the cel match, hahahahahaha. Do you accept or yield? The challenge has been laid out to all comers. McMahon you challenge is pure pathetic. Now I will have to destroy you and pull your soul down into the bowels of darkness. LONG LIVE THE CROW, HAHAHAHAHAHAHA!
[Cut back to the broadcast position.] JM: [sips his 40] Yo, son...dat foo' is MADD funny, yo! AG: Well, we're about to have this contest between McMahon and the
Crow, but keep in mind that this is a non-title match!
JM: Dat punk McMahon is AFRAID of da Crow, dat why. AG: I seriously doubt that, but nonetheless this should be quite a contest. [DING DING DING] AB: Ladies and gentlemen, this is a non-title match, scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Parts Unknown, weighing in at 297 pounds, here is...THE CROW!
[The fans burst into cheers with the biggest applause of the night. "Predictable" by Korn begins to play as the Crow stalks down the aisle, his face expressionless.]
AG: The Crow certainly one of the fan favorites in the AWR. JM: Damn straight, yo! AB: And his opponent, from Providence, Rhode Island, weighing in at 256 pounds, here is the AWR CHAMPION...MARK MCMAHON!
["Daddy" by Korn begins to play as Mark McMahon comes down the aisle to as big an ovation as the Crow. McMahon walks around the ringside area tagging hands and greeting the fans. He raises the AWR Title above his head and rolls into the ring, ready for action against the Crow.]
[DING DING DING] AG: Mark McMahon and the Crow circling the ring, ready to lock up. Collar and elbow tie-up, and the Crow pushes McMahon all the way back against the ringpost. No ropes and no turnbuckles for either competitor to use. Referee Ramon De La Cerveza breaking up the action, and both competitors break clean. McMahon stepping out of the corner, and The Crow with a big roundhouse, blocked by McMahon! McMahon with a boot to the stomach, caught by The Crow, but McMahon counters with an enzuigiri! However, I think McMahon caught the Crow's shoulder and not the side of his head, as the Crow is still standing and now stomping on the fallen champion.
JM: Yo, he's a chump! And a paper champion. AG: The Crow in control, and he pulls McMahon to his feet. The Crow giving the sign for the chokeslam, and these fans want it! The Crow setting up McMahon, but he takes to long, and McMahon with a series of right hands to break the hold. McMahon now scoops up the Crow, and plants him with a powerslam! McMahon drops a leg, but now the Crow getting right back to his feet. McMahon doubles the Crow over with a knee to the midsection, and McMahon hoists up the Crow for a piledriver! He nailed it, and McMahon going down to the outside. Oh no! He's got a steel chair! What is McMahon doing?!
JM: See, I told you dat boy was wack! AG: Wait a minute! McMahon setting that chair up against the ringpost. He's gonna use this as a step to get on top of the ringpost for the flying headbutt! McMahon perched on top! The Crow laid out in the middle of the ring, and WHAT IS THIS?!?! OH NO! DEVON CASE FROM
BEHIND WITH A CHAIR!!! JM: AW SNAP!! [DING DING DING] AG: What is Devon Case's problem?!?! Case pounding away on McMahon in the middle of the ring! McMahon will win this one by DQ, but this is an outrage!
JM: [sips his 40] Dis is phat, yo! Beat dat cat down! AG: Case pulls McMahon up. He's got him up in the torture rack position! Case holds McMahon above an open steel chair! OH NO!!!
HE'S NOT.....BURNINNNNNNNNNNNGGGGGG HAMMERRRRRRRRRRR!!!! BURNING
HAMMER ON THE STEEL CHAIR!!! McMahon is HURT!!
JM: YEAH!! YEAH MUTHA-F#@KA!!! BEAT DAT CRACKA DOWN!! AG: Case laying that chair out on the mat, he pulls McMahon to his feet, and PLANTS MCMAHON ON THE CHAIR WITH THE STRIP!!! Devon Case, executing his finisher on McMahon! McMahon is out cold! Case says he's gonna do it again, this time to the concrete floor! This is the way McMahon broke his neck years ago!
JM: Yeah, son! McMahon gonna do da stretcher job! AG: Case setting him up!! Wait a minute!! WHO'S THAT FROM BEHIND!!! THAT'S TODD PHELPS!!! "GEMINI" TODD PHELPS HAS COME TO THE AID OF MARK
MCMAHON!!! Phelps pounding away on Case! Phelps drives Case to the mat with running bulldog! Case rolls out of the ring, and heads for safety in the locker room!
JM: Awwwww, man.... AG: Phelps now pulling McMahon up. "Gemini" Todd Phelps has come to the aid of Mark McMahon here tonight, and he's helping McMahon to the back!
