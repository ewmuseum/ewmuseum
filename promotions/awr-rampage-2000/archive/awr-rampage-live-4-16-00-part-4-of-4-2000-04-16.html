AWR Live! 4/16/2000 (Part 4 of 4)

2000-04-16
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AG: Fans, it's about time for our MAIN EVENT, which will feature AWR
Champion Mark McMahon defending his strap against the unpredictable
Sleepwalker! Last week Sleepwalker defeated "Hardcore Hero" Tommy
Payne in a grueling contest to earn this match. What kind of condition is Sleepwalker in coming out of that battle? Mark McMahon is well rested since defeating Devon Case in a defense just weeks ago.
Sleepwalker is gonna have to be careful with how he handles himself in this match, in order to prevent himself from getting disqualified.
Mark McMahon will have to be at the top of his game and be ready for whatever Sleepwalker throws at him if he wants to leave tonight with the belt intact!
JM: Yo, enough wit da analysis and let these suckas crack each otha's headz open! AG: With that, let's go to the ring! [DING DING DING] AB: Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is scheduled for one-fall, and it is for the AWR CHAMPIONSHIP! Introducing first, the challenger!
From the "Land Of Nightmares", weighing 255 pounds, here is...SLEEPWALKER! ["Come And Die" by Therapy?/FATAL begins to play. The fans boo as
Sleepwalker walks out from the back. He is dressed in his standard ring attire of his gas mask, a black tanktop, black baggy cargo pants, and black boots. He walks slowly to the ring and stops to look at the same mysterious girl from the last card. This time, she flashes
Sleepwalker a huge smile as he moves a bit closer to her. She takes his gas mask off and kisses him on the forehead as Sleepwalker continues to stare as if he doesn't know what to do next. Confused, he slides into the ring, and gets back into character, looking menacing as usual.]
JM: Yo, dat's the last cat I wanna be seein' in a dark alley. AG: Well at least Entrance Music Boy has finally gotten the theme songs done correctly, and we're proud to say that next time, we'll be speaking with him here live during some of the matches.
JM: Yeah, dat kid ain't bad. AB: AND HIS OPPONENT...[the fans burst into cheers]...hailing from
Providence, Rhode Island, he weighs in at 251 pounds, here is the AWR
CHAMPION, MARK MCMAHON! ["Rock You Like A Hurricane" by the Scorpions begins to play as Mark
McMahon walks out with the AWR title belt around his waist. He takes his time to tag the hands of the fans who are sitting along the aisle, reaching over the guard ropes. McMahon takes a quick trek around one half of the ring, high-fiving several fans. McMahon ascends the ringsteps and steps into the ring.]
AG: WAIT A MINUTE!!! SLEEPWALKER ATTACKING MCMAHON BEFORE THE BELL!! Dropkick to the knee! Mark McMahon goes down quickly, and Sleepwalker is all over the AWR Champion!
[DING DING DING] JM: Yo, he mugged him, kid! AG: McMahon has yet to remove his leather jacket or the AWR title! Sleepwalker dragging McMahon into the center of the ring. Sleepwalker applies a Fujiwara leglock! I certainly did not expect to see this type of strategy from Sleepwalker!
JM: He be smarter than he looks, yo. AG: McMahon struggling to break free, but he has nowhere to go. Sleepwalker yanking away on that leg! He obviously did his homework, and came into this match with a pre-determined strategy! Sleepwalker trying to isolate that bum knee of McMahon, perhaps building up for a submission. Sleepwalker now relequishes the hold and snaps McMahon's leg backwards!
JM: Sleepwalker gonna tear dis sucka apart! AG: Sleepwalker now ripping that protective kneepad off Mark McMahon, and he rips it in two and throws the pieces to the crowd! McMahon over to the ropes, he removes the AWR title. Referee James Roma taking that belt to the timekeeper's table, and now Sleepwalker with a knee to the back as McMahon tries to remove his jacket. Sleepwalker shoves McMahon down on his face and he's pulling the jacket off! OH MY! Sleepwalker rips the jacket away, and he's using it to choke McMahon!! Referee
James Roma lays down the count, and that should be a disqualification right there!
JM: Sometimes I think these referees are sadistic or somethin', know what I'm sayin? AG: Nonetheless, Sleepwalker tosses the ripped leather jacket to the side, as McMahon again crawls to the ropes, this time clutching his throat. Sleepwalker now places a knee to the back, forcing McMahon's throat against the ropes. McMahon gagging for air, as the referee again lays down a count. Sleepwalker breaks the hold before 5, and he's staring right down into referee James Roma's eyes, backing him up into the middle of the ring.
JM: Yeah, dat's right. Beat dat punk down! AG: Sleepwalker turning back to McMahon, but he catches a right hand to the midsection. Sleepwalker doubled over, and McMahon fighting back to his feet! European uppercut sends Sleepwalker reeling backwards!
McMahon with a series of knife-edge chops, and Sleepwalker up against the ropes. Irish whip and McMahon takes Sleepwalker down with a clothesline! Listen to this crowd! Sleepwalker springs back to his feet and is met with another clothesline! Again Sleepwalker back up and down again with a clothesline! McMahon backs up waiting for
Sleepwalker to get up again. Sleepwalker turns around, and McMahon with another clothesline to send Sleepwalker careening to the outside of the ring!
JM: He can't be doin' dat to my man! AG: Sleepwalker seems to be frustrated, and he slams his hands against the ring apron. McMahon motioning for Sleepwalker to get back into the ring. Sleepwalker pulls himself up on the apron and enters the ring only to be met with a kick to the midsection. McMahon pulls
Sleepwalker inside and hits a front neckbreaker! Well executed maneuver! McMahon pulls his opponent back up and hits a savate kick to the chest, and a dropkick to the face! Sleepwalker goes down!
JM: Yeah, but he ain't out! AG: McMahon not letting up, he pulls Sleepwalker to his feet. Another kick to the midsection, and McMahon plants Sleepwalker with a Doctor
Bomb! Cover by McMahon! ONE!
TWO! THR..NO! Kickout by Sleepwalker! It'll take more than that to put this guy away! JM: Hells yeah. AG: McMahon now slams Sleepwalker to the canvas, right in the center of the ring! He may be going for the flying headbutt off the top!
McMahon steps to the outside! He's going up to the top turnbuckle! JM: If he hits this... AG: BUT SLEEPWALKER BACK ON HIS FEET! He rushes McMahon before he can leap! Sleepwalker topples McMahon off, and OH NO!!!! MCMAHON CATCHES
HIS KNEE IN BETWEEN THE ROPES!! HE MAY HAVE TORN SOMETHING THERE!!
Sleepwalker immediately putting pressure on that knee! The referee should stop the match!!! Referee James Roma pulling Sleepwalker away!
Sleepwalker throws a flurry of punches at the knee! McMahon screaming in absolute pain!!! JM: YEAH! Dis is what I wanted ta see! AG: McMahon has finally freed himself and lands with a thud on the mat! He's clutching that knee! Referee James Roma telling Sleepwalker to stay in his corner, as he makes his way to the other side of the ring, where McMahon is laying. This could be bad. McMahon squirming around in pain, not letting go of his knee! Referee James Roma taking a look at McMahon's condition, and now he's WAVING HIS HANDS!! JAMES ROMA IS
GOING TO STOP THE MATCH!!! [DING DING DING] AB: Ladies and gentlemen, may I have your attention please. AG: This place is silent. AB: I have just been informed by the official that the match will be stopped due to a possible serious injury, suffered by the champion.
[Fans express uniform dissatisfaction.] AB: As a result...your winner...and...NEW AWR CHAMPION...SLEEPWALKER!!! AG: Listen to the disapproval of these fans!! Sleepwalker has been awarded the AWR Title! Wait a minute, Mark McMahon pulling himself up to one leg! He's hopping around the ring, holding onto the ropes, and he's grabbed JAMES ROMA by the neck!!
JM: Yo, dat's a fine and a suspension! AG: Mark McMahon screaming at Roma, saying that he can continue! Don't do this Mark, this could be your career! Roma trying his best to convince Mark to just let it go! Mark is unrelenting! Sleepwalker holding the belt, staring at it!
JM: AWW MAN! AG: James Roma discussing something on the side with Andy Burton! AB: Ladies and gentlemen, I have been informed that this match...WILL CONTINUE!!! AG: Listen to this reaction! [DING DING DING] AG: Sleepwalker questioning the referee, as Mark McMahon hobbles to the center of the ring! Something is not right with his knee! Sleepwalker has the title ripped away from him by James Roma, and he's met with a right hand from McMahon! Mark trying to steady himself. Sleepwalker sweeps his leg out from under him! Sleepwalker now drags McMahon over to the ropes, laying that bad leg against the bottom rope. Sleepwalker stomps on the knee, McMahon screaming in pain, and Sleepwalker seems to be enjoying himself! Sleepwalker again, drives himself down on
McMahon's knee! JM: Yo, I should go over there and get me some. AG: Sleepwalker kicks McMahon, who rolls out of the ring and down to the floor below. McMahon should have let this match end! Sleepwalker waiting as McMahon uses the apron to pull himself back up. Referee
James Roma laying down the count. Sleepwalker now with a springboard somersault plancha, and he takes out McMahon!! Sleepwalker is ripping
McMahon to pieces! He seems to be going in for the kill!
JM: He betta be goin' in for da kill, son. Naked City be closin' down in an hour. AG: Sleepwalker rolls McMahon into the ring. Sleepwalker jumps in the ring and slowly stalks his prey. McMahon grabs Sleepwalker by the legs, and he takes him down to the mat. McMahon unable to compete from a standing position. McMahon firing away a series of punches. He's trying to hold Sleepwalker down.
JM: Cripples shouldn't be allowed to fight! AG: Sleepwalker getting back to his feet, McMahon holding onto his pants. Sleepwalker kicks McMahon in the knee, breaking the hold!
There is no way that Mark McMahon can win this match in his condition! Sleepwalker pulls McMahon up, and plants him with a DDT! Again, he pulls him up by the hair. McMahon hobbling on one leg. Sleepwalker picks him up....TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER!!! McMahon is out cold! What's this now. Sleepwalker to the outside. He's grabbed a steel chair! He could get himself disqualified! Referee James Roma warning Sleepwalker!
JM: Yeah, break that chair over McMahon's leg! AG: Sleepwalker now placing that chair over McMahon's body! What is he doing?! Sleepwalker going up to the top!! He's going for the
BIOHAZARD BOMB!!! This will definitely be it! We're gonna have a new champion! HERE COMES SLEEPWALKER!!! MCMAHON ROLLS OUT FROM UNDER THE
CHAIR!!! SLEEPWALKER WITH THE BIOHAZARD BOMB ON THE CHAIR!!! MCMAHON
CRAWLS OVER AND HOOKS THE LEG! ONE! TWO! THREE!!! MCMAHON WINS!!! [DING DING DING] AB: Here is your winner, and STILL AWR CHAMPION...MARK MCMAHON!!! AB: McMahon wins, and he's hobbling back to the locker room area with his title intact! Sleepwalker rolls out of the ring, looking ashamed.
He's standing in front of that girl in the front row. He's hanging his head. She's smiling at him, and kisses his mask! That's all the time we have for tonight! What is the connection between this woman and
Sleepwalker?!
