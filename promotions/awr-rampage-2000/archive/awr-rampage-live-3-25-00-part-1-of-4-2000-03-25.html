AWR Live, 3/25/00 (Part 1 of 4)

2000-03-25
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera opens, with a deluge of trumpets, focusing on a hastily-painted
'AWR' logo on grey brick, probably the back of a building. the music continues for several seconds as the camera zooms rapidly in and out, while focused on the logo, in an attempt at excitement.
After a moment, the camera pulls back and down, focusing on Danny Moore who is standing against the brick wall. he's wearing what looks like a home-made 'AWR' T-shirt and has his blonde hair tucked up under an AWR baseball cap]
Danny: Let's talk wrestling for a moment, guys! Either you're in on the secret or you aren't, but if you don't know, let me tell you about the
AWR. It's small, it's fast, it's hard to see on the radar screens, but it's there!
[grins] My name's Danny Moore. I'm a wrestler. I try my damndest to beat other men for a living. obviously I'm a professional.....so it's important to remember kids, not to try the same things that I do in the ring at home or at school...do not slap your kid brother in a figure four leglock and sure as hell don't try to piledrive him through the coffee table...
[Danny turns away, coughing for a moment, hand to his face. when he glances back at the camera, his face is serious]
Danny: Can we edit that part out? [after an off-camera reaction from the cameraman] Good. As I was saying....We all love wrestling...the athleticism, the spectacle, the competition. sure we do. you're wrestling fans. that's why you're watching this. either that or you're an insomniac who was channel surfing looking for some sort of
Television psychic show and accidentally saw this instead under paid programming.. but ANNNYYYYYYYYYYWAY. that's beside the point. the point is, if you're a WRESTLING FAN, you'll love the AWR. of course..
[shrugs] that's just my opinion. I could be wrong. you could call me biased, you could call me a shill.
but I know my wrestling. and I know the AWR deserves a chance. Danny Moore, remember the name... so come out and see us, in a TOWN NEAR YOU! [Points at the camera, grinning...several seconds go by] off camera voice: Cut..that's good. appreciated. Danny: alright! [takes his hat off, shaking out his hair.] hey, think I could hand out T-shirts in the audience or something next show?
off camera voice: We'll work on it Danny... Danny: good enough. hey, glad to be a part of it. know any good bars around here? Off camera voice: Just a minute...a hell.. I left the camera on.. [Camera swings down and around, being pointed at the parking lot... we can see a shot of someone's dirty sneakers as they examine the videocamera...] Danny:(v/o) Just edit it out man. no worries. c'mon, let's go have a beer... [Camera cuts out] [Scene opens up to the Gay & Lesbian Recreational Center live in Greenwich Village, New York City! We see a nice sized crowd of over
100 fans, mostly wearing pink GWO tee shirts. The cameras get a shot at some cheering fans as we spot none other than the newest AWR signee,
"Dangerous" Danny Moore! He is out among the crowd, dressed in a blue
AWR tee shirt, pumping the crowd up and cheering with the fans! The camera then pans over to the broadcast table where we see Anthony Gallo and James Mack.]
ANTHONY GALLO (AG): Hello everyone and welcome to another edition of
AWR Live! I'm your host, Anthony Gallo and joining me this evening is none other than James Mack!
JAMES MACK (JM): The original pimp daddy playa himself! AG: We are at the Gay & Lesbian Recreational Center and I am very happy with the turnout that we have tonight! Over 100 fans have joined us for tonight's wrestling action, and what a card we have tonight!
JM: No doubt! AG: In the Main Event, the Gay World Order takes on AWR Tag Team
Champions Members Only! JM: I didn't know you were fighting tonight Gallo! AG: Shut up! Anyway, we got a great card for you this evening, so let's kick things off with our opening contest! It is a tag team contest of sorts!
JM: Word! AG: Frenzy set to team up with the Frenzy Fanatics, in a match against
FIVE newcomers, calling themselves 49 DEGREES!
JM: Yo, its about 49 degrees outside right now! AG: Let's go to Andy Burton, who is in the ring! [DING DING DING] AB: Ladies and gentlemen, this handicap tag team contest is scheduled for ONE FALL! Introducing first, weighing in at a total combined weight of 658 pounds, here are JC, DEN, MONGO MIDGET, and FAT GUY IN A
LITTLE COAT...FRENZY, and THE FRENZY FANATICS!
["It's Raining Men" by Carol Jiani begins to play from the "Collection of Gay Classics Vol. 1--Don't Ask Don't Tell" by Various Artists CD.
Entrance Music Boy seems to be digging the tune as Frenzy and the
Frenzy Fanatics come down the aisle to the ring. Frenzy seems particularly attracted to a couple of straight girls in the crowd.]
AB: And their opponents, weighing in a total combined weight of 413 pounds, here are JONATHAN, JUSTIN, AJ, BLAKE, and BRAD...49 DEGREES!
[The 5 members of 49 Degrees come down the aisle each holding microphones. They walk up to the two girls in the front row and put the microphones to their mouths.]
49 DEGREES: [singing in unison with high pitched midget voices] **Though it seems, I may be small **My heart is big **and my love for you will never die **I must confess **That this is true **you are my girl and I love you AG: WAIT A MINUTE!! Frenzy and the Fanatics jumping 49 Degrees from behind! Thank GOD! That singing was terrible!
JM: Yo, dat like da Chipmunks. AG: Frenzy putting the boots to 49 Degrees! JM: Not dat I ever owned no Chipmunks albums when I was a kid. AG: Frenzy rolls AJ Livingston in the ring! JM: I didn't even have the CASH to buy no albums! AG: Frenzy setting up AJ for the 400 WATT DROP!! JM: I was born a poor black child. AG: AJ LIVINGSTON IS DEVASTED!! ONE! TWO! THREE!!! [DING DING DING] AB: Here are your winners, FRENZY and the FRENZY FANATICS!!! AG: What a bone-crushing defeat for 49 Degrees! And now these two girls from the front row, stepping into the ringside area to help 49
Degrees up! HEY! GET SOME SECURITY OVER THERE!
JM: Ain't got no security, son! AG: 49 Degrees being helped back by their fans! Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
