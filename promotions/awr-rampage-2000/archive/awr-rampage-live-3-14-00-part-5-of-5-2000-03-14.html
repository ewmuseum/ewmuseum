AWR Live, 3/14/00 (Part 5 of 5)

2000-03-14

AG: Case looking to take this one to the outside. Remember there are no disqualifications, and no count outs! Phelps and Virtue keeping their distance from the action. Case reaching into the crowd, picking up a chair! OH MAN!! Case CRACKS that steel chair over McMahon's head!
JM: He went down like a ton of bricks, yo! AG: Case now setting up that chair between himself and Mark McMahon. Case gets a running start, and springboards off the chair and lands on
McMahon with a devastating senton splash! Case hitting McMahon with some high-risk, high-impact moves here in the early going. And what is
Case doing now. Case going through the crowd, going for the refreshment table! Devon knocks all the food and drinks off the table and he's dragging it closer to the ring! He's got that set up now and pulls McMahon to his feet. Case may be setting up for THE STRIP!!!
He's yelling at Phelps to get ready to throw in the towel! Case lifts
McMahon! BUT MARK BLOCKS IT!!! Mark now lifting Case up!!! FRONT
LAYOUT SUPLEX THROUGH THE TABLE!!!! DEVON CASE HAS GONE THROUGH THE
TABLE!!! McMahon is pumped up! He's yelling at Virtue to throw in the towel, but Tony turns his back on the action, not ready to give in.
JM: Deez guys ain't neva gonna throw in da towel! AG: McMahon drags Case back into the ringside area, and he applies an
STF!! Nowhere for Devon Case to go! No ropes for Devon Case to grab!
Case screaming in pain, and McMahon just wrenching back even further!! Tony Virtue making his way over to the other side of the ring, hopefully just to get a closer look at the action! Todd Phelps keeping a close eye on Virtue. Referee James Roma, standing patiently in the ring, observing Case get put through what must be an absolute torture!
JM: Yo, son! Dat ain't no torture! Torture is havin' all the fine ho's in the room hang out wit some flat broke sucka!
AG: McMahon, staring down Virtue with a determined look in his eye. McMahon not about to let go of Case anytime soon! Virtue, not about to throw in the towel, and he casually walks away from the action back to the other side of the ring, where he takes a seat. McMahon, frustrated, releases the hold and jumps up on the apron! Case rolls over on his back, obviously in a great deal of pain! McMahon leaps up to the top rope! McMahon from the top, TO THE OUTSIDE!!! LEGDROP!!!
A legdrop to the throat of Devon Case! Case clutches his throat, gagging! McMahon now peels Case off the concrete floor and rolls him in the ring. Mark picks up a chair! No disqualifications! McMahon lays that chair across Devon's face! Mark going up to the top again!
OH MY GOD!! KNEEDROP OFF THE TOP!!! Mark McMahon kneedrops the chair, driving it into Devon Case's forehead!! I think Case has been busted wide open! However, Mark McMahon is clutching his knee as well! He looks like he hurt that knee as he came down on the chair!
JM: Dat wuz a stupid move, yo! AG: McMahon limping over to Case, and he pulls him off the mat. Case bleeding profusely, as McMahon sets up for a piledriver! McMahon gets
Case up! BUT HIS KNEE GIVES OUT!! McMahon's right knee gives out, and he collapses to the mat!!
JM: Yo, I think I heard somethin' pop! AG: Todd Phelps showing some concern as to whether McMahon can continue the match or not! Case slowly recovering, blood dripping down his face! Case, seeing McMahon clutching his knee, quick to take advantage! Devon Case pulls McMahon to the center of the ring, and drops a series of elbows to the inside part of that knee! Case now pulling off Mark's protective kneepad, and he's setting him up! TEXAS
CLOVERLEAF!! One of the most PAINFUL holds you can think of!! Devon
Case now starting to use submission holds! Both competitors too tired to go full speed ahead anymore! This has got to be one of the most grueling contests I've ever witnessed!
JM: True dat! AG: Look at the look on Devon's face! He is straining with everything he's got! McMahon is in a whole lot of pain right now! McMahon straining to get to the ropes!
JM: He ain't goin' nowhere son! AG: On the contrary, James, Mark McMahon inching his way to the ropes, slowly but surely! Mark McMahon almost within reach of escaping this hold! AND YES!! McMahon has the ropes! McMahon has the ropes! But
Devon Case NOT LETTING GO!!! Devon Case flat out REFUSING to break the hold! Mark McMahon screaming in AGONY! Referee James Roma trying desperately to pull Devon Case off McMahon! Case is absolutely relentless! WAIT A MINUTE! Here comes Todd Phelps in the ring!
JM: He's gonna throw in da towel!! AG: NO! Phelps clotheslines Devon Case! Case bounces up on his feet, and he's going at it with Phelps! Case with a low blow! Case now throws the towel out of Todd's hand, and he's claiming victory!
However, referee James Roma not buying it at all! Phelps takes the towel and Roma asking him to leave the ring. Case getting in Roma's face, and Mark McMahon attempting to pull himself to his feet in the corner!
JM: Yo, son, his leg is mangled! AG: Case pulls McMahon back into the center of the ring using his legs! Case quickly applies an Indian Deathlock! McMahon again in some serious pain, but he sucks it up and grabs Case by the hair! Small package by Mark McMahon, but there are no pinfalls in this one!
JM: What kind a goof is McMahon, yo! He forgot da rulez! AG: McMahon forced to break the hold, and both men up on their feet! McMahon manages to stagger and push Case into the corner. Case leaps up to the second turnbuckle and applies a front facelock to McMahon.
Case leaps off! TORNADO DDT!!! McMahon has been planted solidly in the center of the ring! Case quick to follow up, yanking McMahon to his feet. Case sets up McMahon! FACE FIRST PILEDRIVER! Case up to the ropes! CASE DISMISSED! Moonsault off the top and onto the chest of Mark McMahon! Todd Phelps should seriously consider throwing in the towel!
JM: Why don't this sucka just quit?! AG: This type of contest is so exhausting. Case now pointing at the fans, pointing at Todd Phelps. Perhaps signaling that he should throw in the towel! Case literally peels McMahon off the mat and up on his feet. Case picks up McMahon for what looks like an atomic drop...NO!
He places McMahon on the top turnbuckle, facing away from him! Case now putting McMahon on his back! OH NO!! He's going for the burning hammer! Case walks McMahon into the center of the ring! He's letting
Todd Phelps get a good look! BURNING HAMMER!!! Mark McMahon takes the full brunt of that move on his neck! Devon Case staring down Todd
Phelps, waiting for him to save McMahon! Phelps shakes his head NO!
JM: It's on now! AG: Devon Case is furious! Case pulling up McMahon again. OH NO!! NOT ANOTHER ONE!!! OHHHHH!!! BURNING HAMMER!!! McMahon appears to be out cold! You hate to see Mark McMahon get destroyed like this!
JM: Nah, dis is cool, son! Ain't nothin' nobody can do to help dis punk! AG: The Burning Hammer is one of the most punishing moves I can think of. Devon Case now applies a leg trap arm hook neck submission!! Todd
Phelps is going to HAVE TO throw in the towel! Case bearing down on
Mark McMahon! Phelps walking over closer to the action. He's unhooking the towel!!! HE'S GONNA THROW IT IN!!!
CASE RISES TO HIS FEET!!! BUT NO TOWEL WAS THROWN!!! TODD PHELPS FAKED OUT DEVON CASE!!! HE FAKED HIM OUT!! JM: AWWWWW, SNAP!! HE PIMPED HIM!!! AG: Devon Case is still unaware that he was fooled! Mark McMahon pulling himself up with the ropes! Referee James Roma now informing
Case that the match is not over! Case explodes and unloads on Referee
James Roma! Roma goes down on the canvas. Devon Case now pulls Mark
McMahon up and lays him neck first across the top rope! Devon Case to the outside, and he's ascending to the top turnbuckle. Case perhaps attempting to nail McMahon with a leg or elbowdrop! CASE PERCHED HIGH
ABOVE! BUT MCMAHON SHAKES THE ROPES, AND CASE LOSES HIS BALANCE!!
Devon Case falls on the top buckle the hard way! Mark McMahon now climbing up! SUPERPLEX by Mark McMahon!! These fans are on their feet! This is one of the most backbreaking contests I've ever seen!
JM: On the real, I ain't gonna front! Deez boyz gained my respect! AG: Mark McMahon has gained somewhat of a second wind. The blood on
Devon Case's forehead is now a crusty reminder of the earlier part of this match! McMahon pulls up Case...side Russian legsweep! McMahon now applies an octopus hold! Excruciating manuever!! Referee James
Roma just coming to on the side of the ring, but he's still dazed!
Tony Virtue showing no signs of throwing in the towel, and McMahon breaks off the hold! JM: Kid, dis is da s#!T!!! AG: McMahon pulling Case to his feet, and a rake to the eye by Case, stunning McMahon! Irish Whip now sends McMahon to the ropes, AND CASE
WITH THE BACK BODY DROP SENDS MCMAHON OVER THE TOP ROPES!!! Wow! What a move by Devon Case! And wait a minute!!! He's not done yet!!! Case off the ropes, BASEBALL SLIDE INTO PHELPS!!!!! Devon Case catching an offguard Todd Phelps right in the mouth with a beautiful baseball slide and BOTH Mark McMahon and "Gemini" Todd Phelps are down as this crowd lets Case have it!
JM: Can't sleep on Case son! He'll put da hurt on! AG: Case now going over to McMahon, who is still a little shaken up after that exchange, and he pulls him to his feet. Case rolling
McMahon into the ring and he climbs back in! Referee James Roma STILL down and Phelps up on his feet. Tony Virtue watching patiently and look at the intensity of this contest! Case now slapping on the STF on the AWR Champion Mark McMahon!!! Wow, this looks like a VERY painful maneuver by Devon Case!
JM: Better believe it son! AG: Wait a minute! Todd Phelps is on the ring apron yelling at Devon
Case for his unnecessary actions! Case sees him and now he's ORDERING
Tony Virtue to attack Todd Phelps! WAIT!!! WHAT'S THIS??? Virtue is
REFUSING!!!!! Virtue is REFUSING to attack Todd Phelps!!! Case breaks the hold and he is FURIOUS!!!! Case climbing to the outside and he's
ALL up in the face of Tony Virtue! McMahon recovering as is James
Roma! Case and Virtue arguing on the outside! McMahon climbing to the outside! Case SHOVES Virtue!!! Virtue pauses... AND HE SHOVES CASE
RIGHT INTO A FULL NELSON BY MARK MCMAHON!!!!! McMahon's got that full nelson on tight and TONY VIRTUE THROWS THE TOWEL IN!!!!!!! TONY VIRTUE
THROWS THE TOWEL IN!!!!! AND THIS MATCH IS ALL OVER!!!!! [DING DING DING!] AB: The winner of this match, and STILL the AWR Champion... MARK MCMAHON!!!!! AG: Tony Virtue now leaving and I can't believe what just transpired right here this evening! Mark McMahon gets the win over Devon Case, thanks to Tony Virtue throwing the towel in! McMahon and Phelps celebrating in the ring and Case on the outside is FURIOUS!!!! He’s throwing chairs all around the ring as the AWR Trainees attempt to keep him away from Phelps and McMahon! Devon Case has got to be the most hated man here in Amazing Wrestling Rampage! McMahon hates him!
Phelps hates him! Byron Hawke hates him! And now, his own partner
Tony Virtue hates him as well! And because of this, McMahon is STILL the AWR Champion! Ladies and Gentlemen, we are out of time! We will see you next time on AWR LIVE!!!!!
[The camera gets a shot of the celebrating Mark McMahon and "Gemini" Todd Phelps in the ring as we see the angry look on the face of Devon
Case, as the scene fades to black.]
