Potpourri of Champions, Vol. 12

2 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
----------- Fang ----------- [Black screen. It's quiet at first, but then the sound kicks in, and the passing of a few cars, the blowing of a faint breeze, a few shuffling footsteps can all be heard. A young voice, maybe ten, twelve years old.]
Kid: Hey, I know you! [The picture fades in now. In the center is the back of a large wooden bench, not much space between the slats. There's a figure sitting on it, his back likewise to the camera, but it's hunched over, head hanging low. A few stray wisps of dead-brown hair can be seen, caught in the breeze, and what looks to be a black t-shirt with writing on the back can vaguely be made out between the slots. The only other details about the figure that can be seen are the bottoms of the legs of his faded jeans, and the battered-looking black Vans on his feet.]
Kid: I DO know you! [Standing to one side a bit, staring at the figure, eyes wide in what looks to be awestruck reverence, and not a little fear, is a kid.
He's maybe thirteen, tops, and kind of scrawny. His red hair's long, and hangs in a mass of braids, each end secured with a tiny white rubberband. Dressed in baggy jeans, Nikes, and a Tony Virtue t-shirt,
"Pure Athlete" written across the surface of the skateboard tucked under one arm, it's a safe bet who this kid looks up to. The figure doesn't really appear to be acknowledging him much, but the kid seems undeterred.]
Kid: Yeah... you're that hardcore bastard! [A passerby turn to glare at the seated figure, not the kid, as if somehow the vulgarity is their fault... but it's pretty clear they really don't give a damn, if they even notice.]
Kid: Man, you're like, one of the most badassest guys in wrestling! I remember seeing you fight that guy with the stupid name, the big freak... that match kicked it so hard, dude. Even my dad liked that one, and he's always throwing out some bullshit like [deepens his voice in imitation] "these guys don't know a damn thing 'bout
'rassling! It's all just crap and weapons to them!" [The kid shrugs.] Kid: He don't know nothin', though. Hell, he thinks fat guys who tried to light burps on fire and say the word "fart" a lot are cool.
I mean, damn, I remember him telling me once how he didn't "get" that
Eddie Rue guy. I'm all like "Dad, he's just a badass mofo who's pissed off all the time, and studied martial arts. He ain't no superhero or nothing stupid like that!" and he grounds me, says I need to learn to watch my mouth and shit. What kinda crap is that, huh? I mean, I'm thinkin' he was just pissed off 'cause wrestling isn't all fat guys saying "Butt!" real loud, or fruity guys in white jumpsuits screaming "WATCH YOUR LANGUAGE!" and crap, and dorky guys with crosses on their foreheads setting themselves on fire anymore. Nobody wants to see that old junk anymore - it's boring. Hell no, man... you guys are setting each _other_ on fire now, huh?
[A wide grin at the seated man, but there's no response aside from a wettish-sounding inhale, like the figure maybe has sinus problems...
or other problems. The kid stares at him for a minute, waiting for some kind of further response, and then finally shrugs again.]
Kid: Damn, you don't talk much, do you? [A shrug, half-visible, half inferred.] Kid: I can dig it, man. I mean, hell, I seen the things you do - you ain't gotta talk much. You're psycho, man. Like totally.
[He blinks.] Kid: I mean, like, in a good way. Like, the things you do... man... I remember my mom saying how none of it was real, too. I was all like
"Daaamn, bitch, c'mon! Homeboy just pulled a piece of glass out of his skin! How's he gonna fake that, huh?" and she was pissed at me, saying I was disrespectin' her. Man... my parents are some strict mofos, I tell you what, man. Hell, they tried to tell me I couldn't even watch wrestling after that one match, where you lit the dude on fire. Said it all wasn't, like, fit for my consumption. I mean, damn, homey... that was some intense damage, ya know? Like, I talked'em out of it, but... you guys just beat the hell out of each other, man. That was so hardcore...
[The kid really seems enthused now, but all we get from the seated figure is a quiet, hollow-sounding sigh. The kid's head wobbles a bit from side to side, and heaves a bit of an exasperated sigh.]
Kid: C'mon, man, say something. I don't wanna hafta tell my friends I met you, and you never said a damn word to me. I mean, that just ain't cool, ya know? I mean, c'mon, talk about how you're all, like, a demon in human skin and shit, like you used to... I dunno... just say something, 'kay?
[The head raises up a bit, long, dead-brown hair sliding over his not terribly broad shoulders and down his back as the figure straightens.
A quiet, empty-sounding voice, barely above a whisper and rather young-sounding itself.] Man: Daemon in human skin? [The kid's eyes light up and he takes a step back, mouthing "Whoa..." silently.] Man: You know nothing of daemons, child. Nothing. [The kid blinks, and he sounds a bit unnerved as he speaks now.] Kid: Yo, man, I was just... ya know... 'cuz, like, I heard you talk about it, and... stuff...
[Very softly, almost in a monotone, the man answers.] Man: I'm not your hero, child. I'm not a hero at all. Nobody seems to understand that, though... they never seem to understand that...
I'm not a hero, or a martyr, or even a daemon... not of the flesh, at least. I'm not "the baddest ass", or a "hardcore bastard", or a
"psycho". I'm not any of these things... [A long moment, as the man grows quiet, and the kid looks both confused, and a bit frighened. Finally...]
Kid: If you're not any o'those things, then what the hell are you, man? [A pause.] Man: Just Fang... [The wrestling enigma, Fang, rises from the bench now, revealing the
"Let Us Prey" legend on his shirt.]

And you can bet your bottom dollar I’m not about to let it slip by. Ever since I was small I’ve dreamed about bein’ a pro wrestlin’ champion and Zagenski I’m comin’ after your title. I may not be worth eighty katrillion dollars like you but that just means that belt’ll shine a little brighter around my waist.
[Diamond walks from the camera and towards the commercial set. He shakes hands with the director and the shot fades to black]

Lord knows how the AWR media got *inside* the place, but they are there. Appearenlty they were tipped on the arrival of Frenzy back to Massachusetts.]
