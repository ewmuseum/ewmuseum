Saturday Night AWR, 3/4/00 (Part 1 of 4)

2000-03-04

Public Access 63 presents... SATURDAY NIGHT AWR!!! ["Shoots And Ladders" by Korn begins to play in the background. We hear the cheers of about 35 fans who are in attendance. A much better turn out than last week. The camera takes us back to the locker room where Anthony Gallo is just arriving, late.]
ANTHONY GALLO (AG): I'm late! I'm late! How's the crowd? Are we on? Is everyone here? MAN: Anthony, it's fine, everyone's here. Looks like a pretty decent crowd out there. But...there is a.....slight....problem.
AG: Problem? What kind of problem? MAN: Well, the ring setup guys forgot quite an important part...why don't you take a look for yourself, sir.
AG: Okay. [straightens up and readies himself for the impact of what is coming.] Hold it right there.
[Gallo takes a peek out through the locker room door to the ring area. He turns around slowly, his eyes bulging from their sockets, face turning bright red. He puts his hands on his head and begins pulling his hair out while frothing at the mouth.]
AG: WHERE ARE THE ROPES?!!!! WHERE THE F#@K ARE THE ROPES??!?! HOW
CAN WE HAVE ANY MATCHES IF WE DON'T HAVE ANY ROPES?!??!?! [begins pacing madly] Oh my God.....OH MY GOD!!!!! [Gallo storms out into the ring area] AAAAAAAAAAAAH!!!!!
[Gallo slips on the floor as he walks out towards the ring, inciting laughter from the crowd. He quickly gets up and stumbles to the broadcast position. The fans are ready for the action, but Gallo seems to be shutting out the rest of the world. Andy Burton stands next to
Gallo, trying to console him. Gallo lays his head down on the broadcast table, waving Andy Burton to the ring. Andy steps in the ring and puts the microphone to his mouth.]
ANDY BURTON (AB): Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Saturday Night AWR! Allow me to introduce, "THUGGISH RUGGISH" JAMES MACK! ["Clown" by Korn begins to play as James Mack comes down the aisle. James is not happy with Entrance Music Boy, who is banging his head to the music in the corner. James pulls and imaginary gat from his side and "smokes" Entrance Music Boy. James throws on the headset and sits next to Anthony Gallo, who still has his head down on the table.]
JAMES MACK (JM): Yo, what dat kid doing, yo?! Where my fly music at?! AG: Probably where the ring ropes are, James... JM: I swear, yo. If dat kid keeps messing up the music, I'm gonna have to pull my gat for real!
AG: [face down on the table] Fans, we've got a great night....yea....whoopee....we have matches galore....don't we, James.
JM: Yo, son. We went through this talk last week. You ain't gonna be playin' me out like dat!
AG: James, I'm not in the mood. JM: Have a 40, yo! [pops open a 40 and takes a sip, offering to Gallo, who downs the entire bottle.] Yo, dat some hardcore drinkin', yo!
AG: James, I really don't give a DAMN about what happens tonight, because it can't get much worse than it already is.
JM: There could be a fire, know what I'm sayin? AG: Our first contest will pit "Over-Hype" Dr. Barkley up against Canada Dry. HOPEFULLY, this week ODB will actually show up and step into the ring. We ARE on the ground floor this time.
JM: A brotha gots to make his entrance, know wha'm sa'in? Dat kid over dere best not be messin' up my theme song no more or else there's gonna be a bloodbath, real proper lot!
AG: It appears that Andy Burton is ready with the ring introductions! [DING DING DING] AB: This contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, from
Toronto, Ontario, Canada, weighing in at 246 pounds, here is...CANADA
DRY! ["Blind" by Korn begins to play as Canada Dry makes his way out to the ring sipping on a bottle of Canada Dry. He steps into the ring and grabs the mic from Andy.]
CANADA DRY: What's up, eh. Hey, Entrance Music Boy, I was supposed to come out to "Tom Sawyer" by ROOSH, the greatest Canadian band ever, eh?
Now, tell me, what's that all ABOOT? AG: Canada Dry not a happy camper to start off with. JM: Yo, what up wit dat funky accent? AB: And his opponent...from East Orange, New Jersey, weighing 165 pounds, here is..."OVER-HYPE" DR. BARKLEY!
[We hear the sound of a car roaring to a start as the dressing room doors swing wide open to reveal "Over-Hype" Dr. Barkley's bucket, a '77
Subaru. "The Breaks" by Kurtis Blow pumps from his souped up system, with the bass turned way, way up. ODB hits the gas and begins cruising down the aisle. The entrance is not wide enough for him to pull his car all the way
through, as one of the rims rips off. A little kid waiting by the door grabs it and runs off. In addition, the bumper hits the side door, and rips a chunk of the wood out of the door. At the same time the bumper drops down and begins scraping the concrete below, causing sparks to fly every which way. The fans begin pelting ODB's car with full cups of soda, popcorn, and hot dogs. A ketchup laden hot dog hits the windshield with a splat. ODB turns on his gold plated windshield wipers and spreads the ketchup all over the front windshield. ODB gets out and begins strutting into the ring, wearing his three Nautica jackets.]
AG: Quite an entrance by "Over-Hype" Dr. Barkley. I can't say that
I've ever seen anything like that before!
JM: Yo, dat the most wack, junkyard-belongin', bad-paint job, hoot ride
I ever seen, yo. AG: ODB ready for action against Canada Dry. [DING DING DING] AG: We are underway, and Canada Dry moves in for the lockup, but ODB puts his hand up in Dry's face!
ODB: TALK TO THE HAND! TALK TO THE HAND! JM: He dissed dat cat, yo! AG: Dry now swinging away at the midsection of ODB, who is being protected by the three Nautica jackets he wears! ODB with an awkward looking right hand and he applies a side headlock! Canada Dry shoves
ODB off and out of the ring! ODB lands on the concrete floor, but he appears okay, apparently helped by the jackets he has on!
JM: He's a smart brotha, just like OJ. AG: Barkley now back in the ring, and he connects with a chop! JM: Dat the ODB chop! AG: Barkley certainly has his own....style. ODB now with a kneelift to the midsection! JM: Dat the ODB kneelift, yo! AG: Barkley now applies a standing wristlock, perhaps he has a game plan to wear Canada Dry down?
JM: True dat. AG: But ODB now doing a little dance as he has that hold applied! This is ridiculous....[throws a pencil on the table]
JM: Yo, ODB is da man! I'll do the play by play! Yo, it's my man,
ODB, with the funky, standing, robotic, ill, hammerlock, yo! My homie,
Dr. Barkley wit da CLOTHESLINE! AWW SNAP! LEGDROP COMIN' STRAIGHT
FROM DA HOOD!! AG: That's enough, James! ODB in control and he lands a big splash on
Canada Dry! Canada Dry seems not to have felt any pain from that manuever. However, ODB pulling him to his feet. Yes, he's going for his finisher!
JM: AWWW SNAP!!! AG: He nailed it! It's a body slam! JM: No...it's da Over Hype Social Services Slamma Jamma! AG: ODB now stomping around the ring, doing a little dance, perhaps celebrating too early! Canada Dry coming to his feet, and he's sneaking up behind ODB! Wait a minute! Someone just threw a bottle of
7UP at ODB, but it hit Canada Dry right on the side of the head! He's out cold and ODB covers him with one finger! ONE!
TWO! THREE! [DING DING DING] AB: Here is your winner, "OVER-HYPE" DR. BARKLEY!!! AG: ODB victorious in a rather awkward way. JM: Yo, I tolds you dat ODB was all that! AG: Up next, we have our first match in the one night tag team tournament. Former AICW World Tag Team champions, Frenzy will take on the exciting luchador team from Mexico, The Luchador Express, the team consisting of El Aguila Negro and La Tortilla Del Muerte!
JM: Are they managed by Yo Quero Taco Bell? AG: Not to my knowledge they are not! Anyway, let’s take a look at some pre-recorded comments from Frenzy!
[Frenzy is pacing out back, as the Frenzy Fanatics try to calm them down.] Mongo Midget: "Calm down, guys. It's only the Luchador Express." Fat Guy in a Little Coat: "Yeah guys. These guys will probably run themselves silly. Skank."
Mongo: "Hey...what was that for?" FGIALC: "Sorry. Being a rebel now n'all has gotten me a potty mouth......Butt pirate. D'oh! There I go again!"
Mongo: "Aaaaanyways. You guys won't have a problem with these immigrants." J.C Frenzy: "Ohh...ha ha ha ha ha. You think we're nervous? Ha ha. No way dudes. We both gotta piss like a racehorse, and we got Retard Ron whose been in there for a half hour!! Man he must be droppin' a MAD
BOMB. Den: "Damn right. I knew we should've drank all that Jolt Cola before we got here!!" [Group laughter] [[Cuts back to ringside]] AG: Frenzy and the Frenzy Fanatics look ready, and so are we! Let’s go down to ringside! [[The camera gets a shot at a female fan holding up a sign that says "Frenzy Rocks!" The camera then cuts back to Andy Burton who is standing in the ring.]
AB: The following contest, scheduled for one fall with a 10 minute time limit, is round 1 of our tag team tournament. Introducing first...
["Shoots and Ladders" by Korn begins to play from the boombox] AB: From the Barrio, weighing at a total combined weight of 381 pounds, here are El Aguila Negro and La Tortilla Del Muerte, the team of...LUCHADOR EXPRESS!
[El Aguila Negro and La Tortilla Del Muerte make their way down the aisle receiving small cheers and applause from the crowd. The two luchadors climb into the ring and raise their hands in the air in victory. They then make their way to the corner and begin to warm up.]
AB: And their opponents, from Gateway City of Tyngsboro, Massachusetts, at a total combined weight of 470 pounds, here are J.C. and Den, the team of...FRENZY! [The track then switches to "Helmet In The Bush" by Korn, however, not at the beginning of the song, but midway into the song. Frenzy and The
Frenzy Fanatics make their way out receiving a nice ovation from the crowd. They strut oddly down the aisle and clap the hands of some of the fans at ringside. Fat Guy In A Little Coat dances wildly down the aisle, to the delight of the crowd! The team of Frenzy climbs into the ring and begins to warm up in their corner.]
AG: Both teams are in the ring right now, and this has the makings for a very exciting contest! Both of these teams are fast pace, and I for one are looking forward to this contest!
JM: Too bad dem midgits ain’t fighting! Dat was MADD FUNNY da way dey were getting thrown around last week!
[DING DING DING!] AG: Here we go and this match has started. It’s going to be J.C. Frenzy locking up with El Aguila Negro! They lock up! J.C. applies a headlock! El Aguila Negro trying to break free! J.C. spins him around. Hooks him by the by the arm into a backslide pinning position...ONE!!!
TWO!!! Kickout by El Aguila Negro! Both men back on their feet and El Aguila
Negro NAILS him with a perfectly executed dropkick, knocking J.C. off his feet!
JM: Dag yo, dese guys are too quick for me to keep up wit! I wonder if there are any big butt shorties up in da audience for me to bag!
AG: Negro backing up. Charges towards J.C., up in the air for the legdrop, and JC rolls out of the way causing Negro to hit the mat hard! J.C. with another cover! ONE!!!
TWO!!! THREE!!! No! Not enough. Negro able to kick out once again! Both men on their feet! Kick to the gut by J.C. Frenzy! JC spins him around!
Lifts him up in the air and DOWN with the atomic drop! El Aguila stunned! JC from behind! Wraps his leg around him and takes him down with a stretch cradle for another cover! ONE!!!
TWO!!! THREE!!! No! El Aguila Negro able to kick out again! And what an exchange thus far from these two talented young men!
[The crowd applauds the effort of both Frenzy and El Aguila Negro.] JM: Aw, dat’s wack yo! I wanna see some punk catch a big fat chair to da grill! AG: Some of us actually ENJOY wrestling James! JC makes the tag to
Den Frenzy and El Aguila Negro makes the tag to La Tortilla Del Muerte and here we go again! Both men locking up, and Del Muerte slapping on a headlock! Den Frenzy with a pushoff, sending Del Muerte to the ropes!
[Crowd: Ohhhhhhhh!!!!!] AG: Wait a minute! THERE ARE NO ROPES!!!! And Del Muerte goes crashing to the outside the hard way!
JM: Yeah BOY! Now dats what I’m talkin bout! See some punk LAYED OUT! AG: HERE COMES DEN FRENZY AND SUCIDE PLANCHA RIGHT ON TOP OF DEL MUERTE!!!! [Loud cheers and applause from the crowd!] AG: Wow! What a move by Den Frenzy! Den taking a moment to recover and now he brings La Tortilla Del Muerte back into the ring! Tags in his partner JC Frenzy and now some double-teaming action by Frenzy! El
Aguila Negro trying to come in, but James Roma cuts him off! Den picking up Del Muerte while JC climbs up the ring post! Den brings him down hard with a back-breaker and JC NAILS HIM WITH A LEG DROP FROM OFF
THE RINGPOST!!! What double-teaming by Frenzy! JC with the cover!
ONE! TWO!!! THREE!!! No!!!! Almost, but Del Muerte just barely getting the shoulder up at the last minute!
JM: Yo baby come bring dat ghetto bootie over here and sit on my lap! AG: JAMES concentrate on the match! JC tagging in Den once again and here comes Den! Den grabs Del Muerte! Lifts him up in the air and
BRINGS HIM DOWN hard with a SPINEBUSTER right into a modified half-
Boston crab! And Den tags JC in again while he has him in the crab!
James Roma checking for a submission and here comes El Aguila once again! James going over to try and get El Aguila out of the ring! JC once again climbing up to the ring post! JC off the top and he nails him with a GUILLOTINE LEG DROP!!! El Aguila and James Roma still arguing on the outside! JC lifting up Del Muerte! Here comes Den and
HE HAMMERS HIM WITH A NECKBREAKER! Wow, what an incredible maneuver! JC now makes the cover and here comes James! ONE! TWO!!! THREE!!! [DING DING DING] AB: The winners of this contest...FRENZY!!!! [James Roma lifts up the hands of Frenzy as The Frenzy Fanatics climb into the ring to celebrate! The crowd applauds the victory of Frenzy while El Aguila Negro attends to his partner.]
AG: Frenzy picking up a big win and they will advance to the finals this evening. The question is, will they face Members Only, or will they take on The Wild Dixie Boys!
