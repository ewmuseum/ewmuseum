AWR Live! 4/16/2000 (Part 1 of 4)

2000-04-16
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PUBLIC ACCESS 63 presents... ...AWR LIVE! [We open to see a shot of a locker room backstage at the Boys And Girls
Club, somewhere along the row of lockers we hear the sound of some banging against the locker. The camera pans left to reveal Anthony
Gallo slamming his head against the lockers. "Thuggish Ruggish" James
Mack enters the scene from the side and stares at the pathetic sight of the AWR promoter inflicting pain upon himself.]
JAMES MACK (JM): Yo, what da hell happened now? ANTHONY GALLO (AG): I am such a sucker...the caterer we hired to bring our refreshments has run off with our food, and our money. He's not answering his cellphone either.
JM: Yo, just tell me where he was last seen and I'll give my boys a call, know what I'm sayin'?
AG: That won't be necessary, James. JM: Nah, son. It'll be dope. We'll creep down the street real slow, do a drive by on doz mutha-f##kas! BUCK! BUCK! BUCK!!
AG: I don't want to get violent. Let's just forget it for now, and get this show over with. Maybe, just maybe, he'll show up late.
[Anthony walks out to the ring area, prompting a round of applause from the 70+ fans in attendance. The camera follows him out, and we see
Andy Burton in the ring.]
ANDY BURTON (AB): Ladies and gentlemen, here is..."THUGGISH RUGGISH" JAMES MACK! ["Return of the Mack" by Mark Morrison begins to play as James Mack struts out to ringside and takes his seat alongside Anthony Gallo.]
AG: Welcome to tonight's show, James! JM: Yo, dat kid got my music right tonight! I guess he ain't half bad! AG: I did talk to "Entrance Music Boy" before the show, and he assured me that we wouldn't have any difficulties tonight! However, one thing we're sure to have is some terrific action! Let's run down tonight's lineup! In our main event, we'll see Mark McMahon put his AWR
Championship ON THE LINE against Sleepwalker! JM: I give McMahon madd props, but Sleepwalker be too much to deal with. AG: In a non-title match, newly crowned AWR Regional Champion, "Corporate" Alex Zagenski, will face Derek Diamond! JM: Yo, my boy Alex was discussin' some phat stock tips with me backstage and he got a MAJOR ANNOUNCEMENT tonight for y'all!
AG: Hey! Why wasn't I told?!! JM: Maybe you just ain't in da know. AG: We'll certainly be awaiting that announcement, WHATEVER it may be! Don't forget about the big match pitting "Pure Athlete" Tony Virtue against "Hardcore Hero" Tommy Payne! Remember last time Tony Virtue viciously attacked Payne after his match, applying a leglock, and refusing to let go. Payne will be looking for revenge, while Tony
Virtue continues on his crusade to purify the world of wrestling! And how about Frenzy taking on the Yokels?
JM: Yo, where you find deez teams like da Yokels and da Wild Dixie
Boys? Why there always gotta be some stupid crackers on the roster?
AG: The Yokels with the perfect opportunity to prove themselves tonight against Frenzy! And don't forget that we'll see the debut of
"Disgusting" Richard Dorkus! He'll take on "Glass Bones" Jones, right here tonight! Don't forget, the AWR heavyweight champion, Mark McMahon will be here later on!
JM: Yo, is that boy still alive? AG: Yes. Yes he is. Without any further ado, lets take it to Andy for our first... [Gallo is cut off as Danny Moore sprints down the aisle, wearing an AWR baseball cap and a green apron with the letters SNAX on it. he waves to
Andy Burton as he jogs around the ring, and heads over to the announcer's table]
AG: Dan...what are you doing? DM: Well I'm not wrestling this week, so you said I could do some guest commentary..don't you remember?
JM: Hey, watch that elbow, yo! AG: I did? DM: Ah, never mind....Hi James! JM: Boy, you even stupider than Tony here.. [Andy Burton, who's been watching the commotion at the announcer's desk with amusement, rolls his eyes and begins the ring introductions]
AB: The following TAG-TEAM contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, hailing from Uncle BERNIE'S FARM at a combined weight of four-HUNDRED and forty-FIVE pounds....LOCAL and SLACK-JAWED, the YOKELS!
[A frantic clicking as Entrance Music Boy switches from CD to tape, finally popping in a hi-fi recording of 'Duelling Banjos' the fans are silent, or boo as the Yokels make their way down the aisle, clad in matching bib overalls]
FAN: YOU SUCK, YOKELS!! Local: Watch yer mout'! [The Yokels enter the ring, Slack-Jawed standing around slack-jawed while Local scratches himself and spits]
JM: Boy, those be two ugly-ass stupid cracka's.... DM: Well if they're in the AWR, they have GOT to be prime competitors! JM: They do? DM: Sure! I see you're not wearing an AWR t-shirt, James. would you like one? [Meanwhile, in the ring, Andy continues the introductions] AB: And their opponents...hailing from the GATEWAY CITY of Tyngsboro,
Massachusetts..at a combined weight of four HUNDRED and SEVENTY pounds...They are J.C. and DEN...they ARE FREEEEEEENNNNNZY!!!
DM: Alright! I love these guys! JM: Hey, watch the elbow sucka! AG: Will you two settle down? ["Cooler in my crotch" by Frenzy begins to play, followed by the building lights dimming slightly..then there is a red flickering as
Entrance Music Boy frantically waves a handful of laser pointers over the crowd, the little red dots illuminating the darkness...the lights come back on as J.C. and Den make their way down the aisle to a cheer from the crowd. J.C. has his drumsticks and a broken Tom around his neck, and
Den has his predator guitar. The two slap hands on the way to the ring, then hop into the ring, climbing the turnbuckles and celebrating with their instruments]
DM: Now THAT Is an entrance! JM: Boy, the only good thing about it is that those two boys are only
HALF as ugly as the Yokels!
AG: Frenzy are renowned competitors and ring veterans, don't forget... [J.C. and Den have descended from the turnbuckles and are taking off their instruments. Meanwhile the Yokels are sizing them up...after a moment, Local Yokel walks over to Andy Burton and reaches for the microphone]
Local: Hey, Hey, Listen here you two devil-worshippin' satanheads...you like loud noise, huh?
[J.C. and Den are staring blankly at Local, who continues talking] Local: Well me and my's brother here are gonna make you squeal like a couple o' pigs!!! AG: And oh! Here we go! Local drops the microphone, and throws a right hand at J.C. J.C. blocks it!
[DING DING DING] DM: There's the start! JM: Tony already said that, fool! AG: J.C. with a big left hand on Local! another one! Local staggering now..J.C. goes to the ropes, comes back and
DM: OH WOW THAT WAS AWESOME! JM: Would you let him call the match, yo? DM: Sorry. AG: J.C. with an elbow..spinning LOCAL around and planting him with a
ShoulderTop Michinoku driver!
DM: My BOYS! JM: He just gets dumber and dumber, don't he, Tony? AG: That could be it right there... J.C. going for a cover...ONE! TWO! TH-NO! [Slack-Jawed Yokel charges the ring, punting J.C. in the back of the head and breaking the count.]
AG: Local's partner breaks the count..and Den in now..the SPEAR! he just SPEARED the Slack-JAWED YOKEL!
DM: That's why I like these guys...there's the intensity, and they know how to work together....
JM: Can you cut his mike off, Gallo? AG: J.C. now up...he's got Local yokel by the hair..and he's tagging in Den.. DM: Den's going up top!!! AG: J.C.'s got the LOCAL yokel on his shoulders! Den off the top rope..TOP ROPE HURICARANA!
DM: Local is out of it! AG: Slack-Jawed Yokel back in....Den covering Local Yokel....J.C. intercepting Slack-Jawed..there's a boot to the gut! there's a INVERTED POWERBOMB! DM: Den with the count..... AG: One... TWO! THREE!!!!!!!!! AB: HERE ARE YOUR WINNERS.....FRENZY!!! AG: JC and Den, looking great! DM: Gotta run..they need me back at the souvenir table.... JM: Ow! watch who you're stepping on, sucka! AG: The Yokels cutting their losses and heading to the back...Frenzy celebrating in the ring..another victory for Frenzy..
JM: Big deal, Gallo..they still don't have the belts. Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
