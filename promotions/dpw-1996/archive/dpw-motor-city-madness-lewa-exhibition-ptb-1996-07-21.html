DPW FanFest — LEWA Women's Wrestling Exhibition, Russel Mar Memorial Cup (Part B)

21 July 1996 (posted 24 July 1996)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chad: Ladies and Gentlemen THIS is the second rouned of the RMMC tourney. it is scheduled for one fall, and will be held under bunkhouse rules, where the wrestlers will be allowed to bring ONE weapon to ringside. Making her way down the aisle, and led to the ring by Mr. Mi, she is one of the most mysterious women in this great sport today, CAMEO!
Mr. Mi and Cameo drag a large black bodybag behind them towards the ring, and push it under the bottom rope.
Chad: And her opponent, making her way down the aisle, she is none other than "The Dukette" Georgia Bryce. Bryce makes her way down to ringside carrying a steel chair, and steps into the ring. Mr. Mi: Ladies and Gentlemen allow me to introduce you to Cameo's weapon for the evening. Many have called this the ultimate weapon of war...and you'd be hard pressed to disagree with them. Cameo's weapon is...Mr.....JONES!
Cameo bends over the bodybag unzipping it to reveal Jones, who immediately leaps to his feet, attacking
Georgia. Chris: Can she do that??? Rip: Sadly yes... Chris: Bryce is attacking him with the chair...but he remains unphased. Kicking Bryce in the gut, Jones lifts The Duketted into the air by her throat, and slams her to the mat with a chokeslam...this is sick, and I just hope this crap ends quickly!
Rip: Cameo's on him now with a finger, and the official's making the cover. 1...2...3... Chad: Ladies and Gentlemen...your winner via pinfall. CAMEO! Rip: And now to the losers bracket we go. This match is under the same rules, so if nothing else it'll be interesting to see what happesn. Let's bring our wrestlers on out!
Chad: This match is scheduled for one fall, under bunkhouse brawl rules. She is one half of Heirs & Grace...Princess P!
Chris: Again...will Princess have the stamina to make it through this match??? That very well may be the deciding factor here. Princess to the fans cheers carries out a large table with the assistance of her tag partner Stephanie.
Chad: And her opponent, one of the top newcomers to the LEWA...she is,
Kitten X!!! Kitten X, comes out dressed in a bikini, and enters the ring. Rip: Whoa! She's literally falling over the official! And now...Ortiz is turning, and...ATTTACKING Princess P? A dropkick by Ortiz, and
Kitten X is in position for the cover! 1...2...3!
