DPW — Founding

"(Fwd) Legal Notice" — the fed's own founding/renaming announcement

23 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The CVWF has voted and the results are in. The CVWF went under after
[detail withheld]
could no longer continue simulating the cards. At the same time, the head of EWA2 dropped out because of medical problems. I reorganized the two and kept them afloat. The new league has been organized and was to have kept the CVWF name, but because of the numerous people who are in the league, the CVWF name wouldn't fit.
The other day I talked to
[detail withheld]
and he gave me the go ahead.
The
Dawg Pound of Wrestling changed it's name to ICW recently. I asked him and he said it was ok to use DPW. Thus, the Dawg Pound of Wrestling name will live on.

The CVWF will now be known as The New Dawg Pound of Wrestling Promotions
DPW.
The New DPW is only really a name change for the CVWF. While I will continue to keep the titile lineages of the old CVWf and old DPW, this league will really have nothing to do with the old one. As far as I know, most of the DPW guys are still in ICW. A couple are fhere in the EWA, but most should be in ICW.
The Dawg Pound of Wrestling's titles will be as follows.

-Dawg Pound Wrestling Heavyweight Title
-Central Valley Championship (regional to the EWA)
-Dawg Pound Wrestling Skyweight Title
-Dawg Pound Wrestling Tag Team Titles.

The name change was voted for by the majority of it's members as well as by all it's fed heads. From this point on, I ask all CVWF members to put DPW in their subject heads instead when sending their interviews. The new Dawg
Pounds
first card will be on Wednesday.

EWA's

Note that this is also being posted on RSPWF so that I can make known any confussions that might take place when we advertise the new DPW. THE DPW IS

CLOSED AND THIS TIME, SO PLEASE DON'T ASK ME TO JOIN. The DPW web site will be up in early April, and will be part of the EWA web site along with the
LEWA,
WNeWA, and FASW once the merger takes place.
Once again, the DPW will be the accumulation of the original DPW, CVWF, and
EWA2.

Thank You,
