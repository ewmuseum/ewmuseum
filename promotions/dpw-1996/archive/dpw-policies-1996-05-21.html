DPW — Policies

"DPW Policies towards some stuff"

21 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Intra-league Crossovers ___________________ A DPw wrestler who has wrestled a minimum of 3 matches in
DPW, can challenge any wrestler in the EWA or PCW to a match without leaving the DPW. I do send EWA updates and maybe I'll send PCW ones too now. With these updates, your able to see what's going on in the leagues and you can see if their is anyone you want to challenge there. A good example of this was when the Crypt Killas challenged the EWA World Tag Champs. I did the work by making sure the two teams involved got to see each other's interviews so they can build the match. Since I get EWA interviews and PCW (however I ask a PCW/DPW interview to be sent seperate from the full packets for visability.),
I'll keept track on the role-plays and make usre those who need to see them get them.
Now, it's not mandatory to do a crossover. You can if you wish. The main point of a crossover is fun and it brings in a new face to our leagues even if just for one match. Andy PCW or EWA star can challenge a DPW star. They can do this by contacting me first to clear it and bring them up to date on the wrestler that they challenged. Then, I'll put them on the mailing list and they'll role-play just like a DPW Member. Then, when it's over, I will remove them from the list if they wish. This gives them the chance to compete in DPw without having to join full-time.
As of right now, this is the only way to join DPW. DPW IS
CLOSED TO NEW MEMBERS! We have a lot of people here. Since the league started in April, out of all the wrestlers who stepped into the DPW ring, only 4 handlers left DPW. That is amazing. The support for DPW has been tremendous and I thank you all for supporting it. At this point, I think we have enough DPW members. When some leave, we'll bring in more. I don't want to overcrowed the league because it would hurt it. Wrestlers would get lost in the shuffle and tittle shots and feuds would be watered down.
DPW Angles __________ DPW Angles and stuff are a topic I've been meaning to stress. Everyoen has been sending them to me. With the changing of
ISP's, and Finals, a lot of the angles I got never saw action. That is why I'm stressing gain that all angles get sent to the card writer that's writing your match the angle is tking place in. It's the best way to see your angle played out. It's worked with those of you kind enough to use this system. The card writer has the go ahead to work the angle in and play it out. HOWEVER, IF THERE IS A PROBLEM WITH THE
ANGLE, like as if it negates the instructions I gave for that match to be written, THEN THE CARD WRITER MUST CONTACT ME BEFORE PLAYING
OUT THE ANGLE. The card writers are as follows: Wednesday Showdown -> [detail withheld] Burnin' Wylde -> [detail withheld] Saturday Night Fear City -> [detail withheld] and [detail withheld] I of course do Damage Control. [detail withheld] also handles the Hollywood Boullevard show. When sending angles for Showdown, send them both to [detail withheld] and [detail withheld] since it'll be uncertain which matches they'll write until I tell them. Interviews: _________ Once Again, I'm stressing that you all put DPW somewhere in the subject header when sending your interviews in. I thank everyone who has been doing it so far. It makes life easier for those of us in multiple leagues where they don't mass mail interviews (like BCWF which I didn't join because of that.) and league members can role-play in chaos because there is no BCC used.
I guess that's all folks,
