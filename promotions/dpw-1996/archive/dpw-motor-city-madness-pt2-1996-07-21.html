"Motor City Madness" PPV — Part 2: Tom Sawyer vs. Superstar Kent, Diamond Cutters Ball

21 July 1996 (posted 24 July 1996)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chris: "That was a wild one if I ever saw one. Well now onto our next match. This one should be a classic!"
Chad: This match is scheduled for one fall...making his way DOWN the aisle, and accompanied to the ring by Rick Jakes and the Lynchmen...he is the
Modern Day Warrior of wrestling today...TOM SAWYER!!!
Chris: Gee Rip...I wonder if you're going to be just SLIGHTLY biased in this match? Rip: Hell no, I wouldn't trust Sawyer farther than I could throw him...not that that's saying much...and he better not trust me very far either.
Chad: And his opponent, he is one of the most exciting wrestlers in this great sport today. Ladies and gentlemen, he is...SUPERSTAR KENT!
Rip: They're in the ring and here's the lockup. Whip into the ropes by
Kent,
and reversed by the Modern Day Warrior. Ducking a clothesline, and throwing a powerslam of his own Superstar Kent quickly takes the offensive.
Chris: Come on now Rip...are you REALLY going to tell me that your relationship with the lynchmen is meaningless???
Rip: Did I say that??? Hell no...I told Tommy boy I'd get him off any murder charges...and I DID. That's as far as it went. I can respect the
Lynchmen...doesn't mean I give a damn about them though.
Chris: You do understand that interference in any of these matches would mean the end of your stay in the DPW do you not...
Rip: I understand...hell do I look like a blithering idiot? Come on...with the LEWA now full-partners with the DPW, I'd be a fool to jeopardize that,
ESPECIALLY for a guy that really doesn't mean that much to me. Chris: Inside the ring we've got Kent on the offensive, coming off the ropes and catching Sawyer with a nice knee to the small fo his back. Now, grabbing him from behind lifts Tom's legs, quickly securing a boston crab.
The official bends down, and checks for submission, as Kent grabs the ropes. Rip: What the hell! Rick Jakes is climbing onto the apron now...and has something in his hand. Throwing a right cross at Kent, and Superstar's down!
The officail seems puzzled but Jakes on the ground now...and Sawyer's making the cover! 1...2...3, ladies and gentlemen we've got a winner!
Chris: Wait...Chad's up and talking to the official...he's bringing him over to the timekeepers table and they're looking at his monitor...
*DING* Chad: Ladies and gentlemen your winner as a result of a disqualification...he is the one they call Superstar Kent!
Rip: Sawyer's furious! And now he's attacking Kent, and calling the
Lynchmen
into the ring. Rick Jakes, and the One Man Warrior are holding him agains the mat for allowing Sawyer to attack Kent, slapping him across the face and he's just pummeling Superstar Kent. Coming off the ropes, and jumping into the air, springing back off the top rope, and nailing Superstar Kent with a knee to the rib cage, Tom's humilliating him!
Chris: The locker rooms are emptying now...and they're trying to pull
Sawyer
back...pulling him off...NO!
Sawyer charges in, sliding into Kent's neck foot first with a slide tackle. A small river of blood begins to trickle to the mat where the tip of
Sawyer's boot made impact.
Rip: The officials finally have Sawyer held back...but it looks like the damage has already been done...this indeed is a sad day in the life of the DPW.
Chris: Let's just hope that Kent's ok...that gash in his neck looks bad. Rip: It's a tough wound...but I've seen worse. He'll be back, and you just KNOW he'll want to get his hands on the Lynchmen! Chris: "Well now let's go down to the ring where we have Diamond al waiting with the Diamond Cutter's Ball!"
Diamond Al: "Ladies and Gentleman.... Welceome to the Diamond Cutter's Ball... I'm your host... and your ruler... Diamond Al. Some of you might remember this show as part on a different channel, but they didn't renew our pilot for the rest of the Summer Season. So now we're on this channel. And believe me, we're better off. And Why is that?
Well, my guest tonight pretty much proves that point. In DPW you have a lot of Big names. This man has been a big name in e-wrestling for quite some time. He's gotten a little lost in the DPW, but with some many greats, it's expected. But tonight, he's going to reveal to the
DPW, his pressence. From this day Foward, DPW wil never forget him.
You'l all remember his name... The Name of SIR JOE!" <music written by Beethoven plays as Sir Joe slowly walks down the aisle, refusing to slap hands and yelling at the crowd>
Diamond Al: "Welcome Joe... put her there. We've known each other for a while, and I've got to hand it to you, no one in the Wrestling world is more underated then you. Why, these peons don't see your worth to this league. You and Barron took on the Lords of the Rising sun, and the Lords had to stoop to an all-time low to win the match. It's obvious the Griffin's steel plate that he has inserted into his head was a bonified lethal weapon, but your looking past that. Isn't it so?"
Sir Joe: First of all, thank you for having me here on the Diamond
Cutter's Ball. It is truly an honor to be your guest tonight. Now, let's get back to your question. The Peons, er, Lords of the Rising
Sun aren't worthy for me to grace them with my presence. Griffin, that Son of a B*tch, did use his head in that match, and I don't mean by outsmarting us. Diamond Al, after our match with them, I seriously believe that the Griffin should be banned from professional wrestling. If you use a pair of brass knuckles in a match, you get disqualified. But, if you use a body part that has a steel plate inserted in it, it's perfectly legal. But enough about that. I am looking past our losses against the Lords of the Rising Sun. I am looking past those cheating jobbers and I'm looking right into the future. And the future is why I'm here tonight.
Diamond Al: "Now then... the reason your here at Motor City Madness is to reveal your plan... Now then, I've been trying to find out from sources what it is, but they didn't know any better. Some poeple said you were retiring, others said you were going to become a broadcaster here in DPW.. I dismissed all the rumors. I knew that the only way to find it out was to have you here tonight on our show so you could introduce it to the entire world. So... In front of Millions, what is your bombshell?"
Sir Joe: Diamond Al, a few weeks ago I started a secret project. This project had nothing to do with me retiring or becoming a broadcaster. And now, in front of you and all these people I'm going to tell all! Some time ago, there was a federation known as the
Central Valley Wrestling Federation. In fact, in case there are any new fans in the building tonight, the DPW is the new and improved
CVWF. Now, in the CVWF, there was a number of different stables. I even graced one such stable with my presence. And now, what I did as a member of a stable, I will do as the leader of a stable! Diamond
Al, I have put together a team of some of the greatest wrestlers the
DPW has to offer. I have now formed, what I call, the Destruction
Crew!
Diamond Al: "A Stable!? Well, with the Lynchmen and the Gold Corp, a stable to break them apart is a brilliant idea! Especially with you running it Joe... Now who exactly is in this stable?
Sir Joe: Well, Diamond Al, when I first thought of the idea to form a stable, the first person to jump into my mind is my good friend and tag team partner, the Baron, Peter Von Nostrom!
<German music plays as the Baron goosesteps down the aisle and steps into ring> Sir Joe: I then said to myself, "Self, we need a former DPW Heavyweight Champion with us." And then I realized something. The
DPW is home to one of the largest, if not THE largest, former
Heavyweight Champion. Then I got on the phone and called this man up. Ladies and gentlemen, here is Aegis! <"One" by Metallica plays as Aegis slowly walks down the aisle, holding up an American flag with a dollar sign on it>
Sir Joe: Now, what would a federation be without rookies? I found our next member outside a bar in Detroit, Michigan, winning a street fight against a local biker gang. Here is "Too Hot" J.T. Scars!
<"Too Hot" by Coolio plays as J.T. Scars, wearing a leather jacket and leather pants, comes down the aisle and into the ring>
Sir Joe: And now, the final member of the Destruction Crew is a former foe of mine. But the past has been behind us for some time now. Here is the original "Gangsta Power," Sabin Figaro!
<"Gangsta's Paradise" by Coolio plays as Sabin Figaro, wearing a
"Destruction Crew" T-shirt, slapping hands, walks down the aisle and into ring>
Diamond Al: "Well, I'm imporessed Sir Joe. You've alligned yourself with some of the best tallent here in DPW. Kings Men are the future of
DPW Tag Team Wrestling. Sabin Figaro is well known throughout the
Wrestling world... And Then you got AEGIS! You realize he's the former
DPW World Champion Right? Of course you do or you wouldn't have invited him to join. And look at this guy. J.T..Scars... This isn't a man I want to meet in an alley!"
Sir Joe: Diamond Al, this is possibly the greatest gathering of wrestlers since the Four Horsemen were formed! This goes to show you what a little hard work can accomplish. I spent many long and tired hours debating on which wrestlers to accept, and which wrestlers to reject. And believe me, there were a lot of rejections.
Diamond Al: "Well then.... It's obvious such a gathering of talent is going to need guidence. Someone to steer them down the road to the
DPW pinnacle. Someone who can handle your mon... I mean careers. You know, someone who's has smarts and experience. Someone who's got style and can handle even the most maniacle of wrestlers. (Diamond Al smiles) So, who do you have in mind?"
Sir Joe: Diamond Al, you are very right. We need guidance. And the one man that we believe is capable of handling us, the Destruction
Crew, is none other than Cashpot!
<Cashpot walks down the aisle and into the ring> Sir Joe: And now, Diamond Al, I'm sure we all have something to say. <Baron takes the mic> Baron: The Destruction Crew. What do I have to say about this? Adolf Hitler, one of the most powerful men in the world, attempted to control the world. Unfortunately, he failed in his attempt. But unlike Hitler, we will succeed in our attempt for complete domination! Heil Destrucion Crew!
<Aegis takes the mic> Aegis: Well it seems like the DPW is changing all the time. And now a new team has emerged! The Destruction Crew!! With Cashpot in our corner and me carrying the weight (no pun) of the team we will rule the DPW and then everything with it. I am going to be the first one to take the DPW back!! And I will do it very soon!!
<J.T. Scars takes the mic> Scars: DPW, you don't know me. I don't know you. But I can tell you this. I am the greatest thing since sliced bread! I am the greatest living creature on God's green Earth! I am also part of the greatest team in the history of the DPW! I don't know much about my colleagues, but this I do know. They are all former champions of the
CVWF and the DPW. You know what that means? It means that I'm in the presence of true greatness. And soon, I will be a share in their greatness when I secure a championship belt around my waist! You can count on that or I'm not Too Hot.
<Sabin takes the mic> Sabin: Rich and Famous on the scene! We make the challange to any
DPW Tag Teams, who thinks they can match up to the 700 pounds plus of Aegis and the Sabinator. <Aegis takes the mic> Aegis: WE ARE RICH AND FAMOUS... FIGHT US, AND YOU'LL BE POOR AND FORGOTEN! <Sir Joe takes the mic> Sir Joe: And now, if you'll excuse us, Diamond Al, we must go and prepare for our future, but before we leave, I have just one thing to say. There are a lot of wrestlers here in the DPW. And all of them, at one time or another, some sooner than others, will feel the wrath of the Destruction Crew!
<Sir Joe's theme music plays as the Destruction Crew leaves the ring
