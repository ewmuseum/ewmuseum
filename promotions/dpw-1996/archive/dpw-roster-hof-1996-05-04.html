**Boston Battalion**
-Joel "The Mouse" McGrath
-Dan Kelly
Handled by Joel McGrath
This team is known for it's accomplishements in many leagues, but most notable, their reign as WCWF World Tag Team Champions. They held the
WCWf World Tag Titles Twice int heir careers and held it for most of the leagues existence. While feuding with many teams in their careers, no feud can be quite publicized as the Boston Battalion/Made Out of
Steel Feud. That Feud started int he WCWF and has carried to the CVWF,
EWA, and will finish this weekend at the WNeWA UTag Masters Universal
Title Toruney. The Battalion also caputered the Clovis Area Titles as well as some other independent titles. The Battalion was truly THE team of the WCWF with a record of 10 wins and just 2 loses, although
Made Out of Steel has something to say to that. ; ) The team hailed from Boston and We're managed by Doug McGrath who controlled the stable known as the Firm. The Battalion's theme music was the "A-Team"
theme song. May their retirement from the e-Wrestling world be a short one.
