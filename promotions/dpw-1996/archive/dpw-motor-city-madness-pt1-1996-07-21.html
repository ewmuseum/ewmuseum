She leaps up onto the ring apron, climbs to the top rope and hits Mishakal with a flying
drop-kick. Rip: For some strange reason I've got this odd little song going on in my mind...something about being a 'real american'...
The big woman staggers back, but doesn't fall. Roaring in rage, Mishakal strikes back with a punch that could drop a horse. Neko deftly side-steps the blow and doubles Mishakal over with a kick to the stomach followed by an uppercut. The huge woman staggers back and falls through the ropes, down to the floor. Neko climbs to the top rope and hits Mishakal with a flying
cross-body block. Chris: TIIIIIMBER!!!! The two trade punches in the aisle, slowly working their way to the back. Neko steps back and grabs a chair. She sends it crashing down across Mishakal's back, denting her armor. Mishakal runs toward the back, unbuckling her heavy armor.
Rip: Whoa...plastic surgery CAN afterall do miracles! I'm willing to bet she's about to strip down to reveal herself as Demi Moore in the flesh!
Neko chases the dragonlady into the back, chair in hand. She runs down the hall and turns the corner. Suddenly, a table comes crashing down, knocking
Neko to the floor. Mishakal lifts the table off her opponent and kicks her in the stomach. Blood trickles from her mouth as Neko takes another kick.
Chris: Whoa...she's bleeding! Want mommy to come and kiss it??? Make 'ya feel better? Rip: Maybe you WERE right about that extreme thing after afterall! Chad: Your my wonderwall... Rip: Ladies and gentlemen PLEASE ignore the announcer. He has been verified as being mentally insane....

Rip: Oh JOY! Friday the 13th. Part 4,000,000,000,0000! The return of the wildebeast! She shrikes as she sends it crashing down...but Neko catches it and holds it steady! The two ladies lock gazes as they test eachother's strength.
Chris: I wonder what's going on in Neko's mind right now... Rip: I think I can, I think I can, I think I can.... (Rip stands encouraging the fans to pick up the chant in support of Neko Daisuke) Mishakal presses down, but Neko holds her ground. She presses down again, but Neko still refuses to give. Finally, the dragonlady lets go of the pipe and grabs Neko by the arms. Shrieking in rage, she sends Neko's tiny form crashing through the nearby door.
Rip: that has GOT to hurt! The two tumble out into the back parking lot. Neko rolls to her feet first. Rushing in, she kicks Mishakal's face like a football, sending her head snapping back. Mishakal flies back and rams her spine into the side of a van, creating a twisted dent. Neko swings the pipe like a baseball bat, shattering Mishakal's dragon-mask. The big woman drops to one knee.
Chris: tttttiiiiiimmmmmmbbbbbeeeeeerrrrr! And they said the titanic couldn't be sunk... Neko swings the pipe again, cracking it across Mishakal's back. Neko swings it again and again, her eyes wide, unfocused, blank.
Rip: Slowly walking down the hall...faster than a cannonball...where were you while Neko was getting high?
Mishakal begs off, crawling back as Neko begins laughing insanely. Neko steps forward, raising the pipe high above her head, but the big lady dives in, catching her in the stomach with her fist. Neko doubles over and drops to her knees, blood running faster from her mouth. The dragonlady punches
Neko mercilessly, snapping her head from side to side, finishing with a solid blow to the forehead. Neko drops onto her back, unconscious.
Rip: I've FALLEN and I can't get up! Mishakal grabs Neko by the face and hoists her high, leaving her feet dangling two feet from the ground. She begins squeezing, driving her fingers into Neko's temples. The increased pressure of Mishakal's grip causes Neko's ceramic eyepatch to shatter. Mishakal squeezes harder...harder...
Chris: I think she's trying to do Neko a favor! Rip: Hell...you obviously can't make the thing look worse! Mishakal jerks suddenly, her head tossing to the side. She jerks several more times, the sound of cracks coming from behind. She lets go of
Neko absently, then topples to the ground, laying there motionless. Standing behind her is Neko's brother, Kain, steel pipe in hand. Rip: Ok! When Neko started talking about a big brother signs of Kevin Sullivan started flying through my mind...obviously we've made SOME improvement on that package...
Kain's face is still bandaged from the damage caused weeks earlier. He kneels over his sister and shakes her lightly. Neko opens her eyes slowly, smiling as she sees her brother. Kain helps his sister up, and carries her back into the arena.
Chad: I...I think I'm gonna cry!
