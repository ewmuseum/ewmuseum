"To the People of RSPWF and some DPW stuff"

5 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I appologize for posting the EWA letter here that ripped it's fed head. I only posted it so the EWA'ers could actually get my side of the story for once since the EWa fed head never sent my side of the story. Now, it's over so we're moving on. The DPW/EWA relationship is as of non-existent through the fed head. That means no title crossovers, but that does not in any way keep any member of DPW from joining EWA or any member of the EWA from joining DPW. My quarrels were wit the fed head and not the EWA members with the exception of 2. The entire thing started when the EWA tag team of the
Dynamics started dissing on DPW. The DPW dissed back out of instinctual defense. Before you know it, every in EWA/DPW was down each others throats. I made an effort to keep the peace by asking numerous people including the EWA fed head and members of my DPW to stop attacking the leagues and concentrate on feuding with the wrestlers. My outbreak began after Rick failed to comply with my wish to stop the anti-DPw role-plays. He mailed one and put his "stamp of approval" on it. That's when the entire mess began. I'm sorry for posting on RSPWF, but it was the only way I could be sure the EWA ever heard my actual story. It's over, DPW and EWA will go and peace, and let's get on with e-wrestling.
Now then, ever since I started the Dawg Pound of Wrestling, people have asked me for the URL of the DPW Web Page. There was no DPW Web Page in the past. I never had time to do so. Also, I was on Pipeline and they had no Web Space to offer. Pipeline was bought by Mindspring the other day. Maybe the ervice will get better..... Anyways, now that I have a real ISP, I have the space to provide the DPW with a site of some sorts. The site is not up yet, but the initial logo and test design are up. There is also a Java Applett which ticks the league info. There will be great additions to the site in the next couple of weeks. To get to the site, go to my home page at
http://www.geocities.com/sunsetstrip/4803 and click on the e-wrestling option. Or go to the page directly at http://www.inch.com/~bigalan/dapound.htm Thank You and God Bless, PS. If anyone from NCW is reading this, I sent in my wrestlers by [name withheld]'s request three weeks ago. I have yet to here from NCW. I've contacted them several times. Is there any word on the status of my wrestlers? Also, to the head of NCWF, I sent you the Dragon Dancer and you said he'd be an addition to the league after the PPV, you never put me on the mailing list. If you can, I'd appreciate it.
Later, Alan C. [detail withheld] _________________________________________ http://www.geocities.com/sunsetstrip/4803 Web site will soon be updated.
