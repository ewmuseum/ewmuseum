DPW — News

"To members of the DPW"

3 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*I apologize for posting this on RSPWF. It will be the last time* Folks, it's time for a response from the "@sshole" as I am claimed by Big
Alan, yep, it's me Rick. And I have a few things to say, so listen up.
In the past, when DPW and the EWA started, Alan agreed to take over a league started _by_ the EWA. We were going to call it the EWA2, and it was to be ran by
[detail withheld] . Unfortunately, he had a medical problem at the time, and the league had to be shut down. Frantic, I had to find an answer to the situation, having over 25 members in the league
RPing, and I didn't want to let them down. I asked Alan to take it over, and he did. The DPW is formed.
Now, the agreement was, at first, to be that the DPW was to be the "minor-league" of the EWA. I found it strange that he would agree to this term, and I wasn't sure what he meant by it, but he stated that it would be a "stepping-stone for members to work their way up to the EWA.". Fine,
I said. Whatever. It didn't work out that way.
About 3 months ago, Alan and I agreed to send out each league's updates,
EWA Update and DPW Spam Jam, respectively. We did, until about 2 months ago Alan stopped. I continued to send out the Spam Jam's, and after noticing Alan's discontinued sending, I stopped as well. I didn't address the issue, mainly because it wasn't a big loss to the EWA, and they would know of the happenings in the DPW when I address it in the EWA Updates.
Also around this time, I noticed that the DPW started to form into their
"own" league, so to speak, and wanted nothing to do with the EWA. That's cool, I thought. Let them be there own, I wasn't for the minor-league thing anyway. This was right about when the "war" started.
The Dynamics, a team that started in the DPW, but came to the EWA, mistakenly mistook a DPW team, Hardcore Heaven, as Menace 2 Society, and bagged on them. _ONCE_. Heaven answered back, fairly enough, and the
Dynamics apologized later after that. Obviously, that wasn't heard, because the DPW kept a-talkin', and the Dynamics wouldn't let that go by without hearing their side of the deal. Soon afterwards, we had a few
DPW/EWA crossovers, where there was a title scandal between Spector/Stone.
There's a few more wrestlers that got involved that is irrelevant at this time. Now, the thing that pissed Alan off was understandable. It was a roleplay sent by the handler of "Golden Boy" Matt Suitz, which I endorsed at the top by stating a few "E-W-A! E-W-A!" chants to back up his claims.
Because of this, _I_ am an @sshole, mind you, and the EWA and the DPW are now at "war". Guess what, Alan?
IT'S AN ANGLE! That's right! Maybe I wasn't clear enough the first time: _IT'S AN ANGLE!_ Did you read correctly? I don't see how it could not be understood as one. I specifically wrote in an EWA Update a few weeks ago, "The EWA/DPW is strictly an angle. We are on good terms." Do you remember that?
Probably not. That's okay, I understand. But of course, we needed to throw your letter up on RSPWF, so _everyone_ having _nothing_ to do with the EWA or the DPW could know the situation. Why is that, Alan? Why is it that you always have to get tons of people involved in something, which they have nothing to do with? No matter, all of that doesn't matter at this point.
What does, is where the blame should be thrown. Now, I don't want to hear from you that _none_ of the blame is on yourself. You repeatedly, actually _more_ than me, sent rebuttals from the DPW "president", Mr.
W.C.W.F., insulting the EWA and it's wrestlers. You pulled the Dragon
Dancer not once, but _twice_, from the EWA because of the angles, and I do know that's why Alan. You pulled L.A. Style because they lost a match to the Dynamics. They roleplayed better than you, Alan, plain and simple.
IT'S NOT BECAUSE I WANTED TO RUIN YOUR TEAM! IT'S NOT BECAUSE I WANTED
DPW TO DIE! Get that through your head.
I'm asking for an apology, Alan. Yes, from you. I don't appreciate how you handled the situation, taking it on RSPWF, a field that has nothing to do with it, and insulting my league AND me with names that don't have worth of repeating.
In closing, I would like to give the proud comment of stating that I try not to put too many situations under the limelight. The EWA/DPW war was indeed just an angle, providing heat to both leagues, and the opportunity for handlers in both leagues to stick up for what they thought was right.
Contrary to what you might believe, it's _fun_ to stick up for your league in an angle. At least DD, Dynamics, Death, Suitz, Stone, and others thought so. Those that didn't, stayed out of it. Now, the WEW and the
EWA have an angle going. And, unlike the DPW, Aaron and I are on great terms, full knowing that it is an angle, and that no one is going to get hurt.
Maybe it's because we're more mature? Maybe. Maybe it's because we don't call each other names, cuss on RSPWF about others, and post responses that have nothing to do with anyone. Act like a human being, Alan? I have.
Now how about you act like an ADULT, and settle this between US. Thank you for your time. Rick
