DPW FanFest — LEWA Women's Wrestling Exhibition, Russel Mar Memorial Cup (Part C)

21 July 1996 (posted 24 July 1996)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chad: This match is for the vacant RMMC title. Making her way down the aisle, one of the hottest newcomers in the LEWA today, she is Kitten X!
Kitten X makes her way down the aisle, now thankfully in her normal wrestling attire. Chris: Wait...Domingo Ortiz is the only official officially recognized by the LEWA, and obviously he isn't in a position to handle this match... Rip: Well, I guess I could step into the ring for this one. You'll just have to handle the commentary.
Chris: Uh...ok...I guess. Chad: And her opponent, making her way down the aisle, led to the ring by the infamous Mr. Jones and Mi...CAMEO!
Chris: And now...they're starting the match and here's the lockup! Whip into the ropes, and a dropkick by Kitten X. Making the cover, 1...2...thr...NO! Grabbing Cameo by the hair, and Kitten X's up whipping her into the ropes, ducking a clothesline, andd tossing her over the top rope, Kitten steps out of the ring.
Rip: 1...2...3...4....5...6...GET IN THE RING!!! Chris: And Kitten's on the outside, whipping Cameo into the ringpost...REVERSAL! Kitten's down, and Cameo's on top of her, tossing Kitten into the ring, and Cameo's mounting the top rope. Leaping from the top, and catching her with a flying headscissors into a cradle.
Rip: 1...2...3!!! Chad: Your winner and NEW RMMC champion....CAMEO!!!! ========================================== "You smiled at me with beautiful lips Smiled at me just to kiss. I can't believe it's come to this You and me, and my fist." -(C) 1995-Mary Said =========================================== -- End --
