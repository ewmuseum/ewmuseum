DPW — Closure & revival

"The Dawg Pound of Wrestling (DPW) looking for writers"

24 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yeah, the Dawg Pound of Wrestling closed it's doors in April, after exactly 1 year of operation. Yeah, I'm the original Fed Head of the League. Put two and two together, nd yes, I'm reviving the league.
DPW has been on hiatus for a month. If it wasn't for me being away at college, I would have jumped in and taken over the DPW, and it never would have closed it's doors this April.
[detail withheld] was the DPW's last fed head, and he did a very good job of running the league. He tired to find someone to take his place, but no one wanted to, so he merged the DPW with the PCW. Well, I'm reviving the league. I've got an enormous amount of backing so far, but in order for DPW to survive, I want to surround it with the right people, just like I did last year when I started the DPW. DPW wouldn't have lasted the year if it wasn't for all the writers, and fed heads who kept it going. Even the members are to blame for it's success.
Anyway, in order to start the league up again, I need the right kind of people to help run the league. First off, I need writers. DPW will probably go with 2 cards a week at first. So, that's about 10 matches at most per card, and 5 matches per writer. So, DPW is looking for 4 writers. Experience is a plus. If your interested, send me a sample at
[detail withheld] . Everyone will be taken into consideration. We already have 2 probable writers, but the more, the merrier. This time around, the Dwg Pound of Wrestling will be a Stand Alone League. MEaning that it won't be a part of the so called "LOOP" fed (EWA, PCW, etc...) like it used to be. This will allow the DPW to find it's own place int he e-wrestling world. And don't worry, the DPW is going to last. Heck, in the 1 year it existed, I'm sure at least
20,000 under leagues were started and disbanded within a month. 50% of them were probably from AOL alone.
The DPW will follow the original title lineages, I've still yet to write up an application as I want to get the groundwork settled with the writing staff first. If you're interested in joining, let me know, and I'll send you an application when it's drawn up. This includes previous members too. The league was role-play based last year as the best role-player won, but I have looked into other options, like possibly going attribute based, but I'm still leaning towards Role-Play based because attribute based might be hard to run with different match writers.
I guess that's it. DPW is back, - [detail withheld]
