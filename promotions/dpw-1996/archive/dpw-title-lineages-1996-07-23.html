Northeastern States Heritage Tag Team Titles
_____________________________________
This title was part of the World Class Wrestling Federation, but was brought back on 6/29/96, as a part of the Dawg Pound of Wrestling. The title lineages fromt he WCWF are preserved for the belts, andthey are combined with the DPW Lineages for the title. As in the WCWF, the titles will be defended each week as would any Television Title
