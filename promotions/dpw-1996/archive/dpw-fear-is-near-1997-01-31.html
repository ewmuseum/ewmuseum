"Fear Is Near" — the fed's own magazine-format newsletter

31 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in on a montage of last week's DPW Saturday Night Fear. We see Wrath on Rio's Pit, talking. Then, Sampson on the set of Rio's Pit, in Wrath's face. Then, we see Sampson on the ground with Wrath standing overh im, arms spread. Next, Mick Morton in the ring, giving an interview. Then we see Wrath's Dominion in the ring, working over Matt Harris. Then, finally, Steve Sampson getting nailed by the official limo of Wrath's Dominion. Then, the words... /|\ /|\ |=| \ |=| \ |=| \ |=| \ |=| \ |=| \ /\ /\ |=| |\ \ |=| |\ \ /=/\ /\ /=/\ |=| |\\ \ |=| |\\ \/=/ \ /=/\ /=/ \ |=| | \\ \ |=| |// /\=\ \/=/ \/=/ / |=| | // / |=| \/ / \=\ \/ \/ / |=| |// / |=| / \=\ / |=| \/ / |=| / \=\ /\ / \=\ / |=| | \=\ /\=\ / \_\/ \_|_| \_\/ \_\/ ______________________________________________________________ | | | D A W G P O U N D O F W R E S T L I N G | |..............................................................| | F E A R I S N E A R J A N U A R Y 3 1 , 1 9 9 7 | |______________________________________________________________| ... appear on the screen. The camera opens back up on Adrian Stefani and Alex Rio sitting at a desk somewhere in PCW studios. Adrian's wearing black spandex tights, along with a black top, with long sleeves. Her blonde hair is down and she's looking magnificent. Alex is wearing a half t-shirt featuring "Wrath's Dominion" on it, along with cut off jean shorts.] Adrian Stefani: Hello everyone and welcome to the second edition of DPW Fear Is Near. I'm Adrian Stefani, along with my obsessed fan, Alex Rio. Alex Rio: I'm not obsessed with you. Stefani: Sure you aren't, we have some stuff to talk about, though. L A S T W E E K ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Stefani: Last week saw Wrath's Domnion attack the DPW's favorite hunk, Steve Sampson, for no reason at all. PLUS, they had to do it with the entire Wrath's Dominion against Steve. Then, Steven Spector came out and helped Steve out, then it went back into the parking lot... And those assholes HIT STEVE WITH A CAR! That was low, Alex. Rio: Hey, you're making it look like we're the bad guys here... Let me explain it from MY point of view, from WRATH'S DOMINION'S point of view. Okay, we're out there celebrating in the ring, just a bunch of guys having a good time. Then Steve Sampson comes out and picks a fight. I looked him right in the eyes and said "Hey man, we're not looking for trouble." Then, he starts swinging a chair around at us! Then he gets Steven Spector out there to help him! What a bully! Stefani: How come I don't remember it this way? Rio: Adrian, you weren't there, let me finish... So, we see that these guys are just trying to look tough. Us, being the mature adults that we are, decide to head to the back, because there's no point in fighting these guys. We hop into Wrath's limo and take off. I, the driver at the time, was just trying to get us out of there safely. I go around the corner, and there's Steve Sampson and Steven Spector. I slammed on the brake as soon as I saw them, they may be no good bullies, but I didn't wanna hurt 'em either. Stefani: Sure you didn't. Rio: I didn't! Okay, so I slam on the brakes and guess what? [nods] Yep, the brakes didn't work. I figure someone must've been messing around with the car while we were in the arena signing autographs for the Etheopian kids in wheelchairs and buying food and merchandise for all of them as well. So you see... We're not the horrible people that the DPW portrays us to be. Stefani: Wow... If I had the brain capacity of Rufus Mantoof, I might have believed that... I N J U R I E S ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Stefani: Now onto injuries... Matt Harris got some treatment after the attack by Wrath's Dominion last week, as did "The Sexiest Superstar Around" Steve Sampson. [smiles] Rio: Adrian... Why must you say "attack" all the time? I prefer "self defense," because that's what it was. Matt Harris is a nutcase, he should be kicked out of this league before he hurts someone. And Steve Sampson is Satan's spawn, as far as I'm concerned. They're just downright mean people. Stefani: Shut up Alex, no one believes you. Rio: I don't have to take this constant character slandering by you and the DPW, you're all out to make me look like an evil person. The truth is... I'M THE MOTHER THERESEA OF THE DPW! E D I T O R I A L ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Rio: Now... I get to do my special little editorial... This is about something I feel very strongly about... TAMPONS! Did all of you know that the first tampon wasn't even invented by a woman? It was invented by a MAN. Now, what kind of a travesty is that? How did this guy know how to provide maximum protection? Stefani: Alex... Did you actually research this stuff? Rio: Kind of, my research doesn't go beyong watching the commercials during the soaps operas though. Anyway, back to what I was saying... Let me just get these out... [Alex reaches under the desk they're at and pulls out a bunch of boxes of different kids of tampons.] Rio: Adrian... Which tampon do you prefer, and why? Stefani: That's really none of your business, Alex... Rio: Oh come on, it's for a good cause... Stefani: No. Rio: Oh, that's right... You wouldn't know about this stuff, since you're probably on the pill anyway. [Adrian slaps Alex. Alex grabs his face in agony and starts crying.] Rio: You... [sniffle] Hit me... H E L L S H O C K ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Stefani: Well, while Alex cries, I'll continue the show. In the upcoming Extreme Elimination match at DPW Broken Hearts, the DPW has signed a phenomenal athlete to compete in the match, EWA superstar, Hellshock. He's a former EWA World Champion and he should blend in nicely with the DPW wrestlers, perhaps even winning the title shot at the next DPW supercard. Rio: Wrath's... [sniffle] Gonna win... Stefani: Lets take a look at the rankings, upcoming cards, and all that good stuff.
