NEO-J Opens Its Doors!!

6 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<---------------------------------------> NEO-JUNIORS (NEO-J) Frequently Asked Questions <---------------------------------------> 1. What is the NEO-J, anyway? <> The History <> The first concept for NEO-J came as a merger between NEO and the AWE, which took place in September of 2004. Due to the concept not catching on as well as he'd hoped, the owner of NEO-J, Jacob Matthews, decided to cease trying. His sports agency, Elite Sports Management, was booming. His presence in the business was getting bigger and bigger, thanks to the signing of J*STAR Trophy winner Jaiden Andrews and Japanese hardcore sensation Takeshi Tanisaki. So why would he want to keep a failing promotion? Why would he want to keep a dead concept on life support? With all of the recent success, Matthews had the axe held high, ready to kill his creation. That is, until two men stepped in, trying to convince him otherwise. They had the know-how. They had the in-ring experience. More importantly to Matthews, they had the cash in hand. So, just before it died, a contract was signed to resurrect the fallen NEO-J. That was thanks to Justin Cage and Trance Extacy... Youth Gone Wrong. Making the switch from wrestlers to owners of a promotion, NEO-J now belongs to them. With Orlando, Florida being their home state, NEO-J will remain there... quite a ways from its "mothership," NEO. The weight limit that was instituted back from AWE will still be there as well... that will work to truly make this an all-junior fed. With the recent closure of NEO, though, NEO-J hopes to bring some former talent in as long-term and short-term contracts. <> The Concept <> NEO-J looks to bring junior heavyweights into the forefront, mixing wrestlers with lucha libre, puroresu, and traditional wrestling backgrounds. With a large budget to work with, a contract with ESPN2 locked in, along with OnDemand agreeing to televise Pay Per View Events, and a brand new, state of the art arena, NEO-J will stop at nothing to excite even the most cynical of fans. Truly, Cage and Extacy look to uphold the name of NEO-Pro. <> The Goals <> To innovate. To be the best. To uphold the name of NEO. And to carry the torch for the fallen organization. 2. Who runs this thing? FANTASY: NEO-J is run by "All The Rage" Justin Cage and "Techno Dragon" Trance Extacy, known as Youth Gone Wrong. The former FSW Tag Team Champions, KAWF Tag Team Champions, and RSPWF 2004 Tag Team of the Mid-Year now take the reigns... they hope to bring more interest to the promotion, using their wrestling connections to create the best product possible. They've already got ideas running through their heads, and are looking to make them work. REAL LIFE: In real life, NEO-J is run by [name withheld]... me. I've been in e-wrestling since 1997, and I've tried my hand at being a fedhead on more than one occasion. Though those failed, I won't allow my concept die this time around. I've handled numerous characters since I started -- most notably Setzer Van Strife and Jaiden Andrews. I'm a huge fan of junior heavys, and I think a promotion completely dedicated to that weight class would be fun. 3. How are winners and losers decided? NEO-J is going to be angle _and_ roleplay booked. What does that mean? Simple. It means that determining the outcome of a match will be by the quality of roleplays _and_ current feuds/angles that are going on. You might be an excellent roleplayer, but without a good feud, it's a waste... your character would be lost amidst the roster. The same goes for a great feud with great characters, but no roleplays... doesn't make a lot of sense, does it? I'm sure we'll get some negative comments on either side, as handlers have their own opinions. Some handlers like "angle based" booking, while others enjoy "win based" booking. For me, though, I'm right down the middle... call me "circumstance based." You might be in a match where you out-roleplay the winner, but you lose due to a feud you're in. You might also see yourself win a match where you were out-roleplayed, but the other person is in a feud. Think about this... make sure you're okay with it, too. Bitching will _not_ be tolerated. 4. What shows will air for NEO-J? <> NEO-J Skydiving TV! <> Skydiving TV! will be NEO-J's flagship show, running approximately twice a month. This is where the matches, feuds, and angles all take place. The show will take place from the NEO-J Battle Station (formerly known as the AWE-RENA), and will usually take place on the weekend. There will usually be 6-8 matches run on the show, with all of the matches summarized except for the main event... though this _might_ be changing to a fully-written out show. The most innovative thing about Skydiving TV! is the six-sided ring that came along with the old AWE. Every match will take place from this ring, with all "stipulation matches" altered to accomodate the ring -- so Cage Matches, Table Matches, etc. will be inside the ring. This new ring will give competitors more room to work with, plus two new sides to execute their maneuvers from. It will give fans and viewers alike a great view of the in-ring action. <> NEO-J Supercards <> As of right now, NEO-J plans on running a PPV every other month, in place of one of the Skydiving TV! shows. The names and dates of these events have not been scheduled as of yet, but should be in the near future as deals are finalized. Rumored ideas are a multi-promotion junior heavyweight tournament, an all-stip event, and a cage match. <> NEO-J's Shooting Gallery <> The Shooting Gallery is the roleplay show. The roleplays will go here. Need I explain more? 5. What titles are recognized in NEO-J? <> NEO-J World Junior Heavyweight Title <> This is the main title in NEO-J, what each wrestler on the NEO-J roster will be fighting for. Winning this title makes you the best in NEO-J... and perhaps in the entire sport. This title will be limited in its contenders, with only the top 5 wrestlers being able to challenge for it. The title will be defended at least once every 30 days. <> NEO-J Skydiving TV! Title This is considered the Television Title in NEO-J, also open to anyone on the NEO-J roster. This title will probably be the title that switches hands the most often, as it will be defended at _every_ NEO-J event. The champion will need to have endurance, as they'll continually be fighting against all competitors, usually more than one at a time. So where's the tag team division? Where's the trios division? On the back burner, that's where! When more interest comes to NEO-J, and when the singles roster reaches capacity, a tag team or trios division will be discussed and possibly sanctioned. In addition, another singles title is in the mix, but it all depends on NEO-J's interest. 6. Who's who in NEO-J? <> Co-Owners/Co-CEOs - Youth Gone Wrong. Yes, Justin Cage and Trance Extacy take the reigns, with neither man having any experience at running a wrestling promotion. They pooled their money together along with a 3rd, anonymous party, and together bought NEO-J from Jacob Matthews. They want to keep a strictly backstage presence as much as possible, but will make appearances rarely. They've had many ideas shot down by other feds, which they can now utilize here. <> Vice President of Talent Relations - Randall Crane. The former A1 Promotions, VWF, and EMWC champion takes on a role as executive, in addition to his recent comeback as an active wrestler. Always being business-oriented, he was one of Matthews' friends that jumped on board with AWE. Seeing his keen business sense, Cage and Extacy made the decision to keep him on board. He's in charge of signing new talent, resigning current talent, and recruiting. <> PBP Announcer - Jerry Bryson. A columnist for the wrestling website WrestlingWorld.net, Bryson has a _very_ extensive knowledge of wrestling, but up until now has only utilized it in his website columns. He'll work as play by play as well as reporting news from around NEO-J. At only 23, is one of the youngest personalities around. <> Color Commentator - Paul Clarkson. Clarkson, or "PC," as he's known, is anything but politically correct. He's always willing to speak his mind -- in fact, he insists on doing so. He doesn't pull for faces or heels, but likes certain wrestlers. Knows the sport as an analyst, but is a fan at heart and will "mark out" for moves and wrestlers. <> Ring Announcer - Michael K. Cook. Coming from the now defunct TSW and VCWA, Cook brings his trademark Bufferesque voice into NEO-J. With a cool demeanor, an extensive metrosexual wardrobe, and a craving for the after-show drink, Cook is a favorite not just with the fans, but also with the entire NEO-J roster. Is also one of NEO-J's scouts. <> Head Referee - Skip Danielson. With over a decade of referee experience, Danielson calls matches right down the middle and is an extremely lenient referee when it comes to a "blood feud." He's the guy who will answer a "FIVE MORE MINUTES!" chant, and also won't hesitate to kick people out of the ringside area if they're too "hands on." <> Secondary Referee - Meathead. Yes, that's his name. This very large, very muscular referee will also double as the head of security when not calling the match. You _don't_ want to mess with Meathead, as he'll do tenfold what you attempt to do to him. Has been known to knock out a competitor for not breaking a hold, and might do it again. <> Backstage Interviewer - Cotton Candy. You want the epitome of the perfect woman? She's it. With long blonde hair, light green eyes, and legs that go on forever, Candy is the interviewer you want to come and see. She'll flirt with the wrestlers to get the scoop, show a _lot_ of skin, and will more than likely sell a ton of merchandise to fans. <> In-Ring Interviewer - Brett Michaels. The guy from Poison? No, that's coincidence. Though he's straight forward with his questions and digging for answers, most guys don't take this man seriously simply because of his name. Think of Office Space with Michael Bolton... only inside the ring. Plus, let's be honest, you'd prefer Cotton Candy. 7. Is there anything else I need to know? Why yes, thanks for asking! There are a few rules and regulations... and here they are! - WEIGHT LIMIT: The weight limit in NEO-J is 230 pounds. Nobody over that weight will be accepted. - SWEARING: Since Skydiving TV! runs on ESPN2, please keep swearing to a minumum. If it's said on any currently televised wrestling show, it's allowed on Skydiving TV!. If it's not said, then either don't use it or censor it with a [BLEEP!]. I'm really big on realism in NEO-J... so having a character drop the "f bomb" won't live up to that. If it's on a PPV, though, I might be a bit more lenient. I reserve the right to censor swearing at anytime. - ATTACKS: Of course, NEO-J will have it's share of sneak attacks on Skydiving TV! and on Supercards. However, before doing any attacks on NEO-J staff, referees, or other NEO personalities, please contact me for permission. In addition, any attack on another wrestler in your roleplay is frowned upon as well, unless the other handler has agreed to it and has let me know. Just keep me in the loop of things, and we won't have any kind of problems. - KAYFABE: This is a _fantasy_ wrestling promotion... so _real_ wrestlers don't exist. Therefore, don't mention them or their promotions. In addition, please keep mention of other fantasy promotions to a minimum. If it's to increase heat or further an angle, fine. But please, don't list off the titles your character has won and the promotions they've wrestled in their lifetime. I don't know how many people will care about those kinds of things, anyway. - THIS IS A GAME! If you've got comments, questions, criticisms, or anything else regarding NEO-J, us, your character, or anything else... E-MAIL me about it. I'm more than willing to explain my choices in booking, top contenders, or anything else you've got on your mind. What I don't want to see is continuous whining, complaining, and bitching about how your character should be undefeated, the champion, and should have decapitated his opponent. Just keep this in mind... your character _will_ lose matches. Your character is _not_ invincible. Because you've gone 67 - 0 in the JKSBSHWF doesn't mean I have to treat you any differently than someone just starting out in this hobby. Everyone is equal. And if you're not okay with that, we don't want you to become part of NEO-J in any way. It's as simple as that... understand? - HAVE FUN! 8. I'm interested, damn it! How do I get my character in?
