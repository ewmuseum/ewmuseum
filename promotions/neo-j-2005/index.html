An Orlando junior-heavyweight promotion whose founding FAQ narrates itself as a further link in the NEO Wrestling/NEO-Pro lineage, reopened by new co-owners after a 2004 merger and change of ownership. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NEO-J's own founding FAQ, posted 6 January 2005, narrates itself as the latest link in the NEO Wrestling and NEO-Pro lineage: first conceived in September 2004 as a merger between NEO and the AWE under owner Jacob Matthews, the concept nearly died when it failed to catch on, until two new co-owners — the tag team Youth Gone Wrong, “All The Rage” Justin Cage and “Techno Dragon” Trance Extacy — bought in and relaunched it. The pair came in as former FSW Tag Team Champions, KAWF Tag Team Champions and RSPWF 2004 Tag Team of the Mid-Year.

Based in Orlando, Florida, the promotion styled itself as an all-junior-heavyweight fed, carrying forward a weight limit inherited from the AWE and a distinctive six-sided ring from the same source. Its flagship show, Skydiving TV!, was to run roughly twice a month from the promotion's own arena, the NEO-J Battle Station, with results decided by a mix of roleplay quality and ongoing feuds rather than by either alone.

No further sighting of the promotion survives past its own founding FAQ; whether Skydiving TV! ever aired isn't in the surviving record.
