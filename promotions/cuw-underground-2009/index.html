Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Controversial Underground Wrestling opened its hub-hosted site on 25 June 2009 with a story. A Denver promotion, which a retired promoter had left sitting in an armory for five years after losing a casino lawsuit, had been bought for $6.5 million by a multimillionaire called Benjamin Black, who had a ring, a facility and a broadcast kit and, as the fed's first column says with some relish, no wrestlers signed yet. A second column the same day, The Scoop, has its own analyst explain how he came to the job: fired from his last outlet for a style the bosses hated, he took a phone call from Black and signed. The splash page, which asks visitors to pick their browser, signs off with a wish for a happy bout of e-fedding.

That is nearly the whole record: the news post and its byline copy, the first Scoop, an index of the two columns, and a roster page of nine trading cards, two Superstars and seven Staff, whose names are printed on the images. No card, result, title or roleplay survives, and the pages link no forum. The two columns carry the date 25 June 2009; the column index page gives the first one a time stamp of 26 June.
