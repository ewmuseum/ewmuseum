"[GWF] Welcome to the Terrordome"

12 December 1996

The GWF's first Card "WELCOME TO THE TERRORDOME" Held in the GWF Arena. Sold Out The opener. The first match was a Women's Title tournament match. Amber Jane entered with Manager John Schisz to the arena to a rosing round of boos and more than a few objects fling at her. The crowd then welcomed her opponent, Persephone, the to Ring with a huge ovation. The ring introductions were marred by Schiz seeming to have Amber pay him some sort of homage. The match opened much as it had to, with both women going after the other in a rage of flying maneuvers. Amber got the upper-hand early on with a huge spinning kick. She maintained her attack with a number of wear-down locks, including a very well-cinched abdominal stretch. The match was all Amber up 'til the six minute mark, when Persephone managed to grab Amber's arm as she went for a clothesline. Persephone then hit the single arm DDT and then picked Amber up for her finisher Tiger Suplex, but Amber managed to break one arm free and nail Perephone with a solid elbow which sent her out of the ring. Amber then hit Persephone with a huge summersault plancha and followed up with a Cactus-like elbow from the ring apron. Then, in a huge mistake, Amber Jane body slammed Persephone onto a table and went to the top rope and came off with the Shooting Star Press, the cleanest I have ever seen. Her only mistake was forgetting that Persephone had some fight in her, as she had moved before she hit and Amber went crashing through the table. Persephone threw Amber back in the ring and hit the Tiger Suplex for the pin at 8:27. A 4* match to start things off on the right foot. The second match in the Women's title tourney featured Laura Wineinger facing off with Kathy Lynn, Amber Jane's tag partner. The two entered the ring and everybody hated both these women more than a benevolent God would allow. The two stood toe to toe untl the bell rang and the hair started to fly. Laura clocked Kathy with a pair of haymakers and sent her rolling outside, where Laura grabbed a hold of a chair and bashed Kathy into oblivion. She rolled her back in and got only a two count. Kathy then got sent to the corner and Laura tried to follow up with a clothesline. Kathy moved out of the way and Laura went flying to the outside, where Kathy hit her with a baseball slide into a headscissors. Then, Kathy hit a huge Flying Leg drop onto Laura and then set up Laura for the legsweep on the outside. Laura had presence of mind to grab the rail and Kathy went crashing onto the cement. Laura then grabbed her by the hair and gave her a DDT and threw her back in the ring. Laura then nailed a legdrop and followed up with a beautiful elbow off the top. Kathy managed to Get to her feet after kicking out for the third time at two after the elbow. Laura then went for the Chest Basher, but Kathy ducked and nailed her with a Superkick and covered her for a two count. She then took to the top and nailed a flying drop kick, but Laura landed too close to the ropes and got a foot across the bottom strand. Kathy then set up Laura for the legsweep, but Laura weaseled out and gave her the Chest Basher for the pin at 6:13. The Third match in the women's tourney featured the two most contrasting styles in the tourney's first round, Submission mistress Helen vs Lucha bad woman Nadia Williams. The match opened with Nadia entering and the music being drowned out by the chorus of boos and spitting at her manager John Schiz and her. Helen enteres the arena for her first US match and recieved only polite applause. The Match started faster then anyone would have expected, Nadia went for a frankensteiner right out of the first lock up, but Helen saw it coming and turned it into a powerbomb for a two count. Helen then grabs Nadia's arm and puts on the Fujiwara armbar. Nadia makes it to the ropes, and as Helen is releasing, Nadia hits her with a solid backhand that knocks her to the mat. Nadia then climbs them ropes and hits a Senton for a two count. She then goes for a La Majistral pinning combo, but Helen sees it coming and turns it into a reverse cradle submission lock. Twisted into unheard of positions, Nadia makes it to the ropes, and starts yelling with one of the ringside fans. She then climbs back in and is grabbed by the arm and stretched for threeminutes, until she manages to roll out of it and manages a reverse cradle. As Helen tries for a full nelson, Nadia sneeks behind and hit a back suplex which sends Helen over the ropes, landing hard on the floor. Nadia then comes off the top with a rolling senton and follows that up with a huge slap to the chest which looked too painful to discribe. Nadia then comes of the ring apron for a Hurracurana, but is caught and dropped throat first across the ring barrier. Nadia barely beats a ten count outside, but is greeted by Helen going for a cross shoulder choke on her. As she drags her into the centre of the ring, Nadia gets a kick into Helen's face and she staggers back as Nadia then puts a good dropkick into her stomach and then hits the Frankensteiner which sends her to the ground, allowing Nadia to hit her Guillatine Legdrop for the pin at 9:57. A brilliant match. The final first round match saw the best performance in a losing cause. Mary Williams entered second and instantly began to use her solid skills to beat Banshee down. Banshee survived an early Piledriver for a two count and Mary followed it up with a Huge Lariet. Banshee looked like she was stunned by the Lariet when Mary hit a nice back elbow and a solid pair of knee lifts. Mary then hit a bulldog for a two count but Banshee managed to get it all going and turned a hip toss attempt into a DDT. It was all over then, as Banshee hit her Banshee slap. She picked Mary up at two and nailed her with another Banshee slap. Again she stopped the count at two and picked Mary up this time as she went for it, Mary ducked and managed to get a back suplex for a two count, but Banshee kicked out and again hit the Banshee slap, but this time took the three count for the win. Next card , the Champion will be crowned as the winners of Persephone vs Laura Wineinger and Banshee vs Nadia Williams will meet for the first GWF women's title. The Road to the Gold World Title Tournamant Qualifying Round The first first round match saw the entrance of wrestling's answer to Koe Satriani, Heavy Metal Joe, plaing some of his speed metal as his opponent, Luchadore El Jeffe stood across the ring, he then charged into the ring and tried to clock Jeffe with his guitar, but the smaller Jeffe ducked it and went for a drop kick, but had his feet swatted away by the much larger Joe. Joe then grabbed the much smaller Joe and let him have a wild powerslam. Jeffe then kicked out at two and rolled out hoping for some reprieve, but he got none as Joe let him have an elbow from the ring apron and then tossed Jeffe like a lawn dart into the railing surrounding the ring. The Lucha Libre specialist then staggered out of the corner and ran right into a clothesline which put him down to the ground. Joe then got back in and Jeffe followed as soon as he got to his feet. As Jeffe returned, Joe tossed him off to the ropes, but Jeffe rolled under a clothesline and tried for a frankensteiner, but Joe turned it into a powerbomb and dropped the little guy right next to the ropes, so he could stomp him a bit before he picked him up and chokeslammed him into oblivion. 3 minutes and 24 brutal seconds. The next match looked good on paper, the submission specialist with a strange habit of not speeking or showing any emotion Entropy vs Lunatic Louie, who looked like he had the skills to beat on Entropy and put him down for his first big loss. Louie had almost forty pounds on Entropy, and had his manager Scarface as another plus in his corner, but Entropy was a master of Sambo submission wrestling and a GWF Arena favourite. The two entered the ring, Louie getting boos from all sides and Entropy getting loud cheers, but not showing any concern for the fans. HE was all business and his face paint, black dripping from under his eyes, made him look even more dedicated to his victory. The two locked up in the centre of the ring and immediately, Entropy got an armbar on Louie and drove him down to the canvas. Louie rolled forward and Entorpy just went for another variation on the armbar, but Louie made the ropes. Louie then locked up again, but this time Louie got the upperhand and caught Entropy with a solid elbow, then put him in a headlock and took him to the mat. HE maintained the headlock for almost a minute, but then Entropy sat out and made his way onto Louie's back and went for a leglock submission. As Entropy lay back to stretch the leg, Scarface raked his face and forced Entropy to release the hold, Louie then picked up the smaller lad and threw him into the corner and followed up with a big knee. He then threw Entropy into the opposite corner, but the little guy just jumped onto the top and flipped back over louie's head sending him in chest first and allowing entropy to get a leg take-down and put another leg lock on in the middle of the ring. Louie suffered in it for half a minute or so, until Louie managed to make the ropes. Entropy then went for a big back suplex, but Louie dead weighted him and rolled forward sending Entropy to the outside. Louie then slid into Entropy, sending him into the railing. He then went out and threw a few punches and then climbed back in as Scarface began to throw punches at Entropy. Louie then reached down and grabbed Entropy's hair and suplexed him back in, but then as he tried to float over for the pin, Entropy managed to catch his legs as he tried to roll on top and got a two count. Entropy then got to his feet and put Louie in a standing hammerlock. Louie made it to the ropes, and as Entropy was allowing the break, Scarface grabbed his ankle letting Louie get a clothesline in sending him outside, right at Scarface's feet, which put in a couple of kicks. Scarface sent Entropy back in and Louie caught Entropy in a chinlock at the ten minute mark. Entropy suffered in the hold for two minutes, until he manuevered himself to a point where he could get a solid kick off and nail Louie right in the forehead. As Louie relled, entropy kipped up and nailed Louie with a back brain kick and then went for a cross arm scissors, Scarface reached in and grabbed Louie's leg and put it over the bottomsrand, forcing Entropy to break the hold. Louie then managed to throw a punch into Entropy's low blow and doubled him up, allowing Louie to DDT the little guy. Louie then went to the ropes to do an elbow, but Entropy moved and sent Louie to the ground, allowing Entrpoy to put the STF on and get almost a minute of time in on hte move before Scarface threw some powder into Entropy's face. As he staggered backwards, Louie managed to grab his ankle and he fell hard onto the mat, allowing Louie to put on a Boston crab at the 17 minute mark. Entropy pushed out, and Louie went to the ropes, looking like he was going for an elbow, but Entropy rolled forward and grabbed arm, doing a forward roll take down for a two count, then held him in a stretch position, putting much pressure on Louie's ribs. Entropy had to break when Louie made it to the ropes, but then Grabbed him and lifted him to his feet and gave him a back leg sweep. Louie fell out of the ring, and Entropy went for a tope, but missed and crashed to the ground. Louie then tossed him back in and hit a forearm off the top rope. A two count later Louie argued with the ref while Entropy crossed him up and put him in a cross-face chicken wing, which Louie turned into a leg take-down and then followed up with a big leg drop. He then lifted his fallen opponent and went for a piledriver, but Entrop stood up sending him crashing to the mat. Louie then got up and tried for a punch, but Entropy ducked and went for a tiger suplex, which Louie broke out of a got his Sleeps with the fishes Sleeper. Entropy got the arm raised once, then twice, but kept it up at three and got to his feet, turning the sleeper around and hitting a back suplex. Entropy then Picked up Louie for a side suplex at 19:57 and ran towards the corner, jumping up and springing off the middle rope and flying down, driving Louie into the mat just as the bell rang. The crowd was going crazy at this point and Commissioner [name withheld] said they would both be in the title tourney for sure. The next match was going to be a bad ass brawl, as the Stormtrooper of death entered, followed by the cobsessed Holy Executioner, with his chant music playing and his silver dust raining all around. The two of them enter the ring and stood eye to eye, until Stormtrooper threw a big right hand and was greeted with a simultaneous left from Executioner. The two kept throwing punches until Executioner got the upperhand and knocked stormtrooper between the ropes. Executioner then launched himself over the top rope, but met a solid chair on his way down from the Stormtrooper. Trooper then hits the HE with the chair and then goes for a DDT, but Executioner stands straight up when he goes for it and drives him into the ring post, but after he drives him into the ringpost, Trooper brings the Holy one down in a DDT onto the concrete. The Trooper then got up and went back into the ring, clutching his back. He then rolled out again and nailed Executioner with a big fist, but as Executioner spun around, he nailed him with a haymaker and rolled back inside. As Trooper came in, H.E. hit him with several kicks and an Elbow right down onto hisneck. Executioner then picked him up and went for his finisher, but Trooper lifted his head quickly and knocked all the air out of the Executioner. He then picked up H.E. and put him down with a solid Diamond Cutter. He then nailed an elbow off the middle rope and Executioner barely managed to kick out at two and a half. Trooper then went for a Suplex, but Executioner pulled off a reversal and brought him down in a brainbuster for a two count. H.E. then used a big legdrop and followed that up with a two count. Holy Executioner then Picked Trooper up and Powerslamed him into the mat. H.E. went to the top rope and game off with the Leap of Faith moonsault and got a two and three quarters count before Trooper got his leg across the botom. Executioner then went for a Piledriver, but Tropper stood up, which forced Executioner to Roll him up in a sunset flip for a two count. Executioner then missed a clothesline and Stormtrooper went for a Death Drop, but his Holiness managed to get out of it and land a solid knee into Trooper's gut, doubling him up and allowing The Holy Executioner to nail his Wrath Of God Powerbomb for the pin at 8:13. The next match pitted Iceman vs Nightmare. As Iceman entered the ring, Nightmare jumped him and began bringing down heavy forearms into Iceman's back. Iceman tries to get away from the punishment, but Nightmare stays right on top of him the whole time. Nightmare nails Iceman with a huge boot to the face that sends him falling to the concrete. Iceman then gets a solid double axehandle to the forehead and Nightmare begins to wobble a little as the Nightmare hits him with everything on the announcer's table, a pitcher of water, a monitor, a steel chair. Iceman then hits Nightmare with a solid boot to the gut and jumps up onto the ring apron and does a back elbow on his opponent. Then, as Nightmare seems hurt, he tosses a solid forearm into Iceman/s face. As Iceman rolls back in for some way of getting away from this, Nightmare rolls back in and tries for an atomic drop, but Iceman turns it into a rolling headlock. Iceman then hits a series of elbows and a solid spinning kick to Nightmare's jaw. As Nightmare backs into the corner, Iceman runs at him and plants a foot into his chest and does a backflip off of him. Nightmare drops to the mat and Iceman gets a two count. After Nightmare kicks out, he rolls to the outside, where Iceman hits him with a Flip tope and then does a senton while he lays on the outside. As Iceman gets back in, the lights go out. The light stay out for about twenty seconds, but when they come on, Nightmare is giving punches into Iceman's forehead as he is busted wide open now. The ref is down and there ae several objects in the ring. Nightmare uses a chain to beat on Iceman, and he has brought forth a lot of blood on him. Nightmare gets a chokeslam and then puts Iceman in a headscissors, which he kips out of and then lays a solid kick into Nightmare's gut. Iceman then takes to the top and flies off with a spinning kick. As Nightmare lay on the ground, Iceman uses a big Legdrop off the corner for a two count. Nightmare agin rolls out of the ring, and Iceman goes for a big Asai moonsault onto him, but he is caught and powerslammed through a ringside table. As he picks Iceman up, Nightmare catches a stiff Kick and rells a bit allowing Iceman to pull himself up into the ring. Nightmare enters and Gets a DDT. HE then gives his sign for the Nightmare Sleeper, and as he tries for it Iceman spins out of it and puts on the Icefreez for the pin, as Nightmare slinks paralyzed to the mat. King of Pain Three Way Dance: Winner is named GWF's King of Pain The first two combatants enter the ring, the losers of the toss were Cyborg and Graphic Violence. The two of them charge at each other and cyborg stands up to the punches that GV lays into him, but he does not even flinch when he is hit. GV then Tries getting momentum of the ropes, but he does not even stagger him. He tries again, but cyborg nails him with a boot to the chest and then follows up by DDTing him hard and following up with some elbows. cyborg then picks up Violence and puts him across his shoulders, but GV gets one arm free and does a fist into the groin. which distracts Cyborg long enough for GV to get down and clip his knee. GV works the knee for a few minutes, doing knee drops onto it and wrapping it around his body and laying back, adding torque to the mix. The Cyborg looks like he is in trouble, but he gives Vilonce a solid trio of punches to the back of his head. GV lets go and rolls out, where Cyborg follows and the fight begins. Cyborg throws a number of punches, but then Viloence throws a fan's beer in his eyes and as he wipes them clean, GV uses a purse he finds at ringside. He picks Cyborg up and Suplexes him on the concrete. GV then goes for an elbow, but misses and Cyborg picks him up and DDT's him on the outside. Cyborg then rolls him backin the ring, and puts him in a stranglechoke hold when GV turns out of it and sends an elbow into his crotch, stunning Cyborg so GV can nail a powerslam. As he kneels over Cyborg giving all sorts of taunts to the fans, Cyborg reaches up and clutches Violence's throat, and then stands up and chokeslams the big guy. He then uses a rack over his shoulders to take some of the steam out of GV. HE eventaully drops to his knees and is about to cover GV when the ten minute bell is rung and Submarine comes down the aisle with Laura Wineinger. Submarine has a chain with him, and he runs into the ring from behind Cyborg and wraps the chain around his neck, dragging him by it around the ring and hanging him by it off of the side of the ring. Submarine has choked Cyborg for at least three minutes before GV manages to get to his feet and head to the ringside area. Cyborg's hand was being lifted as GV grabbed a chair a nailed Submarine with it, sending him over the top rope. He then Placed the chair on the ground And piledrove Submarine right onto it. Laura Wineinger tries to get involved, but GV tosses her against the railing and Tosses Submarine back inside where he hits a moonsault onto Submarine and gets the two count. Cyborg has started to come to his feet, but GV does a dive over the top rope, nailing him right between the eyes with a chair. Violence then smashes the chair into Cyborg's head a few times, and then helps get him into the ring, where he uses a huge boot to the face to put him down. GV then hits a Hot Shot on Submarine and the top ring rope falls off the corner post. GV then gives the sign and rolls out and sets up a table. As he does this Cybrog gets back to his feet and does an axehandle onto Submarine. Cyborg then Slams Graphic's head into the table and goes to the ring apron and drops an eblow onto his back. He goes up for another, but Gv grabs his foot and pulls himself up. The two exchange punches for a second, and then GV throws a punch to the low blow again, but Cyborg reacts by grabbing his throat and chokeslamming him through the table. Cybrog then Picks him up and tosses him back in, but Submarine comes back and nails Cyborg with a sock full of silver dollars which Laura has thrown to him. Cyborg is knocked cold and Graphic Violence sees that Submarine is still in the fight and pretends to be seriously hurt, so as he turns away him Submarine as he sets up Cybrog for the Sonar Splash, Submarine hits it, GV throws a chair and hits Submarine in the head, knocking him out cold. GV then rolls back in and sets up a table in the ring. He Powerbombs Cyborg through it and then comes off with a splash while riding a chair for the three count. He then grabs the unconcious Submarine and Piledrives him through one of the broken halfs of the table to get the pin. Graphic Violence is crowned the first King of Pain in 17:23.
