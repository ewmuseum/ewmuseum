The Official EWWF Commercial

21 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Scene fades in to the EWWF corporate office of Dan Falk, where he's sitting there, scolding his scantily clad secretary, for her forbidden deeds with
Johnny Lords. He notices the camera and sends the secretary off on her ways. ]
Dan Falk - Ya know, for all those who aren't in the EWWF, you seem to have us
WAY wrong. We're not run by a bunch of little perverted kids, who use sex as their angles.
[ You then hear loud panting noises coming from off the camera. ] Dan Falk - JOHNNY!!!! [ Falk is seen running off the camera ] [ Camera then turns to showing Harvester Of Sorrow with a *Playboy* magazine, cladding the WWF's Sable on the cover. He looks up from the magazine. ]
Harvester Of Sorrow - We aren't unoriginal BASTARDS who have to use giant demonic Christmas toys to give us good ratings.
[ A giant devilish, Tickle-Me-Elmo Satan doll is seen romping behind him, chasing down some corporate suits. ]
[ Camera then shoots over to Guru sitting down on a chair with a Webster's
Thesaurus sitting next to him. Phantom is seen in the distance, setting fire to some suits. ]
Guru - The miscreants of all the other feds, can suck on my uncirCUMsized
SCHLONG for thinking that my bastion of a cipher and I are UN-EXTREME. But worry not, we all are cooperative towards each other, and rarely get in confrontations.
[ You see "Misfit" Chris Douvalas looking over his manager Iam With Eyeconn, laying on the ground, and being beaten with a baseball bat. ]
[ The camera then switches over to "The Shooter" Simon Solkoff, who's walking down a hall, coffee in hand. Enigma is in the background, writing on some papers. ]
Simon Solkoff - We here, treat our legends with respect, and prestige. [ Outsider and FBK are seen walking out from a door as some members of the
Lynch Mob, Preacher, Luni, X-treme, Malice, and Wasteland Warrior walk by, saying such things as "Hey OutHOUSER", "How 'dem hoes been? Ain't they pushin'
60 now?", and "F-B-GAAAAAY!!". ] [ The camera switches away, showing the main lobby of the EWWF headquarters, with total madness everywhere. "Crippler" Chris Rollins and Scorpio are seen sitting at a table, eating some lunch, as well as Great Southern Rapist and
Immortal Doomsday, sitting across from them. The camera scrolls over until they see Kenneth Morlock, with the EWWF U.S. Title on his shoulder. ]
Kenneth Morlock - We're just as normal and sane, as anyone else. Sure, we've had our share of psychos, but we all have mutual respect for one another.
[ Texas Hitman is seen walking through the front doors of the headquarters, and starts beating down on Maxx Carnage, out of nowhere. Higor Rosa is seen in the background, sipping on some Zuckerwise beer, laughing at the giant brawl. ]
[ Camera fades to the outside of the EWWF Headquarters. The entire building is on fire, as Dan Falk is seen walking out of the front doors. He gets up close to the camera, and looks back to the building, which is still a blazing inferno. He looks back at the camera. ]
Dan Falk - Ahhhhh, fuck it. [ Camera fades out to a picture of the EWWF Logo, as it catches on fire, and the camera fades out. ]
