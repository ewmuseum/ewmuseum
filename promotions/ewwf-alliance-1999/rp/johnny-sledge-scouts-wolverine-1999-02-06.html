Johnny Sledge Scouts Wolverine

6 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The picture fades in and you see that you are out in the street, in the middle of the day. Right away you see the number one seed in the EWWF
Alliance Tournament, Johnny Sledge, standing in front of a building.
Sledge is wearing loose fitting jeans, Nike Hiking Boots, and you cannot see what shirt he is wearing because he has his EWA Varsity Jacket.
Sledge also has on a fitted Minor League Baseball hat, the Kissimee
Cobras if you have to know the specifics. He is in his usual good move while he just stands there waiting to begin to start talking. Sledge nods in agreement, as if he was just given the cue to begin.)
Sledge: I am standing here at the last point on my quest to get the information I am looking for on my opponent for the first round of the
EWWF Alliance Tourney, Wolverine. Now any smart person would be able to quickly figure out where I would be. But for those that are not that intelligent, I am at the one place that anybody could find out about
Wolverine, and that place is Marvel Headquarters of course! Yup that’s right folks, I am going straight to the source of where this man came from. So just follow me while I go in and get the information I need.
(With that Sledge turns around, and walks into this building. He makes his way over to the elevator and looks at the map of what is on each floor of the building. He scans it, and sees that the Marvel Offices are on the seventh floor. Sledge presses the up button, but the elevator that is on the third floor and going up this fifteen-story building. So realizing he has some time to kill he turns to the camera, and begins to kill the time.)
Sledge: Wolverine, you try to get yourself all psyched up with all this big tough guy talk. Wolvie, you sound like a twelve-year-old talking to his friends and his parents are not around with the way you have been swearing just to swear. You see that doesn’t do anything for you, all it does is solidify even further that you truly suck. Hell, Wolverine why don’t you just give it up? You must realize by now that all the weight lifting that you do, all the swearing you do, and all the empty threats you make, are not going to change the fact that I am going to beat the piss out of you in that match we are going to have in the first round of the EWWF Alliance Tournament.
(The elevator finally arrives, and Sledge gets in, and he is the only person to get on, besides the cameraman of course. He gets into the elevator and presses the button for the seventh floor, which is his destination. Some crappy elevator music begins playing, and Sledge seems quickly annoyed by it. He turns to the camera and gets out a few comments while waiting to reach the seventh floor.)
Sledge: So Wolvie, what the hell is wrong with that bum chum of yours… you know that guy Jay that was spotting you. He seems to dislike me almost as much as you do, but I didn’t do anything to him. But if you want to be mad about something Jay, it will come after I put your boyfriend in a body cast, and you won’t be able to get any lovin’ from him for a while.
(The bell rings, and the elevator stops. The door opens up and Sledge gets out. He walks right over to receptionist’s desk.)
Sledge: Where is the office of Stan Lee? Receptionist: Do you have an appointment? Sledge: Yeah, I’m on the list right there. (Sledge points down at the appointment list at some random name. The woman has a baffled look on her face when he does this.)
Receptionist: Chang Wong? Unless you are a middle aged Chinese man, I would take it that you are lying to me about you having an appointment.
(Before Sledge can answer, he sees Stan Lee walk into an office at the end of the hall. A devious smirk appears on his face as he begins to talk.)
Sledge: Ok lady I’ll reason with you… Hey is that a ten-dollar bill over there? (The receptionist looks down and away from Sledge, and this gives him the chance to dart past he and get down the hall over to the office that
Stan Lee went in. He knocks on the door, and you hear a, “Come in.” from the office. Sledge walks in and sitting there is Stan Lee. Sledge sits down, but Lee has an absolutely baffled look on his face.)
Sledge: Hey Mr. Lee, I’m here to ask you a few questions… Stan Lee: Excuse me… who are you? Sledge: I’m Johnny Sledge, and I am going to be wrestling one of your creations, Wolverine, in the first round of the EWWF Alliance
Tournament. Do you have any secrets on him that I can use? Stan Lee: What are you talking about, Wolverine is a fictional character. There is no real life Wolverine.
Sledge: So I’ll pretty much be facing nothing in the first round? Stan Lee: I guess. Sledge: You know Stan this makes total sense now! I’m glad we had this talk. Stan Lee: Uh…ok? (Sledge goes and shakes the hand of Stan Lee, and leaves the office. As
Sledge leaves the room, you see the receptionist with three security guards.)
Receptionist: There he is! Get him! Sledge: Oh shit! (Sledge hauls ass down the hall and ducks under the security guards. One of the security guards approaches the cameraman, and the picture then fades out. When it fades back in you see that Sledge is now standing at his 1999 Red Lincoln Navigator, he is a bit out of breath, but he is laughing hard.)
Sledge: So I went through this whole thing to find out something about
Wolverine, I learned what I knew all along… he is absolutely nothing!
Maybe I shouldn’t have gone through all this stuff, but it was fun as hell to make fun of this idiot so easily. In fact I could have done more stuff, but the speedster of a cameraman got caught up there, dumb bastard.
(Sledge opens the door, and he gets in. He starts up the Navigator, and gets right back out. He leaves the door open, and takes off his jacket, and there is another one of Sledge’s now infamous shirts. On the front it says, “I had Wolverine’s mother…” and Sledge does full rotation so you can see the back of the shirt, and it says, “… and she was no good!” Sledge laughs out loud as he tries to talk.)
Sledge: Did you think I wouldn’t bring out another shirt? Come one, it’s been too fun. But I’m going to go home and get ready to leave for this first round, where I am going to pound the shit out of
“Wolverine.” Before I go, though, I have to say just one thing… uh, no
I don’t I just needed a way to lead into my catch phrase… oh well, I’ll probably have a better chance next time finding a way to fit in that I am… The One… The Only… The Original… Johnny Sledge!!!
(Sledge laughs out loud as he gets into his Navigator and pulls out of the parking spot. The picture fades into a picture a decapitated Marvel
Wolverine Action figure, with it’s head in the hand of the Johnny Sledge
EWA action figure. It then fades to black.)
