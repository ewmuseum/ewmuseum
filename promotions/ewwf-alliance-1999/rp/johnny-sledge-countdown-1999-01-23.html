Johnny Sledge's Own Countdown to the Alliance Rumble

23 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the last rp that I did for the... well if you read the subject you should know. Feel free to comment on it all you want. Now without further adieu...
----- (When the scene opens up and you see that you are once again in the gym of
Johnny Sledge. We’ve seen this place before, but this time the gym is quite empty. The lights are all out now, after another rigorous day of training for Johnny Sledge. This last day was a closed training session, because…uh… well who knows why, that’s what Sledge wanted so he got it. Now, though, is a different story. The work out is over, and here you are in the empty gym.
There is one light on though. That light is coming from the room in the back of the gym. This room is Johnny Sledge’s office. You slowly make your way down to the door to this office, and see that the door is half opened.
Carefully you make your way into the room, and see that Sledge is sitting in the chair behind the desk. He is still in work out gear, black mesh shorts, a gray EWA sleeveless shirt, and since he has his feet up on the desk you see that he is wearing black Nike cross trainers. He appears to be very relaxed and at ease, considering that in about twelve hours he will be heading off to where the 1999 EWWF Alliance Rumble, to crown an Undisputed
World Champion, is being held. You notice a dark blue towel on the desktop as Sledge leans over and picks it up. He wipes some sweat off his face and runs the towel straight through his hair. He puts the towel around his neck and then takes his feet off the desk. He spins around toward the camera, and puts his arms down on the desk, slight leaning forward. He cracks a half smile as he begins to speak.)
Sledge: Well, the last day of training has ended. In a short time, I head off to face off against seven of the best the EWWF Alliance has to offer, for the most prestigious title that a person can get here. And without trying to sound too cocky, I truly feel that I am more than ready for this thing. I know that I am going into this thing more prepared than I have ever been for anything. I’ve watched the top stars of this Alliance battle it out with everything they had in the first two installments of this thing, and knew that if the day ever came that I was able to compete for this thing, I would give 220% toward winning the belt. Honestly though, I never really believed that I would ever be in this tournament, well at first anyway. But as I learned more and more, and got better and better, I knew I could not only make it into the thing, but be able to hold my own quite well.
When I had first found out that I was in the ’99 Tournament, even though I knew I was the best choice, I wasn’t sure if I would be able to defend the name of the EWA properly. To start off I really didn’t think so. But as the first week of preparations for this thing went on, I was made aware that
I was in fact at the level of all of my competition. The Tournament is meant for only the top talent in the Alliance, and I am just that one of the top talents. And on Sunday, I will prove that I am not just one of the top… but I am THE top talent in the EWWF Alliance.
(Sledge sits back up in the chair, but still is facing the camera. By now he has a more serious look on his face. He takes the towel from around his neck and throws it onto a chair that is against the wall to the left of his desk.)
Sledge: Like I’ve said numerous times, I don’t care what anybody says about me talking about all of the sentimental bullshit, because I don’t really give a fuck. Yeah, you know I could have come out here and acted like a perverted jackass like… you guessed it… Guru.
Guru, I’m glad I could have helped you out with making you realize that you got were not as smart as you really thought. Hey, Mr. Super Intellect, by what you said about me proved only one… uh… no make that two things. The first of them is that you are not as smart as you make yourself out to be.
How can somebody as “smart” as you not pay attention to something as important as when the card was being held and how many weeks we had for to prepare for this thing.
Like I said before, I trained harder for this than any other thing I have trained for, and that included my knowing how long I had to prepare for this thing. Guru, for such a smart man, that’s one awfully dumb mistake.
Remember what I told you last week Guru, all you needed to do was make one mistake and that would be your downfall. And that downfall Guru will come at my hands on Sunday.
(Sledge gets up and pushes the chair in. He walks over to the back wall where he has all of his wrestling awards. He drags his finger across a shelf that has all of the belts he’s won, resting on it. Right in the middle of the shelf is a space, about big enough to have one more belt. I’ ll give you three guess what belt Sledge is intending upon putting there, and the first two don’t count. Anyway, Sledge turns back to the camera after tapping this opening a couple of times.)
Sledge: And now the second thing that I learned from your half assed, pathetic attempt at trying to prove something, and that being when push comes to shove, you cower up like a little bitch, and… well bitch and moan.
Guru, why didn’t you just admit it was your own stupidity and not somebody’s personal vendetta against you. Let me be the first to tell you Guru, you are not worth jack shit to anybody. Sorry to break it to ya Guru, but its true. You’re just another stupid bastard, but the only difference between you, and the scrub bitch who gets slaughter every week in the popcorn match, is that you know how to use a thesaurus.
What Guru, did you think you were the only person with one of those, or that nobody would be able to figure out your ridiculously idiotic attempt at making yourself appear to be more intellectual. Guru, since you love coming up with more ways to say something I have one for ya… Guru on Sunday I’m going put you through the fucking ring.
(Sledge walks away from the back wall, and goes over to the chair where he through the towel. He picks it up and leaves the office; he doesn’t shut the lights off though. He walks across the dark gym, and when he reaches another door he opens it, and turns the light on. Once the light goes on you see that it is the locker room to the gym. Sledge walks over to the only remaining locker with a lock on it. Sledge opens it up and then sits down next to the opened locker.)
Sledge: As I see it, I will be able to get a good eight-hour sleep before I have head off for the Alliance Rumble. That’s not bad considering I haven’t had that much sleep in the past two weeks. This training to win the
Undisputed World Championship has taken a ton out of me. I expected myself to work this hard for it though, and have planned everything well enough, so
I will have a good amount of time to rest before the Rumble takes place. I’ m getting there more than twenty-four hours in advance so there will be no jet lag. I’ve already made my game plan for the match and have been going over and over it in my head, as well as trying some things out in the ring.
To say that I am greatly prepared for this event is a vast understatement. I am ready, plain and simple. When I step into that ring with my seven opponents, you will all see just what I have in store for this thing.
Simple enough, on Sunday night the EWA go back 2 back, and have the third
EWWF Alliance Undisputed World Champion in their ranks in… The One… The
Only… The Original… Johnny Sledge!!!
(Sledge gets up and turns to the open locker. He begins to pull a couple of things out of the locker as the picture begins to fade out. It then cuts to an ad for the EWWF Alliance ’99 Rumble to crown the Undisputed World
Champion. This ad shows many high spots from all eight competitors, then
Cyris standing in the middle of the ring holding up the belt. It finally goes to a black screen with blood red letters saying, “Who’s next?” This stays on and slowly fades to black.)
