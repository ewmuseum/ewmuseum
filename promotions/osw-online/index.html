A Portland indy fed of 2002 that ran a bi-weekly rotation of full cards and flash shows from its converted-warehouse Schoolhouse, crowning its first Television, Old-School and tag team champions in its first year. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Old-School Wrestling opened in January 2002 as an indy federation in Portland, Oregon, run on a bi-weekly rotation: a full card, OSW Insession, and two flash shows, Flashback and Interactive, all staged from the OSW Schoolhouse, a remodeled warehouse its own FAQ said held about a thousand fans. In the fiction it was owned by the tag team Fire and Ice, Michael Ice and Alex Perkins, with Jeff Cunningham as C.E.O. and ring announcer and “Cool” Keith Robinson and Sam Collins in the booth. There was no jumbotron and no pyro, and nothing happened backstage that the fans could not see: the fed's own rule was that everything that mattered happened in the ring.

Its opening card of 5 February 2002 put Dark Soul over Def Bellows in the first match and “Strictly Business” Lew Spectre over “Delinquent” Kevin Fontaine in the main event, while a masked man stalked Fontaine and Brian Anthony; he unmasked in April as “Darkhorse” Derrek Johnson. The same April card crowned the fed's first champion, Chris Colton, who won a four-man Television Title tournament over Dark Soul.

In June 2002 Rock Solid Enterprises bought a third of the fed, making it the regional member of a three-federation alliance with the World Wrestling Organization and Ultimate E-Wrestling. Colton and Bellows formed the Superiority Complex with Sexual Energy, the tag team whose manager had handed them tag belts the fed refused to recognize.

The Field Trip, an outdoor supercard at PGE Park in Portland billed for 3 September 2002, settled both questions in one night: Sexual Energy unified the rival tag claims and won the first official Unified Tag Team Titles, and Bellows won an eight-man tournament for the Old-School Title, leaving the Complex holding every belt in the fed. It closed 2002 still holding all three, with a summarised House Show added to its rotation and a house-show report posted on 17 December.

Affiliations

the tri-fed alliance

Member of OSW Online (2002) (2002-06)
The fed's own 15 June 2002 news page announces a partial buyout by Rock Solid Enterprises and places Old-School Wrestling as the Regional affiliate in a tri-fed alliance, alongside the World Wrestling Organization (Global) and Ultimate E-Wrestling (National), with a training school also named as joining the same network.
