From the President's Desk ============================= OK, I'm going to leave this (relatively) short and sweet this Update. Only a few notes I'd like to discuss. First, for those of you who haven't been by the OSW Discussion Board... PLEASE DO SO. I have posted something that I would like some feedback on. As well, the OSW Old-School Title tourney has been announced. No final tourney date, as qualifying matches will take at least 3
Insessions. As well, last week's impromptu match marked the first OSW Insession that will be 6 matches long. As you'll note... this is now permanent... AS LONG AS THE WRITING SITUATION HOLDS UP. I wanted to move to 7, and considering our nearly-full roster and bi-weekly schedule, we really NEED to go to 7... but this week's writing issues have left me uncertain if we could fill that seventh match. So...
we're going with 6 for now. If the writing situation holds steady with six... we'll consider 7 again.
Oh, and one more thing... I've said this before, and it's repeated in the FAQ, but I need to say it again... OSW is an indy fed. 75% of it's entire budget is set aside for wrestler contracts, because the focus is not on the flash, and not simply on the wrestling (sorry, Lew ;))... the focus is on the WRESTLERS and the RING. If you want to get something done... you MUST do it in the ring. We do NOT have a jumbotron. If you do something backstage, there will be no camera to see it. The fans won't know it happened. They're there to watch the ring, fans don't come to wrestling shows to watch more TV. If you want to get something said or done... say it on the flash shows or do it in the ring. Otherwise, it doesn't get seen.
As a corollary to that... let me point out something that was commented on in the card, and is also stated in the FAQ. I will only allow a maximum of THREE (3) in-rings a week. Originally that was just for people not involved... but it's changed to 3 per week, period. OSW isn't going to turn into a flash show. There are MANY exceptions to this rule (joint in-rings where someone calls another wrestler out, for example, will be tolerated because it is more than just talking... the confrontation is there for the fans to see), so talk to me if you have an idea. And having your wrestler say a few words after his entrance when he has a match is also fine, because it's promotion of the upcoming match. But NEVER assume that just because you send in an in-ring, it will get used. If I get more than three, I will pick the three most relevant, and use them.
And another note... we're small. The OSW Schoolhouse (a remodeled warehouse in Portland) holds about 1,000 people. If you use fireworks, you will burn down the Schoolhouse. No pyros. Spotlights are OK, having the lights go out is OK, and the P.A. system sounds great... but that's about it.
Any questions or anything, of course, feel free to contact me. - President [name withheld] [detail withheld] 2. News ======== NEW OSW TELEVISION CHAMPION Last Insession saw the crowning of the first champion in OSW history... "Riverside Thriller" Chris Colton. Colton defeated his ally, Def Bellows, in the semi-finals after a controversial match restart by OSW VP Alex Perkins. Then Colton defeated Dark Soul in the finals, thanks to a distraction by Big Sir. However... take nothing away from the new champ, controversies aside Colton earned the title in the eyes of most fans who saw the event.
MYSTERY MAN... UNMASKED! Kevin Fontaine finally discovered who his masked pursuer is in a painful way last Insession, as he suffered a knee injury in a loss to the masked man... who, after the match, unveiled himself to be
"Darkhorse" Derrek Johnson. However, Johnson's fellow masked partner remained masked. Fontaine's injury will keep him out of action for an undisclosed amount of time.
OSW OLD-SCHOOL TITLE TOURNEY ANNOUNCED Last Insession, OSW President Michael Ice announced that qualifying matches will begin next Insession for the tourney to crown the first-ever OSW Old-School Champion. All participants will have to qualify for the tourney except for the finalists in the OSW Television
Title match, Chris Colton and Dark Soul. The champion, Colton, will be seeded second, as he already has a title. Lew Spectre was denied the first-round bye that was given to the TV Champ, due to his abuse of Referee Bob Walter in his Television Title qualifying match three
Insessions ago.
OSW "TAG TITLES" SUCCESSFULLY DEFENDED It took a referee interference, help from their manager Selena,
AND a handful of tights... but Sexual Energy successfully thwarted the
Dime Notes in an impromptu match last Insession. Sexual Energy, who introduced their "OSW Tag Team Titles" two Insessions ago, consider the match a successful title defense. The Dime Notes, however, aren't happy with the way Ian Rose and Andrew Stevens won the match... you can expect them to seek their revenge against the first team to defeat the "cornerstones" of the OSW tag division, much as they did two
Insessions ago against the Workshop.

Upcoming Card ================= For more schedule info, see Deadlines. *~~ OSW INSESSION ~~* 04 / 16 / 02 OSW Schoolhouse, Portland, OR Debut Match 1. "SHOOTING STAR" RYAN DOUGLAS vs. JEREMY STEELE OSW Insession kicks off in a big way with a debut match, as a couple of seasoned veterans make their OSW debuts. Well-known e-wrestling vet Jeremy Steele has come out of retirement due to his love for wrestling... but will his return to the ring be halted by
Douglas, a familiar and respected name by many on the OSW roster?
Tag Team Debut Match 2. PRECIOUS MOMENTS vs. HIGH SOCIETY INTERNATIONAL High Society International saw quite a bit of buildup on the last
Interactive... now they make their debut against another unorthodox addition to the OSW tag team division, Precious Moments. The OSW tag team division continues to grow with the additions of these two impressive teams, who should put on quite a show in Insession's second match of the night.
OSW Old-School Title Tourney Qualifier 3. MAGNUS COLBY vs. "BIG GUN" JIM SERGEANT The qualifying for the OSW Old-School Title tourney begins in the third match of the evening, as Magnus Colby, who lost to Dark Soul in the Television Title tourney last Insession, attempts to earn a shot at the biggest prize in OSW... but to get there, he'll have to defeat
Jim Sergeant, who's taking time from his "Salute: America" promotional tour to try to get into the tourney himself.
4. THE WORKSHOP vs. THE CHANDLER BROTHERS The tag teams are starting to heat up in OSW, especially after the
Dime Notes/Sexual Energy confrontation last Insession. In the fourth match of the evening, the odd Workshop will try to get make a case for the OSW Tag Team Titles themselves, as they take on the rookie team of the Chandler Brothers, who would love to make a name for themselves with a huge victory in their first-ever match.
5. DEF BELLOWS vs. "WHISKEY" JACK COLLINS Jack Collins, the huge Texan, makes his debut make under a huge spotlight... taking on Def Bellows. Bellows record is less than spectacular... but there's no denying he's one of the better wrestlers in OSW. However, will he be able to notch up a win against the
322-pound newcomer? >*~ MAIN EVENT ~*< OSW Old-School Title Tourney Qualifier 6. "FANTASY" BRETT YOUNG vs. "STRICTLY BUSINESS" LEW SPECTRE Lew Spectre gave up his chance at the Television Title to pursue the Old-School Title... and his journey begins this Insession, as he takes on a cold Brett Young. Young will be looking to turn around his
OSW career in a big way... by knocking one of the favorites out of the running before the tourney even begins. But Lew Spectre's entire focus right now is on the Old-School Title... and Young seriously has his work cut out for him if he wants to defeat the man who has main evented four of the five OSW Insessions.
5. Deadlines ============= My deadlines policy is simple. Get it in on time, it gets used. Get it in late, you're taking your chances. If I can fit it in without doing a ton of extra work, then I might, but if you REALLY want it on the show... get it in on time. We're going bi-weekly, you have plenty of time. Just to generalize the deadlines... all flashes are due the night BEFORE the flash show. :) NOTE: ALL deadlines are at 11:59 p.m. PST that night. Sunday, April 7: Flashes for OSW Flashback Thursday, April 11: Flashes for OSW Interactive Thursday, April 11: Strats for OSW Insession (unless other arrangements are made with me in advance... and you better have a good reason to need longer than a week to strat ;)) 6.
