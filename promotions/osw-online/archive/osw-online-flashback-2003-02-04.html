OSW

Jeff Cunningham is a bit surprised to find himself with some free time to ponder over some personal decisions. On this occasion this is no small decision. Suddenly a look of resolve comes across his face and he knows the answer to his question...

Jeff decides to ignore this minor inconvenience and drops 75¢ into the machine before him. As he punches "B4" into the machines with determination he looks up in surprise as he hears a slam and a muffled yell from down the hall.
