Flashback (02/09/02)

12 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A few notes beforehand... some of you, especially writing, have asked for a more specific description of Keith Robinson, our beloved color commentator. Getting Keith down isn't easy... in fact, only one or two writers have even come close. ;)
Keith Robinson, for all intensive purposes, personifies "old-school". Picture Bobby Heenan in WCW, or for you young pups, a less eccentric
Jerry Lawler. He is a former heel himself, and is a total mark for anything "old-school", especially heels. Thus his obsession with Curt
Crane in NCCW/CCW and Lew Spectre in OSW. However, he will also, as you'll come to find out, become a bit of a mark for wrestlers such as
Brett Young, who are classic in their style. He's not a straight heel commentator... but he does have heel tendancies.
Another thing to note... he is also a fan. And, as with any fan, he has his favorites that he sticks with, face or heel. As many of you have noted, Spectre and Chris Colton are his personal favs. A good example of his not being a total heel, however, is his friendship with
"Shocker" Brian Anthony, something I didn't make clear to Tommy when he wrote the match last week. Anthony's a face right now, and Keith dislikes that... but he's still 100% behind Anthony. His use in
Colton's flashes is an example of how he becomes friends with his favorite wrestlers out of the ring. To be specific... his favorites as of now are Spectre, Colton and Anthony. Beyond those three, who supercede everyone else, his favorites are, in order: old-school heels, heels, old-school faces, everyone else.
So, consider yourself informed on the man that is "Cool" Keith Robinson. :) And now, read the show to pick up more about him. Enjoy!
