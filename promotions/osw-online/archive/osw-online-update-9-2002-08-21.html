First off... big thanks again to Don, Rich and Chris for writing this week. You guys rock.
Now, though... it's time for you "emergency writers" to start coming out of the woodwork. OSW Field Trip has 10 scheduled matches, twice a normal Insession, and we're not doing the usual "month build to a special card" that most feds do... this is intended to replace
Insession in OSW's UPN timeslot. It's just another bi-weekly OSW card... except it's not. :) So, IF YOU CAN WRITE FOR FIELD TRIP,
LEMME KNOW. Thanks. :)
Next on the agenda... thanks to Josh for all the work he's done as flashmaster during and just before the delays. I will be taking the flash shows back over personally, but Josh helped out big during a time that I really needed the help. It was greatly appreciated, Josh, and good luck with your move next weekend. :)
Third... big thanks to YOU. Yes, _YOU_! No e-fed runs without a quality effort from it's handlers, and considering we just came off about two months of nasty delays... you guys gave me just that. 13 flashes on Flashback, 12 on Interactive... hell, I see the "big boys"
of the e-w world put out flash shows with less activity than that, and we're carrying a rather small roster compared to many feds. Not to mention well over half the roster stratted for last card, which I know for a fact just made me the envy of 85% of e-fed presidents going today. :) You guys got me and the writers inspired to get this thing out, and you deserve as much credit as Don, Rich, Chris and myself.
Rock on, guys, and keep up the fine work. Once again... the next show will be OSW Field Trip, which will feature the crowning of the OFFICIAL OSW Unified Tag Team Champions... not to mention the crowning of the first-ever OSW Old-School Champion.
Details of this event can be found below in the Upcoming Card section, complete with brackets for the Old-School Title tourney and anything else one could hope to discover. If there's anything else I can answer, please don't hesitate to ask.
Oh, and one more thing... I've said this before, and it's repeated in the FAQ, but I need to say it again... OSW is an indy fed. 75% of it's entire budget is set aside for wrestler contracts, because the focus is not on the flash, and not simply on the wrestling (sorry, Lew ;))... the focus is on the WRESTLERS and the RING. If you want to get something done... you MUST do it in the ring. We do NOT have a jumbotron. If you do something backstage, there will be no camera to see it. The fans won't know it happened. They're there to watch the ring, fans don't come to wrestling shows to watch more TV. If you want to get something said or done... say it on the flash shows or do it in the ring. Otherwise, it doesn't get seen.
As a corollary to that... let me point out something that was commented on in the card, and is also stated in the FAQ. I will only allow a maximum of THREE (3) in-rings a week. Originally that was just for people not involved... but it's changed to 3 per week, period. OSW isn't going to turn into a flash show. There are MANY exceptions to this rule (joint in-rings where someone calls another wrestler out, for example, will be tolerated because it is more than just talking... the confrontation is there for the fans to see), so talk to me if you have an idea. And having your wrestler say a few words after his entrance when he has a match is also fine, because it's promotion of the upcoming match. But NEVER assume that just because you send in an in-ring, it will get used. If I get more than three, I will pick the three most relevant, and use them.
And another note... we're small. The OSW Schoolhouse (a remodeled warehouse in Portland) holds about 1,000 people. If you use fireworks, you will burn down the Schoolhouse. No pyros. Spotlights are OK, having the lights go out is OK, and the P.A. system sounds great... but that's about it.
Any questions or anything, of course, feel free to contact me. - President [name withheld] [detail withheld] 2. News ======== COLTON STILL TELEVISION CHAMPION Chris Colton received the biggest scare his flawless record has seen yet, in an incredible Main Event against Jack Collins. Collins nearly had the title won on numerous occasions, but when it came down to it, Colton cheated Collins out of a victory. However, one can rest assured that the OSW front offices were impressed with the performances of both men... and a rematch is more than likely at some point in the future.
DARK SOUL WINS UNSCHEDULED TRIANGLE MATCH At the beginning of the last Insession, OSW President Michael Ice announced a special one-fall triangle match between Dark Soul, Def
Bellows and Frank Knight based on incidents at a recent house show.
After an awesome back-and-forth affair that saw all three men nearly end up with a win on multiple occasions, Soul ended up with the victory over his rival, Def Bellows. Frank Knight was said to have
"thoroughly enjoyed" the match, even though he ended no careers for the second straight match.
BATTLE ROYAL ENDS ON A PHENOMENAL NOTE An incredible 10-man battle royal ended with some controversy, as
"Phenomenon" Tyro Marvel (formerly Masked Man #2) and "Whiskey" Jack
Collins went over the top rope at the same time. Referee Tony
Corolla, the closest referee to the action, deemed that Marel had hit the outside first, and awarded the Battle Royal, as well as the
Old-School Title Tourney birth, to Marvel. Unfortunately, OSW didn't have a camera in position to catch the finish, though ringside fans after the match had differing opinions on the result. However, the referee's call stands, though Collins is less than pleased with the outcome of this match.
TAG TEAM CONTENDERS MATCH SET Reynolds and Bardon (or Bardon and Reynolds, depending on who you ask) scored a big victory over 3T0 Irish Mafia in their match last
Insession, moving on to face High Society International in a #1
Contenders match and potential title shot at OSW Field Trip. HSI advanced to the match after a mockery of a match which resulted in Mr.
Poe (dressed as a teddy bear) attacking Precious Moments with stuffed animals... you can't make this stuff up, folks. The two teams will meet at the Field Trip, for a chance to move into the OSW Unified Tag
Team Title Match at the Field Trip against the winner of the Sexual
Energy/Dime Notes Joint Tag Team Title Unification match.

6. Rankings ============ Returning by popular demand... ;) Who's Hot: ---------- 1. "Riverside Thriller" Chris Colton (OSW Television Champion) 7-0. Seven wins, no losses. Two successful title defenses. Still the ONLY champion in OSW for at least another two weeks. Until we see a "1" in that loss column, this spot is his to lose. Might even be his AFTER the "1".
2. Sexual Energy Chris Colton's ally, and the "Colton's" of the OSW tag division, if you will. 4-0, hold a win over the Dime Notes, they need to lose before this spot will be given up as well.
3. "Fantastic" Frank Knight The biggest threat among the "second wave" of OSW talent, Knight is sadistic. He's also unbeaten, and has already ended the OSW careers of Steve Johnson and Jeremy Steele. He also holds a singles win over Dark Soul on the last House Show. Far more feared and respected by his opponents than the fans... but that's slowly changing.
4. Dark Soul Everybody's favorite comeback face has struggled slightly since his red-hot debut as OSW opened it's doors, but he's still a major contender for the Old-School Title, a point he solidified with a win over Def Bellows and Frank Knight on the last Insession.
5. "Phenomenon" Tyro Marvel Finally free of his fate as "The second Masked Man", Marvel has erupted in his first two OSW matches, including an impressive victory in the Battle Royal last Insession, hitting the floor before the
Battle Royal favorite, 322-pound Jack Collins, to move into the
Old-School Title tourney at Field Trip. Who's Not: ---------- 1. Precious Moments The fairies and their notorious curse have been firmly blamed for the delays by OSW President Ice, who has stated that another loss will be the nail in the coffin for Precious Moments' OSW career.
2. Superstar Has underperformed thus far in the eyes of many, and looked unfocused in the Battle Royal. Superstar has the talent necessary to turn things around, but the question is... will he?
3. "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre Far from the dominant presence he has been in the past, Spectre has looked unmotivated in his last few matches, including an upset loss to David Cross at the House Shows. The "Franchise of OSW" is starting to show his age, and some of the boys backstage are wondering if Spectre still "has it".
4. Dime Notes Only here due to OSW officials clearing out most of the underperforming contracts. The loss to Sexual Energy appears to have shaken the "Joint Tag Team Champions", but they have their shot at retribution at the Field Trip... and crowds aren't rushing out to bet against them.
5. Def Bellows Another incredibly tentative spot, much like the Dime Notes,
Bellows is only here because of his record and the clean loss to Dark
Soul last Insession. However, like the Dime Notes, don't expect him to be on this list very long.
Most Popular ------------ 1. Dark Soul Everyone's favorite underdog pulled off another huge victory the last Insession in the surprise Triangle Match, which showed once again why the fans LOVE this man.
2. "Whiskey" Jack Collins Has one match EVER done more to establish someone with the crowd than Collins' match against Colton last Insession? Though Collins lost, he threw Colton from one end of the ring to the other, and by the end of the match the fans hated Colton just as much for causing
Collins to lose than for winning himself.
3. The Dime Notes Come on, really... who DOESN'T like Darby Sharpe? Well, other than Sexual Energy, that is. The most beloved tag team in OSW...
eruptions of cheers will fill PGE Park if the Notes walk out of the
Field Trip as the OSW Unified Tag Team Champions.
4. "Fallen Angel" David Cross Another "underdog" type, Cross' big impression came when he defeated Lew Spectre at a recent House Show. The fan's reaction has been growing slowly for Cross... but it's definitely growing.
5. "Big Gun" Jim Sergeant The "American Hero" has been quiet lately... but hey, he's the
American Hero. Most Hated ---------- 1. Superiority Complex (Chris Colton/Def Bellows/Sexual Energy) The dominant group in Old-School Wrestling since they formed, arrogance defines the group. However, they have the ability to back up their words, and have done so often, drawing the ire of the crowd after screwing over nearly every top face in OSW at one time or another.
2. High Society International President Ice nearly sent out security to break up the joke of a match and post-match attack of Precious Moments by HSI, the fans were so angry. Steve Riq and Christopher Black all but destroyed the fairies, and nearly started a riot in the crowds in the process.
3. "Fantastic" Frank Knight OSW's resident "career killer" proudly wears the ended careers of
Steve Johnson and Jeremy Steele with pride, right along side his victory over Dark Soul. Knight is feared, dangerous... and very despised by the fans.
4. "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre No longer the "most hated man in OSW", the fans still despise
Spectre. No name in OSW draws the negative reaction that Spectre's does.
5. "Phenomenon" Tyro Marvel Shedding the mask he once wore, Tyro Marvel has let his arrogance and selfish tendancies flow freely, and the fans have noticed. After that surprise Battle Royal victory, eliminating Jack Collins... the fans noticed that, too.
