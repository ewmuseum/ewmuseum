From the President's Desk ============================= OK, a quick update on my end, to explain things a little bit to everybody. I work for the local high school district, in the food service office. At the end of the month, I get to do inventory for all three Yuma high schools (which took two 10-hour days alone). As well as help stock and deliver 1,000 cases of goverment food in the last 2 weeks that my crackhead boss is ordering with 3 months left in the school year... coupled with the fact that the guy who's SUPPOSED to be doing all this just got back Friday from brochial pneumonia...
that explains my last two weeks. Basically, I was putting in 10-12 hours a day M-F, and at least 8 Saturdays, trying to do all this other work while still fitting in the responsibilities from _MY_ job.
However, I was finally back in the office all day Friday, and the rush should be over. So things will start returning to normal. I know we never really got to ESTABLISH a normal before things got crazy... but let's go ahead and do that. ;)
I'd like to get comments on this card. I think things are shaping up well, if not spectacularly. But I was happy with how it turned out.
Feel free to let me know what you thought as well... I can always use feedback. ;) As I said with the deadlines when I sent out the schedule earlier in the week, we're pushing both flash shows (as well as their respective deadlines) back a day, to Saturday and Thursday. PLEASE LOOK FOR THE
DEADLINES IN _EVERY_ UPDATE. This is the one place each bi-weekly cycle to be sure to find them. I'll also start including them at the bottom of shows, for ease.
Many have asked about the webpage. It'll get updated when I can. :) If not earlier, I plan on spending Tuesday evening working on the page. Apologies for getting behind on that... but I got behind on
everything. :) Oh, and one more thing... I've said this before, and it's repeated in the FAQ, but I need to say it again... OSW is an indy fed. 75% of it's entire budget is set aside for wrestler contracts, because the focus is not on the flash, and not simply on the wrestling (sorry, Lew ;))... the focus is on the WRESTLERS and the RING. If you want to get something done... you MUST do it in the ring. We do NOT have a jumbotron. If you do something backstage, there will be no camera to see it. The fans won't know it happened. They're there to watch the ring, fans don't come to wrestling shows to watch more TV. If you want to get something said or done... say it on the flash shows or do it in the ring. Otherwise, it doesn't get seen.
As a corollary to that... let me point out something that was commented on in the card, and is also stated in the FAQ. I will only allow a maximum of THREE (3) in-rings a week. Originally that was just for people not involved... but it's changed to 3 per week, period. OSW isn't going to turn into a flash show. There are MANY exceptions to this rule (joint in-rings where someone calls another wrestler out, for example, will be tolerated because it is more than just talking... the confrontation is there for the fans to see), so talk to me if you have an idea. And having your wrestler say a few words after his entrance when he has a match is also fine, because it's promotion of the upcoming match. But NEVER assume that just because you send in an in-ring, it will get used. If I get more than three, I will pick the three most relevant, and use them.
And another note... we're small. The OSW Schoolhouse (a remodeled warehouse in Portland) holds about 1,000 people. If you use fireworks, you will burn down the Schoolhouse. No pyros. Spotlights are OK, having the lights go out is OK, and the P.A. system sounds great... but that's about it.
Anyway, that covers the basics. If anyone needs to talk to me, you know my e-mail. A good card last Insession... but I'm expecting big things this Insession. ;) I'll let you get to the update...
everything you need, right in one place. Wow. Oh, and BTW, to appease the masses... it's time to get a title into the flow... ;) - President [name withheld] [detail withheld] 2. News ======== NEW ALLIANCE FORMED! Def Bellows and Chris Colton shocked the OSW world at Insession, when Bellows and Colton assisted each other throughout the Four
Corners match against Brian Anthony and Brett Young just enough to ensure they were the final two remaining, before Bellows let Colton pin him after the PUNCH OF DOOM~! to give Chris Colton the first birth in the OSW Television Title Tourney. The amazing part of the alliance is how well Colton and Bellows hid their intentions, as they seemed to want nothing more than to annihilate each other for much of the
Insession card before joining forces in the end. Without a doubt, all of Old-School Wrestling is taking notice of this new power alliance in
OSW. TWO MASKED MEN?!? Kevin Fontaine, for the second Insession in a row, lost his match due to help from the masked man who's been stalking him and Brian
Anthony. However, this time, the mysterious attacker was assisted by a SECOND masked man, who attacked Fontaine while he was focused on the first masked man. The rumors are really flying now that there are two masked men to consider, and no one is more interested than Fontaine and Anthony, who seem to be the focus points of these attacks.
LEW SPECTRE FORFEITS TV TITLE SHOT! After a great back and forth battle between Lew Spectre and Dark
Soul, featuring Spectre attempting to get himself disqualified numerous times... Spectre was finally able to get himself disqualified as he threw Referee Bob Walter, who was attempting to keep the match going for as long as possible, out of the ring while Dark Soul had
OSW's "Franchise" locked in a kata hajime. The end result... Dark
Soul moves on to the OSW Television Title Tourney via disqualification.
After the match, Spectre explained his reasoning for getting himself disqualified. He, among other things, claimed that OSW
President Michael Ice is trying to "hold Spectre down" by putting him in the Television Title Tourney, instead of waiting for the Old-School
Title to be introduced. Dark Soul then took a huge bump to the outside of the ring, courtesy of a Gorilla Press from Spectre's 6'11",
400+ pound bodyguard Big Sir. Needless to say... Soul is none to happy about the whole situation.
DIME NOTES GET REVENGE... THEN GET ATTACKED! The Dime Notes, who last Insession were ravagely attacked by the odd Workshop, got their revenge this week in spoiling the Workshop's
OSW debut. However, after the match the Workshop got the last laugh, taking out Darby Sharpe and Justin Simmons with the Collector's cane.
This one doesn't look to be over, in fact long from it. 3. Roster ========== COMING: "Fantastic" Frank Knight, ???
