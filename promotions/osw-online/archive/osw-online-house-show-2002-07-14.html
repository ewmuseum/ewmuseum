Opening match: Superstar vs. "Shooting Star" Ryan Douglas Debut match A mixed reaction for Douglas as he comes out with Kaitlyn Starr, a small cheer for Superstar for those who recognize him from the indy's. Match starts with Douglas stalling for time, but Superstar interrupts with a missile dropkick to start things off, throwing
Douglas's timing off. Superstar followed up with some nice high armdrags and then another dropkick, sending Douglas up and over the top rope.
Superstar posed on the top rope, getting a surprising cheer from the crowd. I think that even surprised him. That was shortlived as Douglas came in and started off with a classic, or should I say "Old School" thumb to the eye. A couple of stiff shots gets a warning from referee Bob Walter and then he follows that up with a throw to the ropes that Superstar ducks. He comes back with a flying cross body but Douglas actually catches him and powerslams him down for a one and a half count. Douglas took control of the match for a bit, using a lot of high impact moves that almost broke Superstar in half.
One of them was a crushing tilt-a-whirl backbreaker that he followed up with a senton bomb from the top rope, but even that got a two count.
Then he made his mistake and went for a back bodydrop after throwing Supes into the ropes. Superstar hooked him for a dangerous looking DDT and followed that up with a standing somersault senton bomb. Douglas went to throw him into the ropes again, but Superstar caught himself and came back with a big clothesline that had Douglas doing a 360 in the air. Superstar got him to his feet, then jumped from the second turnbuckle to the top rope to some kind of twisting arm drag off the top rope that sent Douglas halfway across the ring. I don't think I've ever seen a move like that before. Douglas got up quickly but Superstar
school-boyed him for a two count. Cunningham rings in, telling us that we're already ten minutes into the match. Damn. Superstar and Douglas are really gelling together in the ring.
Superstar continued to show why he was a big thing in the indies, as he dominated Douglas for the next few minutes, almost getting a three count on a fisherman's suplex. Douglas came back with a surprise neckbreaker, but he wasn't able to capitalize as Superstar nailed a snap suplex and followed up with a high atomic drop. Douglas reversed an irish whip and went for a clothesline, but Superstar jumped up, hooked both his arms with his own arms and legs and rolled him over for a surprise three count! A nice reaction from the crowd, and if nobody minds, at least a *** match from these two. A nice opener that ended at 11:06, longer than it seemed.
