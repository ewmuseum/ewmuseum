Old-School Wrestling has a new Television Champion... Def Bellows. Bellows, also the Old-School Heavyweight Champion, first came out to fire his stablemate, Chris Colton, from the Superiority Complex. This brought out OSW President Michael Ice, who scheduled a match between
Colton and Bellows later on in the show, with both Bellows' Old-School and Colton's Television Titles on the line. However, the promised blockbuster was not to be, as Chris Colton was replaced by a midget counterpart, who somehow was wearing the Television Title. Referee
Tony Corolla went on to allow the match, which ended quickly and with
Corolla awarding the Television Title to Bellows. Referee Corolla has since been reprimanded for the decision, however OSW officials claim the decision is final, and Bellows will be allowed to hold both titles, proven he lives up to all the title defense responsibilities... the first of which is against "Fallen Angel" David
Cross this Insession, who has gained something of a reputation for pulling off upsets, with victories over Tyro Marvel and Lew Spectre on his resume.
TORITSU REPS MAKE PRESENCE KNOWN It had been little secret the last couple weeks that OSW had been discussing a deal with foreign investors, but few had any idea what kind of impact the representatives of the Toritsu Corporation would have. The Japanese Contingent, former GFWA World Tag Team Champions who had been all but black-balled from the sport after a previous attempt at taking over UWF, came forth as the official Toritsu spokesmen in OSW, joining forces with past ally Gran Sumara X for a hostile attack on Kazuyuki Saga. This came after the JC forced the returning High Society out of the match, and post-match High Society returned to help put a stop to the brawl, as did "Madison J.
Valentine"... but it quickly became obvious that the Japanese Contingent holds some serious stroke in Old-School Wrestling... and only time will tell if that ends up to be a good thing.
MADISON J. VALENTINE IN OSW? In an OSW Update-exclusive, it's been revealed that the man who joined High Society in fighting off the Japanese Contingent was, in fact, NOT the famed Madison J. Valentine... but rather his brother,
McLeod. A few fans and OSW officials in attendance last Insession recognized McLeod, best known for a stint in Knoxville Championship
Wrestling in 2001. Valentine has not been heard from since, and little is known as for the reasons for his appearance, as well as coming in under his brother's name... however, McLeod Valentine has promised to inform the fans of his reasonings on OSW Flashback, so be sure to tune in.
TIGER PAWS UPSET LORDS OF DARKNESS In what would have to be considered an upset, the Tiger Paws defeated the Lords of Darkness last Insession. The Lords of Darkness had been steamrolling their opponents since entering OSW, but saw their undefeated streak snapped at the hands of Ryan Lucero. The Paws were said to be quite jubilant after the victory, while the Lords of Darkness were heard by an onlooker to have promised retribution... 3.
