Recent Results 03/19/02 — OSW Insession — OSW Schoolhouse, Portland, OR Television Title Tourney Qualifier * Magnus Colby def. Crisis via submission with a Boston Crab. Crisis injured his knee in the match. * The Workshop def. The Glam Boys via pinfall after the Workshop Drop. * Dark Soul def. Kevin Fontaine via pinfall after the Soul Crusher. Television Title Tourney Qualifier * Def Bellows def. Brett Young via pinfall after reversing an inside cradle. ~=MAIN EVENT=~ * Lew Spectre def. Brian Anthony via pinfall after hitting a Domination Factor. 02/26/02 — OSW Insession — OSW Schoolhouse, Portland, OR * Sexual Energy def. The Glam Boys via pinfall after hitting Feels So Good on Kenny Jacquet. * Magnus Colby def. Kevin Fontaine via pinfall after Fontaine was attacked by two masked men. * The Dime Notes def. The Workshop via pinfall after Darby Sharpe hit a Front Burner from the top rope on Toy. Television Title Tourney Qualifier * Chris Colton won a Four Corners Match: - Brian Anthony was eliminated via disqualification. - Def Bellows def. Brett Young via pinfall after hitting the Definator. - Chris Colton def. Def Bellows via pinfall when Bellows "allowed" Colton to pin him after only one punch. ~=MAIN EVENT=~ Television Title Tourney Qualifier * Dark Soul def. Lew Spectre via disqualification when Spectre assaulted Referee Bob Walter while in a kata hajime. 02/05/02 — OSW Insession — OSW Schoolhouse, Portland, OR OSW Grand-Opening Match * Dark Soul def. Def Bellows via pinfall after hitting the Soul Crusher. * Crisis won a Four Corners Match: - Jim Sergeant def. Jonathon Talon via pinfall after hitting the Boston Massacre. - Crisis def. Jonny Storm via pinfall after a legdrop. - Crisis def. Jim Sergeant via submission with the New Wave. * Chris Colton def. Steve Johnson via pinfall with some assistance from his manager, Lindsay. After the match, Steve Johnson suffered a neck injury. * The Dime Notes and Brett Young/Brian Anthony wrestled to a no-decision after The Workshop interfered to attack the Dime Notes. ~=MAIN EVENT=~ * Lew Spectre def. Kevin Fontaine via submission with the Star-Island Strangehold after a masked man attacked Fontaine. ©2002 Old-School Wrestling Productions
