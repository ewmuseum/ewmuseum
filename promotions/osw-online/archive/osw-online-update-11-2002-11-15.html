From the President's Desk 2. News 3. Roster 4. Upcoming Card 5. Deadlines 6. Rankings 7. Final Notes 1. From the President's Desk ============================= OK, Insession's out, here's the Update. Sorry it's a day late, but it was unavoidable. Now, onto the important stuff, eh?
First... it should be noted that this is the first week we are going into the duel card format, making use of BOTH the House Show and
Insession. Big thanks to Don, who has agreed to continue writing the format for the House Show that we all enjoyed so much. As a reminder, and so the newer handlers know... Insession is the normal full play-by-play card, while the House Show is only a summary card. LIMIT
OF TWO (2) IN-RINGS ON THE HOUSE SHOWS! Period. In-character, OSW is not only going to allow [name withheld] to use video of in-rings, but they will PROVIDE him with clips from two in-rings (no matches, fans gotta come to the arena for those). Consider it a promotional deal with an internet wrestling writer. OOC, I just don't want it to become a third flash show. :)
Next up... I NEED WRITERS! Come on, guys, no more delays. 3 writers to help with Insession matches this week (I will write the House Show matches every week). I already have one, so jump on board and help out! I swear to god we can get a card out on time, and just think...
we'd be the first RSE fed to do it in months! (Just kidding, Mark ;)) Third... this weekend will be devoted to getting the website back in shape. Look for updates galore there, and maybe even a few format changes. First priority is bios and contact lists, so before you write me an e-mail telling me to start there, I'll save ya the time.
;) I believe that covers the majority of things. Schedules are below (#4), with deadlines just below that (#5). If you don't have an angle and are looking for one, please get ahold of me and I'll help ya out. We have a lot of newer wrestlers on the roster, so there's plenty of people for you to work with. I will be updating the webpage this week, including the Contacts page... but if you want/need an e-mail sooner, let me know. With us kicking back into gear, it's the PERFECT time to get involved in an angle and start giving it some attention. It keeps you more interested, it keeps me more interested, and it raises the quality of the fed in general... so don't be afraid to ask. :)
And now for the obligatory Update reminders... ;) I've said this before, and it's repeated in the FAQ, but I need to say it again...
OSW is an indy fed. 75% of it's entire budget is set aside for wrestler contracts, because the focus is not on the flash, and not simply on the wrestling (sorry, Lew ;))... the focus is on the
WRESTLERS and the RING. If you want to get something done... you MUST do it in the ring. We do NOT have a jumbotron. If you do something backstage, there will be no camera to see it. The fans won't know it happened. They're there to watch the ring, fans don't come to wrestling shows to watch more TV in OSW. If you want to get something said or done... say it on the flash shows or do it in the ring.
Otherwise, it doesn't get seen. As a corollary to that... let me point out something that was commented on in the card, and is also stated in the FAQ. I will only allow a maximum of THREE (3) in-rings a week. Originally that was just for people not involved... but it's changed to 3 per week, period. Insession isn't going to turn into a flash show. There are
MANY exceptions to this rule (joint in-rings where someone calls another wrestler out, for example, will be tolerated because it is more than just talking... the confrontation is there for the fans to see), so talk to me if you have an idea. And having your wrestler say a few words after his entrance when he has a match is also fine, because it's promotion of the upcoming match. But NEVER assume that just because you send in an in-ring, it will get used. If I get more than three, I will pick the three most relevant, and use them. If you have ANY DOUBTS WHATSOEVER, contact me first to secure an in-ring spot on the card.
And another note... we're small. The OSW Schoolhouse (a remodeled warehouse in Portland) holds about 1,000 people. If you use fireworks, you will burn down the Schoolhouse. No pyros. Spotlights are OK, having the lights go out is OK, and the P.A. system sounds great... but that's about it.
Any questions or anything, of course, feel free to contact me. - President [name withheld] [detail withheld] 2. News ======== REYNOLDS AND BARDON EARN TAG TITLE SHOT In the Eight-Man Main Event last Insession, Calvin Reynolds pinned Andrew Stevens to earn his team another shot at Sexual Energy and the OSW Unified Tag Team Titles. The match also featured solid performances by Old-School Champion Def Bellows and his rival Dark
Soul, among others. Noticeable by his absence, however, was Television Champion Chris Colton, who was to defend his Television Title in the match. As punishment for the unexplained no-show, Colton has been forced into a non-title match this Insession... against
"Fantastic" Frank Knight! NATE KNOWITALL APPEARS IN OSW The scheduled match was the Tiger Paws against the Hillside Confessional, but it didn't exactly happen that way. The Hillside
Confessional never showed up... instead out "came" Preston Mayfield, who took a ringside seat. It would turn out the seat was to watch the appearance of WWO superstar Nate Knowitall appearing in OSW and destroying the Tiger Paws, with some help from Mayfield. The link between Mayfield and Knowitall is not known, however Knowitall is officially on the OSW roster, and will be making his OSW debut at the next House Show. And on the next Insession, Mayfield and Knowitall will team with one another as the Tiger Paws seek some retribution!
MORE NEW SIGNEES Despite the delays, OSW continues to attract attention from free agents interested in one of the hotter regional federations in the business. Recent signees scheduled to debut this week are Nate Knowitall, "Sabotage" Taj Collins and the team of Murder Inc. In addition, it's known that there are at least two more contracts signed that have yet to be announced to the public yet, and rumors are swirling as to the identities these two newly signed talents. OSW C.E.O. Jeff Cunningham had little to say on the issue, but did offer a rather cryptic clue: "One name will be familiar to those who've known me for a while, and the other is an unretirement that potentially could shock the entire business." Rankings ============ Courtesy of "The Voice of OSW", Jeff Cunningham. Last Change: Update #10 (yes, I'm THAT lazy ;)) Who's Hot: ---------- 1. Superiority Complex (Chris Colton/Def Bellows/Sexual Energy) Def Bellows... OSW Old-School Champion. Chris Colton... OSW
Television Champion. Sexual Energy... OSW Unified Tag Team Champions.
The SC holds all three OSW titles, and will hold this position until that fact changes. 2. "Fantastic" Frank Knight His only two losses came in a three-way match Knight in which he wasn't involved in the finish, and to Kaz Saga, when he was DQ'ed after already being announced the victor. Uncle Frank has become an ominous presence in OSW, and is undeniably the one man who, above all others, strikes fear into the hearts of his opponents.
3. Kazuyaki Saga Became an instant fan-favorite with the spirit and desire displayed in the Old-School Title tourney, despite the odds being stacked against him every step of the way. The kid took on Chris
Colton, Frank Knight and Def Bellows (all ranked "hotter"), in one night, and lived to tell about it.
4. Reynolds and Bardon Came out of nowhere to nearly become the Unified Tag Team Champions. Solid victories over the 3T0 Irish Mafia and the dangerous
High Society International overshadows the fact that they lost to
Sexual Energy.
5. Dark Soul Despite the loss to Bellows in the Old-School Title tourney. Soul has put together a record to be proud of. Some are starting to question his "big match ability", however, garning the low ranking.
Who's Not: ---------- 1. Superstar Has been unspectacular thus far in the eyes of many, and looked unfocused in the Battle Royal. Superstar has the talent necessary to turn things around, but the question is... will he?
2. The Dime Notes Surprisingly, the Dime Notes have really looked out of focus as of late, and lost their rematch against Sexual Energy in the Joint Tag
Team Title unification match. With the recent influx of tag teams...
are the Dime Notes on the verge getting lost in the fold? 3. "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre Um... what? Brawling? From Spectre? It's more apparent now than ever that Spectre is lost. focus-wise, and OSW officials are carefully watching every move Spectre makes, to ensure things don't get out of control.
4. "Big Gun" Jim Sergeant Despite being loved by the fans, Sergeant has been out-performed his last couple of matches. The Big Gun is looking to turn things around, but the path is far from short.
5. "Whiskey" Jack Collins He won't be here next Update. However, Collins' record is less-than-impressive, despite his ability to get under the skin of the
Television Champion, Chris Colton. Collins is still a great performer though, and only gets this spot due to the pre-Field Trip roster cleanout.
Most Popular ------------ 1. Dark Soul By the end of the Bellows/Soul match, you'd think the crowd was ready to riot. Soul is the world's favorite underdog, though he's been shut out when it counts.
2. Kazuyaki Saga Giving Soul a real run for his money in the "favorite underdog" category. Saga's courage under fire and willingness to step into the ring with Def Bellows after being beaten around repeatedly during the
Field Trip, Saga bought himself a place in the hearts of OSW fans.
3. "Whiskey" Jack Collins Helped Kaz Saga end Chris Colton's losing streak at the Field
Trip, and saved Saga from a post-match beatdown by Colton. Collins has seemed to get under Colton's skin more than anyone has thus far.
4. Calvin "Earth Warrior" Reynolds Despite his odd appearance, Reynolds has gained the fans respect the old-fashioned way... clean, fair wrestling. His partner, Albert
Bardon, isn't quite as revered... but the fans love the Earth Warrior.
5. The Dime Notes Come on, really... who DOESN'T like Darby Sharpe? The fans were disappointed in their loss to Sexual Energy at the Field Trip, but they still love these guys tremendously.
Most Hated ---------- 1. Superiority Complex (Chris Colton/Def Bellows/Sexual Energy) You just THOUGHT everyone hated them going into the Field Trip... wait until you hear the reaction from the crowd next Insession, when the Superiority Complex appears with all three OSW titles.
2. "Fantastic" Frank Knight Almost single-handedly turned Kaz Saga into one of the most beloved wrestlers in OSW. Knight is a vicious, maniacal monster who thrives on pain. And besides that... he's just plain scary. Would
YOU want to step into the ring with him?
3. "Phenomenon" Tyro Marvel Masked Man #2? Huh? Marvel was NEVER as hated under the mask as he is now that he's shed it. Took WWO World Champion Dark Soul to the limit in the Old-School Title tourney, and stole a kiss from Candy
Malone. How's that for a night?
4. "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre His freakish run of violence at the Field Trip did little to make
Spectre less hated. In fact, assaulting the fan-favorite Jim
Sergeant, then OSW's head referee, showed that Spectre is as confused as ever... and possibly more dangerous than he's ever been.
5. High Society International Hell, Christopher Black severely hurt his knee, and the fans
CHEERED as he was helped to the back.
