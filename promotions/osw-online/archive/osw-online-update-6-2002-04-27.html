From the President's Desk ============================= Sorry I didn't get this out last night after the card... I intended to, but just didn't. You forgive me. :)
The Flashback deadline is being pushed back one day to Monday night this week, due to the delays. PLEASE NOTE DEADLINES FURTHER BELOW IN
THIS UPDATE. I may be required to *gasp* actually put out a flash show early morning rather than afternoon the next day this week, so no promises that all those late flashes I get hit with will be in in
time. :) Oh, and one more thing... I've said this before, and it's repeated in the FAQ, but I need to say it again... OSW is an indy fed. 75% of it's entire budget is set aside for wrestler contracts, because the focus is not on the flash, and not simply on the wrestling (sorry, Lew ;))... the focus is on the WRESTLERS and the RING. If you want to get something done... you MUST do it in the ring. We do NOT have a jumbotron. If you do something backstage, there will be no camera to see it. The fans won't know it happened. They're there to watch the ring, fans don't come to wrestling shows to watch more TV. If you want to get something said or done... say it on the flash shows or do it in the ring. Otherwise, it doesn't get seen.
As a corollary to that... let me point out something that was commented on in the card, and is also stated in the FAQ. I will only allow a maximum of THREE (3) in-rings a week. Originally that was just for people not involved... but it's changed to 3 per week, period. OSW isn't going to turn into a flash show. There are MANY exceptions to this rule (joint in-rings where someone calls another wrestler out, for example, will be tolerated because it is more than just talking... the confrontation is there for the fans to see), so talk to me if you have an idea. And having your wrestler say a few words after his entrance when he has a match is also fine, because it's promotion of the upcoming match. But NEVER assume that just because you send in an in-ring, it will get used. If I get more than three, I will pick the three most relevant, and use them.
And another note... we're small. The OSW Schoolhouse (a remodeled warehouse in Portland) holds about 1,000 people. If you use fireworks, you will burn down the Schoolhouse. No pyros. Spotlights are OK, having the lights go out is OK, and the P.A. system sounds great... but that's about it.
Any questions or anything, of course, feel free to contact me. - President [name withheld] [detail withheld] 2. News ======== LEW SPECTRE, JIM SERGEANT IN THE OLD-SCHOOL TITLE TOURNEY Two of the most recognizable OSW wrestlers to the common fan,
"Big Gun" Jim Sergeant and "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre, both secured their bids in the OSW Old-School Title Tourney last Insession.
Spectre defeated Brett Young last Insession, while Jim Sergeant pulled off a big victory over Magnus Colby. Those two join Number One
Contender Dark Soul and Television Champion Chris Colton as half of the participants in the tourney to crown the most desired title in all of OSW... the Old-School Title! Another birth will be determined this
Insession, as Frank Knight takes on Jeremy Steele.
TWO CHAMPS IN A FIGHT? Last Insession, Sexual Energy, the self-proclaimed OSW Tag Team
Champions, took offense to Television Champ Chris Colton calling himself the "only champion in OSW". After a war of words, a verbal contract was arranged between Sexual Energy and the team of Chris
Colton and Def Bellows. However, President Michael Ice has declared that the match will NOT take place next Insession, due to Chris
Colton's mandatory Television Title defense. However, Ice was also quoted as saying, "In my eyes, a verbal contract is a binding contract... the Sexual Energy versus Chris
Colton and Def Bellows match will take place the following Insession."
So, the Insession after this one WILL feature a match between Sexual
Energy and Chris Colton/Def Bellows! Colton and Sexual Energy are both in action this Insession as well, Colton defending his title against Brian Anthony, while Sexual Energy "defends" their "titles"
against the "Multi-League Tag Team Champions", Precious Moments! TAG TEAM CHAMPS? The trend started three Insessions ago, when Sexual Energy declared themselves the OSW Tag Team Champions by virtue of their victory over the Glam Boys. This Insession, the number of unofficial
"tag team champions" was increased, as the Dime Notes introduced their
OWN titles, declaring themselves Joint Tag Team Champions with Sexual
Energy.
As well, this Insession, the odd team of Precious Moments came out of right field, debuting their "Multi-League Tag Team Titles" to further confuse the mess.
So, who's REALLY the champions? It's yet to be determined... though expect to see quite a few title defenses before champions are crowned. 3. Upcoming Card ================= For more schedule info, see Deadlines. *~~ OSW INSESSION ~~* 04 / 16 / 02 OSW Schoolhouse, Portland, OR 1. SEXUAL ENERGY vs. PRECIOUS MOMENTS Insession opens with the self-proclaimed "OSW Tag Team Champions" taking on the self-proclaimed "Multi-League Tag Team Champions",
Precious Moments. Two of the three tag team titles in OSW will probably not be on the line in this match... but with seemingly every team in OSW introducing tag team title belts, one can imagine the OSW front office will be watching this match closely as they sort out final details on the OFFICIAL OSW Tag Team Titles.
Debut Match 2. KAZUYUKI SAGA/"HANGMAN" JAMES MARSHALL vs. "FALLEN ANGEL" DAVID CROSS/ROBBIE GALLAGHER The rookies are getting a chance to make a huge splash in OSW, as four wrestlers who've yet to set foot into the OSW ring were randomly paired off into two tag teams. The bonus? The winning team will fight EACH OTHER on the next Insession... with the winner getting a birth in the OSW Old-School Title Tourney!
3. HIGH SOCIETY INTERNATIONAL vs. THE DIME NOTES The Dime Notes, the OSW Joint Tag Team Champions, defend their belts against one of the few teams to not introduce titles to OSW,
High Society International, who were aided to a victory last week by
Lew Spectre. Again, with the inaugural OSW Tag Team Championships on the horizon, every match is important in a solid, if small, division.
OSW Old-School Title Tourney Qualifier 4. JEREMY STEELE vs. "FANTASTIC" FRANK KNIGHT Both Steele and Knight have looked very impressive in their debuts the last two Insessions... but one of them has to lose. Because only one of them can get into the Old-School Title Tourney. Two classic styles will clash in this match, which should bring a new meaning to the term "old-school".
5. DARK SOUL vs. "DARKHORSE" DERREK JOHNSON Dark Soul, the Number One Contender to the yet-to-be-crowned OSW
Old-School Title, takes on the man who put Kevin Fontaine on the injured list, Derrek Johnson. Can Soul begin another winning streak, or will the Darkhouse start one of his own?
>*~ MAIN EVENT ~*< OSW Television Title Match 6. "SHOCKER" BRIAN ANTHONY vs. "RIVERSIDE THRILLER" CHRIS COLTON (c) The OSW Television Title is on the line, as Chris Colton makes his first defense against Brian Anthony. Anthony has been a bit rusty as of late, but there's no better way to turn around your successes than winning the Television Title. However, Chris Colton has yet to lose in OSW, including his Television Title victory over Dark Soul, and doesn't plan on starting anytime soon.
5. Deadlines ============= My deadlines policy is simple. Get it in on time, it gets used. Get it in late, you're taking your chances. If I can fit it in without doing a ton of extra work, then I might, but if you REALLY want it on the show... get it in on time. We're going bi-weekly, you have plenty of time. Just to generalize the deadlines... all flashes are due the night BEFORE the flash show. :) NOTE: ALL deadlines are at 11:59 p.m. PST that night. Monday, April 29: Flashes for OSW Flashback Thursday, May 2: Flashes for OSW Interactive Thursday, May 2: Strats for OSW Insession (unless other arrangements are made with me in advance... and you better have a good reason to need longer than a week to strat ;)) 6.
