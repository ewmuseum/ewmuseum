Looking to fill out roster...

23 March 2002

Old-School Wrestling is looking to round out its roster with a little more talent. We have a few open spots for both faces and heels, and could specifically use a good foreign heel and a comedy face, and could use a couple more tag teams as well. Since the fed is run by tag teamers Michael Ice and Alex Perkins (Fire and Ice of WWO, UEW,
SCRA, CCW, etc), the tag team division is something that I hope will become truly solid.
OSW does have a solid roster, both singles and tag, established, but we're looking for a few handlers who like a politic-free, fun fed that's based around good angle work and a traditional feel on the main show.
The website, OSW Online, was just recently updated, as was the OSW
FAQ, which can be found on the webpage. You can also find all our past shows under the OSW Archives, as well as any other info you may be seeking. And if there's any questions I can answer, please feel free to e-mail me...
