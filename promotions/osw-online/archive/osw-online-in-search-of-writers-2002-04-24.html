OK... the only thing really holding Old-School Wrestling back from being a really entertaining product is the writing staff. I have three full-time writers... but I could use one or two more, so I don't have to turn to the emergency staff as often.
If you are capable of writing a 10-15k match in full play-by-play format and are looking to write... I could use a hand. We have a solid roster, and a ton of awesome angles going right now... but not enough people to help write them.
Basically... five matches a card isn't enough for a nearly-full roster. I need to expand... but I can't do that without writing help.
We run on a bi-weekly schedule, and as I said my upper card writing staff is solid. I just need one or two more writers to give me an early-card 10-15k match once every two weeks to help keep the cards timely, as I generally have most of the matches done and ready to go on time... I just need to find one or two more people who can get me matches in a timely manner. :)

MORE TAG TEAMS. After filling these spots, we will be closing our roster. Application is on the webpage, addy can be found below.
So, if you've been following our work and enjoy it, but aren't handling here... you only have a little more time to get me in that application before I make some final decisions. :)
Oh, and if you handle a character AND can write bi-weekly... you get special bonus points. :)
