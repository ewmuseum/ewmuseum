The Staff of OSW

The Staff of OSW Michael Ice The co-owner of OSW and an active wrestler, Michael Ice is also the president and head booker. He's the man in charge, and unlike many presidents, he's actually fair... as long as you don't piss him off. Alex Perkins The other co-owner, Perkins is the acting Vice President of OSW, and is also head of security. A monster of a man (6'8", 290) and a very experienced wrestler himself, he has no problems breaking up a fight if it gets excessively out of hand and the rest of security can't handle it. Sam Collins The play-by-play man for OSW, Collins is a rookie to the booth. His knowledge of wrestling is incredible though, and often makes up for his lack of experience. "Cool" Keith Robinson A long-time wrestler who has been color commentating since 1995, Robinson is the voice of experience on OSW broadcasts. He is a huge fan of old-school wrestling, and isn't afraid to share his rather opinionated, yet very intelligent, remarks often. Some say he also has some booking power in OSW, being old friends with Ice and Perkins, but that's an unconfirmed rumor... Jeff Cunningham The most unbiased, straight-forward reporter you have ever met. Cunningham has perfected his craft, and in addition to the resident interviewer in OSW, Cunningham's sharp, crisp voice will introduce the wrestlers to the ring as well. Referee Bob Walter One of the two referees on the OSW staff, Walter is an unbelievably good referee, having honed his skills for years. Rulebreakers will have a tough time sneaking in cheap shots with Walter in the ring, and "Old Bob" isn't afraid to call for the DQ. He seems to always be right on top of the action, in the right place at the right time as if it were a sixth sense. Referee Tony Corolla The other referee in OSW, Corolla... well, he's certainly NOT Bob Walter. Corolla seems to fit the "greasy Italian" stereotype perfectly, and is far from the most talented referee. He's strong, and won't take a bump for anything... but he'll never count you out, rarely DQ anyone, and some say he can even be bribed. ©2002 Old-School Wrestling Productions
