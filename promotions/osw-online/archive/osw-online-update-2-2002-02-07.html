From the President's Desk 2. News 3. Roster 4. Upcoming Card 5. Deadlines 6. Final Notes 1. From the President's Desk ============================= Sorry for this being late guys. I could go into the reasons... but
I'll save you the hassle this time. ;) Due to this being late, I'm going to extend Flashback one more day, to Sunday instead of it's usual Saturday. Please make note of the deadline this week.
As well, I thought it was a very soild card. The size was small, I noticed... but that will increase with time, once things start to get running a little more smoothly. Give me a few mistakes... I haven't put together a card in close to two years. ;)
Now... with the first card out of the way... I'm focusing on feuds and helping those of you who aren't involved in one get one started.
Now... most of you have already gone off on your own little tangents, which is what made the first card seem a little WWF-ish (and drove Lew simply crazy in #osw ;)). I had to get all those started, and I wanted to do it all on one night to get things set up. But things should run a little more smoothly from here on out, and if you're not working something that I know about... expect to hear from me soon. ;)
Oh, and one more thing... I've said this before, and it's repeated in the FAQ, but I need to say it again... OSW is an indy fed. 75% of it's entire budget is set aside for wrestler contracts, because the focus is not on the flash, and not simply on the wrestling (sorry, Lew ;))... the focus is on the WRESTLERS and the RING. If you want to get something done... you MUST do it in the ring. We do NOT have a jumbotron. If you do something backstage, there will be no camera to see it. The fans won't know it happened. They're there to watch the ring, fans don't come to wrestling shows to watch more TV. If you want to get something said or done... say it on the flash shows or do it in the ring. Otherwise, it doesn't get seen.
As a corollary to that... let me point out something that was commented on in the card, and is also stated in the FAQ. I will only allow a maximum of THREE (3) in-rings a week. Originally that was just for people not involved... but it's changed to 3 per week, period. OSW isn't going to turn into a flash show. There are MANY exceptions to this rule (joint in-rings where someone calls another wrestler out, for example, will be tolerated because it is more than just talking... the confrontation is there for the fans to see), so talk to me if you have an idea. And having your wrestler say a few words after his entrance when he has a match is also fine, because it's promotion of the upcoming match. But NEVER assume that just because you send in an in-ring, it will get used. If I get more than three, I will pick the three most relevant, and use them.
And another note... we're small. The OSW Schoolhouse (a remodeled warehouse in Portland) holds about 5,000 people. If you use fireworks, you will burn down the Schoolhouse. No pyros. Spotlights are OK, having the lights go out is OK, and the P.A. system sounds great... but that's about it. I slipped and let someone use fireworks last week... but consider that an illusion that will NOT be seen again. ;)
Anyway, that covers the basics. If anyone needs to talk to me, you know my e-mail. A good first card... but we'll get it MUCH better. ;)
I'll let you get to the update... everything you need, right in one place. Wow. Oh, and BTW, to appease the masses... it's time to get a title into the flow... ;) - President [name withheld] [detail withheld] 2. News ======== TV CHAMPION TO BE CROWNED! President Michael Ice has announced a four-wrestler tournament to be held for the OSW Television Title, the first OSW Title to be put into circulation. Two matches this week will be for spots in that tournament, and next Insession the final two spots will be decided.
The Insession after that, there will be a special one-night tournament for the OSW Television Title.
FIRST OSW MAIN EVENT ENDS IN CONFUSION What was a very good match between "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine and "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre ended in controversy, as Spectre forced Fontaine to submit to the Star-Island Stranglehold after
Fontaine was attacked by a masked man. The man, who also attacked
"Shocker" Brian Anthony earlier in the night, claimed a history with
Fontaine. Rumors are abound as to who the masked man may be, but no one seems to know anything concrete at this time.
DARK SOUL WINS FIRST-EVER OSW MATCH! An incredible OSW Grand-Opening Match ended when Dark Soul escaped a Def Bellows onslaught long enough to hit a vicious Soul
Crusher for the victory. The match, which was under the no-interference stipulation established by President Michael Ice, was just as exciting as everyone anticipated it would be.
STEVE JOHNSON INJURED! Steve "Iceman" Johnson, who was attacked by Chris Colton after their match because of interaction between Johnson and Colton's manager, Lindsey, suffered back and neck injuries in that assault. No official word on how severe Johnson's injuries are, but we're not expecting to see him in an OSW ring anytime soon.
THE WORKSHOP RUINS TAG MATCH What was shaping up to be an incredible match between the Dime
Notes and the team of "Shocker" Brian Anthony and "Fantasy" Brett
Young was ruined when the Workshop came to the ring, intent on attacking the Dime Notes, who've claimed to be the "cornerstone" of the OSW tag team division. With help from Young and Anthony, the
Workshop was fought off, but the match was left as a no-contest.
President Ice has announced that a re-match between the two teams will be rescheduled at a later Insession.

Upcoming Card ================= For more schedule info, see Deadlines. *~~ OSW INSESSION ~~* 02 / 18 / 02 OSW Schoolhouse, Portland, OR 1. SEXUAL ENERGY vs. THE GLAM BOYS Two teams new to OSW debut in the first match. Sexual Energy has been quite vocal lately, and look to be quite focused. The recently unretired Glam Boys look to prove otherwise, as both teams look to get a win in a wide-open tag team division.
2. MAGNUS COLBY vs. "DELINQUENT" KEVIN FONTAINE Colby begins his OSW career in a tough situation... facing Kevin
Fontaine after his screwjob loss to Lew Spectre. Fontaine still has to watch his back for this masked man though, who has yet to reveal himself but has obvious history with Fontaine. Colby could sneak in and steal a huge victory in his first OSW match, but Fontaine is going to make him earn it.
3. THE WORKSHOP vs. THE DIME NOTES Last Insession, the Workshop brutally attacked the Dime Notes during their match with Brian Anthony and Brett Young. This
Insession, the Dime Notes get their opportunity for payback. Will the
"cornerstone's" of the OSW tag team division get their retribution, or will the Workshop turn the match into a repeat of the beating last week?
Four Corners Match, Winner gets in 4-Man TV Title Tourney 4. "SHOCKER" BRIAN ANTHONY vs. "FANTASY" BRETT YOUNG vs. "RIVERSIDE THRILLER" CHRIS COLTON vs. DEF BELLOWS Anthony and Young proved last week that there is a lasting respect between the two of them. However, how will they do when facing each other? And, for that matter, will it even be important in the same ring as Def Bellows and Chris Colton? No alliances are safe and anything can happen when a shot at a title is on the line.
>*~ MAIN EVENT ~*< Winner gets in 4-Man TV Title Tourney 5. DARK SOUL vs. "STRICTLY BUSINESS" LEW SPECTRE The winners of arguably the two biggest matches of the first
Insession will go one-on-one for a spot in the OSW Television Title
Tourney. Both men took huge first steps in their OSW careers... this match will likely determine the favorite to win the Television Title.
5. Deadlines ============= My deadlines policy is simple. Get it in on time, it gets used. Get it in late, you're taking your chances. If I can fit it in without doing a ton of extra work, then I might, but if you REALLY want it on the show... get it in on time. We're going bi-weekly, you have plenty of time.
Note that the Flashback deadline is a day later than usual this week. My fault, not yours. :) Also notice the strat deadline. You've still got a week, don't worry. ;) But that's a permanent deadline... get used to it. ;)
Saturday, 11:59 p.m. EST: Flashes for OSW Flashback Tuesday, 11:59 p.m. EST: Flashes for OSW Interactive Thursday, 11:59 p.m. EST: Strats for OSW Insession (unless other arrangements are made with me in advance... and you better have a good reason to need longer than a week to strat ;))
