Steve "Iceman" Johnson Height: 6'5" Weight: 255 lbs. Hometown: Tulsa, OK (originally from Lawton) Alignment: Face Strengths: 1. Steve Johnson in his present shape now could be considered a 60 minute man. His endurance has been greatly increased, although he still smokes and drinks beer. 2. Brawling. First and foremost, while Steve is an excellent technical wrestler, his money still comes from his brawling ability. He can give just as well as he can take and then some. 3. Ring Intelligence. In general Steve has become a much better tactician in the ring, looking more than one move ahead of himself and not getting caught up in the heat of the moment as often. Weaknesses: 1. High Flying wrestlers still give him a bit of a problem. 2. After Steve's neck was broken in the NCCW, it has been a bit of a weakness as he tends to favor it, taking punishment twice over to keep his neck from being injured again. 3. Still hot headed and easy to light off his temper. Finisher: End of the Line Description: Its a Stone Cold Stunner/Ace Crusher. Setup? Boot to the gut. Wrestling Style: Hm, well he is a extreme type wrestler however if needed he can go and take care of it technical style too. What wrestler(s) is he like? I would say Chris Benoit, Mick Foley, Shawn Michaels, Rock, and Austin. 10 Favorite Moves: 1. Tiger Driver 2. Powerbomb setup to Chokeslam 3. Top Rope Chokeslam 4. Running Lariat 5. Spinebuster 6. Lift Powerbomb (aka Last Ride) 7. Rolling German Suplex 8. Soviet Suplex (aka Rock Bottom) 9. Senton Bomb 10. Standing Sidekick to Jaw Manager: none Description: n/a Info/Background: Um, not too much of a story behind my guy really. He was born in Oklahoma in 1976. Began wrestling at the age of 16 after his parents were killed in a car accident and he hasn't looked back. Has moved on with his life after a nasty breakup and subsequent divorce from his former wife Lori Ann. He left Lawton Oklahoma and moved to Tulsa where he is residing in a 1 bedroom apartment. Ever since the CCW fell, he hasn't wrestled but has been working out every day and is possibly in the best shape of his life. He even went back to a wrestling school in the midwest and had several intense brush up classes to get him more refined in the technical aspect of the game. He looks forward to getting back in the ring and releasing the aggressions he had recently built up out... Physical Description: Long jet black hair that falls in his face, but does not block out his view. He has piercing blue eyes, a dark brown goatee, and a very strong jawline. He is a muscular man, but not so muscular that his build detracts from his speed. He has a tattoo of a bleeding skull on his right shoulder, and a tattoo of barbed wire wrapped around his left bicep. On his back, he has a tattoo of a bizarre pattern, similar to that of Saturn from WWF. His body is covered with scar tissue from his many battles. His back is the main part of his scarring as he has scars from thumbtacks in his back, barbed wire, canes, and fire ropes. His chest has some scars on it, mainly from leather strap matches. His attire worn to the ring consists of a pair of baggy black jeans, no shirt, and black combat boots. Ring Attire: His ring attire consists of a pair of baggy black jeans, no shirt, and black combat boots Theme Music: "More Human Than Human" By Rob Zombie Sample Entrance: [Lights go out. "More Human Than Human" By Rob Zombie begins to play and a red colored spotlight hits the curtains. As the music begins to pick up pace, and the fans begin to get louder with the music Steve Johnson steps out from behind the curtains and begins a methodical walk to the ring, his eyes never leaving his opponent's center of mass. Steve is wearing his normal baggy black jeans, and black leather army boots. His jet black hair dangles in front of his face, obscuring most of his facial features, but leaving his Oakly M-Frames to be seen as the light dances off of them. He gets to the ring and trudges around it looking at the fans, occasionally he reaches out and slaps a hand. He walks by the annoouncers and nods at them before he climbs into the ring. He goes to all four corners, slowly raising his arms to the crowd before turning around, telling his opponent to get out of the way as he bounces off the ropes getting a feel for them. He removes his sunglasses, looks at whoever is unlucky enough to be in the ring with him and motions for them to "Just Bring It".] ©2002 Old-School Wrestling Productions
