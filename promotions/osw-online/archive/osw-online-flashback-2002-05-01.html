He's wearing a white OSW t-shirt and blue jeans, his long, graying-brown hair tied back into a ponytail. Robinson smiles at the camera.]

****************************** == "FANTASTIC" FRANK KNIGHT == ****************************** [The camera opens up to... A room shrouded in darkness apart from a single, small spotlight shining down from above, and in the middle of the circle of light stands a man. Well, isn't _this_ a little bit too familiar to be a coincidence? The man is, not surprisingly, Frank
Knight. And he is obviously using the same setting as last time we saw him on TV, probably because a dark room with a single light doesn't cost a lot of money to set up.
Frank is looking down at the floor, but as the camera zooms in a little he looks up at it, grinning that nasty, little grin of his and the way the light hits his face making him look even more disturbing than usual, probably on purpose. He is a solidly built man in his early thirties with a few days of stubble on his face and short, slightly unkempt blonde hair. He is currently wearing a pair of blue jeans and a T-shirt with the words "Uncle Frank" in blue letters across the chest, then the OSW logo below that and finally, below the logo in smaller print we see written the sentence: "Isn't this fun, boys and girls?"]
Frank: Hello kiddies. [The camera zooms in a little more until we only see Frank from the chest and up. He sounds as cheerful as ever, which in itself is not a good sign.]
Frank: Isn't this _ever_ so exciting, boys and girls? Isn't it _oh_ so interesting how Stevie the Iceman is no more among us? Is it not,
I
dare ask, truly intriguing how Stevie could come back after fighting that jolly nice boy, Chrissy Colton, yet when Uncle Frank was done with him he decided to leave for greener pastures?
[Frank gives a brave attempt at an innocent looking smile. It doesn't really look very innocent at all, when all is said and done though.]
Frank: Uncle Frank honestly doesn't know _why_ Chrissy Colton decided to, to use a phrase popular in this day and time, call Uncle Frank out, but he'll just have to wait in line if he really wants to get rid of your favourite friend, because I have more important and interesting things to take care of first. Things like good, old Jerry Steele.
[Frank waggles his eyebrows at the camera, grinning like a loon.] Frank: Did you see last Insession, kiddies? Well, of course you did. Did you see good, old Jer get the win? Well, naturally you did. With the backslide no less. Now that is something you don't see very often these days, what with all the flying supadupa triple corkscrew whooptee-dooptee powerflipflopbombsplashes, or whatever they call all those fancy suicide attempts. The humble backslide is forgotten. The good, old small package lies by the wayside. The abdominal stretch has been put out to pasture. It's a sad, sad state of affairs, and Uncle
Frank was despairing, yes he was.
[He wipes an imaginary tear from his cheek and gives the viewers an exaggerated look of sadness, then all of a sudden that ugly grin breaks through again.]
Frank: And then Uncle Frank saw something which cheered him up immensely. The backslide being used as it was always intended to.
Gosh, golly and, dare I say it? Jeepers! Uncle Frank's faith in the wrestling business was suddenly restored, all because of your friend and mine, Jerry Steele! Jer, Uncle Frank is in your debt. Yes he is.
Don't try to deny it or make any silly excuses about it not being a big deal. I'm in your debt...
[There's a pause as the camera zooms in on Frank's face, the cheerfully vicious look fully back on his face now.]
Frank: And Uncle Frank _always_ pays his debts. [As we finally fade to black a disturbing sounding chuckle is the last thing we hear.] **** KR: Jerry? Wait, isn't his name Jeremy Steele? [He looks down at a piece of paper.] KR: Eh, they say the thin line between genius and insanity is success... and as long as "Fantastic" Frank Knight continues to tear opponent's limb from limb like he did two Insessions ago against Steve
Johnson, who has since left OSW... Uncle Frank should find some success here in OSW.
[He looks at his paper again.] KR: Well, this next guy is a new guy. Let's see what he's all about, huh? This is "Fallen Angel" David Cross.
******************************** == "FALLEN ANGEL" DAVID CROSS == ******************************** [Fade into what seems to be a brokendown, ramshackle farmhouse. Windows are broken, there's no door in the front, and hey, there goes a tabby cat running across the front of the porch. After staying on the door for a moment, a large man wearing a blue jeans and a leather jacket comes out. This is the 'Fallen Angel'
David Cross.] DC: My Dad always said the things always go in cycles. Corn grows good for a few years, then wheat does. After that, corn comes back, then maybe rice. All depends on the weather, what you do with the crop, all that farmer stuff my Dad knew better than the back of his hand. Not that it mattered when his
pick-up got T-boned by a car out of control. [Quick pause as Cross stands in reflection.] DC: Bad things happen to good people. At least that's what everybody told me, poor lil' boy of twelve at the funeral. But they didn't tell me the whole truth, which is Bad things happen to good people because God's a sun-uv-a-bitch.
[Another pause as Cross walks over and sits down in the rocking chair on the porch.] DC: Doesn't mean I don't believe in him. I'm pissed at him, but that doesn't mean I'm pissed at the whole world because of it. After all, there's too many bad men in this world, somebodies gotta' be a good one.
That's why I got in this business. I grew up watchin' Hogan, Flair, Rock 'n' Roll's, all those guys kickin' ass and takin' names. Hell, after all, there's not exactly a mammoth job market for out of work football players. Even if I did get a higher score than most on the SAT's.
[Quick smile from Cross, then he continues.] DC: I've never been in the big time, but the OSW, it's a start. Bastards like Colton & Spectre, idiots like Sexual Energy. Plenty of asses to kick, and boy am I ready to start. The question is OSW, are you?
**** KR: Heh, I'm sure they are, kid. I'm sure they are. Go ahead, call out Lew, Chris and Sexual Energy... it's your downfall, not mine.
[He smiles.] KR: Gotta give the kid credit for courage, though... or do they call that stupidity? [Shrug.] KR: Here's another new face, Kazuhishi Saga. [A voice, probably the producer's, whispers to Keith.] Producer: It's Kazuyuki. Kazuyuki Saga. KR: Yeah, that too. ******************* == KAZUYUKI SAGA == ******************* [The scene opens in a dark, abandoned warehouse, our main focus on some wooden boxes in the corner of the warehouse. The sound of shuffling movement can be heard and in the shadows, we see a silhouette step out from behind the boxes. Dimness doesn't allow us to make out the identity of the figure, but from what we can see, you'd say this man is around 6'5",
270
lbs. Standing motionless, the figure utters three simple words in a strong foreign accent.]
VOICE: A fighting spirit. [The man starts walking forward and stops underneath one of the faint light bulbs that illuminate parts of the warehouse. The mysterious man's face is covered in white face paint, shoulder length black hair obscuring his face somewhat. Think Brandon Lee in The Crow. Dressed in black tights, fingerless black gloves, black boots, his identity revealed on the black tanktop he is wearing. Printed underneath a round yellow flower in plain white letters is the name "KAZUYUKI SAGA", the name of the newest OSW star and son of the legendary Japanese wrestler SHINJI Saga. Kazuyuki doesn't show any emotion as he looks into the camera with his dark brown eyes, speaking again with his Japanese accent prevalent in his voice.]
KAZUYUKI SAGA: It's something that a lot of current pro-wrestlers in today's society are lacking, and lacking badly. Back when Kaz was a child in Osaka, all the wrestlers Kaz's father worked with showed respect, dignity and nobility.
They used to wrestle _for_ the fans, because they knew without them, there wouldn't be wrestling shows for them to participate in.
How things change, eh? [Saga hangs his shaking head in shame.] KAZUYUKI SAGA: Kaz knows for a fact that most of the wrestlers in my new promotion are wrestling simply for the paycheck. Kaz knows for a fact that there are men in OSW who are wrestling for fame that comes with such a profession. Kaz knows for a fact that those men don't appreciate the art of professional wrestling and how much pleasure can come from executing a perfect maneuver.
Thankfully, Kaz is not like that. [Pauses.] KAZUYUKI SAGA: Kaz doesn't wrestle for money; Kaz wrestles because he loves the sport. If fame comes with wrestling, so be it. Let it be known though that Kaz won't be chasing it, because pursuing recognition has led to the downfall of many men throughout the course of history.
It'll be blissful enough just making a debut in front of a few knowledgeable fans who enjoy wrestling for what it is... a sport, an artform, a lifestyle.
They'll cheer for the good men, and heckle the bad men. That's the way it needs to be and Old-School Wrestling will be the perfect place to start a long, pleasant wrestling career.
It's a place where Kaz will thrive and dominate... [Kazuyuki extends an open palm towards the camera in a "stop" gesture.] KAZUYUKI SAGA: End of Saga. [Fade.] **** KR: Well, one thing's for sure... at least he'll probably be entertaining in the ring. [He shakes his head slightly.] KR: But these overly respectful people bother me. Speaking of which... here are the Dime Notes.
******************** == THE DIME NOTES == ******************** {Fade into a the hallway of a suburban home somewhere on the outskirt of Portland. Our OSW camera crew is just coming through the door into the hallway of the house Justin Simmons's Uncle George which serves as the home away from home for the OSW joint tag team champion Dime
Notes. The hallway is a small simple room done up in simulated wood paneling with a staircase and a end table next to that where sits a phone.
The rumpled moptop Darby Sharpe sits slumped in a cheap department store director's chair talking on the phone. His grey Reel Big Fish t-shirt and ratty old blues jeans looking like they have no clue what an iron is. His teammate, Justin Simmons, looking little better in a
Matchbox 20 tee and jeans matching his partners, is perched pensively on the the bottom steps of the staircase,sneaking quick worried peeks at his partner as the cameraman enters.}
Justin: Hey man, hope this didn't mess you up too badly. Cameraman: So what's up? Why'd you scrap the shoot at the playhouse? Your uncle mentioned the shoot was off but didn't explain why. {Justin nods his head over to his partner.} Justin: Listen in. You'll see for yourself. Cameraman: You sure it's alright? Justin: Normally, I'd say no. But in this case, I don't think Darby will mind. {The camera turns to face Darby in the director's chair. A very brief glance at his face is enough to reveal that the young man's has been ravaged by a hard emotional blow. His eyes red and puffy, his mouth drawn long and low. Gulps of air visibly travelling down his throat as he speaks into the receiver.}
Darby: Yeah, so I heard. Got the news over the transome last night. I tell you, Grandpa, that's the good thing about the Internet. You can't count on the papers to keep you burnt to a crisp on what's happening in the world of the squared circle. But you can with the gees at sites like
1wrestling.com . {As he listens to his grandfather's voice on the other end, Darby starts rubbing his eyes. He sniffles involuntarily as he starts to speak again.}
Darby: Say, Grandpa, could you please send me some of the old matches here? At a time like this, I could do with some Thesz vs Buddy Rogers or "Whipper" Billy Watson right about now. {The camerman turns back to Justin.} Cameraman: So what big news is he talking about? Justin: Oh come on, man. You're IN the wrestling industry. Don't tell me you didn't here about Lou Thesz.
Cameraman: Of course. But you guys are in what? Your early twenties? Justin: Yeah. That's right. Cameraman: Wouldn't think you guys would know of Lou Thesz that well. Justin: Well, I haven't seen many of the man's matches myself. But
Darby's grandfather brought him up on that those classic guys. He's watched Lou Thesz from a very young age. So this is just killing him.
It's like you or I would be if Brody Thunder died. Cameraman: I hear ya. No wonder you called off at the playhouse. Justin: Plus, Thesz lived in Norfolk the last years of his life. And we're from Virginia Beach. We've had opportunities to encounter Thesz as part of our daily lives. In fact, we even waited on him and his wife Charlie a few times when we were working at Freemason Abbey in
Norfolk to make our way through wrestling school. He was a real class act and a great ambassador for wrestling who will be sorely missed.
Cameraman: So no words about your upcoming match with High Society
International? Justin: Afraid not, man. Let me get Darby collected and we maybe can do something in a few days. Right now talking about High Society
International or Sexual Energy or the OSW joint tag team titles would just feel so empty. You know what I mean?
Cameraman: I hear ya. But may I make a suggestion for Darby? Justin: Sure what? Cameraman: Get him a big tub of raisins. I lost my grandfather not too long ago and I can tell ya mourning's murder on the blood sugar. The raisins will help with that.
Justin: Great idea. In fact, I'll follow you out and I can go get them right now. Cameraman: Sounds like a game plan to me. {Fade to black.} **** [A moment of silence.] KR: Moving on... here's Dark Soul. *************** == DARK SOUL == *************** (The scene fades into a Taco Bell, though we can only assume it is since OSW lacks the copyright and all to show the sign so all logos are smudged. Dark Soul sits in a booth, alone, writing something down with Candy Malone across from him digging at a Mexican Pizza with a fork.
Dark Soul is dressed in a green horizontal stripped shirt, the stripes in many different widths. Also, a pair of blue jeans, baggy, and combat boots, barely visible under the wide leg of the pants. His long brown hair is tied back in a ponytail and he seems amazingly focused.
Ms. Malone dresses in...well, what do you care? You're going to imagine her naked anyway. The camera focuses on the pair.)
Ms. Malone: "Do you realize how wrong this is for my diet?" Dark Soul: "Yeah." (He continues to write.) Ms. Malone: "Oh, you gotta match for Insession." Dark Soul: "Yeah." (He erases something before writing again.) Ms. Malone: "I feel like I'm talking to my dad during the hockey playoffs." Dark Soul: "Yeah." (He finally finishes and blows on the piece of paper.) Dark Soul: "I'll be right back." (He gets up and exits the fast-food restaurant via the nearest door. He then proceeds to light the paper on fire and watch it burn until it starts to burn at his hand, from which he drops the paper and stomps on the piece of paper.
He slides his hand together as if to say, "that's done," and goes back inside to sit across from Ms. Malone.)
Ms. Malone: "So...that had a point?" Dark Soul: "Oh, but it did." Ms. Malone: "Funny, cause you didn't order any food...you just came in here and started to write and then...you burned it."
Dark Soul: "I found out very early in my career that if I didn't get out my emotions in a positive way, they would burn me up.
See...that's what that was about. I got to say things without saying them. It allows me to be cool with myself and give a PG-rated version of what was said in the letter to my adoring public."
Ms. Malone: "But, Dark...being a bad ass sells. Just look at the
World Wrestling Fed--" Dark Soul: "Fedoroni? From San Francisco? Yeah, they're good, but
I'm not sure some large gay man who flips off fans is what I want to be."
Ms. Malone: "Yeah, but still...why not just let Colton and Spectre have it?" Dark Soul: "Because, Candy, I have to focus. And if I get out all my feelings about those two in that letter, I can focus on what's next.
Which apparently is not them. It's some "Dark Horse" guy who seems to be rather bad and rather assyish and pretty tough, too. He put
Fontaine out of business."
Ms. Malone: "So what? You beat Fontaine." Dark Soul: "Oh, I know. You know what's funny, though. I admit that
I'm not old school enough for Spectre's standards. Maybe not even for
Ice's standards. I'm my own man and I can deal with that. But I've never once missed a match because of injury. Not once. And that's what makes Johnson apparently cool. He can put somebody out. Well, you know what...nobody has put me out before. None of the OSW greats have...and I think I have faced most of them. And none of any fed's greats have done that to me. So, while Johnson can be happy and pleased as fruit punch about his little victory over Fontaine and I do mean putting him on the shelf, he can rest-assure that won't happen to me. See...while I do have to keep my emotions on a level where I can control them...every punk I see in that ring from here on-in will have a certain degree of Spectre in him. And not good degree...if there was a good degree. One that angers me. So, unlike others...I can deal with this "Dark Horse"...and ya know what...I've been the dark horse since I got here. I guess that's starting to change.
"I'm hungry...let's go to KFC. Me want some chicken tenders." Ms. Malone: "But I got this thing here...it's kinda good...kinda scary." (Dark Soul picks the Mexican pizza up and throws it in the trash.) Dark Soul: "Ready?" Ms. Malone: "Sure." Dark Soul (to the camera): "Yeah, laters..." (The scene fades.) **** KR: Heh, well... Derrek Johnson may have something to say about that,
Darky. He punked out Fontaine like you only wish you were able to have, both physically and psychologically. I'll laugh when your losing streak continues.
KR: Up next, more from the guy who saved the world from painful misery, by knocking Steve Johnson back out of Old-School Wrestling...
here is your friend and mine, Uncle Frank. ****************************** == "FANTASTIC" FRANK KNIGHT == ****************************** [The camera opens to... Gosh, hos surprising. It's that darkened room with the spotlight which we've become so familiar with lately.
Someone is definitely cutting corners as far as production costs go. And standing in the circle of light cast by the spot is, again not surprisingly, Frank Knight. Cheerful, old Uncle Frank. As usual he is dressed fairly simply, wearing a pair of blue jeans and a T-shirt.
The shirt this time is red with a blue OSW logo and the words "Uncle Frank knows what's best for you" scripted in white below the logo. As usual when he's looking at a camera that nasty grin, by now familiar to any
OSW fan, appears on the man's face.]
Frank: Heya, Jerry. Remember me? Uncle Frank? The man who's in your debt because you used an actual wrestling move to win last Insession, unlike some people out there?
[He waves cheerfully at the camera, seemingly in a perfectly good mood.] Frank: Come insession, Jerry, I will repay that debt. See, Uncle
Frank is a good man. Uncle Frank is a kind man. Uncle Frank is a considerate man. And what's more, Uncle Frank knows you came back to this grand sport for your lovely wife and adorable, little kid. Hiya,
Cameron! Hiya, lil' Scotty!
[Frank gives another little wave at the camera, grinning like the cat that ate the canary.]
Frank: Uncle Frank is gonna help daddy realize his big dream, Scotty. Uncle Frank is gonna see to it that daddy will always be remembered in the wrestling world. Uncle Frank is going to make daddy's match this
Insession so memorable it will be played over and over and over again all over the world on all kinds of TV channels for years and years and years. That way Scotty gets to see daddy do what he loves _all_ the time, even if daddy might not actually _be_ there anymore.
[That exaggeratedly sad look comes across his face once more, and he wipes away an imaginary tear.]
Frank: Yes, it's true, Scotty. Cameron. Daddy might not be able to come back home. Daddy might not be able to tuck Scotty in anymore.
Wrestling is a dangerous, dangerous business, or so I've been told. Not at all a place for familymen to work. [He tsks and shakes his head, then suddenly that demented grin is back.] Frank: But that's okay. Uncle Frank has made sure little Scotty will be able to sleep at night anyway. All you have to do, Jerry, is have
Cameron play this to the lil' tyke when he goes to sleep at night, and everything will be okay. Don't worry. I'll send you a tape of it.
[He stands up straight, clears his throat theatrically and then begins to speak in a sing-song voice.]
Frank: Jeremy Steele sat on a wall. [The grin grows even wider and he waggles his eyebrows a bit.] Frank: Jeremy Steele had a great, big fall! [He slaps his hands together and gives the camera a look of mock-sadness.] Frank: All Mike Ice's horses and all Mike Ice's men... [And the grins is back again as he leans towards the camera, snickering a bit to himself.] Frank: Couldn't put Jeremy back together again. [The camera zooms in on Frank's face, a wide grin splitting it and with a less than pleasant look in his eyes. As he speaks it's in a cheerfully conversational tone of voice.]
Frank: I wanted to use "Baa, Baa, Black Sheep" you know, but I couldn't make it work. [Fade out.] **** KR: Well, thus ends "Song Time with Uncle Frank" this Flashback... I'm so glad Uncle Frank got that last tape in... what a perfect way to end Flashback this week, don't ya think?
[He smiles.] KR: Tune in Saturday for a OSW Interactive with Jeff Cunningham at a special time, check your local listings and all that jazz. Until
Insession... this is "Cool" Keith Robinson, and you wish you were!
Good night!
