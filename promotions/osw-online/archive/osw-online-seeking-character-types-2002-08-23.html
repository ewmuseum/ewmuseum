Seeking a few specific character types...

23 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all, this is Jerod, president of Old-School Wrestling, the Regional RSE federation. I'm looking to fill a couple of specific character types. Heavy consideration will be given to other UNIQUE (note the emphasis, please) singles faces and heel teams, but I intend on hiring at least one character of the following types:
- a comedic face that can get under the skin of an arrogant heel - a young, "blue chipper" type rookie face - a good Monster Heel tag team Also, please note that I'm _NOT_ looking for big names (with the possible exception of a "mid-level indy" star for the comedic face).
OSW is a great place for you to test out new characters, or bring characters out of retirement, but it's not feasible to carry a lot of the "big names".
Our most recent card, OSW Insession, can be found on RSPWF, as well as RSPWF.com. We run things a little differently than many feds... no jumbotron, no backstage skits, few in-rings (and usually only those that push angles), a small, hometown, ECW-like fan friendly environment...
It's different from many of the "World" federations, but most of our handlers find the environment pretty enjoyable. We HEAVILY encourage angle work, angles take as much (if not more) priority than flashing... and pushing angles can be a unique challenge considering the lack of heavy backstage/in-ring use.
Anyway... if you're looking for a fun, stress-free federation and you have a character that fits one of the above categories (or is innovative enough to not duplicate another character), please check out our website and fill out an application.
http://www.rocksolidcorp.com/osw/ Thanks. Moo.
