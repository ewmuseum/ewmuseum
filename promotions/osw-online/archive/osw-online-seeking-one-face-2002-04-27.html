Seeking ONE face and ONE face tag team...

27 April 2002

OK, Old-School Wrestling's roster is nearly full. There are two more spots available, and I'm looking pretty specifically for character types...
I need one singles face. Preferably a comedy face, someone who can get over by making the fans laugh. Would also look at a foreign face, or an old-school technician.
I also need a face tag team. Preferably a foreign team, though if they're face and a tag team, and you have some talent, I'm not going to be picky here. :)
We're building an incredible little fed here that's been getting some great reviews. We have a solid talent base, and our tag team division is a featured division. We're just looking for two more characters to fill the final two holes on the roster.
Please stop by OSW Online, read the app, look at some of our past work on RSPWF, and if you think you might be interested, fill out an app!
Again, only ONE face and ONE face tag team will be accepted, so get your apps in now! Moo. :)
