House Show Report <12/17/02>

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[name withheld] here. Okay, I have to admit it. The first thing I saw on the card for OSW's House Show was TWO debut matches. T-fricken'-wo! But then I scrolled down some more. Kaz Saga vs. Tyro Marvel? Hokori Ketsueki vs. Frank Knight? Things were looking good. After talking to a lot of fans at the gates (some guy tried to call his cliq the "School Children" which quickly got shot down) I discovered the House Show fans were forming a small
OSW cult. These fans would take over a part of the Schoolhouse and go to every single show. Which means they actually find out some insider information...
So of course I joined. You see, sometimes Jeff Cunningham (my main contact with the OSW... I think Michael Ice looked at me once) gives me some information to release to the websites, but these guys (and gals) that are part of the OSW Cult have found out a lot of things.
Probably because they have no lives. The first thing I heard was some news about Travis Cade. Not too many people have heard of him, since he's a newcomer to the OSW, but he was spotted training in the ring with BOTH Saga and Ketsueki before the show, learning some techniques from both. He wasn't scheduled to show, but he was there. He was in the audience during the show as well, signing autographs, and traded wrestling tapes with one fan. He was fairly easily accessible, talking to anybody who approached him.
He dropped by the Cult halfway though the show, and actually won them over with his wrestling knowledge, and his being a fan of the sport who loved talking to the fans. I've only seen Cade perform once in the ring the last House Show, and judging by that performance and the fact that he's looking to learn more, it should be an interesting future for him. Will he be the kind of guy to fall into the trap of the veterans not looking to lose their spot? Or will he make an impression of somebody in the back who'll look to protect him?
In my years of being a wrestling fan, I've seen plenty of careers either made or broken by what Cade is doing right now. Remember Mike
Stewart? No? He took the course of Cade, trying his best to make it and he quickly scared some of the mid-card/main-eventers, fearing their spot. It's a game I hate to see being played in wrestling. But after being held down in a certain league, Stewart couldn't take it anymore and quit. This guy had the potential to be the next Dark Soul, but now only a few people will remember him.
Now let's look on the flip side where it actually worked. A man by the name of Superstar. He started out fresh in the NWWF, just a rookie. He was given the name by somebody in the business who liked the spunk of the kid. Of course this was about 6 years ago. Superstar was the kind of guy that Cade is now, trying his best to get to know the fans and learn as much as he could. In some circles he will still be known as the best high-flier in wrestling. He had a very successful NWWF career, but an injury in 1999 slowed him down. As you can see in his failed comeback to wrestling in the OSW, he only showed sparks of the man who Don Cameron ("The Dominator", working on an interview show in the NWWF) once called, not-quite jokingly, "The Next Don Cameron"
which Superstar took as quite the compliment. As for Cade, I wish him the best of luck. Because if he's as good as his potential, he's going to need as much luck as he can find.
As for the Schoolhouse, it was jam packed. Standing room at the back. A lot of Tyro and Frank Knight fans in the crowd, including some who were hoping for another surprise appearance by Def Bellows, who wasn't at the show.
Onto the matches... ~~~~ Double Debut Match 1. Blackout Burns -vs- "Legend" Joe Spear Joe Spear is announced in the ring, and for some odd reason a few fans pop for him. The pop turns to a mixed reaction, as
AC/DC's "Back in Black" hits the P.A., bringing out the newcomer Blackout Burns. Burns clarifies his heel status by heckling a few fans at ringside... one of which draws Burns into an actual argument for a moment before Blackout shrugs him off and turns his attention back to Spear in the ring.
The bell sounds, and the duo with a lockup... Blackout with a knee to the gut, then connects with a vertical suplex to take the early advantage. Spear struggles up, Burns behind him with a solid German Suplex. Burns reminds the OSW faithful of how great he is, and they respond with a slight heel pop. Burns turns back to Spear, pulls him up... inside cradle by Spear gets two.
Both men back to their feet, Spear ducks a running clothesline, armdrag... Burns up, another armdrag, this time into an armbar.
The crowd cheers slightly, as Spear looks around, pleased at the reaction. Meanwhile, Burns is going vertical... thumb to the eye... press slam by Burns draws a heel pop! Burns pulls the
Legend up, full nelson slam! But it only gets two, slight pop on the kickout. Burns pulls Spear up, atomic lift from behind, seats Spear on the top turnbuckle... REVERSE DDT OFF THE TOP~!
Heel pop as Burns gets the three count. Burns hollers "OSW needs to watch out, because it is time!" to another heel pop, before leaving the ring.
Winner: Blackout Burns ("Two Minute Warning", 4:39) Rating: ** -- An acceptable opening, but for those who knew Joe Spear it wasn't really a surprising outcome.
~~~~ Double Debut Match 2. Hate Crime Symphony -vs- The Simpson Brothers The Simpson Brothers also start in the ring, though their announcement brings a complete silence of indifference to the
Schoolhouse crowd. "I've Got the Ice in Me" by enemymine fills the arena, and the crowd jeers loudly as Preston Mayfield appears from the entrance. He smiles for a moment...
... then the crowd catches on, as the two men now known as the Hate Crime Symphony attack the Simpson Brothers from behind. The bell sounds, double Irish Whip on Josh Simpson... and a double Mafia Kick takes the younger Simpson down. Alan Simpson back to his feet, charges and gets a few shots in on Bryce
Shadrich, who promptly shows his boxing history by being the living hell out of Alan. That brawl falls outside the ring, as Josh Simpson stuggles to his feet... only to meet a STIFF clothesline from Angel Escobar.
Escobar lays a few vicious punches in on Josh Simpson, before pulling him up, behind him... low-angle German Suplex! And it gets a three count!
Winner: Hate Crime Symphony (German Suplex, 3:01) Rating: *1/2 -- Squashy, squashy. The Simpson Brothers showed no emotion in the ring. ~~~~ The crowd seemed a bit bored with the tag match, but as soon as Tyro Marvel came out for the next match, the crowd immediately heated up. 3. Kazuyuki Saga -vs- "Phenomenon" Tyro Marvel "Phenomenon" by LL Cool J brings a solid heel pop, as well as the appearance of "Phenomenon" Tyro Marvel. He's dressed in simply a pair of blue jeans and wrestling boots, apparently not feeling the need to dress for a house show. He also pays no mind to the crowd, clearly bothered that he's at an untelevised event. This increases the heel pop, which switches solidly to a face pop as Snap's "I've Got the Power" fills the arena.
Kazuyuki Saga comes out and makes his way to the ring quickly, sliding in... ... right into an attack by Marvel, and we've got a bell. Marvel stomps away at Saga, Saga up, Marvel sends him into the ropes, Saga ducks a clothesline and comes back with a flying cross body that brings a pop! Saga laying in some stiff kicks of his own, as Marvel rolls away in pain. Marvel to the outside, pulls Saga's legs out from under him, Marvel quickly up onto the apron... slingshot elbowdrop connects! But Saga gets his foot on the rope at two, causing a sigh of displeasure from the Phenomenon.
Marvel and Saga both up, Marvel backs Saga into the corner with hard kicks to the gut that seem to have a VICIOUS effect on Kaz.
Marvel smiles as he lays into Saga with a clothesline, pulling Saga into a front facelock, Tornado DDT... Saga grabs the ropes and Marvel has nothing! Marvel takes a solid hit, stumbling to his feet, as Saga sends Marvel into the ropes, riding a second wind, and hits a powerslam to a pop! Both men are down, Saga struggles for a cover... but it only draws two, to the disappointment of the crowd.
Both men slowly struggle to their feet, as the crowd starts a small "SA-GA! SA-GA!" chant to rally their favorite. Saga is indeed the first up... but Marvel with a boot to the gut, and a quick DDT. He shakes off the powerslam, pulling Saga up... hard kick to the groin! Referee Walter scolds Marvel severely... but the look on Saga's face suggests the shot was worth the warning.
Saga on his hands and knees... hard kick to the head lays out Saga... for a three, to a massive, but somewhat confused, heel pop. Referee Walter holds Marvel's hand in the air, then turns to check on Saga... at which point Marvel pulls the steel plate out of the front shin of his boot, to a MASSIVE heel pop.
Marvel tosses the plate away and leaves the ring, ignoring the jeering fanbase. Winner: "Phenomenon" Tyro Marvel (pinfall, 10:27) Rating: **1/2 -- Would have been *** had it not looked like Marvel didn't care to be there. Also the shoddy ending drew a lot of boos from the crowd who were expecting a bit more of an effort from both men. Saga tried to keep up in the match, but Marvel showed his experience in controlling this disappointing match-up.
~~~~ Next up was the Main Event that I was particularly looking forward to. Two men who know how to wrestle old school actually WRESTLING in a Main Event (a joke credited to Steve of the OSW Cult). 4. Hokori Ketsueki -vs- "Fantastic" Frank Knight "Dare" by Stan Bush fills the Schoolhouse, and the crowd pops as Hokori Ketsueki appears from the entrance. A more confident Hokori Ketsueki than we're used to, as he wears a big smile and makes quite an effort of giving high-fives all around the ring before sliding into the ring. The crowd gives a good pop for
Ketsueki... a pop that suddenly turns quite heel as the music changes to The Who's "Behind Blue Eyes". The heel pop grows as out from the entrance comes the violent, demented kiddie show host himself, "Fantastic" Frank Knight. Knight wears a look of imminent pleasure on his face, as he carefully eyes Ketsueki before sliding into the ring.
Referee Walter calls for the bell, and Ketsueki and Knight begin to circle the ring, looking for an opening. The crowd seems pretty electric for this lockup, as Knight goes for it...
Ketsueki ducks away, and catches Knight just as he turns around with a beautiful standing dropkick, to a pop! Knight back up,
Ketsueki with an armdrag, another armdrag... and a Dragon Screw Legwhip, into a single-leg crab! This doesn't last long, however, as Knight pulls Ketsueki's leg out from under him, rolling through into a standing position and a spinning toe hold. The Schoolhouse crowd begins an "OLD-SCHOOL~!
OLD-SCHOOL~!" chant, as Knight turns the spinning toe hold into an elbowdrop to the knee, and a knee hammerlock. Ketsueki takes advantage of his size weakness to reach Knight's head for a loose Dragon Sleeper. Ketsueki eventually wins the battle of submission moves, maneuvering into a more solid Dragon
Sleeper... Knight tries to roll away and the tangle in the ropes, forcing a break. All holds are broken, and both wrestlers quickly struggle to their feet for a standoff... to a pop! Uncle Frank smiles, silently applauding his opponent's technical abilities... and into another lockup! Knight overpowers this one, pushing
Ketsueki back into a corner... and a knee to the gut by Knight. Knight sends Ketsueki across the ring into the opposite turnbuckle, following in with a big clothesline. He lifts
Hokori, seats him on the top rope, begins to climb... Ketsueki with shots to the gut! Ketsueki reaches over Knight, hooks right beneath the arms... SCREAMING GHOST BOMB FROM THE TOP ROPE
TO A _HUGE_ POP~! But Ketsueki is out of it as well, as both men are down and the crowd is on the edge of their seats at this point.
Ketsueki finally manages to crawl over and drape an arm across Knight's chest... but Knight gets a shoulder up at the last second. Ketsueki rolls away towards the ropes, using them to help himself stand, as Knight starts to struggle up as well...
Ketsueki with a boot to the gut, front facelock, looking for a Tornado DDT... and it connects! But again, only a two count. Ketsueki again heading to the top rope, he raises his arms to the crowd, who pop in response... SHOOTING STAR PRESS MISSES!
Knight rolls away, and the pop falls silent as Ketsueki hits the mat face-first. Knight is the first to struggle to his feet this time, he pulls Ketsueki up, sends him into the ropes... tilt-a-whirl backbreaker connects viciously! Knight stomps at the back, drops an elbow on it, then pulls Ketsueki back to his feet, front facelock, leg hooked... fisherman's suplex, but it only gets two to a pop. Knight back up, again stomping away at
Ketsueki's back, as Ketsueki tries to struggle to his feet... SPINNING SPINEBUSTER by Knight, holding the legs and turns Ketsueki over into a Boston Crab! Ketsueki is in the hold for a good two minutes before finally reaching the ropes, causing an
Uncle Frank break JUST at the count of five. Ketsueki struggles to his feet, using the ropes for assistance, Knight sends him into the ropes, drop toe hold, but Ketsueki avoids an elbowdrop targeted at his back. Ketsueki quickly up, and a drop toe hold of his own on Knight, followed by a low dropkick that catches
Knight square in the head. The fans pop as Ketsueki takes a breather, still aching from the physicality of this match thus far. Ketsueki heads out to the apron, waiting for Knight to struggle back to his feet...
SPRINGBOARD HURANCANRANA... INTO A CRADLE, but only for two, as Knight rolls through into a cradle of his own that, again, only draws a two count. Knight and Ketsueki both slow to their feet... as we hear a bell?
The crowd jeers as the time limit has expired for this match. However, Bob Walter, after confirming with both opponents... decides that the match continues, and the fans pop at that announcement from Cunningham. Knight and Ketsueki again lockup, Knight goes low and pulls out Ketsueki's leg from under him. A short legsweep by Ketsueki does the same to Knight, and Ketsueki rolls through to a standing position, looking for a spinning toe hold. But Knight kicks Ketsueki away, kips up... running clothesline by Ketsueki!
Ketsueki heading to the top rope now, Knight slowly up... and hits the ropes! Ketsueki loses his balance and takes a seat on the top rope, as Knight climbs to the second turnbuckle, front facelock, leg hook... SUPER FISHERMANBUSTER! And it gets a three!
Winner: "Fantastic" Frank Knight (Super Fishermanbuster, 24:20) Rating: ****1/2 -- Probably the best match on a House Show since Chris Colton defeated Kaz Saga awhile back. Both men got a huge ovation from the crowd, as this match made up for the mediocre show.
~~~~ BIGGEST POPS - Kazuyuki Saga - Frank Knight - Hokori Ketsueki - Preston Mayfield (who's getting quite the following in the Cult) - Joe Spear - The guy in the crowd who did a perfect imitation of Lew Spectre. BIGGEST HEEL POPS - Tyro Marvel - Frank Knight - The guy who rang the bell in the Main Event - Blackout Burns - Hate Crime Symphony (I think some people were expecting Mayfield to bring in The Reagans for some reason) - Jeff Cunningham (who always gets a bit of a heel pop) ~~~~ Final OSW House Show Rating **1/2 -- A great main event saved this one, as the Saga/Marvel match was a bit of a disappointment. I think somebody needs to talk to Marvel about showing some enthusiasm for the non-televised matches. It was great to see Joe Spear again, and Blackout Burns showed a lot of promise with some good wrestling skills. Nobody knew what to expect from the HCS, but if they keep it up things should get interesting.
