From the President's Desk ============================= Hello everyone, this is the first Old-School Wrestling update. If you're on this mailing list and you don't wish to be, either mail me back or visit our webpage promoted numerous times throughout this mail to get off it. Otherwise... welcome to OSW, everyone. :)
My name is [name withheld], better known to some throughout the e-w world as KrazyAce or [detail withheld] (do you really want to ask? ;)). I handle Fire and Ice, who just happen to own OSW. Therefore, I am your president, here to serve your every demand... scratch that, I don't trust some of you. ;) Regardless, those of you who know me know that
I am a fun, carefree president who completely avoids politics and simply gives a fun federation to handle in. And those of you who don't know me, soon will. I like to get to know everybody who handles for me, because it builds a better relationship, both between us and between you handlers, and I think if you're comfortable in your environment, you will have more fun... and when you're having fun, your work shows and makes the fed more entertaining. ;)
Either way, consider me your new buddy. Any questions, comments, concerns, problems, mailing list joins, raunchy jokes, fantasy sports questions, or free money you'd like to share with me, feel free to e-mail me. I'll answer just about every e-mail you send me...
seriously, I've had chains of e-mails 30-40 replys deep because I never stop replying and neither does the other person...
*cough*Don*cough* ;) As for the technical stuff, this update does a WHOLE lot of things. It announces the date of the first OSW Insession, it announces the flash schedule, which therefore opens flashing, as yes, OSW is now getting off the ground. And if you buy now, we'll even throw in a free cheese grater, no charge.
Wait, one more thing... some of you didn't properly read the OSW FAQ before filling out an app, did you? *cough*Dustin*cough* ;) Just a reminder... OSW is a small-time indy fed, with a restricted budget.
No fireworks, no jumbotron. Spotlights are constructed, but don't get too crazy with the colors because it's basic equipment. Strobe lights are also fine. Fireworks are TOTALLY out of the question, we're wrestling in a rebuilt warehouse, don't forget gentlemen. ;) Please
EVERYBODY check their bios, if your entrance is too extravagant please send me a new toned down one when you have the chance, and I'd appreciate everybody sending me the info I don't have for most characters (anything that has a "???"). Also, check the Contacts List and make sure I have everything right there.
Anyway, all that aside, now to the news. Read on, and enjoy. ;) - President [name withheld] [detail withheld] 2. News ======== TELEVISION DEAL SECURE! 5. Deadlines ============= OK everyone, here's the deal with the schedule. Effective this week, we are starting into the regular bi-weekly schedule. What does that mean?
1. Flashing now begins, so those of you who've been getting antsy to get going... *cough*Brian*cough* ;) can now get going. 2. We are going into the regular schedule... but skipping the card in the first rotation. In it's place, on Tuesday, will be OSW Kickoff. Kickoff is a special, one-time flash show that will be hosted by OSW
Interviewer/Ring Announcer Jeff Cunningham, and will be in a panel format, featuring as guests President Michael Ice, color commentator
"Cool" Keith Robinson and play-by-play man Sam Collins. After that, we will go with the two regular flash shows, Flashback on Saturday then Interactive the following Wednesday, with the card the Tuesday following. I hope you got all that, and if you didn't, here's a visual example, including those ever handy deadlines... speaking of which... ;)
My deadlines policy is simple. Get it in on time, it gets used. Get it in late, you're taking your chances. If I can fit it in without doing a ton of extra work, then I might, but if you REALLY want it on the show... get it in on time. We're going bi-weekly, you have plenty of time.... see? ;)
