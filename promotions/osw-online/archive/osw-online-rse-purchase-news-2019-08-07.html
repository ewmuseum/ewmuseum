RSE purchases part of OSW

Wayback capture 7 August 2019 (site content dated 15 June 2002)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered, in its original English; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSE purchases part of OSW OSW Webmaster - 06 / 15 / 02 Rock Solid Enterprises, owned by [name withheld], has purchased 33% of Old-School Wrestling from Michael Ice and Alex Perkins. Old-School Wrestling will act as the Regional affiliate in the tri-fed alliance, joining the World Wrestling Organization (Global) and Ultimate E-Wrestling (National), as well as the Keening School of Grappling Arts. For more details on Rock Solid Enterprises and their purchase of OSW, and what this will mean as far as Old-School Wrestling is concerned, please visit our RSE Information page. A Superiority Complex in OSW? OSW Webmaster - 06 / 15 / 02 Perhaps the most dominant alliance OSW could witness has occured, as Television Champion Chris Colton, Def Bellows and Sexual Energy have joined forces. A recent victory to a unit consisting of Dark Soul, "Whiskey" Jack Collins and the Dime Notes solidified the Superiority Complex as the dominant group in OSW, as they try to force their way straight to the top in Old-School Wrestling. OSW Field Trip Announced OSW Webmaster - 06 / 15 / 02 OSW Field Trip, a tournament supercard for Old-School Wrestling, has been announced. The date has not been finalized at this point, but the event will take the place of a future OSW Insession. Announced for the special one-time event is the Eight-Man OSW Old-School Title Tourney, as well as the promise of OFFICIAL OSW Tag Team Champions. Check back here at OSW Online for more info in the coming weeks, or join the OSW mailing list and find out details as soon as they're announced. OSW Online sees major changes OSW Webmaster - 06 / 15 / 02 As part of the RSE purchase, OSW Online has switched servers and undergone a few changes. We will be more free creatively on the RSE server, and now that I've FINALLY got the majority of the bios done, page updates should be more frequent. Please look over the site and refamiliarize yourself. Any questions, comments or problems with the website, please contact the webmaster. Thanks for visiting, and enjoy your stay! Old-School Wrestling is no longer doing general hiring OSW Webmaster - 06 / 15 / 02 Due to a nearly full roster, as well as the partial buyout of OSW by Rock Solid Enterprises... we are now only accepting wrestlers by invitation. If you handle a character you feel may be a good fit in Old-School Wrestling, please contact me personally and ask. Strong consideration will be given to foreign heels, comedy faces and tag teams... but be forewarned that we are being picky and only hiring what we specifically need.
