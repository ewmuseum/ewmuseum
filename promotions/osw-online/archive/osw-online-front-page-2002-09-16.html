OSW Online... Home of Old-School Wrestling Page Last Updated: 03/22/02 Old-School Wrestling's looking to fill out roster! OSW Webmaster - 03 / 22 / 02 OSW is still accepting applications! At the moment, we'd like to add a couple of solid tag teams, a couple of solid faces, and one or two more heels, preferably foreign characters or something unique... we have plenty of arrogant pricks at the moment. ;) If you're a young star looking to make your mark in wrestling, or even a veteran simply looking for another home, OSW may be the place for you! Look over OSW Online, and if this looks like the place for you, feel free to fill out an application! New Television Champion to be Crowned! OSW Webmaster - 03 / 22 / 02 Next Insession, the winners of the four qualifying matches the last two Insession will square off in a 4-man single-elimination tourney... the prize, the OSW Television Title! Two Insessions ago, Chris Colton and Dark Soul won spots in the tourney. Last Insession, Magnus Colby injured Crisis in his dominant victory to join those three, and the bracket was filled by Def Bellows, who defeated Brett Young last Insession. This week, Bellows takes on Colton and Colby takes on Soul... the winners meeting in the main event for the OSW Television Title! OSW Online Updated! OSW Webmaster - 03 / 22 / 02 I know, I know... FINALLY. :) As those on the mailing list know, I've been tied up working extra in a rush at work the last few weeks. However, things look to have cleared up for the most part, so expect more frequent webpage updates. Everything's updated and linked except for the Champions page, which will be updated after this Insession, when we have a champ. :) PLEASE, everyone who handles in OSW, check your character's bios and the Contacts List (under Misc.) to make sure I have all your information correct. Any info I don't have, please get to me. :) Any questions, comments or problems with the website, please contact the webmaster. Thanks for visiting, and enjoy your stay! Oh, and sorry for the popups, but banners weren't possible with the menus. So, we'll have to live with them... ;) Mystery Man to Finally Wrestle OSW Webmaster - 01 / 23 / 02 The original mystery man who has been stalking Kevin Fontaine and Brian Anthony, the slightly smaller of the two, attempted to attack Kevin Fontaine after his loss to Dark Soul. However, OSW VP/Head of Security Alex Perkins, who was at ringside for just such an event, informed the masked individual that next week, he will, in fact, compete one-on-one... with none other than Kevin Fontaine himself! After the announcement, the masked man attempted to continue his attack, but was thwarted by Kevin Fontaine, who narrowly missed unmasking the mystery man. OSW Tag Team Champions?! OSW Webmaster - 03 / 22 / 02 After promising her proteges a reward for their victory two Insessions ago, "Sensual" Selena Savage pulled through on her promise... and presented Sexual Energy with the OSW Tag Team Titles. The titles are unrecognized by Old-School Wrestling, but expect Ian Rose and Andrew Stevens to defend the titles nonetheless. ©2002 Old-School Wrestling Productions
