— Part 4 of 4 —

[detail withheld] (NEFW President) wrote in message news:< [detail withheld] >... > i was part of the osw and it was sooooo mismanaged, disorganized, and poorly > booked/written i left. Heh. You MUST be mistaken, must have me mixed up with another OSW. Do me a favor... before you start bashing other feds to try to plug yours, READ THE POST and make sure you've got the right fed.
Cause, to be honest, I RUN OSW, and I've never heard of your punk ass. On that note, every handler that's left OSW thus far has either been dismissed due to inactivity, or has left due to RL issues (and every one of these handlers I know personally OOC, and you ain't one of them, cause they're all intelligent enough to use capital letters).
I've yet to have someone write me stating that they "didn't like my fed" and wanted to be removed from the roster. In fact, I've yet to even HEAR of anyone not liking my fed... I don't run a "top-level",
EMWC/WWO/PAW/BSCW type fed... but people enjoy our work. And that's the only damn thing I care about. And I'm more than secure in my ability to draw new handlers that I don't need to bash your fed, which
I know very little about, to try to sell mine. See, I make my archives COMPLETELY VISIBLE to ALL visitors of my website, and our shows sell OSW. Not cheap plugs and lame smear tactics. "OSW jobs to NEFW"? What the fuck is that supposed to mean, anyway? And why the hell even bring up my fed's name? Just because I happened to post a request for tag teams on the same day you did? And are you REALLY stupid enough to think that's going to HELP you draw people to your fed?
So... here's the breakdown. Either... 1. You're completely full of horseshit 2. You are so ignorant that you couldn't FATHOM the idea that more than one fed uses the same acronym. Shit man, there's at LEAST 2 "NEFW's" in the archives, and if I'm wrong and the presidential turnover is REALLY that horrid, then you have NO ROOM WHATSOEVER to be talking about "poor booking/writing" on MY cards. Fuck, man, you might not have been president during this time, but one of your presidents booked HIMSELF for a 24/7 Hardcore Title victory (nevermind the unoriginality there), then promptly declared the 24/7 rule dead.
Hell of a booking job THERE, pal. Maybe you weren't the fed head then... but consistancy is damned important.
Frankly, in my mind, it's better to look like an asshole than a moron. At least being an asshole is under my control. > will osw be around in 2008? we started in 1996. Want a cookie? I still bet we outlive ya. Especially if you're changing presidents every two weeks. ;)
To be perfectly honest... I wish ya the best of luck with your fed. The only reason you drew my ire is cause I was irritated, and you just happened to try smear tactics on my fed.
Again, if you WERE in an OSW before, that's cool... just be sure you're dissing the right one next time. And if you weren't, and are just full of shit thinking I don't follow my roster meticulously, then shut the fuck up. Cause you were damn sure never in MY fed... period.
