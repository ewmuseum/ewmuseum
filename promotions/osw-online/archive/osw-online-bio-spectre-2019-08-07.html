"Strictly Business" Lew Spectre Height: 6'4" Weight: 270 lbs. Hometown: Star Island, FL Alignment: Prime Evil Strengths: 1. Mat Skill 2. Ring Awareness 3. Confidence Weaknesses: 1. Can't fistfight 2. Can't fly 3. Too fixated on one style Finisher: Extreme Closeup Description: Stungun into Corner Finisher: Hickory Nut Cracker Description: Headbutt off top to groin. Finisher: Sobe Suplex Description: Atomic drop bounced off top rope to backdrop w/ hands locked to make bridging cradle for pin Finisher: Star Island Stranglehold Description: Dragon Sleeper w/ legs wrapped around oppent to pull up and keep shoulders off mat and keep them trapped in hold til tapout or out cold. Wrestling Style: To be blunt, the Greatest MAT WRESTLER in the World. Think Steven Regal. Think Ric Flair. Think Norman Smiley. Dean Malenko. Lew excells on the mat. No one is better at exchanging holds. At delivering "Classic" moves (Atomic drops, suplexes, piledrivers) and knowing where they are in the ring. At his home in Star Island, Fla, Lew sleeps in a regulation ring, so he always knows where he is in a ring, how far from the ropes, etc, even when out cold. Because of this awareness, and his sudden motion (Lew isn't quick in the running/highflying sense, but has quick hands and sudden movements/takedowns) Lew is also able to take full advantage of the rules. As a early teenager, Lew worked as a referee, so he knows every detail of every rule. Lew will only flat out break rules to save himself or when a ref is DQ'ed, but will BEND the rules at every chance. He is not above using a tag rope to choke an oppent or taking off a turnbukle pad, nor using the middle rope for leverage on a pin. Its Lew's smarts that make him deadly. On the other hand, Lew is not a fighter. When it comes time to go fist-to-fist, Lew will always end up on the short end. It's this that causes him to always go to the eyes/throat/groin, or roll out of the ring when fists fly. Lew is deadly outside the ring due to his ability to use the ring steps/railing/post/apron to his advantage, but can not stand up and fistfight with any success. Lew rarely even throws punches, relying on chops and eurouppercuts and forearms. Lew also is not a highflyer, and has very limited agility. You will see no moonsaults here. As you will see no hardcore. Lew's the kind of wrestler who if you gave him a chair, he'd set it up, sit on it, and put you in a chinlock. Simply put, he's deadly enough with his holds to not need weapons...and when he does use a weapon, it's something inconspicous like a fireball or brassknucks he can put in his pants/rolls of coins the fans will grab to get rid of evidence. He does not do dumb things that will get him caught. Lew's a master stratigist. His main weakness would be his inability to do anything BUT wrestle. 10 Favorite Moves: 1. Airplane Spin (to samoan drop, or put back on feet dizzy and hit big move or small package for pin) 2. Hanging Brainbuster (holds the guy for 45-60 seconds before dropping straight down) 3. Rolling Boston Crab (sends guy to ropes and drops down, tripping oppent, then rolls over backwards up into a boston crab) 4. Ten-Story Knee Drop (put oppent in corner back to buckle, climb buckle behind them, put knee to back of head and grab ears, riding oppent to mat and driving head into canvas) 5. Figure Four Leglock w/ Clawhold applied to kneecap 6. Double Arm Suplex (hold for pin or release) 7. Eye Poke/Eye Rake/Rake eyes aganst boot laces/every groinshot in the book 8. Ear Clap (destroys equaliberium) 9. gutwrench suplex 10. ??? Manager: Sir Richard Ray Vaughn The Third AND The One Man Human Hit Squad, Big Sir Description: Sir Richard Ray Vaughn The Third (SRRV3, he's been knighted) is the world's most successful Sports and Entertainment agent. He handles top ENTERTAINMENT acts such as musical groups, TV and Movie actors, celeberties, models, etc.. He is also a Sports Agent, handling the contracts of top athletes in all pro sports leauges (see TV's Arless, Jerry McGuire, etc). Vaughn does NOT handle Sports Entertainers. Vaughn is a short, fat, round man. Think of Paul Bearer. Except, SRRV3 has bright red hair, a brother-love like red complexion and a fetish for wearing bright red suits, bowties, shoes, suspenders and so on. He has also taken to puffing on huge cigars as of late. Big Sir is a silent Beast of a man. Imagine the biggest, meanest, toughest wrestler you've ever seen... Big Sir is badder. No exact height or weight is known on him, because he's not a wrestler so it's not required that the promotions know. Estmates usually peg him ar 6'9-6'11, and weighing well over 400 pounds (closer to 450, sometimes even over that). Big Sir sports a long brown ponytail. He is clean shaven, and always dressed in a busniess suit with the sleeves of both the jacket and the shirt removed. Big Sir wears a black tie, a black fidora hat w/ an Ace of Spades tucked into it, and black reflective lens glasses. He also carries a Golden Crowbar. Big Sir has one well-known move, the Sledge. He simple grabs an oppent with his left hand by the throat, then in an underhanded-all the way over and down motion swings his right fist down on to the top of the oppents head. This blow has always resulted in an immediate KO. Big Sir never speaks, and will never wrestle in OSW. He is simply SRRV3's backup, to insure he has a safe working envoriment for Lew. Info/Background: Most children have fantastic fantasies about what they will be when they grow up. Some dream to be Astronauts, or the President, or even Hollywood Stars. Lew Spectre only had one dream as a child... To be a Professional Wrestler. Lew grew up in South Florida, watching the now-famous "Championship Wrestling From Florida" hosted by Gordon Solie. In the CWF, wrestling was stressed above all else, and only the best mat grapplers lasted. Lew saw this as his gospel, and decided he would become the greatest mat wrestler of all time. Lew spent his childhood in amateur wrestling clubs, and selling sno-cones at the local matches. One week, the referee no-showed, and frantic to get someone in the ring for the live TV, the promoter grabbed Lew, slapped a referee shirt on him, and tossed him into the ring. Having been a fan all his life, Lew knew what to do and did a very respectable job as an offical. After the show, he was offered the job full-time and refereed for a few years, until he grew too tall to be a crediable offical. Lew wrestled in high school, yet never competed in competition. He simply trained with the team to learn. After graduation, Spectre travled the world, learning styles from Germany, Spain, South America, Africa, Asia, the UK and Russia. In 1988, Spectre was even approched to join the US Olympic Team, a invitation he declined. Lew entered Pro Wrestling in the late 80's, competing all thoughout Florida and the Carribean. He then travled to various small promtoions all over the US. By 1994, bored with the US scene, Lew left to compete in Japan and the UK..in 1998, the promise of "Classic" wrestling lured Lew back to join the NCCW, a promotion in which he was a centerpiece of. Among his many highlights, Lew held the NCCW TV title for over 18 months, never actually losing the title but forfieting it as the promotion folded. Lew also fended off an invading NWWF promotion, actually hearing cheers from the fans in the process. Lew is currently active in many promotions, adding titles to his "World Unified Title", a belt made up of championships Lew has won and held until a promotion's folding. The Belt count currently <as of 1/19/2002> stands at 7 <MWWF World, NCWA World, BAL International, NCCW TV, NWWF World, WGP TV, and PWW Intergalatic belts> Lew has only entered the OSW due to the fact they preach old school beliefs. He is a high dollar athlete and is working for OSW as NO PAY simply to raise the status of Classic, Old School Wrestling. Physical Description: long black curly hair w/the sides/back shaved, black pencil thin mustache, vandyke and goatee, hazel eyes big chest, very slim waist/abs (Ric Rude-like, very cut, but very slim, not big all over), muscular long arms (not short/bulky), long not overtly-muscled legs. Ring Attire: I usually pick it before match, but basically black tights, white boots, white singlet top (two strap, bret hart style) which he usually takes down and uses to choke oppents, sparkly, usually sleeveless robes.
