News ======== NEW UNIFIED TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS In a rematch of their classic Field Trip match, Reynolds and Bardon defeated Sexual Energy to win the Unified Tag Team Titles. Calvin Reynolds and Albert Bardon have slowly become the team to beat in OSW, and their victory over Sexual Energy has additional importance as it breaks the Superiority Complex' stranglehold on the OSW title scene. Combine the title change with the recent strong additions to the tag team division (Lords of Darkness, Tiger Paws, Hate Crime
Symphony), as well as the return of the team thought by many to be the _favorites_ going into the Field Trip Tag Title Tourney, High Society International, OSW is slowly forming one of the better tag team divisions in the business today, much to the pleasure of OSW President
Michael Ice. ANOTHER NO-SHOW FOR COLTON "Riverside Thriller" Chris Colton, OSW Television Champion, once again missed a scheduled tag team match, leaving Old-School Champion
Def Bellows alone against both Dark Soul and "Whiskey" Jack Collins.
Although Bellows showed why he's the Old-School Champion in the match, an attack by former Unified Tag Team Champions Sexual Energy backfired and Bellows was pinned by Collins. After the match, Bellows was noticably angry by his "partner's" disappearance... how will Colton respond when he appears on the next Insession? And will there be any punishment from the front offices for a second no-show?
MARVEL, CROSS TO FIGHT "Phenomenon" Tyro Marvel was up to his usual mischief last Insession, taking the announcement of a retired wrestling superstar and OSW guest as an opportunity to insult his former archrival, Scott
Douglas. This brought out "Fallen Angel" David Cross, who holds a disputed (by Marvel) victory over the Phenomenon, to put a stop to
Marvel's advances. This led to a verbal agreement for a match between the two men this Insession, and to heighten the stakes, President Ice has announced that the winner will receive a shot at the Television
Title!
MORE NEW SIGNEES Despite the delays, OSW continues to attract attention from free agents interested in one of the hotter regional federations in the business. Recent signees scheduled to debut this week are former NCCW star Gage "Suicyco" Thompson, young indy prospect Ryan Rose and
Japanese sensation Gran Sumara X. They join the returning High Society International, who have returned from a 6 month stint on the injured list and look to return to their winning ways. In addition, it's known that there is at least one more contract signed that have yet to be announced to the public yet (Gage Thompson was the other), and rumors are swirling as to the identity of this newly signed talent. In addition, the wrestling world is awaiting the debut of wrestling legend Madison J. Valentine... yet for the time being, still no sightings of Em Jiggy Vee.
