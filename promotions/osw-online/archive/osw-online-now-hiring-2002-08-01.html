Now Hiring...

1 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Old-School Wrestling is looking to fill some holes on the roster, replace some dead weight that has been filtered out.
We're looking for all types of characters, specifically faces and tag teams, though we are possibly seeking a couple heels as well. We're
NOT looking for "big name" characters, as we are trying to maintain an indy image, but we're a great place for veterans who may be considered
"past their primes" to prove otherwise. Specifically, I'd like to add a comedy face, a couple face tag teams, a unique heel team, and foreign heels. But all characters will be considered.
Also, for a few basics, we're a booked fed that puts focus on anglework. We run on a bi-weekly schedule, one full show, one house show and two flash shows. We also run by a fairly "classic" set of guidelines... no "hardcore" characters please.
For more information, please head on over to OSW Online, where you can find past cards, our FAQ, and anything else you may be seeking.
