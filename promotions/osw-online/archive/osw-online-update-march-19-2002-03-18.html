From the President's Desk ============================= *sigh* Seems like every time I think things are going good, they screw up. :) But fear not, my confidence is unwavering. :) On a plus side, the writer situation is starting to shape up. I have a full 5-match
Insession crew, as well as a few emergency writers. If I can find ONE more full-time writer (one match per week), we can talk about bumping up to 6-7 matches a card. Which would mean more ring time for all of you, which I know many of you are looking for right now, since wrestling every other show puts a month between your matches in a bi-weekly schedule. :) So, the writer hunt continues... emergency and/or full-time writers, please contact me.
Webpage... will be updated when I get a chance. You'll know when it'll be done. :) Oh, and one more thing... I've said this before, and it's repeated in the FAQ, but I need to say it again... OSW is an indy fed. 75% of it's entire budget is set aside for wrestler contracts, because the focus is not on the flash, and not simply on the wrestling (sorry, Lew ;))... the focus is on the WRESTLERS and the RING. If you want to get something done... you MUST do it in the ring. We do NOT have a jumbotron. If you do something backstage, there will be no camera to see it. The fans won't know it happened. They're there to watch the ring, fans don't come to wrestling shows to watch more TV. If you want to get something said or done... say it on the flash shows or do it in the ring. Otherwise, it doesn't get seen.
As a corollary to that... let me point out something that was commented on in the card, and is also stated in the FAQ. I will only allow a maximum of THREE (3) in-rings a week. Originally that was just for people not involved... but it's changed to 3 per week, period. OSW isn't going to turn into a flash show. There are MANY exceptions to this rule (joint in-rings where someone calls another wrestler out, for example, will be tolerated because it is more than just talking... the confrontation is there for the fans to see), so talk to me if you have an idea. And having your wrestler say a few words after his entrance when he has a match is also fine, because it's promotion of the upcoming match. But NEVER assume that just because you send in an in-ring, it will get used. If I get more than three, I will pick the three most relevant, and use them.
And another note... we're small. The OSW Schoolhouse (a remodeled warehouse in Portland) holds about 1,000 people. If you use fireworks, you will burn down the Schoolhouse. No pyros. Spotlights are OK, having the lights go out is OK, and the P.A. system sounds great... but that's about it.
Any questions or anything, of course, feel free to contact me. - President [name withheld] [detail withheld] 2. News ======== TELEVISION TITLE TOURNEY SET Next Insession, the winners of the four qualifying matches the last two Insession will square off in a 4-man single-elimination tourney... the prize, the OSW Television Title! Two Insessions ago,
Chris Colton and Dark Soul won spots in the tourney. Last Insession,
Magnus Colby injured Crisis in his dominant victory to join those three, and the bracket was filled by Def Bellows, who defeated Brett
Young last Insession. The tourney schedule can be found under
"Upcoming Card". MYSTERY MAN TO FINALLY COMPETE The original mystery man who has been stalking Kevin Fontaine and
Brian Anthony, the slightly smaller of the two, attempted to attack
Kevin Fontaine after his loss to Dark Soul. However, OSW VP/Head of
Security Alex Perkins, who was at ringside for just such an event, informed the masked individual that next week, he will, in fact, compete one-on-one... with none other than Kevin Fontaine himself!
After the announcement, the masked man attempted to continue his attack, but was thwarted by Kevin Fontaine, who narrowly missed unmasking the mystery man.
STEVE JOHNSON RETURNS Steve "Iceman" Johnson, who a month ago suffered a neck injury at the hands of Chris Colton, returned in style last Insession, warding off Chris Colton and Def Bellows after the duo laid out Brian Anthony and Brett Young following the main event. The Iceman received medical clearance to return to action recently... Johnson's official in-ring return will come this Insession, against the debuting Frank Knight.
OSW TAG TEAM TITLES? After promising her proteges a reward for their victory two
Insessions ago, "Sensual" Selena Savage pulled through on her promise... and presented Sexual Energy with the OSW Tag Team Titles.
The titles are unrecognized by Old-School Wrestling, but expect Ian
Rose and Andrew Stevens to defend the titles nonetheless.

Upcoming Card ================= For more schedule info, see Deadlines. *~~ OSW INSESSION ~~* 03 / 12 / 02 OSW Schoolhouse, Portland, OR 1. "FANTASTIC" FRANK KNIGHT vs. STEVE "ICEMAN" JOHNSON Insession opens with a debut match, as "Fantastic" Frank Knight attempts to start on the road to success in OSW. However, he has a tough task ahead of him, as he takes on Steve Johnson. The Iceman has just been medically cleared to wrestle after his injury at the hands of Chris Colton, and he's looking to make a huge return by spoiling
Knight's debut.
OSW TV Title Tourney Semi-Final 2. MAGNUS COLBY vs. DARK SOUL Magnus Colby, who knocked Crisis out of competition with a knee injury last Insession, will take on the equally-hot Dark Soul in the first of three OSW TV Title Tourney matches this evening. Both wrestlers are undefeated and have looked impressive... but someone has to lose. And someone has to advance to the OSW Television Title match.
OSW TV Title Tourney Semi-Final 3. "RIVERSIDE" CHRIS COLTON vs. DEF BELLOWS There will be no screwjobs in the title match, as Chris Colton and
Def Bellows will meet in the semi-final of the OSW TV Title Tourney.
The winner will move on to the OSW Television Title match. Will we see another easy finish, or will Colton and Bellows put their alliance on the side for this match?
4. "DELINQUENT" KEVIN FONTAINE vs. ??? Last Insession, OSW VP Alex Perkins set up this match. The masked man, or at least the original one, will step into the ring with the man he has stalked for three Insessions now... Kevin Fontaine.
Fontaine will finally get an opportunity to take out his anger on his masked rival... and could we finally see who at least one of these masked men is?
>*~ MAIN EVENT ~*< OSW TV Title Tourney Final Winner will be crowned inaugural OSW Television Champion 5. CHRIS COLTON/DEF BELLOWS vs. DARK SOUL/MAGNUS COLBY The main event will see the crowning of Old-School Wrestling's first-ever champion. Chris Colton, Def Bellows, Dark Soul and Magnus
