http://www.angelfire.com/retro/osw/ ==== *~~ OSW INSESSION ~~* 02 / 05 / 02 OSW Schoolhouse, Portland, OR 1. OSW Grand-Opening Match Def Bellows vs. Dark Soul Two men who have dominated in other federations, yet still haven't gained the recognition and respect that those who know them feel they deserve. Tonight, one of them gets that opportunity, in the first-ever OSW match. Will Dark Soul rid himself of the self-doubt stemming from his controversial final loss in another promotion, or will Def Bellows become more than just a "lackey" and take his first step towards true greatness? President Michael Ice has deemed this an interference-free match, anyone who attempts to get involved will be fired as quickly as they were hired. There will be a clean winner, as wrestling goes "old-school" for the first time.
2. FOUR CORNERS MATCH Crisis vs. Jonny Storm vs. "Big Gun" Jim Sergeant vs. "Blackheart" Jonathan Talon Four men go at each other in this match, which will no doubt be huge for the winner. The match will be elimination-style, with wrestlers being eliminated by pinfall or submission. But, with four men in the ring, that will be no easy task. Needless to say, expect chaos to ensue on the second match in OSW history.
3. "Riverside Thriller" Chris Colton vs. Steve "Iceman" Johnson One-time friends step into the ring to do battle in the second ever OSW match. Colton is wrestling with the idea that he can't succeed in singles, while Johnson is looking to take that final step into the upper card that he's yet to take in his career. Who will accomplish their goal on OSW's opening night?
4. "Fantasy" Brett Young/"Shocker" Brian Anthony vs. The Dime Notes Come on, OSW is co-run by two members of a tag team... you think there wouldn't be a tag match on the first show? The Dime Notes have claimed to be the "cornerstone" of the young OSW tag team division, by virtue of being the first team on the roster... but how will they fare against a team of wrestling legends in Brett Young and Brian Anthony?
And, for that matter, considering the sketchy history between Young and Anthony... how will they fare as a team?
5. MAIN EVENT "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine vs. "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre The first two wrestlers to sign on the dotted line with Old-School
Wrestling will do battle in the main event. The two have a history in
NCCW, as many felt Spectre should have gotten his chance at Fontaine when he held the NCCW World Title... will Spectre get his revenge on
OSW's opening night, or will Fontaine be the one to get his OSW career started off with a huge victory?
DEADLINES: (keep in mind, bi-weekly schedule, and everything's listed as Pacific time because OSW is basically a Portland-based regional fed ;))
