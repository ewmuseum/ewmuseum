From the President's Desk ============================= Most of this was covered in the Notes sent out earlier today. However, a brief recap and a couple new things... First off... a BIG welcome to ALL the "new signees". A few of you new guys didn't get debuts this week, as we have a LOT of non-BR debuts to cover this Insession. You "newer newcomers" will get debuts next Insession, fear not. :) Next up... we're going back to the RSE address, as after extensive testing it's given me no problems, and it's an easier way to keep track of things. So, as a reminder for you new folks... REGULAR CORRESPONDANCE goes to: [detail withheld] ALL FLASHES AND STRATS go to: [detail withheld] I can't reply to e-mails sent to the RSE address, so all mails from me will come from this address. The "
[detail withheld] " address is STRICTLY FOR FLASHES AND STRATS. Deadlines for those this Insession are below in the Update, the House Show Report by [name withheld] will return AFTER this Insession.
If you're not interested in staying active in OSW, please let me know so your roster spot can be freed for an active handler. No hard feelings for any reason, just let me know.
I NEED WRITERS. The more writers I have, the more matches Insession can have, and if I get enough... the less you'll actually have to write one. :) Only one match every two weeks, which is far from a big task, so let me know if you can write. Even if you've written for me in the past, drop me a line and confirm that you can continue to do so. Thanks. :)
If you don't have an angle and are looking for one, please get ahold of me and I'll help ya out. We have a lot of newer wrestlers on the roster, so there's plenty of people for you to work with. I will be updating the webpage this week, including the Contacts page... but if you want/need an e-mail sooner, let me know. With us kicking back into gear, it's the PERFECT time to get involved in an angle and start giving it some attention. It keeps you more interested, it keeps me more interested, and it raises the quality of the fed in general... so don't be afraid to ask. :)
And now for the obligatory Update reminders... ;) I've said this before, and it's repeated in the FAQ, but I need to say it again...
OSW is an indy fed. 75% of it's entire budget is set aside for wrestler contracts, because the focus is not on the flash, and not simply on the wrestling (sorry, Lew ;))... the focus is on the
WRESTLERS and the RING. If you want to get something done... you MUST do it in the ring. We do NOT have a jumbotron. If you do something backstage, there will be no camera to see it. The fans won't know it happened. They're there to watch the ring, fans don't come to wrestling shows to watch more TV. If you want to get something said or done... say it on the flash shows or do it in the ring. Otherwise, it doesn't get seen.
As a corollary to that... let me point out something that was commented on in the card, and is also stated in the FAQ. I will only allow a maximum of THREE (3) in-rings a week. Originally that was just for people not involved... but it's changed to 3 per week, period. OSW isn't going to turn into a flash show. There are MANY exceptions to this rule (joint in-rings where someone calls another wrestler out, for example, will be tolerated because it is more than just talking... the confrontation is there for the fans to see), so talk to me if you have an idea. And having your wrestler say a few words after his entrance when he has a match is also fine, because it's promotion of the upcoming match. But NEVER assume that just because you send in an in-ring, it will get used. If I get more than three, I will pick the three most relevant, and use them.
And another note... we're small. The OSW Schoolhouse (a remodeled warehouse in Portland) holds about 1,000 people. If you use fireworks, you will burn down the Schoolhouse. No pyros. Spotlights are OK, having the lights go out is OK, and the P.A. system sounds great... but that's about it.
Any questions or anything, of course, feel free to contact me. - President [name withheld] [detail withheld] 2. News ======== SUPERIORITY COMPLEX WINS BOTH BELTS, HOLDS ALL THREE The Superiority Complex officially became the strongest unit in Old-School Wrestling, by walking out of the Field Trip with BOTH titles! Sexual Energy defeated the Dime Notes to unify the Joint Tag
Team Titles, then defeated the underdog Reynolds & Bardon to win the Old-School Wrestling Unified Tag Team Titles, staking their claim as "the team" in OSW. Then, in the second half of the Double Main Event, Def Bellows defeated Kazuyuki Saga with the Definator after interference by "Alien
Invader 2" to win the Old-School Heavyweight Championship! After the match, AI2 unmasked, revealing himself to be Chris Colton... quite obviously not the REAL Alien Invader 2, who has yet to appear in OSW since unceremoniously leaving his ally, Def Bellows, as the
Superiority Complex initially took shape.
Along with "Riverside Thriller" Chris Colton, the reigning OSW
Television Champion, the Superiority Complex now proudly holds all three OSW title belts. However, that reign could be short-lived, as this Insession the Superiority Complex takes on Dark Soul, Kazuyuki
Saga, "Whiskey" Jack Collins and Calvin "Earth Warrior" Reynolds...
with BOTH the Old-School AND Television Titles, as well as a shot at the Unified Tag Team Titles ALL on the line!
KAZUYUKI SAGA NEARLY OVERCOMES INSURMOUNTABLE ODDS Kazuyuki Saga, whom most considered a relative pushover entering the Old-School Title tourney, performed admirably against three of the most dangerous competitors in OSW, "Fanastic" Frank Knight, Television
Champion "Riverside Thriller" Chris Colton, and eventual Old-School
Champion Def Bellows. Saga scored a major upset win over the
Television Champion, defeating Colton after the Thriller turned his attention to "Whiskey" Jack Collins at ringside. Saga then advanced past the lethal Frank Knight by disqualification, when Knight refused to release "Snap, Crackle and Pop" after Saga refused to submit to the debut of Uncle Frank's new submission move. Saga would eventually lose to Bellows in the finals, due in no small part to interference by
Chris Colton, who donned a mock Alien Invader 2 bodysuit to help his
Superiority Complex stablemate win the title. However, the fans showed their respects for Saga and his "never say die" attitude, and
Saga went a long way in proving his ability to overcome the odds, despite facing arguably the three best OSW has to offer.
HIGH SOCIETY INTERNATIONAL OUT FOR 6 MONTHS Christopher Black, one half of the team of High Society International, tore an ACL in his right knee during HSI's upset loss to Reynolds & Bardon at the Field Trip. Black could not be reached for comment following the injury, however it is expected that High
Society International will be out of action until February or March of
2003. LEW SPECTRE SNAPS "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre, long known as "the" advocate of classic technical wrestling, let his emotions after the betrayal by his manager, Richie Ray Vaughn III, get the best of him at the Field
Trip. He brutally attacked his former NCCW rival, "Big Gun" Jim
Sergeant, with a steel chain before Sergeant's first-round loss to
Frank Knight, then got himself disqualified from the Old-School Title tourney when he refused to heed to Referee Bob Walter's warnings.
After the match, Spectre attacked Referee Walter. As a result, Spectre has been suspended for this week's Insession, and has been barred from even ENTERING the Schoolhouse by OSW President Michael
Ice.
LOADS OF NEW SIGNEES OSW went on a massive recruiting binge, with many wrestlers looking to join the hottest young indy fed in the business today.
Among the new signees include young sensation "The Natural" Travis
Cade, rookie tag team Tiger Paws, the soon-to-be controversial Lords of Darkness, the mysterious masked team known as the Hillside
Confessional, and famed indy superstar Madison J. Valentine! Rumor has it that two more wrestlers are already under contract with OSW, though their names have yet to be revealed... and the recruiting isn't over yet!

Deadlines ============= My deadlines policy is simple. Get it in on time, it gets used. Get it in late, you're taking your chances. If I can fit it in without doing a ton of extra work, then I might, but if you REALLY want it on the show... get it in on time. We run bi-weekly, you have plenty of
time. :) Saturday, September 28: Flashes for OSW Flashback Thursday, October 3: Strats for OSW Insession 6. Rankings ============ Courtesy of "The Voice of OSW", Jeff Cunningham. Who's Hot: ---------- 1. Superiority Complex (Chris Colton/Def Bellows/Sexual Energy) Def Bellows... OSW Old-School Champion. Chris Colton... OSW
Television Champion. Sexual Energy... OSW Unified Tag Team Champions.
The SC holds all three OSW titles, and will hold this position until that fact changes. 2. "Fantastic" Frank Knight His only two losses came in a three-way match Knight in which he wasn't involved in the finish, and to Kaz Saga, when he was DQ'ed after already being announced the victor. Uncle Frank has become an ominous presence in OSW, and is undeniably the one man who, above all others, strikes fear into the hearts of his opponents.
3. Kazuyaki Saga Became an instant fan-favorite with the spirit and desire displayed in the Old-School Title tourney, despite the odds being stacked against him every step of the way. The kid took on Chris
Colton, Frank Knight and Def Bellows (all ranked "hotter"), in one night, and lived to tell about it.
4. Reynolds and Bardon Came out of nowhere to nearly become the Unified Tag Team Champions. Solid victories over the 3T0 Irish Mafia and the dangerous
High Society International overshadows the fact that they lost to
Sexual Energy.
5. Dark Soul Despite the loss to Bellows in the Old-School Title tourney. Soul has put together a record to be proud of. Some are starting to question his "big match ability", however, garning the low ranking.
Who's Not: ---------- 1. Superstar Has been unspectacular thus far in the eyes of many, and looked unfocused in the Battle Royal. Superstar has the talent necessary to turn things around, but the question is... will he?
2. The Dime Notes Surprisingly, the Dime Notes have really looked out of focus as of late, and lost their rematch against Sexual Energy in the Joint Tag
Team Title unification match. With the recent influx of tag teams...
are the Dime Notes on the verge getting lost in the fold? 3. "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre Um... what? Brawling? From Spectre? It's more apparent now than ever that Spectre is lost. focus-wise, and OSW officials are carefully watching every move Spectre makes, to ensure things don't get out of control.
4. "Big Gun" Jim Sergeant Despite being loved by the fans, Sergeant has been out-performed his last couple of matches. The Big Gun is looking to turn things around, but the path is far from short.
5. "Whiskey" Jack Collins He won't be here next Update. However, Collins' record is less-than-impressive, despite his ability to get under the skin of the
Television Champion, Chris Colton. Collins is still a great performer though, and only gets this spot due to the pre-Field Trip roster cleanout.
Most Popular ------------ 1. Dark Soul By the end of the Bellows/Soul match, you'd think the crowd was ready to riot. Soul is the world's favorite underdog, though he's been shut out when it counts.
2. Kazuyaki Saga Giving Soul a real run for his money in the "favorite underdog" category. Saga's courage under fire and willingness to step into the ring with Def Bellows after being beaten around repeatedly during the
Field Trip, Saga bought himself a place in the hearts of OSW fans.
3. "Whiskey" Jack Collins Helped Kaz Saga end Chris Colton's losing streak at the Field
Trip, and saved Saga from a post-match beatdown by Colton. Collins has seemed to get under Colton's skin more than anyone has thus far.
4. Calvin "Earth Warrior" Reynolds Despite his odd appearance, Reynolds has gained the fans respect the old-fashioned way... clean, fair wrestling. His partner, Albert
Bardon, isn't quite as revered... but the fans love the Earth Warrior.
5. The Dime Notes Come on, really... who DOESN'T like Darby Sharpe? The fans were disappointed in their loss to Sexual Energy at the Field Trip, but they still love these guys tremendously.
Most Hated ---------- 1. Superiority Complex (Chris Colton/Def Bellows/Sexual Energy) You just THOUGHT everyone hated them going into the Field Trip... wait until you hear the reaction from the crowd next Insession, when the Superiority Complex appears with all three OSW titles.
2. "Fantastic" Frank Knight Almost single-handedly turned Kaz Saga into one of the most beloved wrestlers in OSW. Knight is a vicious, maniacal monster who thrives on pain. And besides that... he's just plain scary. Would
YOU want to step into the ring with him?
3. "Phenomenon" Tyro Marvel Masked Man #2? Huh? Marvel was NEVER as hated under the mask as he is now that he's shed it. Took WWO World Champion Dark Soul to the limit in the Old-School Title tourney, and stole a kiss from Candy
Malone. How's that for a night?
4. "Strictly Business" Lew Spectre His freakish run of violence at the Field Trip did little to make
Spectre less hated. In fact, assaulting the fan-favorite Jim
Sergeant, then OSW's head referee, showed that Spectre is as confused as ever... and possibly more dangerous than he's ever been.
5. High Society International Hell, Christopher Black severely hurt his knee, and the fans
CHEERED as he was helped to the back.
