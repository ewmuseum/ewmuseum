Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NIWA, the New Internet Wrestling Association, was founded around August 1996 on the newsgroup rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy. Its founding post does not survive in this record; what does is a single follow-up, posted almost a year later, on 3 August 1997.

That later post is not a card or a roster but a plea for help: membership, it says, was “raising very slowly.” In answer, NIWA offered a straightforward trade to any other league willing to send members its way — a link on its own links page and airtime for that league's ads on NIWA's own cards, “to help our membership.” It says nothing of a match, a title, or a wrestler; only of a federation asking the community around it for help keeping its doors open, and admitting, in passing, that it ran on “a weird money system.”

NIWA never recorded its own end. But elsewhere in the same newsgroup's fiction, by November 1997, a wrestler was already billed as its first world champion — spoken of once, in passing, as history rather than as an active federation. Whether the membership drive that August ever turned around is not something NIWA's own surviving record answers.
