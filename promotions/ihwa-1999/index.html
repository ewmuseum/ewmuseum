A hardcore-themed federation run by two co-presidents, staging a five-pay-per-view yearly schedule and a seven-championship title ladder behind an on-screen broadcast team. Six wrestlers are recorded from its founding information pack; no card or result survives. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The promotion opened on 4 January 1999 as the Hardcore Wrestling Association of Rawness, co-founded by “Ravishing” Richard Rainer and “The Unforgiven” Dave Pietka, described in its own information pack as ten-year best friends and former wrestling partners, Rainer having been Pietka's protégé. By 26 January it had renamed itself the International Hardcore Wrestling Association, keeping every rule, show, and title exactly as written.

It planned five pay-per-views across the year, from a 14 February debut, Cupid's Massacre, through an all-gimmick-match Halloween card, Hardcore Horror Nights, to a Christmas-season finale, The Night the Reindeer Died, and seven championships split across “World,” “Hardcore,” and “Bloody Sunday” tiers, each with its own tag-team counterpart.

Its own Seven Commandments set the house tone — no complaining, no favoritism, and the co-presidents' word final and undisputed — but by that 26 January update it was candid about where it actually stood: closed to new heel singles wrestlers and thin on tag teams, “we only have one team. Tres Pathetic, no?”

No card, roster reveal, or result document survives; whether it reached its planned 14 February 1999 debut isn't in the surviving record.
