If you want HARDCORE, be a part of the IHWA!" =============================================================== Index: I. Introduction II. Roleplaying & Strats III. Shows IV. Pay-Per-Views V. Rules VI. Introduction We are the IHWA, Resistance is Futile, you will be assimilated! The International Hardcore Wrestling Association is the brainchild of two of wrestling's most notorious minds. "Ravishing" Richard Rainer and "The
Unforgiven" Dave Pietka, both best friends of ten years. Pietka's name being synonymous with treachery, deception, cunning, and most of all, Wrestling Gold;
he is more infamous than he is famous. Rainer being the brains behind The Fabulous Fisticuffs and the center of a DWO Scandal concerning their Heavyweight Championship Belt; he is considered to be almost as cunning as Pietka. The two being long time partners(Rainer being Pietka's protege, believe it or not), they decided to open up a federation of their own. Rainer wanted something that would attract a crowd, bring in the money, and grow some magnificent talent. Pietka wanted something along the lines of a bloodbath, a web of lies and deceit, and a Japanese Death Match all in one. Thus the IHWA was born between the two minds.
The purpose of the IHWA is to make some talent, while causing them to go to near-superhuman lengths to win matches and titles. Hey, legends are made this way, right? The participating wrestlers are paid major money to fight, and even more to fight in special gimmick matches created by Co-President Pietka. The winners of these matches are usually rewarded in another way by Co-President
Rainer. The IHWA is NOT for the squeamish, but for guys with balls bigger than their brains. So if you want hardcore, watch ECW; but if you want HARDCORE, be a part of the IHWA... IT ONE OF THE NEW FUCKING RULES OF LIFE!
=============================================================== II. Role-playing and Strats There will be Flash Sheets sent out whenever enough flashes are gathered, and a flash show called "Rawness Weekly" will be sent out every Friday. Here are the rules of role-playing.
Item One: A rule that all of you know. QUALITY OVER QUANTITY! If someone sends me 500 flashes that only say "I'm gonna whoop you're ass because I can", and someone else sends me a VERY LONG flash that actually goes somewhere... well, you tell ME who would win!
Item Two: Cursing will be allowed ONLY to a certain fucking extreme. Flashes with a curse word in every fucking sentence won't be accepted. Get the fucking
point? :) Item Three: Any Racial Slurs or Shoots will be immediatedly discarded. The handler who sent in the flash will have his wrestler be exposed to the ULTIMATE
HUMILIATION. Losing five matches straight to wrestlers of JTTS Status and a dishonorable ejection from the HWAoR.
Item Four: For a role-play to count towards a match, it must be sent in TWO DAYS before the match is to take place! Anything after that won't matter because the winners will already be selected.
Item Five: This next one may sound unfair, but it is to make sure that Quality is kept in check. You are limited to one of the two listed below, and there will be no exceptions.
1) Three role-plays a week. 2) Two role-plays and an in-ring Strats are a different story. Strats cannot contain anything involving murder, homicide, disemboweling, or severe injury(paralysis and soforth). Anything else is acceptable. Strats must be sent in THREE DAYS before the match is to take place. You are permitted to have UNLIMITED strats.
=============================================================== III. Shows The IHWA will have a flash show, a house card, and play-by-play card. "Rawness Weekly" - A flash show hosted by Alex Treynor and "Loudmouth" Vinnie Rose. Will feature flashes from wrestlers, weekly rankings, upcoming cards, and rumors(sent in by handlers).
"The House of Hardcore" - The IHWA's house show. A summary card consisting of five to seven matches, usually superstar against jobber, with one or two real matches. Title matches will RARELY occur on this card.
"Sunday, Bloody Sunday" - The IHWA's Play-by-Play show. Hosted by Alex Treynor, "Loudmouth" Vinnie Rose, and "Alluring" Cassandra Candress. Consisting of two summery jobber matches, and five play-by-play matches. One title match is guarenteed on the card.
=============================================================== IV. Pay-Per-Views The IHWA will hold five Pay-Per-Views. "Cupid's Massacre" - Held on February 14th. The year's first card. A tournament is held, and the finalists fight in a Special Gimmick Match created by
Co-President Pietka for either a title, a title-shot, or some other special prize. "Spring Fever" - Held on May 6th. All titles are on the line, and the main event is a Twenty-Five Man "Over The Top Rope" Weapons Rumble, the winner recieving any type of match he wishes.
"Invasion of the Athletes" - Held on August 19th. The IHWA invites superstars from other federations to try to "invade" the IHWA, pitting them against the
IHWA's top superstars(Basically, our top ten against them). "Hardcore Horror Nights" - Held on Holloween. ALL HARDCORE GIMMICK MATCHES! "The Night The Reindeer Died" - Held December 18th. Titleholders pick their opponents from the top contenders in their class. Two "Deadly Drawing" tag matches are schedualed, with the winner of the two going up against each other, then the winning team going up against each other.
=============================================================== V. Rules All feds have to have rules. Here art thy Seven Commandments. I. Thou Shalt Not Bitch. (No bitching of any kind will be tolerated!) II. Thou Shalt Not Suck-up. (Brown-nosing gets you nowhere. We don't play favorites.) III. Thou Shalt Not Blame Thy Presidents For Thy Own Faults. (If you lose, it's because your opponent out-flashed and out-stratted you. It's not because we don't like you.)
IV. Thy Shall Honor The Decisions Of Thy Presidents. (The Presidents decisions are final, and are not open to dispute.)
V. Thou Shalt Not Created Outlandish Creatures Of Fantasy. (Hey, we may be Hardcore, but we're not straying from reality. We will only accept two/three wrestlers between the heights of 7' and 7'6", first come first serve. After that, nothing over 7'. Weight cannot exceed 599 pounds.)
VI. Thou Shalt Not Shoot! (NO SHOOTING IS PERMITTED!) VII. Thou WILL Have Fun. (This is a GAME, people!) =============================================================== VI. Titles & Challenges There are seven belts in the IHWA. "IHWA World Wrestling Championship" - The Granddaddy of them all. The Elite of the Elite hold this belt, and defend it at every Pay-Per-View and at least once every other month.
"IHWA World Hardcore Championship" - The Son of the Granddaddy of them all. The Hardcore of the Hardcore hold this belt, and is only up for grabs during Gimmick Matches. Defended at every Pay-Per-View and at least once every other month. "IHWA Multi-National Championship" - Our "Intercontinental Belt". Defended at every Pay-Per-View and at least once every month.
"IHWA Bloody Sunday Championship" - The only singles belt that ISN'T defended at every Pay-Per-View, but IS defended every "Sunday, Bloody Sunday" card.
"IHWA World Wrestling Tag Championship" - The main Tag Team Title. Defended at every Pay-Per-View and at least once every other month.
"IHWA World Hardcore Tag Championship" - Defended only in Gimmick Matches. Defended every Pay-Per-View and at least once every other month. "IHWA Bloody Sunday Tag Championship" - The only tag belt that ISN'T defended at every Pay-Per-View, but IS defended every "Sunday, Bloody Sunday" card.
This is the way challenging for the belt goes. I. Champions may challenge whomever they wish, but must accept at least one challenge per their title. II. The Top 5 Wrestlers may challenge for the World Wrestling or the World Hardcore Championship. III. The Top 10 Wrestlers may challenge for the Multi-National Championship. IV. The Top 3 Tag Teams may challenge for the World Wrestling Tag or World Hardcore Tag Championship(Number of teams with the ability to challenge will increase as number of teams increases).
V. Challengers for the Bloody Sunday Singles and Tag Championships are chosen at the discretion of the Presidents. =============================================================== VII. HWAoR Staff(Characters) "Ravishing" Richard Rainer - A former Semi-Pro Wrestler. 24 years old, 6'4", and 250 pounds, he's best known for controversy and his personal flair. He won't accept any crap from anyone, not even most of the people he works with.
He doesn't believe in the IHWA's hired Security force, so he has his own personal bodyguard that accompanies him at all times.
"The Unforgiven" Dave Pietka - 19 years old, 6'1", 160 pounds, and a legend in his mind and in the minds of others. His name has gotten him fame, fortune, misery, and just about every other good and bad thing in life. His creative mind makes for a great factory for Gimmick Matches, as he so loves watching others fight in these styles of fights. There are only a few people he loves.
His girlfriend, his mother(don't most people?), and the bat always by his side. He's a cunning person, and is never to be taken lightly. "Dragon's Rage" Xiao Li Kui - The 6'7", 350 pound Powerhouse from Beijing, China serves as Co-President Rainer personal bodyguard. He is a member of the powerful Xiao Li Crime Family back in his homeland. His father is Xiao Li Chan, a known politician and head of the Xiao Li family. Kui attacks when ordered, and protects his employer/friend at all costs. This gives Co-President Rainer two things. A very powerful ally, and connections to the Chinese Mafia.
Alex Treynor - Your typical commentator. He's a former english professor from Arlington, Vermont. Very articulate, and sometimes appalled by all the madness and carnage occuring in the ring. He's a face, but he detests Co-President
Pietka for his so-called "Madness". "Loudmouth" Vinnie Rose - From Da Bronx, Wit Love. This guy is mostly all-talk, but does like to get into a fight or two. He loves the rule breakers, and claims to be Co-President Pietka's Number One fan because of his, as Alex
Treynor put it, so-called "Madness". "Alluring" Cassandra Candress - Voluptuous, Sultry, and able to make any man melt into a puddle of goo with a flick of her eye-lashes. She loves every wrestler that walks into the ring to fight a Gimmick Match, but won't let any of them lay a hand on her... lest they be faced with the wrath of "Marz".
"Marz" - The Head of IHWA's Security, personally selected and hired by Co-President Pietka. This 7'2", 420 pound mountain of muscle could practically be engaged to Candress... if he wasn't her brother. He won't let anyone get too close with her, but Cassandra can easily steady his hand. He ejects anyone he is told to without asking questions.
