Staff IV. Cards V. Titles VI. Pay-Per-Views VII. Application     I. Introduction Many on the internet may know me by the alias of Brickhouse Brown or
Biggz74. I had a very successful 17 month run as President of the now defunct NCWA , one of the better feds on the net in its day , but as do all good things it came to an end but now I have returned!  My newest project ... USxtreme Wrestling.     Now despite the title of the fed , USxtreme does not  brew from the imagination of a twelve year old that dreams of barbwire and razorblades.  No in fact I am in my mid twenties and believe in the traditional ways of wrestling , like the ol' NWA  , the days of Dusty Rhodes , Nikita Kolhoff and T.A. Magnum!     USx is a booked fed , that heavily relies on roleplays(Flashes) to determine win/losses and angles.  Simply put ... you give us quality and we'll give you quality! No you don't have to flash every single day to get anywhere , just give us 2 or more quality flashes a week is all we ask.   One more thing ... we are an original wrestler fed only! No parodies of real wrestlers will be accepted.     II. Rules   1. Each handler is allowed to control up to two ORIGINAL wrestler.   2. You may curse in role-plays. However!  Anything more then what you'd hear on TV requires censoring.  Also NO! racial or personal attacks will be tolerated!   3. Do not use another wrestler in your flash without his/her consent! By this we mean their physical presence.   4. We are a closed flash system , this means that all rp's are sent directly to the president - [name withheld]   5. We require a minimum of (2) flashes a week.  If you cannot handle that then don't join!   6. Strategy's , Interference requests or any other requests should be sent to the president( [name withheld] ).  Please note that match strategy's will not play any role in the match outcome , that is determined by flashs.   7. No crying allowed!  If you lose a match , then you lose a match!  I will do my best to be fair in my decisions and will pick the wrestler I feel worked harder and better for the win. Afterall it is just a game and its about having fun.   8. Remember when it comes to flashing ... It's quality! Not quanity.     III. Events/PPV's   Tuesday Night Xtreme's The USx's main card of the week.  It has 6-8 matches , plus interviews and is in full-commentary.     Saturday Night Rewind Weekly show that looks back at the past weeks action and includes a couple taped match-ups from the untelevised house shows.  Typically will have
4 matchs , plus the
look back.     There will be monthly PPV spectaculars in addition to the weekly events. Each is announced as they arise.     V. Titles   USx Elite Championship Title The highest honor in the USx. Only the best will carry this strap around their waist! You must be among the top three contenders to challenge for it. Defended at least once a month , but can be defended more if champion chooses.     USx United States Championship Title Second highest honor in the USx. It takes alot of heart and desire to get this far. You must be among the top five contenders to challenge for it. Defended at least twice a month.     USx Challenger Title: Not the most prestigous , but it takes alot of heart and soul to carry this strap! Defended every Tuesday Night and the matchs stipulations vary. You must be among the top ten wrestlers to challenge for it.     VI. Becoming a member. Once you have read the above information then you are ready to join!
