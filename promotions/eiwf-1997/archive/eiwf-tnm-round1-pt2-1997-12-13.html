TNM World Title Tournament, Round 1 Part 2 (13 December 1997)

13 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mark Carroll: We want to welcome you to the rest of the 1st round matches in the TNM World Title Tournament. Larry James: By the end of this month, we intend to have a new champion crowned. However, there have been rumors that another wrestler won the first tournament and says he is the champion. Mark Carroll: Well, if he is the champion, then he needs to come forward. Our first match is between the EIWF champion Firecracker and Greg Kam. **************************** Firecracker vs. Greg Kam w/ Sunny **************************** Greg Kam brought his version of Sunny to the ring. Firecracker was in control early on. Firecracker injured his back and Greg Kam used it to end the match quickly. Kam put Firecracker in the cobra clutch and got the submission at 7:43. M.C.: Kam pulls the upset. Firecracker will have to take his belt and go home. L.J.: Kam moves on and proves he is better than the EIWF champ. Our next match is between Ghost and Frapp Assassin #1. ****************************** Ghost w/ Snowy vs. Frapp Assassin #1 ****************************** Frapp Assassin #1 was the better wrestler early on. He used several submission holds to wear down Ghost. Ghost came back and managed to get a cut opened on Assassin's forehead. Ghost executed a reverse DDT to get the pin at 14:48. M.C.: Ghost advances and will face Greg Kam in the next round. L.J.: Our next match is between Beastie Boy and Frapp Assassin #2. M.C.: The Frapp Assassins came looking for the TNM World Tag Team Title Tournament, but entered the singles tournament first. *************************** Beastie Boy vs. Frapp Assassin #2 *************************** Beastie Boy started slowly. Beastie Boy came to life and took control at the five minute mark. From about the 10 minute mark on, the match became a see-saw affair. Frapp Assassin #2 executed the Ripper to get the pin at 18:25. M.C.: Frapp Assassin #2 tames the beast and moves on. Next, The Ghetto Boy will take on the Midnight Frapper. L.J.: How many Frappers are there? M.C.: This is the last one. ********************************************* The Ghetto Boy w/ Chris Furman vs. The Midnight Frapper ********************************************* The match was a high impact, see-saw affair. Both men dished out alot of punishment in the first 10 minutes. The Ghetto Boy executed The Ghetto Bomb to get the pin at 17:46. M.C.: The Ghetto Boy advances to the second round. His tag team partner, Swap Meet Louie will face Bodybreaker Burnet. ********************************************** Swap Meet Louie w/ Chris Furman vs. Bodybreaker Burnet ********************************************** Burnet had a cut over his eye early in the match. This only served to make Burnet mad and he dished out the punishment to Louie. Swap Meet Louie was disqualified at 11:58 for not breaking a chokehold. L.J.: Burnet advances because his opponent can't follow the rules. Our final match is Blueline Basher Lund vs. Jordan The Demolisher. ************************************* Blueline Basher Lund vs. Jordan The Demolisher ************************************* Lund got the advantage in the match around the 4 minute mark. Demolisher was in trouble around the 10 minute mark. Blueline Basher Lund used The Crosscheck to get Jordan The Demolisher to submit at 14:38. M.C.: Thanks for joining us. Tune in Tuesday December 16, 1997 for the second round.
