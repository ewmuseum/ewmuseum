TNM World Title Tournament, Quarterfinals (19 December 1997)

19 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mark Carroll: Welcome to the quarterfinals of the TNM World Title Tournament. Tonight, 8 men will step into the ring and only four will be invited back next week. Larry James: After many threats by lawyers, "The Jiggster" Jimmy Jazz will be included in the tournament. He will face Blueline Basher Lund. Good Luck!! M.C.: Our first match is between The Demolitionist and Kevin Kirker. The Demolitionist has been destroying the competition while Kirker has just been sliding by. ****************************************** The Demolitionist w/ The Lovely Leah vs. Kevin Kirker ****************************************** The Demolitionist lived up to his name and destroyed Kirker in the opening moments. Kirker did mount some comebacks, but all were short lived. Kevin Kirker was disqualified for hitting The Demolitionist with brass knuckles at 11:42. L.J.: Kirker was getting beat and tries to use the shortcut to win a match. M.C.: Our next match is between UPS and Rhett Smith. UPS lost the EIWF North American Title last night, so he should be out for a win tonight. **************** UPS vs. Rhett Smith **************** Smith took control quickly. UPS seemed to be distracted. UPS did comeback and take over the match. UPS executed the Letter Bomb to get the pin at 19:54. L.J.: UPS advances and will face The Demolitionist. Our next match is between Ghost and Frapp Assassin #2. M.C.: If Ghost keeps focused, he will advance. ****************************** Ghost w/ Snowy vs. Frapp Assassin #2 ****************************** Ghost came out on fire and took it to Assassin #2. Assassin #2 did not give up the match easily. Ghost used the Final Breath to get the pin at 12:34. M.C.: Ghost advances and will face the winner of our next match. L.J.: Blueline Basher Lund will take on lawyer boy, "The Jiggster" Jimmy Jazz. *************************************** Blueline Basher Lund vs. "The Jiggster" Jimmy Jazz *************************************** Lund had no tape on his opponent to watch. This did not matter as Lund dominated the matchup. Jazz executed his finisher The Jigger which had no effect on Lund. Lund got the pin at 20:10. M.C.: Thank you for joining us. The semifinals will be held on Tuesday, December 23.
