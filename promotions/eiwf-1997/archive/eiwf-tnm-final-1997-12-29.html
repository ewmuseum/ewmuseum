TNM World Title Tournament, Final (29 December 1997)

29 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Before I add the results here, I want to tell you about the TNM World Tag Team Title Tournament. I will be accepting tag teams that are from TNM V6.2+ or lower. The export file must be zipped in order to be used. So far, 2 teams have entered, Revolutions Raiders and The Hardliners. Here are highlights from the EIWF pay-per view that hosted the tournament final. (Thunder.....Lightning) M.C.: Wait....Whats going on here? There's a lady in black coming down the aisle....I'm being told her name is Sexy Shannon....Theres smoke coming down the aisle....here comes the man who claims to be the undefeated TNM World Champion, "Darkside" Tim Johns. Shannon: Ladies and Gentlemen....the Current...UNDEFEATED..TNM WORLD CHAMPION, DARKSIDE TIM JOHNS! Darkside: First off, Larry James, Watch your tongue, before I have you swallow it. You'll know afraid when Im done with you. Ladies and Gentlemen, I have returned to where it all happened, to defend this title against the winner of the Title Tournament. I went through hell and back to get this title. I went through Iron Man Matches, and Table Matches to get this title and no one will take it from me..I Will wish all the luck to the finalists, but when the winner faces me, you will feel the full power of the Darkside...and REST IN PEACE L.J.: Wait! Now they are telling us that Robert Poole is coming down the aisle....Does this mean ROBERTS RAIDERS will be involved with the TNM World Tag Team Tournament?!? Darkside: Now to hand this time over to Robert Poole Poole: Ladies and Gentlemen....I have helped lead Tim Johns, and others to Championship Titles...and now....I will help lead Darkside and his Tag Team Partner to the TNM WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP...ladies and gentlemen.....heres Big Daddy Cool Justin Donovan...to team with Darkside Tim Johns....YES! Revolutions Raiders will be fighting for the TNM World Tag Titles. Teams Beware....The Raiders are here to stay... M.C.: Well Larry, you asked for it and he showed up. L.J.: Let's go to the ring for next match *************** Later in the card *************** L.J.: Next is the match of the evening. UPS and Blueline Basher Lund will battle for the TNM World Title. M.C.: I have been told that the winner will face "Darkside" Tim Johns sometime in the near future to resolve the title situation. TNM World Title Match ************************** UPS vs. Blueline Basher Lund ************************** This was the finals of the TNM World Title Tournament. [name withheld], EIWF President and tournament organizer brought the belt to the ring. Lund was quickly in control and it looked like he would win the title in record time. UPS did not give up and managed to cut Lund later in the match. UPS recovered and nailed Lund with the Letter Bomb to get the pin at 17:58.
