TNM World Title Tournament, Round 1 (7 December 1997)

7 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mark Carroll: Welcome to the TNM World Title Tournament!! This is the first round. We will have eight matches tonight. More first round matches will follow on Friday. Larry James: Wrestlers have come from various wrestling federations to the home arena of the EIWF, The Mark of The Quad Cities in Moline, Illinois. M.C.: Our first match of the night is between The Hawaiian Crippler representing NeCW and Zanathar from TWD. L.J.: I have no idea what abilities these guys have, so sit back and enjoy. *********************************** The Hawaiian Crippler vs. Zanathar w/ Violet *********************************** Crippler attacked Zanathar before the bell. That did not Zanathar as he quickly took control. Hawaiian Crippler took Zanathar's best shots and still got up. The Hawaiian Crippler ended the match with the Hawaiian Heart Punch to get the pin at 10:27. M.C.: Zanathar was impressive, but The Hawaiian Crippler used the heart punch to stop Zanathar. L.J.: The Hawaiian Crippler advances to the next round and Zanathar gets a ticket home. Our next match is between The Demolitionist from WCF and Deese Truxion from TWD. M.C.: Well, TWD could be out of the tournament after this match. *************************************************** The Demolitionist w/ The Lovely Leah vs. Deese Truxion w/ Violet *************************************************** The Demolitionist started fast in this match. Demolitionist lived up to his name as he destroyed Truxion during the majority of the match. The Demolitionist tore Truxion apart and then used an armbar to get the submission win at 10:58. L.J.: Wow! The Demolitionist is unbelievable. This guy will definitely go far in the tournament. Truxion gets a ticket home with his friend Zanathar. M.C.: Now, we enter a phase of the tournament that is qualifiers for the EIWF. The EIWF is the host of this tournament and all but two wrestlers will square off against each other to decide who their representative will be. L.J.: Let me translate for the humanoids. Kehrberg just didn't have the courage to tell some of his wrestlers no. M.C.: Careful Larry, you could lose your job. ******************************** Kevin Savage w/ Tara vs. The Hartbraker ******************************** Both men have been champions in the EIWF. These two wrestlers know each other well, so there would be very few mistakes. Hartbraker eventually took control and then went to work on finishing the match. Savage became frustrated and nailed Hartbraker between the eyes with a set of brass knuckles. Hartbraker was awarded the match by DQ at 19:23. L.J.: Hartbraker advances and will face another EIWF wrestler in the second round. M.C.: Arn Austin takes on Kevin Kirker next. ******************************* "Double A" Arn Austin vs. Kevin Kirker ******************************* Kirker has been on a hot streak in the EIWF recently. Austin was clearly the better wrestler in this match. The referee went blind temporarily and Kirker hit Austin with a set of brass knuckles. Kirker then covered and got the pin at 17:05. L.J.: Gee, the referee doesn't see it and Kirker wins, but Savage gets disqualified. What is the deal with that? M.C.: I don't know Larry, why don't you go ask them? Next, UPS takes on Onslaught. *************** UPS vs. Onslaught *************** UPS held all three titles at one time in the EIWF, so he knows how to win. It did not take UPS long to take control. Onslaught hung in there and made UPS work. UPS executed The Letter Bomb and got the pin at 19:20. M.C.: UPS executes his patented move and gets anther win. L.J.: Hell's Knight takes on and a wrestling legend in Michael Faulkenberg. ************************************* Hell's Knight vs. Michael Faulkenberg w/ Sunny ************************************* Hell's Knight must not have known about Faulkenberg's reputation, because he was in control from the start. It took Faulkenberg 6 minutes to really get going. Michael Faulkenberg put Hell's Knight away with the Sweet Chin Music to get the pin at 22:07. M.C.: The legend wins again. Faulkenberg advances toward the only world title. L.J.: The Terminator will take on Rhett Smith next. ************************ The Terminator vs. Rhett Smith ************************ Rhett Smith has seen most of his success in the tag team ranks. He started good in this singles match. Rhett Smith destroyed The Terminator and left him laying in a heap outside the ring. Smith won by countout at 6:14. M.C.: Smith has just terminated The Terminator. This is an incredible win for him. L.J.: In our final match, Top Dawg will take on Insane Brian Willerd. This should be fun. A guy with rabies and a guy who is crazy. *************************** Top Dawg vs. Insane Brian Willerd *************************** Willerd held his own until he jumped off the top rope and nailed only the concrete floor. Top Dawg quickly rolled Willerd into the ring and applied a Boston Crab on him. The referee stopped the match at 4:24 when Willerd was rendered unconscious. M.C.: Thank you for tuning in today. Join us Friday night for the rest of the first round matches.
