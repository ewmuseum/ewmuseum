TNM World Title Tournament, Round 2 (16 December 1997)

16 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mark Carroll: Welcome to the second round of the TNM World Title Tournament. Tonight we have seven matches in the second round. Seven men will advance and seven will go home. Larry James: We have heard from the alledged TNM World Champion. His name is Darkside Tim Johns. He claims he is the TNM World Champion and that this tournament is not legitimate. M.C.: Well, my question is where is he? Show up and lets see how good you really are. L.J.: Our first match is between The Hawaiian Crippler and The Demolitionist. Both men won their matches in about 10 minutes, so this should be an even match. ******************************************************** The Hawaiian Crippler vs. The Demolitionist w/ The Lovely Leah ******************************************************** The match was even for the first 6 minutes. Then, The Demolitionist took over. The Hawaiian Crippler had a cut opened on his forehead during a battle on the floor. It was looking bad for The Hawaiian Crippler. The Demolitionist executed The Doomsday Decapitator to get the pin at 21:01. M.C.: The Demolitionist batters The Hawaiian Crippler and gets the pin. L.J.: Crippler is a bloody, broken heap in the ring. He will have to limp home to his federation. OUr next match is between Hartbraker and Kevin Kirker. M.C.: Hartbraker defeated Kevin Savage by DQ and Kirker pinned Arn Austin at 17:05. ************************ Hartbraker vs. Kevin Kirker ************************ Kirker had the match of his wrestling life against Hartbraker. Hartbraker could not shake Kirker and Kirker got the pin at 18:13 to pull the upset. L.J.: Kevin Kirker upsets Hartbraker and will advance to the third round against The Demolitionist. M.C.: Good luck Kirker, you will need it. Our next match is between Michael Faulkenberg and UPS. ********************************** Michael Faulkenberg w/ Sunny vs. UPS ********************************** Faulkenberg made his name in the EIWF and GIWA. UPS did not care and took it to the star. UPS dominated the first 10 minutes. Faulkenberg mounted a comeback only to have his head slammed to the mat from a Letter Bomb. UPS used the Letter Bomb to get the pin at 14:12. L.J.: UPS makes a case for him winning this tournament. The match between him and The Demolitionist in the semifinals should be exciting. M.C.: Next, tag team partners Top Dawg and Rhett Smith will square off. *********************** Top Dawg vs. Rhett Smith *********************** Both men came to the ring together. The winner would get to face UPS. Both men kept it clean. The momentum swung back and forth. Rhett Smith got the pin at 17:27. Both men shook hands and went back to the locker room together. M.C.: These two will still stay together as a tag team. The match went well for them and Rhett Smith moved on. L.J.: Greg Kam made the EIWF World Champion submit in the first round. This should give him a big advantage in the second round. *********************************** Greg Kam w/ Sunny vs. Ghost w/ Snowy *********************************** Kam brought out his version of Sunny. But, she was not much help as Ghost took control from the opening bell. Ghost executed The Final Breath and got the pin at 8:30. M.C.: Well Larry, I guess that defeating Firecracker did not give Kam any advantage. L.J.: I have to admit, Ghost was very impressive. The third round is going to be exciting. Three of the five competitors who have moved on can destroy their opponents at anytime. M.C.: Frapp Assassin #2 will take on The Ghetto Boy next. ************************************************ Frapp Assassin #2 vs. The Ghetto Boy w/ Chris Furman ************************************************ The match was even for a long time. The Ghetto Boy used The Ghetto Bomb on the floor to take over. The Ghetto Boy was on his way to victory when Furman interfered and got Ghetto Boy disqualified at 15:11. L.J.: Frapp Assassin #2 wins the match as Furman cost his man the match. Furman will have a lot to answer for. M.C.: Our final match is between Bodybreaker Burnet and Blueline Basher Lund. *************************************** Bodybreaker Burnet vs. Blueline Basher Lund *************************************** Burnet was better in the early going. Lund turned it around when he smashed Burnet into the steps and opened a cut. Burnet fought hard, but Lund got the pin at 18:24. L.J.: Lund gets a hard fought win and advances. M.C.: I have been handed the pairings for the quarterfinals. The quarterfinals will take place on Friday December 19, 1997. The Demolitionist takes on Kevin Kirker. L.J.: UPS takes on Rhett Smith. M.C.: Ghost takes on Frapp Assassin #2 and Blueline Basher Lund will get a bye to the semifinals. L.J.: Thank you for joining us. Tune in Friday for the next round.
