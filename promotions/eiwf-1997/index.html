Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This EIWF recruited roleplayers through a short Usenet ad on 7 April 1997, pointing them to its own homepage with no further detail beyond an invitation to join. Its surviving record then skips straight to December, when it hosted a TNM World Title Tournament out of its own home arena, the Mark of the Quad Cities in Moline, Illinois, drawing wrestlers in from visiting federations such as NeCW alongside its own returning former champions.

The first round, broadcast 7 December 1997, ran eight matches and settled two of its own outcomes by disqualification — one wrestler brass-knuckling his opponent between the eyes for a DQ loss, another using the same knuckles, missed by the referee, to win clean. UPS, described on air as a wrestler who “held all three titles at one time in the EIWF,” advanced with his finisher, the Letter Bomb.

UPS carried that same finisher through to the tournament final on 29 December 1997, beating Blueline Basher Lund for the vacant TNM World Title in a match the federation's own president, as tournament organizer, personally brought the belt to ringside for.

The title was disputed the moment it was won. A masked figure calling himself “Darkside” Tim Johns interrupted the broadcast billed as the “undefeated” TNM World Champion from a separate tournament this collection holds no record of, and challenged the winner to a unification match. The same segment unveiled Revolutions Raiders, a tag team pairing Johns with Justin Donovan, as the second entrant into a newly announced TNM World Tag Team Title Tournament, alongside a team called the Hardliners.

Neither dispute is resolved in the surviving record: the tournament's own semifinal round is missing outright, and no document survives showing whether UPS and Johns ever met, or whether the tag team tournament was ever run.
