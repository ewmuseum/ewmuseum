NGEN Cherry Blossom Chaos: Willard vs. Astros

10 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 10 May 2010 [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it is time for Donovan Astros against “The Prophecy” Adrian Willard.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: I've been looking forward to this one, Donovan Astros is a three time world heavyweight and one hell of a technician and he isn't afraid to let the whole world know it.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: It isn't like the Prophecy doesn't have an attitude of his own. He may be a rookie, but he holds himself like a veteran. He is going to bring the fight to Astros. He better be ready.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: If Willard doesn't live up to expectations, the Freak will give Willard the backside of his pimp hand.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: I like the freak, he reminds me of myself.[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: You've never been that hip in all of your life. You are closer to hip replacement.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: The Freak is going to stir up a lot of trouble before all is said in done in NGEN. Most people have a mute button, but he doesn't. He says whatever he wants to say and he doesn't care what people think. You have to wonder what he'll attempt to do at ringside to try to steer this his guys' favor.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: I could see him trying to pop Astros in the mouth. He better keep an eye on him.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, lets send this match down to ringside and get this one underway![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif](CUEUP: “All Eyes on Me (Bittersweet Remix) by 2Pac”)[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Los Angeles, California, standing at six foot three, two hundred and thirty seven pounds, a three time World Heavyweight Champion... DONOVAN ASTROS![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]:onovan Astros walks arrogantly down the aisle and enters the ring to the boos of the crowd::[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent...[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif](CUEUP: “[/FONT]Tribulations” by LCD Sound Systems) [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]RING ANNOUNCER: From Chicago, Illinois, standing at six four, two hundred and eighty five pounds, “THE PROPHECY” ADRIAN WILLARD![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]::Willard walks down to the ring with the Freak, with the Freak yelling at the fans not to to touch him and cursing out the fans. Willard enters the ring::[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: The referee is making sure Astros stays back so we don't have a repeat of what happen in the last match when a rookie was entering the ring. Willard is in and the ref is calling for the bell to start this off![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]*bell rings *[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: Willard walks up to Astros and Astros is egging him on.... WILLARD PUNCHES HIM IN THE FACE![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: Ooooh![/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: NOW DONOVAN ASTROS COUNTERS WITH A HARD CHOP TO THE CHEST![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: Those chops can be like a knife to the chest.[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: PUNCH! CHOP! PUNCH! CHOP! PUNCH! CHOP! These two men are taking turns taking shots to each other![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: These two exchanged a lot of unpleasant remarks to each other in interviews and now when it has come time for the match, they aren't holding back anything in the ring either.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: Willard hits him with a kick to the gut, Willard grabs the arm of Astros and he whips him off the ropes.... WILLARD CLOBBERS HIM WITH A FLYING CLOTHESLINE! [/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: Willard has fifty pounds on Astros and he is going to use that size to his advantage just like he did there.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: Willard picks Astros up off the mat... he drops him with a backbreaker on his knee and Astros rolls over onto his stomach![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: Astros may be superior at the ground game, but if his back is acting up, it will hurt his ability to lock in submissions with any authority.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: The back and the knees effect everything.[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: Willard is stomping away at the back of Astros, adding insult to injury...[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: Willard is a big man so when he lays those sizable boots in, they hurt like hell.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: Now Willard is standing over Astros and he hooks the arms... HE IS LOCKING ON THE CAMEL CLUTCH AND HE SITS DOWN ON ASTROS BACK AND PULLS BACK ON HIS HEAD WITH ALL HIS MIGHT![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: Shades of the Iron Sheik.[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: Things aren't looking good for Astros. Even if he breaks out of this, most likely it will cause some damage.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: Astros is pushing his head forward and he is appear to be trying to move Willard upward from putting his weight down on the hold...[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: This is a smart move, but he still needs to figure out a way to break it.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: ASTROS REELS BACK WITH AN ELBOW RIGHT TO THE SIDE OF THE RIBS AND WILLARD LETS GO OF THE HOLD![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: That is bound to leave a mark.[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: While it is harder to do damage to the side of the ribs than the front, it doesn't hurt on the moment of impact any less.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: WILLARD GETS BACK UP AND HE TRIES TO LOCK ON A GERMAN SUPLEX ON ASTROS... ASTROS WITH THE GO BEHIND... HE LOCKS ON THE ABDOMINAL STRETCH ON WILLARD![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: Astros is going after that area he just smashed with the elbow.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: They referee is checking to see if Willard wants to give up but Willard is telling him no![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: Willard knows that a win of PPV will get him noticed.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: ASTROS IS REACHING BACK AND HE GRABS THE ROPES FOR EXTRA LEVERAGE....[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: The referee keeps on focusing on whether Willard wants to give up and he isn't even seeing what Astros is doing.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: Astros is a ring veteran. He knows when the referee is looking and when he isn't, he knows exactly where is in that ring, which is all traits of a great wrestler.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: I don't doubt Astros ability, which is why it enrages me like it does, he has the skills to do it wihtout bending the rules.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: The referee looks up and Willard lets go of the ropes. The referee sees the rope shaking and then looks back at Willard.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: Why does the referee ropes are shaking?[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: The referee can't call what he doesn't see. He isn't a mind reader. For all he knows, the Freak on the outside could have touched the ropes to make it look like Astros was using him.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: But the Freak on the other side...[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: Details, details...[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: NOW ASTROS IS REACHING BACK AGAIN AND GRABBING THE ROPES...[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: COME ON REF![/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: Willard looks like he is in a lot of pain. This move certain is doing tis damage and this extra leverage isn't helping Willard at all.. There referee is checking to see if Willard wants to give in..... THE REFEREE LOOKS UP AND HE SEES ASTROS ARM ON THE ROPES! THE REFEREE KICKS IT OFF....[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: The referee didn't need to kick him, that was uncalled for...[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: WILLARD TAKES ADVANTAGE OF ASTROS DISTRACTION AND HE LOCKS HIM IN FOR A BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX AND HE NAILS IT![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: That is quick thinking by the rookie. He is showing he can hang in the ring with Astros.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: Willard is up and he is holding that left arm. It appears like he is having trouble lifting it.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: For someone who does as many power moves as Willard, this is going to hurt his game.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: THE FREAK IS TELLING WILLARD TO FIND A WAY TO END THIS! [/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: Willard is not happy about his left arm.[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: WILLARD PICKS ASTROS UP OFF THE MAT AND HE IS ATTEMPTING A STALLING VERTICAL SUPLEX![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: While Astros is lifted up by the right arm, you need that left arm to steady your opponent and it is hurt. This is a risky move.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: ASTROS IS TRYING TO BREAK FREE.... HE DOES AND HE FALLS ON HIS FEET BEHIND WILLARD! ASTROS IS GOING FOR THE AZTEC SUPLEX![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: I've never seen this move in person.[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: WILLARD REACHES BACK AND HE RAKES THE EYES OF ASTROS![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: And it looks like you are not going to...[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: WILLARD GRABS THE ARM OF ASTROS AND HE WHIPS HIM HARD INTO THE CORNER![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: It looks like Willard forgot and did that with his injured because he looks like he is in even more pain that before. [/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: Astros turns around in the corner.... WILLARD CHARGES WITH A SHOULDER BLOCK AND ASTROS MOVES OUT OF THE WAY.... WILLARDS LEFT SHOULDER WENT RIGHT INTO THE RING POST![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: Oh man. That has to suck.[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: I think he may have dislocated it.[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: ASTROS SEES AND INJURED WILLARD... AND HE LOCKS ON THE MARTYR'S CROSS.... WILLARD IS SCREAMING IN PAIN BUT HE BEGGING THE REF NOT TO STOP IT![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: Willard should just give up. There is no reason to risk permanent damage to that arm.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: Willard certain is a fighter.[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is taking a closer look... Willard is begging him not to stop it.... THE REF CALLS FOR THE BELL![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match..... DONOVAN ASTROS![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: I don't think Willard gave up.[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: I don't think so either, but the referee made a judgment call to keep Willard from being hurt anymore. Either way, he is going to need that arm looked at when he gets to the back.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we got to take a commercial break, but when we come back, it is time for the NGEN World Tag Title match between the teams of Cameron Cruise & Problem Child and King Krusher & Striker.... STAY TUNED![/FONT]
