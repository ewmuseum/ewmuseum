NGEN Cherry Blossom Chaos: Wells(C) vs Ramey

21 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
John Doe — 21 May 2010 BRANDON JACOBS: This next match in one people have been stirring all night for Jesse Ramey will take on Jared Wells for the BAD World Heavyweight Title.
JONATHAN MARX: Jared Wells has always been a solid wrestler with good control great power, the question is can he utilize it against a faster and more agile Jesse Ramey?
VIC WATERS: Who the hell cares as long as Wells loses the belt. (Cue up: Survive by SICKpuppies as Jesse Ramey walks down the ramp) RING ANNOUNCER: "The following match up is scheduled for one fall and is for the BAD World Heavyweight title, introducing the challenger weighing in at two hundred and five pounds, from Harts, West Virginia! JESSE RAMEY!!!
(Ramey enters the ring testing the ropes. CUE UP: Here to Stay by Korn) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent... (Jared Wells walks on the ramp with his title strapped to his waist walking down the ramp slowly)
RING ANNOUNCER: Weighing in at two hundred and thirty four pounds, hailing from Miami, Flordia! BRANDON JACOBS
(Wells enters the ring and poses on a turnbuckle) RING ANNOUNCER: He is the BAD World Heavyweight Champion! JARED WELLS!! BRANDON JACOBS: Jared Wells staring down Ramey, Ramey pointing at Wells then making a motion around his waist.
(Bell Rings) JONATHAN MARX: last thing you want to do is poke at a champion, he will be sure to make you eat your words later especially one at a caliber Wells is at.
VIC WATERS: Yeah, Wells may be the champion, for now, but if can't hang with Ramey then off with him!
BRANDON JACOBS: Wells circling with Ramey, they decide to tie up, Wells side headlock, transition to a side headlock take down and stands back up with Ramey, showing his technical skills here!
JONATHAN MARX: Like I said you have to be ready Wells is a brute force man, but he does have that speed to make you wish you didn't make a subtle mistake.
VIC WATERS: Wells is a bum, simply put. BRANDON JACOBS: Ramey charging in for a tie up again, Wells with an arm drag! Ramey turning around, right into a second arm drag by Wells!
JONATHAN MARX: Ramey turning around as Wells pumps the crowd with a little taunt, double under-hook belly to belly suplex from Wells!
VIC WATERS: Big moves cost energy, and that will pay off later for Ramey. BRANDON JACOBS: At this moment it is paying off for Wells, Wells with the cover, and two a count from the ref. Wells grabbing Ramey, and a illegal thumb to the eye from Ramey!
JONATHAN MARX: I think at times there needs to be a bigger system than the one in place for such illegal moves, it just seems wrong.
VIC WATERS: There is a system Jonathan, it's called pay attention and be better. Right now Ramey is proving that he is better!
BRANDON JACOBS: Wells leaning against the ropes trying to get better sight of that eye, Ramey off the ropes, slide tackle to the knee of Wells!
JONATHAN MARX: Very smart actually, taking out that knee will cut him down like a tree! VIC WATERS: Yeah, it will also cut this match down so we can move on. BRANDON JACOBS: Ramey with a boot to the knee of Wells, sharp kick, positioning it on the bottom rope, and he is just laying boots to it, Wells looking in pain as Ramey walks away to taunt a bit.
JONATHAN MARX: He should be trying to capitalize on that knee more, try to make Wells useless so he can't defend himself.
VIC WATERS: Wells is already useless! He can't get much more useless! BRANDON JACOBS: Ramey look at Wells who is back on his feet, charging dropkick! Wells in the corner, a second dropkick to the face of Wells, he is grabbing that leg and tying it into the ropes, running opposite of the knee, off the ropes, and drop kick right to the injured knee of wells and he is down!
JONATHAN MARX: very bright set of moves by Jesse Ramey, putting Wells in a position to not defend himself is perfect.
VIC WATERS: What is perfect is Wells is groggy after that dropkick to the face he is leaning over that front rope.
BRANDON JACOBS: OH MY GOD! Jesse Ramey just did a leap frog over Jared Wells and caught his head with his hands for some sort of makeshift street cutter!!! Wow!! Wells has just dumped over the top rope holding his neck!
JONATHAN MARX: People like Jesse Ramey impress me with their not very smart thinking, why risk your body when you can keep this match in that ring?
VIC WATERS: High risk makes high reward Jon! JONATHAN MARX: To hell with that. BRANDON JACOBS: Ramey pointing at the announcing table beside us, he is ripping the equipment off the table!! The Spanish announcement table is being stripped dry!
VIC WATERS: Next send them to Arizona!!!! JONATHAN MARX: Oh that is just ice, Vic. BRANDON JACOBS: Ramey grabbing Wells, double eye gouge to Wells and another kick to the leg that sends Wells back to the ground, referee has began counting.
JONATHAN MARX: Another waste of time, brining him up just to kick him back down this could have been done while he was down, break him in then make him suffer!
VIC WATERS: he wanted to break his hopes more Jonathan, that's why, beat him down more! Treating him like a dog Ramey is jerking that leg as he drags Wells to the table.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wells being pulled back to his feet and he just fell backwards onto the table with no help from Ramey, Ramey shrugging his shoulders as he walks back to the ring and is arguing with the referee about how he doesn't need to do this.
JONATHAN MARX: I think Wells is moving around down there, he just sat up a bit, getting a second wind he sees Ramey...he laid back down, he is playing a hurt possum right now!!
VIC WATERS: Ramey climbing the turnbuckle, turning his back to Wells, he is setting up for a moonsault...Wells is limping standing up on the table still groggy. Lay back down you fool!
BRANDON JACOBS: Ramey jumping off the turnbuckle! WELLS CATCHES HIM IN THE MOON SAULT AND POWERSLAMS HIM THROUGH THE TABLE!!!
JONATHAN MARX: Wow! Good thinking from Wells, but they are both out on the floor next to us.
REFEREE: One! VIC WATERS: Ramey looks like he is in a world of pain. REFEREE: Two! JONATHAN MARX: He may be, that was an attempt for a big impact move, high risk, except it turned in Wells favor.
REFEREE: Three! BRANDON JACOBS: Getting movement from Wells, he is using a near by barricade to try and get up.
REFEREE: Four! VIC WATERS: Ramey you dope why did you go for a Moonsault, never turn your back to your opponent.
REFEREE: Five! BRANDON JACOBS: Wells limping slowly to Ramey and picking him up, dragging him slightly to the ring.
REFEREE: SIX! JONATHAN MARX: Jared Wells using good technique rolling Ramey into the ring, but he is limping really bad I am not sure if he can take more damage to that leg.
VIC WATERS: Good, then we will never have to see his mug here ever again! Referee cutting the count as Wells is having a hard time getting in the ring, ha!
BRANDON JACOBS: Ramey getting up, Wells in the ring, Ramey with an effort to hit the far ropes, rebound, CROSSBODY TO WELLS!!! No! Wells catches him!! He's turning his hands to set up for a Rage Bomb!
JONATHAN MARX: Oh man! Wells leg just gave out from under him with Ramey landing on top of him! Count! One, two, no! Wells gets his hand on the near rope!
VIC WATERS: Wells will retire after this match, he can't roll with the big dogs, look at him, he looks like crap!
BRANDON JACOBS: Jesse Ramey using the ropes to get up, favoring his back as he grabs Jared Wells to force him to his feet, kick to the gut, caught by Wells, spinning kick with a free foot to Wells...Wells ducks! Back the other way! He caught Wells that time!
JONATHAN MARX: That's the thing on these high flying technicians they miss one thing they do have a back up plan, clearly Ramey had one and Wells was not prepared!
VIC WATERS: Of course he wasn't, He is near the middle of the ring, Ramey short run to the ropes, Moonsault off the ropes...HE JUST LANDED WITH BOTH FEET ON THAT INJURED LEG OF JARED WELLS!
BRANDON JACOBS: And Wells just let out a scream of pain, he may be seriously injured! Ramey walking slowly to Wells picking him up, he is calling for the Darkness Buster, He has Wells on his shoulders...HE *HIT IT! HE HIT IT!!!
JONATHAN MARX: Wells looks out cold! VIC WATERS: YEAH BABY!!!!!!!! RAMEY WITH THE COVER!!! BRANDON JACOBS: One......two....THREE!!!! VIC WATERS: Jesse Ramey is the new champ!!! JONATHAN MARX: No he is not! The referee waving his hands saying it is not over! BRANDON JACOBS: Wells got his shoulder up at the very last second! Ramey looking at the referee in complete disbelief! The crowd is cheering for Wells! *Ramey picking wells up again....ANOTHER DARKNESS BUSTER!!!
JONATHAN MARX: Ramey looking at the referee and he just told him it's a three now, make sure of it!
VIC WATERS: Ramey got robbed my friends, straight robbed, how dare Wells kick out! BRANDON JACOBS: Ramey with the cover....one......two......three--- JONATHAN MARX: Shoulder raised again by Jared Wells!! Jesse Ramey is furious, he needs to maintain his composure here!
VIC WATERS: This is not happening, please lord, this is really no happening, take him out already! Ramey grabbing the leg of Wells....LEG LOCK!! Wells screaming in pain and he is no where near the ropes!
BRANDON JACOBS: This is dangerous for Wells, he is dead center in the ring after two Darkness Busters and his leg is severely injured, Ramey locking in tight and cranking back.
JONATHAN MARX: This is the time when Wells needs to learn that enough is enough and to just tap out, there is no sense in further injury to that leg. It can end careers, I have seen it happen.
VIC WATERS: Yeah you heard him tap out! BRANDON JACOBS: Ramey leaning forward and taunting Jared Wells...JARED WELLS JUST KICKED RAMEY RIGHT IN THE MOUTH! AND RAMEY HAS LET GO!
JONATHAN MARX: That shut him up. VIC WATERS: I'm going to shut you up! Dear lord...Ramey is bleeding profusely from his nose, disqualify Jared Wells!
BRANDON JACOBS: Jared Wells, using the ropes to get to his feet, Jesse Ramey cleaning the blood from his nose and is injured walks slowly to Wells, forearm to Wells and a knee to the groin of Jared Wells!
JONATHAN MARX: That man is a cheater! VIC WATERS: That is perfectly legal, sir! BRANDON JACOBS: Ramey pushing Wells into the corner, he is forcing Wells to the top rope! Ramey using the ropes as a bounce...Huricarana off the top rope!! NO! WELLS GRABS RAMEY AND PULLS HIM UP! WELLS HAS RAMEY IN POWER BOMB POSITION! RAMEY TRYING TO PUNCH OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: This is not going to be good if Jesse Ramey can't get out. This is dangerous on both sides of the table.
VIC WATERS: Get out man! BRANDON JACOBS: WELLS LEAPING OFF THE TOP ROPE! RAGE BOMB OFF THE TOP ROPE! RAGE BOMB OFF THE TOP! OH MY GOD!!
VIC WATERS: ...not...cool... JONATHAN MARX: I seriously think I heard a cable snap under the ring there was so much force on that Rage Bomb. Both men laying out cold next to each other.
BRANDON JACOBS: referee counting no man is moving REFEREE: One! REFEREE: Two! REFEREE: Three! REFEREE: Four! BRANDON JACOBS: Both men are still out cold! REFEREE: Five! JONATHAN MARX: Ramey rolling to his back but he looks like he is in a different world. REFEREE: Six! VIC WATERS: Wells looks like he is dead... I think he landed on that bad leg on the way down too!
REFEREE: Seven! JONATHAN MARX: Wells is on his side... Ramey on his back... REFEREE: Eight!! BRANDON JACOBS: We may see a tie here! And if it happens the win will go to Wells! REFEREE: Nine! BRANDON JACOBS: NO! WELLS JUST TURNED TO HIS BACK HIS ARM FLOPPING ON RAMEY'S CHEST!! COUNT!!!! ONE! TWO!! THREE!!!!
(Bell Rings) JONATHAN MARX: Jared Wells has retained the title! What a display by both men! VIC WATERS: I believed in Wells the entire time! I mean I knew he could do it! RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, and STILL The BAD World Heavyweight Champion.....JARED WELLS!!!!!
BRANDON JACOBS: Medics on there way to the ring to help Wells get to his feet Ramey still not moving. What a match!
JONATHAN MARX: Again, a great display of athletics in that ring by these two tonight! VIC WATERS: Well that's what happens when you get two decent guys in the ring. BRANDON JACOBS: More of Cherry Blossom Chaos after this! (Fade on Wells limping a celebration)
