PaulNJ21 — 20 January 2010 RPed by Chris Horowitz WRESTLER INFORMATION RING NAME: Jeff "Greenhorn" Jorgenson AGE: 21 HEIGHT: 5'8 WEIGHT: 170 ALIGNMENT: Babyface APPEARANCE: [Be descriptive as possible.] White, dark scraggly hair, with the build of someone just out of wrestling school. Pale, unshaven but not to the point of having a beard. Bryan Alvarez if he weren't half Mexican.
PERSONALITY: [Once again, be descriptive.] A very excited recent graduate of wrestling school. Mostly friendly and loving life, with a tendency to get caught up on minor details and snap.
BACKGROUND / HISTORY: A recent graduate of the Hacker School of Wrestling, Jonny has been working on the independent scene for just over a year, living out of his van and his grand mother's basement. He's been itching for his chance to make it big, but didn't think it would come so soon! He's just excited to be on the big show.
HOMETOWN: Merrow, Connecticut ENTRANCE MUSIC: Any Way You Want It By Journey RING ENTRANCE: [How your wrestler enters the ring. Be creative.] High energy, Jonny darts to ringside slapping hands with all the fans, shouting COME ON BABY and other inspiring words, and vaults himself over the ropes from the apron.
TITLE HISTORY: FNWS Junior Heavyweight Champion WRESTLING STYLE: [i.e. Technical, Aerial, Brawler, combination.] Aerial with basic mat competency
MOVES: [Required to submit at minimal, 10.] Arm drag, drop kick, head scissors, springboard clothesline, snap suplex, headlock takeover, grounded head scissors, surfboard, standing moonsault, top rope moonsault, tope, victory roll. Basic wrestling moves and basic high flying stuff.
