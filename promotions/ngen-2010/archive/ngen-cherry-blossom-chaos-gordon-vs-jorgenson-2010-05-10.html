NGEN Cherry Blossom Chaos: Gordon vs. Jorgenson

10 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 10 May 2010 [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it is time for our first match in the new New Generation Era, Pat Gordon Jr will take on Jeff Jorgenson![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: Jorgenson always comes close, but then just hasn't been able to close the deal and Pat Gordon Junior is going to be under a lot of pressure tonight. It isn't easy walking in the foot steps of a wrestling legend like Pat Gordon.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: Pat Gordon is a fortunate son, he has all of his Dad's wrestling genetics and his mother's good looks to make up for his Dad's, Pat Gordon is going to be a superstar and it is going to start right here tonight.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, lets not wait any longer, lets send this one down to ringside![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif](CUEUP: “A[/FONT]ny Way You Want It” By Journey) [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Merrow, Connecticut, standing a five foot eight, one hundred and seventy pounds, JEFF “GREENHORN” JORGENSON![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]::Jeff Jorgenson comes down to the crowd slapping hands with the fans and enters the ring::[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent...[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif](CUEUP: "The Rocky Road to Dublin" by Dropkick Murphys)[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]RING ANNOUNCER: Making his PRO WRESTLING DEBUT here tonight... hailing from Boston, Massachutes, standing a five foot eleven, two hundred and thirty two pounds, “THE MAN OF THE 00s” PAT GORDON JUNIOR![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]:at Gordon Junior comes down in one of his father's old robes from the early days of his career, the fans start chanting Pat as he makes his way down to ringside and enters the ring::[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: JEFF JORGENSON RUSHES PAT GORDON AND PULLS HIS OWN ROBE OVER HIS HEAD![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]*bell rings *[/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: Jorgenson isn't wasting any time![/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: A BLINDED PAT GORDON JUNIOR IS SWINGING WILDLY AT JORGENSON! GORDON GETS ONE GOOD SHOT IN BUT JORGENSON AVOIDS THE REST...[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: Only one may have connected, but when your last name is Gordon, you throw one hell of a punch.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: JORGENSON THE COVERED HEAD OF JORGENSON AND HE HITS HIM WITH A KNEELIFT, GORDON REELS FALLS BACK AGAINST THE ROPES AND BOUNCES OFF UNCOVERING THE ROBE FROM HIS HEAD...[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: JORGENSON HAS HIM RIGHT WHERE HE WANTS HIM![/FONT] [FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: JORGENSON NAILS PAT GORDON JUNIOR WITH THE SUPERKICK! JORGEON HOOKS THE LEG WITH ALL HIS MIGHT! THE REF GOES DOWN TO COUNT! 1------------------------------------------------2---------------------------------3! PAT GORDON JUNIOR BREAKS THE LEGHOOK BUT IT IS TOO LATE![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match.... JEFF THE GREENHORN JORGENSON! [/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: Pat Gordon got ambushed as he was getting into the ring and then got his robe pulled over his head. HE WAS BLINDED! That was totally unfair. He is the son of the legend. He doesn't deserve to be treated that way. [/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]JONATHAN MARX: Jorgenson is hungry for climb up that ladder so he can win some gold. He saw his opportunity to get his first win and he took it and now the name Jeff Jorgenson will go down as the first man ever to win a match in this new chapter of NGEN.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]VIC WATERS: It should be the son of the Hall of Famer. That is BS.[/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we are going to take a commercial break, when we come back, Donovan Astros will take on Adrian Willard! STAY TUNED![/FONT]
[FONT=Arial, sans-serif]::a commercial for “Brightest Day” airs, King Krusher and Striker are shown::[/FONT]
