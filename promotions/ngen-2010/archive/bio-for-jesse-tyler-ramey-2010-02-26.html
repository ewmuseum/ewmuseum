Bio for Jesse Tyler Ramey

PaulNJ21 — 26 February 2010 Basic Information REAL NAME: Jesse Tyler Ramey BORN: January 6th, 1976 HOMETOWN: Harts, West Virginia HEIGHT: 6' WEIGHT: 205lbs Ring Information STYLE: Extreme, Technical, High-Flyer THEME MUSIC: 'Survive' by SICKpuppies ALIGNMENT: Anarchist FINISHER: The Attitude Adjustment - Darkness Buster Past Information PROMOTIONS: UWL, Extreme World Fighting (EWF), CWA, Just Electrifying Wrestling (JEW), Cyberspace Championship Wrestling (CCW), Old School Wrestling (OSW), Five Star Wrestling (FSW), PURE, Primetime Wrestling Circuit (PWC), the Asylum (tA), thReat, All-Star Championship Wrestling (ACW), the Squared Circle (tSC)
TITLES: UWL Hall of Fame Inductee, UWL Tag Team Champion (w/ Blackheart), UWL Television Champion (First to win and last to hold) (x 2 and Longest Championship Reign), First EWF Hardcore Champion, CWA Tag Team Champion (w/ Klown), CWA Hardcore Champion (x 2), CWA World Heavyweight Champion, JEW Cruiserweight Champion, JEW USA Champion, CCW Independent Champion, CCW Lightweight Champion, FSW Tag Team Champion (w/ Asa Fountain), First and Only PURE Heavyweight Champion
TRAINER: (Brawling, Psychology, Stealth, and Stamina Training) July, 1986-December, 1990: Juvenile Military Boot Camp, Drill Instructor Major Charles Alexander III; (Basic Professional Wrestling Training) January, 1991- March, 1992: Killer Kowalski's Pro Wrestling School; (Martial Arts Training) April, 1992- March 1993: Tiger Mask in Japan while Jesse worked for AJPW; (Submission and Technical Training) April, 1993- July, 1994: Stu Hart in the Dungeon, with the help of Bret and Owen on Technical training occasionally.
TEAM: Bad Frotitude (FSW; w/ Asa 'Fro' Fountain), Now (LoC; w/ Kyo) GROUP: The White Bloods (JEW) MANAGER: Kid Lightning (UWL), Chass Loraine (OSW), Michelle-Marie Ramey (CCW, OSW), Donny Layne (LoC)
