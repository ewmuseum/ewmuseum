jamesfnx — 22 February 2010 NAME: Donovan Astros FROM: Los Angeles, California STATS: 6'3", 237 lbs. THEME: 2Pac - "All Eyes On Me (Bittersweet Remix)" Donovan Astros has been called a lot of things over his twelve-year career in professional wrestling. From arrogant and misanthropic to dedicated, determined, and driven, opinions on Astros run the gamut between well-loved and truly despised. The truth, as always, lies somewhere in the middle. Astros, a three-time World champion, calls himself "the greatest wrestler on God's green Earth", but has yet to garner recognition on an international stage. Other than a brief tour in A1E, Astros has called New Alberta Pro Wrestling his home for the past three years, even after a lengthy suspension caused by an attack on an unsuspecting company employee. While in NAPW, Astros has been dominant, with two World title reigns and five championships overall in about a year and a half of ring-time.
Partially unsatisfied with the lack of glory, partially to challenge himself, and partially to get the name out of what he calls the best promotion no one knows about, Donovan Astros is making the step up to NGEN to take on the best and show them why it's all eyes on him.
Astros wrestles in a base black singlet with his name in Gothic font and his logo (a Walk of Fame star with wrestlers in the middle) in gray with thorn vines growing around them, much like an Affliction design.
