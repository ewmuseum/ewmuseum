Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NGEN, New Generation Enterprises, was announced on the fwrestling.com hub on 20 January 2010 by the founder of World's Finest Wrestling, who had run that league into its co-branded WFW:NE years with New ERA. His announcements of that day named a vice president, set the first pay-per-view, Cherry Blossom Chaos, for Washington, D.C., and listed the fate of the WFW champions: the WFW World Heavyweight, World Tag and North American Titles were to be sealed and decided anew as NGEN titles, while the BAD World Heavyweight Title was to be kept under its name. His welcome post of 23 January gave the league's rules and named the leagues whose history its characters could use: IWF, GLCW, BAD, LVW, NEW, WFW and WFW:NE.

The league opened on 21 February 2010 with the roleplay folders for Cherry Blossom Chaos, where WFW World Heavyweight Champion Felix Red was to meet Shawn Hart for the first NGEN World Heavyweight Title, and a roster drawn from WFW, New ERA and newcomers posted its profiles. The founder began the broadcast on 10 May 2010, with Brandon Jacobs, Jonathan Marx and Vic Waters at the table; its posted parts tell Pat Gordon Jr against Jeff Jorgenson, Donovan Astros against Adrian Willard and the BAD World Heavyweight Title match between Jared Wells and Jesse Ramey.

On 11 January 2011 he announced that he was officially retired from running fantasy leagues, and the next day he posted the results of the four matches he had not written: King Krusher and Striker won the NGEN World Tag Titles, Minion beat Golem, HAL won the North American elimination match and Shawn Hart beat Felix Red to become the first NGEN World Heavyweight Champion. What this record holds is the 35 threads started in 2010 that are served here, and the 2010 sign-up replies on older WFW bio threads, which are served with those threads on the WFW wing.
