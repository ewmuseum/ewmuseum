NGEN — Roleplay

PC AND CAM'RON CRUISE- TAG CHAMPS LIVING LARGE!

13 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LQJT86C — 13 April 2010 (FADEIN: Slo-motion, PROBLEM CHILD embracing CAMERON CRUISE in the ring, both holding the NGEN Tag Team Belts)
(CUEUP: "Push It To The Limit" from Scarface Soundtrack) (CUTTO: Money counting machine, shifting through hundred dollar bills faster than a deck of cards)
(CUTTO: PC and CRUISE stepping out of a blue Ford van, wearing suits, sunglasses, and the tag straps over their shoulders. Several entourage follow them out of the van, holding big bags of money. As they walk past a bank window, one of the entourage knocks on it, startling the branch manager who pulls the phone away from his ear to check out the goings-on. The men walk out of the frame. All of a sudden, they appear in front of his desk, and the branch manager rises to greet PC with a handshake)
"Push it to the limit Walk along the razor's edge but don't look down, just keep your head and you'll be finished" (CUTTO: PC and CRUISE walking away from a white building that reads "PC/CRUISE MANAGEMENT INC." Cruise is in a blue suit, PC's in a white suit with a red button-up underneath. They walk directly into the camera, which segues into the next shot- both men walking away from behind into a different building, where they are greeted)
"Open up the limit past the point of no return You've reached the top but still you gotta learn how to keep it" (CUTTO: PC and CRUISE posing for photos with their beautiful new wives at a co-wedding) "Hit the wheel and double the stakes throttle wide open like a bat out of hell and you crash the gates (crash the gates)" (CUTTO: Money counting machine, still flipping through all those Benjamins) (CUTTO: PC and CRUISE, plus entourage, walking back to the bank with more bags of money. Standing outside is the branch manager, who can't believe his eyes! He smiles, shakes his head in disbelief)
"Going for the back of beyond Nothing gonna stop you there's nothing that strong So close now you're nearly at the brink so, push it" (CUTTO: Overhead view of the PC/CRUISE Estate, close up on the swimming pool area. CRUISE is on a floatey with his wife, next to PC's wife alone on a floatey. PC jumps into the shot with a cannonball into the pool! He splashes everyone! WATER FIGHT!)
"Welcome to the limit (The limiiiiiit!) Take it maybe one step more The power game's still playing so you better win it " (CUTTO: Flashback to the wedding party. PC and CRUISE lead their wives, Groomsmen, and Bridesmaids down their property to an area where a giant tiger plays! Yeah, they bought him from the zoo)
(CUTTO: Estate worker climbing off a ladder, pulling down a sheet to reveal a giant painted portrait of PC and CRUISE holding the tag titles)
"Push it to the limit (The limiiiiiiit!) With no one left to stand in your way you might get careless, but you'll never be safe while you still feel it" (CUTTO: PC and CRUISE smoking cigars, watching their wives try on expensive new dresses) (CUTTO: PC and CRUISE in front of a GIANT mound of cocaine sitting atop their desk, which they share. Cruise is snorting it neatly, line by line, but PC just BURIES his face in the white mound)
"Welcome to the limit (The limit) Standing on the razor's edge don't look down just keep your head and you'll be finished" (CUTTO: Armed guards wearing dark sunglasses, holding AK-47s, stand at a gate which opens for a limousine. As it drives into the PC/CRUISE estate, we see the license plate which reads: "THEWRLDISOURS")
