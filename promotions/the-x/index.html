A federation known only as The X, named in one line of a wrestler's own résumé inside another promotion's database and nowhere else in anything that survives. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The X is named, exactly that way, in El Terrible’s own XUW bio card — the “Other Federations” field, alongside the UWF, GW, LOW, ACW and NFW Season II. That line is the whole of it. Nothing else in XUW’s surviving record mentions the name again: a search for it across all 1,312 held pages of that federation’s own site and forums returns exactly two hits, the raw and text copies of this one card.

A subdomain of the right name, thex.wrestleonline.org, sits on the hosting constellation that carried PCW, PTC, GW and their sister promotions — and it is a plausible home rather than a proven one, because the name is the only thing tying it to this federation. What the archive kept of it is 66 captures between March 2005 and May 2008, every one of them a robots.txt. Not one page of whatever stood there was ever archived.
