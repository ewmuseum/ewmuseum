Roleplay/Angle/Strat/Interview Guiding -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Roleplaying Now we all know what an RP is hopefully. You must RP to WIN. You don't RP you don't win. Unless you have an angle. All RPs are Sent Directly to [detail withheld] and [detail withheld] Angle- Now, an angle is pretty much what makes the fed, along with Role-playing. If you have a good angle, with someone. This makes everything much more fun. An Angle can tell a story, that can last for a month or two, or even maybe three cards. Its up to. I will add a few angles of my own if I come up with anything. But if you guys feel you have a good angle. Then feel free to email them to me, and I will try my best to use them. Strategy- Now a strat is always useful in matches. A Strat can be a certain way you want you character to act for the night. If he wins or Losses. Any special moves he will do. Below is a simple form to help you along with a Strat. Name: (Wrestler the Stradegy will go for) Date of Match: How Will your Wrestler React?: Any Special Moves?: What Will he do if he WINS/LOSSES?: Anything Else?: Sneak Attacks- Sneak attacks are always fun. See someone you don't like. Want to interfere and maybe cost them the match? Sure why not. These always start up nice angles, or even little feuds. Interviews/In-Rings- If you would like to appear on Jens Show, or Have Alison interview you. These always help start up feuds, and gives the wrestler a chance to answer questions, everyone has been wanting to know. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 10.)
