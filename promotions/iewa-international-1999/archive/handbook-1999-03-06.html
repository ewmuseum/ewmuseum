Lets talk about the fed. About The Fed This fed is called IEWA, which stands for International Extreme Wrestling Association. Here at IEWA, we will be a closed fed. Which means all rps/strats, angls/interviews will be sent directly to myself, and DadooFurby. The VP. I will then put them onto an RP packet which you will receive about Three times a week. In this federation I will have no complaining of a loss. Come now people.
Its only eWrestling. If you lost you lost. Now in case I screw up BIG time. You REALLY feel I screwed you out of the win. Then by all means give me an Email, and I will look into. I will admit to my mistakes but no need for you to piss and moan. Other than that. I intend on running this fed strictly on
Quality/Angles/Strats. So as long as you can do those three things. Your in good condition. Most of all IM here to have fun, as I hope thats the reason you will decide to join as well.

Roleplaying/Strat/Angle Guide/Interviews/Sneak Attacks Role-playing Now we all know what an RP is hopefully. If you don't please X out of this Mail. In the IEWA, you MUST RP to win. However just because you RP doesn't mean you'll always win. I go by Quality of the RP. You will be allowed 5 Rps per Match. The Rps will be Sent to [detail withheld]
and [detail withheld] . The Sunday cards will be sent to [detail withheld] . Now Quantity is of some importance, because if the RP is only 10 lines. Then most likely there's maybe a second of quality in it. So I do require you to have at least 35 lines of
RP, for I to count toward your match. Otherwise your RP will be disregarded. Strategy A Strat is short for Strategy. Strategies always help you win your match. This would include like how you want your wrestler to react. What he'll do if he wins/Losses. What special moves you'd like him to do. Things like that I also
Judge Matches on Rps, Strats, Angles, In-rings, Interviews. If you have all 5. Then your chances of winning are greater. Strats should also be sent to [detail withheld] and [detail withheld] . Strats always help, remember that! Angle Guide Now, an angle is pretty much what makes the fed, along with Role-playing. If you have a good angle, with someone. This makes everything much more fun. An
Angle can tell a story, that can last for a month or two, or even maybe three cards. Its up to. I will add a few angles of my own if I come up with anything.
But if you guys feel you have a good angle. Then feel free to email em to me, and I will try my best to use them.
Ineterviews/IN-rings\ Interviews, can take place at any show. You as the Rper, have right if you like. May use Alison Winchester or Jennifer Susa. Alison Winchester you may use as your interviewer/in-ring interviewer. Just don't rape her. Jennifer may be used on her show "Jens Show" she too can appear on any card. This can also be used as a push into winning your match. Interviews and IN-rings also make up a better card. Starts more heat. An in-ring is what goes right before your match, and interview can be placed anywhere. So Interviews and IN-ring are also good as well.
Sneak Attacks- Sneak attacks are always fun. See someone you don't like. Want to interfere and maybe cost them the match? Sure why not. These always start up nice angles, or even little feuds.
