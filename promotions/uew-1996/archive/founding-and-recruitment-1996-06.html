UEW's Introduction to RSPWF

6 & 17 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Two of the fed's own posts, its first recruitment ad on the newsgroup and its own self-introduction eleven days later, are combined here as the fed's earliest attested appearance in this corpus.] --- 6 June 1996 --- Hey everyone! The UEW is now open for some new wrestlers. The UEW is a strict roleplay league. We have two cards a week, one long, one short. Everyone seems extremely happy with it. The league has been running for guess how long??? 6 or 7 months!!! Wow. We have openend things up because about 60% of the league is on vacation or gone for the summer. That means activity is down because only a sew are still here for the summer. If you are a good roleplayer and don't mind being active sign up! Write me at [detail withheld] or [detail withheld] Later, Greg --- 17 June 1996 --- Hey everyone! i am the prez of UEW. We are just now posting on RSPWF. We are a fun league that has been around, get this, SEVEN MONTHS! We are still going strong. since i am using AOL, all the cards appear on RSPWF about, oh, two days late. If there are recognized feds, i believe we should be one. We are booked been around since DECEMBER, two cards a week, full match write ups and a lot of fun. We are affiliated with the yet to start up league WHICH WILL BE GREAT, Maximum Wrestling Circuit. Join ASAP. We don't realy have room in the UEW except for a tam team, but if you want to get involved with the UEW through super cards and want to join a SUPERB league e-mail [detail withheld] He runs MWC. His league is also affiliated with Dawg Pound Wrestling(DPW). Everyone knows the quality product they put out. Soon the UEW might be linked with DPW. Later, ska
