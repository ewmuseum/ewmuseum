Rebirth of the UEW

15 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome everybody to the rebirth of what was considered at one time by many, one of the best feds on the Internet, Ultimate E-Wrestling. After a bit of a rough period trying to find someone to take the reins of UEW permanently, somebody has been found who can help UEW return to its Golden Age of sorts. At least I *hope* I can do this :) Due to the recent lengthy instability of the fed, we've lost many handlers and characters. But now that we've restored some order to the fed and have an all-new executive board in place who doesn't plan on going anywhere, we are ready to write a new chapter to the UEW legacy with all new wrestlers, angles and the like, to add to the already great base we have. Why did your fedhead send this to you? Simple, we need you. We need as many handlers and characters as we can get. We are going to make UEW *THE* premier e-wrestling fed on the 'net. For that to happen, we need a big, solid base of talented handlers and their characters. You can get in on the ground floor of something that promises to be the very best out there. I know these are lofty expectations of someone who has had the job, what, two days now? But you can believe me when I say that myself and the board behind me are going to make UEW one of the best damn feds of all time...or die trying. (Or at least kill our respective G.P.A.'s) The UEW already has a good core of talented roleplayers and EXCEPTIONAL writers, who range from ECW hardcore style, to technical, to high-flying lucha matches, to 80's WWF style. We even have our own designated "wild brawl" writer whom you have to read to believe. The problem is, there is always room for improvement (although with some of my writers, I'm not so sure). In order to be the best, you have to have the best. That's why this is an invitation to join UEW. HOW WE WORK ----------- UEW will continue to be a strictly roleplay based federation. Simply put, if you roleplay better than your opponent, you win. I am talking quality, not quantity. "Spammers" will be taken outside and beaten my one of my VP's. Just kidding. But please don't confuse length of flash with quality of flash. Quality is what decisions will be based on. If you're good, you're gonna win. Match outcomes will be decided by me. In every other fed I have run, I have decided matches impartially and fairly with very few complaints. However, if you do have a complaint or a question, please take it straight to me. I'm doing this for you and I want to make sure everybody gets as much enjoyment as possible from the product we put out. WEEKLY, PPV, AND SUPERCARDS --------------------------- There will be two wrestling shows per week and one magazine-type show. On Mondays, there will be the No-Action Zone where we will discuss the events of the previous week, along with breaking news, upcoming cards, injuries, rumors, rankings, and out of character comments on how the federation is running and suggestions of how and where we can improve. On Wednesdays, there will be UEW Anarchy. The format will be 6-7 dark matches with summaries, and one commentated main event match. The dark matches will not be big time matches per se, they will get over who needs to get over and will also further angles if necessary. They will debut people as well as promo people for later in the week. The main event will feature a special match with surprises and will also progress storylines for the upcoming PPV. On Sundays, SUNDAY NIGHT HEAT (announcer: "Turn up the Heat on Sunday nights!!", sorry, cheesy but I like it :) The format will be 5 to 6 marquee matches with commentary, usually 2 prelim matches, 2-3 midcard matches, and one "match of the week" main event, which will lead into the recap of week's events in the NAZ. As far as PPV and Supercards go, the schedule and format for them will be determined once we have our talent rosters set. Most likely, we will have PPV/Supercards in cycles of 3. The first of the cycle will be a not-quite-PPV supercard, comparable to In Your House but with a much better lineup of matches. The second of the cycle will be a specialty PPV of some kind, either a Lethal Lottery, or a crossover with another fed, or a Rumble/Survivor Series, tag team tournament, etc. The third of the cycle will be "the big one" where 95% of the feuds would be ended, new main event feuds would be started, etc. UEW TITLES ---------- The top singles title will remain the Ultimate championship. Former Ultimate champions include the Mercenary, Octane, and "Offensive" Alex Adams. The current reigning champion is the man who dethroned ODoubleA, "Top Dog" Rick Styles. The second highest singles title will remain the American title. Former champs include "Offensive" Alex Adams, Professor Nickedemus Payne, and Magus. The current champion is the "New Dehli Mad Man" Kappu, who defeated Professor Payne in a grueling cage match at the last Holiday Horror. The third singles title is the Television title. Former champs include Robski, Danny Ice and Dark Destroyer. Kyle Kamikaze holds the title as we speak after defeating both Danny Ice _and_ Dark D in a NYC street fight, but he faces the challenge of his life as he faces Bobby Whitestar in a ladder match with the title on the line next week. The top tag gold is the Ultimate tag team titles. Teams like the Lethal Leaders and Guts & Glory have held those titles on more than one occasion. Current champs are the Southern Posse who are on their second stint as champions. The next step in tag titles are the Television tag team titles. Former champs include the Leaders and G & G who both used the TV belts as a jumping off point for the Ultimate titles. The team of Havoc currently hold the TV tag titles and are striving for the same feat as the Leaders and G & G before them, to hold both sets of UEW tag titles. They face the Posse next week in what could be their last chance for Ultimate tag gold. A UEW Light-Heavyweight title may be formed depending on interest from handlers and amount of wrestlers who fall into the light heavyweight category. SO, YOU WANT IN? ---------------- If you are interested in joining the next generation of UEW stars, there is an application form at the bottom. The last pay per view of the last regime is scheduled for March 15. The Ides of March promises to be a tremendous card with great writing, great action, and many surprises thrown in here and there. On March 16, the next chapter of the UEW story begins. New president, new additions to the writing staff, new faces on the talent roster, and the same dedication to having the best fed in the game. Give us a try, I can assure you that you will not be disappointed. If you are disappointed for some reason or another, we'd be happy to hand deliver to you a large sum of money from the personal account of our former president Greg (just kidding). Regardless, I don't think you will be disappointed. If you would like, please drop me an e-mail if you want me to send you our upcoming Ides of March PPV so you can see our some of our best writers and wrestlers in action before the new era begins. The application and any comments, questions, complaints, or suggestions you may have can be send to me, Jay, at [detail withheld] Thank you for your time.
