ATTENTION ALL UEW MEMBERS!

13 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The following a Patrick Brodcasting Systems message: Hey everyone, OK, well as we all know, our illustrious fedhead was badly injured in a snowboarding accident about three weeks ago which has essentially put UEW on temporary hiatus while he recovers. I just received an e-mail from Tyran's roommate who told me that Tyran is still offline as his recovery process isn't going as well as everyone had hoped it would. His roommate reports that right now, Tyran really can't do much more than sleep, lay in his room, puke and "...annoy the hell out of us with his requests..." (those are Jared, Tyran's roommates words). It also seems that when Tyran went to see his doctor last week, they noticed that the gash on his side hadn't healed that much and that it was possibly infected or something. So he was sent down to Edmonton to get testing done and has been there for nearly a week now. Before we talk about any of this silly e-wrasslin' stuff, I want to first say that I'm really saddened to hear Tyran's still in a lot of pain and I hope everyone will join me in wishing him a speedy recovery. I know a lot of us (myself included) have been impatient and have been wondering when the game will resume. But the far more important consideration is Tyran's health so if anyone believes in that positive vibe stuff, send a few healthful thoughts in Tyran's direction, OK? Now... as for UEW... Tyran *IS* going to come back as soon as he can and resume his fedheading duties. In the meantime, he has sent me all the materials for the missing SUNDAY NIGHT HEAT and I will be working on putting it together ASAP. There was also an IFF that didn't go out either (plus some other cards) so we're going to try and clear up the backlog as quickly as we can and try to get things back on track so that Tyran can get a well-running fed handed back to him once he's recovered. Stay tuned for further information about UEW as soon as we can get it. One last comment about Tyran's state of mind right now... his roommate reports "...He seemed really upset over the fact that he couldn't get this wrestling stuff finished and told me to apologize "a million times"." Gotta love a guy who's in a lot of pain but *STILL* worries about a dumb game like e-w, eh? Anyhoo... stay tuned for more info as soon as we can get it out... and send positive vibes toward Tyran. Cheers! Patrick This has been a Pat Broadcasting Systems message. :) Jeff
