Ultimate Saturday / Ultimate Sunday (excerpt)

17 June 1996

[Excerpt. This card ran to the newsgroup in four parts; only the cold open, the debut handicap match and the following news segment (from Part 4 of the posting) are reproduced here. The other three parts, carrying the remainder of the card, remain held and are not served, per the Museum's no-bulk-mirroring practice.] <The mighty UEW symbol flashes on and then fades to black. "The Devil's Night Out" by The Bosstones starts to play and The UEW belt is being raised high, held by it's present owner, Octane. "Spartan" Troy Walters is holding his American Belt after a successful defense. Film of Guts & Glory winning their belt shades in. High impact footage of UEW wrestlers performing their finishers like The Black Knight choke slamming a man out of the ring, JT Crash flying off the top rope splashing a wrestler off of Jason Stone's shoulder. Heatstroke putting his arms into the air then flies off the top rope in his "Heatstrike". The screen fades out with The Executioner pinning a loser, then getting up and flexing his muscles> <A tall but skinny man wearing a black suit and headphones sits behind a desk, then a shorter but broad man in a blue suit and a "UEW" pin on his tie sits down behind the desk with head sets> Rock Smith: WEEEELLLLLLLCOOOOOOMMMMMMEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Tommy Thompson: This is the UEW!!!!!!!!! Crowd: UEW UEW UEW UEW UEW UEW!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Rock Smith: We have a greata card tonight. Let's take a look and get onto the matches! -_-_-_-_-ULTIMATE SUNDAY-_-_-_-_- ****DEBUT HANDICAP**** Bobby Whitestar(DEBUT) VS Ska-Core ****DEBUT MATCH**** "Hollywood" Johnny Dreamer(DEBUT) VS Colt Robski(DEBUT) VS Menace ****SINGLES**** The Canadien Kid VS Lucky Williams ****THE DEN**** W/Michael faulkner ****news & info**** w/Shane Fitzgerald Rock Smith: Right now in the ring is the team of SKA-CORE. The are skanking around in their suits under the music of "Rack Em UP" By The Imperials. Tommy Thompson: The Skatelite and The Ska-Aholic want to get their act together tonight and demolish one man off! <Zero by Smashing Pumpkins blasts, a big man wearing a black leather jacket, black jeans with Native American writing on them in red ink, black boots...mostly all black> RS: Here is the UEW introduction of BOBBY WHITESTAR! Bobby, judging by his heat filled interviews with OAA, is pissed at any member of "The White Man". Bobby sure as hell looks serious. TT: Bobby Whitestar locks up with The Skatelite and over powers him pushing him to corner where The Ska-Aholic is located. Bobby Whitestar socks The Ska-Aholic and he falls off the ring and The Skatelite hits him with a clothesline! Whitestar is down, The Skatelite tries on dropping an elbow but Whitestar moves out of the way. Whitestar stands and kicks The Skatelite while he is down, White Star sees The Ska-Aholic getting up to the canvas and knocks him straight off! RS: Bobby Whitestar is a no nonsense man! TT: Whitestar slams The Skatelite's face into the ring. The Ska-Aholic is pissed and he hits Bobby Whitestar from behind with a drop kick. The Ska-Aholic runs off the ropes with a flying body press but Bobby Whitestar power slams! The Skatelite is up but Bobby Whitestar crushes him with a beautiful neckbreaker! RS: What is that noise? <Rumblings over the PA, obviously someone turning it on who is not to familiar with the high tech UEW PA system.> RS: HAHAHAHAHA!!!!!!! PA: Hi-How-Are-Ya Hi-How-Are-Ya Hi-How-Are-Ya Hi-How-Are-Ya RS: Bobby Whitestar is pissed! CROWD: YEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! TT: Bobby Whitestar lifts The Skatelite up to his shoulder and power bombs him onto his neck! RS: THE TRAIL OF TEARS!!!!!!!!! CROWD: YEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!! RS: Why is the crowd cheering for this racist? TT: Look at the aisle! <A man with head feathers, face paint, a phat peace pipe, native American clothing> RS: OFFENSIVE ALEX ADAMS!!!!!!!! TT: Why is he decked out like a native American? RS: MIND GAMES MY FRIEND!!!!!!!! TT: Whitestar looks at Alex Adams and screams things at OAA. RS: Look! OAA is doing a rain dance!!!!!!!!! <Alex Adams starts to dance around the outside of the ring> CROWD: YEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAAHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! TT: Bobby Whitestar looks at The Skatelite and pins him. RS: The Skatelite is still out from The Trail Of Tears. 1 . . . . 2 . . . . 3!! RS: Bobby Whitestar has seen enough!!!!!! Whitestar charges OAA! TT: Look Flex was hiding under the ring! Flex is pounding Whitestar! RS: OAA is calling Flex off... thank god.... TT: OAA is putting on "The Attitude Adjuster"! RS: Whitestar is out. OAA has some face paint and he is spreading it on the face of Bobby Whitestar! Steve Salazar is out from the curtain and his men follow. I have never seen all this before! RS: Let's get the latest breaking news in The UEW CONTROL CENTER!!!!!!!!!!!! Shane? ________________ UEW CONTROL CENTER!!!!! Shane Fitzgerald: Welcome lads!!!!!!!!!! We have some HUGE news lets get right to it! THIS IS News and RUMORS UEW GAINS AN AFFILIATE *Prez Greg has ironed out a deal with the MAXIMUM WRESTLING CIRCUIT(MWC). Inter-fed cards, and a TEXAS WILDMAN BELT will be defended between federations. Updates will be shown between cards. MWC has already signed superstars like Craig Jayhawk, Tommy "Shot Gun" Storm, Black Venom and others. CHAD SHADE FORFEITS TV BELT, TOURNAMENT TO BE HELD *TV Title holder CHAD LIGHTNING SHADE has FORFEITED his TV belt to get a shot for the AMERICAN belt against TROY WALTERS!!!!!!! There will be a TV BELT TOURNAMENT to name the next champion. Chad Shade will hang on the belt for a week and then officialy give it up. The last time that has happened is when Troy Walters gave up his belt to earn a shot against Eric Smart for the whole shibang! "Spartan" troy Walters of course won that belt. 3 HIATUS WRESTLERS RETURN *UEW champ Octane, his partner in crime Ugly Kid Joe are back in action. Also is the UEW monster THE BLACK KNIGHT who has been out since his time limit bout with Chad Shade is reaking Havok again. The match will be remembered for a long time. Expect a rematch, both men hate each other A LOT. LEGION OF LIGHT ON IT'S WAY OUT? *IT looks like The Legion Of Light might be ending. Rumor has it that THE JUGGERNAUT might have ALREADY handed in his notice and he is out of UEW action. Professor Nickedemus Payne might have left the Legion Of Light but is FOR SURE still a highly ranked and feared opponent. That leaves Shade as the last member. But of course i could be wrong SUMMER BELT, DROPPED! *The UEW has mentioned the arrival of the UEW summer belt. The summer belt has been eliminated BUT The UEW will replace it with The Texas Wildman Belt, which will be battled inter-fed with MWC. COLIN DRAKE DRIVES COLT TO RETIREMENT *newcomer Colin Drake faced off on Thusday in his debut match against Colt. Drake worked on the knee of Colt and eventualy polished him off. Later on in the show, Colin Drake clipped the knee of Colt and executed a figure four leglock. Colt was taken to the best hospital the UEW could afford "Uncle Zeke's hospital and bait shop" where Colt was pronounced dead. COLT told the janitor who was amazingly also the doctor that he was alive and well, it is just a knee problem and the heart is located on the left side of the chest not the right. The doctor appologized for the mistake but Colt will probably never wrestle again, or atleast not anytime soon.
