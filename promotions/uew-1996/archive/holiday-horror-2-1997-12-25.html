Holiday Horror 2 (excerpt)

25 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Excerpt. This pay-per-view ran to the newsgroup in fifteen parts; the final part — the concluding stretch of the UEW Ultimate Championship match between champion "Top Dog" Rick Styles and challenger "No Worries" Rob Magnum, and its immediate aftermath — is reproduced here complete. The card's other fourteen parts, including a UEW American Championship Triple Threat defense and a multi-team scaffold match deciding both UEW tag team titles, remain held and are not served, per the Museum's no-bulk-mirroring practice.] the UEW Ultimate championship match between the champion "Top Dog" Rick Styles and the challenger "No Worries" Rob Magnum continues....... -------------------------------- Chad Jenkins- WAIT!! ROB MAGNUM HAS ROLLED THE FIGURE FOUR OVER! Now all the pain is focused on the legs of Styles. However, with injured legs as he has, Magnum must still be feeling the pain himself. Ryan Miller- That's right, and he's still going to give up! WHAT! RICK STYLES HAS ROLLED IT OVER AGAIN!! Now, Magnum easily grabs the ropes. I thought it was over there for a second. Chad Jenkins- Now what is Styles doing? It looks like he's attempting to apply the Sharpshooter. They're right by the ropes though, I don't understand this logic. You would think... Ryan Miller- NOOOOO! Magnum just used whatever strength he has left in his legs to push off, and he sent Styles flying between the top and middle rope. Styles is grabbing his shoulder. He landed right in front of our table, and by the looks of things he may have hit his shoulder as he was landing. Chad Jenkins- Remember, his shoulder was already injured from earlier on this evening. Magnum has stepped through the ropes and onto the ring apron. He flies off with a massive elbowdrop, right onto the shoulder of Styles! Ryan Miller- Styles has been all business this entire match, usually he's more cheerful, so you know how much this match means to him. This could be trouble though. Magnum is on his feet, and he has a renewed look of rage on his face! It's STING! STING IS COMING DOWN FROM THE RAFTERS!! Wait, sorry, I was just having a flashback to every Monday night this year. Chad Jenkins- Magnum picks Styles up and drops him face first across the ring apron! His nose really took the hardest blow that time. It's gushing blood again, and Styles looks like he's really in pain! Ryan Miller- Tiffany is up on the ring apron. I think she's telling the referee he needs to stop this match due to unnecessary violence. You now, she's really got a point. Wrestling shouldn't be glorified... Chad Jenkins- HERE COMES KYLE KAMIKAZE!! He's slugging away at Magnum! Magnum is soaking up his blows like their nothing! He grabs Kamikaze around the throat! __CHOKESLAM THROUGH _OUR_ ANNOUNCING TABLE!!__ Kyle's foot almost hit me in the head. Ryan Miller- Oh, that's great. Like we don't need a table to sit at. I guess we can just stand for the rest of the night, because with these two guys we may be here the rest of the night! {CRACK!} Chad Jenkins- Mark Rose just nailed Magnum in the back of the leg with a chair! AGAIN! Magnum is still on his feet! He spins around and nails Rose with a right hand, sending him to his back! Tiffany is finally done with the referee, but the damage to Magnum's leg has already been done! Ryan Miller- Magnum grabs Styles around the head. DDT ONTO OUR TV MONITOR, which was knocked onto the floor when Kamikaze was slammed through our table. Styles head could be cut to pieces! This is not good for our champion! This is not good for the UEW! Chad Jenkins- But this _is_ good for "No Worries" Rob Magnum!! With momentum on his side, Magnum rolls Styles back into the ring! He pulls himself onto the ring apron, and steps in between the ropes. Ryan Miller- You have to wonder why Kyle Kamikaze didn't stay back by the guardrail and help the rest of the handcuffed New Alliance members escape! Chad Jenkins- Maybe the New Alliance's plan only needs the managers and Kyle Kamikaze at ringside. They probably feel confident just leaving the rest of their teammates on the sidelines. If that's true, then Rob Magnum should not feel too confident in this situation. Although it's not going to stop him one bit. Ryan Miller- Rob Magnum scoops up Styles.....SMALL PACKAGE!!! O N E .. .. .. .. .. T W O .. .. .. .. .. K I C K O U T!!!!!!!!!! Chad Jenkins- AT THE VERY LAST SECOND, ROB MAGNUM JUST BARELY KICKED OUT!!!! WE ALMOST SAW A SUCCESSFUL TITLE DEFENSE BY RICK STYLES!!! Both men up quickly.....Magnum with a scoop and a KILLER BACKBREAKER!!!! Magnum is just pushing down on both ends of Styles....the pain has to be excruciating!!!! Ryan Miller- The referee is checking to see if Styles wants to call it quits. Don't waste your time, ref! Chad Jenkins- Magnum picks Styles up.....slings him over his shoulder......AND DOWN WITH A SHOULDERBREAKER!!!! And that was down on Styles' bad shoulder as well!!! MAGNUM GOES FOR THE PIN!!!! O N E .. .. .. .. .. T W O .. .. .. .. .. K I C K O U T!!!!!!!!!! Chad Jenkins- TWO AND NINE TENTHS.....SO CLOSE FOR MAGNUM!!! WE ALMOST HAD A NEW UEW ULTIMATE CHAMPION!!!! Ryan Miller- Come on....someone do something!!! Can't let Magnum win!!! Chad Jenkins- MAGNUM IS SIGNALING FOR IT!!! THE SOUTHERN COMFORT!!!! RICK STYLES TRIES TO ROLL OUT OF THE RING. Ryan Miller- MAGNUM GRABBED HIS BOOTS AND IS PULLING HIM BACK IN THE RING!!! Chad Jenkins- MAGNUM KICKS STYLES IN THE STOMACH......STYLES BENDS DOWN AND MAGNUM PUTS HIS HEAD BETWEEN HIS KNEES!!! IT'S SOUTHERN COMFORT TIME!!! Ryan Miller- Mark R. Rose on the apron..........ROB MAGNUM RUNS OVER AND ELBOWS HIM INTO THE STEEL BARRICADE!!! Chad Jenkins- MR. Z IS ON THE APRON SCREAMING!!!! Ryan Miller- ROB MAGNUM ELBOWS HIM OFF THE APRON!!! Chad Jenkins- TIFFANY JUMPS ONTO THE APRON TRYING TO DISTRACT MAGNUM!!!!! Ryan Miller- MAGNUM WOULDN'T HIT A WOMAN, WOULD HE?!?! Chad Jenkins- AS MUCH OF A BAD ASS MAGNUM IS, HE WOULD NEVER HIT A WOMAN!!! Ryan Miller- MAGNUM IS LOOKING OVER AT TIFFANY AS SHE ARGUES WITH THE REFEREE OVER IN THE CORNER!! Chad Jenkins- MAGNUM WOULDN'T HIT A WOMAN!!!!! OH MY GOD!!!!!!!!! (CROWD EXPLODES INTO CHEERS!!!!!!!) Ryan Miller- OH MY GOD!!! THAT BASTARD!!!!! Chad Jenkins- ROB MAGNUM JUST SHOVED THE ___REFEREE__ INTO TIFFANY.....KNOCKING HER OFF THE APRON!!!!!!!!! Ryan Miller- MAGNUM RUNS BACK TO THE MIDDLE OF THE RING.......HE'S GOT STYLES' HEAD BETWEEN HIS KNEES!!!!! HE'S GIVING THE SIGNAL!!!!!!! Crowd- OHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!! Chad Jenkins- Rick Styles flung his head up into Rob Magnum's crotch....preventing the Southern Comfort. Ryan Miller- DDT by Rick Styles...and Magnum drops to the mat in a heap. Brilliant series of moves by the UEW Ultimate champion. Styles is also on the mat getting a little of a breather. That was too close for the New Alliance. Magnum came too close to winning the UEW Ultimate title. Chad Jenkins- Rick Styles looks over at the rest of the New Alliance handcuffed to the railing and gives them the thumbs up. Rick Styles has no worries when it comes to the result of this match. Ryan Miller- Rick Styles pulls Magnum's leg over to the bottom rope.......stands on the leg for leverage....and then down with his knee to Magnum's knee. Although it may have hurt Styles' knee a little bit.....it hurt Magnum's knee a whole lot more. Chad Jenkins- Styles drags the huge 361 pound man to the center of the ring. He intertwines his leg with Magnum's......he's got a standing figure four locked onto Rob Magnum. Ryan Miller- Brilliant strategy by Rick Styles. He's wearing him down with submission holds. They are much easier than picking up a 360 pound man over and over again. He's also working on the leg that his New Alliance stablemates injured before Magnum even hit the ring. Chad Jenkins- Styles lets go of the standing figure four and drives his elbow into the knee of Rob Magnum. Magnum's wincing in pain on the mat.....I haven't seen that happen since Magnum's rookie year. Styles is well in control. Ryan Miller- Mark Rose is still out cold on the outside.....Mr. Z and Tiffany are helping him back to the locker rooms. It'll just be Kyle Kamikaze left on the outside for Rick Styles. Chad Jenkins- Rick Styles throws Magnum to the outside...who lands on his knee hard! Styles goes over to talk with the ref while Kyle Kamikaze grabs a chair from a fan at ringside. (((WHACK!))) Ryan Miller- OHHH! KYLE KAMIKAZE JUST SLAMMED THAT STEEL CHAIR ACROSS THE ALREADY INJURED KNEE OF ROB MAGNUM!!! I LOVE IT!!! Chad Jenkins- Kyle just rolls in Rob Magnum like nothing happened. Rick Styles can put Magnum away whenever he wants to. Ryan Miller- Rob Magnum is in the middle of the ring struggling to get to his feet. Rick Styles with a stomp to Magnum's injured knee. Chad Jenkins- Look at the spirit in Rob Magnum.....he's still trying to make it to his feet through the barrage of stomps and kicks to his injured knee! Ryan Miller- Rick Styles off the ropes.......HE TRIPPED!!!!!! Chad Jenkins- MAGNUM WITH A KICK TO STYLES' GUT............ _______SOUTHERN______ _______COMFORT_______!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Ryan Miller- MAGNUM HOOKS THE LEG!!!!!!!!! THE COVER!!!!!! O N E!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! .. .. .. .. .. T W O!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! .. .. .. .. .. T H R E E !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! *DING**DING**DING* Ryan Miller- OH MY GOD!!!!!!!!!!! Chad Jenkins- THE CROWD IS ON THEIR FEET!!!!! THEY CAN'T BELIEVE WHAT THEY JUST SAW!!!!!!!! Rick "The Ring" Announcer- THE WINNER OF THIS BOUT......AND _____NEW____ UEW ULTIMATE CHAMPION........................"NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO" "WOOOOORRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEEE EEEEEEEEEEEEEEESSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS" RRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRROOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBB MAGNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMM!!!!!!!! Chad Jenkins- I DON'T BELIEVE IT....ROB MAGNUM IS THE NEW UEW ULTIMATE CHAMPION!!!!! Ryan Miller- HOW THE HELL DID THAT HAPPEN?!?!?!? Chad Jenkins- LET'S TAKE A LOOK AT WHAT JUST HAPPENED!!!!! >>> UEW FLASHBACK- Moments ago (Rick Styles stomps on Rob Magnum's injured knee...then bounces off the ropes.....and is tripped up by Kyle Kamikaze..........Magnum kicks Styles in the stomach then nails the Southern Comfort for the pin) <<< (camera switches back to show Mike Haynes and Kevin Diamond) Kevin Diamond- NO!!!!! THERE IS NO WAY IN HELL THAT KYLE KAMIKAZE TRIPPED UP RICK STYLES!!!! Mike Haynes- YOU JUST SAW IT ON THE VIDEOTAPE!!!!!! IT HAPPENED!!!!! Kevin Diamond- NO!!!!! IT WAS AN ACCIDENT...IT HAD TO BE!!!! Mike Haynes- NO IT WASN'T!!! TAKE A LOOK AT THIS INSTANT REPLAY!!! YOU COULD CLEARLY SEE KYLE KAMIKAZE GRAB RICK STYLES' FOOT....TRIPPING HIM UP ENOUGH TO BE CAUGHT IN THE SOUTHERN COMFORT!!!! Kevin Diamond- No. I refuse to believe that Kyle Kamikaze would stab his own best friend in the back!!! Especially after what we saw happen to Professor Payne earlier!!! KYLE KAMIKAZE DID NOT COST RICK STYLES THE UEW ULTIMATE CHAMPIONSHIP!!!!! Mike Haynes- Rob Magnum just grabbed the UEW Ultimate title off the timekeeper's table and is walking back up the aisleway. He has the belt raised high in the air and is soaking in the crowd's reaction to this shocker!!!!! ROB MAGNUM IS THE NEW UEW ULTIMATE CHAMPION!!!! Kevin Diamond- I still refuse to believe what I just saw!!! I'll be back!!!! (Kevin Diamond leaves the podium) Mike Haynes- As Rob Magnum walks up the aisleway as the new UEW Ultimate champion, he stops and spits on all of the New Alliance members still handcuffed to the railing!!!! Magnum knows they will all be gunning for him and his new title in the near future!! (Rob Magnum raises his fist in the air and soaks in the loud chorus of mixed cheers and boos) Mike Haynes- Where is Kevin Diamond??? Kevin Diamond- Mike, I am here in the aisle waiting for a word with Kyle Kamikaze. Here he comes. (Kevin tries to keep up with Kyle Kamikaze as he walks out of the ring without even looking at Rick Styles...and past all of his fellow New Alliance members without making any eye contact with them) Kevin Diamond- KYLE!!! KYLE!!!! Why did you accidentally trip up Rick Styles causing him to lose the UEW Ultimate title???!? (Kyle Kamikaze shakes his head and mouths the words "no accident".) Kevin Diamond- What are you talking about??? You two are best friends inside the ring and out!! There has never been any tension between you two in the past. You've never competed for the same belts because he's always been on the Ultimate title level and you've always been on either the American or the TV level........ (Kyle blows by Kevin Diamond who can't keep up anymore) Kevin Diamond- Mike, I don't know why Kyle Kamikaze cost Rick Styles the title tonight, and it looks like he's not talking to anyone about it. Back to you! Mike Haynes- Thanks Kevin....our camera is following Kyle Kamikaze to the back. It's Mark Rose....he saw what happened on the videoscreen in the back and is SCREAMING at Kyle Kamikaze. KYLE JUST SHOVED HIM TO THE GROUND AND KEEPS ON WALKING!!!! Kevin Diamond- Did the New Alliance just break up in front of our eyes???!?! Mike Haynes- Kyle exits into the Astrodome parking lot and is walking over to his car. Kevin Diamond- Kyle wants nothing to do with Rick Styles...Rick Styles is still in the ring trying to make sense of the whole thing. He has less of an understanding of what happened than we do!!! Mike Haynes- Kyle is in his car and is driving out of the Astrodome lot. We have just witnessed one of the most bizarre scenes in recent UEW history!!! Kevin Diamond- And we have a new UEW Ultimate champion in "No Worries" Rob Magnum!!! *(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)* *(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)* Rob Magnum defeated Rick Styles to become the __NEW__ UEW ULTIMATE CHAMPION!!! *(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)* *(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)*(*)* Mike Haynes- We're almost out of time, Kevin, do you have any special plans for the holidays?? Kevin Diamond- Are you kidding? THE ROB MAGNUM VICTORY PARTY!!! WOO-HOO!!!!!! SEE YA THERE!!!! Mike Haynes- Ladies and gentlemen, on behalf of Kevin Diamond, Rick "The Ring" and the ENTIRE UEW STAFF, I am Mike Haynes.....SO LONG FROM HOLIDAY HORROR 2!!!! HAPPY HOLIDAYS EVERYONE!!!! (camera focuses on Rick Styles as he turns his back on the rest of the New Alliance and starts to walk to the back in disgust)
