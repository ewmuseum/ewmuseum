Title Tournament, Round One: The Emperor vs. Peter Starshine

18 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MSGID: RzJrLMSaUf8J X-From: [detail withheld] (Dr Pepper) Match for Absolute Wrestling Entertainment [AWE] by Dr Pepper, aka The Masked Booker Date: 02-05-18 Wrestlers: The Emperor vs Peter Starshine Match Type: Singles Tournament, Round 1 [*Start*] Lester Holland: Before this next match, let's take a look at one of the most eager of the new talent in the AWE. I mean this guy was the first one here this morning, hours before the show. For that matter, he was here before any of the crew. Jerry Brooks: That's because he pulled his car into the parking lot at about 3 am. And since he couldn't afford to stay at a hotel, he just slept in his car. Holland: We are speaking of course, of Peter Starshine. Roll the clip. [It's just after dawn. The back parking lot of the AWE Arena is empty, except for a familiar faded lime green Pinto. Since we last saw it, it seems to have picked up a few more dents. And yes, the rust spots are visibly larger. The muffler no longer drags, it's gone. A security mini truck pulls up. A woman in her mid 30's, dressed in a guard's uniform gets out, and unlocks a small door in the back of the main building. She goes inside. The driver side door of the Pinto opens. Peter Starshine, wrapped in a blanket, falls out. After untangling himself, he retrieves an old battered suitcase and heads for the building. The guard stops him at the door. After displaying his driver's license and a fistful of official letters from Absolute Wrestling Entertainment's personnel office, she allows him to go in.] Brooks: I bet he asked her if she had an older sister. [The scene now shows Starshine stripping off his clothes in a locker room and adding them to a filthy pile beside his suticase. The camera quickly shifts to the side for a few seconds, then returns. The pile of clothes is gone and a washing machine is running. Steam is coming from behind a shower partition and Starshine's voice can be heard singing.] Starshine: "Ya know I might be a little lonely So come on girl and have some bologana You're to shy to take your pants off But I'm not so come on over" [The scene changes to show a work crew unloading a large truck inside the back area. Peter Sunshine, hair wet, shaved, and wearing a large bathrobe, helps himself to coffee and donuts, oblivious of angry complaints. He wanders along a hallway trying doors until he finds an unlocked office. He goes in, lies down on the couch, and immediately goes back to sleep. The clip ends.] Holland: Peter Starshine, getting clean and rested for the show. Brooks: It's sad that a musical genius like Peter doesn't get the recognition he deserves. He should have been put up in a suite and brought to the arena by limo. Holland: You know, i can never tell if you're being serious or sarcastic. "Doc" Hanson: Next up, another tournament match. First, a man born to rule: The Em-Per-Or! [White Zombie's "More Human than Human" begins to blast over the loudspeakers. The Emperor emerges from the back to a huge chorus of boos, wearing a black cape and a black full-legged singlet. He walks down the aisle, paying absolutely no attention to the rabble in the crowd. He climbs the ringsteps onto the apron, then steps over the top rope into the ring. He removes his cape and then stands in the corner.] Hanson: And his opponent, Pe-Ter Star-Shine! [Peter Starshine comes out to the top of the ramp. He's wearing his tight blue jumpsuit with white wrestling boots. His trademark chest hair is combed into a proud, gleaming mass. He's holding a large cheap guitar with no strings.] Holland: No entrance music? Brooks: He's probably having a tough time, choosing from his many great songs. Holland: I'm amazed you can say that with a straight face, Brooks: Look, the Emperor has a fan! [A man in the front row stands up as Starshine comes down the ramp. The man is wearing a tshirt that shows the Emperor dressed as Julius Caesar, with laurel wreath and implacable "thumbs down" signal, and the words "IMPERIAL POWER".] Holland: Starshine is ignoring him. Wait, he's got signs! [The fan holds up a series of signs, one after the other.] Sign: GOOD MORNING, STARSHINE Sign: THE MAT SAYS HELLO Sign: YOU'LL TINKLE IN YOUR PANTS WHEN Sign: THE EMP LAYS YOU LOW Sign: GOOD MORNING, STARSHINE Sign: SO CRAPPY TO SEE Sign: YOUR LAME ATTEMPTS TO GET SOME Sign: ATTENTION FOR YOUR STUPID SONGS! [Starshine scowls and goes the the ring.] Holland: The Emperor, watching Peter Starshine enter the ring. Starshine, pretending to play that guitar. And he smashed him over the head with it, i kind of expected that. Brooks: That blow had absolutely no effect. The Emperor, brushing bits of balsa wood out of his hair, while Starshine just stands there looking foolish with the broken off neck of the guitar in his hand. [laughter] Holland: Starshine just jammed that guitar neck into the Emperor's gut! [big pop] Brooks: The Emperor is doubled over. Starshine throws away the weapon and strikes the Emperor in the back of the neck with a double axehandle shot. The Emperor is knocked sprawling. Starshine, winding up for a kick. Holland: But the Emperor grabs his ankle! [big pop] Brooks: The Emperor dumps Starshine over. Then hauls him up again. Shoulder breaker! [pop] Holland: The Emperor, with a power slam. And a cover. 1! Kickout! Brooks: The Emperor is trying to put Starshine back down with a series of roundhouse punches. Holland: Starshine ducks. Starshine with an atomic drop. [pop] Holland: And a belly to belly suplex! [big pop] Holland: Elbow drop to the sternum! [big pop] Holland: And a sleeper. Sign: GOT CURMUDGEON? Brooks: Starshine's trying to lock in that sleeper hold. But the Emperor just stood up! [big pop] Holland: The Emperor plucks Starshine off his back. He's lifting Starshine up by his temples. Imperial Power! [monster pop] Holland: Power bomb! [monster pop] Brooks: Starshine, looking pretty banged up, but he's still moving-- sunset flip! [big pop] Holland: Both men rising. The Emperor with a clothesline, knocking Starshine back down. The Emperor, sending Starshine to the ropes, and meeting him on the rebound with a big boot! [pop] Brooks: The Emperor with a vertical suplex. Another vertical suplex! The invasion choke slam! [big pop] Holland: Here's the cover. 1! 2! 3! No! Brooks: Starshine barely got his shoulder up that time. Holland: Starshine, rolling out of the ring. The Emperor isn't following, he's just standing there with that menacing stare of his. Brooks: Referee Evan Patterson, starting the count. Starshine is leaning over the guardrail offering to let that woman in the mumu touch his chest hair. Holland: And she's threatening him with mace. He's so misunderstood. But he's got to get back into the ring or he'll be counted out. Brooks: Starshine rolling back in. The Emperor immediately goes for a stomp. Holland: Starshine catches him with a legwhip while he's off balance! [big pop] Brooks: You know, in the Emperor's official profile, he claims that his singleminded focus on destroying his opponent is one of his strengths. But i'm inclined to think that it's also a weakness, since it means he pays less attention to the need for defense. Holland: You could be right, although it's hard to think of a man as big as the Emperor needing to think about defense. See how much trouble Starshine is having trying to apply that crossface hold. Nope, he just dropped it in favor of an arm scissors. Say, what does Starshine's profile say? Brooks: I never got one, i'm winging it here. Holland: It's probably in his car under a pile of big mac wrappers. Sign: I WANNA BE THE EMPEROR'S RUG BUNNY Brooks: Starshine has the Emperor's arm and shoulder immoblized. The Emperor, dragging them both. And the Emperor grabs the rope! [pop] Brooks: Starshine releases the hold. The Emperor jumps to his feet and comes right back down with a knee drop! Holland: Which Starshine dodged! [big pop] Brooks: The Emperor is a little wobbly on that knee. Starshine sends him into the ropes. Starshine with a knee lift. Armdrag takedown. Baseball slide. The Emperor is on his back with his legs on either side of the ring post. Starshine, outside the ring, grabs an ankle... OOF! [big sadistic pop] Holland: The Emperor is doubled up. Starshine pulls him up by the neck and drags him to the ring post. Crowd: 1! 2! 3! 4! 5! [big pop] Holland: Starshine pulls his opponent's head away, and now back to it. But the Emperor blocked! [pop] Brooks: The Emperor knocks Starshine away with a hard left. Starshine back with a snapmare takedown. Starshine, kicking the Emperor in the same knee he hurt earlier. And again. The Emperor is staggering. Starshine with a gutwrench power bomb! [big pop] Holland: The Emperor is down! Starshine goes for the cover! [big pop] 1! No! Brooks: The Emperor hurls Starshine off him with a stiff palm. Never underestimate the Emperor's strength. Holland: Or his staying power. The Emperor is limping a bit on that knee, while Starshine is looking distinctly winded. No wonder, all he's had to eat today is some donuts and black coffee! Sign: LOUNGE SINGER WORLD ORDER Brooks: The Emperor, lunging. Starshine with a left hook to the jaw, but the Emperor ignores it. He grabs Starshine and lifts him up. He's just holding him over his head, walking around the ring. It's the delayed gorilla press slam. This is the delay. THUNK!! Holland: And there's the slam! [monster pop] [The man with the Emperor tshirt stands up and gives the "thumbs down" signal. Slowly, others do likewise until there are close to 30.] Brooks: The Emperor is one of those wrestlers whose show of contempt for the fans ensures their loyalty. And whether it's to please them or not, it looks as if he's going for Capital Punishment, his human crucifix submission hold. [The crowd is silent as the Emperor picks up the prone Peter Starshine. Cheers begin as he positions him, building to a sustained roars as he hangs him stretched out over his back.] Holland: This is it! [As the referee moves to ask if Starshine wants to give up, the house lights suddenly go off. Only random strobes remain, illuminating parts of the arena in succession.] Brooks: Just a technical glitch folks, i'm sure it'll be fixed in a moment. Holland: Look! [The flashing strobe shows a figure running down the ramp. The split second shots catch an impression of long black hair, purple shorts, a purple top, and a steel pipe.] Brooks: That looks like-- Xara Carrera! Holland: Xara Carrera? What would she be doing here? CRUNCH! [Silence. A few seconds later, the house lights come back on. The Emperor is lying flat on his back, a big purple welt swelling on one temple. A pipe lies on the ring apron. Peter Starshine is also down, but he crawls over to the Emperor, and puts an arm over his chest. 1! 2! 3! Hanson: Your winner, Pe-Ter Star-Shine! Holland: And Peter Starshine goes on to round two of the tournament, thanks to the unexpected interference from Xara Carrera. Brooks: Well, maybe. It kind of seemed to me that this person was much too tall to be Carrera. Not to mention, there was a certain lack of, well fullness, in that top. Of course, Carrera *has* been taking a lot of steroids lately. Holland: You shouldn't say such things without something to back them up. Brooks: You're right. I should have said, according to a sometimes reliable rumor website. Holland: That's better. Brooks: Two staffers are helping the Emperor up. Meanwhile Peter Starshine is swaggering up the ramp. He's again offering to let women touch his chest hair. Uh oh, he offered a different part of his anatomy to the man in the Emperor tshirt! Security stopped the man from coming over the guardrail. Holland: And there goes Starshine. I wonder where he'll be sleeping tonight. Brooks: Well, wherever he ends up he'll be having sweet dreams about the AWE title belt. Holland: Yeah, dreams about keeping it in his glove compartment! [*end*]
