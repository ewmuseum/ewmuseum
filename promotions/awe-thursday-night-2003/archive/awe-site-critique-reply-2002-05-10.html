MSGID: zyLJ1dzJnOwJ Well, first things first, let me get out this out of the way...I hate frames. Just a personal thing, not really a critque. 1) Browser loaded it but "there are errors on the page." Looking at your code, I think it's probably just a GeoCities issue so no fault of your own. 2) Menu at the top isn't clearly defined what they go to. The images are a nice touch, but I would recommend a mouseover -- something, perferrably text, that tells the surfer exactly what each link does. The alt text goes a little way to solve that problem, but most people will immediately get fustrated. An idea I might propose is use that flash space -- the one on the right -- a little more effeciently. Use it as an alternative menu using flash -- would be a nice effect. 3) This is the reason why I hate frames: your top frame, your menu, is too wide, causing the scrolling bar on the side to appear with about a 10 pixel space to scroll up and down from. Yeah yeah, I'm bitching about 10 pixels, but to me, it's a little annoying and detracts alot from the seamlessness that I think you are tryin to create. It honestly might be solved by setting the marginheight to zero. If you need to know how to do that, let me know. 4) The AWE is OPEN semi-scrolling message seems unbalanced. Either have it scroll all away across the body frame, or have another message show up there. What might be cool is, if you decide not to do a mouse over on the menu as mentioned in #2, it might be a neat description place -- ie, roll over application and the phrase "Application...to be in AWE" might come up there, though I think that would require you to do away with the frame. An alternative text-based menu on the bottom of every page would also be a nice touch. And yes, I do mean every page -- aids with navigation. CHAMPIONSHIPS PAGE You know that the World Title History Page is missing a link? I would also recommend setting the champion's name off slightly. APPLICATION PAGE Where do you send the application? Put a link somewhere there. FAQ You go out of style on that one. Instead of White Lettering on Black Background, it's reversed. Might want to think about changing it and/or increasing the font size. And on a final, personal note, I'm tired of the the red & black colorscheme -- yes, I'm guilty of it too. It just seems that everywhere I go, that's the color scheme that pops up. I think it's time for some originality in this day and age. Okay...enough with the Rant. I know it sounds like I'm being a little harsh, Adam -- you asked for a critique and I gave you as professional a-one as I can give ya. It's just nuggets of advice -- use 'em, don't use 'em -- it's all up to you.
