Title Tournament, Round One: Adam Simon vs. Keiju Kikai

18 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MSGID: hq7eCnyfZCwJ AWE got suspended by Little Blue Super Jew, but the coming show was never put up so one of the writers put up his matches. One of which was yours truly against Kakai, a guy who wears a giant foam rubber suit made to look like the island of Kakai. Anyhoo, for apparently no reason at all, Starz makes a guest appearance. I thought I'd put it up because it's a good match anyway and a weird opponent. ---------------- Match for Absolute Wrestling Entertainment [AWE] by Dr Pepper, aka The Masked Booker Date: 02-05-18 Wrestlers: Adam Simon vs Keiju Kikai Match Type: Singles Tournament, Round 1 [*Start*] Lester Holland: Before this next match, let's look at this clip from earlier today when our interviewer, Raylene O'Patrick, attempted to catch up with Keiju Kikai. [*Jumbotron*] [O'Patrick is in a hallway. She stops in front of a door with a sign that says "KEIJU KIKAI". She knocks.] O'Patrick: Keiju Kikai! Please, just a few questions! [There is no response.] O'Patrick: Keiju Kikai! What did you think of your previous match, where you lost to a seemingly impaired Chrisopher Pike? [A roar can be heard through the door.] O'Patrick: Keiju Kikai! Some people think you would have won that match if Pike's valet, Angela, hadn't interfered. Do have a statement about that? [A snarl can be heard through the door.] O'Patrick: Keiju Kikai! Tonight, you'll be meeting Adam Simon in the first round of the world championship tournament. Can you give us a hint about your strategy? [The door suddenly pops open and a large ball of paper flies out and hits her in the face. The door slams shut again. The camera shows the ball lying on the floor, then zooms in. It slowly uncrumples to show a floor plan of the AWE Arena. A huge black footprint has been drawn over it.] [*End Jumbotron*] Holland: Wew, Kikai means business! Jerry Brooks: We'll see. [The house lights suddenly dim.] Holland: I believe this is the part where Kikai's manager, Vile Vince Viper, sneaks down to ringside to position himself to, er, support his client. Brooks (putting hands over eyes): I see NUH-Think! [Bull Tong's entrance theme from the film "God of Cookery" blasts over the PA system as a short figure rushes down the aisle, its dead white face and hands contrasting strangely with its jet black hair and tight black snakeskin suit.] Gregory "Doc" Hanson: Introducing at this time, standing at 9'1 and weighing in at 274lbs: he hails from the island of Kikai, hell, he IS the ISLAND OF KIKAI. THE ULTIMATE GIANT MONSTER: KEIJU KIKAI!!! [The camera pans in on the tron where a map of Japan in shown. Suddenly a dissolve takes us to a closer image of the map. At the third dissolve it becomes apparent the focus is on the island of Kikai. As another dissolve takes us closer, the footage begins to shake before a jump cut shows dirt being unearthed as some trees are pulled out of the ground. Another jump cut sees waves crashing away from a large land mass. Then as the shaking stops the an image of a flat earth is shown, this gives way to a cheesy CGI image of a sinister red eye opening out of the earth. Stock footage shows a model of an island rising out of the water. A flash of light then introduces us to the final product of these images, a picture of KEIJU KIKAI.] [Kikai rushes out of the backstage in his goofy giant monster costume, to a surprising amount of delighted cheers, which are quickly drowned out by a chorus of boos. As Kikai methodically paces down the aisle the tron shows stills of him fighting with various movie monsters, including Mothra, King Kong, and Godzilla.] [The lights come back up to show Kikai standing in the ring with his arms folded.] Hanson: And his opponent, weighing it at just over two hundred thirty pounds, from the Happiest Place on Earth, ADAM SIMON! [There is a silence in the arena. Slowly, fans rise to their feet and pump their fists in the air, chanting.] Crowd: SI-MON-SI-MON-SI-MON-SI-MON! [The crowd goes wild as the first chords of Kid Rock's "Cocky" play.] PA: THEY SAY I'M COCKY, BUT YOU KNOW WHAT? IT AIN'T BRAGGING IF I SAY IT AND I BACK IT UP! [Adam Simon throws aside the curtain and steps out into the strobe lights. He pauses and raises his fists high as the crowd cheers.] [Simon walks down the aisle as the firey pyros behind him go off. He points at Kikai as he walks to the ring. Simon gets to ringside and throws his sunglasses into the crowd. Then, in one quick hop, Simon lands on the ring apron and ducks under the top rope.] [10 fans in the third row raise a long sign overhead: ADAMANIACS.] Holland: Both men starting out cautiously and-- woah, Simon with a single leg takedown! Brooks: I think he's going for a stepover toe hold. It's not one of his regular moves, but that costume makes a lot of submission holds difficult to apply. Holland: Kikai is grimacing, trying to resist the hold. Now he's pointing at something. Brooks: He's indicating the top of his costume, the Tombi-zaki Point is sticking 3 feet over the edge. But senior referee William Yeath doesn't agree that counts as being under the ropes. Kikai uses his great strength to pull against the twist and kicks out with his other foot. Simon drops the hold. Kikai scrambles to his feet. Holland: Simon with a drop kick, no good, just bounces off. Kikai capitalizes with a snap fishermans suplex. Kikai with a tiger driver. Brooks: Tiger driver 91, you mean. Holland: Are you sure? Brooks: As sure as i can be until the next "official" list of moves comes out. Holland: Kikai is now climbing the ropes. My, that looks precarious. Split legged moonsault. Now he's kneeling over his opponent, pummelling him with palm blows. Brooks: Simon is wilting under the onslaught, he's got to do something to break the momentum. Holland: Simon with a double forearm block and-- a leg scissors? He can't be serious! Brooks: True, he can't apply enough pressure through the costume, but as Kikai goes to pull him off he rolls clear. Simon now, with a thrust kick. Holland: Kikai is overbalanced, he fell over! [big pop] [Simon runs across his opponent's back, then stops and mimes wiping sweat from his face.] [big pop and laughter] Brooks: It's a new event in Men's Track and Field: the Sprint Across the Island of Kikai! Holland: Kikai getting up. Simon with a bulldog on the upper part of that costume, bending it down. Kikai's vision is blocked by his own costume! [pop] Brooks: Simon's wrestling style has been described as daredevil stunts and showboating, this match seems to provide plenty of scope for that. Holland: Simon, letting go. Kikai's costume pops back up so he can see-- Brooks: He can see, but he can't see Simon! Simon slipped around behind him! [As Kikai looks around for Simon, Simon keeps pace behind him, with a finger to his lips.] [big pop, loud laughter] Holland: Kikai, throwing himself backward, he must have figured it out! [pop] Brooks: Simon is squashed into the ringpost. Kikai turns around and grabs him. He may look clumsy, but remember, Kikai *is* a cruiserweight and a flyer. Exploder suplex! [big pop] Holland: Kikai with a brainbuster. Kikai with an armbar into a faceplant. Kikai pulls Simon up. Side headlock on Simon. Kikai, sitting down with that side headlock cinched in tight. Crowd: Resthold! Bor-r-ring! Brooks: Stupid fans can't tell a resthold from a brilliant piece of strategy. Neither can referee William Yeath, haha! [The jumbotron shows a closeup. Kikai's hold has Simon's face up against a part of his costume that features a pine forest. Kikai's movements are causing the little plastic pine trees to grind into Simon's eyes, nose, and throat.] [big pop] Holland: Simon, straining to reach behind him-- he's got his opponent's arm and-- there! Thumb jab just under the funny bone, one of his martial arts escape tricks! Brooks: Simon, up on his feet, pulling Kikai up with him. Arm wrench and hook kick combo. And an adamatic! [big pop] Holland: Simon, in control now, sends Kikai to the ropes. Simon with a rolling clothesline. Kikai bounces off the ropes again. What's Simon doing, now? Spear! [big pop] Brooks: That one certainly caught Kikai by surprise. Simon now-- hey, where's our beer cooler? [The camera shifts back behind the announcing table to show a man holding a cooler with the AWE logo. He's pulling beers out of it and tossing them to the crowd.] Holland: That's Frankie Starz! Brooks: Damn freeloader! I heard he was out in the parking lot earlier, selling Heidi Leick calendars. Holland: Really? Are there any left? Um, i mean, i have a niece who really looks up to her, and, uh... Brooks: Hey, your fantasy life is none of my business. Holland: Uh, yeah. Both men circling warily now. Brooks: Simon's pretty face is all marked up. Sign: HOGAN JOBBED TO CURMUDGEON! Holland: Kikai with a stiff kick. Simon caught it. Enziguri by Kikai! [pop] Brooks: That's not the sort of move you expect from a man hampered by a foam rubber suit. Holland: Kikai with a wristlock. He's maintaining control by walking backwards, keeping Simon's arm stretched stretched out. Now Kikai is climbing the ropes. Is he going to try a rope walk? Brooks: No, it's a senton bomb! [big pop] Holland: Kikai pulling Simon up, and dragging him over to the ropes. He's slamming Simon's throat into the top rope. Crowd: 1! 2! 3! 4! 5! [big pop] Brooks: Kikai turns around and spreads his arms to show the referee that he's let Simon go. But he's also blocking the ref's view. Holland (shivers): Ooh, that pale boney arm, snaking out from under the ring! Brooks: It's Gollum! Heh, it's Vile Vince Viper, coming out of hiding to lend a hand. [VVV pulls himself up to the apron, then lunges upwards. He catches Simon's head in both hands, then drops to the floor. Simon's throat hits the rope for a 6th time, much harder though. Simon bounces back and falls face first onto the mat.] [monster pop] Holland: Viper, scuttling back into his underground lair. The Island Monster, raising one massive foot over Simon's prone body. Crowd: Stomp! Brooks: Look, Kikai is doing the Godzilla Hop. [Kikai hops on both feet, while alternating his arms from curved overhead, to tight against his belly.] Crowd: Stomp! Stomp! Stomp! Sign: RSPWF 3:16 SAYS WE JUST DISSED YOUR FED! Holland: Simon is still out. Kikai, climbing up the ringpost. He's holding up his fists... [big pop] Holland: Now he's tapping the area above his forehead, it's the part of the island called Kikai-Cho. [monster pop] Brooks: That's the signal for the Kikai-Cho swandive headbutt. The fans are on their feet now. [sustained monster pop] Holland: Kikai-Cho! No! Simon moved! [big pop] Brooks: Simon gets to his feet. The welts and gouges on his face are oozing blood. There's a nasty purple mark across his throat. That puffy area in front is adams apple sauce. Kikai lies sprawled. Holland: Simon going up top. Simonsault! [big pop] Brooks: Usually Simon would do his crowd pleasing ddt sequence now, but it's hard to ddt the Island Monster. Holland: Simon, pulling up Kikai. Implant ddt! Swinging ddt! [pop] Brooks: No joy, the ddt starts too low for the impact to overcome Kikai's padding. Holland: Simon now, driving Kikai back with a series of right hand punches. Adamatic! [big pop] Brooks: Simon, taking Kikai around his bulky shoulders. He's backing up to the rope. He's up on the first rope. He pulls Kikai up. Yikes, Simon just used the evenflow ddt to send Kikai over the top rope! [monster pop] Brooks: Kikai, hitting the floor outside. So *that's* how you ddt a monster! Holland: Simon, climbing to the top rope now. He's raising a finger in the air. Crowd: ONE MORE TIME! CRASH! Brooks: Simon splash to the outside! [sustained monster pop] [Simon struggles to his feet in obvious pain. He poses for the crowd, then bows as they cheer.] Holland: Vile Vince Viper again, look out! CRUNCH! Brooks: Simon dodges Viper's charge. Triple V just gave himself a triple owie against the guard rail! [sadistic pop] Holland: Kikai is getting up. But Simon has a chair. He slams it into Kikai's gut. The Island Monster is down again. Brooks: Viper is up and moving in. Simon throws the chair and Viper catches it. Holland: Viper should know better because there's the Van Simonator! [big pop] Brooks: Viper falls flat. Simon now, rolling Kikai back into the ring. Sign: ADAMANIA RULES! Holland: Simon leaving the ring again. He's running up the aisle, huh? Brooks: He's talking to that fan with the sign. He's bringing the sign back with him into the ring! [big pop] Holland: He puts down the sign, now he's sitting on the back of Kikai. He's applying the simonizer camel clutch. Brooks: Vile Vince Viper is back on his feet, he's trying to climb into the ring. But referee William Yeath, sees him. He's trying to distract Yeath, but the veteran ref just shoves him off the apron and signals security. VVV retreats up the ramp. Holland: If Viper was trying to buy time for his protege, it didn't help. Kikai is too weak to break out of the hold. Simon, pulling back even harder. And Kikai taps! Kikai has tapped out! [big monster pop] Brooks: Simon releases the hold and Kikai just slumps back down. The referee is raising Simon's hand. Now Simon has that sign again. [Simon swaggers around the ring holding the ADAMANIA RULES! sign over his head.] [big sustained monster face pop] Holland: The crowd is just hysterical! I think they'd carry him on their shoulders all the way to Las Vegas if he'd let them! Simon, getting a chair now and returning to the ring. Vile Vince Viper scowling, watching from the top of the ramp. Adam Simon goes on to round two of the tournament, while Viper's man lies helpless on the mat. [Simon pulls up Kikai and drapes him over the chair. The top of the Island Monster's costume lies on the top rope. With some difficulty, Simon climbs to the top rope, then puts one foot onto the end of the costume, right at Tombi-zaki Point. He holds the sign up with one hand and waves it like a flag.] Simon: I claim this land for ADAMANIA! [insane monster face pop] PA: THEY SAY I'M COCKY, BUT YOU KNOW WHAT? IT AIN'T BRAGGING IF I SAY IT AND I BACK IT UP! [Simon leaves the ring and makes his way through the crowd, stopping to autograph the sign and return it to its maker.] [Kikai rolls out of the ring and limps up the ramp towards his mentor.] [*end*]
