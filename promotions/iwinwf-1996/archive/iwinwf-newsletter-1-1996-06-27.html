IWINWF NEWSLETTER Thursday, June 27 The Pico Title Tourney Just two days ago, The Pico Title tournament was held. The final pitted Vader X against Big Daddy Cool Diesel. Vader X, along with his manager Mr. Nitro, came out victorous. Here are the competitors post- match reactions.
Vader X : It is time it is Vader X time!! People don't know if I am Vader maybe I am or maybe I am not!! I am the Pico champ!! The truth is I am
Vader's son! Infact Vader X was my fathers name when he was remade to Vader. I was known as Big Van Blaze which in close to Big Van Vader, I was made to a Vader now but differnent I am his son I ma the new Vader
X! I am the Pico Champ!! My father told me to become Vader X he said" It is my destiny!" I am the Pico Champ and I will kick anyones a#%!!!!
Mr.Nitro: I manged Vader once but he intrusted me with his son because he was already invlolved with his own things!! The WWF wanted him to be a tag team but they said no!! Vader X will rule forever!!!
Vader X : IT IS TIME! IT IS VADER X TIME!!!! Diesel: Big Daddy Cool here. Listen up Vader X. Who you think you are man? Malcom X. You sneak attack me in the dressing room, go after Sunny and then claim to be a great champ. I don't think so. Big Daddy Cool is here for one thing. To kick some ass. Vader X I want a rematch, in a cage. Just you and me boy. Let's see if you can withstand a mack truck roaring down you.
