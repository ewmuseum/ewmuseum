IWINWF — Show Script

"(IWINWF) World Title Part 1!"

20 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Insane Jack Tink stands in the ring holding the IWINWF World Title belt) Jack: Well, this is what it's all about. Tonight, ladies and gentlemen, you will see this World Title belt awarded to a very deserving athelete. One who will go through four men tonight. Welcome to the IWINWF 16-man Tournament for the world title. We are broadcasting live from the beautiful Pittsburgh Civic Arena! Now please welcome my special guest commentator, Mr. Jerry Lawler!!
(Jerry Lawler's "Royal Music" plays as he makes his way to the commentary table) (Jack & Jerry at the commentary table) Jerry: Hey, I thought I was gonna host this show. Jack: No, I host every show. Jerry: Who are you? Jack: Insane Jack. Jerry: Your mother gave you that name, right? Jack: Well,... Jerry: Tonight's event is of Royal proportions. I'm glad to be here, Jack. Jack: Good,... Jerry: What's first? Jack: In the first match of the first round we have two newcomers to the IWINWF, Harlem
Hunk & John Luck.
Jerry: There gonna need all the Luck they can get. (John Luck enters the arena with his manager Fabulous Frank as "Golden Years" by David
Bowie plays)
Jerry: He, won't make it. Jack: You never know. (Harlem Hunk walks to ringside with manager Mr. T as "Free" by Goodie Mob plays) Jack: Oh, look that's Mr. T. Jerry: What's the "T" stand for? Tired? Jack: Shut up. Jerry: I'll say whatever I want to say. Harlem Hunk storms the ring. Grabs John Luck by the face and throws him out of the ring. Jack: This match could be over. He landed head first on the concrete. Jerry: That was great! Harlem Hunk goes to the outside and violently chucks Luck back into the ring. He then pounds on Luck with repetitive fists to the face and body. Luck's nose is bleeding profusely. Harlem Hunk picks up John Luck and sets him up for a power bomb off the top rope. But, John Luck, surprisingly, grasps the top rope and nails the Hunk with a lightning fast Frankensteiner. John Luck then attempts his "Royal Flush". John Luck goes for the pin 1,2,3.
Jack: He calls that manuever the "Royal Flush", King. Jerry: There's no way he'll be able to make through another match. Jack: Next up we have Max "The Cop" Ax versus Steve Stakk. (The arena is silent as Max "The Cop" Ax walks to ringside) Jack: This guy looks like he wants to cause problems. Jerry: Looks like a "Big Boss Man" wannabe to me. ("Bulls on Parade" by Rage Against The Machine blasts as Steve Stakk comes to the ring with his manager, Riki Studd.)
Jerry: Now, this guy looks impressive. Jack: I'd say so. Max Ax locks up with Steve Stakk and is able to execute a DDT. But, Steve Stakk quickly gets up.
Jerry: He's upset now, Max. Steve Stakk runs at Max and knocks him down with a strong forearm to the head. He then delivers a falling elbow drop to soloplexes. Steve Stakk then picks him up and locks him up in a double armbar submission hold. But, Max refuses to give up. Steve Stakk releases the hold and executes the Stakk Stunner. Stakk goes for the pin. 1,2,3.
Jerry: Steve Stakk could make it all the way, I think. Jack: Possibly, but he is also new to the IWINWF. Jerry: This is a new federation, everyone's new to it. Jack: Yeah, that's true. Next up is "Electric" Eric Starr versus the winner of an earilier tournament, The Dragon.
(The Dragon enters with Sean in tow) Jerry: He's REALLY impressive. Jack: He's 7'8" and weights in at 565 pounds. Jerry: That's one big dude. He won't make it. Jack: Why do you say that? Jerry: There's no way he'll have enough stamina. ("Electric" Eric Starr makes his way to ringside) Jerry: You just got to feel sorry for him. The Dragon is waiting for Eric Starr, when he climbs into the ring. He knocks him down with several swinging chops to the back. Eric Starr gets to his feet and is actually able to pick the Dragon up and dump him over the top rope. The Dragon, however, quickly re-enters the ring. The Dragon picks Starr up by the waist and rams him in the corner.
Dragon then climbs to the second rope and executes a variation of the piledriver from the top rope. He then picks Starr up and puts him back down with a powerful chokeslam.
He pins him with his hand around his neck. 1,2,3. Jerry: The Dragon is so stupid. Jack: No, he isn't! Jerry: No, you peon, he wasted too much energy on that wimp! Jack: Why don't you got back to the WWF where you came from. The next match is going to be wild! It is Crazy Lio againest "Death Machine" Davis Freeman!
(Crazy Lio comes to the ring) Jerry: Man, he's scary lookin' Jack: Yeah, and we're not even in the ring with him! ("The Death Machine" Davis Freeman comes to ringside with his manager "the Fuse" W.L. Jamison) Jerry: They both look crazy to me, Jack. Jack: That's 'cause they both ARE! Freeman starts to climb into the ring, but Crazy Lio flies over the top rope and lands on him. Crazy Lio then picks up the timekeeper and throws him at Freeman. Freeman then throws the timekeeper back.
Jack: They're playing catch with the timekeeper! Jerry: Ha! This is great! Freeman and Crazy Lio are now viciously attacking the timekeeper! Jack: What the hell? The ref continues the count. The timekeeper is screaming with pain and fear. Jeff Fircak and The Weizal run to ringside. Crazy Lio jumps into the ring, while Freeman takes on
Fircak and the Weizal. Freeman has been counted out.
Jack: Crazy Lio wins! What's he doin' now? Crazy Lio gets jumps to the floor and looks under the ring. He finds a cinder block and throws it at Jeff Fircak. Fircak falls to the ground and the brawl between the Weizal,
Freeman, and Lio continues.
Jack: What insanity! The brawl makes it's way to the locker room, and is broken up by some officials and other wrestlers.
Jerry: Why is Fircak still out here? Jack: He's in the next match-up! Jerry: Againest who? Jack: Vader X! Jerry: Good luck, buddy! (Vader X makes his way slowly to the ring with his manager Mr. Nitro) Jack: "The Firecracker" doesn't have much of a chance at this. Vader X "assists" Fircak into the ring and sets him up for the power bomb. He nails him with it, he goes for the pin. 1,2,3!
Jerry: That's the way! Don't waste any energy! Jack: He probably didn't need to power bomb him. Now, here comes one of your favorites,
Big Daddy Cool, DIESEL!
Jerry: I hate Diesel! (Nine Inch Nails' "Hurt" fills the arena as Diesel comes to the ring) Diesel crosses pathes with Vader X! Vader X clubs him with blows to the head! Diesel falls to a knee. Vader X takes a camera of a fan and breaks it on Diesel's head! Then,
Vader X power bombs him on the ring barrier! Vader X walks away with his hands in the air.
Jerry: Wow! That's nice to see Diesel gets up and stumbles to the ring. ("Bam Bam" Rick Orton comes to ringside) Orton runs into the ring and pounds Diesel down. Diesel again gets up. Diesel shoves
Rick Orton over the top rope! Orton is uneffected however. Orton climbs back into the ring and executes his powerful lariat. Diesel falls limp to the mat! Orton goes for the pin. 1,2,3.
Jerry: I'm liking this more and more! Jack: You like lowlifes who have to cheat or interfere in other people's matches. Jerry: Exactly! Jack: Only two more matches to go in the first round. Next up, two more newcomers "Painbringer" Billy Sexton and Yul B. Hurt. Jerry: Yul B. Hurt, I like the sound of that. ("TNT" by AC/DC blasts as "Painbringer" Billy Sexton comes to the ring) Jerry: He looks ticked off. Jack: I think he always looks that way. Of course, this is the first time I've seen him. (Yul B. Hurt walks calmly to the ring) Jerry: He's a big dude, too. There are a lot of big men in this federation, aren't there? Jack: Yeah, Yul B. Hurt is 6'8" and weights 450 pounds. Our biggest man, however is a foot taller, and 115 pounds heavier. That is the Dragon.
Jack: Yeah, I remember him. Yul B. Hurt climbs the ring stairs and enters the ring. Sexton runs and smashes him in the corner. Sexton quickly applies a variation of the Figure-4, but Hurt grabs the ropes, and the ref breaks the hold. Hurt stands and, while the ref is looking away, thrusts a low boot into Sexton.
Jerry: Ow, geez that hurt. Sexton quickly regains his composure and whips Hurt into the ref. The ref falls to the floor. Sexton then pulls a screwdriver from his trunks.
Jack: Not a safe place to store handtools. He thrusts the screwdriver into Hurt's mouth and knocks out two teeth! Jerry: I think I saw some teeth fly out! Hurt screams and grasps his mouth. Sexton then knocks him down with a running shoulderbutt. The referee re-enters the ring. Sexton locks on his patented armbar submission hold. Hurt submits!
Jack: What a dastardly way to advance! Jerry: You do what you have to. Jack: Just one more match to go in this first round. The Weizal versus the Ha-Ha Bandit. Jerry: The Ha-Ha Bandit? Jack: Yes. (Coolio's "Gangsta's Paradise" shakes the arena as The Ha-Ha Bandit walks to the ring with his manager, Mr. Laughter.)
Jack: This guy is supposed to be well-knowledged in all aspects of wrestling. Jerry: Not as much as I am though. Jack: Give me a break, dude. (Pauly Shore's "Lisa, Lisa (The One I Adore)" fills the arena as the Weizal walks to ringside with his manager Lisa Buddemeyer)
Jerry: Woo! Who's that? And why is a woman like that with a loser like him? Jack: That's Lisa Buddemeyer, the Weizal's manager. I don't think she'd be interested in you.
Jerry: Why's that? I am royalty! Jack: No, she likes me. Jerry: No way. Here she comes, I'm gonna ask her. Hey Lisa! Lisa: What? Jerry: Who would you rather go out with Me or this Jack-guy. Lisa: Please. (She walks away) Jerry: See, Jack, she wants me. Jack: If you say so, Lawler. The Ha-Ha Bandit runs at the Weizal and executes a beautiful martial arts jump kick to his face.The Weizal falls to the mat. The Ha-Ha Bandit climbs to the top strand and jumps off, landing on the Weizal's back. The Weizal rolls out of the ring, and confers with Lisa. The Weizal then jumps back into the ring. He locks up with the Bandit and nails him with a suplex. He then climbs to the top rope and attempts an elbow drop, but misses. The Ha-Ha Bandit picks the Weizal up over his head and drops him on the top of the ring post.
Jack: I bet he's being reminded of what he had for lunch. The Weizal rolls of the top rope and lands on the mat. The Ha-Ha Bandit sets him up for a superplex. But, suddenly, the Weizal reverses it, and chucks the Ha-Ha bandit to the floor. Lisa Buddemeyer nails the Ha-Ha Bandit with a hardback John Grisham novel! The
Weizal then goes to the floor and throws the Bandit back into the ring. He then locks on his Murder Death Lock Grip. The Ha-Ha Bandit submits!
Jerry: That was a good match. Jack: Indeed. Now let's take you to the IWINWF interview center with Bobo D. Hobo! Well, that's all of the first round. Please send in interviews about the first round. The next round will be as follows: John Luck Vs. Steve Stakk The Dragon Vs. Crazy Lio Vader X Vs."Bam Bam" Rick Orton "Painbringer" Billy Sexton Vs. The Weizal Soon as I get some interviews, I'll send out the rest of the card. So send yours in now. -- P.S. stands for PAULY SHORE!! (duh!)
