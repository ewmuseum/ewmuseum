IWINWF — Show Script

"(IWINWF) Monday Night Velocity!"

1 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Insane Jack stands in the ring) Jack: Welcome to IWINWF wrestling on the Future of America Channel! We are broadcasting live from the Pittsburgh Civic Arena!!
(Crowd cheers) Jack: Now, if you'll please welcome my special guest commentator tonight, Eric Starr! (Eric Starr makes his way to the ring, Jack meets him at the commentary table) Eric: (putting on headset) So, this is the Igloo! Jack: Yeah, tonight's card looks to be very important. Eric: Yeah, I hope that Weizal stays away from me. Jack: Why's that? Eric: I wouldn't want to get in trouble for interfering. Jack: Well, Diesel does and he doesn't get in trouble. Eric: Yeah, but that's not my style, Jack. Jack: As a matter of fact, our first match is The Weizal Vs. "Bam Bam" Rick Orton ("Thank god I'm a Country Boy" by Pauly Shore fills the arena as the Weizal enters the arena with Lisa Buddemeyer and Christina Ricci)
Jack: What's this? Eric: This just makes me sick. ("Bam Bam" Rick Orton walks to the ring) Rick Orton runs into the ring and nails the Weizal with a forearm to the back while he is handing his ring attire to an attendant.
Eric: Yeah, break him! Rick Orton locks on a sleeperhold and jumps over the top rope, snapping the Weizal neck on the top rope.
Jack: I've never seen that before. Orton climbs back into the ring, grabs the Weizal's hair and slams his head off of the mat! He then picks him up, whips him into the ropes and attempts his lariat. But, the
Weizal ducks! Then, the Weizal nails Orton with a spinning roundhouse kick. Orton falls to the mat. The Weizal slaps on the Murder Death Lock Grip. Orton submits after 3 minutes in the hold.
Eric: Dammit! Jack: What a surprising victory. He took that wicked bump during the Subnational Title tourney, plus he's never won a match before!
Eric: I will get my chance at the Weizal, if he doesn't try to WEIZAL out of it! (The IWINWF President, Shawn Tischler, comes to ringside.) Shawn: The next scheduled match is Vader X vs. Diesel for the Pico Title. Jack: Yeah. Shawn: I am declaring it to be a scaffold match. So go to an interview or something, while they constuct the scaffold.
(A crew comes to ringside with tools and large pieces of metal) Jack: Okay, I believe Bobo D. Hobo is back in The Weizal's dressing room. (Dressing room) Bobo: Obe-kabe, so I'm waiting for The Weizal and his entourage to come back from the ring.
(The Weizal enters with his managers) Weizal: Yes! I won! Bobo: Weizal, how does a victory feel? Weizal: It feels wonderful Bobo. You know why the call this the IWINWF? Because it's where I WIN!
Bobo: So, explain this situation with these two lovely ladies! Weizal: Well, Christina going to come with us to the ring for a few months until she starts filming her next movie.
Bobo: So, what about Eric Starr. Weizal: Well, I wanted to apoligise to Eric because I cost him the match during the
Subnational Title tournament. I am grateful that he did not interfere in my match tonight. But, I still want the fight him. The sooner the better.
Bobo: We'll probably see that match up soon. Weizal: Definitly. After I win the World Title, of course. Bobo: Maybe sooner than that, if we don't get more competitors. Weizal: Yeah, I would like to say to all wrestlers. Come to the IWINWF, we'll take all comers. You will all get a shot at the IWINWF title. Too bad none of you will win it though.
Bobo: Alright, back to you Jack. (Commentary table) Jack: Alright, thanks Bobo. It looks like the scaffold is ready. (Nine Inch Nails "Hurt" blasts the arena as Diesel comes to ringside and climbs up the 20-foot scaffold) Jack: Here are the rules for a scaffold match. The first wrestler to fall off the scaffold loses.
Eric: Ouch! I would want to lose that match. (Vader X comes to ringside with Mr. Nitro) Vader X climbs to the top of the scaffold and executes a belly-to-back suplex. He then splashes Diesel. He then attempts to elbowdrop Diesel, but misses. Diesel places a fist to Vader X's temple. Vader X is stunned. Diesel runs into Vader X with a shoulderbutt.
Vader X falls and one leg dangles off the scaffold. He scrambles to get completely on the scaffold. Diesel goes over to him, picks him up by the head. He shoves Vader X's head between his legs and Jacknifes him onto the scaffold surface. He then runs to the other end of the scaffold and runs back at Vader X and kicks him firmly in the face.
Vader X rolls off the scaffold and lands on the mat of the ring, 18-feet below. Jack: Diesel is now the Pico Champion! Eric: Vader X is gonna be out of commission for a while, I think. Jack: You never know, but if he comes back from this he'll be superhuman. ("Death Machine" Davis Freeman comes through the curtain) Jack: This is for the Subnational Title. Eric: Yeah, this guy is a freakin' psycho. He probably has a butcher's knife in his tights.
Jack: For real. (The Dragon enters the arena, the fans boo and cheer for him) Jack: The fans have mixed emotions about this Dragon. The Dragon storms into the ring and slaps an armbar onto an unsuspecting "Death Machine". Freeman then swings his leg backward, nailing the Dragon below the waist. The
Dragon lets go of Freeman, grabs "himself" and falls. Freeman then throws the Dragon out of the ring. Diesel runs to ringside with a wheelchair and breaks it over the Dragon's head. Diesel then throws the Dragon into the ring. Freeman then executes his "Death
Bomb" and pins the Dragon. 1,2,3.
Jack: I don't believe it! Eric: Two title changes in one night! Jack: "The Death Machine" is now the Subnational title. -- P.S. stands for PAULY SHORE!! (duh!) "The Weizal never dies, he just goes to Taco Bell" Please Visit http://homepage.third-wave.com/weiz/index.html The Weiz!@#!(aKa PICO?)
