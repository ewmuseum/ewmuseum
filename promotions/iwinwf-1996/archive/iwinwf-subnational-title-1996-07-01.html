IWINWF — Show Script

"(IWINWF) Subnational Title"

1 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WE ARE STILL LOOKING FOR TALENT eMail [detail withheld] for an application! (Insane Jack standing in front of the commentary table) Jack: Are you feeling bloody ready? (Crowd cheers) Jack: Welcome once again to the IWINWF arena here in beautiful Gatlinburg, Tennesee. Now, please welcome, my special guest commentator, DIESEL!! (Nine Inch Nails "Hurt" shakes the building as Diesel makes his way to ringside) Diesel and Jack shake hands. Jack: Welcome Diesel. I'm really glad you're here. Diesel: Why's that? Jack: Because, now I don't have to talk to Bobo all night. Diesel: Yeah, I could commentate better than that guy, with my tongue down Sunny's throat. Jack: Oh, man. They take their seats at the commentary table. Jack: So, Big Daddy, anything you'd like to say before the action starts. Diesel: If that no good mothertrucker Vader X gets anywhere near victory, I'll say "DOWN WIT' JO ASS!" Jack: Alright, tonight's action will be a 8-man tournament for the IWINWF Subnational Title. Our first match-up will be between "Electric Eric Starr and Crazy Lio. Diesel: Who the hell's Crazy Lio? Jack: Well, here he comes. (Crazy Lio comes to ringside) Jack: He's a pretty big dude. Diesel: Yeah, right. ("Electric" Eric Starr" makes his way to ringside) Diesel: What's the deal between this guy and the Weizal. Jack: I don't know. Maybe we'll find out tonight. And where's Union Kane, Starr's manager? Eric Starr kicks Crazy Lio in the gut and slaps on headlock. He, then, executes a running bulldog from corner to corner. He climbs to the top rope and goes for a moonsault.
Diesel: Stupid. Eric misses the moonsault. Lio performs a questionably low knee drop. He then picks Eric up by the hair and squeezes hard on his forehead. While Eric is lying on the mat, Crazy Lio goes to ringside and takes Diesel's monitor from the commentary table.
Diesel: Hey, asshole, gimme that. Crazy Lio steps through the ropes with the montitor and lifts it high, ready to nail him with it. Then, Diesel chucks Jack's monitor into the ring, and it breaks when it collides with Crazy Lio's head.
Jack: Great. Diesel: Nobody takes my TV! Crazy Lio is unconscious! Eric Starr goes for his finisher "The Electric Slide", and executes it beautifully. Eric Starr is about to go for the pin. Weizal: (from ringside) Hey, ERIC! Eric Starr is distracted. Weizal: Yeah, Starr, we've got something we got to do. Eric: What? Weizal: I've still got to flatten your ass. Crazy Lio is standing. Eric: Anytime, dirt bag! Crazy Lio rolls up Eric Starr for the pin. 1,2,3. Jack: I didn't expect that. Diesel: Me neither. Jack: Starr had that match! Eric Starr runs into out of the arena in pursuit of the Weizal. Jack: Well, Crazy Lio advances. Um, can we get a couple of new monitors here. Diesel: That was pretty decent, what's next? Jack: Bam Bam Rick Orton versus "The Death Machine" Davis Freeman Diesel: Well, who is Vader X gonna get whipped by. Jack: He's going againest Chad Shade. Diesel: Yeah, I think shade can handle that lardass. I want his belt, though. ("Bam Bam" Rick Orton" heads to the ring) Jack: There is a movie star here tonight. Diesel: Yeah, me! Jack: No, look over there. Isn't that Christina Ricci? Diesel: Hey, I'm a movie star too! Jack: Yeah, you played Shredder, right? That's too impressive. Diesel: Do I have to flatten you? Jack: No, just calm down. I'm gonna try to interview her after this match. Diesel: Let's just concentrate on this match. (Davis Freeman heads to the ring with, his manager, "the Fuse" W.L. Jamison) Diesel: He looks like my kind of guy. Jack: Why don't you ask him out? Diesel: Hey, I'm not Dustin. I'm just sayin' that he looks like the kind of wrestler I could tag up with.
Jack: Yeah, I could see that. Diesel: I predict this "Death Machine" to go all the way tonight! The bell rings and the brawler's lock up. Orton grabs Freeman by the ears and slams his knee into his face. Freeman's nose starts to bleed. Freeman then locks a blatant chokehold onto Orton. The ref then breaks the hold after a good ten seconds. Orton falls limp to the mat. Freeman picks up Orton and nails him with the Death Bomb! 1,2,3
Diesel: I know my stuff, Tink. Jack: He really ripped through that match. Diesel: Yeah. This is his day. Jack: Bloody good match. Diesel: Yeah, what's next? Jack: Um, wait, I'm gonna go over and interview Christina Ricci. Diesel: Whatever. (Jack standing by the ring barrier) Jack: Excuse me. Christina Ricci? Christina: That's me. Jack: I was kind of surprised to see you here. May I ask why you are here? Christina: Well, my very good friend, the Weizal, is wrestling tonight. Jack: Do you think he'll win the Subnational Title. Christina: No. Jack: Why not? Christina: Because he's waiting for the world title tournament. Jack: How do you know the Weizal? Christina: I'm Lisa's best friend. I'm also friends with, do I have to call him the Weizal?
Jack: We get your point? Well thanks for coming and enjoy the rest of the show. Christina: I will, thanks. (back at the commentary table) Diesel: Where the hell have you been? Jack: You know where I was! Diesel: Let's get back to the tournament, fast! (The Dragon enters with his manager Sean) Jack: This guy's bigger than you, BIG daddy cool! Diesel: What? No! I could still kick his ass. It's not height that gives me an advantage. Jack: What is it then? Diesel: Diesel Power. Jack: Yes, of course (The Weizal enters the arena, the fans pop) Diesel: I would like to kick this guy around the ring, too. Jack: Yeah. I don't like him much either. But the fans seem too. Diesel: I think they're probably cheering for Lisa. Jack: I know I would. The Weizal runs at The Dragon and delivering a flying clothesline. Dragon falls to the mat, but quickly leaps back up. The executes a roundhouse kick to the ribs. Dragon grabs his chest, and tumbles out of the ring. Sean helps him back into the ring. Then, he is hit with a fierce kneedrop. The Weizal climbs to the top rope. And leaps up six feet. The Dragon rolls east. The
Weizal lands hard and coughs up blood. The Dragon picks the Weizal and chokeslams him over the top rope and through a table. Sean then throws Weizal back into the ring. The Dragon places his foot across the sternum of The
Weizal. The ref counts 1,2,3. The Dragon walks to the locker room, as Lisa and Christina run to the aid of The Weizal.
Jack: Wow. Diesel: That guy just earned my respect. He took one hell of a bump. Jack: Yeah, the Weizal has not had the greatest luck here in the IWINWF. Diesel: What's next? Jack: That last match of the first round featuring Vader X and Chad Shade. Diesel: Vader X will not win, I'll see to it personally. Jack: Whatever. You've already interfered in one match. Diesel: So what. That's why this federation is so much better than WWF or WCW, I can do whatever the hell I want to. IWINWF is the best fed I've ever worked for. (Vader X enters the arena with Mr. Nitro) Diesel: I can't even stand the sight of this guy. Jack: He beat you once. Diesel: Yeah, but lightning never strikes twice. Jack: Speaking of that, here's Chad "Lightning" Shade Diesel: I thought he retired. Jack: Is contract isn't up yet. Diesel: Oh. Jack: If he doesn't win tonight, this will be his last match. Vader X attacks Shade and goes for the pin. Diesel: Here we go. Diesel leaves his commentary position and storms the ring. He quickly Jackknifes Vader X. The punches Shade and throws him on Vader X. The ref is arguing with [name withheld] about something, and doesn't notice. Then the ref turns and counts 1,2,3
Jack: Well, Chad Shade won even though he didn't really want to. Diesel: I told you I would not allow that dipshit to win. Jack: You are a man of your word. Vader X is helped out of the arena by Mr. Nitro. Diesel: My work in the ring is done for the night. Jack: I doubt that. (Crazy Lio walks through the curtain) Diesel: Uh oh, who's this guy facing? Jack: Davis Freeman. Diesel: Oh, good, the Death Machine will take care of him. ("Death Machine" Davis Freeman runs and dives into the ring) Freeman falls victim to a flurry of stomps from Lio. Freeman then grabs Lio's foot and knocks him down. Freeman grabs Lio's biceps and draws blood.
Jack: He had something in his hands Lio screams in pain and fear as blood rolls down his forearms. Freeman picks him up and nails him with the Death Bomb. The ref counts 1,2, Lio kicks out.
Diesel: I don't believe it. Freeman slaps Lio in the face, again drawing blood. He goes for the pin. 1,2,3. Diesel: That was a bit sick. Jack: He must really want that belt. Shawn Tischler walks to ringside and picks up a microphone. Jack: Ladies and gentlemen the IWINWF president, Shawn Tischler Shawn: Okay, get this. Chad Shade refuses to wrestle again. In fact, he has left the building. So, we're just gonna keep Davis Freeman out here and we're gonna bring out The Dragon. The winner will be crowned Subnational champion.
Diesel: Hey, that's not right. Shawn: I have no choice, Diesel. Diesel: Yeah, I guess you don't. That's alright, Freeman will still win, that last match was easy.
(The Dragon makes his way to ringside) The Dragon grasps Freeman's wrist and rips off the bladed gloves. Then, Freeman kicks him in the nutts. Diesel: Yeah, that's it. The Dragon is not effected by the low-blow. He nails Freeman with a knee to the intestines. The Dragon clasps onto Freeman's neck and elevates him. He, then, drives him into the mat with full force. He goes for the pin, 1,2,3!
Diesel: Oh, man. Jack: The Dragon is the Subnational Champion! -- P.S. stands for PAULY SHORE!! (duh!) "The Weizal never dies, he just goes to Taco Bell" Please Visit http://homepage.third-wave.com/weiz/index.html The Weiz!@#!(aKa PICO?)
