User Poets — 17 December 2011 As I understand it, you are looking for a third man at the commentary table who can give a little more of an objective dissection of the wrestling that's being presented. A commentary on the match itself, as opposed to having a clear favorite participant. My name is Brian McGinnis, I'm forty years old and a former wrestler myself. I trained in Terence Cooper's infamous gym in the Bronx, in New York City, alongside my sister Ivy and my brother in law Eli. I was talented, that is, I had good matches with everyone and anyone but I never had the drive to go all the way with it, plus I never really learned how to cut a memorable promo. With a family to support I really couldn't afford to go on the road, but I never stopped watching the shows or enjoying the athleticism. My kids are older now, and my entire family has been very supportive of my trying to get involved again, but the days when I could have wrestled a decent match are long past.
What I could bring to the promotion is that critical eye: my sister always used to tell me that her 'wrestling character' of the Psycho B'tch hated my family because they kept me off the road when I could've been any promotion's top road agent with how I can deconstruct and analyze any kind of match between any kind of wrestler. I hope to at least be given the opportunity to prove myself; if I could be given a tryout at Chain Reaction 4 and don't live up to the task, I will go home of my own free will.
Thank you for your attention, and I look forward to a response. Sincerely, Brian McGinnis
