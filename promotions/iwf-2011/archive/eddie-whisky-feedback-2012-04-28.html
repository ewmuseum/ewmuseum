Showtime — 28 April 2012 Okay, so this is a few months later since I diverged greatly from my usual characters' style or either cocky face or cowardly heel.
What works, what doesn't work and how can I better use Eddie Whisky? Showtime — 1 May 2012 I see. I leave you speechless. Right then. Hell_Fighter — 1 May 2012 Hey sorry I took so long but I was honestly thinking on this before answering but I will do my best to help in any way that I can.
I actually would love to see you use Eddie as kind of a start from scratch and play something that you have never played before or maybe you watched wrestling and saw a gimmick or persona that you always wanted to play but never really had the chance.
You are so used to playing cocky heels or comediac or cowardly characters (which you are really good at) have you ever thought about playing a straight serious character?
I almost envision Eddie Whisky as more of a cool, calm, cold, intense, methodical type of heel. Maybe have him as kind of a Christ Benoit or Bret Hart type of character. Or put your own unique spin to the whole Randy Orton apex predator persona. Someone who is willing to take his time and just systemactially take the time in the art of the beatdown and just break them down. No fear and utterly relentless in and out of the ring.
Maybe take the feud with Mayweather as a metamorphisis to the character and allow him to evolve and grow. Then just go out and be brutal with whomever he faces in the ring.
Man or woman, it doesn't matter he will hurt and maim and cripple you. I would just say take your time and enjoy the art of telling the story of Eddie Whisky, use what you have to help him to evolve into the type of character that you want him to be.
paint the picture and tell the story to us about Whisky. I just kind of see him as not your typical character that you would normally play and I think that you should expand on that.
I hope that helps you out and answers your quary about feedback. Anything else just give me a ring and we can discuss more.
Have a good one and good luck. Patrick BWade — 4 May 2012 Not knowing anything about Eddie Whiskey prior to IWF I have to agree with Patrick, something I probably never would have utter in the MWC days. (Don't think we really had heat, just wanted to bring up MWC. Who can resist? I can't. Word to Warren.)
If you've always played him as the over blown cocky heel (blown cocky?) and/or a cowardly comedic character then switch it up. Maybe you loose to MLW and this puts Eddie in a state of re-evaluation and he starts doubting himself or something like that I don't know.
I think your current direction is fine but it just seems like a one trick poney to an extent, I think you could take it else where, but just judging on what I've read it seems like a 3 episode arch as opposed to a whole new direction for a character. Although I could be wrong, as I have been before.
One of your role plays brought something to mind though... a "Hawkeye" Peirce type charecter would be awesome. His wit and sense of humor. And/or a Trapper like side kick...
Edit: Said Hunnicutt, meant Trapper. Being you RP referenced the book.
