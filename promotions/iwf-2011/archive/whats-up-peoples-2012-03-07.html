Stalker — 8 March 2012 Yeah those were some good days back then. Hell_Fighter — 8 March 2012 Hey Guys... My name is Patrick [name withheld], and I will be handling the new female lucha libra masked wrestler Go-Go Spectacular.
It has been a long time but low and behold have have returned to the very place where I pretty much began my efedding career. (Not IWF obviously but in general) Right here in FWCentral. It has been a long time coming. Like Patrick (Minstel).
Stalker, I think that I remember you from EPW whenever I handled my long time daftly used, the very first female character that I ever created, although when I was EPW I had her with a slight gimmick change but she was then "The Dark Phenom" Nakita Dahaha. But she started out years before that as "The Fem Phenom" Nakita DuBov in the NFW during it's early beginnings back in 2000-01.
But you can thank the other Patrick (Minstrel) for bringing me back. I am not quite prodical but I almost feel like it.
But I am back and handling a brand new character that is just starting out with a lot of story to tell. I am really excited to playing her and now I am excited to be back here to paint the picture and tell the story of Go-Go Spectacular.
I don't know why but of all of my characters, male and female, my most successful and credible and relevent characters have always been female. I have been my most creative and on target with them than any of my male characters.
Go-Go is symbolic for me because like how I got back into pro wrestling back in 1998 and watching lucha libra cruiserweight wrestling stopping me dead in my tracks with my jaw on the floor. That is what hooked me back into pro wrestling. Not the nWo or the attitude era.
I combined a fresh, strong female, that is young and with lots of story to tell who just so happens to be a masked lucha libra wrestler.
I am really excited to be playing this new character and telling her story. Not focused on gaining championships and accolades. Wins and losses are part of the game. But for me, doing my part to make this fed the best that it can be on FWCentral and on the net is what matters to me.
So I hope that you all will see that I am someone that you can bounce ideas off of and how we will both get out what we put like iron sharpening iron.
