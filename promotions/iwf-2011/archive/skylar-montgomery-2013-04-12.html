SkyMont — 12 April 2013 Handler's Name: Nik Character's In Ring Name: Skylar Montgomery Character's Real Name: Skylar Montgomery Height: 6'1 Weight: 198lbs Hometown/Billed From: London, England Moveset: Number Cruncher (SkyMont jumps onto the back of a doubled-over opponent then drops them with a BIG Fameasser)
Angel Dust Press (Inverted Shooting Star Press) 450 Splash Senton Bomb Front Flip Legdrop (off the top rope or standing) 360 Spinning Legdrop (ala RVD) Basment Dropkick (to knees or face) Top Rope Corner 2 Corner Basement Dropkick to Face Suicide Dive to ringside Springboard Crossbody Block Rope-Walk Legdrop to the arm Eye Poke Sit-Out Jawbreaker Running Yakuza Kick Running Cannonball Attack into turnbuckles Finishing Maneuver: Crackskunk (SkyMont grabs his opponent like he's about to deliver a snapmere or stunner then drops to one knee and spikes the opponent's skull onto his extended knee)
Appearance/Attire: SkyMont is extremely pale, lanky and thin with long ginger hair. He wears a black vest with "SKYMONT" written across it in white neon lights style writing. He wears blue skinnyjeans that are torn and slightly blood-spattered with red, black and white basketball trainers on his feet.
Entrance: "Sorry You're Not A Winner" by Enter Shikari plays as the lights turn off. When they turn back on again Skylar Montgomery is stood there in his mask flipping us all off. The lights go out again. And when they turn back on this time, Skylar Montgomery is stood in the ring in his classic crucifix pose.
Background/Bio: Skylar Montgomery is a trouble-maker, no doubt about it. He's the locker-room clown, a diva to all the bookers and is half-assed in the ring.
Some people think that all his backtalk and rebel angst is just a metaphorical mask for a more sensitive side that is a never allowed to rise from beneath the actual mask he wears on his face.
Some people think that below that demonic exterior is simply a scared little boy who's heavily guarded private life is just a guise for the fact that he has no one.
Some people think that's why he throws himself around the ring and off ladders and chairs and announce tables and security barriers. Because he hates himself.
Some people think all Skylar Montgomery needs is a little love. Then again, most people just think he's a spineless scumbag with no soul who'll do anything to win matches.
Who knows which people are right.
