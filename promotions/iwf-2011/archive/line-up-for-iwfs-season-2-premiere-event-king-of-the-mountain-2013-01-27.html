LINE-UP for IWF's Season 2 Premiere Event - "King of the Mountain"

27 January 2013
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RStrawsma — 27 January 2013 The Immortal Wrestling Federation Presents... KING OF THE MOUNTAIN Coming to you LIVE from the Tacoma Dome in Tacoma, Washington FEATURED MATCHES The Battle For Seattle "THE PACIFIC BLITZKRIEG" KERRY KUROYAMA vs. "SUB POP" SCOTT DOUGLAS Emerald City Title Contest PERFECTION (C) vs LEYENDA DE OCHO MAIN EVENT Mount Rainier Title Contest King of the Mountain Battle Royal JOHNNY NILES vs. PAIN GRILLE vs. STEVE "AXION" JACKSON vs. "THE FALLEN ANGEL" STEPHEN WALTZ vs. EL CABRON Promo deadline will fall on February 9th, 2013... around midnightish. View Apex: Episode One for a preview to the event and descriptions of the matches. Main event will remain open to any who wish to hop in. Just post an RP in reply to the thread.
1/29 UPDATE: Steve "Axion" Jackson has put his name into the King of the Mountain match! fugginVOSS — 31 January 2013 So.... what's the go with RP limits? I remember seeing something once upon a time here where it's 2.5 RPs? How does that work again? And how does it work for the Battle Royal?

Post as many RPs as you feel necessary, with as many words as you want to throw in them.

Bigger question, at this point: Do you guys feel we should keep the deadline at 2/9, or should I bring it a little closer to the present? Seems like there's a good number of RPs up and I'd hate to drag things out. Honestly wasn't expecting this quick of a turn-out.

And yeah... any segments or angling in the works, send it my way. Heard some awesome ideas from a few people so far.
jacksonattack — 1 February 2013 I'm cool with moving up the deadline a little. Maybe to Monday? John Doe — 2 February 2013 I have a segment or two I will get in during the weekend. If possible I would like to write the my match except for the ending.

Rivera, if you want to write the main sans the finish, then by all means. I could handle everything else.
brusch — 5 February 2013 On the cusp of having a couple segments ready for the show. You'll probably get them later today, if not then 100% before the RP deadline.
