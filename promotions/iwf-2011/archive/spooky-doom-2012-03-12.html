SpookyDoom — 22 September 2012 As good a time as any to update the following! Guess I can't edit- using bio form I submitted to the ULTRATITLE tournament...
Character real's name: Casper, last name ungiven Bio: Spooky Doom considers himself some manner of lil' Grim Reaper-like thingy, the nephew of a certain six foot ten supernatural zombie/redneck/MMA enthusiast who shall remain nameless here. Growing up in awe of his otherworldly uncle, Spooky wanted nothing more than to join him on the pro-wrestling scene; however there are no free rides in this business and Spooky Doom had to prove himself in battle before being accepted by the much larger company. Thus did the Deadkid end up using his uncle's very famous name to open doors in wrestling schools worldwide, eventually moving to Mexico to ply his trade as his smaller size and propensity to hit high flying maneuvers was better appreciated there.
Since then, Spooky Doom's uncle died. Again. In a manner less embarrassing than the first four times he died, certainly, but he's dead nonetheless and SOMEBODY needs to take his place. Making up for his inability to teleport in darkness or shoot lightning bolts from his fingertips by putting on amazing matches filled with tons of high spots, Spooky Doom joins the wrestling business with the same mandate to bury the souls of evil wrestlers that his uncle originally had.
Spooky Doom's apocryphal origin in RP form Costume: White skin-tight sleeveless hoodie with black Pacman ghost at the center and lime green shoulder openings, similar in design to Cody Rhodes old hoodie from his masked days (Spooky Doom's outfit is closed at the front and worn while wrestling). Matching white pleather pants with green flame design. Mask is horseshoe shaped, black with lime green filigree. Uses white wrist tape and flexible black slip-on sneakers to aid in his high-flying. He also sports large white bicep bands, each marked by a black Pacman ghost.
Moveset: (Finisher) The Doom Reaper (satellite headscissors Diamond Cutter) is the finisher, but is near-always followed by the 450 splash (Wheel of Death). Think about it as a two part finisher much like Rey Mysterio's 619: the 619 doesn't get the pin, that's the diving splash. However, the 619 is credited as Mysterio's finish.
(Signatures) Eeriecanrana: rope walk leaping to top rope huracanrana Spooker Kick, Death Blow:superkick, sliding clothesline to kneeling opponent La Casita embrujada: Signature magistral cradle. Espectro Special: triangle jump corkscrew suicida, Spooky's signature dive Spooky Sit-up: Just like his uncle does!
