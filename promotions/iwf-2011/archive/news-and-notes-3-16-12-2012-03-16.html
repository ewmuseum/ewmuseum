IWF (2011) — News

News and Notes 3/16/12

16 March 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stalker — 16 March 2012 Firstly check out the IWF Rule book (http://www.fwrestling.com/showthread.php?21924-IWF-s-Rule-Book) to check out a new updated policy concerning long-forming vs. short-forming of matches.
On that note this is another reminder of the roleplay deadline which is this Sunday. If you would like to volunteer to write a match in longform or shortform fashion, please contact me via AIM or PM. If you do volunteer, and this is a must, please please please familiarize yourself with how the commentary team interacts with each other and that sort of stuff.
We are kicking at a nice pace right now with an influx of a handful of handlers and the possibility of even more arriving. So let's get this 'High Intensity' West Coast tour started out with a bang and get some good angles, segments and role plays written.
I already have four submitted segments, which is FREAKING AWESOME. Thank you guys so much, if you are thinking about submitting a segment please get them to me asap.
Reply here if there are any questions/comments. Showtime — 16 March 2012 Are any plans in the works for introducing another title, namely a tag title? Too many efeds are moving away from tag teams. It is saddening. Stalker — 16 March 2012 To be honest I envision IWF as a fed that will never have a tag title. Can my opinion be changed? Of course but at this point I do not see a tag title anywhere in the future of IWF. As for other titles... of course there are plans :). But the conception of another IWF related title may be a while down the road.
RStrawsma — 16 March 2012 If I may opine... rather than establish the standard tier system (ie, World, Intercontinental, TV, et al.), why not experiment with more gimmick-based championships? A hardcore title is the obvious addition, but you could get creative with it too. Why not a belt that emphasizes technical wrestling, or a battle royal championships?
Showtime — 17 March 2012 Or have an eventually annual tournament for a prestige title, like the Super J of Crockett Cup.
Stalker — 17 March 2012 Loving the feedback. Keep in mind that I read everything... so yeah i'm thinking about those ideas and combining them with my current ideas. Can't give anything away just yet but I got some good ideas cooking on new titles.
