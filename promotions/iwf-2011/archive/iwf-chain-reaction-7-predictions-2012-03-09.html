RStrawsma — 24 March 2012 Thank you, everyone, for reminding me what a bad idea it was to drag an old character into a federation where he has no business being in.
SETI VS. DAYMON: Guess the popular opinion already makes this obvious... PERFECTION VS. DOUGLAS: Douglas is a solid character, but more of a story-teller whereas Perfection is an outright contender. And yeah, Perfection has a win over a four-time former World Heavyweight Champion, so I give him the rub.
Before I get to the rest of my predictions, I'll admit that I haven't read the promos. I'm answering this survey as I would a random fan on the street just knowing what the line-up is, and a lot of these answers are based solely on familiarity.
MATEO VS. ALLEN: Mateo wins, building momentum toward the PPV event in his hometown of Las Vegas.
NILES VS. GO GO VS. MAYWEATHER: I can see this going toward Mayweather, after a quick, frenzied three-way match with a lot of chaos thrown into the mix.

> > SETI VS. DAYMON: Guess the popular opinion already makes this obvious... [end quote] Only speaking for myself; I had to go with Seti strictly based off the fact it seemed Rocko was on the fast track out of here after his first appearance, of which I thought was a one-off to start with.
I only take the time to say this because I feel he has a place here, even if its only as Kerry's manager/trainer who competes from time to time when story/hype/handler dictates.
I think his some-what convoluted first appearance is rebound-able ... but your lack of 'interwebz' surely hurt what could have been a big entrance.
Honestly I feel Rocko's, Kerry's, Scott's and now, Derrick's Seattle roots are what will eventually make this promotion great. Granted at some point, IWF has to live up to the I ... but as long it never forgets its Seattle base, those four will make for great home-based stories... even if those stories aren't the main events.
Which reminds me of something else, lol ... *soap box *****es* I'd like to make a comment about; someone being a story teller verse an "outright contender" ... And I don't mean this as a personal shot at you Strawsma, just seems like its the old guard of FW's way of thinking and personally I find it incredibly terrible. Although, I once subscribed whole heartily.
Two things I hate, promo only handlers and handlers that half ass a story for a RP session, then abandon it.
I'm not saying everything has to be a story, but for **** sake ... how many times can you say the same **** to different people?
End of the day do what you do you, I suppose. I'm only speaking on personal preference, but to me if we aren't journeying into these peoples lives, feelings, thoughts, etc ... then why are we doing this? To hold pretend titles under pretend names and gloat about our pretend victories? That was cool when I was 15, but those days have long swept by me and personally I need more.
Promo's have their place, and need to be written within this "game" but shouldn't it be more?
