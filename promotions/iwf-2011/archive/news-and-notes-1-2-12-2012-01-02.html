Stalker — 2 January 2012 What's up everyone? Happy New Year's firstly. Now for the news, card work is under way my goal is to get it up by this weekend. If you have anything you'd like specific to do on the card please send it my way asap.
I'd like to thank all of the new sign ups for coming aboard. Hope you guys enjoy your time here.
Chain Reaction 4 will be our last show before our first pay per view... which is yet to be named. (Taking ideas on that btw :).)
Anyways other then that just want to send shout out and thanks to the roleplays we have been getting. You guys are making this worthwhile and I hope as the year 2012 continues, IWF will continue to thrive and grow strong.
So yeah figured i'd update you guys as to what's going on and hope you enjoy the card when it gets posted.
