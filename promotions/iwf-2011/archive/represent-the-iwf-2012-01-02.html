Showtime — 2 January 2012 Hi everyone, this isn't intended as either spam or headhunting. It is just an effort to get IWF represented at an annual multi-fed event.
http://apwprez.proboards.com/index.cgi?board=surviveandconquer2012&action=display&thread=8717 (http://apwprez.proboards.com/index.cgi?board=surviveandconquer2012&action=display&thread=8717)
The above is a link to a sign-up thread to an inter-fed tournament I am participating in. They are accepting free agents for the event. All the information is there if you are interested in this one-off RPing event.
Here is their spiel: ACTION PACKED WRESTLING brings to you, One of the biggest Inter-fed match of all time. Join us in our 4 year anniversary show, Represent your fed and make your fed proud. If you think your better than everyone else, then this is the match to prove it!
This marks the 6th SURVIVE AND CONQUER match in the history of e-wrestling. The first one took place back in 2001. And in the last two years, it has been made into an inter-fed event.
What is the Survive and Conquer match? The Survive and Conquer Match is a match that begins with a Royal Rumble format. Every 1 minute a new wrestler is added to the match and you are eliminated by being thrown over the top rope with both feet touching the ground. Once a Final 4 is declared, a Steel Cage comes down and the final 4 compete in a steel cage match. The first 3 to escape the cage, will advance in the match and will then battle in a Street Fight. The person who gets pinned, it eliminated leaving the final two to fight in a Hell in a Cell Ladder match. The Object is to break through the cell, climb to the top where a ladder will be waiting, climb the ladder and grab the prize.
In the past 2 years, 80 wrestlers have took part in this match and over 30 federations were represented. Past winners include, Level One in 2009, Victor Hades in 2010, and Ryan Ruckus in 2011. The winner will get a cash prize of $500,000*, a Survive and Conquer trophy and bragging right.
*This is kayfabe cash. User Poets — 2 January 2012 Just a quick note about the rules - it looks like a fun event and all that jazz, but 1 RP 4000 word limit is usually a recipe for disaster. You'll either end up with a creative writing exercise that has nothing to do with wrestling like PTC was famous for, or you get a whole lot of mediocre filler.
Harder on you to judge but a more wrestling - centric way to do it might be a 4 RP limit, 1000 words each? Gives a chance to build heat and actual back and forth with the participants.
