Chain Reaction 8 - Pre-RP Predictions

3 April 2012

The Minstrel — 3 April 2012 IWF Presents Chain Reaction Main Event: Emerald City Championship Vizier ta Seti (c) vs. Erik Mateo I'm taking Mateo here even though it takes three times longer to read his roleplays than anyone else's - it's always good stuff once I can figure it - got a feeling the title is changing hands
Undercard: Derrick Allen vs. Mr. Wrestling VIII Allen although Mr. Wrestling VIII is the best name ever Eddie Patton vs. Nasr Patton keeps rolling after his big win last week Johnny Niles vs. SpookyDoom SpookyDoom takes the W The Minstrel vs. Go Go Spectacular I predict the Minstrel will actually wrestle this time... Maybe... Kerry Kuroyama vs. 'The Fallen Angel' Stephen Waltz Kuroyama wins his debut RStrawsma — 7 April 2012 [quoting] The Minstrel said: > Kerry Kuroyama vs. 'The Fallen Angel' Stephen Waltz > > Kuroyama wins his debut [end quote] Actually, Kerry won his debut at Chain Reaction #1. This is his return match. KUROYAMA VS. WALTZ Only I know... but if I were looking at it from the perspective of a fan or a wrestling analysis, I'd say Kuroyama just given Waltz's disappointing record.
MINSTREL VS. SPECTACULAR Provided Minstrel doesn't get himself in trouble before the match, I see him walking away with a narrow victory, but Go Go will get some payback.
NILES VS. SPOOKYDOOM Looking for a big splash from SpookyDoom, so I give him the win prediction, but I wouldn't be surprised if Niles found a way spoil the debut in some way.
PATTON VS. NASR Patton's got momentum, whereas Al Saud have been dormant for a couple cards. I'd expect "Electric" Eddie to come up with another big win.
ALLEN VS. MR. WRESTLING VIII I think Derrick Allen's rise in IWF is only just beginning, so I see him getting a win. MAIN EVENT: SETI VS. MATEO Probably a draw or inconclusive finish to keep the rivalry continuing. Seti will retain the title, but it will be the first stumble in his reign as champion as he fails to put Mateo away for good. Mateo likewise doesn't prove he's any greater, but shows he can hang on the level of the Minister of the Frontier, and perhaps match his intensity in the ring.
