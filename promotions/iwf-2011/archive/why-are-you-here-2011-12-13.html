User Poets — 13 December 2011 So we're three cards in and RP has been somewhat lacking... I know things take time to get started but maybe some discussion would help?
Initial impressions of the IWF and the characters created? Anyone you're looking forward to working with? IF you've created a new character, what was your inspiration?
John Doe — 13 December 2011 Personally I really enjoy the cards, they are solid, well put together and the commentary is really good. As far as my character creation, I literally based his entire gimmick off the song Perfect Gentleman by Helloween
TSiegel — 14 December 2011 I don't have any qualms about the writing....Justin's got the character writing exactly precise to what I like.
The only problem I've had so far is that I've been bogged down with school so much that I haven't been able to keep up with the competition.
But as of today...I'm on break. For about a month. :) ****s on. :) RStrawsma — 14 December 2011 I helped Justin manage IWF about ten years ago, when it was "Insanity" instead of "International". I handled Vice President Nathan Fear and wrote a bunch of matches with guys doing unrealistically violent things to each other (but hey, give me a break... EVERYTHING was about how hardcore you could be back in 2001). Seemed natural to come back.
Stephen Waltz has long been a semi-retired character of mine. He was active in the early days of EPW, and GWE (formerly GXW) before that. His creation was meant to replace an aging Rocko Daymon, but I couldn't let him retire for some reason. Now that Waltz is back with an explanation where he's been, I simply offered him to Justin and Stanton to pad the roster and give the characters a guaranteed win. A jobber, basically... but with Terry Anderson as his pal and trainer, I figured it would make a comical side-story.
Kerry Kuroyama's creation was a matter of perfect timing. I was running an angle in A1E where Rocko's career seemed to be winding down due to injuries and he was taking an interest in opening a gym and wrestling school in his hometown. Then IWF came back and put Seattle as its territory, giving me a reason to put one of Rocko's students in the ring. At this point, I'm not entirely satisfied with the character, as he just seems full of naive optimism and a bunch of handed-down words of wisdom from old-timers, but I'm hoping he'll develop as things keep moving.
I'm pretty excited with the way the roster is shaping up, and I'm happy to see a fed that differentiates itself from the big two major leagues of EPW and NFW. I feel there's a lot of potential for evolving over time.
BWade — 15 December 2011 I have some history on these boards and in MWC/EWI, none of it all that great to speak of but I tried to come back for the CSWA Gold Rush, did decently... but then we all know how that ended. So I layed out again.
Wanted to come back for some reason, applied at NFW with Kevin "K-9" Watson and when I saw IWF was new figured why not... used an old underused character of mines name and repackaged him.
So far I'm enjoying it... back when, I always wanted someone to do a lower level/territory type fed, thought it would be cool.. I even tried but it never took off. Most didn't like the underplayed event size and ECW type feel.
Anyway, glad to be back... not sure how long it will last but so far I like what I see. It's been fun. And an escape from real life...which is always welcome.
User Poets — 17 December 2011 To answer my own question, I'm here because I like the idea of helping build something from the ground up, and much like the original NFW, I like the idea of having to use a new character (or a non-established one as the case may be). I've always been good or at least fair at writing badass types as illustrated with Eli Flair and Poison Ivy, and when I created Impulse I wanted the exact opposite of the big tough badass; I got the small sarcastic hero. Vizier was just a whim, really - listening to a friend's band and really enjoying the tribal beats and thought 'This could be a fun gimmick.'
Plus it's a lot of fun to be able to curse in promos again. :) TSiegel — 20 December 2011 Plus it's a lot of fun to be able to curse in promos again. :) Aww...he misses it. :) Showtime — 30 December 2011 I just stumbled in, I have handled for over 12 years off-and-on, and my old fed (the WWA) has recently gone belly-up.
