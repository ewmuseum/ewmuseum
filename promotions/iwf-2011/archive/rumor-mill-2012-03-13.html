IWF (2011) — News

Rumor Mill

13 March 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stalker — 13 March 2012 The Seattle Times is reporting that International Wrestling Federation's CEO Kimball Cho is currently in talks to acquiring the rights for Las Vegas Wrestling. No more details have been announced.
Stalker — 8 May 2012 The Seattle Times is reporting that International Wrestling Federation is in the process of forming secondary title. According to sources inside the front office the name of the belt is 'Sin City Championship'.
Supposedly this is going to have something to do with the end of the west coast High Intensity Tour, which concludes in Las Vegas Nevada.
BWade — 11 August 2012 The Seattle Times is reporting that the International Wrestling Federation's High Intensity Tour may not occur as previously planned and reported. More details to be released on Chain Reaction 9, and following reports.
