Major apologies for how long you all had to wait on this one... I sorely underestimated my ability to write out an entire card by myself. Definitely going to work on improving that. If anybody has criticism or feedback, in the meantime, you can lay it down here.

The next step is the same as last one - getting some new blood in the fed! Hopefully some strong RP threads will entice new faces to join in the action.

Johnny Niles — 21 May 2013 Oh, yeah... Just checked the "Road to Immortality" thread again, thought Fear was saying Surge II would be where the Triple Threat would take place... I should pay attention to these things. Speaking of Surge, apparently there's gonna be a third title for the IWF:
204 - Surge II Location: Spokane, WA Venue: Spokane Veterans Memorial Arena Notes: First of two supershows on the tour, both titles will be defended, and the third title will be announced.
Wonder what it'll be, maybe tag titles? brusch — 21 May 2013 It looks like a third singles title. The end of the arc looks to be a unification bout between the holders of all three championship, which will become IWF's premier title (perhaps the Washington State title).
208 - Emerald City Experience 2013 Location: Seattle, WA Venue: Key Arena Notes: Second of two supershows, which will either get a primetime spot on ESEN or perhaps PPV, depending on interest. All three reigning champions will compete in a triple-threat main event to unify into one title that represents all of the state of Washington.
RStrawsma — 21 May 2013 Awesome... thank you all for the feedback. Half expected y'all had given up on me at this point. But I'm happy to see an interest in forging ahead.
Adding to our pool of handlers is definitely a priority at this point. I'll do what I can to improve that on my end... and one place I'm going to start is getting the shows out in a more timely fashion. Two ways I'm going to implement that plan is by 1.) Asking for volunteer assistance, and 2.) Doing less volunteering myself in other federations.
You guys have already deduced plans from here on out, but I'll confirm it here... yes, next show (to be titled Tri-Cities Triple Threat) is going to include the non-title three-way for the main event, as well as Douglas vs. Niles to determine the No. 1 Contender to the Emerald City Title. This is the last show before the super-event Surge II. Whoever doesn't come out of TCTT the no. 1 contender will probably be given future consideration for the end-of-season PPV, Emerald City Experience '13.
I had a plan in mind for the third title, but with the roster being as small as it is, I may reconsider bringing that into the mix. It would seem out of place having three belts among five or six handlers. Of course, if we could get more handlers in over the next few shows, that wouldn't be as big of a deal.
Idea for next show... should we just have an open RP thread? That way, anybody outside the fed could hop in at any point should they so choose. Just a thought, but I'm also open to more brainstorming.
