CALLOUT: IWF S02E02 - Road to Immortality

20 February 2013
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RStrawsma — 20 February 2013 If you haven't read King of the Mountain yet, be sure to do so. The next show will be held at the Yakima Sundome in Yakima, Washington.
The Mount Rainier Champion will defend his title on this show. The Emerald City Champion has the choice of defending his title or waiting.
Reply to this thread if you want to participate. Please let me know if you're in for a match and/or segments. Any requests or direct challenges can be made here as well.
As always, this show is open to new handlers or one-time contract appearances. Would probably like to get one or two more handlers involved for this one.
fugginVOSS — 20 February 2013 I'm in brother! If I'm defending the belt I'm clearly in. I'd also like to book the FFL in for a celebration ceremony for your Mount Ranier Champion. :-D
brusch — 20 February 2013 Ocho is in and probably has a thing or two to say to Perfection. Johnny Niles — 20 February 2013 Best in the World is in. I must admit this season's gotten off to a great start. I hope the next shows are just as good if not better.
BWade — 20 February 2013 Scott is in. RStrawsma — 23 February 2013 Groovy so far. I'd still probably like to get maybe two or three handlers in on this to pad the card out a little. The hope is to have a line-up by Monday.
John Doe — 23 February 2013 Perfection is able to be booked, mind you, all cards are subject to change. RStrawsma — 28 February 2013 Sorry I've been slackin. Card will be posted tonight once I've written the next episode of Apex. In the meantime, if you've got something you'd like to add for the hype show (like a direct challenge or a victory speech) send it to me through PM sometime today.
