A forum-hosted federation on the FW Central hub that crowned an Emerald City Champion at its debut pay-per-view, weathered an operator handover and a season-long hiatus, and relaunched with a Mount Rainier Championship before its own record goes quiet. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Immortal Wrestling Federation's own mission statement, posted 27 October 2011, calls it “a hybrid fed” built around both roleplay and staged angles, run under commissioner Art Mori.

Its debut broadcast, Chain Reaction 1, on 18 November 2011, put four wrestlers — Scott Douglas, Kerry Kuroyama, Perfection and Mary-Lynn Mayweather — into a tournament for a new championship, the Emerald City title. A fan-vote $10,000 prize for the night's own best performer went to Mayweather, only for Perfection to snatch the briefcase back and assault her with it as the show closed.

The Emerald City Championship's first reign went to Vizier ta Seti, who won a triple threat over Perfection and “Sub Pop” Scott Douglas on 7 January 2012; Perfection took the title from him in a cage match at the promotion's own first supershow, Surge!, 22 July 2012.

That September a new commissioner, Nathan Fear, ousted Art Mori on his first night in charge and immediately unveiled a second championship for a promised tour across Washington State; by the promotion's own Season 2 premiere the following February, that title had become the Mount Rainier Championship.

Season 2 never completed a full run. The operator's own January 2013 explanation admits a main event built around Scott Douglas was left unfinished amid real-life work and touring commitments, and that restarting the promotion again could not responsibly be promised. A callout for a further Season 2 episode that May went unanswered, and no later document survives.
