Sunday Night Rampage Prevue

XXXXXXXX X X XXXXXXXX X X X X X X X X XXXXXXX X X X X X X X X X X X X XXXXX X X X Stewart Wrestling Federation "Where Legends Are Made" Presents: Sunday Night Rampage LIVE! January 31st 1999, From The Rupp Arena! Brandon Stewart will be LIVE in the house to comment on what exactly happened at "Return To The Ring." Things have been a bit sketchy after Cooper was screwed out of the SWF World Title when Stewart "accidentaly" hit him with the very belt that he should be holding.
Eric Cooper is rumored to be on hand on January 31st. He stormed out of the building after "Return to The Ring" only muttering the words "Stewart's mine.."
Viper will be on hand LIVE with what we may have learned to be his BROTHER! That's right, the man who cost Cobra the match, is possibly Viper's own BROTHER! In the ring.... Hardcore Championship Match "Hardcore Legend" Rex Watson versus Mystical Lord! Television Championship Match "Vicious" Vinny Vaughan versus Cobra! The debut match for VICE Vice versus Peter Thunderbolt _STEVIE COOL_ joins the SWF.. Stevie Cool will be on hand LIVE with a MAJOR annoucement! World Title Defense! Apparently Brandon Stewart has set up The RingMaster's first opponent, but the annoucment of who that opponent will be won't be untill the start of that night!
And of course MUCH more to come along with MANY more surprises! *Card subject to change* *Will be posted on RSPWF Sunday Night*
