Part One: Introduction and history of SWF The SWF is a great E-Fed that has been on AOL for about two and a half years. I did just take a brief hiatus but now I am ready to return with many of the same members, but like any other good federation I am looking to expand. I want to bring the SWF's good name back to AOL while also spreading it world wide on the internet. I have already made the first card up and it is almost done. Also a great web page designer has been signed on to do the web page that will be out any day now. I believe that roleplaying is the key to this sport and I explain that later, but to be apart of the SWF, you really should be experienced in roleplaying, but if not, we'll help ya out!
Brandon Stewart Part Two: Application (not too long) Wrestlers Name Height Weight Apperance Home Town Ring Entrance Music Top 5 Moves Set up for Finisher Finisher (describe unless not needed) Roleplaying ability (1-10) Any other information... Mail completed application too.. [detail withheld] Part Three: Roleplaying (Very important) Obviously roleplaying plays a huge factor in some federations and then very little in others. Well the SWF is like the majority of leagues out there.
Roleplaying is key! The major part is that it is not quanity that counts, it's the quality. To win matches in the SWF it takes good quality roleplays, otherwise you will not be successful here. I have been a great roleplayer for
2 years now and I have found that it helps you a long the way in every federation you get in. I am looking for experienced roleplayers right now, I have a good amount of them, but as you all know, you can always use more!
When your writting your roleplays there are not many restrictions but I do have a few do's and don'ts. If you do use vulgar language don't go on a rampage,
PLEASE avoid any racial slurs or anything like that, don't wanna get involvoed with that stuff. Basicially be creative and you are going to win matches!
Part Four: Cards and PPV's Right now the SWF has it's first card set for January 17th, 1999. After that cards will be on Sundays still and it will be called "Sunday Night Rampage" It will be the only card during the week so it will be written very, very well.
Each month will have a PPV also. All the angles and matches will take place on the Sunday Night show's and there will be a special show before each PPV. Any questions.. Just ask!
Part Five: Titles The titles that the SWF currently has are: World Title US Title TV Title Hardcore Title Light Heavyweight Title (Possible, if division grows) All are up for grabs on the first card! Part Six: Closing Remarks I want the SWF to be fun but yet competitive so I am looking for very serious members who are going to give it a lot of dedication to be successful in the
SWF. I hope that you decide to join when you read this. THank you very much!
