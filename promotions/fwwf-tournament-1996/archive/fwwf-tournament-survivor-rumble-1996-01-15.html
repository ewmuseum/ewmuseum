FWWF (Fantasy World Wrestling Federation) — Roster

"Fantasy WWF Survior Rumble"

15 January 1996 (for a tournament dated 21 January 1996)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's the deal, Sunday January 21, 1996 I will run a tournament where draw two names at a time out of a bowl, those two wrestlers will have to face each other untill one of them have been eliminated by pinfall or DQ, the winner of the match gets tossed back into the bowl for future play, the last man left after going through all the names is the Fantasy WWF Champion, the last man before him will become the Fantasy Intercontinental Champion.
Also the last man that's in the ring from the following tag teams will become the tag champs with his partner:Smoking Gunns<Billy
& Bart Gunn> The Body Donnas<Skip & Zip> Sid & The 123 Kid, Men on a Mission<Mabel & Mo> and Avatar and Aldo Mantoya.
The participents in the tournament as of Monday January 15, 1996 are:Shawn Michaels, Diesel, Bret Hart, Ahmed Johnson, The
Undertaker, Razor Ramon, Hakushi, Marty Jannety, Savio Vega,
Billy Gunn, Bart Gunn, Barry Horowitz, Avatar, Rad Radford, Bob
Holly, Fatu, Henry Godwin, Duke Droese, Aldo Mantoya, Britsh
Bulldog, Yokozuna, Ringmaster, Owen Hart, Hunter Hearst-Helmsley,
Goldust, 123 Kid, Jeff Jarrett, Sid, Jerry Lawler, Skip, Zip,
Zanta Klaus, Bob Backlund, Isaac Yankem, Mabel, Mo, Vader, Jake
Roberts, Tatanka, Bam Bam Bigelow. Expect 4 or 5 more people to be annouced by tomorrow night.
-- President of FWWF. Fantasy World Wrestling Federation
