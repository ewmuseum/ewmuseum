BZWF Thursday Rampage

29 January 2000

<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> THURSDAY RAMPAGE From the Chicago Battledome 27th January 2000 Featuring... Jerry 'Goon' Gordon Vs. 'Latin Lover' Chris Del Rio Anubis Vs.'Suicide' Steven Fry Rob Diablo Vs. Jagged Jeff Jackson 'Deadly Game' Dave Daniels Vs. Excalibur Sean Stacy Vs. Copperhead MAIN EVENT Chase Williams & Steve Swanson Vs. Matt Gray & Barbed Wire Brian <><><><><><><><><><><> [ At he start of the broadcast we saw the locker room area and saw Deadly Game Dave Daniels and Jeff Jackson attack and hit Swanson and Williams with chairs and trash cans and they left and Steve Swanson and
Chase Williams were lying on the floor unconscious and then we cut back]
JERRY 'GOON' GORDON Vs. 'LATIN LOVER' CHRIS DEL RIO [ Both men came to the ring with fans jeering them and they responded. Once the fight got underway both guys used stunning aerial moves like Monnsaults and cross body blocks. Gordon started to get the upperhand and used his speed and his knowledge of the ring and he used hip tosses, piledrivers, powerslams and flying lariats . Then out of desperation
Delrio went to the outside and The Goon followed him and he hit him with the ring bell. Delrio tried to reverse a whip into the steel steps but was given The End DDT and it was too late as the ref got to the ten count]
Decision: DOUBLE COUNT OUT! ANUBIS Vs. 'SUICIDE' STEVEN FRY [ Steven Fry came to the ring with no ovation as no-one knew him. Anubis came to the ring with Insomnia who was watching his back to a massive amount of jeers from the crowd. The fight got underway and Anubis showed us his array of skills as did Fry in what was an even start to the fight.. shoulderbreakers, facebusters and choke slams by Anubis and Fry hit back with chops to the chest , backbreakers , swinging neckbreakers and a DDT. Both men had near pin falls but it just seemed like this one was coming to a draw... Insomnia had seen enough and left for the locker rooms. Then as Anubis had powerbombed Fry and was about to cover him , out slipped Barbed-Wire Brian from under the ring, he grabbed a chair and hit Anubis with it. The ref called for the bell ,and then he hit
Fry with it. Brian picked up Fry and gave him the Public Indecency and then hit Anubis with the 187 . He picked up his bag and spray painted with sliver the NgD logo on both guys and then had a speech and walked away kicking Fry on his way out]
Decision : ANUBIS WINS BY DQ BWB: Anubitch, you idiot, tonight, infront of the people, I proved what I am capable of. This will be just a little preview of our match next Monday night. Because, Anubitch, you are nothing more than the
Cult's toilet paper, they wipe their ass with you. Tonight, I spared you from humiliation, but, don't feel all light hearted, because on
Monday, you'll have more than spraypaint on your back, you will have blood all over your body, and, not just because I hate you and your blood-pissing vampire friends in the Cult, but because you are just plain sick... fuckin' that sheep and all! AHAHAHAHA!
JAGGED JEFF JACKSON Vs. ROB DIABLO [Triple J was applauded as he came to the ring but Rob Diablo was booed all the way to the ring. JJJ started well with rakes to the eyes, knee lifts, neckbreakers, and fall away slams. Diablo never looked fazed and came back strongly with whips to the corners and tilt-to-whirl backbreakers, he also used a boston crab, STF and such to wear down
JJJ's back . JJJ started to come back with spinebusters, lariats and samoan drops. This time Diablo stopped JJJ's offence with swinging neckbreakers, he even powerbombed JJJ onto the turnbuckle and hit with an inverted DDT. A couple of near pinfalls and Triple J came back again with a bulldog, standing sidekick , brainbuster, vertical suplexes and a tiger driver. This time JJJ had a near pinfall. After various DDT's,
Piledrivers, huricana's , power slams, and death valley drivers , Diablo went up top and hit with a flying leg drop. Then with JJJ out of it ,
Diablo pulled him out of the ring and laid him on the spanish announcer's table and went back and climbed to the top turnbuckle and hit him with a flying leg drop that went straight through JJJ and the table smashed completely. Diablo dragged JJJ back in the ring before the
10 count. Diablo then hit with a half nelson suplex and hit the Hell
Blade and pinned him with just one foot.] Decision: The winner by pinfall ...... ROB DIABLO!!!!!!!!!!!! 'DEADLY GAME' DAVE DANIELS Vs. EXCALIBUR [ DGDD came to the ring with fans backing as did Excalibur for some reason. For once there was no interruptions from the NgD and The CuLt in this one. These two highly skilled wrestlers demonstrated their full knowledge of holds, reversals, moves and countermoves in a fast paced encounter that didn`t stay steady for one second. DGDD seemed well prepared for his opponent though, and as fast as Excalibur was, Daniels was a little quicker with his thoughts, and sometimes was able to be prepared for the unexpected from Excalibur , such as DDT off the ring apron that Deadly Game just saw coming. He held onto the ropes and
Excalibur fell to the floor. Excalibur was by no means the lesser of the men. He was able to control the match for longer periods of time, and generally keep DGDD disorientated. It was such a shame then that an encounter like this was spoiled by the way of it`s ending. With the referee slowly getting to his feet after being knocked down when Excalibur ducked Daniels`s clothesline, Deadly Game quickly reached into his shorts and pulled out a silver can of spray paint from a bag. Excalibur could not look away in time, and was blinded by the spray paint getting in his eyes. put him on the top rope and executed a belly to belly suplex. He then executed the
Deadly whiplash ( Side gut wrench into a powerbomb) he them pinned him
Decision : The winner by pinfall ...... "DEADLY GAME" DAVE DANIELS
SEAN STACY Vs. COPPERHEAD SS: Never fear my fans as Sean Stacy has arrivied!!!! [Crowd pop] SS: Next I gotta take on some scumbag who calls himself Copperhead. Copperhead? Why did you choose that name when you could have chose Goldhead, or Silverhead, or even, nevermind, it doesn't matter what name you choose because I will kick your ass just the same anyways.
[Crowd pop] SS: And now with the NgD on my side....... [Huge crowd pop] SS: I am ready to kick even more ass!!!!!! [Big crowd pop] SS: So Copperhead, bring your ass out so I can kick it and go home early. [ Both men were accompanied by their stable mates to the ring but Copperhead was also accompanied by Church. The fans gave a standing ovation for Stacy , the Cruiserweight champ and they jeered Copperhead and his entourage. Once in the ring they got going and Copperhead got the upperhand and used some power moves with some great suplexes to take
Stacy off his feet. He used flying clotheslines, belly to back suplexes, caravan suplex, gut wrench suplexes mixed with a huge piledriver and a samoan drop. He had a couple of near pinfalls but Stacy always got a shoulder up. The ref had a hard time officiating this , as both guys took it to the outside and used chairs, ring bell etc.. Copperhead kept on trying to whip Stacy into the ring post and the steel steps but everytime he did it was reversed and he would end up on the floor. At one time Stacy hit Copperhead with a garbage can and knocked out the ref by accident .. Stacy took adavantage of this and ran off the guard rails and drop kicked Copperhead back to the hard concrete floor. On the outside Insomnia and Rage were battling it out in a kind of prelude to
Monday's world title fight. Back in the ring and it was all stacy , he hit with a frog splash, missile dropkick, double underhook DDT, flying leg drops, moonsault. Meanwhile Insomnia , Rage and Church were battling it out on the outside . A spinning heel kick knocked Copperhead to the outside . Stacy went after him with a suicide dive that hit its mark. He picked up Copperhead and put him on the ring announcers table and then went to the top turnbuckle and hit with a 450 splash right through the table. He rolled Copperhead back in and the ref began to stir. Stacy grabbed a chair and smacked it over his head and left the chair there.
He went up top again and hit Too Kool ( Top rope Senton bomb) right on the cahir and in turn Copperheads face. He pinned him for the three count in this non- title fight
Decision: The winner of this bout by pinfall........ SEAN STACY!!!!!!! THE MAIN EVENT CHASE WILLIAMS & STEVE SWANSON Vs. MATT GRAY & BARB WIRE BRIAN The main event brought together four of BZWF`s finest, but the underdogs here were Gray & BWB of the NgD, if reputation was anything to go by. Both teams came out to chorouses of boo, yet as the match progressed it would be
Gray & BWB who had the fans on their side, if only because they disliked
Williams & Swanson more.
The match began in the aisle as Williams & Swanson - aka Ground Zero - rushed NgD as they were coming to ringside. After a brutal struggle outside, the ref managed to get both teams in the ring, and order was restored, which led to a classic match, but Ground Zero were the better team, tagging in and out more frequently, cutting the ring in half and helping each other out when in trouble.
Often the fight spilled to the areas around the ring, and both teams took the opportunity to get hardcore. Notable points in the match were Swanson &
Williams spike-powerbombing BWB off the ring apron onto a steel chair, Gray having Williams pinned for a three count after a chokeslam which didn`t count because the ref was distracted and also Williams almost taking BWB`s head off with a massive clothesline.
We join the commentators for the final moments of this intense match... DH: Swanson battling Gray now. He's rocking the self proclaimed king of extreme here with well placed rights and lefts.
CH: Yeah, but Barb-wire is in the ring now and turns the table on Swanson. Oh but look, Williams is now also in the ring. All four men going at it now. DH: Oh wow, monster closeline by Wire sending both him and Chase to the outside. CH: You`re right Dan. What impact by Wire to Chase sending both to the outside. We got a real battle on our hands in this great main event. Swanson is battleing Barb Wire in the ring, and Chase is going at it outside with Wire. What a match folks, what a match.
DH: Yes, yes, I agree Chris. What a match on our hands. Oh, wait Swanson whips Gray to the corner. He's fallowing him to the corner. Big splash attempted by Swanson.
CH: Oh man, Gray just pulled the ref in the middle between both men and he got sandwiched, literally.
DH: I don't think the two guys fighting on the outside realized what happened. CH: Gray just pulled something out of his tights and put it around his hand. DH: Brass knuckles??? He just pulled out a pair of Brass Knuckles!!! What a degenerate asshole!!!!!
CH: Swanson now picking Wire up, he doesn't seem to notice the Brass Knuckles. Wire swings back and ...... DH: He just clocked him. Swanson is out. Barb-wire and Chase are somewhere in the crowd fighting off as both the ref and now Swanson are out cold on the mat.
CH: What's this??? There seems to be a rise in the crowd but it's obviously not for BWB. (As the crowd lines off and gives room the camera spots a man in the crowd. It's Bryan Blayze, he jumps the gaurd rail and slides in the ring behind
Matt Gray. Brian doesn't seem to notice.)
DH: It's BLAYZE!!!!!!!! The Next Generation has arrived!!!!!! It is most definetly Show Time folks. And he's about to lay out the big NgD as Blayze would say....Slapnut!!!!!!
CH: Since when have you become a Blayze fan??? DH: I've always loved this guy. He's the greatest the BzWF has to offer. CH: Oh really??? Wasn't it like last week you hated him??? DH: Let's head to the action again. Gray still not noticeing Blayze standing behind him. CH: Oh but he will. Blayze taps Gray on the shoulder. Gray slowly turns around and see's Blayze. Gray jumped back a step. Oh wow, he just swung at
Blayze with those brass knuckles.
DH: Blayze ducks Gray and lands his opening kick to the gut. CH: Doubleunder hook by Blayze to Gray. He plants him. Face first. DH: HIGHWAY TO HELL!!!!!!!!!! Blayze just landed the his highway to hell, pedigree on Gray!!!!!!
CH: He rolls Gray over and now is just looking and pointing to the still fallen Swanson. What is he going to do???
DH: Oh my god!!!!! He's pulling Swanson ontop of Gray. He just put the ref in posistion. There's the count by the ref. This is over with.
1 2 3 (Ding, Ding, Ding,) RA: The winner of this bout by pinfall, Grrrooouuuuuuunnnnnnndddddddd
Zeeeerrrrroooooooooo!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
DH: Bryan Blayze made his presence felt in this match-up. Wow!!!!!! Well this has been another great Thursday night broadcast. We'll see you Monday folks.!!!!!!!!!
(Scene fades as we see Bryan Blayze standing in entranceway with his hands high in the air mocking the NgD basically by using his own 2 sweet sign. He then points to both Chase and Swanson who are now both standing in the ring celebrating, but beckoning Blayze into the ring. He nods as if to say
'soon' and then leaves the arena via the crowd...)
