Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Battlezone Wrestling Federation's earliest surviving broadcast, Monday X, aired 5 January 2000, opening on a promo from “Ice Cold” Steve Swanson threatening two rivals ahead of a scheduled match; the federation ran two named weekly shows through January — Monday X/Battlezone and Thursday X-Plosion/Rampage — staged from the Molson Center in Montreal and the Chicago BattleDome, building toward a first pay-per-view, Clash of the Mighty, announced for 16 January at the Compaq Center in Houston with five championships on the line, including a twenty-man battle royal for the Extreme title and a five-way elimination match for the World title.

A stable calling itself NgD — Blayze, Rage, Matt Gray and Dave Daniels, with Rachel at ringside — entered feuding with a rival faction built around Insomnia and Anubis, called “the Cult” in one wrestler's own promo, while a separate program pitted Swanson, accompanied by Mistress Sarah, against Insomnia himself in a tournament for the vacant World Championship.

The Extreme Championship changed hands in one of the surviving broadcasts: challenger Alex Brody and champion “The Hammer” Harry Holmes fought through the crowd and over a ladder while a masked interloper calling himself Skelatino, seen earlier at the pay-per-view announcement, laid both men out with a chair mid-match; Brody kicked out of Holmes's cover and won the title with a ladder-assisted powerbomb.

Eight documents survive in total, from that 5 January broadcast to a blank wrestler- application form posted 9 February 2000. Whether Clash of the Mighty was ever held, and how or when Battlezone Wrestling Federation closed, is not in the record.
