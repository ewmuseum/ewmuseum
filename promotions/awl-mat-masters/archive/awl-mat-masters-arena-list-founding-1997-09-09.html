Something we can all use: Arena list

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Creed wrote: > > It occurred to me as I was placing my house shows for this weekend that I'm > not too familiar with East coast locations. Though friends and > acquaintances, I've found the following... > > MGM Grand Garden Arena - Las Vegas, NV > Anaheim Stadium - Anaheim, CA > Arrowhead Pond - Anaheim, CA > National Orange Show - San Bernadino, CA > United Center - Chicago, IL > Sports arena - Toledo, OH > CoreStates Spectrum/Center - Philidelphia, PA > Civic Center - Pittsburgh, PA > Hershey Park Arena - Hershey, PA > Knickerbocker Arena - Albany, NY > Fleet Center - Boston, MS > O-rena - Orlando, FL > > I bet if we pass this around, we can all benefit... > > Anyone have any to add? I'm sure I know more, but they all escape me right > now... :) > > Creed Based on this, an ARENA LIST has been added to the AWL MAT MASTERS Homepage. I'm gathering as much info about as many arenas as I can to add to it. If you'd like to use the list, go to the AWL MAT MASTERS Homepage at http://www.matmasters.com and click on 'THE ARENA LIST." There is also a place on the page to email any arenas or info you wish to contribute. My goal is to eventually have a very thorough compilation of suitable venues for wrestling, indoor and outdoor, US and International. Thanks!
