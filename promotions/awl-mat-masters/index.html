A single-author animated wrestling serial styled as "The American Wrestling League," whose Universal Championship Tournament and community Arena List ran across a weekly matmasters.com update through the autumn of 1997. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

AWL Mat Masters presented itself as “THE AMERICAN WRESTLING LEAGUE,” a weekly-updated homepage at matmasters.com rather than a fed with an open roster of outside handlers. Its earliest surviving post, dated 9 September 1997, already describes an established site and an in-progress Universal Championship Tournament, with no founding announcement of its own surviving anywhere in its record.

Its own signature feature was an Arena List, credited to an idea “from Creed's posting earlier in the week” naming venues suited to wrestling cards; the operator built it out as a running resource, promising to add to it “three or more times a week” as a thank-you to the e-wrestling participants who had supported the site, and by 16 September the list already spanned five countries.

The Universal Championship Tournament ran alongside it in stages the site called out by round: a pictorial review of the first round went up by 16 September, and the second round's own images followed on 22 September, timed to run “until late Thursday.” By 15 November the story had gone “now ANIMATED,” continuing a semifinal between Stan “The Equalizer” Stone and The Count that the site called “a geniune high-impact encounter,” alongside a “What's Happened” recap section, an “AWL Intro,” and hyperlinked personality profiles.

Its last surviving document, dated 22 November 1997, updates the animated story to week 28 and still bills the site as “THE name in original animated wrestling action on the internet... now and always!!!” No closing notice, and no result of the Stone-Count semifinal or any later round, survives in its record.
