CWF: The Main Event

3 November 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE MAIN EVENT ____________________________________________________ Jonathen Hartgood>Hello fans and welcome to the CWF Main Event, tonight we have 13 excellent matches, but first I have some news for you,over the past week we have had three title changes, first on Tuesday, Steve
Christopher & Steve Slater won the United Staes tag team title from Thunder & Lightning, then the very next night, The Assassian & 'Bad Boy' Phil W. turned around and won the United States tag title from Christopher
& Slater by submission. Then just yesterday we saw a new Television champion crowned, former World Heavyweight champ the Dean of
Administration defeated Kinji to win it, and tonight they will face off in a rematch. So let's get down to our first match.
******The Headbanger vs. The Crow****** Headbanger just hasn't caught on to his grove yet, and tonight didn't help any, The Crow defeated Headbanger with the Asai Moonsault at 4 minutes and
30 seconds.
******Dumbest vs. Dumb****** These two are so stupid they signed a match against each other, this had to be the worst match on the card, the Lord finally spared us at the time of 6 minutes and 32 seconds when Dumbest pined Dumb after a running body splash.
******Thomas Michaels vs. Crusher****** Thomas Michaels made quick work of Crusher, he hit him with a roundhouse kick at the time of 2 minutes and 7 seconds and got the pin.
******Devestator vs. The Savage Beast****** Deavestator and Savage Beast are bulilt the same and have the same type of wrestling skill brawling, match mostly stayed outside, but the former TV champ Devestator was just to much for him and at the time of 5 minutes and
45 seconds Devestator pined him after a powerbomb.
******Dumber vs. Scorpion****** Scorpion was in control the whole match, like that's hard with one of the idiots, but when he set up for the Deadly Sting Dumb & Dumbest came out and attacked Scorp, the match went outside, Scorpion threw all three idiots on the ground but the ref had reached the 10 count. The match decission was a double countout.
******Brandon Nash vs. Blade****** Nash was attacked by Demon Knight & The Assassian before the match, Timebomb made the save for Nash, Blade came out to boos and garbage being throwed at him, the match was very fast paced, but the end came when Blade threw Nash into the ropes and set him up for a back body drop, when Nash came off the ropes he stopped right at Blade, grabbed him and sethim up for the piledriver, he jumped up and nailed it, went for the cover and got the three count at 5 minutes and 45 seconds.
******Officer Brian Williams vs. Darius Coleman****** Brian jumoped Coleman from behind, he took out his nightstick and started beating him, he then threw him into the ring, he took the turnbuckle cover off exposeing the metal he ramed Coleman's head into it, he then hit him in the ribs with the nightstick, the ref tried to stop him but Williams hit the ref with the nightstick, this caused the ref to DQ Williams at the time of 1 minute and 9 seconds. Williams didn't care he was still attacking Coleman untill the Headbanger came out and ran him off.
******Christopher & Slater vs. The Austin Brothers****** This was the best match of the night so far, Drake Austin was in the match most of the time, he and Christopher, battled mostly outside, the end came when Cristopher had Drake pined and Shawn flew off the top turnbuckle with a legdrop across Christophers head, Drake rolled over and put his arm across Christopher's chest and got the pin at 28 minutes and 29 seconds.
******TV Champ Dean of Administration vs. Kinji****** This was the best match of the evening, former World Champ Dean made his way to ringside but was jumped by the Shadowlord, The Austin Bros. came out and made the save, then the former TV Champ made his way to ringside, he was very determined, during the match the Shadowlord made his way to ringside again, but left shortly, the end of the match came when Kinji put
Dean on the top turnbuckle, he then climbed to the top turnbuckle and delivered a messed up beely to back suplex that hurt him and Dean, both wrestlers shoulders were down, then all of a sudden Dean picked his arm up, the ref counted the three count and at the time of 6 minutes and 35 seconds, the Dean of Administration retained the Television title. After the match Shadowlord came back out and beat Dean across the back with a whip, it took the Austin Brothers and Brandon Nash to get him away, while
Nash was tring to user Shadowlord back to the locker room, Assassian &
Demon Knight came out and attacked him, but it wasn't for long, because
Timebomb came out and attacked Assassian & Demon Knight with a chain.
*US Tag Champions Assassian & Bad Boy vs. Death & Destruction* As the Assassian and 'Bad Boy' Phil W. came down to ringside they were met by Timebomb who hit Bad Boy across the face with the chain, it made
Bad Boy bleed from the forehead, Assassian wasn't left out of all of this he was hit in the ribs and the left chin bone was hit as well, the challengers came out next, they picked up both champions and threw thm in the ring, they set Assassian up in the Doomsday Device, but the Bad Boy was able to take out one of the knees of Destruction, Destruction dropped
Assassian and set Bad Boy up for it, they hit him with then throw him out of the ring, they then proceed to hit Assassian withit as well and at 5 minutes and 35 seconds Destruction pined The Assassian to become the new
US Tag Team Champions.
******US Champ Demon Knight vs. The Shadowlord****** The match was brutal, mostly on the floor, Shadowlord looked to have control whenm the Dean of Administration came out and grabbed the
Shadowlord's manager Godess and took the whip, he walked over the whiped him 4 or 5 times, thus he threw Demon Knight on top of him and got the three count at the time of 16 minutes and 11 seconds.
**World Tag Champs The Heartbreakers vs. Thunder & Lightning** Thunder & Lightning made their way to ringside, meanwhile here come the
Heartbreakers with manager Thomas Michaels, the Heartbreakers are the only champions who wn the tournamnet to win the tag belts and till ahve not lost them, but tonight might end that streak because Thunder & Lightning are mad because the Heartbreakers cost them there US title, the match starts off, Sexy Boy throws something in Thunder's face, which blinded him, the Heartbreakers then start to work over Lightning, then at 3 minutes and 9 seconds the Boy Toy pined Lightning after the Concieted
Crunch(sidewinder) to retain the tag team title once again.
******World Champ Kaos vs. Timebomb****** Great matc, but before the match started Timebomb was jumped in the locker room, by The Assassian and Demon Knight, back in the ring Timebomb just nailed Kaos with the piledriver, one...two..thr wait he got up because here comes Demon Knight, he's pulling out a can of hair spray, he also has a lighter, he sprays andlights right in Timebombs face, he holds his face,
Kaos comes up from behind and rolls him up and gets the three count at the time of 11 minutes and 2 seconds.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- Jonathen Hartgood>What a night of action, new US tag champs Death & Destruction, the fued between Demon Knight, The Assassian, Timebomb, &
Brandon Nnash got worse, and the Dean of Administration retained his title but didn't get any help from the Shadowlord. Join us next time(Sunday
Night) as we will have an interview with The Shadowlord, and will have signed matches for the Survior Series on Thanksgiving Night.
