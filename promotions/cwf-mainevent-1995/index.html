A single, self-contained kayfabe show script narrated by its own in-fiction commentator, "Jonathen Hartgood," crowning a United States Tag Team title change amid continuing Television, World Tag Team and World Heavyweight title feuds and a teased Survivor Series card -- one of a dozen-plus unrelated federations to share the bare acronym CWF across this census. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

CWF Main Event survives in one broadcast, aired 3 November 1995 under commentator Jonathen Hartgood, who opened by running down a week of title changes: Steve Christopher and Steve Slater had taken the United States Tag Team Championship from Thunder & Lightning on Tuesday, only for The Assassian and “Bad Boy” Phil W. to win it back from Christopher and Slater by submission the very next night, and the Dean of Administration — himself a former World Heavyweight Champion — had dethroned Kinji for the Television Championship the day before, setting up a rematch on this card.

The undercard ran through thirteen matches, from a self-mocking bout between wrestlers billed Dumb, Dumber and Dumbest to Officer Brian Williams jumping Darius Coleman with a nightstick and clubbing the referee for a disqualification, chased off only when the Headbanger ran in. The best of the night's early matches went to Drake and Shawn Austin, who took twenty-eight minutes to put away Christopher and Slater on a top-rope legdrop and a pinning bridge.

In the Television Championship rematch, the Dean of Administration held off both Kinji and a ringside intrusion from the Shadowlord to retain, at the Austin Brothers' own cost of running interference for him. The Shadowlord returned after the bell to whip the Dean across the back, and the brawl that followed pulled in the Austin Brothers, Brandon Nash, The Assassian, Demon Knight and Timebomb before it broke up.

Two more titles changed hands before the main event. Death & Destruction won the United States Tag Team Championship from The Assassian and Bad Boy after Timebomb bloodied Bad Boy with a chain at ringside; the Shadowlord then took the United States Championship from Demon Knight after the Dean of Administration stripped the Shadowlord's manager, Godess, of her own whip and used it on Demon Knight himself. The Heartbreakers, managed by Thomas Michaels, kept their World Tag Team Championship over Thunder & Lightning — still undefeated since winning the belts in a tournament — after Sexy Boy blinded Thunder and Boy Toy finished Lightning with the Conceited Crunch.

Kaos closed the show retaining the World Heavyweight Championship over Timebomb, who had already been jumped backstage by The Assassian and Demon Knight before the bell; Demon Knight sprayed and lit hairspray in Timebomb's face at the two count, letting Kaos steal the pin. Hartgood signed off naming the Demon Knight–Assassian–Timebomb–Nash feud as still escalating and promising a Shadowlord interview and a signed Survivor Series card for that Thanksgiving. No further CWF Main Event broadcast survives, and whether that Survivor Series ever aired is not in the record.
