Hey all, This is just an idea I had yesterday, i'm sure it's been done to death before, but i'm going to give it a shot anyway. I currently run a booked e-wrestling federation known as the WIWC. When I decided upon this name, I had the intentions of running a semi-booked, semi-simmed league. However, after participating in such federations as TCW, MWWA (now defunct) and the NAWA (questionable), I have decided that the booked method is much more enjoyable then the simmed method. So, obviously, the name of my federation no longer applies. But, it seems to me like it could fit as a name for an organzation of federations. So, if anyone is currently running a federation, booked or simmed, extreme or classic, real or fantasy wrestlers, and you want to become a member of the World Internet Wrestling Council, reply to this message ASAP. Currently, the WIWC has three members, being myself, the NWF, and the MLCW. As I said before, any & all feds WILL be accepted! Regards, [name withheld], President WIWC & handler of Johnny "Prime Time" Johnston
