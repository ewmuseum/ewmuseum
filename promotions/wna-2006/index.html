A weekly Australian promotion whose surviving Monday Night Chaos broadcasts built a stable war and four title programs toward its Winter War pay-per-view. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WNA's surviving record is two consecutive broadcasts of its weekly show, Monday Night Chaos, taped in Albury, Australia in August 2006 — already mid-run, with four championships (World Heavyweight, Australasian, World Tag Team and Extreme T.V.) already established and a Winter War pay-per-view already on the calendar.

The 14 August broadcast opened on the previous week's tag title change, Team Drunkard's own win over the Black Label Society, and on a crucifixion angle staged that same night: stable members The Rev and the promotion's own on-air boss, Rohan, had bound a wrestler called The Cardinal to a wooden cross with razor wire after a title match. The broadcast's own main event, a tag match pitting Rohan and Australasian Champion Thor against Phoenix and “Iron Fist” Seth Damon, ended in a disqualification when a returning Black Label Society member ran in with a chair.

World Heavyweight Champion Boraalis, sitting on commentary for a number-one contender's match between former champion DreamMachine and challenger Mr. Aristos, interfered to cost both men the win and left them beaten in the ring — “the most opportunistic champion in WNA history,” by the broadcast's own commentary.

The following week's show built toward Winter War: Rohan, announcing his own retirement from in-ring competition, revealed two four-on-four teams for the pay-per-view — one he and Thor would lead against a team fronted by Seth Damon, and a separate main event pitting Messiah X, Matt Frya, DreamMachine and The Cardinal against Gabriel, “The Category Five Superstar” Mikey Jay, Boraalis and the still-absent Rev — while a long-running feud between the stables Australia's Next Top Wrestlers and Team Wrist Slash was set for its own four-on-four blowoff at the same event.

No document past 21 August 2006 survives; whether Winter War itself was ever staged, or what came of any of these builds, is not recorded.
