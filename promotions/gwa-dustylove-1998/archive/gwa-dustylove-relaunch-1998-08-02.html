G\\/\//A GLoBAL WReSTLiNG ALLiANCe --=--iT's BaCK... AnD YoU AiN'T SeeN NoTHiN' YeT!!!--=-- ________________________________________ --=--That's right folks... The GLoBAL WReSTLiNG ALLiANCe is back! And this time, it's here to STaY! The last run was eXCeLLeNT, but it ended kinda short. This time, it will be completely different. Your President will be none other than yours truley... "Dangerous" Dusty Love. The action will be hotter than ever and we're gonna pick up where we left off the first time. All you have to do is read over the info, fill out the application, and then get accepted. You'll know if you're accepted or not. You'll be added to the list if you A/2E... And if you're NoT... You ain't good 'nuff to hang with the superstars of the GWA. If you thought the first run was good... Then you ain't seen NoTHiN' yet!!!--=-- ________________________________________ -[1]- CaRDs -[2]- FLaSHeS -[3]- TiTLeS -[4]- RuLeS -[5]- AppLiCaTioN ________________________________________ -[CaRDs]- The card will be LiVe. LiVe meaning that it will be commentated by the announcers and will be very detailed. You never know what can happen on the GWA card. But if for some reason the card isn't live and it's taped then the card won't be fully commentated and won't have all the details like in a live card. But they will still be exciting and keep you on the edge of your seat. There will be one card a week. It will take place on Friday Night. It will be a two hour program. The show will be called FRiDaY NiGhT FuRY or mostly just called FuRY. The first hour of FuRY will be exciting, of coarse. But not as exciting as the second hour. The second hour (the best hour.) will contain the best action on the card. Such as the bigger matches. And of coarse, the MAiN EVeNT of the night. Of coarse there will be pay per views every month. One a month. I'm not sure what they'll be called yet but I will let you know. The pay per views are ALWaYs LiVe and ALWaYs hold the biggest and best matches. All the titles will be decided on this one (usually.). All cards will be just like seen on TV. They will have surprise matches, sneak attacks, and everything else you see on television of coarse. You will be assigned a match for these cards and you will know ahead of time so you will have time to FLaSH (RP) for it. You can plan sneak attacks on the cards. Just let me know and I'll hook it up for you. The station that FuRY will be on is DDTV aka DustDawg Television. The cards will be commentated by the owner of the GWA, Michael Thomas, and Lane Borden. They will be at the broadcast table and call the action. ________________________________________ -[FLaSHeS]- Flashes are the same thing as roleplays. Just different words you can use, I guess. You can only do one flash a day. I know it sounds bad but it will help in the end. It keeps from cluttereing up the mailboxes. And the one a day really shows who the better flasher is. It helps. And in the subject of your rp please type -[GWA]- and then the title. All roleplays must be in to me by the deadline. The deadline is Wednesday night at midnight for FuRY. No more after that will count for the upcoming card. I beleive all you guys know how to flash so I won't explain it all. Flashes must, of coarse, be a lot longer than 20 lines. Don't use huge letters in your flashes. Use normal size fonts. Also, please don't use color. Just make them black and white. Color just messes the whole flash up. It's not needed. You get the picture. Don't jump anyone in your flashes and don't flash for someone else. You can't make someone say something in your flash that they didn't really say. If you want them to say something in your flash then you have to get that person's permission first. If your flashes are better than your opponents flashes then you win. Bottom line. If you don't flash then you don't win, bottom line. You HaVe to flash or you will be fired from the GWA. Flashing is the main part of the fed. Please do not over cuss in your flashes. You know what words you can and can't use. If you can't say the words on TNT or USA networks then don't say them in your rp unless they are bleeped out. Make your flashes seem real. That's the point of flashing. You act out your character. And by the way, fake wrestlers only. But don't rip off any real wrestler's gimmick. That's stupid. And don't make his nickname something like Stone Cold or Heartbreak Kid. That's very unoriginal. Don't do that. This is a fed that you send your flashes to everyone in the fed. In other words, it's a reply to all fed. That way, you get to see the flash when it's sent. No waiting. And then you can reply off of it when you want. But absolutely NO JuNK MAiL. ________________________________________ -[TiTLeS]- There will of coarse be Titles in the GWA. What's a fed without titles? The biggest title in the GWA is gonna be the World Heavyweight Championship. You'll have to climb the ladder to get a shot at this belt. Since this is the highest ranked belt, it'll most likely be the main event of a card. But not all the time. The second to highest belt is gonna be the North American Heavyweight Championship. This belt will be defended more than the World Title. And the third title will be the Television Championship. This belt will probably be defended on every card. It's the smallest singles heavyweight title that there is. It'll be hard to keep, considering, you'll have to defend it a lot. Then, there's the Championship for the smaller guys. The Cruiserweight Championship. The wrestler has to be 225 pounds or under to win this title. But, a cruiserweight CaN win AnY title in the GWA. But only the one's that meet the weight limit can comptete for the Cruiserweight Championship. The next, and last Championship will be the World Tag Team Titles. The tag titles will be held by the top tag team in the federation. In order to get these belts, both members of the team need to flash. After all that's why it's called TAG TEAM TITLES. These belts can be defended at any time. You'll have to climb the ladder to get shots at the belts just like in real life. ________________________________________ -[RuLeS]- There are only a few rules but you must obey them all. Here's the list... -[1]- Must Roleplay. -[2]- Commissioner Dusty Love and the announcers will not be taking challenges. They're here to run a fed and to do their jobs. They will not be wrestling. -[3]- No crying. We will NoT tolerate cry babies. They will be fired. -[4-] All wrestlers in the fed will be fake wrestlers. You can have two wrestlers at the most. No more than two though. -[5]- If you quit then you have to give us a little time to take you off the list. We'll do it as fast as we can but sometimes it just takes a little more time. Don't harass us about it, it will happen as soon as we can do it. -[6]- NO ToS'ing WiLL Be ToLeRaTeD! NO TOS'ing! NoNe! If you think you're gonna want to TOS someone then don't join. NO TOS'ing! -[7]- No excessive cussing. Don't over cuss in your flashes. It sounds dumb. -[8]- Have fun! That's what this fed is here for. ________________________________________ -[AppLiCATioN]- eMaiL AddReSS - WReSTLeR (Fake Only!) - HEiGHT - WEiGHT - HeeL / FaCe / TWeeNeR - eNTRaNCe MuSiC - RiNG ATTiRe - GiMMiCK - TyPe oF WReSTLeR - APPeaRaNCe (What does he look like?) - FiVe oF HiS FaVoRiTe MoVeS - SeT-Up HoLD (Hold used before Finisher.) - FiNiSHeR - DeSCRiPTioN oF FiNiSHeR - MaNaGeR's NaMe (If you have one.) - SeX oF MaNaGeR - Do THeY CHeaT? - APPeaRaNCe oF MaNaGeR - SaMPLe FLaSH - ________________________________________ -[Send this application, completely filled out, to LoveMan [detail withheld] ]- --=--Like I said before...
