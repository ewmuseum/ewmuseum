XWF World Heavyweight Championship WON BY WON FROM WHEN Vacant *1 January, 2008 Samoa Joe *2 XWF Back in Business, 2008 Big Show Samoa Joe XWF Hardcore Heaven 2008 Brock Lesnar Big Show *3 XWF Genesis, 2008 000 *1: ICWC is re-started as brand extension of UWF under the UWOW Banner. *2: Samoa Joe defeated Paul Wight, Vader defeated Lance Storm, the Dynamite Kid defeated Rob VanDam and Brock Lesnar defeated Ken Shamrock to advance to the KING OF THE MOUNTAIN MATCH (reverse ladder match) which Samoa Joe won by climbing the ladder and fastening the XWF World Title belt to the cable above the ring *3: Triple Threat match saw Brock Lesnar defeat both Big Show and Rob VanDam to become champion. 000 XWF World Tag Team Championship WON BY WON FROM WHEN Demolition *1 January, 2008 Cactus Jack & Kevin Sullivan Demolition XWF Back in Business, 2008 Demolition Cactus Jack & Kevin Sullivan XWF Return to Viking Hall, 2008 Raven & Vampiro Demolition UWOW Ultimate Supercard, 2008 Harlem Heat Raven & Vampiro XWF Genesis, 2008 000 *1: ICWC is re-started as brand extension of UWF under the UWOW Banner. Demolition are named as the new XWF World Tag Team Champions when they join the XWF as the UWF United States Tag Team Champions. To view the history of the UWF US Tag Team Titles, click here. 000 XWF North American Heavyweight Championship WON BY WON FROM WHEN Rick Rude *1 January, 2008 Vader Rick Rude XWF Return to Viking Hall, 2008 Rick Rude Vader XWF Genesis, 2008 000 *1: XWFis re-started as brand extension of UWF under the UWOW Banner. Ravishing Rick Rude was the reigning UWF Television Champion and brought the title to the XWF with him. Paul Heyman announced that it did not make sense for the XWF to have a Television Title considering the format and formally established the North American Heavyweight Championship with Rude as the very first champion. Click here for discontinued UWF title history, which this title is built upon. 000 XWF Cruiserweight Championship WON BY WON FROM WHEN Rob VanDam *1 XWF Hardcore Heaven 2008 Dynamite Kid Rob VanDam XWF Return to Viking Hall, 2008 Lance Storm Dynamite Kid UWOW Ultimate Supercard, 2008 000 *1: Rob VanDam won a Cruiserweight Battle Royal last eliminating Lance Storm to win the newly created title. 000 000 XWF X-Treme Divas Championship WON BY WON FROM WHEN Natalya Neidhart *1 XWF Genesis, 2008 000 *1: Natalya Neidhart became the new XWF World Women's Champion by defeating Madusa Miceli, Layla, Torrie Wilson, Candice Michelle, Maria Kinellis and Lacey von Erich in an elimination match, last forcing Madusa Miceli to submit to the Camel Clutch.
