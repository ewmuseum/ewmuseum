Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The X-Treme Wrestling Federation launched in January 2008 as, in its own title-history ledger's own words, a fed "re-started as brand extension of UWF under the UWOW Banner" — an indy-styled, hardcore-leaning member fed under General Manager Paul Heyman, later General Manager Eric Bischoff. Every named competitor across its surviving record is a real professional wrestler: Samoa Joe won the inaugural World Heavyweight Championship in a reverse-ladder King of the Mountain match, and the title passed to Big Show and then Brock Lesnar (in a Triple Threat over Big Show and Rob Van Dam) before Lesnar's reign was unified into the ICWC World Heavyweight Championship in March 2009.

Two of its titles are continuations of UWF belts rather than new creations. The North American Heavyweight Championship began life as the discontinued UWF Television Championship, brought over and renamed by Paul Heyman when Ravishing Rick Rude arrived still wearing it. The World Tag Team Championship began as the discontinued UWF United States Tag Team Championship, brought over by Demolition. Genesis, the October 2008 card, crowned four champions in one night: Brock Lesnar (World), Harlem Heat (Tag), Ravishing Rick Rude (North American) and Natalya Neidhart, the first X-Treme Divas Champion.

Four of its own dated 2008 show scripts survive whole and are served here — Back In Business (January), Hardcore Heaven (February), Trump Card (April) and Genesis (October) — alongside the fed's own June 2008 roster page and its title-history ledger. Return to Viking Hall (March 2008) and the fed's own share of the May 2008 Ultimate Supercard are named in the served record's own build-up but are not part of this wing's own held corpus.

Storylines

The Rude/Douglas war. Ravishing Rick Rude wins the inaugural North American Heavyweight Championship from Mike Awesome at Back In Business, a Con-Chair-To finish sprung by a masked-no-longer manager reveal: Shane Douglas, out of the ring since his own ECW run, at Rude's side and Terry Funk stretchered out. At Hardcore Heaven, Rude and Douglas are booked against Awesome and a General Manager-sprung mystery partner — Scott Hall, who turns on Awesome within seconds of the bell and joins the beatdown instead. Trump Card settles the Hall/Awesome half in a steel cage (Hall escapes for the win, Funk still hurt from the month before) while Douglas comes out of his own retirement to demand Rude one-on-one, a match GM Matthew Hopkins defers to the uncaptured 2008 Ultimate Supercard. By Genesis, Douglas has taken a front-office and broadcast-booth job instead of wrestling again, and the North American title itself has changed hands twice more on the ledger's own record — Vader won it from Rude at the unheld Return to Viking Hall, and Rude wins it back at Genesis only with a blindside assist from a hooded Paul Orndorff, secretly recruited by incoming GM Eric Bischoff — the war's own last beat this wing's own held corpus captures.

Demolition against Cactus Jack & Kevin Sullivan. Demolition arrives at Back In Business already champions, carried over from the UWF, and loses the World Tag Team Championship in the show's own first defense when manager Percy Pringle slips Cactus Jack a foreign object. At Hardcore Heaven, Cactus Jack & Kevin Sullivan retain against the Freebirds on another Pringle powder shot, and Demolition runs in afterward demanding the rematch Ax promises on the mic. That rematch is Demolition's own win at the unheld Return to Viking Hall, closing the title's own first loop, but the Cactus Jack/Sullivan partnership itself is what actually unravels next: by Trump Card the two are brawling each other in a backstage-to-arena Street Fight, and reigning World Champion Paul Wight runs in to choke-slam Cactus Jack onto a bed of thumbtacks and hand Sullivan the win — the same Wight who Cactus Jack had already been promised at the uncaptured 2008 Ultimate Supercard. The tag title itself, meanwhile, moves on without either man: Demolition retains it against Buff Bagwell & Scott Norton at Trump Card before losing it for good to Raven & Vampiro at the unheld Ultimate Supercard, who lose it in turn to Harlem Heat at Genesis.

Affiliations

United World Of Wrestling (UWOW)

Member of XWF (2008)
XWF’s own title-history records open every belt “XWF is re-started as brand extension of UWF under the UWOW Banner,” dated January 2008.

United Wrestling Federation (UWF, 2001)

Shared continuity with XWF (2008)
Two UWF titles migrated to XWF with their holders: the Television Championship, brought by Ravishing Rick Rude and renamed the North American Heavyweight Championship, and the United States Tag Team Championship, brought by Demolition and renamed the World Tag Team Championship. A third, the Canadian Heavyweight Championship, is said by UWF’s own title history to have moved to XWF with Lance Storm as well, but no surviving XWF title is credited with continuing that lineage.

International Championship Wrestling Council (ICWC)

Shared continuity with XWF (2008)
Edge defeated XWF Champion Brock Lesnar and #1 contender Hulk Hogan in a Triple Threat Match at ICWC Capital Combat (2009) to unify the XWF and ICWC World Heavyweight Championships under the ICWC banner (xwf-uwow-world's own events[], type unified_into, title icwc-world).
