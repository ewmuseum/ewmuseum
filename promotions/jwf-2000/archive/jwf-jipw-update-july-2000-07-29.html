JWF/JIPW Update (July)

29 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Star Warrior Regains JWF Championship Controversy always seems to follow the FDF and the JWF/GWF Title
Unification match between Star Warrior and Massif was no exception.
Star Warrior still had a minor injury from the attack by Bishop Hell at the Survivor Series, but was trying not to let it slow him down.
Massif clearly did not want to further injure his friend and was visibly reluctant to lock up, despite encouragement from Omega, the
Commander, and Star Warrior himself. Massif decided to use his power to wear down Star Warrior slowly with very few high impact moves.
Being a skilled technical wrestler though, Star Warrior was able to slip out of the hold and nail Massif with a flying dropkick that took him down! In desperate attempts to steal the match, Warrior went for repeated quick covers on the surprised Massif, but he kicked out over and over again. Seeing his opportunity, Star Warrior nailed Massif with his Nova Launch finisher. The Commander started to climb into the ring, but Omega grabbed him. As the two got into a heated verbal argument, Warrior got the pinfall winning the match and regaining the
JWF Universal Championship!
After the match there were more questions than answers. Why did the
Commander try to interfere in the match and who's side was he on?
Omega tried to disspell the rumors, "We are all under a tremendous amount of stress due to the recent happenings in the JWF/JIPW. Our enemies outnumber our allies, so we must stick together. I am actively recruiting new talent for the GWF, but I am also looking fro new members to add to the FDF. The Commander is a leader not a follower and it is time for him to get out from under the shadow of my son. I think I have the perfect recruit that will not only make a great tag team partner for my best friend, but will also enhance his singles skills as well. In turn this recruit will learn a great many things from the Commander. As soon as the contracts are all signed I will bring him to the GWF.
"As for Massif, he fought a brave match, although it was obvious he was holding back his best. My son, Star Warrior is almost fully recovered from his injuries and will be in perfect health for his next title defense. Therefore, we will give him a rematch at both the GWF &
JWF Championships..."
At this point, JWF/JIPW owner JJ Dean interrupted the pres conference,
"Just a minute now, Omega. We agreed the the JWF contenders would be determined by us jointly. You picked the last contender, so this time it's my choice. Star Warrior will defend his GWF title against Massif, but the JWF Universal Championship is a different matter. We've already seen Warrior v. Massif two times now, and quite frankly it's getting boring. Massif obviously doesn't have that killer instinct it takes to be a champion, so we need a new contender. But don't worry
Omega, just to show I'm a fair sport about this, I'll let your new opponent be someone from your precious GWF...someone who's killer spirit you've seen first hand...the GWF Interplanetary Champion,
Wolf!" Champion versus champion for the richest prize of all!
DeathAngel Wreaks Havoc on the JCW If one person isn't making too many friends...it's DeathAngel. It all began at the Survivor Series when DeathAngel tried to attack the newly crowned JCW champion, Thunder. Fortunately, Hijack was there to save his tag team partner and thwart DeathAngel's plan. During
DeathAngel's recent match against Mockmaw, Hijack went on the offensive. Hijack came out from the locker room and stood at the top of the entrance ramp to observe the match. This clearly distracted
DeathAngel, allowing Mockmaw to get the advantage. DeathAngel tried to roll out of the ring to regain his composure, but Hijack ran down to ringside and tossed him back in the ring. As he did Mockmaw dove off the top rope with a splash and scored the pin on DeathAngel.
Smiling, Hijack walked back to the locker room. This loss eliminated
DeathAngel from the JWF
But DeathAngel would have his revenge. Crow had just defeated Hijack, including a post-match attack that injured Hijack. Things only get worse for Hijack when he finally staggered out of the ring. As he was leaving ringside, DeathAngel saw his opportunity and attacked Hijack from behind and started choking him with a baseball bat, dragging him backwards. Holding a microphone, DeathAngel then challenged Hijack to a series of 5 matches before knocking him unconcious with a shot from the bat to the back of the head.
Still DeathAngel wasn't though causing mischief. He had recently kidnapped Twister & Mack Mephisto's valet, Medussa, but no one knew where he was keeping her. After a loss to the Flying Titans, Twister &
Mephisto were confronted by a video challenge from DeathAngel. He would take on either Twister or Mephisto in a Titan Death Match with
Medussa suspended in a cage above the ring. The winner of the match would get to keep Medussa. The question now is will Hijack, Twister, &
Mephisto accept these challenges...and will DeathAngel be able to back up his words? Click here for more news from the JCW...
