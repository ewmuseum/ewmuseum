JWF/JIPW Update

22 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thunder Wins JCW Championship In a hard fought Steel Cage Match, Thunder defeated Squid to become the first ever JCW Galaxian Championship. At one point, Sabotage came to ringside, but couldn't get in the ring because of the steel cage.
Officials quickly come down to the ring and pushed him back into the lockerroom before he could interfere in the match. Thunder overcame the distraction though and took the fight to a red-hot Squid by suplexing him and then tossing him into the corner. Squid reversed the throw, but Thunder reversed it again and leveled Squid with a clothesline in the corner. Thunder then slammed Squid face first into the cage and power slammed him before using his Thunderclap finisher. Thunder was then able to climb out of the cage for the victory and the JCW Championship! He didn't have long to celebrate however, as DeathAngel ran to the ring and attacked him, but Hijack was quick to arrive on the scene to save his tag team partner.
DeathAngel didn't see Hijack come up behind and the two Pack members were able to double team DeathAngel with two backbreakers on the concrete floor, moderately injuring him.
On a related note, the winning streak continued later in the night for
The Pack as Sinergy overcame a 3-1 deficit in their Survivor Series match against the Saboteurs to claim the victory. Things looked grim for Sinergy as he found himself singlehandedly facing Twister,
Hurricane, and the Machine, but one by one, Sinergy eliminated each combatant to win the match. Again the pack wasn't left any time to celebrate, as the Machine attacked Sinergy from behind with a series of punches and then threw him over the top rope into a ringside table injuring him.
Bishop Hell Turns Up the Heat on Star Warrior & the FDF The look on Omega's face said it all. When Thantos introduced the world to the newest member of the Royal Court at the Survivor Series, everyone could see the blood drain from Omega. At ringside you could quietly hear him whisper to his son Star Warrior, "This is what has been foretold to me. The epic battle has begun..." Not many heard this cryptic message though, as all the attention was focused on
Bishop Hell and his first entracne in the JWF. Nothing could prepare us for what we saw next!
The Survivor match between the Royal Court and FDF was just as brutal as could be expected when these bitter enemies get in the ring together. Exo-King was eliminated early via a disqualification for beating down Massif with a steel chair. As officials pulled him way, he screamed, "Who's the big man now, Massif?" This was nothing compared to what happened soon after...
It all began when Star Warrior & Bishop Hell were eliminated via countout while brawling outside of the ring. The match was paused while the officials tried to sort out the fracas between Warrior and
Bishop. All of the sudden, Bishop grabbed Warrior in a headlock with his left arm and shot a flame from his right hand into Star Warrior's
face!!! Warrior went down blinded and in agonizing pain! With a wry smile on his face, Bishop Hell then walked off. The whole thing had been a trap from the beginning to take out Star Warrior! Warrior was seriously injured and taken off in an ambulance for treatment.
This clearly distracted the FDF team for the remainder of the match and it wasn't long before the Royal Court pulled off the upset with
Viktor Khezminov and Death Knight bringing home the victory for the team. It's safe to say though that the tension between the FDF and the Royal Court is hotter then ever.
