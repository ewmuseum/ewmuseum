Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

JWF survives through two of its own results columns, styled “JWF/JIPW Update,” dated 22 May and 29 July 2000, reporting news for itself alongside two companion promotions covered under the same banner, JCW and GWF, plus updates on JIPW, a separate competition the column describes as one “where you create your own e-fed and compete against others for ratings.”

The May column opened with Thunder defeating Squid in a steel cage match to become the first JCW Galaxian Champion, Squid's tag partner Sabotage unable to reach the ring to interfere before officials pulled him away; afterward DeathAngel attacked the new champion, only for Thunder's own tag partner Hijack to run in and help beat him down. In a related Survivor Series match the same card, Sinergy overcame a three-on-one deficit against Twister, Hurricane, and the Machine to win alone, before the Machine attacked him afterward.

Elsewhere on the card a new arrival, Bishop Hell, entered the Royal Court and was written into a brutal debut: eliminated from a Survivor Series match against the FDF by countout for brawling with Star Warrior, he blindsided Star Warrior with a flame from his own hand, injuring him badly enough to end the night in an ambulance and clearing the way for Royal Court members Viktor Khezminov and Death Knight to win the match. Massif held the promotion's own Universal Championship after the card.

By the July column DeathAngel had escalated his own feud, kidnapping Twister and Mack Mephisto's valet Medussa and challenging either man to a Titan Death Match with her suspended above the ring, while Hijack traded attacks with him over an earlier ambush with a baseball bat. In the marquee match, Star Warrior, still recovering from Bishop Hell's attack, regained the Universal Championship from Massif with his Nova Launch finisher, a decision that prompted the promotion's owner to overrule a promised rematch and instead name the GWF's own Interplanetary Champion, Wolf, as Star Warrior's next challenger.

No document survives past that July update. How the Star Warrior—Wolf match played out, what became of DeathAngel's challenge over Medussa, or how long the JWF/JIPW circuit continued is not recorded.
