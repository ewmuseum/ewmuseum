Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World eWrestling Alliance, written WeWA on its own pages, was an alliance of e-wrestling promotions reopened in 2010 on the model of the old National Wrestling Alliance: member promotions took regions of the world, a touring champion crossed between them, and the alliance staged events of its own. Its rule book of 18 April 2011 sets out the machinery: a chairman and a board of directors, member and affiliate federations, territories assigned by state and country, a monthly status report from every promotion, two sanctioned world titles and three tournament cups, strategy forms for the matches, and a rule that only original wrestlers may be used by the alliance and its members.

Its title pages trace 16 championships from March 1988 to 1 October 2002 through 361 reigns, naming real professional wrestlers, from Sting and Owen Hart to Rob Van Dam, beside wrestlers the league seems to have made up, and ending, in the pages' own footnotes, when a company bought the World Wrestling Alliance from its owner and closed it. The alliance's own sanctioning notice says the WeWA 'is based off a fantasy wrestling alliance known as the World Wrestling Alliance' and takes its title histories from it. The alliance's pages present the 1988 to 2002 lines as its own predecessor's, closed in 2002; the World Wrestling Alliance fantasy league they name as their source keeps those lines running past 2002 and is still active. The museum therefore reads 1988 to 2002 as the inherited history of that fantasy league, which the alliance's pages present as their own, and files that history on this wing as the alliance presents it. The real wrestlers in it are this wing's own portrayals, each its own record here and never merged with another federation's. The wing's own dated activity runs from 2010 to 2014.

The reopening was slow. News of 27 June 2010 admits Legendary Pro Wrestling to the Mid Atlantic region; on 6 January 2011 the alliance reports that 'last years failed attempt to open' came to nothing for want of regions and sets no start date until two are signed. On 15 May 2011 Ultimate Championship Wrestling and its development league HCW are named the first two promotions to sign on, and on 30 May the alliance says goodbye to HCW, closed by Ultimate Championship Wrestling, which leaves it a single region. The front page still lists the Southeast and Ultimate Championship Wrestling as its regional members and both world titles as Vacant. Its events list names four events for 2011 and seven for 2012, the last on 16 December 2012, but only the card of 24 July 2011 is announced in any detail, with every match 'TBA', and no result of any event survives in the directory. The alliance outlived that site. On a second home on the geocities.ws host its news runs from 1 December 2013 to 12 May 2014: its chairman working to start Pacific West Wrestling (PWW) and World Class (20 January), a new world champion named on 15 February, Hardcore Championship Wrestling's return on 12 April, a Pagano Cup in Las Vegas on 20 April in which Jason Cashe won the Cup and then the world title from Sean Jackson while the Corven Cutthroat Connection took the vacant tag titles, rule changes and new territories on 24 April, and an ION card announced for 25 May at Joe Louis Arena. Its 2014 events list names seven dates, and the other five events are templates with no card. No page is dated after 12 May 2014, and the host still serves the same pages.

The rule book carries one memorial, and the museum serves it with his name. The Steven W. Pagano Memorial Cup is 'a tournament created in the memory of a great friend, lost to a deadly disease': he died on 19 April 2001, aged 31, of cystic fibrosis, 'living two years past the date doctors had given him'. The rule book says the Cup 'dates back to June 2001'; the Cup's own page says 'Since June 2002' and that it 'Originated in WCCW in 2005, and first competed as a Memorial Event in 2002'. The rule book closes the paragraph: 'Rest In Peace Steven W. Pagano. We Miss You.' The Cup is on the alliance's schedule for 22 April 2012, and the results of its staging in Las Vegas on 20 April 2014, which opens with a tribute to him, are served.
