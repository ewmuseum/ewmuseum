Ion Television Presents Championship Wrestling: the announced card (25 May 2014)

25 May 2014
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 25, 2014 Joe Louis Arena Detroit, Michigan Bell Time 8PM EST. Roleplay and Strat Deadline Friday at Midnight EST Opening Match PWW vs WCCW "Black Heart" Jonathan Talon 215 lbs. Jamaica, Queens, New York PWW Western States Heritage Champion vs. "The Original Big Daddy" Justin Dawes 295 lbs. Brownsville, Texas CSW Central States Tag Team Champion ION Presents Championship Wrestling is going to be all about brand supremacy. To start the night off we are going to see the two places that started it all as PWW's Jonathan Talon takes on WCCW and CSW's Justin Dawes. The winner will claim supremacy over the other.
Match 2 UTA vs HCW "The Missionary" Abdul Ahad 245 lbs. Medina, Saudi Arabia vs. Jade Justice 120 lbs. Houston, Texas Once again brand supremacy as we see Abdul Ahad take on Jade Justice. It will be interesting to see what The Missionary thinks about HCW's decision to send a woman as their representative. Can she do it and pull off a win? We will see at ION.
Grudge Match Eight Man Tag Team If Brothers of Light wins Pete Jackson must face Texas Lighting one on one. If New Tradition wins Texas Lightning must retire. "New Tradition" Pete Jackson Lake Storm Nic Cross William Dawes Total Combined Weight 994 lbs. vs. "Brothers of Light" Texas Lightning Mayer Tulio Jacob Tulio Anthony Tulio Total Combined Weight 910 lbs. Late addition to the card, after the events of CSW's Reunion show, CSW's Pete Jackson and [name withheld] requested that this match take place here on ION. The feud between New Tradition and Texas Lightning and the Tulio's is starting to get heated.
Match 4 CSW vs VCW "The Messiah" Ryan Kent 237 lbs. Columbus, Ohio by way of North Hills, West Virginia vs. "Ominous Angel" Lew Smith 216 lbs. Frimley, England VCW Heavyweight Champion In the third battle for brand supremacy we will see the VCW champion Lew Smith taking on CSW's Messiah. Who will be able to come out on top and claim the throne as best brand.
Tag Team Match Number One Contendership Fatal Four Way "ELITE" Max Burke CBR Combined Weight 477 lbs. vs. "New Tradition" Robert Bayley Brandon Myers Combined Weight 525 lbs. vs. "Degenerates" Xander Hayes Yarrian Carmack Combined Weight 470 lbs. vs. "Bipolar Satan" Brian Miles Tony Miller Combined Weight 570 lbs. PWW and WCCW started things off and together took the world by storm with a WWA World Tag Team Championship Triple Threat Match at the Pagano Cup. This time around the four new members get to see who can come together and become the number one contenders for the WWA World Tag Team Championships. The winner will get a shot at the titles at Battle at Busch XVI.
Main Event WWA World Heavyweight Championship "The Natural Boy" Dan Benson 248 lbs. Minneapolis, Minnesota vs. "The Influence of Wrestling" Jason Cashe 230 lbs. Houston, Texas by way of Atlanta, Georgia WWA World Heavyweight Champion After the events at the Pagano Cup, the board of directors has decided to give Dan Benson one more shot. The match that was originally scheduled as a one off, that turned into a first round match, then rescheduled as a second round match, will happen once again. This time it will be for all the marbles. The WWA World Champion Jason Cashe will walk in and make his first defense against the man that started things with him on twitter. Can Benson do the impossible?
