WeWA News - Pagano Results, new regions, new Champions

21 April 2014
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
daman — 21 April 2014 Well the Pagano cup was a spectacular event. We saw the WeWA World Championship change hands to somebody who isn't even a member of the WeWA. That is right, the WeWA World Championship found it's home in 4CW. We also saw world tag champions crowned as well.
Other news we would like to announce a new member of our community. Please give a warm welcome to Valor Championship Wrestling. They will be promoting in the WeWA North Atlantic Area. They are also a development territory of UTA. This should provide interesting opportunities between the two companies. We also have another region being Hardcore Championship Wrestling. We would like to welcome them into our Great Lakes region. We would also like to send a congratulations to our very own Sean Jackson on winning the UTA HeavyWeight Championship.
Be sure to check out WeWA's other regions and see whats going on. Who knows, maybe you will find a new home.
