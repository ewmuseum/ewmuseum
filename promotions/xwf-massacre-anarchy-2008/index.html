Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The X-Treme Wrestling Federation, which its own pages call the XWF, ran two weekly shows on a fantasy-wrestling website: Monday Massacre and Thursday Night Anarchy. What survives of Massacre is thirteen broadcasts from 1 December 2008 to 23 March 2009, set out as a card, the staged segments between the matches and a WINNER line for each, under General Manager Jensen Kent, with the X-Mas Xtreme build, a tournament for an International title in four rounds, and the Own The PPV qualifiers. The fed's own archive index lists two more December broadcasts, kept at a separate address.

Anarchy survives in two stretches: a handful of pages around Rage in the Cage on 4 October 2009 (a card, a build-up broadcast, the pay-per-view's results from Greensboro, and cards billed for Columbia and Madison Square Garden), and the six pages of August and September 2010 in which Jensen Kent returns as General Manager and a Universal Title Tournament runs to its semi-finals. Beside them sits a long run of roleplays, most of them for Famine of the Vile, the fed's Demon King, from 2007 into 2010; one of them stages his funeral in April 2010, and in a later one he is back in the ring.

Its Massacre cards link www.xwf99.com as the fed's home, and its pay-per-view results take their wrestler pictures from that address. A wrestler who returns in 2009 says he is back 'after ten years', and a wiki of the hobby dates the X-Treme Wrestling Federation to 1999. How the fed ended is not in the surviving record.
