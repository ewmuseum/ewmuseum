XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Card

Monday Massacre (23 March 2009)

23 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FIREFOX and MOZILLA are NOT RECOMMENDED for viewing this program. MATCHES: HUNTER RYAN vs. KIMEE MOOKS vs. DOWNFALL - - Standard Match - - 2-RP LIMIT THE “X EFFECT” BRIAN CADY vs. DOUBLE G - - Falls Count Anywhere - - 2-RP LIMIT MAIN EVENT RANMA SAOTOME vs. DRAKE KOMODO - - Non-Title Match - - 2-RP LIMIT Monday Night Massacre takes the air as HUNTER RYAN is shown arriving to the building. He parks his cherry red corvette before stepping out of the driver’s side. HUNTER pops the trunk as the inside crowd gives him a monstrous pop. HUNTER lifts the trunk of his car and leans inside to remove his bags….. SMACK! WHAT!! CHRIS PAGE wallops HUNTER in the back of the right knee with a lead pipe before slamming the trunk across HUNTERS lower back. CCP raises the trunk before slamming it down violently across the lower back once again before raising the trunk. CCP begins to choke HUNTER with the crowbar.
CCP: YOU THOUGHT THIS WAS OVER BITCH! CCP slams the trunk with his left hand before bouncing HUNTER head first off the trunk. CCP takes several steps back. HUNTER slowly turns around into a lead pipe shot to the ribs before bouncing HUNTERS face and body off the concrete with the PAGE PLANT! CCP gets back to his feet picking up the lead pipe. HUNTER pushes himself out of a pool of his own blood.
CCP: I want you to know HUNTER… I DID THIS TO YOU! CCP states before coming forward with a violent swing with the pipe smashing it across the temple of HUNTER. HUNTER falls back completely and totally out of it as blood covers his face and begins to trickle out of the right ear. CCP kneels down putting the pipe on the chin of HUNTER.
CCP: I told you HUNTER; I told you when you least expect it I would be there… CCP slaps a bloody HUNTER across the face. CCP: SPICE says hello… CCP states as CCP stands back up. CCP picks up HUNTER holding him by his face. CCP: Go to hell! CCP comes forward sending HUNTER smashing face first through the passenger side window! Inside the arena the crowd is completely taken by shock as CHRIS PAGE isn’t even suppose to be in the building. CCP looks over his handy work before entering the arena with the lead pipe in his hand. The scene then cuts to JENSEN KENT sitting in his office talking on the phone to someone.
JENSEN: Yes. I appreciate your time and look forward to seeing you next week. JENSEN then looks into the camera after he hangs up his phone. JENSEN: Is that on? CAMERAMAN: Yes sir. JENSEN: Good. Patch me through to the arena. I have an announcement to make. The XTRON goes on as the crowd begins to boo the GM. JENSEN: Yes, yes, go on and boo me if you want but I have an announcement to make. Next week on MASSACRE, I have invited several superstars from both ANARCHY and IMPACT. They will all get what I like to call a shot at greatness. Each one of these men will compete in a match that I have selected. The one who stands out and shines will be given a contract to MASSACRE. The losers? Well, I might just give them another chance depending on how they perform. But at least, they get a shot at being on the number one show in the XWF. So ladies and gentlemen, those men are….JUSTIN KADE, TRAVIS THOMAS, and PETER GILMOUR! These three men have been contacted and will be scheduled to appear on Monday in a triple threat steel cage match. The winner will be the one that climbs out of that cage first. So with that said, I hope you all enjoy the show tonight and I also hope you are looking forward to probably one of THE best pay per views in a long time. PURPLE HAZE!
The XTRON goes off as the cameras go to ringside for the first match of the night. HUNTER RYAN vs. KIMEE MOOKS vs. DOWNFALL - - Standard Match - - With HUNTER RYAN out of commission KIMEE MOOKS heads down to ringside to wait for DOWNFALL. As KIMEE gets in the ring, he turns around to look toward the ramp entrance where he waits for DOWNFALL. Someone makes their way out of the back as KIMEE gets warmed up but it isn’t DOWNFALL! It looks like some guy dressed up as DOWNFALL. Out of nowhere, DOWNFALL comes in the ring from behind MOOKS and hits him with a hard elbow to the back of the head knocking him down. He then applies the DRAGON’S FANG! KIMEE tries to fight out of it but has no choice but to tap out….This one ends before it even began
WINNER: DOWNFALL The camera cuts to the back where we see JENSEN KENT about to sit down at his desk. He is talking to two security men.
JENSEN: Nobody gets past the two of you, got it? SECURITY MAN: Got it, MR. KENT. JENSEN: If there's someone in this arena I want to talk to, I'll have you go find them...but otherwise...no one gets in!
The security men leave the office, presumably to guard the door. JENSEN sits down at his desk. He reaches for his phone and the room suddenly goes dark. We hear KENT’S voice.
JENSEN: Uh-oh. The light comes on a few seconds later. JENSEN is lying on the floor, blood leaking from his eyes and mouth. The blood is collecting into several pools, each pool in the shape of a letter. The pools appear to spell out 'RAGE'. The scene fades out. We then cut somewhere backstage where we see CHRIS PAGE; lead pipe in hand walking towards a door marked HARLEY VICE. CCP kicks the door open catching HARLEY by surprise. CCP takes HARLEY down with a double leg takedown and begins to drive right hands into the skull of HARLEY. CCP stands to his feet cracking VICE across the back of his neck with a shot from the lead pipe. CCP picks up VICE and throws him through his locker room door into the hallway smashing into the wall. CCP comes forward taking a boot to the midsection from VICE followed by several right hands from VICE who attempts to defend himself from the CCP attack. VICE yanks the pipe away from CCP. VICE swings at CCP, CCP ducks and catches VICE with a low blow! Wrestlers and crew members a like watch on as CCP takes VICE through the gorilla position to the top of the ramp hammering VICE with a straight right hand knocking him back several feet.
CCP: I DID THIS TO YOU!! CCP yells as he comes forward blasting VICE across the skull knocking him off the ramp over 15 feet to the concrete floor!! The crowd is shocked yet again as CCP walks over to the side of the ramp peering over spitting down on a motionless HARLEY VICE. CCP turns around and begins to walk down to the ring where he rolls in under the bottom rope demanding a microphone which he’s given.
CCP: CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE HAS OFFICIALLY SNAPPED! CCP pauses catching his breath. CCP: I told the world that it would only be a matter of TIME before I exacted revenge on HARLEY and HUNTER… tonight they got what they had coming to em’ and it’s only the beginning of the destruction that I’m going to start to cause a weekly F*****G basis!
CCP seriously states. CCP: I’m going to get a couple of things out of the way… last weekend I stated I was granting a huge opportunity to one member of the XWF Roster to participate in the United States Championship match at PURPLE HAZE… the man who stepped to the plate first was a surprise to even me. That man is JASON CHILL! So now it WILL be CHILL vs TRENT vs KAIN vs shall I say HUNTER… that is if HUNTER is even medically cleared to compete…
CCP pauses for a brief second. CCP: As Purple Haze draws near the time that Destiny is finally fulfilled comes closer and closer. This SHAM the World Title scene has found itself in will FINALLY come to an end because I GUARANTEE that I will leave the Toyota Center as your new WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION.
The crowd lashes out with boo’s towards CCP as he continues. CCP: VILE and HAIN better be ready… they had better do their homework, they better study the films… they better prepare. I want them at their best because when I BEAT THEM and end all this personal bullshit once and for all they won’t have any excuse, other than they were out classed by a superior talent.
A “YOU SUCK” chant echo’s throughout the building. CCP stares intently towards the camera. CCP: And JON BROWN I want you to heed my warning. You’re banned from Purple Haze, and if you ignore this very crucial rule as GOD as my witness I will beat you back to the sixties. YOU WILL NOT stand in my way from TAKING what rightfully belongs to ME!
C.C.P. stands over a bleeding and beaten HUNTER RYAN his fist raised in triumph as the fans boo. Once again the lights go out. RAGE’s voice fills the arena over the PA.
RAGE: Yea though I walk through the valley of the shadow of Death, I shall fear no evil, for I am the Darkest son of a bitch in the whole valley!
As RAGE’s voice is heard on the PA, his face appears on the XTRON. Suddenly, a bolt of electricity flies from the XTRON directly into the chest of CCP! The lights come up as RAGE’s face fades off the XTRON and CCP lies twitching in the center of the ring! Paramedics and security run to the ring and immediately begin to load PAGE on a stretcher. The crowd watches in stunned silence, not believing what they have just witnessed.
THE “X EFFECT” BRIAN CADY vs. DOUBLE G - - Falls Count Anywhere - - Backstage, we see DOUBLE G walking around, looking for THE “X” EFFECT. Out of nowhere, BRIAN CADY jumps out and hits a double axe handle on DOUBLE G, knocking him down. He starts to stomp away but DOUBLE G grabs one of BRIAN’S legs and takes him off his feet. DOUBLE G is now up on his feet and picks up CADY by the neck. He lifts him up off his feet and tosses him through some double doors that lead to the parking lot. CADY is down and out and DOUBLE G goes for the cover….1……2….3! And this one is over! DOUBLE G then walks away as the ref checks on CADY to make sure he's ok.
WINNER: DOUBLE G The lights suddenly go out and bright white lights shine throughout the arena. “No More Words” by Endeverafter begins to play on the PA. DOUBLE G then emerges from the entranceway and the fans are to their feet. He is still wearing his black Young Guns t-shirt and jeans. He walks up the ramp and high fives some of the fans. He makes it to the ring and calls for the mic. He waits several moments before talking.
GG: Hellooooooooo Buffalo!! The fans erupt and are to their feet again. GG: Now, from what you just saw. I made short work of BRIAN CADY! And in just six days you will be looking at the new XWF True Violence Television Champion. I will be facing none other than JAYMZ DANTE. This is a man that I do respect. However what I do to him at PURPLE HAZE will be nothing personal, just business. So I could come out here and give some fancy speech about how I will beat you this Sunday, but quite frankly I don’t really have the time. I just simply wanted address my match this Sunday. JAYMZ, l hope you’re ready because you will be in for the fight of your life.
DOUBLE G suddenly walks and stands right in the middle of the ring. He looks up and appears to be collecting his thoughts.
GG: BECAUSE I’M SIMPLY…….. DOUBLE G holds the mic out and you can hear the fans say “BETTER THAN YOU!!” Then DOUBLE G drops the mic and rolls out of the ring. He walks back up the ramp as “No More Words” by Endeverafter plays again on the PA. The music then stops and the lights in the entire arena go dark. The XTRON lights up and on the screen we see the words….I AM ETERNAL…..I AM FEAR…..I AM VILE! Master of Puppets begins to play as we see FAMINE OF THE VILE on the XTRON with VIOLET at his side. The music dies down as FAMINE begins to speak.
FAMINE: So, CHRIS PAGE seems to have lost his mind a little. Welcome to the club CHRIS. You know something? Those actions seem to me like a man who is afraid he will lose so he will go out and kick someones ass to prove a point. I don’t need to prove anything CHRIS. I have already proven that things are not like they used to be. Sunday night, you and I will step into that ring one more time. SAM HAIN might be involved in this match but I could care less if he shows up or not. Because once and for all, we are going to end this and there WILL be a clear winner. The rise and fall of CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE, begins this Sunday! I hope you’re ready CHRIS. This would be a good time for you to get on your knees and pray!
The XTRON fades to black as the camera cuts to the ring for the main event. MAIN EVENT RANMA SAOTOME vs. DRAKE KOMODO - - Non-Title Match - - The bell rings and both men go towards the center of the ring. DRAKE and RANMA stare each other down. They aren’t even blinking at each other. The two of them get in close and decide to lock up. No one gets the advantage for the first couple of seconds. Then out of nowhere DRAKE gets the advantage and pushes RANMA to the corner. The ref comes in and tells DRAKE to break it up. He starts a five count. ONE… TWO… THREE… FOUR… DRAKE lets go and they go back to the center of the ring. The two of them lock up and this time RANMA gets the advantage. RANMA pushes DRAKE into the corner and the ref starts another five count. ONE… TWO… THREE… DRAKE lights up RANMA’s chest with chops. FOUR…DRAKE takes RANMA out of the corner and knocks him down. He then takes RANMA’s ankle and slams his knee into it. He then yanks RANMA’s leg and does the same thing. DRAKE starts to stomp away at the knee of the champ. DRAKE drapes the leg of the champ over the bottom rope. DRAKE leaps and drops his weight across the left leg on the champ. DRAKE does this one more time as he is trying to weaken the leg of the champ. DRAKE tries to do it one more time, but RANMA rolls out of the way and DRAKE lands on his butt. RANMA gets up and out of nowhere hits the SAOTOME SERANADE!! He immediately goes for the pin. ONE… TWO… THREE… RANMA continues his winning streak and this one is over.
WINNER: RANMA SAOTOME RANMA stands in the ring, his hand raised in victory. Suddenly, the lights go out and ‘Crush ‘Em’ plays on the arena speakers. RAGE’s masked face appears on the X-tron. Just as soon as it began it ends. The lights come up, the music fades and the X-tron goes black.
RANMA lies in the ring, apparently unconscious. Paramedics come to the ring and within a few seconds, he is up and walking under his own power, with a cheer from the fans. He looks a little confused at first, but heads up the ramp looking around as if trying to find someone.
Fade to black...
