XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Card

XWF Classic Match 2: Hardcore Match: Honkey Lighthouse against Famine of the Vile, ending with a new Television Champion

c. 25 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MAIN EVENT HONKEY LIGHTHOUSE vs. FAMINE OF THE VILE - - Hardcore Match - - HONKEY stands in the ring awaiting FAMINE. Then, FAMINE's theme music plays and FAMINE makes his way down the ring in front of juiced crowd. FAMINE climbs the apron and enters the ring. He stops and stares into HONKEY's eyes. HONKEY stares right back and they enter a stare down. Both these two evenly matched. They exchange a few words with each other. HONKEY smiles at a comment from FAMINE then throws a quick punch. Here they go! FAMINE fires back with a right hand. Then HONKEY again. The two of them engage in a fistfight. HONKEY grabs FAMINE and turns it a side headlock. FAMINE falls back into the ropes and pushes HONKEY off. HONKEY is whipped to the other end. HONKEY comes back and knocks FAMINE down with a shoulder block. HONKEY runs over to the ropes and FAMINE gets to his feet and leaps over him. HONKEY hits the other end of the ropes and back. FAMINE leaps again but HONKEY catches him and hits a massive powerbomb, planting FAMINE! FAMINE holds his back and rolls out of the ring to collect his thoughts. HONKEY stands in the middle of the ring watching FAMINE walk around, taunting him to get in. FAMINE is ready to get back in and climbs the steps. HONKEY walks over to pull him in but FAMINE rams his head into HONKEY's stomach, and again. HONKEY loses his wind and FAMINE grabs him by the head and slams his face into a turnbuckle. With HONKEY dazed FAMINE climbs the turnbuckle and leaps off hitting a missile drop kick taking down HONKEY. FAMINE begins stomping HONKEY. He picks him and whips him to the ropes. HONKEY follows back and FAMINE hits a spinning head scissor. FAMINE, keeping momentum hits the ropes and hits a quick falling drop kick to HONKEY's head. He goes for the cover.1.. 2.. Kickout! FAMINE picks HONKEY up and throws him to the turnbuckle and begins kicking HONKEY in the gut. FAMINE hits HONKEY with a few knife-edge chops. The crowd shouts “Whooooo!” as they hear each chop. FAMINE grabs HONKEY by the hand and tries to Irish Whip him to the other end. HONKEY reverses and catches FAMINE with a short arm clothesline taking FAMINE inside out! HONKEY recuperates and stomps on FAMINE. FAMINE tries to roll out of the ring but HONKEY won't allow it. He grabs FAMINE and hauls him up. HONKEY hits a quick backbreaker on FAMINE, continuing to work on his back. FAMINE holds his back in pain and HONKEY climbs to the second rope. BAM! HONKEY hits a flying knee drop to FAMINE's back. Here's the cover! 1....2...Kickout! HONKEY grabs FAMINE and picks him up. He punches FAMINE in the face a few times before setting him up. HONKEY tries to hit a power bomb on FAMINE but FAMINE reveres into a Frankensteiner! HONKEY is drilled! FAMINE goes for a cover! 1...2.. thr--.Kickout by HONKEY! FAMINE is somewhat surprised by HONKEY's resiliency. He picks HONKEY up and tosses him to the outside of the ring. HONKEY flops on the outside on the mat. FAMINE climbs a nearby turnbuckle and measures HONKEY on the ground. FAMINE LEAPS OFF THE TURNBUCKLE AND SCORES WITH A BIG SPLASH! FAMINE and HONKEY lie on the outside mats in pain. FAMINE grabs the apron and finds his way up to his feet. HONKEY grabs the announce table stumbling up as well. FAMINE turns around and makes a desperate charge towards HONKEY. HONKEY counters with a back body drop onto the table! The table doesn't break though. FAMINE lies on the table in even more pain. HONKEY turns around and gets on the table, picking FAMINE up as well. Each of them stand on the announce table as SCOTT YOUNG steps away, and HONKEY begins to set FAMINE up. HONKEY gets him from below, but SCOTT YOUNG grabs his leg and pulls it off the table! HONKEY straddles the table, crushing his groin on a monitor! OH MY GOD!! SCOTT YOUNG picks up a monitor and SLAMS HONKEY across the skull! SCOTT YOUNG and FAMINE start working HONKEY over, and they both toss him into the ring, allowing FAMINE the easy cover! 1... 2... THREE!! NEW TV CHAMP on a SCREW JOB!! Damn that SCOTT YOUNG!
