Thursday Night Anarchy card (Miami, September 2009)

c. 21 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Coming to you LIVE! From the American Airlines Arena in Miami, FL! MATCHES: Q.C. THUG vs. PETER GILMOUR - - Standard Match - - PREVIEW: Last week Peter Gilmour lost his match to Dr. Emo and was beaten like a Mexican piñata. This week he has a chance to redeem himself by taking on the returning QC Thug who has fire in his eyes and is ready to step up once again.
KEVIN JEWERT vs. LEGION - - Standard Match - - PREVIEW: Last week Jewert proved he had a pair when he outlasted two other men in the fatal four way until he was beaten by Alyssa Ferro. This week Kevin gets a shot at the returning enigma known as Legion. Will the returning Legion take down Kevin Jewert or will he return be for naught?
HADES vs. DARK KAMEN - - X-Treme Rules Match - - PREVIEW: Two former Impact stars face off as Jensen Kent has arranged for these two to be on Anarchy and show him what they can do. Who will come out on top as they fight for a chance to be recognized by the big boys?
MISS TERRY vs. C.H.A.D. - - Inter-Gender Standard Match - - PREVIEW: Last week, C.H.A.D. proved he is definitely clever and took out his mystery opponent in a dangerous way. Later in the show it was revealed that it was actually Miss Terry he faced in the ring! After the locker room attack by Miss Terry, will C.H.A.D. once again prove he is a force to be reckoned with?
MAIN EVENT RANMA SAOTOME & HUNTER RYAN vs. FAMINE OF THE VILE & JAMES RAVEN - - Standard Match - - PREVIEW: The Universal champion and the number one contender for his title are once again in the main event spotlight. This time however, they are without their partners. In a bold yet strange move, returning Anarchy GM Jensen Kent has decided to stick them with new partners for one night. Will the team of Raven & Vile be able to take down the top two men in the company?
