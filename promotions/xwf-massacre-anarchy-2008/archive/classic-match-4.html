XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Card

XWF Classic Match 4: Standard Match: Famine of the Vile against Killjoy

c. 25 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MAIN EVENT FAMINE OF THE VILE vs. KILLJOY - - Standard Match - - And here we go the match is under way and FAMINE puts his hand up and challenging KILLJOY to a test of strength. KILLJOY doesn’t buy into it and drop kicks FAMINE in the knee taking the big man down. Immediately KILLJOY begins his assault, punches and elbows to the face. KILLJOY is trying to keep FAMINE from getting to his feet. He knows that the heavyweight will be tough to beat if he doesn’t keep him down. KILLJOY goes for a drop kick to the face but FAMINE swats him away and has enough time to get back to a vertical base. Now FAMINE runs toward KILLJOY with a clothesline but KILLJOY ducks under and hits FAMINE with a kick to the gut. Now KILLJOY runs to the ropes and comes back with a flying forearm but gets caught by FAMINE who hits a powerful last call throwing KILLJOY all the way to the outside of the ring. FAMINE now gets up and goes to the outside to look for KILLJOY but he is not there. FAMINE looks around and doesn’t see that KILLJOY went under the ring to the other side and is now back in the ring. KILLJOY runs toward FAMINE and hits a baseball slide sending FAMINE to the barrier. Now KILLJOY goes to the turnbuckle and jumps off but gets caught in mid air with a choke hold. OH!! FAMINE just slammed him onto the concrete with a huge chokeslam. FAMINE picks him up wasting no time and tosses him in the ring. He climbs up and hits a hard elbow drop to the sternum of KILLJOY. KILLJOY clutches his chest as FAMINE stands there laughing and looking at his prey. He goes for another elbow drop and KILLJOY moves out of the way. FAMINE is on the ground and now KILLJOY hits a drop kick to the head of FAMINE. Now he runs to the ropes and comes back with his own version of rolling thunder and hits FAMINE in the face. FAMINE starts to bleed from the nose. He looks at the blood and is now furious. But here comes KILLJOY again, this time he hits FAMINE with an elbow to the back of the head. KILLJOY has really shown that he is not afraid of the evil one. KILLJOY now stands over FAMINE to try and lift him up but FAMINE grabs the tights and sends KILLJOY face first into the turnbuckle. Now FAMINE is back on his feet and hits a hard elbow to the back, another! And another! KILLJOY is reeling in pain holding his back. FAMINE now puts him up on the turnbuckle and hits a huge backdrop! KILLJOY is down as FAMINE now goes for the pin. ONE….2……Kickout! KILLJOY looks to still be in this thing and doesn’t look like he is going to give up. FAMINE is now stomping on him, kicks to the chest and head over and over. Now FAMINE picks him up and sets him up for a power bomb. WAIT! KILLJOY hits a hurricanrana sending the big man down. FAMINE is just laying there holding his head as KILLJOY goes to the top rope and hits a splash moonsault! ONE…..2……Kickout! KILLJOY looks exhausted but does not want to quit, he came here for a reason and that was to beat his opponent. KILLJOY picks FAMINE up by the head now…WOW!!! He just hit a giant suplex! But he is not holding his back as the move took a lot out of him. Both men are laying there hurt. They both look like they are trying to get back to their feet. KILLJOY runs to the ropes again only to be hit with a boot to the jaw by FAMINE. KILLJOY is down again as FAMINE begins to choke him, the ref starts to count ONE….2…..3……4…FAMINE lets go of the chokehold as the ref warns FAMINE that he will be disqualified if he does that again. FAMINE now gets back to KILLJOY and hits him with a leg drop. Now he is back on his feet signaling to the crowd that it’s over. FAMINE picks up KILLJOY and sets him up for the End of Days. WAIT! KILLJOY gets out of the move but hits the ref in the process. The ref is now out. KILLJOY takes advantage and hits FAMINE with a low blow sending the man to his knees and down to the mat holding his groin. KILLJOY looks at the crowd smiling as they cheer for him and chant his name. FAMINE is still holding himself and…wait, he looks like he’s taking something out of his tights. KILLJOY doesn’t see him. Now KILLJOY runs to the ropes and performs his own version of the lionsault. He lands on FAMINE and is covering him but the ref is still out.
KILLJOY now gets up to get the ref but wait. What is that? OH MY GOD!! KILLJOY is bleeding! He’s bleeding from the stomach. FAMINE just threw something out of the ring, what was it? KILLJOY now looks down realizing what everyone is looking at. He sees the blood pouring out and has a look of shock in his face. FAMINE is now behind him as the ref is now coming to. BOOM!!! FAMINE just hit "The Blood Driver" and KILLJOY is out. As blood pours to the mat, and FAMINE is out onto the mat also, as that move took a lot out of him. The referee looks to be stirring, as from the entrance TRENT GEIN walks to the ring, as he slams his fist to the ring, as suddenly the sound gives KILLJOY a shocking as his arcs his stomach, from the pool of blood. KILLJOY drops into the pool, as KILLJOY sits up, and his eyes, that look was seen last week! KILLJOY is on fire! KILLJOY stands up and FAMINE stands up as KILLJOY knocks FAMINE down to the mat with a dropkick, KILLJOY lifts up FAMINE to his feet, and "LAST LAUGH!" KILLJOY is walking around the ring, and TRENT GEIN is smiling, at his CREATION! TRENT GEIN points to the fallen FAMINE OF THE VILE, as TRENT GEIN gives a sign of the "Punchline!" KILLJOY picks up FAMINE and hits the "PUNCHLINE!" KILLJOY falls across the body of FAMINE, and the referee comes to life as he fumbles about to the two bodies, and begins to count, ONE…TWO … SCOTT YOUNG IS HERE!! He grabs KILLJOY’s leg and yanks him out of the ring on the other side from TRENT! SCOTT sees the damage already done to KILLJOY, and drops him, almost mercifully, with a DDT on the concrete! KILLJOY is out, and TRENT is running to the other side of the ring to stop YOUNG! YOUNG picks up KILLJOY and tosses him into the ring as TRENT tackles SCOTT from behind! SCOTT and TRENT are wailing on each other!
In the ring, a nearly unconscious KILLJOY is lying on the canvas, and FAMINE puts his boot on the fallen tag champ’s chest. 1.. 2.. THREE! EMT’s rush out to help KILLJOY as FAMINE and SCOTT leave TRENT to tend to his fallen partner.
Winner: FAMINE OF THE VILE Before going to commercial, we get a shot of the knife FAMINE used to stab KILLJOY in the stomach as the blood is all over the stainless steel blade.
