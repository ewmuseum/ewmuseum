The camera pans all over the arena floor and the second and third tiers as the show begins with pyro blasting from the ramp entrance, the XTRON and the rafters. GILMOUR sits up, holding the side of his face, when JUSTIN reaches down and pulls him back too his feet and nails GILMOUR with a knee to the mid-section before hooking up his head and neck and dropping him to the mat with a DDT. JUSTIN rolls GILMOUR onto his back and hooks the near leg, One... Two... GILMOUR shoots the shoulder out at two. JUSTIN pulls himself up using the ropes and waits as GILMOUR slowly attempts to get too his feet. As he finally makes it up, JUSTIN runs at GILMOUR again but, this time, GILMOUR is ready for it and counters the move into a nice belly to belly suplex. After a second or two of try to catch his breath, GILMOUR quickly floats up and over into a pin attempt, hooking up the far leg, One... Two... JUSTIN kicks out before three. GILMOUR climbs to his feet using the ring ropes and watches JUSTIN doing the same. As JUSTIN is now to a knee, GILMOUR charges at him, attempting a Shining Wizard, but JUSTIN is smart. Frustration is clearly beginning to settle in as JUSTIN'S looks down at the fallen GILMOUR, who is slowly rolling over onto his stomach as he tries to get too his feet. JUSTIN grabs GILMOUR by the neck and forcefully tosses him up and over the top rope, GILMOUR'S limp body crashes right down onto the concrete floor. JUSTIN, ever the high-flyer, quickly ascends to the top rope as GILMOUR pushes himself up using the barricade at ringside. JUSTIN leaps off with a Double-Axe Handle attempt but GILMOUR uses JUSTIN'S momentum to drive him, throat first, down onto the barricade!! JUSTIN falls the canvas, clutching his throat and coughing out in pain, as GILMOUR uses the barricade to keep himself up. GILMOUR quickly pulls JUSTIN up and spears him right into the barricade, using the area outside of the ring as a weapon to try and inflict more pain onto an already hurt JUSTIN. GILMOUR pulls JUSTIN across and rolls him back into the ring underneath the bottom rope before sliding back into the ring himself. GILMOUR drags JUSTIN away from the ropes and into the middle of the ring before going for the cover, One... Two.... THRE-KICKOUT!! JUSTIN somehow manages to kick out before the three!! GILMOUR looks shocked and pissed all at the same time as he climbs to his feet and almost begs the referee to declare him the winner by saying it was a three count. He does not get his wish as the official calls a two. GILMOUR stomps the mat in anger before turning to JUSTIN and pulling him up once again. He lifts JUSTIN onto his feet and then slaps him hard across the face before lifting him onto his shoulders and calling for the end. JUSTIN manages to get out of the hold and immediately applies The Shock Collar! GILMOUR is trying to break free. He tries to reach for the ropes so that JUSTIN can release the hold but not luck. GILMOUR has no choice but to tap! This one is over folks.
