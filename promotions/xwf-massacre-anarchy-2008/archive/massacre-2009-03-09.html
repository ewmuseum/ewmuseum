XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Card

Monday Massacre (9 March 2009)

9 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FIREFOX and MOZILLA are NOT RECOMMENDED for viewing this program. MATCHES: SCOTT CHARLOTTE & DOUBLE G vs. HUNTER RYAN & “X EFFECT” BRIAN CADY - - Standard Tag Match - - KIMEE MOOKS vs. RAGE vs. DOWNFALL - - Pin To Win - - MAIN EVENT CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE vs. RANMA SAOTOME - - Standard Match - - As the XWF logo appears on the screen, One by One by Alter Bridge begins to play. General Manager JENSEN KENT makes his way out to the arena to a roar of boos from the crowd. He stops at the ramp entrance and put the mic to his lips.
JENSEN: I cannot believe this! You’re booing me after that horrible show last week? You people have no taste. I mean come on. RAGE turned my MASSACRE into an absolutely atrocious display of nothing but ROAD CREW CRAP!!! But fortunately, that will never happen again and I am now back in charge. Tonight, we will bring back MASSACRE the way it’s been done since I took over. Tonight…….
The lights in the arena go pitch black. “Zero” by Smashing Pumpkins begins to play as green and white strobe lights begin to flicker throughout the arena. A single spotlight hits the top of the ramp. Smog begins to rise at the top of the ramp. Walking out through the smoky haze is none other than CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE! The crowd roar with boos as CHRIS PAGE is decked to the nines in a very expensive looking suit. He is wearing black with red stripes and a red neck tie. CCP points at several signs displayed by the fans as he reaches the ringside area. CCP now enters the ring as JENSEN walks over to shake his hand. The music slowly fades away.
JENSEN: Ladies and Gentleman I present to you the UNCROWNED CHAMPION… The boos begin to go insane as KENT continues. JENSEN: and the man who WILL DEFEAT RANMA SAOTOME right here tonight… The boos grow even louder. JENSEN: Put your hands together for CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE! JENSEN hands the microphone off to CCP to the chorus of boos from the crowd. CCP removes his black Oakley shades placing them in the top pocket of his sport coat. The crowd starts a moderate “CCP Sucks” chant as CCP begins to address the audience.
PAGE: So I guess tonight is the night eh Tampa! They cater with a cheap pop. PAGE: Tonight you fans are in for a treat of a lifetime… not only will you have the distinct honor and privilege of feasting your eyes on this ring later tonight to watch me captivate and thrill you like you’ve never seen…
CCP states to boos from the crowd. PAGE: Oh yeah it’s gunna happen! CCP states to the crowd. PAGE: You have the opportunity to watch as I show the world just BEATABLE RANMA truly is… but… but, but, but… You are also the town I’ve selected to make an important announcement regarding Purple Haze slated to take place MARCH 29th.
The crowd begins to quiet down. PAGE: The first announcement I would like to make revolves are the XWF CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION KIMEE MOOKS…
The crowd garner a mix reaction as KIMEE is mentioned. PAGE: Since there isn’t a lengthy list of credible challengers for your title I’ve decided to lob you a softball… I went to my list of challengers and went with a man who just recently managed to jump off Impact and too the sinking ship that is ANARCHY. He hasn’t attained much out of his XWF career, but maybe this is his time. At Purple Haze KIMEE MOOKS will meet Q.C. THUG for the CRUSIERWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP in a match that will be deemed CCP’S CHOICE. You gentleman have fun because the stipulation for your match WILL NOT be revealed until you’re each in the squared circle and the bell is ready to ring. Your fates rest firmly in the palm of my hands.
CCP states towards the camera as a slight buzz about his selection filters throughout the arena.
PAGE: Which brings me to the WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP… As that is stated it’s like the crowd shuts up! They eagerly anticipate who is CCP selecting for the World title?
PAGE: At first when I thought about this match I was thinking multiple party affair… but after what transpired last week between SPICE ONE and myself I changed my mind.
Changed his mind? PAGE: Over the last week I’ve had PLENTY to say in regards to many topics. SPICE wants this to be about business… so be it. What’s best for business is for the WORLD TITLE to be around the waist of CHRIS PAGE…
BOO’s echo throughout the arena. PAGE: SPICE I always told you if I wanted a shot I would come too you man to man. Well my friend, this is me coming to you. At PURPLE HAZE the CANNABIANS will battle again!
Suddenly the roof explodes! PAGE: That’s right folks… CCP vs SPICE ONE for the WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP! The cheers grow louder. PAGE: I’m not going to hold any advantage over you… Unlike everyone else who will have to WAIT until show time before they find out what match they’ll compete in… I’m going to tell you so you can get the early footwork in that you’ll desperately need.
CCP pauses. PAGE: I’ve decided that I’m going to BACK UP exactly what I profess to be… one of which is the greatest WRESTLER alive today. I’ve decided to take this one OLD SCHOOL. See I often remember back some time ago there used to be a set of rules… CANADIAN RULES!
WHAT?! PAGE: PURE WRESTLING! The crowd comes alive as JENSEN KENT is in shock. PAGE: And SPICE… you’re not going to have ANYTHING or ANYONE to save you from the beating I’m going to give you… nothing personal, STRICKLY BUSINESS.
CCP glances over towards JENSEN with a very intense look on his face before turning back towards the camera.
PAGE: Which brings me full circle to the UNIVERSAL CHAMPIONSHIP! The loudest pop by far is heard as CCP mentions the UNIVERSAL Title. PAGE: This is a spot that I desperately wanted, but was HELD BACK from getting… I’ve spent the better part of two weeks reaching out to current and past superstars and legends to challenge RANMA on my platform… my decision might actually be VERY CONTROVERSIAL consider recent events, however the more and more I thought about it and I thought about the destruction and devastation you’re each going to have to put your bodies through in order to survive and walk out of PURPLE HAZE as the Champion will be nothing but entertain me.
CCP pauses. PAGE: I’ve spoken with JEM WILLIAMS, LEE STONE, SMITTY and STONE but my choice wasn’t finalized until last night. This is it RANMA, this is the moment you’ve been waiting for… FANS are you READY?
The crowd roars. CCP turns towards JENSEN. PAGE: JESEN KENT are you READY? JENSEN nods his head forward… PAGE: It’s…. PAGE: …. IT’S PAGE: IT’S! PAGE: RAGE! The building erupts into a thunderous ovation as RAGE is announced to challenge RANMA for the UNIVERSAL CHAMPIONSHIP at PURPLE HAZE.
PAGE: BUT… but…. but…. The crowd calms down. PAGE: I know it’s rather shocking isn’t it? But I haven’t gotten to the really fun part yet. You boys aren’t going to have to wait to find out what kind of horror I’ve got en-store for you either, because your match will be THREE STAGES OF HELL!
A hush comes over the audience. PAGE: What you will wait on… Are the Stages themselves. You won’t know what to prepare for because the first Stage won’t be announced until the open bell is ready to toll. Good luck boys. I’ll be watching with a smile on my face!
CCP tosses the microphone to the mat as “Zero” by Smashing Pumpkins begins to play once again. What a turn of a events! KIMEE MOOKS to defend the Cruiserweight Championship against QC THUG, CCP vs SPICE ONE under CANADIAN RULES for the WORLD TITLE, but the granddaddy of them all is THREE STAGE’S of HELL for the UNIVERSAL CHAMPIONSHIP pitting RANMA SAOTOME against RAGE! What a card PURPLE HAZE is shaping up to be!
SCOTT CHARLOTTE & DOUBLE G vs. HUNTER RYAN & “X EFFECT” BRIAN CADY The match is ready to get under way as THE YOUNG GUNS are already in the ring, waiting for ROAD CREW members HUNTER RYAN and BRIAN CADY to come out. We Are The Road Crew by Motorhead starts to play as the US Champ HUNTER RYAN makes his way out with his partner BRIAN CADY. Both men make their way down to the roar of the crowd. SCOTT and DOUBLE G look focused as their opponents now get inside the ring. After a few moments, the ref rings calls for the bell. SCOTT and HUNTER are the first two people to start off in the ring. HUNTER and SCOTT try psyching one another out until SCOTT finally just up and slaps HUNTER! Before he knew what hit him, HUNTER bum rushes SCOTT and grabs a hold of his arm only to then Irish whip him across the ring. From bouncing off the ropes and coming back HUNTER hip tosses SCOTT. SCOTT quickly gets back up again and charges at HUNTER only to get hip tossed again! SCOTT once more gets back up shaking it off and comes charging at HUNTER to this time be hip tossed but HUNTER does not let go this time around. Instead, he flips around SCOTT'S arm and drops down with a standing leg drop across SCOTT'S chest. HUNTER then right away applies an armbar while now down on the mat. SCOTT then struggles not to tap out as he fights his way over to the ropes finally grabbing up the bottom rope at the last. The ref the starts up his 4-count as HUNTER lets go. With SCOTT favoring his arm though, HUNTER quickly walks over grabbing up SCOTT'S hurting arm and spins it around while yanking SCOTT down to one knee. CADY then reaches in and tags himself in as he takes a running start jumping and landing with an elbow down onto the hurting shoulder of SCOTT. CADY then pulls SCOTT up to his feet and hooks him as he nails a perfect suplex. CADY then rolls out of the ring grabbing up one of the steel folding chairs and rolls back into the ring. He drags SCOTT over as he then sets his arm in it. CADY then walks back climbing up to the second rope and looks down laughing at SCOTT mocking him and his pain. He then jumps off while landing and stomping on the steel folding chair! SCOTT screams out in pain as CADY then drops down and makes a cover.1......2..... and DOUBLE G with the save! SCOTT and CADY get up and the match quickly becomes a slugfest as SCOTT and CADY exchange punches in the middle of the ring... The two tie up and fall to the outside of the ring and they are both unrelenting in assaulting each other as they battle up the ramp!
SCOTT and CADY have left the arena floor!! DOUBLE G and HUNTER watch in awe as the two disappear into the backstage area. The ref looks at the two wrestlers still in the ring signaling for them to fight. Both circle the ring, lock up and HUNTER takes down DOUBLE G right away. Both lock up again and DOUBLE G takes down HUNTER and works on his left leg. He gets HUNTER into a leg submission when he flips over. HUNTER breaks out of the submission, both stand up, circle the ring again and HUNTER then takes down DOUBLE G applying a single leg Boston crab. HUNTER takes down DOUBLE G with a quick arm drag and DOUBLE G puts his forearm against HUNTER's face to break it up. HUNTER jumps up, gives DOUBLE G an arm drag and again works on DOUBLE G's right arm. DOUBLE G fights back with some stiff kicks to HUNTER's back. DOUBLE G locks in a sharpshooter but HUNTER counters out of a Sharpshooter attempt by DOUBLE G, but DOUBLE G comes back working on the leg of HUNTER. DOUBLE G gets in a few hard rights and wraps HUNTER's right leg around the ropes. DOUBLE G kicks HUNTER hard in the kneecap. DOUBLE G backs up, charges, but HUNTER moves and DOUBLE G dumps to the outside. HUNTER flips over the top rope, DOUBLE G moves, HUNTER lands on his feet, but DOUBLE G hits a quick kick to the back of the head. HUNTER fights back by throwing DOUBLE G face first off the ring apron followed by a backbreaker.
HUNTER hits a few chest claps to DOUBLE G. HUNTER puts DOUBLE G in the ring, DOUBLE G attempts to take out HUNTER, but HUNTER pulls down the ropes and DOUBLE G falls to the outside. HUNTER springboards off the top rope on DOUBLE G to take him out. Both get back in the ring and HUNTER nails DOUBLE G with a right hand to the head. HUNTER gets in a few more chest chops to DOUBLE G. HUNTER hits a clothesline on DOUBLE G. HUNTER applies a modified twisting figure four on DOUBLE G! DOUBLE G screams out in pain reaching for the bottom rope. DOUBLE G tries to counter out of it, but can't. DOUBLE G somehow gets the bottom rope and HUNTER breaks it up. DOUBLE G hits a knee to the chest on HUNTER in the corner and follows that up with a tornado DDT. DOUBLE G covers, but only gets a two count. DOUBLE G gets in a few forearms to HUNTER, but HUNTER fights back with a roll-up that goes into a leg lock over DOUBLE G's head. HUNTER applies another modified figure four on DOUBLE G. DOUBLE G gets to the ropes quicker this time to break it up. HUNTER hits a release German suplex on DOUBLE G. He then gets up on his feet as the crowd roars and cheers for the US Champ. HUNTER the goes to the top rope and signals for the finisher. DOUBLE G stumbles to his feet and doesn't notice that HUNTER is on the top rope. BOOM!! HUNTERS SPEAR! DOUBLE G is down and HUNTER now makes the cover.....1.....2....3!!!
WINNER: HUNTER RYAN & “X EFFECT” BRIAN CADY HUNTER RYAN is pulling himself up following his match, and is handed his United States championship belt by the ref. HUNTER poses for a second, as DOWNFALL comes rolling into the ring behind him, carrying a steel chair. HUNTER turns around, and spots DOWNFALL, ready with the chair to swing it at HUNTER. HUNTER grips the US title belt in a defensive manner, and DOWNFALL stops himself. He holds out a hand in a "wait a moment" gesture, and then he takes a mic from the back of his pants.
DOWNFALL: Wait just a second, HUNTER... I just wanted to be sure I had your attention. HUNTER yells, "You got it!" DOWNFALL grins. DOWNFALL: Still smarting from the shot I gave you last week, champ? Well, in my mind, it was well deserved, particularly since the last time I was in the ring with you in November, you had the last laugh... but you see, HUNTER, when I was approached with the idea of taking on the ROAD CREW... you were the first one I thought of. And now that I have what I want, a match against HARLEY VICE, your ROAD CREWS co-hort... well it just makes this all the sweeter. I have you here... and I hope HARLEY'S watching.
HUNTER: Trust me, DOWNFALL...I haven't forgotten that cheap shot from last week. As a matter of fact...I haven't forgotten anyone who took the bitch route to make a statement against me. Guys like yourself and CHRIS PAGE. You guys are from the same mold, DOWNFALL. I find it funny, though...how you think getting in the ring with HARLEY is going to satisfy your thirst for me. Taking nothing away from my ROAD CREW brother...but you're staring at the United States Champion! You're picking a fight with one of the big dogs and your ass isn't even in the same yard as the likes of HARLEY and I!
DOWNFALL: Well, props to you, champ, for sharing a glimpse of the view from your ivory tower... but let me clear things up a bit. I don't have a thirst just for you. I have a thirst for X-TREME gold, and seeing as yourself and your buddies, RAGE, CADY and the rest are here on MASSACRE and HARLEY VICE isn't, I've realized that to really stick it to the new champ, I've got to make my statement by taking you all out one by one. It started with you last week -- where I can say with no embellishment that I scrambled your brains to the point that it cost you your shot against the Universal champ... and the road through the ROAD CREW to VICE leads to RAGE tonight. So it will go until Purple Haze, I'll just run through all of you until I get my hands on what I really want.
HUNTER: If it's VICE you want so badly, DOWNFALL...you don't have to wait long. HUNTER waves his finger in the air in a circular motion, signaling DOWNFALL to turn around. DOWNFALL turns, and HARLEY VICE is waiting for him with a boot to the gut and a VICEBREAKER! DOWNFALL rolls out of the ring to the aisle. HARLEY and HUNTER stand in the ring, posing to the crowd. DOWNFALL is laid out in the aisle, trying to shake the cobwebs out, and after a moment he is able to look up and see HARLEY, pointing and shouting at DOWNFALL from the ring. DOWNFALL and HARLEY exchange a few choice words, as DOWNFALL pulls himself up, but rather than get back in the ring DOWNFALL walks up the ramp, snarling at HARLEY...From the back, a ref comes out and tells DOWNFALL he needs to come back down to the ring as his match is next. HUNTER and HARLEY leave the ring as DOWNFALL makes his way back down. HARLEY stares DOWNFALL down and says something before walking up the ramp with HUNTER and out of the arena.
KIMEE MOOKS vs. RAGE vs. DOWNFALL KIMEE MOOKS stands in the ring as ‘The Night’ by Disturbed begins to play. DOWNFALL charges out of the back and into the ring. His music fades and he and MOOKS begin to circle one another. ‘Crush ‘Em’ by Megadeth plays and the camera cuts to the entrance ramp…but there is no sign of RAGE. The crowd begins to cheer and the camera cuts back to the ring just in time to see RAGE come out of the crowd, over the barricade and grab MOOKS ankle from behind. RAGE drags MOOKS out of the ring causing him to hit the floor face first. MOOKS gets to his feet just in time to feel a monster clothesline from RAGE. RAGE hooks up MOOKS in a three quarter front face lock…and hits a Dark Moon DDT that takes him face first into the steps! MOOKS is already bleeding on the outside of the ring! RAGE slides into the ring and charges at DOWNFALL as the ref calls for the bell. DOWNFALL is waiting for RAGE and answers his charge with a Beast Kick…but RAGE slides under the Beast Kick with a baseball slide that takes out DOWNFALL’s other leg and takes him face first to the mat!
RAGE stands up, pulling DOWNFALL to a standing position as well. He bounces DOWNFALL off the ropes with an Irish Whip and is waiting for him with a boot to the stomach…but DOWNFALL leaps over the outstretched leg with a picture perfect corkscrew cross body that takes RAGE off his feet. DOWNFALL hooks the leg, going for the early victory here! 1…2…and RAGE kicks out. RAGE rises to his feet. DOWNFALL charges across the ring and hits the Rising Phoenix to take RAGE over once more. DOWNFALL covers RAGE once more…1…2…and a bloody KIMEE MOOKS interrupts the count with a kick to the back of DOWNFALL’s skull! KIMEE MOOKS is standing, but that cut on his forehead looks horrendous. He pulls DOWNFALL up by his hair and headbutts him, driving him onto his back. RAGE is up as well but MOOKS goes for a thumb to the eyes…and is blocked by the mask! RAGE looks at KIMEE with what feels like an air of amusement. KIMEE lashes out with a boot to the stomach that bends RAGE double and drives RAGE to the mat with a DDT. MOOKS is about to go for a cover on RAGE but sees DOWNFALL rising to his feet. MOOKS switches targets and hooks up DOWNFALL for a leg sweep. Several elbows to MOOKS’ skull from DOWNFALL convince MOOKS to let go. DOWNFALL grabs MOOKS by the arm and slingshots him into the ropes and meets him with a huge snap powerslam. DOWNFALL goes for a cover on MOOKS here…1…2…and MOOKS kicks out!
Boy, DOWNFALL is really giving it his all tonight! He’s showing the world just how bad he wants to win this match. MOOKS tries to rise to his feet once more…only to get taken down by DOWNFALL with a Chimera DDT! DOWNFALL’s not done yet. He takes several steps back and begins to motion for KIMEE to rise. KIMEE slowly gets to his feet. And here comes DOWNFALL with a Legend’s End…and OH MY GOD! RAGE came out of nowhere to spear DOWNFALL right out of the ring! DOWNFALL flew out of the ring like he’d been shot from a cannon and hit the barricade face first! He appears to be bleeding from a cut above his right eye! Back in the ring, RAGE is picking himself up off the mat after that spear…but MOOKS has shaken off the cobwebs and takes him over with a pump handle slam! MOOKS pulls RAGE to his feet and takes him back down with a short arm clothesline. MOOKS follows up with a rolling knee to the head and goes for the cover…1…2…and RAGE pulls his shoulder off the mat. MOOKS pulls him to his feet once more and Irish Whips him into the ropes. MOOKS bounces off the opposite ropes and hits RAGE with a springboard spear that levels him in the center of the ring. MOOKS looks to the crowd and nods and smiles. He pulls RAGE up into a neckbreaker…but RAGE counters it into the Hangman facebuster! Good Lord! RAGE just drove the gashed forehead of MOOKS right into his knee! RAGE pulls MOOKS to his feet and hits him with a boot to the stomach. RAGE grabs MOOKS and flips him into the air and hits him with a devastating hanging power bomb!
This has got to be it here…1…2…thr…and a kick to RAGE’s skull from DOWNFALL interrupts the count! DOWNFALL is back in the ring. RAGE tries to rise after that kick but a leg drop from DOWNFALL takes him right back to the canvas. DOWNFALL stomps on the back of RAGE’s head two times for good measure and then turns his attention back to MOOKS. MOOKS is trying to get to his feet…he seems woozy…perhaps from all the punishment he’s taken or all the blood he’s lost…there is no way to know for sure. MOOKS sees that RAGE is down and turns to DOWNFALL…just in time to catch a Beast Kick to the jaw! DOWNFALL’s not going for the cover…is he nuts? He pulls MOOKS to his feet and gets him up on his shoulders…and hits a massive Dragonslayer! MOOKS has to be out! DOWNFALL covers him! Looks like DOWNFALL might win an upset victory tonight…1…2…and an elbow dropped on DOWNFALL’s skull by RAGE breaks up the count! RAGE pulls DOWNFALL to his feet and then takes off his mask. Missing the mask, RAGE spits blood into DOWNFALL’s eyes just before kicking him in the stomach! RAGE grabs DOWNFALL’s face…and hits another Dark Moon DDT! Good Lord…DOWNFALL caught the full brunt of that. Wait…RAGE is heading to the corner…he climbs to the turnbuckle…oh this isn’t good…RAGE takes to the air…and drives an elbow into DOWNFALL’s sternum! RAGE rises once more…he looks down at DOWNFALL with a crazed look in his eyes. RAGE runs to the other turnbuckle. He climbs to the top of it…and takes flight once more…and BAM! nails the second elbow to DOWNFALL’s sternum! RAGE just hit the ‘Cross-your-heart-hope-you-die!’ and the fans are going nuts! RAGE turns to MOOKS now and pulls him to his feet. RAGE Irish Whips MOOKS into the corner and follows him in with a huge splash! RAGE pulls MOOKS to his feet and drags him up to the top turnbuckle. RAGE hooks him up for an Impaler DDT! RAGE tumbles backwards off the top rope…driving MOOKS face first into the mat! RAGE just hit the Legacy on KIMEE MOOKS. RAGE covers him…1…2…3!
WINNER: RAGE As RAGE gets his arm raised in the air and takes a deep breath bending his knees, the lights start flickering in the whole entire arena. RAGE goes to look behind him and he is no longer seen! WHAT HAPPENED TO THE LIGHTS!?!
SUDDENLY, THE LIGHTS GO ON! Standing in the middle of the ring is XWF WORLD CHAMPION SPICE-ONE ALONG WITH THE MOST CONTROVERSIAL ATHLETE IN THE XWF, CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE!!! PAGE grabs ahold of RAGE by the blonde hair of his head and YANKS him towards him. PAGE tucks RAGE's head under his arm and drops down with RAGE under him with such power and force, also sending the elbow into RAGE's chest on the way down. RAGE grabs ahold of his chest in pain. PAGE pulls RAGE up halfway by his head and then rips him up to his feet. CCP talks some trash under his breath and then BACKHANDS RAGE across the face, over and over and over again!
SPICE-ONE is now motioning for CCP to pull RAGE up again off the ground. RAGE is now trickling blood from his lip and is very discombobulated!! CCP throws RAGE over to the XWF WORLD CHAMPION SPICE-ONE and SPICE HAULS RAGE OVER HIS SHOULDERS AND STARTS JUMPING AROUND THE RING!!! OH MY GOD! OH MY GOD! RAGE IS LOCKED IN THE SPICE-RACK!!!
Meanwhile, CCP walks over and grabs a mic from the time keeper. The fans boo louder than ever as SPICE the MONSTER just keeps jumping up and down with RAGE trapped in the SPICE-RACK, as RAGE's back takes the most damage.
PAGE: WHAT THE HELL DID I TELL YOU, RAGE!!! SPICE-ONE stops jumping so RAGE can hear CCP loud and clear, still locked in the SPICE-RACK!!!
PAGE: YOU TRIED TO BREAK A FRIENDSHIP!! You expected us to act as if we were two animals in the ring, and kill each other. We pretty much came close to it...BUT YOU...IF IT WASN'T FOR YOU, RAGE...I WOULD BE XWF WORLD CHAMPION! SPICE-ONE doesn't KNOW BETTER! He only knows to ATTACK, KILL, and BURY when he steps in this F****NG RING! YOU...BASTARD! NOW, I HOPE SPICE IS BREAKING EVERY BONE IN YOUR BODY! You like the suffering, RAGE? You like how that feels? Where are your ROAD CREW? EXACTLY! The consequences with screwing with THE CANNABIANS are very grave, and you NOW...are OFFICIALLY...on my sh*t list. DROP HIM, SPICE...
SPICE-ONE drops the airless body of RAGE onto the canvas....CCP drapes over him, standing tall...SPICE grabs the microphone from CCP...
SPICE: RAGE, you think you are brilliant? You think you are the next Jensen Kent or some crap? Do you honestly think you are cut out to make matches? NO!! What you are doing is trying to break two friends away from each other and turn us into heartless bastards like YOURSELF! You paid tonight for what you tried pulling. I hope it was all worth it...I hope it was fun while it lasted.
RAGE starts to move slowly on the ground, and then CCP and SPICE-ONE START MUDHOLE STOPPING RAGE DOWN TO THE MAT AGAIN! After a few moment, the men step away from a beaten RAGE..XWF officials then rush the ring as they try to get CCP and SPICE-ONE out of the ring. Just then, JEWEL GREY who is SPICE'S manager runs down to the ring to talk some sense into CCP and SPICE. CCP realizes he has to face RANAMA SAOTOME in five minutes and starts to pull SPICE-ONE to run faster as all three leave the main stage area, running up the ramp and going backstage....RAGE lays in the ring...bloody...and on his back... SPICE and JEWEL reach the top of the ramp with CCP. But CCP turns back towards the ring and shakes his head as EMT's work over RAGE. CCP runs back down to the ring sliding under the bottom rope. He begins to toss the EMT's off RAGE like they're nothing until he reaches down picking up RAGE. CCP then under hooks the arms of RAGE before planting him with the PAGE PLANT. The crowd boos as CCP stands to his feet crotch chopping RAGE before he spits on him. The scene cuts to the back where we see FAMINE OF THE VILE and VIOLET making their way out to the arena. They are suddenly stopped by STEVE SAYORS.
STEVE: Excuse me. FAMINE. FAMINE: What the hell do you want SAYORS? STEVE: I wanted to get your thoughts on what happened to you last week on ANARCHY. FAMINE: Don’t you worry about that SAYORS. KRISTOPHER KAIN will be dealt with on ANARCHY. This however is MASSACRE. And tonight, I am here to announce who I will challenge for my shot at the title.
STEVE: Which title? FAMINE: You’ll see… FAMINE and VIOLET walk away leaving SAYORS standing there as the show cuts to a commercial.
When the show returns, Master of Puppets by Metallica begins to play as FAMINE OF THE VILE and VIOLET make their way down to ringside. The crowd cheers for the Demon King who is holding his lady love’s hand as they now get to ringside. FAMINE goes up first and holds the ropes open so that VIOLET can get in the ring. Once he is inside, he asks for a mic as the music dies down. Chants of Demon King fill the air as FAMINE gives them an evil smirk before he begins to speak.
FAMINE: You know something? SCOTT CHARLOTTE and DOUBLE G have a lot of balls to step up to a man of my caliber. They make jokes and try to humiliate me and VIOLET. But you know what? It’s boring! It’s over done. And it’s pathetic. You guys are nothing more than a bunch of Initiative wanna-be’s! Yeah I said it! You and GARY are trying to be what NICK and I used to be. You’re trying to do what we used to do. Let me give you some advice. If you want to make your mark in history, don’t try to do what others have done. It doesn’t work.
