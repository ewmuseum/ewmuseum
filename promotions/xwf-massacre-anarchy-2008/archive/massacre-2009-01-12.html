XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Card

Monday Massacre (12 January 2009)

12 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FIREFOX and MOZILLA are NOT RECOMMENDED for viewing this program. MATCHES: "Iceman" DEAN JAMES vs. HUNTER RYAN - - International Title Round 1 - - 2-RP LIMIT "Chronic" CHRIS PAGE vs. "X Effect" BRIAN CADY vs. SCOTT CHARLOTTE - - Pin To Win - - MAIN EVENT SPICE ONE vs. FAMINE OF THE VILE - - Last Man Standing - - MONDAY NIGHT MASSACRE opens as the nothing but overwhelming boos from the crowd is heard as we see MASSACRE GM JENSEN KENT and CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE standing in the ring, each man with a smile on there face. JENSEN has a microphone in hand and begins to address the masses.
JENSEN: You know. If there is one thing I hate to do is, I hate to say I told you so. BUT…I told you so!
The boos grow louder and louder JENSEN: For the better part of a week I’ve heard nothing but bitching and complaining because I went out and had the balls to bring in a man who I know will put this show back on the map and not disappoint me like some of you in the back. And that is the very man you see standing next to me.
KENT glances towards CCP before directing his attention back towards the camera JENSEN: Last week marked the first of many weeks that I get what I want. It started with you HUNTER, and tonight it will continue with SCOTT CHARLOTTE and BRIAN CADY.
KENT pauses for a second. JENSEN: None of you have to like it, but I’m not accepting FAILURE anymore! A massive YOU SUCK chant begins to echo throughout the arena. JENSEN: Which is why we’re out here now. It’s come to my attention that there have been MANY threats of violence towards my newest acquisition CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE. CHRIS is going to be my ride back to the top and I cannot and WILL NOT allow this to be jeopardized by ANYONE… that being said… IF ANYONE sees fit to put their hands on CHRIS PAGE outside of in this ring then they will be SUSPENDED INDEFINITELY!
The boos grow the loudest as the statement is made. JENSEN turns towards CCP handing him the microphone. As this happens the boos divert into a massive CCP SUCKS chant which draws a huge smile from CHRIS as he begins to speak.
CCP: What a difference a week can make eh? Who would have thought that last week to kick off 2009 each and everyone of you would have the honor and privilege of seeing the absolute best athlete money can buy gracing you all once again with his illustrious presence… and talk about a making a statement!
CCP begins to laugh under his breath to himself as the crowd continues to bring the heat before he continues.
CCP: I couldn’t think of a better way to kick off the New Year than putting HUNTER RYAN, a man who claims to be paving the way for a better future, in the hospital, but there seems to be a misunderstanding. HUNTER and his band of jizz slobbers seem to be putting the blame on ME.
CCP and JENSEN look at each other and shrug their shoulders before CCP turns back towards the camera.
CCP: There is a very popular saying dating back centuries. It’s simple… “Every action has an equal an opposite reaction…”. At Xmas Xtreme RAGE, being the total hypocrite he is attacked the owner of this company with a Snow Shovel…
The crowd pops with a huge ovation. CCP: Sure you cheer for antics like that, yet I’m booed for doing the SAME DAMN THING to HUNTER RYAN… I’m labeled a coward because I picked my spot, while RAGE is cheered for attacking JON BROWN when he had his back turned… RAGE, if you want to blame someone for me being back, blame yourself…
The crowd diverts its cheers back to a CCP SUCKS chant as some mild trash begins to come hurling towards the ring.
CCP: I’ve sat back for the better part of a week and listened as all the so called “top stars” attempt to verbally bash me, to be honest I expected so much more out of high caliber athletes, but yet as usual I was left disappointed. I often wonder why people live in the past, but then once I thought about it it’s because they’ve got nothing better to talk about then something that transpired 6 months ago… I guess that says something about me then doesn’t it?
CCP pauses soaking in the crowd before he continues. CCP: I guess given the announcement made by Mr. Kent it’s pretty safe to say that I’m untouchable.
The boos are by far the loudest they have been so far. CCP: Tonight it all begins, and we WILL NOT stop until we get what rightfully belongs to us!... Speaking of tonight. Tonight, SCOTT, BRIAN. You boys are lost sheep and I’m going to be the big bad wolf. I’ve got you both locked into my sights and in just a little bit I’ll be happy to pull the trigger. Leadership by example folks, I hope that you kids behind the curtain are paying attention, because tonight I show you how it’s done.
JENSEN: And done it shall be! You see, I could have put CHRIS up against high caliber talent like FAMINE or SPICE ONE, but I decided to punish a few superstars while at the same time, showing the world exactly why CHRIS is the most controversial, the most athletic, and the highest paid superstar the XWF has seen in a long time!
CCP drops the microphone as “Zero” by Smashing Pumpkins begins to play as JENSEN and CCP make there exit.
The scene cuts backstage where FAMINE OF THE VILE and VIOLET are sitting in his locker room. She is on top of him as STEVE SAYORS comes in.
STEVE: Excuse me, FAMINE? FAMINE: HEY! What the hell is wrong with you? Knock before you come in here. STEVE: My apologies. I just wanted to let you know that SCOTT CHARLOTTE was seen looking for you earlier.
FAMINE: Is that so? Well, let him come find me. I’ve got something for him tonight! VIOLET: I can’t wait to see that baby. She starts to kiss him on his neck as STEVE just stands there and watches. FAMINE: STEVE? STEVE: Yes? FAMINE: Get the hell out of here! STEVE leaves the locker room as the scene cuts to the arena for the first match of the night.
"Iceman" DEAN JAMES vs. HUNTER RYAN - - International Title Round 1 - - HUNTER and DEAN immediately begin to brawl, and HUNTER takes DEAN down. The two continue to brawl on the mat, rolling around and smacking each other in the head. Both men get up and HUNTER knocks DEAN down with a big clothesline. HUNTER begins to stomp on DEAN. He picks DEAN up and tosses him into the ropes, but DEAN smacks HUNTER with an elbow. DEAN sets HUNTER into the corner and begins to lay elbow shots into him. HUNTER gets his foot up and boots DEAN in the face. DEAN stumbles backwards, and HUNTER runs, hitting DEAN with a running neck breaker. HUNTER gets to the ropes, and stands, taking a breather. DEAN slowly gets up, but HUNTER walks over and boots DEAN in the side. DEAN rolls onto the ground, holding his ribs. HUNTER rolls out of the ring and looks under the ring. DEAN gets up and leaps out of the ring onto HUNTER’S back, causing HUNTER to fall to the ground. DEAN picks HUNTER up and tosses him onto the barricade. DEAN walks over and grabs the International Title, which is on display right by the time keeper. He walks over and slams the International Title onto HUNTER’S head. HUNTER falls over to the other side of the barricade. DEAN leaps over the barricade and picks HUNTER up. He tosses HUNTER over to the ring side of the barricade and then steps over the barricade himself. DEAN tries to pick up HUNTER, but HUNTER reaches and elbows DEAN in the groin. He rolls DEAN into the ring and picks him up. HUNTER now smiles at the crowd and hits the finisher. DEAN looks like he’s on a mental vacation. HUNTER goes for the cover.. 1… 2… KICK OUT!! DEAN gets back to his feet as HUNTER comes back at him and hits a huge spear! DEAN goes down and HUNTER makes the cover. ONE! T... DEAN kicks out before the two count. DEAN gets up and begins lifting HUNTER, but HUNTER quickly gets a bear hug in place and squeezes DEAN. DEAN struggles around a while, pushing and pulling every way, but doesn't get free until HUNTER nails a belly-to-belly suplex. DEAN bounces on the mat, and HUNTER pops back to his feet. HUNTER reaches down and pulls DEAN to the center of the ring and gets a Boston crab on DEAN. DEAN reaches for the ropes, but HUNTER pulls tighter and kneels lower, pulling DEAN even more compact in the center of the ring. HUNTER starts turning the feet of DEAN, twisting his ankles at the same time. DEAN knuckles curl as he fights the urge to tap, but HUNTER releases after DEAN held out. HUNTER looks disgusted at his failed attempt, and leaves DEAN on the mat as he climbs the turnbuckles. DEAN finally gets to his feet, but HUNTER is signaling for a frog splash. DEAN gets up, HUNTER jumps.... DEAN catches HUNTER in a fireman's carry. HUNTER fights it, but DEAN turns quickly to drop down into spine buster. HUNTER spins out of though and rolls across the ring. DEAN looks confused as HUNTER gets back to his feet and comes from nowhere with a huge bulldog and takes DEAN to the mat hard, his head bouncing clean off the mat. HUNTER rolls over and makes the cover. ONE! TWO! THR….NO! DEAN will not give up. HUNTER then runs toward the top turnbuckle and waits for DEAN to get to his feet. As DEAN gets up he stumbles around a bit before being met by a HUNTER’S SPEAR!! HUNTER then goes for what could be a final pin. 1…….2……..3!!! And this one is over. HUNTER RYAN advances to the next round of the International Title tournament. A well fought match by both participants, but only one man could win!
After the match, HUNTER heads to the back as DEAN begins to stand up. He asks for a mic as he holds his mid section.
DEAN: You know what? I’m sick of this crap! This is going to end starting next week I am going to start taking matters into my own hands. JENSEN KENT, I blame YOU for this. All of this is your fault and if I don’t start getting my way, people are going to get hurt.
DEAN drops the mic and leaves the ring as the crowd looks confused. What does he mean things are going to change? And why is he blaming the GM?
WINNER: HUNTER RYAN The scene cuts backstage where we see JENSEN KENT and CHRIS PAGE walking down the hallway. They pass by a few superstars and staff who are not giving them many friendly looks. CHRIS looks at JENSEN and stops him for a moment.
CCP: Hey, i'll catch up with you in a bit. I need to go take care of something real quick before my match.
JENSEN: Ok but hurry up, your match is next. I'll be in my office for a few minutes. JENSEN walks off as CCP is seen walking the other direction as the show cuts to commercial break.
Before the match begins, cameras are backstage where BRIAN CADY is laid out and bleeding. HUNTER RYAN is seen calling for help as he checks on his friend and stable mate. EMT’s can be see arriving as we head back to the ring.
"Chronic" CHRIS PAGE vs. "X Effect" BRIAN CADY vs. SCOTT CHARLOTTE - - Pin To Win - - In the ring SCOTT CHARLOTTE is already in the ring waiting for the contest to get underway. The lights dim to complete darkness. On the XTRON the words “The Most Controversial Athlete on the Face of the Planet” flashes, as it fades a Marijuana leaf hits the black screen in green which prompts “Zero” by Smashing Pumpkins to begin to play. The crowd comes alive with boos as a video package highlighting CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE begins to play. Smoke begins to rise at the top of the ramp. The boos grow louder as we see CCP step through the smoke to the top of the ramp to a pyro display. CCP has his white towel in his left hand tossing it over his right shoulder as he displays a cocky grin placing his hands on his hips soaking in the crowd. CCP begins to walk towards the ring with perhaps on of the most intense looks ever seen. He pays little attention to the crowd as he makes his way to ringside. CCP makes his way up the steel steps and enters the ring throwing his towel into the crowd.
As we get underway, both men begin to glance at one an other waiting to see who will make a move. CCP comes forward getting right in the face of CHARLOTTE shoving him back into the ropes. CHARLOTTE runs back at him and CCP tries to go for a clothesline but SCOTT ducks underneath. This allows CHARLOTTE to come from behind with a double ax handle. CHARLOTTE spins CCP around looking for a right only to have it blocked and it’s CCP who connects with a right. PAGE forces CHARLOTTE into the corner. Calling for a clean break, the referee gets one and CHRIS backs off. CHARLOTTE motions for another tie up, but quickly sticks his knee into PAGE’S gut and snaps on a headlock. After a brief moment of struggling, PAGE is flipped over in a headlock takedown. Responding quickly, he wraps his legs around CHARLOTTE’S throat in a leg scissors until the challenger is forced to let go. Flicking his hips though, CHARLOTTE frees himself and the two get back to their feet. Ducking a clothesline attempt, CHARLOTTE grapples PAGE from behind, but PAGE drops down with a snapmare. With CHARLOTTE sitting upright, PAGE attempts a chin lock that CHARLOTTE counters with by twisting an arm around into a hammerlock. PAGE dances back and forth in an effort to escape it, and soon plants his free hand on the ground and flips himself over to his feet, reversing the hammerlock to CHARLOTTE. Three elbows towards PAGE’s head cause CHARLOTTE to be freed. With CHRIS letting go, CHARLOTTE is able to spring into action bouncing off the ropes and coming straight back at PAGE who drops down onto his stomach just in time for CHARLOTTE to leap right over him. Coming back off the opposite ropes now, PAGE leap frogs right over CHARLOTTE who again goes towards the ropes. He flies forwards from them but is caught in a hip toss. The challenger reacts by dropping down and trying to counter with his own hip toss, but the champ is on form and snaps in an arm drag which he simply rolls through with and both men wind up standing facing each other at the same time. The crowd shows their applause for the quick-paced starting action of this match. Nodding at each other, CHARLOTTE and PAGE circle each other before stepping into lock up yet again. The Cruiserweight Champion wastes no time on this effort by implementing a leg takedown on CHARLOTTE which leaves him on the mat. Sliding up to CHARLOTTE’S head, PAGE uses a Half-Nelson to roll him over into a pinning predicament. 1… 2… kickout! CHARLOTTE gets back to his feet easily but is met with a running boot to his face. Dropping an elbow down, PAGE attempts another pin. 1… TW… not even two! Going back to work, CHRIS whips CHARLOTTE to a corner and runs in with a flying Stinger Splash. Whipping him to the other corner, CHRIS repeats the process. Going for a three-peat, PAGE whips CHARLOTTE once more, but takes a little longer to follow up, allowing CHARLOTTE to barely escape in time, but escape nonetheless. PAGE eats turnbuckle. He turns around into a hurricanrana. Cover by CHARLOTTE this time. 1… 2… kickout! CHARLOTTE now repeats the boot to the face that PAGE used earlier and even the elbow and pin attempt. 1… TW… not even two! He even gets the same outcome as PAGE did. Rapidly making his way to the top rope, CHARLOTTE leaps off just as PAGE stands up and catches him with a missile dropkick that nearly sends CHRIS right out of the ring. PAGE tries to use the corner to assist him in getting up. He’s met with a running knee to the jaw that rocks him, but he continues to try and pull himself up. This puts him in a position where CHARLOTTE attempts a second hurricanrana but is met with a sit-down powerbomb for his troubles. Pin attempt. 1… 2… kickout! PAGE rolls CHARLOTTE away and gets himself to his feet while CHARLOTTE still cradles the back of his head. Getting to his knees and then pushing up, CHARLOTTE barely gets time to avoid a super kick attempt. A quick spinning back kick puts PAGE down for long enough to let CHARLOTTE buy some time and shake his head clear before PAGE is back on his feet. A running knee attack catches PAGE in the jaw. Cover. 1… 2… kickout! PAGE slowly gets up and looks frustrated. CHARLOTTE comes in for the attack but out of nowhere PAGE hits the PAGE PLANT!!! Immediately he goes for the cover….1…..2…..3!! A valiant effort by CHARLOTTE
WINNER: “Chronic” CHRIS PAGE Following the three count CCP is far from finished as he rolls to the outside grabbing a Steel Chair. He slides back in the ring, across the ring SCOTT CHARLOTTE begins to get to his feet holding the back of his neck. He turns a circle before being blasted in the skull with a chair shot that sends him through the ropes and out to the floor leaving CCP in the ring on his own. Suddenly the lights go out. Killswitch Engage – The End of Heartache hits the PA system. FAMINE OF THE VILE is here!! Down at ringside, CHARLOTTE begins to get up as the lights come back on. CHARLOTTE gets to his feet and doesn’t see FAMINE standing behind him. FAMINE has a rope in hand and ties it around the neck of CHARLOTTE who begins to struggle. He tries to breathe but FAMINE squeezes tighter. FAMINE then proceeds to take CHARLOTTE into the ring. He begins to beat him down and then hits a kick to the jaw. DEMON HORNS!! CHARLOTTE is out cold! PAGE just stands in the ring looking at the man who he has a long history with. FAMINE proceeds to have a steel gothic cross lowered to the ring. While that is going on, CCP gets out of the ring and start to walk up the ramp. The roof explodes as HUNTER RYAN blows past JENSEN KENT with a STEEL CHAIR of his own. CCP runs back down and escapes through the crowd drawing nothing but BOOS from the massive crowd. HUNTER glares at CCP with perhaps the most intense stare we’ve seen from HUNTER in a long, long time. HUNTER then looks at FAMINE who is hooking CHARLOTTE up to the cross. He tries to walk up and see if he can help CHARLOTTE but with one look, FAMINE make sure that HUNTER knows he means business. HUNTER walks out of the ring and leaves. FAMINE then grabs a mic and looks at CHARLOTTE for a moment. VIOLET comes inside and laughs at what her new man is doing. FAMINE slaps CHARLOTTE across the face trying to wake him up.
FAMINE: Time after time after time I have warned you all that there is no defeating the demon king. I have yet to see someone beat me fair and square in a match. And you my friend, seem to keep forgetting that I am not a man who forgives and forgets> What you did last week was just piss me off even more. And tonight, not only will I make you see what you’re up against, but later tonight, SPICE ONE will see what the Demon King is all about! So it is written…..So shall it be!!
FAMINE grabs CHARLOTTE by the neck and stares at him in the eyes. CHARLOTTE looks afraid and begins to scream, shutting his eyes. Even VIOLET seems to be a bit scared as she backs up. FAMINE lets go and begins to laugh maniacally. The scene then goes backstage.
JENSEN KENT is in his office when out of nowhere HUNTER RYAN, DAN FIERCE, ARCHANGEL and World Champion RAGE burst in the room.
JENSEN: What in the hell are you doing here? You’re supposed to be on ANARCHY. So with the exception of HUNTER, everyone needs to get the hell out of my office and my show!
HUNTER: I wanna know if you or PAGE had anything to do with CADY being attacked earlier. JENSEN: Look, I don't know who did that but I can assure you PAGE had nothing to do with it. I was told he went to make a phone call before his match ok? I don’t have time to deal with your crap so get out of my office before I call security!
RAGE: KENT, KENT, KENT…get with the program…we’re here to pave the way for a better future for the XWF…and we have been talking it over…and now that JON is gone…the one guy NOT getting with the program is…you! I mean, KENT, you bring in two nobodies from the IWF…and surprise, surprise they turn out to be lame asses that not only don’t win the title…they perform SOOOO badly that you fire half of them before they ever get a shot…and your next big move…to bring in a worthless belt from that sad excuse for a fed you used to run to give Massacre some much needed gold. Then…you bring in another failed star to save Massacre…a guy who left with his tail between his legs when he lost the bid at the XWF World Title the first time around…
JENSEN stands up and gets into RAGE’S FACE JENSEN: You know something? I’ve heard about enough from you and your cohorts RAGE. I think I’ll just call security and get you the hell out of here so I can go about my business.
RAGE: If only it was that easy KENT. It looks like you need to do your job as GM a lot better because if you missed the memo. Let us clue you in on something. Wanna explain, HUNTER?
HUNTER: Sure thing, RAGE, you see when CADY and I were in JON BROWN’S limo last Thursday…we found a lot of stuff that he left behind…Cuban cigars, booze…and three signed Massacre Contracts…all we had to do was sign some names to them and XWF personnel called them valid.
RAGE: In short, KENT, ARCHANGEL is now on Massacre…DAN FIERCE is now on Massacre…and the World Champ is now here on Massacre! In one night KENT, I brought in three proven talents that unlike your boy PAUL FROST…WILL show up week in and week out…and I brought the World Title with me! Not only that KENT, But I am also going to settle the score with your yappy little pot smoking Chihuahua…I single handedly brought more to the show than you have had in the entire time you’ve been here! Get ready for a huge headache…because the Road Crew is all together on YOUR show whether you want us or not!
The crowd cheers as RAGE puts his hand on the World Title and taps it a few times. RAGE then smiles as the ROAD CREW makes their way out of the office. JENSEN KENT looks extremely pissed off as the show goes to commercial break.
When the show returns, we see SPICE ONE getting ready for his match. His back is to his locker room door. He turns and suddenly finds himself chest to chest, nose to nose, and face to face with CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE.
An intense stare down ensues for several seconds neither man blinking, but almost as if in stereo each have a small grin come across there face.
CCP: What’s up brother… SPICE ONE: Good to see you back again. CCP goes to shake SPICE ONE’S hand but SPICE doesn’t return the handshake gesture. SPICE ONE: Sorry bro, no disrespect but I’m under the weather. Don’t want you to catch anything.
CCP: Ah… ok, no problem man. CHRIS pauses for a moment as he can see that SPICE ONE is really not looking well. CCP: Look, I wanted to come in and first wish you luck tonight with FAMINE OF THE VILE, that son of a bitch is a sick individual. That and I know that you are more then willing to partake in a little herbal supplement with your boy! You feeling good enough to puff one out?
SPICE ONE: YOU KNOW IT!! CCP reaches behind his ear pulling out a joint. SPICE ONE: What kind is it? CCP: Dude you know I only roll with the best… this is Pineapple Haze. CCP fires up the joint taking several tokes before passing it to SPICE. SPICE takes a hit before proceeding to cough his lungs out.
SPICE ONE: Dude I need your supplier… CCP: Who doesn’t… CCP states taking a pull from the joint. SPICE ONE: So Chronic Chris, I got an offer you cannot refuse. CCP: Yeah? SPICE: Wanna go kill some undeserving hookers with me? CCP looks up at SPICE, with blood shot eyes, very confused. CCP: What did you say again, bro? I think I'm really high and misunderstood you... SPICE: I SAID...DO YOU WANT TO GO KILL SOME UNDESERVING HOOKERS WITH ME, AND THEN GO EAT THEM SHAWCROSS STYLE!
CCP then bursts out laughing and rises off the seat he is sitting at. He shakes SPICE'S hand.
CCP: You never lost your sense of humor, man. Good to see you again.... CCP then walks off the screen, leaving SPICE still smoking, with a strange look on his face.
SPICE: But.....I was serious..... The scene cuts to ringside for the main event. MAIN EVENT SPICE ONE vs. FAMINE OF THE VILE - - Last Man Standing - - FAMINE and SPICE ONE are standing nose to nose in the center of the center. The two men exchange a few words before the ref pushes them apart the signals for the bell. FAMINE quickly nails a clothesline taking SPICE ONE down to the mat. FAMINE then grabs SPICE ONE by the head and goes to lift him up but is punched in the gut causing him to double over. SPICE ONE then bounces off the rope and hit a running knee lift knocking FAMINE down to the mat. SPICE ONE then grabs FAMINE and goes to it the KRANIUM KICK but FAMINE manages to get out of the way before getting hit. FAMINE slides to the outside. FAMINE taunts SPICE ONE by pointing and shaking his finger. FAMINE then quickly slides back in the ring and is met by SPICE ONE stomping him in the back. SPICE ONE then lifts him to his feet and whips FAMINE across the ring into the far corner and follows him in very a huge splash. SPICE ONE steps back and FAMINE collapses to the mat. SPICE ONE then goes the pick FAMINE up again but FAMINE racks his eyes. SPICE ONE grabs his face and stumbles back as FAMINE climbs back to his feet. FAMINE then bounces off the ropes and nails SPICE ONE with a powerful clothesline. FAMINE then bounces off the other ropes and nails a knee drop to SPICE ONE’S forehead. FAMINE grabs SPICE ONE and hits a DDT
ONE…TWO…Kickout. FAMINE quickly gets to his feet and kicks SPICE ONE in the gut then picks him up again. He then whips SPICE ONE into the ropes and nails a hard spine buster slam. FAMINE gets to his feet and plays to the crowd a little before grabbing SPICE ONE and lifting him up. FAMINE grabs him by the arm and goes to whip him across the ring but it’s reversed and he is sent chest first into the turnbuckle. As FAMINE stumbles backwards out of the corner SPICE ONE grabs him by the head and hits a reverse DDT. SPICE ONE gets back to his feet and grabs FAMINE by the leg and starts kicking away at his knee. FAMINE starts screaming out in pain as SPICE ONE continues to kick away. He then stops and grabs FAMINE’S other legs and catapults FAMINE face first into the corner. SPICE ONE then picks FAMINE up by the head. SPICE ONE lifts him up into a stalling Brain buster and slams him down. SPICE ONE Gets back to his feet and picks FAMINE up again. He then whips FAMINE across the ring into the ropes. Upon returning SPICE ONE goes for the clothesline but FAMINE ducks and bounces off the ropes and hits a cross body block. FAMINE quickly locks in a rear naked choke. SPICE ONE starts gasping for air and trying to getting FAMINE off his back. SPICE ONE’s face is starting to turn a deep red but he still refuses to quit and with a surge of strength manages to grab the ropes causing FAMINE to break the hold. FAMINE backs off and SPICE ONE slowly pulls himself to his feet just in time to get nailed by FAMINE sending both men flipping over the ropes and landing hard on the floor. One…. the ref starts counting. Two…. both men still laying on the ground in pain. Three… FAMINE starts to move. Four… FAMINE manages to pull himself to his feet using the railing. Five… FAMINE grabs SPICE ONE up off the ground and whips him into the ring post. Six… he then picks SPICE ONE up off the floor and rolls him into the ring. Seven… FAMINE slides in to the ring stopping the count. FAMINE taunts SPICE ONE while he circles him. SPICE ONE grabs FAMINE and sends him into the far corner. SPICE ONE then walks over to FAMINE and lifts him up onto the top turnbuckle. SPICE ONE steps up and grabs FAMINE setting him up for a huge Super plex but FAMINE starts punching him in the gut. SPICE ONE fires back but FAMINE dodges it and manages to trap SPICE ONE across his shoulders. FAMINE stands up with SPICE ONE struggling desperately to get free but it’s too late! FAMINE twists and nails the Demon Horns from the top turnbuckle!! HOLY CRAP!!! FAMINE stands up as the ref begins the ten count. ONE…….TWO………THREE……..FOUR………….FIVE…………SIX………….SEVEN………..EIGHT…………NINE………TEN!! It’s over. FAMINE OF THE VILE is victorious here tonight.
WINNER: FAMINE OF THE VILE FAMINE then asks for a mic as VIOLET gives him one from outside the ring. FAMINE: For weeks now, I’ve been dealing with loss after loss after loss. I get a win here tonight and I’m still not happy. You see, no one has been able to beat me fair and square. Have any of you seen the show? Who has beaten me fair and square? You find someone that has and I will take back what I said. But since you can’t do that then I have something to say. JENSEN KENT, you can forget about having a legend like FAMINE OF THE VILE on your show. As of right now, I quit. My contract with this show is up and I’m heading to ANARCHY! Hopefully there will be someone there who can give me a challenge without having to resort to cheating. THAT and I have a score to settle with someone!
FAMINE drops the mic and exits the ring as VIOLET grabs his arm and they walk up the ramp. The show comes to a close with Killswitch Engage – The End of Heartache playing. FAMINE OF THE VILE is gone from MASSACRE? What will happen now that one of the most decorated superstars in the shows history is gone? Tune in next week……
Fade to black...
