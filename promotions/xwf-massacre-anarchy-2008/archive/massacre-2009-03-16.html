XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Card

Monday Massacre (16 March 2009)

16 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FIREFOX and MOZILLA are NOT RECOMMENDED for viewing this program. MATCHES: DOWNFALL vs. HARLEY VICE - - X-Treme Rules - - 2-RP LIMIT DRAKE KOMODO vs. HUNTER RYAN vs. DOUBLE G - - Falls Count Anywhere - - 2-RP LIMIT RANMA SAOTOME vs. "X EFFECT" BRIAN CADY - - Non-Title Match - - 2-RP LIMIT MAIN EVENT CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE vs. FAMINE OF THE VILE - - Devil's Playground Match - - 3-RP LIMIT The cameras are focused on the ring when suddenly Master of Puppets begins to play. The crowd is on their feet as FAMINE OF THE VILE makes his way out to the arena. He stops at the ramp entrance and looks at the crowd who is going crazy! The music dies down as he picks up his mic to speak.
FAMINE: Tonight, it all comes down to three seconds. That will be the deciding factor in my career.
The crowd looks somewhat stunned at this comment. FAMINE: You heard me right folks. Tonight might be my last night in the XWF. But I want to make it clear to everyone. If I lose, that is not going to be my reason for retiring. My reasons for retiring are mostly because of my future family and my health. While I am still in the best shape of my life, I don’t know how long that is going to last. This business takes a toll on your body and mind. And after twenty seven years, I think it’s time for the Demon King to step down and let the light shine on someone else. Now on to tonight’s business. CHRIS PAGE, you and I have done this before. So it’s no surprise that we are at it once again. But tonight…..Tonight is a different story. Because you see CHRIS, this is going to be inside my creation. Inside my cage of fear and pain. And no matter the outcome, I am going to make sure that I give you every single ounce of blood, sweat and energy. And if I………….
Suddenly, One by One by Alter Bridge begins to play. JENSEN KENT makes his way out to the arena and stops next to FAMINE.
JENSEN: Sorry to interrupt you there FAMINE but I came out here because I need to make an announcement for your match tonight. I know all about the history between you and CHRIS. So for tonight I have decided to do something interesting. Because I feel that a match of this caliber should be told with excitement and intensity. So for one night, and one night only, I have decided to bring in two of the best commentators in this business. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce you two none other than DERRICK DIAMOND and his partner FLASH ROTTEN!!
What?! FLASH ROTTEN and DERRICK DIAMOND are former WGWF ring announcers. JENSEN KENT has brought them here? Looks like a few fans know these guys pretty well. Both men come out and wave to the crowd. FAMINE looks at them both for a second and then shakes the hand of DERRICK DIAMOND. He looks at FLASH and doesn’t smile or shake his hand.
JENSEN: Gentlemen, thank you for agreeing to do this tonight. Now, I will let you get back to what you were saying FAMINE.
JENSEN heads to the back as both DERRICK and FLASH follow him. FAMINE: Looks like this night just got better. But as I was going to say before JENSEN came out. It doesn’t matter if I win or lose, but I will not leave here until blood has been spilled. If we’re going to do this CHRIS, we’re going to give it all we’ve got. We’re going to put on a show like no other. MASSACRE will never be the same after this match. Because I promise you, If this is to be my last match. I’m going out with a (censored) BANG!!
One by One begins to play again as FAMINE drops his mic and heads to the back as the crowd cheers and chants his name. As FAMINE heads to the back, "The Night" by Disturbed blares over the PA system, the fans let out a loud chorus of boo's as Downfall steps out onto the stage with mic in hand. He is clad in a black wife beater, jeans and his black leather vest. Downfall looks around the crowd, smiling at the show of disdain from the fans. He walks down to the ring, and he climbs in, and stands to balance on the second and bottom ropes and play to the crowd, revelling in their ire. After a few seconds, the music dies down and Downfall lifts the mic to his mouth.
DOWNFALL: Thirteen days, Harley. The contract has been signed and the countdown has begun... thirteen unlucky days until you will defend that X-Treme Championship against the wrench thrown into the cogs of the Road Crew war machine... yours truly, the Beast Unleashed. Tonight, we give the populace a preview of what's to come... and how ironic that that night, Monday Night Massacre, March 16th 2009, when you and I meet in the ring... is set on the unluckiest day of your life. The number thirteen holds bad portents for you, champ.
He grins widely, as he begins walking around the ring. DOWNFALL: It's unlucky for you, because not only are you going up against me in my prime, no distractions, no coming in behind me with your little Vicebreaker while I'm not looking... but I'm more focused, more passionate and hungrier than ever to get my hands on that X-Treme title you clearly aren't ready to defend, and in the process send a message back to your boss Rage. Anything he has done, I will do better... I intend to show him that I am every inch as great an icon as he was... even as I tear down what the Road Crew, has worked so hard to build.
He continues his pacing. DOWNFALL: It's unlucky for you, also, because you have to come in against me in my domain, and survive, only days before you're set to defend that championship against Jaymz Dante, Luna Chick, and Chasm... (snickers) yes, I'll grant you they're not all of them, exactly the stuff nightmares are made of there... but the danger is there. You could lose the belt before you and I even come to the dance. I truly hope you come through, Harley. I do. Because if that's your lucky night then, and you escape with that title intact... you'll have had a preview of just what it's going to take to get through me. Tonight you stand at the gates of hell... at Purple Haze, your ass is going to have to walk through fire.
He stops pacing and stops right in front of the camera, fixing it with an intense gaze. DOWNFALL: And that, despite today's unfortunate date for you... that will be the unluckiest night of your life.
His face breaks into a smirk... but his eyes remain hard and unyielding. DOWNFALL: So come on out here, champ... and let's see if you have what it takes! DOWNFALL then heads down to the ring and waits for HARLEY to make his way out as well. DOWNFALL vs. HARLEY VICE - - X-Treme Rules - - HARLEY makes his way out to the arena as the crowd cheers for the ROAD CREW member. He walks into the ring as DOWNFALL just stares at him not losing his focus. The bell rings and DOWNFALL comes out in a shoot fighting stance. HARLEY looks at him then at the crowd before moving towards DOWNFALL. DOWNFALL locks up with him. HARLEY is trying everything to overpower his opponent but DOWNFALL slams him down hard to the mat and stomps on the back of his head.
DOWNFALL tries it again, but HARLEY gets out of the way. He springs up and hits a nasty dropkick to the knee of DOWNFALL and DOWNFALL goes down. HARLEY jumps on him immediately with a double foot stomp. HARLEY kicks DOWNFALL in the left arm repeatedly before bouncing off the ropes and what a shot! HARLEY just delivered a low dropkick to DOWNFALL right in the gem bag!! DOWNFALL is rolling around in the ring holding his groin and HARLEY is working over the arm again. DOWNFALL is screaming in pain. DOWNFALL grabs HARLEY by the face and has him fish hooked. HARLEY is screaming, but he isn’t giving up the arm. DOWNFALL still won’t let go of that fishhook that he has clamped on HARLEYS nose. HARLEY is like a man possessed. DOWNFALL with one last great effort pulls hard on the nose of HARLEY and he finally lets go.
DOWNFALL bounces back up to his feet angry. He grabs HARLEY and throws him with great force over the top rope. HARLEY crashes down hard on the floor and DOWNFALL stands at the ropes watching him. DOWNFALL leans back and with great effort does a full flip over the top rope and what a leg drop right across HARLEY’S neck!! DOWNFALL and HARLEY are both down now. The fans are chanting for HARLEY to get up but DOWNFALL seems to be the one listening to their pleas because he is quickly rising to his feet. He has HARLEY and throws him hard over the guardrail into the fans. DOWNFALL follows close behind and grabs HARLEY by the head, tossing him into the stairs. DOWNFALL picks up an empty seat and he throws it down on HARLEY. DOWNFALL lifts him up again over his head in a military. He throws HARLEY over the guardrail and he crashes hard on the mats. DOWNFALL looks angry, but DOWNFALL’S anger isn’t enough to keep HARLEY down. HARLEY catches DOWNFALL with a drop toe hold and DOWNFALL crashes face first into the steel steps. DOWNFALL jumps up to his feet holding his bloody nose. HARLEY gets up on the ring apron and flies off with a cross body block. DOWNFALL falls with HARLEY on the table. HARLEY goes for the cover, but DOWNFALL kicks. HARLEY springs up onto the security wall and OH!! A moonsault down on DOWNFALL! DOWNFALL rolls off the table in pain and HARLEY holds his hands up in the air in triumph. HARLEY turns back around only to see DOWNFALL getting back up to his feet. HARLEY charges at DOWNFALL with a flying forearm. DOWNFALL stumbles backwards. HARLEY comes again and BOOM! A big foot to HARLEY’S face stops him dead in his tracks.
DOWNFALL signals for the end. DOWNFALL throws HARLEY back inside the ring and climbs in himself. HARLEY lies quite still in the ring as DOWNFALL gets back up to his feet. He pulls HARLEY up and presses him over his head. DOWNFALL just laid out HARLEY with the DRAGONSLAYER!!! 1……..2……3! DOWNFALL WINS!
WINNER: DOWNFALL The cameras cut to a scene at the bottom of which flashes the message ‘Recorded Earlier’. A Limo pulls up to the Arena, the license plate of which reads RCREW1. The doors open and out comes ‘THE X EFFECT’ BRIAN CADY, HUNTER RYAN and ‘BIGG RIGG’ JOHN GAMBINO.
They begin to head into the arena when they are approached by STEVE SAYORS. STEVE flags them down just before they walk in the door.
SAYORS: Guys, wait up! Hunter, John, Brian…I just want to know…with RAGE having disappeared after his savage beating last week…do any of you know where he is?
HUNTER looks at BIGG RIGG. HUNTER: You got this, big guy? BIGG RIGG: No problem, RIGG turns to SAYORS BIGG RIGG: No, I can say for certain that we have had no more luck than anyone else in locating RAGE.
SAYORS: With RAGE gone, what does that mean for the Road Crew? BIGG RIGG: RAGE may be a little hard to find…I guarantee you the XWF will still feel his presence…as for the Road Crew…we’ll do what we always do…dominate and pave the way for the future of this business!
RIGG high fives CADY and RYAN after saying that and all three turn and head into the arena leaving SAYORS standing alone outside.
DRAKE KOMODO vs. HUNTER RYAN vs. DOUBLE G - - Falls Count Anywhere - - The bell sounds and we’re underway. All three men stare each other down. GG and KOMODO double up on HUNTER as they beat him back into a corner. KOMONO sends HUNTER across the ring into a buckle, HUNTER comes out of the corner, KOMODO ducks a HUNTER clothesline but GG does not as he’s taken down. HUNTER turns around and is met with a spinning clothesline from KOMODO. KOMODO picks up GG and lands a hard right, GG fires back before driving a knee to the midsection, GG suplexes KOMODO. HUNTER begins to get up in the corner but is met with a running shoulder block from GG. GG scoops up HUNTER. He looks for a running Powerslam only to have HUNTER slide down his back shoving GG forward into KOMODO sending him through the ropes. GG turns around into straight right hands from HUNTER. HUNTER plants a boot to the midsection before planting GG in the mat with a DDT. HUNTER gets to his feet, he runs towards the ropes but KOMODO trips him up. GG gets back to his feet drilling HUNTER from behind with a stiff forearm; GG spins HUNTER around before throwing him over head with a release belly to belly throw. KOMODO slides in the ring behind GG. KOMODO spins GG around taking him over with a Northern light suplex with a bridge…. ONE, TWO, T… GG escapes the near fall. HUNTER meets KOMODO with right hands as he attempts to reach his feet. HUNTER hits a swinging neck breaker before attempting a cover… ONE, TWO…. GG makes the save. GG picks up HUNTER and throws him through the ropes to the arena floor. GG picks up KOMODO and takes him over with a suplex. GG is up and drops an elbow across the chest of KOMODO. HUNTER makes his way to the top rope, GG is to his feet, he turns towards HUNTER, HUNTER leaps off the top rope looking for a flying cross body only GG catches him and plants him with a running powerslam! KOMODO and GG are to their feet, GG charges KOMODO looking for a clothesline, KOMODO ducks and GG bounces off the ropes into a picture perfect dropkick from KOMODO! KOMODO locks in a Dragon Sleeper on HUNTER. He looks for the submission as he applies more pressure. HUNTER manages to counter with a knee strike to the head breaking the hold. HUNTER and KOMODO are up exchanging right hands, KOMODO take the low road with a thumb to the eye, GG comes up behind KOMODO driving him into the mat with a Full Nelson slam! GG looks to send HUNTER to the floor only HUNTER elevates GG over the top rope with a back body drop! HUNTER makes a cover hooking the leg… ONE, TWO, THRE… NO! KOMODO escapes! HUNTER gets to a vertical base. He calls for a Spear. HUNTER gets back in a neutral corner KOMODO begins to pull himself to his feet, GG takes HUNTER down with a double leg takedown from the floor before yanking him backwards crashing him against the ring post. GG slides into the ring bursting towards KOMODO with a spear of his own, KOMODO leap frogs GG who runs chest first into the turnbuckles, KOMODO comes put behind him driving into the mat with the K-D-T! KOMODO goes for the cover but at the last second, HUNTER makes the save! DRAKE gets up as HUNTER once again tries to hit a spear. HUNTER runs toward DRAKE only to be met with a kick to the gut. DRAKE lifts him up and WHAM!! SKY HIGH!! HUNTER is OUT! DRAKE is in complete control! DOUBLE G suddenly catches him with a boot to the groin from behind! DRAKE crumples to the ground! DOUBLE G tries to shake the cobwebs as he wanders around the ring, getting the blood and oxygen back where it belongs.. He turns to face DRAKE, and lifts him up. DRAKE is already in tremendous pain as GG tries to finish him off! DRAKE slides around wildly to avoid being taken out, and he manages to whip DOUBLE G into the ropes with his lower body. Out of nowhere, Drake pulls down GG’s tights! GG’s face is turning beet red as DRAKE bounces off the ropes. WARDROBE MALFUNCTION! DRAKE covers…1…..2…..3!!! It’s over!!
WINNER: DRAKE KOMODO The cameras suddenly cut to the backstage area where JENSEN KENT is wandering around with a clipboard, suddenly a gloved hand drops on his shoulder from behind. The glove is familiar and the fans in the arena cheer as it looks to belong to RAGE.
Suddenly, RAGE’s voice is heard. RAGE: Don’t turn around, KENT, just because SPICE and CCP attacked me last week…don’t think you are safe. Wherever you go…wherever you are…I can get to you…and I promise I will make your life a living hell.
RAGE’s hand moves off of JENSEN’S shoulder. JENSEN spins around and there is no one there. He sees something lying on the floor and picks it up. It is the bent and bloody mask of RAGE. He drops it from nerveless fingers and walks quickly out of the shot, scanning everywhere as if RAGE might at any moment walk through the wall and attack him.
The scene switches and we now see a Black Hummer shown speeding into the arena screeching to a halt. The driver door opens and jumping out is CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE! The crowd boos as CCP walks up to a member of the ring crew taking a hold of him by the throat and forcing him back against the wall.
CCP: Where is HUNTER RYAN. CCP softly yet seriously states. The crew member looks as if he’s going to wet himself as he answers.
Crewman: I.. I…I don’t know. CCP: Wrong answer! CCP drills the crew member with a stiff right hand knocking him to the ground. CCP turns his attention and begins to walk down the backstage hallway with one of the most intense looks we’ve ever seen. Crew members and wrestlers a like part like the red sea as CCP walks through them not even paying them any attention. CCP comes to a door marked ROAD CREW. He looks at the door for several long seconds before kicking the door in surprising RIGG, HARLEY, AND HUNTER who all jump up expecting the worst. CCP show no intimidation as he walks right up to HUNTER starring him eye to eye.
CCP: Listen up and listen good you self centered little prick. You wanted my attention? Well you got it. All I WANT from you is for you to meet me in the middle of the ring live next week on Massacre…. If you’ve got the balls.
RIGG and HARLEY start to advance on PAGE but HUNTER stops them. He steps closer to PAGE and is now nose to nose with him.
HUNTER: That's what I've been waiting for, PAGE. For one of you Mary Jane-Lovin freaks to finally step up and do business the right way around here. You wanted to take it upon yourselves to take us out one by one outside the ring...so last week I decided to play your game and make my own statement on behalf of the ROAD CREW...
He gets very serious now. HUNTER: ...on behalf of RAGE! So it's a match you want? You got it. Next week...US Champ...vs...dare I say...World Champ?
The crowd roars with approval. CCP: HUNTER you just signed your death certificate. Next week leave the girls back here… Your career is about to come to an end.
CHRIS seriously states as he back steps his way out of the locker room. As soon as he walks out, he is met by STEVE SAYORS.
SAYORS: CCP in just a little while you’ll be walking down that ramp poised to become the next XWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, but before I get your thoughts on that I was hoping to get a complete rundown of PURPLE HAZE.
CCP yawns to himself. CCP: Sure… I’ve devised a card that was meant to provide opportunity for younger stars who typically wouldn’t get the time of day. Off the jump DANTE and GILMOUR will not compete for the tag straps, instead DANTE will defend his True Violence Championship against the world’s biggest crybaby GRAPPLING GARY…
Crowd lets out a nice pop. CCP: KIMEE MOOKS defends the Cruiserweight Championship against Q.C. THUG… We’ve got a DRAKE KOMODO open challenge… ever since I announced this contest guys have been begging to sign that contract… well it’s been signed alright, the name on the contract…. MR AMAZING!
Another huge pop is heard CCP: WILD ORCHID defends the Women’s Title against MISS TERRY. DOWNFALL meets whoever manages to walk into the Toyota Center with the strap, Hunter deals with TRENT GEIN and KRIS KAIN, RAZZA meets ANDERSON, ENVY AND ANDY DEABS will meet the TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS CENTURION and PERVA SUNO, RANMA and RAGE 3 Stages of Hell…
SAYORS: What about the World Title? CCP: I can’t so much book a match when I don’t know whose carrying the gold. Since I’m on a deadline like everyone else to put out a full card I’ve decided that a FATAL FOUR WAY ELIMINATION match will take place… It will be FAMINE OF THE VILE, SAM HAIN, AND YOURS TRULY… CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE! Win or lose tonight you’re guaranteed a night of intense action and a night of CCP’s choices. It will be a night NO ONE shall soon forget.
SAYORS: Sounds like you’ve got a pretty solid lineup planned for Purple Haze… but looking ahead to tonight and FAMINE of the VILE…
CCP: STEVE I’ve got a lot going on currently here in the XWF. I’m a hot commodity where as VILE is luke warm. Tonight he NEEDS this a lot more then I do, but this is a goal I set for myself last year when I walked through the doors of this company… this goal should have already been fulfilled, look at two weeks ago… same thing, I was robbed out of the Title by RAGE and SPICE ONE, but tonight… tonight my friend is the night history shall be made. I feel it, the people feel it, and in just a little bit FAMINE is going to feel it…tonight for the first time in my career I step inside SATAN’S STRUCTURE laying it all on the line as I look to achieve my destiny by becoming the XWF World Heavyweight Champion. Vile and I know each other incredibly well, he has NEVER defeated me in 5 attempts… if I was a betting man I would go all in for CHRIS PAGE extending VILE’S record to 0-6 against GREATNESS! Look at VILE because I’m about to roll right over as I stake claim to my 12th World Title.
The crowd boos as CCP walks away from STEVE, leaving him just standing there. RANMA SAOTOME vs. "X EFFECT" BRIAN CADY - - Non-Title Match - - With both men in the ring the ref calls for the bell. They lock up in the middle of the ring, RANMA gets the better of the exchange catching CADY with a well placed uppercut sending him staggering backwards. RANMA quickly follows it up with a big boot to the face sending CADY down to the mat hard. RANMA climbs on the back of the downed CADY and applies a camel clutch. CADY sticks his foot on the ropes causing the ref to have to break up the hold. RANMA breaks it up but not before utilizing the 5 count to inflict a little more pain on CADY. After breaking the hold RANMA tries to stay on the attack but is met with a punch to the midsection by CADY. CADY now plants RANMA with a sick looking DDT! CADY with the cover, 1.. KICKOUT! CADY couldn't even squeeze a two count out of it. CADY goes to pull RANMA to his feet but RANMA counters with a small package. KICKOUT! Not even a one count on that one, its going to take more than that to keep either one of these guys down tonight. They both get to their feet around the same time and lock up again, irish whip by CADY, RANMA bounces of the ropes CADY tries a clothesline but RANMA ducks it. RANMA bounces off the opposite ropes and decapitates CADY with a huge clothesline sending him tumbling over backwards. RANMA quickly scoops CADY up and slams him back down to the mat with a power bomb. Cover by RANMA. 1.. 2.. KICKOUT! RANMA just couldn't keep him down. RANMA now stomps on CADY a couple of times before lifting him up. RANMA now doing for a pile driver, reversed by CADY who counters with a big back body drop. CADY keeps the pressure on as he slaps on a dragon sleeper, keeping RANMA grounded. This time its RANMA who grabs the ropes causing the ref to break the hold. CADY breaks up the dragon sleeper and begins to pull RANMA to his feet only to get a thumb to the eye for his troubles. CADY turns around and BOOM!!! RANMA connects with the SAOTOME SERENADE!!!1.. 2...THREE!!!! And this one is over folks. A brave effort by THE “X” EFFECT!
WINNER: RANMA SAOTOME The scene cuts to the back where we see JENSEN KENT talking to CHRIS PAGE who is getting ready for the main event.
JENSEN: You ready for this? CCP: Ready? This is FAMINE OF THE VILE we’re talking about man! That old bastard won’t last long. Just be prepared to come out and hand me that title yourself!
JENSEN: You got it champ. But be careful, this guy is dangerous. CCP: FAMINE isn’t dangerous. I know him like the back of my hand. Are you ok? You seem nervous.
JENSEN: Me? No, no I’m just a bit jumpy. Too much coffee maybe. CCP: Ok man, I’ll see you out there. The scene then cuts to a commercial break. MAIN EVENT CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE vs. FAMINE OF THE VILE - - Devil's Playground Match - - The show now comes back from the last commercial break and we see DERRICK DIAMOND and FLASH ROTTEN making their way down to ringside where they get ready to call the main event between FAMINE and CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE. The fans erupt as Chronic Chris PAGE and FAMINE both appear on the XTRON. A massive FAMINE chant begins to echo throughout the arena. They both appear to be walking towards the entrance area backstage from separate portions of the building.
DERRICK: My god partner can you believe this? We are sitting here on an XWF show, about to call one of the classics from our days at WGWF.
FLASH: It's almost as if nothing has changed DIAMOND. Once again we get to be a part of history as tonight, CHRONIC CHRIS PAGE, my favorite to win this, takes on the sadistic old dinosaur in FAMINE.
DERRICK: You've never liked FAMINE have you? FLASH: Absolutely not! I hate that bastard. I hope PAGE crushes him in his own creation. DERRICK: A creation we are all too familiar with. But right now, let's get ready for the match of the century!
Suddenly, Zero by Smashing Pumpkins begins to play as the one and only CHRIS PAGE makes his way out to the arena. The entire arena is filled with boos as the CHRONIC one walks down the ramp with a smug look on his face.
DERRICK: And there he is folks, the self proclaimed "most controversial athlete on the planet"
FLASH: Self proclaimed DIAMOND? This guy IS the real deal. FAMINE knows that this is the only chance he has left before retirement kicks in.
CHRIS makes his way into the ring and looks around at the monstrosity that was created by FAMINE. FLASH ROTTEN stands up and claps for PAGE.
FLASH: Ladies and Gentleman, you are looking at your next XWF World Champion! I'm proud to be here tonight to be calling this. Once again I get to be a part of history!
A massive pop is heard as Master of Puppets begins to play. FAMINE comes out from the back and stands in the middle of the ramp entrance as he looks at PAGE and then at the Devil's Playground. He starts to make his way down to ringside as he does not take his eyes off of PAGE for one second.
DERRICK: And there he is folks. The Demon King himself. The man who will not quit even at almost 48 years old. But no matter what you say, FAMINE is definitely a man to respect!
The crowd is on their feet as FAMINE enters the ring and both men lock eyes. The ref closes the door behind FAMINE and it looks like this is it!
The bell rings and both men go nose to nose and don't move or blink. The crowd is deafening as they await the first strike. PAGE makes the first move and talks some trash before slapping FAMINE. FAMINE just shrugs it off as if it was nothing! And then the fists begin to fly. With each PAGE strike we hear boos and with each FAMINE counter their are cheers. They trade several right hands before PAGE drives a knee to the gut gaining another pop from the crowd and then slamming FAMINE on the ramp itself! A CCP SUCKS chant is heard as we see PAGE mouth off to them, he then meets FAMINE who is getting to his feet. PAGE grabs him by the hair and take him towards the steel cage wall throwing him right into it!
FLASH: Ohhhhh I bet that hurt! FAMINE might be in trouble early on if he can let PAGE just man handle him like that.
DERRICK: I don't think that was man handling FLASH. PAGE just got the early advantage. FAMINE gets to his feet as PAGE tries to apply a front face lock similar to a crossface. FAMINE looks to fight his way out of it trying to push himself up to his feet using his hands on the mat. He gets to one knee and then looks to stand but PAGE takes him down with a neck breaker. PAGE picks up FAMINE and whips him across the ring into the ropes. FAMINE bounces back as PAGE looks for a hiptoss only to have FAMINE counter with a hiptoss of his own. FAMINE looks to pick up PAGE but PAGE takes both feet pushing them up in the air kicking FAMINE back to the mat. PAGE is quick to his feet and begins to come towards FAMINE, FAMINE takes PAGE down with a leg sweep. Both men get to their feet and come towards each other as FAMINE snaps PAGE with an arm drag. PAGE is back up and he snaps FAMINE with an arm drag. Both men are up and look for a double drop kick neither man connecting and they quickly get to their feet with a Stale Mate and the fans roar in approval as CCP and FAMINE both nod their heads towards each other before circling each other once more.
DERRICK: Did you see that FLASH? They actually nodded at each other. I have to admit I'm more then a little surprised that these guys are actually wrestling and not trying to kill each other like we expected.
FLASH: I think PAGE is just waiting for the right moment to strike. He's just toying with the old man.
DERRICK: I'm sure there is no love lost between these two but it looks like tonight might just be a whole new chapter in a rivalry that doesn't seem to wanna die out.
FLASH: Unless FAMINE dies. Which from the looks of him death seems to be closer than he thinks.
Both men lock up once again, this time with FAMINE getting the upper hand. FAMINE goes behind PAGE and hits a huge Full Nelson Slam! FAMINE then starts to climb one of the corners, hoping to pull down a barbed wire bat but PAGE plants gets to his feet quickly and hits a forearm into his lower back forcing FAMINE back down. PAGE slams FAMINE’S head into the turnbuckle and then hits a back suplex. PAGE follows up with a series of stomps before applying a standing leg lock. PAGE wrenches on FAMINE'S leg as he tries to find a way out of the hold.
FLASH: This is what I came to see DIAMOND. PAGE at his best and FAMINE taking his usual beating.
DERRICK: Don't we go through this each time? You always say something about FAMINE and then out of nowhere he bounces back.
FLASH: Yeah but this time i'm confident. There is no way that FAMINE will walk out of here the winner tonight.

FAMINE makes it to his feet and grabs at PAGE, who clubs FAMINE in the back with the bat. FAMINE is relentless, though, grabbing at the bat and forcing it out of PAGE’S hand, and then tossing PAGE off the top turn buckle, sending him crashing into the middle of the ring. FAMINE picks up the chair and stalks over, leaning the barb wire onto PAGE'S head and then climbing the turnbuckle.

PAGE rolls around in pain, flinging the chair off of him and revealing that he’s bleeding profusely as well. FAMINE is slow to get to his feet as well with his leg having landed onto the chair. He holds it in pain before starting to slowly stand up. He finds PAGE staggering and hits him with a reverse Impaler DDT.
