XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Card

XWF Classic Match 1: Hardcore Tag Match: Honkey Lighthouse & Johnny Mace against Famine of the Vile & Scott Young

c. 25 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MAIN EVENT HONKEY LIGHTHOUSE & JOHNNY MACE vs. FAMINE OF THE VILE & SCOTT YOUNG - - Hardcore Tag Match - - FAMINE is standing in the ring watching MACE and HONKEY argue about who is going to start the match. YOUNG slides a chair in the ring, and FAMINE picks it up and walks over to HONKEY tapping him on the shoulder. HONKEY turns around to catch a shot to the head from FAMINE. MACE climbs through the ropes, and stands in his corner as FAMINE starts hammering away on HONKEY. YOUNG gets in with a 2 x 4 in hand. He walks over to HONKEY, and cocks the board back, but he turns and smacks MACE off the ring apron. YOUNG slides to the outside and drops the board. He starts kicking YOUNG in the ribs over and over.
On the inside HONKEY has taken advantage and is stomping away at FAMINE in the corner. HONKEY slides to the outside and searches under the ring and he pulls out a Louisville slugger. He slides in the ring and is quick to realize FAMINE is on the outside helping YOUNG assault his partner MACE. HONKEY slowly walks over to the ropes and watches on as YOUNG and FAMINE take turns swinging chairs at MACE. YOUNG turns and looks up at HONKEY who is just smiling. YOUNG leaves FAMINE to handle MACE and slides in the ring with his chair in hand. HONKEY swings away and breaks the bat over his back. HONKEY is amazed at the durability of the wooden bat, but has little time to think about it as YOUNG cracks him across the forehead with the chair laying him out. YOUNG laughs as he picks up a piece of the bat and snaps it with his hands into two more pieces; He throws the pieces on HONKEY and turns to see FAMINE pushing a ladder in the ring. YOUNG lays the ladder over HONKEY and FAMINE catapults himself over the top rope and nails a splash on the ladder injuring himself and HONKEY. On the outside MACE pulls himself together, and goes under the ring pulling out a toolbox. He rummages through it pulling out a torque wrench; MACE slides in the ring and smacks YOUNG in the back of the head with the wrench splitting him open. FAMINE stands up and charges at MACE, but gets laid out at the hands of the torque wrench. MACE slides to the outside and pulls 4 tables from under the ring. He sets up 2 of them parallel to each other, and then sets the other two up on top of them. He gets back in the ring only to catch boots to the face and ribcage from YOUNG and FAMINE who are now standing. HONKEY gets to his feet during this, and grabs FAMINE by the neck and drops him back onto the ladder. YOUNG turns his attention to HONKEY and starts slugging it out with him in the center of the ring. YOUNG takes the advantage with a cheap shot by kicking HONKEY in the nuts. YOUNG pulls a chair in front of him, and lays it flat. HONKEY stumbles into him, and YOUNG piledrives him hard into the chair. MACE pulls himself up by use of the ropes, and moves toward the turnbuckle isolating himself from the action. FAMINE stands up, and points to the tables. YOUNG cocks a smile, and the two of them drag HONKEY over to the tables. YOUNG hooks HONKEY between his legs as FAMINE picks up the torque wrench and pounds it into his back. YOUNG lifts HONKEY up and FAMINE and he deliver a super powerbomb sending HONKEY through the 4 tables stacked on the outside. YOUNG smiles and starts to laugh as HONKEY lies motionless on the outside with blood pouring from his forehead. YOUNG gets down on all fours as FAMINE runs off the ropes and leaps off of his back landing an Asai moonsault on HONKEY. FAMINE pins him 1…2… KICK OUT!
This has been one hell of a match! These four men have shown the fans one brutal match tonight. Who is going to walk out of here? How is this going to end? Will it end? That remains to be seen as this match looks to be just about over…
Wait… Isn’t that… That’s DEMON TED SOUL’s music!!! What the hell? Well this has already been one hell of a night, so whatever happens now will only add to the chaos we have seen. DEMON TED SOUL is just standing at the entrance looking on. The camera goes back to the ring as the match continues. Wait!!! DEMON TED is now running toward the ring. He dives in and stands face to face with FAMINE OF THE VILE. They stare at each other hard. Like two enemies in a battlefield. FAMINE pushes DEMON TED, but he just shrugs it off and comes back with a shove of his own. FAMINE now slaps DEMON TED!!! OH!! That slap could be heard all the way up in the rafters. DEMON TED grabs his face, he is now looking at FAMINE and starts to laugh. He is actually just laughing at that hard slap he just received. HONKEY LIGHTHOUSE comes up to grab DEMON TED by the shoulder., but just before HONKEY could say anything, DEMON TED kicks him square in the groin sending HONKEY down to the floor crashing in pain!!!! Mace is now in the ring! But as soon as he comes in Young and DEMON TED knocks him down and begin to stomp on the Vampire man!!! FAMINE is now covering HONKEY…1……2……3!!!!! THREE!!!! FAMINE HAS JUST PINNED HONKEY LIGHTHOUSE!!!!
WINNERS: FAMINE OF THE VILE & SCOTT YOUNG FAMINE has defeated HONKEY LIGHTHOUSE becoming the first man since HONKEY’s debut to take him out!!! FAMINE, Young and now DEMON TED SOUL are attacking these two bloody and beaten men. FAMINE now goes to get that TV Championship Title. He walks in the ring and looks at HONKEY who is still on the ground in pain. FAMINE looks at the title and then back at LIGHTHOUSE. He holds the title in the air yelling that the title is his as the camera fades to black….
