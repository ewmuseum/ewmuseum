XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Card

XWF Classic Match 5: 'Buried Alive' Gauntlet Match: Famine of the Vile against Andrew Gibson, Shawn Matters, Ace Vincent and JR Blake

c. 25 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MAIN EVENT FAMINE OF THE VILE vs. ANDREW GIBSON SHAWN MATTERS ACE VINCENT JR BLAKE - - "Buried Alive" Gauntlet Match - - The match starts off in the ring, as the camera pans about the four graves in the snow, off to the right of the arena. And FAMINE is standing in the ring, across from ANDREW GIBSON who is still scarred from the XTREME TITLE Match last week. The referee sporting a fur coat designed like a referee shirt is standing in the middle of the ring, as he holds up the TV TITLE and the timekeeper rings the bell. FAMINE wastes no time in charging ANDREW GIBSON, as FAMINE delivers a big boot to the side of ANDREW GIBSON’s face. ANDREW GIBSON falls backwards into the corner, and FAMINE starts chopping at the chest of ANDREW GIBSON. ANDREW GIBSON rolls out, and FAMINE charges forward with a clothesline, but ANDREW GIBSON ducks, and goes for one of his own, as FAMINE drops down and hits a drop toe hold, sending GIBSON towards the ropes. FAMINE, walks backwards and charges the ropes, and leaps through the ropes with a version of a suicide dive, and yanks GIBSON with crashing to the matted outside with a BULLDOG! The crowd chants XWF! XWF! XWF! FAMINE stands up, and stands up GIBSON launching him towards the steel steps, CRASH! GIBSON bounces off and rolls to the guard railing wall. FAMINE waits just a second, as GIBSON slowly starts to rise, and FAMINE charges driving his knee into the head of GIBSON, sending it into the guard railing wall. As GIBSON drops backwards, FAMINE lifts GIBSON up and brings him towards the rampway, of the entrance, as FAMINE bodyslams GIBSON to the pure cement floor, the shivers of the crowd gets louder and louder as they grow near the mound of snow. GIBSON sits up and brings his arm around his back, as FAMINE stands him up and starts punching him backwards, and GIBSON walks towards the mound of snow, as he brings to feel the heat of his native land. FAMINE gets a punch to the mouth, as GIBSON grabs FAMINE about the head and charged toward the mound, and with a bulldog, GIBSON closes the momentum door on FAMINE. GIBSON looks at the grave, and next to it is a festive snowman, with a Snow Job “06” T-shirt, and GIBSON smiles, as he suplexes FAMINE up onto the top of the mound, near the first grave. FAMINE sits up with the coldness finally taking its toll on FAMINE. GIBSON stands up and grabs the shovel from the side of the grave and takes a baseball stance, getting ready for FAMINE, as FAMINE slowly stands, but ducks the swing from GIBSON and pulls GIBSON into the grave with him, as the camera shows the hallowed out grave as GIBSON and FAMINE fight in it, rights and lefts. As FAMINE kicks GIBSON backwards into a sitting position. FAMINE leaps up and stands onto GIBSON’s head to climb out, as FAMINE claws at the snow, piling in a few handfuls. But it’s not enough as the referee can still see GIBSON’s body and face. FAMINE stands fully and launches the snow man into the grave, completely burying ANDREW GIBSON!
ANDREW GIBSON BURIED ALIVE! Now here comes SHAWN MATTERS. FAMINE is standing there waiting anxiously for his next opponent. SHAWN rushes at FAMINE but slips on the snow and lands face first. He pulls his head up and it’s covered in white. He spits some out and wipes his face as he now gets up to a waiting FAMINE. FAMINE goes for a shot to the face but SHAWN takes some snow and throws it in FAMINE’S face giving him a chance to stand up and attack. Forearm shots to the face of FAMINE as SHAWN tried to take advantage and win this match tonight.. SHAWN goes for a suplex and nails it sending FAMINE crashing down to the snow. SHAWN goes in for the kill as he picks FAMINE up and heads toward the empty grave to end this thing and become the TV champ. SHAWN now hits a hard DDT sending FAMINE’S head into the snow. Now SHAWN is on his feet posing for the crowd. It seems to me he is wasting time instead of going for it. If he had any chance of winning now would be the time. He now goes over to FAMINE and picks him up, wait, FAMINE just grabbed some snow from the ground. HA! Oh my god! FAMINE just put the snow inside SHAWN’S tights! SHAWN is screaming and jumping around trying to get the snow out. Now FAMINE comes from behind, BAM!! Polish hammer to the back of the head.
SHAWN falls into the empty grave like a ton of bricks. Another man, down and out, as FAMINE shovels like a mad man the snow over the body of SHAWN MATTERS
SHAWN MATTERS BURIED ALIVE! The crowd is going wild as SOA fans all over the arena cheer him on. Unbelievable, the man has taken out 3 without breaking a sweat. Now here comes the final participant in ACE VINCENT. Ace takes the shovel and whacks FAMINE on the head sending him to the ground. Now he goes over to FAMINE and tries to hit him again but FAMINE moves out of the way and the shovel hits nothing but the cold snowy floor. FAMINE grabs the leg of VINCENT sending him falling backwards and the shovel goes flying back. FAMINE is now up, bleeding just a little from the shot to the head by VINCENT with that shovel. FAMINE sees the blood and has a look of anger on his face. He picks VINCENT up and hits him with a hard knee to the gut. VINCENT is out of breath as FAMINE now suplexes him to the ground nearly sending him into the grave but somehow ACE manages to get out of the way and avoids falling in.
Now ACE is back to his feet. FAMINE comes toward him but ACE hits him with a spin kick to the gut. FAMINE is down on one knee now as VINCENT runs towards FAMINE only to start sliding past him and falling face first near the stage area. ACE VINCENT is now on his feet but doesn’t see FAMINE coming behind him and gets sent flying backwards with a German suplex a la Kurt Angle style. ACE VINCENT is now bleeding from the back of the head as the snow begins to turn red. But VINCENT doesn’t look like he’s going to give up just yet. VINCENT is now up again as FAMINE now comes toward him with the shovel. FAMINE swings it but ACE catches it with his hands and pulls FAMINE in, head butting him in the face. FAMINE reels back holding his nose. ACE now has the shovel in his hands and takes a swing at FAMINE. Just at the last second FAMINE ducks and ACE begins to spin uncontrollably. FAMINE hits a roundhouse kick to the jaw-sending ACE flying off his feet and into the grave. FAMINE quickly hurries to push a large mound of snow into the grave covering the body of ACE VINCENT. The referee signals for the bell….
ACE VINCENT BURIED ALIVE! As the next opponent, JR BLAKE’s music hits, JR BLAKE comes out wearing a snow suit, and he’s ready to be buried, if that’s the case, he’s got legwarmers, and a portable heater. FAMINE walks over to the entranceway, off of the mound, and JR BLAKE points up to the rafters, and FAMINE looks like a goofy, and JR BLAKE hammers the portable heater over the head of FAMINE, as FAMINE drops to the cement. Blood pours from a cut on the head of FAMINE, as he gets angry and sits up, as heating coils drop in front of him. JR BLAKE smiles, as out from his snow suit, he pulls a FLAMETHROWER!
CHEATER, CHEATER the crowd yells, as JR BLAKE smacks the FLAMETHROWER over the head of FAMINE, as JR BLAKE drops the FLAMETHROWER and grabs FAMINE, and drags him to the edge of the mound. FAMINE stands up, as blood pours from his cut, and litters the snow, turning it into what looks like a Cherry Snow Cone. FAMINE punches at JR BLAKE, and JR BLAKE punches back. FAMINE and JR BLAKE flurry back and forth, as JR BLAKE slips a bit on the mound, and drops backwards into a snow pile. FAMINE stands over JR BLAKE, a looks to grab him up, but JR BLAKE starts throwing snow up into the air, causing FAMINE to walk backwards, but, FAMINE picks up the shovel, and swings it, ripping into the side of JR BLAKE’s head. JR BLAKE drops to the mound of snow, as blood pours into the snow, from his head.
SCREEEEECH!! SMACK! NO! It’s the HEARSE! The Hearse is back in the picture and it just smacked into FAMINE! Out from the driver side, comes YURIKO! It’s KILLJOY’S MANAGER! YURIKO walks over to FAMINE as she leaves the door wide open. And YURIKO walks over to FAMINE, as FAMINE swings the shovel at her, but YURIKO parries, and swing kicks the shovel up into the air, leaving the body of FAMINE wide open. So YURIKO front kicks the stomach of FAMINE, as FAMINE sits back into the mound of snow. But suddenly from behind her, comes KILLJOY, and FAMINE stands and swings the shovel, smacking KILLJOY in the back, and KILLJOY turns and clotheslines FAMINE. FAMINE rolls off closer to the crowd, as from the huge crowded group of fans, CHASM COMES! CHASM, is back, and he tackles KILLJOY, CHASM starts punching and smashing KILLJOY, as YURIKO grabs CHASM and kicks him in the leg, and delivers a spinning wheel kick knocking him backwards into the railing wall, behind them. SUDDENLY PATIENCE PRYCE runs, and attacks YURIKO, as they start pulling hair, and punching and kicking. As that, from around the other end of the Hearse, JR BLAKE stands up and falls backwards into the Hearse, and walks out only to be attacked by, a one ZACH RIZZA!
ZACH RIZZA comes over and starts punching at the cut over JR BLAKE’s left eye, and JUGGALO comes out from nowhere, and slams ZACH’s face into the back of the Hearse, sending ZACH RIZZA stumbling backwards. JUGGALO turns to see GLASS CASKET watching in wait, as JUGGALO charges first, and SPEARS GLASS CASKET! Through the wide open driver side door. JUGGALO stands up and walks back over to ZACH RIZZA as ZACH RIZZA delivers a hard spinning neck breaker to JUGGALO! Meanwhile FAMINE is crawling up the mound, and in the place of the snowman …
IT’S TRENT GEIN! TRENT GEIN grabs FAMINE and headbutts him, and from the edge of the mound TRENT GEIN pulls the chainsaw! BUT FAMINE, kicks TRENT in the gem baggy, and TRENT GEIN drops to his knees. FAMINE, kicks the chainsaw on, as gas or whatever spills from one of the compartments, as FAMINE turns and looks at TRENT GEIN, who is wide open, as SCOTT YOUNG scurries from the back and up the mound of snow, and to the side of FAMINE, he’s yelling something at FAMINE, as, FAMINE is covered in blood, and snow, as he looks at TRENT, TRENT looks scared …WHAT…TRENT IS AFRAID! SCOTT YOUNG taps FAMINE, as suddenly KILLJOY spears him from the mound! The tumble down the snow and onto the hard warmer cement. FAMINE turns his attention, away as TRENT GEIN grins with the victory, and grabs the chainsaw kicking FAMINE backwards into a grave! JR BLAKE climbs up the mound, and JR BLAKE looks down, and JR BLAKE tells TRENT GEIN to help shovel the bastard in, but suddenly from behind JR BLAKE is JUGGALO, as JUGGALO pushes JR BLAKE into the hole, and JUGGALO dives of the mound and leaps onto the down ZACH RIZZA! What was that?! What that the plan?! TRENT GEIN smiles as he nods and buries both men in the snow as he hacks away at the snow around the grave with his chainsaw! What is going on?!
But suddenly, from the side of TRENT GEIN, CHASM tackles him, and rolls down the hill punching and kicking at each other, as CHASM wants that TAG TITLE GOLD! As suddenly a HAND!
A hand grows taller and taller from the grave … suddenly from the hand comes a head! It’s FAMINE! It’s FAMINE! As FAMINE climbs out, the referee hands him his TV TITLE and tries to signal for the bell, but falls.
He has definitely proven that he is a force to be reckoned with. He took on four men and defeated them all, retaining his TV title. I don’t think there are many men out there who would be able to outlast four other men in a match such as the one we saw here tonight. The fans go nuts as you hear SOA chants all over the arena. Simply amazing!
WINNER: FAMINE OF THE VILE
