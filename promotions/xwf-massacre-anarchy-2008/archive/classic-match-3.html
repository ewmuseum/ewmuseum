XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Card

XWF Classic Match 3: Standard Match: Famine of the Vile against Johnny Mace

c. 25 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MAIN EVENT FAMINE OF THE VILE vs. JOHNNY MACE - - Standard Match - - JOHNNY MACE comes out to the ring and is cheered by the crowd. They are going nuts in the arena as the match is about to get under way. JOHNNY is now in the ring posing for the crowd and gets a good pop. Killswitch Engage – End of Heartache now plays. FAMINE comes out to the crowd booing in unison. FAMINE has a microphone in his hands.
FAMINE: JOHNNY, JOHNNY, JOHNNY. I told you that tonight you would lose whether you wanted to or not. Now, the first thing I want you to do is look up at the X-Tron.
Everyone including JOHNNY looks up….We see a room, kind of dark and someone sitting on a chair with their back turned to the camera. We also see someone standing up with their back turned as well. Then suddenly the chair turns… Wait… THAT’S ANGEL!!!! THAT’S JOHNNY MACE’S GIRL!!! AND IT’S JENSEN KENT!! What the hell is JENSEN KENT doing with Angel? My god!! She’s tied to that chair. Why would he do this? Why would JENSEN KENT ruin his career by joining FAMINE OF THE VILE and the SOA?
JENSEN KENT: Surprised to see me? Yeah I guess I would be too. If someone had my girlfriend all tied up to a chair and me not being here to protect her, well I just don’t know what I’d do. I might just run to go save her. You wanna do that don’t you? You want to run right out of that ring to come rescue your bitch. But you’re not going to do that. See JOHNNY, you and I have a score to settle. And we will. But right now you may want to focus on your match.
JOHNNY is furious, his face is turning as red as a tomato, you can just see the anger building in him. He is getting ready to run out of the ring to attack FAMINE when FAMINE picks up the mic to speak.
FAMINE: Now, now there killer. Hold on a sec. I know what you want to do, you want to run up here and beat me senseless for doing this. But the second thing I want you to do is to call off whatever reinforcements you may have back there. Let them know this will be a one on one match and that no one will interfere. Once you do that I will be making my way down the ramp and steeping into the ring.
JOHNNY nods. FAMINE now looks at JOHNNY with a smile on his face. FAMINE: Now…This is how the match will go. I win you lose. Simple. You know why? Because you see in about 5 seconds you won’t be able to move. You’ll be on the mat, out like a light and I will be pinning you for the 1…2… 3…How you ask. Well you see it’s like this…
Wait a minute! Look behind you JOHNNY…BAM!!!! JOHNNY MACE just got smacked in the back of the head with that sledgehammer by SCOTT YOUNG. DEMON TED is now in the ring as well. He picks up MACE as FAMINE now begins to walk down the ramp toward the ring. Damn that FAMINE OF THE VILE, we should have expected this from them. It was all a set up to beat JOHNNY MACE easily so he wouldn’t lose that title. Maybe JOHNNY MACE is right. Maybe FAMINE is afraid to face him one on one. DEMON TED just hit a huge power bomb. JOHNNY is out like a light, he is showing no signs of movement. FAMINE is now in the ring, he hasn’t even taken off the damn TV title off his waist. He shoves the ref and tells him to count. The ref reluctantly gets on his knees to count the 1….2…..WAIT!!! JOHNNY MACE KICKED OUT!!! The SOA are now stomping on MACE, kicking him down like a dog. FAMINE signals to SCOTT YOUNG to put him up. But just at that moment, FAMINE tells DEMON TED to put him up!!! The 7’3” giant puts MACE on his shoulders. FAMINE now climbs the turnbuckle. This time he takes off the TV title. FAMINE is in the air. CRASH!!!!! FAMINE just hit the hurricanrana off of DEMON TED’s shoulders sending MACE head first to the mat. Now FAMINE goes for the cover. The ref doesn’t want to count. But DEMON TED grabs him and makes him kneel forcing him to count the three… 1…2… THREE!! Damn it, not like this. This can’t be the way this match ends.
STILL TV CHAMP: FAMINE OF THE VILE
