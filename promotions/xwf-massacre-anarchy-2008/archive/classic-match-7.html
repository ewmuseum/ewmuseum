XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Card

XWF Classic Match 7: Standard Match: Famine of the Vile against Kore

c. 25 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MAIN EVENT FAMINE OF THE VILE vs. KORE - - Standard Match - - Suddenly the lights in the arena begin to flicker like a stroble light. "I'M NOT AFRAID OF A GOD, THAT DOESN'T EXIST!" "I'M NOT A SLAVE, TO A WORLD THAT DOESN'T GIVE A....." BOOM! An explosion goes off in the middle of the ramp as the lights in the arena go out. Fire erupts from all four corners in the ring as Killswitch Engage - "The End of Heartache" Begins to play. The crowd knows that FAMINE OF THE VILE is on his way out. As the lights come back on, FAMINE stands at the entrance and takes off his shades to look around and the crowd. The reaction is deafening as he begins to walk down to the ring. The former Demon King now struts down to the ring as he smirks at the fans and even slaps a couple of high fives. As he gets in the ring he walks over to the right turnbuckle and stands on the ropes putting both arms in the air. He slaps his chest a couple of times and gets down awaiting the arrival of his opponent.
Enter Sandman plays and KORE non-chalantly walks down to the ring and climbs in. The match starts with KORE launching FAMINE into the corner followed in by a splash. KORE kicks FAMINE in the corner a few times followed by a big suplex. KORE covers, but only gets a two count. KORE nails FAMINE with a hard right and kicks him a few more times down to the corner. FAMINE fights back with a knee to the gut followed by some hard rights. KORE comes back with a quick jab on FAMINE followed by a stiff chest chop. KORE adds in two more chest chops and FAMINE crawls around screaming in pain. FAMINE calls for a timeout. When KORE approaches, FAMINE grabs him and tosses him through the second rope by his trunks. FAMINE sends KORE into the steel ring post and then throws KORE back into the ring. FAMINE hooks the leg, but KORE kicks out at one. FAMINE goes after KORE's left shoulder that hit the ring post earlier with a few punches and locks in an armbar. KORE stands up while in the armbar and FAMINE gets in a quick back suplex on KORE for a two count.
FAMINE gets up and KORE hits a big chop to his chest. KORE launches FAMINE again towards the opposite corner and follows it up with a big headbutt. FAMINE comes back with some punches to the gut, but KORE takes down FAMINE and then chokes him with his boot. FAMINE leaves the ring and starts heading up the ramp when KORE cuts him off with a punch to the back. KORE throws FAMINE back in the ring, picks him up and nails him face first off the corner. KORE quiets the crowd and then chops FAMINE with huge impact. KORE looks to do it one more time when FAMINE rolls out. KORE follows and FAMINE gets in a kick to the gut. KORE charges, but FAMINE moves and KORE hits the ring post shoulder post. FAMINE pulls KORE by the arm one more time into the ring post. KORE rolls back in holding his shoulder when FAMINE applies an armbar submission. KORE fights out of it rolling towards the corner and flipping FAMINE. KORE attempts a Kore on the Kobb, but FAMINE breaks it up.
FAMINE nails KORE in the knee when he charges at him. FAMINE then comes from behind KORE and hits a huge back suplex! FAMINE covers KORE...1...2...kick out. KORE fights back with some chops. FAMINE comes off the ropes and hits a shoulder charge. KORE stays up. FAMINE goes off the ropes and finally takes down KORE with a big boot. FAMINE covers, but only gets a two count. FAMINE again applies a submission on KORE's right arm that was slammed against the ring post earlier. KORE fights back with some quick chops. As FAMINE comes off the ropes, KORE executes a big spinebuster. Both men are down as the referee starts the count. The referee gets to 6 and KORE flips up using the ropes! KORE charges at FAMINE in the corner but FAMINE moves and FAMINE take KORE down with a hip toss. FAMINE then applies an armbar back on KORE in the middle of the ring. FAMINE continues to keep it applied with pressure. KORE fights his way back up to his feet and gets in a gutwrench suplex followed with a elbow drop and an Irish whip into the ropes FAMINE fights back with a clothesline and a cover that results in a two count. KORE gets in two big chest chops on FAMINE, but FAMINE fights back with a flying shoulderblock. FAMINE covers, but only gets a two count. FAMINE goes back to work on KORE's right shoulder. KORE gets back on his feet, but FAMINE drives his shoulder into KORE's bad right arm. KORE tries to fight back with some right hands. KORE then gets in a quick back body drop on FAMINE. KORE ducks a clothesline attempt by FAMINE and gets in a few of his own. KORE then drives FAMINE to the mat with a running bulldog, covers, but only gets a two count. KORE sends FAMINE into the corner, follows it up with a clothesline, KORE does the same in an opposite corner and ends that with a clothesline as well. KORE drops FAMINE to the mat with a pumphandle slam and then kicks him in the abdomen. FAMINE hits a big boot on KORE, but KORE stays up and takes out FAMINE with a short arm clothesline. KORE goes up to the top, but FAMINE cuts him off with some hard rights. FAMINE then grabs KORE and comes off with a TOP ROPE BLOOD DRIVER! FAMINE shakes off the cobwebs and covers 1…2…3
WINNER: FAMINE OF THE VILE
