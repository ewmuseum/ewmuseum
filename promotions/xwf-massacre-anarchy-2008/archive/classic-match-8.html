XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Card

XWF Classic Match 8: Standard Match: Famine of the Vile against Brad Pierce

c. 25 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MAIN EVENT FAMINE OF THE VILE vs. BRAD PIERCE - - Standard Match - - FAMINE's music blares as he heads out from the curtains. FAMINE stops and looks at the fans, but is jumped from behind by BRAD PIERCE. PIERCE hits FAMINE with a chair on the back of the head and FAMINE falls to one knee. PIERCE picks FAMINE up and throws FAMINE into the barricade, back first. PIERCE picks FAMINE up and throws FAMINE inside the ring. FAMINE is up and when PIERCE gets in FAMINE stomps him on the back of the head. FAMINE pulls PIERCE up and whips PIERCE into the corner. FAMINE sets up in the corner, charges, and goes for the clothesline and connects. PIERCE gets on his knees and then falls flat on his face. FAMINE goes for a quick pin. 1....2.. NO! PIERCE kicks out. FAMINE goes on the outside and pulls out a steel chair, FAMINE brings the chair into the ring and waits for PIERCE to get up. PIERCE does so and FAMINE swings but PIERCE moves behind FAMINE and when FAMINE turns back around, he is met with a poke in the eye by PIERCE to give PIERCE some more time to recuperate. FAMINE drops the chair to buy him more time and PIERCE picks it up and connects a heavy shot on the top of FAMINE's skull. FAMINE's down and out, PIERCE goes for the pin. 1....2...NO! FAMINE gets a shoulder up just before the count of three. PIERCE is irate at the referee in what he thinks was a slow count. Slowly, FAMINE gets on his hands and knees and crawls as fast as he can to take out PIERCE's knee from behind. PIERCE goes down! Now, FAMINE struggles to get to his feet. He has both of PIERCE's legs and APPLIES A FIGURE FOUR! PIERCE IS STRUGGLING TO FIGHT OFF THE PAIN AND TURN OVER! HE'S ALMOST THERE! PIERCE TURNS FAMINE OVER! FAMINE grabs the rope closest to him and PIERCE breaks the hold. PIERCE is struggling to get up to his feet after the beating PIERCE's leg took already. PIERCE grabs the chair to hoist him up. FAMINE is back to his feet as well, but is brought right back down with a chair shot to the back from BRAD PIERCE. FAMINE winces in pain as he falls to the ground again. PIERCE is now furiously hitting whaling away at FAMINE's back. FAMINE shouts in pain but crawls out of the ring for less damage. FAMINE grabs a fire extinguisher and hides it with his body. PIERCE comes out of the ring and tries to turn FAMINE over. When PIERCE does, PIERCE is met with a bunch of smoke from the hose of the fire extinguisher. The crowd erupts as FAMINE gets up and nails PIERCE between the eyes and makes PIERCE bleed!! FAMINE goes for the pin. 1....2...NO! PIERCE somehow kicked out! FAMINE grabs PIERCE and places PIERCE against the apron. FAMINE's head is laying on the apron as PIERCE grabs another chair. FAMINE gets up and is met with a chair shot to the head. Both men are now fully engulfed in blood but PIERCE has the upper hand as PIERCE throws FAMINE back into the ring. PIERCE goes for the cover...1....2...TH.. NO! PIERCE CAN NOT BELIEVE FAMINE KICKED OUT YET AGAIN. Irate, BRAD PIERCE grabs FAMINE by the back of the head and tosses him through the ropes to the outside, sliding out the ring himself to continue in attacking him. PIERCE continues to stomp away and throws FAMINE into the steel steps. PIERCE wastes no time and throws FAMINE back into the ring. FAMINE scrambles back to his feet drives his knee into the gut of BRAD PIERCE. PIERCE kneels on the ground as FAMINE approaches him, delivers multiple kidney shots. PIERCE reels on the ground in pain, as FAMINE stomps away. FAMINE pulls PIERCE to his feet and hits a DDT. He goes for the cover on PIERCE. 1..2.. And PIERCE kicks out with authority. Frustrated FAMINE brings PIERCE to his feet, but this time PIERCE hits a spinebuster, leaving both men on the ground. PIERCE is the first to stir he begins to stalk FAMINE. As FAMINE gets to his feet and turns around, he is met with a superkick right under the chin. BRAD PIERCE heads to the top rope and hits a frog splash. PIERCE goes for the cover. 1..2.. and again FAMINE manages to kickout. PIERCE is shocked, but he again begins to stalk the fallen FAMINE. When FAMINE is to his feet, BRAD suddenly grabs him by the throat. Is he gonna try to chokeslam him? NO! BRAD just let go and then punched FAMINE who is now on the ground spitting out blood. BRAD looks down and smiles before kicking FAMINE in the chest right by the heart. FAMINE clutches his chest in pain as BRAD now climbs the turnbuckle to head toward the top rope. He looks down at FAMINE who is still clutching his chest a bit and flies off.
BOOM!!!! HE MISSED! BRAD MISSED THE ELBOW DROP BY MERE SECONDS. FAMINE is now slowly beginning to get back on his feet while PIERCE is still down holding onto his elbow. FAMINE slumps over in the corner to catch his breath. Out of nowhere, BRAD runs toward him and climbs up. He begins to punch FAMINE in the face as the crowd counts along. 7......8.....FAMINE blocks the next punch and puts a thumb in the eye of PIERCE who quickly grabs his face. FAMINE grabs PIERCE and gets out of the corner. BOOM!! FAMINE just hit a massive powerbomb that leaves PIERCE flat on his back. The crowd is going insane as FAMINE now walks over to go for the pin when out of nowhere PIERCE grabs him into a small package. 1..... 2..... THR.. NO! FAMINE kicks out at the last second. Both he and PIERCE now stand up and start trading blows back and forth. Lefts and rights from both the champ and FAMINE.
BRAD kicks FAMINE right in the gut and signals for the FADE TO BLACK. He goes to grab FAMINE but FAMINE hits him with some hard elbows to the face. He rocks PIERCE back and then sends him flying toward the ropes. PIERCE comes back and FAMINE tosses him up in mid air.. BOOM!!!! HELLSEEKER! FAMINE hits the HELLSEEKER!! PIERCE is down, and so is FAMINE after that powerful move. He slowly reaches over to get the pin. 1..... 2...... NO! BRAD PIERCE KICKED OUT!
HOLY CRAP! FAMINE can't believe it! BRAD PIERCE has just kicked out of the HELLSEEKER! FAMINE takes a moment to catch his breath and then stands up. A serious look on his face now. He waits for PIERCE to get up. PIERCE begins to move, slowly. He's still a bit woozy after that finisher but he manages to get on his feet. He stumbles a bit and then turns around and is met by THE DEVILS SPIKE!!! ANOTHER FINISHER BY THE VILE ONE! FAMINE quickly goes for the pin... 1..... 2..... THREE!!! IT'S OVER! FAMINE HAS DONE IT!! FAMINE OF THE VILE HAS FINALLY WON THE WORLD TITLE!!
WINNER: FAMINE OF THE VILE
