XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Card

Monday Massacre (16 February 2009)

16 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
r FIREFOX and MOZILLA are NOT RECOMMENDED for viewing this program. MATCHES: RAGE vs. DAN FIERCE - - Non-Title Match - - SPICE ONE vs. HUNTER RYAN vs. FAMINE OF THE VILE - - Standard Match - - MAIN EVENT "Chronic" CHRIS PAGE vs. RANMA SAOTOME vs. SCOTT CHARLOTTE - - OWN THE PPV Semi-Final Elimination Match - - The show opens with the cameras in JENSEN KENT’S office. JENSEN sits at his desk and standing next to him is CCP and standing in front of the desk is DAN FIERCE.
CCP: What’s this? RAGE is wearing a mask now? DAN: Yeah, something about becoming the Weapon of the People, I don’t understand it myself.
JENSEN: You hear that PAGE? You kicked his ass so bad he no longer wants to show his face.
All three men laugh, then CCP turns to FIERCE. CCP: You sure we can count on you? DAN: Oh honey, You have NO idea! I am so through with their BS…I’m looking forward to this!
JENSEN: Good. Just go out there and do what you have to. You help us take them out and we’ll make sure that you have protection in case there is any backlash later on down the road.
DAN: That’s all I needed to hear. RAGE vs. DAN FIERCE - - Non-Title Match - - The bell rings and both men go towards the center of the ring. RAGE and DAN stare each other down. The camera zooms in on RAGE’S mask as the crowd doesn’t know what to make of it.
DAN offers his hand to RAGE to start the contest with honor. RAGE looks down at DAN'S hand. He mulls it over and then decides to shake the hand. DAN pulls RAGE in close and they stare at each other some more. RAGE gives DAN a cocky smile and shoves him back. The two of them get in close and decide to lock up. No one gets the advantage for the first couple of seconds. RAGE then gets the advantage and pushes DAN to the corner. The ref comes in and tells RAGE to break it up. He starts a five count. ONE… TWO… THREE… FOUR… RAGE lets go and they go back to the center of the ring. The two of them lock up and this time DAN gets the advantage. DAN pushes RAGE into the corner. The ref starts his five count. ONE… TWO… THREE… RAGE keeps lighting up DAN’S chest with chops. FOUR… DAN tries to get away, but RAGE is in hot pursuit. RAGE grabs DAN by the head and slams him to the ground with a warning from the ref to not pull on the hair. RAGE grabs DAN’S ankle and slams the knee hard into the mat. He then yanks DAN’S leg and does the same thing. RAGE starts to stomp away at the knee. RAGE drapes the leg over the bottom rope. RAGE leaps and drops his weight across it! RAGE does it again and then tries to do it one more time, but DAN rolls out of the way and RAGE lands on his butt. DAN gets up slowly, but he manages to hit a Dropkick to the back of RAGE. DAN tries to shake the pain out of his leg. He bounces off the opposite ropes and hits a Great Muta Elbow to the ribs of RAGE. DAN jumps up and uses his momentum to hit an Arabian Press. Cover… ONE… TWO… RAGE easily kicks out. DAN hits a couple of stomps to the chest of RAGE and then goes for another stomp, but RAGE blocks it and holds onto DAN'S leg. RAGE hits a Dragon Screw Leg whip that puts more pressure on DAN’S leg. RAGE grabs both legs and turns DAN over for a Boston Crab. DAN is really hurting as RAGE puts pressure on the knee. DAN tries to keep calm, but it really hurts. After a few seconds, DAN seems to be unable to hang on anymore and taps out....The ref calls for the bell as this one is over.
WINNER BY SUBMISSION: RAGE RAGE gets up and looks down at DAN who is holding his knee. He extends his hand to his stable mate as DAN looks up a little upset and with a look of sadness. He grabs RAGE’S hand and is helped up. The crowd cheers both men as RAGE gets an unexpected hug from DAN. RAGE shoves him off as both men leave the ring. Before they can set foot out of the ring, One by One by Alter Bridge begins to play as JENSEN KENT makes his way out to the arena to a roar of boos from the crowd.
JENSEN: Well, isn’t that touching. Two friends who fought hard against each other and still remain good friends. That’s actually a good thing. Because you can both keep your friendship going on another show! As of right now, you and the rest of the road crew are no longer a part of MASSACRE!
WHAT?! What does he mean they are no longer on MASSACRE? The scene cuts backstage where FAMINE OF THE VILE is seen coming into the arena. The bottom of the screen shows the words EARLIER TODAY. FAMINE walks into the arena with a gym bag on his shoulder and no VIOLET in sight. As he walks through the door he is hit from the back with a 2x4. FAMINE is out cold as the camera shows the culprit to be none other than SCOTT CHARLOTTE.
SCOTT: I told you I would not let this end until I got my revenge you bastard! SCOTT then drags FAMINE back out of the arena. Where is he taking him? SPICE ONE vs. HUNTER RYAN vs. FAMINE OF THE VILE - - Standard Match - - The match starts with HUNTER charging at SPICE ONE and nailing him with a few shoulder charges in the corner. HUNTER then hits a huge snap suplex on SPICE ONE that rocks the ring! HUNTER kicks SPICE ONE in the gut a few times, then hits a snapmare followed by a stiff kick to the back. HUNTER is a man on a mission tonight as he takes it to SPICE so early in the match. HUNTER gets in a few rights, sends SPICE ONE into the ropes, but SPICE ONE fights back with a stiff kick to the face. HUNTER then fights back with a huge stiff dropkick to the face that sees SPICE ONE stay up for a second and then topple over. HUNTER gets in a few elbows on SPICE ONE in the corner. SPICE ONE comes back with a chest chop on HUNTER, goes off the ropes, attempts a big boot, but HUNTER moves out of the way. SPICE ONE slaps HUNTER and HUNTER comes back with some elbow shots. SPICE ONE fights back with some stiff kicks to the chest sending HUNTER through the ropes, but still staying on the ring apron. SPICE ONE then backs up, charges and nails HUNTER off the ring apron with force. SPICE ONE quickly slips out behind him, sends HUNTER into the ring barricade, and then slides him back in the ring. HUNTER fights back with a chop to the gut. HUNTER hits a few forearms, but SPICE ONE hits a head butt. HUNTER then backdrops SPICE ONE onto the top rope and SPICE ONE falls to the outside. HUNTER gets on the ring apron but SPICE ONE kicks his legs, tripping HUNTER! HUNTER falls on the ring apron and SPICE ONE grabs HUNTER, hitting a spinning airplane move and then letting HUNTER smack right against the barricade with force. SPICE ONE then Irish whips HUNTER into the ring barricade. SPICE ONE measures HUNTER. When HUNTER stands up, SPICE ONE charges and drills HUNTER with a boot that sends HUNTER to his back with force. SPICE ONE drags HUNTER to his feet and whips him towards the ring post. HUNTER’S face bashes off the post as he staggers backwards SPICE ONE hooks him up and nails the LIGHTS OUT reverse DDT! SPICE ONE lifts up HUNTER and presses him over his head and tosses him back into the ring.
In the ring, SPICE ONE hits HUNTER with a few boots. SPICE ONE then hits another big boot to HUNTER's face followed by a big knee drop to the head! When HUNTER gets up, SPICE ONE chops him into the corner. HUNTER fights back with a few kicks, but SPICE ONE hits a huge kick to HUNTER'S chest. HUNTER gasps for air as he stands up. HUNTER gets thrown up on the mat and lands face first, grabbing his chest in pain. SPICE ONE then hits a big back senton splash on HUNTER, covers, but HUNTER barely kicks out after two. HUNTER fights back to his feet nailing SPICE ONE with a few elbows to the gut. SPICE ONE runs toward HUNTER and hits a huge kick to the chest right in the corner. HUNTER bounces off and appears to be dazed from it. SPICE ONE then does a face wash on HUNTER. HUNTER then grabs the boot of SPICE ONE to block anymore. SPICE ONE sends HUNTER back down to the corner and SPICE ONE this time hits a huge running boot to the face with even more force! The crowd goes nuts starting a loud "SPICE ONE" chant. SPICE ONE gets back up, kicking HUNTER around the ring. SPICE ONE chops HUNTER in the corner, HUNTER gets sent to the ring apron, tries to get back in, but SPICE ONE kicks him. SPICE ONE gets in a few forearm shots on HUNTER. SPICE ONE grabs HUNTER, but HUNTER props up and hits a big Pele Kick! HUNTER springboards off the top rope, but SPICE ONE catches him and hits a big power bomb! SPICE ONE then rolls that over into a Boston Crab. HUNTER does get the bottom rope, but SPICE ONE pulls HUNTER back out and applies even more pressure by sitting on the back of HUNTER still applying the Boston Crab. HUNTER counters out of that kicking SPICE ONE in the face a few times, causing SPICE ONE to break it.
When SPICE ONE charges at HUNTER in the corner, HUNTER sends SPICE ONE over the top rope to the outside. SPICE ONE is recovering on the outside near the ring apron. HUNTER then charges and hits a springboard shooting star press, taking out SPICE ONE! HUNTER puts SPICE ONE back in the ring. HUNTER gets up on the top rope, jumps and connects with a forearm to the back of the head. HUNTER covers SPICE ONE, but only gets a two count. SPICE ONE cuts off a charge of HUNTER’s when he scoops him up looking for a DVD but HUNTER quickly counters into a reverse back breaker SPICE ONE is quick to his feet but is very dazed. A dazed SPICE ONE avoids a swinging HUNTER and attempts a release German suplex, but HUNTER lands on his feet. HUNTER then springboards off the ropes and hits a jumping ddt on SPICE ONE! HUNTER covers... 1... 2...SPICE ONE kicks out. The crowd starts a loud "THIS IS AWESOME" chant. HUNTER then kicks SPICE ONE in the chest followed by a few forearms. SPICE ONE shoves HUNTER away and manages to get up. He fights back with a clothesline, absolutely leveling HUNTER in the middle of the ring. SPICE ONE covers HUNTER, but HUNTER kicks out right after one! SPICE ONE can't believe it. He hits a big underhook powerbomb, but HUNTER once again kicks out after one! SPICE ONE looks at HUNTER in amazement.
SPICE ONE gets HUNTER in a sleeper hold, but HUNTER fights back with a big calf kick knocking SPICE ONE out. HUNTER positions SPICE ONE up on the top rope and he looks to back suplex SPICE ONE. SPICE ONE counters this and hits a front face suplex instead! HUNTER rolls over in the cover... 1... 2...SPICE ONE kicks out! HUNTER can't believe it. The fans go nuts. HUNTER goes up to the top. SPICE ONE pushes the referee into the ropes. HUNTER jumps down into a roll-up, but SPICE ONE counters that into a choke hold! SPICE ONE keeps the choke hold applied with force. HUNTER has his arm reaching out for the bottom rope, he grabs it! SPICE ONE picks HUNTER up and tosses him towards the corner. HUNTER jumps to the top and leaps toward SPICE ONE looking for a seated senton and hits it. HUNTER then leaps up to the top rope and waits for SPICE to get back to his feet. You can see the emotion in HUNTER'S face as he focuses on the champ.....SPICE makes it to his feet and turns around only to be met by a HUNTER'S SPEAR! HUNTER nails it and goes for the cover..1........2.....THR...NO! HUNTER is shocked. SPICE ONE KICKED OUT!!! HUNTER gets back to his feet and begins to stalk SPICE ONE. SPICE ONE gets to his feet and it met with the WHIPLASH! SPICE ONE drops like a ton of bricks as HUNTER goes for the cover on the champ! 1.......2.......3!!! MY GOD! HUNTER RYAN HAS JUST PINNED THE WORLD CHAMPION! UNBELIEVABLE!
WINNER: HUNTER RYAN Backstage, we see VIOLET who is walking around looking very upset. She sees DAN FIERCE and stops him.
VIOLET: Have you seen FAMINE? DAN: I haven’t seen him all night. Is everything ok? VIOLET: Everything is fine. What about SCOTT? DAN: I saw him down the hall about two minutes ago. She storms off and walks in the direction DAN pointed her in. About 30 seconds pass and she catches up with SCOTT.
VIOLET: SCOTT! Wait a second. SCOTT: What do you want VIOLET? VIOLET: Look, I’m sorry about what FAMINE did to you last week. I tried to stop him but you saw what he did.
SCOTT: Don’t you worry yourself about that darling. Things are going to be just fine. VIOLET: Have you seen him? SCOTT: Yea. I saw him. He said something about taking a dirt nap. I didn’t understand what he meant by that. Listen I have to go. I need to get ready for my match.
SCOTT walks off leaving VIOLET standing there wondering what he meant. As he walks away we see him cleaning his fingernails. His boots look like he was working in a ditch or something.
The camera cuts to the back where it focuses on the door of JON BROWN’s office. The door opens, and out of the office walks RAGE, HUNTER and DAN FIERCE. The three men are all smiling and head towards the locker room area. What was that all about? Where’s STEVE SAYORS when you need him? But the camera doesn’t go anywhere as we now see that HARDCORE SMITTY is going into JONATHYN's office.
JONATHYN: Have you made your decision yet SMITTY? SMITTY: Yep! JONATHYN: GREAT! So Universal title match it is? SMITTY smirks. JONATHYN frowns. SMITTY: You see, JON. I can get a Universal Title shot pretty much whenever I want as I am on the contenders page, the number one contender. BUT.
JONATHYN: But what? SMITTY: This match with you is a once in a life time opportunity. So. JONATHYN... Get ready for a Hardcore unsanctioned Lumber Jack match this Sunday at Turning Point. If you impress me enough JONATHYN BOY! You will not only earn my vote... but my respect too. Get ready for the ass kicking of a life time. This one isn't about winning or losing! It's about respect!!
SMITTY smiles as JONATHYN can't believe it. SMITTY shakes JON's hand and leaves the room. JONATHYN gulps with nervousness.
MAIN EVENT "Chronic" CHRIS PAGE vs. RANMA SAOTOME vs. SCOTT CHARLOTTE - - OWN THE PPV Semi-Final Elimination Match - - As we’re set for our Triple Threat Main Event our attention is drawn to the crowd where security leads FUZZ to his ringside seat. The three competitors then make there way to the ring with RANMA coming out last. He stands in front of FUZZ running this thumb across his throat before entering the ring. The bell sounds as we’re getting underway. All three competitors walk out to the center of the ring. Each man stares the others down. Suddenly CHARLOTTE and RANMA begin to double team CCP. They back him to the ropes sending him across the ring with a double Irish Whip. CCP is sent into the ropes latching on to the top rope and sling shooting him self under the bottom rope to the arena floor. RANMA comes over to the ropes pointing down at CCP. CHARLOTTE comes up behind RANMA taking him over with a School Boy… ONE, TWO, TH… RANMA kicks out. On the floor CCP walks around the ring to the time keepers table throwing him from his chair before positioning it ringside before taking a seat. In the ring CHARLOTTE and RANMA begin to trade right hands. RANMA drives a knee to the midsection of CHARLOTTE before picking up CHARLOTTE and driving him across his knee with a back breaker.
RANMA covers. ONE, TWO, THR… CHARLOTTE rolls his shoulder. RANMA gets to his feet picking up CHARLOTTE and body slamming him to the mat. RANMA turns his attention to CCP who sits ringside not with a container of popcorn. CCP softly claps his hands as RANMA tells him to get in the ring. CHARLOTTE gets to his feet coming up behind RANMA spinning him around and landing several right hands before sending RANMA across the ring into the ropes, CHARLOTTE ducks his head sending RANMA up and over with a back body drop. RANMA is to his feet backing up against the ropes. CHARLOTTE rushes forward looking for a clothesline only it’s RANMA who ducks and sends CHARLOTTE over the top rope to the floor with a backdrop of his own. CCP slides in the ring behind RANMA. The crowd begins to get loud as RANMA turns to go eye to eye, nose to nose with CCP. Flashbulbs begin to fire off from all cylinders in anticipation of this showdown. The two begin to trash talk back and forth until finally they come to blows. They trade right hands back and forth with the crowd cheering when RANMA connects and boo as CCP connects. CCP blocks a right hand before planting a thumb to the eye of RANMA before taking him back into a neutral corner where he begins to unload with shoulder blocks. CCP shoots RANMA across the ring into the opposite turnbuckles. CCP charges in after him only to catch a boot to the face that staggers CCP back several feet. CHARLOTTE spring boards off the top rope with a dropkick that takes CCP through the middle rope and to the arena floor. RANMA rushes out from the corner into a powerslam from CHARLOTTE. He holds the cover. ONE, TWO, THR… SAOTOME shoots a shoulder off the mat. CHARLOTTE comes over to the ropes as CCP begins to get to his feet. CHARLOTTE slingshots himself over the top rope landing a cross body block on CCP! CHARLOTTE is back to his feet rolling inside the ring as RANMA begins to get to a vertical base. CHARLOTTE rushes towards RANMA but its RANMA who connects with a Tilt-a-Whirl Back Breaker driving CHARLOTTE’S back into his own knee. RANMA steps through the rope to the arena floor. He picks up CCP by the hair before sending him shoulder first into the ring steps. At ringside FUZZ stands to his feet as RANMA cuts his eyes towards him. RANMA picks up CCP rolling him back into the ring before following him. RANMA picks up CCP sending him into the ropes. RANMA ducks the head as CCP bounces off the ropes planting a boot to the chest of RANMA that stands him straight up, CCP takes a side waist lock before throwing RANMA back with a release German Suplex. CCP gets to his feet. The crowd roars with boos as he taunts the crowd, but he takes his eye of CHARLOTTE who gets back to his feet spinning CCP around before planting a boot to the midsection and plants him with the CHARLOTTE DRIVER!! The crowd comes alive as SCOTT rolls CCP over making a cover hooking the leg! ONE! TWO! THRE… CCP kicks out rolling back out to the arena floor. In the ring CHARLOTTE is to his feet. RANMA is to his feet walking into a right from CHARLOTTE who then looks to send RANMA into a set of buckles, RANMA reverses and sends SCOTT towards the buckles running in after him, SCOTT uses the top rope looking to spring over RANMA but its RANMA who catches SCOTT on his shoulder before walking out towards the center of the ring planting him on his skull with a TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER!! ONE! TWO! THRE…
CCP breaks the count by pulling the official out of the ring!! CCP slides in the ring as RANMA gets back to his feet. CCP rushes towards him taking him down with a double leg take down and starts to hammer down with right hands, RANMA doesn’t take this long as he rolls over now on top of CCP and hammers down with right hands. SAOTOME gets to his feet picking up CCP by the hair but it’s CCP who latches on to the head of RANMA dropping to his knees driving RANMA jaw into his head with a jaw breaker. CCP plants a boot to the midsection of RANMA before hooking him and taking him up in the air driving him into the mat with a Hangman’s DDT! CCP covers the Universal Champ. ONE, TWO, THRE… The crowd roars as RANMA shoots a shoulder off the mat. CCP stands to his feet. He picks up CHARLOTTE sending him into the ropes. CHARLOTTE bounces off the ropes ducking under a CCP clothesline hitting the far side and bouncing off with his CYCLONE KICK that staggers CCP back several feet. CHARLOTTE drills CCP with the GROUPIE KILLER!! The crowd roars in anticipation as CHARLOTTE makes a cover hooking the leg of CCP… ONE! TWO! THREE… NO! The crowd let’s out an OOOOO as RANMA is there to break the count moments before the fatal three count! CCP rolls back out under the ring to the arena floor. RANMA and CHARLOTTE get to there feet and again trade right hands. CCP crawls towards the seat that houses FUZZ. FUZZ stands up reaching in his pocket before handing CCP something. It’s not clear what it is but CCP places whatever it is in his tights. Our attention is taken back to the ring where RANMA looks to send CHARLOTTE into the ropes, CHARLOTTE reverses and its SAOTOME who bounces off into a dropkick from CHARLOTTE. CHARLOTTE is back to his feet with a quickness before heading to the top rope. CHARLOTTE measures RANMA before leaping off looking for a 450 Splash but RANMA has it scouted as he manages to get the knees up. RANMA is back to his feet. He picks up CHARLOTTE before driving him into the mat with a snap suplex. Now it’s RANMA who steps through the ropes and begins to climb to the top rope. As he reaches the top CCP comes up on the apron shaking the ropes causing RANMA to crotch himself on the top rope. CHARLOTTE is shown beginning to stir as CCP enters the ring and makes his way over to the turnbuckles. He steps up to the middle rope before snitching RANMA for a superplex. CHARLOTTE gets to his feet as CCP now steps up to the top rope. CHARLOTTE comes up behind CCP planting a forearm into his back before stepping up to the bottom turnbuckle and taking a Power Bomb position on CCP. As CCP comes off with the Super plex on RANMA it’s CHARLOTTE who drives him into the mat with a POWERBOMB completing the TOWER OF DOOM! The crowd roars as CHARLOTTE crawls over covering CCP! ONE! TWO! THRE… NO! CCP manages to raise a shoulder off the mat! CHARLOTTE now crawls over towards RANMA finally making a cover. ONE! TWO! THRE… AND RANMA kicks out. Nothing but a solid reception is heard from the crowd as SCOTT gets back to his feet. He looks towards CCP before stepping through the ropes and making his way back to the top rope. CHARLOTTE leaps off with the 450 Splash but this time he CONNECTS! ONE! TWO! THRE…CCP drapes his foot across the bottom rope! “THIS IS AWESOME” begins to echo throughout the venue as SCOTT stands to his feet. He begins to fire up the crowd as he’s SHOWING the world he can HANG with ANYONE! He looks across the ring as RANMA begins to get to his feet. He signals for another GROUPIE KILLER as he measures RANMA and as RANMA gets to his feet CHARLOTTE comes forward looking for the Superkick only to have RANMA duck and counter into a vicious ELECTRIC CHAIR! RANMA makes a cover. ONE! TWO! THRE… CCP dives on top of RANMA breaking up the count. CCP and RANMA get to there feet and it’s CCP who laces SAOTOME across the chest with a knife edge chop sending RANMA back into the ropes. CCP rushes forward looking to send RANMA to the floor but RANMA drops down pulling the top rope down sending CCP sailing to the arena floor! CCP is down as CHARLOTTE starts to get to his feet now. RANMA is now back on his feet and focuses on CCP who is also making his way back to his feet. CHARLOTTE comes from behind the Universal Champion and hits a hard elbow to the back of the head. RANMA turns around and SCOTT hits the GROUPIE KILLER!! Incredible, CHARLOTTE just clocked the champ right in the jaw with that super kick. He then goes for the cover.....1.......2......THRE.....NO! At the very last second, CCP comes in and makes the save. RANMA is still down, CCP now picks up CHARLOTTE and immediately hits the PAGE PLANT! SCOTT is out of it as CCP gets back to his feet and looks to finish off the champ. He picks up RANMA and signals for another PAGE PLANT but RANMA tosses PAGE to the ropes with a hard Irish whip. He comes back looking for a hard clothesline but CCP ducks and runs toward the ropes as well. They both come back, only to take each other out. Both men hit a clothesline and are down. Somehow, both men’s arms land on top of CHARLOTTE who is still out of it. The ref sees this and doesn't know what to do but counts the three since it is a pin fall.....1........2.......3!!! The match is over but who won? What the hell happened? One by One hits the PA system as JENSEN KENT makes his way out to the arena once again. The crowd as well as the ref doesn’t know what to make of this situation.
WINNER: ??? JENSEN: Well, it looks like we've got ourselves a little bit of a controversial situation here. Naturally, I would just say give PAGE the win since he is the better of the three men in there, but that wouldn't be fair would it. So the decision is that both men have won this match tonight and will both advance to the final match to see who will own the PPV. Now normally we would have to choose one winner but why would I want to do that? MASSACRE isn't about winners and losers....It's about putting one hell of a show on. And these men did that tonight. So gentlemen, enjoy your win. At Turning Point, we will see which one of you gentlemen can pull off the victory.
WINNERS: RANMA SAOTOME & CHONIC CHRIS PAGE Suddenly, Motorhead’s ‘We are the Road Crew’ blares over the PA as a massive bulldozer rolls down the entrance ramp!
The masked RAGE is driving as DAN FIERCE and HUNTER RYAN hang on the back. The Bulldozer pulls up to just before the ring and stops.
JENSEN: I’m not sure what you three idiots are doing, but didn’t I already fire your asses.?
RAGE: You did, but unfortunately for you, JON BROWN is starting to get with the program…you see, we no longer have Massacre contracts…so JON signed us on as Free Agents! We can appear on any XWF program we want! Including this one!
JENSEN: Oh, this keeps getting better and better! Fine, you guys can appear on any show you want! Don’t you realize that by staying on my show, I’m just going to make your lives a living hell?
RAGE: Well KENT. That depends…how much longer is it going to be your show? JENSEN: What the hell are you… KENT is suddenly interrupted as RAGE shifts gears and the bulldozer rumbles forward into the ring. RAGE is driving the bulldozer into the ring! The ring is collapsing! KENT and CCP have vanished amidst the destruction of the ring! RAGE is actually driving the bulldozer over the remains of the ring! Where’s JENSEN KENT? Where’s CCP?
Wait! CCP emerges from beneath part of the canvas dragging an unconscious JENSEN KENT with him. JENSEN has a cut on the forehead but is otherwise unharmed. RAGE stands up in the bulldozer and points to them both as Massacre goes off the air!
Fade to black...
