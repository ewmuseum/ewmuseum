I know that a great many of you commoners paid top dollar to be here tonight and you'll be rewarded as a result of such...this is no longer a gimmick match, there are no cages to contain this athletic contest, it'll be genuine. Get the referee in here and ring the bell...
RANMA holds the live mic in his hand as the referee makes his way amidst the mess of Heaven and Hell and into the ring. All eyes are set upon the entryway ramp, many are curious as to whom it might be. A BIG SHANK chant rings throughout the arena, but before any and all in attendance can react, RANMA drops to his back and places his right hand across his chest, covering his left shoulder.
RANMA: Count the fall. The referee is confused. RANMA: I told you to count the fall. The referee doesn't know what to make of the situation. All in attendance are confused. RANMA: I TOLD YOU TO COUNT THE FALL! The referee sinks to his knees and counts...1...2...3. RANMA: Now ring the bell. The referee signals the timekeeper, who complies with the request. RANMA comes to his feet and he motions for the Universal and Tag titles. He hands them to the referee.
RANMA: It looks as if he got the best of me...I don't know what to say. Perhaps I should spill a heartfelt goodbye upon you like a great many of those who disappear into the moonlight, only to return weeks later. That's not me, that's not what I do. What I do is win, what I do is stay consistent, I've kept my mouth shut as it relates to the political ordeals that have occurred behind the scenes. It never mattered who was in charge, they knew they were going to make money...now the ball is in their court because their brightest star has yanked himself out of the sky. There are many of you who have defeated yourselves before you've ever set foot in the ring. Some of you can barely show up for work on time, some of you think you are bigger then you are, and one or two of you actually do what you say you're going to do and you never get the credit you deserve, one of those can take this half of the tag titles and formulate his own legacy...all he has to do is reach out and take it. Don't be controlled by what others thought, if I cared what people thought about me, I'd spend hours in the corner of my room. There are those who will hate you simply for being on top, they will loathe you for winning. Some will embrace you. The way to succeed is to be indifferent to it all. My indifference isn't by choice. I really don't care. Perhaps I'll be challenged once upon a blue moon...but I've been bested here tonight by one that I've never been able to defeat and that man stares at me in the mirror each and every day...all I can say is that...
The sound rings throughout the arena. It shatters ears. Cries, screams. RANMA drops to his knees, he looks to the entryway ramp and sees her.
A vision in black. Gun in hand. Shot missed. His eyes go wide as he takes her in. RANMA: It's you... He throws the mic down. He begins to give chase. He literally leaps over the top ring, barely pushing off. He heads up the entryway ramp, but she disappears from view. He surges into the hallway, looking left, looking right. He sees the exit doors, one of them closes and he rushes towards it. He surges out into the night, looking down the stairs leading up to the arena as a Red Diablo peels out. He begins to run after it, but a black stretch limo pulls up beside the arena stairs. RANMA stops just shy of the limo as the camera zooms in. RANMA's associates, TAKI and RAVE, exit the limo and grab the now former Universal Champion in their arms.
RANMA: No! We've got to... TAKI: We will. We will. You see...you can give as much chase as you'd like...after all... TAKI hands RANMA a picture of the vision in black. TAKI: She's first on the list... RANMA's expression of pure, unadulterated shock fades as the sentence hits his ears. He snatches the picture away from TAKI and he turns to RAVE who can't even look him in the eye. RANMA waits and RAVE, he catches a glance from RAVE, who nods his head. RANMA had put the young man through so much and his place is a direct result of something RANMA set in motion so very long ago.
RANMA: Then let's go. TAKI: Don't you want to grab your... RANMA cuts TAKI short. He looks back at the arena one last time. RANMA: There's nothing I need there anymore...but we do have one stop to make before we call it a day.
TAKI: Where's that? RANMA: The PWE arena...I've got to see about a woman... He stares down at the picture. RANMA: Before I see to "her" sister... He climbs into the limo along with TAKI and RAVE. They drive off into the night and so the competitive world is extended no courtesy, no final farewell, no parting glance from one of its brightest stars that removed itself from the sky. RANMA SAOTOME, the time devourer, has folded in upon himself...for who would know that his memory of her had an expiration date, and it was ten thousand years from now. To give chase is to give himself an identity.
After the scene ends, we go backstage to see JENSEN KENT sitting in his office wondering what the hell just happened. The show ends as he has a pondering look on his face. Just before the show goes off completely, he smiles.
