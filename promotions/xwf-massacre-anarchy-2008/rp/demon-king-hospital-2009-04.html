The Demon King: a hospital lunch (roleplay)

c. 18 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Demon King Pain. Something I have learned to endure and overcome over time. But no matter how I look at it. I am still human. Regardless of whether or not I perceive myself to be something more than human. That is all I am. But I am still a monster. Deep within me this will never change. I am a dark, insidious and sadistic. These are all traits that will remain in me probably until the day I die. But no matter. I will go on, I will fight another day. But the thought and memory of what happened yesterday will not be forgotten soon enough. So what do I do now that I didn't win the title? Retire and just say fuck it? Or do I continue on until I have nothing left. Last night I gave it everything I had. I proved to Chris Page that things would be different this time around and I did. Not only did he not win, but we took each other to new heights never before seen by either one of us. I am proud of the fact that I have now decided to change my ways one more time. And this might be the last time I change. Not the way I am and not who I am but the way I see things and live my life. I looked over at my lady love Violet as she rode in the ambulance with me to the hospital. I held her hand and saw from the eye that I could open that she truly did worry about me. She really did care. Not because I thought she did but because I saw the tears coming down from her eyes and knew that this was it. I truly do have another chance at a family. Something I have not had in a very long time. My life is now complete and the only thing I have left to do is walk into Purple Haze and claim a victory over Chris Page AND Sam Hain. Can I do it? That remains to be seen. Right now, in the state that I am in, I'm surprised that I'm not on pain killers or heavy drugs to help me deal with this pain. But that's how i've always been. I smoked my weed, I drank my booze. I lived that life and to me it was pointless. My drug, my vice if you will, was to hurt people. To go out to the ring and show the world that I was more than just a freak. I wanted to show the world that Hadrian Burke wasn't just some skinny awkward kid who grew up with a father that didn't really love him and a mother who worked hard to make sure me and my sister were ok. Sometimes I look back at my life and wonder if things would have been different had I not chosen this road. Would I have become Famine of the Vile? Would I have just settled down and worked with my father and help him manage his career? Or would I have ended up where my sister now works? Some pencil pushing fuck who doesn't bother to stand up for himself when a piece of shit boss gets on your case about not filing something correctly? No matter how much I think about it, I think that this was meant to be. This is who I was meant to become. And now my life has truly begun. But will it end before it truly begins? Will I have a chance at claiming the gold or will I fade away into nothing?
12:30pm. Famine of the Vile is sitting in the hospital bed as Violet walks in with lunch. Violet: Here baby. I got your your favorite sub. Famine: Thank you baby. Have you seen the doctor? Violet: No, they told me he should be here soon though. Famine: Good. I wanna get the fuck out of here already. I need to get ready for Thursday. Violet: Thursday? What are you doing Thursday? Famine: I have a match against Trent. Violet: Last night you damn near killed yourself fighting with Page and you're going to go back at it just a few days later? You don't know the damage that match cost you.
Famine: Hon, listen, i'm fine. I've been through this many, many times. If you had any idea how many times this has happened you would be surprised. I'm sure that the doc will say I can go home. Nothing to worry about.
Suddenly, Famine's cell phone rings. He looks at the caller ID and doesn't recogize the number.
Famine: Hello? Kim: Hey cousin... Famine: Kim? Is that you? Kim: Yeah it is. How are you doing after that hellacious match.. Famine: I'm alive. That's all that matters. How's everything with you? Kim: Busy keeping everyone in check on this end. Famine: If anyone is good at doing that it's you. How are my boys? Kim: Raising hell and kicking ass.. Nothing new there Famine: Glad to see things are still the same. So what can I do for you while I wait for the idiot doctor to show up?
Kim: What?!? I can't call you in concern over your health ? Famine: Of course you can. I just didn't know you had my number. Kim: You're family H. I gotta check up on the legendary member that used to raise hell with my brothers. They still tell stories about your days with them
Famine: It's good to know I'm remembered. Kim: By the way, Mase asked when you're coming down here again. Famine: As soon as I retire. Just need to wait and see what happens at the pay per view Kim: Retire? You sound just like Drew, he always says it.. And he is still at it Famine: but this time things are different. I have Violet now, and with us trying to have a baby I really do think it's time to step down for good
Kim: Yeah I can understand that, how is Violet doing with all of this.. Famine: She took it hard last night. I saw the fear and tears in her eyes and realized that I can't keep doing this no matter how much I love the business. I think Purple Haze might be my last show. Win or lose.
Kim: I bet it was hard on her having to watch that, if she wants to get away from it for a bit she is always welcome at my place here in Florida.. Do tell her that.. You need to find something that pleases both of you H.. You love the business and her.. Don't do something you might regret later...
Famine: You're right. I just need to do this one last match. I don't want to walk away with any regrets. I need to know that I can give it every ounce of energy I have left before I can hang them up for good
Kim: Don't hang them up just yet.. Set them in the closet for if and when the time comes to put them on.. Even if it is for 1 night only that you can just grab them and go...
Famine: I don't know Skittles. I really think that with everything going on right now I may really just call it quits forever
Kim: I still say you should save them just in case. But I respect that. Suddenly, Famine's doctor walks in. Famine: Hey, the doctor just walked in so I will talk to you again soon ok. And i'll keep in mind that offer for Violet.
Kim: Ok. Just give me a call when things are cool. Love you cuz. Famine: Love you too Skittles. Talk to you soon. So....what's the word doc? Doctor: You suffered a serious concussion and you have miltiple internal bruises. I'm afraid I can't allow you to wrestle for at least two weeks.
Famine: Two weeks? Fuck that! I have a match on Thursday. Doctor: Well, the problem is Mr. Burke, that if you suffer any more injuries to the head, you could suffer serious brain damage.
Violet: Hon, maybe you should think about this. I mean you can't risk anything with everything we're planning.
Famine: Look. I understand you're worried about me baby. But i've been through this a hundred times. Some doctor tells me the same crap and yet here I am.
Doctor: This might be, but over the years, the injuries suffered to the head or brain become more and more permanent. I can't stop you from leaving or competing because I am not the licensed doctor for your company but if you want to see the rest of your life I suggest you think about it.
Violet: Thank you doctor. As the doctor walks away, Famine and Violet stare into each others eyes. The silence is a bit uncomfortable as the scene fades to black....
THE VILE MANIFESTO After what happened last night, I'm shocked I can still stand. Page really showed me once again why he is who he is. A very gifted athlete. Nonetheless, I returned the favor and gave him an ass kicking like he's never seen. So it looks like Famine of the Vile has one more chance to capture gold. But not one on one. No, no. This time it will be a triple threat. Am I ready? Of course I am. Despite what the goddamn doctor says, I am going out there and I am going to capture the reigns of the carriage that is the XWF and ride until it's over. But for now, I have to recover and get ready for my match Thursday night. They have me booked against a man whom I am all too familiar with. Trent Gein. The butcher himself and the Demon King once again go toe to toe in the middle of the ring. Trent is a different beast. He and I go way back as well. But this is different. Because we are both monsters. We know what its like to hand out pain and misery. We also know what it's like when there is a man in front of you who will not stop. Who will not quit until blood is spilt or someone is dead. But over time, things have changed for both of us. The only difference is that I am still the same sick bastard that wrapped a melted chair over his head and stabbed his partner. But things change. You stop stabbing people. You stop torturing them. You evolve into something that you never predicted you would become. Trent knows this as well. And he knows that we are both willing to do whatever it takes to win. But no matter the injuries. No matter the broken bones or cuts that have yet to heal, I will come to the ring this Thursday and destroy him before I head to Purple Haze to destroy two more men. And if I should end up getting destroyed myself. I will not go without taking one of those fuckers with me!
