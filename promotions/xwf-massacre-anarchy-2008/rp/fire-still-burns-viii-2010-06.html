XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Roleplay

The Fire STILL Burns!, part VIII (roleplay)

c. 30 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Fire STILL Burns! VIII SLAP! That’s the only sound that can be heard in the room as Marcos slaps Viktor hard. “I’m not going to ask you again. Where the fuck is Melanie!” said Marcos angrily. “I told you, I don’t know where she is. After everything I’ve done for you Marcos, why would you betray me like this?” Said Viktor as he took a breath.
Marcos got closer to Viktor’s face with a serious look on his face. “Why? Do you really want to know why Viktor? Because you’re weak! When I first joined you, you were a ruthless bastard who didn’t give a fuck about anything or anyone. You wanted to control everything. And then somewhere along the way you changed. You became a poor pathetic old man who only wanted to protect these pathetic people instead of making them beg for mercy!”
“Marcos, that is not the way to go about things. These people need our protection. It’s the outsiders we need to protect them from.” Said Viktor as he looked dead into Marcos’ eyes.
“THEN WHY DID YOU LET THAT OUTSIDER IN AND OFFER HIM MY POSITION?!” screamed Marcos. “My reasons for doing so are many. None of which you will ever know. Now I demand you release me. I am your commander.” Viktor said starting to sound upset
“Correction Viktor. You WERE our commander. Now you’re nothing more than a washed up old man. I am the new leader. And as soon as I find your whore of a daughter, I will kill her and you. Donovan will live only because he’s too stupid to ever try to defy me. He will serve well as my lackey” Said Marcos as he smiled.
BANG……BANG……. BANG Gunshots could be heard out in the courtyard as Marcos ran out to see what was going on. When he looked through the hole in the door of the headquarters he could see two of the guards dead on the floor. Both shot in the head.
“GODDAMN IT! Felicio, Antonio! Find those two and kill them. I don’t care what it takes I WANT THEM DEAD!” screamed Marcos.
The two guards ran off with guns in hand following orders. Marcos returned to Viktor angrier than ever.
“I changed my mind. Not only am I going to kill you. But when I find your daughter, IF she’s still alive after my men kill her, I am going to make you watch me chop her into little pieces and feed her to my dogs!”
Marcos walks out of the room leaving Viktor there alone. Outside, Hadrian and Melanie hide on the roof of the hotel across the street looking at the guards scuttle around trying to find them.
“So what’s the plan now? We can’t stay up here forever. Eventually they’re going to find us.” Said Hadrian
“Don’t worry Mr. Burke, everything will be fine. I am just waiting for a sign.” Melanie said.
“A sign? Like from god or something? A café sign that says Now Open? I don’t get it.” Hadrian explained.
As soon as he said that, he looked down at the spot Melanie was looking and saw Marcos. Then he got it. She picked up the rifle and aimed it carefully. Through the scope you could see the head of Marcos carefully placed in between the crosshairs. As she got ready to fire, the sunlight hit the edge of the scope. Marcos looked up and saw it.
“Goddamn it! He saw us!” Marcos yelled at his men as Melanie took the shot anyway. Marcos dodged just in time but the bullet scraped his cheek as he got out of the way mere seconds before getting hit right between the eyes.
“Let’s go!” Melanie said as she dropped the rifle. “How the hell are we going to get out of here?” said Hadrian as he ran behind her “There is a secret room my brother and I used to play in when we were kids. No one knows about it. We can hide there until they’re gone.”
As they ran down the stairs they could hear the guards already inside running up the stairs looking in every floor for them.
“Come on” Melanie said to Hadrian making sure he was close and following. They went in through a secret panel and Hadrian closed it as soon as they were inside. They could hear the guards running around like roaches inside the wall.
“We’ll be safe in here.” Melanie said. “Oh good. For some crazy reason I thought we were safer earlier. But this, this is nice.” Hadrian said sarcastically.
“I apologize. I had to take the shot. That was my one chance at ending it and I failed. Now my father might be dead because of it.” She said as she sat down
“I’m sure he’s fine. Marcos won’t kill him.” Hadrian said comfortingly “How can you be so sure?” she said. “I know how these scenarios go. Don’t you ever watch television?” Hadrian asked “No. We don’t have time to watch television.” Said Melanie “Oh. Ok, well basically what he wants it to capture you and kill me. Then he’s probably going to torture you in front of your father as he watches in horror.” He said calmly
“How could you possibly know that this is what would happen?” she asked “Because it’s what I would do in his position.” He said “Right now, we need to figure out a solid plan that is going to save your father. We need a way to get to Marcos and get to him alone.”
“Donovan” she said “Donovan will help us.” “How do we get to him?” Hadrian asked “This room has a secret hatch to another room. If I know him he’ll be there right now hiding out. Hopefully Marcos hasn’t found out where he is.” She said
“Good, let’s go then.” Hadrian said “No, you stay here. If anything happens to me I don’t want you to get caught.” She said “What? Fuck that! Listen, we had an amazing night together. And while we may not know each other that well I still want to help. Whether you like it or not I’m involved in this too and I’m not about to let you go off and get killed.” Hadrian told her seriously.
She looked at him in his eyes and could see that he was serious. “Okay. Just follow me.” She said as she opened the hatch and they both went down. They climbed down the ladder and made it to another small room where there was a walkie talkie and a small tv along with a bunch of old toys that were dusty and just thrown everywhere.
“Damn it he’s not here. I need to go outside.” She said worried. “Wait, woah. Go outside? You realize that outside is where we DON’T wanna be right?” Hadrian said
“Don’t you think I know that? But what am I going to do? My brother is not here and my father could be dead. For all I know they are both dead and Marcos is just waiting for me to end all of this. I have to go.” She said
“Ok look. Let’s just calm down for a minute and think. Is there another way into the headquarters from here?” he asked.
“The only way to get out of here is through this small door. I can fit through it but you’ll need to go back up to the room we were in first. I’ll turn myself in. Once they catch me you can follow us inside. Marcos won’t be waiting for us outside. He’s not stupid. If you can follow us in, we can take their guns and finish this thing once and for all.” She explained.
“Ok, I guess I’ll deal with the guards inside the hotel” Hadrian asked. “How good are you at fighting?” she asked as Hadrian smiled at the question soon after. “I’m a professional wrestler. I’ve also trained in five different fighting styles.” He said
“Wrestling won’t be any good to you with them. These guys are trained mercenaries. Trained by Marcos himself.” She told him
“Don’t worry. You haven’t seen the last of me just yet. Now go. We’ve got work to do and people to save.” He told her as he pulled her close. “Whatever you do, don’t try to be brave. I will be there to help. I promise” he leaned in and gave her a kiss as she left through the small door and he headed back upstairs.
If there was ever a time that Hadrian Burke needed to step into the role of his alter ego Famine of the Vile it was now. As Famine, he would come out to the wrestling ring and put on a show like never before. His power, speed and agility were a force to be reckoned with. Outside the ring he had his share of battles that were all too realistic. As a young man, he killed a wrestler’s son. He had also sacrificed a woman for the purposes of joining a cult. All things that were left behind never to be remembered again. To him, these memories were as fresh as the day it happened. But he was the only one that knew. This information is only relevant because if he is to help Melanie, her father and Donovan, he needs to tap into that darkness once again and forget everything else. The final battle is about to take place. All that is left to be seen is who wins in the end…….
THE IRREFUTABLE TRUTH So here we are. The semi finals of the tournament and look at who’s the favorite to win. Admit it! You all know I’m in the lead right now and you all know that when it comes time to face off against whoever wins between K-Money and Kieran King that I am the man to beat. For a while now I’ve worked my ass off to get to this position in my career as well as in the XWF. I’ve been in that ring with the best there is and have been victorious on more than one occasion. I have triumphed and I have failed. It took me two years to win my very first XWF World title and now two years later it happens again. It’s as if destiny is calling to me and telling me that my time has once again arrived. Sure, I’ve been given the opportunity to go for the Universal title on more than one occasion and lost. But that is just something I’ve had to deal with and have moved on from. My chance will come again someday. For now, I look forward to a second reign as one of the best XWF World champions that ever lived.
My name has already been placed in the hall of legends once before and it will happen once again. The XWF hall of legends is not an easy place to get into. You have to work hard and demonstrate that you have the will, the passion and the love for this business that no one else can match. You have to go out there each and every single night fighting until you can’t fight anymore. You don’t pick up your ball and go home like a little bitch just because you lost a couple of matches here and there. You win and you lose. It’s all part of this game we have all chosen to be a part of. I admit, I’ve had my share of issues but I’ve never walked away and took my ball. The times I have left were for personal reasons. But somehow, I’ve managed to come back time and time again stronger and better than ever.
This would be one of those moments. I have come back to prove that no matter how old you are, if you have the heart, the will and the passion, that you can do anything you want. And that’s exactly what I am doing. I have fought hard to get to where I am and have proved that I am still a force to be reckoned with. As I stand here before you, I look into the future and I still see so many possibilities. And it starts with winning that World title for a second time. Let this serve as a warning to my opponent tomorrow night and to my opponent at X-Mas Xtreme. Famine of the Vile is coming for blood and he is coming for his title. Sunday night, Famine of the Vile will once again stand as the pinnacle of success. I will rise to the top just as I did once before. And no matter what it takes I am going to prove that not only do I deserve this title. But that I deserve to be where I am right now. At the top of this business as one of the best.
David Dunham. Tomorrow night you step into the ring with one of the greatest superstars and legends in the business. Don’t think for one second that I am going to underestimate you or make any mistakes. I am focused and ready for anything that you have to bring. I’m sure you’ve sat back and studied everything you possibly could about me. Maybe you even got some pointers from a friend or two, telling you that you need to do this or you should do that. Forget everything they’ve told you. Because it doesn’t matter what they’ve said or advised you to do. I always bring something new to the table in every situation. I am as unpredictable as they come. And just when you think you’ve got me figured out, I do a 180 and you never saw it coming. Ever since I entered this tournament it’s been pretty easy. I don’t anticipate that this will happen tomorrow or at the pay per view which is why I am always one step ahead. Are you?
Dave, tomorrow we face off for the first time and probably the last time. But when it’s all said and done, you will do the one thing everyone before you had done. Give me the respect I deserve. I’m not saying that you don’t respect me now, but once you and I go toe to toe, you will see why the name Famine of the Vile is one that you will remember for the rest of your life. And that my friend is the irrefutable truth!
