Famine of the Vile, chapter 17: A New Chapter, the Final Chapter (roleplay)

CHAPTER 17: A NEW CHAPTER THE FINAL CHAPTER My name is Hadrian Burke. I was born on February 11th in 1969. Son of Swedish parents. One of whom was a half human, half vampire named Nikolaos. He didn’t teach me about life. Hell, he was never there as I grew up feeling alone and confused. My mother returned to Sweden after I was born and married a man named Alexander Stone. And while he was a good man, he too neglected to be that father figure I always wanted. After his company went bankrupt, he came home one night, killed my mother and then himself, leaving me and my sister alone to be shuffled around from foster home to foster home. I decided to leave Sweden when I was 16 and started my own life in California. I grew up there and spent most of my days trying to figure out my life and being part of a satanic cult. And while it was fun to be a part of them, I felt I was meant for something more. Something better than just believing in someone who wasn’t real. I still had no idea what I was or what I should do with my life. I wandered the city until I landed a job as an underground fighter. I loved it! I could damn near beat anyone they put in my path. And at 17, it was quite amazing. Guys who were six two and weighed damn near 250 lbs were no match for me. People thought I was nothing more than a strung out drug addict who was hopped up on pain killers and cocaine. If they only knew! So this was my life for at least a year. And then I met her. She was beautiful. Probably the most beautiful woman I had ever seen. Her name was Christina. She was a waitress and the underground club. She served drinks and had to put up with assholes on a nightly basis. Constantly grabbing and groping her. I wanted to help and say something but I never did. I had never talked to a girl and had no clue what to say. So that’s how it went until one night, the club closed its doors and everyone went home. As I was leaving, I heard someone scream. I looked in the alley behind the club and there she was. Two men were trying to rape her and take her purse. I felt the urge to do something. I couldn’t just leave and let her get hurt or worse….get killed. So I walked over, tossed one guy about six feet back. The other guy I just broke his nose and knocked him out cold. And that’s where it all began. After saving her life, we began to talk and got to know each other. I told her about myself but not everything. I don’t know why I never told her but I didn’t know how she would take it. I mean in a world where vampires only exist in movies and novels, how would she even begin to understand that we were real? So I left that part out and never mentioned it. After a few months, we were officially a couple. It was at that time that I decided she deserved better than the life we lead. I thought it was time for a fresh start.
But before that fresh start was to happen, I had to deal with the one man I never knew. My father! One night, after I left Christina at her home, I walk down the street and see some guy following me. So, being the cocky teenager that I was, I yelled something at him. In the blink of an eye, he went from standing a block away from me to being about two feet away. Normally that wouldn’t shock me. But that was something I had never seen before. And wouldn’t you know it? The man was my father. He and I started talking and he revealed that he wanted to watch me grow up but my mother wouldn’t let him because of his violent past. So she took me back to Sweden and he never heard from her again. He then told me he was always watching me. Protecting me. I asked him why he never came to me and told me but all he could say was that he didn’t want to confuse me. My mother had married and had a daughter with that man. He was my stepfather from the moment I could say his name. So he was all I knew. And I guess I can understand. But for a boy who struggled with himself, some kind of clarification would have been nice. Why am I like this daddy? Who am I daddy? Questions that a man should be there to answer. But nevertheless, he and I talked and he finally revealed the truth to me. He taught me what I was and told me that I was meant for greatness. So I spent a couple of weeks with him and he trained me to be a better fighter. He taught me how to control and harness my anger and power. Soon after, I returned to the club where the owner was glad to see his number one fighter return. But the return was going to be short lived. I went back and placed a bet on myself. I bet all the money I had won in the previous months and when it was over, I walked away with everyone’s money. But not before taking out the owner and a few other people who had screwed me and Christina over several times. I took the money and asked Christina to leave with me. I didn’t think she would say yes but she did. And so, we made our way to Japan.
Normally, people don’t just fly off to Japan to start a new life, but we did. People usually just go to another state. But not me. I wanted to get away from it all. Start over in a place where no one knew me and I didn’t know them. I wanted a different life. And boy was this different. On the plane, I met Yoshiro Matsuda. He frequented the club I fought at. Placed large bets and was always a big winner. He offered me a job as a wrestler which to me was a ridiculous notion given the fact that I knew wrestling was fake. Or so I thought. I gave the offer some thought and finally went to see Yoshiro. He talked to me and opened my eyes to a world I never thought I would come to grow and love. It was almost like the underground club except that there was more money to be made. Legally….And you could become a household name if you made it to the big time. I struggled at first, but eventually I got the hang of it. Now I have to be honest. It’s not easy being half vampire and not being able to use your strength. I had to go out there each and every night and pretend to get hurt or pretend I was unconscious. It was also pretty tough to bleed whenever I got hit with a chair or some kind of object. I usually had to cut myself in order to make it more realistic. So I continued on until Yoshiro gave me a huge opportunity to prove myself. He offered me the Dragon Title. A belt that had only been held by Japanese wrestlers since its introduction. Yoshiro knew this would cause problems amongst the wrestlers and the fans, but he trusted his instincts and knew that I would not disappoint. I don’t know how he knew it, but I couldn’t let him down. He had already given me so much that I had to honor that by being the best. And so I went out there and I won. Of course, it was controversial and the fans hated me, but Yoshiro was proud. I admit, I cheated a little. I used some of my strength to win the match but no one knew. I just couldn’t let Yoshiro down after all he did for me. And so I became the first ever American to win the title. An honor that would remain forever in the eyes of the fans and those in the business.
I went from underground club fighter to one of the top wrestlers in Japan in less than a year. I even changed my image and appearance. A lot of people often asked me here in the states why I wear the face paint. Well, I was very influenced by the Japanese culture. I lived there for three years and it’s a part of me. Always will be. I took the kabuki style of face painting and gave it a vampire-ish edge. I then came up with the name Famine of the Vile after Yoshiro mentioned that I was Vile whenever I fought at the club. I then added the Famine part because I always left them hungry for more. Cheesy and stupid I know. But it’s a name no one has forgotten since. But back to my story. Yoshiro was like a father to me. He gave me and Christina a home, and when she and I decided to get married, he paid for everything. Something I will never forget. And to top it off, he even paid for her medical expenses when she was pregnant. That would be the last thing he would ever do for us. Soon after, he went to the hospital because of a heart attack he suffered on his was to get some test results. I immediately ran to see if he was ok. Two things happened when I went to see him. He confessed that he knew my father and was watching me for him, and I met his daughter whom I would later have an affair with. We’ll get to that soon. As I sat there in the hospital room. He told me how my father was once like me and fought in the same underground club. He met him there and they struck a conversation, eventually becoming pretty good friends. Some of the things I thought about my father I took back. But the rest remained. I still hated him for not being there, but I thanked him for putting Yoshiro in my life. If it wasn’t for him, I would not have the honor and respect that I still within me till this very day. It may not seem like I have any given that I like to tear my opponents to pieces but I do.
Yoshiro later died and it was tough for everyone. Especially me. He treated me like his son and that is something I would never forget. After he passed, I fulfilled his final wish; to take on the World Champion Dr. X and win the title. A feat no American had ever done. But I did it. Of course, this was the beginning of the end. I won the title, but I spent less time at home because I was not working with new guys to show them the ropes and what to do inside the ring. At the same time, I was sleeping with Yoshiro’s daughter. She was still grieving the loss of her father, and I was there for her. It was not something I had planned on but it happened. The nights we spent together were incredible. But at home, were my wife and unborn child. And I couldn’t continue to do this to her. One night, I come home and decide we should go out for dinner. That was a bad idea. At the restaurant, Kasumi shows up and all hell breaks loose. Christina finds out about us and causes a scene. Not that I blame her but this is not exactly how I wanted things to go down. And so, I spent the night in my car drunk off so much sake that I was sweating it, and Christina packed her things and left the next morning to New York. So now, my mentor was dead, my wife left me and Kasumi and I called it quits after her mother found out. I had nothing and I had no one. But that didn’t stop me from trying to get my wife back. I mean I wasn’t about to be a bastard like my father and not be there for my son. So I flew to New York to find her and try to make things right. I think that is the point in my life where everything changed forever. I got to New York only to see my wife in a hospital bed in a coma. During the delivery, there were complications which no one bothered to explain to me. The baby was fine but it was Christina who suffered. All the doctors could say was that it might have been due to the fact that she flew half way around the world at 7 months. Something no pregnant woman is ever allowed to do. I sat there, held her hand and looked at her beautiful face. Before she died, all I could say was “I hope you can hear me from your cloud….I love you.” And then I just jumped out the window.
Two floors up and just jumped out like it was a hop over a park bench or something. I disappeared and went into hiding for two years. No one knew where I was and even I didn’t know what I was going to do next. I suddenly began feeling this hunger. This undying hunger that wouldn’t leave me alone. Was it the vampire in me craving for blood? Or was it something else. At this point I didn’t care. I had nothing to live for and my only son had been given to someone else to be taken care of. I realized then that I WAS like my father. He left me to be taken care of by other people and I was now doing the same thing. I told myself I would never do that and here I was actually doing it. Hypocrite! But soon after, I never gave it much thought. I just dealt with the hunger inside me and tried to figure out what it was. Then one night, somewhere in the woods, I spot a deer. I see it slowly approaching me. This to me was strange because normally deer won’t come near you if they sense danger. But this one did. As he got closer, my hunger grew bigger. My blood began to boil and when it got close enough for me to grab it I snapped its neck! I bit down on it and drank its blood. It was disgusting at first, but after a few seconds I grew to like it more and more. My hunger for the time being was satisfied. And then I realized that I had only one choice. Animal blood! This would be the only way to keep my hunger at bay. This would go on for 10 years. When I finally decided to rejoin the world, I decided to return to California. There I would join a small wrestling company called SAW (Supreme Action Wrestling) I thought it was time to try to get back some kind of normalcy in my life. But my feelings and the past wouldn’t let me be normal. I still felt the anger and rage of losing both my mentor and wife. Knowing my son out there and not knowing who his real father was. I could only imagine what he must be going through. I’m sure he felt just like I did. Confused and wondering why he felt strange urges. Eventually my antics would lead me to be known as one of the most sadistic men in the business. Not many people were willing to work with me because I was not playing the game the way it was supposed to be played. When I was supposed to lose a match, I’d end up winning it by just saying fuck it and going with my own rules. I ended up hurting a few of the guys and taking them out for months at a time.
I left SAW and moved on to other companies. Eventually they all got to be the same. Not enough challenges to keep my appetite going. In the end, I landed a job in the XWF. Things were really different this time around. The guys here didn’t mess around when it came to competing. It was like being in Japan all over again. No bullshit, no predetermined outcomes, just pure wrestling and fighting at its best. And so, the story continues. The XWF became my home and would lead me on a journey I never thought possible. Not only that, but it reunited me with my son. I never thought I would see the day where my son would find me but he did. How? Around his 15th birthday, he talked to his “mom” who was actually his aunt Marianne. Who confessed to him that she wasn’t his mother. She told him all about his real mom and eventually me. He decided to look for me and find out why I was never there for him. So he did the only thing he could do. He joined the XWF under the name Soul Bearer. Now, having never seen him since he was a baby, I had no clue that he was my son. A good friend of mine tracked him down and we met face to face in a diner. When I stood up to meet my son, the look on my face was one of shock and awe. Soul Bearer. The same young man that was my stable mate and the same man I taught the business to was none other than my flesh and blood. We kept it a secret for a while and just went out about our lives and worked like we were nothing more than business acquaintances. But soon it was leaked to the people in management. Once that happened, we decided to run the story that he was my son. We revealed everything as part of the story to keep people coming back week after week. And we did a good job at it. But behind closed doors, it was a bit different. He was still angry at me for never being there. And I couldn’t blame him. I was angry at my father too. In some ways, we bonded and spent a lot of time catching up. But in others, there was still that sense of anger which I could never heal. Months after, he left and we haven’t spoken since. I don’t know where he is but I hope someday he comes back so that we can catch up. Like me, he’s his own man and I will never regret the time we spent together in and out of the business. I know he is strong just like his old man and he will always know I am here for him. For now, I will continue on with my life until that day comes. Until then, there is something else I need to deal with. Finding my daughter! Yep, Hadrian Burke also has a daughter. How do I know this? Because my good friend “the watcher” told me. He has known me for many years and has always helped me whenever I needed him. I didn’t ask him to look for her. Hell I didn’t even know I had a daughter at all. But he got some information and looked into it. I wonder what she looks like. I wonder if she even knows that I’m alive. Hell, I wonder if she knows I’m her father. I can’t believe Kasumi was pregnant. I guess that’s what happens when you disappear for so long. Now, my quest is to return to Japan and find her. I need to take care of this and start fresh. Bury the past and move on. I can’t live with old skeletons still roaming around. This will be the final chapter in my life as I begin a new one. The book of Hadrian Burke is now done. And I can now move on and start over as Famine of the Vile. Once my old demons are let out, Hadrian Burke will be dead. And everything about him will cease to exist. The man I once was is gone. There is nothing on this earth left for him to do. I hope I can get my daughter to understand. I hope she doesn’t hate me. I hope…….
THE VILE MANIFESTO Ahhhhhh, revenge. One of my most favorite words. It’s even sweeter when you actually get your revenge. For me, it was five years in the making. I still remember clear as day when that sorry son of a bitch Johnny Mace asked his good friend Boondock Saint to embarrass me in front of the world by performing an “exorcism”. I later got my revenge on Johnny Mace and got rid of his ass forever. He never came back to the XWF. But Dan Malcolm? He was the one man who I never got a chance to get my hands on. For five years we worked for the same company and not once did our paths cross. Until last week. When after all this time, I would finally get him one on one. And what happened? Nothing! I didn’t see Dan Malcolm there. Hell, I didn’t even see Boondock Saint there. All I saw was a man who was a shell of the man he used to be. Dan once said the same about me. That I was a shadow of the man I once was. That I was not the same man who came to the XWF five years ago. But now the tables have turned. And I got my revenge by destroying the legend known as Boondock Saint. And although it was a sweet victory, it doesn’t feel like it. Because he was no challenge. He was not the man I was looking forward to facing. But that’s in the past now. I got my revenge and can now close the door on that part of my career. But now, I get to move on and face Roxy Nova and David Williams in a triple threat. Why? Who the fuck knows? All I know is that once again I have to set foot in the ring against a woman who actually has some balls. Last time we faced off, I was trying my best not to hurt her. But she kept coming at me like she was a man. So I took it to her like a man. In the end neither of us won because some nobody who’s calling himself “the fallen” decided to get involved in the match.
And that’s ok. Because eventually I am going to get his ass too. I’ll make sure he pays for getting in my business. Until then, I have a lot to look forward to. Shank has offered me one of the sweetest deals I’ve gotten in a long time. He offers me Chris Page on a silver platter, and if I beat him, I get to destroy The Unknown. Who last week decided it would be a good idea to get involved in my match. Something I’ve never understood. For as long as I can remember, people have gotten involved in my matches and later realize that it wasn’t such a good idea. At first they think that this is their time to shine. That they are going to make an example out of me by stealing the spotlight with some ridiculous attempt at making me look bad. Well, for the unknown, it’s not going to be any different. I am going to make sure I torture him until he realizes what he did was one of the biggest mistakes of his life. For now, I focus on Roxy and David Williams. Who needs to realize that being in this match does not give him any advantages. He is going to have to either kill both me and Roxy or render us unconscious in order to win. A feat that will not be easy to achieve. I don’t care if he was a champion in ACW. This is the XWF. You’re in the big leagues now son. And you’re about to realize that when you step through those ropes, the only thing you will leave with is a world of hurt.
Roxy, you and I have already danced the dance. We both took whatever the other had to offer and in the end neither one of us came out on top as the winner. This time it’s going to be different. This time it’s going to be me. For the last two weeks I’ve done nothing but severely hurt those put in my path. If you’ve seen what I’ve done to Jason Stevens and Dan Malcolm, what makes you think I won’t do it to either one of you? Last time, I wanted to play it safe and not hurt you. But this time? This time the gloves are off. I don’t give a shit if you’re a woman anymore. I saw what you can do and I know you’ve got what it takes. But will you have what it takes this time around? Will you actually be able to finish the job? It’s no secret that the last time I was in a triple threat match, I lost. So you’re thinking the outcome will be the same this time around right? WRONG! I’ve learned from my mistakes. I watched the tape of the World Title match and I know what needs to be done in order to win. I know what I have to do to come out on top. I won’t rest, I won’t stop and I won’t quit until I see the two of you lying on the mat with your eyes rolled into the back of your head. After I roll out of the ring declared the winner, I will set my eyes on Chris Page. Who think that just because he’s had an edge over me in the last three years that he’s got my number. Chris, we are not going to be facing off anytime soon. But at the Pay Per View? That’s a different story. If you remember correctly, the last time we faced off it ended in a draw. Why? Because I proved to you that you’re no longer a threat. That you’ve no longer “got my number”. Things are very different this time around. And when it’s time to come face to face with me once again, I am going to shut you up….HARD!
After I wipe the floor with you, I get to toss the unknown around like a rag doll. I will take him from pillar to post and make sure he never interferes in my matches again. I’ll do to him the same exact thing I did to those who have attempted to make a name for themselves on my account. And after that, all that is left is my shot at the Universal Title. The holy grail of the XWF. The one belt I have yet to hold. Once I achieve that, I will truly seal my fate as a legend and as one of the best in this business. James Raven thinks he’s got it in the bag. That he’s going to just walk away with the title and that no one is ever going to step up and knock him down. Well, that man is going to be me. And that time is going to be at Rage in the Cage. This is my time and no one is going to take that opportunity from me. I have not come this far to lose it all now. I am Famine of the Vile. I am the most sadistic, ruthless and uncaring son of a bitch this sport has ever seen. And when the time comes to hit the gas and drive full speed ahead, I am not only going to run over Chris Page and the unknown. But I am going to show James Raven that anything CAN and WILL happen when the time is right. I hope those of you whom I mentioned are watching this. Because this is not only your first and last warning. It’s a sign for you to prepare yourselves for what is coming. Your end is near! And I am going to make sure that you never forget the day you stood face to face with the epitome of evil. So it has been spoken……..So it shall be done!
