The Demon King: a chapel on 24 December 2008 (roleplay)

c. 27 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Demon King Wednesday, December 24th 2008, It is about 5:40pm and in the west, the sky played host to a round silhouette, slightly golden in color, and wrapped in a challenging haze. The giant star watched blindly as the third planet held secrets that only God knew about.
In the courtyard, covered in an array of flowers stood a figure of a man in a dark robe. He had chosen the courtyard that evening because of the evil that was perpetually looming in the halls of the church. Evil he summoned through no fault of the church. Evil that plagued the old chapel.
Here at the edge of the garden, the religious man never tired of watching the sunset, was lost in prayer. He thanked God for all the beauty that colored the chapel. He thanked God for the chance to teach his word to the world even though the majority of the civilized world didn't want to listen. Few indeed were only coming to the chapel to cleanse their sins. However, the religious man never turned them away. It simply wasn’t God’s will to do so.
The religious man drew a deep breath of the evening air, then let it out slowly. It had a slightly sweet taste, and it made him feel good. He gazed across the garden, wondering why evil has planted roots in his Chapel.
It was a strange evil that deepened around him as he allowed it to consume his sanity. It was the type of evil that didn’t just hide in the darkness. It penetrated anything it wanted. It was almost a religious evil.
A deep voice whispered. The religious man stared fixedly at the flowers as he wished the voice away. A warm breeze touched him. It was almost like a breath against his cheek. A warm, dry breath.
"Ashes to ashes" Somewhere in the distance, he knew God was watching this. The religious man turned his head, catching a glimpse of something demonic. His dark features were compromised, as evil whispered, his mind would not stay still.
The breeze touched his face again, warmth against his cheek. It taunted him, but he did not challenge it in any way.
"Ashes to ashes." The whisper was haunting as it penetrated his sanity. He knew it was evil, but he couldn’t stop it. It just came against his will.
" Stop it! Stop it!" he demanded. "Ashes to ashes" The religious man cupped his ears and closed his eyes, but that did not stop the whispers that assaulted his mind. It did not turn away the voices in his head.
" Can I pick some flowers?" He was confused. " Excuse me, father?" The religious man opened his eyes slowly, and saw a young girl standing in front of him. She was carrying a small woven wooden basket and a pair of rusted shears.
" Well, can I?" " Flowers," he trembled. She nodded. " You want to pick flowers from my garden?" She smiled. " Yes, well of course you can," he replied. " Thank you." He nodded emphatically, and relieved that it wasn’t evil playing tricks on him. " You have a wonderful garden," she added. The religious man smiled. " My name is Claire Anderson," she offered. The religious man nodded slightly at the young girl, knowing that he allowed her to pick flowers only because it kept the evil voices out of his head. He realized that having another person with him allowed the sanity to flow freely.
Turning away from the religious man, the young girl walked toward the big red flowers that didn’t seem to close up like the roses. They dangled in the breeze awaiting attention. The young girl knew it, and she wanted to be the first to offer those flowers the much needed attention. She envisioned her woven wooden basket completely filled to the brim with those flowers.
"Ashes to ashes." The religious man couldn’t believe that he still had the voices in his head. For a moment he saw a demonic Being standing behind the little girl as she picked her flowers. It was a crazy feeling that raced through his body. He experienced a flicker of amusement. A trace of jubilee entered his heart.
Kill her! The garden was decorated for most part in typical Italian style. Vines were sprawled around the structure of the old chapel. The garden furnishings included water falls, and bird baths covered the courtyard. It was a very attractive garden, and they purposely avoided the bareness feeling, and covered every spacious surroundings with some garden ornament.
A water sculpture splashed a faint mist on the religious man’s face as he walked closer to the young girl. Her blonde hair was pulled back, and held together with a blue ribbon. Her innocense emanated from her tiny torso like the halo would from an angel.
You now would have the chance to experience the flesh of another human. Take her, and use her. She is pure.
" She’s a child!" Take her, and use her. She is pure! The voices in his head continued to talk. they raced through his head like a car in a race going over 200 miles an hour.
" Stop it!" he shouted as he ran back into the chapel. Ashes to ashes. The man stood in front of a large mirror in the entrance way. The mirror had dark red wooden trims, and it was slightly cracked near the bottom. The light from the entrance way came off the mirror and reflected on the man’s face, revealing new age lines that had not been there before. His bones seemed more prominent, and his gray hair, thinner. He had cheekbones showing through his olive complexion.
" Does my age and this insanity travel hand and hand?" Kill the child! " I will not take the life of an innocent child!" he shouted while gazing at his reflection in the mirror. Everything soon fades to black as the voice of a news anchor fades in slowly..........
Anchor: And tonight police are looking into the murder of a young girl names Claire Anderson. Anderson was last seen at the chapel on hope springs drive getting flowers from the garden for her grandmothers birthday. She was found buried in the same garden about three feet deep. Police say that a cleaning lady was taking a break when she noticed something near the roses and found the little girls skirt and hair tie on the ground. Investigators will begin questioning father Riley Thomas who was there around the time of the murder. A motive and reason are unknown at this time. In other news..............
It is undeniable. The power of evil. It is something that lives in all of us yet some of us choose to deny it. Look at this poor son of a bitch. He decided long ago to deny himself the true pleasures in life and become a so called man of god......And now look at him. Probably facing the death penalty for giving in to temptation. I love it. I love when they see life for what it is. I love when they see the truth in life and stop hiding behind the bullshit they believe is wholesome and good. This is what happens each and every day in the world. All the murder, death and destruction is exactly the kind of element I thrive in. Blame the devil, blame human kind. But deep down inside, you know that you must blame me. Why? Because you see me as a reason for this. You look my way and say, he's the kind of monster responsible for things like this. And I AM! When it comes down to it, I am responsible for many things in this lifetime. I am also the monster that hides under the beds and closets of children everywhere! They can't sleep because they know I am waiting for the lights to go out, the parents to go to bed, and the wind to begin howling! It is one of my many pleasures. What do you have to look forward to Nick? Baseball games? Time a long with a girlfriend? If you had one that is. What is it that drives you to be who you are? I guess we'll find out the answer to that tomorrow night.
THE DARK MESSIAH I sit here in my chair, I watch TV and look at the world. I see nothing but death and destruction. Murder, rape, death, robberies everywhere. All a waste of talent. A waste of potential. I sit back and I realize that this is all for naught. They are not evil enough. I get bored watching tireless images of Iraq and Baghdad and things going on in this god forsaken country. They need a lesson. A lesson in darkness and evil. And I will give it to them. I will show them all. I will make them listen. It's the only way they will ever understand that with the power we possess we can rule the world and bring armageddon early. I will show them that there is more than just robbing and killing. There is fear of the ultimate evil. The true dark king of death, fire and brimstone. The one true antichrist! I am the one. I am the true epitome of all that is wrong with this world. And I long to take this rage and release it upon the world for all to see. That is my mission in life. To bring back the dark days of old where I would ride endlessly. Town after town. City after city. Looking for and taking whatever the fuck I wanted. It didn't matter if there were men, women or children. All that mattered was that when I rode in there was life. And where I rode out, there wasn't. To this day people still think of me as the shadow in the corner that will grab them and take them straight to hell just like their mommies told them I would. And just like before, I will do it again, and again, and again, and again until the end of time. I am the one thing that you can never get rid of. You see, without darkness, there can be no light. Without light, darkness cannot exist. It is a delicate balance of life, nature and fate. Something we cannot control nor avoid. See, for many years, my fate has been to be the evil son of a bitch you see before you. Not some comedic, sideshow Bob kind of guy who gets laughed at and humilitated. I AM A FUCKING BEAST! My purpose in life is to destroy, desecrate and torture. It's all I know, it's all I do. Ask yourself if you know anything else. Do you know any other version of me that works? Any other version that doesn't have to commit the sacrifices and cause death to someone just because he has an insatiable taste for blood and gore. You will find your answer and that answer will be NO. Why? Because THIS IS who I am. I can't change. And no matter how many times I try, I continue to come back to the man I am destined to be until the end of time. I am the Demon King. Famine of the Vile. The epitome of all that is unholy and that will never change again. You see, I thought that having a son would change all that. I thought if I took myself on a new career path that it would maybe put me on the road to happiness. That was all lies and bullshit. This is what I am and I shall remain in this skin until my time on this planet is up. I've sat back and looked at myself in the mirror time and time again. I sat at my computer and I looked at the hate mail and comments about me. How I was washed up, no longer the man I once was, a freak, a man who should consider retiring. And I continued to pretend I could be someone else, but the truth is, I couldn't do it. I had to be me. I needed to be the dark and sadistic son of a bitch you see before you.
And so we come to the present. I have learned to deal with everything and accept the fact that I will never be anything else but a freak. A killer. And for those who doubt that I have done so, ask Jon Brown. He hired me KNOWING what I had done in the past. He knows all about the murders and how I got off scott free because of a technicality. Life is beautiful when you can kill with no consequences. I wanted to kill you too Nick. For so long I wanted to just grab you by that scrawny thing you call a neck and just fucking snap it. I wish I could have taken a knife or some kind of rusty tool and shoved it straight through your heart. I can always dream though. See, I don't want to kill you anymore. I want you to suffer for the rest of your pathetic life knowing that you can not now, nor ever beat the dark messiah. You will crawl away from the match wondering what will become of your life after taking the beating of a lifetime and barely surviving. Yes, I am going to let you survive. I am going to make sure you live through the pain so you can tell others what you experienced. In hell, no one is ever free from the pain the suffered in life. And I will make sure that you suffer from now until eternity and beyond. When you are reborn. You will continue to suffer. And so will your children if you ever have any. And so on and so forth. It may sound cliche or repetitive but you and I both know you have no chance in hell of defeating something you fear. And you and I both know you fear me. You're afraid to admit it because like all men, we have the tendency to lie about how we feel. But I don't. You see, I fear nothing and no one. I know what I am and I know what I can do. So why fear when you ARE feared? I hope you have prayed Nick. I hope for your sake you are ready for what will be known as the most deadly and brutal match of XWF history!
PURE EVIL! The scene opens up to a large but mostly empty attic. It looks like an old abandoned house from the outside. But on the inside, the house looks like an old Victorian home, well kept. Back in the attic, there are some unused furniture among other tarnished items lay tucked away in corners. In the middle, two candles are lit. The light flickers, casting a glow on a girls face. All around the attic there are entrances to the rooms below. There’s a larger one under the attached folded ladder. The girls eyes open. She quickly sits up. Her eyes dart all around the attic. She immediately stands on her feet. She hurries to the entrance in the floor with the ladder attached. She pushes on it. It opens. The ladder unfolds - falls down into the floor beneath. She looks back. Suddenly, she sees someone standing there. The figure stalks behind her.
No time to climb down the ladder! She dives into the opening. A knife slices through the air. It slams through her calf and sticks into the wall beside her. She hangs upside down, pinned to the wall. Suddenly she lets out a piercing scream. Blood runs up her leg, dripping onto the ladder and floor. She grips the steps of the ladder, trying to pull herself down. The figure grabs her waist and snatches the knife out of the wall, freeing her leg. The figure pulls her back into the attic. Her screams are heard through the entire house though no one outside can hear. Moments later the screams stop. The ladder and attic door are pulled up.
Later in another room, the door opens. A guy runs out into the hallway. He loks around trying to find the girl he was with but doesn't know where to start looking since they got separated and doesn't know where she ended up. He moves down the hall toward another room. He opens the door, checking to see if anyone is in there. But it's empty. He proceeds down the hall and then stops just short of the blood droplets that stain the wood floor. As he looks up from the blood. His face is twisted in panic. He continues to walk and call for the girl but there is no answer. Suddenly behind him, the attic door hangs down again. Ladder retracted. A silhouette can be seen making its way down. He surveys the living-room from the walkway.
The floor creeks loudly behind him. He turns and the figure thrusts the knife deep into hiss side. He howls in pain as blood runs down his side, staining his t-shirt and jeans. He grips the figure, pushing him back, slamming him into the side wall. The figures hands tighten around the guys arm. He shoves him forcefully. the guy topples over the edge of railing, plummeting. A javelin meets his fall. The pointed tip goes through his neck, piercing his windpipe. The holder tips over as he slides down the spear. The scene is now in the living room as the guy slams into the ground just behind the couch. Half the javelin is through his neck. He chokes, struggling to breathe as blood shoots out of his mouth.
His hands go up, clawing at his neck. His fingernails dig deep into his throat, shredding off strips of skin. The figure begins descending the stairs. The guy looks up at him...his eyes pleading for help. His mouth opens, as if trying to say something but only the gurgling of blood is heard. Tears run from his eyes and slowly descend down the side of his face. The figure crouches down and grips both sides of the javelin which are sticking out of his neck. As the camera zooms in, we can see that it is none other than the demon king himself. Famine of the Vile. He pushes forward on one side and back on the other, slowly twisting the guys neck. Famine thrusts forward quickly on the javelin. We can hear the sound of a neck snapping. The guys grip on his neck slackens and falls limp at his side. Famine lets go. The boy simply slumps to the ground, lifeless.
Famine stands up and just walks out of the house, closing the door behind him. The only thing we can see is a devilish smile on his face as the scene fades to black. The sounds of an evil laughter is the only thing heard......
A few hours later, the scene opens outside a pair of tall iron gates. The camera pans upward to see just how high up they are. They look to be a good ten feet to the top. The shot also catches the darkened skies during a brisk night. The moon is out, but not full, just a little under half is shown. Some stars riddle the heavens, giving it a glittery look. Very few clouds roll in, being drifted along slowly by the mild winds. The shot pans back down to get a good look beyond the iron gates. Not much is really visible but a few shadowy silhouettes off in a short distance. The wind blowing causes the iron gates to creak loudly with age and rust. The shot pans a little off to the right until the sight falls over an old dirty sign. The letters are too worn from age and weather to read, but you can assume safely that it once displayed the name of this place. After the shot gets a good long shot of the sign, the gate slowly creaks open.
The shot slowly moves forward, and some of the shadowy silhouettes come plainly into view now. They are tombstones and large angelic statues. Rows beyond rows of tombstones slowly come into view as the camera comes to the damp base of a large hill. The shot slowly pans to the top of the hill, where the huge shadow of a tree resides. The shot slowly climbs the hill, and in a matter of minutes, it finally reaches the top. At the top of the hill, the whole cemetery can be looked over, but the deep dark sea of shadows prevents that on this night. Since it is the higher ground, the wind is a bit stronger; it begins to carry leaves and debris along the ground and through the air. The shot slowly pans over the whole darkened area before coming back to the trunk of the large tree. The pale light of the above moon shines down quite well on this area, but under the tree are still shadows. Before anything can else can be done, a voice emerges out of nowhere.
Even in death, no one is safe from the clutches of darkness. Just look around, the darkness that engulfs this very place is much stronger than anywhere else on this planet. Why you may ask? The shadows hide the real truth of what’s in everything. To understand the true meaning of a certain thing, we have to understand the shadows that cover it. All those who have perished and lie here tonight beyond my presence, they all had a shadow deep within their souls. Everyone in this world has shadows they don’t understand. Many try to figure them out, but it’s too complicated for a typical mind. I have a shadow much larger than any being alive or dead. I chose to accept my destiny, and my destiny keeps me going every single day. Nothing can stop me until I have accomplished my destiny. You can’t kill what is already dead, and I died a long time ago. I am what no man has ever seen before; I am the true form that evil has taken to prove that this world will always be corrupted. Many have tried to deface me, many have tried to destroy me, but none have what it takes to rid the world of an evil they can’t accept, nor understand.
Nick Ryan who is now a former friend seems to think that he is going to send me back to chasm from where I came. He seems to believe that there is no fear within him. That he can stand up to the darkness that a soul like mine possesses. Nick, why be foolish? Why try to do something stupid like this? Why don't you just forfeit your half of the titles and move on. Find yourself a nice cozy cabana somewhere and enjoy retirement? Before you think you know anything about me, you should pay more attention to the shadows over your own soul. Do you really think I care if you fear me or not? I give you all the credit in the world for not having any fear, but actions speak louder than words. You spoke your part last week and got a few shots in, but when it comes time to play your part once again, will you be able to do what it is you preach? You have never proven to be a challenge. You yourself said I was the better out of the two of us. Yet you took it upon yourself to make it seem as if you were doing all the work and I was nothing, but what will the end result be when you have to set foot in a place you have never been? For me, hell is home. The Devil's Playground match is the quintessence of all I stand for, all I know, and all I live for. I tried to find my way out of the darkness, but it is something just too powerful to overcome.
People like you may get lucky every once in a while, but you won’t get lucky this time. Spew your mindless jokes, and make all the funny comments in the world. I enjoy when the hopes of my prey are high, it gives me much more pleasure when I move in for the kill. All you will be after Xmas Xtreme is just another victim that Famine of the Vile destroyed and humiliated.
The poison of darkness has once again been injected in the veins of the XWF, now it’ll be only a matter of time before the life of it is diminished from the inside out. What makes it ever so sweeter, is that many of my sworn adversaries are finally going to fall as I unleash my wrath. Just as I did when I first came to this company, I will once again, make believers out of all of you, and enjoy the gruesome feast. I beg you, bring everything you’ve got, even then it won’t be enough to delay the inevitable. When I am done with you Nick, I will plow through anyone else they put in my path!!
