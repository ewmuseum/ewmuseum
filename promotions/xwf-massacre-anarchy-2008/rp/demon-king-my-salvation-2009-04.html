And even though I lost my match on Thursday, that doesn’t mean I’m not ready. Thursday was just one of those nights where I was focused on the task at hand and not the moment. It happens. I make no excuses. I lost. I’ll deal with it. And when I do, Kristopher will feel the wrath of the god of darkness.
Now, let me get this straight. You said the World Title rightfully belongs to you? If that was the case then the title should be rightfully mine as well. Hell, it should rightfully belong to Rage, Nick Ryan, Fuzz, Smitty and countless others who feel the same way you do. But you see Chris, the title doesn’t matter to me. I am not here to brag about past championship reigns nor am I here to boast about accomplishments. I am here to prove a point. That Hadrian Burke, Famine of the Vile, The Demon King, The True God of Darkness, The Epitome of Evil, whatever name you want to give me, can still roll with the best. I don’t claim to be something I’m not. I claim the truth. I have been in this business since you were nothing more than a little pot smokers baby sitting in a crib with shit in your diapers. I’ve come across so many men like you I can’t count them. The number is too great. But I will say this Chris. Out of all of them, you are truly a gifted athlete. You see, they may have had your attitude, but they lacked the skill. This Monday we are going to step into the most sadistic creation ever built. And I will put you to the test and see how good your skills are when you have to come face to face with reality Chris. And the reality is that this is not the past. You’re not facing that Famine of the Vile who was too stupid to realize he was being set up. This time around, there will be no one in your corner. No one to save you from the ass kicking I will deliver. No one to cheat for you and get you the title because they know you’re no match for the opponent you’re facing. This time it’s one on one. Mano a mano! As my favorite writer once said “The general who wins a battle makes many calculations in his temple ere the battle is fought. The general who loses a battle makes but few calculations beforehand. Thus do many calculations lead to victory and few calculations to defeat: how much more no calculation at all! It is by attention to this point that I can foresee who is likely to w in or lose.” Now, let me break that down for you since I am sure that a man like you would not even being to comprehend what Sun Tzu speaks of. You see Chris, many great men, choose their strategies wisely. They plan ahead and calculate each and every single move before they attack. Other’s try but fail. In this case, it is you that needs to rethink his strategy. Because to be honest with you Chris, I see nothing but the same old pot smoking Page from the old WGWF days. And if that is who I am about to face, you might as well pack those bags now and get ready to leave the arena without that title. Injury or not, I am going to punish you and prove that there is nothing better than the rebirth of a man with a destiny. I will open the gates of hell and introduce you to Satan himself. Chris, You may be knocking out the big guns, but Monday, it will be you, bringing a knife to a gun fight!
