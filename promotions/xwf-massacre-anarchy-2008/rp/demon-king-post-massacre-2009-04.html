XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Roleplay

Famine of the Vile: after Massacre, in the hospital (roleplay)

c. 23 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Famine of the Vile How do I even begin to define what I went through on Monday night? I sat back in my hospital bed and thought about every punch, every move, and every weapon attack. I took everything Chris had to dish out and still, here I am. Yet he will say that I couldn’t beat him or that he was screwed. Now, why is it that every time a guy like this loses a match or it doesn’t go the way they wanted it to go, they talk about being screwed? Chris went on to try to explain what the meaning was and completely went off track and once again fell back into the past to describe what screwed meant. But he only described what it meant to him. So folks, simply put, here is the meaning of screwed: To take advantage of; cheat: Now, please explain to me how he was cheated out of a victory? Also, how was he taken advantage of? Chris Page seems like a little bitch to me. The type of bitch that just whines and makes himself look like he’s the victim. It’s the same song and dance I’m sure you’ve all heard over and over again. It’s tiring and old. Why don’t you find something else to talk about or maybe get yourself a new gimmick? Normally I go back to the past and talk about things that have nothing to do with the here and now. But that’s not me anymore. I guess when things in your life change and make you see clearer, your perception changes as well. So why would I bother talking about the past when I have so much to look forward to in the future? I heard Chris say that I’m trying to catch lightning in a bottle. That I am trying to do what other XWF alumni have done before; Trying to get back into the spotlight one last time before I ride off into the sunset. Chris, you couldn’t be more wrong. You see, I am not trying to relive my glory days in the spotlight. My glory days never left. I am still as glorious and am a bigger star that you will ever be. You’re just a young up and coming superstar that has a lot going for him. But like I proved to you on Monday, I still have a lot left in me before I’m done. And not only did I prove that once, I proved it twice. After my match with you, I was admitted to the hospital and then went back to the ring on Anarchy to destCliff: Trent Gein who didn’t even bother to put up a fight. THAT right there my friend is a sign that this old man doesn’t play nice. And come Purple Haze, I will prove once again why I am still a man to be feared. But you’re not the only one in this match I have to deal with. Now I have to deal with Sam Hain as well. I ask you, why in the blue fuck is he in this match? The guy hasn’t been around for months and all of a sudden he gets a world title shot? Are you fucking kidding me? I’ll get to him later but I just want to continue to speak on Chris Page and myself. Chris, this might just be our final match in our long history of feuding. Why do I say that? Well, like I said a while back. Win or lose, my time in the XWF is over. I have too much to live for now and I don’t want to end up losing it all because of this business. I know a lot of men who have lost their family and lives because they couldn’t let go of the job they love so much. And even though I love this business, I’ve been in it for too long and it’s time to hang up the boots. I love my future wife too much to be like my father. Even though I have mended things with my father and we are good friends now, I can never forgive him for the things he did to me, my mom and my sister in the past. I can never forgive him for not being there for me when I was growing up. I did it once and I will never do it again. My son Brendan (Soul Bearer to those who know him) grew up without me and hated me at first. The truth is that I never knew he existed until my friend found him. Now that is a mistake I will never let happen again. If I become a father once again, I will make sure I am there for him or her until the day I die. Now this is something you may or may not understand Chris, but when a man knows that his time is limited, he goes all out and does whatever he has to do to make it to the top of that mountain one last time. He wants to jump out of that plane and skydive because he knows that he might die tomorrow. He wants to go cliff diving and so on and so forth. It’s an adrenaline rush! I felt it during our match and I never felt more alive. And when we get back into the ring on Sunday, I will feel it once again. And I will take you and Sam on and give everything I’ve ever had. What do I have left? A last chance at the World Title? A last chance at greatness? No. Because I am already great and every time I step out into that arena, the fans cheer for me no matter what side I’m on. My legacy will live forever and even after I’m gone, you will never be able to shadow it. Famine of the Vile is now and will forever be bigger and better than you! Despite the claims you make about being better than people, you’re nothing more than a man like I am and like everyone else is. You can be defeated just like I can and just like anyone else. And I promise you this Chris, Sunday night will see you defeated at my hands. Sam Hain, I don’t know what rock you’ve been under, but once I’m done with you, you’ll want to crawl back under it because nothing or no one is going to stand in the way of my final chance at glory. I’ve been in this spot before and I will be there again.>
Monday night. Three hours after Massacre went off the air. Both Chris Page and Famine of the Vile were taken to separate hospitals. Famine to Orlando Regional Medical Center and Chris to Winnie Palmer Hospital for Women and Babies! While Famine is being treated, Jensen Kent is visiting Chris Page. In Famine’s room is his girlfriend Violet who is very worried at this moment.
Violet: Here baby. I got you your favorite sub. Famine: Thank you baby. Have you seen the doctor? Violet: No, they told me he should be here soon though. Famine: Good. I wanna get the fuck out of here already. I need to get ready for Thursday. Violet: Thursday? What are you doing Thursday? Famine: I have a match against Trent. Violet: Last night you damn near killed yourself fighting with Page and you're going to go back at it just a few days later? You don't know the damage that match cost you.
Famine: Hon, listen, i'm fine. I've been through this many, many times. If you had any idea how many times this has happened you would be surprised. I'm sure that the doc will say I can go home. Nothing to worry about.
Suddenly, Famine's cell phone rings. He looks at the caller ID and doesn't recognize the number.
Famine: Hello? Kim: Hey cousin... Famine: Kim? Is that you? Kim: Yeah it is. How are you doing after that hellacious match.. Famine: I'm alive. That's all that matters. How's everything with you? Kim: Busy keeping everyone in check on this end. Famine: If anyone is good at doing that it's you. How are my boys? Kim: Raising hell and kicking ass.. Nothing new there Famine: Glad to see things are still the same. So what can I do for you while I wait for the idiot doctor to show up?
Kim: What?!? I can't call you in concern over your health ? Famine: Of course you can. I just didn't know you had my number. Kim: You're family H. I gotta check up on the legendary member that used to raise hell with my brothers. They still tell stories about your days with them
Famine: It's good to know I'm remembered. Kim: By the way, Mase asked when you're coming down here again. Famine: As soon as I retire. Just need to wait and see what happens at the pay per view Kim: Retire? You sound just like Drew, he always says it.. And he is still at it Famine: but this time things are different. I have Violet now, and with us trying to have a baby I really do think it's time to step down for good
Kim: Yeah I can understand that, how is Violet doing with all of this.. Famine: She took it hard last night. I saw the fear and tears in her eyes and realized that I can't keep doing this no matter how much I love the business. I think Purple Haze might be my last show. Win or lose.
Kim: I bet it was hard on her having to watch that, if she wants to get away from it for a bit she is always welcome at my place here in Florida.. Do tell her that.. You need to find something that pleases both of you H.. You love the business and her.. Don't do something you might regret later...
Famine: You're right. I just need to do this one last match. I don't want to walk away with any regrets. I need to know that I can give it every ounce of energy I have left before I can hang them up for good
Kim: Don't hang them up just yet.. Set them in the closet for if and when the time comes to put them on.. Even if it is for 1 night only that you can just grab them and go...
Famine: I don't know Skittles. I really think that with everything going on right now I may really just call it quits forever
Kim: I still say you should save them just in case. But I respect that. Suddenly, Famine's doctor walks in. Famine: Hey, the doctor just walked in so I will talk to you again soon ok. And i'll keep in mind that offer for Violet.
Kim: Ok. Just give me a call when things are cool. Love you cuz. Famine: Love you too Skittles. Talk to you soon. So....what's the word doc? Doctor: You suffered a serious concussion and you have miltiple internal bruises. I'm afraid I can't allow you to wrestle for at least two weeks.
Famine: Two weeks? Fuck that! I have a match on Sunday for the World Title. Doctor: Well, the problem is Mr. Burke, that if you suffer any more injuries to the head, you could suffer serious brain damage.
Violet: Hon, maybe you should think about this. I mean you can't risk anything with everything we're planning.
Famine: Look. I understand you're worried about me baby. But i've been through this a hundred times. Some doctor tells me the same crap and yet here I am.
Doctor: This might be, but over the years, the injuries suffered to the head or brain become more and more permanent. I can't stop you from leaving or competing because I am not the licensed doctor for your company but if you want to see the rest of your life I suggest you think about it.
Violet: Thank you doctor. The doctor walks out of the room as Famine stares at her. She is looking at him with a look as if she wants to slap him.
Famine: Please don’t look at me like that. Look, this might be my last chance at the World Title and I need this ok? Not only will this seal my destiny as an XWF legend but it will mean that I finally shut Chris up once and for all.
Violet: Well, I really think you need to reconsider this. Famine: Why? Violet: I’m pregnant! Famine’s eyes grow large as this news hits him like a ton of brick. He pulls her closer to him and hugs her tight. For the first time in many, many years, we actually see a smile on his face.
Famine: When did this happen? Violet: Three weeks ago. I just didn’t know when to tell you because I know you’ve been looking forward to your shot at the World Title.
Famine: That doesn’t matter baby. I mean this is big, are you kidding? Violet: So you’re happy? Famine: Why would you ask me something like that? I couldn’t be happier. This is what we both wanted.
Violet: I’m so glad you’re happy about this. Famine: I am. But now I need to ask you two things. Violet: Ok? Famine: First. Would you please let me do this last match on Sunday? Violet: You promise me it will be your last match? Famine: Yes. I promise Violet: What’s your second question? Famine: Will you marry me? Violet looks at him in shock and doesn’t know what to say. For over a minute she is speechless until finally.
Violet: Yes! Yes I will! Violet leans in and hugs him real tight and then they share a soft kiss before Jensen Kent walks in.
Jensen: Sorry, should I come back later? Famine: No it’s ok Kent. Come on in. Jensen: Violet. How are you? Violet: I’m doing good thank you. Famine: So what’s going on? Jensen: I was just over at Winnie Palmer seeing Page. Making sure he’s ok and here I am. Famine: So you go see him…..wait. Did you just say Winnie Palmer? Jensen: Yeah why? Famine: You know that’s a hospital for women and children only right? Jensen: What? Who the hell sent him there? Famine: I have no idea. But it’s ok. With that bitch looking face I’m sure they’ll think he’s a woman, kind of like in that movie Junior.
Jensen: Damn it Vile. Famine: Look man, keep your shorts on alright. What’s up? Jensen: Well, I wanted to come by and make sure things were ok and give you a message from Chris.
Famine: Oh yeah? And what did he say? Jensen: He said that he’s going to finish what he started earlier tonight on Sunday. And that your dreams of once again being on top are going to be crushed for good.
Famine: We’ll see about that. He thinks he’s got this one in the bag as usual. But this is one match he is not going to win. And if he does he’s going to have to destCliff: me to do it. And I promise you, that is one thing he is NOT going to do.
Jensen: Well look, I don’t want either one of you to get hurt so please just go out there, handle this like men but be sure you don’t kill each other. I am not going to be responsible for someone getting seriously injured.
Famine: What do you care? I’m on Anarchy anyway. Jensen: I care because we were partners at one point. I don’t just shrug off people I’ve worked with in the past. And from our business deals you’re a hell of a man and I respect you. I just want you to be careful. Not just for you but for your future wife and child.
Famine: How the hell did you know that? Jensen: I overheard you as I was coming in. Sorry. Famine: It’s ok man. Look, I’ll do my best to put on the best show ever. But I make no promises regarding Page. This ends on Sunday once and for all.
Jensen: Ok, I’ll leave you two alone. I have a plane to catch. I’ll see you Sunday. Famine: Ok. Jensen shakes Famine’s hand and walks out of the room as he and Violet go back to sharing an embrace before the scene fades to black.
I don’t think I’ve ever been good with words. I try to be as eloquent as our current Universal champion but that kind of banter does not flow from my mouth like it does his. I am not as refined yet angry as Rage is. But yet I manage to do ok for myself and muster up enough cojones to stand here before my fans and before the world to say that I know who I am. I know that I went out there and put on the performance of a lifetime. Yet Chris Page shrugs it off as nothing more than a good try. A great effort by a man who is trying to cling on to whatever little fame he has left. I have been through this before. It’s nothing new to me. I can deal with it and move on. But I will not move on until I manage to respond to a couple of things. And normally I hate doing this but I feel like for just one time I will do the ever popular yet taboo, promo dissection.
When will you have enough? I mean how bad to I have to beat you before it finally sinks in that you’re nowhere near the Legendary Icon that I am; you’ve been throughout your entire lack luster career. The only way you’ve managed to find success as a World Champion is when organizations hits a low period.
Chris. Let me enlighten you for a moment. You’re what, 26, 27? You’re a young buck trying to get the big dogs to run away so you can have the yard all to yourself. But it has yet to happen and I doubt it ever will. See you’re the kind of man who if he doesn’t get his way or things don’t go as planned you take your ball and go home. That is sad and pathetic. And then you have the balls to say that my career is lackluster? Chris, in the last 27 years, I have won more titles than you could even begin to dream about. I have wrestled in five different countries all with successful championship reigns. What have you done here in the XWF? Absolutely jack shit you loincloth chomping dick breath son of a bitch! I don’t even need to tell you what my accomplishments are. Just go to the XWF website and look around. You will see my name there and a history and bio. You’ll also see yours there. I’m sure you knew that though right? I mean as stupid as you are you at least have to know how to work a computer. But anyway let me move on because I have a lot to cover.
Don’t get me wrong I’ve got mad respect for what you’re trying to do. The whole Rocky and Eye of the Tiger thing is cute but come on… reality eventually has to set in and even you have to know when to call it quits. I now more than ever look forward to Purple Haze, because it will provide us the final stage to end this feud once and for all… because honestly I get tired of embarrassing you, not that it’s a difficult task because you yourself are responsible for it 90% of the time.
Ok, explain to me exactly how Rocky has anything to do with what I’m trying to do? First of all, Rocky was an uneducated debt collector for a loan shark in Philadelphia. I was born and grew up in Gothenburg, Sweden and left when I was 17 to live here in America. I wasn’t a loan collector and I have more education in my right pinky than you AND Rocky put together. So please explain to me how that’s even remotely close to what I’m trying to do? So to answer your comment; reality set in years ago. And this isn’t some last hurrah that I need to prove something. This is a goal. A dream! Something I have worked for that no one is going to take away from me. And as far as embarrassing goes, this would be a good case of the pot calling the kettle black. I mean you took on a 48 year old man and he kicked your ass. You couldn’t even get the job done. So shut the fuck up!
We played the game your way and you failed at getting the job done. Some may say I failed as well, but that’s where I disagree because it’s merely a matter of time before the XWF World Title is securely around my waist… we all know it, but if you couldn’t beat me at your game just how the hell do you think you’ll beat me in mine?
I failed to get the job done? I’m sorry let me make sure that I heard that correctly. You just said I didn’t get the job done right? Now correct me if I’m wrong, but we both ended up in the hospital right? I showed you pain like you never felt in your life. I took you to the edge of hell and proved that I still have what it takes. So I think I DID get the job done. I didn’t go out there to beat you in my own match. I went out there to prove a point. Regardless of the outcome, I showed you what I was trying to all along. That you can’t stop what you can’t kill.
They say God Giveth and God Taketh away. That saying will never ring more true then on March 29, 2009 as history is will be made when I walk out of the Toyota Center as the undisputed XWF World Heavyweight Champion.
I don’t believe in god. Never have, never will. But even for a guy who doesn’t believe in god. I know that it’s The Lord Giveth and the Lord Taketh away you idiot. And if we’re on the subject of giving and taking, then I will say right here and right now that I am going to giveth you the beating of your life and taketh away your chance at becoming the World champion. This is going to be the second time you fail. And it will all be because of me. How is that going to make you feel Chris? How is it going to make you feel, knowing that your shot at becoming an XWF great was taken away BY an XWF great? I hope you’re ready because March 29th IS going to be a night to remember. It will be a day that will live in infamy as the day that Famine of the Vile defied the odds and became the oldest XWF World champion of all time!
THE PRESS CONFERECE The scene opens to a room full of reports for different magazines such as Sports Today, Wrestling Insider, Pro Wrestling Illustrated and many others. They are waiting for several XWF superstars to arrive for a press conference regarding Purple Haze. The upcoming XWF pay per view scheduled for Sunday, March 29th. XWF interviewer and spokesperson Steve Sayors comes up to the podium to make an announcement.
Steve: Ladies and gentlemen, the XWF is proud to bring to you, Purple Haze. The pay per view that was created and designed by Chris Page after winning the own the pay per view tournament. We are going to be taking questions in a moment. Please wait until each superstar is seated and asks you to begin before asking any questions. Right now, allow me to introduce to you, a future XWF legend and hall of famer. And a man, known worldwide for being….Vile! Ladies and gentlemen, the Demon King himself, Famine of the Vile!
Master of Puppets begins to play as Famine makes his way out to the podium. He is wearing a striped shirt with some jeans and waves at the reporters before sitting down.
Famine: Thanks for coming guys. Go ahead with your questions. Scott: Famine, Scott from Wrestling Insider Weekly. How are you feeling after that brutal finish to your match on Monday? Do you think you’re ready for this Sunday?
Famine: Absolutely. I got a little banged up but I’ve had worse happen before. I’m definitely ready for Sunday and I promise that no matter what happens I am going to go out there and put on one of the best performances of my entire career. Next question.
Ed: Famine, Ed Rogers, Sports Today. You had a match on Monday and then on Thursday you went back to the ring to face an old rival in Trent Gein. We’re you trying to prove a point to Chris Page who has not wrestled since your match?
Famine: In a way yes I was. You see, for the last 6 years I’ve been labeled as the old guy or the old man. But time and again I have proven that I am a force to be reckoned with. I have been in this business for 27 years and in that time, I have taken more bumps and bruises than you can shake a stick at. I shouldn’t even be standing here walking or even talking to you yet here I am. Trent Gein didn’t bother to put up a fight which disappointed me. I know Chris Page is ready and is coming for me and not Sam Hain. But you know what? When all is said and done, not only will I earn his respect, but the world will see that this old man still has some gas left in the tank.
Charles: Famine, Charles Cooper, The Pro Wrestler Gazette. What are your plans after the pay per view?
Famine: Well, the truth is, I was hoping to retire but I don’t know. I mean if I win, I have to defend my title right? I made a promise to my fiancée that I would retire but right now it all depends on what happens Sunday so we’ll see.
Bill: Famine, Bill Prescott, PWI Magazine. You’ve mentioned every now and then that you are a future XWF legend. What do you think your chances are of becoming one?
Famine: Honestly Bill, I don’t know. I mention it because I feel that having been a part of this company for as long as I have been, I would be right up there with some of the best in the business. Truth is, whether or not Jon Brown decides to induct me, I will always be seen by my fans as a legend. I’m already a hall of famer and I am grateful for having been inducted, but to me, being an XWF legend is an honor and a privilege I hope to one day achieve.
Roger: Famine, Roger Stern, Wrestling Uncensored. Your former best friend and partner Nick Ryan left the XWF and opened his own company. How do you feel about that?
Famine: Nick Ryan is and will always be a great friend. He chose to leave and start his own business because he felt that there are things in this business that could be better. One day he and I will cross paths again and when that time comes, we’ll handle our business like men. I have time for one more question because I need to be at a few radio interviews.
Thomas: Famine, Thomas Eliss, The Wrestling Torch. What’s your prediction for this Sunday? Famine: In a word? Painful! Thanks for your time guys. Famine gets up from his chair and once again waves to the reporters as he leaves the podium. Steve Sayors comes back to the podium to announce the next guest as the scene fades to black.
Redemption. Theology. Deliverance from sin; salvation. This is the standard definition. To me, it’s a word I know too well. I’m not talking about redeeming myself to the world or to the people in the XWF. I am talking about redeeming myself for me. My career. My life. This is the last chance for me to show the world that the oldest wrestler in the XWF can still do it. That he can still climb to the top of that mountain and prove himself worthy of being called a champion. Chris. You may be a great competitor. A great talker. But the one thing you lack is will. Passion. Heart! You may be skilled in the ring but you lack what I have honed for years. You lack what will ultimately be your defeat. Bring everything you’ve got to the table and you will still come up short because honestly, no matter how good you are or think you are, you’re not going to stop me from kicking the ever loving shit out of you. I am not only going to mess up your face, but I am going to make sure that the name Famine of the Vile is sealed in your fucking soul forever!
When I leave Houston, Texas with the XW World Heavyweight Championship around my waist it will be the pinnacle of my career. It will solidify Chris Page as one of the absolute bests EVER to enter the SPORT of Wrestling. Sure I’ve had a successful career but people are always going to hate on you saying your past doesn’t mean shit, it’s about the present. Presently I’m the hottest thing going today and that’s not something I’m saying to get some cheep heat… when others express how much they enjoy your work it cements it all on it’s own. When you’re able to bounce from Main Event talent to Main Event talent and make them look like chumps; like mere children trying to act grown up it solidifies it for me.
I certainly do hope you mean the XWF World Title because if you’re not then it’s going to be a piece of cake for me to walk out of that match with it. I have no clue what the fuck the XW title is but unless you’re talking about another company, I suggest you get your fucking head on right because this is the XWF and you’re in a match with one seriously demented motherfucker. The same demented fuck that is going to outshine, out think and outsmart you like you’ve never seen. Because from where I’m sitting, you’re sitting there running your mouth over and over and over about how you’re the hottest thing going and blah blah blah. I mean it’s nice to love yourself and think highly but you take the fucking cake. If I didn’t know any better I’d say you had a goddamn man crush on yourself. I don’t know what that’s like and I don’t wanna know but you might wanna keep that shit to yourself and not let the world know.
I don’t think you’re ready to go another round with greatness but I will admit I’m glad it’s going to finally end. You’ve been a man on a mission, that mission is to defeat the ONE man you’ve never been able to defeat… that man is me. Famine you have to understand that this is business and I refuse to allow you to stand in my way of claiming what rightfully belongs to me. We can cut the shit… this is you and I talking right now. You better than anyone else knows that I am currently the uncrowned champion and this match is meant for you to have that one great last stand to be remembered for… you NEED to be champion to feel important once again in a world who FORGOT who you where.
I have never been more ready than I am right now. And you’re right. I didn’t use my best material last week. THAT was only a small example of what I am going to bring this Sunday. Now, the thing that makes me laugh is that technically, you’ve only beaten me once. And I’d like to explain this before you go on some fucking rant about how it’s been four. You beat me in the War Games match we had where it was just you and I left. THAT is one defeat I will not deny. But the rest? The rest are bullshit. You had help. You needed back up to kick my ass and pin me. You know deep down inside that the one time you beat me was the only time you could and ever would be able to. The rest of those so called wins? Nothing but bullshit. This Sunday will be YOUR last chance to prove that you are a man and not a bitch. Only a bitch cheats to win. A REAL man doesn’t need to cheat. And I know that sounds funny coming from me since that was once my moniker but those days are behind me. Now it’s time to man up and shut the fuck up. Get in the ring and fight to see which one of us has enough balls to come out on top and truly prove we are the best. I don’t NEED this title to be remembered. I was never forgotten. I have been in this business since you were in fucking diapers and have always had a following no matter where I’ve been. If this title truly belongs to you, then I want you to get in that ring and prove it. I’ve held the World Title before. And no matter how good I am, was and WILL be, I know there have been greater men than me that have respectively held this title before. For you to run your mouth and say you’re the greatest or will be the greatest XWF World Champion of all time is a joke. And I’m going to prove once and for all that you are NOT the man you make everyone believe you are. I will prove that you, like anyone else they put in my path CAN and WILL be destroyed!
