XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Roleplay

The Demon King: The Return of a Demon King, the Birth of a God (roleplay)

c. 27 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Demon King THE RETURN OF A DEMON KING.......THE BIRTH OF A GOD For so long have I waited to return to my throne. To return to the darkness that I once cherished and held close to my black heart. I have returned to where I am at home. Where I am comfortable. This is the real me. This is who I am and who I should have been all along. The events that lead to this moment were inevitable. Fate guides us. It leads us on the paths we choose to walk. I chose this path long ago and now I am home once again. I can now sit at my throne and begin my plans for dominance. To prepare for the blood that will be spilled at my hands. Ohhhh the taste, the texture. The blood of others calls me. It wants me to release it. To help it go free from it's prison. I can hear the blood call me and say release me. To many, I am nothing more than a character. Acting as if this is real to me. Pretending to frighten people and then go home and remove the make up and the clothing. But they could never be more wrong. This IS the real me. The make up, the clothes, the way I acted before. THAT was the facade. Pretending on the outside to be some stupid, comical, childish and immature man who only wanted to live life by having fun at other peoples expense. THAT was the lie I lived for far too long. And if there is anyone that I have to thank for making me see the truth.....it's Boondock Saint. Yes. You Daniel Malcolm.....It was you that planted the seed and now the rebirth of the demon king has come. Don't remember? Let me refresh your memory. It was you who said
"You have dropped the dark and scary persona for a new clean cut look. You may be smiling inside, but I know that you are really dead inside. You wish you could cut loose and by the man that you once were. I don’t see the Famine of the Vile that once terrorized the XWF. I see half a man and that man is dead inside. I never thought you would stoop so low as to sell out your beliefs for a stupid campaign. The real Famine is dead!"
Those were YOUR words Daniel. And because of those very words, what happens from here on out will be pure unadulterated destruction. I stand before you a new ma.......no. I stand before you as a god. A man who can singlehandedly take his hand and strike with vengeance. You see before you a man who does not fear death. Does not care for a human life and will take it in the blink of an eye. Some see me as sadistic, masochistic and insane. And they would all be right. There is nothing more pleasing than having someone bleed to death before my very eyes. There is nothing more satisfying than to have someone try to hit you as hard as they fucking can to no avail. It just makes it that much easier for me to crush their goddamn skulls in. And let this be a fair warning to those of you who think they will get in my way and try to stop me. Whatever I do to you, and whatever I do to anyone else is Boondock Saint's fault. Blame him. Not me. I am merely doing what I was born to do. What I was MADE for. I am a killer. A lunatic. Any name that falls right along those same lines will do. Prepare for the new and improved Demon King.
FORGIVE ME FATHER. FOR I HAVE KILLED YOU It has been said, that, when any strange, supernatural, and necromantic adventure has occurred to a human being, that being, however desirous he or she may be to conceal the same, feels at certain periods torn up as it were by an intellectual earthquake, and is forced to bare the inner depths of his spirit to another. I am a witness of the truth of this. I have dearly sworn to myself never to reveal to human ears the horrors to which I once, in excess of fiendly pride, delivered myself over. The holy man who heard my confession, and reconciled me to the church, is dead. - Mary Shelley: from The Transformation.
It is 9:45pm. The streets are barely alive with people. It is cold. Almost 32 degrees on this December evening. The camera pans around to get a good view of the city streets but then stops at a church. A beautiful, old, and well kept cathedral.
There are not many people inside, but our cameras follow one man who decides to stand in front of the church for about ten minutes before entering. As he goes in the camera follows. Getting a good view of the inside. And a few people that are sitting down reading the bible. Others are praying.
Over to the left is the confessional. The man enters and sits down. On the other side is a priest who was waiting for someone to come inside and confess their sins.
Man: Forgive me father for I have sinned. Father: What is troubling you my son? Man: I have recently made a dramatic change in my life and I fear that with this change I am going to hurt someone. Maybe many.
Father: Why do you feel like this will happen? Is there something that has made you think or believe this will happen?
Man: A few weeks ago a man said something about me that made me sit back and really think about my life and what I was doing. His words caused me to rethink my life and now here I am. Soon I will be meeting a man and I am afraid he may be seriously hurt by my actions. The funny thing is father. That I want to hurt him to get to someone else.
Father: Have you looked into counceling or maybe coming to the church to get baptized? I believe what you are experiencing is nothing more than temporary anxiety or maybe a small state of delusion. I believe the lord has a purpose for everyone. He helps all who come to him and you are no different my son.
Man: But what if I WANT to do this? What if it's all really me and I want to commit these crimes and hurt people? What if all I am is nothing more than a killer father? What then?
Father: Then my son your only other option is to speak to the police or a councelor of some kind. If you feel that the good lord cannot help you, that would be the alternative.
Suddenly the man punches through the wood that separates the two men and pulls the priest into his side. He holds him by the neck and raises him about a foot off the ground.
Man: See....That's the problem with you fucking priests and so called men of god. When he doesn't have the answer you immediately turn to authorities or other professionals who can't do a goodamn thing for me or anyone else.
The father tries to breathe but the mans grasp is too strong. Man: Do you need some air? Here, how's this? Father (gasping for air): I.......I don't know.......what you cough cough.......are looking for. But this......this isn't the answer.....
Man: What am I looking for? Answers. Answers as to why your so called god is said to perform miracles yet here you are about to die and he can't do a fucking thing about it!
A few of the people in the church hear the commotion and one calls the police. Man: Tell me something father. Why should I let you live? Why should I walk out of here before the cops come and spare your life?
Father: Because if you don't, you will burn in hell with the rest of the sinners who commit crimes and don't devote their lives to the lord.
The man looks at the priest right in the eyes and then snaps his neck. The priests body falls hard onto the ground and the man stands there looking down.
Man: Wrong answer father....You see.....I already AM in hell. Tell your so called god when you see him that I send my regards.
The sound of police sirens can be heard inside the cathedral now. The man leaves unnoticed as the cops make their way inside. When they arrive they see the priest lying on the ground. The killer gone.......
WORDS FROM THE VILE ONE The scene begins with a tape of Monday Night Massacre playing. The scene is of the returning Vile one and what transpired between he and Nick Ryan
FAMINE: Last night……I told BOONDOCK SAINT……That he would be the first person to suffer at my hands…….But I lied!
What does he mean? He specifically said he was going after SAINT. FAMINE: BOONDOCK SAINT is NOT the first man I wanted to destroy. The person I wanted to destroy was someone whom people see as a thorn at my side. The reason I was the man I was before. But now……..Now things are different and it’s time for a change. I have realized that my destiny is not as a comedic, pathetic waste of skin. My destiny is to bring back the days of old where I would torture, torment and destroy. I have realized that this is who I really am. My thirst for blood has returned. And now it’s time to deal with the task at hand. The man I wanted to destroy and get rid of once and for all is this man……
Suddenly, something starts coming down from the rafters. It looks like a cross and someone is tied to it…..OH MY GOD! IS THAT?? IS THAT NICK RYAN?! As the cross makes it’s way down to the ring people take photos and can now see that it is in fact NICK RYAN. NICK is tied up with his half of the tag titles on his waist. NICK’S mouth is taped up and he is seen screaming but can’t be heard. FAMINE walks over and takes the tape off.
NICK: What the F*** are you doing FAMINE? FAMINE: NICK……You’re a smart man. Figure it out. For a while now, you and I have been running around here playing pranks on people. Pretending to be some goddamn five year olds. THAT IS NOT ME! I don’t like comedy…….I don’t like pranks……I came back for a reason……A purpose…….I am not now, nor will I ever be anyone’s sidekick. This partnership is officially over. And what I propose is this……..You……Me…..X-MAS XTREME…….Devil’s Playground match!
OH MY GOD! FAMINE has just challenged his own partner to a match at X-MAS XTREME. What the hell is going on tonight?
FAMINE: You come out here and think you can just challenge people to a match for gold you don’t deserve? What makes you think you’re better than me? What makes you think you can get a shot at the Universal Title before me?
NICK: Because I…… FAMINE: SHUT UP! Now, I don’t care if I AM in the X-MAS XTREME tournament for a shot at the World Title. I am going to destroy you one way or another. We face off, and the winner will get to keep both tag titles. And I think your best bet is to face me. Because if you don’t…..I will hunt you down. I will kill you, your brother, your cousin and your cat if you have one. So it is written…..So shall it be!
The lights go out and when they come back, FAMINE is gone and NICK is still tied to the cross as security and staff come out to help him get down. As they enter the ring, a bolt of lighting from the rafters comes down and hits the ring right near the cross and NICK. Security and staff fall back and get out of the ring. The crowd is in shock and so are the men standing outside the ring…..
So that's what happened last night. It felt SO FUCKING GOOD! The rush of adrenaline, the smell of fear. I could almost taste his blood. The Demon King has truly returned. And the sweetest part of all is that Nick never saw it coming. He thought everything was fantastic and that we would continue to go on with our lives as if nothing had changed. EVERYTHING CHANGED! I hope you heard me loud and clear last night Nicky boy. This thing between me and you has been brewing for a long time now. I was getting so sick of you I damn near puked each time I had to team up with you. Do you want to know the reason why I did that to you? I'll tell you. The world needs to know why this happened so here it is. You and I have been friends for a while now. Everyone knows this. But what they don't know is that you have a control issue. Everything has to go your way. Everything has to be Nick Ryans way. Not with me. Sure, I agreed to come back and take a run at the tag titles and become the new tag team people would come after because to be honest there is no one that can touch us. But now that this has been accomplished, our partnership and friendship is over. I realized that without you I am better off. Do you have any idea? Any clue as to how many people came to me, texted me, called me or emailed me about this shit? That's all I ever heard was "why are you with that loser?" "Why are you wasting your time? you're a former World Champion!" All of these questions and comments came from not only fans and other close friends but they came from XWF Superstars as well. And so here we are. After thinking it over and realizing they were right, I got rid of the dead weight. So we may be tag champions but we're no longer friends. I killed that friendship last night. I hate your guts Nick Ryan. I want you to know that right now. But if you think this is over I hope you're ready because on Monday Night I get to face your brother. Ohhhhhh the joy I will take in punishing him and making him bleed. And you know the best part about all this? There is not a goddamn thing you can do about it. I am going to make an example out of him and when it's all said and done, there might only be one Ryan brother in the XWF. Yes, that was a threat!
Now, on to Hunter. Hunter, you and I have also been friends for a long time now as well. But the friendship is dead and it was your brother that killed it. Admit it. You can't stand the son of a bitch either. I am going to do you a favor and destroy him. I will get rid of him from the XWF and from your life as well as mine for good. The world would be better off without him. You and I both know it. But let's focus on our match. You and I will go toe to toe for the first time ever. But this is no ordinary match. This match is not just any old match. This match is for a shot at the title I once held. The world title. And believe me, I am not coming to lose. Especially not after what you said. Did you not think I watch TV? I saw your little promo earlier today and out of everything you said.....All I focused on was this
Hunter: As if the Lord of the Ring didn't take enough out of me...next Monday night I have to face Famine in the first round of the Xmas Xtreme tournament.
Jodi: So I heard. Hunter: He's a sadistic fuck, Jodi. You saw what he did to Nick tonight. Jodi: Is that a bit of concern I hear in your voice? Concern for your brother, maybe? Hunter: I'm just saying the man is dangerous for the wrong reasons. He doesn't want to wrestle, he wants to destroy people's lives, Jodi.
Jodi: Now I'm starting to wonder if that's fear I sense in your voice, not concern for Nick.
Hunter sits up quickly, a bit offended by his wife's remarks. Water splashes over the side of the full tub and onto the floor.
Hunter: I've never feard another human being in my life, Jodi. Not one! But Famine of the Vile pushes the boundaries of a simple backstage attack to make a statement and a stunt that could kill someone! He's not a wrestler, he's a freak.
Jodi: You're in the XWF, Hunter. Look at the likes of Trent Gein or Spice One. Those two aren't exactly right in the head.
Hunter: Well maybe it's time someone put them in their place. This is a business of professionals. All of us are in this because it's what we know and love. It's what puts food on our tables and is the livelihood of everyone with a contract. Freaks like Famine need to check themselves or be put down.
It's touching. Really it is. To think that you're actually believing in your heart that you can beat me. To think that you're actually trying to be somewhat of a more sadistic Hunter is cute. But cute isn't enough. Bravery is something you are born with from day one. You said you're not afraid of me or anyone else. But I doubt that very much. You see Hunter, you and I both know you're lying through your teeth. You told that to your sweet looking wife so she wouldn't be afraid. You said so yourself. I hurt people. I'm not a wrestler. I'm a freak! Truer words have never been spoken. And I can promise you one thing Hunter. The truth is going to hurt. And it's going to hurt REALLY bad! I am going to punish you, hurt you, make you bleed internally AND externally. I may even break a few bones along the way. What I will do to you will be something that the fans won't want to see. At least those with weak stomachs. After i'm done with you, I will go into X-Mas Xtreme to face your brother and whoever is the winner of the tournament for the World Title. And then I will not only be the Tag Team champion but I will be the World Champion. And that would be the second time I hold two titles in this company. And you and your brother are more than welcome to try and stop me. When it's all said and done, your blood will spill at my hands and nothing will stop me from getting to the top of the mountain once again to rule as the one and ONLY Demon King. So rest well Hunter. Each night you pray, make sure that you tell your god that I will be sending you to him soon. No, I'm not going to kill you. But I will put your ass in a coma for a long time so that you and he can chat about why men.....no....GODS like me, are unstoppable, untouchable, and indestructible. So it is written......So shall it be!!!
