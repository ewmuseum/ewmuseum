Famine of the Vile, chapter 5: Who and What I Am (roleplay)

CHAPTER 5: WHO AND WHAT I AM 6:30am, about four and a half hours since Hadrian left his job and saved a beautiful girl named Christina. After he walked her home, he was being followed by someone. After confronting the man, it was revealed to Hadrian that this was in fact his biological father. But how could it be? He thought his father was dead. This is what his mother had told him when he was just a little boy. But somehow, here he was. Sitting across from him at a small diner drinking out of a flask. By this time, he has already proven he knows his mother, sister, and almost everyone in his immediate family in Gothenburg. But why now? Why did he show up and reveal his existence to Hadrian? These are all questions he will get the answers to soon enough.
Nikolaos: I bet you’re wondering where I’ve been all this time huh? Hadrian: Not really. Mother told me you were dead so it never crossed my mind. Although……. Nikolaos: Yes? Hadrian: Somehow, I’ve always had this weird feeling inside. Like there was something there and it was waiting for me.
Nikolaos: Well, that time is now. Hadrian, there is something you need to know. Because I think it’s time you found out who you really are. And I don’t mean that you were born here in California and then moved to Sweden. I mean, who you are and who you can become if you stick with me and let me train you.
Hadrian: Ok, I’ll bite. What am I a prince to an unknown country or something? Rich beyond my wildest dreams?
Nikolaos: You are no prince. But if you wanted, you could be as rich as you wanted to be. Let’s get out of here.
Hadrian: Ok. Nikolaos and Hadrian leave the diner and head out towards a beautiful 1963 black ford thunderbird convertible. A cherry of a car and just as shiny and sparkling as it was the day they took it off the assembly line and into the showroom. They drive off and head toward Black Chasm Cavern in Volcano California.
Hadrian: So where are we going? Nikolaos: Black Chasm Cavern Hadrian: It’s nice to know you’re alive and all but don’t you think a tour of an old cabin is a little ridiculous? I’m not a kid.
Nikolaos: I’m not taking you there for a tour. I’m taking you to find out who you are. Hadrian: In a cave? Nikolaos: The Black Chasm Cavern isn’t just for tourists. It’s where I live. Hadrian: Oooooook. They continue their drive which takes them across the Shenandoah Valley right before they come to a stop near the entrance to the Cavern. They get out of the car and walk inside. There is no one there as the tours don’t begin until 9am or so. They walk inside and continue down until the very end of the cave where the tours usually end before they have to turn back. It is at this end that there is a secret entrance. Hadrian can’t believe his eyes. He watches as his father opens the entrance to his home which is actually a giant boulder which he moves with his bare hands. Once they are inside, he closes it so no one knows that there is someone there or let alone home which no one has ever seen or known about.
Hadrian: Ok, first you appear in front of me after I saw you about a block away. Now you push aside a giant boulder without breaking a sweat. What the hell is going on?
Nikolaos: That’s why I wanted to bring you here. Son…..You’re half vampire. As am I. Hadrian looks at his father serious for a moment, but then he begins to laugh. Hadrian: Ok, sure. I’m half vampire. Right! What’s next? My uncle is a werewolf and my mother’s sister married Frankenstein?
Nikolaos: Why would I lie to you? I bring you here and show you where I live; I appear after so many years and just introduce myself as your father? Those aren’t coincidences or crazy things that just happened. Why do you think you’re stronger than some of those guys you beat up? Quicker, smarter, damn near untouchable? The anger deep inside that you keep hoping will go away never will. It’s who you are and who I am.
Hadrian: Well how come I don’t go out and suck blood every night? Why can I go out in the daylight and not get burned.
Nikolaos: First of all, those are all myths. A vampire can’t die by going out in the sunlight. Stakes, garlic, crosses and all that crap are just myths written in books. The truth is, we, as half vampires and half humans have all the strengths and none of the weaknesses. Like that movie Blade. Vampires, pure vampires are vulnerable to only one thing. Silver. Just like werewolves. So now you know, and you understand what you must do to become a man.
Hadrian: I AM a man. Nikolaos: No, you’re not. But you will be. I promise you that when I’m done, you will be more of a man than you think you are now.
And so, Hadrian and his father remain in the cave for the next several days, training and fighting one another. Cuts heal; bruises disappear and blood spills but dries up. This went on for so long that outside in the real world, life continued on. The manager of the underground fight club got himself a new fighter, and Christina continued to work and walk home alone fearing for her life since she had no one to walk her home and keep her safe. But that would change soon enough.
CHAPTER 6: NO LONGER A BOY It has been almost two weeks now and Hadrian Burke is nowhere to be found. Things have continued on like he never existed. Tonight though, they will see the new and improved Hadrian Burke. It’s about 11pm and the fights are being set. The manager goes into the locker room to see how his fighter is and make sure he is ready for tonight. But when he walks inside, he sees his fighter unconscious and bleeding.
Manager: What the fuck is going on around here? Out of nowhere, Hadrian appears. The manager jumps back, startled and damn near pissing his pants from the scare.
Manager: Hadrian? Where the fuck have you been kid? Hadrian: Training. Manager: Training? With the way you were winning fights why bother? Hadrian: Well, it was more than training. It was more of a revelation of sorts. Manager: So are you back? As you can see, my boy here is not going to fight tonight. Hadrian: Ok, I’ll fight tonight. But this is going to be the last fight ever. Manager: Come on kid what are you stupid? There’s money to be made here. You and me can be millionaires. I can set you up with some of the best fighters in the country.
Hadrian: Trust me, I don’t need you to get where I’m going. But tonight, I fight and I’m gone. Got it?
Manager: Ok kid, whatever you say. But you’ll change your mind. They always do. The manager smirks and then walks away. Hadrian stands over the unconscious fighter and then walks away. He heads upstairs and begins looking for Christina. He walks around for a bit before he sees her getting groped by a couple of drunken patrons waiting to place their bets for the fight. He walks up to them as she sees tries to keep their hands away.
Hadrian: Is there a problem here gentlemen? Last time I checked, you were here to place bets on fights, not mistreat the beautiful lady here.
Patron 1: Hey fuck you, you faggot! I’ll grab whatever the fuck I want and you can’t stop me.
Patron 2: Yeah what are you gonna do? As the second patron says this, he pulls out a knife. A notion that angers Hadrian to the point where he grabs the man by the neck and lifts him three feet in the air. He begins choking the man as the other gets up with a beer bottle and cracks it over the head of Hadrian. But it doesn’t do anything. Hadrian then grabs the other man by the shirt and flings him across the room like a rag doll. He drops the first man he is choking and walks toward Christina.
Hadrian: Are you ok? Christina: Is this going to be a regular thing with you? I get in trouble and you save me? Hadrian: Maybe. Depends on how many of these losers keep doing this to you. Christina: Where have you been? Hadrian: With my dad. I was finding out who I was. And now that I know, nothing will ever stop me.
Christina: I like your attitude. That’s the way you need to be. Are you still going to fight?
Hadrian: Tonight is my last fight. I wanted to ask you something. Christina: Ok? Hadrian: If I ask you to leave with me tonight, will you? I want to take you away from this place. Somewhere that’s safe and you never have to worry about being mugged or mistreated.
Christina: I barely know you Hadrian. How do I know you’re not like these other assholes? Hadrian: Well, does saving you twice count for anything? Christina: I guess it does. Hadrian: Look, I know we don’t know each other very well, but I can’t just leave and not take you with me. Otherwise I’ll just have to live here forever and trust me, this place sucks to much for me to be here. I know you don’t want to be here either.
Christina: I don’t know what it is about you, but I trust you. I just feel like this is right. Meet me at my place after your fight. I’ll go pack some things to take ok?
Hadrian: Ok. I’ll see you soon. Hadrian stands there as Christina walks away. She looks back one more time and smiles before leaving. Hadrian then heads toward the back where he sits down before his fight. He grabs a drink and sits back as the other fights go on. While he drinks and looks on at the other fighters, Nikolaos walks up to him.
Nikolaos: Hey son. What are you doing here? Bored? Hadrian: No. I just came for one last fight. Nikolaos: And the girl? Hadrian: How did you know? Nikolaos: Hadrian, I’m your father. I’ve been watching you for a very long time. Besides, I saw you talking to her and heard what you said.
Hadrian: Damn amplified hearing. If you’re thinking about giving me any type of advice forget it. I thank you for the training and for teaching me who I really am, but that’s as far as you go. I don’t need advice.
Nikolaos: I don’t doubt that son. I just want you to be careful. Power is a drug all its own. It can consume you if you don’t control it. But listen, if you ever need anything, just come and find me. You know where I live.
Hadrian: Don’t worry about me. But if I ever do need you, you’ll know I’m coming. Now if you’ll excuse me, my fight is next.
Nikolaos: No problem. I’d stick around but I have some business to attend to. Take care of yourself.
Hadrian and his father hug before he leaves. Hadrian looks at him walk away as he steps into the ring for his last fight.
CHAPTER 7: IS THIS LOVE THAT I’M FEELING? With one punch to the throat, the fight was over. Hadrian had won his last underground fight and made a killing. But that wasn’t enough. After the fight was over, he attacked everyone in the place and took all of their money. Now we’re not talking a couple of bucks here and there. We’re talking about several hundreds of thousands of dollars. Remember, these were no ordinary patrons. These were bookies and wealthy business men who loved this underground fighting business. And after it was said and done, everyone was alive (barely) but broke. The manager of the place got the worst of it though. He now had to pay back every single person in that place and the amount that was taken was nowhere near what he could afford. So naturally, he ran off. Disappeared and was never seen again. Hadrian did the same thing, except he was not alone. He and Christina left town and moved to the last place she thought she would ever see. Japan! Christina was nervous, yet excited at the same time. She could not believe that after so many years of living in that god forsaken hell hole, that she was going to be living in another country.
Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, US Airways flight 6841 to Tokyo, Japan is now boarding. Please be sure to have your tickets ready at the gate.
(In Japanese) 女性および紳士の東京、日本への米国の航空路飛行6841は今乗っている。 ゲートで準備ができたあなたの切符を持つために確実がありなさい. Hadrian: That’s our flight. Are you ready? Christina: I don’t think I’ve ever been this nervous in my life. But I think I’m ready for this.
Hadrian: Let’s do this then. I promise. Everything will be alright. They both head toward the gate and present their ticket before stepping on board the plane. As they walk down the aisle, they look for their seats when Hadrian sees a man who frequented the place he fought at. The man stands up and stops him.
Man: Mr. Vile? Hadrian: Yes? Man: My name is Yoshiro Matsuda. I saw you fight many times before you left. I am very impressed by your work. Why are you heading to Japan?
Hadrian: I just want to get away from it all. Start over and make a new life for myself and Christina.
Yoshiro: I see. Well, in that case, I may have some work for you once we arrive. Hadrian: Thanks but, I don’t think I’ll be doing any underground fighting anymore. Yoshiro: Good, because this isn’t underground fighting. This is a very respected and skilled sport.
Hadrian: Oh? Yoshiro: I am talking about wrestling. But I must warn you. It is very different than underground fighting. There is a lot of discipline and technique involved.
Hadrian: Wrestling huh? I tell you what Mr. Matsuda, you give me a business card or phone number and I will give you a call if I decide to try it.
Mr. Matsuda hands him a business card which is in both English and Japanese. They shake hands and Hadrian continues on to his seat. Once he sits down and gets comfortable, he looks at the card and starts to think.
Christina: Who was that guy? Hadrian: He was one of the clubs frequent members. Said he wanted to offer me a job wrestling in Japan.
Christina: Really? What did you say? Hadrian: I told him I’d think about it. I don’t know if I want to do that. I mean wrestling is so fake and everything is set up. Why would I waste my time with that when underground fighting paid more and let me do whatever I wanted?
Christina: Well, maybe because I want you to be safe? I want things to work out between us. I don’t just go running off to another country with someone I barely know. But I just feel like there is a connection between us Hadrian.
Hadrian: I……… Christina: You…….what? Hadrian: That’s just it Christina. I don’t know. I mean I’ve never felt this way about anyone. I never thought that I would meet anyone like you and yet here you are sitting next to me. I tell you what, I’ll go and see what the deal is with this guy and give it some thought ok?
Christina: Ok. And so as they sat comfortably in their seats, they began talking. They talked about their lives, growing up and what led them to this moment. The flight was about 10 hours so they had plenty of time to get to know each other. As they looked into each other’s eyes, they realized that this was definitely something special and different than anything they had known before. They slept for a couple of hours before the plane got ready to land. As they woke up, they looked out the window and saw the beauty that awaited them below. But what awaited them was more than they ever expected.
CHAPTER 8: THE SEEDS OF LOVE Ahhh Japan. A strange and yet beautiful country rich with history and culture. This was their new home. Neither had ever traveled so far before and it was exciting yet scary at the same time. After finding a local tour guide to help them out, they were able to get a place to live. The place was already furnished so there was no need to buy anything other than food. It was going to take some getting used to as the usual American food was nowhere to be seen except for the local American fast food places. But they managed and adjusted. It has already been a week since they arrived and Hadrian finally found some time to visit Mr. Matsuda. He decided to see what this was all about and at least give it a chance. The cab he hailed down drove him to the Tokyo Dome. As he stepped out of the cab, he looked up and saw a giant poster for Big Japan Wrestling. This caught his eye and peaked his interest. Now all that was left was for him to go inside and see what this wrestling business was all about. As he stepped inside, he showed the security guard the card that Mr. Matsuda had given him and the guard escorted him to Mr. Matsuda’s office. Inside, the office was covered in plaques, trophies and awards.
Yoshiro: Mr. Vile. I’m glad you could make it. Please come, sit. Hadrian: You can call me Hadrian Mr. Matsuda. That’s my real name. Yoshiro: Hadrian huh? Swedish? Hadrian: How did you know? Yoshiro: I had a good friend from Sweden named Hadrian. He and I grew up together. His father and mine were both in the military and served side by side. But that was ages ago. Let’s get down to business. I wanted you to come here and see what I have to offer you. I know you’re used to fights with no rules. No referee, no bell. But this sport, is sacred. Filled with passion and intensity. No one pulls punches here either. But the difference is, that we all make a good living but keep it safe. You see Hadrian, the fans, they love it. They eat it up. And so those who choose to get into this business, they know that it’s more than just the stardom or the money. It’s about honor. Never forget that.
Hadrian: But isn’t it all scripted and fake? Yoshiro: No. This isn’t America. We don’t entertain you with predictable outcomes. We don’t bore the fans with something they know is going to happen. Here, while we do have rules in place, we do not determine the outcome of our matches. Now, why don’t you come with me so I can show you around.
Mr. Matsuda walks with Hadrian and they continue to talk as he shows him around the arena. He introduces Hadrian to the likes of Jushin Liger, Antonio Inoki and The Great Sasuke. All men who would become legends in their own right. Hadrian looked at them in awe. He was star struck without knowing who they were. Massive, giant men who look like they could flip a car over without breaking a sweat. It is safe to say that Hadrian fell in love that day. He and Mr. Matsuda agreed to set him up with a trainer and even gave him a place to stay rent free. Two days later, he and Christina moved in and he began to train. But he would soon realize that this was no easy feat. There were days that he could barely walk or move. Sure, he was half human and half vampire, but even with the power he possessed, he was still half human. And that was the part that was damn near worn out each day. Christina would care for him and be with him as he trained since she was not working and didn’t know Japanese. He still had a ways to go but not once did he complain about the pain or the brutal poundings he received at the hands of his trainer. This would continue for several months until he was good enough to fight in his first match.
Yoshiro: Well, this is it Hadrian. Tonight, you will be introduced to the Japanese audience as Famine of the Vile! Have you thought of a gimmick?
Hadrian: A gimmick? Yoshiro: Sure. Everyone does it. But the difference between here and America is that here, your gimmick is who you really are. Are you a daredevil? Are you the fan loving favorite? Or are you someone that will be discarded in a week?
Hadrian: I know who I am. I can’t be anything other than that. Yoshiro: Then I don’t have anything to teach you. You are ready my friend. Good luck out there.
Hadrian: Arigatou. Yoshiro: I see you learned a bit of Japanese huh? Hadrian: Just a little. It will take some time to get better. And so, Hadrian went on to face Tanaka who was one of BJW’s best wrestlers. He fought hard, he did things he never thought possible. At the end of the night, he was able to come up with a victory but just barely. The crowd was still a bit unsure about him but still cheered and clapped since he showed that not only did he have respect for his opponent, but respect for the sport. He would later go home and celebrate with Christina. He was so excited he almost scared her.
Christina: I am so proud of you! I knew you could do this. Hadrian: If it wasn’t for you I don’t think I could have done it. Thank you so much for being there for me.
Christina: You’re welcome. Out of nowhere, she kisses him. He leans back and stops for a second. Christina: I’m sorry. I shouldn’t have done that. Hadrian: No, it’s ok. It’s just that……I’ve never kissed a girl before. Christina: Really? With those good looks and charm I would think you’ve kissed a hundred girls.
Hadrian: You’re just saying that. Christina: I mean it. I think you’re very sexy. Hadrian: I…….I think I love you. Christina’s face went from happy to shocked. She had no idea what to say to that. But then, she walked up to Hadrian and put her arms around him. She kissed him again and he kissed her back this time.
Christina: I think I love you too. They continues to kiss and embrace each other. Hadrian turned the lights off and carried her to the bed. There he slowly began to undress her and kiss her gently on her neck and chest. She removed his shirt and they continued to kiss. With the wind blowing in through the window and the stars shining brightly in the sky, they made love until they could no longer continue. And as the sun came up, the seeds of their love had begun to plant themselves. In his mind, Hadrian felt that this is exactly what he had always wanted. A family. A family to call his own. A family that would never break apart or destroy each other. This was perfect. And nothing was going to change that. But life doesn’t always turn out the way we want. Sometimes, the road takes us to places we never thought we would end up. This is yet another lesson he would learn in time.
TEST EIGHT TEN FOUR THE VILE MANIFESTO Well, here we are once again. You would think that after all these years, some people would learn how to come across as intelligent. Well, that hasn’t happened this week. Yes, I mean you Centurion. You are one of those people who really piss me off because you run your mouth without thinking. You chit chat with your prophecy buddies and try to make me out to be nothing more than a has been or an old dog who needs to be put to sleep or shot like old yeller. But that couldn’t be further from the truth. You see Centurion, for all your talk and cockiness; you seem to forget one thing. I am Famine of the Vile. I’m sure that means nothing to you, and I could care less. But I need to say that because for the last five years, I have been in the XWF and accomplished many things. My departures have not been for long, but my returns are what people are always talking about. Just like they are talking about my return at Bad Medicine. Remember that? I’m sure you do seeing as how I took both you and Daniel Malcolm out. I didn’t do it to TRY to send a message. I did exactly what I wanted. I took you both out and let the world know that I was back with a vengeance. You’re right; I could have announced it in a better or maybe different way. But what better way to do it than to take you both out? What did you do? Nothing! You tried to take me out and it failed. Now, my intention was not to help you win that match because you had clearly lost it. It just so happened that after I took you out, you landed on top of Malcolm and won. Not that it matters because I can take either one of you out whenever I want. This brings me to correction number two. I know that we faced each other a very long time ago. And you can go on all day and night talking about you being the one who tossed me and Dan Fierce around that ring, but we all saw the truth. Now you’re saying that Steve Jason wasn’t at his best. That the only reason I will be there is because people think I’m scary and they should automatically put me in? I have held more titles that you have ever seen in your entire career. Shame? Yes! So that whole title doesn’t make the man business is bullshit. But, that’s not the point here. The point here Cent, is that if you think I am a pain in the ass, and you want to finally shut me up, I welcome you to step up and get in my face. Thursday is going to be my official in ring return. And in that return, you will see a Famine like you’ve never seen before. You may think that you’ve got this in the bag but it’s not that easy. Because Bigg Rigg will not only carry you in the match, but when it’s over…..He’s going to literally carry your ass out of that ring and to the back for medical attention. Next week, he and I face off in the trust your gut tournament. Once he is done with Hunter Ryan, he thinks he will come for me next and take me out. A lot of people are betting on me to lose. But if there is one thing about Famine of the Vile that no one has learned is that you can NEVER count me out before the fight has even started. Forget those who try to run in packs, it’s pointless and worthless to me now. I am not going to join any stables or form any tag teams. I have come back for me and only me. This time around, the entire XWF is on notice. I don’t care who you are, or where you come from. I don’t care how strong you are or what titles you’ve held in the past. All that means nothing. Because once we step inside that ring, you are stepping into my world. And that world is dark……..cold……..and void of any emotion and feeling. Come and play with the devil. I want to see if you have the balls to stand face to face with him and dance! See you all in the ring!
