XWF (Massacre and Anarchy, 2008–10) — Roleplay

The Demon King: Lord of the Ring promo, part 1 (roleplay)

c. 27 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Demon King The scene opens to a Boondock Saint promo that aired last week. Famine of the Vile is sitting down watching just one specific part over and over again.
You were at the top of your game last year. You were one of the darkest and most imposing figures in this company. The Black Order would’ve feared you if they were around. The ring was your Devil’s Playground. You and Scott Young were two of the most dangerous and feared men in the history of the XWF. You were right at the top with me, but look at you now. You aren’t the dark and scary figure that you once were. I’ve seen those campaign ads and you look like you have cleaned up yourself. You have dropped the dark and scary persona for a new clean cut look. You may be smiling inside, but I know that you are really dead inside. You wish you could cut loose and by the man that you once were. I don’t see the Famine of the Vile that once terrorized the XWF. I see half a man and that man is dead inside. I never thought you would stoop so low as to sell out your beliefs for a stupid campaign. The real Famine is dead and I will kill the fake shell of a man that you once this Monday.
The real Famine is dead....... The real Famine is dead........ The real Famine is dead........ Those words continued to echo through my head. I could hear nothing else BUT that over and over and over again. Did Boondock Saint hit a nerve? Did he actually manage to strike a point no one else cared about? A point I myself had forgotten about? I changed my ways because I felt like it was time for me to be something different. Something more. I had no one left to torture. I was done with the darkness and there was no one good enough to challenge me. But I guess I was wrong. Boondock Saint. A man I had never in my life faced off against, made a lot of sense. I was no longer the man I used to be. And now I sit here wondering where I went wrong. How did I get to this point? How did I let myself become.......normal?
THREE DAYS BEFORE MASSACRE The scene opens to Famine of the Vile sitting down having a beer while Nick sits down having a vanilla bean frappuccino.
Famine: You know something bro. I've been thinking. Boondock Saint said something about me in his promo that really got me to thinking.
Nick: What's that? Famine: He said I was no longer the man I once was. That this isn't me and that the darkness I once claimed to have within me made me a feared and respected man. I've done some thinking and I think he might be right
Nick: What? What are you talking about man? You can't go back to that. Famine: Why not? Nick: Because we're a team. And right now we've got a shot at winning the Tag Titles at Lord of the Ring. I mean do you seriously think that any of those other two teams are going to beat us? Think about it man. We're the Initiative. You're Famine of the fucking Vile for gods sake. You've been a World Champion, A Tag Champion and an Xtreme Champion. You're also one of the most known faces on Massacre since 2006.
Famine: You don't understand me man. Yeah we're a team but you don't know what i've been through when it comes to this business, my dark side and the loss of a lot of friends and people I love. And maybe he's right. Maybe I am nothing more than a shell of the man I used to be. I looked at myself in the mirror every day and was fine. I was happy, I was a live again. But after hearing him say that I looked in the mirror once again and I saw something I never saw before.
Nick: And that was? Famine: Nothing. I didn't see myself, I didn't see my dark side. I saw nothing. I don't know why but I saw nothing at all and that actually scared me for the first time in my life. And I am a man who fears nothing and no one. So what does that tell you.
Nick: That you're a vampire which explains why you didn't see yourself? Famine: I'm being serious asshole. I don't know what this means but I'm gonna leave for a few days and think things over. You just get ready for your match monday. I'll be there to back you up so don't worry.
Nick: Ok man, just do what you gotta do Famine: I will. Famine gets up and walks away from Nick as the scene fades with the camera zooming in on Nick's facial expression which does not seem to be a happy one.
A FEW DAYS LATER The scene now opens to Famine of the Vile sitting outside of his car in front of an abandoned graveyard. He is drinking and looking around at some headstones which have crumbled and broken from vandalism or from being unkept.
Famine: What the hell and I doing here? Why do I continue to drive around but stop at this place? I try to stay away from all of this because I know what I am capable of. I know what I can do and a part of me does not want to go back to that. But then another part of me does want it. It wants the carnage and blood. The sacrifices. The destruction. Nick and I have worked hard to get to this point and become the Tag Team champions. I don't want to let him down. He's my friend. But the part of me that wants nothing but death and destruction wants me to just walk away and leave it all behind. I don't know what to do right now. I could walk away. Say fuck it and just disappear like I did a year ago. I could fade into nothingness and no one would care after a week. I guess I have some thinking to do.
Famine then hears his cell phone ring. He looks on the caller ID and it's none other than the man whom he was partners with and one of his closest friends. Scott Young!
Famine: Why is it that every time I need help with something or have a problem you show up?
Scott: I'm your best friend. We were partners for a long time bro. It's like I can feel something going on. What is it this time?
Famine: Conflict Scott: Conflict? Famine: It's a long story but lets just say that I am stuck between a rock and a hard place when it comes to being me.
Scott: You having women issues again? Famine: Not the time to joke around man. Look, basically what happened is I had a match against Bigg Rigg and Boondock Saint with Nick Ryan as my partner.
Scott: Who? Famine: Nick Ryan. Remember him? Well, he used to go by Nick Nitro. Scott: Oh that guy? Why the fuck would you partner with him? Famine: Why did I partner with you? Scott: Good point. So what's up? Famine: Boondock said something that really got me to thinking man. He said I was no longer the man I once was. That I cleaned myself up for a new campaign and that the Famine I once was is dead.
Scott: Well, he's right. Famine: What? Scott: Let's cut the shit man. I mean look at yourself. You're nothing nowadays. I may not be in the company anymore but I keep up with it. Look man, I've seen what you've become and it's disgusting to me. To think that one of THE most influential, deadly, sadistic and powerful sons of bitches in the company has been reduced to this is sickening to me. Where the hell is the Famine of the Vile that I knew? The one that helped me get my brother back? Kill Mr. Payment? Go up against those Russians? When that man comes back let me know.
Famine: I don't know if I want to do that anymore Scott. I know what I am capable of. I know the destruction I can cause and that scares me.
Scott: YOU? Famine of the fucking Vile scared? Famine: I'm scared that I may seriously hurt someone again. I've already killed before and I don't want that on my conscience anymore.
Scott: You're pathetic. Famine of the Vile is a killer! Do you understand me? He is the most sadistic, ruthless, destructive son of a bitch this sport has ever seen. He cares for no one and nothing. The very same guy that I was proud to be partners with. The same man that held championship gold with me and on his own in more than one occassion. You think about that and then get back to me. When THAT Famine of the Vile shows up, tell him he's got my number....click.
Famine puts his phone away and sits there looking at the graveyard. The scene then fades to black......
WORDS FROM THE VILE ONE So here we are. Another year, another Lord of the Ring tournament. But this time i'm not in it. I am going to be at the pay per view but not as a participant in the main event. I will be in a tag team match with Nick against two other teams. My success with teams has only been good when my partner and I see eye to eye on everything. But after this past week I don't know if that's going to be the case. I thought that this would be the ultimate team up. That Nick and I were going to be invincible in this division. But thanks to Boondock Saint, I am now left wondering what my true path is. What my purpose for being in this business truly is. When I first debuted, it was without a shadow of a doubt the most phenomenal debut anyone had ever seen. From that moment on, I went on to become one of the most dangerous men in this company today. Although I never got to face the likes of Lee Stone or Saint himself in one on one competition, I did go up against some of the best in my time here. But as I sit here now, I wonder what would have happened should I have stayed on the road I was on. Would I have ultimately proven my dominance and become the Universal Champion? Or would I have suffered defeat and utter humiliation and soon after disappear into nothing? Now there are more questions than answers. I thought I had my life all figured out. That this sarcastic yet dangerous son of a bitch would be able to continue his dominance without the darkness. But the last couple of days has truly left me wondering. What do I do? Who do I become now? Should I continue on the path of good and bury my demons forever? Or should I return to the dark path I once walked? It's a question that many men ask themselves on a daily basis. Boondock Saint made some great points. And the entire time, all I thought was that he would simply make comments and remarks about the Initiative. He took it a different route and for the first time in a long time, I am left without a road to walk on. I am standing in the middle of a four way intersection trying to decide which way to go. Thanks a lot Saint. I appreciate that.
But aside from all that, let me get something off my chest because I feel it needs to be mentioned. I am upset......No, no. I'm pissed off. Because this entire week, i've been getting calls and people saying that Nick did this or Nick did that. I was the motherfucker that pinned Boondock Saint's shoulders to the mat. I was the motherfucker who won the match and yet Nick is getting all the praise and he's running around like he won the fucking superbowl. Look Nick, you're my boy and I respect you for being in this business as long as you have. I also respect the fact that you have done a lot of different things. I will not deny the fact that you have passion and drive. But don't over step your boundaries man. You and I both know I won that match, not you. And yes, it's killing me that for once in my life i'm not the center of attention. But that's ok, because at Lord of the Ring, when we go into that match for the belts. I WILL be the center of attention. I am going to destroy, decimate and annihilate the other two teams. And I welcome them to step up and take a shot at me. In my wake, I will leave a path of blood and broken bones the likes of which even the Black Order never saw. These other four men will see before them a wrestling god. A man who knows no fear and no pain. I will go out there and do what I do best and that is destroy, attack and sacrifice. I am tired of being this comedic and happy go lucky guy people take as a joke. I am tired of people laughing. I want them to fear me. I want them to know that the Famine of the Vile of old is still here. That he hasn't left and never will. I want them to see me for who I really am. And at Lord of the Ring that is exactly what I will do. Jaymz Dante, Sam Hain, QC Thug, Adrian Adonis. You all have a date with destiny. You are about to face two of the most dominant athletes in the tag team division the likes of which you have never seen. Famine of the Vile has a solid record of tag team competition and so does Nick Ryan. The Initiative is going to enter the arena as challengers and walk out as champions. As a great man once said, Beat us if you can. Survive if we let you. And depending on my mood, I may NOT let you!!
