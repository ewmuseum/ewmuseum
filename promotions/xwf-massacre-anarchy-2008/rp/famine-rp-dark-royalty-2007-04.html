Famine of the Vile: an upset over the Universal Champion (roleplay)

c. 3 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In one of the biggest upsets since Bush winning the presidency for a second term. Famine of the Vile was victorious against the Universal Champion, Dynamic Dynamite. The crowd was in shock. Boos all over the arena were deafening. When his hand was raised, he could not believe it. But it wasn’t completely because of him. Archangel thought it would be funny or cute to come to the ring and try to attack Famine with a steel chair. Unfortunately for him, it backfired. He ended up whacking Dynamite and taking him out of action, allowing Famine to get the pin. Aidan Collins also made an unexpected appearance (only unexpected to some) and got his revenge against DD. And so that night has come and gone and the XWF and fans all over the world are still buzzing about it. What will happen next?
IN THE LOCKER ROOM The scene opens to moments after the show is off the air. Famine of the Vile is seen celebrating with his son and the rest of the SOA. They are shouting and drinking champagne.
Famine: Wooooooo! Un-fucking-believable! I did it! Helmut: Congratulations heir Famine. Brad: Yeah man, good work. SB: That was amazing pops. Still got a kick in your step eh? Famine: I’m gonna slap you if you keep on with those old guy jokes. SB: Just messin with ya. Congrats pops. I knew you could do it. Brad: What now? Famine: Back to Anarchy. I need an answer to my question from Collins. Helmut: Let us go then. SB: Yeah let’s beat it. As they get ready to walk out, Dynamic Dynamite storms in and gets in the face of Famine. Famine: Woah, easy there killer. Looks like you took a beating out there kid. DD: You know that was bullshit man. If it wasn’t for Malcolm hitting my ass with that chair I would have beaten your ass.
Famine: Actually genius, you WOULD have beaten me, had you not gotten the ref knocked out by spinning me around and making me hit him with my boot. So THAT was your bad, not mine. I did what I had to do just like I said. I took the fight to you regardless of how I won the match.
DD: You know what? I should kick the shit out of you right here right now. Famine: I don’t think you’re thinking clearly there champ. Why don’t you have some champagne, relax and think about next week? As for me, I’m on my way to a party. I’ll be seeing ya.
Famine and the SOA walk past Dynamite and head out to the parking lot. As they walk out, Famine slips on the recently buffed tile floor. He falls back and hits his head. SB and the guys pick him up immediately.
SB: Pops, you ok? Famine: Yeah, I’m good. I’ve taken worse blows to the head before. SB: Good, don’t need you getting Alzheimer’s or nothing like that. Famine: No it’s ok. Now lets get outta here. Brad: Where are we going anyway? Famine shakes his head for a moment trying to shake out the cobwebs. Famine: We need to stop at my secret layer. Helmut: You have a secret layer? SB: When the hell did you get a secret layer? Famine: I’ve always had one. Now lets go! The SOA walk behind their leader looking confused. Famine has a secret lair? Three hours later, they show up at a Lowes Home Improvement Store and get out of their limo. Famine stands in front of the store, looking at it and smiling.
SB: Uhhhh, pops, why are we here? Famine: I told you. This is my secret layer. Helmut: Heir Famine, are you sure you feel ok? Famine: Yes. Why do you keep asking? SB: Because old man, this is a Lowes! Famine: What? This isn’t Lowes, can’t you see the damn entrance to the lair right there? Does it look like a Lowes? I doubt it. Now lets go!
The SOA walk inside Lowes right behind their leader wondering if he really is ok. They go into the lumber isle as Famine walks up to a clerk.
Famine: You there, I need you to get my other car from the back. I also need a change of clothes.
Clerk: Sir? Famine: Did you not hear me? I need my other car and a change of clothes. Now get moving or else!
Clerk: Uhhhh, one moment sir. The clerk walks up to a manager who is nearby and tells him what is going on. The clerk and the manager return.
Manager: Sir, is there a problem here? Famine: The only problem here is that I am waiting for this moron to get my car, and a fresh pair of clothing. It shouldn’t be that hard of a task.
Manager: Well sir, I run this store and I don’t believe we have your car or clothes. If you’re not going to make a purchase then I’m afraid you’ll have to leave.
Famine: Oh, is that how it is? After all the things I’ve done for you, this is how you treat me? I let you live in my lair, I feed you and I make sure your family in Guatemala is taken care of you bastard! Insubordination will not be tolerated!
Famine picks up the manager and flings him across the aisle. He lands on a stack of plywood. As he falls off, they begin to knock about other boards and equipment. Suddenly a large piece of a 2x4 falls and bonks Famine of the head. Famine falls down as the SOA drag him out of the way to prevent anything else from falling on him.
Famine: FUCK! That hurt! What the hell are we doing at Lowes? SB: You mean you have no idea how we got here? Famine: No. The last thing I remember is slipping on that damn floor at the arena. Helmut: Well heir Famine, apparently you…… Soul Bearer cuts him off. SB: You thought this was the next arena that we were supposed to be in. That guy over there, you thought he was Honkey.
Famine: Oh ok, well then lets get the hell out of here and head for the hotel. We have a plane to catch in a few hours.
Famine begins to walk away as Helmut looks at Soul Bearer. Helmut: Why did you stop me from telling him. SB: Because this way funny as hell. I’m not gonna pass up an opportunity to see this happen. Now don’t tell him anything.
Famine: You guys coming or what? SB\Helmut\Pierce:Yeah The SOA now leave the Lowes store and get back on the limo heading to their hotel. The next destination is Washington DC. The site for next week’s Massacre. But first, they will be making a quick stop at the RBC Center in Raleigh North Carolina. Anarchy is tomorrow night and Famine has something and someone he would like to address once again.

SB: Uhhhh, pops, why are we here? Famine: I told you. This is my secret layer. Man it feels good to be here. I haven’t been here in a while. I’m sure my workers are still at it. With the tortures and beatings and stuff.
Helmut: Heir Famine, are you sure you feel ok? Famine: Yes. Why do you keep asking? SB: Because old man, this is a Lowes! Famine: What? This isn’t Lowes, can’t you see the damn entrance to the lair right there? Does it look like a Lowes? I doubt it. Now lets go!
The SOA follows Famine inside his location. While the SOA is confused, Famine sees his henchmen working on dead bodies and torturing many people. He stops one of them and makes his demands.
Famine: You there, I need you to get my other car from the back. I also need a change of clothes.
Henchman: I’m sorry? Famine: Did you not hear me? I need my other car and a change of clothes. Now get moving or else!
Henchman: I’m afraid I cannot do that. You will have to wait here a moment. The henchman walks up to someone who seems to be in charge. He looks like he heads the operations here. He walks back to Famine with the henchman.
Head of Operations: Famine of the Vile, is there a problem here? Famine: The only problem here is that I am waiting for this moron to get my car, and a fresh pair of clothing. It shouldn’t be that hard of a task.
Head of Operations: Well sir, there is going to be a problem with that. You see, I run this place now. You’ve been gone for too long and I have taken command. I don’t believe we have your car or clothes anymore. Anything that you left here now belongs to me!
Famine: Oh, is that how it is? After all the things I’ve done for you, this is how you treat me? I let you live in my lair, I feed you and I make sure your family in Guatemala is taken care of you bastard! Insubordination will not be tolerated!
Famine picks him up by his shirt and flings him across the room. He lands on a stack of beakers and other scientific things on a large table. As he falls off, things above him begin to knock about. Boxes and equipment along with a few heavier items. Suddenly a medium sized metal canister falls and bonks Famine of the head. Famine falls down and everything goes black. He comes to, to see the SOA hovering over him trying to pick him up. He looks around and sees the Lowes manager on the floor covered in plywood.
Famine: FUCK! That hurt! What the hell are we doing at Lowes? SB: You mean you have no idea how we got here? Famine: No. The last thing I remember is slipping on that damn floor at the arena. A MASSACRE IT WILL BE! Well, what a night it was. I walked out to the arena, I walked into the ring, and I proved to Dynamic Dynamite that I am indeed a tough son of a bitch! I took him to the limit and he took me as well. We gave each other a fight like never before. Now, the way the match ended is not how I wanted it to go. But I will take a win any way I can get it. Archangel had to be a nosy fuck and get in the match. Some people just don’t know how to let things go I tell you. And so thanks to that moron, I beat the Universal Champion. Yes you heard it correctly and if you saw Massacre then you saw it happen and so there is no need for that look of shock on your face. The festivities were huge but the win was even bigger. But now, after coming off of one of the biggest wins in my career here in the XWF. They have me going up against two of the biggest losers in the company today! Why? To make an example out of them? Because that is exactly what I will do.
Honkey Lighthouse and Hawaiian Hardhead. Why in the hell would you two even agree to this match? Because it’s two on one? Do you really think that the two of you are going to beat me? this may have been before your time here Hardhead, but Honkey will tell you. I have taken on four men in a gauntlet match and won. I took each and every single one of those guys to their limit and held on to my title. Which is the same title I beat you for isn’t it Honkey. Yes, yes if memory serves me correctly, I took you and beat the hell out of you for that very same TV title. And every since that defeat you have never held a title again. A shame isn’t it. Impact to Massacre and you’re still the same loser. Hardhead, my advice to you is this. Take out your own partner and go one on one with me. Otherwise it’s just suicide. Because no matter how hard you try, I will take you both down and out and make you remember for as long as you live, that I was just too damn good for you to stop.
I mean honestly. Look at me. DD said that I was arrogant and cocky like him….like him!? I’m nothing like him. On the other hand I can now go around and say I’m better than him. I mean this is the Universal Champion, the king of the jungle if you want to call him that. But guess what. Unfortunately for him, there’s a new king in this jungle now! And after Massacre, no one will ever doubt that. I look forward to this coming Monday. It will give me a chance to take it easy and have some fun. But don’t be surprised if I make you bleed or break every bone in your body. It’s just what I do. Nothing changes, but things do get a hell of a lot better. No matter what strategies you come up with to try and beat me, they won’t work. When the night is over, Famine of the Vile will once again be the winner. You two better pray I go easy on you. Piss me off and you may very well see yourselves at the end of a Devil’s Spike. Or hell, I could go as far as to bury you two pussies together like the lovely couple you are. Keep your eyes open!
