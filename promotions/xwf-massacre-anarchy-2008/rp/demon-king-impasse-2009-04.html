The Demon King: Impasse, A Matter of Opinion, Deadtime Stories (roleplay)

The Demon King I believe that tonight, Chris Page will come to know what he has gotten himself into. Tonight I state the truth. Chris Page has reached an Impasse. Now I am sure that his feeble mind will not understand what that means due to the many dead brain cells still floating around in there, so I will explain. An impasse is a position or situation from which there is no escape. And I find myself in a good position. Because tonight, I opened my eyes and saw what will happen to Chris Page. I envisioned his defeat at my hands. I envisioned the bell ringing and the ref holding my hand high above me as they name me the new XWF World Champion. Chris, I know you must be saying to yourself that you have this all in the bag and that there is no way in hell this old man is going to beat you. I know you. We've fought for many years now and I know how you think. You are a cocky and arrogant fuck who thinks extremely high of himself. Now don't get me wrong. This is by no means the pot calling the kettle black. This is merely to point out that unlike you Chris. I am no longer focusing on the past. A place you love to live in. The one thing I have noticed about you Chris is that no matter how many things you do in the present. You always refer to the past. To those glory days that will never come again. I could sit here and say that I am going to kill you because you screwed me out of the World Title in WGWF. Remember? Oh i'm sure you do since you were running the place while Adam was on leave. I pinned Jason Creed to the mat. I won the World Title for a second time, and you allowed Mic Ferrari to come out and screw me out of that victory. 30 seconds Chris. That's how long I held that title. But you know what? That doesn't matter to me. Because when all was said and done, I proved to the world that I still had what it took to become the World Champion. And tomorrow, I set out to prove that once again. It's not very often that I get these title shots. And it's also not very often that I submerge myself within my conscience and soul and focus on the task at hand. But this time I will. Because the many times I have faced you, i've had my guard down. I've thought to myself that I would be walking out of there the winner when in reality I never opened my eyes to the truth. But this time Chris. This time you and I will dance the dance one more time. And this time I promise you the outcome will be different. Not because I say so. Not because I know the future. But because deep down inside, you know that this is not the man you faced before. You've talked a good game and tried to convince yourself that I am nothing. But we both know the truth. That my time has come. My frame of mind is like that of a boxer. I am ready for the fight and there is nothing I see other than your face. There is no one else in this world right now except you and I Chris. That's how focused I am. That the only thing I see is your face. My determination and will shall take me to a higher plateau. I will climb beyond the greatness that is Famine of the Vile. I will leap beyond the ego that is Chris Page. And I will soar above the competition to once again claim what will once again be my stepping stone to greatness. Tonight I declare that Hadrian Burke will once again be respected and admired by those who see him as a competitor and not an old man trying to live out his last days at the top of the mountain. They will all see me as the man who stood in the ring, gave everything he had and overcame the odds. I'm sure that to you this is all nothing but drivel and nonsense but to me it is everything. Because you see Chris. That's how a real winner does it. I have found a way to channel my anger and focus it all into the power that will be unleashed on you tomorrow night in my playground. Once you step inside, I will see your true nature. I will get to look at the real Chris Page in the eyes and see the fear he hides from everyone. Because your act is nothing but a facade. You try to bully, intimidate and make people respect you. I am one man Chris, that you do not GET respect from......You EARN IT! And tomorrow night, I WILL make you earn my respect. I will show you that nothing is free in this world. You have to earn everything. I hope you rest well. Because after tomorrow night, you may never sleep the same way again!
A MATTER OF OPINION I've gone over this a thousand times and some how, I can't seem to understand it. Why is it that every time I face a man of this calibre and stature they result to the godforsaken, boring as fuck, tired old recaps of history? Tomorrow I am going to show you and the world another Famine of the Vile. They have been with me through my many changes. This is one more. This is the one they never saw coming, and neither did you. I have waited for this moment for quite some time. And I do find it rather interesting that the man i've faced countless times for a world title. Is the same man I have to face.....For a world title. And contrary to what you think, I'm not on my last leg boy. I may have been in this business over twenty seven years, but in those twenty seven years, I have learned, grown and faced a multitude of things you will never see, feel nor understand. You think your life is all flowers and sunshine. It must be nice to live in that world. Getting high all the time and sneak attacking people. Droning on and on about things no one cares about anymore. Those are things only a man like you would do. It's men like you who can't tell the different between reality and fantasy. I'm not sure if you were high when you recorded that second promo but seriously man, get a grip on shit before you open your mouth. I mean what the hell are you talking about? Side Story? Nothing to do with anything going on in the XWF currently? What?! I don't get it Chris. But it's ok. I understand the drugs are affecting your state of mind. It's normal to see someone go through this when they are as high as you are. All this talk about cutting promos coming from a guy who did nothing but get old tapes of our matches and cut them together to MAKE his promo. Good one Chris. I mean Spice was always high but that son of a bitch knew the difference between reality and fantasy. He knew how to go out there and get the job done. By himself I might add. You on the other hand have always had that problem going for you. Always needing help, always looking for someone to sucker into doing what you want them to do. It's sad being so lonely isn't it Chris. Daddy not love you enough? Mommy not breast feed? Which one was it? Or was it both? Not that it matters to me, but I see that there is something wrong with you. Drugs or no drugs. But you know what Chris, it's ok if you don't understand my promos. My knowledge is too much for people like you sometimes. My artistic works are not meant for everyone. Which is why THAT makes ME better than YOU!!! Creativity, originality, two things you lack. No one is perfect, but you are far from better than me when you cut half assed promo's like those two. I sat here and spoke to you like a man should speak. Just because i'm an ugly son of a bitch who loves to torture folks, doesn't mean i'm not educated. I mean look at Rick Foley. The guy got his brains beat out. Thrown from the top of a steel cage and had one of his ears cut off almost. And he's written books and all kind of shit. Don't judge a book by it's cover or someone will slap you with it. And it really hurts if it's a hardback edition. Chris. I hope you're ready for the fight you've been waiting for. Because it's coming. And whether you want to believe it or not, it's all going to come down to those fateful three seconds. And they will be the longest three seconds of your life. When the smoke clears and all is said and done. I will once again be the XWF World Champion as they carry you to the back wondering what the fuck just happened!
