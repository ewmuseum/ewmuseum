WWWCW On Your Screen, The Beginning

8 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"On Your Screen, The Beginning" (The WWWCW logo covers the screen, then it fades to the OYS logo. The camera then cuts to a camera set up in the top of the arena giving a wide angle shot of the whole arena. All 19,884 fans are going crazy. The Camera then cuts to a picture of the ringside announcers Randal Cruize and D.A. Johnson.) Randal Cruize: Hello ladies and gentlemen and welcome to beatiful Murray Kentucky. We're here at the Murray State University Event Center bringing you the second ever Pay Per View in WWWCW history, "On Your Screen, The Beginning"! D.A. Johnson: Randal I think you're mistaken this is the first Pay Per View. Randal Cruize: Well maybe you need to quit thinking, this is the second Pay Per View. The first Pay Per View was a Royal Rumble. Afterwards the WWWCW shutdown. D.A. Johnson: Yeah, whatever. (Fireworks go off along the aisle. On the opposite side of the arena there is a big jumbotron screen. On the screen clips from the past few weeks are shown, first they show clips of Cyanide and The Udder Guy getting beat up. Then they show a clip of the Wolf Pack attacking The Iceman and The Masked Judge. And finally showing clips of the first and second rounds of the Intercontinental and US Heavyweight Championship tournament.) Randal Cruize: OK it's time to get started, let's go to Ralph Newborn. Intercontinental and US Heavyweight Championship Tournament Semi-finals (Circus music starts playing in the background, as Crazy Phill and his pet midget Louie start down the aisle) {The Crowd Cheers} Ralph Newborn (Ring announcer):Ladies and gentlemen coming down the aisle, weighing in at two hundred and eighty five pounds, from Lexington, KY the craziest wrestler in the WWWCW and one half of the WWWCW Heavyweight Tag-Team Champions, Twin Sisters, Crazy Phill!!!!!! (War Dogs music starts playing. He starts down the aisle with a vicious look on his face) {The Crowd Boos} Ralph Newborn:And now, his opponent standing at six feet and five inches, weighing in at two hundred and sixty eight pounds, the leader of the Wolf Pack, the self proclaimed baddest dog on the block, War Dog!!!!! {Everything Quiets Down} Randal Cruize: Well this should be one heck of a match, so far Crazy Phill is undefeated in the WWWCW. D.A. Johnson: Yes, but War Dogs only loss was in a six man tag match. Randal Cruize: Well I'm putting my money on Crazy Phill. D.A. Johnson: I'm calling a War Dog victory. {The Bell Rings} They lock up. Crazy Phill starts things off with a cheap shot followed by a DDT. Crazy Phill gets a flurry of moves on War Dog. War Dog reverses a suplex and gains control. War Dog wears Phill down with a series of moves. War Dog throws Crazy Phill out of the ring. Both men pound each other. Crazy Phill throws War Dog into the guardrail. Crazy Phill bulldogs War Dog. They get back in the ring. The match then goes back and forth several times. Crazy Phill takes War Dog down with a low blow. Crazy Phill throws War Dog int the ropes. Louie grabs his leg and trips him. Crazy Phill sets War Dog on the top rope. Randal Cruize: It's the Philldriver! I told you he would win. Crazy Phill sets War Dog up for the Philldriver. War Dog blocks and throws Phill down to the concrete floor. War Dog climbs down to the floor. He pounds on Crazy Phill. He throws Crazy Phill back into the ring. War Dog lands a suplex. War Dog drags Crazy Phill to the middle of the ring. He then perfectly executes the War Bomb. He goes for the pin. Louie climbs in the ring and kicks War Dog in the ribs. It doesn't affect him at all. War Dog gets off of Phill and War bombs Louie. He pins Phill again. Referee Kentt Shayden counts one, two, three!!! Time: 16:52 D.A. Johnson: War Dog wins!! Well I guess I was right. You were wrong Cruize! Randal Cruize: Shut up! I didn't ever think Phill would win, I just said that. D.A. Johnson: Let's go to the ring with Ralph Newborn. (Bret Harts theme music starts playing, Iceman comes to the ring) {The Crowd Boos At The Canadian} Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen coming to the ring from Edmonton, Canada, Weighing in at two hundred and forty five pounds, standing at six feet and six inches, one half of Cold Justice, The Iceman! (Seek and Destroy starts playing in the arena, as Fredo and Godfather come to the ring) {He Receives A Mixed Reaction From The Crowd} Ralph Newborn: And his opponent standing at six feet and eight inches, from Italy, weighing in at two hundred and eighty four pounds a member of the black Rose Mafia, it's Fredo!! {The Crowd Finally Quiets Down} Randal Cruize: Well who's gonna win this one Mr. know it all? D.A. Johnson: I trully have no idea about this one. Randal Cruize: Of course not, you don't have any idea about anything, you peon. D.A. Johnson: Well who do you think will win? Randal Cruize: Well...I don't....kno...well....they're both great competitors. D.A. Johnson: That's what I thought. Randal Cruize: No you didn't. (Cruize and Johnson keep arguing as the match begins) {The Bell Rings} The Iceman gets the early jump and takes Fredo down with a leg sweep. Fredo is quick to comeback, though. After a few moves Fredo lands a backbreaker followed by a powerslam. The Iceman lands a snap suplex. Iceman picks up Fredo and piledrivers him. There are a few more moves and attempted pins. Fredo lands a neckbreaker. Fredo slingshots Iceman into the turnbuckle. Fredo climbs to the second rope and pounds on The Iceman as the fans count the punches. Fredo rams his shoulder into Iceman. Later in the match Iceman lands a jawbreaker. The Iceman rolls Fredo up in an inside cradle, referee N.W. Riley counts one, two, thre..he gets his shoulder up. Fredo takes him down with a lariat. Fredo uses a headlock takedown. Fredo then attacks Iceman with a flurry of closed fist. Later Iceman lands a swinging DDT. Iceman lands a scoop slam. Fredo reverses an attempted superplex by turning it into a front-layout superplex. Fredo lands an inverted atomic drop. Fredo lands a sunset flip and rolls Iceman up. N.W. Riley counts one, two, three!! Time 18:45 Randal Cruize: I knew he would win. D.A. Johnson: I'm sure you did. Randal Cruize: let's go to the next match. (The Dogs of Destruction entrance music starts playing as they start down the aisle) {The Crowd Gives Them A Mixed Reaction} Ralph Newborn: Now coming to the ring with a combined weight of five hundred and ninety eight pounds, members of the Wolf Pack, it's the Dogs of Destruction, Beowulf and Death Wolf!!! (Bad Boys entrance music starts as they come down the aisle) {The Crowd Gives Them A Mixed Reaction} Ralph Newborn: And their opponents, with a combined weight of five hundred and ninety nine pounds, from Houston Texas, it's Bad Boys Slash and Sycho Joe!!! {The Crowd Gets Quiet} Randal Cruize: I'm not sure about this one, but you do have to consider the fact that Bad Boys have a weight advantage. D.A. Johnson: What? There's only a one pound difference. Randal Cruize: So. D.A. johnson: This should be a good one. Randal Cruize: This is the first match for both of the tag-teams, let's see how they do. {The Bell Rings} Beowulf and Slash start tthings off. Beowulf takes the early lead. Beowulf lands a belly to belly suplex. Beowulf lands a piledriver. A few moves later Beowulf tags out to Death Wolf. Death Wolf continues to work on Slash. A few moves and pins later Slash makes it to his and tags out. Sycho Joe comes in and beats Death Wolf down to the ground. Sycho Joe then knocks Beowulf off the apron. Death Wolf catches Sycho Joe off guard and attempts a enzuiguiri kick. Sycho Joe blocks and lands a russian leg sweep. Sycho Joe lands a leg drop. Sycho Joe throws Death Wolf out of the ring. Beowulf hits Sycho Joe with a steel chair. Beowulf puts Sycho Joe on top of a table. Death Wolf gets on the top rope. Sycho joe rolls off the table. Slash throws Death Wolf through the table. Sycho joe enters the ring. Slash throws Death Wolf into the ring. Sycho joe pins Death Wolf. Butch Williams counts one, two, thr..... Beowulf interupts the count. All four men brawl inside the ring. butch seperates the men. Later on Beowulf DDTs Slash. Beowulf throws Slash into the Dogs of Destructions corner. Death Wolf chokes Slash. Sycho Joe tries to come in the ring and help Slash. Butch stops him. While Butch and Sycho Joe are arguing The Dogs of Destrucion double team Slash. They land a double backbreaker. Beowulf pins Slash. Death Wolf gets Butch's attention. Butch counts one, two, three. Time 21:43 Randal Cruize: That was a great match up. D.A. Johnson: That might be the best match of the night. Randal Cruize: OK now it's time to go to Ralph Newborn with a special in ring interview. Ralph Newborn: Ladies and Gentlemen please welcome CRY HAVOC!!!!! (The lights dim and everything is dark. You here "Mutiny" the Theme from Crimson Tide playing and a white light appears at the entrance. The music keeps playing, but Cry Havoc doesn't show. Suddenly the music stops and you can hear thunder and lighnting and the lights begin to flicker. Then as the lights continue to flicker a white light comes up at the center of the ring. Cry Havoc emerges and the crowd is on their feet. The lights come back on.) Ralph Newborn: You asked for this special interview to clear something up. I understand there is something on your mind. Cry Havoc: My eyes have fallen short of the light. A presence has moved me from my sole purpose of destiny. The Warmaster has asked my to join the DOD. I have done some true soul searching and at first I was going to take the offer, but after the poor show of honor and pride like true champions should you turned your backs. You have created a fire deep in my soul. These people are the reason why you have a job. They pay to watch you do your job and you have the nerve to disrespect them. Your souls are in a world of trouble and I will make sure you fall to the Black Death. You have hell to pay with your souls!!!!! Take heed to this warning!!! Be prepared to.......CRY HAVOC!!!!! Ralph Newborn: But..... (Everything suddenly goes black. A loud explosion is heard and a gulf of fire spews from the center fo the ring and the lights immediately come back on and Cry Havoc is no where to be found.) (Dark Claws theme music starts playing, he comes to the ring) {The Crowd Gives Him A Mixed Reaction} Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen coming to the ring from parts unknown, weighing in at two hundred and sixty eight pounds, standing at six feet and five inches, Dark Claw!!! (Pains theme music starts playing as he comes down the aisle) {He Receives A Mixed Reaction From The Crowd} Ralph Newborn: And his opponent standing at six feet and three inches, weighing in at two hundred and eighty five pounds, Pain!!!! {The Crowd Finally Quiets Down} Randal Cruize: This should be a great match. D.A. Johnson: Yes I agree. Randal Cruize: I'm calling a Pain victory. D.A. Johnson: I hope this prediction is better than your last one. Randal Cruize: Let's here your prediction. D.A. Johnson: I don't have one. Randal Cruize: I'm NOT surprised. {The Bell Rings} Pain starts this one off with a neckbreaker. Pain lands an atomic drop. Pain attempts his finisher Intense Pain, but Dark Claw blocks. Dark Claw dropkicks pain out of the ring. Dark Claw throws Pain into the guard rails many time. Pain reveres and throws Dark Claw into the guard rail. Pain powerslams Dark Claw on the concrete. Pain stomps Dark Claw. Later on in the match Dark Claw throws Pain off the top turnbuckle. Dark Claw throws Pain into the corner and beats him down to the ground. Dark Claw chokeslams Pain. Pain kicks Dark Claw in the groin. Pain attempts a scoop slam, but Dark Claw rolls him up. Kentt Shayden counts one, two, three!! Time 14:32 Randal Cruize: Dark Claw wins his debut match in the WWWCW. D.A. Johnson: Ithought pain was going to win. Randal Cruize: Then you're dumber then I thought. (The Godfather theme starts playing as Godfather and Fredo come to the ring.) {The Crowd Cheers} Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen coming to the ring from Italy, Weighing in at two hundred andsixty two pounds, standing at six feet and six inches, The creator of the Black Rose Mafia, Godfather!!!! (The Star Spangled Banner starts playing as the All American comes down the aisle) {The Crowd Cheers} Ralph Newborn: And his opponent standing at six feet and seven inches, from Washington, D.C., weighing in at two hundred and eighty five pounds, The All American!!! {The Crowd Finally Quiets Down} Randal Cruize: Now we have a battle of the Nations, Italy and America. D.A. Johnson: Who's going to win this one? Randal Cruize: I don't know. D.A. Johnson: I don't know either. Randal Cruize: That's because you're inferior. D.A. Johnson: Inferior to what? Randal Cruize: I don't know. {The Bell Rings} Godfather and The All American lock up. The All American starts things off with a hiptoss. The All American uses a single leg take down. The All American lands a frog splash. A few moves and pins later Godfather powerbombs The All American. Godfather lands another powerbomb. Godfather goes for a third powerbomb. The All American reverses and lands a frankensteiner. The All American tries to land the Patriot Missile but Godfather catches him and powerslams him. Godfather throws The All American into the ropes lands a flying shoulderblock. The All American takes him down with a dropkick. The All American lands a moonsault. The All American attacks Godfather with an array of punches. Godfather gets the All American in a bearhug. Later on The All American lands a knee lift followed by a piledriver. He goes for the Patriot Missile again but Godfather counters and lands a shoulder breaker. The Godfather takes the patriot down with a series of clotheslines. The Godfather lifts the All American up in the air and executes The Black Rose. N.W. Riley counts one, two, three!! Time 15:29 Randal Cruize: That was one heck of a match. D.A. Johnson: A great match. Randal Cruize: Now it's time to go to the final match of the night, the Intercontinental and US Heavyweight Championship Tournament Final. Intercontinental and US Heavyweight Championship Tournament Finals Ralph Newborn: This bout is for the Intercontinental and US Heavyweight Championship Belts. The winner of this match will receive the Intercontinental Belt, and the loser will receive the United States Heavyweight Championship Belt. (War Dogs music starts playing, he comes out looking determined) {The Crowd Gives Him A Mixed Reaction} Ralph Newborn:And now, coming to the ring standing at six feet and five inches, weighing in at two hundred and sixty eight pounds, for his second match of the night, the leader of the Wolf Pack, the self proclaimed baddest dog on the block, War Dog!!!!! (Seek and Destroy starts playing and Fredo come down the aisle.) {The Crowd Gives Him A Mixed Reaction} Ralph Newborn: And his opponent standing at six feet and eight inches, from Italy, weighing in at two hundred and eighty four pounds a member of the black Rose Mafia, it's Fredo!! {The crowd gets quiet} D.A. Johnson: This is it folks, this one decides the belts. Randal Cruize: Yes, this is the final match of the night. One man will leave with the Intercontinental belt, one will leave with the US Heavyweight Belt. D.A. Johnson: No kidding, that's what Ralph just said. Randal Cruize: I was just reminding the people. {The Bell Rings} War Dog and fredo lock up. Fredo takes War Dog down with a kick to the stomach. Fredo lands a lariat. Fredo chokes War Dog on the ropes. War Dog comes back with a backbreaker. War Dog bulldogs Fredo. War Dog gets Fredo in a side headlock. War Dog throws Fredo into the ropes and lands a scoop slam. War Dog gets a chair from outside the ring. Kentt Shayden stops him from hitting Fredo. War Dog kicks Kentt in the stomach and War Bombs him. Fredo grabs the chair and hits War Dog over the head with it. Fredo throws War Dog out of the ring. Fredo slingshots War Dog into the guardrail. Fredo rams War Dog into the ring post. War Dog and Fredo get in a giant brawl on the floor. War Dog piledrivers Fredo on the concrete. War Dog clotheslines Fredo over the safety rail. War Dog jumps over the rail and onto Fredo. Fredo throws War Dog onto the guardrail. Fredo hits War Dog with a chair. Fredo powerbombs War Dog onto a chair. Fredo suplexes War Dog back over the rail. Kentt Shayden gets back up. He starts counting the men out. Fredo throws War Dog in the ring. Fredo sets a table up besdide the ring. Fredo climbs back in the ring. Fredo throws War Dog out of the ring and through the table. Fredo jumps through the ropes and lands on War Dog. Fredo throws War Dog back into the ring. War Dog lands an inverted DDT. War Dog grabs a piece of the broken table. He attempts to hit Fredo with it but Fredo dropkicks the table and causes it to hit War Dog. Fredo lands a DDT from the top rope. Fredo gets War Dog with the Goodnight. Kentt Shayden raises War Dogs hand in the air, "one!" He raises his hand again, "two!" he raises his hand a third time, it drops down to his side,"THREE!!" Time 23:43 Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this bout and new Intercontinental Heavyweight Champion, Fredo!! {The Crowd Goes Wild} Ralph Newborn: Even though War Dog lost this bout, he is the new United States Heavyweight Champion! {The Crowd Gives Him a mixed Reaction} Randal Cruize: I'd like to thank eveyone for tuning in to OYS and say so long. D.A. Johnson: Good-bye everyone. (The camera zooms out to show another wide angle of the crowd. On the big screen you can see highlight from OYS. Fire wroks go off all over the arena. The camera fades to the OYS logo. then it fades to the WWWCW logo.)
