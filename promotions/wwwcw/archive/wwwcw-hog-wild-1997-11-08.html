WWWCW Hog Wild 97

8 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Hog Wild" 1997 (The WWWCW logo covers the screen, then it fades to the Hog Wild logo. The camera then cuts to a camera set up in the top of the arena giving a wide angle shot of the whole arena. All 20,324 fans are going crazy. The Camera then cuts to a picture of the ringside announcers Randal Cruize and D.A. Johnson.) Randal Cruize: Hello ladies and gentlemen and welcome to beatiful South Bend Indiana. We're here bringing you "Hog Wild" 1997. D.A. Johnson: Tonight we have an Intercontinental Title defense, World Heavyweight Title defense, World Tag Team Title defense, and the introduction of the Extreme Heavyweight Title. Randal Cruize: Yes, we will see Shooter defend his title against Iceman and we will be seeing a Five-Man Chicken Coop Weapons Match to decide who gets the Extreme Heavyweight belt along with a lot of other top notch action. D.A. Johnson: We have that and much much more. (Fireworks go off along the aisle. On the opposite side of the arena there is a big jumbotron screen. On the screen clips from the past few weeks are shown, one of the clips is of Godfather Black Rosing Fredo, there are also clips of Fredo in the hospital declaring that he has lost his memory and is now Shooter. There are other various clips from the last PPV.) Randal Cruize: OK it's time to get started, let's go to Ralph Newborn. (Mr. Moneybags music starts playing as he comes to the ring) {He Receives Cheers} Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen, coming to the ring, weighing in at two hundred and fourty five pounds, from Atlantic City, New Jersey, it's Mr. Moneybags!! (Death Wolfs music starts playing as he comes to the ring) {He Receives A Mixed Reaction} Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen, coming to the ring, weighing in at three hundred and one pounds, standing at six feet and eight inches, it's Death Wolf!! {The Crowd Quiets Down} Randal Cruize: Mr. Moneybags and Death Wolf are set to face off. D.A. Johnson: I wonder if there will be an appearance made by Iceman. Randal Cruize: I don't know, last week he didn't seem like he was very fond of Mr. Moneybags. D.A. Johnson: We will soon find out. {The Bell Rings} Mr. Moneybags starts things out. Mr. Moneybags executes a knee lift. Mr. Moneybags lands a vertical suplex. Mr. Moneybags throws Death Wolf into the ropes. Mr. Moneybags lands a flying clotheline. Death Wolf lands a drop kick. Death Wolf throws Mr. Moneybags into the corner. Death Wolf stomps Mr. Moneybags down to the ground. Death Wolf lands a super-plex. A few moves and pins later Mr. Moneybags takes control of the match. Mr. Moneybags attempt to land a piledriver. Death Wolf blocks and delivers a low blow. A few moves and pins later Death Wolf lands a powerbomb. Death Wolf rolls up Mr. moneybags. Death Wolf pulls Mr. Moneybags tights. The ref counts. One, Two, Three. Time: 15:32 Randal Cruize: There you have it, Hog Wild 97 has begun. D.A. Johnson: Let's go to the next match. World Heavyweight Tag-Team Championship Match {The Graveyards music starts playing as they come to the ring.} {The Crowd Boos} Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen coming to the ring Tombstone and Death, together they are known as The Graveyard!! {The Twin Sisters music starts playing as they come to the ring.} {The Crowd Gives Them a Mixed Reaction} Ralph Newborn: And now, coming to the ring The WWWCW Heavyweight Tag-Team Champions, Crazy Phill and Lunatic Larry, it's Twin Sisters!! {The Crowd Gets Quiet} Randal Cruize: The Twin Sisters are finally defending their titles. D.A. Johnson: Yes, and I feel that they will be succesful. Randal Cruize: I don't know the Graveyard is a pretty good tag team. {The Bell Rings} Lunatic Larry and Death start things off. Lunatic Larry lands a kick to the groin. Then he pounds on Death. Death tries to make a comeback. Larry lands a northern lights suplex. Larry tags out to Crazy Phill. Crazy Phill powerbombs Death. Crazy Phill calls Tombstone out. Death tags out to Tombstone. Crazy Phill hiptosses Tombstone. Crazy Phill throws Tombstone out of the ring. Lunatic Larry and Crazy Phill gang up on Tombstone. Death comes to his aid. All four of the men battle outside of the ring. N.W. Riley seperates the men and gets them back in the ring. Lunatic Larry sets up a table outside of the ring. Crazy Phill throws Tombstone out of the ring. Larry beats on Tombstone. Death comes in the ring and attacks Crazy Phill. Lunatic Larry sets Tombstone on top of the table. Crazy Phill sets Death on the top rope. Crazy Phill Philldrivers Death on top of Tombstone. Larry grabs Tombstone. Larry beats Tombstone with a chair. Crazy Phill picks up Death. Crazy Phill lands a hunankurana on Death. Larry and Phill throw Tombstone and Death back in the ring. Larry gets Death in the claw. Crazy Phill puts Tombstone on the top rope. He goes up for the Phill Driver. The Dogs of Destruction come out and attack Larry and Phill. N.W. Riley disqualifies The Graveyard. Time: 15:32 Ralph Newborn: The winners of this bout, and still the WWWCW Heavyweight Tag Team Champions, the Twin Sisters!! Randal Cruize: The Twin Sisters have won again. They retain the belts. D.A. Johnson: That match was......wait....we have a message from the back....What? Ladies and gentlemen, we have been notified that The Graveyard has quit the WWWCW. Hold on. And so has Sam Switchblade. Randal Cruize: Well, that's a surprise. D.A. Johnson: That's bad news for the AOD. I think their roster is now down to Executioner himself. Randal Cruize: Alliance Armageddon was going to have four alliances, then it was three, and now it's two. Unless the AOD can rebuild their team. D.A. Johnson: Let's head to Ralph Newborn with the next match. Intercontinental Heavyweight Championship Match (Bret Harts theme music starts playing, Iceman comes to the ring) {He Receives A Mixed Reaction} Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen coming to the ring from Edmonton, Canada, Weighing in at two hundred and forty five pounds, standing at six feet and six inches, one half of Cold Justice, The Iceman! (Money starts playing in the arena, as Shooter comes to the ring) {He Receives A Mixed Reaction From The Crowd} Ralph Newborn: And his opponent standing at six feet and eight inches, from Las Vegas Nevada, weighing in at two hundred and eighty four pounds,the Intercontinental Heavyweight Champion, it's Shooter!! {The Crowd Finally Quiets Down} Randal Cruize: This should be a good match, both men are very skilled wrestlers. D.A. Johnson: IYes, Fred....I mean Shooter has done pretty well for himself in the WWWCW, but he is facing a fierce opponent, The Iceman. {The Bell Rings} The Iceman drops Shooter with a flying clothesline. Iceman tries to land a DDT but Shooter blocks it. Shooter throws Iceman into the corner and unloads on him with a flurry of fist to the face. Shooter stomps Iceman to the mat. Shooter turns to the crowd and Iceman kicks him between the legs. Shooter drops to the ground in agony. Iceman takes control of the match and lands a double underhook DDT. After a few moves and pins Shooter lands a snap suplex. He pins Iceman. One, Two, he kicks out. War Dog comes to ringside. Iceman lands the Icedrop. Iceman pins Shooter. War Dog jumps up on the apron and gets the refs attention. Iceman gets up and grabs the ref. Shooter sneaks up from behind and rolls Iceman up. One, Two, Thr.....he kicks out. Shooter beats on Iceman. Shooter goes for the 7-11 roll, but Iceman blocks it and lands a short arm clothesline. Iceman pins Shooter. War Dog gets the refs attention again. Shooter lands the 7-11 roll. Mr. Moneybags comes to the ring and knocks Shooter out with a chair. Mr. Moneybags drags Iceman on top of Shooter. War Dog still has the refs attention. Mr. Moneybags gets the refs attention on Iceman. Mr. Moneybags attacks War Dog. The Ref counts. One, Two, Three!! Twin Sisters come out and attack War Dog. Mr. Moneybags gets in the ring. Mr. Moneybags and Iceman stare at each other. Iceman holds his hand out to shake. Mr. Moneybags spits on him. Mr. Moneybags sticks his hand out. Iceman and Mr. Moneybags shake hands. The rest of the Pack comes out and attack Iceman and Mr. Moneybags. The rest of the Foundation comes out. Both groups have a giant brawl. They finally get seperated. Time: 16:54 Ralph Newborn: The Winner of this bout, and new Intercontinental Heavyweight Champion, Iceman!!! Randal Cruize: We have a new champion! D.A. Johnson: That was a great match. By the way did you notice that The Foundation was helping out Mr. Moneybags. Randal Cruize: Yes, I did. Now let's go to Ralph Newborn for the Five-Man Chicken Coop Weapons Match. Five-Man Chicken Coop Weapons Match (War Dog, The Taxman, Dark Claw, Red Hound, and Executioner come to the ring) Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is a five-man chicken coop weapons match. The five men behind me will battle it out in the chicken coop using anything they can get there hands on. Once a wrestler gets pinned or submits he is eliminated. The last man in the ring will be awarded the Extreme Heavyweight Championship belt. {The Crowd Quiets Down} Randal Cruize: This might be the best match of the night. D.A. Johnson: These guys must be crazy, I don't think the belt is worth enouhg to fight in a match like this. Look at all the junk in the ring. There's baseball bats, violins, guitars, barbedwire, chains, and no telling what else is in there. Randal Cruize: I agree, you wouldn't ever catch me in this type of situation, but of course these guys are extreme. D.A. Johnson: Don't you mean they're insane. Randal Cruize: I guess, all I know is that they are all cooped up. [Randal expects laughter but gets none.] D.A. Johnson: That went over well. Randal Cruize: Shut up! {The Bell Rings} Dark Claw starts things off by attacking The Taxman. Red Hound and War Dog quickly join in on the beating. War Dog grabs a chair and beats The Taxman with it. They beat The Taxman into a corner. Taxman gets a hold of a metal pipe. He takes out Dark Claws legs, and beats Red Hound across the back with it. War Dog quickly grabs a baseball bat and hits Taxman across the back with it. Executioner finally joins in on the madness. Executioner takes a chain and starts choking Red Hound with it. Dark Claw saves him by breaking a piece of lumber acroos Executioners back. Taxman and War Dog are left one-on-one. Taxman grabs a violin and smashes it across War Dogs face. Taxman grabs some barbedwire and starts whipping War Dog with it. Taxman wraps the barbedwire around a cement block and DDTs War Dog on top of it. War Dog is busted open. Executioner is being beaten with a trash can. Dark Claw sees War Dog busted open and goes to help him out. Red Hound goes after Executioners shoulder. Red Hound takes a frying pan and beats Executioners shoulder with it. Dark Claw and War Dog pound on Taxman. Red Hound climbs to the top of the coop and jumps off. Executioner catches in the air with a guitar. Executioner puts Red Hounds head on top of a cement block and lands a leg drop. War Dog grinds Taxmans head across the side of the coop. Taxman starts bleeding. Executioner lands the Dive Bomber and pins Red Hound. Red Hound is eliminated. Executioner attacks Dark Claw. War Dog sets taxman on top of a table. Executioner knocks War Dog down with a baseball bat. Executioner puts Dark Claw on top of the coop. War Dog climbs after him. Executioner sets Dark Claw up for a super-plex. The weight of the three men causes the side of the coop to collapse. Executioner super-plexes Dark Claw onto Taxman as the wall falls down. Taxman, Dark Claw, and Executioner all go through the table. War Dog landed outside of the ring. Executioner jumps over the top rope and lands on War Dog. Dark Claw starts to help War Dog. Taxman grabs him and throws him into a pile of junk. Dark Claw has cuts all over his back. Taxman runs him into the side of the coop. The wall falls down. Taxman hits Dark Claw over the head with a brick and pins him. Dark Claw has been eliminated. War Dog lands the War Bomb on the remnants of the coop wall. He pins Executioner. Executioner has been eliminated. Taxman and War Dog go at each other. War Dog takes part of one of the fallen coop walls and beats Taxman with it. Executioner is being carried off on a stretcher. War Dog pins Taxman. One, Two, Th...... Taxman gets up. War Dog wraps Taxman with part of the chicken wire. War Dog sets up a table. He sets Taxman up for the War Bomb. He War bombs Taxman from the top rope throught the table. He pins Taxman. One, Two, Three!! Time: 29:57 Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this bout and the first ever Extreme Heavyweight Champion, War Dog! {The Crowd Cheers For Him} Randal Cruize: WOW! That was one hell of a match. the ring is a wreck the coop is totally torn apart. There is chicken wire everywhere! D.A. Johnson: And we have an Extreme Heavyweight Champion, War Dog! Randal Cruize: I don't believe what I just saw. D.A. Johnson: Neither can I. Randal Cruize: Now it's time for the WWWCW Heavyweight Championship Match. World Heavyweight Championship Match (The Peoples Court theme starts playing as Masked Judge makes his way to the ring.) {He Receives A Mixed Reaction} Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen coming to the ring from Washington, D.C., Weighing in at two hundred and sixty five pounds, standing at six feet and five inches, one half of Cold Justice, Masked Judge! (The Godfather theme starts playing as Godfather comes to the ring) {The Crowd Boos} Ralph Newborn: And his opponent standing at six feet and six inches, from Italy weighing in at two hundred and sixty two pounds,the WWWCW Heavyweight Champion, it's Godfather!! {The Crowd Finally Quiets Down} Randal Cruize: I hope this match is as good as the last one. D.A. Johnson: Let's get it started. {The Bell Rings} Godfather lands a clothesline. Godfather lands a piledriver. Godfather tries to land a flying cross body. Masked Judge catches him and powerslams him. Masked Judge lands a swinging neck breaker. A few moves and pins later Masked Judge attempts the Lethal Injection. Godfather blocks. Godfather executes a snap mare. masked judge comes back with a flurry of fist. Masked Judge lands a back breaker. A few moves and pins later Godfather appears to be in trouble. Masked Judge lands a jaw breaker. Masked Judge goes for the Lethal Injection. He lands it. The Pack comes to the ring and attacks Masked Judge. The Foundation comes to the ring. The two groups once again break out in a giant brawl. Butch Williams disqualifies Godfather and Masked Judge. The Foundation and The Pack continue to battle. Finally the two groups seperate and leave. Time: 19:23 Randal Cruize: Well we got a double disqualification. D.A. Johnson: I can't wait for Alliance Armageddon. Randal Cruize: I know it. I think it will be a very heated battle. D.A. Johnson: Well thanks for tuning in to Hog Wild 97. So long everyone. (The camera zooms out to show another wide angle of the crowd. On the big screen you can see highlights from Hog Wild. Fire works go off all over the arena. The camera fades to the Hog Wild logo. then it fades to the WWWCW logo.)
