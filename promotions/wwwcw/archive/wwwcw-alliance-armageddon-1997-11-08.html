WWWCW Alliance Armageddon

8 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Alliance Armageddon" 1997 (The WWWCW logo covers the screen, then it fades to the Alliance Armageddon logo. The camera then cuts to a camera set up in the top of the arena giving a wide angle shot of the whole arena. All 23,344 fans are going wild. There are hundreds of signs in the crowd. There can be two Scoops signs seen along with Foundation signs and Wolf Pack signs. The Camera cuts to the ringside announcers Randal Cruize and D.A. Johnson. Behind D.A. Johnson there is a fan with a sign that says "Dumb A$$ Johnson" with an arrow pointing towards D.A. Johnson.) Randal Cruize: Hello ladies and gentlemen and welcome to Baltimore Maryland. We're here bringing you one of the biggest Pay Per Views in WWWCW history, "Alliance Armageddon" 1997! D.A. Johnson: Tonight we will see the Wolf Pack and the Foundation battle it out for the WWWCW Alliance Championship Belts. Randal Cruize: That's right. Let's get it started! (Fireworks go off along the aisle. On the opposite side of the arena there is a big jumbotron screen. On the screen clips from the past few weeks are shown, Most of the clips are of the Foundation and the Wolf Pack. The camera cuts to the ring where we can see Ralph Newborn.) Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen, tonight you will be witnessing one of the biggest events in the history of wrestling. The two greatest forces in the world, the Wolf Pack and the Foundation, will be brutally battling to see which one is the most powerful. So, Let's get it on! (War Dogs music starts playing. He starts down the aisle with a vicious look on his face) {The Crowd Gives Him A Mixed Reaction} Ralph Newborn:Coming to the ring, standing at six feet and five inches, weighing in at two hundred and sixty eight pounds, the leader of the Wolf Pack, the self proclaimed baddest dog on the block, the former WWWCW Extreme Heavyweight Champion, War Dog!!!!! ("I want it all" by Queen starts playing as Mr. Moneybags comes to the ring) {He Receives Cheers} Ralph Newborn: And his opponent, coming to the ring, weighing in at two hundred and fourty five pounds, from Atlantic City, New Jersey, representing the Foundation, it's Mr. Moneybags!!!! Randal Cruize: Here we go with the first match of the night, War Dog facing off against Mr.Moneybags. D.A. Johnson: This should be a good one. Randal Cruize: The World Champion against the former Extreme Champion. Let's get it started. {The Bell Rings} Mr. Moneybags starts things off with a hiptoss. He quickly starts working on War Dogs legs. He lands a leg breaker. He picks up War Dog and throws him into the ropes. Mr. Moneybags bends over. War Dog jumps over him and lands a sunset flip. The ref counts, one, two,...Mr. Moneybags kicks out. War Dog picks up Mr. Moneybags and lands a tilt-a-whirl back breaker. War Dog slingshots Mr. moneybags into the ring post. A few moves and pins later War Dog lands a double underhook piledriver. War Dog sets Mr. Moneybags up on the top rope. He tries to War Bomb Mr. Moneybags from the top rope. Mr. Moneybags reverses it into a frankensteiner. Mr. Moneybags misses a drop kick. War Dog throws Mr. Moneybags out of the ring. War Dog exits the ring after him. War Dog throws Mr. Moneybags into the guard rails repeatedly. Later on in the match War Dog sets a table up outside of the ring. He War Bombs Mr. Moneybags through the table. War Dog picks up a chair and tries to hit Mr. Moneybags with it. Mr. Moneybags ducks and lands a stomp to the gut. Mr. Moneybags grabs the chair and hits War Dog with it. Mr. Moneybags throws War Dog back in the ring. Mr. Moneybags gets back in the ring with the chair. A few moves and pins later, Mr. Moneybags beats War Dogs knee with the chair. Mr. Moneybags then gets War Dog in the Six Figure Leglock. War Dog taps out. Time: 20:32 Randal Cruize: Mr. Moneybags wins! D.A. Johnson: That's one for the Foundation, and zero for the Wolf Pack. Randal Cruize: Let's see who will win the next one. (Dark Claws theme music starts playing, he comes to the ring) {The Crowd Gives Him A Mixed Reaction} Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen coming to the ring from parts unknown, weighing in at two hundred and sixty eight pounds, standing at six feet and five inches, Dark Claw!!! (Bret Harts theme music starts playing, Iceman comes to the ring) {The Crowd Boos} Ralph Newborn:And his opponent, coming to the ring from Edmonton, Canada, Weighing in at two hundred and forty five pounds, standing at six feet and six inches, the Intercontinental Heavyweight Champion, The Iceman!!!! Randal Cruize: It's time for match number two. D.A. Johnson: Let's get to the action. {The Bell Rings} Dark Claw starts things off with a flying clothesline. Dark Claw picks up Iceman and whips him into the corner. He rams his shoulder into Icemans stomach. Dark Claw throws Iceman into the ropes and takes him out with a drop kick. Dark Claw lifts Iceman up in a vertical suplex. Iceman reverses it into a front layout suplex. Iceman lands a power bomb. Iceman lands a neckbreaker. A few pins and moves later, Iceman lands a DDT. Iceman goes for an enzuiguiri. Dark Claw ducks and starts to get Iceman in the Cravers Cribb. Iceman blocks and rolls him up in a small package. One, two, .....Dark Claw kicks out. Iceman attempts the Icedrop. Dark Claw blocks it and gets Iceman in a sleeper. Iceman counters with a jaw breaker. Iceman lands a pile driver. Later on in the match, Dark Claw lands a lariat. Dark Claw climbs to the top rope. Dark Claw lands a hunankurana. Dark Claw goes for the pin. One, two, thre.....Iceman kicks out. Dark Claw throws Iceman into the ropes and goes for a single leg takedown. Iceman blocks. Iceman throws Dark Claw into the ropes and kicks him in the midsection. Iceman grabs Dark Claws neck and lands the Icedrop. Iceman pins Dark Claw. One, two, three! Time: 18:34 Randal Cruize: There's two for the Foundation, none for the Wolf Pack. D.A. Johnson: Things aren't looking good for the Wolf Pack. Randal Cruize: No they aren't. maybe they can pull a victory in the next match. (The Peoples Court theme starts playing as Masked Judge makes his way to the ring.) {He Receives A Mixed Reaction} Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen coming to the ring from Washington, D.C., Weighing in at two hundred and sixty five pounds, standing at six feet and five inches, Masked Judge!!!! (Beowulfs music starts playing as he comes to the ringside area.) {The Crowd Cheers} Ralph Newborn:And his opponent, coming down the aisle, weighing in at two hundred and ninety seven pounds, standing at six feet and nine inches, it's Beowulf!!! Randal Cruize: We haven't seen the Masked Judge lately, I wonder if he is ready to fight. D.A. Johnson: I wonder the same thing. Randal Cruize: No you don't. D.A. Johnson: What? Randal Cruize: You just want to be like me. D.A. Johnson: Uh.....No. Let's get to the match. {The Bell Rings} Beowulf begins the match with a powerslam. Beowulf throws Masked Judge into the ropes. Beowulf lands a armdrag takedown. Beowulf lands a superplex. Beowulf lands a backbreaker. Beowulf sets Masked Judge up on the top rope. Beowulf lands a top rope DDT. After a few minutes of Beowulf domination, Beowulf ends the match with the Dragonslayer. Time: 12:13 D.A. Johnson: Obviously he wasn't ready. Randal Cruize: That's right. Now the Wolf Pack has one and the Foundation has two. D.A. Johnson: The Wolf Pack has a chance to even things up next match. Randal Cruize: Let's see what they can do. (Death Wolfs music starts playing as he comes to the ring.) {The Crowd Cheers} Ralph Newborn: Ladies and gentlemen, coming down the aisle, weighing in at three hundred and one pounds, standing at six feet and eight inches, it's Death Wolf!!! (Animaniacs starts playing in the background, as Crazy Phill and his pet midget Louie start down the aisle) {The Crowd Cheers} Ralph Newborn:And his opponent, coming down the aisle, weighing in at two hundred and eighty five pounds, from Lexington, KY, Crazy Phill!!! D.A. Johnson: This could be the turning point for Alliance Armageddon. Randal Cruize: Let's see. {The Bell Rings} Death Wolf starts things off. He lands a flying head scissors. He throws Crazy Phill into the ropes and lands a scoop slam. Death Wolf throws Crazy Phill out of the ring. Death Wolf rams Crazy Phill into the ring post. Death Wolf sets Crazy Phill on top of a table. Death Wolf runs and jumps at Phill. Phill rolls off the table. Death Wolf flys throught the table. Crazy Phill throws Death Wolf over the guard rail. He climbs to the top rope. He jumps over the guard rail on top of Death Wolf. Death Wolf gets up and beats Phill with a chair. Death Wolf throws Phill back over the guardrail. Death Wolf jumps over the guard rail landing a swinging DDT. Death Wolf throws Phill back into the ring. Death Wolf climbs back into the ring with the chair. He continues to beat Phill with the chair. The referee gets knocked down. A few moves later, Crazy Phill lands a powerbomb. Crazy Phill lands a shoulder breaker. Crazy Phill throws Death Wolf intop the corner. He lands a splash. He snap suplexes Death Wolf into the middle of the ring. Phill climbs the rope. Phill lands a moonsault. Later on in the match, Death Wolf brings a table into the ring. Crazy Phill attacks him with the chair. Crazy Phill sets the table by the corner. He puts Death Wolf on the turnbuckle. Phill lands the Philldriver through the table. Crazy Phill hits his neck on the back of the chair wich was set up next to the table. Death Wolf falls on top of Crazy Phill. The ref gets up. Both men are unconcious. The ref counts, one, two, three! Death Wolf wins. Time: 20:56 Crazy Phill doesn't move. They bring a stretcher out to the ring. Crazy Phill is carried off. Randal Cruize: This is not good. D.A. Johnson: That could possibly be a broken neck. Randal Cruize: We will keep you all updated on Crazy Phills condition. D.A. Johnson: I guess we should go to the next match. (Bushwhackers music starts playing as Bushwhacker comes to the ring.) {The Crowd Cheers} Ralph Newborn: Coming to the ring, weighing in at three hundred and fifteen pounds, standing at six feet and five inches, it's the Bushwhacker!!! (The Dragon Shadows music starts playing as he comes to the ring) {The Crowd Cheers} Ralph Newborn:And his opponent, coming down the aisle, weighing in at one hundred and sixty nine pounds, from Japan, it's The Dragon Shadow!!!! Randal Cruize: Everything is even now, Foundation two Wolf Pack two. D.A. Johnson: This match could decide Alliance Armageddon. Randal Cruize: Let's go to the match. The Dragon Shadow attacks Bushwhacker before the bell. He stomps Bushwhacker down to the ground. {The Bell Rings} Dragon throws Bushwhacker into the ropes. Dragon lands a clothesline. Later on in the match, Bushwhacker throws Dragon into the corner. Bushwhacker rams him with his shoulder. Bushwhacker lands a snap mare. Bushwhacker climbs to the top rope. He lands a bulldog. Bushwhacker gets Dragon in a side headlock. Bushwhacker pounds on his head. Bushwhacker attempts the Bush Hog. Dragon Blocks and lands a knee lift. Dragon gets Bushwhacker in the Dragon Shadow. Bushwhacker grabs the bottom rope. The ref makes Dragon release the hold. A masked wrestler comes to ringside. Neither Bushwhacker nor Dragon sees him. Randal Cruize: Who is that? D.A. Johnson: I don't know, it appears to be a luchadore. Randal Cruize: I've never seen him before. Bushwhacker throws Dragon into the ropes. The masked wrestler grabs Dragons leg and trips him. He lays a bat on the mat and distracts the ref. Bushwhacker beats Dragon down with the bat. Bushwhacker throws the bat out of the ring. Bushwhacker pins Dragon. The masked wrestler leaves the ring. The ref counts, one, two, three! Time: 15:12 Randal Cruize: Who was that?! D.A. Johnson: I don't know! Randal Cruize: Well it's time for the final event of the night. Ralph Newborn: Now it's time for the main event. The following men will be placed in a fianl match to decide who gets the Alliance Armageddon belts. Mr. Moneybags, Iceman, Bushwhacker, Death Wolf, and Beowulf will fight in a five man tag match. Once a man is pinned he is eliminated. The match is over when one team runs out of team-mates. Now, let's get it started. (All the named wrestlers come to the ring.) {The Bell Rings} Mr. Moneybags and Beowulf start things off. Mr. Moneybags immediately lands a powerbomb. Mr. Moneybags lands an Inverted DDT. Mr. Moneybags throws Beowulf into the corner. The rest of the Foundation pounds on him. Death Wolf runs in the ring. Both teams enter the ring. It's a brawl. The teams finally seperate. Mr. Moneybags tags out to Iceman. Iceman runs in and lands a clothesline. Iceman lands a fishermans suplex. The ref counts, one, two, th.... Beowulf kicks out. Iceman throws Beowulf into the Foundation corner again. Death Wolf runs in the ring. Iceman kicks him in the stomach and lands the Icedrop. The he grabs Beowulf and lands a DDT. Bushwhacker chokes Beowulf against the ropes. Iceman Icedrops Beowulf and makes the pin. The ref starts to count. One. Death Wolf tries to interfere, but Bushwhacker takes him out with a clothesline. Two, Three! Beowulf has been eliminated. Iceman tags out to Bushwhacker. Bushwhacker lands a press slam. Bushwhacker sets Death Wolf on his shoulders and tags in Mr. Moneybags. Mr. Moneybags climbs the ropes and clotheslines Death Wolf off of Bushwhackers shoulders. Mr. Moneybags throws Death Wolf out of the ring. Randal Cruize: We have been told that Crazy Phill is at the hospital and IS moving his arms and legs. There are no signs of paralysis. Team Foundation beats on him outside the ring. Mr. Moneybags throws Death Wolf back in the ring. Mr. Moneybags enters the ring. Mr. Moneybags tags out to Bushwhacker. Death Wolf appears to have something in his hands. Death Wolf knocks the ref down. Death Wolf knocks all of team Foundation of the ring apron. Death Wolf also knocks out Bushwhacker. Randal Cruize: Here comes the luchadore! D.A. Johnson: The luchadore is here to save the day. The masked wrestler takes out Death Wolfs legs. He grabs the metal bar from Death Wolf and knocks him out with it. He picks up Bushwhacker. Bushwhacker sees Death Wolf laying in the middle of the ring. He makes the pin. Iceman gets the ref to look. He counts, one, two, three! Time: 20:43 The Foundation fills the ring. The ref raises there hands. The President brings out the belts and hands them out. Bushwhacker and the rest of the Foundation see the masked man and confront him. He pulls off the mask. D.A. Johnson: It's "The Super Jobber"!! Randal Cruize: Will Womack has helped the Foundation win Alliance Armageddon. D.A. Johnson: We're out of time, see you this Friday! (The camera zooms out to show another wide angle of the crowd. On the big screen you can see highlight from Alliance Armageddon. Fire works go off all over the arena.The camera fades to the Alliance Armageddon logo, then it fades to the WWWCW logo.)
