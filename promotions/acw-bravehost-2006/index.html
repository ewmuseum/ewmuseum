Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

American Championship Wrestling, ACW on its own pages, was a website e-fed staged at the ACW Palace in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Its front page of February 2006 showed its four titles, the National, US Freedom, Liberty and Global Tag Team Championships, all vacant, with news briefs, a front desk report from its CEO and a Sunday card for 26 February 2006 that held the second round of a National Title tournament and the first round of a US Freedom tournament. The CEO's report promised a Liberty Title tournament to start on 5 March 2006 and a place in it for any free agent who signed.

By May 2006 the menu listed a rule book, roleplay help, a front office, a contract to sign, a roster, stables, rankings, two weekly events, Lowdown on Sunday and Wildcard on Wednesday, a pay-per-view, a hall of fame and a roleplay board beside an out-of-character board. The news of 5 to 15 May 2006 left the World Title vacant, set a 25-man battle royal at Lowdown on 14 May to crown a new champion, signed John Mercer and Mark Force, both from the GWA, and reported Brien Cage hurt by Rage, leaving The Baller to pick a new partner for the Tag Team titles.

The page of September 2007 names its weekly events Anarchy, on Sunday, and Devastation, on Thursday. Its news runs from 27 August to 6 September 2007: Pierce Cavanaugh, co-holder of the Tag Titles and Hardcore Champion, won the Champions Challenge at Devastation; TC Jagger was to defend the World title against Cap'n Carnage at the Remembrence pay-per-view; Adrian Young, a three-time Cruiserweight Champion, retired; and Badass Brian lost a loser-leaves-ACW match to The Dealer. The legal line of that page begins Asylum Championship American Championship Wrestling.
