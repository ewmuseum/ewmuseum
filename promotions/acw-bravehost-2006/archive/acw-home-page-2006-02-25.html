The ACW is still seeking out wrestlers to sign to lucrative contracts. To compete in the VACANT Liberty Title Tournament. At this time only Johnny Coltranen has shown any guts at all and has signed on board. Now we at ACW would like to press this issue one last time before Lowdown [2], this Sunday. Anyone who signs an ACW Contract by 7am Sunday morning (Feb 26th, 2006), will be granted a slot in the Liberty Title Tournament. They may also feel free to have some interview time during the Lowdown [2] broadcast to speak their piece. So don't just sit there, join ACW now and be granted a spot in this great tournament to become the first ever ACW Liberty Champion. But it doesn't need to stop there. Win or Lose, if you prove yourself in ACW US Freedom or National Title shots may follow. What? You want to come to ACW with a stablemate, friends, etc. By all means be our guest. The ACW Tag Title are also vacant and will soon be decided. So by joining today you'll not only earn a slot in the Liberty Title tournament, but the Tag Title Tournament as well!! Join ACW today and see if you got what it takes... Feb. 22nd, 2006 @11:11am EST. - Lowdown [2] is just days away. Live from the ACW Palace we'll witness the opening round of the US Freedom title tournament, as well as the semi-finals of the National Title tournament. Those at the 1WR website (in our Affiliates section) have called Drake Foster the odds on favorite to take the ACW National Title. Will he be able to withstand the pressure or will Dollar McDougal or Rob DiPietro prove them wrong? Find out this Sunday at Lowdown [2] Front Desk Reports From the desk of ACW CEO Joey Falcone: Feb. 24th, 2006, @11:12am EST. - Hey all, it's a little after 11am EST on Friday morning, and I'm busting my ass to bring in as much talent as I can before this weeks Lowdown. This Sunday we'll witness the National Tournament Semi-Finals & the US Freedom opening Round, which in a sence is also a Semi-Final Round. Anyone who loses in either of this tournaments will be placed in the Liberty Tournament scheduled to start on March 5th, 2006 @Lowdown [3]. Also any free agents who sign on with ACW will also be included with this tournament. This will then leave one title undecided. The Global Tag Team Titles, until we build a stronger more active roster, create some fueds and get the ball rolling these titles will remain that way. So if you're part of the aCW roster now, then I ask you. "Please do what you can to bring in as much new talent as possible" Thanks. From the desk of ACW Commissioner ?????: NOW HIRING!!!
