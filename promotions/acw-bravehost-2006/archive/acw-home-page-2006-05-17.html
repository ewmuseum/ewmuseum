[detail withheld] ***Hit Counter*** Since Jan 29th, 2006 @7:07pm EST - Mark Force & Zero sign with ACW! - May 15th, 2006 @110:43am EST. - The ACW is proud to announce the signing of "The King of Old School" Mark Force. Force is a former GWA wrestler, while in GWA he put together a 4-1 record and was well on his way to being a top performer before GWA closed it's doors. We at ACW have no doubt that Msrk Force can continue his ways here in ACW. Also signed was Zero, this high flyer should add some excitement to the cruiserweight division. We'd like to wish them both the best of luck here in ACW. - White Eagle Vs. Cap N' Carnage - May 15th, 2006 @10:43am EST. - Earlier this week it was announced White Eagle was going to face a Mystery Opponent at Wildcard. Well White Eagle showed up at ACW Headquarters and demanded to know who he would be facing. Mr. Falcone informed him his opponent would be Cap N' Carnage. So this Wednesday we'll see White Eagle Vs. Cap N' Carnage, this should be a good one! - Brien Cage INJURED! The Baller seeks new PARTNER! - May 11th, 2006 @12:15pm EST. - It was just reported no more then 1hr ago that ACW Superstar Brien Cage has suffered several injuries at the hands of Rage due to his brutal attack at Wildcard during the Tour of the Orient: END GAME Sime-Finals match. ACW Doctor James Bouder has informed Mr. Cage as well as ACW that his injuries could keep him out for as long as 3 months. ACW CEO Joey Falcone then contacted The Baller and informed him of the situation, informing him that the ACW Tag Titles will remain with him, but Brien Cage must be replaced. The Baller now has the right to hand pick ANYONE on the ACW Roster as his new partner, and co-holder of the ACW Tag Team titles. If The Baller and the wrestler his picks are still tag team champions when Brien Cage returns, it will stay that way. Now the MAIN question is... "WHO WILL BALLER PICK?!" - Wilcard Report - May 10th, 2006 @11:40am EST. - Wildcard is in hhe books, and it was a huge night. We witnessed the arrival of 2 WWE legends. We now know our End Game finals match, We also saw a major ACW superstar get injured. Just who suffered the injury? Check out Wildcard now for all the details!! - ACW signs former GWA Star - May 9th, 2006 @3:49pm EST. - As of this afternoon, ACW has come to terms with former multi-time GWA Champion John Mercer. Mercer will be making his ACW debut this Sunday at Lowdown. That's right Mercer has been added to the Battle Royal at Lowdown where the winner will be crowned ACW World Champion! - ACW World Champion to be CROWNED - May 7th, 2006 @9:12am EST. - The ACW Board of Directors have announced this morning that on Lowdown [8] to be held May 14th, 2006, a match to crown the NEW ACW World Champion will take place. The next question is, "Who will be part of this match?" Well that question was also answered. ACW will be holding a HUGE 25-Man battle royal with the winner being crowned the NEW World Champion. Questions were then asked about the END GAME Final. And those questions were also addressed. Mr. Falcone announced that the END GAME final will go off first. The winner will still get a title shot at Highway to Hell. And if it just so happens that the END GAME winners also wins the World title, the last man eliminated from the battle royal will face the champion at the PPV. For any more details on questions, contact the ACW Headquarters. - ACW World Title = VACANT - May 5th, 2006 @9:27am EST. - As of this morning the ACW Board of Directors don't seem to be any closer on a solutions how to crown the next ACW World Champion. Several options have been discussed. Here are a few options being tossed around. The winner of the END GAME Tournament is crowned the new champion. The #1 & #2 contenders square off. 3 names get randomly drawn for a triple threat. The list goes on and on. But ACW Joey Falcone has promised that at Wildcard [3] home of Round #2 of End Game he'll decide how a new ACW World Champion will be crowned.
