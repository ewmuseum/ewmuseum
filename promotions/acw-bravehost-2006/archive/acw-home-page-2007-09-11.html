[detail withheld] Hit Counter Since Jan 29th, 2006 @7:07pm EST - Who's got Next: Pierce Cavanaugh?- Sept. 6th, 2007 @11:20PM EST- : Who is co-holder of the ACW Tag Titles? Pierce Cavanaugh. Who's the current ACW Hardcore Champion? Pierce Cavanaugh. And which ACW title holder came away with the victory in the Champions Challenge tonight at Devastation? That's right Pierce Cavanaugh. Cavanaugh is on a huge hot streak, he's currently the only ACW star holding two titles and he was able to defeat TC Jagger, The Dealer, Shawn Garner & Mr. Saturday Night, to walk away as the Champion of Champions. With all of this in his favor the question is, is Pierce Cavanaugh Next? Is he the next ACW star to get a shot at the ACW World Title? TC Jagger will defend the World title this Tuesday at Remembrence against Cap'n Carnage. And there is a huge chance Cavanaugh might face the winner. But with so many great stars already in line it's only a matter of time till we find out exactly, Who's got Next! - The Devils Disciple's Injured, Alters PPV!!- Sept. 6thth, 2007 @11:17PM EST- : Tonight at Devastation both members of the Devils Disciples were injured. Chaos was put on the shelf by The Outlaws (Moonwolfe & Axel Steel), While Havoc was injured by Cartel members Baller & Dean. All three of these teams are set to square off this Tuesday at the Remembrence PPV with the rights to being the #1 contenders for the tag team titles. But we have learned that Chaos & Havoc won't be able to compete this Tuesday thus dropping from the match. This will put the Outlaws against Baller and Dean. And to spice things up it was announced this match will be a Tornado Rules Cage Match! - Davey Bo & OG Released!- Sept. 6thth, 2007 @11:15PM EST- : As of 5 minutes ago ACW has come to terms on the release of Davey Bo & OG. ACW and it's entire promotion would like to wish both men the best of luck in their careers wherever they may go. - BREAKING NEWS: "Rock Star" Adrian Young RETIRES!- August 27th, 2007 @10:00AM EST- : In a major turn of events that was made official by Adrian Young himself as he sent Mr. Falcone a fax, that he has indeed retired. Effective NOW! Adrian Young a former 3x Cruiserweight Champion, 1x Hardcore Champion, 1x Liberty Champion and 1x World Champion will be greatly missed in ACW. Some people refer to him as the greatest Cruiserweight in ACW history. His feud with Jacob Jacobs over the Cruiserweight title are legendary. His retirement was serious shock to the entire ACW. Many of those in ACW whom have become aware of Adrian's retirement link it to Daydreams where he suffered two major defeats. One was to Mr. Saturday Night where he lost the Cruiserweight title and the other was a loss to TC Jagger where he lost the World Title. Two defeats, two lost titles. Some have even said Adrian has lost his touch and that retiring was a wise move where as others question it. Non the less this is major blow to ACW. ACW would like to wish Adrian well with whatever he does now. Enjoy life like a Rock Star! - Badass Brian gone from ACW!- August 27th, 2007 @9:55AM EST- : Badass Brian officially gone from ACW. He and The Dealer were set to square off last night in a lastman standing match for Brian's US Freedom Title. Prior to the match Badass Brian stormed into CEO Joey Falcone's office and demaned the match be made a loser leaves ACW match. Mr. Falcone accepted his request thus the match was set, Badass Brian Vs. The Dealer in a loser leaves ACW, Last man standing match. After a devastating underhook brainbuster, Brian was unable to beat the 10 count thus making Dealer the new US Freedom champion and removing him from ACW.
