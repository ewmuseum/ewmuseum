PRODIGY's Fantasy Wrestling Central This is the place to find updated weekly information about the various league that make Fantasy Wrestling on PRODIGY the best anywhere on the 'Net. If you're a president of a league, and have infomation you'd like put up for a given week, please send us the info at [detail withheld]. We'll make an effort to have this page updated each week, and we'll try to include all the information you send. We'll also be including information that we gather from around the board. We apologize in advance for any mistakes, please contact us to correct them. To find more information about the leagues listed below, as well as to get involved with the action, be sure to jump to the PRODIGY Wrestling Bulletin Board. Note: Presidents, please send info as often as you'd like about upcoming cards and events. We can't update your league's info if you don't let us know what's going on. We need your input to make sure that the changes we're making are more than just cosmetic, that they include the information that PRODIGY members need to know. As part of the new, updated FW Central, you now have two pages of information, news and fun to browse. The choices are pretty self explanatory. Check out the Headlines page for news and notes on the biggest happenings in Fantasy Wrestling. And be sure to check out the League Info page for up-to-date info on the individual leagues recognized by PRODIGY, and find links to their website. Choose one of the icons below to get started.
