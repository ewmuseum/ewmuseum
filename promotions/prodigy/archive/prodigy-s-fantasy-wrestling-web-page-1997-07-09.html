PRODIGY's Fantasy Wrestling Web Pages are here to bring you the latest news on the best fantasy wrestling area anywhere. Unfortunately as of late, these pages have been defunct. We're hoping to change all that, and to do it, we need your help and participation as you go throughout this site. Let us know what you like, what you don't, and what you want to see to further your FW experience. Below, you'll find information on various leagues, special events, the PRODIGY Unified Championship, as well as much more! We're working to add more and more information, so we can make this an easy way to browse over what the Fantasy topics on the Wrestling BB have to offer. We'll continue to add more info, as well as sound files, and other pages to keep the Fantasy Wrestling Pages timely, topical, and fun. And if you've got additions to add, let us know! If you're not already a member of PRODIGY, but like what you see here and would like to join, it's easy! You can download the PRODIGY Classic software and get a free trial membership by clicking on the highlighted text. And if you're watching this from PRODIGY Internet, well, hurry up and get your FREE PRODIGY Classic membership now! Your place for Fantasy Wrestling on the Web Since February 20, 1996 people have visited this page. FW Central is your Fantasy Wrestling headquarters. Here, we'll list the top stories of the FW world all in one place. Want to know what wrestler is taking Fantasy Wrestling by storm? Want to know what league is hot this week? Want to know who's being attacked by yet another "hostile takeover?" Just check FW Central. How can you get on this list? It's very simple, send us an e-mail at [detail withheld] with your information for the week. We'll be doing our best to include everything you give us each week, and more as well. FW Central is now divided into two pages: Headlines and League Info. Want the big picture? Check out the Headlines. Want up-to-date info straight from the league prez, along with a link to your favorite league's website? Check out League Info. To get your news now.....click on the FW Central icon above and find out the details about the wonderful world of Fantasy Wrestling here on PRODIGY.
