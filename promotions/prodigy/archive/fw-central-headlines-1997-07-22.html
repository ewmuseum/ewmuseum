Updated July 22, 1997 Former Unified Champion Returns Almost two years ago, a former Unified World Champion left the sport with hardly a word. The man who ended the first title reign of current Unified World Champion Mike Randalls returned to the ring at the CSWA's SuperPRIMETIME in Orlando card, taking on Hornet in an apparent exhibition match. JT Tyler is back from his tour of duty outside the United States. The question is: will he begin a tour of another kind. New World Champs Galore! The last ten days have wreaked havoc on the top echelon of the *Professional Wrestling world. At May Maelstrom, the UWA title changed hands in a very controversial decision, as Javid Dones gave way to new UWA World Champion Winston Steele with some help from a drugged towel, and a couple of special women. "Mad" Joe Massacre captured his second concurrent World Title, defeating Rocky Ford Meloneer at Warzone to become the EFW World Champion. Massacre adds the title to his long list of championships, including a current reign as MHW World Champ! The "Strongest Arms In The World" gave way to the "King of All Media" at the CSWA's SuperPRIMETIME in Orlando, with GUNS losing the CSWA World Champion to Troy Windham in a classic match which ended fraught with run-ins, including the final three-count by former Unified World Champ and GUNS' nemesis, Hornet. Also, in just the last two days, the AAWC World Title changed hands once again at World War Games 3. Former AAWC World Champ Steve Masters took the title from Scotty Michaels in a special stipulation added during the match. Can Masters hold on to the title? Or will Michaels find a way to regain and begin his third title reign in the last two months? RWL Chat Card Success By almost all accounts, the RWL Chat Card held on PRODIGY last week was a tremendous success! The TSTs took over the show at will, as RWL officials provided interactive viewers with tremendous live action. Also, ASWL President Matt Rosenthal announced his intent to retire from *Professional Wrestling after a special ASWL final card. Randalls, Ryder and More Along with temperature, things have started to heat up in the world of FW. Mike Randalls continues his reign as Unified World Champion, recently taking on the likes of CSWA World Champion GUNS and EFW World Champion Rocky Ford Meloneer. Who will be his next opponent? Be sure to check out the Unified World Championship pages to let us know your opinions. Along with the Unified Title situation, the Robert W. Ryder Invitational Tag Team Tournament is underway! Roleplay has been collected. Look for first and second round matches to be posted this week. Be sure to check out the web page devoted to the Tournament, available from the Fantasy Wrestling Home Page.
