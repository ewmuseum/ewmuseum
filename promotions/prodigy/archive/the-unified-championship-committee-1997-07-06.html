Unified Championship Rankings Unified Championship Committee Updated July 6, 1997 The Unified Championship Committee was created in the summer of 1996 in order to oversee the PRODIGY Unified World Championship. While the purpose of the Unified Title itself is to provide fun and excitement in the world of fantasy wrestling through defenses and special events, the purpose of the UCC is to monitor and decide those events, as well as additional information like the Unified rankings. The current Unified Championship Committee is composed of four members. The PRODIGY Wrestling BB Fantasy Special Contributors serve on the UCC, and are joined by two additional members from the Wrestling BB. These two member positions will routinely be 'rolled over' in order to allow many members to be involved over time. Special thanks go out to Anthony [name withheld], [name withheld], and [name withheld] for their service on the UCC during the IRONMAN of CHAMPIONS. All three did a tremendous job and deserve a great deal of credit for their involvement. The current members of the Unified Championship Committee: [name withheld] Joe is the current president of the WWX, one of most popular mixed leagues on the board. He is a long-time member of leagues such as the NWC, MHW, and EFW with Joe Massacre. [name withheld] [name withheld] is the president of the long-running AAWC, a hugely popular generated league. He is a long-time member of various leagues, including the CSWA and UWA. Stephen Thomas Some call him "Da Tard", but this Fantasy Special Contributor has served as co-president of the CSWA for over four years on PRODIGY. He is the man behind the madness. Chad Merritt Chad rounds out the list of the three Fantasy Special Contributors. He has served, along with Steve Thomas, as co-president of the CSWA on PRODIGY for over four years. He is currently a member of the MHW, EFW, and other leagues. Each Unified Championship Title match is decided by quality of roleplay, which is voted on by the four UCC members. An additional fifth vote is added by the president of the league hosting the title match. A UCC member may not vote in a match where a wrestler he handles is competing, whether as champion or challenger. All votes include the name of the wrestler chosen to win the match, as well as a ten-point breakdown between the two competing wrestlers. This ten-point score is used to decided how 'close' a match is, as well as to act as a tiebreaker if there becomes a need for one. The UCC is currently working on the Unified Title ranks, as well as gearing up for the Robert W. Ryder Invitational Tag Team Tournament.
