FW Central -- League Info f Updated July 9, 1997 This is your place to find the up-to-date information on the various leagues that operate on PRODIGY's Wrestling BB. From real to generated, roleplay to simulator, it's all here. If you operate a league that is not on PRODIGY, we'll be happy to link your page to the "FW Links" page available from the FW Home Page. The information below is provided by those who know best, those who run the leagues. If no information is yet listed, it means that we haven't heard back from the league president yet. If a league website is available, a hyperlink has been added to the name or logo in the "League" field. Presidents, please submit all your information to [detail withheld]. Please also include your league logo (if available) and the URL to your website if it is not yet listed. In order to allow every league some time at the top, we'll be revolving the leagues around with each update. Each league at the top will be moved to the bottom, etc. Any league with no information will forfeit its place at the top. If you have any suggestions on how to organize this page, let us know. MHW Mixed Simulator and Roleplay President: [name withheld] Upcoming Events: Extreme Nights; Alamo Annihilation Current Info: The MHW features singles and tag team divisions for both men and women wrestlers.
