Unified Championship Rankings Unified Contenders Updated July 22, 1997 Thank you. To those of you who voted and helped to determine the latest *PRODIGY Unified World Championship Rankings. As we mentioned during the last update, we've gotten out of practice in determining and keeping the rankings updated. Previously, only the Unified Championship Committee voted to decide who the top contenders were. But with the overwhelming number of votes we received, we've decided to make it an ongoing procedure.....you vote, we listen. Below are the first results from your votes. We took each set of top ten votes and issued points, much like is done with the AP polls for collegiate sports. Points were issued for votes received, and bonus points for championships held. Major championships have been listed next to contenders as of time of posting this list. Please advise us via e-mail if any listings are incorrect or incomplete. The 'championship' listings will not be changed until the next rankings are posted. Under the rankings, you'll find a link to a page much like you saw here last time. It will allow you to input your top ten rankings for the current period. Without any further adieu....here they are.
