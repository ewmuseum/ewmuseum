Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Volunteer State Wrestling describes itself, in an information packet of 12 May 1999, as a reopening: its previous run had folded once its operator lost e-mail access, and this packet relaunches it under the same name. Five championships were named at the reopening — a World Championship, an Americas Championship, a World Tag Team Championship, a World Featherweight Championship, and a Southern Championship, renamed from an earlier “TV title” to fit the promotion's own regional identity. VSW's fictional staff named an owner, Michael Carwile, a ring announcer, Robert Bowen, and two commentators, John Hamilton and Dan Gibbons.

A surviving broadcast of “Southern Showdown,” VSW's weekly card, opens with “Excellent” Arthur Essex arriving as the new World Heavyweight Champion, freshly crowned at an event called True Grit '99, and introduces a new on-screen Commissioner, Gina Carwile, over the commentators' audible skepticism about a woman taking the job. Gina unveils a new championship on the spot, the VSW Hardcore title, and presents it to “The One Man Riot” Alain Fiscus, who had demanded the belt at True Grit after a hardcore feud with Hardcore Carey. She then announces the night's main event, a five-man elimination match among Jin Tao, Shaun Chaos, Scotty Starlight, Raleigh Waffle, and Eric Justice, will also crown VSW's first Southern Champion, and closes by publicly confronting World Champion Arthur Essex over his conduct at True Grit.

No further Southern Showdown, and no closure notice, survives in this corpus. How the elimination match for the Southern Championship ended, or how the Gina Carwile—Arthur Essex confrontation played out, isn't known.
