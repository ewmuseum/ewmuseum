VSW Southern Showdown

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<A white limousine is slowly pulling into the arena's parking area, and slows to a halt just in front of the camera, the right passneger door of the car almost completely filling the screen. The driver quickly gets out and runs over to the door in front of us, and opens it allowing Robyn, wearing a navy blue business jacket, skirt and 2 inch heels, and with her red hair behind her neck in a low ponytail, to step out. She stands a few feet away from the car awaiting the next occupant to exit the vehicle, and it's none other than the new VSW World Heavyweight Champion "Excellent" Arthur Essex wearing a white tuxedo jacket, black dress pants, white shirt and black bow tie. He's holding the World Title belt in both hands, and when he stands upright he drapes it over his right shoulder....>
[DAN GIBBONS:] THE WORLD CHAMP IS HERE!!! YES!!! <Essex obviously couldn't hear those remarks, yet he still smirks into the camera as if on cue as the scene fades to black and Southern Showdown begins...>
[The scene opens up... We see nothing more than a black screen... The sound of "WAR PIGS" by OZZY OSBOURNE begins playing... After each strong guitar stroke, the screen flashes... The first flash shows a VSW logo... But only for a second...]

OZZY OSBOURNE: WHY SHOULD THEY GO OUT TO FIGHT... THEY LEAVE THEIR OLD TO THE BOAR, YEAH!!! [Dylan Rapture pins an opponent... And then, Scottie Extreme makes an entrance to the ring with the fans on their feet...]

OZZY OSBOURNE: NO MORE _WAR PIGS_ OF THE POW-ER... AND DOES GOD HAS STRUCK THE HOUR!!! [Now, we see the VSW World Heavyweight Champion, Arthur Essex, holding the

Then, "Blue on Black" by the KWSB begins to play throughout the arena. A few fans cheer, but a lot of the male gender start to boo, as the new Commissioner of the VSW makes _her_ way down to the ring. And she's carrying something that looks like a Championship belt...
She looks around to the fans and blows them a kiss, as she walks up the ringsteps...] JOHN HAMILTON: FOLKS, THAT'S YOUR NEW COMMISSIONER!!! DAN GIBBONS: PLEASE DON'T REMIND US!!! [Mrs. Carwile enters the ring wearing a very suffisticated blue outfit. Her hair is done very well and is looking better than ever. In her hand, she clutches the belt, as she waves to the fans, as she pulls a microphone out of her pocket. The music then fades and she begins to speak...]
GC: I think everyone would agree when I say this... True Grit was a _huge_ success! [The fans pop a little bit, but reluctant to get on the _woman's_ side...] [DG:] Up until she was announced the Commissioner! [JH:] Shut up, Dan! [Gina speaks again...] GC: Now, look... I know that I'm a woman. And I know that you people think that I can't get the job done. You don't think I can do the job at all. But
I'm gonna show you that I can. And it all starts _tonight!_
[Bigger pop...] GC: Lets take a look at a little footage that we got this week... Take a look at the jumbo screen up there and watch this.
[The fans turn their attention to the jumbo screen. It flickers to life, as we see a few men making something. We see a sheet of gold with some design on it, a leather belt, and a few work tables... Now, the screen changes. We see
Gina Carwile signing some papers and handing them over to another man. After a few more seconds, we see Gina Carwile holding a VSW Championship belt. On the title, it reads, "VSW HARDCORE CHAMPION." There's a logo on the belt with dripping blood and all. The belt looks very cool and very similar to the one that she's holding in her hand... Now, the camera shifts back to her in the ring, as the footage is done...]
GC: That right there was a behind the scenes clip of what it took to get
_this!_ [Gina holds up the Hardcore title... The fans pop a little more...] GC: At True Grit, Alain Fiscus said he wanted it. You fans said you wanted it. So, it's here, and _I_ made it happen. My first line of work right here and now is this... So, Alain Fiscus, get out here and come get _your_ belt!
["Tapeworm" by Pigface begins to play.] [CROWD POPS HUGE!] ["The One Man Riot" Alain Fiscus comes out into view, wearing his black wrestling tights with anarchy symbols on the hips, a "Nirvana: Incesticide"
t-shirt, and that damn barbed wire necklace. His hair is short, platinum blonde, and uncombed. He pauses, raises his fists in the air, and the crowd yell "ONE MAN RIOT!"]
[Fiscus makes his way to the ring. He smirks at Carwille, and grabs a mic.] ONE MAN RIOT: Ya know, I must say.. the prez has good taste in women! [Gina blushes and smiles.] ONE MAN RIOT: At True Grit, I made a statement, and demanded the SLAUGHTER of
Hardcore Carey a slaughter for the VSW Hardcore Title.. and here it is, fresh off the printing press, and ready to be delivered. I do say, I like how you're running things so far, Gina, and despite what that english jackass Essex says,
I'm proud to have a woman as a boss.
[Fiscus changes the subject.] ONE MAN RIOT: And as for you, Cameron. Ya see this? [Fiscus holds the Hardcore Title high in the air.] ONE MAN RIOT: This little trinket is what you're fighting me for tonight. You think you've got what it takes, do ya? You wanna lay out a challenge to the
Hardcore Prodigy, and at that a hardcore match? Son, I'm about to defend this son of a bitch successfully, and I'll be breaking your bones while I'm at it!
I'll burn your little Country Club to the ground, I'll rape and pillage your hopes and dreams. I am the most hardcore man to ever walk the face of the earth. Not Carey, not some kid named Shane Alexander, no one but _ME_! The One Man Riot, Alain Fiscus. So Cameron, when it all comes down to it, and the facts at the bare mininum.. they'll be laid straight.
I'll be the victor.. and you'll be the victim! [Fiscus throws the mic to Gina, holds the VSW Hardcore Title high in the air, and the crowd pops. Fiscus then makes his exit...]
GC: Ladies and gentlemen, that was _your_ VSW Hardcore Champion. It's official, and it's in the books...
...But now we get down to the _real_ business of the night. [Carwile's face looks very serious now...] GC: First of all... I wanna discuss the Main Event match. The Five Man
Elimination match where anything goes. No holds barred. It's been rumored all week long that there's a stipulation in this match...
...And the rumor's _true._ The Main Event match that involves Jin Tao, Shaun Chaos, Scotty Starlight,
Raleigh Waffle, and Eric Justice...
..._Will_ be for the VSW Southern Championship! [The fans pop!] GC: There _has_ to be a winner and there _will_ be a Champion crowned here
_tonight_ in Tulsa!
[POP!!!] GC: But that's not it. No, I have some more to say. And namely, to _you_... ..._Arthur Essex!_ [The fans boo at the mention of his name...] GC: I've seen the tape of True Grit. I heard all your comments before and after the event. As well as, _during_ the event...
...And I'm _not_ happy at all with you. Shrug it off. Yeah, go ahead. I'm the boss, Mr. Essex, _not_ you. And tonight, whenever you decide to make your appearance... I'll be out here to have a little talk, face to face. I have a few things to get off my chest.
And you'll know when I'm done that I'm not playin'... And that I'm the one and only _boss_ around here.
See you then. Thank you very much. ["Blue on Black" begins to play, once again. The fans give her a big round of applause this time, as she leaves the ring...]
JH: WOW!!! THE RUMORS WERE TRUE!!! DG: New Southern Champion tonight! JH: And Mrs. Carwile is gonna confront Arthur Essex tonight! DG: She's in for it. Essex runs the show now! JH: We'll just have to see about that! Folks, we've got a lot of show to get to! Lets step out and we'll get right down to the ring for our opening match of the night! Don't go away!
