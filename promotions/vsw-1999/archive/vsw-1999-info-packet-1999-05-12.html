Yes, I took over the IWC about a month ago and because of James Malloy, the federation was shut down. But I know many of you wanted it to stay open and many of you have asked me about it. But the only way I can get a federation going without an harrassment is to open one myself. So, here it is.
The VSW does have history. It was open before and was forced to shut down because I lost eMail access. But it's back and it's up to you if it stays or not. I need writers, I need wrestlers, basically, I need all I can get. The
VSW is here and keep on readin' to find out more. [ I I . - R U L E S ] There's not very many rules that you'll need to follow. Just a few. Regular member rules... And of coarse, flash rules. Here they go -
A - M E M B E R + R U L E S This part is very simple. You must flash for your matches [See Flash Rules]. No whining or crying about a decision that is made by the President. All decisions are final. You can do a strategy along with your flashes. That always helps. You can even ask to do an in-ring for the card. An in-ring does
_NOT_ count as a flash, but as an extra. It helps! And if you want to, you can even do a commercial for your wrestler's merchandise. You'll be given extra points for that too. Strategies and commercials help win matches... But most of all... Flashes!
B - F L A S H + R U L E S Flash rules are very simple as well. There _IS_ a limit for flashes. You can only do _TWO_ flashes per _MATCH_. That's it. Anymore will not count. That keeps it pretty much fair. That keeps some from spamming others that don't have as much time. And most of all... It really shows who the best flasher is. But with the commercials, strats, and flashes... You have many opportunities to gain the points you need to win. But your flashes will play the biggest part in winners, just as it should. And it is a flash sheet federation, so send them to me only <
[detail withheld] >. The flash deadline will be Saturday night at midnight. The strategies will be due by Friday night at midnight. <Deadlines could change.>
[ I I I . - C A R D S ] There will be _ONE_ card a week. It's on Monday night. It's big and it's live, and in live format. It's a big show for the VSW. Angles, title defenses, and huge announcements will all go down on this one. The deadline for flashes will be Saturday night at midnight. The strat deadline for this card is Thursday afternoon at five o' clock. Don't forget, strats can be very important. ;-) The show will be called VSW Southern Showdown!
[ I V . - V S W + C H A M P I O N S H I P S ] What's a fed without Champions? Of coarse you gotta have belts! There are a total of five Championships. Four singles and one tag. Below are the belts...
A little bit about them and all you need to know. A - W O R L D + C H A M P I O N S H I P Like any other federation out there, the highest ranked Championship title is the World Heavyweight Championship. The very best wrestler in the VSW will hold this one... And only the best out of the rest will get opportunities to win it. This belt will most likely be defended less than every other week or so. The World Championship titles will be the least defended belt in the federation seeing how it's the most presteigious. The World Heavyweight
Champion will headline the VSW's pay-per-views, nine times out of ten. Unless there's a special match that would rank above the title match. ;-)
B - A M E R I C A S + C H A M P I O N S H I P This is the second highest belt that you can win in the VSW. The Americas Championship is similar to the United States Championship... Just with a modified name. This belt will be defended more than the World title... But probably not even once a week. It really just depends.
C - W O R L D + T A G + T E A M + C H A M P I O N S H I P These belts will be held by the single _BEST_ tag team in the VSW. This is the World Championship, but for the tag teams. Well, this one was never given out the first time around because we didn't have too many tag teams. But I hope that changes this time around. If you don't like singles or aren't too great at them, give the tag team division a try. I need tag teams!
D - W O R L D + F E A T H E R W E I G H T + C H A M P I O N S H I P This belt is for the wrestlers that are right at or under the 230 pound mark. This Championship is for the smaller guys that might have trouble in the heavyweight division. This division should be more than action-packed and interesting... Those little guys can really fly! ;-) This belt was never given out either. Though I hope I get some little guys to fight for it. ;-)
E - S O U T H E R N + C H A M P I O N S H I P This is the lowest ranked belt in the VSW. It will be defended the most frequent. Possibly ever single card. It might be the lowest ranked, but the toughest will hold it. It'll be extra hard to hold on to it, due to it being the most defended belt. Don't underestimate this Champion... He will be one of the very best on the roster. This title was called the TV title, but it's changed because of the Southern location of the Volunteer State. :-)
