Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A newsgroup pitch in early December 2000 introduced a three-part structure under one banner: an association meant to supply e-federations with scripts, designs, and roleplaying tutorials; a federation running two weekly shows and building toward a debut supercard; and a league meant to feed talent upward through a monthly battle royal. Only the federation branch was open for business at the time of the pitch, its two other divisions still under construction.

Within days the network dropped its original name for a second one, the recruiting text otherwise repeating itself nearly word for word. The federation branch kept running weekly shows through the renaming and after it, billed toward a supercard timed to the turn of the millennium.

Through the second half of December the network's public voice turned entirely to staffing. A rotating series of notices, each one slightly different from the last, asked for presidents, columnists, commissioners, and match-writers across all three branches, tracking small real-world details along the way: a hosting outage and its repair, a domain name coming due for registration, an opening date circled for the first of January.

No document from the network's own promised opening survives, and nothing records whether the association or the league ever left the planning stage its founding pitch described.
