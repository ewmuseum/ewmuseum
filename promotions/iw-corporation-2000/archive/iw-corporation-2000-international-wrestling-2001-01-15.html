International-Wrestling is proud to announce that its' minor-league, the IWL, has started to have a small influx of wrestlers joining every 2 days, thanks to those who have been reading our ads. However, we need more wrestlers to spice up competition in the league!! As I mentioned, it's a new league so by joining now, you get the chance to become a major-player fast!
Our first-ever show, Tuesday Turmoil, which debuts on 16 January 2001, will determine the holders of the singles' titles. Join now, get a partner and ya could just be the first-ever Tag-Team Champs!! Be the top 8 RPers and you will get the chance to be promoted to the IWF!!
We also require an IWL President & Commissioner. Both will split the results, help maintain the site & come up with angles!! 3 IWL results-writers are also needed so as to ease the burden and ensure everybody has at least one match to write!! In addition to me being the IWF
President, I will also help with the results when I can & be the main storyline-developer, along with IW President, [name withheld]. Once we get the writers we need, we can start Friday Fallout, which will benefit in the development of storylines.
The IWF also needs 2 more results-writer so that we can restart Monday Meltdown. With me, the Commish & another staff-member helping with the results, everything will be split fairly.
We also require IWA staff-members, but we will start advertising for that once the IWA site is complete.
If you are interested, please head over to http://www.geocities.com/int_wrestling and click on the respective links. Thanks in advance for joining & taking time to read this message!! ~IWF President, [name withheld]~
