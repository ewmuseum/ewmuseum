Staff Positions Which Need Filling Up

February 10, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
International Wrestling is proud to announce that its' minor-league, the IWL, has started to have a small influx of wrestlers joining every 4 days, thanks to those who have been reading our ads.
  The IWL doesn't need any wrestlers in a hurry now, as we are nearing our limit of 30 wrestlers. Once we reach the limit of thirty wrestlers for the IWL, and if applications are still coming in, you will be put on the Waiting List. Once a person is promoted to the IWF or leaves, the wrestlers on the Waiting List will take part in a Contracted Carnage, where the winner will in fact get to officially join the IWL.
The IWL has, in addition in the Contracted Carnage, a weekly show called Tuesday Turmoil which usually has around 5 to 8 great matches to boast of. We are also starting our Promotional Pain, which is a show where the top wrestlers in the IWL will face off to see who will be promoted to the IWF. The PP will, as of now, occur twice a month, unless we meet our requirements.
  We require an IWL President & Commissioner. Both will split the results, help maintain the site & come up with angles!! 3 IWL results-writers are also needed so as to ease the burden and ensure everybody has at least one match to write!! In addition to me being the IWF
President, I will also help with the results when I can & be the main storyline-developer, along with IW President, [name withheld]. Once we get the writers we need, we can start Friday Fallout, which will benefit in the development of storylines. Which person wouldn't wanna be a President of a popular & competitive federation? APPLY NOW!!
The IWF also needs 3 more results-writers & a new Commissioner so that we can restart Monday Meltdown. With me, one result-writer & the Commish splitting the results, everything will be split fairly for our current show, Thursday Treachery. Me, the other 2 results-writers, and the Commish will write Meltdown once we get the writers we need.
We are also looking for IWA Columnists & an IWA President. The IWA President must be well-versed in HTML, JavaScript & CGI, as part of his job is to look for CGI Scripts & Javascripts and place them on the site to help those in need of the scripts. You will also be managing several topsites lists. However, since the IWA site is not open yet, e-mail IW
President [name withheld]; the address can be found below. If you are interested, please head over to http://www.geocities.com/int_wrestling and click on the respective links.
