IWW Championship Belts IWW WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP-This is the belt thats awarded to the one person that has shown that he is the Best In The Business. With this title comes alot of hardships and work. You are the top man in the IWW, but you are also the most hunted man in the federation. Trust flies out the door..and freinds quickly becomes enemies. If you have this belt then you will be highlighting the Sunday
Showdown Cards as well as the Pay Per Views..You are the Man..
IWW Immortal Championship-You are the instant number one contendor for the
World Championship. You are the number 2 man in the title scene with the chance to become the man in given time. This is a belt thats cherished and fought for on every Sunday Showdown Card..you win this belt and you become the hardest working man in the IWW
IWW Hardkore Championship-Another IWW Belt thats defended on every single
Sunday Showdown, but in a bunch of different types of matches. All the matches are eXtreme to the fullest, tables,bricks,tables,ladders, you name it and odds are you'll be in it. This belt is not for the weak of heart..its for the true souljahz of the IWW..
IWW Cruiserweight Championship-The Best of the so callled "little men" in the business will be the man under 145 pounds that can beat the rest. These matches are usually high flying with moves that have never been seen.
