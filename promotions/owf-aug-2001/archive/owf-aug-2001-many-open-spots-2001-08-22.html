Online Wrestling Federation: Many Open Spots

22 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is in regards to the OWF: Online Wrestling Federation. I am not the fed head but I am one of the top tier wrestlers in the fed.
Our federation is a booked fed with handwritten results, one card a week guaranteed (Last Man Standing)-- sometimes a surprise second card, one ppv a month (upcoming is "Stable Wars"). We stress quality over quantity. We also stress realism. In other words, if you get hit in the head with a chair a couple of times then chances are you might be unconscious. We also deal with injuries and health percentage levels.
We require a minimum of one rp a week but have no maximums. We allow only one wrestler per handler and only original wrestlers at that.
Don't expect to play real wrestler clones such as "The Big Pump" or
"The Crock" or "The Pebble" or even "Large Sexy". No racism is allowed in rps or OOC. Roleplays are done on a message board so all can see and your opponents can respond.
The better your rps the more air time your program will get as well as the more involved things become. We currently have several belts and have just started a Women's Division but we have no problem if women wish to wrestle men. We don't believe in paper champions and you are expected to rp to defend your belt when in a match for it (unless you chose to vacate or throw the match which has been done).
Very soon the OWF will be moving things to the next level. We are in the midst of some serious stable wars and I strongly suggest getting in now while things are chaotic. Come and add to the carnage!
The email for the OWF Front Office is: [detail withheld] if you have any questions. The website address is as follows: www.angelfire.com/sports/owfproduction/index.html I know you may be thinking "Ewww! An Angelfire fed.!" All I ask is you check it out before deciding. You don't know what you may be missing.
Some of our roster is well-traveled in the e-fed circles and they have all said we are actually one of the better rosters for roleplays. One of our roster, Brendan, actually writes a column for Lords Of Pain -- a wrestling website that tracks WWF and indy feds.
