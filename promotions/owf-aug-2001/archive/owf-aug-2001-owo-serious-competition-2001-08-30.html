OWO Is Looking for Serious Competition

30 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That's right the OWO is looking for more talented wrestlers. We have only 13 on the roster and want to get to 30 quickly! We do have a women's division and we just joined as part of the IWA. We used to be the OWF but after our last PPV Stable Wars, the company underwent a serious restructuring. It is better than ever and all our dead weight on the roster was released so we definitely have openings! We have a weekly card, a monthly PPV and now as part of IWA a Supercard every so many months. We use handwritten results in this original wrestler booked fed. We do use message boards not email for rps. The OWO stresses realism and injuries as well. Getting hit in the head with a chair a couple of times will probably knock you out and leave you with a minor concussion! I highly suggest you check us out before deciding yes or no. The front office can be reached at: [detail withheld] The site address: http://www.owf-productions.co.uk/ I highly suggest checking us out!
