A booked message-board federation of 2001 that stressed realism down to concussions from chair shots, running a weekly Last Man Standing card and stable wars. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This OWF survives in five posts to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy running from 17 August to 14 November 2001, describing a booked federation of handwritten results rather than simmed ones: one card a week, a monthly pay-per-view, one original wrestler per handler, and a strict ban on wrestling real-world knockoffs by another name (its own recruitment posts singled out “The Big Pump,” “The Crock,” “The Pebble” and “Large Sexy” as exactly what it wouldn't take). It also barred racism in and out of character, and ran its roleplays on a message board rather than by e-mail so any opponent could answer in the open.

It stressed realism in its own injuries — two chair shots, its posts warned, would probably leave a wrestler with at least a minor concussion — and refused paper champions, requiring a title holder to actually wrestle to keep the belt rather than vacate or dodge a challenge. Its weekly card was called Last Man Standing; its FTW Champion, Ronin, “the Shadow Warrior” also known as “the Avenging Assassin,” signed one of the recruitment posts himself, promising fresh victims: “Your personal asskicking is here!”

After its August pay-per-view, Stable Wars, the federation restructured and renamed itself the OWO, joining a larger alliance, the IWA, and adding a women's division while it rebuilt its roster from thirteen wrestlers toward a target of thirty. Its site moved from an Angelfire address to owf-productions.co.uk, where its November post points readers for the last time.

No card result, roster list, or title history beyond Ronin's own claimed FTW Championship survives. How the OWO fared once it had rebuilt, and how or when it closed, isn't in the surviving record.
