Vignette in Time: Tandem

Date: Today GCW was a decidedly odd place, Seymour Almasy decided. One could never know what would happen there. That was the only explanation the Final Fantasy had for being forced to team up with Rich Rollins, especially after Super Series, where the hated Renegade had captured the PTC Global Championship, a title that Almasy had held more than any other man or woman. Under other circumstances, Seymour knew, Rollins/Almasy was a dream team, a tandem of two of GCW’s best, heck, PTC’s best. If they were on the same page, they could develop offense, get to know one another’s styles, and even come up with a cool team name. Of course, that wasn’t happening. Not even close. Rollins and Almasy hated one another with the fiery passion of a thousand suns. They had met for two straight GCW PPVs, and for two months straight, had failed to come out with a winner. That told Seymour one thing. They were both nearly even. Sure, they wrestled very different styles, and had very different attitudes, but they were both very, very capable in the confines of the squared circle. Or out of it, for that matter. When it came right down to it, though, Seymour knew exactly why this match had been made. GCW wanted fireworks. They wanted to see two hated enemies have to team, and see how bad the carnage would be. Really though, that would be up to the new PTC Global Champion, because Seymour was willing to put aside hatred for one night. Give the world the sight of what could be, if they ever so chose. Show them the force that two men of their caliber could bring to bear as a tandem. But that was unlikely. Rollins ran off of evil emotions. While emotional himself, Almasy disliked giving into hatred. Were it possible, he would simply compete to prove his strength and worth, a time-honored warrior in the Battle Arena. But of course, even if he could, other warriors would come to knock him off. And honor would not be as important to them. So WorldWide would be a glimpse at the possibilities of an Otherworld, a land where Seymour and Richard could get along. A land as contrary to fact as they came.
