Date: Timeless The heavens broke above and the teardrops of the angels began to fall. Each droplet resonating by ripples through the puddles on the ground was another note in the requiem for the lost. The shadows of the former existence of the world linger around listening for a different tune, maybe something that would bring glee and happiness to their realm of the unknown. Rain is always a sad tune, ringing out like the strike of a bow upon strings of a violin. Aside from the faint harmony of the rain, there is another tune mixed in. This polyphonic song is a cry from the vengeful angels, ones whom have a hate of the world. A conduit for the angels to put their hatred into physical meaning came from an existence who believes he has not been placed on this earth for his sorrow, but for a meaning, a purpose. Every step he takes is one that much closer to the meaning that he has been so desperately trying to see. His foot lands inside the very staff of the heavenly song, and contorts all reason into an unrecognizable song to any musician’s ears. His song is feared by man, for it is not a song man agrees with to hear. Every drip of blood from the never-ending river of death is just a byproduct of weakness and susceptibility to the darkness every human soul is capable of. The purification of the soul is God’s will and the willing of His followers. A man who brings purification by death is not a missionary or clergyman, but a mercenary for the Almighty Himself. This mercenary carries the burden of loss and has the unattainable goal to find all that is good and all that is bad, only to tip the scale with an iron fist. The last culprit to fall into the shades of darkness broke open the heavens above. Revealing the instruments the angels play as they tear onto this world only gives way for vengeance. The threat of loss falls just shy of loss itself, both of which make the human figure fall to its knees in utter bewilderment of logic and/or physical action. Lack of either results in an extreme altered realm of time. Something that catches up with every evil. Only a few years after the Almighty sent the sign to the young boy did the purpose start to set in. Tracing the cracked asphalt down to the center of the city led him to the place of worship that would set him on the God given path of the ancients. The smoldering hot cross pulled from the ruins of his only know realm of happiness now lay cold around his neck at all times. Still pristine in condition and shine, the cross leads with the intent of a passionate action. A crime of passion is still a crime, but when there is the meaning of Godly intent, it is no more a crime than falling to your knees in prayer. The disparity in death by the hands of a criminal and the Almighty’s “mercenary” of Godly vengeance is that when you die by the hands of a chosen man, there is no judgment, for their death is the decision.
