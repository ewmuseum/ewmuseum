Smuggler's run Part 3

Date: Not all that long ago I still couldn't believe what just happened. I stood there in shock and wondered what the hell I should do. Lex the Marauder and I had been having a good old fashioned fistfight on the top level of a spaceship parking complex on a planet that had been dead for nearly half a century. I thought we were the only two living beings on the planet. I was wrong. He had me down and I saw my life flash before my eyes as he raised the rusted sublight drive manifold above his head and I could swear he was going to bash my skull in. I knew Marauders weren't killers, but considering the damage we'd done to each other, he looked like he had gained a taste for blood. We were both bleeding after the nearly half hour fight, my very human side was bleeding red, his totally Marauder physiology was bleeding his bright white blood. Then someone hit him. With a transport. It was quick and I barely had time to regisiter the sight of his body tumbling to the ground before I realized that there was someone else on the planet that had just mowed him down with a transport of some kind. I watched as the vehicle disappeared down to the lower levels of the parking complex before I realized it wasn't coming back. I was torn over what to do. I wanted to beat Lex, but not like this. Should I check on him or should I find out who tried to kill him just now? Was he even dead? I stood up and after my vision stopped swimming, I made my way over to his prone body. I coulnd't even tell if he was breathing. I looked around wondering what to do, then I saw the unmistakable sight of a ship taking off. Must be our mystery driver. Well, I suppose I'd eventually find out who it was later. I looked back down at Lex and wondered what to do with him. I couldn't just leave him here, then againg carting around the corpse of a dead Marauder probably wasn't a good idea either. I nudged him with my foot. I knelt down next to him and put my ear to his chest before realizing I didn't know where his heart was, and even if I did, what a regular heartbeat for a Marauder was. I evnetually picked him up and carried him to his ship, after taking him inside I looked over the control panel and realized I couldn't read the Marauder language. I didn't know if he had left the life support on, and didn't know what switch to hit to insure that it was on, and that I wasn't locking him in an airtight ship. I walked back over to my own ship, snatching up my flight jacket, weapons and my medallion. I looked over the damage to my ship. I looked back at Lex's ship. 'What the hell? Why not, if he's dead it's not like he's gonna need them.' I said out loud to myself. I grabbed a tool kit and started to canabalize his ship. I took his hyperdrive manifold, his shield generator, and just for the hell of it I took his sublight drive as well. Never know when I'd need a spare. It took me nearly four hours to strip the old hyperdrive and shield generator out of my ship and replace them with the ones from Lex's ship. I stored the sublight drive in my spare parts cabinet in the ship's hold. I checked on Lex one last time, I still couldn't tell if he was dead or alive. 'I didn't want this.' I said to his prone body. 'If you're dead...well I don't know what happens to your people after you die. If you have a heaven or hell like we have back on Earth, but if you do have those, I hope you've gone to your people's version of heaven. You were good Lex, just not good enough.' I left the ship closing the hatch as I left, knowing enough about any ship to know that the panel next to the hatch was the open/close panel. I got back into my StarSkimmer and started up the take off sequence, I spared one last look at Lex's ship and wondered for the last time if he was dead or alive. I also wondered how long it would be before anyone realized he was missing, and how long it would be before someone new came after me. As I soon found out, not long at all.
