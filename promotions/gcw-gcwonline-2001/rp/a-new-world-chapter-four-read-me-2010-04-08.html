A New World – Chapter Four: Read Me.

8 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/08/10 From the pages of the “Black Diamond”~ Since I was brought back to life so many things have changed; at the same time somethings have not changed at all. The world we live in is plagued for destruction and has no real chance for survival. People are more corrupted now than they ever have been. Though, the worst part is, some of them believe they are doing the right thing. I can relate in the confusion some people have when forced to make a choice that you don't know to be right or wrong. Death was so much easier, because at least then you knew what to expect. If you led a good life your soul would be absolved and raised into Heaven where all of God's Angels would welcome you. On the flip-side, if you led a life of hate you would descend into the home of God's most beautiful Angel of all, where he would punish you for eternity for your sins. However in today's world none of it really matters, because hardly anyone believes in God, and no one is afraid of the Devil. Everyone considers them self to be an Atheist, but what they don't understand is that an Atheist will never descend nor be absolved. Remaining in Purgatory can be worse than Hell, because you are left with nothing and emptiness is all that will be allowed. Everyone should believe, because all three of those realms exist. I have spent my time in Purgatory, I have battled through the circles of Hell, and have walked past the gates of Heaven. For all of the wrongs in my life I know I do not belong in any of them, I deserve much worse than Hell. When inside Hell, the biggest fear to overcome is the ones inside of you, because the layers of Hell are manifested by your fears, sins, and memories. I spent so much time trying to do what I believed to be right, that I neglected the most important parts of life, and because of that I'm nothing more than a lost soul. My brother Ron... We had some good times together, and gave people some memories they would never forget. Though I guess some of those memories you kept for yourself, even after all of the pain I put you through over the years. I had no idea you were Dagda, I just knew I had a mission to complete to insure that my little girl would be okay. If I knew it was you Ron I can't say I wouldn't have done the same, but you of all people would understand that right? I'm not sure how this story is going to end, but I do know you should not wait for me wherever your soul is resting. To anyone who reads this final post, please understand this not just a good bye from me, but a map to the end of life. A map with no correct destination, but plenty of warnings for the path you take. I'm going to save my daughter now, and I'm going to bring it all to an end. Very soon the words that SurReal once spoke will become true. “Donny Diamond is dead.”
