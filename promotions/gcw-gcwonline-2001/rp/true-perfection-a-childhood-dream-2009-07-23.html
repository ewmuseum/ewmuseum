True Perfection: A Childhood dream

23 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/23/09 TRUE PERFECTION: A CHILDHOOD DREAM……….. Wednesday, July 22, 2009 Miami, Florida (Steele Mansion) (The camera comes on as Bishop Steele is sitting on the couch watching the Tour De France. The camera picks up Bishop commenting about the race) Bishop Steele: “Damn this takes a lot of strength and determination. I’m glad I live in this life of luxury. I’m way to pretty to even think about doing some stupid shit like this.” (Alexis walks into the room holding a old, dusty box. She speaks) Alexis Devon: “Hey baby I was down in the storage room with Jacques and we found this old box with your name on it. I didn’t know what you wanted to do with this box so I brought it to you.” Bishop Steele: “Whoa baby what happened to your hair seriously?” Alexis Devon: “I have been downstairs cleaning asshole. Here take this box jackass.” (Alexis throws the box onto the floor and walks away pissed. Bishop is chuckling as she walks away. Alexis comments from a distance.) Alexis Devon: “Oh and be sure you make a pallet on the couch tonight, because you’re not sleeping with me tonight.” Bishop Steele: “Come on babe, I was just kidding. It was a joke.” (Alexis slams the door to the downstairs really hard. Jacques stands there with a smirk on his face as Bishop looks in the box.) Bishop Steele: “What Jacques? You got something to say too?” Jacques Ramona: “No not at all sir. Just was wondering if I shall get the blankets out of the linen closet is all sir.” (Jacques chuckles after making the comment.) Bishop Steele: “Not funny at all Jacques. Not funny at all.” (Jacques walks away and leaves Bishop alone to look into the box. Bishop pulls out a picture of him and his friend Chris when they were about 7 yrs old. They are dressed in what looks like pajamas and wearing the old plastic wrestling belts that you can buy at the store. They are posing for the picture. He then pulls out another picture of himself and his dad at one of the local wrestling matches held in his hometown. Bishop is smiling wearing a Master of Disaster shirt. Bishop smiles as he stares at the picture. He then pulls out a story that he wrote in when he was in the 4th grade. He reads the story.) 4th Grade Story: When I grow up I want to wrestle like I watch on TV. I mean those guys are really cool and just seem so life like when they do their moves on the opponents. My favorite wrestler would have to be the Master of Disaster. I have liked him from the first moment I saw him do his move called the Natural Disaster. It is a flipping pile driver. My best friend Chris wants to be a wrestler to. We sometimes spend the night with each other and just play wrestling games or I bring my wrestling belt over and we attempt to wrestle each other for the title like we are real wrestlers. He wins sometimes, but for the most part I always seem to stay the champion. We promised one another that we would become the greatest tag team that ever wrestled in history. I really hope someday that I will be on TV and have lots of fans cheering and chanting my name. I also hope one day that I will actually hold a real wrestling belt; of course I would have to get stronger simply because those belts look heavy and I wouldn’t want to scratch it. I also wish my father was here to see me achieve my dream. He used to always take me to matches and shows because he knew how much I loved wrestling and he loved to make me happy. When he and my mom got a divorce he never took me to anymore matches. He just disappeared and never came back. I really don’t see him anymore, but my dream to be a wrestler still exists. I just want to be able to show him that I made it as wrestler. Someday I will be a wrestler and when that day comes I will be on top of the world. (Bishop sits down the story and begins to remember the days when he played with Chris. As he sits there Alexis comes back upstairs and sits down next to him. She speaks) Alexis Devon: “So what was in the box?” Bishop Steele: “It was all my old pictures of my friend Chris and me when we used to pretend to be wrestlers like on TV. I also have pictures of my father and me when he used to take me to local wrestling shows. It’s weird how that was my dream growing up and then I took a different route to get where I’m at today.” Alexis Devon: “Well at least you can say that you achieved your dream. Most people can’t say that. It might not have been the way you wanted to, but you did it and that is all that matters.” Bishop Steele: “I guess you’re right.” Alexis Devon: “What are you going to do with all this stuff?” Bishop Steele: “Well I’m going to put it up somewhere so that I know where it is. Then I’m going to call my mom and see if she remembers Chris’s mom’s number.” Alexis Devon: “Why are you trying to contact him?” Bishop Steele: “Well he was my friend and this was his dream to. So I’m going to see if he wants to come see me wrestle a WW114.” Alexis Devon: “Well that will be nice. I’m going back downstairs to finish. I will be back up here later.” Bishop Steele: “Okay sweetie.” (Alexis walks away and heads back downstairs. She shuts the door. Bishop stands up and heads upstairs. He walks into the back office room where his fireproof safe is. He opens messes with the combination before eventually opening the safe. He puts the box in there and locks the safe back. He then walks back out of the room and back downstairs. He grabs his Blackberry and dials his mother’s number. He waits for an answer.) Bishop’s Mom: “Hello?” Bishop Steele: “Hey mom how is everything going?” Bishop’s Mom: “Oh hey baby. Well you know me I’m doing great. How are you and Alexis doing?” Bishop Steele: “We are doing great actually. She is downstairs cleaning right now.” Bishop’s Mom: “And why are you not down there helping her out?” Bishop Steele: “Mom come on now don’t start please.” Bishop’s Mom: “Boy I raised you better than that. You better get down there and help her.” Bishop Steele: “Alright mom I will I promise. Why do you always baby her anyways?” Bishop’s Mom: “Well she is a special person and she deserves to be treated special and I know you love her, but you need to show her more.” Bishop Steele: “Yeah I know mother. Anyways I was wondering if you had Chris Barton’s mother number.” Bishop’s Mom: “Why do you want his mother’s number?” Bishop Steele: “Well I found a box with all my old pictures of me and Chris when we dressed up as wrestlers and I thought I would try to get a hold of him to see if he wanted to see me wrestle at WW114.” Bishop’s Mom: “Well I have Chris’s number too.” Bishop Steele: “Why do you have Chris’s number?” Bishop’s Mom: “Well he went to college and became a lawyer and now he works here at a local law firm.” Bishop Steele: “What’s his number?” Bishop’s Mom: “[detail withheld].” Bishop Steele: “Thanks mom and I love you.” Bishop’s Mom: “I love you too baby and be careful at WW114.” Bishop Steele: “I will mom I promise. Bye.” (Bishop hangs up the phone and then dials the number. He waits for an answer.) Chris: “Hello?” Bishop Steele: “Chris, this is Bishop Steele.” Chris: “Oh wow Bishop. How are you man?” Bishop Steele: “I’m doing great actually. How about yourself?” Chris: “Just working and doing my part putting criminals away.” Bishop Steele: “Well that is good to hear. Do you remember when we were little and we wanted to be wrestlers when we got older?” Chris Barton: “Ha ha yeah I remember. Master of Disaster was our favorite wrestler.” Bishop Steele: “Yeah we used to dress up and wrestle each other. That was a blast.” Chris Barton: “Yeah it was. Man those were the good ole days.” Bishop Steele: “Well the reason why I brought that up is because I found some pictures of us when we was little and I was reminiscing about those days. I was wondering if you wanted to come see me wrestle at WW114.” Chris Barton: “You’re a wrestler seriously?” Bishop Steele: “Yeah I actually completed my dream.” Chris Barton: “You know I would love to come see you wrestle. It would be a great honor.” Bishop Steele: “Well what I will do is I will get you plane tickets and front row seats at the event. You will sit next to my fiancé at the event.” Chris Barton: “Okay I will tell my wife to get everything ready and we will definitely be there.” Bishop Steele: “Alright well I will see you there and it was good talking to you again.” Chris Barton: “Yeah it was great talking to you too.” (Bishop says bye and then hangs up the phone. He then leans back on the couch. He has a smile on his face as he sits there for awhile. He then gets up and heads downstairs. The camera fades………………………..) Thursday, July 23, 2009 11:30 p.m. (The camera comes back on inside the home of Bishop Steele. As the camera is filming Bishop is standing out on the balcony looking out at the ocean view from their home. He gazes at the ocean as he speaks about his upcoming match.) Bishop Steele: “You know it seems surreal as I stand here and look out at the ocean. I mean never in my life would I have ever thought that I would have all these luxuries I have now. I have a beautiful fiancé, a nice home, a multibillion dollar company and a second career that has always been a dream of mine. Growing up when I was a kid I used to watch wrestlers on TV and I always told my parents that I would one day be on TV like those guys and have fans cheering for me. My parents would laugh, but always tell me nothing is impossible. I took that comment and strived for my dream. Now it all has come to past and my childhood dream has come true. At WW114 I will wrestle against two other competitors that are trying to achieve the one thing that everyone in this business wants. That is a title around their waist. They may want to same thing as me but they don’t have the determination like I have to reach that goal. These two men won’t stand in my way of reaching my dream they will be just mere stepping stones in path on my way to the top of the ladder. Come WW114 I will prove myself to the entire GCW roster that I’m a force to be reckoned with and Joe Milone and Alex Colt better prepare to see why they call me True Perfection.” (Bishop sits down in the chair on the balcony and puts his feet up. He leans back and just enjoys the moonlight night. The camera fades away with a lasting image of waves crashing into the sandy beach……………………..)
