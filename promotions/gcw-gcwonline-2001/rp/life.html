Date: today :: prologue :: Life is hard. Throughout our lives, those three little words seem to slip through the thin gap between our lips on more than one occasion. Whether it be the fact that we were passed up for a promotion that we were sure we were going to get, the fact that we were let go due to downsizing in the workplace a week before that month's mortgage was due, and it could even be possible that you walked in to find your significant other with a mouthful of someone else. Thousands, if not millions, of different possibilities exist in our world today. These three words seem to be a common bond between every single person in this world, a bond that no one pays attention to. It could be that you look over at your neighbor and notice them unloading a brand new entertainment center from their truck, maybe you notice the affection that they share for one another, an affection you do not show to your significant other. Whatever the case may be, you disregard the fact that everyone's life is hard -- that one common bond you share with that neighbor -- and envy him for a life you wish you had. You wish it was you with that brand new entertainment center, instead of the twenty seven inch television that just doesn't seem to look as sharp as it did when you first bought it eight years ago. You wish it was you who could hold your loved one close, whispering all the erotic things you plan on using to please them later on in the evening. You wish it was them envying you for the things you had, instead of the other way around. Yet, you never take the time to ask them how things really are in their lives. You don't realize that they had to save money for two years to be able to afford a new entertainment center to replace the eight year old piece of crap that just didn't have as sharp a picture as it did when they first bought it. You don't realize that an affair the husband had with his secretary brought him and his wife closer together when they took the time to talk to one another and share their feelings, to open up and discuss what they felt was missing in their own relationship. It's easy to envy another person for the life you think they have, but when you sit back and really think about it... Their lives are just as fucked up as your own. :: Present :: Dangerous Games... If you would've asked me a year ago what I thought my chances were of making it into the finals of this tournament were, I probably would have told you that I felt pretty good about them. I may have even been a bit more cocky and told you I planned on winning the whole damn thing. Thing is, times have changed. And not for the better. Things were nice about a year ago. The future of so many of the new signees of Global Championship Wrestling looked bright. A ladder plated with gold for them to climb toward success was placed before them. The majority of those guys jumped past the first rung, eager to prove themselves to the people in charge. Those same people have since came and gone. Skipping past that first rung only to have their foot slip, falling flat on their faces when they realized that things were not going to be as easy as they thought. So they left. They grabbed their stuff, put on their toughest face and walked back the same way they came. They didn't need this place, and they would prove that they were better somewhere else. All of them but one... Me. I figured I'd tough it out, work my way up the ranks and proving myself to the men in charge through hard work and determination. It worked. It wasn't too long before I was given a chance at the Light Heavyweight Title, winning it and defending it whenever I was asked. Which in turn led to a Television Title match. I won that one too. Life was pretty good. Or at least that was what I thought. I knew I was having some pretty good success. Winning matches, carrying two titles, pretty much kicking ass and taking names. All the while competing in GTT5 against some pretty tough competitors... Main Event Champions in their respective circles. That was when life became hard. I was eliminated from the tournament by a fellow GCW Alumni, hard pressed on allowing no one to stand in his way of winning the tournament. We took full advantage of our working relationship to wear down our adversaries, Paddy O'Shea and Jonathan Winters. Then, as I dropped Jonathan Winters with The Final Solution, the unexpected happened. Rich Rollins blindsided me, eliminating me from the match before dropping down and doing the same to Winters. That was the beginning of the end. After that, I lost the Television Title, followed by the Light Heavyweight Title, and before long there wasn't one match I was able to win. I was in... No, I am in a major slump. A win here... A win there... It's all fine and dandy, but not when you cannot seem to get the job done when there is something important on the line... when the moment the stakes are raised your throat tightens to the point where you can barely breathe, and once again you are on the losing end of things. That is, when you're not getting beat down by five or six people because you don't agree with their ideal way of life. A life that seems harder than what it should be. A life that was once so easy. A life you no longer want to live. Life... it happens to the best of us.
