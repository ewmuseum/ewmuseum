Back Together?

26 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/26/08 Thursday, June 26, 2008 12:30 p.m. Sometimes it sucks to be James von Doom? Especially after a break up like the one I had before driving home. As I arrive back into Las Vegas after driving there from West Yellowstone, apparently people are already talking crap about me as it pertains to my involvement in two championship title defenses at Super Show IX. My cell phone has received numerous text messages over the past few hours on the way here. So when I stop to fill up my car with gas before heading home, I pull out my phone from my right front pocket and open it up to see that I have ten text messages on my phone. These are some of the more interesting ones that are either personal or related to my TEAM matches coming up: From: Art Did you hear what Randalls said about you? He called you a retarded monkey. Thur, June 26, 8:30 a.m. ~ From: Mike Savage Tact said you were unmotivated. You should kick his ass because he is a dumbass. Thur, June 26, 9:27 a.m. ~ From: Psycho Ex # 15 U r the stupid~! Tact wil kick ur ass! Thur, June 26, 10:30 a.m. ~ From: Amber James, hurry and get back to Vegas. We need to talk. I’m so sorry. Thur, June 26, 11:38 a.m. ~ The rest of the messages are basically just forwards saying stupid crap like “If you don’t text this to 13 people in under 13 minutes, your love life will be horrible forever.” However, the Randalls one and the one from Amber intrigue me the most. I have to go home and see what this moron said about me on the tube. This is utterly amazing to me. First everybody can’t believe it and now I’m being brought up in the TiT finals by both Randalls and Ravager. This is a long way from being a joke as a member of the Church of the Unholy. So I go over and fill my car up with gas before getting back behind the wheel to drive home. This will be an interesting experience. * * * 1:20 p.m. After driving home, I grab my bags and head inside. Once inside my house, I turn on the lights and there’s everyone I know throwing confetti at me. “SURPRISE!!!!!!!!!!!” I look on in shock as Amber comes over and kisses me on the cheek. “Hi honey!” she says as I look on in shock. “Surprised?” she asks. I honestly don’t know what to say at this point. First she breaks up with me after I found out she had an affair and now this’It’s a little too much to take at this point. “Well yeah?” I mutter out in a stunned voice. “But what about?” “The “affair?”” she asks using finger quotes as I nod my head. “Yeah, what about it?” “I had to throw you off so I could get down here and get this done before you got back.” “So it was all a trick?” She nods her head. “Yes it was and it worked very well.” “But?” She shushes me and starts explaining. “You know that my ex had an affair with one of my best friends, right?” I nod my head. “Now think about it. Why would I do that to somebody else when I was so hurt by that happening to me” I wouldn’t. Besides, I tried everything else but it didn’t work so my friend came up with the idea for the plan and for me to get down here and get it ready before you came home.” I’m in utter shock still at the moment but then a smile comes to my face as I come to the realization about something. “You really do love me, don’t you?” She nods her head. “Yes.” This brings a big grin to my face as we hug and embrace. “I’m glad that you do.” I then motion around the party. “Very nice. You tricked even me which is very hard to do. That’s a major turn on in my book.” She smiles. “I know it is.” “Besides, I was thinking about it and Brian is too nice to pull off something like that.” “True.” “And in the end I got a lot of training done for my matches which only helps me in the long run.” “That works out. You’re going to do fine in your matches.” “And I will win both my matches. There is no doubt in my mind.” “In mine either,” she replies. I look around at the party going on all around us. “I think we’d better stop worrying about what’s to come and start partying with everyone else.” “Good idea.” We hold hands before going to join the rest of the party that has started rocking in my pad. This is the most fun I’ve had in ages and I hope it stays like this until the party is over. * * * 4:15 p.m. The party has still been going for awhile now and only a few people have left. It looks like it’s going to be going on for awhile. I’m already drunk at this point and starting to stagger around. However, most everyone at the party is more wasted than I am. Some are playing Super Mario Kart on my Wii. A few others are outside on the patio chatting about politics and whatnot. One of them is my best friend Art so me and Amber go outside to listen in on this madness. However, they stop talking about politics when Art brings up my wrestling career. He’s talking to Patrick, one of Amber’s friends. Amber and I watch them in amusement as the conversation gets interesting. “James could kick Obama’s ass!” Art exclaims. Patrick nods his head after taking a swig of beer from his can of Bud Light. “Yeah,” he replies. “He’s better than McCain though.” “True. But at least McCain isn’t an elitist prick like Obama is. He’s going to turn this country Communist like Castro did Cuba.” “No he won’t. He’ll make it more like the Iron Curtain of Soviet Russia.” “But he doesn’t have a blotch on his head like Gorbachev.” “Or one on his stomach like Dusty Rhodes.” “I still think James would kick his ass.” “I think so too.” “In fact, James could kick both their asses.” Patrick nods his head. “Damn right’even though McCain is Palpatine.” “McCain is NOT Palpatine!” Art nods his head. “Yes he is.” “Then what does that make Obama?” “He’s the Third Antichrist.” “No, that’s Hillary Clinton.” “I stand corrected.” “Hear about that bull**** going on in Zimbabwe” That’s what it’s going to be like whenever one of them tries to get reelected.” “This country is going down the toilet and no one even sees that we could be like Zimbabwe in four years.” I laugh over this and they both look over at me. I shrug my shoulders. “What”! This is better than most television newscasts. But I agree with you both. Now let’s drink up some more!” I go back inside to get some more alcohol as they go back to having their conversation. Amber is soon to follow and closes the screen door behind her. * * * Journal of James von Doom 26 June.- Today has been a day full of surprises. First the surprise party and then the shocker of shockers. Amber wasn’t cheating on me and it was all a ruse just to get her down her to set up the party because she couldn’t get away from being around me in order to set up the party. Upon this realization, I realized that I’ve been spending too much time with her and keeping her from her friends but she has done the same with me. So it’s basically your usual Catch 22. But at least we’re together and I’m happy. Now the only thing left is for me to do now is to defend both of my championship belts on Super Show IX. That will be the only thing sweeter than what she did for me. Sure, she went about it in a ****ed up way, but oh well. I love her and that’s all that matters. * * * 10:15 p.m. After the party finished up and Amber and I cleaned up everything, I finally had the time to sit down and watch what Randalls and Ravager had been saying about me on the television. First Ravager gave me a huge compliment. He said that me and the rest of the current crop of TEAM champions will make the place feel more special. Then I continue to watch the video feed and see what Randalls had to say on the subject matter. Randalls then came on and basically trashed me. He said that I was “retarded monkey” before saying that he would be “TEAM’s Saviour.” The only reaction to his little shot at me is shaking my head. “Savior my ass. The only salvation he’s going to get is from the lights he’s looking up at after he loses to Ravager.” I then grab the remote and turn off the television. I am not surprised. People have been taking shots at me for years. In fact, Captain Justice took a shot at me a few weeks back and now this clown. They have no idea that I am not the same von Doom that has spent years losing. This is my year of destiny and soon they’ll all see the light. The light of their true salvation?
