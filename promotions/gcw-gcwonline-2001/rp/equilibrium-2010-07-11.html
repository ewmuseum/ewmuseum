Equilibrium

11 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/11/10 Equilibrium is defined as a state of rest or balance due to the equal action of opposing forces; equal balance between any powers, influences, etc. or equality of effect; mental or emotional balance, equanimity; the condition existing when a chemical reaction and its reverse reaction proceed at equal rates. Balance, or equilibrium, is a very important part of nature. Whether between life and death, night and day or even sanity and insanity; balance is necessary. When that balance is damaged or lost, the effects can be quite adverse. As relates to the human psyche, it is imperative that that equilibrium remains untouched, unchanged. Otherwise, who knows what could happen? For one particular individual, that stability is all but gone. After a long and grueling struggle to keep everything together, his entire world has come crashing down around him. For some, it seems that nothing life throws at them can bring them down. For others, all it takes is for life to throw one little curveball to send them spiraling downward into the dark abyss of despair. For AXEL Action, that descent was quite rapid and it all started the morning after NC-17 2010. February 21, 2010 Hollywood, Florida- 6:00 AM: The early morning silence is shattered by the ‘Beep! Beep! Beep!’ of a moving truck backing into the driveway of AXEL Action’s palatial estate. Mistaking the sound to be that of his alarm clock going off, AXEL reaches out from beneath the blankets and with an irritated groan, brings his hand crashing down onto the snooze button. "Beep! Beep! Beep!", it goes on and on, prompting the Angel of Vengeance to sit upright in his bed. He reaches for the alarm clock and snatches it from its socket in the wall, throwing it across the room and watching it smash to bits against the hard surface of the opposite wall. Beep! Beep! Beep! He lets out a sigh and climbs out of bed, pulling on a pair of jeans to make himself decent. Action navigates his way through the hallways and out into the living room where he finds that people have decided to let themselves into his home and make off with his things. The men are all dressed in blue jeans and t-shirts with their company logo embroidered on the back. He grunts loudly as if clearing his throat, attempting to get the attention of the men. "What the Hell is going on here?" One of the men approaches AXEL, and this one appears to be dressed differently from the rest. He sports a three-button polo shirt which is tucked into a pair of khaki slacks, a braided belt and a pair of loafers. The fellow also sports a clipboard in his hand, attached to which are several sheets of paper, seemingly filled with a bunch of legal mumbo-jumbo. "We’re here to collect your things and repossess your home, as per the agreement you made with one, Dan Black. You see, Mr. Black mailed us a copy of the contract that you both signed, and you are both legally bound to the agreement you made. That means that none of these things belong to you anymore and they all belong to Mr. Black." This doesn’t sit well with AXEL, the irritation on his face quite evident. Not only was he jerked out of the most peaceful slumber he’d had in weeks, but now he was being taken for everything he has. He shakes his head and narrows his eyes at the man who seems to be the supervisor of operations. "I’m sorry, but there’s no way I’m going to let you throw me out of my own home. Do you have any idea how many years I had to struggle and how hard I worked just to be able to have a home of my own? You need to just pack up your little ‘worker ants’ and get out of here before I send you packing, myself." AXEL takes a step toward the supervising official, attempting to try and scare him away only to be met with a blast of pepper spray to the face. A scream of excruciating agony escapes AXEL’s lips as he clutches at his face with his hands, trying to wipe the burning fluid from his eyes. His nose runs and his eyes water, giving off the appearance that he’s crying. However, with as much pain as he is in at the moment, it wouldn’t be surprising if he was, in fact, crying. "WHAT THE FUCK, MAN?!" AXEL rushes for the nearby garden hose, finding himself falling flat on his face in the grass by tripping over it. Cursing and grumbling under his breath, he feels around with one hand until he finds the hose, the other feeling for the nozzle to turn on the water. While he’s busy rinsing out his eyes, the repo crew gets back to work. In the twenty-five or so minutes it takes to get his sight back, the majority of his belongings are cleaned out of the house. His attention is grabbed by the sound of a dog barking, whining and yelping. Though his vision is still blurry, AXEL turns to look in the direction the sound is coming from only to see his dog, Captain Awesome, being hauled off by animal control. "Wait! Stop!" He makes a dash for the animal control van, but is far too late to do anything but watch helplessly as the van drives off over the horizon. The Angel of Vengeance falls to his knees on the sidewalk, shoulders slumping as he realizes there’s nothing he can do to stop any of this. Resigned to his fate, he does the only thing he knows to do—begins the long trek to find his brother, Ace. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ What can one possibly do when everything they’ve worked so hard for is gone? It’s not so easy to just pick yourself up, dust off and try again. Especially when it’s your own greed that’s cost you everything you’ve ever known. A lust for gold can bring even the strongest of men to their knees and corrupt even the most saintly of them, too. That lust is the very thing that kept AXEL Action pushing onwards. It drove him to continue his pursuit of the GCW United States Championship, even at the expense of something much more important than any worldly possession: his sanity. June 7, 2010 "It was karma, they said. Well if you want to be like me AXEL, I guess it's time that I even the playing field. Now that you have nothing at all..." The screams of AXEL Action can be heard in the background as Valentine tosses the lit match into the trash can and steps back from the flames that shoot out; the eyebrows nearly being singed right off of her face. As the light dances off her vibrant blue eyes, she looks over at her television screen to watch as AXEL Action sees his own life go up in flames. "...I guess this is a fair fight. Welcome to the big leagues AXEL. For your own sake, I hope you're right." Flash sideways to AXEL Action, he finally realizes there's nothing he can do but watch. He stops struggling, his features falling flat; his gaze cold and emotionless. His upper lip gives a bit of a twitch, as does his left eyebrow as he walks toward the monitor in a zombie-like state. His entire world has come crashing down in a blazing inferno right before his very eyes. Well, what was left of it, anyway. No words can ever express what's going through the Angel of Vengeance's mind at this very moment as he reaches forward to grab the monitor. He opens his mouth to scream, but nothing comes out as he snatches the monitor from its stand. Throwing it against the wall with all his might, he severs his one line of communication with Valentine. The monitor smashes to pieces, shards of glass and innards from the appliance tumble to the floor as the empty plastic shell hits the tile. AXEL drops to his knees, head down and gaze affixed to the floor; a broken shell of the man he once was. "She’s going to pay…" He slumps over in the corner, his eyes closed and his mind off in its own little world as he tries to find his happy place. Completely silent, AXEL pushes himself up to his feet and saunters over to the door. Trying the knob once more, he finally manages to get it open. Stepping out into the hallway, he turns and slowly makes his way toward the exit. Ignoring the voices that call out to him from both sides, he throws the back door of the arena open and walks out into the alleyway. For the following seven days or so, he remains in the darkness, hidden from the view of others. Like a silent predator he waits, bides his time until he’s ready to strike. When he finally uncoils and delivers his vengeance on the United States Champion, he completely obliterates her with a high-and-tight back suplex through the catering table in the backstage hallway. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ With his mental equilibrium shattered, AXEL Action has become a bit of a loose cannon. It is quite obvious he’s off his rocker and all of that pent-up rage and frustration has boiled to the surface. It’s only a matter of time before he either self-destructs or destroys someone else. For the sake of everyone else, they’d best hope it’s the former. Note: Vivica J. Valentine was used with full permission from Billy. Small excerpt from Worldwide 131 was also used to further the plot of the RP.
