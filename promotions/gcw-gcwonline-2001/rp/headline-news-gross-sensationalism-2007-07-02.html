Headline News: Gross Sensationalism

2 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/2/2007 .::Disclaimer- Contains Strong Language and Violence, Viewer Discretion Advised::. ---- “I wouldn’t recommend sex, drugs or insanity for everyone, but they’ve always worked for me.” ~Hunter S. Thompson ---- Monday, July 2, 2007 0333 hours, local time Chicago, IL I am internationally known as an asshole. Reputation precedes me. Misconception make them either avoid me, giving me that wide berth in passing, or makes the stupid ones get right up in my face. Bad, nationwide. Like the song, I suppose, but there wasn’t a person out there who didn’t know something about me. A person that hadn’t heard a story that went along the lines of this: “Jackson” Yeah, that guy’s a total dick. I saw him this one time, and he punched this guy right in the face?” Save the tired rhetoric, please. Stuff the incriminations up your ass. I don’t need your approval, and I don’t cringe in the face of your petty judgments. Fuck, most times I’m itching for a fight. Tension coiled like a spring, wired to explode. Don’t touch me. Don’t look at me and goddamn it, DO NOT get in my face and ask me to hit you. I will. It will not be pretty. I walk the streets where I live, surrounded by filth. Everything is covered in this scummy layer that disgusts me. All the walls are covered in spray paint, except the black walls of my club. No, they don’t dare tag my place. Reputation precedes me. They know. They look into my eyes on the streets, and they know better. I want out. Never before in my life have I craved it so bad. Stop the goddamn merry-go-round, I want off. Revenge. For me, that used to be the best motivator. I could hype myself up, and manufacture some slight to make people pay for. That was always my gift- the readiness to anger. That undercurrent of fury. I wanted to make Victor pay. At first, it was because I wanted her. I wanted everything he had. I’m not gonna lie to you, and Shane knew it too. I wanted her from the moment I saw her. She was perfection, so crass and impossibly beautiful- she was me, with ovaries. It was so easy to forget, for one fucked up blink of an eye that I’m always destined to fail. Call me a pessimist, but really? I’m just talking reality. There are some things that I will never conquer. Yeah, it used to be all about the revenge. Raging against the machine, it’s all the rage these days. All about the punks, and the wannabe anarchists playing follow the leader. The skater kids, and the degenerates. Yeah, raging against the machine, but somewhere along the line I became the machine. Let me tell you something, kiddies, it’s next to impossible to rage against yourself. Sometimes I want nothing more than to break free from this mortal frame. Explode from behind this world-weary face, erupt past these eyes that have seen too much shit. Blam, up in a million glittering pieces, like expensive Fourth of July fireworks. Blaze of glory. Maybe that’s why I do the things I do. Maybe I’m just looking for someone to end me in a spectacular spray of blood and guts? Then, and only then will I know who I really am. ---- “Jax?” He heard her voice in his mind as his breath rasped in and out of his lungs. “I love you, Daddy,” she’d said, begging for sweet release. She was tied up to the bed, locked inside the bedroom right now. Drying out. The night was dark, preternaturally black. His breathing was ragged, the air like icy fire, ripping in and out of his lungs, leaving his insides in tatters. He stumbled, careening into the dumpster with a resounding clang, feet floundering in the trash-strewn water that slicked the greasy asphalt. “No, God no?” he wheezed, looking over his shoulder again, seeing the silhouette of his pursuer at the mouth of the alley. He turned, soaked Chuck Taylor Converse sneaks skidding for purchase on the slick concrete before he lunged forward into his headlong flight again. “Can’t catch me,” he murmured, ducking his head, pumping his bony arms. Former track star, current heroin addict. He dove around the corner, leaning heavily against the brick for a moment to gather his composure. Silence fell around him, the night deathly quiet. Not a single footfall, not a single breath. His heart pounded in his ears; his blood like battery acid scorching his limbs from within. After an agonizing moment, he risked a look around the corner, and found the alley deserted. He fell back against the wall with a sigh, feeling the adrenaline drain from him in a rush, leaving him wasted and shaken as he still looked back the way he’d come. Jackson dropped silently from the fire escape, a wry smile curving his lips as he landed behind the junkie. “Boo.” He whispered, startling the man as he pressed the knife against his cheek. “Honey, I’m home?” he said in a singsong, bringing his lips close to the junkie’s ear- close enough that the wasted freak could feel Jackson's breath, and smell the mint and tobacco on his lips. The junkie flinched, holding in his breath as Jackson pressed the tip of the hunting knife against his pale cheek. “Don’t?” “Don’t?” Jackson chuckled, the sound dark and cold, like the air around them, “it’s a little too late for that, my friend. I told you, and your fucking kind not to deal your wares in my place” and all I get from you is blatant disregard.” “I” I’m sorry? I thought?” he groped for words, his eyes rolling wildly in their bloodshot sockets. “No, that’s the problem, jackass. You didn’t think. I don’t have a problem with blow or mary-jane, but don’t bring your fucking meth into my club. Hell, don’t sell your fucking shit in my neighborhood. Period.” The junkie grinned, revealing stained and broken teeth, “you don’t know nothing, man. Your girls are my best customers” “specially that cooch tendin” bar.” Jackson withdrew the knife, and backhanded the junkie, slamming the back of his head off the bricks. “Don’t,” he warned in a low, and dangerous voice, “don’t fucking try me, asshole. You might not respect me, but you sure as hell will fear me.” ??the fuck’s the difference, man?” The junkie stammered, blood trickling from his split lip. “You gonna kill me anyways, so what’s it matter?” “Oh it matters, it damn well matters to me,” Jackson grinned with the words, revealing his even, white teeth. All the better to eat you with, my dear. “I thrive on that look, the fucking power trip. I break pieces of shit like you for fun.” He said, playing fast and loose with the truth. “There’s a million others like me, man. This ain’t heaven, it’s Chi-town.” “I’m well aware of that fact, asshole. I’m not a paranoid fuck, I don’t fear. Understanding I am, to the point where it went too far” it went to where it hurt, where it scarred and bruised and charred, and took away my goddamn life, leaving nothing but this vacant horror in its place.” “Wha?”” “Shut the fuck up,” he grumbled, a dark look flashing in his eyes. “I can be as fuckin” nonsensical as I please, douche, and there’s nothing you can do about it. You see, I’m the one holding the cards. So you tell me this, kid? you want your death fast, or slow?” He slammed his fist into the junkie’s face, bringing the man to his knees only to haul him back up by his greasy hair. He locked his arm around the scumbag’s neck, and forced him to the ground, choking the air out of him. The junkie’s breath steamed against the puddle as Jackson’s hands closed around his neck. “Take a good look at your reflection, floating in the water with the rest of the trash. There’s your ugliness. Face it.” The junkie gagged, shuddering beneath Jackson, who straddled him like a lover, his massive legs wrapped around the wasted body. “Here’s your little slice of paradise. Embrace the darkness, asshole, and realize that I’m being merciful. Quick death now is better than the few pitiful years you have left, with that monkey on your back, rotting your goddamn teeth outta your head. It’s disgusting. Pathetic. You mock people like me with your very existence, people who give a shit about this world?” Jackson released the junkie a second before he would have blacked out, letting him splash into the cold water. He planted a boot in the junkie’s back, keeping him there, seconds away from drowning. “This place makes monsters from all of us. You can get raped and killed here. Addicted and imprisoned. Saturated and intimidated. Isolated and condemned. The innocent suffer and the guilty celebrate. I’m here to change that, consider me the anti-fear, the harbinger of pain.” He snickered, pulling out his P5 and flicking off the safety as he pointed it at the man’s head, continuing in that same strange delivery. “You might not get what you deserve, but you’ll get something that’ll hurt. That’s my personal satisfaction guarantee. Fuck, that’s a brain splattered guar-on-tee, my friend!” “No?” the junkie burbled, choking on the water inches from his lips. “What the fuck do you have worth living for” Nothing, asshole. Not a goddamn thing. Me? Heh, I’m a family man now. I can’t afford to let pieces of shit like you hang around. Your streets are my streets?” he screwed the silencer in with deliberate motions, taking his time, letting the cold water seep into the junkie beneath his foot. “I hear the same gunshots as you; I hold the same breath, hoping it’s not headed for me. Sadly, this time it is” headed for you, I mean. Your future’s on hold. Indefinitely. Don’t worry, someone will read about you in the paper tomorrow, right before laying it down in the bottom of the cage for their crated dog, or right before tossing it in the trash. Your very own byline. “Piece of shit found dead in alley”. Nobody will bat an eyelash, because gross sensationalism is what this world is all about. That’s the American dream, my friend. Oh! Say can you see?” His voice transformed from a brutal rasp to a rich tenor with the snippet of song, somehow more frightening because of it. A whisper of air accompanied the tensing of his finger on the trigger, and the back of the junkie’s head exploded in a crimson spray. Jackson removed his foot, nodding to himself as he holstered the gun, pausing to light a cigarette before moving away down the alley. ---- I’m in the hot seat tonight, hunted again. Doesn’t bother me. It’s all part of life, the ravenous circle of inevitability nipping at my heels. Fuck Destiny. Screw Fate. Fickle bitches, I don’t need them. Not me. I have my fix. I am a time junkie. I am a user. I have come to the point where I ask myself who’s using whom. I take a look around; yeah, I see what I do to time. I look in the mirror and I see what it’s doing back to me. I’ve come to the conclusion that we’ve got a pretty good thing going here. I abuse time. I starve it. I bore it to madness. Some nights I sit still, in the dark for hours, trying to make time go insane. I have devoted my life to making time pay. You heard me. I want to make it bleed. Make that fucker pay. I want to be the king of scars and then throw it all away. I AM THE KING OF PAIN. I you have a problem with my words, feel free to write an angry letter, but make sure you address it to yourself. After all, I got this from you. You have made me what I am. Does that frighten you? Get your ass out there, Tabloid. Explore the crass depths; take your photos of whores like Paris Hilton and Angelina Jolie. Celebrate the plastic reality you embody. Hit the streets running, my streets. Hit them begging, for tolerance, not money. Perhaps I’ll be moved by your plea. Instead of rest and relaxation, my paradise offers mutation and mutilation. Enjoy your stay. Here I come, running. Running at you with the anti-fear. Here comes the fuckin? pain. Fear that, asshole because I sure as hell won’t be fearing you. I will be the king; I won’t stand in line. I don’t care who you are, just like you could care less about me. Do you even know who you’re facing? No, you spent more time rambling about people who mean jack shit when you step into the ring with me. Focus, kid. Adjust the lens, and take your photograph. I’ll smile pretty for you while I break your face. Shit, I’m such a nice guy...
