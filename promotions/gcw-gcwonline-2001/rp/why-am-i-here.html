Date: Yesterday -=A doctor walks into the waiting room. Sam Wolack is sitting there.=- Doctor: Mr. Wolack, there was nothing that we were about to do. Unfortunately, we just couldn't save it. Wolack: How's Andrea doing? Doctor: We have her sedated. We did check, and other than the termination of the pregnancy, she looks to be completely healthy. In a few months, you can try again. Wolack: Thanks doc. Is it OK if I go in to be with her? Doctor: Sure. I think that it will be good for both of you. -=Wolack gets up and heads towards the hospital room. He turns around and sees the camera there.=- Wolack: Do you guys mind? I don't want that camera in there. OK. -=The camera fades to the outside of the hospital. Wolack is looking through the phone numbers to call Andrea's parents. He finds their number and calls. There's no answer.=- Wolack: Guys, can you take the hint. Get the f*** out of here. This is not a good f***ing time. -=Sam throws the phone at the camera, hitting it square in the lens, breaking it. The screen goes black.=- Camera man: What the f*** did you do that for? Wolack: Because you're being an a**hole, that's why. Don't you know that this is a real bad time to be doing this s***. I allow those cameras to follow me around, not being allowed to have a private life because of this f***ing company. All I ask for is a day's worth of privacy. If GCW can't get that through their f***ing skulls, then they can go to hell. -=The audio cuts out as we fade into the next day. Sam Wolack is nowhere near the hospital. Instead, he's looking over the Arizona desert, watching the sunrise.=- Wolack: Keiko, I need you to give me strength. I almost turned into Perstrictum yesterday, and that's scaring the s*** out of me. -=A small breeze blows through the desert.=- Wolack: I understand. -=Wolack walks towards his motorcycle, puts his helmet on and peels out through the desert as we fade to the outside of a tattoo parlor. He's talking on his cell phone.=- Wolack: Trust me Andrea, after what happened yesterday witht he GCW cameraman, I needed to be reminded what I had become. I don't want to have that happen again. This is going to be enough. Well, everyone is going to see come WorldWide. It's a new ballgame for me. I'm going to go in and do what needs to be done. I won't be the one that's needed to be delivered. I'm going to help deliver the US Title from EVL. -=Fade out=-
