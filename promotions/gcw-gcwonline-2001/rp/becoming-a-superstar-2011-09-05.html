Becoming A Superstar!

5 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/05/11 In a local coffee shop in downtown Tacoma, Washington is where we can find Cameron Garret. Sipping on his low fat macchiato with skim milk, Cameron is not a beast by any means but to the average customer it is quite obvious he does not have the stature of a normal man “Excuse Me” Cameron puts one finger in the air as he looks toward the red headed barista. She slowly makes her way over to him. “Yes sir, how may I help you?” The red head looked at him intrigued not only by his stature but his cunning good looks,Cameron was sitting there in a nice strip button up with a set of brown khakis and a pair of brown loafers, his hair parted down the middle and perfectly in place as usual. “ When you have a moment can you please bring me another beverage along with a coffee cake?” The waitress listens and nods once he is done. She wastes no time in turning and heading right back to her position. As she turns Cameron takes the opportunity to take a quick look at her backside. He nods his head in approval . Cameron takes the last swig of his drink, taking a moment to turn around and see how far along the red head is with his next drink he feels a vibration come from the tablet in his hand. Quickly glancing down he notices it is an email from none other the Steven Caldera of the “World Famous” Global Championship Wrestling.” Cameron has spent his time since his stint on Tough Enough working the Indie market and has made quite a name for himself along the western coast. Hell he had even traveled as faacr as San Diego for a match.Upon opening the email and reading the obligatory introduction Cameron skims to the meat and potatoes of the email. Within two seconds he finds exactly what he was looking for. “Cameron Garret, I Steven Caldera am very eager to meet you. I have not seen but have also heard the noise you have made as a wrestler on the West Coast market. I received your email about interest about possible employment here at GCW. I feel you COULD be a good fit not only in the ring but also backstage. With that I am glad to inform you that I have decided to give you a try out with the company. First I want to see what you are made of not only in the ring but on the road. Please prepare as I am expecting you at World Wide 153 live from CITY, STATE. I am unsure of who you will be facing at that time or if you will have an actual match on the card. However I want to get you into the ring, I want to see if you can work with the big boys. Please take this opportunity seriously as I hope it is one that will work out not only for you but also for me. I look forward to your results” Cameron puts both fists up into the air, trying his best to not only contain his excitement but also to not be noticed by to many people as he would look mildly awkward. However he was unsuccessful as just as he was done with his inner celebration the barista came over with his next round and coffee cake. “Sir is there anything else I can get for you.” She slyly winked at him letting him know she had seen his mild outburst of excitement in public. Cameron knew she had seen it but he felt he had just cause for his actions. He did not need to explain them to her, inside he kind of hoped his chances with her were still good but at the same time he was more worried about the opportunity at hand. “ I will just take the check” Cameron looked at her with a smile like none other. In normal circumstances any woman would love to be in the presence of Cameron as he was one dashing man but in this instance the lady seem almost infatuated with Cameron, however he didn’t mind. Looking back down at his tablet as the barista walks away Cameron knew that it was his time, and this was the opportunity he has been looking for. Quickly he typed on his keypad the url for GCW and was taking a once over of the current roster when she had returned with the check. He looked up quickly wanting to speaking with the red haired beauty one more time but was to enthralled with the opportunity and curiosity he was faced with. Whom he may have to face at the up and coming event? Cameron had gone thru the roster twice thinking of chances of whom it might be. He knew that it wouldn’t be one of the stars but maybe a scrub. All in all he hoped for the shot, this is what he had dreamed of his whole life. It had taken years and years of practice, determination, and success but he finally had gotten there, the GCW. With that he took a bit of his coffee cake and washed it down with a swig from the new coffee. His mind running a mile a minute Cameron was excited to not only get in the ring but also have a chance to make a name for himself. Looking down at the bill he noticed the nice barista had not only written down his total but she also followed it with ten digit number the he presumed to be her’s. Smiling and nodding he thought to himself he was so young and had plenty of game that this would be the first of many, well that of course is once he is famous and on television. With that Cameron finished his drink and his cake and then placed a crisp $10 bill on the table and walked out, he was more then happy to leave almost a5 0% tip for the cute girl, that he would run into again at some point. He took the check stub with her number placed it in his pocket and walked out on his way trying to avoid contact with her giving her the illusion he was eager. September 3rd 2011 His bags were packed and sitting next to the door, planning for his first trip into the world of Global Championship Wrestling. Right besides the car keys and the bags was a print out from the GCW website. You hear a noise from the other room and hear someone yell “Bye I love you.” A soft sweeter voice respond back, “ Please be safe, I love you to,” With that Cameron Garret came running from the other room, almost like a child on Christmas morning this was the opportunity he had been waiting for his whole life. He grabbed his bag and thru it over his shoulder. Nevada was only a couple hour drive, he figured he could make the trip, spend a couple nights in the hotel and then either make his move to his home town or continue on his journey with GCW. Cameron grabbed the door knob slowly started to open it and then stopped with the door open just enough for a breeze to come thru it. He reached back grabbed the folded print out. Opening it up like had done so many times since it went live last week, his eyes started the scan the email which told him about his first match up, about his opporunity to wrestle his first real match. His eyes only had to go up one match to know that he had been booked in his first match against a fellow new comer, William Curr. Cameron has not known much about his new foe, but he did know one thing. For him to be in the GCW he must have a skill set that is worth seeing. Cameron knew his own skills and was not worried about how he would perform but this time it would be on the main stream, on the main screen. He folded the paper and put it into the inside part of his bag, Cameron continued on his path to excellence by opening the door and making the first steps onto his professional career in wrestling. He took three steps glanced slowly back over his right shoulder looking back at his house. His life and dream of being a professional wrestler had been a roller coaster as of late. Coming out of college as one of the better wrestlers in his graduating class, to not being able to find a career. Then finally getting the chance of a life time by signing with Stone Cold Steve Austin and the WWE for Tough Enough. Cameron’s career had finally took off, but came to a sudden halt when he was voted off after only a few weeks. However this was now his time, his time to shine. Cameron looked at it as a real opportunity and a real chance for him to make a name for himself. With that he continued on his journey to the car, and the start of his new life. He placed his bag into the back seat and took a deep breath. On his exhale he opened the driver’s door, knowing this was going to be it, the real deal. Getting in the car he started it and slowly put the car in reverse and started his journey to becoming a professional wrestler.
