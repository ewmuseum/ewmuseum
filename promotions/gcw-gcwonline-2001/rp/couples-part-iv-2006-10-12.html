Josh walked back up and then went to reached down and turned the door knob on his brother's door. As the door opened and the light from the hallway came in like a lance the scene inside was fully visible to both Dani and Josh. Laying in the bed was more then one person. The main form they could make out was the large form of Grayson Brand as he laid there. His arm was draped across another, smaller person with really only the tuft of shocking red colored hair sticking out from where she laid there with him. Gray's eyes opened, shining a red in the mixed light. His head went forward as if to cover his partner more and a low growl escaped from his lips. He wasn't quite awake, but going in right now would not be advised. He was in full protect mode. Dani could swear she could almost see his ears go back and his hair stand up on the back of his head, but he didn't shift. Dani just covered her mouth as she took in the view. Josh smiled wide and shut the door. He motioned for Dani to keep quiet as she almost wanted to just blurt out a laugh and maybe even a scream at what she saw, one of happiness for her older brother. The two almost ran down the stairs. Josh almost tripped over his own feet and Dani had to tell him to shush as they started to head to their room. As they opened their door, a voice came from behind them. It was Alexis' voice. ?Hey you two, congrats. Nice to have you home.? Dani turned around and smiled at her. ?Doing fine, but looks like while we were out, you were able to...? Alexis smiled wide, almost like a child would when thinking of things that made them happy. ?Well, you two and I need to talk since you found out already.? Josh nodded. ?Come on in, if you don't mind me unpacking while you talk. Is he still asleep.? ?I don't know. His growl woke me up. I'll have to get used to that part of it.? Josh opened the door to their room and pulled the bags in as Dani and Alex joined him for the talk. Alex started it off. ?I know you two would find out, you live here.
