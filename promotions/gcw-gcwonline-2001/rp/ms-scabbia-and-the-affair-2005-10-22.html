Ms. Scabbia and the 'Affair'

22 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/22/05 Two weeks ago at WorldWide, Patrick O’Conner witnessed the unfair, unmerciful and savage beating of his son, Coyle. Infuriated and revengeful, Patrick initiated the capture for either Sam Wolack, the assailant, or Ms. Scabbia, Wolack’s female friend. It was inevitable that Patrick would make-up well for this inconvenience. He sat alone in his office as his “associates” were out finding one of the two people. During this, the fury has been stacking upon itself within him since the action, and he was getting frustrated because there has been no success as of yet. Every click of the seconds-hand on the clock made him twitch. It was all Patrick heard in a room full of shadows and vengeance. Suddenly, as he zoned out just imagining what he would do, Patrick alarmed himself up when he heard his pub’s doors swing open. A grin drew on his face as multiple footsteps were getting closer to his office, this is what he waited for during the last 2 weeks. It seemed so unreal and so intense. You can even hear the help-screams from a woman’s mouth, and rustling around, trying to get away. Patrick knew what was going on, it was a way better sound than just a hand-ticking sound from the clock. It was the sound of his “associates” success. His office doors blasted open, and there stood 3 people. The two smiling men on the sides were Shamus and Tommy. The tied-and-bound woman between them was Ms. Scabbia, frightened and stiff. Patrick, “Ah, Tommy and Shamus, fantastic work, just fantastic! Sit her down for me please, and while you’re at it, un-tape her mouth for me, would you?” Tommy, “Only if we can stay here and watch Patty.” Tommy winked over to Patrick, all the same with Shamus, and Patrick just smiled. Patrick, “Why, you know you’re more than welcome to this little “affair”!” As Patrick said “affair”, he exaggerated the word and widened his eyes as they stared into Ms. Scabbia’s. Absolutely calm, but enraged on the inside, Patrick studied his detainee. Tommy ripped off the duct tape from her mouth, causing a loud scream to echo throughout the office. Shamus shook his head, and decided to pet her upper-lip until the pain went away. He backed up as Patrick leaned forward with a recently lit vanilla cigar resting betweens his lips. She looked to the three men nervously, then rested on the sight of Patrick. Patrick, “Tommy, tell me, where did you find Ms. Scabbia?” Patrick still stared into Ms. Scabbia’s eyes. Tommy, “The bitch was at the Scottsdale Links Resort in Arizona, and that kinda” pissed me off, I went all the way to L.A. for no reason!” Patrick, “No worries Tommy, you and Shamus will be reimbursed for that.” Shamus and Tommy agreed with that claim, and rested themselves from the conversation. They just watched as Patrick stood up, and walked over to Ms. Scabbia. He stood behind her, and practically rested his head on her shoulder. He put his mouth close to her ear and he blew the vanilla-scented smoke next to her face. Patrick, “Your little friend Sam Wolack intruded into my family, and caused a huge unwanted disturbance. Now, my son has to wrestle this week with the threat of losing a simple match against a nobody-Vince Jones because Coyle’s injuries were extended. What do you think would be a good way to ease that unfortunate pain” What do you think would be fair for Mr. Wolack?” Ms. Scabbia didn’t answer Patrick. Instead, she showed attitude by letting out a “humph” as she turned her face the other way. Patrick looked to the two men, and smiled once again. Patrick, “Tough chick you got here, huh?” Shamus, “I can take care of her if you want Patty, you know I can woo her in many ways?” As he laughed, Patrick ordered that there would be no need for that; at least not yet. Instead, he continued to talk to her, he needed to find out some things. Patrick, “Listen hun, you can be as uncooperative as you want, but take a look at Shamus. He’s unfair to the weaknesses of women, and I can’t talk him down from violations forever. So, either you answer a few questions I ask and listen to a few things I tell you, or else Shamus will be just as unfair to you as that mother-fucker Sam Wolack was to my son.” Tommy and Shamus looked at each other as if there was a joke between them, smirking and all, then looked back over to Ms. Scabbia and Patrick. They saw her hesitate to answer as Patrick just puffed his cigar as he waited. Ms. Scabbia, “Alright?” The three men all became instantly satisfied. Now, they were in rolling-action. Patrick, “Good, good, let’s start out with a new question then. I’m Patrick, and you are?” She looked around hesitantly, and then gave up. Ms. Scabbia, “I’m Andrea, I’m Andrea Scabbia.” Patrick, “Alright, good. Well, how are you Andrea?” Andrea, “I, um, I was fine, but now I feel a little restrained. If you could just loosen-? Patrick, “There’ll be no need for that.” Andrea Scabbia looked down and sighed. Patrick stood back up from his lean, and rested his hand upon her right shoulder. He puffed his cigar once again. Patrick, “I need you to be in this exact spot for the duration of this “affair”, if you will. It’ll make things a whole lot easier.” Patrick walked back over to his desk, and plopped down into his chair. Tommy and Shamus still watched silently. He leaned back, still with Andrea Scabbia in his sight, and rested his feet on his desk. Andrea, “You aren’t going to kill me, are you?” Patrick, “Hah, that won’t be necessary, pending. Instead, I want to know something. I want to know why your friend Sam Wolack took it upon himself to practically injure my son to a near-critical point?” Andrea, “Well, Patrick, if you haven’t noticed, they aren’t the best of friends.” Patrick, “Well, Andrea, that’s besides the point. He did it in a cowardly-fashion. It seems as if everything he does is in a cowardly-fashion. Not once has he fairly given my son a beating, he had to do it when Coyle was weak, or inattentive. It seems like to me you have a box for a boy, no?” Andrea, “Sam could punish him in any method, it’s just how things worked out. Sam would be able to massacre your son with no problem, even if it wasn’t due to distractions or whatever?” Patrick, “If that’s so, then I’m dying to see some proof. Do you have any proof?” Andrea, “No, but-? The three men chuckled to each other. Patrick, “Then your wrong, little girl! Wolack is a little bitch, and for his insane and stupid actions, I’m giving an eye for an eye.” Andrea raised one brow, she tried to figure out what Patrick meant by that. Tommy and Shamus sat straight up, now interested and entertained. Patrick grabbed a golf-club that was near his desk. Then, he stood up and walked close to her. For a few moments, as he began to talk again, he was pretending to practice his set-up as if Andrea’s head was the golf-ball. Patrick, “Yeah, an eye for an eye. You know what that is, don’t you” Basically, it’s collateral, y’know? And, seriously, it’s a shame I have to do this.” He raised the club high, and whacked down to her face. He didn’t hit her, but it was a scare-tactic that made her jump in her seat. Andrea, “Fuck!” Patrick, “Andrea, tell me. Where is Mr. Wolack?” She huffed and puffed in worry, looking to Tommy and Shamus for a moment. Shamus gave her a wink and a signal, teasing her as she panicked. Andrea looked back over to Patrick and muttered herself an answer. Andrea, “He left my room to go shop for his Halloween costume right before your men grabbed me. He said he would only be gone for a little bit.” With the look of curiousness on his face, Patrick wondered. Patrick, “Andrea, are you and Sam involved?” Andrea, “I don’t think that’s any of your business, Patrick.” Patrick, “But it is. Everything to do with Mr. Wolack is my business. You’re even my business now. Whether you like it or not, you will be a part of this whole situation.” Andrea, “This is unfair, this is-? Patrick, “Don’t talk to me about being unfair!” So heated, Patrick walked away from Andrea. He threw the golf-club, and looked to his wall of Coyle’s pictures. Patrick, “This isn’t going swiftly enough. Andrea, I said an eye for an eye, and I mean an eye for an eye. Mr. Wolack barked up the wrong tree, and he fucked with my son. Now that Coyle won’t be 100%, Mr. Wolack won’t be 100% for his match either. But, I’m going to hit him in a different way.” Andrea, “What do you mean?” Patrick turned around and looked at her with fury throughout his expressionist face. His eyebrows sharpened and formed a downwards angle. He snapped his fingers to order Tommy and Shamus to come over to him. When they came, Andrea just saw the three of them whispering in a huddle amongst themselves. He mostly talked and pointed at Shamus, and it scared Andrea all the more. Just a moment later, Patrick walked out from his office, slamming the door shut. Tommy proceeded to lock it, only then to turn around and give the o.k. to Shamus. Andrea, “Wait, what’what’s going on!”” Shamus walked over to her with a smile on his face without answering her. Andrea started to shake. Shamus, “Tommy, put some tape on her mouth, I can’t have this bitch squealing.” Tommy did as what was directed. When the tape was applied, Andrea tried to kick and scream but she was still bounded. With no hesitations, her body was flipped over and she was stripped from her pants. There lied her body, still filled with life, still panicking, and not a care in the world was in Shamus? head. A dagger came from it’s holster on his belt, and made way to her bottom-half. Shamus then carved “O” on the right hip/cheek. She screamed throughout, but it didn’t do anything for her. Shamus, “Alright Tommy, now that's she branded, would you please step out” There's some business to be done.” Tommy just laughed and nodded his head. Tommy, “Alright, but you should at least wine and dine her first, she's good enough looking for that.”
