The Executioner

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Intro "This is fuckin' bullshit, Jeff!" A seemingly ordinary guy sits on a flat bench in Apollon gym in Edison, NJ staring out of the raised rickety firehouse door. Just outside the door stands two high school athletes, hands on hips, taking in every bit of oxygen in the air to replenish their parched lungs. A grinding/sliding noise churns on in the dark of the night out of the range of his peripherals. "Strength over Stamina...Power over Endurance...Key to Success... Key to bullshit if you ask me! How the hell am I supposed to step back into the octagon again if they change my whole routine?" A very lean guy, standing near the high schoolers, begins to flip huge truck tires out of one of the bay doors across the parking lot and signals to a slightly overweight, Edison High School Football wearing guy. "Move this tire Dust!" he yells over to the kid who instantly runs over to the tire with unexpected speed and begins to flip the tire with little hesitation. "I knew I had to make changes. I have and I will do what is necessary, but you can't tell me that this is goin' to work!" "It's going to get you stronger." "I'm not strong enough for..." "No. No, you're not. You're not tossing around Middleweights anymore. What if you have to get the better of someone like that Indian guy? The witch doctor guy?" Two more athletes come into view of his lusting eyes. Ropes drop to the their feet and the churning noise stops as two steel slabs grind to a halt, the weights shifting with the change in momentum. The two high schoolers whom were wrestling pick up the ropes, turn the sleds and begin to run off out of site. "I've bulked up twenty pounds...TWENTY! And they want me to change to this? How the hell am I supposed to stay lean with...with...Half the cardio, half the reps, no more cross fit training....I'm not becoming a powerlifter! I'm goin' to be a professional wrestler!" The flipping tire reaches the gym's doors, ten feet in front of the flat bench. The all-state lineman walks to the other side of the tire, making eye contact with the infuriated man talking. He squints his eyes at the hooded man and begins to flip the tire back in the direction of the opposite building. "You can't tell me you agree with this?" "You need to get your strength up, and no doubt it will help you in the ring..." "I gotta ask Pat about this, because this...Hal Johnson may know how to train the likes of Chris Bagwell and Andy Murray, but he doesn't know shit about training an MMA fighter..." "And Pat would know how to train a cross-over?" The man on the bench stands up tossing the letter in his hand to the ground. His Tap Out sweatshirt does as the name commands and is drenched in the chest and back. The 5'9" fighter jumps up to the nearby pull up bar and begins to rep out full range bicycle kicks. His form is perfect and seems impossible, but his training is unlike any average workout. In fact, it is far from any ordinary workout. MMA fighters go under the most intense training regimens, and this one is no exception. His anger fuels his determination. A simple workout like this, simple in the respect of no grappling or fight training, can be done with or without a trainer, so he chooses his closest friend Jeff to train with on lax days. Jeff isn't a fighter, nor is he an athlete, but he is good motivation and knows a lot about muscularity and nutrition, making him an asset. Jeff puts out his hands, one above and one under the MMA fighter's outstretched feet. The fighter takes a deep breath and then begins to do pull-ups, keeping his core tight and his legs out straight. After ten, he stops the pull-ups and moves his feet up and down, touching both of Jeff's hands for ten, and then goes back to the pull-ups. Jeff walks over to the back of the gym after the fighter jumps from the bar and points to a crunch bench. The fighter jumps on, wedging his feet in between the supports and tosses off his sweatshirt. Now shirtless, you can see several striations and fibesr in his core and shoulders as he moves around. He stares at himself in the mirror, awestruck and disgusted simultaneously. "What?" Jeff looks anxious. "I never wanted to bulk up..." "Well, you did for the career that you chose. Nobody wants to see you in wrestling..." "I want to wrestle. I want to entertain but..." "Sacrifice." He shakes his head. He moves his hand over his side where a tattoo of the Grim Reaper stands, arms crossed. He takes a deep breath and begins to do his crunches. "So are you going to keep the name, "The Executioner" in GCW?" "I haven't decided, I think I'll take whatever they want to give me..." "I'd keep it. I mean, shit, you earned it.." Jeff looks over his shoulder and sees the two high schoolers and the instructor from the gym in the other building walking up. All three are drenched in sweat... "Shit, nearly didn't recognize you! Where the hell you been the last couple months? And where the hell did you get all this shit from?" The instructor slaps Kris' chest and nods at Jeff. "Bulking up for a career change..." "Career change? What the hell you doing?" Kris puts a smile on his face trying to reenforce what he's about to say. "I just signed a contract with GCW. I'm finally going into the entertainment industry!" "Wait, what? But you're in the entertainment industry! You kick ass in the cage! That is what Kris Michaels does! You entertain in the cage..." "Well," Kris gets off the bench and stands up in a friendly manner, "I guess I'm going to do a hell of a job in a cage match then, huh?" Kris, Jeff, and the high schoolers laugh, but the instructor shakes his head in disbelief. "Can't you learn from Lesnar? A fighter at hear belongs in a cage, not a ring.." "Rick," Kris pats him on the shoulder with a smile. "I'm not leavin the cage! I'm goin to do both. It's in my contract at GCW to be able to do both. I am first and foremost an MMA fighter, and that'll never change. I promise, Rick!" Rick slaps Kris across the face. Kris smiles in return, after all, it is in his nature to be hit. "Will I see you at my gym sometime soon?" "Yeah, Rick. I just need to talk to Pat about my training and diet before I commit, but I'll be there. GCW wants me to change how I train so I can do all their fancy moves, but..." "You're strong, kid, but I can't see you lifting up that freak of an Indian..." Rick crosses his arms, awaiting a reply. "What the fuck man?" Kris looks over at Jeff who is laughing. "Listen to what Pat has to say about your training. If you're doing both, you need to find a balance..." Rick begins to walk away with both high schoolers in tow. "Oh and Kris?" "Yeah, Rick?" "Don't fuck up..." "Thanks, Rick" Kris Michaels...the new GCW Wrestler entering in at the bottom of the ranks. At this point, it seems that the only one with confidence in him is himself, but that is dwindling with every critique of his decision. But it is his decision, and he is going through with it. One thing is for sure...It's going to be one hell of a ride...
