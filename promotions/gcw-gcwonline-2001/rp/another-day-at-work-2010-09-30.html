Another Day AT Work

30 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/30/10 “KATV Channel 7 reporter Anne Pressly here at the scene of the disturbance and the mayhem is grow-AH!!” The sharp-suited female reporter gets tossed aside as the bedlam continues to swell in front of the First Assembly of God church in North Little Rock. A crowd of frantic protestors have gathered before the house of the False God, waving hate-spewing picket signs and shouting denouncing cries at the absolutely bewildered church-goers standing at the entrance. ”DOGS OF A LYING, FALSE GOD!” ”YOU’RE ALL DOOMED!” ”BOBBY PETRINO SUCKS! Their shouts rise above all attempts the church-goers make at reasoning with the fanatical hive. The KATV crew has situated itself behind the group of protestors, catching as much of the mayhem as possible. Anne Pressly recovers after being shoved by one of the protestors and smoothes out her suit. Clearing her throat, she gazes directly into the camera and continues, “As you can see, this highly puzzling situation continues to degenerate into a war of hateful words. I’m not sure exactly what this is about but it looks as though a rival church of some kind is-“ She gets cut off once more by the sudden intrusion of one of the protestors into her personal space. He’s a rather white-bread middle-age balding male wearing a plain brown T-shirt and jeans. Beads of sweat slide down his brow as the afternoon sun beats down upon the gathering. He clutches a wooden picket sign in his hands, its message reads ’HER ANGER GROWS’. Anne looks both exasperated and startled that the man has just suddenly barged into the segment, but her eyes light up as she realizes this could be an opportunity to learn something about the group. The man wears a mean-spirited grin and breathes rapidly, obviously out of breath but yet energized from the function. The news reporter shifts her weight and clears her throat once more, signaling for the cameraman to get into a more interview-applicable position. As she raises the mic to her mouth, the man suddenly leans in and speaks. “HA… I just… want everyone to know… that She hates this entire place. These idiots…” The man looks back at the church, then back into the camera, “… don’t know what’s gonna hit them.” Anne shoots the camera a puzzled side-glance before looking at the man and speaking, “And just who are you, sir? Who are all these people and why are you protesting a church?” The man laughs to himself for a second before shaking his head. “It’s not important who I am. I’m a servant, nothing more. We are Her children, spreading her word and trying to wrangle in the moronic masses before they bake like cookies. Our leader has ordained that we come to this place and make sure they all know they’re an affront to Her name.” Anne gives a quizzical look. “Her? Her who?” The man gasps at Anne as though she just said “Fuck the Bears” in Chicago. His look of disbelief quickly melts into irritation as the man frowns at the obviously confused news reporter. He stares into her eyes, searching for an answer to a question that doesn’t need to be asked. “You’re one of… THEM,” His emphasis on the last word only serves to strengthen the reporter’s confusion as he continues, “One of the blind, helpless sheep of Mother Goose. You disgust me, lady. You ignore the very thing that lights our way and gives us life! You’re doomed, just like all these fucks in this church!” The man then storms off to rejoin the procession. Anne’s eyes widen at the man’s words as she watches him stroll away. She stands in awed silence for a second before returning to the camera. “I apologize for that, viewers. I’m still not sure what’s going on. Stay tuned for additional details.” With that, she lowers the mic and signals for the feed to be cut. The cameraman detaches the news camera from his shoulder and places it down on the grass as the chaos continues in front of the First Assembly of God. The two stand in scant disbelief at the scene before them, trying to take it all in. The cameraman is the first to speak. “This is totally unreal…” He begins, still in shock of the situation, “… what are they even doing? We’ve covered religious protests before, but this is different. These people are almost rabid.” Anne nods, “I know, right? I don’t understand what they’re screaming about, though. They aren’t mentioning anything except the people in the church being doomed and ‘Her’ rage. Who the fuck is ‘Her’??” As if on cue, a gravely, commanding voice booms from behind the news pair: “SHE is life, she is death, she is everything. She is what graces us every morning, grows our crops, oxidizes our air, and brings life to this miserable fucking rock.” Anne and the cameraman whirl around in sync, their eyes falling upon a man who has suddenly come up behind them. He wears a long, flowing black hooded robe; his dark blue eyes pierce messy strands of black and gray hair. Anne and the cameraman exchange looks of confusion and shock at this mysterious sullen figure that has snuck up behind them. They both turn to face the man completely with neither saying a word for what feels like an eternity. The man crosses his arms and cocks an eyebrow. “I realize it may be beyond your intellectual scope to understand where oxygen in the air comes from, but here’s a hint: It’s not God.” The blonde news reporter clears her throat in response and crosses her arms as well, shooting the man a skeptical look. “So are you the leader of these people?” The man squints angrily at Anne and sneers. “You’re observant. It’s good to see that news networks employ something more than brainless microphone scarecrows. Nevertheless, the look on your face tells me that you’re not only wholly misinformed, but possibly mentally-retarded.” His voice is full of venom and amusement, which only serves to piss Anne off. “And who the hell are you???” The man uncrosses his arms and pulls the hood down, revealing a messy bed of black and gray hair. His face is sharp and aged; his expression is unflinching from the contempt he appears to have for the duo before him. The question posed by Anne appears to amuse him, as though she should already know the answer. “I am the Archon, the carrier of Her message to the masses,” The man begins, “I am Dorn Tracer. This…” He motions towards the fevered mass with gleaming pride, “… is The Enclave.” The reporter shifts her weight and shakes her head, “Her message? What message is that?” Dorn’s face breaks into a maniacal grin and he suddenly pushes right through the pair. He strides towards the group of protestors and makes his way to the front. The bedlam is at a fever pitch and now many of the church-goers have made their way outside to yell back at the Enclave. An elderly priest leads the group, waving a Bible and pointing at the protestors. The Archon takes his place at the head of the group and stands a mere 20 feet from the elderly holy man. Suddenly, Dorn’s arms shoot the sky, silencing the protestors behind him. As silence befalls the protestors, the church crowd also quiets. For a few brief moments, there is peace at the First Assembly in North Little Rock. Anne and the cameraman look on in quiet anticipation as Dorn Tracer stands before both groups. After a few daunting seconds, Dorn lowers his arms and turns a steely cold glance to the elder priest. “You, sir, are the epitome of what’s wrong with our world. You’re a blind boy-lover leading hapless sheep into a furnace. You do nothing but spit ridiculous stories about sandaled hobos traveling across the desert turning water into wine! THAT’S NOT EVEN POSSIBLE!” The priest looks absolutely aghast at Dorn’s words and stumbles over his retort. “N-n-n-now see here…!” Dorn’s voice begins to climb, overtaking the priest, “You’re parading around every Sunday in front of a gaggle of idiots reading out of a book a bunch of starving dicks wrote around a fire pit! Your faith is false! It’s WEAK! Disease, famine, the very Earth itself turns against you! The sun becomes hotter every year, slowly drowning this planet in its own cess. I bet your lectures make no note of that, am I correct?!” The priest attempts to articulate an answer, but Dorn’s rant only grows stronger. “Priest, am I to understand you’ve recently found out you’re terminally ill?” A loud gasp sounds from the churchgoers as all eyes turn to the priest. He gulps slowly as beads of sweat begin to roll down his face. He looks around slowly, taking note of their worried glances. The priest looks back at the protestors, who all wear grins of sinister victory. He clears his throat nervously and nods. “God has a special plan for me, yes…” The Archon scoffs and shakes his head, trying to contain his laughter before bursting out, “A special plan?? It’s less than you deserve! May She rot your innards slowly and painfully! You’ll be shitting your own spleen in a month, begging for your release! Your ‘God’ won’t save you. You…are…DOOMED!” I CAN’T TAKE THIS ANYMORE! An enraged voice rings out from the churchgoers, startling everyone in attendance including Dorn. A young man, maybe 20 years of age, comes dashing from the crowd wearing the visage of pure rage. He snatches the Bible from the priest’s hands and makes a beeline straight for Dorn. Though Dorn can clearly see the young man coming, he does nothing but raise his arms in a welcoming fashion. The young man skids to a halt, lets out a mighty war cry, and rears the book back. SLAM! ------------------------------------------- “The paperwork is all prepared, old friend. I’ve already notified the prosecutor’s office, as well as the civil courts.” Lucius Benson looks on in quiet contemplation after delivering his words to Dorn Tracer. The two are aboard Starship Tracer in the ‘office’ area. Dorn just sits at the table, nursing a large bruise on the side of his face with an ice pack. The Archon smiles and nods, seemingly unbothered by his wound. “Good, Lucius. We were running low. I refuse to accept paychecks from Fridge Box Johnny or whatever his stupid name is. Accepting money from blasphemers is like accepting kisses from a cankerous whore.” Benson smiles at the words, but there’s something distant about his face. It’s as though he’s listening to Dorn, but not truly listening. After a moment, he replies, “You’re on the next card, by the way. Have you seen it?” Dorn rolls his eyes and nods. “It’s discrimination, and disgusting at that. They belittle me by sticking me in the ring with weaklings and nobodies. I should be out there, crushing Tessa Windsor and Jay Terror in Her name!” Tracer pulls the ice pack away from his face, revealing a large square-shaped bruise across the right side with the faint indention of a cross in his cheek. The sight amuses Lucius, but he decides not to comment. “Be that as it may, I’ll make short work of the infidels and continue on. I’ve dealt with their kind before. They’re nothing to worry about. They’re pussies, and Glad Bag Donny is an enabler of pussies. The Goddess will see me to victory,” Dorn continues before pressing the ice pack back to his face. Lucius nods and turns to the door. “Of course, old friend. Of course…” And with that, Lucius Benson opens the door and steps out of the office, leaving the Archon to his bruise and his thoughts. Fin.
