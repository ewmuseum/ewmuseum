It's a surprise!

26 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/26/06 'GOTT! Sind Sie das dumm? Ich habe gesagt, das ?l und die Gaseaktien zu verkaufen, und mehr Investition in Pentium mit einem Drittel vom Gewinn zu stellen! Sie wissen was. ..just erhalten es hat gemacht und hat mich zur?ckgerufen.' The phone slammed down onto the cradle as she flopped back on her couch and growled at the incompetence of her investment accountant. How could a man so intelligent be so ignorant? A sigh was released as she allowed her head to loll back and stared at the ceiling. As if she needed this kind of shit to happen when she had other things to deal with; like winning the United States Championship Belt- again. Icy blue eyes rolled as she sat up and rested elbows on her knees to cradle her head. Couldn't life be uncomplicated for one moment? As if to answer her mental question the door bell rang. ' What now, ' she growled as she got up and stalked to the door throwing it open. Surprise was frozen on her features as she found herself gazing at a police officer who looked extremely uncomfortable. Perking a brow she leaned against the frame of her lush penthouse and crossed her arms over her chest in an almost defiant manner. ' What can I do for you officer ? ' He cleared his throat before speaking. ' Are you Ms. Eva VanLorne?' ' Last time I checked. ' ' Well Ms. VanLorne, ' he went on as he removed his hat politely, ' May I come in? ' ' That depends, ' She retorted, ' Are you here to arrest me? ' He chuckled trying to alleviate the awkward tension. ' No Ma'am, nothing like that. ' ' Well then, ' pushing off the door and swing an arm to gesture him inside, ' By all means come in. ' Again he cleared his throat as he stepped inside and took a look around. The diva shut the door eye balling him a bit. Quietly she passed him by gesturing that he should followed she called over her shoulder, ' May I offer you something to drink? ' ' No thank you Ms. VanLor-' ' Eva, ' she stated cutting him off. ' Eva, ' he corrected. In the living area she settled in a chair and crossed her leg while waiting on him to state his business with her. Nervously he took a seat on the edge of her couch and placed his hat on the coffee table. Again, his eyes scanned the surroundings as he made a comment. ' Nice place. ' ' Thank you, ' she continued ' Mind telling me why you are here? ' She didn't like dragging things out unless it benefited her and since she didn't know what this was about, the faster he came out with the quicker she could show him the door. ' Well, it is about your daughter. ' Immediately her gaze snapped upwards to peer at his face with shocked written all over hers. Quickly she recovered, lurching from that chair to pace in front of the fireplace. ' I do not have a daughter. ' The sound of papers rustling drew her gaze back to him as he pulled it from inside his jacket. Unfolding the document he began to read. ' Theresa Snyder born 1994 in Paris France to one Eva VanLorne Age 18 at 2:12 A.M. on Monday June first. ' He lifted his gaze inquiring, ' Are you certain you don't have a daughter? ' Her jaw clamped shut with the force of a vice. In seconds it was causing a headache. Sighing she turned away peering blankly into the fireplace. Yes, she had given birth to a baby when she was 19. She had been too young to be saddled with a child and she had been unmarried. Her adopted parents would never approve of such a thing. Closing her eyes she tried to shut out the memory of lying to them so that she could go away quickly in order to hide her physical state. For ten months she stayed in Paris, under the guise of working for a modeling agency there. She searched through hundreds of applicants for adopting a baby before finally settling on a German couple who were well off and could give her baby a good life. With a deep sigh she turned back to him and answered softly. ' Yes, I gave birth to her. But she was adopted by a good family. Why are you here about her? ' Slowly he stood up and walked over to her his eyes lowered away from her disconcerting gaze. ' A week ago her adoptive parents were killed in an automobile accident on the Autobahn. Neither of them had any living relatives. When the state came to pick her up, they did a bit of research and found the adoption papers from Paris. Eva... I am here because they want you to contact them about possibly taking her in as she is your child. ' The color drained from her face. Her heart quickened and suddenly, she felt the whole world spiraling out of control. Her hand placed a death grip on the mantle of her fireplace as she fought to remain standing. Quickly the officer wrapped an arm around her waist and led her back to the chair. Take her in? Dead? God must be laughing at her right about now. Eyes drifted shut as hands lifted to press against her temples while she sought to sort it all out. Take her in? How could she? She was always on the road. She had no parenting skills what so ever. She would make a horrible mother! That head began to shake side to side in confusion as the officer slowly crouched by her side laying a reassuring hand on her arm. ' Look I know it is a lot to take in. No one is asking for an immediate response. They would like you to contact them either way so that they know what to do with her, ' reaching into his jacket once more he produced a card and laid it on the table next to them, ' There is the number. Take your time and mull it over. I am sorry for the shock Ms. Van-- Eva. ' All she could do was nod. She bent forward, head cradled in her hands as he picked up his hat and showed himself out the door. Her ears pounded with the news he had just bombed her with. Rocking back and forth for what seemed like forever she finally raised her eyes and stared at the card. What should she do? A trembling hand reached out to grasp the card. She stared at as she wondered just that- what would she do? Could she leave a child of her own flesh and blood to be raised as an orphan? She honestly didn't know- at least some part of her didn't. The other part knew all too well what she would do. - TBC-
