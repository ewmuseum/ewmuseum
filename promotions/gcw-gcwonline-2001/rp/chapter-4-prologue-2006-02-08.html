The man had left the reservation a good decade ago, in an attempt to get away from the 'utter crap' as h e called it, of everyday life here. To him, the Sisseton-Whapeaton Sioux here were only trying to avoid the inevitable. Everything from trying to rename the now from ?Devil's Lake? to ?Spirit Lake?. The white man as they called them had taken over decades ago, hell it could be counted almost in multiples of centuries. It wasn't good to fight the inevitable. It wasn't good at all. But he was back in the place he and his family had left years ago. He'd been here before, yes, but he didn't grow up here. In the 1800's his part of the Renville family tree, a line of chiefs and great men, had been banished from the lands here. They were unclean... they had committed crimes against the tribe that equated to practicing of witchcraft and sacrificing all sorts of things. All of this was hog wash of course. Basically the way 'Marc' called it in his modern day thinking, one Renville had gotten jealous of the other and to make sure he got the title of chief, he got he followed the way of the white man and got him outed on the one thing that could get him kicked out. Unable to assume the rightful position, his family had left. But there were few who knew that anymore. The old ways were dying day by day. And even as that wonderful casino went up at 'Spirit Lake', they were slowly adopting more and more ways of their one time enemy. Marc's family had already adapted and learned. Marc had a masters from Oklahoma State in Anthropology with a minor in Archeology, specializing in Ancient American Cultures. He'd studied up this stuff for a long time. But he wasn't the type of guy who walked around digging up shit for people to gawk at while he looked on and some museum made all the cash. No... he'd taken a hint from the Tomb Raider video games and movies, from that horrendous Tia Carrera T.V. show. Why was he back in his family's old home town area? Why would he purposely violate a ban on his branch of the old Chief's line being back here? Well he was here to get paid, pure and simple. And the 'poonanny' as they call it, that he'd picked up since getting here wasn't that bad either. His partners of the last year or so. Both men were white, but that didn't change anything. They were here for a common goal. But this time they were going off faith. Before they found out that they had been there. That would be free money, maybe even enough to retire on. Did he try to woo her?? Layla spoke up. ?No, my lord and uncle. His ambitions to please you failed. She was the protector of his intended target. A woman named Northsky... She used her ability to ruin his mind so that he would forget what he knew. He intended to take her and make her his wife, then bring her to you bearing his child. However it ruined his memories of us as well. We knew we had to act to awaken you after what he found.? The seraph gave her an inquisitive look. ?I got this memory stone off of an operative of Ishram's Order of the Hammer after fucking him senseless and killing him. This Northsky woman is of prime importance to us, and you My Lord.? She pulled out a large crystal looking item from one of her jeans pockets. She blew on it, and then tossed it onto the ground. Immediately in front of the whole group a vision appeared. That of a man and woman in the desert, kneeling on a blanket. The woman's clothes are torn and bloody. They look on as the woman's back suddenly explodes out into a pair of dusky gray colored wings, with a white pattern that's visible at the right angles. The woman floats up a bit as the wings expose themselves to the air of the Material Plane for the first time and burst into an ethereal flame for a few seconds. She then falls back down to earth into the arms of the Native American man who was with her. ?Who is this?? ?Daniel Lynn Northsky, is her name My Lord.? ?What is she?? ?Sir, she is the sixth great granddaughter of the Morning Star, Kokabel. She's a throw back from what we've seen sir. She's already had contact with a few of the others who were aligned with the Morning Star. She's a Throne of the Stars born here on Earth!? Astaroth, Dark Lord of Sorcery had heard enough. Steven had just enough time to stand up and look to be ready to make a bolt for it when the seraph had seen him... His soul was no longer his with a wave of the angel's hand. Steven's eyes became like burning embers and disappeared as the physical energy that was his spirit waifed over in the air towards the newly reborn creature. ?Take me to this daughter of the Morning Star.
