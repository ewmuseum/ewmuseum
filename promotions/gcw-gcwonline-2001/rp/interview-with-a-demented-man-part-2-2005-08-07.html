Interview with a Demented Man Part 2

7 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/07/05 *when i last left you I had unlocked something in my mind in which i didnt want to me opened or revealed to anyone ever again, but the constent pushing abd badgering of my fathers voice and imagae in my head, left me no choice, but to let the secret out.* I killed my father that hot summers day when we were still one big happy family *I just gotten out of high school, graduated, got a diploma, and evrything. The first thing my father says to me is tomorrow morning i am taking to down to the local recruitment office for the Marines, and we are siging you up. Now my father was a marine at heart, retired from the marines after 24 years, the marines and his glory days was all that he talked about. So thinking his son would follow in his footsteps, and become a great marine was a dream of his ever since I was first born* 'I don't wanna become a marine dad', I said *My father turns to me, with a look on his face I havent never seen before in my life* 'What did you say?', he replied 'I don't wanna go to the recruitment office, I don't wanna become a marine, dad. I thought about this long, and hard, but i wanna become a professional wrestler, but not just anything professional wrestler, but one that is feared, and respected for what he does in the ring.' *Now growing up I have been hit by my father from punishment for things I have done, but after i told him this he punched me right in the mouth, and i thought to myself what has come over him? Why did he just hit me? Then just out of basic instinct, and my training to become a professional wrestler i oubched him back, but not in the face. I hit him right in the chest which drove the breath right out nof him, but then something happened in which i didn't expect, my father drops to the floor grabbing his chest, and yells that he can't feel his arm.* *Now im thinking is this some sort of trick my dad would play with me, so when i bend down to see if he is ok, he would punch me back. No, this time something is definetly wrong, as I bend down next to my father i can't hear he breathing or see his chest go up and down trying to take a breath in. So i run to the phone, and dial 911* '911 please state your emergency' 'I think i killed my father,'I said 'Ok, now what happened' 'well i dont him i didn't want to do anything, and he punched me in the mouth, so i punched him back, and i really do think i killed him,' I said 'Ok son, we have sent a polie unit out to your house, you have to stay there till they get there so they can get the entire story, and ask you a few things' 'Wait, i was just defending myself, he hit me, i hit him back thats all it was about, nothing more, nothing less,' I said *After I said that I hung up the phone, and walked into my room, grabbed a few things, walked back into the doorway where my father was lying on the ground, and as i open the front door I can see the siren lights of a police car and a ambulance turn the corner, so i salm the door shut, and run as a fast as i can down the street, and hide behind a dumpster until they get there* *as i peak around the dumpster i see the EMT's take the gurnee in, and wheel my father out of the house on it, with one of the EMT's on top gving him CPR, and then all of a sudden a single tear rolls down my face. I think to myself what is this emotion taht I am feeling right now.* *Now you all see why i couldnt let that part of my past get out, and get free. Now onto the present day, and the future of me Rick Diamond aka Dimensioner, who is also known as The Demented One, who will put his body thorugh anything, anyone to get whatever, or go whereever I want to* X, you say your ready for one of my regualr beatings, my regular poundinmgs i give everyone when they step into the squared circle with the sickest man in professional wrestling history. You say taht you have studied all my matches, you have gotten mentally prepared for me, and this match, well theres one thing i have to say to you X. There is no way, AND I MEAN ABSOULUTLY NO WAY YOU CAN PREPARE PHYSICALLY AND MENTALLY FOR THE BEATINGS THAT I DEAL OUT. You continue to say that i don't know anything about you, you couldn't be more wrong. You little special move, what do you call it, oh the X-Buster, hwo do you expect to get a 7 foot tall 300 pound man above your head without even having your legs, and/or ankles give out, or even shatter from the pressure. *I start laughing at the sound of my own words, this man X who thinks he knows he all that well , doesnt know a damn thing about me.* X, i hate to tell you but your going to be a turning point, a rung on a ladder, a stepping stone for me to climb the GCW ladder, and start having gold around my waste. *with that said a flashback of dangerous games appears in my mind again for some apparent reason, and for some reason, I am getting a strabge feeling like i need to protect thsis person from something, but i dont know what yet* *Erik hits Dimensioner with a missile drop kick right to the face. Dimesioner stumbles back a step, Erik getting on his hands and knees as Eva steps up and launches for flying mantis leap. But Dimensioner catches her!! He hefts her up over head walking a circle and pressing her up and down. Kicking and screaming Eva is helpless. Bryan: Eva still in big trouble here. Pete: This really isn’t pretty. The ref forces Erik out of the ring and turns around just in time to see Eva swing down from overhead like a rag doll and slam into the mat, bouncing horrifically. The crowd cries out with grief as Dimensioner sits there for a moment grinning, moving arrogantly to get back on his feet he looks around and pumps his arm in the air before leaning down over a prone Eva and helping her to her feet. A giant hand wrap around her throat and her eyes widen instantly, in desperation she levels a hard kick into Dimensioner's nether regions.* To finsih this up X, come Worldwide if i was you i would call the bloodbank to see if they have your type of blood in stock, call the local hospital, and get a room ready for you, and then call the local EMT's so they can be standing by for when I hit the End of Reality, and i snap your back into two pieces. *As i finsih speaking three letters pop into my head, EVL, and then my fatehrs voice begins to speak in my mind.* You must protect someone with these intials Rick, EVL. Something terrible is going to happen, and you must protect them from everything By the way son, i am not mad at you anymore for choosing wrestling over the marines, you have done well and im proud of you *a single tear rolls down my face once again*
