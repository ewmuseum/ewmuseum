Sisyphus is happy

24 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/24 'I don't want to be here.' The words more or less all form together in a sigh. I would lean my heavy head back against this comfy brick wall, but doing so would just cause the obnoxiously loud techno music to rattle my skull with vibrations from the brick. I can already feel the bassline shaking my spine slightly, numbing any body part that touches the hard, course surface. Numbing down to where I can almost feel nothing, feel like I'm not here. Like I'm at home, with Sarah. And I've pretty much given up looking around anymore. Standing here, leaning against the wall. Old warehouse can barely stand and needs tearing down. So let's have a rave in it, because we're angst filled teenagers. Take a look at me, Dad, I'm drinking beer! And you can't stop me. Cuz I'm my own person and I can't be stopped by nothing. Ahhh, was I ever that young? Look over to my left. The people and the clothes haven't changed, going in and out like rats in a maze. Never will they find an end. It's all only one big labyrinth anymore, designed not to train or to reward, but to confuse. They're on display in some cosmic showcase, being shown off as the cute little scurriers they are. Typical emo shit rock rebellious losers, all of them. I'll give credit that they don't think they're going to change the world, but most of them are still delusional to think they can help themselves and change themselves at all. They're stuck in more of a rut than anybody I know of, and they don't even have the know-how or the balls to know it. Saddest story in the history of the planet, if you ask me. Show up with the platform ten inch leather boots that go up to the thigh. Black leather skirt or pants, which isn't gender related at all. Throw on a tight fitting fishnet shirt, maybe a pair of angel wings or devil horns, black nailpolish, your favorite choker or occasional ballgag as a necklace, black eyeliner that shows you're a deep and thoughful person. Top it all off, why don't you, with the lipstick and the glitter, the red or blue wig, and glow sticks galore. Fresh marks on the wrist, deep enough to show that you cut yourself but not deep enough to threaten your life, are more or less a signature that someone's been there, that you've been there. That you've felt so much pain and that the world doesn't know what it's like to be you. Your problems are horrid. Your problems are inescapable, so you'll just put them off for a little while by numbing your intelligence and listening to some of the worst music created by man or machine. Good job. You just stuck it to The MAN tonight. 'Would you get out here already?' You could say I'm getting impatient. Been waiting outside this intellectual cesspool for a good twenty minutes now. I'd much rather be at home. Sleeping. Having sex. Jerking off. Watching infomercials. Anything's better than standing here, doing nothing but waiting. The jackass went in twenty minutes ago. Said he needed to talk to the DJ or something. I don't know. It wasn't really deserving of my attention. All I know is that Amos owes me one for this. Hell, maybe he's in there trying to find a wife like he talked about. Talked about settling down and conforming, because that's the new way of rebellion. Get a job, get a wife, get a life. I don't know how many girls in there go for the old timey dressing, hair down to his ass, Punisher fan that he is. But if there's a woman in this rave that actually wants a philosophical bullshit spewing, good music appreciating, Transformers watching prick to marry, then godspeed that they find each other. Speed being key there. Get me the fuck out of here. I think, honestly, it's because I'm too old. That's why I'm so fucking miserable here. Or anywhere. Everywhere. They say twenty-six is young. I say they're completely full of shit. And the reason I say that is because I'm twenty-six, and I feel like I can remember thirty-five years ago. I swear to God I can recall the Carter administration. But in all sincerity, I can't recall but a few moments of those twenty-six years, and most of them are just a large part of a hazy blur. A giant nebula in the middle of the infinite nothing of space that is existence. And being able to look back and actually see, with relative clarity, just how much a person goes through by the time he reaches twenty-six... ...I really don't want to be around a bunch of sixteen year olds. Because I'm fucking old. ...and you know... You know what I would love to do to these little fucks? I'd love to just walk over to them. The three standing right over there by the door, and say, 'Why aren't you guys inside, taking part in some good mind numbing?' Ha! It appears that I have no subconscious anymore. My actions are no longer considered before they take place, but rather I have succumbed to the old way of life I used to live: shooting from the hip. Perhaps, though, it is some sort of gift. Perhaps it is, in a sense, an ability to look into the future...that I'm not actually doing this per se, but that I have already known what the consequences were with definition and thus have come to my current course of action without even having to make a conscious decision! It's like having your cake and eating it too. So long as I don't lose my interior monologue I'll be fine. And, as expected, only one of the three even looks at me for the slightest of seconds. The other two take drags on their cigarettes and callously look away. I don't suspect it's often that some stange, creepy, old man comes up and bothers these youngins. But God damn, it's fuckin fun. 'Get to be too much for ya? A little too much of the laser lights and the fog machines and the whammie bars?' The one girl that paid me some attention would be cute. If she was about eight years older I might be able to justify touching her. The other girl of the three turns even further away, but flicks her cancer stick in my direction and makes herself out to be the leader of this small pack with a, 'Fuck off.' And just like that. The game is afoot! 'I'm just kidding. I actually like these guys. Well...I mean, I like them when I can...you know...understand the words and hear some sort of treble. The song in general loses all meaning when the bass is up so loud all you can hear is the initial pounding, followed by the rattling of the girders of the buildings around. But yeah, I like them about the same as most other neo-punk rock, whiney, self-absorbed bands that are out and around. Shirts in every Hot Topic you can find.' The male of the species has decided to finally grow a pair. There's not much hesitation in his voice, either. Unlike most wrestlers, I don't exactly have a body that you can pick out in a crowd, unless said body isn't wearing a shirt. Then the women come in hordes, pure hordes. But, when I'm wearing a simple black T-shirt, and I've got a hat hiding my perfect hair, it's easy to blend in and look like your average guy. That's what's actually kinda funny about all this. Because I, too, happen to be dressed in all black. It's a stroke of luck. But, because I don't have any leather on my self except for my ten dollar belt I got from SEARS, clearly I do not belong here and should not be speaking to them. And so this kid turns and speaks in a way where his jaw barely moves and the pathetic stubble he would like to call a goatee simply kind of twitches under some sort of inner jaw movement. God forbid his cigarette fall to the ground, and God forbid he use his hand to steady it. 'I thin' we toll ya to fuck off.' Awwwww... This one has a British accent. Granted, this one has a very, very bad British accent. The kid's from Western New York. No doubt about that. But, he's listened to a few Ramones albums and he likes The Cure, and to show that he has a wide range of musical appreciation, he probably enjoys hardcore industrial techno rock and maybe even some Hendrix. But one thing that I know is for sure: he fakes the accent piss poor. 'Well, there's no need to get all agressive about it. I've just been chillin out here for a long time and I saw yous guys and I figured what's the point of sitting there not saying anything? Get out. Live a little. Talk to other human beings. Comm-une-ick-ate. Reach out and touch someone.' Oh yeah. Honestly, I'm having fun now. Don't get me wrong. I still really wish that Amos would get out here so we can go home. Sleep right now would be a good thing. The only good thing I've found so far with a lack of said sleep of goodness is that being tired lowers my inhabitions. And it doesn't leave a hangover, at least not in the same way, like getting plastered on booze. Like last night. ...and the night before. Anyway, the two that like to first ignore then talk toss away their cigarettes and look to head inside. 'You stayin' out 'ere wit the narc if ya want. But yer ain' gettin' any o' me E if yur gonna be hangin' aroun' 'im all noight.' 'Me-yee? What's the deal with that?' Jiles the Confused looks at me with a pulled down forehead. 'What?' 'I asked what Me-yee is. A hooker?' 'Me! E! My E. Bliss. Ebullience. Twilight Zone.' '...' 'Little white pills fall out in a row, ya know?' 'Ahhh...' He looks at me like I don't know what little white pills are all about. He has no idea. Much like Sarah, he has no idea, and it's because I choose not to tell him, or her. Tell them about what it feels like to be addicted, to have your life ruled by a little white bullet that takes the pain and staves it off, sending it to a place where it can't come back until you close your eyes at the end of the day. One little pill can take all your hate and rage and sorrow and regret and condense it all into a tiny little ball, and place it somewhere deep inside you where it can collect compounded interest and then you get the bill in the mail and by the time you can pay it off, too late. You're dead. Maybe my pills never made my spinal fluid flow the wrong way, but I know what it's all about. The two whom I'll refer to as 'The Rude Ones' step back into the mess of the club. Go have fun doing your E. Me...I like my spinal fluid flowing the way it does. And I don't like to be happy. Why would I take a drug that makes me just that? And the quiet girl is still enjoying her cigarette. Arms crossed in front of her, looking like she's shivvering off the cold. And it's fucking August. And I can see her eyes darting this way and that. Trying her hardest to find something to focus on, trying to find something to look at. She has an infinite amount of things to stare at, even if it's just a spot on the wall. And yet, she can't help but look at me every two seconds. Kind of like the end of a life or the starting of something new, when you concentrate on everything but the obvious. 'Want me to fuck off, too?' She almost drops her cigarette. It's funny because she's probably told everybody that it's her dream that she'd be at some rave somewhere and she'd be all alone and some big guy that was like six years older than her would come up and rape her and shit. And it was all talk. She's shitting herself right now because she thinks I'm that guy. I'm the karma that's coming around, giving her what she's always said she's always wanted. And she knows she was lying, and she's convinced that this is her punishment. I have to say, it's almost entertaining to watch her thoughts dart back and forth. They might as well have been transmitted in the air for every TV station to pick up on for miles. But she's trying to be tough about it. Maybe secretly she'll try to force herself to see her apparent fantasy through. She might actually want to get raped in a back alley of some club because that would really stick it to her parents and friends. That way she can go home to them, crying and gaining a pity party and obtaining their sympathy for an eternity to come. And then if she's ever in some sort of situation where she's competing with someone she loves over who has the worst life, she can go like this: 'I work all day and come home to your crap! I don't need this!' 'Well the joke's on you! I was raped in an alley! Top that!' Well, I don't think so, little girl. I'm not the guy that makes your nightmares come true. Sorry. 'I don't care if you fuck off or do whatever you're doing.' 'Sure you do. After all, I'm the creepy old guy.' 'I said I don't care. You're just another fucking pointless life to me. Scurrying in your nine to five life and you can do that if you want.' Inside I can't help but laugh. Outside too, for that matter. 'Nine to five. Yeah. That's what I have.' 'Look, whatever. I'm finishing this stick and I'm going back in there and that's the last you'll ever know of me.' 'And that's the last you'll ever know of me. So we have that in common.' I can see her hesitate on taking that last drag. She really, really wishes I was there for her. She wishes that the world spinned because she was alive and she's convinced that whatever she's not looking at turns stark white and exists no longer. But I can tell this isn't really her. She's one of the ones that dons the makeup and buys the clothes and listens to the music because she wants to fit in with people that already do. She's not one of those raverpunks that is in it for the, 'I hate you Dad, because you told me to mow the lawn! I hate you Mom, because you flushed my weed! I hate you school, because I have to sit in you! Why don't you all fuck off and die!' lyrics that are clearly so freeing and so amazingly comprised for every song you can put three chords to. Or a prerecorded couple riffs and a techno beat to. Or whatever. She's here because she wants to want to be here. Bad. 'Just go back to your fucking Jimmy life.' 'Jimmy life? What the fuck is that?' I must be getting old. I have no clue what that slang is. And I'm ususally on top of the game. 'Jimmy Corrigan.' '...' '...The Smartest Kid on Earth.' '...' 'The best social and personal commentary comic to be written yet.' She must really like Jimmy Corrigan, The Smartest Kid on Earth. Also must never have read The Tao of Pooh. Now that's commentary on how to live your life. Speaking of smartest kid, where is Amos? 'You're a fucking Jimmy. You probably work a job you hate in a life that has no purpose. Your life was planned out and set for you when you were six like you were thirty-six. And you're nothing now that you're thirty-six other than a six year old loser. Dead end everything leading to a dead end life. The only difference is that you are trying to hold on to your youth by hanging out at raves, coming on to girls that are just trying to smoke a fucking cigarette.' Wow. You can't really be more wrong. But it's funny. Or could she be right, in a convoluted way? 'That's what a Jimmy is?' 'I prefer it over the cliche of 'narc.'' It's funny because for as much as she tries to get true, she gets the most important fact false. And as I see Amos finally get out of the club intact, I can't help but smile. Can't help but laugh at it all. She's right. I work a thankless job in a life that I frankly don't know where it's going. Everything is a dead end, as everything's ending with death. And what the hell am I doing at this rave? I'm hanging out with a friend who's trying to get some money from a DJ. Hoping he's gonna owe me somehow, or that he'll buy me some booze or something, honestly. Whatever the reason is, I'm sure it's pretty juvenile. 'You do have a point.' She puts on her badass fascade yet again and turns away, taking another drag. 'Whatever.' Amos is signaling to me that we're gonna get going. I'm betting that he wants to be here about as much as I do, and I'm all for packing up and leaving early. ...but I just have to do one thing. 'You know, girl...you've definitely changed my life.' She still doesn't care. Holds her cigarette in between her two fingers and looks away from me even more. 'I used to think I was the most pointless thing on this Earth. That I was just wandering around, being miserable all the time. But I must say that you've enlightened me. Because while I go out and am miserable because I aim to be, your friends in there that are getting the same chords and beats pounded into their head go out to have a good time, but end up being miserable. And thus, they're complete failures. But you...' She eyes me one more time. She knows I'm right before I can even say it. 'You're faking to go out and have a great time to be miserable. You're the most backwards one of all of us, aren't you? And you're standing here. Now. Because you know it, and half of you wants to go home and half of you wants to go in there and become a drone. And yet you sit here, judge me, and tell me not to judge you. And, when you think about it, your entire life has built up to this moment. Every single action or choice you've ever made has led you down a road, and the end of that road is that you are standing outside this club, smoking that cigarette, with this older guy talking to you. Just like everything that I've ever done, every good or bad time that I've ever had, has culminated in me being somewhere that I don't want to be...and realizing this truth.' Ripping open wounds. It's easy, and it's fun. The parallels are always easy to find if you want to. I could call Bryan Mayhem and Virgil Ambrosi pretentious, but why is that? Because they're vampires or because they hide behind makeup? Maybe they hide behind violence and cling to some sort of insanity as an excuse for acting so foolish. Or maybe, after everything is said and done, they actually are the person that they wish to be, and that's what scares them the most. I know I'm a hypocrite. I know I'm an asshole. And I know that ripping open my own wounds on a constant basis makes me little more than a masochist. And if that's what I am, at least I'm a success at it. If hurting my own psyche is what I do constantly, at least I'm good at it. I am Sisyphus. This is my rock. Every day I push it up this hill, and every time I get it nearly to the top it falls all the way back down. And honestly, I am happy with it. Why? Because it is my rock. It is my hill. And it is my punishment and my life. Not Caldera's. Not Eva's. Not Mayhem or Ambrosi's. Not Sarah's or Amos' or this girl's. It's mine. Ripping open my own wounds is mine, and nobody can take it from me. Amos waves again. He really wants to go. And he unfreezes me from this moment, and until now I haven't noticed that this girl has realized something, anything about herself. Maybe this is going to be a day in the sea of nothingness that she will remember. 'And by the way... I've read Jimmy Corrigan eight times, you convoluted fuck.' Smile on the inside. You've destroyed to construct today, Johnny-boy. The Smartest Kid on Earth.
