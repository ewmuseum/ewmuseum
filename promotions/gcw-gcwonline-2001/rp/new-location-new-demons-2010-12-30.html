New Location, New Demons

30 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/30/10 The light coating of fluffy snow from earlier today finally stuck to the ground for the first time this winter down in El Paso, Texas. The day has slowly warmed up past the freezing point and the snow was beginning to melt, by the end of the day the snow would be gone. Newfoundland lays a bundle of mixed flowers in front of a small tombstone and brushes the snow off the face of the tombstone with the sleeve of his black leather jacket. ‘Forgive me for leaving you behind and allowing the darkness to win’ was engraved into the stony surface and under the saying was ‘Sasha’. Newfoundland runs his pointer and middle finger over the name as he whispers, “It wasn’t your fault that I couldn’t save you from him or from myself. If I was only stronger and wouldn’t allow him to win those mind games he played against me. I made sure he would never return to this plane of existing even thou it partly killed me inside doing so.” “Well, well, the murder returns to gloat about getting free of the killing of his girlfriend and the mother of his unborn child.” says a high pitch voice. KJ slowly stands up brushing off the snow off his pants legs and takes a deep breath before speaking to the person who accused him killing Sasha. “Lowe, you’re still mad about losing your first ever case? If you always win then you cannot get better because you cannot learn from your mistakes. I have nothing personal against you because you were doing your job, but you were barking up the wrong tree.” As he finishes he turns around looking down at the detective that was good foot shorter than him. Lowe is a very short, chubby, and a balding man that has a voice of six year old boy that still believes the girls are icky. From the looks of Lowe’s outfit the times has not been kind to him after losing his perfect record on convictions. He is wearing a ruffle white dress shirt that was nearly see-through after all of the washes it has been through the years. There are deep yellow sweat stains in the arm pits and on the neckline of the shirt. He is wearing light blue overalls with patches on the knees and he is wearing yellow crocks that are one size too small for his feet. Lowe clicks his tongue as he spoke, “You kept telling us about this Golden Mask Killer that was on the loose and was killing people as he looked for you. Each place he killed was the place you just visit early that week, but eventually the killing caught up with you in the same city and that’s why I was called in. Because I get results and I find the person responsible.” The Golden Mask Killer was given this name because neighbors’ of the deceased witnesses a person with a golden mask entering the homes of victims and brutally murdered them with a piece of glass. Newfoundland never actually told the officials that he accidental made the Golden Mask Killer because the mask the killer was using was the mask that controlled his life for three years before his mother freed him from the suffering of listening to the voices in the mask that kept telling him to do evil deeds to feed their blood lust. The mask was created by the souls of pure evil people by the dark lord that offered to bring his girlfriend from high school back to life. KJ truly believed the dark lord lies and was given the mask to do hideous deeds for him. The mask turned him into a demon with a single emotion: hate. With the voices in the mask telling to murder everyone his’ eyes laid on, he could never think for himself and he became numb to all of the blood he was spilling around him. The dark lord asked him to collect the demons that escape from the reach with a wooden sword that he called The Requite. The Requite was the size of practice wooden sword, but the sword had a special feat that made it different from other wood swords. The Requite absorbed the demons into the sword increasing the power to go after the bigger demons. On the last mission for the dark lord, he was supposed to capture an easy demon and the dark lord would revive his girlfriend from the dead. When KJ finally reached the location underground, he was scared to death from what he seen the so called demon. It wasn’t demon at all, it was an angel and the angel was his mother. The dark lord was trying to kill the last person that could show the truth of KJ’s current situation. His’ mother drop a bombshell that she was only allowed a short time on this plane with KJ and his father. She also told that his father was actually a fallen angel and was stripped of his wings of refusing to allow the killing of a young child. The young child was KJ. KJ’s mother freed him from any guilt that he committed during the time he was under the spell of the mask and told him to repay his debt to not only her, but the whole world. She told him that he needs to use The Requite to destroy any demons that was corrupting the population. She doesn’t want the dark lord to gain any more power because each demon that KJ captures was actually stripped off its power and was given to the dark lord for his evil usages. Couple weeks after being freed of the mask and now has a new goal in life to preserve life not destroy it. He jump from job to job as mercenary before landing a great paying job as the right hand man of the leading Russian drug lord. During his time there he becomes the lover to the drug lord’s wife. He finally seen the demon that was causing the Russian be so evil. The demon revealed himself during the dinner after the drug lord told a story of him murdering a whole village on the country side just for the fun. Sasha was the drug lord wife and she wanted to be freed from the evil man. She asked Newfoundland to kill him off, so she could escape to the United States. KJ promised her that he would punish for all of the crimes that he commit against every innocent citizen that was brutally murdered by his’ hands. KJ tied the drug lord in his thrown at end of the long table that he ate at with the entire high ranking members in his’ gang. Newfoundland informed him that he was going die tonight by his hands, but his death was going be slow and painful. Newfoundland slammed the drug lord’s face into a plate full of cocaine. He pulled the head of the drug dealer up and pulled out his tongue, he quickly stabbed a hypodermic needle filled with heroine into his’ tongue. Newfoundland decided to make the death look like a suicide and cut both of the drug lord’s wrists. He is going let the drug lord slowly bleed to death while on an unnatural high from the drugs he was selling. Couple months after the murder of the drug lord, the golden mask resurfaces in El Paso, Texas and was looking for Sasha for hiring KJ to do her dirty work. The poor person who was under the control of the mask kidnapped Sasha and tied her to a cross in an abandoned church. As KJ stepped foot into the church to save his lover, the person engulf Sasha into napalm and burned her in alive in front KJ. In the blood rage, Newfoundland used The Requite with hate for the first time in the many months of being free of the mask. He cut the person in front of him off at his waist and when the top off fall backward the golden mask fall of the person’s face. KJ’s face went ghost white as he seen the face of the person he just killed was a kid no older than six. The magic of the mask cause the false image of a larger person to appear in front the kid. From the day forth KJ was never same because not only did he see the death of his girlfriend, but he just slaughters a person he was trying to protect. He was slowly turning back to the demon that was known as the detrimental dragon. Lowe snaps his fingers in front KJ’s face and growls in his squeaky voice, “Why are you smiling, criminal?” Newfoundland didn’t realize that he had drifted off remembering how he manages to screw up his life up to now. “I was just trying to picture how you could pitch to get clients after losing the perfect record. Frankly, you’re the ugliest bastards I seen in my life and I know that if –points at Lowe’s clothing- this is what people call fashion. I am happy to stay behind the homeless looking era.” KJ smile widens as he continued, “Actually I need your advice on what I should do. Don’t worry it nothing with your short comings with the trial, but what I should do now.” Lowe stares at KJ with his bitty eyes “Either I join a wrestling federation called GCW,” says KJ lifting his left hand up with palm up and then brought up his right hand up with palm up, “Or sacrifice a whole college of virgin Christian girls.” “Christian girls” Lowe asked puzzled KJ says as walks by Lowe patting him on the shoulder, “What I was thinking? There is no such thing has virgin Christian girls. That would mean I only have one choose then. I am going be joining Global Championship Wrestling.” Lowe spins around his heels to KJ, who has pulled hand sanitizer and was washing his hands from the filth of Lowe. “Are you really working with the dark lord again?” “Psh no,” KJ says over his shoulder as he rubbed his hands together, “I have heard that place was full of sick, corrupted bastards and witches. I figured that would be the best place to restart my career.”
