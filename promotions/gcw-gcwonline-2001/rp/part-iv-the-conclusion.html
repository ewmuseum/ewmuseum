Part IV The Conclusion

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: The same night Two hours after Fareed Zakaria raced desperately to the Newsweek building, Gaius Octavianus is in his office penthouse. It is getting close to 3am and Gaius is growing impatient. With him in the penthouse office is "The Turk," a mercenary hired by his friend in Rome. In the back of the office are two young boys, tied to metal chairs with a piece of tape over their mouths and a bunch of wires running from the chairs. The boys are asleep for the moment and Gaius can not understand why the reporter has not yet returned with the evidence. "What the hell is taking him so long?" "Maybe he split, boss." "He's going to leave his two children to die? I don't think so." "Want me to call Carlo?" Carlo was on the security detail at the Newsweek building. He is also on Gaius' payroll and should have brought the reporter and the evidence over an hour earlier to insure Gaius was not double crossed. "Give him ten more minutes. So 'Turk,' was it easy to get the children out of Cairo?" "It was easier than anticipated boss. All it cost was about $10,000 to bribe the airport personnel." "Splendid. Those contacts will come in handy." Just then, the reporter comes crashing in the door, as if he had run up the entire 104 story staircase without stopping. He has a package under his arm. As he is bent over, trying to catch his breath, the elevator opens and out walks Carlos, with a gun drawn. "Thought you could escape from me?" Fareed, still out of breath, turns to the security guard. "You...you...bastard....the gun...is...not...necessary..." He turns around to Gaius. "I have what you want, now release my kids." Gaius turns his chair around so that he is face to face with the reporter. "The Turk" is positioned immediately next to Gaius, standing and looking at the reporter with a sinister smirk. "First hand over what I want." "Both of the tapes are in there, along with all the documentation. I tried to find the old men who could collaborate the story, but they seem to have disappeared." Gaius' facial expression turns to one of a knowing grin. He knows that these "witnesses" will never return to haunt his dreams of a global corporate empire. "They are of no concern to me anymore. That leak has been taken care of. Now hand over the envelop." Fareed looks down at the package in his hand, and then looks at his kids. "Do you really need to think about your decision? They are your children Fareed." "They are, and I love them. But you are the devil incarnate, and you must be stopped." Gaius' actually laughs at this. "Still you attempt to defy me. You really have not learned anything yet have you? Fareed, I'm five steps ahead of you already. So just hand over the envelop and no one else will have to die." Fareed still is reluctant to hand over the valuable evidence, which he knows is the only chance anyone has to take down the Octavianus family. Gaius, sensing his hesitation, turns to "the Turk" and whispers something in his ear. "The Turk" takes out a small remote and hands it to his boss while Carlos continues to point his gun right at the reporter in case he tries something. "Do you really think I didn't anticipate your hesitation? You think I am bluffing. Well, do you know what this is?" "It looks like a remote." "It is. Watch this, you'll love it." As Octavian presses the button, the two young boys awake to an electric charge being run through the chairs. ARRRRGGGHGHHHHHHHH.....PPPPPPPLLLLLLLLEEEEEEEAAAAAAAASSSSSSSEEEEEEE....NNNNNNOOOOOOOOO...... The screaming and agony of his young sons is almost too much to bear. The reporter starts screaming and pleading to stop it. After a minute, Gaius lets go of the button. "Still think I'm playing?" "NO! NOO!....." The reporter finally breaks completely. "Ok. Gaius. You win. Here." And he throws the envelop on the desk. Gaius picks it up and looks inside. All the evidence and the videotapes are there. "Good boy Fareed." The reporter collapses onto the floor out of sheer exhaustion and emotional strain, in tears. "Give my children back to me." Gaius stares at his enemy. Knowing that he has finally been broken, Gaius ponders what to do next with him. One choice would be to get rid of the troublesome reporter and his two children for good, and the other option is to let them go into poverty and obscurity. After a moment, Gaius decides enough is enough....for tonight... "Fareed, I'm telling you this once. If I find out you have any copies of this left, or any of this information somehow finds it's way into the hands of law enforcement. Then your children will be the ones to pay, not you. Do we understand each other?" "Yes Gaius. We understand perfectly." "Excellent....'Turk,' release his sons." 'The Turk' walks over to the two young boys, disconnects the wires, and lets the two exhausted children go. They run immediately to their father, who hugs them as if he hasn't seen them in forever. Gaius stares coldly at the emotional scene. "Now get out of my sight." As the reporter turns with his children to leave, Fareed looks back one last time at Gaius, who returns the stare. The look is one of mutual hatred, and the reporter's expression says 'one day I'll have my revenge for this.' Gaius, interpreting his expression exactly, has them stopped at the door. "Carlos, hold them at the door." The security guards tells the reporter and his kids to halt, which they promptly obey. "One last thing Fareed." "What, Gaius?" "How's that cigarette burn?" "What? The one on my forearm? It hurts like hell. Is that what you wanted to hear?" "No actually. But you proved my point. You'll never stop trying to get me will you? Even though I was merciful. You are going to come back in five or ten years and haunt me until the day one of us dies." The reporter bends down and whispers for his children to run as fast as their hurt bodies can out of this building to the apartment of a family friend. The children do as they are told and leave the penthouse by bolting down the fire escape. The reporter then turns around to Gaius again, after making sure his children were out of harm's way. "The Turk" and Carlos are now directly behind Gaius' chair. "You are right Gaius. I'll never stop looking for dirt on you." "Well then there is only one way to hammer home my point so that you never forget it." Gaius turns to Carlos and grabs his gun and points it at the reporter. "What're you....?" BAM The loud explosion echos as the bullet pierces the reporter's other forearm at the exact spot where the cigarette burn had been on the opposite arm. "ARRRRRGGGGHHHHHH...." "I think we finally understand one another..." The reporter falls to the floor crying in agony. "Carlos, 'Turk,' Take this piece of trash out to the curb..." The two listen to their boss, grab the bloodied reporter, and begin to leave. "Oh and Fareed....Next time I won't miss...." The reporter, leaning on the two large men on either side of him, looks back completely broken at his adversary and then bows his head and passes out. As the men leave the room, Gaius grabs the envelop. He waits for his men to close the door before he goes over to the fireplace. He takes out the two videos, puts them under his arm, and throws the various photos and documentation into the fire. He then grabs the two tapes, breaks them over his knee, and throws the film into the fire. Throwing the remaining plastic pieces into a trash bin, Octavian finally sits down and relaxes for the first time in weeks. He grabs a cigar from his cigar box and lights it, staring out into his new adoptive city...on top of the world...yet again...
