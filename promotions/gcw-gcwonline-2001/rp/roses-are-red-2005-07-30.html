Roses are red...

30 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/30/2005 Dangerous Games had been rough, really rough. Eva stood in the bathroom of her hotel room carefully applying an ice pack to her right elbow with a grimace and hiss. She hadn't won the Dangerous Games match but she felt she had finally proven to the roster she could hold her own. In all honesty by the end of the show several of those male wrestlers were treating her differently. Where there had once only been lust in their eyes now resided a mingled respect for the woman had been one of the three last people in the ring. Sighing the robe clad diva meandered out of the bathroom heading for her bed. She would not make it there, however, as the sound of knocking paused her steps. Peering at the door over her shoulder a moment she withdrew the ice pack from her elbow and tossed it casually on the end table before heading for the door and peering through the peephole. On the other side of that concave glass all she could see was a rather extravagant bouquet of flowers. Aching a brow her hands lifted to unlock the door and slide back the chain lock before pulling it open to peer at the delivery boy. 'Yes?' The flowers lowered to reveal the face of a kid who couldn't be more than sixteen or seventeen. His eyes roved over the exposed expanse of her now infamous cleavage as she sighed heavily and snapped fingers in his face. Immediately the kid snapped out of his gawking reverie and cleared his throat. ' Miss Van Lorne?' ' Da, that is me last time I checked.' Flushing deeply he thrust the exotic arrangement of flowers in her face and exclaimed,' These are for you Ma'am.' That infamous perking of the brow took place as she demurely took the flowers turning to walk back into her room, the delivery boy following closely behind.... a little too close. Setting the vase on the dresser Eva turned practically stepping on the teen. Rolling her eyes she snatches the pen and paper from his hand signing her name. Reaching into her bag lying nearby she withdrew a twenty and shoved it into his hand. ' Thank you, now if you will excuse me I have matters to attend to.' He nodded still looking kind of dazed by her raw beauty standing before him. Planting hands on hips she released an exasperated breath and grabbed him by the elbow steering him towards the door and slamming it shut. Kids these days. Leaning against the door she allows her head to fall against the frame. Finally the German diva moves away and stalks towards the flowers searching for a card. The flowers were absolutely gorgeous. White orchids and roses arranged perfectly in the midst of greenery. It was the one flora in the center that caught her eye. A vivid red rose stuck out almost like a sore thumb. As she shuffled through it all she finally found the little card attach to the crimson rose. Gingerly plucking it from its nest she removes the card from it envelope and flips it over to scan the message. ' The clock is ticking until we meet face to face. See you soon...P' A frown furrowed that high brow as she turned to look at the flowers again. What the hell? Surely she didn't have a deranged fan stalking her now. With a hefty breath she shrugged it off for now and tossed the card aside. Nimble fingers tug free the sash of her robe as she slips it from well-toned shoulder allowing the silk essence to pool at her feet. Free from the burden of clothing Eva saunters across the room and slips beneath the covers from the waist down. Leaning over she snatches up her ice pack and the remote. The television was flipped on followed by the VCR with a recording of Dangerous Games. Pressing play she settles back to watch the tape studiously while nursing her bruised elbow. She planned to watch every match several times in fact. The best way to get to her goal was to know her opponents. An hour would pass before the Dangerous Games match happened, that diva shuffling in her bed for better position as she took mental note of every man in the ring. When Jhub and her clashed those icy blues would narrow dangerously. Picking up the remote she slammed pause right on the moment he grabbed her nipples. Eva was still pissed about that. Idly her gaze would canter downward to peer at her bared breasts. The bruises there were ugly. Those usually tantalizing nipples were different shades of black and blue. With an eyelid twitching she looked back up at the screen and grinned sadistically. Jhub had just made his way onto her shit list. Coolly she pressed play again only to have to stop it as her phone rang. Dropping the remote in her lap she stretched out to grip the phone and dragged it to her ear. ' Da?' The voice on the other end was distorted and crackled. Eva frowned as she heard the words come through the receiver. ' Enjoy the flowers princess. Cause I am going to enjoy you....' Click. Muttering an oath in German she slammed the phone onto the receiver only to pick it up again and dial Erik's room. Cradling it between her ear and shoulder as she kept the cold compress to her elbow she began to mutter when it became evident Erik was not picking up. Pursing her lips with distaste at the lack of an answer she hung up and pressed play again as she settled back on her pillows. Where the hell was Erik?
