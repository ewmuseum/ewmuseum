Forgetting Tessa Windsor

16 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/16/08 START 5:32 pm WorldWide 90 promises to be quite the amazing setup for the forthcoming High Stakes Pay Per View live from Las Vegas. The intricacies of last names, real world locations, and catch phrase mingle together quite nicely for the utmost complex soliloquy. This is where our hero; Clyde Evan Fox comes in. With his wits and mastery, he is able to pull off one of the most confounding stories of this century. Enjoy this sricptual piece he calls “Forgetting Tessa Windsor.” The sun is dieing on a rather smug day over the city known as “Canada’s Vegas,” Windsor, Ontario, Canada. This is the place where the nightlife rules, drinks flow with ease, and so do the women. If you haven’t been, Windsor is somewhat of a Detroit wannabe? at least in the looks department. Underneath the grit surfaces a wild side; one only seen by only those who can afford it. Its convienent location allows for easy access. Whether your states side in Detroit or driving in through the most recognized highway in Cananda; Windsor is both a destination of love and hate. It stays with you. It picks a side and it stays with you. Sure it isn’t as flashy as THE Las Vegas, but it does its best. The bustling traffic streams by consistently onward passing by via The Ambassador Bridge. Off to the bridges right is The University of Windsor. A microcosm of the city in which it thrives. This is where our hero starts out. Clyde Fox emerges from the residence building entitled “MacDonald Hall” with a sour face pasted on. The utter stink of the city bitch slaps our hero across his face as if it wasn’t bad enough that he just relieved himself in the washroom. Fox: Osh Kosh-ammit! This place smells worse than what I just went through? BIG TIME! His newly bought clothes flap in the wind as he figets with his shirt. Fox: Hungry, hungry HIPPOS! I’m a hungry, hungry HIPPO! Oh wait. That’s a unicorn! Haha? unicorn’s are for fags! As Clyde blabbers to himself he proceeds to wander around campus in amazement. What kind of amazment is still unknown, mainly because his face keeps shifting in different ways. After change direction at least three times, Clyde scurries over to the building with signage that read “Administration.” Once there our hero shafts his way to the front desk. The sparkly tiles shine in the light as an older woman pops out from behind the desk. Registration Lady: Oh hi there. I dropped my pen. What can I do for? Clyde scratches his scalp as he configures his answer. He then sucks in all the air he can in one try. Fox: Okay, you see, I’m in Canada right, and I love Canada because I was born-ded here. But not here as in Windsor, here as in Ottawa. And I like Ottawa. I mean it’s okay I guess because it’s my home and it’s cool. Although everyone there likes hockey but it’s okay because I don’t mind hockey. Although I don’t really follow it. But people there are upset because the Senators are out of the playoffs but it’s okay because I’m here in Windsor and they don’t have a team so at least we did better than Windsor because Windsor doesn’t have a team. Did I say that already? Anyways, So I’m here wondering about the gambling age because gambling seems kind of new and fun and I have never done that before because I just never bothered too because I’m only a young guy because I’m only twenty years old or so I think I am, wait let me check. Yes I am twenty and I’m not sure where to gamble if I can but I’d like to if I could if that’s okay with you but you’ll have to show me where to go because I’ve never been here before but I must say that Windsor is pretty dirty and I don’t mean my ex-girlfriend Tessa Windsor; she’s a hottie and I miss her with all my heart and I want her back. But don’t let me bore you with that story. Okay I’ll tell you. So Tessa and I were dating way back and I remember this because they named this city after her because she’s just totally awesome. But that’s confusing that she owns this city because she’s not Canadian. In fact she’s not even the Canadian Champion. She’s the United States Champion, how weird is that? Am I boring you yet? Sorry no time for you to reply yet. So Tessa is the Champion of The United States and that’s even more impressive than being George W. Bush because he’s the president and you don’t see him having a Championship belt let alone one that is recognized by owning the United States. So I beckon the question; what has Tessa done for her city lately? I’ll tell you. She’s dropped it like its hot because she’s the United States Champ because she won it because she’s a good wrestler because she gives men chubbies when they wrestle her and then they fall flat on their faces because I get excited when I see her because she’s my hot ex-girlfriend because I miss her. She abandoned her city for the Title belt of a whole different country. I mean I would too and that’s kind of funny because I have a match against her on the next WorldWide for that very Title. But I want a Canadian Championship so I can be the Champion of Canada. You don’t see that happening do you? I mean all this time I’m living my life trying to forget about Tessa Windsor and then I’m thrown into the fire for the U.S. Title against her. I mean, if I won, it would mean that an American woman with a Canadian city’s last name would have lost the Championship of the United States to a man-child from Canada to become the ruler of a foreign country! NOW THAT’S CONFUSING EH? Don’t answer yet though because I’m simply not finished. Tessa thinks that she can fool everyone in Canada by leaving her city named Widnsor. She thinks everyone will love her because she named that city because she’s the Champion because she won it because she’s confusing everyone! I’m confused too! First I win the right to own Television from DeBow-and-arrow and then he makes me lose it to a sleepy doctor and then he wants a piece of me like I am Britney Spears on a rapid breakdown and then DeBow joins The Established-Mint and then I go crazy and challenge him to my card game at an event that is all about risks and then I want to go gamble because it will make me better in my match at High Stakes, but then I get Gizmondo texted by booking telling me that I have a U.S. Title match against my ex-lover Tessa Windsor!” Her city has gambling in it, its right across the way from America, it is in my country of Canada, and if I win then the whole world becomes even more confused about the hidden storylines. WHAT AM I TO DO? Yes I took my Skittles this morning. You may answer me now. Fox dashes for air as the Registration Lady sits in her chair literally stunned. Her mouth nearly hit the floor, her eyes almost dried up, and she was dang near close to falling asleep too. But what they both didn’t notice was the line of at least fifteen people that built up behind Clyde while his marathon speech was going on. Registration Lady: Ah-- Fox interrupts her. Fox: Oh and if I win the U.S. Title I promise to customize it in a crazy and whacky way. That is all. The Registration Lady slouches back in her chair, sighes and begins to reply. Registration Lady: Nineteen. Nineteen to gamble sir? Fox curtseys for the Lady as he rushes out of the building faster than you can say Moogles in my mouth. Once outside, Clyde yells and screams at the top of his lungs as if he was pulling off a Fox 40. Fox: BUT OF COURSE I’D RATHER CUSTOMIZE A CANADIAN CHAMPIONSHIP! The few people scattered by Fox plug their ears as they fear they might have formulated some ear damage from the yelling. Disregarding the health for others around him, Clyde struts off campus and flags down a nearby cab. The tires screech as the yellow cab halts in time for Fox to jump in. The Arab driver turns to the backseat where Fox resides and begins to speak. Hulhulim The Cab Driver: Where you like to go? Clyde snaps his seat belt in place and replies with a grin. Fox: The closest Casino pwease! Hulhulim flips his meters switch to begin counting the fare as they drive off. He then is quick to crank the radio on as some very strange music vibrates from the speakers. Fox: Hey mister? got any other music to listen too? Say? like? Britney Spears or Faith Hill? I can’t get enough of them! Hulhulim turns the volume down as he adjusts himself in the driver seat. The springs within the seat moan as Hulhulim readies himself to answer. Hulhulim The Cab Driver: You’re in Windsor my friend. We don’t take kindly to tha music of pretty little white girls. You must respect the driver of the cab, no? You listen to Ab-jey El Kashoff. He international superstar where I come from. His singles are number one hit in my country. He come to Windsor in the fall to meet my family and make a boom boom with tha wife YAS! Fox clenches his seat belt with all his might as he replies in fear. Fox: Oh no way! Tessa Windsor set me up! She’s got terrorists working for her! They’re coming to kill me with their unruly music and repetitive beats! LEMME OUT! Clyde slips his seat belt off and spanks the cab door open as it continues to drive down the street. He then tosses whatever change he had in his pockets at the driver before diving out of the vehicle. The cab picks up speed as it drives away and all that can be heard is Hulhulim screaming a bunch of mumbled language that is assumed to be curses and jihads. Clyde picks himself up as he pats his clothes down eliminating any accumulated dirt. Fox: Does Tessa want me back or what? Like Mya said in her one hit song? my love IS like whoa. But I have to be strong. She ain’t getting me back. No way. Nope. Not a chance. Zero. Nothing. Nadda. Ex-nay. Negative? Just then Clyde whips his Gizmondo out and takes a peek at some of the pictures he has saved on it. He hastily scrolls to a photo he secretly took of Tessa Windsor. Fox: Okay let’s face it? I’d give her a second chance. I’m not totally blind. After stashing his trusty obsolete machine away, Fox scans the street up and down. He then targets a nearby Casino entitled; “Windsor Slots.” Fox: See, I knew Tessa hired sluts to work here! Clyde pushes himself towards the Casino in hopes that he might see The United States Champion there. Upon entering the main lobby, a guard named Bubba acquints himself with Clyde. Bubba is a big guy. Probably around six foot five and weighing in at a steady four hundred and twenty pounds of pure beef. This guy doesn’t take shit from anyone? especially some measely little Fox. Bubba: You’re not getting by me sir. You aren’t nineteen? there’s no way. Fox steps back to take in the sight of the mammoth before him. Fox: Maybe you don’t know who I am mister. I’m Clyde Fox. I used to date the owner of this Casino. Tessa Windsor; The United States Champion. You know her? Bubba wipes the greasey sweat from his forehead as he replies. Bubba: Tessa Windsor. You mean from Global Championship Wrestling? Sorry son but she doesn’t own this place. She never did. What did you say your name was again? Fox: Clyde Evan Fox, DDS, PhD, ESQ. Bubba: Never heard of you. Get out NOW! Bubba latches onto Clyde and removes him from the Casino. Once outside of the building, Clyde moves as far away from Bubba as he can. Fox: You asked for it? RO-SHAM-BO! Clyde collides with Bubba full speed, nailing a perfectly executed Ro-Sham-Bo! Bubba falls to his knees as he grabs his destroyed junk. Clyde prances around the fallen guard begin mocking him. Fox: For a biggie fry, you let a cupcake beat you up? BIG TIME! I’m sly like a panda! Fox then proceeds to enter the Casino. The light glitter, the carpet vibrates, and the music pulsates throughout the entire building. The dark earth tones create an underground feeling as Clyde stands in the front hallway in amazement. Fox: Tickle my fun bags and call me a jack in the box! This place is even better than that weekend I spent at The Neverland Ranch! What should I do first? The sluts? The big spinny wheel? The dart board thingy? The machine thingys that have big pully arm thingys? The thingy with the thingy on its thingy? So many choices! In a daze, Clyde wanders into the Casino totally giving the impression away that he is a newbie. Taking advantage of this opportunity, Gus, the Casinos best Blackjack dealer hussles up to the young man and sparks a conversation. Gus: Hey there sport. How are you doing? Want to play some blackjack? It’s the easiest way to win big money fast! Gus is a middle aged guy with that always loveable commercialized smile plastered on his face. Don’t let his grey hair trick you; there’s a cold blooded B.J. dealer beneath it all. But the hair wasn’t distracting Fox. It was the red blazer with gold embroidery on it. Spellbound by the amazing suit, Clyde gets roped in. Fox: I’ll play anything you tell me to mister cutie. Gus: Great follow me this way. Gus leads Fox to a vacant table where he explains how the game works. Gus: Okay guy listen up. You put all your money on the table right? then I take your money, I turn it around three times, hand it back to you? remember it has become three times the money and then you give it back to me okay. Let’s play. Like a hypnotized drunk person, Clyde places around forty Canadian dollars (all in change) on the table. So that’s worth roughly two American dollars for those of you who care. Gus then takes the money and places it in his pocket. Gus: Thanks for playing kid? you almost won. Now see those doors over yonder? Gus points towards the exits. Fox: Yesh? Gus: Go through them. Your prize awaits you over there. Clyde leaves the table and steadily walks over to the exits. Once he leaves the Casino, the effects seem to wear off but still leave Clyde in a state of semi amniesia. Fox: What just happened? Where was I? His Gizmondo beeps. Fox quickly picks it up and opens the new text message. Gizmondo Text: Sorry hunny but there was not any stuff from any girlfriends of yours in the house to get rid of. Mainly because you’ve never had a girlfriend. Love, Momma Fox. Fox shuts his Gizmondo down and rubs his head in frustration? WAIT! Fox’s face lights up as he remembers something. Fox: Oh that’s right! I totally forgot that I had a relationship with a picture of Tessa Windsor from the GCW Magazine! Oh em gee? it all makes sense now. That’s why she never talked back when I said those dirty things. I even got a threesome with her a Very Juicy Val-gina! It wasn’t a conspiracy after all. It was just a bunch of random coincidences? ahhhhhhhh; it’s good to be me. And with that Fox walks off into the sunset leaving more questions than answers. But isn’t that what you should expect from the randomness that is Clyde Fox. If it makes sense for him, then we should just go along with it. You don’t want to get too involved with the inner workings of Clyde Fox. No one has ever returned from that hell hole. Fox: Boy, did I ever violate that picture though? Good on ya boy, good on ya! But the only relevant question remains? can Clyde Fox beat Tessa Windsor at WorldWide 90? And even more intruiging; can Clyde Fox overcome Tyson DeBough and The Establishment? Answers are on their way? stay tuned! END
