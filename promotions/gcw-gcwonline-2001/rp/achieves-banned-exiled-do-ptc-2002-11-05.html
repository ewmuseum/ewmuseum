Achieves : Banned & Exiled~! Do PTC

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 5th, 2002 <color=red>New York, New York. My Kami what a city. The people are so fun of life and joy. Everything is a wonder of nature. "You lousy bum! Where did you learn to drive!" Ok well we don't claim the taxi cab drivers as people. But there is one person we do claim. New York's own bad boy of wrestling. The current World champ, number one wrestler, and top porn star of the wrestling world. You know him, you love him, hell your sister no doubt had sex with him. Yeah, I'm talking about Jackson Rust. . . no wait I mean, Chris Storm. Chris Storm walks around back and forth. He's standing in a street side cafe. Varies people are sitting at their table eating. A women's cell phone goes off and she answers it. He looks at his watch. He seems to be waiting for something. Suddenly there is a ringing. Chris looks around and pulls out a giant, oversized cell phone. People look at him strangely. Chris starts to scream into it.</color> "HELLO? WHAT I CAN'T HEAR YOU. YOU'LL HAVE TO SPEAK LOUDER. YEAH I KNOW SOME PEOPLE ARE BEING RUDE AND TALKING ON THEIR PHONES WHILE I'M TRYING TO TALK TO YOU. CAN YOU HOLD ON FOR A SECOND?" <color=red>Chris turns to the women on the cell phone while holding his large, and clearly fake, cell phone back.</color> "Do you mind? I have a very important phone call and I can't hear a word he is saying because you are talking so loud on your phone!" <color=red>Turns back to his cell phone and starts screaming on it again.</color> "OK YOU WANT ME TO MEET YOU WHERE? OK I'LL BE THERE SOON. HEY ZERO DON'T WORRY. I'LL BRING MY VIDEOS. BY THE END OF THE DAY YOU'LL BE THE MASTER OF THE LADIES. TRUST ME!!!" <color=red>Chris walks off as the people just stare at him. He cuts a corner and throws away the fake phone. He laughs as the people back at the cafe talk about him. Chris looks around for more trouble to cause. He comes across a group of painters working to repaint an old school. Chris has a devilish smile as he grabs some of the spray paint and runs off without them noticing. Chris looks around for a place to cause some trouble. He finds what he's looking for when he comes across a large white wall. Clearly the painters had just finished on this way. Chris admired the lovely white paint on it. Still fresh. It looked so innocent. So ready for Chris to ruin. Chris looks around to make sure no one seems him. There is a cut in the footage. We come back and you see a giant tag on the wall. It simply reads:</color> Beware the Banned and Exiled <color=red>Chris stands back and admires his handy work. He notices a man coming and quickly runs off. As he's running his cell phone starts to ring. He ducks behind a building and unloads the paint and spray cans. He answers his phone.</color> "Hell-o? Yeah you need my help? What else is new? Look man don't worry about it. I got it covered. Look let me run over to my apartment, pick up a few things, and I'll met ya over there soon. You just think happy thoughts or something. Call me back when you get this voice message." <color=red>Chris puts away his cell phone. He looks down at his watch and starts walking off toward his apartment. He pulls out a piece of paper from his pocket and looks at it. It simply has two names on it. He puts the paper away.</color> "Jade and Lacuna? Never heard of them. I know Jade was in DWO at one point but not like I care. Hell she wasn't one of the bitches that torn me up. Oh well I just need to kick their ass. Not write a book about them. Although why Zero is wanting to hook up with Lacuna I don't know. Personally I don't see any reason but shit Zero always did have bad taste in women. No wonder his ass can't get laid." <color=red>Chris reaches into the pocket and pulls out a pack of cigerattes and a lighter. He sticks on in his mouth and lights it up. He takes it out and blows a thick cloud of smoke. He watches it as the air catches it and blows it around.</color> "Man I really need to give up this habit. Tessa wants me to quit, and my doctor tells me it isn't helping my career any. Oh well, I do whatever I want and when I want. I never listened to anyone before. Why start now when my career is at its height? But damnit Tessa hates it. . . " <color=red>Chris stops walking. He drops his cigerattes and puts it out. He leans against a wall between two building. Rain drips down from the roof tops. It had just finished raining. Chris looks at his watch. Deciding he has enough time to stand around and think, he lets his mind wander off. But his mind always wonders to the same place. The girl he loved. Yeah sure Chris was the big macho man that did what he wanted. Yeah he was a bad ass at heart. He said what he thought and caused trouble. But damnit he loved her. Well it didn't matter much because soon he needed to put his hands on another women. But not in a loving manner. No Chris was going into the ring to hurt. Man or women he didn't care. He had his Tessa Windsor. Chris' mind gets back on track. He looks at his watch again. "Oh shit!" <color=red>Looking at his watch he saw he would be late if he didn't hurry. Chris started off toward his apartment. He gets to his building and stops dead in his track. He spots a car he knows all too well.</color> "Shit she's here! What is she doing at my apartment now?" <color=red>Chris hurries up his stairs to his apartment room. He opens the door, finding it unlocked. He looks around to see rose petals on the ground. The lights are all out but he can slightly see inside his apartment because of the hallway light. He opens the door all the way. Inside his apartment, different candles are lit around it. Chris turns on the lights and looks around. Suddenly Tessa Windsor walks into the room. She's wearing a stunning black dress. She walks right in front of Chris. He can't believe his eyes. Time seems to have frozen in that moment. She leans downward, standing on her toes, and kisses him on the cheek. Chris just looks at her amazed. Is it any wonder why she so easily captured his heart? Time starts to move again as Tessa takes Chris by the hand and pulls him close to her.</color> "Josh is watching the kids tonight. What do you say we go out and have dinner and dancing? Just the two of us?" <color=red>Now Chris had already made plans to help his best friend out with a serious problem. He was suppose to meet him in a few hours. Now he would seem like a real heel if he stood up his best friend just because his girlfriend wanted to go dancing. I mean he made plans before. Who is she to change his plans? Chris looked at her. The smooth body was being hugged by that dress. His eyes where nearly watering. Hell at that moment if you asked him who Zero was he wouldn't even know. Chris instantly gives her a passionate kiss. They break just long enough for Chris to speak.</color> "I'll go get ready right now." <color=red>Chris dashed into his room to change. He opened his drawer and threw out a nice shirt and tie. Chris removes his shirt. He opens the closet. He looked around for a good pair of pants. He kicks a box. Chris looks down and slaps himself on his forehead when he sees what he kicked. His box of pornos he made. Along with different adult items he stared in such as magazines and condoms he was the spokesmen for. He looks around to make sure Tessa isn't around and pulls the box out. He opens it up and remembers he's suppose to bring them to Zero. But he can't let Tessa see him with it! He swore off the porn life when he started dating her. He can't let her know he still has all of his old stuff. Chris pushes it back into the closet and grabs a pair of pants. He starts to take off his shirt when he hears a phone ring. Tessa answers.</color> "Hello?. . . .What do you mean you need me to pick up the kids?. . . . .But Chris and I are about to go out! We had the whole night planned. . . . Ok I understand. Don't worry about it. I'll be there in a second. . . .Ok I'll talk to you later. Bye." <color=red>Tessa hung up the phone and let out a depressing sigh. Chris walked out to see what was the matter. Tessa appears to be rather upset. He gives her a soft hug to cheer her up but it doesn't seem to work much. Tessa leans her head onto his bare chest. Chris begins to stroke her hair to smooth her.</color> "What's the matter Tessa?" "Josh just called. He needs me to pick up the kids for him. He just got a case that is too big to pass up. So it looks like we won't be able to make dinner and dancing." "That's ok. My birthday is soon. We can do something special for that. I promise it will be just the two of us." "Okies. I guess that is better then nothing. Do you want to come with me? I'm sure you don't want to stay at home all alone." "Nah actually I might go stop by Zero's place. We need to talk about the PTC's Tag Team tournament. Form a plan on how to beat Blood Lust and stuff. I mean the match is this Saturday and we haven't talked once since agreeing to tag up." "Well ok. Just don't let those evil women hurt my Chris." <color=red>Chris lets out a soft laugh. He kisses Tessa on the cheek and whispers into her ear, "I won't." and kisses her neck. He slowly makes his way down to the collar bone before his own cell phone goes off. He lets out an angry sigh and stops kissing Tessa. She laughs and walks toward the door. She waves to him and blows him a kiss as she leaves. Chris checks his phone to see Zero sent him a text message. Chris just shakes his head and goes back into his room and grabs his box of porno goods. He puts them under his arm and blows out the candles before he leaves.</color> "Damn Zero. Bad enough he is asking me to teach him how to please a women because he can't get it up. Now he's keeping me from getting mine up." <color=red>Chris lets out his anger by punching the down elevator button. DING. The doors open and Chris walks in. The metal doors close behind him. The elevator slowly starts to move down. Chris looks at himself in the metal.</color> "Look at me. The World champ of the WIW:NG and how so I spend my time. Getting ready to help a friend learn to work the magic with the ladies. I should be out with Tessa enjoying a fine dinner and dancing with her as I hold her close to me. Man this is sad! Here I am quite possibly the best wrestler in the whole damn PTC and I'm fighting two women." <color=red>Chris punches the metal doors. The elevator shakes. Chris pulls back his hand to show a small dent. Not really anything impressive but then again, Chris pulled back on the punch. No sense in being a dumbass and breaking your hand before an important match is there? Controling his feelings is what made him the World champ. Knowing that your enemies are the ones in the opposite side of the ring from you. Knows that what is going on outside of the ring doesn't matter.</color> "Women! Not when high caliber women like my Tessa either. My Tessa can defeat World champs if she wants. But rather I face Jade, one whom while I was dancing around in DWO, she made no effort to even stand up for her league. And Lacuna. Who? Oh yeah that women going for the TV title. Well if that doesn't beat all. TV title and IC champ going against a World champ. No wonder I am the favorite going into this match. They will be lucky if I don't slam them into the mat so hard I make them both flat chested." <color=red>Chris stares forward. The metal on the elevator starts to vibrate. Suddenly Chris can see his match against Da D'MoN in the reflection. It's Chris' favorite type of match. Old School Cage match. Chris holds up his World title. Da D'MoN holds up the Hardcore title. The elevator jerks and the image disappears. The image comes back only this time it's of a bloody Chris with Da D'MoN standing over him. Chris' lip snarls up. His eyebrow cups down as the veins on his forehead bulge out. His temper rages on as he watches himself he humilated like that. Him the World champ losing to the lowly Hardcore champ. All because he assumed too much.</color> "Women, men, does it really matter in this business anymore? Have the guys gone soft now where we allow ourselves to be beaten by women? No, it's because we assume we can beat then and go easy on them. One can not defeat another if one does not try to. I don't care if they are women. I only see them as opponents. Challengers. My enemy. TV title, IC title, US, World, hell that doesn't matter. I allowed myself to be beat my the Hardcore champ. Who is to say they will be any different? Yes I am the favorite but I am beatable. I am beatable!" <color=red>Chris yells at his own reflection as if he was speaking to another person. As if he was just a random person standing in front of the real Chris Storm. He stares into the eyes of the reflection. Those soft blue eyes have became hard and cold. Chris' face is red with anger. He raises his figure to his reflection.</color> "Look at you! World champ and you are already letting yourself slip! At the gym, I'll be damned if you didn't screw up on countless moves. Things you should know how to do! You make me sick. Slipping from the game at your age. You are going to let two women walk all over you just because you assumed that because they were women you could beat them. Tessa can kick your ass and she's a women. Hell look at your chest! Callisto Bane fucked you and your perfect body up. She's a women! Jade and Lucuna. Just because they have a set of breast hanging off their chest doesn't mean you can roll over and get a hard on while wrestling them! You know how much of an insult it will be if you let yourself lose this match? Well I for one won't stand for it. Do you hear me! I won't let you screw up my career. Too many years of hard work and training went into it just to have you screw it all up because you let your dick do all the thinking! You let that snot nosed kid Erik Erratic beat you too huh! Well get your act together and beat the hell out of your opponents. Don't even think of them as women. They lost that when they stepped through those ring ropes. They are simply targets now." <color=red>Chris stares coldly at his reflection. It soon splits into two as the elevator doors open. Chris blinks. He soon notices he's on the bottom floor. He grabs his box and walks out. Chris steps out onto the pavement and looks at his watch. He still has enough time to make it to Zero's if he catches a cab. Chris stuffs the box under one arm and signals for the cab with the other. Several cabs pass him by without even looking. Chris just shakes his head. He reaches into his pocket with his free hand and pulls out his wallet. He pulls out a ten and holds it up. A taxi soon pulls up right next to him. Chris spits on the ground near the cab and gets in. He hands the driver the ten and tells him where to go. Chris puts his box next to him and looks out the window as the taxi starts moving.</color> "I take this one match at a time. Jade and Lucuna is all I see for now. Saturday comes and Saturday goes. We'll see which team truely was the best. But in the end it doesn't even matter. Because sooner or later everyone will be banned and exiled." <color=red>Chris smiles as the cab rolls on towards Zero's house. Chris still can't believe he's going to teach that guy how to please Lucuna. He doesn't need to be worrying about that. But hell maybe if he learns how to pin her down, maybe he can actually be useful in this match. Chris just laughs as he pictures a horny Zero chasing Lucuna around the ring. Jade and the referee wondering what is going on. Chris lets out a weak laugh. The rain slowly begins to pitter patter on the window. It rolls down the glass as Chris looks out to the world. The rain starts to fall faster and harder. The clouds far above head grow darker with each passing second. A storm is on the horizon and it looks to be trouble.</color> Beware the Banned and Exiled.
