Can anyone eat in private anymore?

8 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/08/11 “I would like to order some tea, thank you.” KJ asks a young waitress with long blonde hair, “I really don’t need anything to eat at the moment, but I would really like something to warm up my bones. I am used it to being a lot warmer then single digit.” The waitress nibbles the end of the pen with her front teeth as if she was trying to figure out a very complex equation Newfoundland just laid out in front of her. She asks, “We have coffee, I never actually had someone ask for tea. I don’t even think we have tea for you.” KJ sighs as he lifts the menu up to cover his face, “Can you please just go and check for me?” The waitress rolls her eyes as she walks toward the kitchen to check for any tea in this highway pit stop restaurant. Newfoundland stopped to stretch his legs before heading into the big city of Columbus, Ohio. Once he receives word of his first match for GCW was going be in Columbus, he drove all the way up from Texas with no breaks. He was getting stir crazy in his’ car because he was driving alone for long distances. KJ first match is going be a triple threat match with St. Clair and Davis. All three are having their debut at Worldwide 143 and it’s a great chance to show the higher ups what they got. When KJ wins the match, he is hoping that it would be a snowball effect and allow him to be thrust into a line for a title opportunity. He seen the list of the wrestler that are currently on the payroll and if he wants to see any good chances he needs to be patience. “You’re still drinking tea?” KJ lays the menu back down onto the table in his booth and sitting across from him is a person wearing a pitch black robe with a large hood shadowing most of the person face and only shows the person’s mouth. Newfoundland knows only a single group who would wear those robes and they are decuples of Shredder. “Why are you here? I told him that I was never going be working for anymore not after he tried to make me kill my mother.” KJ growls softly, “I am really surprise that he let one of you off the leash and come above ground to hunt.” The person’s mouth twists into a smile and says in a famine voice, “Who said anything about taking me off the leash? I broke off the cursing thing after I seen the master nearly caused all of underworld to come alive when he found out that you failed him and turn your back on him. I made a vow that I would find you and make a complete detail report on how the ‘perfect’ subject gotten off his’ chain. Then I will kill you and bring your body back with me to the master to reprogram you to be the elite monster that he wanted you to be.” KJ swallows couple times before asking another question because when the sisters of the barbwire make a threat they do it no matter how evil or messy its going be. “So, Sister what is your name because you know my name and probable my shoe size.” The sister leans forward with her elbows on the table and says trying to curve the conversation away from her name, “The only name that I know you by was The Detrimental Dragon and I am pretty sure you don’t give a complete stranger your demon name. What do you call yourself now?” “My name is KJ Newfoundland and that is my given name by birth. I was not born a demon and was turn into one by the lies of Shre--“ Before KJ could finish, he was thrust backward into the back of his seat and with all of his might he could not move a muscle. The woman yells, as she leans over the table with her right hand inches from KJ’s throat, “You will not call The Master by any other name besides Master! If you try use any of demonic power against me it will be the end of you and any hopes of being free. Actually, I dare you to use your powers to get free from my grip.” KJ erupts into laughter “What’s so funny, Newfoundland?” “You actually believe those stories that S—The Master was telling you little girl. I have no powers and never did.” Newfoundland says as he slowly moves his right hand up off table and grabs ahold of the woman by her wrist. His’ brown eyes changed into a crimson color as he slowly pulls himself forward, “I take powers from demons that are using their powers to cause harm to others.” She tries to pull her wrist out of KJ’s grip, but she felt considerable weaker with him holding her and couldn’t get free. KJ says in a commanding name, “What is your name devil! So, I know where the power is coming from. I want to tell the Master thank you for the new power.” “My name is Belle” A steaming tea cup slides under KJ’s arm and he finally broke the eye contact with Belle to avoid the tea spilling all over his lap. He begins to say as he looks back up from the tea, “Belle, you will-“ Belle was gone In KJ hand that was holding Belle’s wrist is a ring with barbs twist around the band of metal. The waitress asks, “Sir, do I know you from somewhere? By any chance were you on the television or are you a pro star?” Newfoundland says as he places the ring into his jean pocket, “I am pro wrestler trying break into the main stream of wrestling business.” The waitress sighs in relief, “Good, I was going to say that look like a wrestler. But, when I ask people normally they give me the nastiest look like I just insulted there mother. I know who you are! You’re Kodiak Gordert!” He was half listening to the waitress and was taking drink of tea when she said that he was Kodiak. This name caused him to spray all of the tea from his mouth. He picks up a napkin and begins to wipe his mouth, “I am not Kodiak. My name is KJ Newfoundland.” With that a man with medium length white hair and light blue colored eye slide across of KJ. He says with a cheerful tone, “That was the respond I was hoping for, Newfoundland.” He smiles to the waitress, “Thank you, for making KJ look like idiot for me. I really hope you don’t have the effect on everyone around you.” She tilts her head to the side giving him a funny look. Gordert clarifies, “Causing people spitting their foods onto the people they are having their meals together because that could cause you a great deal of trouble if they wrong people gets wet.” He waves her way with flick of his right wrist, “That’s all I need from you.” Waitress walks away as Gordert spoke to Newfoundland in private as someone can in public restaurant. “I see that you’re trying to kick start your career again after the accident with Sasha and that’s great for you. I wasn’t going to say anything, but you were getting little chubby.” KJ trying to control his angry from the playful jab for Kodiak, “What do you want?” “I want to help” KJ stares at Kodiak like he was speaking in bear “I know you that you need someone around because you need someone to watch you when sleep. You need someone to protect you from yourself.” Gordert says looking down at his hands, “I just want to make sure that Shredder doesn’t get control of your body again and cause a great deal of trouble for the group. Those demons that were summoned thanks to your inability to keep a single power for too long caused us to clean up some weird accidents.” KJ says in defeat, “My first match is against Davis and St. Clair.” Gordert smiles as he pulls out two brown folders from under the table and lays them in front of KJ. “Yeah, I already know that you facing two nobodies that nobody would care if they didn’t even so up for your match.” Kodiak taps the folder that reads Davis, “This guy is a backyard wrestler that is trying out for real wrestling company and all he cares about is championships.” “If you looking directly at a championship belt, you would not realize that there is a giant road block in your way to the top and his name is KJ Newfoundland.” KJ said, “I like the saying when deals with going for a title. It’s not the destination, but the road you travel that would make the journey worth it. I have been wrestling for the last six years off and on because of chances away from the wrestling scene was greater. After a while, you get that itch and you have to scratch it. That’s why I am here in GCW because the itch was too big for a smaller company to full fill my need for challenging foes.” Kodiak points at the other folder, “This is St. Clair, nothing too noteworthy to speak of.” “All l know about him is that he is a second generation wrestler and he plans on bring ‘the man’ down.” KJ says with shrug of his shoulders, “I wish I know more about him, but I have nothing.” Kodiak drinks out of bottle that says holy water and sticks out the bottle to Newfoundland. “You must be kidding me” KJ says “It’s Mountain Dew” Gordert says with a grin, “Or is it?”
