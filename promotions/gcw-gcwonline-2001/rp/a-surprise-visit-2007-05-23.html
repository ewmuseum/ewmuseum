A surprise visit!

23 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 23 2007 Kawanaha Kuno stormed through the office hallways of the High Spirit Casino. The skirt brushed the inside of her thighs. Her stride taking her down each hallway. She was most certainly on a mission.Her business suit looked as though it was painted on, left almost little to the imagination. Her black lacey bra could be seen peeking out through the opening of her shirt. Her high heels combined with her look gave her the air of superiority. In her hands, she carried a clipboard with a few papers that her bookkeepers had sent her. Each page detailed her bosses, financial transactions for the casinos, as well as his professional and personal assets. Her boss, Andrew/Shaman, had made several huge purchases and it wasn't sitting to well with her. "Morning Miss Kuno." One of the head accounts said as he passed by her in the hallway. "Morning yourself. Now where in the hell is he at? If you find him before I do, shove his no good ass my way." "Yes ma'am." He quickly heading into his office, closing the door behind him. One thing he knew for certain was that his boss was in for it and it wasn't going to be pretty. He sighed as he walked calmly over to his desk. He sat down but was quickly jolted out of his seat when he heard what sounded like a door being kicked in. Shaman sat behind his desk looking at his watch as Kawanaha came in angry. He smirked as he stopped his watch. He chuckled as he tossed her the folder containing the investments that were separate from the casinos. "You are quite the angry little fireball today aren't you Kawanaha? Doesn't seem like you to worry about my investments of a personal nature." Shaman stood and walked to the window and looked down at the floor as he heard Kawanaha breathing angrily. "Breath! It will feel better when you can talk and not yell." "I will not breathe until you tell me what in the hell possessed you to buy a military compound? I know this country is going to hell in a handbasket, but there is no need to be buying up old military property. And yes I do worry about the financial aspects. My bookkeepers DO not like financial surprises when there is no logical explanation for them. Now explain yourself!" Kawanaha slammed her clipboard down hard onto the desk and placed her hands on the folder that was marked "New Investment" she flipped through them looking them over for any chance he may have gotten taken in this deal. When working with the federal government, one never knows what kind of 'fat' they'd try to squeeze in to get what they actually want. Shaman folds his hands behind his back as he watches the floor with a slight grin upon his face. His eyes wonder over the crowd below. "Considering the government felt it owed me some back money I came to agreement with them. They sold me the property for less than 200 grand. My personal investment for some property I have wanted for sometime." As he watches her reviewing the material, he motions for security to go after someone and he then turns towards his desk. His eyes narrow at the envelope sitting there addressed GCW. "So instead of taking a fat financial settlement that is easier to write off when it comes time for the taxes you accept a "kickback" from the government? Now what exactly is going on Andrew. All I've ever asked from you is that you be honest with me." Kawanaha drops the folder and walks over to him. She turns his face to her and stares into his eyes. "You hired me to protect your interests. How can I protect them, when you are acting like there is no trust between us?" As Kawanaha begins to stroke Shaman's face, they're interrupted by security. Shaman looks over at the security and motions for them to leave. They scurry out of the office fast as if his stare could destroy civilizations. He turns and looks at Kawanaha. "Kawanaha, trust is hard and I have trusted you enough to handle the business ventures in Atlantic City. There is just some unfinished business and the reason I bought the property is to give a home to some of the people I have helped in the past." He looks down at Kawanaha showing little emotion, but not very much for it is not his style. "I know your heart is in the right place, but you need to make sure I know about these so that when I have my people balance the books.... they balance out and there are no questions." She stands on the tips of her stylish boots and kisses him on the cheek. "Now other then this 'new investment' what is good old Steven wanting of you this time?" “Well it is just a general match against Twizzy. Looks like he wants me to do what I do best and hurt the man." Shaman lifts the letter and smiles. "I don't like being led down a path I cannot control. I made it clear to Caldera I run my own course. Oblivion needs no controller, for we will do what we want and when we want." Shaman smiles looking at Kawanaha as he admires her business attire as it accentuates her looks very well. He chuckles and looks back out the window to the floor below. "They are like ants are they not? Scurrying to and fro and in the end it is just that... the end." "Then you need to tell Caldera that you won't be his puppy. He wants one, I'll buy him one. Or better yet, I'll have Kit train Tessa to be his bitch." Kawanaha smiled as Andrew winced at her attempt at humor. He continued to stare down at the crowd of people inside the casino. His eyes followed there every move, watching them run from one game to the next, throwing there money away like it's water. Kawanaha sensed a disturbance in him as of late. To her, at least, he'd been on an increased spending spree. His purchases weren't outrageous, but more along the lines of the questionable. First, a old fuel station in the middle of South-Eastern California on an old stretch of Route 66, following that up a small controlling interest in a television station, just wasn't making sense to her nor to her trained staff of bookkeepers. She quickly snapped out of her thoughts, and pulled him away from the window. The look in his eyes worried her. She'd only seen that look once before, and it's not a look that she wants to remember. Kawanaha quickly grabbed his office chair and placed him in it, him quickly pulling her down onto his lap, a silly grin on his face. She giggled with an almost girlish delight at his playfulness but this was work and not the time to play. She got up off of his lap, and walked around behind his chair. She placed her long, well manicured fingers on his shoulders and began to work out the tension and knots out of his back. She used her nails to dig into his muscular shoulders, attempting to remove whatever knots she could find. As she continues her work, she notices that he’s managed to relax more then he was before. “Don’t worry about what that ass Caldera wants. His job description allows him to the world’s biggest prick, and he’s going to send anyone after you that he feels can take you down. Besides, I have all the faith that you can take down Dizzy’I mean Twizzy this week. You haven’t disappointed me in that area yet.” Shaman closes his eyes as he begins to relax a bit. Taking a deep breath he looks over his shoulder at Kawanaha. “Plenty of paths lead me away from a given direction. I have been on this course for years and truth been told it’s destructive. Caldera is doing his job. He wants to keep the shows going and his federation on top. Look at the top notch talent making their way in now. They each have a mind blowing resume and yet they are nothing in my eyes. Each individual has to prove themselves and they have yet to prove a damn thing to me.” Shaman pats Kawanaha’s hand softly as he looks at the desk before him. Turning his head he looks at the computer and flips on the screen. He had just shut off the screen and not the computer itself. The screen before was one of the 411’s of the wrestling industry as he was watching the happenings. Kawanaha notices this and smiles. “Could it be that you’re researching some new material?” Shaman looks at Kawanaha and chuckles. “No looking at my opponent’s history. Seems Bradley and Twizzy both have some history people aren’t aware of. Everyone has skeletons in their closets, Including Caldera our esteemed president of GCW.” Shaman swivels in his chair looking over at Kawanaha. His eyes gauge her thoughtfully as he grins. “Dinner this evening sounds wonderful. I’ll pick you up at seven.” Kawanaha puts her hands on her hips and looks at him furrowing her brow. “What makes you think you can tell me I am available for dinner?” Shaman laughs heartily. “That is easy Kawanaha, You cannot resist my irresistible charm.” Shaman laughs as he leans back against the chair as he kicks his feet up. Kawanaha walks by his foot dragging a single finger slowly and seductively across the desk. She stops and knocks his feet off the desk and smiles sweetly. “I expect a four star restaurant and at least flowers.” Shaman looks at her and shakes his head. “Well I would not expect anything less from you.” Standing up he grabs the chair and pushes it under the desk. He looks at Kawanaha and holds his hand out to her. “Are you ready to administer the inspection with me” I have been putting this off for a few days. There seems to be some issues with the valets and bellhops. I personally watched the tapes and think there are a couple of boys using the cars for personal usage.” Shaman opens the door for Kawanaha as she steps through. “So what did you have in mind for them?” Shaman looks at Kawanaha and smiles. He then presses the elevator door button. “You remember the match I had against Steihl?” Shaman looks at Kawanaha and then she raises an eyebrow. “Well I think the boys need to wrestle each other in a no holds barred anything goes extreme match. Truth is both boys are in the Indy circuit in Las Vegas. I am interested in what they can do. Besides the Police will want to make sure the boys pay restitution and what better way to put on a charity wrestling event.” Kawanaha shakes her head at Shaman as they both step on the elevator together. The door shuts as Shaman’s facial features return to the very serious nature it has always been.
