The United States of Wolack

20 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20 Feb 2006 Friday-February 17th.... Scene fades up on a trophy engraving shop where the owner is showing a few examples of the engravings that he does. Owner: Now this one is one of my favorites. You can see the old english script..... Wolack: Can you do that and have the lettering in red, white and blue. Owner: Sure thing. Wolack: Cool. This is the size of plate that I need it on. Wolack hands the owner a name plate that is engraved with Eva Van Lorne's name. The owner takes a look at it. Owner: This is going to be a custom job alright. It's going to cost a little more. Wolack: Don't worry, makes a good tax writeoff. Owner: Need these holes in the plate as well. Wolack: Yeah. Won't attach to the belt without them. Owner: OK. I'll have it done for you in about two days. Normally it would be quicker, but with the coloring in the letters, it's going to have to back for a day or so. Wolack: That's fine. Just make it look good. Owner: Don't worry, I will. Wolack turns away from the owner and walks out of the shop. Wolack: I win the GCW United States Title, and now I have to pay for the custom name plate. Of all the cheap a.... Wolack stops talking as he sees a young child walking by. The child stops and has an excited look on his face. Child: Dad, it's Prime. The child's father comes up, Father: I'm sorry, but my son thinks that you're a wrestler that he saw at a Diamond Wrestling Syndicate show a couple years ago. Wolack: Well, actually. Father: Oh, I'm sorry. You look different from then. Clean shaven and with the short hair. Wolack: Don't worry about it. How about I give this young man an autograph. Wolack pulls out an old DWS ticket from his wallet and signs it, handing it to the kid. He signs it Prime instead of Sam Wolack. Child: Thanks. Father: I was wondering what happened to you. Wolack: I went into a short retirement for a while. I'm now wrestling with an outfit called Global Championship Wrestling. Father: What channel is it on, my son wouldn't mind seeing it. Wolack: It's on XTV, Thursday night at 8pm. But don't let him watch this week. Father: Why not? Wolack: Because I don't think that a child should have to watch a grown man fight with a woman, that's all. Father: Well, I did teach him that wrestling was fake. Wolack: Maybe the wrestling that comes out of Connecticut or Florida, but trust me, those punches that I throw, they're real. Father: Wow, I didn't know that. Wolack: It's those groups that give us a bad name. The only thing 'fake' about GCW is the names of a few of the guys. Father: Thanks. The father and son walk away. Wolack: Parents. Wolack walks up to his car, unlocks it and gets in. He then drives off. We fade to his home, where Andrea is sitting on the couch. The door opens and Wolack enters the room, coming up behind Andrea and kisses her on the forehead. Andrea: Sam, nice haircut. Wolack: I thought that it was time for a change. Andrea: Much like the new tattoo. Wolack: It's just a reminder. Andrea: Samuel Francis Wolack, you know that the tattoo is more than that. I know how important she was for you. I know how much you still hurt because of her. And I also know that I will always be second in your heart to her. I've accepted that. You should too. Wolack: Well, I don't want you to feel that way. I want you to feel that I love you with all my heart. Andrea: You may think that you do, Sam. But I know that you can't. But I'm glad that you love me with all that you can. Wolack: How are you feeling? Andrea: Still hurts. Wolack: I know it does. When you’re ready, we’ll try again. Andrea: I don’t know if I can go through that again. Wolack: Andrea, you’re strong. You survived that ordeal brought about by the O’Conner family last year. You can get through this. Andrea: Thanks. Saturday-February 18th?. We are back at Wolack’s home. Andrea is seen sleeping on the bed through an open door. Wolack comes out of the bathroom wearing a pair of pajama bottoms and a GCW NC-17 T-Shirt. He turns on some music. Andrea: Sam, can you turn that down? I’m trying to sleep. Wolack turns the volume down on the stereo. He then sits on the couch. His phone rings. Wolack answers it. Wolack: Hello?. Yes, I know that my flight for Japan leaves Sunday night. Do you think I don’t know this?.... Yeah, well tell GCW Management that I’ll be at the airport after I pick up a few things?. For one, the nameplate for my championship belt that I’m paying for?. That’s good. I’ll expect to see it on my next paycheck from you guys then?. OK, well, let me enjoy the last couple days I have here. Bye. Wolack hangs up the phone and leans back in his couch. The phone rings again. Wolack decides not to answer this time. He lets it ring until it gets to the answering machine. Wolack’s Machine: Hey, this is Sam and Andrea. We aren’t able to get to the phone right now, but if you leave a someone will get back with you as soon as possible?”.BEEP. Voice over the machine: Mr. Wolack, this is Saul Hudson from XTV, we were supposed to meet a half hour ago to do that interview for XTV’s “Inside GCW” series. Wolack: Oh s@#$! Wolack quickly grabs the phone and answers. Wolack: Mr Hudson, sorry about that. I completely forgot about the interview. If you want to, come on over in about an hour and a half and we can do the interview from my home?. Well, I’ll be ready. Wolack hangs up the phone. He heads to the bedroom and wakes Andrea up. Wolack: Honey, we’re going to have company in about an hour. Andrea: On a Saturday? Wolack: Yeah, I forgot that I was going to be giving an interview, so they’re coming here to conduct it. Andrea: Do it in the basement. Wolack: We will. We head to an hour later. Saul Hudson is there with a camera crew. Wolack has changed into a button down shirt and jeans. Saul: Now, we’re going to go over a few things in this interview. From your beginnings in pro wrestling to your being the new United States Champion. Then we want you to do a quick GCW WorldWide on XTV promo. Wolack: Got it. Cameraman: Saul, we’re ready. Saul and Wolack sit down in their chairs, across from each other. A spotlight is turned on illuminating the basement. On the bar behind them rests the GCW United States Championship Belt. Saul: I’m Saul Hudson, and I’m here with the new Global Championship Wrestling United States Heavyweight Champion, Sam Wolack. Thanks for allowing us to come into your home today. Wolack: Not a problem, Saul. Saul: First, we want to get into why you became a pro wrestler. Wolack: Well, ever since I saw my older brother, Jay Draven, wrestle, I knew that’s what I wanted to do. I passed up an opportunity to play defensive back at Duke University to become a wrestler. Saul: Who got you started in wrestling? Wolack: My initial training was at the Extreme Wrestling Center in San Jose, California. At the time, it was called Extreme Jay’s Wrestling School. Jason Callion, Sr. was my initial trainer. I trained there for about 12 months before starting to train under my brother and his brother-in-law, Michael Durant. Saul: Now your first run in the United States wasn’t very successful. Wolack: No. My first couple matches were losses in MSW. A lot of that had to do with my inexperience in performing some of the maneuvers that would become staples of mine. I decided to head to Japan to get some more training. I trained for about three months there before I started to wrestle again. Saul: What was it like for you in Japan? Wolack: Different. I was always accustomed to the fan reactions here in the US. Going to Japan, and hitting some of the moves that I was capable of hitting, the fans had little to no reaction. It wasn’t until a month after getting there that I had a fan following, and that the fans were more stoic. I also met Keiko, who would later be my wife, during that run. Saul: You came back to the United States near the end of MSW’s run. Wolack: Yeah. Specifically because I had the chance to team up with my brother. Our only match as a team in MSW was a win. Then we found that even though he was a rather sizable name in MSW, that we would learn the hard way in LSW and PCW what the politics in the NWC were about. Saul: After PCW, you went back to Japan, where you would win a Junior Heavyweight Title and was set to participate in Japan’s annual Best of the Super Juniors Tournament?.. Wolack: And the day after my first defense of the title I was in a bad car accident. Saul: Can you tell me what that was like? Wolack: Being in a coma for a week due to the severe concussion that I suffered. That wasn’t fun. Then I had to rehab from knee and elbow reconstruction surgery. I never thought that I was going to be able to make any sort of comeback from that. I signed on to do a couple of one shot deals in HIW where I portrayed Exodus Executioner with my brother. But that was it for a while after the accident. Saul: You finally returned to the ring almost 2 years after the accident. Wolack: NWC Central. Yeah. It was after the reconstruction of the National Wrestling Council, where they went from being a network of affiliated organizations to being an organization that controlled four different brands. That was fun. Especially the feud that I had with Teron Hain. Saul: Your last hurrah was in NWC Pacific. What was that like? Wolack: A nightmare. I brought in my “Extreme” gimmick from NWCC to NWCP and it tanked. Then it became DWS. I didn’t do much there before it closed. Saul: Let’s talk about when you first came to GCW, as X. Wolack: Let’s not. Saul: OK. Well, what do you think about your being the US Champion, and your upcoming challenger, Vivica Valentine. Wolack: I’m glad to finally win that belt. I had a chance once before, against Borealis, but I came up short. This time, when I took on Eva Van Lorne, I didn’t make the mistakes from the previous title shot, and I made sure to finish the match. As far as Vivica Valentine goes, she’s got to work hard to get this belt from me. It’s my first title in the US, and I don’t want to be the first person to have won and lost a title in 2006. Valentine got my attention a few weeks ago when her and the group that she rolls with took out Almasy and Stevens. Not cool in my book. Last week, I had the great opportunity to fire the first shot back at them when I nearly shattered Shadow’s jaw during that match against Ehren Moore. It also gave me the opportunity to screw Moore out of a victory as well. So thanks for that. Now I know that I have a target on my back. Valentine, Shadow, MacFarlane and Ambrosi are probably going to try something funny. If they do, I’m going to have to be ready. But in the immortal words that have been spoken many times in the past, “What doesn’t kill me, makes me stronger.” So if they do try something, they had better be ready to kill me. Because if they don’t, it’s their ass. Saul: Thank you very much, Sam. Good luck on Thursday. Wolack: Thank you. Saul: Ready to do that promo for the network real quick. Wolack: Yeah. Wolack turns and looks right at the camera held by the camera man. Wolack: This is GCW United States Champion, Sam Wolack. Tune into GCW WorldWide, every Thursday Night at 8pm, only on XTV. Saul and Wolack stand up and shake hands. Saul: Thanks for your time, Sam. Wolack: Don’t mention it. Sunday-February 19th We’re back at the trophy engravers where Wolack is looking right at the new nameplate. Wolack: Good work. I like how you made the stripes in there. Owner: You have the belt with you, I can attach it for you. Wolack: Actually, it’s in my bag in the car. I’ll attach it once I get to my hotel. Owner: I made these for you as well, you might like to use them. The owner hands Wolack two screw caps with a white star and red stone in the center. Wolack: Thanks. They look good. Owner: Try to get GCW to use this place for all of the nameplates. Wolack: Not a problem.
