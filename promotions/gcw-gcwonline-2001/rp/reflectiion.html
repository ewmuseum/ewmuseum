Date: Timeless He sits staring at the blank walls of his new home. Months and months ago his palatial palace of despair was brought crumbling down upon him and his opponent. Since than suitcases, hotel keys, and limos were his life. But now his new castle has been completed, and it sits in a new location. He has since left his childhood stomping and bleeding grounds of Belmont, England. He left that place because it’s done, it’s his past, it’s what he once was. It was his home of abuse, rape, torture, and anguish. Bryan Mayhem O’Neil has left the land of his forefathers and has new dwellings. A little town in Ireland called Ozark Hills. It’s a small town, most people living there can stem their ancestry back to the Rebellion. 130 people live here. Now 131, and that one is a huge sadistic monster. His new place of residence is nowhere near the size of Belmont. A small two floor 2 bedroom shack compared to the castle of his past. These walls he’s staring at are plain white. No cracks, no signs of wear and tear. Most of all no signs of an abusive father. Rich beyond his wildest imagination this new house only cost $725,419 American Dollars. He has no family, pets, or many belongings for that matter to live in a house featured on MTV’s cribs. Creeping into his midlife years Mayhem sits on a chair icing down his wounds. The beatings he has taken over the past year have taken a toll on this monster. Years ago he felt no pain. Now he feels every shot, and its after effects. In a year he went through his fourth and final Belmont Match against the most dominant force in wrestling history. He was nearly drowned in the first ever DeadPool match. Than later that day went through almost every wrestler in GCW at Dangerous Games. This year he went through another Buried Alive Match against one of the largest men he’s ever faced. And just the other night, he was involved in one of the most intense threeway matches ever. Mayhem’s getting old, and he knows it. He’s been eluded by one major thing in his rather successful wrestling career. He never had a world title run. Was it out of fear? Only one champion ever put their title on the line against Mayhem. And that was a man like Mayhem that knew no fear. Lestatt Knight. Not even his uber-successful brother, the Legendary Dark Messiah Jason O? Neil put his world title on the line. Owners and Presidents alike never gave Mayhem his due. A champion of his ferociousness and anger are bad for the business. A Champion should be that, a champion, a leader. Not a blood craving monster with the thrill for a kill. Looking at that wall we talked about earlier, Mayhem begins to reflect. Are they right? Can a sociopath really be bad for the company? Most would tend to disagree. His Belmont Match was a part of the highest rated Pay Per View ever. Statistics claim that of those households with cable, 2 out of every 5 houses in the world witnessed WarPath. Rubbing the first phases of the scab on his forehead, than running his fingers through his jet black hair, Mayhem feels the pain on his scalp. Health Magazines Mayhem may end up like Muhammed Ali. Broken and half retarted. Mayhem feels lapses of memory loss, and blindspots. The pain pills help tremendously, but most fear he may develop an addiction to them. What ever happened to the man that felt no pain? The man that never needed medication to walk up his stairs? He shakes his head in wonderment. Mayhem stands up, looks in the mirror, feels his joints ache, his bones pop, and for the first time in his life, he wishes this was all over?.
