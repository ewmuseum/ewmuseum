The Adventures of Johnny Heroic: The Big Bad Wolf

16 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16 His eyes burn red. A wolf's eyes indeed. Two twain pools of rage and pain that shoot out from behind a past that he can't get away from. Veins pump acidic red bile that feeds his emotions, a venom that burns him from the inside out. Repressed memories, a scarred childhood, general contempt for all that is...and I really couldn't care less about why the big bad Wolf wants a piece of me. 'I said drop every last weapon that you have.' He wants to command authority. He wants to bring me to my knees with his voice alone. But he knows as well as I do that there is no chance in Hell I'm gonna comply. And that's what the Wolf wants. He wants it because he's got anger and frustration to take out on somebody, and I happen to be the guy that he thinks he can do that to. Because his mom held him too tight or didn't hold him tight enough. I don't know, and I don't care. 'Better idea. Why don't you go run along and play a great game of 'Hide and Go Fuck Yourself?'' And Wolf almost smiles. After all, this is reason to celebrate. He's about to kill Johnny Heroic, isn't he? And since I have a pretty good idea that the Conclave of Justice, who I think I'd like to rename Justice's Agreeable Conclave Killing Off Future Friends (J.A.C.K.O.F.Fs), sent this assassin at me, I'm more than willing to bet that the White Knight or Carbomb won't exactly be running to my rescue. That is, Wolf's rescue. 'Good. I was hoping you'd put up a fight. You're so much colder than the Johnny Heroic I used to know.' 'And you're a lot more reckless than the Mysterious Mister Mystery that I used to know.' That name sends him into a rage, his smiling dropping immediately. 'Nobody...calls me that... You don't know anything about me!' Not quite. I know that he moves faster than I expected. Apparently, he traded his Mysterious Powers of Mystery for the ability to have bursts of speed and strength. And super rage. And super dickheadery. He's on top of me as I draw my two fun guns. The small 9mm's that I keep up my coat sleeves. Rattle off four or five shots before I even realize that he's kicked my hands out and I'm putting holes in the walls of this alley. What'd the alley ever do? I was just walking home through it when I was attacked. No more, no less. And now these bricks, laid down by the sweat and work of men ages ago, were getting shredded by bullets that they can't dodge. The work of men long past is yet another casualty of this fight, of any fight that has collateral damage. Killing the things that they spent their time and effort building. How is that not murder in some degree? Anyway, yeah he's landed a couple punches and knocked the guns out of my hands and has started pummeling away with his burst power. It doesn't last long, however, as I can notice him starting to slow down and get a bit winded, and after only taking a few hits that didn't put me in, I'm able to grapple, trip, and move away from him before he can get up. 'Nice moves, Heroic. I can see why The Conclave didn't come after you themselves.' 'The Conclave didn't come after me themselves because The Conclave is a bunch of pussies that got bored with World of Warcraft and wanted to see what their jokes for super powers could actually do to the real world. The Conclave is a group of people that sit around and want to do something about the everyday crime in this city, but they're too concerned about things going on outside of the The City or their own image of themselves to go to the extremes needed to pull it off.' Wolf doesn't waste time. 'Wolf Overdrive!!!' I've always hated battlecries. This one engulfs him in a red firey aura, like some cheap Japanese cartoon, as he lunges at me yet again. Two knives go flying at him and one hits him in the leg, but it doesn't deter his onslaught. Knee to the chest, elbow to the head, kick to the guts...then they all kind of just blend together in a pain euphoria that sends me to a sub-catatonic state that I don't really mind being in. In all honesty, this really isn't the worst ass-kicking I've recieved. His kicks are fast and his punches are pretty hard and there's quite a few I can't manage to block. But for the most part, Wolf Overdrive seems to send the kid on a spree of destruction that causes more damage to the building, the trash cans, and the windows around us than me. Wasted energy. Wasted potential. The story of his life. I block his punches much more easily as the Overdrive wears down and he becomes more and more exhausted until he backs off and I'm left standing with what will simply be some sores tomorrow. 'You almost done with your little tantrum, kid?' His teeth clench yet again and Wolf springs at me, no powers this time, and tackles me down. He's trying to gain some sort of advantage, possibly to choke or possibly just to show that he's better than me, while he goes into his sililoquey. 'It's not a tantrum! I'm not a kid! I am the end of what you call your reign, your life! And if I have to kill you over the course of the night or over the course of five minutes, it will get done. Because I am Wolf and I have powers and you're not a hero and you have no powers! Because I have DRIVE!' The red flame comes up again. This time, all his strength goes downward, toward sending me into the pavement further. Nothing a good kick in the balls won't fix. He's reeling and howling like the wolf he is while I roll from under him and spring up, automatic pistol in my hand and that seems to half the overdrive for a moment while I get my two bits in. 'You aren't a hero. Stop trying to be one. You're an assassin. Nothing more than a lowly criminal. And just because a bunch of people that call themselves heroes hired you doesn't make you anything special. Doesn't make you any more than what you are: shit.' He turns the heat on again and runs at me. The automatic bullets don't do anything but miss as he's dodging too fast. A shoulder check into a controlled tackle sends the two of us flying. With the force that he nails me with, we pretty much are literally flying, in fact, toward the building across the street. His arms wrapped around my back take the brunt of the impact on the brick wall as he loses his burst of speed and power yet again and he needs to back off, noticably fatigued. Johnny Heroic, tall and mighty, stands up and shuffles injured a few feet away. 'You're being used. You're being used like you always have been. By everybody-' He cuts me off yet again with a tackle to the ground and begins shouting again. 'Shut up! I'll avenge my past! I'll do it through you, and through whoever else I have to, Heroic! I'll get out from under the shadow of my father, my brothers, my cousins...my whole family of superheroes! I have the strength! I have the speed! I have the power and the rage and the passion and the want... I HAVE THE DRIVE!!!' He jumps at me one more time. But he doesn't realize how I've positioned myself. In his blind rage, in his blind drive, he doesn't even realize that the building he slammed me into was a gas station. I sidestep and his fist goes into the pump and out the other side, probably shredding his hand to pieces, and he finds that he can't pull it back out due to the jagged metal that he just put it through. Screaming in anger and tearing at his own skin, Wolf indeed is nothing more than an animal, caught in a bear trap. The hunter outsmarted, he is now the hunted. Completely at my mercy as gas leaks all over him. 'Pathetic. To think...with the proper guidance...you could have been a great weapon. But nothing more than that.' His yelling is unintelligible as I walk away. A good hundred yards and the gasoline is still trickling near my feet. It's tempting me. It wants me to do it. It won't leave me alone until it's done. I take a look back and see Wolf trying to chew his own arm off, trying so hard to get back at me. Knowing that it's the only way he'll get his revenge on whatever fucked up his past, his life. And it's with pity that I pull out a cigarette and light it. And it hits me. I still have my powers. Not in the way that I once did. But, I always knew that my powers were unlimited luck. I would ask the universe to do something, and it would. Time would speed up, I would move faster, I would heal, I would be stronger...I would get whatever I wanted by asking the electromagnetic currents of anything to do whatever I wanted. And now what? I have no powers. I have non strength. The universe does not do as I command. And yet...I'm taking down superheroes and supervillains left and right. I feel pain, but I might as well be indestructable, because I keep walking away alive. ...I still have my unlimited luck. Even more augmented because that is the only strength I have. Knowing this, I almost feel so much pity and, knowing I can't die, I don't feel threatened to the point where I want to let Wolf live. But...couldn't part of my luck, couldn't part of the universe's design, be to have Wolf in this predicament specifically so that I never have to deal with him again? I guess I'll never know. As the cigarette leaves my hand and drops toward its fate and it catches the river of gasoline on fire and it travels back to the station. I'm clear of the blast. Hell, I'm lucky enough to be clear of any blast. As for Wolf? 'Euthanized.'
