Date: 10/12/07 START He gets up, dusts himself off. Clyde, the sly one, had entered the front doors of "Darion's Gym." "Cheese whiz Squirt Squirt, dis place smells ucky!" Fox complained. A tall and slim African American man, with blonde hair ran up to Clyde to introduce himeself. "Hi there snexy, how are you doing?" The man introduced. "Um I'm good, what's wrong with your voice? And why does your hair look like a sponge?" Fox reacted. "Oh silly, this is just what I sound like. So, what are you here for?" The man asked. "Ummm, I'm just here because I wanna make sure that I'm still in wrestling form!" Fox echoed. "Okay, well you came to the right place sweetie," The man slurred, "My name is Darion and this is my scrumptious gym! We'll whip you into snexy shape in no time at 'toll!" As Clyde took a moment to inhale the smell of musty, sweaty old men, he thought to himself, is this place for real? "Um, okay, I'm reeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeady!" Fox glowed. Darion then snatched Fox's hand and began to sensually walk down the hall and into a big mirrored glass, wooden floored room. "This is where dreams come true for all of our clients," Darion fantasized, "Here, you too shall be challenged in preparation for your wrestling appearance." And just as Darion finished, three other, much bigger men in stature came waltzing in with workout balls. "These are going to be our tools of action!" Darion expressed. Darion took a ball from one of the gentlemen and sent him off with a fluttering kiss. Clyde's face scrunched together with sickness as he could not believe what he just witnessed. But knowing Clyde, he just repressed it and tried to focus on the task at hand.... which was hard enough for him in the first place. "Ok so here, take my ball," Darion instructed. Clyde walked up to Darion and took his ball only to hear a sexual snicker from the man once the deed was done. Darion then instructed Clyde to lay down and place his legs on top of the ball and to start doing some simple sit ups. "Am I correctly positioned?" Fox asked. "Hmmmmm, lemme see, ummmmmm, you could spread your legs a little more hun," Darion endeered. "Okie dokie! Oh Marion, I workout better with music on.... BIG TIME!!!!" Fox tustled. "Okay hun, I'll put my favourite tune on," Darion said. And with a whisper to the two servants, they scurried off and dimmed the lights as Darion prepared a CD. Suddenly.... a song. If listening to Phil Collins doesn't say you're gay, then who knows what does. "I can feel it coming" starting to flow throughout the room. "Here, let me help you with your workout," Darion gazed. And with that, Darion held the ankles of Clyde down as he started to do a few sit ups. Slowly but surely, Darion's soft hands slid up the legs of Clyde. Fox was heavily concentrating on sit ups when out of no where Darion slid his hands right up the openings of Fox's shorts! "HOKEY BUGLES BATMAN, MY BALLS ARE UNDER SEIGE!" Fox screams as he squiggles away from Darion. "Oh shucks, I didn't even get to violently rub you like I usually do to new clients!" Darion said with disapproval. "Double you tee eff mango? What kind of operation is this?" Fox asked. "Well hun, it's a gay gym and we obviously thought you were interested!" Darion replied. "THINK UNSEXY THOUGHTS!!!!! .... EWWWWWWWWWW! YOU LIKE MEN, THAT'S SICK. I'M ONLY A BOY AND YOU TRIED TO RAPE ME!" Fox exclaimed with conviction. "Well sorry hun but you gave me all the signs that it was okay," darion defended. "Your gym is da scariest gym in da world and I hate you! You make Mr. Tinkle puffy! You should burn in heck Mister Marion!" Fox cried. Clyde proceeded to pick up Squirtle and dash for the exit while tears were running down his eyes. He though to himself, 'Is dis a precursor of failure in GCW? If I can't even workout then how will I be ready? I don't think I'll ever live up to my brothers name! I've got to hurry and get to the arena for Dangerous Games if I even want to have a shot at competing!' Run Fox run. Run for the hills, because no matter how hard you try, it'll always be a big sticky mess. No pun intended. FINISH "Oh my gosh, what did I do?" Darion questioned.
