SCANDAL PART II: THE DEFIANT ONE

10 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/10/2008 PREVIOUSLY? "?my employers have a business proposition fer ya. A one time deal. Very lucrative. Low risk." The offer was a simple one: McKail throws his next match at an independent booking and he gets a very healthy payday. Problem is, McKail can’t be bought like most. The man issuing the offer on behalf of his “business associates”, Howson, didn’t understand that and didn’t much care. The man just assumed McKail was an easy target, mainly because the father McKail didn’t even know could be bought and sold for a packet of cigarettes. Seems Howson knows this first hand, as the two of them were friends back in the day; former professional wrestlers who grew up in the industry together and Howson had carelessly assumed such a bond would effortlessly ingratiate him into McKail’s trust. It didn’t. Threats were made and McKail ignored all of them. Now comes the match Howson expects McKail to lose, but McKail has no intention of losing to anybody? AWFA WRESTLEVANIA SUPERCARD SHOW MAIN EVENT JACOB MCKAIL VS BUTCH MASSACRE McKail had perched himself atop of the turnbuckle before Butch Massacre had even hit the mat. The fans were already cheering. Most of them already knew what was coming and just to sweeten the pot, they didn’t care for this Butch Massacre idiot one bit. They cheered even harder once they realised he wasn’t getting up any time soon. McKail stood upright and cast his eyes over where he already knew Howson to be. The elderly ex-professional wrestler sat on the front row of the audience to McKail’s left, flanked by two muscle-bound goons who wore expensive business suits that appeared to be two sizes too small for them. The three of them stuck out like a sore thumb, even more so as they glared at him with angry faces and threatening facial gestures. In return, McKail winked and grinned at them. It was an open show of defiance that McKail knew Howson and the likes wouldn’t let slide, but he didn’t much care. That was the point of it. Despite being told, it seemed Howson and his thugs weren’t too quick on the uptake and the way McKail saw it, he had to clearly illustrate the point to make one hundred percent sure they knew he couldn’t be bought. Howson shook his head and angrily rose to his feet, promptly edging past the other fans in his row, heading towards the aisle. His “friends” followed him, barging people out of the way with ferocity, even knocking a few of them over. McKail chuckled to himself, before diving from the top ropes. He hit Massacre hard, but took the pain in his stride as he’d done a thousand times before. Not long after that, the referee’s hand hit the mat for a third and final time and the bell rang. He was victorious. The crowd erupted as if there were any real doubt about the outcome, but that was the point wasn’t it? AWFA WRESTLEVANIA SUPERCARD SHOW POST-SHOW LOCKER ROOM McKail wiped the sweat from his eyes with a small hand towel, then tied his hair back and out of his eyes, all the time idly considering which bar to hit first and what alcoholic beverage would get him good and numb the quickest. In fact, he was so preoccupied with the decision he didn’t even hear them enter his locker room. "What tha hell wus that, boy?" McKail shook his head. How the hell did these fuckwits get back here? Where the fuck is the security at? Despite the reactionary internal questions, McKail found himself entirely unsurprised to find Howson standing there, flanked by his two goons and maintaining that same extremely pissed off look. The security at these independent shows weren’t worth shit and a few buck greasing the palms of these minimum wage security guards would get them pretty much anywhere. Despite access issues, McKail tried to conceal a grin at how pissed off they looked, but didn’t try too hard. "You just fucked me out there, ya know that?" Howson screamed. "My employers aren’t gonna like this!" McKail shook his head and chuckled to himself. "Like I give a fuck?" One of the muscle bound idiots took a step forward and developed his best menacing expression. McKail found himself mildly impressed, figured it worked on most folk. But he wasn’t most folk and he’d made bigger men than he cry within five minutes flat in the past without exerting too much effort. Howson extended his arm across his protector’s chest effectively stopping him in his tracks. Must be well trained and well paid, McKail reasoned. Why the hell else would they accept orders from this ancient fuckwit? Despite being cut-off, the bodyguard jabbed an angry finger at McKail. "You just got real lucky, punk!" McKail rolled his eyes. "Sure I did." Howson’s anger evaporated into a smile, then a brief chuckle. "Ya know Jacob, yer shouldn’t push these men too hard. Ya ain’t dealin? with yer usual wrestling pansies now. These two’ll rip yer face right off if I give “em tha say so and they’ll enjoy it too." McKail extracted a cigarette and his lighter from the pocket of his jacket resting upon the bench, put it in his mouth and lit it. "Whatever you say old man." He took a drag of the cigarette and made sure to blow the smoke directly into Howson’s face. "As for the other thing, I told you I wasn’t going to do it and I didn’t. Point made." Howson shook his head. "Ya ain’t really telling me ya were na’ve enough to think you had a choice in this, are ya?" "I did choose you fuckwit," McKail told him with a sigh. This was getting real old real fast. "I chose not to do it." Howson cocked his head to one side and grinned. "I meant it wasn’t a choice ya were gonna like." McKail exhaled some more smoke and shrugged. "I don’t know, I liked it well enough." "Tell me ya feel tha same way in three minutes time." With that Howson took a step backwards and his thugs stalk forward, flanking McKail at either side. Well, this don’t look good, McKail sighed. These idiots meant to do him harm and judging by their size and numbers, there was a good chance they’d succeed too. There was nothing he could do about that now, or at least nothing he was willing to do about that now. Fuck it, he decided. Strike first and strike hard. Without another thought, he flicked his still lit cigarette into the face of the hired muscle to his right, whilst immediately diving on the muscle to his left, clambering onto his back and wrapping his arms around the meathead’s throat and head, forming a rudimentary sleeper hold. "Motherfucker!" McKail heard the first thug yelp, desperately trying to bat away the fire and ash from his eyes, whilst the moron currently enveloped the sleeper hold could only gaggle and cough. Over the top of it all, he thought he heard Howson sigh at the developing proceedings. However, it was long before things took a turn back their way. It was almost inevitable, but McKail wasn’t gonna make it easy. The muscle dealing with the cigarette had stopped panicking and from what McKail could tell was now free and clear of the troublesome cancer stick, at least well to stalk closer, carelessly throwing punches in McKail’s general direction. Trouble was, his friend was struggling so hard against McKail’s make-shift sleeper hold, McKail didn’t stay in the same place for too long and at one point one of the wild punches clipped his fellow goon for hire in the side of the head, forcing the unfortunate fellow to groan a little, amidst the choking and the coughing. Ultimately, the struggle got so vibrant, McKail managed to use to the momentum to swing his feet at the other muscle-bound buffoon with force as he attempted to attack McKail from the rear. The blow caught him nicely on the side of the head, propelling him backwards across the locker room floor. Problem was, the manoeuvre loosened McKail’s grip some and acting relatively quickly for his size, the goon flipped him over his shoulders, sending him down to the cold, hard concrete floor with an audible thud. McKail let out a groan, but shook the pain away and attempted to get to his feet as quickly as possible looking to prevent them from gaining the upper hand. He was met by an Earth-shattering blow to the jaw, which sent him back down hard. The two sizeable goons pulled themselves together and set upon McKail like a pack of wolves tearing at the carcass of their prey, plying him with punches and kicks all over. Howson grinned at the sight of the carnage, but soon lost his taste for it. It wasn’t the brutality of the spectacle that got to him at all, far from it, it was more down to the lack of agonising screams and the begging that set him back. It didn’t help matters when the sound of laughter rose high above the curses of the goons and the constant impact of bone against bone and intensified. Howson shook his head. "Yer sick bastard?" The goons backed away from the laughing bloodied pulp of Jacob McKail, glanced at each other disgusted and then back at Howson questioningly. Howson shook his head at the sight before him and edged closer toward the door. "Why did yer make us do this, boy?" Howson questioned. "Nobody wanted this?" If there was a hint of remorse, or perhaps even the last inkling of loyalty towards his long dead friend, McKail’s father, embedded within his voice Jacob McKail didn’t hear it, or more likely just plain ignored it. Instead, he continued to laugh amidst coughing up blood and glared at them all with a distinct glimmer of defiance within his eyes. "Had enough?" McKail spluttered, coughing. "Or do you want some more?" One of the goons allowed a little chuckle at this, which was quickly reprimanded by a stern glance from Howson. He maintained it for a few seconds longer than he needed to, then cast his eyes back over to McKail. "We get tha idea, kid," he told him. "But just so ya know, there’s more than one way to end ya and now, yer leavin? us no choice." McKail ignored him and continued laughing, prompting Howson to curse under his breath as he exited the locker room, his two bodyguards following him in suit. After they left, McKail managed to fumble in his jacket pocket again for his cigarettes and lighter and made a choice. Vodka would be his drink of choice to numb the pain tonight; seemed like the more reasonable option to him. To Be Continued?
