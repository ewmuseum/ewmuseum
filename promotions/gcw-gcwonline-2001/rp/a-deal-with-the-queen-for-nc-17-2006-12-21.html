A Deal with the Queen (For NC-17)

21 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 21 2006 (Side note Keiko's rp was also for NC-17) Everything changes, or so they say. The seasons, space and time, and even your entire life can change within a single moment. Depending on the situations, these changes can sometimes be for the best. A prime example would be my life as a wrestler. I've gone from a world class jobber in a third rate league to being within mere moments a champion. This has only happened to me once before, in that dreaded league, I dare not mention here. I was, well, it was stolen from me because of politics within the fed. I cam assure you that history will not repeat itself! But now, now it's time for a change. A change for the better. Keiko and I have busted our asses to get where we are today. In that time, the two of us have gone through our own personal changes. Unfortunately, these changes have been for the worse. I can't speak fully for my Sensei, though. I do know that events that have plagued her liked have caught up to her. Now she is very apologetic, and quite frankly not the same aggressive Keiko that I know. I on the other hand, well…it's rather hard to explain. There's a certain number of events, changes if you will, that has happened to me. The downside of all this is, I have little knowledge of what I've done. Yet, a part of me likes this side of me. A whole new persona, if you will. There's just one question that remains. I've been told this new side of me is evil. A blood thirsty killer that I know nothing about. Under this power, I have killed and tasted the blood of my victims. I'm ashamed, yet satisfied of what I've done. In the end, UNITE, Josh and Gray, you two have to ask; which version of me are you going to get? The evolved wrestler, or the ruthless murderer, or are you going to get both? Reality wise, I can't answer that. One minute, I'm happy go lucky, where as in the next I can be a rather vindictive bitch! I guess in the end, there is only one way to find out. Hopefully, you won't get the full blown killer. After all I wouldn't want anything bad to happen to you…yet Time: December 21, 2006 (Late Evening) Place: Oahu, Hawaii “Scrub my fucking toilets,” Hope hissed to her lover Christopher. She was badly imitating her Mistress as she went on, “Organize my clothing by size, color, and if it makes me slutty or not!” “I doubt she said the slutty comment,” the blue eyed man laughed, “Besides, I was the one cleaning her sex toys, which by the way, makes me wonder what some of those are used for.” Hope smiled, “Well now, honey, if a woman revealed all her secrets, there would be nothing left for your imagination.” “With you my dear, my imagination has been in overdrive.” The blonde beauty smiled at Christopher and headed towards the bathroom. “Now, you settle yourself down,” she cooed, “I'm going to take a shower and wash off my servant's duty for the day. Then, you and I can spend the night in each others arms.” “That's not all that's going to happen is it?” “Depends on how lucky you are.” Chris shakes his head laughing as Hope disappears into the bathroom. Once in she removes the white robe she was wearing and turns the shower to her desired temperature. The blonde turns towards the mirror and glances at her naked self. She couldn't help but smile at what she saw. “He had a Hawaiian Goddess; who know what else,” she said aloud, “Yet, he fell in love with me. There are just times I ask myself, why.” “It's because you're a blonde,” her reflection responded, “It's a guy thing.” Hope shook her head after the reflection answered her. She rubbed her eyes a couple of times wondering if she really did see her own reflection speak on its own. “Okay, I watch way too many horror movies,” she sighed. “And not give yourself any credit,” the reflection laughed. Hope gasped jumping back into the door. Christopher heard the commotion and yelled out, “Are you okay?” “Tell him you're fine,” the mirror Hope snapped demonically. “He'll know better,” Hope whispered. “Hope, you okay?” “Make something up dammit!” Hope gulped and turned her head back towards the door. “It's okay,” she tried to say calmly, “I—I just saw a spider. I'm sorry I scared you.” “It's okay,” Christopher said softly, “I was just checking on you.” Hope waited for a few seconds then looked back in the mirror at her reflection. Unlike Hope, her reflection was now dressed in a white strapless dress. On top of her head was a silver tiara. However it was when her reflection smiled, that Hope noticed the profound difference. A set of fans replaced her pearly whites. “Leandra,” the blonde whispered. “Very good,” the reflection answers, “You're not as dumb as you let on are you? Then again, you have had me asleep all these years.” Leandra walks back and forth within the mirror as Hope stares on confused. “Lock the door,” Leandra ordered, “I don't want anymore interruptions. Just in case, that ‘man' decides to check up on you again. Without any hesitation, Hope did as she was asked. A small part of her wondered why she was doing this, yet the rest of her knew that, Christopher would not believe she was being controlled by her reflection. “What do you want,” Hope tried to ask boldly. “Look at you,” Leandra laughed, “Trying to be the big bad. Even in your natural vampiric state, you aren't all that powerful. At least not as powerful as me.” Hope, for the first time, was impressed by what Leandra was saying. “You mean I really am a vampire?” “Yes, in the sense of the others of your kind you've ran into with the likes of Sekhet and Shadow, that is.” “Then what the hell are you!” “A past life. A life where our kind reigned supreme. Throughout the years, instead of dying, I've transferred myself from body to body. This was until, I felt confident with my hosts' body, which would house my essence. This was so I could be awakened to bring in the new age of vampires! “You can't be serious,” Hope laughed, “There's too many forces out there that will stop you. The McClancy's, Shadow, even Sekhet would stop you.” Leandra just laughed coldly. The laugh sent shivers down Hope's spine. “You honestly think those people will stop me,” the vampire laughs, “First off you need to know your complete facts. Blaise McClancy is the only threat to me. Mr. Vampire Hunter, that is, along with others in your precious Knightmare Brigade. Yet, right now, he's preoccupied with the arrival of that redhead bitch's offspring.” “Don't insult Patti,” Hope yelled, “She's twice the person you'll ever amount to!” “You think she actually cares about you? She kicked your ass, because you were trying to become like her. You were nothing more than a threat to her.” “She taught me a lot from our encounter. I just never got to tell her. She taught me to be myself, and live my life and mine alone.” “Good advice. Considering that's how I lived my life. Now back on track. Blaise is out. Sekhet has gone into hiding since the Four Winds closed. No doubt, she's shacked up in her tomb with Scarab. AS for Shadow, she's impressive I'll give her that. After all, Lia did only see you the one time, and half assed helped you.” “Lia?” “Shadow's true name. You know, like Hope is yours.” The blonde acted as if she understood. Yet there was still some confusion on her part. “I cast a reflection,” she started to say. Leandra scoffs, “God, if you believe everything that is written, you are just a simpleton. Hope, there's many things about our kind that those authors were able to see. In Bram Storker's place, it's mostly piss and vinegar. Shame though, with my influence he could've been a master.” “Until you were done using him, then you would murder him in cold blood!” Leandra smiled at the defiance in Hope, “A vampire has to eat in order to survive. You of all people should know that. Humans do the same thing.” “Don't compare me to you.” Hope pat, “I didn't kill Conrad, you did!” “Yes, I did because he threatened Ariel, and us. Don't tell me you aren't capable of doing the same thing.” “I'm no murderer!” “Oh yes you are,” Leandra whispered, “Remember, I am every part of you. I remember before you and Christopher was lovers, you were bitter enemies. You killed his parents, who were your adoptive parents and all the farm hands that lived there. From there you burned down the entire farm so there was no trace of what you did! Not too bad using Ariel for the fire and having Keiko and Faith helping you along with all of this.” Hope lowered her had in shame. Leandra laughs at the blow. “I don't want to hear anymore,” Hope dried. “Oh, but there was one more time. Just this, past year, if I'm not mistaken, you killed two more times within the same night,” Leandra squealed. “When you found out who your real father was, you were beside yourself. The great Richard Reeves wasn't it? That man would've been a great asset to my new army. Sadly he's gone now, all because of you. But before you could get to him you took out one of his workers. You snapped her neck like you seemed to have many times. Not only that you enjoyed it, didn't you?” Hope's right hand goes over her heart remembering what she did during her trip to Chicago to meet her dad. A story that she only had told one soul and that was Keiko. She only had it logged in her memories, afraid to even write it down in her own diary. “Yes I did enjoy it,” Hope snarled, “She shattered my friend's skull in. Marina was just doing her job, and I could've saved her!” “That's right you wanted to save her, but your father shot her as he was shooting at you the first time. You watched her die in your arms. At the time though, I wasn't fully awakened, I could've saved her for you.” “You would have turned her into a vampire!” “But she would've lived Hope! You could've saved her if I was raised in you, or if you would've tapped into your true self! You let her die, and everyday it haunts you, the scar where your father shot you is that constant reminder!” Hope touches her left shoulder where her battle scar was from that horrific encounter in Chicago. “It wasn't enough that he wounded you by killing Marina and shooting you in the arm,” Leandra continued calmly, “It was just enough to piss you off. Luckily, Christopher, Keiko, and Kawanaha were flying around on Ariel and the beast sensed you were in distress. I'll admit I've done some destruction in my days, but with the fire and you actually bathing in your dead father's blood. That was magical, almost romantic. It was in that moment, I felt you were indeed the one to help me fulfill my destiny.” Hope slumped to the floor, burying her head in her knees. “How do I know it wasn't you the whole time?” “Because, I just told you I was recently awakened. Quite frankly, I knew you could help me, but I didn't think you were fully ready, but alas the task is done. However, that's not important to you yet. What matters is you also have the urge to kill when needed, so you don't lay your fucking problems on me!” Hope jumped to her feet and leaned in on the sink staring at Leandra. “I banish you back to hell,” she shouted. “Who the hell do you think you are, David Acorah? It doesn't work like that. Even Kyo there, couldn't do that that easily.” “But Kyo can stop you,” Hope smiled, “That is what you are afraid of, is him stopping you.” “Well, I see you are learning. Kyo, yes can get me out of you. So can others, then you could become your true vampire self. Yet he knows, I won't go quietly and his fears are my triumphs.” “So where does that leave me,” Hope whispers knowing she is doomed. “The answers you seek, even about yourself lies within me. You have to trust me.” “How can I trust you? You're a liar and a murderer…” “And I'm all you got!” “Then I choose to fight you!” “A grave mistake,” Leandra whispered coldly. To Hope's amazement, her body was lifted from the floor, than flung into the mirror. The glass shattered while she fell to the floor. Christopher heard the ruckus and ran to the door. Not wasting anytime by knocking he grabs the knob. He cursed to himself when he found it locked. “Hope, open the damn door,” he screamed. Hope was in no condition to answer as her body was violently rolling over the broken glass. “What's the matter Hope,” Leandra taunted, “I thought you were going to fight me.” “Stop this, Leandra,” Hope cried, “I beg you.” “Hmm, been a while since someone's begged me for something.” With a sudden rush the now bloodied Hope was hurled to her feet. She was then tossed towards the door at an incredible speed. The blonde busted through the door collapsing with it on top of Christopher. “Keep fighting me, Hope, and I'll make your body in no shape for NC-17 and Keiko's in a handicap match!” “Okay, you win,” Hope gasped. From below Christopher tosses the door aside with Hope on it. He gets to his feet slowly, as he's horrified to see his lover in a bloody mess. Not to mention, Hope appeared to be having an argument with herself. “Do you trust me then, to show you what you want to know?” “Yes!” “Good then! Now promise me, after NC-17 you will come to Germany. That is where all your answers lie.” “I-I promise.” “Hope, what are you doing,” Christopher said startled. Hope/Leandra turned and faced him. The blonde smiled revealing the fang-like-grin proving that Hope wasn't Hope anymore. “Hmm, well seems my plans have accelerated,” Leandra said licking her lips. “Leave him alone!” “What a better way to test your devotion to this vow. He would make a worthy sacrifice.” “Leave him alone! I'll do anything you want. I'll pledge my soul to you, just leave him al-“ Hope didn't finish as Christopher had grabbed the lamp on the nightstand. Swinging it with great force he smacked the lamp across the face of Hope/Leandra. Hope sailed backwards from the blow hitting the wall with a sickening thud. Hope then hits the floor and is out like a light. Realizing what he's done; Christopher drops the lamp and rushes to Hope. “Hope,” he screamed smacking her face, “Hope! Dammit, I'm sorry! I thought...just come back to me.” Tears streamed down his checks as he spoke. It was then, the saw an amazing task. Hope's body was healing itself. The cuts and the blood were disappearing quickly and her chest was rising and falling at a steady pace. Caught up in the moment, he leaned in and kissed her. “Remember,” a voice whispered into Hope's head, “you break this promise and Christopher is a dead man!” Hope nodded her head to the voice and Christopher raised his head to look into the eyes of his soulmate. “Hope,” he asked. “It's me,” she whispered softly. The two started at each other for a few seconds not knowing what to do or say. “I'm sorry I hit you,” the man fumbled with his words. “You did it to save me. Next week, I wish you could do the same.” “What do you mean?” “I've made a deal with her, and in order to save your life, I must honor it.” “Hope…” “Don't talk me out of this Christopher! I've got her subdued for now. She can awaken at any moment. I must prepare though, for this next encounter with her. I need to talk to Shadow and Kyo!” “Can you contact them?” “Shadow yes, but she's preparing for her match as well, so I don't know if I'll be able to see her until NC-17. As for Kyo I can't just contact him, because I don't know how, so I'll have to see him at NC-17 as well, and I'll have to keep Keiko busy while I hunt them down. But for your safety, keep me as calm as possible. Believe me, right now it's your only way to survive.” Christopher nodded, but apart of him wondered, would he ever have Hope normal again.
