Sky Fox

14 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/14/07 START On a neutral day in the town’s local hospital, Clyde Fox lay deep in a slumber after recovering from his brutal Devil’s Night match. A Nurse enters room E 10, a single bed room with a nice row of open windows to allow for lots of sunlight. “Okay, patient number X-M 7407, let’s see how you are doing today,” The Nurse reported. The Nurse checked the clipboard at the foot of Clyde’s bed. “Hmmm, everything seems to be going good,” The Nurse rejoiced, “All I have to do is check your medication level and see how those stitches are doing.” The stale Fox slowly but surely awakens. “Holy butterballs and smurfs! Where the heck am I?” Fox questioned as he looked around in shock. “Oh hey there Clyde, you’re up now,” The Nurse informed, “You’re in the hospital just recovering from a few stitches that’s all.” “Why do I have to be here if it’s just a few stitches?” Fox asked. “Only because each time you’d wake up, you had no idea who you were Clyde,” The Nurse informed. “Yeah but that’s how I always am when I wake up in the morning. This one time I walked into my Mom’s room and I was naked--? Fox said. “That’s nice,” The Nurse cut off. “Well let’s see” my name is Clyde and I can count to five. One, Two, Four, Three, Five!” Fox exclaimed. “Very good Clyde, I just think that it is One, Two, Three, Four, Five,” The Nurse corrected. “Ahh what you do you know?” Fox debated. The Nurse then proceeds to check the back of Clyde’s skull and notes that his stitches are healing just fine. “What’s your name anyways ma’am?” Fox invited. “Nurse Joy,” Joy answered, “Okay, well your stitches look to be healing just fine. Is there anything I can get for you?” Nurse Joy asked. “Yes peeeeeeze,” Fox said. “Name it,” Nurse Joy comforted. “You don’t happen to have any Pez do you” You know? the candy?” Fox questioned. “No I’m afraid not Clyde. Okay so you just relax and I’m going to go and finish the paperwork and we’ll have you out of here in no time,” Joy said. Just as Joy turned, a tiny box that was in her lab coat caught Clyde’s eye. “Pez” Is it? Grab it quick Clyde!” Clyde snatches without Joy noticing. She then walks over to the door and exits the room. “OHHHH YEAH!” Fox glimmers. Clyde then pours the whole box of white colored pills into his hand and proceeds to gulp them all back. “Morefeene” Mooooore-phene? Hmmm. A new kind of Pez!” Fox sat excitedly. --5 Minutes Later-- Nurse Joy enters the room, only to see Clyde speed walking back and forth near his bed. “What are you doing?” Joy asked, “Get back into bed NOW!” “But those special Morph Pez I grabbed from your lab coat are making me see funny colors! KETCHUP FALVORED SEVEN’S! OH MY GOSH, I HAVE SUCH A CRAVING FOR KETCHUP FLAVORED SEVEN’S,” Fox shouted. “What” Seven’s? As in the number?” Joy pondered. Nurse Joy then noticed an empty box of morphine pills on the ground. “You didn’t take all of these did you?” Joy asked frantically. “Yepppp! I’m a sky high Fox now! YAY” BIG TIME!” Fox glowed. “Stay right here, I’m getting a doctor!” Joy said as she rushed out of the room. Of course, Clyde didn’t listen. He sped walked out of his room and down the hall until he came across a phone at a desk. More specifically, a secretary’s desk. Clyde sat down and dialed an unknown number. “I’m calling Squirt Squirt!” Fox muttered to himself. Ring? ring? rin-- “SQUIRT SQUIRT HEY! MESOHYPERCRAZYHIGHONVERYGOODNEWKINDOFPEZYOUSHOULDTRYBUTPROBABLYSHOULDNTBECAUSEITMAKESMEFEELNAUGHTYALLOVERANDIWANTTOHAVEYOURBABIESENTERSECRETMESSAGEHEREIAMDSOTOTALLYWASTED.” Fox plastered. Apparently Squirtle talked back. “WHAT” I HAVE A MATCH AT THE UPCOMING WORLDWIDE? AGAINST WHO?” Fox questioned. Again, Squirtle talked. “VIVICA JAY VALGINA! OKAY, I KNOW I’M SLEEPING NOW. THERE’S NO WAY I HAVE A MATCH AGAINST SUCH A GREAT WRESTLER! ARE YOU SURE?” Fox asked. A moment goes by. “OKAY THEN, I’M LEAVING THE HOSPITAL RIGHT NOW, I’LL SEE YOU IN A BIT OR FOUR,” Fox hangs up, “LET’S GO LIBERATE SOME WOMEN” LIBERATE THEIR ASS KICKING? BIG TIME!” The high and mighty Fox dashes out of the building doors only to have his sleek hospital garment fly off in the wind. FINISH
