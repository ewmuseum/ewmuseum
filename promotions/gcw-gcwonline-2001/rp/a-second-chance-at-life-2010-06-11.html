A second chance at life.

11 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/11/10 [The sun beamed down from the sky, passing through the clouds, causing my eyes to twitch in and out of focus as they opened. I looked around in a daze as I tried to understand where I was exactly. As my hands rub my eyes I notice in the blur a couple of police officers walking towards me.] Police Officer: Hey there you okay? You look like you're a little lost. Donny Diamond: Lola... Where's Lola...? Police Officer: Lola? We don't know any Lola, but come on let us help you and we can find this Lola person. [The officers grab me by the arms and pull me up to my feet, and I quickly push them back. Again they come forward to try and calm me down, but I quickly hit one of them in the face knocking them down onto the ground.] Donny Diamond: I SAID WHERE'S LOLA!?!? [I scream out in anger as I look around still not sure as to what is going on. Over to the down officer I walk and I grab him by the collar of his shirt, shaking him for answers. Suddenly I fall to the ground as the other officer has hit me with a taser, and the electricity flows through my body. The officers are quick to lock me in handcuffs and carry me into the back of their car, not far from where I was.] [As they drive off I sit back looking out of the window trying to understand everything that is happening at the moment. We pass a sign on the highway where I see the words “Los Angelas, California” written on it, which means I am some how back home. Finally we arrive at the Police Station and the car slowly comes to a stop and the officers get out. After opening the door they escort me into the station for the booking process to begin.] Police Officer: Take a seat sir till we're ready to book you. Donny Diamond: Not until you answer my damn question. Police Officer: I said take a seat sir! [Out of anger I push myself into the police officer knocking him down on the floor. I kick a few chairs over trying to free myself from the restraints until another officer grabs me from behind and chokes me down to my knees until I pass out.] [Almost as if I was living in deja vu my eyes open once again in blurry fashion and I notice I'm strapped down to a bed in a padded room. I scream at the top of my lungs for freedom, but the only reply I receive is silence. Laying in the bed I stare at the ceiling wondering how many minutes, hours, or perhaps even days I have been here. Soon the door to the room opens and a doctor walks into the room, you can tell because of the white coat he's wearing.] Doctor: Hello Mr. Diamond, I'm Doctor Landry. Donny Diamond: Just let me go... I need to find her! Doctor Landry: Find who exactly? Donny Diamond: Lola... I need to find Lola! Doctor Landry: Can you tell me a little about just who this Lola is? Donny Diamond: She was there after the explosion. After I set the soul of my daughter free, she was there. I saw her escape, I just have to find her, that's why you have to let me go! Doctor Landry: Explosion? Now that sounds like some serious business, but I must say I don't remember hearing anything about any explosion. Donny Diamond: That's because they covered it up! You don't understand what these people are capable of, but I know everything. I know their plans for recreating the world in their image, changing everything to how they want it done. Just like when they teleported me over to where the Pope is to assassinate him. That's why you need to let me go, I need to be there to save my daughter from them! [As I continue to tell my story of what has happened to me, I notice he simply nods along as he reads over the information in the folder he is holding.] Donny Diamond: Comeeee on you have got to let me go! Doctor Landry: Calm down, let us figure everything out first and we can go from there okay? I'm looking over your chart and from the x-rays we took earlier, it seems you suffered some serious head trauma. It says here you're a professional wrestler? Donny Diamond: Yes. When I gave my soul to Satan he promised me absolute power as long as I could deliver him souls. And there was no better place for me to this than as a wrestler, but I don't have any head trauma. I'm invincible inside of a wrestling ring and nothing can stop me, I am unstoppable! Doctor Landry: Oh I most certainly understand, but let's just calm down and we'll make sure it all gets sorted out for you. [While talking to me he pulls a syringe from his pocket. He examines me for a moment and notices the scared slashes on my wrists as he injects the medicine into me. Soon my eyes start to become heavy and I slowly doze off...] A few weeks pass... [I'm sitting on the edge of my bed looking towards the barred window as rays of sunshine come in. The door to my room opens as Doctor Landry walks in, greeting me with a smile. Pulling a small chair towards me, he takes a seat and opens my medical folder once again.] Donny Diamond: Hey Doc, bringing me some good news today? Doctor Landry: In fact I am indeed doing just that. We got the results in of your latest tests and it seems you are recovering fine. No more delusional manifestations and in fact it seems you can go home today. Donny Diamond: And work? You know I can't just live off the streets. Doctor Landry: Well for one I don't think you will find a doctor to tell you it's okay to go back wrestling after all the head trauma you suffered. But I also know that as soon as you walk out of that door you will be back training with or without my recommendations. Just be sure you take care of yourself and make sure you don't suffer anymore damage upstairs and you should be fine. Donny Diamond: Hey... I know it was rough with me for a few weeks, but I do thank you for putting up with it all and getting me fixed up. But when I told you I was unstoppable inside of a ring, that wasn't a delusion. I'll be back on GCW television in no time and I will be ending my little problem with one Bishop Steele. Doctor Landry: Just keep yourself safe and stay on your meds. Donny Diamond: Will do. [Getting up from my seat I shake the hand of the doctor. He nods and makes motion towards the door, I nod in return with a smile on my face before making my way out the door. Walking outside the hospital for the first time in months is a glorious feeling. My true happiness however, comes from knowing I am making my way back to doing what I love doing the most, wrestling.] - Some times in life you know things that will cause others to call you insane. When faced with a situation like such the best course of answer, is to just tell them you were joking so you can be on your way.
