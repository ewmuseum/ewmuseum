I Choose Trick

31 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/31/2008 “TRICK OR TREAT!!!” The words rang over and over again throughout the night. Candy, costumes and fun: these were the purposes of Halloween for the majority. Kids around the world claim it to be one of their favourite holidays for a reason. However, the night is not all fun and games; at least not for those on the receiving end of pranks, scares and destruction of all things property. Behind the glistening sugar coating, Hallows Eve is truly a night of terror, largely due to the increasingly rebellious and, indeed, drunker youth of this generation. The holiday has gone from a holy occasion to an unholy one from its inception and the mixture of good and evil is never more prevalent than today. Of course, the earlier portions of the evening are largely taken up by children prancing about as devils, princesses, witches, monsters, super heroes and everything in-between. But, as the night wears on and the streets begin to clear of the young, the “old” come out to play. They come out with their eggs and toilet paper and their minds hell-bent on mischief and mayhem. However, on this night, one particular group of ill-intending teenagers will soon find themselves chomping off more than they can chew. And, their deep devotion to their dark deeds, will, in turn, cause them to discover what it truly means to be scared... 7:20 PM... The night is still very much alive for the precious trick-or-treaters going door to door in hopes of scoring big bounties of every candy imaginable. Some kids are young and with their parents, but others are old enough to be out in groups with their friends. Whatever the case: door bells are a-ringin? and the beautiful night is giving way to a flood of sugary goodness that parents and dentists alike are glad only comes once a year. One particular group of boys are dressed up as their favourite super heroes: Iron Man, Spider Man and Batman. They are one of the many groups of kids apparently old enough to go out on their own and reap the rewards of not having to drag a slow parent around. They make quick work of the street, running over lawns and leaping over gardens to get from house to house in the shortest amount of time possible. They occasionally pose in hero-esque ways as they zoom along and are just having a good time in general. Soon, the street they are on comes to a halt in the form of a dead end. However, the dead end gives way to a path through a ravine that goes to another street full of potential candy, so the boys don’t hesitate in blazing through it with their arms forward in flying poses. Leaves crunch under the feet of the three mighty super heroes as they jog merrily through the forested ravine path, bags of candy in tow. Tall lamp posts provide light for what would otherwise be a very dark path. However, with light comes more concentrated darkness and waiting in the shadows are a couple boys in their late teens looking to get their candy in one fell swoop, having some “fun” while they are at it. The two teenagers wait for the three smaller boys to get in-between them before they suddenly leap out on either side of the group. They are both wearing hideous, deformed masks and are dressed entirely in black. Needless to say, the much younger children are scared half to death at the sudden appearance of the two. The children then notice they are effectively trapped in-between the two teenagers and chills run through their bodies. They cower into a triangle in the middle, standing back to back, firmly gripping their night’s haul. The taller of the two teenagers lets out a short chuckle. Taller Teenager: What’s the matter? You boys scared without your mommies around? The two teenagers have a laugh at this, but the three little boys are obviously in a state of fear and shock. Shorter Teenager: You know, I think they might have wet themselves a little bit. Spider Man and Batman just take the criticism, but Iron Man doesn’t like it one bit. The kid, his plastic metal shimmering slightly in the yellow light from the lamps, shouts out against the two teenagers in an act of bravery. Iron Man: No we didn’t! Go away you big bullies! The two teenagers glance at each other for a few moments and then shrug. The act of bravery was clearly in vain, which leaves Iron Man deflated, along with his two friends. Taller Teenager: Tell you what: You guys hand over all your candy now and we’ll let you go without ruining your stupid little outfits. The three kids look at one another for a few moments, their eyes saying more than words can: they love candy, but they don’t want to get hurt. The bags of candy drop to the ground as a gust of wind whistles through the ravine. The three defeated heroes hang their heads as the two teenagers snatch up the candy and walk away, laughing to themselves as they do. The three heroes then proceed to turn around and run back to where they came from as the two teenagers lift their masks up and start rummaging through the bags for some treats to eat as they walk. They’re so distracted by the candy that they don’t see a dark figure lurking in the shadows nearby in the woods... 8:40 PM... The two teenagers are now beside a beat-up black car, piling bags upon bags of presumably stolen candy into the backseats. They’re in the far end of a parking lot located beside some steps leading into the ravine where they retrieved those bags earlier that night. The shorter one puts the final bag in the back and turns to his friend, who has the driver’s door opened. Taller Teenager: Pretty good haul, eh Rick? Rick: Yeah, sure beats actually going door to door for it like we used to. People won’t even give us candy now because we’re “too old.” The taller teenager gets into the car and closes the driver-side door. The car sputters to a cringe-inducing start and Rick opens up the passenger-side door, but stops when he sees something by the steps leading into the ravine. Rick: Hey, wait up a sec, Travis. I think we left a couple pieces behind. Rick goes over to the steps and bends down to pick up a couple candies on the ground. As he gets back up, a man dressed entirely in black, with a black ski mask on, suddenly grabs him from behind, placing a white cloth over Rick’s mouth and nose. After a few moments and muffled attempts at screams later, Rick is out cold. The man in black gently places Rick on the ground, picking up the candies Rick just picked up. Travis is oblivious to this all as he plays around with the radio in his car, attempting to get a station with good reception. When the man in black reaches about five feet away from the car, he chucks the candy he was holding into the bushes near Travis’s side of the car, causing a rustling sound. Travis turns to see what it is, allowing for the man in black to slip in to the passenger seat and grab Travis’s head from behind, applying the white cloth to his mouth and nose as well. Before long, Travis is out cold as well. The man in black releases his hold on him and gets out of the car, letting Travis drop with a thud into the passenger seat, no longer caring for the element of surprise. With both targets now down, the man in black quickly goes over to Rick. He grabs a single arm and simply drags him across the pavement of the parking lot en route to the car. When he reaches the car, the man in black opens up the back door and tosses Rick’s motionless body in the back with all the candy. He then casually walks over to the bushes he came out of to ambush Rick and leans into them, retrieving a few rolls of duct tape... 1:30 AM... Rick and Travis slowly wake up, the heavy smell of smelling salts overwhelming their senses. They both instinctively try to jolt up, but soon find that they can’t do so due to some sort of restraints on their bodies. Their eyes open with a flutter, but they find themselves blinded by the light being shined directly in their faces. A million thoughts rush through their minds as they try to comprehend what has happened and where they are and why they’re apparently stuck in place. One raspy voice moves all those thoughts to the back of their head. Voice: Welcome back... I trust your sleep was pleasant. A harsh cackle follows. The light blinding the boys? eyes is soon directly elsewhere, allowing for the two to fully take in their surroundings for the first time. They are in a small, dark, concrete room with no windows. The only light in the room comes from a high-powered handheld light that was previously blinding them. The light is now on the only piece of furniture in the room: a small, scratched-up wooden table to the side of the two boys. The light is enough to help them discern the environment and the fact that they have been thoroughly duct taped to the wooden chairs they are sitting in. Their captor, on the other hand, is still largely a mystery, his shadowy figure standing just beyond the reach of the light. However, after a few more moments of silence, Travis finally gets the courage to ask the burning question ? in the most eloquent way possible. Travis: Who the fuck are you!” The man in black just laughs hysterically. Travis: What the Hell man!” Why are you doing this? Rick, tears starting to pour down his face, gives a reluctant answer. Rick: He’s a fucking psycho, man! That’s why he’s doing this! The laughter comes to a screeching halt. Man in Black: A psycho, you say? One would tend to lean to that in this situation, yes. Fortunately, or unfortunately, for you: I’m not the crazy man you assume I am. Travis, who is holding up better than Rick, scoffs at the notion of his captor not being a crazy bastard. Travis: You have us tied up to chairs in a small concrete room. Tell me how this can’t be seen as the work of a complete fucking nut-job! There is silence for a few moments, before three distinct sounds begin to repeat over and over again: Flick. Fwoosh. Crack. The flame from the man’s lighter can be see flickering in the dark each time. Rick and Travis are a bit more uneasy when they see the lighter, but nothing quite prepares them for when the man in black steps forward and removes his black ski-mask to reveal another mask underneath. Of course, this isn’t just a regular mask. It’s simple, but that only draws more attention to its frightening features: black crosses for eyes and a nose and a big, fiery grin. The mask looks like it was taken straight out of a horror film. Needless to say: Face’s mask doesn’t help Rick or Travis in dealing with the current situation. Face: I am not Crazy. Crazy implies that I am out of my mind and do not have reasonable control over my personality or actions. I can assure you that I am quite knowledgeable of who I am and what I do... But, enough about me. What about you two? Hmmmm? Preying on little children in a path through a forest? Not such a saintly task, is it? Travis and Rick look down to the floor, avoiding eye contact with Face. Travis, still the bolder of the two, responds to Face’s inquiries. Travis: We didn’t ever touch those kids, man. You fucking kidnapped us! How can you compare us to you? Not impressed with the answer, Face waves a finger on his free hand. Face: No... it isn’t so simple, my friend. What you two did to those kids was no different than what I did to you... You took advantage of weaker individuals for your own personal gain... The only differences between you and I are what that personal gain is and the fact that there is no one who will come for me. Face lets out a dry chuckle, but Travis won’t have any of this. He shakes his head and speaks in a very matter-of-fact tone. Travis: Yeah, well you’re forgetting something: What we did won’t land us in jail! This only makes Face chuckle a bit more. Face: Is that so? You know, I’d think a little harder about the threats you choose to make... You should take some advice from your friend and start crying like a little baby. You’re not exactly in the best position to be making threats right now and who’s to say you’ll even be getting out of this room to say anything to anyone, let alone the police? Hmmm? With another laugh coming from behind the mask, the two boys suddenly become a bit more attuned to the reality of the situation. Rick continues to sob and now Travis begins to shake his head in dismay. Face: Yes... Now you’re beginning to see the big picture. Hmmm? No one’s going to be looking for you two deadbeats anytime soon. You made sure of that by alienating your family and all potential friends. Hell, no one’s going to miss you one bit; are they? You two are just a couple of pathetic loners. And, to top it all off: no one’s going to hear you scream... Both of the boys? eyes suddenly widen as Face lets out a deep, raspy laugh and walks out of the room. He comes back shortly after and starts to walk around the two, tossing pieces of wood by their feet. The two boys don’t like where this is going and now both of them are in tears, begging for Face to stop whatever he’s planning on doing to them. Travis: C’mon man! What did we do to you!” Why are you doing this!” Rick: We’ll do whatever you want, man! We won’t tell anybody about you; We swear! Face ignores their pleas as he walks around the two several times. When good layer of wood has been placed about their feet and chairs, Face once again leaves the room. Both boys now try to get out of the pile of wood, but find that their chairs are actually bolted down into the ground. Face comes back into the room with a tank of gasoline and Travis and Rick both begin to sob uncontrollably. Travis: Please, man! We’ll give you money! Rick: Yeah! We can work and give you our paycheques! Again, Face ignores them and begins to pour the gasoline on the wood around the boys. Shivering and crying, the two desperately and repeatedly beg for Face not to do anything. However, once Face is done placing oil on the wood around Rick and Travis, he just exits the room, placing the tank of gasoline outside. He then laughs hysterically at the two as they continue to yell out: “DON’T!” over and over again. Face: I’m sorry, but money isn’t what I want... With that, Face brings out his lighter. Flick! Face: What I want... Fwoosh! Face: Is for you to burn. Face begins to drop his lighter down onto Travis and Rick as they desperately try to move out of the way, all in vain. Face now continues to laugh hysterically as the lighter gets closer and closer to the gasoline covered wood. Face: Now, what is it that you boys would say when you went door to door as good little children on Halloween? Hmmm? The two boys just continue to sob. Face: Go on, say it! In-between their sobs, the two boys manage to say what Face was looking for. Rick & Travis: Tri-trick or-or treat! The lighter gets closer and closer, now only inches away from the wood beneath Rick and Travis’s feet. The two boys close their eyes and tense up. Crack! After a few moments, the two boys open an eye each to find Face has closed his lighter just before it reached them. Travis and Rick breathe heavily, still in a state of shock, despite not being on fire as they originally thought they would be. Face is still laughing as he puts away his lighter and takes something else out of his pockets. Face: I choose trick! With that, Face shoves a pill of some sort in both of the boys? mouths and forces them to swallow. Face chuckles to himself as he leaves the room. Soon, Rick and Travis find themselves getting very sleepy... 8:20 AM... Rick and Travis wake up, groggy and soar all over. They both have massive headaches. They take a look around and find themselves at the edge of a river in the ravine they spent their time in last night. And then they see their car, sitting in the river, completely demolished. They then see a path that the car blazed through the forest to get there, somehow miraculously finding a hole in the trees to go through. The two look at one another, rubbing their heads in pain. Travis: What the Hell happened...” Rick: I don’t know, man. I can’t remember last night at all. Travis: Yeah, me neither...But my mom is totally going to be pissed that we wrecked my car... The two just sit there, staring off into the distance, puzzled as to how this all happened. Rick scratches his head and turns back to Travis. Rick: If we wrecked the car...why are we over here? Travis looks down, then up to Rick, a look of utter confusion on his face. Travis: I don’t know, man... Then, Travis suddenly sniffs the air. Travis: Dude, what’s that sme- Travis finds the answer to his question as he looks down to his pants as sees a large spot that is darker than the rest of the black in his pants. Rick sees the same on himself and the two have disgusted looks on their faces...and then they shift around on the ground a bit and notice that the front of their pants isn’t the only thing they have to be disgusted about...
