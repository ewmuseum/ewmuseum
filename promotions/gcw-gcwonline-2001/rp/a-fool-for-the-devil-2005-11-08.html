A Fool For The Devil.

8 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 8, 2005 (Note: This is Post Devil's Night and Pre WorldWide #31.) Indeed, Savige seems to get stronger with each shot. Suddenly he fires one back, though Rei keeps on throwing punches. Cross punch. Quick uppercut. Straight, solid right. Punching a bigger man. Mike Savige. Not having the effect I want. He’s big. He’s turning green. I’m just pissing him off. Thunderous left. Lightning follow-up right. I look up at his face, spying a disconcerting glint. It’s daunting, but I’m not stopping. I turn my gaze to an obviously pleased LeStatt Knight for a moment - CRACK! A freight train full speed into my jaw. Hulk Smash. My vision blurs. An accrual to the existing haze from the assault courtesy of Aiden Jackson and his tag team partner, Dirty Shovel. I’m still too astounded and physically exhausted to worry about the constituent details. I just know that Aiden Jackson and Rei Tiu's mutual respect went flying, holding hands with the tooth I probably lost. But as soon as I’m done trading blows with Savige, Aiden gets my undivided attention. Not a good thing. The two exchange blows as behind them Aiden Jackson crawls into the dozer resting on top of Chrissy-Poo’s grave! Aiden swings it around and guns forward, heading right at Rei and Savige! The buzz of the crowd is a concatenate chain of narcotic cheers and boos. It’s all running together. So much has happened up to this point that I don’t even feel reality anymore. It’s a long and painful nightmare. Bryan: OH NO! WATCH OUT! I’m a puppet. My strings being pulled by a master director. In this case, LeStatt Knight. The dream weaver. Savige, seeing Aiden coming, dives out of the way. I’m swinging blindly, now. The air even more unfazed by my punches than Savige. And a poem. See Savige. See Savige run. See Savige run and jump. See Rei. See Rei spin around. See Rei’s eyes like headlights. I’m betting on another iron punch. What I get is the reason I don’t gamble. Rei spins around just in time to get struck with the speeding dozer! The small vehicle carries enough punch to throw Rei violently into the grave below, and Aiden immediately leaps out for the next dozer over! The bulldozer crashes into me in a drone of pain, muffled crowd and rasping gears. I’m thrown into a grave, the packed earth cold and clammy against my face. Some of it clings to my sweat dampened skin, gritty texture a repugnant feeling against hot, viscid flesh. Bryan: OH MY GOD! Aiden Jackson just ran over Rei Tiu with that damn bulldozer! Rei’s in the grave! As I look up, an overhead torch is flickering with suppressed life, peering down at me as I helplessly begin to climb to my feet. The camera peers down into the grave, where Rei is slowly pulling himself to his feet. But moments later, Aiden moves the dozer above forward, pushing the massive pile of dirt downward. Rei is crushed beneath the weight of the dirt, which fills the grave on top of him! Bryan: GOOD GOD! REI TIU HAS JUST BEEN BURIED ALIVE! Reality is that which, when you stop believing in it, doesn't go away. Philip K. Dick I can relate. The reality? I’m lying in a grave where I’m buried alive in Detroit, Michigan. My premature funeral. No wake. No sobs from loved ones and hopeful chatter about how I’m off to a better place. I’ve always wondered why someone has to go to “hell” to get to “heaven.” It seems to be an exorbitant entrance exam. The crowd unleashes its full fury at Aiden as he drops lifelessly out of the bulldozer. Lying on top of Rei’s grave, Aiden begins to laugh, chuckling with satisfaction as the boos rain down. I can hear Aiden’s laughter as my world fades from black to nothing. Call me Rei. Martyr in the making. [Later] BEEP. BEEP. BEEP. A false awakening. My eyelids creak open, revealing slivers and palpitating blobs of nothing. Maybe light. A sharp twinge stabs my eyes and they close in defensive reflex. There’s a discomforting immutable throb in my head. A smiling dwarf jack hammering my brain. I attempt to move, but the muscles in my body cry out in contempt. I wonder if I’m still buried. BEEP. BEEP. BEEP I feel trapped. My own body holding me hostage. I can move, but it hurts so much that I oblige with the hostage-taker. Body: Move and die. Me: Don’t worry. I feel like I’m dying when I move. I’m a crippled man who fantasizes about walking, but can’t even move to masturbate over it. What a predicament. I am Rei. Fantasizing about fantasizing. BEEP. BEEP. BEEP.Seeping in and out of consciousness at random. Devil’s Night memories are a flash of festering images in my mind. I see Aiden pull himself up, covered in dirt, and he helps as we hook Savige up and drive him hard into the earth with a nice tandem suplex. The air in the room is crisp, a bigger relief in each deep breath. I imagine the room. A lush paradise of tropical plants, swaying and waving at me as the cool, gentle wind sweeps across the land. The sandy beach beneath my feet warm and soft as I grip it between my toes. It’s beautiful, and it’s all mine. The sky is without a cloud, hand delivering my solitude one breath and ray of sunshine at a time. When I smile, reality once again bites. Aiden and I rise and turn to Knight, who turns his shoulder slightly so that the possible future prize of the GCW World Heavyweight title is gleaming under the light of the torches. I’ve turned back to Savige, pulling him to his feet, as Aiden all but froths at the mouth at the mere sight of it. I elbow him in the gut for his attention, and we shove Savige down into the grave. BEEP. BEEP. BEEP. I smell the musk of blood. I taste the sour rust of fresher blood. I’m a broken, battered man, trounced upon and subdued. Choking on my own life. The antagonist in reality stole my halcyon placidness and replaced it with my own recent history in complete mockery. I’m a near defenseless man, fighting my injuries in order to breathe the purified air. I point at the dozer and Aiden nods, heading towards it. Just as I’m moving, Savige snatches out at me with revenge from the grave, pulling me down with him. Savige and I exchange blows once again as Aiden looks on, hesitating. I hear the swell of crowd disapproval, before looking up to see Aiden reaching in just as I get the upper hand, helping me out of the grave. Trying to relax. The rhythmic purr of machinery entices hypnotic numbing, but I’m still unable to give a decent release. We are shaped by our thoughts; we become what we think. When the mind is pure, joy follows like a shadow that never leaves. Buddha Great. I’m f*cked. Savige reaches out and drags Aiden into the grave by his ankle, attacking like a rabid dog from inside. I turn and leave, smiling as the crowd boos. As I reach the MegaTron, I climb its side slowly. I listen as those boos become boisterous cheers while I climb to the top, reaching the upper platform where LeStatt Knight and Typhoid Mary are sitting. I throw a quick gesture at a curious Knight and without thinking, leap off. I’m flying through the air with an enormous moonsault, landing perfectly in the grave, taking both Savige and Aiden down into a massive heap of humanity in the bottom. BEEP. BEEP. BEEP.At least I’m alive. Right? I still don’t want to open my eyes. I don’t want to be a virgin newborn in this house of pain. I hear voices. Low at first, but steadily coming into focus. The flick to the ear of attentiveness. “He’s fine, or will be. His vitals are good, he just took quite a beating.” Husky, deep voice. An older man, tone revealing years of experience. Lids forced open. A brilliant penlight shines down, staring at me staring back. I roll my eyes for good measure. “We’ll just keep him over night for observation, try and take care of those wounds and lacerations. Looks like the poor fellow got hit by a bulldozer.” Exaggeration flaunts the shroud of truth. I hear a sympathetic chuckle, the kind where words are lost and someone shakes their head and forces a laugh. My lids close back and my head bounces to the side a bit. Hit by a bulldozer. He just doesn’t know how right he is. I’m devastated by the move, the sheer impact, but I manage to scramble to be able to climb out, dropping back down first to hoist Aiden on top of my shoulders. I roll him out of the grave, hearing the astonishment of the crowd, before I climb out myself. BEEP. BEEP. BEEP.A soft melody hums in the near silence of the room. I think I recognize the tune, but am too disoriented to know for sure. I feel a hand brush through my hair. “Mr. Tiu, everything’s going to be okay. We’re going to fix you right up, sugar.” Random beeps and chirps of machinery. She continues to hum. I let RipeFist go, after preparing to throw him in an open grave. I go over to help Aiden bury a lifeless Savige, and cross to the opposite side, finding a shovel. We begin thrusting dirt in unison across Savige’s body. More beeps. The humming stops as I hear the rustling of bags and the thumping sound of interrupted suction. The woman grunts as I feel a needle move on the underside of my arm. An extended beep. “This should help ease the pain, sugar.” Hair ruffled, again. There’s a cold sensation in my arm that slowly spreads throughout my body. Before long, I’m out again. [Flash]Out of nowhere, Aiden Jackson takes a golf-swing with his shovel, nailing Rei Tiu in the face! Rei flies off his feet and collapses into the next open grave over! [Flash] The fans explode as Rei suddenly appears on the second level again, running fast! In a flash he’s airborne, and he nails Savige with a huge cross body block! [Flash] Picking up the nearby shovel, the crowd screams a wild warning at Rei as he hoists it over his head. Aiden swings for Rei’s head again, but Rei ducks the blow and twists around with a quick legsweep! Aiden flies to the ground and Rei is on him in a flash, driving his fists into Aiden’s skull with violent vengeance! [Flash] Savige, seeing Aiden coming, dives out of the way. Rei spins around just in time to get struck with the speeding dozer! [Flash] But moments later, Aiden moves the dozer above forward, pushing the massive pile of dirt downward. Rei is crushed beneath the weight of the dirt, which fills the grave on top of him! BEEP. BEEP. BEEP.I was buried alive. Aiden Jackson ran me over with a bulldozer and buried me under mounds of dirt. For now, I feel nothing. Pain anesthetized by forced intravenous injection. I’m stuck in this bed wearing a gown that probably has an open backside. But not for long. I’m getting a reboot, so to speak. A reformatting of my internal hard-drive, because it was damaged over the course of Devil’s Night. Once it finishes, everything will be back to normal. And I’ll remember to hold a grudge. Call me Rei. Coming soon to an arena near you. Rei Tiu Reloaded.
