Date: WHO KNOWS Many people (none) have been wondering where two of GCW's true heroes have been. These patriots (foreigners) among patriots were truly the heart of the company for over eighteen (no) months. Turns out they won some sort of inter-company title belt and spent their time on a budget holiday provided by TEAM... somehow. They find themselves celebrating this victory with a high-priced whore and some deck chairs. THIS IS THEIR STORY. Today, of all days, I have difficulty believing it myself, we find Dr. Dirk Dickwood PhD (Doctor of Love... with severely burnt midgets) and Mr. Phil M. Atken. Dickwood would like to be known he is such a super agent that he is in fact SUCH a Superagent, he represents other agents. No one is quite sure what this achieves or signifies. We find them on a beautiful sandy beach... in the middle of winter... with snow surrounding them. This my friends is the power of a budget holiday. Dickwood: So then I locked her back in the cupboard, there was some scratching noises for a few days but they eventually quelled. Let me tell you these women HAVE to learn. Atken: Isla hands it out to every guest she manages to break out of the cupboard to greet. I personally used mine as toilet paper but someone may actually care about these sorts of things. Like local authorites for example. Dickwood: Surely it'd be separate authorities who would deal with my pyromania and this whores hypothermia. Dickwood: Are you still on that quest for glory and "Entertainment Salvation"? Atken: Well mostly I'm in it for money for little effort these days. GCW didn't want to be saved and that makes me sad. See, this is my sad face. Please make note Mr. Phil Atken is frowning at this period in time. Dickwood: Weren't you the guy who used to aim for the top regardless of where you ACTUALLY where. If you... sorry WE turn this ship around, you would have to pay for Candisqua, you'll have a thousand Candisqua's queuing around the block wait, in the cold I may add, for you. Just think about that. Atken: A thousand Candisquas? Dickwood: A thousand! Atken: EVERYONE IN THE CAR! Dickwood: Isn't GCW in Japan this week? Atken: Hell if I know. Dickwood: We really should plan out this career thing more. Atken: We really should.
