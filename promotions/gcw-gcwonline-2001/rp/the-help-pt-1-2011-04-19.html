The Help Pt. 1

19 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/19/11 The heavy rain beat down on the roof of a van, almost making it sound like a million tiny little horses ran back and forth on the roof. Inside is Lollie and an unknown long-haired goth man. The man has dark hair, a black trench coat, and more black eye-liner than Lollie ever has. Surprisingly, even with his eyes practically painted shut, he can see well enough to drive through the downpour. Lollie: "Hunter, I'm still not sure what you're going to do. He doesn't even like you... for good reason." "Hunter" shoots a quick glare over to her, before returning his attention to the road. Lollie: "You've never been nice to him. You've always just tormented and teased him.... You're kind of a bully." Once again, he shoots a glare her way, but by the time his eyes set on her, the glare has faded. He raises his eyebrows and shrugs, seemingly agreeing with her. Hunter: "Yeah. I am kind of a hard-ass." The goth man smiles, which is a bit awkward to see someone so depressing-looking smiling. Lollie: "You're not a hard-ass. You're a jerk and a pervert. I learned that the very first day I met you, years ago." A flashback reel is triggered in his over-painted mind, but we can't see it. It's in his mind... duh. Hunter: "What are you talking about? It's totally customary to shake when you first meet someone!" The gal in green gives him a "you're kidding" look. He doesn't see it, 'cause his prettified eyes are on the rain-soaked road. Lollie: "You don't shake someone's boob..." Hunter once again grins, apparently still in the flashback and enjoying every moment of it. Hunter: "I.. uh.... missed! I definitely didn't mean to grope your gazonga!" The grin doesn't disappear, just as the guilt still hangs thick in the air. Lollie rolls her eyes. Lollie: "Do you even give a crap about Tommy's condition, or are you just here to ogle me and futilely try to get into my pants?" The driver's grin disappears and is replaced by a look of shock. Hunter: "I'm appalled that you would think such things of me! I don't wish to get into your pants!" She stares at him, not believing a word out of his black-lipsticked lips. Hunter: "I'd rather get your clothes OFF, not get into them!" The devilish grin returns... but quickly disappears as Lollie reaches over and slaps him. He remains in control of the vehicle, but not the moment. Lollie: "I asked for Katsy to come, and she sent you. She hates you as much as I do. Why the hell would she send you?" Hunter: "You hate me?" Lollie: "Sometimes, but that's not the point. Why are you here instead of her?" Hunter shrugs. Hunter: "She's probably trying to help Reese get ready for a new batch of kids to train. That's coming up, y'know." The gal in green sighs, obviously wishing that Hunter was a bit more feminine and bit less... Hunter. Lollie: "What is she going to do? She doesn't wrestle." Hunter: "She's got a great mind for business. She's running the behind the scenes and running through the applicants." Lollie: "And that's something that Reese can't do?" Once again, the goth shrugs. Hunter: "I'm not in the loop on that one. I'm just there to help beat the shit out of the kids..." Lollie: "You mean 'train' them?" Hunter: "That's what I said." Never has a man in goth grinned as much as Hunter does. Lollie: "Fine. If you're here, then you're going to help Tommy." Hunter: "What's wrong with him?" Lollie looks a bit surprised. Lollie: "Katsy didn't tell you?" He shakes his head. Lollie: "He's got amnesia. But only when it comes to certain things...." Hunter's grin is long-gone by now. Hunter: "Like what?" Lollie: "He doesn't seem to remember who Bryan Mayhem is, which is probably for the best...." Hunter: "Then what's the problem?" She takes in a deep breath, holds it, then sighs it out. Lollie: "... he doesn't remember me either." Hunter takes his eyes off the road to see a depressed Lollie in his passenger seat. Hunter: "Nothing whatsoever? 'Cause he seemed fine in his match last week, except for that one thing..." Lollie: "He seems to trust me, but he says he doesn't have any solid memories of me. Just feelings... and what one thing?" Hunter: "The fact that he won." The gal in green glares at him for a second... but it quickly disappears, as she seems to realize that he wasn't just insulting him... but also pointing out something different. Hunter: "Toga isn't typically a winner. And if he ever does, it's typically a fluke or luck. But last week, aside from knocking himself out, he almost dominated! Where the hell did that come from?" Lollie slowly nods, seeing his point. Lollie: "You may not be friends with him, but you know him. And an extra pair of eyes, might be able to help me figure out a way to fix him." Hunter: "I'll do what I can, Booberella." Once again, Lollie reaches over and slaps him. She then crosses her arms over her chest and just stares out of the window. Hunter: "Sorry... just trying to lighten the mood." Lollie: "Just drive." Hunter sighs, but obliges. The rest of the rain-soaked drive is in silence... and awkward sexual tension.
