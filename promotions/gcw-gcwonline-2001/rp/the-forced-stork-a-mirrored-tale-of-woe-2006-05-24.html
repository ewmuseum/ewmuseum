The Forced Stork: A mirrored tale of woe.

24 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/24/2006 The form of Eva VanLorne sat in front of her computer, hunkered over her drawn up knees as she read the contents of the website before. Idly, slender hands lifted a cup of steaming coffee to her lips drawing from the heated ambrosia as she dropped her knees. Setting the cup aside, Eva placed her digits on the keyboard and began typing furiously. She was a woman on a mission. That mission's name was Brandon Youngblood. From the first encounter with him, she had found herself intrigued by his attitude and straight forward nature. He was abrasive, of that, there was no doubt. But, something about him scratched at the back of her mind like a mosquito bite that just wouldn't go away. If he was hiding something, she was bound and determined to find. At this point, she would do anything to coax him into aiding the stable in it endeavor to stand against the combined forces of Dies Irae and Knightmare Brigade. Googles of information had been found on the man, but nothing that he hadn't already told her, or that wasn't already public knowledge. As such, the hunt continued on. Clickity clack clack clickity clack- The keys continued to suffer the expert pounding of her digits as she frowned and attempted to dig deeper. ? Surely there is something somewhere.? She spoke the words in a breathy whisper, thinking she spoke to no one but herself. That was until a sudden crash of a vase had her whirling in her chair wide eyed only to find herself staring into eyes that mirrored her own. ? Theresa,? she breathed with relief, ? You scared me. What are you doing up so late?? As Theresa hesitantly came forward, Eva took noticed of the red rims around the lower part of her eyes. Theresa had been crying. Frowning, the diva turned mother pushed out of the chair and came forward to drop to her knees before the onyx haired babe. Tenderly, she wiped away the remnants of tears and offered a concerned smile. ? What happened honey?? Even now, Theresa refused to answer verbally. Three weeks and still not a word out of her wayward daughter. It was beginning to become a major concern. Sucking in her lower lip, Eva looked at Theresa trying to figure out exactly what had happened to make her cry. She had no experience with this. ? You should go back to bed Theresa.? Theresa shook her head vehemently refusing. Eva frowned. ? Theresa I will not tolerate arguments. Not even silent ones.? Again Theresa shook her head, still refusing to go. The dive simply did not know what to do. For a moment awkward silence hung in the air thick as thieves, before Theresa solved the problem for them both. She rushed forward and wrapped her arms tightly around Eva's neck. At first, she was shocked by this sudden display of affection. But in a moment her mind registered the sniffling and tiny whimpering of a child crying silently. Hesitantly, Eva placed her arms around Theresa pulling her close. ? Shh, there is no reason to cry.? She struggled with the words. ? Did you hurt yourself?? Theresa shook her head. ? Are you frightened of the dark?? Again, the child shook her head. ? Did you have a bad dream?? This time Theresa nodded mutely. Finally, they were getting somewhere. Slowly Eva pulled away to look into her babe's eyes. One look, and she suddenly understood what had caused her child's pain. She had dreamt of her parents. Sighing heavily, Eva stood only to bend down and scoop up Theresa before heading off to her own bedroom...her research forgotten for now. Little did Theresa know, Eva understood this pain all too well. Once in her room she deposited Theresa into her bed and climbed in beside her only to pull the tiny form into her arms and kiss her brow. ? I understand what you are feeling Theresa. I lost my parents at a young age too.? Theresa pulled her head back and stared at Eva suspiciously. ? I know you must think I am making this up. But it is true. My mother died first, she was very sick and the illness stole her away from me. A year later my father was killed in a plane crash. Only last year my twin brother, Erik, was killed in a car accident.? Theresa's eyes widened as she listened to her new mother. Eva managed to smile despite the pain that rose at the mention of Erik. She turned her head slowly to spy his picture on the nightstand and reached out plucking it up to show Theresa. ? This was Erik. Handsome wasn't he?? Theresa nodded. ? So you see, little one, I do know what you are suffering. I stopped talking for a long while after my mother died. I had built this wall around my emotions to hide from the world and the pain I was suffering from..much like you are doing now. But I did not know, how much my not speaking made others suffer. It was selfish-? She never got to finish her words. Theresa suddenly pulled away and scowled at Eva angrily. Blanching, Eva sighed and placed the picture back in its place before turning back to Theresa. ? I...? She drew a relenting breath. ? I am sorry Theresa. I did not mean to make you think I thought you selfish. I honestly do not think that. Forgive me?? Reluctantly, Theresa nodded in agreement before she snuggled up to Eva once more. Slowly matured fingers would begin to stroke the raven locks of her child in a soothing manner hoping to lull her to sleep. It did not take long before she noticed the steady even breathing of a child in a deep slumber. A soft smile touched her lips as she craned her head to gather a better look at her new addition. It was an odd feeling. She felt...pride in something other than herself.
