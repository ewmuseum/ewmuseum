Chances?

28 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28 Mar 06 Her lips tasted oh so sweet on this night. This night was to be special in every sense of the word. A lot had happened since that night when I proposed to Mary, a hell of a lot. So much in fact that we had never really gotten the chance to celebrate properly. Sure we had wild sex, but that was nothing new, although I’m not complaining. What I really had wanted to do was take her out, get her dressed up, eat some good food and perhaps have a dance or two. Then have wild sex. Problem was there were personal problems that got into the way. Namely having a knife driven into my back by the one man whom I thought I could trust with my life. That son of a bitch, RaZiel was never a friend to me, hell I didn’t even know who he really was. It hurt to have everything I thought I knew ripped from me. Of course there was Damen too, fucking Damen? I had a million questions regarding a brother I thought dead for eight years. Now I had nobody to ask them to. At least not yet. I would be foolish to really believe that RaZiel died in that fire, and I am no fool. Damen found him and pulled him out; they will hide until the time is right. Since the blow out I have had a lot of time to think. Mostly about where my life was and where it should be going. I rarely ever regretted any of the wrong I did to people. My logic was do onto them as they would do onto you. In my eyes people could never be trusted, they all would fail you eventually and if they wouldn’t fail you they certainly wouldn’t be around very long. The truth of the matter is, I have grown jaded. My brother, my wife, my daughter, lovers, and friends they have all caused nothing but heartache. What I didn’t realize was that there really are good people out there as well. And I’ve hurt a few of them. It began with Jason Dread when I was hungry for the fame and the respect that came with being the World Champion. Sure out in front of the crowds I justified my stabbing him in the back on being sickened that they loved him. Yes I said that he was nothing to me and that he was undeserving of his throne. Truth is I was lying through my teeth. I had become fond of the man and even gotten some respect for him. Problem was that I had no desire to let people in, it is the one thing I have always been good at, keeping people out. Of course I didn’t know how to handle the emotions I felt, so I ripped him apart. I broke down a good man all because I was afraid of knowing what it was like to have a friend again. It never really was about that Title, as much as I would have liked it to have been. I knew that was coming to me either way. Dread was a man of his word and he would have given me my shot. Leave it to me to make enemies of everyone. But could I stop at one betrayal? Of course not. What I did next may be the worst of the worst. At the time I got some sort of sick satisfaction from it all, but now I realize that what I did to Eva VanLorne was despicable. I never suspected she’d fall in love with me. I only suspected that we may grow a very strong sexual attraction to one another, which did happen. I was fine with all that too, but when I began feeling more than that is when that fear kicked in once again. So what was I to do? I flipped the whole situation around. I used Mary to help it all go down. Even after Eva was raped I hid behind my bullshit macho exterior. I hated to see her in that state but those barriers are tough to rip down when they have stood so strong for so long. I wanted to wipe away her tears, hold her until she slept, and make everything seem right again. I didn’t allow it. I couldn’t. Then to make life even more complicated, Mary found me in GCW. She had a habit of doing this whenever I thought that I had gotten over her completely. Back in 3WA Mary found me while I was dating one Medea Hardy and now with Eva. This time I used Mary ? yes I did, I know that now. Yes I did have strong feelings for her but I used her as an aid in my reasoning for tearing Eva down. Eva was a good person. Jason was a good man. They both hate me with a passion now. Until now I never thought I’d care either way. Now I know how it feels to have someone you trusted completely leave you on the side of the road. Now I see what my life has been missing. It isn’t just the love of a woman but the caring of a real friend. Perhaps I could form that type of bond with Alex. But that’s a man I don’t have the energy to explain at this moment. Sadly the two prior chances I have had, I blew up. I don’t think neither of them would ever forgive me and deep down that eats at me. I put up this front when I am backstage at these GCW shows like I don’t give a damn about anyone or anything. Things couldn’t be more different. Every time I see Eva I want to tell her I am sorry and that I wish I could be her friend, someone she could come to in times of need. I wish I could shake Jason’s hand again and be there to watch his back like I did when Octavian was trying to destroy him. A second chance to make things right is all I’ve wanted since the day my wife died. But do men like me really get second chances? Do I even deserve one? I don’t know but what I do know is that for some reason, I have been given the love of a beautiful woman. One that I intend to make my wife. One that I fully plan to start a family with. Deserving? Maybe not but I will treasure it with everything I got. Hopefully one day this barrier can be broken down and I will be able to let a select few in. Perhaps someday Eva VanLorne will find it in her to forgive me ... that is when I get the courage to actually say I am sorry. “Statty-bear?” Her voice was so soft and full of sincerity, yet it still made me jump a bit. Here I was trying to show my fiance? a good time and I was lost in my own thoughts and problems. I smile slightly and reach out, grabbing her hand into my own. Slowly I rub my thumb over the top of her hand. She cocks her head to the side a bit and smiles softly. That smile makes my heart melt. “Sorry Hermosa, just thinking.” “Its Okies?” She bites her bottom lip for just a moment. “But Um ? Statty-Bear I gots something to tells ya.” “What is it love?” Her eyes drop to the table, as a young child does when they are nervous about telling their parents about a bad report card. Obviously I know something is wrong. I slide my chair around the table and sit right next to her. I bring my hand up and grasp her chin gently into it and bring her gaze up to mine. I smile at her, doing my best to reassure her that everything will be okay. “Tell me Miss Mary, it can’t be that bad.” “Mary is?” Her voice trailed off as she tried to find the words to say. “You are what?” “Well ?” She looks up at me with her dark eyes and instantly tears begin to fall from them. I pull her closer. “Mary just tell me.” “Mary’s ? well ? Both of us ? Is gonna have a baby.” I couldn’t speak I could barely breath. The one thing I could do? Cry. I have said it many times in the past, but happiness is so hard to find. But I have. Maybe, just maybe second chances are possible.
