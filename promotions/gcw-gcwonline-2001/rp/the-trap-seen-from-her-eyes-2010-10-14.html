The Trap Seen From Her Eyes.

14 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/14/10 The clock was nearing one in the morning as I heard the doors open downstairs. All the lights in the room were off, and across the bed I lay on my side as I waited for him to enter the room. Finally enters the room that is only lit by candlelight and I smile at his blank expression as he sees me waiting for him. He closes the door behind him as we hear sirens fill the air, while he stalks towards me, climbing into bed with me. I can tell your little walk did some good for you... Before I can finish my sentence he grabs hold of me and pins me down to the bed, greeting me with a kiss. The night went by fast and before I could really get a grasp on what time it was, I was awaken by the sound of the telephone ringing. He was still asleep laying beside me in a world of his own, and that brought a smile over my face as I turned back over to answer the call. Nudging him, I watch as he rubs his eyes a bit from the blinding morning sun. He turns over to see me holding the phone in my hand as I explain to him the details of the conversation. For as long as I have known Donny, he rarely showed any emotions and there was no exceptions here when I told him the police were on the phone concerning the death of his long time friend Larry. Watching him closely for the entire conversation I waited for him to need me for comfort but it was never needed. Which proved to me that even after all of the years we were apart, Donny Diamond was still the same person he has always been even though he worked so hard for people to think otherwise. They want me to come down to the station for some questioning. They need some information about Larry. Before getting out of bed he leaned in to give me another kiss, and after such a long time away I could not resist. A little time passed as I remained in bed trying to awake as I smiled at the thought of Donny welcoming me back into his life after we parted ways on such bad terms. - Standing in the doorway I dropped my bags down to the floor and turned back to look at him. He remained seated on the couch, looking down towards the floor with his hands covering his face. It has been three years Donny, and it has been a difficult time for both of us. She wasn't just your daughter, she was my daughter too and I will miss her just as much as you do. But you have to learn to deal with it and continue to live life and stop blaming me for the accident. That argument was between the two of us, but the accident was your fault! You're the one that put her in the car and drove off! Looking to me I can see tears running down his face, his lips part, but words are unable to come out. I pick up my bags as I push the door open and walk outside towards my car. As I start the engine I see him standing in the doorway, and I know even after all of the arguments and blaming, he still doesn't want me to leave. I'm sorry, but there is nothing left between the two of us. I know she was what brought happiness into your life, but you should feel the same way about me too. You need to get your life back together Donny, or you will lose it all. - As I sat up in bed with the sheets around me, I saw him walk out of the bathroom. For the first time in years I saw him look to me and smile, and at that very moment I knew he had finally put all the bad times behind us and was ready to start over. He walks towards the door, but I quickly grab him by the hand and turn him to face me. You want me to get dressed and come with you? I know how much Larry meant to you. Even though I knew the answer, he deserved for me to ask anyway. He assured me every thing would be fine, but he needed some time alone to sort everything that just happened so that he could be as much help to the police as possible. Okay, well I'll go do some shopping to pass some time. How about when you're done we can meet up for dinner where we can celebrate us getting back together? Say around five-ish? Agreeing with me he nods as I let his hand go and he walks off. Following him downstairs with the bedroom covers around me, I wave goodbye to him from the door as he drives off. Closing the door behind me I head back upstairs and lay my clothes for the day out on the bed. I head into the bathroom and start the water flowing in the tub as I drop the sheets and step in. Leaning back I reach over for the remote control and turn on the small television that is across from me. As I begin washing myself the news comes on, and the reporter is at the scene of a murder that took place last night. Last night a horrific event happened in this ally behind “Larry's Total Package” Gym. The owner, Larry Sniztz, was beaten and then tossed into a dumpster where he was burned alive. As of this time the police do not have any leads, but the town is in a bit of sorrow. Larry Sniztz was a local hero as he would take kids coming home after school off from the streets and let them train and exercise in his gym. He will be missed by many. The police ask that if anyone has any information regarding this tragedy to please contact them. The more I watch the news coverage the further my mind slips away and back to when I first met Donny. He was just moving into his own place, after his foster parents died in a car accident, and we ran into each other on the sidewalk. After a few more run-ins we decided to go out on a few dates and as things began to get more comfortable, we began to share stories of our lives together. It wasn't until after he proposed to me and we moved in together that he told me about his biological family. His mother died when giving birth to him and his twin brother... Ron and Donny were separated at birth and were never really close. Ron was mentally unstable and last I remember hearing, he was placed in a mental hospital. But his dad... He said he met up with his dad a couple of times, but he was murdered... And Larry was always like a dad to him, but now he's gone as well... But from what Donny told me years ago, his dad was killed exactly the same way Larry was murdered last night. None of this can be true. No way could Donny be capable of doing such terrible things. Noticing that I'm beginning to talk out-loud I splash some water over my face. Stepping out from my bath I dry myself off as I head back into the bedroom where I begin to put on my clothes. Grabbing my car keys from the dresser top I walk downstairs and head outside. The sun is shining bright and the wind is blowing nicely as I seat myself in my car and close the door. - I've been driving around town for a while now, looking at all the shops that I have passed by. I told him I would spend a few hours shopping while he finished business up at the police station before we met for dinner. At the moment however, shopping is the last thing I have on my mind as I soon find myself driving in front of the police station. Turning into the parking lot I find a spot to pull into as my hands grip the steering wheel. I know my assumptions are more than that, after all of the information Donny told me over the years I know he is the one that murdered not just Larry, but his dad as well. He deserves punishment for such terrible crimes... But then again there has to be a reason he did it. Backing out of the parking lot I drive out back onto the street, with no real idea of where I want to go. From inside my purse I hear my cellphone ringing and I reach in to grab it and I see its Donny giving me a call. My thumb is over the answering button, but just before I answer I question myself on exactly what I would say to him. So instead of answering the call, I turn the phone off and let the call go to voice mail. As I place the phone back into my purse I realize I want some answers to all the questions swarming around inside my head. It takes me a bit to get there, but soon I arrive at Larry's gym and I drive past it till I reach the next ally over. Getting out of my car I walk towards the gym, unsure of what I am looking for, and even more unsure of what I will find. At the front door I notice the blood spill on the ground and on the wall, I duck under the police tape, open the door slightly and slide in. Everything is packed into boxes, and as I continue to walk around it begins to feel creepier as time goes on. Noticing the wrestling ring I walk towards it. You have no reason to be here disgracing this place. You're the reason Larry was killed, and soon as I can find some evidence Donny is going to be locked behind bars for good. Startled by the unexpected voice I slowly turn to see Ben standing there. From the words he spoke he obviously knows the same thing that I do, Donny was behind all of this. And from the rage clearly seen in his eyes, he wants revenge at any cost. Why would Donny do such a thing? He loved Larry like a father! Because he was tired of losing, and he was tired of the preaching Larry gave him. When you have a career like Diamond has, you build that career by destroying anything that stands in your way. And from the way things have been going for him in GCW he saw Larry as the one standing in his way. Well if Larry would of led him to victory like he promised, this would of never happened! ...No, I mean... How could I have possibly let such words slip from my lips. Ben is staring at me in disbelief over the words I just said, but soon he snaps out of it as he pulls his cellphone from his pocket... - Nighttime has set in and all the lights in the building are off. My make up is completely smeared on my face from the crying I've been doing as I sit in the back room in a corner. I hear a car driving down the ally and it startles me, soon the door opens and I remain completely silent as I see Donny walk into the building. Quietly he closes the door behind him and stops for a moment before walking out of the room and towards the ring never noticing me. Inching towards the door I watch as he finds Ben crucified via the ring ropes, blood dripping from his face. I was expecting Donny to be surprised, instead he looks like a kid on Christmas morning opening all of his gifts. He passes his hand across Ben's face in the blood and a grin crosses his face for a moment, until he notices me peeking in from the room I was hiding in. Katrina... Did you do this? Instead of answering him I walk out of the room towards him. He turns to face me as I nervously walk towards him, and he caresses my face my face while smearing some Ben's blood on my cheek. We embrace in a kiss until I pull away to explain myself. I did this Donny... I did this to Ben... When you left I went take a bath and put on the news, they were covering Larry's murder. Then I remembered the conversation we had years ago about your dad and this all looked the same. Then I came here looking for something tell me different, but then I saw Ben and he blamed you. And by accident my fears of the truth escaped my lips and he was going to call the police, and I just... I just couldn't let him send you away, not after we just rediscovered each other. He grabs me by the shoulders and stares me deep in the eyes. I know he can feel me shaking with fear and nervousness, but I'm not really sure on how else I should feel given the circumstances. This Katrina... This... You could not have given me a better gift. His words comfort me as we embrace in a kiss once more more, while Ben starts to awaken and groan in pain.
