An Act of War: Part I

13 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/13/06 ~McClancy Residence~ ~Las Vegas~ Worldwide this week was in Phoenix, just a few hours south past the town of Boulder from where Blaise and Ukyo called home. They could stay here for a few weeks and travel to and from the shows without much issues. This also meant that it was time for Blaise and Ukyo to both spend with Kailey and Kylie, his twin daughters. Although their nanny, the former servant of Annie Leannan was always with them also since as a father, Blaise also had other duties. Blaise always loved it when they had these dates that were close to home though. It was easy to get some rest. And the last few weeks had been hectic. Between Dangerous Games, photo shoots, interviews, and everything else, it was nice to go home to something that you could actually call home. But home meant that they could also be called upon for doing other things if they were needed by the Order. As he and Ukyo laid in their bed, dozing... tonight would be no different. Blaise was the first to wake as the static electricity built in the room and the portal formed near the double paneled patio door that overlooked their backyard pool. Only one could portal in here like that, for the place was warded from such. That person would have been the one that instilled the wards into place... Princess Aine Morrigan Leannan. And if she was using her powers to raise a portal, there was something up. Blaise got up and stepped out from his bed. He opened a drawer in the desk that was but a few yards away and pulled out a set of spirit knives. Annie stepped through the portal to take in the view. Blaise glanced over to the clock.. they had gone to bed early tonight, it was about midnight. Shit. ?What do you want?? Annie looked at him as he stood there with the desk positioned so that she couldn't see too much. She knew all too well that he didn't have a modest bone in his body. Not many of the Faery did. ?You're being summoned for an operation. A very important one. I'm to take you back right now. You and Ukyo only for now.? ?What if I say I'm going to get some sleep and you can call me in the morning.? Annie grinned a wicked grin. ?Ah, but this mission invovles something you've been wanting to do for a while.? ?What?? She just took everything in in the room as Ukyo started to get up and doff her sleeping clothes to start to change. Blaise put the extra knives up. ?Tonight, Prince Turren... we move on the Roman's forces. Your expertise in the matter is needed.? ?Call Malik, his people are trained for it.? She laughed. ?You heard The Keeper. We now move on our own. The KnightMare Brigade moves with you as it's leader. You are trained as a hunter and are one of their best ever. You are also to be the Crown Prince of this land in the new world when Queen Maeb is crowned. Not to mention, for the operation that possibly breaks into the Roman's standing, you should be leading it.? Blaise started to pull a pair of pants on. ?Well you're only wanting me. So, this is not a KB operation.? Annie nodded. ?There is more to the brigade then the Brands. But you have someone who is wanting to go with you. You have the support of at least the Unseelie Courts on this.? ?Give us a few minutes then.? Blaise shook his head as he thought about what this could be as he and Ukyo continued to get dressed. ~A half hour later~ ~Through the Portal~ ~The Order's Sanctum~ ~Four Winds Academy~ ~Outside of Dallas Texas~ Blaise and Ukyo now stood in the underground of the Academy that housed the main operations of the Order in the western hemisphere. They were dressed in the black ballistic jump suits that they wore as trained and certified hunters for the Order. But the patches that would have identified them as members of one of the Hunter teams were removed and now they bore a symbol that was undeniably the flaming unicorn of the KnightMare Brigade. They'd been waiting for a few moments as things appeared to be moving all over the place. There are literally hundreds of people manning what would be the command center for the operation. It was a round deck, down below was where the others would enter the portal to take them to where they were going. But this wouldn't be a normal portal. For some reason Ian had gone off his rocker. He wanted to move about thirty people at once and do it with a bang. He'd unlocked the room that housed the Nexus this place was built on. A magical junction of over twenty ley lines that went through this place. You could move an army this way, but anything that would be watching for something like this would have their detectors and senses raped and assaulted by using it. Annie's mark was all over this operation though. She knew what worked for her. The sight of the creatures of the Unseelie court that served their princess operating the devices both modern and mystical was a sight to behold. Annie finally joined them again, walking in with a stoic look upon her face. ?You two ready?? ?Well, if I had my way I'd get about eight hours of sleep first, and then I'd go jumping through a nexus point, fighting and then coming back with some sort of goo or ichor all over my clothing. But since that's not an option, we'll take door number 2.? Annie gave a half smile at his smart ass remark. She held up a remote and the large plasma screen came to life on the wall next to them. The first thing that came up was the view of a manor house with a nice green lawn. ?This is your landing point. La Batte Mansion on the outskirts of Cleveland.? ?LaBatte? The fucker that Alexis used to work for?? ?Yes, a Giovanni Ghoul who's running a sect of the Society of Leopold. He works for both a Camarilla master, or set of them. So he's one day hoping to be embraced as one of the Kindred, and maybe taking over the business aspects of their operations there. This is his estate. No cameras, no nothing. He's arrogant.? Blaise nodded as he looked at the cycling images. ?Arrogance, my favorite personality flaw. What is this mission?? Annie didn't get to say it. They were interrupted by the Keeper of Secrets, Ian Childress as he walked in. ?There are many goals to this mission. Your primary one will be to bring Geffroi LaBatte back here, very much alive. Secondary is the destruction of the Cleveland Den housed under his estate. Third would be to openly leave a calling card as to whom did it for when the Roman's people come to find out what happened. LaBatte will be returned alive. Anyone else, I don't care.? Blaise had one question to ask him though. ?Why tonight?? Ian turned and was looking down at the Nexus point. ?I've had a watcher on him for the last week. Tonight he's meeting with a few members of the Cincinnati Den and a few of the Giovanni. As it is, this will be like dropping a bomb in the middle of times square for them. Anything that fights back is a target and has a bullseye on their chest. Anything that runs and doesn't look official, can run so they tell the cops that we know are on the take. And pick one or two of the officials, even if they're minor to tell the others. We want them to know that we hit it. I'm done hiding.? Blaise took a moment to read his sensai, his teacher, his mentor and try to see what it was that he was thinking This man was also his cousin by marriage. ?What or who am I going in with? I've been told it's not just Ukyo and I.? At that, Blaise realized he should have been watching in both the physical and astral. Men, creatures appeared all around them. All of them bearing the markings and uniforms of the combined Seelie and Unseelie Court in the old country known as Ireland to the mortals, but Tir Na Nog to the Faery. One of them was wearing all black and gold. His helm bore two stag horns that went up about a foot from his forehead. He reached up and opened the faceplate on the helm and raised a salute. ?Hail, Prince Turren of the kingdom of Tir Tairngire.? Blaise smiled. ?Hail, Prince Herne, leader of the Wild Hunt.? Ian looked at the two of them. ?Yes, Blaise. You are assigned commander duty. But I want you and Ukyo specifically to go after LaBatte. But other then that, you have Herne's squad of the Wild Hunt and a Fell Troll and a Dryder for capture if you need it.? Ian turned to Annie and addressed them all. ?Prepare to open the Nexus. The target is generated. Let's get this done so we can celebrate at sunrise!? Annie nodded and then stepped to the side of the room. For only the second time in knowning her, Blasie watched as Annie shifted her form, breaking away from the veil the human reality put around her. Her clothing changed to a gossamer fabric covering her shapely blody. A corset was all she wore over it, leaving very little to the imagination. A pair of black lined dragonfly like wings came from her back. She'd need all the power she could muster to power the nexus, and the constraits of the Veil would not be as open to allow that. Blaise tensed up as things were getting ready to go. He looked at Ukyo and then to Annie's brother, Herne of the Wild Hunt. Things were about to get interesting. ~To Be Continued~
