...To Be Loved (Part 2: Out Of My Way)

7 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 7, 2008 An Introduction... The look on his face told the whole story. With a magazine clinched in his hands he just looked back at me. I'm not sure if it was anger, shock, or disappointment that gave him that look... but it was a look I never wanted to see. It definitely wasn't the look on his face in my dreams. For months I'd dreamt of this moment. Hanging onto the hopes that he'd someday come back to me, and everything would be right in the world. It wasn't supposed to happen like this. This wasn't how the story was supposed to end. "I...." Stumbling through my words I had no idea what to say to him. So instead I just stood there uncomfortably with my mouth hanging open. Hoping that somehow the words would just come out. They never did. Stuck in a lump of self-disgust in the pit of my stomach. "What is this?" He said holding the magazine out for me to see as a million emotions took control of my thoughts, leaving me unable to compute a simple request such as to close my mouth. "It's..." Speaking had never been so difficult. My entire life I've had friends and family bitch to me about not ever knowing when to shut up, and now when I need to talk... I couldn't. "It's not what you think!" "I want to believe you Viv. I really do." His voice cracked as he spoke. I could almost see the tears he was fighting back, every croak in his voice made my heart break just a little more. "Everything I've gone through and this is what I get? This is the girl I was meant to be with?" "No!" Just blurted out of my mouth. It was all I could manage to say, but it was almost like it was already too late. He had his mind set. He had something to get off of his chest. "That asshole kept me a room like a prison cell. Away from everybody else. Cut me off from human contact and treated me like a fucking animal." I watched in horror as a single tear began to run down his face, "Put a collar around my neck, tried to train me like I was less than human. Going to sleep the only thing that kept me from wanting to die was you. It was you! And you do this to me? How could you Vivica?!" My throat closed up like it was caught in a vice as my stomach turned. I could hear my heart racing in my ears as my brain put out unintelligible commands. Looking back at him like a deer in headlights I said nothing. "You got nothing to say do you?" Markus looked down at the ground, unable to look into my eyes. I couldn't look into his either. I hadn't done anything wrong but... I almost did. It was enough to make me feel guilty. Very guilty. "I see. Good luck in the Dual Halo." He balled the magazine into a wad in his hands before throwing it at my feet. "You fucking whore." With those words my heart shattered into a million pieces. As my eyes swelled with tears and my body began to tremble I watched a fuzzy vision of him walk right past me like I wasn't even there. "Markus no! You have to believe me!" But it was too late, he was already walking away... not caring about a word I could've said. Falling to my knees in the middle of the airport, people couldn't help by stare at me in my lowest point. No one offered to help, no one wondered what was wrong. They just walked on by, I assume just being happy that it wasn't happening to them. It was then I realized how crazy this life was. How you could work so hard on something and it could all fall apart with one accident. No one ever said that life was going to be easy. Busting my ass to the top of GCW was the most difficult thing I've ever had to do in my life. But love... love might be even harder. ---- Out of my way! Sitting on the end of her bed, she peered across the room towards the television with bloodshot red eyes swelled with tears. Her vacation to Mexico wasn't exactly what the doctor had ordered After all. Arriving at the airport to be greeted by the love of her life calling her a whore, and then an average showing at the Dual Halo. Seventeenth place might as well have been last for The Fearless Phenom, her competitive spirit didn't take any consolation prizes out of double digit number. No matter if it was out of a field of 50 or 6,000,000. RIIIIIINNNNNNGGGGGG... It had been three days of listening to the unanswered sound of ringing. She could almost hear the cell phone plastered to her ear in her sleep. During the Dual Halo she wasn't exactly on her "A" Game. Mind wandering on what Markus was doing and how this could've possibly gone so terribly wrong. For the first time in her life, Vivica J. Valentine secretly wished that there was a God... so that when she went to hell she could watch that two-faced lying bastard Mark burning for all of eternity. RIIIIIINNNNNNGGGGGG... Now she was still crying, her tear ducts rubbed raw from sleepless nights. All she wanted from Markus was a chance to explain herself. A chance to let him know the truth about everything. She was sure that he didn't have a clue about everything that happened while he was gone. He couldn't possibly know about the fire, or his reported death, or... "AAAAH! WHY WON'T YOU PICK UP THE FUCKING PHONE?!" The Fearless Phenom belted out in frustration before throwing her cell phone across the room. One of the USB port covers on the phone snapped off on impact against the wall as Vivica J. Valentine ran her hands through her blonde hair in complete disbelief. Quickly she winced in pain from the Dual Halo and grabbed her left shoulder. By all intents and purposes, she was a mess. Accompanied only be the glow of the television she felt like she'd been hit by a truck. Her mind couldn't think of anything except for how emotionally hurt she was. Stressing her out beyond her means, all this emotional anxiety had put her on edge. With a deep sigh she tried to collect her thoughts and get her life somewhat back on track. She'd already missed her flight to Tokyo for Rampage. Luckily she could catch the next one, and lord knows she needed the rest... if she could ever fall asleep. Finally she managed to power her way off of the bed onto her feet. It was time to attempt to fall asleep... again. She had a plane to catch in the morning and a life to get back to. Limping over to the television she reached down towards the power button only to notice the GCW RAMPAGE commercial on the television. Although she hardly understood a lick of Spanish, she hesitated and watched the commercial. A picture of her on the screen holding the GCW Title over her head. Looking over her shoulder she glared through her bloodshot eyes at the chair in the corner. Sitting in it was the GCW World Heavyweight Championship. She took a glance just to make sure it was real. The whole thing was still kind of surreal to her, she'd almost forgotten how great it felt to be the champion. Looking at the title she began to think to herself that maybe after getting her ass kicked from pillar to post in the Dual Halo and freefalling from a high like she'd never felt before after that bottom-feeding magazine turned the love of her life against her, just maybe, she still had something to fight for. Herself. ---- Battle Without Honor Or Humanity The corridors of the airport were jam-packed with a sea of humanity. Swarms of security held back hundreds of frenzied fans, crying out for their hero. The airport more closely resembled a rock concert than a place of business as pushing, shoving, and large signs could be seen huddled around the security station just outside the terminal. Flash bulbs went off from all around the crowd as the fans took pictures. Their medium tones of conversation turned into a roar when the flight attendant opened the door to let the passengers off the plane. "そこで彼女はそうです!" Exclaimed one teenage girl in the front row. A sea of lights and eruption of flickers shutters from the fans in attendance went off as a woman left the plane with a black cargo bag over one shoulder, and the GCW World Heavyweight Championship over the other. With a slight, almost unnoticeable limp in her step she moved forward towards security. If just for a moment all the troubles of her life seemed to go away. All the pain, all the stress, all the mental fatigue was lifted off of her shoulders with the roar of that crowd. She felt like she held the world in her hands, if just for this moment. "WE ARE THE IMAGE OF THE INVISIBLE!" The crowd began to chant as security looked over the belongings of The Fearless Phenom. Most of them didn't have a clue what it meant, but it was the passion behind it. Even if they didn't know exactly what it translated to, it meant something to each and every one of them. As did The Fearless Phenom. She was a sign of hope, even in her weakest moment and coming off a loss... she was still their hero. Still their icon. Win or lose... she would always be their champion. With great power comes great responsibility. It was something Markus used to say a lot back when they were together. Never did it have so much meaning as it did now. Of course, it strikes her while he's gone. "VIVICA!" A reporter shouted from the middle of the crowd as she walked towards them to sign a few autographs for her precious fans. "Do you have any words for Shaman tonight?" "Heh..." The Fearless Phenom reached out and signed a poster before trying to put her thoughts into words, "I've lost a lot of things in my life. I lost my home. I lost my family. I've lost gymnastics meets, matches, loved ones. Hell I've never beaten Shaman before." She paused for a moment before looking out at the crowd. "But this..." Vivica patted on her belt and the crowd cheered with approval, "I can't lose this. It means too much to too many people. This is bigger than me. If Shaman wants this he's going to have to pry it out of my cold dead hands. Even if he manages to do that... and that's a BIG if cause I have more lives than a cat sunshine... he'll have to answer to all these people. The Image Of The Invisible! Because at Rampage FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE." The crowd roars in approval and Vivica greets their cheers with a smirk. "And if Shaman thought I fell hard after Dual Halo..." Vivica finishes signing an autograph, "It's nothing compare to how hard I'm going to drop his ass in front of all these fans in the Tokyo Dome." As the hundreds of fans pushed towards the barricade, trying to pass security to get to their favorite wrestler one man watched from the back. With a pair of sunglasses covering his eyes he peered through the crowd and watched The Fearless Phenom like a hawk staking it's prey. Quickly she looked back almost like she felt herself being watched, making the man stare down at the ground and stroll away. It wasn't the right time. It was her time right now. Markus Blaire didn't want to take that away from her.
