Moving On: Part I

15 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/15/11 Aaron had experienced the biggest heartbreak and the greatest feeling of accomplishment all within one week. The woman he had fallen deeply in love with had walked out of his life. Maybe not forever but one second away from her was devastating to him. Just a few days later at GCW’s marquee event, NC-17, Aaron did what nobody expected him to do; he ended El Moreno’s winning streak and earned the right to challenge the United States Champion at any time. His emotions had pulled him from one extreme to the other like the suction vortices inside a tornado. Aaron was strong, though. He could handle it. Wednesday - 9:30 P.M. The door to Aaron’s apartment pushed open as he returned home. He flicked a switch and illuminated the interior of his home. He tossed his keys onto the counter and plopped onto the couch with a loud exhale. His friends had invited him out to celebrate his big win and Aaron obliged. They spent a couple of hours at Fatty’s, a local bar and grill but Aaron left the party early. He cited exhaustion as an excuse after two long days of squeezing internet radio show interviews into his busy school schedule. Aaron closed his eyes and sat on the couch in peace. They only remained shut momentarily as his phone startled him back to alertness. He pulled his phone from his pocket and slid it open to see a text message waiting from one of his students, Jessica. I got a forecasting job! Aaron’s heart sank and his stomach churned. His body filled with an ire he hadn’t felt in a long time. A student, still two months from graduation had found a job doing what she loved. Aaron had been forced to go back to school when numerous job applications had been denied. He replied to the text with a quick “that’s awesome” and threw the phone next to him on the couch. He sighed once more and then reached for his lap top sitting on the coffee table in front of him. He brought it out of its slumber and entered in a password. The screen filled up and Aaron went to work. He was inspired to find a job in the field of meteorology. Gone from his mind were the thoughts of stardom and fame that his wrestling career was starting to produce. Aaron loved the weather and didn’t want to turn his back on it now. Wednesday – 11:30 P.M. Aaron brought his hands up and rubbed his eyes. He had stared at the computer screen for the better part of two hours. His mood hadn’t improved either. He was still submerged in a sense of depression over his student’s fortune. He was happy for them, but at the same time wondered how he was missing the opportunities. After a moment’s pause to take a long pull from his beer Aaron’s fingers went back to work. They furiously hit the keyboard as he answered questions on application number seven. It was tedious work, but Aaron had found and applied for five job openings in the National Weather Service. Working for the government had always been his dream. The hours changed frequently as you hopped from days to evenings to midnights, but the pay was more than decent and job security was immaculate. With the hundred million dollars in cuts that were being suggested by Congress, though, finding a job there would be difficult. He filled out the applications anyway. Getting hired by the government would mean a move too. Luckily it was all to places Aaron had no problem living; Topeka, Kansas, Lincoln, Nebraska and even sunny southern California. He was now finishing up his second private sector application that was pretty local. A forecasting firm in Madison, Wisconsin had an opening that Aaron wouldn’t mind filling. Finally after another five minutes of typing he moved his mouse to the submit button and exhaled deeply. His head had recently begun pounding from looking at the computer screen. He placed the laptop on the coffee table in front of him and took the last long pull from his beer before standing up and heading for his bedroom. Thursday – Early A.M. Aaron stomped his foot on the mat as Karina Wolfenden staggered to her feet across the ring. Aaron slapped the top turnbuckle with intensity as his prey finally turned around to face him. He charged forward and lifted his boot into the pretty face of the United States Champion as she propped herself up in the corner. The crowd groaned at the impact as Fujita freed his foot from the corner and grabbed Karina’s arms in a half Tiger, half Dragon hold. Bryan: Fujita is really rolling now! He’s looking for that half and half suplex! This normally leads right into that QG-Theory piledriver! Aaron lifted Karina up and over, dumping her right on her head and neck. She landed so high that she rolled right to a standing position, out on her feet. Aaron got right back up and took his place behind her. He threw her left arm over his shoulder and lifted her into the air. As he did the building began to creak and whine unexplainably. Aaron looked at the ceiling and saw pieces begin to rip from their frame. Then suddenly, darkness.
