You Grab It!: A Tale of A Broken Man

15 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 15, 2009 “You grab it!” “Okay!” The sounds of gunfire, the tapping of buttons and utter frustration mixed with the voices of Chris Redfield and Sheva Alomar echo throughout the hallways of the Omni Royal Hotel in New Orleans, Louisiana. Screams of rage and irritation ring out from behind the closed door of Room 134, most likely stirring the people in the neighboring rooms from their own activities. “GODDAMN IT, SHEVA! Why can’t you pick up the fucking herb like you were told to?!” A loud, almost guttural grunt followed by the ‘clunk’ of heavy plastic hitting the wall resonates throughout the room, before ending with the quiet thump of the Xbox 360 controller landing on the carpet. Gritting his teeth and clenching his fists, face red with anger as the little vein in his forehead ‘pops out’, AXEL Action makes storms over to the gaming console and presses the power button. The force behind his ministration causes the machine to slide off of the table and onto the floor. “Take it easy, little brother! That cost me quite a chunk of money. I’m retired, remember? My funds aren’t unlimited like yours are.” Breathing through his teeth, the sound of the air passing through them sounding almost like a hiss each time he inhales, the younger Action shakes his head and narrows his eyes. He’s fuming, and if it were possible, steam would be pouring from his ears at this very moment. Cocking his head to the left, the vein in his forehead practically throbbing at this point, he turns his back to Ace. “Take it easy? TAKE IT EASY?! YOU try playing a video game that pits YOU with a sidekick that is controlled by incompetently programmed AI and see if YOU can stay calm!” Knowing AXEL the way he does, Ace can tell that it’s not the video game that has AXEL’s proverbial panties in a bunch. Sighing softly, he stands up from his seat on their suite’s queen-sized sofa sleeper. Ace walks over and places a hand on AXEL’s shoulder, patting him as if trying to be of some sort of consolation. “Come on.” Guiding his emotionally distraught younger brother back to the sofa sleeper, Ace sits him down and takes a seat beside him. Leaning against the back of the couch, he crosses his arms over his chest and focuses on AXEL as he nudges him with his leg, signaling for him to speak. Knowing just what that nudge means, AXEL rubs the back of his head before speaking softly. “Well, if you haven’t noticed, life hasn’t been a bed of roses for me lately. Between losing my championship to Lantlas Anduril and Dan Black, then losing my very first main event against Jay Terror, I’d say it’s been a pretty shitty month for me. I just can’t seem to catch a break, no matter what I do. Every time I manage to take a step up the ‘ladder’, someone or something knocks me back down.” Lowering his head in disgrace, AXEL props it on his hands, one hand resting against each side of said cranium. “I just don’t know what to do anymore, Ace. I’ve fought and scraped my way up from the bottom of the barrel for the last two years, and it just feels like everything is working against me.” Ace leans forward, trying to catch a glimpse of AXEL’s expression, attempting to make sense of things. He was no stranger to the things AXEL’s feeling, as he’d felt them himself in the past. Draping his arm around AXEL’s shoulders, he gives him a little shake before patting him on the back. “Come on, AXEL. Don’t be so down on yourself, you’ve given it everything you have and then some. Not many people can say that they’ve been United States champion, especially those coming from your type of background. You’ve done more in your two years in GCW than I achieved throughout my entire career. So, you’ve had a couple of setbacks, but we all have those. They’re nothing to get your knickers in a wad about.” Narrowing his eyes at Ace, glaring almost angrily, he shakes his head and stands up. Brushing the wrinkles out of his jeans and stepping into his shoes, he heads for the door. “That’s easy for you to say. At least you didn’t lose a championship twice on the same night. If you need me, I’ll be down at the bar.” Before Ace can say a word, AXEL’s already out the door and on his way to the bar. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ “What can I do you for?” AXEL sighs and smacks his lips together as he shifts his gaze to meet the bartender’s. Tilting his head a bit, his left eyebrow perks and he licks his lips to moisten them. “Give me the largest glass of Pepsi you’ve got and make it snappy.” “Pepsi? Sir, I don’t know what kind of bar you think this is, but we don’t--" Quite perturbed by the attitude of the bartender, AXEL slams his fist on the countertop which cuts the worker off, mid-sentence. Leaning towards the bartender and getting face to face with him, the Angel of Vengeance sneers. “I don’t know who you think you are, or who you think you’re talking to, but I asked for a Pepsi. I’ve got a big match tonight, not that it matters much, anyway. Do you honestly think I’m going to allow a harmful substance like alcohol into my body before I go out to compete, much less at all? I’m going to ask you one last time. Where’s my Goddamned Pepsi? If a fuckin’ kid can have one, then I can too.” Without another word, the bartender slowly backs away from the irate AXEL Action and carefully turns around. Getting a clean glass and dropping a few cubes of ice into the bottom of it, he fills the glass with Pepsi, straight from the tap. He places it on the bar in front of AXEL, giving him a frightened nod. “There you go, sir. Enjoy your drink.” “Enjoy your drink,” AXEL repeats in a mockingly sarcastic tone as he takes the first sip of his carbonated beverage. No sooner than he takes that sip, he feels a hand on his shoulder. Shrugging the hand away, he turns quickly as if to defend himself, though soon settles down once he finds that it’s his brother who’d come to join him. “What do you want? Can’t you see I’m trying to enjoy the drink that this nice man fixed for me?” says AXEL as he points to the bartender for emphasis. “If you’re here to try and patronize and pander to me again, don’t bother. I won’t hear any of it.” “That’s not what I’m here for at all. There’s just something that I think you should know.” AXEL tilts his head back and forth from left to right, his hand moving to scratch the top of his head as he wonders what Ace could have to say that is so important. Nodding, he sits down and returns to his drink, lifting a hand and waving his fingers as if telling him to spit it out. “It seems you’ve lost track of time, little brother. Worldwide starts in twenty minutes, and you need to be at the arena before then.” Shrugging his shoulders, AXEL shakes his head, taking a long swallow of his Pepsi. Chugging half of it down, he places the glass back onto the bar and turns to look at Ace. His eyes are filled with doubt, with worry, with questions. For the first time in his life, AXEL was truly questioning himself, his abilities and his self worth. “Why should I even bother? Sure, I’ve got a chance to take back my GCW United States Championship but I’ll probably fail, just like I failed to keep it in my first defense of it, just like I failed to make anything of my TV Championship reign too. I’m content to just sit here and let Dan Black keep it.” Sighing as his lips curl into a disappointed frown, Ace shakes his head. He doesn’t have a clue what to say in this situation and decides it’s better to just say nothing, because anything he says could possibly worsen things. “They’ve broken me, Ace. I’m waving the white flag, surrendering, giving up; forfeiting. Whoever has it out for me has won, and I’m not going to fight back.” Downing the last of his Pepsi, AXEL reaches into the pocket of his jeans and pulls out a couple of dollars before tossing them down onto the counter. “I’m going back to the room and I’m going to bed. If anyone calls, especially if they’re from GCW, tell them I said to go to Hell. I’ve decided I’m hanging it up and calling it quits, like I should have done a long time ago.” AXEL stands and turns to head upstairs, but Ace grabs him by the back of the shirt. Walking towards the exit, he shakes his head as he drags AXEL along behind him. “Oh, no you’re not, little brother. You’re going out there tonight and you’re going to give Dan Black the fight of his life and you’re going to win that championship back! If not for yourself and your own dignity, then for your fans; even if I have to drag you to that ring myself.” AXEL hangs his head as he’s dragged outside and to their rental car. He gives zero resistance as he’s shoved into the passenger side. Ace climbs in on the driver’s side and starts the engine before pulling out of the parking lot and heading off towards the arena, the car’s taillights disappearing into the New Orleans traffic.
