Back to basics

15 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/15/08 Las Vegas, Nevada. Under several months of road travel. The High Spirits Casino and Hotel is doing great while the owner makes his rounds around the world. Outside a crowd of people gather as a Harley Davidson rolls up and parks near the door at a reserved parking spot and two security officers step forward and stand on either side of the bike. Shaman stands up himself and looks around at the crowd oif people and nods his head as he walks into the Casino. His mind obviously on other things as he walks into the building. Making his way through the crowd of people he stops as two people are arguing over the use of a slot machine he turns and walks over to the machine and looks at the two people argue. He reaches down and unplugs the machine and pulls an out of order sticker from the back and slaps it on the screen. He speaks to both people. "Out of order." Turning he shakes his head and heads over to the elevator and enters it as the doors close he presses the hidden button which leads to the office level. He exits and walks towards the door and opens the door to see Kawanaha waiting for him with a disgusted look on her face. She speaks looking at him. "Don't you know how to call or even give us an warning of your arrival? Not that i really care since i am your number two and have to run the Casinos?" Shaman turns and looks at her and then turns as he sits down into the chair. "And your doing a good job, thank you." Kawanaha looks at Shaman and shrugs as she hands him the reports and then Shaman opens them and browses the numbers carefully. "Looks like we are on an upturn even though the country seems to be on a down swing in a possible recession. How are my properties doing?" Kawanaha hands him another stack of papers. Then speaks. "Since you purchased most of them with just cash they are in the profits gained margins. The two properties which were purchased through loans were paid off recently and the banks tried to foreclose because some rich or corporate fellow thought they could snatch them out from under you. Our law team was on top of it and a lawsuit has been filed against the banks and corporate financier who tried to take control of the loan and properties." Shaman nods as he goes over the numbers. He then frowns and looks up. "Contact GCW, Got a letter from Caldera speaking we should negotiate terms on the new PPV. Get me last years buy rates and numbers and see what we can do to boost numbers and advertisements. I have some ideas to raise more money and interests for the show here and Vegas will never be the same after this." Shaman leans back in his chair as Kawanaha hands him an envelope as Shaman opens it and grins he turns and looks at the mirrored window overlooking the floor below as he stands up and opens the envelope and reads what it says as he chuckles. Watches the people below he clasps both hands behind his back as he watches. He turns his head and looks over his shoulder at Kawanaha. "Looks like Caldera has thrown Jackson back to the wolf. Jackson and i had a throw down in our last match and trust me it was for something. Now it is just a match, not something to be impressed about." Shaman grins and reaches up and presses a button near the window and smiles as a large screen TV appears behind a wall as te wall slides apart. Shaman grins as it is the last meeting between Shaman and Jackson. "lets watch what happened at the end of that shall we? Just a little history lesson for all those who have watched the show and those who may not have seen it." ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- NC-17 2007. Shaman bounces off the ropes and charges at Jackson executing a clothesline which Jackson ducks and spins with the chair nailing Shaman as he comes off the opposite ropes. Jackson takes the larger Shaman off of his feet with the shot drawing blood from the huge Native American. Jackson quickly drops down on the unmoving bleeding form of Shaman for the count. 1.. 2.. Shaman stands a bit unsteady on wobbly legs as Jackson hold him up grinning. Jackson turns and grabs Shaman and goes to lift him up as his leg buckles. Jackson angrily lets go of Shaman getting back to his feet and then steps forward punching Shaman and sends him into the ropes and goes to execute a clothesline as Shaman ducks it and stops quickly and executes a spinning neckbreaker. Shaman just lays there unmoving as well as Jackson. Bryan: Desperation by Shaman there? Yale: No preservation by executing a finishing move quick. Bryan: Unsuspecting and deliberate. Yale: Someone has to cover now. As if being told on cue Shaman drapes an arm over Jackson and the referee turns and begins the count. 1... 2... 3... Bryan: Shaman pulls off the victory in a hard fought contest with Brad Jackson. Yale: That was a brawl for all. Bryan: Was a good bout and a bit surprising. Yale: Wait Shaman is staggering around. Shaman looks around and motions outside of the ring as a fan throws a red silk bag into the ring and Shaman grins as e looks around and grabs his title from the referee. He reaches into the bag and pulls out a Santa hat and beard and puts them on himself and looks around. He reaches down and grabs Jackson and drags him to the corner and leans him against the corner ropes. reaches into the bag and pulls out a plastic title. Camera fades out as Shaman is seen walking up the rampway as Jackson is seen sitting slumped in the ring corner breathing hard regaining his breath. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Shaman turns and walks from the window and looks around his office. Running his finger across the top of a book case and frowns as he rubs his fingers together. "Don't they even clean right anymore in this damn place? Get them to do a top to bottom cleaning fo the whole casino. I want this place sparkling completely." Shaman walks over to his desk and grabs a few napkins that were laying on th side. Wiping the book cases top down a magazine falls from the place it was tucked into. It showed Shaman with his first ever championship belt with South Coast Championship Wrestling in California in his CWC days before he joined the WWA/NCW at the time. He lifts the magazine and holds it reading the article. He then grins as he looks at what was there for articles and he browsed a few names he recognized and remembers. "Brings back memories, but this was worlds away from now. Back then i fought to gain everything and had nothing to lose. Look at now nothing to lose and fighting for everything. Makes ya wonder sometimes if it is worth it." Kawanaha quietly listens as he talks and he then shrugs and places the magazine back. Walking around his desk he sits down at the chair once again and props his feet up on the corner of the desk. "Funny how Brad Jackson gets me in his return. Wonder how his rehab went? Injuries aren't soemthing to deal with lightly. I have had to take a few months away to recover from the severe grind of the road and injuries. Funny thing is i feel fine after a huge match with Valentine in Tokyo. In fact the truth is i feel more than fine. I feel like i have a new energy. In fact an energy that which can only be fed by violence and alot of fighting. I am going to enjoy this. Hey Kawanaha call the security tell them to have my Harley fueled. I am gonna do some training i haven't done in a longtime." Kawanaha sighs and shakes her head. She also picks up the phone as she speaks. "I'll call the bars and the local sheriffs department out there and elt them know your coming. Man this is not what i wanted to do to start my week." Shaman chuckles as he exits the office and enters the elevator. Two days later, Santa Fe, New Mexico. Sitting at a Bar drinking a Beer with a guy next t him nursing a bloodied and beaten face. Shaman just drinks his beer saying nothing as the bartender walks up and hands the guy some ice wrapped in a rag. The Bartender looks at the guy and shakes his head as he speaks. "Damn it Jeb did you have to go off and spout off bullshit non sense to this guy. He is bigger than you and has a mean streak ten miles wide at least. This guy here has been coming to this Bar for years and every time he comes here he is fighting somebody. You happen to be the 7th guy in the last 24 hours." Jeb looks at Shaman through the bar rag wrapped over ice. Shaman looks at the guy and smirks. "Drink and shut up best thing to do around me. You come at me i come at you and finish it guaranteed." The bartender turns and looks at Shaman and speaks harshly. "You are going to have to simmer it down boyo. This crap is going overboard all the time." Shaman looks up from his beer mug at the Bartender. "Look Jake because you knew my dad is the reason i don't beat you here and now. Nevermind your like a dad to me and i think your old is all. Whats the use in beating an old man." Suddenly out of nowhere a bar rag snaps Shaman on the arm. "Ouch damn it Jake." Shaman stares bullet holes through the bartender. The bartender chuckled and looks at Shaman. "Good at least i got your attention. Look seriously though man. Get your ass back on track. You haven't seemed to have been yourself since your silent friend departed and came back." Shaman shrugs and shakes his head. "It is all that World of Warcraft stuff. Rots the brains sometimes. Then again i haven't played it in awhile. Might be a good thing to do. Crush little trolls and orcs. Then again i could play Guild Wars. Hell what the fuck am i talking about. You know why i come here. It is an easy place to get drunk, kick some ass, and feel better about yourself." Shaman laughs as does Jake and Jeb through the wrags. Suddenly four bikers walk in and yell at the barkeep. "Hey asshole we gonna get some beers over here or do we have to wreck the place to get it?" Shaman grins and looks at Jake. "Jake i want some whiskey pal." Shaman finishes his beer as Jake drops several shot glasses down on the bar and pours whiskey down in each of them. Shaman drinsk them down and then grabs the bottle. Drinks down a large portion of the bottle grinning as that sadistic gleam appears in his eyes. "I got some business Jake." Shaman turns and walks to the bikers towering over all of them. He grabs the first Biker and smashes him face first down into the table and then turns to the other three. He suddenly grabs one bu the nose shoving his fingers up his nostrils leading him around by the nose. He then kicks another in the balls and smashes an elbow in his face with the free arm. The man staggers and crumples backwards against the wall as the fourth and last man stands there looking dumb. Shaman looks at the two remaining guys and grins. "If you two kiss each other i guess i will forgive you threatening my bartender. Oh yeah what about you wanting to wreck my place? I think you'd better kiss one another." The biker gets angry and steps forward swinging at Shaman who pulls the other biker in the way as he gets hit straight in the face as Shaman wipes the snot off on the bikers shirt. Shaman steps around the biker he just released and knees him in the lower back and throws him out the door. He then turns to the other last standing Biker and grabs him and hoists him up into the air and drops him face first into the jukebox. Smiling Shaman walks outside and drags the bikers out one by one and grins. He looks over expecting to see Harleys and sees Hondas and Yamahas instead. He looks down at the bikers and shakes his head. "What the hell type of bikers you think you are? Oh wait i figured it out. Weekend Warriors right well i will make ya feel right at home over this one." Shaman grins turns towards the bikers and unzips his pants and pisses on them with his back to everyone else. He speaks as he is pissing. "I guess you were just more pissed on than pissed off weren't ya. Next time you feel froggy to come to the bar feel free too. I will be mroe than happy to return the favor girls." Shaman grins as he finishes and walks back into the bar as three police cruisers pull up outside and the main Sergeant walks inside and looks at Shaman. "Ok should i even ask?" Shaman stands up and shakes his head. "Jake i am going downtown for a bit." The Sergeant cuffs Shaman and leads him outside to the cruisers as the four Bikers are beginning to stir. Santa Fe, New Mexico. Police Station three hours later. Shaman is seen leaning against the bar of the jail cell as three men are laying on the floor as the guard walks up and looks at him. "Bails been posted and all charges dropped Mr. Whitecloud." Shaman stands straight and walks to the door as the guard looks down at the other three guys on the floor. "What is with them?" Shaman smiles and looks down at the other three. "Taking a nap." The guard shakes his head and shrugs leading Shaman out the door and into the lobby. As he enters the lobby Kawanaha is waiting for him there. He turns to the guard and whispers. "Couldn't you lock me up until later? I have a flight out of here to Columbus in about 6 hours." The guard chuckles and shakes his head. Shaman looks at Kawanaha. "Did not think so." Walking to Kawanaha he smiles and greets her. "Hello Kawanaha." Kawanaha looks him dead in the eyes. "Don't hello me! Disorderly conduct, Public Intoxication, Also indecent exposure. This is a new one even for you!" Shaman looks at her. Sighs and then speaks up. "Charges were dropped. Jake i am sure explained everything and they weren't going to go even further." Kawanaha huffs as she turns. "Lets go you have a plain to catch and a bars books to look at before you leave. Also in agreement your doing two hours community service while your at it." Shaman looked at Kawanaha. "What?" Kawanaha looks at him narrowing her gaze. "No arguing it was part of the deal that all charge be dropped." Shaman frowns and walks out the door with Kawanaha. He turns to her and speaks. "So where is this community service?" Kawanaha stops and points at the Laundrymat across the street. Shaman looks at her and clears his throat. "You can't be serious?" Kawanaha smiles innocently and walks to her car. "Diapers and Daycare the place next door. You get to be baby sitter for a few hours." Shaman looks at her as she gets in the car and drives off. The four Bikers are seen walking down the street as Shaman smiles at them. "Hey you i got a job for you." The Bikers are looking nervous as Shaman walks over to them. "Ok i have something i gotta do for two hours and i think you two need to go and help me for all the trouble you caused. Besides anybody that drives Honda's and Yamaha's are sissy bitches in my book anyways. Come on now this won't hurt unless you refuse." The Bikers reluctantly agree as they walk into the Daycare with Shaman. Shaman steps back outside the door whispering to hismelf. "Damn woman always making my life miserable." Turning he walks back in and closes the door.
