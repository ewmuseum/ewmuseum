Date: 9-21-05 “Son of a bitch!” Chrissy-Poo’s hand shot up and grabbed the back of his head. The GCW tour bus quaked as it ran over the pot-hole riddled road to Calgary. “Angel really planted you into the ground, didn’t he?” It was GCW referee Cameron Wrigley. He sat next to Chrissy-Poo, on the inside of the row. Chrissy liked the window seats? “Yeah, he did” That fucking dick. I didn’t even DO anything! And he totally flips out and friggin? Urange slams me onto a CONCRETE, which is hard? a CONRETE floor! Did you see the video?” “Yeah” I saw it.” “Yeah” And did you see the way the back of my head bounced off the ground all nasty and stuff?” “Yeah. It was pretty brutal.” “Yeah” He’s such a dick, man. But that was pretty bad ass, if you ask me. That same thing could have probably killed most people.” “Ha” yeah, I wouldn’t want to have that happen to me.” “Well” It’s not that I liked it? but look, I’m still alive? I’m still here? I’m still going to continue wrestling? I’m about to face The Dimensioner? Angel put me down, but not out. If anything, I’m stronger for it. After I bowed out of the Cruiserweight title tournament? I’ll admit that my self-esteem wasn’t the highest. And now? now I’m feeling a whole lot better about myself.” “Why” Because the back of your head was bleeding profusely and is currently hurting like hell?” “No” because Angel is an ass and left me with one thing?” “What’s that?” “A chance to bounce the fuck back and get even.” “Ha” like they say, “Don’t get mad, get even.”” “Oh, fuck that. I’m plenty mad” Why can’t it be, “Get mad and get even.” I like that much much better? and who the hell is “they” anyways? And who let them define our reality with their retarded slogans?” “Probably the same guys who created the “you might be a redneck” jokes.” “Yeah, most likely” let’s crap in his guacamole or skin his gerbils or something?” “Heh” I think you should concentrate on your next match.” “Hey, I’m always thinking of it” No matter what kind of shit comes out of this mouth? The match at hand is always in my mind. So I’m facing The Dimensioner? Who the hell is that?” “Well--? “Don’t answer that. It’s more like” not WHO he is? but WHAT he is? I mean? Dimensioner isn’t even a word.” “It’s not?” “Not to my knowledge. I hope his name isn’t some kind of lame spin off of the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid” If this guy tries to suplex me onto a protractor? I’m going to be pissed.” “Suplex” Not likely?” “Why’s that?” “First off” he’s a former Hardcore champion? I don’t think you’ll be seeing a lot of technical prowess from him.” “Doesn’t mean jack. Just because he’s “hardcore” doesn’t mean he can’t wrestle. To be legitimately good at “hardcore” one needs one or a combination of three things? A, they need a high threshold for pain? B, innovation with weapons and foreign objects?” “And?” “And C, a beard.” “A beard?” “Fuck yes a beard! Dude, everyone knows the best hardcore wrestlers have beards” just think of the greats and put the follicles to the face, man! It’s the truth. The only question is? does the facial hair result from the hardcore power that the wrestler processes? or does the hardcore adeptness emanate from said manly collection of filamentous epidermal projections.” “Umm” what does this have to do with Dimensioner not being able to suplex you?” “Were you not listening!” I have a beard and Dimensioner does NOT! Case closed.” “The man is seven feet tall and weighs 300 pounds?” ?” eep?” “Yeah” I’m pretty sure that he could not only suplex you? but do anything else to you he happened to want to do at the time.” “Pft! So the guy takes dumps bigger than me! Big deal, man” I have something he doesn’t have? I have something that transcends physical strength and stature? I have a very special organ that’s bigger and going to lead me to victory.” “Oh, yeah” I forgot. You’re the little guy with a lot of heart. You’re the underdog who never gives up and just keeps on fighting until your body gives out. You’re the guy that the fans love because of your determination and never-say-die attitude. You’ve got heart.” “Heart?” “Yeah. Heart.” “Heart’Are you out of your mind”! Fuck heart, man! I’ve got fucking nuts the size of Charro’s titties! I’m going to fucking coochie-coochie his ass! My balls, his chin. Got it?” “Oh God?” “MY BALLS, HIS CHIN!” “You better be quiet” I think I saw him at the front of the bus?” “Bullshit, man” Everyone knows the goth wrestlers don’t ride the god damn tour bus?” “No?” “Hell no! They just materialize at the next city. Usually in the basement or a musty broom closet. Unless the janitor lives in there, then they can also appear under a sink or some other dark place. They also prefer locker rooms” but only when the lights are off or the lightbulb is swinging back and forth and flickering “n shit.” “Ok” I got it. Can’t you take anything seriously? I mean? It’s quite possible that this guy? who’s three times your size? might just end your career? or worse.” “Be serious” Fuck no, dude. Look? There’s a reason I come out to Culture Club when they announce my name.” “Yeah” I didn’t get that at all? you’re a metal head? but then your theme music is “I’ll Tumble 4 Ya?” Even Rob Halford wouldn’t do that.” “What can I say” I’m one of a kind. I don’t think this federation realizes just what I bring to the table yet. But at World Wide? I know of two guys who are going to figure it out first-fucking-hand.” “Dimensioner and Angel?” “Have I mentioned anyone else’s name in this conversation?” “Gotcha.” “Right on.” The GCW tour bus continues on down the road Calgary? next stop? The Saddledome? Wait? no? first 7/11 for beef jerky. Then Saddledome.
