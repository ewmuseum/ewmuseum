Cry Like an Eagle: A Tribute to Crybaby Pop-Rock Bullshit

22 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-22-05 ~<>~<>~<>~ GCW Headquarters, Columbus, Ohio? “You want me to what?” Sheer and utter incredulity washed over Rich Rollins? face as he squinted through the triple-thick pane of soundproof glass. Standing alone on a simple blue screened soundstage The Renegade scowled, an expression that had taken up permanent residence across his brow in recent weeks. “Just read the teleprompter, Rich.” The tech’s voice boomed through the room in 7.1 Dolby digital, emotionless and impersonal. The tiny screen lit up once more, and it wasn’t the first time today Rollins found himself looking forward to his next paycheck. “Annihilation,” he began slowly, his voice tinged with a menace he certainly didn’t feel. “A staple event in the annals of GCW history, and an evening that promises one thing: anarchy. Devastation. Complete and total obliteration. A night where men stand face-to-face with their own mortality and must make a simple choice” destroy, or be destroyed?” Trying his best not to gnash his teeth, Rollins continued. “The culmination of some of the most heated rivalries in GCW history, Annihilation has the honor and the privilege to showcase the best talent in sports entertainment” capped off with a live performance by Hoobastank.” The teleprompter continued to roll along, but no more words were forthcoming on Rollins? end. Dead silence. “Is there a problem?” the faceless voice echoed again. “Problem?” Rollins retorted with a sour tone. “Yeah, I’d say there’s a big fucking problem.” “What’s wrong with Hoobastank?” the tech inquired impatiently. “Oh nothing, really.” The Renegade snorted. “I just liked them better the first time I heard them, when it was called Incubus.” There was motion beyond the soundproof glass, but the tinted film and dark backlighting made it impossible for Rollins to make out anything other than blotched shadows. A slow smile crept across his face as he imagined the sound technicians throwing a hissy fit behind their impenetrable wall, throwing their hands up in frustration. Finally the techs responded. “There’s no reason to dislike Hoobastank. We’ve got a laundry list of demographic polls and studies that show our core audience loves the band, and in turn Caldera Enterprises makes a profit from their likeness.” “No reason?” Rollins shot back. “How about The Reason, for Jesus” sake! Christ, they didn’t even write the damn thing. Some music major fresh out of Bellmont got contracted for that one, not to mention half of the other horseshit they shove down our throats. What exactly does Hoobastank intend to play at Annihilation, anyway? Little Too Late?” The Renegade laughed. ?The Reason,” the tech replied, and he wasn’t laughing. “Oh for fuck’s sake, why don’t you go ahead and hire that buck-toothed maple-syrup-sucking bitch who’s sleeping with Sum 41” At least she has tits.” “Avril Lavigne is being considered for NC-17-?” Rollins cut him off with conviction. “I was being sarcastic, you ignorant ass. Damnit, since when did Caldera Enterprises start suckling on the bastard nipple of popular culture?” The tech sighed over the intercom, and Rollins could literally hear his white flag waving. It made him smile inwardly. “What would you suggest, then?” was the exasperated reply offered through the intercom. The Renegade adopted a mock-thoughtful expression, staring into the obscure void of the soundproof window. “Let me think about that one. Let’s see” what’s say we toss Nickelback, Three Day’s Grace and Breaking Benjamin into a vat of boiling battery acid and broadcast it on national television. See how our demographic studies react to that one.” There was a long silence on the other end of the intercom. “We finished?” Rollins finally asked, although it really wasn’t a question. “You’re free to go,” was his instantaneous answer. Nobody had ever accused Rollins of being a team player. He flashed the window a tight-lipped grin and took his leave. ~<>~<>~<>~ As if it preparing for Trey and Hardy weren’t enough, let alone Trey and Borealis next weekend, putting up with this corporate bullshit is definitely not my style. The day I do a promotional spot for Hoobastank is the day my testicles rot off from exhaustion. Needless to say in both instances, that day isn’t tomorrow. I’m getting careless. Trey’s cute little stunt at WorldWide did more than leave me winded? it put me in the most infuriating mood that I can’t even think straight. I went off on some poor techie today for no real reason - except his atrocious taste in music - and while something like that would normally put me in a fairly good mood, all I wanted to do on my way back to the hotel was turn the nearest pedestrian into a hood ornament with my rented “03 Honda Civic. Moral of today’s story? Fuck MTV. ~Fin~ ((Note: Sorry, it's been a long week and I felt like ranting about something. A retarded radio spot caught my ear on my way home from work today, and well... you see what happens. =D ))
