Where von Doom thinks too much and thinks he's going to Die

21 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/21/2009 11/17/2009 Las Vegas, Nevada The night is darker than the black dungeon that I call my own heart. Walking down the sidewalk toward my house, I hear some noises behind me. Probably some homeless guy trying to live behind a garbage can somewhere on the street or something. Might even be one of those feral cats that have been roaming around the neighborhood the past few weeks. A slight breeze blows through the Vegas valley as I can now see my house in the distance. Sounds like a lot of loud noise coming from inside of it. My roommate Rapmaster Rick Malloy must be having another party. I might have to kick some ass and become Champion of the Neighborhood again. Hopefully that lumbering Lumpy Indian Shaman, Tatanka’s cousin, won’t be there. Too bad he’s far from home. Cleveland is a good distance away and he’ll need to be back there for spring training with the rest of the Indians in the spring time. He’s even worse than his tree-like, wooden tag team partner the Berzerker Samuelsson. However he is probably raping and pillaging a neighboring town so I wont have to worry about him. Once at my house, I casually stroll to the front door and unlock it with my key. When I finally get inside, I see the full scope of the chaos that is going on inside. My house is filled with a ton of people and most of them are guys. Very few females at the party. Just great. That makes this whole situation suck even more than a hooker. “What the hell is this, Malloy?” I ask Malloy as he stumbles over to me drunkenly. “Another party.” “Yeah,” he replies, his words slurred together. “It all started like this. First I got up and went to the bathroom. After introducing Mr. Thick Dick to Mr. Urinal Can, I saw some white powdery substance on the side of the bathroom sink counter. After that is saw bunch of balloons floating to the sky and when I looked upon the vast heavens to see where they went, a ghost appeared and told me to call everyone over here and to get drunk so that’s what I did.” The moment I hear this, my only reaction is to shake my head. “Oy vay,” I say. “I would think you were making this up if I didn’t know you as well as I do. I know that it’s true because you’re a cokehead.” At this point, he raises an eyebrow before giving me a strange look. “What’s a cokehead?” Malloy asks me. “Someone who snorts crack cocaine all the time,” I tell him. He looks a bit confused about the conversation we’re having. It’s almost as if he has amnesia or something. “What’s crack cocaine?” he asks, seemingly having no idea what the hell I’m talking about. “That stuff you snorted up in the bathroom,” I reply. “Oh…” he responds, a bit perplexed for some reason. “I thought it was pixie dust.” “PIXIE DUST?!” I exclaim in an exasperated tone of voice. “What the fuck you think this is? Peter Pan where Tinkerbell is around to sprinkle dust over everyone to make them fly higher than a hippie smoking reefer?” He thinks about it for a moment before answering the question. “Well yeah,” he replies as he starts nodding his head like Ed the Hyena from the Lion King. This seriously is the most annoying thing I’ve ever seen since the movie Paranormal Activity. What annoys me more than the fact that the feeble public is scared by simple noises in the dark is the fact that Malloy thinks this is normal. However the pungent and putrid aroma of his beer and drug stench bring me back to reality. “Oh dear lord,” I say, shaking my head as I notice my agent William Smith walking over toward us. “Hey William. Why did you let the situation get to this point?” Smith shrugs his shoulders. I also notice that he has a bottle of Bud Light in his right hand. “Because I felt like we needed a party,” he says with a grin. I’m about to pull my hair out at this point. Malloy had better be glad that there’s no knives around me because I would stab him with every single one of them right now. “NEEDED A PARTY?!” I scream out at him. “Look at this shit!” I start motioning around the living room at all of the chaos going on around us. No one seems to notice because of the alcohol and the loud music. “This looks like the mass panic scene from Independence Day right before everything went to hell. This was not a good idea at all, especially with me being stuck in a match of death this week on World Wide where I’m facing not only Zacharia Taylor but the new GCW United States Champion Dan Black.” “You’re facing Taylor and Black?” Smith asks. I nod my head. “Yeah,” I reply to him. “I’m teaming with Digital Mortality against them.” “That’s not such a bad thing then,” Smith replies. I can’t believe what I’m hearing. It seems to me like everyone around me is turning into an idiot. Maybe they were idiots to begin with and I’m just now noticing it. “It’s not a bad thing?!” I exclaim loudly. “For one thing that coward Black doesn’t want to put his belt on the line against me because he knows that I would destroy him. It would be like Megatron facing Bambi in a cage match. Yes, I am Megatron in that scenario.” “Naturally,” Malloy replies. “And then there’s Zacharia Taylor,” he replies. “A man with no penis. I think Lady Gaga stole it to have sex with some chick she picked up at a Manhattan nightclub. It was probably one of those scummy dives where the sad, pathetic, disease infested slime that make up the wrestling audience congregate to make asses of themselves. They then go to the wrestling shows to live vicariously through the wrestlers because they have no lives, no job, and no self-esteem. I hate them all as much as they hate me.” They both stare at me blankly. In a related story, they both smell worse than the description a hooker told me about how stinky AXEL Action’s vagina smelled. “That’s…nice,” Smith hesitantly responds. Just then I look over to see Count Von Macabre, my Church of the Unholy tag team partner, walking over holding a glass filled with some sort of an alcoholic beverage. It’s reddish in color. Hopefully he didn’t rob a blood bank or commit a murder of some kind. Knowing my luck, he probably murdered one of Malloy’s hookers and hid the body in the garage. “Hey von Doom,” the Count says, tipping his glass toward me. “Hey Count,” I reply to him. “When the hell did you get here?” “A few minutes ago,” he says. “I’ve been drinking a Bloody Mary over in the kitchen since I came in.” “Don’t you mean Blood?” I ask, raising an eyebrow.. The Count thinks about it a second before finally answering. “Yeah,” he responds. “I love the taste of human blood.” “Well I love the taste of human pussy,” Malloy replies, licking his lips like a molesting rapist whose just seen a half naked woman walking alone in the woods after having sex with her boyfriend in a nearby tent. “Rick, shut the hell up before I kill you right now,” I tell him as I glare at him menacingly. “I’m in the match of my GCW this week and now I’ve got to deal with this crap before the match. How do you think that makes me feel? Malloy scratches his beard for a moment as he ponders his answer before speaking up. “I have no idea,” he says to me with still seemingly no clue as to why I am so angry. “It pisses me off is what it does, Rick,” I tell him as he looks a bit shocked. “Now watch. I’m going to come out there drunker than Jake the Snake at the Heroes of Wrestling pay-per-view. This is going to be a disaster in the making. Digital Mortality is going to slash my throat with a knife. He’ll probably want to murder if I do something like that. But that’s not going to happen because I’ve brought somebody back as an insurance policy to protect my interests this week on WorldWide. In fact, I just happened to glance over and see him here at the party so we’ll be in good shape regardless of what those idiots Dan ‘Title Stealer’ Black and Zacharia ‘Murray Loves Me Long Time Every Night’ Taylor have planned for me and Digi this week.” Smith stops me before I can say anything else. “James, one question,” he says to me. “Who is your insurance policy?” I smile at Smith and then look around the chaos all around us to find the person that I am referring to. After a few moments of looking, I finally look over and see him. I nod my head at him and he walks over to us. “May I present to you C.G. Murder,” I say with an evil laugh as I motion toward him. Matt Glass walks over and high fives him. “We’re going to murder some bitches,” he says before taking a sip of his Bud Light. Malloy, Smith, and the Count seem impressed. I then look over toward the bar. “Now I need a drink before I kick both your asses,” I say before pointing at Malloy and Smith. At this point I head for the kitchen to get some alcohol. I’m going to need to keep from killing those two idiots by the end of this night. However I know that my GCW match is going to suffer because of this setback. Oh well, you only live once. * * * Two days later… State College, Pennsylvania The plane arrives in the afternoon at the airport in State College and the moment I walk into the terminal I’m hoarded by the slimy, ugly fans of GCW. All of the mindless zombies of the fan base are begging me and my buddies Rappin’ Rick Malloy, Count Von Macabre, and C.G. Murder for autographs. Rick covers his face so no one can see as the Count shoves people out of the way, Murder threatens people’s lives, and I no sell their feeble requests for autographs as we finally make it through the terminal. Once we’re out of dodge, I start filling their heads with my hate. “All of these sad, pathetic fans are making me sick to my stomach,” I tell Malloy with a look of disgust as some more of the GCW fans come up to us. “Can I have an autograph?” some big breasted woman dressed like a hooker asks me. I stop to look her up and down with lustful eyes. “So are you going to suck my dick or what?” I ask her. She suddenly gets a disgusted look on her face. “NO!” she yells out in an angry tone. “Well then what the hell are you doing over here then?” I ask as Malloy starts laughing over the whole situation. Ditching the hooker, we continue walking to get our bags from the baggage claim. However, once there, more throngs of more fans. The chick fans are just as rabid about the ‘hot wrestlers’ arriving as they are about Twilight. I bet they’re ring rats. What a joke this is. This is how half of the GCW roster got Chlamydia. However the good thing is that Dan Black and Zacharia Taylor are in that group of disease carriers. The only bad part is that they wont die before the match so I’m going to lose anyway. Shit, oh well. A guy can always dream can’t he? However the fact that Andy Murray and Vivica Valentine are having sexual relations gives me nightmares. That’s like Chyna Doll having sex with Sean Waltman but on an even worse scale. GCW fans are probably going to counseling over this entire real life scenario. After retrieving our bags, we head for the nearest exit where our hired limousine is most assuredly waiting for us. More throngs of idiots come rushing at us. This is like one of Rick Malloy’s hallucinations that he‘s always telling me about. The only difference is that it’s real and not related to all of us snorting cocaine off the breast of a hooker. However, as we get closer to the airport exit and the awaiting limousine, I start thinking about ways that I can break those two up and steal that World Title of his. With Cade Carson out of the way, I could also possibly steal the Television Title from Cheap Labor. Hell, I’m the number one contender for the Tag Titles. They might as well just hand them over to me and the Count because we’re the greatest team in sports history. But knowing my luck, none of this will happen. Instead I’ll be stuck doing what I’m doing now. Running through an airport with my minions…I mean, buddies trying to fight off idiots who want autographs. Soon after I think this, we reach the exit. Pushing our way through the doors, we quickly open up the limousine door and jump inside with our bags. Malloy is in first followed by Smith, the Count, me, and then Murder goes in last. Murder closes the door and the limousine quickly speeds off toward the hotel. Hopefully that jerkface AXEL Action isn’t there. If he’s staying at the same hotel as me and the guys, I’m going to be pissed. Hopefully he’s at the other hotel they booked some of the wrestlers to stay at. It’s the gay hotel on the other side of town. I believe it’s called the Tool Box. Wait, that’s Clyde Fox’s locker room every week. Never mind. My mistake. At this point I think that I’m thinking too much, which of course I am. This is why my plans always backfire and I end up worse off than Cobra Commander at the end of the live action G.I. Joe movie. This must not happen. Looking around at my friends, there is an eerie silence. Maybe it’s not eerie. It might just be awkward. However I feel the need to get everyone talking so I try to do so in the least awkward way possible. “So…anybody see any good porn on the internet lately?” I ask hesitantly as everyone looks at me like I’m nuts. This looks like things are going to go very well. If I don’t make it to the show, I hope GCW uses my body parts to implant talent into some of the roster. However, that’s just wishful thinking. At this point, judging bu the continued glares my friends are giving me, I think this will be my last day on Earth. Goodbye world. See you in my next life when I come back as a thong worn by Megan Fox.
