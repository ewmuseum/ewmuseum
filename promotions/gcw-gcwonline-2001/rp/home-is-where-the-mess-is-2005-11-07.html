Home is where the mess is.

7 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-7-05 Your senses are once again greeted with the small single room apartment of Jayce Bradley, though the cracked plaster and filthy floor take second seat to the mess that now litters it. The simple cot used for a bed is upturned and ripped, the pillow and blanket now strewn across the floor. Shattered on the floor, is the small T.V. the milk crate it once sat upon in pieces. The chair that had sat directly across from it overturned and broken beyond repair, white fluffs of stuffing from the seat scattered about the floor. Slowly the door comes into view and with it, the girl last seen laid out with a rather nasty laceration, now standing beside the door. Her thin, delicate hands holding a less then thin, and sure as hell less then delicate, length of the steel tubing that once supported the small cot. She's crying though, more out of anger then anything else, as tears stream down her cheeks; soaking into the bandage covered wound. Shaking, she looks up with that deer in a headlights, 'oh shit I’m fucked' look as we hear the audible click of a key sliding back a lock. The doorknob turns counterclockwise, releasing the latch. The door pulls inward, toward the camera, and... *THWAK* 'What the fuck is wrong with you!' We see Jayce standing in the doorway, his sunglasses broken on the floor, a nice sized red welt rising across his forehead as he looks down at the girl. He takes a step inside, as the girl begins trying to shove past him screaming at the top of her lungs. 'HELP ME! HELP...' quickly she’s silenced, as his hand slides up over her mouth; his other wrapping around her waist. He pulls her into the apartment behind him, shutting the door behind them with his foot. He's rather unhappy by the nasty little mess inside his simple one roomed estate, he practically drags her tiny frame to the window. With one hand behind her head, he pushes it toward the glass; peeling the bandage off with the other, showing the home stitching job on her cheek in the muddied reflection. 'You see that.' He speaks harshly, though quiet, clenching her jaw and turning her cheek toward the window. 'You can leave if you really want to go back to that. I'm not gonna stop you.' Slowly her struggles reside, as he loosens the grip and steps back. She stammered, unable to find words as her mind comprehended the scarring she had received. Soft fingers reached up, touching gently at the wound, before she winced as a small burst of pain shot through her. Her lips began to quiver as she stared mutely into her reflection. Soft, gentle eyes began to flood with tears again, as so many realizations flooded her head. In the background, Jayce crosses his arms over his chest watching her. 'I....I....I...did..didn't think he'd go...this far.' With those words the tears came once again. They flowed in rivers this time. Slowly she slid to the floor, her hands coming to hide her face as her body shook under the weight of her sobs. He knows this feeling full well. Hopelessness, being at the end of your rope, whatever it is people want to call it. It's that knowledge deep down that you know you’re standing right there at the edge and you only have two choices. Turn around or jump. Jayce jumped. Unfortunately enough, the fall didn't kill him and he had to climb back up. 'I’don't want...to go back...' She whispered, her voice choked and hoarse, dry from the sobbing. 'Then don't.' 'Why would someone do this?' her eyes turned upwards, and for that brief moment one could see the inherent innocence in those jade green orbs. 'I honestly can't answer that. I really can't. But I know where you are.' With those words he steps forward and offers his hand, which her small hand reaches for gently, before he pulls her to her feet. The scene changes, as we are taken outside to a rickety fire escape landing, the black iron rotted with rust, beaten with time. Overhead, the moon and a few stars break through the thick haze of smog that blankets the evening skyline. Both are seated on this worn pedestal, stories above the street. Each has their back rested against the worn away bricks. The girl has now changed clothes, dressed simply in black denim jeans, a white tank top and boots, her hair is pulled back away from her face. A fresh bandage rests over her cheek, a cup of coffee clenched in her hands as the steam snakes it’s way out of the cup. The story had been related now in full to the girl, from the beginning with Jayce finding her, until now. 'So you’re a professional wrestler, huh,' she smiles a bit as she asks, a tiny twinkle in her eye. 'Isn't that fake?' 'Does four broken ribs, three concussions, seventy-five stitches, and a punctured lung sound fake?' 'Heh...good point.' She smiles a bit, then turns her head downward, staring into her coffee cup. 'So...so you mean it when you say I can stay here,' she asked quietly. 'I wouldn't have brought it up if I didn’t.' 'Why are you doing this for me?' 'Let's just say I know where you are. Now get some rest, you've had a rough couple of days. Not to mention, you got some cleaning up to do when you get up.' He smiled up at her, as she crept back in through the open window. Jayce watched her for a moment, uncertain about what exactly awaited them in the future. Not exactly sure why it felt as if she somehow belonged in his.
