Shrink

31 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/31/05 'Annihilation! What a show! The fans were impressed, and I'd have to say, Warz and I have a lot to work on before we become something. It was last night, I was sleeping in my twin size bed when I heard it! My front door knocking. I got out of bed slowly, carefully, with my pillow in my left hand and the closest weapon to me...my remote control. BAM BAM BAM. I heard it again. It was last night...hours after Annihilation. Who could possibly be knocking at my door. I scanned to my clock...cant see in the dark! I make a quick mental note to myself, buy a digital clock. Then, step by step, I creep to the throbbing front door. BAM BAM BAM! Im looking at the front door, theres no peep hole! Damn I whispered to myself, well, it might have been 'shit' or 'why?' but who cared at the moment. Some nutcase panny toucher was pounding on my door at dark and early hell o'clock. Then I quickly thought to myself, or maybe I whispered, 'what if there's more than one person out there?' knowing my skills with a pillow and remote I knew I could handle one, but not two or three! BAM BAM BAM! Finally I do it! Dropping the pillow and remote control, I open the door. And all I can see is green light. Bright green light nothing more. I wonder who...I wonder how...then I wonder what! What in the hell is that smell I yell. Or did I? It smelt like my neighbor Miss Landa in a bathing suit. I slam the door! And next thing I know...Im waking up!' 'That's very interesting Mr. Cassidy! Now...you say you dont know if it was a dream or if it really happened?' 'That's correct sir!' 'Well, it could mean a couple of things. One, you could have suffered some mental damage during your match at Annihilation. Or two, you could just be out of your mind!' 'Whoa whoa whoa! I dont pay you big shot mind shrink to call me crazy!' 'First of all Blake, you dont pay me! Second of all...Im not a psycho therapist. Im just Tom, the guy that does all your important things in your life. Like, sign you up for highschool, ask your first girlfriend out for you, and even go to your first job interview...which of course didnt work out!' 'Damn you Tom! Youve been around me all my life and I still hate you! Its a shame! Who are you anyway...?' 'Uhh Blake...Im your older brother!' 'Oh yeah! Thats right! What? You think I didnt know that? I was joking...come on!' *Blank stare* 'Oh come on Tom! Of course youre my brother!' 'Yeah yeah! Whatever Blake! Anyways, you and Warz have a match this Thursday against those Guild fools.' 'Again? No sweat! Did you see me kick ass at Annihilation Tom?' 'Uhh..it looked to me like Warz did all the work! Plus...you guys lost!' 'Whoa! Whoa! WE never got pinned! Which means WE never lost! Until either my back or Warz' touches the canvas...we're undefeated! And we're gonna chalk up our first win Thursday when we kick the crap out of the Guild!' 'Alright Blake! Well, I gotta get going. Im gonna have lunch with Warz right now! I talk to you later!' 'Wait! Why wasnt I invited for lunch? I get hungry too Tom!' 'Well, thats the thing Blake. Warz invited me to lunch...not us to lunch! So...I'll see you later!' *Tom leaves Blakes apartment* ~I dont need Tom or Warz to take me to lunch! I have friends everywhere! Well do I?~ (As I sit down in my 80's couch, I hear the knock at my door again. I wish I wasnt sore...I open the door to see who else, but my landlord Mr. Beckinhager.) 'Ahh...Mr. Beckinhager! You got my rent money right?' 'You sick son of a bitch Blake! I dont know who you think you are, but you have some nerve to leave a dead cat at my porch...but then you go and light it on fire!??! It took me all morning scraping melted cat off of my porch!' ~I never did that! Its damn funny...but I didnt do it!~ 'Hey! Mr. Beckinhager! I never left anything on your porch...let alone a fiery dead cat!' 'I heard from a phone call that it was you Blake Cassidy! And dont deny it! I will not have you running rampid in this fine community!' ~Fine? Its a piece of shit!~ 'But Im telling you Mr. Beckinhager...I didnt do it! I couldnt have done it last night! I was out of town! I barely got home early in the morning!' 'And you sit here and tell me lies Blake! NO MORE! YOURE OUT OF HERE FRIDAY!' *Slams the door* ~What in the hell! Can my luck get any worse? Who the hell put a dead cat on his porch...I know! My neighborhood enemy!~ (Blake peers out of his kitchen window and sees a 10 year old boy with braces staring up at him from the alley, pointing and laughing.) ~Little Mikey Van Horn! That bastard! He's still mad at me because of my cool motorcycle! I'll have the last laugh with you Mikey!~ (Mikey runs off laughing.) ~Well...I better make some phone calls!~
