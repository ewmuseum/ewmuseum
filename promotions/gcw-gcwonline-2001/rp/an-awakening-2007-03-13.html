An Awakening

13 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/13/07 Unfamiliar ground? that’s what GCW is to him. It’s not some place he has grown to know and enjoy, instead it’s somewhere he signed on with but doesn’t honestly have a clue as to how things work. Not yet anyways, he plans on getting to know it from the inside out. Still doing everything the same due to his love for the sport and also the promise he made to his sister after she had passed on. Not many people know the full story of what happened that night of really how she died. Only him self and the police actually know all of the details and he’d like to keep it that way. His wife and child are still doing fine, although Skye has gotten a lot bigger in the past few months.. She’s growing up right before his eyes. World wide sixty three is where he’ll have his true debut in the GCW against some guy named Scott Nielson. Knowing in the back of his mind that he could beat this guy, he isn’t all that focused on what he needs to do in the ring but more so on how to break the news to his wife, Faith that he started wrestling again. ********** Slowly opening his eyes as the sun rises in the morning sky and starts to break though his sliding glass door’s blind. He lifts his head up and opens his eyes all the way now, looking to the night stand to the right of him he sees the clock. It’s only seven thirty in the morning.. Daylight savings time sucks. Groggy and still a little tired he knows he has to get up. Flipping the covers off him and into the middle of the bed he scoots to the side of the bed and stands up. Stretching briefly he looks behind him at Faith, she’s still asleep. Now walking over to the mini fridge they have in there room he opens the door. Bending a touch he reaches in and pulls out a gatorade AM. Twisting the top off he starts to drink it quickly as he kicks the fridge door shut. Now walking over to the sliding glass door he opens it slowly. Stepping outside onto there balcony he pulls the screen shut behind him. Not really worried about his neighbors seeing him in his navy blue boxers he walks over to the railing. Looking out over his three acre backyard he takes another drink of his gatorade and sets it on the railing. Looking off into the yard he begins to think about his up coming match. It's time for him to get back into form in the ring and take things into his own hands. He was put though booking hell in the former federation he was a part of and it's time for a new leaf. Letting out a quick sigh he feels the warm spring breeze hit him and it makes his hair fly back. It was so much better then the cold and the snow that they had to deal with for the last four months. Still he was unsure as to how to tell Faith that he was going to head back to the ring. Knowing that she honestly wouldn't mind to much that he was going back, the thing she'd miss was the time with him and there daughter Skye at home just relaxing and being a family. Picking up his gatorade once again he finishes what was left in the bottle and goes to turn around. Catching him off guard Faith had just stepped out onto the balcony as he was turning around. There she stood, wearing her red silk nightie and a pair of slippers. He just looked at her as her blond hair laid across her shoulders perfectly, like she had just brushed it but he knew she didn't. Smiling now she walked over to him and gave him a hug. Faith: "Good morning baby." He knew it was a good morning so far but he wasn't sure how the rest of it would go, especially after having to give her the news he was going back to what he does best. Kyle: "Morning." Giving him a quick kiss on the lips she took a step back and looked into his eyes. On her face he saw a look of concern and un-rest, she knew something was up. Faith: "Is there something on your mind?" Knowing it was now or never he quickly replied. Kyle: "Yeah, we have to talk hun." The look on her face hadn't changed, although she did smile briefly. Faith: "Well, what's up Kyle? You know you never have to be worried about telling me anything." He knew that was true but still, he didn't know how she'd respond. Kyle: "I know we've enjoyed the last four months together, me not having to travel anywhere to wrestle and us just being a family. Well.. I signed a deal with GCW and I have a match this week in Pittsburgh." Smiling at him now she took another step forward and gave him yet another hug, although she did now have a question for him. Whispering it in his ear as she hugged him. Faith: "When did you sign this?" Kyle: "About a week ago, I faxed it back to them but I have to sign it again in person at the show. I didn't know if you'd be mad about me going back." Still holding him around the neck with her arms she pulled her head back and looked at him. Faith: "I'm not mad baby. Your the best husband and father I could have ever asked for. Plus I know deep down you did this for us as well as Diana, you know we'll be watching from home." With the mention of his dead sister his face went blank, all emotion cleared it self away instantly. Letting go of his neck Faith turned around and started to walk back toward the entrance to the house. Faith: "I'm going to make some coffee and let the dog out, you want anything?" Turning his back to her now he once again leaned on the balcony railing and looked over and out at the backyard. Kyle: "I'm good for now baby." After hearing that she opened up the sliding screen and walked into the house, hearing it close he stood back up. Looking up toward the partly cloudy sky he shook his head. His come back wasn't going to be a let down to anyone as he was once again on a mission to continue on with his career and promise to Diana at the same time. The wind blew again this time harder then before but it was still warm.. as he stood there taking in the fresh air and thinking..
