Precipice

10 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/10/10 Ten months ago. Triumph Frost's 442 sits parked along a stretch of dirt alongside the majestic Pacific Coast Highway stretch of northern California. The car, parked mere feet from the cliff's edge, hangs in the background, as Alexis Frost gazes out into the sparkling blue seas. Triumph sits on the hood, smoking a cigarette, his eyes fixed on his girl. He tosses the cigarette to the ground and leaps off the hood, making his way behind her. She remains astounded by the view. He wraps his arms around her waist and pulls her in close, as she closes her eyes. "This is nice," she purrs. She brings her hand up to his face, and rubs the back of her hand across his cheek. "I could stay like this for days." "I've got something for you," he tells her. "It's in my front pocket." She laughs disgustedly. "Gross!" "Not that," he chuckles. His tone goes serious, if only for a moment. "Just trust me." Her hand goes into his left front pocket, and she fishes around. Her hand stops suddenly, and rises out, pulling out a small black box with it. She opens the box and looks inside. She gasps. She swings around to face him. He displays a rare moment of awkwardness. "So, I'm not real good at this," he fumbles. "You're my girl, and, well..." He stumbles over his words, like a nerd asking the head cheerleader out. She doesn't give him a chance to finish. "Yes!" She nearly screams, as tears stream down her eyes. "Yes..." They embrace, as the sun slowly fades into the horizon. ****** Now. The lights of an empty AT&T Park in San Francisco beat down on the baseball diamond down below. Well past one in the morning, at home plate stands Triumph, staring down a pitching machine. Sporting black Nike sport pants, an open Willie Mays Giants throwback jersey with a black Nike sleeveless training shirt underneath, black Zoom Kobe V’s, and sans batting helmet, he taps the bat onto home plate, resting it between his legs. He tightens up his batting gloves, adjusts his black wristbands, and straightens his black headband. Pulling the bat back up, he rises to his batting stance. The pitch speeds down the center of the plate. Triumph rears back and, with all his might, crushes a liner towards center field. Behind him and off to the right, Alexis, donning black John Stockton-style adidas shorts, black Chucks, and a white Black Flag t-shirt, sprawls out on a reclining chair. As usual, her and her iPhone remain inseparable. After a text or two, she puts the phone into her pocket, looking anxious. Meanwhile, Triumph awaits the next pitch, muttering to himself. "C’mon, you dirty bitch," he huffs. "Show me what you’ve got." The pitch flies in, as he takes a crack, sending it bouncing into the infield, capped off with a disgusted "shit" from Triumph. He catches her out of the corner of his eye. "What’s up?" Her arms crossed, she sits upright. "Don't we have to, you know," she scoffs. "Go?" He places the bat at his side, as a pitch whizzes over the plate mere inches away from him. He pays it no attention. "Go where?" She pouts. "Japan," she bristles. "For Rampage?" He lets out a chuckle, and goes back to his batting stance. "The fuck I care about that for?" She leans forward. "How about the Hardcore title match you potentially have!" "The Hardcore title?" A pitch comes his way, and he smacks it down the left field line. "What's that?" She laughs. "You're kidding, right?" He turns back to her, with a slight smile, and faces down the machine. "I don't really give a shit about that thing." A pitch comes his way. He takes a swing. "Besides..." The pitch goes sailing into the night sky. He watches it with indulgent grace, as it smacks off the bleachers in right-center. He swings back around towards her. "I already beat that shirtless cockbag once on my own. As far as I'm concerned..." He flashes his pearly whites once again. "That's good enough for me." She laughs. "You don't care about titles now?" Triumph exudes nonchalance, as he blasts a baseball foul down the right side. "Then what about the super-awesome Vivica J. Valentine," she beams. "She's the United States Champion, you know, and frankly, she rocks. What about her and her title?" Triumph keeps his eyes focused on the prize. "I understand the individual words you're saying," he says, as a baseball floats his way. "But I have no..." He takes a hack, and belts it up the middle. "Fucking idea what the hell you're talking about." Her indignation intensifies. "So what about your proclamation to Garbage Bag Johnny?" He tilts his head to the side. "Who?" She rolls her eyes. "You know goddamned well who he is!" She shakes her head. "He's the Commissioner of the GCW." His response: a blank stare. Her eyes go wide, and she wags her finger in his direction. "You burst into his office! You demanded title shots! You destroyed the damn place!" Triumph rubs his chin, mired in thought. "You sure?" She shakes her head repeatedly "yes". "I'm very, very sure." His eye goes narrow, his face scrunches. "I dunno," he responds. "I'm pretty sure it didn't happen like you think it did." She shrugs her shoulders. "Whatever," she tells him. "Can we go now?" He rears up in his batting stance. A baseball floats his way, and he blasts it into left field. She awaits a response that doesn't come. She huffs, making coughing noises to garner his attention, but he continues to ignore, as he hits a dribbler into the infield. Finally, she screams out. "Hey!" Still nothing. "Hey!" She hollers even louder and with amplified fury, as he starts to cringe to the sound of her voice. "Hey! Hey! Hey! Hey hey hey hey hey hey!" A pitch comes, and he swings and misses. He grunts, as he tosses the Slugger to the ground and provides her his full attention. She plays coy. "What," he grumbles. "Do...you...want?" She smirks, as she checks her nails. "I'd like to go to Tokyo now." She rubs them on her shirt, and blows on them. "Maybe see some more of your family." He stares daggers right through her. ****** Nine months ago. "You’ve got to be kidding me!" The voice of Henry Thorpe, Alexis’ father, booms out in the den room of Alexis’ parents’ Huntington Beach house. Alexis faces her father next to Triumph. She clutches his hand tightly. Henry gives them a quick once-over, and shakes his head with disgust. On his way to the bar, he pulls his tie from under his sweater vest and loosens it. Alexis remains stunned. "Dad…" "I don’t wanna hear it, Alexis," Henry booms. He can’t contain his disbelief. "You know, this type of nonsense I expect from Clinton…" Triumph lets out an inappropriate chuckle, and all attention suddenly shifts to him. He coughs and goes sheepish. Henry pours himself a drink, as Diana Thorpe, Alexis' mother, gets up from her chair. "Hank, why don't you at least hear them out." Henry chokes down the whiskey. "What's to hear?" He pours another one. "They're kids, Diana. Goddamn idiot kids that wanna throw their lives in the gutter for a feeling that'll fade before they know it." Alexis attempts to pleads her case. "Dad, at least let me--" Henry, instead, stonewalls her. "Save it, kiddo," he interrupts. "Whatever you gotta say to me." He pauses, to finish off his drink. "Save it." "You're being hard-headed, Hank," Diana barks. "This is your daughter! You can at least listen for a damn minute!" "Funny," Henry spits. "Because I think I've heard all I need to." A thick tension chokes the room of all life. Henry pours a third. "I need some air," Henry states, as he takes his drink with him through the French doors out to the porch outside. Diana and Triumph share a look, as Diana walks up to Alexis and consoles her. "C’mon, I’ll make you some tea," she tells her daughter as she brings her to the kitchen. On the porch, Henry leans over the railing and stares at his backyard garden. Triumph sidles up next to him. Henry spots him out of the corner of his eye, and grunts. "I like what you’ve done with the place," Triumph says. "Kid," Henry blurts out. "Cut the bullshit and say your piece." Triumph nods. "Family's really important to Alexis. It always has been." He laughs. "I can't understand that shit for the life of me, but she cares, and I'm cool with that. I mean..." Henry isn't buying into it. "Is a point coming soon, son?" Triumph snorts, and faces Henry. "I didn't come here for your approval," he remarks. "Frankly, I couldn't give a shit whether you even care or not. I'm gonna marry your daughter whether you like it or not." "Is that so?" Henry sarcastically chides. "Yeah, you old fuck," Triumph hollers. "For her, I'd spit right in the devil's face. I'd go to hell and back, and then go back again. I'd fight the reanimated corpse of Saddam Hussein to the death. I'd..." Henry stops Triumph with a grab of his shoulder. "I get it, kid." Triumph brushes Henry's hand away. "I'd do whatever I had to. She wants you at the wedding. Wants you to give her away. You wanna come? Fine. You wanna disappoint her? Whatever. I've already experienced enough of that for both our lifetimes. But..." Triumph suddenly towers over Henry, the ominous shadow flowing over Alexis' dad. "If you think you're going to come to the wedding to stop us..." Triumph gets right in Henry's face, as sweat starts to pour off of Henry's brow. "I will shove your teeth so far down your throat, you'll be shitting calcium for the rest of your fuckin' life." Henry wipes away the beads of sweat, as Triumph backs off. Henry takes a sip of the drinks, and takes it all in. Suddenly, he's laughing, a response that Triumph wasn't expecting. Henry lets out a deep sigh and rests along the railing. "You know, kid," Henry says. "If I talked to Diana's dad the way you just talked to me, he'd have taken off his belt and beat the merciless ever-lovin' shit outta me." Triumph rests along the railing next to Henry. "Well," he responds. "I'm a different kinda dude." "That you are," Henry agrees. A pause. "When Alexis was five, we had this dog. Brownie, a brown...something or other, I can never remember that stuff." Henry clears his throat. "Anyways, I was out of town on business. Brownie was a rambunctious dog. And dumb as rocks. So one day, she ran out from the house into the street, and just like that..." Henry smacks the railing for emphasis. "One pickup truck later, and that was that." "The driver buried the dog, on account his dog was killed in a hit-and-run." Henry soaks in the moment. "But Jesus, did Alexis cry that day. She cried and cried. I told her on the phone it'd be okay, but still..." He laughs. "Funny, how you always remember things like this." He brings himself back into the moment. "After that, she kept going on and on about how she wanted to be a vet, save the poor little dogs, cats, and the rest." "Two years ago," Henry says. "She was looking at fashion schools to attend." Triumph listens the whole time with a silent interest. "The point I'm trying to make, son," Henry continues. "Is that you're too damn young to know what you want. Being young is about making small mistakes, not big ones." Henry smiles. "The big ones are for when you've already pissed it all away." Triumph soaks in Henry's words. Another pause. "This isn't going to change anything," Triumph softly says. Henry nods. "I know. But at least I tried." A final pause. "You know," Triumph tells him. "I've got money." Henry takes a sip from the drink. "Like, Bill Gates money." Henry nearly chokes on his drink, as all of his attention focuses on Triumph, who's all smiles. ****** Now. Triumph stares daggers right through her. She gives a knowing smile, as suddenly, he lets out a guffaw. The guffaw gives way to chuckles. His chuckling suddenly turn to full-blown laughter, as her smile melts into a bit of a frown. He clicks his tongue. "Ain't nobody wants to see me there." He walks over, and picks up the bat. He shoulders it. "Just like ain't nobody I wanna see there, either." "You wanna talk about it," she pries, like a small child asking her dad to explain life. Triumph sighs. "If I tell you, will you quit fuckin’ askin’ me about it all the time?" The response a bit unexpected, Alexis poorly holds back her excitement over Triumph’s moment of openness. "When dad died, he left the controlling interests in IwaCorp to mom and me," he explains, as he readjusts to his batting stance. "After that happened, everyone even with a speck of matching DNA came out the woodwork, armed with slick-talking lawyers and greedy hands extended at the ready." The machine rears up to throw a ball his way. "The blood was in the water, and the sharks circled just as fast." The ball comes flying his way, as he picks his right foot upward. "So for years, mom had to deal with nothin' but..." The fastball rolls towards the plate, as he kicks his foot forward, full swing in motion. The ball lets out a smack as it hits the wood. "A bunch of assholes for what seemed like forever." The ball towers past the outfield, into the leftfield seats. He places the bat at his side, and adjusts his gloves. "When mom died, I got everything." He picks the bat back up. "And before the ink was dry on her death certificate, I decided to give the lot of 'em a nice 'fuck you' to remember me by. Sold everything I had in the place to whomever wanted it." He takes a step back from the plate. "Namely, anyone but them." "Hence," he grins. "They don't like me very much." A pause fills the night air. Alexis suddenly turns the mood awkward. "Kenji came and talked to me a few weeks ago." Triumph stops cold, if only for a second. He refocuses on the machine. "I figured," he mutters. "He said some things," she tells him. "A few things you don't kn--" Triumph drops the bat and confronts her. "Whatever he told you," his voice raises. He rubs his forehead and face. He closes his eyes, and takes in a deep breath. As he exhales, his eyes open. "Forget it." She watches him intently. He grows somber. "The man's a fuckin' plague." He walks back to home plate. "Just...ignore it, okay?" She nods her head, as he returns to his batting stance. He focuses on the ball. "You know," he ponders aloud. "I really like that Brit cat." The ball careens down home plate. "He’s good folk." Triumph takes his swing, and sends it screaming over the right field wall, right into McCovey Cove. He takes a step back, and revels in the moment. ****** Seven months ago. In a Sydney hotel room overlooking the majestic Pacific, Alexis stands in front of a mirror, flanked by her sisters Kim and Sarah, and her mother Diana. They primp her hair and apply makeup, the catty chatter of women free-flowing about. Kim swaths a brush in Alexis’ face, as she suddenly waves it away. "Will you stop, Kimmy?" She rubs at her face, leaving Kim aghast. Kim puts her hands to her sides. "Now I have to start all over again." "I’m fine," Alexis maintains. "Fine," Kim huffs. She suddenly mutters under her breath. "Don’t blame me if you don’t’ wanna be gorgeous." An aghast Alexis fumes, as Diana steps in. "Hush up now, will the two of you?" Diana stands in front of Alexis, brushing her hair out of her face. "All done." Diana steps out of the way, as Alexis finally gets a gander at their work. She’s speechless. The sisters and the mother beam at their work, as Alexis marvels. Her modest white dress shines in the fading sunlight. "Oh my…" The waterworks start up, as the girls rush in. "Oh, no you don’t," Sarah kicks in, dabbing away with a handkerchief. "You’re not gonna do that, honey. Not today." "You better believe I am," Alexis responds, as they all share a laugh. "Oh, hush," Diana reassures her daughter. The hotel door swings open, as there stands Triumph in a suit, his tie hanging around his neck, a bottle of Sierra Nevada in his hand. All the girls look his way, as he's all smiles. "You mind if I talk to my wife?" The girls look at one another one last time, as Sarah and Kim make their way past Triumph. "You look beautiful, honey," Diana says. She kisses her daughter on the cheek, and follows her other daughters out of the room. Triumph remains in the frame of the door, as Alexis looks at him through the mirror. He sips from the bottle. "I say goddamn. Goddamn, goddamn." "You're drunk," she sneers. "Not yet," he retorts. "Not today." He walks towards her, putting the bottle down on a dresser. Behind her, he breathes down her neck. Her breaths become soft. "Are we ready for this?" He whispers into her ear. "Not even close." She rests the back of her head in his chest. "You having second thoughts?" He wraps his arms around her waist. "Every single second." She grabs his wrists tightly. "Me too." He kisses her on the neck. "You wanna skip it? Go home?" She sways her head about. "What about everyone here?" He kisses her shoulder. "Fuck 'em. Who cares." "No," she responds. "I want to do this." He nods. The two lovers' eyes meet in the mirror. He blinks. "We should...get out there." "Can we just stay here?" She clutches his wrists tighter, as she closes her eyes. "Just...a little bit longer." Triumph kisses her cheek, and holds on tight. They remain there. Just a little bit longer.
