Date: Maybe hours, maybe days later The first thing she noticed was the sharp pain in the back of her neck. Something was digging in, hard, and it had been for some time. Already she could feel the stiffness growing, were she to stay in this position much longer, she wouldn’t be able to turn her neck for days. Shifting her position, her arms began to tingle. They were tied to something, up above her head, they’d fallen asleep, but the movement had woken them at least a bit. Soon she’d get that stinging pins and needles sensation. In a wave it all came rushing back. She didn’t see them, but there had to have been more than one. She’d first heard a thud as Lance hit the ground. Alex screamed for her to hide, as she came out of the bathroom. Panic had taken over she’d hidden under the bed, she’d seen a pair of huge feet enter the room. A searing pain as she was dragged out by the hair. She’d been too scared to open her eyes, a hand clamped over her mouth to prevent her from screaming. Next thing she knew, everything had gone black, she must have fainted. Something hot and bright hung too close to her face. Barely opening one eye she was blinded, quickly closing her eye and turning her head away. Her entire body flared with pain, and she realized how tightly she was bonded. Her arms pulled up over her head, so that it pulled the muscles in her stomach just to shift. She would’ve tried to stand to relieve the pressure, but her legs were lashed to the legs of the chair. In that instant all the panic came back, she couldn’t remember when she’d ever been this scared before. “MICHAEL, LANCE, ALEX! SOMEONE HELP!” “Shut up.” The voice was a low rumble. Quiet, but it held a darkness that caused her to freeze. So dispassionate, so cold and calculating it chilled her to the bone. Her mouth dried and even if she wanted to she knew she couldn’t scream again. There was promise in that voice, he didn’t even have to say it, she knew he’d hurt her or worse if she yelled again. She thought she might cry soon if she had to stay like this much longer, but she was too scared to even do that. If this guy was completely crazy that might set him off as easily as a scream would. Deciding she’d try again, she cracked her eye open. She was no longer staring directly into the light, so it wasn’t so blinding. A single naked light bulb hung from the ceiling, it was so bright she really couldn’t see anything beyond it. From what she could tell the room was fairly bare, kind of dingy and dirty, like an old run down apartment maybe. Barely she could make out a figure sitting just outside the ring of light. He was huge but she couldn’t make out any of his features. She tried to calm herself down and think rationally. There had to be someway out of this, but she couldn’t think of anything. She could feel the stiffness settling into her joint, strangely she thought about how worried she’d been about her neck when she’d first awoke. Perhaps she could appeal to the man’s humanity, it’d be a long shot, but it could work. Only problem is she’d have to talk, and that just didn’t seem like a good idea. However, she was starting to cramp badly, and if she didn’t get to a bathroom soon, she might wet herself. She didn’t have much choice, she had to try something. “Please, I need to go to the bathroom.” She spoke softly, almost hoping he wouldn’t hear. If he did, he didn’t care enough to actually respond. Not even a grunt, he just continued sitting. That was the worst thing, since his first words he hadn’t moved, hadn’t spoke. His stony silence, and absolute stillness unnerved her even more than the situation itself. Obviously he wasn’t going to be swayed by something as simple as a bathroom break. Kidnappers wanted money right? That’s the reason they took people, surely Mike would pay to get her back. Of course they’d had their problems, he still cared enough to pay off these people. At least she hoped so, they’d never exactly spoken about kidnapping before, so she wasn’t sure his thoughts on the whole thing. It wasn’t like the subject came up often. He could be bull headed sometimes though even if he did care about her, he might be the type who wouldn’t negotiate with these types. She’d heard that many rich families refused to deal with kidnappers. They thought it was just lead to more of the same, but Michael wouldn’t be like that he couldn’t be. At least Sheena hoped so. “Please, I’ll give you anything you want, I know people, people with money. They’ll pay to get me back, please.” “I said, shut up.” Again that quiet rumbling voice. This time however, a bit of an edge, seemed like he was starting to get annoyed. That wasn’t a good sign, the last thing she wanted to do was piss a man off who could do her such a great deal of harm. She was at a loss there was nothing she could possibly say or do, maybe she’d just have to wait. Sheena couldn’t imagine anything worse. Even if she were being beaten, raped, murdered, at least she’d know what was going on, what was happening. Being here, tied, completely helpless, she didn’t know her ultimate fate. Couldn’t fight against it, just dread what might happen in the future. The room was deathly silent, she couldn’t even hear the big man breathe. Again she tried to move, and more pain flared through her body. She began to tremble, the fear was starting to make her feel queasy. She’d be sick soon, hopefully that wouldn’t push this guy over the edge. Desperately she tried to breathe slowly, calm her stomach, it wasn’t working. Her chest felt tight, as if her heart was trying to push it’s way out of her chest. Suddenly a noise, the sound of a knock on the door. She let out a brief squeal of fear, but quieted herself immediately. Slowly the figure from across the room rose to his feet, and turned. The sounds of his footsteps receding just a bit. He’d gone into another room, and once there the sound of a door opening on rusty hinges. She could hear them, speaking quietly. The words she couldn’t make out, but just the thought of what they might be saying formed a cold knot in her stomach. This was it, his partner had shown up, and now they were going to kill her. They’d probably have their way with her first, that thought sent a strange tingle through her body. Despite the fear, all this was a little exciting, she flushed with the thought, and immediately flushed again with shame. She truly had a problem that something like this could excite her. Two sets of footsteps came back into the room. She still couldn’t see anything beyond the light really. Although it was clear there was two of them, the second one was quite a bit smaller than the first. The larger one hung back, as the smaller continued forward. Coming into the light, her breath caught in her throat. It couldn’t be, not him. Visions of what she’d seen him do to people danced through her head, the most vicious evil person she’d ever had to come into contact with. And he had her, but still in the back of her mind she thought how sexy he was, and how she’d never noticed before. Stepping up within inches of her, the GCW World Champion Lestatt Knight knelt down. His face a hair’s breadth away from hers. His breath was hot against her cheek, and her whole body quivered. The fear coursing through her body was intoxicating, she couldn’t speak fear and excitement choking her. “I can taste your fear’I wonder if your flesh will be as sweet?” His voice spoke volumes, dark, sensual all at the same time. He’d put her through torments she couldn’t even imagine. Pain would mix into some twisted pleasure that only he could fully appreciate. In this moment, in this time, she was his completely. He already owned her body, he’d made sure of that in bringing her here. And now with everything mixing together, she’d given herself over to her darkest desires ones she didn’t even know she possessed. Closing her eyes, she let out a whimper of tear maybe that’d excite him the same way he’d excited her. Maybe it wouldn’t hurt’too much. ~To Be Continued~
