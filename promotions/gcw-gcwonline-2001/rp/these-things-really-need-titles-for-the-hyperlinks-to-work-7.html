Date: Timeless The office is darkened except for a shadowed figure sitting in front of a computer monitor. This figure is seemingly engaged in some sort of dangerous combat by his remarks, He looks to the left and grabs a can of soda or something taking a drink he speaks. “AFK a second guys I need a drink. Yes I know I am the tank.” Stopping talking a second Shaman is obviously seen as he drinks some more of his coke. “Wait a second man you know you are the twinked out mage. Come on cqan’t you defend yourself against a big boss” Oh come on you get owned in the BG everytime.” Shaman seems to be grinning. He then types something on the screen as he continues to move his character around. “I owned you last night with the new Horde character I ran up to 60. Yeah I know that new character is like a year old, But still he took you down like a little girl on a playground in a dress.” Shaman grins and looks through his inventory list and then speaks over the headset again. “Hey Mikey I need some pots man. Want to make me some” Yeah I have the mats for it. I don’t just sit here and gather herbs for my health. I am a blacksmith and Armor smith. No don’t keep asking me if I can make leather stuff. That would be the dwarf and I am not on as him.” Shaman presses a series of buttons in succession. He groans suddenly as Suzy turns on the light and shakes her head looking at him. She walks over to him carrying a drink and places it down and kisses him on the cheek as she looks at the game. “Made Level 59 I see.” Shaman looks up at Suzy and smiles. “Yeah even on the peninsula grinding away to 70.” Suzy Looks at Shaman and grins. “That is right with the new expansion you can grind to Level 70 now. I almost forgot. Heard the new weapons and armor are a lot stronger than the old stuff.” Shaman grins and looks at Suzy again and quickly looks at the screen as his character is fighting some sort of orc like creature. He taps the keyboard and uses a potion. “Yeah the quest rewards are a lot better too. You get a lot more gold and pluses at the end of quests here. Even the Hold where the good guys keep is better. Just a bit crowded it seems though. That part is a headache, but I think I can deal with it.” Suzy picks up the game box and looks at it. “So people actually paid 70 plus dollars for the box set with a map and a little pet that does nothing” That is kind of lame. They should of included a new mount or something. I bought the 40 dollar box and am having just as much fun as them. How is the guild going Shaman?” Looking up at Suzy he shrugs and shakes his head. “They all run to better guilds and don’t really want to play for fun anymore. They all want raiding guilds or some sort of super uber guild. Some guys promised to stay aboard ad see it through, but they forgot where they were at and why the guild was formed and left kind of betraying the trust people had in them.” Suzy nods and shakes her head. “They tend to do that in a game.” Shaman nods and looks over at Suzy as he speaks through his mic. “Hey guys I am going to finish this quest and bail. Yeah yeah I know. Maybe I will make it back.” Shaman pokes the keys as he logs off. He turns and looks over at Suzy. “I have a match this week don’t i” Guy named Sero Blake or something? In front of his hometown fans too right. Columbus, Ohio or something? Bah not a big deal anyways get me the file on him Suzy I need to read up a bit.” Suzy grins and walks over to him and hands him the file as she grins. “I knew your were going to ask for that file.” Shaman stands up and presses a button as a film pulls back from over the glass showing the casino floor below. He looks down and eyes the floor and what is going on. Looking around he seems satisfied with what’s going on below he turns and opens the file and reads it. “So Sero a new guys gets hired and gets a shot at me right away. That works for me. The new guy gets a big taste of his opponent. I’m like those odds. He would be an unknown factor. He seems to be a very talented individual. I say he is good at what he does then.” Shaman walks around the desk and sits back down closing the file and looks at the computer screen. He grins and looks at Suzy. “Suzy no worries about this. I will give Sero a good fight. The kid deserves it. He probably has trained hard to get into this match with me. The upper management guys have seen this and given him that opportunity. I hope he ahs done his homework and prepared for a big fight.” Shaman places the file on the desk and gins again as he simply looks at the file as he turns and then begins staring at Suzy. “Lucky for me I have you Suzy.” Suzy smiles as she looks at Shaman and sits down looking at him showing some leg. He grins and they both chuckle as the camera fades.
