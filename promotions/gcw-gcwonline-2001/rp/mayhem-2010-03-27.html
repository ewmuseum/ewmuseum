MAYHEM~!

27 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/27/10 I. Absolute darkness This is what has surrounded LeStatt for far more years than he has any desire to count. He was once a great ruler that demanded fear and respect. His death was untimely and very unfortunate. Giving up his throne was not something that he looked forward to; in fact he spent years of his life in search of a way to give himself everlasting life. Immortality was always the ultimate goal. Accomplishing that goal however became increasingly difficult for the Pharaoh and he lost many lives and many years in this quest. There is no way for one’s mortal life to last forever; that much he learned. However, once you pass on there are ways to come back and if you do? The power possessed can be unimaginable. LeStatt believed that his people loved him enough to do everything in their power to find the means to bring him back from the underworld. He never imagined he’d still be waiting to be unleashed. With each passing day his anger and his hatred grow stronger, come closer to completely consuming his soul. Finally, it seems that his wish is close to being granted. His people have come for the great resurrection and to ensure that LeStatt can once again lead them to greatness. He can hear the voices from the other side beckoning him to come forward. They want him to slice through the darkness and finally take hold of the world. He can feel the life force trickling back into his body. But this is not the same life as before. This is something new, something powerful, something different. LeStatt can feel the power of this new life force invading his body, changing him into something more than a man. And he likes it. With each passing second, LeStatt finds himself craving it. The power is so intoxicating, so strong that it makes his mind swirl. Confusion sets in, followed by panic. What if he cannot control it? What if he buckles under the pressure? It feels like his soul is on fire. Hell’s inferno burns into him. It becomes unbearable, the pain, the heat, the confusion. He just wants to die again, stay in the blackness. But he isn’t allowed. Suddenly he is immersed in water. It cools him, calms his thoughts and his body. His chest heaves up and down, his muscles ache and his head hurts. He slowly opens his eyes. Now he realizes something very important … He’s back The aches subside, his breathing steadies and his mind clears. LeStatt stands up from his kneeling position and looks to his surroundings, using his eyes for the first time in years. He looks down at his hands and watches as he moves each finger one by one. His amazement in life is distracted by one strong feeling. “I’m not alone.” Quickly he turns and comes face to face with the person who brought him back from the dead. A cowardly looking man reeking of urine and looking deathly afraid. There are others as well, all of them there for a particular reason. All of them unknown to LeStatt, except one. “Murr…” II. Neither Jamison nor Fawkes waited around too long to find out exactly what a resurrected LeStatt Knight was capable of. Jamison and Fawkes sprint until their lungs hurt and their bodies force them to stop. Jamison goes down, hands on his knees and breathing very erratic. When Fawkes stumbles to him a few moments later, Jamison rolls his eyes. “What are you doing? Find your own way.” “Nu-uh, can’t do that boss. Fawkes is scared!” “You should be! Did you see that thing you woke up?” Jamison knew exactly what got brought back to life because he had come to do the same but for more personal and greedy reasons. Jamison stands up straight and looks around. The two have ended up in a dark, narrow corridor with a few scattered torches hanging from the walls. Jamison pulls the closest light off the wall and sticks it in front of him to attempt to lighten up the darkness. It doesn’t help a lot; the path is still very shadowy. “Damn it, this shouldn’t be happening.” Suddenly a powerful gust of wind ravages the corridor, taking all the torches out with the exception of the one Jamison is carrying. Fawkes shrieks and Jamison tightens up, his nerves getting the better of him. Jamison’s eyes look up at the flame and he notices exactly how shaky it is because of his trembling hand. He grits his teeth and grabs a hold of his wrist with his free hand in an attempt to keep things steady. “Let’s go.” Jamison takes a half step forward but Fawkes is so nervous and close to Jamison that when he takes a full step he steps on Jamison’s heel. Jamison’s head turns quickly and he glares down at his new sidekick. Fawkes’ eyes widen and he gives a sheepish smile. “Sowie.” “I could’ve done this-AAAAH!” “GAAAAH!” They are both taken off their feet and thrown into a wall with tremendous force, causing Jamison to drop his torch, the last bit of light in the corridor. Jamison and Fawkes aren’t dropped though, their bodies instead being held up by an invisible force. From the opposite end of the long corridor there is movement in the shadows that catches both Fawkes’ and Jamison’s attention. “Please, no.” In the distance, LeStatt’s face can be seen cutting through the darkness to reveal all but he quickly disappears behind the curtain of blackness. A sinister laugh echoes off the walls and finds its way to the duo’s ears, sending a chill down their spines. Then their breath is stolen from them when pair of glowing red eyes pop up in the darkness. Then a second set and a third … and finally a fourth. “What the hell is that?” The response is a series of growls. Out walks four hungry looking black dogs. “Oh no.” Fawkes cries out before urinating on himself a second time. “Get me out of here.” The sleek looking predators walk slowly towards their helpless prey, eyes narrowed, teeth bared and drool slipping from their mouths. With each step the prey loses hope but also becomes more frantic. They try to break the supernatural hold on them but it’s a useless ordeal. They realize this once the dogs get close enough to lick. Their bodies drop to the floor with a thud. Released from their restraints, a brief flash of hope creeps in. That crazy feeling that they may survive finds a place in the front of their minds. It’s a fleeting moment. They are surrounded immediately by the hungry predators. The last bit of light from the torch extinguishes, leaving only the glow of these animal’s demonic eyes as light. The two men begin to sob. The dogs growl. And they savor their meal. III. “We have to help each other, it’s the only way.” “Sorry but I have a job to do, Pharaoh.” The two stare into each other’s eyes, one defiant as always and the other hoping that the severity of the situation settles in. Murr searches for any hint of backing down in Julian but quickly realizes that it is a lost cause. The Pharaoh takes in a deep breath and shakes his head as he lets the air back out of his lungs. He walks over to a statue in the tomb. “You cannot be serious.” Murr asks. “Deadly.” She says quietly while tightening her grip on her bow. “Fine.” Forcefully Murr pulls a sword that was used as decoration on the statue and moves right into a fighting stance. “Take your best shot.” “You think your sword is faster than my arrow?” “We’ll see.” Julian lifts the bow, arrow loaded and ready to fire. She pulls back and prepares to let it fire at a moment’s notice. Sweat pours down the faces of both combatants. An insane whirlwind of emotion fills their bodies. “Why can’t you see the danger we are in?” Murr asks, still trying to get through to Julian. “I know exactly what is at stake.” She releases, the arrow zinging towards Murr. He is almost caught off guard but manages to spin away and dodge the arrow. Julian is so quick with her bow however that she yanks two more from the holster on her back and load them up, firing them off right for Murr’s body. The Pharaoh drops to one knee and swipes with his sword, deflecting both arrows. Murr stands again. “Let us settle this another time, Julian.” “NO!” She rips another arrow out. “I didn’t come here for the power of some evil spirit. I came here for YOU.” Julian drops her bow and fills her empty hand with a second arrow and breaks both of them in half, basically creating two daggers. She sprints at Murr who readies himself for battle. Julian slashes with her right hand and then her left but Murr dodges both. She lunges forward while spreading her arms outwards, attempting to slice Murr’s throat open but the blades narrowly miss their mark because of The Pharaoh’s bending at the waist and pulling his upper body back. This missed strike leaves Julian’s body wide open and Murr takes advantage by forming a fist and driving his free hand straight into Julian’s stomach. The blow forces a loud grunt from Julian’s throat and causes her to stumble back. Murr dives forward, thrusting his sword but Julian recovers in time to spin away and simultaneously bring her right hand down across Murr’s forearm, gashing it open. The blood flow is immediate as is Murr dropping the sword. Julian thrusts with her left hand but Murr catches her by the wrist and twists so her arm is in a very awkward position. He winds up his free hand and brings it smashing down, first on her shoulder and then to her elbow, breaking numerous bones in the process. Murr releases her wrist but only to deliver a spinning back fist that drops Julian to the sandy ground. Murr takes his boot and crushes Julian’s wrist to hold her in place. “You just had to do this didn’t you?” Murr shakes his head. “You couldn’t just let it be.” “Never.” “Then I’ll see you in the Underworld.” So Murr finishes it, crushing Julian’s skull under his massive foot. All is quiet … Momentarily The entire tomb quivers, the sand begins to whip around. BOOOOM! LeStatt Knight is back. IV. LeStatt Knight and The Great Pharaoh, Murr stand just paces apart. Cautiously Murr bends down to pick up his sword in one hand and one of Julian’s broken arrows in the other. Knight grins from beneath his mask of bandages at the action, feeling very confident that it is futile. Murr considers what an epic moment this truly is, sure he is terrified but he recognizes that all that is standing in the way of LeStatt Knight taking over the entire world is HIM, the Pharaoh. The two have a tremendous history together; their lives will be forever intertwined in the pages of history. There are a million things Murr could say, that he wants to say. He throws the arrow head instead, catching LeStatt off guard, the tip piercing Knight’s knee. The undead Pharaoh howls in pain and falls to one knee. Murr advances and leaps into the air, sword overhead. Murr attempts the killing stroke, driving a blade through his head, but LeStatt hesitates only for a moment in retaliation. Knight points to his left and with a wave of his hand, a boulder soars through the air and crashes into Murr’s body. The Pharaoh collapses to the ground in a violent manner. “Clever boy.” Knight says out loud while pulling the arrow from his knee and tossing it to the side. “You had a chance.” LeStatt stands and immediately brings his hands upwards which also cause Murr’s body to go airborne as well. Knight brings his hands down fast and this pushes Murr down into the ground. Knight repeats this movement twice more before holding Murr up in the air again but this time he shoves his arms out in front of him and this forces Murr through the air until he splats himself across a golden statue of Anubis. “The power is amazing Murr.” LeStatt stalks the Pharaoh but is impressed when he sees that Murr is already trying to get back up to his feet. Another feeling also grows inside of him, excitement. LeStatt did not want this to be so easy, he loves a battle and he feeds off of challenges. “You’re a coward!” Murr screams at the top of his lung. “Coward!” The words freeze LeStatt and cause his head to slightly cock to one side like a confused dog. Knight raises his hand to pick Murr up from the rubble and pin him up against the Anubis statue. “I’m a coward?” Knight laughs. “How so?” “I’m just a man, the only man here to stop you. I’m also then man who put you in this box! Yet you can only fight me with these powers … you’re a coward!” Even The Pharaoh knows that this plan is a last gasp effort. Murr knows he is going to die in moments if he does not do something to change the situation. It’s a classic maneuver; tear at your enemy’s ego until h does something foolish … like give up his greatest advantage. LeStatt knows what the goal is as well. He was the greatest ruler Egypt had ever seen at one point; he was considered a master of war. Even knowing all these things however … “You’re right.” Knight drops his hand which releases his hold on Murr. “Killing you like this would be pathetic.” Knight turns his back on Murr and attempts to collect his thoughts, perhaps figure out a new plan. Murr can’t believe it worked! Quickly he scrambles to his feet and dives for his sword and in one smooth motion he rolls through, coming up on one knee and throws the sword directly at the back of Knight’s head. The blade penetrates deeply. But not into LeStatt Murr watches in amazement as Knight disappears into thin air. Sensing trouble, Murr spins around and is met by LeStatt’s right hand hammering him in the jaw. The blow is fierce, unlike any that Murr had felt before. Knight follows the attack by wrapping his hand around Murr’s throat and lifting him off his feet. “Do you really think I’m a fool?” Knight laughs a sinister laugh. “You truly believe I’d give this all up that easily?” “You ARE a coward, killing me like this …” “But I will not kill you Pharaoh.” A wicked smile spreads across his face. “I am going to give you the same treatment I received when my rule was up.” Knight spins and lifts Murr up high and whips his body down ferociously inside of his former resting place. “You’ll sleep well.” It isn’t until this moment that Murr realizes where he is. He fights, screams and begs but none of it helps. Using his powers, LeStatt closes the lid on top of him, muffling the cries for help. “Perhaps one day, you’ll be brought back too.” LeStatt Knight looks around the tomb, admiring its beauty. Most people in his situation are meant to embrace moving over to the other side. Death is an honor! LeStatt however, doesn’t see it that way. “My people await.” And with a light blow, the tomb is engulfed in darkness V. Lightning streaks across the blackened sky followed closely by the roar of thunder. Dark clouds take over the sky in great numbers, looking menacing and promising a heavy rain. The wind picks up, howling in the night sky. It starts with a single drop that travels down from the heavens and splatters on his shoulder. LeStatt turns his head to glance at the droplet after it explodes on his skin and trickles down from every direction. More follow the first and soon a down pour is in full effect. This is no ordinary rain however, it’s thicker … it’s crimson red. “And the rivers will run red with blood.” The ancient Pharaoh, LeStatt, fresh from an incredible battle to reclaim his throne stands atop the pyramid created in his honor. It has been many years since he was in control. He has watched with disgust from the underworld as leader after leader has attempted to follow in his footsteps and ultimately fail in their quest to become the next truly great Pharaoh. No one was fit to take over for LeStatt, it is an impossible job. Fear however, can spread through civilization like wild fire. On this day, fear has overcome the people. In LeStatt’s mind he saw the day he would be resurrected as a day of celebration; as the day that Egypt’s rightful ruler stepped back into power and led it to greatness. Armed with godlike power, he can do anything. That is what makes people afraid. That power is what brings the people forward. From high above his people, LeStatt glares down with a feeling of betrayal surging through him. After everything that he did for the people of Egypt, his repayment is mutiny. Now they come for him, people from all over the world, they come to strip him of his power. They are coming to take his throne. As far as his eyes can see, the people are storming towards his perch above the sand. Some are just acting off instinct, others fear and even more prevalent? POWER. LeStatt can sense it inside of them; some want nothing more than to be the one to bring down the undead king. They want to be praised by the masses and have power bestowed upon them. They want to be remembered. “It will not be easy.” LeStatt’s words come out as nothing more than a whisper. “You cannot have what has always rightfully been MINE.” Some have reached the base of the pyramid and immediately begin to climb. LeStatt extends his arms out to his sides and turns his head to the sky, letting the crimson rain cover his face. His body begins to tremble slightly and then his feet come off the pyramid’s surface. “The World is mine!” He looks back downwards toward the sea of people. “You cannot have it!” He throws his arms forward, his hands loudly clasping together in front of him. Immediately the earth begins to quake beneath them causing many to lose their balance and fall. The clouds part just enough for the sky to show clearly behind them. Then, the balls of fire and blood burn through the clouds and head straight down … Let the battle begin.
