Back Again: It's good, It fits
Twisted
Global Championship Wrestling Roleplays #14
Date: Soon After GCW Launch Announcement
Location: Twizz's Bedroom

It’d been awhile.

Twizzy stared at that belt for awhile. That circular golden piece that symbolized a lifetime. He’d won it by defeating almost everyone in a battle royal and now, well, now he gets to go back and try to keep it. He’ll walk into an arena, look around at the pictures of many famous athletes of the city. Many famous events. Concerts, plays, sporting events, dog shows, and all kinds of things. None of them will be wrestling moments. In no arena will they show Rich Rollins winning the infamous Iron-Man match. Nowhere will you find a framed piece of memory of Steven Caldera and his Establishment standing tall around their fallen victims. You could search high and low, but you wouldn’t see it. Instead you would see pictures of Elton John, Bill Russell, The Beatles, a golden retriever, the Chicago Bulls, the Rochester Royals, Bobby Or, and all other sorts of celebrities. No wrestlers, that’s for sure.

He’d done this once before. Fell off the couch and headed towards the phone. Picked it up and groggily talked to Bill Goldwater for a few moments about GCW reopening it’s doors. That one phone call led to a win at his yearly goal. He wanted, needed, to win the Dangerous Games battle royal, and he did. He did it. He proved to the world that he was better than the likes of Shane Lane, Toxic, Rich Rollins, “The Dark Messiah”, and John Reed. He proved to everyone that he wasn’t some idiot addict jobber who could only win the tag team titles every few months.

Damn tag team titles. What a joke.

Well, here he is now, staring at his belt that’s in the dresser with his socks. It probably smells like sweat by now. It’s been a few months. Twizz didn’t even know if they’d let him keep the title. They might strip him of it and have a tournament or a match or something at Rampage. That is, if there was going to be a Rampage.

“Well now, look what we have here,” he said to the belt. He was standing up straight with so many memories and thoughts going through his mind. He sighed. He put his hands on his hips. “What will become of you?” He reached down into the sock drawer, the bottom drawer or his brown dresser, and wiped away some sock pairs. He grabbed each side of the title and lifted it up out of the drawer and just looked at it.

No, he stared at it.

No, no, no. He stared into it.

The title was just so good. It just fit. You know that feeling. Where something just fits and feels good. This title, it fit.

He didn’t know if he was going to be able to keep it, but dammit, he was going to walk into that arena and that headquarters in Columbus, Ohio proud. Proud to be the champion. Proud to be who he is. Proud, because he’d hear the cheers.

He’ll take on all comers.

Everyone will know just what they’re messing with. They’re going up against the Global Championship Wrestling World Champion. They’re going up against Twistodium. They’re going up against someone, something, that they can’t beat.

He turned the belt around in his hands so he could see the back. He brought it to his waist and pressed it against his black jeans. He wrapped the straps around his waist and put the belt on. It fit just right. It felt good. He put his hands on his hips and stood up very straight.

He let took in and let out a large breath.

“Goat… is… good, my brutha. It never was not.”

He clapped his hands together and rubbed them. Today was a new day. Tomorrow would be another. Goat was not the only thing good about now. Life was good. Life is good.

Because it just fit, y’know?
