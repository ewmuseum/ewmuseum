A quickie by a demented man

25 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/25 *there hasn’t been enough time, I said to myself. When I last left you all I had killed my father in my house, and ran away, and hid while the police, and the EMTs where there. I now stand in my doorway, the yellow police tape which says DO NOT CROSS now blocks my entrance into my home, the place where all my memories are from, and the place where life began for me back then* *Then I think to myself this is only a piece of crime scene tape keeping me from my past, present, and future. As I reach up, and grab the tape it just falls off the door, and I stroll right into my home, down the hallway, and straight back to my room. Nightfall’s, and so I don't get anyone’s attention I don't turn on any lights, or electric of any sort, I just sit there in the darkness, and let my mind wander like it always did, and still does to this very day* *the one thought that keeps running through my mind is that of my father laying in the front entrance way clinching his chest, as I dart out the door trying not to think what I did to my father that day, that very day in which my life changed, and it didn't change for the worse, to tell you the truth it changed for the better, and let me to tell the thoughts that where going through my head right now could kill any average man.* Why? Why didn't I do that to me sooner?, I said to myself 'These visions that I am having are great, there of destruction, violence, and most importantly there all of me doing these actions to other people' ----Flashback to Reality---- *These visions are why everyone said I was in another Dimension, so hence when it came that time for me to choose my wrestling name I came up with the best name I could think of Dimensioner.* 'Capo you haven’t been around in the new GCW for awhile, and you know why I say this I AM A CHANGED MAN. That tag team match the Guild got lucky, so this is what I am going to do The Guild, where did you even come up with a name like that, but that doesn't matter I'm challenging you to a match at Annihilation.' 'Your going to say but Dimensioner you don’t have a take team partner. HAHA you couldn’t be more wrong there you see there is one man that the GCW hasn't seen in a long time, and no I don't mean X-Calibur we just saw him. This man has the same distorted reality that I got, and do you want to know why?' 'He's my younger brother!' 'Lyger, yes I’m talking about Lyger. So what do you say The Guild. Annihilation you versus and this is for one time only Dimensioner and Lyger aka The Deadly Alliance, oh yes we can be one deadly tag team if I could say so myself' *I start to smirk a little until my mind gets back on track* 'Capo come worldwide you will know what it feels like to be in another dimension, and what a crazy mans sees when he is at the end of reality going to the form of crazy in which I live my everyday life'
