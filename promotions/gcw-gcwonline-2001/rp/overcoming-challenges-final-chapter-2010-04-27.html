Overcoming Challenges: Final Chapter

27 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/27/10 Overcoming Challenges: Final Chapter The scene opens inside a dark room at a closed location. There is a single light flickering in a hallway leading to what looks like a empty warehouse. As the camera pans around the room another light can be seen in the distance. The camera focuses in on the light as a vision of a dark silhouette comes into focus. The sound of dripping water makes a surreal feeling throughout the warehouse. About 5 minutes later a voice speaks in a furious tone. The biggest challenge is the challenge of you Your weaknesses lack of will the times you fail to persist the times where you repeat mistakes or when you learn but get shy about trying again with the new lessons assimilated When laziness, or over comfort, when perhaps just a lack of driving ambition, prevents you being the best you can be even when you are good enough to know how far it can go Overcoming yourself is hard because you may well know all your weaknesses, but you also know how to undermine all your own strengths So you have to get ruthless with yourself in the end, and that is the strangest thing ever - it's when you force yourself 100% with no excuses to be everything you ever wanted do everything you ever wanted live the way you want, with whom you want and enjoy it as you want and when you want Ruthlessness like that is very hard to find, you'll agree. You see what many of you fail to realize about me is that the persona you see and the way I come off is a mere fake image covering the truth that really lies deep inside me. Many of you can’t even fathom what I have been through and the challenges that I have overcome to get to this point in my life and now I have a this little roach named Diamond thinking he has what it take to defeat True Perfection, but the pain he felt from my Last Chance in the hallway before our match was booked, will be the same pain he will endure times ten in our 10 minute Ironman Match. You see this promo isn’t about the fans or making me look good for the match or even talking shit. It is simply about the facts and the facts are you ass is dead meat Diamond, cause I’m going to prove to you and everyone else why I’m someone who should never be overlooked or taken lightly. Sure I got my ass handed to me in numerous matches, but just like the energizer bunny, I keep going and going and going. You see you can bash me, bruise me and even knock me unconscious, but until you actually figure out how to defuse me, I will continue to tick and tick until that final tick has gone by and then I really don’t need to tell you what happens next. Donny you are lower that the dirt that I walk on everyday and in our match I will show you why you are and will always be beneath me. I am True Perfection and not you or anyone here in the GCW will ever be better that me. 10 minutes of hell and pain are coming and the time to once and for all close the chapter and the mouths of everyone to prove why I’m Just Simply Damn Amazing. {End}
