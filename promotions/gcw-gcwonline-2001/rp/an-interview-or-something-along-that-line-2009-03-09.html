An Interview: Or something along that line

9 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 9, 2009 "So what made you decide to join anyways? Money? Fame? Gold?" Jay Terror peered across the room from his chair at Kevin Jacobs. His eyes seemed heavy, baggy even considering the thick circles surrounding them. Still, he remained awake and interested in the way Kevin crossed his legs and slowly bit at his cuticles. "Honest?" "That would be best, yeah." "It was everything. You saw me out there, I wasn't much to watch let alone praise. Steven offered something that I didn't have and it sounded damn tasty. I mean, the worst that was going to happen was I was going to lose some fans and the free beers at the bar. Not like I was sacrificing much." Kevin nodded and smirked at the beer comment. "Not much to watch? Jay, you were the epitome of exciting. Didn't you hear people saying that you were one of the fastest rising stars in GCW history? Certainly you had to know that there were other options than joining up with Steven Caldera." "Certainly," he began, "but I want to make things clear: there's nothing wrong with working for that man. I don't understand how people can dimiss him and say he's the spawn of Satan without ever working with him or knowing him on a personal level. He's smart, enthusiastic, and downright great at all things business. To me, joining him was more like sharpening my future into a fine point rather than allowing it to grow even more dull. Kinda' like what Daniel Franco wanted for me all along if you think about it." "There's a familiar name. Have you had contact with him as of late?" Jay squirmed an inch in the chair to get comfortable. "Honestly, no. He tried to call a couple months back but what's done is done in my mind. He had his shot with me and he turned into someone I didn't want to work with: my father basically." "What do you think your father would say of Franco and Caldera?" "Franco he liked. Hell, they were friends after one outing together." Jay brought his hands to his face as if grasping a cigarette to take a drag. "Caldera I think he would have appreciated even more. He was all about making it to the top at all costs, just something in the Talley blood I presume. I bet if he were alive he would have found Caldera at some point and tried to talk him into having me join him. He did those kind of things." "You can smoke in here," he said noticing the hand gesture. "I quit actually. Just the withdraw kicking in." "Quit? That's almost as trademark as Rich Rollins's bandana." "It's not my choice. Some things have happened and they've made me look at things differently." "What things - if you don't mind." Guessing the question was coming, Jay prepared by sitting up straight. "Look at my win loss ratio as of late. I've dropped one to White, one to Murray, and now one to White, Murray, and Tessa. Sometimes patterns like that can be fixed by small changes in a person's life. Me? I figure cutting the cigarettes out will give me a couple extra minutes in the ring. Maybe it'll be enough to outlast those guys next time I meet them." Jacobs nods approvingly, "Smart choice. Speaking of those guys, I've been wondering a few things. Starting with White, what's going on there?" "Meaning?" "I mean I've never seen a feud come so far out of the ring until I saw you two go at it. How'd it become so personal and how is it now?" "Women man. Women. I swear, if we wiped the world of women there would be world peace. Fact is, he made his move and started seeing Caroline outside of the ring. I know I made some insinuations but you can't tell me that snake was docile around her. She was the horniest girl I've ever known and around a guy as doped us as Jordan things are bound to happen. Sure, he continues to deny it but I know what happened. I know how things played out behind the scenes and that's the reason that I still hate the cat." "Hate is as strong as it gets, especially for a former ally of yours." "Let me put it this way; if I ever saw him on the street I wouldn't hesitate pushing him in front of a car." "Maybe we should move off the subject, yeah?" "If that's what you want, sure." His foot began to tap the tiled floor." "What about Murray? I know you and him never hit it off but do you feel the same about him as you do with Jordan?" "Murray's a whole different animal. With him, it was all about his mouth. Jordan wasn't one to boast and talk himself up. Murray was the kind to rattle off his bio wherever he went. I hate that. Yeah, I talk myself up - who doesn't. With him, it was more than the usual though. I mean, he always had to be right and he always had to prove how much better he was. Everything was beefed up to the point of annoyance with that one. Do I hate him? Nah. Not him. I hate the fact that he moved on though and got to take shots at Statt." "There's another duo with a history." "Tell me about it. I'm sure we'll cross paths again." "I guess that leaves someone new and also familiar with LeStatt Knight: Tessa Windsor." "Remember that comment about wiping all women off the earth?" "Of course." "It holds true." He smirks to himself. "Tessa Windsor is so much different than everyone on the roster. I mean, when you think of gullible and impressionable, Tessa always comes up in my mind. She's just so easy to sway and tick off that it's hard to think no one has told her to jump off a bridge yet. Hell, I may try it this week. The fact is, I don't have that much beef with her just yet; I think it's more or less the side that she fights for, the people she defends. People like Vivica mainly." "So you're in her way because she defends people who stand for the fans?" "Basically. She's one of those icons that people love to see. Sure, she went through a rough patch but now listen to the people when they hear her name; she's so famous it's sick. I just want to knock her on her ass so hard that she goes back to the kitchen where she belongs." "Which won't Chris Storm's." "Naturally." "I just don't get it, what do you plan on doing to her?" "Just wait and see. All icons have their kryptonite and so far I think getting under her skin is working just swell. Soon enough she'll do something that loses her fan support and then where will she be? Perhaps desperate enough to follow in my footsteps and climb on board of the S.S. Caldera." "You want her to join you guys." "Reformed, sure. I'd take the entire GCW roster if it meant they'd quit doing what they're doing. Most everyone has their flaws but fixing them would most likely create a new person altogether. Someone I could add to my belt of tricks perhaps." "I guess that explains your plans for the next few weeks then. After that though?" "I'm gold bound Kevin. That belt is one strong piece of jewlery and I wouldn't mind taking it back around my waist." "Makes sense. Anything else you have?" "I'm through." "Thanks Jay."
