Date: 04/26/10 So, who IS Matt Richter? Wrestling Outsider April Edition 2010 Page 13 When people draw their minds to Irishmen in GCW they draw themselves to one man. The Tribal War Machine SurReal. Now though there is a new face on the horizon, fresh faced and ready to go, Matt Richter. Richter, a twenty two year rookie old hailing from Belfast, Northern Ireland has made his entry into GCW on a fairly low key. This seems rather odd when you consider the fact that his first ever match in GCW will be the Rampage Royale, held at the Shockwave Pay Per View in just four days time. If anything this may show that Richter is very confident in his own ability. Either that or else he's just looking to get his first taste of being the new whipping boy. For whatever reason he chose to enter the Royale it could become one of the biggest stepping stones that we have ever seen in this business. So complexed by this story we were that our very own man with a plan, Jack Coliid, called into Richter's home away from home in Tampa Bay, Florida for a chat. Jack Coliid: So Matt, thanks for having us into your home for a start. Matt Richter: Not a problem Jack, though it is all a bit weird. Jack Coliid: Weird? I don't understand. Matt Richter: Well for someone like me Jack, it isn't very often that I get approached by a wrestling magazine to do an interview. Hell this is the first time. Jack Coliid: Well I'll try to take it easy one you then. First of all, you probably seen this coming, the Rampage Royale. Why? Matt Richter: Heh, I wish I had a fantastic story to tell you but I do not. Basically I had been in GCW for around ten days and was approached by the commissioner himself. Was a great honour to talk to him for the first time and I was really nervous. Maybe that's why I did what I did. Jack Coliid: And what exactly was that? Matt Richter: Well he greeted me, shook my hand and all those pleasantries and then just asked me the question. Do you want to be in the Rampage Royale? At first I thought WHAT? I only just got here.I asked him to give me a moment and he said to come find him once I decided. Jack Coliid: So what made you come to your decision? Matt Richter: I went outside for some fresh air, to clear my mind. It wasn't the match exactly that got to me. It was the fact that my first appearance ever in GCW would be on Pay Per View. I've never been on television before, never mind on a bigger scale. My career up to this point has been entertaining small crowds in abandoned warehouses and hotels. I've been putting my body on the line for the last three years solid just for that buzz. Jack Coliid: Now going back further. How did you get involved with them in the first place? Matt Richter: Well my father, James, had come to me before an event just over two months ago. He sat me down in the reception area of the hotel and told me he had spotted a talent scout out working for GCW. He's a very inspiring fellow my father, always looking at the good things in life and looking at how to make the bad things positive. He told me that I had to go out that night and become better than I had ever been before. So I tried. I went out that night and put in one hundred percent. I don't believe in a hundred and ten percent, that's simply defying the odds. Jack Coliid: Now you said you tried. Were you unsuccessful? Matt Richter: At this point I was. I saw my father speaking with the agent after the event and they exchanged details. Once again he sat me down and explained the situation. At that time the GCW was heading up to its biggest event of the year, NC-17 and they wouldn't of been able to get me in at that point. I was fairly disappointed but my father reminded me that I was lucky to even get the opportunity to be looked at. Jack Coliid: So when did the penny finally drop? Matt Richter: I was sitting here one day, about a month ago, in this exact spot when my phone rang. It was a voice I had never heard before. Turned out it was the talent scout that had been viewing me previously. He said he wanted me to come down to the GCW crowd and have a try out there. The rest I suppose is really just history. Jack Coliid: And here you are, about to embark on your biggest adventure yet. Again a number of great athletes. How do you rate your chances? Matt Richter: I'm a confident guy but I'm also realistic. There are champions in this match, former champions in this match and some down right brilliantly talented individuals. Saying that though I know what I'm capable of and think I can hold my own against these people. If I win the match itself it would be incredible, the ultimate boost towards the Rampage Tournament itself. If I get in the top sixteen I'm still going to Rampage so it can't all be bad. It gives me a chance to size a lot of competitors out on my first outing. Jack Coliid: That's a good way of looking at it Matt. Thanks very much for having us in your home and good luck in the Royale. Do you have anything you'd like to say before we finish? Matt Richter: Thank you for a start, for this opportunity and for being so nice. As for GCW I just hope I can entertain the fans and impress my peers for the future. Jack Coliid: Have a good day Matt. Matt Richter: You too. So there you have it folks. A very genuine man with his sights set on glory in the future. Let's see if he can pull it off in four days time on the biggest night of his life. Tommy Steele. Wrestling Outsider Reporter. -------- In a field of strong competitors there shall be one rookie and his name is Matt Richter. Very few people in attendance at Shockwave will know who he is but he plans to produce a shockwave of his own to eliminate everyone in his path. His lack of knowledge could lead to his downfall but at the same time, his opponents lack of knowledge about him could also become their undoing. Shockwave - A violent disruption, disturbance, or reaction.
