YOUR ALL DUMB FAGGOTS WHO TRY TOO HARD

5 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/11 The scene fades in in the woods. There are trees and it is stormy outside and lightning is cracking down from the sky and it is really loud and the scene is blue and the ground is made of gravel and dirt and the moon is covered so we can't see. The lake is green and bubbling up and we can only help but fear what is brewing inside of it because it is like a cauldron. The scene is bathed in black. The camera closes up on the nearest bubbling part of the water and we see that it isn't water anymore but blood. The trees are suddenly chered off the ground leaving only stumps by a tornado but it isn't a tornado it is the sound of music. The song is known by all. "WHAT I FEEL WHAT I KNOW" We don't need to say what song it is it is Unforgiven II by Metallica. On the moon there are vast stretches of craters but what people do not understand (outside of the fact that the moon is not made out of cheese) is that there are haunting spires jabbed in across its circumference, the ends made sharp over years of space rotation. It is upon the surface of the moon that we see the grand obelisk jutting out of a ripple of earth, the bleu cheese sod crumbling outside in a vast overlay. The camera lens follows around the tree carcasses, a figure shrouded in complete darkness stabbing the ground with ferocious hacks, the spade of a shovel hitting the motherload of moon mozzarella. The moon surface trembles in the wake of such intense and focused manipulation, the faults shifting violently as cream cheese bubbles near a knockoff Mt. Erebus. Once the sun begins to comb over the moon, it is obvious who it is, even though it is pitch black. He stands as a giant, his movements quick even without the atmosphere. He takes in a sharp breath, tossing his shovel to the sun and laughing maniacally as he begins to sooth-say towards the gathering moon rats. It is obvious that he is not asian based on stature alone. Voice: HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHA YOU WHINY FAGGOTS! His voice is unmistakable. Voice: IT IS TIME FUCKERS! <iframe title="YouTube video player" width="480" height="390" src="http://www.youtube.com/embed/LihCeR-DoNs" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe> Clad only in heavenly white, the demon of the deep looks upon us from his perch, effortlessly gliding with one leap to the top of his moon empire. VOICE: WHAT I FEEL WHAT I KNOW TURN THE PAGES TURN TO STONE WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU FAGGOTS GONNA DO...YEAH...WHAT I FEEL...WHAT I KNOW...SO SICK AND TIRED OF YOU COCKSUCKERS MUTILATING THE OLD SCHOOL...BEHIND THE DOOR SHOULD I OPEN IT FOR YOU?! HOW ABOUT NO FUCKERS! Unforgiven is here and he is ready to go and the bottom line is that this narrator really doesn't care because he's done this countless times and he's sick of showing you all how it works. UNFORGIVEN: DORIAN RYUN I THINK YOU CUT A PROMO ON TERRANCE KINGSLEY BUT THAT'S BAD NEWS FOR YOU BECAUSE TERRENCE KINGSLEY IS DEAD AND I KILLED HIM NOW WHAT YOU GONNA DO YOU DID ALL THAT PREPARING AND ALL THAT FAGGOT SHIT AND ALL YOU GOT FOR IT IS UNFORGIVEN WHO IS SIX FOOT SEVEN AND DOES LUCHA MOVES LIKE HURRICANE RANAS AND DRINKS MILK FOR WOLVES UTTERS YEAH YOU SIT THERE AND YOU THUMP YOUR BIBLE AND SAY YOUR PRAYERS AND IT DIDN'T GET YOU ANYWHERE TALK ABOUT YOU PSALMS TALK ABOUT YOUR AUSTIN 3:16 SAYS UNFORGIVEN WHIPS YOUR ASS SO I LOOK AT YOU AND I AM GONNA FUCKING HIT YOUR FAGGOT ASS SO FUCKING HARD THAT YOU'LL GO INTO THE FUCKING XANDER ZONE IF YOU CATCH MY DRIFT HAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHAH RIGHT RIGHT IT'S A JOKE ON THAT FUCKING GIF FILE YOU ALWAYS PUT IN YOUR RPS HAHAHAHHAHAHAHA AI AM ORIGINAL BUT THE FUNNIEST THING IS THAT I'M FIVE BILLION TIMES MORE ORIGINAL THAN ANY OF YOU DICK SUCKING MONGREL SHIT BREATHERS BECAUSE YOU FAIL AT THE CONCEPT OF WHAT DOING IT OLD SCHOOL TRULY IS SO NOW I WILL CUT A PROMO ON THEM SO BE HAPPY DORIN RYAN BECAUSE YOUR FAGGOT ASS IS OKAY BY SOME FUCKING MIRACLE THAT YOU'LL NEVER FIGURE OUT HOW IT WORKS! Unforgiven: SO ANDY BITCHY YOU DESIGNED A WEBSITE AND HAD A MIDI FILE AND YOU MISSPELLED A BUNCH OF SHIT AND ALL U FAGGOTS MISSPELLED A LOT OF SHIT MAN WHAT THE FUCK MAN FUCK YOU FUCKERS I THINK AARON FAGJITA AND KARANA PERIODSOAKED BITCH TITS MCGRAW ARE THE ONES THAT GOT THIS SHIT THE MOST AND LET ME TELL YOU ITS DOUBLE XP WEEKEND ON CALL OF DUTY BLACK OPS AND HERE I AM WRITING SOME DUM FUCKING N[INJA] SHIT SO I CAN WIN SOME OLD SCHOOL MATCH I AIN'T DOIN A WEBSITE I AIN'T DOIN ANYTHING FUCKIN SPECIAL FOR THIS BECAUSE IT AIN'T WORTH IT YOU ALL FAGGOTS DON'T REALIZE THAT THE ESSENCE OF OLD SCHOOL RPS WASNT SAYIN EXTREME A LOT AND MISSPELLING WORDS IT WAS BACKSTAGE INTERVIEWERS AND ATTACKIN MOTHERFUCKERS AND ACTIN A FOOL LIKE A N[INJA] WELL HOW ABOUT THIS HOW ABOUT I SHOW YOU OLD SCHOOL LIKE HOW FUCKING SOME OF YOU N[INJA]S DID IT HUH HUH HUH YOU LIKE THAT HUH HUH HUH HERE'S SOME OLD ANY FUCKING MURRAY FOR YOU The scene slowly fades in, inside a schoolyard, occupied mainly by young children, of course. The sounds of a pre-high school school fill the air … Children laughing and screaming, a basketball being bounced up and down on the concrete ground … It’s all there. The schoolyard, or playground (whatever you want to call it) looks just like you would expect a playground to look like, on a bright, sunny, warm afternoon. The bulk of the playground is taken up by the concrete covering the ground. The school itself, is a single floor, L shaped building, built out of grey bricks. The playground rests on the inside of the L, with a large, wire fence running around the outside, preventing any children from fleeing. Pretty much, the whole place sort of looks like a prison. Typical school, eh? Along the far wall, we can see two basketball hoops, courts printed out below with white spray paint. Several of the senior pupils of this school can be seen on both courts, shooting some hoops. The rest of the playground is occupied by a slide, some swings, a round about, and several picnic tables, that are scattered in various random positions throughout the playground. The school, and playground, are both surrounded by what looks like a small, mountain town. I say this, because there are a lot of evergreen trees evident around the place, with the odd house or two scattered random distances from each other. The black, tarmac road at the front of the school is more of a ‘ghost road’ in the sense that no cars seem to be using it. We pan around the outsides of the school ground on final time, circling the entire area, before finally, the camera cuts to a view, of several male pupils playing basketball. There are about 5 or 6 pupils in total, and appear to be playing a non-competitive game of some sort. One unnaturally tall pupil (well, for his age), wearing a Los Angeles Lakers jersey, stands a good 15 feet away from the basket, which is attached to the wall. The boy bounces the ball off of the ground a few times, before jumping up, and launching the ball towards the basket. The ball slots through the hoop, and down through the net in perfect harmony, a pretty impressive shot for someone who looks about 10 years old. "Whoa … Good shot man!" a small, weasly boy says, congratulating the scorer on his basket. This boy, is significantly shorter than the other boy, and is wearing a school shirt, tie and pants. He is around about the same age. "Thanks Daniel" the taller child responds, catching the ball is it is thrown back to him by another boy. The guy then bounces the ball a few times, before passing the ball in the direction of ‘Daniel,’ who quickly throws it towards another large boy, who stands right underneath the basket. This guy, is slightly taller than the other guy, and like Daniel, is dressed in the school uniform, minus the tie and sweatshirt. He also has the sleeves of his shirt rolled up. He adopts a stance, before leaping into the air, going for a slam dunk. The ball, however, catches the edge of the hoop, and bounces off out towards the rest of the basketball players. "Damn!" the boy who failed the shot says. "Unlucky bro" another boy says, "You almost had it there!" We leave the basketball players, and travel across the playground, past the swings, slides and roundabouts, before finally coming to a halt at an oak, varnished picnic table. 4, slightly younger children, all boys, sit at it, two at either side. All of them appear to be wearing the school uniform right down to the sweater shirt, probably under the influence of their parents. The two boys on the left hand side of the picnic table, are nearly identical, could even be twins. They both have light brown hair, which is beginning to lighten under the sun, and both have those horrible milk-bottle cap glasses on. On the other side, one of the boys sort of resembles a pug-dog, in the sense that he is incredibly ugly, with a mean looking face. The other boy is tall, skinny, black haired, and wears big glasses as well. This is obviously the misfit’s table. We can see, that the children are enjoying a game of Top Trumps … You know that awful car card game thing? Good. "Hmmmm … " one of the twins says, shuffling his pack of cards in his hands. "Come on! Hurry up!" the pug-faced kid says, obviously he doesn’t have much patience. "Alright, alright …" the other kid responds, slapping a card down in the centre of the table. Suddenly, the camera changes it’s viewpoint again however, this time, we are right up close to a segment of the steel fence. Leaning against it, fingers clutched around the steel links, stands Andy Murray, former PCW Rising Star champion. Andy is wearing a pair of black shades, to protect his eyes from the glare of the sun, a long, black, leather trench-coat, opened up to reveal a black "I Am Nothing" t-shirt below, as well as a pair of black jeans, and some black boots. If black attracts the sun, then why would Murray be wearing it? Especially on a day like this. "Friday Fusion … The comeback match of Andy Murray, is almost upon is" Andy proclaims, in an emotionless tone of voice. "You know, they say that a good education, is the key to a good life. Education is the foundations for everyone’s future … And what better place to hold a match, featuring myself? I mean, competing in a match alongside the greats such as Alexander Miyagawa and Kannon, is always going to raise a rookie like me’s credibility. And think of all the exposure, all the hype if I won …" Andy pauses for a second, before continuing … "No, lets not … Exposure … Hype … Championships? They are all materialistic. They mean nothing, they are nothing. You see, I am not competing in the School’s Out match for the extreme title, oh no, I am here to shut up all the critics who have doubted me in the past. All the people who have said "Andy Murray cannot make it to the big time" or "He does not have what it takes" shall be silenced at Fusion, when I walk all over the superstars that they praise so highly … " Andy slowly lowers his head, still clutching on the fence. On the other side of the fence, three children walk up, one girl, two boys. The girl is dressed in a school pinafore, and has her long, brown hair in pigtails. The boys faces are both covered with freckles, both are wearing the school’s uniform. They stop, just in front of the fence, and stare at Andy, as if he was some kind of freak. Slowly, Andy raises his head, staring directly at the children, who look intimidated. "Yes … ?" Andy enquires, staring directly at the children. "Mmmm" one of the boys grumbles. "Who are you, mister?" the girl perks up, obviously she is not as intimidated by Murray as the two boys are. "Who am I?" Murray replies, considering his answer. "That, my child, is a very good question. Am I but a pawn of this world? Or does my existence on this planet serve some sort of cause?" Andy says, the children now look fairly confused by all this. Of course, 10 year olds wouldn’t understand something like that, now would they? "What do you mean?" the little girl replies, Andy chuckles to himself. "You shall see … You shall see" Suddenly, one of the boys chips in; "Hey! I know you! You’re that Murray guy from the PCW!" "How observant …" Andy responds. "You’re the one who used to be friends with BJP! I don’t like you! You’re boring!" "I am boring, am I?" Andy replies, "Well, if you think that, just wait until you see what I have in store …". Suddenly, a voice calls from across the playground, distracting the children. When they see it is not meant for them, they turn around, only to find that Andy has gone. End.
