Operation: FOX IV

18 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/18/2010 Previously on Operation: FOX... Clyde Fox and company ventured back to his contract signing in hopes of changing the outcome of that fateful day. However, their plan was foiled when the gang ran into long time arch nemesis, and natural bitch, Candace Fulmont. After a brief stay, the gang narrowly escaped with their lives. Now their focus has shifted to go back in time and save Clyde’s father before his untimely accident he suffered during his own wrestling career. If our heroes are successful, they might not just save the world, but the “slower” Clyde Fox we have come to know and love might evolve into a normal person! All this and more... NEXT! --- “So wait, what exactly are the rules to our time traveling?” Crayden asks as he scuffs his steel-toed boots across the concrete flooring. Ragner snatches his mug and waltzes over to the coffee pot to pour himself some dark brown goodness. The control rooms knobs and widgets flicker as usual as the gang huddles up. “I mean... you guys say you met this weird time vortex gate keeper guy,” Ragner sarcastically taunts, “And he didn’t even have an extra time traveling handbook to give ya? Must be really busy with all those time travelers and all.” Suzy bends over and playfully smacks Crayden across the back of his head. Ragner struggles to catch his whirling coffee mug before it spills all over the main control panel. “Ouch,” Ragner says as he reaches behind his head, “That smarts.” “Don’t be a wise behind you!” Suzy half jokes, “We have a lot going on here.” Clyde doesn’t even notice as his nose is nearly buried in the magical ‘TO DO LIST.’ It’s not until Kyle comes up and flips the page out of the play master’s hand that he snaps back down to reality. “Hey there, earth to Clyde,” Bold awakens, “You’re usually all over things when Suzy gets physical.” Kyle’s joke flies through Clyde’s head faster than the air that breeze through it. Fox simply snatches the page back from Kyle and keeps reading with his serious face on. Just then, Onjin and Clowl make their presence known. “Well to be honest Crayden, I believe I can field that question,” Onjin replies as he grabs his glasses and some charts, “You see, I’ve done some analysis on the transsuculent goo residue left on Cyrus before and after entering the time vortex and the results are astonishing to be quite honest.” Crayden sips his hot coffee and nearly burns his lips. “Okay, that’s totally cool and all... but how about in English this time?” Crayden spits. Onjin opens his mouth but before a peep is heard, Clowl raises his feathery wing to him. “I believe I’ll can translate to the dummy Jinny,” Clowl accents, “Basically, we can tell how the vortex works based on things we found on Cyrus.” Crayden nods his head as if he actually understands what was being told to him this time. Clowl notices the blankness of his friends face and simply covers his beak in disgust. Suzy logs in a few different numeric codes on the control panel as she explains things to the group. “It’s simple Crayden, listen,” She starts, “We are able to time travel because Onjin equipped Cyrus with a special thruster mechanism in the dash radio. They have to turn it to a certain station and they’ll warp into the time vortex. But, unlike Back to the Future, or Bill and Ted’s Excellent Adventure, we are allowed to alter things and places we go to. It won’t create a paradox or anything.” “That’s right,” Onjin adds, “Other than that, there aren’t really any rules. Just stay alive and you’ll be okay.” “Ah I see. That makes sense I guess,” Crayden nods. Fox finally puts the paper down and readies himself. The rest of the group seems stunned as Clyde finally joins the conversation. “Why hey boss, about time your brain showed up,” Ragner winks. Fox folds his arms like a pouty teenage girl. “Very funny C-Ray. I’m just wewwy concerned about this next mission. I mean, I don’t know if I want to do this,” Clyde weeps. Kyle puts a shoulder on Clyde and comforts him. “Hey man, it’s okay. I promise that we’ll make some progress this time,” Bold remarks, “And look on the bright side, regardless, when we get back, the mission list should change again.” Heeding his friend’s words, they all prepare for their next leap into time and space. Clyde and Kyle pile into Cyrus and before anyone knows it, are zapped off into the time vortex. They are welcomed once more by Lord Transcepticore. The all knowing and powerful keeper of the time vortex. “Why hellllllllo there little friends,” The spitting imagine of a Martix-like Keanu Reeves says, “Welcome back to the time vortex.” “Hi Lord Transcepticore,” Kyle and Clyde rehearse. Transcepticore smiles with joy as his favorite visitors have come back for a second helping. “Where are ye off to this time? Going to the age of Sparta to fight off some scantily clad warriors? Or dare I say the Stone Age to see the invention of the wheel? Or my personal favorite... THE SIXTIES! If ya know what I mean,” Lord Transcepticore inquires. They shake their heads as they wait patiently to be brought to their destination. “Or how about the dinosaur age? Wouldn’t it be cool to see towering tyrannosaurus rex’s and sky high pterodactyls?” Lord Transcepticore spoke so wildly as he tries to keep their interest but it wasn’t working. “Hmph,” A sad, sappy Lord Transcepticore mires, “Why does nobody seem to like my entertainment.” Clyde finally pipes up. “Cuz dawg, you’re just filler,” Fox sparks, “You’re the uninteresting part of the story no one wants to read about. You’d think that the actual time traveling part would be all futuristic and cool, but in nearly every adventure adaptation, that part is the most skipped. And... well... to be honest... you’re kinda boring.” Lord Transcepticore floats there... his smile slowly turns upside down. His high on life drops down to a McDonalds after meal depression. He looks at his badass black leather trench coat and notices that it isn’t really as badass as people might think it’d be. He takes off his Matrix shades and notices how they aren’t designer Matrix shades. “My goodness Clyde,” Lord Transcepticore realizes, “You’re right... I’VE GOT TO THINK OF WAY TO BE A COOL PART OF THE STORY!” Kyle rolls his eyes as Lord Transcepticore does a victory dance. But, at last, our hero’s time in the vortex has come to an end as they warp into their destination. The parking lot of the local wrestling event circa sometime in the late 1970s. “Whoa... where are we?” Kyle exhales as the turbine-sounding engine cranks down. “I believe we are just outside of the arena Kyle. The night of Father Fox’s wrestling accident,” Cyrus indicates. Clyde unbuckles his seat belt and presses his nose up against the windshield. He looks on as a flurry of fans make their way to the towering arena ahead. Without a peep, he grabs his gear and hands Kyle a backpack of things. The two slip out their respective doors and promptly slam them shut. “Okay Cyrus,” Kyle begins, “We’ll be back as soon as we can.” And with that, Clyde and Kyle rub elbows with the hoard of people as they eventually make their way into the arena. The flashing lights and funky music clearly indicates the show is already well underway. “Clyde man, you okay?” Kyle asks, “You haven’t said anything since we’ve got here.” “I know,” Clyde mopes, “It’s just... how do I know preventing my Daddy’s accident is a good thing?” Kyle pats Clyde on the back in an attempt to comfort his friend. “Well it was on the mission list buddy. And plus, it wouldn’t have been brought to our attention if it couldn’t play a key role in the future of the planet now would it?” Kyle persuades. “I guess your right,” Clyde says as he flicks at his nose, “I’m just not sure if I’m ready for this whole normal thing.” Kyle stays mum but nods his head in acknowledgement. The duo finally makes it to their seats. Floor seats, five rows back from the action. Little did they know, they’d get in the action sooner than later. “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN! IT IS TIME FOR THE MATCH FROM HELL!” The moustache touting ring announcer yells, “HELP ME INTRODUCE, CASEY “BIG DADDY” FOX!” Kyle grabs Clyde in anticipation. “Dude! That’s him! That’s... YOUR DAD MAN!” Kyle squeaks. Clyde’s eyes widen. He’s never seen his father in anything but a vegetative, whacked out state because of the injury he sustains on this night. And then... He walks out. The crowd goes CRAZY! Professional wrestling in its purest form. A square jawed, broad shouldered, long nosed, and well-defined showpiece of an athlete stands amongst a ravaging crowd of hooligans. With his head pushed back into his neck to accentuate his already overly pronounced trapezius muscle, Casey Fox struts down to the ring. He doesn’t bother looking at anyone in the crowd; instead, he slides into the ring and is still idolized by the people around him. “Wow, isn’t he great?” “You’re my hero Casey!” Clyde’s ears turn into mini satellite dishes as he picks up random fan comments. “Wow man... he’s like perfect,” Kyle whispers, “He’s like the guy who doesn’t care for the fans, but the fans totally dig that!” Clyde was in awe. For the first time in his life he was able to see his biological father in the flesh, how he was meant to be alive. The only thing was, it took Clyde to get to 1972 to see him. It was all so surreal. To the point where Clyde just sat there thinking he’s watching a movie screen in front of him. Waiting, waiting for it to be torn down in front of him and taken away forever. “Daddy,” Fox mutters. “ANNNND HIS OPPONENT; THE REAPER!” The announcer jumps in. The only thing that breaks Clyde’s concentration is the theme music of Casey’s long awaited opponent. A man dressed in all black spandex and white face paint as if portraying The Grim Reaper himself. The crowd booed as The Reaper jumps into the ring and points at Casey with one finger extended as if “choosing” him. “Okay Clyde now listen,” Kyle directs, “We did some research back at the dock and Crayden said that the accident happens when The Reaper brings out the sledgehammer. Apparently he sets Casey up on a table and accidently hits him with the hammer.” Clyde nods as the two converse beneath the sea of swarming fans. “Otay. Then when we see him pull out the sledgie wedgie then we have to storm the ring,” Clyde proposes, “It’s the best way to save Daddy.” As two bicker back and forth, they neglect to turn and watch the in ring action. The Reaper pummels Casey hard into the corner. But Daddy Fox still has plenty of fight left in him. They fight until Casey brings a wooden table into the ring. This forces The Reaper to rolls under the ropes and regroup on the outside. There he is, the man who supposedly cripples Clyde’s father, just a few short feet away. “YOU SUCK MISTER WEAPER!” Clyde bashes. The Reaper turns and flips off the crowd in Clyde’s general direction. “You’re running scared of my Daddy! You’d probably be best heading back into the ring with a sledgehammer if you knew what was good for ya!” Clyde sarcastically proposes. Just then, a lightbulb went off about Reaper’s head. That’s it! A sledgehammer would be the perfect equalizer to that table. And so, The Reaper dug underneath ring and retrieved the always deadly sledgehammer. “You idiot! You told him to get a sledgehammer!?” Kyle scolds as he shakes Clyde like a small child. “I’m sowwie Kyle! It’s all I could think of. I just really wanted to reach out and punch him but I don’t know what came over me!” Clyde reasons. Regardless, the fact remains that The Reaper just picked up a sledgehammer and slipped into the ring. With panic in his eyes, Clyde moves fast, launching himself from his seat. He dodges a few drunken fans and dives over the railing. Luckily, seeing that it’s the early 70s, there’s no actual security guards close by. “CLYDE! WHAT THE HELL--” Kyle stops midsentence in pursuit of Clyde who is already climbing up the ropes. Casey notices what he assumes to be a crazy fan trying to get in the ring and goes to warn Reaper but just as he steps closer, Clyde vaults over the tope rope, with some help from Kyle and they both crash into The Reaper... sending his sledgehammer flying... WHACK! THUD! OHHHHHHHHHHHH! The ring starts to shake. But not because of the crowd noise. But because of Casey, on the ground, convulsing. Blood gushes from his temple as the sledgehammer lay beside him, plastered in blood. It was an accident. Or was it? Clyde, blurry vision and all, looks across the ring at his fallen father. Clyde reaches out for him with one hand but by this time, security has stormed the ring and has begun to pull Clyde and Kyle away. “NOOOOOOOOOOOO!” Clyde screams. The Reaper and Kyle both seem to be out of it as they conked their heads pretty hard from the fall, but Clyde... he knew exactly what just happened. He was the lone cause why he turns out the way he does. And if anything, he knew, that he just made things worse. “Get these bozos out of here!” A rather rotund security guard belches. Clyde reaches out one final time for his father. He had an all too brief meeting with his once normal Dad. And even though this mission was an utter failure, deep down Clyde could take salvation in just one thing; his Dad was beyond ordinary. He was definitely extraordinary. What does this mean for the mission? Did Clyde do more damage than good? Will Lord Transcepticore actually become cool? Find out all the answers and more... NEXT TIME! To Be Continued...
