Date: Pre WW104 (1/24/08 NC-17) Prologue For the general population our conscious is what guides us to what we do, acknowledging that it is a dialog with our subconscious, trying to work out the components of a certain problem. For some, this dialog is more interactive, sometimes even spoken out loud, emphasizing this dialog of the conscious and sub conscious to work out the components of a certain problem. For few, this dialog is externalized. what makes these different kinds of people special, or different front one another? It would all depend on the person, and how they interact and respond to their method. Those that are "normal" go by as if everything is "normal" where those that interact or have spoken dialogs can experience the same normality. Most break out of the habit, and find the capacity subconsciously. It is the very few that have the externalized dialog, or embodiment that can have issues, or could be a threat to the rest of the community. It all depends on how much they are actually aware of. This can be broken down into a formula of components. Component A is the subconscious or "normal" method of dialog. Component B is the externalization of dialog, or someone else telling you how to act and be. When you study under a specific code, there is a third component that comes into play, which is component C. Component C is the base of your knowledge, morals, ethics, etc from a single text or source. We will say it is a book based in religious philosophies. A+B+C=Unknown What if a person was the unknown? What if Zacharia Taylor plays as the unknown in this equation? After everything he has been through, all the questioning and observing, all the emotional tragedies... What if he gave into all three components? ...then what?
