Date: Written: June 14th, 2008 ?I admire addicts. In a world where everybody is waiting for some bline, random disaster, or some sudden disease, the addict has the comfort of knowing what will most likely wait for him down the road. He's taken some control over his ultimate fate, and his addiction keeps the cause of death from being a total surprise.? - Chuck Palahniuk ---- Adrenaline was pumping through my body. I felt it rush like I never had before. And the sick part was that I was becoming addicted to it. I began to thrive on the adrenaline. I began to feel as though I couldn't function without it. I wanted to feel the rush .. with every passing second. I kept flashing back to Las Vegas. Everytime I shut my eyes, I pictured the light blue fireworks explode around the ring. I pictured driving a certain Scottish King of Cool's shoulders through the canvas. I pictured the referee's hand slapping against the mat. I pictured my victory. The adrenaline it gave me. God, that adrenaline. I was addicted to it. ---- High Times Hotel and Casino Las Vegas, Nevada Room 367 One Day After the Pay-Per-View "You awake yet, sleepyhead?" I opened my eyes and couldn't help but smile as I layed staring at such a spectacular woman. She had her head resting on my chest, peering up at me with an innocent smirk. Her hair was pulled back in a ponytail, slightly off to the left side. I wanted nothing more than to kiss her. And I did. Our lips locked and our tongues danced as I stroked my hand down her neck and lightly around her upper back. Her body quivered and she squeezed me tightly. "What are you doing up so early," I asked. She ran her finger down my bare chest, giving me the chills. She rolled her eyes back up towards mine before she answered, "I don't know." She spoke with a smile, "I guess I just had an amazing night's sleep." I knew exactly where she was going with it. After the show went off the air last night, we'd come back to the hotel room. Call it a victory celebration, if you will. My body was sore and a bit bruised, but she managed to take tremendous amounts of care for it. As soon as we got back to the room, she removed my button down shirt. She was somewhat violent in doing so, breaking off one or two of the buttons. But she made up for it with her soft hands, caressing my body. She threw me down on the bed and kneaded her palms into my back, giving me quite the deep tissue massage. She worked miracles with her hands. I can get myself aroused just thinking about the things she'd do with them. "So what are we gonna do all day," she asked me with curious intent. "We've got ourselves a marvelous room and a few, worry-free days in Vegas!" Her voice reached a higher pitch when she mentioned the sin city by name. She hadn't stopped talking about how much she loved it her since we landed just a day ago. It was her first time in the city and she just couldn't get enough of it. Apparently, her ex-husband wasn't much for traveling. I was clueless as to how I wanted to spend the rest of the day. I was still shot out from the night before. Not so much from my show-stealing match, but more or less from our little 'after party,' so to speak. But I wasn't about to pass up the opportunity to make her smile, "You said you wanted to see that Titantic exhibit in The Tropicana, right?" Her face glistened with a smile, from cheek to cheek. The sight of her eyes lighting up gave my tingles inside. She was absolutely gorgeous and it quickly became harder and harder for me to believe that someone would give her up... or let her go. But that's a Scotsman for you. "You'd really go there?" She asked with such surprise that I'd even suggest it. "I mean, I don't want to bore you to death all day." Clearly, she was trying to look behind my words to see if I actually had interest or was just trying to keep her happy. But the truth is, I really wouldn't mind it. The Titanic was pretty cool and it fascinated me. And I doubt we'd get slammed with any of the Leonardo DiCaprio bullshit at all. So, "Absolutely," I said. "I'm not gonna lie -- I have a soft spot for the Titanic." I kept feeding her anticipation, "I've always had a pretty keen interest in it." She leaped up from the bedside, flaunting her nearly perfect figure. My jaw desperately wanted to drop to the floor and leak out the drool that it had been soaking up. "I thinkkkkk I'm going to take a little shower," she said in such a sexy, serene voice. As she turned away from me and started walking towards the bathroom, I couldn't help but take my eyes away from her ass. What I'd do to get in that shower with her and give it another go. She glanced back at me once she got to the bathroom door. I smiled, trying to disguise my googley eyes. "Well what are you waiting for?" she asked. "Aren't you going to join me?" It took me a second to process what she'd just asked. Naturally I took her up on her offer, practically springboarding myself out of bed. I was still in my plaid boxers, so it wasn't too difficult for her to tell that I was already 'in-the-mood' for a shower. She wasted no time unsnapping her bra and stepping into the bathroom. Once I heard the water start running, I felt a rush of blood throughout my body. A wave of adrenaline kicked in. God, I was so addicted. ---- Tropicana Resort Las Vegas, Nevada Titanic: The Artifact Exhibit The exhibit was grand. Researchers had actually discovered and displayed a number of artificts from the actual ship, such as jewelry and furniture. I had expected a lot less, to be totally honest. I expected merely a few video sessions and maybe a slideshow or two. "I had such a good time, Chris," she said to me with a look of passion in her eyes. "I've always wanted to see this kind of stuff up close." She continued on with such infatuation. For once, I thought I actually did good with the whole female thing. It was always such a weak point for me. Especially ever since Rebecca. I always compared anyone new... to her. "Did you enjoy it, too?" she asked with such concern. The whole while we were inside, all she cared about was my enjoyment. I won't lie. I was astonished at her sincerity. It kind of gave me ... a rush. "It was fascinating," I reassured her. "I'm a little upset we didn't get to catch Leo or Kate there, but I guess I'll manage.." She laughed at my lame attempt to make a joke. But as much as I knew she was just doing it to be nice .. it still felt good. Real good. She took my hand and joined it with her own as we walked down the sidewalk, leaving the Tropicana. She caressed the sides of my fingers with her thumb, sending a chill up my arm. I wanted to sweep her up off her feet again. But we still had a bit of a walk before we were to get back to the High Times Casino. It wasn't more than a mile down the road that a familiar man came into sight. He'd been following for some time, or so it had seemed from the drops of sweat rolling down his face. I led him on for a little while, not letting him know I caught him from the corner of my eye. "Ohhhh, look! A little ice cream shop! You wanna get some?" Her face lit up when she asked. As much as I wasn't in the mood for ice cream, I knew it created an opening for me. "Sure babe," I replied as I reached for my wallet, "You go get us some ice cream. I just wanted to check out some things in this store right here." She smiled as I handed her a twenty. "What do you wantttt?" She drew out her words in such a cute voice, making her more and more desirable. I couldn't help but smile back as I answered, "Surprise me!" Thankfully, a group of girl scouts flooded the line before her. It bought me more time. She scurried over to the ice cream stand, while I slipped into a sporting goods store. All the while being followed by my alledged stalker. I shuffled back behind a few shelves before spinning around to encounter my lush. "Wesley," I mumbled quietly. He smirked, slighlty thrown off that I'd seen him. "I thought I had you this time." "If you're going to try sneaking around me, Wesley, make sure you try fitting in a bit more." He'd been wearing a suit you'd see on a circus ringmaster. Stood out like a sore thumb. Although, in a place like Las Vegas -- he was just another dime a dozen. "Sorry," he said apathetically. He knew I hadn't cared about his feelings nor his opinion. I already made him well aware that I was only interested in his services and finding me the information I needed. "But I just wanted to inform you that I was able to negotiate that match with Bryan Mayhem." It was like music to my ears. "I spoke with his agent and put through all the appropriate paperwork. Mayhem expects it to be a walk in the park." "Beautiful," I said ecstatically. "This is exactly what I need." It became the perfect opportunity. With a pinfall over Vivica and a victory over Andy... why not continue my streak by crushing another former World Champion? Afterall, he had to be in very critical condition after his bout with Shaman at High Stakes. A perfect opportunity to capitalize. A perfect opportunity to rise my own stock. A perfect opportunity to pave my path to the championship. "Oh, and it does get better," Wesley whispered. "Black's got Murray." A part of me wanted to pump my fist, but I knew that it wouldn't go as smoothly as I could have imagined it. Black was indeed a bulldozer. But I worried he wasn't a bulldozer big enough to plough down Andy Murray. Not now, at least. Not yet. He still needed a few horsepower upgrades. "Chris! I got you a vanilla and chocolate swirl!" Wesley slipped away as she pranced into the store. I'd be sure to keep my stake Black's match. Afterall, I still had Jane. And Andy... he was addicted.
