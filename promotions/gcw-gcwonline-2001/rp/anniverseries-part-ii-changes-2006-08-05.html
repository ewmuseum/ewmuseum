Suddenly the wings at the hilt disappeared in a zipping noise. The sound of metal hitting metal came a moment later and then his right hand disintegrated in a mist of black blood and flesh. At the tip of the blade was now the metallic blades, in winged shape. But at the end of them was a large ball of flesh... ... His black, evil heart. His eyes went black, no longer any life or fire in them. The wings suddenly sprung forth, spreading out again and turning the black heart into another mist of black viscera. “To the Ninth Circle of Hell with you, traitor!” With that, the wings zipped back down to their proper home, destorying more of the chest cavity to the body. The body of who had once been the chief astrologer of heaven fell to the ground and burst into a small controlled set of flames, as if brimstone was burning. Dani turned and looked at Astaroth, her front covered in black blood, and the sword in her hands. Astaroth looked around and only saw himself and Layla remained. “Well, maybe you're going to be a fitting host after all.” Dani dropped the tip of the sword a bit and it touched the mace that had once been Baraquiel's. No one else noticed as the mace became like liquid and was absorbed into the sword. “I think this is where I'll take my losses, lick my wounds and vow to see you another day” You've forced three of my men to portal back to rejuvenate after vicious wounds and now taken my chief enforcer. Tis time for me to bid you goodbye... for now. I will see you again Sirius Star, I will see you again.” At that, the two started to glow and faded into nothingness right before everyone's eyes. Gray, Alexis and Ukyo all let out a collective sigh. Dani looked down at the the slowly burning body. Then she looked at her hand, and then she looked at the front of her covered in the black blood of what had just become her first victim. Josh got up and ran over to her. “Dani, you okay?” She looked at him and as the shock from everything was coming over her, the green feeling from before was showing on her again. Josh got about five feet from her when Dani's lunch decided it was better if it came back up instead of hang out in her rolling stomach. She almost hit Josh's feet as she threw everything up and then went into a bought of dry heaves. Gray was right there to put his arms around her. And shortly thereafter, Alexis was there, but she was checking on Gray who'd taken a lot of punishment in his battle. Ukyo was quick to get there also, avoiding the pool of vomit that now sat in front of Dani. Josh couldn't get close enough to her now, but he was still there. Their quiet concern was broken as Blaise's voice suddenly boomed. “You stupid... arrogant... idiotic...!” He was walking right up to Josh. “Do you realize your bullshit just almost got everyone killed” How can you be so fucking braindead sometimes?” Josh got up and took the defense. “We won didn't we?” “If you call this winning! I call it playing bandaid on a fucked up situation!” Josh's mood switched to anger. “Fuck you. Go ahead, write me up or something!” That was the wrong stance to take. “Write you up” Write you up? You think that because you're her boyfriend, I can't get your ass in trouble? Write you up is exactly what I should do! I should write your ass up and hand deliver it to the Keeper himself! He's right over in TaleSmith Tower!” Ukyo noticed it... Gray noticed it... Alexis even noticed it... Blaise's hands were balled into fists. Josh answered. He apparently didn't notice it. “Yeah, what are you going to do about it” Huh? I've been written up before. It means nothing!” Blaise suddenly went sarcastic. “Oh” It means nothing? Well you know what. On that note then, I'm not going to write you up. I'll do something else.” “Yeah” What's that?” Blaise's left hand came up and smoothed his hair back. “Sometimes I just need to remember who the fuck I am, what family I come from. What am I going to do?” He didn't give Josh a chance to answer. His right hand lashed out, connecting full fist to the side of Josh's jaw. Josh dropped like a rock. He started to come up quickly, but Blaise grabbed his hair and pulled his head back. He brought down his left hand into a ridge hand that impacted right on his sternum. The effects were rapid, and Josh almost passed out right then. “Guys!” It was Alexis. Dani looked up and saw Josh now bleeding form his mouth. Blaise was standing over him, but was partially shifted into his other form. He pulled Josh up and then whipped him, sending him a good fifteen feet into the side of a tree. He was quick and was right on top of him again, slamming a knee into the side of his ribcage! “Blaise! Josh! What are you doing?” Dani tried to get up, but Gray held her back. She still had Blaise's sword in her grip. “No, he needs it.” “But he'll hurt him!” “Yes...” Josh went to try to get up, but then fell backwards into the tree. He looked up at Blaise as Blaise shifted back to fully human and grabbed him by his throat. “How's this” This mean something then?” Josh gurgled at him as he tried to focus in on the raging half Irishman. “Let me tell you something Joshua. I could write you ass up right now, and declare you unsafe to be around Dani. You wanna know what that would do?” Josh couldn't answer. “They'd reassign your ass. You'd be sent somewhere to keep you away from her. And that would mean you'd not be around her anymore. I could do that to you right now. But I'd NOT do that to her.” Josh was wide eyed as he felt Blaise's grip around his throat. “I could completely fuck your life up. I know about you going to Lia and trying to get embraced. I know why you came out here. Alex went back into the garage and heard you talking to that bitch. Your selfish wants and needs almost got Dani fucking killed you dipshit!” Josh's eyes were rolling back into his head. “In the meantime, you've fucked me over. Now I've got to figure out how to cover your fuck up over with the bosses in the inner circle. Not just with Ian, but with the Black Lodge too. You are more trouble then your worth and If I was in my right mind I'd dump your ass right now and send you packing.” Dani looked on, not believing what she was seeing. Bruising was evident on the side of Josh's face from the impacts he'd taken. No doubt his chest and ribs would be in similar condition. “But, you know what. If I did that, I'd be doing a big disservice to her also. And I'd not be able to keep my promise to her that I just made a few hours ago. I promised her that no matter what, I'd make sure you were taken care of and kept safe!” Blaise suddenly loosened his grip a bit and his other hand slapped onto the side of the tree. His eyes took on an emerald green glow. As he stood there, the open wounds and bruises on Josh's face began to visibly fade away to nothing but small marks. The tree itself began to whither and die as Blaise pulled it's life force into the mortal man to heal him. “I told her I'd make sure you were never hurt. I put a caveat on that, which you just proved. I can't protect you from yourself. So I'm just going to give you a warning you'd better listen to. You ever so much as think of doing anything on your own again, you'd better remember you go through ME. I'm your captain and if I don't know what you're doing, and you get into shit again, I’m going to fucking kill you myself!” Josh felt his face with his hands, feeling the wounds all go away. “Do I make my self clear, Corporal Brand?” Josh looked up at him and only had one answer. “Yes, Captain.” “Good!” Blaise got up, letting him go and started walking towards Dani and the others. Blaise looked at her. “So far I've kept my promise.” She nodded to him and then held out the sword to him. He shook his head no. “I'm sorry. But I can't.” “What do you mean, it's yours.” “Not anymore. The angel that died to give it to me told me that I'd have it a very short time. That one day another Angel would claim it. And she has.” “What?” Blaise chuckled. “It awoke for you. I couldn't use it's special abilities. You could. It answered your calls.” “What do I do with it” Where will I put it, it has no sheath?” Blaise chuckled again. “All the angels I've seen just will it to go away and come back when they want it. It seems to hide within their body.” She looked at it and using some of her past training tried to will it to go away. It wasn't that hard. The blade seemed to melt backwards followed by the hilt when it got to her hand. Once it was gone, she moved her fingers and hand around, looking for where it went. “I don't believe it.” Blaise looked at her. “Neither do I. I guess there will be no more porcelain doll for you. You just killed a principality of the Stars if I heard his name right when you told him what you were going to do.” Dani looked down at the still burning remains, looking more and more like the sticky sulphuric rock that they call brimstone in the middle east. As it burns it becomes a paste that is almost like napalm. Again, Dani felt ill and spit up some stuff, not that much was left in her stomach. “Okay, lets get out of here before we find ourselves dealing with whatever calls this volcano home.” Blaise was looking around, his eyes glowing as he peered into the veil. “What do you mean by that?” Blaise turned to Alexis. “Ask Gray. According to the natives in the area, this moumtain is home to a creature that could only be called a dragon by normal standards. We just had a bunch of paranormal activity up here. I'd prefer to not be here if the legends are true. And I currently put a lot of faith in legends and myths. If you know what I mean.” Gray spoke up as he grabbed his brother by the shirt. “Agreed. Let's get out of here.” Quickly they loaded back up into the truck and headed back. Things were changing all the time, but sometimes... they changed a little too fast. ~To Be Continued.~
