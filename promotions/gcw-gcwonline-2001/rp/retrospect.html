How the hell did Markus Stone get to be the most popular wrestler in 52 different realities? __________________________________________________________ Markus looks at the manila envelop in his hands. He feels the object within, trying to figure out what his best friend sent him. “Wait until you get home to open this,” Chris told him. Of course, Markus obliged. Though the desire to open was strong, and the willpower of Markus weak, he persisted and the envelope remained sealed. Until now. As Markus sits on the edge of his bed, safely at home, or in this case a hotel room, he tears the seal and tips the envelope over. A reflective disc falls out and misses his waiting hand, bouncing on the carpeted floor. Markus leans down to pick up the disc and looks at the title. “Markus Stone: A Career Retrospective” “Copyright 2009 Chris Fucking Storm Enterprises Markus can’t help but smile as he looks at the DVD, and wastes no time in putting it into the DVD player. The show begins immediately, no main menu, no warning message, just opening credits. “Cheap ass,” Markus can’t help but chuckle at his friend’s laziness. A montage begins playing, themed by We Are The Champions. Quick glimpses of Markus’ WIW Television and World title wins are shown, then the first GCW Tag title win, then the second. The montage ends before the song even reaches the chorus. Markus laughs and shakes his head. The narrator kicks in, some silly fuck with a retarded Scottish accent. Hungry Like The Wolf softly plays in the background as clips of Markus Stone’s first professional wrestling matches are shown. In black and white no less. “The man currently known as Markus Stone started his professional wrestling career at the age of nineteen, wrestling for a small local company called LCWL, or Legacy City Wrestling League.” Markus can’t help but notice the narrator is obviously reading straight from a script, with no rehearsal or second takes. The dude sucks, to be quite honest. “Although quite rusty, the young star showed quite a bit of promise. Going by the name of Zero, he was well known for his self-deprecating behavior despite his obvious talent. He was well known to constantly downplay his abilities while praising the talent of his opponents, despite a long string of wins.” “Before discussing his tenure in World Internet Wrestling, let’s look at the childhood of Markus Stone.” Markus snaps to full attention here. “Markus was born and raised in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. He was a devout Sooner fan despite years of disappointments and letdowns in important BCS games.” Markus can’t help but silently give Chris Storm an imaginary middle finger for that one. “When Markus was finally discovered by WIW, some may argue that his abilities may have already peaked. Although the president of the company, Jason Chae, was a huge supporter of Markus Stone, others in the corporate offices showed little faith in him. Despite this, Markus Stone almost immediately made a strong presence and won the Television title.” A quick montage of the Television title match is shown, Zero versus Benshaw. The end shows Zero gaining the win with a neckbreaker off of the top rope, a move Markus called Ground Zero at the time. “Winning the Television title was considered to be a huge stepping stone for Markus at the time, after he had won eight consecutive matches the sky seemed to be the limit. However, the man then known as Zero turned his back on all of his fans and joined Jason Chae’s collection of wrestlers known as the Corporate Authority. He immediately gave his Television title back to Benshaw, and lobbied for a shot at the World title. Although quite uncustomary to give such a newcomer a title shot, Jason Chae granted the request and placed Zero in a match against Cruel, with the winner gaining a title shot.” “This proved to be too much for the young star to overcome, as the much more experienced Cruel handed him his first defeat. Markus was feeling quite dejected, but instead of accepting the blame for himself, he pointed the finger at his compatriots and quickly turned on the Authority. This inability to stay in a partnership with a group of wrestlers would seem to be a pattern for Markus for many years.” “Zero began to be cheered as the Anti-Hero of WIW by fans, who seemed to enjoy and relate to the multi-flawed star. As Zero put aside his quest for the title for a short time, he went on a quest to single-handedly dismantle to Authority. Nobody questioned his motives, although it was readily apparent that he was simply seeking revenge for a perceived slight from the group.” "For months, the man then known as Zero struggled to win the big one, coming up short in multiple title matches. The most significant opportunity coming with an elimination style gauntlet match. Zero made it to the semi-final of the match, losing out to fellow WIW Hall of Fame alumni Genesis. This sparked a feud that set Zero well apart from the rest of the pack in WIW. While many others had previously tried to use words and jokes to combat the powerful mind games played by Genesis, Zero went on a quest to push back for every test given to him. This quest is, in the eyes of many fans, the culminating point of Zero's career in WIW." "In a dramatic twist, Genesis abruptly left WIW immediately following his victory of Zero, but the result was a more driven and determined man. Zero went on to win the vacated World Championship in a four way match after a drastic management shake up that left former foe Cruel in control of WIW. Unfortunately, the cross was too much for Cruel to bear, and WIW closed it's doors a mere two months after Zero became champion." Markus thinks back to that time, the time when he was on top of the world for the first time in his career. The only time in his career. A bitter pill to swallow, that one is. "When WIW reincarnated itself, adding the suffix NG to it's name for New Generation, Jason Chae was once again firmly in control. He made many calls to Markus Stone, but Stone was attempting to recover from a number of nagging injuries. However, the desire to stand face to face with the current champion, Erik Erratik, was too much for Markus to resist. Returning to the ring, a very unprepared Zero lost not one, not two, but three consecutive matches, the first of which being a title match against the World Champion. Although having never lost the belt, Markus Stone was also unable to ever recover the accolade. He was released shortly after due to his 'performance not meeting expectations.'" What a surprise, another so called friend in a long line turning his back on Markus Stone. Markus briefly wonders what Chae is doing these days. Then remembers, fuck Jason Chae. "This point in time is where Markus finally dropped the moniker and began wrestling under his real name. WIW had joined Primetime Central, and Markus became the first member of WIW to be Banned, and Exiled from the organization. This was immediately followed seconds later by the Banning, and Exiling of his close friend Chris Storm. This small occurrence had a ripple effect that many did not feel until years later. Plagued with slumping ratings and numerous hate mail and death threats, the leadership at Primetime Central reinstated both men, who immediately joined the tag team tournament as the team of Banned & Exiled~!." Yes, that's how it all started. Funny that the duo of amazingness you see today got their beginning by pissing people off and getting banned... and exiled. "The duo soundly defeated their first round opponents, but Markus Stone was forced to withdraw due to injuries yet again. However, he soon signed a contract with the Dominant Wrestling Organization, despite still having a severely injured knee. This injury has been well documented over the years, but some of the lengths that Stone went to in order to continue wrestling have not. This period may be the darkest time of his career, as Markus was constantly taking a variety of pain killers and steroid injections in order to compete in the ring. He was well on his way to becoming an established star in another federation, going so far as to gain the Rising Star award within a few short months of his contract." "When WIW:NG closed it's doors for good, Chris Storm was immediately recruited, and drafted by DWO. The duo of Banned & Exiled~! reunited, this time to team full time, when Markus Stone failed a random drug test. Despite DWO's seemingly lack of disciplinary actions, Markus managed to upset management enough to be fired due to his steroids. From there, Markus disappeared from the wrestling scene altogether for almost four years." "Not much is known about his time away from the ring, but one day Markus appeared at the PTC offices and filled out an open employment application to all of the members. This was not well known to the public, and by this time many fans had seemingly forgotten all about the former star. Steven Caldera, however, had not. He set forth immediately to sign Stone, wooing him away from the competing feds. On an episode of Worldwide, Markus Stone showed up with no prior public announcement or hype, and won his debut match. Most surprising of all, Markus, along with Tessa Windsor, managed to convince Caldera and Chris Storm that GCW would be the place for the Rock Superstar to begin competing. Chris Storm showed up later in the same show, and the reign of Banned & Exiled~! began." When you aren't competing in the ring, you have a completely separate life going on.
