Date: 2 years ago I am still not use to this. It is still mind boggling that everywhere I go there are thousands and thousands of people wanting my autograph, taking my picture, asking me to be in pictures with them. Growing up as a child I would walk the streets of England and it’s citizens would run away in fear, but here, in the states, they look up to me as if I am some kind of legend. I remember walking down the streets of Cleveland, Ohio in the middle of January. There was a WorldWide being taped that night. I was scheduled, the match escapes me. All my victims look the same nowadays. Anyways, it was cold as fuck. So cold it was biting at my cheeks. The wind was fierce, and the snow was falling in bushels. Walking from my hotel to the arena was a chore. It was a five minute walk that took me roughly 20 minutes. I was crossing a main street and when I reached the other side I heard a scream. Not a woman’s scream but a piercing scream from the opposite sex. This person was much much shorter than myself, but he grabbed me as if he could pick me up and throw me around like a ragdoll. “Holy shit! You’re Bryan Mayhem! Oh my God the self proclaimed Lunatic is right here in front of me! This is fucking sweet!” I became immediately annoyed. It was snowing, and colder than a fuck and this moron wanted to drool over my character. To avoid anymore attention I simply nodded and kept walking towards my destination. This freak had a different agenda. “Dude, you remember the matches in Belmont?” How could I forget. I shortened my life and my career in those matches. I knew I was not going to shake this man so I stopped and stared down at him. He was probably 5’9? 160lbs, had long brown hair. He neglected putting a razor to his face, and decided that putting metal rods in his nose, eyebrows, and ears would make him look more menacing. “Dude, those matches inspired me man, you have no clue!” Inspired you? What was this moron talking about. I know musicians with their stories in song can inspire someone, an author, and actor, a parent. They all can inspire someone, but the sheer fact that I ended careers, and spilt blood all over a castle that’s older than time can inspire someone really left a bad taste in my mouth. “Listen young man, those matches were a long time ago, and many people were hurt. So I don’t think inspiration is the word I’d chose to describe them.” With that I turned towards my exit and started to walk again. I felt his eyes widen and focus more on me than before. “Wait, dude, what the fuck” You are Bryan Fucking Mayhem.!” I stopped again, looked towards the sky, rolled my eyes, and turned around again facing this mutant of a human. “Yes, I am.” It’s all I could muster. This demented fuck was really starting to drive me insane. “Hurting those people didn’t excite you” Dude when you chokeslammed Lestatt Knight while your fist was on fire was the greatest thing I have ever seen. Don’t tell me you didn’t think that was fucking cool?” Apparently this man was stuck on the fact that everything in this world is as it seems. Did I chokeslam Lestatt Knight with a fist of fire? Did I break Syko’s back? Did I throw the Grim Reaper off the roof of Belmont? Yes I did. Was it great? At the time yes because while tied up in a hospital for a week recuperating I received a pay check that had not one, but two commas in it. Now as it takes me nearly an hour to get out of bed everyday, as I eat pain pills for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, as I look at a new scar everyday I wonder if it was all worth it? Was all this pain worth being considered the most sick vile man in the business? I think not. “Listen kid, I have to get going, what do you need?” He looks at me in disgust. “Wow, this ain’t you man. I know you, I know Bryan Mayhem, and this is not Bryan Mayhem.” Something in me snapped, my eyes turned to blood and I grabbed this freak by his oversize coat and brought him inches from my face. “How the hell do you know me” You think you know who I am, but all you know is some fucking character. So when you say you know me, tell me, who am I?” I saw the fear in his eyes. He swallowed hard, I saw the Adam’s apple slide north than south quickly. He began to shake, but meekly and feebly he let out “this is Bryan Mayhem.” I looked into his eyes, and what I saw I did not like. Gazing into his eyes my face was painted, my tongue slithered in and out. It was forked, and my eyes were red, yes they were fucking red. He was right, I can’t believe it, but he was right. I was an out of control maniac that was like a shark. The first scent of blood I was there, soaking it all in. I let go of him, straightened out his coat, and walked away. I crossed Ontario and stopped in front of Gund Arena as it was called at the time. I heard him yell my name one last time, and than I heard tires screech, followed by a loud thud. I turned and saw him lying in the middle of the street, his body contorted in a way that no human body can contort. I saw the puddle of blood begin to seep from the back of his skull. The driver of the car got out and quickly dropped to his knees. Those dumb enough to be out in this weather all stopped and rushed to his aide. I did not, for I knew this man was dead, and in the back of my head I smiled and laughed maniacally. I truly was out of control.
