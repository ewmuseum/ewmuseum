Vampyr: Chapter Five

23 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23 May 05 Dracula took in the cool breeze that came fluttering through the window in front of him, closing his eyes and breathing deeply. The sun had just fallen and beautiful black sky of the night came into full view. The moon hung in the sky like a Christmas ornament with millions of small lights sprinkled throughout. Opening his eyes a smile creeps onto his face, a mischievous smile. From behind Dracula a pair of thin arms wrap around his waist. Dracula’s hands roam downward and grab those hands into his before turning around to face the one those hands belonged to. Wylie stood in front of Dracula, her hair wild but undeniably sexy, her eyes seemed as if they belonged to a cat, her skin pale but it only accented her ruby red lips. She was dressed in a red flowing dress that showed off her well worked out stomach. “I’m hungry baby, when are we going out?” “Soon my dear, very soon. There is much work to be done tonight and you are going to help me do it.” “What kind of work my love?” “I have given our kind three hundred years to straighten up, to regain the glory that we were destined to have. Tonight I shall go amongst them and see what they have become.” “What if what you find does not please you?” Dracula’s face turns completely emotionless. That mischievous smile fades away; those black eyes seem to somehow go even duller. Slowly his mouth opens showing off his incredible fangs. “Well then my dear Wylie, I will have a rather large mess to clean up. First things first though, we feed.” Bran city Wylie had just fed off of some poor soul who thought that she was a hooker. Slowly Dracula and Wylie walked through the streets of Bran, everything looked so foreign to Dracula that he had to fight to brush the confusion away. He certainly wondered exactly why everyone who passed him would stop to stare, some even having the nerve to chuckle at his expense. His anger grew inside of him with every new set of eyes that he could feel burning into him. “Why do they stare my love?” “I do not know Wylie ? let us find out.” As another young man walked by Dracula and smirked in his direction, Dracula reached a clawed hand out, wrapping it around the man’s throat and lifting him off the ground. The fear that flowed from the man’s very soul brought that smile back to Dracula’s lips, a smile that made this poor soul all the more frightened. “Are you afraid you fool” Do you fear for your life? I hope you do because your life is the price you shall pay for laughing at me. Perhaps we can make a deal though, you tell me why you are all walking by me and laughing as if I am some clown and I may spare your pathetic existence.” “it’it’s your clothes man.” “What about them?” “Well ? just look around you.” Dracula took his gaze off of the man he held captive and looked from side to side. A crowd had now gathered, but no one dared come too close. Dracula then looked at his own attire which was close to something out of the Revolutionary war. Dracula snarled at this man before tossing his into the brick wall behind him knocking him unconscious. “Let’s go Wylie.” Dracula began walking off now not paying attention to the frightened on lookers that were staring at him. The people that roamed the streets made sure to keep their distance as these two walked by them treating them as some kind of plague. Dracula walked down the main street until finding a shop that suited his needs, a clothing store. The two were only in there for a short time before Dracula came out wearing a solid black suit with his hair tied back neatly and a pair of sunglasses finishing off the outfit. Wylie took Dracula’s arm and he led her through the streets again finally ready to begin the job he had set out to accomplish three hundred years ago. They wandered the streets for hours searching for more of their kind but surprisingly they were scarce. Dracula knew something was off because unless they had died off already, vampires would be crawling around this place, his homeland. It was when Dracula was ready to give up this search was when he caught the scent he was searching for. When the scent hit, even behind those sunglasses and black eyes, a slight red glow could be seen. “This way.” Wylie followed obediently as he knew she would. This scent was coming from the outskirts of town, where no light could be seen. Dracula waltzed into the darkness taking in the scent which continued to grow stronger and stronger. The scent grew to it’s peak and Dracula abruptly stopped, jerking Wylie back. “We’re not alone Wylie.” “You got that right!” A voice from behind them bellowed. Before either Wylie or Dracula could react a set of hands grabbed Wylie from behind, covering her mouth with one hand and restraining her with the other. A group of three grabbed Dracula and held him captive, although his resistance was very small. Numerous footsteps were heard coming from the darkness that surrounded them. “Jared hit the lights.” From all around them torches began lighting up one by one, showing that there were at least thirty people holding Wylie and Dracula captive. Dracula’s eyes adjusted to the lighting quickly and he studied the surroundings, noticing it was but a field enclosed by these torches. This group was all dressed in black nothing really separating any of them. The one who was barking out orders stood in front of Dracula with a smirk on his face. He wore a black leather jacket with no shirt underneath, a pair of black jeans and black combat boots. His hair was dark and cut very short while his eyes were a dark brown. “Now who they hell are you two?” The leader asked while placing a cigarette between his lips. Dracula remained silent doing nothing but staring at this one who stood defiantly in front of him. “Oh the silent type huh” Well listen here you son of a bitch, we don’t like outsiders coming in and thinking they can take our turf. We have been hunting the humans here for years, so you may want to take your happy ass back from where you came.” Dracula chuckled at this comment which quickly brought a look of rage on this so-called leader’s face. Dracula’s chuckles went quickly to outright laughs. Wylie did not share quite the same amusement, she merely grinned. This group of vampires seemed quite confused. A voice from the group screamed out. “Come on Chris show him what the fuck we’re made of.” Chris as he was now known snarled and lashed out at Dracula punching him on the chin, thrusting his head to the side, but barely moving him otherwise. The laughing stopped certainly but all this act did was give Dracula a reason to hate these vampires. Chris looked a bit nervous as Dracula’s stare burned through him. Chris knew he could not falter first so he forced a smile to those lips of his and walked backwards towards Wylie. “And who are you pretty lady?” Chris asked while running a finger down Wylie’s neck then over one of her breasts. “How would you like to join us” I can show you how a real lover does things.” Wylie answered Chris by spitting in his face, Chris answered with a backhand. Wylie hissed at Chris as she looked back up to him. “After we kill that son of a bitch I am going to have my way with this whore of a vampire right here. Actually maybe I should have you watch huh?” Chris says while pointing at Dracula. “I have failed ?” Dracula whispered. “What the fuck are you talking about?” “I have failed in my goal to make us the most dominant species. After everything that I have given you, you all repay me by turning into this pathetic life form that you have become.” “You did not give us these gifts you delusional bastard ? we all know who gave us these gifts.” “Do you” Enlighten me Christopher ? tell me who was it that bestowed these gifts onto you.” “It’s different for each of us ? any REAL vampire should know that.” “No you fool ? any REAL vampire would know that it was the great Dracula that created the vampire.” The entire group began laughing out loud almost uncontrollably. Dracula and Wylie merely looked on knowing the truth. Eventually most of the group regained their composure, and Chris continued. “You are speaking of a myth ? a vampire fairy tale. Everyone knows that Vampires are simply the next step in the evolution of man.” Dracula took his sunglasses from his face showing off those empty eyes. Something about those eyes took Chris in and forced him to stare back. Chris became deathly still as he looked into those eyes, soon Chris began to shake, starting with his hands but quickly going to his entire body. The rest of the group looked on worried, when they felt they should intervene Chris? eyes split open causing him to screech in pain and fall to his knees. Dracula threw his arms back tossing his three captures through the air behind him. A group of five vampires rushed towards Dracula, quickly he lifted his hands up and immediately the attackers stopped in their tracks. With a sadistic grin Dracula raised his hands higher, as he did this the bodies of the five vampires came off their feet and suspended into the air. They struggled to regain control of their own movement but his power was too great. With a growl Dracula closed his hands into tight fists, causing the hearts of these vampires to be torn from their chests, each of them immediately burning up and turning to ash. The rest now try to run away being smarter than to fight something with this kind of power, but Dracula would have none of it. Dracula waved an arm through the air causing the flames from those torches to explode and catch the remaining vampires. Dracula smiled as they all rolled around howling in pain begging for their lives. Dracula pulled his hands, which were balled into fists up to his chest and held them for a few moments before releasing the tension and extending his fingers, which then caused all of the vampires to explode. Most of the screaming stopped, all except the screaming of Chris who still was sprawled on the grass as blood flowed from his eyes. Dracula walked to Chris and hovered over him. He reached down and grabbed Chris by the throat then lifted him up to meet him eye level. “Please ? don’t?” “STOP YOUR BEGGING! This is why I have come back, this very damn reason! You have become weak and now I must start over. Chris allow me to introduce myself ? I am Dracula.” Dracula now opened his mouth wide and bit into the throat of Chris feeding off another vampire. Chris? body twisted and shivered from this ferocious bite, and slowly Chris? body turned to ash as Dracula fed. When the last ash dropped Dracula stood to enjoy the carnage he just created. To his right he saw Wylie who was on her knees staring wide eyed at him now. Slowly she came up to her feet never letting her stare drop. Dracula put his hand out to her, Wylie’s body moved on its own walking towards Dracula. She took his hand and allowed him to pull her close. “What are you?” She asked softly. “A myth?”
