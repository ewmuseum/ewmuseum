Life: Re-Birth

Date: 1/22/93 5 Years Old Fondest Memory: Sibling Birth 4:00 pm January wasn’t too bad in terms of snow in 1993. Now, I know what you’d ask? how would I remember such a pointless detail? Well it isn’t pointless. It’s really the only way I can relate my birthday to my little brothers. That’s right? I said little brother. My Mom and Dad weren’t suppose to be able to have kids but somehow a mira-bull happened and my Mommy gots preg in 1992. The doctors thought it was the weirdest turn of events they had ever saw. I must say that I was relatively shocked too. I didn’t quite understand what was going on and what it all meant. I was at home for the day, like any other, when my Mommy left early in the morning. I had always wondered why she was carrying around a dishwasher under her shirt. Little did I know, that it was my brother hiding under there. “Hey son,” My Father said as he curiously peered into the upstairs washroom where I was prepping myself for the afternoon. “Hi dwaddy,” I replied, “Where’d Mummy go dis morning?” My dad rubbed the back of his neck as he replied, “She just had to go to the hospital today” it’s a very special day today kiddo.” “Oh yeah?” I said as I grabbed my toy Flinstone shaver and shave cream, “Why is that” Is she heeert?” “No, no” it’s a good thing. You see? she’s having a baby Clydie,” My Dad informed. WHAT?”””””” A drooling, irritable, crying, slobbery menace!” Who would want such a thing!” They ruin lives! And after the godsend of not being able to have one; YOU’RE HAPPY? But? NO! You can’t ruin your lives? YOU CAN’T RUIN MY LIFE! I’m your child. Me, me, me, me! No one else! “Now, I know what you’re thinking Clydie,” My Dad spoke, “And don’t worry, this won’t take attention away from you” it’s just that your mother and I really wanted this. You’ll understand someday. Listen? her caesarean section isn’t for another half hour or so. If you feel like you want to come, then I’ll bring you okay?” I threw some shaving cream on my baby-bum-smooth face and stared a hole through my Dad. I was supposed to be the golden boy? the spoiled lone child. Now they were going to make me deal with this? I already had a past history with a sibling that did not sit well with me? you could say I was scared that I’d take my anger out on this new brother. “How do you know it’s my brother” And if so, what’s his name?” I interrogated. “Well son, we know it’s a boy because Mommy had an ultrasound done. And to be honest, we haven’t picked a name. But if you come, maybe Mommy could be influenced,” My Dad provoked lovingly. I slashed away on my face with the Flinstone shaver debating whether or not I wanted to partake in the official dethroning of my own crown. My Dad could see the obvious struggle I was experiencing as he backed off. “It’s okay bud. I’m going to get ready and you can let me know aftert okay?” He proposed before leaving the room. Click! The door shut behind him and I stopped shaving and sat on the closed toilet seat. Why me? Why does my life have to be eradicated by some little chromosome thingy that doesn’t even know me yet! It’s no fair. I’m going on in life splitting candy, presents, friends, space and toys! But on the other hand? maybe this was suppose to happen? Maybe this is my chance to not be like Chris and treat my brother like I wanted to be treated. What would Mommy think of her elder son if he weren’t there to support her in her time of bleed? It was right then that I decided I should go. I quickly shaved the rest of my pretend beard and jolted out of the washroom faster than a speeding bullet. I almost ran into my Dad with how fast I was going. “Whoa little man, no rush. Where are you off to?” Dad asked as he buttoned up his shirt. “I’m coming Dad. I wanna see,” I said dejectedly. My Dad smiled as he tucked his shirt in, “I’m proud of you son.” --- Push, push, push! Almost there! The head is out! I kept having recollections of when I was born. Mommy had me through the chute. You know, the tunnel of mangina, the pink casket, 4th and hole? the goods. So there I was; right where my brother had been before me. Standing on the outside of the operating room, looking in. My Dad was beside me. I looked up at him and he looked down at me and smiled. Then our last moment of solitude was broken by a sound? “WAHHH! WAHHHH! WAHHH!” The wailing cry of a newborn. It both shattered and held my heart together all at once. My Dad rushed the door as the doctor signaled for us to enter. I didn’t say one word the rest of the day? I was flubber-gasted. I slowly followed Dad into the room. Mommy was lying down on the bed with some crazy frizzed up hair. She looked out of it. I stood there and watched my Dad cradle this little bundle of life. For the first? second time in my life I felt insignificant? unwanted. That must be the little rascal from hell. Here to terrorize my life. I think I understand my role now? I’m the backup dancer to everyone’s main act. I’ll never amount to anything more. I stared off into the distance with an emotionless look on my face. Little did I know, but this would plague my life in the future. Each time that feeling would overcome my body? I’d just go limp? standby mode. My Dad snapped me out of it. “Hey bud. Want to hold your little brother?” He asked as he came closer with the warm capsule. Tears welt up in my eyes. Both with fear and angst. I appreciated that I was involved? yet for some reason I didn’t want to hurt the baby. It was the moment when I put my arms out and my Dad gently placed the little nougat in my arms that I felt something I never felt before. What is love? Baby? don’t hurt me? don’t hurt me. The little worm squirmed in my arms as I looked down at him. He was a chubby sucker. He snuggled up against me though. I didn’t dare hurt him. He was so precious. Like a China doll? but it was a real person. “Congratulations you two,” Dr. Haddaway stated as he exited the room. The Nurse walked up to Mom next and asked her a few things. “What will you name him?” The Nurse asked politely. My parents locked eyes and drew themselves in closer. “Zane” I have an idea,” My Mother whispered, "Clyde will notice if his brother doesn’t have a matching last name. If we give him Fairmount as his last name, he’ll surely find out he’s not really our child.” “Hun” he’s going to find out eventually. He already knows that we couldn’t have kids? he must think he was a miracle? when really he’s a special child we found out in the cold. But? I’ll do whatever you say,” My Dad muttered. My Mom turned to the nurse and instructed her, “His name is Clark” Clark Edwin Fox? our little miracle. You like your little brother Clyde?” I clicked my heels and replied with a cute, “Yeah.” The nurse scribbled the name down and headed off to fill out more paperwork as my parents continued to secretly confer. “What’s going to happen when the day comes that Clyde finds out that Clark is normal and he isn’t?” My Dad frantically asked. “You worry too much Zane. This is for the greater good,” Mom deflected. “Yeah” for right now Claire,” Dad objected. “ACHOO!” The baby cooed loudly as it made my Dad jump out of his skin. He spun around and looked at me. I did nothing that permitted such a sound. “What did you do” Give Clark to me Clyde,” Dad demanded as he walked over. I felt like I was under suspicion or something with the over protectiveness shown to the newborn? Are they protecting him because he’s a baby? or protecting him from me? Is? is there something wrong with me? Indeed this was the start of my problems internally. And I think I became most conscious of any potentially defect I might possess on that day. The brightest dark day in history; Clark Fox was born.
