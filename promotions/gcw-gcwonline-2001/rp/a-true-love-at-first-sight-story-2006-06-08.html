A True Love At First Sight Story...

8 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/8/2006 {There were many memories that stuck with me throughout my years. Very seldom would I conjure up a half decent thought that didn't propel me into a dead state of hatred or depression. But there was one that came to mind...} (It was a beautiful sunny summer day. The clouds were perfect marshmellows, and the sun gently laid a casual tan upon my shirtless upper torso. I believe the setting was somewhere in New Hampshire. Ah, yes. Hampton Beach, New Hampshire. This wasn't one of the nicest beaches I have been to, but it certainly wasn't the worst. I had a knack for either flying or driving myself far away from home in order to enjoy a nice fitting vacation. Going from the west coast to the east coast would be my favorite getaway. Massachusetts and New Hampshire would play deeply into my future, as for I was about to meet the girl of my dreams.) (I found myself inside one of the walk in arcades that the Hampton Beach strip had to offer along the entire scene. I remember tossing a few baseballs at the Speed Pitch. Baseball was always my secret passion, as well as music.) "Alright step right up, step right up! Two dollars gets you three pitches. Guess the speed of your third pitch and chuck a STEER-IKE and win a prize. Reach the high speed of the day and have your initials and speed written on the board." The annoying man said. (I procede to the front of the line and remove two crumbled, torn and beaten Washingtons from my left pocket. I slam them down into the man's hand as he juggles me a set of three balls. Fucking show off. My turn.) (I rear back and toss a slight lob and watch it sail into the drop pad with a red box indicating a strike zone. I gaze at the radar gun and see in giant red block numbers. 25 MPH. I get a slight cuckle from the carnival worker. I found it rather funny that he being the useless, minimum wage, dime a dozen, shit worker would attempt to scoff at a 6'4" man tossing a rubber baseball at an arcade. I rear back again. I sling my arm forward and release.) "Strike! 28 MPH. That's two sir, what is your guess?" (I walk right past the bitch with a microphone and stand face to face with the dry erase board. I turn to the man and remove the red marker from his right hand. I turn back to the board and read 83 MPH George Drew. I take my hand and wipe the name and speed off the board.) (With a line of people waiting to throw some pitches watching on, I begin to jot on the board. I turn and walk away from the board leaving my own Babe Ruth type stamp on what was about to happen. TREY ARBOR 97 MPH, that was about to be the score to beat on the dry erase board. I walk over to the prizes as I reach up on the wall and remove a Teddy Bear sporting a flimsy cloth Red Sox hat. I stuff him under my left arm as i reapproch the pitchers mound. I lock eyes with the game host, and never break my concentration from my death stare. I arch back my muscular right arm and shot it forward.) (The host drops his two dollar Wal-Mart microphone as the radar lights up 97 MPH. It was a bit outside, but noone seemed to care. I smirk at the guy as I make my way out of the Speed Pitch. The crowd of kids waiting in line gaze at me like I'm Roger fucking Clemens as I continue to walk right by.) (I find myself back outside on the beach side of the strip, still with the teddy bear stuffed under my left arm. I continue to walk as I come across what seems to be a single mother having a hard time controlling her daughter. She is extremely upset and crying out, clearly bringing attention to herself and her mother from the by standers. I approach the little girl. She is roughly four or five years of age. I drop to one knee and remove the teddy bear from under my arm. I hold him out to her as a token to cheer her up.) "His name is Theodore. He is looking for a nice owner to take care of him and love him. He has been sad all day that he may not find the right owner who could treat him with the love and care he needs. He saw that you were upset and he told me that he would really like for you to hug him. Hugs make everyone feel beeter. Would you like to hug Theodore?" I stated to the little girl. (The little girl uses her hands to wipe her tear drenched eyes, still with a pout on her face and nods her head and reaches out for the stuffed animal. I release "Theodore" into her arms as a small smile forms on her face. I crack a small smile myself as I rize to my feet and pat the little girl gently on her head. I turn to the mother who stares at me with a sense of relief in her eyes.) "Thank you. That was very kind of you." She offers with a soft sigh. (I smile at her and nod and turn back towards my direction I was heading. I begin to step forward as suddenly I feel this hand place gently on my right shoulder blade. I turn my attention back around as I find myself in complete awe of what was standing behind me. There she stood with beautiful hazel eyes, long brown hair that covered her perfectly firm chest. Coon tails that draped from both sides of her parted long hair. A picture perfect stubby little nose, and her gorgeously plump lips, all brought out by the glimmer in those deep hazel eyes. One ear sporting about eight diamond earrings around the lining of her ear. Both ears sporting size 0 gages. She stood at around 5'2" which to me was a perfect size a woman should be. She had the body of a goddess. I just stood in shock.) "I saw what you did for that woman and her child back there. That was incredibly sweet." She stated as she stared at me as if she were just hit by one of Cupid's arrows. "My name is Skyler. Skyler Rayne." (Life would never be the same. I never thought there could ever be a time in anyone's life where you'd be left standing speechless. Yet there I remain, unsure of what to think, how to react. A true love at first sight story...)
