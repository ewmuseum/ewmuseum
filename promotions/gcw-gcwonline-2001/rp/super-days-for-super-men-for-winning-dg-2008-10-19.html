Super Days for Super Men for Winning DG

19 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-19-08 “This city’she is mine to protect!” “Uh’dude, what are you doing?” Chris Storm, the loveable spiky blond member of Banned & Exiled~!, looked back at his partner in crime and shrugged his shoulders. “I dunno, it sounded cool.” “That it did” replied Markus Stone, “but we should probably focus on Dangerous Games, what with that cornball commish suspending us.” “And for no good reason either!” shouted Storm, “I mean outside the fact that we were planning to cause some chaos and throw some shit down.” Nodding his head, Markus pulled out a pen from his pocket and pressed it against the legal pad he had out. “Which would have been awesome. But now we need to strategize on how we’re going to sneak into the event.” “I thought we were going to do what we always did; straight bust up into that bitch and be all like “what now bitches?”” Never confused for the brains of the operation, Chris Storm acts out kicking down a door and throwing symbols. Markus takes notes on the pad, kicking his feet up onto the coffee table in Rikki Roxx’s apartment. On cue the Man of Metal comes walking into the room in time to see and shoos Stone’s feet down. “What’s up GUYYYYSSS?” screams Rikki for no reason other than cause it’s what rockers do, “We rocking hard or hardly rocking?” Markus reaches up for a knuckle bump on GCW’s Resident Rocker, “Rocking hard my man, always rocking hard.” “Hey dudes” Storm interrupts, “we should so totally see about becoming super heroes.” The two pause what they were doing and look at Chris with a befuddled look, “did two brain cells just accidentally bump together?” asked Markus as he continued to draw an elaborate and overly complex plan on breaking into the London Arena. “I dunno, but my head hurts now.” Rikki Roxx started to think on the idea and his eyes lit up like pyros to an AC/DC show. “We could so totally be awesome super heroes! We’d be all super and shit!” Markus Stone again had to be the voice of reason for the excitable duo, “that’s great and all, expect for one thing: we don’t have super powers.” Lucky for Chris Storm and Rikki Roxx, logic never discouraged them. Chris looked to Roxx, “we should so totally get some super powers. We would rock even harder if we had super powers.” Finally looking up from his notepad, Markus put a question to the two; “and just how do you two plan to get super powers?” “Shit, I dunno” How does any of the super heroes get their powers.” Chris looked to Rikki Roxx for help, “radiation?” Markus Stone slapped his head, “good idea, go stick your head in the microwave and turn it on, then tell me how that went.” Watching his friend, Markus shook his head as Chris? eyes got wide, no doubt a sign that Chris was actually giving the idea thought in hopes of gaining sweet-ass super powers. “You’re not sticking your head in the microwave” Markus Stone told him, shooting down any plans Chris had of nuking his brains in hopes of Hulking out. Frowning, Chris looked around the room, “then how do people get super powers?” Rikki Roxx jumped to his feet, “I think I know! To the Mausoleum Of Metal!” “The what?” “My..uh’bedroom.” “Dude, gross” Chris tells him, shaking his head. Rikki Roxx ignored the complaint and turned away from the duo, heading off to the far room in his apartment. Chris and Markus are forced to jump from their seats to try and catch up with the God of all that is Metal and Rock, getting within a step behind him as Rikki stopped at the last door. He looked over his shoulder to them, giving them a five-star smile, “here it is fellas, the Reliquary Of The Rock Gods.” “The what?” He dropped his head in defeat, “my bedroom.” “I thought you said it was called the Mausoleum Of Metal.” Chris shot forth. Ignoring him for a moment, Rikki opened the door and walked in, giving enough space for Banned & Exiled~! to follow behind. “It has many names, for it is many things.” ??Lair of a Thousand Women? certainly isn’t one of them? shot Markus, which caused a frown on their little buddy’s face. Chris, however, threw an arm over Rikki’s shoulder while wearing a grin ear to ear. “DUDE! Where have you been hiding this stuff all our lives?” Covering nearly wall to wall is a massive collection of comic books. In every corner there is a four foot stack high of mint, bagged and sealed books filled with colorful pictures of super powered men and women. “All we have to do,” Rikki Roxx said, “is look through these books and see how they got their powers. Then we’ll see if it will work for us.” Markus Stone shook his head, “guys. I don’t think it is that easy.” Thankfully no one listened to Markus when he spoke logically, and Chris Storm was quickly nose deep in the first comic book he could get his hands on. “Oh man! Radioactive spiders! We could so totally get some bad ass powers that way.” Chris lowered his Spider-Man #47 to look over to Markus, “but I wonder if it would work with other animals so I could get some sweet radioactive tiger powers.” “And get chomped by my awesome gator powers?” OHHHH----BURN! (fuckers) “Or dude, what about a gamma ray bomb being dropped on us” I look frickin? hot in green.” Markus spoke up again, letting his tag team partner know that A: they hadn’t invented gamma ray bombs and B: bombs exploding on people’s head result in death, not Hulking out. “Well fine Mr. Smarty Parts McSmart Pants, why don’t you figure out how we get some super cool powers of awesomeness.” Chris finally told him, crossing his arms and sitting in the desk chair near the window. Picking up a comic book, Markus flipped through a few pages, breezing through the various pictures flashing before him. He grabbed one that seemed to spark his interest, his browsing getting noticeably slower. He placed the Detective Comics #27 down and looked over to Chris Storm, who by this point had gotten bored of not looking at comic books and was singing to himself. “I kick people’s asses for a living! And I’m so good at it because I’m awesome! I can run and jump and be all cool because I’m just naturally talented. Naturally! They pay me so much money to be so good that I swim in all my money because that is what super rich and athletic people DOOOOOO!!!!” It didn’t take long before Rikki Roxx joined in with a air guitar going as they sing the off-key and off-beat song. “We’re so awesome and rich! We have so much talent to beat people UP! And it’s all cause we’re so talented and RICH!” “DUDES!! OMG!” Rikki Roxx and Chris Storm stopped their song, “Dude, I think Markus just O-M-Ged us.” They looked to their pal, “what’s up Markus-o.” Markus Stone jumped to his feet, waving the comic book in the air, “we’ll be vigilantes!” “Dude, I haven’t been a vigilante since I was sixteen and the chick next door had her parents live for a weekend in Chicago and we totally had too much to drink.” Rikki Roxx turned to Chris, “sweet.” KNUCKLE BUMP~! “No guys, totally, this guy in this comic book’this Man Bat’he gets to be a super hero because he is really athletic?” “We’re totally athletic.” ??Can beat people up in a fight?” “We totally know how to beat people up.” ??And he’s filthy stinking rich.” “We’re the filthiest!” Pulling the comic book back down to eye level, Markus took another long look at it. “We could so totally do this.” Chris rubbed his hands together, “alright, let’s do this.” He stopped, looking at Markus, “but uh, dude, what exactly are we supposed to do?” It quickly became evident that neither had thought out the idea very much (not that they ever thought anything out, but that was beside the point), as Markus blankly started at Chris. “I dunno, we get costumes?” “To the closet!” “Dude, seriously, GAY.” Rikki Roxx shook his head again, “not like that.” “Still kinda gay, man” Chris answered back, following as Rikki Roxx headed over to the massive closet adoring the far wall. Throwing up the double doors, Rikki Roxx stood back and allowed the longest reigning champions in GCW history to soak in his polyester glory. Rack after rack of rock-glam attires under off hangers in a dazzling display of colors and rhinestones. Nylon, Spandex, and Denim were each sorted and separated, so not to mix for the trendy Rock God of today. Rikki Roxx stepped back even further, turning to his friends with arms extended wide “Gentlemen, I give you our super hero suits.” The would-be heroes looked at the amass of tights and capes, eyes transfixed on which to pick from first. The entire closet looked like a Halloween costume store gone bad. Markus reached in carefully, grabbing a leather/denim mix jacket with matching pants, and examined it. “Uh,” he said, “this could be a long process.” “Are we super heroes yet?” Storm asked as he grabbed a neon blue jump suit that was decked out in all matters of frill. “I think we should move on to the next step” Markus suggested, looking over the nylon slacks he held. “We should probably go look for some crime to stop or some shit.” Chris? eyes lit up like lights on a Christmas tree, “ah man, that would be sweet. But uh, dude, how do we find crimes.” “I dunno, wander around until we find one?” “Sounds like a plan to me.” Excited over the prospect of being an amazing super hero like the ones in the comic books that liter his room, Rikki Roxx jumped up onto the bed and shouted “May God protect us -- from guys who do the right thing.” Chris and Markus stopped what they were doing and looked in his direction. “Uh”.ok.” “It just felt right” Rikki Roxx justified, trying to save his embarrassment. “Yeah well, next time keep that to yourself.” Markus told him as he grabbed a few articles of clothing that didn’t make him want to burn out his eyes. “Now let’s see if we can’t find some shit to fuck up with our newfound awesomeness.” “To the Man-Bat mobile!” The three hurried out of the apartment with their stuff in tow, rushing to Chris? yellow and black 2009 Camero (Bumblebee style bitches). Stone rode shotgun while Roxx was rejected to the back. “So where’s the crime at?” Roxx asked from the back, poking his head between the front seats. Markus leaned his head back a bit, “I dunno, this is New York City, it should be everywhere.” As if on cue, gun shots could be heard in the background as people scream and sirens blare. The three wanna-be vigilantes look at each other, sweat rolling down in barrels. “Uh’sounds like the police have that one pretty much handled.” “Oh no! We’re the police and he’s shooting us! Help!” Again the three looked to each other, the gun shots getting closer and closer as they drove. “Yeah, certainly don’t want to be stepping on the police’s toes and stuff, gotta let them handle their business.” “If only there was some brave masked vigilantes who could save us! AH! I’ve been shot now too!” The car finally stopped, the men inside looking not just at each other, but inside themselves as well. Letting out a deep sigh, Chris and Markus step out of the car, doing their best to quickly put on their super-suits. As soon as they were set, the three rushed off to the sounds of the sirens, coming to a stop when they see a circle of police cars surrounding a bank. Just outside the doors of the bank, on the sidewalk, was a man holding a high-caliber gun in the air over his head. “You know,” Markus said, “I just realized something some of the super heroes have that we don’t.” “Bad ass secret identities?” “No Chris, I was thinking more along the lines of useful gadgets that help them from getting shot in the head by a semi-automatic.” Chris dropped his head, “that would be useful.” “Guys” Rikki interrupted, “he’s looking this way.” Sure enough the armed and dangerous criminal had turned his attention to the costumed crime fighters. “And on a completely unrelated note, I just wet my spandex.” “What the hell is this?” the gunman asked, looking directly at the three as they crossed the police barricade. “Is Halloween early or something?” Chris held his rhinestone glove up (hey, it matched the jacket), “Hey, watch who you’re talking to or we might just have to mess you up and shit.” “Oh yeah, and just who the hell are you?” “I’m the Storm” he pointed to Markus, “he’s the Stone,” he pointed to Rikki, “and he’s the Roxx!” “If you smelllllllllll what the Roxx is cooking!” “Don’t do that Rikki” Markus told him, “we might get sued.” Despite the hilarity of Banned & Exiled~! usual antics, the gunman found them little more than an annoyance in his quest to rob the bank and shoot a lot of shit. “Alright enough of this, which one of you want to be headshot first?” Pointing his gun directly at Rikki Roxx, the gunman placed his finger over the trigger as he aimed up his shot. “Bye-bye blood stain.” BANG! “Holy shit!” “That was”.” “AWESOME!” The gun dropped to the ground, the bullet resting in the chamber. The man fell to his knees, his entire body giving up any idea of supporting his weight. Face first he crashed into the cold and unforgiving concrete below him, a pool of blood forming around his body and increasing in size with every passing second. Chris and Markus looked at each other, stunned at the events unfolding before them. Rikki Roxx just stood off to the side, his knees shaking violently as they tried to keep up with his heart. “Did we do that?” Chris asked Markus. “I don’t think so.” Rubbing his boot into the grey concrete, he continued to stare at the body that laid motionless on the ground. “Can we at least take credit for it?” “I think so” Markus replied, scratching his head. “Sweet! We’re awesome at this.” The most awesome duo of GCW high-fived as the New York sniper unit came on the scene to check the body. Grabbing Rikki Roxx they headed back to the car before anyone bothered to logically question why three guys dressed in costumes were standing near a dead body in front of a bank. Once back in the car, Markus turned to Chris, “dude, I don’t think we’re cut out for this.” “Maybe we should stick to just being super-badass wrestlers then.” “I think that would be best for everyone, especially my spleen, as it has not yet became bullet-proof.” Markus told him, taking off the goggles and gloves that came with his outfit. “But Markus,” “Yes Chris?” “Can we still keep the costumes?” “Yes Chris, yes we can.” “Sweeeeeet.” And so, the three young heroes retired that day, vowing to never return to their form of vigilante justice; mostly because they realized they really sucked at it. But New York City can rest easy at night knowing that should evil ever strike, Banned & Exiled~! will be ready! (Unless there is a good movie on that night.)
