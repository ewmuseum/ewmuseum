Breaking the Rules

3 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-3-2005 What is it that we are told when we enter the profession of Pro Wrestler? That you have to be a man with really big muscles and for the most part also a heterosexual right? We have also been told that a “Family” tag team of any kind would have no success in the business because they worry too much about covering each other’s backs to be effective correct? Times have changed? In this day and age of acceptance for homosexuality people are seeing the “gimmick” of a homosexual wrestler used more and more by federations that seem too like to poke fun at it more than readily say that its cool. There hasn’t been a wrestler or a pair of wrestlers that were openly gay and NOT have it be the work of an owner desperate for ratings. There have been people openly asking at times when the day would come that someone would have the guts to “break the rules” and wrestle knowing that they might become the targets of people who would do nothing more than harass them or at least ATTEMPT to befriend them for no reason other than just to be friendly. Like I said times have changed? Its been three days passed since the airing of GCW’s “Fallout” and in many ways the reverberations are still being felt as an unthinkable feat happened this past Thursday Night when Joshua Curtis and his younger sister Ami Mizuno won the number one contender’s match AND the GCW tag team titles in THE SAME NIGHT! I know you are thinking *Big deal that kind of thing has happened before when people have won tournaments* and you WOULD be right IF there had been a tournament for the titles but with the timely disappearance of “The Guild” and the tag title match threatened Tiffany York had to do something to keep the fans happy and when she told Stevens and his partner that they had to face “The Bloodlines” that night it killed them and with them being caught off guard Curtis and Mizuno were ready to take advantage even though the match was still a very highly contested one with a betrayal that wasn’t expected Ami and Curtis capitalized and for the first time since Larry “The Ax” and Curt Hennig a family pair had won the tag team titles and they did it without having to tell anyone the truth! What is the truth you ask? Well Joshua and Ami are very much open about their relationships with members of their own genders even though no one really asks around the fed the fans know the deal and they respect it even without Joshua or Ami telling anyone in the GCW community! Joshua has been going with a young man that used to live in San Francisco, California named Zack Johnson and the couple was formed publicly on a program in another promotion when Joshua was just the manager of the man whom would become the Albany Champion in another fed and since then they have been inseparable, which a lot of the fans took notice to right away and applauded them for. The fact was that during the time they were together and when they heard that the owner wanted to try and use them for his own profit they got out when they could and to this day they don’t have to look back at any of it as a “mistake”. If anything the only mistake either made was Joshua not realizing his feelings for Zack sooner! And then there is Ami? Ami’s a lesbian? Yep and she’s quite proud of it! How many people do you know where you live that would be sheepish around boys but if a woman came up to them and showed them some affection they would be quite pleased about it? You’d be hard pressed to find one like her and its interesting because most people thought that she would find a nice boy to spend the rest of her life with when reality was that she wanted to find a nice young lady to be with and that is where Michiru comes in. Michiru Kaiou is a prodigy at many different things, art, music, sewing, etc. etc. and Ami became fascinated with her when the two ended up in a “match race” which always ended up tied between the two even when Joshua ended up a witness to the final match they ever had! So how did Ami end up teaming with Joshua? Dumb luck, no really Joshua was looking for some wrestlers to form his faction that is now called the “Army of Old School” and Ami being the closest family member that he had when he was going to do this was one of the surefire picks for the faction. And this brings us to today? Its been a few days since the PPV and Joshua and Ami have been resting up from the night when they wrestled TWICE to claim the Tag Team titles! Some people think it was a fluke that they actually won the tag team titles while others believe that they can and will be the team that helps the federation become something in the course of the next few weeks, months or even years and make the company more visible to those not associated with the federation. Couldn’t the champions before them have done that? Probably and then again maybe it was actually time for a change of the guard in the division to make it competitive again. Who really knows other than those in the front office of GCW why but the fact remains that Joshua and Ami are breaking every rule that they heard just to show that they “still have it”! “Master Joshua I heard good news about you and Ami winning the tag team titles. Congratulations!” Quatre said as he jumped over the back of the couch and landed on Joshua’s stomach, thus waking him up from the nap that he was taking. “OOF! Hey Quat! Yeah me and sissy won the tag team titles at the PPV. I wasn’t even expecting us to wrestle twice that night. All I know is when I was getting ready to go get my shower Ami told me that we had to go back out for the tag title match and I didn’t mind!” Joshua told the blonde manager and that caused Quatre to get a big grin on his face before tickling Joshua’s ribs causing the leader of the “Army” to laugh hysterically and of course it was loud enough for a certain blue-haired sibling to cease from her studies momentarily to go see what the commotion was about! “Oh if only I had my camera!” Quatre froze and looked up to see Ami with her chin resting gently on her palms and a big smile on her face as she saw her brother being “tickle tortured” by their manager and that was something that amused Ami greatly! “Oh don’t mind me I was just coming out to go get a snack when I heard you tow having the time of your life out here. I DO have a message for you brother from Zack-kun. He said to not be too worn out tonight because he wants to give you a “present” for winning the tag titles.” Ami told Joshua with a wink and that was all Josh needed to hear to know that he wasn’t going to be sleeping much that night! “Oh I’m sure I’ll be plenty ready for what Zack has in store for me. The bigger question is will he be ready for ME”!” Joshua asked Ami and her eyes got as big as golf balls when she realized that her brother had something in store for Zack! What was it you ask? Sorry this is a family show and I’m not going to X-Rate it just for you. Of course Zack ever being the sneaky one snuck up behind Joshua and planted a big kiss on his forehead, which caused Joshua to blush slightly and that got a chuckle out of the San Francisco native. “Oh I’m SURE what I got planned for you is something that you’ve needed for a while now.” Zack told Joshua and Joshua’s face turned beet red! Sounds like Joshua was going to be busy that night? Au Revoir!
