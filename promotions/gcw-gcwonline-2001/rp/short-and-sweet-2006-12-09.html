Short and sweet!

9 December 2006

Date: 12/9/06 Riding his 1968 soft tail Harley Davidson Shaman cruises through the deserts of Nevada. His mind seems on many thoughts as he rolls down the highway as the heat rolls across the road looming like a great sea in the distance. Riding the Harley through the desert looking like a Mighty Warrior saddling a steel horse of chrome he shifts through the gears as he continues to roll down the highway as the sun rages down upon the road. He begins to slow down and pulls into an old rundown gas station that is still running as he walks inside and reaches into an old soda cooler and pools out a Coke and walks to the counter and pays for it as he walks back outside and looks at his ride and looks it over carefully. “You have got to ride them or they look neglected. Some people treat them as trophies, but they aren’t meant to be as such. You’re meant to ride them and be free. Much like my friend Wes Sanders, I am glad to see him coming back to wrestling. He looks a little rusty and worse for wear. If anyone can get himself back into shape and back on track its Wes. I have confidence in my friend, but he also knows I won’t give him anything less than my best.” Shaman reaches down and opens one of his sadly bags and pulls out a mask. A mask made of earthen materials and clay. He holds it up and grins. “I was once known as Famine the first Horseman of Apocalypse. Wes Sanders knows this. He has seen the darker sides of me. The side that hurts people who get in my way. The side that crushes people who think they are better than I am.” Shaman puts the mask back into the saddle bag and then “lans against he ride slightly as he looks out down the road. “What will the road hold for me as I progress down the road into the future. It is known to many that I am dangerous and many in Fuse are beginning to see this. I am dangerous and nobody gets in my way any longer.” Walking around his ride he inspects the tires and the chain as he then sits on the bike and kicks up the kicks her over and starting her with a loud roar he then leans back. He revs her up a few times and grins. “The game is afoot gentlemen and when the time comes there is something at the end of the road I want. There is a fight I must be getting to and in the end there shall be something very interesting.” Shaman roars off down the road and disappears into the heat rolling over the road.
