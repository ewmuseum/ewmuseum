The Forced Stork: Part 2

19 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/19/06 Saturday 6:37 A.M. Ding. The seatbelt light came on signaling the near end of a ten hour flight to Berlin, Germany. A ten hour flight of doubt, second guessing and, ultimately, fear for Eva VanLorne. Mechanically pulling her seatbelt on, Eva cast her eyes out the window next to her taking in the approaching view of Berlin in the new dawn hours. It was almost fitting that she arrive at the dawning of a new day. It seemed to fit the new leg of her life. A life she was still uncertain she wanted to take on. Sighing her eyes moved away from the view as she settled back against her first class seat to wait for the inevitable. ' Good morning passengers. We will be arriving in Berlin national airport in twenty minutes. I hope you enjoyed your flight and thank you for flying British Airways.' ' Guter Morgen die Passagiere. Wir werden in Berlin nationalem Flughafen in zwanzig Minuten ankommen. Ich hoffe, dass Sie Ihren Flug und vielen Dank zum Fliegen britischer Fluggesellschaften genossen haben. ' ' Please raise your seat to the upright position Ms. VanLorne.' Eva's eyes shifted up to peer at the flight attendant with a resigned look as she did as asked. Again, she peered out the window anxiously gnawing on her lower lip. Could she go through with this? Was she cut out to be a mother? How would all of this work? She had no idea. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Saturday 8:37 A.M. The car stopped in front of the orphanage five minutes ago, and still all she could do was sit and stare at the front doors. The building was beautiful. It appeared large and spacious. The walls were a grey stone covered with climbing ivy, giving it an antique appearance she could appreciate.. ' Miss?' She paid the driver no heed. ' Miss?' Finally her strangely withdrawn gaze moved from its perch on the main doors to his face. The look on his face was one of a confusion and concern. ' Are you alright? This....is where you asked me to take you?' She grimmaced and nodded. It was, indeed, where she had asked to be taken. And, once again, she was seriously considering merely turning back and going back to the States without Theresa in hand. Sucking in part of her lower lip, she looked back the building again warring with the decision to stay or to go. ' Miss, if this is too painful for you I can take you elsewhere? Perhaps a park? Hotel?' Eva's eyes snapped back to the driver. He was a kind soul, that much was apparent, but his thoughts were misled, despite his compassion. Sighing Eva finally made the decision on her own. Reaching down she withdrew a few francs and passed them to him. ' Please wait here. I will give you a large tip if you do so.' Money was the common language in any country. Nodding he leaned forward and flicked off the ignition to wait. Eva climbed out of the car and shut the door only to stand there and stare once again. Maybe she didn't have the iron will she always thought she did? Maybe this was a bad idea? Maybe Theresa was better off as an orphan? Maybe Eva knew she could never allow that...it would weight too greatly on her conscience. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Saturday 9:37 A.M. ' And if you will just sign here, I think we will be done with all the paperwork involving your temporary custody of Theresa.' Eva was sitting on the other side of a rather granduer desk with a pen in an aching hand. The process of paperwork seemed to never end and with each time she signed her name, it felt like her life was about too. Leaning forward she pulled the final paper toward her and read the contents. ' In the case of Theresa Snyder, Eva VanLorne is here by given temporary custody.... ....I acknowledge the responsibility of the care and wellfare for the child, Theresa Snyder..... .......Signed by my hand.....' Tap. Tap. Tap. Tap. She stared at the final blank line for what seemed like an eternity before finally ceasing the tapping of the pen against the wood of the desk. Mr. Dresher politely clearing his throat brought her icy gaze to his aging face as she straightened her back and pressed a trembling pen to the paper. Eva VanLorne And there it was. She had just sealed her fate to that of the child she had given up long ago. Silently she watched as he gathered the paperwork together and slid it into the manilla folder to pass to his secretary for photocopying. Slumping back in the posh chair, she allowed her hands to fold together in anxiety as she waited for the next inevitable step. ' I will bring her in now Ms. VanLorne.' She merely nodded as he left the room leaving her with the empty space to, once again, battle with the heavy doubts that were laden in her mind. A leg began to jitter up and down. Soon she was shifting uncomfortably in the chair. Finally she burst from her perch and walked to the window to gaze out at the cab which still sat patiently awaiting her second arrival. She could leave now. She could walk out that door and climb into the cab and be gone before he got back with Theresa. She could flee this whole scenario and be free of the doubt of having to care for a child she did not know. No. She couldn't. Sighing, she turned around at the sound of the door opening again and watched as Mr. Dresher re-emerged followed by a small figure. Her heart was in her throat. The blood pounded in her brain, drowning out almost all sound as she held her breath waiting for that first view of the girl child she had birthed into this world. What would she look like? Would she have blond hair? Blue eyes? Would she hate her? ' This is Theresa.' He pulled the young girl from behind him into Eva's full view. Icy blue met with glacier blue and the whole world seemed to go black around Eva. Theresa had her eyes. In that moment, all doubt fled. In that moment, her fears melted away. In that moment...Eva VanLorne knew that she would do whatever it took to be the mother she could not be when Theresa was born. In that moment Eva........smiled. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Saturday 10:37 A.M. Theresa was a beautiful child. Her hair was long, black and ended in soft curls at the ends. Likely, a trait from her father's side. She was tall for her age, much like Eva had been. Her eyes were the mirror image of Eva's and in them, Eva could see the pain and rage she had felt at the loss of her parents. It was not going to be an easy task breaking through the shell Theresa had built around herself. Eva knew this but, somehow, she welcomed the challenge. ' Theresa, do you speak english?' Theresa merely glanced at her before turning to stare out the cab window once again. Eva drew a deep breath and did the same. It was going to be a long hard road for the pair of them. Good thing, Eva enjoyed challenges. Thereas was more like her than she had first anticipated. The thought made her smile.
