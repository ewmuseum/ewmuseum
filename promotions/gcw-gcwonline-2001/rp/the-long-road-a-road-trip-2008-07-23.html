The Long Road: A Road Trip

23 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23 July 08 There is nothing but open road and generic scenery ahead. It is not often that he decides to drive to a show; actually this may be the first time ever actually. Planes are typically much more convenient and lately with rising gas prices, a lot less expensive. Not that Roberto Abitua is a man who needs to worry about gas prices mind you but it is just something that he likes to complain about sometimes just to give him a sense of what normal would feel like. Make no mistake, Roberto is far from normal. He presses down on the gas, not stopping until the pedal clanks against the floor. His engine roars like a lion and the truck jerks forward, taking off at speeds that you can only drive legally in Germany. Normally Roberto enjoys riding in his Diablo and trying to smash the sound barrier but this trip he felt like going in a different direction and buying a black 2008 Lincoln Mark LT was just the direction he felt like going. At 105 MPH he releases on the gas and allows the vehicle to smooth out. A grin forms on his face, he loves speed. There’s just something about the adrenaline and the thrill, same reasons he started wrestling in fact. He steadies the ship at 70 MPH and decides to coast for awhile. Of course coasting on the open road gives you way too much time to think about things that you probably cannot fix anyways. “Could you turn the radio on” You aren’t the most entertaining host.” I guess I didn’t mention that Roberto isn’t alone on this trip. Sometimes a person is forced to share a small, confined area with someone that you aren’t too fond of. Hopefully you are able to just not talk and pretend that you two do not exist or maybe you can just shove something into their mouths and tie them up so they cannot be a problem ? whichever way you feel most comfortable dealing with it. But what happens when that other person isn’t really a person? What if he is only in your head? “What the fuck Rob” Can you hear me?” LeStatt’s voice called from the backseat with growing irritation. Roberto glared into his rearview mirror, his icy blue stare meeting LeStatt’s black, lifeless eyes. Its one thing to be with someone you can ignore but quite another to try and shut yourself out of your own mind. “Since when has it mattered what you wanted?” Roberto spits back. “Your only mission in life is to sit back there and shut the fuck up. You’ve caused enough trouble for one lifetime.” LeStatt Knight leans back into the leather cushions, letting his head tilt all the way back as he lets out a laugh. Normally LeStatt is cloaked in black, intimidating I guess, but on this trip he is decked out in white with metal pieces scattered all over. On this trip Roberto was taking no chances ? Knight is dressed in a straight jacket. “Trouble is what I do. You can’t hate me for doing what is natural, that’s like buying a snake and then being pissed off when it bites your finger.” “I can hate you.” Roberto puts his eyes back on the road. “I DO hate you.” “Maybe you do.” Knight’s head remains back, his eyes looking directly at the ceiling. “But this has got to be considered cruel and unusual punishment in 50 states.” “Not for people like you.” “But,” He lifts his head back up. “I am not crazy.” “You’re fucking insane.” “Says the man who sees two of himself.” They both go quiet, only the sound of the engine growling keeps them company. Roberto’s eyes stay locked out the windshield and onto the road ? but it is not what he really sees. His mind races, throws around images for him to try and deal with. At the moment he cannot help but have Chris Storm on the brain, Warpath is coming and all. “I can help you ya know.” Knight’s words pierce the silence, destroying Roberto’s thoughts. “With what?” Abitua says angrily. “Storm,” Knight smiles. “I can help. I can make him wish he was never born.” “I don’t want your help.” “No you don’t but you NEED it, if you want to admit it or not is all up to you.” “Your kind of help isn’t what I need alright” It isn’t what anyone needs.” “Really?” Knight’s head nods, not because he agrees but because he is trying to mock Roberto. “Well I guess you just have everything figured out then don’t you?” Roberto doesn’t give Knight an answer; he’s tired of hearing him talk. LeStatt Knight has been invading his sanity since the day that Roberto thought he watched his brother die. Back then he didn’t have a name or a face, it was just a feeling. This undeniable feeling of rage that he didn’t quite understand. It wasn’t until GCW in 2001 that his dark monster got a name ? an identity. Since then it has been a living hell trying to keep it all in check, hard maintaining the proper balance. THUMP! The sound and vibration of the seat that Roberto sits in snaps him back once again. Roberto shakes his head and listens? THUMP! THUMP! Anger surfaces. “Will you fucking stop kicking my chair?” Roberto’s voice is low and calm but there is a hint of crazy hidden inside of it. Knight’s right foot swings back and lurches forward again? THUMP! And again? THUMP! “What’s the matter Roberto?” Knight asks as he kicks the seat in front of him again. “Am I doing something wrong?” Roberto’s hands tighten around the steering wheel and begin to twist. The sound the leather makes as it grinds under his fists makes LeStatt smile from ear to ear. When Roberto’s eyes briefly look up into the rearview mirror he can’t help but see the shit-eating grin plastered on his tormentor’s face. His head begins to throb, starting first at his temple but quickly moving to the back of his eyes. Roberto’s eyelids slam shut as he tries to block the pain out. The once calming sound of the engine is now horribly twisted as it is mixed with leather twisting and his teeth grinding together. He tries to concentrate, he tries to focus. THUMP! THUMP! THUMP! THUMP! His eyes shoot back open and the pain just gets worse. His vision is blurry but he can make out certain shapes. One shape he recognizes is of the trees that are off the road a few feet. He doesn’t hesitate. Roberto’s hands violently turn the wheel causing Knight’s body to bounce to the opposite seat and his head smack against the window, cracking it almost in half. Roberto smiles as he sees blood trickling down the side of Knight’s face. The ride becomes quite bumpy as Roberto takes the truck off road and he heads straight for the trees. “Die you bitch!” The truck smashes head first into the trees. The hood is ripped clean off and tossed away and the front end folds around the base of the tree. Every piece of glass on the truck shatters and falls all around and in the vehicle. Smoke floats from the engine and into the air making quite a strong stench. Inside the truck Roberto’s head is hanging out from the now broken out driver’s side window. Shards of glass have stuck into his face and neck causing blood to drip from everywhere imaginable. LeStatt is laid out in the grass and dirt mix besides the vehicle as he was tossed from the wreckage. His face is now a crimson mask and his legs both broken. Roberto’s eyes slowly open up and they find LeStatt’s shattered form. He smiles at this sight, maybe his first genuine smile in years. Knight’s empty eyes meet Roberto’s relieved gaze. “You win.” Roberto’s smile fades. “You die.” Roberto could feel death coming for him but he fought long enough to watch Knight’s black eyes go completely lifeless and his body limp. Roberto felt warmth as he closed his eyes for the very last time ? - - - - - - - But it didn’t really happen. Roberto’s eyes open back up and he is still staring at the road ahead of him. The headache is still pounding but Knight isn’t kicking his seat anymore. Instead when Roberto looks back up into the rearview mirror and looks at Knight he sees a wide smile. A chill runs up and down Roberto’s spine. LeStatt leans his head back slightly and releases a dark and low pitched laugh. “Oh I love what you are thinking.” “You don’t kn-? “Oh of course I do.” Knight sits up, leaning as close to Roberto’s ear as he can. “Life would be so much easier if I were gone wouldn’t it” If I were dead then you could go on living your life like you think it should be.” “Something like that.” “Or maybe ?” Knight’s mouth drops open. “Maybe that’s not it. Maybe you believe life would be easier if YOU were dead?” Roberto remains quiet. “You do realize that you have no idea what death is really like don’t you?” “Sure I do ? I have you.” “You just don’t understand, you never have. I am not your end ? I am your beginning. I am the shot of life that you need. You don’t want death; I know that for a fact.” Roberto takes in a deep breath knowing that he cannot avoid his other half. No matter how many times he has tried to do away with him once and for all but it never works. At this point he doesn’t even know if killing him is even possible. “What you are is some dirty little secret. A deformed sibling that parents hide in the basement so that people never know that you exist. I intend to keep it that way.” “It’ll never work. Not without killing yourself.” “Then so be it.” “Oh don’t be so foolish!” Knight’s eyes narrow. “You speak of dying like its some cool shit to do, like it is nothing to fear.” “I am not afraid to die.” Roberto says softly. “It’s funny,” Knight whispers. “How many people say that until the day comes when they have a fucking gun shoved down their throat.” LeStatt sits back again this time turning his head to stare out the window. Roberto looks back up into the mirror and watches him for a few moments. LeStatt scares him but it’s something he hates to admit. “You really believe I can’t do things without you?” Roberto finally asks. “I believe that you’ll fail miserably, starting with Warpath,” Knight’s head turns again, this time looking straight ahead. “You don’t have it in you to succeed.” “It’s always about GCW with you isn’t it” My life is more than fucking wrestling!” “No it isn’t.” Knight responds flatly. “As a matter of fact it is all you have.” “I have a family.” “That you never see because of your obligations to Steven Caldera.” Roberto begins to feel sick, he starts to feel weak. He doesn’t have to look in the rearview mirror anymore to know that those cold eyes are burning a hole into him. Knight always gave him a fucked up reality to reflect upon, a reality that he never wants to embrace. But it is reality. He’ll never stop fighting it though it’s almost impossible. “You’re right of course.” Knight says. “About what?” “Our battle won’t stop.” Knight smiles again. “This is our destiny.” A bleak thought but perhaps a true one. “Then?” Roberto adjusts uncomfortably. “How would you beat Storm?” “Violently.” Fucking road trips can be hell. And another thing ... Doesn't this all seem really familiar?
