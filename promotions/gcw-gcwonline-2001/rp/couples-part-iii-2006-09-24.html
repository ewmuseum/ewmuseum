Couples: Part III

Date: 9/24/06 ~KRAVE nightclub~ ~Sanctuary Night~ ~Aladdin Hotel and Casino~ Gray arrived with Blaise, Ukyo and Alexis after everyone had decided to get ready. Blaise and Ukyo were dressed in their best club wear, Alexis was in a nice evening dress with a halter top in a deep black velvet. Gray? Once he found out where they were going, it was really tight jeans and a new Iron Maiden T-Shirt. A casual reception was just fine for him though. It was not something he really had to deal with too much. The funny part though was when Blaise walked in and saw Dani and Josh standing there as Mr. and Mrs. Brand he laughed. Gray nodded to his brother and Dani, giving them a wide, white smile. He stood there as Blaise made his scene though. It wasn't Gray's night, it was the night for his brother and his now sister. Only a few things caught his eyes as he came in. Dawn wearing a zip-up PVC halter top and hotpants with straps on the side at the hip. And Alexis in her evening dress. At least the scenery was pretty good. Although Dawn fit in more with the Sanctuary crowd of blood dolls, fake vampires, fetish freaks, and average metal heads. Although Blaise quickly took center stage when he walked up to the newly married couple, specifically to address Danielle North... Brand. ?So, how much do I owe you?? Dani looked him in the eyes and then went to protest. ?Nothing, Blaise. You don't owe anything. Just let it stand. We did the deed and it's done.? Josh looked Blaise in the eyes and asked the obvious question. ?What was the bet amount?? Blaise stared him right back. ?The price of the preps and ceremony, as well as ten g's for the honeymoon spending money if you did it.? Dani protested. ?That was it, but that was before the purchases were made...? Blaise raised an eyebrow. Josh returned it right back at him as he then went on to tabulate. He grabbed Dani's hand to show the bands he'd bought. ?Well these beauties were four grand. And the ceremony package we saved you some money on by having our own witnesses, but it hit over two grand. And then you include the caps and everything...? Blaise grinned wide at him. ?I'll just round it up. So a check for twenty?? Dani audibly gulped if you were right near her to hear it over the sounds of the club. Josh grinned right back at him. He'd stood up to the bet and the challenge and he was going to reap his rewards... all of them. Blaise pulled out his check book and a pen from his jacket. He put the checkbook down onto the bartop and started writing... When he was done he ripped off the check and handed it to Dani. Josh reached out to take it. ?Hey... I think that's mine.? Blaise shook his head no. ?Nope, my bet was with her. However, thanks to you being a smart ass, it won't be able to be cashed until you take her to the bank so they can change her name on her account.? Dani slapped her hands down onto the side of her legs as she realized what he'd done. He'd signed the check over to pay to the order of Danielle L. Brand. Josh looked up at Blaise after reading the check and gave Blaise a look that wasn't too nice. ?Enjoy it. Don't spend it all in one place. Well actually that's the idea. You two just got to decide where you're going for your honey moon. Oh, and I'm going to up the ante. I already called Annie, you got the plane to transport you for it. Don't bother buying tickets for an airplane unless you really want to.? Josh took the opportunity to embrace Dani, but keeping his eyes on Blaise who turned to the bartender and motioned to the bar. The bartender, a woman dressed in some of the shiniest vinyl that Gray had ever seen motioned back and Blaise hopped up to the top and then moved to get everyone's attention as he motioned to a bottle from the bartender. As soon as he had the bottle, large and black with a gargoyle on the front of it, he tried to get everyone's attention. ?Excuse me! Excuse me!? The crowd started to die down a bit. ?Don't know how many people know me. I'm not exactly a full celebrity anymore. But my name is Blaise McClancy. I'm a professional wrestler with Global Championship Wrestling which is having an event here in a few days.? The crowd started to murmur. ?Ah, some fans? Or not. Doesn't really matter. You see, tonight a few of my friends, also wrestlers took the opportunity to use this good Resort's Wedding chapel to join together as a more permanent couple. So let me be the first to public ally present to you Mr. And Mrs. Joshua and Danielle Brand!? Of course the crowd there were very up on congratulating for their marriage. ?Now to the part you're all hoping about. Yes, tonight in celebration, there is free alcohol. However, I'm the one buying. And the McClancy's have a sponsorship deal...? He smiled to everyone. ?So, if you're drinking the Arrogant Bastard, or you're willing to try it, I'm dropping the ducats for your part of the celebration! So this bartender better start filling this bar with bottles, cause I just bought all of them in the building as far as I'm concerned.? With that, Blaise hopped down back into the area next to the others and popped the top on a bottle himself, and handed it to Ukyo who smirked at him. He then grabbed his own bottle and they started talking with Josh, Dani, Lia and Virgil. Gray shook his head. He wasn't too up on the public partying. It was time to become a wallflower. He looked at one o the bottles and remembered that the stuff in those bottles at one time was an even worse demon then what he'd become. But could one really hurt him? He reached out, took a bottle of the Arrogant Bastard Ale, right as the bartender popped it's cap off and smiled wide at him as he took it. He raised it slightly and nodded to her, but then walked away. He wanted to find a place that was quiet and didn't smell. He found that place over by where the clubs balconies opened up over the parking lot. They had a forced air feeder on top for during the summer to keep the hot air out. But also a heater in it for the winter. Or course there were doors, but people at these clubs sometimes forget how to operate them. He found his spot and moved to claim it as he started to drink from the bottle. He go through the first bottle while watching everyone, including Alexis drink. Although he noticed that Alex was staying close to his brother, who, along with Dani, was buying her different drinks. He'd recognized this type of behavior... she'd never been to a bar like this before and actually 'partied' like the rest of the sheep. Funny he'd call them sheep, when he was the wolf here. He finished one bottle pretty quickly, and as one of the waitresses walked by, he took another one off of her tray. What could two hurt? It wasn't every day his only brother got married... Gray leaned up against the wall, putting one foot up behind him for stability and brought the large brown bottle of Blaise's choice drink to his lips. It had been years, eight of them to be exact since he'd done anything more then a lite beer on occasion. One taste told him that he was in for at least heart burn tonight as the liquid had a slight burn to it. He surveyed the crowd, taking in the laughter and happiness that the others seemed to be having. And the take that Blaise had lost the bet with Dani and that Josh had actually gone through with it. For him, he was happy in his own way. But all the same, it reminded him of his own short comings and demons that he could not exercise, no matter how hard he tried. It was now or never really. Sucking up her courage, Dawn excused herself from Ukyo's company and cut her way across the crowded club. The heavy heels of her black boots beat a staccato rhythm against the floor and stairs as she ascended to where Gray was watching the proceedings alone. She coughed lightly to get his attention and then cocked her head towards the nearest balcony. 'Come talk with me?' He nodded slightly and took another drink from the bottle as he pulled his bulk up off of the wall. He stepped in behind her as she walked in and then took up a position leaning on the railing overlooking the large parking garage that was currently secured off for it's renovations. She took up a spot between them, closing a little personal distance and leaning her back against the rail, alternating looking back into the club and sidelong at him. 'You're hitting it pretty hard tonight, are you okay?' 'Yeah, I'm fine. Just mulling things in my mind. Was kind of a shock to get home from a meeting with Annie and finding out that Blaise and Dani got into a bet that she couldn't' get my brother to commit finally. Although guess it's good to see that he did. But I would have been like Blaise and lost money on it. Although I was half expecting it to be regular Vegas Fair for the club. Wasn't expecting it to fit right in with all of us misfits.' 'Heh, I know what you mean. It's a neat place though. And hey, it's not like we get a chance to dress like this all the time right?' She did a slow spin, showing off her recent purchases. He took in the full view and held back the smile he felt well up from inside. It was one of those moments he had to remind himself about what they had talked about that night a year ago when they'd gone out to a late night dinner after a Global Show. However, the compliment came out, just like it did the last time she'd dressed up. It wasn't often dawn got dolled up. 'Defiantly an eye catcher there. I'm going to assume you found the 'Broken Leash' over on Desert Inn. Although I didn't fully know you had that much ink.' 'Yeah, Viv took me shopping for something she wouldn't be embarrassed to be seen in public with me.' She grinned, 'Yeah it's hard to see with everything in the ring sometime, but a lot of my back is done up.' She paused, looking to see no one was walking by the entryway. 'Did you want to see the rest?' He took another sip and then put the bottle down. 'My brother gets married, and I get a fashion show. You should know that I'd have to hit myself over the head with this bottle if I said no.' Trying to stifle a blush she turned her back to him, reaching up to draw down the zipper on the front of her PVC top. Keeping her arms firmly crossed she let the top slip to her lower back, exposing her pale shoulders and upper back. A Chinese dragon wound down the length of most of her spine, done over a series of jagged tribal markings that spread across her shoulder blades and around her sides to disappear from sight. He only said one word. 'Exquisite.? She looked back over her shoulder, grinning. 'You like it? It hurt like a bitch but I'm really proud of it.' He nodded. 'You should... . I've got very little on my back, but then again, you'd know that. I don't get in trouble and have tabloids trying to destroy my reputation if I go topless somewhere. Although you should really not hide it as much as you do. ' She shrugged the top back on, zipping it back up before turning around. As she did that, she didn't notice as another person had come within listening range just inside the archway of the balcony. Alexis Day had found herself looking for Gray and had seen the two of them step out there. It took her a few moments, and then she got the courage to go and check out what he and Dawn were doing. She listened in as Dawn continued. 'It's not really intentional, I just prefer functional over revealing most of the time.' 'True. You still shouldn't go out of your way to hide it. It makes you unique and you. Was that done in Japan?' 'Nope, got it done in San Fransisco when I turned twenty one. Sort of a happy birthday to me kind of deal. The parlor was Japanese though, it took days to get the whole thing done.' She watched him from underneath her bangs, trying to find the courage for what she really wanted to be talking about. He was looking about, he could sense things going on. But he couldn't quite place them. He could only assume the ale was getting in the way, dulling his senses. However, he wasn't going to show anything of doubt to her. It had been months since they'd gotten to talk for more then a few minutes, and over a year since they'd technically been in any sort of semi-intimate surroundings. That last time at a late night caf? while they just talked like friends. It had been too long if you asked him. 'Similar to the mandala I have on my chest. Took over a month to fully finish it. But it was for my coming of age after doing the longboat races as a gift to myself. Unfortunately I can't get anymore now that I've fully assimilated to my other side. It heals while they're doing it. Unless they wanted to use silver needles, which would be much worse.' She bit her lower lip, mulling over her next question. She didn't want to be too forward, but at the same time...she didn't want to leave any doubt either. 'Can I see it?' 'Of course.' He smiled and moved to take off his Iron Maiden T-Shirt, pulling it over his head. There was no real hiding it. It covered his sternum and the area a good six inches around it on his chest and abdomen. It was a mandala shield, traditional tribal design wiht a wolf howling at the moon on the center part. A couple of feathers came off the side going down on either side of his washboard stomach. Do or die. Swallowing her nervousness she stepped close to him, gently placing one hand on the tatoo, her fingers on his skin. She looking up, face inches from his. 'Did it hurt?' 'Second worst pain I ever felt in my life. Over eighteen hours of work over four weeks.' 'Ouch.' She didn't move, didn't step away. 'What was the first?' He took his hand and grabbed hers. He positioned her fingers so that her index finger was extended out. Once she was pointing, he moved her finger to a point where it was right on top of the moon in the design. She could feel the rough skin that was there, a scar about an inch in diameter. 'That was.' Gently she circled her finger around the rough tissue, her face a mask of concern. 'Somebody shot you?' He actually chuckled at that. It had been years ago, and things had changed so much since then. 'Yeah, he's in the other room playing suck face with a Maxim Model that he just gave our family name to. But I can't blame him. He had a huge animal causing havoc with the livestock and he'd gone out to check on it. He grabbed his hunting rifle and shot. he was too close and it went through me. He also has a tattoo on his stomach, the tribal skull demon covering what I did to him. We got them at the same time as kind of a truce in understanding what happened. If he hadn't used a silver tipped bullet, I would not have a brother right now. In the meantime, we both got some conversation pieces.' She couldn't help but laugh, shaking her head. 'That's a helluva way to bond with your brother...' The smile slipped from her face slowly and she took a step back, just enough so that she wasn't almost pressed against him. 'Uhm... I don't really know how to say what I brought you out here to say, so I'm going to just say it.' She took a deep breath. 'I like you. A lot. I mean really like you. I know I've been a bitch and I know I've been mean and I haven't said anything but I was hoping that maybe...you'd give me a second chance...so that we could give it a chance.' She looked down at the toes of her boots, feeling an unfamiliar sensation of nervousness and shyness well up from her stomach. He couldn't help but feel shock. Every time he'd seen her he'd wanted to say the same things. But that past night with her, one of the things they'd talked about was how all the other guys had always been assholes and only wanted certain things from her. Everyone else, from the asshole who'd abused her that Gray had read up on in his file, and thus hers... to Almasy when he was wearing the latex suit of Emoticon. However, as he looked her up and down, immediately thinking two school boy words of 'fuck yeah' in his head at first, the logic centers took over when he hit one part of her attire that reminded him of things. The collar that she'd given Lia to put on her. The mark of commitment between the two of them. However, for a moment, he remained silent trying to take in everything. In his mind was the comments from the tabloids about her recently being seen in clubs, to other rag sheets making comments about her relationship with Lia and the collar, to even seeing the spots on TV with the likes of Youngblood and Toad giving her a ration of shit over the public display of commitment she and Lia have. He could only fumble words at first. 'Uhm... I... I...' 'Well I mean, you don't have to answer right away if you don't want to...' She looked back up at him and then away again, 'I don't wanna pressure you or anything...but I was thinking maybe we could go...out for dinner again sometime, but like a date date.' It floored him, he had to admit that to himself. It floored him like a mack truck. A year of fantasies, dreams and everything had come to this point. But his eyes, he couldn't peel them away from her neck. For all she knew right now with his taller stature, he was looking right down the exposed cleavage of her top, but his attention was a little bit above that. He'd have to act with his brain and heart before he acted with another part of his body. He couldn't make any decisions that he'd regret, or that she'd regret in the end. 'You'll excuse me, but the ale is weighing a bit on my mind. Slowing things down a bit. Let me take it all in first.' He brought his eyes up to her eyes, trying to read and make sure he wasn't dreaming or seeing and hearing the drink talking. Nodding she brought her head back up, emerald eyes on his and a faint blush to her cheeks. 'If you're not feeling up to it, you can save your answer to a time when there's no drink...maybe we can do lunch tomorrow or something?' The pauses were making her nervous, some of the butterflies turning sour in her stomach. He had part of an answer now, but he had to ask a question. But it was one he had to word properly to not seem like he was trying to offend. 'Does Lia know?' She nodded. 'She's the one who encouraged me to come talk to you...you know, say the stuff I should have said a couple months ago.' He shook his head a bit. Almost looking like he was trying to absorb it all. 'I know you should have guessed by now that I've been under a similar specter. Months ago, no a year ago almost I should have just laid it out on the line for you. But I couldn't. I've caught myself distracted by you. I'm six foot four, almost three hundred pounds. I can stand up to anyone, but I've never been able to flat out tell you that I wanted have more then that one late night dinner.' He didn't move though, his emotions were still flat as he was absorbing the situation. However, he looked up, and away from her, if he eyes were to betray him, she'd have to make it obvious she was trying to look and read him. She smiled, a genuine smile that slipped across her lips. 'Funny eh? We've both gone up against genuine monsters in the ring and we can't say a word to each other.' She clasped her hands behind her, uncertain what else to do with them, and smiled nervously. 'So...maybe dinner after Dangerous Games?' He stood there, still feeling the shock, but his mind was made up. But he was in a tough spot right now. He had to drop the answer down and drop it down now before other things overrode the logic in his mind. He shook his head to the negative. 'I'm sorry, Dawn. But I think that might not be best. If it was a year ago, or even some months ago, I would have been kicking myself for saying this. But so many things have changed in just the last few months, I have to ask if it would be best.' Best for what? I...don't understand.' He sighed and then gently put his finger, curled under her chin. With his other hand he touched the collar around her neck. 'Best for everyone. I'll not be like Youngblood or that idiot Toad. This is something you wanted, something you desired to show how much you loved someone. But I'd have to admit to you that this is something that would cause problems with things. It would be a complication that would be best left untouched. Unlike others, I know what this means. It's a symbol of commitment not unlike an engagement ring or other things. Out of respect for what you and Lia have, I would never be able to bring myself to get involved.' He sighed again. ' 'But...I don't understand. It's an open relationship, Lia herself ENCOURAGED me to come and talk to you.' She rested her hand over his where it touched the collar. Alexis was still standing there, and had been next to the wall. ?Alexis! How are you doing?? Alexis had to act quickly to not let Lia know she'd been there the whole time. ?Doing great. I've heard you've got more information and that. We'll have to talk sometime soon and get caught up so I know where to go next.? Lia was looking over her shoulders, trying to find Dawn. ?Yes, we'll have to do that. I'd like to do that now. I understand you had a meeting with Mr. Childress today also. I'd like to hear from you and get caught up. Are you looking for someone?? Alexis wasn't looking at Lia now. ?Yeah, looking for my bodyguard. Josh and Blasie were beginning to worry about him out here alone.? Lia looked over her shoulder back at the large man who was grabbing another bottle of Ale from one of the waitresses as she walked by. ?He's out here. I was just talking to him too. He seems to be sulking a bit. You might need to drag him out of there and get some party into him right now.? ?Yeah, might take Virgil and Josh to pull him out of there though. But I'll give it a try.? Lia smiled. ?You'll excuse me, Dawn apparently left here, I'm going to go see how she's doing.? ?You do that, I'll give you a call sometime and while we're in Vegas we can see if we can get caught up.? ?Let's do that.? Lia then stepped a bit to the side and went after Dawn. Alexis turned and headed right for the bar where Joshua Brand was, next to the bar. She walked up and without looking at hims spoke directly at him. ?Josh... what do you recommend?? Josh turned to look at her. He'd seen Dawn walk out onto the balcony and had caught the glance of Gray taking his shirt off for her. ?Alexis.... I'm sorry. I didn't expect Dawn to...? Alexis looked at him from the corner of her eyes. ?I need something right now Josh...? ?I know, I'm sorry...? ?No, he turned her down. In fact, he shut her down. Said no, sent her packing...? Josh was floored. He spun around and saw his brother standing alone on the balcony, his t-shirt back in place. He was leaning on the railing looking up at the waxing crescent moon. ?Holy shit...? Josh couldn't believe it. His brother had held strong given temptation itself. ?Josh, I need some liquid courage right now. If I'm going to make my move it'll have to be before Dawn and he see each other at the event this week. He was floundering, but he said no, but blamed it on the alcohol.? Josh looked at her and then the bartender. ?You like the flavor of licorice?? She shook her head yes. ?It's not the best, but it's palatable.? ?Good... Bartender, this lady needs a few shots of Jaeger.? The bartender put the two small shot glasses down in front of her and she tossed them both back and asked for another round and tanked them back pretty quickly. She looked over to Josh. ?I don't feel a thing.? ?You will... you will.? Out on the balcony, Gray was just standing there, finishing off what had to be his third large bottle of the ale that Blaise had spread around the club. He'd done something that was coming across as despicable to him. But in the end... he had to do it. His stomach twisted, but it could have been this stuff he was drinking. He could feel his head getting light off of the heavy liquid. Maybe too light. Vertigo... something he was used to feeling every once in a while when... **crash** He dropped the bottle. Quickly he looked down at his hand, he'd never been a butter fingers before. But this time was something he'd not contended on. His nails were longer, much longer, and retreating back to their normal length. He'd just shifted partially to his lupine form and hadn't been in control of it. He muttered under his breath. ?Fuck...? His senses sharpened as much as he could focus them to, and he could tell he was on the verge of breaking one of the paramount rules of the Order. ?Hey, buddy? You okay?? One of the other patrons came onto the balcony, and went to check on Gray. Gray kept his head down in case he was still partially shifting over, and went to say something. But all he got out was low and rumbling. Not quite a growl, not quite a word. The patron turned back into the club and yelled. ?Hey! This guy over here is having some troubles! He's pretty big, he might need some help!? Josh looked over to where the guys voice came from and noticed who he was speaking about. It didn't take long, it felt to him like it was only a few seconds and he was there, pulling Gray off the balcony and into the club. ?What's up big bro?? Josh was under one shoulder, and Gray reached up and grabbed the other one. He didn't look at Josh, keeping his eyes down to the ground. ?I need to go home... NOW!? ?Hey, no heaving on my boots, okay.? Gray spoke slowly and sternly. ?It's... not... that....? And then the growl came out again. Josh gave the one word answer to that. ?Shit. We're more then likely going to need to pour you into a cab and send you home. Everyone else has been drinking as it is.? ?That's fine.? Alexis was right there, trying to help Josh in a heartbeat. ?I'll go with him.? Josh protested. ?No, he shifts it'll be bad for you too.? She returned fire. ?I'm his ward. Even in his garou form he's sworn to protect me. I'm your best bet in making sure he doesn't have Taxi Driver for a late night snack.? She was dead on the money, something Josh would expect from her. Although he could tell that she maybe had other things on her mind for this. If Gray was leaving so was she. But it was her life, he'd given her his thoughts on it. He'd not interfere. However, Gray was quickly becoming dead weight, and Alex would not be able to move him to the waiting taxis outside the entrance to the Desert Passage shops. Josh sighted the one he'd need to help him out. ?Virgil! Virgil!? Virgil Ambrosi looked over and quickly excused himself from the others around him. He came up from behind and took the other arm of the large man. One thing, Virgil as a tad bit bigger then Gray while in his normal human form. ?Can you help me get him to a cab? We're going to send him home. Alexis is going to go with him.? Virgil nodded and took more of Gray's weight onto himself. Even in a partial shift where Gray would gain more mass, Virgil was one who could handle it. It took them about ten minutes to get Gray down to the area designated for use by the taxi's for the town. Virgil and Josh brought Gray up to one of the blue checker ones with the driver sitting on the hood while he waited. ?Hey, this guy, he is not going to puke on my cap is he? He looks drunk...? Josh spun around as Alex and Virgil tried stuffing Gray into the cab. ?No, he's not going to puke. We're sending him home before he gets that far. But he's not feeling well. Here's the address they're going to. She knows the code to get into the gates, just pull up and let her hit the keypad. Other then that, this should cover the ride and your tip.? Josh handed the man a bill and the driver shook his head to agree to the terms. ?Very well, sir. I will take care of them. You have my word.? At that, the cabbie jumped into the car, as Alex got in on her own and fastened her seatbelt. She put her hands down on Gray's shoulder to check on him, as he dropped his head down between his knees to keep his face hidden if he needed to. For others they'd just think he'd gotten shit faced drunk and was about to heave, as the cabbie though. ?Hey, he better not puke back there. Should I get the bucket I keep in the trunk out for him?? Alexis thought for a moment, and then nodded the affirmative to him. It would help keep the ruse that this was just a man drunk instead of a man drunk, who could turn into a ravenous beast at any moment. The cabbie hopped out after hitting the internal switch for his trunk. He returned with an orange bucket that he handed to Alex. She bent down and told Gray to shove it down onto the floor, between his legs to keep the facade up while they got out of there, and he complied. Next step was the trip home.
