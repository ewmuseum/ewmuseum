Date: Timeless “What do you know about this?” I angrily choked the only known member of the Illuminati against a wall. This had been my father’s best friend but he also had the most connections inside. Gasping for breath he was able to murmur the words, “Nothing?” I tightened the choke a little harder, then realized choking this man was going to do me any good. I let him down and decided maybe I could actually reason with him. “Alright Chris, what do can you do for me” I just lost my father and you are not going to tell me the Illuminati had nothing to do with this.” As Chris looked at the young Vetra, it reminded him so much of his now deceased father. He started to cry but he knew he had to be strong for Peter and for himself. “Peter your right, I have an inkling that the higher up of the Illuminati had something to do with this. But the only problem is it might take me a little bit to get some information out on this. They know that I was his best friend.” Those were not the words I wanted to hear, he wanted to go to the cops. I wanted justice to be served for whoever put that knife in my father’s back. They had the audacity to leave the knife there as if they were leaving a mark. “Well the police are all over me.” Chris got scared by that comment. “What do you mean?” I looked at him knowing that he would not take the next words out of my mouth to kindly. “The police are doing an investigation; they want me to cooperate with them fully,” Chris was really scared now he knew if I had cooperated with the police the secret of the Illuminati would be out and that in turn would cause a whole how can you say it can of shit? “You can’t do that to us! You’re a member too!” Chris was really starting to get scared, he knew the secret was the one thing all of the members had left. “And why can’t I” I am easily excited right now I wanted to start choking him out again, I knew I was still a member. Hell I even know all the good things it had done for my father. But the point of the matter was my father was still dead and everything pointed back to a member of the Illuminati. “They killed my father, and with out some sort of retribution I am going to have to speak with them.” “What if I can guarantee you information” What if you give me a day or two and I can get you something to work with. Will that stop you from talking to the cops?” Chris was trying to plea bargain with me. I wasn’t sure if I could even trust him at this point, how did I know whether he was behind it or not. “And then what” I mean I am going to do the same thing they just did to my father. Then I become just as bad as they are. Sooner or later the secret is going to be out, sooner or later the world will know about your little clique and its hierarchy. Is it going to be me or someone else that is going to tell them I don’t know but you do realize the time has come for you to be exposed.” I took a step back not sure what I should do, inside I wanted to knock this guy out how could he ask me to hold back the investigation on my father’s death. The one and only true friend I had. What was I to do? “You have twenty four hours, nothing more, nothing less. You best get me results!” With that I stormed out the door slamming it behind me. I still couldn’t believe I was trying to make a plea bargain with a man that could out smart me on his worse day. But I had done it but I still needed to go speak with the cops. What was I going to say to them I didn’t know, all that I know is I have a twenty minute drive to the station to figure something out. Brockton Police Department 20 minutes later. “Where can I find Office Guy?” I asked the pasty faced girl behind the desk. She gave me the directions I needed and I was on my way to his office where he would be informed I’d be there waiting for him. Once he arrived at his office shook his hand and took a seat like he asked. He went through the normal routine asking this question asking that question not really prying but looking more for any information he could get that would link him to a reason of death. I sat there and helped as much as I could. Office Guy kept prying and prying I didn’t want to give him too much information as first of all I really didn’t have that much. But as he kept prodding I felt the urge to tell him about the Illuminati. I wanted to so bad and I think it was starting to show. I allowed him the satisfaction of coming into his house something I didn’t want to do but he was happy when I gave him a spare key and permission. At this point that was all I could do, I couldn’t give him the information he wanted. Well at least not just yet this time tomorrow if I have not heard word from Chris then I will be back and showing him the hidden rooms in my father’s home. I finished my conversation with Office Guy giving as much information as I could with not breaking my word to Chris. I shook his hand gave him my own cell phone number and took off. I went to the only place I could think of, my father’s house. Downtown Brockton. I spent my whole evening here looking and trying to find anything I could. I looked thru his most recent phone calls I looked at various bills. Nothing was out of the ordinary for my father this was all things he would usually do. Then I decided it was time to go where he told me he never wanted me to go but knew that if something ever happened I needed to know where it was, his office. My father back in the day was a good carpenter so when he and my mother purchased this house years ago he was able to create and office that no one knew about. Very few had been told about it, I was one of them. I started my descent to the secret room almost as if I was breaking the code that my father had told me, but the truth is I needed to find something, I needed to find someone, I needed to find proof. Once down there I went thru all the information that was spread across his desk. The various notes and what not all lead to him becoming a head honcho at the next meeting which was scheduled for a month from tomorrow. My father was finally going to get it, he was finally going to get what he deserved. And instead some jealous person took it all away from him. I kept looking around I went thru all his notes I went thru his computer I even hacked into his personal files that no one is supposed to know about on his computer. Well I found them, I searched and searched for something. Until that moment, there on his computer under a hidden folder was a Microsoft Excel document that showed a spread sheet of all the members and whom they were most likely to vote for at the meeting. The numbers showed my father winning by a slim margin against some other guy. I printed it out, this was the information I needed to find out who killed my father it had something to do with this election, it had something to do with the knowledge he posses. Who and what did this I do not know but this information is what I needed to help me find that out. I grabbed the print out shut the monitor and quickly got out of the house before there was any chance the FBI would meet me here. That was the last thing I needed right now was them finding his secret office. I fled quickly as I had the proper information. Now it was time to start making phone calls. Although I should give Chris twenty four hours, I wasn’t going to be able to sleep. I had to get right to work. And that is what I did?.
