Getting the fuel in the tank

14 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/14/05 So apparently I pissed in someone's Corn Flakes. I looked at the card again and smiled. Mike Hardy, I recall Jeff telling me that he was a former Cruiserweight and tag-team champion. Didn't look like much of one. In fact the kid didn't look like much of anything. I also recall Jeff telling me he put the kid in the hospital. I saw the sadistic smile tugging at his lips as he recalled that match...or as he properly called it, a severe beating. But I wasn't out to put Hardy in the hospital, or even to end his career, I was out simply to humiliate him and make a name for myself in GCW. And what better way than winning my debut match? I'd try not to hurt Hardy too much, but I was never good at making promises. I had trouble keeping them. I puttered around my hotel room for a few minutes deciding what to do. It had been so long since I'd been on the road, and without Cindy, I didn't really have a clue what to do. I supposed going to get in a workout was the first order of business. There was no ring rust on me, but it paid to stay in shape. If I underestimated Mike Hardy even for a second, the little shit would pin me to the mat, and I couldn't let that happen. I was the Machine after all. I switched into my workout clothes, which consisted of an old t-shirt and a pair of shorts and headed down to the hotel gym. I garnered a few looks from the other patrons, but none bothered a second glance. Either they weren't wrestling fans, or were, and after my tirade last week, didn't like me. Either way, they left me alone. An hour later I headed back upstairs and took a long relaxing shower trying to figure out what to do next. I had already called Cindy twice in the past three days, and Jeff had called me to give me the heads up on my contract and the deal he had hammered out with Steven Caldera. I could tell even by the way he said the man's name that he didn't like the idea of having to deal with him again. But I was the one working for him this time, Jeff was just acting as the go between. My stomach made up my mind as to what to do next as I finished my shower and was towelling off. Food. I glanced at the clock as I got dressed and realized I hadn't had anything to eat since breakfast this morning, and it was now going on five pm. I of course had my options of eating in the hotel restaraunt, or going out and finding someplace to eat. I settled on a diner two blocks away from the hotel. I walked in and grabbed a booth by one of the windows. 'What can I get'cha...holy shit and salvation. Mike?' I glanced up at the waitress and did a double take. 'Selena?' 'God I haven't seen you in...what ten years? 'Sounds about right.' I said to my cousin, 'It has been awhile hasn't it?' 'You're still the master of the understatment Mike.' It had been about ten years since I'd been to the left coast to visit my aunt, Selena's mother on my father's side. Selena was two years younger than I was, and I always had to keep an eye on her when we were kids before her parents decided to leave New York and moved to California. The years had been good to her though. She was still beautiful, even in the frumpy diner uniform she had to wear. Her blonde hair tied back in a tight ponytail, and her blue eyes still shining bright. She was about the only thing in this diner that was bright. 'You still wrestling? Last I heard you were working in Mexico.' 'Yeah, I'm still wrestling. Just got back from Mexico. Spent four years there with Cindy, I blew out my knee during a match and while I was recuperating my contract expired, so after I healed up I decided to come back to the States. I'm working for GCW now, matter of fact I have my first match this week.' 'Listem, I get off work in a half hour. So, if my big, bad cousin isn't too busy, I was thinking maybe we could spend a little time together.' Selena said, giving me a smile that she knew I couldn't resist. I smiled back at her. 'Sure thing. Now what's good to eat in this place?' I asked looking at the menu. She looked around conspiratorially. 'Nothing really. Just get a soda or a cup of coffee or something.' I moved to the counter and ordered a cup of coffee, freeing up the booth. 'Hey, you're that Machine guy, right?' I glanced to my right to look at a kid who looked to be in his late teens. Acne bloomed on his face like the surface of the moon, and his tousled hair gave off the appearance that he didn't own a comb. His teeth were yellow and his breath smelled like some small animal crawled into his mouth, took a shit and died. And had been dead for sometime. 'What's it to you?' 'I saw you last week on the show. You shouldn't have said those things man.' 'Why not?' I smirked, 'Every word of it was true.' 'Like hell it was man!' he said leaning back and proudly showing me a badly stained GCW t-shirt, 'I've been a GCW fan since it broke of from CWU, and I can tell you there's nothing wrong with that company.' 'That's just your opinion.' I said flatly, 'Look kid, you only see what Caldera wants you to see. You don't know Jack about what goes on when the cameras are off. The manuvering backstage. Having to watch your step because you never know who's lurking around the corner with an eye out on taking you down. You think what happens in that ring is a game? It's no game kid. People get hurt in there, that's what we live for. To hurt each other. And I'm gonna show those half-wits what hurting people is all about. I'm going to save that company from the mediocre matches it's been flashing into your living room and raise it to a whole new level.' He looked at me like I had just told him the sky was green and grass was blue. 'Those guys are gonna kill you, and I'm gonna be watching just so I can see it.' He got up and tossed some money on the counter next to his empty plate and stormed out. I smiled, I hadn't even wrestled a match yet and I was already doing my job. Getting the people riled up, and eager to watch me get what was coming to me. Well I hated to disapoint the kid, but he wasn't gonna see me get mine any time soon. I was gonna beat Mike Hardy stupid just for that kid. Selena came up a few minutes later and we headed out. We ended up six blocks away at an open air rib joint. Selena and I caught up on the last ten years in broad strokes. 'You know I loved watching you wrestle back in the IWO and SWO days, of course once you moved to Mexico, I missed seeing you. Although I did catch one or two shows on the Mexican language station at like three am or something, hoping to get a glimpse of you.' I nodded, 'Yeah, AAA did sell a few shows to Telemundo, but from what I heard there wasn't much of an audience for Mexican wrestling in the US. At least not full on shows that were all done in spanish. The most people got was WCW cruiserweight matches. But hey, at least now you'll get to watch me again.' 'So who's your first opponent?' She asked as our food arrived. 'Mike Hardy.' I said slicing up my ribs, 'Supposed to be something of a minor legend under the managment of Steven Caldera, the president of GCW.' 'Man, you must've really pissed someone off to get the president's golden boy in your first match.' she said, 'Don't look at me like that. Just because I couldn't watch you wrestle doen't mean I haven't been watching. It's all your fault anyway, if you hadn't decided to become a wrestler I never would have started watching. So yeah, I do know a little bit about wrestling. I just wish I'd known you were in GCW. I would have loved to watch last week's show and seen your speech.' I gave her the Readers Digest version of what I had said about saving the company, and how I was going to be the future of the company. 'Like I said, you pissed someone off.' I took another bite of my ribs and washed it down with a sip of my Coke. 'Like I'm worried. I've faced guys bigger and badder than Mike Hardy, and I'm still going. I'm in the best shape of my life and I seriously doubt that a milksop like Hardy's gonna put a dent in my career now.' 'Wasn't it you who always told me not to underestimate people?' 'Who said I'm underestimating. Just stating a point. I know what I'm capable of Selena. And I know what Hardy can do, thanks to my agent who's a former wrestler and has actually faced Hardy. You know what everyone else in the GCW knows about me?' She shook her head after taking a bite of her ribs. 'Nothing. Nada. Zero. Zilch. Zippo.' I said, 'And that gives me the advantage. All they know is that I'm an egostical asshole who came out and shot down their company in front of a live audience and a national TV audience. They don't know what I can do, and this week will be my oppourtinuty to show them that I am not a small fish in a big pond, but that I'm a goddamn Great White Shark in a fucking fishtank. I intend to make my mark in that company, and I don't care who I piss off or who I step on to do it.' She looked at me thoughtfully as she ate, for a few minutes not saying anything, allowing both of us time to eat before our food cooled off. Finally after several minutes of unterupted eating she looked up at me. 'You never did pull punches did you? Never worried about what lay around the bend. You just ran in full speed ahead, even when we were kids.' she said shaking her head, 'You haven't changed a bit cuz. You're still the same guy I knew ten years ago. Only now with more facial hair.' I couldn't help it. I laughed attracting a bit of attention, and quickly got my composure. 'You know, some kid in the diner who's a big GCW fan tried to read me the riot act about what I said last week, and I told him I really didn't give two shits about what he thought or what anyone else thought. Nobody goes into that ring to loose, nobody goes into that ring thinking that they're gonna get pounded. We all go in there with one thing in mind, hurting the other guy more than he hurts you. And I personally go into every match knowing I can win. Not thinking I can, but knowing I can.' We polished off our food in a little more silence. Selena finally spoke again after we finished. 'They have no idea what they're in for do they?' I shook my head, 'Nope. And that's just how I like it. I'm gonna take GCW by storm, whether they like it or not.' I covered the bill when it came and once outside, Selena offered me a ride back to the hotel. 'You should stop by sometme before you leave. Aunt Carol would love to see you again.' she said as she dropped me off. 'I wouldn't mind that myself.' I said, 'We'll have to see though, dunno what kind of promotional stuff the GCW has planned for me. If not, tell her and uncle Vernon I said hi.' 'I will.' I headed inside, and earned a few dirty looks from some of the other wreslters who were mingling in the lobby. Apparently I pissed in more Corn Flakes than I thought. I smiled to myself. Good. That was just the way I wanted it. 'Welcome to the Machine boys.' I said to myself as I stepped into the elevator.
