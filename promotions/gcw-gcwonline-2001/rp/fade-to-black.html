Date: Years in the future I remember the day like it was yesterday. Cold, bitterly cold. Windy as hell. Fall in November, especially in Belmont, England is some of the coldest weather you’ll ever witness. The wind strikes your face like knives. The cold shivers you to the bone. Snow has fallen, and the first snowfall stays on the ground well into April. There is no Indian Summer in England. Pure winter, nothing more. That fall according to the Weather Commission was some of the coldest to date. Average high was a balmy 14, and the average low was “6. That’s Farenheit. If I can go back and give you a little history, I promise I’ll get to the good stuff soon enough. My father is not very popular in this town. He’s got millions of nicknames. Here’s a few. “Satan” “Scum” and my personal favorite “Rapist”. People drive by our house, throw eggs, throw bricks, throw their deceased pets on our front step. There was one time when a bunch of punk ass fuckers from the neighborhood brought a cat they must have just ran over (it was still shaking) and tossed it on our front porch. They stopped their car and waited. My father opened the door and began to suck on the insides of this animal. The looks on their faces were priceless. Needles to say I snuck up on them and broke the driver’s arm. They never came around again. Anyways back to my story. My father has a fetish if you will for younger women. Now I’m not saying he’s a sexual predator because I believe 17 is hardly underage. What’s the difference between17 and 18? 365 days, that’s it. Anyways, the town whore’s name was Anna Beth. She was 17 and every man in Belmont had her. Every man except my father. And he wanted her BAD. To make a long story short my father broke into her place when she was home alone and committed the atrocity known as rape. However, back than there was no such thing as DNA. They were never able to prove it. She was apparently on her stomach and was never able to see his face. They assumed it was my father, but there was no proof. Everyone knew it was him, he even gloated at home about it. This is not the first time. I was born of rape. My mother was a prostitute, and my father was a returning customer. He must have gotten sick of paying. Back to that day. I was sitting in my room reading Necronomicon. Jason down the hall playing with his wrestling toys. My mother in her room ice pack on her eye. Father punched her because he came home drunk. This was a constant thing. Swearing, drinking, abuse, and in my brother’s case, emotional trauma. A fireball of some sort was thrown through our front window. I heard the crash of the window and immediately rushed to my window. I peered down into my front yard, and that’s when I saw them. I’ve read about mobs with pitchforks and such, but never knew they existed. Jason ran downstairs to investigate as well. Father, in the living room amidst all his drugs and alcohol, opens his glazed eyes, and see’s the rug on fire. He shouts some obscenities stands up, buttons up his pants and walks towards the door. Most intelligent people won’t open the door. My father is not that person. He opens the door, and begins to scream at the mob. When he appears in the door rocks start to pelt him. The next act shocked me. He grabs Jason and begins to use him as a shield. The rock throwing begins to stop. Father glares into the crowd and begins to smile. Unfortunately I have that same sick, sadistic, hideous grin. “Let go of the boy.” One of them yells. “Get off my fucking property cocksuckers.” “You are a rapist. We know it was you!” “Yes, and that young pussy drove me insane. Better than my bitch of a wife upstairs.” My father was beginning to lose it. He reaches into his pocket and grabs a blade. He holds it to the throat of Jason. His own son, my own brother! The crowd gasps, they have realized the insanity they are dealing with. “It’s your son Anton! What are you doing” You are scaring him? “Yes, my son. I am not afraid to?” I felt the vibration in the bat, and the crack of his skull. He collapsed with rubber legs, I grabbed Jason and shoved him behind me. The mob looks confused. I stare into them, all their faces focusing on me. I can see the aura, I can see their thoughts. They are saying, “He’s just as crazy as his father.” I am not known for talking, but I felt like I had to say something. So I say the first thjing that comes to my mind. “Get off my property before I unleash the depths of hell upon all your souls. Like the Angel of Death, I will appear at night and kill your first born, and chop him into pieces.” A woman in the mob fainted. The leader of the mob takes a step sneers angrily, than takes his original step back. “O’Neils, your time will come!” Well, almost prophetically, the case of Bubonic made its way into Belmont and decimated most of the population. If you look in the history books the population of Belmont was 602 citizens. After the Plague, 39. The O’Neils, were never affected. Oh, back to my father. He never knew I took the bat to his skull. My mother told him he passed out and hit his head on the banister. As I lie here, breathing what’s going to be my final breathes, I realized I turned into what I hated. My father. I was feared, I was sick, and I was insane. I lie here alone, no one is here for me. I am going to die alone. All the pain, all the blood shed, I knew this day would come. I am dying alone, and it was all worth it. Fade to Black.
