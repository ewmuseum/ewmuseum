The Path...

7 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7 June 06 There are things that happen in life that cannot be explained. Case in point, a man seeming to teleport from one side of an ocean to the other within thirty minutes. Since that night I have kept to myself, unwilling and not caring enough to explain exactly what happened. Sure I’ve read the articles, seen the TV shows, and checked out the talk shows but they don’t know anything. It’s all speculation, questions ranging from “How did he move so fast?” to “Is he really alive?” The answers to both of those questions are unique however. Answers that cannot be given in any simple way. But here goes nothing ? My eyes slowly began to open and yet I was surrounded still but mostly darkness. The room felt as if it was spinning all around me but I couldn’t be sure. My body ached all over, so much so that I could barely move right away. I had to give those muscles a chance to warm up. It felt as if I had been in a coma and it wasn’t pleasant. Slowly but surely things began to come back to me, events, situations. Bryan Mayhem. My eyes went wide when I thought of him and my entire body jerked upwards as I tried to get to a standing position fearing that he may still be around. It’s then that I remembered the flames, could still feel the heat actually. I remembered the building collapsing on top of me. I remembered the pain of it all. Which brought me to only one conclusion. I had finally made it to hell. Wasn’t too far of a stretch, besides it’s not like I wasn’t heading in that direction anyways. After all of the years of doing horrible things it was my destiny. I tried making out objects in the room but it was of no use, I couldn’t even see my hand waving two feet in front of me. Hell was going to be mighty fucking boring that was for sure. But then the typical sound of a lock being undone comes to my ears. I looked towards the sound waiting for something to happen. A handle turned and ever so slowly a door creaked open causing light to flood into the room. The light came like the sun, blinding me completely. I turned my head away, unable to handle the sudden burst of light. I could still hear the sound of footsteps walking a few paces into the room. It was then that an eerily familiar voice called out to me. A voice that I recognized but couldn’t believe that I was hearing. “A little too bright for ya Knight?” I turned again doing my best to squint and see through the light. In the beginning all I could make out was the shadow of a man. “Who are?” “Oh please you know damn well you know who I am.” Things began to come in clearer. The shadow turned into a figure which gave way to features and body parts. Colors came in next as did the rest of the details. I stood up straight and glared in disbelief. “Hello Michael.” He chuckled as he stepped forward, only a few paces from where I stood. Mike had always been a cocky one, never backing down and never hiding how he really felt for you. It’s probably why I respected him so much when we went to war. It’s also probably why I would never tell him that. “How has life been treating you Knight” Good I hope.” “Well Stevens look at where I have landed; do you REALLY think its gone good?” Stevens smirked and nodded his head just a tad. “This is true LeStatt, but do you even know WHERE you are?” “Hell.” I said matter of factly. Stevens laughed for a moment. He seemed mighty joyful for someone who obviously wasn’t in good shape either. “Far from it Knight, far from it. You are not dead ? at least not completely. This is the half-way house so to speak.” “Is this where I am supposed to beg for my soul and to be forgiven?” “Hardly ? I could care less about all of that. I am here to open your eyes LeStatt.” “Open my eyes” What could YOU possibly have to tell me?” “Look around you Knight, take a hard look at what you are becoming. You were once the face of GCW, a man so feared and respected that you would have most matches won before you even stepped inside of the ring. You had the most beautiful woman in GCW on your arm, and you had the World Title. You were untouchable; Caldera wouldn’t dare take you off that throne because no one could handle the load quite like you.” “Yeah and what is your point Steven?” I spat back. “That was awhile ago, things change.” “It wasn’t that long ago,” Stevens took steps forward again. “The thing is, is that you can have it all again. I know it and you know it.” “Why do you even care” After the hell that I put you through.” “I am here to help you LeStatt, why ask any more questions beyond that” You have a choice to make Knight. A fork in the road is coming and it is up to you to choose the one that will lead you to everything you have ever wanted. The fame, the fortune, the woman, the family, the love.” “A choice” What kind of choice?” “Which path to take. It’s as simple as that. One will take you to greatness, the other to nothing but pain and loneliness.” I dropped my head down, letting my hair fall over my face. It was all coming at me so fast that I couldn’t grasp it all. Where was I exactly? How did I get there? And Michael fucking Stevens? Why in any world would he be trying to help ME? I took a deep breath and swallowed hard before turning my gaze up towards Michael once again. “So tell me ?” “There is a war brewing in GCW.” He cut me off ? knowing what I was going to ask next. I only nodded slightly to his question. “It is time to choose a side.” Slowly he begins to walk a circle around me, I stay on my guard unsure of what to expect. “On one side you have your beloved GCW. It is the company that you have clawed to make successful, given your blood and sweat to. You couldn’t imagine seeing it crumble without a fight. Yet there’s a downfall there isn’t there” You have made enemies with every last one of them haven’t you?” “One in particular.” I said softly. “And she just so happens to be the ring leader.” Stevens speaks these words with a bit of amusement in his voice. “So Knight let us see why that happened. You sweet talked her, you gained her trust, and you fucked her like few men could ? besides me of course, and then ?” “I shattered a steel chair over her head.” Silence. I felt his eyes burn into me as I was standing there. My head dropped a little bit as a young child would do when scolded by their parent. It wasn’t something I was proud of but it happened and I could never take it away. “Regretting that decision?” “Yes?” “Feel that you could still be with Eva?” “No and I wouldn’t want to be,” I lifted my eyes again “I have the one love in my life, Mary. BUT I do wish things could have been differently with Eva and me. Perhaps allies in this war that is brewing.” “Indeed, well you can still have that you know” It is not impossible.” “How Stevens” How do you get someone to trust you again after that?” I knew I’d never trust someone after such an act unless under extreme circumstances, such as the bond that Alex Grey and I have made recently. But Eva? Frankly a women’s emotions and attitudes are far different than any man’s will ever be. Two men can beat each other senseless and still come out friends with a great respect for one another. This is a different animal all together. “So tell me Knight, why do you want her to trust you again anyways?” Good question and one that I even wasn’t completely sure about. Was it because I was looking for forgiveness in fear that my soul would go to hell for eternity? Could it be that I have had a change of heart? Guilt eating me up inside? Or was it more? I didn’t know but Mikey wasn’t going to take silence as an answer. “She can’t win alone.” “But she’s not alone ? she has a following now. One without you.” This time it was my glare that dug into him. We both knew that the group of misfits that Eva had assembled really did not stand a chance against the threat that is in front of them. “Are you kidding me” What are Mooch and Bammy going to do against Dawn, Shadow and Almasy?” Then I stood up straight. “GCW will not fall, I won’t let it.” “Are you really this righteous” Or are your motives more selfish? They say that people never really change. They just cover their dark side up for awhile. So tell me LeStatt, exactly where are you heading?” “Hopefully the right path.” “Well ?” He stepped right into my face. “Let’s take a look at those paths.” Stevens moved out of the way and pointed towards the open door, where the light is flooding the room. “What is that?” “Your future ? or at least what it can be. Let’s call this the path of light. Are you ready?” I didn’t say anything, instead I moved forward towards the light. With each footstep I could feel my heart beat become faster and harder. It was the fear of the unknown. What was I really going to see? Where was I? Was I alive? All my answers would come soon enough? I took my final step and was engulfed in the light. To Be Continued...
