Hey Diddle diddle

23 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/23/2005 It has been months since last Eva visited her estates in upstate Pennsylvania. In a way one could say it was a refreshing change from the hustle and bustle of the city life, but for Eva it was supposed to be an escape. An hour has passed by while she unpacks her things in hopes of settling in quickly so that she may enjoy a luxurious bubble bath and then a much needed peaceful night of sleep. Tapered fingers gently turn the faucets of her rather large deep pool tub allowing it to begin filling as she goes to collect her night things. Grabbing the last of her clothes those eyes fall on an object with a scowl. Slowly she trails her gaze across the title of that thing which has her attention. ' Women are Venus Men are from Mars' sat on top of the dresser mocking her as she grumbles beneath her breath something about not believing she allowed Mist to talk her into taking it with her, much less reading the damn thing. Still a promise was a promise. Taking it up she moves back into the bathroom depositing her pile on the counter as she grabs a bottle of Chanel bubblebath and liberally pours the contents into her steaming bath. Immediately the exotic scent assaults her senses with the pleasant armoa setting her almost at ease. Disrobing she grips the book and steps into the tub sinking into the liquid ambrosia with a soft sigh of grateful nature. Feet were used to turn off the waterfall as she sat back and open the book to begin to read, disgruntingly, of course. At first she struggles to keep the thing open, not really wanting to read the garbage inside. Seriously, who the hell would take any of this crap to heart? She would. Chapter after chapter is devoured as time passes and , before she knew it, her feet were pruned and the water was cold. Blinking, the book is set aside as she climbs out and towels off with chattering teeth. Bed clothes are donned and with book in hand once more, she was heading into the bedroom. Slipping between the goosedown comforter and sheets Eva settles in and continues to read fascinated by the knowledge held within. The diva is half way through book in no time at all, lips splitting as yawn emits signalling the time for much needed rest. Scanning the clock she notes the time as eleven p.m. and actually finds herself shocked at the early nature of the hour..at least to her it was. Things seem to move slower in this place despite the fact that to her it seemed more time had passed. Shrugging it off, that book is lain aside, body stretching forward to flip off the light, and finally she snuggles under the sheets in hopes of that sleep. It was here things went wrong...or did they? ' MEeeeoooooooowwwwwllll' Eyes popped open at the sudden break of noise which seemed to echo through out her house. Popping up she leans over and turns on the lamp that head tilting inquistively for the sound to take place again. She is not disappointed. ' MEEEEEEOOOOWWWLLLL' ' What in hell is that?' Scowling the covers found themselves tossed aside as Eva climbs out of the bed and follows the sound which by now is almost nonstop. At the kitchen entry she flips on the light, pausing as silence suddenly ensues. Lofting a brow, the woman mutters in German and flips the light off turning to head back to her room. ' Meowwwllll' ' Okay that's it!' Without turning on the lights she stalks across the marble floors and unlocks the kitchen door to look outside. There at her feet is the largest grey cat she has ever laid eyes on and it was staring up at her expectantly. Frowning she makes a shooing motion. ' Shoo! Go away, people are trying to sleep here and your yeowling is loud enough to wake the dead.' The door is shut again. Turning, as she thinks it will leave she takes one step. ' MEOWWWLL!' Squeezing those eyes shut she whips around throwing open the door once more and screams..literally. Startled by her screaming the cat takes off running leaving her standing there with a tired but satisfied look on her face. Nodding the door is closed and locked and she is heading for her room again. Once more she is in bed, curling up on her side after fluffing her pillow and settling in for that rest she has been craving. All is silent at last. Smiling she wriggles in deeper and closes those eyes in hopes of drifting off fast. Ten minutes passes by and she is almost there when suddenly at her bedroom window... ' MEOwwwlllll' Her heart practically leaps out of her chest as she pops up like a jack in the box and stares in shock at the window. ' Oh COME ON! All I want is a little peace in my life. Just one night..is that too much to ask?' Apparently it is. ' Meeooowwllll' ' For fuck sakes.' She curses and flops back down. One hand grabs another pillow slamming it over her head as she clenches down and tries to ignore the incessant meowing of a cat desperate for attention. Managing to drown out the sound she takes a deep breath and attempts sleep once again. It worked..for all of about five minutes. ' MEOOOOOOOOOWWWWLLLLLL' Completely losing her cool she throws one pillow across the room and, quite literally, throws a tantrum in the bed. ' JESUS! Can NOTHING ALLOW ME PEACE? I get it at GCW, I get it at ANARCH...now I AM GETTING HERE TOO?' Nearly in tears from the anger and frustration she slams the covers away from her figure and stalks to the window slamming it upwards. ' WHAT DO YOU WANT!?' This time the cat doesn't run away. Oh no, instead, it just leaps up into her arms which instinctively wrap themselves around the purring ball of fur. Shock is not close to the description she is feeling at this moment. Stunned? A little. Confused? Partly. Suddenly relaxing? That is part of the odd sensation that is building within her body as she stands there awkwardly holding this stray beast who has decided to attach itself to her. Frowning softly she looks down at it, the pair locking gazes before she finally begins to laugh lightly at the irony of this situation. ' You are as bad as Johnny B was when I first came to GCW. Constantly bothering me in hopes to gain my attention. Where he failed..you have succeeded. However...' lifting the cat to face her directly as it dangled in her grasp,' This doesn't mean I like you. It means I am desperate for sleep.' If she didn't know any better she could have sworn the cat grinned at her smuggly. Narrowing her gaze at the beast, she tucks it against her chest as the other hand pulls the window shut. A short trek across the room and she was snug as a bug in a rug within her bed again, and so was the cat. She hated to admit it, but the continuous sound of purring had a tranquil effect on her senses. She almost felt like someone has fed her a sedative as eyelids grew heavy, limbs went to lead and she finally fell asleep. As Eva slept the cat lay there staring at the blonde beauty almost inquistively. Getting up it moves to her face, nose gently brushing her cheek as if it was solidifying something. Stepping back it circles around a bit and finally settles down close to her chest with a heaved breath. One final stare at its chosen pet and it, too, closes its eyes falling asleep....
