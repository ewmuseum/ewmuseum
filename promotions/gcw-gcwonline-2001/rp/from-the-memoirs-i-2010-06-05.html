Date: 06/05/10 It's two in the morning... Along the horizon comes a mist... or in fact a fog or in fact is it a complete and utter darkness? Ask it to yourself as the reader. Is the blackness following you as you read this? If not then there is something completely wrong with your brain that you need to start to justify..... Along the horizon comes a wave... or in fact a tidal wave or in fact it's a full tsunami of complete and utter light? Ask it to yourself as the reader. Is the light following you as you read this? If not then you clearly want to convert to the WRONG way of life and you shall surely lay your own yellow brick road to hell... From the memoirs of Lori Equinox Every night I sit down in the middle of my living room. In front of me there is a large log fire, blistering and warming my soul beyond anything I've ever felt before. Ever since I had it installed years ago I have grown to love it and could not live without it. To me it represents the burning within my own soul. At times it is burning as brightly as possible, destroying a lot that comes within its grasp. Then though at other times it has burnt out, unable to destroy anything or warm the hearts of people all around. Underneath my lower body is a rectangular rug. It is very soft and cushions me from the hard wood surface which is below it. Once I get myself comfortable on this I close my eyes and go through my ritual that I have devised to detach myself from the rest of this world. As I exit this world I can go anywhere I wish to go. Well I wish I could but it would seem that my mind takes me to where it wants to go. For those that do not know, I have a problem with my mind, it isn't something that I can exactly keep under wraps. At some points I have been said to be one of the most loveable people there can be. I am kind, I look after those around me and will do anything to see everyone else happy before I am. This is the way I want to be all the time but I cannot ensure it. On the flip side though I have been said to be one of the most loath-able people there could be. I am hateful, actively seek revenge at every turn and would stand on anyone's head as a stepping stone to get to the top. This is the side of me I would like to eradicate but it just isn't possible. I have tried for a very long time to rid myself of this curse but to no avail. The biggest problem for me is that I cannot control what is going on with my mind. It takes over itself and I only get to see what I have been like when either I watch myself in recordings or people tell me what I have been like. This would happen more on my hateful side. It is like when people say they see red, their rage takes over. For myself though it is like seeing pure and utter darkness. I do not think about what I am saying, I do not think of the feelings of others and also what repercussions could come from my own actions. This has never been so evident as recently when I lost my eye. For months and months before I had even arrived in GCW I had tormented the man known as the War Machine, SurReal. I wanted to displace him from this earth, have him gone. Do I know why? No, I do not. He was there, it was the wrong time for him and a perfect opportunity for me to feast upon utter darkness by ruining his life. When I look back I was unsuccessful, as he is still here and with me in GCW. Now I have had to pay the price of losing my head by losing my eye. My past is truly catching up with me. But anyway. Back to what I was talking about before. This world I enter through no want of my own. There is a very large wall set in a barren landscape and the only other person is myself. A younger, healthier looking version of myself. I would like to think that this vision of myself is how I'd like to see myself. In my vision though it taunts me, telling me that if I had just stuck to my guns and ruined everything around me then all would be fine. It isn't really telling me these things though and I am actually telling myself this. Or am I just trying to make up excuses for the failings that have befallen me? I shouldn't have any excuses. The start of my life wasn't amazing by any stretch of the imagination but it could have been a hell of a lot worse. Now look at me. I wrestle for a great promotion, I'm still breathing and I am happy. The question I must ask myself though is, how long will this last? Someday I could snap forever but for now I must try to keep myself together, for the good of myself... for the good of mankind. End... There is no such thing as “I can't”. There is no such thing as “I shouldn't”. If we put in all are effort and truly reach for the stars then anything can be achieved. This is what Lori Equinox believes and will forever believe until the day he is unable to think for himself...
