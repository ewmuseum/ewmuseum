Live from Wilkes-Barre

4 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/04/11 The crowd was rabid at a GCW House Show in Wilkes-Barrie, Pennsylvania. The referee is still carrying to the back the local jobber that was just fed to Shaman. They keep murmuring as “Sick Of Life” by Godsmack fills the arena. Slipping through the curtain and heading toward the ring is one of the most recent signings to GCW, Dorian Ryan. As he makes his way towards the ring, Dorian greets just about every fan that he can. He quickly climbs up the steel steps and enters the ring, pulling a mic from his back pocket. Dorian: I hope that all of you are having a good evening. After the deluge of snow that hit this area, it’s great to see that there are many fans that will still come out to see GCW perform. Now, for the reason that I came out here this evening, seeing as my paperwork to return to GCW just cleared today, is none other than Terrance Kingsley. I’ve seen all the hype. Help end the career of Coral Avalon, one of the most dangerous men from SCCW….blah, blah, blah, blah. I don’t believe in hype. Now, I have seen you wrestle. You got talent, I will not deny that. You got that blood lust, I won’t deny that either. But I’ve been in matches here in GCW during my first two tenures that would make people realize that I’m just a little bit nuts. Dorian takes a step towards the ropes and looks out into the crowd. Some are sitting there wanting to see more wrestling. Others want Dorian to talk longer so that they can go get a beer. Dorian: I know you’ve got to be asking yourself, why did I come back? The reason that I came back to GCW is for the thrill once again. You deny that which you are passionate about, you will start to lose that which you hold dearest in life. I was gone from this business for over 2 years. I made myself miserable, I made my ex-wife miserable, hell, I even made my brother miserable. As much as I have “outside interests”, I just don’t feel the same way about them as I do about getting into a wrestling ring and performing for all of you. Now, Terrance, I know that you aren’t back there, and I can only hope that someone gets this on video and posts it to YouTube so you can see it later, but I will promise you one thing, pal. That you are going to get in our match a guy that has nothing to lose, and has found what it was in this business that motivates him. Dorian drops the mic as “Sick Of Life” plays and he exits the ring. He walks around the ring, shaking the hands of every fan that he can. He continues his saunter around the ring until he gets back to the entranceway. He salutes the fans and heads back to the dressing room area.
