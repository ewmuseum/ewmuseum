Pain.

11 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.11.2006 I don't feel the way I've ever felt. I know. I'm gonna smile and not get worried. I try but it shows. The memory of vivid blue eyes filled with rage and confusion staring back at her as the car slowly disappeared into the distance brought forth a strangled growl as Eva pounded her fist into the everlast bag. She felted drained, yet, she couldn't seem to stop herself from continuing her abuse of the inanimate object. She whirled thrusting her leg out allowing the back of her heel to bruise the flesh of the bag. A seam split spilling forth the guts of the workout tool to the floor below. A slow step was taken backward as she wiped her forearm across sweat beaded brow and merely stare at the little white balls as they formed a growing mountain on the rubber coated floor. She focused on that growing pile. It felt as if the larger it grew the more her pain and rage grew inside. She was a volcano ready to erupt her emotion all over everything and everyone. It would a devastating display, but it would be the most devastating for herself. Sighing she moved away and went off in search of the janitor's closet. Anyone can make what I have built. And better now Anyone can find the same white pills. It takes my pain away. Some time passed by when she finally returned with push broom and long handle dust pan in hand. Motion was completely mechanical as she set about cleaning up the mess left behind by the ruptured bag. How was she expected to survive the loss of something as precious as a child...twice? The first time had been difficult enough, but she was young then and knew she would not have made a good mother. Besides, what would her family had thought of such a scandal? This time things were different. She was older, wiser, and knew now, more than ever, the treasured commodity a family could mean to anyone. That small thought brought a shadow of a smile to her face as dwelled on the last few hours she had with Theresa after they agreed to forget about what was to come so that they could enjoy their last moments together. It's a lie. A kiss with opened eyes. And she's not breathing back. Anything but bother me. (It takes my pain away) Never mind these are horrid times. Oh oh oh I can't let it bother me. The childlike laughter filled the living room as Theresa and Eva continued their little tickle war. Theresa's high pitched squeal brought a warmth to Eva's heart that had been missing for so long as she poked at the little ribs without mercy. ' Say uncle!' ' NOooooo!' Theresa squealed as she squirmed. ' You can never win this war with the TIIIICCCKKLLLEEE MOOONNSSTTERRR!' This activity continued on for a couple of hours until both the mother and daughter were breathless from it all. Eva finally fell back onto the plush rug and Theresa came forward curling up into her mother's side. That dark little head came to rest in the crook of Eva's arm as the mood shifted to something pensive..then something sad. ' I don't want to go..' Eva drew in a deep breath holding it as she idly stroked Theresa's hair and stared at the ceiling through eyes that were slowly filling with tears. Releasing it with a shuddered motion she turned her head and kissed her child's crown. ' I don't want you to go either little one...' Theresa suddenly rolled over Eva's chest and clung to her with her childlike despair. It broke the diva's heart as she wrapped the weeping girl into her arms and tried her best to console her. ' Don't let them take me away!' If only she could fulfill that wish and not suffer the consequences. Eva squeezed her eyes shut as she tightened her grip on Theresa in effort to stave off the spilling tears. ' I would if I could Theresa, but I can't. If I don't let them take you now, then we would have no life. No peace. This is the only way we can be together in the end.' ' NO! We can run away! We can travel, like you used to do!' Eva choked back a sad laugh as she kissed the small forehead and buried her face into Theresa's hair. Everything was always so simple for a child, if only it were that simple for an adult. ' No, baby, we can't. That is not the life I want for you. I know it hurts. I know you are scared...but...if we do this then When it is all over no one will be able to take you away from me again.' Theresa sniffled and rubbed her nose against Eva's shirt before raising her head to peer at her mother with a child's trust. ' Promise?' Eva smiled as she cupped that cherub face. ' I promise, baby.' I never thought I'd walk away from you. I did. But it's a false sense of accomplishment. Every time I quit Anyone can see my every flaw. It isn't hard. Anyone can say they're above this all. It takes my pain away. The muted sound of her cellphone ringing drew Eva's attention back to the present. She was slow to become aware of the warm salty tears trailing down her face. Sucking in a calming breath, she brushed the tears aside and dropped the broom to go open her bag and retrieve her phone. After fishing it out she stared at the number flashing and very nearly put it back down. It was her lawyer. Releasing a pent up breath she finally clicked the send button and placed the phone against her. ' Hello.' ' Hello, Eva. I just thought I would call and see how you are holding up all things considering.' She scowled. She wanted to rant at the man for asking such a stupid question, but in all honesty, he had done nothing to deserve the receiving end of her rage. Sighing she merely began to pace as she picked out her words carefully. ' I am doing as well as can be expected, considering tomorrow could either reunite me with my daughter or rip her from my side forever. How about you?' ' I am confident we will win Eva. They have done everything they can to paint you as a bad example for a mother and we have proven them wrong time and time again.' ' All of this has me so distracted. I have a match coming up tonight that is already a slap to the face, but with this...I just don't know if I will have what it takes to concentrate with this hanging over my head.' ' Just focus on the face of the man who is trying to take Theresa away and place it on the bodies of your opponents. Focus your rage and you will do fine.' Eva scoffed as she threw a hand up. Damn the man, he was forever the optimist. ' Easier said than done. Look, I have to go. I have things to do to prepare for tonight.' He never got the chance to say anything in return as she hit the end button and tossed the phone back in her bag. A quick glance around showed the dojo to be near empty. It was the best time to get in the workout she wanted. She moved to the speed bag and began to roll jab it slowly at first, but as the vision of different hated faces began to fill her mind the punches began more rapid and harder. Snyder. -Punch- Knight -Punch- Shadow -Punch- Vivica - Double Punch- The cycle repeated itself as she continued to push her body to the limit, not caring if she might be sore tonight when she had to step into that ring. The pain of an overexerted body had nothing on the pain of losing her baby. Bring on the pain. Bring on the rage. She wouldn't let it bother her... ( Italics are lyrics from Jimmy Eat World's ' Pain')
