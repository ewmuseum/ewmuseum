I hear her talking some shit about me to whomever's on the other end, but whatever. I bet I could fuck her if I wanted to. She'd love that...shit! POW! Right into the display. Yeah, you better turn around. I'm about ready to say fuck this and jet when this gay guy gets ready to get in line with his tiny little basket that makes him look like a jackass, and stops short. The checker lady calls for backup, and this guy starts hussing and fussing about. He's going on and on about how unacceptable this is, this is bullshit, all this noise. Damn guy won't shut up. The line shortens, and the first two cats in front of me are on their way to enjoy their boxed wine or whatever, and this guy's still complaining, this time to this older black lady that got in line behind him. Says the checker called for backup, and that no backup's come, and he's pissed and the same gas he's been spitting since he got there. Finally, some jailbait chick comes 'round to the rescue, and says she'll help the next person. He looks at me and says he wants to have words with the one girl. I shrug my shoulders, angle to the next aisle, and start tossing shit on jailbait's conveyor belt. Jailbait up close don't look like jailbait, more like jail-hardened with the shitty tattoos running down her arms. A Volcom logo and the Batman signal. Classy. I pay with my card, I don't even remember how much, but it musta been a lot 'cause fuck, that's a lotta food. On my way out, fussy gay guy finally gets his revenge, and dumps his basket out and throws it down the counter. He's sneerin' and barks out "plastic!" when asked, and I chuckle and get on with it. On the outside, I'm accosted by hipsters forcing their fliers for their friend's godawful band down my throat and I tell them to get that bullshit outta my face. That's when I spot the blonde hottie sashaying that tight little yellow-clad ass and steer my cart in her general direction. I'm chatting her up and telling her about myself when she stops cold, lifts up the shades and rolls the eyes. She says she's not interested in some punk-bitch kid like me, and I should fuck off back to the sticks where I belong. Feisty. I like it. I tell her to chill out and that I'm just a scout for a modeling agency and I thought she might want to talk. She sighs, says she ain't interested, and I tell her to at least take my card and think about it. When I open up my wallet to grab said card that does not actually exist, her eyes dart to the Black Card and she changes the tune real quick. I just pretend like that wasn't my plan the entire time. Stupid girl. She talks up this hot new club and mid-way through her second sentence I tune her out and wonder what Lexi is doing. Don't get me wrong, I don't mind stringing these money-grubbing chicks to think they're on the path to the promised land and a future of yachts and diamonds only to leave them holding their high heels out in the parking lot behind a McDonald's, but....I totally forgot where I was going with this. Wait, who the hell am I talking to? Whatever. I tell her that I'll pick her up 'round nine and take her to the fucking club she won't stop jabbering about, which shuts her up. Thank Christ. She gets my number and says she'll text me the particulars, and that she looks forward to tonight. Yeah. We'll see about that. ****** We pull up outside the club. Some club. This might be the club. It doesn't even have it's name on the outside. What kind of bullshit is this now? I toss my keys to the valet and pray he doesn't reprogram my radio. He's gonna though. I can see it in his eye. You better not, asshole, or I swear I won't tip you. Well, I wasn't gonna tip you anyway. She's in this blue number that might be tighter and higher than that shit she was wearing at the supermarket. What can't I blame on that dirty bastard? Nothing, that's what. She's got an Appletini in her hand and I press up against the bar and scream out for a drink.
