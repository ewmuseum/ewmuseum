Trying to take what isn't his like a goddamned rat. Me, personally, I wouldn't wanna go out like that.

Date: Between Rampage and Worldwide "Youve changed, Frank." "Good change, bad change?" Red hair and electronic lights. "Bad." Fingertips on fresh scar tissue. "Yeah?" "You got violent." "Always violent." "You didnt get in one fight in high school. I never even saw you get mad." "Doesnt mean I." Silence. Nodding. "Maybe." "Just, look at you. All beat up. Thats not the boy I knew." "I wrestle. Happens." "You looked, heh. You looked bad before you got your ass beat. Cant tell me wrestling did that." "I had a good reason." "Mmmhm. Whats that?" "You really wanna know?" "Yeah. I do." "Long or short?" "Which is a better story?" "Both of em suck, I mean." "Oooh. You suck." "You suck." Tiny fist slapping chest. "Then tell me the one that explains it." "Okay." Eyes closed. "There was this chick I was with, Ashtin." "This chick you were with?" Eyes open. "I dont mean. Its not like that. She just, shes not involved with me gettin my face beat in, so I was tryin to just skimp on details. Yknow?" "Wait, if shes not involved, then. What?" Wrinkled nose. "Shes sorta involved. I was seein her for, shit, for some months in there, but like, January I met up with this girl, Erika. Shit was sorta rough for her where she stayed, so she started ridin along with me on, all that GCW stuff. Just, like, shit. Just a passenger for a couple months." "Were you two, intimate?" A scowl. A laugh sardonic. "Hah, nah. Nah. She was 17 when I met her, an I was with Ash, so I wasnt tryin to fuck around." "Youve got such a nice moral highground here." "Hey, I didnt mean anythin by it." "I know. Keep going." Back arched. "Shit. Alright, yeah. So, ends up, Erika rolls out on me, just leaves a message to let me know what was up." "So the girl that wanted to travel with you was let down that you didnt like her back?" "Yeah. Yeah, I know, but its not like I didnt see it comin. I broke up with Ash when E left, because. Well, fuck. Had no reason to not end it, so, whatever." "She wasnt really involved after all. Good to know." "Haha. Yeah. Im a jackass. Sorry. But, shit. I ended up goin back to Erikas, an her ex showed up an, fuck. I shouldve said somethin about him before. He. Hes this, like, twentyfive year old drunk asshole that was fuckin her when she was like, fifteen, beat her up a few times an just. Shit. He rolled through and." Chuckling. Chin to chest. "That was that, I guess. My face ended up fucked up. He got a few broke ribs." "Whyd you go to her house?" "I wanted to fuck Jim up." "Her ex?" "Yeah." "Thats not the sweet little Frank I knew. God. Whend you get so bad?" "Im not bad." "Uh huh. You dont have to lie to me. Theres no way we re gonna start dating. Hah." A snicker. "Girl, I l" "Dont say that." "Oh, shit. Sorry." "You dont have to apologize so much." "Shit. Well, any consolation, its more than I ever said it before." "Thats true. You do owe me a few apologies." "Hah, I dunno. Should be even, dont you think? I mean. You was almost married before you told me about it." "Nuh uh. You cant argue with women. We re right." "Cute." "Yep." She sucked her stomach in, looked down to her cesarean tropic. "I really should get out of here. Soon." "What time is it?" "God, I need my contacts. Uh. Six thirty?" Chin up. "You gonna gimme a call when you get a few more days to yourself?" "Maybe." "Can live with that." "Its better than five years, huh?" "Yeah." He nodded. She swung her legs from the bed and clasped hands above her head. Frank watched the highlight of her silhouette and fell selfconscious. Her grip melted, her arms drifted backwards to support her with pyramid grace. He lied like dead pharaohs long rotten; hands crossed over his chest, eyes recessed into his skull. She stretched back against her spine, twisted at the small of her back with an inaudible exhale. He breathed fumes. "You know where my purse is?" "On the couch, I think." "Hmm. Hah. Yeah." She turned one eye to Frank; he read in profile: A hesitation. A bitten lip, mouth shifted to her far side. "Wanna get somethin to eat?" She released her lip and curled it into a quartersmile. "Not with you. Think about what the neighbors would say." "You aint changed much." An arched eyebrow. "Thats not how you compliment a girl." A burst of laughter, faded slow. "Nah. Never thought you wanted your ego fed that much." "That is more your thing, isnt it?" "Still know me, dont you?" She shrugged minute and stood by the city window. "Were you serious about wanting to know about Lisa?" "Yeah." "Shes Adams." He let his head sink into his pillow. "How long you known?" "A few months." He let it sink deeper. "Huh. Well, thats a fuckin relief." He breathed. "No offense. Just. (Id hate to ruin your marriage. Id hate to be locked into somethin. Id hate to be a father. Id, Id, Id. Fuck.)" His brain fired, mostly lies. His mouth stayed tight. "You know." "Yeah. I know. We always make a horrible couple." "Thanks for coverin for me there." "Anytime. Do you still paint?" "Some." "Huh. Cool. Do you have anything here, or?" "Yeah, but. I dunno. Just gettin back into it." "So now youre insecure too? Cmon." "Serious, though. Its not worth a fuck." She glared. He relented. Sat up, swung his legs in lazy arcs and dragged on a moderate amount of clothes with leper's grace as he stood. More stitches that tugged every time he winced or tightened his forehead. Blood blisters, scabs, that dried and flaked away. Cuts that healed. Skin that mended. A life of itching, of getting injured and healing just in time for another abrasion, another bruise, another hunk of flesh lost to ring burn or another bone fractured hairline. The betrayals, the backstabbings, the disrespect never hurt, outside of the moment of contact. He couldn't even consider it a betrayal; there was a void between Terror and White where trust should've been’and Frank, even under the lights and in the ring with his bullshit name, knew something about betrayal. He stretched and there was a loud pop and he wasn’t thinking about his job anymore. She laughed and pulled his shirt down over her torso; he shook his head with an injured smile. "Aight. You win." "So. You going to tell me what I should expect, or you want it to be a surprise? For the next, well. Few seconds at least." "Be a shitty surprise if I played it for one, yknow." "Frank. Dont be a weiner." "Haha. What?" "Im a mother. Ive got to watch my mouth." "Hahapfahaha. Fuck." She passed him, swung the point of her elbow into his chest and hung her hand on the doorknob while he faked a recoil. "Come on, now. I dont have all day." "Not even day yet, Les." He remembered when it had come up years before. "Lie," so he finished the name and avoided conflict. She pulled the door in and waited in the short hall when he moved ahead of her, navigating through a muscle memory that failed to recall a table in his hallway; he had been away, after all. It stung brief, he trudged through while she giggled. "Frank, you have a smoke?" "Whend you start?" "I didnt. I just like one now and then." He rested his arm crooked along the top corner of a doorframe, low laugh rolling. "I quit. While back." "You know, I didnt expect that." "Killin my lungs is, shit. Sa little counterintuitive to my job, yknow?" "Mmhmm. And that wasnt a bong under your nightstand." "Hey. Was one or the other, decided to stick with the one that was fun. Sall." He sucked up his pride and opened the door to let her in under the hanging arch of his arm before following, before flicking on a light switch that brought white into a tight room of canvasses on the floor, lining the walls mausoleum around the easel center, and individually complete, maybe even coherent, maybe even passable gestalt but just, just nada; just sketches, rudimentary fever dreams of color, forgettable nonrepresentative flash fires compared against the work suspended. She approached, a careful heeltoe over the wooden floor. Her breath was soft, lost under the hum of the air conditioner. Frank, posted in the doorway, graded her opinion from behind and came on frightening possibilities, stumbled over dismissals and fell headlong into disappointment. It was still drying. Clumps, streaked. Thick oils slashed and maimed and hung to dry. "What do you call it?" "Nothin. You wanted to do that, be somethin like untitled in red I guess." And it was red, yes; a study in crimson and scarlet, of vermilion and carmine. It was all red but it was not all reds’it was the violence and the brooding that followed. It was the blood that flowed to coagulate. It was the fire that smoldered. It was the volcano that bellowed. It was focused. It was not an overview; it was a hypothesis and an experiment without the burden of having to hold a single conclusion or being restricted to a conclusion in the singular. He hadn't imbued it with answers, or if he had, it was a subconscious mistake; he knew art was as at home with questions and as it was foreign among statements. He stopped holding his breath when he realized he'd kept it down. "I like it." He nodded. "Yeah?" "Yeah. Its." She faced him. "Its a selfportrait, isnt it?" He sucked air. Guilty. "Howd you know?" "Well, that is most of what Ive seen you paint. If I cant recognize something from you, safe to assume its of you to begin with." "Thats. Yeah, aight. You got a point." "Duh. Youre an egotist. We went over this. Now, if you were willing to come by the school and drop one of these off, Id love to show it to my class and I know Paul would too." "Maybe." "Come on, boy. Those idiots just take it for an easy class. Theyd at least know who you were if I showed them a painting." "You tellin me Bosch and Brugel aint got name recognition?" "Of course they do, Frank. Of course they do." She shook her head, twisted her mouth into a wry frown. "Aight, I should be able to swing somethin by. Just, uh. Lemme know which ones before you leave. Shit, Paul asked to see somethin Id done so, I aint even gotta act all odd about it. Like, 'hey, heres those paintings Leslie didnt ask for after six hours of unprotected sex.'" "It wasnt unprotected." "Yeah. It was a joke scenario. Oh yeah, reminds me. You got time for another round?" "See?" She laughed. "Only ever think of yourself." "Hah. Im good. This one was gonna be for you." "Well, it would be nice after putting in all that thankless work for you, but. God, I think Im alright for tonight. Today, whatever it is." She was giggling and he was ogling and it was the same as it had ever been. He scratched the back of his neck, leaned back into it. "So this is you gettin ready to cut out on me again, huh?" "Yep. As soon as I find my clothes and everything, at least." "You want some coffee?" "Ack. No, no. No coffee. Blech." "Blech?" "Blech." She stuck her tongue out and jammed a finger at her throat. "You know. Blech." "I gotcha. Hater." "I only hate it because its nasty." "You smoke now?" "Well. Smoking doesnt taste that bad." "Except to people that dont smoke." "You werent complaining." "Only because you didnt complain back in the day." "God. Youre almost sweet now." "Dont say that shit." "Well, I did say almost." "Strue." "Hey, Frank." She sauntered his way in the windowless room. "Can I ask you something? Its nothing big, just, well. Can I ask you something?" "Sure." "Okay. Ive been kind of watching GCW since I heard you were on it, and. Okay. I know like, WWF" "WWE." "Whateve. I know its like, fake" "Predetermined. Not quite fake, I mean." "Okay, you know what I mean. And I know that they just sort of like, embrace it and go for the show and everything, you know. But, like GCW, and PRIME and all those others, they seem more. I dont know. Its not so" "Predetermined?" "No, no. Im not gonna ask you about that because I dont want you to shatter my little fangirl heart. And you probably have something in your contract to say its real no matter what, anyways. Anyways, hee. Its not so stupid, I guess. Like. A lot of the times, you people really seem, um, pissed at each other, I guess. Is it just better acting, or?" "I dunno. I look pissed out there?" "Not really. But, the other people. Like, oh god. The chick, and the big guy." "Viv, Shaman, yeah." "Yeah, like them. When the shows over, do they go eat and talk about how nicely things went?" "Hahaha. Shit. I kinda doubt that. I mean. Aight. In GCW, cause I dont know shit bout how the other places operate, the people that really hang out off the show tend to be involved with each other on the show to begin with, and the bullshit drama you see on the show tends to be the same drama bullshit we deal with when the shows off. Im not gonna say its all straight up, what you see is what its like, but, yeah, its more in line with the reality of the situation than WWE or TNA or somethin." "Hm. That makes sense. I guess. So, who do you hang out with?" "Hah. Fuck. Cant say Im chillin wiith any of those cats once we re out the arena. I mean, like. Before Rampage, when I was in Tokyo, I bumped into that dude Jay Terror." "Oooh. Hes cute." "Cmon." "Im just messing with you. Im a married woman, Frank. Give me some credit." He pulled his mouth into his teeth, sucked a smile down his throat to try and block a rise of dumbassed guffawing. "You can laugh, you know." And he laughed, restrained. Breathing loudly in the time and tone of "hahaha." "Come on. Laugh. Dont give me that." "What?" "We ve done this before, Frank. You do that stupid sounding halflaugh thing, I call you out on it, you deny it." "Thats just how I laugh, I mean." "Thats not a laugh. Ive heard you laugh. Thats, just. Kind of creepy." "Aight. Next time I laugh, I really laugh. That cool?" "Yeah. Thats cool. You know. I dont have to pick Lisa up from my moms for another hour. If you were serious about going again." She cocked an eyebrow.
