Changes, Part II: Gray's Story

12 June 2006

Date: 6/12/06 Why? Why me? Why would I be given such a tasking? That was Grayson Brand's thoughts on the whole thing. He had been Dani's guardian for years after the Order had found her. He'd been her brother she thought she'd never had. He'd been her trainer, her confidant. She was even the one person she had trusted to talk to after her first time with a boy after the school's prom her senior year. But now, now due to this other woman coming in, Gray was pulled from the assignment he'd had for years, and given as the guardian to an outsider. Not that he had any problems with Alexis. She was a good person to be around. Although overly dedicated to her task at hand. She was so loyal to Lia that he'd not been able to complete his one task so far. In the last few weeks, she's avoided him by getting up before him and working till odd hours of the night. He's even found her asleep in the back of the truck they have as a mobile lab right now. Gray could respect that. But she had come with only a few days worth of clothes. She was lucky that Dani was one that liked to do laundry daily if she could. But Gray was under orders to get her comfortable, and that included spending a blank check on her to get her some clothing and other needs. Gray also knew that this comfort came with a price for her. When the time was right, Annie would ask for paybacks. And that would be information. They've long been looking for a way to crack The Den and through them, get to the Society of Leopold who they claim as their founders. And from there, the only further path it would take them is to the immortal known as Cassius Longinus. That would be her payment to the Order. Mastercard? Commercial Knockoff time... A shopping spree on someone else... $10,000 Mobile Lab with all the fixings... $1,000,000 on loan 24/7 bodyguard service from a garou... $ at some odd number. Spilling your guts on the one organization that's hidden its existence of it's den's and caches for decades, if not centuries... that's priceless. But first, first he had to get through this day. He'd not completed his task, and he was going to do it... unfinished business irked him more then people like the Guild. But it looked like he'd have to trick her into it. ~Washington, D.C.~ ~Crowne Plaza Hotel (National Airport)~ ~Executive Suites Floor~ BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! The alarm clock went off and Gray reached up and smacked the clock... off the coffee table of the suite. He was used to sleeping on the couch and all, but with a technically strange woman in the actual bedroom, made him a bit uncomfortable. He'd taken to sleeping in a pair of shorts, to ensure he wasn't going to embarrass her. But it's not like he ever really saw her come in or leave much. Usually she tried to get up before he did, and get to work. And it was much later then he was done that she'd finally make her way to bed, after ordering out for pizza or something. But this morning, Grayson Brand had a plan, and that entailed getting up early. He was going to make sure she wasn't in the lab already. He'd been trying to figure out the ideal time to get up. And he'd figured out she as running on basically only four hours or so of sleep a night. They had arrived late last night, on purpose. That way she couldn't start using the lab early. After a dinner of room service, it was off to bed, and now Gray had beaten her to the punch. So to speak. First things first, he went over to the coffee pot and started it brewing. He'd prepared the grinds the night before and filled it, so that it would be a pretty quick evolution this morning. As soon as the small two cup pot was filled with the magical brown liquid, he get the cups ready. Then came the tricky part. He only filled her cup about half way, and then poured in enough cream and sugar to fill the cup the rest of the way. He'd observed her doing this. ?Guess this is called a caffeine and sugar rush, a wake up call that's for sure.? He picked up both cups and then went walking towards the door to the bedroom, that was cracked open slightly. As he stepped in, he appeared to almost trip on something. Setting both cups down on the dresser he then reaches down to the ground to move what ever it was. His hands come back up with the clothes she was wearing last night before they headed off to sleep. His eyes adjust quickly to the increased darkness and he looks over at her laying in the bed... The dyed red hair, her face, and a lone arm that has the sleeve of a t-shirt covering the shoulder. He sighs in relief of the one thing that could have faltered this plan, not knowing if she had any modesty when sleeping. He tossed the pile of clothes out of the way of the door, over to the side and picked the cups of coffee back up. He set both of them down onto the night stand and then reached out to touch her shoulder. ?Alexis... Alexis...? He shook her shoulder a bit, and that was enough... ?WHAT?? She yelled it as she shot up, out from under the covers and to the other side of the bed. Gray cursed himself for not expecting someone who was trained as a soldier, a warrior, to not react to being touched while asleep. She always seemed so small and fragile to him, he forgot that she was actually a trained killing machine. He tried to look her over to make sure she was okay, after shooting awake like that. She was in a t-shirt on top, but then he got down to the bottom part of her. It was covered, but just barely. Things could be said about how a woman is from just that small glimpse. Needless to say, both of their faces turned shades redder then they normally were. Alexis quickly grabbed a pillow and covered her lap with it. ?What do you want?? Gray tried to recover. It was bad enough he had to do this, and how he was having to trap her into this all. But there were things that even his analytical mind didn't really think about. He quickly, but cautiously picked up her cup of coffee and handed it over to her. ?Here, have some wake up juice.? She took it, managing to keep herself covered with the pillow. ?To what do I owe this?? ?You've been working too hard. So I've trapped you a bit.? ?Trapped me? What?? ?I've got the keys to the lab. And unless you think you can take them from me, you're taking today off.? Alexis gave him a look like she thought he'd been smoking something. ?I've been trying now for two weeks to get you to take a day off. I'm under orders for a few things. And they're going to get done today.? She took a sip of the hot coffee, but immediately started protesting. ?I've got work to do.? ?No, you don't. You've gone through all of the samples the Order has. Annie is trying to get us some fresh samples that are untainted by the spirit blades powers. You're burning oil, and I've got another assignment.? She looked confused. ?What's that?? ?You.? ?Huh?? ?You came here with three sets of clothes. If not for Dani washing them every day with her own clothes, you'd be quite ripe right now, shower or not. I've been ordered to take you out, and take you shopping, and make sure you're comfortable being here.? ?But I don't...? ?You don't understand. You don't have much of a choice in this.? She took another drink, and studied him for a few moments. She caught his silvery gray eyes, and then worked her way down to the large tattoo on his chest, the mandala with the howling wolf and moon that covered most of his naturally smooth chest. To herself she thought, ?Well, guess it's true what they say about Natives and having to shave.? And then she blushed a bit in the shadow covered room. She was lingering, she knew it. This was going to get really interesting. She knew that Virgil had caught her lingering in her glances at him a few times back at the estate. But this was something else all together. ?Okay, I'll take today off. But how about I just go out and do it by myself. I'm perfectly capable.? Gray shook his head to the negative. ?No, I'm your guardian now. Where you go, I go. Get used to it. Plus, the card that Annie sent me, it has my name on it. Sorry.? She looked a bit worried about it all. He decided to put his foot down. ?So, here's how it's going to work today. You're going to get some pants on, and then you and I are going down and meet a cab. Then we're going to the George Town Mall down the road and you've got free reign of all hundred and twenty stores.? ?But...? ?No buts. As it is, I'm planning on having the three sets of clothes you came with burned, unless they have any sentimental value. I'm going to call room service for some breakfast. Don't try finding the keys to the lab. I've put them into the safety deposit box downstairs, and asked that only I be allowed to get them out.? He got up and walked out of the room, leaving her to absorb everything and get ready. ~A few hours later~ ~After Breakfast~ ~Union Station Mall~ The cab pulled up and both doors to the backseat opened up at the same time. Gray handed the driver his fare and tip, and then climbed out the driver's side. Alexis climbed out the passenger side and then looked at the large doors that lead into the second largest mall in Washington DC. The Largest was down the road but was right near the political areas and thus, had more tourist then one would really want to deal with. This one had 4 levels to it, and more stores then you could really shake a stick at if you wanted to. Immediately Gray caught up with her and motioned for her to follow him. ?So, can I ask a question?? ?Yes, you can.? ?What's my spending limit?? Gray smiled... in a sarcastic way. ?Well, I guess your limit is what I can carry. But we're not here for anything except for personal effects. I'm not carrying any TVs or anything else out of here.? She chuckled at his attempt at a joke. But it wasn't really, she knew that he wasn't happy with his current assignment, there was a tension there which she didn't really want to fight. ?I'll be good. The sooner we get this done, the sooner we get back to work.? Gray nodded, but his personal thoughts were more of an ?I wish.? type sentiment. ?Do you have anything you have to do, specifically?? Gray nodded. ?Got to pick up the keys for my rental car at the Hertz rental on the other side of the mall by 1 PM. Other then that, I'm following you.? With that, Gray reached forward and opened the door to the large shopping center and walked in. It didn't take long before the woman in the little work-aholic came through. She had a platinum card and she knew how to use it. Well to a point. A very practical woman too, she headed off to a few stores that were more along the lines of jeans and t-shirts, maybe a skirt here and there but mostly along the lines of more casual wear. As she worked on trying things on, and either approving them or not, Gray stood guard, no more then twenty feet from her. And then inside the store called 'Express' something hit him at the scent level. A man, dressed in a suit, walked next to him, and the smell of something familiar hit Gray. A cologne or perfume? No.... it was usually used in an oil in particular. For Alexis, it was somewhat annoying to be with Gray. He was trying to be distant while he carried out this task. Without him being right there, she didn't exactly have the ability to turn and speak to him. She should have just tried to go it alone. But as he'd pointed out, it wouldn't have worked all that well. She couldn't even usually find him when she maybe needed someone else's thoughts on what she should get. It's not like he didn't appear to have a fashion sense. At least he dressed in more colors then his brother did, who was just your always-wear-basic-black type of person. ?Gray?? She collected up what she wanted and walked over to the counter. He stepped over and pulled out his wallet to give the clerk the card and his ID. ?Gray?? He looked down at her. ?I'm sorry.? ?Sorry about what?? She looked up at him. ?I'm sorry that I had to step in on this. I should have spoken to Lia about it. I've disrupted your normal routine, which is never good. I know you don't like this arrangement... probably don't even want to be here right now.? She might as well have hit him with a brickbat. He reached over and took the two cards back and slid them home before grabbing the large paper bag with the shops logo upon it. He started to head out, Alexis at his side while he answered her. ?No, you have nothing to be sorry about. It's not like Lia gave you a choice, and well... I can't blame her either. We... Blaise and I, both agreed to take this on. I guess I wasn't thinking as usual when I agreed to it as to the changes that would come. I've been known to do that from time to time. Sometimes I think my brother's impulsiveness comes from my father's side, and I might have a smidgen of it.? She giggled. ?You and your brother are night and day.? ?Sometimes I wonder.? He raised an eyebrow at her as they walked down the long internal mall structure. He continued though. ?No, sometimes I can act like an asshole though. Usually when I do, people like Dani smack me upside the head and tell me to pull my head out of my ass.? ?Oh? I thought Dani and Josh...? Gray shook his head. ?Why does everyone think that?? ?I'm sorry.? ?No, it's true that Dani and I are close. But not in that way. She's a few hours younger then my brother. It's those two who are dating. I was just Dani's instructor in school, and eventually I became the big brother she nev... shit. Eventually I became a big brother to her.? She giggled a bit again. ?And maybe some day a big brother-in-law, huh?? ?Only if my brother finally gets HIS head out of his ass.? At that, they went to pass by the local Hot Topic Store. And Alexis squealed... ?Oh! Those are just like the pair of Vivica's I like so much!? She absent-mindedly grabbed Gray by the hand to pull him into the shop with her. He had to adjust his footing or either drop her other bags, or fall quickly as she ran in towards a pair of pants that looked like the pair that Vivica Valentine is usually seen in, with the straps and such around it. ?These are so sweet! And these have the red highlights!? She picks up the pair of pants and starts to look at them closer. She then turns around and starts to look up at the wall that holds all of the rock band t-shirts this store routinely carries. She reached down to the bins and started grabbing a few t-shirts out as Gray's sense of smell got assaulted by the thick, spicy scent that he could barely touch on what it was. He looked around and saw the same man as before... black slacks, black business coat, white collared shirt under it, with a striped tie. The man didn't look like someone who would shop at Hot Topic, that's for sure. Gray took a moment to try to study him. His hair was neat, and a sandy blonde. He was clean shaven with a pale color to his skin. The only thing that set him apart was a lapel pin on his coat. Gray zero'd in on it and could make it out.... a cross, with a circle around the cross point, and a thicker oval around the whole thing. It was familiar for some reason, but he couldn't quite place it. Gray decied to try to keep an eye on this guy, but also had to put the other eye back on Alexis. When he turned to find her though, she was right next to him... looking at a bustier that had a baby blue lace around what looked to be an almost leather material to the main part of it, well what could be called the main part of it. He instantly moved to turn away. Alexis noticed this and her face turned red. ?Oh, I'm sorry.? She put the lingerie down and then moved towards the clerk, to pay and leave. Gray followed behind and repeated the process from before, all the while, keeping a look on the man in the suit. After paying, the two walked out and found themselves near the food court. ?You hungry? Cause I'm starving!? ?This is fast food.? ?So? Quick bite to eat. I need to rest my feet a bit, as it is.? ~A few moments later~ ~Seated in the food court~ They had found a small table, just big enough for the two of them. Both had a tray of assorted items in front of them, it looked like it had been a sub sandwich decision from the both of them. Alexis was eating while Gray looked around. ?You know, for how much that sandwich cost, you're going to waste it.? Gray looked at her. He'd been following the suited man who now sat about eight tables from them. ?Sorry, a bit distracted.? She looked around a bit, thinking she could catch a glimpse of what he had been looking at. Off in the direction of the suited man, was a couple of girls who looked like they were some of the more classy dressing, but more then likely 'looser' types. They were sitting there eating salads and drinking their bubble teas. To Alexis that had to be what he was looking at, especially when they had so much cleavage showing. Alexis had seen that trick done by Shadow almost every day. Women who had it, could use it to draw men's attentions almost anywhere, anytime. ?Oh, well, why don't you go ask one out? You've got a few days in D.C.? Gray shook his head and snapped back to her. ?What?? ?The girls. You're staring at them. You're kinda obvious. One looks a lot like someone we both know, huh? Get up some gumption and go ask them.? Gray snorted at that as he then drew what she was talking about into his view. ?Not likely to happen.? ?Oh, come on Gray. I don't want to hear anything about you're not good enough for them or anything like that. Look at you.? Gray's eyes narrowed in on her. ?What?? ?You're statement, of not too likely. You're a bodybuilder. A wrestler, a champion. You could land a girl like that.? He laughed. He actually laughed. ?No, I don't think so. I'm too complicated.? Alexis smiled at him. What he didn't know is that she knew something of what he was speaking of. She'd tested his blood in the lab from the paper towel he'd used to wipe up after a cut. She knew he wasn't normal, but didn't have much in the line of dealing with what it was that he was. ?Well, complication can be dealt with. Either hide your complication or come out front and let someone know.? Gray sighed. She was right, but both had failed miserably for him before. ?No, I don't think so. I tried the first option, hiding, with the woman who was my wife and mother of my son. All it did was drive a wedge between us. Eventually it had to end. And the last time I tried to be open with someone up front about it, even after going out for a late night dinner, I never got even got called a friend after it. Unfortunately my life is too complicated for anything like that. Grayson Brand, bachelor for life.? ?I'm sorry. Who was the one you went out to dinner with?? Gray was caught off guard by the question. ?Dawn...? He didn't even realize he'd answered her until it was too late. He didn't even know why he answered that question. The only other one who knew anything of his pent up, unreturned feelings for Dawn was his brother. ?Oh...? That's all she answered. ?I'm the one who has to apologize now. Very few knew that Dawn and I had gone out for a friendly dinner one night in Vegas when we were in Global. After that, it was more then likely more pain then it was worth. Especially when sometimes I still think Lia would like to skin me and hang my pelt up.? Alexis took the hint and dropped the subject. Although she didn't quite catch the reference to the punishment he thinks Lia would wish upon him. The rest of the conversation was short bits, as they finished eating. Alexis didn't want to press the issue anymore and it wasn't something she found she should push. After they were done eating, they got back up and headed out to a few other stores, all the while, Gray watching the man in the suit. ~ About an hour later~ It was getting to be a long and drawn out day, that was for sure. A few bags later, and Gray was beginning to feel it. Not to mention he now knew they were being followed by that man. He'd been in every store with them since Hot Topic. Gray was glad it would be over soon. She had about two weeks worth of clothing now, at least. Well two weeks by men's standards. But then the dreaded thing for him happened. She decided that there was one part that was really left out. And from the store she was heading to, Gray's morality and nerves kicked in. Victoria's Secret. He looked up at the sign and then down to the manikin in the window who was wearing a matching bra and thong set, covered by a small bit of gossamer in what was supposed to be a short robe of some kind. As Gray slowed down, Alexis noticed it in her peripheral vision. She spun around. ?You okay?? Gray stopped, she could see the look on his face. ?Oh. Well a girl needs underwear too. How about this, you take a seat on the bench. I think I'll be safe in this one. I'll come get you when I'm ready for the card.? ?Sounds like a plan.? He sat down, relieving his arms of the load he was carrying as Alexis walked in. He was fine, for about five minutes. As he looked up at the open entrance to the store, he noticed that the man, had walked up in front of the store. The man hesitated for a moment, and then walked in, pulling a cell phone out of his pant's pocket. He muttered under his breath. ?Shit...? As the two suited men both walked into the store, he stood and picked up the bags of other items. He then walked in and found Alexis right away as she had some things tossed over her arm, and was holding something that looked satiny in her hands. Gray walked up next to her. ?Gray? What are you doing?? Gray looked down and noticed that it was a pair of panties, a cut that wouldn't cover much, but more then a thong, as Dani liked wearing around, would. They were of a silk or satin material in pink, with black letters on what would be the backside that read ?Sexy Little Thing?. Gray's train of thought derailed and crashed down the embankment. ?Uhm... I...? ?Look, guess you got bored outside, I'm going to go try some of these on, if you don't mind. I'll be right back out.? She walked over to the clerk at the changing rooms and got a number and pass. ?You're in Number 3, miss.? ?Thank you.? Gray looked around and all he saw were things that he normally saw laying around the room. Mostly when they'd accompany Dani on a photo shoot. Some of this didn't look like normal people would even think about wearing them. It had to be a good five minutes until the shit hit the fan. Then, the suited man caught his attention again... only this time he wasn't the only one. A second man had joined him, both dressed the same, both wearing the lapel pin. ?Shit, check. We can officially upgrade to fuck now.? It was time to act, and that he did. The clerk for the changing rooms had moved on. He slid the bags he was carrying behind the clerks counter. He then stepped back into the hallway between the dressing rooms, quickly he slid through the curtain into her dressing room. Right into an Alexis that wasn't wearing too much more then a pair of panties. She quickly covered up her chest with her arms. She tried not to scream, and put out a very low, but stern toned voice. ?Gray?? He moved his finger to his lips and shushed her. ?We've got an issue. You need to be quiet. Those yours or the stores?? He looked down at her, and then out through the edge of the curtain. ?Mine, if you really need to know. What's going on?? He turned to address her, and then saw something on her that drew his attention. Her partial nakedness was allowing something to be seen, that normally wouldn't be. He grabbed her shoulder and pushed her awkwardly in the direction of the mirror. ?HEY!? On her back, over her right shoulder blade was a raised scar. More like a brand that had been put there. It was a symbol. A cross, a crucifix with flared arms. A circle around the crosspoint, and an oval surrounding the rest of it evenly. ?Where did you get that?? Her face went pale as she realized what he was talking about. ?I guess you could say that's from my former employer. Can I ask why?? ?Get dressed.? ?Do you mind?? ?Right now, not at all. Get dressed, now.? She turned her back to him and started to do just that. As she did, he was looking through the curtain again. ?What the hell is going on? If this was just a cheap trick to get to see my...? ?Shush!? She shut up and finished buttoning the fly to her jeans. ?I've got two suits, black outer, white inner. Lapel pins, matching your brand.? Those words had her moving quicker. She finished putting her shirt back on and then collected up the stuff she was trying on. ?Suits? That's the Society's snoops. They shouldn't be armed with anything more then a cell phones. They're nothing more then scouts.? Gray nodded. ?You armed at all?? He turned his head to her and smiled. ?As well as I need to be. Collect up the stuff, I hope you like it, cause we're buying it. One of them has been following us since Hot Topic.? She glared at him for hiding this from her for the last few hours. ?Okay, what's the plan?? ?You're going to buy that, I'm going to pay for it and we walk out of here. The one thing we have going for us is that they know we came here in a cab, they don't know I'm picking up a rental. After that, it might be time to deliver to them a message.? ?A message?? ?Don't worry about it. Good thing is, I've got their scent, so I can track them if I need to.? ?What?? He just tried to explain in a simple manner. ?They're wearing something... spicy. I picked it up shortly after we got here.? She just went along with the flow. ?Balsam Oil, it's used in a lot of their anointing rituals. Nasty smelling actually. Not as good as the Drakar Noir you wear.? He didn't notice her cheeks go flush a bit at that last part. He was busy slipping out of the dressing room, and grabbing their bags. She was shortly behind him, and was heading for the clerk. Gray caught up with her and then pulled her arm. He motioned to one of the sale bins of other merchandise. He leaned into her and whispered into her ear. ?Can't just up and leave, they'll get suspicious. Take another minute or two to look around. Buy whatever. Go slow and like we've not noticed them yet.? She nodded and then started to pull some of the other things out of the bin. Gray headed over near the clerk, and waited for her. As he had said, a few minutes later she came up to complete the transaction. Afterwards they walked out and headed towards the Hertz Car Rental that was inside the Sears store. ?Are they following us still?? Gray could smell them. ?Yes, to a point. But maybe it's time to play out hand.? Gray stopped, turned around and stared right at them. Both men stopped dead in their tracks and hesitated. They then split apart and walked in opposite directions. ?Why did you do that?? ?Cause they did exactly as I expected. They headed off out of site. Instead of potentially tip their hand. Now to go get the car.? They almost ran, a very brisk walk to the counter inside the car rental place. ~Half an hour later~ Outside, it was pretty easy. Gray had rented a Mustang Shelby GT-H, the sportiest car they had. He helped Alexis get everything packed into the car. He then decided to drive around the parking lot for a bit. ?You think they're still around?? ?More then likely. I doubt they would be lost so easily. We're going to need to keep the lab locked for a few days until the others get here. If we do any work in it, it's both of us, same time. Understood?? The reality of the situation was setting in. They had found her. The Den had found her. And for right now, she'd have to comply. She couldn't jeopardize her work for Lia. And she couldn't bring others into the fight for her. As she thought that, the car suddenly squealed to a stop. Gray threw open the door and got out. ?Gray?? He walked over towards a large, expensive looking Cadillac. Two men in black suits were climbing into it. He only needed a few strides and he was on top of the blonde one he'd noticed first. He pulled the guy out of his car and slammed him against the large van marked for a local cable company next to it. His hand went instinctively for the throat. ?What are you doing?? Gray didn't play around too much. The other guy tipped his hand, he was trained. He could see it as he walked around the car, heading for Gray. ?Stop right there, or I snap his neck for one, and then give you the beating you are begging for right now.? He emphasized this by shifting partially feral. His Irises went golden, and his pupils went vertical. His teeth became fangs, and his once loose clothing wasn't as loose anymore. The guy stopped and held his hands up. ?What do you want with the girl?? The guy he held was the one to answer. ?She's a traitor to her species! She's to be removed, terminated.? ?That sounds a bit permanent. You sure you guys have the means to do that?? The other guy broke in. ?Who are you? And why are you guarding her?? Gray wasn't one to beat around the bush. ?Let me put it this way. I'm a Sergeant and Guardian of the Order of the Hammer. She's under our protection now. And unless you're wanting to start a war that would be nothing but a war of attrition of your side, I'd suggest you just put the message out to leave her alone.? The man he held started to struggle. He moved to bring his arms down on Gray's elbow. Gray bent ahead of his strike, pulling him toward him. He then pushed and lifted at the same time, bouncing the man off the side of the van, and letting him fall to the ground. The other man backed off a bit. Gray had just thrown him like he'd weighed nothing. ?Deliver the message. And know this, I know you now. I catch your stench near her again, you will regret it for the last few seconds you have left.? Gray started to shift back and turned to leave them. As he climbed back into the car, the other man finally moved to check on his partner. Gray smoked the tires a bit as he backed his rental up, to keep them from getting the license plate number before he turned it around and headed off back towards the hotel. Alexis hadn't seen anything, but Gray toss the man like he was a rag doll. She just looked at him, almost as a biologist would study something now. Or maybe a bit more. Gray pulled out his phone and handed it to her. ?Do me a favor, in the contacts, there is a number listed as Aine, that's A, I, N, E. Call it for me.? She started pushing buttons on the phone while he drove and kept checking his mirrors. ?That's Annie's number. Time to file a preliminary report.? She handed the phone back to him as the ringing noise could be heard through the speaker. ~To Be Continued~
