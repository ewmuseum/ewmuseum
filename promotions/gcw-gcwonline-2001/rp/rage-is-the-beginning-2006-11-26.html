rage is the beginning!

26 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/26/06 Shaman is seen sitting at his desk looking down upon the casino patrons below. His eyes concentrate on a few specific people one includes Suzy his right hand below. He watches her intently as she makes the decisions and walks onwards and through the gambling people. Shaman turns and lifts the cell phone as his eyes are focused on a folder in front of him. “I want info on Ciel and a message sent to Bryan Mayhem that our business has not concluded yet!” Shaman opens the folder and then slams his fist down upon the table as he watches intently and growls low staring at the pictures of the devils Night match. He stared and then watched as he and mayhem went to the limit against one another. He then turns his gaze at the phone. “Yes I am still here. Why would I want to let someone else do my work” I do my own work and I don’t want a hit on anyone you greasy Turk. You know not to throw these suggestions to me or we will have another nasty complication like last time.” Shaman sneers as he reads the file. “Yes yes your apology is accepted. Don’t call me I’ll call you!” Shaman closes the cell phone and turns his gaze and watches. His eye focused on the file and he then turns and gazes out at the people below. His mind playing the events of Devils Night over and over again through his mind. He then turns on the laptop near him as he waits patiently. “Mayhem thought I was going to be easily defeated. Now he failed to realize I was going to walk away on my own power and he was going to not win. Then we had a little altercation with one of his hero’s. I don’t give a damn on who they put in the ring with me, But I will assure those that who face me will know why I am known as the most Sadistic Son of a bitch ever known. Pain is my best friend. Blood allows me to feel free and I enjoy it.” He turns his gaze at the GCW website on the laptop as it shows who he is facing. A woman named Reivyn Mikona. He flips through the website and tries to get more information on this woman. He reads and then thumbs through a magazine on his desk for Japanese federations and calls a number on it. “Hey Inaki whats going on. I need some information on a woman named Reivyn Minoka.” Shaman pauses as he listens. Then he writes something down. “That good really” I am impressed. She is new to GCW, but I think she deserves some respect. She will have a hard match against me. Anyways email me all the info. Thanksgiving has been hell. See you later in Japan when I go on vacation Inaki thanks.” Shaman hangs up the cell phone and begins punching in some stuff on his laptop wondering what he has in store for everyone. He smiles and threads through some Japanese wrestling organizations. He then flips back to GCW and plays Devils Night over and over again. He concentrates hard on what is happening. He pauses for a moment looking at a rope hanging in the background. He grins and then pauses as the rope as it swings. “That is the key to bringing about the pain. I will take the very breath out of anyone who steps in my way.” Shaman turns and looks at the casino below him through the windows. “Reivyn I don’t know you from a whole in the ground. Yet you are destined to face me and thus you are a simple distraction at the moment. You may have skills and abilities I am over looking big damn deal I don’t care really. You’re my opponent and I will treat you like I do any other opponent. I am going to show you ways to hurt and bleed. This isn’t some simple passerby your facing. You may be dangerous, but I don’t care so am i. You may have some history in Japan, But that doesn’t matter you are here in the US and in the ring with me.' Shaman clenches his fist and then folds his arms across his chest looking down at all the gamblers below as they spend their money for him. He then breaths in deep and seems to calm a bit. “Look Reivyn I apologize about you being in the ring with me. You are not my target, but I refuse to go easier on you because you’re in the ring against me. You seem like an interesting person and truthfully you have earned my respect. I am not going to ease up and I expect 100 percent from you. No quarter expected. It shall be all out and in the end let’s hope it is your very best if you beat me. If you lose I hope you gave it your all. Myself I do what it takes to win by any means necessary.” Shaman pauses and presses enter on the laptop as something begins to load on the computer. “You see Reivyn this is a business regardless of what is said or done. I am very rich and powerful, but I do things with my own hands. I do not like to dirty my reputation with hiring thugs or lackeys who will just cramp up my style. I am established and renown to be a very dangerous man. You and I can discuss the match after it has happened. For World Wide will be truly World Wide. I intend on winning Reivyn do you think I would go after you in any less of an aspect there.” Shaman shakes his head and shrugs. “Reivyn you know how dangerous this will be for you. So I am not going to paint a beautiful little picture for you with flowers. The fact is real and you’re in the ring with a dangerous person bent on getting his hands on Mayhem. So before I get boring and in danger of repeating myself I wish to allow you the one privilege of knowing that I am sorry for whatever happens in the ring before it happens.” Shaman walks around the desk and then walks to the windows and looks out of them staring at the Vegas strip beyond. He knows that things will be getting rather dangerous for him, because he is going into dangerous grounds for anyone. He then turns and looks around again. “Mayhem sleep but not too soundly. Your nightmares have become a reality. Things are going to be strange for you in these next few coming weeks and Nobody will get in my way to hurting you. Not you, Not Ciel, not anyone in your little celebrity fit club. I am focused on you Mayhem and I will do anything and everything to make sure the message is sent. You awoke the sick side of me and I enjoyed it. It is time for you to feel the side that has been buried for a few years. Is it easier fr you to stare at broken toys all day” How about is end you a truck load of dismembered toys? It seems to suit you. The day has come and you have met the man that is as twisted as you. I am normal on the outside, but what swirls upstairs is a world of dangerous and I mean very dangerous aspects of a man who will bring you to your knees and beat you down even further.” Shaman grabs the hard oak chair and then looks at the Vegas strip and then grins sadistically as he grabs the chair and throws it at the window causing it to shatter and break as the chair flies through it. He stands there as his hair blows about his head and face as his eyes show a glint of sadistic pleasure upon his mind. “The days grow short to the world of where I am going. First there will be a Reivyn nevermore nevermore. Then there will be a bit of Mayhem. Nobody gets in my way and nobody stops me ever. I merely get delayed. Fear is never boring. Blood tastes great!” Shaman stands there as he laughs deeply and sadistically as a security guard runs into the room and Shaman stands there simply staring out at the world beyond.
