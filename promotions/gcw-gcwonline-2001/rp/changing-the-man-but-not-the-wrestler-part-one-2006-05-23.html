Changing The Man But Not The Wrestler (Part One)

23 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05-23-06 *NOTE: This is a roleplay for Baltasar and not Alexander. I will move it to the Baltasar backstage area once I have one in place. Thanks.* The sounds of children playing and a sprinkler doing it's job could be heard as The Red Devil stepped out of his Ford F-150 and onto the concrete drive of his former manager, and friend, Enrique Morales. It had been months since they'd spoken due to the attack by Baltasar on his former friend and mentor. As he walked toward the front door to the house his mind raced with all the things he needed to say. All the bottled up feelings he was having toward his decisions and actions of just months ago. 'Well here is a sight I wasn't sure I'd ever see again.' The voice snapped Baltasar back to reality and in one quick motion also ended any chances he had at getting cold feet on the idea. He looked to his right and saw Enrique, in his white and red jogging suit, walking around the north corner of his home. There was an obvious limp in his step as he made his way toward his former charge. A daily reminder of the beating that The Red Devil inflicted upon him. The worst part being that it wasn't even a third of the damage he'd done to his former tag team partner Angelus on that very same night. At least Enrique could still walk, even with the limp, cause it was very likely that Angelus would never again rise out of the wheelchair that his attack put him in. A painful look grew over the face of Baltasar as Enrique slowly made his way toward him from across the yard. 'This has already started better than I thought it would.' The Red Devil remarked with only the slightest of a grin. Doing everything he could to not break down at just the site of his former friend. 'Why is that?' Enrique asked as he now stood a mere foot from the giant Spanish wrestler who he'd spent years helping in the business. A serious look spread across his face as he stood there, almost frozen, staring up into the eyes of Baltasar. 'I figured I'd have to duck behind the truck here and avoid at least two or three rounds from your shotgun before you'd at least give me the chance to throw up a white flag of peace.' Enrique simply stood there staring into the eyes of The Red Devil. If he was feeling anything other than contempt he was doing a good job of hiding it at this point. Finally, as he ran his hand through his hair, Baltasar decided to get to the point of why he'd come all the way to San Antonio in the first place. 'Enrique I came here today to try and explain myself and to say sor...' Baltasar began before being cut off as his former friend embraced him with a meaningful and real hug. Shock filled The Red Devil for a moment as he stood there clearly not expecting this kind of reaction from Morales. All the preparing he'd done for this moment, for weeks on end, had been for the absolute worst. This was the one response he never figured on getting in a million years. 'It's alright my dear boy. I forgave you the night it all went down.' The words cut through and shocked Baltasar even more than he could have possibly ever imagined. This was certainly not what he'd envisioned this meeting going like. It was of course a great feeling knowing that all was better than he expected but all the same it was weird to get this kind of reaction. 'You did?' It was all the response that The Red Devil could muster. The shock clearly in his voice as Enrique took a step back and patted him on his massive shoulder with a little smile on his face. 'You're a passionate man Montrell. You always have been and that passion is and always has been even greater when it came to wrestling. I knew you did it out of frustration and anger over how your career was going at the time and I knew you'd eventually come to regret it, just as you are now.' The knowledge of his former manager couldn't have been more spot on. For months now Baltasar had agonized over his decisions made in recent months. Decisions that cost him his friends in Peter Vetra, Morales, and Angelus, who truth be told was much more like a brother to him than a friend. He'd even managed to cause the love of his life, Maria, to leave him and file for divorce. Yes The Red Devil had put himself in position to no longer have anyone or anything holding his career back, but it came a far greater price then he'd realized at the time. 'I was a fool Enrique. Passion or not I was a damned fool. I lost everyone who'd ever stood by me in my life over an over zealous drive to some how be bigger and greater in stature than Deville. All over the desire to be 'known' the way he is I gave away everything else. You can call it passion, and I once did as well, but now I simply call it fool hearted ignorance.' 'We all have our lessons we have to learn my dear friend. Some of us have to learn lessons in a far more drawn out and hard process. You're a human being Montrell and a good one, despite your mistakes, at that. You shouldn't continue to beat yourself up over these things.' An awkward silence feel between the two as The Red Devil began to scratch his head and look down at the ground while Enrique seemed to just sort of look around as if neither knew quite how to continue the conversation. Finally Enrique decided that it was time to move to more pleasant thoughts. 'Speaking of wrestling I heard you've signed with GCW. That is a fine company and one with a great reputation behind it. I'm proud of you.' The change in the conversation seemed to lighten the mood of Baltasar as well. Excitement filled his eyes when he looked up to meet the gaze of Enrique. 'Yeah they are a quality promotion. Much like PRIME in their national reconition and reputation for being highly competitive. I figured when I returned to the ring with my new outlook and attitude toward myself and life that I might as well find a company to get a fresh start in as well. I don't want to continue to live through the bad decisions I made in the past so PRIME had to go along with a lot of other baggage.' 'It is for the best. Everyone can use a change of pace and location from time to time. With a new group of competitors to face, a new set of goals to accomplish, and a fresh outlook on the sport you'll be kicking ass and building that reputation you deserve in no time.' 'My plan is to do just that and to finally be the champion and competitor I always said I would be. Many things have changed about me and my approach to this business but not all of them. I'm still in this to be the best and to gain the honor and respect of my peers but I just don't feel the need to do it by making my name off the legacy of others any longer. No one match is so important that it will make or break my career. It's all about being consistant and doing my best to be the best every single week and not just on the weeks when I'm facing a 'name' or a 'legend' in the business.' 'You've come a long way my dear friend. Why just the other day Angelus and I...' The awkward silence returned as Enrique quickly clammed up and turned his head slightly away. The pain immediately returned to Baltasars face and to his eyes at the mention of his former best friend, ally, and brother. Tears began to form in the eyes of both men as they thought about the way things had ended on that particular ReVolution. The consequences of a miscommunication between the former tag champions had resulted in Baltasar snapping into a rage and then snapping the neck of his former partner. 'How is Angelus?' Baltasar finally managed to choke out the only words that would come to mind. The pain he felt for his wrong doing toward his best friend was almost more than one could bare and he knew it was nothing compared to the pain and loss that he'd put Angelus and his family through. After wiping away a few tears Enrique let out a deep sigh before responding. 'To be honest Montrell he is in better spirits than one might think. He holds no ill will toward his situation and he doesn't even question it to be honest. He accepts that his life has changed forever and he doesn't make any excuses about it. Never misses a single rehab session or complains about how hard any of it is. He still holds out hope that he'll walk again one day and be able to open that cantina he always dreamed of opening. It's truly remarkable to watch the man continue to have such a wonderful and fighting spirit.' 'Does he still hate me? I guess that is a stupid question cause honestly how could he not.' Once more Enrique sighed as he gave Baltasar another pat on one of his massive shoulders. 'Truth be told he never once let himself hate you for it. To this day he refuses to allow himself to ever let a hatred for you set in and cause him to use it as an excuse for everything wrong in his life. Don't get me wrong he is hurt, crushed even, that you were the one who did this too him but he loved you like a brother and in the end that was enough to keep him from ever hating you. If you're wondering if he has forgiven you the same way I have then your answer is no. He doesn't hate you but he hasn't forgiven what you did to him.' 'I don't blame him. I wouldn't forgive me either. I've been trying to get up the courage to go and talk to him, like this, but I've not been able to man up enough to do it. I can't quit being a pussy and force myself to look at him in the wheelchair I put him in.' Once more the tears filled The Red Devils eyes as he fought hard to keep them from making their way down his face. He simply would not allow himself to break down and cry in front of Enrique. There was no way he'd let his emotions get the best of him on this day. 'When you are ready Montrell I know you'll find the inner strength to accomplish that meeting. You can't expect to work through all your issues in one setting. Just don't get your hopes up too high when you finally do go to see him, as you're likely not going to get as warm a reception as you did with me and understandably so. Like I said though he doesn't hate you and you've got that going for you in this situation.' The words of Enrique seemed to console The Red Devil ever so slightly and he finally turned his gaze upward toward his former friend with out any emotion on his face at all as he spoke. 'I'm glad he doesn't hate me. He doesn't need to cause I hate myself enough for the both of us.' *** Do you have any idea what it's like to hate yourself Warr? Have you ever known what it is like to get up every day knowing that you really couldn't give a damn if you live or die that day? If you do then welcome to my world. This debut isn't like any other I've ever had in my wrestling career. This one is different, it's special and unique. It's my shot to make a huge impact on a huge stage in front of a huge audience for quite possibly the only federation on the planet that runs as a legit rival to PRIME. Yet you know what it's about more than that for me? It's about pain. You're pain, my pain, our suffering coming together as one in order to ease my mind. As I walk out there at World Wide I won't be thinking about whether or not I put on a good show. I won't be thinking about whether or not I'm getting my just respect, honor, or love from my peers and the fans. If that eventually comes my way I'll embrace it. This match, just like all the others I have for the rest of my GCW career, will always be about one ulimate thing. Making the pain I feel go away, even if for only a few brief moments, and making others feel as much pain as I do inside. Believe me this isn't personal Warr. It won't ever be personal with anyone I treat with this approach. I know that the pain I feel inside is there by my own bad decision making. My pain is a direct effect from my own personal prison I've placed myself in through chasing greedy and unhonorable goals. I've brought this all on myself and in turn I'm going to unleash it and bring that pain all on everyone I have to face. See I'm a coward when it comes to my pain. I don't like it and so rather than face it, rather than own up to entirely and bare the cross I've made for myself, I'd rather go the easier route and place that pain with someone else for a few moments at a time, just so I don't have to feel it anymore. You're just the first in a long line of painkillers, so to speak, for me to use along the way. I can't help it that I'm not willing to own up to my own pain and take it like a man. It's what cowards do in the face of something they don't like or don't wish to live through. For this week you'll be my saving grace, my guardian angel, the pain relief that I so desperately crave every single waking moment of my life. You'll have to remember this fact for everyone in GCW cause it's not something I intend to repeat over and over again. My pain being everyone elses pain as well, from this point forward, is simply implied.
