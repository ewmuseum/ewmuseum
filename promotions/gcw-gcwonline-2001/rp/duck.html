Date: Duck. Wake up! Wake up! There's much to be done. No time to sleep, to rest or have fun. Toxic is waiting the time is near to bash in his brains, the big bastard queer. What have I done to deserve you so? What can I do to make you go? My mind is made up, my decision is clear, the mask must stay on, I'm safer in here. I don't believe what you've said, you weave only half truthes, now leave me in bed, or fetch me my noose. Don't be over dramatic, it's really not bad. I'm here to support you, we'll make it a fad. Beating Toxic is most important, of this I'm not sad. But to think what we'll do to him, it's maybe a little bad. I'll fight a clean fight, to the pin or a tap. I'll not take a man's career, his life on my lap. He deserves what he gets, he's twisted deep down. It's not my place to judge, I'm am simply the hound. I'll deliver swift justice, but to a point I cannot cross. Listen, please listen, or this will lead to another loss! Why must you fight me, every god given inche, you argue and argue when the plan is a synch. Don't do it for the money, the contracts or fame. Do it for you, you're the last one to blame. Don't make me feel guilty, the tactic is lame. Just drop the match to Eva, and give it a run. Don't say that again or I swear I'll get my gun. What exactly is your plan, what have you devised? I don't know that just yet, I soon must decide. I cannot fight her, I know this for fact, I cannot lose this mask, it's no longer an act. The gimmick has become me, a better person am I. What's you're problem? Are you stupid? Will you ever even try!? Try for what? To be better? To hurt them again? I have tried and I have failed, I've wrestled to the end. I've pulled all the stops, crippled women and men. I played a round with the devil and walked home with a sin. You look in my soul and you search past the pride, you tell me, be honest, will God let me in? The tests they will come, some big and some small, I stand up to defy them, I'll challenge them all. I can't waver when it's hard, what kind of soldier would I be. I profess to be clean, and with a demon have tea. So fuck you and fuck them, I'll be who I want. Lexicon, Marauder, call me a god damned croissant. But a wrestler is a wrestler by any name that he's given, and through challenge, adversity, and defeat I have risen. I'll stand tall when the bell rings it's three chimes clear, I'll meet with Toxic and I'll face him without fear. But I'll do it in a mask, so the people will cheer. They'll scream and they'll chant, so loud maybe heaven will hear. Maybe then, I'll be done, defeated or won. Nice speech, did you save it, practice in the mirror? Leave me be, I want peace, can I make it much clearer? You talk like I'm stupid, like I don't know the why. I was there at the lowest, and rode along with the high. There were moments, in the thick, I thought you couldn't be better. But now I've seen your potential, you could be the pro fedder. You don't need the mask, you don't need it to hide the crimes. It's Lexicon who must face his own demons this time. The crowd is ready, they want you to do it. They don't want a masked poser, they need the bull fuckin dozer! Toxic has committed injustice, he's used you for gain. He attacked you with open malice, he's brought Eva such pain. To let him get away with it is just, fuck it man, that's lame. You fight her on wednesday, you give it a go. You keep your mask, lose the match, I'll stand with you bro. But I'll snicker and sneer every time you're not near cuz you Toxic's bitch might as well suck his dick. I'll get my rematch some other way. You'll find your balls some other day? You can't taunt me and mock me, I don't fear the thrown word. I can't give in to the masses each time theres a change in the herd. I'm the Marauder now, leave it alone. Screw that, you're Lex to the bone! Don't justify to me, your fear or denial. It's not here you'll find redemption, I don't have that on file. If you want saving, you want truth, you must start here at home. Nobody else can find your peace, it's a journey you go on alone. But you won't get the answers inside of a gimmick. You have to face your demons and discover your limit. We know that's not you, inside of the ring. You're playing a different style, you don't even grin. You're doing it all backwards, I know we can win. Like a fencer with a left, your sneaky at best, switching to the right to pull a win on the fight. Alright I've had enough, leave me be just to think. Take a moment to catch your breath and have a drink. I've been bad in the past, but all that is over now. I lived life a little too fast, I wanted the easy path and how. But I've learned my lesson the hard way, and now I'm gonna do things right. The lord is on my side in these days, I've brought into my life his guiding light. But it's the mask, that mask! You have a one track mind, you refuse to see my reasons. All you want is my subission for all of your seasons. In the mask they don't judge, they don't pretend to know the person. All they care for is the entertainment they don't heed moral corrosion. But when Toxic finds his glory, when he looks deep inside. It'll be tainted by his story, his true moment denied. The laugh that is last can only be mine. At least your still plotting, to stop is a crime. But back in the day- Give the past a rest okay? I remember the days when I was at my greatest, the list of my enemies was long and my allies always jaded. I don't want to be a target for another crazies game, I don't need my back broken for a failures hollow fame. I'll figure my way out of beating on my friend, I'll find another to get at Toxic in the end. We'll stand toe to toe, looking eye to eye, and deep inside he'll know... I'll either win or I'll die.
