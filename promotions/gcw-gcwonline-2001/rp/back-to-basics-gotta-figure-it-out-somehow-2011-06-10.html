Back to basics, gotta figure it out somehow.

10 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/10/11 Sitting down he thumbs through several books, The dates date back to 1993. His concentration is unwavering as he closing the book and looks up. His wife Suzy walks in placing a glass off sweet tea down on the desk near him as she sits down looking at the books. Barely registering notice he reaches over and grabs the tea and sips on it slightly as he continues to read. Flipping the pages carefully he doesn't want to miss any details. Suzy picks up a book as she looks at the date which reads May 1994. "Andrew what are you looking for with these old diaries?" She holds up the one she holds in her hand and stares at him waiting for a response. Raising his eyes from the diary he is reading he looks at the one she is holding and reaches a hand out towards her and waves his fingers in a fashion she understands that he wants it. "Searching for something Suzy, an edge i feel i have lost along the way, I need to find that elusive fire that used to burn deep inside." Closing his old diary, he places it down on the table as he looks back up at Suzy. "In here Suzy is where I started leaving something behind with each written night or show. I literally wrote every championship and title reign in here. In fact I wrote the first time we actually met." Smiling a few moments he returns to the diaries before him. He opens one from 1998 as he opens it and looks at a picture he taped there. Suzy takes it slowly as she opens it up. She looks at the picture as she had just gotten out of college and began her career with film and photography. "This was the first job i had after college as a Professional wrestling camera woman." She pulls out the photo and holds it up. " I did not think you'd keep this photograph." Looking back to his diaries he pulls the 1998 diary from Suzy's hand and opened it up. "The Magician was the most brutal opponent i have ever had up until i met Bryan Mayhem, Yet the magician disappeared." Rubbing his fingers together as he looks over the page. His eyes focus as he reads the details he described about The Magician. "Fear isn't something he does nor feels, His drive is interesting to say the least, His inner flame burns almost as intense as my own. He has set his focus on me this time around and could be a fun statistic to take him down a few notches. I won't back down either and his singular personal preferences for pain and torture are on par with my own." Stopping after reading those lines he is a bit taken back by his own tendencies for violence back during that time period. He knew he was a technical wrestler the fans loved during that time. He used his power and extreme tendencies to balance the workload that was on his shoulders to make better matches. He stepped up his talent. he was creative and onto greater things he thinks to himself. Where did it level off he thinks to himself. "You know Suzy ever since losing the GCW Hardcore title i have had plenty of thoughts on how or why i lost it. Truth is it came down to me not taking my opponent seriously. Look at me when did i begin not taking my opponents seriously. I am better than this, a lot better than this." Closing the diary he flips it across the desk, Leaning back he rubs his temple. Staring across the table he looks over each diary, not quite sure where everything sits in his mind. He wants to tell his wife his frustrations, but a lot of the time things seldom work the way he wishes them too. He hates the fact at times his words get jarbled and screwed up. It is like someone turning on a faucet to a drip and the pressure behind it is building in such pressure its threatening to burst. Suzy notices him in deep thought as she reaches over and touches his shoulder softly and kisses his cheek as she sits in his lap looking at him. "You know Andrew that you don't have to stress over not redefining yourself. You can always retire and walk away." Andrew looks up at Suzy through the corner of his eye. Shaking his head in disagreement he looks at the diaries. Pointing over the desk with his hand he frowns. Closing his eyes for a moment he purses his lips as he faces Suzy turning his head. "Nearly twenty years Suzy I see the way my career has developed. I started knowing that nobody believed i was going to go anywhere in this career. I took title after title everywhere i went. I held my own and continued to step up my game. Now it seems like I reached that glass ceiling again." Smacking his hands together he wiggles his fingers, simulating breaking glass falling down. Pointing up, he follows his own finger as he narrows his gaze. Suzy reaches over and touches his hand,she pulls it back down. "Andrew you have broken through every ceiling you have faced before. what makes this one any different?" He looks at her opening his mouth slightly, but cannot formulate any words in response. He lowers his head thinking for a moment he reaches up scratching the back of his head as he grabs a hold of his long hair pulling it tight. He raises his gaze and looks back over at the diaries. "Somewhere in there lies the answer for me to win Shock wave. Barely missing the tournament with a loss to Mayhem, but i was eliminated last year and then got myself back in by beating Mayhem. Then look at me know one year later struggling to get back into the tournament. Eliminated by a damn mistake, personally i feel it was a damn mistake i could have avoided." Slamming his hand down on the desk he focuses on nothing in front of himself. "What have i achieved if i fall before myself or my own misreads every time i get close?" "Damn it Andrew, you have me and a beautiful daughter, a damn business empire most people would give anything just a quarter of. You lived the dream you meant to live. You fulfilled every promise to everyone you came across. If you are going to do anything you just prove it to yourself and nobody else." She grits her teeth in frustration as she stands up and looks back at him. Stopping for a moment she reaches back and slaps him across the face. She looks him straight in the eye as she grabs his chin and speaks clearly. "Waste someone elses time with this crap, you get it together and kick some ass. I am sick and tired of you doing this every time you go through a losing streak." Watching her turn and leave he begins to think as the burn in his cheek slowly intensifies with the swelling. He clearly feels her hand print on his face, he slowly raises his hand and draws his fingers over the welts of her fingers softly feeling each one. He feels alive for one of the few first times in a longtime. Feeling the burning pain that he has not felt since he first stepped into the wrestling ring years ago. He was slapped in the face showing that veterans never respected rookies. The respect is the same nowadays. So intense that rookies have to earn it. Standing up as he pushes the chair backwards as he stretches slightly. Reaching down he grabs the diaries and one at a time begins putting them up slowly. He eyes each one as he does so, he suddenly stops as he holds 2006 in his hands. Opening it he turns each page slowly as he stops noticing something. Nodding his head he realizes that was the specific piece he had lost. He lost the one thing he had always had on his side. Placing the last diary back in its place on the shelf he turns heading for the door. Slipping through the door he stops in the kitchen and reaches up grabbing the key off the key hooks. Turning he looks at Suzy who is looking at him curiously. "I'll be back i need to take the Harley out and clear my head, Don't wait up I'll be gone for awhile." She nods and turns her attention back to the dishes she was washing. Stepping through the large screen door he stops on the porch looking over to the large wood porch swing he used to swing on with his Grandmother. Smiling a bit he leaps off the porch like a big kid. Walking over to the garage he pulls the double doors open and slips inside. Starting the Harley he grins as he revs her up with a loud roar. Feeling the cool wind in his face as he rides the back country roads in New Hampshire, his mind wanders a bit again, back to what was driving him. Suzy had a valid point he brooded over many things lately. If he was going to win he needed to grab the bull by its horns this time. Too many talented wrestlers are going to be in this mess and he needs to take destiny in his own hands and make it happen. “Can I beat these guys on a different level than they expect of me?” He speaks to himself as he rounds a curve and accelerates. His eyes focusing on the road ahead. He drifts on the road like a leaf upon the wind. Feeling the road and the wheels turn on them gracefully. The same questions run through his head over and over, every word in his mind rings true as he wants to figure out each opponent and understand the ways to beat them, Can he do it or is this just another play at nothing and everything at the same time. Mayhem had opened his eyes this time, he was tired of losing and if he wanted something he would have to take it the hard way. Nobody waits for the piper anymore, now it is time to pay the debt that is due and make it happen. The Harley roars down the road rumbling off into the distance where none would know where he was going as he allows his steel horse to take him to places unknown.
