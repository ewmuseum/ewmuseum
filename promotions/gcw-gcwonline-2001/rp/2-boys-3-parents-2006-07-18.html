2 Boys, 3 Parents

18 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18th July 2006 a couple of months ago? “Here you are Mister Lawrence” Or should I say Mister Jackson?” Stephen nodded at the lady behind the desk with a grin as he took the paperwork from her hand. She was cute, with big blue eyes but the grin was for something else. This morning he had woken up Stephen Lawrence and would go to bed Stephen Jackson. Life was good. Glancing over his shoulder, Stephen watched his half-brother Brett nod at the man dealing with him, as he began to rise from his chair. The pair held a knowing smile as Brett walked across the deed poll office floor. “Well hello Mister Jackson,” smiled Brett as he scooped his blazer under his arm and clutched the defining paperwork in his left hand. “Good afternoon Mister Jackson.” Stephen returned the greeting and the two young men giggled together. “Dad is going to be so happy?” Brett added as the pair walked side-by-side through the halls, towards the outside of the building and the baking summer sun. As they walked, Brett glanced ahead. “Shit?” “What?” Stephen glanced up at his brother who had stopped walking and stood motionless in the foyer of the deed poll offices. Brett’s eyes were transfixed on a woman heading straight in his direction. Stephen watched her approach but did not recognise her at all. “Who is that?” the elder brother questioned as the red-headed woman advanced. Stephen turned away from her, towards the look of sheer fear on Brett’s face. “My mom,” he replied through tightly closed lips. He seemed unwilling to move at all; as though hoping the lack of movement would convince her she’s dead. “WHAT IN THE BLOODY HELL ARE YOU DOING”!”” she screamed as she finally got to where the newly-named Jackson men were standing. Brett mumbled in reply. Stephen glanced at his terrified little brother and then at Brett’s mother, whose face was a sea of complete red rage. “Well?” she questioned again, aware of Brett’s obvious inability to reply to her demands first time. Brett mumbled again, but this time two words, at polar ends of the sentence were audible as Brett mentioned “dad” and “wrestling.” Brett’s mom was still fuming. From just those two words however her face seemed to do the impossible and turn any even redder shade of red. “God damn fucking asshole that you call a father. I knew he’d be involved,” she snapped before glancing at Stephen and realising he was part of the conversation. “Who are you?” Stephen swallowed hard as he was questioned by the imposing woman, before clearing his throat. “I’m Stephen Lawrence” err, Jackson. I’m Beverley’s son.” Brett’s mother nodded. “You’re the slut’s son”!” “MOM!” Brett shouted. Stephen did not look happy. “What?” snapped Brett’s mother. “I’m supposed to be happy that your father found himself another woman?” “Actually Miss Chambers,” Stephen interrupted. “I’m older than Brett. I think you’ll find you are the” how would you say it? the “slut?”” Stephen grinned at the disgusted reaction he received from Brett’s mother. If he had of looked towards his tag team partner, he would received the complete set of Chambers? evil eyes. However, Brett’s mother tried to shrug it off as best she could. “Don’t you dare tell me you’ve gone through with that bloody deed poll thing?”” Her attention was back on her son, but still Brett struggled to speak to his domineering mother. “Actually we both did,” smiled Stephen pushing the paperwork of his name change under her nose. “I’m no longer Stephen Lawrence, I’m now Stephen Jackson. And Brett is now Brett Jackson. Collectively we are set to be known as The Jackson Brothers!” “Collectively” Brothers?” Brett’s mother “That’s right, Veronica” The trio in discussion were joined by a fourth voice; a deep, masculine voice that bellowed from over Brett’s mom, Veronica’s shoulder. “My two boys are going to join forces and take the wrestling world by storm.” The trio turned round to look at the man that obviously gave half his genes to the two young wrestlers. “Greg?” Veronica mouthed as she watched her former lover step towards them, putting his arms around his tow boys and pulling them close. “You know, Veronica. I don’t think I’ve even been prouder. These two fine young gentlemen have been willing to accept my surname.” Veronica’s attention snapped at Brett. Sure, Stephen had told her previously but honestly she had struggled to understand his posh New York accent. Brett mumbled again, as Veronica turned on her heel and stormed off. The three Jackson men watched her push through the revolving door and pace across the car park. “Let her go,” reassured Greg Jackson, the father figure as he pulled Brett towards him. “It’s a big change, but give her time to come round and she will” She will? a couple of days ago? “Dude, you just mumbled to yourself as she spoke to you!” Stephen Jackson giggled to himself as he abused his tag team partner and half-brother across the room. The pair were nestled up in a small motel room in Vancouver as they put together the finishing touches in time for their GCW debut on WorldWide#50. The changing of names had worked as The Jackson Brothers were snapped up just eight weeks after changing their names from Lawrence & Chambers, and the duo were nervously excited about their impending debut. Granted it helped that the pair shared 50% of their genes, but that was; according to Greg Jackson their father and manager the main reason they worked so well together. “It’s simple,” he told the journalists that asked about GCW’s latest additions. “They are brothers and therefore, they share a special bond.” However he would quickly deny any fruitiness but claiming, “Now I’m not one of those weird new age people. I just know that siblings are closer than any Joe Boggs and John Doe pulled off the street. By training and working together for the past eight months I believe I mange one of the world’s finest young tag teams.” Of course, all the press attention was completely new to all three men but it was Greg who occupied his time with it as the boys had their rest of their lives to sort out. “Well, at least my mother knows,” smiled Brett as Stephen’s giggling suddenly stopped. “Ssssh!” snapped Stephen silencing his brother. “My mother would react about ten times worse than your mom and I’d even struggle to mumble at her. What you dealt with is nothing on my mom!”! And anyway, I’m waiting until the right time to tell her?” “Oh yeah, and after you appear on TV, that’ll be a good time?” “Sure,” Stephen smiled, nervously; very aware that his mother would have to find it out soon from him, or probably even sooner through the medium of television.
