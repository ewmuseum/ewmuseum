Evil James - An Ace in the Hole (TiT round 1, promo 2)

8 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/08/2008 03/08/08 The scene opens in an Italian resteraunt in Chicago. I forget the name because I just went into the first one I could find and made a call to some people. The cameras are already rolling as I, Evil James, sit at a table and talk to a guy holding a severed horse head. This is just a part of my overall strategy for this match and my plan to make my opponent's life a living hell. "Just go there and put that on his bed if you can find where he lives. I want to get into his brain. I want him to be freaked out and out of his game by the time the match happens." He smiles and then sneaks out with one of his associates who was waiting nearby. I then turn and see that the camera is already filming. So naturally I smile and begin my piece. "Why hello there plebeians, miscreants, and deviants watching at home," I say. "For those of you living under a rock, or, in Mr. Mason's case, living in a hyperbolic chamber, my name is Evil James and I'm here to address the stupidity that my so-called opponent has put out for the masses. Basically, it is the equivalent of this..." http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zbdIn...eature=related "Wow," I say with a grin on my face. "Mr. Viagra. What the **** kind of trash talk is that? Is that from the third grade? Seriously, did you come up with that on your own or did you read it off of a popsicle stick? And seriously, do I give a **** about what you did before this match?" I shake my head. "Hell ****ing no," I say as I continue on. "All I care about is increasing the body count that is sure to be piled up by tournament's end. You are the first sacrifice to Lord Tioneb. You talk about experience in the ring. I've been doing this for six years and I've been kicking as all over the wrestling community. Yet you tend to think of me as some sort of mountain climber trying to reach the highest peak of the Himalayas." "And you compared me to George W. Bush?" I ask with a chuckle. "You sound like a mildly retarded member of some kids related television program with how you're talking. And yes, you are my he-***** man-slave and I will prove it when I beat you in the first round when we meet. And this has NOTHING to do with athletic prowess you cum sucking man-whore. This is about me and you trying to out talk and out wit each other." I smile as I say this. The idiot doesn't realize that I thrive on this kind of stuff and will eventually go to a point where I make him cry for his mommy. "Oh, and I mentionned your 'wins' just so you could bring up the fact that you lost all those matches." I have a hard time saying this since it's so hilarious that this guy thinks he's such a bad ass when he's gotten his ass kicked so many times in a row. "Seriously, you're a bigger jobber than anyone in this tournament," I say with a devious grin on my face. "You say you can't figure out what kind of promo I'm doing? Seriously, do you have some sort of mental disability? Do you have Downs Syndrome? Autism perhaps? Maybe a hint of Cerebral Palsy with your ability to talk me on the same level as some first grader saying 'You're so fat, your belt size is Equator.' Yeah, I'm really shaking in my boots over your abilities Mr. Mason." It's hard to for me to keep a straight face as I say that, but somehow I manage to pull it off. "So, it's been nine months since you've been in a wrestling ring. What did you do? Give birth? Was it a he, a she, an it, or a ****? I bet it has your eyes. And you just happened to mention the one thing that came to mind while I was watching your old footage. Ding dongs. Yes, that really brought such JOY to me seeing that. Speaking of Ding Dongs, since you are a raging man-whore and all, who knocked you up? Was it Fusenhoff? Who? Come on. Let us all in on the baby's daddy secret." "And thanks for letting me know about your little 'leg' problem. I'll be sure to **** it up some more for you or, perhaps if you're lucky, I'll break it for you just so you'll have an excuse to come back nine months from now and do this same old schtick where you try to act all tough but are really nothing but a fragile shell of what you once were. A fragile shell that I intend on smashing with a hammer and splattering all over the place when we meet." This brings a smile on my face like I was a new father or something. I haven't been so happy in such a long time. Makes me take great pleasure in torturing this guy when we meet in the ring. "And you were a baseball player. How wonderful! It's such a wonderful and 'clean' sport. So have you taken roids? Do they shrivel up your penis? Is that how you grew a vagina? I'm sure everyone watching at home is wondering that as well. I do double as a broadcast journalist, so I must as these hard questions for the people at home. It's part of the job description." "Nice going with those tryouts. I'll bet it was so much fun for you to go home, drink your sorrows away, and realize that you will never be able to join any team other than the ones in your local neighborhood softball league that plays every Saturday morning." "And let's be honest here. If you win this tournament, Hell will freeze over. Seriously, you aren't going to win. We both know this. With your bum league and the talking ability of a eight year old, you don't stand a chance in Hell of winning this thing. However, I am in this tournament for a reason as well. See, I'm in this little faction called the Church of the Unholy over in GCW and we need some money to combat the most evil organization on the planet, one even more evil than us. They are...the Cult of Obama." Just saying their name even makes someone as evil as me shudder. "We need to money to keep them from taking over the world and enslaving us all. So I am here to basically 'save you' and the rest of the world from their own doomed future. The Apocalypse is coming and we here at the Church of the Unholy are just trying to prevent the Antichrist from taking over and instead install new leadership. Our fallen elder God Tioneb." "But, until that happens, we will slaughter anyone in our way and that includes you Mason. You can act like a bad ass by talking about your Chicago mob ties. But let me tell you this. I've killed over three hundred people the past two years so nothing you say and do will scare or perturb me in any way. You won't get in my head. Oh, and almost took over the world with a Russian dicatator so I was in more than a five front war so I've got you beat there too small fry. Anything you say and do will only help me. I thrive in chaotic environments like this. You'll just add more fuel to my fire and lose. It's that simple." "You call yourself a 'wounded animal' but all I see and hear is a semi-retarded jackass. You can make all the claims you want, I could care less. You don't scare me. You are nothing but an insolent pile of dead weight that needs to be sent to the slaughterhouse to be put out of its misery. Soon you will sleep with the fishes and go back to being nothing more than the curtain jerker you are." I then grin upon saying this. "Oh, and one more thing. I kill. Trust me on that one." I pull out a knife with obivious blood stains on it just to prove the point and to show my opponent that his 'feeble attempt' to make me in 'awe' of him failed miserably. Just then an Italian guy in a Armani suit walks over. His hair is slicked back and he is a bit husky. I smile at the man and get up to shake his hand. I also hand him an envelope filled with money. "Hello Vito. I've found the guy your boss has been looking for and I will add some money to the bounty on his head as well." I then hand him a picture of Ace Mason and he smiles as he looks at it before walking away. I smile deviously as I know this'll change things up before the match. Psychological warfare at its finest.
