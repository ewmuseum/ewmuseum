The Cash Experience.....

8 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/08/11 *We are taken backstage, where a man in a black suit, black dress shirt, white tie and a cowboy hat is sitting on a stool, in the interview area. It also appears that this man has a rather large belt buckle on as well and a bit of a crowd gathered around him. He is sitting the stool, with a fender guitar strapped to him, resting some on his lap as he speaks.* Man: "Hello, for those of you who don't know me, I'm Christopher....Carter....Cash!" *The Man in Black waits for some kind of applause or acknowledgement before continuing.* Christopher Carter Cash: "You'll hear more about me in a minute. But I would like to introduce you humanoid peons to my little clique of associates. First, that big dumb guy is my bodyguard and occasional tag partner, Izak." *A man easily 7 foot and well over 300 lbs. raises his fist to signal that he is said bodyguard as a female objets* Female: "Chris! You can't say that!" Christopher Carter Cash: "And that would be my legal representation and PR person, Tiffany N. Ashcroft..she never lets me have any fun...Look, we've been over this...Izak is a giant, he doesn't speak so he is mute, and I do believe he is of the male gender...God I hope he is....I'd hate to think there could be a female that hideous....anyway, by defition, he is a big dumb guy....Lets see, moving on....the guy in the tacky green suit is my business associate, Cole T. Profit..." *The business man and self proclaimed "Christian millionare" waves as Tiffany realizes she can't win the arguement and seems to apologize to Izak. The Redneck Rocker continues.* Christopher Carter Cash: "The biker looking guy is my manager, the Hardcore Christian, Golgotha...the guy with the mask is my personal interviewer, MR. E. I know there are people here in Global Championship Wrestling for those things, but I personally decided to bring my own in on my own payroll...rather on Coles payroll, as he is funding this. And finally, the ugly stinky guy is my personal personal trainer.Again, on Cole's payroll...But enough about them, its time to talk about GCW's newest and best commodity...yours truely!" *Triple C tunes his guitar some and smirks, looking right into the camera.* Christopher Carter Cash: "I reckon there are a lot of sci fi geeks out there. Figure since this is wrestling, the geeks watching have to be like quadruple that of a normal show. So I plan to relate to the people who may like BBC, comedies, SCi-fi, and even music...the nerdiest of the nerds...Red Dwarf Fans!" *After one final tuning check, Christopher Carter Cash starts to play his guitar some and then he sings* Christopher Carter Cash: "If you are depressed, he will make your day He's bravery will make you cheer hip horay He's even made straight men gay He's Chris Topher Carter Cash Without him teeth would gnash He's handsome, trim and no not rash He seasons better than Ms. Dash He's Chris Topher Carter Cash More reliable than Hulk will smash His wit is quicker than the Flash He plays guitar better than Slash Master of the ring and the keg party His comand of submission holds is uncanny How come he has genious ab-il-ity He's Chris Tofer Carter Cash He also has fantastic hash And if you play your cards right He just might share his stash He's Chris Topher Carter Cash He rhymes will cut you like a gash He hopes he is always remains brash He's good at talking trash." *The alleged rock superstar stands up and accepts the somewhat forced applause of his clique as the scene fades out.*
