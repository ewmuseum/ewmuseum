Cold Shivers

1 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 1st, 2005 :::It’s been building up inside me for quite some time now. It isn’t by coincidence for me to succeed in my most recent of tasks. I have taken down Octavian twice now. Back to back victories over the Raging Roman. If I were to let it sit for one second more, things possibly could have turned violent. I wasn’t about to let glory slip away. NOT AGAIN!::: (Knocking is heard from the outer surface of an all pine solid door. A voice sounding “Come in” echoing in a graceful manner welcoming me to a room of sheer wealth. I turn the knob and greet myself to the man who should be giving me more. At least offering me much, much more. There in his fine leather chair sits the respectful Steven Caldera, the man who has the power to steal from the rich and give to the poor. Here I see as the poor waiting for wealth, waiting for an opportunity at supremacy, a chance to dominate all the less deserving than me. I should be the one showing these rookies the ropes. I should be the one showing them what GCW has to offer. A pure veteran like me should be receiving more attention than the simple task of polishing off a has been. I wanted Steven to know just how it was I felt inside.) Caldera- “Hey Mike. What can I do for you?” “You can do a lot more than just sit there and feed me these bullshit matches.” Caldera- “Excuse me”!” “Yeah, while I’m off laying the laws out on Octy you’re off denying me another appearance on World Wide! Is that it Steven” Am I just used for marketing sales? To sell tickets to the shows that matter?” Caldera- “First off”..You’re in no position to tell me what shows matter. Believe it or not Mike there are a lot more people who want it more than you do as of late. You come off showing me what I have created once again. An Egotistical son of a bitch! You’ve been showing up late for shows, you’ve ditched a few local autograph appearances for the fans. What happened to the Mike we all loved, the Mike we all wanted to see succeed? The Mike who wasn’t just another sell out?” “Another sell out” You’re telling me that I shouldn’t be soaking up the glory? I have my reasons to brag Mr. President, I have been busting ass here trying to get a shot at what you’re never going to offer me. You’re just going to forget about me. Forget about what I could have been capable of. I’m looking to be back in the GOD DAMN CRUISERWEIGHT DIVISION! I’m not rookie anymore Steven! I am no longer the lovable Mike. This Mike has learned. This Mike has been through it all! And this Mike will not degrade himself back to the nothingness Cruiserweight division again.” Caldera- “Look, you have no right criticizing the division that not only shaped you, but made you who you are. You would not be standing here if it weren’t for the Cruiserweight division. I made you god damn it! I gave you your chances, and more will arise. But not if you’re going to cough that kind of an attitude at me.” :::I can’t face myself to strike back at him with another filthy degrading comment. He has the power to mortify me as his secretary in the snap of his swift, slim little fingers. I have to play my cards right. Maybe I should cool off and take some time to think before I come back in the manner I have today. He may have won this round but I am not goin to give up that easy. Not without a fight.::: (Sounding much more calmer and collected) “Steven, all I want to do is reach my goal in life. I set my goals and standards much higher than you have been setting them these past few weeks. I not only want to make you or the fans proud, I want to make myself proud as well. I know I am there Steven. I’m at the top of my game. And I can’t play my game when I’m sitting on the sidelines watching the time click down on the clock. It isn’t good enough to say I saw the big game rather to say I was a part of it. I want to be a part of this Company. I want my name upon the others, to be up there with my fellow friends, foes, and future colleagues. For once let me be what I know I can be!” Caldera- “Mike, Like I said before, I can’t just hand you the world. It is a long process to suffer through. All the greats of the past and the present have fought through it. I’m not guaranteeing the future will hold great success for you. But I will not deny the chance of dominance as well. Take it one step at a time here. Your chance will shine once again. That’s all I can promise you. Till then, that promise will have to do.” :::He’s got a way with words, I’ll give him that, but he better not make me wait much longer, for if he does, he won’t have the breath in him to speak much except his final words. All I can do is grit my teeth and bear with it.::: “Sorry for coming off as such a dick, you know I appreciate all you have done. It’s just that I want this so bad, and I?”” Caldera- “I know Mike, I know”.” :::I shake my head just to hold back from slapping him for cutting me off. He’s mutual to show he cares that I still have my heart in this, but he has no idea. Not a single FUCKING idea. I will be champion, and no one will stop me. Not NO ONE!:::
