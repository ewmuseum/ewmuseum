The Grind

26 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/26/11 A cold shower soothes the burning limbs brought on by his morning ritual and brings Andy back to the land of the living. His watch reads 09:02, but he’s already been up for 4 hours. 2½ hours of cardio and lifts, then it’s upstairs to make breakfast and ready Marvin for school. He gets back half-an-hour later and slogs through housework he’s been delaying since last weekend. He’s already done enough to exhaust an average man by the time he’s taken the trash out. After showering Andy heads back downstairs. A couple of painkillers get scoffed and that nagging twang around his shoulder stars to ease. Andy gets his day’s schedule from his assistant and skims through it. He sighs. He grabs the phone and it doesn’t leave his ear until lunchtime. The hours pass glacially as every reporter he speaks to ask him the same questions over and over, and by the time it’s finished he just wants to rest. But he can’t. A quick sandwich and it’s off to his grappling coach for the day’s second workout and a technique seminar. Andy showers again at the gym and picks Marvin up on the way home. Five minutes late for a conference call with GCW brass, Andy hurries to set his laptop up and offers his best humble apology when Johnny appears on the screen. Dinner’s cooked and finished by 7, then it’s time to deal with the curveballs he’s thrown daily. Every time he opens his inbox there’s at least one person trying to screw his schedule up, and tonight’s no different. This time it’s the writers telling him they want to take the Terror feud in a different direction, and when Andy reads their email he wants to smash his fist through the screen. Andy takes a moment to get a glass of water and breathe deeply. He replies, hoping that his feigned understanding comes through. It’s finally time for bed after his evening run. Andy puts his head down just before 11, but the aches and pains coursing through his battle-ravaged body keep him awake for hours before he’s able to sleep. When he finally drifts away, Andy dreams of a time when this wasn’t such a chore.
