Vince Jones: A Humiliated Man, A Broken Man...A Lonely Man (Part 2)

4 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/4/2005 The screen is black and the low, gentle voice of a narrator is heard once again speaking in the background. 'Another day in the life of 'The Violence' Vince Jones leading up until his next match. Still he walks with his head hung low. Would today be any different? Today Vince Jones was to meet with 'The Agent of the Stars' Reno Banks as a humiliated man, a broken man...a lonely man.' 'The Lonely Man', the old Incredible Hulk ending theme begins to play once again. The camera slowly fades into the lobby outside the office of Reno Banks. Vince Jones can be seen sitting in the lobby with his head hanging low patiently waiting for Reno to show. All of a sudden the door to Reno's office swings open and Reno appears. He looks at Vince Jones and just shakes his head in disappointment with his pitiful state of being. Reno Banks; Mr. Jones, what are you doing here? Vince Jones: Gotta talk to you 'bout some sh*t, Reno. Reno Banks nods his head. Reno Banks: I see. Well, its nice of you to show up, but I'm a little busy at the moment. Vince Jones: Busy with what? Reno Banks: This is actually my regularly scheduled lunch time. I'll have to talk to you afterwards. Vince Jones; Aight! Whateva, man Reno turns and spots a large Incredible Hulk action figure sitting on a table next to Vince Jones and laughs to himself. Reno Banks: Hmmm...my son must've left that Hulk action figure when he came by to visit with my wife earlier this morning. I'll have to grab that later. Alright. Well, I'm going to go eat lunch now. Be back with you later. Reno Banks vanishes into his office and Vince Jones just sits there alone in the lobby. All is silent with exception to the piano music playing in the background. Vince Jones sits there in self-pity and boredom. After a moment of just sitting there alone he turns and sees the Incredible Hulk action figure. He picks it up and examines it a little. He spots a string in the back of it and pulls it. Incredible Hulk Doll: Hulk Smash!! Vince Jones pulls the string again. Incredible Hulk Doll: GRRRRRRRR!!!! Vince Jones pulls the string again. Incredible Hulk Doll: AAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!!!! The door leading to the lobby slowly swings open and in steps a rather tall and built man who likes like he could be another wrestler. The man has his brown hair slicked back. He is wearing dark shades, a leather jacket on, a pair of blue jeans, and black boots on his feet. He lowers the dark shades and spots Vince Jones playing with the Incredible Hulk doll and chuckles to himself. The wrestler: So, this is what you do with your spare time, Vince? Vince Jones looks up at him and rolls his eyes. Vince Jones; V. Jones waitin' to talk to Reno 'bout some sh*t, Crusher. We supposed to be talkin' after he finishes his lunch. Crusher: That's funny. I'm supposed to be talking with Reno too. Vince Jones: Yeah. Well, you gonna be waitin' around out here for awhile cuz V. Jones speakin' with him first. Crusher smirks at the thought. Crusher: Yeah. Whatever, Vince. All of a sudden the door of Reno's office swings open and Reno's eyes grow wide as he spots Crusher standing in the lobby. Reno flashes his trademark sly grin. Reno Banks: Well, well, well, if it isn't my favorite client, Crusher 'The Human Highlight' Helix. [motions for him to enter his office] C'mon inside. You and I need to discuss business. Vince Jones turns and looks at Reno in disbelief. Reno sees the expression on Vince's face and smiles. Reno Banks: Oh, I forget to check my appointments, Mr. Jones. My mistake. Crusher Helix was supposed to be meeting with me at this time. Well, don't worry. I'll be with you after this meeting is over. Reno steps inside his office. Crusher Helix looks at Vince Jones with a smug look on his face and laughs. Crusher Helix: Well, I guess we know where you stand in the pecking order right now, ya bum. Crusher Helix steps inside Reno's office and shuts the door behind him leaving Vince alone in the lobby with the Incredible Hulk doll in hand. Vince Jones looks down at the doll and just shakes his head. Vince Jones: This some bullsh*t! V was supposed to be talkin' to Reno right about now and he gets bumped down the list by that b*tch ass, Crusher Helix! Sh*t! What the hell you think 'bout that crap, huh? Vince looks down at the Incredible Hulk doll and pulls the string. Incredible Hulk Doll: GRRRRRRRRR!!!!!! Vince Jones nods in agreement. Vince Jones: Yeah. V feelin' the same way 'bout that sh*t, man. You best believe that. So, what would you do 'bout that if you were in this situation, man? Vince Jones pulls the string on the doll. Incredible Hulk Doll: Hulk Smash!!! Vince Jones: (nodding) Yeah. V oughta do that sh*t, oughta bust some heads. Well, what you think the chances are of V's tag team pullin' off of a win at WorldWide this week? Vince Jones pulls the string. Incredible Hulk Doll: GRRRRRRRRRR!!!! Vince Jones rolls his eyes. Vince Jones: You tryin' to say we gonna lose or somethin'? Vince Jones pulls the string. Incredible Hulk Doll: AAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!! Vince Jones: To hell with you, b*tch made! The door to Reno's office swings open as Vince Jones continues to bicker with the Incredible Hulk doll. Reno and Crusher step out and look at Vince Jones with concerned expressions on their faces. Vince Jones: You think V. Jones'll come out this damn slump anytime soon? Vince pulls the string. Incredible Hulk Doll: GRRRRRRRRRRR!!!!! Vince Jones glares at the doll in fury and then lowers his head in shame. Vince Jones: Damn you, b*tch! Reno Banks clears his throat immediately getting the attention of Vince. Reno Banks: Mr. Jones, you are being counseled by a toy. You know that, right? (sighs) Well, I hope your mindset is a little better at WorldWide this week for your tag match. I really want to see you start turning things around. In the meantime, I need to fly out to Jamaica to meet up with another one of my clients. You might know him, Mr. Jamaican Sensation himself. Vince Jones looks at Reno in surprise. Vince Jones: What about V, huh? You said we were supposed to be talkin' business today. Crusher Helix laughs outloud. Crusher Helix: You heard the man. He ain't got time for you, Vince. You'll just have to talk to him some other time. Oh, and get rid of the toys, ya big baby! Crusher and Reno leave the lobby and the camera slowly fades to black as Vince Jones lowers his head in shame.
