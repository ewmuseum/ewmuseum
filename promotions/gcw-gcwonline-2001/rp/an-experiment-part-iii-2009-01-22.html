An Experiment, Part III

22 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 22nd, 2009 An Experiment Part III: Part Time Mad Scientist A newspaper is opened up. Going by the date on it, it’s the 22nd of January. There’s a note on top of the front page of a story further inside. The note reads: “Husband and daughter presumed dead after two months of searching; Widow continues to search.” A crackly voice projects from behind the newspaper. “Well, she won’t be finding them anytime soon...” says Face, a short, prickly laugh following. He continues to read through what one can assume is the article that was advertised on the front page. It is at this point that his “guest” speaks up, his drug-induced sleep now coming to a very unpleasant halt. “Mmm! Mmmmmmm!” is all that comes out from the man on the other side of the desk Face is sitting at. Not bothering to put his paper down, Face responds to the sudden attempt at communication. “What’s the matter” Hmmmm? You got tape stuck over your mouth?” “MMMmmmmm!” Face folds up the newspaper and tosses it onto the floor. “Ah, that’s right.” Face rips the duct tape from the man’s mouth and flicks it off to the side. Judging from this man’s custom tailored suit ? which is now ravaged and torn ? he is someone who is on the “affluent” side of the income spectrum. As soon as the duct tape is removed, the grovelling and deal-making begins and the man’s affluence is made quite clear. “Lo-Look, I...I don’t know who you are, but I can give you the money now. There’s no need for a ransom or anything.” Face tilts his head, showing interest in the suggestion. The man takes this as a cue to continue. “I can write you a nice, BIG cheque and we can both pretend this never happened!” The man waits for a response, but Face remains in the same inquisitive position. “Look, I have a lot of mon...money...” The man stops when he hears a quiet chuckle coming from behind his captor’s mask. What little hope he displayed during his financial offering is wiped completely from his face. A look of confusion replaces it and the feeling that he’s been left on the outside of an inside joke consumes him. Face let’s him in on the hilarity of the situation after his quiet chuckling subsides. “You know...this is the fourth time I’ve talked to you in this exact situation...” The confusion thickens. “And each time...the only thing you’ve done is continuously offer to pay me loads of cash to get out of here.” Incoherent mumbling is all that comes out from the wealthy man as he attempts to remember these past talks he’s apparently had with Face. This is all too rich for Face, who takes out his trademark lighter. He flicks it open and closed a few times, a flame constantly being ignited and extinguished, providing brief moments of an orange glow throughout the otherwise dull and grey concrete room. “Do you think I’ll accept your offer, knowing that you’ve been woken up and put back to sleep at a constant frequency, not unlike the flame my lighter produces?” Face waits for an answer, but his captive just stares blankly, unable to get a full grasp on this situation. After a few moments, Face gets up from his chair and casually heads over to the other side of the desk, where the man is quite helplessly bounded. He pulls out a knife from his back pocket as he gets closer to his latest victim. Tears begin to trickle down the man’s face, his life flashing before his eyes. Thoughts race through his mind as Face stops directly behind him: “This is how my life ends” At the hands of some whack-job? I’m a self-made millionaire who’s about to be killed by someone who looks like a freaking hobo!” The idea of being killed by someone so much poorer than him continues to swim around in his head as he waits for the inevitable stabbing from his captor’s knife. Much to his surprise, he instead feels the sudden release of his arms from his bonds. Face walks back around the desk and sits back in his chair, placing the knife back in his pocket. His now semi-free captive tries to contemplate why his arms are now free and why he isn’t dead. Face makes this clear soon enough. “You have a chequebook in one pocket of your coat and a pen in the other... I’ve already written my account number on the first cheque. Let’s see that money of yours.” And, just like that, hope returns. The man quickly removes the chequebook and pen from his pockets and makes out a cheque like his life depended on it...which is most likely exactly true. A few moments later, he rips off the first cheque from the chequebook and slides it over to Face. Face picks up the cheque and looks at it for a few agonizing seconds. The man becomes quickly becomes antsy. “Is there a problem” Is...is it not enough?” Face shakes his head. “No...a cool million is certainly a nice payday.” Those words comfort the man like no other words in his life have before. He can physically feel a bullet just grazing his shoulder as it flies on by. And that’s when Face takes his lighter...and burns the million dollar cheque right in front of the man’s eyes. At first, he chalks it up as some sort of trick his mind is playing on him. After all, he’s apparently been drugged multiple times today. And then the reality sinks in...along with all that hope that had gathered up. The man can only look up at Face with crushed, befuddled eyes as he watches the last of the cheque disintegrate in the flame. Face lets out another cold-hearted chuckle, snapping his lighter shut to return it to its regular cycle. “You know, that look of crushed hope is just so priceless every time...” exclaims Face in-between chuckles. “But, I think four times is enough.” The chuckling stops abruptly, a closing of the lighter adding a subtle emphasis, as if to point out that Face had been stringing along this man on a steady beat this whole time. Everything’s planned. Everything’s cold. Everything’s calculated. And, unfortunately for the wealthy man sitting in front of Face’s desk...the plan has just begun to unfold. Face has had some experience now with his researching procedures. He’s been able to hone his craft with each successful experiment... ***A month or so ago...*** “Someone will apprehend you eventually. Do you not realize this?” says the man, a Caucasian looking to be in his mid-twenties. Face shakes his finger at him. “You know, another one of my research subjects said that...” points out Face, who begins to pace around his prisoner’s chair. He’s holding a jacket in his left hand and his lighter in his right. The jacket has the emblem of Harvard University on it and the back reads: Dr. William Jackson PhD Physics 2008 “Tell me, doctor...Do you really see a bug or are you trying to diagnose me with the common cold, without looking for any symptoms?” Dr. Jackson raises his eyebrow at the tangential metaphor from his title. “Well, the symptoms are obvious in this case. You kidnap people. You won’t be able to evade sight from outsiders for much longer and your appearance sticks out like a sore thumb. In fact, I would say you’ve chosen perhaps one of the worst disguises I’ve ever seen, considering you want to remain secretive.” Face takes in the very “matter of fact” answer, pleased with the fact that an intellectual conversation is arising. The larger the mental capacity of his victims, the greater their mental breakdowns tend to be. “It’s ironic that you see my stand-out appearance to be detrimental to my research, because that’s not even half the challenge I’ve presented myself with...” Face pauses, letting William’s curiosity rise. “You see, not only is my image unique...it’s broadcasted on national television just about every week. I’m a wrestler in a very popular wrestling promotion that goes by the name of GCW. I’ve just started recently, but millions of people would have already seen my image. And, as time progresses, there will only be more and more people who can recognize me in public.” William’s first reaction to what Face just said is slow laughter, stemming from disbelief. However, as Face continues to stare at him, he soon begins to realize just how serious he was being. The sheer amount of risk Face gives himself makes no sense, statistically. Why would a man go out of his way to make his dark deeds more easily noticed? Why would someone voluntarily put himself in the public eye if his activities required him to be out of the public eye? These questions and more begin to plague the mind of the Harvard graduate, his PhD in Physics telling him to trust numbers and calculations and statistics more than irrational thoughts, which are what his captor seems to run on. Face gets a kick out of the visible struggle Jackson is having within himself. “You can’t comprehend it, can you” It doesn’t fit in with your nice laws and your fancy models and your reliable number crunching nature. Of course, a man such as yourself, with a mind trained so far into one discipline, would be unable to comprehend the thoughts of someone trained into a completely opposite discipline; a discipline of risk-taking, challenges and danger; a discipline of excitement!” Face lets himself be absorbed in the moment, setting Jackson’s jacket aflame with his lighter and tossing it at the Physics major’s feet. The fire doesn’t go out on impact. Instead, it grows as it consumes more and more of the jacket. Jackson is unable to do anything about it as he is tightly secured to his chair. He begins to sweat and panic as the flames tickle at his legs and the legs of the chair he is sitting on. Face just observes at first, taking in the panic and fear and distress of his captive. He truly enjoys torturing people. He loves watching them squirm and beg and get taken completely out of their comfort zone. For whatever reason, Face feels absolutely no guilt in harming other people, to the point where he can actually enjoy the challenge and results of doing just that. Whether Face knows the root of his lack of conscience or not is a mystery in itself, but it doesn’t seem to matter much for those that come in contact with him. In their eyes, he’s just a sick, sadistic freak. And, for all intents and purposes...they would be right in that assumption... *A couple of weeks later...* A woman sits, emotionally paralyzed, in a rapidly growing pool of blood. Her hands are covered in blood thanks to her placing them on the blood-soaked concrete she’s sitting on. Her torso and neck seem to be covered in blood from a splattering, however. The reason for this is clear when two and two get put together: a dead man with a gaping gash across his neck lying in front of the woman and a bloody dagger directly beside her on the ground. It’s truly a gruesome sight for anyone to see. Well, almost anyone. “Well, he didn’t put up much of a fight, did he” Hmmm?” Face chuckles at the entire grotesque situation, which, at first glance, isn’t even half as grotesque as it really is. What most people would not be able to determine from this scene is that the woman is a few weeks pregnant and that the man she just killed was expecting a child of his own. It’s impossible to comprehend how someone could behave so casually around it all, knowing exactly what the situation was. It’s impossible to decipher how a mind can block out all moral values so completely. Regardless of Face’s ability to absorb this scene with actual content, the future mother is forever scarred beyond repair. She doesn’t show any signs of even hearing what Face just asked her, let alone responding to it. She’s just stuck in a perpetual gaze at what she has done to the potential father in front of her. “His wife will certainly be crushed when she realizes her husband is not coming back to her. She’ll be forced into being a single mom, all because of you.” Face waits for a response, a retort, but there is none and there won’t be any. “It must be a terrible feeling...guilt, empathy...” Face paces around his most recent experiment, picking up the man’s knife, which he cast away upon finding out the woman he was told to kill was expecting a baby. “Nobility...selflessness...look where that got him.” Face looks up and down the dagger’s short blade, continuing to pace around and speak to the woman’s deaf ears. “Some people say I’m out of control, but if I was out of control, I’d be in a jail cell or a padded room by now. I’m only a part time mad scientist, after all...” Face is obviously getting a kick out of all of this. He soon stops his pacing, standing directly behind his remaining research subject. “They say I’m sick...” With one fluid motion, Face puts the woman out of her misery, slashing her throat in a similar manner to how she slashed the other man’s. Her now officially lifeless body flops to the ground to add even more blood to the substantial pool beneath Face’s feet. He examines the murder weapon one last time before tossing it to the side. “That may be true, but I’m not the one suffering the symptoms...” Diabolical laughter to end all diabolical laughter begins to fill the room. It’s a wonder Face can even exist within society, let alone an international wrestling organization. What people see is a twisted man. What they don’t realize is that Face only shows the tip of that unpleasant iceberg. They should consider themselves lucky that Face doesn’t blur the lines too much between his public appearance and private intentions. After all, the worst things his opponents have gotten because of him are knee injuries. The dead woman and man below him would consider that fate to be like winning the lottery in comparison to what they had to go through... *Back on the 22nd of January...* The wealthy man looks up at Face, not sure what else he can do. He’s as surprised as ever when Face actually begins to untie the rest of his bonds. Thinking his captor has somehow had a change of heart, the poor man doesn’t bother asking why he’s being “released.” However, when Face unties the final knot and allows for him to stand up for the first time in what must have been many hours, the wealthy man can’t help but question Face for his change of heart. “So, you’ve decided to accept the money after all” You’re going to let me go?” An unseen smirk curls up beneath Face’s mask...
