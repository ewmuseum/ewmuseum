The End of Solitude [part II]

27 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/27/10 "Look, there are only so many times we can go over the same issues, Mister O'Neill. I am only going to make this offer once. I am telling you, I can promise you if you listen to me, you will make it to the top." The discussion had been a borderline argument for some time now. Sean O'Neill's initial interest into how this individual knew so much about him, and how he operated, had now tapered off. He had suspected that this man who sat opposite him would have some ulterior motive. The second he had been able to identify the man, he knew that his suspicions had been confirmed. "I still don't see what you can offer me. It's not like I'm some kind of rookie." O'Neill took another gulp of his Bushmills whiskey. The drink would normally be starting to hit him around now, but this meeting had kept his mind focused enough so that the amount of alcohol he'd put away hadn't reached its usual effect. No doubt it would in the morning. "Yes, yes, we both know you are not exactly fresh blood, but surely that competitive drive you pride yourself on has an interest in what I have to say?" He was right, of course; the intrigue of the possibility of his claims of taking SurReal to the top of the business was probably the only thing that had allowed the conversation to continue on so long. Three and a half hours and counting. But try as he might to envisions conquering Global Championship Wrestling, he just couldn't imagine doing it with him at his side. "Of course it does. I'm sorry to harp back to it. But I made my wrestling debut at fourteen years old. That means I have been wrestling for twenty one years. Twenty one years of learning, improving, expanding skills, mastering holds and techniques others never learn in lifetimes..." "Yes, but no offence, twenty one years without a single major recognised championship! Face it, for all your dedication to mastering your craft, you have neglected the one thing that defines how you will be remembered. Unless, that is, you want to be remembered as the greatest scientific wrestler who never held championship gold?" "I've held titles you know!" "The 'titles' you held on the independent circuit? Those championships you held in Japan and Mexico? Who regards them, Mister O'Neill? Certainly not the fans here in America, and certainly not Global Championship Wrestling. Since you came to the United States, what have you achieved?" O'Neill tries to counter the argument but the words fail him. Whether he likes it or not, he was right. As things stood, his legacy would be that of a man with unlimited potential that was never fulfilled. The professional wrestling industry IS America. These fans didn't care about his overseas exploits, nor the minor titles he had held before making the 'big time'. And he was right again, since breaking the 'big time' he could list his achievements on one hand. A few notable victories aside, he had nothing to show for himself. He exhaled a deep sigh before posing his question. "Ok, why me?" The question raised a wry smile. "Do you not see? It is the perfect match. You are the perfect weapon... your skills-set is practically unrivalled. Certainly, I could have approached one of these new kids on the block, the boy you defeated last week for example, Slash. He is fresh young clay I could have moulded in my own image. But why spend all the time and effort that it would take to build him up to your level of talent? With the right direction behind you, you could be unstoppable!" Perhaps the drink was starting to affect him now, or was it that his ego had been stroked so masterfully? As he replayed those words over in his mind he began to think that perhaps it wasn't such a bad idea after all. There was a period of silence now as both men sipped at their drinks. They were both content to let the idea run through O'Neill's mind a few more times. As he did so, scepticism crept back into his thoughts and raised yet more questions. He took another gulp of whiskey and asked the most obvious of those questions. "And what exactly do you get out of helping me? What benefit is there to you, for you to personally to guide me to the top?" "What do you mean?" "Well... It's not like people will look and think, 'SurReal is only where he is today because he trained him.' They know you haven't played any part in my training." "Ah, but you contradict yourself! Did you not tell me only a matter of minutes ago that you are always learning? It is true that I have not taught you holds or manoeuvres, although there are a few that perhaps you may want to make better use of, but we can discuss that later. You see, a champion is not made on their ability in the ring alone. You know this. It is probably the biggest factor that has held you down all this time. You think only as a wrestler thinks; working with me will train you to think as a champion thinks. Did you know that your profile page on the GCW web-site declares 'SurReal attacks his opponent on two fronts, both physically and mentally'. That is not accurate though, is it? You've gotten cocky in your old age. Gone are the days when you played mind games with the opponent. Let me help you, Sean." O'Neill's eyebrow raised involuntarily as he tried to decipher whether or not the last statement was intended as a compliment or criticism. "If you get to the top, that means power, if I am alongside you, then it means power for me too. That is what I want, power. And let us just say that I have goals that would be best achieved from a position of power within the company." O'Neill couldn't contain a snort at this. He followed up with a sarcastic glance that didn't go unnoticed. "Yes, I am quite aware of the irony, but we have discussed this enough. What do you say? Are you with me, or rather am I with you?" O'Neill lay back in his seat and let out another long breath, slowly he moved his head back into position to stare right at him. He opened his mouth to deliver his answer. SEVERAL DAYS LATER... Having arrived in Hawaii early to prepare for a match that probably ranks as the biggest of his career, even more important than his NC-17 debut. Sean O'Neill had extended an invite to join him to one of the very few friends he had kept in touch with, a retired Canadian wrestler and mixed martial arts fighter by the name of Ryan Khuth, Ryan, who had competed under the name Ryan Dynamic, had been O'Neill's road partner in the early days of their respective careers. They had trained and travelled together in Japan and Mexico and when the time came to try and break into the American wrestling business they had even debuted as a team. Although as O'Neill thought back about his days as one half of 'The Dynamic Duo' posing as Ryan's in-character brother he could barely contain his embarrassment. As they sat out on the balcony of the luxury apartment O'Neill had rented for his stay in the country, they discussed at great length the past year of his career. Despite Ryan's constant interruptions with horribly bad one liner jokes, the Canadian had been listening intently. A certain aura of excitement was evident as Ryan began to take more of an interest in his ever increasing rivalry with Tempest. Ryan, as he well knew, had always had a thing for Tempest when he'd seen her competing for Old School Wrestling. O'Neill couldn't help but feel that perhaps Ryan wouldn't be so against the idea of getting back into the ring if it meant getting up close and personal with the bitch. The conversation inevitably rolled around to the last two weeks, and of course, this new offer of assistance being presented to him. The horny joking about Tempest had passed, and now Ryan looked more than a little concerned for his friend. "Hold on bro, you're actually considering this?" Ryan's face could barely contain a look that was a mixture of confusion and disgust. "You know this dude's the devil right? You are seriously considering selling your soul to the devil?" The slightest raise of his shoulders demonstrated that the Irishman was indeed giving the proposal a great deal of consideration. In fact, by this stage he was really rather coming around to the idea. The more time he spent mulling it over, the more it seemed to be the right thing for him at this point in his career. "Look Ryu..." "No no, hold the phone Sean! You really think it's going to be as simple as that? You would be nothing more than a pawn. That's not the Sean O'Neill I know!" "Will you let me speak you lady-boy fuck!" O'Neill couldn't help the dig at Ryan's Siam heritage, he'd made so many jokes about it over the years that he didn't have any worries that Ryan would take any genuine offence. And knowing his friend as he did, Ryan knew that O'Neill could get really pissy when you don't let him make a point. To that end, Ryan gave a dismissive wave of the hand prompting him to continue. "Yes, I agree, this is not the actions of the Sean O'Neill you know, but he made a point that I just can't get out of my head. I don't want to be remembered as an also-ran Ryan. As a nobody who, lets face it probably won't be remembered at all actually! What was the point of these last twenty years? What was the point in my obsession with perfecting pure wrestling if I can't combine it with an obsession to write my name in history. I can't do it anymore Ryan. I can't look at myself in the mirror and take pride that I've achieved the best I could have, I haven't. I mean, look at Tempest, she can't hold a candle to the techniques I've mastered, the holds I've innovated, the manoeuvres I've perfected. Sure she can wrestle reasonably, but she can't wrestle like I CAN WRESTLE!" "What's that got to do with anything?" "EVERYTHING! SHE IS A TWO TIME WORLD CHAMPION.RYAN!" O'Neill's head drops as the will to make his point drains away. He looks more demoralised now than perhaps even Ryan has ever seen him. "Two time World Champion, what have I got? Nothing. She tasks me Ryan. She tasks me and I shall have her! I'll chase her round the moons of Nibia and round the Antares Maelstrom and round Perdition's flames before I give her up!" Ryan rolls his eyes, he should have known that something geeky was coming, O'Neill ALWAYS has some sci-fi or comic-book quote for a situation like this. "Alright Khan, if you say so! So this is why you're so prepared to sell your soul? For a shot at the big time?" "One last shot at the big time." Ryan's brow arcs instantly as O'Neill statement catches him off guard. "Say again?" "One last shot Ryan. All or nothing." An understanding creeps across Ryan's face. Reading between the lines, he understands that O'Neill means that he will focus one hundred percent of his energy, his drive and his passion behind this 'last' opportunity to rewrite his legacy. O'Neill will take whatever steps he needs to now to ensure he is not remembered as the ultimate underachiever. "You've already told him yes haven't you?" O'Neill looks up staring deep into his friend's eyes and nods gently. "I have." "Can you trust him?" "No." "Does that bother you?" "No. Not anymore" "Did you bring me the whole way out here in the hope that I would give this plan my approval?" O'Neill shrugs once more, and Ryan again nods in understanding. "Well, I can't say I'm thrilled about the idea Sean. REALLY not thrilled about the idea. But I guess I can understand why you are prepared to do it. What can I say? Go get your title, rise the ranks with him at your side, let him pull whatever strings he can. But Sean, if you look in the mirror again in six months and don't like what you see, you know there is no going back?" "You won't change my mind Ryan." "God Sean you don't need to tell me that, in all the time I've known you I can't think of one single occasion where you've ever let anyone change your mind!" The two share a smirk at the jab and the mood lightens a little as a result. "To the last, I will grapple with thee... from Hell's heart, I stab at thee! For hate's sake, I spit my last breath at thee!" "Seriously O'Neill, maybe if this great masterplan doesn't work you should audition for a new Star Wars show..." "Star Trek Ryan, and that won't be an issue, the masterplan will succeed!"
