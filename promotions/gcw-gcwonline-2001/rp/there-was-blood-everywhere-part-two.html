There Was Blood Everywhere: Part Two

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 24th, 1971 Bryan pushes through the crowd. Jason is sprawled out, legs and arms to his sides. His eyes are glossed over, and his nose is spewing blood. The blood is starting to cake in his ears and hair. Bryan is standing tall, looking more at the crowd, than at his brother when someone caught his attention. It was a older woman, she squatted down, glasses falling off the bridge of her nose. Her hair slightly graying. She pulled a blue hanky from her purse and placed her hand slightly under Jason’s head and pressed the hanky to his nose to stop the bleeding. “Who are those kids” Why did they punch this little boy?” Mayhem stares off into the distance, Tom and his clan no longer visible, than turns back and looks down at Jason who is now regaining consciousness. The woman, staring deep into Bryan, lifts her eyebrows in a sign of impatience. Bryan runs his left hand through his long hair, and looks back towards Tom’s retreating path. “Some asshole from Jason’s school.” Exhaling deliberately, “I kind of figured that, but why did they do this” What’s you name young man, where do you live?” Bryan, knowing his next sentence will be the end of this lady’s help. “O’Neil, top of the hill.” Bryan points towards hell, and that’s when the crowd steps back and begins to wither away into nothingness. They want nothing to do this child of Satan. To his surprise however, the woman continued to give first aid to Jason. “You can leave now, doesn’t matter everyone else is.” Shocked and angered she stammers back, “Why would I do that” He’s a bloody child!” “Lady, you don’t know who we are do you?” “At this point in time, No. And to be honest I don’t care. Help me, we have to get him to his feet so the blood will stop.” The entire crowd has dispersed. The O’Neils and this angel lady seemed to be in their own little bubble. The crowd that stopped to check the status of the boy, now seems to not give a damn anymore and just keep waking. You won’t even see a slight strain of the eyes as they walk by. They are either scared, or don’t give two shits about the O’Neil family. Although Bryan has made a name for himself for violent outbursts and animal torture, poor young and feeble Jason has made valiant attempts at restoring the O’Neil name. The exact opposite of his father and brother, Jason loves all of God’s creatures, avoids conflict, and usually sticks to his own. No one really knows why Jason is so different from his violent brother, abusive father, and whore of a mother, but some think it’s for the better. Once in a blue moon, you may see someone from Belmont holding a quick and witty conversation with Jason, however it is often interrupted by his brother, or a quick glance of a passer by. Jason is like the fat girl in the bar. An absolute treat to talk to, but once seen by others, an absolute embarrassment. “I am staying at the Carlisle, let me help you boys get cleaned up and get some food in your system.” Bryan amazed at the generosity of this stranger, shakes in head in disapproval. “Ma’am if I may decline, we live right on top of the hill. Thank you though.” Frowning, “No my treat, please let me help you guys.” Seeing no threat, Bryan thinks it over for a second than obliges. “If we don’t get back before sundown father will whoop us both, so we have to make this quick.” (Make what quick? That didn’t make sense Bryan) “Hogwash, don’t worry about anything, you are good hands. Follow me.” With the handkerchief still to his nose, Jason gains a bounce in his step and begins to follow the lady, and starts whistling! Bryan nudges his brother in hopes of shutting up the god awful racket of his whistling. Jason, ignores Bryan, turns his head and sticks his tongue out at this older brother and grabs the stranger’s hand. Bryan shakes his head (the kid trusts to much) and that’s when Bryan starts to smell something. His entire life every person has attempted to hurt them, and now he’s convinced this lady is here to do the same. Hand in hand, and with a giant ogre following the Stranger and the boy walk into the foyer of The Carlisle. She is smiling, Jason is smiling despite the caked blood, but the ogre is not. His face is red. He almost looks embarrassed, and most of all looks uncomfortable and unwelcoming. The trio step into the elevator and The Stranger presses the 5 button, and gives Jason’s hand a small squeeze that gains his attention, he looks up and those two share a small smile at each other. The walls in the elevator are gold, with a beautiful sheen. You can see your reflection in the walls with a slight distortion. You look a little wider and a little shorter in this elevator, so it definitely doesn’t show your best side. Bryan stares at The Stranger’s reflection (she’s evil) and starts to analyze her features (aura of black). He tilts his head slightly to the right and her reflection looks right at him. Her eyes turn black and that sinister forked tongue that has haunted him his entire life rears its ugly head and makes Bryan jump. Still holding Jason’s hand, The Stranger notices Bryan’s quick reaction to whatever it was that spooked him and looks over at him. She winks, and gives Jason that playful squeeze. The door dings open and the trio exit. They make a quick left. Bryan starts to memorize everything that has happened up until this point, just in case something happens and some sort of testimony is needed. There they are, room 519?.the numbers that changed the lives of those that stepped foot into it?.forever?..
