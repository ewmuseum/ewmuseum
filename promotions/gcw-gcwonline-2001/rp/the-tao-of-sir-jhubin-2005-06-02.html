The Tao of Sir Jhubin

2 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-2-05 O’Hair International Airport, the largest gateway in North America? [iPods.] [Everywhere you turn, another set of pearl-white headphones seem to stem from another set of random eardrums. Men in their thirties dressed in business suits, quietly jamming to Journey. Women in athletic pants swaying to Soundgarden. Children rocking out to Radiohead. Hell, even mothers-to-be strap a set of headphones to their bellies just so their future John or Jane can be cultured by Beethoven in the womb.] [It’s enough to make a grown man’s head ache with Apples.] [Hefting his single carry-on bag over one shoulder, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins disregards these observations as he steps through the metal detector. With his one-way ticket to San Antonio clutched securely in his other fist, he quietly makes his way through the organized mass of humanity waiting around Gate 17. Families flying to Florida for summer vacation, tourists flying in from Oklahoma to “see the big city,” oriental business types from Okinawa heading out to catch a cab? a wide spectrum of interesting characters walk through the gates at O’Hair twenty-four hours a day , and today is no different.] [Despite himself, Rich Rollins can’t help but notice that one in every four people has the damnable white wires of an iPod hanging out of their head.] [Consumer whores.] [Taking a seat in an uncomfortable shit-brown bowled plastic chair, The Renegade sighs as he sets his carry-on bag on the carpet, looking up at the blinking Arrivals/Departures board. What he sees does nothing to lift his spirits.] American Airlines 2:25 One-Stop San Antonio? Delayed 3:35 [Perfect. Just fucking perfect.] [With WorldWide going live in less than six hours, Rollins groans as he tries to figure out a post-landing schedule that offers him enough time to check in at his hotel before arriving at the SBC Center. The numbers, unfortunately, just don’t add up.] [He muses to himself that it wouldn’t be the first time he slept in his own locker room.] Voice: Rich?” Rich Rollins? [Jerking himself back to the present, The Renegade blinks in rapid succession as his head turns around to locate the origin of the voice. There, walking toward his row of shit-brown plastic chairs, is a face that Rich Rollins had not expected to see.] Renegade: Well, if it isn’t Jeremy Gonzales, best damn coffee boy a wrestling company could ever have. [His pace slowing, the subtle sarcasm in Rollins? voice isn’t lost on the young man as he sets his own carry-on duffel bag on the floor next to The Renegade’s. No older than twenty-three and sporting a brand new UWF jersey, Jeremy Gonzales takes up occupation of the seat next to Rollins, beaming with a grin so false it hurts.] Gonzales: Funny I should run into you here, Renegade. How is life? Renegade: It was better about ten seconds ago, before you started talking. [Gonzales laughs.] [Rollins does not.] [Gonzales coughs, then continues.] Gonzales: So, I hear you’re back in GCW? Renegade: Yup. Gonzales: That’s good news, Rollins. I’ll tell you, the UWF sure hasn’t been the same since you left. Did you hear about the buyout? Renegade: Buyout? Gonzales: Yeah, the entire company got bought out and nobody knows by who. Sunday Afternoon Supremacy got yanked off the air just before the main event last week, and it pissed off our network affiliates to no end. They’re trying to pin the blame on somebody, but nobody knows who to turn to. Renegade: Well, it sure sounds like you got your work cut out for you. At least you won’t have to run another cup of decaf to Richard Hayes? grubby little paws again. Gonzales: I don’t do any of that menial labor shit anymore. They promoted me. Renegade: (amused) Really? To what? Gonzales: Journalist’s assistant. I help the editor of the UWF Magazine do his thing, and in return I get to have my own little coffee bitch running errands for me. It’s a nice turnaround. [Despite himself, Rollins can’t help but choke out a laugh. The thought of Jeremy actually ordering around someone smaller and more timid than himself put a smile on The Renegade’s rocky face.] Renegade: It sounds like this buyout is working to your advantage, Gonzales. Congratulations. [Gonzales merely shrugs.] Gonzales: It’s just a job. What about you, though? How has GCW been treating you? [Trying to (unsuccessfully) lean back in his bucket chair, The Renegade looks down at his carry-on as if an answer were written on the shoulder strap. Running a hand through his thick shoulder-length mass of brown hair, he sighs.] Renegade: I’m about to ruin a kid’s career, for one. But before that, I’m heading to San Antonio to finally settle a score that’s been a long time coming. My first GCW match in almost four years, and Steven Caldera gives me The Jhub. Gonzales: Jhubin? You haven’t fought him since? Renegade: “since ACWF III, and it’s been eatin” at me for years, kid. It hit me this mornin?, somewhere between a cold cup of Starbucks coffee and cab fare, that I’ve got an opportunity tonight that I’ll never have again. Ever. (he sighs) This is my last ride, Gonzales. I’ve got so many things that I want to accomplish before I call it quits, so many loose ends to tie up, that I don’t even know where to begin. Gonzales: That’s not true, Rollins. You know exactly where to begin, and it begins tonight. [Finally turning back to look at Jeremy Gonzales, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins allows a minute hint of appreciation to permeate his sour expression.] [He nods once, leaning over his knees and locking his fingers.] [His voice drifts from his lungs like the petals of floating dandelion seeds, like a whisper hiding in shadow.] Renegade: Tonight? tonight I turn back the clock. Tonight I cut through all the bullshit surrounding GCW, burn the books on its troubled past - and mine - and somehow reconcile the two. To do that, I’m finally going to do what I should have done at ACWF III. Gonzales: What? win? [The Renegade shoots him a cold stare.] Renegade: Yes, smartass. I’m going to finish what we started all those years ago, and this time it won’t be Rich Rollins takin? the three-second nap. Gonzales: You promise me that? Airline Attendant: American Airlines flight 207 for San Antonio? now calling flight 207 for San Antonio, seating has begun. [Scooping his carry-on up his arm and standing in one smooth motion, The Renegade grins as he acknowledges Jeremy Gonzales one last time.] Renegade: I promise you that. [Turning on his heels, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins hands his ticket stub to the busty blonde attendant at the gate, finally disappearing into the uncertain maw of the connecting tunnel.] [With a smile on his face, Jeremy Gonzales reaches into his own carry-on.] [And pulls out an iPod.] ~Fin~
