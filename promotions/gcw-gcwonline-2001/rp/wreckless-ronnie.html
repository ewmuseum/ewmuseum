Wreckless Ronnie

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/13/1992 Picture this... The sun beats gently over the Autumn trees. The ground looking like a bowl fruity pebbles from all the shades of leaves covering the cracked and beaten tar. Just your typical Autumn day, nothing far from the usual. Just your average day on repeat as the world continues to spin. This day left no promise to be remembered in the years to come. That was until the sun was to go down on this particular night. Trey, now 16, stays out late with a few friends at a nearby baseball field. His curfew is slowly easing in as he and his friends lose track of time in the innocent fun they are enjoying. The crew soon realizes the hour as they call for the final batter to approach in their friendly pick up game. Trey's team is down a run and there are two kids on the bases, on first and second. A single will tie the game and send all the guys home happy. Trey steps into the batters box in hopes to end the game on a good note for everyone involved. Steve - "Hey batter batter! Swing batter batter!....." Tony - "Hey now batter, SWING now batter batter..." Ronnie - "We want a pitcher not a belly itcher..." Trey stands up at the dish sporting a replica Edgar Martinez jersey with his aluminum bat in hand. Trey, who at this time is a few inches taller than the rest of his friends, holds his brand new 29 ounce 32 inch Louisville Slugger bat in his grip. His hands are covered by his fathers hand me down Nike batting gloves as he stands as solid as a statue in the batters box awaiting on the cheese. First pitch comes hurling down the inside of the plate where Trey watches it take the inside corner. David - "Strike One!" Trey nods his head in approval as he takes the first strike. Johnathan, who is on the mound tossing the ball, knows Trey can not hit the inside fastball. John grins and spits on the rubber as he grinds his foot in his own spit and stand back in the pitching pose. Kris, playing catcher, drops two fingers as he calls for the inside curveball. John rears back and slides out a dirty curve. It heads a little off target as it leads itself over the plate. Trey rears back and unleashes a power house hack and sends the ball teasing the left field foul pole. Trey's team stands in silence as they watch the ball sail through the air. Trey slowly pacing up the first base line, throws his arms in Carlton Fisk fashion and calls for the ball to stay fair. The ball looks to not have enough power to leave the yard but the placement could send two men home rounding the bases for the win. The ball comes crashing down as it hits the line and coughs up a cloud of white chalk indicating a fair ball. Trey begins to hussel to first as suddenly he is stopped by a game altering sound... David - "Foul Ball!!" Treys bench blows up in a frenzy as Trey slows down from running into a light jog as he turns his body back towards the plate to regain his weapon and resume his stance in the box. He smiles in his silent disagreement with the call, but he keeps his composure about it. Trey now trailing 0-2 in the count lifts his club back up and awaits the upcoming pitch. John, has a look of fear in this eyes as he shakes off a few calls by Kris. Finally John nods in approval as he takes a deep gulp, grips the ball and looks over his back shoulder at second to check the runner. John rears back his arm and hurls the pitch. Trey has the pitch dead red in his sight as he cocks back and unloads a mighty swing of the bat. A loud smack is heard as Treys bench stares on in disbelief. Treys face turns slowly from a look of confidence to a slow and blank expression. David - "STRIKE THREE!!! BATTER'S OUT!" Kris removes the ball from his mit and charges the mound to embrace John on his incredible strikeout. Trey who all year long has yet to strikeout, watches on and tosses a friendly smile at john who winks back as if to say, "just once, I finally gotcha". Treys bench lets out a few losing sighs as they begin to clean out the dugout of all the seeds, cups and equipment. Ronnie - "Nice try Trey. We will get them next time." Fast forward to the walk home. Trey, Ronnie, Steve and Tony all live on the same street, so they always walk home together jokingly and relive the game and the events of the day. They are enjoying hazing Trey for his giant strikeout as suddenly they are interupted by the voice of a person behind them. "Hey you. I like your jersey." Trey turns around to see a random group of about 9 or 10 kids who stand in a crowd as he responds. Trey - "Thanks man, Edgar Martinez is my idol." Random Kid - "Mine too. Pretty good hitter I must say." Trey - "The best." Kid - "He sure is. That's why you should give me that shirt." Trey is uneasy about the kids demeanor, but he tries to respond respectfully. Trey - "I'd rather not. It was a birthday gift from my Grandmother. It's very meaningful to me." The group of kids stand chuckling at the sentimental humor in Treys heartfelt remark. The kid who stands unamused at Treys attempt of a passive remark, reaches for his waist and removes a .9mm pistol from his belt line. Kid - "That wasn't a request." The kid stands aiming the gun directly at Treys chest as Treys group of friends back up in sheer fright of the potential outcome of this mugging. Trey - "As much as I do not want to be shot over a shirt, I would rather just give you the money I have so you can buy your own." As kind as the remark was meant to be taken for, the group of thugs took this as an advantage to both rob Trey of his jersey, and now his money. Kid - "That's touching. :::Fake chuckle::: Now empty your fucking wallet and remove the shirt....I will not ask this of you again!" Ronnie attempts an outrageous act of courage and bravery, or complete stupidity as he charges towards the kid with the pistol and receives a slug to the torso. Ronnie stood directly in front of Trey as his once prized jersey now loses all sentimental value and worth as it becomes drenched in the cold blood from the sternum of Ronnie. The group of thugs jolt off into the darkness as Tony and Steven drop to the ground in surprise and disbelief of the events that just unfolded in front of them. The kid stands nervously as he heartlessly stares at the damage he has just dealt out by his own hands. He looks to the ground and picks up the scolding hot shell casing of the .9mm slug he just fired. He runs off into the early night as Trey charges to the aid of his fallen friend. Trey removes his Martinez jersey and stuffs it into the wound in Ronnies chest. Trey is crying as police sirens are heard faintly in the deep darkness that surrounds them. Fast forward to the hospital as Ronnies parents and Treys mother stand anxiously in the waiting room on current status of Treys best friend. Trey, who demanded to be at the aid of Ronnie throughout the entirety of this tragedy, stands next to the bed of his best friend as he remains in a medically induced coma. Surgeons come hastly charging in and out of the room with an abundance of gauze and tools that are crucial to the urgent surgery to save Ronnies life. Trey continues to cry as his eyes fall heavy and his head slowly drops... Trey opens his eyes to see a clock on a nightstand that reads 2:42 AM. He stares into a faintly lit room in complete confusion as he is unsure of his where abouts. He looks to the opposite side of the room to see a tv holding a steady source of light inside this room. Trey finally comes to and removes himself from the bed. He is inside his bedroom and he begins to become enraged by the fact he is not bedside with his best friend in the hospital. Whether Ronnie was standing up for Treys safety or he had just had enough of the charades, Trey feels completely to blame for the situation. Trey charges at his bedroom door and hurls through into the hallway. He steamrolls his way towards the front door in hopes to run by foot to the hospital to be reunited with his brave friend. His mother Kendra sits on the couch in the living room as she sees Trey charge for the front door. She calls for Trey who breaks from his trance. Kendra - "Trey please come in here. I would like to talk to you." Trey releases his hand off of the deadbolt and gingerly drags himself to the couch. His heart is in his chest as he prepares himself for the worst...
