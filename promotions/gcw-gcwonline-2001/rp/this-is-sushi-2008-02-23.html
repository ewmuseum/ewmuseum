This Is Sushi!

23 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/23/08 START 5:20 pm The sun sets as a beautiful purple shade overcomes the city of Osaka. For most, another hard day of work has come to end. But for one other person, or rather, one other Fox, the adventure is just about to start. It’s nearing the dinner time rush at the lovely “Sushi It” restaurant in downtown Osaka. The restaurant is surprisingly slow for a change. However, that’s about to change as Clyde Fox approaches with his appetite intact. The wooden door flexes as Clyde pushes with all his might. The crackling of the floorboards hinder as Clyde waltzes up to the hostess station. The Matride walks up to his post while finishing a conversation with a coworker. Matride Zao: And then I said; that’s what you deserve American! The waiter and Matride laugh emphatically as Clyde caught the tail end of the joke. Stepping up closer to the soft burgundy podium, the Matride finally notices Clyde. Matride Zao: Hi. May I seat you? Fox: No that’s okay; I am able to sit down by myself. Clyde crosses his to maintain a barrier between himself and the Matride. The tense moment tightens as the floorboards are nearly the only thing that can be heard. In sense they scream for someone to break the silence. Matride Zao: Oh? Fox: Yeah you see? I’m the only one who makes jokes during my adventures? comprende? Laying it out there, Clyde resumes a relaxed posture and readies himself for the response. Ever so slightly the Matride shifts backwards as if declaring defeat. He throws his hands up and smirks like a bully stealing lunch money. Matride Zao: Apologies. May I seat you now? Fox: One for now? Squirt Squirt and Pikachu are busy stitching my shorts back together. Good thing I brought this extra pair I’m wearing. Clyde twirls around to display his awesome girlish figure. He’s wearing tight black pleather pants. The best kind. They crackle as he turns with the utmost grace. The lighting really accentuates his groin area. Fox: Wanna feel em? It’s pleather? environmentally friendly. Matride Zao: Yes I would. The Matride’s smirk grows bigger (along with something else) as he stands there raising one eyebrow. Clyde stops twirling and looks at the Matride with an innocent open mouth look. Fox: You wish. Ell ohh ell. The Matride slumps down and drops his smile on the floor, crushing his hopes and dreams and whatever hardness he had acquired. Matride Zao: I do. Fox: I’ll take that table now. Matride Zao: The booth in the corner. The Matride slowly turns and points at a tiny two person booth over in the corner of the restaurant. The gallant Fox nods in appreciation and heads off towards his table. Clyde slinks himself into the booth as his buttocks thanks him by releasing some premature gas. Fox: I hope no one heard that. Satisfied, Clyde looks around and notices the sharp red tablecloth which compliments the blood red wallpaper above the wall’s wooden paneling. Just then, a waiter latches onto a side of the booth and flips open his notepad. Eager to serve, the waiter looks intensely at Clyde as if he was a Samurai warrior preparing for the bloodiest, most brutal fight ever! Even more brutal than Tom Cruise’s battles in The Last Samurai movie? IN STORES NOW! Waiter Ping: I serve you! The intensity radiates off Ping like a hot knife through butter. Getting Clyde all hot, the waiter does what his title says he does? waits. But being the scared bear Clyde is, all that comes out is a little meep. Waiter Ping: Are you table ready? He meant to ask if the table is ready to eat, but you know, things get lost in translation. Fox: Heck yes I’m table ready, I’m quite spruced up thank you very much! Waiter Ping: I serve you! Fox: I eat you! Clyde shouts back as the conversation turned yelling contest continues. Waiter Ping: YOU GET BEST FOOD EVER! Fox: FRAG YEAH! Almost budding heads, the two start yelling and swearing pointlessly in each others faces. They get so close that if you were at the other end of the restaurant, you couldn’t tell the difference between violently making out, or if they were trying to wrap each others tongues around their necks. Eventually they calm down and Clyde resides on his behind again. Fox: GOSH DARN IT YES! I love you people! You’re all so simple with your sentences. I finally feel that I connect with you guys! Waiter Ping: Your IQ 999? Fox scratches his head as if there were lice the size of mice running around on his scalp. Fox: Nope! I think I owe it points. Waiter Ping: Oh okay. Fox: But enough about that, I’ll have the special. Tee why. That’s short for thank you. Ping honors thy Fox’s command with a dignified bow and departure. Clyde isolates himself from his body as he turns his attention to daydreaming. Fox: Dr. Z Z Z Z Z Z Z Z Z Z Z Z Z Z Z. I feel sleepy. He didn’t put a hex on me did he? Trying to avoid shutting down, Clyde looks around and notices some fairly decadent people enjoying their meals. One lady sips liquid for a cup with her pinky finger extended. Fox: I should definitely do that. Fox grabs the empty cup in front of him and practices sipping from it with his pinky extended. Fox: Ewwww. Now I know how Lance Bass feels. So delicate and intricate; I just want a big burly man to come take me home! With food in hand, Ping rushes to the table like a chicken chased by a hillbilly. Waiter Ping: Here. SPECIAL! You eat, you eat! The steaming plate of what appears to be fish guts simmers down. Clyde inches closely towards the steaming pile of something to get a better look. Fox: This looks poisonous. What is this? Waiter Ping: Hamo. IT’S SPECIAL! Only 500,000 yen. Clyde rocks back as if he was just in a car crash! 500,000 yen? You gotta be kidding him! Fox: 500,000 yen? You gotta be kidding me? I just said that Clyde? anyways, back to their conversation. Waiter Ping: You? American? Almost hyperventilating, Clyde replies. Fox: Well I work there, yes. Waiter Ping: No problem sir. American, is two dollars. Fox: Ohhhhh! Icy, icy. Very good then, you are dismissed. Fox bows his head and shoos Ping away with a hand fluttering as the weird looking fish pulsates like a brick of cheese melting in a microwave. Fox: Time to munch down? BIG TIME! Clyde goes to grab his fork? his fork? his fork? There’s no fork! Fox: Ummm, where’s the fork? Wait a minute! This is Dr. Z’s doing! He’s in my head! So much for being my family doctor slash insomniac doctor slash my anesthesiologist, he’s making psychotic! Fox stands up like a rocket being shot into outer space. Unluckily for him, a few ticked waiters approach the volatile volcano known as Clyde Fox. The gang of henchmen? I mean waiters, storm up to Clyde for a good old fashioned confrontation straight out of any Japanese anime. Waiter Vi: You refuse the special? SPECIAL?! Waiter Vi takes an aggressive ninja stance. You can tell that he’s pretty kickass. Waiter Shamou covers his blindside as he informs Clyde. Waiter Shamou: We have come from far away land to fight. The last of the sacred uber awesome ninjas joins in the pose as Waiter Furi steps in. Waiter Furi: Waaaaaaa-sssssooooo, waaaaaaaa-ziki sooooooo! Waiter: Vi: Waiter Furi says he will kill you without even moving. Waiter Furi cracks his knuckles, his head, then his knuckles again, one more time with the knuckles, his feet, his head again, and lastly his knuckles as he prepares to fight. Fox: I see what kind of operation this is! Dr. Z Z Z wants me to be low on energy so he bribed you guys to feed me some gross fish guts! And just to cover his buttocks, he made sure that if I wanted to eat it, I wouldn’t have a fork to eat it with so I’d starve and he’d beat me at WorldWide! Like, he totally cured my insomnia when he knocked me out cold the last time I fought him, but come on! He doesn’t need to resort to these tactics just so I stay a paying customer! The waiter warriors dismiss their awesome ninja stances in disbelief. Waiter Vi: So you talk for a whole paragraph and you don’t even mention how he sent ninjas after you to make sure you eat the poisonous fish? Glaring back at the ninjas like Tony Hawk nailing a sick ollie sent into a kickflip turned 50-50 grind and capping it off with an irreversible lung twist special SK, Clyde retaliates. Fox: That’s because you guys are chumps. I’m a champ. Chumps lose to champs almost like thirty-five percent of the time! Like this? The widescreen view initiates as Clyde pulls some Muay Thai shit and double ax handles Waiter Vi in the side of the head. Vi crashes to the ground faster than Pamela Anderson on Tommy Lee, but Clyde wasn’t done there. Waiter Shamou runs up to Clyde and begins his special attack. He pulls his arms back and collects enough to form an awesome energy ball! Waiter Shamou: KAMEHAMEHA!!!!!! Of course there was no energy to shoot in the first place. I know Clyde’s adventures are unrealistic to begin with, but did you think I would actually write in someone blasting Clyde with a beam of energy? Come on people; put a little more trust in me than that! Standing there with his arms extended, Waiter Shamou gets taken down with a pinky finger push to the head! All that remained was the unstoppable Waiter Furi? who really did fit his name. Body hair protrudes from almost every opening of his waiter attire. Waiter Furi: WAAAAAAAZZZZZZZZZ OOOOOOKKKKKKKIIIIIIII HA! Furi lunges at Clyde only to dive right in between Clyde’s chicken legs. A swift stomp on Furi’s back and Clyde has defeated the most lethal trio of waiter ninjas a 21 year old creative writer can think of! Waiter Ping runs up to Clyde? Waiter Ping: WE ARE FREE! Those ninjas have been hustling us for yen ever since Shonen Jump became huge in America! Just the sound of the name makes Clyde shutter like Jell-o. Fox: I HATE SHONEN JUMP! Friggin GCW kids always try and get me to sign Yu-Gi-Oh cards! How many times do I have to tell them; I’M ALL ABOUT POKEMON, not Yu-Gi-Oh! Trust me, there’s a huge difference! Waiter Ping: GCW? Do you know Docta Z? Toning it down Clyde wipes his forehead as he catches his breath. Fox: Yeah? I kinda work with him. Waiter Ping: Holy crap! He is awesome! More confused than Jessica Simpson about to get into bed with Nick Lachey on heir first night of marriage, Clyde responds. Fox: Do you know who I am? Waiter Ping: Name? Fox: Clyde Evan Fox, the current, and also bestest TV Champion ever! Ping steps over the bodies of the broken ninjas as he mentally checks his database. The ninjas still remain out cold from that brutal display of judo goodness. Waiter Ping: Doesn’t ring a bell. A perky waitress inserts herself into the conversation. The young Japanese woman bends over and begins to drag the battered bodies from below Ping and Fox. Waitress Yimmy: Clyde Fox? Fox: Yeah, that’s me! Yimmy darts back and forth dragging the bodies away quicker than a medic on a battlefield. Waitress Yimmy: HE A LOSER! Taking an offensive position, Clyde readies himself for another taekwondo scrap. Fox: Oh no you did-ant! Clyde snaps his fingers in a girly way as he rolls his body like he was in 1998. Yimmy ends up scurrying to the kitchen area screaming for her life. Waitress Yimmy: MY EYES ARE BURNING FROM AMERICAN DANCE! Clyde ceases his hypnotic moves, especially because Matride Zao was plugging something to himself at the sight of those swinging pleather pants. Fox: Fine. I don’t need your fancy food! As long as I have my pleather pants, all is good in the Fox hood. Clyde busts a pose but unfortunately that isn’t all that busts. The butt seam from his pleather pants rip. Waiter Chinzo: You are fat ass American! Ha ha ha. Fox: EAT THIS CHINA MAN! Clyde grabs the plate of Hamo and tosses it at the waiter. The fish flies across the restaurant, finally slapping Chinzo in the face. Fox: Looks like your plan backfired Mr. Dr. Z Z Z? BIG TIME! Waiter Chinzo: GET HIM! An array of ninja waiters barrel through the kitchen doors and into the main eating area surrounding the Fox prey. Waiter Chinzo tosses the slimy Hamo to the ground and pulls out a Tommy gun from his back. Chinzo yells a very classic 300 line? Waiter Chinzo: THIS? IS? SUSHI!!!!!!!! The battle of all battles raged on when 300 ninja waiters came through canal of the kitchen, where numbers counted for nothing. They met the wily Fox, a foe they wish they never had encountered for they were toast. Even with his pleather pants falling, the Fox stood tall and put up a fight so epic, that it can’t even be written! Or it could be, but I just don’t want to write it! MUHAHAHAHA! 300. END
