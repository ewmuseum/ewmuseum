Twisted Dreams of Reality...

26 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/26/10 January 25, 2010 Miami Florida Twisted dreams of the unsettling heart awaken like darkness on a midnight’s eve. The scene is set outside the lavish home of GCW superstar Bishop Steele. Clouds cover the moon as if a werewolf was about to awaken and ravage the city of Miami. Alexis is in the kitchen making a cake as Bishop comes walking down from upstairs drying his hands as if he has just walked out of the bathroom from washing his hands. He walks into the kitchen and immediately makes eye contact with Alexis. The overpowering sexual attractiveness draws the two together for a warm embrace of the two souls. Squeezing her tightly as if she was leaving for a long time, she lets herself go and enjoys the warmness of Bishop’s touch. Eventually letting go she continues to make her cake, as Bishop walks over to the bar stool and sits down to read the paper. “Baby I’m worried about you wrestling this week?” “What are you talking about Alexis?” “I mean it has been quite some time since you have stepped into that ring and I’m afraid you are still not back to your rare form like before.” Bishop smirking as he contemplates Alexis’s comment, takes a deep breath and continues with reading the paper. “So you just are going to ignore me?” “Look Alexis I’m just as worried about this match as you are, but the only way I’m going to know if I’m ready is if I wrestle.” “I’m sorry sweetie I just enjoy having you around and I know that eventually you would have to go back to your career, I’m just not ready yet.” Standing up from the stool and seeing that Alexis is really bothered by Bishop leaving, he walks over and hugs her. “Everything will be okay. When everything gets back to normal in my career, you will be traveling on the road with me again like always. I promise.” Look eyes with each other, Bishop lets her go and heads towards the front door, grabbing his keys from the in table as he walks out the door. He leaves Alexis with an uneasy feeling that words can describe, Almost as if a dream that she had from her past has come back to haunt her on this creepy night. Getting into his 760LI BMW, Bishop drives off down the long winding driveway. Looking out the window as he drives off, Alexis looks down at her stomach and starts caressing it. The camera flickers and fades to black…….. fear of confusion fear of killers fear of panicking fear of death fear of clowns fear of needles fear of stalkers fear of love fear of getting hurt fear of the future fear of car accidents fear of not being happy fear of being buried alive fear of losing someone you love fear of the past coming back fear of someone not loving you back fear of disappointing the people you love fear of hurting the people you love fear of not knowing what’s going to happen fear of me................. Journal Entry #76 “The fear of not knowing what is on the other side of the wall intimidates me, but the fear of knowing scares me. This match will dictate whether I continue down this road to become the best in this industry. I t has been a few months and during that time I have felt emptiness and a void. A void that isn’t easily described in words, but in actions. These hands have bled, burned, and scared all for the chance to drive a man or woman’s face into the earth in which many of us call home. Keeping these weapons of pain locked up is a challenge and a fight and now the time has come to unleash them once again. Pazu or whatever your name is, I wish you the best of luck my friend in our match. I have no grudge against you, nor do I hate you. You are just merely a road block that is keeping me from pushing on down this journey of being the greatest. In time True Perfection will be back to rare form and that is a promise, but for now this twisted dream of darkness that these few months have brought me will bring all hell, fire and destruction unto the lost souls of the GCW. All I can say now is Fear Me!!!” To Be Continued………..
