Pain and glory..

8 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/8/2005 Two days he had been here and they seemed like years. Erik VanLorne was bone weary and physically exhausted. The bartering for the diamonds had taken countless hours and finally an agreement had been settled upon. He had to fight Hirozuko's five best men....then Hirozuko himself. If he beat all of them the diamonds were his without payment. If he lost even one, he would have to pay three times what they were worth....and lose part of his index finger as punishment for his dishonroable intent. The first five fights had been grueling to say the least. Ribs were bruised, right eye was swollen shut, and he had a nasty gash over his left eye. Lying on his bed mat, the German professional thief turned wrestler stared at the ceiling. Rest was sparce at best. His mind was railroaded with thoughts of the fight to come. He had been one of his former sensei's best students, and yet, like all the others he had never bested the man. Perhaps that was why they called him the 'Man of Steel.' This little man was well known for his ability to snap a young rubbery palm tree with his powerful sidekick. It was an endeavor near impossible to succeed at. The sound of his door sliding open, followed by a stream of light obscured by a shadowy figure of man cause those eyes to cant sideways and stared at the mangled face of his first victim. Erik merely nodded as he rolled to his feet slowly, masking the pain felt in those tenderized ribs. He should have allowed them to heal more before jumping back into the ring so soon after his 'meeting' with Petre. A slow steady pace carried him to stand before the shorter Oriental. The right hand of Hirozuko merely stared at the blonde giant with a stony gaze before, finally, he spoke. ' It was a good fight Erik-san. I am honored to have lost to such a well trained martial artist.' Erik would have smiled if not for the split in his lip and the knowledge that to do so would have been considered rude. Instead he merely nodded and whispered in kind. ' Hai. It was a good fight indeed. I was honored to have faced a man of your prowess.' Silence ensued as the two walked through the halls of the complex. The travel of the pair halted at the double doors of Hiro's private dojo. With great flourish his escort grabbed the door and slide them open wide to reveal a room filled with ten people. Nine of the Yakuza men surrounded the fight mat in the center of the room and there in the center stood his only obstacle left. The one thing that stood between him and the safety of his twin. As the Yakuza right hand stood to the side Erik made way to the center of the room. Coming to stand before Hiro he bowed deeply hand surrounding fist in honor of his sensei of the past. Hiro returned the gesture and spoke in quiet tones. ' Hold nothing back Erik-san. I assure you I won't.' Erik nodded looking up at his teacher just in time to see a powerful roundhouse coming for his head. Instinctively he threw up his arm bent at the elbow and allowed it to absorb the blow. Upon impact he lost feeling in that limb. A testament to the strength this smaller and percievably faster man held tightly reined within that form. The fight was on. The two clash like titans. Perfectly executed fists aiming for critical parts of their opponents bodies as they danced the dance of defeat. Erik's face snapped to the side hard enough that if his head had not been attached it surely would have flown across the room. The larger man staggered and fell to his side. Growling he shook of the stars in his eyes and caught Hiro's movement from the corner of his periphial vision. A foot was hurling for the back of his head. Instantly Erik fell to his back and sent his own foot for the gut of his teacher as the other swept at the ankle of his foundation leg. Scissors sweep, a move taught to him by this very man. Amazingly it worked. He connected on both accounts knocking the man off balance as his high foot sent him sprawling through the air to slide across the mat on his back. Flipping to his feet, Erik went on the attack. Kick, block. Punch, block. Chop, dodge. Sweep, jump. It was like watching a perfectly coregraphed dance...but Hiro had the advantage. Erik had already fought five others. He was tired, slow from the pain in his body. Yet, the German was determined to win this..determined to save Eva...she was all that mattered now. It was the very thought of his sister etching into the fore scope of his mind that redoubled his efforts. Everything Hiro threw he managed to counter. Praying silently for that perfect opening to present itself. He was tiring fast. The smaller man was working him on purpose, sapping his energy in such a way as to finish him off with one final blow. Hiro nailed his knee with a downswpet side kick, the sound of that knee going out of joint heard by all in the room. Erik roared in pain as his fist suddenly tightened and swung upward in an uppercut so powerful and so fast Hiro did not have the chance to dodge as he had hoped. ~CRACK~ His meaty fist clashed with the chin bone of Hiro's face sending his former instructor flying backwards once again...outcold. Erik collapsed grasping at his knee in writhing pain as the other surrounded each of them checking for damage. Erik's knee was ruined, but he had done what he had set out to do. He had beaten them all and in turn he had won the diamonds and Eva's freedom. As last things seemed to be looking up....
