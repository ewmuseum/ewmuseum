When A World Is Turned Upside Down

1 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/01/11 NC-17 didn’t exactly go to plan for Jimmy Thunder. After Red and Ted took out his partner Colin O’Ryan, the night was effectively over. A cheap shot from Red and Ted ended Jimmy’s dream to shine on the most prestigious stage on the GCW calendar. Instead of walking away from the extravaganza with bumps and bruises from a hard night in the ring, which hopefully would have brought himself enough hype for a push to the next level, instead, he left the arena without even a drop of sweat falling from his brow and the weight of disappointment bearing down on his muscular shoulders. Standing outside at his horse corral, watching his horses running around and playing with each other, Jimmy starts thinking about where his career is heading. He’s been at cross roads in his career many times before and this is no different. With Rampage starting this week and Jimmy tossing his name in the hat to compete, this could be a tournament where anything and everything could happen. Even though NC-17 didn’t go as Jimmy had planned, he still feels optimistic about the future and he also feels great about himself. While enjoying watching his horses playing around like school children, Jimmy is distracted by the sound of a car driving up his dusty driveway. He turns and notices that it’s the Owyhee County Sheriff Daryl Crandall. Jimmy takes his leg off the steel fence that circles the corral and walks slowly over to where Sheriff Crandall has parked. “I didn’t do it, no-one seen me do it, you cant prove a thing Sheriff!” Jimmy says with a smile on his face. “Jimmy, if I was coming to arrest you I’d bring all my deputies with me, you’re way too big for me to handle on my own.” Replied Sheriff Crandall as he returns the smile and shakes Jimmy’s hand at the same time. “What gets you out of your office Sheriff?” Jimmy asked. “Well, does the name Bryan Deas ring a bell to you?” Asked Sheriff Crandall whose demeanor suddenly changes from jovial to official. “Yeah, he’s a guy I use to work with at various promotions before I went to GCW. Probably one of the dumbest guys I‘ve ever come across.” Replied Jimmy. “ One of my deputies picked him up not so long ago pretty close to here.” Sheriff Crandall explained. “What in the hell is he doing around here?” Jimmy asked with a concerned tone in his voice now. “The deputy found some guns in his car too, Jimmy” Said Sheriff Crandall. Stunned by what he just heard, he pauses before asking “What the hell is that idiot doing anywhere near here with a gun?” “From what we can gather from him, he was in cahoots with some guys called Christopher Stryfe, Anthony Taylor and Ian Cross.” Said Sheriff Crandall. “They were all part of a company I use to work for, Bloodbox.” Jimmy replied. “From what we could get out of Deas, they’re out to get you.” Sheriff Crandall explained. “Can you think of why they’d want to get you?” He went on to ask. “Apart from dominating them when I was at Bloodbox, I cant think of any other reason.” Explained Jimmy. “The other three are out there somewhere and we’re trying to apprehend them as quickly as possible but if I were I would take every precaution to protect yourself and your family.” Warned Sheriff Crandall. “On the record all I can say is that we’ll make extra patrols around here and try and keep a close eye on your property. Off the record, if they step foot on your property and you feel threatened, they’re open game.” The Sheriff informed Jimmy. Jimmy just nods before shaking Sheriff Crandall’s hand and says, “I better go in and tell Emma all about this.” “Good idea, send her my best will you?” Said Sheriff Crandall. “You bet.” Replied Jimmy. “Can you do me a favor, Sheriff?” Jimmy asked “Sure, what is it?” The Sheriff asked. “Can we keep this out of the media? I don’t want this to turn into a circus.” Explained Jimmy. “Of course, that goes without saying.” Sheriff Crandall responded. Jimmy nods as the two headed off in their own direction. Instead of heading into the house to explain the situation to Emma, Jimmy headed into his sanctuary where he plotted some of his most famous victories. A place where he has shed blood, sweat and tears for his family. The only place he feels entirely at ease, his gym. The thought of someone actually wanting to inflict any sort of harm to himself or his family members has shaken Jimmy to his core. When he actually thinks about anything happening to his children or his wife Emma almost makes him physically ill. Suddenly everything that he loves and lives for is under threat and he doesn’t have to second guess himself when he realizes that he has to pull out all stops to make sure that he can protect his family at all costs. After spending some time by himself in the gym, reflecting on the news that was handed down to him, Jimmy decides to go and fill Emma in on what he had just been told. He walks into his living room and sees Emma sitting down watching an ESPN report on NC-17 and getting upset about the bad review Jimmy has got about his latest appearances at GCW. “Where do they get all their crap from?” She vehemently said. “I wouldn’t worry too much about what they’re saying.” Replied Jimmy in a soft voice. “Doesn’t it bother you that they’re saying you’re a shadow of your former self?” Asked Emma. Jimmy shakes his head before saying, “Why should I care what’s being said by people who hide behind a microphone and have really done nothing in their lives but criticize hard working athletes?” Emma sits quietly for a few moments as she continues to watch the television. “What did the Sheriff want?” She asked while flicking through the channels. “They caught a guy not too far from with guns in his car.” Replied Jimmy. Not being perturbed by what Jimmy just said, Emma keeps her attention focused on the television. “Who was it?” She asked. “A guy I use to work with at Bloodbox, Bryan Deas.” Said Jimmy. Suddenly Emma loses her interest in the television and turns her attention to Jimmy. “Are you serious?” She asks as the shock of the news runs through her. “What was he doing near here with a gun?” She asked. Jimmy shrugs his shoulders as he says, “Sheriff Crandall seems think that he’s plotting with three other guys to do something to me.” “Why would they want anything to do with you?” She asked with a confused tone in her voice. “Who knows why, Deas is brain dead at the best of times. Only God knows what’s going through their mind.” Responded Jimmy. “All I know is while the other three are out there I’m going to pull out all stops to protect myself and my family.” He said. “Who are the other three?” Emma asked. “Ian Cross, Anthony Taylor and Christopher Stryfe. All four of them were in Bloodbox with me.” Explained Jimmy. “You better show me a picture or something of these guys just in case I come across them.” Said Emma. “I’m thinking I might hire a couple of body guards to keep you guys as safe as possible. What do you think?” Jimmy suggested. A little bit hesitant, Emma nods her head and says, “If you think it’s for the best but I really don’t like the idea of a stranger following me or the kids around.” “It’ll be ok, I’d rather have that happen then the worst.” Jimmy said as Emma nods again, agreeing with her husband. The next morning Emma wakes up and immediately notices Jimmy is not in bed. She climbs out and starts walking through house looking for him and he’s nowhere to be found. She then heads over to Jimmy’s gym and yet again, nowhere to be found. Thinking that he may have driven up to the store for his morning paper, Emma walks back into the house and starts making breakfast. Not more than twenty minutes had passed when Emma hears Jimmy walking through the front door. “You awake Em?” Yelled out Jimmy. “I’m in the kitchen baby.” She answered. Jimmy walks into the kitchen and surprises Emma with two strange guys following. “Em, this is Red and Sonny West. They’re Sheriff Crandall’s brothers-in-law and I’ve hired them to be in charge of security for us.” An excited Jimmy announced to Emma. “Hi guys, thank you for taking the job, I think.” A nervous Emma said while politely smiling at them as their appearance catches her by surprise. Both men stood well over six feet, nearly as tall as Jimmy and were just as muscular. Sonny, with long black wavy hair that went down to the middle of his back and Red, sporting a red goatee and short red spiky hair. “These guys will be keeping watch on the house at various stages of the day and if you and the kids leave this place, I want you to take one of them with you.” Jimmy said while putting each hand on one of their shoulders. “This may seem a little extreme but while those three idiots are roaming around here with an agenda against me, for the sake of our safety, I have to go extreme.” He explained. “Yes, it does seem extreme but I understand why.” Replied Emma. “I’m glad you understand.” Replied Jimmy while he hugged Emma. “Come on guys, let me show you around the property.” He said but before he could leave the kitchen, Emma quickly grabs at Jimmy’s wrist to stop him. “I’ll meet you guys outside.” Jimmy informed them as they walked through the living room on their way out the front door. “I don’t know about these guys baby.” Emma whispered so that Sonny and Red couldn’t over hear her. “Emma, these guys are ex-Green Beret’s! They’re trained in all aspects of protection.” Explained Jimmy. “Sheriff Crandall said they’d be perfect for what we need.” He added. “They look like fucking psychos.” Emma continued to whisper but with some conviction in her tone. “Will you just relax.” Jimmy said. “These guys will be the difference between keep us from any harm that those idiots are trying to plot. I make no apologies for doing what it takes to keep myself and my family safe.” He ended with before walking out and leaving Emma in the kitchen to ponder on what had just happened. Later that night, Emma laid in her bed while Jimmy was fast asleep next to her. It was then that the full severity of the situation started to dawn on her. She started to realize that there were men out there who wanted to inflict harm on either her husband, her children or herself. She suddenly felt sick to her stomach when thinking about any harm coming to herself or her family. Her stress levels begins to rise as she could feel her tranquil and peaceful life being turned upside down by a group of guys she doesn’t even know. It just felt like she had momentarily closed her eyes to doze off to sleep when she is suddenly woken up rapid gun fire that sounded like a machine gun. Her eyes nearly popped out of her head as she jumped up and noticed it was daylight. With her heart racing she quickly climbs out of bed and runs down the hall way, through her kitchen then to the front door to see Jimmy, firing of some rounds of a machine gun with Sonny and Red standing next to him laughing while he tries to shoot at a target. “What the hell is going on here?” She shouted. “I’m sorry honey, I didn’t mean to wake you, I was just testing this baby out.” Jimmy replied with enthusiasm. Disgusted with what she had just seen and heard, she walks back in the house to escape the madness that is just outside her front door step. Noticing that Emma seemed not too happy with what she saw, Jimmy followed her inside the house to find her in their bedroom looking out the window and trying to contain her anger. “Babe, I was just firing off some rounds, I don’t understand what the problem is?” Said a confused Jimmy. “You don’t understand my problem? Three days ago our lives were as normal as we wanted it and now there’s guys who want to kill you or me or the kids, we’ve got ex-Green Beret’s hanging around and now you’re shooting machine guns on our property where our children play and sleep Jimmy!” Yelled Emma. “Find me another fucking gun in this house Jimmy! I hate guns, I hate this whole fucking situation and I don’t want to be a part of it!” She continued yelling. “Our lives are possibly under threat Emma, what the hell do you want me to do? Just sit back and wait for one of us to get killed? Sorry no, I wont do that!” Jimmy retorted with his voice raised as well now. “Why do we have to turn our lives upside down for these animals?” Emma asked “Unfortunately baby this is the way it has to be until I know for certain we’re going to be safe.” Replied Jimmy. “Ok, Red and Sonny I can handle but those guns have to go Jimmy. Either you get rid of those guns or I’m taking Chloe and Junior with me back to my parents in Florida.” She declared. “I’m being serious too.” She ended with as she stared into Jimmy’s eyes with anger. Jimmy looks down at the floor before nodding then says, “I’ll get the guys to get rid of them then.” He walks up to Emma and gives her a hug. “I’m sorry.” He said With a tear falling down her cheek she says, “I’m sorry too baby, I’m just scared. I’ve never been in this situation before.” “It’s ok.” Replied Jimmy. “Trust me, everything will be ok.” He said before giving Emma a kiss. Their kiss is interrupted by Jimmy’s cell ringing in the kitchen. He let’s Emma go and leaves the room to answer the phone. Meanwhile Emma heads into their ensuite to wash her face and try and gather her thoughts again. One minute she’s sound asleep, the next she’s woken up by machine gun fire then finds herself in an argument with her husband. Not the start to the day she was hoping for. The feel of the cold water on her face seems to be calming her down. She could feel her heart slowing down to it’s regular pace and possibly some resemblance of normality in her life coming back. It isn’t too long before Jimmy walks back into the bedroom holding his cell phone. “I think I found something that will take our minds off all the craziness around here?” Jimmy announced. “What’s that baby?” Asked Emma with an inquisitive tone. “I just found out who my first opponent at the Rampage torunament is.” Replied Jimmy. “Well, who is it?” Emma asked with eagerness. “It’s the first round of the Rampage tournament and I’m up against Bryan Mayhem and the best part of it all is that it’s for the Hardcore title.” Jimmy said with a smile on his face. Emma’s anticipation turns into horror as she knows what a hardcore match entails. “Baby I don’t know about this.” She nervously said. “Come on Em, it’ll be a lot of fun. Remember, I grew up on the bad side of Sydney, this type of match will bring back some memories for me. This type of match suits me down to the ground.” Explained Jimmy. “How about I just wait for you in the locker room with Chloe?” Emma asked. “HA HA HA!! If you like.” He answered. Several days have passed without incident and as much as Jimmy has tried to use the excitement of Rampage and his shot at the Hardcore title, the fact that his safety and his family’s safety is still playing in the back of his mind. So much so that any suspicious happenings has Jimmy’s adrenaline rushing. On the day that he is scheduled to leave for Baltimore, Jimmy leaves Sonny to watch over the ranch while Red joins Jimmy and his family to World Wide at Baltimore. While entering Boise airport, Jimmy is confronted by Anthony Stewart from KTVB Boise News and his camera crew. “Hi Jimmy, Anthony Stewart from KTVB sports, I was wondering if I could get a few words from you for tonight’s news?” Anthony asked quickly while walking along side Jimmy. Jimmy stops and says, “Yeah, sure.” “Ok we’ll get set up here real quick.” Anthony says while his camera man gets in position. “Good evening everyone, I’m standing here at Boise Airport with Idaho’s favorite adopted son, Jimmy Thunder. Now Jimmy, we’ve just found out that your opponent for the first round of the Rampage tournament is Hardcore champion Bryan Mayhem and this match is for the Hardcore title. How do you feel about this match?” Anthony asks. “Well it’ll definitely be a brutal match, hardcore matches are. It’s just real important for me to get the win, not only for the Hardcore Championship but it’ll give me a head start in the tournament.” Replied Jimmy. “Jimmy, you haven’t been in a Hardcore match for some time, how do you think you’ll be able to keep up with someone like Mayhem who has held the title on three different occasions.? Some would go as far as to say he’s the King of Hardcore at GCW.” Asked Anthony. “Believe it or not, this kind of match is right up my alley. Not only is my wrestling style more of a brawling one than a technical style, everyone has to remember one thing, I was raised on the wrong side of Sydney. The violent streets was my home for many years. There’s nothing more hardcore than that Anthony. Brawling and using weapons is the only way I really know how to fight. People say that hardcore is Mayhem’s turf, well, I tend to disagree. When I stand in the ring to wait for Mayhem to come down the aisle, it’ll be like a bird coming down to play with a cat.” He said while staring down the barrel of the camera. “Before you go Jimmy, I know you’re rushing to leave for Baltimore, I was hoping to touch on another subject. There have been reports that there was a police operation near your house which has you connected to that operation, would you care to elaborate on that?” Anthony asked. “I got no comment, I don’t know what you’re talking about.” Jimmy said while looking to leave. “In fact, I’d appreciate it if you’d cut that from the interview.” He said before walking towards Emma. “What was all that about.” Emma asked knowing that something just happened. “That bastard Crandall blabbed to the media. This will hit the papers and the news any time now. That bastard!” Jimmy cussed with anger. “I really didn’t need any attention drawn to us or those idiots. I didn’t want to freak them out or encourage them more.” He added. “It’ll be ok.” Said Red while walking alongside Jimmy and keeping close attention to everything happening around them. Jimmy holds Emma’s hand tightly as he walks with purpose to his privately charted jet. The scene fades.
