Fences

12 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 12, 2009 The warm desert air swirled around outside the entrance to the PALMS hotel in Las Vegas, Nevada. It seemed like the entire world was a buzz. Hoards of press waited outside the hotel anxiously, mixed with a large crowd of excited wrestling fans. An environment that once seemed absurd but over the past year had become quite the norm. For one person in particular it wasn't just the wrestling world that was interested anymore. The entire world had become fascinated with the little girl from Highland Park, Illinois. Continuing even after her tragic World Heavyweight Championship loss. Finally almost as if on cue the black Chrysler Crossfire rounded the corner into the parking lot of the hotel, setting off a firestorm of flashbulbs all around. A deafening roar erupted from the people around them as the car came to a stop. Quickly two security guards ran out of the front door to her car, trying their best to block the photographers breaking the rules. The photographers who would do absolutely anything to get some sort've moneyshot. Yet like always with Vivica there was no "moneyshot" to be had. Everything always seemed so perfect with The Fearless Phenom. Almost... too perfect. Emerging from the car with a smile she waved to the crowd of wrestling fans who cheered on even louder as she glanced their direction. She'd become something much more than a champion. She'd become a hero, an icon, a celebrity. Fame shone down quite gracefully on The Fearless Phenom as the security guards grabbed her luggage from the trunk. Everywhere she went was a circus. This is the type of life that had driven many before her to tears. As security motioned towards the door and flanked her on both sides, she tossed her keys to a valet who triumphantly jumped into her car at the pick-up hub. "VIVICA! OVER HERE!" "I LOVE YOU!" "MISS VALENTINE IF I COULD HAVE A WORD FOR A SECOND!" Press members shoved out tape recorders and microphones in front of her path as she tried to make it inside the building. With a smirk from ear to ear it appeared that she enjoyed every minute of it, but even the strongest of men (and women) would know that isn't quite the truth. While she did enjoy the attention she missed being able to go out to eat, or to a movie, or the grocery store without a group of fans or media shadowing her every move. Jumping deeply and sometimes obsessively into her personal life. But it was the life of a hero. The champion of the people. The life she signed up for what seemed like a lifetime ago. Pausing for a minute at the entrance to the hotel she looked over her shoulder at the scene that was going on behind her. Camera crews shoving wrestling fans out of the way to get a peek. Fans screaming and reaching out desperately just to get a touch. Just to get a feel of what they felt was greatness. Holding up her hand she waved to everyone setting off another electric roar of cheers from her adoring fans, which quickly turned into an "Image Of The Invisible" chant as the door closed behind her and all those muffled cheers slowly went away into the sounds of the hotel lobby. For the most part the lobby had been cleared for her arrival, thankfully some peace and quiet. It didn't take too long for her to notice the hostess waiting for her, she looked back at her with a smile. "It's quite a scene out there isn't it?" The hostess said with a laugh under her breath. "Yeah..." Vivica said, "You should see what it looks like outside of the arena before shows. It's a nuthouse." "I bet." The hostess responded, her eyes slowly moving towards a television in the corner of the lobby. Her glance made The Fearless Phenom turn around and look herself as the bellhops began loading her luggage onto a carrier to take her up to her room. "In other news today, local celebrity Markus Blaire was checked into a psychiatric facility after what many people close to him are called a tough fight with depression. As many remember Markus Blaire is the now ex-boyfriend of Vivica J. Valentine who first caught national attention when it was discovered that he actually survived a blaze that LVPD claimed he died in and was found months later. A best-selling novel 'When The Levee Breaks' soon followed with a long-term deal for sequels in the works. Lately he's been seen a lot in the local VIP and social scene, showing up with a variety of friends and rumored lovers. The facility has no comment on when Markus will be released." The hostess didn't quite know what to say during this awkward moment. Vivica began shaking her head as it dropped down towards the floor a little. It'd been months since Markus answered one of her phone calls, but she still tried at least once a week. She had a feeling that something was wrong but... "Miss Valentine..." The hostess finally said, breaking the silence. "Yeah?" Vivica looked back up with a forced smile. "Is this why you're staying with us tonight? I didn't know you two still spoke." The Fearless Phenom took a deep breath, her usual perky expression was no one of distraught. She didn't have to answer the young woman for her to know the truth. It was painfully obvious from the start. "Nevermind Miss Valentine. Your room is ready. Enjoy your stay." "Thanks..." The GCW superstar said under her breath, as she looked back with a smile. ---- I'm sitting in a room Made up of only big white walls and indoor halls There's people looking through The window though they know exactly what we're here for Don't look up just let them think There's no place else you'd rather be You're always on display For everyone to watch and learn from Don't you know by now You can't turn back Because this road is all you'll ever have And it's obvious that you're dying, dying Just living proof that the camera's lying And open wide, 'cause this is your night So smile, 'cause you'll go out in style You'll go out in style If you let me I could I'd show you how to build your fences Set restrictions, separate from the world Because the ground that you hate to fight Just blame the limelight Yeah, yeah you're asking for it With every breath that you breathe in Just breathe it in Yeah, yeah well "you're just a mess" You do all this, big talk, yeah So now let's see you walk it I said let's see you walk it - "Fences" by Paramore <ht> THE FALL OF MARKUS BLAIRE ---- CHAPTER ONE: Catching Up On Lost Time ---- Tick... Tock. Tick... Tock. Tick... Tock. The rhythmic beating of the clock echoed across the silent room much like a metronome keeping pace. Never in a million year did he ever think his life would come to this. Yet there he sat in the middle of the room. Big cream colored walls, faux shrubbery, leather couches, and all. Leaning over a bit he held his head in his hands, his elbows resting upon the dark wooden table top. His long black hair flowed over his arms, almost concealing them completely while he let out an audible sigh. How could everything come to this? So many times he'd wanted his old life back. So many times he'd thought he'd had everything he wanted but with every turn of the page came an onslaught or new problems. Many times he'd sit to himself at night and wonder if maybe he just wasn't meant for this world. Maybe he wasn't strong enough. But his life was much like the room he was in, in a metaphorical sense. There was no escape. It wasn't long before silence was shattered. Penetrated like a bullet through a pane of glass as the door was shoved open. Suddenly he held up his arms, bracing himself for the barrage of screaming media and flash photography. Thankfully the hysteria was surprisingly short. The door slammed shut, once again plummeting his surroundings into silence. Awkwardly he moved his long black hair out of his face to take a look at what exactly had just happened. Surprisingly the figure of a slender female began to come into focus. So surprising he felt the need to take off his Oakley sunglasses and examine her first hand. "Don't act so surprised to see me..." The unmistakable voice of Vivica J. Valentine bounced around the acoustics of the rehabilitation visiting room. "Did you really think I'd hear about this and just blow it off? What in the world happened to you Markus?" The unaging face of Markus Blaire looked... exhausted. The quiet yet unmistakable rebellious aura of the man seemed broken and battered. His untamed spirit was now gone. It was a look familiar to a woman who lived her life on the road. "I'd rather not get a lecture from mommy dearest." He finally answered after a long pause, "You have your own life, and now I have mine. I've made it clear for quite some time that I'd rather neither of us involve each other in them." "But this isn't..." "At all." He interrupted her. "Don't you think you've done enough?" Vivica slowly walked across the room and sat down across the table. Her piercing cerulean blue eyes glaring at him from beneath red and blonde streaks. Sometimes he felt as if the woman across from him could glare directly into his soul. She had this uncanny knack to almost read his mind and pinpoint his problems. No matter how much he may try to hide his face away from her, as he was doing now, it'd never do any good. Her gaze crept through him like a virus. "Apparently, I haven't." She responded, "Or else it wouldn't end up like this." "Oh, so NOW you're going to save me." He retaliated. "For months, MONTHS, I waited on you in that God forsaken place. Prodded and tortured by that man. Now he's being celebrated on television while I sit here in rehab. Where were you to save me then? Huh? Where were you?! Lia came. Not you." "This isn't about me!" She screeched over his voice. "I've made my mistakes, just like everyone else. You knew what you signed up for back then when you told me you loved me. You knew my life and what goes along with it." "I didn't know the gratuitous sex with others came with the package." "THAT WAS A LIE MARKUS, AND YOU FUCKING KNOW IT!" She yelled back. "But now I think we both know what the media are capable of huh?" With that Vivica J. Valentine got up from her chair and marched towards a draped window. Grabbing the pull string she ripped the drapes away from the window. Revealing a circus of paparazzi outside. Flashing pictures relentlessly and screaming desperately, hoping their cries would somehow make it through the sound-proof glass. Markus once again cowered behind his long hair and arms. Trying to hide away from the attention. Watching him cower was enough to make her release the string... slamming the drapes shut and once again hiding them away from the press. "This is what they're capable of." She said standing in front of the drapes, "With the fame and fortune comes a horde of relentless scavengers. Fucking vultures Markus. Ready to pick the bones of your mistakes for the big story. Willing to sell their soul for your demise." "And you handle it so much better than I do?" Markus responded. "I've never been here." Vivica stated immediately. "Not until now anyway. But I didn't come to rehash old arguments. I came to help you." "Help me?" Markus sounded skeptical as he responded, "How can you help me?" "I can help you build fences." Vivica stated as she sat back down at the table slowly, making a rectangle in the air with her fingers. "I've been dealing with this for quite a while now. You can't live your old life anymore. You have to make changes. You have to make sacrifices." "Like what?" "Like you have to be careful of who you let in and make sure you keep the right people out. You have to be selective of who your seen with and where. Every action you take is examined under a microscope. If you don't be careful these people will tear you apart but once you build your fences..." Vivica was suddenly interrupted. "I can finally live in peace." Markus finished her sentence. "You make it sound so easy. But things don't just work like that..." "Sure they do." Vivica stated with a look of concern on her face, "Once you know the rules." "What rules?" Markus answered. "I'll tell you..." The Fearless Phenom smirked, realizing that she was breaking through his stubborn nature, "But first I need to know the whole story. Starting from the beginning." ---- CHAPTER TWO: When The Levee Breaks ---- "Well I'm sure you heard about the book. I wanted to tell the story of my forced employment with Zenith Corporation but of course no one wanted to touch it with a ten foot pole. Almost everyone thought it was a joke and a lie. Everyone else was afraid of the wrath that would come down from Z-Corp. Too much risk, not enough reward for their interests. So I decided to repackage the story as a fictional novel, replacing the real names and places. That's where 'When The Levee Breaks' came from. At first the ride was amazing. Tours, book signings at huge stores, meeting lots of cool people in amazing places. But soon enough things would change. The stops would start slowing down, and I'd began to get a lot of free time. I think it was after a book signing one night... Looking up at the half moon in the night sky, it was hard to believe that six months ago he was willing to die to have a moment like this. A chance to see the outside world. A chance to breathe in the autumn air. Something so small that billions of people take for granted until it's too late. Standing on the balcony of his hotel room the time he spent at Zenith Corporation seemed like so long ago. The months that have passed seemed like years. His life now was almost like a different reality in hindsight. The things he'd do to go back to being a normal college student at Notre Dame. Way before he felt like this freak. This thing. Months of experimentation had turned him into something he still didn't quite understand, despite the weeks of discussions with Lia Ambrosi. She'd become his surrogate mother of sorts, despite their appearance of being about the same age. He was part of her "Brood", although synthetically. He was part of her family now. Although slowly the freak like qualities he felt began to diminish, he never could quite shake the feeling of being abnormal. Making eye contact was still very difficult for him. He'd made a habit of wearing sunglasses all day because of it. Many people were drawn to this because it gave him somewhat of a "rock star" quality, despite his intentions to go as far away from the limelight as possible. It's a phenomenon that his publicist Charlotte Correra began referring to as "accidental charisma." KNOCK, KNOCK, KNOCK! A loud knock on the door inside his hotel room rattled Markus Blaire out of his deep thinking. With a couple blinks of his light emerald green eyes behind a pair of high dollar Oakley sunglasses, he found himself firmly planted back in reality... now looking over his shoulder to see if he'd heard what he thought he'd heard. Sure enough as he took a step back towards his room it happened again. KNOCK, KNOCK, KNOCK! "Who is there?" He stated loudly walking back through the sliding glass back into his hotel room. "It's Charlotte." His publicist spoke through the crack in the door, "There is something pretty cool I'd like to talk to you about." "Yeah?" He said as he turned the door handle, then pulling it open to reveal Charlotte standing with her black peacoat on. She had short red hair and a pair of 'Tina Fey' glasses. "Thanks." She said before walking in and getting straight to the conversation, "Earlier today I received a phone call from Zachary Atomic. The lead singer of the band Fallout Shelter and he was just raving about how much he enjoyed your book. So much so that he seemed extremely interested in getting to know you." "I don't think I've heard of them before..." Markus spoke from behind his shades. His long black hair up in a ponytail. "I'm a little worn out tonight and we have to leave for Tampa early in the morning." "But this is a great opportunity for you." Charlotte interrupted, "Fallout Shelter is a pretty big band these days. A lot of people enjoy their music and if people see you with Zachary, they'll be more interested in checking out your book. The more people who check out your book, the more people who'll demand a sequel. Next thing you know you'll be famous and employed for a very long time Markus. You could be the next Steven King you know. You have quite the imagination. In this economy you could use all the help you can get." Quite the imagination. Not even his own publicist believed the story about Zenith Corporation. "I don't kn..." "You know what, I'll go call Zachary back and make arrangements to do something tonight before we head out of Atlanta. You need a little fun in your life. Kick back, have some drinks you know? You've been such a hard worker, you deserve it." She interrupted. Before he could even argue she was out of the room, cell phone to her ear, making the arrangements. Markus sighed as he sat alone in his hotel room once again. Staring back at the moon once again, his mind began to drift away. Trying to find a much more tranquil place to get his mind focused before stepping back out into the public eye. ---- CHAPTER THREE: Coming Undone ---- "After I met Zacy and the rest of the band, we became really good friends. In high school and college they were labeled outcasts, and I felt like I'd met someone who knew what I was going through. Over the next few months Zacy and Fallout Shelter were off tour and my own schedule began to slow down. Every week we began to go out for drinks at upscale VIP establishments. The alcohol seemed to nullify my constant feeling of self doubt and my insecurity about who I was. When I drank I felt normal again, I felt alive, I felt like I was on top of the world. Hanging out with celebrities began to put my name out in the public more and more. Soon I couldn't even go to the store without being recognized and asked to sign something for them. 'When The Levee Breaks' was suddenly everyone's favorite book. The media began to pay more and more attention. Soon they were making stabs at me, calling me everything from a celebrity wingman to a binge drinking alcoholic. The more I heard, the more I wanted to get away... and the only thing that got me away from the constant torture was more alcohol. One thing lead to another and..." Another night, another empty bottle of jack on the table. The sound of "Coming Undone" by Korn floods the senses in the party atmosphere at one of Vegas' premiere clubs. Slamming down the glass, Markus Blaire stares through a pair of sunglasses back at Zacy Atomic with a sly smirk on his face. "Donchu eva fuggin doub me again!" Markus tries to chuckle while everyone else laughs at his inability to complete coherent sentences. "Ica drink man. ICA FUGGIN DRINK!" "Woo man, you're startin to get too much for me." Zacy states, "...but seriously man. I had a talk with my agent last night and you've been getting a lot of press as a boozer. They're starting to tell me it's getting bad for business. They tell me that I shouldn't hang out with you and start getting to work on a new album before people start to think all I do is party and I don't ever do shit man." "FUCK DAT MAN!" Markus is obviously blitzed as he screams that and tries to get up to convince Zachary otherwise, but tries over the step and goes rolling across the floor. Two of the band members look away from the embarrassing situation as Markus begins to dry heave on the floor. After a couple heaves he screams back at Atomic, "CHARLETTE ALREADY LET ME GO MAN! I NEED YOU MAN! I NEED YOU!" "You need a drink of water Markus, you should look at yourself." "FUCK YOU!" Markus screams back, trying to pick himself back up to his feet grabbing onto the table. "Yatell me ya gonna stop bein my friend thenya act like ya care dude? FUCK YOU." Wait I'm coming undone Unlaced I'm coming undone Too late I'm coming undone What looks so strong So delicate Wait I'm starting to suffocate And soon I anticipate I'm coming undone What looks so strong So delicate At that moment Markus tries to take a swing at Zacy but can't even function. He falls flat on his face. The entire club turns and stares at Markus as he's flopping around on the floor like a fish. Zacy grabs his jacket and tells his bandmates they're leaving while Markus continues to be a scene on the floor. Once again as he crawls over towards the table next to them he begins to dry heave. Grabbing onto the leg of a complete stranger, he never even sees Fallout Shelter make their way out of the building. The stranger, in a sense of urgency tries to shake away from Markus Blaire. "Get off me! What the hell are you doing?!" Freaking out the man kicks Markus off of his leg, "Get out of here you damn drunk!" It doesn't take long for security to run to the scene while everyone stops and stares. Trying to pull him up off the floor, security struggles with the dead weight while Markus continues to gag. His shoes skid across the ground as they pull him towards the bathroom door. Markus struggles to break free, but does a terrible job, almost causing no struggle at all. The entire room is spinning and he can't even tell up from down. Everything was just a blur of movement and colors as Korn continued to beat his eardrums. Security kicks the bathroom door open and the men inside stop and stare at the situation. The blur of colors turns into a bright white as they throw Markus into a bathroom stall. "Hey buddy, finish throwing up then we'll take you outside." One security guard said as he slammed the door shut, hiding the scene inside from everyone else in the room. It wasn't a pretty sight. What a year it had been for Markus Blaire. He went from dating one of GCW's media darlings, to escaping a fire, to being help for experimentation in Zenith Corp, to writing a best selling novel, to hugging a toilet in one of Las Vegas' finer clubbing establishments. But everything was starting to unravel. He lost his publicist, his best friends, and his entire reputation. He was a joke, a sideshow attraction that no one could ever take seriously again. He was just another victim of the fame. Or possibly a never-was. An after-thought. A sideshow attraction. An outcast. A freak. In the end that's all he ever was. A freak. Looking down into the toilet bowl Markus managed to see his reflection in the water. His sunglasses long fallen off at the table. His once beautiful emerald green eyes were blood shot and burning. His long black hair once in a pony tail was strewn about and matted from uncontrollable vomiting. His skin was pale as a ghost. He looked at his reflection and saw a corpse. A mere corpse of what he once was back in college. Those days had past and would never come back again. He was stuck living the rest of his life as a freak. Someone for everyone else to stop and stare at. Someone for people to gawk and marvel at. The bar for everyone else to realize how good they have it. Reaching into his pocket he pulled out a small handgun from his jeans. It was something that Zacy made him carry around with him, for whatever reason. He held it down in front of him, staring at it for a moment. Wishing deep down inside that this would be the last thing he'd seen. Not people staring, gawking, or pointing and laughing. Anything except for that. Nobody loves a freak. His hands were shaking from a mixture of intoxication and fear. Slowly he moved the handgun up to his head, and placed the barrel against his forehead. Closing his eyes he took a deep breath. That's right Trigger between my eyes Please strike Make it quick now He went to pull the trigger, but couldn't seem to do it. There was something, way deep down inside of him that made him realize how wrong everything had gone. This man had survived treatment equal to that of a prisoner of war. The constant abuse and prodding of needles couldn't drive this man to the brink, but one thing could. Fame. The life of the public eye. Something convinced him that he deserved better than to die with a bullet wound in his head floating in a toilet bowl. So he dropped the gun in the bowl with a splash, before a eruption of alcohol was released from his stomach and out through his mouth. Through tears running down his face and the burning sensations in his eyes, nose, and throat... he came to the realization that he couldn't do this on his own anymore. He was tired of being alone. He needed help. Not the help of a bottle but real, honest to God help. The kind you can only get from a professional to get his life back on track. ---- CHAPTER FOUR: The Life Of A Role Model ---- "Then they called someone to give me a ride back home... lord knows who, I sure as hell don't remember." Markus spoke as he peered back at the woman he once called his girlfriend across the table from him, "I don't remember a lot from that night. Most of that story was told to me from Rebecca here at the clinic, once she'd talked to the people at the club that night." "You know... you might not have died that night even if you did pull that trigger right?" Vivica hesitated but finally spoke, "Then you would've had a lot more questions to answer." "Who knows what would've happened..." Markus spoke slowly and cautiously, "...I can't say I've ever been in a situation to find out the truth." "You know this life isn't easy for anyone." Vivica finally admitted, "Not even me. I've made my share of mistakes you know. Even though nothing happened I did throw myself at that guy in the club that night. I felt so isolated. So alone. I needed company and comfort just like you did. I was told you were dead you know. I had nightmares for months dreaming about that fire. Then that asshole Bryan Mayhem made it even worse. He set me on fire... you know I was so fucking scared." "But I heard you looked amazing that night." Markus interrupted, "That's what Lia told me." "It's because no matter what happens to you and what you're going through..." The Fearless Phenom paused for a moment, "You can't let this world get you down. You have to keep up appearances and through it all keep that smile on your face because they second you let your guard down there are people in the world who will cut you down so fast you won't even know it until you're on your fucking back. Some people out there are heartless killers. They don't care about anyone but themselves you know?" Markus simply looked back nodding as Valentine continued. "But there are good people out there Markus." Vivica kept going, "Genuinely great people who are out there doing things like feeding starving children, trying to cure cancer, and saving people's lives every single day. This world isn't hopeless. Me? I'm just a girl who loves my business. I love my life. A World Champion is all I've ever wanted to be and how fucking lucky am I to do that everyday? You have to take the good with the bad Markus. People like us, in a mysterious way we've been blessed with a gift. The gift to make a difference. And we can either harness that gift and do some good with it or we can blow it all away on ourselves." "It's so easy to say..." "But you have to be better than those people on the other side of those drapes Markus." Vivica interrupted him before he started making excuses, "People look up to us. We have to build our fences. Set restrictions. It's so easy to watch those famous people on television and think about how great they have it without seeing this side of the business. Some of us aren't made to live like rock stars you know. You need to stop living a stereotype and just get back to being yourself. That's what got us here in the first place. And fuck them for trying to change us you know? Who the fuck are they?" Markus runs his hands through his hair, trying desperately to think of the right words to say or the right things to do. "But we can't blame them for all of our problems because in the end it comes down to our ability to be ourselves and stand up for what we truly and deeply believe in... deep down inside of here." Vivica points to her heart buried deep inside of her chest, "This is what keeps me sane through all of this. This is what keeps me out of The Establishment, and keeps me from turning into a monster like that fuckstain Chris Bagwell or that scarred up creeper Face they're making me fight this week. It's against what I believe in. Deep down inside all of us is a champion." Vivica reached out and grabbed the hand of her former love. "You just have to find it and run with it before those people outside destroy it." Valentine said with a smirk, "That's what being a role model is about. It's not about being on the cover of Wheaties, or having an action figure, or having a shit ton of money, or even telling kids to stay in school..." "It's about showing them what they're capable of." Markus finally said. "By God... I think this kid has it." Valentine said with a smile. "Now it's easy enough to say the right thing. Now it's time for you to show the world what you got."
