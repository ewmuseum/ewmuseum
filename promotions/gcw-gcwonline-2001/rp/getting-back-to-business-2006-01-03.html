Getting back to business

3 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/3/06 Ever since Thanksgiving I'd been pretty much on cool down mode. Caldera had decided to postpone the WorldWide's for Decemeber along with Christmas Chaos. I was kinda upset that I would have to wait a bit to tear people apart again, but at the same time I was greatful that I had some time to just kick back and relax and spend a strech of uninterupted time with Cindy. After the events of Thanksgiving where I had given her brother a verbal dressing down on my job and why I did it and how I felt about Cindy, he and I had been on better speaking terms. Christmas was spent with my sister, Elizabeth, her husband Chris, and my parents. Considering the money I now had at my disposal, I treated my family nice, the rest of my family I mailed gifts to. I cleaned up pretty nicely too. New Years was a nice little blowout. Having a nice roomy penthouse apartment overlooking Central Park was nice for the shitload of people I invited. A lot of friends and family over to ring in the New Year. We were even able to see some of the fireworks being blasted up in Times Square when midnight hit. But now, all the festivities were behind us. 2006 was offically here and three days in I knew GCW would be getting back on track soon, and if that was the case I wanted to be ready when Steven Caldera picked up the phone and started calling us in. I felt a tap on my shoulder and stopped the rowing machine in my personal gym. I stopped the iPod nano and looked over at Cindy, mopping the sweat off my face. 'Yeah?' 'Jeff's on the phone.' I got up and headed into the living room taking a swig of my water as I went. 'Happy new year you funny looking bastard.' I said. 'Yeah, yeah, yeah, fuck you too.' Jeff said, laughing, 'So you ready to get back into the swing of things?' 'Never was out of the swing. Just because I had time off doesn't mean I haven't had GCW at the back of my mind. I've been working out, keeping myself in fighting shape. Just because it was the holiday season, I haven't been slacking off. Can't do that if I wanna be the next US champion.' 'Already got your sights set, huh?' Jeff asked. 'It's a resolution.' I said, smirking as I looked out the window onto the park and saw New York moving 40 stories below me. 'That along with kicking as much ass as humanly possible. Trust me Jeff, I've been in this business long enough to know that I can't slack off for a second, I can't drop my guard for a minute, and I've got my focus back. GCW's gonna see a new and impoved Machine when we get back to work.' 'The Machine version 2.0?' 'Something like that.' I smirked. 'Well I don't wanna keep you, just wanted to make sure that you're still on the ball.' 'Never fell off.' 'I'll let you know if I hear from GCW about when you go back to work.' 'Right, and I'll get in touch with you if they call me first.' I hung up and headed back to my workout. A half hour later after I stepped out of the shower and got dressed in a pair of sweats and a t-shirt I saw Cindy wrapped up in the college football games on ESPN. I chuckled to myself, not really ever having gotten much into college football. Gimmie the NFL anyday. 'OH! Come on! What game are you watching?!' Cindy screamed from the living room, making me chuckle again as I tossed fresh fruit into the smoothie maker and poured some fruit juice over it. I headed back into the living room with two smoothies as I walked back into the living room and watched the game, not getting as much into it as Cindy was. My mind was elsewhere, mainly on GCW and getting back to work. It was a new year, we had new blood coming in, and I had some new titles to add to my already impressive resume. Jeff had called me the Machine version 2.0. Maybe he wasn't too far off the mark after all. I wasn't the same old Machine. I was new and improved baby.
