Raised to be lowered: A tale that started but never finished

24 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 24, 2008 Her words were a sharpened blade, cutting deeper and deeper with each pronounced syllable. Making the sting worsen was the fact that after each word her eyes made a slight almond crest, emphasizing and reinforcing her uncaring for the predicament at hand. 'Yes,' she replied, eyes pulsing, 'what does it matter?' 'What does it matter?' A pause from the bandanna clad man followed by a silent double stomp of his foot under the table. 'Do you know who he is?' 'He is a friend Jay. A friend of mine and once of yours.' 'A friend? Caroline. No. Not him.' 'And why not?' Again, the tightening of the corner of her eyes accented the force of her words. They lingered in the air, unanswered, as if a dejected puppy, until Jay's foot stomp came again. It was becoming his trigger to speak up. 'Because he's a snake. A god damn snake.' A pause followed by a familiar silence. Caroline formulated her words. 'A snake? Of all people -' A longer pause, followed by a sigh. 'Jordan offers comfort, he offers his time to talk about the real snake.' 'I thought we were better. I thought you gave up your pouting. What the hell Caroline?' 'My pouting?' 'Come on. I went a whole week on the couch thinking I was going to drown in those tears.' A shake of the head from the lady followed closely by her legs stretching and planting into the carpet. Her hand shoved her weight to a vertical base and held her there long enough to look Jay deep in the eyes. She placed a permanent hex on her pupils making them seem inescapable. Then she stepped forward. 'Fuck you Jay Terror.' Jay stood from his place. He was quick with it. 'No Caroline, fuck you. Go ahead, be with your psychiatrist. I'm sure Jordan has boxes of tissues beside his bed. Anyone who misses opportunities as often as him is bound to sob himself to sleep.' It was fast and to the point. Jay watched her hand near his face and waited as his head snapped back into place. A red handprint colored his skin sinking in the pores and filling his insides with animosity. On instinct, he meant to hit her. Through courtesy, he refrained. Caroline walked off and to the door. 'Caroline. Wait.' Wait she did not. The door rocked the hotel room leaving Terror to hold his balance. 'Fuck Jordan White.' He muttered under his breath. __________ The smoke sifted to Caldera's nostrils and made his face squint into a pickled mess. His eyes watered and his temper raised. 'I caught word that you've been skipping out on training.' 'Training,' he passed a cloud of smoke across the limo seat and toward his boss, 'training just wasn't my thing.' 'Not your thing? Jay, I hired him to make sure that you're on top of your game. I hired him to make sure that you're healthy enough to last through a whole match.' 'Oh come on. You know me. Ask any girl how long I -' Cutting him off, 'And the cigarettes. I thought you were going to leave them behind.' His voice was sincere, words that a father would tell his own child. Being Jay Terror though, he found the nearest ashtray to rest what was left of his Marlboro 27. It smoked by itself as Jay whipped out a fresh box. He pulled one loose. 'Try one and I bet you die of happiness.' 'I bet I die alright. You're a professional wrestler Jay. That takes endurance and -' 'I dedication, and heart, and blah blah blah. I know Steve. I'm just saying, why ruin a good thing? It's gotten me this far, maybe it's a good luck thing. Like a rabbit's foot.' He shook his head, 'I've invested a lot of time in making you this big. I expect Warpath to go as planned, with yoy walking out with the title. You know that as well as I do. If you can honestly say that you'll make it through a match with two people that utterly despise us and what we stand for, I'll believe you. But if you have any doubt, I expect you to whip it up and smash it. You will be the champion after Warpath.' Jay grabbed up his first cigarette from the tray and lit his second. Taking a long drag, he finished off the first and ditched it for a fresher one. 'I know I will. That's why it doesn't matter what I do. I could smoke three packs a day and get hammered every night and still walk out of that match the champion. It's the perfect set-up.' 'How do you figure?' 'You, Octavian, DeBough - I have a crew.' Caldera inches forward on his seat and swivels toward Jay. His smirk is very characteristic of the billionaire. 'You think that because we have numbers that we're safe?' 'I don't think anything I know isn't true.' 'Yes, we have numbers. That can't be the only thing we have going though. You're the leader of everyone right now, the one pulling the reigns. If you're not strong, there's no way anyone else under you will be strong enough.' He paused only for a second. 'Are you strong enough?' 'I'll show you how strong.' 'How do you suppose we do that?' 'I don't know. Book a match. Gimme' someone to mash.' 'On Worldwide?' 'Yeah. Book me someone, we'll see if I have to go on a cigarette diet.' Caldera freezes for a second and leans against the cold leather again. His jacket pokes at the corners of his armpits and ruffles around his ribcage. 'Who do you want to face?' 'Anyone. Who's free?' 'Kimbusa?' 'Serious?' 'It'd prove that you were really ring-ready for Warpath.' That ape got beat by the tag champions. it's not worth it.' Again, Caldera freezes and racks his brain. With a head turn, 'How about Storm? Chris Storm doesn't have a match and beat Kimbusa. Plus he's a pain in the ass. What do you say?' 'Chris Storm?' Terror now stops, takes a drag on his cigarette, and finds a sudden bubble of comfort. He shrugs, 'Book it.' 'Just don't let us down. There's more riding on this than you think.' 'Come on. I'm Jay Fuckin' Terror.' __________ If I were to write a letter to Jordan White, I'm pretty sure it would start off something like, "Hey assfuck, I hope you die." Something like that. Yeah. Or maybe something like, "I hope you get sand in your fuckin' vag you waste of life." Maybe. Not quite sure. Still, it'd have to tell him how much I can't stand him. Something that he could put on his mantle and read before going to bed. Maybe "Sweet dreams you fuckin' twat." That'd be a good one too. God knows Caroline would end up reading it when she rolled off his dick though. Sometimes I wonder. Why do I try? I give the girl everything, make her life amazing. And then one night I roll over and she's not there. I can't penetrate, I can't do anything. God damn Jordan White and that night in Japan. Figured he was a better guy than that. I mean, the chick he was holding onto that night. Damn I'd fuck her. Fuck that. Shit, just rambling again. I need to get Caroline back. I need to kill Jordan White. Good, I have a plan. Kill the fucker, take Caroline, fuck her till she believes in us again. Perfect. Fuck Jordan White. __________ 'You know what Jay, I don't think I've ever seen a better wrestler. You're damn good out there.' Her voice was sweet and drunk, smelled something like rotting whiskey. The air seemed to float around Jay's head as a constant reminder of how shitty barstool talks with women really were. 'Yeah. I'm pretty good. I know.' 'Not just pretty good, you're damn good.' She offered an over exaggerated wink and a shit-faced grin. 'You want to get out of here?' 'No. Not yet. Let's talk. More.' 'About?' 'About you. Who is Leanna Heston?' 'Heston?' 'I thought that's what you said -' 'No,' she corrected, 'Hester. Leanna Hester.' 'Same name kinda'.' He smirked and spun the Jack Daniels around the edge of his glass. 'I'm sorry. Just been out of it.' 'You want to get out of here?' 'No. More talking.' 'About what?' 'I don't know. I'm out of it. Give me a second.' Being drunk, a second really meant don't stop talking. Her lips continued to flap, smacking each other over and over. 'Is it someone else.' 'Yeah. Kinda'. I love her.' 'Love?' 'A lot. Yes. But I don't know. I think she loves someone else.' 'Oh. Bummer.' 'Yeah.' 'You're a great wrestler.' 'I know.' He finished the contents of the drink and asked graciously for another. The bartender refilled the glass with a smile as he looked over the wavering woman beside him. Her bra was hanging out of her low-cut v-neck dress and her panties were exposed through open legs. Pink lace. 'You ever fall in love?' 'Once. Yeah. Maybe. Can I have another?' The bartender typically shied away when the customer had too many but he was enjoying her drunk stupor too much to care. She was replenished. 'How was it?' Sipping her drink, 'How was what?' 'Love. How was love?' 'I wasn't in love.' 'But I thought -' 'You want to get out of here?' Jay shook his head again, another sip of whiskey. 'Soon. I want to hear about the time you were in love.' 'Oh. That time. I was young. High school young. He was so hot, and damn he fucked me good. But we ended up in love. He did the cute things and I made him dinner and shared everything. It was really great.' Terror leaned in as if interested, 'And how did it end?' 'He slept with my best friend.' 'Oh. Harsh.' It almost looked like a tear was going to form in her eyes but she drank it away with another shot of whiskey. 'You have a great body.' 'I know.' He watched her finger run down his front and followed it with his eyes as it hovered over his midsection. It playfully traced his zipper. 'What are you doing?' 'You want to get out of here.' 'Yeah,' and a sigh, 'fine.' __________ Note to self, decisions are one of those things that shouldn't be made when you're drunk. Never. Especially when it involves drinking so much that you know it's only so you can stand the sex. Caroline is going to hate me. __________ Waking up beside some girl named Leanna called for an early morning cigarette and four Excedrin. Jay crawled out from under the sheets, pulled on last night's pair of boxer shorts, and stepped out onto the hotel deck wondering how he had ended up giving her a chance. She was a great catch but nothing like Caroline. Nothing at all. Pulling his cellular phone from his pocket, Jay speed dialed the number two and brought the speaker to his ear. One ring. Two rings. Voicemail. 'Hey Caroline, it's Jay. We really have to talk. I'm sorry about the other day. I am. Really. I was just upset. You know. Things have been so stressful with wrestling and this relationship. Don't get me wrong though. I love us. We're perfect. I just get upset easier and I know that's bad. But -' He was cut off by the timer. Slamming the phone together, he smoked off the deck looking at the storm clouds turning in the sky. Their gray faces all looked at Jay and criticized the way he held his smoke and the way he placed his feet on the railing. They called him stupid for leaving Leanna alone in the room. Jay ignored him. Shutting his eyes, he tried to imagine a perfect world. Caroline was always naked and swimming in the money he gave her. He was world champion, a mogul like no other. Jordan White really was dead. Vivica Valentine was his maid. Everything looked so perfect in his head but opening his eyes, he knew it was far from the truth. Breaking the moment, Leanna Hester opened the sliding door and sat in the chair across from Jay. The only article of clothing she thought to put on was the ribbon from her hair. 'Morning,' she said. 'Morning.' He offered a cigarette but she denied it. 'Feeling better?' She wondered. 'What do you mean?' 'Last night. You were out of it. Upset kinda'.' 'You remember?' 'I wasn't that drunk.' Jay laughed off her denial, 'Uh huh.' He took a drag and let the smoke enter his lungs. 'I'm better though. Yeah.' 'You sure.' 'No. Not really.' 'The girl?' 'Yeah. I don't know what I was thinking. No offense of course. Just, you know. I fucked you and fucked myself at the same time last night.' 'She doesn't have to know.' 'No. She'll know. She knows everything.' Leanna nodded and looked away at the clouds. 'It's going to storm.' 'Pretty hard I bet.' 'I'm sorry you know,' said she. 'Sorry?' 'For ruining things. I'm sorry.' 'No. Don't worry about it. It was good. I just. Just don't worry about it.' 'Ok.' Silence overtook the porch but if either Jay or Leanna took a look behind the glass, they would make as much noise as humanly possible. Before they could see her though, Caroline was gone and out the door leaving only the rustling shades as a witness.
