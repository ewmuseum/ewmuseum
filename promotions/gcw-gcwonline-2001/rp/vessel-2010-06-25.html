Vessel

25 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/25/10 The choke. I thought about it constantly. An albatross, around the neck, one that suffocated all life away. One that pressed its fingers, firmly across the throat. The bruises formed. The vessels clotted. The capillaries snapped. The pressure that forced breath from mouth. I was stupid to think that it wouldn’t work that way. Once was an aberration. Twice was on me. But three times? Three motherfucking times? What could it be, but sheer ignorance. Arrogance. Bullheaded nonsense. I was Ahab. And that damned whale had me dead to rights. I sat there, watching the tape. Over. Over. Over. Over. Over. It never changed. It never flipped. It was him. That mask, of my girl. My bitch, on his fucking mug. Laughing at me. Staring back at me. Like I was the fucking punk this whole time. Like everything I knew about him…meant all of jack-fuckin’-shit. I tried to reserve a hotel room inside his twisted fuckin’ mind. Turns out, he checked into mine ages ago. ****** In her office nestled in one of the middle floors of the Transamerica Pyramid in downtown San Francisco, Dr. Mariah Hawthorne, renowned psychologist, stares at Triumph Frost through her designer glasses. She’s of beauty; a Sophia Loren knock-off of days past, a stunner of simple epic proportions. In a setting of elegance that overlooks the cityscape, she fidgets, tapping her pen to her legal pad. "I’m glad you’re opening up, Mister, umm, Frost," she opines. "But you understand you’re not an actual client of mine…" He interrupts her trailing voice without pretense, his head rising from the comfortable leather couch. "I wasn’t done, you know," he states. Dr. Hawthorne sighs, removing her glasses and wiping them down. "Yes, but you barged into my office, and threw Mr. Delano out while we were in the middle of a session." She places her glasses promptly back on her face. "He was a sixty-seven year old man. Then you threatened my secretary with extreme bodily harm when she tried to help him, and sat on my couch and started spouting non-stop since." Triumph, from his rested position on the couch, eyes faced towards the ceiling, wastes no time in easing her concerns. "How does the Hawthorne Library at Harvard sound, then?" Dr. Hawthorne’s mouth goes agape. Triumph rises from his rested position, smiling. "So how about you shut your fuckin’ hole and do your job, bitch?" Her nod is slight, but evident. Triumph slides back into his previous position. A moment of silence. Dr. Hawthorne breaks it. "You were saying?" ****** So as I continue, save from the rude interruption from the audience…I was aghast, you know? Flabbergasted. It wasn’t like I didn’t plan. It wasn’t like he wasn’t every piece of thought that crossed my mind. All it was... Just him. His contorting face. His unidentifiable nomenclature. His...being. His complex being. I couldn’t get a fix on it. Every time I tried, it switched. He’s a chameleon. A changeling. A mime. And I stand, wondering where I let go. The enigma. He had my number, each and every time. What a goddamned joke… ****** Triumph rubs his face with urgency, as if attacking a failure that’s presented itself. Dr. Hawthorne springs into action. "Do you find yourself as some sort of action junkie," she presented, as she scribbled into a notepad. "Someone that just looks for a violent release?" He positions himself in a heightened seating position, curious at her inquiry. "You sayin’ this shit’s my fuckin’ fault?" She shakes her head furiously ‘no’. "Hardly," she reasons. "But you want me to get to the root of the issue, don’t you?" "I do," Triumph responds coyly. She adjusts her glasses once again. "Then you have to trust my methods," she tells. "Otherwise..." Her voice fades away. She rubs her nose. "Why bother tracking me down in the first place?" She pauses, mid-thought. "How did you find me, any--" ****** I used to think I knew what beauty was. You know what it was? A thousand-dollar shirt from Lacoste. Five hundred dollar jeans from Abercrombie. A hundred-thousand dollar watch. And then... Beauty came in the form of a wrap around my neck. A twist of my arm. A last gasp of my breath. The end of my journey. You ever watch "Blue Velvet"? It’s a deadly fuckin’ movie. I watched that thing all the time. Frank Booth. "You know what a love letter is? It’s a bullet from a fucking gun, fucker!" I always wanted to be just like him, you know? Confident. Fearless. Not givin’ a fuck about anyone or anything. Just doin’ things my way. The right way. ****** She taps her pen along her notebook. "I think you’re getting off-topic," she remarks. Triumph turns to her, and gives her a longing look, as she chews on the pen. His face changes. "You’re beautiful," he croons. "You know that?" Taken aback, she coughs, and beams. "Well, umm," she stutters. "Thank you. But--" "You know what I’d love to do," he continues, his voice oozing with assurance. "I’d love to take you out on the town. Dinner at Gary Danko. Chill out at Ruby Skye." He goes full-on lothario. "And then drinks on my 100-foot yacht." Her eyes wide, she’s unsure of what to say. "But," she stammers, holding out her ringed hand. "I’m married." Triumph raises his, displaying his own wedding band. "So?" She nearly bites through the pen to the ink inside. As she drops it, her office door swings open, where Alexis Frost stands perturbed. She glances through the room, before turning her stone-cold gaze upon Triumph. "What the fuck, Tri?" He shrugs, as Dr. Hawthorne leans forward in her chair. "Excuse me," she spouts. "We’re in a session." Alexis burns a hole through the good doctor. "And who," she blares. "The fuck...are you?!?" Dr. Hawthorne remains unmoved. "I might ask you the same thing." "I," Alexis sternly replies. "Am married to this good-for-nothing shithead. I only went to the bathroom, Triumph!" As Dr. Hawthorne displays surprise, Triumph merely shrugs his shoulders and stretches back out on the couch. "I got bored," he sighs. Dr. Hawthorne chimes in. "You’re married?!?" "I was only gone five friggin’ minutes," Alexis shrieks. "And," Triumph yawns. "I got bored," Alexis rolls her eyes. "Whatever. We have to get ready to go to China." Dr. Hawthorne muddles through her thoughts. "But you said we would…" Triumph chuckles. "Why the fuck would I want to go and do that?" "You have commitments," Alexis responds, as she tires of his act. She grabs Triumph by the arm and drags him off the couch. "And I’m not going through this again." Triumph relents and helps her pull him up. As the two exit, Dr. Hawthorne jumps up. "Wait," she yells, as they swing around towards her. "You can’t just leave!" "Don’t worry, lady," Triumph snorts. "You’ll get a plaque in a Stanford dorm or whatever, just like I said." She doesn’t take his response well. "But you said Harvard!" Triumph acts indignant. "The Harvard of the west. Whatever." They walk out, leaving Dr. Hawthorne simply aghast. ****** Triumph pushes through the double doors to the outside, with Alexis at his side. As they walk along through the hustle and bustle of downtown San Fran, she pouts her lips exaggeratedly. "So," she says amusedly. "Why’d you choose the therapist?" Triumph raises an eyebrow. "What do you mean?" She sort of skips alongside him. "Well," she revels in the moment. "You could have bothered anyone in that building. You chose a therapist." "Psychologist," he interrupts. "Why her?" Alexis queries. "Why not the stockbrokers, the lawyers, the fucking janitor." Triumph takes in a deep breath. "Just..." He exhales. "...‘Cause." She smirks. "Yeah, I bet." They make their way to a parked SUV with tinted windows, and a man standing next to the back end, with former spook written all over his dark sunglasses and black suit. The driver opens the door, as Alexis goes to get in, before she stops and confronts her man. "You’re going to go home and pack," she remarks. He’s awash with confusion. "Huh?" She extends a finger right in his grill. "No fooling around," she chides him, a mother admonishing her child. She wags her finger as she checks off her mental list. "No drinking, no drugs, no fatty foods, no TV, no video games, no watching porn, no surfing the net, no naps, no aimless driving trips ‘round town…" If not for her grabbing her wrist, she’d continue. He deftly grins. "And what if I refuse?" She pulls her wrist away, finger back in his eye, as he jumps back. "I’ll drag you to the goddamned airport in your underwear," she growls. "Kicking and screaming, if I have to." "Alright," he concedes. "Fine. Whatever." She boards the SUV, and the driver shuts the door. She immediately rolls down the window, leaning her head out. Noticing he’s on the outside looking in, he gets the picture right quick. "Wait," he states. "Where the hell are you going?" "Shopping," she retorts. "I’m not gonna wear the same ol’ stuff to Beijing!" She winks his way, as he shakes his head in disbelief. "Just remember, no--" He closes his eyes, walking away. "Yeah, yeah," he demurs. "No fun." The window rolls up, as the SUV pulls away. Triumph walks down the sidewalk alone, walking towards his parked 442. He jumps in the car and adjusts his rear view, before catching his own eyes. He stares off into the distance. Into nothingness. A million miles away. ****** Surrounded by bags filled with designer clothes, Alexis takes a load off at an outside bistro. Sipping her espresso, a shadow approaches that engulfs her. She turns her head, and, from the cover of her wide-brimmed Dolce hat and Gucci sunglasses, she spots the older man, flanked by two suited bodyguards, in Kenji Iwamura, Triumph’s uncle and the controlling owner of IwaCorp. He smiles politely, the sun shining off of his own pair of sunglasses. "Do you mind if I join you, my dear?" She turns her attention back to her drink, cradling it with both hands. He waves the bodyguards away and takes a seat across from her. She does her best to avoid eye contact with him. He takes off his shades, folding them and putting them in his suit-jacket pocket. "I’m sure you’re wondering who--" "I know who you are," she interrupts. She finally acknowledges he’s there. "From the party. He told me all about you." "Really," he says. "Like what?" She lowers her shades for a moment, displaying her stone-cold eyes. "Like not to trust you." Kenji takes her comment without a word, his gaze seemingly affixed to her as she adjusts her sunglasses to their previous position. He raises his arm, signaling the passing waitress. She pulls out her notepad, order taking at the ready. "I’ll have what the lady’s had, if you don’t mind." The waitress nods and leaves to fetch his order. Alexis’ interest wanes almost immediately. "Look, I don’t know where Triumph is," she states. "That’s quite alright, my dear," he responds. "I was looking to talk with you." She lowers her shades, her interest suddenly returning. ****** Meanwhile, back at Triumph’s lush pad, the man lays out on the couch, without a shirt, in Nike shorts, sipping from a bottle of Glenlivet, eating pork rinds, and watching his large television. He’s entranced by the bright images, as he flicks through channels nonchalantly. He briefly makes his way to the Entertainment channel, where the show being displayed is of the Armenian women that are famous for being absolutely worthless and devoid of any and all purpose. "Where are my shoes," whines the attractive yet annoying one. "Right here," bellows the sickeningly ugly one that moonlights as a beard for a basketball player. "Let’s talk about me," bleats the attention-whoring one with the large ass and sex tape. "What the fuck is this shit," Triumph sneers, eyes burning with hatred, as he quickly returns to his channel surfing, taking a large gulp from the bottle. The monotony of flipping through the cable catalog suddenly gets broken up by the sounds of the doorbell. Triumph ignores it at first, but it rings out again. He leans his head back. "Lex, get the fuckin’ door, will ya!" He stops. "Oh, right." Turning off the television, Triumph grabs a Perry Ellis short-sleeved button-up and throws it on, without bothering to button it up. He makes his way to the front doors of his mansion, bottle of whiskey still in hand, mumbling to himself the entire time. "Jesus, why haven’t we hired any maids or butlers to do this shit. It’s not my job to answer doors." The doorbell rings once more as he pauses in front of the stained-glass paned oak double doors. He cups his hand over his mouth to smell his breath, grimacing slightly, before answering. At the door stands a stunning Japanese beauty in her early forties. Her jet-black hair flows down past her shoulders and onto the spaghetti-strap blue dress that drapes over her. He eyes her over, doing everything save howling like a cartoon wolf in a zoot suit. He fires the first salvo. "Shit, are you my happy ending?" She laughs incredulously, shaking her head from side to side. She places a hand on her hip. "I’m looking for my husband," she declares, her brown eyes sparkling in the sunshine. "Maybe you know him. He’s your uncle Kenji?" The balloon pops, and the hate returns, as Keiko Iwamura, Kenji’s wife, makes her presence known. "Yeah?" Triumph spits out fire. "You see him, tell him I said to go fuck himself." Triumph tries to slam the door shut in Keiko’s face, before she cradles her matching blue stiletto in place. "Wait," she hollers as she grips the side of the door and pushes it back open, with Triumph not putting up much resistance. Triumph opens the door and lets her enter. He walks away from her, taking a sip from the bottle as he does. He wipes the excess off his face with the back of his hand. "Look, I don’t know what you want, or what you think. But as far as I’m concerned..." He swings back around to face her. "Me and that fucker are quits." Suddenly, he’s a bit taken aback, as Keiko sports the look of a mother reunited with a lost child. He takes it in, bringing the bottle back up to his lips for another sip. "You don’t remember me, do you," she asks. He’s at a loss for words, as she sidles over towards him. She rubs a finger down his chest. "My, how you’ve grown." The finger stops right above his shorts, and makes its way back upwards. "It only seems like yesterday I was sipping wine with your mother in between changing your diapers." The finger makes its way to his lips. "How things change." He’s mesmerized by her siren song. The finger crosses his lips and falls by the wayside, as she gazes into his eyes, craning to be as close to eye-level with him as she possibly can. "I was sorry to hear about her, by the way." "Yeah," he says, with faux nonchalance, as his head pulls closer and closer to hers. "Things happen." With faces inches away, she purses her lips...before suddenly pulling away and taking in the breathtaking lobby of Triumph’s castle. "Your house is simply amazing," she gushes. "I see luxury is not something you take lightly." A bit confused, Triumph attempts to steer things to a point. "So, uhh…" "Keiko," she shines. "It’s Keiko." "Right," Triumph says, as he rubs the back of his neck. "So, Keiko. Umm...how can I put this..." He ponders, choosing his words carefully. "What the fuck do you want?" She seems to ignore him at first, gawking at the lobby. Finally, she brings her attention back to him. "You got a place to have a drink?" She saunters right past him. "I’m thirsty." ****** Kenji sips from his espresso, Alexis with her elbow on the table and fist propped against her cheek, both holding her head in place. He takes a napkin and methodically dabs around his mouth before placing it back on the table. "Triumph thinks he knows a lot of things about a lot of things," he says. "But he doesn’t actually...know things." Alexis returns a blank stare. "What are you getting at?" Kenji’s tenseness fades, if only for a moment. "I’ve no doubt he’s told you about the unpleasantness at his father’s funeral." Alexis nods. "He was right about one thing," Kenji says. "I did come on to his mother. But…" He pauses in thought, staring off in the distance. He blinks twice quickly, and returns to reality. "My interest was not necessarily unrequited." Alexis pulls her head away from her fist and leans in. "You mean...?" Kenji nods. "We were both struck. It was so sudden. Out of the blue. He was always such a cautious man. So fastidious." He wipes his face, laughing almost in disbelief. "I often joked with him that it would be some jilted lover, come to take their revenge." The disbelief washes away. Kenji places a palm flat on the table. "Have you ever lost anyone close to you, Alexis?" Alexis shakes her head in agreement. "A cousin of mine. He died in a car crash, drunk driver in the wrong lane." Kenji closes his eyes. "We both knew the fragility of life," he tells her. "Death is a dagger, sticking you between the ribs on a cold night from a dark alleyway. Even if you see it coming, you know what it ultimately means." "For a time, we found comfort in each other. Triumph was so innocent, so precocious. So...oblivious. We both agreed some sort of strong male presence around him would do him good. But..." Kenji’s voice trails off. Looking away, he wipes his mouth with his hand. He refocuses. "There were too many factors, pulling and prodding and pushing." "I was…" Kenji quickly corrects himself. "Am married. Even then, to the same woman, and even though there has always been an unspoken understanding, it proved to be tenuous, at best. And Triumph’s mother, well…" Kenji trails off once again. He chuckles and rubs his eyes. "She still loved her husband, as any good wife should. But, unfortunately for her, unfaithfulness courses through the Iwamura veins. She saw him in me. Both the good...and the bad." "So you just broke it off," Alexis asks, fascinated. "Just like that?" "Have you ever thought if you gave something enough time, it would work itself out, only to have it disappear before you realize it’s too late?" He rubs the rim of his cup with his finger. "It’s the worst feeling in the world." Kenji dabs his finger on the napkin. "Just like that, she was across an ocean, and it was over." He muses for a moment before collecting himself properly. "So I did what I could, and moved on. It is life, after all. Until the spin stops, we march on." Alexis prods. "Does Triumph know about this?" Kenji laughs. "Even if I had, he’d hardly believe anything I had to say to him. And our last encounter was hardly one that worked in my favor." He sighs. "Triumph was told what he was told, and he believes what he believes. And while I find it discomforting to know that he has broken ties with his past, I do not hold it against him." He knocks on the table. "He is my nephew, after all." "So...what? You think he’ll listen to me?" Alexis chuckles all her own. "You must be joking." Kenji shakes his head. "I have no intention of putting you into such an unenviable position." Alexis takes it all in, her own seconds of pondering, before asking her question. "So why are we talking, then?" Kenji smiles a knowing smile. "When she left, I felt this emptiness. I gave into temptation, much as my brother and our father had before me. Yes, I was married. No, I didn’t care." He reaches across the table, cradling her hand. "I have gazed through the looking glass. The view does not bode well." She pulls her hand away and crosses her arms. "You know he knows better. Triumph is nothing like you. He’s nothing like his father." "I thought the same thing once, as well," he replies. "And yet, here we are." She’s not buying into it, as he pulls out his sunglasses. He goes to put them on, but suddenly stops. Holding them in his hand, he places them flat on the table and reasserts himself. "Sometimes, Alexis, chaos overtakes order." With tension thick in the air from Alexis, Kenji remains emotionless as he picks up his sunglasses and puts them on. He reaches into his pocket and pulls out a hundred dollar bill, tossing it on the table. He gives one last knowing smile, Alexis as displeased as ever. Kenji passes by her, placing his hand on her shoulder. He leans slightly her way. "I hope you enjoy your time in China." He pulls his hand away. "The both of you." As he leaves, Alexis remains, arms crossed. Her surly demeanor becomes melancholy, mewling in thought. ****** In Triumph’s den-cum-library, bookshelves littered with countless works of literature are flanked by architecture and design that would make the prototypical nuclear family dad jealous and Hugh Heffner cry in his sailor’s hat. Behind a bar with every liquor bottle imaginable lined up against the wall to his back, Triumph pours a fresh bottle of Maker’s Mark into his a glass filled with ice while Keiko sits on the opposite side, while the sounds of Dave Brubeck float along the air. He goes to pour into her glass filled with ice, but she stops him. "I like mine neat." Triumph grabs the glass and dumps the ice out, and pours away. They clink glasses and take their sips, with their gazes affixed firmly upon one another. She slides off the barstool and strolls over to a collection of vinyl records. She flips through them, her painted nails clicking off the covers. She peruses through the works of Coltrane, Davis, Parker, Getz, Fitzgerald, and Simone, among others. "You have quite the ear for music." Triumph sidles behind her. "Those aren’t mine." Keiko turns her head slightly. "Your father’s?" Triumph downs his drink. "You here to talk about my father?" Her perfect pearly whites peek from between her ruby-red lips. "We can talk about anything you’d like." Triumph’s nose hovers inches over her shoulder. He breathes down her neck. "Anything I’d like?" She turns her head more, their lips centimeters apart. "Anything." He pulls away, and walks back over to the bar. He slams his glass down on the counter. "I’d like to talk about," he says, as he twists off the cap of the whiskey. He pours liberally. "What the fuck you are bothering me for." She moves across the room, and grabs the bottle out of his hand. She places it on the counter. He goes to take a sip from the glass, but she snatches that away, and puts it on the counter, as well. Keiko bats her eyes. "Do you think I’m beautiful?" Triumph raises an eyebrow, before holding up his hands and backing away. "I don’t buy the merchandise," he says. "I just take in a little window shopping now and then." As his last word barely finishes, she catches him off-guard, and kisses him on the lips passionately. He rips her off of him and pushes her away. "Whoa!" He nearly screams at the top of his lungs. "What the fuck did I just say?" She tries to move back in, but he rebuffs her again. "It’s okay," she states with frustration. "Just go with the flow. Kenji and I have a very open relationship." He’s unmoved. "So?" She becomes an ice queen. "You think I’m stupid?" She puts her hands on her hips. "You’re telling me that you don’t have girls stashed away all over?" Triumph couldn’t be more incredulous if he tried. "Fuck no." She cackles away. "God, I can’t believe you would lie to me." She gets right in his face. "Lying to me, the adulterous little Tsuyo--" Triumph grabs her face, pinching her cheeks. His voice booms with the wrath of gods. "That is not my name." He throws her head back, and he storms over to the bar, grabbing his drink, and downing it. She rubs her face, and shakes off his attack. She sighs deeply. "You can believe all that you want to believe. But their poison flows through your veins all the same. You have to face it." She can’t help but take a sickening pleasure. "Your destiny is infallible." She gathers up her things. "You’ll come around." She flashes him a wink as she leaves. "You always do." Triumph pours another drink, and finishes it with urgency. ****** I’ve walked along an odd path in life. I may not have the years yet, but that does that really matter? If a kid travels all over the globe in only eighteen years, does that make him better than the man that’s never left his town for seventy? It’s all a journey, all of us converging, looking, avoiding, praying, reaching, escaping, feeling, running, searching. Searching. So many people searching for their beauty. I thought I knew what my beauty really was. I think I finally do now. For some, it’s wealth. It’s fame. It’s destruction. It’s hatred. But I know what my beauty really is. It’s a smile. It’s a kiss. It’s a face. Her face. Her. Always her. ****** On the tarmac of a private airfield, Triumph sits on the hood of his 442 next to a Gulfstream jet, two black bags at his feet. He stews, the only thing missing being steam pouring out from his ears. The black SUV pulls up alongside the 442, as the driver opens the back door and Alexis jumps out, her shopping haul in tow, her mood hardly any better than his. She walks up to him, dropping the bags at her feet. Two attendants run from near the plane and scoop up her bags and his bags, and bring them to the plane. He eyes her over. "You’re late," he utters. She sniffs. "Don’t start," she growls. They look at each other, saying nothing. He leaps off of the hood and makes his way to the plane, as she follows, a good few feet apart. As they grow closer and closer to the plane, suddenly they converge. When they do, Triumph wraps his arm around Alexis’ waist and pulls her in close. She rests her head along the side of his chest, as the tension eases. Alexis breathes into his chest. "You think our kids will end up that fucked up?" Triumph shakes his head with discontent. "There’s always adoption." The two walk up the stairs and into the cabin, as the door to the jet slowly shuts behind them.
