The Only Present we have for you is Chaos

26 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/26/2009 “Hey! If any of you are looking for any last-minute gift ideas for me, I have one. I'd like Frank Shirley, my boss, right here tonight. I want him brought from his happy holiday slumber over there on Melody Lane with all the other rich people and I want him brought right here, with a big ribbon on his head, and I want to look him straight in the eye and I want to tell him what a cheap, lying, no-good, rotten, four-flushing, low-life, snake-licking, dirt-eating, inbred, overstuffed, ignorant, blood-sucking, dog-kissing, brainless, dickless, hopeless, heartless, fat-ass, bug-eyed, stiff-legged, spotty-lipped, worm-headed sack of monkey shit he is! Hallelujah! Holy shit! Where's the Tylenol?” -Clark Griswold, ‘National Lampoon’s Christmas Vacation’ 12/23/2009 Dusk… The days grow colder and the nights grow darker. Winter has taken hold of Vegas which has made this urban jungle sprawl even more dark and forbidding than it normally is. At dusk the neon signs light up the desolate landscape as the scum that inhabits this city come out from the shadows to roam about during the night. Partying, gambling, and unprotected sex are the norm during the night in this town. I for one know that for a fact. I know this because I am a native of this town and have seen the lowlife scum first hand since I was young. Living here has made me go from being a normal human being into the soulless, heartless, uncaring bastard that I am today. All of the humanoid wrestling fans and all of my weak, pathetic opponents have this town to thank for the pain that I inflict upon them on a weekly basis. They have this town to blame for my current state. Right now I’m walking through the new City Center complex that has just sprung up downtown. My tag team partner Count Von Macabre is there for some horror show he’s doing. Once at the sprawling complex I head toward the Mandarin Oriental where the Count is located. Walking past the throngs of people past Aria and toward my destination, I reach the Mandarin Oriental and go inside the lobby of the beautiful building. So ironic that this complex is so beautiful and yet this town hides such an ugly, nasty darkness beneath all the glitz and glamour. After going inside, I head over to the lobby front desk where I find the Count with his camera crew who look to be getting ready for filming. The Count sees me and tells his two crew members to take five. He then turns and greets me. “Hello von Doom,” he says in his usual melancholy tone. I give him a look, rather annoyed by this. “Hello Count.” The Count motions around the lobby. “This place is a magnificent masterpiece,” he tells me. “This is more grand than I ever imagined it would be. I thought the hype was overdone but it isn’t. This is truly the grandest hotel complex in Las Vegas.” The Count may think that I’m here to talk about his show or about this casino but I have other things on my mind. “Listen Count, here’s the deal. GCW booked the Christmas Chaos show quick and our little ‘promotional video’ is due tonight. So I came here so we could have this little talk and…” At this point I motion to the camera that I brought with me. He looks at it as I continue. “…Tape it so that we don’t lose because we were too lazy not to get something done.” “Ok,” he replies. “What’s the plan?” This is what I’ve been waiting for him to ask. I’ve been thinking about this ever since the last World Wide. A plan has formulated in my head. “Well we’re going to be in an unknown match so this is the plan. I want this image of us being a comedy tag team to end. I want to hurt people. From now on, I have no feelings, no remorse, no nothing toward my opponents. GCW and everyone in wrestling views me like I’m a fucking joke when the opposite is true. We as a team are the most evil sons of bitches in all of GCW.” “James, what are you saying?” he asks. “We’re going to beat the shit out of whomever gets into the ring with us. And then we’re going to find the little magical action figure so that I win the American Dream. I want to make all of these wrestlers and fans suffer for all the years of torment that they put me through. They laughed at me. They told me that I was a joke. Well now the joke’s on them. I’m going to create as much chaos and destroy as many lives as possible. I got the idea watching Dark Knight. Wrestling doesn’t really have anyone who disrupts entire federations to the point where no one is safe from what I do. Main eventers, midcarders, curtain jerkers…no one is safe.” The Count seems to like this idea and is nodding his head as I continue my speech. “Everyone is too busy free floating through their federations like jellyfish in an ocean. They are complacent and any sort of change will throw their lives into a tailspin. That is the effect I want to create. I want them to be in a state of complete chaos. I want everyone to feel unsafe and be at their most vulnerable. I like chaos…I’m an Agent of Chaos…and everyone is going to be a victim.” “NICE,” the Count replies loudly with a smile on his face. “I love this idea. The fact that it’s starting on Christmas is only going to make it even better.” I get a chuckle over this response. He’s oblivious now to all the dressed up people walking around us or the beautiful surroundings. He’s thinking about the task at hand and that’s exactly what I want. “I looked at the whole situation like this. If I strip away all the fun comedy stuff and just leave what’s left, there’s going to be nothing a soulless, heartless, ruthless individual with no remorse toward his opponents and no remorse for anything that he does. That’s me right now. With you by my side, GCW will be cowering in fear of the Church of the Unholy.” This brings a smile to his face as I continue with what I was saying. “So after you get done here in Shitty Center,” I say to him before looking around at the crowds of people all around. “You come to my house and we figure out how we are going to inflict a lot of pain on our opponents.” "Alright, I'll see you then," he says. I then turn and leave from the bustling hotel lobby with the camera in tow. I'm looking forward to the strategy session. GCW will never see what we have in store coming. Chaos is will be the only thing left once we are finished with their pathetic organization. I can't wait for it all to go down. * * * An hour later… Driving up to my house in the darkness of the night I park the car in the usual spot. After parking in my drive-way, I see some guy in a bee constume buzzing around my garbage can. He’s either that Spanish costumed guy from the Simpsons or it’s one of the Hive. I close my car door and lock the car before walking over to the man who is inspecting my garbage. He looks likes he’s homeless and smells like it too. He might also be some drunk guy who works for a kids store or something. “What are you doing?” I ask him as he looks over in my direction. The idiot bee man ignores me. This irritates me greatly to the point where I want to inflict bodily harm upon this fucktwat. I clear my throat and repeat what I said. “What are you doing?” The man looks up and me and finally speaks up. “No habla ingles,” he responds. I glare at the man after he says this. Looking toward my house, I see a rake by the garage. Turning back to the man, I quickly look back at the rake. A very evil idea is going through my head. Should I break the rake over his back or should I let this bee costumed man live another day? As I ponder what to do, my front door suddenly opens and my buddy Rappin’ Rick Malloy comes running out screaming about something. “THE SPIDERBOTS ARE ATTACKING MY BRAIN!” he screams out. Both myself and the bumble bee man look on as Malloy, in a black suit and wearing sunglasses when it’s pitch black outside, Malloy runs through the grass before rolling around on the ground and clawing at his suit. “Dear lord,” I mutter out loud. The guy in the bee suit agrees with me. “Ay dos mio,” he says, shaking his head. At this point I’m about to flip out. The most likely scenario is that one of my idiot neighbors is going to call the cops because I have a drugged up idiot rolling around on my grass screaming about invisible creatures that are attacking him. The screaming is getting to me. I can’t take it any more! I start convulsing and trying to keep from going off the deep end. My hands start to clamor and shake as sweat drops down from my brow. Looking over at Malloy screaming and yelling, I lose it. Trying to keep from pulling my hair out, I then get filled with rage and run across the driveway before running through the grass and kicking Malloy right in his big fat fucking head! The sickening thud of my foot hitting his head echoes in the air as I look down at his prone, injured body as it lays in my yard. I’m hyperventilating and trying to keep from beating the crap out of this drug addicted piece of crap that’s laying in my yard. Hearing a noise, I look over and see the guy in the bumblebee costume staring at me. I snarl at him and he quickly scampers off. That loser is probably a spy for that new GCW stable the Hive. I’ll deal with them later. But my best move would be to get the body off my lawn and take it inside so that’s exactly what I do. I pick up Malloy off of the grass, put him on my shoulders, and carry him to the front door before going inside. * * * Two hours later… I hear a knock on the door and get up from my lounge chair to answer the door. Upon opening the front door, I see that it’s the Count. We shake hands and he comes inside the house before I lock the door behind us. Upon entering the living room, the first thing he notices is Rick Malloy’s unconscious body laying on my couch. Motioning toward him, the Count then looks in my direction. “Why the hell is Rick Malloy sleeping on your couch?” he asks, a bit puzzled. “Is he on drugs or something?” I shake my head in response. “No…and yes,” I reply. “How can it be both?” The Count asks. “The idiot was screaming on my lawn and rolling around yelling about invisible aliens. I couldn’t take it anymore so I kicked him in his head. He’s been on the couch ever since.” The Count, instead of being mad, smiles after hearing this. This pleases me to no end. Glad that one of my friends and allies is as heartless as I am. My agent William Smith was kicked in the head yesterday by me and all of my buddies freaked out. Now with this Malloy thing, a lot of them will probably have the same reaction. I could care less. “Now about Christmas Chaos,” the Count says. “What’s the plan?” “This is what we’re going to do,” I start telling him with a devious grin on my face. “We’re going to split up to find the Markus "The Molester" Stone: Creepy Christmas Edition Action Figure. I will also make a lot of fake alliances with people to try and help us win it. IF we control the American Dream, then we control GCW. I want these people to fear us. They don’t fear us yet, but they will…” This brings a devious smile to his face too. “Let’s do it,” he says. “We already have the REAL World’s Tag Team Championships and if one of us gets the American Dream, then we will hold GCW in a stranglehold that they will not be able to escape from. This is going to be our night. I have that feeling.” “I agree. And I hope that they award us with the actual GCW Tag Team Championship belts because if they don’t, we’ll just find System-X and beat them to death before taking them for ourselves. I don’t care if GCW management says that we’re not the champions. Not at all because we’re the uncrowned tag team champions and everybody knows it. Mortifera fears us, the Schizophrenic Demonoids want to be us, the Hive are irrelevant, and System-X is a worthless joke of a team. None of them can defeat us. We’ll crush their skulls…” Hearing a noise coming from the couch, I look over to see Malloy waking up as I finish my sentence. “…with our bare hands.” I then stroll over to the couch to greet Malloy as he sits up and rubs his eyes after taking off his sunglasses. The first thing he does after doing this is look up at me. The Count stays where he’s at so he can watch this. “What happened?” Malloy asks. I glare down at him with a look of disdain and disgust. This pile of crap is one of my best friends and that saddens me more than anything else. “You pile of shit,” I growl at him. “You were on another one of your little drug hallucination scream fests and were screaming on the lawn yelling about aliens.” Malloy looks really confused at this point. His beard is dirtier than usual and his nostrils have white powder around them. The more I look at this man, the more he disgusts me. “I was?” he asks as I nod my head. “Yes…” I respond with a snarl. My glare is cutting through him like a knife. “You were screaming like a little girl. The neighbors were going to call the cops and if the police came, what would they find in your possession? Drugs of all kinds because you’re nothing but a drug addicted scumbag. You’re the filth that is stinking up this society. Maybe that little pansy ass AXEL Action had it right the whole time with his straight edge lifestyle. You, Rick Malloy, ARE A DISGRACE! Now go get out of my sight and clean up before I kick your ass like I did earlier. NOW GO!” I motion for him to go clean up in the bathroom by pointing in the direction of the hallway that has the bedrooms and the bathroom in it. He looks at me and then looks where I’m pointing. He then gets up from the couch and runs in the direction of the bathroom. The Count laughs after hearing a door slam in the hallway. “You don’t put up with any crap, do you?” he asks as he continues to laugh about the whole thing as I shake my head. “Nope, not any more. I used to be a weak, pathetic leader that no one took seriously. That is why I would always fail. Now I don’t put up with any crap from anybody and I expect to see immediate results starting with Christmas Chaos. That last thing we need is a fucking drug addicted idiot distracting us from the task at hand. They will fear us soon enough. By the time Christmas Chaos is done, they’ll see just who is the dominant force in GCW. It’s not Murray or Caldera or Mortifera, it’s us. And we’re going to show them the true definition of the word chaos and then the Age of the Unholy will begin…” The Count smiles, nodding his head to show me that he’s in. I don’t know what to expect on this show, but if it’s anything like what just happened with Malloy, it’ll truly be Christmas Chaos.
