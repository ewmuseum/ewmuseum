Prologue to 666: The Story of Bryan Mayhem due out Jan 4, 2009

2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2008 As I sit down and begin to collaborate all my notes and scribbles into words, I have come to the conclusion that we lived in a world of disdain and evil. This giant beast of man you are about to read about and dissect, as you already know, is a celebrity. He is known in 6 of the 7 continents. His DVD’s have sold more copies than you can imagine. His merchandise is still one of the organization’s best sellers, even after 7 years. Yes despite all this, he’s a tortured soul. He’s a man from a past that most only see in horror movies. I did not realize what I was getting myself into, and to be honest, in the very beginning, I did not want to do this story. The Life of Bryan Mayhem? What kind of recognition would I get in the literary world writing a book about a 7 foot tall giant with a painted face. I could have sworn John Carpenter or Stephen King already conquered that avenue. I turned down my boss atleast 10 times until he told me to give it a week. “Just live with this man for one week, if you do not see why we MUST get this book done, we can scrap it all.” I believed that was a fair enough option. So I packed my bags and made my first stop in Columbus, Ohio. This is where Global Championship Wrestling(GCW) is located. I walked in, greeted kindly and told the receptionist about my meeting with billionaire and Forbes Magazine 22nd most powerful man in the world Steven Caldera. I was shocked to discover this meek and fairly attractive YOUNG man was the quarterback of this billion dollar company. We chit-chatted for a few minutes. After all he’s in Columbus and I am from Ann Arbor, so needless to say we didn’t see eye to eye about everything. Mr. Caldera sat there, arms folded and told me what he knew about my newest subject. There were points where I almost burst into laughter. During my bouts of disbelief and laughter Mr. Caldera would continue with his story. There were times where I wanted to pack my things and head back home and tell my publisher to blow it out of there rear end. After Mr. Caldera told me everything he knew I asked him to quit joking around and tell me the real story, not some storyline of a fictional character that was created for ratings and dollar signs. Staying stonelike he told me, and I will never forget this ever, “I wish everything I was saying was for the story only”.but?..what makes Bryan Mayhem all that more interesting is’everything?.and I mean everything about him and his story is real. The public knows it, we as an organization know, and most importantly so does he.” I left Mr. Caldera’s office confused and excited at the same time. Confused: How could a man this size go through the torture and abuse that you will read about and come out as a goddamn celebrity. Confused: This man is allowed to walk the earth freely? Excited: I finally have my own Da Vinci Code. Let me explain that last statement. Musicians call their greatest audio achievement “Our Sgt. Pepper’s” named after the great Beatles Album. Author’s call their greatest literary achievement their “Da Vinci Code” because it is so amazing and well written that you question it’s sincerity. Throughout the course of this book you will read about actions so disgusting and revolting that you find it hard to believe that the human soul can be capable of such atrocities. You will also read about events that make you wonder how this man is still alive and breathing today. Those of you that are parents will cry and often cringe at how parents can take their own flesh and blood and destroy their inner spirit. I have read through my notes and journals from the past year(looks like over 25,000)pages of notes and wonder how am I going to have the heart to write this, and will the reader believe me? Can I tell this story to make you know its real and not some novel. I have seen the scars, and tasted the tears, and I assure you, the reader, that what you are about to succumb yourself in is 100% without the shadow of a doubt REAL. About 3-4 months ago was when I realized I had absolutely NOTHING about his career as a celebrity and a professional wrestler. I even interviewed other wrestlers, and had to go back and ask about the wrestling because they all told me about Bryan O’Neil, not necessarily Bryan Mayhem. That’s when I came to a fork in the road. Do I write the biography of Bryan Mayhem, or do I write the biography of Bryan O’Neil. Sure they are one in the same, but the lives are almost schizophrenic, almost two extremely different people. I am not an advocate of Prologues, I just think they are the author’s way of filling up a page quota they must reach, and to give the reader another reason to find us strange. I never intended to do a prologue for this book. Why should I? It’s a biography, everything should be explained in its context, not prior. I wrote this portion because I knew nothing of this man, and had literally no time or space to fill in my visit to Columbus and my doubts about this person. I fear sending this into an editor, because I fear their reaction. I fear them telling me to make it sound more realistic, it sounds too Stephen Kingish. Please be advised reader, that what you are about to read is by far the most intense, most disturbing, and most difficult thing I have ever done. Please keep in mind every word I write, and I am writing with the fear that no one will believe this story, and in two weeks 666: The Story of Bryan Mayhem will be on the Fiction section of your local library, or on the bottom of your birdcage. Well reader, that’s enough of me being such a worried perfectionist. Sit back and enjoy this book. It contains a lot of blood and sweat, none of which is mine?. A.Nedved 2008
