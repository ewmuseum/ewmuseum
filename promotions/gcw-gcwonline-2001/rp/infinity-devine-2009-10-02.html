Infinity Devine

2 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/2 A new time begins, the grandfather clock strikes once....strikes twice.....strikes three times. It was 12:00 am and I couldn't get to sleep. The events that transpires before left me unsettled. I accomplished something on my own, I was there to win and I did it, the goals of my might and my strength knew no bounds. Yet, I felt uneasy, this passing moment was supposed to be filled with joy and happiness, but there was a hole, a hole left burning deep inside me. I could always and forever prepare for events like this, events that could very change the way I live my life, but first I needed to take the time to prepare for any consequences that would arise, I know it would happen, I knew that I would not be fully prepared for what may come, but what did come my way, I would find a way to get over it, get over that hump. I wanted nothing more to be happy, but there is a lot to sacrifice before that can happen. The dreams and wishes that I have been praying for every day and night in reality do not come true, I can keep telling myself that I will get what I want, but then I realize I am not in a story book, I can't write my own wishes to reality, it just wasn't going to happen. I needed something more, something more to fill this void, to fill this desire. I knew all this wasn't going to happen. But I have to try. * * * "Forget it, I don't want to do it." I had made up my mind. I wove off any suggestions that Chris had made. "But you don't understand, Lucas, it is in your contract, you have to defend that thing every week on television or they will strip you of that title." He shakes his head, he tries to reason with me but it will never happen. That strapped filled with gold lays upon my dinner table, the light gleams off it, I can see the reflection bouncing off the wall. "Doesn't matter to me, mate, this thing means nothing. I was literally given a title, I don't like it, but I don't feel I should go out there and defend it." I russle with my hair, I take the tip of my finger and run it through the belt. "You have chance to make something of yourself, so what if the match wasn't the way you wanted it to go, you have this now, albeit it is not the World Title, but you now have a change, Lucas, you have a chance to show off what real talent is all about. Baby steps, you need to use this opportunity to show everyone who you are, forget those matches you had before, they mean nothing to us now, you will become the greatest TV Champion ever." I did like the sound of that. I never forcasted such a reality, I always did what I wanted, I always got what I wanted. The time and care I put into things always were to pay off, but this, this is differemt this a whole new world I am stepping in. Championships mean nothing to me, not at all. What is meant to me is the each step I take as I climb up that ladder and ascend to the very top. I turn and look at Chris, his eyes meet my eyes, it was like one of those shitty romance novels, but we did not lock lips and do the nasty, no, we both had that look where you knew what eachother was thinking about. "We need to change your image..." Was the first thing out of his mouth. We weren't thinking the same thing. "What? What do you mean change my image?" I scratch my head. I get up from my chair and walk towards him. "You know what exactly I mean, I don't mean to be an asshole or anything, but you look kind of a spoiled brat. You have all this built up charisma, but you look like a tool." "How.....what.....I am not some Hollywood actor that needs his hand held every step of the way." I was getting angrier, one wrong word and I was going to severly beat him. "You might as well be, as I said before, you look like a tool, and nobody is going to cheer you on because of it, you are a nice guy, I can see that, but standing there looking like you came out of a men's swimwear catalog isn't going to help. You have this devine gift and it is a shame you won't use it because you have nobody at your corner. These last few months have changed you, and I have no idea what the hell to do about it, it's all up to you." * * * I took his words deeply into cosideration, it was a change for the best, right? I had to redirect myself into new horizons, teach myself a new way to live. If I didn't do these things, I would fall deep into the quicksand and have no hope of ever getting out. It was a fool's paradise, why do I have to go out of my way to make other people feel good, I wanted to worry about me and only me, yet I had this burning desire to break apart form my mold and do good. He leaves the room as if he is ashamed of me, ashamed as to what I was becoming. Through it all, through all my pain and suffering, I had no doubt that the words he said were right. The death of my family, the girl that once was mine, but now isn't, and a culmination of all my failures in life have molded me into some kind of person, I don't know who I am, I don't know where I am going, I would have liked to think that was up, but sadly, I do not know anymore. I want to believe I have it all, that I have the talents that I can share with others, the talents that I can prove to every single person that stepped in my way, they will realize I am infinite, I am devine.
