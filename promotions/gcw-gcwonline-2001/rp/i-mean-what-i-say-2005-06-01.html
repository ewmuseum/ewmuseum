I mean what I Say.

1 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/01/05 [ The scene opens up to an American Airlines 747 flying in the wide open air. We are taken inside the plane to see the people of coach trying to sleep and relax but can not do so due to the excess noise and bumpy ride. We are then taken to first class where we see the GCW’s new star Bryan Marshall. Marshall is eating peanuts drinking a diet coke. There is a camera man set up in the seat across from him. Marshall gets the crumbs off of his hands and looks to the cameraman.] Bryan Marshall: All my life I have heard I will never make it. All my life I have been told I was to small. I wasn’t strong enough. I wasn’t all there mentally. Well time and time again I have proven all of those doubters wrong. I have done more then anyone has ever expected of me. So when I come t the GCW and people don’t expect much of me it isn’t anything new to me. It Is something I assumed would happen. But just like all those times before, I will prove all of the doubters wrong. Which is why I plan on winning the battle royal at Worldwide! [ Marshall takes a sip of his diet coke and places it back down in the cup holder.] Bryan Marshall: The TV title is a great title. It is a stepping stone to bigger and better things in this great company of the GCW. Holding the Television champion alone is a hell of an accomplishment but if you have a long successful reign as TV champion then your future is set. The money comes rolling in and your title shots will never stop. When I was told I was going to be in this battle royal my jaw dropped. I didn’t expect such a great gift. I am very grateful. No one is going to take this opportunity of becoming TV champion away from me. I don’t care if I am stepping on anyone’s toes when I say this. I don’t know anyone around here and I don’t care to, all I care for is winning that TV championship! [ Marshall watches an attendant walk by. Marshall stares as at as she makes sure everyone is comfortable. She finally gets to Marshall.] Airline Attendant: Is their anything I could do to make you more comfortable? Bryan Marshall: Yes there is. I want you to come on camera right now and tell everyone that I am going to win the battle royal at worldwide. Airline Attendant: Excuse me? Bryan Marshall: DO IT! Airline Attendant: I have no idea what your talking about sir. But I would be happy to get you a pillow or something to drink. Another diet coke perhaps? Bryan Marshall: I DON’T WANT ANY DRINKS! A pillow!”! How do you expect me to sleep when I am so wound up and ready to begin my run as TV champion! What I want is that belt! What I want is to be recognized at the TV Champion of the GCW. And only then will feel comfortable. But not for long because I will not stop there! I will do whatever it takes to show the GCW that I am a force to be reckoned with. So far what I have learned from the GCW is that the people here are not all there. Which is great because it is the same for me. But there is a man I have heard from that seems to think winning the title isn’t going to be to much trouble. [ Marshall runs his fingers through his hair and sits on the edge of his seat while looking into the camera deeply.] Bryan Marshall: Ryan Stephens. No body cares about your personal life. So don’t bring it to the GCW. I talk because that’s the only thing I can do for now. That is until the battle royal on Thursday. You seem to think that I am just another newcomer who in a few short weeks will be out of here without ever being heard from again? Well your dead wrong my friend. I am so much more then that and you don’t even realize it. Everything I say I am going to do, I will. I don’t bring my promises. And I promise that you will be the one on the outside looking you. YOUR going to be thrown over the top rope by ME! When I do, your going to realize that you made a big mistake in questioning my ability. I have eaten my words before and I am not afraid to do it again. That’s why I make the bold statements I make. I know I can back them up. But can you? [ Bryan finishes the rest of his coke and tosses his can in the middle of the aisle.] Bryan Marshall: The GCW is full of talent. It is a whole new beginning for me. But all I heard is Ryan talking about what has happened in previous federations. All I hear is bullshit. Ryan, I don’t care what you did before because I wasn’t there. But I am here now and you have to deal with me! Your wrestling for all the wrong reasons. Not because you have the heart or desire of a true champion! Your doing it to get your kisser some publicity. Granted, you still may be very dangerous. But my heart and my desire will be able to get me a victory over you. Your proclaim yourself as the smartest man in wrestling. Well I proclaim myself the craziest man in wrestling. The difference is I mean it , and when it comes to wrestling I rather be crazy and willing to pull out all the stops then just book smart, which is all you are. Thursday is just a day away and I will prove to all you doubters just why I will become the TV CHAMPION! [ Marshall gets up and walks down the aisle where he is stopped by some attendants.]
