A blade and a voice

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: WW 118 Thunder rattles the joints of the house, sending creeks and brittle noises of snapping throughout the house. The rain sounds more like an angry hive of bees after a bear storm's their home for honey. The bee-like rain pummels the boards of the house and shingles of the roof, trying to gain access to the warmth within. The rain has a hushed sound against the family room with the plywood board covering the shattered front window. In front of the window lays Taylor reading "Paradise Lost" by Milton under lamplight. On the adjacent couch lays Christian, also reading, but he sheds a different light on the devil, reading "Good Omens" by Neil Gaiman with a sinister smile put on his face, casually looking over at Taylor at every page turn. The next thunder bolt crashes loud, shaking the foundation of the house and putting a small amount of spackle dust onto Taylor's book. He casually wipes it off and finishes the paragraph before getting to his feet and making his way to the refrigerator. The journey isn't a long one, but the walls feel elongated and the fridge feels like it is forever away. Another crash of thunder or two later and Taylor opens up the fridge door and reaches in for a bottle of Vitamin Water and closes the door, revealing Christian sitting on the counter in front of the kitchen bay windows overlooking the dark lake. Lightning strikes the lake off in the distance casting long, bright shadows around Christian... "You know, I've been thinking about this living arrangement and I think that we can consolidate..." His wry smile plagues his face while Taylor looks on in disgruntlement. "Yeah, I mean, we don't need so much junk around here, like, for instance, that table next to the door.." "It's for mail and the phone book..." "For the little mail you get and the phone you don't have?" Beginning to enjoy himself, he opens up the draw directly under him, the draw slightly going through his leg. Noticing this, he moves subtly. "What about all this shit?" He holds up a whisk, "is this really necessary?" Taylor lunges forward and pulls the whisk out of his hands and tosses it into the draw, sending a few items inside into the air and out of the drawer. "Now look what you did? Can't even keep the damn utensils in the draw! Come on numb nuts...oh, what do we have here?" As Taylor is quickly gathering everything from the ground, thunder cracks again and he slowly raises up to meet what Christian is holding in his hand; a potato peeler. Taylor eyes it up and down and then quickly looks up into Christian's eyes. "You know, Taylor, Father Emanuel would want you to do it. He always wanted you to help yourself instead of going for help. I mean, only he would send you out into the middle of the night to work out your issues on an innocent stranger..." Taylor looks at him without uttering a word, Christian is doing all the talking for him... "Come on, you know you should. Do as good Father Emanuel would want you to do..." There is a slight silence that is quickly broken by more thunder and more creaking of joints in the house. Taylor eyes the potato peeler and Christian slowly puts it into his hand, smiling uncontrollably like a child that deceived his friend into thinking something outrageous. Taylor looks at the rogue knife in his hand in a look of near disbelief. He remains mindless at every enunciation Christian makes. So...Christian keeps talking... "To think, all you need to do is cut through some flesh and you will get what you truly desire, but then again, won't things kinda be lonely without me? I mean, really? Do you want a party for two or a single for lunch? Huh? Huh?" Christian laughs, but Taylor puts the blade of the potato peeler to his arm. He presses it flat down, cutting into Taylor's outer arm. Christian silences himself but can hardly contain himself until he can no longer take the anticipation... "Go ahead..." Christian's voice grows dark and commanding, "Carve me out of your skin..." Taylor pulls back on the blade slightly as fresh blood pours from the knife. Tears already begin to flow from his eyes and he stops, but keeps a firm grip on the kitchen utensil. "Oh, come on. You need to do it faster and harder. I mean," Christian holds up a hand to his clean shaven face, "you may have shaved a couple hairs from my 5 o'clock shadow, but, yup, still fully in tact. I mean, why not just cut your arm off, that should do the trick!" Christian's laugh penetrates his ears like daggers flying through the air. Taylor begins to take quick, deep breaths. Every breath quiets Christian more and more and more. His heartbeat becomes the only thing still audible in the room. The little flow of blood out of his arm is oddly cooling and therapeutic. All until another crack of thunder breaks the meditation and Christian's laugh stabs at his heart. Tears wallop in his eyes before finally... "UUUUHHHHHHHHHH..." Saliva pushed between tightly clenched teeth with great force and yelps of pain mix in the evening air. Christian's laugh disappears with the next crack of thunder, leaving a bleeding, wounded Zacharia Taylor on his knees, peeler on the ground next to him. He crawls to the counter and grabs for his kitchen towel and presses it against the eight inch gash on his arm. Taylor falls backwards onto his back, taking in deep breaths and clenching his arm trying to suppress the pain. The towel quickly soaks through as he moves about on the ground. His grip never loosens to stunt the flow, and his eyes become dilated over the course of a couple minutes. He takes a couple deep breaths and steadies his heart. He resists the temptation of closing his eyes and blinks away the tiredness. Blinking to keep his eyes open. Blinking to keep his eyes from closing... It is nearly ten a.m. when Taylor makes his way out of the kitchen. His head throbs in the sinus canal, stinging his eyes with fatigue and weakness. He tries to blink away the invisible needles in his eyes and stumbles to the couch where a bag remains from the night before. He awkwardly pulls out a small medical kit from the gym bag and goes for the gauze and peroxide. The nightmare of what was the night before slowly fades away from memory and all that remains is the feeling of a drunken stupor from the night before. Taylor thinks to himself that he has never before been so wiped out of energy, not even after a PPV match against Garbage Bag Johnny. His legs are weak and awkward because of the way he passed out clenched up last night. His arm feels like precisely twenty-seven mice ran ramped on his arm and began to take chunks out of it, not to mention the crusted towel that was stuck to the wound when he woke up... Finally, Taylor makes it over to the kitchen sink and takes a deep breath before dumping a thimble amount of peroxide on his open wound. Nobody could possibly be ready for that kind of pain.... Taylor wretches and punches the counter with his good arm, splashing the peroxide across the counter. He grits his teeth and struggles to voice sounds of agony. Taylor has been hurt before, but he was always in the aid of Father Emanuel or a hospital. How would you explain a potato peeler wound to the RN? Then again, he got away with a blistering second degree burn across his whole chest from a bum's burning barrel... The pain from the peroxide was subsiding, and the foamy discharge was nearly gone, so Taylor took another deep breath and poured more on, but this time he expected the pain and was able to recover quicker. After several minutes of cleaning, Taylor was using a clean rag and wiping away at the wound's surroundings and dressing it with ointment and gauze before wrapping it several times in surgical tape. It still throbs, but it will do for now.... The once Hallmark kitchen now looks like a murder scene with blood splatters and dried puddles everywhere. The site of the kitchen can even make Kimbusa lose his appetite. Taylor decides to call a cab to his house and ride over to Fred's Diner, the hot spot in Salem. In what feels like hours, Taylor finally makes his way over to the diner and stands at the front. The diner is nothing special, but not something you would expect in plain Salem. The diner has been finished with mirrors and neons all over so it looks much more like a fun house than a place that serves breakfast and lunch all day. The diner looks more like a tourist attraction at night. Several people walk past Taylor and give him the ol' Salem nod of hello and keep walking into the diner or elsewhere on the central strip. Taylor double checks his bandage, which is under a loose workout jacket, and makes his way into the diner. Taylor looks around the diner. Quaint like he expected. It has its regulars and some friends and family for lunch, but Taylor barely recognizes anyone. He is seated in a booth next to the window, in the direct view of the front door. A waitress named Peggy comes over to the table and fills his empty cup of coffee with some really dark and mediocre temperature coffee that smells more like chocolate and hands him a menu, quickly walking with the coffee urn to another table. Taylor barely looks at it. The smell from outside has filled his nose and he cannot but help think Taylor ham and whites with wheat toast on the side. Peggy comes back. Peggy is a very special girl in the fact that she holds herself like someone that knows what they are doing, and someone that doesn't care if she is under the inquisitory eye of every customer that she comes in contact with. She looks like she can handle herself at night if she needed to. Doubtful if she is a stranger to dirty old men grabbing her ass, or even the persecution by her parents for wanting to wait tables the rest of her life. But who knows, I'm only the invisible eye watching the scene unfold. She stops and smiles at Taylor, her confidence pouring out of her eyes when she stares at him. Taylor wonders if she looks at every customer this way or if it is because she's never seen him before... "Well, nah, I don't think I seen you 'round here before! Nah, no way! Ya look like one of does guys dat dish out a regular beatin' here n nah. What's your name sugar?" Taylor stares at her like he would any woman that is coming on to him. He can't help but feel like he is staring at someone like Tessa Windsor or Vivica J. She has a confidence in her and a spunk like someone from wrestling. "Should I wear a name tag like you do and question everyone I come in contact with or should I say my name?" Peggy smiles, getting at what he means. Introductions first, conversation later. She's quick, and witty all in the same... "Well, sur, I'd say my name's Peggy, but what would ya say yours is?" "Zacharia." "Well, Zacharia," she blinks her eyes in seduction, and Taylor almost buys into it, but the constant reminder that his arm is gashed open plays with the strings behind his eyes. "Where you from? You don't talk like no person round here..." "Cliffton. I wouldn't expect you to know where that..." "Near Montclair..." Peggy laughs sweetly. "Went there for some schoolin'. Tell me, Zacharia, what would a strappin man like yaself want for breakfast?" "Taylor ham and whites with a side of dry wheat, if you could." "Taylor ham n' whites with dry wheat? Ya got it sweetheart..." Peggy walks away as Taylor attention is caught at the front door. Four people walk in, around the age of college students, but the one guy with them looks oddly familiar. Taylor stares right at the guy as he walks down the isle, not paying any attention to Taylor, but sits only three booths away. Taylor gets a good look at him before he sits down. The guy is at around 5'9. And stocky. He is in really good shape actually. His arm makes the short sleeves fit pretty tight and his back shows ripples of muscle though the shirt. Maybe from Bagwell's gym? The guy takes off his Gaspari Nutrition hat and puts it down on the booth behind him, messing up his dark brown hair. Taylor takes a sip from his room temperature coffee and looks over at the guy again, who is now looking at Taylor, but not in a "I think I know that guy look" but in a much more "He doesn't fit in here" look. This guy was definitely not from around here by the way he dresses, but from where? The guy in the booth hits the girl next to him in the arm and points over in Taylor's way when Peggy comes sliding in from nowhere and slides a white plate with blue and red checkers in front of him. The egg whites are mounding on the plate with three thinly sliced pieces of pork role still sizzling from the grill. Taylor nods in appreciation and looks back over at the booth, but the table no longer pays any attention to him. Taylor closes his eyes with hands folded and says a short prayer to himself and reaches over for the ketchup. Taylor pours the ketchup over the eggs. With hunger holding at his tongue, he dives right into the eggs. After the satisfaction tames the hunger, he reaches for his knife and begins to cut up the pork roll. Taylor reaches for more ketchup and smothers the meat. For an odd second, Taylor looses his appetite. The mangled meat and diced eggs covered in ketchup sends chills down his back. He struggles to come to words, but then just reaches towards his arm. He attempts to settle his stomach and then takes a deep breath and continues to eat... He no longer feels the satisfaction of his food hit his taste buds. Peggy comes back with a check in her hand once Taylor is finished eating, only leaving a few burnt pieces of pork roll left on the plate with a dollop of ketchup. Taylor pulls out two fives and goes to give the bill to Peggy but she's already gone. Taylor looks up and sees someone go up to the register with a bill like his own and pays, so Taylor throws a five on the table and takes the other five and the bill and gets up. As Taylor approaches the table where the guy is sitting, a knife slides off and the table laughs as the guy bends over to pick it up and Taylor hits him. "Sorry, man. I didn't see you." "Nah, it's ok. It's cool..." They both stare at one another for a moment and the guy reaches up and pats Taylor on the back and sits back on the booth. Taylor turns around after taking a couple steps and sees the guy looking at him in the mirror behind his friend's head in the booth. The guy's eyes squint and he jumps up from the booth, over to the register where Taylor is now paying. "Hey, hey man, wait up. Are you?" Taylor stands there, change in hand at the door by the time he catches up to him. "Am I who?" Taylor doesn't want the attention to draw to him, so he heads outside, leaving the guy in surprise behind him, but he follows him outside. "Taylor. You're Zacharia Taylor." The guy isn't acting like a fan, not with his tone of voice. Just, who is this guy then? Taylor feels himself getting tense, but the other guy shows no sign of violence. "You are then, aren't you? Shit, I thought so. Don't you remember me? It's been years, but we trained together three different times in..." "Bagwell's gym. I thought you looked familiar. What the hell you doing down here?" "I'm just visiting a friend that lives down the road. What the hell are you doing down here? Much different than Cliffton!" Taylor pushes his toes into the ground and tries to get an answer from the asphalt, but nothing comes to him. "You don't have to answer, its cool. Shit, I can't believe that you are here man. You are the last guy I expected to run into. It's a small world man." "Yeah, no kidding." Taylor feels his arm becoming wet and looks down at the jacket, blood now surfacing. "Damn, you better get that taken care of. I'll see you soon man, my breakfast is getting cold anyway. Take care of yourself Zach!" And with that, the guy runs off into the diner and Taylor's heart begins to settle as the visitor leaves. He pats his arm a little and feels the wound becoming more and more wet. Taylor struggles with his thoughts. Who is he? When did I train with him? What does he mean, see you soon? Years ago? Taylor gets over to the pay phone and uses the change he has from the diner to call for another cab... See you soon?
