Building The Wall, Brick By Brick

13 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/13/10 I. His senses are in a heightened state. Adrenaline pumps through his body, fueling him the way gasoline does to automobiles. He can hear and feel his heart thumping inside of his chest. Everything around him seems to move in slow motion, from the heaving of his chest to the beads of sweat dripping down his face. He cannot help himself, he grins from ear to ear. “You’re ready?” “Let’s find out, Boss.” Roberto’s grin fades and he nods to the man standing across from him in the ring. Dominic Wall, the GCW rookie, cracks his neck and rolls his shoulders. His heart is pounding inside of his chest as well but for different reasons than Roberto’s. Roberto hung up the LeStatt Knight persona months ago now, he is no longer fighting for pride and for a legacy. Stepping through the ropes for training sessions with Dominic are all he has to hold onto these days, this is his weekly WorldWide show. For Dominic, he is anxious to show a legend that he is ready to perform. Failure is not an option. Disappointing the man who formerly went by LeStatt Knight would be massive failure. So, like Apollo Creed before the sparring match against Rocky Balboa at the end of Rocky III, Roberto Abitua yells out two words. “Ding, Ding.” Roberto is the first to make any sort of move as he begins circling the ring, looking for an opening to attack. Dominic steps to the center of the ring but rather than circle around as well, he merely shifts his body so that he is constantly facing the former GCW World Champion. It has been more than three months since Dominic first caught the attention of Roberto Abitua inside of Knight Time Gym. On that day, Roberto was coming in for his first post retirement workout. The moment he stepped through the doors he could just FEEL that something was different. After years of being a dominant force, feared and admired, he then felt like nothing. To Roberto, he was a King without a Kingdom. And that is no King at all. “If you see any opening,” Roberto slips in, quick striking like a snake and catching Dominic with a right fist to his ribs before bouncing back outside of Dominic’s reach. “You have to take it. Hesitation can cost you.” Dominic tries to conceal the grimace on his face from the blow but is unsuccessful. He shakes his head, loosens up and tries to focus harder. All through Dominic’s life he has been able to overcome obstacles and odds stacked against him. He has come to never expect anything to come easily and this mentality has formed him into a very driven man. He makes mistakes, everyone does, but he never allows the same mistake to happen twice. He evolves, he adapts, it is one of the main things that Roberto loves about training him. Roberto slips in again and tries to perform the same move again but this time Dominic swipes down with his hand and swats Roberto’s fist away and with a very quick movement, Dominic shifts his weight, lowers his hips and brings his right leg across, connecting with Roberto’s left leg, right at the knee. The strike causes Roberto’s leg to buckle, sending him to one knee. Wall throws his left leg forward for a straight kick to his downed mentor but Roberto catches his foot and stands up in the process. Options fly through Dominic’s head. “Not fast enough.” Roberto speaks out before spinning and sweeping Dominic’s other leg from underneath him causing the young man flat on his back. Roberto takes his foot and lays it flat across Dominic’s throat. His puts enough pressure for Dominic to feel it but not enough to cut off the flow of oxygen completely. “You have to use your instincts. They can make or break you.” Roberto takes his foot off of Dominic’s throat and backs up to allow him back to his feet. “You cannot, for a moment, second guess what you are-” Roberto is quieted when Wall shoots in with a double leg takedown, bringing the unsuspecting GCW Hall of Famer to the mat. Roberto immediately begins to scramble and Dominic goes after him. Roberto uses his significant size and strength advantage and continues to keep Dominic away from him and with one powerful push of his legs, Dominic is tossed back far enough that it gives Roberto enough time to get back up to his feet. Wall is relentless however and goes right after him, pegging Roberto with a quick flurry of punches as he stands, forcing him to the ropes. Wall latches onto Roberto’s right wrist and presses his body up against Roberto’s to gain momentum to whip him off the ropes. Again the size advantage comes into play and Roberto reverses the move, sending Dominic across the ring where he bounces off the far ropes and sprints back. There is no hesitation this time. No fear either. Dominic leaps into the air, gaining control of Roberto’s neck with his right arm and spinning him around with something resembling a tornado DDT but instead of coming down and driving Roberto’s head into the mat, he holds on to lock in his guillotine choke, The Last Breath while still suspended in midair. “It’s OVER!” Dominic growls out as he sinks in the hold tighter. Immediately Roberto feels the oxygen being cut off from his lungs. He feels his head becoming hot and his body becoming weak. The King without a Kingdom was falling further, allowing the kid that he is training to best him so quickly after learning the ropes of the sport that he has loved for over ten years. There is a grand mix of embarrassment, resentment and foolishness swirling around in his head. Roberto falls to one knee and fading fast. “Just tap, Boss.” A grin spreads across Dominic’s face. “I won’t tell anyone.” Roberto can’t imagine that he’s become soft. He is too proud to admit to himself that maybe the edge is gone. What he does know is that the air is almost completely gone from his lungs and that soon, he’d be passed out in the center of the ring. Beaten. By a move straight from Mixed Martial Arts. Mixed Martial Arts … What? A surge of power zaps Roberto back to life. He wraps his arms around Dominic and with everything he has left he pushes off his knee, standing straight up and bringing Dominic up with him. The grin is removed from Dominic’s face, replaced by a look of shock. Roberto roars like a lion before going off his feet and driving Dominic’s back damn near through the mat. The World turns upside down for Dominic Wall. One thing is for sure. He still has a lot to learn. II. “How is that a common occupational hazard?” “It wasn’t that bad.” “He tried to set me on FIRE. How is that NOT bad?” The car ride back to the hotel had been fairly uneventful and quiet until Roberto asked for more details on Dominic’s first night at a GCW event. Dominic didn’t waste any time with talks about his interview with Barry Goldstein or the matches he was able to watch from the locker room. The topic of conversation immediately leaped to David Spencer and what happened as Dominic was leaving. “Things could’ve been worse. He could’ve actually set you on fire.” Roberto says with a chuckle. “I don’t get how you can be so ….” Dominic’s voice trails off as video footage of a couple of big LeStatt Knight matches that he has watched flood back into his mind. He shakes his head and laughs, wondering if Jason Dread sounded the same after Dangerous Games in 2005. He considered what Bryan Mayhem sounded like after being hit in the head with a flaming chair as well but knows better. He doesn’t want to have nightmares. “Alright … I DO know how you can be so nonchalant about this shit.” Dominic adjusts in his seat. “But what do I do now?” “Your mind shouldn’t be on Spencer right now.” “Then where should it be?” Roberto gives a quick glance over at Dominic before returning his eyes to the road in front of him. Dominic recognized that look. The way his forehead wrinkled up, one eyebrow lifted and the slight frown … Roberto was either disappointed, worried or both. Roberto sighs while shaking his head. “Spencer is secondary right now, Dominic. Your main concern is Rediker. That is who you are stepping into the ring with.” “So I just let this asshole slide for what he did?” “Fuck no.” Frustration begins to settle inside of Dominic. “Then what?” “You focus on Rediker. You win, you destroy him. When you are finished with that THEN you go and hunt down David Spencer. No, you cannot let people get away with thinking that they can just push you around. You’ll never gain respect that way but you cannot go into a match with your focus divided. You are going to lose if you do. First impressions are everything so don’t fuck this one up, understand?” “Yeah. I got it.” The rest of the ride was quiet. Dominic just stared out of the window and into the night desert sky. He felt Roberto’s eyes on him from time to time but he needed to be left alone with his thoughts. He gets frustrated sometimes, not because of Roberto’s tactics but because he feels he should know everything already. He doesn’t. It’s a work in progress. III. “Night-time in the Army is a lonely time.” The repeating of the famous (one of many) Forrest Gump line puts a momentary grin on Dominic’s face but it melts away just as quickly as it had appeared. Night-time hasn’t just been a lonely time for him during his days in the United States Army but rather anywhere he is. Lying in his bed and burning a hole in the ceiling of his hotel room with his eyes is lonely too. His mind simply races too fast. His brain doesn’t shut down like it should so without the help of pills or medicine or alcohol, Dominic doesn’t sleep much. Another problem is that he is afraid to use the medication or pills or alcohol for sleep … he is terrified of becoming addicted. He has seen what that can do to people. This problem is why he has so enjoyed his time with Roberto and training so vigorously. His mind has to work overtime to learn what needs to be learned. Roberto tosses so much knowledge at him that he doesn’t have to shutdown and he doesn’t have to feel the loneliness that fills him up. It scares him sometimes, that feeling of being the only one around who cares. There are times when he feels like he is swimming in the Ocean, no land in sight and he doesn’t have a life jacket. He feels like he is going to be engulfed and drown. Roberto has helped. At least it feels like he now has an inflatable flotation device to help him along. A Yellow one. Shaped like a duck. He grins again. At least his World seems to be coming into focus again, something he hasn’t felt for over five years. High school graduation, Basic Training, AIT, Iraq, Afghanistan … It has all been some jumbled mess. People he met while in the Army, people whom he still calls good friends don’t even have faces right now. He tries to remember them but there is just something about the night that clouds everything. Roberto has helped bring life back into a grey and blurry world. Now Dominic has meaning and purpose, he has goals and ambition. He has no idea if he will ever make it out of the roaring waters of the Ocean. He may never feel the touch of land again. But … For the first time in a long time, he has hope. He feels like he is swimming in the right direction.
