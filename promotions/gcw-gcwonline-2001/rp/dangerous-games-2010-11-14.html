Dangerous Games

14 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/14/10 “I didn't know what else to do...” Just by listening to her voice you could tell she was scared, and as she leaned against me I knew she was hoping for some form of comfort. I kept my arms around her, but my real interest was the groaning crucified body of Ben as he remained hanging down from the ring ropes. “So what shall we do with him?” My question is more of a formality as I would much prefer to leave him there as an artistic picture that fulfills my sadistic dreams. However I know the more logical thing to do would be to dispose of him before the police returned to sweep the crime scene over one final time before allowing it to be cleaned. Katrina however startles me with an unexpected question. “A few years ago I remember hearing about a kid that was murdered out in an abandoned building in Death Valley. Was... Was that you?” I wasn't expecting her to ask me such a question, but after the devotion she has created for me, I know there was no need for hesitation. My face shows a sign of emotion as a smirk crosses over it while I nod to her. As I look back towards Ben I notice he is slowly starting to regain consciousness more and more as each minute passes. His body has been brutalized so much from Katrina's assault that I know he poses no threat even if he fully awakens. “Yes.” “Then we should take him there.” That is exactly the answer I was wanting to hear. I watch as she parts from my not so comforting embrace and makes her way out of the back room. For a few more moments I stare at Ben letting my eyes take a permanent picture of the wonderful display she made for me. I hear the engine to my car starting up outside the gym as I peel the bloodied Ben off from the ring apron and toss him across my left shoulder. As we walk towards the door that Katrina holds open his wounds leave a dripping trail of blood behind us. “D..eat...h... Va...l.l...ey....” Ben's voice is barely audible from the damage he's received. Katrina opens up the trunk to the car and I toss him inside, he slowly reaches for the edge which prompts me to punch him in the head a couple of times. I slam the trunk shut and make my way to the driver's side of the car as I notice Katrina is wiping some of the blood off the side of her face. As I get into the car she leans towards me and gives me a kiss on the cheek before I close the door and start to drive off. “So what did the kid do?” As she asks me that question I'm not sure which confuses me more; the fact that she wants to know why I did it or how she managed to find out about it. We finally get out onto the main highway and the traffic seems to be flowing at a nice pace. Her stare burns deep into me and it let's me know it will continue until I give her the answers she lusts for. “When you left me, it felt like my entire life was coming to an end. I had nothing left at all and deep down inside I knew there was no reason for me to continue living. So I went into the bedroom and I sliced both of my wrists, so that all of my suffering could come to an end. My blood was spilling out fast and I was fading even faster, but instead of seeing all of my fondest memories flashing before my eyes; I saw the worst.” Her eyes are as wide and bright as a kid in a candy store as I continue to tell her the story. “Just when it felt like it was all about to come to a closing I was offered a deal. The Dark Lord was impressed with my rage, and told me that he could rid me of my suffering and promise me the chance to create a world I could rule. I could become everything everyone told me I would never be, and all I had to do was deliver to him the souls he wanted for his eternal army. He showed me the mercy God never will show, and for that he had my loyalty.” The moonlight shined brightly as we drove closer to Death Valley. I noticed Katrina roll the windows up on the car due to the chill of the wind blowing in from the incoming desert. Not many cars pass us by, which is good since Ben has to be getting hot inside the trunk. “So the boy was nothing more than payment. I had a change of heart down the road though, and it almost felt like God had forgiven me for all of my sins. He brought me back from the darkness and showed me a second chance at life, so I decided to give it a chance. I got back in GCW and decided I was going to try and do things right. But I quickly learned that the world would never change. People like SurReal... Bishop Steele... Tempest... They were all still puppets waiting for their strings to be pulled! It took you walking back into my life to see all of the lies and deception, and then I knew what had to be done. In order for me to succeed I needed his help once again, and with you at my side I would be unstoppable. Larry had to be the sacrifice in order for him to see that I was still loyal to him.” “Now it would seem we're both loyal to him.” She smiles to me as I put my arm around her. Our drive shouldn't last much longer seeing as we just left the city limits. - Noise clutters the air making it hard to even hear your own thoughts. Bodies are everywhere, police officers, criminals, even local people trying to claim stuff that isn't theirs from the lost and found. Detective Jordan sits at his desk watching the madness outside his office, another normal day for the LAPD. The phone on his desk rings but he lets it continue on for a moment before he decides to answer it, with a rolling of his eyes as he notices the caller on the caller ID. “Look I have told you the same thing every single time you have called! WE HAVE NO LEADS IN THE SNIZTZ'S MURDER!” He slams the phone down in anger and with a little disappointment as well. The door to his office opens and a gorgeous woman walks in. Her flowing red locks cover a portion of her face, as the red dress she is wearing fits her nicely; showing off her curvacious frame. Detective Jordan is a little confused as he wonders why such a beautiful woman would be in his office, as she walks towards his desk. “Detective Jordan, my name is Robyn Fox. My husband Ben never came home last night and I'm really worried something may have happened to him. This isn't like him at all; he's very proud of his family and he would never let a night pass without telling his daughter good night.” Detective Jordan rubs his chin for a moment after hearing what Mrs. Fox had to say. “Maybe he stayed at the gym to finish packing things up and just forgot to call you?” “No. Like I said he would never do something like that. I'm just really worried something could of happened to him since this killer has yet to be found.” Her voice is real shaky and Detective Jordan knows there is only one way to calm her worries. “Okay well I need to go on over to the gym and cut off the police tape anyhow. How about you ride with me and we can both yell at Ben for not calling you when we see him there. The drive only takes about twenty minutes from here anyway.” Detective Jordan escorts her from his office and out of the police station towards his car. The two of them get inside and begin to drive off. “Mind if I ask you a couple of questions while we we're on the way? Maybe it can lead to some new leads in the investigation.” “Well before we decided to start a family I was a prosecution lawyer. So naturally the job creates a lot of enemies, but I don't think anything would happen to Ben over it. Ben is an overall nice guy and easy to get along with I can't really see anyone having a problem with him.” She seems confident in her replies which makes Detective Jordan believe that she's telling the truth. It doesn't take long before they pull into the parking lot of the gym. The two of them get out of the car and Robyn watches on as the Detective removes the police tape from the front door. They walk inside the building and call out to see if anyone is inside, to no reply. He leads Robyn into the office and pulls her out a chair to sit in. “Wait here I'm going to go look around and see if I can find Ben.” Jordan walks out of the ring and looks around the other nearby offices to make sure Ben isn't just sleeping in one. After no sign of him he walks off towards the ring area where he notices that someone has been here very recently. His eyes survey the area, noticing the blood on the ring ropes and the trail of blood leading towards the back room. He reaches into his pocket for his cell phone as he continues to survey the area. “Robyn stay in the office!” “Yeah this is Detective Jordan I need you to send some officers down to the Total Package Gym right now. Someone has been in here and there is new evidence, possible a second murder, get some people here now!” “Oh my God... Detective... DETECTIVE! You need to get in here now!” Just as he hangs up his call to the station he hears Robyn calling for him and he races towards her. As he dashes into the office he notices her standing in front of a small television, her hands over her face as she cries. He looks back towards the screen and all he sees is a black video with no real imaging on it. “What's wrong?” “It's Ben... He's... On the video, you can hear him...” Jordan takes a second look at the screen as Robyn rewinds the security video tape. Again nothing can be seen on the screen, but at the end of the video something is barely audible. He edges closer to the screen to try and hear it better as the video is played back one more time. “Does that say Death Valley? It sure does sound like Ben was trying his best to tell us where he was being taken. Okay we're going to form up a team and get right down to find Ben, we'll find him Mrs. Fox I promise!” - A car can be seen pulling alongside an old weather-beaten house. The side doors open and out steps Donny and Katrina, he motions for her to head towards the door as he makes his way towards the trunk. As he opens it he notices Larry is barely alive from the lack of oxygen during the long ride. Donny lifts Ben out of the trunk and over onto his shoulder as he walks towards the house. “Just open it. It's never locked since no one ever comes way out here.” Katrina opens the door for Donny to walk inside with Ben. The floor creaks as you walk across it and as the morning sun beams inside you can see dust in the air. He leads Katrina towards a back room as he uses his free hand to find the keys inside his coat pockets and then hands them to her. She opens the door and the two of them walk inside. “Welcome to my home away from home. Lock it back, I don't want anyone else in here.” Nothing is to special of the room, nothing really different from the previous rooms except for the set of stairs leading down. Together they walk down and past the open cellar door where a room made of cement and bricks is seen. In the center is a steel table with straps attached to the side, and that's where he lays Ben down. He proceeds to place the restraints on his limbs and the final one across his head, and once he's done he goes back to close the cellar door while Katrina looks on. “This is where all of the magic happens baby. This is where it all began, and tonight it will all continue.” Back towards Ben he walks and he slaps him across the face a couple of times to wake him up fully. He struggles slightly, but he is far to weak and the restraints are to strong. Katrina pulls a wooden chair next to him for Donny to take a seat on and she proceeds to sit on his lap. As if she kind of felt sorry for Ben, she passes her fingers through his hair as Donny starts talking to him. “I remember when I came back to Larry's gym and everyone was mocking the man named Donny Diamond. “How can someone be afraid of someone with a feminine name like Diamond?” or, “Look at the big bad emo that thinks he can actually get back into the game again”. Yeah Ben, I remember all of the jokes and I remember seeing you in the locker rooms laughing alongside them.” He pats Ben on the arm as he shoots him a smile. “But it's okay man, I'm not the type of guy to hold a grudge or anything. I can understand completely how you could miss the concept of the name Diamond being a little disturbing, not everyone gets it and I'm okay with that. The problem really comes when people forget that a Diamond is able to cut through anything, it can withstand tremendous pressure and just when you think it can't take anymore, it turns into something beautiful. And that is why I kept it in my ring name, because just like a Diamond I am capable of cutting my opponents down to their very soul.” He passes his hands along side Ben where some large needles rest on the table. Slightly he scratches it across Ben's skin which causes him to panic. “Training with you and Larry was fun at first, it really was, but when my lovely came back into my life I knew it was time to play a new game. I needed to find someone who could play the game with me, but its so hard to find that type of person; because this game is very dangerous. No game is more entertaining than tracking down someone and watching them every single second as you take their life. It's intoxicating! But I didn't know she would pick you Ben, just bad luck I guess buddy. I tell you though; I didn't really know if she would play the game, not until she answered the phone the next morning. Well Ben, it has been fun, but now I think it's time for us to play the game so we can be blessed by him once again.” Donny lets Katrina slide from his lap as he stands up and over Larry with the needle in his hand. He slowly moves it around in the air as if he was trying to amuse Ben, and slowly he aims it towards the bottom of his right eye. Just as the needle begins to pierce the skin of his eyelid he stops, retracts it and looks towards Katrina. “How rude of me... Would you like to do the honors my love? Now remember, he will scream from the pain, but think of it as your favorite musical playing on the radio”
