The Face of Face

10 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/10/2008 Continued from “What Goes Through His Mind” The sky seems to get darker as soon as Jeremy sees Face step out from that old abandoned barn. He begins to wonder why he bothered coming all the way out into the middle of nowhere to get an interview with this guy. Of course, Jeremy then remembers the money and exposure he’ll get interviewing a guy signed to the best wrestling promotion out there, no matter how insignificant he may seem now. So, Jeremy sucks up his feelings of regret and starts to head towards Face, who is ever-so-slowly stepping forward as well, letting the reporter do most of the work. Flick. Fwoosh. Crack. These sounds repeat every few moments as Face flicks his lighter on and off like clockwork. Jeremy seems to be distracted by this at first, but he wisely decides it wouldn’t be in his best interest to tell Face to stop. So, Jeremy swallows his opinion and, like a good reporter, gets right to the subject at hand. Jeremy: So, David- Jeremy doesn’t even make it to his first question before Face interrupts ? his raspy, nails-on-a-chalkboard voice almost causing Jeremy to cringe. Face: You know, Jeremy...there’s a reason people elect to call me Face all the time instead of David Spencer...would you like to know why? Jeremy goes to speak, but Face once again cuts him off. Face: Of course you would, Jeremy. Stories are strings that control all those puppets we call reporters. It’s the very reason you’re here, in the middle of nowhere, talking to a man behind a mask who has only just begun to start his televised career...is it not? Hmmm? Jeremy tries to answer, but Face intervenes once more. Face: It was a rhetorical question, Jeremy. I know it’s hard for you ? being in the bottom rung of a career path that is essentially full of bottom-feeding, sleazy people that might as well be leeches trying to suck little pieces of crap off the bottom of society’s shoes in the hopes that there is some juicy blood within. Jeremy just looks blankly at Face for a few moments. He’s only been talking ? or, rather, listening ? to Face for a few minutes, but he already feels like punching him in the face. Of course, he doesn’t. Face just observes Jeremy with his seemingly sightless eyes; the only sounds to be heard are the sounds of Face’s lighter, which continues to be flicked on and off without a single beat missed. Face: I bet you feel like punching me in the face after what I just said...hmmm? Jeremy doesn’t confirm or deny that statement, but Face clearly sees through this attempt at secrecy, letting out a short, horrible cackle before he continues. Face: Of course you won’t, Jeremy...You wouldn’t dare put a finger on me...and I know why. You wouldn’t openly admit the reason because of the inherent foolish pride that comes with being a human these days ? but there is no denying the reason is there. Yes, even now the stench of it is in the air around you. Jeremy flinches backwards, bringing his hands up slightly in defence as Face takes in a sudden breath of air, as if to draw the scent in. Face lets out a low chuckle. Face: Fear, Jeremy...Fear is what keeps you from punching me in the face as I insult you. Fear is what makes you flinch-Face suddenly juts forward a couple inches, causing Jeremy to flinch once more-every time I move an inch in your direction. And that brings me back to why people choose to call me Face instead of David Spencer... I am not your typical person, as I’m sure even you can see. I do not follow the same rules; the same values; the same morals as the rest of society. So, a normal name like David Spencer just never did me justice... Face shakes his head from side to side and begins to pace around. Face: No...David Spencer wouldn’t do. So, they looked for a name to call me by...and as soon as they looked at me, they knew what it would be... Jeremy waits with anticipation, despite the obvious answer that will come. Face stops pacing and turns back towards Jeremy. Face: Face...a name that is short and abrupt, yet so full of meaning ? and, of course, irony. You see, Jeremy...no one has ever peered below this mask I wear. So, in effect, no one knows what my face looks like; no one knows my identity. But, you see, Jeremy ? that’s just the thing with me: My entire image ? all that you see before you ? is a statement of my identity...Therefore, my face, if you will, is simply me in my entirety...and I am, in turn, Face... Face tilts his head slightly to one side, observing Jeremy as he absorbs all that. Face: Of course, my name is just one of the many things you’ll come to know and understand about me as time passes by. You still have only a taste of what will come from me. You still can only dream and fathom of what lies ahead for those I encounter. Face raises a hand, peering into it as if he holds those dreams within his very palm. He suddenly clenches it, however, and turns back to Jeremy. Face: And that’s the beauty of it all, Jeremy. That’s where the fun is for me...because people normally have their limits... People have a fixed line that they do not cross and, in turn, another fixed line they don’t even think of crossing. But, as you shall soon find out, I don’t have those lines holding me back. I don’t have limits. Where most men and women would stop and be satisfied with the damage they’ve done...I’ll just be starting. And this, Jeremy, more than anything, is what induces the fear in you. And soon, it will also induce fear in all of those who come in contact with me... A sudden feeling of dread sends shivers down Jeremy’s spine as Face stops talking, choosing to stare directly into Jeremy’s eyes instead. Silence fills the air once again as Face is no longer speaking and Jeremy doesn’t dare start. Jeremy is visibly uncomfortable now as this period of silence draws attention to that permanent, twisted, fiery grin on Face’s mask. Flick. Fwoosh. Crack. Face: Mmmmm, yes...now you begin to see what you’ve gotten yourself into, Jeremy. But, don’t worry; I won’t do anything to you this night. Sure, I could easily leave you beaten and bloody in the middle of nowhere without anyone being the wiser... Jeremy begins to back up slightly as Face looks down to his fist that doesn’t have a lighter in it, clenching it and unclenching it as he does. However, as soon as Face looks back up, Jeremy stops dead in his tracks. Face once again lets out a low, raspy laugh. Face: But, I won’t, as fun as it would be. I’ve got to let you publish this to the masses, after all. I have to let you serve notice to what is about to come... So, Jeremy, I’ll let you run along now. I’ll let you get in that car of yours and drive off. And, in turn, I expect you to let everyone know that, from now on, business in GCW will be far from usual. Jeremy takes his cue and nods, not even bothering to say anything in response as he turns around and begins to walk back to his car. However, he soon speeds up as a blood-curling laugh begins to pick up from behind him. Jeremy reaches his car and quickly turns it on. His tires squeal as he turns around and drives back to where he came from, never wanting to experience what he just went through ever again...
