The Next Step: Part II

18 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/18/11 Ever since Aaron had returned to school in search of his Master’s degree, a million things raced through his mind. Gone were the days of simply being a student. Now he was representing Northern Illinois University as a graduate student and a Teaching Assistant. The thought had crossed his mind about having the red head he was so attracted to in one of his classes, but thought he had avoided the awkwardness. Alas it wasn’t to be. After tagging along with Aaron to go apartment hunting, Chloe insisted on treating him to dinner to catch up on the six months since their storm chase trip ended. That night was uncomfortable to Aaron and today was too. It was his first class as a Teaching Assistant. Thursday 2:30 P.M. “Looks like it’s about that time.” The words had barely escaped Aaron’s mouth before the fifteen or so students in his first lab period stood up and began gathering their things. For most this was their last class of the day and all that stood ahead of them was Thirsty Thursdays. Aaron knew the religion-like ritual of drinking Thursday nights away very well. As he watches his students put their coats on and begin towards the door, he remembered to mention something. “Remember the lab is due next Thursday at the beginning of lab!” “Will you be in your office next week?” Aaron simply nodded to the question posed by the passing student. The room was beginning to empty now and that allowed Aaron to begin gathering his stuff. One of the things he hated the most about his new teaching duties was the dry erase markers. He had made fun of his professors for years for constantly carrying them around. Now he lived that reality that markers in a classroom disappeared quicker than anything he had ever seen. As he wrapped the rubber band around the six markers he had brought into class he heard a familiar voice call out to him. “Aaron, what are your office hours again?” “Monday, Wednesday and Friday from ten in the morning until two in the afternoon.” Out of the corner of his eye he watched Chloe, the last student left in the room, approach the desk his books sat on. He didn’t say anything else, trying to treat her question as a serious one, but Aaron knew better. He stacked his books from largest to smallest and then finally looked up to the beautiful red head. “But that was sarcasm in your voice wasn’t it?” “You can read me like a book.” Chloe laughed as she completed her statement. She reached out and placed her hand on his shoulder, as she had done the week before. It made Aaron uncomfortable again too. He often thought about how he played an arrogant, apathetic prick on television and how much further from the truth he could get. Aaron never liked stepping on anyone else’s toes in any situation, but especially when it came to relationships. Despite the chemistry he and Chloe had felt in the early part of 2010 Tyler had swooped in and swept her off her feet. “So I heard you were having a party tonight.” “I’m having some people over. Kind of like a house warming.” He hadn’t told many people about his get together that night, but it didn’t take much for news of a party to travel through a college campus. He knew the ten or so people he had invited would quickly turn into thirty or forty. He was okay with that, but Aaron was really hoping Chloe wouldn’t catch wind of it. “Where’d you hear that?” “I’ve got my sources.” Chloe retracted her arm and leaned on the desk in front of her. Aaron avoided eye contact with her for a moment but it was inevitable. Once he did they shared a smile, awkward on Aaron’s part but flirty on Chloe’s. Aaron cleared his throat and sighed as Chloe paused, hoping he’d know what she was getting at. “Do you mind if I show up?” “I don’t know if you should Chloe. It may be a conflict of interest. I am one of your instructors now.” Chloe immediately began to laugh. It wasn’t an obnoxious laugh, but just a small chuckle to show she thought he was joking. It wasn’t a joke and Aaron was barely lying. He did think it would be a conflict of interest, but not with the school. Tyler had called Chloe in the midst of their dinner the week prior and Aaron could tell he was upset with her. “You know?” “Oh you know other people from the meteorology department are going to show up.” His cover was blown. Aaron’s mind raced around trying to find a way to cover back up, but nothing he thought of sounded reasonable. He did have a lot of friends remaining in the program from his undergraduate career and knew they would show up too. Before he could say anything Chloe spoke once more. “Oh. Don’t worry about Tyler either. It’s not a one on one situation so he’ll be fine with it.” “Of course he will be. It’s not like there’s anything going on between us.” The words seemed to skip right over the filter between brain and vocal chords. Inside Aaron was worried about his comment damaging any chance he may have had with Chloe in the future, but outside his expression didn’t change. He watched Chloe’s ever-present smirk shrink a little bit and her eyes dart away from his. “What the hell? Everyone is going to start showing up at six.” “Awesome. I’ll come over after work.” The smirk grew back a bit and her eyes lit up again. She gave him a friendly punch on the shoulder and began moving towards the door. He followed her after picking up his books and double checking that he had all of his things. Once satisfied he wasn’t leaving anything behind he followed her to the door and shut the light off as they exited. She took a turn towards the staircase and Aaron continued towards his office. “I’ll see you later tonight then Aaron.” Thursday 7 P.M. Rowdy wouldn’t be the term to describe the atmosphere inside Aaron’s apartment. Then again, quiet wasn’t right either. There were about eleven people inside the spacious abode all doing various things. Aaron sat on the couch with the new Miller Lite vortex bottle in his left hand. He doesn’t seem to be doing much of anything at all. He takes a sip from his beer and wipes away the little bit of dribble from his chin before looking up at the door. It didn’t open and Aaron looked to his watch. It was just after seven o’clock and she hadn’t been there yet. He found himself on the fence about how he felt about Chloe showing up. Aaron wanted to see her, but at the same time feared how his alcohol consumption would affect the words coming out of his mouth later that night. “Aaron, come on man. We’ve got the table set up.” Aaron turned his head to see his friend Brad standing just to the side of the couch. He motions over his shoulder and side steps for Aaron to see. Aaron looks past Brad to see a white table in the center of the room with ten red plastic Solo cups set up at either end. At first Aaron doesn’t move and looks back to the door. His stare remains there for a second. “Come on man. I need my partner to step it up.” “All right.” Aaron takes another pull from his beer and pushes himself to his feet. He smiles and pats Brad on the shoulder before pushing past and walking to the table. Aaron picked up the ping pong ball sitting at his end and bounced it on the table a few times. He snatched it back up into his hand and looked across the table to Brad and his opponents. They were two guys he recognized from campus, but didn’t know personally. He figured he’d offer the first show of friendship. “You guys want to become members of the Buffalo Club? It’s a lifelong drinking game.” “Hell, why not?” The two of them had looked at each other for a moment before nodding their heads. Brad and Aaron look at each other with smirks on their faces and clink their beers together before taking a drink. They turn back to the two guys at the end of the table ready for the inevitable next question. “So how do we join?” “You guys got a penny?” Aaron asked the question as he came around the end of the table and began making his way down towards the newest members. They dug in their pockets and each came out with a penny, handing them to Aaron. He inspected them for a second, turning them into the light before handing them back. “Can you both identify the year on your pennies?” “Mine says 2010.” Aaron nods and accepts the shiny coin back. He turns to the other guy and waits as he inspects his currency. The coin he holds in his hand is old and dirty, making it difficult to identify the date. Finally the guy’s eyes brighten up and he turns back to Aaron. “Mine says 1974.” “Okay then, my next question; could each of you identify your dominant hand?” Both men raise their right hands and Aaron motions for them to put them down. Aaron snatches his beer up with his left hand and takes another pull before belching. As he exhales he begins speaking again. “From this moment on you two are no longer allowed to hold an open container of alcohol with your right hands. If another member of the Buffalo Club catches you with a drink in your right hands and calls ‘Buffalo’ you are required to finish that drink as fast as possible. The only way to get out of the club is by returning your pennies to me. Now to make sure you’ll never get your pennies back, I’m going to flush them down the toilet.” “Oh that sucks. We are so screwed man.” Aaron laughed at the two guys looking to their hands to make sure their beers are in their left hands. Brad watched them like a hawk as Aaron turned and began making his way towards the bathroom. He turned into the room and shut the door before tossing the coins into the bowl, causing a loud clink to emanate from the throne. He reached down and pressed the handle, causing the toilet to flush and laughed as he did each time he inducted somebody new. As he turned and reached for the door handle he stopped and reached into his pocket for his phone. He brought it out and opened it to see no new text messages. His look was that of disappointment but he didn’t allow it to remain there as he exited the bathroom and came back to the table. “You dumbass.” Aaron spoke through a laugh as one of their opponents was already chugging the beer in his hand. Brad had called the first buffalo on the two guys and was getting a huge kick out of it. Aaron snatched up the ping pong ball and tossed it across the table. “Just for that, you guys can have first shots.” Aaron took another pull from his beer, emptying it. He belched again and walked a few feet to the fridge to snag another. He brought back two, handed one to Brad and set his empty back down on the table. As his two opponents spoke of their strategy, Aaron reached into his pocket and brought a can of Skoal out. He got a pouch from the container and placed it in his lip while he waited for the first shot of the game. The first guy took his shot and missed wide right. In a rapid fire motion, his partner bounced the ball off the table and it landed in the back row, floating inside a cup. Aaron and Brad both exclaimed, too busy chasing the first ball. Brad grabbed two cups and moved them to the side before each of them took a long pull from their beers. Aaron always insisted on putting water in the cups and drinking from bottles to avoid possibly messes later in the night. And there would be a mess. “This is going to be a long night.” Brad’s words went into Aaron’s head and made him nod. His mind still wasn’t fully into the game. He looked up to the door and shook his head before taking another pull from his beer. Thursday 10 P.M. “Are, are, you guys ready to um, um, lose another match?” The slurred words escaped from Aaron’s mouth. He and Brad immediately broke into drunken laughter and fired high fives that were just off the mark, flying past one another. Aaron turned and lined up his shot at the only remaining cup at the other end of the table. He closed one eye and swayed from side to side. Finally he was able to steady himself and tossed the shot into the air. The white ball sailed through the air and graciously fell into the center of the cup, splashing a bit of the water onto the table. Aaron immediately turned and fired another high five in Brad’s direction. This time, with sheer luck, their hands connected and Aaron turned back to their opponents. His eyes immediately went to their beers, being held in the right hand. “Twelve and three on the night. And boys. Buf-Buffalo.” “Shit.” Both men sighed and began chugging their beers. Aaron watched the two drown their sorrow of losing another match in another chugged beer, but something else caught his eye. The door was just closing and standing at the front door was Chloe’s figure. Aaron hiccupped before taking the last pull from his beer and headed to the fridge. He grabbed two beers from it and headed back towards the table. Brad tried to take one, but Aaron pulled it away and staggered past him towards Chloe’s position. “Hey Aaron!” She accepted the beer from him and opened it to take a long drink. He motioned for her to follow him and they walked through the apartment to the back door. Chloe shivered as they stepped outside, but Aaron was warm even in just his t-shirt and jeans. Aaron slowly lowered himself to a sitting position on the wooden steps and placed his two bottles on the step below. Chloe followed suit and turned to look at him. “I got here as soon as I could. I got off work late and then Tyler called.” “Yeah? How’d that go?” Aaron hiccupped again as he took the can of dip from his pocket again and snatched another apple flavored pouch from it. He placed it in his lip and took a drink from his beer. A moment after taking the drink he turned his head and spit off the steps to the ground below. He turned back to Chloe and spoke again. “Was he-he pissed?” “That would be an understatement. I never even told him about last summer and he already doesn’t trust you.” She took a long pull from her beer and sighed after she had swallowed it. He shook his head and lifted his empty bottle to spit into. Once his mouth was clear he took another swig from his beer and looked back to her. Even through his horny-drunk-male state he didn’t feel the need to make advances on her. He was perfectly happy just talking. “So how many have you had tonight?” “I’m not sure, I kind-kind of lost count after thirteen.” He took another pull and laughed obnoxiously. She let out a chuckle herself and took a very long chug from her bottle as if to say she needed to catch up. Aaron spit into his empty bottle again before speaking. “To quote a line from one of the greatest movies ever. EVER; I love talking and conversing with you.” “I love it too.” Chloe spoke through a chuckle at his emphasis on the word ever. She leaned her head over and rested it on his shoulder for a second before straightening back up. She took the last drink from her beer and watched Aaron follow suit. She stood and took both of the empties. “Against my better judgment, I’ll ask if you’d like another.” “Pl-please.” Aaron watched as Chloe disappeared back into the apartment to retrieve a couple more beers. He sighed and spit into his bottle once more as he waited for her to return. Like the last week, a million things raced through his mind, most not making sense. He was almost startled when she tapped him on the shoulder with another beer. He grabbed it and stood up as she re-took her seat on the stairs. “Chloe, what happened with us?” “What do you mean?” She asked the question and took a sip from her new drink, knowing well and good what he was asking. Aaron stammered over a few words as he paced in the small yard. The two avoided eye contact for a moment until Aaron looked back up to her. “We’re close Aaron.” “Not as close as we used to be or could have been.” His words were flowing straight from the brain and out of his mouth now. The idea that sober thoughts were drunken words was becoming his reality. He kicked at the ground and spat before looking back to her and speaking. “We connected on that trip. I felt something special.” “So did I Aaron. I don’t know what happened.” Her words served to put not only his mind, but his heart at ease. He took a long pull on his beer and sighed. The two enjoyed an awkward silence for a few moments until Aaron staggered back over and sat down next to her. “I was falling in love with you.” “You changed me, Chloe. You made me see things dif-differently.” He spoke through a hiccup and took a deep breath of cold Illinois air. He stammered over a few more words before he heard her comforting giggle and felt her hand wrap around his left arm. “All I’m saying is drop the hero and get with the zero.” “You mean drop the zero and get with the hero.” She giggled again and laid her head on his shoulder once more. This time it remained there longer than it did previously, which was fine with Aaron. He smirked a bit as he spit into his bottle and set it back down. “Right?” “Yeah that’s what I sa-said.” Aaron looked into the clear, cold sky and stared at the bright sparkles of light millions of miles away. No matter what his state of mind was he could always get lost in the stars. Just like he always got lost looking directly into Chloe’s eyes. “I was falling in love with you too. I think I st-still am.” “Really?” She sat upright suddenly and reached into her jeans pocket for her phone. Aaron snuck a peek at the screen and saw she had an incoming call from Tyler. He knew their moment was over, but his drunken mind made a good decision by not saying anything. He waited for her to walk away, but instead watched her ignore the call and slide the phone back into her pocket. She reached in and grabbed a hold of his left arm again, laying her head back down. “I lied to you. I think I did fall in love with you.” Her words were ones that would remain with him for a lot shorter than he expected.
