The Return of the Juveniles (Sith Legacy, Episode VI)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: A Long, Long Time Ago "Are you ready?" the hooded visage of Emperor Stoner asks. Darth Boner turns to his master, and silently nods his head. This is it, the culmination. Any second now now Evan Skywalker and Chris Solo would make their way to the meditation chamber the two Sith Lords currently occupied. Darth Boner couldn't help but look at the stars through the glass walls behind his Emperor, reminding him of days long ago, when this all started as a simple game. Who would have thought that buying lightsabers on eBay would lead to galactic conquest and domination? "I can sense your doubt in the Force, Boner," the Emperor casually states, probing for any further reactions from his brooding servant. "Yes, Master," comes the reply. "He will not turn to the Dark Side." "Then you will kill him," the Emperor watches Darth Boner, searching for a sign of weakness in him. Satisfied that his servant will act accordingly, the Emperor turns his attention to the Force, searching for the young Jedi. At that instant, the mechanical doors in front of the two slide open. Evan Skywalker walks purposefully through the opening, Chris Solo trailing slightly behind, blaster pistol in hand. Evan makes eye contact with the Emperor and his lackey, and quickly surveys the room, wary of a trap. Luckily, the room is utterly bare save for the throne upon which Emperor Stoner sits. "The Emperor has been expecting you," Darth Boner addresses his son in his cold, mechanical voice. "I know father," Evan Skywalker answers back, trying to mask his feelings. "So, you have accepted the truth?" Darth Boner asks his son. "I accept that you were once Anakin Skywalker," Evan replies. "Wait, what? I've never been Anakin Skywalker! Chris Storm bitch! I'm Chris fucking Storm!" Darth Boner's rage rises inside of him, feeding the Force. "Oh... My bad, Dad," comes the half-hearted apology from Evan. "Let's get it on!" With that, the two warriors unleash their lightsabers. "Dude, this isn't how the story went," Darth Boner turns his head to look at Markus. "Are we rewriting history here?" "Um, I think the more important question to ask, is whether or not it's safe for you kids to play with lightsabers and laster blasters," Emperor Stoner states, turning Boner's attention back to his children just in time to deflect a laser blast and prevent possible decapitation. "Boba Fett! Boba Fett! Where!?!" Chris Solo screams in a mixture of surprise and terror, shooting his blaster randomly about the room. "Goddammit, we'll never finish the scene at this rate," Markus sighs, putting his head in his hands. "Dude, language," admonishes Chris. As he turns back to face his son in Mortal Kombat, Chris can't help but smile inside. This is way better than playing The Force Unleashed! Before he can become too pleased with events, however, his son rushes forward, striking randomly with his lightsaber. Chris, er... Darth Boner is barely able to keep up with the surprisingly quick child, and almost gets his hand removed from his wrist on more than one occasion. "I'm telling you dude, we should've sent them to a karate class, or something. He's got no technique at all," Emperor Stoner observes with a look of disdain. "Dude... I'm glad... We.... Didn't... Help?" Chris gasps as he quickly runs out of breath, still desperately trying to block the intensifying attack. "Oh well..." Markus casually flips his hand up, and Evan's lightsaber deactivates and flies from his hand into Emperor Stoner's waiting grasp. "Be the Emperor, you won't ever have to work. Just sit around all day while Darth Boner fights everybody. Yeah, that's working out just dandy." "Dude, he's quick," Chris says in defense of himself. "Never. I'll never turn to the Dark Side. You have failed, your highness. I am a Jedi like my father before me," Evan defiantly spits out. "What? Dude, did you even read the script, or are you just picking your favorite quotes from the movies? Chris wasn't ever a Jedi, we said from the start we wanted to be Sith!" Markus pounds his fist on the arm of his throne. This is all going to be ruined because some silly ten year old can't be trusted with the minor responsibility of memorizing a three thousand and six page script detailing one overdrawn and blatantly plagiarized fight scene which involves stealing quotes from all six Star Wars movies in a randomly pieced together attempt at dialogue. Whew. Say that three times fast! "Evan, I am your father," Chris deactivates his lightsaber and holds out his hand to his son. "No shit pops," Evan dismisses him and turns to the Emperor. "Hey! Language!" Chris scolds his son, giving him a light smack on the back of his head. "OW!" "Dude... I give up..." Markus sighs as he rises from his chair. "I quit." "Noooooooo!!!!!!" Chris screams in agony and terror. Rushing towards his former master, Chris lifts him into the air in an overhead press, walking over to... "Wait, where's the big hole in the ground that I'm supposed to throw you down?" Chris asks, looking around the room completely confused. "Um, remember edit two hundred and six? I took it out. No way you're dropping me on a shaft!" Markus explains to his partner. "Damn," Chris says, disappointed he drops Markus. "Hey kids, how 'bout some popcorn and a Star Wars marathon?" Chris asks his kids. Of course they agree, what kid wouldn't? Except for those children whose parents are so atrociously horrible that they haven't forced their children to sit through an entire six episode marathon once a year since they were born. Yeah, Chris' kids are THAT fuckin' lucky. Markus looks up as the three exit the meditation chamber, watches as the walk across the hall and into the television room. "Damn that hurt. Oh well, there really wasn't any way to finish this trilogy off that made any sense. At least I didn't let Whore Lucas and Steven Auschitzberg ruin everything." Yeah, that's it. The end. How the fuck else did you think it would finish, something that made sense? This is B&E~! for fucks sake. Don't worry folks, in twenty years, we'll have Episode 1, 2, and 3 come out. But maybe, due to the horrendous quality of these, those will make sense?
