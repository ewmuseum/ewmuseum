Deliver me from EVL : Part three the Story of Borogard

28 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/28/2005 Back again? I suppose you want to know what happened with Treyial? The simple answer is, I lost that first fight, but do not worry there was a second later on. I will tell you of that one another day. Today I find myself wanting to tell you what happened with Borogard. Have a seat and get cozy. Now let's see where should I begin? Ah yes, I will start with the meeting that took place after my recovery from the injuries Treyial had bestowed upon me. It had taken two weeks of recovery before I had the ability to get out of bed and walk around without fear of opening the sutures in my stomach. The injury to my head had been less than I had thought but it still made my eyes extremely sensitive to light for most of that period. On the fifteenth day of recovery I stepped outside for the first time to a chorus of cheers and hellos from those loyal men of Eve's ship. It made me smile. As fate would have it Cedric had informed me earlier that day that we were headed to Stevis's homestead for another meeting of strategies in hopes that the next battle would go better than the last. It seemed we were already there. The ship was docked as the officers and I left port and made the short trip to Stevis's permanent camp. It was a place that kind of reminded me of the old pioneer forts in the untamed west of the North America, at least from what I remembered of the stories in history. Tall walls made of rather thick logs surrounded the place as a form of protection from attack. Inside there were around five or six actual building built for the purpose of housing and storage. Most of the rest of the wooden fortress was comprised of those large canvas tents. For as rudimentary as it was I found myself rather impressed with it. Though rustic, it had a homey feel to it and the people there seemed content. It was difficult not to be jealous of that. We had gone to the largest building in the center, which turned out to be Stevis's main quarters. It was luxurious all things considering and well kept thanks to the red headed woman whom I can only guess was his lover. She was a sweet thing with a soft personality that I might have considered a weakness at one point. Today I am well aware of it being a strength. Mick was cordial enough, I suppose, but it was rather evident that his liking of me was strained at best. Later I found out that he and Eve had often butted heads over strategies and plans. Oddly enough it reminded me of myself and Michael Stevens. It almost made me want to apologize to Michael...almost. It did not take long to find out we were the second to arrive for this prearranged meeting. Mystra was already there, punctual as always. All who was left to arrive were Borogard and Seymorus. The wait was not a long one as the two showed up almost simultaneously. Seymorus was pleasant and cordial asking me of my injuries and the like. I am guessing he and Eve were friends, but at this point I really do not know much. Borogard stepping into the room had me on edge immediately. The man despite his good looks reminded me of a snake slithering about ready to strike at anything as he tried to lure women to eat his forbidden fruit. It was rather sickening to be honest. At first I hadn't even noticed the dark smile on his lips and in his eyes, but I can say I was the first to sense something wasn't right. ' Alright, As we all know the last battle did not go well at all. It is almost as if they knew what was planned.' I merely slide a glance over to Borogard as Seymorus spoke of his thoughts. It was Stevis who spoke next, his voice rather cold and emotionless as he offered information none of us had...well almost none of us. ' They did.' All eyes, including my own turned to him in surprise. ' Excuse me?' Mystra was looking rather aghast at his words as she questioned him. I merely glanced at Borogard again noting a slight tightening of his jaw as the color in his face paled the slightest bit. Mick had risen from his seat to come around the chair and lean on it as he spread his hands in a gesture of shrugging. ' I do not believe I stuttered Mystra. They knew what we had planned.' My patience was thinner then usual due to the injuries and lack of comfort. ' Yes, Stevis. We understood that. but HOW did they know? Stop beating around the bush and tell us.' ' The answer is simple. We have a traitor.' ' One of the men?' ' No, Seymorus. The traitor is in this room as we speak.' My heart stopped as I caught Borogard's hands clenching the table out of the corner of my eye. It didn't take a genius to know exactly whom it was that had betrayed us and nearly cost me my life. I played along for now as everyone fell eerily silent staring at each other in question. Not one to play games unless they amuse me, I spoke up. ' Are you going to tell us or just let everyone question each other's loyalty in silence?' Mick slid a glance at me that could be described as scathing before giving a fleeting look at Borogard and finally sighing. ' I had hoped they would be man enough to speak up for themselves.' ' In other words you do not know who it is.' ' That is the truth. I only got enough information out of the man to know it was one of us before he died on me the bastard.' Mystra shook her head. ' Was he dying from a wound gained in battle or one gained from you?' Mick flushed and scowled as Mystra muttered under her breath and answered. ' Dead men tell no tales Mick. You really need to learn the art of interrogation.' ' How was I supposed to know he was as weak as he was?' Mick was obviously angered by her accusation as the pair began to argue. I said nothing as my eyes went to Borogard once more to find him smiling slightly and appearing more relaxed. A Snake in the grass. The argument became heated as Seymorus finally slammed a fist o the table and bellowed to get everyone's attention. ' ENOUGH! All this arguing will not change that man's fate nor will it change the situation at hand. We have a traitor among us now the question is who?' Borogard was the first to speak up. Somehow I wasn't surprised. ' Personally I say it is Eve. She was with them once. Who is to say she isn't working for them still? That could explain how they always seem to know what we are up to.' If I had not been in fairly bad shape still I would have pummel his handsome face into a pile of mush for that nasty accusation. What a perfect ploy he had initiated by casting doubt on my character..or rather Eve's. ' Why you son of a--' Mystra spoke up first. ' How can you honestly think that. Eva nearly died from that battle. IF she were working with them I do not believe Treyial would have injured her.' ' Then you are a fool Mystra. He injured her to make it look real. If she were not working with them do you not think he would have just killed her? I think it is all a very clever ruse.' My teeth clamped together so hard I swear I felt them cracked beneath the strain as Cedric put a staying hand on my shoulder urging me to remain seated. I bit back my stream of nasty comments. Believe me that was a tough thing to do considering my unruly temper. Instead I merely looked to everyone looking at me. Mystra was the first to answer again. ' No, I do not believe she is the traitor.' ' Nor do I' ' I do.' My eyes snapped to Stevis as he stared at me with a venom in his eyes. ' After all, Borogard's words make perfect sense. Treyial had her right where he wanted her. She was unconscious, unable to fight back. Why didn't he just take her head?' Good question, not one I wanted answered to be honest. I was in a bad situation now. Borogard had Stevis convinced I was the traitor while Mystra and Seymorus thought I was not. I looked back to Borogard again as all four of them began arguing over what to do with me. ' I say kill her!' ' NO! It is not her. You make a move toward her and I will kill you myself!' ' Lock the bitch up!' The hand on my shoulder squeezed in gentle reassurance as I listened on at the pandemonium Borogard had started. It was soon evident that the alliance was falling apart. It was then that it dawned on me...that was exactly what he wanted to happen. With the alliance split he was weakening our stance against Jordius! It was one of the oldest tricks in the book and one I have used often in the past. Sad to say, I almost admired him for the handy work. I had underestimated the man and as a result he had caught me off guard. He did it once. It would not happen again. In the end Stevis agreed to allow us all to leave unharmed, but under the statement we were never to return lest we wanted war with him. Once we returned to the ship Cedric turned to look at me. ' You okay Cap'n?' ' Do I look okay?' ' If it helps, I don't believe it is you.' ' That is because it isn't.' ' It is Borogard isn't it?' I stared at him in bit of shock before finally smiling. ' My thoughts exactly.' ' So what do you plan to do about it?' I grinned maliciously. ' Fight fire with ice.' Cedric chuckled. ' Woe be to that man when you are done with him. But what of the alliance?' ' What of it?' ' What are we going to do?' ' Exactly what needs to be done. We are going to get it back.' ' How?' I sighed running fingers through my messed blond hair. ' I do not know that just yet. But I will..bet your ass on that.' ' Aye, Cap'n. You always seem to pull out miracles when needed.' ------------------------------------------ Pardon? Oh I do apologize, I had not realized the hour was growing late and you had other things to attend too. Please go. Do what you need to do. I can always tell you later how I managed to regain the Alliance of Stevis and how the new alliance crushed Borogard in the end. Thank you for listening. I look forward to the next time you come around.
