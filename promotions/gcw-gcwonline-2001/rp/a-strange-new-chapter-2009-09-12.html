A strange new chapter

12 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/12/09 It had been weeks since I saw Erika die in childbirth. Weeks since I sent her son, a helpless baby, into the mazelike system of social services. Since that time I had barely been able to sleep, eat, or even think because of the pressing guilt that I was experiencing. I had tried to justifying my actions to myself countless times, but with every explanation I dredged up came with an undeniable counter. I don’t have enough space in my apartment (but I have more than enough money to move to a bigger place). I know nothing about raising a kid (neither does any other new parent). He was not my son (but you told his mother you’d help raise him). I desperately tried to ignore the little voice in my head as it berated me, but every day that damn voice grew louder. I grew so despondent that I began to cut off all human contact. I refused to answer the phone, barely left my apartment; I even refused to speak to my brother. Of course Sean was not the type to allow his brother to stop talking to him without an explanation. So he decided to pay me a visit on Sunday, awakening me by ringing the doorbell at seven in the morning. I lay there, lacking the motivation to get up and answer the door. The ringing continued insistently as time passed. It became a waiting game to see whose patience would run out first, and after ten minutes I got up and answered the door. “S-Sean,” I stuttered stupidly. “What the hell are you doing here?” “Well for one thing I came to find out why you decided it was ok to stop talking to me,” he said heatedly assuming the authoritative older brother role. It was a role that I respected by habit acquired over years of listening to his wise advice. “Secondly,” he growled as he took in my despondent condition as well as the overall mess of my apartment, “I want to know why you have decided to kill yourself through attrition. Then I’ll kick your ass, help you overcome what was undoubtedly caused by your stupidity, and kick your ass again.” His tough guy act brought a sad smile to my face as I sat down on my couch and looked up at him. I licked my lips and worked my jaw, but a lump had formed in my throat. I tried to force the story out, but my body betrayed me. All I could to do was stare helplessly at my brother as he stared back. Finally his face softened and he sighed. “Is it something to do with work or money?” he asked probingly. His mouth tightened in frustration as he tried again. “Is it something to do with Erika?” he asked again. I cringed as I realized I had never even told him that Erika died. A tear escaped from my eye and rolled down my cheek; at the sight of it my brother’s eyes widened and he encouragingly placed a hand on my shoulder. “What happened,” he asked with intense and demanding eyes. “Just spit out.” I opened my mouth and the entire story came out. I spoke rapidly, knowing that if I stopped I would not be able to start again. I told him how Erika died in childbirth, how her son came out deformed, and how I essentially abandoned the baby. His emotions were clear on his face, from shock to sadness, back to shock, and ending with intense anger. I looked at him pleadingly after I finished searching his face for some sign of compassion or understanding. I just knew that if he told me that it was alright, that I was not in fact a complete bastard I would be able to finally to pull myself out of this black pit of shame that I forced myself in. But instead of giving me a kind word, instead of granting me absolution, he delivered a backhand to my face. The blow did not hurt, at least not physically. Truthfully while I have out of necessity kept myself in shape (and heavily muscled), Sean has more or less let himself go. But this was the first time that my brother had ever hit me. As my head snapped to one side I absurdly had a flashback to when I was fourteen. I was on the couch, laughing as Sean paced before me, threatening to bring his “divine hand of retribution” down upon me if I even thought about getting less than a B in any of my high school classes. But there was no humor in his face when I looked back up at him. There was only contempt, disgust, and shame in his eyes. His jaw worked furiously for some time, while I sat silently hoping that our relationship was not permanently damaged. “You find that poor kid,” he spat out at me, “you bring him home, and you act like a fucking man for once in your life. Until you do, don’t bother talking to me.” He stalked out the door while I stared at his back. I wanted to implore him not to leave, to beg his forgiveness, but I sat silently on my couch. I continued to sit there after he left turning his words over in my mind. It was obvious that I would have to find the boy, and get social services to let me take him. As I tried to decide just how I would go about finding out the location of a boy whose name I did not even know, I heard my doorbell ring again. I literally jumped off the couch and ran to the door. I was certain that it was Sean, come back to take back his harsh words. I eagerly opened the door, and a steel pipe crashed into my forehead. I came to maybe a later with a massive headache and a gash where the pipe hit me. I stood groggily and tried to figure out what had happened. My first thought was that I was the victim of a standard robbery. But upon closer inspection I realized that neither my TV, nor my computer had been taken. For that matter nothing else of value had been taken either. It was hard to think considering the attack I had just undergone, but eventually something became horrifically clear to me. Only two things had been taken. The first was a stack of pictures that had me and Erika in them, and the second was a video we once made on a whim where we saying hi to her baby. We were going to show that video to the baby when it turned five, but I could think of no reason that anyone would want to take it. As I began to think a bit more clearly I came to the conclusion that someone wanted to erase all evidence of my relationship with Erika. The only question I had to ask was, why?
