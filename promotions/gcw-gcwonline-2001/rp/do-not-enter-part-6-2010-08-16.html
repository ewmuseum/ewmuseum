Do Not Enter Part 6

16 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/16/10 Aron Levy starts his walk towards the Coffee Cup to meet with someone he hasn’t seen in years. Hell he hasn’t really thought about this man in years as well. Aron has a very simple mind when it comes to people as a whole. He never dwells on the past, and very rarely ever thinks about the future. People he has come across over the years will hide in the back of his brain only to be locked up and never revealed again. Who gives a damn is his common thread of thought. The walk is not that long, obviously only minutes by car. He has a car, he can afford to have 15 cars if he wants, but luxuries are exactly that, just luxuries. Aron pities those that lock themselves in a world where material things are at the center of their lives. Aron decided to walk instead of drive because he needed to unlock that chamber in his skull to remember everything from past visits with this man. He recalls their first ever encounter…… The man was short and stout. His facial hair was a scraggly mess, his teeth were yellow, and his hair was a bit all over. They met in a public park right behind the Haunted Belmont Mansion(or so they say it’s haunted) where they can be discreet but not too much so as to be a center of attention. The man gave Aron a briefcase, Aron flipped it opened, nodded his head in agreement and shook this man’s hand and told him all will be done. That was well over 25 years ago, and now this man needs his help again. This time the help is well, far more against any law Aron has ever broken before in his life. His own cockiness and determination though makes him confident that this deed can and will be done, but obviously with such a hefty price. The good doctor sat at a table by himself, his laptop opened, the internet running wild, his coffee getting cold and his heart pounding away like a jackhammer. He can’t believe what he is about to do, but it is something that MUST be done before everything gets out of hand. Landis is on a website called “Walkwithmeinhell.com” It is a highly visited free website that is popular amongst the young kids and those with a sick bloodlust. Every single serial killer, mass murderer, and violent rapist to ever walk this earth are on this site describing their youths, the crimes, and their deaths(if applicable) to anyone willing to know what goes on inside their sick depraved heads. On the main page there is a search engine where you can put any word or set of words that you can think of and any article can pop up that has keyed those words. Landis put “Bryan O’Neil” inside the search block and more than 600 articles/stories appeared. That’s pretty extraordinary due to the fact that those with the names of Charles Manson, Jeffery Dahmer, and John Wayne Gacy topped off at 800 articles. Landis starts to scroll using his mouse for any title that catches his eye. Everything that is need to be known about his murders were already considered public knowledge so investigating this is most likely redundant and unnecessary. Landis was not paying attention to the articles until he see’s a name that stops his heart and his fingers from clicking. Annabelle Hoggins. She wrote the article in the Belmont paper. Dr. Landis was seen on the cover of the paper pushing a body covered in a sheet. That picture will live in annuls of history in Belmont. Dr. Landis the “hero” pushing away the body of the “villian” Bryan O’Neil. The article read: The beast is dead. Bryan O’Neil committed his last heinous crime this world will ever see. At 2:19 PM Dr. Don Landis pronounced this beast dead. O’Neil was sentenced to die in what will most likely be the last ever public execution via firing squad this country will ever see. When I arrived there were throes of spectators waiting to see the death of the man that had instilled fear in the hearts of not just Belmont but all of England. Young and old a like, some laden with picnic baskets, others with cameras and recording devices stood in front of the execution chamber, most smiling and talking about various topics including the weather, football, and the crown. No one would ever have known that just in a matter of moments a man was going to be killed for his crimes. When O’Neil came out, cloth bag covering his face, he was greeted with boos, cuss words, and even various objects from picnic baskets. The crowd insisted the cloth be removed from his head. After minutes of ridicule the Executioner allowed this. There was no face paint, yet no fear in his eyes either. He looked out at everyone and almost scoffed at us. Dr. Landis was at the side of the chamber, waiting for his cue…… Landis sits at the desk recalling everything that happened that day. Ms. Hoggins had a skewed version of the day obviously. Sure there was people sitting down picnic style, but there were those who lost loved ones that day that just wanted it to end. After they put the bullet into Bryan O’Neil, who’s expression never changed, not even when the bullet hit him, he fell down as the blood began to exit his body in heavy droves. Once again he looked out at the crowd as they all cheered his death on, expressionless. He never once feared death, it was almost as if he welcomed it. It took 6 men to lift the body onto the gurney, than Landis pulled out the stethoscope, and declared the monster dead. The heart slowed down, the cold sweat vanished, and the years of fear, emptiness, and despair all seemed to disappear. They covered the body where Landis would take him into a separate room to properly dispose of the body. The town voted on the body being burnt. So let it be written, so let it be done. Landis recalls pushing the gurney, which seemed to weigh a ton, and talking to himself yet to Bryan O’Neil at the same time. “You might have been able to kill my little one, but it is I who is laughing last.” He grits his teeth and refrains from spitting on the body until he’s out of view. “I am the only one that knows your secret.” He is finally out of the crowds view, closes the door and begins to pound on the chest and stomach of Bryan O’Neil. “You mother fucker! You killed my baby!!! You fucking baby killer!!” The punches become harder and harder. Landis falls to the ground out of breathe and begins to weep. He looks up at the dead man on the gurney than looks behind him at the oven that has been heated up waiting for the body to be extinguished of the way the town voted. Outside the crowd looks up at the chimney waiting for that plume of smoke to smolder out to let them all know that the ghost story finally has a happy ending. The body is still, the sheet soaking up the blood from where the bullet entered. Dr. Landis stands up and decides to go against the rules and removes the sheet from O’Neil’s face and stares at the man that has wreaked havoc in their tiny town for so many years. The oldest son of a devil worshipper and a whore. Now a executed murderer. Landis stares down and smiles at O’Neil. The secret will forever be a secret, and the money paid is well worth it now. Landis stares down at the body, and pushes the gurney towards the oven in which flames are starting to lick out of the opening. He pushed the button as the elevator to the top start to ascend. He does not see it nor does he expect it. A giant claw springs up and clenches its hand around his throat. The unexpected actions cause Landis to jolt backwards and instinctively put his hands to this throat. It took him a second to realize that the hands were not a claw, and these hands belonged to the person he just declared dead not too long ago. The hands begin to squeeze harder and harder. Landis can feel his throat begin to collapse as his legs turn to rubber and his body comes crashing onto that of Bryan O’Neils. Landis lands inches from the face of England’s most notorious serial killer. He can smell his stagnant breath, and the heavy breathing. He looks into his assailants black eyes and see’s something he never thought he’d ever see. He see’s fear! The hand let’s go and Landis drops to the floor as the elevator still ascends slowly. He grabs his throat and begins to hack for any breathe he can muster up. His body starts to convulse and his head begins to throb. He almost forgets the monster that nearly choke the life out of him until he see’s a boot followed by droplets of blood. “Hey, Landis, wake up.” He comes from his trance brought on by Ms. Hoggins article. He looks up and it’s a face he recognizes but barely recognizes. Landis stands up, extends his hand, and shakes Aron Levy’s hand, hopefully for the last time. He pulls a chair out and invites Aron to sit down. “Can I get you a coffee?” Aron looks around, and nods. “Sure. Black.” Dr. Landis walks towards the counter, looks back at Aron Levy and almost decides to leave. He starts to think that maybe this problem isn’t bigger than he is. Maybe, just maybe he can stop this himself without having to go through a ‘middle man’ of sorts. He could be a hero again. No one even knows what happened anyways. Only two people know the truth, and one of them is not likely to speak because that one person is hell bent on blood and murder. He orders Aron’s coffee and sits down, tries to think of a way to explain to him that the deal is off when Aron says, “They found another body, this one dismembered beyond all belief. They identified her as Margaret Beamer.” Landis almost falls from his chair. This will never end now, unless something is done. The deal must go on. Landis looks at Aron, “2 and half million?” Aron nods and walks out.
