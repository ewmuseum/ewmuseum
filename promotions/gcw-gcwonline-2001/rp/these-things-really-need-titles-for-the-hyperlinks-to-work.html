Date: Tonight 'Sticks and stones will break my bones, but words will never hurt me.' The old kids rhyme about being teased jumped to mind as stood on the porch of my hotel room as night fell over Oakland. I watched the cars speed by below and the handful of people out and about. An argument across the street which escalated into a street fight which was now being broken up by the police was what brought the words to mind. It was one of those arguments that I could hear over the TV. Oddly enough during an episode of 'Cops'. Or is that ironically? I don't know, I was never good with what really defines irony. Anyway after the brawl was broken up, I headed back inside and helped myself to another beer from the mini bar in my room. An elbow to the head also hurts like hell too. I hadn't really thought too much about my return to the ring since I actually made it. Only now did I really think about it as a promo for Annihlation came on. There's nothing that can describe that feeling when you step through those curtains and hear the fans react, postively or negativley to your very presence. I've been on both sides of the fence and I have to say I like the fact that my being thre got people riled up, especially taking on a favorite like Mike Hardy. I may not have had the seething hatred that people reserved for LeStatt Knight and Rich Rollins aimed at me. But I heard at least one 'Fuck you' shouted at me from somewhere in the crowd. The ringing of my cell phone pulled me away from the TV just as the image of Michael Stevens and LeStatt Knight glaring at each other exploded into the Annihlation logo. I looked at the number. Ah, my wife checking in on me. 'Hey babe.' 'Hey yourself.' she said, I could hear the smile in her voice. 'How ya doin'?' 'I'd be better if you were here with me. I told you it's weird being back out on the road without you.' I said crossing the room to shut the porch door, as the traffic noise was escalating. 'It's almost like I don't know what to do with myself.' 'Well as long as it keeps you out of trouble I think you can find things to keep you occupied. I mean you are staying in top of the line hotels, and they do have cable.' I chuckled, 'I am not watching the Playboy channel!' 'I said nothing. Paranoid much dear?' she said, and laughed. 'Well why not go out and check out the nightlife. I remember when you Jeff and Malcolm would do that. You usually ended up getting in fights.' 'Yeah, but it's not the same without them either.' I said after taking a sip of my beer and settling back on the couch. 'I mean I did go out the other night and some asshole tried to start a fight with me. I gave him a good shot to the ribs, but I was a good boy. I allowed him to walk out of the bar under his own power.' 'Gasp!' And yes, she actually said the word Gasp. 'You mean that temper of yours is actually under control?' I rolled my eyes, 'Yes dear. I let him live. Other than that I haven't really done much. Watching a lot of TV and working out getting ready for Annhilation. You're still coming to that right?' 'Wouldn't miss it for the world. I gets to watch my man kick ass.' 'And you can give me a rubdown afterwards.' 'Pervert.' 'And you love me for it.' I said, grinning althogh I knew she couldn't see it. 'Yeah, I'll have to get myself checked. Loving a lunatic like you can't be normal. Anyways hon, I just wanted to check in and see how you were doing without me.' 'I'll be okay sweeite. I'll see you sunday morning then?' 'I'll be there.' After saying our goodbyes I hung up and killed the last of my beer and channel surfed. Not finding anything worth watching on I decided to check out the nightlife. There had to be something for me to do that wasn't illegal, or would encourage me to cheat on my wife. Anything to get me out of the hotel, staying cooped up for too long would drive me stir crazy. The guy I shared the elevator ride down with kept looking at me as if he wanted to ask for my autograph, but the fact that I activley ignored him and kept cracking my neck and knuckles kept him at arms distance. I got into my rental car and once I pulled out of the parking lot, I randomly turned right and figured I'd let the night take me where it would.
