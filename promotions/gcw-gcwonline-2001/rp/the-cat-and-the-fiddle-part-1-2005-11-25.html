The Cat and the Fiddle ( part 1)

25 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.25.2005 'What the hell is all that rumbling I hear?' It is the first thought to come forth to a slightly incoherent mind as Eva woke up the next morning. Her next thought is the realization that there is a steady stream of wetness sliding across her cheek. Immediately she opens her eyes focusing blurry vision on the obscured view of a grey ball in her face. Tugging that head back with a jerk she refocuses only to grumble as she instinctively pets the culprit responsible for disturbing her rest. The grey tom continues with his purring moving things up a notch by headbutting her repeatedly as he demands even more attention from his chosen pet. Wrinkling her nose, Eva shoves up to a sit position taking that cat in her lap as she gripes hafl heartedly. ' You know it is usually customary not to kiss on the first date big guy. While I appreciate a forward man, I kind of like to keep it in my species.' He just looks up with large blinking green eye before knocking his head into her chin and rubbing along her cheek. Fingers slipped through the silky softness of his fur, Eva losing herself in the natural beauty of the beast. She was losing the battle of wills, in fact, truth be told, she lost that battle last night when she gave in and took him in. The grey was here to stay and she knows it. Canting her head to the side, Eva eyes him a bit in silent wonder. Something deep inside is telling her this cat knew something she didn't. He came to her at a time her life could not be any more chaotic. He came at a time she needed to find that inner peace again. Pursing her lips she sets him aside and slips out of her bed heading into the kitchen. ' Alright big guy, let's get some food and lay down some ground rules. If you plan on sticking around I have a few things that are a must.' Looking over her shoulder at the cat still perching on her bed like a king in his chambers she smiles. ' Coming?' He was up and out of that bed in a heartbeat closely trailing her path. In fact, he is too close, getting under her feet and causing her to trip up some. As the German beauty catches herself she scowls and looks down at him sternly, being greeted with innocent eyes. ' Rule number one...no walking under my feet. Me with a broken neck or ankle...or any bone would not be good for either of us.' There is that look again. She swears he is mocking her silently. Scrutinizing him a moment, she decides to beat him at this little game. The answer is simple. Eva bends down and scoops him up carrying him into the kitchen. Flipping the light on with her elbow she deposits him on the island counter and turns opening the fridge. Bending slightly she hangs on the door wit her head halfway inside the fridge. ' Okay so I have some milk, eggs, vodka....' Turning she looks at him. ' So what will it be? Milk? Eggs?....Vodka?' Grey yawns widely at the mention of vodka as she chuckles in return. ' Fine fine no vodka for you. Let's see what I have in the pantry.' Hip checking the refridgerator door closed she walks to the walk in pantry and tugs the doubl doors open. The cat pays no attention merely flopping to his side and cocking that head back to give himself his ritual morning bath. Eva steps inside scanning everything her delivery man had brought upon request and some he brought as extra. Hands move boxes around, cans are shoved aside to give her a view of other cans and finally she finds something the cat might actually enjoy. Grinning widely she pulls the small glass jar down and turns back heading into the kitchen. ' I think I have found something you will like.' At this the cat perks up stalling his bath to see what his pet has to offer him. Smuggly Eva sets the jar down in front of him for inspection. That little black nose stretches forward and sniffs just before his body lurches upright and begins to coils around the jar lovingly. Eva can't help but giggle as she moves to her cabinets pulling down a shallow bowl. ' I thought that would agree with you. Seems you and I have something in common after all..expensive taste.' Setting the bowl down Eva takes up the jar pulling it to her midriff to give it a hard twist to the left. The lid is difficult to work off but finally it comes free releasing the strong scent of ...you guessed it.....cavier. Immediately the purring grew loud enough that she could hear it loud and clear. Smiling softly at her new companion Eva dumps the fish eggs into the bowl and pushes it under his nose. Without hesitation he digs in with the delicate eating nature of a cat as she settles on a stool and just pets him while watching and pondering. ' What am I going to do with you? I travel all the time. So keeping you here is out of the question. A kennel just doesn't sit right with me. A sitter maybe? Of all the people to annoy late at night you had to chose me. ' Sighing she rests her head on an arm and simply stares as he eats. She knows her lifestyle will not be suitable for keeping him, yet she knows she likes the annoying beast. Wrinkling her nose she scruffs up his fur and rasies from her perch to find something of her own to eat as she thinks on what to do. A little while later she is sitting on the den couch, feet curled up beside her with that same cat in her lap as she talks on the phone to an unknown voice on the other side. ' Dr. Micheal's Animal Clinic.' ' Hello. I need to know if you are accepting new patients?' She feels odd calling a cat a patient, but it is an animal clinic after all. ' Yes ma'am we are. ' ' Terrific. I have a cat here who needs his shots and a..' Eva turns her eyes to the cat who is looking at her curiously. Immediately she feels almost guilty for what she is about to say. Turning away from him slightly she cups the voice end of the phone and whispers into it. ' Nuetering.' The lady on the other end chuckles slightly having known what just transpired. ' Yes, ma'am we can do that. Let me see when we have an opening...' Eva sits patiently, glancing at her new friend apprehensively as he stares at like he knows what she just said. The guilt gets to her. Covering the phone with her hand she looks at him pointedly muttering a bit. ' Look if you plan to stay here, this has to be done. Lords knows I don't need anyone coming after me for you sewing your wild oats.' ' Ma'am?' The tom gives her a whithering stare as he leaps off the couch to prowl around the house. He hadn't explored yet, and this seemed like a good time to do so. ' Ye..yes. I am here.' ' We have an opening that opened this afternoon at 3 pm. Will you be able to bring him in at that time?' That was fast. ' Yes ma'am I will.' ' Great. Now I just need your name, the name of your cat and a general description.' ' Eva VanLorne and his name is..' Wait. She hasn't given him a name yet. Wrinkling her brow she peers over the couch to look at him as he sniffs at one of her plants. What should his name be? Annoyance? No, too obvious. Pest? No, though it did fit honestly. Johnny's parents should have named him that. That is it! ' Ma'am? The Cat?' ' Oh...My apologies. His name is Johnny B. J.B. for short. He is pure silver.' Suddenly the thought of having him nuetered didn't make her feel so guilty. What better way to deal with her angst against Johnny Borealis than to have the cat she named after him nuetered. It is almost...poetic. Chuckling silently she listens to the instructions given by the woman as to how to get there and nods. ' Thank you. We will be there.' The phone is hung up as she turns on the couch kneeling, and leaning on the back of it to gaze at J.B. ' You have a name now big boy. It is Johnny B. J.B for short. What you think about that?' J.B merely lifts his head and gives her a look she could only interpret as a ' I should have known' look. Laughing lightly Eva climbs off the couch and heads for the bedroom to dress for the day. She has things to do before the afternoon. One of those things is cleaning out the attic, something she has put off for years. It held too many objects that held many memories of her life..both good and bad...mostly bad. ' Come J.B. You can help me decide what to keep and what to toss. Then later on you and I are taking a trip.' Curious, he follows her, this time staying out from beneath her feet. Perhaps this J.B was smarter than the one she knew in GCW? Time would only tell.
