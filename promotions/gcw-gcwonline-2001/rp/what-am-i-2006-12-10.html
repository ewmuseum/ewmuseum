What am I?

10 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/10/06 ~Freeman, South Dakota~ ~Broken Spoke Tavern~ Steven Matoskah looked around as he got out of the old Jeep Cherokee that he drove around. His two friends looked around also, as if they were expecting something bad to happen. ?I don't like this man...? Steven waved it off. He didn't care. This was their chance to start something that would eventually come to pass as their rise to power. He looked at his other companion as if to warn him about speaking against what they're doing. He didn't. The trio entered the tavern and drew the attention of about ten or so white patrons. It wasn't common for residents from the rez to be this far outside. And they usually didn't show up at as clich’d a redneck bar in the middle of cowboy country as you can get outside of Texas and Oklahoma. Steven walked up to the bar. He wanted to openly just say what he was here for, but a few seconds passed as he looked at what was on the Television. GCW's WorldWide from last week. He watched as his cousin was there in the ring, making like a fucking acrobat and then eventually helping her impromptu partner get the pin over the others. He pulled up a load of snot from his sinuses and tossed it at the TV leaving a large smear on the screen. ?Hey, asshole, you going to clean that up?? Steven just glared at the guy and made it known that he was not going to listen to this man. He was better then him, and he had the physical backup if he needed it. And he'd not have his constitutional right to protest Dani getting anything resembling recognition to this man. He's probably cleaned up piss and shit from the floor, he could clean up some nose butter from a TV screen. ?I'm here for a meeting with Ms. Layla.? The bartender nodded, even though he didn't look to happy about it, to a couple of guys who were watching one of the doors. They pulled back and opened the door that swung outwards. The three walked in, looking like caged rats as they did. The white presence in the room was a bit much for these racists from the Sioux Nation. But this is where the woman had said to meet her employer, so this is where he was going to go. They stepped into the room and looked around at the rag tag band that had congregated there. One guy was quite obviously Native, but his hair was a stark white, and he had facial hair, and a lot of it. Another couple looked pretty normal. But two others in the room drew Steven's attention more. The woman, he'd seen her a few times. Layla was her name. And she was gorgeous as usual. Long black hair, light skin, but she did look native. He'd asked her before and she said she was more native then he was. The one thing that he couldn't believe was how much she had come to be in his dreams at night. Dreams that Terra could never know about. ?Well, nice to see you guys finally made it.? She mocked him, and he took it. But then again, she wasn't as white looking at the woman most compared her to that he found. One of the people who wrestled with Dani's group in her bullshit job as a wrestler, Dawn McFarlane. But then you had the main person in this room. Young looking. Very Sioux looking. Long silky black hair. A man who looked like he had power. His suit was perfect, and it showed he had a lot of money. A man who looked a lot like someone who Steven had grown up respecting, a man he knew only as Jaymes. Steven didn't say a word about the resemblance, but the man showed he knew what he was thinking. ?Jaymes is a relative of mine. The last I spoke to him, he said very good things about you.? It took Steven off guard to hear that. Layla then spoke up to him, walking over and touching her hand to his face as she did the last time they had met in private. Blood instantly rushed to places it didn't need to be at for a business meeting. ?He spoke very highly of you indeed Steven. This is part of the reason why we are doing this. To help you reach the potential that he had been training you for. Now, sit and let's all talk about your destiny.? Steven sat down and looked up at her. His eyes didn't go too far above her chest though. ?Being on the council?? The sioux man leaned forward in his seat. ?Council? That's only a start. As far as I'm concerned, one day you could very well be Chief.? Steven smiled wide at hearing those words... ~Monumento Nacional de Santa Cruz del Valle de los Caidos~ ~Outside of Madrid, Spain~ The meeting was set. And she was here for it. A big church outside of Madrid, but not the one that Ishram himself used as his base of operations. This one was something else all together. Under Ishram chapel, there was an intricate series of tunnels and rooms to hide the operations center of the Order from the rest of the world. Under this was was 40,000 bodies of people who had fought in the Spanish Civil war. Danielle paced nervously as she watched the clock on her cell phone, waiting for the time it was supposed to begin. She and Josh were still outside the main building in what they called the explanada. In front of them the two sides of the monument's basilica spread out like open arms. Above it all a huge stone cross came out of the building, carved from the very mountain it all sat upon. She didn't know who she was meeting with, and the man who set it up for her for some reason she had a slightly hard time trusting. After all, Ian Childress... he was only a half breed but yet he was her only chance to figure out what was going on with her. And he was the one that HE had chosen to carry his spirit in this world, and he was her guiding light right now. She could trust Ian Childress because he had the spirit of the fallen angel, Suriel inside of him. She could see him, every time she looked at Ian. Suriel was taller, more defined, and for lack of a better term, more beautiful then Childress. Then again, Childress had taken his body to extremes long ago, with his tattoos and piercings. She could handle the tattoos, she had some herself, and Josh had even more. But the self mutilation was something she wasn't into. But she needed answers, and they were here to finally gain some. Right now she was lost, she didn't recognize herself in the mirror half the time. She had many questions of this person she was going to meet with. Ones that she knew only he would have some answers for. She was taking Josh along not for support but as a tool. His empathic abilities allowed her to have an upper edge. No one would be able to hide in the room even if they were not wanting to be found. She had made it known to him this was not something he was in charge of but she was, it might have been the order that set this up but she wasn't order, she was a lab rat to them. A lab rat that was getting tired of being watched, with no questions that she had being answered. Danielle looked once more at her phone then looked at Josh, he sat quietly one a bench so as to not interrupt her thoughts. He was one of her biggest questions, after her out burst at the Keeper's house, she wondered if there was a chance she would end up hurting him one day, or losing him. And what about their children if they ever had any what would be their fates? She wanted what was best for everyone not only just herself. As she stood there thinking her hand went to her neck, she fingered the bones that now where apart of her choker. Something she only took off for the short periods of time that she was in the ring, other wise she wore it night and day. They gave her comfort and in some way a feeling of greater control of herself. She didn't long as much to be with him, was far as she could tell she didn't night travel anymore either. But she really needed to know what was going to happen. ?You ready, baby?? Josh stood up and looked towards the building. She looked at the phone just as it started to ring with the sound of her usual ring tone, it was set as an alarm. She took Josh's hand and they walked towards the door. It didn't take them long as they walked with a purpose. They opened the door, letting a few other tourists walk past them and then stepped in. Dani had to take a few steps back as soon as she was in. Immediately within the doors were two statues, angels, with two wings like hers, holding swords in their hands. The statues were made out of metal, polished and shining in the sun as it came in through the doors. ?Now I know why he picked here. Where did he say to meet?? Josh took a moment to remember. ?Near the tomb of Fransisco Franco, the man who led the revolt and then had this place build.? They started walking down the hallway, heading into the mountain, into the crypt of the fallen soldiers here in the ?Valley of the Fallen? as it's called. Josh noticed one person standing out from the others. A man who looked like he could have been a Spanish or French male model. Lush blonde hair on his head, it looked natural. But one thing set it apart. Josh couldn't read him at all. No emotions, nothing. He tapped his hand on Dani's hip as they walked along holding hands and then nodded his head in the direction of the man. Dani saw him and nodded to Josh. She had him thanks to Josh's talents, but now they had to meet. She walked up towards the tomb of the former Spanish Dictator and took in the Mosaic on the ceiling above it. The artwork depicted what could only be the Spanish people paying homage to the Christ, and the Virgin Mary as Angels were all around it all. She felt him come up upon her as he then whispered in her right ear, on the side that Josh wasn't on. ?Don't I look good up there? I'm the angel to his right with only the skirt covering what would have made them blush with the great pecs and perfect hair.? Dani frowned at him, he was a narcissist, a follower of the sin of Pride. She quickly changed it to set the tone for their meeting. ?They didn't do you justice. Must have been a male painter.? She smiled wide at him, playing his game. ?Ah, a sly one. You are your grand father's heir to being a smart ass, aren't you? I have heard many murmurs and rumors coming form the new world about you Sirius Star. Come, let us take this to someplace a bit more private.? She didn't follow him right away as he walked off. ?You have me at a disadvantage sir, so I can't really take you up on your offer yet. You know so much about me, yet I don't even know who you are.? He stopped and looked at Josh who was most assuredly following her in from the look on his face. ?This meeting is between me and you.? Dani looked at Josh. ?I have taken him as a husband and partner. I go in, he does too. And if there would be anything happen to him, you can rest assured that I would ensure that you would regret it.? The man's features softened at that threat. Dani had something here, something that she didn't even realize. Then the man answered her, but in a way that Josh couldn't understand. Dani knew he'd go that route, and had actually been hoping for it. He spoke to her in the tongue that all Angels know, the true form of speech of heaven. ?Come then, we have things to talk about. I grew weary of war a long time ago, and I'm not about to let Astaroth start a new one.? Dani stopped upon hearing the true name of her enemy. All the angel in front of her did was point up to mosaic and other things about. They were in a church. A church that had been blessed by Pope John XXIII and made an official basilica of the Roman Catholic Church. They were on holy ground and thus... safe. ?Very well.? She motioned for Josh to follow her and they took a position behind him as they headed off to one of the more private areas, an are of tombs that were of people who had very few families. It was gated off, and the angel obviously had been here for a while, for he unlocked the gate and then let them in. He did not relatch the lock though, but did shut the gate. As soon as they were inside he looked at Josh. ?I know of your position, but you are a son of man. I will have words with your wife, but you will not understand them. It is best if you don't in some cases. Are you fine with this?? Josh just nodded. ?Good, mortals do not need to hear some things.? He stopped talking in the human voice and then turned to Dani and spoke in Angelic. ?Now I will introduce myself... I am Mephistopheles, you can just call me Mephisto.? She hesitated. ?Mephisto, as in the tempter of Faust?? He chuckled. ?Ah, my dear Johan. He was such a catch, it's just too bad he figured a way out of it. He was so smart.? ?Johan?? He grinned. ?Johan Gorge Faust, his real name. But don't let that bother you. I've been told by Ishram, who kindly gives me some protection here, that you have questions about your nature, Sirius. So ask your questions. I'm bound by my word to answer them for you.? She nodded. ?Fine, then we'll start off with WHAT AM I?? She had to yell the last part to him. He jumped as he could see the arcs go across her fingers and down her long hair as her new traits after taking the heart of the angel Baraqiel started to show. He took a breathe and then answered her. ?You are a novelty. A strange occurrence that someone had prophesied before. You were born on the earth to a human man and woman, the man having been in the blood line of a Gibborim. A genetic trait that humans have for seven generation throwbacks has made you exactly what your family was that many generations ago, that being a Virtue. A member of the second choir of angels in the second tier. But you're an anomaly also, as you are neither aligned with Mikhail or Sammael's forces.? ?A virtue? Second choir? Second Tier? Mikhail? Sammael?? ?A virtue, you are supposed to be in charge of an aspect of the world. But you don't have one. Which makes you even more of an anomaly. Your grandfather is in charge of the stars and their movements. And the angelic choirs are split up into three tiers. The lowest tier are comprised of Angels, Archangels and Principalities. Angels follow people, Archangels are a bit more open. Principalities are Angels that look over entire countries. The second tier are Powers, Virtues and Dominions. They actually control things that happen in nature. The third tier are the assholes all around. Thrones, Cherubs and Seraphs. Astarosh is a seraph. They are as powerful as you get in heaven without being the big man himself. Mikhail, or Micheal is a seraph and the captain of the holy army. Sammael you know more likely as Lucifer. Sammael is his real name. He was also a seraph. It seems the nature of 'absolute power' is a true statement even in most of my own kind. You could think of it as a phone dial. 9 is regular angels, and 1 is a seraph. The closer you get to a 1, the more power they have.? She nodded. ?What are you?? He didn't smile. ?I am an archangel.? ?Isn't Michael also called an Archangel, there are only supposed to be four of them.? ?Bah, the Jews only recognize seven, the catholics four... I'm an archangel. I know this. There are quite a few of us. Uriel is a seraph, Michael a seraph. Gabriel and Raphael are both Cherubs. Figures humans would fuck it up.? She giggled a bit. ?So, tell me...? ?Oh, I am. I already know two of your questions from looking at your eyes.? She glared at him. ?You and your reproductive cycle. You can have children with him. It's not problem unless they come out as giants. Then you might have a visit by Michael himself to kill them. But we've been having children for years without making the same mistake Semyaza and Azazel made before they caused the flood. I've got two sons right now, myself. They just don't know that they're father is the best tempter outside of heaven and the Vatican themselves.? She hestiated, but he continued. ?As for his status of being mortal. I guess you need to figure out how you could make him immortal.? She snapped up to him. ?How?? ?Give it to him.? ?What?? ?Look, I'm an archangel as I told you. I'm third tier, second choir. You're a tier ahead of me by birth. I've been able to make people immortal, rich, and powerful. Don't think you couldn't either. All I ask for is their soul, which is funny.? Her head cocked to the side. ?Why is it funny?? ?Because It's funny to see people give that which they don't own so quickly. Especially when if they read that book they hold so dear, there is no way I could claim it. But in the end, if I can get them to denounce everything when kingdom come finally does... then I will laugh. However, less about me. More about you.? She inhaled sharply as she then went to ask him more. But she had a lot more to ask. And hopefully this person had at least some of the answers. ~To Be Continued~
