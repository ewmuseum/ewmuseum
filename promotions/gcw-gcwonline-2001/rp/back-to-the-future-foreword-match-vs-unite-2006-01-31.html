Date: 01/31 Saturday, Jan 28th 8:34 AM, EST Miami Florida --- 1. Pleasant Dreams [To the naked eye, This vast paradise had no end. It stretched for miles around in every direction. North, South, East, West.. All covered in sugary delicacies disguised as common things, such as Grass, Water, and Furniture. It was like a scene out of Willy Wonka. But not the one with Gene Wilder, the one with that faggy guy. Not that I have anything against people who choose to live a homosexual lifestyle, of course. As Johnny Depp led the ugly replacement children through the room of edibles, a primal scream was heard. Launching himself out of the Great Glass Elevator in a dead run was none other than One Half of GCW's favorite duo Team Baked, Mooch. Butcher knife in hand he pounces on Depp, causing the Children to scream in terror. Since this is a G rated movie, we don't -see- the shanking, but we do see all the children's individual faces as they're sprayed with blood. Waking, he shot up. Drenched in cold sweat and breathing heavily, Mooch looked around the messy apartment. Bammy sits watching 'Girls Gone Wild' ads on Comedy Central.] Bammy: Dude, what happened..? Mooch: I Just had.. the.. BEST DREAM EVER! Bammy: The one where you stab Johnny Depp? Mooch: Yep. Bammy: I've had similar journeys into dream land, but they involved Tommy Lee. Mooch: You hate Tommy Lee? Bammy: Penis Envy. [Mooch rises to his feet, crushed doritos and food wrappers falling off of his Black Towlie hoodie. He takes a seat next to Bammy who begins digging under the stained couch cushion, looking for their stash.] Bammy: .. Did you move the green, Dude? Mooch: No? [With a smirk on his face that tells us he's discovered the location of the ziploc baggy, Bammy pulls his arm up to reveal the goods. .. Only for both men to scream in terror.. at the sight of the empty bag in front of their eyes.] BAMMY: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! MOOCH: ERAGHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! [Both men let out blood curdling screams. The thought of being sober from more than an hour is enough to justify their sudden insanity. They begin to trash the apartment, which is no simple thing to do in an apartment so.. trashy? Flipping over couch cushions, chairs, do no good. They're dry. No more Weed in the entire house. No Money. No Prospect of blazing in the near future.] Mooch: THIS CAN'T BE HAPPENING TO ME! Bammy: Calm Down Man, Calm.. [Mooch grabs Bammy by the collar and throws him against the wall.] Mooch: I CAN'T CALM DOWN! I'M DEPENDANT YOU IGNORAMUS! DID YOU HEAR THE SIZE OF THE WORD I JUST USED?! MY BRAIN CELLS ARE COMING BAAAAACK!!!!!!! [Before any more panic can commence, Bammy comes up with a solution. He attempts to convey his ideas in a calm manner whilst being held up by the neck against a wall.] Bammy: Dude, what about the Internet? Mooch: Eh? Bammy: I've heard tell of a thing.. in a far away land.. called the internet. It's a mass information source, and can tell you anything you want to know. If only we could find the secret.. Mooch: I see you haven't come down all the way? Bammy: Nope. Mooch: Anyone with a PC can access the internet you douche. You can use it free at the Library. [Without anymore debate the two drop everything and exit the messy apartment, not even closing the door fully in their rush..] --- 2. Welcome to the Miami Library [It was only her second day on the job, but Cherie Swanson hadn't seen anything too 'out there' during her first two runs through the gauntlet known as the local library. Her superiors had all informed her of the strange characters that populated this particular area, but she dismissed the notions as some light hazing of the new girl. That was until this morning. As he entered the main corridor to hang the open sign in the large glass doorway, she saw two of the most.. stereotypical stoners -ever-. They sat on a stone bench outside the doors, seemingly engaged in a full on verbal battle. Scared for her safety she puts the sign up, turns on her heel, and power walks back towards the receptionist desk. With speed only super wrestling stoners have, they notice the open sign instantly and spring into action, sprinting to the door. Before Cherie Swanson can get anywhere near the safety of her desk, she's bombarded.] Mooch: Where do you keep the PC's? Bammy: INTERNET NOW PLEASE! [Struck with terror she holds a shaky finger in the direction of the PC Bank near the corner. Our hero's scurry off in that direction, ignoring the Librarian as she turns her brisk walk into a run back to her seat..] Bammy: Type in Google.. Mooch: Let me turn it on first, Butt plug. [Powering up the PC, Mooch begins typing. He actually seems to have some skill at it, which only means he's too sober. The duo realizes this and exchange a glance of desperation.] Mooch: Google.. 'How to Get High at home'.. that'll work. Bammy: .. Two Million Results?! Mooch: They're all like that. Remember that secretary job I got a couple years ago in Seattle? This is all I did, search things, watch porn, smoke in the break room and etc etc . [A Gleeful Bammy begins reading.] Bammy: CNN.com, the horrors of getting high.. no.. Glade.com, stop sniffing the plugins you fucking punks.. no.. 'The Bongslinger', How do get High when you run dry!.. THAT ONE! QUICK. [Mooch quickly complies and brings up 'Bongslinger.com'. The website is an homage to all things Green. A haven for people with similar interests to our Hero's. How to Grow it, How to smoke it, Joint Etiquette, Experience Stories, methods of ingestion.. and of course the section M and B want, the rarely ventured to 'alternatives.' With a click the mouse, Mooch navigates his way to a listing of experiences Bongslinger users have had and documented over the years.] Bammy: 'Green Tea.. WORKS LIKE A DREAM!!!1!!11!!!' Try that! [Click.] SUBMITTED BY: Token Thomas Tidsworth Date: 07/98 Comments: HEEEEEE!@~! now i no u aren't goin' 2 believe this but it totally works. last week my mom found my stash in my laundry basket and confiscated it. leaving me with absolutely no resources. i quickly attempted to smoke various things in hopes of achieving the buzz i so needed, and did i ever find it! i searched the cupboards and saw my sisters green tea she drinks for her yoga class. i opened a package and packed a bowl, smoking it. sure i got a little sick at first but it went away and i was buzz for a good 15 mins!! u need 2 try it! [Mooch exits the page.] Mooch: No way. Bammy: Agreed.. how about.. 'Nail Polish Remover and YOU! - it rulzez!' [Click.] SUBMITTED BY: Jimmy Hash Date: 08/01 Comments: totally 100% true stoners honor!! so i ran out of weed the other night cuz me and my girlfriend smoked it. i needed something the next night so i raided the medacine cabinet. i saw nail polisher remover and heard that could give u a high righ? so i started sniffing it. after about a half an our i was dizzy and completely wasted! [Mooch Exits.] Mooch: That sucks. Bammy: Agreed, freak.. Hmm.. 'I don't have a cough.. But I like my Nyquil!' that one. [Click.] SUBMITTED BY: JAY JOHNSON ESQUIRE. Date: 10/05 Comments: Read. [The two rise from their seats and run towards their local Target store.] --- 3. Back to the Future [We rejoin our hero's quest as they exit Target, plastic bag in hands. They take a seat on a bench and wrestle the bottles out of the bag, each unscrewing the cap. They toast eachother..] Mooch: One Bammy: Two Mooch: Three [CHUG.] Bammy: *GLUG* Mooch: *GRAWP* [They finish, and must to their chagrin.. nothing.] Bammy: I feel fine. Mooch: Me too man. Bammy: What a WASTE! WE COULD HAVE GONE TO TACO BELL. Mooch: I'm pissed. [The two are cut off by a sound.] *TSH-TSH.. TSH TSH.. TSH HHHHHHHHHHHHH* [Materializing in mid-air is a Delorean. Flashing lights and all it comes to a stop in front of our heroes. With a hissing sound the door rises, smoke billowing out as a man with white hair in a lab coat jumps out. It's obviously Doc Brown, and if you haven't seen Back to the Future I'd stop reading and disregard part II..] DOC BROWN: MOOCH! BAMMY! .. WE HAVE TO GO BACK TO THE FUTURE! To Be Continued.
