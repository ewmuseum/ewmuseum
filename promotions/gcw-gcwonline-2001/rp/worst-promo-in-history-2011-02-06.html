Worst Promo... in history...

6 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/06/11 The sounds of mongolian drums echo the small sound stage as we fade in from a blacked screen... Colors everywhere, psychedelic excess as the drums grow louder and louder, louder and louder. A familiar voice, chanting comes from off screen. Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, Ungah... Slowly, from off the right side of the stationary camera, a man emerges. Wearing almost a technicolor dreamcoat, the mysterious man makes his way across camera, as he continues to chant. Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, Ungah... He disappears off the left side of the screen, but the chanting continues, fading off, but then slowly coming back, almost in tune with the heavy drums. Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, Ungah... The mysterious psychedelic hooded man makes his way back on screen. He slowly turns towards the camera. Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, Ungah, UNGAH, UNGAH, UNGAHHH, UNGAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHH!!!! The mysterious man flips open his cape and drops it to the floor, and with crazed eyes and facepaint and his trademark boots actually painted across his chest. Jimi Thorne, the Highway Child, snarls his face, with verbose intensity. Jimi Thorne: THE ULTIMATE JIMI HAS ARRIVED!!! I HAVE CONSULTED THE SAGES OF TIME AND WISDOM, AND THIS TIME IS INFINTATELY FINITE!!! YOU SEE GCW... IN GRAND FASHION FROM THE LIVING ROCK OF DESTINY........................... THE SAINTS HAVE ARRIVED!!! AND LO, IT WAS PERSCRIBED... THE SAINTS ARE FROM THE CELESTIAL KINGDOMS OF ALPHA CENTURIOUS AND WE ARE HERE FOR ONE PURPOSE... TO ANNIHILATE THE AMPHIBIOUS OVERLORDS AND UNDERLINGS OF GLOBAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING... MAY YET SO IT CAN TAKE ITS PLACE... THE THE SCHEME OF THE MILKY WAY!!! AND ON SUCH A WEEK WHERE WE LOOK TO THE GREAT WARS OF YOUR HUMAN ERA OF "MONDAY!!!" THE ULTIMATE JIMI WAS DESTINED TO APPEAR, TO HELP HIS COMRADE IN THE STRUGGLE AGAINST THE AMPHIBOS POISON THAT INFECTS THIS GLOBAL WORLD!!! Jimi turns around and raises his hands up to the heavens, revealing another pair of psychedelic cowboys boots painted on his back. Jimi Thorne: AND FOR YOU KJ NEWFROGLAND AND THEOLAMANDER PALMER... THE FIRST SALVOS FROM THE SAINTS INTERGALATIC WARSHIP IS FIRED AND WITH THE BLADED BEAMS, YOU WILL FALL, AND YOUR KIND WILL FOLLOW SHORT-LY!!! MARK MY WORDS... ON MASTADON-BACK... THE SAINTS ARE MARCHING IN... OH YES, THE SAINTS ARE MARCHING IN!!! [b][i]To the beat of those same mongolian drums that have been playing throughout, "The Ultimate Jimi" turns around and marches off stage.[/i][/b] Jimi Thorne: OH THE SAINTS GO MARCHING IN!!! OH THE SAINTS............. Off Camera Director: CUT! Jimi Thorne: *Hugggghhhh* God... that was fucking stupid... Jimi Thorne and the rest of the cast chuckle and laugh as the scene fades to black.
