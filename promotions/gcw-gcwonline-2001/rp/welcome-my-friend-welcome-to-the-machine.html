Date: 7/28/2007-8/3/2007 .::Disclaimer- Contains Strong Language and Violence::. .::Viewer Discretion Advised::. ---- "For every moment of triumph, for every instance of beauty, many souls must be trampled." ~Hunter S. Thompson ---- Saturday, July 28, 2007 0105 hours, local time backstage, Adelaide Entertainment Center: Adelaide, AU Shawn Porter. I shoulda been walking backstage with my head held high, a smile on my face and the fucking Television title belt over my shoulder. Instead, I was barely walking at all, head killing me, bruised gut and nothing more than a heavy feeling on my shoulders where MY gold should have been. Asshole. What the hell? I mean, seriously. I did NOT ask for this match, Porter. I didn’t get out there and demand a title shot, and it’s not my fucking fault that I’m simply better than you. Shit, buddy; I can’t help it that the powers that be around here recognize talent when they see it. Let me tell you something, and I’ll be sure to use SMALL words so your puny brain will get this: you picked the wrong man to fuck with. You don’t fucking know me, punk. Let’s get this shit straight. You’re a small time puke who thinks he’s got it all. You think you’re a made man, when you’re a goddamn nobody. A poetry writing, tea-sucking faggot. Let me tell you something about me, Porter. I don’t like to lose. I’m sure that makes me no different than the next guy in that locker room. I mean, honestly, who likes to lose? The thing that pisses me off the most is getting screwed out of a win by some asshole with an axe to grind. Yeah, I’m pointing a finger at you, douche. Porter. Trained so hard my hands bled, my muscles ached for days. All for nothing. Took that bastard to the limit, and walked away a loser. That hurt my pride, my ego, whatever. The match was a fucking joke. Guess whose fault that is? Yours, bitch. Makes me sick, just another meaningless feud with some jackass who thinks he can best me. I’m getting tired of this endless routine; the continual parade of idiots with delusions of grandeur who want to become King of Shit Mountain by taking me out. Not gonna happen, Porter. Only thing you managed to do was piss me off. To borrow and horribly misuse a pop culture phrase: you won’t like me when I’m angry. ---- The baleful glow of the solitary exit sign above the door reflected off the Wild Turkey bottle as he brought it to his lips. Probably not a good choice, given the fact that he was flirting with a concussion from the unexpected impact of Shawn Porter’s finishing move. He didn’t care. Darkness filled every corner of the room, an impenetrable blackness. In the furthest corner, there was a hiss, and a flicker of a deep crimson. A deep-throated rumble accompanied the pulsing of the ruddy light, bringing to mind the belly of a beast. A medieval scaly lizard of the fire-breathing sort. There was a scrape in the otherwise ominous silence, and then a telltale snap as Jackson clicked open the lid of his Zippo lighter, producing a flame. He brought the light towards his face, dragging the smoke deep into his lungs as he lit it slowly. The orange glow reflected in his eyes, transforming his hard features into something almost demonic. A grim smile graced his lips as retreated into the shadows again, finding more comfort in the darkness. Dressed all in black, he seemed a part of the darkness itself. A rusted metal grating covered the floor, warped with time and age, resting directly beneath the cone of crimson light spilling from the sign. It rattled, and moved under the weight as Jackson’s footsteps drew closer one by one. He moved into the feeble illumination, holding a black folding chair in one hand and the half full bottle of whiskey in the other. Dressed all in black, from eyebrows to combat booted feet, he appeared as though he’d materialized from the thick shadows. He opened the chair slowly, and then straddled it, leaning his arms across the back. For a moment he simply sat there, peering into the darkness. He grunted softly, almost rousing himself from thought. His voice was a hoarse rasp, the perfect accompaniment for booze and cigarettes. "Do I look like a fuckin? pi’ata?" He raised the bottle, tilting it away from himself as though toasting Porter, "heh, you’re a real piece of work, Porter. I don’t know who the fuck you are, or why in the hell you decided to insert yourself in my business?" he trailed off, his eyes drawn to the blood that still trickled sluggishly from his torn knuckles. It glimmered in the light, like precious rubies. Setting the bottle between his legs, he brought one hand closer to his face, plucking a shard of glass from between the pointer and index fingers. He flexed the hand, a muscle twitch in his jaw the only outward sign of pain. "Punched a mirror," he muttered, inclining his head in the direction of the wreckage. There on the wall, a thousand hellish versions of himself watched, twisted and warped by the cracks that radiated from the center of the glass. Jackson shrugged one shoulder, his voice soft, "pain. You see, for me it’s all about pain. I feed on it, thrive on it. So what, I broke the looking-glass? you know what? It wasn’t the first, sure as hell won’t be the last. I’ve punched a lot of things in my life. People, inanimate objects? nothing’s safe from my wrath. A friendly warning, I guess. Don’t push my buttons? don’t piss me off." A chuckle escaped his lips, the sound as dark as the room. "Ah, what the hell am I thinking? You’re not gonna get this? it’s like talking to a goddamn retarded child." A sly smirk crossed his lips, and he banished the expression with the press of the cool glass bottle. The Wild Turkey burned a path down his throat, the familiar warmth setting his insides on fire. Welcome, that feeling was after all this time. An impulse rose in the back of his mind, sitting here nursing the hurt. He wanted to seek out Porter’s hotel, pull out his P5 and blow the bastard’s fucking head off. He shook his head slowly, exhaling sharply. He wouldn’t, of course, but there was a time, up until quite recently that he might have. That’s what scared him. That potential was still there, he was still nitroglycerine, but without a fuse. Still unstable. His burning eyes took in everything in a glance, rimmed with the evidence of too many sleepless nights, and balanced on a jaw line as sharp as razorblades. But his eyes were bright, keenly intelligent. The eyes of a predator. A sigh emphasized the silence as he swiveled that baleful gaze back towards the camera, plucking a fresh cigarette from behind his ear. He lit this with the remains of the previous one, dropping the butt and grinding it out beneath the heel of his jackboot. "Stick a cork in it, Porter," he said with a smirk, his voice low and gruff, "I will not hear your useless chatter. Really that’s all it is. Bravado to cover the fear that I can see in your eyes. By now you know, you know you’ve gone and fucked yourself. Start digging, my friend. Your grave, I mean." Inhale. Exhale. The neutral mask slipped back into place. Only the vibration of bass in his voice betrayed the current of fury running beneath the surface. "I have to tell you, I’m inclined to walk away from this match vaguely insulted. You don’t know shit about me, kid. You don’t understand the first thing about what you’re about to unleash. You pick at the scab; not knowing what sort of poison lurks beneath. Foolishness, but then I expected nothing less of you. Hell, I almost expected this entire match to become one massive clusterfuck without the reach-around. Territorial pissings, and I know? oh, I know? I’m the new kid on the block. You don’t have to drive that point home, Porter." Another listless shrug, more extended silence. Contemplation as he brought the bottle to his lips again, draining the contents. He rolled his shoulders, tilting his head from side to side, working the tension from his neck. His hands dangled over the back of the chair, only the right moving up to his lips with automatic motions as he took another haul from the cigarette. "So, bring on the words, bring on the bullshit I can only expect from a cowardly shit like you. Hide behind that bravado. Go nuts, man. You talk about pain, and what you want to do to me inside that ring. Talk about how you deserved that title match before me? blah, blah? shut the fuck up. You want to make me hurt, to make me bleed, sure? they all do. You can’t make me feel, kid. Bring it all, throw it all at me and leave me broken and bleeding. Watch me get up, and ask for more. That’s why they call me the machine. I am made of scars, years of abuse has taught me how to take it all without a sound." The smile was fleeting, cold and deadly as it flashed across his lips. Another inhale, glowing cinders reflected in his dark eyes for just a moment as though the very fires of hell were trapped in those unreadable windows to his soul. "Let me tell you what hurts, nigga. The part of me that screams non-stop, yet escapes words. The shrieking horror inside me that begs for release. This is why they fear me. It’s this thing that keeps me in solitude, away from the others. No one can ever truly know me; I will never understand anyone. Not on that level. See, I understand you just fine, knowledge is power? but I do not feel like you do. It is this unbridled rage that makes me do the things I do. I have to keep moving, endlessly. To stop is to give it power. That’s my reality, my grim truth. Do you understand now? Can you see?" His breathing grew rapid as the fantasies played out in his mind, breaking Shawn Porter, smearing his blood around the ring. Painting his face with the warm entrails like a goddamn savage. Something elemental and instinctual. His fist clenched, knuckles white as the words continued to pour forth. "This is why I have chosen this business. Why I have become an alien, living on the road. I can’t stop seeing, can’t stop being. I know if I stopped, it would take me. Cull me from the herd like a hungry predator, and devour me. I would fall apart. No, my end will come in that ring, between those ropes. Someday soon, but not today, not at your hands. All I can do is keep on, keeping on. Get the poison out. These days it fills so fast, and then it’s boiling over. I’m hardwired, the angry man. Fuck yeah, tripwires in my veins, nitro in my soul. Just need that one little jolt in the wrong direction. BLAM! All I can do is break things, and they label me an asshole. Psycho. Unstable. What a joke." Inhale. Exhale. Down to the filter now. He dropped it, grinding it out beneath his boot, the paper and bits of tobacco dropping between the holes in the grating. Lost forever in oblivion. "You don’t know me, Porter. No one will ever know me..." His voice was low, haunted and filled with anguish as he bowed his head, clenching both fists now. Breathing slowly. Slowly. Inhale. Exhale. Repeat. ---- Friday, August 3, 2007 0333 hours, local time Sydney, AU I want to break the chair I sat in over your head. I want to grind your face into a bloody pulp along the metal grating. I want you to do the same to me. Quid pro quo. I want to feel your pain. The more pain I take, the stronger I become. Strength is everything to me- all-important. I am not a physical powerhouse; I am no Goliath. I do not want you to cower before me. No, I want your fists. I want to feel the impact. Hit me. Your pathetic attempts at attacking me intellectually, if that’s what you were intending, do nothing but reveal your fear. I can see it in your eyes. You fear me on a pure, instinctual animal level. That makes you mad because you know I can see your fear. You know you’re revealing it with every move you make; every nervous tic is a tell. You know I’m in control of this situation. That’s why you feign ignorance, holding on to your illusion of bliss. You know I won’t spare you, yet at the same time I care nothing for you as a person. To me, you’re another faceless entity. Grease for the gears of the machine. You know what you’re up against, even as your convictions scream with denials. You know, Porter. You fucking knew when you jumped in that ring. You knew precisely what you were doing; like some retarded kid on a dare, poking the hornet’s nest with a stick. You know that our little throw down will change EVERYTHING. Frostbite? You’re fucking kidding, right? Seriously. Right. Rocky Balboa you are not, my friend. Jesus. And they call me nuts? And then? wait, wait? I need to stop laughing for a minute. You wrote me a fucking poem? I appreciate the gesture, really I do, but you’re not my type, Liberace. I mean, shit? flowers, and you might have had a chance. But me? I’m not into the poetry- girly shit. What I didn’t hear was any talk about how you’re the underdog who will triumph, David toppling the Philistine giant. No, no, instead I hear all this shit about how you’re better than me? Dude, hook me up with whatever your shrink’s been handing out. My, you have quite the ego, don’t you? Here’s a deal, my friend. I’ll be all that’s bad and you can be all that’s good. Does that make you happy? Then you can be right all the time, and I would be wrong all the time. Seems reasonable, no? Try and keep up, my friend. Following these rules, then, you cannot try to change me, and I will respect the same and not change you. We will remain untouchable like this, polar opposites. Can you deal with that? No? Nah, I bet you don’t get that. Too highbrow for you. Wait, let me "street" it down for you. Nigga, ain’t no way you be walking outta that ring under your own power. I be bustin? caps all up in yo? jive ass. Werd. Ok, maybe that was a bit out of line. I’m not racist, and it normally bothers the shit out of me to stoop that low. For you, I’ll break that rule. Fuck. You deserve it, you little shit. Totally predictable. What. The. Fuck? A couple years ago, I found something strange in a bookstore. Kid you not, Porter. I have to wonder if someone stole it from you. The yellow and black format, one of the popular "For Dummies" books. This one is aptly titled "Wrestling Promos for Dummies". What? Seriously? I shit you not. Y’know what’s really funny? I was paging through this, and Porter, apparently, is a fucking genius. All the steps are right here, in black and white for the wrestling impaired. Your cookie cutter promo. First come out to the ring, either with or without a manager, to your standard ring entrance fanfare. Ok, so he skipped that, opting instead for the unorthodox doctor’s office setting. Next, select your target, and call them names like you’re in high school. Holy shit Sherlock, he did that too. Yeah, I heard my name in that mess, and you know what? It did piss me off. Not for the reason you think though, chumpstain. No, you pissed me off because you had the audacity to talk like this fuckin? belt is yours, and I’m the fucking has-been who wasted my shot. Back the fuck up there, Einstein. I’ve been here for a month? and my opportunity has PASSED ME BY? Excuse me? Listen up, fucktard, that’s now how this works. Who the fuck do you think you are? I have to assume, at this point, that frostbite is the least of your concerns. Y’know, what with the chairshot induced dementia you’re sporting over there. You’ve insulted me, and that doesn’t sit well. Like you, my ego doesn’t like that. I like to pretend that I am above it all. Most times I can convince myself. I urge you to check your misconceptions at the door. This sort of delusion is nothing short of torture. Heh, don’t look at me; I’m braiding my own noose now. I can get you thumbscrews cheap. You can do it at home, by yourself. It’s all the rage now, everyone’s doing it. Watching your endless pedantic rants is painful, but I endured. The hour of watching you stoke your ego, for fun and profit. Watching you work out in the meat locker, or whatever in the hell you were up to. Watching you talk some stunted, Kevin Smith-esque scripted shit to your doctor like he cares about your 5-week goal for GCW. The symbolism wasn’t lost on me, my friend, but I don’t care. Flattery gets you nowhere. By the end of the hour I was hallucinating about a world without you and your massive ego, about a world where you’ve been taken down a peg. Coming down was hard, but you have to sometime. That’s inevitable. I feel sorry for you, Porter. I feel sorry for the ones who won’t make it, for the ones I have left behind. Not everyone succeeds all the time, kid, and that makes me sad. I feel bad for bursting your bubble, really. Start mutilating yourself now, start the flagellation. Make it hurt now, so the pain will be less later. It’s gonna hurt. Learn to fear willfully, before I have to beat it into you. It’s easier on the ego. That way, you can pretend this was all your idea. Normally this sort of thing isn’t my bag. I’m not into this competition thing. Lately, I’ve been happier in the shadows than the limelight. It’s ironic, really, just how much of my former self I see in you, Porter. I used to be the asshole picking the fight just because I could. Chip on the shoulder. The world owed me a favor. Turned out the world owed me jack shit. Now, I’ve become the man of few words, the guy who only speaks to reveal something profound. Action Jackson, they called me. Tongue-in-cheek, of course. Truth is, it was fitting. I’m no silver-tongued devil. I am not a clever wordsmith. Words tire me. I try to use as few as possible now, for fear of repeating myself. I’m more in your face than words can express, like a nice sucker punch. Lately, I don’t cut the straight-laced promos. Nah, it’s not me anymore. I don’t take to the ring with the microphone and talk the shit. I prefer to exist in the real world now, where my actions mean more than my words. Wrestling is what I do, but it sure as hell isn’t what defines me. Peace of mind, my friend. Can’t say I didn’t warn you. Want some thumbscrews now? No? Have it your way, then. Ignorance is bliss. Hold onto the illusions as long as you can, buddy. Save all your anger, and all your hatred. I want everything you have, all of it, on the line. Let’s leave it all inside that ring. Man versus Machine. Are you ready to grease the gears, Porter? Are you ready to face your fears inside the machine? I was born ready.
