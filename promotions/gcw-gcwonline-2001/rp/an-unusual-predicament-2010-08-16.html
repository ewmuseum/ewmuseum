Date: 08/16/10 “Bloody good morning to ya,” a voice chirps from somewhere within my bedroom. I open a bleary eye to see Josh Bloom standing at the foot of my bed. That kid is honestly the only college kid I know who could look that cheerful at…holy shit seven in the morning. “I’m trying to recover from being choke slammed by a 300 pound Freddy Krueger wannabe,” I grumble and stubbornly close my eyes. “Your bad Irish accent is not helping. Go away!” I can’t see him, but I know Josh he’s probably grinning at me right now while flipping me off at the same time. “I would love to be in bed right now but as your personal assistant-“ “I only gave you that job because your uncle begged me so much I was worried he would try sucking my dick if I said no, “I interrupt. “And Dave only did that because your parents were tired of you spending your life playing video games. No one actually expected you to take it this seriously.” “Uncle Dave wouldn’t suck your dick,” Josh points out, “there’s no way its kosher after all the places it’s been!” I can’t help it, I laugh out loud at Josh’s antics. I grudgingly open my eyes again and stare at the ceiling; with a sigh I motion with my hand for Josh to get onto the reason he’s here. “You’ve got appointments out the ass today,” he says adopting a brisk businesslike tone that I’ve come to admire. The kid might look like your typical stereotypical skinny Jewish boy, but I can’t deny he’s on top of things. “Edgar’s worried about the numbers from last month, I told him to talk to Dave but you know he likes to have his face to face with the alleged real boss.” I snort, and Josh chuckles in agreement with me. I may own the three night clubs, but anyone with half a brain should know that Dave is the one who makes all the decisions regarding them. Which makes me wonder why Edgar hasn’t been fired yet. “Nancy called; she wants to know if you’re interested in dinner Friday night.” “Nancy is –“ “I told her you’re not available already. Hopefully she’ll get the hint and stop stalking you. Anyway, the other big thing is that you’re accountant called. There’s something going on with your finances that will require you; probably all day and full of paperwork.” I groan until I hear Josh make the expected noises of sympathy. It’s almost amazing how much I’ve come rely on the kid in just a short time, it almost makes e worried about when he has to return to college in the fall. Sure he said he planned to continue working part-time for me, but damn it I’ve become used to having him whenever is needed all day for five days a week. “Alright give me something that I’ll actually want to do,” I finally request hoping that Josh will come through for me. I have this weird combination of an aversion to work coupled with a sense of paranoia that things will go horribly, horribly wrong if I am not there to oversee things personally. Thus, I have finally reached an unofficial compromise with myself. If I can find something to do that I can at least stretch into a proper justification of avoiding work I take it, otherwise my unwanted sense of responsibility takes over. “Well I suppose,” Josh is hesitating which is weird. The kid’s the exact opposite of superman in a lot of aspects, but you can all counts on him to be completely on the ball on what needs to be done when it comes to my affairs. ‘I suppose you could always help me out with the problem I’m having.” “Problem?” I question as I sit up to look at him, “what kind of-“ I’ve literally fallen speechless at the sight in front of my eyes. Josh is blushing furiously and I’ve gradually become aware that my mouth has fallen open during my state of shock. Almost on their own my hands bring my blanket up to my chest; a form of protection against the weirdness that I am dealing with. “Josh,” I whisper as calmly as I can. “Please, please tell me that you’re problem is somehow related to a very logical, very rational explanation as to why you’re in my bedroom wearing nothing except a shiny, silver thong.” ************************* “I thought NYU didn’t really have much of a Greek life,” I say around a mouthful of omelet. Once I had outfitted him with one of my robes and got him started on making me breakfast I started grilling him about the mystery of the silver thong. It was an interesting story to say the least. “Eh, they’ve got on or two frats and sororities lying around. They don’t really do much besides go to the clubs together and call themselves exclusive, but you know me. If there is a party in which girls will be drinking copious amounts of alcohol I shall attend without complaint.” I raise an eyebrow at him. “Fair enough, but that still doesn’t explain why you’re wearing a thong. Unless there’s a certain discussion you need to have with your parents, and I refuse to be part of that can of worms in any way.” Josh rolls his eyes at me and I breathe an inward sigh of relief. Not that there’s anything wrong with being gay, but I really didn’t want to be the one Josh came out to considering what he was wearing around me just now. “It’s nothing like that. Last night I went to a party over at Beta Gamma, one of the only times they threw a party instead of going out to a club. Twelve beers, and five shots later I apparently made a bet with the president. If I can spend the entire week wearing nothing but this thong I can choose five of the sisters I want. For one month after the week I can sleep with any of the five at any time, even more than one at once.” I made a small sound of surprise. I knew more than one guy who would happily go through all that for such a prize, hell at one point in my life I would have been at the top of the line. “Not sure I see the problem here kid, “I respond. “So you hide out in your room for a week, question your sexuality for a little while, but then you’re right back where you should be with five girls at your call. It’s actually a pretty sweet deal.” “I already thought about that! Turns out that during the course of the bet we made a normal life clause. It means pretty much what it sounds like.” I frown as I realize that the situation might be a bit more serious that I thought. Well for a college kid anyway, after what I’ve gone through this is almost like a day off for me. But I’m not going to let Josh know that; I’ve a feeling that he’ll get a little pissed off I make light of the whole thing. “You know that seems somewhat sophisticated for a bunch of drunken sorority girls. But if that is the case why not just lose the damn thing? I mean are five std laden girls really worth the price of your dignity and self respect?” Josh shoots me a look that manages to combine disbelief, derision, and accusing me of hypocrisy all at the same time. “Fair enough; in that case is it worth the fact that once the month is up you’ll probably never get laid in the city of New York ever again?” Josh sighs, “it gets worse.” Holy shit, how the fuck can it possibly get worse than this? That’s it, I am going to make sure to train Josh in the proper way to handle his alcohol. The poor kid just gets into too much shit when he’s drunk as it is. “If I lose the bet, then they are going to distribute flyers all over the city with my face on them. The flyer will say to watch out for me because, because, well because I have genital herpes. They had already made a flyer when I woke up in their house the next day.” I should not laugh, I shouldn’t laugh, I really need to make sure that I don’t laugh… My stomach hurts from laughing so hard. Poor Josh’s face is turning red from how hard he’s blushing, but I truly can’t seem to help it. I mean seriously, who the hell gets themselves into these sorts of situations? “O-ok, ok,” I finally brought the laughter under control. “Look, this is all very easily fixed. Bring me to the sorority house; I’ll work something out with them to get you off the hook and into some clothes.” Josh looks down with a frown. For a second I wonder if he’s considering trying to deal with this himself as some sort of weird pride thing, but the real reason for his disgruntlement becomes clear when he discards the robe. “I’m glad your car has tinted windows.” ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Beta Gama house is really impressive. And not in the, oh isn’t it nice that these drunk college girls still manage to keep their house looking clean and respectable sort of impressive. More like the, holy fuck one or more of these girls has a rich set of parents (or lovers) and in exchange for their daughters’ love (or their lovers…lust) they’ve financed their ability to live in a fucking castle. Beta Gama’s president is sitting in front of me. She’s your typical blond bombshell, about 5’4 with blue eyes and a rack that was almost certainly grown with a shit load of plastic. “Alright Lena,” I say with a heavy sigh. “I realize that a bet’s a bet, but come on. You have to admit that the terms are really harsh, can you just let the poor guy back out of it?” Lena glares at me. I’m doing my best to fake a look of respect and concentration for her benefit, but it’s proving to be a chore. “Hey, he didn’t like have to say yes to the bet but he totally did!” she borderline screeches in a voice that is about two octaves too high. “It’s not our like fault that the virgin was like that desperate.” Wait a minute Josh isn’t a…holy crap his face is redder than a tomato. The kid who boasted about picking up a ten every time he went to the club, the self-proclaimed “NYU Campus Casanova” hadn’t gotten it in a day in his life. I honestly don’t know whether to be impressed at his ability to deceive or to be depressed on his behalf. “His lack of sexual experience aside,” I say to Lena who’s amusing herself by chewing bubblegum, “there has to be something that you want more than humiliating him. Sure, if he goes with the bet you get to bust his balls for a whole week, but then he can hate fuck you into oblivion for a whole month. If he loses, well then he commits suicide, an investigation is called for and you’re just shit out of luck. Using this bet as leverage for something that will last you awhile is the smart play here.” Lena bites her lip; suddenly I’m struck by how young she is. Sure she’s a bitchy, self-entitled, rich, daddy’s girl…but actually no I have yet to see any redeeming qualities. “Look, you and your sisters like clubs right? Well how about I throw you a few VIP passes to three of the hottest clubs in New York City. You’ll be able to get in instantly, have access to the VIP Room, all that good stuff. That’s gotta be worth more than torturing some poor virgin.” Lena twirls her hair uncertainly. I’m pretty sure she’s gonna go for it, so I’m just sitting back silently. Out of the corner of my eye I can see that Josh is trying to give me some sort of signal, but I can’t figure out what he’s trying to say. “Alright!” Lena chirps happily, and immediately Josh begins to dress in the spare clothes that he brought along. Dave is probably gonna bitch to me about giving away so many free VIP passes, but screw it I’m the owner I can do what I want. Besides, I did it to help his nephew…that’s got to count for something. ------------------------------------------------------------------- “So…do you want me to get you a hooker?” I ask Josh as we walk back towards my apartment. “No, can we please just drop it?” he responds through gritted teeth. I’ve given him a few minutes of peace to compose himself, but fuck this is way too good not to torture him with. If I’m not getting paid to save his ass I’m at least going to get some entertainment out of it “It’s nothing to be ashamed of you know. Plenty of people save themselves for marriage, though to be fair those people actually intend to. But, hell let’s be fair you’re in a tough spot! You’re one of the few straight guys in a whole campus full of gay guys and straight sexually frustrated women! There’s just no way to win with those odds man, no way at all. “ “Twenty bucks,” Josh says fishing out his wallet. “If you drop it. This day has been embarrassing enough Dan, I really just wanna go home and turn on the tv.” “Make it fifty and I’ll forget the whole thing ever happened.” Josh quickly slaps two twenties and a ten into my outstretched hand and I smile in triumph “Not all those girls were twenty-one you know,” Josh mutters as we trudge along. “But those passes are going to make sure that they get in without ever having their Id’s checked.” “Somehow I think having underage girls drinking at our clubs will only mean that we are just like every club…in the world. Dude, considering the fate that I just got you out of you really should be down on your knees worshiping me as your savior.” “I’ll get right on that,” he responds sarcastically. “But you know what really bothers me about this whole thing?” “That by standing next to me you’ve become painfully aware that you have the body of a twelve year old?” “That to,” he admits with a familiar roll of the eyes. I admit I somewhat happy to see him slowly returning to his old self. Life just isn’t as interesting if Josh isn’t his usual smart-ass self. “But have you noticed that the girls didn’t really get anything out of the deal?” I shoot him a questioning glance. From my end it looks like they got a lot out of it, to the point where I was probably gonna have Dave throwing bad sounding numbers in my face for days and blaming me for them. “You saw their house,” Josh continues. “They whole lot of them can probably afford quality fakes if they need them along with the VIP passes we sell and anything else your clubs offer without breaking a sweat. But Lena was actually really happy about the deal, what’s going on there?” “Maybe there money supply isn’t as secure as you think it is,” I reply casually but a cold feeling has found its way to the pit of my stomach. Truth be told Josh is far smarter than I am, and his instincts serve him pretty well when he’s not piss drunk. If he thinks that there’s something off kilter going on here I’m inclined to believe him, but I can’t figure out what else might be going on. “We can worry about that later, “ I finally answer cheerfully pushing the issue to the back of my mind. “Because it’s pretty clear that Dave hasn’t done his duty as a male role model and taught you in the ins and outs of the fairer sex! Well don’t worry, I’m here and I’m going to teach you everything you need to know.” “You said that you were going to drop it!” “Lesson one, the dick goes into the vagina.
