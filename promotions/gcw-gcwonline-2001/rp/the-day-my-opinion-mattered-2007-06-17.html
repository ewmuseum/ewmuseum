The Day My Opinion Mattered...

17 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/17/2007 "Trey your father is dying, he doesn't have much time..." (Not the best way to start your day if you ask me, but who would care to ask me anything anyway? My opinion never really mattered. If it did my mother would have left that fucking scumbag husband of hers many years before she considerably became addicted to the physical and emotional pain he would lay on her. That fucking wife beating Nazi will perish when I seek my revenge on him for what he put US through. But till then I suppose I have more shit to worry about...) (I respond with a barrage of useless careless questions about his health and how diminshed it has become. I haven't heard from or seen my father since my early early childhood. Why should I care if he was to die now? Is this his fucked up way of getting me back into his life? Son I'm dying...Who the fuck cares? Should I? Anyway, they proceed to tell me his life expectance and the reasoning behind it. It wasn't long at all. He was staged in deep to a very serious liver condition...) "You're father has Cirrhosis and there shouldn't be more than a few monthes. If that, even." (Typical outcome for a drunk right? He was my father, and he never lived down to the expectations my mother laid out for him. She left him due to the drink. He had the option to give that all up and fight to be better than his disease, but he sat spineless in his dark depression and drowned his sorrows like a fish in a desert. Poor Logan, the single father with Cirrhosis. He never cared for his own health, seriously, so why the fuck should I?) "What do you want from me Doc? I am useless." "Well Trey, that's where you are wrong. You have every chance at saving his life. You see, Cirrhosis is a disease that leaves holes in the liver. The liver is the organ that circulates blood and can monitor any sickness or bacteria that my infect the blood cells. But something you may not know about the liver is that it can regenerate itself, in most circumstances." (I will cut to the important portion of this memory so that I don't lose your attention. They want me as Logan's Liver donor. How fucking selfish can one man get. No birthday cards, no Christmas gifts...No call on graduation, no appearance at his mothers funeral? I had the choice of watching a still rather young, yet helpless drunk die to his choices he made. What if I were to give this man a chunk of my liver? What if he recovered, and said to himself, "I have a new toy to use and abuse for many years more to come!" That's my fucking liver. Not yours "DAD".) (There is alot on my plate right now. Do I give the man his second wind? Do I play guardian to the person who ironically on paper for years, was MY "LEGAL GUARDIAN"?) (Many questions consumed me as I played most options and outcomes. It started with me hating the "Man" who was never there. I was able to release twenty-fve good years of built up frusteration into choosing whether he lived or he died. Call it sadistically therapeutic if you must, but therapeutic none the less. I gathered myself from my rage as I began to think about the solemn story of a father who maybe never had the luxury of being there. He was basically forced out of a marriage he could have very much still wanted part of. He lost two kids who he could have lost to restraining orders, or no contact clauses. There were alot of outcomes, but in the end, there I lay for hours under the knife on some chopping board in Houston. The day my opinion really did matter...)
