Betrayal Stems A Blast from the Past

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 1996, October 2007 FLASH. October, 2007 Chris Bagwell :: "You ungrateful little bitch. When the fuck will you learn? Are you that goddamn incompetant that you can't curb your obsession with blowing all of my money? You're a filthy little whore!" [ Back in New Jersey, we step into an arguement between Chris Bagwell and his live-in girlfriend Michelle. Michelle is sitting at the kitchen table, hands covering her face, hysterically crying. Chris is screaming at her relentlessly. ] Bagwell :: "Don't you dare fucking sit there and sulk. I'm not falling for that garbage anymore. You have a problem and you need to really straighten yourself the fuck out." [ Michelle glances up from her hands with a look of pure hatred in her eyes for Chris. She slowly lifts herself from the chair and begins stepping closer to Chris. ] Michelle :: "I'm ... I'm sorry, Chris." Bagwell :: "Sorry. Sorry? SORRY!" Michelle :: "I never meant for it to be this way. I never wanted it to happen like this.. " [ Michelle takes a few steps closer to Chris and gently places her hand on his shoulder. She leans in and kisses his cheek softly. ] Bagwell :: [ confused ] "What are you trying to say, Michelle?" [ There's a sudden bang at the front door. Chris snaps his attention towards the doorway as two large men come barging through. ] Michelle :: "I'm sorry Chris. We weren't supposed to get this attached. At least I wasn't supposed to... " [ One of the men is dressed in a black silk Armani suit and is very cleanly shaven. He's obnoxiously chomping away on a piece of gum while rubbing his leather glove wrapped hands together. His acquaintance gracefully pulls a steel pipe into view from beyond his back. He rotates his wrists, stretching them out, and quickly cracks the bones in his neck. The two men begin pacing towards Chris.] Bagwell :: "I can't believe it." [ Chris' eyes light up in terror. ] Bagwell :: "Augustus." [ The man with the leather gloves smirks at the sound of his name. ] Augustus :: "It's been a long time, Chris." Bagwell :: "What the hell are you doing in my house?" [ Augustus begins walking closer as Michelle backs away from Chris. The second man, with the pipe, remains standing in the same place. ] Augustus :: "Twelve years ago, you and I had some unresolved conflicts. Do you recall that time, Chris?" Bagwell :: "You mean when I snapped both your legs and left your groupies in a bloody mess?" Augustus :: "Haha! Oh Chris, always a jokester. Well now its my time to collect." Bagwell :: "My money ... Michelle..." [ Augustus starts laughing as his acquaintance steps into the kitchen and moves very briskly towards Chris. ] Augustus :: "She wasn't just spending your money, Chris. She was paying it into my accounts. I'm going to ruin you just like you ruined me. I want full compensation for every single dime you caused me to lose after I got locked up. EVERY SINGLE DIME!" [ The second man lounges at Chris, swinging the pipe fiercely. Chris throws up his arm his arm to block. The pipe connects along his forearm. He immediately tries twisting his hand to snatch the pipe from the large man's hand, but the large man is too quick. He swings again at Chris' ribs, but this time Chris manages to jerk his elbow down and catch the pipe at his side. Chris retaliates with a swift kick into the man's gut with a simutaneous jerking back of his left side, allowing the pipe to be cast free. ] Augustus :: "Michelle, get the hell out of here and wait in the van. This is going to get ugly." [ Augustus shoves Michelle towards the doorway as tears stream down her face. She quickly flees. At that same instant, Bagwell gains full control over the pipe and smashes it down a-top the second man's clavicle, forcing him down to one knee. Bagwell follows in with a right elbow smash across the second man's face, smashing into his nose. ] Augustus :: "Hey Chris ..." [ Bagwell diverts his attention up towards Augustus, as Augustus begins pulling something from an inside pocket of his Armani suit. ] Augustus :: "Rebecca sends her regards." [ Augustus reveals a 9mm handgun and fires it twice at Bagwell. One connects in his left arm and the second in his lower right gut, approximately in the area of his stomach. Bagwell falls back and to the ground. Outside, Michelle drops to her knees and covers her ears. She starts getting hysterical once again. Augustus grabs the second man by the arm and pulls at him. ] Augustus :: "MESSINO, GET UP! WE HAVE TO GET THE FUCK OUT OF HERE." [ Messino slowly gets up to his feet and rotates his shoulder a bit, trying to loosen it back up. Augustus instantly flees the house, with Messino following shortly behind. Outside, Augustus comes up to Michelle crying on her knees. ] Augustus :: "You would be on your knees, you little cunt. GET UP!" [ He snatches Michelle's arm and starts dragging her towards a silver van parked in front of the house. The sliding side door is already cracked open. Augustus rips it fully open and tosses Michelle inside, climbing in behind her. Messino jogs up and climbs in behind then as Augustus slams the door shut. An un-revealed driver starts the van and the four speed away. ] [ Back inside. ] [ Chris is holding his lower abdomen tightly as the blood begins to pour. He drags himself across the floor towards his kitchen table, where his cell is sitting. Halfway to the table, he drops to the floor and starts losing consciousness. He lowers his head and starts to breathe heavy, trying to keep himself awake. ] [ He pushes himself forward, blood smudging across the floor. Chris grabs hold of a chair and climbs up to one knee, where he lounges himself forward onto the table. His eyes cringe at the pressure he's putting on his wounded left arm as he manages to get hold of his phone. ] [ He collapses backwards onto his back and flicks open his phone. He slams the number 5 key and presses send. ] [ The phone rings twice. ] [ And three times more. ] Voice :: "Dukey, how may I help you?" Bagwell :: "Dukey..." Dukey Booty :: "Chris? Chris, is that you?" Bagwell :: "Michelle.. gone. Augustus..out. He...shot.." [ Chris drops the phone and loses consciousness. ] Dukey Booty :: "Chris? CHRIS? CHRIS, ANSWER ME! WHATS GOING ON?" FLASH SIX HOURS LATER "I felt my life flash before my very eyes. Was this the end? Had I finally crossed over? I was at a loss of words .. or thoughts .. and my shock value was soaring at a twenty-five on the standard scale of ten. Augustus. How had he been released? Where has he been hiding? Has he been plotting this very moment for the last twelve years? He found where I lived and he stole all my money. He stole my heart and he stole whatever was left of my life. Where the hell did he find Michelle? And then, I remember." FLASH Mid-October, 1996. Inside an Irish Pub in Union County, New Jersey just minutes from Newark. >> BEGIN NARRATIVE "I remember that night even still. I came in for a drink with a few friends after coming off the injury that would ruin my chances at a full scholarship to play football for Rutgers University. I never intended on being a super-athlete or make it into the NFL, but I would never have minded the opportunity to go to a home-town school and play with some of the best. I wasn't totally shit out of luck. Coach said there was still a chance that my highlight videos and my skillset would be enough to warrant me a scholarship and a guarunteed spot, although I might not have been given the same priority or treatment. Yet that was a risk I was willing to take. I shouldn't have been in the pub that night. I was still only 17, itching for my 18th birthday to come fast. My friend and I hadn't been carded here a single time and we'd already been coming for upwards of three years. It was laid back and people just didn't give a shit. Not to mention, we looked a bit older then we really were. I remember it getting late and a couple of my buddies heading home for the night. All but my one friend, Brian, had been gone. Brian was notorious for trying to be a ladies man. He'd hit on every and any chick who looked good with no regard as to who they were or who they might have been with. And it all fell into place." >> END NARRATIVE [ Brian, standing at about six foot three, approaches a very pretty blonde, who looks probably twenty-two or twenty-three. He brings her over a Grey Goose Martini as compliments and his way into conversation. ] Brian :: "Hey, how are you sweetheart? You looked like you could use another drink?" [ She smiles at the gesture and gracefully accepts the free drink. ] Young Lady :: "Dare I ask what you saw fit to get me?" Brian :: "Only if I can ask for your name." Young Lady :: "Well I guess there's not harm in that. My name is Michelle." Brian :: "Michelle. That's a pretty name. And I suppose I'll hold up my end of the bargain. In front of you is a date rape martini. The finest date raping drugs available in the continental United States. And if you could just hurry up and drink it, I'd be glad to take advantage of you and then go into hiding so you can wake up with my son nine months from now." [ Michelle laughs at Brian's gesture. ] Brian :: "Oh, but I'm not kidding." Michelle :: "Is that how you pick up all the girls, Brian?" Brain :: "I'd said its got a 60-40 success rate." Michelle :: "Well then, I better start drinking before it gets warm and the drug's effects wear down." [ Still smiling, Michelle takes a sip. Its at this moment Chris Bagwell, who is sitting bar-side across the room, glances over. He locks eyes with Michelle for an instant. She blushes at him as she places her glass back on the table. She refocuses her attention back on Brian. ] >> NARRATIVE "That was it. That was her. She was the girl in the bar that night. But what was her affiliation to Augustus? How would she be conned into dating me ... and robbing me blind?" >> END NARRATIVE Michelle :: "A Grey Goose Martini, ey?" Brian :: "What makes you think I'd be so daring as to buy you a top shelf liquor before I'd even gotten your name?" Michelle :: "Part of it might have to do with the fact that the waitress denied my request at another drink, stating that a man had already purchased another for me." [ Brian glances back in the waitress' direction, somewhat disgruntled she ruined the surprise. ] Michelle :: "And perhaps you can call the other part women's intution. Brian :: "That's a bit upsetting. Here I thought.." Michelle :: "..that you were being slick?" [ Brian's smile glistens just as there's a sudden commotion across the bar. It attracts Michelle's immediate attention and her eyes instantly fill with fear. ] Michelle :: "Oh no. You have to leave. You have to get away from me." [ Brian looks around, confused. Chris' attention also gets drawn to the commotion. ] Brian :: "What? Why? What's wrong?" [ A group of men come storming across the room in the direction of Brian and Michelle. Bagwell stands from his bar stool as a man in a black Armani suit steps out in front of the group, insinuating his leadership. He glances in the direction of Bagwell, making eye contact, before grabbing the shoulder of Brian fiercely. ] >> NARRATIVE "Augustus." >> END NARRATIVE FLASH Back to October 2007. Two days later. [ A cool breeze brustles through the slightly cracked window of Overlook Hospital in Summit, New Jersey. Enough so to awaken the otherwise medically-induced comatose Chris Bagwell. ] Dukey Booty :: "There he is. How you feeling, big boy?" Chris Bagwell :: "I know who she is, now." Dukey Booty :: "Who who is?" Chris Bagwell :: "Michelle ... that girl I've been seeing for a little over a year." Dukey Booty :: "I heard those rumors. I didn't know they were true. Hell, I would've never guessed that it would have even been that serious. I guess it has been a long time." Chris Bagwell :: "Augustus." Dukey Booty :: "Talk about a blast from the past..." Chris Bagwell :: "It was his sister. I remember now. I was just dreaming about it .. recollecting the entire night. The night I met you." Dukey Booty :: "What the hell are the chances you'd bump into her again?" Chris Bagwell :: "I'm under the impression that it wasn't just a coincidence." Dukey Booty :: "You don't mean..." Chris Bagwell :: "I think there's still a lot more I don't know. I think he knows what happened to Rebecca..." Dukey Booty :: "Chris, don't get yourself started on all that." Chris Bagwell :: "No, Duke. I'm certain he knows. He said something about her ... I think he's got her. I think she's alive. I'm certain she is." [ A nurse enters the room. ] Nurse :: "Excuse me sir, but its time for Chris to go in for his second surgery." [ Chris and Dukey make eye contact, Chris very sincere. He gives a nod to Dukey, who returns the gesture. ] Dukey Booty :: "I'll make some calls for when you get out." [ Chris smiles as the picture fades. ]
