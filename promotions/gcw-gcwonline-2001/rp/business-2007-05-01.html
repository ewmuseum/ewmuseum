Business

1 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/01/07 Day 1 Early morning around 9:33 pm. Standing at a busted slot Machine several men are giving reports to a police officer. Sitting on a chair nearby is a man cuffed who seems to ranting and raving about the machine being possessed and being a thief. The Police officer looks over at him and says something and turns away as the man rants and raves some more. Walking forward Shaman is seen shaking his head as he looks at the slot machine. “Sometimes you think they would respect the machine. How did he open it?” The police officer holds up a large screw driver in a zip lock styled type of bag. “The gentleman seems to have opened the machine with a screw driver. Charges thus far are destruction of private property and attempted robbery.” Shaman reaches up and rubs his temple as he lifts a radio to his mouth and speaks. “Rafael contact Kawanaha in Atlantic City and tell her we need her back in Las Vegas as soon as possible.” Shaman listens to the squawking and chatter on the radio. Shakes his head and just puts it away in his pocket. “Better add on the prosecution of trespass and public disturbance as well. Seems this gentleman has been in the Casino before and kicked out. He was told never to return and thus he has done so. I’ll be in my office of you need me.” Shaman turns and walks through the casino as some people walk up to him and ask for his autograph as he smiles and signs it and then moves on. Something about the most sadistic man in GCW is different. It is as if he does what he wants when he wants and it makes no difference to him. Slowly he enters his elevator to the offices that are upstairs. The elevator dings as he steps out and walks over to his office as he enters and sits down at his desk and rubs his temples again. He punches up the computer screen and begins to type some information in on it. “Just another day in business land.” Shaman turns his head as the phone rings as he grabs the phone and answers. “Hello” Yeah this is Shaman. GCW meeting before the match against Rufio? Not likely make it a conference call between my people and yours.” Shaman frowns. “It is a match plain and simple no numbers needed. Go with the previous match details and show splits on profit. Yeah yeah forget about complaining just make it so. I pay you remember” Good goodbye!” Shaman hangs up the phone and goes back to work. Day 1 Lunchtime around 1:23 pm. Walking through the kitchen area Shaman is seen browsing the food. He picks at a few pieces of lettuce and celery as he munches as he is waiting obviously for something as the chef walks forward and speaks. “Hey Boss, Just wanted to get with you about a possible menu and entr’e selections list, I know you put me in charge, But I’d like to hit some new ideas past you.” Shaman turns and looks and shakes his head as he pulls out a pad of paper. “Go ahead all I have is time today. Especially with all the crap security seems to be failing to do. Anyways Angelo what do you want for a recipe and what is your idea?” Angelo the Chef steps forward handing Shaman some papers and smiles. “As you know there is a whole wrestling themed idea in the works for the museum here in the Casino. I was thinking on the days you have big matches maybe we can have meals and everything geared towards you and your stabled mates in GCW. What would be your preference for a meal?” Shaman rubs his chin thinking about the idea. “Steak and mashed potatoes, this idea actually has some merit. Give discounts of ten percent to people who are wearing GCW specific gear and also to those wearing any gear of mine from the past. T shirts hats etc etc.” Shaman begins writing ideas down as Angelo smiles and looks over at him. “Thanks Boss speaking of steak yours is almost ready. Do you want to make sure that drinking times are set up as well?” Shaman raises his head and looks at Angelo. “Yeah be sure that everyone is carded if they ask for a drink. Be sure the waiters and waitresses are familiar with all forms of ids.” Shaman takes the plate of food and sets it down before himself. He begins to eat it as Angelo nods and walks away as he begins writing ideas on the pad that he was holding in his apron. Shaman looks up as he eats his meal working about mixing the gravy with the steak juice as he smiles as he eats happily. Day 1 Clockout time 5:00 pm Shaman stands in front of his window watching the shift change below as he waits. His time to leave is following behind very shortly as someone walks into his office. He watches this person and nods. “Jason you know you’re supposed to leave like everyone else at five.” Jason looks up and nods smiling. “Sorry sir, I just want to make sure every last bit of the daily business is finished.” Shaman nods and looks back out the window. “Just be sure you have everything prepped for my meeting this evening. I do not want to be left hanging with direction and times.” Jason looks up and lays a folder on the desk. “On your desk sir, made sure it was all prepared myself.” Shaman turns and walks over to the desk and picks up the file and looks up at Jason. “Have a good evening Jason.” Shaman turns and heads back to the window holding the folder as he watches down at the floor. The shift change seems to have gone smoothly as he always enjoys the efficiency of the changes lately. He opens the folder as he turns his head to watch Jason leave the office. He browses over the details of the meeting this evening. He shakes his head as he looks at some of the details. “Almost forgot how some of those will go. All these wrestling meet and greets for public purposes and relationship purposes to have you be more personable with the fans.” Shaman flips through the papers in the folder as he looks about and cracks his neck from side to side as he walks over to the desk. As he gets to the desk he reaches down and lifts the phone. “Tyrone go ahead and bring my truck around. I am going to be out of the office the next two days. Kawanaha should be back by them forward all details to her office.” Shaman hangs up the phone and reaches over and shuts down the computer. After the computer is shut down he turns a key on his desk and locks it after he slides the laptop into the drawer. He stands up and reaches down and grabs a duffle bag and makes his way to his door. He opens the door and looks behind him as he shuts off the light and closes the door behind himself. Day 1 7:45 pm Meet and Greet. Pouring himself a glass of water the shows host sits in his chair waiting for Shaman. He looks back and forth remembering the last time he had Shaman on the show. He is nervous obviously since he ticked Shaman off and got himself beaten severely. He also tried to press charges and failed miserably. He knew that he had to make this right and figured this was the best way. Hold another live show and ask Shaman some questions and try not to tick him off. He knew he had the personal knack for ticking off a lot of people because that was his style. He looks at his camera men. “Where the hell is he at?” The camera men shrug as a young woman walks forward and speaks. “He is in the building and he is having us sign paperwork for the network. It seems there are some precautions being made and something’s being put in stone. The network guys are in full agreement with Shaman.” The host looks at the woman and grows angry. “What in the hell is more important than me and my show” I make this network its damn money and funding by its sponsors!” Shaman steps forward and into the light and looks at the host. “Hello Scott and no you do not mean crap to the sponsors of the show and officially you are not the number one rated show on the network. Officially the network has picked up an Independent all access wrestling channel. Your sponsors are still staying, but I am the networks largest sponsor as of 15 minutes ago. Let us begin our meet and greet interview shall we.” Shaman sits down staring down the host as the host sits back and rubs his temple. The cameras light up as they begin to record live. The host sits up slightly and grins all smart ass like as he looks at the camera. “Well ladies and gentlemen we have here another wild day on wrestling 101 and we have a return guest that you folks have requested Shaman.” Shaman looks at the camera and raises his hand and makes a non chalant wave. The host looks over and then grins. “Shaman how does it feel to buy your way into securing yourself in a position of power, Lord knows you bought yourself a security blanket in the network.” Shaman leans back rubbing his hands together grinning. “Well first Scott I want it to be known you’re fired after the show conclude, Second of all the paperwork that was signed was an investment made in the network long before you interviewed me the first time. In fact it was your network president that suggested I make an investment for my future and I agreed.” Shaman smiles as he watches Scott squirm nervously. “You cannot fire me since I have a contract for another five years. You are bluffing and have no leg to stand on.” Shaman smiles and grins as he motions over at the woman who was standing there. “She is your replacement Scott. In fact the truth is I have plenty of leg to stand on. Since I own twenty five percent of the stock it allows me to make decisions based on morals. Morally you are no longer good for the network and the investments of the board of trustees. Now lemme see here questions questions oh yes. Your termination was due to breech of contract. You were stated to have been seen at a competitive network giving away documents and information for a buy out. As I said you are fired and truthfully you were set up. Some of us sent a private detective to follow you last time after you tried sabotaging my own personal purchase of specific wrestling business.” The host named Scott sits there in shock as he watches. Shaman raises his hand as two police officers step forward and handcuff Scott on live television. Shaman leans in near Scott’s ear and whispers. “One of my associates who come to my Casino told me of your ventures into trying to get this network bought out. He owns one of the networks you approached. Maybe next time you will learn that messing with me will cost you more in the end. You will never be able to work on television again due to your contract breech and the embarrassment you just received here on live national television.” Shaman stands up and looks at the camera. “Fans of wrestling this display was not to harm any of your views and opinions of those of us in the board of trustees. We are here to bring you the most enjoying wrestling talk show entertainment. The young lady who will be taking over the show is Cindy Cassermann. Once again we apologise for the verbal and mental abuse placed upon you by Scott Shelton and will be rectifying the situation with a free wrestling show put on in the greater Cincinnati area for our viewers. Also we will be playing the favourite shows that you wish to see in the next few coming weeks. Call in on the hotline and place your requests and we will place the shows in the order of greatest to least of the wanted viewing. Thank you for your time and I do hope to see you at ringside again.” A shaman sand still as the cameras flashing light goes out and he walks away. “I do so hate dealing with the masses in such a fashion, but Scott is an idiot and needs to be dealt with harshly. Now get the shows up that were forwarded. I hope I do not have to comeback for the board of trustees. I do not like doing a meet and greet like this through lies. I am a straight forward in your face individual. I have other business concerns as a Casino owner.” Shaman turns and walks out the door as he walks down the hall stopping in front of a picture of him holding his WWA titles of the past as he spits on the picture and turns walking away. Day 1 Evening bedtime 12:33 am. Shaman steps out of the shower, the steam floats easily throughout the open expanse of the bathroom. He walks over to the mirror and wipes the steam off with his hand. He looks at himself in the mirror finally looking as if he is beginning to relax as he turns and exits the bathroom and dresses in the dark as his shadow is seen dressing through the moonlight shining in from the window. “So Rufio thinks he can kick my ass, I don’t listen to second raters who plan on boring me to death. Rufio has a reality of an awakening when he steps into the ring with me. He could not beat Vetra and I did. His skills are woefully low compared to mine and truthfully his time has passed.” Shaman walks across the room and turns on the television. “Rufio I don’t know you and I am going to treat you like anyone else I step into the ring with. Your going to hurt and bleed and be beaten down like those who are stupid enough to cross me.” Shaman shuts the television off and makes his way to his bed and sits on the edge. “Just another day in the life isn’t it. I don’t really give a damn of how people perceive me. I am doing what I like to do plain and simple. I have things set in motion for my future not somebody else’s. Look at it this way the tournament I want to end it being the winner. Who else is there that will step in my path” Absolutely nobody will get in my way and stop me. There are plenty of talented individuals out there and the truth of the matter is simple. I have ten plus years of in ring experience. You do not truly think I am not going into this without a plan do you? The simple truth is I am a veteran and I prepare the same way I do everytime. Know your opponent and you know how to achieve victory.” Shaman frowns and lies back on his bed. His eyes stare at the ceiling and he just watches nothing. “You know there was a time when everything was so much simpler. I mean when you were a child you had dreams and not a care in the world. When you open your eyes eventually you see what is really wrong with the world. There are goals and dreams everybody has now, but those dreams and goals can be met only through hard work and determination. Nowhere near the bull and cock stories you get from these so called support speakers or even motivational gurus.” Shaman closes his eyes and nods off to sleep as he ignores the camera leaving a lot more questions than answers.
