An ending to a previous event.

6 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/6/05 (Really!) It was a poorly planned subterfuge, but it worked. I lit a cigarette on the balcony, caught in the weak lights of the motel's overhanging roof. The glow of frayed wires popping and cracking danced along the broad black-and-white whatever that was splayed out in the center of the parking lot, and beyond that lay a wrecked something that was hard to make out in the dying of the light. Electricity ran an erratic grid along the ground, spreading in a pool of water that was certainly poorly placed, as the horrible wailing of the amorphous blob preceded the smell of singed flesh. Tulz was pushed against the railing in front of me, and Trey hung in the doorway like most of the others that had been staying at the motel. Rooms' lights popped on in no real order, the occupants spilling out to impotently watch the horrific scene with us. Hushed whispers broke the still that followed, quiet questions that rose into the night even as I was coming to understand what a fried whale looked like. And nothing's gonna change my world--yeah. 'Be cool,' I told the crowd. 'It's just an omnipotent asshole's will. Everyone can just go back to bed, peacefully.' They shouted, in protest or offense. 'It was just a message to me, people--not you guys. Water trucks carrying killer whales crash into power lines and topple over into parking lots all the time, this is absolutely nothing special, so if you'd just trust me-' 'And who the fuck are you,' questioned a voice beneath me. I stepped beside of Tulz' and leaned over the damp railing to stare into the face of objection: a mid-20s guy in a wifebeater and boxers, bleach blonde hair stuck to his forehead from sweat and frizzing out everywhere else. 'I'm Jordan fuckin' White, and everything's completely copasetic. So chill. Sleep. Bone your girlfriend.' 'How about I bone your girlfriend,' emphasis on the 'your.' 'You're too drunk to try talking shit to an artisan in the craft. Don't you know what 'be cool' even means?' He clenched one fist by his side and held the other up at me, almost as a challenge. 'Why don't you come down here an' see how drunk I am?' Definitely as a challenge. 'Because I've got a goddamned broken shoulder, asshole.' The people hung around, pushing their heads slightly out of their doorways to keep eyes on us. Tired, then, I shouted: 'Everyone, just fucking go back to sleep. I'm a professional here, and I need to have some peace to do my job,' and most of them accepted it as a just reason. I wondered how many had written up as a hallucination, a bad dream, and at what point they'd figured out what was going on. It was quiet. I looked over the railing again and the heckler had disappeared to leave my personal trifecta alone in this holy moment. 'S'yeah, J. What now?' 'We've got a whale to investigate!' 'What the hell do you know about whales?' 'They're mammals. Besides, it's pretty fuckin' dead--and I doubt even a real marine biologist could be much help to that poor bastard.' 'True.' The air felt ionized and crisp at the bottom of the steps and the smell of burnt flesh filled my fried nostrils instantly; I cringed for what Trey and (especially) Tulz had to be going through. I saw the whale first of us, with its skin fried/bubbled and its eyes staring blankly into what's gotta be the most confusing thing it'll ever know, and I couldn't help but be disappointed at how small it was. Really, though killer whales are pretty large, I'd expected a truly massive beast. I hadn't expected to find it in sprawled in a parking lot and cooked via special recipe, sure, but in that event it should've at least been a large whale. What kind of message does dropping something the size of a few SUVs send when you could've gone with a monster that left buildings in its shadow? An amazingly sublime one. Christ, was that the warning? I ran it through my head a few times, trying to anticipate any other feasible signs than the implicit message of, 'it'll be worse next time.' A grizzly man, sobbing on the sidewalk when I came down, rushed to block us off from getting too close. 'Wires might still be,' break to cry, 'live.' 'Were you a big fan?' 'Huh?' 'Of the whale.' 'No,' he pushed out under heavy tears, 'but I know this is gonna come outta my pay.' He reeked of gin, I'll add now. 'What happened?' 'Something darted outta the,' you know the drill by now, 'parkin' lot and I wasn't thinkin'. Oh Gawd, I'm fucked.' He was right about being fucked. There was no cheap help for the man anymore, so I didn't bother reconciling. 'What jumped in front of you--did it resemble an omnipotent, omnipresent, omniscient being?' 'Looked like a dog to me.' Trey nudged me in the ribs. Quietly: ''Dog' backwards is 'God,' J.' Loudly: 'I'm not a fucking idiot, Trey. Now, uh, I never caught your name.' 'Ed.' 'Okay, Ed, was anything odd about anything tonight?' 'Who are you, anyways?' 'A marine biologist. I happened to see the accident through my window, and need to examine the whale. Let me do that, and I'll make sure you're in the clear.' 'R-really?' 'Yeah.' 'Thanks...' 'Ezekial Stanhope. Dr. Ezekial Stanhope.' 'Thanks doc.' 'Anytime.' We passed him and went to the whale, careful to not walk in the path of its dead gaze. The eyes, cold glassy orbs, glinted in the ambient glow known as 'city' and were hard to actually figure out a path for, so we advanced towards its back. I counted cigarette drags, 7 before I could truly make out the damage done to its hide, 9 before the stench was too much, 11 before I forgot the smell entirely, 14 before we finally got to the whale and began circling it. Nothing particularly abnormal stood out to me: there were no flickering street signs warning of impending doom or messages discernable only in the reflection of the water, no angels on high singing songs of prophecy or of fatalistic melancholy. Moses had his burning bush. I broke a limb off of a nearby tree and prodded the whale with it, trying to draw forth some ethereal voice--to no avail. Ed stared on from slightly closer in than where we had been, but didn't dare question my credentials as my shouts raged on towards eternity. 'Speak, foul beast! I command it of you, deliver the message that the cruel creator hath sent you down for, lest I lay waste to him and his world!' 'Yo, J.' 'Fuck you, Trey. This is personal--did you see what he did to this fucking whale?' 'Jordan, man.' 'Didn't you ever watch Free Willy?' 'Yeah, but...' 'Then show some goddamned respect!' 'Look, I respect the whale, but there's a fuckin' Post-It note over here.' 'What?' I left my stick hanging out of the whale's blow-hole and quickly made my way to Trey. In his hands was, indeed, a Post-It note that stood out as a pale green smear in the absence of light. I called Ed over and scrawled his information on the back of the note in the flickering glow of a struck lighter, then turned to go back towards the room. But lo, something blocked our path with treachery and foul deceit! It was the nght's earlier heckler, inspired by the booze he'd been pouring down while I had conducted serious research, bitter at my growing sobriety the whole time. 'You wanna fuck around now? Y' gimp ass...' 'J, I got this for you, man. It's nothing.' 'You've got one eye. What are you gonna do to him?' 'Beat him like I beat everyone else that fucks with Pirate Trey?' 'Solid point. Tulz, you wanna take him?' She shook her head. 'Well, it's either you or me, Trey. Wanna flip for it?' 'Like I said, I got this. Ain't shit.' 'You're more than a friend now, you know--you're an investment. You could take the fight as a friend, but I'm not throwing an investment up against some drunken guy that might have a knife. Think Ed'd do it?' 'Dude.' 'No one says 'dude' anymoe.' 'I got it.' 'You've got one eye, Trey.' 'One good eye. Takin' the patch off someday.' 'When you stop secretly thinking it looks cool?' He put his head down for a second. 'Look, I'm taking this fight.' I didn't have time to answer because I was getting hit in the head with something very much fist-like. It staggered me backwards and almost knocked me down--more importantly, it separated me from the actual event that was going on. Trey grabbed the guy's head instantly, slamming it down into a rising knee. There was a crunch, and a blurred punch that extended down into the man's neck as he dropped down to the concrete; before I was even balanced. 'I told you, I had the fight.' 'That wasn't a fight, you jackass. That guy's broken--drag him back into his room and shut the door, for fuck's sake. Cops always show up sometime, you know.' He nodded and followed orders, quickly fireman's carrying the man through the open door and slamming it behind him as he left alone. Feeling less encumbered then, I led the posse back to the room just as the inevitable sirens started howling. Shutting the door was almost better than sex. 'What's it say, man?' I laughed and held it up to Trey, who mumbled it softly back to me: 'wrong whale, give me what I wanted--OR ELSE! -Ahab,' in a cursive hand. 'Heh. That is kinda funny, but, uh, what the hell's it mean to us?' 'Keep hunting.' Tulz laughed then, harder than Trey. Then we drank ourselves into oblivion!
