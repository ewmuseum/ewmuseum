Missing....Part 2.....The Road Behind

Date: One Year Ago Bryan Mayhem starts to stir. It's still cold, and his left side of his face is swimming in a puddle. The rain falling from the swollen sky above splatters against the right portion of his exposed face. The warm feeling coming from his head starts to subside, as the pain and throbbing from the same location begins to come to fruition. His eyes are opening, and his lips begin to part. The rain tastes so good, and almost refreshing and rejuvenating. He opens his mouth to its fullest extent allowing the fat rain drops to tickle the back of his throat. He lets out a groan, and immediately puts his hand to his head. There's a large lump, and a small hole that hurts when its touched. Ignoring the pain Bryan digs his massive paw into the hole and realizes it's an injury, but how did he get it? Holy shit this hurts. What the hell just happened? I start to look around and my memory begins to come back. The alley, the coffin, the lights, the voice.....THAT GODDAMN VOICE! I sit for a few in this cold puddle of god knows what and let the pass few minutes absorb. I wake up in this alley to hear a voice. What the fuck was it saying? I probe the hole in my head and feel a pop. With that pop comes this warmth running down my face. It feels good mixing with this cold rain. I stare down at my black jeans and notice little patters begin to drip separately from the rain. They are red, than diminish into a maroon on my black jeans. I must have busted my head open, there's no other explanation for it. But how or why? I stand up, and almost fall down because the spins begin to succumb me. Wow I am punch drunk, and I havent been punched in days. The spins slow down to crawl, and my hand grasps something. It's cold, hard, and made of steel. To prevent the spins from slapping me in the face again I turn my head slowly and notice my crutch. It's a dumpster, and that's when it hits me..... 10 minutes and 500 feet earlier. "Look homey, Imma be straight wit you. Dis shit right here is da shit aight. I don't fuck around wit bitch shit. So you got da scrilla?" "Man, I dunno. I was floatin wit da last shit u hit me up wit. I jus wanna feel chill, but yo shit dunt do dat." Smiling, "Man nigga u trippin. Dis shit is chill. I done got rid of dat utha shit." Both men all of a sudden hear a loud blood curdling scream that makes them freeze and immediately look in both directions. "What the fuck was dat?!?" The dealer catches a glimpse of a rather large man pacing in the alley running his fingers through his hair screaming at someone to stop. "Man who da fuck is dat?" All the other man can do is shrug and watch in amazement at the giant down the alley. He is pacing and scratching at his head screaming at someone. His jaw is dropped, but instinct has caused him to keep his hand on his gun. He grew up on the streets and you learn to realize that nothing is ever to strange, but he will admit this is a bit odd. "Man, das da biggest dude i ever seen. How tall u think he be?" He's still in shock. He puts finger a to his lips to give the international sign for be quiet as he watched. He pulls his gun out and aims it at the psychopath screaming to himself. "Nigga what da fuck you doin?" "I am jus gettin ready. Dat muthafucka is bound to snap and see us starin at him, and if he come dis way im cappin him straight in da fucking face." "Aim high." They continue to watch this event, ready to pounce if they must. Than this took a turn from Odd Ave, to Strange as Fuck Street. The giant drops to his knees pulls his head back and smashes it straight into the base of the large dumpster. A loud echoing thud permeates the alley. The two thugs bounce back. The dealer drops his gun in shock but quickly maintains composure bends down without removing his eyes from the dumpster and swipes his hand across the ground and finds his weapon. "Ok, let's get the fuck outta here!" "No wait man, that nigga might need our help!" The dealer looks at his "customer" and shakes his head in disbelief. "You see the size of that man? You wanna fuck wit him go right ahead. Imma dip. Peace." With that the dealer, followed by the customer left GCW Superstar Bryan "Mayhem" O'Neil to lie in a pool of rain, piss, and blood. That...was the turning point. Mayhem looks at the dumpster and notice the dent he made. The rain must have washed away the blood, but the dent will be prominent until they decide to destroy this thing. Mayhem runs his hand over the newly formed dent, than puts the hand back to his head and winces with the touch. Mayhem looks left and right than left again. He see's traffic blowing passed the alley and decides to walk towards it, walk towards where he believes there will be civilization. I must figure out where I am. I walk towards the onslaught of traffic. I hear the roar of mufflers, chirps of birds, and mumbles of conversation. I am limping. My knee that was destroyed in Belmont at the hands of Lestatt Knight is starting to ache again. I haven't had my routine Cortizone Shot in what seems like forever. The end of the alley seems to be forever and a day away. I pick up my pace, and with that my limp becomes more extreme. As I walk towards life, I look behind me and catch a final glimpse of the alley. My womb, the burning red of a new life. Bryan Mayhem's new beginning started on this very day. I come out of the alley and I met with a gust of a heavy carbon monoxide infected air. I keel over to catch my breathe. Spittle forms at my lips as I spit out the bad I just inhaled. My stomach is empty and all I am able to vomit is bile and spit. There are a few people on the streets, and those that noticed me stared as they walked by. It's not everyday you see a man of my size keeled over and vomiting. Besides I must look a wreck, because if I look half as bad as I feel I would stare too. Cars are zipping by, throwing mist from the wet ground into the air. The sky is still a murky grey, and there's not a sign of the sun ever coming out. I limp towards the curb, most people looking at me as they walk by. I put my hand up, and start to wave down a taxi. At first a few pay no mind and keep hauling ass down the street. I am starting to get angry. I am in public however, and I must be able to keep a hold on my anger or otherwise I will be sitting in a jail cell with someone else's blood on my hands. As fun as it may be, I have to get back. I have to find whatever it is I lost. I step immediately into oncoming traffic, and turn to my left and stare down this taxi that's coming at break neck speed. I hear the screech of tires on wet pavement and watch as this taxi slides inches from my knees. The stop is so violent I can see the driver's neck jar back quickly. He opens his door and gets out rubbing his neck. "What the fuck?" He than takes a good look at me and cowers. "Man what the hell happened?" I look at him, I know I am expressionless, because I feel no muscle movement in my face. "Take me to the airport." "Airport?" He shuts his door and walks towards me. He's a little white man with a horrible bald spot. His glasses are crooked, his nose is red and veiny showing that he sucks down a bottle or 2 after a shift, or hell maybe even during. He hasn't shaved, but his hair is growing in disgusting patches. There are coffee and god knows what other kind of stains all over his white button up shirt. His name is Paul. "Big guy, you don't look so good. You need a hospital not an airport." He is testing my patience. Who is he to tell me what exactly I need. "I said take me to the airport." He's hesitant than looks back at his cab. "Buddy you are a bloody mess, I don't want you bleeding in the back of my car, because my stomach can't take it." I walk towards him and get within inches. He stands barely 5'5". My towering bulk creates another shadow over him. My fist clench, as do my teeth. My head is throbbing, my knees are shot and this piece of shit wants to tell me what to do. "I am not going to ask you again. Take me to the airport. If you fail to do as I wish I will be forced to crush your larynx with my bare hand." With that I show him my broken and battered right hand. He smiles. You can tell he's scared for his life, but people are looking at us, and this man must have some reputation to uphold because he's attempting to mask his fear. "OK partner, get in, but if you spill one drop of blood I will kick your teeth in, you hear me?" I squat down and stare at him. "Threaten me again. Try it." I stand back up straight and head to the backseat. Like all taxis this thing is made for the average size person. I have little room, but I am not looking for comfort at this particular point in time. I must get to an airport. "So big guy, I don't know if I should but I am going to ask. What on earth happened to you?" He looked back at me before accelerating. My eyes stayed locked on his, and I simply pointed towards the front. He must have understood what I wanted because he hauled ass away. Although I was cramped and a little disoriented the silence and stillness that was surrounding me put me at ease. I felt comfortable almost, than he opened his mouth. "Seriously man, if you were attacked let me know I can help. Than again who's dumb enough to attack a giant like you. Goddamn how tall are you? So what happened? When..... I began to tune him out, his bantering was really getting on my last nerve and if I started to discuss anything with him I am probably going to rip his heart from his chest. "....cause man you look fucked up. Is it drugs?" I clench my fists, and politely, well as politely as Bryan Mayhem gets, say "What happened to me is none of your concern, shut your mouth and take me to the hospital." He gave me a dirty look in the rearview and than kept driving. He turned on the radio, and began to mouth the words to this awful whiny British sounding shit. My head was really starting to ache, and I felt an urge to snack this man's neck and just watch as the taxi runs blindly into whatever it feels necessary. Oh my god, I think I am going to snap, when all of a sudden we come to a stop and he turns down his shitty fucking music and says. "All here pal, that will be $23.75" I reach into my pocket and pull out my soaked wallet. I hand him a hundred and say "You almost got yourself killed today. Keep the change." The look on his face was priceless, but when he saw the tip he got out quickly opened my door. "Hey good luck. You ever need a ride you ask for Paul in car 45542, I'll drop what I am doing and come get you." As I walked passed him and his annoying jibe I lifted my elbow and felt a crunch than heard a thud as my little taxi driver fell to the ground unconscious. I looked at him briefly, smiled, than looked ahead and saw the Airport Terminal. I am almost there.....
