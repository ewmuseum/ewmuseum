finding yourself

18 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18 Oct 05 11 OCT 05 Alone, it was a word I was used to by now. Most would find this troubling; to be alone a lot of the time but for me it's almost a sanctuary. It gave me the time to let my mind wander, to let my emotions get in check. God knows that if anyone can let their emotions get the better of them it is me. That's how I have come into the situation that I am in today. Depression, anger, and sorrow filled me to the brim and I was an easy target for him. I had fought so hard to keep balance and control, but what most will find out is that at your most fragile of moments any kind of outlet for the pain sounds like a genius idea. He knew I was ready to crack, how could he have not? The man probably knows me better than I even know myself. I should not have listened to him, I know this and I go over what happened every moment. I was set up, Mary had her reasons for leaving and it wasn't the ones that I found in that letter. The problem I have been having is figuring out exactly who it was that set me up. There are only a handful of people who would do such a thing, and two of them are dead. I had no way of knowing what to do next or what direction I was even to head in. I am lost in the darkness with no form of light. -Roberto Abitua- 'It was a successful mission RaZ, end of story.' I brushed passed my rather large compadre with a slight roll of the eyes. I know his intentions are always in the right place but there are times when his constant worrying just really pisses me off. 'But look Roberto I just think..' 'Well good thing I don't pay you to think David.' I said cutting him off, narrowing my eyes at him. His posture straightens all the way now. A look comes across his face that was similar to a scolded dog or a child with it's feelings hurt. 'Look Dave I know what I am doing. I brought you back because you are my friend and I trust you to watch my back. Now the other stipulation you had was to do what I ask regardless of who it may hurt. Why are you acting all chivalrous anyways? Its fucking Eva VanLorne, its not like I am plotting against Wylie or something.' 'You've done that before too!' He blurts out, this causes me to sigh and cock my head to the side. “Amigo you worry too much, you always have.” I say this as I put a grin on my face and move closer to him. “You’re compassionate, far more than I am, which is another attribute you’ve had since I’ve met you. What you need to learn is that not everyone deserves the benefit of the doubt. Eva VanLorne is just some cold bitch who got what was coming to her, and it was far overdue if you ask me.” “Look Roberto, as your friend I am just beginning to wonder why you are doing these things. You may one day end up making far more enemies than you need to. Its okay to attack but choose your spots wisely. You cannot fight them all at once.” “Says who?” I bark this, allowing the smile from my face to wipe away. “Who do you see capable of beating me” I have taken them all on David, every last one of them that mean a damn have felt my power. Eight months! Eight fucking months and I have lost ONE match that meant anything. Trey fucking Tremendous pulled a fast one on me, caught me with my guard down because of Jason Dread. Since that day I have won in dominating fashion. Sure Stevens came close but what happened with a second bout? Not only did I rip him limb from limb but he has disappeared. Its becoming the “IN” thing to do after being obliterated by my hand. First Jason Dread not Michael Stevens.” “Look you are right, you are the best. I have never seen you so determined, so full of fire. One on one you are unstoppable, but that’s just my point. I cannot be everywhere. When you continuously make new enemies it makes both of our lives harder. It doesn’t matter how smart you are or how powerful you are ? you cannot defeat the entire world at one time.” “Ah yes.” I back up a few steps before turning slightly and leaning my body weight on the bar in my hotel room. “The exact downfall of Hitler. He tried doing too much, too fast and ultimately it brought on his fall from power. You make valid points RaZ, you always have.” “Thank you amigo, now just listen?” “NO! I am not finished.” I could tell my voice came out in almost a growl, and my cheeks began to warm up from the rage that was beginning to boil up. “I love you like a brother, but this is not personal. When it comes to GCW it is strictly business. You are paid to do a job, so stop asking questions and do your fucking job or else I will end this partnership.” “You are willing to throw away our friendship again?” “No RaZ, I am not going to throw away this friendship but if you are too chicken shit to carry out the orders I give you, then you don’t need to be in this line of work. If I want to dominate the opposition and I mean ALL of the opposition then for fuck’s sake do it with me. If you are scared, then say you are scared!” My eyes turn to meet his. For the first time in a long time I saw that same fire, that passion burning in his eyes. Anger and frustration were building up inside of him. I almost wanted to smile, that all knowing smile but I kept myself in check. I glared back into those brown eyes of his, showing him that I will not back down. “I am not afraid of anything.” His voice was low but something in it was threatening. I stand up and face him. “Excuse me” I am AFRAID that I did not hear you.” “I DO NOT FEAR ANYTHING!” he roared. “Good.” I smile now. “Then do as I say. Ruthless aggression is what its called. No mercy, no remorse. We cannot show signs of weakness. You and I are so close to immortality that I can damn near TASTE it! Don’t throw it away now. Together we will rule the GCW with that iron fist that a man like Octavian, Jason Dread, or Michael Stevens could never understand. RaZ you and I can be gods amongst men.” He didn’t say anything back but that slight smirk told me what he was thinking. I knew how to push this man’s buttons just as I knew how to push just about everyone else’s buttons. I could convince this man to commit murder for me right now and he probably wouldn’t even think twice. I am the King ? GCW is my kingdom. Everyone else ? simply pawns for me to play with. Including RaZiel. 17 OCT 05 No matter what I try he keeps his tight grip around my throat, slowly squeezing the life from my body. Darkness and rage make up who I am now, and in some ways I enjoy it even though I fully understand I shouldn’t. LeStatt Knight is despicable yet he seems to always make sense to me. He puts people through pain and sorrow, yet he comes out on top of it all. Even if I managed to escape this dark prison, would I even have the power to destroy him? Do I even want to take back control? -Roberto Abitua-
