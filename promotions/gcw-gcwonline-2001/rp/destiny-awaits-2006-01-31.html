Destiny Awaits

31 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 31, 2006 ---- Prelude: The Black Mariah ---- Once when I was a very little girl, my mother told me that if I ever felt alone I could look up to the sky and Jesus would be there with me. What a load of crap that is. Sometimes you just feel out of place, and that's the way things are meant to be. It's like I'm lost on the streets of Chicago again, with no place to live. Going from day to day making guys buy you things because they think their checkbook is the way into your pants. Wondering as you looked into the stars at night before you fell asleep on a rooftop if this is really the right decision. Hoping the old saying was false and you really could go home again. These are different times now though. Now when I look around I see nothing but familiar faces, walking through these strange corridors with me on our way to make a name for ourselves. Global Championship Wrestling, how in the blue hell did I end up here? Never in a million years did I think that professional wrestling is where I would end up one day. This is my new life... surrounded by a cast of characters that I would've laughed about 3 years ago. Virgil Ambrosi, vampire extrordinaire and one of the most stubborn bastards I've ever met before in my life. I find comfort that he's so protective of Lia, but sometimes I wonder what would happen if he were to meet me along in a dark alley somewhere. He's cold and cunning, everything you could ever want from your lap dog. Lia has always seen something more in him though. Maybe if he ever took the time to show that side to me instead of making me as uncomfortable as possible, I might get to like the guy. Dawn McFarlane is lost upon me, as she was upon Christian and almost every other person she's ever met before in her life. Tall, beautiful, and talented. Almost everything a girl could possibly want to be in their life. She's also completely nuts. Sometimes I understand her, we used to be able to talk but these days... I don't know her anymore. She's in her own little world, trying desperately to retain the sanity she once had. She is getting better now... at least now she's acknowledges my existence in the world. The assassins... honestly their names escape me at the moment. I've never been very good at remembering names as it is. We just seem to exist in the same plane. We have totally different lives from completely different backgrounds. Add to that the fact that I've never seen them speak a word, and you might understand why I never remember their names. They are just there to me. Maybe some day I'll get to hear their stories, and they will get to hear mine. For now though... my life is a bit too complicated to be worried about two people who won't even speak to me. Lia, most people know her as Shadow. Never in a million years did I believe when I first met Shadow when she was trying to kill me in a tennis court outside of the Zenith Corporation building in the rain that we'd become such good friends. I believe it was that day I earned her respect, and she's spent the last year earning mine. My tag team partner and the only girl I've been friends with since High School. She's the reason I'm here afterall. I wanted to be by her side, and she wants me here. There is so much I could learn from her, and I hope one day to be as successful as she has been in her life. When it all comes down to it though, we're a team. Like each other or not, we're in this together now and we have to learn to get along. In the ring, all bets are off and whatever little spats we seem to have go away. Surrounded by vampires and cat assassins. It sounds like some crappy horror novel doesn't it? This is my life now... although now that I think about it, is this really for me? I stopped when 'I Want To Conquer The World' by Bad Religion started playing over the arena in the distance. The cheering crowd, the spotlight, the celebrity... I know I could succeed but is this what my life want meant to be? Isn't this almost the same thing I ran from in Highland Park so long ago? ---- First Passage: The End Of A Dynasty ---- Global Wrestling regrets to announce the retirement of 2005 Fighter of the Year and 2 time former World Champion Christian Zenith from the octagon. His contract had expired and he had taken the option not to resign. With his contract also expired those of Vivica J. Valentine and Jason LeMaster, they also took the option not to resign. We wish Christian luck with his business problems in Chicago and hope that he will return one day. - Brandi Sights - Global Wrestling Chairwoman A flash of light ignites the sky, silhouetting the Zenith Manor North in Chicago, Illinois. It's size and peaks remind people in the area of an old English castle. The children are scared to approach the Manor on Halloween and have started rumors in Elementary School of the Manor itself being haunted. Not surprising, seeing as the Manor has been more or less vacant for the past year, other than the two housekeepers who live inside that only leave to pick up groceries and other necessities. As the gates opened to the massive estate people from around the area watched as a black limousine pulled up the winding drive for the first time in what seemed like an eternity. A cold frozen rain pounded on the metal roof of the limo, making it sound as if it were under fire from the inside. His cadet blue eyes looked outside at the scenery he remember being landscaped when he was a boy. He was home again, like it or not he'd have to get used to the easy life. Dressed in a black custom made suit, he adjusted his black necktie before looking back in the limo across from him. She sat there with a black leather bomber jacket on, over top a black As I Lay Dying baby-tee and a pair of baggy black cargo jeans. Her long blonde hair pulled back in a ponytail, exposing her greenish cerulean eyes between two thin strands of hair hanging down over her temples. An obvious look of distraught was across her face, as she looked blankly out the window towards the Manor. 'You know what I had to do Vivica. If I didn't do this we would lose everything from all those incompetent bastards who made their way up to my front office.' He said aloud, 'They were running everything we've worked hard for into the ground. We'd be penniless and in shambles by the time they were done. Selling our secrets to other companies. I'll be damned if I become the laughing stock of the business world.' 'So you're just going to lose to Jake Hix and walk away... like nothing happened?' Vivica J. Valentine replied, taking her eyes off the Manor to shoot daggers back at Christian Zenith across from her. 'I can take care of Jake Hix when I so please. One on one I would've walked away from that place a champion.' Christian replied, 'Jake Hix will get his you can count on that Vivica.' 'When?' Vivica replied, 'When are you going to do anything about it? After you're done playing monopoly? What in the hell is wrong with you Christian? This isn't like you at all, these little things always work themselves out.' 'Enough.' Christian said, narrowing his eyes as he glanced back at Vivica, 'This will be fixed, this company is my life and my first priority. Always has been. Global was nothing more than a game for me... a hobby, a pastime. Like collecting baseball cards or playing video games. Nothing more. Maybe you're the one who got her priorities messed up somewhere.' 'Zenith Corporation was never a priority of mine.' Vivica replied, 'You are my priority, not your business or your money. What in the hell do you expect me to do now Christian?' 'You'll always have a job with Zenith Corporation.' 'I don't want a fucking job with Zenith Corporation.' Vivica's reply seemed to take Christian a little off guard, 'I'm not a business woman Christian, I'm not cut out for this world. It doesn't interest me... it's not what I want to do for the rest of my life.' 'Then what brilliant idea do you have without Z Corp?' Christian said, taking his eyes off of The Black Mariah as the limo came to a stop. 'I don't... know.' Vivica said, 'I really don't know.' The door opened to the limousine and the sound of sleet bouncing off the metal and concrete turned into a roar. Defiantly she walked through the storm, staying a few paces behind Christian... maybe that way he'll get the point that she's serious about this. After a few seconds of weathering the storm she made her way inside the Manor, which seemed to come back to life upon their arrival. The dark gloomy halls grew light in rejuvenation with a flick of the switch from the Lord of the Manor. 'Maybe you should take a little bit of time to think about your future Vivica,' Zenith said as he hung up his suit jacket revealing his trademark sleeveless white dress shirt under his black suit vest, 'because Z Corp is a more fulfilling job than you might expect. I'm sure I could find you something in research or development.' A single glare from her eyes let Christian know that she wasn't exactly entertaining offers. Turning her back she began to ascend the staircase up to the Master Bedroom. Upon entering the room everything looked exactly as it did the first time she'd seen it so many years ago. It was like nothing had changed... but in reality everything had changed. Her entire life had changed. No longer was she a penniless runaway, now she had a home and a life... no matter how in doubt it might be. Vivica walked over to the window and looked down at the city of Chicago over the horizon. Maybe something was out there for her, afterall it was a big city. Pressing her hand against the cold glass she sighed and closed her eyes. If only they didn't have to come back, why couldn't things just go as they had for the past year? A series of steps caught her attention as they grew close. Someone was coming up the staircase. Looking over her shoulder she saw Marques standing in the doorway. 'Miss Valentine?' Marques said, waiting for a response. 'Not now Marques.' 'But it's very important Miss Valentine.' Marques said as he walked into the room and Vivica simply shook her head. 'Tell me la...' 'I have a message from Miss Lia Vlaszy.' Those two words made Vivica's eyes open up wide. It was Shadow... she was alive. Quickly Vivica spun around and looked at Marques in anticipation. She'd spent so many hours trying to find Lia and now she just appears out of nowhere. Vivica was so relieved her dreams of Shadow's death was a lie. 'She told me to tell you when you arrived Miss Valentine that she was staying at the McFarlane Estate, and would like to speak with you immediately.' Marques said before turning around, 'I suppose I'll leave you alone now, I hope you feel better Miss Valentine.' 'Wait Marques...' Vivica said, as the housekeeper stood in the doorway. Christian didn't have the answers she was looking for, but she was certain that Lia could help her. Lia always helped her, 'I'd like for you to get a limo ready for me. I'm taking a trip to the McFarlane Estate immediately.' ---- Second Passage: An Estate From The Past ---- Snow danced down from the heavens before being swept away by a mighty wind. The bright beam of oncoming headlights slices through the cold, dark Canadian air. Slowly the limo made it's way up the drive towards the front of the Estate. In front of the complex the limo came to a stop, the back door opened, and out into the winter wonderland stepped Miss Vivica J. Valentine. The dim lighting on the front of the Estate highlighted her beautiful blonde hair as she pulled her bomber jacket close, trying desperately to stay warm in the freezing Toronto air. Snowflakes swirled around her while she marched towards the entrance. Reaching out she pushed the doorbell, and could hear the chime inside echo throughout the Estate. Waiting outside Vivica took a deep sigh... her cheeks turning bright red from the winds beating against her face. She watched as her breath danced up to the sky, wondering if anyone was going to answer the door. It wasn't long before her question was answered and the door opened slowly behind her. She turned around and saw a familiar face... it was Alexis, Virgil and Lia's assistant who greeted her with a smile. 'Miss Valentine!' She said with a smile, 'It's been a long time.' 'It's been too long...' Vivica said before stepping out of the cold and into the warm mansion, 'How is everyone doing?' 'Everyone is fine now...' Alexis responded, 'You missed all the excitement.' 'I don't think I have.' Vivica responded, 'I think now that I'm here... the 'excitement' may just be getting started.' Vivica walked past Alexis and towards the stairwell, much to Alexis' dismay, who followed closely behind. Upon ascending the staircase, Vivica quickly spotted two familiar faces... and the conversation in the room suddenly went completely silent. Slowly she began to walk towards the two people in front of her. Virgil Ambrosi and Dawn McFarlane, both with their eyes shooting daggers back at The Black Mariah. Sensing the situation, Alexis backed away and made her way back downstairs... seemingly to safety. 'Where's Lia?' Vivica said and looked over at Dawn McFarlane, who simply responded by glancing back at Vivica and then walking past her and making her way down the staircase to join Alexis on the 1st Floor of the Estate. Vivica watched as Dawn walked away, then looked up at Virgil Ambrosi who just looked back at her. She didn't have to say a word to him, he knew that he didn't keep his promise to her, and she knew damn well that he didn't care. The squeak of a door cut the tension in the room like a knife, as Shadow stepped out from the darkness and into the room. She paused in her steps and looked over at Virgil, and then back at Vivica who stood in the room seemingly isolated from the person standing almost right next to her. 'Welcome to the McFarlane Estate Vivica...' Lia shoots a look back over to Virgil, 'I'm happy to see you, let's talk in the other room over here shall we?' With a bit of an arrogant smile Vivica looks over at Virgil, almost taunting him as she followed Shadow into the other room. The darkness swallowed Shadow and Vivica, as they closed the door behind them. With a flick the dim lighting of the room sparked on, allowing Vivica to examine her new surroundings. Two chairs and a bed is all the room consisted of. The bed looked very well used, almost as if Shadow hadn't left it for months. 'It's been a while hasn't it?' Lia said before taking a seat in one of the chairs, motioning Vivica to do the same in the chair across from her, 'How is Christian?' 'I don't know about Christian...' Vivica said, 'He's been acting so strange lately. As long as I've known that man I've still never quite figured out what goes on in his head. I've been told by a lot of his associates that enigmatic personality is the mark of a genius. I think they're just trying to kiss ass personally.' 'That could be true.' Lia replied before getting a little more comfortable. 'Where in the hell have you been? Are you okay?' Vivica blurted out, looking over at her friend who almost predicted the question before it happened. 'Basically... the me you saw last was a mindless puppet.' Shadow explained, 'That is why I seemed not of myself. I'd actually left for a bit of time to find out what was really going on in my life. I ended up in the desert in this small bar with a bastard named Dominic. I'd gone days without feeding, I was really to weak to get up and walk away. He took me off guard and kidnapped me, locked me away in his basement.' 'I'll show that fucker...' Vivica was quickly interrupted. 'Don't bother...' Lia stated, 'Apparently he was a servant of another vampire like myself, who used Global as a test to see what I was capable of doing. Eventually Virgil figured out everything, and came and got me... I don't know exactly what happened from day to day, Alexis had actually explained most of it to me on my arrival back here to the Estate. I've hardly left this room, I was very weak but I'm feeling much better now. As much as I may hate to say it, I missed your company so I invited you to stop by.' 'Thanks...' Vivica said with a smile, 'But your little lap dog was supposed to give me a call when they found you and tell me what was going on.' 'Don't mind Virgil,' Shadow explained, 'he isn't exactly very accepting of mortals in general. I can say that I've been guilty of much the same. I've just grow close to very few on my time on this planet... mainly Dawn, yourself, and a few others. It's difficult for us to associate mortals with friendship... it goes against our genetic makeup.' 'And Dawn?' Vivica stated. 'Sometimes I don't even understand why Dawn acts the way she does. Be patient and give her time, I'm sure he'll come around...' Shadow replied, 'Don't mind them... I have a feeling they won't mind you anymore soon enough.' 'I seriously doubt that.' Vivica responded. 'Listen, I have a business proposition for you.' Shadow said relaxing a bit looking into her tag partner's eyes, 'As you may or may not know, around the same time Christian retired from Global Wrestling they decided to close and then reopen immediately, in an effort to rehire their expensive talent at a much cheaper rate and make money in their otherwise floundering business. It's a scam used by casino's and businesses all over Vegas. The only downside is the talent has to willingly resign... and I decided against it, as did Virgil and Dawn soon thereafter.' 'Alright...' Vivica listened in anticipation. 'We've been fielding offers from federations ever since, and this is the one we're looking into,' Lia said before handing Vivica an envelope, 'Global Championship Wrestling. It's a federation in PrimeTime Central... just like Global was. Based out of Columbus, Ohio... being a Midwest girl yourself I thought maybe you could lend us your services. I do need a tag partner afterall.' 'Have you talked to Dawn and Virgil about this?' Vivica questioned. 'Not yet,' Shadow said with a smile, 'It's a non-issue. This isn't Hunting Grounds anymore Vivica, this is my ship. If they're willing to come with me, they have to accept my decisions. My decision is that I want you here with me, and they'll like it. Whether they want to like it or not.' Vivica sat there in silence looking down at the information on the envelope in front of her. She hadn't done anything without Christian since she was a teenager. Going on her own had never crossed her mind, regardless of the circumstances. It'd been such a long time she'd almost forgotten what it was like... but with Dawn and Virgil? No matter what Lia told her she knew deep down inside the they'd never accept her. Virgil had always looked down upon her, so she'd never had favorable opinion of him. Once upon a time she'd considered Dawn a friend... but somewhere she'd lost Dawn to her own demons. 'I don't know...' Vivica responded, looking back at Shadow. 'It's okay,' Shadow said before she got up, 'but I want you to think about it for a long time. Just remember I want you to be here with us. Forget about what happened in Global, we all know you're capable of so much more than distracting referees and taking cheap shots. With us you'll be a valued member of our team. You won't be shoved out of the spotlight like Zenith did to you. Don't you ever wonder how well you'd do if you had a fair chance for once? Just think about it. Give me a call when you make up your mind, because if you ask me, right now I'm looking at a champion.' 'A champion...' Vivica said to herself in her mind as she looked back at her tag team partner with a smile. Maybe it was her time to achieve the greatness she'd spent a year watching Zenith have. Lia had taken the time to escort Vivica outside back to her limo. 'A champion' echoed in her mind repeatedly on the long drive back from Toronto to Chicago. Vivica would swear she even heard it in her dreams that night. ---- Prologue: Embracing Destiny ---- 'A champion...' I said to myself before I snapped back to reality again. I didn't realize my short pause had caught the attention of everyone around me. Everyone was staring at me, how long had I wandered off. Looking around I didn't know if they were concerned or frustrated as I stood alone in the middle of the hallway. Suddenly I made eye contact with Lia, she looked back at me with a look of confidence and certainty. 'Are you okay?' Lia asked me, the sound of her voice was like music to my ears. 'Yeah, I'm fine.' I said with a hint of a smile, it was forced. 'I was just thinking, that's all.' 'Then come on, we can't be late.' Lia responded with a smile, 'We'll miss our grand debut.' With that said I started forward again. Everything was beginning to fall into place. Maybe this was my new home, I just needed to be more patient. As I walked towards curtain everything became clear to me. The years of uncertainty of my life, the intense training I'd received under Christian, and meeting all the right people along the way. This wasn't the wrong decision at all. When I left home 5 years ago, it wasn't a mistake... it was my destiny. This is my new life, this is my destiny. Global Championship Wrestling better be prepared... my destiny awaits. My destiny, begins now.
