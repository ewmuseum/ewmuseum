The Freaks Ride Again

1 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/1/05 *A full moon its in the clear midnight sky, it illuminates all that it can see in its brilance. A building can be seen in the distance which a light that flickers on and off every few seconds, as I start to walk closer the flickering light starts to take shape, and as you get right up in front of it it reads 'Steve's Bar'. The front door swings open and you get a big breath of the atmosphere inside.* *The hair on the back of your neck starts to stand up, as you can smell alcohol, cigarette and cigar smoke, and theres one smell that you can't pick up on, but you know what it is, and its a smell you remember. You open the door, and step into the bar, which is covered in a thin layer of smoke coming from everyones cigar, and cigarette smoke in the room.* *You make your way up to the bar, and take a seat at the only empty barstool thats there. As you sit down the guy next to you taps you on the shoulder, and you turn to him* 'Hey are you that giant man from the GCW, that i saw on worldwide who attacked his own tag team partner, because he didn't get to fight in his tag team match.' says the gentleman next to you 'Yes, I am', I reply *I wave down the bartender and order a my favoirte drink, a tall cold glass of Budweiser beer. I take a cigar out of my jacket pocket, and proceed to join into the smoking like everyone else had* *As I start to drink my beer, and puff on my cigar, I feel another tap on my shoulder. So, I grab the ashtray and place my cigar down onto it, my beer i sti back on the bar. I turn around only to see a familar face, a face I haven't seen in ages.* 'X', I Said 'Yes, It's Me';he replied *I think to myself when the last time i saw X-Calibur, and then it comes to me the last time i saw him was that big tag team match in 3WA, when he got badly hurt, and had to leave do to his injury, its been almost 2hrs since this man, the man i spilled my own blood with, the man that is my equal, and maybe even my brother* 'X, where have you been? I haven't sen you in almsot two years, and then I get back into in GCW, and i get a phone call from you out of the blue, whats up buddy?' 'well, to answer your first question I've been in a german hospital trying to get my injuury healed, and the reason why I called you out of the blue is that I was watching GCW, and I saw what EVL and that Jordan White guy said. They wanted a real tag team, they wanted a tag team that could give them a run for there money at those tag team titles, so I was thinking why not get The Sick Freaks back together to teach this younger generation what it means to give your blood, sweat, and even more blood to get those titles, and to even keep them' 'Well your are right X, EVL and Jordan white did say that, and I know Caldera knows that we were one of the best, one of the most suicidal, homicidal, genocidal tag teams in GCW history, we would put our bodies on teh line to get gold, and even bleed to get them. SO at Dangerous Games we get a chance to fight EVL, and whoever these Bosom Buddies are in a tag team match for the tag team titles. What they dont know is that this might be there last match as tag team champions, and maybe even there last match in GCW history if The Sick Freaks get there way.' *I wave down the bartender and order a beer for X-Calibur, the bartender sets a beer down in front of him, he picks it uop, and downs it in one drink. I reach into my jacket pocket once again, and pull out another cigar, and hand it to him. He lights it up, and takes a puff off of it, and sets it down on the ashtray. I pause, and look around to room to find a open table, I see one in the corner.* *I stand up, and make my way over to the table, I pull out a chair, and X-Calibur pulls out the other one.* 'So what do you think about Dangerous Games?' 'Well X, I know we got this tag team match, but I also got this 30 man BAttle Royal thing.' 'You do D' 'Yup, and all i gotta say is that a 30 Man Battle Royal, which got its beginng from Mr.Danger. The thing is that I haven't won a battle royal once in GCW history, and there is a first time for everything. So what Im gonnado is let everyone else fight each other, then me, the big man will come in a tag advantage of there weakness,a nd live my destiny to win a battle royal in GCW' 'OK, well i need to get going D, its late, and I gotta start preparing for the match, and get into the right frame of mind' 'Ok X, well ill see ya at dangerous games, oh and by the way make sure that blood bank we go to has your blood type again.' 'Ok D, that was funny' *As i see X-Calibur walk out the door, I finish my beer, and my cigar, and i see a loyal GCW fan walk over to me* 'Mr. Dimensioner can i get your autograph, and ask you about your match at Dangerous Games, and what your gonna do?' 'Ok Kid, heres the autograph, and for your question at Dangerous Games EVL and Bosom Buddies better call there local blood bank to nake sure they have the right blood in for them, because there blood will be spilt I guarentee you, and I'm sorry to say Miss Eva Van Lorne even thought your pretty damn hot your blood has to be spilt to becasue once you step into the ring with the most Demented Man in GCW history blood will flow' *The fan walks away from the table, and i stand up, and head towards the door, and as I do the bartender stops me, and trys to give me the tap for the two beers. I stare down at him, and he shutters, and walks back to behind the bar. I open the door to go outside and the bouncer steps into my way, the bouncer which is the same size, as me trys to grab me by the neck and push me back itno the bar, all i can do is laugh, as i lift him up into the air, and drop him onto my knee.* *I walk out of the bar, and quietly say under my voice* 'At Dangerous Games those tag team titles will be The Sick Freaks again, and the blood of EVL will be all over them'
