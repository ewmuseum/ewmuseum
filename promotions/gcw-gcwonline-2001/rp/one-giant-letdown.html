One Giant Letdown

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: The day after WW 1? 2? Before the referee had even reached three, Tessa realized that the match was over with. She’d never make it into the ring to make the save. Groggily, she stood to her feet as the number three was announced by the referee. It sucked, but while that number rang throughout her head, she had come to terms with the fact that she had let Vivica down. More importantly than letting Vivica down though, was letting her own damn self down. There she was, looking to break away from Banned & Exiled~!, looking to climb back up to the peak of the mountain she’d fallen off years ago. Instead, she found herself taking two giant steps back. Not only did her and Vivica lose, but here she was looking for Vivica to save her. Now, the only thing Tessa was looking like was a total hypocrite. “I’m so tired of this crap!” Were the first words to spill from Tessa’s mouth the minute she stepped through the front door of her home in Chatham, Massachusetts. The words bounced off the portrait covered walls and into the ears of anyone who was within two hundred feet. If that weren’t enough to catch their attention, the crashing of her gym bag against the closet door might have done the job. “I take it you didn’t have a good time last night?” The voice of Tessa’s thirteen year old niece turned daughter caught her off guard. Not that it really should have, considering just how loud Tessa was being. Without bothering to even look at her niece, Tessa immediately went to work on the strap of her plaid patterned pumps, which complimented her red halter top quite nicely, “You could say that.” “At least you came home in one piece.” Christa shrugged as her mother kicked off the first shoe, “You’re in a lot better shape that what you’ve been other times.” The second shoe landed just short of Christa’s feet, “I’d much rather be in bad shape right now.” Christa’s looked up from the shoe to her mother, whose hair was a prime example of just how much of a mess she was right now, “Why’s that?” “It’d be a much better excuse for the lackluster performance I gave last night.” Tessa’s coat was the last thing to come off, this time actually landing in Christa’s arms. “You’re too hard on yourself.” Christa commented, hanging the coat on the doorknob rather than inside the closet. She presumed her mother wouldn’t be staying long should Scott actually be willing to stay home a bit longer to watch the kids. Already halfway up the stairs, Tessa turned to comment, “I am not!” It wasn’t until she turned around that she noticed, Scott Drake, known to GCW world as Chris Storm, was coming down in the opposite direction. By then, it was too late. WHAM! With how anal Scott was about having a broad, muscular, and highly talked about body, walking into a brick wall might have hurt less that it did plowing straight into his chest. Luckily, with all the training he did, he also learned to be quick to react, and caught her before she fell down the stairs. Rather than show nay sort of concern for his fianc?, Scott went straight for the teasing, “Wow. With eyesight like that, it’s not a wonder you lost.” She didn’t bother to catch the breath she lost from that scare, “SCOTT, DON’T START!” Instinctively his hands started to lift, until he realized that his hands were helping Tessa keep her balance, “Whoa, whoa, whoa, I’m kidding killer. You lost, big deal. Make up for it by beating Terror this week.” Even if Terror was a candidate for highest rising star of 2007, it still wouldn’t make up for her letting down Vivica. She shifted her weight to the right side, hoping to escape from his grasp, and the rest of this entire conversation, “Just let go of me.” For the first time in a long time, Scott grew tired of her always trying to weasel away, and opted to continue his clinging, “No.” Tessa looked up, her eyes wide as can be, “I need to feed Aislinn.” “I already did.” “Great.” Automatically she smiled, and tried to pretend that this miniature meltdown had never occurred, “Than I can get the laundry started.” “Did that too.” “Oh?” She peeped out in surprise. She moved down her mental list to the next task at hand, “Well the boys?” “Stop Tessa.” Scott said firmly, pushing her chin up so that her eyes were facing his, “You need to tell me what the hell has been going on with you.” She pulled her head back down, refusing to look him in the eye, “I’m fine, Scott.” He loosened his grasp some, realizing that in her eyes, he was probably coming off as an controlling asshole, “If you’re fine, then why are you insisting on doing things on your own” That’s not like you.” She ceased this opportunity to dramatically rip her arm away from him, as if he were trying to beat her, or something, “Is it wrong for me to want to have a life outside of your shadow, Scott?” It left him speechless. He climbed two steps ahead of her, to let her see that he respected her distance, but it did little good. Her voice only increased, as did the dramatics. “Of course it’s wrong!” She preached, “Because that’s the role everyone wants me to play. They want me to be your freaking damsel in distress. Then, all of a sudden I decide I want to be Ms. Independent and actually do something for myself and what happens” Everyone hates me. Because lord knows I’m nothing but eye candy without you. ? Not surprisingly, this humored Scott, who always liked to feel like he had some kind of importance in her life, “Done yet?” It certainly wasn’t the response she expected, or was wanting, namely because it wouldn’t feed her more material for a second dramatic monologue, “It’s not fair Scott. I’ll never get the respect I deserve because I can’t go out there and entertain like you. I’m as good as nothing alone.” “You’re being too hard on yourself.” This time the words came from Scott’s mouth. Of course Christa had been eavesdropping from the downstairs hallway the entire time, “That’s what I said!” “I’m not.” She protested, finally brushing past him to reach the top of the stairs, “Just ask anyone whose been watching me over the last few years. I always choke before I can reach my peak.” “You don’t think I’ve been watching you?” Scott inquired, a bit hurt that his opinion apparently didn’t count. “You don’t count.” She replied, wandering off down the hall, no doubt to the bedroom, where she’d lock herself up and mentally abuse herself for slipping down a notch in her own eyes. His ego was cut down a block or two, but that didn’t stop him from trying to save her from the mental punishment she was about to inflict upon herself, “Its one match, Tessa. If you win this week against Jay Terror, do you know what everyone will be saying?” The self loathing trip continued, “That I got lucky.” Of course, the man known to GCW as Chris Storm, had to have something quirky to say, “Fuck them then, just not literally.” Scott hurried down the hall to catch up with her, “Because YOU and I know better than that.” “I don’t care about you and I!” She shouted at him, even though he was less than a foot behind her, “You and I can’t make up peoples minds for them. I actually want people to believe I’m good! If I keep hanging around you and Markus, do you really think that’s going to happen?” Scott didn’t bother to fuel the fire this time. He offered only his upmost condolences, “I’m sorry.” It dug at her heart some, but not enough to change her mind, “Just forget about it.” She opened the bedroom door, and reached over to the opposite side of the doorknob to grab her purse. “I’m going shopping.” She informed Scott, heading back down the same set of stairs she’d just ascended. “Don’t you think you need a rest?” “This is a rest.” She informed him as she headed out the front door without looking back. The door shut, and just as every other time before, him and Christa were left in silence. His angel has slipped yet another step away from him. Now, he only prayed that he could pull her back before she slipped to far away into the devil’s hands.
