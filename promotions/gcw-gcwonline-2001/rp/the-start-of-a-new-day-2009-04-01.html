The Start of A New Day

1 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/01/2009 I ignore the loud, insistent beeping of the alarm clock and keep my eyes closed determined to fall back asleep. But despite my efforts I am unable to keep my eyes closed, and instead sit up in the luxurious bed. It is a far cry from what I once had even before my apartment building was burned down, and I plan to enjoy it as much as possible. A wave of guilt hits me as I recall that while I am enjoying it Max is probably on the run but I determinedly ignore it. I had already wasted enough of my life ever since I started my insane quest to save Ben. Well Ben was saved, I am protected from anyone who wants me dead, and Max has been paid. As far as I’m concerned any further troubles of his are his problem. Besides, if Max is smart he’ll leave the country for a year or so, but I don’t have that option in front of me. I won’t pretend that the benefit of having this nice hotel room has no bearing on my decision. It’s not necessarily a four-star hotel, but it is the very least a three and the furniture is nicer than what I had in my apartment originally. Add in the fact that the maids clean it up everyday and that I don’t pay a cent for it, and I have to admit that it is a pretty good deal. Stretching contently I slowly make my way out of bed, reluctantly stepping out into the cool air. I am reluctant to leave the room because today I know I have to start training in earnest again but its been so long since I’ve trained as I should that I know its going to feel like torture. But hell, all I need to do to motivate myself it imagine myself pinned by Mr. Danger and I am completely ready to run around the entire fucking planet as a warm-up run. To be honest I should have gone back into training the second I moved into the hotel, but I held off for a week. Mostly because I could not make myself believe that this wasn’t some sort of elaborate trap created just to fuck with my mind. But, as the days went by and nothing happened I began to live my life again. I began with a restaurant on Monday, a movie on Tuesday, and so forth. I happily jog to the gym enjoying the sight of the city as I do so. I know many people see New York City as ugly or disgusting but I’ve never been able to view it that way. If nothing else the sight of the skyscrapers never gets old, though I admit that the people of New York are disappointing me today. Its been five minutes and I have already ran past a couple that obviously had issues with domestic violence, a junkie, and a mother that was paying attention to everything but her five year old son. But I refuse to give them more than a moment’s consideration. I have learned my lesson by now, and that lesson is to never get involved with other people’s problems. For instance, if in the middle of the night I hear a woman getting slapped around I will promptly close my windows and go back to sleep. I know that might sound a little harsh and inconsiderate, but the consequences of being unable to mind my own business have proved too troublesome. I finally reached the gym but my grumbling stomach has decided to remind me that I’ve forgotten to get breakfast. Oh screw it, I’ll lift through the hunger pangs and my breakfast will be the reward. Once inside I head straight over to the weight room to get in my squats, but the knowledge of what they will do to my legs after all the time that has passed has me cringing. I begin to chuckle to myself as I stretch, because I’ve just remembered the first time I had done a squat when I was thirteen. *Thirteen years ago* “Um, excuse me sir,” I mutter at the gigantic man’s back. He turns around and looks down at me with a grin for which I am grateful. He has a balding head, a bit of a gut, and also looks like he could pick me up with one finger if he wanted to so I am hoping he’s not going mind me asking me how do this absurd packet. “Yea kid, what is it?” His tone is thankfully jovial which raises my confidence a bit. I have trouble enough dealing with jocks that were my own age, much less grown muscle men! “I am sorry to bother you,” I stammer uncomfortably, “but I was kind of hoping you could to tell me what a squat is?” The man looks at me for a moment before bursting out laughing. I can feel my face flush and my hands clench angrily. If there was one thing I am absolutely sure about it is my intelligence and I hate feeling dumb. But on the other hand it would be stupid for me to expect to know what all this crap means, I’ve haven’t ever been inside a weight room until last week. Even then all I did was walk around and watched kids my own age lift my body weight’talk about lowering your self-esteem. “So what is it that you’re doing this packet for anyway,” he asks referring to the packet of exercises the coach had passed out as he leads me over to the free weights. “Wrestling,” I mutter all too aware that my body is not the type one would associate with that of a wrestler. “My dad wanted me to play a sport and he said there was a lot of strategy involved so?” I trail off hoping that he’ll get the drift. Apparently he has because he is nodding. “I know what you mean kid. Some wrestlers see the sport as one big physical chess match, and you need to be smart if you want to play it that way.” *Present time* “I bet you thought you were pretty smart to play it that way didn’t ya?” A familiar voice tinged by harshness interrupts my reverie. I feel myself go cold and slowly turn around hoping that I am only imaging things. Max is standing in front of me with a gun in his hand and a pissed off expression on his face. Moreover it does not look like life has been good to Max. There’s a long ugly scar on his right cheek, at my best guess I would say it was caused by a knife. There’s at least four days worth of growth on his face, and its complimented by the heavy dark circles under his eyes. He’s wearing an old dirty sweatshirt and ripped jeans that have a strange stain on one side. To sum up Max has had it rough for awhile now, and he’s holding a gun aimed at me. The guy who sold him out and has been living in a pretty fancy hotel. I know Max is a horrible shot but there is no way he can miss considering how close he’s standing to me. I consider yelling for help and hoping he doesn’t want to shoot me in such a public place, but I quickly realize Max has probably already gone around the bend if he’s willing to aim a gun someone in broad daylight. Nor am I even going to try and disarm him, the only time I’ve seen that done is in the movies and I have a feeling that won’t work out too well if I attempt it. I open my mouth but Max speaks before I can get any words out. “Don’t say anything,” he hisses with malicious. “Don’t even do anything except walk, not run, out the door you backstabbing piece of shit. You think you can get the good life and just leave me behind” You even try to run away and I swear to fucking God I’ll shoot you even if we’re in the middle of a crowd of cops.” He means it, I can tell that he means it. My legs have turned to jelly but somehow I force them to carry me through the gym and out the door. I desperately try to think of something to say or do that will save me, but the gun that I know is pointed at my back keeps interrupting my thoughts. I can’t help wonder how Max managed to get a gun into the gym, then bitterly realize that the gym wasn’t exactly known for its high-class security. Normally there would be too many people around for Max to pull this off, but the gym was basically deserted when I had gotten there. Of course there is still the question of how he got into the gym at all since he’s not a member. Though its academic at this point, even if I figure out how he got in that won’t suddenly make him put down the very lethal gun pointed at my back. Why the fuck did I decide to lift before breakfast? My mind begins to gibber with fear as Max leads me into one of those stereotypical dark alleyways. Even though I can see people walking parallel to the entrances of the alley none of them spare a glance into the alley itself. They have no reason to, besides they know they might see something that they shouldn’t. Better for them to look straight ahead and ignore any possible wrongdoing. I force myself to calm down because if I don’t I might as well tell Max to shoot me now. My only chance is to look for some kind of opening to exploit and hopefully get the gun away from him before he managed to kill me. “So after all I’ve done for you,” Max snarls at me while I stare obsessively at the gun in his hand, “you stab me in the fucking back. You leave me out to dry while you get to relax in your fancy ass hotel. Guess what, while you’ve been sleeping I’ve been running for my fucking life. I called you a hundred times’and you don’t pick up. You don’t even give me the courtesy of telling me that you’ve decided to screw me over!” Wait a minute?.Max never called me at all. So either he’s become delusional or there’s something going on here that I don’t understand. The question, is how Max expects me to figure out just what he’s trying to pull, and what he expects me to do once I figure it out. “Max,” I say slowly to buy time, “you’ve gone insane. Look, you can’t beat them their too big for you. That’s why I had to leave like I did, I don’t like fighting for lost causes.” “So what am I supposed to DO!” he roars, “LAY DOWN AND DIE?” “No, no, of course not,” I say soothingly trying my best not to antagonize him. “Look maybe’maybe if you took off you know” Left the country and went somewhere that they didn’t have so much power. I doubt they’d chase you all the way to Europe.” Even I have to admit how weak that sounds, but I am willing to try anything at this point. “Yes they would,” he says bitterly but I can see a gleam in his eye, “still I guess its my best option.” Then without warning he strikes me across the face with his gun. I fall back my ears ringing from the force of the blow. I open my eyes back up and see the barrel of the gun aimed straight at my head. Then, right when I am about to say my final prayers?.Max winks at me! “I guess I’ll let you live for old times sake,” he says with that same menacing tone, “but you better now get in my way again. Now if you don’t mind’I got a plane to catch!” With that he runs off as I stare at his retreating form incredulously. I just sit for a good ten minutes trying to comprehend what has just happened, before finally standing up and shaking my head in amazement. Unless I am misreading this entire scenario Max had just used me to make the mob think that he was running away to Europe. At that thought I force myself not to look every which way for the man that was apparently spying on us from some unseen location. Given my luck so far, Max’s scheme is not going to work and I am going to be blamed for helping him. The smart thing to do would be to somehow get a message to the mob, somehow tell them what Max is planning. But I don’t think I will. Because, deep down I kind of hope that Max does succeed in whatever crazy scheme he is planning. I doubt it will, and if he actually asks me to do something to help it along I’ll shut the door right in his face. But I can extend this final helping hand to him, and allow him to work unwatched and unmolested for a little while. Besides, there is always a chance that he might succeed’or more likely that when the entire thing flops my part in it will be overlooked. But despite my sudden optimism I am well aware that I may not get away with this one. Its very possible that in a day, a week, or a month someone is going to barge into my hotel room gun in hand. Someone a whole lot more ruthless than Max ever could be. Suddenly I don’t feel like lifting any more.
