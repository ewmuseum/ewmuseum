Supporting the Family (or why I bothered coming back to waste my life here)

25 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th of June SUPPORTING THE FAMILY I “Mister Paddy?” It was this that woke him from his slumber. Eyes, initially fluttering from the brightness of the day, were soon met with a hand shielding his vision from the rays penetrating the window. He groaned before pushing himself to his feet, a freshly brewed hangover clouding his senses like a mist. “Mister Paddy?” the Mexican in his caravan repeated, not at all disheartened by his unanswered questions. Paddy staggered past him, making his way to the door. Upon opening it, he rejected the impulse to revel in the beauty of the morning and the majesty of the bird’s song, instead he brought forth a semi-digested cheeseburger he’d found and devoured from a trashcan the night before. The taste of stomach acid had become a familiar sensation in his mouth and one which he had accepted with mild displeasure. “Mister Paddy?” “Jus gimme a minute here Enrique!” Paddy hurled once more. Paddy didn’t have many friends. He didn’t really need them he supposed - he was always travelling from place to place in his motorised home. Anyway, if he ever needed a friendly voice, all he had to do was call home. Nevertheless, when he had awoken one morning to find a pleasantly smelling Mexican huddled up beside him, he had decided to keep him about. He would find out later that day he had in fact employed the green cardless Enrique. Things had started off well but soon Paddy realised his lack of money presented a problem. Of course by this stage he realised he couldn’t go back to life without Enrique. “Mister Paddy, will joo be requiring the breakfast this-a-morning?” Enrique continued as Paddy straightened up, a trail of vomit streaked down his bare chest. “Enrique special please.” Enrique nodded in his usual deadpan fashion and turned quickly to the kitchen (which was part of the sitting area and bedroom), his apron swishing to the side ever so elegantly. “Oh yes, Mr Paddy” Will I be expecting the wage today? I is needing to send money back to my family.” Paddy cursed under his breath and pretended not to have heard Enrique, instead scrambling quickly to hide behind a week old newspaper. Apparently some gypsy curse had turned a mother’s child into a monkey. Paddy wondered if he could turn children into monkey’s. “Mr Paddy” The wage?” “Yes well, Enrique.. The thing is, aye’m a lil strapped fer cash this week’soooo aye’ll sort ye out next week.” Enrique continued to stare at Paddy, his face completely devoid of emotion. “Mr Paddy” my family is needing to be fed back in Meh-e-co. If joo cannot pay me, I must go somewhere else for work.” There was a pause. For a minute Paddy contemplated life without Enrique - no breakfast’s in bed, no diligent cleaning, no chocolate spread foot massages? No damnit’he couldn’t let him leave. “Please Enrique’wha” if aye paid ye double next week.” Enrique shook his head. “Maybe you should get a job essa” Pfft. Subliminal messaging! That’s how he would keep him here! “Okay’tomorrow ye get paid. Aye got this bet on a horse called stay-Enrique-or-God-will-smote-you. It can’t lose! Ye’ll get paid then!” Enrique continued to stare, emotionless as ever. “Bollocks. Fine, aye’ll go look fer a bloody job. Ye better be a good wifey and have the dinner ready fer when I get home then!” II “Now Paddy, before I let you begin, here’s a few quick pointers.” The voice that whispered into his ear was soft, the hand on his shoulder was mild. Paddy noticed this but not much more, gazing idiotically at the telephone that sat before him on the desk at which he was seated. So far he had been employing a technique of ignorance to try and keep Mr Green out of his brain. He was a warm, pleasant person, an all round kind person, but by Jesus did all that niceness grate on him like a sander on one’s genitals. So he had pushed those bad thoughts away. He needed this job and wouldn’t intentionally balls it up. He had a family to support after all. “First of all, you need to know that the Samaritans is a great asset to the community. When someone is feeling depressed, we are expected to be just a phone call away for a source of relief. I’m proud to say, last month, we talked twenty six people out of attempting suicide and with you on our side, we hope to do more.” The man smiled warmly at Paddy and patted him gently him on the back. Paddy gazed around. “So all aye have t” do is stop people toppin? themselves?” Green blinked a little before smiling that Cheshire cat smile once again. “Welllll’that’s an interesting way of putting it, but we prefer to call it giving people a little mental helping hand.” “Same difference?” Ring ring! “Oh Paddy! Your first call. Let’s do this! Go Team!” Ring ring! Paddy glanced once again at that stupidly affixed banana grin, watching that beaming face nod frantically up and down. Apparently he had no intention of leaving just yet. Ring fucking ring Paddy! You going to answer me or what you stupid Mick?! “Hold yer horses fecker!” Paddy swooped the receiver up irritably. “Hello, Samaritans helpline, Paddy the Destroyer o” Worlds speakin? .” Click? “Yeah, aye’m Irish, what’s it t” ya?” Click? “Aye” an? wha? part would yer Father hail from?” Click? “Bahaha, they still sharing the one brain cell down tha” way?” Click? “Hey, feck ye too!” Click? “Look, have ye a problem or not?” Click? “Okay, let’s hear it” Click? “Uh huh?” Click? “Uh huh?” Click? “Chief, ye sure talk a lot about yerself.” Click? “Fine, tell me the rest.” Click? Click? Click? Click? “Yawn.” Click? Click? Click? Click? “Really” With yer mother? Jesus chief’that’s fecked up? Click? “Aye may be here t” help but’that be just wrong.” Click? “Ye did what with a cat”!” Click? Click? “Wow’aye’m scarred’aye really am?” Click? “Advice” Well, aye think ye should stick with plan A.” Click? “Definitely. Ye are beyond a hope me friend.” Click? “Aye hear a whole load of painkillers can topple ye” aye’d recommend tha?” Click? “Well okay then, bye bye now. Best of luck with tha” and remember, don’t be a quitter!” Job done. When he turned around, he was looking at the visibly shaken Mr Green, his mouth bent into an almost perfect “o”. O for outstanding job Paddy, have a promotion. “Not bad eh” Weedin? out the garden, eh?” “G-g-g-get out!” “Pardon?” “G-g-g-get out’n-n-now!” “What” But aye thought-? “Now!” With a look of exasperation, Paddy got up and walked towards the door still shaking his , wondering what he’d done that was so bad. It must have been because he was Irish. “Well feck ye too, Yankee Doodle!” 21st century and some people still don’t have an ounce of sensitivity. III “Bless me Father, for I have sinned’it has been two days since my last confession” “Proceed me son.” The dog collar strangled Paddy somewhat but he had to admit, the black clothing was very slimming. Attired as that of a priest, he had decided he liked this job already. Listening all day to people making bastards of themselves, it made him feel so much better about himself. “I have sinned Father”, the male voice whispered through the vent. “Yes, we’ve established tha”. Now, dish the dirt.” “Well Father” yesterday, I had jealous thoughts about my neighbour. He had a new veranda build in his garden and I ? grew envious.” Paddy frowned and leaned forward towards the vent. “Bah, that’s crap. Anythin” good? Adultery, murder? Sodomised any children have ye?” The man shuffled a little uncomfortably. “Well no Father” I did walk past a homeless person who asked me for change and I’I’I did have change. Please forgive me Father!” “Bollocks t” that, no forgiveness fer you. Go do somethin? interestin? and come back? then we can talk about this forgiveness malarky.” “Father?” Paddy grew exasperated now. “Go do something sinful! Somethin” tha? aye’d give a shit about. Go feck a hooker. Or burn a church. Aye don’t care wha? ye do, just make it interes-" “WHAT IN THE WORLD DO YOU THINK YOUR DOING”!” The door had swung open, the voice that rattled his brain around his head had come from the figure that stood framed in the opening; a figure that wore priestly attire. “Well. Aye?” “Who the hell let you in here and what do you think you’re doing wearing MY BLOODY CLOTHES!” Paddy scuffled in his seat nervously, thought about standing up then changed his mind and sat back in his seat. “Hm. Aye was y’know’doing God’s work?” “GET OUT!” “But aye thought-? “NOW!!!” With a look of embarrassment, Paddy got up and sidestepped past the priest, grinning nervously. The air of sheer antagonism of the latter did not relent. “And I want my clothes back!” Paddy nodded in a childlike fashion and scuttled off. What was his problem anyway? He was only trying to give him a few hours off’it must have been because he was Irish. IV He hadn’t wanted to be there. Coming out of retirement was one thing’but coming back with his cap in his hand was another. He had hesitated before pushing through the door of the front entrance, then sucked in a huge gulp of the cities finest smog and entered. Scuttling quickly over to the reception desk, he assumed his “Employ Me” face - huge beaming smile, a confident elegance in his swagger. An expressionless female face met him. The face was middle aged and unsympathetic, disinterested at best. “Oi oi (looks at name badge) Dolores! How the feck are ye this fine mornin?”” “It’s afternoon” Paddy’s smile went unreciprocated. “Oh silly me. Well how are ye anyway?” “Significantly more dejected since you entered the building.” What a nice lady. “Erm. Okay. Anyway, aye’m here t” see Stevey C? “Who?” “Stevey C” Big Caldera? The guy who pays yer wage?” The woman, clearly not impressed continued to stare blankly at the increasingly awkward O’Shea. “He’s not seeing homeless people today.” “Oi! Aye’m not feckin” homeless! Aye used to work here. Aye want me job back.” “Really” I don’t remember you. You must have been pretty insignificant.” “Look just let him know aye need a meeting now.” The woman merely shook her head and her eyes delved back to the document which she was originally processing. Paddy grew frustrated. “Now listen here, bitch. Aye’m not leavin” here until aye see-? SLAP! Paddy only seen the closed fist right before it connected with his temple. He spiralled elegantly in two rotations of a pirouette before collapsing to the floor with a thud. And the judge’s award 10, 10, 10! “You’ll watch who your talking to. Security!” What a right hook was the thought that sloshed around his brain as he vaguely noticed four muscle bound freaks entering his peripheral vision, now scooping him to his feet. “Okay buddy, time to go. You shouldn’t hav- Wait! Paddy! Paddy O’Shea” Paddy merely gurgled his rolling to the back of his mouth. He slumped into the security guard’s arms. “Dolores, what the hell” You know Mr Caldera would want to see him!” Dolores merely shrugged, disinterested by the affair now, she returned completely to her work. “Stevey C, can aye have me job back please?” Paddy babbled, feeling the saliva slosh around his mouth. The security guard smiled pleasantly. “That’s not up to me Paddy but you’ll know very soon.” Paddy attempted to dance as he was carried off by the security guards, then felt himself go under. V “Good news Enrique! Aye’m back at GCW, just need t” finalise me contract at the next WorldWide and- Enrique? Where the feck are ye?” Standing at the entrance to his caravan, his eyes looked about for his Mexican compadre. “Enrique?” “Just a second Mr Paddy!” The voice came from the bedroom. A few moments later, Enrique emerged at the doorway, attired in a French maid’s uniform, a plate of what looked like Irish stew in one hand. “Erm, Enrique’wha” the feck?” “Mr Paddy, I am being the good wifey, just like joo asked of me. Would joo like the dinner before or after the consummation of the marriage?” Paddy frowned’then shrugged. “Why no” at the same time?” And with that he skipped into the bedroom with his moustached friend. “Jus so ye know, aye only pitch. Aye’m not gay or anything”.” END
