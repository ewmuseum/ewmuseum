Getting Ready for Shockwave

17 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/17/11 *The camera zooms in on a Belt...er..a Belt buckle..It looks to be modeled after a World Title Belt from somewhere known as FPW:SH...whatever that is. The camera zooms out and we see one over confidesnt Christopher Carter Cash standing in his all black suit, shades, and cowboy hat, showing off his "bling." His Legal Representive, Tiffany N. Ashcroft is there albeit she doesn't seem to happy.* Tiffany N. Ashcroft: "I wish you'd take that damn thing off." *The Man in Black looks over at her puzzled and confused at first, then smiles as if he realizes what she is talking about* Christopher Carter Cash: "You just want to see me drop my pants..." Tiffany N. Ashcroft: "Excuse me?!" Christopher Carter Cash: "You want me to take off my belt buckle so my pants will fall down and you can yet once again catch a glimpse of my family jewels..." Tiffany N. Ashcroft: "When have I ever even came close to seeing your family jewels?" Christopher Carter Cash: "But you don't deny you want to...." Tiffany N. Ashcroft: "Look, I don't now, nor have I ever wanted you to drop your pants...." Christopher Carter Cash: "I knew it! You're a lesbian!" Tiffany N. Ashcroft: "How on earth do you get to calling me a lesbian just cause I want you to take off that ridicoulous looking Belt Buckle off? God! you've been wearing if for months and months...and this isn't even Flaming Pig Wrestling: Slaughterhouse. It doesn't mean anything here in GCW!" Christopher Carter Cash: "Oh, it means something to someone out there...and I am intentionally sending him a message right now...one of I am better than you and your mentor..." Tiffany N. Ashcroft "You know what? Fine! Whatever...Look I got you in the Rampage Royal at Shockwave..." Christopher Carter Cash: "And I don't want you to think I'm not appreciative, because I am...really. What a better way for me to prove myself and test the waters here in Global Championship Wrestling, than being put in a match with almost the entire roster. A match where the odds are against me, but I've never let that stop me before..." Tiffany N. Ashcroft: "I sense a but or something coming...." Christopher Carter Cash: "First ya wanna see my big package, now you're talking about my butt? Really, Tiff, whats gotten into you lately....Its very unprofessional...." The Blonde looks like she is about ready to lunge at the Redneck Rocker, but she regains her composure as he reconizes he is on very thin ice and continues.* Christopher Carter Cash: "But I did have a list of demands...things I wanted in order to join GCW...signing bonuses like a $1,000,000 per appearance, all religious holidays off, and so on. You inform me that I'm in and I don't even get press release or announcement, like that Japanese guy...Ryujinuke Kamigwuawau...you kinda dropped the ball here...I'm just glad you managed to get me this sweet suite for the PPV so I don't have to go to that stinky, smelly locker room with the lower class wrestlers. But thats fine because I will earn my repect, like I've been doing all my career. I am smarter, faster, more skilled and awesomer than everyone on the roster combined. No wonder I'm in a match against everyone...because it will take the combined mentalness, might and mojo of everyone in this federation to defeat me. Because there is nothing better than Cash!" *Truple C shines his Belt Buckle into the Camera, playes with it some right before he starts to get ready for the big event.*
