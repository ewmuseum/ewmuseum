IG46 Roleplay - Loose Ends

23 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 23rd, 2008 Scott Drake sat alone in the dark, staring out of the near window and watched a single light in the distance. It was a street light from down the road, but just for tonight it seemed to serve a greater importance. For Scott it was a beacon. It was far too later but the famed and troubled wrestler could not sleep. Seconds ticked off the clock and he just stared. Inside his cursed, he cried, he wanted to jump from his seat and break anything within arm’s reach. He just stared out in the distance at his beacon. Even if it did not seem so, family had always been very important to Scott. The reason many people did not think of him as a family man was because they never understood his concept of family. It wasn’t just blood that made two people family. It was the miles they counted together. It was about the trip that bounded two people. For Scott, Markus Stone was his family. It didn’t matter that they didn’t share a drop of blood. That was his brother, through thick or thin. Tessa was his family. Not by marriage, that had no happened yet. She was his family in a deeper sense of the word. Sitting in the dark, alone, he wondered where his family had gone. It seemed like it had just been yesterday when he was able to laugh and smile with Tessa and Markus, the three of them enjoying their family time together. They could be themselves when they were around each other. They had a bond he thought would never break. Turned out it wasn’t so strong after all. In one faithful night he was able to see it for what it really was, a thin wire easily snipped with the right pair of scissors. It broke his heart to watch his family fall apart. Scott badly wanted to make things right. He wanted to call Tessa, to convince her to come home and wrap herself in his arms. She had a beacon to guide her to him, she just needed to follow it. They could pick up Markus and just hang out with the children. He wanted to believe it would be that easy. The problem was that he knew it never could be that simple. Life did not work that way. There was too many factors involved. One of those factors was LeStatt Knight. Scott grinded his teeth, mashing them together as he cursed the very name of that man. His fist came down, hitting the table. He knew better than to let Knight get under his skin like that, it was in large the reason why so much shit had came his way. He allowed Knight to get to him and it made him lose focus. That made him lose so much more. Still, even though the thread was now short, he still clung to what he could. He may not have his GCW Tag Team title anymore, he still had his PTC Infinite Gauntlet. It didn’t seem like much, and by all accounts it should not have meant for anything. Yet Scott clung to it as if it was the only life raft on an endless ocean. It was, after all, the only thing he still had from before that night. Even that, though, was being threatened. Not just from the likes of his opponents come up; Charlotte Ramone and Jericoholic Anonymous, but from a rather unexpected source. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Bursting into his locker room with renewed vigor, Scott Drake looked around with a board smile for the woman he loved. His Virginia, Tessa Windsor, was nowhere to be found though. She had left him to die, not knowing he had crawled from hell and could still breath. What passion he had took a blow as the realization settled in. His victory was for naught. He had his championship, his legacy, but he had no one to share it with. Tossing the Infinite Gauntlet trophy aside, Scott turned to leave and look for his one true. He couldn’t even turn to leave before someone shoved him from behind and Scott nearly took a tumble onto the ground. Spinning on his heels, he never had a chance. Once his front side was exposed he had a fist planted into his stomach that dropped him to his knees in pain. Looking up, Scott Drake could see three men standing in front of him. They all wear beautifully crafted suits of fine Italian origins and seem to share the same choice of hair style and gel as well. "What is this, Angelo Deville look-alike day at the arena?" Scott tried to crack them a smirk but a fist came down on his face and stopped any chance of that. From behind the three men came a sharp laugh. The goons parted like the red sea, making room for the boss of the operation. His white suit clashed against the black of the others and his red hair burned as fiery as his laugh. "Still trying to crack a joke, Mr. Drake?" Lifting his head back to look, Scott started to get back to his feet only to have a foot come onto his shoulder and keep him down. "It would be kick ass if I knew your name as well, seeing as you seem to know mine." He stopped to think on that for a second. "Then again I’m a world famous professional wrestler and you are a nobody with a bad haircut." All four paused, looking at the crop of black and blonde resting on Scott’s skull. The center man’s laugh resonated off the walls, cueing up the others to know it was alright to laugh. "I admire your spunk, and would love to jaw with you for a while, but I’m afraid I’m here on business." He wiped his hair back and applied more pressure to Scott’s shoulder with his foot. Drake winced in pain, but tried to hide it behind a fierce snarl. "I’m afraid I don’t endorse sneaks, but maybe if you want to make a sweet action figure of me we could talk." He wanted it to sound like a light-hearted jab, one to show he still had his cool, but his words seeped through his clinched teeth like venom from a rattler. Rather than shoot back another retort, the man with the fire hair reached into his breast pocket and pulled out a small note pad. He flipped it open, scanning through a few pages, then came to a stop on what he was looking for. He glanced over the note pad to Scott, "a quarter of a mil, in cash, and you have a week. You’ll be contacted later about the when and where." The statement took Scott by surprise, so much so that he didn’t even bother to try and get the boot off his shoulder. He just blankly stared at the floor, trying to figure out what the man’s deal was. Seeing this confusion, the man just smiled more broad, getting a good laugh. "Didn’t think we would just let it slide, huh? Not that kind of money. Just because it’s been four years doesn’t mean anything but interest." The puzzle pieces started to fall one by one into place. Scott Drake’s eyes lit up and his head turned upward toward the man. "That debt’s been settled, I made sure of it myself. So you’re barking up a dead tree." "Money like that isn’t settled so easily. You have a week." Then, before Scott could mount a protest, the man’s boot left his shoulder and found its mark on his face. Drake’s body buckled back, the force shoving him flat against the concrete floor. The lights in the room went dark as the four men left him there. Scott wouldn’t wake until the night janitor poked him with the handle of his broom, jarring him back from his forced sleep. "Tessa?" he mutters, a smile trying to make its way to his face. His eyes settled, coming into focus, and his smile faded. He is quick to get back to his feet, embarrassed by the state the janitor had found him in. The janitor, to his credit, had a wide toothy grin on his face. "Looks like someun? had a party." Scott Drake looked around his locker room, noting that it had been trashed. The contents of his duffle bag were ripped and torn, torn all about. The janitor casually picks the torn bits up and tosses it into a waste cart he drags behind him, "musta been hell of a celebration." He shot Scott another wide and toothy grin. In a panic Scott rushed from the locker room, not bothering to check and see if there was anything salvageable. As if on autopilot, he navigated through the arena halls as he made his way toward the exit where he hoped his car still remained. Bursting through the double doors leading to the garage, a wiping wind of warm summer air hit his body, sending a feeling of nakedness throughout his soul. He suddenly felt very lonely as he looked out over the empty parking structure. Thankfully his car was not flipped over and set ablaze. Climbing into the Coupe Deville, Scott headed to the first place that came to his mind, the hotel. He wanted to see if Tessa was there, partially for her safety and mostly because he had been dying to see her. His disappointed reached a new peak when he discovered the hotel room empty, including all of her stuff being gone. Panic mood set in, and Scott started playing all the different scenarios out in his head of the men doing something to her. He was so wrapped up in the illusion that it took five minutes before he came to his senses that he should call her. Each ring of the phone made him worry exponentially more than the last, and by the time Tessa finally did pick up, he could hardly breathe. "Hello?" she asked with a strong twinge of annoyance in her voice. "Are you alright?" Scott asked, his voice trembling with the worry. "Why wouldn’t I be?" He finally let out a sigh of relief, "I just wanted to make sure you were safe. I saw all your stuff gone when I got back to the hotel room and I just started to worry." "I’m surprised you would worry, seeing as you didn’t care enough to even come back to the hotel after your match. But I guess that was more important than your family." Her words were bitter, enough that it left even a foul taste in Scott’s mouth. What made it worst was that he knew he couldn’t explain the situation to her without her worrying about him. With nothing else he could say for himself, he told her the only thing he could, "I’m sorry." He made sure to ask one more time though, for conformation, "but you are safe, right?" "Yes!" she said with a sigh, "if you must know, I’ve already left Phoenix when you didn’t show up after your match. I’m at the airport on my way to Milwaukee with LeStatt." The last word, that name, grinded against Scott’s skin so badly he wanted to slam the phone shut on her and throw it out the window. He did his best to let it go though, he wanted to keep hearing her sweet voice. "I should be heading that way soon" he told her, "just as soon as I can find a flight." There was a pause, presumably for her to look at her watch, "at this time of night? Scott, it is too late." "Well I can still try" he told her after a hesitation. "Well fine, then I’ll let you go so you can look." "NO!" He tried to stop himself but the word slipped from his lips. "I mean, nah, you don’t have to go. I’ll just look in the morning." Tessa’s voice suddenly went softer, sounding a bit more concerned. "Is there something wrong, Scott?" "Nothing, I, huh" he tried to think of anything he could say to her, "just still feeling spent from the match." Another sigh came across from Tessa’s side of the line. She didn’t want to hear about the match and he didn’t seem to want to talk. "Look, I’m going to let you go, I have to board the plane. I’ll talk to you when you get in Milwaukee." He tried to stop her but it was too late, the line went dead after she hung up the phone on him. Scott tossed it onto the desk in the hotel room, not bothering to watch it slide across the desk and fall to the ground. He turned and allowed his body to drop back into the plush bed. It felt far too large for him now that Tessa wasn’t there. "Nothing to do but sleep, I guess" he said to himself, and he did just that. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- So’you want to be a Rock Superstar Scott’s eyes fluttered open as he was wakened from his sleep by the sounds of his ring tone. Once he realized it was his phone, Scott leapt out of the bed and dove to the desk, only to see it not there. The song continued to play, and he quickly found it, flipping it open. "Tessa?" The caller on the other end was NOT Tessa. "No, you frosted-Jew fro retard! Listen you homosexual sociopath, if you’re trying to pull any of grade A bullshit in our match, you’ll be getting one serious non-title ass stomping." Scott looked at his phone, trying to place the voice. It sounded so familiar, sounded so’rude. "JA?" "Repeat it three times and an elbow appears in your face." It was Jericoholic Anonymous alright. "What the hell are you talking about man?" "I’m telling you, you festering pile of rancid meat, if you try any crap in the match, you’ll be having hell to pay. Got me or do I need to repeat myself slower?" For the second time in a row, Scott found himself on the receiving end of a hang up before he could utter a response. Looking at the phone as if it had just started speaking in tongues, Scott flipped it shut and slid it into his back pocket. "What the hell is that man’s deal?" Turning to the one place he felt he could always find out what is going on, Scott turned on his laptop. The Sony Vaio hummed to life, greeting him with a beautiful sight of Alex Box stadium at dawn. Clicking open Firefox, Scott typed in the best source he knew for the latest in wrestling news: www.primetimecentral.com. Front and center is a story about Chris Storm possibly taking a dive at his first Infinite Gauntlet defense. Clinking the link, Scott’s eyes scan over the page. Reigning Infinite Gauntlet champion, Chris Storm, may not be holding the title for much longer. The latest on his first title defense is that the former Global champion may be taking a dive to pocket some spare change. One insider backstage at the arena where Chris won his crown from former champion Hunter Sabuani cited seeing four men with known underhanded dealings in wrestling leaving his locker room after the match. It should be noted that this is not the first time Storm has been involving in such situations. Back in 2004 there was a story circling around that Storm had refused to take a dive and gotten into trouble over that. This was around the title of his Global title victory. Chris Storm lost that title on his first defense, though there did not seem to be any talk of him throwing that match. At the time there is no connection between this event and that in 2004, though we will keep you posted on any updates we receive. Slapping the laptop closed, Scott sunk back in his chair. It figured that JA would be so angry now, even though the story wasn’t even remotely close to what actually happened. It did create quite the situation for Scott though. Primetimecentral was a widely popular site for wrestling news, visited by at least a million people a day. There was no telling how many people had already read that story and took it as truth. He needed to clear the air, though for the life of him he couldn’t figure out how to do it without exposing what actually happened. Nothing seemed to come to mind, so he did the first think he could think of, he made a post on a forum! That User: Chris Storm would never take a dive! The guy is way too awesome for that. Hell, he’s so awesome he could take a dive and still beat them both! This guy doesn’t know what in the hell he’s talking about if he thinks Storm is going to give up his Infinite Gauntlet. They might as well just call that shit the "Trying to beat Chris Storm but fail like a little bitch" Gauntlet, cause that is what it is, just a bunch of little bitches losing to Storm. Though that Charlotte Ramone is one hot little bitch. I bet if Chris wasn’t all with Tessa that he would so mack that. But Tessa is way WAY hotter anyways and I couldn’t see Tessa with any other awesome dude but Storm. So yeah, Chris isn’t taking a dive and rocks the world. Feeling much better, Scott picked up his phone and scrolled through the list of numbers. Finding the one he was looking for he hit the small, green call button. "Hey, is this Charlotte?" "Uh yeah, hey Scott" she replied back. "You didn’t happen to hear anything wild stories about our match did you?" he asked, going straight to the point. "You mean like you taking a dive?" He cringed, realizing the story must have spread faster than he thought. "Look, I can explain." "Nah, it’s cool" she told him, "I mean I don’t really want to be getting into that stuff right now. I’m kind of?" there was a pause on her end, "busy at the moment, you know?" Being the wise and subtle man he was, Scott answered back, "I didn’t interrupt you and Krissy having sex did I?" "No, no, no!" Charlotte stammered, "Krissy’s got some company over, and I’m just not feeling good." Scott felt better that she seemed fine with it, though he was kind of concerned about her last remark. "Well, I just wanted to call and let you know personally not to believe anything you read online, unless it is by that user fella. He’s pretty damn smart." "Look, like I said, it’s cool. I trust you not to be a bag of douches." "And regardless what happens" he added, "I’m still planning on making the next Temptation like you invited." The Punk Rock Pixie picked up a bit, thinking of what is coming up with Temptations, "yeah should be awesome. Hopefully everything goes cool." Again Scott tried to drive home his point, feeling very self conscious about it, "should be a blast. And like I said, you don’t have to worry about me taking a dive. I’ll bring it when the bell sounds." Ramone didn’t response, making Scott think she might have already hung up on him, but after her few seconds her voice came across from the other side, "Scott, it’s cool and all, but I gotta go. I have a lot of stuff here I have to deal with. We’ll talk though at the show." "Yeah" he managed to get in before he was hung up on. He let out a sigh, wondering if that was going to become some running theme. At least he got in a word before she hung up, he figured he was doing much better then. While on the phone with Charlotte, Scott Drake had walked out of his hotel room and down toward the lobby to check out. After paying his bill, he headed toward the car, still nervous he would find it wrecked. His phone was in his hand, the situation weighing heavy in his mind. Looking down at the small LCD screen, Scott scrolled one more time for a number. To his surprise he had not deleted it yet and again pressed the small green call button. He never heard the phone ring, as it went straight to a message letting him know the number had been disconnected. His shoulders slumped down and he felt a bit down on himself for even bothering to try that. But there was something, maybe a feeling in the air, that made him get in the car and decide to go for a drive. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- It took him longer than he realized it would, and Scott found himself out too late to catch a flight to Milwaukee. Tessa, no doubt, would be deeply upset with him, but he had to push that aside. This, for him, was far more important than her being slightly more than usual anger with him. Pulling up to a large mansion behind an equally large wrought iron gate, Scott Drake looked out at the last known home of the Pretty Hate Machine. A sign, planted by the road where there was a patch of ground, told that the house was up for sale. Looking over the sign, Scott sees a number listed to call. He did so, hoping whoever was on the other end wouldn’t hang up on him. It was a realtor, and by the sounds of it, one very interested in selling the home. Scott wasn’t interested in the purchase though, he wanted to know about the previous owner, more importantly a new address. Sadly the realtor is of no use to him, coming up empty of both grounds. She could only tell him information about the house itself, not who had lived in it. Thinking he saw something in the house, Scott turned karma and hung up on the realtor before she could even get knee deep into her sales pitch. His eyes darted from window to window, swearing he saw something. Feeling this could be his one chance, Scott returned his phone to his pocket and started to jump the fence. Reaching the top he cut himself on one of the sharp accents, though it was nothing more than an annoyance to him. It was more important he try and find what it was he saw in that house than to worry over some small cut. It turned out he luck continued to run dry. The person inside the house wasn’t who he was looking for, but rather an elderly looking butler, or at least someone who dressed the part. That didn’t stop Scott from opening up with a barrage of questions on the old man. "Hey, excuse me, do you know who owned this house?" The man stopped what he was doing and turned to face Drake. "I do" he plainly told Scott. "It is owned by Ms. Chrystal Fellini, and I am currently tending to the home while she is away." Scott’s heart started to pound in his chest with excitement. "You mean you know where she is?" The butler chuckled heartily, "oh yes, indeed I do." He placed his broom off to the side as he reached for a pen and something to write on. Scott’s entire body nearly shook as he watched the old man write down a new address. "You can find her here." Scott snatched the piece of paper from his hand and looked over the address. A new wave of energy surged through his body and he suddenly felt better than he had in days. Thanking the man, Scott rushed out of the house and back to his car. Alone again, the butler picked up his broom and resumed his sweeping, cleaning up after Drake’s tracks. He smiled as he moved the broom back and forth, "she’s been waiting for you." At the new address, Scott has to double check it to make sure he had it right. He looked around, trying to figure out if the butler had just been yanking his chain or not. But he had came this far and he would make sure to check every lead he could get. Marching up to the number he was given, Scott knocked on the door to apartment 34B. "Who is it?" Scott froze. It WAS her! "I said, who is it?" "It’s uh?" Scott said, now at a least for words. He didn’t have to finish though, as an eye peered through the small hole in the door. "Oh’it’s you." She said as she opened the door for him. Scott Drake stepped inside the apartment, amazed to find the former DWO World Champion now living in a small apartment filled with all sorts of memorabilia. Obscure S.I.D.S. posters, promotional material, and other merchandise hung from every wall. Scott looked around, stunned. As he walked through the room his eyes scanned for anything charting her days as an insanely popular professional wrestler. To his shock, there wasn’t a single piece of it. The door slammed shut behind him, yanking him back to reality. That was when he noticed the cane. A knot as big as Kimbusa’s belly formed in the pit of Scott’s stomach. He knew why that cane was there, and it was all his fault. He tried to look away, to inside focus on the band merchandise. "What" her words were freezing cold, "do ya? want?" "Chryste!" he exclaimed, "you have to help me!" A loud and sharp laugh escaped from his lips. "And jus? why should I help ya??" "I was just hit up by four large men for our debt!" he told her, "the debt that was settled long ago. And here they are telling me I owe them a quarter of a million dollars!" Chryste crossed the room and Scott had to advert his eyes so as to not watch her use the cane. "So?" she said, "you seem to be doing pretty well, you can’t tell me with your career you don’t have that kind of money." He was shocked, but expected her response. "Not that much in that little of time, they want it in a week. I only have that kind of money in the children’s college fund, and there is no way Tessa would dare allow me to use that!" A much softer laugh left Chryste, "nah, Tessa wouldn’t." She looked at Scott even though his eyes wouldn’t meet her back, "but a lot has changed since 2004, Scott. A new guy is in charge, he came from the inside and ousts us. We were blindsided. He’s been goin? through the books and settling all the old debts, trying to raise as much cash as he can in a hurry." She paused along enough for him to be able to follow, "he’s been getting? the loose ends." Her cane found Scott square in his chest and suddenly he had no other choice but to acknowledge it. "Ya?" she said, looking him straight in the eyes, "are a loose end." "Please, you have to do something!" he once again begged. Chryste removed the cane from his chest and turned her back from him. "An? tell me jus? why I should help ya? when ya? the one that last time I saw ya?, ya? was tryin? to rip my knee out the socket an? beat me with it." "I’m sorry" he told her, his head hanging low in shame, "that was a bad time for me, both mentally and professionally." He tried to search for the words that would explain how he felt, knowing he had wronged her, "I burned a lot of good bridges back then, I know that. My head wasn’t on straight." He stopped to see if he could read any of her body language. Chryste was too good for that though, and kept everything she wanted a secret, so Scott continued, "but it is now." "BULL!" she fired back like a pistol going off in the night. "If ya’r head is on straight, then why is Tessa with that Knight?" A tinge of annoyance made its way through Scott, as he did not want to hear that name right now, nor did he want to think of that ordeal. "What is going on between me and Tessa is our business, got it?" Chryste turned her back to him again, not taking kindly to his attitude. "I can’t help ya" she told him. Scott literally dropped to his knees, begging her. "What is it that you want?" he asked, trying his very best to figure her out. Even after six years, he felt he didn’t have the slightest clue. Looking at him on his knees in front of her, Chryste can’t help her. "I hate to disappoint ya? but ya? aren’t half as good with ya? tongue as ya? think ya? are. So if that is how ya? plan to be tryin? to convince me to help, ya? better come up with a plan B." Scott pulled himself back to his feet, feeling ashamed that he gave her such an open shot, but Chryste shot back at him, "actually, ya? look better on ya? knees where ya? belong." Though he had imagined it might go something like this, Scott still felt extremely frustrated with the entire deal. "If you aren’t going to help me, just say so. I have better things to waste my time with" and he turned himself toward the door. He got three steps before Chryste called him off, "I’m jus? messin? with ya?" she told him. Scott turned and faced her. "I’ll help ya." His face lightened up, his steps had an extra bounce. "Really?" She nodded to agree. "Well, what should I do?" "Throw the match." "I'm not taking the fall." Scott said with power behind his words. There was a brief pause from Chryste. "What do you mean by that?" she asked. Scott added even more power behind his words, his chest now puffed out, "I refuse to willingly lose to JA or Ramone. I will do everything in my ability to defeat both of them and actually give PTC a real Gauntlet champion instead of some jokes they’ve had. I am a real wrestler, not some actor. I do not go out there to entertain the fans. I go to wrestle. That is why I must not and will not take the fall. I gave them my word." Chryste laughed, not impressed by his speech. "Oh Scott, I swear I hear it all before. But that doesn’t change the fact that if you want out of this, you need to throw the match." "And throw everything I’ve worked for, everything I’ve sacrificed for!" he was yelling now, not giving a damn who heard him, "I built my legacy and damn possibly drove my family away for it. And now you want me to throw that away too? To throw the last thing I have away?" "Yes." "FUCK THAT SHIT!" Chryste smirked as Scott threw his arms into the air, he was always so animated. "I’m not throwing shit!" He pointed an accusing finger at her, "and you know what? I’m going to go out there and I’m going to put me a fucking whooping on two people and not only not throw the match, but fucking win this shit like it meant to be." In such a fit of rage, Scott turned from her and marched out of her apartment, He swung the door open but stopped, looking over his shoulder, "so fuck you and the horse you rode in on, I’ve got my ass an Infinite Gauntlet to fucking retain!" Then he slammed the door behind him with all the force he could still muster. Alone and waiting for the ringing to stop, Chryste just continued to smile. "Oh," she said to herself, "I just love these little games we play." Reaching into her pocket, Chryste pulled out her cell phone, tossing the cane off to the side. "Hey, Angeal, yeah, everything is set. Still same ol? Chris Storm he always been." She flipped the phone shut with a smirk, "still the same ol? Chris Storm."
