An Underdog Story (I Dare You)

17 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 17, 2006 ---- Prelude: Adrenaline Hello, are you still chasing The memories in shadows Some stay young, some grow old Come alive, there are thoughts unclear You can never hide ---- I could feel the butterflies in my stomach as I walked down the hallway. The bare, white painted concrete walls are a far cry from the luxeries of Zenith Manor, they look much more like a prison. Yet these prison walls have become my home. Honestly, at this point in my life I wouldn't have it any other way. Approaching the door I wondered if anyone had even come here yet? I was a bit anxious to say the least. This is the opportunity I've been waiting for, for at least the past few months. It was my chance to carve my name into the history of this sport. It was my chance to step out of the shadow of that bitch who people claim is the best female in the history of Global Championship Wrestling. I suppose on the other side I wanted to see friends and familiar faces, but instead I was met with silence. The only company I was going to have was my black duffle bag and my United States Championship. After six months it still felt the same, like a drug related high. That insatiable need for more, that craving that drove alcoholics to the edge and drug addicts to overdose in their basements penniless. The ultimate high. Every night it's different, every night it's the very thing I crave to get me through. Every goodnight kiss I've missed from my fiance, every tear I've shed in pain, this is what makes it go away. I'm a junkie... my drug is adrenaline. The crowd, the lights, the sounds... the spectacle gets me through. The rush of soaring above the ring like a bird just doesn't go away. Ever since I was a little girl I've wanted to fly. I think every little kid dreams of flying, sometimes even impossible dreams like mine can come true. That wasn't all though, I wanted a lot of things as a little girl. The people who know me best know I'm not one to hold back or be thrifty. Yet every single week I go out there and put my body and my life on the line, because this is my life. My new life. People like Brandon Youngblood, they just don't fucking get it. If I didn't give a damn about being a wrestler why would I do this to myself? Why would I deal with not being able to move when I get out of bed every morning? Who in the fuck would put themselves through this hell if they didn't want it? I need this. There are many parts of my life, and some things still don't make much sense to me. They don't have to when I walk through these doors. When I walk into the arena, this is the only thing that matters in my life. This medallion of leather and gold is the manifestation of all my hopes and dreams, and everything I've ever done and everything I ever want to be... ---- The Things Little Girls Do Even in madness, I know you still believe Paint me your canvas so I become What you could never be ---- The joys of childhood, what some would do to have that feeling back. The almost endless amount of energy blessed upon you every morning when you awake. The sun beat down into the room of young Vivica J. Valentine. Her shoulder length blonde hair drooping down over her eyes as she sat up in bed and awoke with a huge yawn. Looking around the room she blew the hair out of her eyes before smiling. Afterall it was Saturday morning! No school, cartoons on all morning just for her, not having to eat crappy elementary school cafeteria food. Plus she got to spend all day with her mother, who was not the easiest person to get along with for most people. Yet for little Vivica her mother was always an idol. Such a beautiful woman, she wanted to look just like her when she grew up. Little did the hyper-active 7 year old girl realize at the time, that was almost exactly what was going to happen. Sprinting down the stairs at full speed, little miss Valentine finds the living room at the bottom of her descent to be empty. Where did everyone go? Her father must've been gone, as usual. He was never home... he was a busy big name attorney in the Chicago area. Jack Valentine, Attorney At Law. Sometimes she would see her father's face on television more times in a week than she would see him in person. What about mommy though? She was always home, taking care of her baby girl, grooming her to be a prim and proper princess. Perhaps she was out tending to the garden that surrounded the place Vivica called home, or gossiping with a neighbour. The lack of a maternity figure didn't much phase the young woman who would one day become The Black Mariah, as she continued her usual morning routine of gourging on Saturday morning television. Pulling on the knob on the right side of the television, the set sparks to life and Vivica runs back to the couch to watch whatever may come on the tube. Expecting cartoons Vivica is surprised to see a bearded man dressed in a black suit with dollar bill signs on it with a belt around his waist. Inside the belt were what looked like millions of diamonds. 'Coming down the aisle, The Million Dollar Champion... 'The Million Dollar Man' Ted DiBiase!' Vivica watched with intrigue as the man entered a roped stage. It became obvious to Vivica soon that she stumbled across a professional wrestling show. Although she never really watched the spectacle before, she often heard kids in her class talk about Hulk Hogan and The Ultimate Warrior. Soon through the show cut away to show a man in a blue mask and cape coming down towards the ring, he looked almost like a super hero and went by the name of The Blue Blazer. Soon little Vivica was fixated on the program that began to unfold on the television. She watched and cheered as The Blue Blazer flew through the air to try and defeat the evils of Million Dollar Ted. Halfway through the match, she'd forgotten completely about the cartoon she came down to watch. No wonder all the kids at school were talking about wrestling! It was like a comic book, come to life. The super hero was dazed and helpless as his nemesis The Million Dollar man approached him from behind. He rendered the Blue Blazer helpless in a submission move he called The Million Dollar Dream. Vivica screamed for the Blazer to break out, but before the outcome became clear the television suddenly turned stations. 'Hey! I was watching that!' Vivica proclaimed as the beginning to the show EUREKA'S CASTLE began playing through the room. Looking across the room, she looked up at her mother who had an almost disgusted look on her face. 'Let's watch Eureka's Castle like all the other little girls watch.' Her mother reassured the little girl, with her typical holier than thou tone that Vivica grew quite used to growing up. Sarah Julian-Valentine, neighbourhood gossip queen. Spreads trash about everyone else to make herself look better. A transplanted Los Angeles socialite met Jack Valentine while he was defending a case in the LA area. Now she wasn't much more than a trophy wife and housewife... and she didn't even do a very good job of that. Spending more time on the phone and primping herself than taking care of the house and her daughter. 'But mom, Jessica talks abou...' Vivica didn't get very far before she was interrupted. 'I don't care about Jessica, and she isn't my daughter. Or are you a little boy?' Her mother ended her statement with a very harsh and sarcastic tone. 'No mommy, I'm a little girl.' 'Good, then act like one.' Sarah said before she walked out of the room to start a new phone conversation. Irritated and a little guilty, Vivica turned her attention to the Nickelodeon program and watch the muppets Eureka and the dragon Madgellean talk to each other. Although her mother tried desperately to stop it, she was already taken in by the world of professional wrestling. Often times she'd watch it on the sly at a friends house, or have friends tell her what happened at school. At even a very young age, her mom was very concerned with looks... what Vivica looked like, how Vivica acted, what she watched, what she ate. Yet the day she ended up running away for good as a teenager, her mother had no idea what she had done wrong. Even to this day Sarah has no regrets about raising Vivica the way she did, and Vivica has no regrets about running away from home. ---- Epilogue: Showtime I dare you to tell me to walk through the fire Brand my soul and call me a liar I dare you to tell me to walk through the fire I dare you to tell me I dare you to ---- Along my way through life I've been told that I'm not allowed or not able to do a lot of things. I'm a career tag wrestler and will never be able to win a singles championship, when I join GCW I'll prove to the world I was just a big fish in a small pond, I'm just a poor person's Eva VanLorne, I'll never hold the United States Championship for more than a week. All of them... every last one of them was a lie. What can I say, I love being the underdog. Now they're saying that I'm going to lose my United States Championship to the first Woman ever to hold this title. Eva VanLorne might've been the first, but history shows I'm the best. Eva held this belt for 4 days, I held this belt for 4 months... and I hate to break it to all the little kids out there behind their keyboards, but this isn't over yet. This is about much more than being better than Eva now, this is about being the best United States Champion this place has ever seen. Now that I'm already the best woman to hold this belt... it seems Eva VanLorne is quickly becoming the poor person's Vivica J. Valentine. What a cruel sense of humor destiny has for the weak. As I stand in the locker room alone and look at everything around me... how many people have stood here before me, and how many people will stand here after I'm gone, I think about what the historians will have to say about this day. They day Dies Irae member Vivica J. Valentine nailed the final nail in the coffin of The Ascension before they ever got off the ground. The day I solidified myself as the greatest United States Champion of all time. Some say I have big dreams about small fish, but this is my fish. The only way anyone is taking this away from me, is from my cold rigor mortis stricken hands. My friends... no, my family is counting on me. It's showtime.
