Date: 12-09-06 Monica Trellis sat alone in her Father's office, thumbing through old pictures of him in the ring. With her feet propped up on the edge of the dark oak veneer, she pushed herself to the point where her chair was resting on the two back legs. A pair of black track pants, white cross trainers, and a white sports bra made a cute little ensemble, dressed for a day in the office I suppose. Looking at an old photo of her father leaping from the top rope, she could never forget all the time she spent backstage at the different events, all the people she met during the course of his career. It was a shame it had all ended so abruptly, but sometimes life just had a shitty sense of humor. ?Hey Mar-? Startled, she nearly fell out of her chair when she turned her head over her shoulder to find Jayce Bradley standing at the doorway; she had completely forgot she was leaning back on two legs of the steel framed seat. ?I'm sorry,? he apologized, his hand reaching out for the chair that did not topple over. ?I didn't mean to scare you.? Composing herself, Monica shook her head and scoffed at the notion. ?Please, I wasn't scared. I just lost my balance a bit.? ?You looked a bit scared,? he told her as she stood up, adjusting the chair before turning to face him. He was beautiful. No other word could describe the man she was looking at, he couldn't be called cute anymore than a statue of a Greek God could be called such a term. His blond hair was shaved down to about an quarter inch of stubble, framing his perfect features and crystalline blue eyes that were locked onto her with a bit of troubled innocence. ?Are you sure you're alright,? he asked, his gaze never wavering. ?Of course,? came the quick and snappy reply, coupled with a roll of the eyes and a cock of the hips. ?Why wouldn't I be?? ?Well, you looked a bit out of it for a second there.? Jayce moved away from the door, the sound of it clicking closed echoing out through the awkward silence, as they both just stand there staring at each other for a brief moment. Then Monica snapped out of her trance. ?I just wasn't expecting anyone to be here.? She began, placing the photo album atop her father's desk. ?Is there something I could do for you?? Her eyes focused on something else. It didn't matter what, as long as she wasn't losing herself in his eyes. ?I was looking for your dad,? He noticed she was avoiding him, though he had no clue as to why. ?He said to meet him here at seven.? ?You're a little early, aren't you?? He glanced down at his watch, a bit confused for a moment. ?No, it's ten past eight now.? She giggled a bit, turning back to find his eyes locked on her. ?You do realize you're not on the East Coast anymore, right?? He could have slapped himself, but he held back. It's not something you do in front of a girl as gorgeous as Monica Trellis. Even dressed down she was sexy as hell, especially the way her clothes just seemed to hug her body -- accentuating each and every curve. ?Sorry,? he said in a low tone, embarrassed for the most part. ?I always forget to change that.? ?It's okay,? She says with a laugh. ?I do that sometimes too.? A cute laugh to go along with a warm, inviting, smile. It made him feel a little warm inside. ?Well look,? he said, as he turned to look over his shoulder toward the door. ?I'm just going to wait for Mario outside... Let you get back to whatever you were doing before I scared you.? ?I wasn't sca-? she stopped short when she noticed the smile on his face. ?I know,? he chuckled. ?I was just teasing you.? She blushed slightly, her eyes sparkling as he walked out the door. She hoped he hadn't noticed. ~{ OO }~ On the other side of the door, Jayce just leaned back against it; his hand still on the knob. ?You're early.? His eyes flashed open, the grinning face of Mario Trellis slowly bounding in his direction. ?I didn't expect to see you for another half an hour at least.? A gym bag was slung over Mario's shoulder, a practically bare shoulder as the scrap of material left of his homemade wifebeater dangles onto his arm. Jayce lifts his arm and points to his watch, words not even necessary to get a nod from Mario; he understood completely. Mario had spent time out on the road as well, that mistake happens from time to time. ?What did you want to talk to me about,? Jayce asked as Mario tossed the duffel bag onto the ring apron. ?Helping you with your little problem is working out perfectly,? his face had a bit of a grimace on it, it pained him to finish his words. ?But your ring work looked horrible in that tag match last week. So I'm thinking we knock off some of that rust you seem to have holding you back.? ?Do you really think you can keep up with me,? Jayce said with a grin. ?Jordan White didn't even look like he wanted to win, and he did.? It was Mario who had a smile on his lips now. ?I think I'll be okay. Besides, Brandon Youngblood is going to be a lot tougher than White, so winning that US title isn't going to be much easier.? ?Yeah... well,? he said with a nervous smirk, his eyes shifting slightly to keep from looking at the man. ?I have to get that far first.? ?You will, Jayce,? Mario said with confidence, more than Jayce seems to have at the moment. ?You will.? Jayce slid under the ropes to get into the ring, while Mario took the long way up the stairs.
