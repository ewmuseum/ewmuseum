Dust to Dust

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: The Next Day ---Detroit--- Jason Ryan wasn’t quite so lucky to be asleep at this very moment. He was back at his warehouse watching the Olympics and eating a bowl of Cereal. The amazing thing about TiVo is the fact that he could watch Michael Phelps winning a Gold Medal in the Individual Medley days after it happened. He was too busy to watch it when it first aired, but that doesn’t stop him from watching the young man that went to school less than an hour from where he was currently eating his Rice Krispies do something great, something that no one currently from the Ohio State University was doing. Ryan goes to shovel another spoonful of the cereal into his mouth when he notices that the bowl was empty. He gets up and takes the bowl into the kitchen. He opens the door to the fridge and pulls out a bottle of Corona. As Jason sits back down in front of his TV, The Who’s “Baba O’Reilly” starts to play. “What the hell?” Jason puts the bottle back and walks over to where his put his cell phone on to charge. Before the phone stops ringing, Jason answers. “Hello?” ---Wyoming--- “Jason, I’m glad you’re there. I’ve been trying to get a hold of Wyatt or your brother. Neither one of them were answering. I don’t know where to turn to.” The makeup that Araceli had completely run down her cheeks. She didn’t know where she was, she just got as far from the ranch as she could. “Jake”.Jake kicked me out of his life. I don’t know what to do.” Jason on the other line offered her the only advice that he could. Get to Detroit, which is the only way that he could help her. ---Detroit--- The next day is much clearer. Araceli walks into Jason’s warehouse. She sets a bag down next to the wrestling ring that was in the middle of the floor. “You know, you take being a wrestler a little too seriously.” Jason walks down the stairs from the apartment. His long black hair was pulled back in a pony tail. “You have to be comfortable in your own home, that’s what my father always said.” “Funny, for a man that sent Dorian away.” “One day, he’ll understand that his being sent to live with Samantha was the best thing for him at the time. Our father wasn’t going to be able to take care of him.” “Well, at least you knew your”.” “Yeah, yeah, yeah. I know you have your own sob story, but if you don’t mind, my soaps are on.” “Soaps?” “ABC Daytime, better writing that what most people are going to be seeing on TV. By the way, Dorian and Wyatt should be here in a few hours.” “What do you think they will say when they find out you watch soap opera’s?” “Do you think I give a shit” It’s either that or video games, and last I checked, I was considered too old for video games.” “Well”.” “Don’t even say it.” “Say what.” “Listen, I get cracks about my age all the time in SCCW, I do not need to hear it from my younger brother’s associates.” Ryan then turns around and heads back up to the apartment. Araceli giggles and then says under her breath, “Much younger.” “I heard that.” Araceli walks around the warehouse, looking over the momentos of Jason’s career as Jay Draven. “How’quaint.” Araceli rubs her fingers over one of the display cases that holds one of the many replica championship belts in Jason’s collection. “National Wrestling Council North American Championship. Impressive’not.” “Araceli, you may not understand this, but there are things in the wrestling industry that are more important that money and belts.” “Like what?” “Namely, giving the fans a show. I’ve been doing this for years, and that’s the one thing that I will continue to do every time I come out to the ring. I give the fans a show that they will remember.” “What are you talking about” How can you say that you wrestle for money, but you don’t do it for the money?” “Simple. If SCCW asked me tomorrow to take a paycut, but they are going to give me even more bookings, I would take it in a heartbeat. I’m a wrestler and that’s what I do. You, Wyatt, Dorian. You are business people. You are in wrestling for the money. “Look around you. What do you see” You don’t see a business person. You see a person that has come to accept what he is. I have accepted that no matter what I do in this life, I am a wrestler. “That is why you lost Jake. He saw that he wasn’t a wrestler, nor was he a business person. Jake is a rancher. What he’s done in this business, he did for the sake of his ranch.” “But, I care for Jake.” “I didn’t say that you didn’t. Trust me; this business has a way of destroying relationships. See, I’ve gone through three marriages. After every divorce, I burn the marriage certificate to remind me that with this business, you have to appreciate it, or it’s going to burn you down. Ashes to ashes. Dust to dust.” “Why do you continue in this business if it causes you pain?” “Because, Araceli, it’s what I do. You don’t look around you and see a motor pool. You don’t look around you and see computers and files for clients. No, you look around you and you see momentos of my life. A life that at one time Wyatt and Dorian were interested in. A life that Jake was interested in. A life that you’ve corrupted. A life that you have destroyed for three people.” “How dare you say that?” “IKD ring a bell” You brought him into FUSE, and when he couldn’t provide you a meal ticket, you kicked him to the curb. Cowboys From Hell? You saw the potential to make money off those two boys and you took it. When Jake got hurt, you didn’t feel any guilt whatsoever.” “Jason, you have some gall.” “I do, do I” Tell me something, Araceli. Why is it that you don’t even use your real name in wrestling when Jake, Wyatt and Dorian have done so? You think I don’t know what’s going on, Constance.” “What did you call me?” “Constance Black. I know more about you than you want people to know. You see, you don’t have to fool me. I’ve been in this business long enough to know when someone is full of shit. No Mercy Enterprises, your idea” Doesn’t matter. It’s a bad idea that makes for a good gimmick. “One day, your entire world is going to come crashing down. The two people that you can trust to be there, they won’t be there for you, because you will have burned them out of your life. You can spare your crocodile teams over what happened to Jake, because I know that it won’t be long until they find out who you really are. “Unless you make a conscience effort to change.” “You have no idea what you’re talking about old man.” “Constance, if you don’t think I know what I’m talking about, go to the Compuware Building and head up to the sixth floor. Find the offices of Damage Inc and talk to Kelly Durant. She will tell you everything that you need to know so that you can see that yes, I do know what I’m talking about. But you should hurry.” “Why?” “Because if you aren’t back by the time Dorian and Wyatt get here to pick you up, I will let them know about your dirty secrets. I’m talking about more than your name. See, Kelly may have been you at one time, but now, she’s made that change. I leave the choice to you whether or not you want to make that change as well.”
