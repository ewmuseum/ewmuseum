Breathe In, Breathe Out

1 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/01/07 A seasoned traveler like Scott Drake has been to many places. This, however, was not one of them. “As come on Tessa, do we really have to be here?” His distinct voice whined loudly as he was dragged down a white hallway and through a door in an unknown building. Once inside, Scott became surrounded by a number of pregnant women as he trekked across blue rubber mats. Beside each woman was a clueless man, trying to keep up with the woman as she practiced such a simple act as breathing. Sadly the men in the room seemed to be having problems with that simple act. Scott related with them all. Tessa moved past him, heading to a free corner of the mat where she took her place. Giving Scott a sharp look, he knew it would be best if he joined her as quickly as possible. Passing by the people, he managed to overhear two females conversing about the father of their child. The one to the left, a blonde haired white woman, seemed to be alone but unfazed by it as she talked to the brunette beside her. “So, like I’m so excited about this baby. His daddy is going to spoil him, I just know it.” The brunette took a moment to look around the room, seeing no lone guy that could be the father of her child. “So is he here today?” Quick to roll her eyes, the blonde replied back “oh no, he’s far too busy with filming a movie.” “Oh my goodness! A movie” Who’s the father?” This obviously picked up the attention of the woman, and Scott as well as he passed the two by. Scott took a moment to slow his pace towards Tessa down so that he could hear more. The blonde looked around, trying to lower her voice but doing a poor job of it, “well, don’t say anything but the father is Brad Pitt!” Everything came to a halt. The right eyebrow shot up to a questioned look for the brunette, as she tried to process if this white woman was just messing with her or not. “Really?” “Yeah, totally, but I like thought he was white and junk. Weird.” The eyebrow didn’t seem like it could go any further, but on that statement it still found another inch to crawl. “So, the dad isn’t white?” “No, weird huh” You see his movies and he totally looks white. But the guy totally said he was Brad Pitt. No worries though, I’m not that stupid.” The brunette was almost ready to question that statement, but bit her tongue. “I asked for proof that it was really Brad Pitt. But he had his driver’s license. So that so proves it was him. I guess it must just be, like, movie magic or something.” “This is one dumb bitch!” Though Scott didn’t mean to yell that out, it kind of slipped. Tessa’s face instantly turned red as everyone in the class looked over to see the six-foot five wrestler. “You might want to go ahead and buy yourself a coat hanger, because that baby’s going to be more pathetic than a three-legged cat in a room full of dogs.” “SCOTT!” she yelled, trying to cut him off before he could say too much. Many people in the room seemed appalled, rightfully so. Sadly the blonde got lost somewhere along the line, asking “why would a three-legged cat be in a room full of dogs” Wouldn’t he get hurt?” Scott shot his head around to look to Tessa, “see! She really is that dumb.” But Tessa was not having it tonight, demanding the man known as Chris Storm to the wrestling world come and behave himself beside her. After years together, Scott knew he had better do what she said, or it would be the couch for him tonight. While the blonde was left to try and figure out the riddle of the three-legged cat, the Lamaze instructor walked into the room, shutting the door behind her. “Welcome class!” she said as she took her place in front of them all. Everyone’s attention shifted from the blonde and Scott to their instructor. “Ok class,” she started, giving everyone a broad smile that must have been practiced; “we’re going to start with some basic breathing exercises. Remember, breathe in then push as you breathe out.” Scott rolled his eyes, Tessa didn’t seem to notice. “Come on Tessa, you already know how to breathe.” His wife to be wanted to ignore him and his sarcastic comments, but Scott kept pushing the issue and showing his boredom over the entire ordeal. “Look, stop being a pain. You agreed to come to these classes with me. We’re doing this together, just like how we made the baby together.” “Yeah,” Scott whined, “but making the baby was so much more fun.” Tessa shot Scott another death gaze. “Well if you ever want to have fun again, I suggest you behave and relax.” Beaten, Scott dropped down to the rubber floor and crossed his arms over his chest. “I’m just saying; we could be doing more important things.” “Like training, that’s what you want to say, isn’t it?” Tessa almost seemed tired of this discussion, “you would rather be in the gym.” She almost seemed to be setting herself up, as Scott rolled his eyes and gave out a sigh. “Oh course I’d rather be in a gym. Let’s not forget that I’m in that stupid tournament against Katie O’Clare.” “Yeah, but you promised me you would be here with me.” Scott crossed his arms over his chest, ignoring whatever the instructor was going on about, and turned his head away from Tessa. “Stop being so pissy, just because you have one match to wrestle by yourself. Get over it.” Not about to just get over it, Scott shot his head back toward her. “Yeah, but I’m not supposed to be. I’m the tag team champion, not a contender for the World title. Legacy is giving me a migraine over the entire ordeal, and Caldera won’t budge an inch.” Tessa didn’t seem to see what the big deal was, “well they will just have to get over one stupid match.” “And one stupid injury could be the end of my contract,” he snapped back, letting her know it wasn’t so simple. The instructor for the class decided that she had had enough of the two’s bickering interrupting her class and cleared her throat loud enough to draw their attention. “Is there a problem?” she asked, trying to sound as polite as possible. The two quarreling lovers nodded their head “no” and allowed themselves to be distracted as the instructor showed the class the type of things they would need to bring to the hospital with them during labor. It didn’t take long for the two to start back up though. One groan of boredom from Scott was enough to cause Tessa to turn her body. “Look, just good. You don’t want to be here. I’ll just find someone else to help me.” “Why does it have to be like that?” his arms finally left their crossed position and flew out to his sides, “Come on, I’m just worried about my career.” “Then just go, if you’re worried so much.” Scott rolled his eyes, but the instruction was getting annoyed now. “Excuse me,” she said, “is there a problem?” Both looked toward her and again nodded. After a brief moment of silence from the two, the instruction continued on with her class. This was when Scott was able to speak up again. “Don’t worry, I’ll be there. After all, I took time off for this. I’ll just come up with a gameplan.” At the mention of game plan, Tessa couldn’t help but worry. This was, after all, one half of Banned & Exiled~! She never knew just what he was up to.
