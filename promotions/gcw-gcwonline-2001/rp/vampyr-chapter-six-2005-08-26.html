Vampyr: Chapter Six

26 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26 Aug 05 The cool water from the shower hit Roberto's body and it instantly relaxed his muscles a great deal. Another long night had just come and gone and now it was time for another day to flash by as he slept through it. The shower came before sleep though as did food, eating was something he was learning to do very rarely; there was just something about his line of work that took his appetite away. His eyes closed as he let the water take him in completely. It was the same each day, nothing about his routine changed not even what he thought about each and every time that he stood in this shower. How long would he be doing this? Roberto's life has always been far from normal, or at least every since his family was murdered. He thought that he would feel content with just killing the ones who took his family from him, but something inside of him snapped that night. Hearing their screams as they burned only excited him more, almost as if he knew this was what he was born to do. Could his life ever be the kind of life that people dream of having? Are a wife, kids, pets, and the ever so cheesy white picket fence in his cards? Roberto could never be sure, as a matter of fact he knew it was impossible as long as he remained on the path that he led now. How could he bring a child and family into this world? His thoughts always roam to the idea of one day having to slaughter his own child because he or she is transformed into the one thing he hates the most. No matter how many different things he can dream up or think up, Roberto's mind always falls back on the one image he misses from his life the most. That angelic face, her picture perfect body, her sweet smell, and an unforgettable kiss. After all of these years, Kari was always engraved in his memory. He purposely stayed away from her all of these years, knowing that the story he gave to the police was forcing everyone to look at him like he was some maniac. Who would believe that VAMPIRES were real? Roberto couldn't bear to have Kari look at him like all of the others, so he left never to see her again, except for in his dreams everyday. Those blue eyes opened again and he reached out turning off the cold shower, but Roberto didn't move right away, instead he stood there lost in his thoughts. A deep sigh follows as he slides the shower curtain to the side and quickly grasps a white towel that is handing up from a hook on the wall. Carefully Roberto steps out of the shower and onto the hard floor and begins drying his hair off first. When finished with his hair the towel gets wrapped around his waist. Roberto walks to the mirror and looks at himself, almost studies himself. He would never fully understand why he continued to hunt them; it has consumed his life so much that he is almost totally alone, with the exception of a few close friends whom would help him whenever a problem came about. By now he could not even begin to count the amount of these 'demons' that he has sent to the afterlife anymore, he just knew the number was very high. He also knew that one day this emptiness in his life would have to be filled by something. The problem was, if it wasn't going to be filled by killing them, then what could it be filled by? Not only that but what would he do when or IF they are all defeated? Could he be happy being some karate instructor at the local gym? Maybe a telemarketer? Or what about a journalist? Hell maybe even join the military, a place his attributes would be put to good use. He chuckled slightly at all of these thoughts, but also knew that one day he may have that type of decision to make. His life has always been about death, plain and simple. Anger, rage, and revenge have been with him every step of the way with his other good friend, LONELINESS not too far behind. Roberto shook his head at his own reflection, realizing that he barely even recognized who he was anymore. As much as he wanted to love again, or at least go back and find Kari and do the things to her that have stayed in his lustful thoughts since he was young, how could he? To love someone the right way is to love them completely ... he didn't even know who he was, so how could he love another? Roberto shook his head to try and get these thoughts from his mind; it wasn't the time or the place. He knew when he hit the dirty mattress in the main room that his dreams would take him to the very same places. He flipped the light off as he walked from the bathroom and into the main room, as he got to the mattress he ripped the towel off and tossed it to the other side of the room. On the mattress laid a pair of white boxer shorts, black nylon shorts and a white t-shirt. Roberto slid the clothes on and quickly tumbled onto the mattress, making himself as comfortable as possible. His arms came upwards and rested behind his head as he looked up into the ceiling. The same patterns in the ceiling stared back at him, almost laughing at him, maybe even mocking him. Roberto got a look of disgust on his face as this thought came to him and immediately he turned to his side, keeping his eyes away from the ceiling. Sleep would do him good that was for damn sure, and just as his eyes began to flutter closed something interrupted him. KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK-KNOCK-KNOCK! Swiftly Roberto sprung up from his bed and immediately pulled his pistol out from underneath his pillow. He slid towards the door, putting his back to the wall next to it. The 'click' from the safety lever being flipped is heard. 'Who is it?' he said, not yelling but not being all that quiet either. 'Open the door Rob, it's just me.' The voice on the other side of the door brought a smile to Roberto's face. Quickly he unchained the locks and swung the door open revealing a middle sized African-american man, probably in his mid 20's. He wore a solid black, fitted baseball cap turned backwards, a red t-shirt and baggy, black jeans. 'Que Paso amigo?' Roberto asked as the man walked in and Roberto shut the door. 'Some news for you man, big news.' 'Alright Paul, lay it on me.' Roberto says as he falls to the bed again. “We found IT Roberto.” Roberto abruptly jolts up into a sitting position, his eyes going wide as he stares at Paul with a blank expression. Slowly a smile spreads across Roberto’s lips. “Don’t fuck with me Paul, It is too early.” “No I’m not kidding, Alex was leading a crew in Brazil, one of those city names that I can’t pronounce, I leave that up to you and Alex. They were digging when they found the black case, there is only one problem though.” “What is it?” Roberto asked. “Well it was suppose to have been broken into two blades right?” Roberto just nodded. “Well from what I know it was then formed into two legendary swords almost identical to one another.” “Alright look Paul, we both know that I know the legend, just tell me what is wrong.” “Okay, Okay well there is only one in the case. Is there any chance that it never broke?” “There’s always a chance Paul, but for some reason I highly doubt it. The other has to be around there somewhere and we must find it before they do.” “Then get your shit together, I’ll bring the car around.” Roberto was left alone now to get his things together, not that he had too much to collect but he did as he was requested. This was a huge break through, because finding the legendary “Swords of Nazarus” would unlock many doors in this war that he found himself in the middle of. Roberto pulled out a black gym bag and filled it with money, guns, ammo, blades and a wide range of weaponry that he may find himself needing on this venture. After collecting his things, Roberto nods in approval and heads out the door and right outside Paul awaits in a black Chevy Avalanche. Roberto tossed his bag into the backseat before climbing into the passenger side and adjusting himself. They had a long drive ahead of them and Roberto knew that sleep was the one thing he needed the most. He was excited though, perhaps a bit too excited since this could turn out to be something completely different. With the revelation that only one sword was found, this may still be an incredible find but not the find that they need most. A lot was riding on finding these swords, more than even Roberto could fully understand. “Have you gotten any sleep yet Rob?” Paul asked as he quickly examined his friend. “No not yet, I had just gotten in from “work” and finished taking a shower when you popped up.” “Do you ever wonder if you put too much of yourself into this man?” Roberto’s head jerked to the side to look at Paul as if he were crazy. “You of all people should understand why I do what I do.” “I know why you hate them Rob, but is it worth taking over your life over?” “They took my life!” Roberto yelled this but not really meaning to. He caught himself as he felt his blood pressure rising and calmed himself. “They are all I have left amigo, I have no family to look after so its them that I look after ? and when I find them I send them back to hell.” “When does it end though?” Paul asked with genuine concern for his close friend. It was the question that Roberto had asked himself everyday since this began. Its ironic that every time he pushes these questions to the back of his mind, someone or something stirs them up again. Roberto chuckled to himself at this, knowing he could never escape his own sorrow and fear. As much as he hated to admit it, fear consumed him. He feared that one of those CREATURES would someday take his life and that all of his fighting would be for nothing. Roberto feared that even if he lasted through this war, he would die alone and without ever claiming that love he lost once before. Most of all he fears that his family will not accept him in the afterlife. Paul wanted an answer to his question and Roberto felt obligated to give him the only answer he knew. “It never ends Paul ?” His voice trails off at the end. Paul’s expression is filled with concern now. Paul always tried looking out for Roberto every since they were young. The two met in High School almost by accident. Roberto was a loner at their school, didn’t talk, didn’t play sports, etc. Teachers loved having him in class because he was the perfect student, attended everyday, never spoke when he wasn’t requested to, did his work, and passed with ease. There was something about him though that Paul wondered about. One summer night Paul was driving home from a party, not dissimilar to any normal high school bash you would encounter when he saw someone standing on the ledge of a small bridge. Paul instantly stopped, thinking someone was trying to commit suicide. Paul rushed to the person’s aid and after a few moments of negotiations was able to get them to come down. It was a very attractive young woman, so Paul had no problems consoling her as she cried on his shoulder. That’s when Roberto showed up, ripping this woman from Paul’s arms and tossing her to the ground. Paul was pushed to the ground also, at first he was pissed off at Roberto for this, thinking he was just some lunatic ? until he looked back at the woman he “saved”. Her eyes were red and fangs had somehow slid down and poked from out of her lips. She hissed and dove after Roberto but in one quick motion he leaped backwards, pulling out two silver blades and tossed them, causing them to dig deep into her chest. Paul watched in awe, amazement, and horror as she slowly deteriorated into nothing. Since that night Paul has helped Roberto with anything and everything, and as strong as he was, Paul knew that Roberto was scared. “One day you need to have a full life Roberto.” “Perhaps one day Paul ? ? Roberto’s eyes became heavy and soon he was almost completely asleep. A faint smile spread across his tired lips as he spoke these words. “With Kari.” Paul looked over at his sleeping friend and smiled. His friend was human after all.
