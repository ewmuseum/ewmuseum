Skeletons ... One

28 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28 Oct 05 “Mind if I smoke?” “Not at all ? didn’t know you smoked though.” “I don’t ? adds to the dramatic effect.” He chuckles as I light the tip of my cigarette before taking in a puff and releasing the cloud of smoke overhead. I watch as the smoke swirls up, eventually disappearing but not before spreading all over from the winds of the small fan that rests in the window. The man’s voice was deep, calm, soothing in a strange way. Even as he chuckled, that voice was almost hypnotic. His laughter died out and eventually just blended in with the sounds that came from the fan. I take in another breath full of smoke, blowing it out soon after. I take my free hand and place it behind my head, relaxing more. I turn my head slightly to make eye contact. “So where do we begin?” I asked softly. “The beginning perhaps. Its always the best place to start.” “No.” I said matter of factly. My tone seemed to startle him. “Well why?” “Why not” Its been told already Doc. Who doesn’t know about the unfortunate tragedy that befell my young wife and infant daughter? People know the story, and are tired of it. It helped shape me yes, but it did not define me.” I turn my head again, no longer making eye contact. “There is something else. Something that deserves more attention. Perhaps something even darker than anyone ever imagined.” “Go on.” I smile, a knowing smile. They all say they want to know it all, but when it finally all does come out they wish they hadn’t. The good Doctor will feel the same way. I reach over and put my cigarette out, placing it in the ash tray. I exhale the last bit of smoke from my lungs and place my other hand behind my head as well. I feel my body begin to relax, then again I guess that’s what these offices are for. “Do you believe that mot all men dubbed “evil” really are? I mean do you think that what they do is evil just because a good portion of people do not agree?” “I think a man knows what’s right and wrong. I believe that a man chooses which road he takes.” “Fate?” “non sense.” I chuckle on my own accord now and lay my head back, allowing it to sink further into my pillow. I couldn’t say how many times I have been asked why I am the way that I am. Why is it that I take pleasure in other people’s suffering? Why does the sight of blood and tears excite me, even push me to greater feats? Does a man who can make most anyone fear him, have any fears of his own? They try hard to dig into my mind, but my answer is always the same. You won’t like what you see. They don’t believe me of course, just as this doctor doesn’t now. People have tried breaking the seal and gathering every secret of mine they could muster to take me down for good. It has yet to work though. You see digging in the past can be a very dangerous game, because when you dig up the past, all you get is dirty. “Fate does exist Doc. Sometimes things happen that you can’t control, almost as if you weren’t meant to stop them from happening.” “You mean your family’s death” Starr and Cindy?” “NO! Have you not been listening to a damn word I’ve been saying!” I said this is beyond them. Beyond some accident. Yes their deaths took a toll on me but it is not what turned me into the?” I pause. The word was right at the tip of my tongue but was I really about to say it? People have been saying it since DWA but I simply pushed them aside. It was a natural thing in my profession. But what if its more than that now? What if I truly have become lost in this thing that I have created? “Monster.” No words were spoken between us now. I was concentrated only on the blades of the fan going round and round, over and over again. Emotions were beginning to swirl inside of me as well, emotions that I had blocked out long ago. THIS place did it. I never wanted to come back here after it all happened, I promised myself that I’d stay as far away as possible. House Shows would come here and I’d make sure I wasn’t booked and luckily enough nothing bigger has come until now. Could I step out of this room and face everything that there is out there again? Did I want to? “Roberto.” the doctor’s voice interrupts my thoughts. He gains my attention but I do not turn to look at him. “You fear this place yes” (I nod) Well to conquer that fear, it must be battled head on. Whatever happened here can be forgiven and more than likely already has.” I laugh Not just any laugh I laugh outright. I can feel the doctor staring at me like some raving lunatic. He must be wondering exactly what he said that was so funny. I try my best to calm my laughter down, although I can still feel the tears streaming down my face. I take in a deep breath and speak to him. “Some people are never forgiven Doc,” I take in a few quick breaths, getting my self under control. It takes a few moments but it all comes together. “I am who I am, it cannot be changed now. All I can hope for is understanding. Everyone wants to know exactly who LeStatt Knight is and?” “No.” He cuts me off, making my head jerk to the side and stare at the doctor. “No!” No what!”” “Knowing who LeStatt Knight is, is not our main objective here today. There is a more pressing question that needs to be answered, a question that only you can answer.” “Yeah and what would that be?” I asked a bit annoyed. “What ever happened to Roberto Abitua.” My face wiped itself of all emotion. I could see myself flushing and having my skin turn to white. I swallowed hard and searched for answers inside of myself. I wanted to dodge the question, hope to find a way out. The thing is, it is the exact question that I knew needed answering. I could feel the warmth of all of the emotions that were tumbling inside of my body were coming to the surface. “That’s a dangerous road Doc.” “Take me there! Its time to face it head on. NO more hiding from the past, no more allowing your fear to overrun you.” Fear. Yes even I had fears, still do as a matter of fact. I hide them so well that I appear to be a rock, never letting anything bother me. Most people never get to know the entire truth about me, it’s the way it has been since I was seventeen. Death. I fear it just like most other men. I am mortal just as every other person who was ever born on this earth. People can say what they want about it being natural, but death scares me. Thing is I have already gotten my fair share of experiences with death. Each and every time it gets worse. Loneliness. As much as I value my privacy I am afraid of being totally alone in this world. It is probably why I have always latched onto the women that I have. As much as I want someone with me at all times, I have hard time letting them in ? and not pushing them away. Its not just love affairs, but friendships as well. I have given David every reason to hate me, yet he continues to be the one constant thing in my life. Acceptance. Its all I have ever wanted. I got a taste of what it is like to have the world love you, but it was but a small taste. They hate me regardless of what I do, or don’t do. They want to hate me, they look for reasons to do so. I block it out, act, put on this mask of hatred towards the world, when really it scares me that they could hate me with so much passion. My fears. They have overrun me for so long. I wouldn’t even know how to end it all. I turn to the doc with a slight grin and offer him my pack of cigarettes. “Smoke?” “No thank you.” “Trust me ? you’ll need it.” He lifts and eyebrow and slowly reaches over, taking a cigarette from the pack before sitting back slowly. He looks concerned, as if he knows he is in for a story that is like nothing he had heard before. He probably hasn’t either. I turn my gaze from him and concentrate on the ceiling. I take in deep breaths over and over again. My body was tense. This is a part of my life that I had never reopened. I guess all doors are meant to be unlocked eventually. “25 Oct 1998...” And I begun the story that changed my life forever.
