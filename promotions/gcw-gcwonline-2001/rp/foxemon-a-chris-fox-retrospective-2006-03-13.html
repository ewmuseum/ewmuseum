Foxemon (A Chris Fox Retrospective)

13 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/13/06 START TIME CAPSULE It has been a long time since any action has taken place in the wrestling world that still involves Christopher Elliot Fox. The one time best selling wrestler, numerous fed champion and chocolate chip junky had fallen off the face of the known world. He didn’t pull a Sean Desman did he? Flash in the pan? Just a glare from all the stars out there. Lots of time has passed? The scene is set on a regular two story house, rather late on a Sunday night. The house where the now hugely popular superstar grew up in, the Fox household. Ever since disappearing from he wrestling world sometime in late 2004, early 2005, the “Kid” had resigned back to his roots and had been living with his all important mother and father and newly adopted younger brother, Onjin. (Yes, Onjin still works at McDonalds.) Fox, now 28 years old, is an upstanding citizen of his classy neighborhood. In the summer he goes around and trims everyone’s lawns until his face turns red. He trains in his basement by dieting on no carb skittles and a heavy dose of Xbox Live during the winter. Yeah times have changed for Chris. He’s barely starting to grow some peach fuzz on his face and he has expanded his horizon of video games and hobbies. He still has all of his attire in his closet, nicely ironed courtesy of Mommy Fox. To say that Chris has matured some is an understatement; he’s still as rambunctious and crazy as he was in the ring. Just like most nights, Christopher relaxes and stays up late doing a variety of things. But soon enough Chris? fortunes will change. As some have said “once you’re out, they can always pull you right back in” and Chris was going to find out what this meant very soon. The lights are all out. Only a white light can be seen. Yes. The basement. The best place to dwell if you are an unemployed, 28 year old who still lives with their parents. The “Kid” was resting on a couch playing some Hexic for his Xbox. See? with all that cash lying around Chris was able to move back in with his parents and still have enough to buy whatever his little heart told him to. Onjin and the rest of the family were already in bed. It was a late Sunday night and Father Fox had work the next morning at the local cookie factory. Fox thought about a lot of things in that basement of his. Such as if he was ever going to get back into wrestling, whether or not he’d ever lose his virginity, or if the way his life has been going was the way it would stay from now on. “Ugh”.” Fox stammered, “I’m weeelly tired.” The cold basement has seen everything from Fox’s fun wrestling matches with friends, to nights with mommy and a movie. “I wuv hanging out. here”. BI--? It wasn’t there?. The charm had left. Had he? Had Fox boiled down into some pile of useless nothing? “Remember, they will pull you back in,” A ghost voice echoed. “Who was that” GRANDPA?””” Fudge cock, I thought you were dead you old lame dirty bastard! YOU STILL OWE ME A PACK OF JUICEY FRUIT BITCH!” Fox hallucinated. The eerie feeling had never quite left Fox’s head about wrestling. It felt to Fox more like unfinished business. “I’m all red in the face! I’m out of shape. I know I always dream of wrestling again. I just don’t think I can.” Fox explained as he put down the controller halfway through a survival mode game. “My parents have gotten used to me being home now; they wouldn’t want me to go away again would they?” “Lets give the fans something to cheer about,” The ghost echoed again. “YEAH! MY FANS DO NEED SOMETHING TO CHEER ABOUT!” The time expired on the Hexic game. “HOLY HOT ROD! MY GAME! Oh well” I love being here, there is no doubt in my mind that I could live da rest of my little life in this great town. I NEED AN ADVENTURE TEAM! I need some fun to get my mind off wrestling and my life and everything!” Fox bustled as he hoped up and turned off the system and TV. “I know exactly what kind of an adventure I can go on! I remember I buried my Pikachu when I stopped wrestling as a sign that I could move on. It has been hard, but Pikachu always had the answers. I GOTTA SAVE HIM! Now if I could just remember where I buried him?” As Fox paced back and forth, he was joined in the basement by Onjin, who had been woken up for some reason?. “Hey,” Onjin said, “I can’t sleep” Having another game night? “No Jin Jin, I’ve decided who is going to decide whether or not my decision to possibly go back to wrestling is a good decision! Get it?” Fox splattered. “Uh” Ye-- no?” Onjin questioned. “I’M GOING TO FIND PIKACHU AND NOT ONLY WILL HE BRING THE POKE POWER BACK IN ME BUT HE WILL KNOW WHETHER OR NOT I SHOULD RETURN TO WRESTLING!!!!!” Fox said excited. “Dude, didn’t you like bury that toy like a few years ago?” Onjin said as he fixed his glasses. “Yeah I know but you know how I’ve always been thinking about going back” Well this just hit me, Pikachu holds the answers! And only I can find him cuz after allz, I’m da FOXEMON MASTER!” Fox posed. “Isn’t it Pokemon” Not Foxemon?” The 19 year old Onjin rebuttled. “You know nothing Jin Jin” I mean YEAH DUH, its called Pokemon but once you become a master, like me, and catch “em all then you can call it whatever you want. Besides, haven’t you seen my drawings for my own anime that’s coming out in early 2009?””””””””” It’s totally original, it’s called FOXEMON! A kid named Ashe goes on an adventure to become the ultimate FOXEMON master! He also does porno on the side for a little spending money. Notice he’s totally different than Ash from Pokemon cuz he’s got a silent E on the end of his name, pretty cool eh?” Fox boasted. “Um yeah real cool”. (not),” Onjin backed away, “Well you should get some sleep, you need rest for that big adventure”.” “I’m leaving right now and I’m not coming home until I find Pikachu!” Fox guilted. “Oh”. Okay, Mom won’t like that her baby is out wandering the streets at 2 am?.” Onjin convinced. “You’re the baby now bitch” LAUGH OUT LOUD? That’s right I said laugh out loud,” Fox said. “Okay, you’re cool. Just uh, tell me one thing; why 2009 for your anime?” Onjin asked. “Cuz no one suspects that year! Everyone will be thinking 2010 cuz it’s a whole number right” BUT I CAN CASH IN ON THE ODD YEARS! Boo yeah one for the Fox!” Fox exclaimed. “Riiiiiiiight, well Chris, I’m tired now, talking to you really confuses me,” Onjin said as he walks up the stairs. Fox ends up whispering to himself?. “I hope you didn’t flip your pillow around” cuz I jizzed on it thanks to the latest edition of playboy! You should know, the one with the snexy cover, boo yeah!” So as you can see he really hasn’t changed that much. Other than the fact that he isn’t so strictly Nintendo obsessed anymore. He doesn’t have enough time in one day to play all the games he has. One time he paid one of Onjin’s friends to come over and play on the 360 to get gamer points just so Chris could fit in a bowel movement before the days end. Yeah I know, he makes me sick too and I’m just the narrator. We will have to see what Fox has in store for his adventure because sadly he fell asleep while walking up the stairs after Onjin left? oh god. END TIME CAPSULE
