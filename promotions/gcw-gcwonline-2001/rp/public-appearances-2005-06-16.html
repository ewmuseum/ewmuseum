Date: 16 June 05 Some days there seems to be this little voice nagging at the back of your mind telling you that this day is going to be different. Perhaps not bad but different in a very unique way. It’s not a feeling you can quite comprehend or grasp onto but its there, that’s something you can not deny. Every step you take seems like it has a purpose, every breath, ever twitch of your eye could be the move that brings you closer to some treasure or maybe even death. The air around you seems thick with a mysterious intensity, so thick that sometimes you can feel it building up in the back of your throat. Your eyes begin to burn and tears can fall down your cheeks and travel until they fall off your face. I’ve had days like these far too many times in my life time and they usually end up with someone leaving my life. I had that feeling the night Cindy and Starr were taken from me, the night Wylie left me and fucked Alex, even the day that I exposed RaZiel for the back stabbing asshole that he really is, and of course right before my heart attack. It’s a scary feeling for me now and although I have had a few good times happen from that feeling. I became the DWA World Champion after that feeling, I fell in love with Mary, and I even got my first wrestling job. Some people don’t believe in fate or destiny, they claim that they make up what happens to them as they go. I can honestly say that I don’t believe that. I think that no matter what you do and no matter how hard you try your life is set in front of you. It sure as hell wasn’t my actions that killed my little girl and wife. It certainly was no fault of mine that Wylie ran off with my most hated enemy and fucked him like some five dollar tramp. Believing in fate though doesn’t alter the fact that it does exist and it’s that feeling that gives you signals that a major turn is coming. Luckily for me though today is just an ordinary day. Every since I stepped outside the sun had been beating down on me. The skies were blue and clear of clouds of any kind. I always liked Florida, one of the few states that I truly love to come to. More often than not the weather is beautiful, the women are gorgeous ? even if looking is the only thing I can do anymore, and the nightlife is usually solid. Only a few things can ruin my time in Florida, seeing someone I really don’t care to see and the other? Well it’s the very reason I am here ? a damn Public appearance that Steven Caldera has so graciously put me on. I waited outside for a little while before going into this damn autograph signing just taking in the scenery. A slight breeze rolled in which made my baggy Khakis float backwards. My top, which was a white “wife beater” didn’t move as it was just about skin tight. I tied my hair up neatly because the heat wouldn’t allow me to keep it down and about. I wasn’t dressed as the GCW fans would probably expect, they are probably expecting my devil incarnate get-up that I wear to the ring, and actually it was what I was advised to wear by the GCW associates. Frankly they can kiss my ass if they think I am going to wear solid black midday in Florida. I took in a deep breath, figuring it was time to stop stalling and get this over with. In all honesty I don’t even know what fan would even want my autograph after the events that have transpired in the past few weeks. Not that I would be too heart broken about that though, I’d rather no one come so then I wouldn’t have to be put on display like a damn animal at the zoo for a bunch of people I don’t care for in the first place. The St. Pete’s Time Forum was the place, home of some hockey team ? fuck if I paid any attention to that lame sport though. The complex was nice though, that I could see from walking in. As soon as I stepped through the doors I immediately heard the roar of a small crowd of people. My head jerked to the side where the crowd had gathered and I already could tell it was going to be a long day. From the grouping of people, three jolted out and ran towards me. To me they all looked too much alike to differentiate but I knew they were helping work this little event. They all had sweat dripping from their foreheads as they approached. “Mr. Knight we need to get you in the back quickly to brief you. This won’t take long plus the fans showed up early and often so we need to get you signing some autographs quickly.” “Yeah whatever let’s get this over with.” “Follow us.” We walked out of sight from the fans but their screams were still heard. I could have waved, smiled or done something to acknowledge them but the way I see it is that they can watch me tomorrow night if they want anything more from me. Although perhaps I should cherish it since tomorrow night the boo’s that go hand in hand with my name are incredible. Going to the back was quick and painless actually, all they told me was just to be in character just not too over the top to the point where I’d be insulting them. “Have you seen the people here” Going out in public like that is insulting enough.” I said this with a bit of a grin because I only said it to get myself a bit into the character. They gave half hearted laughs and began directing me to my “booth”. We walked through curtains and I was almost blinded by all of the camera flashes that came my way. I quickly turned my head to one of the assistants. “If any of these male fans think I am putting an arm around them for a picture they have another thing coming.” He only shook his head and laughed but in all reality I wasn’t joking. I gave him a quick look and then sat down in front of a long black table. I was concentrated on the crowd of people when I was tapped on the shoulder and was a bit surprised to see Kevin Jacobs standing behind me. He gave me a slight smile and then sat down a few feet from me. The crowd slowly quieted down and Kevin was handed a microphone. “Hello fans as you probably know I am Kevin Jacobs of Global Championship Wrestling! On behalf of Steven Caldera I’d like to welcome you here today. We’ve got a few things happening including front row tickets to tomorrow’s show, some merchandise giveaways, you can win dinner with the wrestler of your choice and you’ll hear from some of the stars of GCW. First I am here with a man who over the past few months has made himself a household name. He shattered records with his incredible United States Title reign and is a shoe in for the World Title shot at Dangerous games. Please give a warm welcome to LeStatt Knight!” Boo’s were mixed with cheers from the crowd here. The boos probably came from those who are thinking IC and the cheers? well I couldn’t explain that any other way then they are just happy that the festivities were actually getting under way. Kevin let the crowd’s reaction die down before speaking anymore. “Well Knight is going to sign some autographs in a little while first we are going to have a little Q & A with this superstar and even take questions from all of you so let’s get started.” A confused look spread across my face momentarily because I didn’t know I was going to actually have to speak to this mob! I flashed Kevin an angry look and he looked worried but being the professional that he is, he brushed it off quickly. “LeStatt first of all I’d like to welcome you to Tampa and congratulate you on an excellent showing as our U.S. champion. Trey Tremendous will certainly have his hands full keeping those shoes filled.” “Indeed Kevin?” I took a moment to collect my thoughts as I was unprepared. “Trey is’well, let’s just say that when his reign is over people will fully appreciate what it is I did with that belt exactly. Trey will hold onto that title and make it into the thing I hate ? jewelry. He’ll flaunt it and sure it’ll shine as it always does but when you don’t defend it consistently it’ll lose that luster. I placed the bar so high that even if Trey keeps that title for longer than 105 days, he will never be able to claim that he brought the prestige back to the title like I can.” “There is no doubt the bar is incredibly high. You have much more going on at this very moment though Knight, that being your quest for the World championship and more importantly Jason Dread.” This got a small pop from the on lookers. “Everyone is dying to know when and if the match between you to will become official.” I felt a smile form on my lips now and then a low chuckle escaped my throat. “Well Kevin that decision isn’t my own. The booking committee and Jason Dread are the ones with that power. Hopefully Dread turns out to be at least a fraction of the champion he seems to think he is and will accept the challenge when it comes his way ? which will be very soon, pay attention to tomorrow night amigo.” “World Wide indeed will be a big night as it will be a preview of what we can expect in the coming weeks.” Jacobs said with a bit too much excitement. “You team up with another former U.S. champ in Michael Stevens to take on Dread and Trey Tremendous.” “You know Kevin, you’d think that I would be incredibly excited for this opportunity. I mean you have the man who just beat me and took my U.S. Title from me and you have the man who holds the one title that I REALLY covet. The thing about it is that I see no point in this meeting tomorrow night. Did the booking committee really think that it was a good idea” Sure the fans will tune in because they are anxious to see us four tear into each other, but then again these fans are content with most anything. I feel that the stakes aren’t high enough for this match for me to warrant any real excitement. I don’t feel this lust for blood that most seem to believe I do. I do not NEED to cripple Jason Dread nor do I need to beat him yet. In good time Dread’s ass is MINE, and that time is Dangerous Games for the World championship.” “Very well Knight I will wish you luck anyways?” I abruptly cut him off. “LUCK Kevin, has nothing to do with it.” A short and awkward silence takes place now both of us looking at each other with emotionless faces. I grin slightly which eases Kevin as he continues on. “Well moving on we will now take questions for the fans.” Hands shot up all over the place as this little Q&A began. Randomly Jacobs would choose a fan and most of the questions were petty like “Why did you betray Dread?”, “How did it feel to lose to Capo?”, and “Is Mary good in bed?”. Inwardly I detested every last one of these bastards and was ready to leave. The questions were foolish and the fans were disgusting ? but it was one question that caught my attention. “Will you treat Dread as you did Payne!”” a fan shouted out. My body froze at the mention of the name, a name that I hadn’t really put into my head for some time. In a strange way I missed that sick fucker, in a way that bastard was apart of me. I swallowed hard and glared out into audience that was in front of me. It took me a moment to come back to that moment in time as in one big flash I could see the entire feud of Knight vs. Payne. I shook my head to get the visions out. “Comparing Dread and Ale’I mean Payne is like comparing a Lamborghini to a dodge Neon. Payne was such a destructive force that no matter where you were, what you were doing, or how you were doing it you had to be careful, or else he may have slit your throat. Dread is nothing compared to that ? Dread is simply an after thought. After I destroy and embarrass Dread and take his World Title he will simply be left to rot in hell. I will not think twice about him nor will I worry about him coming back for me. Dread’YOUR champion is going to crumble. After I am through with him he will be begging for me to end his pathetic existence, and if he is lucky I may oblige.” The crowd grew eerily silent after the last comments, all eyes were fixed on me. I hadn’t noticed it but my breathing had become very heavy. My chest heaved up and down, over and over again. I finally turned my head to Kevin who looked just like the rest of the crowd. “Signing autographs just isn’t my thing.” With that final comment I stood up from my chair and turned my back to the crowd of people who instantly began booing like crazy, just as they will tomorrow night at World Wide. The only thing is that tomorrow night I will truly give them something to boo me for. 'Sweet Dreams?' I whispered this just as I walked back through the curtains ... letting myself indulge in those boos which were music to my fucking ears.
