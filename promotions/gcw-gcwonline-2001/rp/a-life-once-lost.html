A Life Once Lost

Date: here and there Walking out of the jail, awaiting the person(s) that bailed him out. Accused of murder, bail set at $5,000,000. Someone bailed him out like it was nothing. But Who? The murderer has no more family, has no friends. As a matter of fact he never meant to murder, it was something that just happened. Kind of like cheating on your wife. You never meant to stick your dick in her, it just kind of happened. His release papers signed, court date set, the murderer walks out of the prison holding him captive. Standing next to a 2006 Dodge Charger is a man he’s never seen before. About six feet 5 inches tall, average body. Shoulder length dark hair, and mystifying blue eyes. Who is this man? The murderer approaches the man, stops a few feet from him and starts his episode of staring. The man puffs his cigarette one last time before flicking it to the cement. He exhales and looks back at the murderer. “Get in.” “Wait a minute, who are you, and why did you bail me out?” “All will be explained Bryan, get in if you want to live.” “Hold the fuck on. I have no clue who you are, why the hell would I get in a car with you?” Sighing, opening the passenger door, the stranger shakes his head slightly. “I just dumped 5 million on your scumbag ass and you are going to insult me by not accepting my ride?” Bryan stares deep into this man. Who the fuck is he? Where the fuck did he come from. And most importantly why did he spend all that money to bail him out? This day has just taken a turn towards the creepy. For some sick sadistic, or can it be masochistic? Reason Bryan accepts the ride. Bryan climbs into the passenger side. The stranger slams the door, smacks the roof, and runs around the car via the back. He opens his door, and slithers in. The interior is an off black leather. It looks as if someone accidentally spilled some grey into the paint and said fuck it, they aren’t going to waste their time and finished it like so. It’s an automatic, something which made Bryan sneer. Everyone know’s a real man can ride a stick shift. This was a fucking Charger after all. 350 plus Horse. Man these fuckers use to blow anyone off the road back in the day. Now they have a fucking automatic engine. Who would have thought. The stranger puts the key in, pops it in drive and explodes like a bat out of hell. Bryan grabs the “oh shit” handle and strains his eyes. The stranger plows through some cones, and pulls out onto I-85 and floors it. They hit 85 before Bryan finally says something. “Say man, slow the fuck down, I don’t want to die tonight.” “Neither did she.” Replied back the stranger with a mysticism in his voice. His eyes never leave the road, they stay glued. “Before you say anything else I am going to lay down the rules.” “Rules” Who the fuck are you to?.” The stranger smacked Bryan with such viciousness that Bryan spat up blood. He looked at the stranger who still carried that blank look. Bryan puts his hand to his lips, pulls them away and analyzes them. A small droplet covered his middle finger. He looks back at the stranger who now eyes the road. “Now as I was saying. You do not speak until spoken too. If you speak out of turn I will smack the hell out of you. You do not make an attempt to jump for I will turn this car around and run you over numerous times until your insides wrap themselves around the wheel. You hear me?” Bryan licking the spot on his lip that is split just nods. “Good. You are getting a second chance at life Bryan. I don’t care how fucking big you are, I don’t care how mean you are. I am now god in your pathetic little life. I am your ruler and controller. You disobey me I will fucking kill you with no remorse at all.” Bryan feels like shitting himself. Who is this freak? He has never been scared in all his life like he is now. “I remember once when you were little you and Jason were out back playing some game when you came across a stray dog. Jason loved it immediately. The dog came up to him licked him and begged to be pet. Jason was such an animal lover. He wanted to keep that dog. You remember what he named that dog?” Bryan looks at this man. How hell does he know this. For all he could remember Jason and he were the only one’s to witness it. Bryan’s eyes widen as the stranger stares at him expecting an answer. “Boy I am asking you a question, you remember the damn name.” Almost as if on cue both Bryan and the stranger said “Mercy.” Bryan’s jaw drops and he stares back at the man. Bryan is pleading to this stranger with his eyes to tell him how he knows this. The stranger smiles. His teeth yellow, his breath can be seen. His fucking breathe can be seen, almost as if it was a mist, or some sort of demon leaving. “Do you remember what you did next Bryan?” Bryan nods, tears begin to fall in heavy thick drops. They make a patter sound when they collide with his jeans. He remembers, but doesn’t want to. “Yes, right in front of your little brother you slit the poor things throat. You understand Jason idolized you” That little boy wanted to be just like his older brother. But you scarred him for life. You made Jason rub the blood all over his naked body, than you began to beat your little brother. What kind of sick freak are you?” Bryan for the first time in his life began to feel guilty for his actions as a child. For the first time in his life he regretted everything. But it’s too late is it not?” Bryan looks back at the stranger and those awful fucking teeth. His shoulder length brown hair blowing in the wind, hair getting in his mouth. “Do you know what I did next Bryan” Do you know how I punished you?” “Punish me” Ok fucker who the hell are you?” The stranger’s eyes widen, they glow red, his tongue becomes forked, he grabs Bryan by his hair and smashes his head through the passenger side window. Glass shatters everywhere. Bryan blacks out. When he wakes up he’s young again, sitting at the foot of his mother’s bed. She’s paper thin, pale as a ghost. When she inhales it looks as if her bones will break, when she exhales it looks as if she’s getting crushed by a ten ton hammer. Bryan looks at his child hands. They are covered in blood. It drips on the floor, some gets on the bedsheets. His mom begins to cough. It’s such a vicious agonizing cough. She looks like she’s in pain. She looks at the foot of her bed, staring at her, soaked in blood is Bryan. She smiles, the fruit of her loins, her miracle baby. “Don’t be scared Bryan, I am going to a better place soon. You won’t ever have to see me like this again. Don’t worry, I will check in on you from time to time.” “Where you going Mama?” His voice is that of a young child, his tears of the same. “Don’t cry baby. I am going to heaven. God has a special plan for me Bryan. Like he has a special plan for you too honey.” “There is no God Mama. Daddy say’s there’s only suffering.” That mist begins to appear in her mouth. “Please don’t say such atrocities my baby. There is so a God, just wait, you’ll see one day.” Her coughing gets more and more violent. Her body shakes and convulses with each cough. She looks at her son one last time, her eyes turn red, her tongue forks and hisses at her. Bryan falls off his chair, as a being looms over him. “Wake up kid, hey wake the hell up.” Bryan wakes up and he’s in the car, covered in glass. His head feeling like a ton of bricks, his eyes glossed over. He looks to his left and that man, that piece of shit man, is still driving the car. “Damn, you were out a long time there buddy. But yeah anyways, that’s what I did. Everytime you piss me off I take something from you. Remember Melissa” Ahh, damn she was so damn good.” Is this freak really talking about his childhood love? Besides his mother, Bryan had feelings for only one person. Melissa. She was beautiful. Long dark hair, bright green eyes, and a pale complexion that was immediately attracted to. How does this man know about her? How does he know anything? “Who are you?” “I’m the Devil Bryan. Who else could I be?” That hideous rotted teeth smile appears on his face again. Bryan pushes his head back against the head rest, totally in awe at the motives, or lack there of, of this psycho. He looks at him, and that’s when he realizes he can take this man. He can over power him and put an end to this madness. “Keep thinking that way Bryan. Like I said, I don’t give a shit how big you are, I will fucking destroy you.” Bryan’s eyes widen, now this sonofabitch can read his mind? “Yes I can, and I know you want to strike me down, and I dare you asshole, I dare you to strike thee down, because I will make you join your little bitch Melissa in hell!” Bryan snaps at the mention of her name, he grabs a shard of glass on his lap and swings it the direction of the stranger. He lands it straight in the neck. Blood gushes and squirts. A suction sound is heard as the glass is pulled free from the strangers neck. When Bryan pulls the glass out, his begins to feel a sharp sting in his neck, he’s getting cold. He looks down and notices blood gushing from his very own neck. He looks at the stranger who’s laughing, not a single mark on him. Bryan’s losing consciousness, he’s getting extremely cold. The stranger continues to laugh. “I work in mysterious way son.” He wakes with a jolt staring into his bathroom mirror. Sans corpse paint is a sweaty Bryan Mayhem. He turns the cold faucet on and splashes his face with cold water a few times. He grabs a towel and begins to dry his face. When he looks up, written in blood on the mirror is “Black Mariah” Bryan shivers. Not again, the dreams are back?. He stares deep into this mirror and realizes that this may not be the encounter he was expecting. This foe has been haunting him for sometime now. He can see her in his thoughts, taste her in his mouth, feel her crawling under his skin. Staring into the reflection he see’s her, staring right back at him. He knows that if he turns around he won’t see her anymore, instead of making her disappear he stares right back at her. Her grin is evil, the way her brow creases when she turns that grin into a sneer can cut glass. She erupts in flames, this time however there is no screaming, no melting flesh, just her, burning alive feeling no pain. He can see her expression, its of nothing, no fear, no pain, no nothing. Mayhem nods into the mirror and turns around. Obviously, she was not there, but he knows the time has come’burn the blue sky, make another victim?.Extinct.
