A prophecy

14 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 14th, 2005 Blackness. It was all around him. It was as if his very soul formed an aura that bled through his skin and stained the outside world. His head was held low -- long, dark strands of hair fell before his face, and shadows covered the eyes that would send a chill down any man's spine, and tell of a life that must end. Deep and raspy breaths grumbled from his lungs, and gently pushed on the hair that obstructed his vision. He stood, completely naked, in the darkness of his own soul, as -- within a single instant -- blood squirted and stained the surrounding blackness. He cringed, and his thoughts echoed throughout space, Hurt! they cried, Hurt! A scream followed -- a shriek -- more blood sliced through the blackness, and splattered against the blackened void. Hurt! A burst of pain exploded within his head, and he let out a rasp-filled cry of his own. His eyes burned, and tears of blood rolled down his cheeks, and dripped off his chin into an increasingly large puddle beneath him. Another bolt of pain ravaged through this head, and another scream -- hurt! -- escaped. His knees buckled, and in fluid motion, he collapsed to his knees, let one heavy breath of air escape his tiring lungs, and sunk forward. He would have plummeted into the steadily increasing puddle of blood beneath him had he not caught himself with one arm. There, he was faced towards the puddle that had formed. His cries stopped -- his breathing stopped -- everything stopped. He gazed into the puddle of his own blood, only to see his very reflection gazing right back at him, grinning -- smiling -- laughing. Soon, he found himself doing the same. Hurt! He, the man known only as Alex Blaq, was certain it was a dream. It was the same dream that had occurred countless times -- night after night. It was the same dream that would end with his own death -- a suicide aided by an angel with the most hellish of halos. He'd wake, however, only to find himself living, thus starting a bad day. However, it was different, this time... Alex looked into the puddle of blood before him -- his own reflection morphed into that of another. Heavy breaths jumped from his lungs, and panic began to overcome him. The person whom he stared at was a man of the past -- a man that had not been seen for years. For a second, he saw himself in this man, but only for a second. ''No...'' Alex grunted through heavy breaths, ''No!'' His breaths became more rapid, ''Damnit, no!'' Using the arm that was not supporting him, he reared back, and with one furious scream, he thrust his fist into the puddle of blood -- it shattered, as if it were nothing more than a red-tinted mirror. The pieces of the bloody mirror evaporated before him, and suddenly, he was, once more, standing -- surrounded in blackness. He was not alone, however. ''Hello, Alex...'' a voice echoed from the shadows. Alex stood, and looked about the darkness, the panic now gone -- maybe simply hidden -- replaced by the scowling face that would usually be found upon him and growled, ''You...'' Before Alex, the man whose reflection he had seen appeared. The two looked similar in a very profound way. Alex was a much bigger man, easily out-sizing the man before him by thirty pounds; Alex's hair was longer, and his face was more chiseled. In fact, the two looked nothing alike, unless you looked into their eyes. Their eyes were not the eyes that could be forgotten -- they were haunting. Both pair of eyes were capable of speaking when words were not meant to be said. ''You can't be here...'' Alex spoke coldly. ''Oh, I assure you,'' the other replied, ''I am here.'' ''You aren't real -- this is a dream.'' ''Seems to be more of a nightmare, now.'' ''I don't have nightmares.'' The other chuckled, and shook his head, ''Alex, Alex...'' ''What do you want?'' ''You seem surprised to see me, Alex, why is this?'' ''Because I know you aren't here -- I--'' He was cut off. ''Oh, Alex. I assure you that I am here -- wake up, and see for yourself -- Wake up!'' Alex's eyes shot open, the morning sun pierced through the cracks of the blinds in his bedroom, stinging and burning his sensitive eyes. His eyes painfully adjusted as necessary, and a groan rumbled from his throat. Alex looked about his ravaged room -- shards of glass, pictures, papers, and clothing scattered the floor while dressers, chairs, and end tables lay overturned. There was only one window in the room, and the blinds hung before it, however; the morning sun would always manage to creep through the blinds, and make contact with Alex, but the rest of the room remained in the darkness. Everything seemed just as he had left it the night before -- there was no physical sign of the other -- the one from his dream. But, Alex felt him as if he were breathing down the very back of his neck. He felt him as one would somehow know he was being watched. Where are you...? He thought, Damnit, where are you hiding? Alex sat up in his bed. His bed had only one thin, white sheet as covers -- it was all he ever needed. Underneath that sheet, Alex was as he was in his dream, naked. He didn't care who might have been in the room -- he didn't care at all. Where are you? I know you're here. His eyes roamed the room, searching for the other. Alex's patience began to go thin, and another grumble escaped his throat. ''Damnit, come out!'' Alex screamed into the darkness of his room. The other did just that. Alex noticed movement from the darkest corner of his room, and out stepped the one he had seen in his dream. ''Ah, you've finally woke. Good morning, Alex,'' The other spoke calmly, as if to taunt Alex. ''Jason...'' Alex replied, ''What do you want?'' Jason raised an eyebrow as he treaded through the wrecked room. He found a chair that had been toppled over, and reset it on all fours, with the back facing Alex. He sat down, rested his forearms on the top of the chair and smirked, ''Oh, Alex -- you always seem to think I want something of you--'' ''Because you do,'' Alex interrupted, ''Now what is it that you want? Why are you here?'' Jason shook his head, and glared at Alex. His eyes locked onto Alex's and the two went into a stare-down. It ended rather quickly, as Jason once again smirked, then raised a finger and rubbed under his nose, ''You might want to take care of that,'' Jason chuckled, pointing out the blood that began to flow from Alex's nose. Nosebleeds, along with those dreadful headaches, were not uncommon for Alex. He had quite a bit of them, often multiple times a day. His nose would bleed as if it had been broken, blood would sometimes literally gush from his nostrils -- the irritation caused by the excessive blood flow would often lead to a sneeze that sprayed a mist of blood into the air that would stain whatever it came in contact with. ''It's fine,'' Alex replied sternly, ''Now what do you want?'' Jason turned his head, and closed his piercing eyes before speaking, ''Alex, tell me -- why do you think I want something of you? What if I just wanted to pay an...'' He paused, and spoke on the sarcastic side, ''...old friend a visit?'' ''Then I would say you're full of bullshit.'' Jason laughed, and stood from his chair. Alex followed suit, and stood from the bed, and locked eyes onto Jason who raised an eyebrow -- after all, Alex was naked. ''Dress yourself,'' ''I'm fine.'' ''Stubborn as always, I see.'' ''Anything to spite you.'' ''Haven't changed a bit.'' ''Don't plan to.'' ''Really?'' ''Really.'' Jason let out a sigh, and shook his head. He looked back down at the chair, which now seemed out of place, ''Well, Alex...'' he said turning the chair upside down, and dropping it back onto the clustered floor, ''What if I told you I have a... message for you?'' Jason failed to ignite Alex's curiosity, ''Let me guess -- one of those god-forbidden prophecies of yours.'' Jason looked up and nodded, ''Yes, a prophecy...You--'' ''I will not hear it.'' Alex interrupted. ''I figured as much.'' ''Then why waste your time?'' Jason laughed, ''Anything to spite you.'' ''You sonuvabitch...'' Alex grumbled. ''Alex, Alex...'' Jason trailed off into a sigh. Jason was dressed casually -- blue jeans and a long sleeved black shirt. His short, dark hair was neatly spiked at the front, and gelled forward. He stared at Alex, who seemed like a wreck. Unlike Jason, Alex remained naked -- his hair was tussled into a mess that hung over his frowning brow, and his nose continued to bleed. The blood ran down into his mouth, and onto his chin, occasionally dripping onto his chest. Alex shook his head, and looked towards Jason, who smiled, ''I just hope you know that you'll be seeing a lot of me in the near future.'' ''Good.'' Alex, sharp tongued as ever, returned the smirk, ''Seeing you is always a refreshing reminder of what I did to you. I'll --'' ''You need not remind me, Alex.'' Jason interrupted, ''I remember it just as well as you.'' ''I'll never forget the look on your face, Jason -- I'll never forget --'' ''Enough.'' Alex ignored him, and continued, ''I'll never forget the way you fell to the ground --'' Jason closed his eyes and turned his head to avoid eye contact with Alex, ''Like a lifeless ragdall -- I've heard it all before, Alex -- now enough!'' Alex continued to ignore Jason, ''You're lucky, Jason. I could have done much worse. By the way, Jason, I have been wanting to ask you how your neck is doing.'' ''It's fine, thank you. It doesn't bother me at all. You could have done worse, Alex, I know you could have. But, it wasn't enough to get ride of me -- I'm still here, Alex. And I know you hate seeing me -- yes, it reminds you of what you did to me, but it's also a reminded that what you did wasn't enough.'' Jason was now looking directly into Alex's eyes, the smirk now lining his young and smooth face. ''You shouldn't be worrying about what bothers me, Alex -- you have much bigger things to worry about.'' Alex let out a careless chuckle, and shook his head, ''Hardly.'' He sat back down onto his bed, but Jason remained standing. ''Your lies won't hold much longer.'' Jason spoke sternly - almost matter-of-factly. ''People are easy to deceive, Jason.'' ''Yes, I figured you would know that. After all, you've been deceiving yourself for quite a bit now, haven't you?'' Before Alex could respond, Jason continued, ''No use in denying it, Alex -- you know you are.'' Alex was silent. ''Just look at you,'' Jason went on, ''You're degenerating -- completely falling apart! You've lost your will to survive, and you've grown weak. You're no longer the man who snapped my neck as if it were a twig, and I'm ashamed to have fallen to a man as pitiful as you. As every minute goes by, you grow weaker -- you fall into you're own ridiculous lies - trying to present yourself as a man you are not! ''GCW Hardcore champion. I've seen how you hold that title when you're not throwing it about. I've seen the glimmer in your eyes when you rest it on your shoulder -- Alex, since when do you even give a damn about championship belts?'' ''I don't give a damn about it, and you know I don't. I have it becau-'' ''Bullshit, Alex!'' ''I have my reasons!'' Alex shot back. ''I have my reasons for everything.'' ''And what's the reasoning, Alex? It's because you're weak -- afraid--'' ''I fear nothing!'' ''That's not the case any longer. And that's because you are losing control, Alex. The man that I fell to is dying. You would be able to see this yourself, Alex -- but like I said. You've grown weak -- your heart is softening.'' ''You're wrong.'' ''Please!'' Jason practically laughed, ''The Alex that I fell to would have annihilated Lestatt Knight with ease--'' ''Don't mention him.'' ''Why not, Alex? Are you afraid of him?'' ''Oh right, right. Running from him is all part of some plan -- it's all part of your deceptions, isn't it?'' ''Like I said. I have my reasons for everything.'' ''And, your reasoning for wanting the United States Title?'' ''Who ever said I wanted it?'' ''Your actions have made it pretty clear that you want it.'' ''Lestatt is the one who cares for it. I don't. It's more than that god-forbidden thing. He wants it, and he holds it proudly. I want it so he simply can't have it. I want it to prove that he is not the most dominating champion in GCW. I want it to so I can open the eyes of everyone in this god-damned world, and show them -- and especially show you -- that I am to be feared.'' ''Since when do you need a title to show people that?'' Alex froze, and his gaze left Jason. His ghostly eyes lowered to the floor. ''No...'' His voice was low, almost inaudible. ''Like I said, Alex. You are losing control, can't you see it?'' ''And why do you care?'' Jason ignored the question, ''The real battle hasn't even begun, Alex. Mind over matter, Alex. The more you think Lestatt is a threat to you, the more he is a threat to you. The more you lie to yourself, the more those lies turn true. The more you deceive others, the more you deceive yourself. Keep it up, Alex, and you know what will happen. It's happened once before, and it'll happen again.'' Alex looked back up towards Jason. His nosebleed had subsided, and the blood had crusted underneath. He tilted his head, before shaking it slowly, ''You didn't answer my question. Why do you care about me? Don't you want me to suffer for what I've done to you?'' ''I have my reasons.'' ''That's not good enough.'' ''It is a good enough response for you.'' ''It's about that pathetic prophecy of yours, isn't it?'' ''Yes, it is. But, as you said, you will not hear it.'' ''You're right, I won't. Because I'm in control -- I'm in control of my life. Not some god, or fate -- nothing else! Just me. You're prophecy simply doesn't mean a damn thing to me.'' Jason chuckled, ''You've already heard it. Either way, that does not change what will happen -- it's inevitable. Time heals all wounds, and the wound is healing. The door will unlock, and sun shine through. Hear me well, Alex. For both our sakes, hear me well.'' Jason sent one last glare at a motionless Alex before turning towards the door. He walked through the mess, and walked to the door. ''You can't prevent it,'' Jason began, ''But you can fight it.'' Jason opened the door, and stopped for a second before speaking his final words, ''Bless you.'' What? Alex raised an eyebrow as he heard Jason let out a slight laugh before heading out the door and shutting it behind him. Alex sneezed, new wave of blood began to spill from his nostrils as a fine mist of blood sprayed from his nose. His nostrils stung -- blood bubbled down his face as Alex growled under his breath -- strings of mucus mixed blood dribbled and dangled from his crimson-stained chin. Damn you, Jason, damn you..
