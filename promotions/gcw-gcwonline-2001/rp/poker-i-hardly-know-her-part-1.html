Poker? I hardly know her! Part 1

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: A lazy saturday afternoon I waited until the plane taxied out of sight before I turned and left the airport. I knew Cindy didn't want to leave, but there was still business to be taken care of back home. Bills to pay, plants to water, and she's pretty sure she forgot to wash the dishes before she left. I could see the regret in her face as she realized that she did have to get back to New York. She had seen my GCW PPV debut and watched me win the TV title. I had to convince her that was enough for now. As I headed out of the airport I pulled my cell from my pocket and turned it back on. I had left it off for the last couple of days. Two missed messages, bet you anything they're from Jeff. As I stepped outside into the hot afternoon I played my messages. 'Yo, Mike. It's Jeff, listen, I got you that title bonus. And I got a call from the Coca Cola people for their new Vault engery drink, they want you for a commercial. It's basically you getting your ass beat by a bottle of the stuff. Something about it being really intense, let me know what you think.' I tried that stuff. It wasn't too bad. I'd have to think about that. Second message. 'Okay, Mike, put the title belt down and pick the damn phone up. You got a match with a guy named Angel on the next WorldWide, I'm e-mailing you everything I got on him. Keep up the good work, and on a side note, I figured a few things out. Like namely that is Johnny Lexicon under that mask, it's gotta be. The fighting style is slightly different, looks like he put on a few pounds, but there were some things that looked the same. After all I fought the guy three times. If it's not him under the mask, then I don't know who it is. I'll send you everything I got on Lexicon too, so you can prepare.' Prepare? Did he know what I was thinking? Probably? The man's been in the business for eleven years. Several of which he spent with me. He knew what I was thinking then and probably what I'm thinking now. Either that or he knows like I know things aren't done between me and the Marauder, Lexicon, whoever the fuck he was. Because they weren't. I hit the Mickey D's drive through and munched on my fries and chicken nuggets as I drove around thinking. The thing I still didn't get was why me? Was it because I was the new kid on the block? Was it just something about me that he didn't like? Did I screw the guy over somewhere in the past? Or was it just a random thing, he looked at me and said 'There's a guy I'd like to piss off and get him good and mad at me so he can kick my ass six ways from sunday.' Hey! A pool hall! I haven't played pool in a long time. Yeah, I know, random tangent. But it was true, I hadn't played pool in a long time. I wondered if I still had it. One way to find out. I headed in tossing the last McNugget in my mouth. The place smelled of stale cigarette smoke, sweat, and beer. Beer? Well damn, this keeps getting better and better. Beer and pool! Throw in a couple of hookers and I'd never leave! Wait. I'm married. Never mind the hooker part. Throw in some porn and I'd never leave! I ordered a beer and looked for an unoccupied table. I located one and moved over to it. After chalking up and racking up, I went for the break. Well hot damn! I sunk two balls on the break! I still got it baby! I cleared the table in less than two minutes. 'You always were a damn pool shark.' 'Richard?' He had less hair than the last time I'd seen him. At least on his head. The blue Polo shirt showed his arms and chest were still hairy as a Silverback gorilla. His name was Richard Clark and he was the first wrestling promoter I ever worked for after graduating from KIller Kowalski's school. 'Rack 'em up kid.' I set up the rack, and Richard broke. 'Nice job with the VanLorne chick.' he shook his head and paused to light a cigar. 'Dames in wrestling. Whoda thunk it?' Richard's from the old school when women fought other women, and back then it was just a gimmick. Not considered anything more than eye candy. 'Times change Richard. People and business practices evolve.' I said as he missed his next shot, I sunk the six ball. 'You can't be a dinosaur forever Richard.' 'I am not a dinosaur Mike.' he said, jabbing a fat finger at me. 'I just...fell behind the times.' 'Looks to me like you fell into the buffet line.' I said walking past him and patted his stomach to make my point. He laughed, 'Well you look about the same, except for the tats. So what were you doing before you joined GCW? I lost track of you after the SWO closed.' 'You've been keeping track of my career?' 'I keep track of everyone's career. The great thing about the information age Mike. You can find out almost anything about anyone, and with literally thousands of websites out there dedicated to wrestling, you can find out anything about anyone.' I rasied my eyebrows and took a pull from my beer as Richard made his next two shots. 'Well one place I signed into wanted me to play jobber for them. I told them to go fuck themselves, got married and moved to Mexico for four years.' 'Really? That would be why.' he said, starting to work the table, I sat down, 'I figued most guys would stay in the states after hitting big. Especially after winning seven world titles. I mean you were huge for a while there.' 'I didn't appreciate being pushed down to curtain jerker and I'd heard some good things about AAA, so I moved South of the Border, put on a mask, went under the spanish version of The Machine and wom a couple of tag-team titles. Blew out my knee, came back home. Matter of fact, Jeff Wylde's managaing my career now.' The cue ball jumped off the table and rolled across the floor and hit the wall. Richard almost tore the felt. 'Jeff Wylde?' he said. I knew Jeff had passed through Richards old IWA promotion a couple of years before I did. Now he was a succesful businessman. Apparently Richard didn't know that last part. 'I thought the kid retired!' Kid? Jeff was in his thirties! 'At least last I heard, he wrestled some place called XCW after GCW, and then DWA, then nothing.' I filled Richard in on 3W and Jeff's eventual final retirment. 'Listen kid, why don't you take some of that GCW money and buy me a beer?' 'But I haven't finished kicking your ass at pool yet.' 'Oh the game's not over. I just want a beer.' I laughed as I headed over to the bar and ordered Richard a beer. Then I missed my next shot and he proceeded to clear the table. 'You fat, little, greasy hustler.' I said as he sunk the eight ball. 'Damn right kid.' he grinned. I shook my head and smiled. 'What'cha doin' later on tonight kid?' 'Not a fuckin' thing Rich.' I said, 'I was gonna head back to the hotel, get in workout, maybe watch some TV, order room service, and eventually go to bed.' 'Tell ya what. There's a place about half a mile from here. I got the card here somewhere.' he said digging out his wallet, he handed me a card. 'Hi-Heelz? A strip club? Rich, I'm married, I can see a naked woman anytime I want to.' 'Yeah,' Richard said, 'But how are your poker skills? There's a game that runs in the back, a little under the radar if you catch my meaning. Buy in's only five grand, come on Mike, just for shits and giggles.' I raised my eyebrows and killed my beer. 'Illegal poker game?' I asked dropping my voice, 'I get caught in something like that, my fuckin' career is over. Does the mafia run this place?' 'No, the maifa runs this pool hall. The guy who owns the strip club and runs the game isn't affiliated with any criminal orginzation. Hell a couple of our regulars are cops. It's just a little fun me and some guys have. Come on Mike, one night ain't gonna clean you out is it?' I flipped the idea over in my head for a moment. 'No funny business? Just a bunch of guys playing poker?' 'That's all it is.' 'You realize if you fuck me over on this, I'm gonna forget that you gave me my first break in wrestling.' Now I stood a good foot or so over Richard, outweighed him by about thirty pounds and was in much better shape. That and my eyes closed to slits and my voice dropped to a whisper, the way it did when I was royally pissed. 'Just be there kid.' Richard said, patting me on the shoulder and turning and walking out. I should have known better that to try and intimidate a wrestling promoter, even if he was retired. But if anything, at least I had plans for tonight.
