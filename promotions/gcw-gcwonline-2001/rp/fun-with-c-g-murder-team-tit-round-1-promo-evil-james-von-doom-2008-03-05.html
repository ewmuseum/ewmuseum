Fun with C.G. Murder (TEAM TiT round 1 promo, "Evil" James von Doom)

5 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/05/08 03/05/08 The scene opens in the O'Malley Murdering Salon in Glasgow, Scotland. There are a few scattered people standing around or sitting on the table. Two of these people stand out front the rest. One is a guy with brown hair sitting at the bar drinking a Coors Light wearing a Church of the Unholy official GCW T-shirt and the other guy is a Hispanic-American man with a goatee and black hair. He is drinking a Bloody Mary and has a hunting knife hidden under his jacket. The guy with the brown hair is getting some stares from some of the patrons because he is some sort celebrity. In fact he is a professional wrestler and he is talking to his friend about the upcoming TEAM Invitational Tournament he is taking part in as the mysterious "Mystery Entrant." He is, in fact, me. "It's 2008. Another TEAM tournament is upon us. But this year there is a mystery entrant. And that mystery entrant... Is 'Evil' James ****ing von Doom." A smile comes across my face as I utter these words to my friend C.G. Murder who is sitting with me at the murdering salon. I am starting on my Twisted Flesh World Tour 2008 starting with this match and am ready to unleash some pain on the first dumbass who gets in my way. "Hey Murder," I tell my buddy. "I'm in this tournament starting this week and I'm the mystery entrant. Nobody had a clue it was me until then." C.G. Murder has a smile on his twisted and evil face as he takes a sip of his Bloody Mary. "Yes, man, yes," he replies with his Spanish accent. "I want you to, uh, kill him. I want to see his blood dripping from the ring like syrup off of a pancake." My mouth is agape with sheer shock of the image that popped in my head after that but then I suddenly smile. It's one of those sudden things that you can't control. "Yes," I reply with a devious grin. "I'm facing some ass ramming ****face named Ace Mason. This guy has no ****ing clue what he has gotten himself into. I am the most psychotic and insane wrestler of all time and this guy will not know what hit him when he shows up for the tournament. He's going to turn on the television set and be like 'what the **** did I get into?' Seriously, he should go back to his day job licking women's feet for porno magazines." Murder gets a chuckle over this. He seems really amused by this. "I know all about him too," I continue to say. "I know that he lost to tournament semifinalist Jason Payne last year. I wonder if that will effect his fragile psyche in any way leading into this match. I also know that your teacher called you a bad boy, that he went to a Diamondbacks and White Sox game last year just to beat up some drunk guy, and I know that you had unprotected sex in Memphis with some girl named Amy. Then he preceded to jump off of a cliff and lose. Well, I'll make him jump off of a cliff alright. The moment he steps foot into the ring with me he will want to jump off of a cliff in fear. I'm not one of these people whose going to give him any easy win like he had last year. I may not be a number one seed but I've got as reputation as being a beast in the ring and I'm going to show him that. He hasn't done much since that day. In that same time frame, I've won three championshipa and started the revolution of professional wrestling in GCW's Church of the Unholy, the only satanic stable to have their own Myspace page in wrestling history. Ace may have beaten such 'formidable' adversaries as Lumberjack O?Malley, Doc Silver, Hoss Garrison, Jimmy Donovan, the Thrillbillies, and your very own tag team partner in the International icons Fusenhoff. But nothing you have done can prepare you for the Hell of facing me in a match." "I have a reputation as being the most bad ass, psychotic, crazy wrestler of all time in both the PTC and the NWC," I say with a smile. "Now that's saying something. So this guy may have gone far last year in the TiT, but I've seen more tits this week than he has in his entire lifetime so I'm confident that I'm going to make him my he-***** man-slave and give the Church of the Unholy the glory that it so desires. This guy has no clue what he is up against. He will soon be fallen to the power of von Doom like many have before him." "This George Mason," Murder says with a sly wit. "You will kill him with a knife in the kitchen." "Dude, his name is Ace Mason," I reply with a grin. "Like I give a **** what his name is," Murder replies. "There's no way you're going to lose to this guy. You will gut him and then drain his blood like an unholy vampire that was sent from the depths of Hell. You will gut him like the lamb sent to the slaughter that he is. Blood will fall and it will be his." "Exactly what I was thinking!" I exclaim with a surprised look on my face. "It's almost like you read my mind! The crimson red will be all over the ring during our match. I will make this guy my he-***** man-slave and wrestle him to the point where he goes home and cries because Evil James kicked his ass all over London. And also made him bleed until he died." Murder has a sick smile on his twisted face now. He almost seems to take sick pleasure in the thought of death and murdering. He is almost sociopathic in his thinking in that regard. Murder has always been that way since I've known him. "I want you do do this for the Church of the Unholy," he says to me with a tinge of evilness in his voice. "I want to see some blood spilled. Belial told me to tell you this. He has been very displeased with our lack of bloodshed over the last few weeks." This makes me get a strange look on my face. I can't believe what I'm hearing from my number two bro. He's telling me that we haven't been spilling enough blood over the past few weeks. Has he lost his mind? Of course, knowing me, I'm going to just tell it like it is no matter who I'm talking to, ally or enemy. So that's when I go "Evil James" on him. "What the **** is this bull**** you're talking about?" I ask with a hint of anger in my voice. "I mean, we've killed like fifteen people over that time frame. That is some stupid bull**** statement made that makes no sense whatsoever. And why the hell are you believing that? It makes you sound like a ass ****ing twat just for saying it to me." Murder doesn't even bat an eye as I say this. He simply finishes off his Bloody Mary and smiles at me. This surprises me. "You are right," he replies in a surprisingly normal tone. "That was a stupid thing to say. We're in a murdering salon. So let us murder some stupid people. That son of a ***** will have it coming. Yes, man, yes. We will, uh, kill people tonight." Murder reaches into his jacket and pulls out his hunting knife. He begins staring at it and eyes it like a woman he wants to ****. Murder looks around and sees three prime targets for murdering. He turns back to me and smiles. "It's murdering time!" he exclaims with a sickening smile that comes across his face. All of a sudden he turns deadly serious and gets up out of his chair. I watch him quietly go over to the door and turn off the lights. The murdering salon turns into chaos and then I hear screams. That's when I get up and spring into action by listening to it all go down and staying in one spot before he kills me. The lights then come back on and Murder is back standing by the door covered in blood. He smiles at me and simply says, "I told you we would paint the down red."
