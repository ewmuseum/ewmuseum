TRUTH: Vol. 1

27 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27 Feb 06 The long road that we all know as LIFE is full of unexpected bumps, turns, curves, and potholes. We don’t really understand exactly where this road is going to take us, sometimes it’s not really that important. There are no right and wrong turns really, because in some strange way I think we will end up at that same final destination either way. Actually it isn’t too strange, because everyone knows what that final destination is. Death. So really why would the destination be important? No, the important part is of course how you reach that finality. The journey, if you will. Sometimes you may feel like you have reached the end of that journey only to find another path to go down. Sometimes one of those turns will be around a dark corner which will throw off your plans. Okay maybe I’m beginning to lose you, the point is that regardless of how many turns you may take there is always one that catches you off guard. One that you never saw coming. One that could end your life. On this night I ran right into one of those turns. “So it was you ? you son of a bitch.” My voice wasn’t more than a whisper at this point. My body was beaten and broken. I could feel the blood dripping down my face, taste it as it trickled onto my lips. My eyes burned from the tears that had formed and fallen since it all happened. A mix of blood, sweat, snot, and tears stained my face and my clothes. I had to concentrate hard to breath normally, if I did not I may have stopped breathing entirely. “Yes it has always been me. You were always too self involved to really see beyond your personal pain and anguish. That was something that I have counted on for years.” The voice was so familiar yet at this moment seemed so foreign to me. The person who stood there in front of me wasn’t the person I thought I knew. It wasn’t the friend or the companion I once believed. Now it was a stranger, a thug, an enemy ? a murderer. They say you see things most clear right as you are going to die. If that’s the case then I was simply waiting for death to swoop down and take me away. The surprising thing is, I am not afraid. Life was funny like that sometimes. I have a life that many would envy. A loving fiance?, a great job, money, fame, power ? yet there has always been a piece of me that hasn’t been filled. There is a piece of me that on this night feels I would be better served as a lesson, a myth, a thing of the past. A part of me knows that THIS is the path that I was meant to follow. For years I have used others without remorse, without regret, without pity. Like a parasite I had latched on and drained people of life. No I have not killed them in the literal sense, but I have killed parts of them. Women like Medea Hardy and Eva VanLorne. Men like Loki, Johnny Lexicon, and Alex Grey. They served a purpose and when that purpose was finished I let them go ? violently. I guess you really do begin looking at your life, that journey, when it is about to come to an end. “You’re a coward ? you always have been.” “No Roberto it is not cowardice. It’s intelligence.” There was something about those eyes that stared back at me. Normally those eyes were dark, somewhat empty, sometimes sad. But now? Those eyes seem very alive, happy as if this is what they were meant to see. Suffering and pain, violence and bloodshed. In those eyes it is as if I can see the devil himself. Funny thing is, I have always been the one known to be Satan incarnate. One of those pesky turns in the road I guess. “You will pay for your sins ? I promise you that.” Ok ? Now before I continue any further I guess now would be a good time to tell you what all good stories have. A good beginning. Of course I didn’t just find myself betrayed, battered, and bloody all in one night. No this kind of treachery takes a long time to think up. In this case, years. Never does one expect this kind of thing to happen. Your first instinct, well mine anyways, was to assume I did something wrong. It was my own sins that created this separation. What if I deserved it? Well not only did I find out that it wasn’t my fault, but that there was someone even more heartless than I ever could be. So where to start? 1 November 1998 I expected to cry, any normal person would. Two days earlier I had seen my brother murdered in cold blood. The newspapers said it was just another in a line of random murders, gang related of course. In Detroit the police seemed to blame everything on gangs. I didn’t really blame them, the only thing was when they did this they never would look further into it. Yeah they tell the families what they wish to hear, that they are doing everything and anything possible, that the killers would be found and brought to justice. Fucking cops didn’t know anything about justice. “Roberto ? Are you okay?” Mi Abuela. Such a beautiful woman, strong. Even through a tragedy, which she lost the boy that was like a son to her, she pushed through it. She refused to cry in front of people, putting up a front so that she could always be the one that others came to. I had only met her a year earlier but she became the strongest influence in my life. “I will be Abuela. I miss him.” She walked over to my place on my bed and sat down next to me. I didn’t turn to her, just continued to stare out the window as I had been since I got in that morning. I took in a deep breath to fight back all of the emotions that were being bottled up. Bottling up emotions is the one thing I had always been good at. “Aye mijo me too, me too.” She placed her hand on my shoulder. The only feeling of real comfort that I had in years. It’s amazing what a mother’s touch could do. My eyes closed, allowing her touch to overwhelm me. In that moment I felt completely safe, innocent, like I was a child once again. Sadly much like the rest of my life, this amount of happiness only lasted a few moments. Her hand lifted and my eyes opened. That hatred, that fire, and that loneliness found its way back. To most people the sun was shining brightly that early Fall morning. Yet through my eyes that sun never came up from beyond the horizon. Would it ever? Well that one was up for debate. “Grandma ? I Should have saved him.” I felt my hands begin to tremble. “I was right there but didn’t do anything. I could have walked in there and done som?” “No don’t do that Roberto. All that would have happened is that I would be going to both of your funerals. I know you loved your brother. You are not the police.” “You’re right about that.” I finally turn to her, tears finally forming in my eyes. “Because I did something about it.” Her face lost all emotion and her eyes begged me for an explanation. An explanation that I never cared to give. “What did you do Roberto?” Silence ??Qu? hizo usted!”” Silence ??Qu? hizo usted!”” “I made them pay Abuela. I took their lives the same way that they took Damen’s. I ripped their damn hearts out.” With almost cat-like reflexes she slapped me, whipping my head sideways. The pain didn’t come right away. A few moments later the sting lingered. I swallowed hard before turning and looking her in the eye once again. “You aren’t God Roberto ? how dare you try to play his role in life?” For reasons that I do not understand and more than likely never will, I laughed. Not a simple chuckle, grin or smirk but instead a full blown laugh. I laughed so long and loud that eventually my cheeks begin to hurt and my stomach ached. Her eyes became confused a lot like the way one would look at someone that they think is crazy. But crazy I was not. As a matter of fact things had begun becoming completely understandable and sane. Eventually I calmed down, took a deep breath, cocked my head to the side slightly and spoke. “God Abuela” Don’t lecture me about God. God?” “Do you believe in God mijo?” “I believe ?” I turned away once again. “And I hate him.” She knew better than to ask questions. Instead she left me there alone, staring out of that window. I felt the tears falling down my face, warm like blood. It seemed that happiness was always one step ahead of me. First my wife and child in Spain, now my brother in America. I had no other choice but to hate God and all he stood for. If he was all powerful, good, and allowed his son die for our sins ? why would he let innocent people suffer? I never did anything wrong yet he always filled my life with heartache. So what else could I have done? I couldn’t stay I didn’t want to go But I did. For One long year. 28 December 2005 It was a cold night and I really didn’t want to be out. I had far better things I could have been doing with my time. GCW had recently gone on a bit of a hiatus, giving me more free time to spend with Mary. It was the one thing that had kept me grounded, even if she was a bit crazy herself. Perhaps two wrongs do make a right sometimes. I chuckled at this thought as I drove through the empty streets. I hadn’t been back to Toledo since the day I ran away after my brother was murdered and I took my revenge. Tonight it was important though. Up until this particular night I had always believed I got justice for my brother, killing his murderers and burying their sins. On this particular night, I’d find out that things are never as they appear to be. I pulled into an apartment complex, Pine Place Apartments to be exact. They were a shit hole that is for damn sure. Which meant that of course this would be the place I’d meet my new informant. Well I guess informant isn’t the right word, this man had plenty of credentials to back up his work. Most importantly he had worked the streets of Detroit for quite sometime. It didn’t take long searching a parking lot at 3 AM to find the one running car of the bunch. I parked next to his Black Camaro, turned my ignition off and stepped out. The cold air hit my face instantly knocking any bit of sleep I may have still had right out. The Camaro turned off and out came the man of the hour. He stood about 4 or 5 inches shorter than me, very stocky, muscular. Chin length brown hair, brown eyes, cloaked in black. Seemed to be the new trend. As he approached he extended his hand for a handshake. “Mr. Knight it’s a pleasure.” “Save the formalities for now Mr. Devereaux. I don’t care to hear them. Do you have anything for me?” He didn’t seem to be too bothered by this lack of respect or better put blatant disrespect. I liked that actually, showed he had tough skin. “Of course but I feel we had better go inside.” “Forget it. I am not leaving this car out here alone. I lived in these parts for a year of my life, I know what happens.” I nodded at my Lamborghini “Come inside.” We moved inside the vehicle. “Alright here’s the deal Mr. Knight. I’ve learned that the man who actually shot you?” “Victor Monterro.” “Yes ? how did you know that?” “I do not pay you to ask questions.” “Very well.” He said after a beat. “Victor over saw a lot of drug and weapons shipments. The police always had their suspicions on him but couldn’t find the right evidence to put him away. Surprise, surprise. Not only that but Victor was more or less the dictator of the Detroit inner-city gangs.” “Yeah Victor played like some sort of GOD amongst these thugs. He sold and distributed the largest amounts of drugs the city had ever seen. Give me information that I don’t know.” “Well the thing the police never understood is how he became such a driving force so quickly. It was almost as if he fell from the sky with money, drugs, and weapons.” “There could be hundreds of reasons.” “Yeah but if what I have learned is correct ? it’s because he wasn’t the real source of it all.” My eyes darted up and met his. I could feel the emotion in my face draining. “What?” “He was merely a pawn in it all. Victor never sold anything or killed anybody without this overlord’s say so.” “He never killed anybody?” “Not without this man’s consent.” “Is it possible that he could have?” “Well ?” His voice drifted off. “Tell me Mr. Devereaux.” “Victor was killed just days after your brother’s murder. He was burned alive, among other things. So perhaps he was punished for his actions.” I had to fight a smile at this point. In that regard I knew the truth. Somebody did punish Victor, but it wasn’t his “master”. Which brought me to my next point. “So who is this overlord?” “I am still working on that Mr. Knight, but soon I will have the answers you seek.” “You had better Dominic. If not you and your brother can kiss your chance in the wrestling business goodbye.” I turned my head and looked out my door window. “Now go. I’ll call you in a few days for a progress report. I have to know the truth.” The truth. Sometimes that was a hard thing to dig up. A lot of times it was a painful thing to dig up. In this case, as I would find out later it was both. And so much more. If only I had seen it all coming sooner.
