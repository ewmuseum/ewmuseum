The Symphony plays on.......
Tobias Fang
Global Championship Wrestling Roleplays #17
Date: 1/8/03
Location: Chicago, Illinois

Several weeks ago.....


For about 4 years now, ever since late 1998, wrestling had been the biggest part of Tobias Fang's life. Sure, he had a deal of experience in indy feds before then, but at that point it was more of a pastime. When he joined the TWO, it became a career. Ever since he won that World Title in his debut match, he ate, drank, breathed, slept wrestling. Whether he was quarreling with The Almighty One, having chance encounters with the Spectre of Death, or fighting of satanic lunatics, he didn't care. It was truly his calling. His passion. He really loved what he did. Until one day, early last year when he just............stopped caring. No one's quite sure if he became disgusted with the industry, got tired of wrestling, left for personal reasons or whatever. And truthfully neither is he. At the very least he knew he needed a break. A very LONG break.....

Fate finds Tobias Fang on a clear, Chicago evening. The bright city lights make it nigh impossible to see the stars, but the moon was out in full, which always put a smile on his face when he took to the rooftops to relax and let thoughts run in and out of his head. At this moment, however, he sat inside a run down brick building on the South side of the city with a blue, neon sign flickering in a window completely shrouded in black drapery so no light could escape inside. The sign read three simple letters: S-O-D. Inside the building, the appearance was just as decrepped. It was a simple office, one that looked like it had been there since the twenties. However, Tobias seems to have made himself quite at home in the place. A drum set and multiple guitars sat in one corner of the room, excersize equipment and training gear in another, a large entertainment center full of games and movies covered one wall, a makeshift armory full of guns, swords, and other handy utensils of chaos as on the opposite, and various personal possessions were littered about the place as well. And at a desk in the corner sat Tobias Fang, asleep in an office chair with his feet up, clad in attire that one would consider typical of him in a casual state. Jeans, boots, some sort of band t-shirt.......all black. His deep blue hair was tied back into a pony tail and the sunglasses he more often than not wore were sitting on the desk next to a telephone which all the sudden rang obnoxiously, jolting Tobias from his slumber. His pitch black eyes which were dotted by glowing blue irises fluttered open and he promptly let out a groan. Yawning, he reached over, grabbing the black phone, and placed it up to his ear.

'Symphony of Destruction................yeah...........uh-huh.........'

His eyes, which were sleepily half closed, suddenly shot open.

'How much?!'

He then grinned, enlarged canine teeth apparent.

'Yeah, sure.....'

He then swiveled in his chair, putting his feet down, and opened up a drawer. He removed a notebook from it and grabbed a pen, and pushed it closed. He scribbled something on it.

'Yeah, I got it. I'll get the job done before midnight.'

He hung up the phone and jumped out of his seat, nearly skipping over to his makeshift armory. There he grabbed a couple hand guns and tucked them under his belt, and grabbed a giant, black, sawed off shotgun, giving it a kiss. He then walked towards his door, grabbing a black, leather trench coat off a coathook right next to it. He put it on, and almost walked out, holding the shotgun in his hand, but stopped suddenly and walked back to the desk, grabbing his sunglasses. As he again, headed for the door, the phone began ringing. He looked back at it, deciding whether or not to pick it up. He figured he'd let the machine get it.

'A bounty hunter's work is never done.....'

He walked out the door and closed it behind him. Eventually, hisanswering machine beeped, and a voice spoke through it.

'Hello, Tobias. You don't know me, but I know you. I know about Kippo. I know about your mother and father. I know that you still like watching Cartoon Network all day......'

At this point, having overheard the message as he was leaving, Tobias burst back in the door and darted over to the phone, snatching it up.

'Who is this?'

'...........And I also know that you're not doing what you really should be doing.......You can call me Pete.'

'Ok.......what the hell are you talking about, Pete?'

'I'm talking about the fact that you are a very, very talented wrestler, and your abilities are being wasted as you spend your life hunting lowlifes and murders for money.'

A look of something like disappointment came across Tobias's face.

'Is that what this is about, you're a wrestling promoter?'

'Vice-president, to be exact.'

Tobias quirked a brow. He didn't guess it was very often that the front office of a big federation called somebody personally to recruit them. It was probably some ass-poor indy fed looking to boost their ratings by hiring people major feds didn't want anymore. Or people that didn't want to have anything to do with major feds, in his case.

'OK, I'll play your game, what's the name of your fed?'

Pete gave a sort of small chuckle, sensing the condescending tone in his voice.

'I think you've heard of it. GCW.'

This came as a bit of a shock to Tobias. This was the fed he'd walked out on all those months ago because he was so sick of wrestling, and their VP was personally calling him to get him back.

'You're kidding, right?'

'No, I'm not, Tobias.'

'What's the catch?'

'No catch. We're just looking for wrestlers who know what they're doing for our re-launch.'

'Ok then, Pete.....what makes you think that I even want to come back?'

'Steady job, no shortage of low lives to pummel, lots and lots of zeroes...'

'Anything else?'

'Let's face it, Tobias, the number of bounties in the windy city has been dwindling ever since you got on the job. You have a major bloodlust that this occupation can't satisfy anymore.'

Pete had just made Tobias realize something he knew was true. He was becoming bored. And there had always been a nagging part of him that missed his old job.

'.....'

'How about making it more of a part time job and coming back to wrestling?'

'Well.........I have had a nice, long break........'

'Indeed you have. I think your fans will be delighted to see you again.'

Tobias nodded, even though such a thing went unseen over the phone.

'Ok, Pete, you've got a deal.'

'I thought so.'

It seemed as though Tobias would be making an unexpected trip to Columbus to finalize his long awaited return. But as he hung up with Pete, there was still something left to be attended to before he left. Tobias walked back towards the door, a grin of self satisfaction on his face. However, when he reached for the knob, the door slowly creaked open, and on the other side was The Harbinger of The Apokalypse himself, The Grim Reaper. His massive arms folded, he looked down at Tobias, who's grin grew even larger upon the site of him.

'Greetings, Fanged One.'

Only 4 words escaped the mouth of GCW's newest superstar.

'Well I'll be damned.....'

The Reaper nodded his head subtly.

'Indeed you shall be. Much time hath passed since our last encounter.'

'Too much time, Grimmy, too much time.'

Tobias very happily reached up and patted The Reaper on him massive shoulder, to which he received only a confused glare.

'So what brings you to Chicago, Big Man?'

'I come to this city of wind not as the bringer of doom to humanity.....but as a messenger.'

Tobias was intrigued by this. There was only one person with kind of influence over his massive friend.

'Yes, continue......'

'I deliver to thee a small, flattened, rectangular scroll of business.'

The reaper then produced a business card and held it out, Tobias took it and looked at it. The card was black, and written in purple in the middle was 'James Kippo, Almighty Industries'. Tobias snorted.

'You're kidding....'

'Thou hath known The Reaper long enough to judge for thyself whether or not I am 'kidding you', Fang.'

'Kippo sent you here to recruit me?'

'The powers that be wish your presence once again be felt with The Four Almighty Horsemen.'

Tobias gave a confused look to the giant.

'How did you even know I was coming back to GCW?'

'The Spectre Of Death hath known thou long enough to judge for himself when the Symphony of Destruction would play once again.'

'Touché...'

Tobias let this idea run around in his head for a while as The Reaper glared down at him, waiting, statuelike. It'd all be worth it if he could be a pain in Kippo's Almighty ass from so close.....but he figured that maybe, just maybe Kippo had finally grown to respect Tobias and knew he'd make a better ally than an enemy.....he did send The Reaper all the way here from Philly or wherever the hell he lives to enlist his services......maybe Kippo was serious this time.....maybe he'd finally grown up..........and after al, Tobias was in a good mood.

'Alright, Grimmy, I'll do it.'

'Indeed you shall. Come, we must make haste.'

Tobias, now smiling more than ever, walked over to the giant and reached up, wrapping on of his arms around The Reaper's still massive shoulders. The Grim One could only guess at the nature of this as Tobias lead him towards the door.

'Say Grimmy, how would you like to go murder some scumbags before we leave town?'

'Finally, thou hath learned to play The Reaper's game..........'

The Grim Reaper's mouth ever so slightly titled upwards as the two of them disappeared into the night.
