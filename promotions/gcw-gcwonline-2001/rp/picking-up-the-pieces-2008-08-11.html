Picking Up The Pieces

11 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Written August 11th, 2008 One Week Ago Warpath "...Rebecca?" Six years of hopelessness and heartache flashed before my eyes. I could feel a burning sensation in my chest. It stung so much so that I thought I may have been having a heart attack. "That.. it can't.." The words just wouldn't escape through my lips. "Hello Chris," she said softly. Her voice seemed familiar yet unfamiliar at the same time. I remembered it, but it became skewed from only hearing it in my dreams over the last six years. "It's been a long time." I couldn't help but let a tear slip out from behind my eye. It rolled down my cheek, glistening in the light. I knew she saw it. She watched her reflection in it as it rolled down my face and dripped off my chin. I gave her a nod and tried to force a smile. My body language was very inviting, prompting her to come inside. I knew it was the wrong idea, as Jane was only in the other room. Yet I couldn't turn away the only woman I'd ever truly loved. "This can't be happening," I said in almost a whisper. She brushed her hand across the side of my face. "It is, Chris." "But how," I asked. It made no sense. She was dead. I'd gone to her funeral. I'd visited her tombstone for the last six years. "It's such a long story, Chris." I knew what she was insinuating. This wasn't the time nor the place. And as much as I'd have loved to hear it all, from top to bottom, I knew that I was better off waiting. Afterall, it was only a matter of minutes before Jane would walk out here, unclothed, looking for me. "Chrrrisss," her voice shouted from the other room. "What's taking so long?" The look on Rebecca's face was just as I'd have expected. She was genuinely upset to hear another girl's voice. We'd been soulmates and she was sitting there, staring at her soulmate getting ready to have sex with a stranger. "I guess this isn't a good time," she said with a sulk in her voice. "I'd better go." She took a step backwards, away from the doorway. I reached for her, rubbing my fingertips gently down her forearm and along the top of her hand. "Is there any way that I can reach you," I asked her sympathetically.. "No, Chris." Her words peirced my heart. "I'll find you. It isn't safe yet..." Another tear dribbled down my cheek as I tried to force a smile. Rebecca leaned in and kissed my cheek -- my tear. And it was then that I watched her walk away, never knowing if I'd see her again. -- -------- Flashback. Six Years Ago August 6th, 2002 Odessa, Texas The picture opens in the warm, beautiful city of Odessa in Ector County, Texas. Two beautiful young girls are sitting close together on a nearby park bench. Their hands are intertwined and their eyes locked. Each are clothed in vibrant colored sun dresses, wearing glasses that take up nearly half their face. "So this is our new home," asks a familiar face. Rebecca glances around the park, watching children playing frisbee and a few couples walking their dogs. "Yeah," Abigail replies. "This'll have to make due." "It's very pretty," Rebecca says, trying to reassure herself. "It's something I can definitely get used to." Abigail brushes her hand through Rebecca's hair, sensing Rebecca's wandering mind. "It'll all be for the best, Bek. Before you know it, we'll be able to get back to our normal lives." "I just..." Rebecca searches for the right words. ".. I don't understand why it had to happen like this." "Your family's political influence made them a target. And with all the crime that began resurfacing in Salem, it was only a matter of time before they came after you too." Shaking her head, Rebecca replies, "I still don't get why we couldn't take Chris with us. Or at least let him know that I'm okay." Abigail sympathizes, "I understand, Bek, but its just too much of a chance. Everyone knows Chris. Someone will recognize his face and that can ultimately lead back to us. It's just better this way. I know it doesn't make sense now but it will in the future." Abigail squeezes Rebecca's hand. "I promise!" Rebecca smiles at Abigail. Deep down, she knows that Abigail is the only one she can trust and although she feels lost, she also feels certain. Certain that she'll find her way. ---------- Present Day August 7th, 2008 Union, New Jersey "I can't get over it, Timmy. She was right there in front of me..." It'd been the first time I'd spoken of the account with anybody. It became extremely hard for me to bring up around any of the new guys like Dan, Axel, or Kincaid, because they all saw me in a different light. To them, I was a cold-hearted, ruthless prick who really only cared about himself. And although that was partly true, I did have a soft side. But a soft side in this industry can really get you buried. It can tear you down. Look at Andy Murray, for christ's sake. He exposed a soft-side and I capitalized on it by bringing his ex-wife into the picture. I'll give the fellow some props, however, as he still managed to pull through what was no doubt the toughest match in my career -- and I'd hope his, as well. Nevertheless, I was out of options -- out of friends. Timmy was the only guy left that I could really, truly talk to. As much as he was a jokester and a party animal, he would always be willing to lend a listening ear. "I don't know why you didn't just tell Jane to fuck herself and sweep Rebecca right off her feet, Chris." Timmy always had a way with words, too. Everything was so easy to him. I had to admire it at times. His decisions were made on a whim. No thinking twice. No reprocusions. "It's not like it was that easy, Timmy. I haven't seen this girl in SIX YEARS and she suddenly shows up at my hotel room door? Like what am I supposed to say!?" I felt as if I was making excuses for why I didn't snatch Rebecca up in my arms and hold her tightly. And I guess I was. It was just.. I don't know. It was just so hard to comprehend. "I don't get what was so difficult about it, Chris. This is the girl you've been infatuated with. The girl you've lost sleep over. You've DREAMED about seeing her again and you finally did. And you gave it up because of some two bit whore in your bedroom." And Timmy was right. I never really loved Jane. I wasn't in love with her. She was just there to fill a void. Ahhh, who am I kidding? She was there to try and psych Andy Murray out. But instead, she psyched me out. I guess karma is most definitely a sonuvabitch. "I don't know, Timmy. I haven't seen the girl in six God damn years. For all I know, its some little tramp that Andy paid to come and try to mess with my head. I did it to him so why wouldn't he do it back?" I'd venture to say that I was in denial. I wouldn't admit to fucking up. There's my ego hard at work. "Are you serious, Chris? Listen to yourself!! There isn't a remote possibility that Andy Murray would have ever known about Rebecca unless he'd done some serious research into your distant past. He had to dig deep, Chris. This isn't the kind of stuff you stumble upon on GCWOnline.net. It isn't in some Wiki. This is in your memories. SHE is in your memories!" He had such a valid point. But I couldn't figure out why the hell he'd be pushing so hard for me to believe that I had really encountered Rebecca. He didn't see her. It's not like he'd known she was alive, let alone there. "Yeah, but..." I was practically speechless. I had so many random thoughts racing through my mind. And that exact same one continuously reoccurred. Why was Timmy being so persistant in me believing? "How would she have found my room, Timmy? How'd she know where I was staying? I mean, I was in a completely different country for God's sake!" There was a silence on both ends. I felt as though Timmy was diving deep to try and find the perfect answer. But I really didn't think he could. I had so many unanswered questions and he was the only key to help me try and solve the puzzle. "I'm not really sure, Chris. She must've been watching you or following you for some time," he said, making a very valid point. Yet I still had to play the Devil's advocate. "But if she'd been following me, wouldn't she have already known about Jane? The look on her face when she heard Jane's voice was nowhere near what a person should look like when they KNOW about their former lover having sex with someone new. Rebecca had no idea. I feel like somehow, someone must've told her..." It was the only answer I could've come up with. Someone had to have known Rebecca was alive and had to have given her my exact location for her to show up there. But the question was who? "Ahh, Chris. Enough of this." Timmy was obviously getting drained out by the topic. It had always done the same to me, too. I've been infatuated with Rebecca and the fact that I let her slip away only strengthened my infatuation. "You let this harp on your mind a week ago, going into a steel chamber match with Andy Murray. Look at the outcome that had. You lost a lot of blood and you battered the living hell out of your body. And you still came up short." It was the last thing I wanted to be reminded of. Another loss to Andy Murray. I'd been on such a monstruous streak, toppling over the GCW greats of yesterday and today. Only to see myself fall to Murray, of all people. Every antic I pulled. Every cheap shot I hit. None of it worked. "Clyde Fox is no Andy Murray, Timmy." I had to rebutt. "No," he said. "He isn't. So how do you think that would look if you took a second straight loss to a half-retarded kid who's got an obsession with magical unicorns? You need to get your head back in the game. You need to focus." It was so the truth. I really did need to focus and especially right now. I just boarded the train to the biggest match of the year in Dangerous Games and my car is all in shambles. The seats are torn and the lights are dim. I desperately need to get back to the Chris Bagwell that I once was. I needed to feel the spotlight beaming down across my chest. I needed to read my names across each and every headline. I needed to steal the show week in and week out. I needed to feed my hunger and taste the taste of gold. Just one more time. "Timmy..." Most importantly, "I need to start picking up the pieces." "The pieces of what, Chris?" "..my life."
