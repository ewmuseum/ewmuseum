Date: 2 June 05 I could feel her eyes burning into me as I stood by the window staring out at the horizon just as the sun began poking up. We had just finished making love ? well actually I couldn’t consider what we just did as making love, what we just performed was out of lust. Sex. Fucking. Sweat dripped off my face and chest as if I had just gotten out of a hot shower and for good reason. I enjoyed our activities that was for certain but even though she loved every moment of it Mary knew something wasn’t right. She moaned, clawed and squeezed like she had never done before ? but the look in her eyes afterwards was one of confusion. The questions in her heart were more or less the same questions that I had been flooded with for the past few days. Mary had seen people in her life change and turn their backs on her for no good reason, now she was worried I’d do the same. I knew the questioning would happen she would question my love for her and she’d ask if I was going to leave her broken the way Damien did. Of course the most common question is “WHY?”, this was a question that I really did not wish to hear or read anymore but at the same time if there is anyone I owe an explanation to it’d be Mary. “Mary?” I whispered. “You’re awake, yes?” I heard the sheets shuffle a bit as Mary maneuvered around on the bed, probably to a sitting position. “Si Statty.” Her voice was low but it carried to my ears. The sweet sound of that voice put a small smile on my face, a smile that only came to this cold heart when she was around. I didn’t turn to face her though, I didn’t feel as if I had to. “I know whats on your mind Hermosa ? just ask your questions.” She was silent for awhile I didn’t hear the sheets and I had to listen very closely to even hear her breathing. After sometime I heard Mary sigh and then the sound of her stepping off of the bed. Soon her small hands had wrapped around my waist and I felt her head lean up against my back. Slowly my hands made their way down and gripped hers into them then I turned to face her. Mary’s raven curls were messy and small beads of sweat dripped from her forehead and shoulders. Besides a pair of black stockings Mary was free of clothing. I pulled her close pressing her skin up against mine. She looked up at me with those brown eyes, full of wonderment. Those eyes looked into mine as if she were looking into my soul figuring out if I was still the man she fell in love with. Normally I would wonder the same thing because I have tried so long and so hard to keep that dark side of me chained down. Every time I have lost my grip on this I have hurt someone I love be it Wylie, RaZiel, Cindy, or Starr. I was lost in my own darkness that I could not see what I was doing until it was too late, blinded by lust, carnage, or success. Things are different this time though, this time I purposely allowed my aggression out. This was a calculated move ? this time I would control it. “Yous still wit me Knightie?” “Always senorita ? always.” “I hopes so, I saw dat show of yours Statty and you scared Mary somethin” awefuls.” “How love?” “Your eyes Statty, Mary seen that look before.” I knew what was coming because I witnessed the events she was probably right now revisiting in her head. I also knew that there was nothing I could say or do at this very moment to help her keep her cool and believe that I was not turning on her. I wish there was something I could do but nothing short of playing things low key would gain her full confidence and at this point I could not back off. Events had been put into motion that I refused to stop because its damn time that my legacy in GCW and wrestling in general be cemented. “I know you’re scared Mary but you must trust me on this. I am not leaving you and I never will. No matter what happens in that ring or on the shows when I am with you things are different. Everything about you is perfect Mary, perfect for me that is. When I am with you everything else plays second fiddle.” She smiled a bit but it was a very quick smile. “Mary wants to believe yous Knightie, she wants to real bad but ?” She swallowed hard. “After all things that happen to Mary, she’s always scared.” “I am the same person I have always been. What I did at War Path isn’t a sign of me changing to someone that doesn’t care about anything, it was merely me coming to grips with who I really am. I have tried appealing to those fans and those bosses of mine but it never got me what I really needed. I tried fooling myself that I could be a stand up guy Mary ? those fans never fully accepted me and they never would. The media and the fans love to build someone up but more than that they love to rip them up when they get tired of them. I was not going to be another one of those Mary, I COULDN’T. I took matters into my own hands ? I am sure you can understand that.” “Knightie its not just in GC-dub.” “What are you talking about Mary?” “Tonight ? Mary loooooooves the freaky-nasty stuff don’t get her wrong but didn’t seem like yourself. Like you were somewhere else.” I bit my lower lip and dropped my head a bit now. I had no real answer for that because even as things were happening I knew that it was different. While we had sex I had so much more fire inside of me, with each thrust, each kiss, each scrape of finger nail that fire would grow that much stronger. “Mary?” “Just tells Mary if you love her.” My head snapped up now and my eyes found hers instantly. I couldn’t believe she’d ask me such a question, it kind of hurt inside knowing she questioned it. “Te quiero mucho Mary ? you know that. Listen Mary I am going to be here for you no matter what. Jason Dread, those fans, Steven Caldera and the rest of GCW mean absolutely nothing to me and never have. I will use them, take what I want and then leave them with nothing but shattered dreams and ambitions. I love you Mary ? I know where to draw the line in the sand. You are the only person that has a place in my heart anymore Ms. Mary. Now with that being said ? I am pushing on. I am going to place a black cloud over GCW. If you do not wish to see that side of me then don’t watch Mary. If you don’t wish to see the things that I will do to Jason Dread then turn your head and love me for who I am when I am with you.” Mary reached up with both of her hands and took my face into those small hands of hers and looked into my eyes. “Do whats you needs to love ? Mary be here no matter what.” I smiled again and leaned down and pressed my lips against hers, softly at first but quickly the kiss became more passionate. My hands lowered to her waist and then I muscled her body up, off reflex her strong legs wrapped around my body. She hungrily kissed back and allowed my hands to roam around her body. Quickly I spun around and pinned her against the wall behind me right next to the window I was once staring out of. Grabbing her by the wrists I put her arms above her head and pinned them to wall and pulled my head back a bit. Our eyes caught each other briefly before hers closed and she let out a low moan as I found my way inside of her. I looked back out that window again ? the sun had completely risen from beyond that horizon again. I had seen the sunrise from this window for years but for some reason, some reason unknown to me it looked so much more incredible this morning. A new day has arrived.
