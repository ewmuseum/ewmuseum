Date: August 5, 2006 “You can never go home again.” My voice carried through the empty arena, subtly ricocheting through the halls and empty seats, drifting to silence with each recurring echo. It played for the custodial skeleton crew, leisurely but effectively ridding the Pacific Coliseum of the remnants of WorldWide Fifty to prepare the now hollowed dome for the next big event! It was to be a momentous occasion, not just as Global Championship Wrestling’s rebound after Warpath, not just their single step back to a federation worth being feared, but it was supposed to silence the critic’s twisted view of a federation that had all but slumped into a downward spiral since the beginning of the year. Mission accomplished. If only it were so easy for me. In the hours after the conclusion of WorldWide, I found myself hunched in the nosebleed section overlooking the partly dismantled ring. I tried, with the desperation of a wounded animal, to let my frustrations trickle from my mind the way I let beads of sweat dribble from my golden tresses. But, after what seemed like hours of licking the still fresh wounds following my victory over Marvin Harris, the sweat had dried to white blotches of salt on the corners of my mask and tights, while the dissatisfaction in a match that was supposed set things straight and reassure me of my path? still left a bitter taste on the tip of my tongue. Wrestling was far from the honor filled arenas that I had left. It was far from the handshakes and pride filled battles that I had come to appreciate. It was a shell of what I had come to expect in a sport that I had been raised to be a part of. There was so much bloodlust in the fan’s shrill cheers, so much selfishness in my opponent’s eyes, and so much drama in an industry that, years ago, had seemed so simple. In a phrase? Marvin was unworthy! It was a long road through the competition and I had already begun to doubt the validity of my fight. The blood thirsty voices of revenge that lingered in my mind still remained; pleading with the gods to strike me down for my past transactions. Begging a worthy soul to step up and thrust unto me a pain that would ease their suffering and seal my disgrace. In a match that was supposed to quell their thirst and soften their hate filled banters? ? I had only strengthened their resolve. The voices were louder than ever and pushing me to a point of a mental implosion. Thousands of individual fan’s screams of terror, hundreds of Luchador’s silent stares of condemnation, dozens of family member’s snide threats for a family’s fallen name, and one brother’s silenced destiny. They pushed down on my shoulders with the weight of the world; assembling strength with each new thought of self-doubt that plagued my mind. “AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGG” My howls poured through the vacant arena, catching the attention of the crewmen below me as the pain became unbearable. I closed my eyes, summoning the strength to restrain the voices that pierced my soul, but felt myself wavering under the force. Finally, when I could stand it no more, I yanked the mask from my face, staring with glossy eyes at a piece of cloth that had become my own personal gauntlet of despair. I stared at it as the whispers of dishonor seemed to emanate from it. Slowly I covered it in a silk handkerchief, taking a second to glance at my brother’s picture before carefully placing it back into my bag. “Here he is. JD, the man with a plan!” The voice caught me off guard as my ears perked to the sound of heavy footsteps making their way from behind me. I turned, greeted to the site of my manager, elatedly staggering toward me with a bottle of champagne and a pack of sub-sandwiches curled in his arm. Brack stumbled toward me with a smile pasted to his sweat covered face; obviously keyed up about my victory over Marvin Harris just a few hours earlier. POP I dodged a cork narrow heading in my direction as Brack took a seat next to me with an exhausted sigh. “I’ve been searching for you for the better half of two hours. But, I’m not complaining. It gave me time to grab some supplies for our celebration.” He boasted, taking a sip from the champagne and shoving it to my chest. “I don’t do champagne, Brack. You know that!” I mumbled, pushing the bottle back in my manager’s direction, “Besides, there is nothing to celebrate, it’s not like I walked out with a title around my waste.” “We are celebrating your victory over Marvin Harris. I don’t think you realize the validity of the man you just defeated! He was somewhat known in these parts.” He glanced at the bottle with a pout, then reached into his pile of wrapped sandwiches and pulled out a chilled Corona, tossing it in my direction. “Harris was a fluke; beat by himself before the sound of the first bell, Brack.” I bit off the bottle cap and took a sip of my Corona letting the liquid linger on my tongue for a moment before swallowing it down. “There is no honor in a victory when your opponent has already conceded defeat before the first punch is thrown!” “Nonsense, Julio! You came out here and put on a show for the people. “Sides, people are talking?” He took a bite of his sub, with a grimace hitting his face as he spit it out remembering to remove the plastic before taking a another, speaking to me with a mouth full of ham and lettuce, “Dey like wat dey see, kid. Dey want mawr! But, ders a coch!” “A couch”!”” “No?” he said in the middle of the most disgusting swallow I had heard to date. ?” A catch!” “What kind of catch?” “GCW” they’re trying to shed some dead weight. A lot of the recently signed talent is not performing to standard. So they have been marked for release. But, not before some of the more established Superstars get their paws on them. Meaning the singles matches are all but booked up for the next WorldWide.” “You mean I don’t have a match?” ??Not true!” he pointed a finger to reinforce his words. “Those broads from Fiery Passion are still in need of some competition.” “A tag team match?” I grumbled, taking another sip and shaking my head in disbelief. “Was my, singles talent not enough to impress the bookers?” “Just the opposite kid. The tag team talent is starting to spark up and the booking agents are looking for people who can carry the torch to their Tag Champions here” hopefully into PTC as well.” He smiled, quite proud of himself. “It will be you and a newcomer Ciel. Both of your improvised styles will be a perfect combination for a tag team.” “Brack, I?” “Imagine it; La Sombra, with, of course, his manager, taking on all challengers, in any form, and any type of match, with any partner. People will flock to you, fans will adore you, superstars with respect you, women will throw thongs to you. It will be like a Wayne Newton concert.” he exclaimed, waving a hand over an imaginary banner on the wall as he tugged me closer to him. “I’ll” we’ll be gods, kid.” “Brack, we are jobbers being thrown to wolfs. They aren’t looking for a tag team in me and Ciel. They are looking for people who will have the least experience with each other that Fiery Passion can take apart one by one. I just think that my talents would be better utilized in any other kind of match.” “Julio, you need to realize that opportunities like this don’t come along very often. You don’t even know if you will be getting a booked match for the entire month. The key to wrestling in the big leagues is getting your name out there, advertising, promoting, selling, letting the fans know that La Sombra is willing to take on all comers, in any type of match-up.” Brack said adjusting himself in his seat. “I mean think about it; a third generation superstar, educated on the traditions of Lucha, technical, and counter wrestling, who steps outside the box to take on all comers in this Tag Team match. Hell, even if you don’t win this is more publicity than we will have had thus far. I mean, you have fought in tag matches in the past, right!” “Yes Brack, and you need to know your partner, at least more than his mere name. It entails trust in his ability and mine to compensate for our combined weaknesses.” I snapped. “Fine.” He said sardonically. Brack fluttered his eyes as he turned his head and added, “I guess we can just sit here, next to the phone, hoping day by day, that the GCW will call to offer you a booked match two months down the road.” In all honesty I really didn’t have a choice. If I wanted to move up the card to face more respectable opponents, I would have to impress the world in any circumstance. They would have to see my willingness to impress in any situation. “Christ, I’ll do it, Brack!” I said, finally caving in. “But, you had better be on your P’s and Q’s here. Cohesion will depend a lot on you making sure that me and my partner can communicate with each other.” “You won’t regret this Julio!” answered my, suddenly excited manager as he took another bite of his sub! “I hope not!” ~~~ “Find it.” The sound peeled me from my slumber pulling on my mind. Each word seemed to cut itself into my heart slowing each beat with a sudden slothfulness. My eyes tried to adjust through a thick fog that seemed to flow from every direction. I sat, leaned up against an unseen wall as a silhouette emerged in front of me. “Find it, Julio.” Slowly screams began to fill the background; so terrifying, so scared, and so unrelenting. My face pulled into a grimace as I struggled to my feet, feeling the awesome weight of disgrace pulling on my legs. I felt as if I had the strength of a child trying to move around in a man’s body. Slowly I staggered, toward the figure, following it through the maze of smoke that seemed to rise from the ground itself. I reached out, trying to grab a hold of the figure for support but fell through and back onto my knees. “You have to find it, Julio.” It persisted. “Have” to find what?” I gasped under gobs of smoke that filled my lungs. To date it was one of the largest Arena’s that I had ever competed in. Yet, from up here it seemed so small, so insignificant, and so fragile. Though I knew that was very far from the truth. Rumors had it that this dome would fit a bigger crowd than the upcoming Annihilation PPV coming in just a few short months and in front of twenty-three thousand plus fans I would have to make my presence known in the GCW. It would be the biggest victory for me or, likewise, the failure that would give credit to the voices that plagued my path. “How do you keep up with it?” I asked, finally breaking the silence and turning to my manager. He was visibly still shaken from my reaction to the car accident and had been all but silent since reaching Washington. Though, the doctors were amazed by the fact that both of us had left the vehicle without a physical injury, Brack was still skeptical and dealt with me delicately. Most people overlooked the fact that although he had a loud mouthed and sometimes boisterous personality, he always had my best interests in mind and cared for me like the son he never had. Since the car wreck, I couldn’t so much as take a leak without him shadowing closely behind me. “Keep up with what, kid?” He asked with eyes still fixated on his view of the Tacoma area. “Wrestling! I imagine it’s changed since your days. All the drama, the wars, the heartache, the cheating” it’s all filler. It surrounds the same simple goals of success. Trust me; you will do great things here, kid! You should already know that. It’s just that?” “What is it, Brack?” “Next time I’ll drive, ok?” “Bite me, Brack!” ~~~ So here it was. Staring me right, smack, dab in the face. Like a pair of fiery eyes that loomed over my shoulder, watching, waiting, and counting the seconds until I cracked wide open. The day had arrived and all my complaining, preparations, and boasting would be put to the test. It had begun again. I could feel my nerves deteriorate, replacing a once calm demeanor, with an anxious quiver that flowed through my hands. The tension had, once again, began to build in the pit of my stomach; twisting and contorting to the, all too familiar, apprehension I had learned to loath. I could feel the adrenalin flowing through every inch of my body; amassing, assembling, and ready to explode. My reflexes were as sharp as they had ever been, my joints were as flexible as I had remembered them to be, and I was faster than even I had remembered. I was a hunter, bounding through the forest with the scent of blood well within reach. This is how it starts. This is how it always started. I could remember the days of old; training until I could no longer move, pushing myself until there was no more to give, and fighting under the certain assumption of defeat. I could remember every instance in which I had been out-weighted, out-wrestled, and out-numbered; yet, somehow, someway, by a pure fortune of fate, walking out the victor. I remembered the want, need, and drive to be superior to the man or woman standing across from me. I remember the narrowed vision, the way the world would begin to revolve around my match, the way everything else, seemed to dim behind the thought of my hand being raised in success. I remembered everything. Yet, my thoughts ran full circle to my colossal failure. I could see myself collapse; overwhelmed by the weight of the family name De Hoyas. It was a name that beckoned me around every corner; adding pounds, upon pounds, of knee buckling weight to my already exhausted shoulders. I remember previously caving in and surrendering under the weight of it all. I remember my farthest relatives mocking my tribulations, picking at the wounds, and laughing at my short comings. I remember being powerless to defend myself. I remember losing control! So we start clean, innovative? anew! I washed my hands of my family’s burden and cleansed myself of their plights. I was a lone man, standing face to face with the darkness, and acknowledging the Sword of Damocles that hung dauntingly over my head. This sword had materialized itself into one team, already disturbed by a defeat and ready to make a stand. The one thing they failed to realize was that regardless of the amount of sheep that flocked to their side, the years of teaming they had under their belt, and the fact that me and Ciel had never teamed before; I had my purpose! I was still the hunter and they were still the prey. “Viva La Lucha Libre”
