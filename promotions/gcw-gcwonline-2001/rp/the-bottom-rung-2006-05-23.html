The Bottom Rung

23 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 23, 2006 The getting there is the hardest part. It's like a 22 hour car drive to your favorite amusement park, or a commercial in the middle of the big game. You just can't wait to get there. But, as is logical, I start out once again at the bottom. The opener. The curtaaaaaain-jerker. I don't take this as an indictment on my abilities as a performer, nor do I take this as a slap in the face. We all have to start somewhere, and I apparently have gone back to square one. It makes me very nostalgic, looking back to the days when I was a fresh-faced young grappler, you know, before the 'y'. Eager and full of cheer, I would go out there and take the beating, then dutifully return to the back. An honest days pay, for an honest days j-o-b. Only this time, I don't plan on counting the lights. See, I really have paid my dues in this business. I went through my rookie hazing, then through my first small push, then my first world title shot. Then came the booze, the coke and the women, and I got clean. We have indeed come full circle folks. This time however, I have the competitive edge. This time, I am fired up. Sure, going through the motions when you're at the top, and making all kinds of money SOUNDS like fun, but when it comes right down to it, all the trips to Mexico, all the tequila, all the naked women just isn't all that great. Ah. Who the hell am I kidding. It's fantastic. And when I get to the top again, i'm sure that's where i'll be. Only this time, i'll make sure I don't puke in Ted's car. He was pissed. Anyways, back on track. I have dealt with opression all my life, people tryin' to hold the white man back, and I have risen above it all. People don't like me, folks. Fans don't cheer me, the suits don't stick up for me, and I have to do it all by myself. Remember how I said I was fired up? That's the key. Intensity. I personally don't give a rat's ass if I go out there and break this guys leg on WorldWide. I really don't care if I kill him. If I have to gnaw his arm off to get to rung #2, I will. And you had better believe that this is a trend that will continue. The typical GCW fan cannot comprehend the level of passion I have for this sport. Of course, what you all couldn't comprehend could fill a book, but that is neither here nor there. It is imperative that you understand how absolutely hellbent I am on climbing that ladder, as quickly as humanly possible. The tick-tock of the clock is looming louder, and a day wasted could set me back for a lifetime. Tick...Tock....
