Blisters

22 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 22, 2005 With just a few days to go until his debut match, Coyle O’Connor understands that fitness just may be the defining key of who wins this match. Day in and day out, he strictly motivates himself to a healthy diet and a great workout plan; it seems as if he is taking this moment in life seriously, something unheard of before. It’s as if he wants to win, as if he wants a name, that way people know who he is. He doesn’t want to be a second class worker anymore, he wants to be Coyle O’Connor, the main event. Not even considering luck as a factor anymore, Coyle stands up straight and confident, ready to take this endeavor on. Whether or not something is wrong with him right now, he still feels capable and able to pull out of this match with a victory; and he knows he will. Coyle looks at this match this way, if he wins he’ll be at a point in life that he never achieved, and that’s a place where he wants to be at. But if he doesn’t win, well, he hasn’t thought about it yet, because he honestly feels like he shouldn’t have to; it won’t happen. It’s all about the urge to get to the top; Coyle O’Connor, GCW World Champion. Today, on his daily routine, Coyle started sprinting down his street. It was a great day, birds were chirping, families were laughing, winds were blowing, and the sun was shining. He was thinking about how important it was to defeat Angel, some newcomer, like him, that has everything in the world to fight for, like him, who needs this, like him. Its undeniable that this was always on his mind because it was something he had to think about, this way he could remain in the zone. He wants to remain in the zone so much, in fact, that he jogs 4 times a day, and workouts twice. He basically hasn’t lived a life for the last week. Today isn’t any different. Already 2 jogs down, and 2 workouts done, he still has more to do. But he won’t be able to finish everything today? “Ahh!” Coyle completely faints to the ground. His hands quickly lock to his right ankle, holding it firmly. “God-damn ankle!” Sadly, a little sprain on the ankle just completely startled him. It isn’t something that will stop him on his warpath, but it is something that will stop him from jogging for the day. He sat up after his little moment, and pulled his sock down a little; and just as he thought, his ankle was swollen. He thought of it as nothing, and ignored it. All he had to do was take his shoes off and let it sit for a while, so he did. After he took his shoe off, he decided to take the rest of the rolled up sock off. As soon as he did, 2 blisters appeared on the back of his ankle. Picture that, a swollen ankle with 2 blisters on back of it, not good. “Great, just what I need, blisters that’ll ache?” As anyone would be, Coyle was pissed. It’s not something so terrible and wretched, but its something that’s annoying and something that might get in the way. So, without contemplation, he did nothing other than popping the blister. “There is no way I’ll lose everything for you two?” Knowing that his ankle was a little sprained, he felt it was a good time to go home and relax. He walked the rest of the way home with his shirt off, while he was drenched in sweat and breathing hard. He had a little bit of a limp to him, and caught himself stopping and grabbing his ankle every once in a while, but as you know, it wasn’t anything serious. When he got home, and plopped down onto one of his kitchen chairs, he sat there and looked around. This match was still on his mind, despite the boulder-for-an-ankle on his leg. It’s something that he could not-not think of no matter what, it was just so damn desirable to him. For instance, he could picture himself hitting Angel hard with the Clover? “He’ll go down with the Clover, it’ll take him right down, no question about it.” Not even the Clover though, he could picture him just taking him on with generic moves? “And he still won’t get up.” It is inevitable that he feels he is going to win this match, no matter what blisters grew, or what ankle is sprained. Besides, to him, it’s the next step in life. “But those damn blisters?”
