Night Terror: Act I

14 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14 Dec 07 I. The Encounter His wide eyes were glued to the small television set that sat atop his wooden dresser. With legs crossed Indian style and his favorite blanket draped over his head like a cloak, Jamison “Jay” Talley remained motionless as his vision was flooded with disturbing image after disturbing image. Like most kids his age, Jay enjoyed watching things that his parents weren’t too thrilled with him watching, in this case a horror movie and to be more specific, Nightmare on Elm Street. The scene on the TV was silent for the most part, the exception being the leaves that were kicked around by Nancy as she wandered around the dream world in search of her burnt adversary. As the tension built, Jay moved closer and closer to the edge of his bed, anxious to find out if Nancy was going to run into the man with the claw or make it out of this sticky situation alive. The young boy’s eyes darted all around the screen to search for anything that would be considered out of place. In the shadows, is that Freddy? Maybe behind that tree? Around the corner? “COME OUT KREUGER!” Nancy screamed out into the night air, her voice probably carried for miles Jay figured. “ARE YOU THERE!”” A low growl of a voice answered. “I’M HERE.” Jay’s entire body twitched as Freddy popped out from behind some bushes directly behind the heroin of the movie. Jay didn’t realize it but he had also let out quite the yelp from being so afraid. He also didn’t realize that he was now halfway onto his bed instead of on the edge because the jolt forced him backwards. Inside of his chest the THUMP, THUMP, THUMP of his heart quickened to coordinate with his breathing pattern. On the screen Freddy was in full pursuit of his next victim, his claw ready to slash and his cackle ready to be unleashed once he achieved his ultimate goal. Nancy side steps a swipe of Freddy’s claw which caused Jay to bob his head as well as if he were in the movie instead. Jay remained fixed on the screen as Nancy runs home, locking the front door behind her. The famous scene where the stairs turn into much is next forcing Nancy to fight for every inch she gets in her struggle to reach her bedroom. Again Jay inched closer to the edge of his bed, prepared to be frightened again. Nancy slams her bedroom door and stares into her mirror. “THIS IS JUST A DREAM, IT ISN’T REAL!” She pleaded to her reflection. “THIS IS JUST A DREAM IT ISN’T” CRAAAASH! Just as the mirror shattered because of Freddy diving through, Jay’s bedroom door flew open revealing the shadowy form of his mother. This time Jay falls off of his bed because of the fear that ran through his body. “OH SH?”.SHOOT mom.” Jay said, catching himself before any further trouble came to him. “Uh huh,” She walked into his room, not moving past the TV. “You know you aren’t suppose to be watching this crap.” “Aww c’mon mom.” His pleas didn’t help him much as Mrs. Talley turns the knob on the TV which caused the screen to go black. From the floor (where he still is laying) Jay peers into his mother’s green eyes (even if was impossible to tell what their color was at that point ? dark and all) to try and gauge her seriousness. “Get up Jay.” She said with a bit of a chuckle. “Time for bed.” Jay rolled onto his bed. “Fine Ma but you should know that you are stunting my maturity.” “By now letting you watch murder on TV?” “Exactly.” She smiled at her son but shook her head at the same time. “Those things will give you nightmares.” She leaned in and gave him a quick kiss on the forehead. “Good night.” “Night Ma.” As soon as his mother closed his bedroom door again he contemplated turning the TV right back on and finishing his movie but the comfort of laying on his back and relaxing made him realize how tired he really was. Laying in bed for a few minutes also made him realize something else ? His heart was still pounding. Naturally his eyes peek around his dark room, checking any shadow for what could be perceived as something dangerous. Without realizing it, Jay pulls his blanket up to his nose so that only the top part of his head is peeking out. This is when all of the typical sounds and sights of the night seem to come to life. The wind sounded like a low growl The shadow made by the tree outside the window looked like a creature The eyes of his action figures all appeared to stare at him In his mind, Jay told himself that he would stay up all night if he had to. No damn monster was going to get him! Of course this plan only lasted five minutes before his eye lids seemed to gain 100 lbs. In no time at all he is comfortably asleep. He didn’t know how long he had been asleep before being awakened by a strange sound coming from inside of his room. Fear and nervousness invaded his body, the result was the tightening of his muscles which made it damn near impossible for Jay to sit up like he wanted to. From his back his eyes scan the shadows of his room looking for where the noise could have originated from. Of course he found nothing so he began to think that maybe he had just imagined it, mama told him he’d have nightmares right? This thought comforted him and allowed his breathing and heartbeat to come back down to normal levels. He smiled when thinking about how foolish it was that a movie had him so freaked out. Then the noise came again. It was a scratching sound, that much was for certain. Not a bunch of scratches like it would be if someone was trapped and trying to escape but more like one long scrape as if someone was purposely messing with him. It was a feeling that Jay didn’t find too comforting. Slowly Jay fought the stiffness that took over his muscles so that he could manage to sit straight up. The screeching sound hit Jay’s ears again, this time it was clearer, making it apparent where it was coming from. Young Jay Talley felt a knot form in his throat and another ball up tight in his gut. Beads of sweat formed where his hair and his forehead met before making a trail downwards on his face stopping only when they hit the cotton of his white t-shirt. Jay swallows, attempting to force the know down but it’s of no use. The scratch fills the dead air once again and this time Jay has no doubt where it is coming from. Talley’s eyes lock in on a large black toy box that rests in the corner of his bedroom. “Mo-Mom.” He tried but it only came out as a whisper. The lid on the toy box quaked and Jay’s eyes grew double. Slowly the lid lifted off of the toy box and out came a visual that terrified Jay beyond repair. Four short blades cut through the blackness to show their true form. The claws slide across the edge of the toy box which causes quite the unpleasant sound. The lid rises up ? “MOM?” The black trench coat and the clawed glove are evident even among the shadows. Piercing blue eyes stared back at Jay, eyes that could cause most men to cry and beg for their lives. “MOM!” Jay’s voice finally reached the bellow that it was supposed to be the other few times. Those once stiff muscles had come back to life and Jay wasted no time leaping off of his bed and sliding to his door. A warm and sweaty palm wrapped around the cool metallic door knob and turned? it didn’t budge. Jay pulled harder the second time but the result remained the same. Jay looked up at a spot on the door that had been illuminated halfway by the moonlight coming through his window. Like a black liquid, the shadows quickly took over the light. Jay spun around and threw his whole body back onto the door. The dark figure takes a foot out of his toy box and stomps onto the floor followed by the other. Jay stared at the large man that stood in front of him with sheer terror. He didn’t bother telling himself that this couldn’t be real, that Freddy Krueger didn’t exist. It took Jay a few moments to realize that something wasn’t right about this Freddy. Even through the shadows he spotted differences. No striped sweater, no fedora and the mother fucker had long hair, and of course those icy blue eyes that stared back. That low growl of a chuckle came out from the blackness. Jay watched as a clawed hand rose up and extended outward towards him. His gaze was fixed on the blades once they left the shadows, moonlight caused them to glisten. Those blades were coming for him, they were going to get him, he was going to die. “FUCK!” In one last move of desperation Jay turned back around and pulled on the door ? IT OPENED! A wave of relief washes over the young man and he ran out of his bedroom. It couldn’t be sure but he would swear that he felt a swipe from the claw at his head. Every muscle in his legs worked overtime to pick up the speed needed to run down the narrow hallway to make it to his parent’s bedroom. It was a straight shot, not a long one either but under the circumstances the run felt like it took an hour to Jay but he made it, diving into the room and slamming the door shut. He caught a glimpse of his shadowy attacker moving down the hallway. A reflection of himself stared back at him, a mirror on the door of his parent’s room made sure of that. He waited for something to happen. And waited. Then a hand grabbed his shoulder. “Jay?” his mother asked. Jay was terrified briefly but soon relieved and he turned and hugged his mother tightly. Soon his father would also come to where they both were standing. “What’s wrong Jamison?” His father would ask. He would try to answer but all that would come out were fragments of real thoughts in between hysterical sobs. His father reached down and picked him up. “It’s alright Jay.” “Yes sweetie,” his mother began. “It was just a dream, it wasn’t real.” Jay’s heart sank again, hearing that phrase said once again. He twisted his head to stare at the mirror on the wall. In his heart he knew that any moment now that mirror would shatter and all three of them would be dead. A small, shaky finger that belonged to Jay points at the mirror. “He’s coming.” What the family didn’t realize was what was happening behind them. The figure rose up from seemingly nowhere and towered over the family. Jay heard the evil growl that passed as a laugh. Then the claw drove into the back of his father. II. Meet the Doc I examine every motion that Jay makes as he finishes his story. They teach us to look for certain things from our patients, little movements that they may make that proves that they are lying. Some kids come here because they want attention, others to hurt their parents. I know it’s my job to understand the child psyche but to be quite honest that’s one tough job. At times it’s impossible to understand why these kids do what they do. Jamison Talley hasn’t been one of them. Either he is an incredibly good liar or he has real issues, ones that will not be easily resolved. I’ve been working with him for about a month now, trying to get a feel for the kind of person he is. I never rush into the issues that may have brought my patients into my care. It’s best to make them feel comfortable with you first, discuss them and their likes, their dislikes. “So that’s it.” His soft voice pronounces. “That’s the first time.” “So you believe that this Freddy killed your parents?” “No ? it wasn’t Freddy. Freddy is a character in a movie.” “But you said that?” “NO!” His tone sets me back a bit. “It only took the form of Krueger because it knew I was afraid. It survives off of fear.” “Then tell me Jay,” I lean in, folding my hands on the table that separates us. “What was it?” His head turns away, his eyes move to the window. “Jay?” “Doctor ? Ab-Abi?” “Abitua ? but you can call me Roberto.” “Doctor Roberto, were you ever afraid of anything as a child?” “Like what?” “The boogeyman, the monster in your closet or maybe under your bed.” “I think all kids were at some point.” “Well,” he begins, he also turns his head back towards me. “What if that fear never went away” What if you found out that there was something lurking around in the dark ? waiting to get you?” “Jay, there’s nothing out to get you.” I lean in further, trying to keep his attention on me. “You are suffering from something we call night terrors. It is a parasomnia sleep disorder characterized by extreme terror and a temporary inability to regain full consciousness. The subject ?” He cuts me off? “Wakes abruptly from slow-wave sleep, with waking usually accompanied by gasping, moaning, or screaming.” His eyes stare back at me, although he is only fifteen years old you would swear he was much older based on the way his eyes look. “I know all about it Doc.” “Then you should also know that these things cannot hurt you.” “Yeah,” He looks away again. “That’s what they keep telling me.” The room goes silent for a few moment as I try to recollect my thoughts, try to focus the session. The kid is obviously disturbed and I feel there’s more to his problems than meets the eye. “So this figure, do you know what it wants?” “Me.” “Why you” Why not any other kid?” “If I knew all of the answers I wouldn’t be here now would I?” He spit’s the words out at me more than asking. The first hint of frustration I’ve gotten from him in any of our sessions. “No I guess not.” I sit back in my chair, always studying him, always trying to get a proper read. “Well maybe I need to hear more.” “About him?” “Yes about what you are afraid of.” “There’s a lot to tell Roberto. I see him everywhere.” “Wait,” I slightly cock my head to the side. “Everywhere” This isn’t just when you are sleeping?” “Not quite, no.” He takes in a deep breath. “I think it’s more than just a fucking nightmare. This thing is more than just something I’ve thought up to cover any screwy shit I’ve done.” “Tell me then ? what do you think it is?” “Evil,” he answers immediately. “Pure evil.” Again silence takes over the small room. I can’t say for sure how it is I’ll process this information. It’s dangerous to put Jamison Talley in the same category as other trouble makers looking for a way out. I believe he actually believes that there are things that go bump in the night. It doesn’t matter that it’s impossible because if he believes it then it’s gospel. Fear is one of the most powerful feelings, it can make a person to incredible and vicious things depending on the situation. The key in this case is to find out what Jay is afraid of. All of these shadowy figures have an origin, they serve some kind of deeper purpose. I’ve seen these entities be a manifestation of everything from a child’s father to a clown they saw on television. There is only one aspect of all of these stories that remains the same, the fact that their attacker is not real. Today I will prove to Jay that he has nothing to fear either. I will give him the power to see through the darkness and reach the light. “Tell me Jay,” His eyes lock in with mine. “Tell me about the event that got you sent here.” It’s as if the memory sucker punches him. Immediately his eyes water up and a couple of tears trickle out, making a trail down his cheek and stop only when they splash on his black t-shirt. “I don’t want to.” he answers back. “You must! It is important to face this thing head on Jay ? you cannot be afraid, you cannot back down.” More tears follow the first few. “F-Fine.” And again he tells a story?
