A Girl, An Egomaniac, and A Sidekick: Part 1 (A.K.A how B&E~! and Tessa came about)

16 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/16/08 (Takes place August 2002) August 2002 Nothing in the professional wrestling business could possibly be more grueling than breaking yourself in and making your first or last name, a household name. Tally up a dozen chair shots, being dropped on your head on more than a few occasion in a match, and being tossed around like a rag doll, and you still would not be able to account for the amount the anguish experienced by a newcomer. It’s not just the physical matters that take a toll on a rookie. It’s money. It’s friendships. It’s going out there and hoping not to make a fool of yourself, that wear a person out the most. Despite the fact that females in the professional wrestling circle were rare, and certainly prized, Tessa Windsor was no exemption to this rule. Despite being surrounded by hundreds of others, who’d come to wrestle in the third Golden Turnbuckle Tournament just as herself, she found it far more difficult than one could imagine, to pinpoint someone, ANYONE, that she could start a casual conversation with. Standing amongst a crowd of comprised mostly of men a foot, give or take a few inches, taller than her, Tessa, standing at only five foot four inches, stood out like a sore thumb. Some presumed she was nothing more than a member of the press, others presumed she was one of the other guys girlfriends, but absolutely no one in the massive crowds that day would have guessed that she was due to the step into the ring with Terry Wood later on that afternoon. On the opposite end of the hall, it was a total opposite story. Shouting and throwing his arms around, Chris Storm seemed to fit in with the testosterone-injected crowd. Yelling, cursing, and laughing; he was just one of the guys that was sure he was going to win. They all thought they were going to win, each one of them. That was why they were there. Chris Storm was being accompanied by his close friend, Markus Stone, as Chris did not want to be the only person from his home promotion there. Markus was not entered in the tournament, but he still went along to give support to Storm and to check out the scene. The Primetime Central offices were flooded with different professionals going in and out, all wondering around while they waited for the meeting to start. Because of past problems with the Golden Turnbuckle Tournaments, the wrestlers had to attend a short meeting on business ethics before they could find out their road agent assignments and the likes. With thirty minutes still left before the meeting was set to start, wrestlers were still showing up by the minute, and this was just for a portion of the brackets. Other brackets would have their own meeting at a later time to save space and to avoid any fights between opponents. Needing to take a break from the crowded room they found themselves in, Chris Storm and Markus Stone headed out into the hallway and were looking for some place where they could stretch and talk over possible opponents. Cutting down the hall, passing the water cooler, Chris turned his head toward Markus and nonchalantly told him, “That’s the woman I’m going to marry.” Markus came to a full stop. “Wow, what?” he asked, as he looked around to see who Chris was in reference to. The only female he noticed was a dainty woman clad in a pink halter top as well as pair of hip hugging jeans, which complemented her hourglass figure quite well. Markus zoomed in on the petite in pink as she strolled over to fetch herself a drink. “Her?” he questioned, before noticing that Chris had never stopped walking. Having to double step just to catch up, the smirk on Chris? face remained as he looked back at Markus. “Do you see any other tits in this sausage fest?” Chris dauntingly teased, as he cut a left into the bathroom, so that he could assure that was still looking his best. Markus assumed his role as WIW’s clown as he looked back to Chris just in time to avoid walking into the wall, “Well, that Kimbusa guy did have a rather impressing rack.” That was certainly enough to warp the grin on Chris? face. Suddenly, breasts were the last things he wanted to think about. “Okay. The only set out there under two hundred pounds.” Of course Markus had a retort there too, “Your good friend Cally Bane comes in close there”..” Chris? face contorted again, this time into a snarl, “Could you really see me making a film with her?” As much as he would have loved to have her and her significant other in the bed, he’d wanted nothing at all to do with her psychotic personality and bedroom antics. Markus, of course chuckled. He was well aware of the love relationship between those two, and the fact that the only thing about Storm that interested Bane, was his wrestling ability. “You wouldn’t stand a chance. That chick is fucking nuts. ? “Agreed.” Chris? mind, however, was far from Callisto Bane. Instead, it floated to the side pocket of his cargo pants, where his comb was stashed. If he wanted to get anyone, he’d have to make sure he kept his physical charm well intact. “What makes you so sure you’re going to marry that puny thing out there though?” Markus questioned, as he peered out the door one more time towards the woman, who sheepishly caught sight of Markus and waved, in hopes that he’d dismiss her from his view, “you’d fucking break her before you even got her in bed.” “Then she can’t get away.” Chris smirked, as he ran his comb through his artificially blonde locks, before smoothing it back into a pony tail, “She’ll be the one lucky one that I’ll take home, Markus. Mark my words.” Though the idea had dwindled in his head since the two had met, Markus Stone now had solid proof that his partner, Chris Storm was in fact crazy. Chris Storm getting married? It wasn’t going to happen, at least not in this lifetime. The kid part, maybe. But marriage, certainly not. In fact, while the two conferred in the bathroom, their darling diva was already being chatted up by their adversary Erik Erratic, the youngest member of the WIW roster. Unlike his colleagues, Erik at least had some form manners. Taking her hand into his, he lifted it to his lips and stamped it with a gentlemanly greeting. “And what would the name of this charming sweetheart before me be?” Blushing from ear to ear, the woman certainly couldn’t hide how flattered she felt by this gesture, “I’m Tessa. Tessa Windsor, from XCWF.” Despite the fact, that he obviously wanted to have a good first impression, the widening of Erik dark brown eyes gave away how taken aback he was that she wrestled, “You wrestle and keep that kind of figure?” Glancing her up and down once more, Erik grinned and of course came up with the best gentlemanly response he could muster, “quite impressive.” She was far from that impressed by herself though, “I only wrestle other women. It doesn’t take much build to beat someone your size.” To a guy though, that left even more for the mind to play with, “Nonsense. I’m sure it’s quite a pleasure to step in the ring against you. So much so that I kinda wish that I had entered this whole shebang.” Tessa’s eyebrows lifted slightly, as she gave the man before as she gave him another quick look over. He certainly wasn’t bad looking, but he looked to be a bit younger than her, which said quite a bit considering Tessa herself just turned twenty, “You’re not in this” This gave Markus and Chris the perfect opportunity to cease their eavesdropping and drop a bomb on the unsuspecting Erik Erratic, “They don’t allow children to enter without their guardian’s permission.” Echoing what his friend had to say, Markus wrapped his arm around Erik’s shoulders, “We couldn’t take a chance of our darling little boy getting hurt. Maybe we’ll sign his permission slip next year.” The semi show from the two was enough to catch Tessa’s attention, “And you guys are?” Chris? ego was taken aback by this comment. How could she not possibly know who the World Champion of WIW was? “The greatest fucking things since baby Jesus.” Markus interjected for Chris, who was still contemplating how in the world this chick could not have heard of him. An angered Erik piped in, “The biggest egomaniacs you’ll ever meet.” Markus made sure that after that insult; he built Chris and himself back up their rightful level, “That’s not the only thing that’s big. Ain’t that right Chris?” Markus nudged his partner, whose eyes never once removed themselves from the woman standing before them. Big was also the one word that could describe the size of the grin that spread across Markus? face at that moment. Once again, Erik was trying to top the two, “Pardon them. They’ve never dealt with a classy woman.” ? Yes, pardon us.” Markus spoke up once more. This time his hand was placed Tessa’s shoulder, “we’ve actually surpassed puberty years ago unlike our good friend Erik here. We’re nothing more than horny studs.” To say that Tessa felt awkward would be more than an understatement. Right about now she was mortified, still she played along to avoid any potential attacks that could be thrown her way, “And what does your partner have to say about that?” For a moment Chris removed himself from the hell of a hot woman not knowing his name, to throw forth a wave of disgust. If she didn’t know his name, she wasn’t worth his time. Right about now, he was busy praying that she’d get squashed ASAP by the likes of Gaz Green, Jackson Rust, Digital Mortality, or one of the one hundred something others that entered this tournament. “Chris?” Markus questioned, hoping to get some sort of smart ass comment out of his best bud. “We’re leaving finally?” Chris questioned back hopefully, not into this whole socializing thing when it came to people who didn’t care about his accomplishments. “Well I guess?” Chris finally sprung back to life, only given that he now had the opportunity to be a total jackass, “Good. I can’t stand to be around here anymore. Between a woman who wrestles and an underage boy in a singlet that shows off his lack of balls, I feel like this whole damn business is going to waste.” Markus went to retort, but found himself being dragged off by a agitated Chris Storm. “That’was’awkward.” Tessa found herself accidentally spilling aloud. “You haven’t seen the half of it yet.” Erik replied. Truer words have never been spoken from Erik Erratics lips. Without a shadow of a doubt, things were only bound to get crazier for the Petite in Pink.
