Luncheon with Warz

Date: 8*31*05 'Where are you?' 'Im getting my car valeted...i'll be right there!' 'Alright...' *Click* ~As I sat there waiting, I didnt know how to tell Tom. I had invited him for lunch earlier in the day for a specific reason. But how do I tell someone who's good friends with Blake Cassidy? I give the waiter a nod when he asks me if I want a refill on my iced tea. And then I seen him. I dont usually get nervous around people...but this time it was different. Tom approached me, so I stood and we shook hands.~ 'How's it going Tom?' 'It's going great! So, this place any good?' 'Never been here but from what Ive heard its good.' ~As I look at Tom, I dont know how he's going to respond to what I say to him. I owe him for starting my pro wrestling career. The waiter comes up and asks Tom what he wanted to drink.~ 'I'll take a water please. Thanks. So Warz...whats up?' ~He knew! He knew something was up. He knows I have some bad news.~ 'Well...I have some thoughts about The Regulators Tom.' 'Oh? Well you know you and Blake have a match this Thursday at World Wide right? Its a number one contenders match for the Tag Team Titles against The Guild!' 'Nice.' 'Yeah, I know! You can take the Guild by yourself Warz! They cant handle you!' 'Yeah Tom...thats what I wanted to tell you.' 'What?' 'Well...' '...' 'I dont think Blake is the best tag team partner for me! I need someone who has heart, who wants to win, who takes life and wrestling serious.' ~Damn. That was too harsh. I should have laid it on him lighter than that.~ 'Alright Warz. If thats how you want it.' 'Well...I like the guy. But I dont think our personalities mix.' 'No, no! Its cool. I'll just have to break the news to the poor guy. And he was looking forward to The Regulators future. But shit happens...right Warz?' ~Ah, he's throwing the good ol' guilt trip on me. I wont succumb!~ 'Well Tom. How about you talk to him and maybe we can work this out.' 'Alright! I'll talk to him immidiately about his whole attitude. Thursday night...you will see a new man! I promise!' 'Well...I just want to win. And we both have to want it, being that we're a team and all.' 'Alright...maybe you should talk to him.' 'I cant. He annoys the hell out of me. I feel its better if I dont say a word...then he wont say a word.' 'Yeah, I know the guy can be immature at times...but deep down he truly is a great athlete.' 'Im sure he is Tom. But Im not seeing it.' *Cellphone begins to ring* 'Just a sec Warz! Hello? Hey! We're just talking about you! Uh huh? Yeah. WHAT?! HE KICKED YOU OUT?!? Ah man! Alright...I'll be right over! Okay! Bye!' *Hangs up the phone* 'Well Warz, that was Blake. He just got kicked out of his apartment for something he didnt do. I gotta go over there. Sorry about lunch.' ~Ah great! I bash the guy and then find out hes been evicted. I guess Im just a horrible person!~ 'Oh no problem. I totally understand. Maybe you should wait a until later to mention anything to Blake.' 'Yeah, we'll see.' ~Tom stands and we shake hands. I watch him leave, just thinking to myself. Did this conversation just help The Regulators or hurt us. Thursday night Im going to find out.~
