Goat

16 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/16/06 Following World Wide, Barty Bale flew home and bound himself to a week of rest. He took a real beating, receiving multiple chair-shots to the head from Vetra, then being jumped by Kyo Kushinaga unmercifully. Barty Bale was bothered by the fact that he was tossed around 'like a cheap whore' this week. After what happened at World Wide, there was no public interest for him anymore within the GCW; it made him look like just another 'jobber' to say the least. And how could he spin this? How could he make this week go away and win over the attention of both the media and the fans? What would he have to do to make himself stand-out? Nothing came to mind for him. Hell, his over-paid agent couldn't even come up with a solution. It'll take going back to his roots for this one. 'Yah mahn, It's like I'm meant to get screwed over hard in that damn organization. It's like...' He paused to take a huge rip from the pregnant joint. He blew the smoke through one nostril, and then the other, and then alternating between the two. He let out a funny geek-noise. 'I don't know, it's like I was signed just to be the laughin' stock of the company. But mahn, that's not how I roll... I'm an entertainer dude!' He took another rip and passed the joint along. The man to his right, Terry Barkley, a friend since child-hood, decided to chime in. 'Listen Barty, you were born to entertain. But you gotta' remember dude, you were never really a wrestler. This is something new for you, this is something you gotta' alter up a bit. You gotta' take control of the situation, don't let 'em control you. Bring what you got to that GCW place, mix things up a bit; give them something they never saw before. You can do it dude, you just gotta' commit now.' These words echoed in Barty's head. Mentally, he zoned out and twisted into a dark portal of stone with these words inscribed on the walls. Little creatures climbing the walls would repeat these words over and over again in a high-pitched voice. Light would shimmer through cracks in the walls to shine on him. The dark portal turned to a colorful paradise suddenly; and that's when it hit him. 'Well, what do I do about Kyo? I'm really beating myself up because of that guy, I gotta' get him back mahn...' 'And you will. All you gotta' do dude is get him back in a way that you can.' The joint made it's way back to Barty again. He proceeded to take a few monster hits while he listened to Terry. 'Barty, you're good with manipulation, and I'm saying this in a positive way. You're good with setting things up in the background, then being the man and throwing people off-guard, letting what you set-up hit 'em when they least expect it. Thas' how you're goin' to have to go about this one. I say, first get him the way he got you, then, make a big deal about it. Let it run longer than it has to be, but don't do it physically. Do it with words, and thas' no problem for you. Win the love of the viewers that way, they'll suck themselves right in, dude.' Barty passes the joint to Terry. 'Hmm... I think you're onto somethin' mahn. I actually have a plan now...' He looked over to Terry and saw him pressed back in his seat with his eyes bolted open. Barty waved his hand in front of Terry's face, but there was no one home. He looked over to the other man they were smoking with, Silent Ray. 'Heh, do you see who I'm takin' advice from?' The two men snickered. 'Alright Terry, time to quit bogartin' the joint. Pass it on.' Terry came back to his senses, and handed the joint over to Silent Ray. Silent Ray kept to himself and puffed on the ciggaweed stick. 'Woah, woah, guys, I just thought about... wait...' Terry had their attention, but his pause started to worry them. He stuttered up again. 'I... I... I think I just shat my pants!' Terry rushed to the bathroom and slammed the door behind him. As he was running he was pulling his pants down, and he managed to trip over them when he made it to the bathroom. When the door shut, they heard a loud crashing sound. Barty and Silent Ray laughed real hard amongst each other. So hard, they'd be bumping into each other, and eventually start knocking each other off of their seats, and then start laughing about that. Then it sort-of became a game. A few hours later, Barty woke up from a good nap. He looked over and saw Terry sleeping without any pants on; just tighty-whities. He saw Silent Ray passed out in the recliner on the other side of the room; his jaw dropped with drool spewing. Barty itched his crotch, then raised his arms to stretch and yawn. He walked into his kitchen and opened his overhead cabinet (which he happened to leave open). He grabbed a bowl, a spoon, a box of Frosted Flakes, and some 2% milk. He tossed the items onto the counter near a small television, and plopped down on the stool nearby. After he prepared his treat, he turned on the tube. Well wouldn't you know it, it was an interview with Kyo. Within the interview, he referred to Barty as a 'goat', a moaner, a complainer... As if it wasn't a smack in the face. 'Oh, mahn, you gotta' be fuckin' kidding me!' He stood up in a rage. He turned into the cabinet he happened to leave open which also happened to be wedged against the refrigerator. The sound of his body hitting the tile floor alarmingly woke up the other two. 'What th'fuck!? What was that?' Terry shouted as Silent Ray kept silent. They both rose from where they were sleeping and crept over to the kitchen. When they looked over the island only to find Barty holding his head, they laughed. 'Gotta' be careful dude, that head has been takin' a lotta' shots lately.' Terry grabbed an ice pack from the freezer, closed the cabinet door afterwards, then kneeled down to help Barty out. 'Shit man... that hurt!' Silent Ray shook his head. Terry dragged his friend over to the sofa and propped him up. Barty held onto the ice pack while Terry went over to the recliner to sit down. 'And who the fuck is Gus, mahn? He said I was Gus... does this dude even know who he's going after?' Terry chuckled while Silent Ray stood up half asleep. 'Barty, this guy wants a chunk of you... I think he needs to be given a little lesson about you...' 'I think that's a great idea Terr, that's what I'm gonna' do. When I get the opportunity to, I'm really gunna' let it hit him hard. I'll give him a real reason to call me a Goat...' 'Nows the time, bro... now is the time to shine. How could he even gloat about this anyway? He did nothing to you, he attacked you when you were already down; by chair-shots nonetheless. He's just digging his own grave. But like I said, now is the time. And that means now is the time to push him into that hole he's dug so deep.' Every word ignited determination and inspiration in Barty's mind. It soaked quickly, and allowed him to start foreseeing his future. Pieces of plans bunched together, and apparently became clear. First, Barty will have to take Wes Sanders on at World Wide. And this is where his new mission will start. It's a match setup by the booking committee, and it sort-of serves no purpose to him. But Barty knows the wisest thing to do is to use this. He's an entertainer, he can manipulate things to work out how he needs them to. And that's what he's going to do. Next, Barty will forcefully grab the undivided attention of the audience. Somehow, someway, he'll get it. He doesn't know how, but he will. And from there, that's when things will blossom. His victory over Wes Sanders will start the transition. After that, it's all what the Goat needs to do to get his horns up someone's ass. His new-found hatred for Kyo will show.
