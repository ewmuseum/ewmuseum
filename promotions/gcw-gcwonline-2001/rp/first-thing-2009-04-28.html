First Thing

28 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 28 2009 There’s a story to be told, a story about a young man whose life had been flipped; turned upside down. No, his name isn’t Will Smith either. The young man’s name is Darek Machida or Macho as his friends call him. Darek had grown up in a rough manner and a very awkward lifestyle. To be honest, ‘grown up’ is probably too strong seeing as how Darek is all of seventeen years old. Young Machida never had luxuries in life, in fact it wasn’t surprising to find him going without basic necessities for long periods of time. He’s been alone for the most part, bouncing from foster home to foster home but never really settling in. Money was tough to come by and that led to him doing some things he’d regret to get enough money for a McChicken from McDonald’s. Maybe some fries too. Today things have changed for Darek, in a major way. At the age of seventeen, Machida has signed a contract with Global Championship Wrestling, one of the premiere companies of it’s kind in the world. Because of this great opportunity, Darek has a chance to better himself, improve his quality of living. He also has the chance to show millions of people that e has what it takes to hang with the best of the best in a brutal sport. It was a proud moment for Macho, that day he officially signed his GCW contract. It was also a day of great monetary gain, he’d sure as hell never seen a check with so many zeros. It’s a typical reaction that once a person comes into money that they go out and buy something memorable. Who doesn’t love to go to their friends and brag about some expensive new acquisition? These items are usually easy to pinpoint. Some people go out and buy a fancy new car or truck, something luxurious. Maybe you love boating and go and get a yacht. Could it be that you’re a thrill seeker and want to get a motorcycle and zoom through the streets? Perhaps you have a family that you love and you buy brand new homes in a great neighborhood so that you can all be together. Usually these are flashy materials that you can show off to the world. But not Darek, no this young man has much more modest tastes. The house, car, clothes, jewelry and motorcycles will come later. Dee Macho just wanted to throw a big fucking party … and smoke the best weed that money can buy. Darek sits at a glass table preparing the night’s party essentials for distribution and use. Around the same table eagerly awaiting Darek to finish are four of his closest friends. There are probably hundreds of other people at the party as well but to these five individuals none of that matters. Although the friends are silent, their anticipation for what is to come is very apparent. None of them could ever afford the good shit before. “Dude,” Darek smiles wide as he breaks the silence amongst the circle. “Smells amazing!” “Dude? Amazing? When the fuck did you turn to a bitch?” Phillip Escamilla. Darek’s closest friend and a very straight forward (blunt) type of person. He is a couple years older than Darek but took a liking to the kid when he saw him beat the hell out of two bullies during lunch in Darek’s Freshman year of High School. Phillip always respected people who didn’t let themselves be pushed around and didn’t back down to anyone regardless of their size or stature. “Fuck you Bink,” Darek finishes rolling the first joint. “You get the last hit, bitch.” Oh yeah, Phillip’s nickname is Binky or Bink for short. Phillip had never told anyone where he got the nickname, not even Darek. Most of his friends figured he’d ask them to stop calling him by the name once he got older … how many ladies can you impress with a name like Binky? But he never did and he still wouldn’t tell anyone where the name came from. Like the story behind the name is more embarrassing than the name itself. Darek just figured Phillip took it as a challenge, if he can get girls and have people afraid of him with a name like BINKY then he must be doing alright for himself. “Let’s light it up.” Darek goes through the motions: Light it, Puff, puff … pass. Them are the rules. The rules didn’t say anything about coughing uncontrollably. Everyone around the table laughs as it seems that Darek is about to choke to death but the coughing can’t get rid of the cheesy smile plastered on his face. The joint is plucked from his fingers. “Fuck me, hit’s hard yo.” They each go around and take their turns, each one succumbing to the same fate as Darek. “So Dee,” Binky turns to Darek after passing the blunt off. “I guess this groping other mother fucka’s thing is gunna work.” “Wait you’re gropin’ niggas?” “Say wha?” Darek takes a hit and shakes his head while doing a mixture of coughing and laughing. “Bink is jus’ an asshole guys. It’s wrestling, I get to kick some silly dressin’ mother fuckers asses and get paid for it son.” He takes his second puff and passes it on. “As you can see, it pays well.” By no means is the setting an extravagant one, at least not by rich people terms. It’s a large house, one that Darek just paid someone a chunk of cash to rent out for the night but it’s far from a mansion. It’s away from neighbors, fits a lot of people and doesn’t have a lot of breakable things inside of it. Perfect for this kind of party. “Then what’s up wit it?” Binky asks. “When you throwin’ down for the first time yo? I’ll tune in if I’m not busy.” “This week.” Darek leans back in his chair. “Going against Axel Rose or some shit like that man.” “Axel Rose?” Everyone else at the table asks in unison. “Yeah.” “From Guns N’ Roses?” “The fuck is a Guns N’ Roses?” They all go quiet and lean back in their chairs, their minds struggling hard to figure out exactly who or what an Axel Action or Guns N’ Roses is. Eventually they all just start laughing and the memory of Guns N’ Roses fades away. “Shouldn’t you train or something yo? For real, don’t wanna get fucked up out there do ya?” Binky asks Darek this question without actually turning his head to look at him. Binky thinks he’s turning his head but his body won’t really work the way it’s suppose to. Doesn’t seem to bother him any though. “Train? Shit yo, you can’t out fantastic, fantastic. And I’m fucking fantastic.” Binky laughs and lifts the blunt to his lips, killin’ it. “Touché my nigga.” “’Sides, can you think of a better way to relax before something stressful?” Again no one has an answer. “Anyone want a shot?” The voice interrupts the boys’ train of thought. It’s a woman’s voice too so it also stirs up some other emotions. Standing in the open space between where Binky and Darek are seated is standing a young black girl, smooth light skin, shoulder length black hair and bright blue eyes, probably contacts. Her curvy figure is shown off brilliantly by the tight fitted clothes she’s wearing which brings a smile to Darek’s face. “Fa sho, I’ll take one. Give us all a round.” The girl smiles and lays out six shot glasses on the table and fills them up to the top with the Patron tequila in her hands. Binky is the first to grab his glass and lift it up over his head, spilling a drop on his lap. For some reason this irritates Darek slightly. “Yo foo’ don’t spill alcohol. That’s against the god damn code.” “Chill nigga, lift your damn glass up. It’s time for a toast and shit.” Darek’s irritation melts away easily, he’s in no mood to be pissed off. He lifts his glass up the same way everyone else has theirs up. “To you Dee, you’ve made it man. Now don’t fuck this shit up, ya heard?” “No doubt.” And back goes the drinks … they don’t go down as smooth as any of them would like them to. “WOOO!” Binky howls. “Now let’s hear it my nigga.” “Hear what?” “Lay a flow down for us, wrastlin’ style.” Darek smiles and pours himself another drink before standing on the chair he had been sitting in. Most of the party doesn’t pay attention but the people closest to him focus in on the man of the hour. He doesn’t waste any time, just let’s the words flow. “The world ain’t ready for me son, my name’s Machida Ya can’t man up wit me, you’ll hafta be a cheata’ It starts this week when I face AXEL Action But I ain’t sweatin’ that cat, fuck I’m jus’ relaxin’ Imma punch, kick and straight come out swingin’ AXEL won’t know what hit him man, his head will be ringin’ I know I’m the underdog, but I like it that way After I stomp this bitch into the ground, GCW will know I’m here to stay. Can’t none of these clowns hold me, I’ve got an uncontrollable rage. Don’t matter who gets in my way cause … I’m winnin’ Rampage.” Darek takes his second shot … And blacks out. He doesn’t remember the party the next morning.
