The Long Road: Part One

4 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4 June 08 I. Have you ever had the feeling that you were being watched? I’m sure you have. It’s not a pleasant feeling, nothing frightening or weird, kind of annoying really. I usually get looked at a lot when I go out in public. Forget the fact that I am a celebrity; I’m also a 6’6?, Mexican beast of a man. That usually catches people’s attention. I’m accustomed to people asking for autographs or stopping to throw insults at me because I did something damaging to their favorite wrestler. I’ve even grown to appreciate the many media outlets that try to get interviews as you sit and try to have a peaceful dinner. You know, the types that won’t stop asking the same questions over and over until you give them the answer they are really after? Well tonight is a bit different, I don’t think the person staring wants an autograph or an interview. Maybe an insult, but I’d put money that’s not what he is after. It’s a bit more annoying than usual tonight however ? I mean for fuck’s sake, I came to a shithole bar just to get away and have a nice, quiet drink. These small, shitty bars are typically my favorite. The short circuited jukebox, the flat beer, the dusty tables and the strong stench of cigarette smoke are usually enough to cause most decent people to stay far away. Digging deeper you’d find the most unsanitary of bathrooms with the most sickening messages written on the walls and stains that will never go away on the floors. Yes, these places are disgusting, but where most people’s eyes may water up and their nostrils burn from the smell ? I find peace and peace is something that is tough to find in the YouTube era. Fuck YouTube. Anyways, I don’t look right at this voyeur, would hate to give him a real opening to come over and sit down in front of me. The hell with answering the same damn questions that I’ve been answering for years just for this one person, if he wants to “get to know” me I’m sure he can log into a computer and Google me. Besides, there are plenty of other things to keep my mind on at the moment. High Stakes is coming up, pretty important I’d say. My time to make history once again in GCW, always a treat to fans around the world I’m sure. I don’t get why it is they hate me so much ? alright, so I don’t slap their hands every time I walk down to the ring and I don’t shower them with love and praise at every interview. Sure I’m also probably the most arrogant son of a bitch walking around in GCW. Does that deserve hate? Or jealousy? I take the last swig of my beer and place the glass on the table in front of me. I move my eyes quickly around the dim lit bar, briefly coming in eye contact with that bastard that’s staring at me. A shiver runs down my spine. There’s something about him, something unnerving? Immediately a knot forms in my stomach. I have to leave. II. I inhale, the smoke flows into my body, and the nicotine makes me tingle all over. I close my eyes as I release a white cloud into the muggy air that has taken over the bar. Without looking my fingers find the sides of the shot glass, filled to the brim with some cheap tequila, that’s placed on the bar to my left. The glass is cool on my fingertips, a huge contrast to the rest of my body. I take the glass and quickly put the drink away, enjoying it as the smooth tequila rushes down my throat and rests in my gullet. My body warms up more almost instantly. I flick the glass away and concentrate more on the figure that’s beginning to stand at the other end of the bar. I’ve made him uneasy, made him nervous and that’s exactly what I hoped for when I came here tonight. He doesn’t know it yet, but his world is about to change, turned upside down really. No, I’m not going to kill him. In fact it’s quite the opposite. I intend to help the man that the world knows as “LeStatt Knight”. I’ve known him since the day his eyes first opened when he was born, sometimes I was distant, at others I was right there with him but I’ve always cared for the way his life goes. I guess one could say I’ve had a soft spot for Roberto Abitua since the day I saw him all those years ago. I take another long drag of my cigarette before releasing another cloud of toxin filled smoke. I almost feel bad for the way his career has been going. I saw the signs of his demise months ago; I considered attempting to change things then but figured I’d give him a chance to right the ship on his own. Now it’s all becoming too much, too hard to swallow. Those talents are being wasted and it’s about time to put a stop to it, the hard way. Roberto moves towards the doors, stopping only for a moment. Is he thinking of approaching me? Punching me in the face? Is he afraid? I guess I’ll have to find out later ? He disappears out the old doors and into the night. I finish my cigarette and stand from my seat at the bar. For a long time I questioned if this was the best course of action, would Roberto even accept me after all of these years? Would he want to hear the advice I have to give? It’s a conundrum that I will have up until the time we meet face to face. BUT? With each step closer to the same door that he strolled out of I can feel my conviction, my purpose grow stronger, more concrete than ever before. Roberto NEEDS me, LeStatt Knight NEEDS me. It’s because of my guidance that he is where he is today and it’ll be because of my guidance that he’ll reclaim his past glory. 2005 isn’t so long ago is it? The cool breeze and the crispy night air slap my warm cheeks; the fresh air is as refreshing as leaping into a beach with crystal clear water on a sweltering day. I close my eyes and take it all in, imagining the moment that Roberto and I are face to face once again. A reunion of sorts My eyes open. This is it “no backing down, no taking NO for an answer. I raise my hand, signaling for a cab to stop. III. “Going back to the hotel isn’t really an option.” “Then where to sir?” “Just around.” The window slides up, leaving me alone in the backseat of the limo. I don’t want to go back and sleep yet, well, CAN’T is probably the word I’m really looking for. Too much going on up in the old brain, too much restlessness. I play this cool and collected SOB on-screen, my tough exterior is enough to mask a lot of my problems, but people just don’t really get it. Sometimes I don’t get it. It’s been like this before every big show that I’m on since early 2007. I get nervous, those butterflies begin to flutter. Doubt creeps in, anxiety and worry come next. It wasn’t always this way, not in 2005, never, ever. What the fucks happened to me? And there I go, doing it again. It’s no wonder I haven’t held the GCW World Title in about a year and a half, not since being dropped by Lia in the Triple Threat match at NC-17 2006. What kind of a champion takes themselves out before even stepping into the ring? It’s why I’ve been a failure since my last run at the top. I lost the title I lost the Warpath tournament I lost Dangerous Games Lost the Round Robin Couldn’t hack it as the leader of The Establishment Its tough looking myself in the mirror these days, knowing that the person I see isn’t the man that I want to be or need to be. It’s like looking into a sheet of fractured glass, everything’s distorted. Probably how Superman feels when looking at Bizzaro. I guess the question becomes simple: How do I fix this? The answer hasn’t been easy, I’ve been trying to get back to the World Title since I lost it but I always come up just short. Is that a decline in my ability and dedication? Or is it actually a silver lining in disguise? On my worst day I can be right in the thick of the Title hunt. FUCK WHAT AM I THINKING!” Since when have I settled for second best!” I grit my teeth, anger builds up. It’s inevitable. “And I’m going to make history?” The words come out soft and weak. My eyes wander to the window next to me; I can’t help but notice the irony of it all. Here I am, sitting in luxury, sitting STILL while the world whips on by. Just like me, living off of the fame I built in 2005, pretending to be some Almighty Being in GCW but not doing anything to prove myself. All the fucking while people like Valentine, Terror, Bagwell and Jordan White have leaped in front of me ? Is that all that I am now? A Ghost of the past? It’s a thought that makes me want to throw up. I roll down the window and yell to the driver. “Take me to the hotel now.:” IV. He’s certainly done well for himself, only the best of the best stay in hotels as fancy as these. Not surprising though, the man had money before he became this wrestling “living legend”. Roberto led himself a charmed life, if you discount the death and destruction that’s filled it since he was born. I walk around the lavish hotel room, the only light helping my vision being that of the moon. The dim light casts shadows at every corner, turn and crevice in the room. Every chair, door and coat rack gives the look of a dangerous intruder. Things aren’t always as they appear to be. Just like Roberto Abitua. I sit down in a chair that I’ve set up directly in front of the door. In my left hand is a freshly poured glass of Patron and in my right, a brand new cigarette. It’ll only be moments before he returns here and knowing him? He won’t get upset, angry; throw a tantrum or any of that other nonsense. Sure I’m breaking and entering but he’s been through this before ? and when guys like Alex Grey did it, guns were involved. I don’t need any such weaponry; this isn’t the same type of visit. Footsteps in the hallway ? My muscles tighten up. The sound of the key card sliding into the door! The handle turns, the door pushes open Light floods the room. The door closes and latches shut. “Good evening Mr. Abitua.” Silence. What will he do? His gaze meets mine, his icy blue eyes very bright even behind all of the dark and shadows. He, himself is nothing but a shadow with glowing eyes at this point as well. As I suspected, he doesn’t resort to violence. “Who are you?” He asks in a whisper. I stand before responding. “An old friend.” I let out a short chuckle. “Listen-? He’s so fast and strong that I don’t have time to think let alone react physically. His massive hands wrap around my throat, his strength forces me back to the wall behind me. The same wall that was once a good 20 feet behind me is now causing pain to shoot throughout my entire body. “WHO THE FUCK ARE YOU!”” His voice is now sinister, threatening. “I-I-It’s (I gasp for breath) Joa-quin” His grip loosens “He drops me on my ass. After a long silence, he finally speaks again. “What are you doing here old man?” I smile even though he cannot see it. “I’m here to help.” “With what?” “Your legacy.” He doesn’t say anything and neither do I. I don’t think neither of us has to. The deal has been made.
