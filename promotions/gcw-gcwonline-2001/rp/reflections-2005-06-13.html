Reflections

13 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13 June 05 I looked into the mirror and the face smiled back at me. That face was one I was all too familiar with and also all too afraid of. It was that face that haunted my dreams causing bloodshed and havoc. Funny thing is that with all that fear he has never lied to me. I used to think that he did, made myself believe that the man in the mirror was some sick monster that was here to do nothing but destroy my world. I’ve learned through my own mistakes and the mishaps of others around me that he has done nothing but help me. Without him I would be nothing, hell I would probably be dead and buried. LeStatt Knight is who I get my strength from and deep down I’ve always known. His black eyes stared at me for what seemed like an eternity but he spoke first, his voice sounding almost ghost-like. “So we finally lost Roberto, I never thought it would happen quite the way it did but hell it was bound to happen sometime right?” “No LeStatt YOU lost that one. I had things under control Trey was beaten, down and ready for the taking ? it was your blood lust for Jason that caused us to lose that match.” His smile widened and those black eyes had a flash of red in them for a moment. “Let’s not fool ourselves Roberto, you want Dread’s blood just as bad as I do and don’t claim other wise. I could see it in your eyes and feel it in your very essence. That chair shot at Warpath made you forget everything ? it was that feeling of releasing all negative energy.” “You are negative energy LeStatt.” He chuckles now obviously amused. “You are right I am negative energy, but look at what we have become since you have embraced this gift that I bring to you! GCW reopened back in early February and you have just now gotten your first significant loss. I’d say that we are making waves that no one can match at this point in time. Our name is every where Roberto, in magazines, newspapers, websites ? hell even that Prime Time Central place has people admiring our work and we both know how you felt about that place not so long ago.” “This is true Knight but as dominant as we’ve been we cannot afford any lapses like we had at Worldwide last week. You need to keep that temper of yours under control.” “Alright, alright I’ll give you that one Roberto, but are you really that distraught about losing the United States title” You held it for a record time, convincingly may I add. It was time to move on.” “I don’t like to lose LeStatt.” “Sacrifices MUST be made! The U.S. Title was only going to hold us back. To achieve greatness’IMMORTALITY” we must set our sights higher. Jason Dread is the key to our quest. Not only must we defeat him but we must completely and utterly destroy him.” “Look LeStatt I know Jason, and he will not simply lie down on his back and let us walk all over him. Besides it’s been done, did you not watch Octavian try his best to take everything from him” Dread still won ? even if it was because of us.” “I am not talking about taking away his material things Roberto. I don’t want his house, cars, money, or clothes. What I’what WE want is his very essence, his LIFE!.” “You want to kill him?” I asked a bit nervously “NO not at all ? not in the literal sense that is. You remember what I did to Alex” I know you do it’s not something either of us are liable to forget.” I nodded my head as he spoke “Well I think it’s time we stepped our game up from there. It’s not about making him broke and living on the streets. It’s about making his mind such a fragile thing, slowly but surely taking bits and pieces of it, then at the right time wrapping our fist around it and crushing it.” I felt an anxiety building up inside of me now. It was almost like a virgin getting ready for their first time of having sex. I was ready to destroy this man that once thought I was his friend and I would enjoy doing so. “I am ready LeStatt.” “What we need to do is take over his life ? you have connections, so use them Roberto.” “I will get on it but first things first.” He gave me a questioning look “Our approach must be altered. We have appeared too anxious Knight, that’s why Dread got the upper hand. How could we have been out smarted by Dread” You keep your anger in check ? I don’t need you lashing out every time you see him. We need to take a more mellow approach, we need to be more nonchalant ? or to use the word of a new friend of mine, we need to use more “Finesse”.” He shook his head and smiled that same devious smile I have seen many times before. Those eyes of his flashed with that crimson red. “We’ll play it your way then Roberto. Just know that when the time comes I will take over and destroy him.” “There is only one place that can happen.” “Where would that be?” “Dangerous Games.” “You will challenge him then?” “Knight you should already know that even if he wanted to get out of it, the writing is already on the wall. Dread will fall by our hand at Dangerous Games. I believe it was said that an era ended when I lost that United States Title?” Knight cut me off now? “But when we win the world title a new era will begin ?” “An era that will never be matched.” “That’s the spirit Roberto ? this is what I have waited years for!” I simply nodded at the dark reflection that stared back at me then with a wink I turned my back and walked out of the bathroom and stepped into my bedroom. Mary greeted me with a wide smile and a warm embrace. “Whos’ya talking to Knightie-bear?” “No one Mami.” I leaned down and kissed her now, feeling more alive than ever even though I had just lost to Trey. I knew I’d get him back eventually so there was no reason to rush into that. Dread was the target and I never missed my mark.
