Finding What You Look For: Part I

undated

Date: today-ish Rainclouds had been creeping in overhead, closing in on my destination faster than I had been. Minutes before arrival, a complete downpour began to cover the cabin, the uncut grass, the shed, the dirt driveway, and the car. I can see some lightning in the distance, colored purple due to the strange hue of the Northern Lights above. Sitting in the car, looking at the dreary cabin with the rain coming down in sheets and the earth stinking of peat and moss and ages past, there's only one word for the sort of depression that I can feel upon coming to such a lonesome place. '...Canada...' It's honestly something out of a Robert Frost poem gone horribly, horribly wrong. I mean, if you had told me a year ago that the Devil...and I do mean the actual Devil... Lucifer Satan Beelzebub The Prince of Darkness The Ruler of Hell ...lived in Canada...well, I wouldn't know what to think of you. But I wouldn't think too much of you, that's for certain. If you threw on top of the mix that he's a scraggly, scrawny, twice divorced, Gennessee drinking, military reject from rural New York, I'd laugh at you. And if you told me he was one of my best friends, I would have had you committed. But, somehow, deep in my gut, I would have known you were right. 'Lousy son of a bitch.' And that's probably why I don't fear him. In the slightest. It's hard to fear someone when he couldn't make the football team and you were star quarterback. I still remember when Don first told me he was the Devil. Actually, a hallucination of my father told me. Though 'hallucination' isn't what I like to call it. The proper term is 'psychic projection,' but the doctors made me sign some papers and take some medicines and undergo some treatments that convinced me to call it a 'hallucination.' But sure enough, when I asked Don about it, and he admitted it with the most straight of faces and the least emotion I'd ever seen, it was like I'd always known. 'Yeah, Johnny-boy...what can I say? You're fuckin Matlock to solve this case.' 'Well, I didn't really do anything, Don. My-' 'Yeah, yeah, yeah. Your dead father's ghost of Christmas past came to you in an alleyway and convinced you that one of your friends is the devil, cut off your finger, and you believed him. It's really the only way to decide things.' 'I believe him.' 'What is he, the voice of God?' Ha. My father. The voice of God. 'Well, are you the Devil or not?' I can remember to this day that I expected hesitation, decision, and thoughts whizzing in and out of his head. I thought he'd suck down his cigarette, be on his way to lighting another one, scratch his crotch, and dart his eyes up to the upper left corner and look off in the distance so that he could think of what to say. But I guess he didn't have to think. After all, he had been the Devil since time began. 'Yeah, Johnny. You got me.' And I really have no idea why, but- 'I believe you.' Which brings me to Canada. A little over a year or so later. Old wounds heal as my lost finger finally stopped itching a couple months back and pride gotten from pride lost perpetrated its sick cycle of selfishness and I just continued to tumble along, like everybody else, through it all. And looking up through the rain and the lightning, to the Northern Lights, the Aurora Borealis, it's the first time I've actually seen the thing that I took the name of. Sweeps of colors rippling through the air. Like some sort of badass, not gay rainbow. Taking the shape of an electronic pulse or magnetic thingamawhatsis, up in the heavens. Where you can't touch it, but you want to. You can't find it, but you can see it. It's like so many things we dream of. But it's nothing we can know. And that's enough sentimental crap about lights in the sky. Cold rain greets me and begins to mat down my perfect hair as soon as I step out of the car and into a mud pit. Step by trudging step brings me slowly closer to the cabin in the middle of nowhere that you can only find by following power cables through a field to a dirt road through some woods. He says it's because he likes privacy. I think it's because he likes the idea of someone having to seek him out actively, whereas he's usually the one looking for some lost soul. And as I'm nearing the porch, the door opens. Underneath the canopy of beam and roof stands the Devil himself, smoking himself a menthol and holding a can of Genny. His buzz cut the same length as his stubble, he reminds me of a cross between Denis Leary and Gollum. With a buzz cut. And stubble. Narrow eyes pierce the rain and see right through me. 'Johnny-boy...you should call first. I woulda bought more beer.' I hold up a pack of High Life. Not his favorite, but it's mine, so he'll have to live with it. 'Your phone's been disconnected, Don.' '...those crazy bitches...' Ex-wives, two of them. At least that's how many are still living. Best part about Don's ex-wives is that now they're lesbians with each other. And they've been taking legal action against him for years to try to make him raise their nine or ten children. Guess they might have won alimony and he's been having to pay up. Or something. Maybe in Canada they just take your socialized Welfare and give it to someone else. Maybe in Canada, there isn't a system of writing checks, per say, as much as a system of trading Welfare benefits. I don't know. The mud is easing its way into my boots, through any little hole it can find, as the cardboard box is slowly getting pulpy underneath the rain. Dim lighting in the cabin, as well as the obnoxiously loud hum coming from behind it, indicate that Don's got his own power generator going. They must have shut the juice for that off months ago. Hand him the case of High Life. Prefabricated high living that is so much like the rest of the world today. It's good feelings in a bottle that they package and sell for more than they should, and people go on and keep on buying it because they can. Because they make more than they should and they buy stuff because that's what life's all about. A piss yellow colored liquid that sums of the story of my life and makes all problems go away. Well...the pills help too. 'So it's been a while, Johnny Borealis. Johnathan Fitzwillis. Whatever the hell you're going by these days.' 'It had been a while.' 'I haven't seen you since the night I sprung the fact onto you that I've been seeing Kate- ...ooooooooooooooohhhhhhhhh...' The Devil didn't really need that long to put two and two together. He knew, from get-go of when he started dating her, that it was going to do this to me. He knew that I wouldn't be able to stomach seeing him, my Judas, my Brutus, for a couple months. He knew damn well that it was going to slice me to shreds on the inside, knowing that a scumbucket like him was fooling around with the woman I love. He probably did the whole damn thing just for that very reaction. But he acts surprised because he's supposed to. And I act like I don't know that previous paragraph because I'm supposed to. 'You mind if I come inside, man? Sit down. Open a can or two. Watch some-' Power's out. No TV. '...get out of the rain?' 'Yeah, sure, no problem Johnny-boy. You're always welcome in my home.' That would sound one hundred percent less creepy and evil if this wasn't Lucifer I was talking to. But, to tell the truth, I trust him. Just like he trusted me not to fail in toppling the Baptist Church. His reason was because Baptists just annoy him. Something to do with literal interpretations of the Bible. Whatever. He trusted me not to fail, which I have not yet. I trust him not to do anything that would damn me. And, in all actuality, I don't think he gives enough of a shit about anything to even try to do that. So we're fine. He leads the way out of the rain and into the proverbial fire. His cabin even smells like Canada. It's like liquified moss and the inside of a refridgerator that's been sitting in a field for twenty years and 'new baby smell' and stale beer all mixed into one, strong aroma that can only be described in one word. Canada. Okay. That is the last 'everything bad boils down to Canada' things I'll be making tonight. Makes his way six steps into the door and sits right down on his favorite chair. It's literally designed so that only he can sit in it. The chair has this metal pole that at one point served some purpose and it pushes up against the cusion hard. Only Don has the body shape required to sit in it and not be sodomized. Truly the Devil's throne. 'So, what is it Johnny-boy? Usually the only times you come to me anymore are to either report on your mission to bring down the Baptist faith or to get some sort of advice on some sort of problem because you have the delusion of thinking that I have any of the answers.' He looks over at me and throws his cigarette, burned all the way to his lips, to the ground. Lights another one in the half second between thought and speech. 'Or did you just want to talk about me and Kate?' 'It's been eating away at me, man. You can't do something like that.' Now, I've taken my best friend's girl before. I've taken my ex-best friend's girl. I've taken my best friend's ex-girl. I've taken my ex's best friend. I've taken my ex-best friend's ex-girl. I've taken my girl's ex-best friend. I've done every combination that involves the words 'taking' 'best friend' and 'girl,' with or without the prefix 'ex.' But this is different. Because he's the fucking Devil. And he's a scumfuck. He knows. 'I know.' 'Well, if you know, then why'd you do it?' 'You want me to lie, buddy? The full-hearted truth of it is that I wanted to fuck her. Real bad.' Speechless. 'Like, Johnny...I've wanted Kate since the moment she started to sprout. I've been watching her almost as long as I've been hanging out with you. And she's easily one of the top hundred choices of a woman that I want to put my seed into. And not even for the usual 'inch toward armageddon' reasons either. I just really, really, really, reeeeally wanted her. Bad.' I really don't know if I'm gonna throw up or hit him. Or both in any order. Red tunnel vision narrows on him and his words hit me one at a time. 'But don't worry man. I didn't.' 'You didn't what?' 'Didn't what? What did I just say? I didn't fuck her. I didn't fuck her, she didn't fuck me, we didn't fuck. So don't get all pissy. Sit down. And open a cold one. Cigarette?' Still standing there shocked, I don't even notice that he's holding out the cigarette until a couple seconds later. 'I-... You... You've never offered me a cigarette before...' 'You've never looked like you needed one before. Chill out. Sit down. Suck some ash from a paper tube.' His words still coarse through my mind. Words I'd never thought I'd hear him say. I didn't fuck her. I didn't fuck her. I didn't fuck her. Cigarette? And before I know it, I'm holding a nice cancer stick in my fingers and breathing in black death as Don retracts his sweet-ass metal lighter with a skull on it and the flame shoots up to like the freaking sky when he lights it. A man's lighter. The Devil's lighter. 'Just be careful with these things. Smoke 'em for two, three lifetimes and they really grab ahold of you and they don't let go.' 'Is that a fact?' 'I was addicted when I was six years old once. Not that I really mind or anything, but it's kind of embarassing to call yourself the ruler of Hell and to die when you're not even old enough to check someone in hockey.' 'I'll be careful.' Ahhhhhhhhhhh... I haven't smoked since high school. Had a cigar at graduation, but of course that doesn't count. I haven't smoked a cigarette, a pointless cigarette with no culture, no taste, and no general point, since I was like seventeen years old. Pretty much a decade. And to feel the pretty poison sliding down weightlessly, perforating my throat and then my lungs, and going into my bloodstream and clinging to the inside of my very self, blackening that which once was red, there's only one word for it. Fine. I lied before. Say it with me. Canada. Seriously, though, that's the last time. 'So yeah, she wouldn't make freaky love with me. We called it off after a couple dates. We had different views and they didn't match up, and that's really all there is to it. I mean, she's unbelievably gorgeous. But she wasn't up for the hanky panky. And she was just kinda going places with me to get out and go places. She doesn't leave the house much. And after the second night when she still said 'no,' I figured it wasn't worth the pursuit.' 'You date the girl of your dreams and you break it off because she won't screw on the second date?' 'I broke it off because by now I can read people. And I knew there wasn't much chance of her agreeing to actually screw me.' 'But aren't you the Devil? Don't you have, like...powers, man? I know you said once that you're bored and tired of using them. But if it's something that you want that bad-' 'That's not how it works. I seduce. I swoon. I charm. I sway-' You scratch your ass and light up another one. '-I don't control minds, Johnny. If you don't take someone of their own will, there's nothing for it. There's no gratification, not even to the sex. It's no different than how your precious God doesn't just will it that all people follow him without question. Free will is what makes humans humans. And free will is what makes Lucifer, or Don, Lucifer. And as much as people say I'm 'evil' and I'm 'a liar and a thief,' I'm not a hypocrit. I can't make Kate love me. I tried everything. And that is bar none the most infuriating thing I've experienced in a very, very long time. But it's also the truth of the matter. You know...I am aware of what you're always thinking about, Johnny. You've always got your mind set on inevitability. Unchanging things that you cannot affect no matter what. How you know and realize, for instance, that one day you are going to die. And then you're either going to stop existing or you will somehow go on forever. And the kicker is that there is absolutely nothing you can do about it. Or I suppose an even more fitting one for you is the endless inevitability of selfishness, or the sick cycle as you call it. Try to be a selfless hero, but you know you're only doing it so that people can know you're a selfless hero. You want to be Superman, but you know that even he isn't superman, he's just a shell of a being that hides behind a costume and an excuse and one more reason after another to be an attention whoring child. And so you go out there, trying to make a difference and trying to save the world, when you know in your heart that you're never going to save anything, you're never going to change the world, and there is always someone that you will let down. And there's nothing you can do about it.' Don stops speaking. He knows damn well how on target he was, nailing into the ground the most negative of negative beliefs. For the first time, I can see him actually work to savor the cigarette instead of just sucking it down. I do the same. It feels good as it pours in and slowly numbs the lungs. True addiction gripping ahold. And after a short eternity, he leans foreward again. 'That's why I didn't screw Kate. Because it was impossible.' Strange as it sounds, I pity him. Maybe that's the greatest trick that the Devil ever played. Fuck not knowing that he was there, it's knowing that he's there, knowing who and what he is, knowing what he aims to do to the world, yourself included, and you end up feeling sorry for him. Sorry because one person didn't make love to him. Because she didn't, couldn't, love him. But it only takes half a second to remember that he was going after the girl I love. 'Welcome back to reality, asshole. After everything you've thought, everything you've ever done, do, and will do, and everything you cause others to do, you expect me to feel sorry for you? You're delusional! You're out of your mind! You're saying things, firing off synapses in your brain, that don't make sense, can't make sense, and you expect me to feel some sort of pity for you? You tried to steal Kate from me!' 'You never had her!' It's amazing how close to punches we are. I didn't even realize how much I was in Don's face until he returned the fire and got in mine. And the two of us are standing, each with a can of beer and a clenched fist, about two inches from each other. And for the first time, I can actually see the Devil that he is. His eyes are fire and his voice is the boom of cracking ice. 'You left, her Johnny! You got up, walked out, and left her! And why? No devil tempted you. Nobody suggested it. You loved her, you were happy, you wondered what would happen if you got up and left, and you did. And that was that! Katherine Jagozynski was no longer your 'girl' because of you. She was heartbroken for a while because of you. She got her shit together, she moved on with her life, put up with being alone for six years, and started to date me because I asked her. And then I got my ass dumped by her, and do you know why? Because. Of. You!' 'What?' 'She didn't not love me because of me. She couldn't love me because you have completely infected her mind. You're on her inside and it shows through her outside, and she misses you to the point that she can't stand being around anybody else. For all intents and purposes, she should hate you for doing this to her. But, defying logic and reason as you emotional meatbags tend to do, she loves you too much to do that. Isn't that just the sweetest fucking thing you've ever heard? But it gets better, Jack. Both of you have been rotting from the inside out, slowly at first and speeding up as time goes by, and the only thing that is going to help either one of you is to do that one little thing that you won't let yourself do.' 'And what's that, smart man?' 'You've got to lose, Jack. You've got to lose yourself. You've got to just give up and go back to Kate.' Bullshit. I'm Johnny Borealis. I don't lose. 'I mean, seriously, Johnny...you call me delusional. Do you even realize how much your reality has been slipping away? When was the last time you talked to anybody real?' 'What are you talking about? You're real.' 'I'm real, but I'm not a real person, man. I'm not a real person who's only got one life to live and works to make the best of it. I'm not someone who goes out and puts their nose to the grindstone to get to their ideal living style. When was the last time you talked to your friends Rob or Amos? The last time you slept with a girl and said more than two words to her? The last time you ever even fucking talked to anybody, man? For over a year now, I'd say, you've been a complete walking mess. First you start talking to your ghost of a father, who's alive, so it's really more of a psychotic projection-' 'Psychic projection. Look it up, it's real.' 'And the two of you argue until you block him out to go away. God fucking forbid you go talk to your real dad. Your father in the flesh. You haven't told anybody how you really freaking think about anything, and the only person that you talk to about anything is me. And I'm the fucking Devil. So what, my friend, does that say about yourself?' Logic pierces even more than the harshness of words as realization falls into place like an intricate puzzle. He's right. Don sits there, with his haircut and his stubble and looking like Denis Leary and his beer and his constant lighting and smoking of cigarettes, and he's right. It's been a good six months since I've been able to stand looking at anybody in the eyes. Months since I've been able to be with a woman. I realized some time ago that I loathe even the most sensual of human contact. Touching a woman, gorgeous or not, churns my stomach and brings up feelings of dread and hatred that life seems so pointless. Like this brief moment of pleasure is nothing but a sick fucking joke and I don't want any part of it. Kind of like the savings at Wal Mart. And for all the evil Don does... He's right. 'So the question is, what do you do now?' 'Now...I'm going to finish this beer. I'm going to finish this cigarette. And then I'm going to go back out, into the freezing cold rain. And I'm going to drive home.' 'You came all this way just to bitch to me and bring me beer?' 'I needed to know.' 'That's what I like about you, Johnny-boy. You can't even see the obvious. You knew the entire freaking time. You just couldn't bring yourself to tell yourself.' 'Maybe.' A last swig and a last smoke. I stand up and nod to my friend. Start walking to the door. 'Johnny.' 'What?' Turn to him. Sometimes, Don doesn't look anything like the Devil at all. He's just a person, like you or me, living his life. Living his long, many lives. But going through it like the rest of us. 'You know you owe me something for everything I do for you. And I think it's time you paid up for what I do for you.' Churning feeling in the gut again. I think I've had this coming for a long time. 'My soul?' But his smile, for once, actually brings comfort. He's the most human of humans at times, I swear to God. 'Your friendship. Go to Kate, John. And just remember who your friend is. Not your ruler or your temptor or your taskmaster. Just your friend.' Nod. Turn and walk outside. The rain coming down hard. Take a look up at the Northern Lights. The Aurora Borealis. Up there like a divine hero in the heavens. And only one thought can manage to come to mind as I whisper it out in secret to the night. 'Never thought the voice of God would come from the Devil.' The paradox of paradoxes. The contraiction. The dirty truth behind the fascade of clean-cut impossibility. There's only one word that I can use to describe it. ...but I swore I wouldn't do that a fourth time. So I'll just leave it at that.
