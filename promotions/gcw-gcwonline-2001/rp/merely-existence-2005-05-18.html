Merely Existence

18 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/18/05 Let's see... Things I'd rather be doing: 'ID, ma'am.' Working out. Nursing my hand before my match. Drinking heavily. Smoking a nice cigar. Play some music. Play a video game a-plenty. Earning money. Eating. Playing poker. Relaxing. Sleeping. Having sex. Having romantic relations besides sex. Holding hands. Winking at somebody. Driving somewhere. Driving nowhere. Going to see Star Wars. Hell, I'd rather be going to see House of Wax. 'Oh, shoot. I don't seem to have my ID on me. Could you make an exception?' Of course. We can all make exceptions, lady. I made the exception, and the mistake, of letting your fat ass in front of me in line. And as I stand here, holding two bags of groceries in one and a half hands, you are indeed making me regret that exception. Soak my head in an algae infested pond. Get stabbed in the shoulder. Smoke three packs of cigarettes in two minutes. Buy some Support Our Troops bumper stickers. Kick a box full of kittens out of a plane onto my mother's grave. Drink an entire bottle of tobasco sauce straight. Fuck a tree. Jerk off with a cheese grater duct taped to my hand. 'It's strictly against our-' 'It's okay. I know this one. She's of age.' Enter the security guard. I'm sorry. Enter the security guard slash boyfriend of the dumb bitch that doesn't have her ID. And just like that, everything is made okay. Except that nobody cares about 'by the book.' So why should I? 'You're next sir.' About fuckin time. 'Thanks.' 'Cindy' rings me out. Cindy always seems to be the one that rings me out. Or maybe that's a different Cindy. Perhaps they're all named Cindy that work here. I wouldn't put it past this shithole town. And as much as I'm trying to watch her scan my food, my supplies... And as much as I'm trying even to stare at her breasts, for something to do, I can't deny that te sparks shooting off inside my cranium are all pointed to having to get on that fucking plane, fly back to Europe tonight, and fight tomorrow on no sleep and a busted hand. Typical, really. 'Paper or plastic?' I don't even know what I say. Don't even notice what I get. Load the stuff in the car. Drive home. Pack the bags. The time just goes by without notice. Five, ten minutes flicker in an instant, and it hits me. Merely existing. RING!!! And the cell phone rings. 'What?' 'Glad I caught you before you left, Johnny-boy.' The devil himself has a...has a voice. Not an entirely annoying voice. Not an entirely abrasive voice. It's smoke-stained. It's slightly monotonous. It's tired and bored and intrigued all at once. It's really just...that he has this voice. And, well...he's got a voice. Story of my life. 'What's your problem, Don?' I know he's going to pester me about that job he gave me. And honestly, he should have known not to give it to someone with a busy schedule. Taking down the entirety of the Baptist Church isn't exactly something one can do when their only free time is on the weekend, like me. I'm even starting to doubt my abilities to crumble an entire belief structure by myself, but that doesn't mean I'm doubting myself. And I'll kill the man that says otherwise! 'No problem.' 'You call me when there's a problem. Religiously.' Heh. I didn't even mean for that irony. But he feels the sting of it as I say it. The man's grown numb to most feelings, but he's not a fuckin robot. He knows that I know that he secretly misses it. But, it's his pride that keeps him from doing anything about it. Which might be why he always says that my vanity is his favorite part of me. Where was I? Chewing glass. Suffocating. Giving a horse a rectal exam. 'I just wanted to tell you that I hope you have a good trip.' 'Oh really? No...favors? You don't want me to pick you up a trinket or two this time around?' 'Not really. I have all the trickets I need here in my cabin.' In case you're wondering, the devil lives in Canada. I'm sure that means something, but I haven't figured out what yet. 'What's eatin you, Johnny-boy?' 'Nothing's eating me.' 'You sound like you got some chest-get-offing.' Sigh. No. Siiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiigh... I fuckin hate him. 'Did you ever think that there was really, honestly, no meaning to anything?' 'Every damn day of my life.' 'Well, I'm serious. Ignore the fact that you've seen and talked to God. Ignore that.' 'I try to. Every damn day of my life.' 'Don... I haven't been able to actually think I was doing what I was doing while I was doing it all day today.' 'You lost me.' I lost myself. 'Okay...let me try to put this how it is. ...I'm driving a car-' 'And on the cell phone? That's illegal.' 'I'm not driving a car right now. I'm hypothetically driving a car. But I'm not driving a car. I'm merely existing. Like right now. I'm explaining my thoughts to you. But I'm not. I'm just...being. I'm not doing anything but continuing, and prolonging my existence, and there's nothing I can do about it.' 'You can kill yourself.' '...' '...you can try that.' '...' 'It's really easy. Just take an ice cream scoop-' 'I kill myself. What does that do? I live on somehow. Some way. In some form, my consciousness survives, and I keep existing. This time, with no way of 'ending it.'' 'Not really. There's nothing after life.' The devil's a nihilist, eh? 'Did you just say that there's nothing after life?' 'You told me to ignore my whole knowing that God exists.' ... ...being under the tire of a moving truck. Being impaled on a spike...slowly. Getting sucked into a black hole. ...be surrounded by clowns... 'What are you thinking about for so long?' 'Things I'd rather be doing. Can you even try to follow along?' 'I'm just being a bastard. It's what I'm good at.' Whatever. My bags are packed. I'm ready to go, and it's just a matter of getting into the frame of mind that should be ready to wrestle. Fuck my hand. Fuck sleeping. The only thing I need to do for a while is try to get revved up and ready to fight. Because I've got a big one on my hands. And what everybody's been saying about this match is true. Luke is going to beat me down. He's going to rip me and throw me from pillar to post. And he's going to grind me beneath his fucking monstrous boots before spitting on me for good measure. And then...I'm going to embarass him. 'Have fun in Europe, kid. And don't do nothin I wouldn't do.' Forgot you were there, Don. And I didn't even notice I was in the car. Everything's running on autopilot. Let's just hope that driving to the airport will be a smooth venture, because I'm not all there to concentrate at it. It's not even like I don't like Luke Hanson. He seems like an alright guy. Real straight shooter. Real go-getter. And a million other shallow attempts at me trying to boost my own view of him. The fact of the matter is that the man is nothing more than a man. And just like a man, he has his strengths and his weaknesses. And just like me, several of his weaknesses are quite exploitable. Regardless of size, bones will break. Skin will bruise severely. And arteries will get cut off until passing out is inevitable. And while I'm nothing more than a stepping stone for him, as he ventures on to take out The Jhub- -The Jhoke is more like it- -it's true that the man is more of a mountain than a molehill for me. Not only due to his grotesque size, but also his status. He's the big man around here. He's the bee's knees. Perhaps not to the performance caliber of being a champion, but he's recognized. And with his defeat, I'm recognized. Simply. Put. But for all I'm trying right now to get the blood moving, and for all that I'm trying to get psyched... I know I'm not driving to the airport. I know I'm not getting ready to wrestle tomorrow. I'm merely existing...
