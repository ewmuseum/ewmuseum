Forever, My Love

13 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/13/2008 .::Disclaimer- May Contain Strong Language and Violence::. .::Viewer Discretion Advised::. ---- Tuesday, May 13, 2008 0645 hours, local time San Diego, CA She watched him from across the room, through the tangle of her hair; he was quiet- moody and sullen. He hadn't taken a nap with her after checking in, despite the long drive. The single overhead lamp wasn't harsh. The glow was soft, nice on his tired eyes. His arm ached, but the pencil knew what it was doing as dawn crept over the horizon. He was in the zone, somewhere beyond exhaustion, between genius and madness. He clamped the cigarette between his teeth, running the pencil gingerly over the page, adding definition to the striations in the eyes. He looked down at the beauty his hands had wrought, a troubled expression on his face. Ashes fell on the paper, and he brushed them aside absently. He felt almost enthralled by the portrait, almost as though he was studying someone else's artwork. As he looked it over, he almost caught himself feeling guilt. The expressiveness in the sweep of graphite over the paper was uncanny, but there was something else there. Obsessive, perhaps, but there was more to the subtle rendering of the image he'd conjured from his mind's eye. He dropped the pencil, hearing the muted sound as it bounced off the carpet. "Beautiful," he said softly, almost with a tinge of shock in his voice. Realization struck him dumb, and he didn't notice as she moved up behind him, peering over his shoulder at the realistic rendering that balanced against his knee. He reached down, instinctively snagging the pencil, and moving to shade the unkempt, dark hair with more depth. Her arms encircled his neck, lips pressing against the skin below his ear as she leaned over his shoulder, looking at the beautiful portrait of herself. "Oh, Brad," she whispered in awe, startling him so much the pencil skipped across the paper, marring the perfection. Thankful she was behind him, and oblivious to the flush that briefly colored his cheeks, he turned the image away from her. "Hey, babe." Her hands pressed down on his shoulders, just a slight pressure, and then she patted him softly. "Get some sleep, Brad." She murmured, crossing the room to the minibar fridge. She pulled out a bottle of water, tossing her hair from her face before drinking some. He watched her, not bothering to hide his inspection. Every time he glanced her way, she looked somewhere else. It was like a game of cat and mouse, it wasn't that she didn't want him to see her looking at him but she enjoyed those stolen moments when she could watch him, and not see that piercing look in his eyes. That burning, deep gaze that sometimes saw right through her. "You hiding from me?" He asked quietly, standing up slowly. His shadow seemed to come over her as he crossed the room to where she stood. "What's wrong, pudding?" Georgie looked up at him with a faint smile, shaking her head, "nothing. I just like watching you, is that a crime?" Raising both of her eyebrows, she studied him for a moment. He shook his head; there were no words within that moment as silence came between them. He watched as Georgie sat down on the couch, and wrapped her arms around her legs. Her blue eyes looked into his, and it made his heart hurt to see the sadness there. "You tend to forget where you are sometimes, and you let that guard down, and you have these... expressions you make." A small smile on her lips, she was looking at him, yet she was also seeing the past. "I remember.. I remember one time when we were in Chicago." Swallowing hard, "I saw that sadness." Her voice soft, low, as if it pained her to remember. "I remember..." looking up at him she sighed, "wanting to take it away." Shrugging her shoulders, she fell silent. Jackson stood there like an idiot. Frozen and silent- with that muscle twitching in his jaw as he ground his teeth together. "It? kills me sometimes." Her head tilted up, resting against her bicep as she looked at him. "When you look at me with that same sadness? sometimes I wonder if I make you hurt more than... anything." It wasn't as if she was doubting herself, or maybe she was. She wasn't sure at that moment. "I'd rather rip out my own heart than ever be the person to cause you to hurt like that." "Pudding..." He trailed off, she didn't look at him. He could feel the pang in his chest, as if someone had stuck him with a knife, right to the heart. "Georgie.." That brought her eyes to his, "I?" He trailed off, shaking his head. "Fuck sakes. What do I have to do to show you, you don't hurt me?" She shook her head; the last thing she wanted to do was end up in an argument, fighting with him again. The last time it nearly killed her. She had already almost lost him twice, third time's a charm? Not fucking likely. She was afraid she'd set him off in ways- in different ways that would make him disappear forever. She couldn't have that, wouldn't have that. She sighed softly, slowly looking up at him. "I said forever, and I meant it... But? I want you happy." The words trailed off, as his eyes panned away. "I want to be the one to make you happy, and I try so... damn hard." Tears rolled down her cheeks. "You don't think you're good enough for me." His eyes snapped back to hers. "Don't even try to deny it, Brad. I'm not an idiot. You think that you're... this waste of human flesh, nothing more than a built up scarecrow, no heart, no brain, no soul." Her voice was rising, without her even realizing it. "And I hate you for it." The fire was in his eyes, and he was about to start railing, she could tell. He didn't get the chance. "Don't, just stop right there." That made the fire burn even brighter in his eyes. "I hate you because it isn't true and you refuse to believe it. You blame yourself for every fucking tragedy that goes on in my life, even if you weren't there, even if you weren't PART of my life yet. Don't you get it?" Shaking her head softly, she kept going, "You blame yourself because I almost got ran over. You blame yourself because some mental fan attacked me. You blame yourself, because that fucking asshole cheated on me before we were ever together, before we were real with one another." Standing up, she stepped towards him. "You blame yourself... for being who you are. With the insecurities, the deceptions, the secrets, every single little imperfection, every single nightmare you wake up from, every single time you get the hot sweats... You blame yourself because you can't be perfect. And no matter HOW many times I have to tell you I don't want you to be perfect, you continue to push for it." Her eyes sought out his as she took in a deep breath. "I love you because of all of that and even more, and I need to figure out a way to get it through that thick ass skull of yours, so you can stop beating yourself up." He had to wonder how long she had been bottling it up inside. Surely before their little fight in the car at the rest stop. In one stride, he was upon her, his hands on her shoulders and he began to shake her roughly. He didn't see the worry that came to her eyes, the alarms that were set off. His voice was a low growl, "you don't get it do you? This is who the hell I am! I can't change it. I can't change into this person you want me to be. It's not all fucking roses." He shoved her away, turning and building this wall between them. One brick at a time, he shut himself off. "You want to be my Yoko Ono? is that it? Change me into some starry-eyed asshole? I'm already a fucking joke, Georgie. What more do you want from me? You want me to commit career suicide for you? Well?" The words came spewing forth, and he didn't even try to hold them back. He was tired and angry, itching for a fix, a drink. Anything to take the edge off. Georgie took in a deep breath, her eyes closing for a moment and her jaw tensed. "Don't you get it?!" She shoved him as he tried to move away, which caused him to stop dead in his tracks. "I don't NEED you to be anyone BUT your fucking self. I don't WANT anyone but the Brad Jackson I have known for what seems to be a lifetime. But what I DON'T want is for you to continually blame yourself for the simple fact that this world is a fucked up place! It's not your fault that your psycho ex wanted to hit me with a god-damned car. You didn't make her mental, she was already that way! You didn't make him, you didn't make Matt try to do something stupid! I'm not going to be this fucking weak willed little emo bitch, who walks around eggshells when you get into your moods. I can't do it, and I won't. If you walk out of my life, because you can't stop beating yourself up for a moment, if you walk out of my life because you can't understand that it kills me to see you put yourself up on that cross every damn time you look at me, then fine. I can't make you stay, I can't make you try to turn around and understand. I'm with you because I WANT to be. Good or bad. It's us against the world, that's what you said... Unless you didn't mean it." Jackson's hands clenched into fists as he turned around, glaring at her. "So what, I'm a fucking liar now? The fuck does it matter if I meant it... you hate me, remember? That fucking hurt, you know that? That fucking cut me deep? but then you knew that. You fucking said it?" He raked his hand through his hair so violently he nearly tore it out. "You want me to stop caring? Huh? You ripped apart your room in Chicago because I shut down on you, and now you want me to shut it all off. Pretend like I don't give a shit about you...is that what you want? You want me to shrug and pretend I'm fucking retarded, watch while you blame yourself for everything just like I do? Oh, don't try to act like you don't. I'm not blind, sweetheart." Georgie's eyes glared right back into his, burning into him with recriminations. Her tone was soft and level- too soft, too level. "I hate you when you get like this, there's a big difference there, just like you fucking hate me when I beat myself up over the simple fact that I FEEL like I make nothing better in your life. That I only make it worse. Don't you get it, Brad? I'm not this strong girl that you think I am. You've gone through more fucking hell since you've met me, since we've been together, since that... one... glorious night that you told me I held your heart in my hand. That I stole it? I?" The tears that fell went unchecked, yet her voice didn't rise above that soft timbre, "how can I not blame myself? You went cold turkey because of me, because I asked you to. I pinned you up against a wall, with nowhere to run. I push you, and I push you and I push you, and you wonder why I blame myself? Why I think that I don't make it any better?" Shaking her head, she was filled with the desire to run into his arms and cry, or just run in general. "I want you to fucking care, Brad, but I don't want you to care to the point that you put MY burdens onto your shoulders too. I'm not that weak, I may not be as strong as I'd like to be, but? at least give me some credit." She turned away from him, walking towards the door. Barefoot, dressed in flannel pajama pants and his oversized Bad Religion tee, but it didn't matter. She needed to breathe, she needed air. She felt her heart pounding in her head, in her throat. For a moment it seemed as if he was frozen in time. He heard the door open, he heard it shut behind her, and yet his legs were encased in cement, immovable. He wanted to call out to her, but he couldn't. He couldn't get the words. In no less than a heartbeat, he was able to move again, yet she was already out of the room. It didn't take long for him to get out the door, but it was as if she had grown wings and flown. He looked up and down the hallway, and she wasn't there. "Shitfuck!" His hand raked through his hair again, as he heard the door to the stairway shut. His long legs took him there effortlessly, and he swung open the door. There she was, moving up the steps. "Georgie!" He said her name, a vehement whisper as he took off up the stairs, taking them two at a time to reach her. She had paused in her steps, and she kept her back to him. Her jaw clenched, and she took a deep breath. She said nothing. "Georgie." His hands moved to touch her shoulders, the shoulders which he had shaken just moments earlier, this time though his own hands were shaking. She took a step away before his hands could fall upon her. His hands dropped to his sides. He let his head hang, looking down at the floor, staring down at his feet. "Don't push me away like this. Please." "I run into your arms, because I love you." Whispering the words softly, she shook her head. "Hell I came here? heh? I came here so I could be close to you, even if I had to put on the mask, I was happy to be the buddy again." Her bottom lip trembled as she let out a soft sigh; her hand came up, the heel dragging against her cheek to wipe away her tears. "Just to be by you." Shaking her head once more, she took in a deep breath. "You may think you put me in danger because I'm with you. I think I make myself better. You make me better because I am with you." Biting her bottom lip. "When I see you... when I see you get into that mindset, when you look at me with that thousand-yard stare, it's like the sun got taken away. When you blame yourself for all of my problems, it's like I'm watching you take a knife to your body again... and again... and again. You say what I said hurts you? What you do... when you heap this burden on your shoulders, kills me, a thousand deaths in the span of a second." Speaking softly, as if he were afraid if he spoke too loud she'd disappear right before his very eyes, "look at me... please?" His jaw tensed, the words were difficult for him to say, difficult to get out. Georgie slowly turned, looking up at him. He could see her tear streaked face, the redness in her eyes, and she was still so beautiful to him. It made him ache in ways that he neither understood nor wanted to. "Do you know why?" He was met with an uncertain gaze, questioning. "Do you know why I feel like I have to fix everything?" Shaking her head gently, it was her turn to be silent now as she watched him. "Growing up... I couldn't do anything, not with him there always laughing at me for fucking up, or making me fuck up. With that cruel smile, the way he'd just itch for a chance to lay into me. I didn't have the ability, I didn't have control over anything in my own life." Shaking his head, he kept on talking, his voice shaking, "now... now that I do? I want to make everything perfect for you. Have to make everything perfect for you." The words were heart wrenching to her, the way he said them. As he reached for her this time with both arms, closing the divide between them, she didn't pull away. His arms enveloped her, crushing her to his chest. His hand smoothing her dark hair back, a constant battle he always had with her unruly mane, and he kissed the top of her head. He felt the tears stinging, and as her arms moved around him, holding onto him, the floodgates were released. He cried silently, holding onto her as if he were a man lost at sea, and she were his only salvation. In her words, he could tell she had been crying as well, the soft little snuffle, the way she sounded ever so plugged up, "you already do, Brad. You don't have to do anything." Her arms squeezed him gently, he held her tightly, yet she didn't care about the constriction. His voice was tormented, his whisper hoarse, "doesn't mean I don't feel like I have to. Like when you feel like you have to blame yourself every time I get distant, every time I turn away. I don't turn away from you." His whisper tickled her ear, as he put his head on her shoulder, resting against her, needing her in so many ways. "I turn away because I expect you to one day look at me with those beautiful eyes of yours, and tell me I disgust you, that I let you down." His entire body shook as he tried to regain control, but it wasn't working. There were no threads to be found to pull the shades down again. Somewhere in the back of her mind there was knowledge that her shoulder was becoming soaked, but it didn't seem to matter. She turned her head and buried her face against his neck. She cried as well, silently. It was as if a torrential downpour had started, and there was no sign of it stopping. "I'll never look at you that way. Ever." The words were a vow, the vehemence in them, the sincerity, the honesty, it rang through to the very core. "I don't know what I'd do without you." There was fear in his voice as he spoke those words, he held onto her even more tightly, "I don't want to think about what it'd be like without you." There was desperation in his voice, shaking with the emotion of those words, the weight of them falling down on him, like crashing boulders. "I can't think of what it'd be like without you." He whispered, re-stating the words as if he had to, to make it clear to her. She said nothing, that fear in her eyes still as she looked away. She slipped her finger between those lips, and nibbled on her ragged fingernail before replying. "You won't have to." The words were a promise, as her hands moved down his back. Her own hands were shaking, as if every ounce of energy was being sapped out of her within that moment. "I'm not going away Brad. Not until you tell me with every breath, every bone of your being, every fiber, every blood cell, and mean it from the bottom of your heart, that you want me gone, that you want me to disappear. I'll go then, and only then, and not without a fight first." He pulled back, as if the words were a slap in the face, he had to see the honesty in her eyes. He was about to speak, but she beat him to it. "God I love you, Brad..." For once, she beat him to it, those words, that epitaph, "God help me? but I do." He whispered, finishing off the statement, their eyes boring into one another. ---- Crashed out, he was already asleep when she slipped between the sheets beside him, moving slowly so as not to wake him. Today had been taxing, and it wasn't just the long drive to San Diego. For the last leg of the trip, Jackson drove in silence. He'd said little, locked in his own contemplation of the road with a grim expression on his face. He seemed eager to get to the hotel, and get off the road, at least that's what he'd said when she asked, after enduring the silence for nearly an hour. She watched while he chewed on his bottom lip, worrying the bruised skin between his teeth. She knew he felt bad for scaring her two weeks ago- he'd said as much. Maybe he was holding her responsible for the entire fight, if that's what it could be called. He'd stonewalled her with silence for more than two hours when she finally broke down, cuddling close to him, close to tears. Her embrace almost lured him from the glum currents of his introspection, but he looked down at her for only a brief moment, before diving back into that brooding silence. Now they were here, and while she'd hit the shower to freshen up after their last spat, he'd gone directly to the bed, collapsing in a heap. Lifting the sheets, she saw he was still wearing his jeans. She smiled at that, and wrapped her arm around his broad back, cuddling against his side. "Mmmmm," he grunted, rolling over slowly as his eyes opened. "Sorry," he said in that gruff, sleep-clogged voice, flopping over on his back. "Love means never having to say you're sorry, Brad." She said softly, laying her head on his chest, and listening to the strong and steady sound of his heartbeat. She could listen to that for hours. "I know," his voice was filled with exhaustion, both physical and emotional. "Just? touch me, and tell me it's going to be alright. I believe you, always." His voice was soft, missing its usual cadence. It took her a moment to realize that he was drifting in the place between asleep and awake. "Open your arms and hold me? open your eyes and see me. Tell me what you see? and then tell me you still love me. Everything? open? and easy?" "I still love you, Brad." She whispered in the dark, hugging him tightly as he began to snore softly. "Always." ---- Mechanical Rhetoric I feel whole again, when she wraps her arms around me, and presses against my chest for a stolen embrace. Could it be I'm falling in love? No, already fallen. Hopeless cause, but I don't even care. Laugh. Point your fingers, but tonight, when I'm trying to fight back the desire for some sick poison, I'll be with her. She makes me want to become something more. Something better. I slept for more than eight hours last night, and I had nightmares. Capering monstrosities in my mind, ghosts from the past. It's always bad when I'm not using. The crash and burn keeps them at bay. Run to the point of exhaustion, and then fall flat and die. I'll think back on this day, and realize, at eleven in the morning, I knew who I was. In California, I was broken, broken and spilled out all over the ground. Someone I once cared for killed the man I thought I was. I came staggering back from oblivion with a name on my lips. Hers. Georgie, forever my love. In San Diego, I will show no mercy. I know you're waiting for the other shoe to drop in the back of your mind. I know you're fearing that as much as me. I have been conditioned to that, so much so that I manufacture the failures. I create situations where I can control the pain that is dished back to me. I was taught how to take a beating. By my father. By life. I know hurt. Because of that, I could never hurt you.
