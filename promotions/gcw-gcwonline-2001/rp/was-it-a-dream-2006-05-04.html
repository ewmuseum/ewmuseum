Was it a Dream?

4 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/4/06 Josh was alone for the night, and he hated it. Although Dani was but fifty feet from where he was, he was alone. She was really suffering inside from her run in with the law down in Jacksonville. It had been a harrowing experience for her. The sheriff had come to where they were staying and had actually arrested her for her clothing slip during the match. Dani had a police record now. Fortunately it had only been a five hundred dollar fine for what happened, and they wrote it off. Dani had enough to pay that anyway. But they were currently fighting with GCW to get that repaid. Seeing as how she was taking part in a match they sanctioned. But now, now she knew that she was being followed by some of the wrestling paparazzi. Bad enough her mug shot had been taken for public indecency, a 'nuisance' crime in Jacksonville and then posted on the internet. Now she was afraid to go out right away. Bad enough she'd been in negotiations with another men's magazine to do a photo shoot. This one pulled out and suspended negotiations until they find out if the fed is going to back her up. ?Fucking politics... I can't believe a state known for spring break and Girls Gone Wild videos is trying to control women who show a bit more at the beach. Moronic bible belt...? No, Josh wasn't happy. They had chosen a smaller bed and breakfast in Atlanta when they got here. He and Dani were, of course, sharing a room. But tonight she had volunteered to help out with their friends. Dani was in the living room attached to the room that Ukyo and Blaise had taken. What was she doing there? She was watching Kylie and Kaylie, Blaise's twin daughters. Dani had volunteered to take care of the two girls while Ukyo and Blaise took a night out on the town for a bit. But, Josh didn't want to intrude on the sanctity of the children. He knew he'd get into trouble if he went over there. What about his brother? Well, his brother was more of a night owl then he was. For reasons that weren't fully obvious to everyone. ?At least this gives me time to continue my research, since Shadow wasn't very responsive to my desires.? He poured himself a drink of coffee from the provided coffee maker and sat down on the recliner. He turned on MTV, which of course wasn't showing any real videos, and turned it down to a low muttering, just to have noise in the room. He finished a gulp of the hot drink and then put the cup down. After that it was time to grab the laptop that his brother used over to where it belonged, his lap. He quickly called up the proper program and put in his brother's identification and his password, ?Catharine? to launch it. He started working on the project he had been working on for the last few weeks: How can he make himself like his girlfriend... Immortal. He types and moved his hands across the touch pad as he continued to look through the extensive library of the Order's research department. Some of it having been scanned over the last decade, but most of it only reference material that at least pointed him in the right direction of books to look at and study. But this was tedious and boring work. He continued on until he couldn't hold his eyelids open any further, and drifted off to sleep. He sat there, snoozing pretty good, until he felt the laptop leaving it's spot. Instantly he awoke, startled, knowing full too well what would happen if he let that small computer hit the floor and break. ?SHIT!? However, when his eyes opened, he was shown a visage that he really wasn't expecting. The face, body, everything said that in front of him, taking the computer from his lap was the GCW World Champion, Dawn McFarlane. ?Dawn?? The woman held her finger up to her lips and gave out a sound like a snake hissing. ?What are you doing here? You're not supposed to be in my dreams, you're supposed to be in my brother's.? The woman chuckled a bit as she folded the screen down. Josh's eyes came fully into focus, and that's when it hit him. This wasn't Dawn. But it was very close. This woman was a bit shorter. Her chin a tad sharper. Her ears were slightly pointed, and her hair was a deep black that looked like a starless night. ?You're not Dawn.? The woman smiled at him. She moved closer, revealing that under her long coat, she wasn't wearing much more then maybe one of the Pussycat Dolls in their latest video that had been on MTV before he dozed off. ?Nice Dream... yeah.? The woman put her leg up, showing some thigh over her high boots. ?Well, looks like your dreams have come true, huh? So, what is it we'll be having tonight?? Josh shook his head. ?I've heard better lines from girls on Pike Place in Seattle. Can I ask how you got in here?? The woman just looked over at the window that was open to let the mild Atlanta air in. ?Okay, well we're on the third floor. So how about we set straight and get to the point. It's my dream and I don't feel like taking too long of a nap. I've got stuff to do.? She laughed a bit. ?Yes, the little human boy trying to become like us. How has your research been since that bitch of a vampiress turned you down?? That shook him a bit. As far as Josh knew, no one else knew about his talks with Shadow. That had to prove that this was a dream. It had to be coming from his subconscious. He was worried about anyone finding out about it. ?Well, I'm still trucking along. What's it to you? Dreams have a meaning. Why not just get around to it. Don't make any of my work harder.? The woman shook her head this time. ?Impulsive, impatient. You don't like to wait? Well, that would mean that Ms. Vlassy's denial must really hurt. Have you found another way yet? Have you found another solution to your desires?? Josh wasn't getting any more amused then he had been. ?If I had, would I be dreaming of someone coming to rag on me about it?? That made her smile. She leaned forward, draping her arms around his head. Driving his head almost into her ample cleavage. ?Well, there could be an easy solution to what you are looking for. And I might be able to help you find it.? Josh's mood changed a bit. ?Okay? Fill me in.? ?Pushy? She pulled back from him. ?Yes, I am. I'm running out of time. I'm not getting any younger. And everyday, my mortal life slips a bit more. While Dani continues to look forward to a very, very long life. If I'm to be with her forever, then I have to be able to join her for that life.? The woman was unimpressed. ?Well, I can see why she'd maybe want to be with you. You're quite a hottie. But why would you want to live with that cow forever. She's got too much meat on her.? That was not what Josh wanted to hear. ?I'll not answer your question on that. Many men find Dani to be attractive. She's a model, but not some rail thin bimbo looking thing like, say Eva VanLorne. Now, what information do you have for me?? She shook her head and then pulled the laptop open again. She started typing something into the search area provided and hit the enter key for him. ?Here is a hint. And once you've looked it all up, just call me. Just say my name and I'll be there for you to answer more of your questions.? Josh looked at the screen, and saw what she had pulled up. It was an entry on a book, a very old book. The Ars Goetia, first part of a larger collection called The Lesser Keys of Solomon. ?What's this?? ?Your key to your desires. That's what you're looking for. In there, you'll find the hints you need. And of course, I'll help you.? ?Why would you help me?? ?Because, I can. Because the Morning Star wants you to be with his grand daughter.? Josh started to read what was on the screen, he was engrossed with the details presented just there, let alone what would be in the book. A few moments later, he looked up, and saw that she wasn't there anymore. ?Wait... you said that I'd have to just say your name when I was ready, but you didn't...? At that very moment, a small piece of paper floated down from somewhere up above him. It landed upon the keyboard. He picked it up and read the one word that was written down upon it. Layla ?Layla?? He quickly typed the name into the search feature on the electronic library. And it returned... nothing. ?Okay. That was weird. I guess I can wake up now.? What happened next, wasn't him waking up though. The door to his room opened and in walked a man who was much larger then him. Gray hair and eyes showed him quickly that it was his brother, Grayson or just Gray. Gray stood there, and sniffed the air, being quite blatant with it. Josh went to cut a joke. ?What's up man? You fart over there or something?? Gray looked down at him, and didn't look all that amused with his attempt at toilet humor. ?No, but I do smell something. Has Dani changed her perfume latley or something?? ?What do you mean?? ?A smell. Like roses or something. Pretty flowery. It's lingering here.? Just then it hit Josh. He'd actually been smelling it since he'd seen the woman who came to him in this dream. ?I don't smell anything. Must be coming in through the open window. The neighbor's house had those big rose bushes and that, if you remember.? Gray nodded to him. ?So, why are you still awake?? Josh turned the computer off, and tucked the paper with the name away into the pocket of his sweat pants. ?Waiting on Dani to come back. I know she's watching the girls, but I don't think Blaise and Ukyo would take too long.? Gray nodded. ?Get some sleep. Dani will be back when she's back. For all you know she's conked out over in the other room. You need your rest, cause tomorrow it's time in the gym.? Josh looked over at the clock, it was two A.M. ?Shit. Guess I've dozed off a bit as it is. We're hitting there in what, five hours?? ?Yeah, we are. And don't even think about getting out of it. For the first time in over a year, we've not only earned a tag title shot, but we're getting it. We're going into Meltdown with the belts on the line. So you'd better be ready.? Josh and Gray met eye to eye across the room. Josh knew his brother was counting on him to pull up his side of the team. Josh was still a rookie. And his brother had spent many years after dropping that moniker, teaching rookies like him. Now it was time for both of them to finally step up and do what was needed of them. They needed to take the GCW World Tag Team Titles, and put them around their waist. ?I'll be ready, Big Brother. I'll be ready.? Gray winked at his brother. ?You'd better be, Little Wolf. You'd better be. We've faced every team in that ring already. And beaten them. Except for one. We know only that Eva VanLorne has been confirmed out of the three for the other side. The other one? Could be Virgil Ambrosi or Seymour Almasy. I'm more worried if it's Ambrosi then that little runt, but we need to be ready for anything. So make sure you're not trying to pull an all nighter on this project of yours.? Gray went to turn away, but his brother spoke up. ?You don't like Almasy, do you?? Gray stopped and then responded. ?Almasy to me is an example of what I don't want to be in that ring. And outside of the ring? Well, He bugs some people. And his time as Emoticon didn't endear to many people.? Josh then said something very stupid, pretty normal with him. ?Let's not forget that he's gotten further with Dawn then you have.? ?What's that supposed to mean?? Josh puckered up and made a kissing noise. ?First base, a few months ago, on TV. While you have...? Gray was looking to be getting more and more agitated. ?I don't know what you're talking about.? Josh stood up. ?Bullshit. I'm just glad Dani hasn't figured out where your password here comes from. I know it's all great and that, with you allowing us to do our little projects using your research login, but Catharine isn't a really secure password.? ?I don't know what you're talking about.? Josh crossed his arms over his chest. ?Bullshit again. You're a worse liar then I am. Damn scientists don't get much practice, huh?? Gray didn't answer. ?I happened by an article about the World Champion a few days ago. Funny, that her real given name is Dawn Catharine McFarlane, huh? Up until then, I'd been wondering just where the hell you got that name from. You're not hiding anything from me. Why don't you just get it over with.? ?What?? ?Get it over with. Ask her out, dude. You know, be a man?? Gray's amusement was really gone now. ?It's not like that. Get it out of your head, already. Now, stop thinking about your made up love life ideas for other people, and think about how we're going to beat three other teams for those belts, alright?? Josh only nodded to him. Gray opened up a side door and stepped into the small room he was using off of this one. Josh turned around to secure the laptop computer into a stable condition so it would not fall. He then turned off the lights and headed off to his bedroom.
