(L)ast (S)econd (D)ecision

28 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/28/11 [William sat quietly in a recliner, his feet up and resting, while his mind raced. His clothing consisted of a pair of black sweats, and a white wife-beater to go along. His eyes were red, bags beneath showing signs of fatigue and exhaustion, yet he could do nothing but think. There were many things to think about, such as his match against Markus Stone, and the anger which had been built through the pure idiocy of Zip Gordon. His head rolled to the side and he glanced over at another man sitting on a couch beside him.] William: God, I feel like a ton bricks just hit me in the fucking head. [He paused and waited for a response, but there was nothing.] William: Tony--you listening to me? Tony: Wah...huh, what's up? [Tony's eyes slowly parted and drifted over towards the location of William, who began to speak again.] William: I said, I feel like a ton of fucking bricks just hit me in the fucking head! Tony: Hey, it'll be alight. A little LSD never hurt anyone. William: A little? Four fuckin' hits, tony, four! That's not a little LSD, that's a cocktail that'll put you out, and keep you out. Jesus, I feel lucky to even know who I am, because there for awhile, I had NO FUCKIN' CLUE. Motherfucking Zip Gordon, that no-good piece of shit better never take another step in my direction again. "Oh, you need some damn water," I should have suplexed him right then and there, and put his old, scrawny ass in the Sharpshooter. Tony: I don't think I've ever seen you like this, and it's kind of nice. Jesus, I don't think you've even said fuck that many times in our whole time of knowing each other. It's like Zip Gordon turned you into the best friend I've always wanted. Except, the point was to open your mind up, experience new things and enlighten yourself for a change. He meant no harm. William: What the fuck are you talking about? You know, I'm really not in the mood to hear you side with that prick, he poisoned me, Tony, do you understand why I might be a little upset here? Tony: Yeah, I guess--but William: But what? I know you're just dying to get high but there's other things in life, man, so stop acting like this while ordeal has been a good one, and not what it actually is, a fucked up one. Tony: I never said it was good, I just said why Zip may have done it. He felt you're just too straight edged, and not willing to step out of the comfort zone and experience a thing or two. William: Hell, I'm all for experiencing things, but usually at my own discretion. Not being tricked into drinking a bottle of laced water, I mean, who the fuck does that besides some psycho? He's lucky I'm not heading the legal rout, because honestly, I just want to break his hip and force feed him the contents within his shit catheter. Tony: Um, you do realize that makes you the fuckin' psycho, that's just wrong, not to mention downright disgusting. William: Do you have a brain, do your ears function correctly? Jesus, how can your mind just skip through the importance here and ignore the fact that I was violated by a fuckin' 60 year old!? Tony: Because you mentioned force feeding him his own shit. Let's face it, that was an attention grabber. William: Whatever. Tony: C'mon, Bill. Focus on the task at hand. William: Don't call me that jackass. And what the hell are you talking about? Tony: Hello, you're employer's called this morning, and because you were passed the fuck out, I was informed that you've been booked against Markus Stone for WorldWide 154. William: Yeah, I already know. I overheard your loud mouth blabbering to speaker phone. In fact, that's what woke me up in the first place and put me in this shit mood. I just want to sleep, clear my head, and get the rest of this acid out of me. Markus Stone is unfortunately going to receive a lot of anger that's not even directed towards him, but he's going to have to be my punching bag until I can get my hands on Zip. I'm marching my ass into that building and demanding a match against the geezer, and I'm going to get it. Tony: Yeah, if you keep up this attitude up I'm sure you will. William: No, this is just the acid talking. I need to sleep. Tony: Me too, wake me up tomorrow William: Yep. [The two men rise from their positions and go to their respected rooms as the scene slowly fades to black.]
