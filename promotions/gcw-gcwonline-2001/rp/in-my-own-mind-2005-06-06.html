In My Own Mind

6 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 6th [i]As darkness fills the screen, a single light peering through the darkness like a single star in the cloudy midnight sky. As I walk across a open area where the wind wipes against my head, and my hair goes flapping in the wind. To my dismay the dark skys opens up and the once single light illuminates the enter area, and I seem to find myself in the middle of a open field, where I can't see anything for miles in any direction. I stand in one stand pondering where I was, and what I was doing here.[/i] [i]All of a sudden a white flash appears for my eyes, and im blinded by it. I stagger around trying to get my sight back to normal, as I do i feel a tremendous heat coming from around me. As my eyesight returns to normal i finally see that the once peaceful grassy area is now all on fire, and im in thenly spot that hasnt caught fire. As the fire gets closer to me, and the temperature keeps rising, I hear a voice, not just any voice, but a voice from my past that i thought I had lost forever.[/i] Rick, I know you remember this voice, and i know you remember the last time you heard this voice. Im here to tell you that you need to wake up, and rethink what you are doing right now with the life you have left. Dad? Yes, Rick I have come here to tell to wake up Why Dad what did I do? Just wake up son, wake up [i]As I remember the voice which I just heard, my eyes close shut and I can't seem to open then again. Then again a white flash opens before my eyes, and it seems that I am laying face up in the middle of a wrestling ring, with a serious pain in my back, and it keeps shooting up to my neck. When i try to stand up, I can't seem to.[/i] [i with all of my strength I manage to stand up, and begin this interview about my match with one of my good friends who has seemed to have been infected with the same sickness I was when i first got into this business Mike Hardy.[/i] Hardy, I thought I knew you well before, but it seems that you have changed in the short time I was away from the ring, and a good change that was. You have been infected, and that infection is the same one I got 15 years ago when i first started in this business Do you get that tingling sensation on the back of you neck when you smash a steel chair into a huma skull? Do you like the smell and sight of your own blood? Hardy, this week on worldwide I have to face a man I once was a friend with, but now has changed to the better, and that I respect you for. [i]A white light appears from the ceiling and stop right on me, and then a flashback happens when I smashed heads into steel chairs, smashing a kendo stick upside peoples heads, seeing my own blodd run down my face, and finally hitting my finishing move to win the hardcore title for the first time[/i] Hardy, if my memory serves me right you have a slight concussion from that top-rope DDT at Warpath, so if your not at 100% I would not feel like I fought you at your best, and say that I beat the guy who took The Dark Messiah out of GCW So come Worldwide Hardy I will add your name to the list of the men who have gotten into the ring with me, and have fought there best to only get hit by the End of Reality [i]With that said I step out throught the ring ropes, and walk back up the same ramp, as Down with the Sickness plays in my mind.[/i] [b]Fade to Black[/b]
