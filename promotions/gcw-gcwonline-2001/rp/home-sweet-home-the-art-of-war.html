Date: March 3, 1996 Detroit was a fucked up place. It didn't take me long to realize that I couldn't make it as a rapper, just about the only successful career choice you could make around here. I knew I sure as hell wouldn't slave my life away for the Big 3 just so I could drive a Chevy truck and make an honest living. So I had to improvise. If I wanted to make a living, I had to do it with what I knew best. The only way you can make it in this world is doing something you enjoy. If it's shitty work, the money better be good. If it's demanding and time consuming, you better have a trophy prize loyal wife to go home to. If you don't do what you love, you'll crumble and fade away. I saw what had happened to some friends from the 'hood and I realized I couldn't just chop crack the rest of my life. So after football didn't work out. I turned to the only other thing I knew best. I started fighting. For money. March 3, 1996 The local pub was open rather late and the owner wasn't serving anymore booze, however that wasn't going to send the late night regulars home. When Gus called last call, people weren't rushing to the bar for their last couple of Buds. Instead, they were beginning to salivate at the thought of being tonights main event. The rules of engagement were that you had to be over 18 and you couldn't wear any shoes, necklasses, ear rings or use any foreign objects. Fighters were only allowed if they weren't visably dirty. Nobody wanted to fight some bum with aids. It was straight pitfighting. Brock Alyas was in the corner sipping on the same Budweiser he was when he got here. In fact, it was the only Budweiser he could afford after his drug money was "confiscated" by Detroit Police. The last sip of liquid courage was warm and sour. He'd need all that he could get if he wanted to come out of this thing alive. He didn't look like the average 16 year old. He had a full grown beard and weighed nearly 200 pounds. However, he was out-sized by nearly every single other guy in the bar. He wasn't a man yet. But by the end of the night, he would be. Gus locked the doors and turned the bar lights off, opening the cellar in which he used for the local pitfighting league. It was perfect because one of Gus' best friends from childhood was a well respected cop. A dirty, but well respected cop. He made sure that there wouldn't be any interference and there hadn't been so far. Gus had been running this pitfighting league for three years and the ammount of money that was spent on the fights kept the low-rent bar afloat. When the lights shut off, that meant everyone was to head downstairs. Fights usually took five minutes. The best fight ever took fifteen but that was extremely rare and you got your moneys worth if you were there to see it. Brock Alyas made his way through and downstairs. He didn't exactly fit in with the crowd but he kept to himself so nobody would find out he was under age. Surrounded by hard working white-collar men who hated their jobs, Brock was still much a boy compared to these men. However, that didn't scare him away. He needed some money because there wasn't much of a future for a high school drop out in south Detroit. The rules of betting we're simple. There were no side bets, guys weren't even allowed to bet eachother. All transactions we're through Gus' right hand man - Takeo. And you thought twice about fuckin' with Takeo because if you did - you were up against a fucking bear and not only that he had a loaded Glock 45 in his waist line visible to everybody. The fights were usually 50/50 unless somebody had made a name for themselves or the fight was a clear mismatch. Gus made his living off these bets and the bar was just a front and it wasn't hard to tell. The ceilings dripped from the rusty pipes and it smelt like piss and whiskey. This night, there was a rather large crowd. That meant that there would be at least three fights. Which was the equivalent of an HBO pay per view boxing special. Everybody seemed to be having a good time. After all, this was what everybody sweat their bag off all week for. Going home to explain to the wife that they got into another bar fight this week was the worst part of the night. But it all made up for it when you landed that one, huge blow on somebody else. After the fights the bar opened back up for a couple more rounds and the rule was that the loser had the buy the winner his favourite drink. Thankfully, drinks weren't very expensive at this shit hole. Gus didn't put up with no bullshit and he made sure there weren't any sore losers after the fight. If you lost, you lost. You weren't going to be a fuckin' baby and sulk about it or Takeo would put a hot one in your dome. Nobody thought twice about chuckin' you in the Detroit River afterwards either, it was fucking Detroit. "Alrite you mongrel fucks. Welcome to the twenty-third addition of the League. I've been waiting all week for this and I know you sick fucks have been too. Last week, if you missed it - Tommy gave Bobby a fuckin beating from hell. I made a shit load of money off it, too. But you can all forget about it because the ammount of free drinks I gave you leeches made up for it all. Thankfully, it didn't end up costing me much because that whisky was three years old. And I'm not taking three-years aged," Gus' raspy voice was loud and well heard. He was the spokesperson of the League and he didn't put up with any shit. He loved this just as much as the next guy and as much as he'd love to be able to go in there and fight too, he realized somebody needed to remain neutral so that this could all take place. The crowd all burst out into laughter, all except Brock who was here for his first time. "I see that there's a couple first-timers. You guys know the rules, if we don't know you, you'd better do you're fuckin' best to show us who the fuck you are by the end of the night. Without further adeiu, I think the first fight will be Chris against..." Gus looked around the room looking for some new competition. Brock knew he would be the guy to fight Chris, who was a beast of a man. "You." Gus pointed right at Brock. Brock gulped but knew the reality of what was to come. This is why he came after all. The buzz around the cellar was that Chris was going to destroy this guy. "Alrite! No offence, kid but there's going to be 16:1 odds in favor of Chris. You've got five minutes to place your bets with Takeo and fighters you've got five minutes to prepare yourself for the fight," Brock looked across the room at his opponent for the evening, Chris. This guy was no joke. He'd been here since the first addition and he was one of the Leagues most feared competitors. Brock however, tried to remain calm and removed his hoody, t-shirt, Nikes and silver chain before entering the circle which the group of men surrounded. The circle was as big as, as many people had showed up. Tonight the circle was big and Brock figured it'd work in his favour he'd have to try to out-match the beast by using his speed to his advantage. Chris, on the other hand was getting ready for the fight as well. Instead of just removing his clothing, he had a friend inject some HGH directly into his shoulder while his eyes, reduced to slits were starring right through Brock's less than intimidating figure. Brock wasn't the type to complain or bitch. Inside, he was shitting his pants and regretting the decision he made coming here. However, he was here and he couldn't deny that adrenline was pumping. After all bets we're in place, Gus brought both fighters in the middle of the circle for the rules and a handshake. "Alrite scumbags. You know the rules, don'tcha? Anything goes. The only way the fight is stopped is when I fucking say so. Knockout, Submission, Death whatever it is it's up to me and you better fuckin' listen or my boy over there will get trigger happy." Brock looked over at Takeo who had put all the money away in a safe and cocked his Glock 45 letting everybody know what time it was. Brock extended his dominant left hand for Chris to shake and Chris, the seasoned vet around here quickly slapped it before smashing Brock in the jaw with a straight right hand that put him right on his back. The fight was started and the "ring" was screaming in anticipation for the next blow. Brock fell to his back and the taste of copper filled his mouth in the form of blood. He was attempting to shake the cobwebs out of his head but it was no use because the behemoth was in the midst of pounding his head against the floor in the form of a mounted vicious attack of right hands. The mutual conception of the fight was that it was a complete mismatch. This kid stood no chance against one of the Leagues best and not only that, this guy was juiced up and he'd been waiting months for this fight. WHACK THUD WHACK THUD WHACK THUD The sound of Brock getting punched in the face was enough, but the sound of his head slamming against the cement ground after each blow was enough to make you sick. Unfortunately, the crowd continued to egg Chris on which meant hell for Brock. Still trying to collect where he was, the tough son of a bitch in Brock Alyas was trying to peel himself from the cement. Chris gave the kid a minute to recouperate and showboated by walking away from the fight and to get a drink of water. The crowd was loving it and nobody was paying attention to the underdog, who was now on his fight barely shaken the cobwebs out of his head but on the attack. Brock retaliated in the form of a stiff shot right in Chris' breadbasket. The monster doubled over for a second, just enough time for the underdog to land a sickening knee directly in the face of his opponent, immediately breaking his nose and it would've put him on his back had Brock let him go there, instead the use of the Muay Thai clinch prevented Chris from any attempt at recovering. WHACK WHACK WHACK Three sickening knees to the face made Chris' face swell up like a balloon and Brock didn't appear to be anywhere near finished. Thankfully the impact of each knee was enough to ensure Brock wasn't going to get lifted into the air and slammed onto his back. The crowd was in awe of what was happening to their acclaimed "champ". "Aweee FUCK" was heard from the majority of the audience, most of which placed bets on Chris and realized they'd be a little short on rent this month. Brock's last crushing blow to his opponents face was one that put him on his back as Brock let go of the clinch. He was in control of the match and he wasn't about to let his focus slip away for any reason. Calm and calculated Brock waited for his opponent to attempt to get up and that's when he placed a perfect Rinaldhino-esque kick square in the face of Chris whose face was now unrecognizeable. Brock looked over at Gus whose eyebrows were raised nearly to his receeding hairline and waited for him to call the fight. The crowd was enjoying it too much, though and Gus knew that Chris was a tough son of a bitch. "Still not done?" Brock asked. And that's when Brock's favourite move crossed his mind. He'd done this before but never in an organized fight. Usually only when in a street fight or somebody picked a fight with him at school. Chris laid on his back barely moving instead just convulsing most likely due to the affects of the steroids. Brock slowly jogged across the circle and in one fell swoop left both feet, balled his knees into his stomach and delivered the sickest, most ruthless and disgusting blow the League had ever seen. A double stomp to Chris' face. The after affect was Chris' head getting pancaked into the cement and Gus immediately intervened grabbing Brock from Chris' limp body. "Alrite, alrite!" The crowd was in awe. Nobody said a word for two minutes as Brock walked over to Takeo who might have been most speechless of all and asked him for a damp towel that he was in charge of supplying to the fighters. Takeo took a minute to acess the situation and handed him a towel, eyes never leaving Brock. There couldn't have been a single blink in the room for two minutes as the crowd was speechless. The look on Gus' face was priceless as he made his way over to the safe and looked at the debt list. KYLE - 18:1 $40 ON CHRIS JAMES - 18:1 $150 ON CHRIS WAYNE - 18:1 $45 ON CHRIS JUSTIN - 18:1 $75 ON CHRIS ROB - 18:1 $90 ON CHRIS Not a single person bet for the under dog and that meant Gus got paid. $400 exactly for only one fight. "Who wants some old whisky?"
