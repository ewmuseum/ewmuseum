Whacha Lookin' at Willis? (A Holland/Windsor production)

31 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-31-07 A dull haze filled the room, as goblins and ghouls paraded around the area as if a low rate horror movie was being filmed. That was not the case, and the song “Monster Mash” being played by the overweight Frankenstein behind the DJ equipment further solidified the case. This, is what most alcoholics around the world like to call a Halloween party. Slutty nurses with half the cheeks hanging out the back of their skirts stumble around after one two many beers, as inebriated drunks with hospital scrubs on promise to give them a full check up if they allow them to take their temperature. Yeah, things are really screwed up in this world. “You know, you really need to get over that match, man.” Ray Holland lifted his head up off his arm, and looked into the smiling face of his best ? if not his only ? friend and shook his head slightly. “I had that thing won, man. You don't realize what that could have done for me.” “But you won the match, Ray,” he replied with a surprised look on his unshaven mug. “You don't need that title to prove it.” “Need...” he waved the back of his hand when he spoke, as the other wrapped itself around the cold frosty bottle of Budweiser in front of him. “I didn't need it, but I sure as hell wanted it.” “Don't get all Hollywood on me now, bro.” Ray just laughed off the comment as he lifted the bottle and tossed it back, then allowed the contents to empty into the back of his throat. No Halloween party would be complete without the greatest ghoul song ever created being played fifty times before midnight, and as Ray put the bottle down it hit twenty one. “Look man, this is a party, so you need to get off your ass and go dance with one of these half naked girls!” His eyes lit up, his eyebrows lifted up into high arches, and his tongue practically wagged along his jawline as he looked toward the door. “Like that one right over there... Damn!” Ray turned to see what all the commotion was about, only to find the woman he had invited here in the first place had finally arrived. Through the doors, walked Tessa Windsor, who was dressed in a bunny costume that covered a very small portion of her body. Marching in tow came one half of GCW’s tag champions, Chris Storm. His eyes scanned the room not for women, but rather anyone who dared to look at his woman with googly eyes. “Hey Ray!” Tessa called out, lifting her arms up some to ensure Ray would see her. Conveniently, he had already been point in her direction. Though, he hoped that for his friend’s safety that the woman he had been referring to wasn’t Tessa. Ray waved back in return, and motioned for her to head his way. Chris looked at the man as if he were crazy. The last he wanted is for his woman to be squeezing through a crowd packed closer than sardines, when there was an occasional pervert lurking around. Tessa paid his fussing no mind though, and pushed her way through the crowd towards Ray. “Looks like everyone decided to come.” She comments after making a quick scan of the bar, “Oh Ray, this is Chris. I’m sure you’ve probably heard plenty about him around the locker room.” Chris politely extends his hand, but his eyes remain on guard for anyone who dared to stare at the wrong person. “You looking for someone,” Holland asks as he takes Storm's hand and gives it a firm shake. “What,” Chris finally turns his attention to the homeless man shaking his hand, and hopes it's just a costume. “Oh, no, at least I hope not. But you never know with these types of people.” “Right.” Holland turns back to Tessa, mouthing the word 'okay' as he does. “Look, it's a nice night out, and I'd hate to ruin it with shop talk.” He motions around the room. “Feel free to take a seat and enjoy our evening... The drinks are on me, okay.” “You flirting with my girl?” Chris stands between Tessa and Holland, his eyes filled with rage. “No,” he answers, a bit surprised at Storm's accusation. “She's my partner at Devil's Night and I just wanted you two to have a good time. No harm no foul, man.” Chris turns to Tessa, “You sure he wasn't flirting with you?” “Chris, will you stop it.” She playfully slaps Storm on the chest. “Don't mind him, he's always like this.” “Don't worry about it,” Holland replies with a laugh. “Just make sure he stays out of trouble, cause there's always someone in this place looking for some.” “I will,” Tessa responds, “and thanks for the drinks.” “No problem,” Ray says with a toast of the bottle of beer now in his hands. “Damn, hot as she is...” Sam the bartender said with a smack of his teeth. “That boys gonna end up getting killed.” “Yeah, well let's just make sure it doesn't happen tonight, okay?” Ray shakes his head as he sits back down on his stool, hoping he can make sure it happens. “What the hell are you looking at?” Of course, that may end up being harder than it sounds. “Here we go,” Ray spins around on the stool and rushes over to cut the head off the snake before it strikes, the first, but surely not the last, time this evening.
