Sins of the Father

25 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25 July, 2008 Dorian Ryan was walking into the Minneapolis based "headquarters" for No Mercy Enterprises. He’s carrying with him something that has become almost a trademark of his during his wrestling career, a black baseball bat. "Whoa there, killer, where you going with that?" Andrea has come to become almost like a receptionist for the NME. "Just bringing it back." "Now, honey, you know I don’t like it when you take dangerous jobs." Dorian just grins at this sentiment. Wyatt hadn’t come back from their latest job, protecting a rather large cash transaction, making sure that the man that received the cash got to the airport alright. "You know me, I just have to make sure that I carry protection." Dorian continues to walk and looks out a window that’s in the office, down into what the NME are calling "the pit." Two motorcycles, two cars and an SUV were all parked there. There was also a series of black lockers. That’s where they kept their other toys. Dorian enters the pit through an office door, descending the stairs. The steel that are on the heels of his street boots make a clank with every step down the steel stairway. "Honey, I think you should know that your brother called." Andrea yelled through one of the windows. Dorian knew what it was about. A special project that he was being asked about. He didn’t want to know what all it was going to be about. He walks up to the lockers, opens it up and places the baseball bat inside. He then pulls his shirt off, and tosses it into a basket that is sitting next to a washer and dryer. "Well, well, well." Dorian turns around and sees a man that he hadn’t seen in years. "So, this is what my son does for a living now? Beat people up." "Eat it, Glenn. I’m not your son." Glenn Ryan washed his hands of Dorian when he was real young. Instead of being a father to his youngest son, he sent him off to live with his sister and her husband. "What did I do to you, Dorian, that made you so angry?" "Gee, Glenn, where should I start? You sent me to North Carolina to live with my sister and brother-in-law because you couldn’t have your half-white, half-Samoan bastard son hanging around. You all but disowned me when I decided to follow my older brother into the wrestling industry, and when I got married the first time, you ignored the invitation. "I think that might just cover everything." "Listen, son, I was?" "Save the “I was just trying to save my marriage” speech. I heard it all before. Jason was wrong about you, old man. You aren’t worth anything. You drive away your kids because they don’t happen to live the same blue collar life that you lived. "Sorry to be a disappointment to you, “DAD”, but I had better things to do than waste away in a plant, wondering whether or not I was going to be laid off." "Hey, I made mistakes. Your brother forgave me. Frank forgave me. Why can’t you?" "You weren’t even at Sam’s funeral. You were too busy getting it on with a flight attendant to even care that your own daughter was being laid to rest." Dorian pulls a work shirt out from the locker and puts it on. "You want to see my family. Look up in that office. That woman, I would die for. Go to the Kawahara temple in Japan and take a look at the graves of Keiko and Dan. Or you can wait a few minutes and Wyatt will be here. "Family isn’t about love, it’s about loyalty. You don’t have any, old man. Why don’t you just get lost, and leave me alone." Dorian starts to make his way back towards the office, his father hot on his heels. "Dorian, I have cancer." That announcement from his father made Dorian stop in his tracks just as he started to climb the stairs. "Then why don’t you just die already." The years of acrimony between Glenn and Dorian don’t fade away with his father’s saying that he’s pretty much going to die. "You threw me away. Why should I care about you?" "Do you think that if I had a choice, that I would have sent you to live with your sister?" "You did have a choice, old man. You made the wrong one over and over and over again. You weren’t there when I needed you. You weren’t there when the kids in school called me a mongrel." "I’m sorry." "No, you’re not. You just want to make amends before you die, that way you can enter heaven with a clear conscience. It’s not going to work. You have a lot more work to do to be forgiven. Saying sorry isn’t going to be enough." "Yes, you’re right. I came, because when I die, I want to have my family, both you and Jason there. I know that you are going to have a hard time ever forgiving me for what I did to you, but I ask that you try." "You ask for too much." "Son, what do I need to do to you to prove that I’m a different man?" "Take back what you said to your wife when the two of you talked about sending me to live with Sam and Frank. Take back the fact that you called me a mistake, that I was nothing more in your eyes than a fucking mutt. Take them back and mean it." "I’I didn’t know you heard that. I never meant those words. I was drunk, and I was angry. There is no excuse for what I said that night. There is no excuse for me pushing you away." Dorian turns and looks towards his father as the sound of a large metal door closing can be heard. "It’s still not good enough. You had all these years to make amends for what you said, and you didn’t. You had opportunities to try and be a family, and you didn’t. You see, the cancer isn’t going to be what kills you, it’s that emptiness that you have inside you because you were a piece of shit father, a piece of shit husband and a piece of shit human being." Dorian hadn’t noticed that during what he just said, a tear came to his father’s eye. And that Wyatt Colton and Araceli Negra had found their way behind him. Araceli just looked at Dorian in disbelief as he walks back into the office. His father turns and looks. "Very funny, isn’t it?" Glenn said as he looked at Wyatt. "No, sir." Wyatt responds with a quiet respect. "It’s not funny at all. Listen, I don’t know you. Hell, I still don’t know Dorian all that well. But if it makes you feel a little better, I thought that you were sincere in what you said to him." "Thank you." "I’m Wyatt, and this is Araceli. We’re?.um?.associates of your son’s." "I’m Glenn. I’m just a tired old man that wants to make amends for all that I’ve done wrong in my life." Andrea comes down the stairs. She looks at Glenn and notices some similarities between him and Dorian. "Andrea, can you get Dorian’s dad something to drink." "Yes, Wyatt, of course." Andrea walks over to the refrigerator that they have in the Pit and pulls out a bottle of water. She opens it as she walks back and hands it to Glenn. "Here you go, sir. We never met, but I’m Andrea, Dorian’s wife." "Thank you, young lady." "Please, call me Andrea. Sit." "What did Dorian tell you about me?" "He never talked about you." "Well, that doesn’t surprise me. That’s something that I unfortunately passed down to him. You must believe me when I say I don’t want to hurt him or cause any problems." "I know. But you don’t know what Dorian’s been through the last few years. He lost Keiko and Dan, and he nearly lost me. I was having problems, and it took all of his strength, determination and warmth to bring me back." "Jason told me a little about what happened." "You need to know, then, that Dorian isn’t the same young kid that he was when you sent him away, but in many ways, he still is as well." "I never meant to hurt him. That’s why I didn’t go to Sam’s funeral, that’s why I didn’t go to his wedding to Keiko. I knew that even though I should have been there, that my being there would cause more problems than it would have solved. "I made my mistakes, and every day I pray to god to give me one more day just to try and fix them. So far, God’s been on my side." "Glenn, give it time, Dorian some of Dorian’s scars will heal." "Hopefully. I also hope that he feels like I didn’t move to Minneapolis to try and invade into his life." Wyatt perks his head up. "Well, I doubt that he’s not going to see it that way. I have taken enough superkicks from that man to know that he doesn’t always see things the way we want him to." "Well, Andrea, can you do me a favor." Glenn hands Andrea a card. "That’s the number at the place that I’m staying. I’m now a patient at the Mayo Clinic. Give him that number." "I will." Glenn gets back to his feet and starts to walk towards the door. Before he makes it there, the door to the street from the pit starts to open up. "Mr. Ryan, let me give you a lift." Araceli shouts. Glenn turns and walks back towards where one of the cars is parked as Dorian looks on from the office. He turns away from the window as a tear starts to stream down his eye. He then regains his composure and takes a look at a file that was on Andrea’s desk.
