We are counting on you." * * * Act I – "Dr. Kasidy Williams Drake Comeback Tour" Chapter One – "Begin Again" Written by [name withheld] I've become a creature of the night in recent years. I realize that sunlight is better for the body, but for my part, I enjoy the stillness that only comes in the dark. When the sun is alive in the sky and beaming down on the world, the streets become crowded with humans and their sneakers and high heels conquer the concrete like small armies. I can see them from my window sometimes, rushing by one another trying their best to forget that each other exists. They chatter on their cellular phones and slurp at their coffee cups bustling this way and that. Some have somewhere to be and others don't but they all clamor for that sunlight and flood the streets packed together like sardines. I can see it in their eyes; how they trust for the illusion of purpose and the confines of structure and I marvel at how eager everyone has become to fill their days with as many distractions as they can to hide from the obvious truth. Life is fleeting and there may be some higher meaning to our short existence here in this world that makes it all worth it but chances are, there probably isn't. We're just here. I can't be around them anymore-- other people, I mean. Their minds are always lost in something that doesn't really matter and they lie. Everybody lies (and usually, to themselves). If I have to spend the time that I have left immersed in anyone's lies, I'd rather they just be my own. Life is complicated enough just dealing with one's own mind and intricacies-- why spend it constantly exposed to everyone else's. For these reasons, I've retreated to the night and spend my days sleeping lost in better worlds that can't seem to escape my dreams. It's quieter here and now under the moonlight. It's peaceful. I like it here. There was something that made me feel small about being the lone geek in a room somewhere looking through a microscope while others were sleeping or at play. It was like I was thirteen again putting dustbunies under the lense to keep myself distracted from the rumblings and screaming coming from my parents room as they argued and thre furniture at one another. I remember feeling so powerless back then. Seeing my mothers black eyes and bruised arms the next day haunted me at school when I was young. I wanted to figure out how to fit in with the other kids and know what it was like to have a friend I could confide in about the reason I was always falling asleep in class or why I sat alone at lunchtime. The beauty of microbiology as that you can get lost in the infinite smallness of things. You can see how the little pieces become big pieces and the big pieces become the world as we see it with the naked eye. The nights I spent over the microscope as a teenager made it easy to go to another place in my mind and to another world where things sucked just a little less. Science is a good way to block out how crummy people can be in this world. For years I had strived to perfect this serum. I didn't want to perfect it for glory or thanks but for the unmistakeably unique passion in any scientist to know. To know if it can be done or if it can't be done. To know why either outcome becomes a reality. I only had a few more months at most to live. My death certificate had been pre-certified and signed for by my own hand. By the very intriciate series of calculations I had spent years on to perfect my dissertation. I had wanted to help the world but instead I had helped so few. Hurt so many. If I could just connect the dots in this blood sample I could make it all right again. The deaths will still be there, but maybe I could quench that unquenchable thirst my scientific mind has. To find the answer. The truth. All of a sudden, everything changed. It was like I was looking at myself in some kind of funhouse mirror. Panic began to arise but just as I was about to scream for help, it was all gone. Everything became blurred and distorted again and a feel of revlusion passed through me like I'd suddenly gotten seasick. Just before I could begin to keel over though, everything clicked back into place. Stone Cold Kasidy Drake stands in the middle of the ring staring silently at his enemy as he comes out onto the stage with a $500 shirt and his signature sunglasses on.:: And again somewhere in the back of my mind I know that none of this is right. None of this could be right. I felt like I was watching all of this unfold before me like a television show but in these brief moments of lucidity, I couldn't seem to do anything about it but look in horror as the universe seemingly melted around me as if it were made of butter. What's more, something seemed ever-more disturbing and ominous about this Handler character. Something horrifying. No one seems to notice-- they're cheering him! If only I could warn them before I-- Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, making his way to the ring from the Other Side-- THE HANDLER! It clicks again somehow that things are not the way they should be just as the curtain parts and this mysterious being makes his appearance. "What the hell is this?" I exclaimed into the microphone. Then, wondering why I was still verbalizing innermost questions that I was pondering into a microphone, I dropped it and tried to turn away from the entrance ramp when suddenly, everything was gone. The world was nothing more than a pure white light. A figure stood before me. He was a black male with abysses for eyes. "Hello Dr. Drake," he said to me in the most cordial of fashions. I gasped and wondered why the world around me had melted away. Wondered where I was. If I had died and this is that the afterlife was like. "Don't be startled. Sorry for the theatrics before but I wanted to show you how the e-wres... how your world used to be." "Who the hell are you?" I demanded, but he continued on as though I had said nothing. "Things were so much simpler back then," he lamented. "Kasidy, I am your creator. The world you live in is, in my world, but a lense that we gaze upon. We create you. We mold you. But I have made the gravest of errors Kasidy, and I am sorry. You were to be so much more than you are now. But I made the error of giving you free will. You have destroyed your life with it." In the midst of my petrification and what was going on I still managed to somehow laugh. My creator? "You are determined to spend your dying days immersed in science," he continued. "You think that you've put that chapter of your life behind you. The one within the squared circle. I've come to tell you that you've chosen incorrectly Kasidy. The world will not remember you as some great scientist." Suddenly, a flashback. Suddenly, I'm Smiley again in Core Wrestling, at the peak of my career dismantling opponents in the ring. Suddenly I'm that guy being cheered on by the crowds and idolized by the children although behind my mask I'm crying. In my mind I know that I am destroying my body by using myself as my own test subject. A test subject for the Prometheus Serum. A non-steroid that increases the potency and flow of adrenal secretions. My masterpiece. All in the name of science. Yeah, right. I see a girl in the distance. A petite angel with a face the shape of a china down and just as delicate-looking. She smiles at me. Oh, how I miss that smile. "Cassie..." I utter in the midst of my apparent hallucination. "Cassie, I'm sorry..." I see myself as I created a medicinal empire on the back of my miracle drug that, though incomplete, I marketed to the mass public. I see the tombstones of the people who died because they took that drug in the name of my perverse research. I remember pouring over the notes of the obituary Llevar Kintu, a wrestler I managed and promised the world to from Sierra Leone. I remember how I told him it was safe. I remember having to break the news to his family that he had died under my watch in a strange country he'd never known. And the part that disgusts me is that I remember thinking it was all worth it. Just to get the data and perfect my magnum opus. My Mona Lisa. What if we could make even the weakest and most pathetic of physical specimens like myself into real life Hulk's. What if we could make the slowest man on Earth the fastest one with just an injection. What if. Was it really worth it? Just for that what if? Cassie, dead. Llevar dead. Alexander Strider, dead. All for a what if. Suddenly, I snap out of my daze knowing that my daydream had been induced by this man. He looked at me very seriously. "This is how the world will remember you Kasidy," he said to me squarely and mirthlessly his words like cold ice. "As a drug addict. As a murder. As a user. A failure who started with nothing and ended with nothing." "You're wrong," I replied trying not to let him see that I was angry. "I've done my bad deeds and I've done my bad. I'm trying to use the time that I have left to make as many things square as I can. That's all any man can do. I've done the best that I can and I think I can go peacefully like that. And like you and your so called 'handler' friends are any different. You screw with people's lives, kill people, abandon living people that you suposedly created because you get bored with them. But what are any of you doing that makes you any better than someone like me?" He took pause at this argument for a moment and looked as though he was mulling it over. I felt smuggly satisfied to make this arrogant little man who'd gone to such lengths to make himself seem so grandiose. The rational side of my brain still couldn't believe I was considering any of this as being true. After a moment, he looked up and gazed into my eyes with an icy seriousness about him. "Your world needs you Kasidy," he said. "More than I can tell you now. You've always used science as an escape. A way to disconnect. When I gave you free-will, I wanted you to realize that that is what you were meant for. Not wearing a suit behind a desk somewhere trying your best to maintain your reputation as a legal drug dealer. Give this forumula up now Kasidy. It will destroy everything around you." I gazed at this man knowing that even though he spoke in riddles, he was telling truth somehow. At least, whatever hallucination I was having wasn't about a liar. I kept pinching myself trying to awaken but somehow got carried away with the dream. For whatever reason, some part of me wanted to believe it. "I'm offering you another chance Kasidy," he said in a low voice. "In my world, not too many men get second chances, but I put my soul into crafting who you are Kasidy. That's what we do on the Other Side-- we make up the people we wish we were. Our characters speak in the voice we wish we could speak saying the things that we wish we could say. Messed up as your life may seem to you, I wish that I were you. I wish that my creator would come down from wherever and give me another chance at glory." I scoffed. There he was again, pumping theories and speech up to be something way more serious than the situation called for. Now he was talking of abstract dribble like creators and glory. A good part of me just felt like someone going along with a dream that they'd had lucidly. Might as well have fun while I was here, right? "Listen to me Kasidy," the man sighed. "When you wake up, you're going to know that I'm telling the truth--" I cocked an eyebrow. "So what, you read minds too?" I said with a hearty belly laugh. "You're really something, aren't you? I've got to lay off the cheap vodka. Or, drink more. Either way, I'm good." "Just promise me that you'll give it your all if you're given this second chance." "Yeah, whatever." "Is that a yes?" "Yes." Beep! Beep! Beep! Beep! It was morning. 6 am. I creeped out of my slumber and rolled over wondering why my clock was set for the morning. And then I say her, exiting the bathroom, her scarlet hair bouncing slightly as she walked into my view. My love. "Kas', come on, I already hit the snooze for you once," she said with half-hearted exasperation. "We've got to get to the airport-- or do you WANT to miss your comeback match?" I gazed at her dumbfounded. I knew that it couldn't be real, but I wanted it so badly to be. "One of these guys is called Eddie Whiskey," she laughed as she threw me my pants from atop the dresser. "Maybe if we make it into town soon enough we can catch him during happy hour, buy him a few rounds and avoid the whole thing altogether. I mean, you can probably beat the crap out of both of them, but why should my little K-bear have to break a sweat? Now come on, get dressed. I know your holding out for morning sex, but not today baby." She laughed, and I reslished the familliar sound of her childlike cackle. Then she noticed that I wans't laughing back and cocked an eyebrow at me. "What's the matter?" she said with a suspicious smirk. "You look like you've seen a ghost. All the more reason to get. Out. Of. Bed. Sleepyhead." And with that she lef the room in all of her glory. I sat there under the white quilt totally at a loss. For a moment, the room started spinning, I was so confused and disoriented. Just what the hell was going on here? I hadn't wrestled professionally since 2008. My fiance Cassidy DeMoore has been dead since 2005.
