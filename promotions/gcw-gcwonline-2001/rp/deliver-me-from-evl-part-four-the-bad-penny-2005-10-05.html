Deliver me from EVL : Part Four- The Bad Penny

5 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/5/2005 Hmm? Oh its you again. Here for another story? I don't know. I am actually kind of busy at the moment. I have a match to prepare for in Prime Time Central. The closer it gets the more I need to train and focus. ....Look perhaps tomorrow evening or something? Okay, okay...fine have a seat and let me grab a drink while I collect my thoughts on it all and figure out where to take you today. ...... ..... ...... Okay, I am back now. Let's see....have I told you about Rolthar? No? Good. I think you will enjoy this one as he is a rather..interesting fellow to say the least. I had been in that world for going on about four months when I first crossed this man's path. Like everything else, it wasn't the first time he had crossed paths with Eve, in fact he seemed to have a nasty habit of turning up at the most inopportune times. This happened to be one of those times. You see my men and I had been traveling toward the south in pursuit of a small contingent of Jordius's army in hopes of hijacking the supplies they were taking to his southern outposts. Rains from the past three days had caused a land slide in the canyons ahead stopping us from further pursuit until the men could clear the path well enough for us to get through. My first suggestion had been to simply go around the mountain but Cedric quietly informed me that to go around would lose us a week which was not something we could afford if we still hoped to be successful. As a result I had given the order to clear a path wide enough to get through which should only take two days time. Two days could easily be caught back with a hard pressed march. As they worked I decided to take the liberty to explore this area wanting to know more of my new home. In my exploration I discovered a small hot spring only a few hundred yards off to the east. It had been so inviting I could not resist taking a dip in the hopes to cleanse my body and soak the aches and pains away. My clothes had been deposited on the smooth rock surface and I had gingerly stepped in allowing my form the time to adjust to the heat. It was a heavenly feeling. I had perched myself on an under water shelf leaning back to allowing a jet stream of the heated water to lightly pound on sore lower back muscles. For a moment in time I actually found I didn't want to leave that place. I had begun to realize I felt more alive and useful there than I ever had in my life here. Kind of ironic don't you think? A self realization moment in one's life is a rare and rather disturbing experience more often than not. Have you ever had one? My apologies I got off track. As I said I was thoroughly enjoying the soak when my little slice of heaven suddenly shattered bringing me back down to earth. My eyes had drifted closed and when they reopened my clothes were being dangled off the end of a sword in front of my face. Startled I instinctively snatched them only to find them lifted out of my reach as a dark chuckle sank into my senses. I froze for a second every recently relaxed muscle suddenly clamping down painfully as I tightened my jaw and slowly sank further beneath the water to hid my anatomy and turned to see whom it was that had happened upon my private moment. When my eyes fell upon the culprit for a brief moment I could have sworn I was looking at Rich Rollins. This man was a spitting image. ' Rich?' He merely perked a brow before laughing out loud and shaking his head as he deposited my clothes out of my reach. Crouching down he placed his sword across his lap and tilted his head raking my form as best he could. ' So....it is true. You have lost your memory. Well that is a shame.' At that moment I knew this was not Rich Rollins. Narrowing my gaze I tilted my chin defiantly. ' Give me my clothes you cad.' He chuckled. ' Or what Eve?' Or what indeed? It wasn't like I could kill him to get to them. I was unarmed and naked. Two strikes against me. This man held all the cards in this sick little game. ' Who are you? What do you want?' He said nothing at first merely standing to sheath his sword before settling back down to look at me inquisitively. The way he was studying me made me uncomfortable. It felt as if he were staring straight into my soul, as if he could see past the shell that was Eve's body and into the true entity that was housed within. It was a frightening experience. ' You seem...different somehow.' ' Lots of things change Rol...' He smiled. ' Rol...you haven't called me that since that night.' What night? ' What night?' ' Why the night you attempted to seduce me Eve. Don't you remember? You wore that slinky little red number that barely hid your assets.' Red? Euw, I hate red and from what I had been told so did Eve. Maybe this was the reason. Lord knows the man was not a refined male as evident from his scraggly appearance and rough demeanor. ' You already established I lost most of my memory. Of course I do not remember.' The smile he gave me was enough to make my skin crawl seeking solace from his rather sickening display of checking me out. ' Ah, Eve, that is too bad, We were quite the pair in bed.' ' I thought you said I attempted to seduce you...' ' You did. I turned it on you though my dear and seduced you instead. I must say I have never regretted it either. You sang for me beautifully. I played your body like a finely tuned musical instrument. I--' ' You are lying!' I had snapped at him. Something buried deep in my dislike of Rollins had come through with this man. Somehow I knew Eve would never have given herself to this....beast. He chuckled. ' You are right I am. It seems though your mind does not remember your body would know none the less.' ' Some things are even beyond the power of the mind. Now give my clothes.' He turned and looked at my clothes pensively for a moment before turning to look at me. ' I could do the gentlemanly thing and do that...but then again, I have never been a gentleman.' He stood up and back up ten paces and merely waved me to my clothes. ' By all means Eve..get dressed.' I was stunned. He seriously didn't think I was going to get out of that hot spring naked and dress in front of him did he? Apparently he did. I won't lie my cheeks were probably heated with embarrassment. I struggled with the urge to merely stay in that hot pool until I was nothing more than a prune to keep him from having the satisfaction of putting me in such a position. The look in his eyes made me want to scratch them out. He was enjoying this little scenario and I was really in no place to stop him from doing so. Finally I relinquished my pride and stared him directly in the eyes. Slowly I stood up the water sliding over my form to join the pool circling my waist as I boldly walked to the edge and climb out. Never removing my gaze from his I bent down and snagged up my clothes hurriedly pulling on my blouse and latching it shut. As I bent to grab my pant pulling them on I had made the mistake of moving my gaze off of him. He took that moment to slither up behind me like a serpent. When I straightened I could feel his breath on my neck. ' You are still a beautiful woman Eve. It is really too bad you are allied to my nemesis. We could do great things together.' I slowly turned my head to look him in the eyes. ' I am sure we could in your fantasy world.' He grinned and gently moved my hair off my neck. ' You wound me with such venom my dear. Why not reconsider?' ' There is nothing to reconsider.' ' You know I could kill you...' This time I grinned. ' And yet you haven't. What is stopping you then Rol....' He grinned deviously and stepped back. ' I hate wasting beautiful things. I will only play this little game for so long Eve, sooner or later I will tire of it and you will end up on the business end of my blade or a bullet.' I turned to him fully and grinned back with my biggest saucy grin. ' And here I thought you liked games. In fact...' I stepped up to him crowding him. ' Let's play a new game.' His brows rose with intrigue. ' What game is that?' ' There one where you take an involuntary bath.' Before he could react I shoved with all my might while dropping a foot behind his ankle depositing him unceremoniously into the hot pool. I had to cover my mouth to keep from laughing so hard it hurt my still healing wound. When he came sputtering to the top the look on his face was murderous for only a breath before he grinned devilishly. ' Touche Eve.' ' CAP'N!!' My head whipped in the direction of the sound of Cedric's voice. ' OVER HERE CEDRIC!' I turned back with a grin to play with Rolthar again only to frown. He had vanished. Immediately I searched the area on the other side of the pool with my eyes seeking his presence. I only found wet boot prints leading into the dense jungle beyond. The sound of twigs snapping behind me had me spinning around again to spy the arrival of Cedric. ' Ye okay Cap'n?' ' Aye, Cedric I am fine. I merely found this hot spring and wanted to bathe.' He nodded while staring hard at my face. Somehow he knew I was hiding something. ' Eve...ye look like ye've seen a ghost.' Turning to stare at the wet path quickly evaporating away from the heat. ' Something like that Cedric...' We headed back to camp again. That night when I tried to sleep I could not shake the scene with Rolthar from my mind. The man was like a ghost. He merely appeared then disappeared so to speak. What was to stop him from doing the same in my tent and this time killing me? Needless to say I would begin losing sleep after that day with the constant threat of Rolthar on my mind. Eventually we would meet up again, but as with the others, I will save that story for another day. I am late for my previous engagement. You are welcome to come by again soon. Perhaps next time I will have more time on my hands and tell you of Eve and Lleyz'th. Have a good night.
