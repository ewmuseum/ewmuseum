Date: Timeless Draven liked it best when it was all quiet. His mansion was still, all the waitstaff were gone for the day. He was sitting in his study. The room had three walls covered in books, the third was occupied by a large desk. He had just got home from Worldwide 53. He knew Claudia was standing there looking at him, leaning on the door frame. 'Did you bring me anything?' He turned to face his beautiful wife. She was wearing a pair of very short cotton shorts and a tank top with her hair pulled back out of her face, a rarity. 'I brought you the well wishes of the McClancy's. They were really disappointed that you weren't with me. But they understand.' Claudia entered the room fully, walked over to Draven and sat in his lap, running a finger down his chest. 'Did you find anything while I was gone?' Claudia stuck out her bottom lip and pouted. 'Nope. The only thing I could find is that my mother last lived in London before dying but I already knew that, why else would I have ended up in a London orphanage?' Claudia was on a search for her real parents, wanting to know that she was better than the asshole that had adopted her. 'Don't worry, Claudia. We'll be going back to England soon and I'll help you find out who your mother was. Her pouty lip went away and she smiled. 'How did it go?' Now it was his turn to smile. 'You would have loved it. I used your weapon of choice.' A bloodthirst flashed in Claudia's eyes. 'What the hell happend?' 'Let me show you.' Draven walks over to his bag on the floor and grabs a dvd out of it. He places it in the computer and finds the right chapter. Ukyo moves in like a tiger on a fallen gazelle and boots Sombra hard in the side, so hard that it earns her the ire of a few fans at ringside. She boots him again, with a spiteful glare in their direction, which only makes it worse, of course. She pauses in her assault to give them a few nasty asides, and that sparks an argument. Ukyo takes a couple of threatening steps closer to the fans, and the security guards have to step up to keep the fans from diving over the barrier at her. Ukyo, disgusted, rolls her eyes and turns away. She goes to boot Sombra again, but suddenly... Sombra grabs her leg and twists, taking Ukyo by surprise and hurling her unbalanced body to the floor! The fans let loose a mighty cheer, as Sombra wobbles to his feet. With a little viciousness in his own gestures, he reaches down to grab Ukyo by the hair. The fans are cheering him on, as it finally looks like Sombra will get a little vengeance for all this ill treatment. But as he starts to pull her up to her feet, suddenly the same black-hooded figure from earlier slides easily over the barrier and raises his shinai. The crowd starts yelling in warning, but Sombra turns around too little, too late, and gets NAILED right in the forehead with a shinai. The crowd, recognizing him from earlier, sets up another loud chorus of boos. ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Vicious shots to the ribcage with the shinai rain down on Sombra. The man is ruthless, not giving Sombra a chance to even recover from the first shot before another rains down. The man pauses for a moment and slowly reaches up to his hood, pulling it back. A wicked grin comes over his face as he raises the shinai as high as he can... and brings it down mercilessly on La Sombra with a crack that echoes to what seems like the entire arena. His hood falls down around his sholders revieling a sight that none would expect. Bryan: Is that? Yale: Holy Crap, the widowmaker, the innovator of violence! Childress: That is a multiple time hardcore or gimmick title champion, former TCW Champion, a division of the soon closing UWF. A former member of the UWF, GIWF, PWW, SCWF and 4 Winds Wrestling. That is Draven Lox. Bryan: Also an original member of the Knightmare Brigade! Childress: Yes, you have to go to my inlaws to get someone who's been in the KB longer then Mr. Lox here. Draven hits pause on the computer. 'So what do you think?' Claudia grinned. 'Well you do know how to make an entrance, don't you?' 'Of course I do. I couldn't let him do that to Ukyo. She said your ass better be there next time, by the way.' 'Oh she did, did she?' Draven grabbed Claudia and set her in his lap. 'She did. They were excited to see me but the first thing they asked was where you were. You know you have a family with them right? I know you want to find out who your mother was but don't forget that the KB has been more of a family to the both of us than any flesh and blood.' She leaned back into him, resting her head on his chest. 'I know but something is telling me that I really need to know who she was. I don't take the KB granted for a second, though. I can't wait to see Maeb and Ian.' 'You can see them next week, I'm already booked in a hardcore match. Against a new guy like me.' 'Blaise worked fast didn't he?' Draven kissed the back of her head. 'Yes he did, and now that I'm back in the game, all hell is going to break loose.'
