Newly Hired... Now where? (To anyone; Clyde Mentioned)

7 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/07/11 * Once again, the little handheld camera that could lights up. Toga's ugly mug is shown staring straight into the camera, from what appears to be an office building. He closes a door, with fancy lettering on it that says "GCW Commissioner Garb ge Bag Jo nny"..... it might be missing some letters. Toga's smarter half, Lollie's voice is once again heard from behind the camera. * Lollie: "That was surprisingly painless...... yet verrrrry awkward.... Especially that part where he asked us to sit on his lap..." * Toga picks a wedgie out from below his skirt giving any unlucky viewers from behind him an..... well, an unlucky view. * Toga: "Well, you were a party pooper... you refused to sit on his lap.... even refused to sit on mine!" Lollie: "Yeah.... I don't think we've known each other long enough to play Santa... Which reminds me, how long has it been?" * Toga scratches his empty lil' head. * Toga: "Um, he said something about Rome... so I suppose, at least 60 years..." Lollie: "No, not the... 'holiday'.... which would be thousands of years, by the way... but I was asking how long have we known each other?" * Once again, the scratching from atop the head commences, as he mumbles to himself, trying to do the math. * Toga: "Hm... subtract the 5... carry the 8..... divide by bananas... add a pony.... Mmm... pony......." * A sigh can be heard from behind the camera. " Toga: "I'd say about 46 years...." Lollie: "We aren't even that old.... Try again hun...." * He frowns, then starts drawing numbers and math tables up in the air... * Toga: "Subtract the pony... aww... pony.... multiply by-" Lollie: "Forget it... it's been 8 years." Toga: "That's what I said!" * Lollie passes the camera to Toga. * Lollie: "Yeah... you were real close.... Anyway, we should go celebrate your new job!" * The camera shakes up and down as a rattling can be heard behind it... probably just Toga's brain bouncing around as he nods. * Toga: "So, what do you think he thought of me?" Lollie: "Well, he had you sit on his lap. Tell him if you got everything you wanted for... 'Saturnalia'... then gave you a 2-month contract, to see how you'd connect with the GCW audience. Then he called you stupid..." Toga: "DID NOT! He said I was intelli... um... intelli.... intelligentle!" Lollie: "Well actually he said that your intelligence is only matched by your stupidity." Toga: "Which means I'm intelli........ whatever that word was." * Lollie rolls her eyes. * Toga: "I'm intelligentleable!" * She pats him on the head and motions for him to walk with her. The duo starts walking down the halls of the GCW corporate office. The little handheld that could is still in the mitts of the klepto. * Lollie: "So, he asked you to think about who you'd like to face off against first. Have you decided?" * Toga passes the camera back to Lollie. * Toga: "I don't know. Everyone is so mean here." Lollie: "It is wrestling. It's an industry built on violence. You weren't expecting puppy dogs and rainbows were you?" * With that, the child-like mind of Toga soars off into imaginationland with the puppy dogs and rainbows Lollie mentioned. She sighs, realizing she just sent him on wonderful cruise for the next few minutes. * Lollie: "Anyway... normally, I'd suggest challenging the women... but they are ultra vicious here...." * Toga's eyes are still glazed over, as he continues going through the motions of walking down the hall with zero thought involved in the reality around him. * Lollie: "I suppose you could always challenge another newcomer here... but that's dangerous, as you wouldn't be able to scout them... except for the Bradley Cruz jackass...." * His eyes are still more glazed over than a dozen donuts. She continues rambling, knowing full-well that he's not hearing a word said. * Lollie: "I didn't like him before. I certainly don't like him now. "Prophet?" Not in his wildest dreams. I'd just prefer you stay as far away from that annoying brat as possible." * She continues pondering, then suddenly stops, slapping her hand in front of Toga, snapping him out of his trance. Toga quickly turns his head to her, flinging drool over the camera and his partner... * Lollie: "Now that's just disgusting..." Toga: "Sowwy..." Lollie: "You'd better be... anyway, I got it! I dunno who you should face, but you should look up that Clyde Fox fellow. He seems to be similar to you, albeit a bit moody. He's been successful here in GCW. Maybe he'd have an idea of where you should start!" * Lollie wipes the camera off with her shirt, accidentally turning it off in the process..... *
