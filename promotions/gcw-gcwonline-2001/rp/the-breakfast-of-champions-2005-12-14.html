The Breakfast of Champions

14 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-14-2005 Scene fades up as Sam Wolack is sitting in a Washington DC diner. He’s sipping from a glass of orange juice, reading the paper. Sitting next to him is a little kid that just keeps staring at him. Kid: Are you famous or something, mister? Wolack sets the paper down. Wolack: Why do you ask? The kid points to a group of men in suits sitting behind Wolack. Kid: They’ve been watching you. Wolack: It’s just the FBI. Nothing to worry about. Unless you’re a terrorist. Are you? Kid: No, I’m just a kid. Wolack: Didn’t think that you were a terrorist either. They’re probably here to make sure that there is plenty of Cheerios for everyone. Kid: Why? Wolack: I don’t know. Maybe I’ll go ask them. Kid: OK. Wolack gets up from his seat and heads over to the table where the FBI agents are sitting. They start to read their newspapers. Wolack: Any news, gentlemen? Or are you just following me hoping to score tickets to the next GCW show here in Washington? FBI Agent: We’re here for the?”..coffee. Wolack: I bet you are very interested in the news. Especially seeing as you have your paper upside down. The agents all set their papers down, the agent that Wolack has been talking to sighs. Wolack: I get called to Washington because I’m told some there’s some important news about Andrea. Now I have the Four Amigos following me. Tell me, what’s the news. Agent: We’re here to make sure that you get to the J. Edgar Hoover Building. Wolack: Agent, you don’t have to worry about that. I’ll get there when I get there. Personally, I think that I would enjoy the opportunity to have my breakfast in peace. Agent: Well, after your breakfast, are you going to be coming with us? Wolack: I have some things that I have to do before then. But I will come right after lunch if you pay for my breakfast. Agent: Fine. Wolack: One other thing, I don’t want anyone following me where I’m going. Agent: Deal. Wolack heads back to where he was sitting and picks his newspaper back up. A rather large person sits down right next to him. Wolack: Good to see you, Mike. Mike: You know, you’re hard to find. Wolack: Tell your sister that the next shoot will take place in Vancouver. Mike: I’m not here to talk about that. I was wondering if you’ve heard. Wolack: Heard what? Mike: You’re going to become an uncle. Wolack: Yeah, I talked to Jay earlier. Serena is thrilled. Mike: You know, this is something that he’s wanted for a long time. Wolack: You really think you need to tell me that. I remember how hard it was for him after Kelly miscarried. Mike: How about you? How are you holding up? Wolack: I’m holding up just fine. I try not to think too much about it. After all, it’s been over a year since Keiko died. Mike: Still, it’s got to be tough on you. Losing a wife and son like that. I think that if I ever lost Mel or Kylie, I wouldn’t know what to do. Wolack: How is Mel, anyway? I haven’t seen her in a long time. Mike: Making sure that Kylie is healthy and happy. Glad that I’ve completely retired from the wrestling business. She just doesn’t like that I’m getting my law degree right now, feels that my going to Georgetown is taking away from time that I can spend with her and Kylie. Wolack: Well, I’ll talk to ya later, Mike. I’ve got things to take care of. Mike: Sam, I’ll see you around. Wolack: Not if I see you first. Scene fades to black.
