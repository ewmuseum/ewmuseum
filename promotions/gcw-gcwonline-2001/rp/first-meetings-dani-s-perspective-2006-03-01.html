First Meetings: Dani's Perspective

1 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/1/06 (Note: Advised to read Blaise McClancy's RP for this show first.) This was turning into a living hell of a night. First they had all decided to unwind and have a little get together in one of the hot tubs. It was going great. Not to mention that eventually she and Josh would have finally ended up going back to their room and... well... unwinding in a much different way. That was all fine and dandy, and with Dani's upcoming match against Jayce Bradley, she needed it. The hot tub and the hot night with her boyfriend. Her schedule over the last few weeks, especially since dropping the GCW hall of famer and former world champion, Jason Dread in her first match in GCW. Now she was in this Rampage Tourney that had just changed so much... so much... a chance at a world title. Not just a shot, but the friggin' title itself. But first she had someone step up and challenge her for her tourney spot. He was Jayce Bradley. A man who'd made it quite far in the GTT5 tourney that she had gone out in the first round of. Of course her first opponent was a world champion of Global Wrestling. He apparently got pretty far, but Dani didn't know the quality of his opponents. But it didn't matter. She knew how he'd be. He'd write her off completely. She was a woman. She had boobs, she had an ass that was made for thongs. She had indoor plumbing to his outdoor. So by definition she was inferior to him. But he fails to see that the rookie sensation that is Danielle Lynn Northsky She's held titles. She's done well. Yes, she's never been considered a 'great threat' like him. But that could change at any moment. But let's see just who is more hungry in this match? Shall we? Jayce Bradley wants something she has: He wants her spot in the Rampage Tourney. This spot means a potential chance of getting close to the World Title. It means she could prove herself to the federation. She could walk in the shoes of those that came in a week or two before her. Shadow, Dawn, and Vivica who just took the GCW U.S. Title. But a shot at the world title. A title that Dani has never, ever held in her short career. It would server her career immensely to finally, in under two years of being rated a pro, even if she is still a rookie, getting a major federations World Title. And this isn't some shit hole like the ?J.H.? Show over in her second fed. Or even the fed that closed while she was their North American Champion. Or even the one that she left after unifying three belts under their Platinum Title. This is GCW. A workhorse in the Wrestling World. A legendary place. Jayce Bradley has something she wants: He has the GCW Cruiserweight title. While it's not the World Title. It's a start. It's gold. It's a belt. Dani could do well by becoming the third ever female title holder in GCW. Let alone one of the youngest, and newest to the federation to gain such an accolade. You see, wrestling is Dani's life. Well it's what she tries to make her life. There were forces outside of this that tried to rend that to nothingness. She wanted to concentrate on her career, her relationship with a man who, while annoying to some, she knew to be the most wonderful human being But that wasn't always allowed. It was her destiny to live the life of someone beyond normal. Then again with what she was, she really had no choice. Today was one of those days where the choices were slim. It had been a while, since before Blaise came that an enemy had openly attacked them. And the poisoned food tray to crash their party with had beens strange. The invitation to take the bullet train to Osaka had been even stranger. Especially seeing both Ukyo and Blaise finally dress as their main job in the Order deemed. The combat bodysuit of the Order's Hunters. She had never thought Ukyo was a hunter like Blaise. She could see him fighting vampires, demons and other ghastly things. But sweet little street rat grown up Ukyo? Dani had no problem dressing as she needed to for the club the invite had drawn them to. A lace and satin bustier in black, stockings, garters, the other needed parts and a vinyl miniskirt to top it off. The right makeup and she looked downright normal for the rest of them. But she wasn't dressed for what would then happen. When Blaise had headed off running, Dani had been drug by Josh, right behind Gray in pursuit. He'd found who had sent the message. He knew it was a trap, an ambush. But for some stupid reason the red headed half breed had decided that setting off the trap would be a better strategy then just changing the inn they were staying at. She'd tried to ignore the fact that both her surrogate big brother, maybe one day brother in law and Blaise were both not only supernatural, but technically what others would call 'werewolves'. But seeing Blaise basically outrun her, Josh and the others when... was something she wasn't expecting. She knew Gray had held back to help protect her. But Josh had been a track star in High School not too many years ago. Then when they had entered the warehouse, all hell broke loose. She had seen the light come from the woman they were following. She had seen it go right through Blaise and Ukyo. She felt it go through her. She saw it stop at both Gray and Josh. Both stopped in their tracks as if they had been a DVD playing and put on pause. **Slam** The large metal door behind them had slammed shut, and more of those big ugly things with tusks came out from behind the crating. She looked around, trying to see what was wrong with Josh and Gray. She saw Blaise when he started fighting the ones at his end. And then she saw the ones coming towards her and Ukyo. That's when Dani's vision of her sweet friend started to become shattered. Ukyo whipped out something from the inside of her coat, looked like she pulled them from some sort of holder on her thighs. Three of them to be exact. They each extended out to about two feet long and then were screwed together making for a small stick... a staff, in her hands. She watched wide eyed as Ukyo then appeared to concentrate slightly and her eyes go into what could only be described as embers, on fire even. They say the eyes are the windows to the soul. She'd seen it on herself before her change. She'd seen it on others. Invariably those things that were beyond natural in this world had something to their eyes that made them different. And now she had finally picked out what was unusual about Ukyo. And then she noticed that the staff itself had started to glow with the same inner fire. Things were going to get very interesting, very quickly. She watched as Ukyo moved and spun with a grace that was so unlike Blaise's high impact style. She dodged and weaved constantly, moving out of the grasp of her assailants. The whole time keeping them from Dani. And it was Dani that looked to be their target. She was trained to fight in the ring, not fight things like this. These big assholes were larger then that freak in CCWA that had chased around after seeing her flash her boobs in one match. Dani felt herself flinch every time Ukyo's staff struck home though. Bit by bit small explosions would tear away at the clothing these things were wearing. And they would drop. Sometimes they'd get back up, but then she's hit them again. It was if she was slamming them with M-80 fire crackers or something. She had known that Ukyo wasn't normal, but she had never guessed she would be a walking battering ram. **boom** She had pulled something out of her pocket and tossed it into the direction of a couple of the damn things. The small device had looked like a racket ball when she pulled it out and threw it. But when it landed it had exploded an entire crate, taking two of the things down with the shrapnel. It almost looked like she was dancing as she fought the things down. ?Well, what have we here? I think I need to see just what is so interesting about you that your friends would need to be tested to see if they can protect you. A little double cross, yes? But who cares, since I've already been paid.? The woman popped up right in Dani's view... and she had a knife. A big one with cryptic things engraved on it. She couldn't scream. ?Gray? Josh?? ?No, the mortals can't help you. You see they're not to be harmed. I made a promise. Although your friends are quite interesting. My spell would only trap a mortal soul. They're obviously something more then that. But my Oni servants will make short work of them.? Behind the woman, Dani watched as Ukyo dropped into a horse stance and slammed her staff into the midsection of one of the large warriors. It flew back into some of the boxes with a small contained explosion. Her attention was divided as Ukyo then, with a flourish spun the staff and sent it behind her back, and then tossed it in the direction of Blaise and his opponents. The flash from the impact blinded Dani for a short time. **CRACK** She had heard it loud and clear. She felt her eyes force themselves to adjust to the light levels quickly. Guess her kind needed to be able to adjust to such things as auras and nimbus's quickly. When color returned to her eyes, she saw the woman in front of her with the knife still. But the hand that held the knife was held by Ukyo. The woman's neck was cocked at a strange angle and her tongue protruded from her mouth in a sickly fashion. Ukyo had snapped her neck as if it had meant nothing. But Dani's survival. ?You okay?? Dani just shook her head as Ukyo let the woman drop down. She could still see the fire in her eyes, but the sweet woman she knew was still there. She couldn't say the same for Blaise. She'd only guessed what it would be like to see him in this form. The large white creature, half man and half wolf stood there, red covering it's chest. He was looking around for something else to kill. ?Holy shit, what the fuck happened?? It was Josh. Apparently when taking the witch woman out, Gray and Josh had been freed of their hold. As one more of the enemy warriors stared down Blaise, she noticed Gray also shift forms. She'd seen his one other time before, as if it had been in a dream one night in Las Vegas. She thought he was going to help Blaise, but that wasn't the case. She felt the presence all of a sudden. Strong and forceful. ?NOOOO!? Dani yelled towards Gray as a dark figure stepped out of the shadow. Danielle stared at him, she knew him some how, some way. As she shouted her warning to Gray it was to late. He spun around, his eyes still full of the rage. It was obvious that his snare had set him off. Gray was a warrior wolf. She'd heard him mention his blood line before on the wolf side had come from the Vikings that had landed in Puget Sound hundreds of years before the first American Expeditions. And like his Viking forefathers, he could and tended to go into a berserk when he shifted. This was the reason why he had sent his ex-wife and child away. His rage, so unlike his educated and intellectual self, was dangerous to those who were... normal. She watched as Gray reached out and grabbed the man by the neck. The man showing no fear, just smiled at him. ?Gray Muzzle this is not necessary, I already know your heart to be true and pure. It was their's that I wasn't sure about. Release me now.? She knew who it was. It was her grandfather. ?The Star of God? himself. A Angelic Virtue of the divine who was the chief astrologer of heaven. Her people had called him the Morning Star. He was her sixth great grandfather. Dani had had enough of this night. ?STOP IT!? At that point Danielle released. She could feel it. She knew it. She could feel the nimbus of energy form around her. She felt her wings unfold from her back. She stood fully up right, her eyes shone with the light of the North Star itself. Her face full of anger... her hands clenched in tightly balled up fists. Around her neck the medallion that was her grandfather's own angelic symbol shone with a brightness that rivaled her eyes. ?MORNING STAR! Grandfather! That is enough! This is unneeded and dangerous. How dare you risk my friend's safety for your little game.? The man reached up and removed Gray's hand from around his neck. He then pushed the large wolf man aside as if he was nothing. As soon as he was away, the man's... angel's... eyes shone and Gray slowly shifted back to his more natural form. Blaise and Ukyo both watched the man with Suspicious eyes. His fluid movements hid not even his sword that he carried, this white wings mirrored Danielle's Ashen ones. ?My little Sirius Star, the time has forced my hand. I had to find out the company you keep, what their true hearts where. I had to make sure they were prepared for the coming battle with thine Enemy.? Dani didn't care. ?Why now, why this? You could of cost them their lives in such a meaningless act. What would of happen then?? His face was soft when he looked at her. It showed the paternal love he had for her. ?Your concern for your friends is a good thing. Hence the reason I must be quick and not linger. Your time is growing close. Jaymes' father knows of his plight, and his punishment. He's not happy. His minions have managed to find a human to release him from his prison. He now seeks you for his and now his human born son's revenge. Where his son failed he will not.? ?Who? Who comes? Who spawned that worthless...? He put up his hand to silence her, indicating that she should not ask further. ?You will know him by the name your earthly people gave him. For he is my opposite in all things divine. Where I was named for the first light of the morning, he is the last light of the day, boding the coming of night and it's darkness.? Dani's face dropped a bit. She looked down and mouthed a name. ?The Evening Star...? ?Yes, that is what you must call him. The evening Star is coming for you my, granddaughter. You must be ready for him. Do not ever speak his name, do not share his name with others.? She looked confused. ?Why? Or should I ask, why not?? ?In Speaking his true name it gives him the power to find you. So it is something that you must never say. And it's why I can not tell you his true and divine name now. For I do not want him to find you.? Dani was still confused. ?How do you know he is coming? How do I know you're right?? ?The device that kept him locked in the prison I made for him has returned to me. After hundreds of years I carry it by my side again.? Dani looked up at him and saw the sword, one just like the blade that is jammed in the earth next to Blaise's Soul Tree back in Vegas. ?Your sword.? ?Yes, it has long kept him pinned to the earthly realm and kept you and your family safe from all but his earthly agents.? Danielle shuddered at that thought. The legends of the Evening Star and his cruelty ran through her head. She had danced to the songs that told of the fight between Morning Star and Evening Star many times when she was learning the native dance routines. She found herself staring at the sword he held, though. Her hand reached out for it. But, as she caught herself she quickly pulled her hand away, almost as if pulling her hand away from the burning heat of a fire. ?Your friends are great, they are more then they seem, The one you call Blaise, he is Native but is not. His mind if of two sides of the world, one of the new and one of the old. His Female companion is a great warrior also. Her kind, though unknown to me, has gained my trust in this task.? ?I'll let them know.? Why did she just say that? She knew they could hear this, right? ?Seek out the man with two souls he is of our kind in both ways. He can help you with answers that you seek.? ?A man with two souls?? ?You know of this man but do not know this man. He is housed in the house of the Order of Knights that now take it upon themselves to protect you. Their kind will know who to direct you to, when you're ready.? Morning Star turns and starts to walk away, he stops and looks at Blaise. ?I am sorry my friend, Blaise... or Turren, yes... that's the other name for you. Prince Turren MacOgma. I know your kind now. I'm sorry for this test, but I had to make sure you where worthy of protecting my granddaughter. She's very special to me. She's... one of a kind.? Blaise just looked at the man standing in front of him and bowed him head as a show of respect. But his eyes told him a different story. However the Divine Virtue of the Heavens didn't press the issue. He knew when he should leave, and he did so. It was a rush of air and he was gone. Blaise looked around at the destruction that had been wrought here. ?Holy Shit!? It was Josh again. ?What?? Dani said as she turned towards Josh, her wings dissipating into the ether and leaving her looking human again. ?Hey, I might not have been able to move, but damn. I did get to see. Fucking 'A' Blaise, you weren't kidding when you said that Ukyo was the artillery. She's a fucking tank!? Ukyo smiled a wide, white smile, and looked to redden in the face a bit. Blaise walked over and wrapped an arm around her waist. ?Yeah. People usually don't believe me when I say she's a real firecracker.? Dani smiled at the joke, but turned to hit the button that raised the large metal door to the warehouse. She was done for the night. She didn't want to think of anything but a good shower, and maybe that time with Josh alone that she had been looking forward to. She didn't notice Blaise reach up to his belt and flip a small switch as he reclaimed his long coat. It would be a long trip to their temporary home in Japan. Why couldn't she just go home and forget it all?
