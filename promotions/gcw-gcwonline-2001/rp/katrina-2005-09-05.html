Katrina

5 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 Sept 05 “Yeah I’m landing now Kevin’Yeah tell Steven he’s an asshole.” I hung up the phone, making sure Mr. Jacobs knew exactly how unhappy I was with my present situation. Steven wants us all to be some kind of Public Relations gods, help the world when they could just help themselves. I could have just sent a million dollars to some charity fund, posed for a few pictures, gotten my name in the paper with a nice phrase underneath it and called it a day. No of course not, they want to send the CHAMP to the source of it all. I didn’t know anyone caught in that damn thing, not only that but I have been so busy that I haven’t had much of an opportunity to really understand all of the damage, and quite frankly I didn’t want to know about it either. I wasn’t out to save the world, never have been because it can’t be saved. Some people live and some people day, sure its not always fair or right but then again since when has it been fair? So here I am, right on the outskirts of New Orleans, where the aftermath of hurricane Katrina is spread out all over. I had read in the papers that it was bad, many dead, more wounded but that didn’t mean that me coming here was a good idea or I felt like I needed to come. I am alive and well, I don’t believe that I am in need of reminders of how quickly death can come for us all. I stepped from out of the private jet, grabbing my bags in the process. A car was waiting for me, not a limo but a respectable car. I tossed the driver my bags which he quickly tossed into the trunk. I didn’t get into the car right away, first I had to get some information. “Where are we headed?” I asked. “Your call Mr. Knight, Steven Caldera said that there are a number of shelters and such that you could choose from.” “I’d rather be at home.” I said under my breath. “What was that sir?” “Nothing ? just take me to the first one that pops into your head.” “very well sir.” We both climbed into the car now and settled in. I felt tired but it wasn’t from lack of sleep or the flight, it was the weather out here. Rainy/cloudy weather has always made me drowsy. With a deep sigh I laid my head back and began replaying the events that have happened recently in my mind. I had barely had time to just sit back and recall Annihilation where I came out the champion but I have a hard time saying I WON that night. Sure the record books will claim that I got the better of Michael but did I really? He certainly did not dominate at me, I gave him a beating as well but that bastard snapped my shoulder out of place, then left me laying after the match. Now everyone backstage seems to petrified of him and why? Because he showed some kind of mean streak for once in his life? I have been doing that for years and I don’t see them running and hiding when I come around. Am I losing my edge? Well if Stevens thinks that I am going to fear him like every other sad excuse for a wrestler backstage then he is sadly mistaken. I will stand toe to toe with that piece of dirt and grind him into submission, at Fall Out. Fall Out is the end of the line for the Michael Stevens vs. LeStatt Knight war, at least for now. That night is when I beat Stevens so bad that he realizes that he has no business stepping into my domain. Some are already talking that I have signed my title away for getting into a submission match with the “technical wonder boy” Michael Stevens, and I hope they continue to feel that way. I have been trained since I was ten years old in technical wrestling, martial arts, and other forms of fighting yet no one seems to acknowledge that. All is well though , perhaps it is better for then so that I will have the element of surprise. The world is expecting Stevens to be the aggressor now, to be the more technically sound of us two but they have no idea what I am capable of. I am just getting started here in GCW, what they’ve seen is only the beginning and everyone better hope and pray that it is not them that gets caught in my path. Although a certain Trey Tremendous was just sent in the tornado’s path, so to speak. The rematch I have been looking forward to since Trey ended my record breaking United States Title reign. I could STILL be holding that title to this day if it weren’t for Jason Dread because no one capable of beating me has even come close to it. SO just as I will rip apart Stevens at Fall Out, Thursday I get to right a wrong from a few months back and physically dissect Trey, proving him to be nothing more than a wannabe. He will feel? “Alright sir we are here.” My thoughts were interrupted, my eyes shot open and I looked around. “There’s nothing here amigo.” “It’s that building right over there, the car can’t get to it because of the water. My cousin is down there and he will take you on his small boat the rest of the way.” I was in disbelief not only would I be sent here for some bullshit PR, but now I was going to have to trudge through water too? This day just keeps getting better and better. So what if some of the city flooded? It would drop eventually and these people could get on with their lives. I climbed out of the car and slammed the door shut. The driver had already popped the trunk, letting me snatch my bags from inside of it. I moved towards where the boat was at, water slowly creeping higher. When I reached a small platform that was used to get inside of the boat the operator of the boat smiled and tipped his hat. I forced a smile and climbed in. “Its good to have you Mr. Knight, some of the kids heard you were coming and are very excited.” “Gracias, let us go.” He nodded and started the small boat up, getting us moving towards the red cross building. When we got there I tossed my bag over letting it land on a part that didn’t have water on it as it had a series of cement steps that lead up to a walkway which in turn went to two swinging doors. I shook the man’s hand who had driven the boat and climbed out, splashing water up where I landed. The water was freezing that was for sure, so I hurried to get out of it. I picked up my bag and walked towards the double doors where I watched as a few workers. Or volunteers probably opened the doors. “Hello welcome to the red cross.” A short older lady said. She was probably in her late 50’s, early sixties. “Nice to have you sir follow us and we’ll take you around.” A probably 17 or 18 year old young woman said. She had long black hair that was tied up and kept in place by some unsharpened pencils. I didn’t say anything at first just allowing them to explain where everything was and what they did there. As we walked around I half listened and half wondered what was going to happen at World Wide this week. Eventually I figured that I had better try my best to pay attention to them in case they asked me a question or something. “When will the power be back up?” I asked. “We don’t know for sure, it could be quite sometime.” “So what happens at night?” “Candles and battery powered lights and heaters. Slowly they are coming and taking the ones here away but its hard finding places for everyone. Some have been separated from their families.” I nodded and pressed on moving until we reached another set of double doors, the two volunteers pushed them open and what I saw froze me in place. I looked around and saw a sea of people crammed into this room. Sure the room was rather large but with all of the people who were probably without homes, food, and clothing it was packed. It took me a moment but I took steps forward into the room and looked around, barely noticing the doors shut behind me. It wasn’t until this moment that things kind of came into perspective. “All these people ?” my words were cut short, not by another voice but just by me. I couldn’t finish the sentence. “Yes Mr. Knight, these people are all without homes. Some have lost family and friends, we have children here that?” “Children?” I asked not really meaning to cut off the young girl. “Yes, some children have been separated from their parents, or their parents did not survive the tragedy.” Suddenly I got a sick feeling deep down in my stomach. My mind raced and my lips trembled. Immediately the thought of Starr slammed to my mind, my daughter, the most beautiful of god’s creations. Then the thought of her being alone, not me or her mother to watch over her, to love her like she deserved to be. I could feel the burning forming behind my eyes, but I couldn’t let it flow over ? I held them back. “I’d like to meet the people now if you don’t mind.” “Of course not.” I gave a quick thank you and began walking around the room. Time blew by as people came up and asked for autographs, pictures, and just for conversation. Normally I would hate to speak to fans, conversations with them are something I hate more than, well ? I don’t know but I hate it. Yet today it felt right, at this moment I felt as if these people needed something to get away from all of the pain and suffering they have felt. Not only that but with each person I met, it amazed me with how many compliments and well wishes I’d receive. I haven’t done anything lately to prove worthy of their praise, as I have only dismissed and insulted the fans. Yet they smile when they see me, cheer when I do one of the poses for a picture and laugh as we joke about different things. Not since my days as the challenger for the DWA World Title have I been this embraced. Maybe it is because of the tragedy that has befallen them or maybe I just don’t give the fans enough credit. Regardless today isn’t about that. Michael Stevens, the GCW title, Trey Tremendous, Eva VanLorne, Mary, Caldera, none of it mattered at this point in time. I think about all of the hardships that have come to my life but realize that I was able to pull myself back up from them all. This tragedy is something completely different, they are left with nothing but aspirations or maybe broken dreams. Things settled down eventually, and I got to talk to some of the people more one on one. I got to discuss business plans with a young man who was trying to get his own business together before the hurricane hit. I handed him my card, telling him to get a hold of me for any financial or business assistance. Another family lost everything, including their teenage son who went back to help friends, but never made it back. I told them I knew how it was to lose a child, gave them whatever advice I could before moving on. There was an older lady who I spoke with, sure she had no idea who I was but she thought I was a “hot little number” so she let me hold her hand and speak to her as she fell asleep. Person after person, story after story I went through never getting bored and never wishing I were somewhere else. I hadn’t noticed how late it was getting as I looked out and saw the sun beginning to dip down below the buildings and water that flooded the city. I looked down at the window that I was staring out at, and something caught my eye. A little girl was sitting on her knees, her hands clasped together and staring out at the sunset. I hesitated for a moment before walking up next to the girl. She was a young Hispanic girl, probably seven or eight years old, her long black hair fell down to the middle of her back. The clothes she wore obviously were not her own as she basically swam in them. I listened to her words that she spoke, it was a prayer in Spanish, a prayer that I had actually heard many times growing up. She finished and opened her eyes and jumped slightly when she realized I was standing there. I smiled at her, trying to put her at ease. Those bright brown eyes stared back up at me for a moment before she set them on the setting sun once more. I take a seat on the window sill and look at her. “Hola muchacha, que pasa?” “De nada senor.” “Are you alright?” “Si, just waitin for my mama and papa.” “Where are they?” She points out towards the water. “They said they be right back, that they will never leave they baby.” “Whats your name senorita?” “Cynthia.” I swallowed hard at the sound of that name, a name that has filled my heart with so much love and at the same time so much guilt. It wasn’t surprising though, my wife was beautiful as was this young girl. I smile at he. “Your parents will be with you again, they love you very much.” “In heaven.” “What did you say senorita?” “They will see me in heaven, that’s where they gone to senor.” She was smarter than I gave her credit for, she was also much stronger than any young girl her age had the right to be. I could see her lip beginning to tremble and slowly one tear drop fell from her eye. It glistened from the sun reflecting from it. I grabbed her hand which makes her turn her gaze to me. “I miss them?” It was all she could get out before tears just pours from her eyes. I pulled her close and hugged her, letting her tears soak up my shirt. I couldn’t say much, in all reality I didn’t think there was that much to say. She cried, and I forced myself not to do the same. “Mr. Knight, your car is here for you to take you to your hotel.” I continued to hold Cynthia as she cried, then I looked up at the young volunteer who brought me in to this place. “Tell them to go, I am staying here tonight.” She smiled and rushed off. It didn’t feel right leaving now, in their darkest hour. The sun had completely set now and a chill came through the air. A volunteer came and brought some blankets, which I thanked her for. I laid Cynthia down after she finished her crying and covered her up. We spoke for awhile as everyone settled in for sleep. She told me about her parents, their vacation to Disney World, Six Flags, and Cedar Point. She told me about her dog, a black Chihuahua they named Mouse, for obvious reasons. I told her about my daughter and that she would see her parents in heaven. I covered myself up with an extra blanket and leaned back, staying close to the girl. “Thank you Roberto.” she said. “No ? thank you Mija, you’ve helped me realize something I had thought was long forgotten.” She didn’t ask what it was surprisingly, as if she understood where I was coming from. I saw her eyes begin to shut as she fell asleep. I looked down and her hand was inside of mine and for this time I felt that love of being a father again. A smile comes across my face as I lay back and close my eyes, trying to fall asleep. There are times in life when you must realize what is important and what is not. Right now GCW isn’t important, my fucked up love life isn’t important, and the hatred I feel towards Michael Stevens isn’t important. What is important are these people, and the way they are in need of everyone’s help. My eyes shoot open and I dig into my pocket and pull out my cell phone. After a few rings I get the answer. “Kevin Jacobs.” “Kevin, its Knight.” “What can I do for you?” “Tell Steven that I will be at the benefit show.” “Will do Knight.” “And one more thing ? tell Steven I said thank you, for sending me out here.” I hung up the phone and closed my eyes once more. Tomorrow may bring back that other side of me that makes everyone fear me or hate me, but for this day and this night I feel completely human again. LIFE ? It’s a funny thing sometimes.
