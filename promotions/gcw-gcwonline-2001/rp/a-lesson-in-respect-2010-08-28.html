A Lesson In Respect

28 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/28/10 Thursday, 2:41am Maynard McGarrity had wrestled in Japan, Mexico and Puerto Rico. He’d been inside the ring with some of the most brutal and sadistic wrestlers this business could offer. But even he couldn’t remember ever seeing this much blood. It was everywhere, despite the fact that the rain continued to pour down, washing some of it away with every drop. His body was screaming, still ripe with the blows from that Lousiville. He ran his hand down his face, feeling for his nose. A hard knot tied in his stomach as he realized the slimy lump protruding almost from his left cheek was actually his nose. It felt as though it had been smashed across his face like play-doh. It felt nothing like a human nose. He could tell by the way he was gasping for air, that some of his ribs were broken. Every breath seemed to stab a new knife into his side, twisting and scraping against his lungs so that he’d wished he could stop breathing, just for a few moments. He wished so much that he could remember clearly Joey’s face. He wanted to find him and hurt him the way he’d been hurt. He wanted to make him feel this horrible pain. He wanted him to feel as small as Maynard did in this moment. Maynard knew he had to try to get to his feet; to get out of here to safety. But he found himself quite unable to muster the energy to do so. He wanted to just lay here, to sleep away the pain. But he could feel himself flirting with unconsciousness; his head was beginning to spin. Whether it was a result of blood loss or because of his short, shallow breaths, he could not be certain. He reached into his pocket, finding his cell phone and pulled it out. Praying that it had not yet become too water-logged to function, he flipped it open. The screen lit up without a problem. He dialed out, and pressed the phone to his ear. "Come on," whispered Maynard weakly. "Be home. Pick up .. please .." "Hello?" said a groggy voice on the other end of the phone. "Clayton," said Maynard. "Maynard? What’s wrong? Where are you?" "Clayton, " said Maynard again. "I’m in trouble .." Wednesday, 10:12pm Maynard was feeling good. Really good. He couldn’t remember a time he’d felt this good. He had that warm, fuzzy feeling in the pit of his stomach that only his best friend, Jack could give him. He was finally home from the GCW overseas Rampage Tour. And things were actually going wonderfully with Gertrude: something even he hadn’t expected. He felt, for the first time in as long as he could possibly remember, as though he was a whole person. Sitting on a hard stool in a bar felt different now that he wasn’t hear to pick up women. He felt almost silly in this moment as he sat, his head swiveling around the club watching countless men with gelled-up hair and popped collars did their best James Bond impression in hopes to catch the attention of one of the beautiful girls. He wondered if he looked like that. Like he was a desperate schoolboy looking for his first fuck. He hoped not. He hoped he had a more elegant way of going about it. But it didn’t matter anymore, really. Ever since he made the agonizing decision to call her that one night, they’ve been talking every night. It was actually a smooth transition, from heartless, womanizing prick, back to caring, romantic boyfriend. Boyfriend, he thought. That was one word he’d, up until recently, thought he’d never have to use again. He smiled as he threw Jack against the back of his throat. It felt good. He was surprised at just how good it felt. Wednesday, 11:02pm There was a tap on Maynard’s shoulder. He whirled around to see a short, but rock-solid looking man with a scowl on his face. He was well-dressed in his pinstriped sports coat and designer jeans, and had a hard look in his eyes. "Ya know who Joey Coda is?" He asked abruptly. Maynard shook his head, turning back towards the bar. "Well, he knows you. He wants to buy ya a drink." Maynard turned around, drink in hand. "Well, I’ve already got a drink. And I usually only let women buy me drinks. So unless this Joey’s some kinda new-agey chicks name, can’t say I’m interested." Maynard tried to turn around again, but the man’s hand was like a vice-grip on his shoulder and he was forced back to facing him again. "Joey wants to buy ya a drink", he said simply. "Joey’s gonna buy ya a drink." The small man motioned Maynard to his feet and led the way through the sweaty mass of dancing humanity, across the club to where Joey Coda was. He was sitting at a roped off booth is the farthest east corner of the club. There was another man, dressed similarly to the man leading Maynard, standing at the corner of the booth, facing the crowd. Joey Coda was a small, mousey man with the facial features of an overgrown rat. He wasn’t good-looking, so one could only assume the half-dozen women sitting with him in his booth was the result of either a ton of money, or an enormous cock. Given the man’s stature, he doubted the latter. Joey threw his arms into the air when he caught glimpse of Maynard. "All hail, the Magnificent Messiah!" Maynard looked down at Joey, face screwed up. "I’m sorry?" "Well, that’s you, a’int it?" He asked, smiling a gap-toothed grin. "The Magnificent Messiah? The wrestler?" "Oh. Yeah," said Maynard, thrusting his hands in his pockets and looking down at his trainers. Being recognized in public was always the most awkward part of being a pseudo star. "Excellent!" cried Joey, taking a huge swig from the pint in front of him. "Sit, sit!" Joey motioned two of the women away, making room for Maynard right beside him. Maynard obliged. "So, what does the Messiah like to drink?" "Jack," Maynard said simply. "The Magnificent One drinks Jack!" exploded Joey joyously. "I figured ya’d drink some elegant shit or something. With a name like ‘magnificent’, I didn’t expect ya to be throwing the hard shit back!" "Well, you know," Maynard smiled, "It’s all TV, right? A show. I’m not exactly magnificent out here in the real world." Joey let loose a giant, booming laugh, leaving Maynard to wonder what he’d said that was this hysterical. "A’int that the fuckin’ truth, huh?" "I guess so," said Maynard, with a polite smile. "So tell me, man" said Joey, catching his breath from his laugh-attack. "What’s it like being on TV every other week? It’s gotta be great, huh? Tons of free fucking pussy, I bet. Right?" Maynard chuckled. "Used to be." "The fuck you mean, ‘used to be’? You not getting so lucky as of late?" shouted Joey, slamming his glass down on the table. "Nah," started Maynard, looking down at the table, "got myself a, you know ... steady girlfriend." "So the fuck what?" laughed Joey, throwing his arm around the beautiful blond girl on the other side of him. "I’ve got me a wife and two kids at home. Since when does that I mean I can’t get a little somethin’ on the side?" Maynard laughed politely. "Guess it takes a better man than I to hook two at once." "Two?" laughed Joey, scanning the club. "Friend, all these fuckin’ bitches around us right now wanna sit down with Joey Coda. This is my fuckin’ place. These bitches come here for me!" Joey extends his pint towards Maynard for a toast. Since he has yet to receive a drink, he picks up one of Joey’s empties from the table before him and obliges the toast. "Jesus! A’int no one grabbed your drink yet?" yelled Joey. "Yo, Maggie!" A red-headed women who was sitting at the very edge of the booth turned her head. "Get the Messiah a drink, would ya? A double of Jack. Tell’m it’s for Joey." The woman sighs, stands up and walks off into the masses towards the bar. "A’int this the life?" asked Joey with a smile, tossing another large swig of his pint back. "Life don’t get no better than this, that much I know for sure." Joey raised his pint, only to have Maynard raise one of his empties again. "Yo, Maggie! Hurry the fuck up, would ya?" Thursday, 1:30am "Oh, man!" exclaimed Joey, waving his arms animated in the air. He was clearly somewhere a little past very drunk. "When you made Samuelsson tap, I thought I was going to pass out! You locked in the -- the ... whatcha call it?" "JFT", replied Maynard, barely able to hide his thorough boredom. Even with the thick, drunken haze now settling on his head, he couldn’t ignore the fact that this conversation had long gotten old. "Yeah, the JFT!" cried Joey and he slapped Maynard hard on the back. "When you locked that shit on that giant bastard, I was like, ‘no fuckin’ way is this scrawny motherfucker gonna make the Viking tap out!’ And then -- BAM! He taps the fuck out and I am just left, like --- Whaaaa? That was fuckin’ legendary, man! Fuck!" Maynard, despite the persona of the character he portrays on television, is a very level-headed, modest person. So you can imagine his awkwardness having Joey Coda recite Maynard’s entire GCW career, which has not been very long in itself, seven or eight times throughout the evening. Maynard feigned a smile. "Yeah. Thanks, man." "So what’s next for ya, man?" asked Joey, leaning back in his seat. "Too bad that limey got one over on ya, slimy bastard. What’s that little shit’s name again?" "Shipley," replied Maynard, monotone as he picks absentmindedly at the label of his beer. Joey looks genuinely angry. "Yeah, him! Fuckin’ snake. Hope Murray gives him everything he deserves at High Stakes. I wanna see that motherfucker’s head kicked in, you know?" Maynard grunts his expected approval, still not looking at Joey. "What about you, man? What’s this ‘Luck of the Draw’ bullshit about?" Maynard shrugs. "Not sure. Guess that’s the point." "That’s bullshit!" shouted Joey, slamming his glass on the table and sloshing its contents across the table. "That’s what really pisses me off about this Garbage Bag Johnny fella, you know? He never seems to know what the fuck is going on! I mean, are ya runnin’ the show or not? How fuckin’ fair is it for you to go into a pay-per-view match-up, not knowing who you’ll be facing, when you’ll be wrestling or the type of match you’ll have to compete in? It’s not fuckin’ fair at all! It’s completely unprofessional! It’s BULLSHIT!" Joey shouts this last word with such volume that for a moment it appears as though the whole club stops and looks at him before returning to their dancing. "You’re gonna be alright though, yeah?" asked Joey, genuine concern etched across his face. Maynard nodded. "Yeah, man. Of course." "Oh, man!" cried Joey, throwing his arms in the air again. Maynard looked up at him for a moment. "The Rampage Royal! Man, you may have only --" "Listen, give it a fucking rest would you? For five minutes?" Maynard wasn’t sure exactly what made him say it. He was normally far more polite in social situations. Perhaps one could blame the alcohol. "Excuse me?" asked Joey dangerously. "We’ve been talking about my career all night," said Maynard, looking directly into Joey’s menacing green eyes. "Don’t you think that maybe, just maybe, I came out tonight to forget about my job? I live my job, Joe. I know who I am, I know what I’ve done and the last thing I need is some fucking overzealous super-fan jamming everything down my throat, when all I wanted to do tonight was relax!" For a moment, Maynard was worried the little bastard’s head was going to explode clean off his shoulders. His face went a deep shade of red usually reserved for fire engines, and he didn’t appear to be breathing. He looked dangerously at Maynard for several moments before he finally let loose a long, deep breath. He smiled in this moment, but it wasn’t a gracious smile. He made some kind of motion to the larger of his two bodyguards. He nodded and then moved towards Maynard. There was nothing he could do. Before he knew what was going on, he saw a meaty fist flying at his face. It connected clean with his jaw, knocking him clean out. The bodyguard picked Maynard up and lead his colleague and Joey out through the masses. "Way too much to drink. Ya know how it is," was Joey’s excuse to passers-by as to why Maynard was being carried in the arms of a man twice his size. Thursday, 2:20am When Maynard came to, he was inside a limousine, but they were not moving. He felt the aching pain across his jaw before he remembered the punch. It was at this moment that he sprang to life, knowing he was in trouble. He was wedged between the two bodyguards and sitting across from Joey, who sat alone on the largest bench across the back of the car. He had a Louisville Slugger across his lap as he fiddled with his Blackberry. "Where are we?" asked Maynard weakly. Joey looked up from his phone. "Oh, look who’s awake boys!" The goons chuckled menacingly. "Where are we?" asked Maynard again. "Never mind that," said Joey, looking back down at his phone. "What I really want to talk to you about is your attitude." "My attitude?" Maynard felt as though he was being scolded by a school teacher. "Yeah," said Joey. "Your fuckin’ attitude. Ya gotta learn how to keep that in check. Gonna get you -- in trouble." Again, the goons chuckle. "Listen, man--" "Nah, I’m done listenin’." said Joey roughly, shoving his phone into the pocket of his jeans. "I listened enough when you ripped my head off for being a fuckin’ fan." "Man, come on--" "Shut the fuck up!" shouted Joey. "You know, ya make me sick, ya famous people. Ya think that just because your on TV and shit, you get to treat all the regular people like something smelly under your shoe." "I didn’t--" "Talk one more time, I’ll break your jaw. We clear?" asked Joey, clamping his hand down on the bat in his lap. Maynard reluctantly nodded. "Good. Now, me and my boys here, we’re gonna be teachin’ you a lesson in respect." Joey picked the bat up and looked at it longingly. "This might hurt a bit." The smaller of the two goons grabbed Maynard roughly by the collar of his shirt and pulled him out of the car and onto the cold, wet pavement. He could feel the rough pitter patter of the rain; hear quiet cracks of thunder off in the distance. Maynard looked around, hoping he would see someone who could stop this. But who would stop this? Even if there was someone around, wouldn’t they just run in hopes they wouldn’t get stuck in the middle? That’s what I’d do, Maynard thought. As he tried to scramble to his feet, he saw the yellow blur of a boot screaming towards his face. It connected flush with his nose, and he heard an audible crack followed by the hot sensation of blood flowing down his face to his neck. He ate another boot to the face, and two to the gut. He was being worked over by the two goons while Joey stood by, cracking the bat against his open palm. Feet, knees, elbows and fists came raining down on him as he tried the best he could to cover up his face to limit the damage. He could hear Joey laughing from his place off somewhere to Maynard’s right. It made him sick to know how much enjoyment this son-of-a-bitch was getting from watching this beating. "That’ll do, boys", said Joey, and the beating ceased. Maynard coughed and wheezed, trying to catch his breath. He felt blinding pain pounding through his entire body. It was as though they’d not missed a single inch of him with their strikes. He could feel blood still streaming from his nose. He chanced a look down at his shirt and found it to be soaked almost halfway down his chest with blood. It made him queazy to look at, so he quickly shut his eyes. "How’d that feel, huh Messiah?" asked Joey playfully. "You like that?" Maynard didn’t answer. "Answer me when I talk to you!" cried Joey as he wailed a hard boot to Maynard’s gut. Maynard gasped at the unexpected shot, clutching at his stomach. "Come on, man ..." "Come on? You want more?" "No, I--" But it was too late, the bat had already been swung hard into his ribs. He felt bone bend under wood and the pain was indescribable. He felt as though his entire rib cage had shattered on impact. He howled in pain. Joey just laughed. "Hurts a lot more than that rubber shit they use in your business, huh?" Maynard could feel what he thought were tears running down his cheek. Though, admittedly, it was tough to tell the difference with so much rain and blood abound. "You had enough yet?" asked Joey. Maynard didn’t hear him through his own whimpers, and as such, he didn’t answer. Joey clicked his tongue at Maynard like a mother towards it’s child. He’d been ignored again. He swung the bat at Maynard’s ribs again, connecting flush. Maynard howled again as pain shot through his entire body. He wasn’t sure how much more of this he could take ... "Have .. ya had .. enough .. yet?" "Yes!" screamed Maynard. "Please!" "Feeling a bit more respectful, are we?" "Yes!" "Yes," said Joey, raising the bat above his head. "Sir", he said as the bat came crashing down into Maynard’s bruised, probably broken ribs once again. "Sir!" sputtered Maynard, barely distinguishable as it was from his grunts of pain. Joey smiled down at his handy work. "That’s better. See you around, Messiah. Let’s go boys." Without taking a second look at their handy work, the trio stepped over Maynard and got back into their limo. The engine started up and the car roared off down the alley and back onto the busy street. Maynard McGarrity had wrestled in Japan, Mexico and Puerto Rico. He’d been inside the ring with some of the most brutal and sadistic wrestlers this business could offer. But even he couldn’t remember ever seeing this much blood ...
