The Beginning Of Something Beautiful

15 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/15/10 "If you don't win at Worldwide there will be consequences, severe consequences. Our employer will not allow you to fail..." These were the last words Lori Equinox heard as he was being dragged along a hard and cold tiled floor in a location unknown to himself. He had just been sprayed with some form of sleeping gas and was quickly losing his ability to do anything at all. "You will not fail..." With only those words ringing in his ears, Lori's grip on reality was lost and he slipped into a deep sleep. The next thing he would recall would be waking up on his bed at home, with his girlfriend shaking him. -Two hours earlier- "Oh yes, Texas. Such a beautiful place" Lori smiled as he took a sip of his cafe ole, the house coffee he had ordered in the Dominican Joe's he had decided to chill at for the day. Opposite him sat Kyle Abbram, a reporter from the Austin Chronicle. He too took a sip of his coffee as he prepared to get down to business with Lori Equinox. After having a mouthful of coffee, Kyle took out a small tape recorder and waved it infront of Lori. "Do you mind?" With a shake of his head, Lori acknowleged to Kyle that he didn't mind being recorded. "Now before we start Lori I just wanna say that if you don't want to answer anything I don't mind, it's just great that we have a chance to talk to you before the upcoming GCW event in our hometown here." Lori smiled, feeling at ease with this. Recently in the last couple of months he had come under alot of scrutany from other people accusing him of things he hadn't done and asking him questions that he didn't at all care for. "To be honest Kyle I'm, glad, I'm sick of people asking me stupid questions." Even the thought of the last month of two was enough to get Lori annoyed but he knew he mustn't lose his cool in this situation. He would come across as the hero here, the person who was trying to become the better man, not the one out on some twisted revenge trip. "Now Lori, it's time to begin..." Kyle clicked in the record button and set the tape player down on the table. "First of all, as I said to you before we began this interview it's great to get one of the newest GCW superstars to have a good chat with us. How you doing Lori?" A tough question to answer for Lori, taking a moment to think he started his part of the bargain. "It's been a mixed month for me. If you know much about GCW you'll know that I've only had two matches so far in all the time I've been there. One of them ended with me being chokeslammed hard into the mat and the other ended in defeat so I wouldn't say things are going great on that level." Looking at his notepad before him and nodding his head slowly, Kyle continued with his questions. "Do you think you're going to be able to come back from them losses? I mean alot of people have been getting to you over the last weeks. First of all SurReal coming at you with which are now known to be false allegations and then this Donny character from Surreal's past getting involved." A large grin appeared across Lori's face and he nodded in agreement. "I can totally relate to what you're saying, there's gonna be obstacles put in my path but there's nothing I can do but get over them. I have been known in the past to mess with people's heads. I kind of gives me a good insight into how to get rid of those problems. Surreal was always going to be a problem, I tormented him for quite a while back when we were in rW so I guess it's just right of him to accuse me of things. It just gave me great pleasure in a way when I saw him being chokeslammed along side me. Well... when I watched it back I mean. I had no idea where I was at the time" The two men laughed before Lori continued. "Now, as for Donny Diamond... I was in DWO, and it was hard not to know who Donny Diamond was. I mean he was DWO champion and PTC Global Champion at the same time. He won them both on the same night. The thing is though that I had never got involved with him in DWO so I don't see why I should get involved now. I know he wrecked me slap bang in the middle of the ring but I suppose I was just in the wrong place at the right time for him." "Now, onto your opponent Lori. Daria Ngozi is who you'll be facing this coming week. Any thoughts?" Lori chuckles a little to himself before straightening himself up on his chair and getting serious for a moment, something he doesn't do very often. "To be honest Kyle. I truly don't have a clue who Daria Ngozi is. I have never heard of them. Is Daria a guy or a girl? Who knows? Maybe you do Kyle but I couldn't really care. The fact is that I have a job to do. I need to go into Worldwide with a head screwed on. I need to go in their and get a win. It's kinda came to a point where I will do anything to win. More for my self pride than anything." Kyle nods his head, wriing down a few notes to go along with his recording. After a few moments he picks up the tape player and switches it off. "O.K Lori we're done here. Thank you very much." Lori reaches his hand over and the two men shake on a well done professional interview session. "Thanks Kyle, I appreciate this." Lori's tone was a very greatful one. Getting himself back out into the limelight was one of his main goals for 2010. The more people could see that he had changed forever would be a main aim, along with picking up some wins in the ring also ofcourse. "No problem Lori. There's something I'm going to admit though..." A bad feeling began to swirl in the depths of Lori's soul. "What?" As the handshake continues, the two mens eyes bore into the others, both tightening their grip. Then out of nowhere a clubbing blow takes Lori off his seat. His attacker is a large man, maybe 6 foot 5 in height and built like a house with a couple of extensions added and a few 5 car garages. He drops the baton he used to attack down beside the fallen Lori and grabs him by the back of the neck. He props him up, his knee swelling into the back of Lori. Kyle walks over with a spring in his step, grinning as if he'd just won a kareoke contest for a million pounds. "Turns out Lori, I'm not really a reporter." Lori shakes his head, unable to reply. His head being almost taken off by an extendible baton. "I'm just a messenger Lori, actually more like a delivery man. And today I deliver you to my employer. Please be nice to him. And don't think these people in the cafe are going to stop us. They have been well paid, very well paid." With that out of the way the large enforcer picks up the baton once again and carresses it against the side of Lori's head. Kyle gives him a little slap on each side of the face to see if he's out. "Lights out. Let's move him out. The boss is waiting." ------------------------------------ -Some time later- Unable to move and just about able to breath, Lori is dragged along an unknown route. Pulled out of what he assumed was a van he was now flailing around on a cold concrete surface blindfolded. There is absolutely no sound, no scent, no sight.... nothing. Being in this kind of environment is a scary thing, espeically when not even a hour ago you are sitting having a coffee. It shows how vulnerable we truly are as a species, people can be bought, that had been seen in the cafe where no-one was willing to help. If anyone wants to do anything to us they can, we have such close interactions with people in the modern world than anytime they could turn around and stab you in the back. Not saying that's going to happen but it is a plausible theory. As he is being dragged along Lori finds it hard not to let his mind freak out a little. With very little to distract him such as light and sounds his mind begins to go wild with thoeries. Is it Donny? Is it Surreal? I hate Surreal... Bastard.... I know it's him... Or is it? Who are these guys? Where am I? Am I dead? No, can't be dead... Too warm... Cold ground.. Inside? Changing.... Carpet... Fire??? Ouch! Lori is thrown to the ground and his blindfold is removed. He takes a moment to grasp the light with his eyes, everything is blurry, the fire to his right, a chair to his left and figures everywhere... five, no ten, no fifteen?! After a few moments his site returns to normal and he sees only two men standing infront of him. Well he assumes they are men as they are wearing black tuxedos with white shirts and white shoes. The oddest thing about them is their faces, which are covered by beak shaped masks, painted white and black in the style of an eagle's head. They stand arms folded, both exactly the same height and standing infront of an emblem of some sorts. The emblem is also black and white. One black sword and one white sword, joining together in the middle to form a contorted face, some may say a serpent type face. The two figures look down, in unison and move forward to pick Lori up. They place him down in the chair he had seen, a leather wingback. The two amsked figures then flank Lori, standing at each of his sides to ensure he doesn't try anything stupid. Then a large thud. "He enters..." One of the figures voices is loud and clear, very smooth also. Both of them stand to attention as a new member of this cult enters the room. Lori watches him enter. He isn't as big as the other men. Obviously the main man, using the muscle to get what he wants. He too wears a mask, identical to the other apart from 5 small red marks painted across the forehead. As Lori examines him the silence between the two is broken. A slow, melodic tone eminates from the mask, the kind of voice you would use to cure insomnia. "Lori Equinox... you will know me as Reo. Nothing more, nothing less. If you decide to call me something else then so be it, but there will be consequences. You will be hearing that alot too as there will be consequences for every action you make from here on forward. Now I will not mess about, I will come to the point abruptly because there is no time to waste. You see we are not on a personal vendetta again you Mr Equinox. We are just doing our jobs, making sure that our employer gets what he wants. You see our employer knows you very well. You do not know him though. That is all I can tell you. You see he wants to see you succeed in your life as much as possible. It makes him proud, so we're going to ensure that this will happen. Every week if you have a match on Worldwide you will be required to win it. In time our employer may show himself to you but till then you wqill answer only to us. Do I make myself clear?" The whole time Reo is taking Lori is being his usual self, looking around the place and not really paying attention, pretending to yawn and closing his eyes to pretend to sleep. As the question settles into his mind he stands up infront of Reo and smiles. "Reto or whatever you're called. How about you go f**k a pig?" No words could have hit harder as immediately Reo lifts up his arm and sprays Equinox in the face. "If you don't win at Worldwide there will be consequences, severe consequences. Our employer will not allow you to fail..." These were the last words Lori Equinox heard as he was being dragged along a hard and cold tiled floor in a location unknown to himself. He had just been sprayed with some form of sleeping gas and was quickly losing his ability to do anything at all. "You will not fail..." With only those words ringing in his ears, Lori's grip on reality was lost and he slipped into a deep sleep. The next thing he would recall would be waking up on his bed at home, with his girlfriend shaking him.
