Date: Unknown I can remember it like it was yesterday. My first match, my first paycheck, and my first scam at success. Growing up as a kid in Philadelphia you learn a thing or two. That usually involves hustling, robber, and violence. I was never the type of child who would kick dirty in your face because you couldn't play basketball. I'm not the type guy who would get you jumped by seven dudes because you couldn't pay me for a three dollar bet. Well maybe ten dollars but not three, my brother Shawn would've got you crippled messing around with his money. I was always the "pretty-boy" and the "clean cut" guy. I've done a lot of stuff i regret but of course there's nothing you can do about it now. Sometimes you got to sit back and remember the things you did to people and the things you should have done for them, but instead you decided that ruining their lives for the sake of your own is cool. That shit will just come back at you hard. Everyone my name is Pegasus and here's just a simple of my beginning career in the sports entertainment industry. This is chapter one of the chronicles. In the chronicles i will either give you my past, or present. Its mixed up depending on my emotion. Chapter 1: The Ins and Outs of the ropes. Philadelphia, PA May 19th, 2005 Its my first day at the ACPWrestling academy in Philadelphia. I'm going to learn the basics and then shift over to the main roster in about a month. I sat there thinking to myself that the price of this school is way to cheap, one hundred dollars a month is one hell of a bargain but it sounds like a rip off. I'm standing in front of the school nervous like a broad on her prom night. I close my eyes trying to visualize the great success I'm going to receive. " Hey buddy..." a mysterious Puerto Rican called out to me. I asked him whats his problem but he looked at me as if i owed him something. "Can i get a cigarette?" "I don't smoke" i told him. My guess is he's nervous as well going into this gym of unknown. I walk in, i see only two rings in the gym. Now how in the world can 45 wrestlers practice on two rings? Unless we're doing a royal rumble. I walked up to the head trainer, "Hey how come there's only two rings and so much space in this gym for more?" I said to him. He looked at me with a devilish look. I wanted to back off but my ego wouldn't let me. "Kid stick with me and you'll go places." said the trainer with a secretive voice. He appointed me to his main office, i walked over there and entered the room. It was nice and cozy with leather everywhere. My guess is that this school is doing a fantastic job raising money. I sat in place awaiting his arrive but he just stood out there looking at the talent pouring in. He began proceeding to his office where I'm currently at. The first thing that comes to my mind is this guy is going to make me a deal i couldn't refuse. For a 19 yr old college kid from Boise state, there isn't much i would take to get on my grind [grind: get on my feet] He entered his office and sat down across from me. He pulled out a note pad and a pen from out his draw. He told me to sign my name, current address, and phone number. " what do you need all of that for?" i asked him. "Well i know you've heard of Trent Acid, Right?" "Yes i have" i replied. "Well he's going to be your personal trainer for 120 dollars a month." Of course he through a damn bargain i would be nuts to refuse. I accepted it in a hurry. I wrote my name, address, and cell number down on the pad. "Um not to sound rude or ungrateful, but what the hell me?" i asked him. "Kid to tell you the truth i can see you are a college kid on vacation and looking for something to push you in a career field. We currently have 21 students including you. Plus 24 active superstars on the roster needing those rings to brush up on their skills." he told me. I looked at him with a confused face but it took me a minute before i realized that maybe 6 out of that 21 was going to make it in 3-4 months but the rest will either linger on for years giving their money up at will to these cons. "How can you guys do this to us?" i asked him in a angry manner. "Its just business" he said. That word just business actually pisses me off. So you rather me look like a complete jack ass, than help me become a great athlete shining for your damn lime light? Its funny though as i watch his sweet and sour bitch-ass scam everyone in this gym. I nodded my head at him, if he only knew what i was saying in my head he would be pissed off. "Your right, Its just business." I shaken his hand and taken the offer and had my one way ticket to the Indies. I watched everyone face as i left that night. I felt like i was apart of this scam. I knew somebody was going to make it big in this gym. But the question is who's willing to go over the limits to teach themselves the wrestling world? As i was walking to my car i seen this lovely female walking over here. I felt even worst thinking that a great looking chick like that should get a chance at fame. I approached her with confidence. "Hey, how are you?" I asked her. "I'm fine. I'm going in here to get my valet license. I hope i can make it to WWE or TNA." She told me with the most sexiest voice I've ever heard. "Uh good luck." I stated before walking away. I stopped and looked back at her, she smiled at me. "Wait!" i said to her "Your making a big mistake going in there." I added. "Why you say that? This is a really good school. They have like 40 students." she said. I looked at her not wanting to say the true. "Actually there's 21 students and only 4 athletes will make it. I've talked with the head trainer and he didn't tell me anything about valets making it. But don't worry i thought of a plan to get us both in the bag." i stated to her, i just watched her joy bright up. We then walked back into the academy to comfort the head trainer. I knock on the door and he peeps through the window. "Um miss can you give us some privacy?" Said the trainer. "Look here! I want in!" She replied to the trainer. I was really impressed that a woman held her ground in the wrestling business. "Who do you think you are?" he yelled out at her. I told him he's bringing to much attention towards himself. "Okay...Okay...OKAY, Follow the same instruction that Robin received. We then leave that piece of garbage alone in his office. We get outside, "So robin whats the instructions? Also my name is Michelle Queen" she said. "Thats a beautiful name, well the instructions are simple we head off to see international superstar Trent Acid." I said to her. "We I'm so stoked now!!" Michelle said "Um ok...you ready?" I asked her nicely We both walk to our own vehicles. I kept looking at her beauty from my rear view mirror. She had a million dollar smile and hundred million dollar body. She was everything a man want and needed in there life. We started to proceed towards North Philly to continue our wrestling careers. After about 20 minutes of driving we finally get to Trent Acids place. He has a ring in the back yard. "Robin?" Said Trent. "Yeah thats me." i told him, then i introduced Michelle to Trent. "Oh cool, she's your Valet?" Trent asked me but i had absolutely no answer. She quickly replied "YES." I was so shocked at her actions. "Well lets go inside and grab a bit to eat." Trent invited us into his place. "Nice home, Trent." Michelle said Trent nod his head agreeing with Michelle. I knew today was going to be a fantastic day. So, we started to train massively. He shown me the basics and Michelle had private sessions in front of a camera learning the ropes of be a "mouth-piece" for me. Training started to die down, Michelle said she was leaving. Me and Trent escorted her out of the house. I took another look at her as she looked at me. We exchange numbers and called it a night. ~ Trent and I stood outside, he had a smoke while we were talking. "Trent, whats the deal with the head trainer at ACPW ripping people off?" I asked him with concern. "Well Robin, Its Just Business...I guess." He answered with a sigh. This scam must have been going on for years now and it will only get worst as the tide roll. I told Trent i would see him later, i got in my car and called it a day... Dinner and a Movie? Media, PA July 4th, 2005 3:45 PM Two months pasted and me and Trent parted way. He taught me everything he could have. Me and Michelle are still friends and partners but last month we were more than that. I was hers and she was mine. Right now we're keeping business..business and pleasure...pleasure. So far I've had two local house shows and goes by the name Pegasus since my tattoo on my back happens to be angel wings. It looked pretty cool but it took a long time for it to be done. Tonight i promised Michelle to dinner and a movie. Maybe well go see that new spider-man or something. I'm sitting at home waiting for her to call me. Sometimes it seems like it was just yesterday that i first met her outside of the academy. Boy the times changed, A soft knock is heard coming from the door. It took me sometime to get up and answer because my back was a bit damaged during a local show. I opened the door and it appeared to be my brother Shawn. "Robin! Wassup!?" Shawn yelled out at me. I looked at him with happiness but still looking a bit under the weather. "come sit down Shawn" i told him. Me and him sat down in the living room. He turned the TV on to BET. We'd watched a little bit of 106 & Park and then i turned off the television. "Whats wrong robin?" Shawn kept asking me but i keep telling him nothing. "Something must be wrong kid. Oh...you not getting any?" he asked in a soft voice. "Of course i am Shawn!" i yelled out. "oh good because you are a Porter and Porter's do it right." he replied. He started to get on my nerves as the day went on. Shawn told me he came over because he knew i was leaving for Boise, Idaho in 12 hours. The phone rung as Shawn was leaving, i answered it and it was Michelle. Me and her talked for about an hour and a half, i told her to meet me in Media, PA. Its now around seven o'clock and I'm waiting for her at the theater entrance. She then pulls up in a green Audi. She looked stunting as usual. But then i noticed the car right behind her was the car of the head trainer from before. "Robin whats wrong?" she asked me, i pointed right at the dude. "Oh god...its that creep from the academy." Michelle said out loud. The man started to walk right up to us. I quickly stopped him. " Don't worry we didn't report you." i told him. "I know, i just waited to tell you guys that i got work for you two. Pro Wrestling Unplugged is having an annual gathering of Independent superstars. "Okay...And?" Michelle said. "Um well i wanted you guys to represent ACPW." "You must be out your mind dude."- Queen "Actually he has a point" - Pegasus "How is that?"- Queen "He gave us a chance at something we wasn't supposed to have and thats pro-wrestling skills. I can wrestle and you can valet."- Pegasus I knew he was up to no good but its always about Business. Me and Michelle took his offer and we went in the theater to see spider-man 2. ~ The movie ended and i must say Toby Maguire did a good job. I'm hoping for a part 3. "Robin you think we can trust this guy?" Michelle asked me. I didn't want to tell her that i was going to Boise and leaving her here to do her own job with the Indy circuit. " Michelle I want you to know that i love you. Even though we are friends." I told her with so much emotion. She looked at me with tears coming down her face as we share this moment together for what could be our last time. With emotion i told her I'm leaving to night to Boise and i wont see her for a while. See quickly broke down and begged me not to leave her. I wanted Michelle to be with me where ever i go. PWU: Annual Indy Gather! Philadelphia, PA August 28th, 2005 Well a month has pasted and me and Michelle are coming back from Boise. I really couldn't wait to compete in this Philadelphia Indy Gathering. With names such as Sandman, 2 Cold Scorpio, New Jack, and Trent Acid. We arrive just in time to catch the first bout which happens to be an ECW reunion match involving Sandman and New Jack. The match was tasteful but kinda boring. "This match blows!!" coming from someone in the crowd. I wanted to laugh at his disrespect for two originals but i knew i had to set an example for the others here tonight even if that means being the nicest asshole in the building. "Pegasus! You made it" Coming from Trent Acid. Me and Michelle walked over to him, the arena was packed tonight and you can really feel the energy coming from these performers and fans. Tonight we make history in Philly. "Mommy i want to become a wrestler!!" some kid yelled out. I told him to work hard and stay in school. A few minutes passed and my match is up next. Me and Michelle can see that smiley trainer from ACPW running over with a sheet of paper in hand. "Whats that Trainy?" - Pegasus "These are two official ACPW contracts for you guys" - Trainer "Um...nah we're good" - Queen "WHAT!?:"- Trainer "Its Just Business" - Pegasus and Michelle shout out at him as Stricken by Disturbed is heard in the background.
