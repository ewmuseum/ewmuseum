A Bad decision

5 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/05/08 Like everyone I have good days, bad days, and days where it feels like the entire world is dumping its shit on top of me. Two days after I demolished Jake Reed I had one of the latter, and a woman was the reason for it. Two woman to be precise which is perfectly fitting when you think about it; after all when only woman causes trouble that just means marriage. The doorbell rang precisely at eleven in the morning, a mere hour after I rolled out of bed. Since I became part of GCW my sleeping patterns have been erratic, and I’ve found myself training even at midnight. It might be my conscience telling me that I should be running as far as I can from Bagwell and his movement, but the money I’ve made since joining Bagwell tells me that I just happen to be a night person. Regardless I opened up my door to see an epic beauty in front of me. I had fallen in love with the sight of her when I was young and dumb, but while I had fallen out of love long ago her mere presence was still enough to make me lightheaded. I could try to describe her, but it would honestly be similar to describing a rainbow to a blind man. Suffice to say she had raven black hair, complemented by piercing exotic eyes. The figure of a model (not an anorexic one), and a grace that made it seems as if she was gliding across the floor. But despite all that I felt nothing after the initial dizziness passed. My lack of feeling towards her was not caused by the bulge in her stomach indicating that her child was on the way, though I admit I was shocked by the sight. Nor was my lack of feeling caused because of some horrible flaw in her personality, she was honestly a sweet girl though a little snobby because of her looks. But the fact of the matter was that she was just too?.fragile. About two days after I had resigned myself to being nothing more than Erika’s friend I had fallen in love with another friend known as Christine. She was pretty enough, though average when compared to Erika. But there was toughness to her, a strength that I could not help but be drawn to. But whatever trait that Christine was looking for I didn’t have, and both woman phased out of my life two years ago. "Erika," I breathed in surprise when I finally spoke, "to what do I owe the pleasure." Hastily I indicated for her to come in and sit down. I may be a selfish son of a bitch, but damn it I still have manners. "It’s good to see you Dan" she said softly after making herself comfortable on the couch. For a second I stared at the sight of her, and then gave myself a firm mental slap. The last thing I needed was to fantasize about having a loveless affair with a pregnant woman that I used to have a crush on. "Its good to see you to," I replied honestly, "but you still haven’t told me why you’re here. Last time I saw you, you told me that I was wasting my life with the wrestling thing, and that I should call your boyfriend Mark if I ever wanted a real job." The last part of the sentence was said in a truly cold tone. Even back then when I had transferred my passion elsewhere, her uncaring tone stung me deeply. Though I suppose in a way I should thank her, the day she told me that was the day that I won the match which caught the eye of the first major wrestling organization I ever worked for. I was later told that my opponent had been sent to the hospital for three broken ribs, a fractured arm, and a concussion. She winced and was very quiet for a moment before replying in a cracking voice. "I’m sorry" she said unhappily, "but I guess you proved me wrong in the end anyway. From what I hear you’re doing good in that organization you’re in and might be a champion soon, Mark left me with this" she patted her stomach "for another man." I wanted to laugh, but not because of her predicament. She believed I was doing well in my job, but that was barely true. I was nothing more than a violent thug and my success rested on the continued assumption that I was useful to the Movement, and that the Movement was strong enough to keep whoever I beat down from getting retaliation. Lost in my sad thoughts I sat down next to her, and without thinking we looked at each other. What happened next was obvious from a mile away, and I tried to hold myself back the entire time. I knew that I was asking for wracking bouts of doubt and guilt, and I knew that I should just help her out and escort her out. But whether because I was weak, lonely, or just too horny for my own good, I stopped fighting when her lips touched mine, and instead helped her take the belt of my pants Later that night as I listened to her softly snoring while I lay in bed I took a moment to think of all the fucked up things going on in my life. I was in a relationship with a woman for whom I felt nothing, but couldn’t seem to get rid of. My entire career was relying on the good graces of a world class asshole, and now I had fight my way through a load of wrestlers just to get a shot at a championship. The thought should have depressed me, instead for the first time in a long time I began to feel excited about getting into the ring. If I managed to win that title it would be mine, unconditionally mine. It wouldn’t be Bagwell’s or the Movement’s, no matter what he thought. It would be my ticket to establishing my own identity in the wrestling industry again. I smiled as Erika began to stir. "You know," I said softly in her ear as she awoke with a yawn, "I am not entirely sure that what we did would be classified as smart, but I’d be lying if I said that I regretted it also." I was telling the truth. While I know that no strings attached sex is, by definition, every guys dream I’ve never been good at it. Call me crazy, but I can keep myself from feeling some sort of connection to someone I’ve had sex with. With a sudden surge of guilt I looked at Erika wondering whether we had managed to hurt the baby. I heard somewhere that it was alright to have sex with a pregnant woman up to a certain point, but I had no idea whether that was true. After a few moments of worry I settled down. Whether or not what we did was wise, there was simply nothing that I could do about it now. "This is not what I thought would happen," she said with a quiver in her voice, "I was depressed and I needed someone to talk to and I knew you were here," she stopped as tears rolled down her cheeks. Gently I wiped them away, "look," I said a bit helplessly, "if you want to pretend this never happened I am fine-"I was cut as she kissed me fiercely then curled against me. Completely confused I resigned myself to the knowledge that I would never understand woman and simply closed my eyes. But as I could feel the wetness on my thumb I could not help but think that Christine wouldn’t have cried, and wondered what that implied for what had just happened with Erika.
