Bouncin' Back

11 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/11/05 There come those points in a man’s life when nothing can seem to go wrong. These are the times when one feels that he is on top of the world. He feels as though he is riding the high of life and will never come down. The world is his oyster and its precious pearl gleams brightly for him like a shimmering star that will never extinguish. All is well, all is bittersweet, but life sometimes has a way of quickly turning the tables for the worst. A win streak quickly turns into a losing streak. Joy quickly turns to depression. A prosperous dynasty goes from having it all to being nothing but an afterthought in the blink of an eye. Things change and they can change without notice. It is a fact of life that we all have become accustomed to and have learned to deal with. We enter “The Violence” Vince Jones, a man that once felt he was on top of the world, a man that had all eyes on him in Lights Out Wrestling. No less than a month ago the man was a star, a superstar at that? [A picture of “The Violence” Vince Jones in his all-black street gear on the front cover of an issue of Lights Out Magazine flashes up on the screen. It slowly fades away and the screen goes back to black.] “He was considered one of the most feared and dangerous men to ever step foot through their doors. Vince Jones was making waves, making his presence felt while on a steady rise to the top of the ranks, but then came the onset of hard times. Business quickly went sour in Lights Out Wrestling and the federation closed its doors. “The Violence” Vince Jones was no longer a part of LOW and had to find a new home. Just as quickly as he was out of a job he gained a new one in Global Championship Wrestling, a powerhouse in the world of sports entertainment. In no time flat Vince Jones had gone from being a championship contender to being a man hoping to get booked in a match, desperate to make an impact once again. He went from being one of main attractions to being just another face in the crowd. He went from having his spot near the very top of the mountain to sitting at the bottom of the barrel once again trying to claw his way back up. The journey begins. The journey will be long and hard, but “The Violence” Vince Jones is a fighter, a man that is willing to do whatever it takes to make it. And when he wants to accomplish something he does it by any means necessary? [Scene] [The camera slowly fades into the dark streets of New York City, The Big Apple. The camera pans around the scene and we see that all is surprisingly calm in this part of the city. A few lonely stragglers can be seen walking up and down the streets, minding their business and paying no attention to the other citizens that they pass by on their way. Each and every one of them is wrapped up in the ups and down of their own lives. One of those citizens is “The Violence” Vince Jones is spotted in his usual all-black street gear. He can be seen walking down the dark, city sidewalk in utter silence. A few streetlights are seen here and there that illuminate the ground below them. A few Police car sirens can be heard off in the distance ringing out in the night, but he continues marching on with his thoughts racing through his mind.] “So, who would’ve thought things’d be like this” The Violence has worked like a dog, forever tryin? to get where he’s been. For months on end The Violence was doin? his thing in L-O Dubb, takin? names, and sittin? on top of the world. Everything was lookin? good and just like that it’s all gone. Now The Violence is back at square one once again. The name of the game has changed. V. Jones is now in Global Championship Wrestlin?, just another place to take over, that’s all ?” [Vince Jones stops in his tracks as he walks past a small newspaper and magazine stand where an old gray haired man with glasses is seen sitting there alone in his chair. The old man is busily straightening up his wares and he looks up and sees Vince Jones who has a GCW magazine in hand staring intently at the cover.] Old man: Hello there. You looking to make a purchase? [Vince Jones looks up from the magazine cover and glares at the old man with a look that says “Leave me alone!] Old man: Okay. I’m sorry to bother you there, sir. [Vince Jones just glares at him and then refocuses on the GCW magazine cover and just shakes his head.] ??some may think that its gonna be tough startin? over. Some of these b*tches prolly thinkin? a man like Vince Jones don’t got what it takes, but V. Jones? been there before. [Vince Jones sets the magazine down and the camera zooms in on the cover of it as he walks away. A picture of Vince Jones is seen on the front cover and the title reads “Vince Jones: The Next Big Thing or Just a Bunch of Hype?”. The camera turns and catches sight of Vince Jones once again as he is walking down the street towards a wire fence. He reaches the fence and looks inside at what appears to be a caged in basketball court. There are a few lights that are shining down from above gently illuminating the empty basketball court. Vince Jones presses against the fence and looks inside at the court as more thoughts begin to race through his head.] “This place brings back memories of the old days. Vince Jones hasn’t been back to this spot in years. This spot was just the beginning, the beginning of Vince Jones going from nobody to a king of these streets.” [The camera slowly fades out and fades back in as we see a much younger Vince Jones leaning against the same fence on a bright spring day in New York City peering through the fence and just gazing at the court. All of a sudden a large hand comes to a rest on his shoulder and he turns around to see a black, street thug standing beside him looking at him in amusement.] Thug: So, you ready for your initiation, kid? You look like you ready. [Vince Jones nods his head in agreement as he turns towards the thug.] Vince Jones: Yeah. I was born ready, Let’s do this sh*t, man! Thug: Okay. Well, it’s like this. You betta be ready cuz I hear from K-9 that he’s really gonna be puttin? you cats on the grind today. Now, come with me. He should be up in this spot pretty soon. Nah mean?” [Vince Jones nods in agreement.] Vince Jones: Yeah. I’m feelin? ya on that. Let’s get goin?. [Vince Jones follows the thug through the gates leading into the court and they meet up with a small group of other tough looking teens that are there for the same reason Vince is. They all turn and look at Vince Jones and then turn away as they await their initiation.] A voice: Good to see all you cats made it here. [The camera turns and catches sight of a large black thug wearing all-black street gear. He is followed by a small legion of other thugs that follow him in through the gate at the opposite side of the courts. As they approach the teens look on at them in admiration, fear, and respect.] Thug leader: The Big Dawg was thinkin? some of you lil boys were gonna punk out today. Guess you boys got more balls than I thought. Or you cats think you got some balls on ya atleast. [As the Big Dawg and his legion of thugs stop in front of the group of teens he laughs to himself and looks at all of them in amusement. He begins to pace back and forth eyeing each and everyone of the teens up in down and measuring each one of them up.] The Big Dawg: Lot of you boys out here thinkin? you gonna get a spot with us. You cats tryin? to act tough. Look at all you. You cats tryin? to look hard like you think you foolin? me. What you all show up here for anyways? You all think that just cuz you look hard and cuz you might’ve beat up a couple cats at the playground during recess that you boys EDW material here? [shakes his head] Don’t think so! You cats don’t know jack “bout the clique. We the best of the best here. When you with the EDW you “bout gettin” sh*t done by any means necessary. You don’t know what it means to be down with us. [The Big Dawg pauses and looks around at the group of fellow teens.] The Big Dawg: If you want in the clique you gotta really want it. You gotta want it so damn bad that you’re willin? to do whateva it takes to snatch it. ? [The Big Dawg stops in front of Vince Jones who looks up at him intently. The Big Dawg looks down at Vince Jones in amusement and laughs to himself.] The Big Dawg: So, I see you listened to what I said and took it to heart. Good man. Good to see you want a spot in the clique, but now K-9 wanna know somethin? here. You want a spot in the clique, right? Vince Jones: Yeah! K-9; You sure, kid? Vince Jones: Damn straight! [K-9 nods at Vince’s comments.] K-9: You willin? to do whateva to get in? Vince Jones: Hell yeah! K-9: You willin? to go through each and every one of these other cats [points at the other teens assembled around Vince] here one by one to get a spot? [Vince looks around at all the other teens assembled around him that are also eager to get a spot as well.] Vince Jones: Ya damn right I am! K-9: I like that. I like that, kid. [K-9 walks away from Vince Jones and looks on at the rest of the teens.] K-9: You see that? That’s what I’m talkin? about here. That’s the kind of attitude you gonna need if you even thinkin? bout bein? down with us. Got it? But when it comes down to it, not even attitude like that is enough for you cats to make the cut. You can have all the attitude you want, but if you can’t do nothin? with it then your attitude means jack sh*t to us! I see a lot of you showed up here today, but when we’re finished I’ll guarantee that a lot of you cats either gonna be laid out cold or runnin? back home to mom cryin? like the lil b*tches you are? [K-9 turns around and looks directly at Vince Jones. All of a sudden the sound of a cellphone can be heard ringing. Vince Jones snaps back into reality. He is still leaning against the fence surrounding the court in the dark of night in NYC. The cell phone rings again. Vince Jones reaches in his pocket, whips out the phone, and answers.] Vince Jones: Yo. V here. What’s up? A voice: Ah, “The Violence” Vince Jones, just the man I wanted to talk to.” [Vince Jones raises an eyebrow in confusion.] Vince Jones: Huh? What the.” What you want, Reno? Reno Banks: You and I need to talk. [Vince Jones shakes his head.] Vince Jones: You and V’ll talk when the time calls for it. Aiight?” Reno Banks: No, we really need to talk, Mr. Jones. I’ve got a few issues that I need to discuss with you like for starters, your sudden vanishing act after your match at LOW’s UnEarthed and a few other issues. [Vince rolls his eyes at the thought.] Vince Jones: And why is it so important for us to talk about that sh*t, huh?” It’s the past. Its done with. We move on! No need bringin? up old sh*t like that! L-O Dubb is dead! When L-O Dubb fell off the map all that sh*t was buried with it! Reno Banks: I know, I know, Mr. Jones; but, I have plans. I have big plans for you and I need to know where your head’s at. What do ya say? You know where to find me. Stop on by and we can discuss a little bit of business. I can put you back on the top of the world in no time flat and you’ll be setting the Global Championship Wrestling scene on fire guaranteed. [Vince Jones pauses in thought.] Vince Jones: Okay. We can talk business, Reno. That’s fine. V. Jones is out. He’ll meet you wheneva. Reno Banks: Got it. Bye. [Vince Jones hangs up the cellphone and sticks it back in his pocket. He leans back against the fence and just shakes his head as he tries to clear his head a bit. The camera slowly fades to black.]
