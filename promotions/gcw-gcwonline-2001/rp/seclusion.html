Seclusion

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: WW115 Light drips through the spotted wet windows. The low droning of the raindrops hitting the roof and overhang over the outside porch provide for a soothing ambiance in the early morning. In the rays of light through the window, some from the sun beckoning at the dark rain clouds but mostly from the porch lights, smoke and dust particles dance freely through the house. The fireplace remains smokey from the fully burnt logs resting on the fire rack inside. Littered through the soot, small yellowed pieces of paper with text stare out into the room. Unreadable fragments of words and sentences mix and match to make gibberish, but a few strong words still remain unburned; God, Jesus, Father... Next to the fireplace lays a couple of old pillows and blankets which look to be recently thrown aside, but the restless soul didn't get far. He lays a couple feet away, still restless against the hard wood floor. The grotesque scars on his chest stretch with every breath, but this usually stoic figure shows anguish behind his restless eyes. Although asleep, any observer would think he was very much so awake. His eyes rush from side to side as if searching for sleep. His hands clench and his face contorts in pain, but then relaxes. Behind his dark eyes, God only knows what he is experiencing. *THWAP THWAP THWAP* The restless soul stirs, panning his eyes around the room looking for the source of his disturbance. *THWAP THWAP THWAP* He jumps up quickly and rushes over to the door, looking out the peep hole first. the curvature of the glass makes it difficult to see the man standing outside the door. Maybe he is heavy, or maybe it is the glass, but the shining badge on his chest is easily distinguishable. "Zacharia Taylor, I need to have a word with you!" Zacharia turns around and finds his hoodie on the ground. He stretches through it, his joints cracking and popping before righting himself and reaches the door. The force with which he opens the door startles the police officer to where he reaches for his gun, but quickly rights himself once he sees Taylor standing before him. The rain stains on the officer's jacket and on the elder man's mustache and beard show strain and tire. "My name is Sheriff Brohnson with the Salem County Sheriff's Department. Do you mind if I have a word with you?" Taylor doesn't respond with anything more than stepping back and a welcoming hand showing him the way inside. The sheriff shakes off as if shaking off a chill and pans his head to observe the room. The room, much like most of the house is pretty empty besides a few pieces of furniture left from the previous owner. Taylor walks past the sheriff and makes his way to a polished table and set of chairs. The sheriff follows. "Mr. Taylor, I want to first off say it is a pleasure to have a celebrity live in our quiet little county, " Taylor raises an eyebrow at the word 'pleasure', "I know you had a little run in with one of my men yesterday." Taylor is already becoming bored with the conversation and rubs his eyes of sleep. Why are all the cops so welcoming? Don't they have something better to do than sit with me at my table and bullshit? "I know that with being a celebrity and all, you may run into a few things here and there, but we don't want any trouble. We are a quiet little county, and we want to keep it that way. We don't want you to be taking anything into your own hands, son. I've been a cop for many years and I can tell you that getting your nose deep in those kind of problems just makes it worse for yourself." The sheriff smiles as if impressed with himself with the authority in his voice, but it all changes when Taylor opens his mouth. "Sheriff, I'm not quite sure what you're talking about. A cop came up to me last night after I saw an intruder in my house. Nothing came from it, nor did anything come further, and now you're here sitting at my table in the early hours of the morning. I'm finding it hard to see where I'm at fault..." Sheriff Brohnson clears his throat and attempts to straighten his back in authority, but the actions go unnoticed by Taylor. "Zacharia, that's what I'm talking about. I don't want to see you going after someone and getting yourself into some sort of trouble. You know that celebrities fall harder than the common man if the press gets involved! Now just, if you find something, oh I don't know, suspicious...give me a call." Taylor takes the business card that is pushed across the table and looks at it briefly before bouncing it on the table in front of him. "Thanks Sheriff, but I'll be just fine." The sheriff stares at him for a long second and then stands up and nods, touching his hat in acknowledgment. He heads for the door with Taylor in tow, before turning around. "You know, for being a celebrity, you sure do live on the down. You can really jazz this place up with some..." "Sheriff Brohnson, thanks for your concern but..." "Alright, I overstayed my welcome. Well, take care now, I'll be in touch..." Almost immediately after the sheriff finished his sentence, Taylor slammed the door closed between them and slid the dangling lock chain into the slide. Taylor turns around and stretches out of the remaining strain of sleep on his body. He leans over and picks up the old blanket and pillows and tosses them to the corner of the room and out of the way. He takes a look at the fire place and stares for a few moments before pulling himself away to the kitchen to make his breakfast. ******************* The rain finally subsides, but the moisture lingers in the air. The mailman hands Taylor his mail and attempts at a conversation with him, but Taylor waves him off the best to his ability and walks away. He used to love conversations with strangers, but lately, he just has the urge to be left alone, and he does just that. The two envelopes that were handed over to him contrasted the other to the fullest extent. One was an Avon booklet asking him to be a distributor and the other was from Global Championship Wrestling about his upcoming match with Mark in the Dark. Funny how quickly GCW changes their records when told and it was only told in passing at 114. Taylor follows the footsteps of the mailman with his eyes to his mail truck and sees someone sitting in a car further down the road. Taylor pans to observe the street and looks back to see the car still there with someone inside. No other houses were there except the three he bought, yet this car was there. Taylor takes little more notice and heads back to his house. Taylor tosses the envelopes on the table and grabs the shaker resting on the counter and begins to chug it. He wipes his chin of excess and immediately heads out the kitchen door and towards the lake. Against the dock rests a simple row boat with old style oars. Next to the boat is a yellow rope with a single hook anchor attached to the end. Both of these comprise Taylor's work out for the first half of the day. He makes a B-Line for the rope and anchor. Next to the set up, there is a small rope attached to a piling. He pulls up the rope and ties the rope around the handle of the gallon of water he took from the house and tosses it in the lake to keep cool. He then begins to stretch his arms and back before picking putting on gloves which are in his pocket. Taylor lifts up the anchor in his right hand and the end of the rope in the other. He whips the anchor out into the lake and quickly holds the end of the rope with both his hands until it hits the bottom of the lake. He takes a deep breath and begins to pull, hand over hand until it comes to the edge of the dock. He switches hands and throws it out again, but with his left arm it doesn't go as far, and he repeats the process. The bright sun at noon has no mercy and hits Taylor with everything it has. After a few throws, he tosses off the hoodie continues. After twenty five throws per arm, he begins to stretch again and drops to the deck for pushup and crunches. Sweat begins to bead on his head and rush into his arms, forcing them to close and burn. After about ten minutes, he grabs the gallon of water he tossed in the water on the rope and begins to drink it until about half of it is gone. He tosses the gallon back into the lake and jumps into the boat and begins to row straight out across the lake. The lake is massive. When Taylor goes straight across the lake he ends up in a swamp down the line, so he turns and goes along the swamp grounds and follows that which leads to the center of town. Really starting to feel the heat on his back and the burn in his muscles, he pulls the boat up onto a slope of grass and begins to stretch his back. A few people at the nearby dirt beach stare at him, but not because they don't know him, but out of curiosity of his scars. Taylor immediately becomes self conscious and steadily walks to the side of a building and rests up against the shaded wood. It's odd how the people here react to him as opposed to up in Clifton. Clifton, many knew him as "that boy that trains at Bagwell's gym" or "the religious leader from the church." Nobody knows him here. And if they do, it is the wrestler, which they've seen his scars and his action in the ring, but very few know Zacharia Taylor. The police now know him, and a few in the town, but nobody knows him. Thoughts start to swirl around Taylor's head. He thinks back to the other day at WW 114. The thought makes him feel secluded, alone, distant... Taylor really has nobody left in the world. He has no Chris Bagwell to call for advice or to train with. He has no Father Emanuel or Christian Thomas. You know, strike that last name from the record. But it is true, there is nobody left. Nobody but enemies... Why did Bagwell turn on him? That question swirls around Taylor's head everyday and nearly all day. His head begins to hurt when searching for an answer. Why did he lie to him about his death? Why did he come out and help FishBough take him out at the Rampage Finals? Bagwell is always in it for the glory, and now that he has a taste of it, running the Establishment, there is no way to tell where Bagwell will turn and who's back he will stab next. Will it be FishBough and GBJ? It is hard to tell because Bagwell is just as ruthless as Caldera, and everyone saw on national TV what happen to the old Establishment. The big question is...What will WW115 bring? Taylor bows his head deeper, arms resting over his shoulders. He looks out over the calm lake. Oh, how it would be nice to just relax and not have to worry about anything. He could. He could just give up wrestling and live off the money he has for as long as it will take him. But then again, Taylor does have a restless soul. Total seclusion would be impossible... Taylor slowly drifts off to sleep while deep in thought against the shaded building. The wind picks up, blowing the trees and everything into a sweet chaos. Soon, he will be in the ring again, surrounded and outnumbered... but that is the story of Taylor's life...Seclusion, Outnumbered, Helpless...
