Road Trip!

Date: Yesterday Today we find the Rt. Hon. Phil Atken MP and Sir Dirk Dickwood (Superagent and man) sitting in a car. Not just any car mind you, Dirk Dickwood's gold plated car. Never ask him about this decision unless you value you wind pipes. Sitting in the back seat of the car is found to be the always delightful Candisqua Nosecondname, that saucy temptress. We join our heroes and their whore in the middle of their road trip to the next GCW show, which may have just ran into a slight snag. Dickwood: I thought Japan was a state. Atken: I'm almost certain it isn't and oh look, I'm right! Dickwood: It's alright, we'll just have to drive for a little bit longer. Atken: I'm pretty sure you can't drive across the ocean Dickypoo. Dickwood: The world being separated by ocean is one big conspiracy, everyone who know the cold hard facts knows that the liberals try to perpetuate this myth so as to destroy free trade. Atken: I have no idea what you just said but I'm certain my brain is now melting due to it. On the other hand, it may just be simply trying to escape from my head after being a poor innocent victim to quite possibly the stupidest thing I've heard in the past ten minutes. Dickwood: What did you hear ten minutes ago? Atken: Candisqua said that midgets were people too. Dickwood: They're not. Atken: Well clearly, otherwise your current wife would have all sorts of legal cases against you. Is she still locked up in that cage the cat crapped in? Dickwood: We don't have a cat... Atken: Okay, well I crapped in it but in my defence I was really needed to go and someone was in the toilet. Dickwood: I have three toilets in my house... Atken: OH! Yeah, I also hate your wife even more than you do. She's just so... creepy. I think it's all the burnt flesh. Dickwood: I do regret doing that. Atken: So does any guest at your house. Candisqua almost threw up, ain't that right? Candisqua: I'MA GONNA KILL YOU BOTH. Atken: Oh Candisqua, you are a constant delight. Dickwood: One day I think she may actually kill us both. Atken: I'm certain it will happen. She is an angry lady. Aren't you sweetums? Candisqua: DIE. Atken: The wit flows from her like her menstrual flow. Eurgh, that was horrible imagery, even for me. Dickwood: I'm mildly... Atken: I'm going to stop you there. I don't know where that was going to end and quite frankly I don't care. I am saving myself from ever knowing. Candisqua: SICKO. Atken: It's like she reads minds. I swear. Sometimes I wish she had teeth, thou...YARGHLE Candisqua, in a moment of outrage, or perhaps passion, jumps out of her seat and drives her head full force into Phil's crotch. Dickwood: Saw that coming. Candisqua: THAT'S CONTACT! THAT'S ANOTHER FIFTY! Dickwood: Why do you have to yell everyth...EYURGHS Candisqua: MAKE THAT A HUNDRED. Atken: That's it! Phil takes his seatbelt off (safety first!) and after a good five minutes of the most pathetic display of male masculinity in quite some time, manages to drag Candisqua out of the car. The last sound she ever hears is the screeching of the gold plated Primus and the word "spelunking". Returning to the car... Atken: I miss Candisqua, you could always rely on her for good road stories. Dickwood: You just kicked her out the car in a fit of rage and now suddenly you miss her? Not to mention she just headbutted us both in the crotch, with that steel plate of hers too! Atken: Still, I'll always wonder if she was truly the one. Dickwood: Here's a tip, if you have to pay for 'em, they're not the one. Atken: You paid for Isla... Dickwood: I think I just proved my point. Atken: Things not going well? Dickwood: How many times do we have to go through this. I set her on fire and keep her locked in a cage, things are going perfectly fine on my end. Atken: Oh that's why! I keep trying to remember why you're still my agent, despite thinking Japan was a state we could drive to. You're fucking terrifying. Dickwood: Only to friends and family, I'm a pure professional with clients. Atken: I'm a client! Dickwood: Don't be silly, you're a pro-wrestler, you're not real people. Atken: I'm also your only client at the moment, so shush your mouth with shush juice or I'll force it down your throat. Dickwood: Point taken. Atken: No, the point is exactly what you're trying to avoid. Dickwood: Oh god, that was just unnecessarily foul. Atken: I actually feel ashamed of myself for saying it, to be honest with you. It's like pain in my brain, perhaps it is trying to escape. Dickwood: I saw that happen once. Atken: Yes, odd that brain went flying out the man's head just mere moments after you repeatedly bludgened him with a baseball bat. It truly is one of those oddities of life. Dickwood: What can I say, I really hate the homeless. Who doesn't? Atken: Those with a kind heart? Dickwood: They're all religious freaks who want to turn them on to god, is what I do really all that worse? Atken: Yes! Dickwood: Really? Atken: What you do is technically and I stress it is only a technicality, murder. Dickwood: Should I speak to a lawyer? Atken: Nah, it'll be fine. Well unless you start attacking real people. Still, you sicken me. Dickwood: What else is new? Atken: Your complete lack of ability to find Japan? How the hell am I meant to kick some buttock if I'm not even there. Then there's that whole ?you better show up this time or you're fired, you druggy scum? thing. Your mother can be so cruel some times. Dickwood: I keep telling her your into hookers now, not drugs but does she listen? Does she my arse. Atken: DON'T SAY ARSE. SAY ASS. Dickwood: Last time you said that we were cultured gentlemen and I should say arse. Atken: Well that was before I was booked in a match with some Scottish guy. After living in Scotland for a scant 22 years of my 28 year life, I can gladly say I'm finally glad I curbed that addiction. Dickwood: Addiction? Atken: Everyone in Scotland is required to produce at least five crackbabies in their lifetime, otherwise they are exhiled from the country. Dickwood: Did you produce too many? Atken: I'd rather not say. What I will say it Andy Murray is a cocaine snorting man of shame who deserves to be beaten with a large stick and if we ever make it to Japan, I will find that stick and that man and well... I think you can put two and two together. Dickwood: You'll make crackbabies so you can gain back your citizenship? Atken: Why do you exist? Dickwood: On the first day, god created agents. Atken: Weren't you just mere moments ago going on about how awful religion was? Dickwood: I change my mind on things very quickly. Atken: I'm changing my mind on the thought of ?you know, I do need an agent? very quickly. Dickwood: FINE THEN. Atken: That was god damn childish and you know it. Dickwood: If you don't start respecting your agent, I'm turning this car around and we'll never go to Japan. Atken: Firstly, no one respects you, not even your burnt to a crisp midget wife. Secondly, if we don't make it to Japan, we both lose money, not just me. Thirdly, YOU CANNOT DRIVE TO JAPAN. We've been through this already. Dickwood: So, should we go to an airport or something? Atken: I think that'd be a good idea.
