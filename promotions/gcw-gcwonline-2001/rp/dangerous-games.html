Date: Today [The sun is about to set as Shawn Johnson lay in his bed. He looks up into the gazing Sun to spot an Eagle...Wait a minute, what the fuck?] [Let's try that again. We pick up to Shawn hammering into a piece of wood for his home in Calgary........Alberta, Canada when he walks over to the door. He walks in, then begins speaking to himself] 'the Franchiser': Ah, it feels good to be back home. In Calgary, where I don't have to worry about anything because, well, I am one of the most respected men in the city. It all started back in 1982 when I was kicking the hell out of Stu Hart in the Dungeon.. [In that instant, Keri Fellows drops magically out of the sky as Shawn looks at her with a brazen stare.] 'the Franchiser': Ah, hello, Keri. How nice of you to drop by. Keri Fellows: Anything for you Shawn... SJ: Well fuck you....[Confused] Huh? KF: I love your work. SJ: [Shawn is obviously caught off guard] Well, of course, I am sure many people do. KF: [Gazing] Uh huh.... SJ: Well....right. I don't want to pull an LeStatt Knight and put you to sleep with a long epic. Let's get this boat on the water. [To himself] What a nice new analogy. KF: What's that? SJ: Ah, nothing. Anyways, do you have some questions? KF: ....Shawn, this is only your third match here in GCW, and you are already in a 30 man Main Event. How do you get these things so fast? SJ: ...well, you see Fellows, I am Shawn Johnson. [...with that, Shawn says no more...] KF:...alright. Would you mind... SJ: ..before you say anything else, let me reiterate for you. I am Shawn Johnson, the freakin' 'Franchiser' for God's sake! When you hire me to a federation, I am going to put the asses in the seats....the bottom line is..Shawn Johnson sells. KF: Hmmm, alright. First, I want to dissect your team mates. How do you feel about Toxic ? SJ: ...what can I say? I know nothing of this man. I don't know if he is a luchador, I don't know if he slept with some men to get into the fed. I don't know. I think he might be Hardcore of whatnot, something I personally never was in to. KF: ....and why's that. SJ: Well, look at my body... [...Shawn stands up and flexes his ripped muscles...] SJ: I am essentially the perfect male.. KF: ...oh, I know. Personally. SJ: [in Shock] KERI! We are on live television! KF: ..uh, Shawn, I am a news reporter. SJ: Ah, yes, of course. Uh....[Stumped] Anyways, no, I do not know Toxic. KF: Have you heard the comments from the man known as LeStatt Knight? SJ: Of course I have Keri, you Pagan fool. I have seen him prancing around the right here and there. I don’t know if this guy thinks he’s a wrestler, or a reject from Interview with a Vampire. Yeah?.I said it. KF: So, are you saying that AP is overrated? SJ: In a way, Yes. Take nothing away from him, he is a great athlete. But you have to stop and think. . I am Shawn 'the Calgary Crusher' Johnson. If any big time government scientist want a perfect specimen to clone, Damnit, I am the man. I mean, I can come out here and I can talk for about 45 minutes, as LeStatt does and you know whats the best thing? KF: Whats that? SJ: He will never ever know I talked the talk about him cause he thinks he’s too good to listen to my interviews. [Break] [ Right then, Keri gets up and walks out. And in walks Lopsang. Yes, the wait is over. Lopsang has arrived back to the GCW. Lopsang comes by Shawn and picks up an axe and begins to split some wood.] SJ: [In shock] I didn’t even know that you could lift that axe handle, Lopster. [Lopster looks up to Shawn and drops the axe. He walks down to Shawn’s lake and jumps in the water. That all.] P.S. What a weird ending to a terrible roleplay, yes? That’s mah new gimmick, a really really bad interviewee, lol.
