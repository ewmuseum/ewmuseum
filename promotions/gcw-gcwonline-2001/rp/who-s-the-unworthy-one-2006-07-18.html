Who's the unworthy one?

18 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/18/06 Home Sweet Home Well it was a few days ago for Dani. They had all taken the sixteen hour a day drive, for three days straight run from Washington D.C. back to the town they had all called home since they had been in that other fed before. Las Vegas, Nevada. Between them all, they had two houses in the Queen's Ridge neighborhood. Blaise and Ukyo had one, where Annie's hand maiden, Kaylee acted as their nanny for the twin girls, and the other house was for Dani, Josh and Gray. And as of right now, also Alexis. Fortunately they had seven bedrooms total. Quite a few extra if needed. But there had been a few surprises right after they'd gotten home. Dani had just wanted to see Angel, her Hyacinth Macaw that Kaylee and Annie had made sure was taken care of for her while they were gone. She'd even talk to him over the phone sometimes. She watched as he puffed up his feathers and then shook everything back into shape as she walked into her bedroom. Although she had to admit one thing, the living room looked really good with the GCW Tag Team and TV titles sitting on the mantle over the fake fireplace. But Annie had something else for her. Something that the others hadn't known about... They had a new landlord for the last month or so, Blaise had been given both houses when the company that the Order owned them through liquidated a lot of it's resources. However, apparently Blaise didn't think he needed to own both houses... And this one, he was signing over to Dani. It had almost put her on the floor when she found out. The house was huge, it was bigger then some of the hotel rooms she's stayed in, some of the bed and breakfasts they'd been in. It was just big. And now it was going to be hers. But it couldn't last long. Not a few days later did the fax come from GCW saying where they had to be, and what was going on. The brothers weren't booked, but Dani was... against the man who'd decided to hit her with a chair when he interfered in her match at Warpath just a few days before. She still had a band aid to hold the cut on her forehead closed... She would be facing this masked man who then went on to call her undeserving? The man who laid down for Shadow quicker then then a Las Vegas whore drops her panties for a twenty spot. A man who wasn't even considered to be booked in the opening gauntlet at Warpath. A man who needed to use a chair to get his point across. Well he can have his shot. But when you look at it, Dani has earned this title, and she's not planning on giving it up all that soon. She beat Jayce Bradley, who at one time held this title and the cruiser weight title at the same time. She'd beaten Jacob McKail who'd gone through not only Bradley, but quite a few others in the federation before he finally met her, and his match. The man who took on how many others in the gauntlet and went right through them like a hot knife through butter. And then he stepped up and faced Dani and this masked idiot had to get involved, cause he thinks we're unworthy? Who the fuck does he think he is? Brandon Youngblood? At least Youngblood has a winning record, huh? Asshole or not, he can so far back up his words. But this masked idiot, he's got a screw loose. And it'll be Dani who tightens it up for him this week. Then again, he whacked McKail too. Got to expect the expected here. McKail doesn't seem like one to let something like that slide. The KnightMare Brigade's rules are pretty clear on this. We don't know if McKail could have pulled out one more win that night at Warpath. We don't know if he'd bitten off more then he could chew either. It was something that wasn't sure now that some self righteous asshole had decided to take matters into his own hands. But, for all of this we had to go to Vancouver. Dani had been there before, a long while back in another federation. She wasn't to up on the place, really. So she had been really happy when Josh had suggested they cut a few hours south for a few days... to Seattle. So they did. This was the place that Joshua had grown up, and Dani hadn't really been here with him before. At least not with him for a real visit. They'd passed through, and Dani had met his mother before, but never for a long period of time. And now she was here, with him, with her, and with Blaise and Ukyo too. Gray and Alexis? Well they didn't have the option to fly up with needing to watch over and lock up the mobile lab. They were going to secure it and come up a few days later. Dani could swear that Gray was getting a thing for Alexis, but she had nothing to go off of. But then again, she might have been seeing the same thing that he'd shown towards Kaylee, and she'd even tried to kill him before. Does this seem like rambling? Well it is. Cause this is how Dani's mind is right now. So many things going on at one time. So many things that are cascading down upon her. She's the TV Champion, she's dating, should be engaged to one half of the tag team champions. She's involved in a war in the federation she's joined, cause the others don't seem to get it... things change, things don't stay the same. And if they don't adapt, they'll loose. Then again, this masked idiot was just a prime example of this. He sits there and talks about how he deserves this and deserves that. Just like the rest of the fed, he thinks you can sit on your ass and get things handed to you. Not anymore. ?And this is the old Marketplace. Of course everything that used to be here has now been moved up to the street level when Seattle was rebuilt to what you now know as Pike Place Market.? The tour guide was boring, but Margaritte had insisted they take the Seattle Underground Tour. Everyone entertained it, but she seemed to not get the fact that Blaise and Ukyo had lived here less then a year ago, and Dani wasn't too concerned with old shops that were buried in rubble, burned out and previously flooded due to poor judgment in building the city the first time. ?Isn't this so exciting, guys?? Margaritte wasn't one to hide things back. Her French accent was still there from coming over to the states so many years ago. She had been a student when she'd met the father of Grayson Brand, and became his second wife. And then they'd go on to produce between them the man who she loved. So she had to put up with her. ?Yeah, but where are the monsters and alligators?? It was Josh, poking fun at his mother. ?I thought that was New York. Too cold up here.? Ukyo had answered him with a grin on her face. Margaritte just laughed at it. Dani moved up near Josh and took his hand. She wasn't trying to think about this or anything else. Her head hurt, and her mind was racing. Something was going on, something wasn't right here. ?So what are we under right now? I know Pike Place is actually a few blocks north right now. We entered right next to it.? Dani was sweating. ?Oh, we're under the tower that's currently being used by a production company that's based out of Asia. I believe they call themselves... Telesmith?? Blaise spoke up from the back of the crowd. ?TaleSmith... TaleSmith Productions.? The tour guide smiled at him. ?You are familiar with them?? Blaise didn't smile, he was his usual emotionless person. ?I'm a former employee of theirs.? ?Oh, okay. Well, yes. We're under the tower used by TaleSmith Productions, it's their U.S. Operations office. But their main offices are somewhere over in Asia.? Nice recovery, Dani though. This blonde girl wasn't the sharpest knife in the drawer, that was for sure. All she knew was that right now, she couldn't breathe, it felt like she was in a steam room. Or better yet for her, a sweat lodge. ?Now, if you'll go just this way.? She motioned for them to follow her, Dani let go of Josh's hand and held back a few moments. She was getting dizzy, and didn't want to show anything to Josh. Josh and his mother stepped ahead to continue their chat from before. She didn't know what they were all up about, but they had been quiet when she was right there. The world was spinning. Dani reached out, and grabbed the old concrete wall. She felt like she was going to drop to the ground when she felt a hand go under her arm and wrap around her. ?You okay, Dani?? It was Blaise. Ukyo right by his side. ?You look a bit pale, and you're sweating.? Dani swallowed back to clear her mouth. She didn't know what was going on. Blaise looked at Ukyo who nodded and then backed away towards the wall. ?Nothing much, just feeling a bit dizzy... nauseas.? She smiled at him and continued. ?And no, I'm not pregnant.? He actually chuckled. ?Good, cause you're the one booked this week. Would hate to ground you when you're just starting to get off the ground.? That took her smile away. ?You know who I'm facing this week, right?? ?Yeah, that moron with the bad Emoticon impersonation act. Shouldn't be too hard for you.? Dani frowned, he was right, but she had to ensure something from him, as well as run interference for right now. ?I need you to do me a favor, Blaise.? ?What's that?? She nodded in Josh's direction. ?I don't need this idiot having use provide him with any excuses. He hit me with a chair at Warpath. He didn't use a wrestling move, or anything like that. He went the weapon route right away. You know Josh will be apt to try to pay him back.? Blaise nodded. ?As long as it's not during the match, it meets our honor code.? Dani nodded in return. ?Yes, but I don't want anything happening at the beginning of the match either. Not before, not during. He wants a piece, he'll need to wait till after I've shown this guy just who is unworthy.? Blaise nodded in agreement. ?Very well. Let's go ahead and catch up with the others. ? Blaise held back as Dani walked up towards the others. He turned and looked at Ukyo who was just getting back into view. ?What's up?? Ukyo looked a bit worried. ?You know someone who drives a black hummer truck, license plate reading T1SMTH1?? Blaise frowned. ?Yeah... Ian. Or in this case, I should say 'The Wrath' so I don't say his name out loud.? Ukyo nodded in agreement. ?She's showing more and more reactions when he's near, and when he's not. There is a connection here that we need to look at a bit more.? ?Yes. Guess we need to catch up. We need to learn some more on this damn prophecy that Kokabel mentioned. I think it has something to do here. But I can't quite place it. Let's go. I'll call Annie later.? Quickly they caught up with the tour guide as she was explaining something else to Josh, Dani and Margaritte. ~To Be Continued~
