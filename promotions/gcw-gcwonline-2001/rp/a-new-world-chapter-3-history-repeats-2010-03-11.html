A New World - Chapter 3: History Repeats.

11 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/11/10 From the pages of the “Black Diamond”~ I remember the beginning, where this all started, but every day I see myself trying to forget it. Like any normal person I was born into a dysfunctional family, and my life was set to be tainted for the rest of eternity. A mother who had lost a battle to depression, a father who gave his two kids away after his wife passed away. The separation from my twin brother at birth, I was forced to deal with all of these hardships from the moment I was given life. For the first twenty years or so I would lead a normal life, not really knowing much of where I came from, but the older adoptive family I lived with treated me as their own. However, it would be on the eve of my nineteenth birthday that everything would change for me. My daughter was so precious and the most important part of my life, but it wouldn't last long. Her life was taken before it could even start, and anything I had left my wife made sure it became her's. Moving forward I was greeted with lies and betrayal from my very own father. That was the day when everything was changed for me, the day my life was controlled by me. Tossing his body in a dumpster and setting “dad” on fire is a very fond memory. On that day I realized there was no longer a need for a soul, because I had no reason to feel any guilt. Agreeing to have the devil own my soul allowed me to see the world, more importantly, humanity for what it really is. The deed was a promise that could not be broken, all I had to do to reach absolute perfection was deliver defeated souls to him. What better cover to complete such a job, than to remain a wrestler. Wrestling is how I started my life and it was also the reasoning for me to continue, the only thing to satisfy my cravings would be to become the best. At the beginning I had no idea how strong I could become without a soul. I lost nothing of importance, and my body no longer knew what pain felt like. Fame and fortune was coming at a fast pace in the business, and with every victory I became that much stronger. Climbing the ranks of the DWO allowed me to see several souls that needed to be obliterated, and doing so was becoming a bit boring. I was at the top of my game with no one able to stand in my way, the nickname “The Living Nightmare” was given to me, because when my opponent faced me that's exactly how it felt. Though life some times has a way of changing it all up on you. I believed I knew everything there was to know about the souls I was delivering. The human race had no reason to be saved because every soul was the same. As a child I remember my foster mom showing me a picture of my real mom and behind it, it said “Remember Donny, good or bad every soul deserves to be saved. When faced with death everyone is of equal importance.” I never knew what she meant by writing that on the back of the only picture I had of her, well until I met him at least. In wrestling we some times make up these “characters” to hide who we really are. It was after I was betrayed by my father I knew I would never become another alias to hide who I was, I always wanted everyone to know Donny Diamond is the man they feared. SurReal was his name, and he was not well known when we first met, but when we battled he never backed down. Even though he knew what I was capable of doing, it didn't matter because he never gave up. That very night I was defeated by SurReal in a glass table match, and when the bell rang I remained in the center of the ring. The shards of glass piercing my skin was easily tolerable, but the taste of defeat was bitter. I knew I gave him everything I had to offer in the match, and he kept coming at me... That match has replayed in my head for years, and I had the chance for revenge, but history has a way of repeating itself. My lesson learned from history is this, somethings cannot be changed no matter how much you want them to... Headquarters of The Society: [Two men dressed in complete white can be seen walking into a room. Being held between them is Donny Diamond, completely bloodied and bruised following his match at NC-!7 VII. Shards of glass can still be seen digging deep into his body at various points with dried blood covering him. The two men lay him down on a bed and proceed to strap him down as the leader of The Society walks into the room.] Jason Nickles: Well this could be a problem indeed. We had no idea how human you would become after Dagda summoned you back to life. It would seem however, that you are not much different from an every day Joe. Donny Diamond: You don't understand... SurReal was not the same person that I attacked a few months ago. This was the SurReal that had the drive to outlast my punishment and defeat me like he did years ago. Jason Nickles: So we're making excuses for losses now are we? You're looking at this the wrong way; SurReal isn't the one that has changed. Your body is just like ours now, and because of that you have to become better! You have to become stronger! Donny Diamond: I don't need to do anything... Jason Nickles: Has your mind been destroyed as well? Do you forget the loss against Tempest? Have you forgotten that this week you will be facing two opponent's and one by the name of Bryan Mayhem? While Bishop Steele may not be much of a worry for you Mayhem should be your focus. Donny Diamond: And yet you have failed to give me a reason as to why this Bryan Mayhem is so important. You're just a suit who has an infatuation of absolute power, and you have no idea of how the wrestling industry works. Jason Nickles: I may not understand how the world of wrestling works, but I do know that Dagda called for your rebirth in the GCW. It is because of that single reason that we must help you become stronger, because the very source of your being draws it's energy from wrestling. And in order for you to do the work we have planned, you must once again become a success. So when we learned of your next battle, we knew it would be a battle much more difficult than SurReal, because Bryan Mayhem is not much different from you. And to make matters that much worse you will be fighting a Champion who was just stripped of his gold, and you remember how angry you were when it happened to you. Donny Diamond: That's nice and all Nickles, but you seem to have forgotten one little detail. Jason Nickles: And what would that be? Donny Diamond: I never agreed to help you do anything, and there is nothing you could do to change that. So keeping me here is no different than keeping a prisoner locked in his cell. Jason Nickles: Don't be so arrogant to think we wouldn't have a plan in place for times like this. No matter who you are, there is always a weakness to be exploited. Sometimes it just takes a little research to find out what it is. Donny Diamond: I have no weaknesses and if you did all of the research you said you did you would know that. [Walking away from Diamond for a moment, Nickles grabs a remote control. After pressing a few buttons a screen is lowered into Donny's view and the picture soon fades in showing a white room. In the center of the room is a crib with a baby shackled down with the same type of restraints holding Donny down. Nickles makes his way back to Donny and points towards the screen while showing the signs of a small smirk.] Donny Diamond: So you captured a baby? Congratulations, but I hate to say it, that doesn't motivate me a bit. Jason Nickles: That's not just any baby, that's the soul of a baby captured using our technology. To be a bit more exact, it is the soul of your daughter Amanda. How's that for some motivation? Because if you fail to do what is asked of you, she will be destroyed and believe me Donny, not even God will be able to save her then. Donny Diamond: Amanda... What!? YOU SON OF A BITCH! Jason Nickles: No need to be vulgar. Get some rest and heal up, because your first mission will be arriving soon. [As Donny continues to scream at the top of his lungs, Jason just laughs it off as he makes his way outside the room. The lights in the room turn off as he stairs at the screen while fighting with the restraints. From the shadows of the room walks Lola and unexpectedly she injects a needle into his neck with medication causing him to pass out. She begins cleaning his wounds and removing the shards of glass from his body.] “She was so beautiful... Her innocent laughter made the world a better place... And just like that she was taken away from me... “ [Soon he awakens from his slumber and he sees Lola sitting beside him. Trying to gather his thoughts he looks around a bit and notices the restraints are gone and he sits up from the edge of his bed. Attacking Lola and trying to escape is not something he is thinking of doing, not after what he saw.] Donny Diamond: Lola... How can you sit there and not do nothing? I have roamed this earth for many years and have done more wrongs than I can remember. Capturing souls after they have passed on is not right; you have to know that! [Lola has no response as she remains standing in front of the door to the room. Donny's face is planted against his palms as he tires to make some sense of what just transpired. Again the door opens as Jason makes his return with some papers in his hands.] Jason Nickles: Believe me Donny, I really am sorry it had to come to this. But sometimes you must take extreme measures to insure your goals are accomplished. Amanda will be safe as long as you do exactly what you're told to do for us. Donny Diamond: I hope you know that you have better not drop your guard for a single second, because at the first chance I get I will kill you. Jason Nickles: Ahem. For your first mission we need you to recover the book that was used to summon you back. The “Black Diamond” where all of your life has been recorded is still in the hands of Dagda, so it is time for you to pay a little visit. Shall we? [The doors to the room open once more and Lola places the bracelets on the wrist of Donny. Bound as if he was a common criminal she leads him forward, behind Jason Nickles. They walk down a long hall, painted in white and lined with various paintings from the day's of the Crusade. Doors to another room slide open and after walking inside they slide shut. The room resembles something you might see in the X-Ray labs at a hospital. Lola removes the bracelets, but attaches another on his wrist as she leads him into the middle of the room. Jason makes his way into a smaller side room and makes motion for Lola to join him inside.] Jason Nickles: This is what we call the transition room. With the use of the bracelet Lola placed on your wrist you are able to change the realm of which your in. As long as you have a vague memory of the place from which you wish to go, you can appear there. If at any time you get in trouble, Lola will walk into the room and remove the bracelet destroying the bond between the two realms. So now is the time for you to pay Dagda a visit. [For a moment Donny turns his head to Jason, staring at him through the small window leading into the other room. His fists close as his eyes roll into the back of his head, the torches on the wall erupt into flames as the floor begins to turn to ash as it floats into the air. Everything begins to shake and the room changes back to the basement where it all started all over again. The shaking slowly comes to an end and Donny realizes exactly where he is now.] Donny Diamond: DAGDA SHOW YOURSELF! [He continues to scream out the name of the man who decided it was a good idea to bring the living nightmare back to life. Becoming annoyed with no answer he begins beating on the door over and over while screaming even louder.] Donny Diamond: DAGDA I SAID SHOW YOURSELF TO ME! [It doesn't take long after the banging started for the door to open. Before he can fully make it into the room Donny grabs him by the throat and pulls him into the room. Using all of his strength he swings him around and slams him hard against the wall causing a few of the cobwebs to fall.] Dagda: Your back... Donny Diamond: Your damn right I'm back and believe me it's not on a positive note. Dagda: What do you mean...? Donny Diamond: Because of your meddling in the afterlife; some not so nice people have decided to take my daughter's soul. And as payment for it, they want that little black book of yours. Dagda: Well to bad... Remember you cannot do anything to me, because I am the one that gave you life! I am the one that controls you. Now get your hand off from around my throat! [Acknowledging what Dagda just said Donny releases his grip from the masked man's throat. A sigh of relief is heard as he steps forward, but only a moment passes before Donny slams a fist into the stomach of Dagda before tossing him down to the floor.] Donny Diamond: Wrong! You were my boss, but you called me back in revenge, and I have achieved it. Maybe not in victory, but I did have my revenge, which breaks all ties with me and you. Now give me the damn book! Dagda: Here... It's here! Take it! Take it! [He crawls towards a table on all four before getting his hands on the black diamond. Quickly he tosses it over to Donny who grabs it, and he begins flipping through the pages to make sure he wasn't fooled.] Dagda: I just hope you know what you're doing... [Hearing the words of Dagda he looks up and sees Dagda racing towards him with a dagger in hand. Swiftly he grabs him by the wrist, turns his arm inward and thrusts the dagger into his chest as he presses him against a wall once more. Blood freely rains down on his arm from the stabbing of Dagda and onto the book, releasing the hold the final breaths of Dagda is heard. Grabbing the mask Donny begins pulling it off to see who the identity of his former boss was, and at the same time Donny feels his arm being pulled and the bracelet removed. As the merging of realms begin separating the mask falls and Donny see's an identical version of himself, now without life.] Donny Diamond: R... Ron...? [Ashes begin falling from the sky and things start returning to normal as Donny sees Lola holding him by the arm and Jason grabbing the black diamond. He falls to the ground, drained completely of all energy as his eyes are locked on Jason before they close...] From the pages of the “Black Diamond”~ Ron Diamond my dear twin brother how I miss the good days we had. Capturing Tag-Team Championships together and etching our names in history as one of the greatest Tag-Teams to date. Of course this was all before we knew of the lies our father would separate us with. I'm sorry for the pain we had to live with and the mistakes I made when I tried to bring you closer. Now I see you have done the same for me. Either in Heaven or in Hell we will be reunited once again, but until then my dear brother know I love you and that I am sorry. May you rest in peace and let your legacy shine as brightly as a Super Nova.
