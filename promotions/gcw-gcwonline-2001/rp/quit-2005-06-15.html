Quit??

15 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06-15-05 -- The MCI Center was vacant. The ring which was set in place from the latest fight that had happened. Capo is seen sitting on stool near one of the turnbuckles. A towel draped over his head. The look of a worn out fighter. -- Capo: I had a goal. My goal......was to not only regain my title, but to also regain my swagger. To retrieve that which I've lost. At one time, I thought that I was the best technical wrestlers in this company. Yet, I didn't have what it took to reclaim my title from someone whose nothing more than a novice in this sport of mine. So what does that make me? I don't want to insult this sport anymore. If I cannot vanquish that caliber of a fighter, what's the point of me being here? I think it is in my best interest for me to walk away. Throw in the towel, as they say..... -- Capo takes the towel that's draped over his head and tosses it to the middle of the ring. -- Capo: Earlier this month, I got to watch a distinguished champion, like myself, give up. With only one round to go. He had the opportunity to perhaps knock him out and hold on to his belt. But instead, he opted to quit and fight the last round. Just 5 days ago in this very ring, another former distinguished champion did the exact same thing. In a fight he was clearly winning, things took a turn for the worse and he too opted to quit. This month alone, 2 champions instead of fighting on, gave it all up. So now I ask myself this question. Do I want to be #3? Sure, the fans would love for that day to come. They would love nothing more than to see Capo walk away from wrestling for good. They would love for me to quit just like Mike Tyson and Kosta Tszyu did this month. -- Capo takes off his shades and shares directly at the camera with a fierce look in his eyes. -- Capo: When I leave GCW........................ I'm not going to be remembered as that guy who sat on the stool and did not come back to fight. I want people years now, when they look back in retrospect to remember me as not only the greatest TV Champion of all time. But as the 'Greatest' wrestler that has has ever graced this squared circle. That legacy, will start tomorrow night with you, Mike Hardy. The past is the past...... what you did in your past is neither here nor there. Your past will not save you from your future. That my friend isn't a statement............... It's a BONIFIED.........FACT!! -- Capo stares at the camera as he places his shades back onto his face. Scene fades to black. --
