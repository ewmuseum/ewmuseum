House of Genesis: Part 6

22 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 22nd, 2007 Scott Drake grumbled as he walked into the gym. Or rather, it was walked back into the gym. Having lost the urge to practice today, Scott found himself with no other choice when he discovered that Tessa had taken his car as she stormed out. If he wasn’t in the mood to work out, than he needed to find it fast, because he was going to be spending some time there until Tessa had a chance to cool off and come back to get him. He could walk, but in New York, in December? A man would have to be completely crazy. Pulling out his sleek black cell phone, Scott made sure to give his fianc’e a call to see when he could expect to be picked up. It rang, and rang causing him to have to wait until she was ready to pick up for him. “Tessa?” He paused, waiting for her “yeah” to know she was listening. “Hey, just wondering when you plan to pick me up.” Out of left field she strikes back, “I didn’t know I needed permission to take the car.” Scott really didn’t know how to answer her back. She seemed like she was just trying to look for an argument with him and he certainly did not want to give her one. “Well I was just wondering because I would like to go home sometime today” he said with a laugh, hoping a joke would help ease the situation. It didn’t. It never did anymore. “I thought you needed to train” she told him before she hung up on him. This was going to be a LONG, LONG day for him. Passing back by the owner’s office, Mac leaned his head out as he noticed Scott. “You back so soon” Changed your mind?” “My mind was changed for me” he shouted back as he passed. “Looks like I will be training after all.” Though the entire time Scott had been going to this gym, he and the owner had never really talked, but Mac couldn’t help but ask “everything alright?” as if they were closest of friends. Truth be told, Scott would have loved to just unload on Mac and tell him all his problems. He felt like he needed to tell someone before he exploded. But it just didn’t feel right, he hardly knew the man. The look on his face just told the whole story though, or at least enough for Mac to put two and two together. “Look,” he told him, “I may not be Doctor Phil, but I’m more than willing to lend an ear for anything you want to get off your chest.” “I might take you up on that later” Scott said to him, giving him a polite nod, “but for now I’m just going to head back to the ring and work up a sweat.” “Good for the body, good for the soul” he said as Scott walked past his office and disappeared. Picking his newspaper back up, Mac went back to his business. Heading through the small hallway that led to the back room where they kept the ring set up, Scott could still hear voices coming from inside. He paused just as the door, finding himself close enough to hear them better but far enough to be seen. “What’s the matter with you!” he could hear Darson yell. Scott pulled back from the door, fearing that they were actually closer than it seemed. “You can’t even pass one little test” One stupid fucking test.” For a bit, Scott felt bad for Cleveland. He knew what it was like to be on the blunt end of Jerrod’s temper. Though he wanted to step in and defend his pupil, Scott knew that if Cleveland couldn’t even stand up for himself against Darson, than he wouldn’t make it in the business. So as much as it bothered him to hear Darson verbally berate the poor kid, Scott never moved from his position to help. “You’re useless, kid!” Darson shouted, slapping Scott in the back of the head as he tried to get up off the mat. Scott tumbled back down, his body too weak to keep himself from fall and too tired to even try to defend himself. “Look at you” he told him as his disappointed eyes watched his student, “and you are supposed to be something special?” The last thing Scott saw was the bottom of Darson’s boot coming at his face. Then it all went black. Scott snapped out of it just in time to notice Jerrod and Paige heading toward him. He instantly leapt behind the door so as to go unnoticed as they pass. Once the two were out of view, Scott finally allowed the breath to escape from his lips. With the ring area now free, Scott Drake headed on in and rolled in under the bottom rope. Inside the ring, everything seemed so much more peaceful for him. He didn’t have to worry, he just had to be. He enjoyed those moments of silence, with only the sound of body hitting mat echoing off the walls. He was able to lose himself in all of it. His thoughts just drafted seamlessly from one to the next, passing through the ocean with a gentle wind to guide it. Lazily it traveled, burning the minutes into hours, until he lost all track of time. The entire thing gave him time to reflect on NC-17 and his tag team with Markus Stone. Scott loved being a part of Banned & Exiled~! and after WorldWide 79, when the record was broken, it had felt like someone had removed a heavy weight off their shoulders. No longer were they chasing time, but now front and center to command it. For the first time in quite some time, the two could focus on just their match at hand and not worry about any records or history. They just need to beat the Establishment. They needed to make Octavian or Branson King tap out or stay down on the mat for three seconds. Do that, and the road is paved with gold. Or at least that is to say that the paved road continues. But should it end? Could that really be so bad? The two were tag team champions for longer than any other team in the company’s history. If anyone deserved a break, it was them. Break? They didn’t need no break. Scott and Markus enjoyed their work. They were paid to do what they love and they are extremely successful at it. Why take a break? It’s so simple. Beat the Establishment and wait for the next set of jokes to come along. It’s been like that for months, rinsed and repeated so many times that the joke stopped being funny. “Are you coming home or what?” Scott turned, ripped from his thoughts by the sound of a familiar female voice. Tessa stood there in the doorway with her foot rapping against the glossy floor. “Well?” Rolling out of the ring and grabbing his stuff, Scott first looked to see what time it was before responding. Three hours had passed like sand through the hourglass, unnoticed and unapologetic. Tessa just shook her head, knowing where his head had been the entire time. “Don’t tell me you are starting this up again.” “What?” he asked, trying to figure out where her anger was coming from. “Don’t give me a “what?” she shot back at him, “you know what. Don’t tell me you are going to start overdoing the whole wrestling thing again. We’ve already gone through this several times. I’m not putting up with it again.” He knew she was serious too. That was the sad part. “I’m not” he told her flat, “I just lost track of time.” That part was true enough, though the look on Tessa’s face showed that she choose not to believe it. “Let’s not forget that you did ditch me here after all.” Tessa’s nose wrinkled. She didn’t like that reply one bit. Stuck with no sign of a solid retort, she spun on her heels and started to leave, “let’s just go. I’m tired and I really don’t feel like bickering with you right now.” Scott nodded, not wanting to bicker either. Staying a few steps behind her, Scott followed Tessa back to his car and watched as she climbed into the passenger seat. It didn’t take long for the two to travel back to their condo. Once home, Scott tossed his stuff into the closet by the door and headed for the kitchen. His eyes caught Tessa head off in a different direction, toward the bedroom, but he decided that he wanted to relax for a bit before he headed off to meet her. “Where’s the mail?” he asked as he searched the blank table over. Christa walked by, sipping on a juice box, and shrugged her shoulders. “Ask Tessa. ? she told him as she passed without another word. Rubbing his chin, Scott debated which would be more important; the mail or sanity. Actually his paycheck might be in the mail. Mail wins. It had been two days since he’d gotten any, which was a bit odd. Pushing away from the table, Scott took in a deep breath as he headed toward the bedroom. He slowly crept up to the door, stepping lights as to not make a sound. Peering around the corner, he leaned his head into the doorframe to look in. What he saw lightened his heart. Lying on the bed, Tessa cuddled up with their newest addition to the family, three month old Aislinn. Peacefully they slept next to each other. They looked like such angels lying next to one another that Scott couldn’t bring himself to interrupt this moment of heavenly bliss. Drawing back, he headed in the opposite direction to go back to the kitchen. Grabbing a sheet of paper that was clean, he took out a pen from their junk drawer and sat down at the table to write. An Open Letter to Tessa Windsor My dearest love, For far too long there has been a rift between us. Our careers, I fear, cause us to be star-crossed lovers like in romantic lore. Our paths are seemingly entwined together, lapping and crossing over each other in a vast array of patterns that shape our lives. We feud, we love, we hate, we hug, we fight, we kiss; we never leave the other’s arms. This has been true for the past five years we’ve known each other. Five long years my fate has been mixed with yours, creating a fabled story of love in a cold business. I look into your eyes each day and am reminded of those special moments when our eyes first met. It still brings a sly grin to my face, recalling the times when I would steal a glance of you as you walked through the halls of Primetime Central. You never saw me looking, but I was. I watched as every curve on your sensational body moved in perfect harmony, creating a tapestry of poetic motion. Hungrily my eyes would feast on you and my soul drank from your beauty until it could no longer. I loved you since the moment I first laid eyes on you. In fact I will go so far as to say that I have loved you since the moment I was conceived. Our love was something embedded on our very DNA, the building blocks for what would turn into our own modern day fairy tale. You were the fair princess, I the knight in shining armor. Things never always worked out that way, though, did they? How many times did I show the dents in my armor? How many times did I come rolling into town as nothing more than the Black Knight. I’m sorry, I’m so sorry for that, Tessa. I never should have hurt you. I never should have lost focus of what was important to me. Wrestling be damned! if it means you would love me like you use to. I would damn it to the furthest pits of Hell if you just looked at me with those same soft blue eyes you always did. I miss those eyes staring at me through the darkness of the bedroom. I miss the way you touched me, the feeling of your gentle hands against my skin. Most of all, I miss you. Please come back to me. Once he was satisfied with the letter, Scott folded it up and placed it inside an envelope. He scribbled “Tessa” on the front and closed the envelope shut. Leaving it on the table, he headed to the living room to enjoy some ESPN. Maybe he could catch a Celtics game on TV or something. Or better yet, a great piece about how LSU is so much better than Ohio State.
