Reprisal (IG 23 roleplay)

17 September 2006

Date: 9/17 Simply put, who’s stupid enough to WANT to face Seymour Almasy in the first round? -Seymour Almasy, <URL='http://community.primetimecentral.net/showthread.php’t=19782'>Insanity Prelude: The Beginning</URL> *** You can not escape your past, no matter how hard you try to run from it. There are items in your life that can destroy you. Nobody is immune to them. And even understanding the damage done, it still continues on and on and on. Those little devils resting upon shoulders, with their pointed tails wrapped seductively around base of the ear, lusting towards mortal beings trying to tempt them into the desires that go beyond carnal. Their allure is exotic. Tempting to the point where it tickles your common sense and breaks its will with a casual tease. One drink is acceptable. Pop a pill, it's okay. That pain tightening its grip upon your neck and knees makes it acceptable. The doctor gave out the prescription without questions, as usually happens when you get the good one's that want those front row seats to the show. Can't fight against the invaders? Why not simply join them? Why not piggyback them and garner your own glory? In your swansong, everything you say and do can simply be forgotten, correct? Television and culture screams out in choked breathes through it's own obese sense of importance just how important it is to live a life of excess. Fast cars, fly honeys, and fine wine are all requisite. But lost is a sense of moderation. A loss of control. And in that loss you slip headlong into a Hellish demise with those little devils chuckling like school girls at your downfall. And when you hit bottom, you become alone. Nothing is there to greet you, not even those devils that you so instinctively listened to. Nobody cared for you anymore. In the darkness, you begin to form a realization of exactly what has happened to you and what you've done. The moralist within you breaks down and sobs, trying the feign some sort of ignorance as you sit in an indian position rocking back and forth. You talk to God, hoping he will listen and give you the salvation needed to walk out of your self made emptiness. But those prayers go unheard, and you find yourself having to pick yourself up off the ground and figure a way out. The pratfalls of realization and self made redemption are many. At any point you can slip off and fall into the chasm again, none the better and none the wiser but completely disheartened. That leads to your demise. And even if...IF you somehow scale the rock walls of your own downfall, you find yourself with an entire world sitting there before you. Laughing. Shouting. You can feel a boulder be placed upon your shoulders, and from here on in must wade through your life with this albatross. And then the devils return to your side, promising their sweet nothings of how the trek was tolerable. But you shrug it off, and instead decide to break the rules. You made your own bed, but decided that lying in it is not correct. You shrug as Atlas could not, and make a break for it, looking for a way to fit in with the rest of humanity even with a scarlet letter of hypocrisy etched into the flesh of your scalp. You seek redemption, with your sins weighing heavy upon your conscience. The silence in the black abyss making you feel tragic in that you will not be remembered forever across the sands of time for what you did in this life. You understand that you flushed it away and destroyed your character, yet still tried to rise from the ashes of mediocrity into a brilliant villainous phoenix. The role was one you could not get into, for you wanted to be human. You wanted to be a machine that made those who doubted you fear for their lives, but also a human that those in the between could sympathize with. But it was outwardly rejected. You were left to shift into nothingness, your own doubt of remembrance punching your mind into submission and it cost you everything. There was one last thing that could save you. A gateway to immortality. Many have tried, few have succeeded. And with it came a price: you became the target. And you smiled and put up a faux sense of confidence, and made the entire world believe that even in the shell of a body you had, that you were the baddest man on the planet. The Infinite Gauntlet. Round twenty three. Too bad the abyss came to seek you out, catching completely unawares. *** The Past 'You got a real anger management problem...you know that?!' The aftermath of WorldWide 51 of GCW was precise. Seymour Almasy had, weeks before, started a feud. Within the confines of GCW, a war had broken out. A group of defectors from Global Wrestling came into GCW and dominated the landscape. With Shadow as a figurehead, nobody seemingly could stand up to the group within GCW. Even 'The Renegade' Rich Rollins disappeared off into the night, facilitating a hostile takeover akin to Nazi Germany upon marching to France. GCW's talent did not completely surrender their weapons, about face and run though. Eva VanLorne tried to stem the tide, with the help of another man. Seymour Almasy. 'Slicin' somebody up like that? Don't you got a conscience?' Almasy was known world wide as a hero. A sort of lovable underdog figure that had a fighting spirit that could not be contained within his lithe five foot seven inch frame. His lack of gusto belied his wealth of accomplishments. But in reality, his lust for success was kept deep inside. Near and dear to his heart, he saw his career slipping, the visage of Father Time tapping him on his shoulder. But he outright refused his advances. And out of personal tragedy, Seymour became bitter. 'Larry...they say it took like five or six security guys to keep him from rippin' outta his cuffs and attacking that Final Fantasy dude. So I'd ease off of him 'least you want him jumpin' yer ass or something.' And in his bitterness he sought a fountain of youth. The Final Fantasy turned into the Maester of Misery, forsaking his heroic and storied career. Joining the forces he so desperately tried to fight off, Almasy did what he had never done before: quit. And in so doing, the accumulated mediocrity that had become the life of Seymour Almasy took a turn for the worse. Almasy became a lapdog, a grunt in the battle to bring GCW into a new age of pseudo-enlightenment according to the Dies Irae perpetrators. Almasy's fountain of youth turned out to be a mirage, his status in the sport of professional wrestling plummeting. And as a soldier, it was Seymour's job to rid GCW of a new entity. A sort of luster came with the assignment for Seymour. Finally he would be able to destroy someone who had been considered an up and comer. Someone who in many ways taunted his very existence, given that he simply could not ever hope to physically destroy him. Thus the challenge of destroying his target from the inside became the nature of the game, with Seymour fashioning himself a cerebral assassin. He did not realize that Brandon Youngblood's nature prevented him from even making a dent via conventional means. Hoping to instill a sense of fear in Brandon, he tried to cripple him. And throughout the time of him being away from the ring, Almasy sent him care packages. 'Craig...it's a fuckin' show. They said the guy refused to take a breathalyzer, we got to follow through. Plus this guy's a former drunk anyways. Not like it ain't unprecedented.' Of assorted pills, liquors, even a framed print of out a police report from 2005 for Youngblood striking his wife. Almasy tried to taunt him, tried to push him out of GCW. But Youngblood got up and brutalized Almasy. And given his attitude, the Only Diamond In The Sport had in one swoop changed the dynamic. It was Almasy, his legs quivering and his eyes welling with tears, in complete mental disarray. The specter of his own poor decisions and greed for an heroic exodus had caught up with him in the visage of a newfound rival. Death, in the form of the relentless Youngblood, had come for him. 'Yeah. Report on the guy says he beat his wife too. Sounds like a world class winner we got here Larry.' The sirens of the police car are a blast of harsh reality. It caused a sense of calm for Brandon, his hands shackled behind him, the blood of his foe still stained on his hands. A smirk formed on the corner of his lips as he rested back, closing his fists. He wanted to cherish this. *** The Past, Tacoma Washington Looking into Almasy's eyes, Brandon saw a man devoid of confidence. Fear was all he saw as the Diamond began pummeling him across the chest with wicked knife edge chops. The sound each made upon connection was affiliated most closely with the wallop of a shotgun shot at point blank range. Almasy's numeration upon his chest began to be accompanied with welts and blood vessels bursting within the epidermal layer of his flesh. Spit dropped from his mouth as his eyes hung open in shock as he tumbled to the canvas, grabbing at himself like a wounded animal. The cheers from the audience were Roman in their bloodlust as Brandon grabbed Almasy’s wild mane of hair and lifted him up, measuring him and slamming an open hand palm strike across the face with a left hand, then throwing a compact punch with his right, instantly dropping Seymour. And in that moment the crimson began to flow as Almasy pushed himself on his bottom with his hands towards the ropes, grabbing hold of them like a comforting teddy bear after a traumatic experience. And the Diamond's eyes gleamed in delight as he grabbed hold of Almasy and lifted him up, throwing him head over heels to the outside. He connected with a solid thud, all the air escaping from his body as the sweat and blood poured from him like a faucet. Josh Briggs walked over to Brandon Youngblood, a look of awkwardness on his face as he tapped the Greatest Wrestler Alive on the shoulder. 'Let him go.' Briggs stated. 'I'M GONNA FUCKIN' DESTROY HIM!' Brandon yelled, grabbing hold of Briggs hand and pushing him back. Briggs had no choice. He called for the bell instantly. 'The winner of this bout...SEYMOUR ALMASY!' Joey Andrews, the GCW ring announcer flatly stated as the Tacoma crowd went wild with boos. But Almasy was dead to it, grabbing his head and backpedaling from the ring, his eyes etched in something too tame to be called fear. Looking in the ring, Almasy saw his maker. And it chilled him to the core. And even worse, the fans were not cheering for him. They did not care for him beyond being beaten to a bloody pulp by a relentless beast. Youngblood snarled in the ring, understanding that it was a cumulative process. Seymour Almasy was at his wits end. It was mere toying with the former Final Fantasy now. To destroy him like he needed to, he needed a grander stage. The battles were numerous, sure. But the war would need to make a statement that the entire wrestling world would remember. Not of Seymour Almasy and his ability to defy time...but of Brandon Youngblood and his savagery. *** The Present The smoke emanated from expensive cigars, a sweet mixture of cherry filling the air. It was akin to the stereotypical storybook appearance of bars, and this one is literally no better. Even with the expensive clothing and twenty dollar drinks, it was not as if people would just stop smoking. That's what the dry cleaners were for. The haze was thick enough to choke a maggot, but probably would have been tolerable had it not been for the thick rich accents used customarily by those trying to puff out their chests to show superiority. Patrick Crayton's touchdown catch from Drew Bledsoe gave the Dallas Cowboys a ten point lead over their rivals the Washington Redskins in the first quarter. But because of the regal trappings, the commentary was left on mute. It was replaced by the smooth tones of John Coltrane and Dave Brubeck's Take Five. Brandon was exceptionally pleased with this as he rocked back on his bar stool. The bartender was not used to seeing someone in a cream blazer and matching slacks acting so relaxed without something powerful in him. Given that this was his fifth club soda, the bartender could merely sigh as his usual generous tip probably would not be coming. Rocking with the music with a pair of silver Oakley's, Brandon was an alien in the room, getting a couple of glares from the patrons of the Mandalay Bay's bar. It was playing to an audience, going against expectations and considered social norms. It was time to chill. Seymour Almasy was busy getting himself worked up. Brandon was too busy exuding moxie. Almasy's glass jaw shattered the second the Only Diamond exacted his toll upon the myopic former Final Fantasy, leaving him broken and scarred. Now HE controlled Almasy. And to a grave his career would go under the forceful tutelage of the Greatest Wrestler Alive. It was a picture perfect moment- the champion in his demesne, relaxed and confident that nothing could go wrong. Like a rain cloud descending on his parade, a curvy woman in a dark red cocktail dress leaned forward and with the hand that wasn't holding her wine glass, tapped him on the shoulder. 'Sorry hun...ain't takin' nobody up. Got things to focus on in the mornin'. Find some other gullible little lad...Vegas got's enough of em.' The accent of the state of Texas was slight rather then texturally rough as it usually was. The voice did not match the attire, thus getting some more glares from the folk. But he was dead to it, looking at the television as he took a sip on the fine glass holding his club soda while the commercials for 30 Rock began to play. Uninvited, she sat down on the stool next to him and propped her hand on her elbow, a sardonic smile curving her lips upwards. 'Oh dear, what a pity. You got my hopes up, Brandon.' It froze him. His calm smile dissipated to an expressionless state, his head turning slowly towards the offender of his good mood. No matter what, he could not escape them. It was quite possible that he did not want to though. It was the fight that made his life meaningful. 'Lia.' His words had a bluntness. Pulling his shades off, he put them in the pocket of his cream colored blazer and adjusted his seat with his right arm draped over the hardwood of the bar. 'You certainly are dolled up tonight aren't yah? Didn't think the goth in you let you wear something outside the color black.' She took a slow sip of the merlot in her glass. It was good merlot- certainly not the best she'd ever had, but good nonetheless. His quip seemed to roll over her with little effect. 'Yes, well, even black gets tiresome sometimes. You clean up so well yourself. I wasn't aware that you owned anything other than a singlet.' If you'd just walked in on this conversation, you wouldn't know that these two beings despised each other with the fury of Hell. But it was barely palpable in the subtext of every word. 'Your left hand too scared to come for me?' Another sip of club soda is quick to follow, the green eyes catching the embodiment of lust sitting next to him. Brandon's expressionless glare permeated the exchange, cutting quickly the juvenile existence of vocal one-gunmanship. She shrugged in a rather disconnected manner. 'Maybe he was. To be honest, I haven't seen him in a few days, and I don't much care what he does on his spare time. I've had other things to think about this week.' Brandon adopts a fake apologetic sigh. 'That's too bad Shadow. Sucks when your lapdog can't do what you set it out to do. And then it goes to snap on yer fingers? Jeez...you'd be better pullin' an Ole Yeller and putting that punk down.' Far from being annoyed, she smiled, before taking another sip of her wine. 'Perhaps so... but I think you'd probably enjoy doing it a little more than I would.' Brandon cracks a smile as he puts his drink onto the table. 'Trouble in paradise I take it?' 'Oh, it was never quite paradise,' she said, sighing softly, 'Just that lately he's been getting it in his head that somehow, Dies Irae is all about Seymour Almasy. Then again, there are few things in this world for him that aren't about Seymour Almasy.' Brandon can only chuckle at Shadow's newfound plight. The tones of Coltrane change to Duke Ellington's Isfahan, and Brandon tries his best to stay away from going with the beat. 'Sorry...jazz is my thing. But Seymour...Seymour is a funny little kid. Don't you love how he's been trying to act all hero and macho?' She snorts slightly. 'A charming act from a man who calls himself the Maester of Misery. This Infinite Gauntlet thing is just the latest and worst of it. I think he wants all of PTC to remember the Seymour he used to be, as though they don't see the Seymour that he is.' Infinite Gauntlet? Peaking his interest, Brandon decides to pursue. 'What the heck is he doin' in the Gauntlet? I thought he'd be focused in on his crap in GCW.' At that, Shadow can't help but chuckle, her eyes crinkling slightly. 'Well, it's about GTT time... you know what that means. Seymour's got to jump on the self-promotion train and ride it hard until the first round of the tournament. I don't even know how he managed this one, but whoever beats him faces him in the first round of GTT6. He wins, he gets to pick his opponent, I believe.' Brandon's face turns, more into a smile with his eyes beaming. In reality, it resembles more his teeth showing in a sinister manner then anything else, right up the alley of a Cheshire Cat. 'So...he's got other...desires. And what happens if he was to be...knocked out of the tournament? He told you?' She shrugs. 'Hasn't told me much of anything. In fact, didn't even tell me about this IG round, I found that out on my own.' 'Infinite Gauntlet...I reckon I'll have to try that out. See for myself.' Brandon turned, taking hold of his drink and sipping on it. 'Are you sure you ain't comin' to me trying to eliminate a problem for you? Because if that's the case...that's gonna look bad for you.' She smiled. 'I'm sure. It has nothing to do with in-DI justice. I've got someone to deal with that. Just thought you might be interested to know.' 'Care to toast? I mean...for one moment in time I can forget we got issues.' Raising his half filled glass of club soda skyward, he turns towards Shadow and smirks. A smirk writes over her own features as she lifts her wine glass. 'A toast to victory... as fickle a goddess as she may be.' The glasses clink resoundingly over the jazz. 'To the end of Seymour Almasy.' *** In the movie Dogma, Bartleby and Loki tried to secure their place in Heaven by exploiting a loophole. And you try in many ways to do the same. You were never in the league of Angelo Deville or The Renegade. It had been years since you'd gotten up enough from within to make a realistic dent into the sands of time. But it belonged to you in your mind. It was your final stand, and nothing could stop that last gallop to remembrance. At first the task is simple. The mountain top to immortality is not implausible, especially given how you survived the trek out of your own hole of self doubt and pity. But the muscles of your body are exhausted. Trying to summon the strength of the past, you find your youthful exuberance gone. So you run, hoping that your legs can carry you past the hordes trying to destroy you for no reason. The sins of the past can not be forgiven, especially by those who survive your transgressions. And as you approach the mountain, diving headlong into it, you sigh a gasp of relief. You had overcome the hordes. From here on in, it was predictable. Masking your true nature, you hope that they will remember you years from now as a courageous conqueror. That the good times would outlast the bad. Nobody remembers Willie Mays bobbling balls for the Mets, just his amazing catches with the Giants. As you begin your arduous climb, you feel a mighty hand grab hold of your shoulder and pull you down. 'NO!' you scream in a panicked breath. You fall to the cement, knocking your head upon the ground and gashing the back of your head. The blood spurts out at great velocity. Your eyes grow weary as your perception of reality rattles like a shaken martini. Looking up, you can not see his face under the hood of his attire. You did not think he was serious. You thought his predictable nature was merely a routine to mask his own doubt. When he said he would hunt you to the ends of the Earth, you played it off as simplistic trash talk. Nobody could be so relentless, could they? 'Your time is up Seymour.' You hear and gulp. You scream as he reaches down for you and you realize it was never meant to be. For when you dance with the Diamond, you fall. And Seymour Almasy was absconded into self made irrelevancy. His reprisal going unheard, his finality a lesson for all.
