Lost Dog: Captain Awesome

25 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/25/10 Who needs luck? Apparently, I do. The last several months have been nothing short of grueling for one AXEL Action. Between losing the United States Championship on his first defense and spending the majority of 2010 so far as Dan Black’s ‘bitch’, things haven’t exactly been great for him. As if those events weren’t embarrassing enough, he suffered loss after loss to the very man who enslaved him. AXEL Action has always been a man who believes that we, as a sentient race of beings make our own luck in life. He’s never placed much stock in fate or destiny, because things are always subject to change at a moment’s notice. He’s held true to these beliefs until he lost everything he owned all because of one Blackout; all because he underestimated the King of Kosher. He’s been impoverished before, but never since he started providing for himself has he had to struggle like he’s done in the weeks since NC-17. CRAAAAASH! The sound of aluminum garbage cans tumbling to the ground shatters the silence of night. Startled awake by the disturbance, Ace Action shoots straight up in bed, gazing around his bedroom for any sign of an intruder. He hears another crash, this one not quite as loud as the last and he determines that the sound is coming from outside. Ace pulls his robe on and grabs his baseball bat from the closet before heading toward the back door. “I don’t know who or what you are, but I’m coming outside!” Reaching over and flipping the light switch on the wall, he causes the back deck to be illuminated as the filaments in the incandescent bulb surge with heat from its fixture. Sliding the back door along its track, he steps out through the opening, readying the Louisville Slugger in his hands. Choking up on the neck of the bat, he prepares to swing until he realizes that the thing rummaging through his garbage is a human being. “Who are you? What are you after?” The man responds only with slurping and snarling, keeping his back turned to Ace as he hunches over and snarfs down the garbage in his hands. Slowly stepping closer to the fellow and raising the bat above his head, he readies himself to strike at anytime. Upon inspecting the man before him more closely, he catches sight of the intruder’s dingy white wrestling gear and platinum blonde hair. “AXEL? Is that… you?” Ace attempts to reach out and place a hand on his younger brother’s shoulder, only to be greeted by AXEL’s teeth clamping down on his wrist. Ace’s eyes widen and his jaw drops as he attempts to scream, but it catches in his throat as AXEL gnaws on the flesh as if it were a barbecued rib or a fried chicken drumstick. Snatching his arm away from his younger brother, he clutches it to his chest as he stares incredulously at him. Unable to believe his younger brother tried to make a meal out of his arm, he nudges the younger male with the tip of the baseball bat. “Dude, what’s your problem?!” “Hungry…”, AXEL murmurs softly as he gazes over his shoulder at the other male. His eyes are glazed over with rage and his face is smeared with what one can only assume is rotten garbage. His beard is unkempt and caked with a mixture of clumps of face paint and dried garbage, platinum blonde hair greasy and matted to his scalp. Rising to his feet, AXEL slowly stalks toward his older brother in a zombie-like state. “Hungry…? How long has it been since you last ate something?” No response. AXEL looms closer now, attempting to take hold of his brother. There are quite obviously some bad intentions on his mind. Backing away slowly, Ace soon finds himself against the fence with nowhere to go. He raises the bat once more, readying himself to swing but finds himself unable to do so. Dropping the bat to the ground, he does the one thing his body will allow him to do: bitchslap AXEL. SMACK! “Snap out of it!” exclaims Ace, watching as AXEL falls to the ground. Shaking his head to clear the cobwebs, AXEL looks up at Ace curiously while rubbing the side of his face. “What hit me?” is all the younger Action brother can manage as he drags himself to his feet. Completely confused, he tilts his head to the left as he awaits an answer to his question. “I hit you! First, you raided my trashcans and then you tried to gnaw my hand off!” Shaking his head, AXEL denies the accusations fervently. He wouldn’t dig through garbage, let alone try to eat his brother, would he? That’s when he catches a whiff of the scent that wafts from the scraggly mass of facial hair that hangs from his chin. Choking on the scent of rotten garbage, it takes all he can do to keep from hurling. “I didn’t…! Did I?” he asks, his eyes wide with shock as he tries to do something other than dry heave. “You did.” Ace places a hand between AXEL’s shoulders and walks toward the back door, guiding the younger of the two siblings along with him. Following along behind him, AXEL keeps some distance from Ace, as if he’s afraid he might try to cannibalize the elder male again. “Oh, man. I’m so sorry. Last thing I remember, I was curled up and sleeping inside of your tool shed,” says the Angel of Vengeance, his voice distraught and cracking slightly as he fights back his tears. “Forget it,” Ace replies as he leads AXEL through the back door. “Let’s go get you cleaned up.” Snatching away from his older brother, AXEL shakes his head, his gaze affixed to the tile of the kitchen floor. Knowing he probably should go and clean up, he still refuses. Bewildered, Ace narrows his eyes at AXEL before forming a fist and knocking gently on the top of his younger brother’s cranium. “Anyone home in there? I’m offering you a chance to bathe for what I’m guessing is the first time in two weeks.” AXEL swats Ace’s hand away, sliding to his knees in the middle of the kitchen and burying his face in his hands. Months of torture and embarrassment at the hands of Dan Black coupled with the fact that everything he owned had been taken from him was finally getting to him. That previously “unbreakable, indomitable” spirit has been damaged, and it’s starting to show. Sobbing loudly into his hands as he clutches at his head, AXEL lets go of nearly six months worth of pent-up anguish and depression. “Go fuck yourself! I don’t need your help! I don’t need anyone’s help!” exclaims AXEL as he drops his hands from over his fact and plants them palms-flat against the cold tile that covered the floor of his older brother’s kitchen. “Where were you when they were taking my things? Where were you when they took the clothing not only from my closet, but from my body too? Where were you when I was being evicted from my home? Huh?! Where were you?! UGH!” AXEL pounds his fists angrily against that hard tile, punctuating each question with a bone-jarring thump. He’s reached his wit’s end, and that’s clear to see. No longer the same strong-willed man he once was, he’s become a shell of his former self. “They took everything from me, Ace. My home, my car, my clothes… They even took Captain Awesome,” he says, Captain Awesome being the name of his Pitbull. “If it weren’t for the fact that GCW’s new commissioner is a good friend of mine, I’d probably be out of a job, too!” Ace kneels beside his brother, patting him softly on the back while simply listening to AXEL’s laments. Trying to be as comforting as he can without actually getting close enough to be able to smell the stench that his sibling absolutely reeked with proves to be quite a difficult task. “It’s okay, little brother. You can stay with me as long as you need to under one condition: Take a shower!” Ace says, chuckling quietly to himself. Shaking his head, AXEL bats Ace’s hand away once more, moving away from him. Though his spirit is broken and his will is shattered, he still has his pride. That pride is the very reason AXEL is still alive and breathing at this point. A lesser man would have probably taken his own life by this point, but not AXEL Action. He has too much pride in himself to go out like a coward. “No, I won’t accept any help from you. I HATE being indebted to others, Ace. You should know that by now.” Knowing this is a battle he can’t win; Ace simply nods his head and gives AXEL his space. “So, what are you going to do? You can’t show up at Worldwide, much less a house show looking like that, can you?” AXEL shrugs his shoulders lightly, still mostly slumped over and lacking the will to get off the floor. “I don’t really know, to be honest. But what choice do I have? If I don’t work, I’ll never get my life back. Besides, what’s Captain Awesome going to do without me? What am I going to do without him? He’s my best friend, Ace! Well, aside from you. I can’t and won’t let that dirty Jew have him!” Ace nods, pondering the situation silently. “That’s all well and good, bro, but I have a question.” AXEL lifts his gaze from the floor, shifting it to meet Ace’s. “What?” AXEL inquires simply, wondering exactly what Ace could have in mind. “What, praytell, are you going to do?” Taking a deep breath, AXEL shakes his head once more before exhaling sharply. Forcing himself to his feet, he brushes himself off. His shoulders remain slumped as he heads toward the back door which still stands ajar. “I’m going to do the only thing that I can do at this point,” he says, stopping just shy of the back deck. Ace stands, gazing at AXEL in wonder as he awaits an answer to his question. Growing impatient by the moment, the long pause certainly wasn’t helping any. Placing his hands on his hips like an irate mother and tapping his foot quietly on the floor, Ace blurts, “Spit it out already!” “I’m going to talk to Johnny.”
