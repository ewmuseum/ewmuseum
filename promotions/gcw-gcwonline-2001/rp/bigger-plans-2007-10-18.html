Bigger Plans

18 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Oct. 18, 2007 SPLASH The tide tumbles in from the gorgeous ocean scattering seashells and jelly fish across the sandy beach. The sun is seen just beginning to rise at what seems to be the opposite side of the water and glistening sparkles cover the pearl white water as the fluorescent red ball of fire continues to grow larger and larger. The surroundings are very peaceful this time of morning with nothing but the sounds of seagulls and the ocean rolling in splashing against the barrier of stones. Branson King is standing with perfect posture on an illustrious deck branched off from a large home over-looking the ocean. He is holding a large coffee mug that reads "Top Dollar" in bold green letters with one hand, and what appears to be a Cuban cigar in the other. The golden tassels on his red velvet robe are waving frantically in the air as the rustling wind sweeps by him faster and faster with each tide. A familiar grin rests upon his face, one that would seem out of place considering the outcome of the Dangerous Games match from a few nights ago. Branson inhales deeply enjoying the fresh morning air and lets out a large sigh of relaxation. Branson: There is nothing more sensational than watching God's second greatest creation on a beautiful morning like this. King turns his head to face the camera just long enough to show a wide grin displaying his astonishing white teeth then focuses his attention back onto the ocean. There is no question that he is number one on God's list of greatest creations, in his mind, anyway. Branson: One tide rolls in, then another, and another each crushing the one before them but they keep on coming. They are bound and determined to reach their destination, by any means necessary. He chuckles a little. Branson: When I see this, I can't help but think of the Dangerous Games match. One helpless soul after the other enters the ring, only to be eliminated by the next man. One whole year of hoping, praying and training for this one match to get a shot at the World Champion. Everything is shattered in one quick thrust over the top ropes and twenty-nine men... or women are sent back to the drawing board to figure out another way to achieve fame and glory. A muzzled humming noise begins to explode out of the deep pocket of Branson’s robe and he reaches his hand in with excitement. Branson: This is the call I’ve been waiting for! His smile quickly turns to astonishment and embarrassment as he slowly pulls a vibrating aqua blue shaft out of his pocket with a deeply confused look on his face. The patio doors swing open and Sharon Peters walks onto the deck with her hair tied up and wearing a long t-shirt to cover her body which appears to have nothing else on it. Branson turns to see what is going on, still confused, only to see Sharon holding a small black object in her hand. We can only assume this to be a remote control of some type with the motions she is using to wave the device around. She smiles at him, then reaches over and snatches the shaft out of King’s hand. Sharon: I’ve been looking everywhere for this! A look of disgust covers his face as he turns away from her and mumbles under his breathe. Branson: Groupie. Just as he says this, another muzzled humming sound begins to burst from his other pocket and Branson quickly turns his head and attention towards Sharon once again with an irritated look. Obviously he doesn’t want anymore unsuspecting surprises this morning. Sharon: Hey, this is the only one I’ve got?. with me. She turns around and walks back into the house shutting both doors behind her. Branson reaches into his other pocket very hesitantly and this time pulls out what he originally meant to, his cell phone. He looks at the digital screen to confirm who is calling before flipping the phone and answering. Just who he was waiting for?. Sally Ford. Sally: Branson King, it has been quite some time since we have spoken. Branson: It sure has. Sally: How are you on this beautiful morning? Branson sets his coffee mug down and switches the phone into that hand. He begins to wipe his hand back and forth on his rope trying to get any germs off. Branson: I’ve been better. Sally: I can imagine you’re probably upset about not walking out of Dangerous Games the number one contender for the World Title. Deciding to save face and not reveal the embarrassing story that just happened, he chooses to change the direction of the conversation away from the greeting. Branson: No, you really can’t imagine Sally. There is an awkward silence. Branson: Twenty-nine men and women arrived at Chicago the other night with one thing on there mind. Twenty nine men and women walked into the Dangerous games event with gold on their mind. I walked into Chicago and into Dangerous Games with something else on my mind. I don’t need a piece of gold to hang around my waist or shoulder; I have enough hanging off my neck and wrists all day. Sally: So you’re not going to attempt to capture any gold in GCW? Branson: Let’s just say that right now, I have other things on my mind; much bigger plans than chasing after some silly woman pretending to be a champion. I had no intentions of walking out of that arena the number one contender. The match was just a warm up for me, a good way to shake off a little ring rust and get focused on my real plans. Sally: And just what would your real plans be? Branson: Dear sweet naive Sally, I know it’s been a long time but you should know me better than that. I don’t talk business to the public?. or to women. Sally cleared her throat before asking the next question. By the tone of her voice she was obviously insulted by the last comment. Sally: Well I hope your plans aren’t spoiled by your upcoming match against Christian St. Pierre at the next World Wide. Branson begins slowly pacing back and forth across the deck as he’s talking. Branson: Are you being serious? Sally: Yes, Steven Caldera has scheduled a match with you and Christian St. Pierre. Branson: I know I have a match with some no-name rookie looking for a quick push! But I also know you can’t be serious about this idiot spoiling ANY plan of mine! If I wasn’t being paid to wrestle, and paid well, I wouldn’t even show up to give this chump the time of my day. I saw the footage of Pierre trying to say good-bye to his wife so he could be a star. Branson stops pacing to chuckle. Branson: I do want you to deliver one message to Christian for me, however. I want you to tell him not to worry about his wife being lonely. I don’t want the thought of him not being able to wrap his arms around her at night to distract him in our match. I’m a veteran in this business, and he won’t be ale to afford rookie mistakes with me. Instead, I want him to worry about me wrapping my arms around her at my house just like I did last night with Sha?. Branson quickly cuts his sentence off before revealing any humiliating news to be leaked to the public. Sally: “.. Branson: The truth is, I plan to make an example out of Christian St Pierre at the next World Wide to anyone who thinks they can top the Top Dollar! So my advice for Pierre, don’t slip up, because it will be the worst mistake of your short lived career. Sally: One last question Mr. King. Can you tell me anything about your relationship with Steven Caldera? Branson lets out a laugh from the entertaining question before answering. Branson: Sally I have no relationship with Steven Caldera. He is quite possibly the most hated and the most respected man in GCW today. Caldera has a good mind for business and I admire that. After all, business is what makes a business run. Sally: Well thank you for your time and I hope to be hearing from you again soon. Branson closes his phone and takes one last glance across the rippling ocean. He spins around and swings the French doors to his house open. Branson: Where’s that bitch at? Branson walks in shutting the doors behind him. The scene begins to fade off overlooking the ocean.
