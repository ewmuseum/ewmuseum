Lessons to be learned...

14 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14 Aug 05 I was sitting in this dark room with only the sound of her deep breathing to keep me company. She had been in and out of consciousness a few times, but I hardly would have noticed if it weren’t for the sounds those hospital beds make when a person shifts in them. I have had to ask myself a few times today, “Why exactly am I here?” I know what happened to Eva this past World Wide and I find it despicable but it is done and over with ? bad things happen and all we can do is move forward. I know better than anyone what it takes to move forward after something horrible happens, not from personal experience but that fuck Roberto has went through some hellacious bumps and bruises in his life. I had to train him as well, how to harness that anger and direct that aggression into something more effective than going into some sort of deep depression. After that sorry bastard “murdered” his own wife and child I watched as he bottled up and hid away from the world. It took him a year to find his twin brother that he didn’t even know existed, and even when he did he still took the wrong route to getting over his grief. Roberto is gone though, so there is no more need to worry about his screw ups because I will never allow it to happen. If Roberto were still here he never would have been able to prove the point that I did this past week, and what a wonderful point it was! I have heard stories of this Bryan Mayhem, stories of him being some sort of boogeyman. Naturally I had to find out for myself because I may not be the boogeyman but I sure will have no problem of putting the fear of GOD into you. Its sad really because I am sure when they told Bryan that he was getting his chance to be the GCW World champion so soon into his return to GCW, he almost had a fucking orgasm. The man probably begun writing out his victory speech, calling his loved ones ? if the ugly bastard has any, and maybe even plotting against Michael Stevens since that is who he would have been facing at Annihilation if he would have somehow pulled off the miracle of cleanly beating ME, something NO ONE has done. So Mayhem walked out, pumped up with pride and maybe some other drugs, thinking he REALLY had any chance of beating me. Sure he was large and sure he knocked me off my plan a little with a couple nicely timed blows but that entire match was me destroying him. He may have taken those chair shots and kept asking for more, which I almost respect him for, but he fell just like the rest of them eventually ? and when he did it was all over for him. Of course no LeStatt Knight match would have been complete without some fireworks ? and in this case almost literally. There have been many internet sites and fan polls wondering, “Would LeStatt Knight have really set Jason Dread on fire at Dangerous Games?”, which got me to thinking about it, would I or wouldn’t I? On one hand Jason is a human being, and a former GCW champion, then on the other hand I can’t stand the man and would loved to have snapped his neck inside of that ring last month. So would have I set him ablaze? Well I was out to answer that question Wednesday night, and I can honestly say that when I saw that explosion go off right on Bryan’s head, a rush went through my body that is almost unrivaled! The thought of having another man’s life in the palm of my hand was exhilarating, the power of it all was amazing. So would I have put that flame to Dread? DAMN RIGHT I WOULD HAVE! Now I regret my decision to let him off without first smelling his flesh being scorched, and hearing his screams ringing in my ears like beautiful music. I let him walk away just with the knowledge of knowing he gave up to ME, and in turn handed me the one thing I have scratched and clawed for since 2001 ? and no its not the title of being WORLD CHAMP, it’s the fact that now everyone knows that I am the BEST. People can hate me and curse me all they want, but the fact remains that they understand exactly who I am and what I am capable of ? although Michael fucking Stevens is soon to understand better than most, perhaps even better than Mr. Dread. Since that sorry excuse for a man won the battle royal at Dangerous Games he has done nothing but try to make a mockery of me and what this world title stands for. He has played his games and I will admit, has done his job well because I could not help but get flustered by his blatant lack of respect for what the world title means. He needs a gut check, and I am sure that by now he has found my little present for him ? even if he doesn’t know that I left it for him. It was simply time for him to understand that this entire process is not a joke, that I AM NOT a joke or someone simply here for his petty amusement. I will make that silly bitch fear for her life, hell after I am done messing with her mind, she may just ask me to finish her off. Then again ? that look she gave me when she saw me for the first time was a bit strange. She was afraid that I have no doubt, but there was something else lurking behind those eyes, something that is almost comical to me. Even the slightest touch of my breath on her neck made her quiver ? in that moment she not only was afraid, but she lusted for me as well. Perhaps there is more fun to be had with this Sheena than I even expected. I certainly will find out more when I visit her next. “Mr. Abitua?” I hadn’t even noticed that a nurse had stumbled into the room to check on Eva. Slowly my gaze met hers and I gave a crooked smile. “What is it?” “Has she woken up at all?” “She has come in and out a few times, nothing solid though.” “Okay well if you need anything, then just come and get one of us.” I didn’t bother acknowledging her anymore, and the nurse left quietly. I am sure I seemed concerned with Eva’s well being, hell I don’t even know how long I had been sitting here anymore. Eva would make it through this I had no doubt about. She is a strong woman so she doesn’t need my sympathy, in fact she doesn’t need anyone’s sympathy. For one to get through events like this they must do it on their own. If Eva gives in and dies in this hospital then her will to strive was not strong enough and quite frankly the world would be better off without her. She’ll make it though, if for no other reason than for REVENGE. She will want to hunt down those responsible and make them pay, and they indeed deserve to pay. I promise to not baby her though, I will not console her when she cries, I will not pretend to want to protect her from every shadow that pops up behind her ? she must show no signs of weakness or else she is already dead. If She hides behind this and locks everything out, changes her life and goes into some hole then her life has already been taken, she would simply be a cardboard cut out. Now I slowly stand up, my eyes fixed on this woman. She had been through a lot, but her road back to a real life will be an even greater challenge. As I looked upon her, those eyes of hers began to flutter open once more until they were half open, probably because of all the drugs they have pumped into her system. It may have taken her a moment but it appeared that she recognized me, as a slight smile formed on her weak lips. Slowly her hand closest to me stretched out, her asking for my hand. I looked down at the outstretched hand but did not take it ? I couldn’t take it. I looked back into her blue eyes and simply shook my head and turned my back on her and walked out of the hospital room. She probably wouldn’t remember this event happening, but I hope she did. If she really wants to be something special, more than just another woman in this world ruled by men then she has to take her fears and use them to her benefit. Today Eva learns a valuable lesson ? a lesson in life, and death. And THAT ? is the story of MY life.
