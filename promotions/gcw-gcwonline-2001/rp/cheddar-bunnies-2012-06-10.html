Cheddar Bunnies

10 June 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/10/12 (This is the RP for Dangerous Games that I had planned to post "Anonymously" and thus wrote anonymously. but it ended up being submitted by PM. Anyway, enjoy.) “Cheddar bunnies.” “Ooo, I love those.” “Me too.” “So you’re sure about this?” “Yes, bring me some.” “No, I mean Dangerous Games.” “Oh! Yes, I’m sure. It’s time. It’s past time.” “Things haven’t gotten any better.” “No, they’ve gotten worse. That’s why they need us.” “Us?” “Yes. Us. Not just me. Not just you. I’m the past; you’re the future. But it’s going to take us both to make a present.” “Not that anyone will think of us as a gift.” “All the more reason for both of us to go, because we know the truth of it.” “ Are you always gonna talk like this?” “I sure hope so. I sound wise and that’s such a change of pace.” “Right. No one’s gonna know what to make of that.” “Nope, not even me. It’s like I’ve undergone some change, transformed to Jedi master or something. I don’t even recall constructing a light saber.” “Well you are pretty short. You could do the Yoda thing.” “Hey! It’s not like I’m that old, or wrinkly, or… green. You just…” “Ow! That hurt. Why’d you hit me so hard? I’m gonna get enough of that come Sunday.” “Then you might as well get used to it. GCW is a brutal place. Brutal. You should get used to the idea that people will be out to get you make a name for themselves at your expense.” “Sounds more like you than me. I’m nobody.” “Today. Maybe tomorrow. For awhile, but not that long I don’t think. Although you may be obliged to carry my baggage for awhile.” “Ah shit. That’ll break my back before I even get to the ring!” “Ok ok, I’ll pack light. But you’re missing my point. There’s an upside and a downside to being the heir apparent. People make positive associations, sure, but you also inherit my enemies and those who didn’t hold me in high esteem.” “But everybody loves you.” “Not everybody; a lot of people, for sure, but not everyone. Fans and friends and those who got it, even if they never wanted to admit it will be fine and they’ll probably see you for who you are. My past will only color your future a little bit for them. Others… well, we’ll just have to see.” “Well, you know I’ll have your back. What with your current state and all you’ll need some looking out for. And after all you’ve taught me, it’s the least I can do.” “Thanks. You know, this may be the most challenging part of my career as well as yours. You face a steep learning curve and some of the best wrestlers in the world, as well as some of the potential fallout from my career. And I’ll do whatever I have to to help you through that. But it’s going to be tough not to be… well, me through some of this. I’m not used to not doing everything myself, and just doing whatever I want.” “Yeah, I feel ya. But you can’t anymore. I mean doctors orders and all that. I know it’s gonna be hard, but that’s what I’m here for too, not just to be the next greatest thing or whatever. And I like that. I don’t want to be a man with just a selfish purpose you know. I’ve always given back, and I feel like that’s the best way to start, to say thank you right up front.” “You’re gonna be awesome. And they won’t have any idea what to do with you.” ~~ Awesome. That’s high praise considering the source. And I believe it. I have to. I need to, facing the biggest challenge I’ve ever faced - Dangerous Games. Like it wasn’t a big enough stage in the first place, it’s gonna be filled with 24 other dangerous and maybe desperate people looking for that magical shot at glory. They want it, they need it, some more than others. What do I need? I’m not sure exactly. Just being there is a dream for me. Do I dare to dream about winning it? Nah, I do what my Jedi master, my sensei, my Yoda says, “Live in each moment. You can’t do anything about the rest. You can only fight whoever’s in front of you, with whatever seems like the best move at the time. Matches are won when someone runs out of good ideas.” And there are so many people with so many good ideas in there. I won’t say I’m not intimidated. Just that that’s never stopped me before. Am I ready? You bet. That’s not cocky bullshit talking, it’s training, it’s listening to the best in the business, it’s knowing. Yeah I’ve got a big, bright future ahead of me, and I’m coming for it with a running start. Someone’s going to face each opponent down moment by moment and find themself alone in that ring in the end. Might as well be me. It’s complicated. That’s what I tell people, and I’m not a complicated guy, so that just makes things more complicated. How do I get to feel honored and happy, no scratch that, overjoyed, and still sad, regretful, maybe wishful, at the same time? My big break, just entering Dangerous Games comes at the expense of my greatest hero. It’s just not how I would have chosen for it to go down. But I didn’t have a choice. Things don’t always happen just the way we want them to. Everyone doesn’t get to win. The fact that I was chosen to win, by the one who chose me, is the greatest honor of my life. I’ve already won. And that’s my real dream for this match – to make my hero proud. To show that I was worth it. ~~ Hero. It’s a word that gets tossed around a lot, but seldom discussed. What is it really? What does it take to be a hero? Is it always being on the side of right? And if so, whose right? Is it about winning the love of the masses, and being universally adored? And what if gaining that love and adoration means doing things that are clearly wrong? Where does being true to one’s self fit into all this? And true to those you love? What sacrifices for them are heroic, whether right or wrong in the eyes of those standing at a distance? And the most important question: if you call me your hero, can I possibly live up to that? It will be tougher now that I can’t do it hands on. Leading by example makes sense to me. I haven’t always made the right decisions or had the most success, but I’ve been able to live with myself at the end of the day. Some people call that heroic. I think they’re too easily impressed, or maybe too cynical. But what will I do now that I have nothing but the past to look back on and a future to give away instead of forming one of my own will? I always thought I would come back, just not like this. I’ve done it before, raring for a fight, brimming with new energy, and in better shape than when I left, recovered from whatever injuries I’ve suffered. I have remarkable powers of regeneration when I set my mind to it. I can rehab, work around and seemingly will myself to heal. But not this time. Time, it would seem, has run out. I suppose I knew it would happen, and in some part of my mind I must have known I was on borrowed time. My career has been longer than anyone knows except those closest to me and perhaps the occasional diehard fan who’s willing to do the math. There’s the rub, we’re TV superheroes, but we’re not really immortal. The closest we get is through our records and championships and maybe that’s why we’re so desperate for them. We think that somehow our name in the record books attached to those tags like “World Champion” will live beyond us and someone will always sing our praises, even when we’re gone. And if we die trying for that so be it. But who will sing my praises if I didn’t leave an heir? What will the record books say about me? That I won so many, lost so many, was this or that champion, will the details escape? What will a belt in a dusty glass case, with a faded picture show the world of me that a living, breathing person couldn’t tell so much better? Will the blood and fury of my rivalries be remembered by those who sat in the 23rd row, imperfect and impersonal? Will anyone remember I loved, I built, I left a legacy that was more than shattered dreams and broken bodies? Only if I build it. And now I have no other legacy to leave. If the future I dreamed of has been taken away, I know it’s only so I could make this new one, so that I could make it for someone else. Everyone in this business is after one prize or another, after the love of the crowd, after all they can get. Everyone wants to be a legend. I’m not a legend. A legend is what you get when you’re dead. I am so very alive. And I have so much left to give.
