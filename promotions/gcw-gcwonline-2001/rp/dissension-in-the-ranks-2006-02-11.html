Dissension In The Ranks

11 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 11, 2006 ---- Prelude: The Silence Tells A Story ---- Click... Click... Click... Silence consumed all that was around them, except for the clicks that announced every step they took down the tiled halls. The seas of humanity parted around them and everyone glared, they didn't know what to say or what to expect. Some were still in shock, others turned their heads away in fear. Reguardless of their personal emotions concerning this recent turn of events, one thing stuck in their minds. They were going to remember this night for the rest of their lives. What they saw before there very eyes will now forever be entertwined with chaos personified. Now it followed them wherever they may roam. The lines were drawn, the first shot was fired. It wasn't a question if it would happen again, it was a question of when... where... and who. Click... Click... Click... She could look into their eyes as she marched past and saw that her goal was accomplished. First LeStatt Knight and Bryan Mayhem fell, two monsters whose names strike fear in the masses were defeated in the middle of the ring... by a girl. Then came PrimeTime Central Sweetheart Rich Rollins and the legendary former Champion Jason Dread. An arrogant smile crossed her face when she looked down at the chain which brought the legend to his knees. What she saw in their eyes was terror in the purist form. Something that couldn't be conveyed with words. Something that you couldn't see in a dictionary. Click... Click... Click... 'Cowards... every single one of them.' The words came out from between her pink lips. Her voice was like an angel, but her actions were of a demon. In reality she was neither, she was her own entity that could only be described as Vivica J. Valentine. 'They all have something to say, but none of them have the balls to say it. They just bury their heads in the sand... hoping what they don't see won't exist.' Vivica continued, looking up at her massive partner. A brooding giant reminded her much of a former Russian compandre turned backstabber. Only this giant seemed to have that killer instinct that Demitri lacked. He was a machine in the truest sense... feeding on souls as if they were the electricity feuling his motors. She'd be a liar if she said that she didn't fear him. 'Do you talk?' Vivica asked stopping in the hallway watching as Virgil Ambrosi continued on in front of her. The man she knew only as Shadow's significant other stopped several paces ahead of her. His eyes locked with Vivica, who stood behind him... waiting impatiently for an answer to her sarcastic question. At first he thought about ignoring her, much like he had done for most of the night, but then he realized that it would only make her speak more. Which ends up being a much crueler fate. 'Yes.' He responded before turning his head and moving forward again and walking away. Vivica picked up the pace to catch back up to him. It didn't seem as if Virgil cared much if she did. 'Then why don't you?' Vivica J. Valentine asked of her new tag team partner. 'Because.' Virgil answered abruptly, not even bothering to make eye contact with The Black Mariah as they made their way back to their locker room. 'Because why?' Vivica asked, becoming frusterated with Virgil's obvious apathetic stance towards her thoughts and opinions. 'Because I don't find it necessarily to continuously talk about non-important things.' Virgil answered, also becoming aggrevated by his small blonde partner's barrage of stupid questions. Sometimes Virgil wondered if Vivica even listened to herself speak before she opened her mouth. Most of the time, Miss Valentine made a habit of proving to him that she didn't. She was talented though, he couldn't take that away from her. Backing up her own words was rarely a problem, it was constantly listening to her say them that was. Vivica shot a look back at Virgil Ambrosi that would make a grown man fall to his knees. Fortunately for Virgil, he wasn't just some ordinary man, and shrugged off Vivica's obvious attempt to inform Virgil of her emotions towards his last statement. The image that was now in front of them was definitely a blessing, as Lia made her way towards the duo. 'Is something wrong?' Lia asked the members of her new little 'clique.' 'No.' Virgil Ambrosi said before looking over at Vivica who tried to force a smile. 'Everything is fine...' ---- First Passage: Indecision ---- The world of wrestling was a world that Virgil Ambrosi was not acclimated to very much, as the whole 'experience' had just begun as a bit of fun, something to do while Lia Vlaszy had her fun with it. Of course, he didn't do it for the same reasons she did, as he never found himself quite interested enough in humans to experiment with how much pain he could cause them. No, he only did it because it was simply something to do. Fighting was reasonably entertaining most of the time, and for occupying some of his own personal eternity, he felt it was practically worthwhile. That is, of course, with the exception of a few people, whose numbers had continued to grow throughout his career. It had started, simply, with Dawn McFarlane, who couldn't seem to keep her hands off of Lia. Of course, Virgil couldn't have simply dispatched her, and now the two shared an uneasy truce of sorts. In the least, they weren't always at each other's throats about something. After that, it was Emoticon, later known to be Seymour Almasy, someone Virgil didn't particularly care for regardless of attire, mostly for the same reasons he'd disliked Dawn. However, that was more or less done for, something Virgil found himself being rather thankful for. The others weren't particularly important now. Only rivals of various natures and levels of animosity, nothing particularly pressing or interesting anymore... But all of them had one thing in common, they'd been tying Virgil closer and closer with the world of mortals, something which he likely wouldn't have approved of, had he not been dragged by the heels into it by the powerful arms of 'l'amour', a weakness he could never fully comprehend or combat. Unfortunately, with this new level of concern for his hobby, there came with it interest in who he associated with, and thus how he had gotten involved with the several members of Global Wrestling that had jumped ship and left for greener pastures. One of them, in his honest opinion, wasn't quite up to the standard that the rest of them had set, none other than his tag team partner once again, for the second show in a row, Vivica Valentine. It had been his intention to complain to Lia about her inclusion for some time now, but considering how busy everyone had been, it had been difficult to corner her long enough to do anything but forsake wrestling in order to spend actual time with her. Now, though, it was different. Things were quite settled at the moment, and now that he was able to spend the days with her as well as the nights, he could finally drop the ridiculous pretense that he was perfectly fine with this perceived anomaly in the ranks. He slid open the curiously clean sliding glass door that led into the backyard of the McFarlane estate, where Lia was stretched out on a lawnchair, clad in a somewhat skimpy bathing suit, to his delight. He sauntered over to her, looking up at the half-cloudy sky. 'Good afternoon, amour.' She stretched in the chair, catlike, before pulling down her sunglasses with the tip of her fingernail to look at him. 'Bonjour, Virgil... what brings you out of doors?' Generally, he wasn't one given to enjoying the sunlight, even now that he could. It had practically become ingrained into Lia's personality ever since she'd come back from her captivity, though, to spend as much time out in the daylight as she could. He smiled, reclining in one of the chairs next to her, interested by a heavy-looking gray cloud that looked suspiciously like a snow cloud. 'You do, of course. Certainly not the weather. Do you enjoy sunbathing next to a half-frozen pool?' She smirked slightly, pushing her sunglasses back up as she adjusted in the chair to turn towards him. 'I enjoy sunbathing. The weather is of very little consequence. Does it bother you, mi leon?' The words were teasing- they often were as involved Virgil and Lia, if only because the two of them could be so different sometimes. Having grown up in what was the Czech Republic, snow bothered her very little, even more so now that the sensitivity of her skin was somewhat blunted to it. Virgil, of course, had grown up in Spain, so this was probably quite intolerable to him. 'I prefer warmer climates, usually... But weather isn't so important to me. Honestly, I only thought it funny to think of you reclined here next to a frozen pool with snow on the ground. Although, I'll gladly join you, if you don't mind. I had something I'd like to talk with you about.' She nodded. Lately, this had become a trend, people coming to her to ask for advice with their problems. If that was even what this was about. She tucked her arms underneath her head, tilting her head back to stare at the clouds floating past. 'What's on your mind?' He turned his head away from the sky and toward her, a chill wind blowing strands of hair behind him. Somehow, he felt odd all of a sudden coming to her with a complaint about her partner, but he knew no other way to handle it. 'I have reservations about Vivica being granted an automatic entry into our 'clique', if you will. I find it somewhat discomforting to be forced to rely on her for a second week in a row.' An eyebrow raised behind those sunglasses, and no doubt, some hackles as well. 'Forced to rely on her? Did she let you down last week?' 'No, her talent isn't what concerns me. Her loyalties are more pressing.' Lia sat up bolt upright in her chair. 'Loyalties? Who else would she be loyal TO?' Virgil slid his hands behind his head, as a snowflake drifted past his line of sight. 'Christian Zenith, of course. I've never had the highest level of trust for him, and she has always been closely associated with him. I fail to see why this would be any different now.' 'Zenith isn't even a part of this anymore, Virgil. Even if he were, I trust him. Isn't that enough for you?' Most unfair tactics, he thought. 'Yes, but I've never seen much indication that he or Vivica won't merely be using us to pursue their own interests.' 'Quite simple. If you don't trust her, don't wrestle with her, Virgil. If you can't let her into your little world, then don't. I'm tired of trying to convince you that I know what I'm doing, so if you don't believe it, don't.' Her tone of voice was getting more than a bit snappish, but she wasn't doing much to restrain it. He forced a sigh. It was clear that whatever concerns he had weren't particularly worth considering, apparently. He knew that it was entirely possible that he could be wrong, despite what he'd been told many times over the course of his existence, but he was having a difficult time convincing himself that he was, this time. He decided that it was probably worthless to keep arguing, or holding on to his suspicion. Guarded cooperation was the order of the day, for the moment. 'You've never led me into something I've regretted before. I suppose there's no use holding on to suspicion.' 'Hold on to suspicions if you want to, Virgil, but I can't sit around and play mediator between the two of you every second of every day. Work out your problems yourself, or just no-show the match. I'm not trying to write you off, there are just not very many options in this case. I'm not telling Vivica to pack her bags and walk away, because she was loyal enough to follow me this far, and whether or not you see it, the girl has potential.' 'I don't doubt it... I suppose I've only just had no contact with her previously. It takes me some time to acclimate myself to new people. I apologize for being difficult about this.' She shook her head. 'You're not being difficult. I depend on you to back up my perceptions, to make sure that everything is going in the proper direction. Your misgivings are important, as are Dawn's, as are Vivica's. What makes Dawn different to you now, where Vivica is not?' He smirked, as a few more flakes of snow began falling to the ground from the poofy clouds above them. 'Dawn has proved herself to be worth some modicum of respect, after not turning and fleeing at the first sign of danger when we found you in the desert. She is still not among my favorite people, but the respect I found for her that day, combined with the fact that both of us are incorrigible and thus would have no productive fights makes her acceptable to me. I suppose there's nothing preventing Vivica from finding the same from me.' Lia smiled slightly, but didn't quite allow Virgil to see that. 'Perhaps you should give her a chance.' He turned back toward her, closing his eyes and relaxing in the chair, something he rarely allowed himself to do. 'Perhaps I should.' ---- Second Passage: Stereotypes ---- With the slam of a door, her arrival home echoed through the empty halls of the massive estate. Taking off her black bomber-style jacket and throwing it onto a nearby coat rack, Vivica J. Valentine slowly creeped into the dark entertainment room. Her greenish-blue eyes peered through the darkness, like a wolf stalking it's prey through the woods. No one was home... no one except for Marques of course. 'Christian must be working late again...' Vivica said aloud to herself as she turned away from the darkened room, her blonde hair spinning around behind her as she navigated through the halls. He was always working late these days. Many theories had crossed her mind for this. Perhaps it was to get his mind off of wrestling, which was apparent to Vivica at least that he never really wanted to leave. As much as he may tell you that's a lie. She couldn't possibly imagine the amount of damage done by his greedy appointed management being as bad as he claimed. Then again, she never fully understanded Christian and his actions. No one did. 'Welcome home Miss Valentine.' A familiar voice startled Vivica, who was daydreaming on her way to the kitchen for a glass of water. 'Oh, it's your Marques...' Vivica said after making eye contact with the eldery Frenchman. 'I didn't mean to startle you Miss Valentine.' 'Don't worry about...' Vivica said trying her best to force a smile for the Zenith Manor Housekeeper, 'I was just in my own little world for a bit. Do you perhaps know where I can find the phone? I must make a phone call.' 'Master Zenith has told me to inform you that he is working late tonight and won't be able to accept any phone calls. Although he did say to make it up for you he will have breakfast with you in the morning, if you accept his invitation.' 'I didn't need to call Christian.' Vivica answered Marques, who looked back at her in confusion, 'I need to call Lia. Something has been bothering me lately.' 'Very well. The phone is sitting on the island in the kitchen Miss Valentine. If you need anything just page me. I'll be in my room catching up on today's news.' Marques said with his typical inviting smile. How someone could smile all the time completely eluded her while she opened the door into the kitchen. Maybe she just wasn't a happy person, reguardless of the reasoning she thought to herself that she was certainly more suited to ripping people's ego to shreds than smiling all day. Picking up the phone she dialed the number for The McFarlane Estate... hoping dearly that either Lia or Alexis would answer the phone. If Dawn or Virgil got ahold of it, she wasn't sure if she'd get the chance to speak with Shadow. Vivica waited patiently for someone to pick up on the other end as she walked over towards the sink and picked a glass out of the cubbord. Turning on the facet, she watched as the water filled the glass growing anxious as every ring went by. 'This is the McFarlane Estate, Alexis speaking can I help you?' Alexis answered the phone with her usual out-going demeanor. Once again, this lifestyle eluded Vivica as she tried to chug down a class of water before Alexis asked again. 'Hello.' Vivica finally answered after a few seconds, sitting the glass back down on the table. 'Miss Valentine, it's nice to hear from you.' Alexis replied, 'I take it you'd like to speak with Lia?' 'Yes please.' 'Give me a moment, I'll get her for you.' Vivica J. Valentine walked out of the kitchen and towards the stairwell. Ever since she'd walked in the door she'd wanted to crawl into bed under her favorite comforter. Only after one match Vivica was starting to feel the effects of the life as a full-time professional wrestler. She hadn't had the time to rest the savage beating she took at the hands of two enormous gorillas at WorldWide #38. 'Vivica?' A voice asked from the other side of the phone as she opened the door to her personal quarters. 'Hello Lia.' 'Did you want to speak with me?' Shadow asked, almost as if she could tell something was wrong. Her first instinct was to tell Lia that everything was okay... just as she had done in Japan a few days earlier. Now though, she had to discuss what was bothering her... she'd picked up the phone, it would be rude to just hang up on the person she considered her best friend. 'Yeah...' Vivica paused, realizing that she'd never even spoken to Christian about things like this before, 'I came with you guys to help you out and make a new career for myself. Now it just seems like I'm repeating my Global Wrestling stay all over again.' 'Why do you think that?' Shadow answered, obviously confused about Vivica's state of mind, 'You're doing a good job out there. You're in matches, people backstage are talking about you.' 'That's not it,' Vivica replied, 'I'm tired of everyone telling me I'm a one trick pony. I came here to do well on my own too, make a name for myself, try to claw my way out of Christian's shadow. Instead I'm booked in Tag Team Matches with Virgil. Half the time I'm in the ring I feel like if I look at him wrong he'll rip me in fucking two.' 'Trust me... you don't have to worry about that.' 'That isn't the point thought...' Vivica stopped to think about what she was going to say. Reguardless of what Virgil might think, she did occassionally think about her words... sometimes, 'I know you. You're a great friend of mine, we used to tag up all the time and I never had any problems because I knew you had my back. Dawn was in Hunting Grounds with Christian and I, although she did turn her back on us and she's a little on the nutso side I do know what her priorities are to you. Virgil on the other hand... he doesn't even fucking talk. I don't trust him...' 'He kept you out of trouble at WorldWide didn't he?' Shadow responded, 'I trust him, you should too. He hasn't failed you yet, he hasn't failed me yet.' 'I'm just tired of this stereotype.' Vivica responded, 'Everywhere I go... tagging with Virgil, tagging with Zenith, tagging with you. I get one singles match on my own and I win, then I'm back to the tag circuit. It gets fucking rediculous hearing all the time about how you're a tag specialist. You aren't good enough to make it on your own and I'm not even getting the chance! You got the chance, Dawn got the chance, hell Virgil was a Platinum Champion and was in GTT5. What do I get? I get to escort Christian to the ring when he faces the only person I've ever faced in a singles match.' 'I understand, but listen to me.' Shadow stopped Vivica, who was obviously about to go on a much longer rant, 'When you came here I told you that you could be a champion. What's so bad about being a tag champion?' 'I don't want to be the Marty Jeannety of PTC.' Vivica said with hostility. 'Just give him a chance.' Shadow said trying her best to ignore Vivica's trademark mouth, 'You think this is any easier on him? Just play this out and see how it goes. Down the road you'll get your shot. If there is one thing you have to learn, it's you need to learn for once in your life to be patient.' Vivica J. Valentine took a deep breath. Although she didn't necessarily agree with her best friend, she accepted what she was saying. Afterall, Vivica had never really had a close friend before in her life outside of Christian. Perhaps this is part of what being a friend was about, more so than calling them to see if they are still alive and sharing silly dreams you had. 'Okay...' Vivica said, swallowing her pride for a split second, 'I'll wait it out. Just keep Virgil in line, and get him to talk more. I always think he's plotting to kill me.' 'No promises, but I'll see what I can do.' Vivica could almost see Shadow trying to hold in a bit of laughter about Vivica wanting Mr. Silent to talk a little bit more, 'Take it easy, you have a really big match next week. A shot at being a champion, just like you always wanted.' 'Yeah... you're right.' Vivica said in response. That is a reason that she came to GCW. She really did believe that being a champion was her destiny. Perhaps the tag team championship was her destiny... what a cruel fate that would be. Yet it was a championship, and she couldn't argue with gold... but at what cost would it come? They say only time can heal all wounds. This night... Vivica was hoping that it was fact. ---- Epilogue: Reluctance Upon The Pines ---- Virgil stared over the snow-capped pines that surrounded the McFarlane estate, from the bedroom he shared with Lia. The snow had started to fall a little too heavily to justify staying outside on a lawn chair, if only in the sense that the two of them would have been covered at some point, which would have just been rather silly. He supposed she was right. There probably really was no particular reason for him to harbor animosity or suspicion toward Vivica. It wasn't entirely stopping him from doing so, but it was, at least, convincing him that he should at least wait for her to prove herself with actions. It was the only way he could make both sides of this now silent argument in his mind agree to allow him to think about anything else. Only time would be able to make up his mind.
