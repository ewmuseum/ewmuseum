An Old Friend

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/14/06 It was just another day for the team of Fiery Passion in the FCW workout room in the arena. Both Keiko Yoshimoto and her partner in crime, Hope Clarkson was working out getting ready for their up coming match with the team of Ciel and La Sombra. All was going well until the doors burst open to the room. Since losing at Dangerous Games, the two hadn’t returned to Hawaii to face the wrath of their Mistress Kawanaha Kuno, and her vile sister Kit. There were reasons for this, and most of involved drastic torture on their parts. Keiko feared the worst of it, as she was a victim of losing to her own high flying moves. Hope on the other hand, was more vicious in the ring trying to take out Tessa Windsor with choking attacks, only to be eliminated by her viciousness. Scared for their lives, they spent their nights hiding out in Hope’s farm house in Gerald, MO. So naturally when the doors flew open the two of them screamed and ducked for cover? “Only to be surprised by an old face from their 4WW days, Mary-Anne Candler. “Well, well, well,” she said laughing as she walked into the room, “I knew you two were nothing more than circus animals jumping through the hoops.” “Mary-Anne,” Keiko snarled getting to her feet, “What gives us the honor of your presence?” “Looks like she’s looking for a job,” Hope laughs. “And it looks like you just pissed your pants,” Candler replies, “I’m here to interview the both of you’unfortutnalty. You see, you two ducked me at the pre-show for Dangerous Games, and I’m trying to get a job here. I thought I’d interview you two worthless bitches, and you would get me a job.” “And what is in it for us,” Hope smiled, “I mean you just can’t expect us to give you an interview after talking to us like that.” “Agreed,” Keiko says taking the bait, “After all I could turn my student back on you, like before. Remember that lingering kiss you two shared, before she slapped the taste right your of your mouth. If I didn’t know any better, I would say you actually were enjoying yourself.” “That didn’t happen, Keiko Yoshimoto! There never was a kiss from that blonde lap dog of yours.” “I would watch what you say about that lap dog, if I were you,” Keiko whispers taking out her dagger. “Hey, I’m standing right here,” Hope yells. “Let’s give the little gold digger what she wants, and then she can return to the hole she crawled out of.” “You’re lucky, Mary-Anne. I’m actually feeling compassionate today. You may proceed.” “Very well, then. Coming off your humiliating eliminations at Dangerous Games, how do you feel you can beat the team of Ciel and La Sombra, a team you have faced before in battle and even offered you a deal for the Ascension.” “Let’s get your facts straight here,” Keiko says, “It was not those two that gave us the offer for that wretched team. It was actually Tessa Windsor, and let’s face it who wouldn’t want to have us in their stable.” “Now Sensei, you shouldn’t show vanity there,” Hope chastised her teacher, “That isn’t honorable.” “Quite correct you are Hope,” Keiko smiles, “Please forgive me, the both of you.” “You’re forgiven.” “Yeah whatever, just answer the question,” Candler huffs. “As my Sensei was saying,” Hope jumps in, “We were offered a deal by Tessa that we turned down. Ciel and Sombra were just there at the time. Yes we did fight them, and which we won. So facing them again will have the same result. We will be victorious.” “You were supposed to be taking on the Jackson Brothers as well in this match. Don’t you find it rather funny, that they are suddenly missing.” “I would think it’s because they are at some tobacco spitting contest,” Keiko laughed, “Or some other redneck thing. You would know all about those wouldn’t you Hope.” “You can kiss my redneck ass,” Hope pouted, “Just because I’m from the Midwest doesn’t make me a hic.” “Anyway,” Keiko continued, “You make it sound like Hope and I planned this all. How could we, we’re not the head honchos in charge of scheduling. It would seem you need to take that up with someone else of you acquaintance, Mr. Ian Childress. He can gibe you that answer. Also for the record, our eliminations in the battle royal were not humiliating, as in all losses you learn from them, and you see that you don’t make the same mistake twice.” “Oh so this time when you go for the tag tiles, you are not going to do like you did in the 4WW and choke was you are in a title match. Both of your records in that area are not so good,” Mary laughs with a hint of winning this argument. “Can I kick her Sensei,” Hope snarls, “I’ve been itching to take out a bitch!” “Now Hope, she isn’t a trained wrestler. The only thing she’s ever done in the ring is strip her announce partner at a Pay-Per View, and marrying a guy who can actually cry and bleed glass! We just can’t go around attacking people who can’t defend themselves.” “Watch your tongue, Dragon,” Candler shouts, “I could have you fired for those comments!” “This isn’t the 4WW, bitch,” Keiko smiles, “You don’t have any pull anywhere anymore. Mr. McClancy has turned it over to Ian. You are shit compared to what you were before.” “And that would just be a step below shit,” Hope interrupts, “I think that’s scum?” “Shut up for a second, Hope,” Keiko rolls her eyes, “You have no power before and you have no power no. You will always be remembered for marrying an idiot who had the nerve to hit on the wrong woman. You will be the one that stripped Joseph Morales in the ring, who I may add has more talent in his little toe, than you have in your entire implants.” “How dare you,” Mary-Anne says slapping Keiko hard across the face. “Oh bloody hell,” Hope cries, “Now she’s done it.” Keiko just turns her head to Hope who is standing there with her eyes as big as saucers. Keiko just shrugs her shoulders and looks at her student. What Mary-Anne can’t see is the fact that Keiko is smiling. “As you can see my student,” Keiko says winking, “This is what I was talking to you about with turning the other check. You see when a person who can’t really do any harm to you but a slap, it’s dishonorable to strike them down and beat them within an inch of their life. However”.” In a lightning flash move, Keiko spins on her toes and sprays her Fiery Green Mist into the face of Mary-Anne Candler. Mary falls to the ground crying that she’s blinded for life. Keiko just stands over her laughing, the green mist hanging from her lips. “However my student there is nothing saying you can’t spit in their eyes for being an asshole. That is what you Americans call these kinds of people.” “Yes, it is Sensei,” Hope says wrapping her arm around Keiko’s waist and kissing her, “There are other things we can call her, but after all we don’t want to scare the children watching. After all this is a live interview.” “You mean we’re on TV right now?” “Yes, Sensei, we are. We’ve been on since she’s walked into the room.” Keiko badly acts that she’s scared of the camera, and then looks along with Hope at it. Keiko smiles, “As you can see Ceil and Sombra, this is just a taste of what you will get. I didn’t forget your words about honor Sombra, and I certainly won’t let you get away with the pangs you left on me. I should have done to you what I did to Mary-Anne here and spit in your face like you spat those words out of your mouth. Everything you and the Ascension does is like a virus and we will all see to it, that the Ascension is no more.” “Starting with the two of you,” Hope chimes in, “We’re ending this with you at World Wide! The tag team leaders of the Ascension will fall, and it will be Fiery Passion that goes on to face the team of Future Shock!” With that the camera fades out with Keiko saying, “Dammit Hope, why didn’t you tell me were on TV!”
