Date: Timeless -=Staring into the dark of night, high above the city of Belmont, Bryan Mayhem stares out into an abyss of nothingness. The only resident of this once fruitful and populous town, he reigns supreme. Being ravaged at night by nightmares from which he seems to never wake up. As he stares out to the barren land he calls home, a figure, blue in hue comes from the depths of the fog carrying a wooden box, about the size of a small microwave. He doesn't recognize the deliverer. He blue man comes to the front porch of the castle, looks into the window Mayhem is staring out of. He is still holding the box and begins to mouth something. Mayhem can't hear because of the thick pane of glass he lies behind. He opens the door and steps out onto the balcony.=- Mayhem:'Who are you? State your name now.' Man:'Who I am does not matter, for tomorrow I will no longer. Once you awake tomorrow my existence will be extinct.' Mayhem:'Than explain your reasoning for being here at my house.' Man:'I have been sent by someone more powerful than you. Nothing more, nothing less.' Mayhem:'May I have the honor of knowing this supreme being's name, or are you going to continue screwing with my head and say something biblical.' Man:'Biblical I am not, but sent from elsewhere I am' Mayhem:'You have done your job, now leave, I have no time for your games.' Man:'Very well. I shall leave this gift on your footstep and I shall be gone.' Mayhem:'What's in the box?' Man:'Good morrow Mr. O'Neil, the man called Mayhem, nicknamed The Lunatic.' -=The man lets the box down, and begins to head back the way he came. Mayhem calls for him but the blue man seems to vanish into the thickness of the fog.=- -=Mayhem walks down his steps wondering what waits in the box outside his door. He opens his front door and there sits the box. It could be anything, bomb maybe? Letter? Treasure? There so many things it can be. Only one way to find out.=- -=Bryan looks around suspiciously, and begins to slowly open the box. His nerves are on edge, and when he opens the last flap, he screams in horror and turns away to vomit. It has to be an illusion, there's no way this can be real, not now. He slowly turns back and staring up at him is the head of his deceased father.' Head:'Bryan, why did you do this to me? Why? This is the end of the everything.' -=Bryan wakes up screaming, his sheets soaked with sweat. Another nightmares of his deceased father. He slows his breathing and moves his hands to his stomach for some instinctive reason and feels a warm murky goo at his abdomen area. He sits up and looks down to investigate. Seeping into the sheet is a midnight dark red substance. It takes Mayhem only a few seconds to realize its blood leaking from his gut. He begins to scream.............=- -=Waking up with a jolt sitting in his chair Mayhem realizes once again its a dream within a dream. He can't decipher fact from fiction anymore. Could this all be some demented dream? Could he actually be a 12 year old still staring at the corpse of his dead father. Mayhem slowly stands up and looks over at his bed.
