Focus

6 December 2005

Date: 12/6.05 I should have known better. I should have fucking known! But I let my anger get the better of me, I let my emotions rule and they were the downfall of me. I let one man destroy everything I had worked these past three months for. I fell right into his hands and everything fell out of mine. 'Borealis...' I hissed out the word like a curse as I threw another shot into the heavy bag. I could see his arrogant face on it and I couldn't wait until thursday night when I would be throwing real shots into his real face. First I made the rookie mistake of letting his presence at ringside distract me during my match with LeStatt's dick warmer, Typhoid Mary. I kept my mind on him at ringside and not the nutcase in the ring with me and it cost me a win. Then at NC-17, the biggest fucking show of the year, I let the fact that I had three opponents to face distract me and I lost that match as well. I had lost my focus, I had lost my concentration. I had lost my fire. I could almost hear the words of Killer Kowalski ringing in my head now, 'What the hell is wrong with you? Focus goddamn it, FOCUS! The thing you gotta understand kid is forget the fans, forget the noise, forget everything except who's in front of you. The fans aren't going to help you win the match, and if you let your concentration slip even for a split second, then you might as well just lay the fuck down and let them cover you! You gotta concentrate on your opponent and only your opponent, everything else is immaterial. Now drop and give me fifty!' So after NC-17, I started getting back into fighting shape, I trained harder than I ever had before. I pushed myself harder. I blocked out everything. When I was running laps around the park the other day, all I concetrated on was pumping my legs and getting one foot in front of the other and moving at the same steady pace. When I was here at Jeff's gym, during my workouts, all I concentrated on was rep after rep after rep. No matter what I was doing, whether it was lifting weights, doing pushups, situps or whatever. When I used the pool, all I thought about was keeping my arms and legs going and moving the length of the pool. No matter what I did to keep myself in shape, the exercise was all that mattered. Not the people around me, not the sounds around me, just me pushing my body to the limit. I was almost happy when I found out I'd get Johnny in the ring at WorldWide this week. Because if I didn't get my shot, I would have made it. Because truth be told it wasn't really all about the US title, sure it would be nice, but it was more a case of knocking that arrogant smirk off of his face. Johnny was an arrogant little prick who needed to learn his place. And I was going to be his teacher. I decided to pack it in for the day and took a seat at the juice bar to get something to drink before heading home. As I let my body wind down and ordered my drink I had time to gather myself and think back on the mistakes I had made. I had watched the tape of the match and saw where I had fucked up. I had cringed when I saw how the mistakes and miscues had cost me, and I grumbled curses under my breath each time the match ended and Eva VanLorne stood tall in the middle of the ring with what should have been mine. Not the US title, but a beaten Johnny Borealis at my feet. I'd get my shot at Eva later, right now I had to settle things with Johnny because things weren't over between us, not by a long shot. The battle may have been over, but the war had only just begun.
