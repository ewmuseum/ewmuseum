What is a Super Poke?

5 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/11 Once again, Toga and Lollie are seen walking down the sidewalk. This time, without Lollie's trusty golden retriever, Daisy. From the look of things, it's just before dusk, as the sun is setting in the distance. Your Friendly Neighborhood Klepto is watching his index finger as he stretches it out, then reels it back in. He repeats the process over and over. His complete attention seems to be on his finger. Lollie is just looking around at the shops surrounding them, taking in the joy of being a tourist. Toga: "How does he do it? What's his trick?" The Green Gal looks over to her friend, who is still fixated on his finger. She rolls her eyes, then goes back to absorbing the sights. The skirt-clad warrior... (Warrior?!!!)... stops for a second. Lollie continues walking, unaware that he's stopped. He slowly starts to raise his opposite finger to the one he was focusing on. He touches the tips of his fingers together, wearily... and OH MY GAWD!!!... nothing happens whatsoever. He frowns as he lowers his second hand, and goes back to stretching his focus finger. He starts walking again, proving that even the village idiot can multitask. Lollie glances back, finally noticing that Toga's lagging behind now. She stops and waits for him to catch up. Lollie: "Give it up already, Tommy. There's nothing special about Theo's finger. He just-" Toga: "DON'T YOU DARE TRY AND TELL ME IT WAS ALL A SET-UP! I SAW IT WITH MY OWN TWO EYES! IT WAS REAL!" For the first time in GCW, Toga appears upset. Lollie shuts her mouth and leaves it alone... but only after she releases a sigh. Toga: "If I could master his Super Poke, I could be unstoppable! Just think about it! 'Toga: GCW's FINEST POKER!" Lollie isn't quite sure how to respond to that, as her mind went deep into the gutter, and Toga's mind is, well... clean as a whistle. Toga: "Besides, Theo and I are friends now! I sent him a Tweet and he replied with, "Why won't you leave me alone, pipsqueak?!" Which means, he's already given me a nickname!.... Now I just gotta think of one for him...... Hmmmmmm....." Lollie's attention has faded from her nonsensical partner, and is once again admiring the local shops under the light of the setting sun. Toga's scratching his chin, racking his tiny brain for a nickname for Mr. Palmer. Toga: "Theo... Theo.... Theo......... isn't that short for something?" Lollie: "Theodore." Just that second, Toga stops. Something has just clicked. It must be the thought of a genius, because, let's face it... Toga's incapable of anything else. Toga: "I'VE GOT IT!" His green-clad compadre also stops and turns her focus back to him. Lollie: "I'm afraid. Should I be afraid?... 'cause I'm quite afraid." Toga: "From now on, Theo Palmer shall be known as....... DUN DUN DUN........... THEODORABLE!!!" There's nothing but awe and silence in the air, as Toga grins from ear to ear, quite proud of his own brilliance. Lollie: "....... oh...... my..... gawd............." Toga: "I know, right?! A Perfect Fit! Because he's so menacing with his Super Poke, but yet still adorable! He must get ALL the cats!" Once again, silence. Lollie is completely dumbfounded by the, well, dumbness of Toga. Toga: "Maybe I can find him before the show and see if I can get him to teach me the secret to the Super Poke! Then that icky-icky Brad Cruz and that.. um... other guy.... won't stand a chance!" Tommy starts walking again, daydreaming about making his first GCW match a victory due to the amazingness of the amazingly amazing Super Poke.... or possibly dreaming of ponies. Pick one. Lollie stares at him with a very worried look on her face, but starts walking beside him again regardless. The sun has almost completely set, yet Lollie hasn't even noticed. They continue walking. The Green Gal's expression hasn't changed, and neither has the giddy boy's. But suddenly, Toga once again stops. His jaw drops. Lollie glances over to what he's looking at, then back to him, then snaps back to his focus. An "Oh no..." look comes over her pale face. Toga prances over to whatever it is that caught his attention. He stops in front of some electric, glass doors that welcome him in. Lollie: "No..... Absolutely not!" Toga: "Oh yes! I've got HUGE plans for this place!" The camera pans up from Toga to show the store sign..... it's called, "Super Toy Land"..... Lollie: "Tommy... No... PLEASE don't go in there! We'll never leave!" Her pleas fall on deaf (and dumb... HAHAHA...) ears as Toga frolics in like a kid in a toy store.... 'cause he is a kid... and he's now in a toy store. Lollie sighs in defeat, before reluctantly following him in. Just as soon as she steps inside the doors, there's a loud crash from inside! Toga: "Oooooopsies!" Lollie quickly rushes in as the sun sets and the scene fades.
