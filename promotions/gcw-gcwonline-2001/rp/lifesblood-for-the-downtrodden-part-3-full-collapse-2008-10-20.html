Lifesblood for the Downtrodden, Part 3: Full Collapse

20 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th October He stumbled through the curtains in an almost zombie-like trance, the raucous din of his theme music still pounding through the sound system at a near-deafening decibel. Keen to reach the sanctity of his locker-room, Andy staggered forward, his bad leg trembling like a new born foal’s, causing him to fall against a large, black flight case. The Scottish King of Cool was drained, both mentally and physically. His battle with LeStatt Knight had been everything he’d expected and a whole lot more. He knew that he would be stepping into the ring with the most intense, brooding individual he’d ever crossed paths with, but Goddamn, that bastard just wouldn’t quit. Even on the brink of annihilation, GCW’s most recognisable talent refused to give Andy the satisfaction of toppling him fair and square. Andy thought he was prepared. Not once had he let LeStatt Knight get inside his psyche in the build-up to WorldWide 100. Not once had he lost his composure. Not once had LeStatt been able to truly manipulate Andy Murray and pull his strings as if he were a puppet. Until now. No matter how many hours you put into down at the gym, no matter how you build yourself up mentally and focus your attentions, you cannot prepare to face LeStatt Knight. For stepping into the ring with GCW’s biggest icon is more than just a contest of physicality and athleticism; it’s a psychologist’s wet dream. No matter how much research you do on the man, you’re never going to be able to figure him out. Andy thought he had LeStatt Knight’s personality and mannerisms worked out. He thought that he was winning the psychological battle by resisting LK’s early mind-games and waving a middle finger in front of his face every single week. By doing this, he figured that he would have succeeded where others had failed by not letting LeStatt Knight get anywhere near his psyche. How wrong he was. The history books will show Andy Murray going over LeStatt Knight at WorldWide 100 following a technical knockout, but Andy knew as soon as he stepped back into the backstage area that there were no winners tonight. There was no honour in this victory, no glory. Andy might have brought LeStatt Knight to one of the greatest extremes of his long, decorated career, but the Hall of Fame member had cut a scar in Andy’s mind so deep that it may never heal. That shit-eating grin, that laugh? every time Andy threw his fist into his adversary’s face, he begged for more. Even with a sledgehammer hovering precariously over his head, blood pouring down his face, the fucker was begging for more, egging his aggressor on as if he were getting some kind of sick, perverse kick from it? Whatever it was LeStatt Knight was trying to do to Andy, it certainly worked. He was just seconds away from crashing the head of the hammer into LK’s skull, moments away from aiding the curtain call on one of the most impressive careers in GCW’s glittering history? ? or worse. Only now, as he shambled his way towards his locker-room, sweat pouring profusely from every pore, could Andy even begin to comprehend what could have happened if he hadn’t been able to snap out of LeStatt Knight’s trance. If he’d actually connected with the sledgehammer he would surely have caved LK’s skull in, reducing the man to the vegetated state for the rest of what would certainly be a miserable, joyless existence. This wasn’t the Andy Murray GCW knew and loved. This wasn’t The Scottish King of Cool, The Admiral of Awesome. The charismatic newcomer to the GCW Main Event scene who’d won the fans hearts through a combination of hard graft and showmanship. No. This was a side of Andy Murray that even he didn’t know existed. And it was all thanks to one man. Andy tried to shake LeStatt Knight from his head, but he couldn’t. Every time he closed his eyes LK’s grinning, bloodied face was there, mocking him. A terrible reminder of the fact that Andy Murray ? the unbreakable rock of the GCW roster ? had finally been had his cage rattled. Andy Murray might have won the battle, but LeStatt Knight had definitely won the war. Refusing to even acknowledge the congratulatory greetings from a couple of English-born backstage workers, Andy finally tracked down his locker-room and fell through the door, struggling to maintain balance. Eventually he slumped backwards, the wooden bench creaking under the pressure of a 275lbs man suddenly dropping his weight. After a few moments of heavy breathing, Andy reached towards his holdall and grabbed the plain, white towel that had been conveniently left on the top. He leaned forward, elbows resting on his thighs, pressing the towel between his forehead and his hands. His sense of relieved solitude was shattered almost instantaneously. “There he is!” a soft, instantly-recognisable female voice called from the threshold of the door. Andy glanced up briefly, before sinking his brow back into the damp towel. Kayla Jarosz, his partner, sauntered into the locker-room, a grin beaming across her olive complexion. “I knew you could do it!” she proclaimed, excitedly clapping her hands together. The shattered Scot could only respond with a laboured sigh. He leaned backwards, the sweat on his back making the sharp coldness of the wall all the more relieving, before pulling the towel down from an expressionless face. “Kayla, I-,” he began to speak but his voice deserted him. “Oh, I’m sorry, you probably want to shower and stuff, right?” she back-pedalled. “I’ll be back in twenty with a bottle of bubbly and a bucket of ice!” “No. Wait.” She stopped in her tracks as Andy rose to his feet. “Don’t go. Not yet.” “Andy?” Kayla raised an eyebrow as she took a pensive step towards her boyfriend. “Are you alright” It’s just that you’re usually so happy after a big win, but tonight? I dunno. You just beat LeStatt Knight, figured you’d at least be smiling?” “I’ve nothing to smile about,” Andy said, his voice cold and devoid of all humanity. Pulling a bottle of lukewarm mineral water from his bag, Andy twisted the cap. “I-? I’m not myself tonight.” He cocked his head bag, taking a good, long swig. “I don’t know what came over me; I mean, I was actually going to do it, Kay. I almost ki-? “Andy! Don’t talk like that,” Kayla interrupted, almost like a concerned mother. “Look, you did what you had to do, and it worked.” ??Did what I had to do?” You don’t know what you’re talking about.” “Andy!” she barked, folding her arms across her chest, disappointed with her boyfriend’s conduct. “I’m sorry, Kayla,” Andy immediately made amends. “This has never happened to me before. I’m the guy that’s supposed to stay noble and honourable all the time, regardless of the situation. Hear those fans” I’m their hero, and tonight, he almost drove me to do the unspeakable.” “But you won, Andy!” Kayla enthused. “Yes, my name might go down as the official victor tonight, but what have I lost as a result?” Andy shook his head. “Did you see what I had to do to end that smirking dickhead” I’ve never done that before, Kay, never, and it fucking kills me. I grew up with this sport, I’ve lived it for more than half of my life, and not once have I disrespected my own personal code of honour.” Kayla’s face contorted into a curious look of bewilderment. “What exactly are you trying to say, Andy?” Andy inhaled heavily only to expend the contents of his lungs in one big, long sigh. “I don’t know, Kayla. I don’t know. Well” I’m not a religious person in the slightest, but maybe tonight was somebody’s way of telling me something.” “What the hell are you talking about?” Kayla asked the clearly distracted Andy, the level of worry in her voice increased with every word. “It was LeStatt Knight and nothing else?” This time the Scotsman offered no verbal response, instead lowering his head and shifting his weight from his left side onto his right. Without warning a barb of pain shot up his right calf, as if his veins had suddenly been pumped with cyanide. “Ahhhh,” he winced, immediately taking the weight off his right foot. “Are you all right”!” Kayla said as she rushed over to the still-sweating Murray. She wrapped her arms around his waist. “I’ve got you,” she said, as she aided his attempts to sit down on the bench. Again offering his girlfriend little acknowledgement, Andy set about rummaging around in his holdall, this time pulling out a bottle of the small white pills that had become an ever-present in his lifestyle since his steel cell war with Chris Bagwell back at Warpath. “This fucking leg,” mumbled Andy, sounding not unlike a man who had given up. He twisted the bottle top and tossed two tablets down his throat, using a build up of saliva in the back of his throat to wash them down. “Well, at least LeStatt didn’t injure you,” Kayla pointed out naively, still failing to fully understand the ramifications of her boyfriend’s “I Quit” match. “Look, you can’t be thinking about this, Dangerous Games is right around the corner?” Andy cleared his throat. “Dangerous Games”! How the hell can I be thinking about Dangerous Games right now?” He leaned back again, stretching his leg out in front of him. His words were quite relevant; his battles with Chris Bagwell had left him with a few bumps and bruises, sure, but nothing quite like this. After defeating Chris Bagwell, Andy was elated, overjoyed. He’d put the biggest ego in GCW history well and truly in his place, and he couldn’t have been happier. The man had waged a personal war on Andy Murray to the point where he even dragged the man’s own ex-wife into the mix, and yet Andy had risen above and conquered the man known as The Torn Soul. Chris Bagwell was nothing like LeStatt Knight. Not even close. While Bagwell had left Andy with a painful, lingering injury, LeStatt Knight had left a dent in Andy’s state of mind that would take a long, long time to go away. Who knows, in time Andy may well forget all about his battle with LeStatt Knight, but at that point in time, a matter of mere minutes after almost crushing the man’s skull, LK seemed like the only thing that mattered. “You’re letting him get inside your head, Andy,” Kayla said, trying her best to be supportive by clasping Andy by the wrist. “You said all along that you weren’t going to let this happen, so why now, of all times?” Aggravated, Andy furrowed his brows. “What, you think I just let this happen” You think that this is what I had in mind, huh?” “Andy, I nev-? “Oh screw it all, what does it matter. Maybe I’m not cut out to play ball at this level after all.” Shocked, Kayla pulled back from Andy, rising to her full 5’8?. “What”! You’re not seriously thinking about? that. Are you?” “Calling time certainly isn’t the least appealing idea floating around in my head. I mean, if LeStatt Knight is going to do this to me over such a short spell of time, what damage is he going to try to inflict in the long-run?” “But you can’t, you can’t!” She was almost pleading with Andy. “You’ve come so far?” “Yeah, and what do I have to show for it?” Andy’s head suddenly snapped upwards, locking eyes with Kayla, his voice brimming with malice. “I might have all the fans in the world or all the fucking championships on the planet, it doesn’t matter a shit, because as much as I love this sport, it’s tearing my life to pieces.” Kayla swallowed hard, barely able to comprehend the words that were coming from the Scotsman’s mouth. She’d never heard anything so cynical, so dismissive in that smooth, Scottish accent before, and she didn’t know how to respond. “So I’m one of the top dogs in Global Championship Wrestling, who fucking cares”!” Andy continued, the volume of his voice increasing. “Thanks to this business I’ve been divorced twice. I’ve lost my son, and I’ve had my name dragged through the mud on so many occasions I’ve actually lost count. Not only that but I’m about to sue my ex-fucking-wife. Talk about dysfunctional?” “But think of all the goo-? “And now Jane, the mother of my child, is in hospital because of what I’m doing to her,” Andy continued, completely blanking Kayla’s input. “She tried to fucking kill herself, Kay, and it never would have happened if it weren’t for this damned sport.” He paused, trying his best to contemplate his next words even in this damaged state of mind. “And all this for a paycheque, some magazine covers and a couple of wins. I’ve had enough.” Defeated and downtrodden, Andy bowed his head solemnly with his final words. “But you’ve put so much work into this! Blood, sweat and tears, Andy!” Kayla’s voice was wrought with concern as she sat down beside her husband. “You can’t just throw it all away.” “I can’t?” Andy rhetorically asked without looking at her. “Watch me.” “Wow, he really got you, didn’t he?” Kayla enquired, clasping her hands together nervously. “Look what he’s driven you to! Talk of retirement” Come on! You’re Andy Murray! You’re the hottest thing going in professional wrestling today, and you’re just going to let LeStatt Knight crawl inside your head and undo over a decade’s worth of good work? That’s not the Andy Murray I know.” “Maybe it isn’t, Kay. Maybe it isn’t,” Andy mused. “It’s funny; when I was just breaking into this business, they told me all about the pitfalls, the sacrifices you’d have to make. I never paid any attention to any of that until now. Guess LeStatt gave me the wake up call I was looking for?” “Come on! This is BULLSHIT!? Kayla almost screamed, catching Andy off-guard. She rose to her feet, cheeks flush with red passion. “Wake up, Andy, would you”! Ever since I’ve known you you’ve been the most driven, most determined man on the face of the planet! It’s like LeStatt Knight’s taken GCW’s Andy - MY Andy ? away and replaced him with a defeatist slob!” Kayla surprised at the levels of venom she was able to conjure up, but nowhere near as surprised as Andy. Hearing her talk like this was like a slap in the face, and he could only look on, wide-eyed, as she launched into another fervent tirade. “You are not thinking straight, so quit the knee-jerking, please. I know, deep down, you don’t want to quit. You don’t want to let LeStatt Knight have the last laugh, do you” You won’t turn your back on the fans, on GCW, on your brothers, your son? on me. No, Andy Murray wouldn’t do that. Would he?” “I?” Andy began. “Shit.” Unable to compose himself, Andy leaned forward, holding his head in his hands. Clawing at his hair, a strained look drew itself across his face. “And do you know why he wouldn’t do that?” She sifted her way across the floor, parking herself down beside the hunched Andy again, facing him. “Because not only is Andy Murray the greatest wrestler in the world, he’s the greatest man in the world.” Andy glanced at Kayla between his fingers for a moment. “Andy Murray busts his ass. Andy Murray takes every single roadblock that’s put in his way and blows through it like a tornado. Andy Murray is a gentleman, a humanist. He’s charismatic, a genuine runner for the title of the most likeable man on the planet. Andy Murray is bigger than those who seek to bring him down. Andy Murray conquers every evil he encounters with a aplomb.” Kayla leans closer to Andy, gently rubbing his back with the palm of her hand. “But most of all” Andy Murray is his own.” Almost relieved, Kayla struggled to bring a wonky smile to her mouth. Andy leaned backwards, looking skywards with a heavy exhalation, eventually turning to look right into his girlfriend’s deep, hazel eyes. “So. How are you feeling now?” The words sounded inappropriate, but it was all she could think of. “Thank you,” Andy mouthed, the faintest hint of a smile finally returning to his youthful complexion. He leaned forward, wrapped his arms around Kayla and holding her in a warm embrace. “It’s okay,” Kayla softly said into his ear as they pulled apart. “Just promise me I won’t lose you like that again.” “I promise.” “Good. Now, are you ready to win Dangerous Games or what”!” she smiled broadly now. He didn’t need to respond. He’s Andy Murray. Of course he was ready.
