Any Info, Contact Madison Roberts 145 Cherryhill Road, Atlantic City, NJ (378) - 333 - 9374 $$REWARD$$ The Morning sun was covered in menacing dark clouds, which threatened to explode into a full blown storm at any minute. The foreboding weather was not enough to keep our hero's locked in their hotel room however. They were in full 'stoned-wander/mode' and in a slight pickle. They Were Broke. 'Dude, I thought Pro Wrestlers made like.. money' Said Bammy, scratching his forearm needlessly. 'We can Barely afford to eat, we had to run out of IHOP last night while that butch lady chased u-' He's got off my his heterosexual life companion, Mooch. 'Shh, Look at this.' He pointed at the poster, which was plastered to a darkened streetlamp. They both studied it closely. 'So,' Said Bammy 'You want us to go to that address, and hit this chick up for cash?' He received a punch in the arm. 'No Dipshit, The cat. Look, there's a reward. All we have to do his find the cat, return it, and start rolling in the bills.' said Mooch. 'Oooh, right. Mr. Whiskers.' 'Right. Mr. Whiskers.' 'Atlantic City is pretty big, Mooch,' said Bammy 'How the hell are we supposed to find a cat?' Mooch stares at him, before ripping the poster off the .. post.. and Holding it up. 'Don't you watch cop dramas? We need to go around looking for suspects, and fishing for info. Here, lets start here' said Mooch. They both begin to cross the street, walking up to your average suburbia influenced house. Brushing by the white picket fence, Bammy knocks on the door. Then Mooch backs up and kicks it in. Or, he tries. He just succeeds in falling back, grasping his foot in pain. 'FUCK FUCK!' He b egins shouting. The door creaks open, as a 12 year old boy steps onto the porch. 'What the fuck do you cunts want?' he says. Mooch climbs up, and gets into the kids face, waving the poster. 'WHERE'S THE CAT?' 'Uhh..' 'WHERE'S THE FUCKING CAT?!' 'Err..' 'Where are you from?' '..What?' The Kid responds. 'What?! WHAT AIN'T NO COUNTRY I'VE EVER HEARD OF! THEY SPEAK ENGLISH IN WHAT?!' '..What?' He replies. 'ENGLISH MUTHAFUCKA DO-YOU-SPEAK IT?!' 'Pulling off an accurate Samuel L. Jackson Impression: Priceless' - Mastercard. Bammy attempts to restrain his rabid friend, pulling him up. Mooch begins kicking until he's loose. 'Listen up Punk,' Mooch says 'I'm looking for a Cat. THIS Cat. I Know, you Know, where we can find him.' 'Dude, you just walked up to a house, how the hell should this kid know?' says Bammy. 'TELL ME YOU PUNK ASS BIATCH!' says Mooch, getting in the kids face and doing several gang related signs. The Kid steps back. 'Okay.. Okay.. I'll tell you everything I know' says the Kid. Bammy looks shocked. 'The cat's name, is Mr. Whiskers. He lives up on Cherryhill Road, it's down that way about ten miles. Biggest House on the mountain. See, the owner is a known cocaine and marijuana trafficker. Me and my pal Joseph were pulling a job for him last month. He fucked us, and he fucked us good. Shots were fired, and I grabbed the first thing I could off the property and ran.' says the Kid. 'Turns Out' he continues 'I grabbed this cat carrier. Mr. Whiskers was inside. When I pulled him out, the motherfucker scratched me and ran. See?' He holds up his arm, which is Heavily bandaged. Mooch is taking notes on the back of the poster, and Bammy is standing there, jaw to the floor. 'Dude..' he says to the kid 'You're like 12.' 'So?' 'You're running drugs at 12?' 'Shut up you dickless prick,' whispers the kid. He discovers his neighbor listening in and continues louder 'NO! I was selling Boyscout cookies for my troop. If you want to find the cat, you'll have to start at the house. be careful.' With that, the boy promptly slams the door. As our hero's take off to the drug shack on the mountain, the boy mutters to himself. 'I can't believe those douche bags believed that' Right before his mother called him in for Lunch.. --- Will Mooch and Bammy find out The Owner of the House isn't really a Drug Cartel? Will Mooch's fake police Intuition come into play? Will Mr. Whiskers be returned home? Will the Handler of Team Baked Get off his Lazy Ass and Rp sooner so this story makes sense?
