Date: One Year ago The chants buzz around inside his head. The Admiral of Awesome looks on at Mayhem, almost trance-like in his concentration on the fallen grappler. He snaps out of it and remembers the task at hand, grabbing Mayhem and with an almighty heave placing him in the casket. Hunched over, breathing heavily, exhausted; Andy Murray kicks down the coffin lid, but not before Bryan Mayhem has voluntarily folded his own arms over his chest… Bryan: … did you see that? Yale: Yes. Yes I did. What’s going on here!? Bryan: I-… I really don’t know, Dave. That was surreal to say the least, but Andy’s on his way to the cab… Still a little dizzy from the earlier DDT, Andy walks around the side of the truck as fast as his ailing legs will allow him. Momentarily he stumbles into the side of the truck but the cheers of the fans are enough to pull him back to life. Soon enough he reaches the front and climbs up the steps. Hanging out of the cab, one hand on an unseen lever, Andy looks over his shoulder one more time before yanking back on the lever. And the bed is raised. And the dirt tumbles. Bryan Mayhem is buried alive. The sound is a hollow thunder. I can hear it all around me. I can feel it coarse through each and every vein. Each sound is a Boom Thud countdown to my demise. As I lay in this silk, this soft smooth entity of the end, my thoughts begin to race. My life flashes before me, each moment overlapping the next. Each moment of sodomy, abuse, and torture. Every drop of blood, sweat and tears. Every smile, frown, and stare, are all molding into one big fucking miserable moment. My time is done, and I must accept this fate, for this is my one true destiny. The crowd's roar is more like a bellow now. They sound so far away, yet so goddamn close. I know if I reach for them, not one will reach back. I will be smited, boosed, and spat upon like a gladiator in the Coluseum awaiting his sentence. The Thumbs Down, my death sentence.I have forced them to hate me, and I hate them back equally, maybe even more so. Eye for and eye, or so they say. Don't worry the end is drawing near. Soon I will suffocate, my own warm exhalation becoming the very vice grip on my life. I will feel my lungs grow hot, and my head becomes light. I will instinctively begin to scratch and claw at the silk roof of my new house of death. I want to go peacefully, but nature does not allow me. I want to sit back and enjoy the ride, the thrill of taking my....final.....breathe. Hours Later: The light is extremely bright, and all I can see is blurry silhouettes. My vision is like staring at the sun for a few seconds, than attempting to focus on one object immediately after. Am I in hell? Don't I belong in hell? There is not suppose to be bright lights like this in hell. I am suppose to feel the burn, feel the flames lick my flesh like the serpent of the night. My paint is suppose to melt to my skin, mold it into a swarming vulgar mass of infected virulency. Isn't my hair suppose to singe like a rag doll? For being in this "hell" I feel completely relaxed and at home. My eyes are starting to adjust. The men and women around me are all sorts of shapes, colors, hues and tones. Some are hairy, some are not. Some are accented, and yet some are not. Some even remind me of people of my past, while others are just strangers. I take a large gulp, a quick pain shoots through my chest, my head becomes light again, and than after a few seconds I feel rejuvenated. My first breathe of...hell? I feel like a newborn child. My throat cleared of my mother's juice, and I catch my first gulp of oyxgen. But this can't be oxygen, I am dead. I am heading to hell for my sins. My left eye begins to adjust to the light far better than my right. I recognize the face of the man standing over me. He looks....damnit who the hell is he? The voices are starting to make sense, they are not 100% clear, but they are not the senseless psychobabble mumble they were only seconds ago. I hear a few people saying they are glad I "made it." Of course they would be happy to see me in hell. The world is full of sadistic people, none moreso than myself. The face that's becoming more and more recognizable by the second gasps as my eyes being to widen. He realizes I know who he is! He walks away and says to one of the other bearded men, "Make him vanish." Vanish? What the hell is going on? I feel a small pin prick into my left bicep. Seconds later my body begins to numb, and than the famailiar blackness overtakes me again.. The next morning: Jesus Christ it's cold! I am seriously beginning to think I am not in hell. My vision starts to clear and I realize I am lying in some alley. The cold biting rain is chewing away at my flesh. I sit up quickly, my hair flinging dirty water across my face. I run my hands over my face. The paint has been washed away, and it appears I have been washed away as well. I look at my hands. These hands were once made for destroying, but now they looked gnarled and mangled. The lifelines are filled deeply with mud and blood. I am in the most physical pain I have ever been in. The death match with Shaman, Belmont with Knight, Home Depot with Jason O'Neil are walks in the park compared to what I am feeling now. I am worn down, almost afraid to move out of fear of something falling off or breaking. I am cold, and feverish. My hair hurts, my feet are numb. My stomach begins to turn as my as I start to retch. Like a volcano my stomach erupts all over me. The smell and sight makes me erupt again. What a sight. Bryan Mayhem, the most feared man on earth, is now lying almost crippled in his own vomit. My mobility causes more pain through my body, but I need to get onto my feet, I need to move, and leave whatever hole I am in. I look left, than right, than left again. Traffic moves at a moderate pace to my left, and I hear a dumptruck and some workers to my right. How has no one not seen me? How have I now become invisible. "Because you are weak!" The bass infected voice rattles my brain. I swerve to almost avoid the voice, than begin to look franticly around me for the location of this deep thrawl of a voice. "You will not find me O'Neil. I am a figment of your imagination." Like a cornered animal I begin to lash out in violent swipes at nothing, even though I feel like I am hitting something, I know I am hitting nothing. My heart is racing, and the wetness on my face is no longer rain but long beads of sweat. I am breathing heavy, almost as if I am out of shape. Who the fuck is talking to me? My violent tirade against oxygen, nitrogen, and hydrogen finally comes to an end. I lean up against the cold brick wall and start to slide down in fear, cold, and exhaustion. My knees are pent up againt my chest like a scared child....funny...scared child because that is what I feel like, a scared child. Bryan O'Neil is scared of nothing, except whatever this is that's scaring him to death. "You done?" The strange yet familiar voice asks. I take a deep breathe and nod, and thats all I do is nod. "Bryan, you have disgusted me, and disgusted everyone. You gave up! You fucking gave up! Bryan O'Neil never gives up. He's a sick, twisted, evil freak of nature that relishes upon the scent of another's blood. He's a man that will watch someone take their final breathe of life just so he can see the final shrinkage of life glimmering away in his iris. What the hell happened to you?" I am still looking around slowly, wondering if I can somehow find this person's point of origin, but like before to no avail. "Stop looking for me! You will never find me! You want to know why you big piece of shit?" I mouth yes, hoping that's all I needed to do to find out who this man was. I am exhausted, my throat feels like sandpaper, and this cold rain is not adding to the scenario at all. "I am you!" Me? "Yes, I was the driving force that made you kill father. I was the one thing that forced the pillow into your hand to kill our disease ridden mother. I am the sick thing in your head that forced your hand into burying Jeffery after mother and father realized he was born deformed. But most of all, I am that ticking sound in your head. That tick that allowed you to be the most hated, most powerful, and most destructive force this world has ever known. You are Charles Manson, you ARE William Sowell....you are Evil Incarnate. But now look at you. A pitiful mass of confused, old, worn out flesh that rather give it up than realize why he is here today!" I start to groan. My head is beginning to ache, my bones are becoming brittle as we speak. What the hell happened to me? "YOU GAVE UP!!!" The scream was deafening. "GET ON YOUR GODDAMN FEET!" Just go away. "BRYAN MAYHEM HAS TAPPED OUT!" Tapped out? "DO US BOTH A FAVOR. PUT THIS DISGUSTING MASS ON THE END OF A ROPE!!" Tapped out? What the.. "YES GET ANGRY YOU WORTHLESS PILE OF SHIT!" Get the fuck out of my head? "WHY O'NEIL? WHAT ARE YOU GOING TO DO? RUN AWAY? ISN'T THAT WHAT ALL THE O'NEIL'S DID?!?" GET OUT OF MY HEAD!!! "NO ONE IS SCARED OF YOU ANYMORE! YOU WON'T DO SHIT. LIKE THE PAST YEAR. YOU WON'T DO SHIT!!" AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!----black
