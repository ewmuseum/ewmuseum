Date: Today, by tomorrow it'll be yesterday Outside the storm raged, one of those violent angry storms. You could feel primal nature of the night given form in the blowing of the wind, the crackle of lightening, the baritone rumble of thunder. The awesome display of power was such that he couldn’t simply remain in the hotel room, he had to get out, embrace it. A short walk had brought him here, a deserted twenty-four seven diner. Soaked to the skin he’d entered nearly an hour ago, ordered coffee, and sat. Not talking, save to request more to drink, he simply sat, and smoked. The ashtray, a thick black plastic affair, the type you’d buy at the dollar store for fifty cents, was quickly nearing full to overflowing. A haze of gray blue smoke surrounded the young red haired stranger. As he sat, he watched, the cook leaned opposite the grill, playing a hand held video game, the waitress seated at the front both smoking her own cigarette and reading a cracked spined paperback. Every ten minutes or so she’d set the open book down, grab the coffee pot, and check on her one costumer. He watched the swaying of her hips as she walked, the slow lift of her breasts as he breathed in and out. She wasn’t exactly what you’d call beautiful, but she was definitely attractive. A little bland maybe, her blonde hair a little washed out to a dull dishwater color, still passable though. He considered the prospect as she filled his cup for probably the eighth time since he’d been there. A flash of a smile, she walked away, having decided more than half an hour ago he wouldn’t be eating. Yeah she’d definitely be going home with him tonight, it’d just take a little time and effort. Of course he really had nothing but time right now. No where to go, no where to be, in the late hours technically morning, but no one would be waking up anytime soon. The restaurant would be deserted for more than a few hours, plenty of time to work. Really that’s all he’d need a little time, and the determination to get the job done. It was exciting, the thrill of the hunt, the joy of the chase. He imagined this would be the type rush an explorer must have felt, discovering a new world, or the hunter about to move in for the big kill after having stalked his prey for a number of hours. It was glorious, the energy that filled him put him right back outside in the storm, it was these moments he’d come to live for. Sure the wrestling was great and all but it was times like this were the fun was really to be had. Quickly formulating a plan of action, he slid quietly out of the booth and stood, stretching his neck a bit. Still sore from the beating in England, he would stiffen up quickly if he sat too long, which he had. Plus the high humidity of the storm had caused the facture in his wrist to throb. No matter he’d be loosing himself in ecstasy soon enough. Padding towards the restroom, it was his intention to catch a glimpse of the book she read, figure out her interests from that, it was all too easy. Hearing him approach, she set her book down and stood, prepared to cash him out if that was his intent. Smiling he met her questioning look. “Are you ready for your check sweetie?” “Nah, just had to hit the head, thanks though.” She flashed him a smile, and he almost felt himself flush. That was odd, he must’ve been out of the game too long, focusing more on just getting through the Europe trip than women. God, if he was so out of practice that a simple smile would make him flush he could only imagine how the rest of the night would end up going. Oh well, no matter he’d get through it no matter what it took. As she sat back down he headed off, it wasn’t that he really needed to use the bathroom, he just had to make it look like he was going. Always maintain the illusion. Immediately upon entering the grimy little room, he was assaulted by the stench. Obviously it hadn’t been cleaned in sometime, stale vomit, and old urine, typical smell of most diners open at this hour. A cold splash of water against his face, he toweled off and took a look at himself in the mirror. Most of the obvious bruising from the old beating had disappeared, although his eye was still slightly puffy from swelling. At least it wasn’t that noticeable. Satisfied that he was ready, he stepped out of the bathroom. Taking a deep breath, of the somewhat cleaner air in the diner proper, he began heading back towards his table. Then he saw her, and stopped cold. The shock of pink hair was a dead give away. It didn’t make any sense how could she possibly be here, he hadn’t spoken to her in weeks, at yet there she was, even sitting at his table too. What the hell was going on? What exactly was going on in his mind, is what’s commonly known as a “fight or flight” reaction. Either he’d sit down and definitely end up in a fight, or he could just sneak out. Hopefully she wouldn’t notice him, her head was down and she couldn’t have seen him yet, right? Nope, she was already looking up at him smiling, this was gonna hurt. Oh well, best bet was to try and smooth things over before they got too out of hand, the thunder outside almost sounded like it was laughing at that one. Burying his hands in the pockets of his jeans, and hanging his head, Stevens headed back to his table. Well, might as well scrap the plan about going home with the waitress, damn she looked bendy too. Oh well, turn on the old Stevens charm, and maybe just maybe he’d still be getting some. It’d definitely be a lot harder this way, but he should be up for the challenge. Twisting his face into the most blinding smile he could, he slide into the booth across from her. Damn, this was going to be harder than he thought, that smile of her’s disappeared awfully damn quick, and he hadn’t even started talking yet. “Hey cutie.” “Hello, Michael.” “Good to see you, how’d you know where to find me?” “Wasn’t that hard, GCW’s travel schedule is posted on their website, so I knew you’d be in town. I always know you stay in the best hotel that’s got a twenty-four hour diner next to it. And well the storm would obviously bring you out, I know how you can’t bear to stay indoors during the rain.” “Sounds like a lot of work though. Sorry you had to go through all that.” “Well you know since you never answered my calls while you were in Houston I figured it was worth the trouble. Especially since I have an important question for you.” “Really, what’s that?” God he knew it was a mistake to ask, but what else was he supposed to say? Either way this was going to be bad, real bad. “Who the FUCK do you think you are?” “What?” “You heard me asshole.” “Uh well..” “No, first you call me, and tell me’that, and then all of a sudden it’s like I’ve got the plague and you won’t even return my calls. So yes just exactly who the fuck do you think you are?” Yeah, he figured this was how it was going to end up. Damn drunk dialing, they can put a man on the moon, but they can’t figure out a way to prevent liquor from making idiots dial up ex-girlfriends. Oh well, such is life, best try and calm her down quick. “Listen, sweetie, I’m sorry I was just um I dunno.” “What you were what?” Gotta make this seem real, gotta be real damn smooth. “I don’t know, embarrassed I guess. ? “Embarrassed” About what?” “Listen, Sheena, you know me. I’m not the guy who goes around talking about my feelings or anything. I won’t lie I was pretty blitzed when I called you a couple weeks ago, and well I said some things that I probably shouldn’t.” “So what were you lying?” Danger! Danger Will Robinson! Quickly back peddle, tread carefully. “No, not lying exactly, or well at all. I just shouldn’t have let the booze get to me like that. Think about it, how the hell are people supposed to take me seriously when I go around talking about how much I’m in love with my ex-girlfriend?” God, hopefully that’ll work. “Who the hell cares about what people think, you never seemed to before.” Damn, should’ve seen that one coming. “Well, you know deep down, always cared. Damnit Sheena why do we have to do this?” “Because you put me through hell over the last couple weeks, calling me up, telling me that and just disappearing. It’s bullshit Mike, and you know it.” One chance, it worked saying it when he was drunk, maybe it’d work this time. He might not even slur. “Sorry.” “What?” “I said I was sorry ok” I’m not going to say it again. Or at least I don’t want to, please don’t make me ok?” YES! That struck gold he could see her expression starting to soften already. “You said you were sorry, again. You never say you’re sorry.” “Well, I mean?” “It’s kinda sweet. You know what Mike, I think we’ve got a lot we need to talk about.” “If you say so.” “I do.” Well that was that, maybe just maybe he’d end up getting a chance at some action when it was all said and done. Either way it was going to be a long night. Where it went from here was going to be anyone’s guess. ~To be Continued~
