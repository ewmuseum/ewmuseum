Vampyr: Chapter Three

13 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13 May 05 His eyes, blood shot and tired, slowly opened. Darkness surrounded him, not that this was anything new to him though. The entire span of this being’s life has been filled with darkness as well as emptiness. Every since he was separated from his father he has had a hole in his very soul, a hole that no amount of pain or suffering to others could ever fill. This does not stop him from causing death and destruction though; it is what had fueled him since the beginning of time. His body lay dormant yet his mind moved at 100 miles per second. His awakening could mean only one thing that it was time to fulfill the dark prophecy that he, himself spoke of three hundred years ago. He smiled inwardly thinking about the three hundred year slumber that consumed him until this point. So much had most definitely changed since his last reign that he would have a lot to catch up on. Ever so slowly the muscles in his ancient body began to come back to life. It started in his fingers began to scratch the wood that he was sprawled out on then went to the rest of his hand and wrist with a violent twitch. The feeling of life flooded his body from his fingers to his toes and every where in between. The pale lips that rested on this creature’s face opened up, letting out a low growl and showing off sparkling white fangs. A pair of shaky hands which had rather long and sharp claws as fingers, moved upwards and rested on the wooden lid that kept the world from his sight. With all of the power that rested inside of this tired body, these hands pushed the lid aside and with a loud and echoing thud, it fell to the hard floor. Those same clawed fingers reached up and clasped the sides of this wooden coffin and began pulling upwards raising the creature out of the box. It took only moments to reach his feet but no movements came next. Breathing was fairly difficult and erratic. His chest went up and down slowly as he stood admiring his surroundings. It had been 300 years since the last time everything had come to these eyes, it was almost like being born again and perhaps that is exactly what this was, the rebirth. The rebirth of Dracula. The world had been fortunate for the past 300 years because it had only had the INFLUENCE of Evil in its society, but now evil would walk amongst society. It was time the one creature that could destroy humanity to come back and claim his throne. He had begun to build a kingdom many years ago, a kingdom that was suppose to thrive and perhaps one day make humanity its slaves. Somewhere over the years this race began going through devolution, forming weaknesses that were far too apparent to any that would wish to cause them harm. Dracula had no apparent weaknesses, he could stalk in the sunlight, allergies to silver were non-existent, Holy water and crucifixes were jokes to him. There was but one way to destroy Dracula, not that he was going to reveal that to the world though. No matter how hard he tried to keep the race pure and free of weakness, it was always in vain. The only ones that were even close to his own power were the ones he turned himself. Sadly even they could not even fathom the power that was flowing in their blood because of their humanity. Each human’s body reacted to the curse in their own way. Unlike the vampires of now, a bite from Dracula did not ensure your transformation however he had to want you to be turned. All of this played a hand in Dracula’s decision to go into his deep slumber. He could not stand watching his race to be destroyed from the inside as he stood by and watched. He lowered himself into his coffin that night in 1705 hoping that his creations would evolve into something far more powerful than they had been. 300 years later and it was time to start the quest to revitalize the vampire nation. If revitalization was impossible then the entire race would be killed off by Dracula’s own power. The Dark One’s movements have become a bit more fluid now, but he still carried a certain rigidness about him. Although his body was fragile at this point in time, the powers that rested in his mind were as strong as ever. He made his way to a window that over looked the town of Brasov, a town in which had been haunted by his presence for centuries. The sun had just dipped below the horizon giving in to the darkness of the night sky. A slight breeze flows through the air giving the air a bit of a chill. Dracula’s long black hair flows behind him moving as if it had a life of its own. The long fingers that came from his hands took a hold of the edge of the window; a methodical tap is heard from the claws on those fingers. Since he went to sleep in the 1700’s his outfit was very similar to that of a British Red Coat. A black dress shirt rested, unbuttoned, underneath a mostly red wool jacket with black trim. A pair of black boots were on top of a set of red “nylons”, with the outfit finished off by a pair of black pants from that time period. Time had certainly passed Drac up but things would fall into place in due time. Before having to worry about such things as clothing, the vampire race, a war, or any of that type of stuff, Dracula had to accomplish a different goal. This goal was perhaps the most important aspect of a vampire’s life. Dracula needed to feed. Staring out at the world, Dracula searched for the one thing that would restore his vigor and youth. Eyes as black as the night sky glared out for any signs of life. After a few moments of searching something caught his eye. A man and a woman, all in all a very beautiful young couple. They were sitting together as the sun went down enjoying each other’s company. A dark smile creased those pale lips then a dry chuckle escaped his throat. “Welcome home ?” Dracula tilted his head back while closing his eyes carefully. Slowly those old, decrepit hands reached upwards, towards the heavens. In a voice that was no more than a whisper, Dracula began chanting. The words were of a language that could not have been known by any historian living these days; it was some sort of ancient language. The wind began to pick up and clouds rolled in to decorate the once completely black sky. The moon seemed to have grown in size by two fold, making it look magnificent. Even though the voice was low the winds carried it away from the castle window and towards the unsuspecting couple. The winds howled louder and louder as Dracula chanted faster and faster. Just as the chanting was reaching its peak a bolt of lightning struck down. Dracula’s eyes opened, no longer was he standing at the window to his dark castle. Now Dracula was standing at the top of the hill that oversaw his castle. In front of him was a young man, a young man who seemed to be in a trance ? of course Dracula knew why. His gaze trailed passed the man and was locked onto the young woman who now had curled up like a baby, shivering to the point of near convulsion, with tears streaming down her face. Dracula raised his eyes to look at the young man once more. The man did not move, out of fear or fascination it is not quite apparent, but probably a mix of both. The two stood there, motionless and speechless for what was probably only seconds. Dracula changed this by walking forwards and eventually passing the young man by. He did not stop until he hovered over the young and frail woman. Her teary blue eyes looked up at Dracula, almost pleading with him to save her from this nightmare that she was involved in. One of those clawed fingers rose up to his lips signaling for the girl to quiet down. Surprisingly her sobs died down to barely a whimper and her shaking settled soon after. A smile spread over Dracula’s lips perhaps reassuring the young woman that everything would be fine. For some reason she felt comfort in this and smiled back. “Wylie ?” Dracula’s voice was still nothing more than a whisper. Wylie’s smile faded at the revelation that this man knew her name. She seemed concerned now, but something deep inside fought the urge to run or scream for help. Dracula reached out with his right hand, offering Wylie a way to get back to her feet. She merely stared at the old looking digits, reluctant to take this particular hand. Everything in her mind was telling her to stay away. Her body was moving on its own though, something she could not explain. With a shaky hand Wylie took Dracula’s and allowed him to pull her up to her feet. Strangely she was drawn to him, drawn to those dead eyes. Dracula let go of Wylie’s hand and stretched out his arms offering her the comfort that her husband, Alex would not. Momentarily she glared at Alex with spite before rushing into Dracula’s arms and tears began falling again. Slowly Dracula spun so he could see into the eyes of Alex, as he gently stroked the hair of Wylie. Alex still did not move, not because he didn’t want to but because he could not. Everything that was happening he could see clearly, but his muscles were paralyzed. He fought to move but it was of no use, he was simply a spectator now. A flash of red went through Dracula’s black eyes as his mouth opened, showing off his razor sharp fangs. Never letting his gaze off of Alex he sunk those fangs into the ripe and tender neck of Wylie. She let out a small scream and wrapped her arms around Dracula’s body even tighter than before. She could feel her blood trickling down the back of her neck and making a trail down her spine. The pain was great but for some reason she found it pleasurable as well, an unexplainable pleasure. To Dracula this blood was refreshing. As it flowed through his body it was like a heroin addict getting his nightly fix. He could feel the youth and strength returning back to his body and it was a great feeling. Dracula used this new found strength to lift Wylie off her feet to continue to drain the blood from her body. Those once decrepit hands began to turn back to their normal selves, he could feel his muscles beginning to grow, and his sight even began getting better than it already was. When there was just enough taken from Wylie’s body Dracula released her, causing her body to crash to the grass below. Dracula leaned his head back and let out an animalistic roar which echoed through the air. Dracula now looked down at Wylie, her blood oozing from his mouth. Making the symbol of a cross he fell to one knee next to her body. She was still alive, but her breathing was very shallow. “In the name of Jesus Christ ? I give to you the dark gift.” Placing his index finger in his mouth, Dracula used one of his fangs to slice that finger open. The droplets of blood fell from the wounds and dripped into the mouth of Wylie. Immediately her breathing stopped. “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!” Alex had finally broken his silence and his movements were his once more. Being smarter than to try and avenge his wife’s death, Alex ran the other way. He pumped his legs faster than they ever had moved aiming for the car. As he reached the car he heard a laugh come from directly behind him, and Alex spun his body around but to his surprise ? no one was there, not Dracula or not his dead wife. Frightened beyond belief he turned around to get into his car, but Dracula’s hand wraps around his throat and Alex is lifted into the air, feet dangling underneath him. “I am so sorry Alex, I thought you would enjoy watching. Death is so much more interesting than life don’t you think?” Alex didn’t say anything mainly because he could not. His struggles to free himself from Drac’s grasp were futile. Dracula now pulled Alex’s face closer to his own, until Alex could smell Wylie on him. Tears spilt out and ran down Alex’s cheeks, which only heightened Dracula’s lust for blood and suffering. “Don’t worry Alex they all cry ? they all suffer.” No more words were spoken as Dracula violently tore at Alex’s throat causing blood to gush and squirt from the wounds. This was not the exotic and sensual way that he took Wylie’s blood. “Drink “.” From behind Dracula walked Wylie who now was obviously quite different. Her face was almost completely white, which made her ruby red lips and light blue eyes stand out. A smile came across her face and she showed off her pearly white fangs. “Sorry Alex I guess Marriage just wasn’t meant for us.” So she fed off her now late husband. Dracula was back to his old self and had already found himself a partner. He watched with excitement as Wylie drained the blood from her former lover’s body. His excitement heightened as she finished and forcefully threw Alex’s body to the ground, causing his head to bounce off the bumper of the Stratus. Her blue eyes went upwards to stare into Dracula’s. He spoke to her ? without vocalizing anything. She came to him, found comfort in those arms of his once again. A hand reached down and curled Wylie’s chin, tilting it upwards. Dracula leaned down and kissed Wylie deeply, she did not fight but aggressively kissed back with a hunger that she had never experienced. Dracula had returned.
