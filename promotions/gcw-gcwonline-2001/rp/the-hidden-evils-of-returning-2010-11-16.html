The Hidden Evils Of Returning

16 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16/10 Things were quiet at Jimmy Thunder's ranch. Jimmy's wife Emma was flicking through an old raggedy cookbook her mother had given her years back, Jimmy Junior was in his room studying, Chloe was with her grandparents and Jimmy had disappeared as the sun was rising. Emma just assumed he had taken a trip down to Home Depot to get some more material for the project he was working on. Emma was in high spirits, her marriage to Jimmy was stronger than ever, she adored his son Jimmy Junior and was the proud mother of their five year old daughter Chloe. Little did she know that Junior running frantically to her in the kitchen would turn her life, unexpectedly, upside down. "Emma quick, turn to channel seven, dad's wrestling again." Junior said while trying to get his breath back. "He's WHAT?" Emma replied with extreme shock in her tone. She quickly turns on the news break on channel seven where a KTVB news reader gave a quick thirty second news bulletin. "It's official, Boise's favorite adopted son Jimmy Thunder has come out of retirement and has returned to Global Championship Wrestling. In a brief press statement made just after he signed the contracts, Jimmy mentioned how great it was to return to GCW. He also mention that he felt he was still in great condition and was looking forward to competing with the best again. We'll have more in our main bulletin at 6pm tonight." Emma, in shock, slowly turns her head to look at Junior who has his head dropped and clearly in as much distress over the news as Emma is. "Lord have mercy!" Emma says softly as she tries to digest her thoughts as to what she had just witnessed. After getting out of the privately charted jet that GCW had organized for Jimmy to get back to Boise with, his excitement showed on his face as he quickly hopped into his car and sped off to surprise his family with the news. While driving, all Jimmy could think about was how excited Emma and Jimmy Junior would be when they hear he's getting back in the ring, especially with a leading brand like Global Championship Wrestling. As the sun started dropping behind the Owyhee Mountains that lay within the landscape of Jimmy Thunder's ranch, Jimmy speeds up his drive way to see all the lights are out in the house and no television or music sounds can be heard from the house. The total quietness that surrounded the ranch made him nervous. He walked up the steps and opened the front door to find both Emma and Junior in the living room, both with a serious look on their faces. If there was ever a time when Jimmy realized he was wrong about something, now was that time. Instead of welcoming him home with cheers and open arms, the two most important people in his life were oozing disappointment with the somber look on their faces. "I'm guessing there's no welcome back cake in the kitchen." Jimmy said with a sarcastic grin on his face. Emma stands to her feet and looks at Jimmy, angry and disappointed as she says, "How dare you go and make an important decision like this without consulting me or your son. Who do you think you are?" She quickly leaves the room with Jimmy helplessly raising both hands waist high before he lets them drop to his sides again knowing it's hopeless trying to talk to her. He turns to his son as he stands up as well. "Do you agree with her?" He asks with a pleading tone. Junior just nods his head that bares an angry face as well. Jimmy walks out of the living room, disbelieving what had just transpired. He walks into his state of the art gym, filled with high tech equipment and a full sized wrestling ring. Jimmy walks up the steps that lead into the ring, steps over the top rope and then starts to walk around inside it as the realization as to what had just happened in his house is slowly starting to sink in. Thinking he would get his family's full support, Jimmy was sorely mistaken. Yes it would be safe to say that Jimmy is feeling disappointed that things didn't go as planned but you can say that his pride, for the first time, had taken a beating by the people he loved. The door to the gym open with the tunes of squeaky hinges, breaking the cold silence that lingered inside the gym. Jimmy quickly turns around and sees his son standing in the door war. Jimmy starts to feel a cold breeze flow into the gym from behind Junior as a smile slowly creeps onto Jimmy's face. "Here to tell me that you changed your mind?" Jimmy asks as he slowly shuffles his feet from one shoe to another, in front of him. He leans on the ropes as he watches his son shake his head. Any hope of support that Jimmy bared sunk to the bottom of his slowly braking heart. " I can't believe you dad!" Junior uttered with a soft painful voice. "All my life I've had to grow up as the son of the great Jimmy Thunder, do you realize how hard it is growing up trying to walk in your shoes? All through out school I was picked on and now people are starting to forget about you, I can slide into college under a blanket of anonymity and then you go ahead and do this. Why do you always have to make my life so difficult? Junior asks with a frustrated tone. "You are probably most selfish person I have ever known." He adds while he stares into Jimmy's eyes before Jimmy's head drops. "Are you finished?" Jimmy asks as he slowly lifts his head to look back into his son's eyes. "You think I'm selfish? Did you think I was selfish when I went in the ring to buy us everything we have now? Did you think I was selfish that I did it all again when I nearly went broke?." Jimmy asks as his lips turn in showing his anger. "Did you think I was selfish when I used the money I made from wrestling to pay for you to go to college?" With an angry state of mind Jimmy decides to let it all out. "You have never said to me you are proud of your dad, while I tell you everyday that I'm proud you're making your own life. All the guys I wrestled with, their kids are following in their footsteps but not mine, he's going to be a doctor and I couldn't be prouder, I tell everyone how proud of you I am but not once have you been proud of your old man. It all has to be about you and it's you that is selfish, not me, you!" Jimmy's angry tone becomes evident while he points his finger at his son. Junior drops his head while Jimmy goes silent when Emma walks in the room. "Jimmy, we're just worried you're going to get seriously hurt, for what?" She explains. "We've got this beautiful house, we've got cars, we've got more money than what we know what to do with. Why Jimmy? I just want to understand why." She begs "Cause I'm a fighter, Emma. That's the way I was born, that's how I grew up and that's what I'll always be." Jimmy replies with a determined tone. "All through my life I've always done things for other people, for the first time I want to do something for me, why is it so hard for both of you to understand I need to do this for me!" Jimmy continues with while he pokes himself in the chest with his index finger. Emma walks up to Jimmy and looks him right in the eyes as she says "I can understand where you're coming from but understand from our point of view, I have to help you out of bed some mornings cause your knees are shot. You have to get cortisone shots in your back every few months. How much more of this can you take?" "Emma, Junior, I understand your concerns and you have every right to be concerned. Let me put it to you guys this way, it's easier to live with someone who has damaged body parts than to live with someone who lives with 'what if's' in his life." Jimmy explains. He looks at both Emma and Junior who start to see his point of view. "I think I'm going to take my bike for a ride to clear my head." He finished with as he slowly walks out of the gym. While Jimmy is out riding his Harley Davidson, Emma decides it's time to bring in some reinforcements, to try and talk Jimmy out his plans to return to the ring. If there's someone Jimmy would listen to it's his former brother-in-law, Theo. Jimmy and Theo have a long history together. Apart from wrestling, Jimmy was married to Theo's sister, Aurora, before her untimely death. Emma calls Theo on the phone and after hearing her plight, he decides he has to do something quickly to talk Jimmy out of his return to wrestling. Jimmy walks back into his dark, state of the art gym after he finishes his late night motorcycle ride. His foot steps echoed through out the gymnasium as Jimmy walked up to the photographs hanging up on a lone lit up wall. With a smile on his face, Jimmy reminisces on the epic battles that made him a household name across America. Legendary matches with guys like Tony Aielli, Maher Reims, and his own brother-in-law, Theodoric Solomon. “We had some great times.” Jimmy instantly turned around when he heard an unexpected voice at the door way of the gym. “Who’s that?” Jimmy asked with a slight curious panic tone in his voice as the dark night disguises the mystery man from Jimmy. The mystery man steps slowly forward into the moonlight to reveal himself as Theodoric Solomon. “ "Theo?" Jimmy asked, just for his own confirmation. "Why are you doing this Jimmy?" Theo asked while tilting his head back and taking some steps forward. "Do you hear that Theo?" Jimmy asks "It's quiet, isn't it. When you're retired it's too quiet." He adds with a smile on his face. Theo disagrees and starts shaking his head before he says, "Jimmy, even though my sister Aurora passed away several years ago, I still love you like you were brother." Jimmy stares into Theo's eyes as he prepares himself for what he is about to say. "Jimmy, I have been in many wars with you, both with you and against you. Nobody knows you better than me. Your mind is telling you to keep going but you body can't go on, you know it too." Jimmy drops his head in frustration then lifts it and stares at Theo, "So you're on their side on this too?" Jimmy asks. "It's not a matter of picking sides, I could be wrong but sweet Jesus Jimmy, I don't want to find out. I know you're bored but we'll find something to do, together. Think of something and we'll do it together. I'll be your friend and be by your side." Theo finished with a hopeful smile on his face. "Stand by myside at World Wide?" Jimmy asked. Theo closes his eyes and takes a deep breath as an overwhelming feeling of disappointment goes through his body. "I didn't even make a dent, did I" he asks. Jimmy just shakes his head. Theo looks at him and nods as he walks away leaving like a failure. Theo tried to help both Jimmy and his family and he had failed. He leaves Jimmy standing in his gym fearing the next time he sees his former brother-in-law and the only man he can truly call his friend, in a hospital bed. The day before World Wide 139, Jimmy packs his bag alone. Feeling a sense of hope that either Junior, Emma or both would come along with him. He walks into his living room and nobody to be found. He peeks into the kitchen and sees Emma sitting, alone, drinking her coffee and staring at the wall opposite the table, deep in thought. Jimmy closed his eyes and prepares himself for another bout of Emma trying to talk him out of going to World Wide. Jimmy pushes the door open and says in a soft voice, "I'm leaving." "Bye." Emma replies with a cold tone and without turning her head. Jimmy just nods his head, turns around and leaves. He walks out to his car and realizes that Junior's car is gone. That was his last hope, it's clearly evident now to Jimmy that the latest adventure he is embarking on in his life, will be a lonely one. Just before he climbed in his car he took one last look at his house and notices Emma standing in the window. They exchange short smiles at each other before Jimmy climbs into his car to head to Boise Airport and his return to GCW at World Wide 139. The event was a huge success for Jimmy. He prepares to leave Gaylord Entertainment Center in Nashville with an overwhelming numb feeling running through his body. Up until the beginning of World Wide 139, Jimmy had no idea how he would be welcomed back by his fans. It had been over two years since he graced a GCW ring. He thought the fans would have forgotten him by now. All that was forgotten when his music hit the arena speakers and his fans belted out an all mighty cheer for him. He can still hear that cheer an hour after the show had finished. As he prepares to leave the arena and head to his hotel, Jimmy is unexpectedly mobbed by some fans in the car park area. They weren't in the same frenzied state when he first walked into the arena, instead more subdued but still excited to see their hero return to GCW. After spending nearly thirty minutes signing autographs and taking pictures, he manages to hitch a ride back to his hotel with Sally Ford. The two exchange some small talk before she drops him off at his hotel. Even when he walks into the hotel foyer, Jimmy gets an applause from the hotel's guest who have gathered at the bar. Jimmy gives them a smile and wave before heading up to his room. Once showered, Jimmy was able to sit back and finally kick his feet up and relax. It was then loneliness hit him like a sledgehammer, right in the chest. It's been years since he was alone somewhere in a strange city and he hated every moment of it. He was use to having his little girl Chloe's wild antics in his face seven days a week. Now all he has is total silence. Jimmy's harsh reality now is setting in. Does he go back home or does he move onto St Louis Missouri for World Wide 140? The thing that is keeping Jimmy away from going home is the guaranteed guilt trip he will get from Emma, Junior and Theo. Emotionally, Jimmy realizes he cant handle another dose. Even though a huge part of him is missing, Jimmy knows he has to push forward and head to World Wide 140. Sitting in his dressing room as World Wide 140 comes to a close. The empty feeling Jimmy had inside was made worse after his defeat at the hands of Alexander Redding in his first match since returning back to GCW. The big question everyone is starting to ask now is this the best that Jimmy Thunder has or is he leaving something behind? One thing has been made clear to Jimmy is that with Dangerous Games next week, the beginning of the road to the yearly NC-17 extravaganza, Jimmy has to put an end to all this. He either puts an end to the emptiness within him or he walks away from the world of professional wrestling forever.
