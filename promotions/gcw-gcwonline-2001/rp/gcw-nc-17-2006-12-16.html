GCW (NC-17)

16 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16 Dec 06 [ The scene opens ] Three letters. Big, bold, and certainly familiar. These letters light up in front of an all black background like fireworks illuminating a dark 4th of July sky. The power behind those three letters is incredible. Slowly they become brighter and brighter until the entire place is engulfed in their light. An Explosion. The eerie notes of “O’Fortuna” softly play in the background. Those once bright letters are now large and black on the screen. G-C-W The large letters fly forward and move out of sight, one by one until the screen is left with nothing but darkness. The camera slowly pans down, eventually showing a typical interview set. Global Championship Wrestling interviewer, Kevin Jacobs sits across from GCW World Heavyweight Champion, LeStatt Knight. Kevin waits until “O’Fotuna” finishes up before uttering any word. KEVIN JACOBS: Hello wrestling fans, I am Kevin Jacobs and welcome to NC-17: In Depth. As you all know, GCW’s biggest spectacle of the year is fast approaching and with it comes an assortment of different activities, interviews, autograph signings, and backstage access. Tonight is a very special night indeed as we have been granted an exclusive one on one interview with GCW’s most decorated superstar, LeStatt Knight. ( Jacobs turns from the camera and to Knight. ) Welcome LeStatt, it’s good to have you here tonight. LESTATT KNIGHT: Good to be here Kevin. KEVIN JACOBS: Well I don’t see no need to beat around the bush, what do you say we jump right into what we are here for? ( Knight nods in agreement ) Knight, your story here in GCW has been well documented, particularly these past two years. You have become a legend and an Icon but things were not always that way. As a matter of fact your start in GCW was a bit rocky yes? LESTATT KNIGHT: It really was Kevin, it really was. I was so new to this sport when I came to GCW that I wasn’t even scouted by Caldera. I was at an Indy show when I was approached after the match by former GCW World Champ, John Reed. At the time he actually wanted me to try out for an upstart promotion that he was backing. For one reason or another, the project fell on its face, so he told me to give GCW a try. Reed put a good word in for me; got me a try-out and Caldera signed me to a developmental contract. KEVIN JACOBS: A lot of fans may not know but you actually didn’t begin as “LeStatt Knight.” LESTATT KNIGHT: ( gives a short chuckle ) No, no I didn’t. I put my career in the hands of the GCW writers in the beginning. They slapped a mask on me, told me to be arrogant. Basically I was the original Triple H with a mask. Let’s just say that shit didn’t fly with me for very long. KEVIN JACOBS: When did LeStatt come about? LESTATT KNIGHT: It was a couple weeks before Dangerous Games of 2001. I told the creative team that I’d have something else. I knew right off the bat that the mask would have to go. I knew I needed a name that was smooth, intelligent, but could be frightening as well. I don’t need to tell anyone where I got LeStatt from ( he laughs ). I loved the movie Interview with a Vampire, and actually I enjoyed Brad Pitt’s performance more ? but LeStatt is just a much more intimidating name. Knight just seemed like the best surname to cap it all off. KEVIN JACOBS: You had a hard time finding your place in GCW even after the switch. Why do you think that was? LESTATT KNIGHT: I guess you’d have to ask Steven about that. For a long time I resented Caldera for holding me back for people I felt couldn’t touch me. The Jhubs, Octavians, John Reeds, etc. There was a time when I thought maybe I wasn’t really built for this profession. I seriously thought about quitting before my career ever really even got started. ________________________________________________ So there I stood, staring at the big double doors that led to the arena. It was my first Pay Per View Event, Dangerous Games. The World had known me as Khris Krowe up until that point but on that night they would learn of a new one. For some reason I couldn’t move, hell I could barely breathe. After the things that went down with my brother and his murder ? or so I thought at the time ? I had become jaded as well as very reckless. There was not a person I wouldn’t fight or a challenge I wouldn’t stand up to. I thought I was fearless. I was not. On that night I felt fear mixed with anxiety. I felt as if the world could come tumbling down upon me at any moment. What if I failed again? What if LeStatt Knight couldn’t earn the admiration and the respect of the fans and wrestlers alike? Could I fail at something I had worked so hard to achieve? Well I was going to find out soon enough. Sure one night doesn’t make a career but this was a first impression ? actually a second first impression. Something I now know I was blessed to have. So I stepped through the double doors. I put on that confident smirk ? or arrogant smile, depending on whom you ask about it. I kept telling myself that this night was about me, my shot to really show that I belonged. So much so in fact that began believing my own bullshit. In hindsight I should’ve known better. “Hey you!” I yelled out to a GCW backstage worker. “Where’s my locker room?” I guess it was after his next comment and gesture that brought me back down to earth. I strolled in there expecting something luxurious ? “The bathroom sir.” “Beg your pardon?” The bathroom!” “Yes right through that door.” Talk about humble beginnings. I don’t remember the exact place I finished in that battle royal that night. I don’t remember who I eliminated or even who eliminated me. What I do remember is that bathroom. I remember changing in an area with 15 other people that was half the size of the locker rooms I get today. Looking back I laugh ? how young and na’ve I was to think things would get handed to me. Every story has to have a beginning. The Legacy of LeStatt Knight began in a dirty bathroom with a broken mirror and a leaky faucet. ___________________________________________ KEVIN JACOBS: If nothing else your story should give hope. Starting the way you did had to be discouraging. You have lifted yourself up and turned what was once garbage into gold. I don’t know how many people would have stuck around. LESTATT KNIGHT: Actually for awhile I did quit. I went to small fed called Dragon Wrestling Association and even became the World Champion for a very short time before I was forced out. From there I bounced around a bit, even found myself a new nemesis in former GCW star Johnny Lexicon. I had some fun but GCW was my birthplace, so-to speak. Every time Caldera brought it back to life I was one of the first in line. KEVIN JACOBS: Well let’s talk about your historic run here in GCW, shall we? What happened to get you back? LESTATT KNIGHT: It was late January 2005 and I had been out of work for a short time. I actually was enjoying my time off, working on a few business ventures I have going. I got a phone call from one of Caldera’s lackeys telling me that Steven was considering bringing GCW back. I was interested of course, but I had my doubts. Everyone knows Steven has had his moments when he was too controlling, not to mention the fact that I felt he held me back for years. It was said that this time GCW would be about the wrestlers and the fans. Caldera didn’t care about worldwide exposure or money making; he just wanted to have fun again, with the old school roster. I signed that very day. KEVIN JACOBS: Since you’ve been back you have done things that very few people, if any, in this company’s history can claim. You won the United States title and held it for a record time, at least at the time. More impressive though is the fact that you defended it EVERY week, as if it were more of a TV title. LESTATT KNIGHT: I wanted a challenge. I feed off the competition Kevin, I think you know this. I didn’t want the title to sit idle. I figured that if I wasn’t defending the title then I didn’t deserve to hold it. I have been around the block a few times and watched as so-called champions only fought for their title once a month. Where’s the prestige in that? No I was going to make my title reign mean something more. KEVIN JACOBS: Now we go to your two World Title reigns. Your first also record breaking. How rewarding was it to finally capture the GCW World championship? LESTATT KNIGHT: As you can see ? I can’t help but smile. _________________________________________________ “I QUIT!” The boos rained down at me like softball sized hail. Those fans hated me, or they loved to hate me. I gave Jason Dread a beating that he wasn’t soon to forget and I won in a fashion that the wrestling world would be talking about for years. Would I have really caught that bastard on fire? I’ll never tell. The only thing that mattered was that Jason thought I would, and so did those fans. It was like music to my ears. However the best feeling? That strap of gold and leather that fit perfectly around my waist. I had been a World Champion once before and yes that felt great. However this was a different feeling. Back in DWA I got the greatest pleasure because of WHO I beat for that title. Payne. Breaking that man was more satisfying than any single accomplishment I had achieved up to that point. If you would have told me just a few years later that he’d be my closest confidant ? I think I’d have shot you. Now Jason Dread was no slouch, not by any means but I didn’t HATE him like I hated Payne. Dread was just the next in line to get to where I wanted to go. The greatest feeling in winning the GCW title was putting it back in Caldera’s face. Not just his but all of the doubters, writers, fans, and wrestlers who thought people like Tristian Mayhem and Toxic were better prospects than myself. Like the Phoenix I rose from the ashes. Dangerous Games 2005 marked the day that GCW became LeStatt Knight’s stomping ground. I remember standing there on that ramp, holding the title, hearing the boos, staring back at Jason Dread and thinking only one thing. “Welcome home Roberto.” Home Sweet Home. ______________________________________________ LESTATT KNIGHT: It doesn’t get any better than that Kevin ? it still hasn’t. Things have changed so much in GCW since then; it’s almost like a completely different place. KEVIN JACOBS: It is true that a lot has happened since last year’s NC-17 when you lost the World Title to Michael Stevens. One thing that I have noticed is that you have been fairly quiet when it comes to voicing your opinion. The invasion of Dies Irae, multiple GCW stars leaving, the new blood that has been injected into the roster, etc. What has been the reason for your silence? LESTATT KNIGHT: (he sighs) Some of it has been hard to watch in all honesty. I take pride in what I have helped build here in GCW. I’m confident in my abilities and everyone knows that so I have no problems saying that if it weren’t for me this place wouldn’t be half as successful as it has been. I’m not foolish or blind however, I know others had a hand in our success. Trey Tremendous, Michael Stevens, Jason Dread, Eva VanLorne, Rich Rollins, Bryan Mayhem, Octavian, among others. To see GCW begin to slip as it has been has been the most difficult thing to watch. KEVIN JACOBS: Slip? What do you mean by that? LESTATT KNIGHT: No one can deny that GCW has some of the most talented individuals in this sport today. Talent doesn’t always equal success though. GCW is in a transitional period and it’s been a bumpy road thus far. Dies Irae has come in and declared war on GCW but what they have failed to notice now a days is that GCW isn’t GCW anymore. Who are they battling? Bryan Mayhem and I are the only originals left. Bryan has sided with them for whatever crazy reasons he has for doing ANYTHING he does and this “Ascension” felt it didn’t need me. So you have a bunch of people who don’t care about the GCW name fighting for it? Give me a fucking break. KEVIN JACOBS: So you don’t believe that this Tessa Windsor-led Ascension can get the job done? LESTATT KNIGHT: Them getting the job done isn’t even the point. It’s that they don’t know what they are fighting for. Sometimes it doesn’t even feel like this is GCW anymore. ( Kevin adjusts his body and takes a peak down at his cards before continuing. ) KEVIN JACOBS: If I can adjust fire just a bit, what are your thoughts on the Rich Rollins and Eva VanLorne “sagas” that have occurred or are occurring? LESTATT KNIGHT: It’s sad amigo. (he gives a laugh) Look I always thought Eva was worthless inside of that ring. She sold some T-Shirts and got the guys in the audience to pay more attention than they normally would to certain matches. Sure she was good in bed but what else did she do? Sorry but it takes more than a couple of incredibly short title reigns to impress me. Frankly I thought she got the exact kind of send off she deserved ? going out with a whimper. She didn’t deserve the popularity or respect she had gotten. Hell since she arrived I said that she only got noticed because she went against me first. Her asshole of a brother hit me with a chair and immediately they got exposure. Eva is and always will be pathetic. End of story. As for Rich Rollins? (LeStatt snickers) Who cares? That’s Caldera’s fight. Rollins left GCW to go to PRIME and has now decided to bash GCW every chance hes gotten. Rollins would be nothing without GCW and he knows it. Only reason he says what he does is because he knows it eats away at Steven. Frankly I think that Steven is wasting his time. Just like VanLorne, Rollins can go fuck himself. We spend so much time worrying about who isn’t around anymore when we should look at who is still standing. Rollins turned his back on GCW? Fuck him. Caldera is going to lose far more than Rollins if he wastes all of his time and influence on taking him apart. He has a federation to run and that part may become lost if he doesn’t get his ego in check. In a way I find it insulting. There are some of us that have bled GCW through thick and thin. Yet who gets the bosses attention? People who decided that they were too big for GCW. People who think that PTC fame is better than making their HOME the best it can be. Caldera might as well have just slapped us all in the face. If he doesn’t destroy Rollins in ACWF then he will have completely and utterly failed. And with Almasy as his partner? God help him. KEVIN JACOBS: LeStatt let me just say that it has been a great pleasure having you hear tonight. It isn’t everyday that you grant interviews like theses. LESTATT KNIGHT: Or ever (Knight chuckles) KEVIN JACOBS: Or Ever. Before you go I’d like to ask you one more question. LESTATT KNIGHT: Hit me. KEVIN JACOBS: What does the future hold for LeStatt Knight? LESTATT KNIGHT: The future? (his face turns to a look of contemplation.) The future? ______________________________________________ I stand at my window staring out at the snowy night landscape. I couldn’t sleep, I tried though but there is just too much on my mind. Mary came home earlier tonight, our baby as well. I finally have the family that I have longed for. Things finally seem clear ? but at the same time as cloudy as ever. Makes no sense right? Trust me I know. I have everything I’ve ever wanted. The love of a woman, the admiration of millions of fans, money, fame, respect. I am the GCW World fucking Champion! No doubt that Caldera will send me into the Hall of Fame someday. It has been such a wild ride so far, can’t even begin to describe what it feels like to accomplish everything you’ve set your mind on. Another year is coming to an end but it feels like much more than that. I turn my gaze from the outside to the inside. Literally and figuratively. Mary is so beautiful. A slight smile stays imprinted on her lips. She deserves to be happy as much as or even more so than I do. She has been through hell and survived. I owe it to her to give her every bit of love and devotion that she has given me. And our child deserves a father. Slowly I lay down next to Mary. Her slender arms slowly slide down and her hand connects with mine and she pulls my arm around her body. It’s amazing what her soft touch and the warmth of her body can do. I feel safe when I’m with her. Like nobody can touch me, nothing can hurt me. She is my future. _______________________________________________ (LeStatt’s lips form a smile.) LESTATT KNIGHT: The future is very bright for LeStatt Knight. Good night Kevin. (Knight now stands up and walks off the set. Like so many of their backstage interviews Jacobs is left alone but this time he looks satisfied.) KEVIN JACOBS: I’m Kevin Jacobs, signing off. ( The letters G-C-W flash on the screen once again. ) [ Fade to black? ]
