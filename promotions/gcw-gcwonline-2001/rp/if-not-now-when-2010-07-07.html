To eschew that -- to rebrand himself a reader, not a writer -- to forget the woes of abortive creation, the contradictions of temporary celebrity, the sharp and absolute pain that came with sitting at the keyboard when life outside moved on without him, the winds blowing, the leaves changing, the children growing old and the elderly growing young -- was to save himself.
