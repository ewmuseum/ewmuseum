Technical Mastery and Dastardly Deeds

25 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25 Nov 05 Quick OOC Note: Sorry about it's length. Rough spot for me, just wanted to throw something together so you all wouldn't flog me for no-showing. Enjoy! -- --------------------------------------------------------- ---[Technical Mastery. It's a pride point for me. There is nobody in GCW that can match with me technically, but they have totally circumvented that, because now I am in a scaffold match. A SCAFFOLD MATCH. So much for displays of technical mastery, eh? I kind of feel like Kunta Kinte at a Merle Haggard concert. A little lost, a little afraid, and completely out of my element. But I have to do what I have to do, I have to even the field.]--- [[A ringing phone. It's Thanksgiving day, I am carving the turkey, and my god damned phone is ringing. So, of course, I answer it.]] Shayne: What! Oh, hey man. Didn't know it was you. What's up? *****: Just got back from Japan, I've been touring over there since the last time I saw you. Big time scaffold match, huh? Shayne: I suppose you can say that. I was just thinking about how hard it is for a technical master such as myself to embark on such a spotmonkey-esque journey. It's really sad that people can't get over using their heads anymore. They have to use their bodies. *****: And that is yet another broken wall of Kayfabe. But hey, it's what we do. Anyways, I'm back in Tuscon. I was going to take some time off, but I realized that you had come back, so... Shayne: I hear you, man. We'll talk about that later. Sounds like a plan though. *****: Alright, go eat. Kiss the wife for me. Shayne: Later, asshole. [[Once I hung up, and sat down to eat with my wife, I realized what had just transpired over the phone. I was once a part of the most controversial tag team in professional wrestling, and it was a ride. Trips to Mexico, months on the road chasing tail, and all the money and women. I could have slapped the President of the United States and gotten away with it back then. We were power players, true life superstars. I gave that up to chase the world championship that has eluded me since I was 18. He had already been a world champion, all over the globe, and he came back to reform the band. I would have to be an idiot to turn this down. We comprise the single best tag team on the planet - Not an exaggeration, we really are. It is widely known. Read a book, or something. Suddenly, NC-17 has become less about a scaffold match, and more about a revolution.]]
