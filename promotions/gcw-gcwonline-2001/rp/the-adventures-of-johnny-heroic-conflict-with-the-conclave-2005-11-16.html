The Adventures of Johnny Heroic: Conflict with the Conclave

16 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16 'I concur with Thunderclap. Something has to be done about this situation before it escalates further.' 'Fraulein is right. First it's Fate, Fortune were murdered in cold blood out of a want for revenge. And then Destine, his arch nemesis, driven to maddness until he committed suicide. Then we each have run ins that are a little less than productive. Not anywhere near as friendly as they should have been. And now this. The Masked Avenger is dead, stabbed with a knife while trying to apologize to Heroic. The man has to be stopped, no question about it.' 'Perhaps, but to what depth do we stoop, Carbomb? We cannot sink as low as he, for fear that we would simply replace him.' I've been more than deathly silent for the past two hours. Fraulein, one of the most renouned if not most obnoxious of heroes that have recently moved to The City, has called a meeting among the Conclave of Justice to discuss matters involving none other than me. Johnny Heroic. And how they feel that I haven't been living up to that name lately. Let me stress, by the way, that they have come to my city in the first place. You know, the place that I've had under control for a long, long time. I was told it was because I lost my powers. Heroes flooded here in a rush to 'assist' me once they found out that I was no longer omnipotent, that I wasn't able to control electromagnetic fields, that I was no longer the luckiest son of a bitch in the universe. Fraulein, with the power to persuade even the most dead-set person to turn on himself. Thunderclap, with the ability to make a lot of noise, though he never seems to damage anything... Carbomb, with the power...of being Irish. I guess Guiness is some sort of source of fuel that allows him to turn on the afterburners and get into fights like nobody's business. MVP, with the power of stealth, sneaking up on people and unleashing a terrible fury when they're not expecting it. Samanuske Yokoto, with the powers of flight and slowing down time and being a Kung Fu master and every other power you'll see in a cheesy martial arts movie. The Christening, with the power to drive anyone nuts without barely even trying. And, their leader, glistening in his armor of sweet, sweet justice, none other than the White Knight, with the power to serve and protect even the most meek of peoples. If you ask me, it is more the power to deal with whining babies that can't get by themselves. Pathetic. All of them. 'How can we allow him to run about unchecked?' 'Which one of us would stop him?' 'Any one of us. He has no powers any longer.' 'No. But he's got guns. And he's not afraid to take a life, as he's already shown several times.' The White Knight has been watching silently. He is moreso the keeper of order, not the leader of this group. He is above that. Beyond that. Believes himself to be without need for concern over the petty matters of the 'lesser' heroes. He believes himself to be superior to Johnny Heroic, who indeed has no powers, and would only try to get into a street fight with him which the White Knight would surely be victorious in. My ass. But, as I said, he does keep order. And his little minions of good obey. 'Alright, let us stop squabbling. This bickering will get us nowhere. Now...it is Fraulein that has called this meeting. And it shall be Fraulein that will address the issue. I now call this session of the Conclave of Justice to order. The lady has the floor.' I've done enough evesdropping. Sitting up here any longer will only serve for boredom. And to make my entrance from above, through this skylight, will merely give them the knowledge that I know that they were speaking of. Not that it's any strategic advantage for me to know that they hate me. That they envy me. That they pretend that they look down on me while secretly wishing that they could do what I do with as much in the way of balls as I do it. But I leave them for a few minutes for Fraulein to say what I already know. That I've murdered The Masked Avenger. Granted, none of them were there. None of them saw the scenario, and thus none of them could possibly appreciate my side of what happened. But that's okay. She'll explain that I broke rule number one of being a hero. I killed my arch nemesis' two bodyguards and then the mental anguish drove him to putting a bullet through his head. And apparently, that's not what I'm supposed to do. Instead, I'm supposed to save absolutely everybody. Which means that instead of putting a bullet and a knife into Fate and Fortune, I should have let them run loose and murdered God knows how many more people over the last few months. And I should have been there, making sure that Destine didn't do himself in, and while I was babysitting a mass murdering criminal, the everyday people of The City would run around in complete chaos without their ever-present hero to guide them. Climb down the fire escape and hit the street. I figure the direct approach would be the best. That's the way I've been doing most any/everything lately. I've got about eight guns and four knives that they'll find on me. One gun and a knife that they won't find. And, of course, a couple grenades and the like are brandished right across my chest, for the world to see. It's the theory of walking tall and carrying a big stick. Only I mix that with moving fast and hoping I don't get the shit beat out of me. I guess you could say that now my power is super balls. With the ability to piss off an entire city with a single action. And the power to kill you. That is, I make you dead. Reach the front door and there's no need to knock. It's a room full of superheroes, so there's no chance that they'll lock it. But I know that as soon as I go to open it, Fraulein hears the creaking of the door, the molecules of wood moving through the air. Her ability to detect even the slightest disturbance is staggering. No matter how hard you try to keep something under wraps, you can be guaranteed that Fraulein will show up and stick her gorgeous little nose in it. I can hear her stop talking about me as soon as I push the door open. So, I figure that with the element of surprise behind me, there is no use for subtlety any longer. With a thrust, the double doors to the Conclave of Justice's meeting hall open up and I am standing before the bunch of pretentious bastards that call themselves heroes. People that have literally moved into my city, the one that I've been protecting alone for my entire life, and started telling me how to run things. There isn't one of them I don't hate. 'Friends...you called a meeting without me?' I can almost feel the flegm in my throat coat the word 'friends' and slide it out onto each one of them. The White Knight and Fraulein are the only ones that don't immediately take a defensive stance. And I don't know where MVP is. Probably sneaking around, looking to attack me when I would least expect it. Carbomb takes a swig of his beer and rolls up the sleeves on his leather jacket. It's amazing the people that call themselves heroes these days. Fraulein speaks for the group. 'You've got more audacity than I thought, Johnny Heroic. Coming here to take us all on.' 'Take you all on? Take you all on...' I find that repetition of a phrase really makes people think that you're considering what they're saying for the first time. Makes it sound like I'm surprised that she suggested such a thing. In reality, I've put together a good two dozen scenarios in my head where I could, in theory, take most of them out. But the numbers game is a hard one to beat, and the only way that I could have a good chance of killing everybody in this room would involve more firepower than I brought. Anyway, back to telling them off. 'Now why, my German mink, would I try to take you all on? After all, there are so many of you...and there's only one of me. And it's not like I'm running around with the cosmos at my fingertips any longer. I'm not able to move faster than Yokoto or protect more people than the White Knight. I'm not able to put out a stronger aura of charisma than you, dear. And in general I only have the powers of any everyday person out there. Except, of course, that I have the resolve that none of you know about.' 'And what resolve is that, you piece of shit! The resolve to kill someone needlessly!' 'Harsh words, Christening. Harsh words. I have the resolve to do what I need to do. This is my city, and I must be the one to protect it. Protect it from outsiders and insurgents. Protect it from criminals and false senses of security provided by would-be heroes. You sit on your throne, Knight, and you judge me. You order you little delusional toadies to help carry out that judgement while you look on to the horizon. You contemplate grand battles on a planetary scale with the most vile of creature that we know of. Well I don't care about the rest of the world, and I don't care about what villains and spaws of Hell do as long as it doesn't interfere with The City. As long as it doesn't risk the people that I protect.' The White Knight looks bored, as usual, and wishes he were off valiantly slaying the bringers of darkness in far away lands. He is no hero. He is merely someone who wants to become a legend. A Beowulf. A Hercules. 'The people you protect are through with you, Heroic. They're through living in fear of what you might do to them. You kill indiscriminately-' 'I kill when it's needed.' '-You fight with fellow heroes-' 'You get in my way.' '-You have turned your back on the citizens-' 'They've turned their backs on the law! They've turned their backs on me!' Didn't I tell you that Christening was great at aggravating people? The ping-pong attack on my ears from him and Fraulein was almost too much. Had half a mind to pull out the guns and start wasting people right then and there. But, the fact that I only had half a mind to do that is what separates me from the criminals anymore. The fullness of my consciousness will always remain on being a hero. And until these guys start getting in my way of protecting The City, they are citizens all the same. The White Knight still sits on his throne and says nothing, white Carbomb and Yokoto remain steadfast, ready to spring into action if I do anything that will require bloodshed. A motion from Fraulein stays Christening's annoyance powers for a minute and she looks at me with gorgeous, longing eyes. Makes me feel at home in them, like she'll take care of everything that's happened, everything that's gone wrong. Forgiveness is in them. And hope. And charity. And love. She reels me in like a fish and I don't even realize it. 'We're doing this for you, Johnny. We're doing this to save you from yourself. So that you don't become the monster that we know you fear. And you think none of us can feel the hate swelling in you, churning in you, threatening to capsize everything that you are? You can trust us, Johnny. We are what you were, and you can still come back to us. Have a home in us. Trust in us. Just close your eyes, Johnny...close your eyes and rest with us. We will always be here to help you...' Part of me didn't even feel my feet going toward her. Eyelids got heavy and a heart longed to rest against hers. I have half a mind to give up on my entire pursuit. To sit back and allow the Conclave of Justice free reign over The City, to protect it as they see fit and to allow me my time to rest. Relax, Johnny Heroic. They reinforcements are here. Like in the end of Doom 3, when you kill the bad guy and all the other marines come in and save you. They'll handle it now. You've been the protector, the hero of this city for so long that there is nothing but numbness felt in constantly protecting it, constantly enduring it. Rest is so easy... And right there! There is that glint in her eye. She almost had me fooled. She almost had me convinced to rest in her and give myself up. But that small look in her eye revealed all in just a flash. A flicker of hatred. A small speck of complete and utter loathing from her tells me how much she loved The Masked Avenger, who I had stabbed to death only a few weeks ago. She's not over that yet, and why should she be? And that's enough to snap me back to reality and realize what she's doing; she's simply putting on the facade to lure me into a trap. A quick flick of the wrist and I'm digging the barrel of one of my magnums in the chest I was about to lay my head on. The entire room springs to action and MVP finally makes an appearance, grabbing me from behind and getting me in a bit of a chokehold. Samanoske pulls out his samurai sword and Carbomb breaks a bottle and they start coming toward me, and even The White Knight takes some action and readies himself to fight the rabble. Christening puts his hand on her shoulder and Thunderclap finally makes a loud appearance, yelling for me to stop. Of course I don't stop. Nobody ever listens to Thunderclap. But me and Fraulein...we just stare each other right in the eyes. And my gun doesn't leave her chest. The cocking back of the hammer is warning enough to cause MVP to loosen his grip, knowing from the stories of what I've done that I'm not afraid of killing her if I'm about to go down as well. The commotion lasts only a few seconds before the White Knight raises his hand for silence. He allows Fraulein to speak silently to me. 'What, Heroic? Will you kill me as well? Will you put a bullet in me because we disagree.' 'You're interfering with my life.' 'It's not your life, it's your job.' 'No! You just don't get it! Just like The Masked Avenger, you don't get it, and you're gonna get yourself hurt because of it! He hid behind a mask. You hide behind pretentious ideals that are extinct in this day and age. Decayed. Gone and over with. And, sure enough, you haven't evolved with it. You are stuck where you were. Your jobs are being heroes. My life is being a hero. Maybe you don't hide behind masks, but you hide behind going home at the end of the day and hanging up your tights, knowing that those that you change shifts with will take over for you. When you wake up at four in the morning because you know, from instinct, that every time you sleep, people die...then you can say what you wish to say to me. And only then.' 'Or what? You'll show up and start waving your gun around again?' 'I showed up...and I said hello. I walked in without hatred, and you brought it to my face. And why? Disagreeing. You don't think I should do my job the way that it's done, and you look to stop me. You say that you stand for justice and for freedom? Yet you don't stand for my freedom to accomplish my duties the way that I need to.' Another cold moment crawls by as she stares at me with hating eyes. In this chess game, for once I am the first to move. A sign of trust. A sign of decency. If nothing more, a sign of confidence in their ineptness. I lower the gun from her chest. And I know that she's considering having MVP break my neck, remembering that I killed her former partner, The Masked Moron. But, after a moment, her eyes float to MVP's and a quick nod of her head makes him let go. For a moment, I survey the room. A group of idiots, true and true, playing dress up hero and wanting to be what I was. What I still am. I had omnipotence, and they wish they did. I have balls, and they wish they did. I am a hero. They wish they were. I turn around and begin walking to the door. My gun still in my hand, just in case any of them wish to come after me. 'Just stay out of my way.' ****** And thus, Johnny Heroic left the Conclave of Justice, never to return again in friendship. And as he crosses their threshold, leaving friendship and partnership behind forever, The White Knight and Fraulein signal to Thunderbolt to bring in the assassin that had been waiting a room away during the entirety of the scene. The man known as Wolf, formerly the masked hero called The Mysterious Mister Mystery, strode into the room. Holding much more dignity, commanding much more respect for himself now than when he was but a masked crusader, Wolf is not one of them. But, the old saying goes that the devil's weapons work well against the devil's work. Fraulein speaks up. 'Can you handle him, Wolf?' Young. Brash. Eager. Wolf grits his teeth and remembers the recent events that Johnny Heroic has been responsible for. He travels to his own past and tries to blame Heroic for things that were not his fault, that he couldn't have been a part of. Wolf has never been able to control his remorse, his passion about his broken past. And one of the reasons he isn't considered a hero by many is because he dips into that vat of regret and anger to feed him strength to continue fighting. 'I can, Fraulein. For this, I had better get what I deserve.' 'A clean record amongst us. Of course. And the ability to call yourself a hero once again.' To right current wrongs and avenge a shattered past. What else could Wolf ask for? 'It will be done.'
