A New World. Chapter 2. Not So Immortal.

12 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/12/10 [On the streets of Los Angeles...] It's mid afternoon yet the alley is still dark and only a few rays of light beam down. In the back is where I am as I try to sort some answers for all of the questions swarming inside my head. Donny Diamond: Pain... It has been over a decade since I have felt you... The tone of my voice is low as I speak softly out loud to myself. When I mention pain I am of coursing speaking of how my body feels at the moment. I say decades because the last time I felt pain was before I gave my soul away. It was nothing to worry about as I knew once I hid in the darkness I would regenerate, but here I am and the pain remains. Donny Diamond: I was reborn... And at the same time I'm now mortal... I remain in the alley, mostly because I have no place else to go. If I was to go back to Dagda and try to explain this; it would fall on deaf ears. Ziek failed to mention any of this to me, or was me being reborn out of revenge the reasoning for this? Ever since that day it would seem the darkness has only blinded me instead of leading me forward. I am the only man on Earth who can say that there really is a God, but more importantly a Devil as well since I have done battle against both. Donny Diamond: For so many year's I accepted the darkness inside of me, but now I must pay... Since my rebirth I have been engulfed with rage, and the only time I felt alive was when I was punishing someone. Now I feel the pain because maybe after all of these years God has decided it is time for me to be punished. I have made the innocent people of the world become involved with my quest for revenge and all the while I knew it was wrong. If only my heart could beat without the thought of revenge, I could accept the reasons for my punishment, but instead I will continue. Donny Diamond: For every cry an angel screams I feel a little bit better inside... All this while I thought I was alone in this alley and that I was talking to myself, but I forgot you were here. My arm crossed around this old ladies neck and I can feel the beating of her heart. It increases with each second as the knife I'm holding in my right hand presses into her chin. The blood drips slowly down the blade and, soon a smile could be seen over my face if there was light, as it drips onto my hand. Donny Diamond: This is the part where you start praying for a miracle from God. Old Lady: Please God... Donny Diamond: To bad miracles don't exist! My heart is racing as fast as hers as I slowly slide the knife down towards her throat causing the blood flow to increase. Hearing her beg and cry for mercy is so thrilling, but it becomes even better when you know for a fact there is no chance God will ever save her. There are so many different names you could use to describe what I want to do to this old woman, but there isn't one to describe how good this makes me feel. Pulling the blade away for a moment gives her a bit of hope, that is, only until I turn it to face her, ready to slice her throat. "Ugh!" The knife falls from my hand and my hold on the elderly woman is loosened, which allows her to escape me as I feel something catch me around the throat. I hear her shouting thanks as she leaves the alley, I try fighting off whoever is choking me, but due to the darkness I cannot see anything. Soon I fall down onto my knees coughing my final breathes away and my eyes become heavy, forcing me down the same way all of my victims of the past have gone. [A few hours later...] My eyes begin to open, but it seems like I am in the same place as all I can see is complete darkness. No. This cannot be the same place, because I do not feel the breeze hitting my skin, in fact the only thing I feel is restraints on my wrists. My arms are held out and I'm down on my knees, exactly how I was when Ziek found me so many years ago. I know I'm not dead, I can still feel my heart beating lightly. "Excellent! He has finally awakened!" I hear the voice of a man standing not too far from me. With all of my strength I try and pull my arms towards me, and I hear the sounds of chains hitting together. As I continue my struggle for freedom I watch as some torches around me are lit. The man I heard a few moments ago stands in front of me dressed in a nice suit. I look around and see my arms are bound by chains that are bolted to the wall. He grabs my face and forces me to look up to him, looking into his eyes sends a feeling of coldness into my spine. "We have been watching you ever since Dagda re-summoned you to the world of the living. I'm sorry for the way in which you were brought here to us, but we just couldn't stand by and let that old woman be killed. That is the reason we exist, to make sure being's such as yourself are captured before you can do harm to the world. My name is Jason Nickles, and I am the leader of this organization know as The Society." So Jason Nickles is the name of the man who enslaved me here, how I cannot wait to be released so that I can snap his neck. Obviously now is not the time for this as I still bound here, for now all I can do is play along. My eyes pan around a bit taking in my new environment, but they soon snap back to Jason as he begins speaking again. Jason Nickles: I have done my research on you and it would seem that you are far different from all of the others that we have captured. You seem to be in a league of your own indeed and The Society shall use you end our crusade once and for all. Donny Diamond: Others? There is no one else that is capable of doing the things I have done. Jason Nickles: I have no choice but to agree with you there. Dagda has tried this so many times in the past, but every demon he has summoned has been less than stellar. None of them have presented to us the possibilities like you have Mr. Diamond. Donny Diamond: Possibilities? What exa... Before I can finish my sentence a whip comes lashing against my back. I scream out in pain which only caused them to do it again. The pain was unbearable and I continued to scream over and over as my eyes began to glow red in anger. On the wall the flames from the torches began to rise and dance around, and as my screams continued the room began to shake. Jason Nickles: That, that is exactly what I am talking about! Just look at what you can do when you put your mind to it. Or should I say, when I put your mind to it! I had no idea that I could still create such manifestations in the real world. When Dagda re-summoned me everything was so different, and after my recent trials where I felt pain, I believed I had really become human again. But now I see that with enough emotion I can change the world around me! I force myself to calm down from the lashings I just received and things begin to return to normal as Jason stands before me nodding. Jason Nickles: You will do The Society good indeed. Donny Diamond: You keep speaking of this Society, but you have yet to tell me exactly what it is you people actually do. Jason Nickles: The Society has been around for generations, and we have lived in the shadows just as you have. Only we work for God and we were made to make sure people like you were destroyed so that humanity could survive. It wasn't until the death of my father that we were able to change our image I guess you could say. My dad and all those before him were content with being slaves to the human race, but now that I'm in charge I see so many possibilities for us. Donny Diamond: If your job was to dispose of people like myself, how come I am chained up here? Did SurReal or Equinox put you up to this? He laughs a bit at my questions and I look to him in complete anger, knowing they did this to me. Jason Nickles: Someone seems to have a funny side I see. It's laughable to think that your "day job" as a wrestler would have anything to do with this. While I know you have no choice but to fight for Dagda or you will vanish once again, it's still funny to think we would even care about that. Donny Diamond: Then stop wasting my time and give me an answer! Jason Nickles: Indeed you do deserve an explanation at least. For generations we have watched the world become more and more violent with no hope for salvation. It would seem to us that God has taken an extended vacation and the destiny of humanity is left in our very hands. The Society believes that it is time to stop sitting on our hands and take action. We plan on using you to rebuild the world, to rebuild humanity. Donny Diamond: Then why am I here? I am not devoted to God! Jason Nickles: We tend to think of you as God's final fallen angel. While Satan rests in the depths of Hell, looking for soldiers to wage his war against God and his angel's we will use you to end the war. You will destroy the demons before he can sway them into hell, and you will destroy the angels before they enter the gates. Donny Diamond: By doing this you will become nothing better than the devil himself. Jason Nickles: That is where you are wrong. In today's world where everyone believes the devil is just a bed time story designed to scare kids, I will show them the way. In today's world where the messenger’s of God are raping children of their innocence, I will be the one to show no mercy. The Society will stand behind me in doing so, because we are the last chance the world has! Donny Diamond: I will have no part in this! Jason Nickles: Oh I believe there is a way to persuade you... Lola if you will, please take our newest employee to his new residence. He turns his back to me and a woman dressed in a white cloak is seen walking into the room. Her eyes never meet mine, as she keeps her head lowered and her fiery red hair falls over her face. The chains holding me in place suddenly drop, and I know this is my chance. I leap forward at this Lola and quickly grab her by the throat. Jason Nickles: How predictable! Did you honestly think it would be that easy? Lola is the one that captured you after all. The grasp I have on her throat weakens as she looks into my eyes. My insides begin to burn in pain as I see dark flames in her eyes; again I am forced down onto my knees. She goes behind me and pulls my wrists with her as she binds me with some sort of bracelets. Lola: Get up... As if I had much of a choice here, I climb back up to my feet. The doors open in front of me and Lola takes a few steps forward and nodding for me to follow her. This place cannot be an ordinary business building, not with all of the odd-ball things going on here. Or maybe it is ordinary to see something like this in today's world; I was dead for five years. Lola: Instead of being so judgmental from the start you should consider giving Jason a chance. It took us centuries to build the equipment needed for us to make the world into a new Utopia. But in order for that to happen, everything must remain unseen from the real world, that's why we never go onto the surface. After hearing her side of the story I guess things really didn't change that much after all. We finally come to the end of the hallway and another door slide opens. I take a couple of steps inside and Lola releases the bracelets as the door slides shut, leaving me in what would appear to be my new home for now. Turning around I grab at the bars in the door window and speak her name. Donny Diamond: Lola... Lola: What...? Donny Diamond: You know what Jason is doing is wrong; you have to stop this from happening. Lola: You tried to do the same thing once; the only difference is Jason is doing it to help others. You did it for destruction and dominance; he's doing it for salvation. You’re still a monster, we just know how to control you now... I watch as she walks off and I take a seat in the corner as I remember everything she said about me. During my life I did some horrible things, but I also did my best to make it all go away. There was no denying the fact that I really am a monster with no emotions. Dagda proved that point when he decided to have me reborn out of revenge. I have no idea how they plan on controlling me, but as I sit here in darkness once more, deep down inside I cannot wait to create havoc once again. ¤There are seven deadly sins¤ ¤I'm the eight¤ ¤Time for you to make your move¤
