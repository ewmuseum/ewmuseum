Past, Present, and Future

22 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/22/08 PAST It was just one of those days. Me, my name is James von Doom by the way, and my best friend AXEL Action were walking around Rome one day on vacation. It was a sunny day, typical for the Springtime there. Some birds are flying overhead as we walk through the central square where we are. They appear to be pigeons as we look overhead. I secretly wish that none of them will shit on our heads as we walk by. Luckily none of them does as we walk past some locals and head toward the marketplace. Once we get there, we see all kinds of shops and peddlers. I look around and notice some unsavory characters walking past wearing black robes and heading toward a dark looking church in the distance. I point them out to AXEL as we walk in that direction. He notices them too. I then notice a guy with a British hat on so I walk over and tap on his shoulder. The guy turns around and smiles. "Hello mate," he says with a grin. "What's up?" "Well," I say with a smile. "Me and my friend were wondering about that old church over there. Know anything about it?" I point in the direction of the church and the man looks over at the Gothic style cathedral. After looking only briefly, he shudders and turns back to me looking like he just got the chills. "The locals tell of that being a bloody evil place, mate," he says in a grim tone. "A lot of bad things have happened there. I mean, I've only been here for a few days and I'm already scared of that place. It gives off bad vibes, mate. I hear they call it the Church of the Unholy. I hear bad things happen there. Lots of things about human sacrifice and other horrible things. It's a church but it is not a holy place." The man turns and starts to walk the other direction. "Don't go there," he says as he walks away. "It's a poison place. Anything there is nothing but pure evil." The man heads off in the other direction as some storm clouds roll in overhead like weary travellers coming into the city from the countryside. I turn to AXEL and he seems really excited. "You're not thinking about going there, are you?" I ask a bit hesitantly but by then he's already powerwalking over there. "Shit," I mumble out before sighing and heading off to go after him. AXEL moves like a cheetah down the street and heads toward the Church while I'm hot on his heels. It's hard to keep up with my friend. He's almost turned into a spaceship being pulled into a tractor beam. By the time I catch up with AXEL, he's already up to the gates of the Church. I stop running and slowly walk over as he is mesmerized by the building in front of him. I walk to where AXEL is and stop next to him. "Uhm, AXEL," I mutter out but I don't think he hears me. "AXEL?" AXEL continues to stare at the Gothic structure as I look at him weirdly. "Yes James," he mumbles out as he continues drooling over the building. "I hear you." But I can tell that he can't really hear me. He's too busy looking at this 'Church of the Unholy' or whatever the British guy said it was. "I want to go inside," he mutters as he starts walking through the gates. I try to pull him back but it's too late. "AXEL! AXEL!" I shout out but then sigh and then follow him up to the doorway where he stops. "AXEL, what the hell are you doing?" I ask as he just continues to stare at the building like it's a pair of fake breasts. "Can't resist..." he mumbles as he heads into the doors. "AXEL, what the hell are you doing?" I ask again as he goes inside. "Are you insane? There could be savage cannibal rapists in there or something. I don't want to get shanked by some Crips or something. Come on! Back out here!" I hear something inside and get a little worried. It's dark and eerie inside the church when I peer inside through the open doorway. I decide against my better judgement to go inside. Hopefully it doesn't bite me in the ass too much in the end. * * * PRESENT This story begins in the autumn of this past year in the lonely streets of West Yellowstone, Montana. I've been thinking a lot about life here lately. Things for me have been changing for me as much as the weather has been changing here lately. Last week it rained for two hours then an hour later it was sunny before there was a windstorm and then it ended up raining again and this was all on the same day. This has been a weird couple of weeks for me. First my first real relationship ends when she tells me that she's in love with her ex-husband that used to abuse her. Then my best friend's psycho girlfriend threatened to commit suicide if he broke up with her. I also had to help with an intervention with one of my close friends after he had gotten his third DWI arrest in two years. Now I almost need one myself because of the depressed feelings that have been bundled up inside me for years and are only now starting to surface. I was just about to go out and get some more alcohol to consume since I'd already spent two whole days in the house drinking my sorrows away when the phone suddenly rings. Of course I was pessismistic and didn't want to pick up the phone. But, giving into temptation, I find myself walking over and picking up the phone. "Hello," I say into the speaker. After a moment I listen as my stepdad is calling to inform me that my mother has died in a car accident and they want me to come back to Las Vegas for the funeral. Upon finishing tell me the "wonderful" news and making me more melancholy than I already was before, he doesn't give me a chance to respond and instead hangs up the phone in my face just as I begin to speak so all I get out is "So..." I then hang up the phone and begin to think about what other horrible things are going to happen to me because this has been a wild few weeks for me. So instead of going out into the "exciting" nightlife in this shithole of a town instead I find myself packing my bags and going online to book the next flight to Vegas. Once I've done that, then I get to the packing which consists of a weeks worth of clothes, unread magazines, a 24-case of music cds along with my cd player, and my laptop case. I shut off all the lights in my house and grab the printout of my reservations from the printer before heading out the door. I then drove to the airport that was a few miles away and got on the flight out at ten pm Pacific Standard Time. Being back in Vegas was a very weird feeling for me. In all honesty, I didn't see myself coming back here after all the crazy shit that went down there before I moved. I go and grab my bag before heading outside to get a taxi and head home. Once inside the taxi, the driver asks me "Where to?" So I give him the address and we're on our way. As the taxi is driving through the darkness, I look out through the window and see nothing but lights in the distance. Most are from streetlights on the many streets of this town, others are from the casinos towering above the city streets. And yet other lights are coming fromt he various pubs, strip clubs, and gas stations that are open at this ungodly hour. After a short little drive the taxi arrived at my mom's house. I paid the driver his money for the ride and close the door. I could smell the exhaust as the taxi drove off into the darkness leaving me outside of the house. After standing outside for a minute staring off into space, I go up to the door and knock. The next day, after a good night's rest, I went to the funeral. During the long and quite boring ceremony, my eyes started to wander to another funeral in the distance. As I gaze over, I just happen to notice one of my old friends from before I left. From the looks of it, she notices me too. Once the funerals were finished, I walked over to her as she came in my direction. Once we were close enough, we both stopped and smiled at one another with two of those weird, awkward smiles that you give people when you don't know what else to do. As I look at her, she looks the same as she did back when we knew each other but different. She's Hispanic of Mexican ancestry. Her hair is still brownish in color and she still has the same smile and nice body that she had all those years ago. "Hi," I say to her with a smile. "How have you been?" "I've been good," she replies back. "I haven't seen you in ages." "You still in college?" I ask her. "I remember you wanted to get your teaching degree back when we were in school together." She smiles at me with knowing eyes and replies, "Yes I did. I've been teaching first grade now for three years." "That's great!" I exclaim before quickly calming down. "I mean, it's good that you're getting to do what you want to do." "I've seen you on the television," she says with a grin. "You mean the wrestling?" I ask with a little cocky smirk on my face. "Yeah," she replies. "You're a big celebrity now." "Well I wouldn't say that," I quickly reply, trying to be modest about it. "I saw on the news that you were the one causing all that trouble over in the UWF and then the same thing over in GLOBAL after that and then the COP thing. You're just a trouble maker, aren't you James?" I get a big, evil grin on my face. I can tell she thinks I'm some sort of bad boy and it's probably turned her on for quite some time now. "Always have been, always will be." "I know." "Oh do you?" I reply with a smirk. "Of course I do. I always liked that about you." "Why's that?" "Because you weren't like the other guys I knew. They all had that Mexican machismo about them. You didn't." "That's because I'm a white guy." "Of course." "So what are your plans for today?" "well there is the family dinner and then after that nothing." "Cool, cool." "Why? What did you have in mind?" I get the usual 'James von Doom smirk' on my face at this point. "Oh nothing, just the usual." "What's the usual?" "Just drinking, drugs, and premarital sex with some smoking and gambling thrown in for good measure." She starts laughing. "I always loved how you say things." "Figured that." "How?" "Your reactions." "Oh, ok. So what are you doing tonight?" "I thought the guys were supposed to go after the women." "Well I didn't mean it like that..." I get a good chuckle over this. "I was just joking. Nothing going on tonight other than some party some of my old friends want me to go to." She looks intrigued by this. "Can I come?" I nod my head. "Sure. I'd love to hang out with you." And that's how it all began. I didn't expect to meet my true love at a funeral. We started dating a day or two after this. I ended up getting married to her the following year. We even had a daughter named Judith before the bad times that were soon to come... * * * FUTURE In the not too distant future after the past events, World War III started up. The predicted Antichrist came and got elected as President of the United States. He soon wanted to make people and unite everyone to lull them in a false sense of security. I was there to witness all of these events. In fact I am one of the few people alive today who was living before the war started. I rememberd the days when I was trying to warn people about the antichrist and they called me crazy. They labeled me as "insane" and thought I was psychotic. That was until they were rounded up and had microchips implanted into their hands to be monitored as well as take part in his "Six-Part Six-Figure Six-year" economic plan, or as I call it now, "The Marking of the Beast." During my exploits with AXEL, I eventually saw what was going on and decided to do something about it. I soon ended up joining the Rebellion against the Antichrist. The war was a long and hard one. Our leaders kept dying in street battles with the police and the United States military. Many millions of people ended up dying because of the endless war. When the war became nuclear, that is when the world suffered the most. I'm sitting down at the campfire under the night sky. The desert sand blows in the wind all around us as the cool night breeze starts up. The young adults are sent out from their tents to take part in this portion of the rites of passage. The young women and men walk over to the campfire and sit down around it as I watch them from my seat. They are all wearing tribal garb which consists of tannish colored khaki shirts, tannish colored loose fitting pants or jeans, black boots or tennis shoes, scarves wrapped around their necks for protection from the sands when they blew too hard, and some of them wore jackets. As for myself I have on a typical Rebellion strike team leader outfit which consists of tannish colored pants, a white T-shirt, a black jacket, and black boots. I don't have on my black hat, black backpack, or bulletproof vest. I don't need those for this. This is nothing more than a campfire chat with a bunch of naive kids, nothing more, nothing less. "I remember that the world was going through some strange events before the election of 2008," I say with a sly smile, my heart growing warm from the remembering of how wonderful the world once was. "I was dating a woman starting that Spring and we were in love. I had just started my new job a few months before. But there were signs years before the elections of the troubling things to come. There was an entire city flooded after a hurricane, a bridge came crashing down for no reason, hijacked airliners slammed into buildings in the United States, tsunamis destroyed parts of Asia, weird weather was all over the United States, political upheavals were happening all over the world, and droughts and famines were happening in Africa. That was before the new President was elected. That was before the Antichrist was elected." The young adults seem to notice that I have a grim look on my face. "But I had a feeling about him before he was elected," I continue. "And everybody told me I was crazy. He seemed to prove me wrong for three years with his 666 economic plan. He also then started having microchips put in people for 'security purposes' but it was really just to track us. It was around the time that he started to end his 'false peace' that the rebellion started. I was one of the original members." "How did you get involved with them?" one of the young men asks me. I smile back at him and reply back, "They found me on the internet. I had posted a small article on my web page about my frustrations with the current regime. One of their members saw it and showed it to their leader who contacted me and I soon joined the cause. That was also around the time the regime cut down on free speech and took total control of the nation. We started attacking other nations. That is when the rebellion took full force against him. I took part in the battles of Los Angeles, Denver, and Wichita. The battle in Wichita is where I became a top rebel leader because of my strategy and tactics that helped us turn the tide. Obama also feared me because I was one of the few 'rare birds' that still lived after his extermination squads killed anyone who had predicted him being the 'antichrist' before the election. I also had dreams that kept coming true. That also made me a valuable asset to the rebellion. I would dream things and they would happen a few days or even a month later." "But the other thing that made me valuable to them was that I was a natural leader," I continue as I feel the eyes of the young adults watching me as I speak. "I could motivate the troops and eventually ended up being the number two person in the rebellion during the World War III years." "World War III?" one of the young women asks me. I smile and reply, "The war of Armageddon." "Oh, ok," I hear her reply as the rest of the young adults muttering to each other. "I was there and witnessed it happen first hand," I continue as they turn their attention back to me. "I saw the first missiles get fired toward China and Russia. They didn't see it coming. Obama denied it and blamed it on us. Luckily for us they didn't buy into his excuse and that is when the war officially began. The rebellion was on the side of the Allies. This time America was an Axis Power. I didn't like it at all, considering I was a history buff." "What are the Axis powers and the Allies?" another young man asks. I smile, knowing that the young people know have little knowledge of the history of the past as they once did. "The Allies were the good guys and the Axis powers were the villaians in both of the first two World Wars," I reply with a knowing smile. "But America was always on the side of good in the first two World Wars. In the Third World War, the one you all know as the War of Armageddon or the Great War, we were on the side of evil. I knew this. I was one of the few non-religious people involved in the rebellion. I'd been on the dark side all of my life so what made me dangerous to Obama and valuable to the revbellion was that I thought like one of the bad guys. There were others like me, of course, but none as intelligent. I was so dangerous to Obama that he tried to have me killed at least five times but it never worked." I smile just thinking about how pissed he got over that shit in some of his official press conferences. "Those were the dark days," I continue. "Before the end of the antichrist. I was there as the witness to history. They used to say that for every major event in history, there was a witness to it. In this case, it was me. My old friend Axel Action and I were leading a small rebel force onto an American military base where Obama was at. He was going to use it as a launching pad for his Final Attack on his enemies. Our force busted in there and battled his troops for hours before me and Axel were able to get inside the base. Once inside we headed for the control room. After a short skirmish with his security forces, we made it to his command room. There we killed his four Secret Service protection guards. After killing them, we then burst into the command room to confront evil itself." "What happened?" one of the young women asks me. I smile as I get near the end of the story. "Once there, he immediatley laughed in our faces and told us that we were too late. The cocky fuck was mocking us. So he presses a button and then some nuclear missiles were launched. Me and AXEL then went at it with him. Unfortunately he had a gun and shot AXEL in the head, killing him right before my eyes. I then grabbed the fucker by the throat and slamemd him into the control panel. As we fought over the gun, he kicked me low so I punched him in his big, fat ugly head in retaliation. At that point he pushed me backwards and shot me in the upper arm. I was on the wall bleeding with him about the pull the trigger when a bright light came through the ceiling. Both me and him looked up to see a ball of flame coming through the ceiling. The flame then engulfed the body of AXEL and I literally watched him awaken before my eyes!" "AXEL was possessed by our dark lord Tioneb. The antichrist was in shock when the flaming AXEL grabbed a hold of them and both he and AXEL exploded into flames. After an explosion, both were gone and I was left there by myself. They used to say before the dark days the Antichrist took over." "What was it like back then?" one of the young men asks me as the wind picks up and starts blowing sand through the ruins from whuch we currently are located. Some of the young adults cover their faces with scarves they brought with them while others bear the brazen wind that is blasting through the valley. "It was a pretty chaotic time actually," I reply with a smile. "There was a complete dumbass in charge of the country and we were in a recession, which means our economy was sucking majorly and the gas prices were sky high. And lest I for get the mortage crisis. Basically our country was going down the toilet. That spring I fell in love with my future wife too, which was great. I mentionned that earlier, if I'm not mistaken. I also got the Doomsday and Prophecies book that I used to help the Rebellion out later on as well as my Nostradamus Prophecies book as well. But to answer your question, the country was actually going pretty good and headed in the right direction before the idiot took over. Millions of people were happy and we were considered to be the greatest nation on the planet. Everything was going well until the idiot took over. Then nine eleven happened and it all went downhill from there. The Iraq War and all the other mistakes made by the idiot's regime led to the rise of the antichrist. "After the antichrist took power from the idiot, he pulled our troops out of Iraq very quickly and made two years of peace with everyone. Then he started his build up that eventually led to World War III and the state of humanity that we are in today." "AXEL Action was possessed by Lord Tioneb and they sacrificed themselves to save humanity. We are forever in their debt. With this knowledge you have been given, you can now venture forth on your quest to complete the rites of passage and do what must be done to complete your quests. Go now. To the wilderness. Some of you will not survive, others will make it back. But, no matter what happens, do your best. Survival is your only hope. Now go." The young adults then grab their things that they sat down in the sand earlier and head out into the desert wastelands alone to survive. They must stay out there for a week. Everyone must go through this. As they head out into the darkness, I put out the fire with some sand and get up from where I am sitting. As I head back to my tent, I realize that I've gone from being a battle hardened warrior to village shaman of one of the Survivor tribal bands that wander the endless wastelands. This is what has become of me. Just being able to survive the end times warranted this much needed cushy day job. Only ten thousand people, give or take, survived the day of reckoning. That is a tale in and of itself. But here I am, a Survivor. And with survival comes the one thing we have plenty of and that is hope.
