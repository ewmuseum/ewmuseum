Sometimes you meet a woman so unique that you don’t even know how to get at them. There’s just something about them that calls to you and makes you wonder and worry. This could be anything from their beauty, their smell, their smile, or their money. “So little lady,” I said as I slid next to the most beautiful woman at the bar. “Can I buy you a drink?” Now here is where I found out how special she truly was.
