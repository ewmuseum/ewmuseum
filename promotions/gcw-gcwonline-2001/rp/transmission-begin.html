Date: Do you care? There are times in your life when you realize that you've hit bottom. That you sincerely have amounted to absolutely nothing in your life. These are the moments that define who you are. You sit back in your chair you purchased at a thrift shop several years ago, that you just can't seem to let go of because you have some odd notion that it has molded itself to you, and only you, and ponder your life. Have you done anything noteworthy? What about that time you helped your next door neighbor give their dog a bath? Or the time you won that award for best...something or other at your corporate annual party. Do those things count?? You'll light up a cigarette, or open a beer. Or, perhaps you'll crack open a soda, or tear open a bag of potato chips (for the non-substance abusers in our midst) and continue mulling over your life. Or at least the parts that stick out to you. Funny thing about pondering. Generally the only time your entire life flashes before your eyes, is when something horribly tragic happens to you, or you are just about to die. Otherwise, we pick the most substantial points to think about. Your most recent relationship. Why you got fired. Why whatever horrible thing that is currently plaguing you, is happening. Whatever it is, you'll dwell on it. So much that it starts to eat away at you. It starts to become who you are. You begin a slow decent into the depths of your brain. Into an area that is usually forbidden to most, but it's where you'll stay until some ray of light appears. If it does. I have now sufficiently set a scene for you. Now it's time to put something behind that scene. Some "meat" if you will. No one has seen, nor heard from Tyson DeBough for quite some time now. He won the GCW Television Championship a while back, defeating Jay Terror in what was one of the most exciting, and noteworthy matches of the year. Everyone loved it. Everyone loved him. Then? He defended the title a few times, winning by cheating tactics here and there. And then he dropped off the face of the planet. Where did he go? What has he been doing this entire time? We saw him put up a valiant effort against Clyde Fox, only to end up face up on a mat, watching the ref pound out a three count out of the corner of his eye. And, again, there was silence. If you were expecting some sort of update from Tyson himself as to where he's been, you should just stop paying attention now. I don't have it. But I can tell you where he is, and what he's doing. Tyson DeBough is washed up. He is currently sitting in his apartment, smoking a cigarette, and watching whatever is currently on channel 12, because he can't get any other channels on his fucking TV screen. Or, perhaps he's just to goddamn lazy to try and find the remote, and he sure as hell isn't going to get up and walk to the TV itself to change the channel. There are no plans for a comeback. There are no glorious return ideas, or anything that is going to spark a huge dynamic entrance back into the wrestling world. All he plans on doing is sitting in his apartment, going to the GCW shows until his contract runs out, and then wasting away into nothing. Tyson DeBough has hit rock bottom. Why? Fuck if I know. But I know it's going to take a whole helluva lot of convincing to get him out of this. Who am I? You really don't need to know. Just believe anything I tell you and you'll be completely fine. I promise. If you don't understand, just go back to your daily lives, and stop paying any attention to me. Tyson DeBough is nothing. He is worthless. He is nothing. As are we all. If you think about it...
