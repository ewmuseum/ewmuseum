I'm Not Eva (But I Will Kill Her)

29 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 29, 2006 ---- First Passage: Hell Hath No Fury... ---- Like cattle to slaughter the backstage area moved slower than the usual free exchange the night of Rampage. The Japanese and American wrestling press swarmed through the hallways in the press area, trying to catch a quick picture or a quick interview with anyone they could. This task sounded easy, just talk to someone walking by. Of course, being pinned to the wall by hoards of people with the same mission as you made this quite a bit more difficult. This was the position of Jack Underwood. Welcome to the circus known as the press. 'Hey!' Jack yelled, trying to squirm free from the cold concrete wall strapped to his back, 'Excuse me!' Jack shoved aside a young Japanese reporter with his foot and finally found some space to move. Navigating through the press area was like trying to make your way to the front of a mosh pit. Possible, but required a little bit of creativity. Luckily for Jake, he was a smaller man... standing about 5'8' and 176 pounds. Ducking and weaving through the masses, he worked his way towards freedom. An elbow to the side of the head made him stumble a bit, but no pain, no gain. Just when his attempt to get somewhere seemed useless he got a quick peak of a blonde girl walking down the hallway. Unfortunately, it wasn't the press area and GCW Security would have his head if he left his pen. He didn't have much of a choice though, he was accomplishing nothing in the media area... and if he came back home without a single thing he'd lose his job. Desperate times called for desperate measures. Not to mention an interview with Eva Van Lorne might be just the break he'd been looking for. 'Wait!' Underwood yelled from the middle of the pack of wolves, all trying to speak over each other. There was no doubt his voice was lost in the crowd, he had to get out of there. Pushing and shoving his way through the herd, the crowd became less dense and there was starting to become light at the end of the tunnel. With a bit of a hesitation he looked at the exit, where two GCW security guards stood not letting anyone in without Press Passes. With a deep breath, Jack closed his eyes and sprinted towards that entrance. Out of it he ran, startling one of the security guards who quickly picked up his intercom and called to have him removed. Jack didn't care, it was the chance to get the interview of a lifetime. Where did Eva go? Through the clean air he ran, looking down every hallway as he sprinted past. He hoped to find her soon, he wasn't in the shape he used to be. His breathing began to stagger as he quickly went around the corner so he could lean up against the wall to regain his breath. Breaking the glass ceiling of a picky media was hard work. Security was after him, he couldn't rest for long. Back down the hallway he ran when suddenly he saw her again. Long blonde hair, dressed in black. It was Eva Van Lorne, the woman he'd been looking for. 'Eva!' He screamed, but to no avail. She continued to walk down the hallway, oblivious to the stalker-like journalist who grew ever so closer from behind. Slowing down a bit as if not to run her over, Jack began to breathe heavily from exhaustion. 'Eva!' He yelled again before putting his hand on her shoulder to grab her attention. Suddenly, she turned around and slapped his hand off of her shoulder. A pair of cerulean teal eyes glared back at him with a look that made him want to drop to his knees in shame. What had he done? He watched his career crash to a halt as her eyes narrowed looking back at him. 'Do I look like Eva Van Lorne to you?' She spoke back to him with a hint of anger and frustration in her voice. With the GCW United States Championship around her waist and baby blue tips in her blonde hair, it quickly became apparent to Mr. Underwood that this was not Eva Van Lorne. As a matter of fact, it was probably the last person in GCW that you wanted to call Eva Van Lorne. The Black Mariah crossed her arms, glaring back at Jack... watching him sweat under the pressure. He tried to speak but the words wouldn't come out. 'Are you going to answer me pig? I'm talking to you.' Vivica snarled in frustration. Jack never knew something so beautiful could become so frightened up close. His heart sank and his throat closed, trying desperately to answer but nothing came out but a series of high pitched grunts. 'Hmpf.' 'Aren't you going to apologize at least? Say 'I'm Sorry, Miss Valentine.'' Vivica said to the man who still looked much like a deer in headlight. Her teal colored eyes looked over towards a door to her left before looking back at the man. So pathetic, he looked like a small child... The Fearless Phenom was just waiting for him to wet his pants. Luckily for both of them, that never happened. 'Okay then, let me show you the way out.' Suddenly Vivica took Mr. Underwood by his shoulder and led him over to the door to her left. She reached out with her free hand and opened the door before shoving Jack inside with her foot. Slamming the door behind him Viv grumbled and stormed her way down the hall. Aggravated, she was tired of not being recognized. She was tired of being in not only Christian's shadow, but Eva's too. All she heard about was how she looked like Eva, she won the US Title like Eva, and she'd heard about all she could handle. Looking up she saw two security guards walking down the hallway. 'He's in the broom closet.' Vivica said pointing over her shoulder towards the broom closet down the hall. They quickly ran past her towards the room, but she didn't care. What did a girl have to do to get recognized for herself around here? Shadow had made a name for herself in PTC and Dawn was the new World Champion. She was just the second coming of Eva Van Lorne. There had to be some way to prove herself. But how? ---- Second Passage: ...Like A Woman Scorned ---- A low setting fog swirled low to the ground, giving the manor an almost haunting presence as a black limo traveled up the driveway towards the massive complex at the end of the road. Many memories of former glory passed through her mind as manor came into view. She brushed aside her long black hair as the vehicle came to a stop in front of he dimly lit mansion. Opening the door she stepped out into the fog. Moonlight breaking through the cloud cover glistened off her pale skin as fog swirled around her. Her eyes pierced through the surrounding darkness as she hugged a black traveling cloak closer to her at the bottom of the staircase. Click... Click... Click... The sound of her heels on concrete announced her presence in the silence of the night. A warm yellow porch light grew closer as she ascended. As long as she'd known them, she hadn't traveled to this place often. It reminded her much of the place she calls home now, it was the type of place she could get used to. Perhaps even the type of place she'd have her own home like someday. Atop the stairwell she paused before pushing a round gold button next to the large cherry wood door. Inside she could hear the chimes of her arrival. The gold doorknob turned and opened. Quickly she was greeted with a familiar face, it had been a while since she looked into his cadet blue eyes. Strangely she felt reassured to see him in person. 'Welcome to my home Lia.' He said, his face stoic as usual. She stepped inside and he took her black traveling cloak. He was dressed in the usual, a black custom made suit. It was almost as if time hadn't effected Christian Zenith at all. He looked, exactly the same. Perhaps how much things had changed in the past couple months Shadow expected that time was moving much faster than it was. Seeing so many things come and go as long as she'd been walking the earth, it was known to happen from time to time. 'So how have you been?' Lia spoke as Christian finally closed the door behind her. This was the first time she'd spoken to Zenith in person since their departure from Global Wrestling. Only a few months ago, but it seemed like much longer. 'I've been very busy.' Christian said leading Shadow through his expansive estate, 'Zenith Corporation was a wreck when I took control back in December. I'm at where I was when I left to join Global Wrestling now, but if I release control it'll fall back into the same pit it sank into in my absence. I'm not entirely sure if I'll be able to pursue my favorite hobby ever again.' 'Your favorite hobby?' Lia questioned. 'Hunting people... of course.' He said with a bit of confidence, 'Reading books can only bring so much. Everything written on paper seems like just another rip-off of another story before it. Reading people on the other hand, that's interesting. Everyone is different. It's fun to play with them. See what they do. They still surprise me sometimes to this day. It's part of the fun. Not knowing what's coming next.' 'How's Vivica?' Lia asked, inquisitively. 'Why don't you tell me.' Christian said, pausing in front of a door with a window built into it. Shadow looked inside and saw Vivica inside punching and kicking a large bag hanging from the very tall ceiling. The training facility was so extensive you couldn't see the entire room from the window. 'How long has she been in there?' Shadow asked watching Vivica strike the bag fiercely. 'Three days.' Christian responded, 'Of course she's come out to eat, sleep, and occasionally shower. That's about it. She's spent more time in that room than I have at the Zenith Corp building downtown this week. Something must've really upset her. She hasn't told me, we haven’t had much time to talk unfortunately. With myself at work and her doing... well, this. I'll leave you to speak with her, I'm afraid I have a report to finish.' 'It was nice to see you.' Lia said, extending her hand to Christian Zenith. He grabbed it firmly and shook hands with her before walking past her back towards his office. Slowly Shadow opened the door and stepped into the training facility. Never taking her eyes of Vivica she closed the door slowly behind her. The Black Mariah never turned around... she just kept punching and kicking. In the almost a year and a half she'd known Vivica, she'd never seen her quite like this before. Lia approached cautiously as Vivica screamed out with each strike of the bag. 'Vivica?' Shadow said peeking her head around so that Miss Valentine could see her. Vivica glanced over at her before throwing a strong right hand and making a solid impact on the bag. 'Hello Lia.' Vivica said before a high right kick, 'What brings you here?' 'I hadn't heard from you in a couple days.' Lia responded, 'You left Japan alone without talking to anyone. You're okay right?' 'I'm fine.' Vivica said before punching the bag and hitting it with a left knee, 'Thanks though.' 'No, you're not fine.' Shadow said, taking a seat on a bench nearby while Vivica continued to train. 'Okay, so maybe I'm a bit aggravated.' Vivica said before hitting the bag with another hard right, 'I lost my first singles match. I had that match won, and then I made a stupid mistake. Dawn beat me fair and square, and then went to win the World Title. I could've beat her. I could've been the World Champion.' 'I certainly understand what that's like.' Shadow said, remembering her own loss to Dawn McFarlane in the finals. 'It's a little different for me.' Vivica said before hitting the bag with a left cross kick, 'You lose to Dawn McFarlane you're still great. You almost never lose a match, and people respect you. I lose to Dawn McFarlane, it's another reason I'll still never be as good as Christian. So obviously, I need to get better.' 'Dawn is good.' Shadow reassured Vivica, 'Losing to Dawn is nothing to be ashamed about.' 'I'm never good enough for these fucking people.' Vivica screamed out before punching the bag again, 'I come in and win the United States Championship. No one tells me I did a good job, everyone just tells me how much I'm like Eva. I look like Eva, I won the title like Eva, I act like Eva. I'm sick and tired about hearing about EVA!' Vivica hits the punching bag with a right roundhouse before punching it. Lia can hear Vivica breathing hard from where she is sitting. How long had Vivica been at this? 'So what are you planning to do about it?' Shadow asked. 'I figured there is nothing I can do to make people be smarter.' Vivica said, 'So I'm going to do the next best thing. I'm going to destroy Eva Van Lorne. Right in the middle of the ring so everyone can see me do it. Watch the pain across her face when she's beaten so badly she can't even move without bursting into tears. I'm going to watch the looks on their faces as they have no choice but to realize who I am. Children are going to cry, her biggest fans are going to scream, and I'm going to enjoy every second of it.' 'I'll make people fucking respect me for who I am, whether they want to or not. I refuse to be nothing more than the second coming of Eva Van Lorne. I'm the first coming of Vivica J. Valentine.'
