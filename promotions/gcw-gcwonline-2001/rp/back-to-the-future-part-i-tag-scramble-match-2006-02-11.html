Date: 02/11/06 [Readers Note: If you haven't read the Foreword posted last week, I'd strongly suggest taking a look at it before embarking here.] 1. Nyquil: Not for the weak at heart -------- [Smoke, flashing lights, and all sorts of noise accompany the Delorean as it drops out of the sky, seemingly floating down to the parking lot of the Miami Target, where Team Baked sit. Having just ingested more than the reccomended dosage of Nyquil, Mooch and Bammy don't know what to think at the sight of this monolith. The door rises (80s style, bizzitch) and out walks Doc Brown. Or.. sprints. His face is completely disheveled, and sweat is pouring down his face.] Doc Brown: MOOCH! BAMMY! WE HAVE TO GO BACK TO THE FUTURE! Mooch: .. Bammy: .. Doc Brown: Yes, I said the future! [The Doc attempts to smile, hoping to garner a more enthusiastic reaction.] Doc Brown: Th.. The Future! Where all your wildest dreams may come true! [..] Doc Brown: Just get in the damn car you cunts. [Mooch and Bammy get up, heading towards the car. Mooch stops.] Mooch: Wait. Why the fuck are we getting into a Delorean that just FLEW from the sky, with a guy we don't know, who has an erradic hair style and is wearing a lab coat. Bammy: Hey! Right! FUCK OFF RAPIST FREAK! Doc Brown: I'm not a Rapist, I'm Doctor Emmet Brown! I'm here on a matter of great importance! I trust you've seen the GCW Advertisements this week. Bammy: What week is it? Doc Brown: .. Mooch: We usually don't bother checking these things. We get there, generally win, get high, go home. Doc Brown: You're in quite a match this week, gentleman! So Vital.. that it effects the future of the world! Here, have a look at this! [The over enthusiastic Doctor pulls a sheet of paper out of his lab coat. It features an ad for GCW WW 39.] Doc Brown: You're in a match with PTC Global Champion Rich Rollins! Virgil Ambrosi! Jason Dread! GCW's greatest Tag Teams! Some of the most prosperous talent in the wrestling world today! and.. Seymour Almasy.. Mooch: PFFFFFFFFFFFFT! Doc Brown: Don't let the presence of Almasy lull you into a false sense of safehood! The rest of the participants are fully qualified. Bammy: You still haven't explained what this match has to do with the future. Doc Brown: I'm getting to that. If you don't win the #1 Contendership to the Tag Team titles, the future changes dramatically. It's course is altered! It's not meant to be! I feel the only way I can convince you of the ramifications of your loss is to show you. [Doc pulls the passenger door open. Mooch and Bammy sit, unwavering.] Doc Brown: Ugh, Sonuva.. [The Doc removes his right shoe, pulling from it a back of previously rolled joints. He tosses it into the back seat of the Delorean, and is nearly knocked to the sidewalk as he tries to put his shoe back as our Hero's sprint towards the time machine, fighting eachother for position. Doc Brown closes the door and takes clambers into the drivers seat, as the Delorean shoots into the sky.] 2. The Future is.. Gay -------- 'Time Circuits Set for the Future! We travel to the year 2006.. the Date.. Feb. 20th!' [We rejoin our team in the midst of time travel. They coast through the time circuits as Doc Brown adjusts the clock in the front seat.] Mooch: Thats like less than two weeks from now. Doc Brown: Yes, but it doesn't take long for the effects of your loss to take place young man! Bammy: I know this is a bad time, but that Nyquil just hit my system, I've gotta pee. [Doc Brown glares into the back seat and checks his watch.] Doc Brown: This is a thirty second journey, hold it. Bammy: Not an option, I hope this isn't leather. [We hear a zip, and the familiar sound of.. liquid.] Doc Brown: It's pleather, I believ- WHAT IN THE WORLD ARE YOU DOING?! Bammy: Take a chill pill Doc, I'm making use of the Nyquil bottle. [Doc Brown Cringes and watched the road.. err.. time?] Doc Brown: GREAT SCOTT! That's Unsanitary! [Finishing up, Bammy screws the cap onto the bottle, rolls down the window, and promtly disposes of the evidence.] Doc Brown: WHAT DID YOU JUST DO?! THESE ARE LIVE TIME CIRCUITS YOU FOOL! YOU CAN'T THROW THINGS INTO THEM.. YOU.. RASH BUFFOON! Bammy: It's just a little Pee Pee, what bad could it do..? ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Meanwhile, in 1860.. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Abraham Lincoln: Four Score, and Sev- [We hear the familar zapping noise of something entering the Atmosphere from the Time Circuits. It causes Honest Abe to turn his head up, just in time for something to hit him. It drops to the stage, and he lifts it to his nose.] Abraham: Is this.. Urine? What the Hell? You know what? Fine. You Negro's can stay slaves for all I care, throwing pee! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Back to Present.. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Doc Brown: Your right, it's not like R. Kelly was around back then! Haha! Mooch: The R. Kelly stuff is pretty played out, Doc. [With a loud crack the Delorean emerges in it's destination of February 20th, 2006. Even though it's only a two week journey, we see a lot has changed. The cars fly, the skyscrapers are futuristic and look like something out of a 50s comic book. Several large Billboards line the Skyway, all seeming to feature Michael Jackson in a provocotive pose.] Mooch: Whats with all the Michael Jackson stuff? Doc Brown: In the future, Michael Jackson is the President of the United States. On February 15th, he managed to overthrow the Bush Administration and instate himself in the high office. He managed, on February 19th, to overthrow Congress using creative Filibusters featuring himself in a cocktail dress. The videos are horrifying. Mooch: How the hell did Michael Jackson take the presidency? Doc Brown: He was, and is, widely supported by the citizens of the United States. You see, allowing the likes of Rich Rollins or Seymour Almasy to win this tag match will in fact turn everyone.. gay. It doesn't stop there, boys. The cabinet is Outragous. Mooch: What happened there? Doc Brown: We have Secretary of State.. George Michael. Tom Cruz is Secretary of Defense. Ellen Degenerous is Jacko's Press Secretary.. and the guys from Queer Eye handle the Supreme Court. Needless to say, clothes like we're wearing have all been outlawed. Bammy: All this because we lost one match? Doc Brown: Great Scott, Yes! [The Delorean floats to the ground, and comes to a halt in an alley adjacent to a very flambouyent looking cafe.] Doc Brown: You'll need to change out of those clothes, boys. Here, I've brought some proper attire.. [Doc produces a bag full of strange clothing, handing Mooch and Bammy each a pair of Leather Pants, and a smart/casual sweater. Mooch and Bammy throw the clothes on, looking quite handsome.] Doc Brown: Now this is very important. Across the street you'll see the Cafe 80s. It's completely occupied by the gayest of the gay here. The biggest factor in the change we've had is the speech delivered to the world by a PrimeTime Central Delegate, named Harvey Blownuts. He gives this speech in approximately one hour. It's important to note that Mr. Blownuts is a recovering alchoholic, who managed to curb the habit in early January. His speech is so.. queer, that it becomes the point of no return. He was so convincing, even the remaining straight citizens saw the light and turned homosexual. Thus.. ending the world. You MUST get him to drink something, if he arrives to the meeting wasted he won't be allowed to speak, and this series of events will not happen. [The two walk towards the Cafe.] Doc Brown: But remember boys, failure is not an option! Oh.. and if someone offers you a Cleveland Steamer make note that it isn't a drink.. it's far more perverse. Don't ask how I know, be off..! 3. Cafe 80s -------- [We Rejoin our hero's as they walk towards the Cafe 80s. Each are nicely dressed, even if their hair is still a mess. They pull the door open and are instantly greeted with the sounds of Michael Jackson's 'Thriller.' Playing it cool they stroll in, looking for their mark.] 'Here you go Mr. Blownuts, you silly-goose you!' [They they don't have to look far before they find Harvey Blownuts, sitting at the bar. He's sipping a club soda, and making conversation with the bartender. Mooch and Bammy appear to be the only straight men in the room, but then again it is 80s themed. They each take a seat realizing what they'll have to do in order to fit in.] Mooch: Hey.. there, you.. sexy beast.. [He quivers from the statement, but gets Blownuts attention.] Harvey Blownuts: Mhmm, who might you be sweet cheeks? [Sitting to the back of Blownuts, Bammy heaves. He covers his mouth in time to stop the noise.] Mooch: I'm uhh.. Leanord.. Cummings.. Harvey Blownuts: Ooooh, Mr. Cummings! Would you like to go to the back and get a .. private room? Mooch: Uhh.. No! No I mean.. not.. yet? Lets have a Drink first.. I'll take a Jack and Coke. [The entire room stops and stares at him.] Mooch: I mean.. a long island iced tea? [They turn their attention back to dacing.] Mooch: What'll you have.. you.. tight assed.. morsel.. Harvey Blownuts: Just a Club, for me. [As the Bartender leaves to fill the order, Bammy begins wildly motioning his hands. Getting the Bartenders attention he scurries over, whispering.] Bammy: With a Splash of.. Uhh.. Arbor Mist. [He nods and goes to make the two drinks as Mooch and Blownuts continue their conversation. The gay Banter has turned Mooch green in the face. The slow ingestion of alchohol finally hits Blownuts system after a while, and he begins ordering harder drinks, until he's fully wasted. Sensing their work is done, Mooch and Bammy rise to their feet and run towards the exit.] Harvey Blownuts: WAIT.. WHERE'RE YOU GOIN'! I'LL GIVE YOU A CLEVELAND STEAMER! JASON HUGHES STYLE! [This only makes our hero's run faster.] 4. Back to the.. Past?! ----- [Scurrying into the alley, they rejoin Doc Brown whose sitting in the Delorean. They climb in also.] Doc Brown: Good Work, Boys! I have an edition of tommorrow's paper, and instead of giving his speech Harvey Blownuts was arrested for indecent exposure in front of Caldera Enterprises! [Bammy puts his feet on the dash in a relaxing pose, only to accidentally kick the time circuits. Doc Brown turns and slaps him.] Bammy: Hey, you never hit Michael J. Fox! Doc Brown: Michael J. Fox wasn't a fuck up like you. Now get out, I have to fix the time circuits, we only have enough Plutonium for one more journey back to the present time, so you can win that match! [The two get out.] Doc Brown: I suppose we should probably swing by my future residence, so I can get some more. It'd suck to get stranded without feul, boys! I also have the back up Delorean stowed in my garage. Hurry, get back in so we can take you to February 11th. [The two get in, and we resume our Journey through time, coming to a stop before the very same Target where everything started. Mooch and Bammy get out.] Doc Brown: I have just enough plutonium to go back to the future and see if seeing what you've seen has changed the course of the time circuits. Wait.. Dec. 28th.. 1695? Why did you have to kick the time circuits you fucking toolbox! Shit! [While trying to reset the time fixing, the Delorean begins to smoke.. and then.. BANG. It disappears. Rain begins to fall on our hero's, and a car pulls up where the Delorean sat. Out walks a man in a trenchcoat, holding an Envelope. Man: Are you Mooch and Bammy? [Team Baked nod.] Man: I'm from Western Union. We recieved a letter over 311 Years ago, when we were a small Prude-Founded shop. The letter give explicit intruction to deliver it at this date, at this time, to a Mooch and Bammy. We had a poll in the office to see if this mysterious Team Baked would actually be here. Looks like I lost, haha. [The man hands Mooch the Letter. He tears in open.] Dear Mooch and Bammy, When your retarded friend kicked the dashboard he reset the time circuits. Rather than going to the future to see if anything had changed, I was sent to the past. I've been living in the year 1695. I'd like to say comfortably, but what the hell? it's 1695! these people are savages! I've nearly been burned for Witchcraft three times, and nearly hung for missing church. You heard me, they hang people for that! I'm in hiding.. the Villagers are merciless.. and they're out for my blood. So.. if you guys could.. come on back and get me that'd be prett damn cool, since I'd rather not be killed! Just go to my house.. 1492 S. Oak Street.. the spare Delorean is in the Garage. Come to Dec. 30th 1695 at Promptly 2 AM! No one should see us, and I can be rescued. Your friend in time, Wait. Fuck you both. I hate your guts. I'm sent to show you the future, and you screw me. Lick balls, cunts. Doc Brown. To Be Continued.
