Vampyr - Chapter Two

12 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12 May 05 MAY 6TH 2005 Bran, Romania The countryside was breathtaking that was for certain. The skies were powder blue, no clouds could be seen for miles, which allowed the sun’s rays to come down as if they were coming from God himself. A cool breeze rolled in from the east keeping the heat from being too hard to handle. The birds that flew overhead made no sound except their wings flapping and echoing through sky, the wind carrying the sound to anyone who would care enough to listen. An ancient castle rested in the middle of it all almost calling to those around it, forcing you to give it it’s full attention. Tourists come here year after year to see the monuments and stand in awe and wonderment. Not only that but young couples come here for a romantic getaway. It’s a place where one can get away from all of those problems that haunt their everyday lives, and just escape into nothingness. A man can take his woman in his arms and make love to her over and over again as if perhaps they’d never get the opportunity again. Women can come to relax, kick their feet up and enjoy their man’s company. These reasons and more are the reasons that Alex Grey chose this spot for his honeymoon. He and his new wife, Wylie, were sitting on a small grassy hill wrapped up in a red blanket, staring out as the sun began falling from the heavens. Alex sat upright as Wylie laid back, falling into his strong arms. She brought her head back to that her eyes could meet Alex’s and gave him a joyous smile which Alex returned then leaned in and kissed her lips softly. Things seemed to be going right for the young couple who had seen a lot of early hardships come their way. When he was 18 Alex joined the United States Army leaving Wylie, but promised that he’d return and take her hand in marriage. Nobody gave their relationship a chance saying that it was just the “typical thing” for people going into the military to try and cling on to something back home. Rumors kept coming back to Alex as well that Wylie had been seeing other people and he was so confused on who to believe. Things turned out for the best though, rumors were crushed, liars were exposed for what they were, and love prevailed in the end. The thing about fairy tale endings is that they aren’t a part of reality. In reality things always find a way of turning and sending you on a path that you never expected. Sometimes life seems to laugh at you, poke fun and point fingers, sure it doesn’t seem fair but what can you really do about it? This was a lesson that humanity always had a hard time learning, and unfortunately for Alex and Wylie it was their turn to learn. The sun completely fell below the horizon, changing the entire look of the beautiful countryside. The once blue sky had now been covered in black, white clouds rolled in and settled around a large full moon that now dangled in the sky. A slight breeze crept into the air, sending a chill through Alex and Wylie’s bodies. Wylie shivered and nuzzled closer to her husband and Alex wrapped his arms around her tighter trying to help her stay warm inside of their blanket. Slowly but surely the wind began to pick up and the clouds began to seem to take a life of their own. Wylie and Alex decided it was about time that they got out of dodge. Wylie tossed the blanket off of them and stood on her feet. The woman was quite breath taking indeed. Her skin was a cream color that brought out her light blue eyes wonderfully. Shoulder length golden locks fell down about her shoulders which were almost completely bare from the white spaghetti strapped tank top she was wearing. Any man would be in awe at the curves this woman had as well, which were accented by a pair of skin tight black slacks. To complete the pair you could say Alex wasn’t too bad to look at himself. He wasn’t an overly tall man, standing about 6’2? and weighed close to 230-240lbs, but he was well built muscle wise. Short bleach blonde hair covered his head and a set of dull blue eyes sat inside his skull. A tight red shirt showed that he works out plenty with his outfit finished off by a pair of black jean shorts and some black tennis shoes. Alex reached down, scooping up the blanket and wrapped it up in his arms. Wylie quickly latched on to her lover’s arm and they began to walk away from their place on the hill. After some time of pushing through the wind the couple reached their vehicle, which was a 2003 white Dodge Stratus. Alex pressed a button to pop open the trunk to toss the blanket inside, after he does this the trunk is slammed shut. He fumbles around with the keys for a few moments trying to unlock the doors, just as he gets it right something stops him in his tracks. The wind howled but it wasn’t quite the same as what he had heard the whole time prior. His entire body froze and his face formed a look of confusion. Wylie was puzzled by this, maybe even growing a bit worried. Alex’s head turned slowly to the right where he looked into the night sky directly at the moon that rested above. He seemed to be in some kind of trance. To Wylie it didn’t look like he was looking at the moon but through it, seeing something that wasn’t really there. “Alex what’s wrong!”” Wylie yelled repeatedly to him but it was no use. Maybe it was her imagination but it seemed that every time she called Alex’s name the wind picked up and drowned her voice out completely. Frustrated and now certainly frightened she runs around the car to check on her husband. She stands in front of him, wrapping her hands around his forearms and shaking him slightly trying to get him to notice her even standing there. Alex stands silent and motionless at first not even acknowledging Wylie. After a few minutes of no motion what so ever his eyes finally blink and his head turns to look down at his wife, who lets out a sigh of relief. Alex’s facial expression doesn’t change though even as he looks down at his wife, which doesn’t ease her feeling of nervousness. She continued to try to reach him and couldn’t understand why he would not answer her pleas to tell her what was wrong. Now Alex turned his gaze again this time to the left, this time though he began to walk that way leaving Wylie by herself. This time she doesn’t bother trying to call out to him or get his attention but she merely follows him. What else could she do? It seemed like Alex knew where he was going, making a straight line towards a rather large hill. As they ascended the grassy hill the winds picked up, heavier and heavier they got until the howls of the wind turned into what sounded like blood curdling screams. Wylie’s hair flew all around, only some parts remained stationary and that’s because her hands were cupped to the sides of her head, covering her ears. She tried her best to block out the screams of the wind but it’s all she could hear. Her eyes slammed shut as she concentrated harder on blocking everything out, but visions of death and sickness crept into her mind. She didn’t know the people she was seeing behind her eyelids, nor did she care to find out who they were, but that didn’t change the fact that they were embedded in her mind. Alex walked effortlessly up the steep hill; it was almost as if he were floating. He didn’t bother looking back for his wife, he couldn’t something was calling to him. His eyes remained wide open, never leaving their spot right in front of him. As the wind swirled about him he heard a voice. This voice did not seem human, it was a voice that would haunt his mind forever he thought. It echoed in his mind yet it seemed he knew exactly where it was coming from. It took only a few more minutes of walking for him to reach the top and look down to where the voice was coming from. His eyes remained fixed on what was in front of him. His breathing became shallow, so shallow in fact that you would have been hard pressed to even notice that his chest was moving up and down. The only movement to come from his body was the slight twitch of his fingers, a twitch that he didn’t even notice, nor could he control. Wylie was only a few paces behind him when she crumbled to her knees, giving in to the painful screams and the visions of bloodshed. Her mind couldn’t handle what was happening, especially since it made no sense to her. Why was Alex acting so strangely? Who were these people that were being tortured in her visions and why couldn’t she block them out? Why did Alex choose Romania of all places for their honeymoon? Tears began to stream down her face as all of these emotions and questions boiled over. Her chest and back heaved up and down from the violent sobs that came from her body. Alex stood mesmerized at the sight in front of him. Although it was very dark every detail stood out in his mind. He couldn’t quite explain what it was that had pulled him in this direction, but it wasn’t finished pulling. Something was telling him to go forward, go forward and stare his fate in the eye. With everything that was flooding his mind he didn’t even realize that the wind had finally died down completely. When Alex came to this realization a look of confusion spread on his face because he still heard the screams of the wind, what he did not know is that the screams weren’t coming from the wind. Alex’s gaze turned once more back to Wylie who was cradled up, sobbing ? screaming. A force beyond his explanation kept his muscles from moving towards his new wife to give her the comfort she required. He swallowed hard and turned back to look at the awesome sight in front of him. A castle ... Castelul lui Dracula.
