The Void That Cries Through You | One | I Want Peace, I’d Whine Out

3 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/03/10 The Void That Cries Through You - Zero - Is It Bright Where You Are? was the teaser trailer. The main feature now begins. Sincewe'refeelingsoanaesthetisedinourcomfortzone Morning had broken. “Mommy!” It prodded and poked, inquisitive. “Mommy, where did it come from?” You didn’t feel the splintering point of the stick stab through your clothes, just the queasy rocking motion produced by its arrhythmic jabbing. The glory of the morning sun was absorbed through the medium of migraine as it flash-bombed your timid, freshly woken eyes. As you blinked, spasmodically, the sleep-grit which had been ground into a paste with the morning dew began to desiccate and crumble. Birds chirped and chattered, a private joke at your drowsy expense. As you groaned, a gut-churning belch rode its slipstream. A bilious belch perfumed by grain liquor and stale ash. It left an abdominal-cramping void at the pit of your stomach, a hungry black hole that tugged and crumpled your skeleton into the fetal position. “Leave them alone, Molly,” a mother urged, the morning school run having slowly chipped away at her patience. Disguised within the nagging tone was a subliminal plea, that her child shouldn’t go anywhere neat the likes of you. That you were unsavory. Suspicious. Dangerous, even. Her words then took on a crushing weight of disdainful judgment. “You don’t know where they’ve been.” I don’t know where I’ve been, your thoughts reflected. The clickity-clack of her heels on the brick path stabbed you through the temple like an ice-pick. It made you think of Leon Trotsky; a lesson taught by The Stranglers, as opposed to any Modern History professor. No more heroes, anymore, you hummed, a new-wave punk lullaby to sooth the pain. No more heroes, anymore… So, this was where Rasa had left you once it had finished with your body. It was a most inconsiderate of mental constructs, sometimes. Like an unruly child, it grasped and exploited any semblance of freedom you offered it to explore; your thoughts were becoming more and more porous. Your cheek had bedded down on a lush patch of grass; damp topsoil molded to your features. No weeds, clover or daisies. Just a sterile carpet of unblemished, emerald green. And you. Around it, a border of uniformly-spaced flowering plants; begonias, geraniums and gladiolus. A blade of grass tickled around in your nostril, antagonizing untrimmed nasal hairs. Your face gurned, a wincing duck-face forming in irritation. “You should be ashamed of yourself, young… whatever you are,” another parent condemned, shielding their child’s eyes. Every word strained to contain a twanging New York accent; her true voice rattling the bars of their prim and proper prison. “Do you think our children need to see this? This is a nice neighborhood.” Deep within the lint-clustered pocket of your hooded sweatshirt, you gave the prissy bitch the finger. Remindsmeofthesecondtimethatifollowedyouhome Right time, wrong place. Kevin Crane felt a sudden plummeting sensation as his tired head dropped. Groggy consciousness suspended on a caffeine-soaked bungee cord, his head jolted back upright, cushioned against the Ford Festiva’s threadbare headrest. His dry, aching eyes blinked, refreshing over and over until the light no longer stung them. “It’s 8 o’clock, so it’s over the Kristen for our headlines at the top of the hour…” As late night had passed the torch to early morning, the radio’s volume dial had traced the hour hand of the dusty dashboard’s clock. “Rasa, Rasa,” he sung, weary voice hushed but heavy with raw, grating twang. “Don’t lie to me. Tell me where did you sleep last night?” His teeth ground through the lyrics, a jealous temper brimming up from his rumbling stomach. Kevin Crane longed to see the face behind the mask once again, slippery and newborn in the shower tray. To deny him such a voyeuristic reward stung of betrayal in his obsession-warped mind. “Rasa, Rasa,” he seethed. “Where will you go? I’m going where the cold wind blows…” Youshowermewithlullabiesasyou'rewalkingaway Midday. Same day. Far away from home you‘d stayed. The midday sun was in mourning behind a veil of heavy graphite rain clouds; its warmth shredded by the serrated chill of the wind. Flung recklessly by the wind, delicate rain drops peppered your pale face with icy freckles. When you were beneath the asphyxiating mask of Rasa, imprisoned within in its claustrophobic suit, this world didn’t exist. It had no history; the most it had lost was a wrestling match. Its heart was as blank and black as the leather grafted onto it. You envied its solitude, sometimes. Every mangling zip closing chewed through the heartstrings connecting you to the outside world. Unbound by leather, your humanity bled out. “I hate feeling like this,” you spilled. The confession dripped from your dry, cracked lip. The rest gushed. Your eyes flinched away from a connection, slipping and shying away to chaperone your trembling words; ensuring they remained only between you and… them. Stony silence. “Since you there’s been nobody else. How could there be? It’s not like you’re someone I can just move on from, is it? I can’t exactly put it all behind me.” You received no reply. The teasing wind carrying your words off into the rustling treetops. “It’s only since you’ve been gone that…” was as far as you could get before the message from brain to tongue was hijacked and overridden. Every emotional guard deployed, only to be crushed by the torrent. “Ugh. I can’t believe you’re making me say this, but I can’t carry this about any more. I’m too tired, and it’s… it’s something - a memory? - that’s not pure anymore. If it ever was. I dunno. I’ve had to wake up every day with these words stuck in the back of my throat; like I’m swallowing my tongue.” For one fleeting second, the fire of the sun valiantly roared through the suffocating gag of the clouds, burning the chill from the air. For that fleeting second, you were both golden in its glare; then grey once more. “So I’m going to say them,” you declared, unapologetic. Finite. “And that will be that.” “I love you,” you don’t pronounce the words as much as you forge them, deep within a brimming furnace at the pit of your soul. By all rights, they should have hissed and steamed as the rain lashed across them. “I… I love you. Even more than I hate you. And I miss you. I miss you so, so much. And now I’ve said it, I can’t see you again. I can’t come back here.” A mourning, cawing chorus sounded out overhead as you stood. “Goodbye.” As your back turned, sodden moss let slip a droplet of rain; a parting tear, trickling down the lonely headstone Remindsmethatit'skillingtimeonthisfatefulday There was you, then there was him… now there‘s them. It was a train of thought which the two men had divided between themselves. “One minute they’re there,” the much larger man began. His voice was high-pitched and wheezy. A far cry from the sonic-boom delivery his stature would have suggested. Despite the stale, muggy heat which wallowed, trapped in the basement room, he wore a heavy black moleskin coat whose buttons strained to contain his stout-barrel chest. “The next minute they’re not,” the smaller man continued the curious tale of the vanishing wrestler. His words hissed, lashing off his tongue so sharply that they could almost be seen to slash through the heavy smog of his associate’s cigar smoke. Unlike his associate, the smaller man’s bronzed, leathery skin was slick with perspiration, which drizzled down through a gaping, 70’s-style collar into a thick brush of chest hair. “Nobody just disappears,” said the larger man. As he stubbed his cigar out, a blood-curdling wail lashed out from the crumpled body at his feet, who was now sporting a scorched crop-circle in his spiked, straw-colored hair. “Not unless we make them.” the smaller man concluded, his pointy nose twitching disapprovingly at the stench of singed hair and burned skin. Roleplay title and chapter headings are from The Bitter End by Placebo. Series title is taken from The Beginning is the End is the Beginning by Smashing Pumpkins. The roleplay includes lyrics from the songs No More Heroes by The Stranglers and In The Pines/Where Did You Sleep Last Night.
