The Diary of RipeFist

11 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-11-05 The Diary of RipeFist I am besieged on all sides from the various villains, scoundrels and scallywags that seek to extend their foul influence over the unsuspecting world. What perplexes me most of all is that I seem to be the only one that sees this happen. Perhaps it is simply my lot in life to be the only one who sees. If this is the case I must use the visions for good, when decency is threatened the power of Fruit-fu must be there. As I sit contemplating the road fraught with peril and other nastiness before me, I think back to the days of yore. Yore was a strange man, it was he who taught me Fruit-Fu. The days of Yore were often silent, the master had taken a vow of silence you see. Still he had mastered a delicate form of communication involving origami, of course mastered is a relative term. I have as yet failed to understand the hundred different meanings of a paper swan. I, being in a rebellious state of youth, played the villain in my first encounter with Yore. I remember it as if it was yesterday, the scent of the cherry blossoms still tease my nose. Speaking of which, my nose itches, excuse me for a moment whilst I scratch?.I have returned, the itch has been vanquished, along with his followers whom had chosen to attack my rump. As I was saying before I was so rudely interrupted, the cherry blossoms were in full bloom?.DAMN THE ITCH! Text resumes, in a different colored ink. Though I searched, scraped, and plotted for many a weary hour, I have been unable to locate the source of the dreaded infection that has caused my itch. I believe it to be located somewhere in the mountains of Tibet, however I cannot be sure. I am only certain that wherever it resides, it mocks me with it’s callow laughter. Someday, Master Itch?.someday. I believe I was speaking of Yore, and my first encounter with my mute master. At the time I was nothing but a ruffian, a brigand accosting whatever travelers had the misfortune of venturing down the road I called home. I hated myself then, reduced to such a sad state, but I had been left with no other options. I had recently been ejected from a prestigious martial arts academy, apparently chopping salami is not a proper use for the antique blades of the order. Left to my own devices, I regret to inform you the reader that I succumbed to the dark side. Hunger my only guide, I would ambush the traveler, use my admittedly limited knowledge of the martial arts, and abscond with whatever food I could carry. I tried desperately never to hurt any of the travelers, but at times it was necessary. I still seek to atone for my actions at the time. I spotted Yore, much the same way I would spot other potential victims, walking. He traveled alone, on foot. Normally I would not have gone after the man, but I was desperate, and if I could gain but a crust of bread from the man, it would be worth it. Leaping from my perch, I fell upon Yore, and twister of nail, teeth, and hair. He batted me away as if I were no more than a gnat. Instantly I recognized the calm power in the man as he stared down at me, eyes revealing nothing of his intentions. Immediately I leapt to my feet, and came at him again. This time he took a more aggressive position launching at me the most vicious and devastating assault in all of Fruit-fu. The battle was ended quickly, I could not stand against the wrath of “Monkey Grabs The Peach”, I fell unconscious from the pain. Awaking sometime later, testicles still aching from his vice-like iron grip, I found myself in a cave. Assuming I must be bound in someway, I waited, unmoving. I was certain my captor would make himself known shortly. At least I hoped he would, beyond the pain my nether regions the pressure on my bladder was slowly building, as well as that on my bowels. I feared that soon I may soil myself. Later I would kick and berate myself for not actually checking to see whether or not I was actually bound. Now, I’m extremely glad I didn’t. He appeared first as a silhouette against the light, as he stood at the mouth of the cave. I winced, awaiting what I knew must be coming. Torture, agony, these were my expectations. As much as I feared the pain, in a way I felt glad this day had finally come. I would finally pay for my crimes. He reached down towards me, and instinctively I curled into a ball to protect my boy parts . It was then I realized that I wasn’t bound in any way. At the same time I noticed he was offering me a hand up, as opposed to striking, relief washed over me completely. Unfortunately so complete was my relaxation that I forgot what I’d been holding in, and I felt a warm and smelly mess gush forth, my soon to be mentor’s nose wrinkled in disgust. He edged back then, as I felt a warmth on my cheeks as I blushed. My embarrassment was complete, not only had this man molested me into submission, but I had also befouled myself. Slowly I pulled myself up to my feet, doing my best to ignore the slowly cooling ooze that now trickled down my leg. Vainly I attempted to banish the red hue from my cheeks, I couldn’t bare to show any more weakness towards my captor. Once I was standing he continued to regard me calmly, before finally reaching into the inner folds of his attire. Pulling out a sheet of paper his fingers nimbly folded it into a shape vaguely resembling a weasel. Slowly he stretched out his arm, clearly offering it to me, as if it were some sort of prize. Confused I spoke, telling him I didn’t understand. He merely shook his head, and nodded towards the weasel I held. Pulling forth another sheet of the delicate material, he again went to work, this time crafting the first of many paper swans. He held the swan to his heart, pointing to himself with his free hand. Next he pointed towards the weasel in my hands, then towards me. I was starting to understand, albeit vaguely. I was the weasel, he was the swan, it was this distinction that would be the definition of our relationship for the coming months and years. He allowed me enough time to clean up, and offered me some spare clothing to change into. It was then the training began. This is how I met Yore, and mastered the fine arts of Fruit-fu. This is how I became Ripefist.
