No Running Away

17 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-17-06 There were times when Virgil Ambrosi missed the complacent silence that used to surround him, what seemed like eons ago. Really, it was little more than a year ago that everything around him underwent a drastic change. All of a sudden, the rather dull roots that had dug in around the Las Vegas home of the Global Arena were yanked out of the ground, and just as quickly, things began occurring that actually merited his attention. People began to loathe he, Shadow, and their comrades more viciously than ever before, and naturally, what amounted to an all-out war had erupted. He had always tried to remain aloof from the politics involved in his chosen hobby, but they came to a point, eventually, where they were too intense, too close to home for him to avoid. When GCW entered the picture, what he came to refer to simply as 'drama' followed very closely afterward. This, of course, excluded the happenings outside of the arena. The disappearance of his paramour, along with their mutual realization of their pasts, was certainly not something he'd ascribe such a pejorative term to. However, Dawn's rapidly fluctuating mood, Seymour's rapidly fluctuating devotion, and the Ascension's rapidly fluctuating existence grew tiresome quickly, though he refused to let them simply go. Dawn was, for better or worse, close to him. It was possible that he'd get the chance to pummel Seymour at some point. The Ascension was the enemy. There was no running away. He found himself becoming all the more distant from the other members of Dies Irae. Though he had little direct interaction with Vivica, and had begun to hold a grudging respect for Dawn, he found it increasingly difficult to 'bond' with them, as it were. Of course, as he was well aware, his own efforts to do so were nearly nonexistent. The only member he felt anything more than a basic, proscribed comraderie with was, of course, Shadow. However, he had noticed from the very inception of Dies Irae that she was certainly the glue that held them all together, even if it wasn't quite enough to overcome Seymour's greed. However, Seymour's various indiscretions had actually caused him to feel a bit more positive about the others. This, perhaps, was one of the only positive contributions to Virgil's career that he could ascribe to Seymour. Fleeing his enemies was intolerable, and avoiding his allies was even less productive. --- His cell phone buzzed madly in his pocket one evening, a warm night. He didn't remember where he was at the time, and assumed it didn't matter. The call coming in, however, proved to matter quite a bit. Dangerous Games? He stood in silence for a moment, his eyebrows turning downward, but the call was over before he could voice his protest. This wasn't his chance! After all, he was currently the Hardcore Champion, and that was all he needed to keep himself occupied. Why would he harbor such avarice as to actually compete for another title, much less the one that Dies Irae's fearless leader had been striving to obtain? He did not recall authorizing this, and was fairly certain that he never had. Although, he supposed that there were enough people in the event that participation was practically mandatory, so his inclusion shouldn't be particularly surprising. What would he do with the World Championship? He wasn't a leader, and had little desire to be. He hadn't begun wrestling to mimic the matches of old, sticking to rules and fair play. How he won his matches, and, depending on his whim, his success, mattered little to him. To him, there seemed to be no real point in even wasting the energy necessary to walk to the ring when his number came up. Of course, that would be positively embarrassing, not only to himself, but to Shadow as well. And wouldn't that suit the enemy's plans just perfectly? The idea that followed proved the most worthy he'd had since his silent reverie was interrupted by the shrill ring of his phone. It was an idea so obvious, that he felt slightly ashamed at its tardiness in his train of thought. In his useless irritation, he'd overlooked the most useful of options. Simply, to be a thorn in the side of every competitor who had aspirations at taking the World Championship for themselves. After all, it was Shadow who obviously deserved such an accolade. He would only be helping to ensure that there were no questionable tactics used against her. With that, his purpose was set. Dangerous Games, like all other things related to Global Championship Wrestling, would belong to Dies Irae at the end of the night.
