Date: 434444 I barely made it away, I barely escaped. I had no more reasons to be where I once came, and now I find my way here, it was not a chance of luck that I ended up here, but yet it was fate, it was devine fate that I came here. For some reason I ended up in Connecticut, all the way from Minnesota I came from, I didn't care how long I traveled, just I had one image in my head that I wanted to get out. I know what you are thinking, Lucas Quinton, what are you doing? No, no this is not Quinton. I would like to introduce myself, my name is Arthur Vandergriff. I am a drifter, a loner, yet I am drawn here, I could not see where my road was going, but I knew a few stops on the way I had to make. My hearts dragged down into the dungeon of pain, metaphorically ofcourse. I have lots of demons I need to confront on this path, yet I am here. I look at him on the TV, the closeups, my eyes brighten, I see him and I know he has a key to my past. I have no past. As I've mentioned before, I am a drifter, I thought I could get answers where I used to live, but there is no cause for me to be in one place at any one time, I need answers, I need them before it's too late. I hope and dream and wish, but these are just words, these words are not the ones who assist me in my time of need, I want to remember who I am, just like Quinton wants to retain his glory. I know him. I know a lot about people just by looking at him, he wants to revel in the glory, but he has no drive, no determination, he can't look at the wider picture. So many things to prove, but he falls short every single time. I know he can do it, I know what he has to offer, but he does not follow through, not enough. I want to help him, I want to get to know him. It's time for a meeting. * * * Rain. Nothing but rain. Lucas Quinton sits there with the fireplaces roaring, the raging inferno hits his face, bursting, beating the heat off him. He seems to be finally at peace, he doesn't care about wrestling or life in general, finally some peace has given hope to look foward to the new day ahead. The rain still pours, it beats harder and harder until finally something happens. No Power. "God Damnit..." he yells. Another good night ruined, ruined by the drumming of the rain. He wants to break things, but just doesn't know where they are. "Where the bloody hell are those flash lights?" BANG BANG BANG The beat of the door syncs up with the lighting. Lucas snaps his head towards the door. It was like out of a bad horror movie. He slowly walks towards the door, no weapon in hand, just a cup of tea, and he wouldn't dare use that against any would-be attacker. BANG BANG BANG It still would not stop. Lucas moves closer to the door, he tries to peak outside, to try and get some sort of advantage, but he cannot see anything within 2 feet of him. It was not going to be a pleasant night at all. Quietly, he turns the brass knob readying himself. The tea drops on the floor. A man lays on the floor of his home, who was this guy? What did he want? Is he dead? All these questions where racing through Lucas' head, he was panicking, his heart was beating faster and faster and faster. He quickly drags the mystery person inside and closes the door. He finally turns the man over, the suspense was killing him. The man is lying there openening his eyes very quickly and very creepily. "Nice to meet you." He coughs as he passes back out on the floor. TO BE CONTINUED............................
