Arrival in San Antonio

1 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/01/05 [ The scene opens up to an airport in San Antonio, Texas. The camera is focused in on a gate that is just being opened. People begin to flow out of the gate. Several important looking business men come out decked out in suits with briefcases. Standing out amongst these men is Bryan Marshall. Marshall gets off the plane wearing torn jeans and a black GCW t-shirt. Marshall has a carry on bag in hand and heads off into no particular direction. Marshall continues to walk through the airport with his carry on bag draped over his shoulder. He gets looks from several wrestling fans as he walks towards the exit. Once Marshall gets outside into the sunlight he winces and puts his sunglasses on. Waving at Marshall is a familiar face in the GCW . Barry Goldstein. Marshall walks over to the man not knowing exactly who he is.] Bryan Marshall: You waving at me? Barry Goldstein: Indeed I was. My name is Barry Goldstein, I was sent here by the GCW to go to your hotel with you. On the way I was hoping to get a few words with you regarding to your status here with the GCW and your first match. The Battle Royal for the Television title. Bryan Marshall: Uh, yea whatever you wanna? do. I don’t even know where I am going. I just got out of the hosp?.the last hotel I was in and took a cab to the airport in Providence. I didn’t get any instructions or nothing. Barry Goldstein: Well follow me and we can talk about everything on the way to the hotel. [ Marshall follows Goldstein. There is a long black limousine and a yellow taxi cab. Marshall walks up to the cab and Goldstein gives Marshall a confused look. Marshall looks back at Goldstein awkwardly.] Bryan Marshall: You got a problem Goldy locks? Barry Goldstein: No,it’s just were in the limo, not the cab. Mr. Caldera wants to make sure his stars are comfortable. Bryan Marshall: Well that is a switch. You make sure you tell Mr. Caldera thank you. I could get used to this special treatment. Barry Goldstein: He does this for all of the guys in the GCW. This isn’t special treatment. [ The chauffer gets out of the limousine and opens the door for Bryan Marshall to get in. Marshall throws his duffle bag into the back seat and gets into the limo. Barry Goldstein follows with a GCW cameraman. The chauffer shuts the door and gets into the driver seat as the limo slowly drives off and we are taken inside of the limo.] Barry Goldstein: Bryan, first let me welcome you to the GCW. How do you like it so far? Bryan Marshall: Well, I haven’t had much time to experience anything really. So far all that has happened is the first class ticket on the plane which I did love. This beautiful limousine which is great. And now here I am talking to a guy who I don’t even know if he really is with the GCW.How do I know your not some crazy fan? Barry Goldstein: I assure you I am not. Bryan Marshall: That’s good enough for me. I have also experienced the words of Ryan Stephens. “Scandalous” Ryan Stephens that is. I have an understanding that the self proclaimed smartest man in wrestling doesn’t like me all to much. It’s to bad that this is just the start of everything I have to say about the GCW and winning the TV title. Action speak louder then words and he is going to hear me loud and clear tomorrow night at World wide. Barry Goldstein: Well you have been doing a lot of talking for someone who just entered the GCW. I am sure you are a well accomplished wrestler but in the GCW your accomplishments are few. In fact you have done nothing here. But You will be looking to change all that in just a day as you step into the ring tomorrow night. Bryan Marshall: Well Barry, it’s funny you talk about my accomplishments here in the GCW because I have done some research. I don’t see anything that Ryan Stephens has done. I have made a few phone calls and it appears to me that Ryan Stephens isn’t as good as Ryan Stephens thinks he is. I will prove that tomorrow night when I go out of my way to throw his ass up and over the top rope. He will be eliminated by me. [ Marshall looks into the camera.] You hear me boy? Your losing this thing all because of the man you see before you! Barry Goldstein: Ryan Stephens made it clear he doesn’t like you and you have had nothing but bad things to say about him. What about some of the other competitors in this Battle Royal? There are eight other competitors. Bryan Marshall: I wish I did have some more to say about the other eight in this thing. But truth is I don’t know much about them. I don’t know where they are from, I don’t know what they are about. But I know this. I know they are not as hungry as this man is. They don’t have the desire to become TV champion like I have. Barry, did I tell you the first title I ever won in my wrestling career was a Television title? Barry Goldstein: No you didn’t but was it the GCW TV Title? Bryan Marshall: No, but still it was a TV title. Barry Goldstein: What are you getting at? Bryan Marshall: I am getting at me becoming the GCW TV champion! That’s what I am getting at! Stephens thinks I am spewing from my mouth but what I say he better pay close attention too! Because if he wants any shot at winning the Battle Royal he is going to have to avoid me at all costs! I don’t know who he thinks he is messing with. He thinks I am going to have my ass handed to me week in and week out around here. Well I Have had my ass kicked plenty of time. I am past that stage in my career. But I think you still have a few ass kicking’s coming you way Mr. Scandalous. Ryan, if your more concerned wit this Borealis character or the Vanlornes, well then that’s your first mistake! If your not even worried about me, you won’t even see your elimination coming! So I am doing something I usually don’t do and that is warning your ass. Barry Goldstein: Don’t you think maybe you should be worrying about the Vanlornes or maybe Borealis? I mean these are talented individuals you are stepping into the ring with. They are going to be out there for a reason. Mr. Caldera knows what he is doing, that is why the GCW is so successful. He hand picked the guys in the battle royal and girls for that matter. Bryan Marshall: Well I realize that Mr. Caldera knows what he is doing. I am not a stupid man! I may be a little crazy but I know this match isn’t going to be easy. I am more then ready to do whatever it takes to win this match. The smart thing to do would be not even show up until it is done to the last few. But I don’t run that way. I want to fight for the title, I want to prove to everyone that I am not just some rookie talking smack trying to make myself appear better then I really am. Barry Goldstein: Let’s go back to Ryan Stephens for a moment. Bryan Marshall: If we have to. Barry Goldstein: Ryan may very well be the smartest man in wrestling. He is successful in and out of the rin? Bryan Marshall: I KNOW! I know all of that. I wouldn’t pick a fight with someone that I have not done proper research on! I know that this guy used to be all about wrestling. I know he used to have the heart of a lion! But Barry, that isn’t the case any longer! He is more concerned with his business aspects. He is more concerned with making money then stepping into the ring and winning a championship title. Which is the reason I am here, and should be the reason everyone is here. The GCW may be one of the most respected federations ever , but the fact is the GCW is bridged off of a business. Everyone that I have ran into around here seems to be more concerned with business then wrestling. Where I come from, business is wrestling! Barry Goldstein: Ryan Stephens may be smarter then all of us. He has a career to fall back on just incase he is injured in this sport. Let’s face it injuries happen all the time. That is the nature of the sport. Ryan is already a successful person. So he must have love for the sport. That is the reason he is still wrestling. He has money, there is no need for him to continue in this sport. Bryan Marshall: This is true. Very true. Before people misunderstand me, which seems to happen a lot. Me say that I respect Stephens. I respect anyone who has the balls enough to come out and wrestle week in and week out. But I just honestly don’t see how he could beat me when he has all these distractions going on around him. One of them being his ex-wife. You see while I am focused one hundred and ten percent on this battle royal, it appears to me that Stephens has other things on his mind. Barry Goldstein: He is obviously concerned about the fact he has to wrestle and prove himself for the first time in quite awhile. Bryan Marshall: We all are. This isn’t my first trip around the block either Goldstein. I have been in several different federations in my 12 year career. I started out in this business when I was just eighteen years of age. I didn’t know what I was getting myself into. I got beatings so bad I never knew if I was coming or going. But I sucked it up and took by beatings, did my job and put the guys that I was suppose to put over. Somewhere in between getting my ass kicked and stealing a victory or two, I did the dumbest thing I have ever done and I fell in love with the sport of professional wrestling. Just like any relationship we have had our share of up’s and down’s. Good times and bad. We have been on again and off again but this time around we are on. I feel like I am in the best shape of my career. Barry Goldstein: That’s good to hear because now a days, 30 years of age is considered to be the downside of a career. With most guys winning championship titles in their early 20’s. Bryan Marshall: Ah yes, I know how that guys. It makes me crazy to think that guys are being given the opportunity to win these respected championships at such a young age. They don’t deserve it. They are nothing, they are scum. They are at the bottom of a food chain and should be looking up at just about everyone else in the company. But somehow, just because they have the physiques of roman gods they are allowed to carry a world championship. Even if they won the title unjustified. I know this is something Ryan and I would agree upon seeing that he will be turning 33 years of age this August. I respect that. Barry Goldstein: Both you and Ryan appear to be in good shape. Both fast and powerful. It if came down to you two at the end then it should be very interesting. I don’t know who I would put my money on. Bryan Marshall: If you wanted to win you would put it on me. Barry Goldstein: Well I don’t think I will actually be placing any money on anything. For fear that I may lose my job. So I am just going to stick with being neutral on this. Bryan Marshall: No balls huh? You can’t pick between two guys. I am not even asking you to select one of the ten. Between Ryan and I. You can’t pick a winner. That upsets me. Barry Goldstein: I didn’t mean to offend you?” Bryan Marshall: NO ONE EVER DOES! BUT THEY ALWAYS DO! Where the hell is this god damn hotel!” Barry Goldstein: Just on the left here?..But I am going to get out now?.good luck to you tomorrow! [ Goldstein bangs on the window as the chauffer lowers the glass. Goldstein asks the limo stops and rushes out of the limo as Marshall begins to rock back and forth slightly and becomes agitated.]
