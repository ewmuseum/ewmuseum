Date: 11-3-06 Rei-Rei's Automotive Tokyo, Japan October 21st, 2006 3:45PM 'Yo, Rei-Rei!' The deep, male voice called out over the noise of the mid-sized automotive shop. Decorated to the point of almost looking more like a club than an mechanic's wallowing-hole, Rei-Rei's was pretty much the 'kickin' ' place for the younger demographic of racers and grease-monkeys. Several of the bays were occupied, a few with cars, and a decent amount with smaller street bikes. 'What ya want, Tso,' the loud, but sensual voice of Reivyn Mikona, or 'Rei-Rei' called back in question.' I'm busy!' 'Tso' began down the few steps seperating the main shop and the workbays, his leather cargo pants nearly catching on a few hooks as he circled the flame-scarred street bike, formerly known as a Kawasaki. Pushing his blonde-dyed hair away from his eyes, he blinked, staring down at the coal-mopped Reivyn, who sat close to the bike, one foot situated near the front tire, and the other near the rear tire, a true display of the length of the young woman's powerful, bare legs. 'Damn girl,' Tso exclaimed! 'Don't be sitting like that, I don't want a heart attack before I'm thirty!' 'Keep talkin' smack about my legs every time you see me, dude, and I'm gonna kick ya...' Tso laughed in response to the casual joke, kneeling down to watch Rei pry off at a salvagable part of the bikes engine. 'You go mad injecting that shit into your legs, you'd break my scrawny ass, girl. Think I'd rather have them squeeze the life out of me.' 'Rai says no,' she casually, and dryly responded. 'And the winstrol's legal, prescription drugs, baby.' But Tso just shrugged, ignoring the prescription remark, 'Yeah yeah, I know. He's got you keeping Swedish-Ass Anders happy, rival turf and all that shit. Dude's a cheeseburger though, you'll see.' ''Course he is, but he's rich, and making us all richer. Besides, he's a pretty wild fu---' 'Lo called!' Tso quickly cut her off, not really caring to go into further detail and choosing to drop the subject for the original. Reivyn just grinned. She knew it'd change the subject. She loved mixing business and pleasure, but Tso was a good guy, and doing the work she did at times, she didn't need to fall in love with someone she may potentially have to set up for a fall one day. Pulling a blackened hunk of aluminum from the engine, she mumbled a curse and tossed it aside, adjusting her glasses with a greasy finger and a nod. 'What's she need?' 'Well, in her words, she said to 'get your Afro-Injun ass to the race tonight, cos' The Chinaman's been making static and Rai wants 'em learned.' Said she'd feel safer with you at her back.' Rei just rolled her eyes, 'Dude needs to stop being a punk and be glad we don't laugh his ass out of there every week.' 'It's all about potential,' Tso answered back. 'I remember someone we could have laughed out a few years ago, and now she's drawing a legit half-million American yearly, on top of other things.' Reivyn quirked her lips into a smile and glanced sidelong at Tso. He was more than right. Once The X had ended, and Rei was good and addicted on the Big-O(1), the Saikyo could have left her in the gutter. Instead, though, they helped her through it, put her to work, and now, a few years later, she's respected pretty much like family. She nodded a few times and reached up, streaking a finger-width of grease across his cheek. 'It's in the blood, kid. You give an Indian a strip of land and we'll turn it into a cash-cow!' 'Hah!' Tso laughed in response and grabbed her hand, helping the tall woman hop to her feet. 'So what happened to this one?' Tso pointed at the bike with a free hand, keeping his hold on hers as long as possible. Rei didn't seem to care, and just kept holding for the hell of it. 'Wannabe fixer thought he was gonna dump some weight and grab another mile an hour or some stupid idea. All he did was leave some bare wires crossed and smoke his junk on the highway. Insurance isn't having it, so I dropped 'em a hundred to take it for spare parts. Little jerk got the better deal, it seems.' 'Win some, lose some,' Tso casually responded. 'Want me to close up?' Rei scratched her shoulder, adding yet another smudge to the tight,g rease-stained white shirt she wore. Turning and glancing over the shop, she pursed her lips and shrugged. 'Keep it up as long as ya want, dude. I'll pay ya for however long---' Reivyn suddenly paused, her emerald-gaze trailing up to the wall-mounted TV at the back of the shop. On it, and ESPN special, detailing profiles of several wrestling promotions across the world with some current news on their shows. Watching a profile of several stars in the company roll by, she tugs her hand free of Tso's, folding her arms across her chest. 'Fuck me,' she quietly spoke, prompting Tso to step forward in a comical fashion. 'I'll start the shower!' Reivyn didn't even respond to the remark she'd normally laugh at. A slow scowl crept across her lips, and she soon turned, just casually walking past Tso, and up into her living quarters.... 'The Hellhole' 10:26PM 'Ow! Hey! ......Damn, them legs too sexy to be hurtin' like that!' 'Stop acting like you own Japan, Chinaman, people you're getting in with don't play, and you about to find out! Now shut it, punk, I'm on the phone.' Reivyn growled lightly from where she stood at the side of the road, a small ways out of the city and at the bottom of a hill. Dressed in a tight, low-cut brown-leather vest, black, 2-finger gloves, and tight, bell-bottomed jeans, custom fit to the length of her legs, she pressed her booted heel down on the neck of the man speaking with her; a rather small Chinese man of about twenty years. He squirmed a bit and tried to rise, but she just applied more force, easily keeping him pinned down with the powerful limb. finally, he gave up, letting her finish her phone conversation while just laying there patiently, not wanting to make her any more upset. 'So yeah, Lo,' she spoke with a slight slur, lifting a bottle of beer to her lips to take a drink. 'I'm feeling a bit... Yeah, I got the little punk here. Just holding him for ya, don't really feel up to the kick tonight, ya know?' She paused for a long moment, sucking at the bottle as a voice ran at a million miles an hour on the other side. 'Yeah, I talked to Rai on the way here, he's gonna have Tso watch over the shop while I'm out. I need to do this, you know; I miss it. Kinda miss home in the states and just gotta get it out of me. I won't be gone long, just some business to take care of - personal stuff with a certain company.' She pulled her ear away for a moment, cringing. 'Hell no, I ain't goin' back to that life, Lo! I haven't even looked back to that shop since y'all drug me out her from The X.' The kid under her boot sighed and rolled his eyes, starting to speak, but quickly stopped as the boot applied more pressure. 'Hell yes, we'll party it up tonight then! Spyder's here and alone, and I plan to abuse that dude before I leave. But try to hurry, 'kay? I think chinaman's about to cry on my new boots. Yeah, catcha then.' Reivyn clicked the phone shut and glanced down at Chinaman, sighing softly. GCW Headquarters United States of America October 23rd, 2006 12:56 PM Reivyn stared up at the well-kept building as she kicked down the stand to her American Ironhorse brand Texas Chopper. Pushing her shades down with one hand as she slipped to her feet, she adjusted the belt to her chaps with the other. 'Nice one, kid, let's go do this...' She dug into her saddlebags and came out with a small bag; contents being a few demo tapes and paperwork that one might need for her intent. Tucking away the keys to the bike in her jacket pocket, she slid the shoulder-strap of the bag over her shoulder and assesed her own appearance. Deciding herself sexy enough, she started towards the building, a confident bounce in her steps.
