The emotional journey

28 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/28/10 Truth Will Triumph. When gossips' talk and traitor's scheming, And jealous fiends on evil bent, With basest vices unredeeming, Brand the names of the innocent; When hope's bright sun seems nearly setting, The future dim and cloudy lies, And you mere tired of life are getting, And tears of sorrow dim the eyes; Cheer up, take courage, do not falter, Truth will triumph by and by, Time all evil things will alter, Vice and wickedness must die. __Colfax Burgoyne Harman ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Caribou , Maine. Sunday Morning..... Watching the white lines of the highway go by as if they were flashes of light from a distant future. Looking out beyond the road you see the pine trees of the northeast covered in snow. The fresh snow still falls as a blizzard warning is in effect. Peering out the window Shaman stares, his eyes focused on nothing, as his mind is wrought with turmoil and deep thought. Turning his gaze downwards, he quietly pokes at the rubber seal surrounding the window. Gazing around the huge RV with interest, Observing its plush comforts which was supplied to them since the plane was grounded due to weather. Shrugging he turns his attention back to the weather outside as the snow falls heavier. He reaches down suddenly and unzips his backpack and pulls out his laptop. Placing it on the table in front of himself. Turning it on he listens to the groans and whines of its electronic innards coming to life as the hard drive whizzes in its wild frenzy to boot up. Casually he yanks a mouse out of the pack along with the power cord, Plugging them up he looks at the screen waiting. As the machine finishes its starting process, he simply stares at the screen as he types in his password. Browsing the screen slowly he clicks on a file that reads diary. To himself this is a collection of his thoughts over the past few weeks. What am I going to write about, being stuck on a damn RV in a snow storm that could bury most states. Problem is if it wasn't for the GCW schedulers I would have been out of here seven hours before all of this mess hit, But no some fucking reject in an office says “oops sorry we did not think of the weather implications for your travel.” How in the hell am I supposed to travel and be happy while riding on the road during a damn blizzard. Opening the diary section he begins to set a new date. Stopping for a moment he looks at the file. “No more thinking just writing for a bit, I gotta clear my head by putting my thoughts down somewhere.” He stretches his arms and cracks his fingers as he begins to type. Snow storm Maine day 1..... Not having done this in awhile, I think it is time to put more of my thoughts to file. Coming back from an autograph signing the small airport grounded all flights back to Portland. So the corporate idiots at GCW thought it was kind of them to send an RV for me to continue my trip. Cooped up in there warm little offices.....damn it getting off track here, gotta clear my mind fully. Ok my next scheduled opponent is some kid named Dynasty, sounds like some bad card game. Hey wasn't Dynasty some old 80's soap opera drama? Nevermind that, anyways the kid is supposed to be defeating me in a full on match. Guess what the usual is observed, he is expected to win and me the underdog as usual. Somethings just don't change do they. So why am I angry and what the hell do I do about it? Truth is I think I have had this answer all along. I just do what I need to do. I have to worry about this Dynasty kid and then figure out how to fend off and beat three other guys. Unless for some freak reason Cruz beats Mayhem. On with this Anarchy Rules schtick, GBJ dropped something interesting in our laps. It is going to be an all out fight in this matter. So here are my thoughts on the matters at hand... Cruz possibly winning against Mayhem for some ungodly miracle, I have to include him in my Anarchy Rules plan and introduce him to the sermon of pain. Dynasty focusing on an opponent for once and not some two dollar whore down the street. Beating him and focusing my attention on the three other ass jacks named Diamond, Tracer, and Mayhem. Gives me a lot to think about......Just give me a damn headache too..... 12 Hours later Wells, Maine rest stop on the Maine Turnpike..... Tromping through the snow back towards the RV Shaman carries a large Burger King bag and a drink in his hand. Opening the RV's door he enters and looks around at the other guys. Shaking his head he kicks the snow off his boots, turning he pulls the door shut and heads over to where his seat is and his laptop sits. Placing the food down he looks over at the driver. “Ok so let me get this straight, you have never driven in this stuff and you're scared? Come on man you drove tractor trailer trucks and went over the Rockies in blizzards and this freaks you out? Fucking weak man.” Disgusted Shaman sets the drink down in a harder than usual manner. He turns towards the driver as he speaks. “Look man I drove us twelve hours through blizzard conditions on freaking backwoods country roads to get us on 95, this thing here doesn't handle like a Rig.” Before the man speaks up again Shaman cuts him off. “Settle your ass down king of the hill, before I knock your ass out. I did not ask you to drive this RV, If I had my way I'd have made the plane fly regardless of the weather.” Sitting down he opens his Whopper and begins to eat it. He begins to tap on the keyboard with one finger as he continues. Snow storm day 1, 12 hours in...... This fucking weather is beginning to make everyone testy, I am going to hurt someone before this trip is through. The driver looks to be the likeliest target. I am here eating Burger King food rather than eating a home cooked meal from my wife. Stuck on an RV with a couple of guys who could probably use proper hygiene and fresh clothes, Wonder if I should tell them there is a shower in the rest area. I tell you though, I have been having some weird dreams of late, dreams of struggling and finally breaking loose. I feel their bones breaking in my hands as I grab them by the throats , arms, legs. It is like they are made of weak Popsicle sticks. I keep struggling to try and figure this out, somehow it still seems lost to me. It is almost as if it is at my fingertips and I cannot grab it. Invisible to the touch in my minds eye. Am I losing it or is there much more to this, I am tired and weary from my own struggles, do I end them or is there more meaning to this? Raising his head he looks at the driver who climbs back to the front drivers seat and starts the RV. He turns and looks back towards everyone. “Ok I am going to drive us some more, Thinking about things we should do ok, because the Turnpike Authority says the turnpike isn't closed yet and should be clear for awhile. If it gets too bad we are gonna stop.” He turns and puts the RV in gear and begins driving towards the on ramp. Rubbing his chin Shaman is satisfied with this turn of events. He smiles as he closes the top of the laptop and lays his head against the cool window. He speaks for a moment as he closes his eyes. “I am going to try and sleep for a little while. Leave me alone or you will suffer pain unimaginable.” Drifting off to sleep from the vibrations of the RV traveling through snow and drifts. Several hours later, Somewhere in a deep dream............... Three men stand within a blood filled lake with no shores, up to their waists they stand looking at each other. Bryan Mayhem, Donny Diamond, and Dorn Tracer stand observing one another unmoving. The eyes simply stare unmoving. Wading through the blood comes Shaman towards them. As he trods closer they seem to constantly move further away from him. He quickens his pace, but the blood seems as thick as molasses. It gives very little as he strains and struggles to find his way to them in an expedient manner. He stops suddenly and raises his blood covered hands, He roars as no sound comes from his throat, the frustration upon his face is evident. Bringing his hands to his throat he feels confused as he can no longer make a sound. He struggles with himself to make a sound as the blood in front of him begins to bubble. Watching he backs away slightly as he tries to escape to a safe distance, but the blood is restricting him slowing his movements. The top of someones head is seen with jet black hair rising, The blood blocks the face as it flows thickly over it. Whoever this man or being is he is not revealed very well as he rises up dripping blood from his arms and face. He opens his eyes, stark white compared to the blood surrounding them. A deep raspy voice speaks as Shaman suddenly gazes in shocked recognition of who he is seeing. “Glad you recognize me this time, I could not stand to be in your dreams recently, you are weak and pathetic, nothing about you showed any type of strength. You were fighting back everything I am.” Wiping his face, he reveals himself to be a mirrored image of Shaman. The anger and rage upon his face is evident. Pointing an accusing finger at Shaman the blood covered Shaman smiles. “There was a time you'd embrace who you are and go above and beyond. Now look at you beaten by a Mime, a hack, and a Religious Cult leader.” Punching a fist down into the blood Shaman winces a bit as he struggles to speak. The blood covered Shaman looks at him and shakes his head slightly in pity. “No, no, no you are going to listen to me now. I will be your voice of reason. Vengeance has always been a cruel mistress, she is a hard taskmaster you have ignored for a longtime.” The blood covered version waves his hand behind himself, the three men loom larger than life over them looking down upon them both. He narrows his eyes at Shaman. “You have to fight all three of them until NC-17 and you are not even getting worked up or releasing me. Why have you become so pathetic of a man, I am almost embarrassed to be part of you. Remember the days when you'd just cut loose and make them all suffer?” Smiling through the blood caked visage he reaches back and strikes Shaman. Shaman turns his head from the force of the blow. He turns his eyes back upon his bloodied counter part with no emotion. “Look at them, they look down upon you and watch you struggle with nothing but disdain and disrespect.” Pointing directly at Donny Diamond as he shakes with seething rage. He backhands Shaman once again, but this time he turns his head back as the muscle below his right eye begins to twitch. Finally Shaman lashes out grabbing the throat of his Bloodied twin. His eyes narrow looking at him. “I have grown beyond you now you pathetic fucking shadow. They will suffer through the Sermon of Pain.” Pulling his pale imitation he pulls it within himself, absorbing the essence of rage and anger as if it was needed for him to become whole once again. Turning he stands staring eye to eye with the other three. Breathing hard as he feeds off the rage, murderous intent in his eyes as he lets out a massive bestial roar looking at them. Monday Morning 4 am Upstate Vermont....150 miles outside Burlington....... “RRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHH!!!” Shaman bolts upright clenching his fists ready for a fight. Everyone within the RV dives away from him as the driver swerves on the highway suddenly spooked. “Wha...what happened? Everyone ok?” He looks up in the mirror trying to see what was going on. Turning his gaze Shaman looks at the driver and just stares. He leans back and looks out the window as snow still falls. “How far away are we?” He observes the drivers reaction as he looks over to the right at a sign. Clearing his throat the driver responds. “About 150 miles or so.” “Good shut up till we get there.” He leans back and gets comfortable again, as he suddenly speaks up once again. “By the way you three sleep in the RV, I sleep in the house, it is my grandmothers house and nobody, but myself will stay in it since she is now gone.” He leans back a bit as he opens the lap top once again. Snow storm day 2, Closer to reality than ever before..... I think I have finally awoken from the nightmare of being what everyone expects of me. I will be myself from now on, I am going to release the inner rage now, no more trying to keep it pushed down deep inside. Since coming to GCW I have hidden my rage and anger that have made me one of the most brutal men to step foot into the extreme. It was as if I was chasing the guys at the top and not being myself. Did I have to reinvent myself to play on a different level? No, I reinvented myself to become something more from which I may have failed. I needed to stay loyal to who I was. Now the simple question is I am not simply a man of anger and rage. I want to remember who I was, perhaps it is time to find that man once again. Maybe this man was never gone in the first place. I haven't felt alive for several years now, It is hard to realize I came to GCW in 2006, wide eyed full of dreams of change and actually believing I came to the promised land. For awhile I actually made a huge impact, gained the tag team belts with Mayhem, beat him for the Television title before that, became a two time GCW US Title champ. What have I accomplished since? Nothing, but winning the tag team title with Samuelsson. Question is I feel empty now, like I need to come full circle and get my hands on the Hardcore title from Mayhem, Strip it from his hands like a lolli pop from a toddler. Just how do I reclaim that all consuming fire I once had? Sitting back he looks outside as the RV pulls slowly up the snow filled drive to his grandmothers empty home. Where she once dwelled when she was alive. He watches the trees pass by as he remembers many things from his childhood here. He smiles softly for a moment as he heads to the door as the RV stops. He opens the door and steps out into the deep snow and walks to the house. Once upon the huge wrap around porch he looks over at the front porch swing as he remembers something she once said to him when he was younger. He watches as his memory literally places images of himself and his grandmother on the front porch swing as she spoke to him. Kissing young Andrew on the forehead she causes the swing to rock back and forth. She smiles with soft loving smile. She looks out down the drive as he looks up at her. “Memmierre when will Peppierre be home from work?” She looks down on him with loving eyes of dark silvery blue as she respond. “ Your Peppierre will be home soon, he has to work late this evening.” She stops and takes a deep breath as she speaks more, this time her words are more serious and thoughtful. “Andrew remember you cannot take anything for granted in this world, You need to always fight for what you have. In the end it is all you really have. Gaining of wealth and materials means nothing if you lose your fighting spirit.” She reaches over taking his hand into her as she smiles. “Never forget who you are either, because once you do you will find it hard to comeback again.” As the memory fades a lone tear falls down his cheek. He turns to the door, reaching down he slowly unlocks it and steps inside looking around. Closing his eyes, he can still smell the feint signs of her cooking in the house. The spices she used still hung in the air as if they were raising one last effort in tribute to the woman who used them. He walks to the living room after he closes the door and sits in his grandmothers chair. Looking out at the snow falling outside he sighs and slouches down into the recliner that he grew up sitting in. Clearing his throat he smiles slightly at the memories, suddenly he remembers something very important to himself. Reaching his hand over to the night stand he lifts her rosary beads and smiles as he kisses them. “Memmierre I have comeback, I am finally home this time.” Closing his eyes as tears slowly stream down them, as for this time his emotional journey has finally come full circle.
