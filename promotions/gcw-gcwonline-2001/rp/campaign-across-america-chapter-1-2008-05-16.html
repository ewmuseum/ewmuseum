Campaign Across America, Chapter 1

16 May 2008

"Nothing in the world is more dangerous than a sincere ignorance and conscientious stupidity." Prelude Coming off a tremendous upset to Andy Murray in the Rampage finals, Chris Bagwell had been at a loss of words. His momentum building up to that highly regarded bout made it seem as though he couldn't be stopped. Bagwell had, in only a few short months, launched his career from a nobody into the leader of perhaps the fasted rising stable in GCW history and the single most hated man in all the Primetime Circuit. Andy Murray managed to construct a brick wall to put a stop to Chris Bagwell. But the wall didn't stop Bagwell -- it only just slowed him down. After pouring his heart and soul into his psychologist, Chris ended his relationship with her in a manner of seduction. He left her office after his final appointment, displaying clearly his old ways and his undeniable ability to disrepect a woman. Since his Rampage loss, Bagwell has put together a campaign to boycott all Andy Murray matches. He did just so until Murray finally agreed to a rematch, that would come to take place at High Stakes. In leiu of his Murray-Boycott success, Chris decided he wanted to campaign across America to "fix the wrongs" of society. ---- "Ahh! Baltimore, Maryland." Chris steps out from a black 2007 Lincoln Town Car limosuine, inhaling the ever-so-fresh Baltimore air. He's dressed elegantly casual in a pair of beige khakis and a golf-necked t-shirt. He drops his shades over his eyes to kick out some of the glare from the glistening sun. The streets of Baltimore are full of life. Businessmen scurry from one office to the next with their briefcases firmly in hand. Teenagers stumble around the streets with Pepsi bottles that leak the scent of cheap rum. As Chris surveys the area, a second voice can be heard from inside the car. "Your appointment with Jessie Sigmon is in an hour." Chris steps away from the car, still admiring the city and ignoring what the other voice was instructing. The second person climbs out of the car to follow Bagwell. The man accompanying Bagwell posesses features that strike as familiar, yet his identity can't quite be pinpointed. Chris tilts his head back and, in a whisper, calls out the man's name. "Wesley," he says. "Isn't this city wonderful?" Wesley hands the driver of the limo a white envelope. The two exchange a handshake and the driver shuts the door. Wesley walks along side Chris, trying to capture the same beauty that Chris seems to have found in the city. "It's grand, Chris. Grand." Wesley, still unsure at what Chris sees in such a disaster of a city, agrees merely to keep peace. Chris smirks, "You don't need to kiss up my ass, Wesley. You're going to make the same amount of money from me whether I like your company or despise it." "I.." Wesley tries to answer, but Chris cuts him off. "Just be frank with me, Wesley. Be straight forward." Chris starts walking down the sidewalk, still gleaming off at the architecture within Baltimore. Wesley scurries up behind him as the limo driver pulls away. "So what is it that you're admiring, Chris?" Still with a smirk on his face, Chris gives himself a moment to brainstorm. "I'm admiring the simple fact that a city that's home to a fantastic aquarium -- one of the best I've ever seen, actually," replies Chris. "So you're into the whole fish thing?" "I'm not." Wesley isn't quite on the same page with Chris. "Sooo, then.." he begins to ask. "I'm admiring more than a pretty aquarium. I'm admiring the central hub of a state. I'm admiring the hundrends of millions of dollars that flow in and out of this city every single day. I'm admiring the way this place was built from the ground up and admiring the people who dedicated their lives to building it." Wesley catches himself scanning the city, appreciating all the same things as Chris. Chris continues, "But then I think of the shames that are brought to the city." "Shames?" Wesley asks. "Did you know that this city's aggrivated assault rate is twice the national average?" "I was unaware," answers Wesley. "Do you realize that Baltimore's rate of robbery is THREE times the national average?" "No, no I didn't." Chris stops in his tracks. Wesley nearly trips over his feet trying to come to an abrupt stop, as well. Bagwell looks at Wesley as his expression grows angry. "And the murder rate," he says, "is FIVE TIMES the national average." Bagwell shakes his head in absolute disgust. "FIVE TIMES greater in Baltimore than the average murder rate across the entire country. That's just despicable." Wesley seems a bit thrown off by the statistics and at a near loss of words. "Why do you think that is," he asks. Chris is quick with a response, "Why? Just take a look around, Wesley." Wesley looks around, seeing only a number of people walking briskly to wherever their destination may be. He shrugs his shoulders. "I don't see what you mean, Chris." Chris throws his arm around Wesley and points across the street. "Look there," he says. He points in another direction. "Or over there." Still unsure of where Chris is going with this, Wesley responds, "But you're only just pointing out... OOHHHH!" He got it. "Niggers," Chris exclaims in a whisper. "They steal. They assult. And they kill. All of our countries problems are the greatest wherever there is the highest concentration of this worthless race." Wesley is absolutely shocked at Bagwell's comments. "Then why did you want to schedule a meeting with Jessie Sigmon of the NAACP Fundraising committee?" Wesley asks. "I thought you wanted to arrange the meeting to help raise money?" Bagwell nearly falls to the pavement after Wesley's comment. "Raise money? You think I want to raise money for a bunch of fucking monkeys? I already donate enough money out of my paychecks every God damn week in taxes so their lazy asses can collect unemployment. I donate even more money to pay extra police officers so the lazy fucks don't steal any more than they already do. Why in the HELL would I want to raise any money for a 'people,' and I use that term loosely, that should have never even gained their independence?" Wesley's eyes nearly buldge out of his head. "Then what would prompt you to want to meet with Sigmon?" A grin crawls onto Bagwell's face. "I want to do what the rest of this country is afraid to do. I'm going to let him know EXACTLY how I feel. And you said it yourself -- none of the rest of the officers would accept a meeting. But of course, the sonuvabitch who's in charge of fundraising accepts. Thats all the niggers want...is money!" ---- An hour and a half passes. Chris and Wesley are now in the waiting room in the office of Jessie Sigmon, Executive Assistant in the Fundraising Department of the National Association for the Advancement of Colored People (NAACP). "Mr. Bagwell?" asks a young, dark skinned secretary with a very slender body. Chris stands from his chair and acknowledges the girl. "That's me," he says in a stern voice. "Jessie will see you now. If you'd just follow me this way." The secretary motions Chris and walks through a doorway. Bagwell follows close behind her with Wesley trailing a few steps back. They travel down a small corridor before arriving at a door with a golden plate that reads... "JESSIE SIGMON, EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT," Bagwell says aloud. "This would be Jessie's office, Mr. Bagwell," acknowledges the secretary. Wesley chimes in, "Thank you dear." The secretary turns and heads back down the hall from the direction in which she came. Chris and Wesley remain standing outside the door for a moment in silence, waiting for the secretary to exit the corridor. "Lets get this show on the road," says Bagwell. At that moment, he twists the doorknob and pushes the door open. Bagwell and Wesley enter the room with Welsey shutting the door firmly behind him. Nonchalantly, he flicks the lock shut as Jessie Sigmon peers up from a pile of paperwork. "You're a woman," Chris says in a bit of surprise. "Yes? Is that not what you were expecting?" Jessie replies. "No, actually it wasn't." "Well," Jessie says with a snotty tone, "I'm sorry to disappoint you. And for future reference, we knock when we want to enter rooms around here." Wesley butts in, "We apologize for that. We were just very eager to speak with you, thats all." Accepting the peace offering, Jessie nods her head and waves her hand at the chairs. "Please, take a seat." Chris and Wesley sit down in front of Jessie as she shoves a bunch of papers to the side, clearing some space off the desk. Jessie begins pitching her speech, "Well let me start by saying that I'm glad you two have come here to help raise money for the NAACP and try to create equal opportunity amongst black Americans." Chris grins, unable to control himself. He remarks, "Equal opportunity. A wonderful thing, wouldn't you say?" Jessie nods her head, "I certainly would. We've come a long way with getting blacks to be treaten as equals but there are still some hurdles to climb." "Hurdles?" Chris asks intuitively. "Yes, Mr. Bagwell, there are still a great deal of hurdles." "So," Chris begins to say with the sound of bad intent in his voice, "what would your opinion be if a television station began broadcasting under the name 'WHITE AMERICA TV'?" "That would be completely unethical and hold a total racial bias," she responds. "And your thoughts on the achievements of the highly popular B.E.T. in your demographics? Not realizing where Chris is going with this, Jessie answers promptly, "B.E.T. has done a fantastic job of giving Black Americans a common ground to vent, be free, be themselves, and be proud. B.E.T. is no more than a station dedicated to being proud of being black." Chris leans in and rests his hands on the desk. Wesley gets nervous that he may snap. "You said you're hoping for equality among Americans, right?" "Yes," she responds. "So why don't you go ahead and tell me how its EQUAL TREATMENT that you dumb niggers can have a television station that bashes white people and shows pride in being black, but its RACIST for whites to have a television station that celebrates our skin color and our achievements?" "SIR!" Jessie screams. "SHUT UP!" Bagwell screams back. Jessie reaches her hand towards her phone, but Chris snatches it and pins it to the desk. "You listen and you listen good," he threatens. "If a black man calls me a cracker, white trash, whitey, or any of that crap -- its perfectly fine. If I call a black man a nigger, I'm racist. If a white man is more qualified for a job that is predominately but a black man applies... he gets the position because he's at an 'economic' disadvantage. And thats equal?" He pauses for a moment, glaring into Jessie's eyes. "You people kill, you steal, and you don't work and you expect the rest of the world to erase this image that it has for you? You've given the EXACT same opportunities as everyone else. As an American, you're given the opportunity to go to school. But instead, you pieces of shit all drop out and go rob convenience stores. Then you decide a few years later that you're going to take the easy road and take a few courses to get a G.E.D., which you cheat your way through anyway. And you want to say that you weren't given the right education?" The frustration in Bagwell's voice grows stronger. "You have a God damn fucking organization dedicated to the advancement of COLORED people. WHERE IN THE HELL IS THE ORGANIZATION FOR THE ADVANCEMENT OF WHITES? And you say you want equal opportunity. MY FUCKING ASS!" A look of fear fills Jessie's eyes as she worries of Bagwell becoming abusive. "I'm sick and tired of media America pulling this whole racism card at every God damn opportunity they can find. You people have been liberated for a hundred years and you mean to tell me that you can't squiddle yourself into society?" Chris stops and gives Jessie a chance to say something. She opens her mouth, but words don't come out. He continues, "Give me a break. I'm not here today to help you raise any money for your pathetic organization. I'm here to give you insight into reality. If you want change and want to fit into this world, you better stop being so fucking bitter at it. Stop digging your own graves and blaming it on the white population. It was NOT the white man's fault that you fucks were all enslaved. How the FUCK are you going to let some short, fat, hick boss you around when you outnumber him 10 to 1?" Wesley nudges Chris, trying to get him to calm down a bit. But Chris goes on, "YOU people created slavery just like YOU people created racism and oppression among your own race. So why don't you use that for a fundraiser idea. 'Help a Nigger Stop Being Lazy Day'." Chris thrusts Jessie's arm back at her. She grabs it and holds it, clearly in pain. Chris stands from the chair, knocking it backwards and to the floor. Wesley pops right up next to him. The two walk towards the door and Wesley scrambles to unlock it and open it. Chris peers back at Jessie with a smirk, "Now you have yourself a pleasent evening, ma'am." Chris follows Wesley out of the office and the two instantly take off on a sprint down the corridor. Back in the office, Jessie reaches for the phone to call in security. She lifts the handset and stops. She places it back on the reciever, shaking her head, with tears streaming down the sides of her face.
