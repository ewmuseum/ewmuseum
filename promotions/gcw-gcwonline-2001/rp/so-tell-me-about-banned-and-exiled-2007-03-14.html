So, Tell Me About Banned and Exiled~!

14 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14Mar2007 Well, it's only a few days from Worldwide 63 now. Perhaps another night for Banned and Exiled~! to make an impact? Well, a betting man would say yes. On top of the long list of names of people that Markus Stone and Chris Storm have been pissing off quickly adding up, what could be next? As Markus waits in the lobby of the Omni Willaim Penn Hotel in Pittsburgh, the plans for this weeks event run through his mind. Does B and E have a plan? Of course they do. Seconds pass by before the clerk brings a key to Markus. Markus thanks the clerk and heads up to his room. A big nicer than the last place he stayed, but then again, the checks just got a little bit bigger from GCW. Markus barely has time to set his bag down before his brand spanking new Razor rings. Star Wars theme song, no less. Markus answers, and is greeted by a voice he hadn't thought about in years. After a few short moments he sets up a meeting with the person on the other line. _________________________________________________________ A short time later, and Markus is sitting at a table inside the Primanti Brothers Restaurant, a place definitely recommended to all you visiting Pittsburgh. Across from him is a man in his late thirties, possibly early forties. Graying dark brown hair, bushy mustache, everything you'd expect from a wrestling columnist. The man, to be more precise, was Leslie Jameston, of Pro Wrestling Periodical fame. The two have just finished their meal, and Markus is washing his down with an Amber Bach. Leslie has ate his meal in relative silences after the initial greetings and catching-ups, but soon breaks down to business. "All right, so are you ready?" Markus takes a shallow sip from his beverage, enjoying the taste. "For you man, anytime." Leslie reaches inside of his coat pocket and pulls out a tape recorder. Setting it on the table, he presses the record button. "Thank you, Mr. Markus Stone, for you time. Allow me to start this interview off with a congratulations on what has so far been a mostly successful return to the ring." "Mostly?" Markus grins back at Leslie. "C'mon now, you've known me long enough, haven't you? I don't always judge success on wins and losses, now do I?" Leslie smiles back, holding his hands to the side as if to say 'Who me?' "Sure, I didn't get the win last week, but me and Chris have been making quite the statement, haven't we? In fact, I wouldn't be surprised if we were in line for some sort of title shot soon. I mean, you can't deny the ability that the two of us have." "No, there's no denying your ability." Leslie begins, but his tone implies that there may be more coming. "However, the two of you will get a chance to show how well you work in the ring together this week at Worldwide." Taking another drink, Markus pauses as he considers how to continue. Normally, he wouldn't be so polite with an interview, but him and Leslie go way back. If nothing else, Leslie is a fan of not just Markus, but the struggle Markus has endured to continue wrestling. I respect him for that. "Yes, it'll be our first match teaming in quite some time. You know, I like to think of our team as years in the making." Leslie looks curious, not quite sure of what Markus means. "Please, explain." Of course Markus will explain. Leslie may not know all the details of Markus' life, and the casual wrestling fan will know even less. Curious minds want to know. "Well, for years myself and Chris tried to get together as a tag team, but something always seemed to get in the way. I mean, long ago Primetime Central held a tag team tournament. Chris was still working for WIW:NG, and I was employed with DWO. We were the only tag team from two different federations, and we were the odds on favorite, at least in the mind of all the smart people out there. What happened? DWO wouldn't let it happen, and buried me under a workload in the federation that forced me to withdraw from the tournament. Chris got saddled with some sideshow performer and nearly won on his own. We tried teaming again in DWO, but my knee took me away from action. In between now and then, I've actually tried a few times to get back in the ring, but I wasn't ready yet. Now, what you see today, is the culmination of all our years in this business, and the fruits of our labors. Banned and Exiled~!" Leslie is enjoying himself immensely, as he always does when interviewing Markus. No other wrestler seems to let him dig as much information out as Markus does, and Markus is happy to oblige. Why would I not let my friend, or the world, know exactly what it is that motivates me everyday? "Now, that leads me to another question, why the named? Is there any significant meaning behind it?" Significant meaning? Of course there's a meaning. It's the very definition of who they are. "Obviously. Banned and exiled. That's what we had to deal with. We are two survivors of being banned from PTC. We were kicked out. Why? Because our mindset wasn't the same as the Primetime community, and we refused to conform to their beliefs. For being different, we were ostracized and made an example out of. We carry the scars on our backs for staying true to ourselves, and to each other. We proudly wear the moniker that PTC gave us, and spit in their faces for it. And what did Primetime get for their troubles? A cohesive unit that bonded even more closely together than ever before. Now, we know the rules, and we know the limits. They are two very similar ideals, but we hold neither close to our heart. Me and Chris, we do what we want, when we want. We proudly shun all morals and beliefs that people like the Primetime community, or even the wrestlers of GCW, hold dear." Markus pauses briefly and takes a sip from his beer. Morals? Who fucking needs them? "You see, Les, me and Chris, we realized long ago that the rules just weren't made for us. We aren't going to change ourselves just so others can shackle us down with petty concepts of what is right and wrong. We do whatever it takes to be successful, and we have a good time doing it. Once we were banned from Primetime, we realized that we no longer cared what anybody else thought of us. We were outcasts, and nobody wanted to stand up for us. Not Jason Chae, WIW President, or Callisto Bane, owner of DWO. They wanted to let sleeping dogs lie. Me and Chris, we're the type to stir up the shit, and use whatever trouble arises to fuel ourselves. This unit we have formed, it will dominate Les. Mark my words, and when you type this up for your report, put it in bold font, we will dominate." Still wanting more information, Leslie changes his approach. "Alright, with that said, last week you and Chris Storm announced that the two of you made your surprise reunion a plan. What prompted this plan, and how did it come about? Was your time away from the ring any motivation to have a partner there to back you up?" Sounds more like Leslie is asking if Markus was scared to go it alone. "No, not at all. In fact, when I first signed my GCW contract, I had no intention of teaming up with Chris. I was all ready for a chance to show the world that I still had what it takes to compete. Chris and I... Well, our whole partnership just kinda fell into place. With Tessa leaving, I saw an opportunity. I talked to Tessa, Tessa talked to Chris, and the rest will go down in GCW history. Why did I want Chris to come in? Well, like I said, years in the making. That last piece, what with Tessa get knocked up by the massive member of one Chris Storm, fell into the right place at the right time, and the right opportunity for both of us was provided. If you want to look at the big picture of things, the grand scheme, so to speak, Markus Stone single handedly brought a former PTC Global champion, back to PTC, and GCW gets to be the lucky company to play host to his abilities." Yes, that should just about cover that. "Do you sometimes feel like you play second fiddle to Chris Storm?" Whoa, what was that? "Wait, what was that?" The look of surprise on Markus' face said it all. How could somebody say something like that? Leslie quickly rephrased his question, not wanting to offend Markus. "Well, not to offend, but it seems to some people out there that your friendship with Chris Storm is a little unbalanced. Sure, he helps you, but it seems that you give more than receive, and the end result is Chris looking the superstar and you looking the henchman." Wow. Words could not describe the flash of anger that pass through Markus. Second fiddle? Not a chance. "Listen, me and Chris share a friendship. Through blood and sweat has our friendship grown and perservered. I do Chris a favor, and Chris does me a favor. I watch his back, and he watches mine. It's cut and dry. Sure, he may be better with words than I am. He may carry more weight on the mic than I do. That's fine. I am content with knowing that while my name was forgotten over the last five years, Chris has made headlines. In all reality, one would say I benefit more from this partnership than Chris does. What would Chris Storm gain from teaming with a nobody? An unkown hack that most people never knew existed? Now, what does a nobody gain from teaming with a former PTC Global champion? I'd say I'm regaining a lot of notoriety that I once lost, wouldn't you?" "Well, putting it like that certainly does make more sense." Interrupting, Markus presses forward. More sense? It makes perfect sense. "You see, I could go the low road and just obliterate every no-name that gets thrown in front of me, but instead, I am suddenly springboarded into a match with Dawn McFarlane, the lousy tramp that she is. Why? Because Banned and Exiled~! do whatever they want, whenever they want. Now, we go against P and P, why? Because Banned and Exiled~! is a name to be recognized. Caldera quickly grasped onto this concept, and we are now main attractions at GCW. So the world can think what they want of how equal mine and Chris' partnership is. The end result will be the same, the name coming from their lips won't be Markus Stone. It won't be Chris Storm. It will be Banned, and, Exiled, tilde, exlamation mark." Period. "Alright, now then, I do have a question regarding your past a bit, and where you are now." The past? Never really was a good subject, but how can you say no to an interviewer? Well, while being polite, anyways? "Okay, shoot." "Well, you used to be considered a relatively unstable human being. I mean, you once carried a stick around, named it, and had conversations with it. I recall more than one instance in which those conversations cost you a match, or a title shot even. What has changed?" Ah, fond memories. Jolly was his name, to be precise. And more than one head being split open as the tandem of Jolly and Zero tore into them flashes into the mind of Markus Stone. Fun days those were. "Well, besides getting rid of the cane? I kid, I kid. Well, for me to say I've buried my demons would be a lie, Les. I mean, look at this right now. I swore off drinking when Maria left me, and he I am drinking again. No man is perfect. Me, personally, I fought my demons. I struggle with them everyday, to be honest. The only difference between now, and back then, is a level of control. I've learned to control my demons. And in doing so, I've become a stronger person than every before." "Thank you Markus, for your time, and for lunch." Leslie pushes the stop button on the recorder. After a bit of meaningless small talk, the two make their farewells, and Leslie leaves. Markus takes a drink from his Amber Bach. I've learned to control my demons. If they only knew the truth. I've learned to control my demons. That what you'd like to believe, isn't it? "Shut up." Markus forcefully pushed the Voice back down, battling with it yet another day. I've learned to control my demons? If only that were the truth.
