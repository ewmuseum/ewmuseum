Injury!

8 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/08/10 “Lets have the diagnosis doc” The room was warm, some would say too warm, the elderly would probably disagree, yet sweat was dripping from the forehead of Kid Cool as he looked at the bemused man who sat before him. “I'm sorry what diagnosis?” Kid Cool rolled his eyes in frustration, quite clearly in-front of him was a career threatening injury. The pain was seething through his body every minute and yet somehow the doctor still can't see it. “I'll talk you through the story again Doc, I can call you Doc right? “My name is...” “Thanks Doc” Kid Cool paused for a moment rubbing his chin in a vein attempt to appear in reflection. “It all begin last Wednesday...” “You mean yesterday?” The doctor slumped back in his chair feigning interest. Kid Cool ignored the doctor and continued painting a picture that Monet would have been proud of. “I was walking around minding my own business, getting into the zone before a workout session, did I tell you I'm a professional wrestler?” Kid Cool looked back at the doctor from his moments reflection attempting to get some acknowledgement. The doctor sighs heavily and nods, putting his glasses on the tip of his nose to inspect the leg of Kid Cool once again. “Anyway, there I was, clearing my mind, when all of a sudden I was attacked! I never saw it coming Doc... There was more than one of them.” Kid Cool paused deep in thought “TWO of them in-fact!” Kid Cools voice breaking into an almost high pitched squeak. “And then, and then, you've got to tell me what's going on Doc? Can I fight this week, I need to know, I'm a professional wrestler after all!” The Doctor raised his eyebrow and cleared his throat, he needed to be blunt and to the point. “Mr. Eriks, I don't believe that you need a doctor...” “OH GOD IS IT THAT BAD!” Kid Cool interrupted, I knew it, I just knew it! The pain, oh god the pain just wont, go away! I'm going to have to phone up GCW and tell them its over!” The Doctor rolled his eyes and took a pen out from his shirt pocket. Jotting down some notes on his plush mahogany desk Kid Cools interest became less on talk and more on the doctors notes. “Good, good, I need a sick-note, just as a precautionary measure, so many fans are going to be disappointed about me not wrestling... Did I tell you I'm a wrestler? Born and raised. Check out these muscles” Kid Cool begins to flex his muscles but drops his arms after a moment upon seeing his leg again. “Attacked Doc! Attacked by these great... huge...” “Wasps?” The Doctor interjected A tear begins to well up in the eye of Kid Cool as he relives the moment in his mind, then the realisation sinks in. “You... You know? No Doc it wasn't a wasp. It was much bigger than that. I mean look at my leg!” Emphasising with over-exaggerated hand gestures. The Doctor rips the piece of paper from his notepad and hands it to Kid Cool “Here is a prescription for some ointment, just put this on the infected area and you'll be fine within a day.” Kid Cool gulps “Surely a sick note will suffice...” Kid Cool pleaded with the Doctor “No, No I insist. Only the best treatment for my patients besides...” The Doctor paused adding for effect “You're a professional wrestler after all, we need to get you into the ring to face that opponent” A bead of sweat drips from the forehead of Kid Cool as he steps out of the Doctors office, “Crap!” he thought solemnly to himself, there must be some way out of this. It would just need an extra bit of cunning.
