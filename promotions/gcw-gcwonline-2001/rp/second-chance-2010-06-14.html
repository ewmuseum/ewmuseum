Second Chance

14 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/14/10 A large wooden hammer smashes against a pallet three times and resonates around a cave in an unknown location. “The meeting is now in session.” It is a rather small cave, no more than ten metres by ten metres and sitting directly in the middle is a round table. The table itself is carved from fine wood and has obviously been completed by a master carpenter. Around it there are twelve chairs, also made of the same material. They are high at the back and have the letter carved into them. Sitting in each chair bar two are figures wearing a black robe with a hood, all completely upright with their hands set on the table. They have no faces so to say but they have mouths, that is all. Their other features are simply flat, a smooth layer of skin. “This meeting has been called together today to settle the dispute between Lori Equinox and Lori Equinox. There is a battle coming and if these two cannot put their differences aside for this week then surely we shall all be destroyed. So hence we call forth these men.” Out of the shadows the two men appear. One with red hair, the other with black. One with two eye and the other with just one. As they walk side by side they give quick glares to one another. The Lori with black hair starts muttering to his namesake as they head towards their seats. “You brought this upon us you know?” “How exactly did I do that?” “Cause you have become weak.” “Hardly” “Well you have. Ever sense you went into this mood of having a better life and making friends and not hurting people, where has it got you?” There is a moment of silence. “It gives me a feeling of self satisfaction. Is that so bad?” “So when it it all comes down to it you're only really thinking about yourself in the end. Make others feel good so we can feel good too hmm? What a pathetic excuse. We should be taking what we want, no bargaining, no nothing. Take and then take some more. If someone will not let us take then we should destroy.” They reach their seats, still muttering to one another. As they sit down the ten other figures around the table turn their heads to face them. All their mouths open in unison as they speak deeply and slightly distorted to the Lori's. “You have been called... yes you have. You have to come to terms with one another. The battle is coming and if you are not ready then you will surely be slain. Compile your differences just for once in a hope that order can be restored to our once great life. One of you has the moral capacity to do good, help others and in doing so this will help yourself. Good karma we shall call it. You want to end the pain that has stricken your life all this time. What do you have to say?” The red headed Lori stands, giving a quick look down on his other self before turning to the council which awaits him. “All my life I have done things to hard way, trying to fight all my own battles, using others not to help me but to stand on to give myself a greater advantage. Just for once I want to have a friend, just for once I want some support. Is that too much to ask? It is all well and good trying to fight everything on my own but after what has happened recently I cannot help but think that things could have been different.” The black haired Lori interrupts, standing up and pushing his alter ego to one side. “It was your wrong doing that got us into this situation in the first place. We were doing fine until you decided that you wanted to change.” He puts on a weak and puny voice before continuing. “Oh, I want to be nice to people, let them stand all over me in the hope that somehow this will help me. I'll forget the fact that I lose my eye due to this and the fact that I've had my ass beat more times than enough. But sure it's all good cause I'm making friends.” The black haired Lori shakes his head in disgust before continuing in his normal tone. “What needs to happen here is that we need to go back to the way things were. Sure you had your moments and that's all it takes, you can have them but I need to be in control if we are to succeed. No more making friends, no more sucking up to the higher powers that be. No more trying to get into girls pants who have no other intention than kicking our ass.” Red haired Lori stands up once more, now interrupting his counterpart. “We can still be successful. It we hadn't been insane in the first place we wouldn't be in this mess. Of course though we had to make enemies. Make other peoples lives hell. Not for one second though did we think we would become a target for these people. We haven't been able to destroy anyone's lives up to now. No matter how hard we try we always fail. How will that change? I can't see it happening. I think we should act in that we get these people on our good side then they won't be gunning for us. It's as simple as that. Black haired Lori stands up to retaliate but before he begins both him and his opposite are shoved back into their seats by an invisible force. “Silence!” The ten robed figures now stand and turn their attention to the two men. “We did not come here to bicker, no matter how much you want to. Outside there is a battle that we must surpass if we are to reach the mountain top on the other side of the valley. It is a one way path, straight to the top but the obstacles but in our way are momentous and it will test our character. If you cannot put your differences aside then you will kiss the mountain goodbye and the earth will envelope us all. Now Choose!” The Lori's look to each, both equally confused. “Choose?” “Yes, only one of you can go into the battle. But only by resolving your problems can you become one to endure through this task before you. Choose.” The red haired Lori speaks but his words are cut short by a gutting feeling in his mid section. He looks down to find a blade has been passed through him. Without noticing, his alter ego had got behind him and cut through him with a sword. The black haired Lori kneels down beside him and whispers in his ear... “Sorry Lori, but I won't let you take charge in this, the most delicate of situations.” As he withdraws his blade the red haired Lori decays into dust before vanishing altogether. “The choice has been made, go forth and do battle.” Lori drops the sword, with a loud clang, onto the ground and exits the cave. Immediately he is greeted with a large green pasture. Along the skyline stands a mountain with a path leading all the way from the cave to it. Though there is a battle about to begin on the horizon. He begins his walk along the path towards what would soon be his army. Before he gets to it though one of his followers comes rushing towards him. “Sir! Sir! The creatures! They are coming from the ground! Foul demons of the earth.” Lori walks straight past him but ushers him alongside with his right hand. “Oblivion has come amongst us then I presume.” The follower, still catching up gasps a little so he can reply. “Yes Sir... the worst I've ever seen. We are being overrun!” Lori stops and turns to his compatriot. “Take a seat good man. We wouldn't want the Oblivion to see us as weak as you hmmm?” “But Sir... I want to figh...” “Sit your ass down.” Lori doesn't look back, he knows that his orders have been followed. It is his mind after all. The area before him now begins to rise into a small hill. He makes good speed up it and reaches it's brae before coming to the top and looking out onto the battle. Down below thousands upon thousands and mend and women battle against creatures of great darkness, all of which come from a hole in the ground. The hole itself seems to engulf the ground as it gets larger and larger, never showing any signs of swelling too much. As it travels along the surface it envelopes anything in its way. “And so it begins...” Lori rushes down the hill towards the hole, he has nothing to protect him but the clothes on his back and no weapon now to produce an assault. As he gets closer and closer to the hole it begins to rise up like a wave towards him, towering higher and higher until it is at least fifteen feet tall. “Come on baby, come on...” The wave now rushes towards him, its black mist like texture rifling through the air. Closer and closer they come, both speeding up as they go. “Ahhhhh!!!” Just as they meet they both stop dead. Lori stands and catches his breathe while observing the wave morph in front of his eyes. From the feet up it begins to create a human form, piece by piece the figure increases, eventually cumulating as a known enemy. “Mayhem...” Without hesitation Lori drives towards the apparition in front of him and grabs him by the waist. He lifts him over his shoulder and runs faster and faster and faster before leaping into the air and literally driving it through the earth. As he does so wails can be heard from the dark beings roaming the battlefield as the retreat to the Oblivion they came from. All the battlefield the followers begin cheering as if the battle is over. As he turns to face them though his vision is blocked by a large man. “Shaman” The apparition does not return any form of look or reply but simply stares straight through Lori. “You never liked me Shaman, when I arrived in GCW you were accusing me of doing things that I hadn't done. If I had my way back then it would have been me doing those things. Alas he we are and you are in my way. You have improved recently Shaman, I must say. Even I am impressed but you will not be able to survive my assault. I will be relentless and you shall feel my pain.” Without hesitation one again Lori twists his body to the right before swing back round and punching the apparition straight in the mouth. As he does so it disappears into the wind. Lori looks upon his followers and for a moment enjoys the happiness they are feeling. They honestly believe the battle is over. Unfortunately for them they fail to see what is coming. Lori gathers everyone together and they move towards the base of the mountain. When they arrive he turns to them. “My good people. You have done just to yourselves and to me this fine day.” He nods towards a number of the followers. “Hope, glory, anger, hate... you have all played a big part in the victory thus far but there is still some way to go. The next part of the task I must do alone, for surely you would all be destroyed if you were to come with me. Give me a sword. I am not a weak man and I will show this right here and now!” The crowd cheers as Lori lifts his arms above his head. “So bring forth... THE TEMPEST!” The sky that has been so bright up until now grows darker and fills with black clouds. The wind picks up ferociously and the rain begins to pound down on all those who stand at the base of the mountain. Lori looks up to the sky and screams... “Bring it on Tempest... Give me one more chance!” A lightning bolt flashes before Lori's eyes and Tempest appears. Lori looks her up and down before screaming with rage... “YOU WILL GIVE ME ONE MORE CHANCE!” Lori grabs the sword he was given and rips it through Tempest. ---------------- Lori emerges back in his home, sat in front of his fire. “Wow... I really need to get my head fixed.”
