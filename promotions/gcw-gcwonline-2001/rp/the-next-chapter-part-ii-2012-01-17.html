The Next Chapter: Part II

17 January 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/17/12 Aaron quietly slid his key into the lock and turned it slowly. His face cringed as the sound of the pins releasing echoed into his head loudly. As the final pin unlatched and the door began to push open he looked down at his watch. It was close to two-thirty in the morning. He sighed quietly as he carefully slid the key from the lock. The entire drive home he had wondered if he’d be able to make it back into his apartment quietly enough. Amanda was down for the weekend and had fallen asleep around ten. He hoped he wouldn’t wake her. The door creaked as he began to shut it, causing him to shut it more slowly. “I’m in the front door.” He thought. He kicked his shoes off at the door and gently set his keys on the counter. Aaron knew he had time before climbing into bed would challenge him. He needed a shower to wash away the dirt he felt covering his skin. He was almost uncomfortable living in his own skin. As he made a few steps towards the hallway that would lead to his room and the bathroom, Aaron slid his hoodie over his head and dropped it on the couch. The floorboards creaked with every step he took, almost screaming out him for what he had done. The door to his bedroom was halfway open, so he slid between the door and the frame without much trouble. His eyes fell on the frame of Amanda’s sleeping body in the bed and he let out a small sigh. “How could I?” His mind asked. Aaron shook his head and reached into the basket at the foot of his bed. He grabbed a clean pair of boxers and moved back towards the door. This time his hip caught the door and pushed it further open, causing a creak to fill the dark room. Amanda stirred, but only momentarily as she rolled over and pulled the covers p over her body further. Aaron let out another sigh as he slowly pulled the door closed behind him and walked into the bathroom across the hall. Never in his life had he ever felt this dirty; felt this wrong. The shower started with a turn of the faucet handle and quickly Aaron brought it to the right temperature. Quickly he slipped out of his clothes and pushed them to the side before stepping into the shower. “She’s so sweet.” He thought to himself. Aaron reached for his body wash and squirted so much soap onto his rag it dripped down his arm and to the floor of the tub. He began to lather the cleanser up and couldn’t get it onto his skin fast enough. He frantically washed his body clean, rinsed and then repeated the process until he had gone through an entire bottle of body wash. He washed his hair three times and then stood with the water running down his face. He was trying to rinse the evil from his skin. He wanted the sin gone. The shower turned off and Aaron stepped out to dry off. Two minutes later he was dressed once more and walked across the hall into his bedroom. “This will be tough.” He moved across the dark bedroom and dropped his dirty clothes into a pile that had formed against the wall. He took a step away but then moved back to the pile, burying his clothes under other dirty garments. He sighed quietly once more and walked to his edge of the bed. Slowly he lifted the covers into the air and lowered himself gently into bed. The mattress jostled some causing Amanda to stir a bit more. Aaron froze and allowed her to stop moving before he swung his legs into bed and laid down. As soon as he had himself in bed on his back, Amanda turned over and wrapped her arms around him. Her head landed on his chest and he couldn’t fight the urge to cradle her body with his arms. “I feel so guilty.” She squeezed him tighter as he thought this. He laid there with his girlfriend sound asleep on his chest and his eyes wide open staring at the ceiling. He had done it and gotten away with it. It felt so good, but he felt horrible. All he could think about was how hard it had been. The only thing that seemed to put his mind at ease seemed stupid. Chloe was going through the same thing. That made Aaron feel okay. It made him feel right. It made him forget about the sin he had just committed. And it made him want to do it again.
