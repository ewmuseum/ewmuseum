Date: Timeless -=Shirt removed, chest against the back of the cold patent leather chair. When he's finally done and pulls away his skin will stick to the chair, and slowly pull away. The constant buzz heard behind him drives most people insane. A tiny needle driving into his flesh repeatedly. His back art says 'O'Neil. All five letters represent something to Bryan. His body will one day be covered with all of them. But for now just his chest and his back. O'Neil is not just his last name. It's a lot more. O-Once upon a time to abused children escaped the clutches of their madman father and claimed their freedom and individuality. One a legend, the other a legend, but a dark monster that fears the hearts of many. It's only a matter of time where Legend vs Legend; Brother vs Brother will succumb us all. N-Nor heaven, nor hell, shall ring its fury into the heart of thy fellow O'Neil. A name, a statement, an entire story being told before your very eyes. God has no place in this story, has no existence in the outcome....Just O'Neil. E-Entropic. The state of chaos and disorder. Nothing is ever the same, but always different. Blood, guts, sweat, and tears. There is no straight line, only twists and turns, into the hands of evil. I-Individual. There are many of the bloodline, but none the same. The two chosen, are exact opposites. Their blood will spill and taint the soil. The blood of the good will be shed and drown the world. These two individuals, only one can be left standing....only one. L-Ladders are climbed everyday. This O'Neil has climbed ladders and mountains and more. There is no looking down, there is no bottom of the ladder now. Only the top. Carpe Diem, or be buried by the light. -=The buzzing stops. Bryan pulls back and stretches. Piece number 2 of the puzzle, many more to go. He stands up, stretches again and puts a light button up shirt on.=- 'Go get 'em Bryan. All of us here support you.' Bryan looks into the man's eyes 'What hell hath Mayhem wrought? Ask the diseased and feeble minded. Judgment day is near, are you ready?' Bryan walks off to a wide eyed tattoo artist who just soiled his drawers in fear. He turns away and looks at his Pro-Wrestling magazine that has Bryan O'Neil and Chrissy-Poo on the cover separated by a ladder. He glances carefully and Chrissy and notices.....notices blood swelling up in his eyes. The blood streams down his face, his chest, legs, and falls off the page onto the floor of the tattoo shoppe....three tiny drops...three of many
