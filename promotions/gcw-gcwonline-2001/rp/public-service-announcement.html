Public Service Announcement

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: TODAY ** This is a Roberto/Phyl/Jason Joint ** ( The cameras can only catch a blank white screen in the beginning. It remains on this white screen until three black letters slam onto the screen. 'G-C-W' then a familiar voice plays in the background. ) 'This Video is created and distributed by Global Championship Wrestling, which is under Caldera Enterprises. Enjoy the show.' ( The letters fade away and the white screen blinks a few times until it changes to a studio set. Nothing fancy just a five or six foot long wooden table and three chairs. Out from stage left comes Kevin Jacobs, GCW interviewer. He walks out to center stage to address the small crowd in attendance. ) Kevin: Hello fans I am kevin Jacobs and you are here on GCW's own 'Public Service Announcement.' brought to you by Caldera Enterprises. Tonight we are going to give you an inside look on how our world of wrestling really ticks So without further adue let me introduce you to the host of tonight's show ... Roberto Abitua! ( The small crowd claps as this Roberto walks out on stage, waving to the fans before sitting down in the middle of the three steel chairs behind the plain wooden table. ) Roberto: Hola amigos, I am Roberto Abitua and I am a GCW superstar. Now I know what you are thinkin ... something along the lines of 'WHAT THE HELL IS THIS GUY TALKING ABOUT?' Yes it is true I am but 5'6' and barely 140 lbs. yet I am the number one contender to the GCW World Heavyweight championship! yes that's right folks I am LeStatt Knight! ( the fans boo ) Now I am sure you are dying to know what my secret is, right? Well to answer that question I must first bring out my two co-hosts ... Phyl and Jason! (Still a little confused by Roberto's statements they clap at the name Phyl, not knowing exactly who he is, but assuming he's probably ok, since he's on a GCW show. Phyl comes out from behind the curtain, a man just Roberto's height but on the complete opposite end of the weight spectrum. Weighing almost three hundred pounds he almost waddles as he walks, close cut curly brown hair, frames.. a pudgy face with thin wire framed and smudged up glasses perched at the tip of his nose. Sitting down to the right of Roberto, he speaks) Phyl: (in a somewhat southern drawl) Hi everyone I'm Phyl, and I'm one of the favorites going into the Dangerous Games battle royal. That's right nearly three hundred pounds, and I can't walk five blocks without getting out of breath, and yet somehow, someway in the coming months I could be your GCW World Heavyweight Champion ( Roberto's blue eyes light up and an eyebrow perks upwards. ) Roberto: Ya know ... HEAVYweight doesn't begin to describe you amigo. Phyl: Ass.... Roberto: My point exactly! (Without cue, another figure steps out from behind the curtain to join the two men. In the midst of the former weight jokes, this man seems on the exact opposite of the spectrum from our portly friend Phyl. At 6'5 and looking to weigh slightly just over 160 pounds, the gangly figure in front of us is indeed a sight. In a humoured tone he speaks to the two men which he now towers behind.) Jason: And now there is me, your two-time GCW Heavyweight Champion that couldn't bench press 200 pounds if his life depended on the matter. My name is Jason. Hi everybody. (Jason sits on the far right chair, accompanying the duo in their Public Service announcement. Before Lestatt can speak again, Jason blurts out) Jason: And my penis is an astounding 12 times larger than Phyl's... (Which no one seems to care. Roberto sighs, and continues onwards.) (Phyl acts all pissed off) Phyl: Hey three and a half inches of flinging fury bitch! (Jason's eyes roll, smirking all the while) Roberto: And who said wrestlers are just muscle bound idiots with the brain the size of a peanut ? Roberto: And who said wrestlers are just muscle bound idiots with the brain the size of a peanut ? ( Roberto chuckles at this for a moment before putting a straight face on almost instantly, which causes the crowd to laugh a bit more. ) Roberto: Back to business ... Now once again I am certain you all are wondering how we do it right? How do we look like this now yet when those cameras roll we look like hulking beasts ready to rip someone limb from limb. ( Roberto turns and looks at Phyl and Jason for a moment, both give him a slight head nod. Roberto nods back and then turns back to face the studio audience ) Jason: Is it because we have professional make-up artists? Roberto: And the survery says ? ( a short pause ) EHHHHHHHHH! WRONG-O! (Jason pouts, which is obviously fake. Roberto continues.) Roberto: You need two major things to accomplish this incredible feat my friends. One is ... ( he is cut off by a ringing noise. Jason, Roberto,and Phyl all begin searching their pockets and almost in unison they all pull out cell phones. ) Group: Hello? Jason: Not mine ( Jason and Roberto put their phones down a bit disappointed ) Phyl: Scuse me its the Pizza guy ... hes here, I'll be right back. Roberto: but we're in the middle of ? ( Phyl gets up and takes off before Roberto can protest anymore leaving Jason and Roberto alone. They just shrug their shoulders. ) Roberto: Where was I? OH YEAH! Two key ingredients to this miracle. One is a high speed computer. Jason show us the model we have here on set. (A minute later, Phyl comes back with a huge smile on his face, the box already open balanced on one hand, in the other, a greasy slice of pepperoni, cause really what else for the fat man) Phyl: You guys want some, I got an extra large so you could split a slice? (Jason shakes his head and stands up, walking two feet to his right, where an object is covered under a dark blue sheet Jason makes a lavish motion towards the object and uncovers it! Only to reveal a calculator. Phyl speaks through a full mouth.) Phyl: Not that one jackass, the other table. Seriously guys, it's damn good pizza.. (Jason winces as he walks another two feet to his right to another identical table. Pulling the cloth from this one reveal a brand-spanking new Dell factory PC. Everyone in attendance shares a round of 'OOOs!' and 'AHHs!') Roberto: Yes that's what I am talking about right there guys. This is a Dell Pentium Four processor with built in DVD and CD burner. This baby is loaded with all kinds of gidgets and gadgets that I don't feel like detailing at this moment. This baby can be bought for a low price of ... um ... well ... shit I don't know I took it from the office. I am probably going to get fired for stealing company property. ( Roberto shrugs and continues on ) ANYWAYS ... now I am sure you may be wondering how THIS computer, or any like it can still pull of these miracles of science correct? Well there is one other thing that you need ... and this is the part that is most important and also the thing that most find tough to get a grasp on. A brain that works. Phyl: (Finishing up the last slice, and closing the box) God that was good. Anyway, yeah a brain is very important. You've got to be able to have the creativity to come up with something new and exciting. At least that's in a perfect world. The point is, Jason, Roberto, and I, we're all just normal guys, but we've got one thing in common, we're writers, and damn good ones too if I don't say so myself. Jason: Damn right Phyl: Our methods aren't always the same, in fact I can't really write unless I've got a full stomach, and a pot of coffee brewing, not to mention I tend to take a lot of smoke breaks....Speaking of... Roberto: We are three bad mother ... Jason & Phyl: Shut your mouth!! ( Roberto looks around after being told to shut his mouth. ) Roberto: I was only talking about us! Phyl: Hey, can I smoke in here, you guys mind? ( before anyone answers Phyl lights up ) Jason: You see, this whole world which we participate in is really just a small part of what there really is to experience. We all have characters in this World known as GCW, but that's all we are. Outside of GCW we have plenty of thing to attend to. A job, family, friends, (looks at Rob)a girlfriend, (looks at Phyl) a boyfriend, (stays looking at Phyl) or even constant masturbation. Phyl: I don't think about gays all the time....wait! no, I mean ever. Dude did you have to bust out a 'Phyl is gay joke' I thought those were confined to the OOC board. (Phyl looks sullen) Phyl: And three times a day isn't what I'd call constant. (Phyl opens the pizza box back up and searchs for crumbs) Roberto: AND SO IN OTHER WORDS? We aren't really wrestlers, its just imaginary creations of ours. LeStatt Knight is everything I've ever hoped i could be but nothing more. Hell they don't make mexicans THAT BIG! Phyl: And hey I just wish I had as much money, talent or punk rock cred as Stevens had. Roberto: ( chimes in ) and Gay midget male strippers... Jason: But I really do wrestle as Jason Dread, guys! ( Everyone goes quiet as they stare at each other for awhile after Jason's last comment ) Roberto: riiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiight ... Phyl: Oh yeah that reminds me, despite what our character, we're seriously friends, and poke fun at each other, it's just a game we play to meet new people sometimes. Phyl: Of course sometimes that means tolerating our friends delusions... Roberto: Occasionally you will meet some of those who seem to really think that this stuff is life or death. Some people actually get huge egos from this HOBBY ... thats right HOBBY! (Phyl perks up at this) Phyl: yeah it's a hobby just like my extensive porn collection. Roberto: Or like jason's fantasies of being a real wrestler. Phyl: Indeed Jason: I hate you people Jason: No, really. He's right though, Phyl, this is only a hobby and should stay that way. There's no reason for egos. No reason to whine or cry. It's all in good fun! Roberto: This is exactly why we are here today though. TO help egotistical sons of bitches to kinda get a handle on reality again. (Phyl laughs slightly) Phyl: Oh you mean THAT guy! (Jason rolls his eyes) Jason: Him too, Phyl. Him too. Roberto: Yes Phyl THAT guy ... perhaps after seeing our announcement here on live television he will understand that games are to be fun. So what if you will never get close to the GCW World title? Hell even the Woman's title, that doesn't mean you must get upset. Phyl: Cause it's all about having some fun, with a bunch of usually really cool people, right guys? Roberto: Or as close as Phyl can get to cool. Phyl: I take back everything nice I just said. ( Roberto chuckles but continues on ) Roberto: Now just to lighten the mood a little more, Jason here has a complimentary gift to send to our self centered amigo ... Jason show him what we have for him. (As modeled by GCW's own Disco Duck, the aforementioned man of honor recieves his prize. A shirt reading simply 'Ducking Retarded'. All three smile and nod.) Jason: I'll bet it's the perfect size for him, too Roberto: Nah his head won't fit. Phyl: I've worn worse..... But you're right Rob, he'd DEFINATELY stretch out the neck hole, maybe even tear it. Roberto: ( with a smile ) Well folks it's been fun but thats all the time we have for this segment. Remember this is a game ... enjoy have fun, and always... use your brain! Guys any closing remarks ? Phyl: I'm not gay, I swear...but I could definately use another slice, and a beer, a beer would be great. Jason: Bite me you piece of shit dirtbag. (Oddly enough Jason wears the largest grin on his face as he makes his closing remarks.) Phyl: I'm hurt Jason: not you Phyl Phyl: Oh, wait THAT guy Roberto: So from these guys to THAT guy ... Have a wonderful stay and ... uh ... reach for the stars. Jason: Yeah. Right here buddy. (Jason flicks off the camera.) ( That same white screen flashes again and the letters GCW come back. ) 'THis was a Caldera Enterprises Production'
