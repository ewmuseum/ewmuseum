Keepin' him in mind

5 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/5/05 *Narrorated by Blake Cassidy* Inside the Mind of Warz. Inside the mind of the beast known to all of GCW as Warz is quiet. Warz doesn't speak of his past, his family, or much of his emotions. So being that Im such a genius, I brewed up an idea! With my GCW signing bonus finally going through to my bank, and being that Im tired of sleeping in Tom's kitchen, I put a down payment for a house. Not the big 6 room 4 bathroom 18 kitchen mansion. But just a cozy, relaxing, house that I can now call...uh, home. Well it was two days after The Regulators big win, when I decided that in order for The Regulators to be a championship team...I need to find out about Warz more. So then my mission started. 9:00 p.m. Pst Warz' large Newport Beach Estate I was standing outside the gates leading towards Warz' property, when they finally opened. I walked halfway up his driveway when he stepped out to greet me at his porch... 'What the hell are you doing here Blake?!' I couldn't just tell him the real reason, so I thought up a clever excuse. 'Uhh...I need to borrow your restroom.' Of course he gave me the good ol' Warz blank stare. So I quickly added more information to make it seem valid. 'My house hasnt had the plumbing done yet, so Im out of a toilet and shower until Sunday. You dont mind do you?' The look he gave me was the warmest welcoming look Ive ever seen him give. 'Yeah, I mind! Go to Tom's house!' Warz jabbed back. But deep down inside of Warz, he meant 'Oh sure buddy, stay as long as you want.' So without any further words, I walked right into his house. 'Blake, I know we're a team and all...but that's at work! I don't want to see you any more than I have to...got it?' Warz yells to me. I quickly came back with this, 'Warz, we have a big match Thursday! And this is how you're acting? You're the one who said you're 'ALL ABOUT WORK'. You told me to change my attitude...and now Im saying for you to change yours! You're gonna have to put up with me, and Im gonna have to put up with you. Thats it! Now let me use your shitter....PLEASE!' Warz didnt say a word. He just stood there...looking at me. Finally he broke the silence. 'Alright already. GO! Use the damn bathroom...but make it quick! AND THEN YOURE GOING HOME!' 'Fair enough Warz!' Then it was time to strike! 'By the way, what's your real name...cause I know its not Warz?' 'Does it really matter Blake?' 'Think of the team Warz!' 'Its irrelevant to the team!' 'Warz! Teamwork!' 'Argh...I was born with the name Wayne Narz. There! You happy?' I held back my laugh, and then continued. 'Well...why didnt you choose that name to wrestle under? Why Warz?' 'No reason at all!' Warz quickly replied. He was hiding something! 'Warz...?' 'Alright...alright! I didnt keep my original name because back in grade school the kids would say my name really fast...and then run off giggling.' 'So? Wayne Narz...wayne-narz...' I didnt understand him, until... 'Wieners! Thats what they called me! Okay you happy? So I took the first two letters of my first name and added the last two letters of my last name, and came up with Warz! There...I hope you sleep better at night!' Cha-ching! At that point I knew I had him. 'Its okay Wayne...' I said. 'Its Warz!' He spit back. 'Sorry Warz. But thats not as bad as I had it. They used to call me Piss Pants. And Im sure I dont have to tell you why.' 'I feel you Blake. Kids can be so cruel at times!' 'Uh, Warz...that was in High School.' Once Warz laughed at that, I knew it was going to be a long night.
