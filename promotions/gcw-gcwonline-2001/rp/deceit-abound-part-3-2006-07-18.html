Deceit Abound: Part 3

18 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/18/06(Takes place August 2005) August 25th 2005, 11:05PM At any given minute now, the final bell on Global Wrestling weekly show, Evolution, was due to sound, signaling the end of Tessa Windsor’s brief stay. And at any given minute, Tessa herself had prayed that the man that ran it all, Bubba McCoy, or Christian Zenith for that matter, would walk in, laugh hysterically, and tell her the jokes over. So she watched the second hand on her pink Prague make another revolution. Then another. And another. And another. Then it became obvious that to both Zenith and McCoy, she was nothing but a number. There were no tearful goodbyes, or even a half hearted good luck, from even her closet friends Dawn and Vivica. She was on her own now, and the final bell she’d hear in a Global Wrestling arena had already rung. If those two men weren't willing to show up, though, one man was. Clad in black latex, he watched her carefully, before finally saying something. 'Leaving,' the man in the mask asked, curiously. 'It's a shame to see you go, Tessa.' Turning her head back, she looked to see who her one apparent friend around here was. And to no surprise, it was Emoticon. Granted it made her feel like even more of an ass that half the time she was here, she’d brushed off his presence, or steered clear away from him, because of everything she’d heard about him from the Hunting Ground. Shyly, she replied, “Don’t worry about it”.I’m sure it’s for the better anyways.” 'What are you going to do now,' he asked, curiously. 'You’re too talented to not be wrestling. Find anyplace else to go’wait, I’m being gauche. Need any help carrying anything?' The Soul of the Poet was nothing if not courteous. Eyeing her gym bag around her shoulder, she contemplated whether or not she should take him up on the deal, until she stopped dead just inches before walking into the exit. Then, she figured she might as well take him up on the offer, “Sure?” Tossing her gym bag over, she lead the way out the door, offering few words but the answer to his question, “You’re too kind. I haven’t found any place yet. I’m in no rush though, figured maybe I ought to stay at home with the kids anyways?” Emoticon caught it deftly, slinging it over his shoulder. 'I know some places you could go, if you like. I’ve got connections with a few places, like the UWF.' Looking up from her purse, she offered little gratitude there. “Already been offered, and declined. Don’t feel like making the same mistake twice.” 'And what mistake would that be, ' he asked, reaching up to rub some of the facepaint from the other portion of his face. Returning to her purse to fish out the keys to her rental, she made sure to hide her face well in the process, “Nothing worth revisiting, just the same old relationship crap I’ve been through before.” 'Storm,' came the one word inquiry from the Soul of the Poet, who by now had taken off his mask to rub the white makeup from his face. Of course, in doing so, he'd forgotten that no one was supposed to know who he was. Not only did her head perk up, but her eyes and ears did as well, nearly causing her to drop her keys in the process. After all, she’d plenty of reason to be in the shock she was in right now. “Uh yeah”..how’d you know?” Not only did this man apparently know about the constant problems between her and Chris, but this man apparently also was Seymour Almasy. 'Fuck. Forgot I’m supposed to be a secret.' He looked at Tessa, shaking his head at his own error. 'So, um, hi?' She’d already met the man on a few occasions, one of which was to wrestle, and yet somehow, she still found herself totally speechless, “Hi.” Then she’d realized what she’d just seen, and panicked all the more, “I swear I wont tell anyone, I promise!” 'Thanks,' he replied, and meaning it. 'That means a lot. But seriously, Tess, you're too good to just stay at home. What's wrong with UWF? Chris giving you crap again?' “No?” She answered, till she realized she sounded too hesitant for it to sound like the actual truth. “But chances are if I were around UWF, he’d begin to. And I don’t want to ruin things for us again like that.” 'Storm's an asshole,' he replied. 'Plain and simple. But if you want to go, I don't think he'd dare do anything. They've been talking to me about coming back too. To manage someone.' “Really?” Call it a bit selfish, but on the mention of him being around, she figured maybe she could take him up on that, “Do they already have someone in mind?” 'Not really,' he said, a small grin on his face, 'but they said they'd trust my judgment if I found someone...' “Better not take me then,” she giggled as she wondered why he’d even want to extend someone with a track record like hers an offer, “wouldn’t want to tarnish your image around there. You’ve got a great thing going for yourself.” 'Oh, come now,' he said, grinning, attacking the smaller competitor with a very Emoticonish glomp. 'We'd have fun, and you know it.' Half grinning herself, she decided to throw in a little tease of her own, “Maybe we would’but then again, I can’t go without a guarantee.” 'Of what,' he asked, cocking his head to one side. Eyeing her rental out of the corner of her eyes, she led the chase to her car, and to Seymour’s offer, both mentally and physically. “That I’d actually enjoy my time there, and benefit from it. After all, I’m tired of the whole losing bit.” Almasy quirked an eyebrow, and looped his arm with hers. 'UWF's a fun place. Besides, I can help you train. I know how to wrestle there, know what it's like. Besides...I can train you to beat Chris. Get revenge for both of us, you know?' It sounded like a good offer, but she still turned herself around to face him and make one thing clear. Even was if it all came out as one giant laugh. “I don’t need to beat him in order to enjoy it there...but I’ll take you up on that.” And just like that, the deal was sealed. Shame there was no sort of guarantee that it’d benefit her in the long run?
