The Tattered Cape, The Second Thread

30 August 2010

Date: 8/30/10 Last time... It might have been a tale of two very different Fox’s, in two very separate galaxies, but rest assured; the message remained the same. Whether he’s a seven-year-old child in the late 60’s, or a greasy-fingered knob in modern society, Clyde Fox is forever doomed to be... a hero. Whether he likes it or not, that will be his burden to carry with him for the rest of his life. The only catch is that no matter what timeline he is in, no matter what situation he gets himself into, he will almost always be oblivious to the fact that he is indeed the hero of the story. He’d even go as far as pretending to be a superhero, when in reality, he actually is. He’s just not smart enough to recognize it yet. But that was last time. This is now... Clyde Fox. Age 23. Current universe. 2010. The wind whisks by as papers cyclone down the baron streets of Las Vegas. Yes, they are indeed baron streets. Or at least they are in this section of town. Las Vegas always has a great night life no matter where you go in the city, however, things can’t be said the same for in the mid afternoon. Odds are everyone is still comfortably in bed, recovering from last night’s binge drinking fest. Either that or they are just preparing for another crazy night ahead. “Arrrrgggg,” A cranky Clyde Fox yawns. He scuffs the bottoms of his shoes against the warm pavement. The sun dodges around all the tall downtown buildings and beats down on Clyde’s lanky body. He steps into the shadow of a massive business building and embraces the cool fresh touch of the wind. “STOP!” “DON’T MOVE!” “GET HIM!” Clyde stops dead in his tracks as his ears perk up after hearing three separate voices blurt out dialogue faster than blinking. Clyde shoots his eyes down the nearby alleyway and listens for anything else. Nothing. He continues back on his way to The High Spirit Casino before being stopped by another random set of sounds. BRAKKA-BRAKKA-BRAKKA! SLIT! “UGH!” “Double you tee eff is going on in there?” Fox mumbles. The sounds were similar to that of a gun being shot and a knife slicing up against concrete. Not shaken, nor stirred, Clyde decides it’s a grand idea to hop scotch down the nearest alley and investigate. He blindly turns the corner and witnesses a pack of three men hovering over a battered woman. Yes, even though it’s two in the afternoon, there seems to be some sort of back alley assault in progress. What the heck is going on here? Why are there three biker-looking dudes on top of this lady? And furthermore, why is her makeup all smeared? Her clothes look tattered... her lip looks busted... and it doesn’t look like she’s wearing any under-drawers. And why does that guy have his juice loosener hanging fwee? Clyde’s eyes pulse in shock, as he can’t believe what he sees. Three men are clearly gang raping this whore. Granted though, she is a whore. So who cares? Maybe she deserves it. Maybe she asked for it. Maybe she owes them money. Maybe she likes to be treated this way. The world has never been a black and white place and this situation oozes with that exact sentiment. But being the hero that Clyde is, he can only see it one way; black and white. That’s just how most heroes call the law. This is no exception. Three men. One girl. Game on. “HEY!” Clyde screams, getting their attention... Clyde Fox. Age 45. Alternate universe. 2324. “Check the thrusters,” Clyde directs. CLICK! “Check. They are golden,” Another voice responds. “Sweet,” Clyde acknowledges. The two of them float there. Zero gravity isn’t exactly something you can ever train for. And for astronaut, Clyde Fox, he has never really gotten used to it. This wasn’t his first space mission after all. He’s been an astronaut for close to seventeen years now, and he’s loved every second of it. The job has had its ups and downs but for the most part, this has been a life full of great experiences. “Wana play space pong again?” Buddy asks. That’s Buddy, Clyde’s longtime astronaut partner. He’s a couple years younger than Clyde but they get along as if they were twins. Clyde has always taken a liking to Buddy because of how much Buddy reminds him of himself. You could almost say Clyde’s taken Buddy under his wing to a certain point. After all, they spend long missions out in space together. What else are they going to do other than bond as friends? “Sure. Space pong it is. Let’s use the empty tomato can from lunch this time,” Fox suggests. Buddy snatches the can from the magnetized lunch table and head-butts it over Clyde’s way. Fox flails his arms to try and gain some momentum towards the can but can’t make it in time as the can just floats on by. “SCORE! ONE NOTHING, ME!” Buddy jokes. Fox looks back at Buddy and smiles. “Lucky shot,” Fox excuses. Clyde gently corrals the can and tosses it back towards Buddy. Clyde Fox. Age 23. Current universe. 2010. “And who the fuck are you!?” One gang banger immediately turns and shouts towards Fox. Clyde begins to tremble as he realizes that he shouted by impulse. He couldn’t help himself. He heard some strange noises and decided against his better judgment (as he usually does) and now finds himself standing as a witness to what could be a rape incident. “Uhhh, ummm, ohhh,” Clyde stammers. “HELP ME!” The hooker shouts before getting fed a knuckle sandwich. Clyde takes another impulsive step forward. No one hits a woman. Especially in front of Clyde Fox. But he quickly backs down as all three men, even the one with his wiener hanging out, align in front of the whore as if begging the lanky lad to take another step forward. “What the fuck are you doing, man?” The roughest dude of the three asks with genuine emotion, “Do you have a fucking death wish or something coming back here alone?” Fox brings his hand up to the side of his head. He rubs his temple as he begins to feel the pounding from inside. His legs start to shake. He doubles over in pain. The three gang bangers just look on in astonishment. Clyde Fox. Age 45. Alternate universe. 2324. The can gains some considerable speed as it flies by Buddy and hits the nearby supply cabinet unit. The latch on the hit door shatters, allowing some supplies to float freely in zero gravity. Buddy and Clyde get a good chuckle in as their most recent game of space pong didn’t go quite according to plan. “Haha, oh boy, Clyde. This game sure wasn’t a defensive game of space pong,” Buddy jokes as he collects some of the floating materials. “Not at all,” Fox replies, “Which is kind of odd because last time we went at least three rounds before one of us scored.” They float around and gather up all the goods. Clyde looks over and sees Buddy holding some sort of book. He swims over to join Buddy. “Whatcha got there?” Fox asks. Buddy holds up the book and Fox nods nostalgically. “I forgot I packed that!” Fox says, holding back some happiness. It’s an old Commander Skip Vignette comic book circa 2295. One of the last ones to be printed on actual paper. “I didn’t know you were a big Skip Vignette fan, Clyde,” Buddy inquests. “Yup. I still am. I always wished I could be a great superhero like Commander Vignette,” Fox nods, “Tis why I became an astronaut. Clyde takes the book from Buddy, glances at it for a mere moment before tossing it behind himself. “But that’s the past,” Fox says in a lower tone, “Everything I ever did could never seem to measure up to anything he did.” Fox rests his arm against the wall, accidently hitting a switch. CLICK! WARNING! WARNING! WARNING! “What was that!?” Buddy asks. Clyde looks down and notices his just accidently hit the decompression keypad. “Shit... we’re in trouble,” Fox says as he stares into Buddy’s eyes. Clyde Fox. Age 23. Current universe. 2010. “ARRRRRRRRRRR- RUN AWAY WHILE YOU CAN!” Fox shrieks louder than a wolf howling at the moon as he just can’t take the pain inside his head anymore. He bursts out of his lowered stance like a sprinter in a race. He darts towards the men and crashes into them head on. They all fall back on the assaulted hooker as Fox wildly whales away at them. He pummels the middle guy into a quick bloody pulp before getting pulled off by the wiener gangbanger. “LOOK OUT!” The whore screams from under the pile as the wiener man strikes Fox in the kidneys. He doesn’t even flinch as he simply turns as breaks the gangbanger’s jaw with a shot across his face. Clyde then turns to the last one left conscious. It takes less than a split second before he defecates himself and runs away screaming. Clyde promptly returns to normal as if his “video game rage bar” seemingly runs out. He brushes the dust off his shoulder and doesn’t go anywhere near the slimy slut. “Thank you! Oh my gosh, thank you kind sir! They were... they were, ummm... well you know... and you just came in and saved me! Who are you!?” The lady asks. Clyde doesn’t reply as he turns his back to her and begins climbing up a nearby set of stairs, towards the closest apartment building roof. He might be silent, but the symbolism would be more than clear. Upon reaching the top of the stairs, a nearby clothesline has freshly washed garments fluttering in the wind. The one hovering right above Clyde is a tattered blanket. Symbolic of a cape. The woman looks up and from her vantage point it looks as if he’s wearing it. “WELL THANK YOU! THANK YOU FOR HELPING ME!” She shouts one last time. Fox just shakes his head before disappearing onto the rooftop... Clyde Fox. Age 45. Alternate universe. 2324. “What do we do!?” Buddy asks frantically. “We have to go and manually close the hatch before it completely decompresses and opens! We’ll lose all our oxygen is we don’t!” Fox informs. The two astronauts make haste down the hallway, as it only seemed fitting that Clyde would make such a mindless mistake just after rediscovering a former beloved icon. Commander Vignette held so many stances in Fox’s life. It didn’t matter from what era or what universe it was. Clyde always had mixed feelings towards Skip. Partly because he knew he wasn’t real. “Grab the latch!” Fox screams. Buddy and Fox arrive at the main exit hatch of which they were leaking oxygen out into the dense vacuum of outer space. “It won’t close!” Buddy shouts as the sound of oxygen flying through a pinhole increases in intensity. “HOLD ON!” Clyde yells. With a quick turn, Fox throws on an oxygen pack and tosses one to Buddy. He puts it on as they work on the door, but it’s no use. “What if I try to work on it from the outside!?” Buddy asks. “Are you kidding!? You’ll get yourself killed!” Clyde declines. They keep pulling on the door in hopes of closes the small open gap. Just then, a loud creaking noise rings throughout the entire space station. “MOVE!” Buddy shouts as he shoves Clyde backwards. SNAP! SHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHOOOOOOOOOWWWMMMMMMMMM! And just like that, the door comes flying off its hinges! Clyde grabs a nearby pipe and watches helplessly as Buddy is sucked into the empty vacuum of space. It’s literally milliseconds before the security hatch closes and seals Fox safely inside the space station. He throws off his oxygen unit and pastes his mug against the window. He sees nothing. Buddy is gone. Clyde slumps to the floor and mires in his own self-hatred. “What the hell did I do!? Buddy! NOOO!” Fox cries as his emotions get the better of him. All the while, the ever presence of Commander Skip Vignette looms larger in the background than anyone can notice. At the same time and at different times, Clyde Fox is both the world’s hero, and his own arch villain.
