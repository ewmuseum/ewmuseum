Complacancy

28 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/05/06 14:00pm 28th May 2006. Chicago, Illinois. Some Local Gym. Four hours into his workout, McKail was only just beginning to feel the callous talons of fatigue finally take a grip of his already battered and beaten body. He was just warming up and he could - no, would - do this all day. After this weeks edition of GCW Worldwide, the events that had took place fuelled him for this marathon workout, which for all intents and purposes, would last until the owner of the gym kicked his ass out onto the streets. His first defeat within Global Championship Wrestling, to a whorish woman who’s blatantly using the wrestling industry to open certain doors within the world of professional glamour. McKail fought the urge to be sick and clumped his two weights down into their allotted slots, in order to wipe away the sweat from his eyes with his small workout towel. Before entering the match with Danielle Northsky, McKail actually questioned whether he should hold back in the ring against the woman. Of course, he understood that she was a superb athlete. He knew full well that in the end she could get the job done, no matter what opponent the sadistic bookers threw at her. This was sufficiently proven to him when she’d proudly brandish the title from her previous long-dead wrestling organisation and despite the belts lack of standing within the GCW, it still meant something, to both of them. His mind didn’t consider the decidedly uncharacteristic waver in conviction solely because of her wrestling skills, nor her gender. He did so because for the first time since returning to the world of wrestling, he couldn’t actually say with any kind of conviction, he’d do anything to get the job done. In his previous matches, in the matches pitted against solely male combatants, he did everything and anything he could to win the match. Punching, biting, thumbs to the eyes or even the low blow. The morality of the action didn’t bother him, he just wanted to get it done - he just wanted to succeed. Coming face to face with a woman in the squared circle put his need for success into a new light. Some primitive urge, deriving from the dawn of human sentience, to protect the females from all danger. To treat women with respect. To allow no harm to come to them. Just thinking about Mary and the integrity she carried around with her on a daily basis, the integrity he now took on in honour of her memory, made him question his very tactics. The tactics that’d proven more than effective thus far within his tenure for the GCW. Could he really step into the ring and use all of the dirty tricks that’d come so easy to him? How far would he go to get the victory his heart so desired? All of those were questions and if they were still in his head by the time he’d made it to the ring, he would’ve readily attributed his loss to those very reasons. However, before the bell had even rung, his mind was clear of any such distraction, though not by his own doing. Backstage, deep within the heart of the arena, he and Northsky met face to face and during that enlightening encounter he came to the conclusion that this Northsky woman wasn’t worth his restraint. She wasn’t worth wasting his integrity on. She was fair game. At the end of the night, it came as a harsh reality to him that she’d come out of the entire thing victorious. It came as a harsh reality to him because deep down he knew that he didn’t lose because he was holding back, he lost because she was better than him. It was a very difficult think for him to admit, even to himself, but as soon as the thought materialised in his mind, he knew it to be the truth. A hard pill to swallow, but he had to swallow it nonetheless. McKail placed his sweat towel around the back of his neck and made for the indoor running track, taking up a modest, but steady, pace immediately upon reaching it. True enough, he took the loss to Northsky more to heart than perhaps he should’ve done, but he considered that to be the reason why he was destined for greatness within the GCW, rather than common mediocrity he’d experienced so many times in so many wrestling organisations before. He took this loss to heart, but he didn’t allow it to get on top of him. Instead, he took it as a sign. A sign that he was getting more than a little complacent. Hence the marathon workout session. He allowed his training schedule to slide in order to drink from the challis of success and it had come back to haunt him. He could allow this no longer. Complacency will no longer be an issue to him now, no matter how much success heads his way and if he’s so determined to gain his measure of success, he was glad this lesson had been taught to him at this formative stages of his run for ultimate dominance. Sure there’d be other lessons, at other times, but this one was pretty damned important and to understand it’s meaning now would provide him the edge he needed to reach the next level and the next level of his career begins next week at Worldwide 49. Now he was prepared to embrace the future for whatever it would bring.
