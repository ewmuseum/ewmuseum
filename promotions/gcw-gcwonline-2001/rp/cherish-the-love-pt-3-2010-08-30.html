Cherish The Love Pt. 3

30 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/30/10 Rampage 2010 Main Event 1… 2…. 3…. RRRRRRRRRRRRRRRAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHH Cue an explosion of flashbulbs and a defeated whimper in Clyde Fox. Sprawled out in the middle of the ring stomach first across Fox, Tessa Windsor could hardly process just what was going on around her. She had heard the bell ring. She had felt Fox’s body twitch in some sort of attempt to revive itself. She had tasted the blood, sweat, and tears that trickled down her face and encompassed the corners of her lips. Her eyes opened ever so slightly, desperately trying to process it all. The only thing any part of her body could process was the surrealism of the moment. This HAD to be a dream. “Holy crap.” She blinked, trying to clear her eyes of the hundreds of illusionary white boxes that overwhelmed her vision. Anytime she figured her sight was finally credible enough to process just what was playing out before her very eyes another camera snapped, and snapped, and snapped. She imagined that maybe Jay Terror had curb stomped her brain into an oblivion, or that she had passed out in the car on the way to Rampage and dreamt up the entire evening. It all seemed entirely plausible until a firm hand planted on her shoulder, shaking her back into reality. “Congratulations Champ.” Then it all sank in at once, starting first with the sheer amount of exhaustion she was in. Her brain had managed to walk out intact enough to be aware that the belt was not going anywhere. Before she had the chance to reach out to grab what she had spent the entire evening working towards, her body collapsed under its own weight. Boy was she going to sleep good tonight. “Oh my god.” She inhaled, replaying the entire scene in her head. There was no way she was going to let her body was win out this time. She forced herself to her feet, recollecting what little breath she had left. “….come on now…” Before she had the chance to exhale she was pulled in the opposite direction by a man she figured would be the last to want anything to do with her right about now. Well, besides Jay Terror that was. “You won Tessie!” Even with Clyde Fox’s hand extended out her way, she could hardly believe it. Instead of shaking it, she jumps towards him with her arms open wide. She figured by this point, if this were a dream, she would have leapt right through Fox and woken up with her husband’s arm slung around her tiny frame. She closed her eyes, still expecting Chris to whisper something about her taking all the covers. Instead, a fountain of tears trickled down her cheek as she clung onto Fox, relishing in the moment. This was as real as it gets. *** Rampage 2010 Backstage “Holy crap.” Tessa had no clue whether that was victory speaking, or Terror’s curb stomp finally starting to wear in. All she knew is her head was spinning, and it didn’t feel good. She sprawled out across the bench inside her locker room back first, praying her husband was almost ready to leave. Instead, he wandered around the locker room with his cell phone glued to his ear, occasionally nodding along in agreement with whoever it was on the other line. “Chris?” She figured being helplessly sprawled out across a bench clad in a form fitting V-cut shirt and hip hugging jeans would elicit her husband’s attention. Instead he pressed his index finger to his lips to stop her from cutting back in. “Just a favor?” She whispered, her index finger extended half way. Instead her husband’s palm turned upward as he turned to pace the other way. If she could have narrowed her eyes without speeding up the world around her, she would have. Instead she sealed them shut completely. She imagined maybe he was planning some kind of special getaway for them. She’d always talked about how they’d celebrate if they won. A bottle of champagne, a white sand beach, seclusion, it all sounded really good right about now. “Tomorrow?” Chris inquired to the person on the other line. He sounded as if he’d been caught a bit off guard with that remark. He gritted his teeth then nodded again, “Yeah. Tomorrow could work.” Tomorrow sounded damn well to Tessa too. She figured by tomorrow her body would need any and every kind of massage she could get after the fight she had put up to take World Title home. “There’s a flight out of Bejing to Moscow at seven tomorrow morning? Perfect.” The Moscow bit did not sound nearly as perfect. But she figured as long as there was a spa somewhere around, she could make the best of it. They had been to plenty of worse places after all. “Alright man, thanks.” Tessa bounced to her feet. It probably was not the wisest of ideas with everything around her refusing to stay still. But she imagined, or at least hoped, Chris would catch her if she were about to hit the floor. The moment he turned around Tessa nestled up against his chest. Her baby blues glowed with excitement as she looked up his way. “Is now a good time for me to admit you were right?” He shrugged. Tucking his cell phone away into the pocket of his carpenter jeans, he looked down with his own half smile. “That one’s up to you champ.” She paused to think on it. With a haughty little smile she looked back up at her husband, proud as she had ever been. “Well…nah I won’t. I think you were rooting for Clyde the whole time. Would have saved you at least some pride, right?” He quietly chuckled. “That’s just mean.” “I learn from the best.” “Exactly.” He smirked, eyeing the title on top of her gym bag from the corner of his eyes. “Meaning by default that I should be the world champ, since I’m one hundred percent responsible for everything you learned.” “You’re full of it.” His grin grew exponentially. Clearly his mind was in the gutter. “I’ll make you full of something too...” “That’s just plain wrong.” Her head shook side to side. She pushed away playfully and headed for both her title and gym bag. “We ready to go now perv?” “In much of a rush?” “You really want to stick around here?” Tessa was plenty confused about that, especially with the press lurking around. She figured if anything Chris would want to get back to the hotel where they could have some peace and quiet without having to worry about mouthy colleagues and snoopy reporters. Some time alone would have been nice too. Chris already had his own plans though. “I figured it’d be a good time to talk since you’re in such a good mood.” Tessa’s eyebrow perked straight up. “About?” “You know.” Her other brow lifted. Clearly she had no clue. “I do?” As much time as he had spent that evening preparing what to say in his head, Chris had not a single idea what to say from there. She was on cloud nine, he did not want to impede on that. Not when she had worked so hard for that very moment. He waved it off, reaching out for the door. “Forget about it. We need to celebrate.” He had forgotten all about his wife’s curious nature. It was impossible for Tessa to just ’forget’. Much like their kids, Tessa tugged Chris’ arm pulling him back towards the room as she plead. “I want to know, please tell me?” “Nah. We’ll talk when we finally get back home. It’s nothing special, I promise.” Curiosity had already taken over the World Champ. Her beautiful baby blues popped wide open as she looked up his way. He should have never looked down to acknowledge her. From the very millisecond he locked onto them he was a goner. Now he had no choice but to proceed on. “Just tell me now. It can’t hurt any, can it? I mean shoot, what’s the worst you’ve got?” You need time off? Because I’m totally fine with that. People get hurt in this business all the time.” “Nah, I’m fine. I’ve taken far worse beatings. ” “I’m not really sure about that one.” From the corner of her eye Tessa glanced over at his heavily taped ribs. It was pretty obvious why she was convinced otherwise. He turned back her way just in time to see her grimace as her eyes trailed over the outskirts of his wounds. “You sure you’re okay?” She questioned again, this time trailing her fingers alongside the gashes. He nodded before instinctively swiping her hand away. Understandably she seemed a bit insulted, before she could turn for the door though he pulled her by the pit of her elbow back towards the bench closest to them. Plopping down first he gently guided Tessa down beside him by the waist. It was obvious her husband was uncomfortable, but Tessa chalked it up to nerves. As most already knew, Chris was never too good with expressing himself. That was why it was Tessa who had to break the ice. “So what do we need to talk about?” Even after having rehashed everything he had wanted to say then and there, Chris’ head deterred off to the side. There were plenty of ways he could have could have phrased it, but none of them seemed proper enough for a woman who had stuck by him for years through thick or thin. “Maybe we should talk about this later.” “Why later?” “Look…” He sighed as he turned back her way, feeling like the biggest prick in the world. She had just accomplished what was easily one of her biggest dreams yet here he was, her own husband, about to piss all over it with one little sentence. “Well? What is it?” His eyes closed before he exhaled. It was the only way he would be able to sleep that night. “We need to talk about those pills Tessa.” It caught her off guard to say the least. She blinked before slinging her gym bag over her shoulder. Here she was, half expecting her husband to burst out laughing as if this were another one of his untimely practical jokes. She figured he needed to get it out of his system after the way he had been acting the past few weeks. Instead disappointment and dismay carved its presence across her husband stone face. “The pills…” She thought aloud. This was no way she was going to let this was happen here. Not now. Not if she had any power over it.“…tonight? Of all nights Chris?” He shrugged. She was the one who had promised to address the issue with him not even a week ago; funny how quick she seemed to forget that when things were going well. “You promised me we would.” “After Rampage!” “You won the Main Event, did you not?” He had her there, though she’d never admit it. Instead she stood to her feet, refusing to look his way. “Give me a chance to enjoy it, please? You know how much this means to me Chris.” He shrugged, passing the ball off again. “You were the one that asked.” “Why were you even thinking about it in the first place?” “I told you to forget about it for now.” “Forget about it? Maybe you ought to take your own advice there.” Marrying the most stubborn man on the planet certainly helped her chances there none. When Chris Storm thought he was right, there was no convincing him otherwise. All you had to do is ask GBJ of that. “That’s pretty hard considering you promised me Tessa.” He stopped himself there. That was probably the weakest comeback he’d ever come up with, though it always worked for the kids. He tried again, this time a bit more emphatically. “It’s not like I’m going to bite.” It was not the sweetest way to approach the subject, but what would you expect from a man notorious for his bad temper? Based on the way Tessa pursed her lips it was obvious that she wanted a lot better reasoning than that. “I already told you I don’t have a problem. What else do you need to know?” “If that’s what you insist.” Chris shrugged yet again. “I just don’t understand how….” He stopped himself while ahead this time. Too bad for him Tessa knew exactly how he planned on finishing that sentence. That right there was waging forth a completely unfair battle. A battle Tessa wanted no part of. “…you don’t think every parent makes mistakes Chris? Honestly?” “I know that. I’m not suggesting you feel embarrassed over it, Tess.” Embarrassed far from summed up how GCW’s newest World Champ felt after having found out that her youngest had stumbled upon the Oxycotin she had been prescribed. Tessa was mortified. She knew how quick people were in this business to jump to conclusions. She would have liked to believe her husband knew her better than that. Yet there he was laying the cards out on the table again. She could hardly believe her ears. “Then what do you want? For me to admit I screwed up and almost cost my daughter her life? We already established that two weeks ago.” “I’m not trying to blame you for that Tessa! You weren’t even there when it happened.” It was completely the wrong choice of words there on Chris’ behalf. In a matter of seconds the ecstatic woman that had walked through the door not even twenty minutes ago had suddenly been replaced with a woman scorned. “You know why I wasn’t there, don’t you?” What was intended as innocent suggestion consumed the very essence of his angel right in front his eyes. Tessa refused to even look his way. “Because I thought it’d be nice to actually have some time alone with the man I married. You know, the man who I’m lucky to spend an hour with each week because he insists on keeping busy away from home?” He held his arms up in surrender. “I don’t want to fight Tessa. You’ve got to believe me.” If she would have at least bothered to look his way it would have done him some good. Instead Tessa stood at the door scrambling through her mind for any kind of way to get out of this mess. “Then let’s just drop it altogether, please?” “I can’t just do that.” “You would if it was you.” “What if it was me, then? Would you just drop it?” She was caught there, hook line and sinker. “That’s beside the point.” No amount of deterring would change the subject, but it would certainly buy her some time. “Would you?” She hurried back to her escape guide in search of another emergency route. Something more palatable, something he’d actually buy into. There was absolutely nothing. Tessa panicked. There had to be some way out of this. “I’ll talk…” Both stood there completely helpless. Chris hated doing this to her. He hated it more than anyone would ever believe. It was always the right thing always hurt the most. At least, that’s what he was taught. “….to the media. I’ll finally make a statement about it.” Or maybe it was when the right thing was avoided that one’s heart crumbled the most. Chris swore it had to be more along those lines. “You don’t HAVE to do that, Tessa.” “It’s what you want, isn’t it?” He refused to grace that with an answer. Instead he watched on with a heavy heart as his wife tossed the World Title over her shoulders and marched on out of the room as if he didn’t exist. He had always preached to her the importance of independence, about learning to stand her own ground, and to never put up with anyone’s bullshit because she was better than that. Never in a million years did Chris Storm stop to think just how bad it would hurt when the one person he truly cared thought to use that against him. It hurt. But damn was he ever proud of her. *** If there was one thing Tessa had come to learn while on the road, it was that no amount of time was great enough to prepare to face the wolves that called themselves the press. It did not matter how many times you rehearsed a speech, what allegations you denied, or who was with you. When they wanted answers, they were going to get them. And if they failed to, they always made sure to make you regret every ounce of silence you batted their way. To Tessa, there was absolutely no sense in trying to avoid them. The more she refused to comment, the worse the scenario surrounding Aislinn started to sound. There was Oxycotin. There was her husband. There was Aislinn. And of course, there was Tessa. Just those four elements in themselves were enough to stir a dozen or so rumors. Then you add in the screaming, the tears, her other kids, the critical care unit and you get speculations that do not even sound feasible for a woman who spent a good amount being pursued by relentless reporters. And now there was the World Title to add in to that equation. This was certainly not going to be easy, but then again life rarely was. “Tessa!” “Ms. Windsor!” “Hey Tess!” “Champ!” Tessa had no clue which way to look first. Instead she peered down at the World Title slung over her shoulder and glanced back up with a sparkling smile. Flashbulbs illuminated the small press room inside the Toyko dome, making the World Champ’s location entirely apparent to everyone else backstage. All the while Tessa made sure to block Chris out of her mind the best she could. When it had taken him this long to get to the top, the last thing she wanted to be portrayed as was a scared little girl. “Ms. Windsor. Can you tell what it feels like to finally be on top?” “Tessa! I’ve got a ton of questions!” “Hey Champ! Look this way!” Somehow above all the commotion Tessa managed to get a word in edgewise. “Let’s make this quick, okay?” In a room littered with reporters, quick certainly was not the best choice of words. Quick, they were more than willing to work towards. Orderly, was a whole different ballgame. It took but only ten seconds for Tessa to be barraged by a sea of microphones, notepads, and cameras. Thank god her kids taught her all about handling commotion. “Whoa, one at a time please!” Her index finger extended upward, silencing the small crowd around her. She first pointed toward a buxom blonde reporter in a pencil skirt that reminded her of an older Sally Ford. “Tessa, can you tell us how it feels to finally be GCW’s World Champion?” It was an easy enough question. “It’s amazing.” The broad smile on her face said it all. “Really, I don’t know how else to phrase it. It’s easily the proudest moment of my career. It’s nothing short of incredible.” Her head drifted the other way, prompting plenty of flashes from those who managed to catch one of the most genuine smiles in GCW. As much as Tessa hated reporters, she could not deny feeling blessed to have one of the biggest instances of her life caught on film from multiple angles. Her finger drifted over in the direction an older man looking to be on the verge of retirement age. She figured he deserved a chance to speak, given that his inability to rally as rabidly as others around him likely caused him to be overlooked by other superstars. “What are your plans from here, Ms. Windsor?” “Good question.” Tessa complimented. “For one, I’m going to celebrate. Chris already made sure of that. From there? Well, who knows. But I certainly have no intentions on losing this anytime soon. Not when I’ve worked for years for this. Next?” This time a young man looking to be fresh out of college won Tessa’s attention. “Do you have any words on how your husband’s reaction to this? How does he feel about losing against the man you eventually went to on to beat?” She shrugged. “I’ll let Chris answer that one later. I’m not going to put words in his mouth. I’m pretty sure he’s disappointed some, but angry? I doubt it. I’m sure he’s plenty proud in what I’ve managed to overcome.” Next came an Asian woman in her early thirties who innocently inquired, “How about your knee, is that one hundred percent yet?” Tessa knew better than to conquer that head on. “It’s gotten better.” “Rumor is you’ve had to take a rather high dosage of prescription pills to cope with it, can you confirm or deny that?” Silence. “The fuck kind of question is that?” Before Tessa had a chance to formulate a comprehensible sentence the door closest to the platform flew open stealing all attention. Under any other circumstance Tessa would have been thrilled. It would have bought her time. It helped her seem far more sincere. But Tessa rarely had the benefit of living under normal pretenses. Instead she found her interview session being cut short by the booming presence of her husband, a man who insisted on keeping his private life out of the public eye. “Ms. Windsor?” The woman inquired again, completely disregarding the sudden appearance of the boisterous Storm. It was a really poor decision on her behalf. Just that one little innocent inquisition was more than enough to stir Storm’s ire. “Nuh-uh. Not happening. Not now, not ever. That’s not anyone’s business.” “Chris, its fine!” Tessa meagerly attempted to jump in. It did absolutely no good. Instead her husband jumped right back at her and the rest of the room. “We’re done here. We’ve got places to go, things to do. None of it involves any of this bullshit.” Tessa blinked. She had not put it past her to have Chris make an ass of himself, it’s what he was best at. But right here, right now? She was absolutely speechless. She would have liked to think he had more respect for her than that. She thought plenty about tussling. About ignoring everything her husband had to say. About opening up right then and there. That was until the headlines flashed in front of her eyes. Storm/Windsor Split Trouble In Paradise? Domestic Dispute. With Storm’s recent actions as of late, they all seemed pretty feasible. Especially given the show he was putting on now. Instead of warding Storm off, she sighed and followed suit with his actions. It was the only way she could escape without enduring one of her husband’s infamous temper tantrums. Wrapping his arms around his wife’s shoulder Chris pulled her alongside him to the door. He stopped at the door only to shout back at the visibly disgruntled crowd. “This shit is over!” Regardless of his wife’s earlier accomplishment it was Chris that got the final say. At least this time. *** “So what the hell was that all about?” As calm, cool, and collected as her demeanor managed to stay in front of fans and the media, Tessa Windsor was a force to be reckoned with by the time her and her husband arrived back at their hotel room. In the privacy of their own room, Tessa had plenty she wanted to get off her chest, and it started with Chris’ actions towards the media right before the couple departed the Toyko dome. Even as she laid sprawled out atop her husband’s battered chest exchanging affectionate glances in between pillow talk, Tessa had a way of commanding respect. Perhaps Chris Storm feared the consequences of neglecting to respond, or the self proclaimed Iron Man believed he was in the right. Either way he actually took it upon himself to provide his wife with what he saw as a reasonable explanation. “Saving your ass.” “Saving my ass?” "That’s exactly what it was.” As crazy as it sounded, it was hardly anything new to Tessa. According to Chris Storm, he was in some sense saving Tessa’s ass, even it hurt her more than half the time. As flawed as his logic was, Tessa had learned to accept and acknowledge it. The only thing she had yet to figure was how to approach these defects without somehow offending her husband. It’s why she always made sure to stop and think before speaking, even in this instance. “By cutting in and making an ass of yourself, as well as me, you’re saving my ass?” She paused again to regain her composure. “Can you explain to me how that works?” “They don’t care about you having the title Tessa. They were setting you up from the very start.” Chris was never good with words to begin with, let alone meaningful explanations. “Cause that’s everyone goal Chris. To screw me over, right?” He shrugged. He had no clue how she wanted him to respond to that. “It’s part of the reason it took you this long to get where you are tonight. Am I not right there?” She gave up. “I can’t believe you sometimes.” She had every right in the world to hate his guts. Yet instead of rightfully slapping the taste of her husband’s mouth Tessa nestled her face up against his and closed her eyes. It gave Chris the opening he had been looking for since their sudden departure from the Toyko Dome. He had plenty of unanswered questions of his own. “You know I love you Tessa, right?” “I do, even if you’ve got an odd way of showing it.” Her eyes slit open just enough to acknowledge his sentiments. “Then can you answer me this?” “What’s that?” “Why were you going to tell them about the pills?” She sighed. There was no right way to answer that. Not when her husband expected answers first and explanations later. “It just seemed right.” “To tell me about the pills through them rather than face to face?” “Yeah.” Her bottom lip curled inward. There was no way for her to put it any more gently yet above all else she still felt like shit for what was about to follow. “I wouldn’t expect you to believe me from the get go.” The back of her hand softly traced against his cheek. She prayed her wide eyes innocence would win him over. Anyone who knew Chris could tell you that his biggest weakness was his wives vulnerability. It was what drew them to this point in the first place. Chris hated the press. He hated them more than just about anyone. Couple that with the media trying to desecrate and diminish his angel into a human being devoid of morals and one could see that this was a disaster waiting to happen. Chris exhaled, pushing Tessa off his chest. The truth hurt enough in itself. Having to look her in the eye as it trickled out would crush him dead. “You’re afraid of me?” He rolled over onto his hips, away from her. Between losing to Clyde Fox and having Tessa frightened to the point where she refused to open up to him, Chris figured tonight would be the death of him. He might as well pass on comfortably, void of any guilt. It’s what their kids would have wanted. “I’m not afraid of you!” Her words would have been far more believable if she had thrown her arm around him in attempts to save him from despair. “I just….I don’t know….I let you down….I almost ended up killing our daughter… do you have any clue what that feels like?” “To watch on helplessly as my wife falls apart? Nah. I’ve got no idea at all how it feels. Clue me in there?” Tessa sighed. This was going nowhere fast. She laid there and pondered what it would be like to go back to night where Jay Terror decimated her knee. What if she would have fought harder? What if she would have been better prepared? If she would have put up a better fight and not been so focused on watching out for everyone else around her, would she have needed to nurse on pain pills just to take care of her kids? Jay Terror might not have beaten her that evening, but his handy work two weeks before sure had taken its toll on her emotionally. Silence had long become her only saving grace these past few weeks. “I love you.” It was the only thing she could think to say. It did little to tame her husband’s heavy heart. He cared too much to carelessly brush it off with watered down words of affections. Instead he fought back the only way he knew how, with concern. “You’re going to end up killing yourself Tessa.” “I’m fine. Can we just drop this please? We’re going away to Moscow for a few days to celebrate. Can we just enjoy that?” Chris would have gladly obliged if he had any clue what on earth she was talking about. “Moscow?” She grinned. Obviously this was one of his coy little games to try and win her back in his favor. It actually worked quite well. Her chest brushed up against his back as she slung her arms over side pulling him closer. Seduction was not her intent, but boy did it ever win over his attention. He need not look back her way to know exactly where her shirt end and chest begin. “You’re the worst at keeping secrets. You know that, right?” With a whisper, she innocently beckoned on. “I already know about Moscow. I can’t wait to finally get away from all this. We deserve this.” Chris Storm bit down on his tongue. He hadn’t a clue what to say. Here he had an angel with her arms wrapped him, whispering of how much she loved him, begging for his affection. Yet there he lay with a pair of scissors in hand waiting for just the right moment to clip her wings. It did not seem right. Chris knew it was not right. Yet if he wanted to keep her grounded, to keep her safe from the rousing winds of disappointment, he had no choice. “About that….” Her arms tightened around his chest. His presence was the only thing that mattered to her at this point. “Not the best choice? I know. But hey, it’s a vacation, right?” “Tess…” “You worry too much.” She smiled. “Besides…” “You’re NOT going Tessa.” He finally cut in. “That was for me, only me.” “What?” Chris had stated himself clear as day, but Tessa refused to comprehend a matter that made no sense. Not on a night like this. “Alone? Tomorrow?” “Yes. Alone.” “To Moscow? For?” “Business.” “Business.” She groaned. “Of course, what else would it be?” It sounded almost sarcastic. It was only a day removed from her birthday, and the very night of her big Rampage win, and Chris still had no time for her. For as much as critics accused Tessa of needing Chris to survive, it sure felt to her that she and the kids spent the better part of their time without him. In some sense, it had become their way of life. She rolled away, taking the blankets alongside her. It seemed like the only power she had over Chris these days. “How long’s this one going to last?” “I’m not cheating Tessa.” “I never said you were.” She huffed, her head collapsing onto the pile of pillows below. “I’d like to think you know better than that.” “Then stop acting like that.” “If you’d take more than ten minutes a week to spend with your family I gladly would!” It was far from the first time Chris had heard such a plea, and he knew damn well it would not be the last. Even with that bit of knowledge his instincts easily overpowered any bit of common sense that beckoned him to think twice before speaking. “Don’t act like I haven’t been trying Tessa! You know what happened to my plans last time? I spent the night in the fucking critical care unit because she got into this stash of pills that you still refuse to talk to me about! Is it MY fault that you’re drowning alone because you’re too damn stubborn to admit you’ve got a problem??” Any pain lingering in Tessa’s head after Jay Terror’s curb stomp subsided in exchange for what might as well been a fatal gunshot wound to the heart. She hoped her ears were deceiving her. That her husband merely got tongue tied and would backtrack faster than Roxie Sykes after having cost Tempest the Rampage finals. “I…” “Just stop Tessa. Stop now.” “I don’t have a problem, Chris. Please believe me!” “I can’t.” He stood, grabbing his shirt off the floor beside him. She watched in slow motion as he tossed it over his bare chest and headed for the door. If she thought his words were toxic to the heart, he was about to piss all over her dying spirit by grabbing his bags along the way. “Watching Markus fall apart was hard enough. I’m not watching you do the same just so you can ‘live your dream’.” “Please Chris?” The more she said it, the less her husband seemed to believe it. Nothing she could say or do seemed acceptable enough to believe. Here she was, not even twelve hours into being World Champ, ready to tap at the hands of her husband, a man who swore up and down the last few weeks to do her no harm. “Please?” He dared not look her way. He knew he would be a goner if her eyes merely watered up. Then again, Tessa was far from being any better off. If anything she felt backed into a corner. She was terrified. She felt insulted. But even more so she felt betrayed. She had single handedly took in his son from another woman as if it were her own. She put her own wellbeing on the line to try and salvage his friendship with Markus. No matter how many times he ran away, she always welcomed him back with open arms. Yet here he was ready to leave her high and dry, on the verge of tears, over one easily preventable mistake, a mistake that seemed bound and determined to haunt her forever. She panicked. Normally that was never good. But this evening, it might have finally set her straight and put her on track to take control of her own life, her own goals, and even her own destiny. “I swear to god if you walk out that door Chris you’re eventually going to lose the only thing you’ve got going for you.” He waved as he walked out. “See you in California then.” So much for taking control. *** Present “Thank for doing this for me, you really don’t know how much I appreciate it.” If there was one person in the world Tessa felt actually reciprocated the same amount of love and respect she poured out to others on a daily basis, it was Rachel Tyrell, known best to the world as Tempest. “Girl, you know it’s not a problem.” “Really. It means a lot.” And it did. If there was one thing in the world Tessa absolutely refused to fail at, it was being a mother. That said there was absolutely no way she was going to let her youngest go without some form of family around on her third birthday, no matter what Tessa had to do to pull off the stops. Luckily with Rachel being there to help, there really was not much else to pull off other than keeping the peace which was a job within itself some days. “Really it’s no big deal.” “It really is. You don’t know much you pulled through for me right now.” “It’s no big deal.”Rachel shrugged it off. She really did not see it as that big of a deal. She needed something herself to help her bounce back after the grueling match she had with Triumph Frost earlier in the week. “I’m sure you’d do the same for me.” “Course.” Tessa smiled. Rachel could not have been anymore right about that. Without question Tessa would have bent over backwards for the one companion she knew she could count on. After all, these days Rachel seemed to be about the only one not looking to take advantage of her. “Sides, you’ve got a moon bounce. How could I miss out on that?” Tessa’s lips curled even higher. It was not a wonder the two got along so well with a mentality like that. “Point taken.” At that mention her head drifted over in the direction of the inflatable contraption that spared her hours worth of trivial arguments. Not that she believed in hands off parenting, but sometimes she needed a break. With today being three years removed from the trials and tribulations of childbirth, it seemed as good of a day as any. Shit, if their father could get off the hook 300 days of the year, Tessa deserved at least three hours to herself while at home. “So how have those sweet little monsters of yours been holding up? Do they know where Chris is?” Tessa shrugged. She had not a clue and absolutely no desire to find any of that out. “They haven’t asked. Guess that means they know. I’ve kind of left that up to him to answer.” “Good choice.” “Yeah. I hope so.” “Think he’ll show up?” Her shoulder lifted again. She had not the faintest of clues. “If he remembers.” “Think he will?” “Course not.”Her answer to that was a lot more certain. She knew her husband’s scheduling tendencies, if anything he would remember the weekends were devoted to training his so called “protégé” Johnny. “You sure you’ll be okay?” Tessa feigned a tough smile. It was all she could do. “Not me throwing my life away for my career. Funny that I’m World Champ and I can still find time for my kids, yet he’s always on the run. It’s like he feels his life has no purpose unless he’s on the road.” “Need to hug it out?” “Nah. I’ll be okay. Promise.” “You sure?” Tempest pointed to her own neck, which Tessa knew had to be killing her best friend after the way Frost had handled her earlier in the week. “Don’t want Terror doing the same to you if you’re not focused.” “Shoot. I’d almost rather that than my knee again.” “Still hurting?” “Only when I’m off the pills. Your neck?” “Same, only without pills.” “You’re a much better person than me.” Tessa sighed, being careful not to sound too frustrated. The last she needed was to trouble Rachel anymore than she had Chris about the issue. “If it weren’t for the belt, I swear I’d never touch them again.” “Well, it's not like I never take anything.I just can't take them for too long. They make me too loopy. I mean, like beyond my usual loopy." She coaxed a smile out of Tessa. It the first time the World Champ had managed to laugh out of the topic since she had almost lost her youngest to nothing more than a sheer accident involving her Oxycotin. “I feel you there.” She thought back on the incident the almost claimed Aislinn’s life. If only she could have claimed to have the same side effects. It might have saved her from having to worry about defending her title pill-less, keeping a constant eye on her kids, and trying to save her marriage, all at the same time. “I need to stop.” “Does Chris know?” Rachel felt horrible interjecting, but as a fellow woman she could tell this was eating Tessa up. And she hated to see that, especially on a day where Tessa, Chris, and the kids should have been celebrating all together. “Nah. I can’t tell him. He’d kill me.” “He’s not doing that to you now by running away from his problems?” “He says its just business.” “I don’t want to jump to conclusions since I’ve got no problem with the guy, but he’s lying to you dear.” Tessa’s head dropped. “I know.” And that she actually did. But trying to call her husband out on any of that was a whole different story. She would never win that battle, much the same way she was never supposed to win the World Title. Instead, she basked in the silence of the moment praying that god would bestow upon her the same kind of power he had to help her become World Heavyweight Champion. “You need to tell him.” “Tell me what?” It was a voice neither of them expected to hear, especially on his own accord. Needless to say Chris Storm had no clue how to react when both women glared his way, waiting for him to make the first move. “Tessa?” He inquired again. Tessa looked up, only to catch glance of the unfashionable headpiece intended to protect his face from any further damage. She thought to comment on it till she realized it was as a result of him walking away from facing her. Then she figured, he deserves it. “Girls?” He prodded for a third time, clearly not satisfied that he was not getting any answers. It was Tessa that finally jumped in, but not to answer his question. “Wow. You actually bothered to show up.” “Why wouldn’t I?” There were plenty of answers to that. The question was which one was most acceptable in the presence of company. While Tessa had no problem tearing into Chris in front of Tempest, it probably would not look good on her behalf if it caught the attention of the other parents present. She eyed the small crowd around the moon bounce. For once, Chris got the message. “Save it for later.” “Yeah.” He nodded, focusing in on their kids. “Later.” Rachel caught his glance and knew exactly what he was suggesting. She jumped right in. It gave her the perfect outing from this awkward third wheel position. “Want me to watch them so you two can talk?” “N-“ “Yes.” Chris overrode. “If you’re up to the challenge.” “What he said.” Tessa jumped in, as if she intended to agree all along. “We’ll get the cake ready.” Tempest saw right through that but did not bother to call Tessa out on it. She realized what this opportunity meant for her best friend. It was a chance for redemption. A chance to prove she could rise above the challenges of the business, be a champion, and still manage to keep her family intact.That is, if Tessa would confess. “Not a problem.” Tempest assured. The moment Chris turned his back and headed for the house she was sure to mouth to Tessa her concerns. “Tell him, please?” Tessa nodded. ”Promise.” She turned back towards the house hoping sunlight would pour in through her window at any moment now and pull her out of this nightmare. She had no clue how she would make it out unscathed if Chris thought she has a problem. "Wake up, please?" She prayed. Chris eyed his wife, running his down her flushed cheeks. In a matter of seconds her rosy complexion lost all its character. “You okay?” “Yeah.” She lied, catching her breath in between steps. She ran her hand across her forehead pretending it was the heat getting the best of her. “I’m fine.” “Good.” For Tessa it was anything but. ”Oh god.” This was as real as it gets.
