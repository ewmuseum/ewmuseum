Triple Kill

28 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/28/08 START 5:55 pm Many things happen on your typical Friday. Your co-worker flirts with the hot associate director in hopes of getting that always desired weekend score. Banks and other various businesses close down early to avoid the rush hour traffic. People have places to go, things to do? yes! Friday! Everyone is just in that mood. Even your TV Champion, Clyde Evan Fox. Speak of the devil! Clyde calmly walks down the street of a local Japanese neighborhood in which the latest GCW venue is being held. He slams on the brakes and confronts his Gizmondo. Fox: Hmmm, I don’t wanna be late. Fox secures his always handy Gizmondo in his pocket as he quickens his walking speed. Clyde rushes up the steps of the house he’s renting while on the Japanese tour. The keys jingle as Clyde inserts them into the lock. Fox: Oh yeah you like that don’t you? Nice and key like. It’s like you two were made for each other. The door unlocks and slowly creeks open to Fox’s delight. The TV Champ waltzes in and immediately heads for the basement. You know? the hip place. You know? the cool place. You know? where all 12 year olds have their hang outs and smoke dope and whatnot. Anyways, Fox marches down the steps and nestles into the corner couch. The leather crumples as Fox adjusts. There he sat in front of a mammoth 4 inch portable television set. Fox: You know Clyde, for the TV Champion, you didn’t get an impressive TV. Shrugging it off, Clyde leans forward and flicks his nearby Xbox 360 Elite system on. What else would a guy like Fox do on a Friday? There is no other answer than Halo 3 of course! Fox snatches the controller and assembles himself in a semi-laying position. Fox: Freaking Rampage. It’s no fair! I have to defend my TV Title against free guys! That’s like A LOT! No one else has to defend against free guys. Clyde pouts as his clicks through the Xbox dashboard and enters Halo 3. He then connects his headset to the controller after putting it on. Fox: It’s okay I guess. With the help of my copy of Halo 3? IN STORES NOW! I will be able to win at Rampage with a Triple Kill! It will be like taking baby from a candy! “AAAAAaaaaaaaaaAAAAAAAhhhhhHHHHhhhh” The Halo 3 music radiates from the awesome integrated speakers of the 4 inch TV. Fox: Now my quest begins? I’ll just enter my password. It’s so sly; no one would guess it? foxrox69. It reflects my rad Gamertag; FoxRox69! Halo 3 eff tee double u! That mean “for the win!” The game is on? Discouraged, Clyde gets booted back to the matchmaking menu. Fox: I NEED A TRIPLE KILL! 5 hours later? His dream comes true? The game ends as Clyde literally chucks the controller across the room in happiness! But his celebration like it was 1995 was cut short as his Gizmondo begins to ring. Like a gallant race horse, the kid scrambles to the phone only to realize that it’s his Gizmondo. He hastily flips out the retro technology and answers the call. On Phone: Hey Clyde; it’s Chris! Clyde’s head twitches as he scratches his away, trying to think of something to say. Fox: I stole your Xbox! Oh shit! I mean I did NOT steal your Xbox! The speakers on the Gizmondo get worked out as Chris curses through it. Chris: Why did you take it? Fox: Cuz I am in an elimination match at Rampage and I needed to find a way to kill free guys within four seconds? BIG TIME! Clyde tightens his sweaty palm on the Gizmondo in preparation for the onslaught of evilness from his older brother. Chris: You know? it’s an elimination match. You don’t have to eliminate all three of them in a row. Fox: Wh--What? Clyde sits down on the couch as he holds his head in frustration. The beads of sweat sink into both the phone and the couch, leaving Clyde sitting there, uncontrollably upset. Fox: Then you’re telling me that I JUST WASTED HALF THE DAY FOR NOTHING!” Silence? finally Chris answers. Chris: Well, now you can say you got a Triple Kill! Fox: I hate you! Clyde shuts his Gizmondo off in disgust as he sits there in the silence of his home. Nothing could be heard, not even a mouse. For this Fox had tripled killed all within his house. What’s next you ask? Fox: Let’s see if I can get a Quadruple Kill! And that’s why A.D.D. should be praised. Clyde Fox, moving onto bigger and better things and not letting his shortcomings annoy him? although he has many! END
