Outside of the arena, you’re less likely to run into him than you are to run into Elvis Presley’s Half-Venusian Love Child ™.” Katie giggled. “Or Versatu,” she quipped. Stuart pulled at his collar. “Well... no. Versatu liked to meddle in another people’s affairs. You’re thinking of the post-retirement Versatu, after he retired from SAWF... and burned the headquarters building down.” The diminutive Irishwoman decided to change the subject. One of her favorite holidays was looming, her being especially fond of it the last three and a half years... and it was certainly a more pleasant thought than that of some madman burning down a building. “So sure and what are ye getting Maggie for Valentine’s Day?” she asked him, grinning a little mischievously. I think that’s best kept between Maggie and I,” Stuart laughed. “What you and Percival plan to do probably isn’t the kind of thing you’d tell just anyone, is it?” “Aye and ye have me there,” Katie admitted, blushing a little. “In fact, his brother should be coming to pick me up from here in...” Katie checked her wristwatch. “Ninety minutes.” The exercycle signaled the end of the 15-minute pedaling period, and Katie completed her cool-down stretching exercises smoothly. She smiled a little impishly at Stuart as she headed to her locker room to shower and dress. Stuart chuckled. “Maggie sometimes smiles at me that way,” he said. “I’ve learned what it means when she does.” Katie had brought a bar of oatmeal soap and a bottle of conditioning shampoo with her in her gym bag. None of the other women in the locker room thought anything of it. Valentine’s Day was just around the corner. Of course, Katie was very unorthodox, and she had some plans in motion. I’ll pick up Denise at school and drop you off at home so I can pick up John. I’ve already verified your phone call to the principal there.” “Thankee, Stanley,” Katie’s voice rang with gratitude. “Denise always loves the sleepovers with Uncle Stanley.” She chuckled lightly. “I know what it’s like to be deeply enamored with someone,” Stanley said simply. The Monarch Butterfly smiled a little sadly. Marriage seemed to just not be in her brother-in-law’s destiny, which was unfortunate because the ‘gentle giant’ would make a splendid husband for any woman. But things fell out as they fell out, and Stanley was content. Denise was content, as well, with the sleepover, as Stanley always planned well for them. She kissed Katie goodbye and winked at her in a way that showed she understood the need for her adoptive parents to have the house to themselves that night. The girl was too young to be in a serious relationship, but old enough to understand what they were... and what they involved. John started to cry as he was strapped into the toddler seat; understanding that his mother was leaving. Katie and Stanley smiled in soft sympathy for the child. But Katie had brought some of John’s favorite toys to put in the car, and they knew John would find comfort with them... and with his Uncle, whom he had learned to trust despite his very young age. The Rottonbottom Home February 7th, 2007 1842 hours The delicious scents drifting from the kitchen reached well into the hallway, so it didn’t take Percival long to figure out what Katie had been doing since she got home from the gym. It wasn’t a surprise, since Katie liked to fix a wonderful dinner for special occasions. He knew his wife would be dressed up nicely too, as was also her wont. Percival still being clad in his business suit and tie, it would complement the diminutive Irishwoman’s dress nicely. Opening the door, he saw that her attire was anything but formal. The Monarch Butterfly had romance on her mind. People get jealous 'cause we always stay together, Yeah, baby I guess they really want a love like yours and mine... Together, forever I never thought that I could ever be this happy, Yeah, baby My prayers were answered boy, you came in the nick of time! Whoa, whoa, whoa, whoa! They say the way to a man’s heart was through his stomach. While Katie seemed to take that to heart, she knew that the stomach alone wasn’t enough. The eyes also had to come into play... as did the nose... and the mind. Clearly she had all four bases covered. Katie’s dress was shiny black satin, gathered with ruffles above her bust and at the waist. Sleeveless and strapless, save for thin, ribbon-like halter ties from a single point at the top of her chest around her neck, the dress hugged the shape of her body in a way that nothing off the rack could do—clearly she had paid a seamstress to make it for her. A swing skirt ended at a beaded hem about a hand’s breadth above her knees. The beads clinked together with a soft susurration of sound as she walked. Black evening sheer hosiery and black evening pumps enhanced her legs as well as making her appear a little taller. Her hair was fluffed full around her head and the bottom parts tied back in a simple, single braid down her back. A minimal of foundation and blush gave proper color and glow to her cheeks and face. Her lipstick, like her nail polish, was cherry red, carefully pre-dried after application. The small, bright-blue aquamarine stones formed a neat frame around the larger, dark blue sapphire set as the center stone capping her engagement ring. A bracelet set with multi-colored tourmaline gemstones adorned her left wrist, and a silver Celtic torque set with an oval-cut sapphire drop pendant encircled her neck... the same jewelry she wore on their wedding day. The scent of her perfume hinted at orchids: Katie’s favorite flowers. “Buon Giorno,” she said to Percy as he closed the door behind him; her brogue making the greeting sound not the least bit Italian... but bringing smiles to both their faces. Percival put his briefcase on the sofa and walked over to give his wife a big hug and kiss. “I hope what I got you for Valentine’s Day compares to this,” he teased. “Sure and I hope not, lest me competitive streak shine through,” she teased back with a light giggle, before returning his kiss with a smooch. I've got this feelin' that you're going to stay, I never knew that it could happen this way! Before I met you I was fallin' apart, But now at last I really know that we are: The Monarch Butterfly put on a painter’s smock over her dress so she could turn off the stove and arrange the food on the plates without getting any sauce or oil on her dress or skin. She did a French dip, bending her knees to keep her upper body erect, as she placed the plates on the table. Percival, however, ever the gallant, refused to let her serve him directly with the plate placed before him, because he still had SOME traces of traditionalism left from his old life as a nobleman: he was insistent on properly pulling out Katie’s chair for her as she sat down. Katie doffed the smock before sitting so that, with the lit candles on the table, they looked like a couple in elegant evening attire sitting down to candlelight dinner at a high-atmosphere restaurant. “Exquisite, Katie,” Percival said as he took his first bite of the meal. “With all I’ve learned about the culinary arts from running the inn, I doubt I could match the quality of this.” “Ye will, in time,” Katie responded with a soft smile. Good cooking was a tradition in the whole of “the Mulvane clan,” as Katie’s whole family was affectionately called in County Kerry back home. The men were no less proficient than the women: Katie’s late brother, John, was quite the accomplished chef, and Jessica’s fianc?, Timothy, was becoming that way more and more each month. “So... your first match since your return,” Percival said thoughtfully at the end of the meal. “Denise is excited... what about you?” “Back to work, as the saying goes here in America,” Katie quipped lightly. “Sure and it feels good knowing that I can go back to work after the accident.” “And the man you’ll be facing... Virgil Ambrosi,” Percival mused. “He’s something of an enigma in the league, as I understand.” The Monarch Butterfly giggled as she reminisced back to the workout. “Ask Stuart to tell you what he told me about him,” she said, and winked as she took a bite of her meal. “What was it like finding out he’s married to... what does she call herself, Shadow? The new league champion?” “She dropped the name Shadow after NC-17,” Katie corrected. “Sure and when I ran across her in that hallway, and we... talked... I saw a lot of meself in her... or perhaps I should put it the other way around—a lot of her in me. Of course—” she smiled again—“Virgil be nothing like ye are.” “I imagine not,” Percival chuckled. “From the pallor he appears to have, looking at him on television, I would imagine he would never go here.” Percy pulled an envelope out of his inside jacket pocket and slid it across the table to Katie. The diminutive Irishwoman opened it up to find two plane tickets for a three-day stay at Bimini Island in the Bahamas... a Valentine’s Day getaway for the two of them. Katie gushed with delight as she read the tickets, and glanced up to see Percival take something out of his briefcase—an elegant jewelry container that held a beautiful pair of teardrop diamond earrings. Tears of joy glistened in her shamrock green eyes as Percival carefully placed them at Katie’s earlobes. Two of hearts, two hearts that beat as one, Two of hearts, I need you, I need you! Two of hearts, two hearts that beat as one, Two of hearts, come on, come on! Her eyes shining with affection, Katie picked up the remote for their stereo and pressed the play button. She sidled up to her husband as soft music began to play, and placed her dainty hand into his; using her other hand to lightly pull out her swing skirt away from her leg as Percy put his other arm around her slender waist. The Monarch Butterfly smiled in approval at the grace and ease at which her husband twirled her around in a circle. “Very good,” she said. Percival’s own smile was almost a complacent snicker, as if his reply was stating the obvious. “Being the husband of Mary Katherine Mulvane naturally makes one an accomplished dancer... whether they seek to become such, or not.” Katie giggled again. Of course Percival was right. She had danced all her life, and probably would have done so professionally on a theater stage if she hadn’t gone into wrestling. The next song on the track was a softer, slower-paced number, and Katie leaned in close to lay her head on Percival’s shoulder. She let go of her skirt and put her arm around Percival to hold him close to her. The scent of his aftershave was always something she liked, and the feel of snuggling close... well, that had been a staple of their romance since the day they first kissed. The only thing that ever made her feel safer or more secure, was when she kneeled in prayer at Church. But God intended for husband and wife to be as close as two mortal people could be; He meant for them to share love... romance... and of course, something more... She lightly pushed her husband down to a seated position on the couch and leaned over him. She picked at the knot at his tie and untied it, taking it into her teeth to pull it away from his neck. She half-kneeled on his lap and grinned wickedly at him, holding his tie in her mouth. “Ummmm... what have you done with my wife?” Percival asked, sliding his arm back around Katie’s waist. The diminutive Irishwoman dropped Percival’s necktie onto the sofa beside him and untied the halter neck-strings of her dress. “Sure and she shall be returned to ye safe and sound... after the proper ransom is paid,” she teased back. Percival played along. “And what is the payment?” Katie reached behind her and unzipped her dress, letting it slide down her body and off her legs. Underneath she wore a strapless red lace bustier that revealed a generous amount of cleavage and the curve of her breasts. The strapless design allowed her to wear it under her dress without its being noticed. The panty portion opened with a snap closure at the crotch, and from the bottom hem of the bustier extended four lace, red-ribbon adorned garters that held up Katie’s stockings. “Do ye think ye can afford the ransom?” she asked him in a mock-severe tone, looking deep into his eyes. “I... I’ll see what I can do,” he answered. “I’m sure I can hit the bank up for a loan...” Katie smiled again and smooched Percival lovingly. Her breath lingered on his neck as she slowly, reluctantly drew her head back after the kiss. Percival picked Katie up and held her in his arms as he stood up off the couch. They smooched again as he started to carry her into the bedroom. “Happy Valentine’s Day, Mary Katherine Mulvane,” Percy said softly into Katie’s ear. “And welcome back to work.” “Work comes later,” Katie admonished as her husband laid her down on the bed and she started to unbutton his shirt. “Right now I am happy to have but one card from the former house of cards I have built.” “What card is that?” Percy asked. “The deuce of hearts,” Katie said, as the CD player switched to a new track and began playing a jaunty little love song. “Let our hearts truly be one tonight...” Two of hearts, two hearts that beat as one...
