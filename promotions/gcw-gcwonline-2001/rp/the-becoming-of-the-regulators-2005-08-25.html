The Becoming of: The Regulators

25 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08.25.05 (I sat anxiously on my appartment sofa, waiting for a phone call or an email or even a damn telegram! Realizing Warz doesn't own a phone or a computer, I lose my cool. I quickly flip open my cell phone and speed dial my useless agent Tom. Hell, I wouldnt even call him my agent! The guy spends most of his life sleeping and eating. He hasnt received a wrestling contract for me in 3 years!) (~Ring....ring....ri~) 'Yaaaawn...Hello?' 'Tom, it's Blake, You schmuck skin bastard! You promised me...you PROMISED ME...a tag team partner! You know I hate wrestling alone! Too many lipo lickers wrestle these days...you cant leave an arena without being attacked by five guys! So I politely asked you to GET ME A FUCKIN' PARTNER or Im not wrestling!' 'You done Blake?' 'God damn you Tom! You keep givin' me that attitude and you're gone!' 'Blake...you don't even pay me!' 'Thank god!' 'Anyway, listen here Blake! Warz is my man! I met him when I was in Europe! He's pretty shy though...you know, he's not much of a talker. But he'd be a perfect fit for The Regulators! He's big, he's mean, and he doesn't take shit from nobody!' 'Yeah, yeah! You told me all that shit before....I just want to know where the hell he is!' 'I sent him off in a taxi to your place an hour ago. He should be there any minute...' (~Bang...bang...bang~) 'Gotta go Tom...I think he's here!' 'Alright Blake...I'll call you later.' (~Click~) So lets just see how mean this Warz character is. (~Bang...bang...bang~) The bastards gonna knock my damn door off its hinges... (~Door opens~) Ah shit! 'Mr. Cassidy! Its been 3 weeks since you promised me last months rent! THREE WEEKS!!!' 'Mr. Beckinhager! How ya doin'? Such a pleasant visit! On such a lovely day I didn't think it could get any lovlier...then you show up, and prove me wrong! By the way the haircut...spiffy! I bet Mrs. Beckinhager cant keep her hands off of y....' 'Mr. Cassidy, cut the crap! Im your landlord...not your boyfriend, you pervert! I want my money! And I want it by Friday! Thats one week!' (~Slams the door~) That old wrinkled ass! I aint payin' you shit! (~Bang...bang...bang~) Ah shit... (~Opens door~) 'You will have you money friday Mr. Beckinhager sir!' Uhhh...its him! Holy shit...hes big! 'My name...is Warz! Im looking for....Blake Cassidy.' 'Uhh...thats me! Nice to meet you Warz. Tom made you seem a little cuter, but he didnt cheat your size! Well come in. Welcome to my wonderful apartment. I actually have a house in Newport Beach, but Im just staying here to get away from the A-List life...you know what I mean?' Great! A blank stare. This'll be fun! 'So Warz! Would you like somethin' to drink?' (As I make my way to the fridge I realize he had no luggage.) 'I have Sprite, Coke, Water, purple stuff...alright Sunny D!' 'Im...fine!' 'Alright...suit yourself Warz! You mind hangin' out a second? I gotta grab something from the room?' Awesome! Another stupid blank stare! Making my way to the bedroom, I whip out my cell phone and redial Tom's number. (~Ring...ring~) 'Halllo?' 'You sleeping still Tom? Its four thirty in the afternoon you woman dick!' 'What the hell do you want now Blake?' 'I want to know why you sent me an oversized retard. The guy doesnt even have luggage! What's he going to wear?' 'Oh yeah, I forgot to mention that. They lost his luggage on his flight here. I was thinking he could just bum some clothes off of you!' 'Ha.....' '.......' 'You're serious Tom?' 'Uh, yeah Blake!' 'The guy wont fit into anything I own! You're a woman dick again! Talk to you later!' (~Click~) Wear my clothes, bullshit! I made my way back to the small living room to find Warz standing in the same spot looking in the same direction he was when I left. 'So...you excited to get in the ring Warz?' '....' 'I understand. Im nervous too. Do you have any past experience?' '....' 'Really?' I hate this thing! Hes a god damn rock! 'You tired Warz?' '.....' 'You're gonna have to sleep on the couch for now. I only have one bed. But don't worry, if you use the couch cushions as pillows, its like youre sleeping at a three star motel in New Mexico...isnt that cool?' '.....' 'Alright, well lets go grab a bite to eat. You like sushi? You know...suuuu-sheeeeeee! Say it with me now....suuuu-' '....sure!' Alright! He spoke! Now I need to get him out of the room. 'Well follow me.' (I grab my motorcycle helmet and a wooden spoon from the kitchen, always carry the wooden spoon on me when Im nervous, and also the beat-up extra helmet thats been in the closet since I found it in the dumpster three years ago. Warz and I made our way to my parking slot, where my beatup but cleaned up '91 Yamaha stood on its peg. Again, Warz gives me that blank stare.) 'Yeah I know this is going to be awkward Warz, but there is no other means of transportation! And plus, if you hold on to me tight enough people might not even see you there!' 'Im...your tag team partner in the ring. Not in bed!' What's with the gay bashes today? 'Whoa whoa whoa! You better watch your ass in my neighborhood! I know people here that would ruin your life...' (Thats when I seen her...just as Im trying to show this pushover who's boss, 82 year old Miss Landa steps out of her apartment.) 'Are you okay Blakey Pooh? I was getting ready for bed when I heard some loud talking, thought it was the trashmen. You know, they keep refusing to take my urinal bags! I dont know what it is with them!' Jesus Christ! This lady's insane! 'Okay, sorry about that Miss Landa! We're were just leaving! I didnt realize it was almost five o'clock already. Have a good night *under breath* you saggy sack of wet toiletpaper!' (Just as Miss Landa gives me the wobbly nod, a limo pulls up besides me and Warz. I see the limo drive get out and instantaneously open the back door. But there's nobody in there! Why is Warz giving me that stupid look?) 'Warz, why are you giving me that stupid look?' (As I put two and two together, I see Warz hop inside.) 'You son a mountain cream pie Warz! I didnt know you had a limo! This is totally freakin' sweet!' 'No...this Warz limo...not us limo!' (And with those words...my own tag team partner leaves me standing alone with little dignity and my godly motorcycle in hand.) (~Ring..ring...~) 'Hello?' 'Hey Blake! Its Tom! I got some exciting news!' 'Yeah!? Well heres some for you first! Your stupid tag team partner you found for The Regulators...is a fraud! He has a flippin' limo pick him up!' 'Oh Blake...forgot to tell you! Warz is loaded with cash! But anyway, even bigger news! The Regulators are scheduled to wrestle at Annihilation!' 'What...?' 'Yeah....and better yet, its for the Tag Team title!' (Thats when I felt him! God was petting the top of my head!) 'Nice Tom...' (Now a cold chill just ran down my spine. A wet cold chill...a stinky WHITE CHILL! BIRDSHIT!) 'TOM I GOTTA GO...A SEAGULL LANDED ON MY HEAD AND SHIT DOWN THE BACK OF MY SHIRT....' ----END----
