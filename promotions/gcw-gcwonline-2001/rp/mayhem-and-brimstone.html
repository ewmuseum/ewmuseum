Date: Like now 'YOU STAND NO CHANCE!' Clap Clap Clapclapclap! 'YOU STAND NO CHANCE!' Clap Clap Clapclapclap! 'YOU STAND NO CHANCE!' Clap Clap Clapclapclap! Oh lord, not the ever repetitive insert chant with clapping motion. WHAT I EVER DO?! 'But didn't you hear? Bryan Mayhem is a vile evil monster!' Yep, read it. Seen it on the front page. 'What do you think? Scary end of the world kinda shit huh?!' Not really. Just because someone is a complete fucking putz in the ring doesn't get me shaking in my space boots when I got to take care of my business. 'BUT HOW?! WHY NOT?!' Simple. It's overinflated chest pounding. Lions mouth kinda shit. The story is basically from his own mouth, and all it does is go into beating his chest. Why should I be scared? In his tenure here has he ever won a title? Has he even really had a shot? In MMA, this guy is comparable to Joe 'Diesel' Riggs: look tough, act tough, but when hit in the face by someone with some skill and he trembles like a little girl. 'BUT HE'S DAMN NEAR KILLED PEOPLE!' Like that fucking matters. He hasn't killed me. I ain't sitting in a damn wheel chair. 'BIG TALK!' I'm undefeated. Why exactly should I give a damn what some article says? What some people think? Hell I could point to the 2005 Mark Award for Wrestler Of The Year and say 'DUDE'S IN TROUBLE' but I'll refrain. 'BUT HE'S BIIIIIIIIIIIIG!' Yeah, and he's got KISS paint on his face. What's the point? Dude got a match with me because he's been a part of this place for a long time and simply put is going to be another one of those nice little names to put on my list of beaten opponents here. 'Whatever you say man...' Hey...it's not my windpipe that's going to be crushed by the Gridlock. I'll bitch slap that Muta paint job before he can think twice about feuding with Khali here again. *** Me=Car Mayhem=Kid *** Eva VanLorne had these rocking headaches ever since Warpath, thanks in part to the concussion received from her nemesis Vivica J. Valentine. It was a cascade of emotions that throttled her that seemingly made it all the worse. Her head throbbing, the doctors saying that her career had a high probability of being over...she couldn't necessarily cope easily. She wanted vengence, some form of validation. Vivica would be limp in her fingers at some point, and she would relish the chance. Or so she hoped. And as she laid in her hospital bed, Theresa sat in the sterile waiting room with crayons. She sighed meekly, bored of the dated coloring books the nice lady at the desk gave her. There was only so many times you could color the Power Rangers before you seriously felt like curling up and nodding off. She tried to watch the television, but it was all in spanish. Walking into the waiting room, Brandon Youngblood looked over at Theresa as she stared out the window at the parking lot. He put pulled his Oakleys up onto his shaved head, putting his hands into his khaki shorts. Having just coming from the gym, he threw on a black BloodWorks shirt, not really expecting to dress up to see one of his many benefactors with his business endeavor. 'I know what yer thinkin' kid...and unless you're next to a hydrant you'd shrivel up from heat exhaustion.' Theresa almost instantly lit up, turning her head and running towards Brandon, grabbing him for a big old bear hug. 'BRANDON!' 'Lookin' pretty bored. How's mom?' He said, returning the hug. 'Well...they say that she's gonna come out of it. Her career might be over though...' Brandon could only sneer. 'Fuckin' dumb bitch.' He seethed under his breath only to get a surprise smack right across the face from Theresa. 'What did I tell you about your mouth mister?!' Brandon just looked at her. 'You know if you were any other person...' Theresa batted her eyes. 'If that was the case you'd just take it.' Brandon could merely smirk. 'You seem pretty chipper. How's she really like though?' 'Well...' Theresa trailed off, 'I think it's upset her. I mean, she's got bad headaches is what she keeps saying. What's happened is like those bad dreams. I think she never thought she'd get hurt by those bad people.' 'Well...never think of the bad things...you know. If you worry about it...they can get in your head. Play with your mind. Have you questioning yourself. Your momma was brave for taking them head on...' 'But what are you going to do about it?' Theresa said bluntly. 'I'm not in this to fight a war. I'm not a hero Theresa. I've explained this to you. When I go out and fight them...I'm not doing it for people...I'm doin' it for myself.' 'But your better then that!' Theresa yelled. 'I know you! You're always acting so tough but beneath that you're a nice person.' She was the only one that didn't see him as a monster. Maybe it was fitting that this little girl had faith in the humanity of this callous assassin. Maybe there was something beneath the surface. Tell children enough things and they make it up in their own minds. Santa is real, the Tooth Fairy will come, and the monsters can't hurt you with the night light on. 'You give me way to much credit kid.' But there were real monsters. And Brandon Youngblood was just as much one as the girl that hurt Theresa's mother. 'Tell your mom I said hey...alright?' And with that he left, almost as if he could be burdened to continue this happy little facade. He had much more important things to worry about then the problems of others. Himself. *** I segway because this is important. This is from the hip kinda shit and it needs to be said. No jokes, no plot devices, no nothing. Bryan Mayhem fashions himself a monster. This atrocity against all of mankind that should be put down. Dangerous to even look at. And he's crippled men in his wake and has all these added elements to make his seven foot body look badder then it already is. See...I'm going against a monster, but the monster doesn't understand what it is getting into. I don't have a KISS paint job...I don't have to put my women in bondage to beat them up...I don't need to climb out of a pile of rubble like Super Shredder to stake claim to my inhumanity. I am supposed to be afraid of you. Your very shadow lurking over my like some vulture should make me piss myself where I stand. I didn't do anything but break my wife's jaw for giving me lip. I did drugs upon drugs upon drugs. I've faced evil men and taught them a thing or two about themselves. All I live for is the sport of professional wrestling. My life is dedicated to the small portion of my time in a ring. I am not a deep person. I am not even an emotional person. Why is that? Because emotion is weakness Mayhem. See, I might not be seven foot tall. I might not do the crazy, over the top, sports entertainment things you do. I live in the reality that I was put on this earth to wrestle. And no matter what...nothing else can possibly satisfy me. I won't have a white picket fence and a huge house on the praire. I won't hug my kids on their way to the school bus. I won't have a life after the mat because a life without wrestling for me is worse then death. But I do not fear it. I do not fear anything...not a thing. See...I looked into the Deville's eyes. I heard Mike Randalls snarl. I've dealt with OD's and withdrawls. I've dealt with pain. All I do is lift, train, and prepare. So while you sit at home hallucinating blood falling from your ceiling, I will be preparing myself to beat you. See...your goddamn clumsiness is what has made you into this monster. Take away your face paint and your goddamn fake ass unreal gimmick and you're just a Joe Blow with a role to play. I don't play roles. I'm grade A, one hundred percent real and will not back down no matter what someone tries. It's how I'm undefeated Mayhem. Undefeated. And I didn't just walk all over a bunch of jobbers to get to where I am. I got here by beating some of the most reputable names in this sport. And while I've been blazing a trail I've had to find myself carrying deadweight shit like you to a new era of promise. Which...I have no problem doing. But for some punk ass guy with an apparent violence fetish to think that I am some sort of stepping stone...pfft. Get it right dude. You're the stepping stone. You're my forth win. You'll throw some shots at me like the Undertaker and I'll grab your lanky arm and snap it in two, then shove it up your ass. And you want to know why? Because there is only one real goddamn monster in this place. There is only one person that should be feared throughout. And that's me. As someone once said: Beat me if you can. Survive if I let you. This is Brandon Youngblood, making sure to dust my goddamn shoulder off.
