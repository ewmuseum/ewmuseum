The Day The Dead Walked III

18 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/18/11 Inks sits on his couch, his legs up on the ottoman, icepack to his forehead as the EMT works on the gash in the back of his head. He winces with every probe and prod into the opening. The collar of his shirt is soaked in blood, droplets staining the back of his shirt as well. “Mr. Inks we advise you to come down to the hospital, this is going to need some stitches.” Inks shakes off their request. “Put a bandage on it or something. I don’t have time, to worry about this right now.” “But Mr Inks…” “You heard the man! Bandage and go!” Inks looks over his shoulder and his partner, Wilson, still wearing the exact same clothes we saw him in the night before, walks into the room and sits next to Inks. “You OK man?” Ink’s puts his hand on the gash and winces with contact, “Yeah I think so. I feel like I’ve been hit in the head with a blunt object.” Wilson lets out a cheap smile, “Yeah something like that?” “We got any prints?” “No, just yours man.” “If I would have turned on the lights I would have seen the blood everywhere, and maybe I could have caught this sonofabitch?” Wilson nudges his partner’s shoulder. “We don’t have time for what-ifs my man. We don’t have an exact ID but it appears the gift you were left was Martin McCoy, the father and husband of Linda McCoy.” Inks looks at Wilson in confusion. “The house we were at last night?’ Inks closes his eyes. “Sorry, yeah I figured that out when I saw the hands missing. I just hate being right.” Wilson looks at his partner with a blank expression. “I told the local PD there was going to be more bodies. Now all we need to do is find the children.” Sighing and rubbing the stubble on his chin, “Yeah, let’s hope these sick freaks have some sort of compassion and leave the children be.” Inks looks at his partner and shakes his head. “Both you and I know they are as good as dead. Atleast there’s no phone call we have to make to worried mother and father that their children are dead.” “I guess.” Inks, holding an ice pack to his head, sits in the passenger seat of Wilson’s car after being questioned by the locals and some members of the FBI. The body was removed shortly after the authorities arrived. Inks house is labeled a crime scene so he was able to grab a few articles of clothing and some toiletries before they made him leave. Wilson offered his couch for his partner for however long he needs it. As Wilson walks down the steps towards the car something really starts to bother Inks. How the hell did the police get there? Wilson gets into his car just as Inks opens the door to step out. Wilson looks at his partner as Inks walks towards the officer in charge. He does not hear the pleas of Wilson to get back into the car. “Captain, mind if I ask you a question?” Captain Markakis turns away from the press and walks towards Inks, almost as if he’s happy to have been interrupted. “Thanks for tearing me away from that. What you need detective?” “Ok look, my head is sore, my brains are scrambled right now, but can someone please tell me how the fuck you guys got here? I closed my door when I came in, and the only other person in the apartment with me was decapitated. So who called?” The detective looked around, than motioned for Inks to move away from the crowd. He put his arm around Inks’ shoulders as if they were old college buddies reliving their youth. “Well that’s where we are confused. 911 was dialed from your phone, with a rather cryptic message. It said something like, ‘I’ve done a very bad thing. Man is obsolete, erased, extinct.’ Than he ended with Welcome to Oblivion, but he didn’t hang up.” “Did you print the phone? Swap the mouthpiece for DNA? We have to have some kind of lead!” “We have people examining the tape. We ran prints but there was none. I’m sorry, but we have nothing right now.” Inks grabbed the detective by the collar and brought him closer. Other officers reached for their guns as Wilson jumped from the front seat. “Goddamnit. You have nothing? You better get something or there will be more blood spilled!” Inks let go of his collar, straightened it out, and patted his shoulder. “Sorry, I normally don’t act that way.” Inks turned back towards the car, when Markakis grabs his shoulder. “Detective. As far as we know, he…they…whoever has brutally murdered everyone they’ve come in contact with. Why were you allowed to live?” Inks looks into the eyes of the detective, “I asked myself that the second I woke up.” The silence and stare between Inks and Markakis was awkward and deafening. Both men stood there clueless. Why was Inks allowed to live when everyone else was bludgeoned and decapitated? Inks was at the mercy of a lunatic, but instead was beaten over the head and left to tell the story, or partial story of what happened. Would this person have allowed Wilson or Markakis to live? Or was it just Inks? If it was a copycat or a sick deranged follower of O’Neil wouldn’t they want Inks dead for killing their hero/god? Nothing is making sense at all, and all they know is that this murderer enjoys cutting off body parts, taking bodies and leaving them in strange places, and burying people in someone else’s grave. “Hey! I’ve got some information on that group Agents of Oblivion you asked about!” Wilson and Inks both jumped when this man shouted as he came running from the house, holding his IPAD. Markakis looks confused. “You never heard of The Agents of Oblivion?” Markakis shakes his head, “No is it some sort of cult?” “You can say that.’ blurts out Wilson, “ they are an online group of lowlifes that have dedicated a site and most likely their lives to the idol worship of mass murderer Bryan O’Neil. Most likely a group of Goths that stand around doing nothing all day but jacking each other off listening to Marilyn Manson. Just a guess obviously.” “Uh, well we have an ID on the person who runs the IP. Jim Ciolek. He’s 25 lives in the trailer park called Rolling Meadows about 2 miles from here.” The officer was still tapping away at his IPAD trying to find more information to help the team. He looks up and notices everyone else staring at him. “Oh, sorry he has no real major criminal record, just some petty stuff, theft, couple 420’s, but that’s about it.” Wilson chuckles, “Rolling Meadows! Why do they name every single trailer park some stupid happy name like that when we know trailer parks are havens for meth labs, gun nuts, and perverts. Just name the place “Abducted Meadows” and get it over with.” The team lets out a small laugh. They needed that, their brains have been wrapped around this case and something to take their heads off of it, something even as minor and stupid as Wilson’s comment can always revitalize the brain, even if it is just for a few seconds. “OK, Inks and myself will go check this trailer park trash out, you guys keep us updated on anything you find. I don’t care if it’s a used condom, I want to know about it!” Inks and Wilson jump in the car and head towards Rolling Meadows, both of them hoping with every iota of hope they have that this leads to somewhere. The car ride was silent, both of them trying to wrap their brains around what exactly has happened. As they pull in front of the address they were given, they were immediately taken aback at how well kept the place was. There is no way in hell this could be the place. “Wilson, uh, is this the right place?” Wilson says nothing, just squints at the address on the shack, than back at the information he was given by the officer back at Inks’ apartment complex. He gets out of the car and slowly makes his way to the door hiking his pants up. Inks gets out and follows suit, not hiking up his pants obviously. Wilson knocks on the door than yells, “Jim Ciolek, open up FBI!” Inks has his hand on the holster of his gun just in case things start to turn interesting. Wilson knocks again, only this time to be greeted by a man in his mid twenties. His eyes are blood shot, his jet black hair is unkempt. He’s wearing a black T Shirt with some Satanic looking psychobabble written on it. The smell is strong of Marijuana. Inks and Wilson both pull out their credentials. “FBI, we need to have a few words with you,” Inks blurts out before his partner can. “UH-UH, it’s for medicinal purposes officers.” Wilson retorts back, “We aren’t here to discuss your skunk issue, we need to talk to you about your hero Bryan O’Neil.” The druggie wrinkles his forehead and puts his arms up in confusion. “What about him? He’s dead.” “Can you just step outside for a second Mr. Ciolek.” You can tell in Wilson’s tone that he is getting frustrated. He is ill-tempered and has a short fuse. He has been cited by the higher ups numerous times for flying off the handle at others because they are not doing what he wants when he wants. Ciolek steps outside and crosses his arms. He looks nervous and a bit wasted. He just stands there and looks at the guys standing in front of him. He says nothing, smart enough to know. Inks starts the questioning, “How long have you been in charge of the Agents of Oblivion?” “Is this what this is all about? I start a web blog group in dedication to someone? Because if this is all you want there’s nothing more to be said. It’s a simple memorial to a serial killer. Dahmer has his own, so does Manson, what’s the big deal if I made one to O’Neil. Freedom of speech guys. If that’s all I am going back inside.” Ciolek turns his back and grabs the handle of the door. “We have reason to believe that almost 10 people have been murdered and mutilated. The body of O’Neil is missing, and Welcome to Oblivion is carved into bodies and what not!” Inks shouted so loud it startled Wilson. Ciolek stood with his hand on the door, frozen to the world. He turns back around. “Wait a second. The body is missing? Are you accusing me of stealing the body? How the fuck could I get into Potter’s Field? Where would me? I mean look at me I’m smaller than you carry a 400 pound dead body?” Wilson erupted, “I don’t know! Maybe you and your devil worshipping friends decided to have some fun with it? Surely more than 3 or 4 men can carry the body!” “Where? Why? What the fuck is going on here? I assure you I know nothing about this! Unless you have a warrant or a reason to arrest me I feel you guys need to leave!” Ciolek bolted back into his trailer park and slammed the door. Inks and Wilson are both left standing outside, Wilson’s face turning red. If steam could erupt from ears like they do in movies one could assume that steam would be emitting from Wilson’s ears at an alarming pace. “This little fuck knows something!” Wilson yells. “Hey pothead, we will be back!!” Wilson kicks up some dirt and rushes back towards the car. Inks stares at the house, thinking. This guy does know something, but he’s smart. He knows better not to incriminate himself. Inks spits, than gets back in the car, sitting in utter silence. Not even the radio crackled on their way back to the station, an eerie silence that can only be culminated by a life changing event. As Inks and Wilson get into their car Ciolek is on the phone pacing, biting his finger nails. “Yes they were here.” He peeks through the blinds and see’s the car drive off his property. “Are you sure?” Ciolek is seen smiling, than rushes towards his computer, the smile getting even bigger. “Sure thing sir. It’s as good as done!” He hangs up the phone and begins typing away at his computer. Inks and Wilson are both back at the station. Wilson leaning back in his chair his feet up on his desk looking through the information that was given to him about The Agents of Oblivion. He pages through the material quickly, than goes back to the beginning and peruses it more before slamming it down on the desk. Inks is hunched forward, his hands over his mouth as he stares into the blackness that is his computer screen. Wilson rubs his eyes and Inks never once blinks. The gash in his head is starting to throb, but he hardly recognizes it. A loud blip is heard followed by a countdown from 5 on Inks computer screen. He jumps and blurts “What the fuck!” Wilson nearly falls from his chair trying to run around the desk. Both men stare at the countdown. Neither of them take their eyes from the screen. “What is going on?” “I don’t know, my fucking computer is off!” An image appears on the screen, an image that Inks nor Wilson will ever forget as long as they live. Stripped naked, gagged, and tied to a pole is Patricia Inks, the soon to be ex wife of Richard Inks. Inks screams her name. Wilson stands up and yells throughout the office. “Someone get me a fucking trace on this computer now! Get the fuck up and get me a trace!!” No one moves because no one knows how to do a trace. The IT guys are not in the office. Inks begins to shake as he looks at his beaten and bruised wife on the computer screen. He stands up, his chair flying behind him like a projectile. There is no sound on the screen, and the image is gritty. “GODDAMNIT GET ME A FUCKING TRACE!!” Wilson screams again. The office starts to pour around Inks desk as an image is seen moving in front of Patricia. She is sobbing, as the image stands next to her. All that is seen is from the torso down. Black shirt and pants, enormous arms covered in tattoos. Inks is frozen in fear. Wilson turns around to see the room watching the screen. “WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU DOING HERE I NEED A MOTHER FUCKING TRACE RIGHT NOW!!” The entire rooms watches as the image pulls a huge knife from a holster on his leg and in one quick motion slices the throat of Patricia Inks so brutally that her head dangles by her vertebrate only with blood launching out of her neck like a geyser. “PATTY!!!!!!!!!!!” That’s all that Inks can manage to scream as his legs buckle from under him. He goes to his knees not taking his eyes from the screen. His eyes fill with rage as he grits his teeth. The killer squats down and stares into the screen. Long black dreadlocks, face painted, smile on his face as he licks the blade of the knife. The screen goes to black again. “DID WE GET A FUCKING TRACE?” An officer in the back of the group blurts, “Who the hell was that man with the painted face?” Inks, eyes not removed from the screen at all says, “Bryan O’Neil.” Wilson looks back at his partner and runs his hands through his thinning hair, “What the fuck?”
