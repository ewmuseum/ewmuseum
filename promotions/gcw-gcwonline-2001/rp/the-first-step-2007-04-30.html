The First Step

30 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/30/07 Seeing his image reflecting back at him from the chrome exhaust that he just shined on his beloved Harley Davidson is an image that Jimmy thought he'd never see again. Along with his contract payments coming through as well as winning the one hundred thousand dollars from his match at High Stakes, Jimmy Thunder not only bought back his Harley Davidson from his brother-in-law, he also bought back some of the pride he lost when his fortune went down the proverbial shit hole. With his finances no longer in dire straights and his drug controvery behind him, Jimmy can finally walk around with his head held high up in the air as his self confidence was given a one hundred thousand dollar injection. Jimmy climbs on and listens to the sound of his jeans rubbing on the crisp leather as he wraps his huge hand around the the throttle. He takes a few deep breaths before turning the ignition and with the flick of a switch from his thumb, the beast wakes to make a deep rumbling sound from the chrome exhausts powered by the heart and soul of the motorcycle, a shining eighty eight cubic inch Harley Davidson power plant. Jimmy revs the motorcycle a few times before kicking it into gear and riding it out of his garage and onto the road towards Boise with nothing but the surrounding mountains and the warm gleaming sun shining on his muscular shoulders. With the wind blowing in his face, Jimmy feels like all his problems have finally been blown past him. At the moment Jimmy feels that his road in GCW is going just as he planned and in his private life, things are slowly getting back to normal. The only thing that he feels he needs to work on is his feelings for Emma. Outwardly, Jimmy hasn't shown his hand when it comes to his feelings for her but inwardly, he is really attracted to her. He hasn't contacted her over a month, mainly because of his financial situation. There was no way he could afford to take her out on a proper date, he wouldn't dare subject himself to the humiliation of offering her a glass of iced water at a bar because quite frankly, that's all he could afford at the time. Nowadays, things have changed, he could afford to take her to any restaurant in Boise and order her anything she wanted. Where GCW is concerned, with Jimmy's contract coming up for renewal in a few short months, Jimmy has found himself in a predicament. With the Warpath tournament in full swing, Jimmy could either just take the easy matches and see out his contract or he could take one last shot at glory. With his luck turning dramatically the last few weeks, Jimmy really doesn't have much thinking to do when it comes between seeing out his contract and retiring again or busting his ass for one last shot at glory. Just the thought of being known as "World Heavyweight Champion" one last time is enough to raise the hair on the back of Jimmy's neck. For the first time in what seems like an eternity, Jimmy is able to bare a positive smile as he nears Emma's house in Boise. Jimmy pulls up in her drive way as she comes walking out through her front door. Emma slowly walks towards Jimmy, making it obvious to him that she isn't too happy about him not contacting her in over a month. Jimmy, as hard as he tries to hide his feelings for her, smirks at Emma, showing his hand when it comes to his feelings for her. As much as she hates that Jimmy has ignored her calls for the last month or so, she cant help but feel drawn to Jimmy by the strong aura that encircles him. Emma's defences come crashing down as a broad smile lights up her face which immediately let's Jimmy know that she has forgiven him for his hiatus status the last few weeks. "Feel like going to World Wide?" Jimmy asked like he had the art of arrogance perfected like a seasoned pro. Emma smiles at him as she nods and gives him wink which brings a million dollar smile on Jimmy's face. He slowly backs out of her driveway, gives her one last look while he puts his bike into gear. Their gazes at each other are locked in so tightly that Emma can't bare to watch him ride away from her that she runs down the driveway and jumps on the back of Jimmy's bike, wrapping her small arms around his large, muscular body, Jimmy wastes no time and accelerates at a rapid pace to the end of Emma's street and around the corner like they were two teenagers running away from the world. With nothing but the open road in front of him and a beautiful woman hanging on to him, this picture is one that Jimmy has yearned for a long time now. There was one last thing for Jimmy to do and that's Warpath. It was at that moment that Jimmy decided that he wasn't going to be just another number in the draw for someone to just use him to leapfrog their way into the final match for a shot at the coveted GCW World Heavyweight Championship. If someone was going to leap frog their way over Jimmy to advance further in the competition, then they're going to have to earn their spot. The only way Jimmy is going to make a difference in this tournament is to take it one match at time, one night at a time, one show at a time. A loss will not disappoint him if he gave one hundred percent in the match, to lose in a match in where he gave less than one hundred percent was simply be unacceptable. It's clear in Jimmy's mind that he is the stand out under dog in this tournament, and without the pressure of being one of the favourites to win the Warpath tournament the best way to tackle this tournament is to take on the "Nothing to lose, Everything to Gain" mentally would make him an extremely commodity in this tournament. There's nothing more dangerous in any situation is a psycho taking the fight right up to his opponent and just going for broke. Jimmy senses something special is about to happen in thsi tournament and continues to smile as the camera's fade.....
