SOMETHING ONLY ALL OF THE TIME.. Mooch: ugh.. All of the tiiiiime.. Bammy: THERE'S NOTHING I CAN- [Bammy stops as Simon Cowell leaps from his seat, taking him down with a harpoon style spear.] Simon: SHUT THE F*** UP YOU BLOODY FOOL! ARGHH!~ [Cowell is taken down as Mooch leaps into the battle, pushing the angered Englishman off his fallen partner. Cue Randy Jackson and Paula Abdul jumping to their feet. The FCC has urged us to cut the broadcast for the time being] ================ 1. The Fox Hunt. [We re-join our Hero's at Seattle International Airport, ready to catch a flight to Japan, where Mooch has the biggest match of his career waiting. Rich Rollins, and that one dude from The Guild.. Winner advances in the Rampage tournament for the GCW World Heavyweight title. Caught up, Great, on we go.] Mooch: I still don't know why the black man had to call me a stupid ass and kick me in the head. Bammy: Maybe it's because you pulled a wad of Paula's hair out? [Mooch pulls his hand from his pocket, wielding an enormous bunch of black hair.] Mooch: That whore deserved it! [They continue their jaunt towards Gate 227, but stop abruptly at the sight of a midget waddling through the crowded airport. He has shifty eyes, and stops in front of a trash can. When he thinks no one is looking he pulls the front of the can open, revealing a secret door. Walking through he drops down and disappears. At this point Team Baked do what they normally do when they see a midget walking through an airport; follow it.] Mooch: MIDGET! MIDGET! [They break into a run, throwing up the secret door in front of the trash can. Expecting stairs, they're surprised as they begin to drop for 20 seconds down a shaft of some sort, landing in a heap on a padded floor.] Mooch: AWWW Fuck, I think I broke something. Bammy: You're sitting on my leg, gerofffa'me. Mooch: We really need to stop following midgets, I'm tired of falling through fucking trap doors in airports, man. [.. make your own conclusion.] [It's then they notice the GIANT door ahead of them. It's huge, 20 feet tall, seemingly solid gold. Two giant eagles adorn each side of the door, and two large gargoyles sit on pillars with flames in their mouths on each side of the hallway. Our hero's make it to their feet, and attempt to knock.. only to notice the texture of the door causes knocking to.. hurt. So they kick the door instead.] Bammy: HEY, OPEN UP! Mooch: PIZZA HUT! CHINESE FOOD! ROOM SERVICE! [A small piece of the door snaps open in the middle. You know, where you can only see the eyes of the person on the other side, and etc.] Person: Waht's teh passwurd? Bammy: Liverwurst. Person.. 'Ow the fuck did ju' know that? Mooch: Liverwurst is always the password. Person: But'chu guessed it, so I can't let'chu in, see. [The man on the other side of the door apparently realizes just who the hell he's talking to, and changes his tune.] Person: Oh shit, you's Team Baked right? Mooch: I'm Mooch. Person: I was talking to the both'a you's. Righ' the boss want a word with'chu, come in. [The double doors begins to swing open, revealing.. exactly what you should have predicted by now. A giant fucking drug workshop, owned and operated by midgets. Any sort of illegal substance you can imagine is being wrapped and shipped, in this shop. The short Italian Door Guard leads Team Baked through the mass display of vice.] Tiny Guido: See, I ain't know why teh boss wants'ya. I just know that he tell'ed me to let'chu in when you came. He said if one of our carriers let you see em', you'd follow em' for some reason. Mooch: We do like to follow midgets. Tiny Guido: Aye! Thats what he said, verbatim. 'Dey's like to follow midgets.' [The trio arrives at a small wooden door. Tiny Guido Does a special secret knock and it swings open. He departs, leaving Team Baked to duck into the frame and then the office. It's dark, damp smelling.] 'Have a Seat.' ================ 3. World War III. [The voice echoes through the small room, as Team Baked sit in a pair of small wooden chairs, built for a midget. The tops of their heads nearly scrape the roof as a small lamp is turned on. Standing opposite is THE LEADER. He's 3 feet of drug running fury, and he looks pissed.] Lead Midget: I don't know if you know this yet, but welcome to the largest underground Drug syndicate in the world. Any questions thus far? Mooch: Why the hell are you all midgets? Lead Midget: Simple, you feeble minded weed zombie. Tell me.. who suspects a midget? When you see a midget.. what do you do? Turn and snicker.. and keep taking small peeks. Would you ever accuse a midget? Ever stop and search a midget? No sir. So next time you're watching a midget waddle his way through a busy airport terminal, remember said midget is carrying 20 pounds of cocaine in his rectum. Score one for the good guys. Bammy: .. Genius. Lead Midget: Your damn right, you burned out disgrace. Mooch: Why did you bring us here? Lead Midget: I thought you'd have guessed that by now. Bammy: Duh, you want us to test your product! [Lead Midget walks over and smacks Bammy in the face.] Bammy: WHY THE FUCK DO I GET SMACKED EVERY FUCKING TIME? Lead Midget: We're not the only Syndicate. We're the west coast branch. For years trouble has been abrewing between us and the east coast faction over who gets the world wide accounts. After one too many tussles we've decided to go to War. They have.. a secret weapon. We're not quite sure what it is, but we need an answer.. Mooch: Let me get this straight. You're midgets, and you run drugs. You're going to war with a bunch of other drug running midgets, and you.. want our help? Is this my life, is this really what it's come to? Lead Midget: Bingo. Bammy: I don't know, we buy all our hash on the east coast. Lead Midget: Your file says that you're from Seattle. Bammy: Seattle weed tastes like Seymour Almasy's ass, and Tessa Windsor told me that's pretty bad. Lead Midget: .. What? Are you insulting my product? Mooch: No, of course not. We just like the green stuff.. not the brown. Lead Midget: That's it.. TAKE THESE TWO TO THE BOILER. I don't want any cops around here. [A hoard of midgets surround our hero's in a cartoonish 'HOW THE FUCK ARE WE GONNA GET OUTTA THIS?!' fashion.] ================ 4. The Secret Weapon. Mooch: Thanks a lot, Bammy. You just haaad to follow the midget. How the hell am I going to beat Rollins now? [We continue the tale with our hero's both tied up and ready to be shoved into a large fire place.] Bammy: You weren't going to win anyway. Mooch: HELP! HELP ME! Lead Midget: They can't hear you, fucktard. Shove em' in- [The leader can't finish his sentence before the door to the workshop is thrown open.. and standing their amongst a glowing aura is.. The Savior.] Bammy: It can't be.. Mooch: IT IS! .. .. .. .. .. .. CHUCK NORRIS! Lead Midget: NOOOOOO! NOT YOU AGAIN, NORRIS! Chuck Norris: Did I hear someone call for help? [Another gang of midgets attempt to restrain Norris. Pft. He dispatches all 30 of them with a might Roundhouse kick. A Monk enters the room behind Norris as The Chuckster continues his domination of the evil drug running midgets. 35 Seconds later their bodies adorn the floor of the workshop, and Norris stands victorious.] Monk: So concluded the slaughter of the evil midget drug running coalition- Bammy: They called it a Syndicate, actu- [The Monk smacks Bammy.] Bammy: AGAIN?! WHAT THE FUCK?! Monk: - At the hands of the mighty Chuck Norris. Chuck Norris: Remember Kids.. If you're ever about to be brutally attacked by a group of druggy midgets.. you know who to call. [We close this tale as Team Baked, the Monk, and Norris pose to an 80's style freeze frame as the Walker Texas Ranger theme plays.] Mooch: I actually like the MacGyver theme better, Narrator. Narrator: The story is over. Mooch: Dude, we PAY YOU. Narrator: Motherfuck.. [.. As the MacGyver theme plays.]
