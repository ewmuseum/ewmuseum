The Beginning Of Something Beautiful II

26 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/26/10 A win is what Lori Equinox required last week and that is exactly what he got. He went into Worldwide =124 with the pressure of having to win on his shoulders and still came out victorious. At the time of the win it brought a smile to his face. Also the fact that he was able to get the last word when he collided with Donny and Surreal made him greatly pleased. With another week of Worldwide to come he wondered what would be the next step in his journey. One that was obviously going to take a turn for the worse. His encounter last week with a mysterious group had left him uneasy at the best of times, looking over his should and wondering when he would be taken next, for he knew it wasn't over. He would turn out to be correct... or would he? Lori Equinox lays on his motel bed in Ft. Worth, Texas thinking about his upcoming match with Bryan Mayhem. Or the Lunatic as he also goes by. Lori knows all about lunatic's, obviously in believing he is one himselfm, making him ideal for this sort of match to be thrust upon him. Mayhem, a former Hardcore Champion, former Tag Team champion, two time TV Title Holder and ofcourse he has been at the pinnacle of GCW before holding the World Title. Instead of thinking to himself, Lori speaks outload to the ceiling. With his eyes focused on nothing but dust and molding paint. "So, Bryan Mayhemtihs time around. This'll be a tough one. Not like that Daria girl I beat last week. This guy is properly huge. I won't be able to go head on with him. I could but then I'd just be taking a beating and I don't really want to be doing that. I have seen this guy and I repsect that he can be a little insane and can enjoy hurting people. Then again he did get beat by Tessa Windsor last week. I mean, even I could beat her. I could probably beat everyone in this federation but I must take my time, show that I can believe in myself. In time others will believe in me." He stops for a minute and sits up on the bed. At the end of a room his reflexed stares back. He gives himself a good slap and talks to himself once more. "Lori, back to business." Lori falls backwards once again and hits the bed. "Bryan Mayhem is a big guy meaning he will be slower than me, so that mean that I'll have to use my speed to my advantage. I'll run rings round him, disorientate him and when that happens I strike... BANG!" Lori stops for a second after yelling and looks to either side, waiting for a patron in another room to smash the wall in anger. Nothing but silence engulfs the room though and as he takes a deep breath his body sinks into a peaceful trance. He closes his eyes and begins to speak once more, softly this time. "Don't want to wake anyone up, that's the last thing I need. So when I get into the ring with Mayhem a will strike when he least expects it and hell even when I least expect it. That way I'll definately catch him off guard. If he tries to send me to Oblivion I shall laugh in his face once I'm up in the air and punch him straight in the head, that always words." Lori chuckles to himself a little before tunring onto his right side, he takes in the silence once more, allowing it to flow through his body like some form of energy. Before he starts to drift off to sleep, as he does though strange things start to happen in his room. "What the...?" He opens his eyes to look at the wall he was facing when he turned over. Instead of being a slimey green colour starting to rot from the bottom it has changed to stone. He tries to find his bearings and looks around the room. Half of it is the room he remembers being in before but the other half resembles a castle's dungeon. A place they would keep traitors before having them executed. He tries desperately to stand up but now finds that his arms and legs have been shackled to the floors and the wall behind him. Beginning to panic he shifts himself about, trying to break free. This however just takes all the energy out of him. He lays back down but is not greated by a slightly comfy bed but hard rock. Not even flat rocks but pointed once that stab into his back. He repells off them in pain and kicks himself sitting upright. "Seriously... What the hell is going on here?" As he observes his new surrounds the stone walls begin to roll over the motels and eventually the entire room is encapsulated in darkness. Now the silence is no more, broken slightly by the shuffling of feet and dripping water. Lori closes his eyes once more, hoping that when he opened them once more that he'd be back where he belonged." "This can't be right. This can't be real." Lori begins to shake his arms violently to make as much noise as possible with the chains from the shackles. He screams out in rage more than anything. "What the fuck is going on here?! Let me out of here! I swear when I find out who you are you are dead, you can't stop me. You don't know who the hell I am! Show yourself you coward. Let me free and you shall surely know the wraith of..." Before he gets to call out his name his mouth his covered by a hand. It pushes his head against the stone wall cracking it failing hard. Lori falls to his side which cripples on impact with jagged rocks. He splutters out some blood but as he does some light returns to the room and he sees a figure standing beside him. As the figure moves further and further back he recognises who it is. It is Reo, the man who told him to win last week or there would be consequences. His mind begins to spiral wildly. I won last week! What does Reo want now? Was he not pleased that I won? Maybe he wanted me to lose? What does he want now? How did I get here? Damn my head hurts. Damn my side hurts more! Am I bleeding? I'm gonna kill him! Lori tries to summon up the breath to speak out to Reo but just can't do it. As Reo moves further back he does something unexpected and starts to pivot his mask bewards revealing himself to Lori. As Reo stares a hole through a truly stunned Equinox he only speaks two words. Two that would confude and shock Lori to the core... "I'm you." In an instant Reo disappears and the room is thrust into darkness once more. Lori shakes his head violently, not believing what he has just seen. That was me! He looks exactly like me! I can't take this! I have to get out of here! With all the energy he can use Lori through himself forward attempting to rip the shackles from the walls. He has no need to though as as he moves forward he is put back into the real world, in his motel room. In a cold sweat he checks his head and body for injuries. He also looks around the room to see if everything is still the same. He breathes a huge sigh of relief as he looks himself in the mirror. "A god damn dream! I've never been so happy to see mold in my life before. I must tell Elle." ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Meanwhile in Baltimore, a young, sharply dressed woman walks towards her car. She is around 6 feet tall and has long flowing black hair down to her shoulder blades. Her face is very defined but cute at the same time. The suit she is wearing is tailored to fit her perfectly. It is Elle Fortune, the girlfriend of Lori Equinox. Elle works as a real estate adviser all over the country. She just finished a job in Baltimore and now is about to take her hire car to the airport before she flies out to see her man in action. She lifts the keys out of her pocket for her Toyota Prius hire and unlocks it. Before she gets she brings her phone out and starts to dial Lori. "Hey babe." Before Elle even gets a chance to response the line is disconnected. She gets being dragged into a nearby car and taken away by two large men, quite likes the once who had similarly taken her boyfriend away just a week ago. In Texas, Lori dials Elle's phone franticly trying to get a response but there is nothing. He is almost resigned to believe she has just turned it off or her battery has died but then receives a test message... -Win or face the consequences. Do NOT tell anyone of this message, if so your girlfriend will feel severe pain. You better get ready for your match, we'll be in touch. Your friend. Reo- Lori is consumed by rage, he can't believe this is happened. Why would anyone do this to him. Unless it would be Surreal or Donny but even they wouldn't go to these lengths. "Bastards!" Lori slams his fist into the wall of the motel. "Once I beat Bryan Mayhem to within an inch of his life I will end this, once and for all." Lori lays back down on his bed and tries to calm himself down. It takes a matter of hours but eventually he drifts off to sleep.
