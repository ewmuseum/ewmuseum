The awakening.

10 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 10 2007 Quietly he walks in a slumber type of state. His mind is gathering information in the haze. The fog is thick as he walks. The earth beneath his feet is moist and muddy at times. Walking amongst the clouds or so he thinks. He feels as if he is standing atop the world until he comes to a stream, a stream flowing with blood. The blood looks dark and old as if it was flowing for years. His eyes are bloodshot and his face looks drained. He stares at the reflection in the water. Strength does not seem to be failing him as he walks past the stream flowing with blood. A large fire is burning in the distance. He begins walking towards it. Sitting before the fire is a large man with a cloak around his form. The man rivals his size he begins to notice. He looks at the man as he rises up in front of him and begins to speak. “Your past and destiny has been shown before you. Your eyes do not deceive you. You are looking at the unknown. Do you fathom the fact that it is yourself that has chosen each and every chosen path?” Looking at his own hands Shaman closes them slightly making a fist as he raises his eyes to speak. Realizing he does not have a mouth to speak. He has no voice or even the slightest way to speak. He narrows his eyes as he grows angry. He looks at the stranger clenching his fists. A fire burns in his eyes as if the flames of rage are being stoked. The man begins to chuckle as he looks at how frustrated and angry Shaman is. “You’re about as threatening as a child with a milk bottle. ? Smiling the cloaked figure slaps Shaman across the face as Shaman slowly turns his gaze back to the man. Steam begins to rise from his body as if he is over heating the burning blaze in his eyes grows brighter as a voice from out of Shaman rumbles deep and threatening as the cloaked figure steps backwards. “I control my destiny here. I control my voice and you are the one who has crossed the line.” Reaching forward Shaman grabs the man and throws him towards the fire watching him fall into it. He simply stares as he turns and walks away. “My destiny is my own regardless of the demons from my past that I have buried comeback and haunt me, Fear is for the weak and I fear nothing.” Shaman begins walking through the mist and fog again. His eyes are focusing as he watches and stares studying as the world becomes daylight before him. A single lane asphalt road looms before him rounding several bends and twisting through the green lush forested area surrounding him. Stopping he watches as bag pipes begin playing somewhere in the distance. He begins to walk towards the bag pipes as deep drums and a chanting is heard. He walks towards the sounds as the road slowly turns into a trail a small dirt trail winding through a deep jungle. As he walks a snake slithers out upon the path. This snake is a large python and rises up to look Shaman in the eye. He watches the snake unmoving as the snake watches him swaying slightly back and forth ever so slowly. The snake strikes forward at Shaman who nimbly grabs the snake just behind the head as he side steps it. Shaman turns the hissing snake and stares at it in the eyes. The snakes hisses out as it speaks. “Traveller why have you come to my lands” You have come to be consumed by me. You are my meal for you are not strong for I am the strongest there is. The world around you is not of your world. This is where I am one and all at the sametime.” Reaching up Shaman grabs the snakes mouth and forces it closed as the snake struggles to no avail. Shaman looks at the snake closely and shakes his head as he squeezes drawing blood from where his fingers press into the snakes neck. Shaman looks at the snake and stares as blood finally pours through the mouth of the creature. Shaman speaks in a low monotone voice to the snake. “These are my lands now. You control nothing here. I am in complete control and nothing will stand before my ultimate goal. I am reclaiming all and the word has begun to spread. There is no fighting amongst my inner demons or peaceful lands. I am destiny and I am the chosen to control my dreams.” Shaman drops the snake upon the ground as the snake is in its throws of death as Shaman disappears into the underbrush looking at the world as the sun shines through the canopy high over head. As he walks he comes to a wall a great wall stretching high above as he stares at it his mind studies the massive structure. He looks down and notices a single sledge hammer and reaches down and grabs it. Holding it he looks at the wall and stares at it. He reaches up and looks at the wall as he places a hand upon it. “I have broken down greater walls in my life so far and this wall is no different.” Bringing his hand back down to the handle of the sledgehammer he raises it and bring it down upon the wall with a loud slamming motion that thunders and echoes through the lands nearby. Moments later?. Waking Shaman raises up and looks at the window as lightning flashes and thunder rolls. His eyes focused on the world beyond the room he is sitting in. He turns and notices a naked and beautiful Hawaiian woman in a deep slumber. Kawanaha had been his closest confidante and he feels the have become friends as he and she have shared pieces of their lives and past that would make most people run away in fear. He did not care what others had thought he gently ran a hand across her skin. Knowing she was a complex and dangerous woman in different ways. Knowing this he watched as the beauty slept and the inner turmoil that raged within her. He pondered why she seems to have his best interest at heart at times. He was not fearful of her and to an extent he trusted her. Trust came hard for him he thought. Standing up he slide out of the bed and walked to the window. His features only brightened by the lightning. He reaches over and checks his messages on the cell phone. He frowns as he picks it up and types in his code for the messages to play. He listens as the first one played. “You Shaman this is Jimmy. The bets are opened for the wrestling and it seems that many have come to see what is going on. Do you want in on the pool big man” I am sure you will make a splash. Well anyways I am heading over to big Jimmy’s a little later give me a buzz.” Deleting the message he turns and looks at the lightning roll in the clouds. Shutting his cell phone he sets it back down upon the dresser as he watches the storm rage outside as much as the storm that is raging within his being. “Jayce Bradley our time is closing in on us quicker than you have thought. Things are going barreling out of control. Neither one of us are grasping the reality of the outcome. Rest assured though regardless I am prepared. Are you?” Shaman turns and walks across the dark room and reaches up and touches a mantle that holds all of his previous titles including the belt he wore as GCW TV Champion. He reaches up and touches each one with his hand gently looking at them and then brings his hand to a mask. A mask he wore as Famine the first Horseman of Apocolypse. His path of destiny had come full circle once again. He turns his head away as he looks out at the darkness and whispers. “I have become a master of my own fate. The fate of others has become Oblivion.” Rubbing his forehead he looks back at Kawanaha. He then turns and looks back out at the storm as it slowly begins to pass him by. He knows that the world continues to turn. Knowing he can control everything around him and allowing those to believe he plays a deadly game of cat and mouse with Jayce Bradley. They have cost each other titles and matches. They both have endured many interesting situations to only comeback out on top once again. His hand slowly taps a folder on top of the dresser once again as he returns to it. He opens it up looking at it closely. He knows what is going on and realizes that each every little piece of business is systematically placed before him. He waits and has always watched and now the moves are being made much like a chess board. Bradley is merely a piece that has come to be in his way. A place where Bradley does not wish to be. A place that will be very painful for him if he continues to follow Shaman down this path. “Bradley you have followed me this far. Do you intend on keeping pace with me or are you starting to realize how dangerous your path is. Your following me into this may be cause for your own concern. You will never be able to return to where you once were in this situation.” Shaman turns and walks away as he walks over to his bed and sits down on it. He reaches over and drinks a glass of water as a hand is reaching over his shoulder as it reaches up and turns his face and his face turns into Kawanaha as she kisses him deeply and she smiles at him and lays back down in the bed as he turns and gazes at the window. Laying back on the bed Kawanaha lays herself up against him as he gazes at the ceiling as he smiles. “Jayce Bradley do you even realize that I am in control. It is tiem to put up or shut up. The future is now and the end is nigh.” Smiling he then lays back as he gently runs a hand slowly through Kawanaha’s hair as she smiles contently and slumbers as he soon begins to fall asleep once again.
