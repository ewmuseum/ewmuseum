Chapter 26: The Rise and Fall of a Leader- (Part III)

9 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 9, 2007 __Start Typical. Everything is so damn typical. What ever happened to the asshole? What ever happened to the man known mostly for belittling and degrading? Simple. He died. And taking his spot, a resurrected half eaten version of the man he once was. Chivalry and curtousy become the foundation in which greed and disgust once lived. A heart of darkness deflates and is filled again with a new air commonly found in a hero. The man becomes kind and swallows every insult thrown his way to make sure the one person he somehow feels for lives with a smirk on her lips. Some call it being whipped. Others refer to it as love. No matter which end you look at it though, it's no more than an excuse. A plain and straight bullshit excuse. Chapter 26: The Rise and Fall of a Leader Part III Two weeks ago The trail of dust jumping from the sands of the dessert leapt into his hindsight making it impossible to see anything behind or around him. Luckily, he didn't have to worry about anyone following or any obstacles in the terrain he was in. The Camo Green jeep pushed onto the fourth mile of six and still Jay wasn't quite sure where he was heading. It wasn't like him to take orders from a piece of paper, especially orders relating to saving another. He wasn't exactly the helpful type. Still, there was something in his chest that reminded him, every chance it got, that somehow what would happen to Candice was his fault. He was the one with the ego. He was the one pleasuring himself on her attraction to him. If he had let her go to do her job she wouldn't have waited the extra five minutes for him after her shift. She'd be driving home thinking of what she could do to spice up her mediocre life. And for that reason, Jay felt compelled to pursue. The mile marker rolled over for a fifth time as he focused his eyes back on the sand in front of him. It was a blank canvas, so to speak, where endless possibilities laid waiting for him. Only if he were some almighty omniscient narrator or something. His eyes narrowed and in the near distance, he could see the van from before. It was still as if it was waiting for him. Jay slowly picked his foot off the pedal and shifted it to the brake until there was no more movement. The engine cut off and his heart pounding was the only sound left. 'Candice.' He was scared to leave the Jeep just yet. There was something fishy about the scene that kept him from doing anything stupid. Only if it would kick in more often then maybe he wouldn't find himself in such predicaments. 'Hello.' He called again. No answer. His heart picked up louder and clogged his ears with a constant thump. It was time to look around. Terror opened his door and looked backward and forward before stepping out onto the dirt. His shoes kicked up a cloud of the brown smog with each step he took away from safety. 'Candice, Hello.' No one called back once more creating a greater feeling of fear in his stomach. If you ever wondered, Fear in your stomach hurts like no other. 'Anyone...' He stepped to the other side of the van and that's when he saw her. Bound and gagged, eighteen year old Candice tried to wiggle free. 'Holy shit.' __ Like any story about a kind man, the obvious occurs: He lets down his guard for just one moment. In that moment, anything could happen. You name it, it's gone down. It's almost like an unwritten rule in life, kinda' like Murphy's Law or something. Whatever you want to call it, it takes over the generous soul and BANG! It happens. Sometimes it's less discrete and even nothing to worry about but one thing that's for sure is the fact that something happens. And to think, they figured nothing could go wrong. __ Jay, like a valiant warrior drove his knee into the dust and dirt of the ground and took his place at the side of Candice. The bandanas around her arms and in her mouth were fairly easy to take off and once free from the bindings, Jay looked over her. She caught her breath slowly and after a minute seemed relieved he had saved her. 'Hey.' It was all he mustered. 'Hey.' She breathed hard still. 'Thank you.' 'No problem.' And then came a silence. There was never anything good about silence and that's why he made up his mind. He would lie to keep things going. 'I came back and you had vanished so I figured you didn't want to see me.' 'Then how did you find me here?' The sentence was long enough to make her gasp. 'Well that's the rest of my story, the people left a note.' 'A note?' Terror nodded, 'Yes, a note.' And that's when he froze and brought his own silence. Everything rushed back to his head and suddenly clear thoughts took over his charming lies. 'Shit.' He turned around and as he did the side door of the van swung open. Bandanas veiled three men's faces and at once they leapt out of the vehicle. Candice screamed. 'What do you guys want?!' He could feel something sharp dig into his thigh. The last thing he heard was Candice's scream as it faded away to nothingness. __ So what's a man to do when he's been outsmarted? Does he sit back and let his tormentor take the best of him? Does he say 'yes sir' and 'of course' with hopes of being freed? Or perhaps he stands up and shows some fortitude with a few words of his own? Of course, it all depends on the man in the situation but if it were any true male the third option would be very satisfying. So with the bollocks of a drunken Irish fellow, the outsmarted man takes it upon himself to say a little something. Is he making the right choice or is it possible that he is sealing his own casket? What if they don't listen and instead feel like ripping the tendons in his neck loose and kicking his head around the room? Unfortunately, no one ever knows until they try. __ Jay had grown use to waking up in strange places. More often than ever before, the last few months had been filled with odd places and new people. This time, his eyes found the walls of the van instead of a room. The floor, thankfully, wasn't moving and he could see the faces of his tormentors. How didn't he guess? 'Disappointing Jay. Very disappointing.' Art Jankey, the voice of the bandits, spoke in a low and thoughtless voice. His bandana wrapped around his neck as if to keep it warm. 'What are you doing Art?' 'What am I doing?' He paused to get closer to his friend. 'Jay, what are you doing? I thought you were a bandit for life. I thought you told us that you would live the life of the bandits forever. What the fuck Jay.' Terror tried to move but like usual, he was tied down for safety precautions. 'You can't just go kidnapping girls Art.' 'I can't?' He smirked a sick smirk; it was almost too gross to watch. 'Jay, I can do whatever I want. Remember, we all can....or most of us at least.' He looked up and down Terror with distrust in his eyes. 'What do you want from me?' 'We wanted our leader...but I guess that has gone down the shitter, eh?' 'You want me to lead you guys to what? I'm a professional wrestler, not a villain. I don't spray-paint walls and take candy anymore guys.' Art made his anger clear with a stiff backhand to Jay's face. A spot of blood popped out from his lips and painted his cheek. 'Fuckin' disappointment, that's all you are.' He followed with a second slap, this time connecting with the opposite side of his face. 'Just a no good fuckin' disappointment. Where the fuck did Mr. no rules go? Where did the guy that disrespected women go? You were a man among boys back in the day and now all you are is a sniveling hero. Fuck you Jay.' He wanted to check his lip but due to the restraints on his hands he couldn't. Instead he licked the blood and tasted the pungent liquid. 'You don't know me Art.' 'I don't? You tried to save the girl you blew off, didn't you?' 'She was kidnapped.' Art shook his head, 'You tried to fuckin' save her you son of a bitch. Bandits don't save people like her.' 'Bandits don't give up on brothers either.' And then he paused. His face showed apprehension but his actions were frozen. The scene resembled a brilliant still life portrait made to show the tension between brothers. Magnificent. 'You're not a fuckin' bandit. Not anymore.' 'Then kill me. Do something more then belittle me.' 'Maybe I will.' 'Good.' Silence. They stared back and forth at one another for a solid three minutes without making a noise. The men on the left and right of Art, silent as ever, watched and wondered what was going to happen. Art was the first to break the silence. 'We need you back Jay. We need direction and a cause.' Terror nodded and blinked a few times. 'Direction to what? I'm done rebelling against society like I used to. I may be vulgar but I'm not the man you want to be.' 'How about wrestling? Can we join with you?' 'You can't wrestle.' 'Neither can you.' 'True,' Jay paused and found too much reality to enjoy in the statement, '...but you guys can't. I work alone out there.' 'Then we will help keep you in line.' It was odd seeing Art jump from vicious to helpful so quickly. 'How?' 'We'll keep you focused. You can't be a hero and win matches.' Truth again. For being a thug, Art Jankey made a lot of sense. Nearly everything he had said in the night hit the nail on the head. Surely him helping would end in the same results. 'Untie me and you have a deal.' __ So you try and succeed? Kinda' makes you wonder what would have happened if you would have nodded and said 'yes sir', huh? Nevertheless, everything comes around and just like that, something comes over your body and mind that has been absent for far too long. Power, leadership, domination....all of it combines in one bubble and bursts in your mind creating an explosion of ideas. Life becomes better than it ever was. So what do you do with freedom? What do you do with control beyond the horizon? Use it. Live by it. Abuse it. __ Today He was so confused. A lost soul traveling the halls of a busy school. How could he go from being on top of the world to feeling like he was melting in the earth’s core? It just didn’t make sense to him. His hand struck the punching bag with thunderous clap opening his mind to real life again and allowing the waters of confusion to pour out. “What’s wrong Jay.” Art had been around Jay for long enough since their reunion that he could tell something was troubling him. Perhaps it was the way he hit the bag. “Who am I?” “The Television champion, Jay Terror.” Art reassured him. “I don’t feel like a champion. Where’s my belt” Where’s my trophies? I have nothing but scars and humiliation to show.” Art shook his head, “Stop this Jay. Fuckin” stop it now.” Jay seemed confused by the request. “Stop what?” “Stop getting all emo on me. You know what I mean. You shouldn’t be depressed about DeBough and GCW; you should be pissed. The only place you’re going to end up with this kind of attitude is with the federation’s psychologist.” “I don’t think they have one.” “Not the point. You need to sound and look like a champion. Champion’s don’t cry and ask who they are. They know who they are. They know where they stand in this business. You’re the golden boy in GCW and you’ll prove it when you walk into Dangerous Games.” He liked the sound of it but there was something about his monologue that reminded him of a politician’s speech. “How do I get anywhere when I can’t touch the guy I want to kill?” “In time my friend, in time. You need to forget about not being able to attack DeBough though and use that anger toward beating everyone else in the way. You can’t dwell on Caldera’s tricks and schemes like this.” “Right.” “You just need a fuckin” cig Jay, that’s all.” He couldn’t agree with him anymore. With one last, forceful might I add, hit to the bag Jay and Art walked to the bench with their stuff. Jay found his box of 27s and lit up two for the pair. It took all of two minutes to attract the attention of the gym attendant. “There’s no smoking in here.” “You work here?” Jay asked laughingly. “Yes, why?” “I figured people had to be fit to work at a gym.” Art looked at his friend and giggled a little. His abrasive attitude and harsh choice of words were what made him so great in Jankey’s eyes. “Look, you’ve got to either put those out or leave before I get help to remove you two.” You could tell he held his sadness in from the fat comment. “Call “em?” Jay stopped Art. “We’ll leave. We’re done anyways. Just promise me you’ll take my place today and work off that pouch of yours.” The man walked away without saying anymore. Most likely if he would of things would end up being a scene. Jay and Art kept to the promise though and off they were. Bandits for life. __End
