Expert Analysis

20 August 2007

Date: 20th August Phil: Is this thing on? Dick: Yes of course it's bloody on. Why else would the on light be on? Phil: Voodoo magic? Phil Atken and Dick Dirkwood found themselves in a room. In this said mentioned room there was a camera facing Mr. Atken and Mr. Dirkwood was hiding far away behind said filming object. Phil: So I should start doing that intense promo thing all the cool kids do? Dick: Yes, I'm not starting this again, just bloody well start the thing. Phil: But why won't you start again. Dick: Last time I had to re-record the tape caught on fire. Phil: Is that why I smell barbecue? Dick: No, that's a barbecue. This was weeks ago. Phil: Were you making this kinky sex tapes with your wife again. Dick: No, I was recording over them so I didn't have my children asking what a scat fetish is. Phil: ... Dick: What? Phil: You just said it on this tape. Dick: Oh, that's fine. My kids would never watch wrestling. They're far too cool for that. Phil: I should be offended about that, yet I'm still trying to imagine your seed producing cool children. Dick: WOULD YOU JUST DO THE BLOODY PROMO. Phil: Who am I facing again. Dick: Tyson DeBough. Phil: Didn't I beat him that one time already? Dick: Pasts don't count in wrestling. You beating him does not get you a title by default. Phil: He has a title? Dick: He STOLE a title. Phil: Good man, I've done that many a time. Best way to get a title shot if you ask me. You steal the guys title, pretend it's yours say you'll give it back if he puts it up in the title defense, you punch him in the nuts with brass knucks and become the champ. I remember in my younger days it was a trick that always went down a treat. Dick: Your younger days were five years ago? Phil: Yes, why? With a situation like this, I can offer my expert analysis as I'm all too familiar with it. Dick: Just wondering. Phil: I remember this other time when I beat up this chick and stole her Cruiserweight title. Then I drilled a ladder into her head and won the title officially. That was good times. Dick: So you steal titles to get title match's often? Phil: No, sometimes I win a title then take it to another promotion to make myself look important for a debut. Dick: Why haven't you done this these days? Phil: Well these days I suffer from being lazy. Honestly stealing belts was too much effort, those things are bloody heavy. Then you have to deal with all the legal challenges and BLAH DE BLAH BLAH BLUR. I admire the spirit of someone who can pull it off though. Never seen a flaw with such a plan. Dick: So you won a title out of each of your kidnapping. Phil: Most times, apart from the time I was handcuffed to the ring ropes and crushed with a steel chair. It backfired that time. Dick: So what about Tyson, how do you fancy his chances. Phil: I'll be honest here, as more or less an outsider of the situation. He's completed phase one, which is very much all well and good, the problem is he's now cocky about his situation. Now this can work, the problem is if you go around stealing title belt's you really shouldn't then go on to say how you're going to destroy someone like myself in the ring. The problem is since you've stolen the belt you are already perceived to be a coward as you haven't actually won a match to show yourself as being a title holder. A smart guy will obviously play up this angle and lull the actual title holder into a false sense of security. This is why DeBough's plan is flawed. Dick: Why is that exactly? Phil: Well he's now doing title defenses with the belt, which is stupid in itself because it leaves the belt open to a counter-steal. Not only that but his first fake title defense is against me, a man who has mastered this situation time and time again. I mean fuck it, if I still had the energy I'd just steal it from him and run off. By putting up and false front of courage, he's actually putting Jay Terror on alert for his abilities. He's putting himself out there for easy scouting, not to mention if you really NEED to have a fake title defense, you really should do it against some jobber. I've done that before too. Defeated a jobber to call myself an official champion. Dick: And you're not a jobber? Phil: Well this is where things get interesting Dicky old boy. In a way, I could recently be classed as a jobber, I mean last time I saw ON the show I was wrestling against a man who fashioned his name after a sewing pattern. What the christ is that, I have to wonder. Being the new guy is always a smidge annoying as you're meant to prove yourself but a man of my standards really shouldn't have to waste his time with such awfulness. However, I did try to use the Penis Spike in a previous federation and as such I have to climb that ladder. However, the reason why it's interesting is although I am VIEWED as a jobber to this untrained chimps, me and you know very differently. We are both thouroughly aware of my knowledge and experience. Dick: Well, I'm mildly aware. You certainly did suck a lot less before I met you. Phil: Why do you always have to bring that up, I do have feelings don't you know. The heart of this matter is that I do find it amusing that someone has went in front of a camera, sat there and opened their yaps to say they're going to destroy me. Now that's something I would have never said in my life unless I was talking about their pathetic careers. When you sit in front of a camera and promise you will be beat a self-proclaimed veteran of this industry as an EXAMPLE after you just stole a title. That's as they say in Boston, the state not the band, wicked retarted. Dick: Retarted? Phil: I dunno, I rarely go to Boston. Dick: I watched Boston Legal once. Phil: William Shatner is quite good in it. Dick: As true as that may be we should probably get back on topic. Phil: Alright, I'll keep this one short and simple, DeBough I'm too smart to be used as an example and the only thing you can do in this situation is lose. You've left yourself open to letting Jay Terror gain his title back DURING the match. You've left yourself open to LOSING your fake title defense. Most of all, you already lost any tough guy credibility by stealing a title. You had so many chances to do something good the minute you stole that belt that it breaks a former belt stealer's heart that you could fumble so much. This looks like a fumble so bad, the ball's ended up in the river ten miles down the road. Adding me into the mix just means the ball was then eaten by a shark. Phil: To make a long story short, you should never try and make the master of this very game plan your example. Dick: That was a lot of words. Phil: Well, you have to be serious on camera because then people will think "this man means business". Although I miss the people who would think I meant business from weapons shots. Those days were much simpler times. You would jump out of the crowd and drill a person with a steel chair. They'd fall over, you'd win a title. Those were fun times and because you already had a title they knew you meant business. These days you have to make an effort and win matches to prove yourself, it's all very depressing. Dick: Is it depressing because you have to make an effort. Phil: Certainly is. However those are the hurdles of life and you must leap over the to defeat communism. You can use the hurdles as a weapon after you leap over them to defeat communism. When trying to defeat communism you should always try and have the upper hand. Dick: What are you talking about. Phil: God knows, I'm going to go piss. And with that the camera in the exciting back offices of the Dick Dirkwood agency was cut off. "Phil, wait up" yelled Dick as he was heading for a slash. "I really need to pee Dick, can you make this quick." replied Phil while he enjoyed a rendition of the peepee dance. "I'm just wondering. You seemed a little off there. A bit more... heavier and less smug. I just found it odd, you seemed like you were off your game and you're never off the ball promo wise." "It's just a bitter crash back down to earth. As much as I hate to admit it, the struggle of going from being a name player back to a sidelines guy, it takes you off your guard. I can be cocky, I can be smug, I can be as much an asshole as possible. I can grab that megaphone to yell about cock sucking, I can still very much do all that but I can't do it from the bottom of the ladder. You have to have pull before you can move up a step Dick, I've known that for ages. It's why I always ALWAYS made my debut's impactful. This time around I barely beat some idiot named after knitwear. It just reminds me of those who trained me." "Those idiots were never names though." "Well that's not strictly true, one of them may never have been an on-camera name, but he certainly knew how to be a backstage puppet master. Sadly a few years back the strings got cut, I say sadly when I don't actually mean it. The guy was a fucking scuzzbag and actually fired me from my first job inside the industry. The point is, I was there when that penny dropped and he told me that once you fall in this industry, the climb back up is practically non-existent. Now I sit here in GCW, fully aware that I'm no longer a player but very much a pawn and I remember those words." "Surely he was just saying that to screw with you. I mean he knew the hammer was going to fall the minute he got kicked out the door, he probably said it to screw with your head." "That was my first thought when I found sweet gigs elsewhere but as time went on, my name value dropped. The less value I had, the less television time I would be allowed, the less time I really had to rebuild myself." "You have that opportunity again Phil." "Yeah, yeah, sure whatever. I'm going to take a dump." "I thought it was a piss." "That too."
