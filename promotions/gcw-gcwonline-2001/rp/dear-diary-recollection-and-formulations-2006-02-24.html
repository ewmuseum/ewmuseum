Dear Diary: Recollection and Formulations

24 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/24/2006 Dear Diary, There comes a time in everyone's life when they are forced to re-evaluate who they are and the path they have chosen to walk. Some would tell you this major epiphany only comes once in a lifetime. These are the people I call fools, charlatans even. If I were to sit down and count the times I had a major re-evaluation of my life the total would five now. The first was upon the discovery of betrayal by a man whom I was betrothed too. The second came not long after my entry into the ranks of the Global Championship Wrestling roster. The third and, likely, the toughest I had faced up to this point was just after the attack that left me hospitalized for a week and emotionally crippled for much longer than that. The fourth...well everyone knows the fourth. A chair across the back by the man whom you were involved with for six months is a definite eye opener. And then.... There is now. It has been said that one must hit the bottom of the barrel before they can start the climb back up. I personally believe I crashed through the bottom and landed in the emotional pits of hell itself. As with all my problems that have caused me to look back on my life and wonder what I have been doing wrong, this one also involved a man. Men...the bane of my existence. His name is Johnny Lexicon. Over six feet, around 230 pounds, chiseled like a Greek god, hair of sun kissed blond and a crooked smile that would make you believe there really was ocean front property in the state of New Mexico..... That was Johnny, the man who broke my heart. I guess in all honesty it all started just before I joined GCW. I met Johnny through a mutual friend of ours. One Jordan White, a real piece of work who had initially hired me to help him take Johnny to bank....ruptcy. As always I took to the part I played like a bee to honey, luring that Adonis like man into my web only to destroy everything he held dear. Unfortunately for me, I had never expected the one thing to happen that did. I had fallen for the man and not even realized it. He vanished for a while after losing everything to his good 'friend' Jordan only to resurface a few months later a changed man. Gone was the ego that corrupted him replaced by a sense of spiritual worth. With the loss of that ego so, too, did I lose my weapon against him. His charisma, however, had remained intact. I may have never known it was him behind that mask if not for the incidents leading up to him protecting me from others, including myself. In the end he turned out to be the best friend one could have and I loved him for it. I still love him. Oh what a tangled web we weave and I have been tangled in my own for far too long. Only recently have I realized that my attention has been split for far too long. When I should have had my focus completely on wrestling and putting on a good show for the fans, I was focusing my attention on whatever man held my fancy. No more. No more will I allow my heart to override my brain. No more will I allow men to dictate my life's path. No more will I be a victim to my own faults. It is time that I show what I am truly worth. It is time I take back what is mine. It is time for me to become a true contender and set my eye on the gold. I lost gold because of my split attentions. A fool's folly to allow my emotions to overrule my want for success. Sam Wolack....of all people, I lost to Sam Wolack. I am not downplaying his abilities in the ring, but damn it, I know I am better than the pitiful performance I put in. It is down right aggravating to watch as the title that should have been mine still is defended against another. Poor pitiful me? No. Honestly it doesn't matter who wins it. I -will- get it back and this time someone will have to pry it from my dead cold fingers, or I may just give it back in favor for something bigger. The road to the gold will be longer than usual this time with so many new faces out there..speaking of new faces. There is one I recognize from a recent exhibition match I was in. Shadow... The memory of her ripping my clothes off still remains fresh in my mind. Cheap shots seem to be the M.O. of this new group. Watching the shows has shown them to be a nasty underhanded group with no sense of honor...kind of reminds me of myself back in the day. The mere thought brings a snide little smile to my lips. With that thought other thoughts begin to formulate. Perhaps it is time someone does something about them? They want to play games, then maybe it is time someone shows them how it is really done. Yes....I think it is high-time GCW gives them a right warm welcome..but first I need to set my sights on my upcoming match. Time to break out of my shell and reintroduce everyone to the true diva of GCW...time to bring the bitch.... Back.
