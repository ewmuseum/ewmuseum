Happy People (Part 1)

Date: This week Though the meteorologist's reports for the greater Houston area the day before Devil's Night called for partly cloudy weather, everything was bright under clear blue skies for the festivities. They, Ascension, were at least accomodated in the way of cooperative atmospheric conditions. Ciel arrived first, a number of people in tow. There were his daughters: Tania (15), Rafaela (11), and Ines (4). One who knew Skylar would remark that the children looked nothing like him, to say nothing of the fact that he'd have to have started fathering these kids at the age of 12. It was more than just adoption between them; he'd known them by his friend Javier long before they were his responsibility through bequested guardianship. As well, Wulong (16), Ciel's tennant/pupil, accompanied him to help watch Green's daughters. Last, and perhaps least depending on who was asked, there was Saadra Mehta (25) in one of her rare appearances -- anywhere -- with Sky and his family. They were sitting out by the water of Lake Houston, just absorbing the sun and the scenery. But they were further off, out of earshot; one wouldn't think that they were a part of the festivities. This was a vacation for them, but still it was primarily what Tessa Windsor's voice-mail explained to everyone in the stable: a picnic to help connect everyone together. “I’m sorry I’m”..” After catching glance of Ciel, his kids, and Bradley scattered around the field, the leader of Ascension suddenly felt less guilty about the call home that ran a half hour longer than she’d expected. Apparently, things weren’t as well coordinated as she’d hoped. That whole leadership thing seemed so much easier when she was nothing more than Lestatt and Eva’s apprentice. Now, it was obvious that if things were going to go the way she hoped at Devil's Night, the whip was going to have to be cracked on someone. Just who, she wasn’t sure of yet. Hey, look who's here! Tania: Only a few minutes late... she must not be as stressed as you said she was. The man only known as 'Ciel' during GCW events shakes his head. The lack of tact his daughter could display was amazing at times. Rafaela most likely had no clue what social faux pas that her older sister had committed; Ines, being only 4 as of November 1st, definitely had no clue. They could be forgiven though, as the younger sisters who didn't have much experience with people outside of their classmates and family. Sky could expect more of the 15-year-old Tania... particularly that if he holds her in regard enough to speak to her about the things he observes and deals with as an adult that she prove that she has that maturity by not then going up to another person as though she has any real business talking about it to anyone but her father. 'Late? Only in the instance of the designated time,' Green waved her over. He was wearing a yellow solid Polo T-shirt and jeans, a far cry from his usual ring outfit that he's always seen wearing on GCW television. 'In relative terms, you're still doing better than three others. You're normally early, though, so I take it that some important personal business kept you.' The way that Sky spoke with Tessa, he made connections in his mind about what may have happened. Plausible occurrences, most likely events... but he wouldn't box her in to his idea of her. Even now, hypothesizing why she was late, his tone indicated that he was only wondering aloud about it. If she wished to fill him in, the inviting gesture, smile, and inflection signalled as much. If not, that would be fine; there was plenty else they could talk about. Her eyes shifted down, not to hide, but to keep modest. She never was one to brag, even about her big life events. Instead she lifted her eyes back up away from her hand and offered a mild smile. “Just a few things that I’m finally getting around to.” Given that her dressy-attire lacked pockets, she merely folded one hand on top of the other and sat down on a bench, looking back up at Sky to ask the all imporant question, “So where’s everyone else?” Tania: Not here. Oh yes, she's definitely win the Miss Obvious award for 2006. It's a very prestigious title, usually reserved for the women on The View. But a young one is in the running this time around. Even for being disappointed that Tania felt the need to add that, Sky had to stop himself from worrying about it too much. He realized that it was something he would say; she was getting to be like him. But she didn't have a mother figure, really; only Green as a role model of any kind. She was always a bit tomboyish, but him being her sole guardian it reinforced, perhaps encouraged it. There was no way he could serve to guide her on the finer points of womanhood. That she figured out puberty on her own for the most part was something for which Sky had thanked the deities of many religions, and prayed that the other two would be the same. 'Jayce is over at the tables,' Sky said, pointing to the covered picnic table area. There was room for 10 tables seating about 60 people comfortably, but far less space was needed as this wasn't a very people-heavy picnic and it was still the middle of the week, the middle of the work day. Not many people would be here, or around, or anything like that. 'Not very talkative. If I were to guess, he's got extreme trust issues, to the point of not even being able to engage in small talk much. Whatever the reason, hard to inquire into it.' “We’ll break him in sooner of later.” General curiousity left her turning away from Sky to peak and see just what he considered anti-social. Sure enough, there was Bradley, alone, seemingly bored out of his mind. It wasn’t a wonder they werent a cohesive unit if they couldn’t even manage to entertain each other. It bothered Tessa to the point that she started to veer away from Sky, and over towards Bradley. Thankfully Sky was smart enough to realize it wasn’t him, rather her own friendly nature. So rather than take offense to seeing her backside, he just went back to preparing the food on the grill. The burgers (both of the filet mignon -- that's right, nothing but the best -- and Boca varieties) needed to be flipped anyway. Meanwhile, Ms. Windsor steered herself over toward Jayce Bradley. He sat there in the shade provided by the painted wooden roof. Unlike some other parks, Houston kept this one well-maintained. The red-hued paint that covered the picnic roof structure as well as the tables was covered with a vinyl weathering finish that kept it smooth to the touch while also being hard to stain. And there weren't any holes above to drop the sun into his eyes, Bradley watching as she came closer and closer. She was stressed, not totally relaxed as she approached. She was still less on edge since the events of WW53, with La Sombra and Ciel being attacked by the Brigade. That could not be allowed to happen again. 'Tessa... I need to talk to you about everything that's going on,' he starts before she even sits down. Tessa folded her arms across her chest. 'I'm listening,' she says, letting him be the first to speak before she goes into anything. She really did want to figure out what was going on in Jayce's head, much as did Sky. Even Dorian was more upfront with her. Bradley began apologetically. 'I tried my best to help out at Dangerous Games, but it wasn't enough.' He wasn't just talking about the First Blood match against Vivica Valentine, though that was absolutely stolen from him. No, it was also the actual Dangerous Games match, which saw Jayce provisionally ally with members of The Ascension to stay in the match and fight against the then-newly-merged Knightmare Brigade:Dies Irae. Bradley did well for himself, benefitting from the brief team-up by placing in the top ten in the battle royale. 'Then, after I saw what they did to you all...' he went on, referring to WW53. For a moment, Jayce trailed off, but came back in short order with a line that seemed to snap him out of whatever funk he was about to get into for bringing up some memory. 'It brought it all back. Tessa Windsor had the patience of a saint most of the time, but understandably she wants him to get to the point. Jayce has had a while to loaf about and think things through; even to explain himself. By now, he needed to cut through the fat to say what he needs to say. And she prompted him with, 'What are you trying to say, Bradley?' The former Cruiserweight champion, almost U.S. Champion, nodded. 'I want in. We can't keep running around with our heads cut off, and while I'm no leader... I sure as hell know how to fight.' Tessa nodded, saying nothing quite yet. Bradley went on to say, ' KB:DI need to be taken down, but in order to do that... you need to bring us together. You need to do what Eva failed to do.' While Tess agreed, she admitted, 'I can't do it alone. You of all people should know that.' She'd work on her confidence starting yesterday, but confidence doesn't mean that a person can do anything they want and do it alone. Tessa still needed help from her friends, she always has. If that made her weak in a sport that seemed to reward and encourage the individualistic asshole, then so be it. 'I know that I'm tired of watching them squeeze the life out of GCW,' Jayce said of KB:DI. 'We have to loosen their grip, even if it is just one finger at a time. We strike them where it hurts the most...' Windsor went to the first thought, the most logical answer. 'The titles...' The Brigade was a title closet, owing in part to their skill (no one will take it away from them that they know how to wrestle) and in part to their numbers. Over 10 people together in the same super-stable; you're going to collect gold. Blaise McClancy held the Cruiserweight Title won from Tessa Windsor on WW53. Bryan Mayhem took the TV Title off of Jordan White on the same show. UNITE remains the tag team champions, and the next two teams in line are Future Shock and Fiery Passion, both on the KB:DI roll as well. Virgil Ambrosi holds the Hardcore Title for KB:DI. Not to mention that Vivica A. Valentine has a World Title match and just wrapped the longest US Title reign in GCW history. Shadow and Dawn McFarlane were going to wrestle at NC-17 for the World Championship. And Draven Lox could quickly establish himself somewhere beltwise. It's easy to see why Tessa went to that line of thinking, Jayce shook his head from side to side. 'It's more than just the titles,' he declared. 'It's their strength that we need to take from them. They talk about an honor code... bell to bell. Yet attack at will, like a pack of wolves.' Strength in numbers is a powerful thing, hard to overcome. And while prevailing military and combat training says that a well-trained elite unit can deal with a larger force, the fact was that the Brigade was 12 strong and cohesive. Ascension, even counting Jayce Bradley, was down with a 1:2 ratio against KB:DI. Even if they wanted to strike out, it had to be done carefully. The only reason that Tessa Windsor and her friends weren't absolutely crippled as soon as a show started is because KB:DI didn't feel like it. She agreed with the above assessment, not being stupid. 'We need to take them down one at a time. Divide and Conquer.' Bradley nodded his head as he leaned closer. 'Exactly.' 'And how are we supposed to do that?' Windsor looked to drag it out of him. Bradley smiled a crooked little grin. 'That's what WE need to talk about.' Pulling her legs out from underneath the table, she gracefully swivelled them around to the opposite side of the bench, and pulled herself back up to her feet. 'Great, but now that we’re on the same page, I think it’s time you get to know the others. They wont bite, I promise.” Grabbing Bradley by the excess fabric of his t-shirt hanging by his elbow, she yanked him along and followed the mouth watering fragrence that stemmed from Sky’s grill. That served enough as a reminder to the pint sized diva that the better it smelled, the more she’d need to push herself at Devil's Night. She couldn’t afford a pot-belly after all. Just think of the rumors that’d fly around then. “Are they almost ready?” Tessa shouted to Sky as they neared closer. All the while, Bradley seemed rather intimidated by the bond they had. After all, how could he possibly feel comfortable when he was virtually the third wheel? Green looked up, spearing a piece of chicken with the grilling fork. 'It's nearly there, even if some people might not be.' The flames licked the meat directly, the taste of the flames and the seasoning being the only thing that people would taste when they took a bite. He arrived early and cleaned off the grill before beginning the cooking. Along with the burgers and chicken, he was grilling up some vegetable kabobs (salad would not stay cold, the freezer was stocked with diet sodas and water). It was a very large grill. Not only that, but there was an especially sweet smell coming from a separate double-layer of foil on the back of the grill. From far away the aroma was overwhelmed by the smell of the meat, but up-close anyone would detect that olfactorily-pleasing scent that would be welcome and familiar... though probably not believed to be what the observant thought it was. After all, it would never be a familiar smell in the barbeque environment. “What’s cooking anyways?” Cocking her eyebrows up she inhaled once more to try and formulate a guess. But her mind was far too concerned with getting whatever it was into her mouth. So instead, she attempted to peer over her colleagues shoulders, but found that even her wedged shoes couldn’t still left her an inch or two short. Skylar looked back, and to the left, catching a glimpse of her head out of the corner of his eye. Green had excellent peripheral vision, even if he spent a couple of years of his professional career working blind (he still practices that way to keep his skills sharp). He didn't turn his head down to Windsor... didn't like looking down at Tessa. Green was a tall man, nearly a foot taller than the average height of a male in the United States (holding steady at 5'8', even if that's short for wrestling). Rather than being proud of his height, he treated it more as an obstacle to deal with. Much of society still isn't built for a tall man. He didn't sit at booths in restaurants most of the time, as he'd be bumping knees with the person on the other side of the table if he was with another diner. His long legs couldn't easily fit into certain vehicles to make him feel comfortable driving (don't even bring up the coach class on an airliner). And in wrestling, it was owed to Sky's impressive skill at mat wrestling that he could excel and more than hold his own in his preferred style. Being tall and lanky would normally work against his kind of offense... but he makes it work, taking it as a challenge. As it goes for dealing with people like Tessa Windsor, standing close to them always leaves him feeling awkward. He's forced look down... it makes him feel larger, overbearing, even if his personality doesn't carry that in the slightest. And as a personal dealing with Tessa, he didn't like looking down to her because the triangle of viewing was awkwardly positioned. For most people, being close to the same height, the triangle of viewing tracks from the eyes to either shoulder in straight lines, forming a triangle of acceptable viewing range to still be considered looking directly at the other person. With Ciel, being that much taller than Windsor, the triangle of viewing at close range would pretty much leave him staring down at her chest. It's not intentional, it's just what happens. Skylar didn't like to take advantage of any woman like that (it could be claimed that the Fab Five from Queer Eye are closer to being 'real men' than Sky), even if he was somehow a heterosexual male. Much of the time, then, he wasn't actually looking at Tessa, but rather to her side. She's come to understand as much. And as for what was on the grill that smelled so sweet... Sky informed the others that it was, 'Mixed Chocolate Chip cookies, along with some sugar cookies.' Jayce Bradley questioned, 'You can cook those on a grill?' 'Yes, sir,' Sky nodded. 'With the proper equipment, you can cook anything on a grill.' Peter Vetra, showing up late to the party, looked at was cooking on the grill. Not having heard what Jayce had just said he raised an eyebrow at Ciel's comment. 'Is that healthy?' he asked, his purple Izod shirt tucked into khaki pants. Ascension's tallest member raises the spatula over his head, long arm waving in the air. Jayce Bradley and Tessa Windsor noticed him, following suit. Jayce was a bit more subdued, gesturing little more than by nodding his head. The cookies were a pleasant scent wafting on the air, but Tess could easily leave them to come forward and meet Peter on his way over. The smooth-crowned man wore a hat that matched his shoes, a clear indication of the style that he could show off to the world when he was in casual mode. 'There you are,' she said, greeting him with a light hug. 'How have you been this week?' 'I have not been all that bad since that lost last week my mind has been down, but this seems like the perfect party to keep me up.' 'Well, you see we're for strong, now. Sky's doing the cooking for us and he says it'll be ready in a short while. And there's Jayce... he might be a little quiet at first but he's a part of the team now.' A tiny voice on the side asked, 'Who's this?' Tessa turned and croucheed on the soles of her feet, knees bending forward. The little girl to the side, teeny-tiny even compared to the leader of Ascension, is Skylar's daughter Ines. 'This is one of your dad and I's friends. His name is Mr. Vetra. Say hello.' Before the little girl could say anything, Peter crouched down to bring himself to her level. He is pretty good with children, but everybody is unless they're purposefully a jerk. 'How are you little girl? You have pretty eyes.' The mousy child gave a left-handed wave and a 'Hi' before taking her plastic red ball and going back to play. Tania kicked hard... she needed to watch and reserve her power when doing anything with the younger sister 11 years her junior. Tessa stood up again, her sundress not having grazed the ground even a little. 'She's the shyest one, but she's still really young. She likes people but doesn't talk much. 'Kids these days,' Peter shrugged his shoulders, not taking much offensive to the little kid. He starts looking around and turns back to Tessa, 'Looks like we have had a good turn out so far.' Tessa shook her head a little. 'Dorian isn't here yet, and I still haven't heard from Julio or Brack. Left a message for them but nothing yet.' Back over at the grill, Ciel flipped the foil containing the cookies. Given that it's a grill rather than an oven, the heat isn't equally distributed. But he arranged the dough so that the chips are in the center rather than sprinkled throughout. Allows the chips to hold together. And the sugar cookies would be fine anyway. As for the burgers, the meat still needed to cook. The Boca Burgers, however, were finished. Pointing to the table, Sky asked Jayce, 'Could you put a couple of paper towels into that rectangular pan and bring it here, please?' Bradley said, 'Okay,' but not more than that. Here he was, only 10 minutes ago he was all but silent and distanced from Green, now he was assisting him in food preparation. Jayce wondered if this the kind of effect that Tessa's friendship caused in people. But he didn't linger in doing as he was asked, even if he had no interest in what he considered 'fake' burgers. But what they hadn't noticed was a man that stood there, just watching them for a moment. In one hand, a couple of boxes of Thin Mints. In the other, a bag from a local deli. 'So you got started without me. At least I know that the salads that I brought probably won't go to waste.' Bradley looked up with a start. He wasn't expecting Dorian to approach from the lake side of the park. There was an entrance and parking lot over that way, but it was more for people who wanted to do the kayaking and swimming. Dorian Ryan didn't look ready to go for a nautical jaunt of any kind, the way he was dressed up. 'Hey,' was all that Jayce gave on his way to the table. He was at least participating instead of sitting to the side, but getting fully involved and conversing would take a little time. Ryan let it go and walked over to Ciel, who was still at the grill. 'So, any turkey burgers on the grill?' Dorian said with a slight grin. Sky shook his head, gesturing in the negative. There was only red meat and veggie burgers. Turkey? A joke. Peter noticed the others joining in a group conversation, Tessa saw that Peter looked over. Peter Vetra walked over to the group and surveyed everyone with a greeting of, 'Hey guys.' 'Hey, Vetra.' Dorian opened up the bag and removes 5 pound tubs of Potato Salad, Noodle Salad, and Tuna Salad. He then pulled out some serving spoons from the bag as well. 'You have any real salad to go along with the bowls of mayonnaise here?' Tessa joked. She didn't avail herself of these kinds of foods from the grocery stores. She always said that it went straight to her hips, and not in the good way. (To Be Continued...)
