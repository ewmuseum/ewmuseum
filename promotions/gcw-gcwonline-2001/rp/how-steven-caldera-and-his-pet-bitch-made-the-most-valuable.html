Date: Today “You know, this would be a heck of a lot more exciting if we were travelling at 5mph.” GCW President Steven Caldera's patience was wearing thin. He glared disparagingly at his hunchback manservant known only as Skwarek dragged the presidential rickshaw through Northern Kansas' deepest, darkest rainforest. The Jersey-born halfbreed paid no heed to Caldera's pissing and moaning, only thought that if the roles were reversed, it'd take the GCW figurehead at least six years to get the job done. The forest was thick and impenetrable. The JC Penney suit wearing president (he's a cheap bastard, really) could see no further than a few feet beyond the trees, and the thick canopy above eclipsed all but the slightest rays of sunshine. Unseen wildlife twittered and fluttered around them as they made their way through the thick bush. Skwarek was sweating like Josef Fritzl in a kindergarten class; his neon green, popped-collared polo shirt sodden with his own ghastly sweat. Which is hardly surprising, really. Would you want to haul Steven Caldera's gargantuan cranium through the jungle on a motherfucking rickshaw? “Not long now, son,” Caldera said as he leaned forward and patted his son's hunched-back. “Not long now.” Long and arduous the journey had been, but they were drawing closer and closer. For fifteen days they'd trudged the Kansas forests seeking the almighty ethereal being the knew only as “The Wolfeinator”. It had been tough, but together they'd accomplished much (don't even asked how they got the damn rickshaw up that waterfall), and soon the most lucrative signing in GCW history would be within Caldera's grasp. Steve leaned back in his extravagant perch as Skwarek dug his feet into the soggy insect-ridden soul and ploughed-on. A flying cockroach with a turban and a magic carpet flew over his head. “Outta the way, asshole!” it squeaked. “Fuck you, bitch! Don't you know I'm the most evil boss in sport's entertainment?! I have entrance music that doesn't even exist and I'm REALLY POWERFUL AND EVIL!” The GCW President rose and shook an angry fist at the mystical flying bug, but it was to no avail. The roach had long since disappeared into the brush and Caldera was left alone with the fuck-ugly beast he called a son. Soon he found himself drifting into a Radiohead-induced trance (because that's the only band he likes, and Nine Inch Nails but Trent Reznor sucks so we won't dwell on that too much), helped-on by the prospect of another boring day staring at trees he'd sooner chop down and pave over than admire the natural beauty of. It was then that something caught his ear. Soft and distant, but rhythmical and almost melodic. The same word repeating. “What's that... 'mudderfugger'? 'montyfoory'? 'mortgage famers'?” He raised an unseen hand and Skwarek stopped in his tracks (but shifted his weight from side-to-side so that his webbed feet would not sink into the mud). Leaning forward on the rickshaw's cabin, Caldera reached into his ear and pulled out a two-ton chunk of earwax so that he could hear better. Mortifera... mortifera... mortifera... “Oook ook?” quizzed Skwarek, momentarily dragging himself away from the great pleasure that scratching his scrotum with a big stick seemed to give him. Caldera's grin was a mile wide when he figured out what the unseen tribesmen were chanting. “'Mortifera'! We've made it, boy! We're here!” His excitement was almost tangible as he jumped down into the slop and kissed his balding, parasite-ridden son on his greasy forehead. So delighted was he with the prospect of finally meeting The Wolfeinator that he barely felt the poison darts that stuck in his giant arsehole and put him out like a light. * * * * * Caldera's mouth tasted like Lindsay Troy's armpit and his head felt as though someone had squeezed it into a vice. His heavy arms drooped downwards and his skull filled with warm stodge, and when he opened his eyes he realised he was hanging upside down. He panicked as beads of perspiration rushed down his features. Tried to pull himself up and free his feet from the rope they'd been bound with, but he couldn't get up because his massive fucking belly was getting in the way (you can't see this belly in his poser image because he wears a corset btw). “oookOOKoookakkkk!” Skwarek yelped from beside him, looking at Caldera with his big, hopefuly monkey eyes. It brought Caldera some degree of comfort to see that he had not been separated from his faithful companion, but he was still hung up to dry. Deeply confused and incredibly disorientated, Caldera tried to get his bearings. Both he and his Jersey chimp appeared to be tied to a couple of trees in a clearing in the forest. The foliage wasn't as dense here and they could make out the brown outlines of a few huts and yurts through the greenery. In the background, a familiar chorus of “mortifera” echoed. “You're alive, Skwarek! Praise be to Scott Stevens!” Caldera said, clapping his hands together like a sea lion. But his companion did not share his exuberance. “OOOaackackOOOOk! OOK OOK!” barked the hunchback. “What's the matter, boy?” “AHEM!” The voice that yelled at him was deeper than that of a blue whale (if a blue whale could speak). The words resonated in a dull baritone, sending shockwaves rippling across the leaf-covered ground. Caldera cast his upside-down eyes upwards (or should that be downwards?) to the source of the voice, but he could find nothing. “Daddy, is that you? My trust fund's running low...” “My child, I am not your father.” It spoke again and still Caldera couldn't find where it was coming from. Skwarek spotting it first. “OOOK! OOOOOOOK!” he said, throwing his chubby little fingers at a tiny ball of fir in the muck. The GCW President couldn't believe his eyes. There, stood before him, was all 4 inches of The Mighty Gerbil. Clad in silver chain-mail punctuated by a purple cloak and a shiny golden crown, the furry denizen eyed the suit-clad Caldera with a curious gaze. “I know who you are, yes. You are Caldera – they one they call Tea-” “DON'T SAY MY REAL LAST NAME OMG!” “How dare you raise your voice in the presence of The Mighty Gerbil?” quizzed the rodent. “Oh I'm so sorry Almighty Gerbil, sir! Please forgive me!” “State your business, my child, and I may consider it.” Caldera was panting, struggling for breath. The sight of the splendiferous Mighty Gerbil was almost too much for the GCW President to take in one go, and he had to reach for his asthma inhaler before he finally calmed down. “I seek The Wolfeinator, they one they call 'Steve', for I hear he is the mightiest of all warriors, and I really need someone to kick the shit out of Jorge Samuelsson's lurgy-infected arse...” The Mighty Gerbil folded his arms across his tiny chest and raised an eyebrow. He snapped his fingers. POOF! “How, Eddie Fowler.” Out of nowhere a massive bastard with a wolf's face appeared in front of Steven Caldera. Skwarek ook'd with excitement, before pulling a worm out of his nose and eating it. “I am not Eddie Fowler, I'm Tea-- err, Caldera.” “No, you are Axel Action.” “I'm not Axel Action.” “Ha, I have fooled you. I am really Axel Action.” “So who's Shaman?” “You.” “... what?” “It was an illusion, conjured by the mighty orbs of the Skywolf, sent down from the ethereal hunting grounds by the great master of the seven winds and the old lady of the forest herself, forged in a myth and threaded with the needle of my forefathers and bound with the wool of the secret yew of Northern Ohio.” The Mighty Gerbil interrupted. “Err, sorry about that. He gets a little carried away.” “HHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLL!” “Anyway, Mr. Teat--” “-- Caldera.” “Right, Mr. Caldera,” The Mighty Gerbil scowled at the GCW President. “What makes you think I would want to give to you my finest warrior? The pride of our tribe? The most fearsome beast that the planet has ever seen? I struggle to see how this transaction would prove beneficial to myself.” “Because I want him to join GCW, so that one day he may reach the stunning heights of DonniePalooza...” The mental image of DonniePalooza was enough to bring a tear to The Wolfeinator's eye, as images of Josh K eating a $25 sandwich bounced back and forth inside his skull. “Brb, going to ask Mom.” The Mighty Gerbil waddled into the darkness. * * * * * They waited for roughly the same length of time it takes Ben to finish writing a match these days (about eight years, by my estimation) and until eventually The Mighty Gerbil returned. “We have come to a decision, my children.” All four looked back and forth at one another. “The Wolfeinator is yours. Valuable is he to our tribe, but we cannot stand in his way of the grand opportunity to make it to DonniePalooza on this, the day of his daughter's wedding.” “Kthx!” said Caldera. “But there is one condition... “ORLY?” “Yes. You take Wolfeinator...” The Mighty Gerbil cast his gaze across to Skwarek, who'd actually gotten even uglier over the eight years that had passed. “We take the boy...” Skwarek's revolting face twisted into a look of sorrow. His lips sagged downwards and he began crying, making various monkey noises and whatever the hell else kind of noise they make in New Jersey to communicate one another. Caldera looked across at Skwarek but he couldn't let his glare last, otherwise he too would burst into tears. Choking up, Caldera nodded. “He is yours...” “OOOOOK! OOOOKSWAKWKWKAKAKAKAOOOOOK! OOOOOOOOOOKNEWJERSEYISAMAZINGEVERYWHERELSEISSHIT!!!!!!!!!” He ignored his son's distressed calls completely, and soon he found himself being cut down from his bind by The Mighty Gerbil himself, who hopped up on a space hopper and cut the rope with one of Dan Black's razor-sharp pubic hairs. “Pleasure doing business with you, Mr. Tea--” “OMGDONTSAYIT!” “Okay, Mr. Steven.” Caldera stood and dusted himself off as Skwarek died of sadness and nobody cared LOL! The Wolfeinator looked at his new master, panting. “For my father, the son, and my mother, the moon, my master, The Skywolf, my grandfather, Jeff from Northern Ohio, my dog, Chris Stuart, my sister, Big D's dradle, and my cat, Ryan Rayne, I will not let you down, Mr. Steven Caldera. But if you fail to take me to DonniePalooza, September 7th, Michigan State Fairgrounds, I will cut your tits off.” Steven Caldera gulped and then everyone fucked off. Roleplay over.
