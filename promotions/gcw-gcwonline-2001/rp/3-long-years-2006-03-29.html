3 long years... (RP for Special Challenge by Moraless)

29 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Three years of freedom. It's been three long years since the man known to the world as “The Fallen One” Nephilim has graced, or disgraced to some, a wrestling ring. The last time he openly competed, it was in a federation known as the Superior Championship Wrestling Federation after having served for months as their commissioner. He had been pulled into the competitive side over a woman. That woman was now his second wife. What happened to his first one? After finding out that she could not bear him children, ever, she removed herself from the equation on a permanent basis, and then left everything she owned to the woman who would become his new mate. That woman, his now wife and mother of his two children: Maeb Fey McClancy. You say? I know that name, but from where? It's really easy, for a bit over two years ago she wrestled for her last time. She made the final eight in the last Chris Williams Memorial Cup. And her born surname, now changed to Childress to reflect Nephilim's real name, is relatively infamous in this sport also. She's the fraternal twin sister to one Sean Micheal McClancy, former PTC Extreme Division Champion, and former Breakdown Wrestling Champion for the short time it was in PTC. Sean is now the president of Four Winds Wrestling, a company that Ian and Fey started. But now it's run by an umbrella under their main company. It's funny, you start something from scratch, build it up until it gets too big for a small group to handle it. You turn it over and then loose any touch in it. It's the way big business goes. TaleSmith Productions owns Four Winds Entertainment. But the two heads of it haven't touched anything dealing with the 4WW in over a year. Not since the test of the woman who then became Sean's wife. To top it all off, in the introduction here, they have a cousin who currently wrestles in PTC also. In Global Championship Wrestling, a man named Blaise McClancy. And American born cousin, but family all the same. To top that all off, things come full circle. For that man, Blaise, was the best student to ever study under one Geoffrey Ian Childress III... Nephilim's real name. But that's what everyone else sees in the real world. Nephilim, Ian Childress, is a former wrestler who's taken to a company that he started many years ago with his late first wife, Xian. And now with his second wife, they're continuing that work after raising a federation up from the ashes of the couple of federations that influenced it. But that does lead to one thing to the real world: Boredom And what about that which no one else sees? The fact that the underground organization is also run by Ian and Fey Childress? Known as the Keeper and Mistress of Secrets, the organization that sponsors every faction of the KnightMare Brigade out there, is under their control. But then what do you have? More boredom. Analyzing PTC's factions only. In the 4WW the faction is run by Sean's wife, Patti Anderson. And in GCW the KB:NAN is run by Blaise McClancy. Both good, solid leaders with backups in place if needed. But in the outside world, things are changing. Things are brewing that will draw the Order into a veritable war. And it's Ian's job to be prepared for that. To commit the forces into the battles that no one will see. To face up to the things that the Order tries to protect the rest of the populous from. Such as the fact that vampires and werewolves are real. The fact that there is an entire world outside of the one we live in, where all the old fantasies and fairy tales are true. This is the world that we live in, some more then others. To fight that which others can not even see, you have to be one of them, and you have to be prepared. >Dallas, Texas >Four Winds Academy Compound >The Order's home in the States >Childress Mansion “Now, Bryan, don't be mean to your sister. Share your toys.” The words trailed off from the small blond haired girl who was on duty at this time. She was in her early twenties, dressed similar to a nurse's uniform. She looked to be watching two small children, both still in diapers. One child, the boy, had a healthy head of black hair going. He looked to be in more then a healthy state. His sister, fiery red hair on her head was crying. The boy, Bryan, had a couple of toys suitable for toddlers their age in his hands and he was just looking intently at his fraternal twin. The nurse/nanny reached out and took the toy from his left hand. She then handed it back to the little girl. “There you go Breanna. Now don't let him grab it again.” She turned back to the boy and put her finger up to him, so that he could see it. “Now you be good and play nice with your sister.” The boy then reached out to take the toy back again. The red haired girl pulled it back, but not in an attempt to keep it from him, she almost looked it it was going to become a bludgeon. “Be careful what you tell her. She takes after her mother. She'll just as likely knock him over the head with it as she is to cry about it.” The nanny jumped in her skin at the sound of the voice from behind her. She knew the voice, it was the father of these two eighteen month old children, her boss. She turned and saw him walking into the room. He had long black hair, the same shade that his son had. He was just over six feet tall and cut in the body of a man who constantly works out in a very aerobic style. This man was a master of a few forms of martial arts, and the headmaster of this academy. No one could ever say that he was lazy in his time away from the ring. He'd trained more wrestlers and fighters then anyone else, successful fighters at that. In the dim light, his ice blue eyes almost glowed with their own radiance. The truth was, they did. But this girl wasn't to used to being near one of the two people who contained the genetic code from Lord Ishram himself. “Colonel Childress, I didn't expect you up this early.” He nodded, a new girl. “I've been up for about four hours already. I've got a class in an hour and a half or so. I usually stop in here after my morning warm up.” He walked by the girl, and the little redhead that was his daughter suddenly dropped the toy she had been so concerned about and stood up as best she could. She hopped up and down as she worded the one word she spoke very clearly. “Dada!” Ian reached down and scooped up the little girl. His son just looked up at them as he then reclaimed the second toy. “They're so different.” The nanny had noticed this, but it was something that Ian had known for some time. “Yes. He takes a lot after me. But don't let them fool you. They're twins and they need each other. The connections there. He's just more in a shell to others. Later on, just let them down in their play area. Go over to a chair and pick up a magazine or something and observe them. You'll see a world of difference once they both forget your here.” The nanny nodded to him in acknowledgment. She was visibly shaking though. “You're new here?” She nodded again. “Heh, don't worry about me or the Lady. We just float around here. We don't bite though.” She nodded at him again, not wanting to answer. To those who work in the Order, this man was their boss. Second only to the man they knew as Lord Ishram, who stayed at the compound in Spain for the most part. And to those who knew about that which was secret, that which was contained in the veil, they knew that Ian Childress was the same type of creature as Ishram. He was not a vampire, but very similar. His kind, of which there were only three known to be about, fed off of emotions while engaged in passion. Any normal human who tried to engage in intimate relations with them would surely be consumed. And then there was the other... Ian Childress had another voice in his head, one that scared most everyone else who knew it. Sometimes in a random mirror strewn about, when he was in it, another figure would appear to be standing behind him. A beautiful man with tanned skin and blond hair. A man? No, the disembodied spirit of an angel. The spirit of the Angel known as Suriel, “The Wrath of God”. To newcomers, this man was frightening . Then again his visage wasn't that of a man of class, or anything like that. He had more piercings in his face then most would consider normal: A bullring in his nose, multiple pieces of metal in his eyebrows, and a large spike usually from his labret. And as he stood here, in his tied sweat pants, his chest showed the few piercings it had as well as the multitude of tattoos. The new nanny watched as his daughter up in his arms traced over a tattoo of a scroll that was on his right bicep. She'd heard the stories of that one before, twelve names. Twelves names of people who's lives had been taken by this man, in their native languages. The last one, the previous Keeper of Secrets. “I'll remember that, Colonel.” “Please, if you're assigned to the house staff, you can call me Ian. I'm not one for titles when I can escape them. This is my house. I'd prefer some break from the formalities of the Academy and the Cathedral.” “Yes... I...I... Ian.” She fought to get it out, as she looked up at his ice blue eyes. A color that had been passed on to both of his children. Ian bent down and put his daughter down. She giggled a bit and then started looking for her bottle. She quickly found it and started to drink from it. Ian didn't bother trying to force his son up into his arms, instead he kneeled down and rubbed him on top of his head. Bryan quickly moved to fix his hair and voice his displeasure with having his play time interrupted. Ian just pulled him in a bit and have as much of a hug to him as his son would take right now. He then stood up and took in the full setting of the room. “Ye let me sleep in again...” The nanny jumped again. That was the voice of the Mistress. Fey Childress stepped into the room, coming directly from her bed room nearby. She was wearing a gossamer gown that was open in front. It was almost indecent in the way it barely covered what she had. This was a woman who had carried and given birth to twins, yet had already regained her washboard stomach that she had as a wrestler. Her slight Irish accent, depleted over the last three years from hanging out with Ian, still rolled off her tongue. “Ye need t' stop doin' that.” Ian just smiled at her. The new girl tried to cover her face as Fey greeted her children. She was a European bred and raised woman, so the thing material of her gown, and what almost showed was of no concern to her. “You were asleep and you apparently need your rest. It's your week off from teaching, get some rest.” Fey stood and looked at the new nanny they had. “Aye, another one. Ye keep scaring them off, love?” Ian chuckled at that. “No, as I understand it, it's a thing with Annie. She says they get to rotate out. Keeps the youngin's from being too attached to one person other then us. And it makes all the nurses down there look at it as their turn at cake duty.” “Just wait until these two awaken on them. Lord knows wha' they'll end up being.” The nanny spoke up. “You don't know what they are yet?” “They're too young t' be testin' them yet. Although we 'ave our guesses. Bryan has too many traits of his father. We may have our fourth Vampyle here. Breanna... may be taking up the mantle of another of my kind. It's a first, tha's fer sure.” “But they're twins.” Ian interrupted her. “Fraternal twins. They share a birth date, birthplace and a few physical similarities. But other then that, they're as different as night and day.” As the nanny girl took Breanna up into her arms to check her, Fey turned to Ian. “Now that I'm awake though, I can be askin' ye about something I got yesterday.” Ian looked a bit puzzled at her comment. “Okay? What's up?” “I got a request from Blaise's current federation, Global Championship Wrestling. It was addressed to the CEO of TaleSmith Productions, which is me. But it should 'ave gone t' Sean.” “Well send it to him, if it's for the 4WW. He's the president, not me or you.” “It's na' about the 4WW though.” “Okay?” “They wanted Sean for a match. Apparently it's someone's last match, he's retiring. And he wanted to face Sean... again.” Ian's face contorted into a physical question of “Who?” or “Whom?”. Fey answered it. “Moraless.” Ian perked up a bit. Moraless... the man who injured Sean's shoulder and led to him retiring a few months later when it wasn't healing right. The man who'd taken Sean McClancy's PTC Extreme Title from him. A man who has faced the challenge of “The Irish Bastard” and took him to his limit. That was a match that Sean should have won, in a construction zone for a building the two had fought for the Extreme Title.... but in the end, Moraless left the champion and had sent in as the catalyst for Sean's ultimate retirement. “Well, that's all nice and fine, but Sean's retired. And he's the president of another federation. A rival fed as it is. So what do you want to look into?” “Well I'm thinking that since the GCW wants to have it, why not get Blaise to do it? 'E is Sean's cousin. But I don'na want to sound like we're forcing him to take up Sean's old fights. You know how those two get along. I don't even know why they tried.” “Eh, they're just trying to give a washed up man one final chance at some sort of glory. There are many who claim that had it not been for Sean's shoulder, he would have won. Just too bad he'll get to go out as a wet noodle instead of a blaze of glory.” Ian put his arm around Fey, his hand dropping down to her hip. They then started walking back towards their private quarters. They entered and headed over towards the the still unmade bed. Ian stepped behind Fey and wrapped both arms around her. He then nuzzled up against her neck, raking his teeth against her skin. She inhaled sharply at the sensatation as her hand went up to find his head. “Now, don'cha be starting anything. You've not taken a shower after your workout. Go get washed up first.” “Well, if we continue, we can both save some of this fine Texas water. We have that large shower for a reason, for sharing.” Fey giggled, followed by a slight moan as he kissed her neck again. She turned around, emerald green eyes looking into his blues. He still went for the neck, her weak spot if you would. He kissed right on top of the choker necklace she wore at all times that had his family crest upon it. Some would call it almost a collar... But, before things went any further they were interrupted as the equivalent of a doorbell went off. “CHRIST!” Fey almost screamed that out with frustration. But after she said it, the door to their quarters opened up. In walked a woman with long, wavy, black hair. A woman who's usually seen speaking with Blaise McClancy or some of the others in GCW's KnightMare Brigade Faction. Her name is Annie Leanan. Those that followed the career of Sean McClancy's time in PTC would know that she also served as his valet for his time in the UWF and BDW. An Irishman like Fey, but from the North-Eastern coast. At one time she would have been presented to be Fey's sister-in-law, but that had changed. Now she served the Order of the Hammer, reporting directly to Fey Childress as the head of their research department. “Am I interrupting anythin'?” She smiled wide with a perfect white smile. Fey was busy pulling her gown around her, tying it in front to keep it closed. She turned and glared at Ian though when he answered. “Just an attempt to make another set of twins...” If she wasn't busy making herself presentable, not knowing who else might be with Annie, she would have punched her husband in the arm for that. But she really didn't expect anything less of him. She knew he dealt only in facts and truths. He had been that way for years. To him, it would be idiocy to claim they didn't do those things, since they had two children already. “I can tell. The pheromones in this room are pretty thick. Maybe I should 'ave waited another few minutes before I came in. Maybe I could 'ave 'andled a bit o' a show.” Annie, a member of the Unseelie courts of Ireland, was almost the same creature as Fey. So she knew how their kind worked it's attraction magicks, or glamories. “What do ye be needin' Annie? Ian 'as a class t' get t' in abou' an hour, an' 'e needs to be physically presentable t' the new recruits. An' seein' as 'ow ye did'na knock, it 'ad better be important.” Annie sat down at a small, round table. She set her attaché case down and opened it. She then proceeded to pull a few small items out. One of them a small digital device like an MP3 player with built in speakers. “Oh, it is me Lady, it is. Very important. I'd na' of done this if I did'na 'ave t'. I know Ian's schedule, tha's why I'm up 'ere now. Fer if I don'na get this now it could be days befer I do.” Ian stepped up and started looking at what she was pulling out of the brief case. “What's going on?” Annie continued, but motioned to a manila folder she had already pulled out. “This is a new issue from me case in Vegas. Th' one Blaise is on.” “The Amerindian Girl.” Annie looked up at Ian as he opened the folder and started flipping through things. It was a thick one, full of paperwork and pictures. Ian was a bit familiar with this. “Yeah, Dani.” Dani... Danielle Northsky, one of GCW's wrestlers and the one that the KB:NAN had formed around to protect. She was special in every way. A beautiful woman, but also more then that. She was an Angel. Not just the fact that she was pretty or she was generous and a good person... She was an Angel in the sense that her family had been descended from a real Angel of Heaven and seven generations later Dani had shown signs of being a full throwback. “So, what's going on?” Annie seemed to finish up what she was getting ready for. “Okay, they're on their way 'ome riogh' now. But a few weeks ago, they ran int' a small situation. Someone hired a group o' Japanese thugs with connections t' a witch and some Oni t' take them on. It ended up being tha' someone was testing Blaise and Ukyo t' be seein' 'ow they were in protecting Dani.” That triggered something in Ian. His eyes took on a sheen of their own, and his voice almost sounded like two people were speaking at the same time. “A grandfather's love, ensuring she was protected, I would presume.” Annie shivered at the spectacle. She knew of the being inside Ian's head. He had been paramount in proving to the others what Dani was. At one time the being known as Suriel had been the one to rain down hellfire and brimstone on the cities of sin. He'd been the one to bind Azazel in the deserts for his crimes that led to the floods of Noah's time. Now he shared a body and was able to continue some semblance of existence. In return he acted as a source of information to the Order. “Yes. After th' fight, Kokabel presented 'imself to them. 'E confirmed it, an' then 'ad a talk with Dani in the presence o' th' others.” Ian nodded. “It wasn't in any language you can decipher, was it?” Annie shook her head to the negative. “I think it's th' language o' th' Angels, an' since they can talk t' anyone. The only thing I ken think o' calling it is: Babel.” Ian's head nodded and he gave a slight grin. But the voice was still that eerie echo. “That is as good a name as any. Some would call it 'tongues' though, since even our fallen brothers use it still.” “They would both mean th' same I would guess. Bu' we need yer services. I can'na decipher this. It's 'opless since their kind 'as never 'ad their words written down. Blaise recorded it an' sent it to me via the secure terminals a' the South Wind Academy.” Fey made the next snide comment. “Boy, 'e's learnin' finally. I'd na' normally put that boy t' remember such stuff.” Annie wanted to laugh but didn't. She had picked Blaise for his abilities to do this. Although it's known that his temper rivals his cousin, Sean's. But he's not as apt to fall into that line. Ian continued as Annie was finishing the setup. “So what's this about? Do you have any clue what's mentioned at all?” Annie finished and then looked up from the chair to look Ian in the eyes. “All we know is that Dani has figured out Sergent Brand's password and has been doing searches through the research library. She's found some things and we keep collecting that data too. But it would be nice to know the same clues she's been finding. I brought some of the books that her searches have come up with. But I've got nothing else.” “Give me one clue before you play the recording.” “Well the name: Evening Star. That's the main one she does.” Ian nodded. “As her grandfather, Kokabel was called the Morning Star. I'm familiar with the Zuñi myths of boat. Mortal enemies they were.” Annie nodded and then hit the play button on the device. What could then be heard to Fey was nothing but a series of squeals, clicks and other strange noises. It sounded almost like someone had distorted a taping of a whale's song in the ocean. There were two distinct voices though, both speaking in the same melodic way. “Ah, yes...” Ian grabbed one of the books laying on the desk after looking at a couple of titles. “Well, let me fill you in here. First, apparently this mortal enemy has been released. He's been imprisoned by Kokabel for some time. But he's out now. He's connected to your gibbering idiot down in that padded cell.” “Jaymes? In what way?” Ian's face was stone like as he listened. “The Evening Star is his father. He mentions that he can't tell her his name, because of the fact that his name would attract his attention.” “Is that true?” “Only in the case of some types of Angels. Upper Tier: Seraphs, Cherubim, Thrones. Not the lesser kind.” “So he doesn't give a name?” “No, but he gives a clue...” Ian starts flipping through the book, marked as the Ars Groetia on it's cover as he continues to listen. Suddenly he breaks out into a curse that would only usually be used by his wife. “Bloody Hell!” The two Irish women in the room ring out in unison. “What?” “He knows about me. He knows about 'The Wrath'. In fact, he specifically told Dani that she'd need to look for me, find me. Marked me as the man with two spirits. One born of the earth, the other born of heaven. And he used the 'call sign' for me.” Annie was writing away in her notebook as he spoke. Her face down towards the paper, she asked him some more questions. “Who do you think this new enemy is?” “One of the fallen. But not from the second batch. It's one of the first ones.” She looked up. “One of them tha' fell with Luci... Sammael?” She corrected herself from her training. For many years she was told the name of the devil was one thing, but since coming to the Order, she's learned his true angelic name. And she's learned that at one time, he approached Dani in Global Wrestling. “Yes, and this one is special. He mentions this beings abilities in sorcery. That can lead me to only one conclusion.... the one that got away.” “Ga' away?” Ian looked in her direction, but the look on his face shows who is in control right now. It's Suriel, Ian's a backseat passenger in his own body at this moment. “Yes, one that has always caused trouble. I chased him over a few continents in my days when I had my wings. Before his siding with the Poisoned one, he was the chief sorcerer of Heaven. But when his brother was cast out, he joined him.” “'Is brother?” Ian's face smiled wide, but in a way that Ian himself would never do so. “He was one of the first created. Right behind the other archangels. We knew he got away, and rumors had it he had headed to the part of the planet that were still full of what the Europeans and Arabs called 'savages'. But we could never find him. The key thing here is, that Kokabel confirmed that his imprisoning him was what allowed him to get his repreave and re-enter the abode of the Angels. That can lead to only one guess.” Ian put the book down, opened to a page. On that page was a drawing of a lanky man with wings, six wings to be exact. In one hand as a sword, on the other a serpent was held aloft. He was riding on the back of a great beast that could only be called a dragon. In the upper left corner of this page was a circle with some of the script of the magi's who had written the book included in it, it was a representation of this former Angel's symbol. A name was at the top of the page, written in a very strong font of script. That name: Astaroth. “Ast...” “No, don't say it. He's right. If he has any hints as to the Order being involved, then we need all the secrecy we can cover. You have your clues, and your confirmations. Now take it back and get the stuff ready to deliver to Blaise. He'll need it.” “He's fought an Angel befer.” “He fought a Throne who was in charge of a abstract. Not a seraph that invented half of what we call sorcery himself.” Annie started to pack up the stuff she had brought. She knew Suriel could get testy. Then again you would to if you were forced to co-habitate a body with a man like Ian. As she did, Ian's voice started to return to normal. “So, what would it take to arrange a meeting with this young lady you call Dani.” Annie stopped in her tracks, and looked up at him. “Her grandfather had pointed her in my direction.” “Well I was thinking of lettin' 'er try t' do th' research, 'erself. She's 'it some things that we didn'na know about. I don'na know if I'm ready t' undermine the trust she's still building with Blaise, though.” Ian's hand went to his chin to stroke the slight bit of hair from his goatee there. “Understood, and commendable. She needs the confidence to know that she can do such things. Make sure Blaise knows though. He's good at keeping secrets.” “Yes, as for you meeting her. I don't know.” As Annie was still packing things up, Fey interjected with a thought. “Actually, Ian, there is a way for you to meet her this coming week without raising any suspicions.” “There is?” Fey grinned as she reached over for a folded piece of paper on her nightstand. “Yes, you said that you don't think Blaise should fight Sean's battles. What about someone else?” “Who?” “... You.” Annie spoke up. “Wha's this?” Fey continued. “A man had issued a challenge to Sean for his retirement match. But Sean's not available. You might remember 'im, Moraless.” Annie had been Sean's valet when he'd fought Moraless. “I remember 'im well.” “Well, GCW is givin' 'im a retirement match. 'E requested Sean, bu' as ye said, we can'na 'ave our president goin' on another fed's show. So, 'ow abou' Sean's Brother-In-Law.” Ian grinned even wider then he had before. “Well it may not be official business. But maybe this is the right thing to get this going. I can also use it as a chance to scare the shit out of Blaise. Tell him the Keeper is coming to do an inspection of his troops there.” Fey shook her head at that. But before she could shoot back at him, Annie spoke up. “I'll make the arrangements. If ye give me that letter I'll act as yer agent with GCW. I already dealt with them t' get the Order's insurance setup when our boys and girls signed there. I know who t' be talkin' t'.” Fey just held out the letter to Annie. She could tell by the look on Ian's face that he was going to go through with it. “Yes, Annie. Tell them that Moraless' challenge is answered. Due to contractual agreements he can't have Sean McClancy. But Sean's sister will be there in the corner of his brother-in-law.” “As you wish, Me Lord.” Annie finished, and sealed up her case. She then quickly scurried off. As the door shut, Ian walked back over to his wife. “So, where were we before we were rudely interrupted?” To be continued...
