Interview With A Phenom

4 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 4, 2007 ---- Prelude: Getting To Know The Black Mariah ---- Walking into the room was a lot like walking onto stage for the first time. Throughout her entire wrestling career, The Black Mariah had managed to never be interviewed (except by herself of course). It was a new and exciting experience. While most girls would be shaking, Vivica greeted the experience with a smile. She was an addict after all. She was addicted to adrenaline, just ask her for yourself. Wearing a black leather bomber jacket with a black My Chemical Romance tank top on along with a pair of form fitting blue jeans and a belt made out of a chain and padlock from Home Depot, The Black Mariah amazed even the most experienced Maxim editors. No matter how many times they'd seen it, they never ceased to be amazed by the beautiful girls that come out of professional wrestling... of all places. Her heartbeat as she greeted everyone with a smile before making her way towards Office 220. That's what the business card said anyway. She still couldn't get over the name on the card. Jessica Hundley, who would've thought it? A girl interviewing her for Maxim Magazine, the number one magazine for men. Instantly The Black Mariah felt sort of a sistership with the woman after phone conversations. They had a lot in common. They were both strong females in a male dominated enviornment. For someone who usually generally hated everyone in the past, The Black Mariah was slowly opening up to people. Perhaps it was due to the fans slowly opening up to her. It was a little bit of give and take, but in the end it was hard to say she wasn't becoming a better person because of it. 'Come on in!' Vivica heard a voice from inside the room before she even knocked. A bit surprised Vivica waited a minute to make sure Jessica was really talking to her. Then eventually she opened the door and walked inside. 'Vivica!' The woman stood up from her seat and extended her hand to The Black Mariah, who looked down at it reluctantly. In her line of work, these usually ended with a club to the back. It had been almost two years since Miss Valentine had done purely business work on her own. The life of an independant woman had it's up's and down's. 'Take a seat there, and we'll get started.' 'Alright, no problem.' Vivica said as she took off her jacket and hung it on the coat rack behind her. She sat down slowly as Jessica grabbed a tape recorder and a pad and paper. Hitting the record button she looked across at the former GCW United States Champion. ---- Chapter One: The MAXIM Interview ---- So the first question that comes to my mind is how does a girl like you, who looks like she should be in a photo shoot instead of a wrestling ring become the longest reigning United States Champion in GCW History? Well that's actually a weird story. I wasn't really ever supposed to be a wrestler. My former fiance Christian Zenith joined an organization called Global Wrestling a couple years ago and I just sort of hung around. Eventually I really wanted to get involved, and they gave me a chance. A couple of helpful friends along the way showed me the ropes. Look where I am now! Definitely, so was wrestling something that you ever thought you'd be doing in a million years? It doesn't sound like something most girls plan to do when they're in high school. I wasn't exactly a normal girl in high school either. Because I was so strong I tended to have a lot of girls cling onto me for attention, and the fact that my parents [lawyer Jack Valentine and socialite Marie Cartwright-Valentine] were wealthy drew people to me. It so fake, it was kind of depressing. Well speaking on fake, a lot of people would say that about your business. Well a lot of people are idiots then. Step into the ring with me sometime, I'll show them just how fake the business really it. I think I'll pass. So do you have any role models in the industry? There are always girls who blazed the path for the type of wrestler I am. I'd have to say I'm definitely a fan of Karina Wolfenden [currently of PRIME] and well the mexican luchadore heritage has always sort of interested me. I'll be honest and say I really don't know much about lucha libre, but it's fascinating. To me, anyway. So does a busy girl like you ever get time to go out to a club? Have some fun and let loose? Ha, right. Little known fact boys and girls. I don't drink or smoke. I guess you can say I just play a bad girl on television. So for that I guess, the club scene never really interested me. There is limited time to go out, but I usually spend most of my time relaxing at home. It's a hard life you know? Yeah, I can relate. Not half as bad as your road schedule I'm sure. So how do you think you'll be spending Valentine's Day? Probably wrestling in a ring in some far corner of the globe, or I might just be at home listening to music. I don't have anything planned yet? I really don't know. Hopefully I won't be sobbing in a corner somewhere like a little baby. That's not likely though. You mentioned music. You are kind of the hard rock and roll girl in GCW. Does that reflect your real listening habits? Definitely! I love music and rock and roll is amazing. Atreyu, Underoath, Lullacry, My Chemical Romance, Killswitch Engage, I love it all. Love it, love it, love it! Can't get enough. I swear if they'd let me, I'd probably try to change my entrance song every night. Do you ever think about being a role model to other little girls who hope to grow up and get into such a male dominated business? Are you a good role model? ... ---- Chapter Two: To Be Inspired ---- Many years earlier... Like a little kid in a candy store, young Vivica J. Valentine raced across the house after coming in from the school bus. Like usual, her father was at work and her mother was out being a terrible mother... the usual. Busting through her bedroom door with the enthusiasm that only a seven year old child could have, Vivica dove under her matress to pull out a VHS tape. One she'd been stashing for just such an occassion, when her mother wasn't around to lecture her. Fumbling the tape in her hands she shoved it into the VCR on top of her television and waited for the tracking to stablize the picture. Suddenly the picture came in and she recognized everything immediately. The bright blue steel cage surrounded the ring and she watched as her favorite, Jimmy 'Superfly' Snuka climb to the top. Watching with her mouth opened, she watched as he looked down from the top down at 'Magnificent' Don Moraco. The next moment in time seemed to go by in slow-motion as Jimmy Snuka leaped from the top of the steel cage. Flying through the air like a super hero, Vivica watched him with a smile as he nailed the superfly splash. 'Wow...' she said out loud, 'Kenny was right... that was rad...' 'VIVICA!' Suddenly she shot up to her feet as the screech of her mother caught her by surprise. The face of Marie Cartwright-Valentine told the whole story. Instantly Vivica knew what she had done wrong. 'What did I tell you about watching that... BLOOD SPORT?!' Vivica looked down at the ground suddenly. 'I'm sorry mother...' she muttered under her breath, only to have her mother walk across the room by her, hit the eject button and pull the tape out of the VCR. Watching in disappointment as her mother threw the VCR tape into the trash, Vivica had seen the impossible. Whether it be in the front of her mind or in the back, it was embedded into her mind for the rest of her life. She wanted to fly, she wanted to be a super hero, she wanted to be famous. It was her destiny. ---- Epilogue: The Final Answer ---- Do you ever think about being a role model to other little girls who hope to grow up and get into such a male dominated business? Are you a good role model? ...Yeah, I think I could. I havn't done the best job of it in the past, but things are changing in my life. Like things tend to do. I'm trying my best to change though. That's why life is I guess. You live, you learn, and you overcome your weaknesses. Without that, we'd all be the same. It's all steps on the way to destiny. Sometimes you go the wrong way, but at the end... you get back on track. - MAXIM MAGAZINE (Coming to shelves February 2007)
