Fork this, road!

undated

Date: with the power of restrospect Sit on the balcony, overlooking the Gold Coast, contemplating the path in which my on life has forged. There is emptiness inside of me. A hole. This desperate void that I cannot quite put my finger on but my subconscious speaks to me and tells me I know the reason for this hole and the remedy all at once. At stages in our life, we make decisions that out-weigh other decisions we have previously made. Someone metaphorically might explain these as forks in our road. Do we go left? Or do we go right? Interestingly enough, there are always forces that help guide you down certain paths. You have forces and I have forces. I understand those that have shaped me and ushered me down paths. Morag Jane happens to be that force for me. My wife. The mother of my children. Alex and Leslie. She is the force that has pushed me particular ways down the split ends of the forks in my road. And I use the term “pushed” loosely, as I struggle to recall a time in my life where I chose which path of the said fork by myself. Here I sit, on the balcony, on an all expenses paid trip from New York to Melbourne, then Melbourne to the Gold Coast, on a business venture. A particular business in which I have not invested in with my heart, but my mind. I do the work, without fail, without effort. I just do it. So, I sit on the balcony, overlooking the waves crashing upon the sandy beaches of the Gold Coast, drinking a scotch and coke, having a shoulda-woulda-coulda-been moment with my own existence. What would have happened if I had said “no?” What would have happened if I stood my ground and took my own turn, instead of being pushed down one here and there. If I’d have made my own choices. Path one: girlfriend falls pregnant. Do I just support the kid and extinguish an already failing relationship? Do I make an honest woman out of her and support them as a “family?” Path chosen: Path B. Path two: job stability. Do I stop my constant travel on the road as a professional wrestler? Do I take the father-in-law’s offer of a job with his IT company solutioning for major corporations with their IT needs? Path chosen: Path B. Path three: relationship stability. Do we conclude that we married for the sake of our child to give them some sort of “family” rock to cling to and part our ways as friends before we turn completely into foes? On the other hand, do we have another child and see if that helps the situation? Path chosen: Path B. And here I sit, an a smorgasbord of path B’s in my life, never once doing the right thing by me over the ideals it wasn’t the right thing by everyone else. However, with the power of retrospect I can see now that what I thought was the right thing by everyone else is definitely not the right thing by anyone. The scotch in my system and the bravado of free time away from my wife puts me at this standstill in my life and it clear to me now what I should have done five years ago. Get back on path A. And path A meant getting back inside the ring.
