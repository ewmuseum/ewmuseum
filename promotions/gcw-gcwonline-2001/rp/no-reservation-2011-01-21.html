Date: 01/21/11 Las Vegas , Nevada Friday 6 pm January 21st :: Scene opens up at a gym with very few people inside but in the far left of the gym is Playboy johnny Davis and he's unloading on a speed bag, after about 100 straight short jabs the bag unloads and Johnny wipes off his forehead and cracks his neck , Johnny then walks to the bench and grabs a towel and dries himself off the rest of the way, as he glances up to a small 13'' television he see's there replaying his lost during last week's worldwide from Colombus, As the film shows, Playboy was never pinned. Door slams in the gym and in come's Damian Dawkins, Damian then greet's Johnny with a hug :: Damian : A FUCKING PINK TUTU ?!?!?!?! Johnny Davis : Don't Ask Damian : Let's Ease both our minds man, A few drinks and then we will go and head Hunt Shaman ! Johnny Davis : That sounds like it could be a great time.... Damian : Does'nt it though ? Johnny Davis : Where are we gonna look for this guy at ? Damian : Indian reservation's DUHHH??? Johnny Davis : Wait !! I thinkl i know where he works...... Damian : He works for the GCW... Johnny Davis : No, I think he works for the casino, they even has a statue of him in front smoking a pipe . Damian : Word. Let's ride :: They waste no time as they hop into there rentall car and head down the street toward the indian reservation, As they arrive they pull up to a huge Tee pee , they are approached by the reservation ranger's. :: Ranger : You can't park here this is a private establishment, of the harrah's casino, Chupeepee tribe,... Johnny Davis : Hola Como estas' Damian : Nah Johnny, he won't understand that...... HOWWWWWW!!!!! ranger : sir i am 70 % indian and i am 0 % spanish and im goin to ask you to leave.. Johnny Davis : Im gonna ask you, Have you seen this man.... :: Johnny then pulls out a picture of Shaman from a GCW magazine :: ranger : No...wait....maybe.................is that John redcorn? Johnny Davis : No that's Shaman GCW hardcore champion until wednesday. Where he will walk with the after life, and end up crippled selling tobacco or dancing for the village people. Oh well , We will go inside the casino now. Ranger : Have a good day fella's. Johnny Davis : You too.... :: Johnny and Damian then march up a trail towards the casino, after stopping by a atm they sit down at the bar called. No Reservation. After a few to many shot's Johnny Davis begins to speak out loudly and the whole bar is now listening :: Johnny Davis : SHAMAN !!! where ever you are I want you to know one thing, everyone said be careful what you wish for, I live life on the edge, im a lear jet ridin, Moet sippin, chair shot chippin' kinda guy, Ive bleed, Ive left on a stretcher, Iv'e fought in the King of Fucking Extreme Tournament where I lost to FWO'S Boston Strangler !!! When you put on your raw hide bear skin underwear i want you to remember, you might be wrestling a rookie to GCW But im a Wrestling Legend especially in the Hardcore games, Most wrestlers say there R rated, remember im the only one that can say im XXX Rated. :: Damian grabs Johnny and leads him out since his rage is causing a disturbance as they arrive to there vehicle they realize they shouldn't be driving so they flag down which appears to be a Indian local. He also appears intoxicated :: Johnny Davis : Ask him.... Damian : Most are....there indians.....better him a DWI than us, Hey migo 50 dollar you drive us please ... Indian : Yes..... Damian : What's your name, Indian : Dave....Dave redballs Damian : How and the Hell do you all get your name's... Johnny Davis : REDBALLS!!!! Whitecloud? :: Dave redballs begins to drive them to a local landscaping company :: Johnny Davis : Ya'll seen a man named.... Andrew Whitecloud ? Owner : No sir...Try the Firework display on the way outta town.... Johnny Davis : Alright of coarse. :: They hop back in the car and drive towards the firework display apparenatly they had told dave something that made him upset so they asked him to pull overe where they ditched Dave redballs at a 7 /11 gas station, Johnny and Damian proceed toward the firework display :: Johnny Davis : Howwwwww!!! Im looking for Andrew Whitecloud, Does he work here? owner : No , sorry he sure doesn't you should ask the old indian path tracker he lives in the river bed...
