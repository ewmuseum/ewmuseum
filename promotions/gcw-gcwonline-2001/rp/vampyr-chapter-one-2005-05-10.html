Vampyr: Chapter One

10 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10 May 05 May 5th 2005 Mexico City, Mexico The sun was beating down hard, bringing on a heat wave that no one saw coming. The temperatures were ranging anywhere from 95 degrees to 105, give or take a few degrees if you were in the shade. The heat was only matched by the humidity, which made things about 100 times more uncomfortable. There hadn’t been a drop of rain in weeks which was making everything dry up quickly, destroying crops and creating desert where there wasn’t any before. Of course this is Mexico City though and on this day, Cinco de Mayo, the people of this city weren’t going to let anything stop their fun. On the day where Mexicans celebrated their independence, Tequila flowed where water did not and men’s hunger for food was satisfied by flesh, the flesh of the whores who roamed the streets looking for some cash. Decorations flooded the streets, along with children in very festive costumes and peddlers trying to sell every and anything they can. A parade began coming down the main street, which was more like an oversized dirt road that you’d find in the back ways of Tennessee. You could find all sorts of creatures and religious symbols lurking in this parade, although there were simply masked men and women. Inside of a small bar, a bar very dissimilar to any other you’d find you shall find some interesting happenings. Inside of this bar there is a man, a man whose destiny is about to unfold right in front of his very eyes. He sits at the bar alone, just as he does most every day, sipping slowly on a glass of Tequila. His long dark hair is tied back, allowing only a few strands to move about freely. His Icy blue eyes shine even brighter when put up against his dark skin and stone cold face. He is cloaked in black, black A-shirt, black trench coat, even in this blazing heat, black slacks and black boots. He kills the last bit of his tequila and slams the glass on the counter, the universal sign of needing another drink. This man has seen a lot of things in his lifetime, some good but many bad. Once upon a time he was happy, although it seems like ages ago now. His life was shattered one night by something that he could not explain at that time. Since that night seven years ago, he has vowed to never rest until he has rid the world of this plague. Tequila is his sanctuary, sort of like his calm before the storm. He understands that soon, as the sun drops below the horizon and the moon comes out, that the world changes, it changes into something much more sadistic. That sugar coated world that humanity lives in is nothing more than a fairy tale, a fairy tale that will never come true. “Tequila amigo?” He didn’t speak as the bartender approached him with this question, he merely nodded. The bartender filled the glass to the brim and leaned over to face this man who had been coming into his bar for weeks, but never said a word. He wiped the spot in front of the stranger, and leaned over the counter, placing the bottle of tequila to their side. The stranger raised his glare to look at the bartender eye to eye. “Que es tu nombre amigo?” “Roberto, Roberto Abitua.” The bartender extended his hand, and after a few moments of thinking about it, Roberto took the hand and shook it firmly. The bartender then left the bottle with Roberto and went on to take care of some other customers. Roberto grinned behind his glass, what the grin really meant no one may ever know. A few hours passed as Roberto sat at that bar, slowly and methodically drinking his tequila. A new wave of party goers flooded the bar now as the night was about ready to fall on the city. Roberto put down his final empty glass of the evening and turned his gaze to the window, and saw that the sun was creeping below the horizon. He was almost in a trance as he witnessed the sun fall completely behind the horizon, allowing the darkness of the night to engulf the sky. He took in a deep breath as he stood from his stool. He laid some money down on the counter and then began to slowly move through the crowd of people to go to the outside, to walk in the world beneath the world. He pushes the door open and steps out into the warm night air. The wind carries a scent of tequila, tortillas, and ? something that only a select few would ever catch. Although the parties spilled out into the streets, to Roberto it was deathly quiet. The only sound that could be heard in his mind is the thudding of his boots crashing down on the dirt beneath. He doesn’t search with his eyes, he lets his ears do that for him. He takes in everything letting it go into his mind and wander around until he gets the results he is looking for. He finds what he is looking for in a building down the street ? where there is almost no light what so ever. The low rumble of music flows from the building, and Roberto slowly walks towards it, almost as if it were calling to him. As he approached it, he cracked his neck and took in some deep breaths, clearing his mind of all the clutter. He walked into the old saloon style bar with the swinging doors and looked around. The place was very dimly lit, and had soft music playing in the background. There were men all over trying their hand at finding a woman to bed with that night. It was a whore house, or at least at the surface. There was a guard at the door, a fairly large man wearing blue jeans and a black leather vest and even though it was dark he had on sunglasses. He placed a hand on Roberto’s chest as he walked through the doors. “Que Paso amigo?” That smell, this guard reeked of it. The smell was so disgusting that as it filled Roberto’s nostrils it almost forced him to gag, almost. Roberto first looked down at the hand that now rested on his chest, then slowly trailed up the arm to look the man in his eyes. His face remained stoic, emotionless for only a few moments before a smile creased his lips. Roberto sighed and shook his head before grabbing the guard by the wrist and turning his arm almost to the point of breaking it, which brings the guard to his knees howling in pain. Roberto swiftly pulled out a silver spike from a holster at his hip and drove it into the back of the guard’s head, and immediately the guard bursted into flames. The entire room then leaped to their feet, some screaming with fear and others angry and seeking revenge for the actions that just took place. Roberto pushed his trench coat back a bit to reveal his arsenal of weapons, and he pulls out two black pistols from his side. He quickly fires off two rounds which connect with two incoming attackers, and just like their counter part, upon contact with the bullets they burst into flames. He rushes forward and leaps into the air, leap frogging one more. He spins mid air, landing on one knee and fires three shots, each connecting and sending another into flames. Leaping from behind the bar another attacks, Roberto jumps into the air with a front kick, catching his attacker with a front kick which send the attacker falling hard on his back. Roberto stands over him now with a boot on his chest, and then fires a round from each of his pistols, destroying the beast in the same way as the others. A fist comes from the corner of Roberto’s eye, connecting with his cheek and sending a jolt of pain through his face. Swiftly he lifts his leg and sends a kick to the chest of the next attacker, sending him a few paces backwards. Immediately after, another wraps up Roberto from behind. After a few moments of brief struggle Roberto brings his head back slamming the back of his head into the nose and mouth of the one holding him captive, which automatically loosens it’s grip. Without turning to look he spins one pistol around and fires three rounds into the chest of the victim behind him, but wastes no time in turning both pistols on the one he kicked away just moments earlier and sending four bullets flying. The room has been completely cleared out now, Roberto stands alone until a door at the far corner opens and in comes five very large, “men”. Their eyes were glowing red with hatred, and their fangs slithered down their mouths. The one in the middle let out a low roar, as if he were some kind of animal, and in many ways these creatures were just that ? animals. Roberto just stood motionless as he watched these creatures surround him. They all seemed to be measuring up the others, waiting for the first to attack, but all being nervous to do so. Then the first one did, swinging wildly at Roberto’s head, he ducks underneath the blow and raises up, placing both pistols into the chin of the first and blasting away, sending him to ash. A front kick crashed into the chest of Roberto, sending him flying into the wall behind him, cracking it a bit. He quickly raised his weapons and pulled the triggers. “FUCK!” Roberto bellowed as all he got was a metallic click, signaling that his weapons were out of ammo. With some force he slams the pistols together which forced two blades to slide out from below the handles. H spun the weapons on his fingers a bit before spinning and dropping to one knee and tossing the weapons, having the blades sink into the head and chest of one of the other beasts, ending it’s life span. A fist came flying but Roberto avoids it, knocking it away with his forearm, then coming back with a reverse elbow that connects with the side of one’s head, causing it to howl in pain. Another comes flying in with a kick, but Roberto dives out of the way causing the kick to connect with one of the monster’s comrades, sending it off it’s feet. Roberto stands up to face the three adversaries that are left, and he puts his hands into his trench coat and comes out with a pair of silver and black gloves, he extends his arms out and two 6 inch claws extend out from each glove. He goes in for the kill swiping at the first, which ducks the first blow but cannot move fast enough to dodge the second blow which connects with its forehead, sending it in flames. Roberto quickly sends a kick backwards hitting one of the beasts square in the chest, without hesitation he now spins and leaps into the air with a flying punch, driving the blades deep into the chest of this most recent victim taking it’s life. It’s now only one on one, and from the looks of things this creature of the night doesn’t stand much of a chance, and it knows that. The two stand there watching each other’s every move, from the heaving of their chest to the twitch of their eye. They slowly begin to circle one another, preparing themselves for the battle that was sure to be coming next. Roberto’s blue eyes seemed focused and determined, while the beast’s red eyes that were once filled with rage seem uncertain and nervous. “Who are you Human !”” The beast’s voice trembled, but still had it’s eerie tone. It seemed to come from every direction in the room as if it came directly from a ghost. Roberto cocked his head to one side and smiled at the creature. The smile came as a shock to the beast, and it caused his anger to flare up. With a growl he rushed Roberto but was very sloppy in his attempt, so Roberto grabbed the beast by it’s throat and then put the blades on his other hand up against it’s throat. The creature stopped struggling knowing full well that it would perish if it did so. Roberto leaned in closer, still carrying that arrogant smirk, and sniffed the air around the beast. His smile faded and a look of complete disgust came over him. He leaned in even closer than before, so close that he could smell the blood of the last person this THING ate. “Call me GOD.” Without saying another word Roberto drove the blades into the throat of the beast, then with all the power in his body he lifted the blades, and before the scene could get too gruesome it burned up like the rest. Roberto stood in that room now, alone. In that brief moment he chuckled at that thought, being alone that is. Being alone is the only way of life he has known for years, so now was no different. He looked around the deserted place and felt a little satisfaction on what he had accomplished that night. He had killed hundreds of them since that night seven years ago, but it never took away from the feeling of doing something right with each new one. After standing in the room for minutes, but to him it felt like hours, Roberto took of his gloves and put them back where he got them. Then he digs into another pocket and pulls out a black cross, which has a few red buttons on the front of it. He closes his eyes and puts the cross on his forehead, before whispering something in Spanish, a prayer perhaps. Then he presses the middle red button and tosses the cross onto the floor of the place and turns and begins walking out. He pushes the doors open and walks back out into the night air. It was as if nothing had ever happened, people were still partying, drinking, and the men were still trying to find women to bed with. Even at this hour children still wandered the streets without a care in the world. Roberto knew that not all of these people tonight would wake up tomorrow the same as before. A good number would wake up tomorrow night with a hunger never felt before. When that happened he also knew he’d be there ? he’d be there to put an end to their dark ways, and to toss them off their mortal or immortal coil. After getting about twenty paces from the whore house it exploded, flames burst into the air and screams were heard whistling through the air. People from all around ran to the scene to see what had happened, but not one. Roberto walked passed the crowd that had gathered from a far. He placed a cigarette between his lips and lit the tip, sending a swirl of smoke into the air. The only sound he could hear once again was the sound of his breathing, and the sound of his boots crashing on the dirt beneath him. The night was young and he had plenty more stops to make.
