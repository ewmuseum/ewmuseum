The answers...are right before you.

16 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-16-06 “We’ve been through this a million times, Nick.” “And we’re going to keep going through it until we find out what the hell is going on.” Hunched over in front of a computer, Detective Nicholas Pride tapped away at a few keys on the keyboard. His head was tilted to the side and resting on the palm of his hand, propping himself up as to not fall face first into the nineteen inch, flat screen monitor. His eyes were narrow, with bags large enough to carry a week’s worth of clothing. Lately, Pride had been obsessed with finding out more about Jayce Bradley. A man he knew nothing about until a few months ago, when he risked his own life to save the life of Pride’s daughter. Most men would consider that an act of heroism, considering the fact that Pride’s flesh and blood was being held at gunpoint by a sociopath who had already murdered countless innocent people, but not Pride. He was suspicious of a man who came out of nowhere, risking his own life when the murderer was already surrounded by a small army of local authorities and the entire S.W.A.T team. He became even more enthralled with the idea that Bradley was somehow connected to the whole situation, when he noticed the serial killer; Russell Braun, mouth something toward Bradley as he watched the incident in slow motion back at the precinct not more than a month later. By this point, the witch hunt he had embarked upon had occupied much of his free time, as he began to visit numerous sites where skeletal remains had been unearthed - in hopes of finding some sort of connection to the serial killer who had nearly robbed him of a child. Dead ends. He was making little to no progress, becoming even more impassioned to prove that he was not wrong in his assumptions. Which is what directed him toward searching the past of Bradley’Opening up a whole new can of worms in the process. “You have the test results right in front of you, Nick. How can you ignore the facts because of a few words some old lady said?” Only his eyes moved, lifting up to gaze over the monitor at the very well kept body of an olive skinned woman with light brown hair that seemed to frame her features before cascading gently over her shoulders. “She knew him, Sam. The only person alive to have any knowledge of him, and she said he was dead.” His eyes grew in size, his nostrils flaring as he exhaled deeply. “But the-? The sound of his fist crashing down onto the hardwood finish of his desk cut her off. “I know what the test results said.” His breathing grew deeper as he stared down at the white sheet of paper tucked neatly beneath his keyboard. “But he ain’t Jayce Bradley.” He lifted his head once more, his eyes welling up with tears as the emotions begin to course through his veins. “Jayce Bradley is dead.” =?=?=?=?= “Jayce Bradley!” The ear drum splitting scream nearly knocked him from his seat. Composing himself enough to glance in the direction of the scream, as he wiped away a bit of the mucus that had gathered in the corners of his eyes, Jayce Bradley answered. “Yeah.” “The doctor will see you now.” He stood, flashing a nervous smile to a kid with braces that kept him from fully closing his mouth, then made his way toward the nurse holding the manila folder with his dental records. “Is he in a good mood,” he asked nervously. “I’d hate to catch him on a bad day, you know.” “The doctor is perfectly fine.” She walked a few feet in front of him, flipping through the contents of the folder as she made her way through the hallway without lifting her head even once. She knew the place by heart, probably spending more time at the office than she does at home. But he doesn’t mention it. Instead, he follows her to the last door at the end of the hallway. Where she turns, and motions for him to enter. “The doctor will be with you shortly.” And like that, she was gone. A few minutes pass, when a portly fellow wearing a long white lab coat walks through the doors. He pays Bradley no mind, instead he keeps his eyes fixed on the same manila folder that the nurse could not turn away from. “Mister Bradley, after looking over your records, I cannot find --? When the doctor looks up, he sees someone he was not expecting to see. His eyes grow wide in disbelief. “You” But?” “Calm down, I’d hate for anyone to get a bit suspicious,” Jayce said in a calm and collected voice that could lower the arms of two thousand rabid fans at a rock concert. “He promised you would not be back.” “And I planned on keeping that promise, but I need another huge favor.” The doctor shook his head from side to side, clearly wanting to keep himself from being involved any deeper. “I’m not sure if I can-? Jayce never allowed him to finish that sentence. “I need you to make me disappear.”
