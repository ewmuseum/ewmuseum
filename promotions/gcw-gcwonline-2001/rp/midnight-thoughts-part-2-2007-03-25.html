Midnight Thoughts: Part 2

25 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 25, 2007 The ceiling fan takes three seconds to make a full rotation. I know this because I’ve counted it thirty-seven times now. There isn’t much else to do when you can’t sleep. Sometimes it looks as if there are more than five arms on the fan, but it is just my mind playing a trick on me. A lack of sleep does that to the brain. My eyes, tired and weary, glance over toward the illuminating neon numbers on my alarm clock. [12:18 AM] Rubbing my eyes, I lay my head to the plush feather pillow. It is another night, another sleepless night. Rolling to my side, pulling the blanket up to my shoulders, I lean into the woman beside me. Her eyes were sealed shut in beautiful sleep, something I envied deeply. Her soft and silky arm raises up, feeling my shift, and fell across my body. Mumbling incoherently in her sleep, Tessa pulled me in to her body. I could feel her warmth pressed up against my body. It was soothing, in a sexual way. I tried to let myself slip away in somber rest, but there was some block keeping me from my destination. My eyes crept back open, peering at my love lying in the bed with me. “How is it' my mind asks, 'that she can love me when so many others seem to hate my very existence?' To that, I seemed to have no answer. The question had been poking me in the back of my mind for days now, tossing itself around for debate. I had done my best to suppress it, hating to think like that about her. But the question would not leave me alone. The attacks against my mind were usually the worst at night, when my defenses were down. I had to try and create answers, to utter words I still did not know. Once my barrier was down, the questions flooded to my, creating a tidal wave of doubt. Did she use me as a way to boost her career? That seemed silly to me, to say I was nothing more than a stepping stone. If that was true, then why would she still be with me to this day? But she hasn’t always been with me, now has she? No’she hasn’t. As much as I have tried to forget about them, I could not always leave them behind. Dawn McFarlane, Seymour Almasy; these were two names that would always haunt the back of my mind every time I glazed into Tessa’s eyes. She certainly never spent much time alone that was for sure. I know about Seymour, I was there for that. It doesn’t bother me nearly as much as it use to. I learned to get over it; it was something that happened in the past that I couldn’t change. It was a different story with Dawn McFarlane. Maybe it was the fact that I still did know what just what happen during that relationship. Tessa and Dawn were dating, traveling around with Global, while I was stuck in Chicago. I was too bullheaded to call her, and she wouldn’t call me. The blanks are what ate me up. What did the two do? How did they decide to start dating? What made them take that first leap? For what reason did Tessa change from being straight to being homosexual? That is not a decision to make over a cup of coffee. Never once have I ever asked her just how far she went with Dawn Did she have one too many drinks after a show and clothes fell to the floor? Did hormones flair after a show and passion ignite? What would she think if I asked her? Would she view me as invading her privacy? Or worst yet, as some weird pervert who just wanted the down and dirty details of a lesbian love? Since this is me we are talking about, her seeing the second option would be very likely. It is a legacy I have built for myself, one as a man of great sexual interest. It’s my own fault, I know. But in this case, it is not just me wanting some mental images of the woman I am to marry getting it on with another woman. I love her. Let that be known now and clearly. I can’t always explain that love, I just feel it deep within my heart. I feel it now as I stare into her soft face. I would but lean toward it and lay a peck of my lips to her rosy cheek, but I fear I would wake her. My interest in her relationship with Dawn is not meant as a means to perk my sexual interest. Rather, I view it, as best I can understand it, as my being overprotective. I must admit, I am no saint. I have never once claimed to be, nor do I ever try to be. I am who I am, that is all I need to be. Tessa is who Tessa is. She is sweet and innocent, like the first snow fall of the year. It’s pure, soft, and delicate. You almost don’t want to disturb it, but rather preserve it to be kept safe for years to come. I simply am worried if her soft and delicate self was preserved, or was it defiled into the same disgust that the rest of us have slithered into. She wasn’t meant to be tarnished like the rest of us. I can’t lose that mental image I have, for so long, held dearly onto. Looking over to her, I begin to recall Dawn's words. Just how far did she and Tessa take their relationship? Ha, listen to me, wondering about this. Most guys would only dream about their woman with another chick. It is the type of thing you read about in Hustler magazine. Deep down I know it’s my fault. This is my entire fault. I should have done this, or I shouldn’t have done that. I drove her away from me. What scares me though is if I could, I’m not sure if I would change anything. I know what I have done in the past is wrong, and I am sorry, but I still believe there was a reason for my actions each time. They may not have been good reasons, or justified reasons, but they were ones I believed in. I dunno, I think too much. That’s why I hate these sleepless nights. My brain is not one that should be left to roam. Tessa, Dawn, it is all too much for me. Spinning and spinning, my head goes around in circles until I am sick. I guess it would be important if I do clarify one thing. Even though I am not sure if I would change my past, I do feel guilty about it. I feel guilty for every kiss I forgot to give. For every embrace I wasn’t there for. She has stuck with me through all of it, though the long business trips to the expected tribulations in our lives. She is more than I could have asked for. She has put up with more than I would have thought. Again, like a virus, that nagging thought tries to beat its way into my mind. “Why?” it asks. I have to stop and ponder for a moment on why she had, in fact stayed with me for so long. It seemed like a rather odd thing to think about. A wise man would accept it, be happy, and move on. I think it has long been established that I am not a wise man. A sigh escapes from my lips. As much as I hate it, I pull myself from my lover’s grasp and rolled out of the bed. My eyes darted over to the bathroom door, the thought of last night still fresh in my mind. No, not again, I’m not going to try that approach again. These thoughts are not just going to go away because of a few moments of self-pleasure. They may recede, but they will never leave until I have let the wheels turn. On autopilot, my body hovered over to the kitchen and came to a rest on one of the wooden chairs at the table. It always seemed like the kitchen, for whatever reason, was the best place for me to let my thoughts roll around in my head. I’ll be honest; these sleepless nights are getting on my nerves. It is disrupting my life, these thoughts. I was happy, for once in my life, to just let things be. But I’m not allowed that happiness, am I? There is a hole deep within me, growing by volume each day. Something is missing, a thing I can not put my finger on. It’s eating my from the inside, yarning for that food. That hole is stirring up emotions in me that I had thought I had buried long ago. I’ve been more prone to anger, to rage really. It is not fair to call it anger, it feels different. It is more of an animalistic hunger. Sociality is telling me one thing, but my body and mind are telling me another. It is becoming confining, confusing, and I hate it. Snake-like arms of darkness wrap my body tightly and squeeze my soul. Liquor might help. It’s too bad I don’t drink. I’m sure the devilish fires would calm the beast inside of me. It’s Dawn. I’m nearly sure of it. Ever since I stepped into the GCW, these emotions have been stirred and slowly becoming a tsunami of my soul. I’m not sure how, or why, she is doing it to me. But each time I see her, I want to hurt her. At those fleeting moments when I feel sleep brushing against my cheek, I see her blood spilled on my hands. Or at least I believe it is her blood. Who else’s would it be? Mine? Tessa’s? Thinking about it, I’m not certain. No. It is Dawn’s. It has to be. She has to pay; she has to pay for what she had done to my Tessa, for trampling in that pure snow. I want vindication. These thoughts scare me. It’s not who I am. I’m not that person; he’s just a character, a figment of my imagination. Chris Storm is nothing more than an illusion of the television, a fictional character who has taken on a life of his own in many ways. Maybe he’s taken on my life?
