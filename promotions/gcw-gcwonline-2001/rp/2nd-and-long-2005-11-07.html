2nd and Long

7 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/07/2005 After WorldWide... “Man, this is bullshit! That punk cheated me! That was MY win!” Varsity kicks the door open to his locker room, shoving aside anything that might be in the way, like that poor, defenseless trash can. He growls, stomping over the bench and slams his fist into the lockers. There might be a bit of blood, but he doesn’t particularly care about that at the moment. Instead, he’s focused on the BS that just happened to him moments ago... Styles enters after him, much more calm and shaking his head. Which is a very vast change, considering that Styles is usually the hyper one. He pulls Varsity’s towel off his shoulder and drops it down on the bench. “V. V, calm down, man...” “Calm down”! What do you mean, calm down?! I hate this place! Ever since I got here, I’ve been gettin? shafted. My first match? TV title shot! Whee, right? WRONG! Not only do I get completely OWNED, but my locker room gets trashed to hell and back. Then, I’m stuck on my ass for two weeks, while the GCW “superstars” get their time in the limelight, when God and everyone else knows that I could take one anyone of them and get the 1-2-3! I finally get back into the mix, right? Tonight! Me and Jayce Bradley! One on one! Mano y mano! And what does that little sneak do?” Styles just leans up against the wall, hardly even paying attention to the rant, but he responds anyway, “He cheats.” Varsity nods, shaking his head and throwing up his hands. “Yes! Exactly! He cheats! He gets rewarded and I get another check in my loss column! He gets to move on up the ladder, while I get to have a seat here on Loser’s Row. He gets to have a victory party, while all those fans out there get to wonder if they got their money’s worth tonight! Is that fair”! Huh?! Is it?!” Styles, still leaning up against the wall, and picking at his nails ever so nonchalantly just shakes his head once more. “Nope. Not fair at all.” Varsity sighs, finally slumping down onto the bench and shaking his head. “I shoulda” stuck to football. I can’t take this. It’s like... this wrestling? There’s no rules. There’s no honor. There’s no code. There’s nothing but selfishness, bastardism, and...” “Hot chicks?” “... yeah, hot chicks.” Varsity frowns, turning and glaring at Styles. “Not. Funny.” “Sorry.” “You know what I’m talkin” about.” Varsity’s voice is finally starting to become more calm. The initial shock of being cheated out of his first win is starting to wear off. “I’m not about all that. I can’t be about all that. I can’t just... shut out the world and be in this only for myself. How can these people live like that?” “I dunno, man. I dunno.” Styles shrugs a bit, peeking down at his cell phone and then looking back up. “Maybe it’s got somethin” to do with the homoerotic undertones of two big sweaty men groping each other in front of hundreds of people?” “...” “What?” “...” “What”!” “...” Varsity can’t contain his smile any longer and falls over into a fit of chuckles. “Shut up, man!” He recovers fairly quickly and gives himself a toss of his bangs. “Sorry “bout the rant, man. It’s just... this stuff is harder than I thought, y’know” I’ve never failed at anything in my life.” “And you won’t fail at this. You just gotta” hang in there. This ain’t football...” “But in a way it is, Styles. In a way it is.” Varsity shakes his head, turning to start putting his stuff into his duffle bag. “It’s 2nd Down. I just got sacked for twenty yards. Got a penalty for five. The clock’s running and I’ve got no Time Outs left. It’s tied up and I’ve got time for maybe one more play...” Varsity grabs his football off the shelf and turns to look at Styles. “What do I do?” Styles shrugs a little bit and offers, helplessly, “Wink at the head cheerleader?” Varsity rolls his eyes. “No.” The football gets tossed across the room, slamming into Styles? gut and he fumbles to get control of the ball. Varsity pulls his bag up onto his shoulder and starts towards the door, feeling better now that he’s gotten all that stuff off his chest. “I score.” Varsity smiles and heads right out the open door, disappearing into the hallway. Styles just continues to stand there for a moment, peering down at the football. Finally, he looks back up with a confused look on his face. “Wait... score” With who? The cheerleader?” And with that, Styles rolls out of the room, following after his best friend, while everything else goes into that classic fade...
