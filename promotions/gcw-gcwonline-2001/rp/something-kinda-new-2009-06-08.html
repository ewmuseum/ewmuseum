Something Kinda new

8 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/08/2009 “So I’ve been thinking,” my uncle Erik announces as I enjoy my sweet and sour chicken. I eye him speculatively and wonder what he has in mind this week. Ever since I had been hospitalized right before my match with Jake Reed, my uncle has been coming up with increasingly stupid plots to keep me safe. His last one somehow involved me living in India, but I had basically stopped listening after the first five minutes. “Lorenzo is not going to stop pursuing you until he has you locked up for life,” he continues, “and he’s got enough pull in this town to possibly make it happen.” That statement is true enough to force me to pay attention. A few weeks ago I pounded Lorenzo’s head into the wall to stop him from beating his wife. Unfortunately, it was only after I taught him the value of being a gentleman that I learned Lorenzo basically owns the town. So far he has had me arrested…and I suspect that he might have arranged to have me poisoned though my uncle disagrees. “So I think that the best solution to keep you away from him is to leave the town. There’s not much that he can do to you once you move away, and besides I’ve already achieved what I wanted with you.” I bristle with indignation. “Like I’ve already told you,” I growl, “I let Jake Reed go because I was done kicking his ass, not because of your stupid morality lessons. And I’m not about to run from a short little prick whose ass I could kick with one hand behind my back!” “He’s a little prick who can bring down the entire police force down on you with one hand tied behind his back,” Erik points out to my great annoyance. “Besides, there’s someone else you have to run from…or have you forgotten.” I grit my teeth and stare down at my plate. I am entirely convinced that Lorenzo had been behind the attempt to poison me last week; Erik is equally certain that there is no way Lorenzo was behind it. If Erik’s right it would behoove me to go into hiding. “Look, I made a few calls,’ Erik says finally getting to the point. “What you need to do is frankly go into hiding while I find out who the hell is after you. You don’t just need to go somewhere else…you got to take up a whole new identity.’ I stare at him for a moment in stunned incredulity. “I…am” I begin in the tones one would use with a moron, “a wrestler on national television. Going into hiding is not really an option for me.” My uncle smirks at me, and with a sinking feeling I realize that he has already worked out all the details. “You’ll be in disguise most of the time Danny. We’ll smuggle you into and out of the wrestling events, and the rest of the time…” I want to tell him that there is no way I am going to go into hiding. But I know that I don’t have much of a choice…hopefully into won’t be too bad. (Three days later) Three days ago I was Dan Black, a successful wrestler in GCW. I lived in Ohio and spent my days training; and I could get laid whenever I wanted to. My days were spent training, my nights were spent partying, and if not for the enemies I seem to have a talent for making…I would have considered my life to be pretty damn near perfect. Now my name is, of all things, Alex Goldberg. I wear a wig to replace my black hair with blond, and contacts that turn my eyes green. In my view it’s a piss poor disguise, but it’s better than nothing. I live in a pleasant little apartment, and my next door neighbor is an old lady with cats. Ironically I am back in my home state of New Jersey, and I live in an suburb known for its quality educational district. But the real kicker is how Alex Goldberg makes a living. As it turns out, I am the brand new High School gym teacher. The very thought makes me shudder. I did not have a fun time in High School. College was where I finally broke out of my shell and started to have a social life. But my memories of high school are painful at best, and I have no desire to retry the experience even from the other side. Still I suppose it could be worse, it’s not as if I actually have to do too much anyway. I take attendance and handout the dodge balls. At most I’ll probably have to prevent the kids from killing each other…but besides that I don’t think I’ll have too many problems. It is with that cheerful thought in mind that I walk into the high school for the first time. It is, I swear to God, the most stereotypical school I will ever see. I can already tell that the kids are all segregated according to social status, the hot girls and jocks versus the band geeks and so forth. Well, it does break from the mold in one way. There is surprisingly little physical bullying going on, though plenty of verbal exchanges. I look on with interest as the popular kids continue to mock their less popular classmates. All the standard insults are flung, but it does not progress farther than that. I am guessing that there are teachers around where I just can’t see them, but I have a feeling that there is something to the dynamic that I don’t see. “You must be Alex Goldberg,” a voice says next to me. I turn and find a rather pretty girl…wait no I take that back. She is not pretty, not even a little bit. She has all the potential to be hot, even beautiful. Unfortunately, it seems as if she went to the beach, then visited five tanning salons right after that. Her skin is so orange that I feel like I am getting skin cancer just looking at her. “I’m Alice Stockhom,” she says with a smile so white that it almost blinds me. “I teach the students how to like; you know, cook and stuff.” She places a hand on my arm and I try not to shudder as I imagine skin cancer transmitting from her to me. “I can tell that you’re going to teach gym.” “Very intuitive,” I remark as I try to think of a way to politely extract myself from her. “Well let me show you the way to the gym,” she chirps and I can think of no reason to object. Resignedly I allow her to lead me to the gymnasium, though I manage to tune out her incessant rambling. I use the time to explore the school itself. Based on the decorations it’s a far more spirited place than my high school had been. There are decorations on almost every locker, and the halls are plastered with posters and signs. Almost everything I see is dedicated to praising Monroe High School and its mascot, the Green Knight. I admit I wonder whether the spirit is genuine, or whether it’s just a poor attempt by the administration to rustle up some spirit among the student’s population. “Well here we are,” Alice announces breaking into my thoughts. With a sigh I open the door and witness the place that I would spend five days a week for the foreseeable future. It’s pathetic. No…that’s too kind. If we fixed the leaky roof, taped down the mats, cleaned all the shit, and hired a professional and gave him a blank check then it would be pathetic. As of now, it looks like someone took a shit in it, smeared that shit all over the walls, and finished by detonating a bomb. “W-w-wow,” I stutter out too shocked to speak. “Yea, it kinda sucks” Alice replies though her tone of voice does not change at all. “Some of the parents wanted a new gym, but the school got new computers instead.” Suddenly everything makes sense. I have stumbled upon the rarest of all phenomena. A high school in which interest in academia prevails, and the athletes are on the lowest end of the spectrum. I think back and wonder whether the teasing I heard when I first entered the school was just a desperate attempt at self-defense. Well not self-defense…but oh hell you get what I mean. “Hey, are you our new coach?” a voice behind me asks. I turn and find a group of five boys standing behind me. They are huddled together, and their combined lack of self-esteem is so low and apparent it’s almost visible. They are all white, but diverse in their sizes. One seems to have the potential to eventually grow into my size, while I could probably pick up the smallest with one hand. “Um, what the hell is he talking about?” I whisper to Alice. “Aren’t you the new wrestling coach?” she responds entirely too loudly. I could say no, explain that I never agreed to such an arrangement. But there was something in the eyes of those five boys that told me I had to teach them to respect themselves. Ok fine, I just like the idea of being able to mold the five guys into ruthless wrestling machines and unleashing them onto the school. Wait a minute…I should probably start training for my match with Shaman shouldn’t I? “Come on boys,” I say as an evil light enters my eyes, “we have a run to go on.”
