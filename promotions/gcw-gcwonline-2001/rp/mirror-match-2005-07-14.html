Mirror Match

14 July 2005

Date: 14 July 05 ** not sure what exactly I feel about this RP BUT its my Infinite Gauntlet RP ... eh nothinspecial i guess! ** His eyes were closed tight as he bowed his head and quietly spoke a prayer in Spanish. His long black hair was falling about his face, and it glimmered from the water that doused it mixed with the lights from all around. His body was tense, this was evident by the veins in his arms and hands bulging out, running through his body like underground pipe lines. His words were finished and slowly his eyes opened up again. Even through the cloud of dark hair, those icy blue eyes were shining through, almost as if they were tiny lights deep inside those eye sockets. His massive chest lifted slowly as the breath came into his lungs, and then pushed out as the wind was exhaled in the same rhythmic matter. “LeStatt ? two minutes.” LeStatt did not say a word, just slightly nodded towards the member of the Global Championship Wrestling crew that came by to cue him. It was almost that time, that time to showcase everything he had learned over the years, inside and out of that ring. It was this moment that he lived for. No matter how bad things were at home, in his relationships, or how fragile his mind may have become, inside of that ring was his sanctuary. All eyes were on him and his next move. The roar of the crowd, the flickering of the flashes from the cameras, the stomps and claps, the thunderous applause, it was all part of what made this his HOME. It was his own personal battlefield, a place that very few could touch him in, and that was something to be proud of in his eyes. Since returning to the GCW, the birthplace of “LeStatt Knight”, he had indeed have proven his worth ? to an extent. Not one of them had dominated him, and not one of them had proven their superiority over LeStatt in any fashion, in fact his two losses could be contributed to other factors all together. The fact that he has lost but two matches is a testament to how extraordinary his run has been thus far. Yet he knew that there was more to unlock, more glory to be had, and more respect to be earned. In LeStatt’s mind, RESPECT is what made a man a MAN. One can have all of the glory, riches, fame, women, fortune, and good will in the world, but if he is not respected as a man then he has no purpose. Inside of that ring he could demand respect, not only that but he could TAKE the respect of others simply by destroying them. That’s when the eerily familiar notes of “O’Fortuna” began to slowly leak out from the sound system and find its way to all of the fans in attendance on this night. One more deep breath, one more hard swallow, and one more quick prayer and he stepped forward to face his destiny. The man known as LeStatt Knight walked towards those familiar curtains, reached out and swung them open wondering what would greet him on the other side. His blue eyes glistened from the sun that beat down on his face and all through the Coliseum. The crowd was thunderous in their rage towards him, as they always were. Slowly the warrior walked forward, inching closer to what could prove to be his death. After about a hundred yards Knight stopped and stood in the middle of the dirt battlefield that he now graced with his commanding presence. Those evil eyes trailed down at his trembling hands which were covered with black, ripped up gloves. Slowly those fingers extended and spread out before being forced together again, creating strong fists. On his body rested the armor fitting a Dark Knight, a Knight that loved nothing better than to spill your blood all over his hands, and then lick them clean. LeStatt Knight stood in the middle of the coliseum and looked out into the mob of humanity and let out a faint smile. That smile may not have been much but the look behind those eyes told everything needed. Arrogance seeped through this man’s pores, an arrogance that led to the hatred that he was shown from most everybody else. Yes he was despised for his cockiness, arrogance, and flat out disrespect to some of the warriors he did battle with, yet at the same time he commanded the respect that he lusted over. With each swing of his fist, each swipe of his blade, and each drop of blood spilt, his legacy grew and that respect broadened. It wasn’t Knight’s quest to make people love him, because Fear and Respect go hand in hand. LeStatt slowly dropped to one knee, kicking up the dust that filled the floor. Next to him rested one sword, the sword that would keep him from joining the world of the dead in this next battle. As he rose back to the standing base, the sword came with him. A tight fist wrapped around the black but dirty handle and thrusted it into the air, letting the sun gleam of the blade, causing the crowd to roar. Knight’s eyes closed once again as he took in their boos, their disgust, and their lust for his head and he loved every moment of it. Slowly again those eyes opened and again the setting was familiar but it was not the same as the previous one. Knight stands atop the middle turnbuckle; in front of a capacity crowd with one arm high into the air causing the barrage of boos to flood the arena. Knight lowers him arm and then leapt off of the ropes and onto the mat. Knight stood tall in the center of the ring, now awaiting the one who would face him on this night. The sound of thunder is heard coming from the speakers and going into the ears of the LeStatt. All throughout the arena the lights dim and the big screen flickers a few times before coming on solid. Dark clouds in front of a black sky are all that are seen. Confusion spreads, but LeStatt shows only a slight change in emotion. From behind his mask of darkness, those eyes were the only things that he could never hide. Sure the emotions that surely flooded his very being could be kept locked up like a dirty secret, but as he always has said, the eyes are the key to a man’s soul ? and despite LeStatt’s best efforts, it was true for him as well. It was then that a dark figure stepped from those very curtains that he had just moments earlier. Like him, this figure had long black hair that fell over his face and was cloaked in black. It didn’t even seem as if his new adversary was walking, but more like gliding down the path of steel. LeStatt did not budge though, he kept his ground not one to be out done by mind games. His opponent stopped at ring side shortly before reaching up with both hands and clutching the middle rope and slowly pulling himself up to the apron. From behind masks of hatred, darkness, and of course long black locks these two glared at each other. It could have been seconds, minutes or even hours that they tried to measure each other, perhaps even read each other and neither would have known the difference. The tension that great and was continuing to grow. The crowd was silent, only the sound of the thunder was heard still rumbling through the arena. Even if the crowd had made noise, LeStatt may not have heard it as he could only concentrate on the eyes that lurked inside of the one he faced. The stare only broke when the mysterious figure climbed into the ring. LeStatt lifted a hand and brushed the hair from his face, and for the first time gave a clean look at his facial features. Still everything but those icy eyes remained emotionless, but those eyes were focused and digging deeply into the very core of the man standing in front of him. The figure lowered his head slightly, but only for a moment as he thrusted his hands into the air causing a huge white light to blast inside the arena, blinding Knight and everyone around. Knight’s eyes burned from the light and instinctively they closed. Immediately a hand came up to rub the burn from the eyes. With blurry vision Knight looked out in front of him but only saw dust and a shadow in front of him. The roar of the Coliseum was thunderous again; Knight’s red eyes looked down at the sword that was burrowed inside his vice-like grip. Out of instinct Knight rose the blade and almost simultaneously his enemy brought a sword down as well, having the blades clang together signaling the beginning of the epic battle. The force of the blow took Knight back a few steps and out of the cloud of dust. LeStatt’s eyes desperately tried to refocus, slowly they did. From behind the cloud of dust walked that dark figure. Now he was head to toe in black armor with a red dragon down the chest. His face was all covered by a helmet, the only part of him staying uncovered were his dark eyes ? the devil’s eyes. You see to most the world of wrestling was just a job or even a love of theirs. To Roberto Abitua, the one that brought life to LeStatt Knight, it was much more than that. To him being a wrestler meant that you were a warrior, or even a gladiator. To win a simple match is fun but to win a war for your life is the ultimate prize. He doesn’t see punches, kicks, pin falls and chair shots, no in those Icy blues of his there is Armor, swords, blood, and the roar of the roman mob. To fight for your life, is to fight with everything you have. He dove forward and rolled out of the way of an overhead swipe of the dark warrior’s sword, immediately getting himself to one knee. Knight lashed out with his sword but his swift adversary leaped over it, this gave Knight the opportunity to get back to his feet and thrust forward only to have his sword knocked to the side and a fist slam into his face. Anger built up inside of him as he stumbled back. The warmth of his own blood began to flood his mouth and tingle his taste buds. His eyes grew wide as he saw the blood trickle from off his lip and onto the dirt beneath him. His eyes fixed on that blood, his blood. The emotions inside of him ranged from excited to fearful to lustful. It wasn’t often that his own blood was the first to be shed. He broke his trance and sprung up to his feet again and threw a round house kick which connected with the oncoming nemesis to the side of the head. An animalistic growl came from the attacker, but Knight couldn’t allow this to stop his offensive. Knight spun and swiped his blade at the man’s head, but it was dodged, the spinning backhand by knight was not blocked however and it connected with the head. The impact of the blow caused Knight’s adversary to stumble backwards. Now the two rush at each other lifting their swords high into the air and bringing them down simultaneously, the clash of the swords causes a deafening screech ? and the blades shatter into pieces in their hands. Each of these gladiators look briefly at the shards in their grips before dropping them and turning their anger filled gazes at each other once again. That same fierce growl escaped the figure’s mouth once again and LeStatt snarled towards the one who stood between him and immortality. It was LeStatt’s greatest battle to date; that he was certain of, this creature was powerful, intelligent, and frightening. It seemed to know every move he would make, perhaps even before he made it. LeStatt came forward first with an overhead strike that was blocked by the dark beast, whom spun and tried to land a backhand similar to the one Knight connected with moments before. Knight would have none of it as he ducked down and under the blow, he came up swiftly though and drove a fist into the ribs of this powerful being. A howl of pain ripped out from beneath that helmet as the beast pulled away and grasped at its side. Knight drove forward with a front kick that was caught only an inch away from its destination. The beast uses all of its strength to lift the foot of Knight, causing LeStatt to do a complete back flip. Knight loses his balance a bit but doesn’t fall until The beast moves forward and delivers a fierce uppercut, which sends Knight flying back. His body spins in mid air as he falls face first into the ground with a loud THUD. The blow forces his eyes closed for just a brief moment, but when they open he is laying on the mat ? staring out at an empty arena. He shifts his head from left to right, no longer are there fans packing the wrestling arena, no commentators, no security guards, cameramen ? hell there are barely any lights left on. He reached a shaky hand out and grasped onto the bottom rope, and that’s when the sound of the footsteps behind him were heard loud and clear. LeStatt pulled himself to one knee and looked backwards, seeing his enemy standing in front of him, half engulfed in shadow. They both remained motionless; Knight though was trying to discover whom his greatest threat was. He tried and tried to see through the darkness, but it was so thick that if he would take a step towards it, he would probably be lost in its glory. It was the kind of darkness that made children scared to sleep at night, the kind of darkness that nightmares were made of. Deep inside of his very being something began to stir. That strong and emotionless face began to crack, and those Icy blue eyes began to soften. Somewhere and somehow it hit him, Knight knew exactly who it was that could bring him down. There was only one being in the world that could break him to the point that death would be the only way to be really free. The only one who could put the fear of GOD into his heart. Warily Knight rose to his feet once again. Everything seemed so much clearer now, almost as if he had looked into some sort of crystal ball. Those emotions boiled inside of him and soon they would be unleashed. With a battle cry Knight rushed towards the one in darkness only to be caught with a stiff boot to the chest, forcing him into the air for a brief moment before his back collided with mat. The pressure of the beast’s boot came crashing down on his chest and slowly put more and more pressure down. Knight looked up ? and saw the Armor clad nemesis once again. The Skies were black and the coliseum empty. The Dark knight before him stood victorious over him, with the shard of blade wielded in his hand. Knight could not struggle anymore; the only thing left was to give in to the inevitable. Through the taste of his own blood he hissed at the one who had defeated him. “SHOW YOURSELF!” The Knight obliged slowly bringing his hands upwards and grasping the bottom of his helmet and sliding it off, letting it fall to the dirt behind him. The one that now stood above LeStatt was exactly who he expected, but yet it did not stop the fear from rushing to him. Indeed he knew what lay ahead in his future. Blood would be shed and lives would be destroyed. There was nothing he could do about it because it was not him pulling the strings. Outside forces do not create evil, it comes from within. Slowly his eyes looked upon his adversary, the greatest adversary all men have. HIMSELF. It was that dark half, the inhuman side of all of us. It is the only thing that can destroy us if we let it. LeStatt Knight simply was not strong enough to fight it off so now he must give in to its power. The Evil creature now smiles and raises that shard high into the black sky. A tear runs out of the corner of Knight’s eye just as he slams his eyes shut. Everything goes black and the only thing that is felt is the sharp pain that runs through his chest ? -FIN-
