'Bad' News

12 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/12/05 He walked around furiously, looking for any substance that would work as relief for his back. Only because moments before, he managed to slip backwards and crack his back against a 2-inch thick bar braced by the wall. From this, you can probably tell his physical therapy wasn’t going so swell, and all week it’s been like this. Nothing but a painful hell, or rather, agony for Coyle O’Conner. When he forced himself up into what would become a scavenger hunt for relief, the doctor’s in the room rushed over to him, trying to help him; questioning and talking a mile a minute. Coyle, “Leave me the hell alone! Get away from me!” Yelling so demandingly and outrageously as he pushed out to the sides with his fists, alerting everyone to step back. Coyle, “I’m not’really’ready’to be touched.” Grasping and grunting as he had eye-contact with every individual in the weight-room. He was pointing to his back, yelling that it was in pain. The last thing he wanted was for some “dip-shits” to ask him what happened. But how would they understand what he has been going through for the past 2 weeks? Suddenly, some random guy yelled out what would become a personal-regret. Guy, “Perhaps you shouldn’t be wrestling ya” dumbass, you obviously aren’t in th’right shape, X should’ve taught you a lesson, we know he could’ve.” As this was being shouted, Coyle had his back turned to everyone. But, of course, this caught his attention. He turned around with a malicious grin on his face, eyebrows perched and all. He found the anonymous trash-talker, and took just 4 steps to him. Coyle, “Heh, you got “tude?” Guy, “All I’m sayin” is leave everyone alone, never mind your problem, we frankly don’t give a damn, it’s our therapy session too?” ??tude? Coyle called it, or, absence of respect. “tude was the reason why some poor smart-ass got his ass-kicked after his witty statement. Coyle taught the guy a lesson, and taught the others to back away. So, they did just that. And after minutes of searching with no satisfying results, Coyle figured his medication would do the trick, but that was all the way back home. They even moved to give him space as he left the premises. 15 minutes later, Coyle was stepping into his front door. After he closed and locked it, his cell phone started playing “Fallen Angels” by Ra. A swift look to the caller I.D. turned out to become a phone call from a familiar face. Coyle, “And good day to you Mr. Marky Rahl?” Marky, “Coyle, how are ya” doin? buddy ol’pal?” Coyle, “You know, struggling, but what bad news do you have for me this time?” Marky, “Oh, you know, you’ve been signed for a straight-up title match this Thursday night.” Coyle, “No shit?” Marky, “I shit you not.” Coyle, “Well that’s good news, for what title muh’friend?” Marky, “thee Television title. Apparently, after your terrific and stunning performance at FallOut, the GCW officials felt it was necessary to bump you up as a number one contender, and Mike Saviage is going to be putting that leather on the line for you to win, which we both know you’ll do.” Coyle, “If it’s what needs to be done, you know I’ll be doin” it.” Marky, “That’s the spirit! But Coyle, I’ll call you back in 20.” A quick flip-down hung up the call, and a slight swoop into the pocket finalized the happy session for Coyle. Coyle, “Just to think, I’m getting a shot at the Television title. That’s more exposure?” But despite the “bad” news, wink, Coyle’s back was still in pain. He had to get those pills, but it seemed like an adventure to him; his muscles tightened up, his body relaxed from exhaustion, his broken-back beating with his heart. It was such a task but he eventually got them. And boy, did they feel good.
