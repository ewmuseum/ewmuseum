Date: Timeless The building is packed. Thousands upon thousands sit in anticipation, awaiting the moment when the deed is done. Today is the GCW Hall of Fame Induction ceremony. Today GCW inducts Shadow, whom will be inducted by Virgil Ambrosi, Johnny Borealis will be inducted by James Bryan, and Bryan Mayhem, whom will be inducted by Lestatt Knight. Over 20 years ago these contestants made GCW the largest sports entertainment company in the world. Most in attendance are older, because the younger generation only knows stories about these amazing athletes. He stands in the middle of the aisle, looking around confused. His arms are crossed across his massive chest. He recognizes some faces, sitting on stage there’s an older Steven Caldera, also an older James Bryan. How the hell did they age so quick. He rubs his hands across his face, feeling for wrinkles. He feels none. No one in the crowd seems to notice this giant lurking in the main aisle, this beast of a man is staring at the very same stage they’re all affixed with. He stands still and listens to the speeches of Shadow and Johnny B. He keeps asking himself how the hell they got so old? Why are they inducted in the GCW Hall of Fame anyways? He just saw them the other night at Devil’s Night. You can’t still be on the roster and be in the Hall of Fame. He’s extremely confused. The older Steven Caldera comes to the pulpit and says a few things. He can’t decipher what this older man is saying. The only thing he can comprehend is “Ladies and Gentlemen, Lestatt Knight. His arms drop, his eyes become furious. His arch rival, the man he hates the most. He begins to make his way to the stage when he notices how old this man is as well. That’s not Lestatt. Lestatt just beat the holy hell out of Dawn McFarlane just the other night. What the fuck is going on? This supposed “Lestatt Knight” limps slightly to the pulpit, the crowd is explosive. This is the part of the night everyone has been waiting for. The beast in the aisle creeps closer, hoping he can hear whatever this impostor has to say. “20 years ago I had one of the most painful, most bloody, most sadistic feuds of all time. Both of us were at the very peaks of our careers, but that match in Belmont made headlines. To this very day whenever I’m recognized on the streets the first thing I’m asked is how the hell did you survive Belmont. Having the longest World Title run of all time takes second to what happened in that haunted house. But that was 20 years ago, and this is today. Today, after years of anger and hate, today, after all the blood has been shed I can call this man one of my best friends. When he called me and asked me to induct him into this Hall of Fame today I was never more honored. He was one of the most feared men in all the land. He was one of the largest men I’ve ever seen, and he took more beatings than anyone on earth, yet still walks today. Ladies and Gentlemen, it is my honor to induct in the GCW Hall of Fame class of 2027 The Man Beast, The Lunatic Bryan Mayhem!” He jumps in the aisle as the place erupts into a roar. They are cheering him? Why is Knight saying all these nice things about him? That’s when he drops to his knees. Coming from behind the curtain, walking with a limp with his cane. His left arm dangles down, only moves when it comes in contact with his body. This battered and beaten old man comes to the pulpit. The crowd stands on its feet, and the old man just stands there and soaks it in. A tear falls from his face. His good arm lifts, and gives a pathetic little wave. The crowd explodes even more. The beast kneeling in the altar puts his hands to his face. He recognizes the old man. He knows the man. It’s him! The beast now realizes that he’s stuck in some fucked up dream, or some twisted time continuum. Bryan Mayhem is watching his decrepit self. The older Mayhem is talking, but the younger Mayhem can’t hear him. It seems as if his time has slowed down. Because those in the crowd are moving in a slow motion action. The Younger Mayhem turns his head towards the right and see’s in the upper deck a young man, with flowing blonde hair, almost like Goldilocks, reach down and pull out a gun. The Younger Mayhem screams, but no one can hear him. The older Mayhem keeps talking in his silent lip movement. Younger Mayhem makes a run towards the stage, but like those in this world that he’s in, he legs move like sludge. His using his muscles to their furthest extent, as much as they will let him, but he’s still moving in a slow motion replay. As he trudges along he turns right and notices Goldilocks eyeing down the older Mayhem through the scope. Younger Mayhem can see the red dot on older Mayhem’s chest. Younger Mayhem, silent scream, slow motion see’s the bullet exit the barrel. The sound is a slow thud. The bullet whizzes through the air and disappears into the older Mayhem’s chest. His entire cavity explodes outward through his shirt. His whole back is blown out. His mouth begins to vomit blood. The younger Mayhem somehow exits this slow motion and almost trips over himself when he reaches full speed. His runs towards the stage. No one in the crowd or on stage even seems to react to what just happened. The younger Mayhem jumps onto the stage and grabs the older Mayhem. These two lock eyes, and both back up against each other, almost in fear. Blood is spurting from the hole in his chest. His skin is getting cold, face is turning white. Older Mayhem lifts his hand, and runs it across the younger Mayhem’s face, and says something that causes younger Mayhem’s jaw to lock. He looks into younger Mayhem’s eyes and says “Bones are crushed, souls are lost, in the end, it’s the dead that are tossed”..overboard.” Younger Mayhem watches the older Mayhem perish in a puddle of blood. He looks up, and looks out. The building is empty. He looks back down, the older Mayhem is no longer there. No body, no blood. Mayhem stands up, looks down and notices blood seeping from his chest’slowly leaking from his skin?.
