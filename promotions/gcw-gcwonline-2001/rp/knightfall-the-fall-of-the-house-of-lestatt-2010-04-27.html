KnightFall: The Fall Of The House Of LeStatt

Date: 04/27/10 Teen-age Mut-ant Nin-ja Tuuurrrrtles Teen-age Mut-ant Nin-ja Tuuurrrrtles Teen-age Mut-ant Nin-ja Tuuurrrrtles Heroes in a half-shell, turtle power! Absolutely fascinated by the television, the little blonde girl smiles as the day is saved once again by the antics of Raphael, Donatella, Leonardo, and her personal favorite... Michelangelo. The Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles did it again, just like they had countless times in the past. Although the outcome was highly predictable, to a six year old girl the show just never seemed to get old. As she got older, however, there was one question that always seemed to perplex the child's mind. "Vivica." The sound of her mother's voice almost made her jump off the floor unexpectedly. Perhaps going and changing the channel while her mother was trying to watch one of her shows wasn't the smartest decision that she'd ever made, but her father called. Lord knows those call take an eternity... at least, in little girl time. "Yes mother?" Her response was short and sweet, she tried to ooze the charm but like most times, it was a lost cause. "Why do you always go up and change the channel the second I leave the room?" The expression on her mother's face, the tone of her voice... Vivica knew this wasn't going to end pleasantly, "I've told you time and time again not to touch the remote!" "I'm sorry!" "You're always sorry but you still keep doing it. I don't think you're very sorry at all." Her mother continued, now in full lecture mode. "I leave for a few seconds to talk to your father and the second I leave you always go and turn on this... Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. If it's so important to you we can record is on the VCR and you can watch it later, but the television is mine. You don't touch other people's things without permission." "But mom..." "Don't 'but mom' me, young lady." Vivica's head dropped as she rose to her feet knowing the punishment that lied ahead of her, "No more television for the rest of the day." "MOM! That's not fair!" "Well sometimes life isn't fair. The earlier you learn that, the better." Her mother reminded her for the trillionth time. Sticking out her lip with her blonde hair hanging over her head staring straight down, Vivica pouted her way out of the living room as her mother picked the remote up off the coffee table. With a sigh, Maria sat down in her favorite chair and flipped the television back to where it was. The Golden Girls. As she stomped and temper tantrumed down the long hallway, otherwise known as the walk of shame, she finished a thought that she'd started earlier. Why is it that no matter how many times the bad guys fail, they keep trying to do bad things? The Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles were surely going to stop them, or else there wouldn't be a television show named after them. Yet they simply refuse to be good guys, and insist on remaining bad guys. Even more importantly... Why do bad guys even exist? Pausing in her steps, Vivica turns around and walks back down the hallway towards the living room. Surely even in her upset state her mother would have an answer for her. Her mother knew everything. Stopping at the doorway, she wasn't quite sure how her mother would react by her presence. Instead of marching into the room, Vivica simply held onto the doorframe and peeked her head around the corner towards her mother. "Mother?" Vivica meekly questioned, preparing herself for the unleashed hell she might be awakening. "Yes, Vivica?" Her mother answered, marginally less annoyed that previously thought. This was a good sign. "Why are there bad guys?" "Well bad guys..." Taken off-guard by the question, Mrs. Valentine tried to formulate a proper response. "Bad guys... are evil. They're born that way. They're not like us. Their brains work differently than normal people, and they are unable to stop doing bad things. Most of them have some kind of issues." "So..." Vivica questioned, "I'm not a bad guy for changing the channel?" "It's not the same." Vivica's mother couldn't help but smile, "You're not a bad guy, you just need to learn to listen to me when I talk to you. Understand?" "Yes mother." The answer was far from perfect, but it was a rationale in which a constantly questioning six year old could get behind. Raising her head, she simply walked back to her room now content that for the rest of her life... there was no way she was ever going to be a bad guy. Of all the memories I've seen, this one is by far my favorite. These days it plays over and over again in her head like an old home movie, trying to convince herself that she's not a bad person. That despite the things she's done and the way people feel about her, that she's doing the right thing. Oh Vivica, if you only fully understood the way the world works. Bad guys aren't inherently evil, they're just misunderstood. Outcasted by society because of what they are brainwashed to believe the standards of an "upstanding citizen" are. That's the difference between people like us and people like them, Vivica. We're not able to be controlled or manipulated to act a certain way because big brother wants us to. We're not willing to bite our tongues or neuter our actions because such behavior might be frowned upon. Frowned upon by the very people who like to sit around and believe that they're morally perfect. They drink alcohol, they cheat on their wives, they beat their lovers, they steal stuff from work, and they lie about it every step of the way. All in an effort to be loved... that hits home a little hard for Vivica. They're the evils placed upon society, not me. You're probably wondering who I am right now, how you hear my voice, how I got here? I've been known to go by a lot of names in the past. Some you'd recognize more than others. Most simply know me as an unnamed voice in their head. I'm the one you're told not to listen to. The one everyone is always trying to get rid of but can't, no matter how hard they try. Hell, I might even be the reason religion exists... but I'm not such a bad guy. Just an easy target without a way to defend himself. Outside of the mind, I don't have a voice. People may view that as a weakness, but I have my own ways you see... I play by my own set of rules. Not anyone else’s, including Vivica's. I bet you're expecting a song as soon as I'm finished, aren't you? Poor little Vivica, so predictable... but that's why this is going to be so much fun. No one ever saw it coming. No more pandering. No more need to be loved. Those things are so very trivial. The best part for me? No more little songs for The Bulletproof Blonde to get her through the day. They're far too distracting from the thing she really needs to hear... me. Why? Because she isn't playing by her own rules anymore either. She's playing by mine. KNIGHTFALL The Fall Of The House Of LeStatt ---- There is something about being behind the wheel of a car that tends to send the mind racing. Perhaps it's the long drives with nothing better to do or just the feeling of being on the road, but Vivica J. Valentine often felt she made her best decisions behind the wheel. With the sound of "Demon Speeding" by Rob Zombie blaring through her speakers and the wind blowing through her hair, everything that seemed to plague her suddenly disappeared. It was just her and the road. A burden lifted off her shoulders that finally allowed her to relax and feel like herself again. Things are quiet... More than anything she was just excited to get home again. It had literally been months since her last visit to Highland Park, Illinois. The schedule was just too unapologetic to make it back. To have a chance to sleep in her own bed... to be inside her own home. To feel safe again. She'd almost give up her entire world. Pausing at the red light, she knew she was just mere minutes away from dumping her bags on the floor and crashing on her own couch. A luxury that many take for granted. A little too quiet... Pushing her foot on the accelerator, she could feel her heart roar as she made the left turn. Closing her eyes for just a second she took a deep breath, hoping to release months of tension in one last exhale. Instead she began to cough violently and unexpectedly, almost forcing her black Chrysler off the road and into one of the dozens of cars parked on both sides of the street. Screeching to a halt, her eyes grow wide as she watches the red lights of a seemingly endless line of fire trucks sweeping down her little side road. A group of police officers turned to look at her as she turned the key, shutting off her engine in the middle of the street. The scene she view in front of her looked just like one of those disaster movies. Panic. Calamity. Chaos. She was almost afraid to leave the safety of her car as one of the officers approached. Her hand was numb as it gripped the door handle and popped the door open. Leaving the car she looked upwards, watching the black clouds as they billowed into the blue sky. Her first two steps felt almost as if she was walking on air. "Miss, you can't park your car here." The officer spoke upon deaf ears. The entire world was silenced around her as her life suddenly started to move in slow-motion. Her heart sank more and more with each step, focusing down the street on what was almost certainly a horror to behold. People lined the streets as she went past, following her with their eyes as she began to weave in and out of police officers, firefighters, and paramedics. "It's not mine. It's not mine..." She muttered to herself over and over again, trying to ignore the people whispering as she passed. With each house she passed, she lowered the odds. Each step made her heart beat a little harder. Each step made her breaths a little more shallow. Her hands began to shake as cerulean blue eyes navigated two doors down. Yellow house. White shutters. Covered in black. Her jaw dropped as the scene she was so desperately trying to convince herself wasn't true unfolded before her very eyes. Her hard work and dedication laid in messy charred heaps outside of her windows, shattered like her dreams of a single day of relaxation before leaving tomorrow afternoon. It froze her in place, unable to make it to the group of firefighters and police officers trying desperately to contact the homeowner. It went straight to voicemail. She didn't want to face the truth, but oddly couldn't look away as nearly one hundred people had their eyes focused squarely upon her. They desperately waited to see her reaction, perhaps even her demise. Her knees wobbled under her as she tried to take a step forward. Body was too weak. Everything began to blur. A single tear ran down her cheek before she collapsed onto the pavement. With her last few seconds of consciousness she heard the shriek of a little girl, followed by the fading image of a man covering the eyes of his youngest son. Fade to black. Everything could've been saved... Everything was gone. ...but they didn't even try. ---- "Miss Valentine, can you hear me?" A voice broke through the smoke and the flames. Lights began to bring blurred images to her eyes. As everything came into view, she looked to her side to see a man sitting next to her wearing a black dress shirt and a silver badge. Reading it as it became clear, she spoke the word aloud. "Saunders." She answered before putting her hands on her head. It felt like she'd just taken a piledriver off of the top rope. "Nice to see you come around Miss Valentine." He spoke, watching as her eyes tried to bring the world back into focus, "A shame we met under these circumstances, but unfortunately this happens. My name is Captain Dustin Saunders of the Highland Park Police Department, you had a bit of a tumble back there, you feeling alright?" "No worse than normal." She spoke, trying to sit up before being quickly shoved back down by the EMTs surrounding her. "You hit your head on the pavement." Saunders spoke, "So I'm going to have to ask you a few questions." "Shoot." "Your name?" "Vivica Julian Valentine." "Your date of birth?" "Halloween. Nineteen Eighty-Two." "What is today?" "Thursday." "Have you been drinking in the past twenty-four hours?" "That's a joke right?" "Just answer the question, miss." "No. Just water... I think." "Only water?" "Yessir. Maybe a Gatorade? Not sure how long ago that was." "Are you currently under the influence of legal or illegal anti-biotics or narcotics?" "With all due respect sir, I'm straight-edge." "Excuse me?" "I don't drink. I don't smoke. I don't use prescription or over-the-counter medication." "Or recreational drugs?" "Or recreational drugs." "How about head trauma?" She chuckled to herself when he asked that question. Obviously he didn't have the slightest clue who he was talking to. Perhaps that was for the better. "Can I get up now?" The EMTs looked over at Capt. Saunders as he glared into her eyes, looking for any sort of awkwardness before giving the go ahead. "She's fine, let her up." He motioned for the EMTs to give her a little bit of breathing space as she struggled to push herself up to a seated position. Suddenly the ambulance felt like it was spinning around her, but that was normal due to the circumstances. If anyone knew this series of questions, it was her. "Now... head trauma?" "A laundry list." She replied, "Can I have a glass of water please?" "What do you do exactly?" Saunders asked curiously, "I haven't had a second to breathe, not to mention check out your file. This place is swarming with media. You're going through the motions like a pro and tell me you have a laundry list of head trauma? I've been with the force a long time miss and I have to tell you this looks incredibly suspicious. The thing that baffles me is that I've never even heard your name before." "I'm a professional wrestler." Vivica answered slowly before a small solo cup full of water is handed off to her. "With the spandex and the baby oil?" He asked her, "No offense miss, but you don't look the type." "None taken." She responded before taking a drink. Who burned your house down? "I want to see my house." She quickly demanded, catching the officer off-guard. "Can I go?" "I'm sorry, I can't let you leave this vehicle." He answered, "Too much press out there." "I deal with them all the time." "Miss, give it up. You're staying in here. You're better off. You'll have plenty of time to walk around in that scorched hell in a few moments." She saw his hand slowly move over towards the tazer located on the right side of his belt. "I'll tell you what, it's not pretty." "Then let me talk to Patrick." She explained, "The housekeeper. I want to know who did this." "I'm sorry ma'am, but I'm afraid I can't do that." A man said as he entered the ambulance. Taking off his fire hat, he placed it up on the shelf and signaled for the EMTs to leave. They filed out behind him as he slid off his jacket, taking a seat next to Captain Saunders as it flopped down on the ground. "My name is Fire Captain Jake Nichols, I'm with the Highland Park Fire Department. I'm going to run down what happened today." "Arson." She replied immediately. A strange look crossed Nichols' face as he simply shook his head no. "Electrical fire." Bullshit. "We got a phone call about two hours ago from a neighbor, telling us that they saw smoke coming from a nearby house. By the time we got here, the entire motherfucker was up in flames. Took us an hour to fight them off, but by then it was too late." They just stood around and watched. "I'm sorry to let you know that we couldn't save much of anything, but if you ask me miss you're lucky to be alive. If you would've been inside that house you would've been a goner for sure. Almost got one of my men." He responded, "Unfortunately, it did get one of yours." "Patrick." Her eyes grew wide and her heart came to a screeching halt, "Is he okay?" "He's dead. Smoke got him." The Fire Captain sounded sincerely apologetic, "I'm sorry. Nothing we could've done. Was he family? A husband?" They all wanted to see you burn. "Housekeeper." Valentine responded, her emotions still numbed from the events. "If you give us some information, we'll contact his family." He handed a notepad over to The Bulletproof Blonde, who proceeded to stare down at the blank page in a state of shock. "My condolences." "No offense, mister." Vivica slowly looked up from the piece of paper, "But I don't think this was an electrical fire." "Miss, with how much damage was done here it can be difficult to assess what happened exactly until the next day." His answer almost seemed rehearsed as he went on, "But from the information we've collected at the scene thus far, we're positive that this was an electrical fire started in you living room. Our guess is a power strip behind your television sparked this whole thing. Set fire to the carpet and when your couch went up, it burned so hot it shot a fireball up through your ceiling from the pressure." Listen to his voice for Christ's sake, he's acting like it's no big deal. "It was only a matter of time until it spread. We got a hold of your insurance company, they tell us you're on the road a lot and won't need much as far as a place to stay here. When you do, they'll take good care of you and fix up your house for you." He stood up, taking another step towards the door, "It'll take about six months but everything will be as good as new. You'll have brand new everything, and while I know that doesn't matter to you right now... it'll be nice when the time comes." "Is insurance going to replace Patrick, too?" Valentine questioned sarcastically, obviously not thrilled by the answers she'd received thus far. "Miss, we've done everything we can." Nichols understood Vivica's plight as he opened the door, "Now if you'll excuse me, I have to go clear the area for your walk-through. Afterwards you're free to dig through those piles outside of the windows to try and find anything that you'd like." He wants to see you burn, too. Valentine watched helplessly as Nichols left the vehicle, slamming the door shut behind him. Now she was alone with Captain Saunders, who simply shook his head thinking about the whole situation. "If it's any consolation, my wife and I lost our..." "How do they know for sure it's an electrical fire?" Vivica interrupted him, "I just think it's funny that everything in there is all scorched to fucking hell and back but they can identify what happened in there by looking at a bunch of ash and rubble." "Well there are distinct things you can look for..." He tried to explain, but was cut off once again. "What if he's wrong?" She questioned, "What if someone did this to me?" "You'd have to hire an independent fire investigator, I'd assume." Saunders pondered, "See if he can find anything different." "And to press charges?" "We can't press charges without a cause, Miss Valentine. Why do you feel someone would do this to you miss?" "I can think of a few people that would. That's all." "Any names in-particular? We can look into it, I suppose." LeStatt Knight. She paused for a moment, taking a bit to process all the information in her head. Why didn't she realize it earlier? It was that bastard LeStatt Knight... it just had to be. There was no other explanation. "Roberto Abitua." The words escaped her lips with a seething hatred, almost as if she wished a curse upon the name as it left her tongue. "That sick son of a bitch..." ---- Wading through the blackened, smoke filled hallways was almost as if she was navigating her way through a nightmare. One by one she looked on helplessly as everything she'd ever purchased or anything she'd ever known were destroyed. It's amazing how little you think of something until it's gone. Suddenly the smallest most mundane things become the most important parts of your life. Things like soap, toothbrushes, clothing... those are the things that cross your mind. As she hit the walls with a shake flashlight, wondering if there was anything at all left to scavenge. Even the smallest thing to make her feel like home would do, but there was nothing... nothing at all. Picking up a picture off the hallway table, she smeared her hand across the soot revealing a picture of her and Tessa Windsor years ago before Global Championship Wrestling. With a sigh she laid it face down back on the table again, accidentally shattering the fragile glass on impact. Taking a step back, she knew it was time to call it a night and leave. It was almost getting too dark to see. There was nothing to be saved. As she turned around, she could've swore she heard a rustling from behind her. Holding her breath, she stood completely still hoping to hear the noise once again. There's somebody here with you. Turning around very quietly, she inched towards a door partially open at the end of the hallway. Torched wooden floorboards cracked under her feet as she closed in, hearing the faint rustling sound grow louder and louder as she approached. Reaching out towards the door, she watched it slam shut in front of her without warning. The impact made her pull her arm back in surprise, forcing her heart to skip a beat. He's still alive. "Patrick?" She said out loud, desperately hoping to hear his voice even after seeing his body being carried away by the paramedics earlier. There was none. Reaching back out towards the doorknob, she bit her lip a bit nervous about what could be inside of Patrick's room. Maybe it was a wild animal who decided to take shelter in the home since the windows were knocked out? Grasping the doorknob, she twisted it open and slowly pushes her bodyweight into the door. Creaking open slowly, she reluctantly peeked around the door to reveal a... YOU TREAT ME JUST LIKE ANOTHER STRANGER IT'S NICE TO MEET YOU, SIR... The sound of her voicemail ringer shot her straight out of bed like a cannon. Both unexpected and startlingly loud, she was breathless as she grabbed the cell phone off of her nightstand and looked around her hotel room in a slight panic. Another day, another hotel room. She still couldn't believe her home was gone as she flipped the cell phone open, hoping to hear something... anything at all to prove to her that everything that had just happened was a dream. "You have three new messages. First new message..." "Hey Viv, this is your mother. I really hope you're not still planning on leaving today to go to that wrestling match, you have much more important things to take care of at home. You need to help plan Patrick's funeral and set up the new living arrangements. I know your career means a lot to you, but this is kind of a big deal. With how much time you spend on the road, I really do think that you deserve some time off. Call me back, Viv. We're here to help you." Let's be honest Viv, she's not exactly mother of the year material and now suddenly she's here to help you? Where has she been your entire life? Where was she when Roberto was torching your house? Where was she to let the police know Patrick was inside? You could've been inside and she made no attempt to save your life. She's never loved you before Vivica and she's always hated what you've done. She's just trying to use this fire to get you back and force you to be like her. Fuck her. "Message deleted. Next new message..." "Hello Miss Valentine, this is David Young. I stopped by the house this morning and took a look around to see if I saw anything different than the fire department did... and boy were they right about there being a lot of damage. It was hard, but according to the floor plan you sent me it looks like the fire started behind where your television would've been in the living room. There's a V-Shaped pattern on the wall and that's usually a tell tale sign of where the fire started. Although I suppose arson isn't completely out of the question, I regret to inform you with the information we can collect on the scene there would be no way to prove it in a court of law." What a waste of your money. How many hundreds of dollars did you have to spent for this incompetent asshole to tell you everything that you already knew? Fact of the matter is that he couldn't prove it wasn't him. He's just wasting your time. "Message deleted. Next new message..." "Miss Valentine, hello there this is Captain Dustin Saunders again. We looked into Roberto Abitua at your request and unfortunately he has a pretty rock solid case here. Looks like he was on a flight on the other side of the country. Some fans even have a video up on YouTube of him walking through the airport just a few hours before the fire started. You said you had some other leads, perhaps you'd be willing to come down to the station and talk about them with us? We'd appreciate it, thank you." Bullshit, he couldn't have been... "Who are you?" Vivica spoke out loud to herself for the first time, acknowledging the lack of control she's had over the voice in her head. "What do you want from me?" I want the same thing you want. "And that is..." Respect. The respect you deserve, Viv. The respect WE deserve. "And we're going to get that by falsely accusing people of burning down my fucking house?" He did it, Viv. I know he did. "I'm out of my fucking mind..." Focus, Viv, focus. With my help you can beat LeStatt Knight at Shockwave. "I don't need your help." You always say you don't need any help Vivica, but look where it has taken you. You have no friends. No home. No fans. No lovers. You haven't won a match in over a month... let's face it, you're a wreck. You're even worse off than you were when you were on that losing streak. "Get the fuck out of my HEAD..." You NEED me, Viv. I'm all you have left. You just have to give me a shot. Give me the chance to kill that bastard once and for all. Think about all the things he's done to you. Are you really going to tell me that you don't want to see that man suffer? "WHO IN THE HELL ARE YOU?!" Look up. Sitting on the edge of her bed, Valentine almost doesn't want to listen... but has to know the truth. Slowly her head rises from the ground, cerulean blue eyes behind locks of black and blonde gaze across the room at the mirror. Standing up, she slowly moves the hair out of her eyes and looks back at her reflection... but it's not there. What she sees in the mirror is LeStatt Knight, smiling back at her. "Fuck you..." Vivica mutters to herself. Haha, please allow me introduce myself... I'm a man of wealth and taste. "You're LeStatt Knight." I've been called that by one person before, yes. "I'll fucking KILL you..." But I've been called a lot of things before. Everything from The Devil to The Sickness... but you don't need to be afraid Vivica because with me, I can make you everything that you've ever wanted to be. The unstoppable force you've always dreamed of being. All you have to do is let go. Give me a chance and I'll show you results. "No. Not the way you do it..." Why not? It worked for Roberto, didn't it? "I'm nothing like Roberto." She snapped back, "I'm better than that." That's precisely why I want to help you. I think you're right. I think you are better than he'll ever be. You see, after all the hype at the end of the day, Roberto Abitua isn't the immortal LeStatt Knight... he's just a man. He bleeds like you bleed. He makes mistakes like you do. He puts his pants on one leg at a time. "Your point?" I know his every flaw, his every weakness because you see Vivica... without me he's nothing. Just a shell of himself, a man who just wants to retire and go home. He's a quitter with no heart. What you need to do is to show the world just how weak he really is. "How am I supposed to do that?" You make God bleed... you make God suffer. You show the world that God is fallible and they'll lose faith. They'll stop believing in him Vivica, just like they stopped believing in you. Don't let him fool you into thinking he doesn't care about those people. He does. He wants to go out as a God. He wants to go out on top at your expense. With my help, you can maim him in front of millions and bring him back down to Earth where he belongs. Looking back down at her hands, she notices they begin to shake violently. She really was losing control. "What do you get out of it?" She asked reluctantly, for the first time in her life she was legitimately scared of the things going on in her mind. Fearful of the very thing she needed to beat him. He threw me aside like he never needed me, after everything I did for him. He pulled the trigger and thought everything would be all over. You're not the only person who he's pissed off. With your help, I can teach him a lesson he'll never forget. Running her hands through her hair she watches LeStatt's lips move on the mirror as the voice resonates through her brain. For the first time in her life she wasn't able to drown out the voice with a song or go on a jog to make it go away. It was stronger than ever, eating away at her moral fiber and her soul. Trying to change her into the very thing that she'd always promised herself that she'd never be. But he was right. Look at where her moral values got here now. Look at what doing the right thing had brought her in life. She had absolutely nothing besides her car and the clothes on her back. If she turned her back now there would be nothing to lose. They always said that you have to hit rock bottom before you could look up. It doesn't get any lower than this. Opening the drawer of her nightstand, she clinched a pocket Holy Bible in her hand. She's never been a religious person and grew up thinking the whole thing was kind of a joke. Yet if there was any one time that she needed God to come through for her and give her an answer, to give her any kind of sign to do the right thing... it was now. Looking back at the mirror, the reflection of LeStatt Knight was now gone... all the was left looking back at her was her own baby blues. Complete with matted hair and bags under her eyes from the total lack of sleep. She looked like hell. There had to be a better way than this. Certainly this wasn't the way she'd imagined destiny playing out. The dreams of gold and grandeur from a few years ago were far gone, replaced with the broken hopes and aspirations of a mutually hated fallen phenom. Those people didn't think like she did, she wasn't like them anymore. She wasn't normal. "LeStatt thinks he's sooo bad..." She muttered to herself as she backed away from the mirror, looking down at the pocket hardback Holy Bible she clutched in her right hand. Lifting it up she looked at it for one last time before cocking it back and hurling it as hard as she could at the mirror. Shattering, she watched her own reflection fall to the ground piece by piece. "If LeStatt thinks he's so bad," A smirk crossed the face of The Bulletproof Blonde as she got down to one knee and picked a shard up off of the hotel room carpet, catching a glimpse of her own reflection in the jagged shrapnel, "just wait until he sees what I can do."
