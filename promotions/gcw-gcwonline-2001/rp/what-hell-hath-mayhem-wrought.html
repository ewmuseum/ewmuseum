What Hell Hath Mayhem Wrought

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Timeless -=The Dark Occult, a demented twisted form of Satanism has succumbed the good of the earth into a world of dark, wrath, and anger. There are over 7500 churches of the dark occult all over the world. The most reside here in England and a tiny city in Norway called Oslo. Most churches follow the teachings of Aleister Crowley, or more commonly Anton Szandor Lavey. Bryan Mayhem, annointed a minister here years ago returns for some soul searching and advice from legendary Satanic Priest Oystein Aarseth. Mayhem sits in the back of the church as Aarseth begins his service, all in Enochian (The Satanic Language) His opening speech has been translated into English=- Aarseth:'Behold!, saith Satan, I am a circle on whose hands stand the Twelve Kingdoms. Six are the seats of living breath, the rest are as sharp as sickles, or the Horns of Death. Therein the creatures of Earth are and are not, except in mine own hands which sleep and shall rise! In the first I made ye stewards and placed ye in the Twelve seats of government, giving unto every one of you power successively over the Nine true ages of time, so that from the highest vessels and the corners of your governments you might work my power, pouring down the fires of life and increase continually on the Earth. Thus you are become the skirts of justice and truth. In Satan's name, rise up! Show yourselves! Behold!, his mercies flourish, and his name is become mighty among us. In whom we say: Move!, Ascend!, and apply yourselves unto us as the partakers of His secret wisdom in your creation!' -=Mayhem sits in the back and smiles. He's home, and welcomed here. He is missed by his minions and followers. During the service, the very service he has memorized he quietly exits the church and stands outside. It's not your typical church, as a matter of fact it looks like a giant home instead of a church. Mayhem face painted, black T-Shirt exposing his demonic tattoo's, black jeans, and combat boots stands in awe of the presence inside those hallowed halls.=- Mayhem:'I think it's time for me to call it quits. No not as in retirement, but quits on this normalcy, quits on my behavior, quits on my weakness. Coming back here has made me understand who I really am. I Am the LUNATIC, and I am the voice of the Amarth.' -=Mayhem begins to pace slowly, almost in anticipation, for he knows no fear.=- Mayhem:'The Angel of God appeared to me like the Arch Angel to the Virgin Mother. A lot bigger than I anticipated (devilish grin) It seems as if this apparition of God has tried to convince me of his presence. Foolish, extremely foolish, for now I know not when to stop the punishment. I shall smite thee upon thy cheek with such a smashing force that I will know not the outcome of your future was once said by Lord Lavey upon being backstabbed by his mistress Marilyn Monroe. An Angel of God has decided to interfere himself in the business of madman that fears no one, and shall feel no pain.' -=Mayhem smacks his hands together twice in rhythm and instantaneously his minions flock from what seems like out of nowhere to his side. All 10 black cloaks, bald heads, pale faces. Mayhem grins at his minions and quickly turns towards the church, and motions his minions to follow. In step like a marching band they follow Mayhem one bye one single file into the doors of the church. A loud boom is heard as Mayhem pushes open the large wooden door to the worship room. Worshippers, and clergy alike stop in their tracks and stare at the one interrupting their service.=- Mayhem:(loudly) 'I invoke the 6th Key of Enoch, follower and prophet of Dom Sathanas.' -=Those in attendance gasp at his request.=- Aarseth:'Against whom?' Mayhem:'The Man in White.' Aarseth:'Very well than, so let it be written, so let it be done, that Lord Mayhem has invoked the 6th key of Enoch. For it is said on this moment that a Holy War has been waged.' -=Mayhem bows to Aarseth, and motions for his minions to follow him out of the service.=- Mayhem:'Pray for his soul minions, because I am the demon God warned him about. Welcome to Oblivion.'
