The Return of the Church, Part 1: No Partner?

25 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/25/2009 “ALLIANCE, n. In international politics, the union of two thieves who have their hands so deeply inserted in each other's pockets that they cannot separately plunder a third.” -Ambrose Bierce 10/19/2009 Afternoon Inside of von Doom’s house… It’s just another crazy day at the von Doom house. My new tag team partner Count Von Macabre and I are plotting and scheming what their plan is in regards to GCW’s pay-per-view event Dangerous Games. Firstly we’re in a tag team match against Mortifeira for the number one contendership to the GCW World Tag Team Championship. Then, later on in the evening, I will be participating in the biggest match of the year, Dangerous Games. The Count, in his usual vampire costume that he wears for his weekly syndicated television show Count Von Macabre’s Saturday Nightmare at the Movies. He’s doing a dress rehearsal for his show later on in the evening. Right now is the only time we’re going to have to plan until next week. So far, the planning session has just been brainstorming and no real plan has been forged. Most of the talk has had to do with my ‘Avant-garde’ t-shirt, however we are really making a lot of progress in the wrong direction. “von Doom, come on!” the Count growls out with an angry scowl. “This is not going to work. We have no plan that is feasible to help us win the match at this point. We have no chance against Mortifeira. This is going bad real fast.” I motion for him to calm down. He seems to be getting more and more angry. A vein is about to explode on his forehead like a zit on a teenage nerd. “Ok, we’re sitting in a great position,” I tell him with a devious smirk on my face. “We have secret alliances with about seven or eight people. Nobody knows who’s with us. Nobody. I have it all worked out.” The Count still doesn’t look like he’s buying what I’m saying. He looks more annoyed than anything else. “Dude, it’s hilarious! I’ve got all of them fooled and no one is even close to figuring it out.” Naturally he isn’t buying my bullshit. I wouldn’t either, but he’s a green rookie so he should have no other choice at this point. “But how does that help us out?” he asks, still not really into this whole idea. The house is creaking like one of those haunted houses at this point. Every noise is annoying me more than his reactions to my suggestions. “We’re facing Mortifera, two men who have nothing in common except for the fact that they like other men. So what? We have nothing to worry about. This is going to be a cakewalk. These guys couldn’t wrestle out of a wet paper bag. All of the smarks, marks, and hypocrites in the audience are usually bored to sleep whenever these guys wrestle. However they’re still dumb enough to pay money to see this garbage live. How pathetic.” “What shall we do?” “Count, we got this! This is going to be easy! We’re just going to casually walk in and then walk out with out heads held high while they do what they normally do and that’s stare off into space like mindless zombies.” Just then there is a knock at the door. I get up and walk over to the front door. Opening it up, the person knocking is my agent William Smith, who is the coolest agent in the whole world. He’s in his usual get-up of a black business suit. “Hey man, what’s up?” he asks me as he comes in and I close the door behind him, locking it. “What are you doing here?” I ask as he walks into the living room. He walks over and sits down on the couch. He looks around. “Just chillin’” he replies. “What are you up to?” “I was planning some GCW stuff with my new partner,” I tell him, looking around but not seeing the Count anywhere in the general vicinity of the living room. “However he had to leave unexpectedly to go film his little television show.” “Oh,” he responds, a little taken aback by what I just told him. “Well that sucks.” He looks around, tapping his hands on his lap. “Ok, so what are we going to do now?” “Nintendo Wii?” “What are we? Ten years old?” “No.” He has a good point. Well, there’s only one thing left to do at this point. “Bar?” He nods his head and replies, “Bar.” “I’ll call Malloy.” “Oh bother.” I pull out my cellphone from my right pocket and open it up. Look up Rick Malloy on my contacts list and press Send. Wait for him to pick up but nothing happens. He’s probably fucking a hooker. That’s better than what Steven Caldera does at his house. I leave a message and then turn to William. “Looks like it’s just us,” I tell him as I stand up and head for the door as he stands up. “Let’s go get some drinks and get wasted,” he says with a grin. “TIME TO GET BUZZED!” “And get a hangover, like the U.S. economy did last year.” “SNAP!” He says with a laugh as we exit the house and I lock the door behind me. I can tell this is going to be an interesting evening. I can almost taste the alcohol in my mouth already. I’m ready to get wasted at this point. Great, I should be training but instead I’m going out to party. Typical von Doom shenanigans. Fuck it. Time to get drunk. * * * The next day… As I’m setting up party decorations inside my house, the Count finally shows up and walks in. He starts looking at everything inside the room strangely. He seems to be a bit perturbed by what the room looks like. “von Doom, what the hell is going on?” he asks, giving me the evil eye. I motion around the room. “I’m setting up for our big victory at the pay-per-view,” I tell him with a smile. “I’ve got a banner, confetti, and a ton of party music.” He looks around once again and before going back to glaring at me. “Why?” he asks, putting his hands on his hips like an angry girlfriend or something along those lines. “We haven’t even performed at any GCW shows yet and they ran you out of there before for opening your big mouth. Why would you be this confident?” I smile and try to explain what the whole plan is once again since he’s already been told “It’s simple. I want all of these people to think that we’re non-threats even though we are. It’s all about mind control. If people think the way I want them to, then they won’t be afraid of us.” “What the hell kind of strategy is that?” “It’s brilliance is what it is. Between this and the ninety-seven alliances I’ve made for us, I think we’re sitting in a good position. We have secret alliances, we’re one win away from a title shot, and I’m in the Dangerous Games match.” “Why just you?” “They would only let me in because of my past history in the fed.” “But this isn’t fair to me, though. What am I supposed to do? I’m a vampire, unholy to core and I’m left out of the biggest match of the year. That does not bode well for the Church as a whole. It’s just you in there against twenty nine other wrestlers. That’s like a death wish!” I shrug it off. I’ve been in matches against more people than this. Hell, I’ve had whole feds want to kill me at the same time. This is going to be a piece of cake. Nobody thinks I have a chance. Hell, most of them don’t even know I’m in the match. I don’t care. A smirk comes across my face as I begin to tell the Count how it really is. “Don’t worry about it. This is why I’m in eighty two alliances at the same time. I’m sure that some of my secret alliance people will help me out along the way.” He doesn’t seem to care. He’s too busy worrying about getting more horror movie wrestlers for our organization or something. I don’t care. I really don’t. “We still need to think about how we’re going to bring in more Church members. Most of our membership was arrested last year and some have never been seen or heard from since our organization was disbanded.” “True, however you forget that I got connections.” “What do you mean connections?” “I know people in the business.” “James, I don’t care. Either get us some more Church members or I’m out of here.” I shrug my shoulders at this point. What the hell does this guy want me to do? “Count, calm the fuck down. Get that tampon out of you vagina and listen to me. I know a lot of people in wrestling and we can easily get more members for the Church. Just relax and we will be fine. Relax.” He sighs. “Alright, but this doesn’t seem to be going like I’ve foreseen it.” “Yeah, that makes two of us.” I roll my eyes. He gives me a dirty look and I shoot him a look back. “What?” “Nothing,” he replies. “Nothing at all.” He’s lying, I can tell. Hopefully he’s not one of these gay Twilight vampires that’s going to try and ass rape me or something. Already dealt with that shit when I was teaming with AXEL Action. Had to use a ninja sword on the fucker. He didn’t try it again. However the tension on this team doesn’t sit too well with me. I think this is going to go bad very quickly. If this vampiric idiot screws me, I’m going to be pissed. Only time will tell if it’s what I’m thinking or if it’s all just paranoid delusion. * * * Two days later… Night. Inside the Church of the Unholy, I have brought a whole bunch of guys who found off the street and paid five bucks each to become new Church of the Unholy members. Some of them are clearly out of shape. Some of them are homeless guys I found on the street. The rest look to be ex-cons or fugitives running from the law. In other words, they’d fit right in with the rest of the GCW locker room. The Count looks them over, not seeming too pleased with the additions I’ve made to our Unholy organization. He just doesn’t seem to understand that our membership is lacking. We lost two thirds of our members to a police raid last year. We’re at the breaking point and this is the only way we’re going to get people to join our organization. He just doesn’t see it the same way I do. “von Doom, who are these men?” the Count asks in a condescending tone. He glares at each and every one of the men with a look of disgust and disdain. In my opinion, his standards are too high. We went from having a bunch of humanoid monstrosities, rapist ghouls, undead fiends, and vampiric entities to this. I’d say this is a step up from where we once were. Nobody wants to see horror movie monsters anymore. They want actual wrestlers and I realize this. However, the Count would rather us continue along the same path as before. The path that leads us to financial and organizational ruin. I will not repeat the same mistakes as before so I try to be a good salesman and sell him on my plan. “They are the most dangerous wrestlers in all of the world!” I say with a smile, motioning to the men who are lined up like the bounty hunters from Star Wars, Episode V: Empire Strikes Back. “They look like a bunch of guys you just hired off the streets,” he mutters with disgust in his voice. I don’t think the new members like his tone of voice, either. At this rate, I might have to get myself a new tag team partner. “They are,” I reply to him as he gives me a disapproving look. “Well we need more members, don’t we?” I ask. “Yeah, but…” At this point, instead of finishing his sentence, he motions toward the new minions once again much to my dismay. “von Doom, come on,” he says. “We’re already viewed as a joke by most of the wrestling world and this isn’t going to help change that way of thinking.” “True, but we need the numbers.” “This sounds more like we’re going to suffer from the Stormtrooper Effect with all these guys. Use your head, von Doom.” Now I’m getting annoyed. This guy is talking like he’s been a veteran for years instead of some green new rookie wrestler. “So what? I got a bunch of useless mesomorphs. It’s better than having no one at all.” “No it’s not, von Doom!” he exclaims in frustration. “What are you talking about? The Church is a virtual non-existent entity at this point. We need all the help we can get at this point. I don’t know why you’re being so pessimistic at this point.” “I’m being realistic,” he responds. “This is going to make us look like a joke.” “We already are a joke!” I say in an exasperated voice. “We were a joke before any of this! And now we’re even more of a joke because you’re walking around here sparkling in the daylight like some gay Twilight elder vampire which is the last thing we need right now.” He glares at me and looks at me very menacingly like he wants to commit bodily harm to me at this point. “I quit.” He then turns and leaves. I throw my arms up in disgust before finally bringing them back down in disbelief. I can only shake my head at this point. Despite my ninety-seven wrestling alliances, I have no partner for the pay-per-view against two gigantic freaks of nature. “Great,” I mutter out. “Just great. No fucking partner.” Turning toward the “New Church goons” standing behind me, I know I don’t have a chance in hell of winning with any of these guys as my partner. “I think I need to make a few calls.” I’m fucked. Yeah, fucked in the ass. Like Gay Voice and Tyson Debough in the backstage area of GCW Worldwide every week. This is not good, this is not good at all.
