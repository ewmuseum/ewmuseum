Date: February 12, 2009 Don’t you just love it when a plan comes together? When a dream is realized? When the fruits of your labor are harvested? AXEL Action loves it. In fact, he’s been on cloud nine ever since NC-17, when he won the GCW Television Title. A couple of weeks prior to NC-17, he did the unthinkable and turned his back on “friends” in the Movement. The term, “friends”, is used loosely here, though. After all, everyone knows that the only reason the Movement wanted him around was to protect Dan Black and keep him from losing the TV Championship. The Unholy One was wise to their game and devised a plan to bring everything to a screeching halt. At Worldwide 103, he singlehandedly brought an end to the Movement. In Dan Black’s US Title shot against Garbage Bag Johnny, AXEL ran down to the ring, Kendo stick in hand. He led everyone to think that he was going to hit Garbage Bag Johnny with it, but instead, he blasted Dan Black in the chest with it, which led to Dan Black losing the match. Fast forward a bit to NC-17 and the TV Championship fatal four-way match. AXEL withstood the brutal Brazilian Jiu-jitsu onslaught of Dan Black, the dirty tactics of Mr. Danger and the sheer, brute strength of John Adams to win his first GCW Television Championship. Everyone’s asked him what makes him think he’s going to be any different from previous GCW Television champions. His only reply has been? ?Because I’m not afraid to do whatever it takes to keep this championship around my waist, where it belongs. The reign of UNHOLY has begun.
