Basic Training: Part Three

5 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.05.06 “Three hundred fourteen. Three hundred fifteen. Three hundred sixteen. Three hundred seventeen.” Ehren mutters as he continues the up and down motion. Ehren hasn’t stopped. No. He’s really done three hundred twenty push-ups. Most other men would have faked it. Upped their count and laid there for a while. Ehren knows better. He’s made that mistake before. His previous training taught him that someone is always watching. And that someone always punishes you for it. He continues to count underneath his breathe. The rain has stopped, but the ground is still soggy from the downpour that lasted nearly an hour. The rain stopped almost an hour ago, but Ehren has lost his sense of time. The air has been fairly quiet since the rain stopped with the exception of a few stray cracks of thunder. But something breaks that silence. The sound of a screen door slamming shut a few hundred yards a way. This sign of life makes Ehren realize what he’s going through. “This is fucking bullshit. Three hundred twenty-six.” He mutters. The air doesn’t stay silent. Footsteps are heard. The heat that fills up the summer Missouri air has helped to dry up some of the gravel-like dirt leading to the buildings of the base. The footsteps are first scuffling through the dirt, but then turn to squishing as they advance into the soggy part of the drive. They continue to come closer and closer, eventually stopping a few feet from the private. Moore doesn’t stop his push-ups; yet he continues to move his body up and down, counting with each repetition. “Three hundred thirty-four. Three hundred thirty-five.” “On your feet, Private.” The stern voice of the drill sergeant orders. He seems to be calmed. Calmed compared to their previous encounter. Ehren quickly places his right foot up near his right hand and pushes himself to a vertical base. He stands at attention as the sergeant approaches. The instructor comes within a couple of feet of Moore and looks at him with a small smirk. “Welcome to MY camp, son.” Moore doesn’t move. He just returns the eye contact given to him from his instructor. The sergeant glances down to the ground and spots the outline of Moore’s body, defined by the spotty dry area that his body had covered. This brings a small chuckle from the ARMY veteran as he looks back up to Moore, not attempting to hide it. “Do you know how long you have been doing push-ups, Private Moore?” The sergeant asks. Moore takes a deep breath, actually thinking about the question. He had no idea. For all he knew, he had missed his first meal in the ARMY. “I do not, Sir.” He answers. This time he doesn’t crack a joke or even a smile; he just answers. He knows he had better straighten up. If he didn’t he would be setting himself up for the longest nine weeks of his life. Ehren knows that as long as he follows orders he’ll be fine. He’s in great physical condition. This basic training will be a breeze. “Private, you have been doing push-ups for two and a half hours.” The sergeant states with an unenthused look on his face. He begins pacing around the frame of Ehren, keeping his eyes locked on the bigger man. Ehren remains in his “attention” stance, barely even moving as he breathes. “But have you been doing them the ENTIRE time?” The instructor asks as he reaches the right side of the private, pushing his face into view from the corner of Ehren’s eye. “Yes, Sir! I never stopped for more than a few seconds, Sir!” Moore replies, wanting to make sure his instructor gets the right idea about him. Instead, the instructor chuckles and steps back in front of Ehren. “Did you, Moore” Or are you lying?” The instructor inquires forcefully. Ehren wants to let out one of his “What the fuck do you mean” Lying?” come backs, but just stands there. He clears his throat as the sergeant steps back around to the rear of Moore. “I am telling the truth, Sir. I did as you asked, Sir.” Ehren responds. The instructor looks at the mud-covered hands hanging behind the recruits back. The mud is thick, and beginning to dry all the way through. He next looks at Ehren’s buttocks. Nothing but scattered wet spots from the rain water that had soaked his shorts and begun drying when the sun returned. The sergeant next stepped around to Ehren’s front side again. He looked down to the bare knees of the recruit. Mud. The mud began just a centimeter or two above the knee and continues down three quarters of the way to his ankle on both legs. “I believe you Moore.” The sergeant says looking back to Ehren’s face. “The mud on the front of your legs and the lack of on your ass tell me so.” Ehren finally cracks a smile. He’s happy that the sergeant knows this. His first impression may not have been great, but this second one is doing well so far. The sergeant sighs at the sight of Moore’s relaxed state. “At ease, Private.” The instructor says, allowing Ehren to relax his muscles and stand semi-normally. He takes a deep breathe as he locks eyes with the sergeant again. “Private, you have to know a few things about my way of doing things.” Ehren nods, as if to give the sergeant permission to continue speaking. “Number one; respect me and you will not have any problems getting out of Fort Leonard Wood. Two; follow my orders to your best ability and I will not give you any problems. Three; do not show me up in any challenge I lay down to you. If you do, I will make your stay here an experience close to that of spending nine weeks stranded in the doldrums of the Atlantic Ocean. Do you get me?” Moore begins to nod, but stops himself. “Not completely, Sir.” “Well, what is it?” The sergeant asks. “Sir. I am a competitive person, Sir. I have the ambition to succeed in whatever challenge is given to me, Sir.” Moore states. A confused look comes across the Drill Instructor’s face. “What are you saying, Private?” He asks. “Sir, I’m not sure if I could purposely fail when you challenge me.” “Well, Moore. I guess we’ll just have to find that out.” The sergeant says with a chuckle. “Grub is in forty-five minutes. Go get yourself a shower and enjoy the rest of your evening. I will personally be in to wake you up at oh five hundred tomorrow morning.” The sergeant replies. Moore’s look changes to concern, causing him to enter a spaced-out state. The sergeant stands and waits for Moore to walk away, but he remains. “Get going.” Moore shakes his head and comes out of his day-dream. “Yes, Sir.” He says as he goes back to attention. He brings his right hand up and places it at the top of his forehead, saluting his superior officer before walking away. An astonished look comes across the face of the instructor, who doesn’t have time to react before Moore rushes away. “How in the hell does he know how to properly salute” That’s tomorrow’s in class training.” Is all the sergeant can mutter as he slowly begins walking back to the center of the base.
