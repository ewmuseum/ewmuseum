Friendship

4 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 4th, 2005 Friendship, a word known by many, and a word that leaves the prints of one on the heart of another. The word held with the highest regard to many, especially if they consider one a true friend. Friendship holds true under all tests of time, will, and testament. Friendship can be corrupt by so many things, these things obscure the vision that one holds for a friend, and they lead a “friend” to a more spiteful, evil side. The night was cold, a dreary night, the sky filled with only stars, not a cloud in sight. I sat on the patio, swinging on the porch and gazing off into the distance. Awakened from the trance I was in was a large dark figure with smoke coming from his face. “Aiden?” the mysterious man called out to me, I recognized the voice but couldn’t match it with the character in which it was. As he grew closer to me the porch light shone over his face, I stared at him in shock. A man, who I would have never thought to have seen after the things he had done to me. A friend, whose betrayal to me was with the worst intentions possible, this man tried to rob me of the life I lived. “Jakob’Shane?” I said softly as he threw the cigar to his side and blew out smoke one last time. “Coming to finish the job?” I jeered at him as he walked up to the porch and sat next to me on the bench closest to the large door of the house. “Aiden, I need your help. I know that you cut me off a long time ago but Aiden this truly is a life or death matter.” He said to me with a sense of worry in his voice. I sat there speechless for a moment, staring into his dark green eyes. He wore a face of regret and I knew he was seeking forgiveness. I thought of the man above, weeping for this man’s troubles, Jesus at this moment was seeking that I would give him the forgiveness that he gave those who denied and killed him. I looked him directly in the eye and spoke. “Ja...Jakob, you tried to kill me.” I said this shakily as he stared back to me, a tear now rolling down his cheek. You as a reader must be slightly confused at this moment, so many times before we have gone into the past to explain something that has happened, some key event that changed my life forever. As you may have guessed by now this is another one of those key events, a story of trust, brotherly love, and the ultimate betrayal. [Flashback] Location: Jackson Residence Time: 2:37 A.M. The phone rang as I sat in the study reading a book, startled by the phone I jumped. The phone ringing wasn’t exactly the first thing I was expecting, especially at 2:37 A.M. I knew this by glancing down at my watch. The time had passed quickly in the study every time I went there, a place where I got away from the troubles of wrestling, the complications that come with the business. I picked up the phone softly and cleared my voice to talk. “Hello?” “Aiden” I need your help. I need X’s help please help me my brother.” The man spoke into the phone with worry, instantly I knew that something terrible had happened. I knew that something in this man’s voice didn’t seem right, the man sounded as though his voice was filled with tears and an uneasy feeling. “Shane, Is that you” Are you okay?” I said to him quickly. “It’s me. Aiden it’s...it’s Jenna. She’s dead.” As he spoke these words, he instantly broke down into a loud sob. “Shane, stay where you are at I’m coming to get you.” I said to him with worry now in my voice. Jenna was the fianc’e of Shane Carbon, a man who I hold dearly to this day although his intentions in the past were not properly rectified. Jenna was stole from the grasps of life after her and Shane got in an argument, that same night she was killed in a tragic car accident. Shane became overwhelmed with the constant stress of Detroit, living in the same apartment that he had lived in with Jenna, not being able to experience the love that he had everyday. The only time he felt closest to home was when he wept over the grave of Jenna. Shane Carbon was also member of a secret society known as X, something formed by ten men of the city of St.Louis to keep the cities murder rate low. X was somewhat of a mob, an organization that controlled and saw to it that men looking to commit crimes were punished after the crime. Onward though, Shane Carbon was important to me because he taught me my ways around the wrestling ring and the octagon, Shane began training with me in fight clubs and we eventually moved to the wrestling ring. X is an underground organization created by ten men, hence “X”, for the sole purpose as to bring the men involved in several murder cases to justice. The members include the 4 Jackson brothers, Shane Carbon, X, the mayor of St. Louis, 2 police officers by the names of George Gaynes and Jason Meyers, and lastly the man known as Cassian Jaymes. The story of X and its members will unfold soon enough, the story of X resembles in its own light a story of sin, redemption, and vengeance. Something that you’ve probably gotten used to by now from my stories is that memorable quote this story is a different one for a different day. Shane had to get away from the burdens that he felt with the apartment that he lived in. Shane tried to murder me; he called me to his house and tried to fake his suicide with my body. Shane changed his name to Jakob and got as far away as he possibly could from Detroit, Michigan. The ultimate betrayal, he betrayed Aiden Jackson, and he betrayed the organization X. Shane Carbon could not complete the job of killing me because obviously I am still standing here today. [Flashback End] “You tried to kill me, Shane why did you do it?” I said to him sternly. “Desperate times Aiden, I needed money and there were men who put a lot of cash on your body. They paid me but I have troubles with them now, they saw that you were released from the mental institution.” He said to me. I grabbed Shane by the throat in anger and began screaming in his face. The look in his eyes grew to a fearful look. He sat there motionless with my hands around his throat. I eased up realizing that it wasn’t worth it. “Why would they want me dead” Shane tell me that.” I said to him quietly. “Their names were in the book, the Mason Diary. They still haven’t found it but in exchange for your services I can find the Mason Diary for you. Please Aiden, I know that the trust isn’t here like it used to be, the brotherhood. I’ll admit my fault; I tried to kill you Aiden that was the ultimate betrayal as you call it. Forgive me please. I stared for a moment, looking at him. The look on his face showed that he was fearful of these men, the look on his face showed that he truly was seeking my forgiveness. My mind raced through the bible searching for the proper portion of the scriptures. Jesus bent down and wrote with His finger on the ground. And as they continued to ask Him, He stood up and said to them, 'Let him who is without sin among you be the first to throw a stone at her.' And once more He bent down and wrote with His finger on the ground. But when they heard it, they went away, one by one, beginning with the eldest, and Jesus was left alone with the woman standing before Him.' (John 8:6-9 RSV) I must be the man to offer forgiveness, although I murdered for a reason that I felt right. God truly is the one to handle the fate of others; I acted in an irresponsible way acting as though I was the God of this world. I nearly damned myself to hell had the bible not guided me through jail and helped me to correct my sins and seek Jesus once again. “I need no offer of the book. All I need is your word written in stone as the truth. Although I don’t believe X will welcome you back so easily. I give you my word that I will do everything in my power to protect you.” I said softly looking him directly in the eyes. “Aiden, I’ll find the book.” He said with determination With those words, I knew that this man was truly accepting of my forgiveness, that he had realized his fault. He had realized his mistake and was willing to correct it; Shane Carbon was an unblemished man on this day. After these words were spoken, we parted ways. I continued to sit on the porch staring into the darkness of the night. Shane got into his car and drove away. Nearly 2 hours later, I walked inside away from the darkness; I stared at the GCW Cruiserweight title that was slung across the living room couch. I moved it aside sitting it on the table and fell asleep. [Fade] I awoke to the images of the news, horrific images at that. A car smashed into pieces. Come to find, Shane Carbon was killed in a horrific car accident. I sat there for a moment, the shock factor just as it had been so long ago when my parents died. I was glad that Jesus had sent me that message last night, the message to forgive him. I sat there wondering what I would have felt like if I hadn’t taken the way of Christ last night. I knew deep down inside that Shane Carbon was in the place above with his dear love Jenna. His grave would be dug directly next to hers, and I would stand above his grave weeping for him, weeping for Jenna but a smile would come over my face knowing two people who loved each other most would be together. The man, who taught me it all, passed on his life at the mere young age of 23. He accomplished more than many could imagine at his young tender age. Shane Carbon, will be remembered in my heart, Shane Carbon will be the spirit that rides with me every time my music hits. His will, courage, and strength will live within me day to day, every time that I enter that ring. Everything happens for a reason? “Remember that Aiden, don’t be mad. He did it for a reason; God does everything for a reason.” I said to myself softly as the tears were streaming down my face. A brief candle; both ends burning An endless mile; a bus wheel turning A friend to share the lonesome times A handshake and a sip of wine So say it loud and let it ring We are all a part of everything The future, present and the past Fly on proud bird You're free at last. “You’re free at last, I love you Shane Carbon. You’re enigmatic character; let it rest in peace my brother.” I said to myself. I think the lesson to be learned here is not actually for me myself but it is for a member of the organization “X”, coincidentally he goes by the name X. Life comes at a cost, and life can be taken on the drop of a dime. Useless mistakes happen brother, you must put them in the past and realize that you shouldn’t live dwelling on the faults of others but you must prosper beside your living breathing brothers because you have no clue how much longer they will be here. Your time on this earth is in no way guaranteed. [Fade Out] In Loving Memory, of the character that I learned to love, the character that I developed to become what he was. The character that brought me into the business and brought me great success. R.I.P. Shane “The Assumed One” Carbon/Jakob “The Enigmatic One” Bach 1982-2005
