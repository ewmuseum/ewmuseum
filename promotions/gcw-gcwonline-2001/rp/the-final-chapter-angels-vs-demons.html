The Final Chapter: Angels vs. Demons

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Shockwave Sometimes, it is best to burn the past to let the future rise from the ashes. The sun begins to fall behind the tall buildings of downtown Newark, New Jersey. The last rays of hope for the day scorch the sky in reds and oranges. The hum of traffic on the streets adds to the ambiance of the old gym before Taylor. The yellow cab behind him, door still ajar, awaits payment for the trip from Clifton. The broken Pakistani-English flows over statuesque Zacharia without much influence. Zacharia's eyes focus on the large "For Rent" sign plastered across the front of the run down building before him. A few of the words flying in his direction, taunting his ears. Zacharia throws a hand towards the driver and raises a finger to silence him. "Please, sir, give me a moment..." "I got ta be 'round runnin' my clock..." "Then leave, I will find another cab." The cab driver gets out of his cab. He adjusts his turban as he approaches the teenage boy in front of him, trying to be as pompous as possible. Zacharia reaches into his pocket and pulls out a twenty before the driver can emit anything from his vocal cords. "Keep the change." Zacharia has yet to turn around to take his eyes away from the building. The cab driver shakes his head and grabs the twenty out of Taylor's taunt hand. He mumbles "kids" and sits back down in his cab and starts the engine. Zacharia doesn't notice that the cab has pulled away, nor does he care any. He approaches the dirty, boarded up door and reaches for the handle to open it up. The door creeks to life and allows access to a vacant open room with a desk in front of the door where computers and sign-in pads used to occupy. Across the base of the desk, wooden letters are broken and pulled off from vandalism. Other signs of vandalism are visible around the open space, mirrors broken and windows shattered, but there is no sign of anyone remaining inside. Zacharia drops his bag to the floor, pushing the crumbs of the desk and walls aside with an invisible force, and he walks to the left towards a graffitied door. The door opens up into a tall room, also feeling the effects of the outside room, falling into itself. All that remains is the broken plaster from the ceiling laying in the center of the room, and a speedbag stand leaning from the far side wall. Zacharia freezes at the sight of the decaying room. He looks to his left to see a bulletin board falling from the wall. He gently glides his fingers over the ends of it, but the force is invigorating to the board, causing it to come alive, and fall onto a pile of garbage. Zacharia shakes off his hood and takes a few steps farther, towards the door in front of him. Faded, ghostly images of letters remain above the door, shadows of their former selves, untouched by the sun's rays. The letters spell out "Bagwell". Zacharia walks closer to the weak door, also battered and destroyed by the artwork of street artists. He grabs for the handle that looks to be inching away from him, but through pursuit, he grabs it firmly and opens the door. Bright, fluorescent lights sting the eyes of the intruder as he steps into the room. This room, unlike all the others, remains untouched, just as he remembers it; Chris Bagwell's private gym. Several promotional posters of the prince of trash talk look out into the small gym, evenly spaced out. Most of the posters were promotional from Ultimate Championship Wrestling Pay Per Views. His face never leaves the solum expression until he pans the room further and sees a grotesque blood trail leading up to the ring against the far wall, with someone laying inside. Zacharia runs to the ring and notices the bloody heap of a human being laying in the center of the ring. He uses a loose end of a robe around the person to wipe away the blood covering its face, and to his surprise, it is not Chris Bagwell, but Father Emanuel. The shock causes him to fall back onto his back from his crouching position. The bloody heap sits up with ease, still bleeding from a gash across the head. The beaten priest raises his broken, twisted arm towards Taylor and beings to speak. "My son, I thought you would never make it. It is very important that you listen to what I have to say." Zacharia, still in shock, begins to tremble. His lower lip looks to be having a seizure as he forces out the inquisical like statement of, "You're dead" which bounces away from Father Emanuel, unnoticed. "My son, you must reason with yourself. You are not a bad man." Zacharia struggles with the words and shakes his head. He looks to his right to see a mirror facing towards him. He comes to his senses and touches his face, staring into the mirror. The look of shock comes over him as he forces his fingers over his face. He opens his sweatshirt and presses his finger across his chest, where his scar no longer remains. "listen to me, you must...I want you to look at yourself. Look at what you used to imagine yourself as. You are pure..." "How am I..." "No questions, just listen, my son. You are Zacharia Taylor, not who you think you are. LOOK AT ME!" Zacharia's head pivots to Father Emanuel, eyes narrowed in thought. "I am Zacharia Taylor...I know, Father, who else would I be?" Zacharia begins to look back towards the mirror. "ZACHARIA!" Father Emanuel's voice shakes the room around them. Two posters fall from the walls in the distance and Zacharia looks back at him, trying to maintain eye contact. "i know the temptation is there, but you must try to stay with me. You need to pull yourself through the bad." "What are you talking about, Father? I know what you are trying to preach, but you left me with such a...." "I am not preaching, Zacharia. I am pleading. You need to listen to me, I cannot force you to do something that you do not wish to do. You must remember what I taught you. Everyone deserves a chance, everyone deserves a chance out, a chance for change." At the word change, Zacharia begins to turn his head towards the mirror, but his eyes remain on Father Emanuel. The priest crawls towards the kneeling Zacharia Taylor and reaches up towards his shoulder. "My son, fight it. You have the power. You are not like HIM." As Father Emanuel reaches his shoulder, Zacharia begins to cry out in pain. He falls forward and makes fists and punches the ground, remaining in a pushup position, yelling out. "Fight it! You are not like HIM." Zacharia looks up as tears begin to hit the canvas below him. Blood dribbles out of his nostrils and meshes with the salty puddles nearby. His chest becomes bright red and his scar bubbles through the smooth skin on his chest. Blood begins to profusely emit from his body in slashes all over his body. The bleeding subsides in one area, clotted with scar tissue, but then another cut appears. He struggles to keep his eyes forward with the pleading of Father Emanuel, but his body starts to twist towards the mirrors. "These are the pains from your past, my son. Fight them. Pain is weakness so control it. you have the strength to resist." "Father...ugh...please..." Zacharia's eyes close but his body faces the mirror, a lot of the blood clotting, but his body is still rigid and tense. "You need to resist him. You are Zacharia Taylor. You are not..." Zacharia's grimace turns into a wry smirk. His body slightly releases and his eyes open. "Christian, right, Father?" Zacharia stares at himself for a few moments, and then turns to Father Emanuel who suddenly jumps at him. The priest is in a rage of anger. He claws and tries to choke Taylor. Taylor easily fights back. Emanuel cries out in pain and sorrow. Taylor pulls his arm back for a punch, throwing it towards Father Emanuel. Father Emanuel lets out a resonating, bloodcurdling scream which echoes through Taylor's head. Taylor suddenly jumps up from a laying position, sweat beading across his forehead. He looks around the room and probes his body for open wounds and blood, but comes up with a sigh of relief. He swings his feet over the side of the bed and looks around before he steps down, cautiously. The twilight of the hour is complimented with the moonlight hitting off of each eastern wall, casting grey shadows across the floor and opposing wall. He stumbles across the halls, dancing with the shadows in every step until he makes it into the bathroom. He flips the light and stares at himself in the mirror. Methodically, he turns on both faucets. He blinks mysteriously at himself for several seconds, the steam from the rushing water below him starting to alter the image in the mirror. He dumps his face down and splashes the water over his worn face. For good measure, he tosses another trapped amount over his face. Zacharia fumbles for the towel hanging up on the wall and wipes away the lingering water. He pats himself dry and then looks once more into the mirror. His eyes strain, nearly bloodshot, at himself. His body is still there, but his face has taken on that of Christian's. Taylor's expression and mannerisms are still there, but Christian clearly shines through. He struggles to wipe his face once more, but Christian still remains. He slowly stands up straight, but is afraid to look away. The intrigue keeps him there, but the fear also prevents him from looking behind him. His hand touches his face, afraid of the texture, but it is that of his own. He quickly turns away and then looks back at himself, but still, nothing changes. "What's the matter, Taylor... Don't like what you see?" The walls seem to be closing in on him in deeply casted shadows. The steam seems to be spreading around the mirror instead of conquering it in a natural way. "It's inevitable, you know. You already accepted it. It's just a matter of time." Zacharia turns off the faucets and quickly escapes the darkening room and runs through the door that leads to the alter. Upon making it through the door, he closes it quickly and rests against it, breathing heavy. "I am Zacharia Taylor, not...." "How can you hurt my feelings like that, Taylor?" Taylor opens his eyes to see Christian sitting on the alter, wearing the same hoodie that Taylor always wears. He pulls the hoodie over his face, concealing it from the light. "You forget, I am you, and you are me. We've been though this too many times." Christian's hands slide inside the hood and using his thumbs, he slides the hood off his head once more, revealing a wry smile. The hood falls behind behind him, revealing a doppleganger of Zacharia Taylor on the alter. The voice and trademark expression remain unaltered. "Christian, I just....I don't know if i can handle this anymore. You don't exist. You are only in my head, and you know that... You are trying to play off of that..." "Zacharia..." Christian slides off of the alter, no longer looking like Zacharia. "That hurts! How can you say that I don't exist?" Christian slowly glides across the cement-like carpet and quickly contorts his face in rage and jumps towards Zacharia. Zacharia remains still against the door as Christian lunges. Christian grabs Zacharia's head and throws him off the door. He then throws Zacharia down the four steps in front of the alter. Zacharia, still with his eyes closed tight, mumbles under his breath on the ground, still statuesque. "How can you still think I don't exist while you lay there on the ground after standing against the door with all your free will? Let me let you in on a little secret..." Christian leans into Zacharia, nearly inches from his face. "I DO EXIST!" Zacharia's eyes open as Christian's fist flies towards Zacharia's face. The impact knocks him out cold. Christian paces Taylor for a few seconds and then grabs his arm and begins to drag him towards the front of the church. Once next to the outside doors, he lifts him up and opens the door, pushing Taylor outside of it. Several hours go by without Taylor stirring. His eyes rapidly move behind closed eyelids, his body growing tense at moments, but he still doesn't stir. Sweat starts to fill in the weather torn crevices of his face. Finally, he jumps up in the same manner he did in his bad several hours earlier. When his eyes open, the first thing he sees is Christian. "Rise and shine sleeping beauty! We have work to do..." Zacharia wipes the sweat from his forehead and looks around, confused as to where he is. He goes to stand, but slips on the church steps. After adjusting his body, he sits up straight and begins to stretch out his legs. "There have been several rapes in the park over the last month, and I want to catch this man. This is the only opening to the park that isn't on a populated street, he will come through here. Victims have been runners." Zacharia, now taking interest to what Christian is saying, starts to crack his neck and hands. "Lord knows what else he has done, so I think it is a great opportunity to finish him before he can harm anyone else." Zacharia nods faithfully and begins to stiffen up, ready to fight. All previous events have left his mind, not focusing anymore on Christian than he needs to at the moment... ...Blind Faith... A door slams down the road as the sun begins to come up. Christian stiffens up as he looks down the street and gives his wry smile, reaching for the attention of Zacharia. The two men take notice to a man in a sweatshirt and sweatpants jogging just a few steps behind a younger woman in her twenties. "Look, Christian, he is wearing gloves. I think this..." Before he can say anything more, the man grabs a hold of the unsuspecting woman and drags her into the park while picking up a bag next to the garbage can. "Christian, let's go!" Christian remains standing at the church as Zacharia sprints for the park. He looks both ways to see nothing more than a couple cars parked on the side of the street, but nobody else is in sight. he turns behind him and sees Christian casually walking behind him. Zacharia turns into the park and slows his footsteps in the grass to remain undetected. The crunching of glass is heard underneath Zacharia's foot. He looks down to see a syringe with a strong smelling liquid expelled around it. He takes little more notice and makes his way around the bushes to his left near a park bench. Zacharia stops after hearing something, and chills go down his well accustomed back. Thwap, thwap, thwap.... The sound of what seems to be someone jumping in thick mud comes from a short distance behind a tree. Zacharia slowly steps forward, afraid of what he is going to see. A flashback of walking this way six years ago when he killed his first man come to mind. He tries to push it out of his mind, but he needs to struggle no longer, because something more terrible has replaced it. The man that was jogging behind the girl on the street is now on top of the girl with a knife being plunged over and over and over into her throat and upper chest. Zacharia cringes and freezes, without the guy even seeing him, but another set of eyes sees him. Her eyes plead with him to end it, but they are faintly rolling out of existence as her blood plasters the grass around her like molten lava. A last tear rolls down her cheek before she loses the strength to resist. The man stops and pulls the knife away from her weakening body. Using the same knife, he proceeds to cut off her top. Zacharia is powerless to do anything. So many images and so many voices flutter around his mind. "You need to be Zacharia Taylor, that is who you are...." "See how you picture yourself? Pure...." "You are an angel unleashed by God himself to kill..." "You can change your life, be different...." "I don't want to kill anymore...." "Are you going to kill him? I know you want to Zacharia. Oh, I can see your blood boiling from here. This monster deserves everything you are thinking. He deserves to be at the wrath of God's Mercenary..." Zacharia's fists clench and Christian steps back into the shadows of the tree. Zacharia looks down to see the man's bag next to him, with a large knife inside. Without breaking eye contact with the weakening girl's eyes, he picks up the knife and steps slowly closer to the man, now cutting through the girl's sports bra. He gently pushes the knife against the man's bare back. "Slowly toss the knife to your side, and God forgive me if you do anything rash." The man's back tenses up against the knife, but he complies and slowly tosses the knife to his side. He starts to plead and reason, but his words make no connection with Taylor's ears. He lets gravity push the knife against the man's back, and he drags it up to his neck, leaving a clean line of streaming blood fall to the ground. The man's tears start to run and his mouth continues. Zacharia opens his mouth: "Stand up and face me." Trembling, the man stands up and slowly turns around. The man's face becomes suddenly familiar. Zacharia stands there, taking him in without a word, and then the man's voice hits him. "Father Taylor, please..." Zacharia's grasp on the knife stiffens. "How do you know who I am?" "I go to your church, Father, I'm sorry." Zacharia takes in a deep breath, his heart begins to race. "I just couldn't take it anymore! Your words moved me. You told me to follow my passion, the inner voice inside of me. I felt God running through me..." Zacharia Slashes across the man's chest, leaving a small line of blood in its wake. "Don't use His name, telling me that His words were running through you. You killed an innocent girl! You were going to rape her! And you have the audacity to tell me that this is God's WILL?" The man cowers and turns tail to run, but Zacharia's fury makes him faster and more calm than what the man can muster. The blade spins around in his hand and twirls through the air like the hand of a fast spinning clock, landing in the man's shoulder blade. He slowly walks closer to the man as he whimpers and cries, crawling away. "Tell me your name." The man lets out several cries of pain, but no words. Zacharia puts his left foot next to the knife in the man's back and reaches down into his pocket for his wallet. The license is an older picture of the man, the Taylor drops the card to his feet in anger; Christian Taylor. "Taylor, you need to finish him, but do it...slowly. He must suffer. He shares our names, our faith, and has tainted them both. He needs to be set free though pain and suffering, like his victims..." "Christian," Zacharia's voice is calm but laced with acid, "I no longer want to hear a word out of your mouth, is that clear?" Christian smiles and rests against the tree. He looks down at the girl, eyes still open against the faint morning, and shakes his head with a sadistic grin. He whispers something to her in a sarcastic voice: "Your boyfriend just crossed the wrong person. Don't worry honey, he will get his, hang in there." Zacharia goes without hearing the words, and continues about his ritual. "Get on your knees Christian." There is extra emphasis on the name. Christian's smile grows wider behind his back. "I want you to face away from me, on your knees and repeat after me." The man slowly rises, but Taylor kicks him to the ground and rips the knife out of his shoulder. Zacharia pulls the man to his knees by his hair. "Raise your arms at your sides." The man complies, but the left one, where the knife was, only goes up six inches. Christian laughs behind him, anxious like before a movie starts. "Again, repeat after me..." "Our Father, who art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come, Thy will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Say it!" "Our Father...who art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name... [sobs] Thy kingdom come, Thy will be done...[screams]. Zacharia slams down the knife on the man's left hand as he says the word "done". Blood sprays follow the falling hand. The man falls forward and holds his stub of a hand to his chest. Zacharia leans forward and grabs his hair again, and uses the warm knife to nudge the other arm out. "You say you are a religious man, Christian Taylor. Make me believe it!" "forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us. And lead us not into temptation..[screams]" Zacharia cuts off his other hand, but this time closer to the elbow. Blood profusely pushes out of the open wounds. The man now is twisted on the ground facing Zacharia, whom is now bloodthirsty. Christian walks up behind him, smoothly talking into his ear. "Son, I know what you are thinking, but you must be the one to take his life, not the Devil. Do not let him bleed out at the Devil's will, take him with your own." Christian falls back against the tree, watching his words circle Zacharia's brain, causing him to tremble with inconceivable thought. Zacharia turns around, but not to look at the grinning Christian, but to see a trembling hand, covered in blood, reaching out towards him. He drops the knife and slowly walks towards the silently pleading girl. "She's suffering, Christian. She's still alive..." Zacharia falls to his knees and pushes the sweat soaked hair out of her eyes. She is beautiful, and finds comfort in his hands. "What should I do?" Zacharia's eyes grow weak and push out a few tears onto his cheek. He attempts to wipe away some of the blood from around her mouth. "Zacharia, you don't need to ask, you know what you should do. You need to do what needs to be done." Zacharia closes his eyes and shakes his head. "I can't. I can't kill someone who is innocent." "Fine, then let her suffer. Let the Devil get his way, once more tonight." The words dance around his shoulders and then go right to his heart. It goes against everything he has ever learned, but it isn't right to let the innocent suffer any longer than they need to. The girl's eyes start to grow faint and distant as Zacharia puts his hand over her mouth and nose. The girl faintly resists, but Taylor's tears continue to fall. Blood bubbles out of her neck for a couple seconds until all life expels. He gently removes his hands and places her head down. Zacharia proceeds to say a pray under his breath and crosses her arms. He kisses her forehead and stands up and looks into Christian's eyes which are a lot stronger than his, almost in an entertained way. The side of Christian's mouth twitches and he nods over at the man attempting to crawl away a few feet away. Zacharia walks over to him, picks up the knife and straddles the man. "There is no place in God's kindgom for you." He takes in a deep breath and closes his eyes. "In nomine Patri, et fili, spiritus Sancti." Zacharia drops the edge of the knife onto the back of the man's neck. A devastating crack echoes from the man's neck. He rips the knife out and uses his hoodie to wipe off the fingerprints. He walks past Christian without looking at him and heads for the church. *** "Stop pacing back and forth!" Zacharia is sitting on the alter steps while Christian is pacing in front of him like a predator. His hands are still trembling and covered in blood. He is breaking out in sweats while his counterpart is looking like a little kid on his Birthday. "You need to lighten up!" "Lighten up? I just killed someone that didn't deserve to die! By your direction no less!" "Whatever, Zach, she was dying anyway!" Taylor stands up and gets into Christian's face. "You told me to kill someone that was dying. There was no reason for me to interfere! It's bad enough that I didn't get there quicker to save her! Oh, mature, Father! I can't do this anymore. I can't!" Christian stops moving his mouth in a mocking fashion and stares at him more serious. "What are you talking about? This is all you know!" Zacharia leaves the room and grabs his bag in the priest's room. He starts to throw clothes into the bag. "Where are you going?" "I don't care, anywhere but here! I need to get away from here!" "If you are going somewhere, you might need to grab that money!" Zacharia stops and then throws the money into his bag. "So, where are we going?" "We are not going anywhere. I need to get away from you." "Funny, because I thought that we were one in the same? If you're going, I'm going!" Zacharia throws a stack of passports into his bag. He turns past Christian and walks out the door. "I kinda like it here, why do we have to leave?" Christian starts to laugh but it goes unnoticed by Taylor. "Hotels? Motels? You really think that is going to be a relief of evil, do you?" Zacharia heads for the door. Christian chases after him and cuts him off at the doors. "You can't just ignore me, I will always be here. As long as you stand by your faith, hell, as long as the faith EXISTS I will be here!" Zacharia tries to walk though Christian, but he is grabbed by the throat and thrown into a pew, breaking the piece that sticks out into the isle. Zacharia, looking shocked stands up and grabs his back. Christian, no longer smiling, comes for him. Zacharia stands there, hands at his side. "You can play this game all you want Taylor, but you are going to have to kill me to get rid of, oh wait...I AM YOU! What the hell are you going to do?" Zacharia side steps him and goes for his bag, but is then thrown from behind into the baptism bowl, knocking it over, sending holy water all over the entranceway of the church. Zacharia, frustrated, gets up and tries to throw a couple punches at Christian, but all fail to land on their intended target. "As long as Father Emanuel and this church survive, so will I! You created me in this church, and I will always be here!" Christian punches Taylor, sending him into the wall. Zacharia evades the incoming Christian and runs for the alter. "You have it in you to kill a young girl, but you can't even stand to fight a man worth something in this world..." Zacharia turns around and tries to hit Christian again, but fails. Zacharia is tripped up and falls to the ground. He tries to stand up, but Christian kicks him down again. He is now pinned down with a foot across his throat. "I will not fall to your level, Christian. You exist if I think that you do. If I burn every memory of you, you will never again be able to alter my judgement. I shall not become you, Christian Thomas!" Zacharia throws the priest off of him and lands a devastating elbow on his nose, causing him to stumble to the steps of the alter. He goes to jump up, but Zacharia is there, smashing a candle holder over his head. Christian's eyes go wild and he closes his eyes. Zacharia hits him once more to be sure and then looks around him for something. He frantically runs around the alter and then suddenly looks up and lets out a small smile of triumph. He takes the bloody candleholder and starts to hit at a crank next to the alter on the wall which leads a wire to the large cross hanging against the wall. After the third hit, the crank lets go of the wall and the cross comes plummeting down, crushing the chairs and benches beneath it. The cross falls forward, narrowly missing Christian, but destroys the alter like it were made out of toothpicks. Zacharia picks up Christian and places him on the back of the cross. Using the wire that hung the cross, he wraps it around each arm, tying it to the cross, mirrored with Jesus Christ on the cross behind him. Christian comes to as his feet are being wound to the cross. His look is not of bewilderment or disgust, but of sheer joy. He roots on Zacharia as he frantically runs from side to side with the wire. "Look at you, taking your life into your own hands for once! Father Emanuel would be so proud!" "Father Emanuel would be disgusted that I allowed myself to become you!" "What, do you think tying me here isn't going to raise suspicion? I mean, you destroyed half the church already, what more do you think you need to do?" Zacharia runs over to a leaning bookshelf next to the crushed alter, full of copies of the Modern Testament. He knocks them over and throws a copy towards Christian. "That's right, but don't forget, the master copy is around here somewhere. The original needs to burn for me to go up in smoke." Christian starts to laugh uncontrollably, fighting against the wire around his extremities. Blood begins to pour out of the lines of wire around him. His eyes begin to tear, flowing evenly with the bloody around him. "Don't worry, Father. The book was on the alter, which is now under you. How fitting." Zacharia takes one of the books and the candle laying on the ground next to him. "Need a light?" The laughter fills the church. "Christian, you will never be able to plague my mind again. If you truly do exist, somewhere, may God have mercy on you!" Zacharia takes the lighter out of his pocket and lights the candle. He puts the flame to the book. The thin pages, much like tissue paper go up in flames quickly. "Any last words, Christian?" "Yeah, let God have Mercy on your soul, Zacharia!" Zacharia drops the book under Christian, the cross nearly instantly going up in flames due to the lacker coating. Christian remains smiling, no longer saying a word. Zacharia closes his eyes and looks down. "Please, God, forgive me for what I have done. Deliver me from evil tonight, for I have made the most grave sin. I have killed the innocent, and burned Your Kingdom. Lord, please, if you can find it in your heart, forgive your child..." The fire reaches Zacharia's feet. He starts to cry, watching the flames engulf Christian and everything around them. His hoodie catches fire as he stands up and slowly walks away. On his way into the isle, he takes off his hoodie and throws it into a pew. His stomach remains red from the heat of the fire as he leaves. He picks up the bag near the front of the church and walks outside. Before taking a step out of the church, he uses the bowl of holy water to wash his hands. He tosses some water into his face and walks out of the church. Upon reaching the sidewalk, he turns around and drops the bag. His tears seam to evaporate and everything around him starts to spin. The back of the church starts to engulf in flames, pressing against the pink sky of the early morning. Sunrise shows rebirth, but is it really a start of something new, or the end of something old? Zacharia holds his ground and starts to flex his hands. His body becomes tense and he pushes his chest out to resist, but is heart rate grows at an exponential rate. His hands fight between tense and relaxed, and he struggles to remain still. His body finally gives in. The side of his mouth twitches and a wry smile comes across it. He marvels at the flames. He picks up his bag that lays at his feet and walks off down the sidewalk without any direction.
