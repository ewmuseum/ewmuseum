Surprise, Surprise

9 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9 May 05 “Sometimes things happen in your life that make absolutely no sense. You aren’t quite sure how to react, and instead of reacting your body simply freezes up at first. Your mind goes blank, searching for the right emotion to bring up, like a search engine on the internet. Your breathing becomes forced almost like you have to keep reminding your body to continue to do it instead of it just automatically happening. Good or bad these things happen and they give no warning or no signals, they simply smack you upside your head like a blow from a sledgehammer. After everything that has happened to me in my life you’d think that these sort of events have become second nature to me. That proverbial hammer has come down on top of me so many times in my life that death sometimes seems like the only logical step. I continue to drive on though, sometimes not understanding how or why I am still left standing, but I simply do. I think that when you make it through these events it makes you stronger, physically and mentally, even if you don’t see it right away. When Cindy and Starr were buried I thought about suicide more than a little bit. I invited death at every turn and wished for someone to take my invitation. When my Uncle banished me from his home after their death I thought I’d be killed on the streets alone. I had never felt so empty than I had at that time, yet I remained standing. When Damen was murdered right before my very eyes, I thought surely that my sins had finally caught up to me and I would be next ? but it was not meant to be. When Alex attacked me right after I proposed to Wylie and then touched her, and took her ring I thought that I would lose my sanity and simply kill the man, but something inside of me didn’t allow it. When I saw Alex and Wylie together on that rainy night ? holding each other, KISSING each other, a little voice inside my head told me to walk into that bar and slice both of their throats and laugh as their blood trickled down into my hands ? but it didn’t happen. When Johnny Lexicon and his Anarchy tried crippling me by destroying my ankle, I thought about what I’d do as revenge the entire time. I thought I’d end their careers one by one ... but yet I didn’t allow myself to go through with it. When I saw that my “best friend” RaZiel had been fucking my ex-fiance? and didn’t have the balls to tell me, when I saw them kissing, it was like standing in the rain in front of that bar all over again. I didn’t see RaZiel and Wylie being a happy couple in my eyes, I saw Alex and Wylie, and blood was all I saw. Other events weren’t as life altering but very confusing points in my life that I wasn’t sure how to handle. When I first began falling for Wylie I was so unsure of myself because I kept thinking about Cindy. It wasn’t a fair comparison as how could she replace the ghost of my dead wife and child ? Then came my first run in GCW where I was dicked around for the entire time yet remained true to Caldera like a fucking lap dog. Then there was my DWA World Title win which was the highlight of my professional career, defeating that sick fuck, Alex Grey and almost crippling his sorry ass. Then there was Mary ? How sweet it is. Although it wasn’t always so. My feelings for her scared me away in the beginning, and I fled the United States for awhile to help my Aunt because of my Uncle’s death. I didn’t tell anyone but RaZ, even though Mary and I had become quite close. Then when I came back to the states I still didn’t bother getting in touch with her, figuring I’d never see her again, even moving on to a senorita named Medea. Of course right as I started becoming happy and things were looking up ? I ran into Mary again, throwing a monkey wrench into my entire life. As you now know though, that turned out to be the best decision of my life, choosing Mary.” I didn’t really notice that I had spoken for so long and it kind of startled me. I opened my eyes and looked over at the Doc who just sat in his chair with a stoic look on his face. He never let out too many ranges of emotions, but I guess that was his job. He was good at what he does I have to give him that though, because no one gets me to talk the way he does, but something just makes it seem right when I am in these sessions. “Tell me Roberto does your time in GCW now confuse you?” “No, not really Doc.” “Well it’s either it does or it doesn’t Roberto.” “In a way it does because even through all of the success I have had I still am just waiting for Caldera to screw me over, send me back down the ranks never to even get a sniff of the big time again. My time here has seemed to be too good to be true, like I am simply waiting for that crash landing back into reality.” “Are you enjoying yourself?” “Yes I actually am, which is why I continue to do what I am doing.” “Very well, we will save the GCW for next session ? let’s talk about the visitor you had come to your hotel room Wednesday night after the show.” My body tensed up on me involuntarily. I didn’t mean for it to do that, but every time I think about what happened it just sends a chill down my spine. I indeed had someone knock on my hotel room suite the night of the show, and it was someone I hadn’t seen since I was eight teen years old. “It was late and I was just stretching out so that my muscles could relax after everything that had happened, plus I had a headache like you wouldn’t believe after that sorry son of a bitch Alex hit me with that chair. The knock was so unexpected that I didn’t even raise my gaze to the door, because I figured it was the room over’s door. When the knock came again it finally came to me that it was indeed my own door. I walked to the door almost annoyed, expecting some room service mix up or some idiotic fan wanting an autograph. I opened the door and just sighed at first. I didn’t even really look at who it was at first and simple told her that I didn’t want any towels, and tried closing the door.” “Take me back to that moment Roberto ? close your eyes, lay your head back and just take me back there ?” I did as he said and let the scene in my head come into focus. I could still see everything in the hotel room very clearly, from the bed to the big screen TV, to the Jacuzzi in the corner, to the half open bathroom door. My muscles were tight and uncomfortable, and would hurt something fierce every time I stretched them out, while it hurt it also was very comforting in a strange way. The first knock on my door came and went without me so much as blinking, but the second one was more assertive, and I jerked my head up to look at the door. I rolled my eyes as I forced myself to stand up and begin to slowly walk to the door. “This better be good because my head hurts too much right now to listen to some bullshit.” I whispered under my breath just as I reached the door, before pulling it open. I barely caught a glimpse of who was standing in front of me before giving a half grin. “Don’t need any towels, gracias.” “Is that any way to treat an old friend?” I stopped from shutting the door and froze in place. That voice was so familiar yet seemed so different as well. Slowly I opened the door fully again and finally got a good look at the person standing on the other side. She was quite beautiful that was for certain. She was just a shade under 6? tall, had short brown hair with streaks of blonde in it. She was wearing a pair of tight black slacks with a red halter top, and a black jacket over that. She seemed to be in very good physical condition, as even through black slacks her leg’s muscles were very apparent, and her stomach very toned under her halter top. “D’Do I know you senorita?” “It’s been awhile Roberto, but I would hope you’d remember me, after all you were married to my sister.” My mouth fell open and the feeling in my entire body flushed from me. I almost lost my footing but I grabbed onto the wall next to me, or more like fell into it. My breathing picked up it’s pace, and if my head was screaming at me before it was going about ten fold now. “Kari !”” Even though my vision was shaky at best now, I could see her smile at the knowledge that I did finally recognize her. I did everything to regain myself, forcing myself to breath and trying to put the pain in my head to the back, so I could concentrate. I hadn’t seen Kari for almost eight years now, and when I did she had just turned fifteen years old. She looked very little like the woman that stood here in front of me now ? a very beautiful woman. “Yes it’s me Roberto.” “Um ? I ? Come in then ?” I had no other choice but to invite her in really, and even if I did my mind wasn’t working to it’s fullest. This was one of those moments that you aren’t prepared for ? and that sledgehammer has just fallen once again.
