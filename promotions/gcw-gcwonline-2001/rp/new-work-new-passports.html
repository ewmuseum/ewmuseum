New Work, New Passports

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Over the last week Meeting the School Board Once again, a strike was going on. This has become the NME’s forte of late, being there when the little people needed someone to watch over them. The school board didn’t know whether or not to trust these three men that stood in the superintendant’s office. All they knew that the big one, wearing a hood and sunglasses intimidated everyone that was looking at him. The other two?.not so much, but they were intimidating as well. Then again, the fact that Dorian Ryan was carrying with him a baseball bat would make anyone not used to violence in the workplace a little skittish. “Now, ladies and gentlemen, I have heard a rumor that you are going to be going to court to try and end this work stoppage. Now, you see, that would be great, that going to the courts would actually do something.” Dorian said as he took his sunglasses off, folded then and put them in the inner pocket of his leather blazer. “You see, there is one problem with that. The teachers are willing to defy that very court order until you agree on a new contract. You see, this is a no win situation for you. Especially with the MEAP testing coming up soon.” “What do you want?” Dr. Baracy, the superintendant of the district asked whilst sitting at his desk. “What I want is what the teachers want. They want you not to touch their insurance. They want you to reduce class size. It’s not that hard when you think about it. All it takes is the hiring of a couple of new teachers instead of putting all the money that you have into new football fields and tennis courts.” “Those were needed at the time.” “Excuse me” FieldTurf on the Football and Soccer fields were more important than making sure that your students had a quality education? I don’t think so. Dr. Baracy, don’t take me to be an idiot. I’m not. I look at those teachers that are striking, and I see men and women that are doing the best that they can to reach out to the kids. “And you are telling them that in order to do their job possibly, you are going to pack their classrooms so full that there is no possible way that they can give hands-on, one-on-one instruction to the kids that really need it. “I talked to a young lady before we came in here that said that she sits at a lab table in chemistry every day because they simply ran out of desks. How can you justify that, Dr. Baracy” How?” “Well?” “You can’t. So don’t even try to. You see those men and women out there, they are looking to do a better job for the kids. Simple as that. They aren’t marching because they want more money. They aren’t marching because they feel that they deserve extra sick days. “No, Mr. Baracy, they are marching because you gave them no other choice.” “Is there any way that we can end this” Without cutting class sizes.” “You’re going to have to fire each and every teacher out there. But have fun trying to replace them, as I doubt that there is going to be any teachers that are willing to come and work for this district.” The Flight Home The next day, the three men that made up the NME arrived back in Minneapolis feeling that they did a good job. The teachers were back talking with the school board, and finally there seemed to be an end to the work stoppage near. NMEsis was driving a rather large moving van, which was followed by two black pickups. New gear for the new gym that they had put together an the Pit. The tell tale “chirp” is heard by Wyatt as he pulls his phone from the center console of the truck. “Yeah, Ara?” Over the two-way, Araceli responds, “It’s good to have you boys back home.” “Well, we aren’t there yet. By the way, has my package arrived yet?” “Just came in today. You know, Wyatt, you should have thought about renewing this thing months ago. But now the three of you can head over to England and finally wrestle.” “It’ll be good when we get back in the ring. We’ll see you when we get there.” Wyatt then searches real quick for another number in his phone, and again, the tell-tale chirp can be heard. “Yo.” “Dorian, we’re back in business. My passport just got here.” “That’s great. Now, if only chatterbox would have gotten his taken care of, we wouldn’t have to worry about things.” “It’s good to finally have one again though.” “Well, just make sure that your resident visa doesn’t expire either. Otherwise, it’s going to be a long flight from Calgary.” “Dorian, Dorian, don’t worry about it. Besides, Cammy just got her Resident Alien card in the mail the other day. I think we’re good to go.” “Well, I’m not worried about it, I just want to get back in the ring and do what it is that I do best.” “Sucking at Madden.” “Laugh it up, jackass.” The three vehicles pull into the pit. Dorian opens the door and doesn’t even have time to get out before he’s met with a armful of Andrea. “Honey, I missed you.” “I missed you too, sweetie.” Wyatt walks past them, shaking his head. “Get a fucking room.” Wyatt then opens the back of the truck and sees boxes upon boxes of gym equipment. “You gonna come over and help.” NMEsis shows one of the other things that he was brought into the group for, as he reaches up, grasps one of the heavier boxes and takes it off the truck with ease. Even Wyatt is impressed by the strength of this guy. “Wyatt, you gonna let him show you up like that?” Dorian asks as he escapes his wife’s loving embrace and pulls a box out of the bed of the pick up truck.” “Yes. I am.” Wyatt then pulls a box from the truck, considerably smaller than the one that NMEsis had pulled out. “Because I know that he can already out lift me. There is nothing for me to prove against him. Yeah, he’s bigger and stronger. But remember Dorian. I’m bigger and stronger than you are.”
