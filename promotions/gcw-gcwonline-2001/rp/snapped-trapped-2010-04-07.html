Snapped...Trapped

7 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/07/10 The fire is crackling and the radio is discussing the American Politics. Apparently the President of the United States(a man of color, can you believe that? The leader of the free world finally put years of bigotry behind them and elected a man of color) has finally just passed the controversial Healthcare Bill. The announcement has the entire world in an uproar including England. The only people that don’t seem to mind are Ava, Elijah, and Brittany Simpson. They (ages 9, 6, and 3 respectively) are in Belmont visiting their grandfather Alexander for the weekend. The Simpson children now reside in Wales but have been begging their parents to come see Grampy Al. Well they finally won mum and dad over and are spending the week in Belmont. Ava the oldest was told about Belmont as a little girl. “Don’t say anything about the town to Grampy, he has spent his whole life there and has seen a lot.” Ava promised her mum she would do no such thing. She was also told not to stare at Grampy’s fake leg because he had “bad dreams” about that day his leg got hurt. Once again Ava made that promise to her mum. Unfortunately, mum never told Elijah and the baby Brittany to remain quiet about it. “Grampy?’ Asked Elijah, “how did you get that baseball bat foot?” At first Grampy was a little stunned, almost floored with the question. He has not talked about that day in years. “Well Elijah I was playing with a little boy about your age and I got it stuck in a bear trap and off it went.” Elijah did not comprehend the excuse but he was quickly hushed by Ava. Grampy smiled at Elijah told him it was ok, and flashed a wink at Ava, informing her that it was fine he was just being a curious little boy. The rest of the night was a quiet one. Smore’s were being made over the fire, the radio was finally shut off and the TV was on, and the kids were put to bed at 930pm. He tucked the little one’s in and went to the living room and sucked on a pipe. He sat back and reflected on the day. The kids made him feel young again. Turning 52 this past winter was a rough day especially since this passed fall when he lost his wife Edna of 31 years to the cancer. The family stopping in and visiting was always a real treat even though they never seemed to stay longer than one night. He kept telling them that Belmont is fine, the ghost family died 8 years ago in a freak accident. But the stigma of Belmont left a bad vibe and ice in your veins. There was nothing he could say to change their minds. He stood up, the creek in his leg caused his teeth to squeeze and seethe in pain. He limped to his sleeping quarters and looked at himself in the mirror. He was a good looking man, much better looking than that day of hell, that day Bryan O’Neil took his leg more than 30 some years ago……. Alex and his friend whom they simply called Buzz were chasing Jason O’Neil through the apple orchard. Jason zigged and zagged through the tree’s to escape his tormentors. Buzz shoved Jason at the cross walk today because Buzz was selling pot to the kids apparently and Jason, obviously miserable and sick from all the torture and beatings he took, stood up for him and scratched Buzz’s cheek. An adult broke up the incident and Buzz vowed revenge. The chase began the very second school let out. The worst thing to happen is that Bryan never went to school today, he was nowhere to be seen today, so he could not help his brother out at all. Jason zigged one way and tripped over the stump of an apple tree crashing to the ground bucking his chin against the ground chattering his teeth together. The pain was intense and paralyzing. Jason could not even think about moving because the only thing registering in his brain was the pain in his jaw. Buzz and Alex finally caught up to him, both breathing heavily. Buzz looked down at Jason, his body heaving in and out from the chase that finally ended, “You…think you…can..hit..me?” Jason simply did not hear him. He seemed to be in his own little world, floating above the scene with a jaw that hurt more than anything he could imagine. Buzz gritted his teeth and almost hissed like a snake and began to stomp on Jason’s head and chest with his feet. Alex, afraid of Buzz, joined in the assault. Jason came back to reality and realized the situation at hand. Each kick and stomp caused more pain. Alex connected with a kick to the face that made blood explode from Jason’s mouth and nose. Jason swallowed three of his own teeth and his eyes rolled in the back of his head. He was out cold. Even though Jason was out cold Buzz kept the onslaught going. Alex twice tried to stop Buzz only to be elbowed or shoed away. “This kid will pay, and pay big!” Alex began to grow weary of the attack and realized that they were on O’Neil property, and Jason’s father or worst yet brother can be lurking anywhere. Alex stood and shoved Buzz off the beaten lifeless body of Jason O’Neil. “Goddamn it man it’s over! Let’s get the fuck out of here before Bryan shows up!” Buzz blew him off. “You scared of that freak? If Bryan showed up I’d do the same thing to him as I am doing to his punk little bitch of a brother!” His wish was answered. Coming from behind a tree, directly behind the left shoulder of Buzz was Bryan O’Neil. He came from out of nowhere so fast that Alex didn’t even have time to react and warn his friend. Bryan grabbed the long hair of Buzz, jerked his head back violently and landed a fist to the face. His face exploded breaking the Maxilla along with his noses and jaw. Buzz went out cold and almost like a sickening horror film Bryan stood there holding him by the hair while the rest of the body laid lifeless. Blood and snot and who knows what else was running down Buzz’s face staining his shirt and the ground below him. Jason began to stir and moan as Bryan dropped Buzz. He walked towards his brother and squatted down assessing the damage inflicted. He was feeling the face for any breakage and looked up at Alex. He gave him the look of death and immediately unleashed the floodgates. Alex pissed his pants. He was 19 years old, 3 years older than Bryan yet he feared Bryan like death! Bryan stood up and stepped towards Alex, cuing Alex to make a run for it. He turned around and booked for an exit. Bryan did not run, instead like a bear he stalked his victim. Bryan knew this apple orchard like the back of his hand, and if you don’t pay attention you’ll end up lost for good. So chasing someone blind to the orchard was much more fun than running after them. Alex made one mistake. He was only feet from the exit and hopefully free from the beating of his life and instead of watching where he was going he turned to see where Bryan was at. The chase ended with a snap than a crunch followed by a scream. Some kind of teeth bit into his left leg. His momentum launched him forward causing a violent rip into his leg. Alex dropped and screamed in pain. It took him nearly 2-3 minutes to figure out what happened to him. Alex’s leg was caught in a bear trap. The teeth were sickly sharp. He looked down at his leg finally and saw some of the teeth were stuck into his shin. If he moved the pain would intensify. The trap went through his shin, sawing right through his calf muscle and locking itself a quarter way into his bones. The blood leaking from his leg was dark red with a odd gooey white mixture flowing freely. Alex would later learn that the white goo was his bone marrow exiting his body. Alex slowly turned his head, he was coming in and out of consciousness, and the few seconds he came back to this world he saw a giant of a man, his long black hair hanging in his face, his breath, my god his breath was intense, beating down on him. The world was spinning than stopping, spinning than stopping. He was in shock. He could not move because any movement would cause another pain infused blackout. Bryan grabbed him by his hair, and stared deep into his eyes. “What you are feeling is something you will never forget. The blade is deep into your leg. I do plan on leaving you here. If you die, you die. That is neither here nor there for me. If you happen to live, well your leg will become infected. Gangrene will settle in and your leg will begin to rot from the inside out. The stench very well may cause you to black out again.” Bryan turned his head, hacked, and spat. “I guarantee you will lost that leg and you’ll never be able to use it again. You’ll never run, you’ll never use it to kick and innocent teen, and you’ll never use it to get leverage when you are fucking your pregnant whore of a girlfriend again. Maybe I should go pay her a visit.” Bryan grinned stood up and walked off. Alex immediately screamed for him to stop. His mind left the orchard and went towards his girlfriend Edna. She was 4 months pregnant. All he remembered before the black was “How am I going to take care of her with one leg?” Alex will never forget that day. He remembered every single detail to the size of the apples on the ground by him, even if it was well over 30 years ago. He does not remember how he was freed and rushed to the hospital, but he believe Buzz came to and found him lying there.(little did he know it was Jason that freed him from the trap and called the EMT’s) Buzz and Alex made a vow to never talk about it again. The EMT’s and police questioned them but they came up with a story that they got into a fight over a girl, Alex beat Alex up pretty good. Buzz was knocked out and it scared Alex so he ran to get some help and that’s when he ran into the trap. Bryan was never implicated in anything. Alex’s only explanation to himself was that he feared revenge if he told the officials what really happened. He had nightmares his entire life to the point where he developed insomnia throughout his life. He spent the rest of his early adulthood in a wheelchair. His leg was amputated at the kneecap. He didn’t receive this Prosthetic piece until 9 years ago. It’s some what comical that after all these years he still feels an itch in his ankle or under his knee and he will go to scratch without hesitation only to scratch at air up until nine years ago. Now he scratches a fake leg. He catches himself and usually let’s out a chuckle. Alex turns away from his wife’s vanity mirror and sits on the edge of the bed. He undoes his leg and puts it on the ground next to the bed. He says a short prayer than turns back towards his pillow. The tan sheets have turned brown in some spots. This was strange because he recalls washing these just the other day. He removes the sheet and he falls from his bed in fear. Lying in a bloody mess in the middle of his bed was a bear trap. Alex falls from the bed to a loud thump, his chest screaming at him in pain. He starts to sweat to. His left shoulder feels like it’s broken, but he knows it’s not broken. He wanted to scream, but he didn’t want to wake the children up. Instead he lied there peacefully, back against the rocker staring at an empty bed. He smiles at his foolishness. The Lunatic got him again. At least he will be able to see Edna’s beautiful face again….
