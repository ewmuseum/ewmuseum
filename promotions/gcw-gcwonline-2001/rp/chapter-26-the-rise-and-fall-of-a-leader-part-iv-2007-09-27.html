Chapter 26: The Rise and Fall of a Leader- (Part IV)

27 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 27, 2007 __Start A kick to the face. Metaphorically speaking, most people see a man's hard work being tarnished by the freshly polished boot of a corporate scum bag with his only intention being ruining your life. You envision the rise of the now fallen man and his place on the top of whatever he has managed to conquer. He holds his hands up proudly and smiles every time someone says hi to him in the hallways. Just as easily though, you imagine him falling off his mountain due to a single kick. He tumbles down his pile of success and watches his hard work drain away like the remnants within a toilet bowl. Until he reaches bottom. Here he lies, unconscious to reality and everything around him. Nothing makes sense anymore and no one knows who he is. Banished into obscurity, the man is broken and humbled with one move. Ironic, huh? Chapter 26: The Rise and Fall of a Leader Part IV Following Worldwide 74 He could feel the boot marks on his face still, a permanent implant of disgrace carved into his flesh for an eternity to come. He had no dignity left, no spunk, he had nothing. DeBough's size twelve shoe drove everything he came to know and love into the mat in an instant. And so now here he is, a "leader" to few, sitting dejected in the corner of the hotel room. A half empty, pessimistic of course, bottle of Jack Daniels keeps him company along with his good friend cigarette. The smoke paints a picture of sorrow before Jay's eyes and he swats it away with relative ease. 'Fuck me!' He exclaims to his two friends. His gear bag still sits, untouched, on his bed taunting him with every inch of its body. It laughs at him-calling him names and mocking his very existence. Jay watches the tirade and tosses his 27 at it. 'Fuck you.' He shouts. No reply. It is then that Jay remembered everything he knew about wrestling. He could see his father pushing him into the business. Under the name Talley, he was bound to be successful. It was a sure shot and one that Jay neglected to take. Jay was different from most people and with little thought he took the low road and decided that he would make his career into whatever he wanted it to be. Wherever the fights brought him, he'd show up and do his work. It was how he liked it. And then came Daniel Franco. What a great opportunity he was. From the start, Jay found something he hated about the guy but there was no way he could turn down success again. Hell, he was a day away from his own death. So with Franco's helping hand he found GCW. He walked into the project like anything else he'd ever done in his life, hands in his pants, and figured everything would come to him. It didn't. He fell flat on his face to Jayce Bradley and kissed the mat with a fat lip. He wasn't a champion like Franco promised, he wasn't the best like Franco said- he was a loser jumping too high for his own good. And that's when it all came together. Jay found that he couldn't let it take him down that easily. He fought back. One match, two matches, three matches, five matches in a row he fought back claiming the television title from Jimmy Thunder's waist. He tied it around his own waist and showed the ranks of GCW that Jay Terror wasn't your typical kid. And that's when things started to shake. Franco was dead weight, a murderer in all senses. With the loss of his manager, Jay took off again and held the belt longer than anyone before him. Life was his high and he didn't go anywhere without taking a drag of the air. Enter Tyson DeBough. Who would have known he'd steal the championship and Jay's heart. 'Bastard.' He muttered. Everything began to get worse and worse until he came to the point he is at now. One boot to the face and everything he'd accomplished in his short GCW career had been set back to zero. He was no hero and he never would be. He wanted to strangle himself and shout as loud as he could. He wanted to do anything that would pass the time and ease the pain. Nothing worked though. It continues to linger in his head and nothing will release it. Unless, that is, he can capture his title back. The bright idea has been glowing for long enough but finally Jay knows what he must do. It's time to make his wrongs right and the things he does right perfect. __________ In the rubble of his shattered career, the man stands. He finds it hard at first but, like any other obstacle, it becomes easier. Walking turns out to be simple again and talking closely follows. He finally realizes that he isn't as big of a wreck as he thought he was and that the longer he laid in the pieces of his old life the more and more dead he becomes. So he walks, proudly, and holds his chin up for others to see. He acts like the champion he knows he is and believes in the man that he was. In fact, he knows that he has always been that guy on top of the mountain. He knows that he never had a problem touching the sky but instead it was his mind telling him not to. He was scared and couldn't understand and therefore didn't know the potential he was eclipsing. Risen again, the leader among men. Never to vanish again. Today Confidence fills his lungs as Jay steps along the sidewalk at the side of Art Jankey and Phillip Indisio. Three men, bandanas around their necks, walking in a straight line attract much attention and many unwanted stares. They watch the whispers start and grow from ear to ear and with each addition to the secret they laugh in unison. They weren't bank robbers, they weren't killers, they weren't anything really. Not anymore at least. Just a few old friends. 'It's funny you know.' Art spoke. 'Very.' Phillip answered. Jay nodded and kept his stride. 'I bet they got to the one where we're all from the past. You know...the one where we took Doc. Brown's De Lorean and came here from the wild west?' 'That's always a favorite.' Art replied. They laughed as a cohesive unit much like they had a few times already on the walk. Jay leads the pack of misfits around a brick corner and onto a new street. They don't have the slightest clue where they're going in all honesty but that's probably what makes it all the better. 'Feel like eating?' Jay questions. 'Always.' Says Phillip. Art agrees and they continue down the sidewalk. The secrets spread and the whispers continue to follow them. It truly is flattering to have people talking about you. I mean, even if it's not kind it's still pretty sweet to have people notice you and get the impression that makes them ask questions. It shows a sign of superiority really when you think about it. The person doesn't know anything about you and you know everything there is to know. You have the answers to their questions and giggle as they continue to ask others. Anyways, they keep their quest going and move two streets down until they find a diner. "Rob's" to be exact. 'This'll do.' Art said. They walked inside and found the looks were the same as the ones on the street. Still, Jay felt almighty and wonderful. 'Just you three?' A fairly good looking waitress asks. She gets the customary nod and she turns around to grab menus. Typically this is the part where she curses under her breath and rolls her eyes. 'Follow me then.' They did. She led them down an aisle and then down another until she stopped at a wet table. The menus scattered across in one toss and she smiled. 'What would you guys like to drink?' They look at one another consulting with everyone using their eyes. In unison, 'Chocolate Milk.' She definitely rolled her eyes on the walk away and the guys laughed at the thought of it. 'She seems wonderful, huh?' Phillip brings up. 'Yeah, quite the woman.' Art replies. 'I wouldn't mind going a round with her though; she'd be nice and feisty.' Phillip joked. 'Yee-haw to that brother!' Art amused himself with the cowboy noise. It was only customary for them after all the looks they received. 'So where's the mischief in this town anyways?' Phillip asked. Jay stroked his unshaven chin and looked off to a couple across the way. 'Who knows,' His attention turned back to the table, 'All I can say is I need to smash someone with a bottle or something.' 'Here,' Art lifted the ketchup bottle into the air, 'Use this.' He smiled accordingly. Jay chuckled. 'Yeah yeah.' 'What's the matter anyways, you seem like you've been stomped on.' Phillip wondered. The funny part about it was that he actually did get stomped on. Literally and metaphorically Jay Terror had been smacked in the face with a boot. 'Wrestling...isn't it?' Art tried. 'You got it. Tyson DeBough still.' 'You gotta' quit that shit, it messes you up. Remember Benny from fifth street? Well he went and did that wrestling thing too and ended up dying when he fell off the fire escape. Shit was brutal. They still got his chalk outline lying around like some in ground statue or something.' Phillip added. 'Eh, this is different.' Jay started. He didn't care about Benny and his side alley wrestling matches. He didn't care about the blood he spilled or the brains he lost on that sidewalk, it just wasn't the same. Benny wasn't going anywhere in life, he wasn't high on life like Jay was. Not the same at all. 'You know what I mean Art.' 'I know what I told you last week.' 'And look where it got me.' Jay sighed. 'Look Jay,' Art started, 'You can't keep getting down like this. DeBough's a cunt, plain an' simple. The guy has no balls and can't seriously have a spine made of anything other than Jell-O. You have to stop beating yourself up over spanky newcomer and start living life as a belt-less champion.' 'I know...' Phillip butted in. He was the least educated in the discussion but felt the need to chime in anyways. 'You can't know. You look like some welfare line dude these days. All depressed and lonely looking isn't going to get you anywhere chief.' 'Yeah.' Jay started. He watched Art's lips begin to move so he cut out. 'All I'm saying is that shit happens. You get knocked down, so what. If you can't pick yourself up what are you worth?' Phillip giggled. 'As much as this jelly packet.' He pointed to the jam packs and found himself completely hilarious. It was hard not to laugh though. 'So I do what?' 'Well to start, you find a weapon or something and beat the living shit out of DeBough. The guy is messing with you and technically there is no rule about weaponry attacking him.' Jay's face lit up, he loved the idea. Art continued, 'Then you take that belt back and make him bleed all over the fucker. Make him feel how you feel now.' Jay's smile kept floating upward in a larger "U" shape, 'And once you've done that, show the world who Jay Terror is and what he does to people. Then you'll be that guy.' Jay idolized Art for this very reason. No matter how sticky things got, he had advice coming out the wazoo. It could be test advice, girl advice, sex advice, whatever...he knew it all. He was a worldly man who'd only ever seen New Hampshire and Australia (thanks to Jay). 'You sir, are a god.' The waitress came back with the chocolate milks and delivered them sloppily on the table. She made it a little too obvious how she felt about being their waitress. 'You guys decide on what you want yet?' It must have been how she said it or something like that. Jay looked her in the eyes and shook his head at her. 'Yeah.' He found that ketchup bottle and gripped it in his hand. The waitress, "Sally" said her name tag, didn't have time to react and before she knew it her tray full of other drinks came crashing down. The ketchup bottle shattered on the rim of the tray and exploded on her and Jay in one loud pop. 'A new waitress please...' She was astonished and didn't even say a word to him. Instead, she trotted out of the dining hall and to the back. Jay was sure he brought tears to her eyes. 'What was that for?' Phillip asked. 'I was practicing my swing.' Art smiled and placed his hand on Jay's shoulder, 'That's the leader that I knew you had in you.' __________ He is god. __End
