Train

14 September 2005

Date: 14 Sep 05 Training. Yeah even I did that, you have to in this fucking business. I am sure people look at me and just see some physically gifted machine that runs on solar energy and never sleeps. I know what people think about me, what they say behind closed doors and what they feel every time they take in my image. Many more than likely don’t feel I deserve any of the attention I have gotten. People like Rich Rollins and Octavian feel they are king shit around GCW simply because they were here in the beginning. The new guys are just arriving and sure they know I am the champ but do they know LESTATT KNIGHT? Of course not. As a matter of fact it seems that lately EVERYONE has seemed to forget who I am. It began at Annihilation with that bastard Michael Stevens. He decided to grow a backbone, to fight back with some insane kind of intensity. Now what I ask is, did that intensity come from nowhere? Did Michael Stevens flip some switch and become a sadistic human being? No that son of a bitch did not. It was me. I was the one who made him what he turned into. I gave him the fuel he needed to bring that sadistic side of him out and into the spotlight. Sure he took it to me, he gave me a “taste of my own medicine” as they like to say. Did it really do anything to help his cause though? Of course not, I still stand here as the fucking world champion. Yet something is wrong. I stand on top of GCW as I have for the past eight months, yet people are looking at Michael Stevens as if he is the man around here. He has instilled fear into the minds of the roster and the ones who work around GCW as if he is some sort of monster. For god’s sake he couldn’t even get a match because they were “afraid” of what he would do! Yet I have a line of people who think they can take me on head to head. Michael Stevens was nothing before he got involved with me, and now he rules the world? Give me a fucking break. The GCW is forgetting that while Stevens is acting as if he is some sinister bastard that I wrote the book to it. Overlooking the champ? Has that EVER happened before in the history of this sport? Ever since I got this title I have been set to the fucking side like I was a wannabe challenger for the Television Championship ? my name isn’t The Marauder! The Marauder? Hes guilty as charged as well. But for old time’s sake lets call him, Johnny Boy. Now Johnny Boy decided to try getting involved in my personal affairs, and ya know what? I thought it was humorous and so just gave him some friendly advice and what do I get in return? A cheap shot. A fucking cheap shot. Now I have always known that Johnny Boy was low, maybe even a coward but for him to stoop to that level is sickening. I HAD respect for that man, as he has accomplished a feat that no other man has since, and I plan never to allow again. Now Johnny is set to get a rude awakening, just as the rest of the GCW shall as well. Its time that things are revealed for what they really are. Steven’s BAD ASS act is a fa’ade, a mask that will soon be torn off. I am sick of hearing about SPITFIRE and how this match is tailor made for him and that my technical expertise do not come close to Steven’s. These people have no idea what I am capable of, but come Fall out they will. So here I am in a gym not dissimilar to many others around the globe. I am here alone doing what I must to get myself ready for this match. I am going to break Steven’s down, nerve by nerve and not even the way that most would assume. Will I bring out the chair that I have been so good with these eight months? Maybe I should bring the fire and burn his ass up and send him on his way the same way as Jason Dread? No ? The way to do this is the exact way he tried to end my reign. The only way to prove myself and destroy Stevens completely is to turn the tables on him. He did his best LeStatt impression so now it should be my turn. I tune up my technical skills and unleash them on an unsuspecting world ? an unsuspecting Michael Stevens. To make a man who thrives on never giving up, TAP OUT or scream “I quit” do those exact things is like destroying their very foundation. I know I have done it once. I know what it almost did to my career and my mindset when I finally said those words ? it hurt. I have had bones broken, my heart crushed, I have watched my brother be murdered, and I have stared at my child’s grave ? just like those events quitting is more or less having a piece of who you are deep inside being stripped away. Now I intend to make Michael Stevens do the same. Before that though I need to bait him even more, to beat up his state of mind. He thinks he is sick, he thinks he has the one up on me, well he thought wrong. I will show them sick, I will show them sadistic ? and then come Fall Out I will turn into someone completely different. I will become the kind of technical mastermind that Michael Stevens used to be. Perfection. Its what I am aiming for. Destruction. Its what I live for. Blood shed. Its as close to inevitable as you can get. Victory. Its mine. So the sweat pours from my body, the tears stream down my face, and my muscles tighten up. I work hard to be this dominate, and its time to work even harder. I have given everything to this business and at Fall out that will not change. At World Wide that will NOT change. I don’t care who is out there and I sure as hell don’t care that Mike Savige is my “partner”. I will seek and destroy ? The Villain of this story is just getting started. Sweat Blood Tears Sacrifice Its what makes me the champion that I am. I am ready for the Fall Out.
