666: The Story of Bryan Mayhem. Chapter 1

Date: feb 3, 1965 According to many esteemed prophets, Nostradamus to name the most popular, and sketches on the walls in the Great Pyramids, the Apocalypse, the very end of mankind was set to take place in 2012. Apocalypse, the term in and of itself, is not something that happens in one big bang. Like creation, the Apocalypse is a numerological event that happens over a course of time. September 11, 2001(do I have to explain this?) and December 26, 2004 the date of the Great Tsunami are all dates in this Apocalyptic gala of horror we are presently living today. Ok so just say these prophets are all correct and the end of time is October 10, 2012. When did the Apocalypse begin? I have an idea, how does February 3, 1965 sound? Birth of a child is suppose to be the most precious thing on earth. The blood, sweat, and tears are just the beginning. The pain and torment that leads to years of bliss and smiles. Most babies are welcomed into our world with open arms and tears of joy. Mothers and fathers alike begin to count the ten fingers and ten toes each child possesses. “Oh he looks like me but has your nose” is the only conversation ever heard. Grant it, most births happen in a hospital, unless of course you are Anton Alexander O’Neil of Belmont England. Anton born August 9 1933 was the only child of a hog farmer. He was very intelligent, but always had a sense and understanding of the macabre. The slaughtering of hogs wasn’t just something he did for money and food, it’s something he did for the sheer state of nirvana. I wish I could tell you what caused this tortured man to become so destructive and abusive to his own blood. I walked around Belmont for nearly six months asking and protruding into people’s lives about one Anton O’Neil and most shunned me with a slammed door or a simple sneer than walked away. His records do not even exist in the city hall or the library. The only thing I’ve ever really discovered about his past is the bit about being a hog farmer, and his distant relationship of Blood Countess Elizabeth Bathory, the worlds first and most renowned female cannibal. That portion I will cover later on in this story, for it’s a spooky and fascinating story. Anton O’Neil was often to himself, and reveled in the company of local prostitutes. Laure Lincoln seemed to be his favorite. She was short, little thick, and to be quite honest a total wreck. She resembled a modern day meth addict without the chapped lips. She has stringy dark hair, rotted teeth, and a lazy eye that made you look over your shoulder when you were talking to her because it looked like she was staring off at something else. Why would Anton chose such an ugly atrocious woman to copulate with 3-4 times a week? Bryan thinks its because she allowed men to beat her and abuse her for only a few pounds more. It was the 1960’s and use of condoms was not a very popular thing, especially in a small free loving town of Belmont England. When Laure approached Anton with the notion that she was pregnant, he was insistent that the child was not his. He beat her so bad that she was laid up in a hospital bed for about four days or so. Most thin that Anton knew it was his, because the local prostitutes around the area began to refuse Anton services because was insistent on ejaculating inside of them. Along with abuse, this seemed to be another sick fetish of his. The most disturbing will be introduced later on. Anton visited Laure numerous times in the hospital, and while they were alone demanded numerous sexual deeds to be done while she was recuperating. Laure wanted to keep the child, and Anton knew no matter how bad he beat her or how bad he tried to convince to destroy the child, he was fighting a losing battle. Why did Anton, a filthy violent man whore let Laure move into his home with him? I prodded into Laure’s past and family the best I could and I could find no answer to this question, but apparently he started to show a slight interest in his pregnant prostitute and unborn child. Throughout the pregnancy Laure began having numerous difficulties including excruciating pain and very heavy, thick, spotty bleeding. She was bed ridden at 5 months, and that’s where she would stay until the child was born. She was treated for bed sores later on in the pregnancy, and even began having bouts of depression because Anton was still entertaining prostitutes in the house while she laid in pain. It was said that Laure became so impressed that she took the needle that was lying on the table next to, which was used for her pain medication injections, and started to carve into her swollen stomach. The needle obviously not used for carving snacked in two leaving a sliver of it inside her stomach. She was able to carve about a 4 inch groove into her stomach before the needle snapped. Anton never once questioned her about the cut and her motives. He was a horrible provider and was suited to be a horrible father. Whores, walking in covered in hogs blood asking his pregnant hooker to perform oral on him, excessive alcohol abuse, and disappearing for days started to become a consistent lifestyle in the O’Neil household. He was born a few weeks early, nothing to be concerned with. 9LBS 11OZ, 23inches long. A massive child to say the least. What makes it even worse was Anton refused to have his child born inside a hospital, instead he delivered the child himself, and was later rumored to have fed the afterbirth to the family dog. Like I said, just a rumor. Laure went through 23 hours of labor, 23 hours of zero pain medication. She passed out from the pain well over 5 times in the 23 hours. There was no need for an Episiotomy, she was split from rear to front during the birth, something which Anton failed to realize, and would later chastise her because her “pussy looked like mangled meat” was his insult for a while. Bryan almost died at birth, because both not being efficient in the delivery of a human life did not realize that the mucus is the lungs needs to be vacuumed out. Upon his delivery Anton held his son, gave him the name of Bryan (Celtic for strong) and placed him in a basonet and left him there to die(?) and went out and had fun with a hooker. Laure passed out after the delivery. She laid there in her own blood, urine, and feces because Anton dared not clean her up, because he had major issues with filth. Little did she know that her first born son just feet away, was seconds away from dying. After a while that mucus from the amniotic sac begins to break down in the lungs, and can form clots in the esophagus. This started to happen to Bryan as this little innocent life began its first hours of life neglected by an evil father and a whore of a mother. Laure was awakened by what seemed like a choking sound. She was weary at first, not sure of where she was, but when she looked down and saw all the blood and filth soaked into the sheets between her legs. She gasps and sits up with a jerking motion ignoring the pain and begins to look around frantically. Turns her head right, than left and notices the basonet and a tiny life of motion. She slides herself over and grabs her baby who is quickly turning an awful shade of blue. Laure begins to hyperventilate and starts to bump her baby up and down, almost a little too violently to be honest, when a quiet(loud?) little burp is heard and a mountain of mucus emits from her baby’s mouth and lands on her left forearm. She ignores the mucus and notices that the color begins to come back to her baby. Laure begins to cry and holds her baby tightly to her left shoulder and starts to rock back and forth, once again ignoring the pain that erupts from her midsection. She pulls out a breast and begins to allow her child to suckle, the very first act of bonding between mother(whore) and son(devil). She smiles, smiles a smile she hasn’t smiled since the day her father came home from the war when she was a little girl and gave her a huge hug and kiss. She hasn’t been as happy since that day. Well today, she becomes a mother, and she promises to raise this child better than her mother raised her. Little did she know she was holding Belmont’s very own version of the Anti-Christ, their own rendition of Adolf Hitler, there very own Josef Stalin Jr. Also, little did she know, while feeding her child, his father sat in the shadows of the hallway seething in anger and jealousy, plotting his revenge. Today we don’t celebrate our Independence Day, yet we celebrate the first day of mankind’s extinction.
