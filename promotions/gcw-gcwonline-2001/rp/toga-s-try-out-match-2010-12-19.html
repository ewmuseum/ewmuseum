Toga's Try-Out Match

19 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/19/10 * On the outskirts of a small town that no one remembers the name of, and let's face it, for good reason: There's nothing here; a camera flickers to life. It shows that the past statement isn't entirely true, as a bar sits along the street. Alone. So, so alone. It's lonely. Like an old west saloon. A lone saloon. A salone. Get it?.... Anyway, a female voice is heard from behind the camera, as it seemingly approaches the worn-down salone. * Female: "Can you tell me, one more time, Tommy, why we've dragged ourselves to this miserable little town?" * As she finishes her sentence, Toga walks in front of the camera, leading her and the audience of three (yeah, that means you, bucko!) toward the salone. He peeks over his shoulder to his audience. * Toga: "Because, Lollie, this is supposed to be where my GCW try-out match is supposed to be held. Y'know, to see if I'm up to snuffles." Lollie: "That's 'up to snuff.' Not 'snuffles.'" Toga: "Watev's toots..." Lollie: "..... yikes. Please don't EVER say that again!" * Toga stops at the door, and turns all the way around to her. He nods, as he reaches for the door handle... miss.... miss.... miss.... that's your elbow...... still nope..... that's inappropriate... miss..... Bingo was his name-o! After repeated failed attempts at trying to find the handle without looking, Toga finally gropes it (ew) and opens it. He holds it open as Lollie and the portable handheld audience-showing-thingamajig walk inside. There's a few local ruffians inside who don't look too happy to see strangers in there's 'ere parts, but no one speaks up or moves their eyes from the tumble weeds blowin' on through. In the back of the bar, there's a squared circle that looks like a Jenga tower would be more sturdier than. Lollie and Toga approach the bar, cautiously. * Lollie: "Um... hello, sir. My friend, Toga, was told to come here about a try-out match for GCW. Do you know if a GCW representative is here today?" * The bartender stares at her for a few seconds, as he wipes out a glass. His stare is so blank it's... um........ a blanket....? Lollie nods slowly after a few seconds, obviously realizing that he's not going to be any help. However, this dynamic duo is in luck on this dusty day. A man approaches them. He's in a suit. He offers his hand up to Lollie. * Man: "Hello Miss, maybe I can help you. What did you say your name was?" Lollie: "I'm Lollie. But my friend, To-" Man: "That's an interesting name. 'Lollie.' Like a lollipop?" * She passes the camera off to Toga, then slowly nods to the man, unsure of where this is going, and whether she wants any further communication with this well-dressed man. * Man: "So, I'm guessing by your outfit that you taste like a green apple? Sour but mighty yummy?!" * She quickly pulls her hand away as he tries to kiss it. She quickly grabs Toga's arm and hooks it into her own, which can barely be seen in the corner of the video frame. The man snickers as he walks away. * Lollie: "I don't like it here. Let's leave!" Toga: "Nooooo! I can't! I needta have this match! I needta wrassle!" Lollie: "'Wrassle???'" Toga: "I think this place is rubbing off on me...." * The camera slowly turns to Toga's side (opposite Lollie), where the well-dressed man is now humping Toga's leg. * Toga: "Or maybe it's someone in particular that's rubbing ON me..." * Lollie smacks the guy screaming at him to leave them alone. He drools a bit, then stumbles back and collapses on a table. The camera turns back to Lollie, with the bartender standing in the background, still wiping out his mug. Lollie turns to the tender. * Lollie: "What the hell is wrong with that guy?!!" Bartender: "Oh, don'tcha worry's about him. He does that to alls the strangerses." Lollie: "Creeeeepy." * Tommy sighs as he hands the camera back off to Lollie. * Toga: "I wanna wrestle!" Lollie: "Well, let's face it. The GCW representative isn't here. We've been duped." Toga: "... orrrrr.... maybe.... he's lying in wait... hoping to see how dedicated I am. And whether or not I can solve this puzzle!!!" * Toga jumps up and down clapping and giggling, like a giddy schoolgirl. * Toga: "I LOVE PUZZLES!!! THEY'RE SO PUZZLETASTIC!" Lollie: "Um... sorry to burst your bubble, but I doubt that." * The skirt wearing GCW-Wannabe (that's Toga by the way) completely ignores her as he races to the ring. He stops just beside it. He turns around and yells out to the entire salone. * Toga: "I know you're in here. And I'ma gonna solve ya puzzle! You just watch, GCW person!" * With that, he hops into the ring that sits lopsided, about a foot off the floor. Dust blows off a bit as he lands inside it. The camera moves a bit, then lowers. Lollie sets it on a table, facing the ring, as she sighs and sits beside it. * Toga: "... so... uh...... where's my opponent anyway?" * Lollie sits in silence, trying her best not to be the bearer of bad news to her client and friend. He looks around the bar for someone to take up his offer... but no one budges. No one is even paying attention to him anymore. * Toga: "That's okay! I can do this by myself! You just watch!" * Lollie sighs, as Toga bounces off the ropes. The ring moves a bit, and almost topples over. He reaches the middle of the ring and "locks up" with... um.... dust? He pulls... dust.... into a head... no.... dust-lock. He struggles to keep the "hold" locked in, as he appears to be trying to wrench it. He jerks to the side, then to the other, then suddenly, gets "lifted up" and slammed to the mat with a "backdrop" of sorts.... I think. Dust explodes all over the bar from the canvas and under the ring. Patrons cover their glasses, but otherwise pay no mind whatsoever to the insane kid in the broken down ring. * Lollie: "Um... go Toga....?!" * Everyone turns their attention to Lollie, who shrugs as she tries to support her friend's futile "match." * Toga: "Ooo! Nice counter! But watch this!" * The crazy skirt-clad warrior pulls himself back to his feet, and "locks" back up with "Dusty." He ducks behind (?) and goes for a backdrop of his own... possibly. The non-existent foe seems to be too heavy as Toga appears to be straining to lift... him? Her? It. He clubs "Dusty" in the "back" and tries again. Once again, his attempts seem hopeless... much like his own existence. Tommy sighs, ducks what might be a back elbow, or clothesline, or spotted pony.... who knows. Toga "tackles" It to the canvas and starts punching it in the.... face? Chest? Elbow? He follows it up with a..... a......... a........... aw, screw it. He's having an imaginary match with himself in a broken, dusty ring. He does stuff. Then does a bit more. * Lollie: "Wooo! Kick it's ass, Tommy!" * Suddenly there's a strange singing sound coming from within Lollie's pants.... not like that you pervert! It's a phone! She pulls it out and flips it open. Yep, flip-phone. It must be a text message. She reads it, then giggles. He fingers start tapping away with a response. Meanwhile, Toga continues to do... um... whatever it is that crazy, loser wrestlers do when they are having a match with dust. Lollie finishes her text. Sends it. Then apparently notices something on the phone. She pushes a few more buttons, then puts it to her ear. * Toga: "Uff..... TAKE THAT! AND THAT! AND A BIT OF THIS! BUT NOT THAT!" * Lollie slowly closes the phone, stands up, puts it away and approaches the ring that's surprisingly still standing. Lopsided, but standing nonetheless. She puts her arms on the middle rope, leaning forward. * Lollie: "Um, Tommy.... you said it was a voicemail that you got that told you to come here right?" * Toga appears to be in an excruciating armbar and fighting his urge to tap out, but manages to muster the strength to nod to his cohort outside of the ring. * Lollie: "Okay. I thought so.................. Do you realize how old that message is?" * The suspense is certainly on the rise as Toga's hand gets closer to tapping against the dusty canvas. Once again, he shows superhuman strength and manages to shake his head to Lollie, barely managing to breathe in the process. * Lollie: "It was actually from Scott Wyld." * Toga is clawing at the mat, fighting against the pain. * Lollie: "It's over 3 years old..." * He can't fight it anymore. He taps out with every bit of energy he has left!!! * Lollie: "... you do remember Scott Wyld right?" * Tommy rolls over, trying to hide his tears of loss, and crawls to Lollie. She sits on the apron next to his head. * Lollie: "He was the interviewer from PoW." * He slowly rolls over, lays his head down on the apron, under the bottom rope and stares at her. * Toga: "I... I.... *sob*... I wasn't good enough...." Lollie: "I'm pretty sure this was supposed to be a prank a looooong time ago. You don't ever check your messages do you?" * Toga blinks blankly. * Toga: "The Wyld-Man tried to set me up?! Is... is.... PoW back?" * Lollie sighs. * Lollie: "No.... You only hear half the words I say, don't you?" * The skinny skirt man nods. * Toga: "I do like hair." * She pets his head, showing sympathy for her mentally challenged friend. * Lollie: "I wonder what Wyld wanted you to do...." ~* Meanwhile, back in 2007, Scott Wyld is standing around in the bar, alone. * Wyld: "Y'know... I don't think he's coming...."~ * Back in present time dusty-old-bumfuck-salone, Toga slowly rolls out of the ring and sits beside his manager. * Toga: "You mean there's no one from GCW here and I just got beat up, and had my lunch money stolen, for nothing?!" * Lollie slowly nods, patting her friend on the back. * Lollie: "But look on the brightside... you... um......... got valuable life experience!" Toga: "I swallowed a bug." Lollie: "... and apparently lunch.. for free!" Toga: "It tasted like tripe." * She stares blankly at him. * Lollie: "I'm not even going to ask.... but..... I will say.... eww..." * Once again, there's a strange singing from Lollie's pants. She pulls out the phone. This time it's a phone call. She answers it. * Lollie: "Hello. This is Lollie. Who's this?" * There's a pause, as the person on the other end replies. A shocked look comes over her cute-like-a-button face. * Lollie: "Yeah. He's right here." * Another pause. * Lollie: "I'm his manager." * After a few more moments, she hangs up and turns to Toga. Her reaction is in awe-mode. Not aww-mode. Completely different. * Toga: "Wha.... what was that all about???" Lollie: "That was um... a representative from GCW. They said they're interested in meeting you. They want you to come to their corporate headquarters. Tomorrow." * Tommy cocks his head to the side...... then faints. Lollie gasps, as Toga falls the incredibly dangerous one-foot-length of the apron, to the floor. As always, the patrons of the salone pay no attention to the tumble weeds, as the story prepares to pass on through this dusty ol' town to the next... *
