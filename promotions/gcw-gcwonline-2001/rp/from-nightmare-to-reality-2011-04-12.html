From Nightmare to Reality

12 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/12/11 Razorback Point, Montana... A place the locals have forsaken, for many reasons; one reason could be the old stories of Native American burial grounds and the stirring of evil spirits. Another could be the legends of pilgrim settlers who made the trek north, but lost their minds doing so, resorting to cannibalism. Another could be of the legends that a witch, burned hundreds of years ago, haunts the woods. But the true reason that no one dare’s step foot there, is more than likely the point’s current resident, the man who has destroys everything put before him, the man known only as Copeland. For fifteen years this man has contorted his image, his mind and his destiny into the ultimate of weapons. The man standing in the light of the full moon was not always this man. A life of triumph and tragedy has moulded Copeland into the emotionless machine that he is today. For years past, Copeland was the model of what a husband and father should be, but betrayal and pure hatred ended that in a heartbeat. His wife of many years revealed her true self in a way that would have driven a lesser man to take his own life, but not this man. Driven by hatred and anguish, Copeland used those feelings to dominate and punish those he saw in a different light. The thing that his victims had in common was the blatant over confidence that time and again caused their downfall. Copeland needed to take his anger out on those serve up before him, but his greatest battle would be the battle over his state of mind, that was until Gabriel Roth came into his life. ********************************************************************************* Gabriel Roth stands on the porch of the battered old log cabin as the wind howls and the stars disappear behind a blanket of thick rain clouds. He watches his silent monster trudge off into the thick woods with nothing more than a compound bow and a few arrows; soon he is left with just his thoughts as night begins to fall. The darkness and his thoughts are disrupted for a second though, as the entire lake and surrounding area lights up as clear as day. The lightning cuts across the sky illuminating everything for miles, moments later the thunder puts the exclamation mark on his senses and Roth’s eyes are forced shut by the reflex of the deafening noise. Gabriel opens his eyes and peers out from his dry perch, as the wolves howl in the distance. The lighting cracks again, streaking across the sky and followed again by another clap of thunder. Roth is momentarily joined by his assistant Eli Cain and both men watch as the skies open up and the rains drench everything in sight. The lightning strikes and a sign on the hill can be seen, which reads; RAZORBACK POINT - Trespassers will be shot, Survivors will be shot again! ********************************************************************************** Inside the comforts of the cabin, Roth and Cain sit opposite each other, in front of the fireplace. Gabriel has a whiskey in his hand and Eli holds a glass of bourbon; as they chat in an unfamiliar, almost civilized manner. “Well it would appear that all this waiting will finally be over for Copeland”. Cain says as he sits back in his chair. Roth takes a sip of his whiskey and sits forward, looking out the window as the light show continues. “Yes Eli, the wait will soon be over…Copeland has waited almost five months and that does not spell good news for the person waiting in that other bracket of this Rampage tournament”. Roth’s eyes light up with pride as he speaks of his machine’s impending return to the ring, as he continues. “The last time Copeland stepped inside a wrestling ring, he would annihilate another challenger to his WWA United States title, a title he never lost and still has possession of. But that is in the past, the WWA closed and we moved on, to Global Championship Wrestling…now it’s time to start anew, it’s time to make an immediate impact and continue his dominance, but in a new domain”. His eyes still wide with unhinged pride, Roth sits back in his seat and smirks as the corner of his mouth turns up and Cain nods his head in agreement. Taking a sip of his drink, Eli speaks again in that thick Irish accent of his. “What do you think people are going to say about Copeland, I’ve read this Barry Goldstein’s thoughts on his bracket basically picking him because of what he looks like…not sure about that, but Copeland does intimidate even the baddest of fuckers”. A short chuckle comes from deep down in Gabriel’s throat, as he sits forward swallowing down the last of his whiskey. Placing the glass on the side table, he reaches across and picks up the bottle; an old cork stuck in the top with bite marks in it, is once again pulled out by his teeth and he pores himself a double, before replacing the cork and sinking back into his chair. “Copeland doesn’t need that mask to intimidate, but it has still has the desired effect in building my confidence that he’ll, at least, have the psychological advantage going into his first round match…and when my confidence is high, so is Copeland’s”. Pausing to watch the sky light up again, Roth takes his first sip of his new drink and continues. “I doubt anybody here knows too much about Copeland…well except Shaman, who knows all too well what Copeland is capable of…and I like it that way, they won’t know what has just run them over. The one advantage that Copeland has coming into this company, is that he has nothing to lose and everything to gain; nothing will stop him from proving to the world that he truly is the “God of Gods”. Gabriel’s eyes roll around the room as Eli grins, nodding his head. A few moments of silence pass until Eli stands up and throws another log onto the fire. The crackling of the flame and wood combine as the orange glow engulfs the room. “I’ve seen the guy that Copeland meets at Worldwide and after his match last week, I will say that Copeland isn’t meeting a slouch, he’s got some talent this kid, what’s his name again…Dorian Ryan, yeah that’s it”. Eli says as he turns from stoking the fire and looks at Gabriel, who is sitting back staring right at him. His mouth tightens and then he raises an eyebrow. “I’ll keep that in mind Eli, but that will not drive my confidence down, it will merely drive Copeland to hurt him more. This company needs to realize that though he’s untested here, he is the real deal and he will show it from the get go”. The lighting strikes again and is closely followed by a loud crack of thunder as Roth looks outside, unconcerned for the wellbeing of his machine, who is stuck out in the harsh climate of Northern Montana, hunting his next meal. Eli watches too as the lightning forks across the sky. “That's some rough weather he’s caught in, but I doubt the possibility of getting hit by lightning bothers him too much, he’s fearless”. Producing a cigarette, Eli stops speaking and puts it between his lips and sparks it up. Roth shakes his head as Eli draws back and exhales, blowing smoke in Gabriel’s general direction. Roth waves the smoke away from him. “Jesus Christ Eli…were you born in a tent boy? Roth pauses as Eli takes the cigarette out of his mouth with a puzzled look on his face. “What the hell have I told you about smoking around me…hmmm?” Still looking puzzled, Eli crushes it in a nearby ashtray and raises his hands up in front of him, as if to keep the peace. “Sorry Gabe, you know me, things slip my mind”. Looking annoyed, Roth’s expression changes, as he lifts his glass back up to his lips and takes another drink. “Yeah well… just try and keep that in mind. You’re one of the few people Copeland allows into his inner sanctum…if other people wandered into Razorback Point, he’d consider it an intrusion and you can imagine what would happen to them…hell the sign up on the hill says it all. But he’s allowed you in here, so pull your head in or you know how easily he will take it off”. Nodding his head quickly, Eli takes a nervous sip of his bourbon and looks out the window as the wind howls and the rain barrels down. “But Gabe, after everything you’ve done for me, I have to ask you this…why is Copeland the way he is? I don’t me to intrude but he is an extreme personality, a man who never talks nor shows emotion. His sole purpose in life, it would seem, is to compete and obliterate those he comes in contact with in that ring. But my question is how did he become this man, the man who hides behind that mask?” Gabriel stares at Eli again, taking another sip of his drink. He nods his head and stands up; walking across the room he speaks back over his shoulder. “So you want to know why he’s this way huh? Well let me read you a passage straight from Copeland’s own diary, well his old diary; I doubt he has any reason to keep one nowadays. But nonetheless his diary was my way into Copeland’s mind, it is what helps me to connect with and control him. Without this little leather-bound book, I’d be just like every other person who tried to read him; they were at a loss, but not me”. Scrounging around in a drawer, Roth pulls the small book from the most unlikely of hiding spots and opens it. Flicking through the pages, he almost seems to know the exact spot he is looking for as he stops somewhere in the middle and looks up at Eli. “Wait a second, I need my glasses”. He turns quickly and picks up his narrow rimmed glasses from top of the table and puts them on, looking down at the book, he begins reading. “Lies...her truth has been revealed, at the cost of everything I knew. My last safe-haven falls behind me, with the self that once consumed. Into the void, I'm pursuing only answers. I'm shedding skins and faces, past loves and bitter hatreds. And it has come to pass; a path set in stone now breaks like glass. The failings of yesterday become the catalyst to seek my change. So here I stand, a stranger to my newfound freedom. I am a vessel without destination, devoid of direction. No roles define me, yet conflict embraces me. At the mercy if lucidity, I am adrift in a world all too real. I am a hollow man”. Roth looks up and Eli is as puzzled as before, he shifts his eyes up and thinks to himself again, before shrugging and then takes another drink. Roth closes the book and puts it back in its drawer, locking it away with a key that was in his pocket and then walks back across the room. “Okay…what the hell was that?” Eli says as Roth sits back down, grasping his glass again and polishing off the rest of the aged liquor. He reaches down and picks up the bottle as he speaks. “Copeland wrote that the day his wife left him for another man and took his daughter away, that was the day “Seth Copeland” died and “Copeland” was born. That day was the turning point, no longer was his life about somebody else, it was from that day forth all about him and nothing has stood in his way since”. Looking across the room, Roth moves his head around and Eli turns to notice a torn picture of his daughter sitting on top of a smashed piano, before pulling the cork once again and pouring himself yet another glass of whiskey. “When that day came, when Brooke took Grace from him, it was the final nail in the proverbial coffin for Seth. His life ended that day and he was reborn as “Copeland”, but that picture there means that there is some semblance of his old self left, which isn’t a bad thing from where I sit…he uses it to inflict punishment and that has allowed him to taste success”. Still looking at the picture of Grace, Eli turns back to Roth and asks another question. “But if there is a part of his old self left, don’t you think that he has the ability to come back from the dead?” Roth looks at him again and the corner of his mouth turns up in a wicked smirk, before he shakes his head slowly. “Not a chance in hell…Copeland will never forgive or forget and if he ever saw Brooke again, I fear the worst for her, not that she wouldn’t have it coming though…she took his daughter away and he has not seen here since she was two years of age, she’s now seven…five years of personal hell that has reached boiling point and is ready to erupt on GCW”. Both men smile and bring their glasses together as they toast the beginning of Copeland’s next chapter. “I’m looking forward to it, it’ll be good to see him back doing what he does best…I kind of feel sorry for the kid he’s put in there with though”. Roth laughs even louder as the door flies open and a rain soaked Copeland stands in the doorway dragging a dead buck behind him. Roth turns and looks at the man in the steel mask and grins. “You feel sorry for him huh? Well I don’t…he’s just someone who will meet the same demise as everyone else, GCW is just the next stop in his path of destruction”. THE NIGHTMARE HAS A BECOME REALITY
