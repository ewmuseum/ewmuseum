Date: Timeless With his teeth gritting, he cinches tight the noose. Letting out a little grunt with the final cinch, Mayhem holds his work in the air. The perfect noose, the perfect ending to Ciel’s final match. -=flash flash=- “Jason, you’ve got to make sure it’s looped tight before you cinch it, otherwise your victim will fall right out of it, and that’s not what we want.” Little Jason O’Neil looked at his brother in confusion. Why was he being taught how to make to a noose? Some of the things Bryan showed him were crazy. Bryan smacks him in the back of the head. “Hey you listening” Don’t make me waste my time with this. This kind of shit is important Jason, especially out here.” Jason nodded that he was listening. But how could this be important? This is Belmont England, the O’Neils are the crazies, everyone else is normal. Jason than looked in the eyes of his big brother. Bryan smiled and rustled Jason’s moppy hair with his hand. -=flash flash=- Mayhem’s head jolts as he comes back to now. He recalls showing Jason a lot of life lessons, none more important than the tying of a noose. Bryan didn’t live with his family long enough to find out if Jason ever used some of the knowledge he was shown. Bryan can only hope so. He must have learn something. His little brother is a Hall of Famer afterall. Bryan starts to grind his teeth in anger over that thought. -=flash flash=- “Damnit Jason, just hold onto it so I can slip it over his head. Jason felt miserable, he was holding down this little dog. Bryan saw it from the distance and they began to chase it. Jason was so scared of his big brother that he often went along with whatever he said. When they caught it, it was Jason’s job to hold it down while Bryan pulled the noose out. Jason kind of sat on it to hold it down. His left leg overtop the torso, while his hands pinned its head down. Bryan adjusted the noose to fit the dog’s head. He slipped it around the dog’s neck and shoved Jason off of it. Jason backed up and stood in disgust as Bryan lifted the dig with the noose. The poor thing was kicking, it couldn’t breathe and Jason knew it. A tear filled his right eye, while a gleam filled Bryan’s. Bryan began to twirl the noose like a lasso, with the dog still attached to the other end. Jason cringed when he heard the snap. Atleast it was out of it’s misery. -=flash flash=- Bryan remembers hearing the crack as well. He snapped the dog’s neck. He doesn’t recall what he did with the corpse, most likely jut left it there. He looks back to the noose and pictures Ciel’s head cinched tight, swinging around like a rag doll. He can hear the snapping of the neck, He can smell the bowels being moved and exiting the now deceased corpse. -=flash flash=- Jason use to get beat up constantly, and today was no different. Jason was hugging the tree, arms tied with his own socks. This bully was smacking Jason’s ass, violating him as well. He was just about to do something awful with a stick when Bryan appears from around the tree. The bully stopped, jaw dropped, and just froze. You can tell he wanted to run, but he almost seemed cemented to the ground. Bryan grabbed him by the back of the head, and smacked him in the face. His blood began to ooze from his nose. Bryan pulled from his back pocket his now infamous noose, ties it around the bully’s neck?.. -=flash flash=- He stares back into this noose. This tool of killing, this mechanism use to extinguish life. This sick, hideous grin appears on his face, this sick sadistic smile of a madman, this vile smirk of the devil himself. He slips the noose over his own head, smiles again, cinches it, and begins to pull?..
