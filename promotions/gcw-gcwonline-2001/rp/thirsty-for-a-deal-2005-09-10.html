Thirsty for a deal...

10 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/10/05 Ricky stands in the bathroom in front of the mirror with a handful of styling gel in the palm of his hand. Still smiling he runs his hands through his hair and shakes them wildly and with no real purpose. As he pulls his hands from his hair he rinses his hands and dries them on the towel, hanging behind him. He flips the switch on the wall leaving the room dark as he exits. “Gonna be a good day today!” Ricky heads into the kitchen, pausing in the living room as the phone rings. He fetches the cordless from the holder and raises it to his ear as he continues into the kitchen. “You got the Rickstah! Talk to me!” “Hey Ricky it’s me. You see the booking list for this weeks show” They have you facing off against some pimp?.wait’I mean thug. I always get those guys confused. Calls himself “The Vortex” Vance Johnson or something?” “Who” I just looked at the roster and saw nobody by that name.” “Hold on Ricky, let me check my notes. Oh, Sorry about that, it’s “The Violence” Vince Jones.” “What” Why did I get stuck with a jobber?” “Ricky, he’s not a jobber, he was in LOW. He is pretty seasoned wrestler, don’t underestimate him” “He was low” Like on money or something? If he needs money I will give him a loan, but I mean, I don’t wanna hurt anybody, ya know?” Ricky, the Wrestling Federation, LOW. Lights Out Wrestling? “Oh!! Indy talent! At least he has some background! This wont be as bad then” “Ricky will you ever learn” Do you know anything about wrestling? I mean hell; you were in a few other places before GCW yourself you know.” “Yeah I know, anyhow, got anything else for me or is that it?” “That’s all I have for now Ricky. Remember, The Vor”.I mean “The Violence” Vince Jones. If you want to scout him, the best place to start is old footage from LOW. I have some here if?.” “Ok, thanks for calling, I’m gonna grab some food, and head over to the beach I think. Talk to ya later.” Before the voice from the other end of the line can say a word Ricky has hung up the phone. He tosses the phone on the counter and swings the refrigerator door open. He stares for a minute then grabs a bottle of water. He turns the label outwards and cracks a huge smile. “Zephyrhills water! The most refreshing water there is!” Ricky then takes a huge drink from the bottle and caps the lid back onto it. He sits the bottle on the table and grabs the phone from the counter again. He punches in a few numbers when a voice picks up. “Thank you for calling Zephyrhills bottling company, how may I direct your call?” “Yeah, can you get me to someone in your marketing department?” “Excuse me?” “Marketing, advertising and the likes” “Oh, I’m sorry sir but we don’t have a Marketing department per se” “WHAT”! Well you should! I wanna do a commercial for you guys? “Well sir, we have a typical paper advertisement, but no commercials or endorsement type deals.” “Well I wanna have my picture taken with the water then!” “Our executives handle that sir. They recruit who will”. “have their picture taken with the water”.” “What do I have to do to get noticed then because this stuff is super sweets!” “Well sir, normally someone from corporate will contact you, but if you are that serious, you could pay for a photo shoot form your own pocket and mail/email the pictures to us” “Then I will do that! Where do I send them?” Ricky jots down some information, and thanks the woman on the other end of the line before hanging up the phone and moving it back to the charger. He grabs his bottle of water from the table and his keys from the counter top. He heads out the front door of his apartment and to the driveway, where his 1967 cherry red mustang sits. He approaches the car and runs his hand across the hood smiling from ear to ear. He then jumps in the rivers seat and fires up the ignition and pulls out of the driveway. “We’re sorry, you have activated out inventory control system, please return to the nearest register” Ricky stops on a dime and looks down at the bag he is carrying. He turns around and a guy wearing a dark blue vest is standing there. “Excuse me sir, may I have a look in your bag?” “Sure.” Ricky hands him the bag, and the man starts to rummage through it. He pulls out a pack of AA batteries, and smiles. He runs them across the closest demagnetizer, and returns them to the bag. He pulls out a white envelope, with some picture proofs stapled to the front. “I don’t think those set off the alarm” The man tosses them back into the bag and hands it back to Ricky. Ricky passes through the door quietly this time and heads out across the parking lot. “Man, some people are pretty nosey” Ricky speaks quietly to himself. Ricky’s cell phone starts chirping away in his pocket, as he heads to his car. Ricky fetches the phone and checks the caller ID prior to answering it. “You got the Rickstah! Talk to me” “Hey Ricky its Jimmy. I was just wondering do you have an agent?” “An agent” For what? I mean, hell I got signed by myself, and I am in the middle of closing a HUGE endorsement.” “Really” With who?” “A local beverage company. So I don’t think I really need an agent” “Well I was thinking Ricky, that maybe you do, and don’t quite no it yet.” “How would I not know it” I mean I am on top of everything? “Ricky, when I called you this morning, you had no clue who you were facing, you had no clue what LOW was, and now you claim to have an endorsement deal in the works.” “Well I’m a little wet behind the ears still, but I am getting there.” “I assume by “wet behind the ears” you mean you are at the beach already?” “Of course not, I do more then hang out at the beach ya know” Ricky hops into his car and tosses the bag in the passenger seat, and heads down the road while still on his phone. “Ricky, I’m not tryin to get you for your money, I have plenty of that. What I do want is to make sure you don’t get in over your head. I’m will to represent you if you need it. I have run feds, I have promoted feds, I have recruited, I have endorsed, I have been an agent, I have done it all. Now I am extending an offer to you because, well you are a friend” “Well Jimmy I appreciate it, but let me think about it ok?” “Sure no problem Ricky. So have you bothered looking into your opponent for this week?” “I went by the library” Ricky lies “but couldn’t find anything on a “Vortex” in LOW? “Damn it Ricky, I said “The Violence”, didn’t you listen at all?” “Ohh! No wonder I didn’t see anything. I will check into again in a bit. I have a meeting set up for today that I can’t miss, so let me get back to ya ok” I need to call and confirm.” “Meeting huh” That’s good. I’m glad you’re taking this seriously. If you need anything don’t hesitate to give me a call.” “Gotcha, take it easy man. Hope your day isn’t as full as mine” The two men share a small laugh as Ricky hangs up his cell phone and smiles at the road sign “Daytona Beach 32 miles”
