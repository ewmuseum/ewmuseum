The Emma Situation

2 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/02/07 It has been two years since Jimmy had been a member of the winner’s circle, which in normal terms would have been a nonchalant experience for him but after two years away from the ring, Jimmy was feeling rather surreal about the whole experience. Coming back to wrestling was something Jimmy tried to avoid at all costs, but after leaving the arena with a victory under his belt against Stephen Adams in his debut match at GCW, Jimmy is now looking forward to the Battle Royal in Ohio. The thing Jimmy is starting to realise is that perhaps it wasn’t so much the wrestling ring that he was fiercely avoiding, but it is now starting to dawn on him that he was actually running from the fear of failure. Jimmy, for the most part of his career, he has always been put on a pedestal. That kind of perception comes with spending the majority of your career with some gold strapped around your waist. What amazes him the most is that after the referee slammed his hand down on the mat for the three count against Stephen Adams, it was as if the dark clouds of self doubt that surrounded him, for the better part of his time away from the ring, had disappeared, and the warm sun of confidence shone on him once again. Perhaps knowing that his his son Jimmy Junior and his best friend Theo were supporting his return to the ring, is the main reason what brought back Jimmy’s brimming confidence, none the less, Jimmy didn’t really care because his confidence is back and so is he, and at the moment, that’s all he cares about. The Day After World Wide 60 Jimmy wakes to a piercing alarm from the clock radio on the bedside stand next to him. He tries to reach out to it by rolling on his right side, which is thwarted by a sharp pain rushing through the left side of his rib cage. As he turns the alarm off, the pain is a realistic reminder to Jimmy that he grossly lacks ring conditioning, and that is an aspect to his in-ring repertoire that heeds some serious attention. The only training he can do to counter that is to compete week in and week out, till his body is hardened for in-ring combat. No matter how often he wrestles, Jimmy will always wake the following morning with a degree of stiffness. In the past he would treat himself to a massage to soothe his aching muscles, but while Jimmy is on a budget that is thinner than a starving Ethiopian, all he could do is rely on a hot shower and some intense stretching inside his hotel room. Accustomed to large, spacious five star rooms, Jimmy has to endure his new reality which is a shoe box sized room that only the modern comforts of a three star hotel can provide. With the hot water blasting on his weary shoulders, Jimmy then soaks his head and lets the hot water hit his muscular chest. As the steaming hot water trickles down his body at a rapid pace, so does Jimmy’s thoughts of his immediate future. Finding himself in isolation from any forms of distractions, Jimmy uses the time to put his future plans into a better perspective. Now that his career has kick started once again, the question that lingers for Jimmy is that does he take the money to pay his debts then run, or does he bust his ass and accept a challenge that only a select few have had the privilege of achieving, that being a GCW World Heavyweight Champion. Hundreds before Jimmy have all signed a GCW contract sharing the same aspirations, yet fall by the way side. Every now and then a wrestler comes along with that has the rare ability to push himself to reach those heights and become a World Champion, which in Layman’s terms means being the elite, the best of the best. Knowing full well that you don’t just wake up one day and tell yourself that you’re going to be a World Champion one day, Jimmy strongly believes in the fact that this honour is only earned after you have driven down are torturous road, filled with copious amounts of blood, sweat, tears and taking it one step at a time, one match at time, day by day. Another realisation to Jimmy’s changed lifestyle occurred after he checked out of his hotel room. Normally there would be an awaiting limo to pick him up from his hotel suite, not today. Jimmy makes his way to a taxi stand around the corner from his hotel, at the same time; a voluptuous brunette arrives from the opposite direction carrying the same amount of baggage as Jimmy. The first thing that he notices about the woman is her piercing green eyes that shined when she smiled. Her breath taking appearance immediately told Jimmy that she was a woman who certainly knew how to take care of herself. “Waiting for a taxi?” Jimmy nervously asked. “Yes, I am.” The woman responded. “If I was a betting man I’d be willing to say that you’re heading to the airport. Am I right” Jimmy asked confidently with a hint of sarcasm. Equally as sarcastic, she replies with “now what would give you that impression?” “Lucky guess I suppose.” He answers. “And I’m willing to guess that you’re heading to the airport too.” The woman asked with a smile. “Yeah, yeah I am.” Jimmy replied as her attractiveness is responsible for his stuttered answer. A taxi suddenly arrives as the woman and Jimmy both start feeling comfortable with each other’s presence. The driver hops out and asks “Is this fare for one or both of you?” The woman turns to Jimmy and asks “do you want to split the fare?” “Sounds like a good idea to me.” Jimmy enthusiastically replied. The woman climbed into the taxi with Jimmy eagerly following behind her. After buckling in his seat belt Jimmy informs the driver to take them to the airport. “What’s you’re name?” Jimmy nervously asked as he extends his hand to the woman. “Emma.” She replied with a warm smile as she shook Jimmy’s hand. In turn, he replied with a big smile “Nice to meet you Emma, I’m Jimmy.” "I'm heading out to Boise later this afternoon." Emma announced. "You're kidding!" Jimmy instantly replied. “I’m going there too!” He added Now that Jimmy has her intrigued, she asks “Are you flying with South West Airlines?” “As a matter of fact I am.” He replies with some confidence in his tone. Shocked by their similarities, Emma leans back into the seat and lets out a gasp of disbelief as she says “this is really bizarre.” “Or it’s fortuitous, depending from which angle you look at things.” Jimmy added. “Are you going for an in particular reason?” Jimmy asked. “My sister lives there with her family.” She replied. “How about you?” She then asked. “I live there.” Jimmy quickly said. “With my son, that is.” He added before Emma had a chance to ask him. The cab pulled up out the front of the departure terminal where both Jimmy and Emma enter carrying their respective luggage. Normally Jimmy would be on his own at the airport but having a companion is something excitingly new for him. Not since the death of his wife has Jimmy even entertained the thought of spending some quality time with another woman. His devotion to his wife extended beyond her untimely demise, but when Jimmy retired from wrestling he had come to the conclusion that even though he closed the chapter on one aspect of his life, he felt it was time to open another by treading into the dating scene again, if the right woman came along. The only forces that are against Jimmy at the moment would be his financial situation. Even if Jimmy wanted to ask Emma out on a date, at the moment the best he could do is peanut butter sandwich in his car while listening to the Elvis channel on Sirius Satellite Radio. That’s definitely not the best way to make a lasting impression on a woman that he was becoming increasingly attracted to. Jimmy and Emma boarded their flight with Jimmy sitting next to the window. Being a nervous flier, Jimmy always found it most comfortable having a window seat. Every time he experienced turbulence, Jimmy would put all his fears at easy by peering out the window to make sure the wing was in tact and if the flight attendants were going along their business as usual. It’s not too long until the pilot asks for the cabin crew to prepare for take off. That was Jimmy’s queue to transform from his charming, jovial front to a complete nervous wreck. The turbines begin to rumble from the pilot igniting both engines to maximum power so to ensure a successful take off. The rumbling engines send cold chills down Jimmy’s back as the plane suddenly thrusts forward, sending Jimmy deep into his seat as the pilot sling shots the aircraft down the runway. With a new nervous sweat drop forming on his shining forehead with every bump he feels, Jimmy’s stomach starts to turn as his body leans backwards slightly from the aircraft’s nose lifting into the windy air. His heart skips a beat with every air pocket the aircraft hits which feels like the plane is hitting rocks as it rapidly climbs to its flying altitude. Once the aircraft levels out and the plane is flying smoothly, the flight attendants jump out of their seat and straight into their designated duties as if they were on a time trial. While the hype of the take off slowly subdues, so does Jimmy’s anxiety, he looks down at his hand which is tightly clenching Emma’s hand without him realising. He looks at Emma, who in turn finds his reactions to the take off rather amusing and smiles at him. Embarrassed by it all Jimmy lets go of Emma’s hand and smiles back apologetically about his whole ordeal. “Not comfortable with flying I see.” Emma said as she tries her hardest not to giggle at Jimmy. “Not at all.” Jimmy quickly replied as he swallowed in an attempt to calm his nerves. “HA HA HA!!! This is so funny.” Emma added as she giggled. Feeling like his ego is being deflated; Jimmy huffed and gazed out the window for a while. Emma smirks as she leaves Jimmy to his own devices. Emma is the quintessential all American girl who grew up with her family and their pet dog in a house bordered by a white picket fence in a typically quiet Kansas neighbourhood. She looked wholesome and had the personality to match, which is what Jimmy was attracted to immediately. It wasn’t too long until Jimmy overcame his ego problem and engaged in a lengthy discussions with Emma, mainly discovering each other’s traits and vices. It was clearly apparent to both that there was a connection between them but the biggest problem they faced was who would be the one to have the nerve to act upon their feelings. Jimmy was nervous after spending an extended period from the dating scene and his confidence in his skills with women wouldn’t be on the same level as his confidence in throwing an opponent over the top rope this weekend. Once the plane had touched down in Boise, Jimmy and Emma made their way to the baggage carousel and collected their luggage. Emma handed Jimmy her phone number and asked him to call her sometime. All Jimmy could do was smile but when she kissed him on the cheek before leaving, you could have knocked him over with a feather. No matter what happened from that moment on, nothing was going to ruin Jimmy’s day, especially after meeting Emma.
