The Lexicon Saga: Catalyst

16 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16/05 You wouldn't believe me if I told you, the smell of burnt flesh. It's the one horror of war that sticks with me the strongest. An unrelated odour on the breeze can trigger memories of the first time I witnessed men slaughtering each other. I was just an apprentice then. It was just the first battle of many. I wonder if Lexicon is afflicted by the same psychosis... The Lexicon Saga Created by Robert Kristian Footsteps echo ominously down the long hallway, vaulted ceiling arching high overhead. Fires in their wall sconces crackle and dazzle merrily, illuminated the tapestries commissioned by past gifted ones to commemorate their greatness. The shackles and belts, buckles and buttons, jingled and jangled as Lexicon approached the great double doors at the end of the hall of fame. His long blue cape sweeping the floor behind him he presses two hands firmly against the doors and throws them wide. Straddling the entrance his glare sweeps past the gathered audience to the figure at the head of the table. The Emperor himself gathers the ropes about his knees in one hand and rises from his seat. 'Why was I not invited to council?' Lexicon's voice rings loudly throughout the large room. Out of date armor and shields adorning the walls and corners. 'Excuse me?' Barxalot's reply comes coldly. 'By birth I am entitled to sit at council.' 'You have no voice nor power here Lexicon.' 'You've never denied me my place at this table before.' His tone changes slightly. 'You have never been under suspicion until now.' 'Suspicion of what?' 'For inciting war within my lands!' Barxalot screams suddenly. 'For rallying my own people against me!' 'I had nothing-' 'Get out of my court Lexicon, get out and never presume to return! Your innocence or guilt will be dealt with as I see fit.' Lexicon's face never wavers, his mouth shut and his eyes steady beneath the wrath of his emperor. It lasts. The silence. For a long time. Finally Lexicon blinks and looks around at the twenty or so odd non-gifted nobles. He then turns to look at the armored behemoth that steps to his right. 'Just leave Lex, I don't want to-' 'You don't want to what?' Rooks hand falls to the hilt of his sword. Lexicon's jaw flexes once as he appears to contemplate his chances. 'Damn gifted ones think they can do anything they want.' The human's voice drifts beyond the circle and into Lexicon's ears, as he passes the open doors, a grin comes to his lips. 'I said nothing.' Lestatt's unmistakable accent drips with skepticism. Between him and Lexicon, both wearing simple leather tunics and cotton pants, is a map. And upon this map is set up a series of intricately carved peices of seige equipment and soldiers, archers, infantry, and cavalry. 'Zodiac-' 'No.' Lexicon cuts him off. 'She has nothing to gain, she is in the fools graces, her chance to wear the belt is guaranteed.' 'Nobody has seen her since...' Lestatt's voice drops. 'I have scouts looking.' Lex's voice drips with impatience and he steps away from the table. 'She is the only one to get away, she has to have the belt.' 'But she can't rightly claim it until she's defeated Draven.' 'Which she can't do because we killed him.' 'Bravo.' Lestatt grins. 'You planned this perfectly.' Sarcasim. 'Enough, we have bigger things to worry about. Any minute now they could come knocking at my door with a witness we missed.' 'They'll stretch your neck for sure.' 'Bah!' Lexicon shouts at Lestatt's lack of compassion, turning away he crosses his arms in frustration. On second thought. He spins around to face Lestatt. 'You're in this as much as me. Like it or not we have to work together or they'll stretch both our necks and I have a feeling that's gonna be really uncomfortable.' Lestatt sneers and looks to the chess peices, moving a peice he leans both hands on the table. Lexicon moves his own peice lightning quick and sets back to pacing as Lestatt, unphased and expectant of such mulls over his options. 'So.' Knight says. 'We find her, you fight her, I witness, and we end this entire ordeal nice and quiet, right?' Meanwhile Head dropping to the earth the exhausted dappled mare struggles on wobbling legs to stand back up. The burden too great it collapses, rider pitched to ground gracelessly. 'No!' Zodiac's voice cracks, scrambling towards her horse. 'No please don't quit on me baby, we're so close to home. You can drink, and eat, and rest, I promise you, but you have to work for me a little longer.' She pleads to no avail. She stands and the golden belt catches a ray of the dying sun and sparkles. Thick gray clouds pull across the sky like curtains, threatening to release their deluge any moment. The air takes a chill crisp, one that's turned Zodiac's lips blue and skin a pale shade of it's former beauty. Her green eyes flicker suddenly. Breath pours from her mouth in a light fog, a branch crackles, the silver bow on her shoulder comes loose. She leans over to draw an arrow from her saddle quiver in a single swift sided moment she nocks, draws back, and releases the bolt. Like a hammer pounding a nail the silver bolt takes the first man to break the bush line in the chest. Her second takes his fellow. She draws another and circles, they come from all sides. She drops to a knee beside her fallen beast and fires a shot, a scream, she turns her bow cockeyed and spins back into a standing position three arrows nocked and fire. Two men fall to their backs dead before they meet the earth, but the third survives to swing an upward slash. Zodiac leaps back to avoid and drops the bow. The man stabs forward with his blade, Zodiac dances out of it's way and piroettes close. Her armoured elbow catches his chin, his grip on the sword fails and Zodiac has it. As she spins away a macabre arc of blood hangs in the air, in the same swing she deflects the assault of another sword and breaks the defense before a halberd armed templar can bring his mighty weapon to bear. A soldier charges, she fakes left, breaks right and chops the sword down into the man's ankle as he over compensates his momentum. Breaking into a run she's chased by an arrow that finds nothing but dirt. Meanwhile 'So the challenge for the belt must be publicly accepted to be validated?' Lestatt queries. 'Of course.' 'This is not so different than other lands.' 'And of course, she is not the rightful holder of the belt.' 'So we are presented with a problem.' Lestatt moves his little archer diagonally and takes Lexicon's white seige tower. 'For every problem.' Lexicon moves his infantry to take Lestatt's cavalry. 'There is a solution. Check.' Lestatt sneers at the chess board and leans over it in thought. 'If we just had the belt, we could pursuade the emperor to open a tournament.' 'The emperor is open to no persuasion from the likes of you.' Lestatt counters mildly. 'I have no voice in his court, but I have alliances among the gifted.' Meanwhile Zodiac runs, her legs pumping furiously and kicking up dirt in her trail. Specks of arrows arc high in the air, shimmering beautifully for a moment, like shooting stars. They descend with savage velocity, Zodiac spins and a trio of 'tings' sound as she deflects the bolts away from her body. A dozen lines of blood stain the groun and a one slams into her shoulder guard like a poke from god. She screams and falls into a seated position, the sword limp in her fingers. Gorgeous green eyes scan the heavens as hoofbeats approach her bleeding form quickly. Meanwhile Lestatt slides his infantry into position. 'Besides, if you show up with the belt it's as good as admitting guilt.' 'Indeed.' Lexicon moves his remaining seige tower across the board. 'Check mate.' 'I hate playing this game with you.' Lestatt mumbles. Meanwhile The first rider approaches, a net dragging along the ground behind him. Zodiac's eyes fall from the sky slowely, emotionless, cold. The bounty hunter pulls his net up and hurls it towards the injured warrioress. Muscles explode, silver flashes as Zodiac splits the net and dives forward with a cross body spin slash, the riders foot falls from his leg. Zodiac draws the sword up and back down to drive the point of a lance into the earth, vaulting it's rider into the air as Zodiac flies into his place. Meanwhile 'So it's true what they say about you.' Lestatt tips his king over. 'There's nothing you wont do.' Rising from his seat Lestatt levels Lexicon with the most serious of glares. 'I'll not spark a rebellion in your name, for a god damned belt.' Lestatt snaps. 'This isn't about a belt anymore, this is about our rights as humans.' 'We're gifted, we're better than all of this.' 'Says who?' Lexicon counters. 'I was born of un-gifted parents, because I am stronger and faster means that I can't lead my own people?' 'You hide your true intentions with lies, you have no more desire to indulge in pheasantry and hard work than I have to be hung for your crimes.' 'Our crimes.' Lexicon reminds. 'It wasn't I who slaughtered women and children.' Lestatt's lips curl into a sneer. The door flies open and both sets of eyes tear away from each other to look at a breathless young lad. 'What is it?' Lexicon demands. 'Zodiac.' 'What about her? Have they caught her trail?' 'She's here!' The first drops of rain descend from heaven as Lexicon takes to the front steps of his manor, the broad stair case stretching out before him and onto the cobble stone road. Soldiers and guards all gather close but a single hand waves them off and Lexicon hurries down the stairs. Lestatt emerges from the house a moment later, his hood left down as he stands with the servants to spectate. Zodiac storms up the road in a flurry, a blaring clang sounds as she deflects a cross bow bolt with the blade of her sword. Lexicon raises his hands as she draws closer, her mounted pursuers pulling up their beasts and coming to a stop. Zodiac's horse stops with a lowered head as Zodiac lands soundlessly, her eyes steady on Lexicon. She moves to him quickly, the weapon gripped tight. A guard cries out. She swings and Lexicon back pedals, she slices and chops and thrusts. Lexicon, ducks and weaves and dodges his way towards the stairs. 'Is it a fight with me you want?' Zodiac snaps 'Or would you rather slaughter innocents?' She punctuates her word with another swing, wincing at the pain in her injured shoulder she slips, Lexicon steps on the blade, his kick is blocked against Zodiac's bracers and she backs away. Lexicon toes the blade against the stair and the handle slaps into his palm smoothly before leveling the blade between him and Zodiac. 'So where exactly do we go from here?' Lexicon queries. 'I've killed for it once, I might do it again.' His voice is low. They start to circle. 'To hold it is nothing, it's how you earn it.' Lexicon grins. 'Take the belt and leave.' 'What?' Zodiac snaps. 'Take it and go, get off of my land and tell everyone what you know, what you've seen.' Zodiac glares at him, the light drizzle turning into larger drops that pelt against the warrioress's armor. 'Have you gone mad? What have I to gain to see you die.' 'If the emperor kills me before we meet again, he just might save you the trouble.' 'What are you talking about.' She frowns in confusion, and maybe, hurt. 'When next I come for that belt.' He speaks low, leaning close. 'I'll kill everyone I have to. I will earn it in blood.' 'You ruthless bastard.' Zodiac backs away towards her newly aquired mount. 'She killed six men!' A men protests. 'And injured three more!' A second soldier adds his voice. 'Let her leave.' Lexicon demands. 'Where's Rook these days?' A quiet voice queries. 'Rook be damned!' Lexicon shouts, the rain coming down harder. 'The emperor weilds that man as his puppet, he cares not for us out here. It doesn't matter to him if your family eats at night.' A dissadent murmur spreads throughout the crowd. Lexicon watches Zodiac's horse charge down the road through the building tempo of rain. Lestatt moves towards the door as Lexicon climbs the stairs to his mansion. His face an expressionless mask, he watches the cowed people part for their employer, their self appointed leader. Lexicon assumes to brush past his guest, but Lestatt falls into step beside him. 'That was quite a show.' The doors slam shut and Lexicon turns on Lestatt. 'With me. Or against me?' 'What are you talking about?' 'The emperor will send an army, I need to know who my enemies are and where my allies stand.' 'You're talking about war Lexicon, I'm no general.' 'People will follow you, they'll follow your strength, your courage.' Lestatt looks away. 'You ask too much, the cost of your quest is too great. I cannot be a part of this.' Lexicon raises a hand and Lestatt reacts instantly. But it's too late. 'I had a feeling you might think that way.' Lexicon grins. 'And I can't afford anymore mistakes.' The four armed men move in on Lestatt fast, he tries to fight but unarmed he is no match. 'Meet The Anarchs.' Lexicon moves close to Lestatt, forced to kneel with his hands behind his back. 'You should have learned from all those games of chess Lestatt. I'm always one move ahead of you.' 'I'll kill you.' Lestatt begins. 'Promises, promises.' Lex cuts in, leaning in close. 'You know it's funny, in Draven's last moments, he said the exact same thing...' Fades to his grin.
