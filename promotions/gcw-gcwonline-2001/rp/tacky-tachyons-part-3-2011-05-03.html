Tacky-Tachyons Part 3

3 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/03/11 Brad: "What do you want?" "I want what you clearly want, but for myself." Brad: "Fine, come with me." "It’s not that simple" Brad: "It never is, but we can work something out." "Alright, I want to learn as much as I can about you." Brad: "Alright…" "Make it through that… and I’ll consider giving you the emitter." Brad: "…and if I don’t make it through?" The mysterious voice begins to chuckle. "Then you stay here… and I go, in your body." Bradley stands still seemly the middle of nowhere, in-between dimensional plains as the words spoken by the "Mysterious Voice" hit him like a freight train. This creature wanted possession of his body and to learn everything about Brad that he could. Cruz couldn’t help but think for a moment that he may never see home again. His eyes wander probing the dull dirt filled landscape once again, but this time a slight sense of déjà vu gives Bradley the feeling that he’s been in this place before. Bradley shakes his mind clear; he has no time to analyze these feelings now. Bradley had to focus his mind on getting out of this place, body intact. Brad turns his attention back the Mysterious Voice while acting cavalier about the whole situation. Brad: "Let me get this straight… you want my body?" The Mysterious voice simply replies. "Yes." Brad: "Well, get in line! You think you’re the first to ‘Crave the Cruz’? Of course not, do you know why?" "No." Bradley flexes his muscles. Brad: "Quality! Be honest, what was the first thing you noticed?" "I beg your pardon?" Brad flexes his arms. Brad: "You know, on my body. Was it my powerful biceps?" Cruz runs his hand down his torso like a washboard. Brad: "Was it my sultry abs? These aren’t situational, there here all the time." Brad profiles his face to the left and then to the right. Brad: "Could it have been my classically rendered features?" "I’m afraid not." Brad: "I know what it was!" Bradley reaches down pulling up his pant leg exposing his leg. Brad: "It’s my calves! I know, I know their perfect, and if I were a gentleman horse rider in Spain back in the day, they would be throwing all sorts ladies and land at me, all because of these bad boys. Which would be cool, but the tradeoff of having to speak in Spanish with that funny little lisp would be way too much for me to bear." "Your external appearance bared little influence on my decision." Brad: "Well that’s good. I don’t want you to think my body is "COMPLETELY" perfect. I mean it has its ascetic appeal and deserves a place amongst the gods, but it really does have its weaknesses." "…such as?" Brad thinks a moment. Brad: "Olives, my body is inexplicably repulsed by olives." "Is there anything else?" Brad: "Oh so much more. I can’t carry a tune, peanut butter mucks up my complexion, I watched the movie "Deep Blue Sea" twice …so there has to long term damage there, and I’m allergic to polyester… well not really allergic… more morally repulsed by polyester. Then there's the addiction..." "Really? Addicted to what?" Brad: "Frappuccinos..." "That doesn’t seem too serious." Brad: "Well I haven’t told you the worst of it, there’s this … thermal … exhaust port … and it’s only two meters wide, and if you shoot it… it’ll cause this chain reaction and BOOM!!!" "Two meters you say?" Brad: "Yeah, two meters… no bigger than a whomp-rat." "Are you lying to me?" Brad’s eyes dart to the left, to the right, and quickly down. Like a child hiding something form his mother. Brad: "No…" Bradley’s head slumps forward as he sighs. Brad: "Yes… what tipped me off?" "Your anatomy does not allow for an opening that large. I did for a moment consider your mouth, but it only just sounds abnormally big." Brad: "W-" "Enough! No more diversions and meaningless ramblings. It has been decided. I will study you rigorously and should you "survive the process" I will surrender the emitter, but if you are lost then your body is forfeit. We have established the parameters and concessions so let us be-" Bradley abruptly interrupts. Brad: "What concessions? You must be talking hotdogs and sodas because you haven’t compromised or yielded a damn thing." "If you feel that I’m not being fair, then by all means state your terms." Bradley ponders a moment before speaking. Something comes to him and he makes his first demand. Brad: "You don’t lay a hand on me!" "Agreed, I had no intention to come into ACTUAL physical contact. The physical aspect is only a small part of this. It’s something deeper, where the lines of mind, body, and soul are blurred… where these fragments become whole, and they surrender their beginning and end. It's difficult to explain." Brad: "Well whatever that is, I’m sure that Hallmark has card for it." "Is there anything else?" Sudden a though surges through Brad’ head like a bolt lightening and quickly fires it out. Brad: "Yeah, there is one thing. I’m sick of this nameless faceless omnipresent voice looming over me. If we’re to go any further I need to see and know who I’m speaking to." "So you require a visual presents. That is understandable. I will manifest myself into a suitable form, of your choosing." Brad raises and eyebrow inquisitively. Brad: "How do we do that?" "Choose a likeness, place an image in your mind, and concentrate on it. I will do the rest." Bradley closes his eyes probing his own mind for a suitable likeness, but really can’t think of one. His mind wanders a bit when suddenly the corners of his mouth lift revealing a mischievous smile. Bradley squeezes his eyes nice and tight concentrating and forming a picture in his head. Gradually particles begin to form around the head of Brad slowly orbiting around his cranium like a halo. The particles glow looking like fireflies running in a circle, gradually they speed up forming almost a pure beam of light. They swirl and swirl until finally they shootout merging into a large surge of light and energy at least ten yards away from Bradley. Brad opens in his eyes as the energy slowly begins to fade revealing only the figure of a man. The man just stands there looking over his body up and down with wonderous curiosity. "Fascinating… I feel… I feel… everything. I knew you humans were a knotted ball on nerves, but those things don’t mess around." The Mysterious Man gazes into his own hands marveling at them, like a collector fawns over his life’s obsession. "Amazing… these hands are strong enough to build and maintain a society and yet graceful enough to create beautiful pieces of art, how inspired." Brad: "Yeah there nice…" "Nice? Do you not fully appreciate their grace? There are beings eons older then the human race that never evolved anything remotely this sophisticated. Some make due with a mangled mess of claws and tentacles." The Mysterious man makes a upward gesture with his hand and out through the dirt springs up a large full length mirror held up by a foundation of dirt. The Mysterious Man looks himself over from head to toe. He begins touching and grouping his own face trying to get a feel for it and then smiles trying to get a good look at his teeth. "This body will do nicely. Is it modeled from someone you know?" Brad: "Sort of… I almost chose Star Man from Nintendo's Pro Wrestling, but he's dimensionally challenged, so I chose the actor John de Lancie. In Star Trek: the next Generation he played an omnipotent being called ‘Q’. He was a snarky ‘know-it-all’ and a pain in the ass, filled with half baked philosophies and delusions of moral superiority, sound familiar?" The Mysterious Man turns to Brad and gives him smile. "You flatter me. In fact in honor of the man I will take the name of… ‘M’. It’s short and memorable." ‘M’ looks back the mirror fussing over his white suit. M: "Now that I’ve conceded to your terms, I don’t mean to be a impatient but I believe it’s time we begin." Brad’s face is stern as he stands up straight showing that he’s not afraid. He prepares himself for whatever comes next. He grits his teeth and sharply nods yes. M glances over to Cruz with a chuckle. M: "Oh come on, don’t be so stern about it." With a quick gesture ‘M’ lifts Bradley off his feet levitating him three feet off the ground. Brad resists but with other quick motion ‘M’ peels Bradley’s arms and head back, holding him in a pose that resembles a scarecrow being hung on a fence. Brad can no longer struggle against the Mysterious Being’s hold. M: "I’m excited; it’s like peeling back the pages of a much anticipated novel. I can’t wait just submerge myself into its contents. Reading away drawing closer to its climatic conclusion, all while fighting the urge to cheat and skip to the end to see what happens, but I assure you, I plan to take my time as I dissect your past and mind, so with that being said... let’s start with the beginning." To be continued…
