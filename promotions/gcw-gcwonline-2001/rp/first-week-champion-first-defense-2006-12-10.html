First week Champion.. First Defense

10 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.10.06 I had forgotten how much I did not trust the opposite sex. Well maybe it was my own fault; maybe I shouldn’t have trusted Rufio. I mean a man I thought to be my friend was nothing but like every other pig headed man on this planet. Out for only one thing in life, well that might be all well and fine for him but for me there is a bigger meaning, there is a better reason I have been put on this planet. Well last week while Rufio was probably putting the moves on my girl once again I was capturing the Hardcore Title. I took the title from a man who no longer deserved it I beat him fair and square and showed GCW that unlike my counterpart, Rufio, I am not here for just fun and games I am here for business. This time last week I was sitting here preparing for my chance to get into the Hardcore Title scene. A match I adamantly was not expecting to get involved in but when given the chance I was not going to let it go down. So I enter the week looking forward, my girlfriend was coming to see me wrestle, and I have a title match. But upon my arrival to the arena I find something out that I didn’t want to find. I had entrusted my friend and at the same time entrusted the girl I was calling my girlfriend. After being in the arena for no more then forty minutes I was able to find out that the girl the supposedly loved me had cheated on me with someone who had been my friend as of late, if not my best friend. This is the point I lost, I couldn’t take it anymore the choice had been made, I gave both of them a second chance but the simple fact of the matter is I got screwed over again. With everything that was going on that night I was still able to take advantage of a beat Virgil Amrbosi. This week I am quickly entered into a rebound match. I have taken the win against the champion and declared myself the new champion of the hardcore title. But I guess being a champion isn’t always easy. This week I am forced to take on a new comer someone who has yet to prove himself, Barty Bale. I spent my time working my way up the ranks. I won my share of matches and I won’t lie I lost my share of matches. Barty, you haven’t and for some unforeseen reason the booking committee has decided you are a worthy opponent for me. I however do not think you are but that is not my decision. You will have to step into the ring with me and learn the hard way. I see that the Booking Committee does not like the idea of me holding the title for long, the various emotional and physical struggles I faced last week have put an extreme amount of pressure on my body. I have lost the will to fight every week. I don’t want to sacrifice my body week in and week out but that is something I am going to have to do for the betterment of GCW. Barty Bale you are going to made into a example, I am going to show you and the booking committee that you must earn a title shot vs, Peter Vetra not be given one. Barty you are one lucky man you are getting me when my spirits are down, you getting me when the proverbial phrase “Down and Out” is in take. I may have one the title last week, but at the same time I am drained from the humiliation I suffered at the hands of Lindsay, the feelings I had for her were genuine. But the point is still that I was betrayed by a female once again. Barty Bale, I am sorry but you are about to get the repercussions of their actions. My first week as Hardcore Champion, and I am going to make your first Hardcore Match here be a memorable one. Well at least by DVD and television because when I am done with you, you won’t remember the pain I inflicted on you.
