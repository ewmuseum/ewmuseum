A Knight's Tale: Kingdom of Darkness

1 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1 Oct 05 Bloodied, bruised, and battered I remained on one knee trying to figure out what was to come next. I have thrown everything I can think of at this man and yet he continues to stand, to fight, to claw. He moves again pushing himself to his hands and knees, slowly his head looks upward, those eyes piercing into me. Using my knee for leverage I now push myself to a standing position. My body is weak but I can’t show that to him, never show weakness. “Say the words Michael ? it is not worth this kind of pain.” “Never!” It was the answer that I expected but had hoped for the opposite. I watch in some kind of amazement as he pushes himself to his feet. Normally this is when I would attack, go for the kill but if anyone deserves to finish this on his feet its this man. In my historic reign on top, this warrior has stepped up and challenged my authority. Sure plenty of others have done the same but they easily were ripped apart and broken down. But not him. For some reason no matter what I do, he just comes back for more. I defeated him once already, yet he has come back again. Now I have punished him in ways that only a very few of my nemesis have felt. Yet he stands in front of me, wobbly legs, bloody face, and glazed over eyes and all. Maybe I would never say it out loud but he has earned my respect. The bad part about that is there is no room for two Kings in this world ? respect means that he is a threat to my reign and that means he must perish. And PERISH he shall. He charges me with a wild right hand but swiftly I duck under it, bringing a quick uppercut up into Steven’s ribs which automatically brings his body falling to its side to cover up the exposed area. With my right hand I rise up and hit a right cross punch that connects with Steven’s jaw, forcing him back and onto his back. He lands in the dirt and rock that rest beneath us with a loud thud. I could hear the air escape his lungs with the impact. I move towards him wrapping both of my hands around his throat then lifting with all my strength, bringing him to a standing position. Michael brings his own hands up and wraps them around my wrists, to try and lessen the choke hold’s impact. I reach down deep, pulling out more strength to lift Steven’s off his feet. I could feel the fire in my eyes burning. “SAY IT MICHAEL! OR YOU DIE!” He didn’t answer with words, instead he simply spit blood in my face. The anger now was ready to explode, like a volcano erupting. With an animalistic growl that even surprised me I toss Steven’s to the side, his body lands and again kicks up dirt. I scan the battle field, which is nothing more than a rock and dirt peninsula, but instead of water covering all three sides, it’s the eyes of the kingdom. I feel a slight grin spread across my lips as I think about this. I kneel down to Michael’s broken body. “How did you think this was going to end Stevens?” I whispered to him. “Did you think I would allow you to come in here and shed light on my Kingdom of Darkness” Did you really believe that I was ready to relinquish my throne? I am the greatest leader the world has ever seen. You have failed in your quest of ultimate good, face it ? You don’t have what it takes.” “I ? I ?” He tries sputtering out words. “Excuse me Michael?” “I’ll see you in hell.” I smile now, not a grin or a chuckle but a full fledged smile. “You first.” Now I reach down clutching Steven’s by his hair and begin to drag him to the edge. I look down at the Kingdom, which appears to be engulfed in darkness. Their eyes give them away though. They are all looking on, waiting on the outcome of this battle ? their lives hang in the balance. I place one of my hands around the back of Michael’s neck and thrust him forward, to expose him for all of the Kingdom to see. The silence is deafening. “Is THIS what some of you want ruling!” Is this the man you wish to follow? Those of you who willingly followed this man will suffer the same fate that he will. Let this be a warning to all! I am here to stay! Come challenge me if you wish, but if THIS is the best that you have got then you had better only come to me if you wish to be released from your life.” Sometimes you have to make an example, sometimes you get made an example of. I rule with an iron first because these pathetic fools aren’t capable of handling the load that I carry everyday. Some talk big, some act bad but none of them have that killer instinct. To do what is necessary not what always is considered the RIGHT thing, is what separates the great leaders from GODS. Make no mistake, I will be looked upon as a GOD amongst mortal men. But sometimes these men can surprise you. A sharp pain rushes up my side as I felt Stevens send a side kick to my ribs. I couldn’t help but loosen my grip, which gives him a chance to send a fist to the side of my head. I drop Michael now as the ringing in my head begins. Another fist smashes my nose, and I can feel blood begin dripping from it. Before I can counter anything a front kick crashes into my chest and before I know it, I am on my back looking up at the night sky. I could hear the mob below roar in approval, it was unbelievable. They stay in order only because they know of my power but as soon as its threatened they cheer and applaud? Treachery like this cannot be overlooked. It is not my time to lose, Stevens is not the one to bring down the walls of my Castle! I feel Stevens come up on me, quickly I lift a foot up which he runs right into, Michael doubles over. I rock forward and grasp him by the head and flip him over me. Almost immediately we both rush to get to our feet, when we get there Michael goes for a clothesline, I duck it and leap into the air connecting with a vicious roundhouse kick that almost decapitated the man that stood before me. Michael flies backwards landing right on the ledge but the force continues his motion and he stumbles over. His hands latch onto the edge which is the only thing keeping him from falling to his death. I move forward, standing over him watching as a mix of fear and anger fill up his eyes. “Its over Stevens.” I catch my breath now before continuing. “You had your chance, but its just not your time. Don’t worry I will take good care of your disciples, they will bleed for you.” “NO! leave them alone.” “Perhaps” if you say the three words that I want to hear!” His teeth grind together as he fights to muster up the courage. His eyes lower for a moment before again looking up at me, anger now taking over the fear. “I ? surrender.” I close my eyes and take in the scent of the battlefield, the smell of victory. As I open my eyes a bolt of lightning streaks through and momentarily lights up the sky. The sound of thunder booms right before a light rain begins to come down from the heavens. “Stevens just know that you never had a chance of succeeding ? just as these people have no chance of living.” Before he can say another word I send a kick that connected with his face, the impact forcing him to lose his grip. All that is heard are his screams as he falls into the abyss. The rain pours now as I stare out over the edge. Their eyes burned into me and just like Stevens it’s a mix of fear and hatred. Their distain with me is evident, but what else is there for them to do but follow suit? I have won once more, taking another step into cementing my legacy. Just like all of the rest, the blood will be washed away, the wounds will heal, but my VICTORY, my power, and my name will last forever. I dare others to come and challenge me. They can join Stevens in hell. The sound of thunder once again fills the area ? The sound of victory. LONG LIVE ? THE KING!
