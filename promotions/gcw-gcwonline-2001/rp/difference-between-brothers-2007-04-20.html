Difference between brothers

20 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 20th, 2007 Yoshiyuki is sitting alone on the corner of his bed in a shady looking room while soft droplets pulsates and shakes through his mind. It seems to be the near by sink, the sink is all rusted and needs fixing. It seems that he is located in some sort of motel room. Yoshiyuki though seems to have something on his mind. He slowly raises off of the bed and grabs his near by tank-top that has been laying on the floor all night. He puts it on and then continues to trail towards the bathroom. Yoshiyuki stops at the urinal and then he inhales before pissing. Yoshiyuki nods his head to wake himself up. Then Exhaling before flushing the urinal. Thoughts of seize the moment begins to run through his mind. He gets mix reactions as he thinks about his brother Yutaka and his former girlfriend. Yoshiyuki turns the sink and only a few drops comes out of the sink. Yoshiyuki begun to look frustrated and angered. Yoshiyuki with force kicks the sink head. A loud thud is heard and then water began to come out. The water is a bronze colored and doesn’t look drinkable. Yoshiyuki turns the basin off. Yoshiyuki backs up, then charges the basin with force. Kicking the basin, he watches the sink head fly upwards in slow motion he can even read the in graved makers. Which reads "made in Spain". Yoshiyuki picks up the fallen sink head, he re-twist it on to the basin. Turns the knob and then clean not so clear water comes out of the basin. He then drinks some of the water and spits it back out. He lowers his head and then splashes some water on to his face. He turns the basin off and looks into the mirror. The reflection of his father's legendary past is on his mind as he stares motionless into the mirror. Showing a lot of emotion, Yoshiyuki wipes his eyes and heads towards the room once more. His face frowned thanks to the smell of some sort of sewage coming from outside the motel. Walking towards the window as the dim sun rises from behind the mountain area, Yoshiyuki opens the window and stuck his head out. The fisherman also known as the guy who sells Flounder, he rides his bike into the distant. "Eh!" Yoshiyuki yells from out the window area towards the fish seller. The man is districted thanks to Yoshiyuki, the fisherman begin to descend as he hits a dumpster. Yoshiyuki cracks a sneaky grin at the fallen man as if his day is now made. Yoshiyuki's cellphone rings... "Yeah" "Yoshi you ready?" The familiar voice of Yutaka spills through the cellphone. "Give me a few minutes to get my things on. My guess is that you've been hanging around the area lately." "Well i do take my morning stretch along with my exercise. I must stay fit if we are going to compete in various matches every week." "Yeah i hear you Yuka." "Brother get up and meet me in the garden." "Ok my brother" Yoshiyuki hangs the phone up and place it on the charger. Yoshiyuki slides his leather pants, boots, and cap on. "Damn forgot the keys" he shouts with a frustrated look. Yoshiyuki grabs his keys and heads out the door way leaving his room door to close on its own. He's walking down the hallway with his head held high like if he was some sort of god. Yoshiyuki reaches into his pocket and pulls out a pair of sunglasses. Crimson colored sunglasses, he puts them on as he makes it to the lobby of the motel. Yoshiyuki stops at the front desk with a smirk on his face. Knowing him he's got some type of scheme to get out of paying for his hotel expense. Yoshiyuki see a nice looking American couple who doesn't look to familiar with the surroundings. "Hello-How are-You-Guys-Today?" Yoshiyuki saying it in a broken english. "Well we're doing pretty dandy. But i got this 3 nights free coupon but i don't know when or how to use it. Do you have any ideas when to use it? Before or after the night?" said one of the spouse of the couple. "Yes-Give-it-to me-and i will help you on your way" "Sure" "Thank you, We'll be with you shortly" Yoshiyuki takes the coupon away from the couple. "Umm hurry back please" Yoshiyuki walks off with the man's coupon in hand. Yoshiyuki stops at the front desk as he see a beautiful Japaneses woman. "That will be 59 US$$" "Okay...How about i use my free coupon. Then you meet me at Roppongi Hills around 9'o clockish?" "Sounds nice but you make it eight thirty and you got a deal cuite" "No problem 8:30pm." "okay thanks and come again" "I will, by the way what's your name?" "Azola Ping" "I love the name Azola. Its mysterious." "Really!?" "Yeah! Its the new Japan!" "Oh okay Mr.." Azola saying it in a flirting language. "Yoshiyuki" "okay Yuki" "I'll see you at eight thirtish" "adiosu" "Later" Yoshiyuki walks right pass the American couple and right through the doors. The busy streets of Tokyo is roaring at Yoshiyuki. Yoshiyuki is pacing down the sidewalk with his sunglasses on looking very cool. Yoshiyuki stops at the wall he tagged before during his debut on High Stakes. Yoshiyuki Cellphone rings again. "Yuko?" "Yuki where are you?" "I'm coming down the street from the Mori Garden" "Thats good hurry, there's a small little festival going on and i know you are going to like it. This right up your alley." "Yeah Yeah Yeah" "Plus they got some very interesting women here" "Yuko its Hot not Interesting." "Yuki its not my problem if you speak with the American slang. I speak my proper Japaneses-English. Either they understand or they back off." "Alright" Yoshiyuki breathing hard "I'm almost around the corner now." Yoshiyuki jumps on a near by bench thats located in Mori Gardens. "I see you." Excited Voice, "Whoa I see the festival from over hear Yuko." "Interesting?" "I've seen better on television" "You just don't get it." "Whatever, I must hang up the phone now. I'm coming right now" Yoshiyuki hangs up the cellphone and proceed towards the noisy festival. The festival has many Japaneses traditional props all around the garden. Yoshiyuki walks parallel from Yutaka. Yoshiyuki waves at Yutaka, Yutaka bows at him which makes Yuki nod his head in shame. Yutaka walks in front of Yuki, he gives him a rice cake for a snack. Yoshiyuki takes a bite of the cake and smirks at Yutaka. Just as Yutaka turns his head towards the festival, Yoshiyuki flings the rice cake over the meadow. He starts wiping his mouth "That was incredible." Yutaka looks at him trying to make him crackdown from his lie. "Alright that cake was horrible, Thats not how mother makes them!" "I know thats Ms. Fuji's Cakes" said Yutaka. "no wonder it taste like death ran over twice" "So thats dead ran over and then ran over again?" " I think.." "Anyway lets move over there towards the crowd" Yutaka is greeted by the older people of the crowd while Yoshiyuki watches on and ducks behind the younger crowd. Everyone appears to be in there late 20s maybe 30s. "gu-tenmorugen! My name is Kirogen Kufi" He is stopped by a mid-aged man. The man looks like he has nothing going for him just a gold watch if that. "Umm Good Morning to you too" Yoshiyuki said with a confused look. Yutaka is dancing with the crowd as they listen to some soft Japaneses tunes. Yoshiyuki walks away from the mid-aged gentleman and starts walking towards the ladies but before he gets there he is stopped by a sports fanatic! "dateotoko! You are Yoshiyuki of Japan!" "Yeah, You've heard of me?" "You appear at High Stakes" "Yeah me and my brother Yutaka." "I'm such a fan of you guys." "I guess you've been watching the indy circuit?" "All day man! Yutaka is like a legend of Japaneses Indy Circuit!" "He's alright, wait until we debut in GCW." "Yeah when is that anyway?" "I don't know. Yutaka is supposed to give me the okay." "Oh well...good luck Panda!" "Supashi-bo" The fanatic walks away as Yutaka walks up towards Yoshiyuki. "I've got word from GCW officials Yesterday." "Saying?" "We're booked and we need to head to the states." "About time!" "Our opponents are a team named Pleasure and Pain." "Ah..never heard of the poor people. Oh well they'll learn by the end of the night." "Lets not get cocky my brother. We fight with honor and respect." "Bullshit! We fight for respect and worry about honor later." "Lets Go" Yutaka says it with a disappointed voice. ________ Airport 12:30pm Tokyo, Japan "I'm not missing this flight Yuko!" "I'm right behind you. I am the one holding all the stuff around here. As you do nothing but run your mouth the entire trip. Yoshiyuki you are going to start doing your part in the business. I will carry us as long as i can but when it comes to the clutch hour, you will be needed to step up for us." "I understand" Yutaka and Yoshiyuki walk towards the metal detectors. After about 3 minutes of process they are done. Yutaka is on his way through th walkway to get on the plane. Yoshiyuki is chatting with a sexy Korea babe who just got off her flight. Yutaka grips Yuki's ear and drags him towards the plane entrance way. He releases Yuki's ear as they walk through the walkway and through the door of the airplane. "That was uncalled for." "it was need to be done brother." Yutaka says it in a mature voice. He basically belittled Yoshiyuki in front of a large set of people on the plane. Yoshiyuki knows he don't have much to say to his older brother. Yoshiyuki chucks it up and takes his seat on the plane. A large rounded fella taps Yoshiyuki on his shoulder getting his attention. "What is it?" "Are you Yoshiyuki Takahashi!?" "Yes" "Oh ok" "What!?" "I did say anything else:" Yoshiyuki is even more pissed than before thanks to the non-fan who tapped him on the back. "YUTAKA!" "What Yuki?" "I forgot i met this hot female at the motel!" "Well she'll see you on T.V" "No!" "We have no choice, either that or we go broke." "She's not worth that much." "I thought so." "Damn Yuko, she was probably the one..." Yuko laughs out loud before speaking, "Yeah Brother anything is possible in your head. Be a genius on your own time, leave the brains to me and you keep the athlete gene." "You got that right!" "Hello everyone this is your captain speaking. We are ready to depart. Thank you for flying Air Japan" The Captain speaking in straight Japaneses. "Well Warpath is going to be pretty wicked Yuko!" "I know, but we need to focus on pleasure and pain first for our debut match against them. There's no stopping them at this point unless we stay on the same page the entire match you understand Yuki?" Yoshiyuki Nods with a smile. "Good Now take a nap we got 20 hours." "20 hours!! I forgot how far we are from America!" Yoshiyuki does a fake cry. "I'm gone.." "adyu brother"
