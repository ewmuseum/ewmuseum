This Man of God

17 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17Jul06 The journey of a thousand miles begins with the first death. But pretty words will get us nowhere in this battle of faith. I write today as a report of my progress, my even steps down this war of souls. Each day brings me closer to the start, to the first of what I have no doubt will be many conflicts. Each second is an eternity as I strive to remember my teacher's words in the Monastary of St George. I speak prayers of strength, prayers of victory, prayers of war. I pray my training has prepared me. I pray my arm strikes true. I pray that I will see my mission through to the end. I pray that God will hear me, and in His infinite mercy, grant me, if not victory, then the strength to rise to meet my enemies again. But I'm sure you have no wish to hear my words to God. My first challenge is against two fierce opponents. One is a new comer to this heathen blood sport, like myself. Hm, I say like myself as if I debase myself to their level by appearing here. No, my purpose is higher then theirs. They come to revel in their bloodletting. To glory themselves in the broken bodies of their enemies. They are boastful, proud. And their pride will be their fall. Their cries will be unhear before God, for they have already recieved their reward. Their fast cars and pretty houses. Their thousands of adulterous affairs with thousands of loose women, men and God only knows what else. They have turned from the Lord of Hosts and now he sends his avenging angel to give his message to them. And I will arrive on firey wings of righteousness to tell them that God is not pleased and that their labors for evil are at an end. They will turn to the light, or they will be burned by it. Consumed in the firey furnace of God's Judgement. They think they are the arbitors of their own destiny, but they relenquished that right when they took the gifts they were given and turned them to the darkness. Like the man who buried his money, they will be stripped of their gifts and sent out to wail and gnash their teeth. If they choose the darkness, then into it they will be cast. But I digess. My second opponent is a new hero among the warmongers. He gloats and boasts and is prideful. He thinks himself better than his peers, and loudly chants is for the world to hear. He thanks the Lord that he is not like the others of his ilk. But he has his reward. He has the admiration of his flock. He has the bowing and the pretty words of the masses. And now he will pay for his pride. He will be shown the light of God and he will cow in the might of His Judgement. He will be found lacking and his pretty costume will be thrown off to reveal the cowering, self-loathing, blood loving warmonger that he is, no better than the rest of these hethans and heretics. And yet, here I am, proclaiming the inadiquacies of my enemies. Here I am shouting that they are nothing but hethans and heretics as I imply myown greatness. I bask in the light of righeousness and glory as I raise myself up with my words. I proclaim my greatness with louder voice than I proclaim my Gods. If I have a flaw, my huberis is it. It will be my downfall as surely as it may be theirs. And yet, I cannot approach their alter of sin with doubt in my heart. I must be strong. I must tread a narrow path between humility and confidence. If I stray to far in either way, I will be decimated. Though I am closer to this then any other in this den of sin and hatred, I must all the more watch my actions, I must remain apart from them, lest I fall into the trap of self-aggrandizement and self-worship. I am only one man. I am the messagner of the Lord. I am the servent of my Destiny, not the arbitor. I pray this letter reaches you swiftly and finds you in good heath. My the Lord bless you and keep you. My his face shine upon you and be gracious too you. May he look upon you with kindness untill we meet again. In Noimine Patris, Et Filii, Et Spiritus Sanctus. Signed, Michael Balthazar
