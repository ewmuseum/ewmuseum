Date: 9/30/08 .::Disclaimer- May Contain Strong Language and Violence::. .::Viewer Discretion Advised::. ---- I painted your room at midnight, So I'd know yesterday was over. I put all your books on the top shelf, Even the one with the four leaf clover. Man I'm getting older. ---- Mechanical Rhetoric Things end. Of all the people in this fucked up world, I should know that. Glory is fleeting- love even more so. I feel sick. I feel precisely like a bug under glass. My back hurts, my face hurts. Too many cheap shots, at the hands of Bryan Mayhem. Beginning to think that I'm a washed up hack. I don't belong here. Sitting here in this airport lounge, watching the sun come up over the hard-baked tarmac at LAX. Flight's been delayed, engine trouble, and the unease is settling over me in waves. Sit here and stare at the fading bruises in the glass and pretend I'm not looking into the future. Stare at this face like I'm studying a stranger. Haggard, broken, fucked up with bloodshot eyes and too much grey in the hair. Drifting down into that bad place. And I can feel their eyes on me, watching me watch myself. They probably think I'm this vain motherfucker, getting a sick thrill out of this inner gaze. Every day is a little better. Distancing myself from the problem. Gradually, I'm getting used to the idea of no longer being the champion. Don't get me wrong. It was a good trip, and I loved every second of it, but it started to feel a little bit too much like a security blanket. Complacency rearing its ugly head. I never aspired to be one of those names that could be dropped. I don't want that fucking hero worship shit of yesteryears. I appreciate it, I really do. Don't get me wrong. I'm not taking a shit on my fans. Hell for those who have stuck by me through the years, I owe them something more. Thing is, I want to make it on my own. Not on some fucked up reputation from the past. That guy's gone. That version of myself died a long time ago. I'm just the hollow shell that's left for sadistic jesters like Bryan Mayhem to punk out. Sure, go for it. I probably deserve that. I can't fucking believe I came back here. What the fuck was I thinking? Back and we're running in circles. Wasn't I facing Shaman this time last year? Jesus, was I here a year ago? Doesn't matter, because you don't know shit about me. I am not that same asshole I was then. I've changed. Evolved, you might say. That prospect scares me. I'm not going to lie. I'm too fucking old for all this shit. ---- I took all your pictures off the wall, Wrapped them in a newspaper blanket. I haven't slept in what seems like a century. And now I can barely breathe. ---- Thursday, September 25, 2008 1045 hours, local time Los Angeles International Airport, Los Angeles, CA The man in black slumped his shoulders, head buried in his hands as he attempted in vain to rub the exhaustion from his face without disturbing the bruised cheekbone and eye socket. It wasn't happening, and that grim reality kicked his irritability up another notch. A frustrated growl escaped his lips as he reached for the tumbler of warm Scotch on the table. He brought it slowly to his lips, eyes still fixed on the glass. The sun's glare bleached the features of the man as he paused beside Jackson's table. Jackson grunted, downing the warm liquor and grimacing as the fire burned down his throat. He couldn't make out the eyes, couldn't discern the face of the visitor, but that didn't stop him from visibly flinching as the hand fell on his shoulder. "Mr. Jackson?" Jackson said nothing, letting his eyes close as the room pulled in closer. He could feel it crushing, constricting his chest. Claustrophobia. Agoraphobia. Probably the latter. He felt the panic tangibly now as sweat trickled down his back. Hands trembling. Awesome. "What do you want?" He ground out between clenched teeth, struggling to keep the discomfort off his face. "Just... a minute of your time..." Slowly, Jackson turned his head, a muscle in his jaw twitching as he ground his back teeth together. He let the man see the bloodshot gaze, the bruises on his cheek, and the disturbingly fake smile. He looked like hell, and he knew it. "Can't oblige that. I don't own time." The man shifted from one foot to the other, looking sheepish as his eyes moved away from Jackson's ruined face. "I just wanted to ask you a few... questions about..." Jackson looked down at the tabletop, and the empty glass that was still in his hand. "I don't think so." He said firmly, steely resolve resonating in those four words. "Mr. Jackson, please. I just want to know your feelings heading towards Dangerous Games." Jackson hesitated, maybe a split second too long as he weighed the options, deciding whether to straight up answer the question, or be a dick. The prospect of another eight hours of hell wedged into a business class seat with barely enough legroom was enough to make him opt for the latter. "Feelings? Are you fucking kidding me?" He fixed that angry glare on the reporter. "You want to know about my feelings? When the hell did feelings come into play? Huh? I'm in the business of kicking ass, not sitting in the dark cutting myself and FEELING like the lost member of My Chemical Romance. I'm fucking pissed off, is that what you want me to tell you? I'm ready to kick ass. Is that what you want me to say? That Bryan Mayhem is going to be a greasy smear on the canvas when I'm done? Is that the kind of sound byte you want? Huh? Tell me, fucktard. What do you want me to say? I'll make it easy, so you can fuck off, and I can?" He broke off abruptly, trying to clamp down on the anger that was boiling towards the surface. It was never far these days. Not since Georgie had betrayed him. "Well, no, that's not what I meant-" the reporter stammered, taken aback at the vehemence in Jackson's voice. "Listen up, you little shitsmear. I hate people like you. Think you can walk up to me in a public place, where I'm minding my own fucking business, and insinuate yourself into my personal space. Do I walk up to you when you're taking a piss, and ask you stupid shit? Do I fucking sit down at your table when you're in a restaurant, reach over and steal a forkful of your meal? No. I don't. Common courtesy, asshole. Try it out." He raked a hand through his messy hair, heaving a sigh. "That's the fucking problem I have with this shit. Listen close. I. Am. Not. A. Celebrity. I'm just some guy, ok? Another faceless asshole in an airport, waiting for a flight to Scotland. Shit, if I was so fucking important, why can't I even get first class seats?" The reporter blinked, genuinely shocked at the blatant hostility. "Mr. Jackson, I'm sorry. I didn't mean anything by it. I just saw you here, and thought it might be nice to get a few words before everyone else gets their crack at you. I saw your comeback match. I watched you and Mr. Pierre beat The SoCal Stretching Crew. Hell of a match." Harsh, self-deprecating laughter burst from Jackson's lips as he reached for the fresh drink that appeared on the table as if by magic. He nodded to the departing bartender, and then looked back at the reporter. "That was bullshit. A fucking joke. You think those two asswipes are in my league? No. No fucking way. You want to know my fucking feelings, man? This isn't about word of mouth, or fucking nonsensical reputations. Physical prowess? No, hell no. This is going to be a mental war. Just standing between those ropes, facing what may as well be absolute strangers is going to be a study in stamina, in courage. The best of the fucking best. That's what Dangerous Games is all about. Shit, I don't even know if I'll be in that match. The powers that be get their rocks off screwing me out of opportunities like that, after all? but to outlast other champions and contenders, you need to have gravel in your guts. Do I think I'm good enough to stand alongside Vivica Valentine? No, hell no. Grit and determination. I have that. I'm not a stranger to madness, or the drive that goes with it. Not by a long shot. But I still know there's no fucking way I'm going to win?" The words were bitter and defeated. The reporter shrugged, "man, why are you so angry?" Jackson was already way too into ripping a strip off this innocent kid with his Hollywood shellacked hair and his bleached out smile to pull it back now. He grinned, knowing that this wasn't one of his nicer smiles. Nah, this was like the shark going in for the kill. "Let me tell you something about me, you little puke. Every day, every damn day I wake up furious, angry, horny, judgmental and argumentative. Stiff dick, stiff middle finger." He waved said finger for emphasis. "It's people like you that piss me off. With your retarded questions, and your fucking programmed misconceptions! How do you feel about your match? Do you think you'll win? Why are you so angry? I'll tell you how I feel. Fuckin' energized, like that bunny. I want to go out there and maim everyone. I'll fucking start with that asshat Bryan Mayhem. My chiropractor will thank him, I'm sure, since that was one fucking hell of a bill. Do I think I'll win? No! I don't think. I do. Action motherfucking Jackson, I'm not a thinker, I'm a doer. Why am I angry? Because, I'm alive, and breathing? I'm a thinking man with a brain in my goddamn head. The shit that happens each and every day pisses me off. That's why I'm angry. I'm sick of all the BULLSHIT!" The last word was roared in the reporter's face, making him flinch and stumble back. Jackson didn't get to say anything else, unfortunately because the kid turned tail and ran. Grinning to himself, he downed the rest of his fresh Scotch, just as the loudspeaker announced the boarding call for his flight. "I think that went well, considering." Jackson said, totally deadpan. ---- Mechanical Rhetoric I fucking hate flying. Easy to blame the undercurrent of anger on that one simple thing. I could take it further. Blame it on that cookie-cutter, faceless fucking clones they're pitting me against, again. I never even asked the reporter where he was from. Fact is I didn't much care. He could be writing for the Pro Wrestling Tabloid Insider Illustrated Times, and is about to give me so much bad press it'll ruin my career, and I can't even muster a single twinge of care factor. Let him print what he wants. Let them say their shit about me. Call me names. Sling the dirt around. Tell me I don't matter because I'm coming in there as an unknown. Tell me I have to win back the shit I lost when I left. Fucking pledge week bullshit. I need to feel pain to feel involvement. Validation of life. I need to see a reaction in your eyes to feel satisfaction in what I'm done. You, Mayhem. You looked me in the eyes, and spit in my face. You tried to end me in my first match back? Why? And then you turned your back on me like I don't matter. You stroked your own fucking ego like some faggot who gets off on masturbation. I get it, fucker. Oblivion is THE GROUP to contend with. Face it, idiots. You will NEVER be as good as Banned and Exiled~!... EVER. But quick! I need to know what you think of me so I can form an opinion about myself.
