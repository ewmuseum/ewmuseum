Date: Must've lost track of time somehow “That little asshole, it should’ve been easy, but he ruined everything. I’m the golden boy, I’m the fucking number one man in pro-wrestling. What the hell does that damn loser kid have that I don’t?” “No idea son, I just pour the drinks.” It wasn’t a fancy bar, or even a clean one for that matter, but for the last few days since Worldwide, Michael Stevens had been calling it home. There was probably somewhere else he needed to be, like on a plane or train heading for whatever country he was supposed to be going to next, but he really didn’t care. It wasn’t like he had a match or anything anyway, so what did it matter. So he was here, on this stool, drinking whiskey and pint after pint. The owner had a backroom, it wasn’t much a little cot for his regulars to crash in if they wife wouldn’t let them go home that night. Stevens had been sleeping there since Wednesday. At first the barkeep, had been surly towards the young man with the obviously dyed red hair, but that’d changed once he’d begun to see all the cash Michael was spending. He’d ever paid a few of the locals to go on smoke runs for him. He wasn’t exactly popular by any means, but his money sure was, and it was nice being a place where no one knew who the hell you are. “I’m telling you man, I’m the fucking greatest, and that talent less little hack steals my thunder. It’s fucking bullshit, give me another.” God knows many the next one would make, he’d lost count a long time ago. It wasn’t as if it really mattered, Steven had made sure he wouldn’t be wrestling at the next Worldwide anyway. Two damn weeks and not a single match, apparently the bastard was trying for some petty vengeance. Oh well, who the hell wanted a guy like that to be his mentor anyway, someone who’d just toss aside their prot’g? at the drop of a hat. Sure things had changed, but that didn’t mean he was any different, he was still the guy that Steven had helped mold. So why wasn’t get good enough any more. He just couldn’t understand what Hardy could possibly have the he didn’t. In fact Hardy was so much less than Stevens it wasn’t even funny, the little SOB probably wasn’t even old enough to buy himself a beer, he didn’t have enough spare cash to pack himself a pack of smokes much less dine at all the fancy little places Caldera loved. Michael was rich, experienced, and more over he could wrestle. That’s something Hardy could never claim, sure he could throw himself bodily at you, but lets see him try and lock in an arm bar just the right way to make someone pass out from the pain, lets see him execute perfect suplexes time and time again. Stevens was his superior in every way. So why didn’t Caldera see that? “Another.” He’d taken the last pint as if it were a shot, and the foam covered his mouth. Wiping it away, he noticed for the first time, at least the first time he remembered, that he was starting to become disgusting. Almost a week without showering or shaving, stewing in liquor and smoke, he must look like hell. He tried not to think about it, thinking about it would breed self pity, and that wasn’t a road he wanted to walk down any time soon, he’d had enough of it lately. It’d been almost a minute since he’d asked for the beer, and it hadn’t appeared yet. Pulling his attention away from the bar top, he looked up towards the bar tender. It took a few seconds for his eyes to focus, and several more to recognize the expression on the man’s face, before he ever said anything, Stevens knew he was about to be cut off. Even though he’d been expecting it to come anytime now, it still pissed him off. “I think you’ve had enough, kid, why don’t you just go to the back and sleep it off?” Wobbily he rose to his feet, placing his palms flat on the bar top, he braced himself. At his full height he would’ve had to look down at the older man, but hunched as he was, they were eye to eye. A sneer curled Michael’s lips as he looked across the bar. “I’ll fucking tell you when I’ve had enough. Don’t you fucking know who I am?” He felt the hand on his shoulder immediately, even before he heard the voice. “We don’t fucking care you nancy wanker.” Instinct kicked in, and Stevens spun on his heel. Without so much as a thought he’d hooked the regular’s arm, continued turning, and within a second he had the man in a double chicken wing. Driving a knee into the guy’s back, Michael drove his head into the bar splitting his forehead open. “I’m fucking Michael Stevens you asshole, I’m fucking perfect. Who the flying fuck do you think you are?” He should’ve been smarter than this, realized this was exactly what they’d been waiting for since the moment he entered the bar. The booze must’ve clouded his brain a lot more than he’d thought, falling for this was a dumb mistake, but he’d started it, so he better damn well try and finish it. Apparently he hadn’t had enough to drink, he still felt the pool cue snapping over his back. The pain was sharper than he’d felt in a damn long time, and he immediately went down to his knees. On the plus side the agony had cleared his vision, and he could see himself surrounded. This was gonna hurt, bad. Coarse hands nearly ripped out a fist full of hair, and a meaty smashed into his face just above the eye. His vision blurry again, this time from the blood dripping down. “How does that feel you fucking ponce” Come in here waving Daddy’s money, trying to be a big man. Fuck you git.” A boot snapped his jaw shut before he could respond, he tasted blood before he felt his skull bounce off the floor. Tighten up in a ball, it was the only thing he could do, protect his face, groin, ribs, his most vulnerable areas had to survive the encounter. He rolled onto his stomach as he was bombarded by the mass of humanity he’d pushed too far. That sound ringing in his ears could’ve been a broken wrist, it was too difficult to separate the myriad of aches any longer. He could feel himself starting to slip away, and he was almost thankful it’d be over soon. “Take the wallet and roll him into the street for the bobbys to pick up.” Even through the haze he recognized the owners voice. It was the last thing he heard, just before he slipped away.
