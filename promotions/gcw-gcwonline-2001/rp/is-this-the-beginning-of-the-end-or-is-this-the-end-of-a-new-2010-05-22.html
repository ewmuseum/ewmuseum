Is This The Beginning of the End, or is This The End of a New Beginning? (Part 3)

22 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/22/10 If there was one thing Tessa Windsor never prided herself on, it was the way everyone around her seemingly treated her anytime she walked away with even a scratch. No one backstage got coddled and cradled as much as Tessa did anytime there was any kind of hint she was in pain. After the beating she took at the hands of Jay Terror and Clyde Fox earlier on, tonight was no doubt going to be one of those nights where getting any moment to herself would be few and far between. Regardless, she kept her head hung low as the EMT’s assisted her to the back in hopes that maybe everyone missed what the hell just happened out there. Sparkling brighter than fireworks on the fourth of July, one would have expected that Tessa had stumbled upon a pile of gold with how bright her baby blues were upon opening. If only it were that grand. Instead, the pint sized diva was merely relived by the blurred sight of GCW’s makeshift medical office. “Thank god.” Tessa whispered quietly to herself. The EMT’s who helped to plop her down in the first open seat they could find no doubt assumed it had everything to do with the tremendous amount of pain the five foot four beauty was in at the moment. In reality, it had absolutely nothing to do with any of that. Instead Tessa Windsor was simply relieved that she made it back there without everyone kicking up a giant fuss about her condition. “Ms. Windsor?” Her head tilted upwards enough to acknowledge whoever was calling her name. Catching a side glimpse of the only other she saw around, it looked to be an older guy who was walking in her direction. Tessa very slowly propped her head up slightly more to acknowledge his presence. It was beyond her why he was the one asking who she was. Even with blood trickling down her swollen face, there were not too many other five foot four brunette’s crawling around these parts taking a licking as large as she had just managed to. As harmless as he seemed, she kept her eyes glued on him as he stopped right before the chair she had planted on to. The last thing she needed was a matching wound. Thankfully enough for the guy she managed to keep her eyes focused long enough to notice his medical badge. “You don’t have to move,” The man apparently named Dr. Chang commanded far too late. By then Tessa had pulled her head back up to conversational level in time for his next prompt, “I just want to ask you a few questions.” Being in the business as long as she had, Tessa Windsor already knew how it all worked anytime the suspected a concussion. Instead of having Dr. Chang’s piddle paddling around with lights and mind numbing question, Tessa jumped straight to the chase, “You’re not holding up any fingers, its 2010, and yes I am in pain! Simple enough?” Well, that took care of that round. Yet medical wise there was plenty she had to do to keep off the hook. After the shot she had taken to the head via Jay Terror and a chair, she would be lucky if she managed to get out of anytime within the next few hours. Regardless, she tried to salvage some relief in Dr. Chang’s reassurance, “Good. Someone will be in to those nasty wounds up of yours in just a second.” Although it was against her better judgment, Tessa very slowly moved her head up and down to confirm what she had heard. God did that ever hurt. Quickly she sealed her eyes shut and gritted her teeth together as the blood surged through her brain vessels. Every last one of them felt as if they would implode at any second and snap Tessa Windsor into an unconscious state. At this point, Tessa almost welcomed that. “Ms. Windsor?” Yet another voice prompted Tessa to pull herself out of her only half there state. This time it belonged to a woman in her late twenties, not much taller than Tessa herself. Stopping just in front of Tessa, she tossed the handful of medical goodies she brought along with her to the side, “You alright?” Though nothing within her field of vision seemed to want to stay put, Tessa waved off the nurses concern with her hand. It was much easier for her to do that than to risk speaking and coming off as dazed and confused, which would no doubt send the nurse into a panic and get the EMT’s involved once again. Dawning a pair of latex gloves, the nurse advised Tessa, “Don’t move, this is probably going to hurt some.” Though shut Tessa’s eyes rolled full circle. Did that woman sincerely believe that Tessa had never really been through anything of this sort? Tessa would have been the one to ask that had she not suddenly been prodded smack dab in the middle of her forehead with a needle, which suddenly made it ten times more difficult to comply with the nurse’s orders. It certainly helped none that although her eyes were sealed shut, every bit of the stitching process vividly flashed through Tessa’s imagination. “Wow.” A deep voice from entryway proclaimed quite surprised, “I heard she took quite a beating, but I figured it was exaggerated.” Turning to assure it was no one who’d interfere with the process, the brunette who was in the middle of stitching Tessa up turned to shush the man standing behind her. Obviously she had to be new to the medic crew. No one else, aside from Vivica Valentine, would dare shush the presence of the legendary Lestatt Knight. Surprisingly enough, LeStatt headed the warning, tucking his hands into the pockets of his baggy black sweatpants. Turning her attention back to Windsor, the nurse ceased this opportunity to make an important note to Windsor that she already knew Tessa would likely ignore. Maybe this guy, whoever he was, would see to it that she followed through with it. “You should probably take a week off and get some rest, you’ve got an awful lot of recovering to do.” Words of advice that no one in GCW ever seemed to heed. Tessa would be damned if she had to be the exception to that rule. She played it off the way she always did, “I’ll see how things play out.” “You have kids?” If Windsor was able to nod, she would have opted to do that instead. Unfortunately doing so would have likely resulted with a needle in her eye, so she was forced to speak again, “Four.” “It’s a great excuse to spend time with them.” Still perched in the doorway behind the nurse, Lestatt snickered to himself. If there was one thing Tessa could not stand, it was any sort of suggestion that she did not spend enough time with her children. Especially from someone she hardly knew. “I need to find excuses other than the fact that they are my children?” Tessa questioned. If she could have glared a hole through her nurse, she would have. Instead she refused to lift her eyelids for fear that simply seeing what was going on would make the pain that much worse. “It’s not exactly like I only see them once a year.” Thankfully for her own sake, the nurse was quick to apologize, “I’m sorry. I didn’t mean it like that.” Quickly, the man who’d been sitting in the doorway the entire time plopped down beside Windsor, advising with his hands for the woman to change the subject. Luckily she didn’t have to, as her job there was nearly finished. “And we’re done here.” Immediately Tessa started to push off her seat, “Okay. Let’s get going!” “In much of a rush senorita?” In an instant Tessa’s eyes shot wide open. She could hardly believe who was sitting there beside her. While she was wading in the border between Lala land and reality, Tessa had failed to realize he had been there all along, “Roberto?” With his trademark grin spread wide across his face Knight answered, “Good to see you at least remember my name.” He expected Tessa to respond with her little grin that was delightfully cute considering her angelic looks. Instead the only thing he got in response from her was a bemused expression, “What are you doing here?” “I’m not done yet?” “Oh right, I forgot.” “Terror didn’t hit you that hard. Least he didn’t hit Chris though, right?” Tough as he was on her, he was far more successful at calming Windsor’s nerves than anyone else had been that evening. “Yeah, awkward. Not exactly my proudest of moments.” “I’d figured as much. I imagine that boy of yours is having a fit, or very close to one.” He casually suggested in yet another attempt to ease her nerves. Oddly enough, it succeeded. She smiled ever so slightly, “Can we just pretend that’s not going to happen?” “Done deal. Let’s just make sure it doesn’t happen again.” “I’ll try.” Tessa nervously chuckled, fully aware that either of them would be a tough promise to keep given the type of environment they both worked in. How on earth anyone could keep from getting the shit kicked out of them on a consistent basis was beyond Tessa. But she would find a way somehow. God knew she needed a way after the way the last few weeks had gone for her. Bluntly, LeStatt assured, “You will.” “I will.” Too bad her promise would likely be broken by the time next week rolled around. She had made a similar promise to Chris not too long ago; then tonight happened. Trouble had a way of following her around regardless of the corners she took to avoid it. “You remember what you promised me, right?” She blushed. Of course she did. How could she possibly forget promising to keep her head high and walk out victorious over five other people who wanted to win just as much, especially when most were nearly twice her size. “Course. I told you I’d win. Did I not?” “It certainly proved you’re ready for the big one.” He blinked, adding to it. ”Question is, are you actually going to do anything to finally step up and take that belt you’ve worked so hard for? Cause tonight certainly did not look promising.” He would not have been a good mentor if he let her get away without any kind criticism. It was the one thing that Tessa admired most about him. LeStatt Knight was not afraid to tell her exactly what was on his mind. She bit down on her lip. “I fucked up. I know.” “Chair shot or not, losing to a twenty two year old with the mentality of a preschooler does not look good for you Tess.” Clamping her lip tighter, she gulped and admitted, “I know. That’s why I need to fix it next week.” “The question is, will you?” There would have likely been a bout of awkward silence between the two if their attention had not been shifted to a voice screaming in the next room. “Damn it, I need a nurse!” Normally this is when surrounding people either panicked or rubbernecked their head in the direction of the demand. But being as familiar with the voice as she was, Tessa could have easily painted a thorough picture of exactly what was happening without so much as batting an eyelash in the direction of the commotion. Familiarity in this case however only meant panic. Quickly diving behind LeStatt to conceal herself, Tessa commanded through a whisper, “Whatever you do, don’t move.” She was hardly worried about the man doing the demanding, it was what he’d say or do if he saw her here there in her current state. It certainly was not the same confident Tessa Windsor that had just attempted to put on her “I’m a big girl now” face. Peering over Knight’s shoulder, there was little surprise from Tessa. There was Chris Storm throwing up a fuss again in attempts to get himself a pink slip. She watched on, only half amused, as Chris continued on his with little diva like tirade. As much as she was admittedly head over heels when it came to him, there was plenty of spitefulness bottled up inside her after last week’s antics; certainly enough to allow her to take some pleasure in him currently making a fool of himself. Watching the sculpted six foot five man father of her children limp around as if he were handicapped, there was very little question as to where their children’s whining surfaced from, “I need an icepack god damn it!” Finally, the self proclaimed rock superstar got the response he was looking for, “Just a second sir.” Then he fired back with the response he’d tossed around to anyone he deemed under him, “I hope they fire you too.” Ironically enough, it was his own career he really wanted terminate. But Garbage Bag Johnny would not honor that liberty. Ratings were far more important than that. “Hurry the fuck up! I’ve got kids at home waiting for me.” It hardly got him what he was searching for. Instead the nurse turned in the opposite direction informing Storm, “You’ll have to wait a second sir. I need check up on Ms. Windsor one last time.” LesStatt need not need to share a glance with Tessa to figure out exactly what she was thinking at that moment. “Oh shit.” It was an ‘oh shit’ moment indeed. At least he didn’t flip a shit like they half expected him to. Rather Storm continued his miserable attempt to pretend that he was still king shit around these neck of the woods as he was in the days of Banned & Exiled~!, or rather Banned & Established~!. “As in my baby momma?” “As in Tessa Windsor.” Now Tessa was totally wishing her stare would have actually burned a hole through that woman. Well maybe not that violently. A good sucker punch would have sufficed. Either way the whole patient confidentiality bit was completely dished out the window for the sake of chasing off Storm. “Where’s she at?” Given that he now knew she was in there it mattered absolutely none that the nurse had no answer for Chris. It was a matter of seconds before Storm’s little bit of intelligence kicked in. From the corner of his eye he closely watched the nurse’s expression, waiting for that ‘oh shit I blew it moment’. Surely enough Tessa’s presence was confirmed by the sudden dip of her bottom lip, which flipped Storms own in the completely opposite direction. “Thank god.” And thus began the complete one eighty on behalf of Chris Storm. Storm’s whispered relief was loud enough for everyone around to hear, including Tessa. Relieved as he was, Tessa’s own heart felt like it were about to pound straight through her chest. Chris would no doubt flip a shit all over again when getting another glance at the damage Jay Terror had caused with a chair shot intended for him. Easing out from behind LeStatt, as if he had been in the way of her leaving, Tessa lowered her head in hopes that maybe Chris would have nothing to say. Sadly Chris Storm would not be Chris Storm unless he had something incredibly stupid to say. Upon laying eyes on Tessa he melodramatically threw his arms up in the air as if he had just spotted her facial wounds for the first time all over again, “That mother fucker! I swear to god I’m going to kill him!” With her baby blues narrowed as far as possible, Tessa growled, “Please don’t start now.” After having known Storm for almost eight years now, Tessa should have been well aware that by saying that she was only setting herself up further. Storm innocently threw his arms up in defense. “I’m just concerned, that’s all. You look like you got ran over by a damn mac truck.” “And survived? Yeah. Genius Chris, just genius.” Instead of responding to her sarcasm, Storm’s attention drifted over to the man beside her. His eyes narrowed which elicited a wave from LeStatt Knight, who absolutely cared less about anything Chris had to say. Knight knew just as well as anyone that Tessa’s biggest flaw was Chris Storm. She overestimated Chris, underestimated herself, and blew her big chances away for the sake of keeping Storm’s legacy alive. It was the one thing everyone hated about her. Try as he may, Chris Storm could never best Lestatt Knight whenever Tessa was around. There were countless times Chris had done everything he could within his power to try and sway Tessa from Knight’s influence yet failed. Thankfully, tonight was the last he’d have to see of Knight. Chris was one of the few that relished in the moment. Interjecting himself between the two, Chris added in with a hiss, “I thought you two got your goodbyes in last week.” Chris fully loathed the idea of Knight being anywhere within arms distance of Tessa. Prior experience had taught him that if anyone was capable of swaying Tessa out of his grasp, it was LeStatt. Thus he kept his most prized possession under scrutinized security anytime LeStatt lingered around for millisecond too long. “Afraid she’ll leave along with me, Chris?” With the kind of power he had, Knight was hardly swayed by Storm’s innuendos. If anything, the biggest name to ever come from GCW welcomed the challenge. Call it one last parting shot. “Fuck off.” Tessa’s screech cut them both off, “Chris, stop!” Somehow, no matter how many times she saved Tessa’s ass, things were always his fault. Her choosing to chaperone him and Markus in the days of Banned &Exiled~!? His fault. Her choosing to side with Vivica Valentine over him and Markus? His fault too. Her choosing to align with LeStatt and throw her best friends under the bus? Again, his fault. So why would this time be any different? After all, he was only the guy who had to endure her tears in the moments where Vivica Valentine straight up ripped into their relationship, Markus Stone straight up beat the shit out of her, and LeStatt Knight drifted away. This time he wisely surrendered from the get go before she grinded him down with guilt. Those plea full baby blues would bring a man with even the least bit of sympathy to his knees. “Fine,” his shoulders dropped down in defeat, “meet me at your room when you’re ready to go. I’ll get your stuff ready. Don’t get into trouble on the way. I already feel shitty enough about what happened out there.” “Chris, wait!” Arms outstretched wide, her feet planted on the floor. Even with her pitifully staring back at him there was no way in hell he was turning back around. This time, it was her choice. Thankfully, LeStatt had enough decency to realize what was best for them both. “Go ahead. We’ll catch up afterward.” “Promise?” “Promise. That said she was hot on Chris’ trail. There was no way she would give any more reason to leave again. Not when he was already far too ready to go once and for all. They’d already lost their match; there was no sense in them losing each other too.
