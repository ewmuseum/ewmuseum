NACHOOOOO!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: A long time ago yet somehow in the future Nacho and his faithful duo are totally kickin' it backstage to some block rockin' beats, if you know what I mean. Not only did the great Nacho Grande manage to finally earn a contract with GCW, but his buddies get to come with him. What more could a lucha libre superhero ask for? His trusty companions, Quesa Dilla and Beef Supreme, are each at their own taco shaped laptops, closely studying Nacho's upcoming opponent for his debut in the company, that being Brock Alyas... Or something mildly resembling such an activity. Beef Supreme- Five years ago, few believed a simple capsule could make a man larger. Quesa Dilla- Oh, don't tell me it's that creepy date rapist with the ExtenZe pulls again? Beef Supreme- Hey, it's not a gimmick, it's REAL science! Quesa Dilla- Hey, throw on a lab coat and make some shit up, that means we can be scientists too by those standards! Nacho Grande walks in the room, swinging a long cape around his neck. Nacho Grande- I sent in my diamond wedding band from my twelfth marriage, and got money the very next day, smile and look surprised... Oh wait! Beef Supreme- Nacho, you silly bitch, you better get ready for Alyas. Nacho Grande- Alyas better what? What are we, in Pittsburgh again? Quesa Dilla- Alyas is the name of your opponent. Nacho Grande- Alyas guys? I'm fighting you two? Beef Supreme: Nacho... Have you been smoking the hooka again? Nacho pauses for a few seconds before a guffaw can be heard escaping the mask. Nacho Grande- What in the name of Babyfart McGeezax gives you that idea? Beef and Quesa- Babyfart McGeezax? Beef Supreme- Sounds like you got hit in the mouth with a baltine hammer while trying to quote Larry the Cable Guy. Quesa Dilla- Nah, there'd be an "er" in there somewhere. Nacho Grande- What the hell are we talking about? All three look at each other and shrug. Beef Supreme- I feel like there was something important we were supposed to do. Nacho Grande- I think we needed to get ahold of Zoey. Beef Supreme- Why yes, wasn't he going to further his investigation on Jeff? Quesa Dilla- What's there to investigate? He's from Northern Ohio. Nacho Grande- Not so loud, teh Mighty Gerbil can hear you! Quesa Dilla: Oh shit, I don't want him to do something! Like... chew at me with a glazed look in his eye! Beef Supreme- You speak blasphemy, sir! For all you know, we'll now be attacked by a Gerbilsauras Rex! Nacho Grande- You've doomed us all, you selfish son of a bitch. Quesa Dilla- It's my explosive amnesia, you guys! This lack of memory confuses and infuriates me! Nacho Grande: I'm infuriated because I still don't know who my opponent is! Nacho walks over to Beef's computer. Nacho Grande- Fail blog? Quesa Dilla- I figure if they gave you a match, it'd be a good place to start looking. Beef Supreme- Yeah, I mean... Come on. We're looking for Jeff. Quesa Dilla- He's from Northern Ohio. Nacho glances over at Quesa's computer. Nacho Grande- You've mapquested Northern Ohio? Beef Supreme- Yes. Nacho Grande- Beef! Why are you answering? Beef Supreme- Because he answered for my computer screen and I didn't want him to get to talk more? Quesa Dilla- Yeah, or I'll Fail Blog you, dillweed. Beef Supreme- Shyeah right, you make me touch your hands for stupid reasons, you Somalian Pirate-tess! Quesa Dilla- Well yar metey to you too, douche nozzle. Nacho Grande- CAN ANYONE TELL ME WHO MY MATCH IS AGAINST? Beef and Quesa freeze for a few seconds. Beef Supreme- I don't know, let's put in some New Kids and go find out. Quesa Dilla- NEW KIDS OUT! Beef flips the tape in the boombox, and a familiar beat plays. NACHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! OHHH OHHH OHHH OH OH! OHHH OHHH OHHH OH OH! OHHH OHHH OHHH OH OH! OHHH OHHH OHHH OH OH! Listen up everybody if you wanna take a chance Just get on the floor and do the New Kids dance Don't worry about nothin' cause it won't take long We're gonna put you in a trance with a funky song! CAUSE YOU GOTTA BE HANGIN' TOUGH HANGIN' TOUGH, HANGIN' TOUGH! We're ROUGH! As the confident trio does their trademark handhook dance, it's a synchronatic mix of synchronasity and hangin' tough with the New Kids as the scene closes.
