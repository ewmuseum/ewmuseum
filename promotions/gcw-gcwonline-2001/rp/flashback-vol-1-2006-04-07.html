Date: April 7, 2006 Damn, what a night I just had. Yeah, you hear a line like that and your thinking sex...drugs....booze. Well, 2 out of 3 ain't too bad. Unless you consider the circumstances. Usually a man like me spokes my dope and drinks my booze only when I'm not on shift. I'm a cop err, was a cop you could say. Was a dream of mine for years, until...well, last night? I remember waking up that Wednesday morning, the weather like everyday in Daytona Beach. Absolutely beautiful and clear. Crystal blue waves crashing on the beaches, seagulls in the air..perfect ten girls walk on the sand. A dream, and a nightmare. The day just wasn't the same for me, no, I had feelings I never felt before inside me. Guilt, sadness, wonder, I couldn't shake any of them. I had slept maybe a total of 40 minutes. My father had just died, early morning I find him hanging from pillars out front. Why? How? Even my friends at the Police department had nothing. We searched that scene on day long...nothing. Not a fucking trace to be found. 'Mr. Monroe....Anthony dear, are you awake' a voice came through my door. I rolled over onto my side facing the window and the view. 'Yes, Miss Nikki,' I managed to drag along 'I'll be down is just a bit.' She knew how I was feeling, being not only the 'housekeeper,' but also my dads 'Personal Assistant.' Her..being the age of thirty-five. A tall beautiful redhead, as if no one could tell what she was to my dad....a piece of ass. She was nice however, always helping out and doing the same things a housekeeper would do. So I stumbled my way out of bed, stretching and yawning. Slowly I drag my feet and walk over to my black leather love seat sitting in the corner. I had the thing sense I was in the seventh grade, a present from my father. I reached over to my right, feeling no stress in my forearm, which is always a good thing with me. I grabbed the one thing I could always count on, the one thing that I could always get...marijuana. I have smoked pot sense high school. A thing to calm me done so to speak, not that I would tell anyone, but I was depressed. No because of my father dying, I'm talking in the way you see on commercials. You know...it's not your fault....it's just a chemical unbalance in you're head....horse shit man. There was nothing wrong with me, I was fine...with this....pot. The coffee she poured me was hot, too hot. I had to set it down and let it cool down first. I remember thinking about the site of my father hanging there. The way his body swung stiff from side to side with the sea breeze. Never in my wildest dreams would I ever believe I would see such a thing. I can tell you just about everything. I can tell you how the air smelled and tasted. That there was a slight breeze coming from the ocean that rattled the palm leaves. I can even tell you that I stopped my car just enough to see the shadow reflect on the house....and that's when it hit me. I DO remember something about that night....another shadow, a...running shadow. 'You know what I mean Anthony? Anthony? Hey, are you listening?' mudered Miss Nikki. I guess I had just missed out of the conversation she was trying to have with me. I looked up at her, she was a good looking woman. I could see why my father had kept her around. 'Anthony,' she started again 'I want you to know I'm here for you.' She slowly walked around the table I had been sitting at and stood next to me, leaning down and placing her on my thigh. ' You don't have to be alone here in this bog old house, I can stay....if you like' Slowly she started to unzip my pants. Never before had she ever done this. She always treated me so nicely...so respectful. Now, she comes on to me? Why? Why would she be doing this now? 'Say something Anthony. Your fathers death doesn't have to be so bad' I stared at her with hatred in my eyes. She was my fathers, now she...she....wants the fucking money. 'You....you...slut! How dare you speak of my father and with your hand down my pants! He treated you like gold...like nothing I had ever seen before!' I yelled leaping out of my chair, knocking her back to the floor. 'Can you blame me? Your father is dead, my ticket to the good life is dead. WHAT AM I SUPPOSED TO DO?!?!' This just made the rage grow. EVERYTHING I have ever seen has been about money. Everyone around me either needed it or stole it. Now this woman, a woman who took care of me whores herself out for money...MONEY? 'That's what you want? Money? FUCK IT!! FUCK IT ALL!!!' I yelled finally getting out some aggression. I lifted my right air high into the air and with all my might brought my reconstructed forearm down onto the chair shattering it right down the center. Miss Nikki screamed at the top of her lungs as I lifted my arm high into the air and again brought all my force down upon the table smashing it as well. 'Take it! Take the house, the pools, the cars....' I began looking down on her as she lay back on the floor '...take the jewels, the butlers, the boats. Take Them all!!! AND SHOVE THEM UP YOUR FUCKING ASS!!!' I stomped out of the room, leaving the pile of broken wood and lifted dreams behind me. Not knowing what was going to happen to me next.
