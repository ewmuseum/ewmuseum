Going way back...

8 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8 Aug 05 There is a constant cloud over this man’s life. For some reason his body can never remain his own, for some reason he can not fight back those dark urges that he always hopes will lay dormant. Roberto Abitua has fought valiantly to keep his bleak past and dark future locked up because he knows that it is far stronger than he can ever imagine to be. Mr. Abitua has longed for a life that was never his. He says that he wants to go back to that normal life that he led when he was a youngster back in Spain, but what he doesn’t realize is that his life was never normal. His life began in blood and death, and sadly it will end in the same manner. He doesn’t know it yet sadly, because he still continues on his ridiculous crusade of sending his better half away for good. For a man who was responsible for killing his own mother he sure seems to fight so hard to be some fucking saint. He may have blocked that memory out of his mind ? since hell he was YOUNG, but I was there too and I sure as hell have not forgotten ? November 10th 1979 ? We’re almost there baby, we’re going to be parents!!” A smile is seen underneath Humberto’s mask as he spoke these words. Maria forced a smile, but again the pain over took her. Her breathing is getting very heavy and faster paced. She looks up at her loving husband, who is breathing with her, maybe not purposely but he is doing it. ? I Love you Berto ?” Humberto doesn’t get the chance to say much else, just as he is going to Dr. Rodriguez cuts in. ? Okay this is it, Maria when I say so ? start pushing !!” They were so foolish indeed, they really thought that people like them were really meant to have a happy existence but how could they? They were dirt poor, and Humberto had to sometimes steal just to be able to put food on the fucking table ? how damn pathetic. I can’t say I’d wish what happened to them on anyone ? but hey if they didn’t see it coming then they deserved it. The action begins, Maria begins pushing and Humberto screams every step of the way with her. He tries his best to comfort his wife as she is giving the gift of life, but he is just so mesmerized by it all himself. “There’s the head ?” The Doctor screamed out. “Now Maria just one more big pu” His voice trails off as he looks up at Maria, who doesn’t look well at all. Now giving birth won’t make you the next Beauty Queen, but He knew better. He yells out a few orders at his assistants, who scurry to find him some stuff frantically. Humberto’s look changes from joy to confusion. “What’s going on Doctor?” Humberto asked. What is going on indeed? Life takes unexpected turns all the time, they can not be seen ahead of time nor can they be stopped. It is like a small insect getting pulled into the ocean by a strong tide, they fight, squirm, twist and turn but all they can do is watch as their lives fly through their eyes ? until death comes in and swoops them up. Maria was beautiful but she had no right trying to bring children into this world! She knew the kind of life that they would be forced to have, yet her and this sorry bastard of a husband of hers insisted on doing it anyways. Its sickening sometimes to sit here and rethink all of what happened ? “Some women have difficulties giving birth ? like their bodies aren’t ready for it, and your wife seems to be having problems ? SHIT!! There’s too much blood here! Angelica, get some towels!! And you Hector, get the RD-45 alright !” MARIA! MARIA! You need to push or this Baby isn’t coming out!! Just one more !!” Humberto looks down at his wife, who is really struggling now. She just seems completely exhausted ? her brown eyes seem almost black as if they’re losing their shine. Humberto rubs the top of his Love’s head and whispers ? “One more baby ? and it’ll all be over’We’ll have our baby Boy ? Roberto Juan Abitua” Maria let’s out a low moan but then puts all of her strength into one last push ? and the next sound is like music to their ears. CRYING ? The baby begins to cry as it comes out just as all normal children do. Immediately Dr. Rodriguez hands the baby over to get cleaned up and wrapped in a blanket. Tears of joy begin to stream down Humberto’s face, and he gives a smile to Maria who returns it back ? There he is “That cancer of a man known as Roberto Abitua. The doctor should have shoved that worthless piece of shit right back into the womb. Well I guess I shouldn’t say that because without him I wouldn’t be where I am either, but Roberto has always been weak and it sickens me like nothing before. The man has so much promise yet he hides behind this mask of sensitivity, caring, and emotional distress. It was his weakness that probably drained the life of his own mother. But the joy only lasts a moment ? “DAMN IT ? Angelica, Hector why wasn’t I warned about this !”” The two nurses give a confused look at their doctor ? “About what Doctor?” Dr. Rodriguez looks up at Maria and Humberto and gives a very worried expression. He grits his teeth and closes his eyes ? “Did you know you were having twins?” asked the doctor. Maria barely could respond as she is in bad shape ? and Humberto just freezes, unable to move ? his voice comes out frantic. ? This can’t BE!! She can’t go through this again !! Look at her now, THIS CAN’T HAPPEN!!” ? But it is Mr. Abitua ? she’s bleeding too much though, and this baby isn’t waiting he’s coming now ?” Maria’s breathing is very shallow, the two nurses have pulled in a few more nurses, because this is going to take a lot more. They are all telling her to push ? and Maria fights with every ounce of energy she has. Humberto Is holding onto her hand tighter than ever, waiting for his second child. They fight and fight for her ? shuffling around, cleaning, putting needles in her, using different tools. Then more crying is heard ? a long drug out cry ? Humberto looks down at Maria, and she is very still. He looks towards his second baby, but his crying wasn’t very loud. The screech he is hearing ? is from the machine that his Loving Wife was hooked onto. Dr. Rodriguez screams ? We got a flatline!! We need some juice in here !!” Humberto just backs away ? and slumps down in a corner as the doctor’s fight for Maria’s life. Tears flow down his cheeks ? as he watches the doctor’s put volts of electricity into his wife’s body, and all she does is lay there still and lifeless. Blood is soaked on the floor, on the doctors, and on his wife ? He thought about raising his children, being a husband. The Love him and Maria shared ? that would never be shared again ? They never knew what they were getting into with Roberto and his twin brother. Life is funny sometimes because when it goes wrong you begin to look back on all of the bullshit decisions that you made and the ones that you KNOW you could have done differently but for some reason did not. For making the choices they did, Humberto ended up trading one life in for two ? but he didn’t want that trade. He was heart broken, devastated, and beaten. The father of these two young sons could not handle the fact that he would have to do it alone ? so what did that man do? Well, that is a story for another day. So I guess you could say this story is “To Be Continued?”
