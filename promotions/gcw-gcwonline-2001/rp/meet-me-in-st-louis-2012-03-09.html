Meet me in St. Louis

9 March 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/09/12 As I stand here at the security check-in at Lambert-St. Louis International Airport, I must that I am not perfect. And if I had a failing (if), it would be my single-mindedness. In my zeal to let the GCW know that I had arrived, I made the assumption you all have heard of me. It never occurred to me that there is a whole generation of wrestling fans who've never even heard of Eddie Whisky. "Sir, I'm merely doing my job, please do not moan and writhe suggestively while I pat you down." These TSA officials are no fun. So here I am, hopping on a quick flight up to St. Louis for my big-time GCW debut. Dangerous Games 2012. It's a name with a history. And now, part of that history will be me - Eddie Whisky. "Thank you sir, but I do not require you to remove your pants for this search." Spoilsport. But back to my point. Many of the goobers drooling in the stands have no clue who I, Eddie Whisky - Man O’ War - am. And it is their own damned fault. If they ever took their collective thumbs out of their collective bums and read a magazine, they would know Eddie Whisky – who is me – has been an enormous name in wrestling since before they could read. Now some ofthem can’t read right now. So it’s not my fault they don’t know who I am. Dammit, I am not their kindergarten teacher! “Sir, if you continue to mutter this ‘internal monologue’ under your breath, I will be forced to call my associates over to assist me.” And let us not forget the conspiracy to keep Eddie Whisky from winning Dangerous Games! It is no doubt in part the fault of them that all these stupid fans stupidly don’t know who I am. I mean where was my debut montage? What about an exciting music video demonstrating how ‘Blood Will Run Cold’ or how ‘GCW Will Never Be the Same’? I’ll tell you where they are; not being made because they don’t want me to feel welcome! And they hope if I don’t feel welcome I’ll just wet myself again and run home crying! Again. “This is your final warning sir. KEEP YOUR PANTS ON DURING THIS SEARCH!” They really take the fun out of a full-body search. But back to my point. The Conspiracy Against Eddie Whisky Holding the GCW World Title, or ‘CAEWHoGWT’if you will, still keep trying to not have Eddie Whisky in the Main Event at NC-17. They fear the changes that I, Eddie Whisky, have already implemented. They know that their sweet little spots are all threatened by the Eddie Whiksy guy. Who is me. "No sir, it is not your turn to ‘do me'." The GCW was a bleak and terrible place before Eddie Whisky arrived. There was no spirit. No pizzazz. Hell's bells, it was dull. Everybody respected everybody. There was no conflict. No life. In fact, I could go on and on about what there was not. What there is is a boring world champion. In other words, NOT ME (Eddie Whisky). "No sir, I cannot give you my number. This is the last time I am warning you." So I am going to use a bit of the ol' Man O’ War Magic and make the GCW a fun and happening place. Of course as is my way, I shall waltz in and win the GCW World Championship, just like that. But that is talking about the things I will do. "For the last time, sir; you cannot 'browse' through other peoples' belongings. Please just take your items and enjoy your flight." Damn, I liked those shoes. But that's Eddie Whisky and who he is. Dangerous Games winner. Inevitable GCW champion. Ask anyone, they will agree. Those who disagree are liars and jealous. "Sir, please step out of the line. I have already searched you." No, I tell him, I'm sure you missed a spot in the back. “THAT’S IT! Rogers, Parsons, back me up here! We got us a terrorist!” And with that, my new blue-gloved friends proceed to Taser me. Why is everybody out to get me?
