A Fleshy Predicament

17 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/17/10 [Part I: In the mind of the PAZUZU.] It’s pretty ironic, PAZUZU thinks to himself, that there are billions of spiritually-minded people spanning all corners of the world, who, regardless of their specific religion or sect, have the common dream of losing the “prison” of their fleshly bodies at death and trading it in for immortality. And yet here he was: desperate, angry, confused, and stuck in the exact opposite situation. He had traded in his immortality for a fleshly prison. Or, more accurately, it had been forced on him. His father, of all people, had done this to him. A man who had not even bothered to drop by and say the occasional “what up, dawg?” for over 10,000 years, had recently decided to curse his long-forgotten son. With the AMULET OF KROOG now wrapped firmly around his neck, PAZUZU is now drained of his demonly powers. Yes, the pendant is only a plastic toy from an arcade machine, but it feels much heavier than that. Without his demonic super-inhuman powers, PAZUZU is merely a shadow of his former self. What bad timing, too. With NC-17 around the corner, PAZUZU now finds himself in the biggest match of his short GCW career while at the weakest point in his physical existence. Without his super strength, he’s merely three times as strong as little Kris Michaels, and only fourteen times as strong as that dumbass, Bishop Steele. The heavy burden of being trapped in the human form is almost too much for PAZUZU, THE DARKESS to take. He has even contemplated suicide. No, not because he’s an emo bitch. But because after doing much research at a local Christian fundamentalist church, PAZUZU has concluded that (1.), suicide is the easiest route to return to hell and thus regain his immortal powers, and that (2.), those Christian fundamentalist people are fucking crazy. Unfortunately for PAZUZU, his father, Satan, has sealed off that potential loophole by enlisting the help of some demonic lawyer from Minnesota, who managed to insert some underhanded clause in fine print, the crux of which states that if PAZUZU dies by his own hand, he’ll end up at the gates of heaven. But there’s no quit in the PAZUZ. The potential to still inflict death, despair, and general misery by his human hands inspires him to soldier onward. …That, and the morbid fear of ending up in heaven with the Mormons and having to dine on vegetarian meals with Jesus, discussing soccer, organic gardening, and the finer points of naked golf. “No thanks!” he thinks. Still, the torture and sorrow of being trapped in mortal flesh isn’t what’s really eating at him. Instead, it’s the anger and the sense of betrayal of being cursed at the hands of an immortal that for all PAZUZU knew never even recognized that he ever existed. For a short time, PAZUZU’s mind wonders back to his childhood… [Part II: A brisk frolic down memory freakin’ boulevard.] [The following ditty is sung to the tune of “Father of Mine” by Eve-6, Creed, Everqueer, or whatever generic neo-alternative 90’s rock band that originally sang it. And by “original,” I mean the lyrics, certainly not the over-used melody and all-too-predictable chord progression.] “Father of mine, Tell me where have you been? 10,000 years of hell, Now I’m back on Earth again! But you show up one night, And I’m all like “what the heck!?” And then you ground me from my powers, And put this shit ‘round my neck!” There’s a black and white “home video” style recording of PAZUZU, THE THIRD GRADER! standing confidently in front of home plate, dressed proudly in his torched unicorn hide little league uniform. A fiendish looking hellion throws a literally-flaming fastball toward our young athlete. Grinding his teeth in staunch determination, PAZUZU swings hard and knocks the angel’s tit ball over the horizon. PAZUZU smiles, basking in the glow of the appreciative roar of the crowd. But he looks up and sees an empty seat adjacent to his hideous 5,000-pound troll of a mother, and sighs. [Flash forward to high school graduation. ] PAZUZU steps forward to shake the hand of the school master, an academic-looking Minotaur, wearing horn-brimmed glasses, who also has a noticeable erection. PAZUZ gladly accepts the golden plaque that reads: “PAZUZU: CLASS VALADICTORIAN, ACADEMY OF VILE DECADENCE AND OTHER ASSORTED EVILS.” Again, he’s warmed by the ovation of the crowd. But again, his heart sinks when he sees the empty seat next to his 5,000-pound troll of a mother… [End of the brisk frolic down memory freakin’ boulevard.] [Part III: The doctor.] PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS sighs, prematurely cancelling his trip down memory lane due to the soft gurgle of his empty stomach. If there’s one thing that PAZUZU had yet to get used to with his new earthly body, it was the unending maintenance. The eating, sleeping, drinking, cleaning. These were all chores too tedious and meaningless for him to consider worthy of his time. Take eating, for example. Although he certainly would indulge himself with a light, low-carb snack every now and again in demon form, it was merely for pleasure. It was never done for sustenance. The relentless torture, pain, and anguish that he inflicted on innocent souls were what kept him alive and gave him his energy, not calories. Besides, gorging on human flesh chased by pints of fermented pickled faerie juices probably wouldn’t cut it for his new body. It was having a hard enough time digesting tacos. Despite the fact that PAZUZU has had a growing pain in his stomach for over a week now, he has not lowered himself to the level of humans by engaging in such acts as “studying” the essential functions of his new body. And to read-up on these things, or to otherwise inform himself about them? Hell no! He’d rather be stuck on an 8-hour bus ride sitting next to Bishop Steele. But after a week of trying to feel out the intricacies of his new body, the awkward pain had built up to an almost unbearable level and his patience was wearing thin. Thus, having no other recourse, PAZUZU found himself in his present situation: Sitting on the observation table of a petty human doctor. PAZUZU is almost overwhelmed by intimacy of the doctor’s hands over the course of the observation and on several occasions has to talk himself out of his natural impulse to rip off the physicians head and pull out his puny spine. The doctor is a friendly middle-aged man. He has a round, pudgy face, with matted gray hair and a thick beard. He wears a blue “grandma-knit” sweater. Every time he speaks, he slightly bends his head down so that his eyes can look out over the top of his spectacles and meet PAZUZU’S gaze. After a few moments of placing his hands in various positions around PAZUZU’s abdomen, the doctor finally steps back and nods his head ‘yes.’ “It sounds to me like you’re just hungry, sir,” the doctor confidently diagnoses. PAZUZU shakes his head ‘no’ and says, “I’ve had this aching pain in my stomach for three fucking days now and without so much as a test or examination you claim to know the cause of this illness? That’s absurd!” “Yes,” adds Gnark, faking a tone of authority. “You, sir, are no doctor.” The doctor shakes his head, slightly confused. “It’s just that when you told me that you haven’t had anything to eat in the last week…it, uh, wasn’t too hard for me to diagnose what the cause of that ‘illness,’ is. What I mean is that you should just eat food. And then you’ll feel better. That’s all.” “My evil god! Are you saying that I’m just hungry?” PAZUZU takes the opportunity to hop down from the observation table. “Afraid so,” says the doctor. PAZUZU gets up and paces back in forth across the room, which is none too pleasant for the poor physician, considering the makeup of the demon’s attire: A skull cap with Viking horns and sunglasses, a Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtle “Turtley Awesome!” tee-shirt, snakeskin cowboy boots, and of course, a bright orange thong. “How much food do these pathetic little machines require, anyway?” PAZUZU asks while punching himself in the gut. “Weekly nourishment, I suppose?” The doctor raises a brow before replying, “Daily, actually.” “Oh!” PAZUZU raises his arms above his head and shakes his hands mockingly. “‘Daily’ is it? Absurd!” PAZUZU snaps his neck around to bark a command to his sidekick. “Gnark, tripudiate!” Obediently, Gnark begins to dance happily in place, showing his displeasure toward the doctor in the most disrespectful form that he knows of. The imp continues to dance for several awkward moments and declares “Sir, I dance in your general direction!” PAZUZU nods approvingly. He turns back to the doctor and says, “Yeah, and let me guess: you also recommend that I eat multiple times each day! Hmm? Is that right?” The doctor is beginning to look genuinely confused, if not a little scared as well. “Actually, yes. Three times a day,” says the doctor. “If not more.” PAZUZU grits his teeth in anger. The demon rips the stethoscope off from the doctor’s neck and bends them into a ring. PAZUZU hurls the ring across the office, catching Gnark around the waist. Gnark continues to gyrate around in circles, although somewhat more limited in his mobility now. “Uh, yeah,” says the doctor, “that’s not meant to be a hula-hoop.” “Enough with this!” PAZUZU declares, emphatically. “Come, Gnark. We must be on our way. The so-called ‘grocery store’ awaits our presence.” Gnark is trapped in the manmade lasso but still manages to hop on over to PAZUZU. The doctor takes the opportunity to cautiously begin his backward retreat into the hallway, not wanting to spend any more unnecessary moments with these patients. “But before we go,” says PAZUZU, “there’s just one thing.” The doctor raises a brow. “I’ve heard it through the grapevine that you make offerings of plastic rings and lollipops as concession for meddling with my private parts? Is that true, sir?” The doctor’s jaw drops a moment before he recovers and responds, “well, that’s not exactly why we give them out. And it’s generally just for the kids.” The doctor looks up into the massive demon figure of PAZUZU and swallows hard. “But in your case I think I can make an exception.” “Grand!” PAZUZU reaches out to high-five the doctor who awkwardly accepts. “Right this way…uh, gentlemen,” the doctor says, leading the two out of the office. [Part IV: Munchies.] A moment later PAZUZU and Gnark sit happily on the concrete steps of the hospital surveying the passing traffic with glazed-over eyes. PAZUZU’S pockets are loaded to the brim with a variety of smiley-faced lollipops and rubber hospital gloves. Gnark has an assortment of cheap plastic rings on each of his fingers as well as a bulky tank of NOS mounted onto his back with duct tape. Gnark pulls the mask attachment over his face and takes a deep breath from the tube leading to the tank. He releases the hit with a soft sigh before passing the mask back to the PAZUZ. Gnark rolls onto his back in a fit of laughter. “I’m glad GCW has such a fine health care plan,” PAZUZU says, releasing his own hit. “Yeah. I just wonder why there wasn’t a co-pay,” says Gnark, still recovering from the last hit. “At the very least I was expecting a pile of paperwork at the very least.” “You already said ‘at the very least’,” says PAZUZU as the two fall down in laughter. “Seriously, they seemed to want to get us out of their pretty fast.” [Snap shot back to the inside of the hospital. Under the a pile of wreckage composed of papers, destroyed heart monitors, and smashed fax machines, doctors and receptionists alike are huddled under their respective desks. They hold their collective breath as sparks shoot off from the severed ends of wires that have been ripped out of the wall. Finally, one brave doctor peeks around the corner of his office and says, “I think they’re gone now….” ] “It’s nice to have health insurance,” says PAZUZU, thoughtfully, “but it’ll be even better when I can fill up this aching belly of mine with some disgustingly human sustenance.” The companions somehow manage to stumble down the steps and begin to walk down the empty sidewalk. “You wouldn’t actually consider eating cooked meat would you, master?” “Gnark, I may be trapped in human body for now. But I’d never entertain something as revolting as that.” PAZUZU shudders, as if to make a point. “Although, I must admit that the delicious smelling pork I ate from the dumpster last Saturday, I’m pretty sure now that the green color wasn’t food coloring for St. Paddy’s day.” “You don’t say?” PAZUZU comes to a sudden halt. The two look across the street and smile at the sight of a McDonald’s. “Gnark,” PAZUZU says, slapping his companion on the back, “today we feast.” A couple of minutes and blood-curdling screams later, Gnark emerges from the drive thru window with an armful of parfaits, french fries, and assorted packets of condiments. PAZUZU quickly snatches the load from Gnark and tucks them into his cloak, much to the dismay of his impish companion. Gnark emits what can only be described as a squeamish puppy whine. “Christ, Gnark. You’re four-foot-fuckin’-two. How many calories a day do you need?” PAZUZU sighs, holding out a package of plastic utensils. “This will hold you over for now.” Gnark greedily mows down the gift of sporks and paper napkins. “As for the rest of these delicious culinary treats, I have plans for them,” says PAZUZU, with a wry grin spreading across his face. [Part V: The food bank.] Indeed, PAZUZU does have a plan for the food. PAZUZU and Gnark now stand in front of a small brick building. It’s a one story building with freshly painted white walls and tall elongated windows covered with thick metal bars. Above the dented metal door entranceway is a metal engraved plaque that reads “FOOD BANK” in tall golden letters. “I’m never doing another parfait enema again,” Gnark says, raising his hand as if to take an oath of celibacy. “Don’t be silly. It’s good for you.” PAZUZU lifts his hands high above his head before bending down to complete a few toe stretches. Gnark rolls his neck back and forth and begins a series of fast-paced jumping jacks. PAZUZU looks down the street in both directions before he reaches into his pocket and comes up with a black ski mask. He needlessly pulls the ski-mask down over his skull mask. He looks down at Gnark and nods. At the end of a silent count of three they burst through the doors and bum-rush the food bank. They catch the desk attendant by surprise. The gray-haired hippie women jumps out of her seat and dives for cover. A drunken hobo who was waiting optimistically in line, takes one look at PAZUZU, tosses his paper bag of cheap vodka aside, and falls down into the corner, pissing himself. PAZUZU and Gnark whip out a pair of chicken fajitas and point them at the terrified woman. “Empty your drawers.” There’s an awkward pause as PAZUZU’S nose cringes at the scent of some foul odor. “No, not those drawers…those!” he says, pointing toward the row of cupboards in the background. “Just shittin’ ya,” PAZUZU finally says. A wide grin spreads across his face. Gnark lets out a freakishly high-pitched laugh before devouring his makeshift weapon. “No. We’re actually here to make an investment,” PAZUZU says, taking the time to adjust his red tie in a business-like manner. Which is no small feat, because the tie is literally nailed to his chest. The women recovers -as well as anyone can recover after shitting their pants in public- and gets back to her feet. “Wh- what? I’m afraid I don’t understand.” “Oh, don’t play stupid with me Mrs. Bernie Madoff! We’re onto your food pyramid scheme,” declares Gnark in an accusatory tone. “Now, now,” says PAZUZU, putting an arm in front of his giddy companion, “we need to be professional about this.” PAZUZU slightly bows toward the woman. “Sorry, madam. We’re just such eager investors and we want to make sure that we receive our fair share of the market.” “What market?” the woman asks. “The food investment industry!” says Gnark. “Alright. Let’s cut the bullshit,” PAZUZU says. “Strawberries, Laffy-Taffy, soy burgers…we don’t really care which market. We just want a fair return on our investment.” PAZUZU reaches into his cloak and pulls out a shiny black suitcase. He sets the suitcase on the desk in front of the woman, punches in the combination, and opens it up to reveal a dozen or so leaky McDonald’s parfaits. “Now we’re realistic about this,” PAZUZU says. “We realize that you’re only a local food bank. So we don’t expect more than 5 or 6% interest annually.” “I’m afraid you don’t understand,” the women says. But PAZUZU puts his up his hand and shakes his head ‘no.’ “And about this co-called ‘calm-pound’ interest…” says Gnark, with vitriol in his voice. “Compound interest?” the woman asks, even more confused. “I don’t know how it works in the financial foods sector,” says PAZUZU, “but in my line of work ‘calmly pounding’ anything never works. I don’t care if you need to pound my yogurt or not. You can even get a little frisky with my Ring Dings. Work ‘em over if you have to, ma’am. Just do whatever you must in order to extract interest.” By now the food bank attendant is beyond disbelief. She shakes her head and takes in a deep breath. In a desperate attempt to get these crazy people off the premise, she says “okay…um, you’ll just need to fill out some forms.” She turns and reaches for her phone. “Let me, uh, just get my manager on the line…” “Oh, there’s no need to do this deal on record,” says PAZUZU. He motions for the woman to put the phone down before reaching into his pocket and slipping the woman a banana over the counter. PAZUZU leans toward the woman and winks. “Perhaps we can keep this food investment under the table, hmmmmm?” “Except for the oranges,” says Gnark, “which generally go on top of the table, in a fruit bowl, perhaps.” PAZUZU motions for the imp to be quiet. “Sure,” says the woman, after a long pause. “You can just leave your, uh, investment here. And I’ll give you an update when your investment grows.” “Splendid!” says PAZUZU, closing the briefcase. “We’ll expect weekly statements,” says Gnark. “Of course,” says the woman, feigning her best smile. “Sounds like we have a deal,” says PAZUZU. He reaches out and roughly shakes the woman’s hand. “And you might want to change your pants, mad’am. Shitty britches may be all the rage on Wall Street, but out here it’s highly unprofessional.” With that, there’s a sudden eruption in the air and the room is momentarily covered in smoke. A moment later the woman is left standing… “Oh, shit!” says PAZUZU, who is still standing in the same place as before. “I forgot about the lack of superpowers thing...” He reaches up and angrily brushes his hand against the AMULET OF KROOG! “Better to save the GCW special effects smoke grenades for a rainy day, master.” “Indeed,” says PAZUZU, as they turn to exit the food bank, having successfully invested 11 parfaits, 2 small fries, and a diet Coke, at 5% annual interest. Just another day in the life -or lack thereof- of PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS!
