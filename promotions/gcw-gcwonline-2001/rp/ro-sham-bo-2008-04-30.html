Ro-Sham-Bo

30 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/30/08 START 6:00 pm It’s the usual downtown scene during the late afternoon hours in Sacramento, as the local hoodlums run around the streets and parks wreacking havock. The oily sun projects its UV rays upon the land as we pick things up at the south end b-ball courts. The leader of the local amateur basketball team known as "The KiLLers" Yawny, and his b-boi friends are tearing up some hoops. The sound of sneaker rubber meshing with the rugged terrain bounces off the nearby sky scrapers as Yawny passes to his blood brother; Karl Dee. This guy has mad skills when it comes to dribbling, but he ain’t no shooter. Getting up in the face of the defender, Dee swivels and shovel passes the ball over to his cousin; Lil Jim. LJ takes “er to the rim and pounces down a huge jam. The backboard tiders back and forth from the aftershock from the thunderous dunk! After it crashes through the net, the ball takes an odd bounce off a random pebble as it is redirected to the somewhat busy intersection. Yawny: Oh shit mang, get da ball! Like a mouse running from a cat, Lil Jim "Reggie Bush’s" his way towards the ball, but it was just too late? or was it? The ball comes to a halt resting under the foot of a certain white boy. Lil Jim looks up in a surprised fashion as Clyde Fox stands there, hands on his hips, looking off into the distance like Captain America. Lil Jim: Yo mang, thanks for the save mang. Clyde relinquishes his foot from atop the ball as he reconfigures himself to face Lil Jim. He suddenly points sharply at Lil Jim. His index finger cuts through the air like a sharp knife through butter. The tension raises as Clyde narrows his eyes in on Lil Jim. Fox: I challenge you. Making light of the situation, Yawny bursts into laughter as he approaches what he believes is a crack addict (he’s not too far off). Yawny stares down Fox with evil eyes that suited the traits of a serial killer. The rest of the gang files up behind Yawny and starts jawing. Gang: Awwww shyte! Yawny is going to make you his bitch! With Lil Jim backing up behind his faithful leader, Yawny takes the forefront and folds his arm in a power stance. He then does what his nickname describes best? he yawns as if indicating Fox is no threat. Yawny: What you challen’gen him for? Clyde promptly lifts his left hand up and twirls it around like a spazoid. He then points sharply at Yawny. Fox: RO-SHAM-BO! And with that, Clyde rushes in like a freight train and destroys Yawny’s crotch sector with a massive shin kick! Yawny’s baggy shorts bunch up near his junk as he jolts backwards in indescribable pain! His hands immediately grab his junk for further inspecting. Once all items are accounted for, Yawny, hunched over, gnarls at his goons to get Fox. Karl Dee is first up as he runs in swinging! Clyde ducks a pair of lefts and rights like he was Muhammad Ali. Fox comes up countering with a kick. But not any kick. Fox: RO-SHAM-BO! This time Fox connects with his toes to the junk, going a successful two for two. Karl Dee falls to the ground in defeat as he reacts to the pain. By this time, LJ has entered the scuffle. Lil Jim: What the heck are you doing man? What’s a Ro-Sham-Bo anyways? Fox suddenly acts like a robot and talks in a monotone voice. Fox: Ro-Sham-Bo? as defined by Websters? The act of kicking your opponent in the pee pee and grapefruits. Used in a sentence? “Oh how do you like that bitch” I kicked you in the balls.” Alternative meaning? a game played between two people. Country of origin? America. Ro-Sham-Bo. Are Owe Dash Ess Atch Aye Emm Dash Bee Owe. Ro-Sham-Bo! Just as he finishes his sentence, Lil Jim quickly gets his kick in the groin area of Clyde. However he does not twitch. Lil Jim: What the hell are you? A machine? Fox blinks robotically. Yawny: A man? Fox: No? not machine? nor man. I am more than meets the eye! Yawny, now sweating excessively, starts shaking as he is beyond puzzled. Yawny: You’re a transformer? Clyde pauses for a brief moment? then he snaps out of the robotic like trance and starts giggling like a grade ten school girl after Jimmy, the grade twelve pedophile hits on her. Fox: No silly guys! I’m only messing with you goof balls! I’m a real boy! And with that, Clyde sends off Lil Jim with his third incredibly blatant Ro-Sham-Bo. But that still doesn’t answer the mystery as to why Fox didn’t feel that kick. But that my friends, is another story. With all of the potent basketball players unconscious, except Yawny, Clyde moves in for the final kill. Yawny drags his body across the ground in hopes of escaping the onslaught to come. Yawny: Look man, you want cash; I can hook you up. You need women? I’ve got like three sisters I think. You look like you’re a virgin man; just take them man, JUST TAKE THEM! ANYTHING, JUST DON’T RO-SHAM-BO ME AGAIN! Fox: That doesn’t satisfy my itching. You see, I’m facing THE Ro-Sham-Bo, live next week on WolrdWide. I need to be ready! Sorry, but? you’re time is like the sky? UP! Clyde rushes in for the final kill as Yawny covers his face and braces for impact? RO-SHAM-BO! Clyde swiftly smacks his shin firmly against the shaft which in turn flings backwards and crashes into the grapefruits, sending a painful feeling to Yawny. He screams as if he were being raped by a bunch of gang bangers? but no one can hear him. Satisfied with his kill, Clyde removes himself from the crotch of Yawny. Fox: You have a splendid day now. Oh and, I’d have that looked at if I were you? BIG TIME! Clyde dusts his hands together for a days work well done. He has now become the ultimate player at Ro-Sham-Bo. But will it be enough to beat Ro-Sham-Bo himself? Find out at WorldWide 89! Ro-Sham-Bo defined? END
