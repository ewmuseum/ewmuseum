Mortifera Revealed Pt. 1

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday Oct. 25th Piece by piece each little twist of the wooden pins. The life brought to them by the strings of the masters exquisite hands. Each little motion of his hands and the puppet jumps and twirls and sometimes with a hammer bashes the other puppets. Yet once in awhile a new puppet comes along. Something about the new puppet catches the eyes of the crowds. They are flashy and imaginative, but yet still doesn't compare to the more popular that had come before them. Usually they are just flashes in the pan and answers to a slow fading essence. Then they leave or are sold to the highest bidder by their creator. Then again there are many similarities to the real world isn't there? For example before you stand two men, two men born of sweat, blood, and tears. Both men known as Mortifera. One man has a long hard road before and behind him. Another man here is a warrior of heritage and ancestry. Both men have come before a stage known as GCW and upon this stage is a monstrosity of a performance known as Dangerous Games. What better way to demonstrate Dangerous Games with Dangerous Things otherwise known in Latin as Mortifera. Come with us as we explore the world of Mortifera in more depth than many have known. Begin to understand what is in the disturbing mind of Shaman and the unpredictable thoughts and actions of Jorge Samuelsson. Come lets look at Jorge Samuelsson as a child. Before he became the man he is now. Let us look at the humble beginnings. Trondheim, Norway October 1974, The air was thick with fog this evening coming from the gold licks from the oceans and forests. The air seemed to carry the cold forever these days as the man watched through the windows. The old man reached up and rubbed his scraggly whiskers. He turned and looked to the right where his youngest daughter laid. The father of her unborn child had abandoned her. He was young and ignorant. Forgetting that he had a strong heritage of upbringing and pride. He was looking out the window as his daughter opened her eyes and spoke. "Father where is Sven?" Her father shook his head as he turned away. "That coward of a man ran off to America four days ago. He was crying something about being rich and famous." His daughter let a few tears fall down her cheek as she whispered quietly. "Father it is time." He turned his head at her and nodded softly. He was not going to be angry at her nor the child, but his anger would be towards the unborn child's father. The old man walks across the room and picks up the receiver on the phone and dials the old rotary. He waits a moment as a voice picks up on the other end. "Send the House Mother it is time." He listens then hangs up as he turns and watches his daughter as she begins to form light beads of sweat on her forehead and cheeks. He tapped his sleeves impatiently as his arms were crossed. He looked over his shoulder as the door opened and the House Mother walked in and hurried over to his daughter. She looked her over carefully and looked to the Father. "It is time to leave sir. Please be patient this should not take long." He turned quickly on a heel and exited the room. He headed down the corridor after he slowly shut the door. His mind was on something now and he would not be stopped. He opened the door to his study and walked across the room and sat in a wooden chair against the wall. He tapped a brass ashtray and began packing his pipe. He slowly and carefully packed the pipe with each leaf carefully placed in the ivory holding part. He then nodded as he tapped the base on the ashtray. He reached over and pulled a wooden match out and lit the pipe as he took slow drawn puffs to evenly light the tobacco inside. He then puffed the pipe casually as the smoke swirled about the room as if the wisps of smoke were tendrils grasping for something unseen. The old man raises his eyes slowly as if focusing on something. His hands turns and grasps the phones receiver and dials a number. He waits as the slow rotary dials each number. He then waits patiently as a voice on the other end answers. He speaks carefully and thoughtfully. "Bring me Telford from his office. He used to be old Scotland Yard and now he is an Investigator. Tell him i want him to find someone and keep tabs on him. The cost is of no concern to me." He hangs the phone up and takes a long slow puff on the pipe as the smoke billows much like and old coal driven steam locomotive. The old man stood and tapped out the ashes and remaining tobacco from the pipe. He placed it down carefully as he had done a thousand times before. He thought to himself about the times before he became a father. A military man on the high seas as a Naval officer. That is why he chose this place as his home. Here he met his wife the woman who changed his path and destiny. Gave him 3 beautiful daughters and a son he had lost not three winters ago in a vicious crime of hate and rage. He pondered for a moment as his heart pounded in his chest with a pang of pain for the loss of his son. He whispered to himself as he remembered. "A father should never bury his children before himself." He turned and walked down the corridor back towards his daughters room. he suddenly heard a cries of a child. A young strong newborn child. He slowly walked to the door and stopped his hand short f the brass door handle. He listened as he reached, but before he could open the door the House Mother opened the door and spoke. "You may enter and see your Grandchild. He straightened himself and walked into the room looking at his daughter as she seemed to glow with the child in her arms. He looks over at his daughter as she looks up at him and speaks. "Father i have named my son. His name will be Jorge. Named after my brother Yorminen for whom we lost 3 winters ago. Yor for Yorminen and the Ge as in good. A strong heritage and lineage he will carry Father. I would like you to teach him all of it." She smiles and hands the baby Jorge to her father as he smiles broadly. "A fine man he will grow to be and a strong member of the Samuelsson clan." As he speaks with pride holding his newborn grandson. Finally the scene is surreal with the arrival of one of the futures prominent wrestlers from Europe. The pages begin to turn and write the future from this point forward. Present day.... Now you see that Jorge Samuelsson has had a pretty common beginning. How, who, or what had changed him? What had played the chords within the man to make him angry and uncaring about the world about him? What would have caused this man to join forces with someone he had hated? Jorge Samuelsson teamed with Shaman for unknown reasons and the beginning is merely a beginning chapter in the mans life. There is no means to an end here, but yet there is a means to understanding the fires that have been lit in the past leading to these forwarded days. Shaman is just as complicated an individual. Most of his story has already been told, but what happens when we put you in the place of his childhood and show you the beginnings of his happiness. Will you the reader be here to begin the journey of understanding? We will return to this course and path as we seek answers for both of these men and what makes them who they are. We will return from the search for knowing by Jorge Samuelsson. He will come to travel around the world in search of his father. Right up to his own beginnings as a wrestler and his fateful first meeting with Shaman. As we will delve deeper into the mystery that is Shaman. His anger and confusing tendencies as of late. The loss of one of the few men he saw as a true brother in the ring. The turn of events that caused him to join forces with the one man whom had ever injured him and put him on the shelf with injuries. Shaman may be unraveling at the seems or merely retooling for what is yet to come. Yet to understand this we will have to understand the man and the child of the past. His hard fought days. The losses of his father and mother, the why's and how's of his Grandmothers importance in his life. We will learn why there is no surrender in this man. As we learn about these two men there in lies the answers to what the make up of Mortifera is. Bringing light to the darkness through the rage and anger. There will be more questions as we seek answers. Yet i am here to try and ease that curiosity and attempt to find the truths hidden within the very heart and soul of Mortifera. Who am i you ask? I am the narrator, i am the protagonist, and of course i am the librarian of history. You could say i know Mortifera most interestingly. From the beginning and till the ends of their days i will chronicle their journey and answer all that there is. For this story is merely beginning. Come with me on the journey and i will reveal the answers you wish to seek, but be warned some of this is not for the feint of heart. There are things that should be best left alone. Soon i will reveal more. For now i bow out and leave the mystery to you the readers.
