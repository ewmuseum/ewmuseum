True Perfection: Chapter I

1 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/1/09 True Perfection: Chapter I Tuesday June 30th 11:33 am (Miami, Florida) The camera comes on inside the home of Bishop Steele. As the camera is filming Bishop is just walking downstairs from the bathroom. He heads into the kitchen and opens the fridge. He grabs a Miller Lite bottle and then heads out to the poolside where his fiance Amelia Devon is lounging in a chair getting her tan. He sneaks up behind her and starts rubbing sun tan lotion on her back. She gets startled for a moment but then relaxes. As he rubs the lotion in he speaks: Bishop Steele: "Hey baby I just got a call from the GCW and I have been signed to a contract." Amelia Devon: "How do you know for sure that you have a contract with the GCW? I mean did they actually say you were signed or did they say you have to tryout?" Bishop Steele: "As far as I know, I have a contract with the federation and why do you think that I'm not signed with the GCW?" Amelia Devon: "Cause babe, when you was in the KWWF for those 4 yrs you got screwed out of money. You wrestled for them and was the champion for a long period of time but you never got paid the amount of money you was worth while you was wrestling. I just don't want that to happen again like last time. I'm looking out for you baby and I want you to make the money you are suppose to make and what you are worth is all." Bishop Steele: "Well I'm glad you care mamcita. and I love you so much." Bishop hugs Amelia and then walks back into the house. He goes into the den and starts to think about everything that is going on with his career. He begins to speak: Bishop Steele: "I have a opportunity to wrestle for one of the biggest and elite federations known to man. I have the opportunity to prove to everyone who ever doubted my career and skill in the ring. Most importantly I have the opportunity to become the best wrestler that ever stepped into the squared circle. The GCW has never witnessed a wrestler of my caliber. They have never seen a wrestler with a perfect physique like this. They have never heard such proper speaking and charisma like this. They have never seen swagger like this. Thats right you are looking at pure perfection. You are looking at the next true icon of wrestling. You are looking at Simply the best wrestler to ever be apart of a wrold recognized federation. I will come into GCW and be the best. I will walk the fine line of perfection and greatness. I will domintae all who dare face me in that ring. I will deliver pain and suffering to all who dare cross my path. When I make my debut in the GCW, you better believe it will be a night to remember for all ages to come. GCW prepare to reach new hieghts and new measures as I make my presence known soon. I'm Simply Sweet, Simply Perfect and Simply Incredible." Bishop puushes the camera out of the way and walks out of the room. The camera gets a lasting impression of Bishop walking back outside...... Tuesday June 30th WW113 Live The camera comes on backstage of the Live WW113 event. As the camera is filming Bishop Steele walks infront of a camera backstage so the crew pans on him and follows him. Bishop turns down hallways left and right. Eventually running into a dead end. He anxious. He turns around and sees the camera man. He smiles and then walks towards the camera. He speaks: "You will stay filmed on me cause the GCW needs to hear this. I might not beable to make it to the ring but I can make it backstage for sure. I know everyone in the arena awaiting for the next match has there attention faced on me now. If not you better watch now. I decided to make myself known alittle bit early. I'm here to just show you what true perfection looks like. I mean as soon as I walked in this venue the people could feel real greatnes entering. I told I will make people turn heads and make rating just sky rocket. Come next World Wide I will be in the arena and I will be able to make it to the ring area. I will humiliate, desimate and simply destroy someone in that ring and when it is over you will see why I'm Simply Sweet, Simply Perfect and Simply Incredible!" Bishop shoves the camera to the floor.
