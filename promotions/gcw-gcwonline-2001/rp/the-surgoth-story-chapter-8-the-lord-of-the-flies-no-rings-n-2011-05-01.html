The Surgoth Story - Chapter 8 - The Lord of the Flies... No, Rings... No, Lord of Nothing!

1 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/01/11 It was coming up on two weeks and I had still not found Celis Grom. Time was running out for Diamond City and possibly for the whole world. It goes far beyond that even. The fate of the Spiritual World was in my hands. If Celis Grom truly can see into the future and if that future really is possible, it had to bed stopped. My partner, the Vamlisk, who I recently found out was my former partner Ronan, had a few leads on Celis Grom that pointed to a warehouse down near the docks of Diamond City. That was my destination now as I drove in the direction of the docks in a black, 2011 Ford Mustang. I knew I was breaking the speed limit but at this point I really didn’t care. Time was short and that became more of a reality as I seen a bright blue light shoot up into the sky from the direction I was headed. I put the petal to the metal once I had seen this. Such an awful, creepy feeling came to me as I seen this ray of light. It wasn’t a ray of hope but a ray of sheer terror. My hair stood on end as I was staring at this light. As I got closer, my fear became greater. I pulled up to the warehouse and a large shockwave came from it leveling the warehouse. The power was so strong it sent my car, with me in it, into and end over end crash. I held on as best I could, hoping that I would make it out alive. If this is only the start of the end, I can only imagine what is to come after. The car came to a sudden crash, sending me out of the windshield and onto the parking lot of the warehouse. I had made it out of the crash with a few cuts and bruises, but otherwise I seemed fine. I got to my feet and brushed myself off. As I looked up, a mob of people were coming after me. I heard a thud next to me and there stood my best friend and partner, Ronin. “You’re late!” I yelled to Ronin as the sound from the ray was almost defining. “Me? Late? Never!” replied Ronin. “I don’t think we’re making it to Celis without a fight!” “It looks that way, but it should be fun!” I pulled out my gun ready to fire once the mob was close enough. Ronin pushed my police issue Barretta down. I looked at him as he shook his head. “I don’t think their’s enough bullets in that gun to take all those down!” Ronin yelled at me. “Then what would you suggest?” I asked. “Let’s do it the old fashioned way,” replied Ronin as he pulled a sword out from under his trench coat and handed it to me. He then pulled another sword out and got into a fighting stance. “Could be fun!” I said as I looked up and down at the sword. “Ready?” “As ready as I’ll ever be!” We went rushing into the mob with are swords at the ready. As we got closer I began to realize that it was more clones of me. Once close enough I began slashing my sword in any direction I possibly could, taking off arms, legs and heads. Blood splashed everywhere. After slashing about fifteen of them I was just about covered in blood from head to toe. Just as fast as we brought them down, more and more seem to come at us. We were overwhelmed and I knew that Ronin and myself couldn’t continue to do this for much longer. Something had to give and it had to give soon. I began to Punic, much like I had done while in the apartment when I was under attack from clones before. The scar on my right hand began to itch and burn. I knew something was coming and not only would it hurt the clones around me but I knew Ronin was in danger. “Ronin, run! I’m about to blow!” I yelled as loud as I could over the loud sounds coming from the rays and the growls and moans that came from the clones. Ronin began hacking and slashing his way away from me as quick as he could. I didn’t know how much longer I could hold the power within me. It was no use, I hoped that Ronin would be far enough away. As the power grew, the more pain I felt. It brought me to my knees and clones began to swarm on top of me. A few seconds later I felt the energy leave my body in a large white explosion. Just as quickly as it had all started, it had ended. I was able to survive the blast of energy from my body this time and I seen the fallout from it. Nothing around me was standing. All the clones were now on the ground and the only thing I could see before me was the ray of light. At the bottom of the ray of light stood Celis Grom at a computer on a metal platform. He was controlling a device that was shooting the ray of light into the clouds that were now swirling above. I looked around for some sort of sign that Ronin was still alive. “Ronin!” I yelled as loud as I could. A few yards away I seen a few of the clones bodies moving. Out from under the bodies came Ronin, not looking to well. He got to his feet, holding his left arm to his side and his right hand was holding onto the left shoulder. “How about being a little extra cautious with that when you’ve got friends around!” yelled Ronin back to me. “I thought you would be able to handle more than that, guess you still are weak,” I laughed. “Smart ass as always!” Ronin yelled. “Some things never change!” “Enough talking!” said a bombing voice that I could hear all around me. I knew the voice well enough to be Celis Grom. “I told you, you failed experiment, that this day would come and all you could do was laugh in my face when I said it!” “And I told you that I would stop you!” replied Ronin. “You’re to late, the plan is already in motion. Soon, a portal to the other side will open! Demon and angel alike will come through and start the epic battle that will assure in a new world! A new leader will rise up and write the new laws that will govern the physical world and spiritual world alike!” “You forget Celis, I stopped you once and I will stop you again!” I yelled to Celis. “This time I won’t hesitate to put a bullet right in your head! Turn off the machine!” “Nothing short of a miracle will make that happen Van! The only way to stop what is coming is for me to input the code that will shut down the machine! If you kill me you will never get the code, but I won’t tell you the code no matter what you do! I was assured everything will be made right once my new God is in control!” I ran at Celis Grom and with no resistance, I tackled him to the ground. The sound coming from the ray of light that shot up into the clouds was almost defining once I got so close. I held onto Celis’s lab coat and lifted him up toward me so I could make sure he heard me. “What you are bringing is the end of the world! Who are you to decide the fate of the entire world!? You’re no more than a murdering piece of shit! Either way. I will kill you, code or no code! You get to decide when you die!” I began slamming Celis Grom’s head into the metal grating to the platform we were standing on. With each time I busted his head off the grating, it shook the entire platform, making it feel like it would just fall apart under us. I brought him back up to me, blood dripping from the back of his head. “Are you ready to tell me now!?” Celis Grom just laughed at me and said, “Kill me, I don’t care! I will be reborn! You are simply making sure that happens sooner! I will be one of his Lords!” “You’ll be the Lord of nothing!” I started beating his head into the grating more and more. I didn’t stop this time. He didn’t once scream, only laughed with each blow. I kept going until I could no longer hear him. Celis Grom was finally dead and he could no longer hurt anyone else and that was all that mattered. Justice was finally served on the unjust murderer. Everything around me began to go muffled. “Vaaannnn..... V..aaa..... aaa.....nnnnnn.....” I heard a voice yell to me but it was all muffled and choppy. I wasn’t sure who it was or what was even going on. Things began to spin around me faster and faster. I felt like I was going to throw up. Then suddenly everything became clear again. I glowing ball of light surrounded my hand. The energy from it was incredible. I could hear the voice yelling clearly now and it was Ronin, yelling my name. “Vaaaannnn! You’re beginning to find who you are! Use it Van! Use the power to destroy the machine!” I nodded back to Ronin. I looked back around at the control panel. Then I heard it. Loud voices and screams were coming from above. I could see it, the swarm of angels and demons that were fighting as they were coming into our world. I practically could see the spiritual world. It was both beautiful and terrifying. I looked back down at the control panel, brought my fist into the air, and slammed my fist down into the control panel. The energy left my hand as I demolished the control panel. The energy began to flow through the wires of the machine, frying every circuit in the machine. The machine shut off and the ray of light no longer shot into the sky. Everything was suddenly quiet. I fell to my knees in disbelief. I knew that when I started this job that things would be interesting. I never expected to be like this. I thought I was only helping the people of the city when, in fact, the fate of the entire world was in my hands. I felt a hand on my shoulder as I took everything in, “You did what you had to do Van. He was a bad man, and should he be allowed to live, he could begin his plan all over again. Celis didn’t need to be out of prison to make this happen. There are others and we need to stop them as well. We’ve stopped them for now, but they will come up with a new plan.” I said nothing as I just looked all around me at all the carnage this had actually caused. Clone bodies we’re everywhere. Blood covered the whole parking lot of the warehouse. I realized how much damage this thing could have done if it had not been turned off. I heard police sirens now, in the distance, along with the sound of a helicopter coming from a distance. I looked at Ronin and said, “You might want to get going. I’ll find a way to help you but you have to hide for now. They think you’re dead and it’s better off that way for now.” “Thank you Van, you’ve done something great today. You’ve become a hero.” “So have you.” “But you’re the only one that knows that Van, and you’ll be the only one to know,” replied Ronin. “For now friend, but they will know, I promise.” Ronin nodded at me and then vanished. It was great to know that my old friend was still alive, and it was almost as if he has guided me this whole way. He’s helped me understand this power inside of me. It now felt like the energy was gone and that my power had disappeared, but I have a feeling that when I need it, I will be able to use it again. Police cars pulled into what little was left of the parking lot. A helicopter spot light began to shine on me. I heard a voice from the helicopter. “Van! Are you alright?” it was the voice of Lily. I simply just waved back. I was fine now. The world was safe, my best friend is still alive, and I have her to go home to. Things couldn’t be any better. Epilog Everything is dark, but a male voice could be heard, “Celis has failed, the plan has failed.” “So it has,” replied another male voice. “Celis Grom was to weak to accept the task he was given. We have only been delayed. The new world will be ushered in. It’s time to go to plan B.” “I’m sure this one will be a success.” “We need Van, but something tells me we won’t be able to persuade him that easy.” “I have an idea said a third voice that was female.” End of Book 1
