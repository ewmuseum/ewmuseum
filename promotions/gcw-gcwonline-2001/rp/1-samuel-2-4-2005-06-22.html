Date: June 22 The Book of Genesis; SJV - Shawn Johnson Version - Chapter 1. - In the beginning, there was Lopsang. Act l If ever there was a Buddhist sherpa that was the raddest man on the planet, this man was the one. His name is Lopsang. If your wondering what a sherpa is, it’s one of those mountain guys in the hills of those icy mountains you see in movies with Ben Affleck. More Recently, a lot of people have been wondering where the connection came in with Lopsang, and Shawn Johnson, the hero of our story. The story begins in the mountains of Tibet. A weary and tired Shawn Johnson has come upon a temple or sorts at the base of a mountain whilst on a backpacking trip through Asia. “Wha..” asks Shawn as he looks up to the large, majestic temple almost as if he forgot where he was going. After all, this WAS a intense trip. “Just a few more miles..” Shawn kept telling this to himself, as if he STILL had something to prove. As if him leaving behind his family and the one thing that loved the most behind. It wasn’t his fault that he was all the way out here, Shawn always thought. What else are you supposed to do when the fans you used to so love don’t love YOU anymore. Shawn pauses and looks at the gigantic set of stairs in front of him. “Son of a bitch.” quipped Shawn. It seems like it never ends. Always some obstacle, my efforts are never good enough. Always the last mile. What happens when you stop having that desire to go the extra mile? “You end up in Tibet” Shawn cracks as he chuckles and begins to walk up the stairs. This building was like one Shawn had only heard of. He half expected Jet-Li to do a flip out the window and come to attack him. Shawn finally makes his way to the top of the stairs and stops to breath for air. Its been many of seasons since Shawn had done so much work. He got lazy there towards the end, everyone knew it. When Shawn realized it, it all hit him at once. At 25, he was washed up. I guess that’s what you get for starting so young. A pair of bad knees. Speaking of, Shawn’s knees are burning with heat so intense, Shawn wouldn’t call you a liar if you said it looked like hell unleashed. The mixture of his frozen skin from the weather and the deep burn in all of his ligaments made him want to pass out. “Maybe I can just take a rest inside this temple for a bit.” said Shawn. He knew he had no idea where he was. He wondered if they if was still feminine to ask for directions even if he was stuck in some hillcrest. Shawn dropped his backpack and walking stick and pulled back the hood of his tattered brown cloak to reveal dirty looking, coarse dirty blonde hair. Shawn used to be so good looking. His looks used to make up for his talent actually, or to tell the truth, lack thereof. “Here goes nothing” says Shawn as he rings the giant latch on the wooden door. About two minutes go by and Shawn is getting impatient. He lifts the latch once again and slams it on the door, this time letting out a much louder noise. “What am I supposed to do now” Fuck.” wondered Shawn. He looks around and sees nothing in sight. This is the only place for miles and not a damn person here. Just then, Shawn hears shuffling inside. “Hello, is anyone in there?” asks Shawn sternly. The door cracks open slowly and a small man peeks his round head through the door. - Temple in the hills - Act ll “What a odd looking man” thought Shawn as he really good a better look at this?”?different looking man. He must be a Tibetan monk of some sort. A man of no more than 5’4, I would say about 65 years of age. Maybe somewhere around 130. Balding, grayish brown hair accents his blue eyes in a eerie fashion. He has no facial hair and wears a orange tattered sheepsman cloak with a yellow belt. ? Can I come in for a bit, little friend?” Shawn asked the man, backing up incase he was a ninja of some sort. It had been years since Shawn played Ninja Gaiden, but he still remembers some moves from the game. Better yet, he also remembers some Shinobi thrust kicks. There is a awkward pause as the man just looks at Shawn, in a death stare of sorts. The men then moves back and opens the door. Now this place?.this was a place of brilliance. It was so complicated in its architecture, but the simplicity of the temple also shown through. The colors of the walls, all different seemed the merged in the middle of the room to form one ultimate color. There was no one else in the temple, well, as far as he could see anyways. The man scurried off to God knows where, just leaving Shawn behind. “Thanks man! I guess I will just make myself?”?at home.” yelled Shawn. What in the hell was that little guy about anyways? What goes on in a place like this? Shawn had no idea. Tell you what though, it wasn’t much warmer in here than it was outside. In the corner sits a little mound of fresh snow. Shawn HAD to sit down, he had no concept of time whatsoever. What day was it? Who knows. What time was it? Since the sun was still out, probably day. Shawn walks over to a pew in a corner of the building and sits down. There is a large bowl of water with a ladle next to it. He picks up the ladle and lets the water pour into his mouth. Oddly refreshing. There were murals of what seemed to be a mural of the battle between Satan and the archangel Michael battling for supremacy. “A Christian mural in a Tibetan temple” Odd to say the lease? Shawn knew that something was up here. He had the strangest feeling he had been here before. It was all being to look so familiar. Right then, the small man from before comes walking from around the corner. There is no sound to be heard in the temple, except for the shuffling of the mans feet. The man walks up to Shawn bearing gifts. “Thank you.” says Shawn to the man as he takes a couple of items from his hands. Shawn unfolded the big, white cloth to reveal a fantastic hand made robe. Accompanying this robe was a brown rope belt, a Bible and something in a box. “Har-har. I get your point” says Shawn as he opens the box to reveal a piece of home made soap. A Revelation - Act lll Shawn felt nice and refreshed after that shower. Its been a long time since his body was actually clean. Too long to remember to tell you the truth. Shawn finishes drying himself off and puts on his white robe. He ties it around the waist with the brown rope belt. “Ha, too bad my hair isn’t brown, or else I would look just like a certain Jewish carpenter” Shawn remarks as he brushes the knots out of his hair. It was true to, Shawn would look like Jesus. That’s what happens when you don’t shave for a month. Speaking of Jesus, what was that shit all about when that man gave him the Bible? Isn’t this a breeding ground for Muslim and Buddhist extremism? I guess not everything they teach you is true. Shawn finishes cleaning up and walks out of the cleaning area. Shawn wanders around the temple for a minute or two looking for a sign of life. “Hello?” asks Shawn. “Anyone here?” repeats Shawn. No replies. Shawn hears what sounds like a clang dishes or plates or something of that matter. He follows the noises towards the end of a hallway. He turns left to find the man from earlier in what seemed to be a kitchen. The man stops moving to look over and see Shawn. They exchange glances briefly, then the man goes back to what he was doing. Shawn looks over to the left and sees a table, he walks over to it and sits down. The man continues to cook food as Shawn sits at the table. “So”.my name is Shawn.” Shawn spurts out nervously towards the man. “Lopsang” replied the man in broken English. It might be broken, but at least it’s a form of English. It’s been awhile since Shawn heard his native tongue. ? Thank you for having me in you?..home, I suppose? quipped Shawn. Shawn waited for a response from Lopsang, but revived nothing. Maybe he didn’t speak English at all, maybe Lopsang wasn’t even his name. But at this point, it doesn’t really matter. It doesn’t seem like an insane amount of dialogue is about to ensue. Lopsang walks over to Shawn and drops a plate in front of him. “What in the world is this..” Shawn wonders under his breath. “Eat.” barks Lopsang. Fine’What’s the worst that could happen. Shawn begins to eat the slop that’s in front of him. As he expected, it tastes like absolutely nothing. Bland slop, my favorite. Shawn takes a moment away from feeding his gorde to pause and notice a cross on Lopsang’s neck. “Jesus?” Shawn asks as he points to the cross on Lopsang’s neck. Lopsang just stares at Shawn. Shawn points again and asks the same question to no avail. He does a crucifixion pose to Lopsang. “Jesus?” asks Lopsang to Shawn. “Yes, Jesus.” laughs Shawn. Lopsang walks away and comes back with a black book in his hands. It looks like any ordinary Bible, but when Shawn opens it, he is most compelled. Shawn had never seen a Bible in a Asian language. Well, to tell the truth, Shawn very rarely saw any kind of Bible, period. ? I suppose that’s why you didn’t know Jesus? said Shawn. “Ya-Su?” asks Lopsang “Ya-Su is Jesus, eh” Chinese, Hakkata, Mandarin? Maybe Tibetan.” said Shawn like his AA degree in Communication studies really was going to help here. Just then, Lopsang reaches from behind his chair and grabs another book. He brushes it off and hands it to Shawn. Shawn grabs it and.. “English Bible, eh” Shawn remarks as he opens it up. This thing had to be 300 years old. And how in the world did it get here? “Thank you.” said Shawn to Lopsang as Lopsang bows to Shawn. Shawn was stuck. He always wanted to read the Bible but always told himself he was too busy. It was always a half-truth at least. He was SHAWN JOHNSON, a former Champion of the World. Who was this man holding this book right now? This surely was a shell of the former man. A broken man. A once proud man. “What could it hurt” says Shawn as Lopsang stands up and walks out of the room to leave Shawn alone. “Here goes nothing?”” says Shawn as he opens up the book for the first time in a long time.
