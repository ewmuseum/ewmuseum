Date: Timeless Ashes to ahses dust to dust. Am I dead or is this just some wicked dream. I open my eyes, yet i still see black. I hear black, I feel black. I fear black. I'm lying in silk, come take the lid off this block. I don't remember much. My brother? Can that be true? My brother whom I thought was dead has come back for me? Didn't I kill him with my father? This must be some sotr of dream, wake up. Ok, here I go, I will open my eyes. Black. What the fuck? All Black. What is this black. I must be buried alive. That's my only conclusion. This must be my end. My brother and father got the best of me. This is the end of me. Or is it? I can't decide which way to go. To the light or stay in the black. Someone help me I'm confused, I'm...I'm...fuck I'm scared. What is this goddamn black, what is this deafening silence? I will close my eyes, hopefully this is all a dream. How long have I been out? There's still this deafening black. This sinister silence. Let's start from the beginning. I'm beaten and bloodied in belmont. I'm in the ring, crippled and pissed. I hear music, there's this huge giant motherfucker. His face is bloodied and chancre sored to holy hell. He walks with a hideous limp, and his left arm looks broken. I remember he takes my most brutal onslaught, and he, what does he do? Fuck I can't remember what he does. Did he put me in this black? He had to, that's the only logical reason....Who's there? I hear someone outside, it sounds like a group, who the hell is there. I hear this disgusting chirping hellacious laugh. Is that my father? No that can't be. Are they trying to open this black? I'm beginning to like this black. It's evil and dark like my own psyche. Look at me analyzing my own psyche. I dug up a dead girls body for Christ's sake. Oh hell, they are opening it. That's one bright light. I see silhouettes. Five of them. Is it my minions? No, come one eyes focus, who are these people. They are black, total blackness. Once again blackness overcoming me. Eyes, focus for fuck's sake, focus. Blurred blackness, blurred, blurred, this damn blur. What are they putting on my mouth. I breathe in, breathe out, breathe in, breathe out, wow my eyes are heavy. I'm falling asleep, catch a glimpse, who are they, w....h....o............. What's with this bright light? Why can't I move, why can't I think clearly. There's some talking, but I don't understand what they are saying. I feel a jolt! What the... 'Stand!' My legs lift off the table and up I go. 'Brilliant. He will make us more powerful than ever.' What the hell? 'Back to your bed.' My legs carry me towards the casket. I climb in, lie down, close my eyes, and WHAM! Lid closed shut. I'm no longer in control here....
