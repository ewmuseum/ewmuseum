The Journey continues

9 January 2011

Date: 01/09/11 At the show,     Standing before a mirror Shaman simply looks at himself as he has the Hardcore Title draped over his shoulder. His eyes focused on himself as he seems to be searching his own soul for an answer. He closes his eyes as he lowers his head staring at his hands. As he bring them back up he notices three figures behind him.   “So you have come once again to try and get to me before the show. Look closely Gentlemen and realize I hold the Hardcore Title.” He smirks slightly as he pats it while it sits on his shoulder. “Not going to be easy for you to take it, as I had come and taken it last week. You thought me down and out, which was your first mistake.”     Pulling the hardcore title from over his shoulder, he traces the metal printed letters and ridges.     “It is kind of funny you know, Mayhem takes this from the Tag Team Champions Mortifera by sheer luck and determination. I take it from him last week by brutal force and determination. The wheel seems to turn once again.” Placing the title down on a table nearby, turning he looks back into the mirror. Staring at the three figures once again his eyes focus and begin to narrow.   He looks right at Donny Diamond. “You are pathetic and claim to be able to drive anyone out, yet you get one victory and you believe you’re invincible.” Shaman waves his hand in front of him.     “Ask yourself three simple questions Diamond. One is simple really why are you the lackey for someone else? Religion isn’t your thing, but then again if you continue to cross my path you will find it quickly.” Grinning a bit, he raises his hand, this time with two fingers.    “Two is the more curious question, Why the obsession Diamond? It is as if you’re looking at me as the type of person who will be like your damned prom date?” Raising his eyebrow, he chuckles. “I am flattered, but don’t swing that direction.”     Raising three fingers, he narrows his gaze once again.   “Three did you really think that you were the wonder child that you believe yourself to be? I mean seriously you posture like an eight year old bully in a preschool playground. Problem is you are nothing in front of everyone else in this fight.”  Shaking his head he sighs slightly.     He turns his gaze to Dorn Tracer, No emotion upon his face as he speaks. “Ah yes you, the one who isn’t quite the fit in the picture.” Raising his finger, he taps his temple as he turns.   “Your angle is a bit confusing at the moment, but I think I have it figured out really. Has been done before and is no secret. Bring your game through your new lackey, when you blink I will drive the fist through the back of your head.”     He turns and looks straight at Bryan Mayhem. He points at the man he knows very well. “We don’t have to go over this again. You and I know the stakes and in the end we will do what we do best.”     Reaching down he lifts the hardcore title and turns walking away into the darkness. Las Vegas Reservoir 3 am , Several officers from the Las Vegas Police department are walking around the edge of the water. Two FBO agents walk forward flashing their badges as a Detective walks up to them and motions them to come forward as he escorts them down onto a beach. The Detective lights a cigarette as he points out over the water. “Gentlemen I am Detective Reischmann, We found the body of your undercover agent here with a message.” He handed the FBI agent the message and turned and pointed at the covered body on the beach. One of the FBI agents turns and walks over to the body and flips back the blanket covering him. Shaking his head he stands up turning back towards the other two men. “That is Thomas, so Detective any idea who shot him in the head?” “Agent Malone was it? Well truth is we have no damned clue what you Federal Agents have in mind in the first place. You stick a damn rookie in our midst in an undercover sting and he winds up dead? Sounds to me you need to get your house in order.” He flips the cigarette at one of the FBI agents and turns pointing at Las Vegas beyond lighting up the skyline. The Agents turn themselves facing the city as the other one speaks. “What happens in Vegas stays in Vegas.” Frowning he looks down at the dead undercover agent slowly being covered back up. “Malone prep Thomas for transport back home, I am going to contact the DEA and Homeland Security. We need to retrace Thomas's steps.” Turning he looks at the LVPD Detective. “We'd like your assistance on this matter.” Waving his hand over his shoulder nonchalantly at the FBI agent. “Yeah yeah Agent Cristoff, you'll have our cooperation.” Cristoff walks along the beach as he inspects the footprints near the body and then he raises himself up looking out over the water. He thought to himself that there is a possibility that the body was dumped in the lake. Kneeling down he lifts some of the sand and grasps it within a tight fist. “I swear this Thomas, I'll find the bastards that did this to you.” Cristoff lets the sand drop back down to the beach as he stands up. He brushes his hands off as he walks away quietly as a Police officer comes up to him and hands him a piece of paper. Looking up at the officer he nods and then looks at the piece of paper. “High Spirits Casino, good a place as any to make a start.” Las Vegas, Nevada. McCarran International Airport 2:33 pm, Walking down the ramp Andrew Whitecloud is returning from one of his many wrestling show adventures. As he walks he notices three men in suits and sun glasses observing him. He exits the boarding ramp and stops near the ticket counter as he looks at the woman and nods. “Yes I'd like to reserve the seats for next week. Wednesday I believe it is for Boston, Massachusetts.” He watches her type it in as from the corner of his eye he watches the three men carefully. The lady at the counter hands him two tickets and he nods as he waves the tickets towards her in a salute of sorts. He casually walks towards the windows and then turns to his left walking along the row of chairs. Facing the windows he watches the reflections of the three men. Noticing they are keeping their distances, but close enough to not lose him. Reaching into his pocket he opens his cell phone and makes a call. “Hey Jason do a check for me and text it back to me. Three individuals are following me here at the airport and I would prefer to avoid any violent actions today as I have just returned. Good thanks man.” He begins to walk again this time towards the parking area. The men are still seemingly closely monitoring his actions. Raising his curiosity and suspicions at the same time. After a few minutes of walking Andrew steps out into the pick up area as he waves down a cab. He looks over at the three men as they climb into a Lincoln Town car. He leans forward looking at the cab driver as he speaks. “Take me here to this bar. I think there is some business needed to be attended too.” He leans back as the driver nods. Suddenly his cell phone bleeps stating he has received a message. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ From: +19168675309 Watch those guys, they were seen in the Casino a week ago trying to get you to sell to them. Kind of strong armed the staff a bit. He has some businesses that are struggling. A couple of sellers in each one. Jason ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Closing his cell phone Andrew simply rubs his chin in deep thought. The driver pulls slowly up to a bikers bar on the outskirts of Las Vegas. Paying the man Andrew gets out and walks on into the bar. Few of the bikers nod as he walks past and sits at a far table. Having done this a few times the patrons know the routine. He will get down to business shortly. Slowly the three men enter the bar and suddenly realize they are way out of their element quickly. Looking across the room Andrew motions for them to come and sit at the table. They look around nervously and follow as they walk to the table and sit down. “Welcome gentlemen, May I buy you a beer? If so let me piss in it first.” He motions at a waitress and smiles. “Get me a beer and these three piss water.” Sitting back he looks at the three closely and takes in a deep breath. “So what do you three want? This is Las Vegas and nobody follows you around, unless you are a criminal or they want something.” The bigger of the three leans forward pointing the finger at him. “The Boss sent us three to convince you to sell to us. So we can do this either one of two ways. One you sell nice and peaceful or two you can do it the hard way, Me I prefer the hard way.” Smiling he looks over at the three men. “Let me see here the three stooges are telling me they like to do things the hard way. Truth is I like it that way as well. It is the reason I made you guys follow me here.” Standing up he towers over the three men and grins as he points around. “Gonna send a message back to your boss, He tried to strong arm the wrong guy this time.” Everyone crowds in slowly around the three men as Andrew known in the wrestling business smiles a sadistic smile. Las Vegas 4:30 pm the Bellagio, FBI Agent Cristoff walks below the glitter and glitz of the lights on the strip. His mind simply on back tracking the disappearance and murder of his Agent. He walks around the Casino looking at people as he passes. Some of the security patrols the floors and one decides to stop him. He leans over saying something and Cristoff flashes him his badge and the Security nods and simply walks away. Cristoff simply rubs his bald head trying to figure things out. He knows who is in town and they were the ones that his Agent was undercover with. The Delingrio cartel isn't very big, but they make big enough waves to make people nervous. He slowly walks his way into the tables room and notices in the corner booth is Carlos Delingrio flaunting his large gold rings and his pin striped Armani suit. Smiling he turns and makes his path direct to him. Stepping around him he taps Carlos's hand messing up the roll of the dice as carlos turns quickly. “Hey who the hell do you think you are eh? Do you know who I am?” Suddenly Carlos recognizes the badge quickly enough and steps back. “Ay Agent Cristoff, Almost didn't recognize you in that Kmart suit and the bald glare under the neon lights.” Cristoff raises his hand and motions for Carlos follow him. They both step away from the table as Cristoff leans over and speaks. “Why don't you tell me why you are in Vegas and save me the ehadache Carlos.” Carlos raises his hands and shrugs his shoulders looking around. “It is Vegas Babaaay, Why else would I be here. You could say this is business and pleasure.” “Yeah Vegas Carlos, I will tell ya what Carlos. This is a lot different than Chicago. You may find a snake here even more dangerous than you are.” Cristoff smirks and walks over and takes two chips off the top. “Thanks for dinner Carlos.” Carlos looks at Cristoff and smiles as he reaches over and grabs a thousand dollar chip and flips it at Cristoff. “Here have another thousand and by yourself a real suit.” Cristoff walks away thinking he has just found his rat in Vegas, Now he has to prove it. Las Vegas 9:13 pm , High Spirits Casino, Shaman sits at a desk observing the Casino below through his observation window as two larger guys escort Carlos in by his arms and shove him in. He turns looks at the two men threatening them. “You two made the biggest mistakes of your lives, I own this town. Nobody and I mean nobody puts their hands on Carlos Delingrio.” Standing up he shoves a chair towards Carlos, He simply stares at him. “Sit down Carlos before I break both your legs and make you sit with them behind your back.” He turns and walks around his desk as Carlos sits down uneasily. “Comfortable I fucking hope so Carlos, You see here is the problem I have.” Tapping his fingers on his desk he looks around the room. He rocks back in his chair he brings his hands together. “I busted my ass for years to have all of this. I worked very hard to get to where I am.” Reaching across his desk he flings a vanilla colored envelope over at Carlos. Carlos catches it and looks at Shaman. “Open it Carlos.” Watching Carlos open the envelope and begin to read it carefully. He grows angry and stands up slamming both hands down onto the desk. “You can't do this to me! You know who I am?” Rubbing his temple he looks at Carlos. “Yeah I know who you are Carlos, a two bit thug who beats women and tries to muscle into peoples businesses. I simply bought out every business you have shares in. That is what a few Pizza joints back in Chicago and two minor league baseball teams? You see Carlos I bought majority shares in everything thusly giving me the power to force you out with no footing.” He then stands up grabbing Carlos shoving him backwards causing him to stumble backwards. He sits down hard into the chair. Walking around the corner of his desk he stands with arms across his chest he looks down at everyone below. “You see Carlos I have been going through this bit of bipolar changing lately. You know identity crisis type of thing.” He looks over his shoulder at Carlos. “ You see I like being who I am , but in truth I don't my being the asshole either. Sometimes to make an omelet ya gotta break a few eggs.” He places a hand on the window and gazes down upon the crowd below. “In this business Carlos the snake you think you are is always eaten by some bigger and badder monster. Look at me Carlos I knew once you were here how to find you and end your little power struggle here. Besides I gave the FBI the videos of the Casino here, I'd advise you to play it smart and leave before they find out you screwed up and accidentally killed and FBI Agent by letting the drug deal go down badly. You chose the cowards way out and ran leaving the man to die in the street, That will get you some form of manslaughter.” Turning around he simply stares at Carlos. He looks towards the door as there is a knock on it. “Oops too late, maybe next time you will think your power plays through.” The door opens and in walks Agent Cristoff looking at Shaman and then too Carlos. “Well, well, well, Carlos seems to me that you were tied to all of this. Cooperation will get your ass out of this sling.” Cristoff motions for Carlos to stand as he looks over at Shaman. “Mr. Whitecloud we aren't finished here at all. Your cameras revealed some interesting happenings around your casino that I would like to look into.” Shaking his head he looks at Cristoff. “Fine I will be here tomorrow and we can look into things.” “I haven't found my mans murderer and he was on your security cameras with a lot of your people.” He watches them leave as he turns around and picks up a phone. “Bring me the videos of everything involving Carlos Delingrio and the dead FBI Agent.” Hanging up the phone he sits down looking up at the ceiling. He leans forward and opens the lap top and begins typing. What the hell is going on here since I left? I go do a wrestling show in Ohio, become Hardcore champion and come back with a murder investigation going on in my casino. What the hell is going on here. Seems right when something is going good for me it literally falls apart elsewhere. I don't like this one bit, but rest assured I am going to fight. I don't know how to do anything else. It is like these dreams I have of myself drowning. Struggling to hold my head above water. Then suddenly I sink just below the surface, occasionally I can make it up for air and then I am enveloped all over again. Slamming his fist down in frustration on the desk he simply eyes the computer. His mind heavy with the coming consequences to come in his life. What path will he have to choose and what will he have to do to end this investigation in his Casino. He only hopes that this time it won't be a hard thing to deal with. He reaches over to his Rolodex on his desk. He flips through a few of the phone numbers and picks up the phone. He dials a number carefully and deliberately as he listens for the rings. Someone picks up on the other line as he speaks. “Yeah hey man, been awhile. I need a lawyer and a good one, Think you are up for it this time?” He sits in silence as the voice on the other end speaks to him, simply listening with no response.
