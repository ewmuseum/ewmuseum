With that sound the door to the house on 3390 Old Mill Road slowly creaked open. Click... Click... Click... As her shoes tapped on the hardwood floors she looked around at what she secretly believed was the crown jewel of her wrestling career. Her dream home. Although it'd only been a little over a week since the last time she smelled the tangerine air wick plugged into the wall on the far right side of the room, it'd felt like months. Every time she walked through the door it felt like the first time all over again. It was home. Home sweet home, unfortunately for her... the day job kept her from living in it as often as she'd like. This time, just like every other time would be a short trip. A quick repack before heading back out onto the road, and back into the daily grind that is the professional wrestling business. Heading around the corner, she began her trek to where many young men across the nation would someday hope to make it. Her bedroom. Unfortunately for them, she doesn't even sleep in her own bedroom these days. Pushing the door open slowly she slyly entered the room and took a quick look around to make sure her things weren't tampered with. So far the housekeeper she had hired had done a fantastic job. He often reminded her of Marques, Christian's old housekeeper at the Zenith Manor. Those days were long gone in her mind, but admittedly she did miss little things like Marques. Maybe if he wasn't a self-absorbed, elitist asshole... she could go back and visit one day. As she pulled open her drawer she began to unload several pairs of jeans onto her bed. Pulling a rolling suitcase out from under her bed, she placed it gently next to the jeans and began to pull the zipp... Click... Click... Click... This time, the footsteps were not her own. At first she tried to convince herself it was nothing more than her imagination. Yet reality began to sink in slowly. She knew what she heard. "...Patrick?" She said the only name aloud that she could think of, the name of her housekeeper Patrick Duquesne. Not making a single movement, she waited for an answer... not wanting to miss possibly the only opportunity to stop the paranoia that suddenly overwhelmed her cerebral cortex. There was none. With a sigh she slowly walked towards the door once again, reaching her hand out and gently pulling the door farther open. Easing her head into the hallway she looked in both directions. Nothing was there. The past couple of months certainly hadn't been easy on her and this sort of thing was definitely not helping matters anyway. With Bryan Mayhem lurking around in her life... anything could happen. The Fearless Phenom had almost expected to turn around and find her clothes on fire at this point in time. Luckily they were not. Moving back to the bed Vivica opened a drawer once again and began weeding through dozens of t-shirts, almost looking like a Hot Topic warehouse as she dug through for her favorites. Pulling out an old At The Drive-In shirt she put it up against her chest. Walking towards the full length mirror across the room, she looked at what the shirt looked like on her. Cocking her head sideway she tried to line it up better before moving over to the side. In her peripheral vision she saw something move. Without warning a figure slid in behind her. Before she could react it stepped forward towards her. Vivica J. Valentine dropped her shirt before turning around and screaming out... "Come on!" In front of her she watched as the man once standing tall fell to the ground. Holding his hands up in front of his face he was reacting as if he was being attacked by a bear. The cerulean blue eyes of The Fearless Phenom peered down upon him. "Patrick?" Vivica stated questionably as a look of intensity quickly faded. Her eyes were almost apologetic as Patrick Duquesne moved his hands away from his face slowly. Moving up and down quickly, his heaving chest showed just how startled the former GCW United States Champion had made him. "I'm sorry! I thought..." Vivica tried to explain her actions before Patrick, now sitting against the wall interrupted her. "You called for me did you not?" Pushing himself up to his feet, he brushed off his white polo shirt and khaki pants with a sigh. "I just came to let you know that you have a message from your mother that I think you should hear." Her mother. Quite possibly the woman in this world Vivica J. Valentine hated more than any other... yet she always found herself trying to impress her regardless. Consistent disdain from her parents about her ability to succeed in this world without a law degree had taken them off her Christmas card list years ago. For some reason after moving back to Highland Park she felt like she had to keep in touch with them. Whether it was some standard society had pushed upon her as something she should do or deep down inside maybe she really loved them. Regardless, it was irritating and often times infuriating for The Fearless Phenom. This time would be no different. Pulling a tape recorder out from his pocket, Patrick hit the play button. "Message One. 5:24pm..." "Vivica, this is your mother. I just heard today that you're planning on fighting some lunatic named Bryan Mayhem and I think it's time for this little wrestling thing to stop. Some of the girls at the country club have children who watch GCW and told me some awful things about him... being a Satan worshiper and making animal sacrifices. They were worried about you and so am I. So I talked to your father and we decided that if you give up this wrestling thing, we'll pull some strings and get you into Yale. It might be a little pricey but it's something we're willing to do to make sure you're safe and make it through law school. You know I think it'll be good for you, give you a chance to be a normal college kid and get yourself an education. After you're done you can work at your father's firm and can make a lot of money like your father does. Wouldn't you like people to look at you as an intelligent, successful, and rich woman instead of cat calling at you when you walk by like you're a piece of meat? I know how you are and that you're going to say no but I really think you should consider it. Call back and talk to your father about it and he can tell you what you'll need to do. Please Vivica, just give it a chance. We're very worried about you and we love you." Click. The tape recorder stopped and Vivica just simply shook her head. Still after all these years they don't understand and won't give up. Perhaps it was the Valentine stubbornness in all of them. Bryan Mayhem was nothing but another reason for her parents to convince her to give up on her dreams and conform to their vision of success. The ladies at the country club may have called Bryan Mayhem a lunatic... which he was... one that wore makeup even, but were they much different? Pushing people around telling them what to do like they're born better than them. What made them so great? Who gave them the birthright to push people around and belittle them for being different? It was amazing how much life worked in cycles. Through her entire life she seemed to encounter these situations over and over again... but I guess being bullied is part of growing up for everyone. ---- Chapter Two: Red Rover ---- Ah, the first day of school. New classes brought about new friends and reunions with old ones. It was new beginnings for everyone. It was a chance for the outcasts to become popular, or the most popular kids to fall from grace. The slate was cleared, and while some children took advantage... most stayed the same. A cool summer's breeze danced through the lawn of Ravinia Elementary School, carrying the sound of laughter through the hollows. It was every child's favorite time of the day, Recess. Where everyone could put away the books, tests, and just play and be themselves. It was a time of enjoyment for all, but unfortunately not every child took enjoyment in the same things. Some took enjoyment... in the sadness of others. A young 5th grader walked across the field. His brown hair cut short into a military style look. As he approached a circle of other kids they all stopped to look at him. Some were his friends waiting to hear from him, others looked for their own safety. His name was Cody Jackson, Class Bully. Across from him stood a young blonde girl, her hair flicking off her shoulders. Her cerulean blue eyes were mesmerizing, and would've been more so if they look on her eyes were not that of disdain. "Hey guys, can I play?" Cody said looking around at everyone. There was an awkward silence for a little bit as everyone looked around at each other. Suddenly the eyes of another boy named Jason Jenkins perked up. "Sure!" Jason spoke up. Leave it to Cody's sidekick to jump in with the save. Jason was the type of kid who the others hated more than the bullies of the world. He was a leech, a kiss-ass. He made his way through school by flattering the Cody Jackson's of the world. This incident was no different. Several months had passed, but 5th Grade so far was business as usual for the "dumbnamic duo." ...So 5th graders aren't that creative. The crowd of people reluctantly separated into two groups of six. The young blonde haired girl followed a smaller girl with reddish hair. Freckled covered her as she squinted towards the sun walking to the other side of the field. Jason Jenkins wasn't far behind as Cody Jackson made off in the other direction. "Vivy?" The girl with the reddish hair said out loud as the turned around. Everyone formed a straight line and began to clasp hands together. The two sides looked at each other as the red-headed girl grabbed Vivy's hand and looked over at her again. "Do you think letting Cody play is a good idea?" "I don't know Rachel..." Vivy said looking over at the other side. "You first!" Was shouted from the other side of the field as Rachel and Vivy, looked at each other and then farther down the line as someone shouted. "Cody first!" The voice was that of Jason Jenkins. Go figure. Slowly they begin to swing their arms in unison and sing. Red rover, Red rover Send Cody on over! Without as much as a hesitation Cody exploded away from his line. Sprinting at full speed his eyes narrowed in on the only pair of girls on the field. Vivy and Rachel tightened their grip as she approached, more like a tiny bull than like a child. They could hear footsteps before he lunged almost directly at the young blonde haired lass. Vivy tried to dodge out of the way but her shoulder was nailed as their arms were unbound and ripped away from each other. Vivy laid on the ground holding her shoulder as Cody laughed. "Vivy are you okay?!" Rachel asked kneeling down next to her friend. "We'll take Jason." Cody said with a smile, "With these girls playing... this is going to be easy!" Sitting up Vivy watched as Cody and Jason walked away from the line. Rubbing her shoulder she stood back up. Even as a little girl she wasn't one to cry out in pain. Plus, it didn't really hurt that bad. "I'm fine." Vivy said to Rachel before reaching her feet and reconnecting hands, "Cody is a jerk." Rachel went to answer but before she could the hands on the other side of the field began to swing back and forth. It wasn't long before singing reached their ears. Red rover, Red rover Send Vivica on over! Pulling her arm away from Rachel, Vivy took a couple steps backwards. Her eyes narrow as she took a small hop and ran off as fast as she could. Even as a child many were surprised with how fast she was, always winning all the races during gym class. Her blonde hair flapped behind her in the breeze as her cerulean blue eyes had Cody in her sight. She could see his grip on Jason's hand tighten as she approached. Leaping into the air, a young Vivica extended her arms and hit Jason and Cody square under the chin. Their arms pulled away from each other as the three of the crashed awkwardly onto the ground. Vivy rolled across the ground before pushing herself back up to her feet. Jason was holding his mouth and began to cry. Cody quickly reached his feet and went to go tackle the young Vivica before everyone in the group reached out to stop him. He tried to break free of their grasp but it wasn't long until the whistle blew and teachers were all over the place. "Break it up!" They screamed out as Vivica turned her back to the scene and walked off. The teachers attended to Jason on the ground. "That still doesn't make you a boy!" Jason screamed out as Vivica walked away from him. She ignored him and walked away angrily towards the inside of the school building. Being so angry she didn't know how to control herself. Never did she want to be a little boy, just wanted Cody to stop being such a big jerkface. "At least people like me..." she said to herself as she opened the door of the school building and slammed it shut behind her. ---- Chapter Three: The Consequences Of Vigilance ---- Sitting in the office was a very lonely place. Quietly she waited in a room alone with no windows, only a pencil to fill out her name, age, and story. ...Tick ...Tock ...Tick ...Tock ...Tick ...Tock The sound of the clock counting down the seconds until immediate doom awaited as Vivy looked at the ground. She couldn't get herself to look at anything else... she didn't want to see the principle coming and she hoped if she wasn't looking. It'd never come. Much to the chagrin of little Vivy J. Valentine, the door swung open and in walked Mrs. Banas slowly. Without say a word, the principle glared over at the young lady. She couldn't look up from the ground, Vivy just couldn't stand to see the look on her face. Trouble was something she was used to, but it never seemed to get any easier. With a squawk the chair was pulled out and Principle Banas sat down and got comfortable. "Miss Valentine..." Mrs. Banas says looking over the blonde haired child sitting across from her, "how many times are we going to have to talk about things like these? You can't just punch people whenever they say or do mean things to you." "I didn't punch them Mrs. Banas!" Vivy stated in response, "I clotheslined them." "Clotheslined?" Mrs. Banas asked in confusion. "Like the Ultimate Warrior in WWF!" Vivy said, "Whenever there are bad people who are jerks and push people around, The Ultimate Warrior knocks them down with clotheslines and pins them and they don't mess with him anymore." "Well Vivica wrestling isn't real." Mrs. Banas said, "It's make believe, like a movie. The Warrior isn't really hurting those people, they're acting." "Jason made it look like it hurt pretty bad. Are you sure?" Vivy responded. "That's because you did hurt him, and you need to apologize." Mrs. Banas said, "In real life it hurts, in movies and wrestling it's fake. When you get mad and hit people it's exactly what they want you to do. They want to get you in trouble. Do you like being in trouble Vivica?" "No..." Vivica mumbled. "Excuse me?" Mrs. Banas asked. "No ma'am..." Vivy answered louder. "Good." Mrs. Banas said, "When you get back to class you're going to have to apologize to Jason and Cody for hurt being mean to them and I'm going to call your mother and talk with her about you watching wrestling. Tomorrow you're going to have to stay after school again with a detention so you can have some time to think about what you've done." "I'm sorry Mrs. Banas." Vivica answered. "It's okay... you can go back to class now, but you be a good little girl." ---- Chapter Four: Leave Your Heart After The Beep ---- It was from that day forward where her mother's nagging hatred of professional wrestling started. Just from a little incident about knocking out two little kids for being complete, obnoxious jackasses. I guess when you're a stuck-up bitch who only cares about yourself and what others think of you... it's not hard to be convinced that something is a bad idea. Especially a rich socialite who has to own up to the fact that her daughter is a foul mouthed professional wrestler who makes a living on beating people up. Not quite the prim and proper upbringing the parents Valentine had in mind for their only child. "Miss Valentine? Are you alright?" Suddenly the voice of Patrick snapped Vivica J. Valentine back into reality. Looking around Vivica sees that Patrick must've taken the time to pack her suitcase for her while she was zoned out. "Yeah... I'm fine. Was just thinking." Vivica answered as she reached down and grabbed her suitcase. "Maybe I should get going? I have a plane to catch." "Would you like to call your mother back?" Patrick asked. Vivica hesitated for a moment wondering if she should check in to make sure everything is okay. In the end it'd just be more of the same. Talking about their different sets of dreams and career goals for her life, and ending in an argument that keeps them from talking for another two weeks. So what if her mother didn't approve of her life? It was her life... and she was going to live it as she pleased. "If she calls tell her to order Tsunami, I'll say hi into the camera for her." Vivica said before extending the roller handle on her suitcase and pulling it off the bed. "Take care Patrick." "You too Miss Valentine." Patrick said as Vivica walked out of the room. Tangerine filled her nostrils as she made her way down the hallway. It was home. In this case though, home wasn't where the heart was. Her heart was on the road, next to Lia Ambrosi on her way to getting back Bryan Mayhem for everything he's done to them one and for all. This day has been a long time coming for almost a year, she couldn't wait to step into the ring at Tsunami. Outside of her house she opened the trunk to her black Chrysler Crossfire, gently placing her luggage into it and slamming it shut. Looking up she saw the house on 3390 Old Mill Road one last time with a grin. The crown jewel of her accomplishments... for now. Opening the driver's side door, she sat down and slammed it shut behind her. Fastening her seatbelt and turning the keys into the ignition it was back on the road again. The road to Tsunami. This motherfucker's gonna get my metal The sound of "Lunchbox" by Marilyn Manson brought a grin from ear to ear on Vivica's face and a small chuckle to go along. Backing her car up out of the driveway she looked over her shoulder, singing along as she took off down the street. For nearly two years I've watched many succeed where I have failed. For nearly two years I've seen many new and familiar faces come and go. For nearly two years I've watched the wars Bryan Mayhem has ravaged throughout GCW. I've seen people stand against him... big and small, man and woman, legend and rookie. I've seen Mayhem fight jobbers, mid-carders, main eventers, world champions and hall of famers. I've seen Mayhem from my back on the floor of my own locker room as I lost consciousness. I've seen Mayhem stand above my best friend powerless at his feet. Most of all... I've seen everyone who has ever stepped into the ring with Bryan Mayhem stare back at him with fear in their eyes. This is where history fails to repeat itself. When I look into your eyes, Bryan Mayhem... I don't see an unstoppable force or a ruthless monster. What I see when I look at you is a coward, someone who uses mind games to try and psyche out his opponent before they as much as step foot in the ring. Sure you're big... sure you're strong, by all intents and purposes you could very well be inhuman. Yet I have something deep within my chest that makes me better than you. My heart. The difference between me and you is although I'm small, I'll never give up or fear the likes of you. No matter how many broken bones I have to endure, no matter how high I have to fall... I'll get back up. I'll come back harder and stronger than before. Unrelentless, I will not stop until I beat you... and don't fool yourself I WILL BEAT YOU. No matter how many times it takes. Some people call me stupid. Others call me brash. You can call me fearless. And you can bet your ass I'll have my revenge... I don't take kindly to bullies.
