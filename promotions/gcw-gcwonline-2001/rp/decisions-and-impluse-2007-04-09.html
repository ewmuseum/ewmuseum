Decisions And Impluse

9 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/09/07 The High Spirit, in Nevada. A former friend and now just an figure that looms in his surroundings. It seems has done pretty well for him self. It’s Shaman’s casino and it’s a classy place. High Stakes will come to the air waves live from this very place and on the day of the show how could you not walk around. Tensions are running high and blood will be spilled. Wearing a pair of black adidas shorts and a white GCW sleeveless t-shirt, a pair of black killer loops dawn over his eyes and his dirty blond hair is pulled back in a pony tail. Strolling casually though the arena that is still in the process of being set up as the ring crew raises the lights up into the rafters. Stopping for a second he glances down at the ring which isn’t fully set up yet. He’s still not sure what the gimmick match he’ll be involved in tonight will be but he’s ready for anything. Weather it’s a table match, a ladder match, a first blood match or a falls count anywhere match. Knowing that it could be any match, he doesn’t care much. Draven Lox isn’t someone he knows about or someone he fears. He’s simply another man who is standing in his path to championship gold. Lox is as guy who calls him self "The Innovator Of Violence" like he’s someone to be feared in the ring. That couldn’t be farther from the truth. Slowly lifting the shades off his eyes and up onto his head he stairs down at the ring. The ropes are just now being put up. Tonight in the middle of that ring he has a lot to prove, not to a lot of people but to him self and the rest of the GCW. Coming over from the WWA was a huge step for him and last week didn’t help much when his title match was canceled. He was held back. The WWA was a place that ran on backstage politics and he wasn’t one of the more liked guys, he spoke his mind and he continues to do so. Although he does have one solid memory from the WWA.. That would be Shaman. Not to many people remember that Shaman was the one guy who looked out for him there and was a genuine friend. It was almost as if Shaman wanted to take him under his wing and show him how he could showcase his skills more in the ring. He did that for awhile, all the while up until he left. Slowly walking down the steps toward the ring, he finds him self in the same situation. No friends and no one to prove anything to besides him self. Holding his own and showing he can take out anyone who gets in his way is what seems to earn him respect. He doesn’t need it but he earns it from others. The WWA was a fight he could not win, he grew tired and needed time off, that’s why he came to the GCW. Reaching the barricade between the ring and the seats he takes a seat. Now ringside and looking at the ring again he closes his eyes. Recalling the last real conversation between him and his sister Diana, just hours before her death. She was the one who use to be at all of his matches and was there when he needed someone to talk to. His wife has filled that void but it will never completely go away. Diana told him to always look out for his self first and others second, he’s never quit doing that. Knowing Shaman will be watching from backstage, he knows that tonight he needs to shine. He can picture the flash from the cameras and the cheering of the thousands of fans that will later fill the arena. It’s something he drives on and has become accustom to. Needing to make an impact is what he has set out to do against Lox, it’s something that will get accomplished. Regardless of match choice or type, he will get the job done tonight. Standing up from the chair now, he pushes his shades back down over his eyes and jumps over the barricade. With the ring apron not on yet he looks under the ring quickly. Seeing a Singapore cane, some tables and a sludge hammer he grins sickly. All of these weapons at his side as he disposes of Draven. Learning his major lessons from Shaman he will use those lessons along with the bottled up emotions from years of holding back. It will become a one man show out in the middle of that ring but in the end it will be him standing over the prone body of his fallen and bloody opponent. Something doesn’t seem right now, like he has to clear his head. Never before has he felt like this or wanted to do so much damage to something let alone someone. It’s his mindset, it’s something he’s not use to, it’s changed to much. It hasn’t changed to the point where he shows it at home or it even effects him there, but in the ring it has. He has become the aggressor instead of waiting for his time to strike and make his move he jumps at any hole and strikes. Something he did for years, until he stopped kick boxing. Time will tell what happens with him next or even what his next move is. Wanting to land a title shot in the near future he knows he needs to step it up and he plans to do just that tonight. Making his move and showing he’s an impact player is the only way to get the job done. Nothing will get in his way and he won’t stop until he has to, it’s all that he knows. With his sister watching over him he has to do this, for her, for his family but most of all for him self.. Determination is running though his veins like blood.
