Cry havok!!! (or 'Got any Mountain Dew?')

15 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Aug 15th, 05 The flickering light of oil lamps cast their warm glow across the darkened room. Row after row of hardbound books lined the shelves as the light glinted off their embossed titles. A cracked leather sofa sat in the middle of the room, facing the empty fireplace. Above the fireplace, a pair of swords were crossed behind a coat of arms. The scene could have been out of any number of European castles, were it not for the repeated ringing of that infernal AIM chime and the pale blue glow of a computer screen which was nearly blocked out by the face to the man rapidly pounding away at the keyboard and muttering to himself. “Damned neophytes,” the man at the keyboard grumbled “if it weren’t for the new moderators they’d have been banned by now.” Upon this tidy scene appeared a great shadow, one cast by a great man (at least “great” if we’re talking sheer size). Jim Brandt, better known as “Meat” looked down over the head of his friend at the screen of the computer and grimaced while chewing on handful of Cheetos, jumbling his face up into something that looks like a rather confused camel. “What’s the prob Dave,” said the big man to his diminutive companion, “get caught hacking on Warcraft again?” “Worse,” replied the man at the keyboard (whom is now presumably, “Dave”) “some noob on mugglenet is totally bashing my post about Aberforth Dumbledore being the barman at the Hog’s Head. He’s only got like 12 posts to his name! Who the hell does he think he is!” Meat continued to chew on his mouthful of Cheetos, wiping some of the orange crumbs from his bushy facial hair. “It’s probably just a noob that don’t know any better.” “Not know any better!” exclaimed Dave, “I’ve got over 3400 posts! Someone needs to teach people forum etiquette! I swear, other people’s children?” Meat swallowed his mouthful of Cheetos and took a long tug off the two litter bottle of Mountain Dew he was carrying. His thirst temporarily quenched, he rolled his eyes as if trying to search the inside of his skull for a thought he’d misplaced. One would have to believe that he found it, as a slightly startled look came over him. “Hey Dave, did “ya send out those promo tapes yesterday” I’d prefer that we got some work before the Estrella War starts up. If I’m going to miss the best Heavy-Armor large force battle of the year; I want to be doing something productive.” Dave stopped his rapid fire typing for a moment, pushed his hair out of the way and looked up at Meat. “Yeah. I sent them out,” he said, ? but it’s not like we could have gone to Estrella anyway. We’re banned, remember?” Meat took another tug off his bottle of Dew before replying. “Dude, how could I forget! If you hadn’t talked me into driving you over to Lord Montague’s house, we’d be able to go this year!” Dave stood and turned to face his friend, or more correctly face right into Meat’s chest. (Dave is as short and skinny as Meat is tall and wide. Activate mental theater now. Quite the picture isn’t it.) “Look, he blatantly refused to face me in the lists when I challenged him. It was an insult, and I refused to let to let it stand. If you want to blame someone, blame him for his cowardice.” “I know, but still. I want to crack some skulls.” “I know and we will,” said Dave, “very soon. I just wish he’d have answered my challenge. I’d have figured that sacking his stables and storehouses would have brought him to action.” Meat nodded in agreement. “I’d have been totally pissed if someone trashed my garage.” “Yeah, but it was SO awesome when you put that axe right through the hood of his Honda. I know that one had to have got to him, the cowardly whelp.” said Dave, waxing poetic about past adventures. “Yeah?” said Meat, also lost in reminiscent thought for a moment. Meat went to take another pull on the bottle of Dew and found it empty, much to his chagrin. He looked once again to his friend and flashed him a sheepish grin. “Got any Mountain Dew?” ~fin~
