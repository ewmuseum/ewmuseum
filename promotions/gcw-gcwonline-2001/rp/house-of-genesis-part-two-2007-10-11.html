House of Genesis: Part Two

11 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 11th, 2007 Today is the first day of the rest of your life. Isn’t that what they tell you when they want you to believe you have a new start? Today is the first day of the rest of your life, as if all the other days can be so easily forgotten. But they can’t, and uttering a stupid phrase won’t suddenly change anything. Today is the first day of the rest of your life, and could be the last day of your life if you get hit by a bus. They don’t tell you that in the motivational speech. Don’t mind Scott though, he’s in a horrid mood. Arguing with Tessa typically did that to him, but the fact that he stood outside a Cleveland gym in fifty degree weather, raining fifty degrees at that, did not serve him any better. Don’t misunderstand, Scott loved going to the gym. He relished the time he could spend among the weights, machines, and sweat. He wasn’t happy unless he knew in some way he was bettering himself. But this wasn’t for his betterment, this was for someone else; someone who was late. Heading back inside the gym to escape the cold rain that fell like needles, Scott took shelter in the place he cherished the most, the ring. Practicing his single legs and double legs, Drake figured there was no point in wasting a trip to the gym just because some punk kid felt like skipping out. He wasn’t skipping out though, he was just running late. “Sup” Tilting his head toward the lone door that led to the ring area, Scott spotted a white male entering the room and dropping his bag on the ground. Scott instantly hopped out of the ring and made a straight dash toward the man. “Sup?” Scott mocked, thinking to himself that this man had better not be the kid he was supposed to train. “Sup, yourself. Where the hell have you been?” Lowering his head to the side, the man wondering at Scott, trying to figure out what had crawled into his ass and died. “Man, chill, I’m just a bit late. Ain’t no thing.” As if really wasn’t a thing to worry about, the man dropped himself into the chair against the wall and reached into his bag for a new pair of shoes. Scott shook his head in disbelief. This wasn’t the kind of man he had planned to train. “No” he told him, “don’t bother changing your shoes. I want to see what kind of condition you are even in.” The person looked down at their shoes, all nice and clean, than again at Scott. He had to be thinking the man was joking; theses were after all brand new shoes. Too bad the guy didn’t know that Scott never joked. “Now get up” came a barking order. Rising to his feet, it looked less like obeying a commend and more of replying to a challenge. Going nose to nose with Scott, the man’s eyes showed that he had already gotten enough of Drake’s mouth. “You got a problem?” the man asked, never once backing down. “I think we do have a problem” Both men turned away and looked toward the door, where the lumbering frame of Jerrod Darson entered the room. The man previously known as Genesis was used to a career of causing chaos and mayhem. Yet here he had to step in to play babysitter between two grown men with very bad attitudes. “Now how about we all sit down and calm it down.” “Fuck this shit” shouted Drake, “I don’t need to train his ass, no one is paying me for this.” Not to be outdone, the man quickly fired back with “like I needed you to train me anyways. This is bullshit that some chump thinks he can even train me anyways. I bet I could whoop his ass anyways.” Big mistake. Like NASA, “we have a problem”, big. “We need Mr. Roboto” big. Scott didn’t waste time. He simply spun on his heels, went low, and rose up before the poor guy could even realize he had just been caught in a Double Leg Takedown. Twisting his body, Scott brought him straight down into the concrete ground. The guy tried to lock in a headlock, to his credit, but a swift Crossface broke it and had the man pinned down to the ground with too much ease to be funny. Jerrod had to separate the two to keep any egos or bodies from getting bruised too badly. Scott was easily to pull off, who just let go and stepped back. But the other one still wanted a piece, despite being easily manhandled just a few seconds ago. Darson shoved him back a step and made sure to cut himself between the two. “Calm it down” he ordered, “that is enough.” The two stared at each other, ready to go again, but stayed at bay from making a move. Taking a moment, Darson turned to Scott. “You want someone to train, someone to pass your knowledge to” He’s perfect for it. He’s got the raw talent; you just have to mold it.” Switching to the other person, Darson gave him the same treatment, “and you, Paige, you have all the talent in the world but no idea how to use it. You won’t find a better person to train you than him. I should know, I trained him.” Stepping back, Darson waited to see if Paige and Scott would go at each other again. There was a pause of silence, nothing being said or done. Than the waves dispersed along the shore and the entire thing seemed to be forgotten. Scott scoffed as he climbed back into the ring. “What are you even doing here, Darson?” “Just making sure you don’t screw up” Jerrod joked, “I’ve got vested interest in this training session, don’t forget that.” The comment, a reminder more than anything, caused Scott to roll his eyes. He had figured this entire series of events had some shrouded meaning behind it, a belief confirmed by his admission. Finally getting to change his shoes, Paige climbed into the ring to join his apparent trainer. Scott sized him up, taking a second to look him over. “What’s your name?” “Cleveland Paige” came a swift and confident reply. Scott raised an eyebrow, mouthing the word “Cleveland” loud enough for Paige to hear. “As in the city?” “As in the President” Paige responded; his chin high and eyes forward. A laugh managed to make itself audible despite Scott’s efforts to prevent it. “Cleveland was a fat ass with a bad mustache. I can see why you were named after him.” Turning his head to Darson, Scott just had to continue on, “who the fuck names their kid after the twentieth President anyways” Were all the good ones taken?” “I think he was the twenty-second President” Darson corrected him, “Along with the twenty-forth President.” “Whatever” he fired back, “still a stupid name.” Scott faced Paige now, staring right at him to gauge his reaction, “a fucking stupid name.” He wanted to punch him, to just sock him right in the mouth. It would have felt so good, so right. But Cleveland didn’t. Instead he just cracked a smile and punched back the only way he could. “Still isn’t as stupid of a name as Storm.” Rolling his neck, Scott laughed with Paige. Then promptly took him off his feet and planted him on his ass for a second time with another Double Leg Takedown. Even having experienced the move once before, Paige still found himself caught completely off guard by the attack. Pushing Paige away before he can recover, Drake stood upright untouched. Cleveland scrambled to his feet, both fist up. He was hot and ready to go. It only made things worse to see an arrogant grin etched across Scott’s face. He was enjoying this. “Come on” Drake dared him, signaling for him to come at him. It didn’t take much for Cleveland to oblige, going straight for his trainer. A Dragon Whizzer and Thigh Pick later and Cleveland found himself right back where he started, face down on the canvas cursing. Scott moved away, letting Cleveland up again. Once move Paige moved it for an attack, this time attempting a slower pace. The speed didn’t matter though. Scott slapped on a Russian Armbar, dropped down to the mat, landed a Heel Pick, and yanked the leg right out from under Paige. The entire process took less than two seconds to complete, but Paige could feel each step happening in great succession. It only added to his frustration that he couldn’t find a way to prevent it. This time, rather than face down, Paige found he was on his back looking up into the disappointed snarl of Scott Drake. “Do you know why I was able to take you down so easily?” The question sounded retarded to Paige, and at the moment Paige wasn’t in the mood for retarded questions. “Cause this shit is fucked up” Scott shook his head, stepping out of the ring. “Until you can figure out why I was able to take you down so easily, this practice is over” he told him point blank. The kid sat up in the room, watching as Scott headed out of the room. Not far behind Drake was Darson, who had made his exit right after Scott. “Hey, do you have a minute” his former mentor called out. His cry didn’t get Scott to stop or even slow down though. From over his shoulder he called back, “I’ve got stuff with the family” and never looked back. Cutting out of the gym, Scott is surprised to see a guest actually waiting for him there. Even more surprisingly, or maybe it shouldn’t have been a surprise at all, was who the guest was. Tessa stood out there, battling the cold and the rain, looking as if she had been out there for longer than she wanted. Worry instantly started to set in with Drake. “Tessa, what are you doing here?” his lips trembled with each word, but not from the cold. “Is there something wrong at the house?” Tessa just shook her head calmly and slowly. “No” she replied, “there isn’t.” Her eyes lazily fell on him, studying his movements and reactions, “I just wanted to see you.” Scott had released a deep breath, grateful that everything was fine. “Oh,” all the rain flying from the different directions finally started to faze him, “well do you need a ride back” My car isn’t far from here. We could get out of this mess.” “Is the workout over already?” Tessa asked. She didn’t seem worried about the rain or the cold now, not while she had Scott there with her. Just nodding his head in agreement, Drake kept trying to point her in the direction of his car, wanting to get out of the path of the tiny shards of frozen glass. Tessa didn’t seem willing to budge though, something obviously on her mind. “So you don’t have to go back again today than?” He nearly couldn’t believe it. Again Scott shook his head in agreement, “yeah, we’re done. It wasn’t much. Now look” he told her, “do you mind if we get out of this” I’m dying out here.” Finally snapped from her world, Tessa looked at Scott as if it was for the first time today. “No, I’m fine” came the reply, “I brought my car. I just wanted to see you.” Scott gave her a smile and a kiss on the cheek. Not to be friendly, but rather to help end the conversation. “That’s nice, well, look if you have your car, then I’ll just meet back up with you at the hotel. Is that fine?” This time it was Tessa who nodded in agreement, heading off to her car after another kiss from Scott. As the two lovers split, a pair of dark eyes stayed focused on them, watching and waiting. Jerrod Darson wore a grim expression on his face. Things were only just beginning. Wheels were just starting to turn. All would go full circle. It was only a matter of time.
