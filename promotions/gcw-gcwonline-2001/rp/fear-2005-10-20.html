Fear

20 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20 Oct 05 The air around me was hot and I could feel the dust around me filling my lungs. Slowly my eyes fluttered open yet the darkness all around me did not fill with light like I had expected it to. I tried to let my eyes adjust to what was around me but it was obvious that I was not going to get any light. I took in a deep breath but instead of the oxygen I craved it was dust that came barging in causing me to cough and choke. Where was I? My hands roamed around a bit, I could feel that I was lying on wood. My fingers slowly traveled across the wood until I reached a part where the wood I was laying on met with another wall of wood. I moved upwards until what I feared became a reality. I tried adjusting my legs but my movements were stopped by my surroundings. I was in a rather small box. I did not know how or why I was there nor did I know who had put me there. As a matter of fact none of this really felt all that important to me, not until I found my way out. I tried to muscle the top off but its of no use, the wood makes noises like cracks and pops almost as if it were laughing back at me. I swallowed hard, unsure of what I would do, I tried to stay calm and not let myself panic. I made sure my breathing became shallower as to preserve the oxygen around me. Again I lift my hands, placing them flat on the wooden seal of my small prison. I can feel the veins popping from my arms and head as I use every bit of strength to push this top off but again my efforts are in vain. I can feel the mixture of emotions begin to boil up, any moment I knew they would spill over unless I did something about this. With as much power behind it as I could muster I slam my fist into the wood, again it laughs at me, almost asking for more. Another strike and again I get the same result only this time dirt falls into my eyes. My eyes begin to burn but not just from the dust and dirt but I can feel the tears beginning to form. I wouldn’t be able to hold it in much longer. I feel a tear slowly trickle out from my left eye; it makes my body stiffen as I feel the drop travel down my cheek, to my chin and as it slips down crashing on my shoulder. First my hands begin to shake, slowly at first but as everything comes to me it becomes uncontrollable and violent. The tears stream down my face and the emotions I feel can hardly be described any one way. I was angry, afraid, nervous, excited all wrapped into one package. Life had meant so little to me before but now that it was soon to be taken away, I longed for just one more moment. I have done many bad things in my life, hurting others in every way imaginable and never have I felt bad about it after. I am a monster, this is what I know and I have prided myself on it. I have joked if my room in hell has already been reserved and will it have cable. Its true what’s said, that you don’t think about what you do in life and consequences of them until that life on the line is your own. It can’t end like this can it? I am on top of the world! GCW World Champion, got a freak in bed, millions of dollars in my bank account, the respect and FEAR that I have always dreamed of. Does it really end this abruptly? I can feel the oxygen in my lungs being sucked out and yet not being replaced with more. My breaths become shorter, almost wheezing out. The anger turns strictly into fear and the tears seem to feel like droplets of fire protruding from my eyes. Life? I have never cared for it and I always knew it never cared back. God? I always said I believed in him its just that he hated me. “Fuck Him” were my most common words regarding him yet he is the one that I chose to speak to when all I could see was darkness. It could all be taken away from you in the blink of an eye and no matter how much you cry or beg it will not be given back. Eva VanLorne Michael Stevens Wylie Bick Alex Grey Martin Smalls Typhoid Mary Jason Dread RaZiel All of the people that I have damaged to an unheard of extent in my lifetime. They have finally gotten their vengeance on me, karma is a bitch. I always knew my demons would come back to haunt me but not in such an ironic way. These people never deserved to feel my wrath I simply pushed them too far, and now I must pay the price. I deserved death and perhaps much more. But no one goes down without a fight. I kicked and punched at the coffin lid, tailor made for me. I dug my claws into the top and began scratching at it. Pain rushed up my fingers and into my hands but I brushed the pain aside. Blood dripped from my finger nails and splinters burrowed them a place deep inside my skin. I continued to fight. Blood poured from my knuckles, tears flooded my eyes, and sweat dripped from my forehead. I howled hoping that someone could hear me ? but deep down knowing that no one would. I was alone and it was my own fault. Eventually my movements died down, it was no use to struggle any longer. The trembling of my body had gotten to a controllable manner, my tears had dried up, the sweat remained still, and the blood caked to my hands. There were no sounds, just the slow steady gasps for oxygen from my own lungs to keep me company. My mind went blank as death slowly came for me. Death is nowhere near the glorious thing that I had once thought it would be. Tomorrow no one would care, no one would pay attention. I wasn’t the legend or icon I thought I was. I would simply be a man that was killed. Alone and dark. A man buried alive ? but yet dead on the inside. Everything went dark now, but it was a different kind of darkness. I closed my eyes, for the last time. Thunder. Thunder? THUNDER!” I sat up in bed sweat dripping off my body even soaking the sheets that I was covered with. It took me a moment to let everything come into focus, when I realized I was in the confines of my hotel room a slight smile came to my face. I looked over in my bed and there laid Mary, the one woman that I knew I could trust. The nightmare had been so vivid, I could still taste the dirt, smell the blood and feel the fear. I slid out of bed and walked to the bathroom quickly turning the light on and moving to the sink. The cold water that came from the faucet was soothing. My hands were still trembling slightly as I cupped them under the faucet. I brought the water up and splashed my face with it trying to bring my temperature down. The cool water dripped down my face and rest on my bare chest. My eyes travel up to the mirror and even behind the thick coat of black hair I can see my eyes staring back at me. Yes even men like me have nightmares. Its how I push myself to the next level. That fear that I felt inside of that box is something that normal men wouldn’t even wish on their worst enemies. The problem with that is I am not a normal man. They will feel that same fear, that same anxiety. To be the greatest you must stand up to that which you fear. Devil’s Night. The GCW stares fear in the eye. I smile at my reflection before flipping the light off once more. Darkness.
