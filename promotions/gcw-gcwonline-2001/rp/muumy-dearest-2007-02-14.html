Date: Feb 14th *Credit goes to Kat for the use of one of her other characters in the world of e-fedding. Thank you Kat, hope you liked It had been weeks since Hope Clarkson had been home to Hawaii. Actually it had been since NC-17 since the young blonde had been to the land of paradise. For those that didn’t know her, they would’ve assumed that she had taken the time for a little rest and relaxation. Sadly, that wasn’t the case. Her trip to Germany was successful for the most part. The blonde learned of Leandra’s intentions and how she entered her body. She even learned on that same night that Leandra had entered was the same night; Hope herself had become a vampire. On top of that she made friends amongst the Ambrosi’s, and learned that Lia was a damn good fighter taking on the evil vampire insider her and her forces. Then there was something else. She and Lia had rescued two twin girls from Leandra’s castle in Germany. The girl’s names were Elkie and Gisela and they looked to have gone through their own personal torture at the hands of Leandra. Starvation and beatings were evident in the girl’s bodies. Before these girls were rescued, it was revealed that Leandra knows everything Hope knows and vice versa. Because of this, Hope begged Lia to take the twins and hide them until Leandra was defeated. Then Hope would raise the two as her own children. Yet, with all the things she had learned, there was still more questions left unanswered. But, now wasn’t the time to ask these questions. It was time to go back to her home. Home to her Christopher, where she was always happy. She arrived on the beaches of Oahu, Hawaii just as the sun was setting. She rode in on her faithful companion Ariel, the red dragon wrapped in a black cloak that Lia had given her. This cloak was designed to protect her from the sun. Once on the ground, Hope thanked her "pilot" and Ariel set off to her volcanic cave home. The blonde, known as Blonde Ambition, then takes the moment to look out at the beautiful site of the sun setting. "It’s sad, I’ll never be able to enjoy these kinds of moments," Hope sighed, "To think, a vampire living in sunny Hawaii. Talk about hell on Earth." Within seconds she arrived at Kawanaha Kuno’s mansion. She was amazed she could still move around fairly fast for someone who was well, dead. As she arrived she could see just off to the distance of the mansion was a new building. It appeared to be a smaller two story version of Kawanaha’s mansion. Shrugging her shoulders, Hope proceeded to the front door. As she reached for the handle, the door flew open. Hope jumped as her Mistress was standing there before her. "Welcome home," the olive skinned beauty hissed. Before Hope could respond, she was pulled into the house by her blonde locks. The blonde was then sent flying face first onto the hard marbled floor. "Where in the hell have you been?" Kawanaha yelled slamming the door behind her. "Out," Hoppe spat getting to her feet and walking towards Kawanaha. It was rare when the blonde spoke with such boldness to her Mistress. Ever since Leandra’s presence in Hope, she had become more vocal than she ever had. Yet, Kawanaha wasn’t the kind to be talked to in that manner. In a quick flash the Hawaiian smacked Hope with such a strong force, that the younger woman fell back onto the floor. "I don’t know what more I have to do for you to realize who’s in charge here," Kawanaha snarled, "I’ve given you everything?" "And you’ve taken it all away," Blonde Ambition interrupted, "I’ve heard this all before, time and time again." Kawanaha just ignored her. "Believe it or not, I’ve been worried sick about where you’ve been! The only reason I know you’ve been wrestling is from the books." "Because all you ever care about is money and more money." "Without it, you wouldn’t have all this!" "I don’t have all of this! I’m just a fucking slave to you now!" "Hope, shut the fuck up! You left without me knowing. You haven’t been home in about a month now. Both, Keiko and Christopher have lied about your whereabouts and have been punished for that. For all I have known, that possessed bitch inside of you has taken over and you were out doing god knows what!" "I assure you that weren’t the case. You would’ve have been dead by now had she taken over me." "Then where the hell were you then?" "I just needed some time t get away," Hope lied, "I’ve been acting strange with Leandra living inside me. I’ve been mouthing off to you and Keiko. Hell I’ve even injured Kit, something I’m not really proud of, since it’s costing me money. I thought maybe sometime away would help calm my nerves and to please you with me coming back to normal." "Well, with you smarting off to me just now, I would think that, that didn’t help." "In my defense, Mistress; you did attack me before questioning." "I see. I guess you are right. You have been getting more of a backbone as of late. Just a little advice; just watch who you are mouthing off too. It’s going to wind up getting you killed one of these days." Hope nodded her head. It was then she felt another presence behind her. She could sense that someone was hurt and struggling to breathe. She turned on her heel and gasped at the sight. There in front of her, limping from the shadows was Christopher Clarkson. At least, what was left of him. His right eye was swollen shut, while his left was completely bloodshot. It was as if a blood vessel had burst in his eye. His right arm was in a sling. Tucked underneath that was a crutch he was leaning on to keep him upright. Instead of a shirt, the man was wearing bandages across his rib cage and abdomen. His right leg was covered in a walking cast. As he limped toward Hope, she could see his lips had been split open, and his nose was broken. "Christopher," Hope whispered. Turning to her Mistress, her eyes blazed with a haunting red glow, "What the hell did you to him? I know you said you punished him, but this is extreme, even for you!" "The only from of his punishment was he was locked in his room as a prisoner. But before that, I saved his life." Hope couldn’t help but laugh, "You saved his life. From what’yourself?" Kawanaha just glared at her, "There’was an incident while you were gone. Perhaps you would want to tell her, Christopher." The broken man tried to speak but only grunts came out. "Oh, I forgot about your jaw," Kawanaha sighed, "From all the head damage, and damage to his mouth, his jaw had to be wired shut." "What the fuck happened," Hope yelled. She could feel her blood boiling. This was the only time she would allow Leandra to take control of her. Yet this time, it appeared that the demon was sleeping this one out. "If you insist. While you were away, Christopher decided to play. He raped a young woman. This woman was a personal friend of my sister." Hope felt her heart shatter. She just kept shaking her head. "You’re lying," she said with a trembling voice. "He has changed. He wouldn’t do that!" "You dare call me a liar in my own home? Let’s face the facts, shall we? This woman was a virgin. She’s never had a man between her legs. Does this sound familiar to you in any way?" Indeed it had, as Hope couldn’t hold back her tears anymore. "It does, doesn’t it," Kawanaha continued, "It’s the same scenario the night he raped you. He’s also raped several of my servants as well over the months. He’s also been with Keiko many nights. She however went willingly, but that doesn’t justify the matter at hand here. Your lover is nothing more than a rapist who preys on virgins. I believe he used to say, “The tighter, the better.?" "No," Hope cried, "He loves me!" As she cried, Hope’s cheeks were stained red. This was because she wasn’t crying teats. She was crying blood. Little droplets of pure red liquid was leaking out from her eyes. Christopher grabbed her arm, but the blonde vampire pulled away from him. "So you believe me now," Kawanaha said softly. "I believe the reasons," Hope admitted, "I don’t believe the story." "Very well, then. Just know this. The victim too was badly beaten up, but she did manage to fight her way away from him. Once Kit found out what had happened’well you can see for yourself what my sister does when angry." "Then how in the hell did you save him?" "I pulled Kit off of him. Had I not, you would’ve been coming home to a funeral." The blood tears continued to flow. Deep within Hope’s body, she could hear Leandra laughing about this. "I guess, I should thank you," Hope gulped. "You could. Yet, we both know you wouldn’t mean it. Instead, think of it as this. You owe me one now, Hope. You owe me big for this. I will not tolerate his actions anymore." "How much," Hope sighed. "Excuse me?" "How much will this cost me?" Kawanaha smiled, "Oh, there’s no price this time. Believe me; you’ll know when it’s been paid. Now clean up those damn blood tears. If it stains my floor, you ass is mine!" Hope reached into her cloak and pulled to some tissues and started to clean up her face. Several servants came in rushed Christopher out of the room. Hope was about to stop and protest but Kawanaha stopped her. "He needs his rest and time to heal. He’s not brining me in money as you r manager in this state. Also, I told you he’s being punished not only for his actions as a rapist, but that he lied about your whereabouts." "Fine! I would’ve liked to spend time with my lover, but if you insist!" "As for you, I would suggest you get some sleep. When I come back here, you better be in your room." "It’s the bloody evening, and I’m a damn vampire. I’m wide awake at this time!" Kawanaha just laughed, "But of course. You are just a little vampire now aren’t you? Then just go to your quarters, perhaps a rat will come in for you to suck on." Kawanaha then left the mansion slamming the front door behind her. Hope sighed and walked into her and Christopher’s room in the servant’s wing. Once inside she opened the window in her room. She was saddened her lover was not there, but at the same time she needed to think on the things Kawanaha had said to her. She couldn’t fathom her own lover, returning back to his old ways, no matter how strong the temptation. "Why would he do this to me," she said, "He loves me! I know he does and I know he’s changed." She looked out towards the beach from the window and smiled at the darkness. She closed her eyes to try to hear the water crashing in to relax her some. Out of habit she drew her cloak tighter to her body. She couldn’t really feel the breeze anymore, but she still wanted some sense of her former humanity. It was then a strange musty perfume tickled her nose. In the past she would’ve have sneezed upon smelling it. However, she had this strong urging to find the smell as it was almost beckoning her in some way. Climbing out the window she began to walk towards the beach. Walking a few steps, she would sniff the air to see if she was walking in the right direction. After a few minutes, she arrived to a section of the beach she was familiar with. Hope often came out to this area when she needed time to think, or just to be alone, even as a servant to Kawanaha. She wasn’t far from the mansion though as she could still see it somewhat in the distance. The perfume smell now was so strong it was overpowering her nose. As she looked around, she couldn’t see anything but darkness. "Well at least one of your senses is working right," a familiar voice said. "I didn’t think you would be able to smell my perfume." Hope turned going into a fighter’s stance. There as no one behind her! The voice then laughed from beside her. Still keeping her stance, Hope turned and came face to face with an old enemy to her. "Sekhet," Hope hissed, "What do I owe the displeasure." The woman addressed as Sekhet walked from the shadows of the night into the moonlight. She was an ebony skinned woman with long flowing black hair. Her eyes shined in the color of gold making Hope shiver at the sight of them. Like Hope, she too was dressed in a black cloak that was hiding a black dress. Yet with all this, the sight that was all to familiar with the blonde was a set of fangs, announcing that she was in the presence of the Vampire Goddess. "Now, Hope," she spoke softly, "It’s been sometime since we met. Must you be so crude?" "Considering Keiko and I waged war on you rand your followers, you can see why I act this way?" "Hope, you still astound me with your stupidness. First off, I’m unarmed. Secondly, you only smell me, so you know there’s no one else here. Finally you are about 100 feet from the mansion. Someone would hear you scream." "What the fuck do you want? The 4WW may be closed, but the war is far from over! Min and Keiko would still be tearing the hell out of each other, if they ever met face to face." "Perhaps, I think those two will always hate each other from now until the end of time. However, this is about them, this is about us." "What, you still can’t get the cat piss smell out of your temple?" Sekhet growled remembering that incident. Back in the 4WW days, both Hope and Christopher had invaded her Egyptian temple and infested it with cats. Hope even went as far to say that Egypt was nothing more than a glorified litter box. "By right, I could kill you for that," The Goddess whispered. "Only thing stopping you is mere opportunity." "Is that any way to talk to your mother?" Whatever Hope was going to way was stopped with a sudden, "Huh?" Sekhet smiled a toothy grin as she watched Hope’s face trying to comprehend this. "I think I need to sit down," Hope rambled. "I think you need too," the ebony vampire said leading her to a fallen palm tree. Once sat down, Hope looked at the Egyptian with a questionable look. "Well, I must admit that this is a new form of strategy from you. Um, you’re an Egyptian and I’m all American." "Hope, you’re not thinking this logically. I can explain everything." "What’s there to explain? You’re in no way, my mother. My mother is a woman by the name of Christine Lockhart. She brought me into this world, not you." "She is also the woman who put you up for adoption and once reunited, didn’t have the guts to admit to you that she was your mother. At least I’m doing the decency of doing that. I’m letting you know right off the bat that you are my mother. Granted, I thought you would’ve found out what you were a long time before this." "My mother didn’t tell me because she was trying to protect me." "So you become a slave to a Mistress." "She offered me more than my mother ever did." "But she’s taken it away when you start to stand up for yourself." At this point, Hope as getting annoyed. She wanted to punch this woman and beat her into the sand. Sekhet just happened to sense this and addressed the blonde. "Hope, I’ve kicked your ass before, don’t think I can’t do it again. Now listen to me. Christine is your, earth bound mother." Hope sat there as if she was shot. Sekhet sat waiting to see what Hope may say, but since Hope wouldn’t say anything, she continued. "I’m you’re mother, well in the sense of a vampire. I’m the one that made you what you are now." Now it was Hope’s turn to turn the tables on this former enemy. "I know you are lying now. I was told that a woman by the name of Sexy Amanda was the one who converted me, one year before I ever stepped foot in the 4WW and met you." "Who told you this," Sekhet questioned. "Leandra did." Sekhet laughed, "So, Leandra is in you now?" Hope looked shocked, "You know her?" "Not personally. I’ve heard of her. Then again, it’s not hard to forget the name of a person who tries to wage a civil war among vampires." "A civil war?" "Yes, this woman wanted to raise the vampires of the world and to destroy humanity. She suffered a terrible fate as a human, and once she became a vampire and happy with the lifestyle, she decided to destroy those who harmed her, whether it was vampire or human. She killed the one who made her." "So what does this mean with you claiming to be my mother?" Sekhet sighed, "I guess, you don’t know anything about us do you? In the realms of vampires, when a vampire changes someone, in essence they become the parent to that person. So since I’m the once that changed you, I’m your mother, and you’you are my daughter." "But I already have a mother." "Yes you do. Christine is your Earth mother. I’m your vampire mother. After all, weren’t you the one with the fetish about becoming a vampire? I just gave you what you wanted in life. I gave you the chance to be your fantasy." "But I didn’t mean’yes I did love the idea. But now?" "Now you are one, and you regret it." Hope lowered her head, "Yes, I do. But it’s because of all the things, I’ve done since?" "You weren’t in control of these things. I don’t know if you care or not, but I’ve been watching you and your actions. I must admit, no daughter of mine would pull of what you’ve done. Yet, finding out that Leandra is in you, is a whole new story. After all, you’ve only tapped into your true self once, and recently I can tell. How did it feel?" Hope pondered for a moment, "It felt wonderful, for once I was in charge of what I was doing. For once, Leandra wasn’t in control." "What if I told you, that you could have this all the time?" "Then I would consider the life of a vampire." "There are other things you have to consider as well. All your friends you will outlive as long as you take care of yourself. Even your lover will die way before you will. Keiko, Kawanaha, all of them. Such is the life of a vampire; it’s a rather lonely one. But you’re not alone, as long as I’m around." Then how do I get her out of me?" Sekhet shrugged her shoulders. "I do not know." Hope sat there in silence. She wanted to cry, and the blood tears were forming. Sekhet saw this and brought a finger to her eyes. "Don’t cry my daughter. There’s a way. After all, Leandra has been doing this for years. Transferring her body from soul to soul. I do have a theory about why she chose you. She chose you, because you’re just like her. You have gone through the hell of humanity, even though you both could’ve walked away from it. I liberated you, but I didn’t know there was something else in you dying to get out at the time." "Would you have changed your mind, had you known." "No I wouldn’t have. You see, you did something to me that deserved to be punished. You not only disrespected my temple, but you had to constantly remind me of my so-called connection with my arch-nemesis Patti McClancy. That I couldn’t allow." "So you punished me, by making me this?" "Yes, I did." "How?" "Well, one night after one of the shows in 4WW, I sneaked into your hotel room. After all, you don’t have to be invited into a hotel room since no one actually lives in a hotel. Once there, I seduced you in your sleep and drained the blood from your body. Then I fed you, and well you became a vampire. But it came at a price. One, I knew then there was another presence in you. I decided to do something that vampires rarely do. I erased your memory of the incident. In turn, I decided to use you. I made you my mole into the Knightmare Brigade. I wanted you to take out Patti. I hated her, but if I went after her, it would’ve been to obvious. So I used you to go after her, and it did work, you almost broke her leg." "I remember that match, you were there. I lost because of your inerfernce." "I interfered because my daughter, was being hurt. I’m very protective of you." "Then where were you when I needed you? There’s been times when I really needed you?" "You have me in my brother. Kyo has been doing rather well in helping you." "Helping me? He threw me away! He said I was nothing more than a damsel in distress!" "Well, he called it as he sees it. You do tend to cry, and often." "Look, I don’t know what’s going on and I went to him for help. He decided not to help." "Or maybe he is helping. You have gotten bolder, I’ve seen it. I hear it in your voice. You just need to fully accept it." "But I don’t want to be a vampire!" "You don’t have a choice anymore in that matter! The only choices you have is accept it or die." Hope laughed, "That’s the same advice Keiko got from your niece." Sekhet then slapped Hope hard across the face. "If you want to stay in mommy’s good graces, then don’t mention that bitch around me." Sekhet then grabbed Hope’s hands. "However, she does have a point. There are such things as the Fates that dictate the path we take in life. You’ve been on the wrong path for sometime. Each time you were dealt with a difficulty, you chose to let it roll right over you. You could’ve walked away, but you didn’t. Now look what it’s gotten you. You’re possessed by one of the evilest things this world has ever seen, and you are a slave in your own home. It’s time for you to evaluate your life, and change things." Hope sighed, "There’s many things I regret. One is what I’ve done to you." Sekhet looked shocked, "What do you mean?" Hope shook her head, "I don’t know. But after hearing all of this, and seeing you before me in a caring way’maybe’maybe?" "Accept it, Hope. You don’t want the consequences." Hope nodded, "You’re my mother aren’t you?" "Yes, feel the left side of your neck. That’s where I marked you." Hope did as told, she gasped when she felt the bump. It was like an awaking. "Mother," she whispered, "I’m so sorry. The temple’the war’I was just following Keiko?" "Shhhh," Sekhet whispered, "You’re forgiven. It’s in the past. Everything is in the past. Now listen to me, you need to feed on blood. The simple human nutrients are not enough. For now, I can help you, but you will have to stay fed somehow, I can already see you weakening now." Sekhet holds out her arm. Gesturing Hope, she guided her lips down to her own arm. As if on instinct, Hope bit down on the arm and began to suck. Soon blood was pouring out and around the lips of the blonde. "Alright, that’s enough," Sekhet ordered, "That’s enough for now. There’s many ways to get blood, you’ll just have to look. As for, Kyo, don’t underestimate his advice. He can help you. Get that bitch out of you. Become the daughter, I know you can fully become. In time, I will ask to meet with you again; for there is something I will have to give you." "The power to walk in the day?" "We’ll see my daughter, but you will have to earn it. No more of this damsel in distress shit. You’re my daughter. This means you’re a warrior, you’re destined for better things now. The Hope Clarkson that was born to this Earth is dead. Has been for two years, now. This is the new Hope, the Hope that I was able to give a better life to." "That came with a price." "Yes, and now it’s time to collect. You need to make amendments in your past life. Then you will be back on the right path. That, I can promise you, Hope. Now, get your ass back to the house, and work on our senses. You have the ability to see, hear, and smell above the average human. Besides, Kawanaha will be wondering where you are at again." Hope nodded. She started to walk away, but Sekhet grabbed her. "I forgot to tell you," she smiled, "I’m so proud of you. It had taken some time, but now you are a champion. Keep those titles for as long as you can. After all, it’s still in the books that I’m the South Wind Champion." Hope smiled and hugged her mother. It wasn’t much of a shock that Sekhet actually hugged back. Hope then journeyed back to the mansion with this lingering thought in her head? "At least, my mother is someone I actually know. I guess that war is over’but a new one’has begun." "Leandra," she said aloud, "The time has come for you leave this realm, and by god, I will succeed?.or die trying!"
