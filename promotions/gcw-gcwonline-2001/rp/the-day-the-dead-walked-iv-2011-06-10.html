The Day The Dead Walked IV

10 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/10/11 Inks sits at his desk, ignoring all the commotion going on around him. The entire office is in a frenzy over what they just saw. Wilson is on two different phones screaming at two different people about the same thing. The video the room just saw is still fresh in their brains, the morbid decapitation of an innocent women will never leave their brains. This is something that will stick with the entire room forever. When Lt. Anthony is mowing his lawn next Wednesday he will be thinking about the video and how her head dangled by a single thread. When Cpt Mullberger is washing the dishes after dinner with his family the gruesome image of the man with the painted face will send shivers down his spine. Of all those in the room none will ever be more traumatized than Inks. For the rest of his life this thought will haunt him. The ruckus around the room would be deafening to most people, but Inks, he’s in his own little world. He keeps begging himself to wake up, lying next to her in their bed as their kids jump into bed with them waiting to get attacked by the tickle monster. He’s begging and pleading to wake up from this horrible nightmare, but to no avail. Inks is nudged, but barely moves, the nudge is a little harder this time around. Inks shakes his head rapidly and stands up. The entire room is now looking at him. Wilson eyes the room, and in some sort of silent exchange the rest of the room began to act busy. “Hey, I really don’t know what to say man…” Inks interrupts, “There’s nothing you can say.” Wilson stood there, speechless as his only friend walks away, head down, and most likely a complete mess. Wilson runs his hands through his thinning hairline and turns towards someone in the room. “We get a trace?” That person, who’s actually just a steward names Samanatha shakes her head and mouths ‘I don’t know.’ Wilson shakes his head and slams his hands on the desk and screams every curse word he knows and marches towards the back of the office. Inks is in his office. There’s still pictures of his now deceased wife all over his desk. Even though the divorce was inevitable, one still can’t fall out of love. Her smile, her lips, the way she looked at you after the first kiss all went through his head. He met his soul mate, but this fucking job ruined it all, this fucking job got her killed. Inks erupts into tears, the violent seizure shaking tears where his moan and cries are heard from outside the office. He sits back in his chair, picture of Patty and their kids still in his hands. His knuckles are white around the frame as his body shakes violently with each sob. Where the hell was this lunatic at? Inks knew from the beginning the murders were being done by Bryan O’Neil. He has never once believed in ghosts or the dead coming back to life, and the thought that Bryan O’Neil was murdered at his hands last year has never come to the forefront. Bryan O’Neil is back, and he’s not going to stop until Inks is dead….Who knows if he will ever stop after that…. Wilson just got off the phone with IT guys and the captain to let them know what happened. Within a matter of minutes the desk that Inks was sitting at during the transmission was being raided by the FBI and others. The computer was removed. Wilson saw all this through the cracks of the blinds in his office. After the tower was removed, his twisted the cord and closed the blinds. He paced around his office for a few minutes, his brain racked with guilt, confusion, but most of all fear. Wilson has worked with some of the most twisted and vile criminals on earth, but he’s never met one so cunning and smart like the one he’s dealing with now. Wilson’s pacing become more rapid and has more of purpose almost. He’s fighting back the tears that are looming on his heavy eyelids. He’s known Patty for a long time. She was always a great hostess whenever Wilson went over there for a gathering. Sure the past few years have been rough for her and her husband, but no matter what, every time Wilson saw her she had a huge smile on her face. Their two kids Cole, and Latricia, Wilson can only imagine what the hell is going on with them. Two smart beautiful children, both with good heads on their shoulders ready to take the world by the balls. Now they are motherless, and who knows, maybe fatherless. Wilson felt a tear streak down his face just as his phoned buzzed. He looks at his computer desk, the light flashing and a friendly, yet destroyed voice came through the little intercom. “Sir Captain Sedlak is here to speak with you.” Wilson wipes his eyes, sniffs his nose, “Send him in.” Wilson sits down at his chair, runs his hands through his thinning hair again and readies himself for the captain. The door is open and enters Captain Sedlak. Sedlak is very tall and lanky. He has a deep face, with this bushy eyebrows. The years and stress of the job has worn him thin and gaunt. Wilson stands up and extends his hand. “Wilson, what the fuck just happened?” Wilson sits back down. “Well we got some sort of transmission onto the computer Inks was at. It was his wife stripped and tied up. A giant painted man slit her throat, put his face on the camera and than the transmission ended.” “We got IT on it?” “Yes sir we do. They just removed the computer.” “How’s Inks?” “I don’t know. He was a stone after it all happened.” “The papers know?” “As far as I know they know nothing. Just those in the room that saw it all.” “Any suspects?” “Well sir, this is where it gets strange. Inks says it’s Bryan O’Neil.” Sedlak chuffs, “Impossible. Inks put 20 bullets into the man. Must be a copy cat.” “That’s what I said, but…” “But what Wilson?” “But the man’s tattoos are identical to O’Neils.” “Like I said copycat. The suspect has delusions of being O’Neil so he decorates his body with them. My question is how the hell did he know where Inks was going to be sitting at that particular time?” “Sir, your guess……Wait a tick.” Wilson bolts up from his seat and flies through the door to his office and begins to look around the office for some sort of clue. He paces around, the room of people beginning to get uncomfortable. He looks up, towards the front of the building and notices the three way camera. One pointed towards the door, the other in the room, and the third outside. “Are those cameras through satellites or the internet?” Sedlak exits Wilson’s office. “Yes satellites why?” Wilson screams, “SONOFABITCH!!” Inks is lounged back in his chair, eyes shuts, body still convulsing with each sob, but the crying has come to a slow crawl. He wishes he could just keep his eyes closed right here forever. But that wish is interrupted by a loud ding coming from his left. He opens his eyes and looks towards his left. Blinking on his computer screen is a little cursor in the bottom right corner meaning he’s got a message from someone in the office. He sits up and moves the mouse towards the cursor expecting some jack-off from the office here to say how sorry they are and blah blah blah. He opens it and the name is not one he recognizes. Otto Neilson? Curious, Inks gets into a conversation with this Otto Neilson person as he racks his brain trying to see if he knows who this person is and where they are in the building. Otto Neilson: You there? Inks: Who is this? Otto Neilson: I fucked her before I killed her. Inks sat up and looks around his office and through the cracks in the mini blinds. Who the fuck…. Otto Neilson: Look at all you want you won’t find me. Inks: Who the fuck is this? Inks waits for what seems like an eternity for a reply. He keeps peering through the blinds to see if someone is up to no good. All he see’s is Wilson running around the office like a mad man. Finally the ding comes again. Otto Neilson: I thought I made it pretty clear in the video feed….. Inks says out loud, “O’Neil!!” Inks: You understand you sick bastard when I catch you I will gut you like a pig. Otto Neilson: I made her cum. Just thought you should know that. Inks can feel his blood pressure shy rocket, he clenches his fist. Otto Neilson: And I will do the same to your children Inks stands up and screams at the computer screen. How could he be so stupid. His kids! If he had Patty this sick freak has to have his kids too!! He leans over his computer and types away Inks: Where the fuck are you? Inks paces around again, waiting for a response. The ding comes again. Otto Neilson: Mother is the name of God on the lips and hearts of all children. Inks stares at the screen, confused and in a fit of rage. The Otto Neilson name signed off. Sitting staring at the last line…Inks begins to sweat..Mother is the name of God on the lips and hearts of all children…What the fuck does that mean???
