The World Is Not An Oyster

29 May 2009

Date: May 29, 2009 ---- Darenimo mirenai yume wo mite iranai mono wa subete suteta... ---- Mechanical Rhetoric So here I am, the most pitiful sight you've ever seen. Bravado stripped bare. Trembling body, burning muscles. I punished myself to the point of exhaustion and back again. Reborn from a puddle of sweat, as if I think running ten thousand miles in that smelly freak's shoes is the way to go. It's not, and I know this. I could never become like him. A million lifetimes could pass between us, universes could spring up and fade away, and we'd still be here, total opposites. How fitting, I guess, that we finally get to clash, inside a barbed wire cage... at RAMPAGE. Preparation begins now: I'm hunched over a toilet bowl at the Helmsley Park Lane Hotel, watching the water swirl. It's not the Ritz Carlton, but it's fancy enough. Surrounded by glittering gold fixtures and gleaming white tile. Ol' New York, New York... here we are again- this time in style. I figure it's as good a time as any to treat myself. I washed my hands of the LA scene. I purged the Chicago bullshit from my mind out there, amid the plastic debauchery. I'm ready to come back to this place as a winner. Got a nice view of Central Park, untainted by this vantage point. I almost wish my position within GCW looked this good from above. Unfortunately, I can see that I'm drowning, pulled under by this fucked up ego undertow that made guys like Andy Murray into stars. I remember that time that little prick was in my face, sucking on a can of cola like he didn't have a care in the world. Fucking effortless. God, it makes me sick. Starting to feel like this will be my last hurrah here. Like maybe this wily little shitstain is actually going to get one over on me, in that structure that DEFINED MY GODDAMN CAREER. Binge and purge, choking on the last vestiges of that cocky motherfucker I used to be. Throw that trash out the window and watch it splatter on the street below. Traffic wouldn't stop for a second, because nobody gives a rat's ass about this shit. It's not life or death- at least not to them. Whispering to myself that this is not the end of the world, and it's killing me to swallow that lie. I'll be fine now. I found peace at the bottom of my own reserves, without the benefit of any sort of liquid lubrication. Stone cold sober, sweating the sick fear out my pores. Here, between these walls I can pretend for a little while that I'm the King of the world. For a few hours, I can be ten feet tall and bulletproof. Free as a bird, like the song. Flying above the clouds, and under the bullshit radar to escape this mindfuck that my life has become. Eventually you have to come down off that high and see reality for what it is. You fucked it all up yourself, and ended up here- alone and afraid. Been trying all day to rationalize this shit. Depression's digging in deep. Sitting on this bleached out floor, feeling the cold on my aching body. Fingers are numb, tingling as I touch the tiles, pressing down hard enough to crack my wrists. Nerve damage. Nothing to really worry about. Another toll paid in full to exist in the limelight. I'd pay it again, over and over simply because it felt so damn good to be up there. Feels worse to be denied that moment, only to watch the endless parade of undeserving assholes get that push that I wanted. Burning in my guts, a sickness of avarice that I can't do anything about. Garbage Bag Johnny is going to pay for this. Fucker flings his pathetic little gesture of comraderie at me- tag team Lightning Shark, and he thinks that's going to make it all better? Yeah, I'm five years old, and cake is the best thing to placate me. I grew up, Johnny. I grew up the hard way, with reality slapping me into line every time I fucked up. I'm looking for a sorrow. Something to manufacture, and drown in. Heh, there's a pathetic moment. Trying like hell to understand myself. I'm trying to rest easy, just fucking chill. Sleep was the only thing on my mind. Precious commodity. It's no good. I can't sleep when I'm like this. Sleep and relaxation, shift myself into neutral and just lay there like a slug. And now I was so amped up I felt like I was flying on a nice coke binge. I wanted to punch your lights out for making me feel this damned low. You blink in confusion, and wonder just what the fuck my major malfunction is. I could tell you, but it doesn't get through to you. I hate you. Hatred is nice. It makes sense. It's easy. Fuckin' crackpot weirdo. I can only imagine what you'll be spouting at me, high on yourself, and whatever the cocktail du jour happens to be. Never been an abuser, not like that. I hate you. Feels so goddamn liberating to tell you that. Yeah, why the fuck not. Might as well be honest, and throw it all in the open. I never liked you. I want to hurt you. Hell, last week it made me feel pretty damn good to grind your face into that little peace offering cake of mine. Heh. You're clever, Johnny. Lots of guys would have accepted that at face value. Not you. Nah, you were too good for my cake. For my little gesture of pseudo-friendship. I'm glad. It made the destruction of your face and your careful little mask of composure so much better. Another weakness to exploit. Another face to break. Yeah, it's fun, damned it's so much fun these days. Anger is way better than chocolate, I kid you not. I'll bait the hook for you, ok? Sure. Not that you'll take it, since you never say a fucking word that isn't self-serving, naked hippie bullshit... but maybe you'll surprise me there too. Potshots, and extended middle fingers. Fuck, yeah! We'll have a badass contest. First one to hit a hundred words of intelligent trash talk wins. Come on, it's easy. There's so much shit you can run with. The Internet's full to the brim of lies and suppositions about yours truly. Hop right up on the bandwagon, we saved you a seat! It's getting harder and harder to stay at the top of this heap, especially when everyone wants to take you out before you even crest through the midcard filler. Call me another James von Doom, another Axel Action... another Scott Stevens. Yes, the last is most fitting. I'm a loser who doesn't even realize it. Hit me below the belt. Stab me in the fucking back. Take your knife, and do yourself in, you shallow bitches. See, I'm the real deal. Bradley motherfucking Jackson. I am the winner of this match. I will break that asshole's streak. I should have done it back in October. Failure doesn't sit well with me. Never did. I am the best fucking thing you've ever seen, GCW, despite the fact that you haven't bothered to pay attention to that little fact over the last TWO GODDAMN YEARS. Buy the hype, two for the price of one! Special introductory offer, if you act now. Talk about unwanted pressure. I almost gave Mark Bishop a call before the plane touched down, offering him an interview, an exclusive. Then I remembered that whenever I sound off, be it in print or in person, I tend to come across as this super-arrogant, ultra-elitist prick. Certainly not a good thing when I'm trying to paint myself as a martyr in this whole tired drama, right? Besides, I would never dream of stealing someone else's claim to fame. A solitary beast even to the last. I prefer to waste away in silence. Now where the fuck did I leave my bitch beating stick? Priorities...... ---- "I could kill you," he whispered, breath hot against her ear as his arm encircled her waist from behind. His teeth grazed her pale skin as he swept her hair away from her neck. She felt the tingle all the way to her toes. "Total stranger and you've let me this close… in the middle of Central Park." Words fell between them in the dark. Barely conceived whispers of conspirators. Muffled laughter. Words spoken as if in that moment between asleep and awake. "Whatever you say," she murmured, her breath coming in short little gasps. Her blue eyes were wide, gleaming with excitement and lust. She stood against the railing, in nothing but that gossamer silk dress. His teeth grazed her pale skin as he swept her hair away from her neck. Her arms were outstretched, splayed against the wood as though she wanted to embrace the lights of Central Park that shone through the mist. "That's right," he pressed his lips to the tender spot where her neck met her collarbone, supporting her slight weight with his own bulk. "Whatever I say. That's how I like it." She leaned back against him, feeling his closeness. Opposites. His rock hard muscles flexed against her back, his chin resting on her shoulder. Her eyes closed, her heart racing as she froze, alternately aroused and frightened by his insistent touch. He knew her innermost desires, preying on her thoughts like always. "Tell me you love me," he said, testing the waters between them. Words spoken in the anonymity of that one blissful moment where the certainty of their rekindled romance wasn't frightening. "I love you." She replied, leaning back against him as she rolled her head against his broad shoulder to look into his face. Those eyes, they were so beautiful, so captivating. Deep blue, like the night sky. Eyes you could drown in. She turned in his embrace, placing her hands against his chest, feeling the strong and sure beating of his heart. The eyes reminded her of the past, and all the pain that was tied there. Slowly, she reached up and cupped his cheek. The gesture was so damned familiar that he instantly lashed out- stricken with remembrance. "I don't believe you," his voice was cold, filled with rebuke that she instantly recoiled from. "Don't lie to me. I hate liars." "I won't," she assured him, pleading now, "I do, I love you. Forever." Words passing between lovers now. She met his probing gaze with a small, secretive smile. "I'm standing on a bridge, waiting in the dark…" His eyes blinked closed as he dragged in a deep breath. She stared into those bottomless eyes when they opened again, and he returned that stare, his expression carefully neutral. Rain fell in a solid sheet beyond the gazebo, shielding them from the view of anyone that would be out and about in Central Park in this godforsaken weather. Loneliness was eating at him, winnowing itself into his very soul as he looked deep into her jade-green eyes. "We're social creatures," he said finally, breaking the odd silence with a haunted smile as he took hold of her hands. "We want companionship, we need that on some elemental level. A touch. A glance. To hear our own name on someone else's lips. Some of us want that more than anything." "Do you really believe that, Brad?" She whispered, enthralled as he spoke to her softly, that raspy baritone barely above a throaty rumble. He chuckled and the sound was more like the purring of some massive jungle beast than a man. This was some sort of strange foreplay. His smile was slow, curving his lips and revealing his fang-like incisors. "Don't you?" Once upon a time they'd gone so well together. Now her face blended into a mishmash of fucked up memory. He studied her features, her wild ebony hair and the smattering of freckles on her nose. He clenched his teeth against the emotions that welled, groping inside for the numbness that usually accompanied the anger. "Isn't that why you're here?" "Yes," she readily admitted, stroking his neck. Perfect teeth closed over her bottom lip, gnawing on it for a moment in consternation. "You know what I want? I want to find that one person... the one who completes me..." she broke off, looking up at him from under those dark lashes. He snorted in derision. "Yeah. And which one might that be? Kazen Croix? Ely Braxton? Stanley Schwartz-Rottonbottom… or some jackass I haven't met yet?" "I missed you… not some random passing feeling. You, Brad." She pushed the jacket back over his shoulders, and he let it fall, listening to it slap against the damp wood. Her cold fingers slipped beneath the hem of his t-shirt, lifting the damp cotton away from his skin. "Babe… I would lie to you, and pretend to be everything you ever wanted… but we both know that moment's gone-" he broke off, skin twitching under the mere graze of her fingernails. "I am not the same man," he thought, "and there is no past." He smiled at her, pulling her close as she shivered. She could read it in his eyes. If she asked him to, he'd take her right here, right now. "You know," she teased, "you really are handsome when you smile." He laughed, reaching out to tuck a lock of hair behind her ear. Words and actions between lovers that were as familiar as the looks they read on each other's face. An unearthly scream shattered the stillness, and Kitty flinched, her eyes going wide. "Brad?" She whispered, but his look shut her up. The scream came again, closer, the wail cut off sharply. In that moment, he longed for his trusty P5. Unfortunately, it was back at the hotel, safe in the drawer with the Gideon Bible. "Fuck," he growled, eyes narrowed as he peered into the darkness. Nothing but the solid sheets of rain. He felt his anger rising, and with it came the darkness, bubbling up from the hole he'd confined it to. His eyes slid closed; lest she see the irises darken. He breathed in slowly, feeling the air around them, listening as hard as he could. He didn't hear a thing. "Something's out there," he murmured, as she shuddered in his embrace. "Good for it," she snapped, fear and irritation in her eyes. She watched his face, saw the coldness fall over him. His eyes were dark as they fastened on her, and he gave her a tight lipped smile that hid his teeth. "Don't-" she started, but he pulled away from her. "I have to. Stay here." He pulled her close, resting his chin against the top of her head as he pulled her to his chest. "Don't," she protested, giving herself over to his embrace. Her fingernails grazed against the tattoos on his biceps, feeling the unyielding steel under the flesh. "It's New York… it's probably a mugger… or some rapist." "It doesn't matter," he murmured, stroking a hand through her hair. "I hurt people for fun. Because it gives me a rush." His hands stroked down her back, and she shivered at the touch. Abruptly he pulled away, stepping into the rain. Already soaked to the skin, he disappeared from her sight. ---- Mechanical Rhetoric There are some things I do simply because I am stubborn. I refuse, outright refuse to admit any sort of defeat. I will charge headlong into anything, even if it's a fucking suicide mission. I have to stop and think sometimes- before I open my mouth and fire off a good tirade. These days, people don't appreciate that as much. They don't like being put in their place. They call me a hater. Say I'm just in this business for the wins, to gloat over crushing assholes. I could do that anywhere, you know. I honestly don't give a shit about the wins. I mean, they're nice, don't get me wrong. Nothing's better than having your hand raised in victory while that crowd goes apeshit, but it's not everything. For me, the action is a hell of a lot more rewarding. So maybe I should have thought that through a little better before charging into the dark to find the source of that screaming. ---- He staggered down the hall, watching the lights strobe and then fade to darkness. He was damn close to passing out. The pain kept him moving, flaring in his guts with each step. One foot in front of the other never seemed as hard as it did then. His legs shook, knees coming perilously close to knocking together as he raised his fist, knocking on the door to the suite. Somewhere in the madness, he'd lost the keycard. The door opened, and the carpet inside loomed up to break his fall. She tried in vain to catch him, instead getting a handful of wet leather for her troubles. He lay on the floor, supine at her feet. The bumps of his spine showed through the skin on his bare back. Ribs poked through the flesh on his sides as he gasped for air, his stomach heaving. Kitty stood over him, one hand pressed to her mouth to stifle the screams. His shirt was in tatters, and there were ragged gashes all over his skin. Jackson awoke from a strange and somehow terrible dream to find someone touching him. He jerked away from the point of contact; instinctively reaching beneath his pillow for the gun he kept there. The last vestiges of sleep fled, and his hand immediately dropped to the floor as he pushed back the instinctive response. He wasn't in bed. Reality flooded in, and he could smell the carpet fibers. An ancient miasma of stale cigarettes and shampoo. He awoke in a fever, his blood boiling as though he was cooking from the inside out. He was drained, physically. "Brad," she whispered, her voice breaking, "what happened?" "I don't know." The words tasted like a lie, but he didn't. His thoughts were scattered. He scarcely remembered making it back to the room, and passing out on the floor. Kitty reached out, lifting the edge of his mangled shirt, and then recoiling as though scalded. "You're bleeding," she started, but he cut her off with an anguished groan.
