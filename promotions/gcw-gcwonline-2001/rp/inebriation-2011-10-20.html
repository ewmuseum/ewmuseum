Inebriation

20 October 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/20/11 May 4th, 2011 – 10:30 P.M Charlotte, North Carolina Theo Palmer/Johnny Borealis vs. Desade/Terrance Kingsley Alcohol. As he lay across my chest, the pressure forcefully causes me to deeply exhale, rum and the slightest hint of coke the only thing I can smell on my breath. The lights above rip through my eyes as if I was traveling towards the sun, wide eyed with no intention of blinking. My head spins and my stomach churns, not because he just planted my head into the canvas, but more so due to the amount I have drank since the early A.M. Even so, I can't wait for that next drink... ONE! Painkillers. With the spinning head and churning stomach, I can't help but assume the painkillers are taking their toll as well. After the vicious force in which my head was just slammed into the mat, I feel no ill effects from it. If anything, I am perfectly fine. I feel as if I could lift my shoulder at any point to break up this count, but I am in such a state of bliss, why ruin it with exerted movement? Why not enjoy this feeling of invincibility, until it begins to wear, and I need another... TWO! Sex. Three times today, three different women. All their names, unknown, actually paid for two of them. The other? Essentially the female version of myself. An alcoholic with no self respect, willing to fill that sexual void with anyone. As he lift my leg for the sure count, surely he can smell my days adventures as I haven't showered yet. As much as I love wrestling, my head just isn't into it tonight. Now would be the time to kick out, but all I want is to bust a nut once more, even if I have to pay for it a third time. THREE! The pressure on my chest is relieved as he rolls to my side, the ringing of the bell to signal the end echoing out repeatedly in my head. As I shift my head left to right, my addictions battle each other in my head, fighting to see whose need will be filled first. The names of Desade and Terrence Kingsley being announced to the excited crowd are muffled by this battle. I stagger slowly to my feet, the blood rushing through my body in its thin state. My destination ahead, only fifty feet: the curtain call to these vices. One problem: the likes of Borealis, Kingsley and Desade battle it out on the rampway, blocking my path to ecstasy. Before I know it, my body is gracefully flying over the top rope, floating gently down towards the three warriors. I feel weightless, and it seems as if this ride will last forever. The contact made, my body against theirs doesn't trigger any pain receptors. Instead, that of the reward function of my brain. They are out of my way, and my destination has no blockades anymore. May 4th, 2011 – 10:59 P.M Charlotte, North Carolina Red and Ted's Locker Room "I hope you're fucking happy!" Lawrence screams down at me as if I am a four year old child who has done wrong. Nothing is registering with me at this point. The coke o cola is bypassed as the rum is hitting my lips solo this time, straight from the bottle. I look up from my seated position, as Lawrence glares down with a disappointed/angry look at his little brother. To his left is Grady Patrick, shaking his head in disbelief. He paces back and forth, before finally taking his bowlers cap off, looking at me with a look of remorse almost. "You're done kid. They are suspending you indefinitely. You've lost four straight, and it's clear you're head isn't in it right now. They want you to get a hold of yourself, and get better." I feel great. My head is in it. I have the perfect balance between wrestling, painkillers, alcohol and sex. I have to say, I didn't see this coming, nor did I want it to come to this. I feel like shit. My head isn't in it. My thoughts now battle each other as my addiction have battled one another for supremacy. "Aren't you going to say anything?" Lawrence says. "You won't be paid during this suspension Theo. Your lively hood was just taken away from you. You won't be able to pay your child support anymore, your rent, anything. You went too far this time." I can't look up at him. Not because I don't want to, but I have hit that point were invincibility has turned to complete incoherence. What I want to say won't come out. What I want to express, I can't. I don't have the coordination in my legs to stand and seek a comfort hug from big brother. "I'm out man. I can't be your babysitter anymore." Lawrence turns and walks to the door, before turning back in what would be the last time I've seen him. "You can't help someone, that doesn't want help. I'll pray from you, and I hope something triggers in your head, but I'm done." "Get well kid." Is all Grady says as he follows Lawrence, exiting the locker room. I really have done it this time. I have went to far. I have lost my weekly pay check. I have lost my spot in the Rampage tournament, which surely would have led to even more money and fame. I have seemingly lost everything. Everything except alcohol, painkillers and sex. A change is needed...but that change will have to wait...
