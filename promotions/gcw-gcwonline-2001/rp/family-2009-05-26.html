Family

26 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/26/2009 Night fell; no, it wasn’t your typical kind of night, this night was different. This night on the outside didn’t look any different from other night’s past, but I could tell it was different, I could see through this, this farce, as you would call it, and this night is a lie. Tonight I am going to do things different, tonight I am going to be a different person. As things change around me, I must change as well. Adapt, adjust, be familiar this new life, this new persona, this new me. Why was the night different, you ask? Well you will soon find out. Deep within us, is a different person, this person struggles so hard to get out, that at times you don’t know what is what and who is who anymore. Your struggles and conflicts all come out in the open. You want to fight yourself, but you don’t want to damage yourself in the process. Is this making sense? Do you understand? We all have our angels and demons, but it’s how we see ourselves in each-other. Still don’t understand? In time, you will, everyone will. * * 5 YEARS AGO * * My mind is unraveling. Why would my brother try and do this? All things considered I am the good person, I am the saint, yet I am treated with disrespect because I don’t share in his views. My brother, my little little brother always watching and listening, he knows how to manipulate everyone around him, he can make people do what he wants and when he wants, that’s all he ever was good at. I am the ying to his yang. I am his counter balance, but my times of overlooking him are over. He lost his friend, he lost his brother. “Listen to me.” “No. I will not, I have had enough of you.” “Leonard, you are being unreasonable. I think I have a little more common sense than you.” “Really is that so? So all these times you claim to be looking after to me; you were just looking after yourself?” I don’t get him at all. He wants this money so badly that he will even manipulate our parents to get it. He wants to open a business, or so he says. I can see through his eyes, I have always been able to. There was nothing new about them, the same old brother. The same old cheat, the same old con-man working his next score. One has to realize that he didn’t need the money, but he got it anyway. I need to set him straight. He needs to realize the error he is making and that it will not serve him justly. “That is not what I said; you are only hearing what you want to hear. I am older than you and if you go through this there is no going back. I can’t watch over you all the time, if you want to participate in this little scam of yours, even though father and mother do give you everything you need, but you do you have the decency to thank them? No, you ask for more, and more, and more, and more and this will continue until they have had enough of you.” “Shut up, just shut up, all you do it bitch and bitch and where has it gotten you? No where, you really think that I care what you think of me? You think that I don’t know anything? Who are you to judge, who are you to come around here and tell me my business. Let me let you in on a little secret. I am NOTHING….NOTHING LIKE YOU. I do what I do because I can, and if that is not good enough for you, you can bugger the hell off, brother.” Kleptomaniac? Yes. Sociopath? Probably. Charming? When he wants to be. He can talk his way out of almost any situation, yet when he tries to reason with me, he can’t. I at least have some sort of hold on him. All the reasoning in the world can’t help this poor soul. If only things were different. I would be a better person, I may love my brother, but I could honestly care less about him. I can’t help him, not anymore, I can’t reason with him. I try to reason with my parents, but they wouldn’t listen. They are behind him 100 percent and there is nothing to change that. Do I care he is the favorite? No, not really, it’s not about that, it’s about the life he lives, once he turns that way, there is no going back, not for him. “Father, you need to reason with him.” I decided to have a chat with my father. I thought I could reason with father; make him understand my point of view. “He says it’s a lucrative opportunity, I think I can trust Leonard, Lucas. Why are you so much against him? He has accomplished a lot; you should be more of a thinker like he is.” He doesn’t understand, this is a never ending struggle. “You need to listen to reason. He is scamming you, you think by handing him over a 50,000 pound check; it will help him start up his own business or whatever the bloody hell he plans on using it for? How many times have you had to bail him out of jail? How many times have you had to talk to your judge friends to get him off? What’s it going to take for you to finally realize he is nothing but a con artist?” I slowly get up from across my father. I know it and he knows it, he isn’t going to do anything or say anything. I try to protect them, and I am the one who is made out to be the bad guy. I turn around and stare at the moon light. Just for a moment I was in my own little world, the one where I control the actions, the one where I make the life or death decisions. “You need to listen to me, father; help me help him.” From now on, I need to become my own person. Far too long have I been in this house, carrying this name like it means something. I am tired of all the people saying how I should be like my brother and father, how far have we come. “Oh, Lucas, you should be more supportive of your brother.” I turn around and there is my mom. My dear old mum; she was once like me, a reasonable person, a well rounded individual, but she too, like my father before her, has bitten into what Leonard has thrown down. He feeds them all this bullshit and they just absorb it all. “I mean what have we got to lose? He says he will pay us back and I trust he will do that.” They didn’t understand. He walked away with that money, he did the exact opposite of what he was going to do. Again and again this goes on, he does something bad and they praise him. I do something good, and I get a reprimand, why can’t you be more like your brother? This is enough, this was the last time I had any contact from any of them. It’s a circle that goes on and on that comes back to bite you in the ass, and I wasn’t going to be there to prevent it. I feel like I am betraying my family, over what, some money that isn’t even mine? It’s not about that, it’s about wrong and right, and if they decide to encourage him in his endeavors, then so be it, I can’t protect them forever, and when they soon realize who he really is, they will know I was right, but for right now I am going to make my own way. * * * PRESENT DAY * * * I lay in my bed thinking about events that brought me here. What if I did something else in my life? What if events of the past changed, how would this affect me as a person? Would I be more of a snob? Would I be hanging around the pub with my mates talking about nonessential things? Why here, why now? Why do I need to think about these things? Now I look back and realize the kind of life I have once lived and the one I am living now. Is it so different, do I do myself justice about thinking about the wrongs in my life? “Bloody headache…” maybe another drink will sooth this aching pain I have in my head, or is that what caused the headache in the first place? I have no idea, I don’t care, I really just need something to relax me, calm those nerves. The phone goes off and my head is pounding me. “Shut the hell up…gah!” I pick up phone and slam it back down. Peaceful serenity this has become once more. It rings again. I am going to lose it, whoever is on the phone better hang up now or I am going to strangle them through the phone. The voicemail finally picks up. “(hiccup) Y-you haf reached the manor of Luca(hiccup)s Quinton, leave a mess(hiccup)age after the tone(hiccup) mate! And if this is your calling Barbara jean then you’re not getting those hedge clippers back you sod (hiccup) goodbye!” What the hell happened last night? What could possible make me record that. Think Lucas, think, what is the matter with you? You are better than this. “Uh, hey, uh, Lucas? Yeah, it’s Chris, I know you are probably awake right now, but you need to seriously call me back, brother. I need to talk to you about something important. Just…just call me back, okay? See you around.” The message ends. I groan at that fact, he may know me very well, but not well enough. This is my life in a nutshell, eat, sleep, drink, sleep, wrestle a bit, sleep some more. “Oh what the hell, I have nothing better do to…” I get up off my ass and put on some decent clothes. I wasn’t going to let this hangover stop me. The phone rings again, that sound, that horrible sound bleeding through my head, it’s ringing enough already, I don’t need any more assistance from you, thank you! I pick up the phone like a champ and put on my best, like I always do. “Oi, what you want? I’m about to go out.” “Hey Lucas! Glad to see you’re up!” That was too loud for my comfort . “Eh? Can you tone it down a bit, mate? I got a ridiculous headache; I don’t remember even drinking at all.” This is going to kill me all day. “Listen, what did you call that was so important? I am kind of busy you know, I have to like…do stuff and all that, you know busy adult grownup manly things.” “You do know you have a busy schedule in the next couple of weeks, right?” I haven’t got a clue what the hell he is talking about. “Sure why not.” You did it Lucas, you are an ace in the hole, a card, a royal flush. He didn’t see that one coming. “You forgot didn’t you?” Damn, he is smarter than I took him for. “…Yeah, pretty much, mate. Pray tell what I have that is going on that is more important than what I am doing now.” “You have a very important match coming up. You lost the last week and now you have a very important match you really need to get on your game with.” “Oh, oh yeah, I have GCW all covered, who am I facing anyway? Are they giving me that grail or cup or a title or whatever it is now? Just have ‘em send it through parcel and I will pay post for it.” “No, no listen you have a few top established people in the match with you.” “Top people, like whom? Don’t tell me I am fighting Kimbusa, oh oh I know, it’s that Seymour Almasy guy! Yeah, I know that bloke! Or mayb---….” “No, no listen, you are going up against Mark in the Dark and Nacho Grande.” He made no sense. Who were these people? “Who and who now? Listen I don’t need a lecture from you about what I do and where I go and what happens when I get there. Listen these three blokes are nothing to me, I have no reason to be worried about all three of them…wait three people? What? Anyway, let me be clear, nobody will stop me on my way in getting to the top. I am at the pinnacle of my game, the peak, I am the essence, the nuance, everything and everyone. If they don’t know who I am, then they will soon find out, it’s only a matter time.” That rant took a hell of a lot out of me, a bunch of nonsense I have no time for. I get so worked up that I hung up the phone so hard it almost broke it. The phone rings again, I can’t stand this anymore, this is driving me mad. “What the hell do you want?”All I can hear is the heavy constant breathing on the phone. Whatever it is-is making a lot of noise with its mouth. “Who is this? Talk or I am going to hang up on you.” There is still no response on the other end. “Talk right now, ‘for I come through that phone and beat you senseless, mate.” “Lucas…” Wait a minute, I recognized that voice, it couldn’t be. How did he get his number? What does he want. “What? Hey…Leo? How did you get this number, Leonard? I have absolutely nothing to say to you, so go back to climb whatever hole you climbed in and get bent!” That went over went, hopefully he got the message. I am in no mood for games, not anymore, not this time, I can’t hear another word from this poor sodding bastard. “I need to tell you something, brother. It’s about father.” What? What the hell? First I meet the man who shoots me a few years ago in a bar in some way out of the way town in Connecticut, and now I get a call 5 years later from my estranged brother. Why is everything coming back to me now? What could he possibly say to me that is so important? . .. ... “He’s dead …” These two words, these are the words that I can’t bear to listen to. Why? Why? Why? I keep asking myself. These words put me to a complete halt, the rest of the world slowed down, every man, woman, and child is stopped in their tracks. I can hear a pin drop that it is so quiet. I don’t know what to do. I can’t speak, my mouth is moving, but my vocal chords do not want to commit. “W-what?” It was the only word I knew at the time, the only word I could think of that could sum up all my feelings into one big ball. “He’s dead Lucas, he died last night. Mom wants you back home, I want you back home, please come as soon as possible.” This was the first time that he cared about anyone but himself. Maybe he has really changed; maybe he is the brother I wanted to have. Whatever it took, I was going to be there for them, it was time to put these things behind us. * * * Do you finally get it now? Do you understand? We all have a role to play. Big or small, there is one thing we all have in common. What’s that in common, we are all human; yes, believe it or not, the people you meet day to day are human, when you look at their eyes, they small beady little eyes, I swear you can stare into their soul. You can tell a person’s age, or if you might say, their experiences. The way they look at you, the squints, the blinks, the tears, they all tell a story, a story in which you are help writing. Don’t underestimate anything or anyone because you eyes paint a picture of your life, they can tell the truth or they can lie to you. All these things we bring to the table make our paths in live, everything we do branches out, every person we affect, they affect another person, and those people affect other people, it’s a never ending branch. We make mistakes, we live, eat, sleep, love, hate, and eventually die, these are the paths we choose…
