Mortifera Revealed pt.3

26 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 26th The past, the future, and the present bring you to one common point. That point is who and what you are and what you are about to become. Each of these cannot exist without the other. Asking yourself these simple truths, will you be able to count on the past to provide a path to the future, or do you have to use the present to forge the road to nowhere, yet everywhere at the same time? These simple truths are not so simple and neither is the road to destiny. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- December 26th 2009. Quietly an old man walks across the room and sits in a chair. His eyes narrowed as he gazes upon a book sitting upon a table in front of him. Slowly the man reaches forward and lfits the book. His eyes narrowed at the hard old leather cover. Grinning slightly he opens the book and speaks. "We have spoken before many a time. I have given you some history on Mortifera. Perhaps a little more for the masses would bring about the ticking time bomb." The old man grins as he opens the book to a different section and turns the page. Placing a bookmark in this chapter he traces his finger downwards. He then nods as he gazes forward. "Ahh yes i remember these days fondly." He flips the few pages back and forth as he reads. "Let us cover Shaman first shall we? The dates will be a bit backwards with future first then past last." "Remembering the past is a great way for all to remember the reactions to the present. At this point Shaman had just been in GCW a few mere months. Setting an example for those who stood before him he was fighting to make it known he was not some mere passerby in the night. He was going to establish his foothold." Tapping the page with his index finger he began reading aloud as a look of joy spread upon his face... ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- NC-17 2006 Backstage locker room. Shaman sits quietly in the locker room with the GCW TV Title draped across his lap. His eyes focused upon the metallic surface. He traces his fingers across the letters. His wounds stitched and bandaged from the brutal bout with Kyo Kushinaga and Bryan Mayhem. After a few mere months in GCW he had risen up and snagged a title in a different federation. A new home and new start. His eyes focused as he spoke. "They said i could never do it anywhere else. They stated i had only won titles because they felt guilty for me. Now i have shut them up and now they will learn to never ever again underestimate my achievements." Shaman snapped his gaze up as someone walked up to him. "New to the place and already your making a name. Could have simply stayed quiet and not put your name into the hat." Shaman raises his eyes and notices one of the GCW officials standing in front of him as Shaman responds in kind. "Not my style to simply sit idly by while others build up in front of me. I came in and did what i had to to gain this title." The man reached out and placed his hand on Shaman's shoulder. "Things could be much easier if you simply follow the lead of others." Shaman reaches over and grabs the mans hand and twists as he pulls it off his shoulder. He stands slowly and grabs the TV title with his free hand and looks at the man in the eyes. "Never place your hand upon me ever. Second of all there are no rules to my liking. Finally there is only one man in this place i respect right now and that is myself. Bryan Mayhem and myself are going to have to settle our differences in the ring where i am sure it is going to get beyond bloody." Shaman shoves the man back and points to the locker room doors. "You may want to leave while i am in a forgiving mood." The GCW Official backs out the locker room door slowly looking back as he closest it. "We aren't finished here." The door shuts as Shaman looks up at the door. "We never are are we?" Shaman finishes by pulling his t shirt over his head and stands up. He places the last of his ring gear into the travel bag he had brought with himself. He lays the Tv Championship over the bag and looks down at it. His eyes focusing on it. "Step one is complete in my new venture, but there awaits issues and problems ahead. First i need to make it known i am an unpredictable element. I need to let the rest of the roster know i won't back down from any challenge be it too dangerous or too over the top." Shaman lifts the GCW title and looks at it curiously. Shaman turns his head and looks at the door as someone walks in. "Mr. Shaman i have a message for you!" Shaman waves his hand waiting for the message. "Mr. Caldera wishes to see you in his office." Shaman looks at the man and nods waving him away. Shaman reaches down and grabs the travel bag and slings the TV Title up and over his shoulder. "So now Caldera wishes to see me after signing me. No doubt to try and get me aboard his corporate express." Shaman grabs the door handle and looks around as he smirks. "Gotta start at the bottom and move to the top." Shaman walks down the corridor towards his GCW future as several of the other wrestlers in the corridors look his way and speak amongst themselves. Shaman notices this through the corner of his eye and seethes deep annoyance at this. Feeling they should be human enough to face him face to face about the comments they hold to each other secretly. He grins at a thought. "Eventually they all bleed at my hands." Chuckling he reaches up and knocks on the door and enters. "You wanted to see me Mr. Caldera?" Shaman closes the door behind himself. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The old man turns the pages once again as he hums and sings. " Turn the page, turn the page another mans past is beginning to be revealed." The old man grins as he looks up. He nods down at the book as he speaks. " Both of these events are specific for title reasons. Both of these events show that both these men are capable of gaining championships on their own." The old man thumbs down through the page as he grins and begins to read again...... ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- FUSE LiveWire Championship - Jorge Samuelsson def. Andre Jensen; KillZone 7 (08/08/06) Samuelsson makes his way through the back of the arena heading to his vehicle with the Live Wire championship draped over his shoulder. Just mere weeks before he had received his walking papers from the WWA. Surprisingly on the same he heard mention of them thinking of renegotiating Shaman's contract or releasing him. Shaman was the only guy in the WWA Samuelsson respected and counted as a rival at the same time. He made it a point to beat Shaman at his own game. Being the only man to ever put the big Native American out of the game with an injury for a short time. He turns and walks to the vehicle as Valkyrie his manager steps out and walks over and kisses his cheek. "Congratulations for the win of your first Fuse championship." Samuelsson looks over at her and nods as he gets into the passenger seat. "You drive Valkyrie i need to sit and think a bit." Samuelsson looks down at the LiveWire championship as he looks over at Valkyrie. "Seems kind of a hollow victory doesn't it?" Valkyrie looks over at Samuelsson. "What do you mean?" Tapping the title with his forefinger. "It seemed like a hollow victory against a nameless opponent who will be nothing more than a foot note in history. Probably to never be seen again." Samuelsson looks down and frowns. Valkyrie looks over at samuelsson and grins. "Shaman has been released by the WWA. Well more quit from what we had heard." Samuelsson looks over at Valkyrie. "He has been in contact with a place called GCW and will be meeting with them at the end of the month." Samuelsson nods and looks at the Fuse LiveWire title. "Shaman in GCW could be the new start that we all are looking for. Call his people and set up a meeting with him. I think it is time to bury the hatchet and try to team up with the guy." Valkyrie looks over in shock. "You think he would easily set up a meeting with you without putting a baseball bat through your skull?" Samuelsson thinks on this for a few moments. "Good point better wait a year for that meeting. I do have a Fuse title to defend and people to hurt." Valkyrie looks over at Samuelsson. "You sure this will still be a wise move?" Grinning Samuelsson begins to laugh. "Could you think of a more deadly combo between men other than Shaman and Samuelsson?" Valkyrie looks at Samuelsson concerned. "Jorge are you sure you want to do this? I mean after all he is out to hurt you what in the world could you say that would sway his decision about ever wanting to be in the same room with you?" Samuelsson looks over at Valkyrie. "Something will happen that will sway his decision and when whatever it is that happens will give me the opportunity to forge an unbreakable agreement. He and i both seek greater things and nobody will get in our way till we achieve them." Valkyrie sits back and adjusts herself in the seat as she drives. She ponders what it all means for Samuelsson to want to team with the man who has sworn to take out Samuelsson by any means necessary. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The old man chuckles as he closes the book. "Oh they had their fight and it was bloody." Rubbing his chin he scratches at the stubble as well. "You see Samuelsson did make his way into GCW in late 2007. You remember the shadowed figure in the background at points in time. Backstage watching and observing. In fact he had made the agreement with Shaman already. Hell this agreement carried over with Bryan Mayhem at said time as well." The old man looks up as Shaman and Jorge Samuelsson come forward looking down at the book. The old man speaks. "Well Gentlemen for what do i owe the pleasure of your visit?" Shaman steps forward looking at the old man. "No no the pleasure is all ours. You see you have been doing us justice and i...no we thought that a Christmas gift was in order for you." Samuelsson sets a wooden crate abut 14 by 14 inches on top of a table nearby. He grins as he looks at Samuelsson. Shaman nods over at the table and grins. "It took awhile for us to find it, but i am sure you will enjoy it." Samuelsson walks over and pats the old man on the shoulder. "Thanks Grandfather i could not think of a better narrator for our history and future." The old man chuckles as he stands up and walks over to the box and opens it up. With surprise he lifts out an ancient Viking helm. He holds it aloft as he studies it. "Such a prestigious gift indeed. You two have fun at the expense of the other GCW tag teams." Shaman looks over his shoulder and grins. "We intend too, oh yes we intend too indeed." Shaman and Samuelsson chuckle as they walk away from the area. The old man walks back to the chair holding the viking helm in hand. He slowly sits down and places the helm upon the book. "History indeed has come full circle. We will further explore the ramifications of this union and the decision on why these two were brought together by fate." He lifts the helm once again and admires the ancient piece. He raises his eyes once again and smiles. "Happy Holidays." He chuckles as he looks at the book. He then turns his gaze to the helmet. "You are going to need it."
