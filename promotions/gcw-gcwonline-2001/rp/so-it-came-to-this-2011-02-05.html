So It Came To This.

5 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/11 When I rejoined GCW I did so with my old trainer Larry. For countless hours Larry and Ben worked with me so that I could get back to being at the top of my game. As time passed frustration was setting in as I was met with defeat week after week. My former wife Katrina would soon come back into my life and things would take a drastic turn. Katrina reminded me of the glory days of my career and she told me Larry and Ben were not pushing me to my full potential. It didn't take more than another loss for my sanity to be lost, poor Larry. I met him late one night for a private training session where I voiced my concerns. Only instead of giving me some reassurance he tried to infect me with more lies. To me Larry was a father figure, so it was only fitting to see him put to rest the same way I did dear old dad. When that night peaked, so did my rage and I bashed Larry's skull in. I took him out back into the alley, tossed his beaten old body into a dumpster and set him on fire. A smell I had come to miss dearly. The next morning I would be awaken by Katrina with a phone call investigating the murder. To keep the guys in blue from being suspicious of me, I gave as much information as I could to lead them in the “right direction”. Life however has a way of changing your plans. I agreed to meet Katrina for dinner after talking with the police, but as I arrived at the restaurant she wasn't there. First instincts told me Katrina had fled from my life once again, until I saw her car parked in front of Larry's gym. Going inside it played out a horror movie when I saw Ben's body crucified to the ring, I just knew it was all over for me. I was then shocked to see Katrina come from the shadows in tears and desperation. She explained to me that she knew what I did to Larry and how Ben provoked her into telling him. So she had no choice but to stop Ben from telling anyone, but something more important happened at that moment, she understood the monster inside of me. We knew we couldn't just leave Ben here, so I decided to take him to my little family home in Death Valley. Ben's wife, Robyn, would then get involved in the investigation as she presented Detective Jordan with some new evidence that would let them know I really was the killer. It wouldn't take long before police followed the trail back to my adoptive parent's home, but while they did I allowed Katrina to have her fun with Ben. It was a shame that her play time had to be interrupted, but once they arrived the place had to go up in flames. To bad Detective Jordan survived the fireworks, but at least Ben will never be able to tell anyone what happened. On the ride home I learned how Katrina found me again, all thanks to The Following, so it was a really good bonding experience. When the sun finally started to rest we made a surprise visit at Robyn's home... - “Katie how about you go upstairs and bring me some of your favorite toys.” As little Katie walks upstairs its easy to tell she has no idea that I am the man that killed her daddy. Robyn cowers in fear in a corner as I slowly step towards her. “I must say you do have a lovely home here. From all of the pictures on the wall I can tell Katie had a very affectionate family.” Donny leans into her in the corner and pulls a knife out from his trench coat and presses it into the front of her neck. “Don't kill me please...” “Shhhh, no need for any of that. No one is going to get hurt as long as you do exactly what I tell you to do.” Slowly he trails the knife down her throat cutting into the fabric of her dress and with a grin he slides it back up. “I live life with the theory that no one can be trusted, so I had some friends of mine follow you around. So I know everything you have been doing as of late, but what I don't know is what you told the ever so annoying Detective.” “Just please don't hurt Katie! Please!” “Did I not just tell you no one is going to get hurt as long as you do as I ask? Now tell me what you told Detective Jordan!” Out of frustration Donny presses the knife harder against her neck causing a few drips of blood to flow. Robyn begins to cry and tremble in fear as she looks into the eyes of the emotionless bastard. “I... I showed him some... Pictures of the gloves you used to wear. ...They matched the ones found in the dumpster where Larry was...” “Wow you linked me to the murder with some old pictures of me? If we had others here with us now I demand a standing ovation for that detective work. Okay well I believe its important to make sure that when someone takes a strike at you, you make sure to strike them back harder.” “No...You...You said no one...” Donny grabs Robyn by the throat and throws her down onto the floor. He turns and kicks over a vase before turning back towards Robyn. “GODDAMNIT! How many times must I repeat the same thing to you!?” He walks towards Robyn and helps her up only to place the knife against the small of her back. Holding her in a loose choke hold, the two of them walk towards the bottom of the stairs. Donny makes sure to use his coat to cover the knife for what is coming next. “Here's what we're going to do: You're going to call Katie downstairs and tell her to go get in the car with Katrina. Then we will go to the telephone and you're going to call Detective Jordan and have him come arrest me. Now don't worry about Katie, I would never hurt a child and I promise you will see her again. Everything is going to work out fine, but if you stray from our plan this knife will bring you lots of pain okay?” Robyn slightly nods, making sure the knife doesn't slip. “Katie... Come downstairs.” A small stutter in her voice is heard as she calls for her daughter. From her room on top of the stairs Katie comes with several different dolls embraced in her arms. Sunlight beams in through the windows, reflecting off of her as she walks down the stairs. “Yes mommy?” “Honey, I need you to go in the car outside. One of Daddy's friends is going to take you for a little ride and Mommy will join you later okay?” “Okay!” Katie reaches out to give Robyn a hug as some of her dolls fall to the floor. Donny retracts the knife a bit so not to cause a scene, but quickly extends it back as Katie goes to pick up her toys. He watches on as Katie opens the back door where Katrina is waiting for her. Just as Katrina picks her up to place her inside the car Donny nods to her as she closes the door back. “Let's make the phone call shall we? Remember, you are to tell Detective Jordan I'm here at your home and I'm ready to surrender for the murder of both Ben and Larry. You are not to mention anything about Katie, and if you decide to stray from our little plan... Well this knife shall be given a chance to taste more than a drop of your blood.” Slowly Donny releases her from the choke hold and lets her walk towards the phone. He follows close behind making sure she doesn't do anything foolish. Robyn picks up the phone from the small wooden desk and dials the number Detective Jordan gave her to his cell phone. It rings a few times before someone answers. “Would you damn escort services stop calling already!? I dialed your number one time out of curiosity and now you people won't leave me alone.” “Jordan what? ...No. This is Robyn.” “Ack! Robyn, I'm sorry it's just this one time...” “Jordan shut up and listen! Donny Diamond is in my house and he's confessing to the murder of both Ben and Larry! He told me to call you personally, he is asking for you to come and arrest him!” “Woah! Seriously?!” “Yes Jordan! Get your damn ass here now!” Tears begin to fall more dramatically as Robyn hangs up the phone. Donny watches as she sits back in a corner crying as she hangs up the phone. He places the knife on the wooden desk next to the phone as he pulls out a chair and takes a seat. Leaning over he stares at Robyn as he passes his hands through his hair. “You know Robyn I really should be thanking you for all of this. I mean, I'm a very bad person and have done some horrible things so I do deserve to rot in a cell. Don't you agree?” “Can I have Katie back now please? I've done everything you've asked.” “Why does everything we do have to be about you? I told you, you will see Katie again very soon. Katrina is very good with kids, Katie will be fine I promise. We once had a daughter of our own...” “Please, just please...” “Stop with the damn begging already! None of this would of even happened if Ben would of just left Katrina alone.” Just as Donny looks down at his watch and notices around twenty minutes have passed, he hears sirens in the distance. Standing up from the chair he slides out of his trench coat and extends it towards Robyn. “Well from the sound of it, its about that time. Can you do me a favor and hold my coat for me please? I've come to be very fond of this coat and wouldn't want it to get messed up.” “Fuck you!” Acting a little surprised Donny cracks a grin in Robyn's direction as the front door opens from behind him. “Freeze dirt bag!” “Dirt Bag? Are we in like a western movie or something? Just put on the cuffs and take me away Detective.” “That's exactly what I'm going to do!” Gleaming like he just cashed in a winning lottery ticket Detective Jordan pulls the arms of Donny behind his back and places the cuffs on his wrist. “Before we leave Detective, send some medical help for the nanny upstairs.” Roughing Donny up a bit he turns him around and walks out of the front door. Other officers on the scene are seen running upstairs for Joyce while others tend to the severely traumatized Robyn. Clouds are overcasting as an officer opens a door to the car and Jordan shoves Donny inside. - “So after admitting to be the culprit of such horrendous crimes, why would you give up so easily? You didn't even ask to call a lawyer man! Maybe you want me to hold a press conference to the public to give you some attention? What is it you want!?” Trying to be as intimidating as he possibly can Jordan slams his fists down on the table in front of him. Donny simply shares a chuckle with himself as he surveys the questioning room where he's being held. His eyes focus past the Detective and to the double-sided glass window where he knows Robyn is listening in on the questioning. “Now why would I want anything like that? With all of the work the infamous LAPD has done to find out who committed these crimes, I'm more than certain people know who I am. What people need to understand however, is that I did not do any of this out of hate. In fact it was the complete opposite, I did all of these so the world could share in her love!” “Her? Your wife Katrina put all of this together?” “Hahaha! I love your sense of humor there Detective. No. Katrina is newly converted to her love.” “Give me a name Diamond!” “A name? She has no name. She is not a false biblical prophet. She is real and she is everywhere!” “So this some kind of freak cult movement! I knew it all along!” Becoming annoyed with the line of questioning, Robyn buzzes in from the adjacent room on the speaker system. “Jordan ask him where he's holding my daughter!” “I told you Robyn she's safe with Katrina.” “You're holding Katie hostage? Tell me where she is now!” Donny slowly gets up from his seat and walks towards the mirrored widow and smiles to Robyn and the other officers. He slowly turns back to face Detective Jordan and while doing so he reaches up and grabs his hair pulling it, to expose a wig. “Did you really think Father Tracer would allow something to happen to Brother Diamond? Brother Diamond is the center piece of our masterful plan! I told you she is everywhere and so are we. She has said that in certain hours there must be sacrifice and for those whom are willing, they shall be the ones to reach immortality.” The pale imposter leans back against the window with his arms wide and his body implodes. The glass shatters and smoke fills both rooms as fire alarms are going off throughout the precinct. As the smoke subsides Detective Jordan can be seen making a dash for the door. “Stop it right there Jordan!” Quickly letting go of the doorknob Jordan turns around to see the unknown pale man's body misplaced along the wall in various pieces. Inside the other room one of his fellow officers is holding a gun to Robyn's head. “Myles, what in God's name are you doing?” “God? No. This is much bigger than God. This all for her!” “What are you doing man? Put the gun down!” “Sorry sir, but you no longer call the shots here. I'm going to push this monitor towards you, and you're going to watch it and follow the instructions given.” Officer Myles keeps Robyn at gunpoint while pushing the TV stand closer to the wall with his foot. He starts the video and it starts with Katie sitting in a dark room playing with her dolls. It soon zooms out to show Donny Diamond standing behind her with Katrina at his side. “Did you honestly think it would be so easy? Of course you did. A man of my caliber will never be placed inside such a place. This moment however is not about me, nor about my future. As of this moment the police station is in chaotic form over recent events, which will make it that much easier for the two of you to follow my orders.” Donny walks around for a moment with the camera following him until he takes a seat beside Katie. He puts his arm around her and gives her a little hug as Katrina smiles. “She really is such a magnificent kid. Now if she means anything to the two of you, you will allow Brother Myles to escort you both out of the building to his squad car. Once inside he will drive you both to my resting place and we will see how much little Katie means to you both. Now then before we start, if you have no bond to this child feel free to walk away unharmed, but do anything stupid and the two of you will see more than this video come to an end.” “So who all is ready for a little joyride?” Officer Myles puts his gun back at his side and walks towards the door. Robyn walks behind him as Detective Jordan climbs into the room to join them.
