Deliver me from evil

Date: ?? The monk’s of St. Batholomew in the Sierra Nevada are some of the most strict clergy of monk’s in the world. They rarely speak, they only communicate during the Lighter Silence. During this silence the only words spoken are words for the betterment of the community in which they live. They have no small talk. They only speak what most laborers call “shop talk”. During the Greater Silence not a word is to be spoken. The Greater Silence is from 7pm-7am. These monks believe that Jesus Christ did not speak during these said times, and to be closer to Christ, they believe they must live like him. They take certain vows before becoming a monk. Poverty, silence, and chastity are the three vows they must take. It’s human nature to want riches. It’s human nature to want to communicate. It’s human nature to want to fornicate and procreate. It takes discipline, strict discipline to become a monk of St. Bartholomew. Father Mark Bonner is the head clergymen of St. Bartholomew. He performs all 5 weekday masses, and the two on Saturday and Sunday. He’s been here since 1971. He just celebrated his 65th birthday just last week in complete meditation. Most would think he’s a humble man, someone that looks forward to each and every day. No one understands he’s a haunted man. Haunted from his past. Haunted from a beast he knew years ago. Haunted from his hometown?.Belmont, England. Our story begins about 40 some years ago, during the peak of the Vietnam War. America and England are torn. They both have innocent men dying for this war we didn’t belong in. He approaches this huge, old, decrepit mansion on top of a hill. This mansion overlooks all of Belmont. You can see the ocean from the attic of this house. Fr. Bonner’s skin crawled, he broke out in goose flesh, and a cold sweat started to leak from his brow. This house gave him the creeps, and the voice on the other end of the phone whom asked him to come immediately. You have to understand we are in the time of house calls. This house call was a little different. In the 1960’s most priests were also medically trained, and this call pertained to a man with the last name of O’Neil and his laboring wife. Before he was able to knock a snaggle toothed, balding, crater faced older man came to the door. He was nervous, biting his nails. Fr. Bonner saw something in his eyes, a look of fear. This man, aged beyond his years, was about to become a father for the first time. “Father, she’s upstairs. I think she peed herself. The bed is soaked and she keeps screaming in pain.” Fr. Bonner moves up the front steps and into the house quickly. He will have to put his reservations aside for this moment and do his duty. He comes in and hears this poor woman screaming. He looks at the man, points to the stairs and he simply nods. He almost floats upstairs and enters the room. The man was right, the bed was soaked. Her water broke. It was close, her child was close. “Is she going to be OK?” “Easy sir, this is routine. What happened is her water broke. Meaning the sac in which the child lives in has become to crowded, and the child is ready to come into this world. Don’t worry Mr. O’Neil, your time of joy is near.” “Joy” I don’t want this fucking child!” Fr. Bonner looks at this man. How could he say this? This man should be excited. Maybe this whole pregnancy was an accident. “Well Mr. O’Neil, I can take the child and give it to a family that wants it.” “Fuck that, it’s mine. I’ll deal with it.” The woman screamed, and he heard something. He must forget about this ungrateful man and focus his attention. He can already see the head?. Fr. Bonner looks in the mirror, and starts to clean up his hands. His job here is done. A rather large child is born to a doting mother. He peers out of the bathroom and looks at her. She’s making goofy faces to the child, this brings a grin to Bonner’s face. During the delivery the man left, and has not returned since. What kind of man does not stay for the birth of his child? This begins to creep Bonner out and he must realize that it’s time to leave. He walks back into the room and gives the lady some directions and a number to reach him at. Almost as an instinct he bends over and tickles the chin of the newborn. The child opens its eyes upon contact, and drives Bonner back, crashing into the vanity just feet from the foot of the bed. The woman covers her baby and screams in fear. Fr. Bonner leaves the room and runs down the steps two at a time. He stops at the door, turns around and peers back up the stairs. He must have spooked himself. There is no way that a newborn is born with red eyes, it’s impossible. What happens next is a blur. He remembers opening the door, and feeling a crash against his skull. He remembers lying down on the hardwood floor, his cheek swimming in blood, but that’s it. Most in the community thought it was Anton O? Neil. However he had an alibi. He was down at the pub sipping on some of grandpa’s cough syrup. The regulars at the pub even vouched for him. It couldn’t be the woman, she just went through a painful labor. The crime was never solved, however Fr. Bonner knew who it was. The child, the child with the red eyes. The Devil Child. Every night since than Bonner has woken up with wicked disgusting dreams about this devil child. Most dreams consist of the child being the child. However there is a select few in which the child is an adult. Extremely tall, built like a bear. He wears a black trenchcoat. His arms, legs, and chest are carved with numerous symbols of death and Satan. His face is painted up like the dead. Jet black hair, and a growl for a voice. He does not know why this description exists in his head, but this description is real. Fr. Bonner does not know that the beast that haunts his dreams is real. He does not know that the beast that haunts his dreams is the same child, that he believes only minutes after birth was possessed by the devil, that knocked him out cold. Fr. Bonner rests on his knees in deep rest. He takes his left hand and rubs the back of his skull. The scar from the gash still exists, and can still be felt. He shivers with fear. Once again he will be scared to sleep tonight. After a few minutes, he gives the sign of the cross and opens his eyes. Standing before him, peering down in a snarl is the beast that haunts his dreams. Bonner stands, and looks up at this giant. Bonner begins to tremble, for he knows this is not a dream. Trembling Bonner speaks. “YOU!” The beast peers down, says nothing. Bonner shakes uncontrollably. With lightning quickness the beast’s hand moves and crashes into the chest cavity of Bonner. On contact Bonner lets out a yelp, looks down and see’s the beast’s hand in his chest. Blood erupts from Bonner’s mouth as he begins to choke. The beast pulls his hand from Bonner’s chest and grasps his heart. It’s still beating, blood dripping from his hand, down his forearm to his bicep. Bonner’s eye’s widen. The beast grabs the back of Bonner’s head and shoves his own heart into his mouth. Bonner drops, body convulses and shakes violently. The beast stands over Bonner, staring down, sneering with hate. Bryan Mayhem has ended chapter 1 of his nightmare. He took the life of the man that delivered him to a life of hell. Welcome to Oblivion.
