Lesson's Learned (Breathe)

4 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 4 , 2006 ---- Prelude: The End Of A Dynasty Jealousy is raining down on me right now As the fear of losing you is setting in But I'll continue to do my best Although its scary Wondering if this will be the very first time to lose And not to win And I've got no lack of plan But I'm not a quitter I'm not a quitter ---- Somethings wrong... Her eyes began to open slowly as lights passed back and forth over her face. Chaotic sounds began to take over her senses, the once blurry pictures of hysteria began to come into focus. Below her body she could feel her feet not touching the ground. What was the last place she remembered being? A switch flicked inside the head of The Black Mariah, there was still time. Trying to conquer these fears I've thought were gone... Flailing her arms and legs wildly Vivica J. Valentine struggled to break free of The Gridlock. Suddenly her fist connected with a man wearing a black polo shirt, she watched in confusion as he tumbled down the ramp towards the ring. Shoving away everyone around her, The Black Mariah began to back up the ramp in disbelief as she looked into the ring. Brandon Youngblood stood on the top rope... holding her United States Championship over his head. And it's been so long... The chaotic sounds began to make sense, and disbelief overcame the now former GCW United States Champion. It was the sound of 'Wait' by Earshot... Brandon Youngblood's entrance music. It was almost completely muddled behind the roars of the crowd. Falling down to her knees, The Black Mariah tried to come to terms with what she was watching unfold. It'd been so long, she almost didn't know how to act. Without the title around her waist she felt... naked. I'm dying to live in a world I don't belong... The EMTs tried to pull the recently conscious Vivica J. Valentine up to her feet, but met the same fate as their portly friend in the black polo shirt. Some fans around the ramp gasped as another EMT helplessly fell to the ramp and another ran for their lives behind the curtain. Turning her back to the scene, The Black Mariah made her way towards the backstage area. She just couldn't watch anymore. Her brain was having a hard time coming to terms with her eyes. A light stream of tears began to flow down her eyes, it was nothing noticable of course, not after the hard fought match. They could've easily been confused for beads of sweat. Shoving the black curtain aside, Vivica tried her best to block the sounds of 'Wait' by Earshot out of her mind. There were things more important than the hit her pride just took. What would Shadow think? Losing the championship to that rat bastard Brandon Youngblood of all people. 'Are you okay Miss Valentine?' She heard a voice ring out from beside her as she walked towards her locker room. 'Fuck off.' That was The Black Mariah's only response, her mind was far too cluttered to think of anything more creative to say. Yet, even given the time and clear mind... the response probably would've been the same. Suddenly she found herself the weak link of Dies Irae. Somehow she had to make something of it, some saving grace to keep Dawn McFarlane from rubbing her nose in the dirt. For some reason that girl loved to watch her fail. There had to be something she could do, something that could be of use. As she turned the corner to head towards the locker room, the sound of clapping rang through her ears. The first thing that caught her attention was the GCW World Championship around his waist as she lifted her eyes off the floor, and looked into the eyes of the devil himself. ---- Chapter One: Drifting Apart I'm trying to scream but I can't breathe Can anybody hear me? I'm trying to dream but I can't sleep Can anyone shield me? I shut my eyes and hold my cries to myself My pride's in the shitter But I won't quit, never quit ---- Looking out the window, it was a rather dreary but beautiful night. The moon glowed high in the air, but was often hidden behind the clouds that moved quickly across the twilight. The wind whistled across her second floor window, slightly interrupting the sounds of conversation in the other room. Down below her she watched the trees sway back and forth in the breeze. Everything seemed... normal. The world didn't come to an end, the earth didn't split open and demons didn't overtake the earth. Everything was exactly the same... with or without the United States Championship. Just then, the door squeaked open behind her. She looked up into the glass and saw a man wearing a black hooded jacket walk in the door. Never turning around, she simply grazed away back outside. Slowly Markus Eaux D'Artifice pulled the hood off of his head. While he was certainly growing more accustomed to his new being, he had actually grown quite fond of the new look. It seemed to suit him well. Concerned about the silence of the always chatty Black Mariah, Markus walked up behind her before speaking. 'Is there something interesting outside?' Markus looked over her shoulder, looking down at the ground below. 'Actually, it's something inside.' Vivica said standing up from her seat and walking away from the window. Her body language screamed of an uncharacteristic unconfidence, as she made her way over to the bed. With her head fixated on the ground she sat down on the bed. Markus was the only one who seemed to notice her existance these days, with all the new faces being around. 'It can't be that bad.' Markus said sitting down next to The Black Mariah, 'You wouldn't rather go back to Zenith Manor and be a lapdog would you?' A small giggle escaped the lips of Vivica J. Valentine as she brushed her blonde hair with baby blue tips out of her face. Her brilliant teal blue eyes looked up at the one time business student. Living in the McFarlane Estate was nothing like his days of going to Notre Dame. Then again, that seemed like so long ago now, even though it'd only been a year. It felt like a lifetime. 'Fuck no.' Vivica responded, 'This is different. I mean... everyone seems to fit into place so well. Lia is a great leader, Virgil... he's the right hand man. Dawn owns this place and she's a former World Champion. They all have this whole supernatural thing going on, and I'm just a normal girl. Where do I fit in? What use do I have in this fucking place? Even you belong more than me... you're one of them.' It had been a very long time since The Childe Of Destiny had been told that he belonged to anything. Although he scowled a little bit in disgust of the comment, as if worded to portray him as an enemy, he didnt' speak up about it. He understood, more than most beings, what it's like to feel out of place and like you don't belong. To this day he still looked in the mirror and saw a freak, he's just learned to accept it. 'Then with Blaise and everyone in Knightmare Brigade...' Vivica sighed, 'I guess it's so easy to get lost in the shuffle around all these interesting and supernatural people. I don't know what I have to do to be noticed, or feel like I'm worth something.' 'Maybe that is what makes you different.' Markus suggested. 'Heh.' Vivica gave Markus an unenthusiastic laugh before pushing herself up off the bed. 'I have a World Championship to win, I have to prove myself. An opportunity like this doesn't come everyday. This is starting to sound way too much like a Hallmark Movie anyway.' The Black Mariah turned her back and walked out of the door, leaving Markus in the room alone. Alone to ponder his own problems, staring out the same window. Glaring up at the same twilight. ---- Chapter Two: Drawing Together Confidence is coming back to me right now As the strength to earning you is coming to Cause I've continued to do my best Although it was scary wondering if this would be my very first time to win And not to lose Cause I've had no lack of plan But I'm not a quitter I'm not a quitter ---- SMACK! The punching bag hanging from the ceiling swings back and forth after the hard right punch. SMACK! With another shot the bag heaves backwards before lunging forward again, right into a stiff kick. SMACK! Her teal blue eyes narrow from under the golden blonde locks of her hair. She wore a black sports bra and a pair of dark grey sweat shorts. Black and silver wrestling shoes bounce off the punching bag again, sending it soaring backwards. Beads of sweat begin to trickle down her body. Some would say that she almost looked possessed, at least that was Markus Eaux D'Artifice's observation from the corner. She'd been in the training room for almost four hours. 'Still at it?' A voice cut through the tension in the room, making The Black Mariah stop in her tracks. Turning around Vivica J. Valentine gazed back at the former GCW World Heavyweight Champion, Dawn McFarlane. Slowly walking towards The Black Mariah, it was obvious Dawn had something on her mind as well. 'You know, you're never going to win like that.' Dawn told Vivica as she stopped next to her. Her statement was quickly followed by a menacing glare from The Black Mariah. 'Sure you're faster than he is, and you can probably hit twice as many moves. It's not going to matter, he can break you in half Viv.' 'And you're obviously a master at gameplans that involve defeating LeStatt Knight...' Vivica replied sarcastically. Just like Dawn told her, always eager to open her mouth, never willing to listen. The Steel Rose rolled her eyes before walking up to the punching bag. 'I've been in the ring with him before... and I have a World Title reign.' Dawn retaliated. 'I don't need your help Dawn, I can do it on my own.' Vivica said standing between Dawn McFarlane and the punching bag, before turning her back on her Dies Irae teammate. Dawn's hands balled into fists and turned her back to walk away before stopping. Reluctantly, Dawn looked back towards Vivica, who had begun punching away on the bag hanging from the ceiling. 'Lia is right about you...' Dawn said, 'You don't keep an open mind to anything. You have your little preconcieved notions about everything, and you just act on pure instinct. One of these days you're going to learn that sometimes, that doesn't always work. We've never been the best of friends, but I hope you learn before it's too late. For your own sake.' 'Wait...' Vivica stopped training to stop Dawn McFarlane from walking out the door. They locked eyes for a few moments before Vivica reluctantly continued, 'I suppose I could use a little help... if Lia says so.' With a bit of a smirk the former GCW World Champion walked over to Vivica J. Valentine. The Black Mariah made eye contact with Markus over in the corner, but he sort of gazed towards the window as if to ignore that she was in the room. Some reassurance that was. Vivica looked down at the ground before looking up at The Steel Rose. 'Okay, get into your fighting stance.' Dawn ordered Vivica, quickly The Black Mariah put up her fists and eyed Dawn. Suddenly the right foot of the former World Champ slammed into the left side of Vivica's knee cap. The Fearless Phenom screamed out in pain before falling to one knee and then pushing herself back up. 'Effective... isn't it?' Dawn commented as Vivica reached down and grabbed her knee. 'You know, a couple of those to LeStatt Knight... and he'll be right down to about your size right? From there I would suggest a few boxing style punches... at least some strikes of some sort. Most of your other moves will be pretty useless until he's worn down. He just has such a big size and power advantage, he can counter almost everything else you do.' Vivica J. Valentine begins to move her knee a little bit, still recovering from the surprise shot she got from Dawn. 'Should probably think of a way to finish him off too.' Dawn suggested, 'He's too big for The End Of Heartache.' 'Oh, I've been working on something...' Vivica returned a michievious smirk before asking Dawn a question, 'What do you have planned for Devil's Night anyway?' 'Nothing... really...' Dawn shrugged before she started walking away. 'You know, not everything is going to be like the Rampage Tournament.' Vivica suggested, 'One of these days you're going to have to speak out. Instead of sitting around and waiting for opportunity to come to you, you should go out and take it. Make it yours, you know?' Dawn just gazed back at The Black Mariah. 'Destiny doesn't make itself.' Vivica smiled as she finally took a seat on the ground and took a drink of her water. Dawn McFarlane looked back at her, and they shared a moment in silence as Markus readverted his attention to the pair. Without saying a word, The Steel Rose just turned around and walked out the door slowly. As much as they're different, they both have a lot they could learn from each other. Sometimes... it just takes a little bit of a reality check for them to realize it, after all you learn something new everyday. 'Hey Markus,' Vivica spoke aloud, finally verbally noticing his presence in the room, 'Destiny awaits.' ---- Epilogue: Taking One For The Team Confidence is coming back to me As the fear of losing you is setting in But I'll continue to do my best Although it's scary Wondering if this could be my very first time to win And not to lose ---- 'I'm sorry, I'm not going to be able to make it today... can I reschedule my shoot for some other time?' ... 'I think next week should be great, if you are available to do that.' ... 'Yeah... something important came up. I'm in a bit of a hurry, I'll try to get in contact with you tonight. Talk to you then?' ... 'Bye.' With a click The Black Mariah flipped her cell phone shut before clipping it to the side of her black and red Tripp pants. The sun was setting over the Dallas, Texas sky. She remember the days when her wrestling career started out here in Global Underground, and then Global Wrestling when it was based out of the area. There were some fond and not so fond memories of Dallas, although it was great for things to come full circle like they have, she had more important things on her mind. Her relationships had almost begun to fall apart at the seems, and no matter how much a photo spread for a rather popular men's magazine might have been... making it up to her best friends meant even more. She had spent most of her time in recent weeks consumed with Eva VanLorne, becoming no better than the very thing she ran away from back in Chicago. Vivica wouldn't let herself become that, she was better. A little quality time with the stable couldn't do any bad right? Especially with Devil's Night on the way. Looking up at the hotel towering above her small frame, The Black Mariah took a deep sigh before taking a step forward... almost right into Miss Lia Vlazy. Shadow jumped to the side and Vivica J. Valentine stopped in her tracks as they almost collided with each other. Obviously Lia's mind was elsewhere... and Vivica's was much the same. Shadow took a long stare at Vivica, looking very surprised to see her there. 'What are you doing here?' Shadow asked. 'I've been doing a lot of thinking you know...' Vivica began to speak, 'I think you're absolutely right. I do have a lot to learn about this business and being a good teammate, and what it takes to make a stable successful. That's why you took me under your wing in the first place. As you know Christian isn't exactly the best teacher in the world.' 'You could say that...' Shadow responded. 'So... this is where it starts. This is where my destiny begins.' Vivica said, 'Right here, where my wrestling career started. I know I had a shoot to go to, but I'm not going to miss this meeting for the world. This time next week, I may or may not be GCW World Champion. But I'm going to show you and that asshole everything I got. I'm never going to give up.' Lia looked back at Vivica J. Valentine and didn't see the same person she had her not so pleasant conversation with three days ago. She saw the Vivica J. Valentine that wanted to badly to follow her to Global Championship Wrestling. The spark was in her eye today that was in her eye then. Maybe... just maybe... things were panning out of the best after all.
