2. A Belt to Hold Up My Pants

12 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/12/09 That was easy... And I'm not talking about that stupid ass red Staples button that every cubical junkie feels the need to have and press at every ad lib moment of the day just like when you get done making a serious point and the same asshole you've been talking to the whole time who wasn't even listening says, "That's what she said." No, I'm talking about my entrance into GCW. I was told I had to go up against CJ Woods, an epic badass out to set shit straight in GCW. To be honest, going up against a guy in the Main Event right before he went on sabbatical was a little intimidating for a debut, but once I was in the ring I felt more alive than I've ever felt! I mean, you should have heard the crowd! I went through this and that move, and CJ gave me the sign for the next spot and I just fed off of the crowd and then before you knew it, I was on the top rope and jumping out to land the craziest thing I've ever done, THE EXECUTIONER! I had to play up the fact that I was beaten and tired more than I would have liked, but CJ was definitely a great opponent to stand up against. Man, that guy would be great in the cage. I have to say, I'm really starting to turn around on my feelings of these guys that are wrestlers. I thought that they only knew athleticism for entertainment purposes, but these guys are in great shape. I don't think they even realize the kind of damage that they could do fighting! Now, on a serious note, if any of these guys stepped into a cage they would probably get their asses beat. In the Dangerous Games match, what a fuckin' mess that was, but I hit someone by accident, like hit them hit them, and I knocked him on his ass and he wouldn't get back up. The grown man was fuckin' sulking! I want to first say, I had no idea what the hell the rules were. I got some kind of memo at my house about the match and all, but I'm in the process of getting ready for a match in the WEC and I never read it. Shamrock tried to tell me what the rules were, but he went on talking about the fuckin' Indian and Viking and I never heard that you couldn't touch the ground. Well, I did, but I thought you had to be thrown over the top rope or something! I took out James Mercier with a take down over the top rope, but I was ready to get back in until the ref told me I was eliminated... Well, on the bright side, I didn't have to stay in the match and get hurt... Meal 2 for the day: Six egg whites, One whole egg, a half cup of oatmeal, a banana, and orange juice My agenda book says that I have training with Jeff at noon in Edison for WEC, a run through of my upcoming GCW match at six, and a...oh shit...I FORGOT! I have an online interview with Scott Peppers with Fight Magazine at eight. I better bring my computer to the GCW thing... I got a letter from President Caldera yesterday saying that I am up against Cade Carson for the TV Title this week and wants me to prove myself in practice to show that I can be a champ. I laughed when I got the letter to be honest. I've been in GCW for a whole three shows and I have a shot at their title? That just doesn't seem right but I'll take it and run with it. Carson seems like a stand up guy, but he looks run down and tired. Not all there if you ask me, but we'll see what this match has to offer. Oh, speaking of letters, Dana White sent me a letter after my first win against CJ Woods and congratulated me and all... ...then I got another one with the letter from Caldera... Dana all out laughed through the whole letter, bastard. He wrote "Nice takedown champ..." Real funny White, really funny, but thanks! Well, if I take the TV belt, which I think I might, I won't have to go to Finish Line to get a belt to hold up my shorts... I like to solve problems, and the belt is just the solution....
