The Porcelain Mask

28 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/28/2005 10 a.m. ' Damn it the tie on this top just came unattached!' Eva VanLorne scowled but said nothing else. The backstage area of the Diva Fashion Show was crowded and slightly out of control. She was busy working on a button for one malfunctioning shirt and it just felt like the whole world was caving in on her here. Why did she agree to do this anyway? Oh yes...Mist insisted on it. ' Eva!' ' I HEARD YOU! Toss it here I will work on it in a minute.' Eva had her own line of clothing now, she should have been flattered but who want clothing that falls apart? Looking up she reached out to snag the top out of the air but got nothing as another hands appeared and caught it for her followed by a voice that was both welcome and a bit irritating at this point. ' I just came by to see how things were going Eva. From the looks of things I think I will stay and help out.' Icy blues looked on the darker toned Mist with a mingled relief and frustration. ' Tell me again why I have a clothing line..' ' Image is everything Eva and you are a hot commidity. All the men want you and the women want to be you. Clothing is the way to make them feel that they are.' ' Ah yes. Tell me who scouted for the clothing designer and construction?' ' I did of course.' ' Next time, think you can find someone who makes clothes that dont fall apart.' Mist chuckled pulling up a chair as she grabbed a needle and thread and began to sew the tie back onto the backless rag time top. She knew Eva was over exaggerating only two pieces had malfunctioned out over a hundred. ' Eva, calm down kiddo. The clothing line is well made and like anything else there will be a couple of malfunctions.' ' I dont like malfunctions. ' Well not everything can be perfect.' Eva's eyes snapped to Mist once more evidently having caught some hidden jab in Mist's words that irritated her further. Mist didn't seem to notice as she bit off the end of the thread and tossed the top back to the half naked model waiting. Stabbing the needle back into the small tomato looking pin holder she stood up and began taking a mental inventory of everyone backstage. Frowning she turned back to Eva. ' I thought I said we only needed fifteen models for this. Why do I see twenty five?' ' Mr. Caldera wanted me to get the fans involved so a scoured the crowds and found some women who fit the bill. Call it their big break.' ' Ah, Good ole Caldera. Smart business man. Good call, it will be good for your public relations.' ' If you say so.' Eva stood up and tossed the repaired shirt to another model standing by waiting.' You know if you are going to be backhere I am going to need one of two things from you. Either model the clothing or coordinate the models for me and be the announcer for the show.' Mist wasn't phased by those words. Turning to Eva she looked her up and down and allowed a grin to toy at the edge of those succulent lips. ' I will take option two. You, however, will take option one.' Eva froze. ' Excuse me?' ' Come on Eva, if you want the line to sell, you need to be seen wearing it, which by the is part of your contract with the new image company that Jordan hired.' Sublime lips tightened into a grim expression as Eva stared a hole through Mist. A silent battle of wills and logic took place only for a minute or so before Eva finally gave in and threw her hands up. ' Fine fine, you win.' Mist nodded and immediately swung into action taking control of thing in the back. Moving to the clothing racks she shifted through the ensembles and pulled out a black leather criss cross tapered tail skirt and the matching backless top to go with it. Immediately she was tossing it to Eva. ' Start with that.' Mist turned away and began barking orders to other models. Switching outfits here, changing accessories there working everything into a semblance of order that honestly had Eva admiring the spunk the woman had. Useful was the word that kept coming to mind. Maybe this fashion show would go over better than she thought afterall... ================================================================================ 3 p. m. ' Who do I make this out too?' Eva face hurt from smiling so much while signing autograph after autograph. Whoever said being a star was easy obviously wasn't a star and certainly did not work for GCW. ' Emily.' The young teenage girl exclaimed almost shyly. Eva nodded offering the young woman a wink as she put the sharpy to her rather provocative picture and signed away for...what was the count again? Bah, it didnt matter she had lost count by now.' I am your biggest fan Eva.' If only she had a dime for everytime she had heard that tonight she could have an expensive meal at the cushiest restaurant in Los Angeles..not that she couldn't anyway. ' Aww Emily thanks sweetie. Going to be at the show tomorrow?' ' Yes ma'am I bought my ticket online as soon as they were open for sale. Kind of expensive but I wanted a front row seat at the ring side.' Eva smiled as she passed the picture her way. ' Be careful in the front row Emily. Things tend to get a bit rough there.' ' I know, but I figure if you can handle it so can I. You are tough Eva and beautiful. I only wish I could be like you.' Something about the hope in the girl's voice brought a pang of pride to that wounded heart. The procelain mask that was her smile threatened to break free as she nodded and leaned forward. ' The trick is self confidence Emily. Start with building that, work out everyday, show courage and you will have a good start. Good luck hun.' Emily smiled gratefully as she turned away allowing the next fan to come forward for an autograph. Eva glanced at the line again and inwardly she groaned. She would be here forever it felt like and she was getting thirsty as hell. As if her thoughts had been read a familiar hand extended in front of her face holding a bottle of chilled Daimani Lemon Water. Mist Demeanor was there again. This time she felt nothing but gratitude as she took the offer liquid ambrosia. ' Thought you might be getting parched by now. How's the hand holding up?' Eva grinned. ' You are turning into an angel, thanks Mist. It isn't cramping yet, but my guess is it will soon from the likes of this line.' ' I could massage it for you Ms. VanLorne.' Eva's eyes turned to the guy who was probably not more than a few years younger than her. She smiled, though inwardly she wanted to punch him. Mentally she blanched at her thoughts. Jesus, since when did she want to hurt a guy for offering help? Politely she responded. ' Thanks doll, but I think it will survive. Who do I make this out too?' So the cycle continued, a fan would come up, Eva would sign an autograph, make small talk and wait on the next one. All the while Mist stood off to the side watching her progress with a keen interest. Another autograph ..another porcelain smile... =============================================================================== Midnight She had been exhausted by the time that body hit the bed of the five star hotel she had been booked into thanks to Mist. It seemed that woman knew everything that needed to be done. Most of Anarchy Agencie's signed wrestlers were staying here. Groaning Eva rolled to her back arms extended over her head as her feet dangled to the floor. She was staring at the ceiling. She didn't even feel this tired after a tough match. Heaving a hefty sigh she pulled to a sitting position and stood up walking off into the bathroom to start a nice hot bath hoping to work away the soreness before tomorrow's show. Soon she was in the depths of that steamy water sighing with pleasure and relief as she sank further in and allowed her head to rest against the little blow up pillow the hotel provided thoughtfully. Slowly those eyes drifted shut as the heat lulled her into trying to nap only to snap open in a panic as her body slipped. Scooting up she grimmaced. ' Okay drowning is not an option.' Fingers damp from the heated liquid reached out and plucked up the novel she had been slowly working on since her stay in the hospital. Pulling it open to where she left off she began to read again. By the time she closed the novel the water had cooled considerably, but so had her aching muscles. Shivering the blond diva climbed from the chilled depths and wrapped a thick terrycloth robe about her form to warm up as she headed for the bed and read some more. 2 a.m. The robe was taken off, the book put away and Eva climbed between the sheets to try and sleep for the night. Sleep....it wasn't something that came easily nowadays, not since the attack. She rolled over and tried to find a more comfortable position to no avail. This was getting her no where. In the end she was just staring at the ceiling through the darkness when the phone at her side suddenly sounded off startling her. Jerking away she stared at it hesitating about picking it up, a reaction due to her recent experiences. Guarded she rolled to her stomach and slowly reahed out plucking it up and dragging it to her ear. ' Hello?' ' I just thought I would call and check up on you Eva.' It was Mist. Relief flooded her form as she flopped back onto her pillows. ' I appreciate but everything is fine Mist.' Pause. ' Just as well Eva. Look there is a bottle of prescribed sleeping pills in the drawer. I took the liberty of filling your prescriptions for you since you didn't. Should you have trouble getting to sleep-' ' I am fine Mist honestly, but thank you for the thought.' ' Alright then. Get some rest. You have a long day ahead you yet again tomorrow.' ' I will.' She hung up without the option of saying good bye. Call her fickle, but that kind of barbed her with the woman acting more like a guardian than a business woman. Rolling she punched the pillow several times to 'fluff' it and slammed her head into staring at the wall. She was never going to sleep at this rate but she was refusing to take one of those damn pills too.... 3 a. m. Still no sleep. Taking a deep breath she sat up and swung her feet to the floor rubbing that face briskly as she released a low sound of aggravation and turned staring at the nightstand drawer thoughtfully. Muttering she yanked it open and pulled out the bottle to stare it like it was the bane of god. ' You win.' Popping it open she scoops out a pill and pops it in her mouth swallowing it down praying it was fast acting. Closing the bottle she tosses it back in and slides between the sheets again getting comfortable. She couldn't even remember shutting her eyes as sleep took over. 3: 08 a. m What were those pills anyway? -fin-
