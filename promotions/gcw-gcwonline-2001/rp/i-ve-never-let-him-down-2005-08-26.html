I've Never Let Him Down

26 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 26th, 2005 Grey smoke fills the air as the remainder of an in scent comes crashing into ashes. As the smoke dispatches into the air, Mike Hardy’s figure is seen through the fading smog. Dressed in a different set of apparel, Mike sits comfortably with his back firmly against the wall with one leg laid out and the other hunched up as he rests his arms and head on it. Black sweatpants and a torn off-white wife beater are all he rests in. Steven Caldera enters his room and places his suitcase on the desk. He walks over to his mirror, mumbling something to himself as he loosens the tie that hangs from his neck. He unbuttons a few of the top buttons and removes the tie from over his head. He turns to take a scare from Mike who remains relaxing against the wall. Steven- “Mike, How long have you been here?” “I’ve been just relaxing here for awhile, maybe like 2 hours or something.” Steven- “What brings you to my office” You’re to hours early for our training session.” “No I know Steven, I have just come to appreciate the softer side of life. I’ve learned to mellow down a little bit. Take life as it comes. You know, roll with the punches. I just want to thank you for making me who I am today Steven. You’ll never know just how much I appreciate all you’ve done for me.” Steven- “Mike, although I like the kind gesture and the gratitude, you are the one who made you who you are today. I shaped you, but You formed You. I merely showed you the path that you decided to take. So you should be thanking yourself for having what it takes to become this new you. I’m proud to say Mike, you’ve never let me down.” I feel although I have accomplished something. A Feat that most anyone else couldn’t accomplish. I have something that most wrestlers spend their whole career aiming for. I have His Respect. I have Steven Caldera’s respect. I have reached a new stage in my life. I feel as though I stand on the pedestal one step below perfection. All I need now for that final step is the Heavyweight Championship. I can’t come from my glory to return the man a response, I simply nod my head and shake his hand and walk off back out of this room. He knows I’m excited, he senses my pride. He knows just as much as I do that I’m ready to take that final step. I walk down the corridor back towards my locker room. As proud as proud can be, I come upon Octavian’s door. With a sly grin I read his nameplate and shiver in utter enjoyment. I can’t express the excitement I feel at this point. It’s like winning the lottery twice in one week. I know that in two days I will get to bring my career to the next level. I will be able to carve my name into this sport. It will be Octavian and myself. Nothing else matters? “See you in two days Octy?”
