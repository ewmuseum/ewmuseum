A visit in the land of the rising sun. Pt 1

29 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/29/07 Through the darkness comes the destiny forgotten by all man. In the end you are left alone as your partners and allies desert you. Oblivion holds no sway in the final judgments and endings. If you want power all your own you will have to grasp the dangers with your own hands and crush them. Your own strength is the strength needed to obliterate destiny and create your own path. You are the master of your own fate. You use people as stepping stones and then you crush the ones who aspire to your own greatness. Shaman 2007 Osaka , Japan November 2007. Standing well over most of the crowd Shaman walks with a small rather impish looking Japanese translator. Shaman looks down at the man as he motions to a building nearby. He enters and says something to the man at the counter and looks up at Shaman and nods. The translator smiles and speaks and the man nods and bows to Shaman. "Hai Hai right this way Shaman san." Shaman raises an eyebrow as he walks Shaman to a small rolling door and opens up a door to an area no bigger than a tanning booth with a tv. He looks at the man and then to the translator. He looks inside and then turns looking back at the translator. "Ok what in the hell have you put in your wasabe?" The translator looks at Shaman and shrugs. "You asked for accommodations and this is what i had come up with for you sir." Shaman points at the small accommodation. "If i wanted to be shoved into a hamster cage i would have asked for it. Damn it lemme make a phone call." Shaman opens his cell phone frowns looking at the man. He looks rather annoyed as he speaks. "Konichiwa Inaki!" Shaman smiles as the man on the other end is speaking rather fast and excitedly. Shaman smiles and listens and hands the phone to the translator who listens and then looks at Shaman in shock and then grins as he is handed the phone back quickly. "Arigato Inaki see you in a bit." Hanging up the ell phone Shaman grins. "Lead the way Monago san" The translator named Monago looks at Shaman questioningly, but does as he is told and escorts Shaman out of the hotel over to a waiting taxi. They ride for a few blocks and stop in front of a large mansion type building. Shaman gets out and out comes his longtime wrestling friend Inaki Hotaka. Inaki Hotaka who used to be a member of the WWA having wrestled in smaller circuits around the world returned to Japan to wrestle in his homeland to prepare and train new students and others whom he wanted to see excel in the outer world. In fact he thought about returning to the United States so it is a good thing his friend Shaman was here. He turned and opened the door and motioned for Shaman to enter behind him. They entered the sitting room after removing their shoes. Shaman knelt at the tables as Inaki joined him and poured some saki. "Longtime no see Andrew san." Shaman looked at Inaki and smiled. "Yes it has old friend. How has the business been tretating you?" Inaki sipped the saki and looked up at Shaman. "It has been tougher than usual, but i just signed a new contract with Asian West Pro and they want me to be their representative to the United States. Yet surprisingly enough alot of the Japanese folks won't go to the states because of the money business lately. Anyways that leads me to question you on something." Shaman raises his head and looks at Inaki and nods. "What is it?" Inaki looks at Shaman and grins. "Shaman where has Bryan Mayhem gone and where is the other guy?" Shaman purses his lips and then looks at Inaki. "Last report Mayhem disappeared over in the United Kingdom. The other guy has returned to Norway for awhile, but said he'd be back after he handled some family business. You are the detective of sorts Inaki maybe you should look into where my brother is. He was around these parts recently." Inaki smiled and shrugged. "I saw your brother two weeks ago at a show at the Tokyo Dome. It was a huge event and it was alot more fun than the WWA shows. The fans here put their hearts into it." Shaman sighs and leans back on his legs a bit and turns looking at the cultural paintings of Inaki's household. He looks at Inaki and nods and slowly stands to his feet. "Inaki where is your dojo my friend?" Inaki stands up and turns showing Shaman the way as they both enter Shaman looks in and notices the temple Buddha across the room on the temporary alter of sorts. Shaman walks in and sits Indian style in the center of the room as Inaki bows and departs closing the door behind him. Shaman opens his eyes and watches the candles flames flicker. "You notice the small things and the changing of cultures as a learning experience. Yet in the end i still have not done anything for myself to better my situation. Mayhem disappears suddenly to do what only the spirits know. Oblivions true goal is on the back burner unless i approach the plans myself and adjust them or i simply do what i was meant to do and make people bleed and suffer in pain." Shaman grins closing his eyes thinking of that violent goal. That would please him most as he places both hands upon his knees. He grips them slightly as he whispers a quiet prayer to the great spirit. His Native American side showing once in awhile knowing he has not stopped believing in what his father and grandfather had taught him. He knew there was a war to be won and this was his war now. Opening his eyes he stood up and walked to the sliding door and opened it and exited out into the garden nearby and stopped looking down into a water pool with gold fish and carp swimming around in it. He watched the fish swim together as he pondered. "GCW is much like these fish. Alot of big fish and alot of even smaller fish wanting what they cannot have. It is a fight for survival and i will gain my utmost survival and destroy everything and anything that steps in my path. Call this smack talk or simply call this the rantings of a man bent of having his way. Doesn't really matter to me either way. I am going to do what i have to do and in the end that will be enough." Shaman turns and walks away across a small wooden bridge and stops n it leanign against the railing and looks p at the night sky. "These people and this culture. Have endured some of the most violent wars and destruction. Nagasaki and Hiroshima the most recent events that come to mind, but they rebuilt and grew once again. Mother nature takes away from them and they rebuild again. Now perhaps it is my turn to take a page from that book and rebuild and re mold for what i have in my future plans." Quietly Shaman disappears into the darkness as Inaki Hotaka stops near a door and slides it shut uttering no sound or word as the crickets chirp under the cool moonlit air.
