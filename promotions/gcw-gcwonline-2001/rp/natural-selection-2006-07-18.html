Natural Selection

18 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18 July 2006 * WHAP * * WHAP * * WHAP * The sound of a pair of taped fists hitting a weighted punching bag in distinguishable. The sound of taped flesh hitting a bag made up of vinyl and sand at a rhythm that it almost feels like it’s music. * WHAP * * WHAP * * WHAP * It almost seems as if every punch that hits the bag is imagined as a punch hitting a person. The cadence that the punches take a furious pace. * WHAP * * WHAP * * WHAP * It’s almost like pounding away on this bag is bringing the combatant to a Zen like state. His upper body glistens as the little bit of moonlight shines though the boards that are hammered up, covering the windows. The dimly lit gymnasium shows very little of what this man once was. A broken boxing ring. A taped up weighted bag. Two broken basketball rims hang overhead. Above them, the burned out lights that once illuminated this gym. Shades of what this man has become. In the center of the floor is a painting of a phoenix. Much like the portrait, this man is planning to rise again. Once he was proud. Then he was broken. Now, there is only one thing for him. Vengeance. Pain. Suffering. This man doesn’t see the world in black and white anymore. For he’s become a shade of gray himself. No more, he thinks. No more will those that have stood in his way be able to do so. No more will the ones that hurt him be able to do so. No more will he live in the shadow of an older brother with his own agenda. For Dorian Ryan, his return match to GCW is about more than a win. It’s about more than trying to be the best wrestler in the match. It’s about starting to get redemption for what has happened. Not only for himself. But for those that haven’t been able to stand for themselves. “Hey, mister.” A bum. Homeless for the last ten years. He found his home in this dank old gym. “Hey, mister.” The man would never hurt a fly. His teeth having been rotted out due to years of neglect. This was once a proud man himself. He lost his job when his plant closed. He wasn’t able to find another job. His wife left him. He had lost everything. “Hey, mister.” He’s seen this man come into his home, but never thought to try and talk to him. Tonight was different for some reason. This homeless man wasn’t seeing the same person. He was seeing someone a bit more confident in what their path had in store. “Hey, mister.” Dorian sees him as a person that hasn’t tried to help himself. He doesn’t see pity on this man. Rather, he sees contempt. A man that shows apathy towards himself was of no interest to him. “Hey, mister, I’m talking to you.” The bum was more forceful in his tone. Something that didn’t settle very well on Dorian, who starts to kick away at the bag. Each kick causes a creaking in the chain elevating it. “Hey, mister”.” The bum steps right up to Dorian, who throws a spinning back kick into the bag. The force of the kick was enough to break the chain, bringing the heavy, sand filled bag to plummet to the floor. “Hey, mister, could you spare some change?” Dorian didn’t respond as he grabbed his towel from one of the remaining ring ropes and headed to the door. “You asshole. All I wanted was some change to get some food. Can’t you spare any change?” Dorian just keeps walking and stops on the picture of the Phoenix. He drops a piece of paper right on the center of it and then continues walking. The bum walks over and looks at the paper. To his delight, he sees that it’s a twenty dollar bill. As he looks up to thank Dorian, he just sees a door closing. -- ---------------------------------------------------------- Dorian Ryan finds himself on the outskirts of town. This was just like many towns that he had visited in the past. In fact, a person will be raped here. Another person murdered. People that didn’t have a way to stand up for themselves. A gang turf war will break out at this very spot. For some people, they would think of it as just another symptom of the violence in America. For others, it would be called Natural Selection. Police sirens blare. Gunshots are heard. Cries for help are ignored. It’s the dark truth of this world. Dorian wanted to bring a son to this world. Now, as he focuses on the shades of gray, doesn’t know if he wants to do that anymore. Maya Angelou once said to go with yourself. In a world like this, it’s hard to do. Everyone relies on drugs, or television, or fast food, or sex, or some other addiction to get them through their day. Dorian was now seeing that. He was now seeing the conformity that the everyday world was suffering from. It was that conformity that caused his injury. It was that conformity that allowed Dies Irae to run roughshod over GCW. It was that conformity that has nearly rocked the world. Planes crash, towers fall, bombs explode. Terrorists fight, soldiers respond, innocents get hurt. For a person of reasonable morals, it’s too much. Maybe this is what has caused Dorian Ryan to throw away everything that he was about in the ring. Maybe this was why he effectively killed Sam Wolack with the swipe of a pen. He sees that the world of the real, and the world of the fantasy were no longer separated by a fine line. He looks at GCW as evidence of that. Vampires and video game characters fighting along side a group of Native Americans. This is what makes up the most dominant force in GCW. Dorian sat idly by for four months. After an injury and being terminated, he watched as the company that resurrected him like a phoenix was being torn apart. Once again, he finds that he needs to be resurrected. This time, he doesn’t come back as X. He doesn’t come back as Prime. He doesn’t come back as Bishop. He doesn’t come back as Exodus. He doesn’t come back as Sam Wolack. He comes back as the Deadpool. In a world of gray, he’s going to stand out. The white knight with the black heart. Dorian Ryan looks towards his return match not as a man that wants to win, or as a man that has to win. Dorian Ryan looks towards his return match as a man prepared to start a war to bring the chaos to an end. Dorian Ryan sits there. Watching. Waiting. Listening.
