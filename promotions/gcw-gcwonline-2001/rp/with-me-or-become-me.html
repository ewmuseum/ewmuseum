With Me or Become Me

Date: present "For this, I remain in solitude" */*/*/*/*/*/*/*/*/*/*/*/*/*/*/* "No, I know that I killed you, Christian. I killed you in the courtyard!" Zacharia stares blankly at the ground, trying to project the past into his eyes. He struggles immensely trying to work out the truth from the fiction. Flashes of Christian laughing while Zacharia chokes him, then he swings down the shovel in fast motion, seeing Christian fade into the grave. "Zachy boy, I'm still here. How could you have killed me if I'm standing right here?" Christian walks over to Taylor and puts his hand on his shoulder, but it still doesn't break his concentration on the past. "I know that you feel that. How could you have killed me if my hand is clearly..." Zacharia shakes his head quickly and then squints his eyes in pain. The image of the grave dissipates but then Christian appears before him in the courtyard, not wearing his mask while Taylor finishes digging a grave. "Who are you? Christian? Jerome?...." Christian instantly appears before him, leaning in his ear. "I am you" His laughter lingers in the courtyard as he disappears. "I am jake Fenning..." Zacharia shakes his head again as Christian paces around him, taking in the confusion of Zacharia. He pictures the courtyard once more with Christian's body in the grave, dirt being piled onto him, but then Christian disappears from the grave and Zacharia stops shoveling, but then Christian appears once more, but his eyes are open and his face contorts into a maddening silent laugher. "NO! I watched you die! I buried you at an unmarked...you were....I dug a grave...." Christian is now sitting in a pew in front of him. "Really? I'm dead? Son, you have some serious demons to work out, clearly. I'm right here, in the flesh!" Zacharia struggles internally, sweating profusely. He shakes his head again and looks up at Christian in the pew, smiling up at him. Zacharia shakes his head, hoping to see him gone, but he is still siting there, raising his hand in a sadistic greeting. Zacharia turns around and stares at the candle next to the alter. The fire flickers into Zacharia's eyes and turn into a burning fire pit in an alley. He looks down at the two men beaten and asking for forgiveness below his feet. Christian comes in behind him. "You do as you are instructed." Christian steps up next to Taylor as he responds. "Instructed? By who?" Taylor keeps up the dialog with Christian, as Harry the bum stares at him in fear. "You, you've been following me. You followed me into the alley! You, you watched me and tried to get me..." "..to do what? I'm no killer, you are, Zach. You wanted to kill them." Zacharia squints his eyes again and shakes his head, but Christian disappears out of the alley and Zacharia continues to argue with himself, staring at the blood in his hands. Harry looks up petrified, yet confused of the conversation. Zacharia contorts his face and Christian's voice comes out of it. Zacharia shakes his head and looks at Christian, slowly walking towards him up the isle. "..wait..." "Maybe...I'm no psychiatrist, but you just may have to entertain the idea that I don't exist." Zacharia shakes his head and yells. Christian laughs to match the magnitude of the outburst, only making it resinate against the barren walls of the church. "NOOO! You exist! You have talked to people, you've been around people with me... You've..." In Zacharia's head he starts to see Christian standing with him talking to the bums in the alley, but then disappears as Zacharia continues to talk to them, contorting his face. Then, the two of them are standing in the park, talking to the cop about GCW, but as Christian starts to talk, he merges into Taylor and they begin to talk together. The confusion on the police officer's face expresses the confusion of Taylor inside the church. "I talked to people, son? No, you've talked to people...." Again, the two appear in the church, with the two boys sitting near the alter on the steps. Christian is in the distance watching Zacharia and the boys talk. He becomes frustrated and the boys begin to cry uncontrollably. Zacharia gets up and walks towards Christian. "Son, you need to be more gentle. They will tell you want they saw.." Zacharia's voice contorts and becomes more natural looking as Christian merges into his. His voice starts off as Christians, but then it turns into Zacharia's. The boy looks up madly confused as Zacharia turns around, no longer frustrated, and talks to the boys gently. Zacharia shakes his head madly and holds his hands over his ears, and begins to yell, tears gently sneaking out of the corners of his eyes and glide between the fine hairs on his cheek. He opens his eyes and sees Christian toe to toe with him, staring him down, very seriously. "You do the talking and I help you come to your senses." "Like telling me to kill two men who have suffered enough?" Zacharia holds back tears of confusion and fear, but has no control over his trembling lower lip. His face starts to fluster and the sweat dances on his brow. "No, you try to mess with my head. You try to use the words of Father Emanuel against me to make me a rogue!" "If I am using words against you, yet I am you, then where do we stand? This is no song and dance, Zach! We are..." "William Wilson! That bullshit that you put into my head. You are my..." "Not quite..." Christian takes a few steps back and puts his hand on the alter where a small book lies open. "William Wilson, my son," Christian cannot help but laugh, "was a doppleganger which..." Christian's words filter into the background of his head as he struggles to maintain composure. A film strip struggles past the eyes of Zacharia of the story of William Wilson. The final sequence of Wilson killing the twin slows down as the knife is jabbed into the stomach of his twin... "You have conquered, and I yield. Yet, henceforward art thou also dead-dead to the World, to Heaven and to Hope! In me didst thou exist-and, in my death, see by this image, which is thine own, how utterly thou hast murdered thyself." Zacharia looks up at Christian as his voice once again comes into audible decibels. "You are my William Wilson. You are me! The story was never about a twin or doppleganger, but another half of oneself..." Christian smiles his maddening expression. "he existed as much as i exist to you. How maddening!" His laughter infuriates Zacharia to no end to the point that he lunges forward and attacks Christian. He just stands there, punches landing every which way, but no damage is scene from the wild punches. Zacharia stops and then looks at Christian, still standing unharmed. He swings, enraged, and his fist goes right through the target, sending him falling down the stairs. Zacharia punches the ground and charges back at Christian. Every punch that is thrown is swatted away by Christian, knowing where the punches will land before they are even thrown. He comes in for a haymaker, but is tossed aside crashing into the alter. After catching himself on the alter, he opens his eyes to see the title "William Wilson" staring at him. He picks it up and throws it in the direction of Christian, but the book is caught. "If you don't exist, how the hell can you catch the damn book?" Christian points to the alter. Zacharia looks back down at his hand, where the book still remains. Zacharia goes to throw the book again, but Christian is no longer standing beside him, instead he is behind the alter, pouring some wine into a sacramental glass reminiscent of the memorialized Holy Grail. "Have I become..." "Crazy?" Christian raises the cup to his lips, some wine dripping off his lips like blood. "Yum, good year..." He wipes the excess and puts the cup down. "Crazy is a word that we do not like. I like to think of it as empowered. At least, my position is more of an empowerment. I mean, look at yourself, and by that I mean Zacharia Taylor. You are a mess. Don't know real from fiction." "I'm a lunatic..." "Again, we do not like those words. And I really mean you as well. Such a contrived word if you ask me. What is the prognosis of this one, well that will be up to you, but yelling and attacking your imaginary friend, frankly, that is something we need to put an end to..." Taylor turns, running down the stairs and down the isle, looking back to see Christian raising his glass again, but upon return, Christian is right in front of him, causing Taylor to trip over his feet and fall through him. Christian laughs, acting like his cut of wine was going to be compromised. The echo from his laugh hits Taylor like acid, forcing him in a rage to rush out of the church. The street lights and mystified moon cast elongated shadows upon the city of Clifton. Taylor looks up and down the street to see an unusual amount of people to be out at this time of day. He looks back at the church doors that close behind him and see them much more stretched out than what comes to memory. He nearly falls down the steps as they seem to move in erratic fashion. Someone walking by looks up at Zacharia, their face dark and sullen, nearly disdainful. Zacharia looks around, confused. The city looks like it is completely being taken over in shadows. He can barely believe his eyes, until something snaps him back to realization. "I can make your life a living hell." Every enunciated word makes Zacharia cringe. He takes a few steps out to the sidewalk when Christian appears next to him. "Where do you think you're going? Think you can just walk away from everything I've done for you?" He looks more annoyed than confused, and turns Christian as if ready to fight again. "Look, you need to get out of my head, Christian or..." "...or else what? You will hit me? Yes, that worked soooo well before." Zacharia nearly walks into someone as he turns around and the guy looks at him abruptly and gives him the finger. Taylor shakes his head and sees the guy smile and wave back apologetically, but then quickly becomes shadowed and gives Taylor the finger again. "Oh, I know. You want to kill him. You want to hurt him, don't you son?" Taylor grabs his hood and pulls it down on his head tight and shakes his head back and forth. "No I don't. Stop." "I can feel it build up inside of you." "No you don't. You are messing with my head." "You never needed me before to go after someone. Zacharia Taylor, THE MERCENARY OF GOD, backing down from someone that just gave him the finger." "Please... please stop..." Tears start to drip from his eyes as people start to stare at him. The shadows shudder like jumping film between light and dark, strict and fluid. Two men start to approach Zacharia, jumping between a soft expression and a stern one. "Do you need help, sir" Zacharia looks up at them in a maddening rage. "Get the fuck away from me! You want to end up...." He shudders away and begins to weep. "please...please just walk away from me. I don't want to hurt you." Christian's laugh starts to echo through his head. "Kill him, Zacharia. You know you want to. You need a release, the only thing that makes you feel human is to take the life of another, to teach them a lesson in the way of the Lord. To see blood..." "GET OUT OF MY HEAD!" Everyone in the vicinity of the church stops as if time is stopped. Nobody knows how to deal with a man that is losing his mind. The light starts to return to the street and everyone's faces fill with fear and sympathy, losing their stern desire for blood. Zacharia takes the chance to run back inside of the church. "You know, Zach, we can do this the hard way, or the easy way. I prefer the easy way..." "I need to know certain things. Some stuff needs to be resolved..." "Oh," Christian appears sitting in the corner of the church, "Like a Q & A? I can do that for you. I guess it is the least that I owe you..." Zacharia stares at him and then tilts his head slightly towards the front of the church, and just like that, Christian is gone. "I want to know how you knew everything about the Modern Testament. How did you quote it? Who wrote it?" Christian appears sitting behind the alter as Zacharia climbs up towards a chair near the back wall, not even taking notice to Christian's reappearance. "You aren't using your head, Zach! How would someone know something they were never told if they didn't exist? I am part of you. You made me. I know everything you know. You made me smart, strong, witty. You made me everything that you wanted to be. You projected your wants and needs into me." Zacharia holds his head up with the back of the chair. His eyes pierce the back of the alter. "But I do not think that you..." "No? You were!" Christian closes his eyes and in turn, so does Zacharia. Zacharia starts to see himself in the courtyard, the first time he fought Christian. Christian is striking Zacharia and is a lot faster than him. "See, I was faster, stronger, and better than you in every way of fighting." Zacharia connects, but then loses his footing and gets taken down. "You wanted to be better. You wanted to be better than me, then what you thought you were. You made me better than you, so you can learn from your flaws. You eventually became better..." Now it is when Zacharia fought him after he recovered from his wounded leg and arm. Zacharia is faster and is beating him in every punch and kick, every takedown. "You became confident and stronger. You learned..." Their little moment of realization causes them to open their eyes. Zacharia becomes less tense and runs his hands through his hair. He stands from his seat and walks over to where Christian was behind the alter. He raises the mug that Christian was drinking from and notices it to be empty. He laughs to himself and pours out some wine. "Now, I want to know about Father..." Suddenly, the doors fly open at the front of the church as six police officers and two EMT's come in with a gurney. Zacharia, perplexed by the situation, puts down his wine and walks to the edge of the stairs as the emergency crew closes in. Christian appears in the pew next to the stairs. "Sir, are you alright? Please stay calm, and remain where you are standing." Zacharia looks over at Christian who is smiling while shrugging his shoulders. He turns back to the police officer who spoke. "Yes, officer, but I do not understand what would call for such a disturbance." The police officer who spoke a moment ago waves down the rest of the men and takes his hand off of his holster. "Sir, we had reports that you were distraught." The man hesitates on the final word, distraught being very specific i his word choice. "We got a call from someone outside the church saying they witnessed you yelling a screaming, and threatening a man." "No, sir. I mean, I received some," Zacharia looks at the smug Christian next to the police officer, "devastating news. I was distraught sir, but now I am perfectly fine. Thank you for the prompt response." "Sir, would you want to came into the station and talk with someone?" "Why, I think this conversation suffices?" The police officer looks around the church and then turns to his right where Zacharia keeps looking, but sees nothing. He tries to tilt his head to his fellow officers to signal to check the pew. The move over and begin to look. "If you don't mind, we just want to look around for a moment." "By no means do you have to worry, this is a church, all are welcome under the bearings of their own purpose." Zacharia turns around and walks back to his cup of wine. He notices the book that contains the story of "William Wilson" still lays open, with a piece of paper sticking out of it. He flips around the book and takes out the paper to see his own handwriting littering the page in notes. Looking closer he starts to make out some of the notes: Jake Fenning......Who killed Father Emanuel?.......Why when I needed him so bad.......I still have so many questions....Christian.......What will happen with the church?.......Chris Bagwell.....Thomas....Edgar Allen Poe.... Zacharia looks up from the book, infuriated with himself. He wrote out the words Christian and Thomas on this paper, but nowhere near one another. This was before Christian came to the church. This is before he came to be. Why was this paper in this book. He remembers the paper and writing it out, but he never heard of Poe or the story before Christian mentioned it. Why would the names be there? He looks up from the book to see Christian looking on no more than three feet in front of him. "What do you have there?" A police officer looks up at the alter and sees Zacharia becoming more and more angry. He calls out, but his voice is inaudible. "You will either be with me...." Everything starts to grow faint and silent. Several officers and the EMT guys are running up to the alter as everything goes black. "...or you will become me..."
