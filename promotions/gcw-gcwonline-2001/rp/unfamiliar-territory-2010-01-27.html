Unfamiliar territory

27 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/27/10 Samuelsson walks forward looking over at the guy pointing at the arcade game. It has paintings of massive Vikings doing battle upon huge ships. Samuelsson looks at the video game and shrugs slightly looking at the man nearby. "Why in the hell am i here again?" The man clears his throat looking at Samuelsson chuckling. "Because we paid you to be here and you're the market here for the game. So just play it and shut up." Samuelsson looks at the much smaller man as his anger builds. He grabs the controls and begins playing the game. Alot of blood and carnage he kind of likes that. Suddenly his cell phone rings as he stops playing the game. He listens to the voice on the other end a bit and runs his fingers through his beard. "Ok ok look i'll be there in a few hours. No Valkyrie i don't give a damn about who i am facing." He gets quiet and looks around him. "Yeah i am kind of stuck here at the moment. Shaman set me up in a mess. Yes it was a favor!" The smaller business man smacks Samuelsson's arm and points at the video game. Samuelsson shrugs and looks at the video game. "Yeah i will get to it. You are on my dime." Samuelsson hangs up the cell and begins playing the video game again and looks at his watch as he does so. A camera steps in close filming him playing the game. Samuelsson turns his gaze to the camera man with annoyance. The camera man backs up a bit as the business man smacks Samuelsson on the arm again. "Hey moron your supposed to let the camera guy here video tape you playing the game." Samuelsson looks at the business man thinking to himself. He smiles a bit knowing that he could break this guy in three and crush his balls beneath his feet just to feel them pop. Samuelsson chuckles as the businessman marches back and forth annoyed. He looks over at Samuelsson laughing. He steps forward and shoves Samuelsson on the arm. Samuelsson narrows his gaze and looks at the businessman shoving him. "You have a death wish little man?" The businessman mumbles something as he speaks up. "Why we hire idiotic moronic Norwegian's like you i never know. We can just stuff some weak willed idiot from anywhere in Viking movie props and sell this." Samuelsson turns his gaze to the Video game and then back to the businessman. "Well as my tag team partner would say to me. This isn't your choice, but what you have to do." Samuelsson shoves the little man away from him. He grins as he lowers his gaze back to the video game. His thoughts drift to crushing this little man between his fists against the skull. He imagines the frontal lobes being turned in to a gelatin matter. The bones cracking beneath each meaty club that are know as his fists. Perhaps even a few ways of breaking a mans back physically stroll through his brain as if was a casual walk in the park. He stops as the Businessman looks at him again. Suddenly the man steps forward firing a punch hitting Samuelsson in the back of the head. "Come on big guy i am going to knock you down a few pegs. You aren't so big and bad. So bring it bitch." Samuelsson turns slowly looking at the smaller man. His anger raging within him like a storm battering the Norwegian coasts. Samuelsson takes a few more punches from the man. Samuelsson snaps his arm forward quickly as he rotates the man so quickly and effortlessly. The momentum of the man flying off his feet swings the man like a ragdoll through the air. Samuelsson turns releasing the man as he flies through the store front window bouncing out onto the sidewalk. Samuelsson turns as the man slowly gets up. "Is...tha...that the best you got you Viking bitch. Let me show you how we do it in Boston." The man steps through and charges Samuelsson as Samuelsson grabs the man at the same time as he connects. Samuelsson hoists the smaller man up and into the air. He slams him down with a sickening slap sound onto the concrete. Samuelsson grabs the video arcade game and lifts it up slamming it down on the man as he seethes with anger. He looks at everyone around him. "Show is over now. If you want to walk away from here run now very fast." Samuelsson looks down and moves the wreckage of the Video game and lifts the broken business man who is bleeding down his face. Staring at the man who has his eyes open in fear. Samuelsson pulls his face close. "Good you now fear now. I promise you one thing now little man. You have never felt the fear you are feeling now. You will understand that you never will again unless you cross me." Samuelsson tosses the man back down upon the rubble. He turns and walks out through the broken window of the store and begins walking down the street as a familiar voice is heard. He turns and notices Shaman leaning with his arms crossed against the wall. Shaman laughs as he looks at the carnage. "Took you more than the ten minutes i expected you to explode. Hell that guy should have been crushed the first time he back talked you. Must be getting weak there Samuelsson old boy." Samuelsson looked around and then to Shaman. He turns and walks down the street. Shaman chuckles as he walks with him. "So what are you going to do about your match at World Wide 125?" Samuelsson walks on looking down at the ground in deep thought. he raises his head and then looks at Shaman. "Perhaps i will remind people why i am dangerous. I am one of the more feared in ring Submission artists. Perhaps a message needs to be sent to the O'Neill brothers on why they shouldn't take neither of us lightly." Shaman chuckles as he shakes his head. "Mayhem doesn't need to be reminded. he and i have had major wars in the past. This thing at NC-17 isn't any different." Samuelsson smirks as he walks on. "Good then i won't have to worry about hurting anyone's feelings when i beat the family heritage right out of an O'Neill." Shaman shrugs as they both walk away down the street.
