Deceipt Abound: Part 1

22 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/22/06 (but takes place 8/05) August 2005 There comes a time in everyone’s life where priorities need to be examined, and the weight of a career needs to be adjusted. For Tessa Windsor, that time had literally launched itself at her, in the form of an unexpected phone call from Global Wrestling owner, Bubba McCoy. A very disappointed Bubba McCoy at that, granted it should have been no surprise to the brunette beauty considering her commitment, or lack thereof, to the company. Anxiously twirling her brunette locks around the tips of her freshly manicured nails, the five foot four beauty could not help but walk on eggshells as she listened to the lecture that continued to pour from Mr. McCoy’s mouth. “Listen Ms. Windsor, I just can’t afford to pay you a six digit salary, if you’re going to be giving it a four digit effort.” “I understand.” She compliantly answered, all while crossing her fingers that the conversation wouldn’t get worse from here. “That's great, but those words mean nothing to me, until you actually start proving that you truly do understand.” “I know.” Unfortunately, vocally, it meant nothing to the man who had bills to pay, and fans to please, which only meant the worst for Tessa. “Which brings it down to one thing Ms. Windsor, you can show up next Monday ready to wrestle, or you can take the cut.” In her head it sounded like a fair ultimatum, but, down on paper the odds were against her. She couldn’t bear to miss another one of Evan’s baseball games, but at the same time she couldn’t stand to lose the income she’d grown well adjusted to. “But the kids?” She pleaded back with every bit of sympathy for her case she could possibly muster up. “Maybe you should have thought about that before you signed the dotted lines,” McCoy answered back without a bit of remorse. “You should know from previous experience Ms. Windsor that parenting, and pro wrestling, doesn’t work well together. So, once again you can show up, or, you can take the cut.” “But what about the Hunting Ground?” By all means it was her last resort to save her already waning career from slipping into the bottom of its slopes, “I didn’t come here for you, I came here because Christian offered me a spot. By all means it should be HIS choice to let me go.” “They want you gone too.” The remark left Tessa speechless. Could that really be the truth, or was it something forced to keep her from inquiring any further into the situation. After all, she’d thought that her bond with Dawn, as well as Vivica and Christian, was one that’d keep her safe in their pockets. They were her friends. Or so she thought. Indeed she thought. She thought very wrong. “Now you can wrestle, or you can leave.” The solution certainly wasn’t desirable, nor was the answer that Tessa had to give Mr. McCoy. “Then I’ll take the cut Mr. McCoy. I’m not missing out on the chance to make things right again for you, or any kind of salary you have to offer.” “That's fine my dear, it’s not me that’s losing the money.” As heartless as it sounded, the man did have the point. Her loss was his gain, either way. That’s the way the business worked, regardless of what others may say. Rather than give him any sort of response, she tossed the phone aside, cursed the man with everything she could think to say, and flopped down on the leather couch she’d taken quite a liking to lately to vent. On the bright side, at least now she’d have more time for Chris, and the kids. But for now, the shopping sprees, and remodeling plans, would have to be put on hold, even with the cushy salary Chris was currently pulling in. Speaking of the devil, the door opening alerted Tessa to the arrival of Chris Storm. Walking in though, it seemed the Perfect Storm was having equal amounts of bad luck today. A sour look stretched from his bottom lip to the floor. The kind-hearted Tessa instantly forgot about her own problems and began to worry about his. “Something the matter, Chris?” He waved her off with his hand and faked a smile, “No I’m fine, just a lot on my mind.” He brushed past the couch, hoping that she’d tag along and flopped down on the adjacent leather love seat, which had endured many of their past heartaches and headaches. His tired eyes reached up to hers. “How about you” How was your day?” “Actually”.?Taking a deep breath in, she attempted to ready herself for her latest sob story, until she too fell into that same pit of guilt. The only difference is, she had a plan to escape, and still get at least half the truth out. “I did a lot of thinking today.” Propping his two hundred and fifty pounds up as if were more like four hundred, he sunk beneath the layer of leather, knowing he’d never truly be ready to listen. Sure turning his ears would be good to get his mind off his own problems for a few minutes, but it still didn’t change that fact that at this point, Chris in his own mind, had no clue what he could offer to solve all of their problems. “Whatcha been thinking about?” Even though he’d yet to find written law of absolutely having to listen attentively to your significant others rants, he still sat down for yet another round. “Us’the kids”.the future.” Staring aimlessly at the ceiling above, she seemed to be in too much of a daze to actually be speaking the truth. “I can’t stand it anymore Chris. I hate not being there for them. I hate not knowing when I’m going to see you again. I hate it so much that I had to call Bubba and call it quits.” Shooting up from the love seat as if he’d be stung, he quickly shot a glare over towards her that demanded open ears. “What do you mean “quit?”” He stuttered, as his brain played a more advanced version of the game Chris had introduced to their son just a few years back, connect the dots. “Just that, I quit” Without a pause, she finished the outline for him. For Chris Storm, who’d lived his life according to wrestling, the whole situation made no sense. Why would someone give up a perfectly good wrestling job for a national company? “Okay’that’s fine”.but why?” Not surprisingly, he’d missed out on the whole family bit before, or if he had heard, it didn’t seem like a logical enough reason to just up and leave the ring with a company and a salary of such large proportions. “You aren’t hurt or anything are you” Because I know this doctor that can help you out and get you back into the ring in no time!” “Chris, stop, I’m fine!” She protested back without trying to provoke an argument of any sort. “I just want to get on with life. Don’t you think it’s about time we go on with life and finally fulfill those promises we made to each other years ago.” The look on Chris? face gave Tessa little hope that he had even bothered to remember any promise. Rather than incriminate himself, Chris tried to divert the conversation, “Retire” I tried that before. Twice actually. It doesn’t work out well for me. I’m just one of those guys that need to be kept busy. I go crazy sitting around the house all day.” Tessa sighed, as the panicking possibility of this conversation going nowhere but backwards surged forward in her mind. “You don’t need to. I can watch the kids. But don’t you think it’s about time we got on with life and tied the knot?” This was the last thing Storm needed to be thinking about, given the debate that raged on inside his mind. He knew she would be crushed if he turned her down at the moment, but at the same time he was too messed up to be popping the question at a moment like this. “I’ve been thinking about that as well. But not yet Tessa, I’ve finally started to carve my notch in UWF.” Just in time to save his case, a light bulb went off in his head. “Hey I have an idea, why don’t you manage me in the UWF” This way we could spend more time together. We could even bring the kids. The UWF is based in Chicago, and it doesn’t travel.” Still, he’d managed to avoid the topic at hand, which might have bought him some time, but not much. “Chris, I just want to settle down for a bit with this wrestling stuff, I hardly feel like a mother. Besides, I don’t need the kids seeing that crap that goes on in this business. Don’t you think it’s about time we started focusing more on what we’ve got here?” “It wouldn’t be hard at all; I’ve been looking at houses in Chicago since I signed the contract. I’ve just been putting it off since I don’t want another Charlotte. We could get something together. Move in together, enroll the kids into the local private school, and really move forward.” If the whole thing sounded planned out, it was because Chris had been planning it all out every night he spent alone, debating what he wanted more. Fame, or family. To wake up with Tessa by his side every morning. Or to wake up with a nagging knee injury, all for the chance of his second shot. Unfortunately, every time he thought about it, he usually found himself brushing it aside once more and trying to forget about it. Things were good for him now, and truth be told, he was afraid of change. As much as she hated to give in, especially on something she felt so strong about, she realized she was doing nothing but wasting her breath, “If that’s what you want”..” Her head noticeably drooped as she said this, most likely due to the weight of her soul tugging at her heart. It begged her to be persistent, to finally quit all this submissive crap, but her mind always brought her back to him walking out the door. It was her ideal future, or it was him. And quite frankly at the moment, she wanted him more. Or maybe not. Just as their relationship, her emotions had flipped flopped back and forth between her options, and now, she was back to where she wanted only her way. “Good.” He breathed a sigh of relief. At least he’d managed to avoid the sore subject of marriage once more, for the time being. “Besides, I already know you didn’t quit. I got the call awhile ago. I just never bothered to tell you, because I knew what’d it lead to.” Shamefully, she had to admit he was right for once. Why would he want to be the one to break the news to her, when it’d end up with another one of her heartaches in his lap. It wasn’t a wonder the man felt so much damn pressure. Still, with that said, Tessa felt the desire to try and act like she was tough enough on her own to change everything. “Actually, on second thought, forget what I said.” Though a bit reluctant with her words, it was obvious by the tone of her voice that she’d better things in mind. “I’ve got other plans.” While he wasn’t quite crushed, considering the stuff that’d fallen from his tongue were nothing but all the usual bullshit promises he’d used to calm her at times like this, he was rather taken back but this sudden change of heart. “Okay’and what would those be?” “I’m going to Toronto.” There was only one place of significance in Toronto that Chris could connect with his brunette beauty, and it wasn’t one he wanted her at. “No, Tessa?” “I need to.” It wasn’t often that the pint sized diva was firm with Chris, but when it came to her career, she wanted none of his say. Their goals might have been the same, but their hearts for the business were on two totally different levels. “You don’t need to. They are slum, Tessa. Absolute slum!” While Storm had a violent nature, it wasn’t the cause for this offset. This was out of concern. Pure concern Something that happened to little, that the pint sized diva never adjusted to it. Out of Tessa’s mouth poured naivety at it’s strongest peak, “They’re my friends, Chris. Dawn will help me out. So will Vivi and Christian. They have as long as I’ve known them.” They say if you love someone, you gotta let them go, right? Well, that’s exactly what Chris decided he needed to do. “Suit yourself, I’m not responsible for the repercussions.” Just as all the others who had been roped in, Tessa had her doubts. “There won’t be any.” If only she knew.
