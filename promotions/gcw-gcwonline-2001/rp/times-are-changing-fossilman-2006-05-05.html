Times are changing Fossilman

5 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/05/06 'There's one thing I hate more than cockroachs.....' A familiar voice sounds out. 'Rich Rollins...' Mike attired to the usual yet unusual taste of style. Worn out jeans with holes at the knees....and a navy blue shirt that reads, 'You fear hell, but hell fears me'. His hair tossed around in a mess, it's the same old Hardy. 'Renegade, you are a tick. A tick attached to my, and Steven's flesh. And no matter how many times we are to stomp on you....You just don't die off. And quite frankly Steven and I are sick of it. It is not to be tolerated any longer. You think because you are a legend or a Hall of Famer that you can treat people the way you do? You don't deserve to breathe our precious air. You are not worth of the oxygen that flows through your lungs. Hell you're not worthy of the life you possess. You're just another has been who can't face the final stage in his life. RETIREMENT! 'It's alright Rich, we know your past, how great you once were. But let's face it Renegade, you're merely an eighth of what you use to be. You're prehistoric, you are the fossil of the industry. Times are changing Rollins and just fess up that you can't deal with the changes. You think it's all about taking down the president of the company, the one who signs your paychecks, in order to win peoples respect. You're a god damn joke! No, your the god damn punch line that never was! And I along with others, I'm sure, are through with the same ruitine you live by. So that's why I am putting a stop to it. I want you Rollins...However you want whenever wherever you want. It's time to bleed for the right cause.'
