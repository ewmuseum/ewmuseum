Carson was not much of a sunshine kinda girl, already at the age of fourteen she preferred to stay inside, and as a result didn’t have many friends. In fact, if she didn’t have an older brother, she would have no friends. On this day, Carson was awaiting her brother to come home from the video store. With their parents away on vacation her brother was staying at the house with her. Her parents didn’t trust her, why she didn’t understand because unlike most kids her age she saw no appeal in the things they did. She was a pure straight edge, no drugs, no alcohol and definitely no sex. She had a strong Irish Catholic background, and wanted to save herself for marriage. Carson sat in the living room of her family’s house; she wore a pair of bibbed overalls, and a plain white shirt. Her long red hair was pulled back into a braided ponytail that lay softly against the small of her back. A glance at the clock told her, that Keegan should be back anytime now; he had already been gone for nearly an hour. The jiggling of the front door handle catches Carson’s attention. “He must have forgot his keys.” Carson said to herself, rising from the comfort of the living room chair. She approached the front door, and grasped the bronze handle with her delicate fingers, unlocked the door and began to open it. The door however, flings open, as a large barreling man charges through the doorway. He has deep black eyes, and long black greasy hair. His momentum knocks Carson onto her backside, and the large man slams the door behind him. Carson looks up at the man, her light green eyes filled with fear. The man looms over her, with a twisted look in his eyes. By guess, she’d say he was almost seven feet tall, but when you’re fourteen, and not even a full five feet yet, everything looks bigger then it is. The man yanks his filthy white shirt off, from over his head as his fingers begin to fondle his belt buckle. It doesn’t take Carson long to figure out what this man has in store for her, and tries to scurry away. The man grasps her ankle in one meat hook of a hand, and yanks her closer to him. A thin line of sweat forms over his brow, and Carson can almost feel his hot, stale breath on her face as he hunches over her. She kicks, she screams and she hollers for her brother, but he isn’t here. All she is left with is fear. The man grabs a hold of the clasps holding the bib of her overalls up, and rips them straight from the denim. She whimpers, but it only seems to excite him more. With one quick movement, he grabs the bib and yanks it from the buttons holding the bib along the sides, ripping the overalls off of her frail little body. Feeling degraded, and ashamed Carson tries to hide her face, as she lay in her white shirt and underwear. She knows what’s coming, and she chokes back the sobs. She can’t understand why God would let this happen. I’ve been so good, why this... why me... why now... she thinks to herself, as her mind struggles to block out what comes next... Keegan makes his way up the long sidewalk to the front entrance of the house. In his right hand he grasps a Block Buster bag, with a selection of movies to watch with his sister. His brown hair drapes over his eyes, which remain focused on the cobblestone below his feet. Even through his green hoodie, the chill still gets to him, and staying hunched like this is the best way to avoid the stings of cold. As a child, Keegan use to count his footsteps up the cobblestone walkway. As a child, it use to take him exactly one hundred forty five steps... though Keegan hasn’t counted in recent years, he is sure it is nowhere near that many steps any longer. As he reaches the step to the front door, his eyes dart up, noticing the mahogany wood door wide open. Concerned, and effectively alarmed Keegan shoots up the step and through the grand entrance of his parents million dollar house. As he enters the receiving chamber the bag he had been faithfully carrying drops to the black and white tiled floor. As mere feet in front of him, lay the body of his sister. Keegan shuts his eyes, trying to vanquish the sight before him, but even with his eyes clenched tight images of his sister’s naked body flash in front of him. He feels sick. Keegan shakes his head, trying to tell himself he’s imagining it, and as he opens his eyes again he sees that he is not. Carson’s body lay across the tile floor, her precious red hair a mess around her, her arms red and welted, and her neck... Her neck is covered with large purple bruises in the shape of fingers, and her tongue lay just out of the corner of her mouth. Keegan panics, still not believing what lay before his eyes. He drops down beside her, and checks her pulse... Nothing. He shoots to his feet and twirls around the house, as he has suddenly forgotten where the phone is. He needs to call 911; he needs to get the police, and the ambulances here. After a moment of confusion, it finally clicks and he rushes straight through the living room to the kitchen, where on the wall rests the ivory white receiver of the telephone. He yanks it off of the wall, and quickly punches in the three numbers, and waits. It takes only two rings, as a woman answers on the second, “911, what’s your emergency.” “Hello... my name is Keegan O’Conner, and I just got home, my sister... she’s... she’s...” Keegan tries but he chokes on the words, the tears roll steadily down his cheeks, the hot salty liquid burning his sweaty skin. “She’s dead...” He finally exclaims. Keegan sits in the living room chair, feeling at a loss for himself. The Coroner is preparing to take his sister’s body away, while Forensics continue scouring the floor and room trying to find any clues. Two detectives had been here as well; they were the men who had called his parents to deliver the bad news. Keegan could not have brought himself to do it no matter how badly he needed to. The detectives had informed Keegan his parents would be catching the first flight home, and expected to be here by this evening, tomorrow morning at the latest. Currently the two detectives were interviewing the neighbors, to see if any of them may have any useful information. A chaotic fire burned within him, knowing there was a man out there that had taken little Carson’s innocence, and her life. Keegan knew that should he ever find the man he would be hard pressed not to kill him. Keegan knew that his father had a .45 in the nightstand drawer of the master bedroom. His parents had no clue that he knew it was there but Keegan made a note of it, just in case. Keegan got up out of the chair, he felt like a caged animal for all he could do was pace. He had nothing to go on to find the pedophile that attacked his loving sister. Keegan almost jumped out of his skin when he heard the front doorbell ring. When he opened the door he saw a stout balding man in his mid-forties. He was wearing a simple black suit with an obnoxiously patterned orange tie. He flashed his badge at Keegan letting him know that he was one of the detectives working on his late sister’s case. “Mr. O’Conner I am Detective Joseph Holleran my partner and I spoke to you earlier. The woman across the street gave us some information that might lead us to your sisters killer.” Holleran told him bluntly. Keegan stood there with a stone face waiting for the information from the detective. Information that would hold the key to his sanity. “Well, what did she say?” Keegan finally asks him impatiently. “She said she saw a man roughly about seven foot tall, on the heavier side with long dark hair get out of a banged up, late model Monte Carlo. The car shouldn’t be too hard for us to track down because of the Confederate flag decal in the back windshield.” Holleran explained in a tone only a hardened officer of the law could ever use. Keegan absorbed all of the information like a sponge. He nodded at the detective. “So how long do you think it will take you to catch this creep?” He asked with a fire burning behind his eyes. “We will put out an APB on the car and we are going to have your neighbor come down to the station with us to look at photos of prior felons. Maybe she will recognize one of them.” For the first time Detective Holleran showed a glimmer of hope in his eyes. “Well if there are any advancements please feel free to call. My parents should be home soon now.” With that he closed the door and decided to begin a search of his own. Keegan knew that the police took their sweet ass time in cases like this.
