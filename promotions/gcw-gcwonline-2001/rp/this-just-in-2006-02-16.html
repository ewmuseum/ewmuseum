This Just In!

16 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-16-06 When asked for a press statement concerning his upcoming GCW Rampage Tournament qualifier against Kimbusa, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins was more than happy to oblige: “There’s only one fat wrestler I ever liked, and Pete made a better seamstress than a grappler. Whoever calls the shots these days in GCW, they honestly think they’re makin” things difficult for me by tossing a fat Kimbusa-sized roadblock in my way? What they don’t realize is that I don’t give in to pressure, even when it weights four million pounds and smells like my dead mother’s vagina. I can say that in print, right? Anyway, I’m about to slap the shit out of everybody’s favorite RPG nut in GTT5, the man who, as it happens, also eliminated big “ol Kimbo last round. “I made Angelo Deville look like a Downs patient in the Olympics for Christ’s sake. You really think Kimbusa scares me” Oh wait, I actually wrote a poem for the occasion: Hey diddle diddle, I play Mrs. [name withheld] like a fiddle When my hands get too tired to fight Kimbusa ain’t shit, I’ll make [name withheld] submit As we go bumpy-bump all through the night “I should have been a poet,” Rollins concluded as he headed off to catch a flight back to Chicago.
