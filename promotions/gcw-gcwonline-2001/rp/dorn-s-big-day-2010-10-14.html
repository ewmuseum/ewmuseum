Dorn's Big Day

14 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/14/10 “Congratulations, old friend…” Lucius Benson’s voice sings of praise, but his mind and thoughts wander elsewhere. The sound of the hot, bubbling torrent before him causes a lapse in focus as his eyes stare past Dorn into infinity. Lucius is seated in an office chair, wearing one of his traditional sharp business suits, before a medicinal massage tub. Slouched against the warm metal of the tub is The Archon Dorn Tracer, drumming his fingers impatiently along the steel and giving a troubled sneer. “Congratulations are unnecessary, Lucius.” Dorn clears his throat and shakes his head before continuing, never meeting eyes with Lucius, “It’s a story as old as time. A discriminating, bastard company sends twin idiots in my direction and I proceed to crush their skulls into dust. The Goddess’s energy and rage flows through me. They never stood a chance.” Dorn slides down the wall of the tub for a brief moment, lowering more of his back into the churching waters. A look of relief washes over his face and he lets out a long exhale. Lucius Benson adjusts his glasses for a moment before raising an eyebrow to Dorn and giving a somewhat condescending sneer. “Yes, and apparently your old bones didn’t stand much of a chance either…” The Archon snaps his gaze in Benson’s direction for the first time in their encounter and grimaces, almost in a disgusted manner, “You better watch yourself, Lucius. Every day you sound more and more like one of… THEM. I am driven by divine purpose. She watches over me and keeps me upright to deal with the moronic piles of rat shit that we encounter every day. I am Her messenger; she won’t let anything happen to me… “Dorn’s voice trails off as he turns his head away from Lucius once more and slowly closes his eyes. Words go unspoken for a few long moments before Lucius Benson rises up from his office chair and begins to walk towards the door of the therapy room. Leaving the sounds of the bubbling Dorn Tracer soup behind him, he opens the door and begins to step out before the sound of the Archon’s voice freezes him in his tracks. “Have you received the card for the next sideshow, yet?” Benson only slightly turns his head, meeting Dorn’s question out of the corner of his eye. “I have.” He can hear the churching waters sloshing around as Dorn repositions himself in the tub. He crosses his arms across the steel and stares directly at Benson now, suddenly interested in ANYTHING Benson has to say. Lucius decides to turn around and well and folds his arms across his chest before continuing. “You’re in a championship match…” The Archon’s eyes light up from behind his salt-and-pepper hair and he raises himself up slightly. “Well, I guess the blaspheming masses of downs robots in the GCW front office decided they were better to give themselves to Her than doom themselves to a miserable, fiery death.” Dorn gives an almost maniacal laugh, obviously delighted at the news. “Tell me, Lucius. Are they letting me destroy Jay Terror? Vivica Valentine? There are so many unbelievers and fakes, I’m afraid there might not be enough time. Perhaps the GCW brass will let me throw them all in a barn and set it ablaze…” Dorn continues to speak, but Lucius’ mind drifts off for a moment. The words of the Archon don’t quite register as Benson becomes lost within his own thoughts. This may be getting out of control… We hadn’t planned this. “LUCIUS!” Dorn’s voice rings out like a gunshot, snapping Lucius Benson back into reality. He clears his throat for a second before giving a blank smile to an obviously annoyed Dorn Tracer. “I apologize. I was just thinking about what we’ve set in motion. This is already moving quite fast, don’t you think?” An attempt to reason with the Archon is an attempt that cannot and will not succeed. Dorn scoffs at Lucius’ words before shaking his head. “Your words are becoming troublesome. Thank the Goddess for the strength she gives us to deal a staggering blow to backsliding GCW and its false, paper champions. Allowing them to dance around with championship gold is an insult to Her, to me, and to anyone that watches this fucking circus.” Dorn rises off the steel of the tub and relaxes against the back wall. “What title are they handing me?” Lucius adjusts his eye glasses a moment before speaking. “The Hardcore championship is what you’ll be contending for, held by Triumph Frost. I’ve already begun the research…” Dorn scoffs and waves Lucius’ words off dismissingly, “Yes yes, I know. Another opponent, another sacrifice. It will be glorious to mangle the little shit in front of the Goddess and everyone. Since I’m sure it will be Hardcore rules, I suppose they cannot say anything if I decide to roast Frost like a pig on a spit.” An evil grin slowly grows on Dorn’s face as he continues, “Our day is coming, Lucius. Soon I’ll have this backsliding, miserable company by the balls. I’ll strangle the life right out of it, as I did to PCW.” Lucius Benson nods in agreement before turning back to the door. He turns his head to meet Dorn’s gaze out of the corner of his eye and speaks. “We can only hope…” With that, Lucius pulls the door of the therapy room open and steps through. He slowly shuts the door behind him before shaking his head and walking away. Fin.
