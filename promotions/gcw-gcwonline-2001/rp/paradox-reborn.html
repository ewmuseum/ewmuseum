Paradox Reborn

Date: 6/16/2205 ~ NOTE: This is just one of the many stories I wrote..I may start putting more of them up just so you guys can read.~ The sun was at high noon; heat was rising in waves like wraiths escaping the bonds of earth. Sounds of men hammering away at wood, orders being giving and whips cracking would reverberate through one's ears. Amongst those hard at work a young man, at the tender age of sixteen grunted pulling hard on the heavy rope to help lift sails as a whip cracked across his bared back. Pain lanced up his form a hiss all that was issued as the deck master bellowed in his ear ' FASTER DOG! THEY ARE COMING!' Again that whip lashed across the bulging flesh of his overworked back as he tightened his jaw and pulled harder with the rest of the slave that worked on the Spanish Galleon. Secretly he hoped that the ship bearing down on them caught them and killed all of the bastards who thought to treat he and his comrades like animals. ' Pirates bearing down on the port bow!' The Crow's Nest called out, his hand lifted and pointing urgently at the grey and purple ship that was sailing towards them. The sound of cannon fire filled the air, echoing as a whistling sound crowded the ears of all on the French ship. ' TO ARMS! MAKE READY THE CANNONS! RETURN FIRE!' The crew scrambled to their battle stations, yells grating on his nerves as the dreaded command came.' Take the slaves below deck! Lock them down!' A forceful hand shoved him along with the others, nearly pushing him to the floor with the weight of the shackles that encased his wrists. As he shuffled along with the others, dark eyes lifted to peer at the nightmarish ship that was bearing down on them. A cupped palm cuffed his head brutally, something snapping inside as he turned on the offender like a feral animal back in corner. Snarling he reared both fists locked together and punched the slaver in the face. Immediately those hands separated throwing upwards and crossing to lock his chains around that man's neck only to pull straight again. The muscles, well built from the months of hard labor bulged as he growled lowly choking the man to death. Pandemonium broke out on the ship then the first of a volley of twenty cannon balls blowing apart parts of the deck. Wood shrapnel scattered everywhere. The men and slaves alike were dropping like flies. The Devil's Nightmare came along side swinging about to allow the fearsome pirates to toss grappling lines over the banister of the La'Amour Da?Nast. The bellows of those mariners struck fear into many of the hearts of the French Slavers. Above it all a female voice could be heard ' No MERCY NO QUARTER! KILL EM ALL!' Bodies began to swing across, the sounds of muskets filled his ears as smoke stung his eyes. His guard was dead. Crouching low he searched the carcass for the keys whish would release his bonds. Soon they were procured, unsteady hands working feverently to unlatch the shackles. With a clank they dropped to the floor as the willful teenager bending over that body again to pull free the saber and musket. Skittishly he cast his gaze about spying one of the slavers barreling towards him in a berserk rage. Screaming his rage he twisted lifting his pistol and fired. At first he thought he missed the man still coming towards him, but soon, the Frenchman stopped wide eyed and fell over dead, a crimson stain spreading slowly across the center of his chest. Among those that boarded the ship was a Spanish sailor by the name of Rie Sinmor, a new mate of the Devil's Nightmare, and a loyal member of the Fallen Company. His face was twisted with rage as he cut down each person who came upon him. Blood began to flow across the slightly bowed deck in rivers of sanguine. Valiantly he thrust his cutlass into the gut of a man in front of him only to look over his shoulder to see another coming up behind him. A sharp elbow was brought up with his free hand as he savagely twisted his cutlass pulling it free to twirl it and turn with a downward slash cleaving the man in half at a diagonal line. He moved starboard bound slaying anything that stood in his way, following his captain's orders as he always did. Soon he found himself finishing off frantic slaves as they thought to fight the fate of death.... In the meantime Diego Dela'Luz was fighting for his life and a possibility of freedom. The two unaware of each other cut through their enemies with a vengeance. Soon French slavers surrounded them, the two of them backs facing each other swinging their swords and muskets killing anything that stood in their way. As the last of their enemies died the two came to press back to back. Whirling the pair lifted the swords swinging heavily, steel meeting steel only to freeze and stare dumbfounded. ' Deigo ?' Rie's low hoarse whisper would drift in the air. ' Rie?' Slowly both weapons were lowered as the two gazed each at the other in surprise mingled with relief. ' I thought you died....' ' As I did you.' At the helm of the ship the reigning Captain of the Devil's Nightmare cut down the last of the enemies, a cheer lifted into the air, as the Fallen crew celebrated their victory. Slowly her cold violet eyes scanned the damaged deck of the Da'Nast looking for more of the enemy to dispatch. Those amethyst gems fell on the sight of Rie and another slowly lowering their weapons as she bellowed' RIE KILL THAT MAN!' Rie heard the order and spun to face his captain hollering back' Begging your pardon Captain, but all enemies are dead! This man is my friend, thought dead.' Her gaze narrowed as it moved over to her first mate that stood stoically next to her and nodded in a gesture to move towards the strange young man. He nodded once and moved down the stairs without hesitation, she following until the two officers stood in front of Rie, her gaze never once leaving that of the Spaniard. ' Friend you say? From where? How do you know this man?' Rie glanced at the first mate, whose musket was drawn aimed at the blood-covered boy before turning his determined gaze back to that of his captain raising his chin. ' He was on the ship I was on over a year ago that was destroyed.' At last she looked to Rie and spoke once more ' You are speaking of when we found you adrift that barrel?' ' Aye Captain.' Pursing her lips, she nodded once and gestured he move aside as her fiery gaze fell on the individual again. Slowly she sized him up, that gaze traveling his form, taking in the varying wounds he had sustained during his fight. ' You seem handy enough with a sword. ' Her head canted back to Rie, though her gaze never left that of the captive's ' Tell me Rie can he be trusted? Is he loyal?' ' Aye, Captain. He saved my life and I his on many occasions. He is a worthy man to keep around.' Her look was one of deep study, those eyes seeming to delve into his soul as she practically purred ' Well then there you have it.' She fell silent as her mind drifted into pensive thought before speaking again... ' All the men on this ship are dead.... ALL of them. Understood? As such so are you. Now tell me your name...swab.' Diego refused to back down, his gaze locked with hers, that fierce spirit never wavering as he mulled over her words coming to understand what it was she had said without saying. ' My name is...Paradox.' At last a smile wafted upon deceivingly soft lips as the elven captain grinned and nodded ' Welcome aboard the Devil's Nightmare Paradox. As of this moment you are to take on the duties as my cabin boy. You will take orders from my officers and myself. Prove yourself loyal and one day will you be considered a member of this crew. Do you understand?' Her smile was infectious. He found himself smiling in return. ' Aye ...Captain, that I do.' 'Good. Now you and Rie are in charge of destroying this ship after my crew is finished with taking that which is usual or of value.' A crooked grin formed with a wry amusement. She had just told him to do what he had been wishing to do for months now. With a sharp salute he turned and moved with Rie to set the fuses which would destroy the La'Amour Da'Nast. As the two worked together, the rest finished their duties moving back to the Devil's Nightmare. Finally he and Rie joined them, the fuse lit and burning quickly. Standing at the banister of his new home he watched as the ship pulled away, waiting for the expected explosion. A thundering roar ripped through the air, as fire erupted on the dead ship. Wood exploded, lifting upwards in some surreal picture, a small mushroom cloud rising in fiery hues of red, orange, blue and yellow. He barely noticed the Captain as she came up beside him and looked at the handy work of her demolition crew. A soft hand lifted squeezing his shoulder as she murmured' Good work.' Nodding she turned to Rie who stood quietly next to him and ordered' Show him his quarters, give him food and drink and find some real clothing for him.' As she walked away Rie pounded his old comrade's back chuckling ' I think she likes you mate. You will do well here.... Welcome home.' Home. The sound was almost foreign to him and, yet, the sound of it brought a fierce pride to his breast. In the growing distance his prison sank and with it the name Diego Dela'Luz. Paradox was born...and he was home.
