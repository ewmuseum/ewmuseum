Date: December 24th, 1971 December 24th, 1971, a day that will in infamy. Belmont England, was colder than shit. The thermometer read “11 Celsius. The sky was dreary and gray. It’s almost like the sun never shines in this godforsaken town. That morning the children were huddled up in front of the television watching Christmas Cartoons, waiting another 24 hours where they can wake up and see what’s waiting for them under their Christmas Tree. Children go to the windows and look up and hope they can see that maybe Santa Claus was feeling eager and started his trip early. Like those before them, they were wrong, and like those before them all any can see is that house. That miserable, dilapidated, disgusting house up on the hill the children have labeled “boogey man hill.” Staring at that house would often send chills through the spines of all Belmont’s residents, young and old alike. Only that house can ruin the holiday spirits of its citizens. That day, although never proven by law, would forever haunt Belmont, and it’s long history of bloodshed, demonseed, and violence. Shall we begin? The O’Neil brothers, the town freaks walked into town to retrieve some groceries for their whore of a mother and their devil of a father. Most people stray away from these two, but only a select few, a select evil few that make it a goal to tease and bully these freaks. Bryan is 10 years old and stands over 6 feet tall, and his younger brother is 6 and is a runt. No one knows what the hell happen in their genetics, but whatever it was, it happened. Walking low, head down, caps covering their recognizable faces, they never expected what came next. Bryan hears a loud thud, than the scream of his brother. He looks up and see’s blood gushing from Jason’s ear, than notices the rock lying afoot. Lifting his head, looking due east, towards the laughter and see’s Tom Stafford, Jarred Johnson, and Willian Levering, town bullies, laughing hysterically. They are two years older than Bryan, and also just as sinister. Bryan’s eyes stare deep into them. Tom, the self proclaimed leader, stops laughing, hits Jarred in the arm than points back at Bryan. These two stare at each other, and that’s when William stops his laugh as well and joins in the stare down. “Freak, you just going to stare at me, or you going to bring your gorilla ass over here and take the ass beating that people have been waiting to do.” Bryan blinks, a bead of sweat falls from his brow, and looks back down at his little brother. The blood flow stopped, but has begun to cake in Jason’s brown hair. It’s begun to dry in the cartilage induced folds in his ear. The sobs have stopped, but the tears were still flowing down his cheeks. The crying has made his cheeks rosy red, and his nose resembled a certain seasonal reindeer. “You ok?” Sobbing, Jason nods, and grabs Bryan’s coat sleeve. “Let’s just go Bryan.” Confused, and angered Bryan questions his younger brother’s decision as the taunts from across the street continue to flood in like a tsunami. “Bryan if you go over there, they will beat me up when school starts. Let’s just go.” Bryan puts his hands into Jason’s armpits and pulls him to his feet. He brushes the salt from the sidewalk that stuck to his knees and they continue their trek for perishables. “You fucking pussy O’Neil!” Bryan puts his arm around the shoulder of his younger brother and looks back over at the taunts. Tom has his arms out and motions the come here sign with his hands. Bryan decides it’s not worth it, not yet atleast. “Hey O’Neil, the little fuck! Ever figure out who your father is” My older brother claimed he fucked your tramp , whore of a cunt you call a mother. So that makes us brothers, so come over here and let me teach you some brotherly love? Tom grabs his crotch than spits on the ground. His lackeys follow suit, they follow suit with everything their fearless leader does. As hard as it was the two ignored the taunts and turned a hard left and walked into the grocery store. Jason knew they were safe because Tom and Jarred are not allowed into the grocery store because they were caught shoplifting beer and cigarettes, and they were told if they were caught they parents would be told. Tom’s father is in the House of Commons, and Jarred’s father is the sheriff. “Bryan, promise you won’t touch them. Their father’s are powerful, and they will beat me up in school.” Clenching his teeth and fists, “Okay, you already told me. But they will get there’s I promise.” Jason shakes his head. “Please Bryan, don’t, for me. I know you want them dead, but it’s my head, not yours.” Bryan grabs Jason by his shoulders, shakes him and brings him inches from his face. Jason’s toes are hanging 7 inches from the floor. “Than learn how to fucking defend yourself, and stop being a fucking pussy.” He puts Jason back down, rustles his hair with his fingers and turns back towards the eggs and cheese. That’s Bryan’s way of apologizing. He never ever said it, and will spend the rest of his life failing to do so, even if it was his fault. They spent the rest of the escapade in the grocery store in silence. They didn’t even ask what kind of cereal each other wanted. They would look at the all the shoppers rustling around trying to get the last minute fixings for their big Christmas dinner. The O’Neils spent Christmas like any other day. Jason would lock himself in his room doing puzzles and other sort of brainteasers while Bryan would be out in the yard torturing the animals or testing his strength by splitting wood and baling hay. They paid for their groceries, Bryan carried all the bags while his brother lead the way out the door. When he opened the door he was greeted by a fist to his face. He saw a flash, than felt the crunch of his nose, followed by the fierce flow of blood. Bryan hesitated, not really comprehending what exactly happened. Like most bullies they hit Jason, than ran off talking all sorts of shit. Bryan saw his brother lying on the cement, whilst a crowd of people began to flock towards him to check his status. The rage brewed from the pit of Bryan’s stomach and reached his eyes?..he saw blood, he saw rage?..he saw Mayhem. To be continued.
