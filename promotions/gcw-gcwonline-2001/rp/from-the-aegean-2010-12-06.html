From The Aegean...

6 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/06/10 *The camera focuses in on a poorly lit room, a room with deep royal red curtains draping the walls. A woman dressed in a rather revealing chiton is standing in front of a behemoth silhouette only highlighted by the dim light above,the monster faces away from the camera looking down to his right shoulder letting his bleached hair hide his eyesight. The woman looks into the camera with a sinister gaze and smile. The light catches her platinum blond hair as well as the edge of her earrings that shimmer throughout room. The mammoth standing to her back breathes steadily each inhale lifting his monstrous back higher, each exhale lowering it below his massive trapezius muscles that seem to engulf his neck.* Alexia: "It seems we have found a place decent enough to build my Behemoth's legacy. A place which offers not only his competition, but my prize, my gold." *Alexia turns her head seductively to her left looking behind her at the menacing figure which stands at her back. She closes her eyes and turns back to the camera only to open them again with a sinister stare.* Alexia: "The age of Xanthus is upon Global Championship Wrestling. The Golden Colossus will emerge from the Aegean to mark his legacy on not only the world of professional wrestling, but on the face of the Earth." *Her smirk grows into slight laughter, steadying herself she looks toward her feet, she quickly snaps her sight back to the camera in a serious manner.* Alexia: "You will not know when his presence will be made, but you can trust me when I say you have never.....Never seen the carnage that my Colossus will unleash upon his arrival. You will fall into a state of frozen shock as if you locked eyes with the gorgon Medusa..." *She reaches her hands up to grasp the back of Brecca's shoulders.* Alexia: "The gods have blessed us with our moment, a moment which is shortly at hand, the moment that you gaze upon a higher being than yourself, a Demi-God, Brecca Xanthus........." *The camera slowly backs away and fades to darkness*
