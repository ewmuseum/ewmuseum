Operation: FOX

Date: 10/11/2009 ??/??/???? KAAAAA! ERRRRRRRRR! ? “Oh no! They’re here!” The man said as he jerked his head around, doing his best owl imitation, “QUICK! TAKE IT! TAKE IT AND BE FREE! NOT JUST FOR US, BUT FOR THE REST OF THE WORLD! I believe you can do it, friend.” The buzzing of helicopters and Special Force vehicles created a surreal panic. The man’s forehead dripped sweat nonstop as a spotlight peaked through the open rooftop of the warehouse. ? “You can count on me!” The shadowy figure squeaked, grabbing the item. The situation didn’t call for a heartfelt goodbye as troopers were surely closing in on foot. The man in the shadows looked back at the other and gave a simple salute before carrying onward. CLICK, CLACK, THUMP, CLICK, CLACK, THUMP, CLICK, CLACK THUMP! The array of firearm bolts locking into place accompanied the dense pummeling of the grassy field beneath the soldier’s feet. They were going to kill ‘em. They didn’t even care. This wasn’t one of those heroic scenes in the movies where the S.W.A.T. team converges on the target only to humanely deescalate the situation. It was going to be done. The limit had already been reached and every soldier, especially the commanders, were itching for some action. A happy trigger finger would be applied as an understatement. BA-BUMP! The Delta squad leader slammed into the aluminum siding of the building, not overlooking any tactical aspect. He then signaled his troops in through the wide-open front doors. The fodder. The Tango and Bravo squads crashed through the windows. The fear and energy flowed through their eyes. The infantry smelt of young, scared blood. Untamed and yet untapped. Too pure to know what would happen in a real battle situation. The attackers. Lastly, Alpha squad rappelled from high atop the helicopter, onto the small ledge sidings that covered the roof. Collectively, they held confident, stoic glares. One by one they swooped downward and into the far back of the warehouse. Their helicopters were their horses. The cavalry. Together they stormed the lone man. No warnings, no direction, no silence. They all screamed as the man turned and saw them running straight for him. He noticed their malicious, possessed looks. Their face paint seemed to jump right from their flesh with their widened their snarls. The unarmed man stood against an army of many. BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- Violently, the guns pulsated as empty bullet shells flew around and clanked against the ground. The army closed in on the man. Like a ragdoll having pins shoved through it, the man jostled and shook as many bullets seared right through him. Steam shot out from the piercingly hot gunshot wounds. With his body filled with lead, the man dropped to the floor, dead. Some men stopped at the arrival of his dead body, but some kept going in search of the last man. Their words were clear to each other but the resonating noise from the guns would have deafened anyone without ear protection in a five-mile radius. The actions of the commanders were understood as they yelled and pointed down the warehouse, sending troops forward. Suddenly. ZWARP! SWOOOOOOOOOOOOOSH! A massive white light cascaded through the warehouse. Whatever the man had, and whatever he could have activated; it was done. The final man was gone with nothing but a flash of white light… --- BOLD MEDIA PRESENTS A CLYDE FOX FILM DEVELOPED IN ASSOCIATION WITH PARAMOUNT PICTURES CLYDE FOX CRAYDEN RAGNER SUZY YATES CANDACE FULMONT ONJIN WIZNAASKI KYLE BOLD YAROSLAV BORIS KEANU REEVES as LORD TRANSCEPTICORE WITH RYAN REYNOLDS as voice of CYRUS THE ISUZU GIGA AND INTRODUCING FEATHERTON CLOWL THE OWL OPERATION: FOX 10/11/2009 RING! RING! RAAAAAA-RING! RING! The Mickey Mouse clock rang as a picture of the famed mouse had his little hand pointed at the eight, and his big hand pointed at the twelve. Clyde rolled over in his bed, clearly not wanting to get up. Suddenly, a large paw protruded from the entanglement otherwise known as his bed. With a quick slap, the clock got knocked off the nightstand and shut off. IF YOU WANA BE MY LOVER… If it wasn’t bad enough that Mickey Mouse tried to wake Clyde up, think again. This time it was the Spice Girls as their hit single “Wannabe” emanated from Clyde’s Gizmondo. It is indeed his preferred ringtone. The urgency was more profound as Clyde knew he couldn’t stand missing a phone call, even if he had to answer it half asleep. Once more, a paw protruded from the mess of a bed and snatched the Gizmondo. CLICK! FOX “Herrow,” Clyde muffled. PHONE “CLYDE!? Clyde… ? It’s me Crayden. Are you awake? HELLO? CLYDE! WAKE UP!” A familiar voice pounded out of the phone’s speaker at a high volume. Fox pulled the phone away from his head in irritation, as he was still able to hear his longtime friend. RAGNER “Just groan once if you can hear me and groan three times if you can’t. Don’t bother groaning twice, it’s too hard to decipher your groans and double groans,” Ragner directed. GROAN! RAGNER “Okay I only heard one groan I think. Can you confirm that I heard one groan with either groaning three times to confirm or once to disconfirm? I’m switching it up just to make sure we don’t get confused here Clyde,” Ragner directed once more. GROAN! GROAN! GROAN! RAGNER “That was three! Excellent, you can hear me. Hey listen buddy, I know it’s early on a Sunday morning for you but man, I gotta tell you, I have some huge news that you won’t believe! I mean for reals dawg! I’m even more serious than the time that I told you that I met that guy and had a pick up home run derby with that I beat at Coors field in Colorado,” Ragner ranted. FOX “Jose Canseco?” Fox questioned, still half asleep. RAGNER “Yeah! That’s the guy! Anyways, hey man… get some clothes on and get down here fast dude! This thing is for real!” The exuberant Ragner insisted. CLICK! And with that, the line disconnected. Clyde moved to his front, still with Gizmondo in hand. He wondered just what the heck could Crayden be talking about? Oh em gee. I should not be awake at this hour. I will never get any rest as long as that guy is around. He gets me drunk, keeps me up late, wakes me up early, and tells me the stupidest stories. I wouldn’t be surprised if one day, he ends up killing me! Why am I even friends with him? Oh wait. He got me into GCW, that’s why. Ell oh ell. Aye. Time to get up and see what bacon he’s cooking this time. Probably just a squirrel lodged in his eaves trough like last time. --- KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! Clyde stood outside of Crayden’s house, wrenching his neck as he slept funny on it. A muffled schlep is heard as someone walks up to the door. ? “Who’s there?” The man asked. FOX “It’s me, Clyde,” The Prince of the Playroom responded. Clyde had enough time to look up at the rest of the house. It was probably the most vertical house he’d ever seen. Two and a half stories… if there even is such a thing. White panels held it all together. Clyde always did admire the wooden finish on both the front and garage doors. ? “Clyde who?” The man clearly joked. Clyde huffed, as he didn’t seem up to his usual antics. FOX “Clyde, I wana come into your fudging house now Crayden!” Fox spat out. ? “You’re no fun,” The man sourly spoke as the doorknob turned. The door swung wide open revealing none other than Clyde’s friend Crayden. He stood on his ceramic tiled floor barefoot and in shorts. With a welcoming smile, Crayden brought Clyde in. RAGNER “Come on in my friend. Boy, do I have something to show you! It’ll knock your socks off son!” Ragner remarked. FOX “I can see it already did to yours,” Fox smirked. That typical sly smile cracked across Crayden’s face. He knew the fun-loving kid was shinning deep down in there somewhere. Clyde followed his fellow comrade down into his unfinished basement where the usual antics happen. The two waltzed over to a table cluttered with beer bottles, papers, mail, and food containers. RAGNER “Okay. Take a look at this!” Crayden said as he dug into the pile. Fox waited patiently as Ragner pulled out a FedEx Ground envelope. One of the big kinds that has that stripy thing that looks cool especially when you open it. RAGNER “TAAAA DAAAA!” Crayden showed off. Clyde’s eyes dart over to Crayden, unimpressed. FOX “An envelope. You got me out of bed, counting sheep, for this!?” Fox complained. RAGNER “No, no, no, no, no! Don’t you see!? First off, it’s just not any envelope. It’s from FedEx. Secondly, look at who sent this dude!” Ragner implied as he handed the parcel to his friend. It read… ----------------------------------------- | To: Ragner, Crayden | From: Fox, Clyde | (222 Portal District) | ------- | FedEx Ground © 2074 |__________________________ Clyde nearly bounced off the floor after noticing the obvious on the package. FOX “Double you tee eff mate!” Fox gasped, “It’s a piece a mail! What the heck about it!?” Crayden paused and took an intentional double take at the folder and then to Clyde and back again. RAGNER “You seriously don’t notice anything odd eh?” Ragner inquired. Playing it cool, Clyde tossed the mail back down to the table and looked Crayden square in the face. FOX “It’s some mail I sent you. Although I never send you mail through FedEx, it’s too expensive because I’m cheap. I send you mail through LetsHopeItDon’tBreak Corp,” Clyde concluded. RAGNER “EXACTLY! WAIT! NO! Don’t you see? You never send me mail! I got it this morning and I was flabbergasted. I opened it up and some cyber-kinetic video thingy played right in front of me… it was like a total rip off of those hollow tech conversations in Star Wars!” He rambled, “It was this grizzled old dude who talked to me about the world and stuff… kinda poetic but more hippie like. He knew things about me, and everything… and he claimed he was like, totally you man! Dude… THIS CAME FROM THE FUTURE!” Fox’s head nearly turned itself inside out at the stuff Crayden spewed. With his heart nearing his esophagus, Clyde tried to gather himself. FOX “ZOH MY GOSH! This is horrible!” He cried, “I use FedEx in the future!?” FedEx!? Really? I mean sure, they’re America’s leading brand in delivering goods around the globe, but I of all the people to trust to send my stuff! Especially back to the past? That was a big gamble on my part. Oh wait… THE FUTURE? But how? No way! I don’t believe that dribble drabble. How can something from the past be here? Crayden must have something up his shorts. The two stood there. Silent. Crayden looked over at his bud in hopes of him saying something. Finally, his prayer would be answered. FOX “Crayden,” Fox started, “I need to be super serious with you right now. No jokesies. Realsies. Are you number two-ing me?” RAGNER “No way friend. I’m being real man. That’s why I had to get you over here as quick as humanly possible! I don’t know if some droids from the future are being sent through time to come kill us man. I need your help,” He spoke. FOX “Well I guess the only thing we can do is watch the video again,” He dared. Crayden nodded as Clyde picked the parcel back up and opened it. BANG! ZAP! ZOOM! TAMBARINE! With a mystical flash, the basement is illuminated in a lucent blue shade. Indeed, an old grizzled man appears, about the size of a traditional action figure. OLD MAN “Greetings. Zamba. Musha-milla,” The man spoke in different languages. FOX “How much did you watch?” Fox whispered. RAGNER “Not a lot man. As soon as he claimed he was you I got frightened, turned it off and called you,” He whispered back. OLD MAN “My name is Fox. I’m creating this message for one, Crayden Ragner in the year two thousand nine. The date here is the fourth of January, twenty seventy-four. I seemingly haven’t much time,” The voice progressed, “The government has initialized a pre-death analysis program that accurately identifies the time of ones demise. I’ve heard lots of things about this program… that’s it’s a sham, a fake, a lie. The government wants to control its population. It wants to exterminate the weak, the old, the poor.” The old man went on… OLD FOX “Well I for one will not be so easily undone. My P-D-A is scheduled for sometime near the end of June. I’m making this video with enough time to save up my funds and send it through an underground time portal posed as a FedEx package. You see, I strongly believe that the government has gone mad with power. I am therefore contacting you to change the future by changing events both in the past and future. Two thousand nine is the channel of which to operate out of. The government is in its most elevated state of peace. Clyde and Crayden lock eyes momentarily… OLD FOX “The past of wars, dictators, and evil doers are over by this time. That, and I need a rather spry, young self to do this with. It is here that I need the assembly of the greatest time traveling team of all time for the greatest time traveling adventure of all time! Ha ha… I said time a lot in that sentence. Along with this package is a list of very important events that you will have to travel to. I believe the changing of these events will not only bring hope to the future, but save both the world and my life.” He takes one final breath before finishing his message. OLD FOX “Crayden, I trust you the most with this parcel in two thousand nine. I’m shipping it with FedEx because they are super rad. That, and I know Clyde is too cheap to ship with FedEx. They are coming for me. It’s only a matter of time before the government tracks me down and kills me. Avenge me, my fellow past people dudes. You will see… everything will be clear soon,” His voice echoed as the video faded away. Silence. After all that jibber jabber there was nothing but silence between the two. Could it be confusion? Disorientation? Or just pure disbelief? Crayden looked around his basement with eyes, trying to focus, while Clyde stood there completely blank. FOX “…” FOX “Yeah,” He started, “And the clouds rain skittles,” He laughed. Ragner took a second but eventually chuckled at the lighthearted joke. RAGNER “Yeah… yeah… and there’s a full set of instructions and things to do and people to add to our awesome, sick, rad time traveling team in that envelope,” Ragner said with a ton of sarcasm. Clyde giggled as he shook the envelope and out flew a page with step-by-step directions. FOX & RAGNER “Sh*t!” To be continued…
