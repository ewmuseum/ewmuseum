What The Hell Just Happened?!!

28 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/28/11 The air is still. The world is in awe. The monkeys continue flinging their feces, but that's life. In an exclusive GCWONLINE.NET interview, Kevin Jacobs catches up with "Your Friendly Neighborhood Klepto" mere minutes after his tag team match. Toga is holding something that should never have graced his fingertips... but alas, the stars aligned, and Fate shat all over the legacy this "precious stone" once had. Toga's eyes are beaming brighter than a child's on Christmas morning with a pony in the living room eating the family tree, while his manager, Lollie's, eyes are twinkling in much the same way. Toga's little yellow backpack full of goodies rests on Lollie's shoulders, as Toga was WAAAY too occupied with his new treasure to remember to grab his bag before leaving ringside. Tommy's focus is glued to the shiny piece of metal in his hands, as Kevin approaches him. Jacobs: "Toga, this is truly a night of firsts for you, isn't it?" Twinkle, twinkle little title... how I wonder how the hell you ended in in Toga's sweaty hands!... is probably the thoughts passing through Toga's and/or Lollie's minds. The skirt-clad CHAMPION doesn't even notice GCW's veteran interviewer before him. Lollie steps up. Lollie: "Kevin, yes it truly was. I mean, this right here is Toga's first actual GCW interview. That was Toga's first tag team match in GCW. His first championship match. His first match inside of a GCW ring for gawd's sake! And as you can see by his trance... his first championship win................ EVER!" Kevin glances past Lollie to Toga to see if he's reaction has changed. It has.... there's a little bit more drool than two seconds ago... He looks back to Lollie. Jacobs: "Most people have no idea what to think of your client, Toga. To say that he's a wild card, would be an understatement, I believe. But I do know that the general consensus in the GCW locker room is, and I don't mean to be rude, but... He's a delusional, mentally-challenged, unfocused, nonthreatening wimp. I don't know of a single member of the locker room that ever expected him to ever even pick up a single win... But look at him now!" The gal in green glances back to him and smiles sincerely. She stares at him, as he drools on the title, and sees something that the rest of the world has never and possibly will never see in him...... and I have NO idea what it is she sees, so don't ask. She reluctantly pulls her focus back to Kevin. Lollie: "Tommy is a very skilled wrestler.... I'm pretty sure I just heard cackling coming from down the hall as I said that, but it's true. He's just got two huge obstacles that he must overcome in each match, before he can show it... his size... and his attention-span. To say that he's a fun-loving guy would be an understatement. He loves wrestling, but he sometimes has a hard time wrapping his mind around the fact that it's not play-time in the ring. It's hurt-time." The GCW Head Interviewer nods, as he once again glances to the tranced Toga behind Lollie. Jacobs: "Due to this huge and completely shocking win tonight, on one of GCW's biggest shows ever, here in the Garden, Toga is now set up to participate in the biggest event of the year, NC-17! I hope I don't have to tell you how extremely challenging it will be for your client...." Lollie: "You're right. You don't have to tell me. I know that Tommy is going to get broken at NC-17. There is no doubt in my mind about that..." Kevin looks a bit surprised to hear a manager, seemingly admit that their client will lose. Jacobs: "Are you saying that you don't think Toga can walk away successful once again? Are you admitting that tonight was a fluke?" "Your Friendly Neighborhood Klepto" suddenly licks the title.... the slurping sound catches Jacobs' and Lollie's attention. They both look at him. He starts to make "ucky" faces as he tries to scrape the taste off his tongue with his sweaty and probably more disgusting fingers. Lollie sighs, and turns back to Kevin. Lollie: "No. I am not saying that he's going to lose. Nor am I ever going to admit that any of his wins are a fluke. Like I said, he's very talented. No matter how lucky his wins may be, his talent helps bring him into a position where sometimes, luck can make all the difference. At NC-17, I promise you that he will be ready for the challenge ahead of him. And I don't care whether it's a "fluke" or if it's just plain luck, or even if it's pure skill... Toga will retain his new GCW HARDCORE CHAMPIONSHIP! Because he is a once-in-a-lifetime superstar, and no matter what happens, good or bad, his unorthodox talent is going to overwhelm anyone that comes in contact with it!" Lollie smiles, as she steps back to Toga and wraps her arm around him. He glances up and notices Kevin Jacobs for the first time. Tommy looks to Lollie, then to the title, then back to Jacobs. Toga: "..... who the bananas are you?!" The gal in green giggles a bit, as she pulls Toga away. Lollie: "I'll fill you in later. For now... let's do something we rarely get to do because of your wrestling accomplishments...." There's a moment of silence as Toga and Lollie start to walk away from the camera. Toga: ".... go ball-pit fishing?" Lollie: "Oh hun...... we do that every night regardless!" Toga: "Sweetness!" The moment has passed and so have "Your Friendly Neighborhood HARDCORE CHAMPION" and his entourage. They slink away, as the camera fades out.
