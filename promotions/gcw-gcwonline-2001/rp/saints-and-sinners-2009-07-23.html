Saints and Sinners

23 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/23/2009 Saints… Who was really a Saint? Was the infamous Archangel Michael a Saint? What did he do that was so important? He is the most well-known Archangel and has led the charge to the battles against Satan. He is known as the protectors of human virtues and humanity as a whole. He is a great warrior. His triumphs are unseen by any man but are told through the passages of light. He was severely underestimated in his battles with Satan, but yet came out on top. A victor, a victory for all that is good. His conquest of the darkest things is what protects us in the night from the shadows. Shadows… We all have our shadows to conquer. We have all our demons to fight. We need to overcome our opposition for the good of mankind or we are doomed. History is doomed to repeat itself in one way or another. World War 1…World War 2…Korean War….Vietnam War, all these are the same thing over and over again, the same countries fighting for their same causes. All the things that they endure: fatigue, famine, death, this is what our world has come to. I am going to change it all. It will all come unraveling soon enough. The man behind the curtain will not ignore my call. The man behind the mirror will not reject me from the glory that I deserve. He will not send me back home; I will not become another pawn in his game. I am the great and terrible. My journey has come long and far, I will not rest, I will not stop until each and every person has witnessed my revival. To usurp me is the ultimate sin. I will become more powerful than anyone has before me. Envy… Victory will be had by me, against all the purity that the world has to offer. Against every single person who dares oppose me, the good, the bad, the up and down, I will overcome, no, I shall overcome. I am a warrior, a champion, the blood rushes through my veins. I am going to go in there and give it my all. I have to fight. Fight for me, fight for my life, fight for those who are close to me. I will overcome adversity and I will stand at the top of the mountain and scream my name for all to hear. Damn them all… * * * Crisp wounds cover my body as I lay upon my bed. I couldn’t help but think about the past fights I have had. All these scars tell a story, from my chin to my stomach, they all tell a story about my life, my experiences; it was the way I was brought up. I could not get rid of these scars even if I wanted to, they were like a memory scorned within each rip of the flesh. My eyes close, I want to drift, I want to drift off into sleep, just dream another dram, wish another wish, and I knew they would never come true, but I had to do something to alleviate this pain. Sloth… It wasn’t the physical pain that bothered me so much; it was the emotional pain that I had to endure. With life comes lessons and it is those lessons that so happen to shape and mold you as a person. Each ‘lesson’ across my temporarily fragile body tells a story that has a lesson…a forewarning, to learn from those mistakes and carry on your journey. No Remorse… I had no remorse for what I have done or will do, these scars may tell a story, but that doesn’t mean I regret ever getting them. They are like a tattoo on a person’s body, they show what your personality is, what kind of person are you, how you affect the people around you. My scars are my journey, my aforementioned story of my life. KNOCK KNOCK Once again I am interrupted in my solitude. Yet I knew it was to be expected. She didn’t like to be alone for long periods of time. That comfort in a companion seemed really important to her. I cared, though, but not as much as her. “Déjà-Vu?” she asks me as she swings open the door. She looks at me and smirks. She realizes I have been in the bed most of the day, not wanting to move. She knew me so well. “What?” I gazed at her with an obviously sarcastic look on my face. “You know what I am talking about, Mr. Quinton. You did this a few days ago, you are lucky that I am here to kick your ass out of bed. You sulk in here day after day whether you win or lose your little slap-fight wrestling matches or whatever. Maybe you shouldn’t be travelling all those days just to impress an audience of be on T.V. I am starting to think why I ever left London. This place is so…poor.” Lucy was a very direct person. She always had a response to every single question you may have. She could be a bitch most of the time, but she sticks around because I am as much as a bastard as she is and she seems to like it. “Have fun in England…” I laugh like a mad man as she stand there shaking her head. “You aren’t going to get rid of me that easy, Lucas.” She walks over to the and surprisingly pushes me off the bed. I laugh some more as I get back to my feet. She didn’t look like she was in the mood for any of my games, yet she couldn’t resist laughing at my expense. I walk towards her and try to hug her, to tell her that everything is alright, that I will be her hero once again. She wanted no part of it and pushes me back onto the bed. This was going to be a long day. * * * I hate Connecticut weather. Every day in which we get sunshine, it is just another three days of rain. Who ever heard of 4 seasons? I would probably enjoy it more in California, but I jest, this place isn’t all bad, that is if you can deal with the whiny and moaning of people day in and day, but it could be tuned out rather easily. Her arms are interlocked in mine and she put her heads on my shoulder as we walk down the street in our gated community. Her eyes are closed as she gives a warm smile. I was her seeing-eye dog right now, but I didn’t care, the day was nice, rather cloudy, but I would manage. I would always manage. “Take me somewhere nice, Lucas.” “What do you mean?” “I want to go somewhere nice. This place isn’t as good as Sweden or Europe. I want to travel, meet new people, and have new experiences. I can’t be stuck here all day every day. We should just forget all our previous arrangements and just take the next flight across the world. That would make me very happy, Lucas.” “You have more financial resources than I to do that, I don’t see what the big deal is. Years ago we would used to go to a different country almost every other week. We have basically seen and done it all. I don’t know why you can’t just stay in one place for a few months and take a breather. I actually am starting to like this little town. It has grown on me.” “Don’t be so daft. This place is horrid, you know it and I know it. I put up with this place for too long, it is giving me the chills. I try going to the market like the Americans, but they are all rude and prudish, they stare at me like I am mad or something and I become the villainess.” “You need to calm down. They aren’t all that bad. Not all of them are obese beer-swelling slobs you make them all out to be. You should open your eyes.” I didn’t understand her deep hatred for this place. It seemed so out of character for her, sure she was a bitch some times, but she would come around and appreciate the aesthetics for a place such as this. She takes her arm off of mine and gives a big sigh. She starts walking faster than me; she keeps on staring back as I chase after her. I didn’t have patience for those games that she played. I take long strides as to keep up with her, but it doesn’t work, her legs moved quicker than mine, left then right, in rapid succession. When I catch up to her…. She slows down to a crawl and I finally catch up to her, out of breathe. She gives me a stern look, then chuckles like it’s the funniest thing she has ever seen. “Well there, old man, you got to be quicker on your feet if you want to catch me.” “Aren’t you only a year younger than me?” She slaps me in the face teasingly. All the abuse I take from her. Lust… “Shut up, Lucas.” She takes me by the head and kisses me. I try and breathe as she sucks the life out of me like a succubus. She gives me back my set of lips. I check them to see if I actually still have them. “Don’t be such a baby.” She laughs once more as she skips down the road. “You do all this talking and rambling like the world is going to end or something. I hear you at night, your little fantasies about how you are the savior of mankind. You act as if you are some kind of saint, some sort of force of good. You are neither a savior nor saint. You are like me, Lucas. You sit there and shove your face full of money and expensive food, not giving a damn what people in 3rd world countries are doing. Do you listen to yourself? You need to grow up and become the man that you used to be. You are a sinner.” Gluttony… * * * I had dreamed of day in which we could all be free. Free of the society that has put us to work, make us pay them every single cent we own, make us work endlessly to meet demands that have no business with us. I dreamed of a day when man can truly be free; a glorious day. Hope… Hope is what drives us towards our goals; to fight the injustices that have plagues us day after day. We are given a choice in which to lead our lives, but all we do is cower back in our holes and wait for somebody to take control for us. No, no, we aren’t free, we are never free. We are the slaves to ourselves, and we as humans, the rulers of this planet, have no desire to break free of these reigns of madness. Greed… All the greed in the corporations of the world who care nothing about others, but all they want to do is horde and horde all the resources they can and sell it to people for an outrageous profit. These are the mad-men; they are the ones drunk with power. Second chances aren’t given in this one life we have. We have the option to go down the different paths in life, to reach out and touch somebody, hoping they will become something better, but that’s not how things turn out in the end. We need to fight to survive, fight to protect ourselves and our pack. The right to fight to keep sane in this world; are we savages? The things we do will conflict with the morals that society had put forth into us. Change… We can’t change who are or where we come from. We can’t change what we have done; there is not a way to go into the past and fix it all, the mistakes we make are the ones that live for us forever until we die. In the end we are just human. In the end, we are all just… Sinners…
