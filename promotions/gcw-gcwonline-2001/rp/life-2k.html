Date: 12/29/99 12 Years Old The Year 2000 / Ice Age 11:00 pm The wind had its certain way of whistling during those finger-numbing winter nights. It was no fair how we had to live in our igloos atop the world while others could walk around in next to nothing depending on how close they were to the equator. I was determined to do what I loved to do during the summer months of my youth; star gaze. Forget the fact that it was freezing out, I was determined to see the stars. I grew some sort of bond with them. It was like I could look up and be reassured that everything was going to be just fine. I was out on my house’s back porch on December 29th, 1999. Two days before the alleged apocalypse. My eyes twinkled scandalously in the moonlight. I was the galaxies whore, letting each star shine through me. “Clyde,” My Mom sparked, “Come inside kiddo, it’s too cold to be out there tonight.” She hastily closed the back sliding door, as it was rather frosty. My nipples were hard enough to cut through fifteen layers of aluminum-alloy-platinum-steel-ice. Now that’s razor sharp. I remember not being too fond of my parent’s advice as I neared teenage hood. My natural rebellious tendencies were forming faster than the hot spicy rod I got from watching a Madonna music video back then. I pulled my fur-rimmed hood over my head, leaned back against the railing and turned skyward. I closed my eyes and reflected on the past and how it was all seemingly going to change in a few short days. -- 130,000 Years ago? The following contains subtitles for the Neanderish impaired. “BUUUUUUUURAMAG! Roamf, roamf, ramo!” A gutsy growl rumbled. “ATTENTION! Let’s move! Move! Move!” The clouds covered the massive ice sheath that held five humanoids on it. They all looked like they were wearing thick wooly bodysuits, when in reality it was their natural coating. These things weren’t like any other animal seen before it. In fact, you couldn’t be certain they were animals at all. They stood upright. They walked on their hind legs. And yes? it was a temperature that hadn’t been seen in over twenty thousand years. We’ve returned to the ice age. The biggest one out of the five kept repeating that same directive in order to motivate his troops. There they all stood, hacking away at a huge block of ice. It was not unusual to them that there were snow flurries whisking around. Nor the fact that the wind whistled even louder than in the present. These humanoids were iron men. Not just any iron men; Neanderthal iron men. GONG! A loud, earth shattering gong noise rings throughout the land. It was quittin? time at the local ice mover branch. The crew of five stopped what they were doing and walked off the sheath and onto and much larger ice plain. Dofenhus Yim Seltner wiped at his brow as Claudermauk Wyde Willotoske took a seat beside his comrade. “Mucha mucha, mei sei foo tungo mamo Doffy?” Claudermauk asked. “So what do you have planned tonight Doffy?” Doffy naturally dislocated his leg in order to slurp the ice shards from under his toenails before replying. “Mucha, qet wet nife. Sex tin stun hiya hiya shavre doper,” Doffy replied. “Not much, just a quiet night in with the wife. These sixteen stone shifts are starting to kill me. I’m looking forward to my next shaving though” should give me more mobility.” Claudy sat there scratching his backside as drool escaped from his oversized jaw. Just then, Mopey Dyck Tuney jumped down into the ice shelter after another hard shift. “Gamey gamey sam guff. Utoolitech stem. Ate meam stun fry fry masha!” He gargled. “These guys are slave drivers. Not anywhere near the quality of what we had with the last management team. I work eighteen stones solid and I’m still having trouble putting fried Masha on the table!” “Manja!” Claudy and Doffy replied. “Yeah!” They certainly weren’t the spryest hunters in the pack but they knew when the man was pushing them around. No Neanderthal ever likes to see another Neanderthal get shaved in the ass, and this pack was no different. Claudy was your typical dumbass. Not too bright but when it came to smart remarks, he was the best at grunting them out. Last semestial, he won the Ice Grunter award for his tribe. Doffy was a more docile individual who put his family first. His uncle was the great Goffy, the Neanderthal who founded the ice movers. And then there was Mopey. He was the most outlandish character who most say that many sitcoms of the 90s ripped off. Any character such as Kramer, Al Bundy, Homer Simpson and even Elmo were theoretically based off Mopey. They had been friends for over six semestials. That’s quite a feat seeing that most ice movers get killed by flagrant ice chunks during their third semestial together. Indeed, these were pro ice movers. That’s why they got paid the big stones. Life was reasonably good in these times and parts. The ice age was in full swing but there had been rumors that the devil’s eye was going to appear one day and melt all the ice. This would destroy the life that everyone knew. The ice movers would no longer be needed to chop, destroy and move threatening ice chunks close to their villages. If the devil’s eye had its way and melted all the ice, every Neanderthal would sweat to death due to the heavy coat of fur each has garnered. Little did they know, but these three co-workers were the key to the survival’err? genetic adaptation that humans would evolve from. GONG! The second gong signaled the start of the second shift as many Neanderthals crossed paths. Claudy grunted goodbye to Doffy as Mopey pulled out his Blackberry Sundial. “Yacka yacka, cheq mech!” Mopey commented. “I have to check my IceScribe page to see my friends status updates!” IceScribe is what Facebook is to us humans. It was a very popular application back in the ice age but gradually lost over popularity over centuries due to its inability to have any useful privacy settings. Plus it was the end when Romeo updated his IceScribe status to “In a relationship with Juliet Capulet” which led to shit hitting the proverbial fan. That and it was only compatible with Mac Sundials. A sudden shutter overcame Claudy while Mopey was fooling around with his primitive toy. Suddenly a floating blue sphere rose from the ground and spoke directly to Claudy. “Claudy. You hold the key to the universe,” It spoke in a cold and echoed voice,” You might just think that you’re a simple ice mover but the truth is that you hold the key to the universe. You, Claudy, cannot begin to fathom what you hold’the key to the universe!” Claudy grunted as he tried to shoo away the awful floating sphere of destiny. Suddenly the sphere flew towards him and starting licking his face. “The devil’s eye will rise and melt everything! But you! You will be spared” after all if I didn’t make it clear enough; YOU HOLD THE KEY TO THE UNIVERSE!” The sphere licked away. -- 3? 2? 1? HAPPY NEW YEAR! I remember hearing the loudest cheers of all blaring throughout the house. I quickly snapped my eyes open. My dog was licking my half frozen face. As it turned out, I accidently fell off the porch and hit my head on some ice. I hit it so hard that I was out for a few days? missing the century turn. “What a weird dream,” I remember saying. I looked up at the stars and saw one twinkle. It couldn’t have been a more clumsy finish to a perfect start. Year 2000 and beyond? Words: 1,300 approx.
