Operation: FOX V

26 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/26/10 Previously on Operation: FOX... Clyde, Kyle and Cyrus headed back in time to the point when Fox’s father suffered his tragic wrestling accident in hopes of changing his, and in turn, the rest of the world’s future. Turns out things didn’t go as planned as Clyde ended up slugging his own father with a sledgehammer. Now, after being transported back to the present, Clyde has mysteriously vanished for the night, leaving his friends worried sick back at the dock. Will Clyde ever resurface? Will he ever forgive himself for being the reason for his Dad’s accident? Will he continue his mission? All those answers and more, NEXT! --- “Urrrrrrrrraaaaaaaaaaggggggghhhhhhh,” The Kid groans. The sunlight beams through the crack of his eyelids, causing a rather sensitive reaction. Clyde, tattered clothes and all, leans against the hard brick surface behind him. He tries to figure out where he is but can’t remember a thing about last night. “Wh-what happened?” Clyde asks. Fox manages to gingerly lift a hand to his eye and shield his view from the sun. As far as he can tell, it’s rather early in the day because of the birds chirping, and people walking by, nearly kicking his extended legs. A parent and child pass by as the little boy stops and gently kicks Clyde’s long legs. “Mummy, dis guy smells tinky!” He spoke in his childish gibberish. The mother being very protective obviously pulls her son in close and looks shamefully at Fox. “Stay away from him. He doesn’t look like he wants to be bothered,” The mother directs. Fox moans and lunges forward, nearly scaring the kid out of his pants. The child latches onto his mother before they take off down the street. “Get a life loser!” The mother screams as she carries her son to safety. However, in reality, there was nothing dangerous about Clyde. He really just attempted to ask for water but like most things in life, he failed miserably at it. Clyde leans back and in the process, he pulls his legs up towards him. It had seemed like his legs were extended for hours as he feels the twitch of each leg muscle contract. “Owwwwwww,” Fox says as he rubs his forehead, “I think I took last night too far.” “CLYDE! OH MY GOD! THERE HE IS! CLYDE!” A loving female voice sparks from across the street. Fox droops his head as he notices a being like his girlfriend Suzy, and a feathery lad like Clowl rushing towards him. Upon arrival, the talking owl adjusts his monocle and examines Clyde. “Hhhhhoot, I must’n say my dear Suzy, but it looks like Clyde went on what you people call, a pretty mean bender, hoot!” Clowl diagnosed. Suzy drops to her knees, as she never has done with Clyde before, and pulls his head up by his chin. She notices all the symptoms of a certain drunkenness only ever seen in Clyde. She licks her thumb on her free hand and rubs away some of the dirt across her boyfriend’s cheek. “Awhck... look at you,” She says sympathetically, “You took the last mission really hard, eh?” Fox reaches up and latches onto a boob. “Water... water,” Fox yelps, “I dwank too much last night. They just kept coming, left and wight. Terrorible. Left and wight and Terrorible won’t stop it... at least three dozen cows.” Clowl and Suzy look confused, as they can’t quite piece the hidden code together. Nonetheless, Suzy stands Clyde up. His legs wobble like a pair of wet noodles before Clyde grabs both her breasts for stability. “Hoot!” Clowl flutters, “Madam, I believe I might know what happened here last night. You see, I think Clyde drank too much... of something. But that something, I do not know. Hoot!” Suzy bites her lip as she looks defensively at some passersby before replying. “Milk,” Suzy mutters, “I’ve seen him do it before. He’s got level eight cow poisoning from drinking nearly three-dozen gallons of milk last night. And if my guess of this reference to ‘Terrorible’ is right, then that means he spent the whole night under Jay Terror’s supervision. God, one can only imagine what he’d let Clyde do.” Just then they notice a small flask poking out of Clyde’s pocket. Clowl scoops it out with his feathery wing before opening it and smelling the sour milk goodness. The stench makes his beak turn a shade of green. “Hoooooot! Yucky! Good thing he hasn’t been injecting this either,” Clowl notes. But that statement is immediately proved wrong as a syringe, crusted with dry milk, and various milk products also falls out of his pocket. Suzy turns Fox’s face to the sun and notices a dry milk mustache above his lip. The evidence was obvious after a thorough inspection. “My goodness Clyde,” Suzy chokes up, “How could you do this to yourself?” Clyde finally gets some life into him and pushes Suzy away! Clowl’s eyes nearly fall out of his feathery skull at the sight of Clyde being so unbelievable. Suzy manages to catch herself before falling on the pavement. “What was that for? Hey, Clyde! We’re here to help!” She invites. Clyde just simply walks down the street and gives some sort of a hand motion before disappearing in the distance. “Hoot... come my dearest, we should return to the others. Let him go, hoot... he clearly has some sort of issue to work out on his own. I’m sure he’ll return to us, hoot. At least we can tell the others that we saw him, “Clowl rationalizes. And with that Suzy and Clowl return to their Geo and drive back to the base. --- “And that’s what happened,” Suzy finishes her story as Crayden, Kyle, Onjin and Cyrus are gathered around the control station, listening. “Wow,” Crayden says, “I gotta admit, I didn’t think the kid had it in him to pull something like that. But, at the same time I mean, in all fairness he did just cripple his own Dad.” CLUNK-SCHREEEEEECH! Just then, the back door flings open. The sunlight cascades through the rectangular doorframe like something from the other dimension. A tall, somewhat gluttonous shadow appears within the frame. Everyone is quiet. CLICK! CLACK! CLICK! CLACK! The sounds of his footsteps are heard as he walks into view. It’s Clyde Fox. But there are no cheers, no welcomes, and no jokes. The group that Clyde has called his family for the past number of months just stares at a dirty, ruined and depressed Fox. “I’m... I’m just here to get my stuff. I’m weaving,” Clyde states before scurrying off to get his belongings. “H-hey,” Crayden interjects, but Clyde keeps on walking, “Hey man listen, it’s not your fault,” Fox exits the room, “We’re still going to do the mission... even... even without you man. There’s more to do!” Fox reappears with a duffle bag full of his stuff. He doesn’t stop, not even once to say goodbye to anyone of his friends. Not even Suzy. Not even his favorite burping spot. This is it. Exit Clyde Fox. ... Or is it? SCHHH! Fox suddenly stops at the cusp of the backdoor, violently scuffing his shoes into the cement in the process. He lowers his bag and drops it to the ground. Suzy steps forward with tears streaming down her face. “We love you Clyde, we all do! We never want to see you go! And we don’t care what you did last night... we can work through it! Please! Just stay! Just stay, Clyde!” Suzy cries, as she can’t hold anything back. Clyde turns, a tear forms in one of his eyes. He can’t help it either before running and confiding in the arms of one of his best friends in the whole wide world. The rest of the group falls in, showering Fox with hugs and love. “I wuv you guys! I’m sowwie! I’m sowwie!” Fox cries and the group embrace everyone. It was like a rebirth. The kind of stuff that only happens in Hollywood. Except, it happened here. Exit Clyde Fox? I don’t think so! Not now, not ever. But what does this mean for the mission? And how can Clyde regain his once hero status? Stay tuned for all the answers and more heart pounding drama, next time! To Be Continued...
