An Experiment, Part II

20 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 20, 2008 An Experiment Part II: Parts Unknown Provide for a Morbid Reality *Several hours later* Grunting fills the room as a man, the manager Face kidnapped earlier tonight, desperately attempts to break free from his constraints. It’s no use, though; the ropes are fastened tightly around his arms and torso, as well as his legs. The knots are sturdy, making it clear that this isn’t the first time Face has tied someone tightly to a chair. It won’t be the last either. The manager’s mouth is free, but he refrains from shouting wildly at his captor, who is sitting behind a desk only a few feet in front of him. With the manager’s silence, sounds of scribbling and jotting fill the small, concrete-walled room. It seems even I have underestimated this subject’s will and resolve. However, this is certainly a welcomed surprise...I imagine that even greater results than originally anticipated will be observed in tonight’s research because of these new findings. Of course, it remains to be seen if these strong mental walls will last until the final experiment. Face stops his writing to glance up at his subject. The manager’s blue eyes return a firm glance of their own in Face’s direction. A short chuckle escapes from Face’s mask. I have a strong feeling those walls will come crumbling down before then... With his last thought put onto paper, the twisted “scientist” takes his worn out sneakers off the desk and onto the cold floor. Slowly and deliberately, Face reaches his feet and begins to walk over to the man he just kidnapped several hours ago. He takes a lighter out from a pocket in his worn out pants and begins his usual ritual, flicking it on and off to the beat of silent drums. Face’s slow and deliberate walk is not unlike that of a professor pacing in deep thought before his classroom. Ironically, the manager is not unlike a student, squirming and waiting for the moment he can leave this demented lecture. Unfortunately, this lecture has no time bounds on it and the professor at hand likes to take his time. “Tell me Mr...” Face let’s himself trail off to get his captive’s name. He is unsuccessful, however, as the manager remains silent and once again leaves his name out of the equation. Face shrugs, knowing that his name has no use other than something to call him by. He decides to make do with the information he has. “Right, Mr. Manager it is then. So, tell me, Mr. Manager: what do you value most in life?” Face awaits a response, but “Mr. Manager” continues to give Face the silent treatment. “Alright, let me put it another way then...” Face stops his pacing directly in front of the manager, staring directly into the man’s eyes from behind the dark, empty crosses on his mask. “If I were to put...” Face motions with his hands as if he’s placing a large pile of something beside him. “All of the money you have into a big pile and...” Face stops his rhythmic flicking of his lighter, letting the flame remain lit. “Set it on fire in front of your eyes...how would that make you feel” Hmmmm?” The manager shakes his head. “Money is just paper. It is not permanent.” Face nods in agreement, letting the lighter’s lid snap closed, flicking it open and closed at a rhythm once again. “Alright, so what if I were to light all your money on fire...inside your house...and make you watch it burn to the ground” Would that bother you, Mr. Manager?” Once again, the manager shakes his head. “It would bother me, but my house and my money are just material possessions. You might have some desperate need for things like that, but I don’t.” A short cackle escapes from under Face’s mask at that last retort from the manager. He waggles one of his horribly scarred fingers at him. “No, Mr. Manager...I do not require material possessions, nor do I really even care about them at all. In fact, I think you’ll find my needs are very...simple. For example, if I had a daughter and she were burnt alive in front of my eyes...” The manager’s eyes suddenly widen. Face smiles devilishly behind his mask. “I wouldn’t mind it one bit. Now, I bet if I were to take your precious daughter-? “Leave my daughter out of this!” Yells the manager, his eyes wide and boiling with a rage no one’s seen from him in quite some time. Face stops talking, tilting his head to the side inquisitively at the sudden outburst. The devilish grin behind his mask grows. “Well, well, well, I guess we have a winner! Hmmm” I had a feeling the lovely girl would be your most prized possession. That’s good...It confirms your motive.” “What motive” What did you do to my daughter!” Where is she!”” The manager grunts, trying to break free of his constraints with a reborn interest in doing so. Unfortunately for him, that sudden interest doesn’t provide him with any more means of escaping and he still isn’t going anywhere. Face begins to laugh, which doesn’t exactly help the manager’s nervous thoughts. “Tell me! What did you do to my daughter!”” “Oh, I didn’t do much, Mr. Manager. I just brought her along with you. Don’t worry, she slept well.” Face’s hoarse laugh continues as his captive squirms uncontrollably in his chair. “And so they crumble...” Face thinks to himself. Once his laughing subsides, Face heads back to his desk and opens a drawer. He snaps his lighter shut one last time before pocketing it and reaching into the drawer. The manager looks on with interest, shaking his head once he sees Face’s scarred hand holding a syringe filled with some sort of liquid. Face shuts the drawer and steps towards the manager, who begins to plead with his captor. “Please, don’t hurt my daughter...She means everything to me. You can do what you want with me, but don’t hurt my daughter.” Face seemingly ignores the request as he walks up to the manager, flicking the syringe to get rid of any air bubbles. However, before he moves to insert the unknown liquid into his captive, he provides some reassuring words. “I won’t hurt your daughter, Mr. Manager. No, it’s not me that you have to worry about.” The manager’s face tells a tale of confusion now, but Face is quick to clarify. “You don’t have to worry about me hurting her. You have to worry about an incredibly selfish narcissist hurting her.” The manager’s eyes widen larger than ever now, but before he can say anything, Face reveals more. “There’s no guarantee she’ll be hurt, though. It’s all part of the experiment.” “Experiment” You’re telling me you kidnapped me and my daughter for some twisted experiment?” The tale of confusion depicted on the manager’s face goes from a single novel to several. “Well, I didn’t think I’d be able to find a wide variety of people willing to kill one another in a controlled study. Do you know a group of people willing to kill one another who all have different motives for doing so?” At first, the manager almost thinks Face is kidding with those questions, but he quickly realizes just how serious they are. And then it dons on him: he’s part of the experiment and he has a motive Face was talking about. “I...I have to kill someone?” “That depends on what you define “have” as. You technically don’t have to kill anyone. If you don’t, however, you will indeed die...and so will your lovely daughter.” Face leaves no room for interpretation in that last sentence. And so the manager realizes the structure of the experiment he’s going to take part in: Kill or be killed, along with his daughter. It’s an incredibly morbid thought; one that brings tears to his eyes. Face moves towards him now with the syringe, but he stops just short as the manager quickly looks at him from behind teary eyes. His mental walls have indeed crumbled and Face isn’t about to ruin the moment by putting him to sleep. “You should really reconsider this before your crimes become worse. You could let us go now and your sentence wouldn’t be as bad.” The manager’s words are clearly an attempt to reach out to Face and stop him from continuing with the morbid experiment. Unfortunately, that’s about as useful as him squirming around to try and break out of his constraints. Face shakes his head and responds with undeniable confidence in his answer. “I won’t be caught, Mr. Manager. I’m a very...cautious person when it comes to these sorts of things. Your store has no security cameras and you and your daughter were the only two people there when I confronted you. No one was around when I sabotaged your engine. Nobody was around when I kidnapped you and your daughter. No one lives anywhere remotely close to where we are now and we are in a solid concrete bunker underneath the ground, where no one will see or hear what goes on here. My shoes and clothes are worn out beyond recognition and my finger prints are no longer a viable way to identify me thanks how warped and scarred they are.” Face pulls away from the manager to stare directly into his eyes. “I am also quite good at seeing people for who they are and, thus, it is easy for me to spot subjects for my experiments in terms of what motives they would bring to the table. It makes everything much more...efficient when you don’t have to worry about kidnapping multiple people just to find the one with the right motive.” Face begins to pace back and forward in front of the manager, continuing to deliberate into his plans. “Once I’ve spotted someone from afar, I go in and extract them for my experiments, without a trace, just like I did with you and your lovely daughter.” The pacing stops now and Face once again returns to the manager’s side, ready to inject him with the mysterious liquid. The manager is completely perplexed with all the information he’s absorbing, to the point where he has no expression on his face at all. “By the way...you should know that you, your daughter and your narcissistic friend are the first ones to be involved in these experiments...although I doubt that comes as much of a consolation to you.” Face chuckles and that’s the last thing the manager hears before the mysterious liquid is released into his system. It doesn’t take long for him to completely black out for the second time tonight... The manager’s eyes flutter open and he soon realizes he’s being shaken and screamed at by someone. “Dad! Wake up! Wake up dad, please!” The manager suddenly snaps to full attention, realizing exactly who is beside him. He turns to find his daughter sobbing uncontrollably beside him. The two quickly embrace, the father fighting back tears of his own. “It’s okay, Vivica. I won’t let the masked man hurt you anymore,” says the father, in an attempt of reassuring comfort he knows won’t last long. In fact, it only lasts a moment, as the girl suddenly screams and buries her head into her father’s chest at the sight of what is behind him. The manager doesn’t even have to look, knowing full-well who it is. “So, your daughter’s name is Vivica” I know someone by that name as well. She’s a feisty one.” Face pauses only for a moment before getting to what he came here to do. “The experiment’s about to begin, Mr. Manager. Here’s your weapon.” A knife lands with a loud CLINK on the concrete ground beside the manager. “Your friend is waiting for you in the room to your right.” Face heads towards a door to the left. He opens it and enters it, but before he closes the door behind him, he reminds the manager of something. “Remember: it’s either him...or you and your daughter.” With that, Face closes the door behind him. An audible lock is heard, leaving the manager and his daughter, Vivica, to themselves in their room. Vivica looks over to the knife on the floor beside them, then into her father’s eyes. “What is that for, dad” Why did he give you a knife?” The father’s face drops for a moment before he gets up to his feet, his fists both clenched tightly. “I have to do something that...I would never normally do, Vivica. You need to stay in here. I’ll be back soon.” The manager sounds certain with his words, but he knows that there’s always the chance he won’t return. It’s a chance he is not going to allow to come to fruition, however. His daughter means too much to him. His fellow “research subject” will have to understand. The father kisses her daughter on the forehead, picks up the knife beside him, and heads over to the door on their right. He enters it, closing it behind him. Sure enough, another man is waiting in the room for him. Both men are about the same height and weight it seems. They both have the same knife in their hands. It turns out neither men have any combat training and have never gotten into a physical fight in their lives. Face was thorough in his selection of test subjects, trying to eliminate all factors outside of their motives. Aside from the horrible purpose of the experiment, it is indeed as scientifically sound as it will get. Both men just stand still outside their doors, neither one wanting to make the first move. The whole idea of killing another man is just something neither of these men would even think of, yet they must. Or do they? “By the way...if you refuse to kill one another, I’ll kill you both,” rings the answer on cue from a loud speaker in the room. Meanwhile, in another room, Face sits comfortably at his desk, watching the two on a TV screen in front of him. He once again scribbles away at his book while the two men remain at a standstill. Someone has to die. One man fights for himself. The other fights for his daughter. The first experiment is about to be carried out by two involuntary subjects. Of course, that’s part of the experiment as well. If they would have voluntarily killed one another without any motives, there wouldn’t be much to research...and it wouldn’t be nearly as fun... Back in the room, the two men begin to circle slowly, their knives held out in front of them. The manager is quick to try and reason with his opponent. “Neither of us is more valuable than the other...but I think we can both agree an innocent girl should not die tonight.” The other man stops circling, prompting the manager to stop as well. The man sighs, lowering his weapon. “You...you’re right...You’re daughter shouldn’t die.” “Then...you’re willing to die so she doesn’t?” inquires the manager. He begins to step slowly towards the other man now, who nods and, sure enough, drops his knife. Face watches on closely as the manager continues to slowly step forward, until he is within a few feet of the other man. “I’m sorry,” says the manager as he lunges forward with a killing intent. “No, I’m sorry,” says the other man as he dodges the blow and kicks the manager square in the balls. The manager goes down hard, eyes wide in both pain and surprise. Face lets out a short laugh as he watches the event unfold on his TV. The man, who is now clearly the narcissist Face described him to be, picks his knife back up and plunges it down towards the manager on the ground. However, the knife doesn’t make it to the manager before the manager’s knife makes it to the narcissist as the manager suddenly juts his hand up and stabs the narcissist straight through the heart. Blood shoots out of the man’s chest and onto the manager’s whole upper torso. The manager quickly turns to shake the man he just killed off his knife, visibly shaken from the blood that has been spilt by his own hand. After a few moments, the manager throws down the knife and gets to his feet. He looks over to the dead man beside him and shakes his head before heading towards the room his daughter is waiting in for him. The blood is impossible to hide, so the manager doesn’t attempt to hide it. He figures he’ll just have to tell his daughter what he did and why he did it. He opens the door and finds Face standing in the middle of the room, a bloody knife of his own in his hand. It takes a moment for it to kick in, but then the manager’s eyes slowly look over to the bloody carcass of what used to be his living daughter strewed across the floor. Tears begin to roll down his face uncontrollably and he drops to his knees, completely broken. “But...but...you said you wouldn’t hurt her...” murmurs the manager between sobs. Face shrugs. “Well, the man in that other room said he would let you kill him. Apparently, he and I are both very good liars...or you are very gullible. Either way, what did you expect me to do once you killed him” Hmmmm? Let you go?” The manager doesn’t respond, but this time it’s not an act of strength. Rather, it is a product of complete hopelessness. Face steps up to the manager, spinning his knife around in his hand once. “If it makes you feel any better, I didn’t have to do much to take down Vivica. She died rather quickly.” Face just watches as the manager breaks down and cries. After a few moments, drives the knife in his hand cleanly through the manager’s neck, silencing the sobbing. The manager drops to the ground and blood flows out instantly. Face takes in his surroundings for a few moments: the dead bodies, the blood, the knives... “This is going to be hard to clean up every time...”
