Curiosity killed the cat..

7 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/7/2005 Tick...tock.... tick.... tock... The sound of the clock on the far wall seemed to be the only thing that registered within that constantly calculating and scheming mind as the flaxen haired vixen of GCW went over the recent events in her mind. She had successfully closed the deal on Johnny Lexicon as she had promised and in turn, Jordan had given her due. It should have been enough for her, but as always it was never enough. Legs shifted sweeping upwards to curl at her side as she began to tap pen against pad idly pondering her next move. Those icy globes stared at the name Lestatt that mind unable to remove him from the forefront. This man interested her in ways many men failed. He had intellect, cunning, but most of all he had thirst...an unqunechable thirst that seemed to match her own. The event surrounding Blaq had been rudimentary at best, but the Dread incident had given him some redeeming qualities. Lestatt was a dangerous man. Danger...she was drawn to that. Tap tap tap tap tap tap tap. That pen kept tapping away drowning out the sound of the clock as her eyes moved on to the next man on her list...Borealis. Glacier chipped orbs rolled at the thought of him even as a sneer wafted at the edge of those tempting lips. He was a handsome man for sure, but he lacked that edge that could garner anything more than a coy smirk and cutting remark from the likes of her. No style. Erik had delivered the flowers to her in a not so genteel manner, the evidence of his irritation written in the depths of his eyes. Borealis would have to be dealt with, but for now it was time for a game. The pen would be tossed onto the pad as she slinked from her perch on that couch to cross the marbled floor. Bared feet left tiny heated imprints on the cold slab that would fade away in seconds as she moved to a low table set between two grounded cushions. On that table sat a chessboard with piece strewn across as if in the middle of a game. She scrutinized the setting, looking over each piece and its position with careful consideration. Strategy was of the utmost importance in this particular genre. Lips tightened into a small grimace until at last she saw it and a grin appeared. Gingerly a hand out stretched plucking up her rook and moving it forward three squares. ' Check brother dear...' He would lose if he were not careful in his next moves. In fact if all went to plan he would lose either way. Eva played for keeps. It was that very thought that had her mind wandering back to the visual of Lestatt. Eyes narrowed, a soft growl emitting forth as she spun on bared heel and marched back to the pad that held his name on it. It was infuriating! Like a cobra her hand snapped forth slapping that pad off the arm of the couch, pen and paper clattering across the floor in different directions. The man had gotten beneath her skin. She hated that. Staring at the pad from her place she gritted her teeth and set her mind to the next task at hand. She needed to study him, to get close and figure out what it was that made him so blasted entertaining to her. There was a lot that needed to be done. First, to handle the minor stuff.... Eyes skimmed over Borealis's name. He needed to be taught a lesson, and she knew just the girl to do it. Slowly those lips twitched sliding upwards into devious smile. What better way to teach him a lesson than to humiliate him publicly? Let the games begin. ' Okay big boy.... I promised you a date in the ring...I think it is high time I kept that promise...' She leaned over the couch and snatched up the phone. With two digits pressed she ringing Caldera's office... Who said being a woman couldn't get you places?
