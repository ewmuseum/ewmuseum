Date: 10/30/06: Character Development Skygreen 79: Van, I need something. Whole Freaking SHO: Whoa there... who's this again? Skygreen 79: You know me. Whole Freaking SHO: Do I really? You don't return my love letters. And I stood outside in the rain all night with 80s hair-band ballads pumping from a boombox I was holding over my head. Skygreen 79: When I said 'I need something,' your corny humor wasn't quite it. Whole Freaking SHO: No, but that always comes as a bonus. Anyway, what do you need? Skygreen 79: A media specialist. Whole Freaking SHO: I'm your man. Skygreen 79: Not in this case, no. You're still an active wrestler, you needn't be worried about trying to assist me. Whole Freaking SHO: But I can do it just to show everybody that I'm better than Seymour with his ridiculous 'Almasy Office.' Skygreen 79: Don't worry, that'll die in about two months. Whole Freaking SHO: *Pelvic thrust at Almasy* SUCKAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA! Skygreen 79: ... Anyway, I need you to loan us a PR representative. Whole Freaking SHO: 'Us?' Skygreen 79: I'm working for GCW now. You probably don't watch it. Whole Freaking SHO: Not really. You know that anything that doesn't involve me will inevitably put me to sleep. Skygreen 79: You're sure that it wouldn't affect you at all? Apparently our companies are going through some kind of rivalry spat at the moment. Whole Freaking SHO: Really? Why? Skygreen 79: Apparently it's to do with ratings, sniping, and general antagonism. Whole Freaking SHO: Sniping? Who got shot? Skygreen 79: ... Sniping, in wrestling terms (which you should take care to learn at some point), refers to targetting a wrestler from another roster and bidding him high to get him to set his sights on your federation. It's not a contract buyout, as the bidding company does not deal with the management under which the wrestler is currently under contract. Instead, to streamline the legal process, the wrestler simply invokes the escape clause of his/her active contract, walks away, and joins the higher-paying federation. Whole Freaking SHO: Good thing that I'm rich... PRIME has no need to fear me getting sniped. Skygreen 79: Honestly, I don't think many people would be trying to snipe you anyway. Whatever you've done in the world of kickboxing is irrelevant; in PRIME and PTC, the name 'Vangellus Oxios' doesn't mean very much thanks to lacking mat-wrestling skills and a weak neck. You got the raw end of the deal with PRIME's tag team division going on indefinite hiatus. Whole Freaking SHO: I know. Working with Scott was fun times. He's a big old teddy bear. Skygreen 79: I'm sure his wife says the same thing. Whole Freaking SHO: Yeah, but she actually gets to cuddle with him. Skygreen 79: Going back to what I was originally talking about, before this goes on an even more awkward tangent, you've got an inexplicably large media conglomerate. Anyone you can toss our way? Whole Freaking SHO: Hm... really, I haven't had much for Adrienne to do since I've returned. I've got passive contract renewals going through, and I'm trying to get more active in handling my own day-to-day. I had to, anyway, while I was over in Japan due to that whole Cult of the Poison Tree thing that happened. Thanks for filling in for me and Scott with Shawn Stewart. He liked it so much he came back to PRIME. Skygreen 79: He's a consummate professional, good he's found steady work on this side of things again. He didn't seem to like it in fWo. Whole Freaking SHO: Not really, but I heard he busted some heads Skygreen 79: So Adrienne? She's not doing anything? Whole Freaking SHO: Nope. I'll let her know that she's working for you guys now. Skygreen 79: Well then. Thanks. Whole Freaking SHO: Don't mention it. Whole Freaking SHO: Actually, on second thought, mention it as many times as possible. Whole Freaking SHO: I love it when people remind me of how generous I am. Skygreen 79: Whatever. Later. Whole Freaking SHO: Peace out, nigga! Skygreen 79: ....
