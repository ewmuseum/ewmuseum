The Lady In My Life

26 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26 March 2008 January 4th 1999 Everything in his life was full of uncertainty on this day. There was never intent on being in love but since it did happen his life was tipped upside down. Roberto awoke that morning with an indescribable feeling deep inside of his stomach. On a day like this, words would always come at a loss and nobody really expected otherwise. His heart and his mind were being pulled in a million different directions, directions that he did not know how to deal with. Roberto checks the clock and it reads 4:30 AM. He did not have to be up and out of the door until 8 AM but he couldn’t sleep. He forces himself to lie in bed for a bit longer, hoping that he’d fall asleep but knowing that it would never happen. His bright blue eyes stare into the blank white ceiling of his room as if searching for the answers that he desperately needed. Was this what was suppose to happen to him? Was love supposed to do this to him? The answers never came. - September 3rd 1993 First day of school was usually one of the more exciting days of school for Roberto. At thirteen years old and just beginning High School things were brand new for him. He somehow managed to find his classroom just before the bell rang. Most of the seats were all taken up, as they usually were but when he scanned the room he did indeed catch one that was open for the taking. He moved towards it, making sure not to make too much noise or bring much attention to himself, shy he was. Roberto moved a step or two from that chair when his eyes went wide, his mouth went dry, and his heart began pounding. Of course it was a young woman who took his breath away. A young Hispanic girl, long black hair that fell freely, beautifully tan complexion, and although her eyes were concentrated on what she was writing in front her, those beautiful brown eyes shined bright. Roberto was forced to regroup, straightened his tie which is something he hated wearing anyways but the school uniform dictated it. He stood up straight and moved to the chair, careful not to look over at the young girl. He sat in his chair with a loud noise as all his weight came crashing down at once. A few faces turned to look ? but he didn’t want to make it look as if he were nervous. "Damn." He heard her whisper as her pencil had broken on the paper she was writing on. As was expected, Roberto moved his eyes to get a glimpse of her but made sure to not turn his head too far. She looked up from her paper then turned to Roberto. "Excuse me, do you have an extra pencil?" Her words might as well have been words of God, as his reaction was the same. Slowly he turned his head, looking at her with semi wide eyes. He didn’t say anything though. She lifted an eyebrow after a moment of silence. "Um ? are you okay?" His lips moved but at first nothing came out. He cleared his throat and forced a smile, a smile that was part nervous, part goofy. "So’Sorry, yeah I do." He moved to his book bag and pulled out a pencil. "Right here." He extended the pencil to her and she reached out and took it from him, just momentarily brushing his hand. His heart stopped in that one moment. To him his young life was now fully complete as he was touched by an angel. She smiles at him, a smile that could brighten up the darkest corner of the night. "Gracias, my name is Cindy." "Roberto." - A half hour later Roberto was sitting at the edge of his bed. The thought of his first meeting with the love of his life brought a quick smile to his face. He could still feel the nervousness and the butterflies that roamed inside of his stomach. It was an incredible feeling. There are just times that you seem to just know that someone is special. Sometimes at the age of thirteen your heart will play tricks on you but not this time. Somehow he knew it. Although it was so early in the morning Roberto saw no point in wasting time. He got to his feet and walked across the carpet floor towards his closet. The door opened with some light creaks but nothing too noisy. He reached a hand in and pulled out a hanger that had a black Tuxedo hanging from it, with plastic covering it from dust or dirt. Those Icy blues of his looked at the tuxedo for quite sometime, as if seeing the life that was about to stand in front of him inside of it. He swallowed hard then moved back towards his bed, laying the suit on top of the mattress. A shower was needed that he knew for sure. Ten or Fifteen minutes later and the shower was done and Roberto returned back to his room with a towel around his waist. He checked for everything he would need, Tuxedo, shoes, black socks, and undershirt. He finished his quick check and sat down on his bed. He begins to dress himself, staring with the undershirt, losing his towel, sliding his boxers on and then the black socks. He took a break, letting out a long breath and running his hand through the damp hair that matted his head. His eyes traveled around the room which was almost completely dark. It felt lonely in that room. It isn’t often that Roberto had felt this way in his life. Growing up he was happy, energetic, and had loving "parents". He felt contentment for the longest time, but when Cindy came into his life it seemed complete. In his left hand, Roberto lifts the dress pants up, pulling them on, standing to get them straightened. The dress shirt came next, he slid it over his shoulders and let it fall down his back. He walked to a mirror that rested on the wall and began buttoning the shirt at the wrists and then the body, only leaving the top button undone. His eyes shift to the left to a dresser, where a picture of him and Cindy was perched on top of. He smiled. Roberto moved towards the picture, taking it in his hands, taking in the sight. They were older than 13 that was for sure. Cindy was on Roberto’s back standing on a beach, smiling as if they had no cares in the world. Today would be the day that their lives met together and Roberto and Cindy were to be happily ever after. - July 13th 1996 It was a hot day, a very hot day. The sun beat down on the world like its own personal whipping boy. The beaches were packed because of the weather. Roberto didn’t mind the large crowd as much as he minded the men that would continue to stare at Cindy in her bikini. Part of him was angry, a part of him wanted to laugh at the droolers and let them know, loud and thunderously that she was his woman. He decided against it because Cindy would just scold him for being jealous, even if she had done the same thing at times. Roberto wasn’t the confrontational type of person, as a matter of fact he felt the urge to back out of any conflict not enjoying when others were upset with him. He chuckles to himself at all of these thoughts but turns his attention to his beautiful girlfriend. The two were friends for two years before they actually admitted their feeling for each other. It was much more than just a boyfriend/girlfriend relationship but they felt they could tell each other anything. Most people watched this relationship grow to this point and encourage their thriving love affair. They had only been actually "dating" for a bit under a year but it was much stronger than that. Roberto held Cindy’s hand in his and all over again remembering that feeling he had even the first time he laid eyes on her. He leans in and kisses her on the lips softly. "Want to go for a swim Mami?" "GOD do I! I’m melting out here." Roberto turned to his other side to let his Tia Sandra know that they were going to the water. No the two weren’t alone. At sixteen it wasn’t so easy and with Cindy’s parents as strict as they could be, parental supervision of some kind was USUALLY involved although things had began to get better. Roberto helped her up and turned his back, Cindy automatically leaped onto his back. Roberto spun around once and looked at his Tia who quickly snapped a photo. They all shared a laugh before Roberto ran towards the water with Cindy on his back, not stopping until the water forced him forward, taking them both under. They separated for only a moment before Cindy’s legs wrapped around Roberto’s waist and her arms around his neck. They share a deep kiss. The two don’t share any words, just look into each other’s eyes, knowing full well how happy they are together. They snuggle closer. "Te Quiero Mucho mami." "I Love you too." She whispered. - Roberto kissed the glass of the picture frame and placed it down. Every memory with Cindy was in his mind as if it were yesterday. Today’s memories would last a lifetime as well. Time ticked away as he let the thoughts flow through his mind, considering every twist and turn that life could give to him from this day forth. The clock read 6:45 AM. Roberto tucked his dress shirt in before reaching down for the black tie. The silk tie wrapped around his neck nicely and he began to tie, remembering the steps that his Uncle Juanito had taught him to make a tie nice and neat. Next Roberto slid into his dress shoes and although they were a tad uncomfortable, he made due. Today wasn’t a day to complain about such minor setbacks. Today meant so much more. Back to the bathroom he went, to comb his ear length hair. It took some work but eventually Roberto got his hair into a presentable manner. The solid black outfit that looked draped over his body stood out to him for some reason. It was such an ugly color, he thought. Black was always something he tried to stay away from, even preferring charcoal grey or something, but Black was the color Cindy loved him in most and on this day it was about what Cindy loved. Looking at that reflection Roberto wondered if that was even him standing there. He never thought things would come to this, but they had. Behind those blue eyes and black tuxedo lied an unsure man. He could put on a front about not being nervous and knowing that things will be alright but it was a fa’ade. He didn’t know how things would go or if he could even handle the pressures that came with it. He flipped the light switch off and headed back to his room. The clock read 7:30 AM. His nerves kicked into overdrive now. He liked having a plan of action but shit happens. Roberto moved to the small dresser that had the alarm clock on it and pulled open the one drawer. Only one thing rested inside of it, a small white box. A trembling hand picked the box up and opened it. An incredible diamond ring stared back at him. Memories flooded once more and a smile came again. - December 25th 1997 Roberto loved Christmas even if it did help in his being broke the rest of the year. The look on Cindy’s face was always worth the price he paid. Her happiness always meant more to him than life itself. His eyes fixed on the diamond ring in front of him. He had just bought it the other night and planned to ask Cindy to marry him. Although he KNEW she would say yes, nerves still crept in. He sat at the restaurant table by himself. Cindy had just went to use the restroom to "freshen up" or whatever that meant. Quickly Roberto snapped the box shut and placed the ring in his jacket pocket, knowing the right time to pop the question was coming, plus he saw Cindy step from the bathroom and ruining the surprise now would almost certainly kill the mood. Roberto didn’t know it yet but Cindy had just found out that they were to have a child. Cindy planned on telling him tonight, which was the real reason she left the table. She was nervous at what he would say about it. It was time. She sat back down with that killer smile and took him by the hand. "Babe there is something that I want to tell you." "Really? I have something as well, you first." Cindy’s smile faded and a look of nervousness came about. She looked around for a moment to collect her thoughts. She swallowed hard before beginning. In her mind she had a long and drawn out speech prepared, but instead it came out another way. "I’m pregnant." Silence. Cindy didn’t know if the silence was bad or good but she was nervous all the same. Roberto’s eyes dropped and his face went blank. He felt the tug of her hand pull tighter which made his eyes meet hers once again. To her surprise, but relief he smiled. "A BABY? Oh that’s wonderful Cindy!" He got off his seat and kneeled next to her, holding her hands tight. Tears began forming in both of their eyes. He asked when she found out, how far along, and all of the typical questions. Then he knew it was his turn. "This makes what I have to say even better." He reached into his pocket and slowly opened the box. "Cynthia Yvonne, will you marry me?" Her tears told the whole story. So did his. - A single tear drop fell from Roberto’s eye and trickled down his cheek. It was the happiest moment of his life to that point. The box snaps closed and he places the ring into his pocket for safe keeping since it would be used a bit later. The clock read 7:55 AM. The limo would indeed be here now, so Roberto threw his black jacket on to finish off his suit and went out of the door and towards the street. The morning was cloudy and a light rain was evident from the night before. The long black Limo was waiting, the driver standing on the outside. He greeted me with a slight tip of his hat. "How are you doing this morning sir?" Roberto just looked at him for a moment, trying to think of the right word. It didn’t come. He got into the Limo and the door slammed shut behind the passenger. Mr. Abitua clasped his hands together and put them close to his face as he began to watch the world fly by. They drove and drove, many different settings passed by from kids playing football, to swinging on a swing set, people sitting on their porches, cars zooming by, etc. It was all the same to him. Roberto could only see her face, feel her touch, and smell her scent. Cindy was his world, along with his young daughter Starr that is. He remembers her birth as a blessing, when his life became COMPLETELY whole. - October 3rd 1998 It had been a long labor but after the hours flew by, Starr Abitua was born. Her cries were like music to the awaiting mother and father’s ears. The doctors did what they needed with the child and quickly handed her over to her parents. Roberto was bursting with happiness and excitement and with every right. Him and Cindy had yet to be married but that was being planned for not long after the child was born. "Everything is perfect now." He whispered. "Si ? we are now whole." They cried together again, tears of joy were sweet like candy. - They approached the church and stopped. Roberto looked at the structure, knowing he’d never be anymore ready than he is now. He didn’t wait for the driver to open the door, moving out himself. Although it wasn’t too far of a walk each step seemed to be a mile and each stair he climbed towards the large doors that allowed you to enter the house of god seemed to be a mountain. The winds picked up, whipping his jacket and his hair around slightly. It didn’t stop his quest to reach the doors. The final step. It seemed almost like a victory in life. The doors were within reach now and it could all be over. His hand reached the handle and Cindy’s face came to his mind. He could imagine opening that door and seeing his family’s happy faces staring back at him. A sea of white, blue, grey, and red just waiting to give their well wishes. He could picture Cindy, more beautiful than ever in her white wedding dress and that veil over her face. He could taste the sweet as sugar lips that were attached to Cindy. That kiss would be the best one of their lives. Roberto could see them growing old together, sending Starr on her first day of Kindergarten, eventually High School, family vacations, and the like. He pushed the doors open. Its here that it hit him. The faces did turn and there was a sea of color looking back. The faces were twisted with pain and agony, the color was black. Am emptiness came into his stomach as everything came back to him in this moment. It didn’t seem real, yet it was. He could feel eyes of hatred and suffering burning into his soul as he walked down that aisle and towards his destiny. Each step echoed in the large building. The crying was loud but for some reason his foot steps and the pounding of his heart seemed to be a hell of a lot louder in his mind. At the alter was his beautiful daughter and wife. They did not smile, laugh, hug or kiss. Their eyes were emotionless and their faces colorless. His body trembled now, more so than ever before. The tears that burned behind his yes and the emotions that ripped him apart from the inside, out came flowing over like a volcano. His body lunged forward and he laid his face on the casket of his Wife. With one trembling hand Roberto pulled out the diamond ring. He placed it on top of the casket. "I’m sorry ? I Love you." Never again did colors seem bright, never again had happiness been the same. Never again did that hole seem to fill up.
