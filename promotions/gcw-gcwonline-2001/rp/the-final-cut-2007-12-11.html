The Final Cut?

11 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 11th, 2007 FLASH. Its December 11th, 2007. The last known location of Chris Bagwell had been laying in a hospital bed, wounded from an assault and two bullet wounds. His re-encounter with long time friend, Dukey Booty, left his mind in a daze and his life in need of some serious redirection. Chris was left without his girlfriend of two years, whom he had planned to propose to. He was left without any money, as she'd alreay had it siezed. Chris was left with nobody. He'd long since lost contact with the very men he'd went through life with. Particularly because of the memories they carried with them. Chris was lost and Chris was alone. Chris was without will and was soon thereafter without his job. Chris needed his escape, and there was only one man who could bring him to that escape. After leaving the hospital that one evening in October, Dukey Booty made a phone call to a long-time friend and ex-promoter in regards to Chris Bagwell's recent scenerio. Whether or not anything could -- or would be done was to be seen. It is still December 11th, 2007 and with the year's end in sight, it became Chris' phone that rang. It was a call that would reshape the course of his life -- again. And it went something like this... - - - - - - - - - - - - - Place: Chris Bagwell's home Time: Approximately 9:57 PM Date: December 11th, 2007 Perspective: Chris Bagwell (first person, slight past-tense) The blood had left stain marks across my counter and sat in piles along the floor. How I did not die that night still baffles me, as my kitchen looked as if i lost gallons. But to my dismay, I was still alive. I'm still here kicking. It's my first time home since Michelle and Augustus dropped one of the most sick and sadistic twists I've ever experienced in my life. I'm at a loss for where I go from here. Sitting in the hospital over the last month and a half has forced me out of work. Rehabilitation wasn't an option. Michelle whiped my accounts clean and there's just been no way for me to front these sorts of hospital bills. Perhaps it'd have been better that I just dropped dead. I remember the feeling. The shiny, sharp silver blade blistered firmly along side my right arm vein. I can still feel the pinching and laying witness to what could have been the closing chapter of my life. Sometimes the decision to permanately erase the pain outweighs any other option. There's only so much a person can take -- only so much they can withstand before they finally break. My breaking time had long since passed. My heart hasn't stopped aching. My bones have never felt so fragile. My head has not went a single day without splitting. That was it. This was the end. And then my phone rang. "Who in the hell could be bothering me at this hour," I mumbled to myself. Almost negligent to answer, I let it ring ... and ring. And finally, after the sixth or seventh ring, I decided to pick up. At first hesitant to speak, I soon whispered, "Hello?" I thought it'd been a ghost on the other end when a somewhat familiar voice replied softly, "Hello Chris. It's been a while." "Who is this?" I asked, but I didn't need to. I knew very well who it was. I knew VERY well... who it was. "I'm a bit upset you don't remember my voice, Chris," he responded. He was never one to be front with answers. But he did have the sound of dismay in his voice. He did hope that I'd remembered him... and I did. I remembered him VERY well. I hesitated once more with an abrupt silence. I waited. And waited. I waited for him to say something else. But the cards were already on my table. The ball had landed in my court and he was not going to cross the line to pick it up. He saw it at my feet and patiently waited for me to lift it from the ground... ... and throw it back, "How did you find this number? Why are you calling me?" And I did. I had no reason to be sincere and so I wasn't. He and I had a long history together. Some days were fantastic, while others weren't so much. Somewhere inside my body, I knew why he was calling. Nevertheless, I faught my reason and denied any claim. I played stupid. I wanted to hear it from his mouth. I didn't want to speculate. I wanted to know straight forward what he wanted. And so he told me. And he said, "I heard about Michelle, Chris. I heard about Augustus. I heard about the whole thing and what happened to you. You know exactly where I got your number. You practically asked for me to have it. And you know that." I was silent. Deadly silent. But he continued, "You wanted my help, Chris. That's why you made every implication for me to find you. You wanted it and now I'm here to give it." I maintained my silent stance, toying with the idea to simply tell the man off, hang up the phone, and call it a night. Yet I knew that if it were any other night, it would end at calling it a night. But tonight was different -- tonight I would've called it a life. I was in no way taking things lightly. Afterall, he was right in saying that I was looking for him. He might have been pushing buttons in claiming I was asking for his help. But by calling Dukey the night of Augustus, I was reaching out past that. I knew Dukey would lead to him. I just knew it. And maybe... Subconciously... Maybe I was reaching out for help. Shane's help. And so finally, I let up. "What Shane? What can you do to help me? You know my story. You know where I stand. You know what I've been through. And now, to top it all off, I get that delicious red cherry to dip on top. HOW THE FUCK CAN YOU ... HELP ME?" And perhaps I blew up. There's a chance I was out of place. But justifiably so. I'd spend most of my years mourning the death of a girl who'd had me at hello. And when the time finally came where I walked away from everything I knew to forget about my lesser moments... when the time finally came for me to find somebody else and really rediscover myself... It all got taken away again. My past came back to haunt me. But Shane was well aware. It was almost as if he'd scripted his response, because he promptly replied, "I'm going to help you the same way I helped you before. I'm not going to let you keep running away from your problems. I'm not going to encourage you to forget or to try and erase any memory that didn't end with a smile." My heart started racing. He went on, "As a matter of fact, I'm going to bring you back. I'm going to make sure you face each and every demon... and CRUSH THEM." I knew where he was going. I knew EXACTLY what he was getting at. And he continued, "I spoke to an old friend who pointed me in a particular direction and by the time we hang up this phone tonight, you're going to be headed in that direction." I insisted on playing devil's advocate and so snarled at him, "and what makes you so sure? What if I don't?" But he played it cool. He always did. He merely chuckled back at me, "Then you've just wasted the money I spent on having a stretch limosuine pick you up with your airfare and hotel details." It was as if he'd planned for that moment to happen on a movie set because just as he finished speaking, I could hear the sound of a car engine pulling in front of my house. I looked outside and there it was -- a white stretch Lincoln. He went on saying, "I'll be seeing you soon Chris. And I'll be sure to have a check sent to you when my real estate agent closes on your house next week." I didn't even have the opportunity to speak. He hung up the phone as I peered aimlessly out the window. What if I had said no? What if I hadn't answered the phone at all? He'd already sold my house, for christ's sake. The fact is... I did answer and I didn't say no. And I'd find out that I had me an interview to return to the squared circle. And not soon thereafter, I was on a flight to Columbus, Ohio. - - - - - - - - - - - - - FLASH. And so with that, we bring us to today. It's no longer December 11th anymore, and as you may have guessed, Chris followed Shane's words that night on the phone. It was a night that could have very well been the final cut for the likes of Chris Bagwell. But instead, it was not. It was the start of something new. That final cut became the first cut. The first cut of a very, very long slash. So with that said, I ask you to let it be known and let it be sworn... and let Andy Murray know that he WILL BE TOrN! FADE.
