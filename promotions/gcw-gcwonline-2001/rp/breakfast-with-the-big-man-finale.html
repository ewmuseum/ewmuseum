Breakfast with the Big Man (Finale)

undated

Date: Tomorrow Once he woke up, feeling better than he had in a very long time, they ordered breakfast, to be brought up to the room. Thanking her quietly under his breath, he stood, and slunk to the bathroom, snatching a small carryon type bag from the pile the bellboy had left. He closed the door behind him, and locked it for good measure. Hands still shaking a bit, he turned on the facet, which sprang to life pouring out clear, cold water. Feeling his knees start to weaken just a bit he quickly moved his hands to support himself on the sink. It was then he caught his reflection in the mirror, he stared hard. Eyes clear and bright, skin smooth and not a bit puffy. He tried to remember the last time he’d slept without the aid of a night cap, he couldn’t. It felt good though, thoughts more crystal, mind sharper. Taking a closer look at himself, he realized he must not have actually succeeded in tearing any hair out, thank god for that, he never looked good bald, more like an onion than anything menacing. Cupping hands together he lowered them into the water and brought it up to his face. It felt good, he was still a little flushed and the cold lowered his temperature. Strangely he was unashamed of his behavior, exactly the opposite of what he’d expected. Trying to figure out why he searched through his brain, recalling the events of the night as much as he could. Mostly it was a jumbled darkness, pain, lots of pain, and anger to match, desperate crushing loneliness. Vaguely he recalled, crying whispering sorry more times than he could count. All the time though there was that sweet angelic voice, telling him everything would be ok, he supposed she cared after all. Still he should have been ashamed of himself, he’d cried like a baby, but there was nothing of the sort. With a shock he realized there was no shame, because he was empty, everything was gone. It felt damn good, no more mental walls to patch up, because he’d blown them all completely apart last night. This wasn’t the same kind of emptiness as before, it wasn’t a hallow loathing of himself, more a complete lack of anything. Inhibition and everything else, gone he was a clean slate, ready to become something better, something new. A dry chuckle, the irony was he had to be torn down to nothing before he could grow. Funny how that works. Finished washing his face, he brushed his taste, the film of smoke would become truly disgusting if he didn’t. The minty flavor made his stomach rumble, he was starving, couldn’t remember that last time he’d been so damn hungry. He was drained to the point of empty, and his body and mind needed a great deal of food if was going to continue on doing anything. Opening the door to the room proper, he found Alex, and Sheena quietly talking. Not even a twinge of anger, even though last night he hated both of them with an all consuming passion. Moreover, there was no guilt left, that was truly nice. “Morning Mike.” There was affection there, it was really hard to keep it out of Alex’s voice. No matter how pissed the big guy got, he couldn’t quite shake that familial affection from his voice. At any point up to last night he probably would have called that a curse, now it just seemed right. The guy was secure, knew that no matter what happened Stevens was his cousin, always would be, and that bound them together in a way that was impossible to separate. This brought a smile to his face, not the smirk it would’ve been but an actual smile. Goddamnit, he felt good, and he would show it for once, before things started piling up again. He had no idea how long this state of clarity would last, and if it was short lived, he’d be damned if he didn’t enjoy it for all it was worth. “Morning Alex, Sheena. Breakfast here yet” I’m starving.” “Nah, not yet Mike. Hey man, what happened last night, there was some yelling the guys at the desk asked me to come up and check on you, see if everything was alright, but I couldn’t get answer.” “Oh it was nothing, just a little trouble in paradise, you know how we are.” She’d spoken before he’d even had a chance to, and it snapped his jaw shut. He wasn’t sure what he would’ve said, the truth or a lie, neither had seemed like a great option. Behind Alex on the bed where they were both sitting she shared with Stevens a secret smile, just for the two of them. He was a little shocked, but he wasn’t complaining. Just then a knock on the door, breakfast had arrived. He could smell the heavenly aroma, bacon, eggs, freshly buttered toast, and coffee. The coffee seemed like it would be the greatest thing in the world right now. Standing quickly, Sheena made her way to the door, took the tray, and gave the bellman a small tip. Turning she laid out the meal on a small table located in the room. “You know what, I’m not that hungry, I think you boys should eat while I clean myself up. No offense but a bath sounds great, and I wouldn’t mind a little me time. Can I have borrow the key to your room Alex?” “Sure, no problem.” Alex tossed her the key, and they both watched her go. Slipping silently out the door. Shaking his head, Stevens went for the table, she must’ve known he’d want to speak with Alex alone. He had to give credit where it was due. The two men seated themselves, and immediately dug in. That was always the greatest thing with two men around a meal, you weren’t expected to talk, just eat. If there was something you needed to talk about, you could always talk about it afterwards. For some reason women could never seem to understand that, oh well another mystery of women. The silence hanging between them, was comfortable, completely devoid of tension. He knew it wouldn’t have been the same if they’d had a meal together the night before, he would’ve burned holes in Alex, malice filling his every thought. Quietly they finished the meal, quickly, but not too fast, just slow enough to actually taste the food. At almost the same time they pushed themselves slightly away from the table. Alex belched with contentment. And he became the object of intense scrutiny. Chances are Alex hadn’t picked up on any of the things Stevens had been feeling lately, as Sheena obviously had. He lived in the land of the blissfully ignorant, which wasn’t always a bad thing, at least there was the bliss. He was just happy to be back in the mix, back in the fold of things. “So Mikey, planning on taking over the world?” It was a strange sort of question, definitely not the question he was expecting. Still it was the right question, he knew that the second he heard it. It was almost as if a switch turned on inside his head. All at once thoughts filled him, the easy knowledge that if he really tried he could take over the world. It’d always been something of a strange little dream he had. Conquer the world, change what he hated, and really crank up the things he loved. It was the reason he’d gotten into the business in the first place. The reason they both had. Sure they loved the sport, couldn’t deny that, but it was a little world all to itself. A self contained little universe that with a little effort, could be molded and shaped to fit the will of the powerful. In the days when they were both first starting out, they’d often referred to their matches as taking over the world. It was just something they’d said. “Hehe, actually I wasn’t. But I am now. Why the fuck not, we can do it.” “Damn right we can Mike. I’m just sorry I can’t be there with you.” The plan had always been he’d be the one at the top, with Alex by his side holding some minor version of the big belt around his waist. Maybe it was when Alex got hurt that everything started to go wrong. He’d been of the things grounding Stevens, knowing he always had someone watching his back, and if push came to shove he could always step in and take over, with Stevens leading from the rear. And then Alex had gotten himself hurt, couldn’t wrestle anymore. He just became an underling, a guy who could watch his back, but never step up if the time came. Can’t very well have a guy who couldn’t get a clearance to wrestle anywhere in the world holding any belt. Sure he could bodyguard, occasionally get involved, maybe even pay off the right people to wrestle once or twice, but he’d never be able to do it full time, it just wasn’t the same. “You know what buddy, you are with me, and you will be. You, me, Sheena, we’re going to fucking conquer the world. Not just take it over, conquer it, destroy it, break it down, and rebuild it in our image. It’s going to be great man, I can just picture it now.” “Damn Mike, you haven’t called me buddy in years, and I haven’t seen you so fired up in a long time, what gives?” “Back to my roots buddy, back to ALL our roots. We’re gonna conquer the world, and there isn’t a damn thing that’s gonna stop us. Do me a favor, go find Sheena, she needs to be here for this, we’re going to do some planning, get everything set. Oh yeah, and if she’s still in the tub take my phone and try and get in touch with the Allied Chaos boys. I think there’s a place in our new revolution for those two kids.” “Sure think boss, whatever you say.” He didn’t object to the word boss. It was what he should be called, at least for right now, they were working. Professional life and private life are two completely separate things, and now they would be, finally at last. Alex sprang to his feet, and darted out the room, guess Stevens enthusiasm was contagious. As the big man left, he leaned back in his chair, steepling his fingers in front of him, yeah he had a lot of planning to do.
