No Trey: Quit Grinnin'

31 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/31/06 I always wondered what absolutely nothing was like. Turns out it's where I've been headed for, well, too many years now. I'm not in the mood for counting or caring or even the slightest damn-givery. Turns out, it's the difference between who I was and who I am. Turns out, it's always been pretty close to my heart. So, what used to make me? Anger, stupidity, brash actions, thoughtless thinking, and heavy motherfucking addiction. Now? Not much. I got stupid, started thinking about what it was that comprised my head, and lost all of it. Thought I was smart enough to figure out what tricked people into liking me, ended up losing that in trying to accentuate the positive. Guess I'm not meant for marketing, huh? Or maybe I just wore out my welcome. I don't feel that different, honestly. Knocking on my ribs still lets out the same hollow thud it always gave. I couldn't be anymore bitter that I've been before--things haven't been that bad--and I'm less aggressive. Not that there's been as much to get worked up about. It's been a long few months away, back on the mountain so to speak. Call it whatever you want, I've named it fearful pussification and I won't let that be taken away from me; it's too true. I guess I said everything to Trey that I thought the world needed to hear and it convinced me that, as much as they used to care, they're far past giving a damn. Most likely wouldn't even be into what was going through the thinkbox if I were still as big as I ever was. I mean, I never tried to concern myself with if a celebrity was happy. They tried to mope, make things personal, I was apt to break off and find a new escape. This is the new one. Maybe the last one. I'm still months from where I need to be. The old house is worn down like I'd never lived here. Did I? I can't remember how long it's been. Almost a year since the last time I even left America, I'm sure. So, maybe four since here. Maybe more. Either way, it's still not hospitable and it's half-way home. The plants aren't growing fast enough, that's it. Once they come in, I'll be set. There won't be any escape unless she wants it. At any moment she could say 'leave,' we'd be gone. She knows it. She knows we both made a mistake, but she doesn't want to say it anymore than I did. We tried, at least, and that's more than most people ever get to say with any honesty. Turns out that neither of us were cut out for this life away from the world--I think I might need that fame. No man's crazy enough to be contented with his own self-worship. She, well, she just romanticized things more than they really should've been. It was all Trey's idea, honestly. He planted it in me when I first met her and I didn't doubt that abandonment was the best idea. Sure, it wasn't bad for a little while, but there's only so much catching up you can do before everyone just wishes I'd been smart enough to stick with my original plan. Leave her. Never communicate again. Turns out, I didn't need family. Turns out, things would've been a lot more fun if I never had to admit who I really was and just kept being that guy without thinking about it. This is what second-guessing brings you, kids. Glory, Gloria steps out of the house with a pitcher that looks like iced tea. She smiles at me, baring teeth, and brushes her scraggly purple hair from her face. She does look just like me. It's scary. 'Jordan!' 'Yeah?' I pretend like I was staring at something instead of narrating a fast-forward video of my entire life. 'Lunch.' 'What time is it?' She shrugs. 'Already? Fuck.' One last hit clears the last bit of resin from the last dirty pipe, and I realize exactly what I've done to myself. O, what brave new world! O, pitiable foolish young man! Sobriety. You are my bane. There's no allure in a drunk without being able to gush forth to a close friend or in a sexual object, and everything but eludes me. This isn't my land, mentally or literally. I don't know these people, I don't know their language. I just know that one night I felt worse than I thought I could feel (I'm wrong very very often) because I'd ditched her instead of trying to establish that I had a family. I didn't think much more than that, I just ran with it. What a mistake. She smiles from across the table. Her drunk's started, and I'm intimately aware of the difference between the ones that drink to remember and the ones that drink to forget. We're both fucking ourselves over out here. But, there's nowhere I've ever felt more at home than at the top of the waterfall. It's give and take out here. There's love, and there's hate, and there's not much that falls in between. Not peace of mind, but it's close. I make peace with things out here, though. Come to terms with things. Stare out at the trees all day, listening to the white noise until my head drowns it out. Phrases flash by, like 'I'm just going to smoke, drink, and die of cancer like the rest of us' and I'm almost tempted to never go to another home. I'll run Glory off soon--there's no way around it. I'm a bad brother, and I'm worse trying to be a father that's trying to be a friend. Isolation. At least for long enough to start remembering people for what they could be instead of what they are, more than enough time to start missing the motherfuckers. The slime coated, jizz encrusted, cocksucking motherfuckers. No. Remember this. Hate made me. Hate's kept me going, long after the fixation dropped out. Betraying it would be like severing my arm--the right one--to sew a small rubber duck on in its place. Wholly destructive and without benefits. What is Jordan White? Good one. The hum of a plane's engine fades in. I dart from the house and stare as the red-and-white Cessna floats over, dropping its load in the same field as always. It's good to have friends. They do things for you without always questioning how goddamned stupid of an idea you might be running on. Too much time away from people, too much time to think, all the hate gets turned inwards. She's not a factor; a small mole, or a kindly parasite. No personality other than doe-eyed idol worship that faded into a smiling resentment. Whatever. Cancer, just like everyone else. I can't be specific enough in that. I can't smile enough to get the point across. I can't smoke enough for you to really understand. I'll just say it until, tired of me repeating the point, you take it at face value so I can move on happily. You? I've fucking lost it. It helps, yeah. I can center, focus; keep doing that thing where I figure out how I work and ruin myself for it. The hate's got a good reason, but it's too fucking sappy. It's all about potential, about the way things could be opposed to the way they are. And, maybe I'm wrong here, but shit's fucked up. People are fucked up. The world's fucked up. There was a time for heroes, dreamers, warriors, saviors, revolutionaries, gods, demons, heavens, and hells, and that time's as gone as the dinosaurs. Sounds familiar. Used to try to be one of those--whichever suited my mood--but it never worked. The world operates by its own whims, and mankind is a beast greater than the sum of its parts and lacking the merit found in any random one. Sticking around this society thing is a losing bet, no matter how much money you put down and which side you put it on. By the time the winner's been announced, I'm dead. You're dead. We're all rotting. Maybe our heads are slipping through the ether, our souls intermingling into one shapeless glowing blob. I mean, that's not what I think, but maybe. Nah. Not happening. Cocaine would be nice; meth divine. The crowbar's going to work and my fingers are crossed for some heavenly intervention on top of this month's supply of cigarettes and booze. Pointless. Need to call the friend, let him know that my needs are changing. That I'm going back to how things used to be; except without the status, the fame, the health, the body, or the idea of a productive future. The wave has crested. I'm away from that little part of me that said 'be nice' and 'just because you're an asshole, doesn't mean you can't be helpful.' Yeah, it does. That's the point of being an asshole. Not gonna delude myself into whatever bent it'd take to think I did it to make people tougher, or smarter, or any of that bullshit. I'm a bad person. A hopeless addict, a by-product of a lame generation; a generation that needs to be taken out back and shot for its own betterment. Let the world turn. Peacefully. Drink, and be as honest as you can be to yourself. Hide in the brush and stare at your crop, counting the days and finding the tally at already-too-many gone and still-too-many yet to come. Glory's not calling your name. That was a double entendre. Or a pun. I don't know. I care, though. There's too many people to apologize to. That'll keep me here at least until the harvest, and the harvest will keep me here until it's all been smoked and my brain's caked in gooey resin like tar. Carve another notch into the tree at another name on the list, and try to remember who the other scratches are. They'll be deep in this bark long after the people aren't anymore--unless that deep down dirt part of my head takes the wheel and everything goes up in a storm of smoke. Ashes to ashes. Peace. When I'm backed so far into a corner that the only peace is in a complete disconnect. It's either heavy sedation or it's complete separation from their breed. Or both. Soon. I know there's something better out there for me. A happier life, brighter teeth, a straighter spine. All I have to do is step up and claim it as my lot. All I had to do, having turned down the best bet and not being up to finding a good second chance at this dreamy shit I'm rambling about. Danielle. In the end, she wasn't my type because I'm too much of a tremendous fucking douchebag to keep people around for long. So I can blame myself for that one; in that if I volunteer for a lobotomy I'll grin more often as a direct result. So, this is Jordan the borracho. The man who, in spite of massive Hispanic decent from both Spain and Mexico, isn't sure if borracho is the noun or the adjective form of 'drunk.' Mainly because it really doesn't matter, and I really don't care other than clarifiying to the lovely listener in my head that I know every single fuck up I make. I mean, if I step back into the world, all I'll want to say to it is 'fuck you.' Kind of a long drive for that. I could scream it from the peak of the waterfall, but only poor undeserving nature would hear it. Maybe I'm a cynic, now that I think about it. The animals have gotten used to me, at least. At least most of them. At least something. Things don't scurry like they used to when I'd advance, and I don't hear mass evacuations every time I go out back and start shooting at the trees. Out back meaning: my entire world right now. Never saw the virtue in trying to not be myself. Feel like I was betraying the personality that had built up over all these years if I went that far to consider the way other people looked at the world. It's easier to just say that they're wrong as fuck and completely ignore how hilarious it is for a hermit to spout some shit like that to the active participants in society. It's simple, though: you can't get an accurate view of something until you step outside of it and disregard all your biases. That means I'm halfway there. I tell myself, one more cigarette and I'll quit thinking. Now that the damage is already done and I've made myself clear as a lunatic with no real story to tell. Just my own voice in my own head with nothing that matters to say, no one that matters to say it to, and no reason to exist other than the sake of existence. Because I'm a little too young for suicide, since there's still some time for fun left on the clock and maybe even a few surprises. I'd love to have my head ripped open and spread out some more; I seem to hate myself enough that I'd love to be wrong. It's only because I love the world. God damn, that's worse than emo. That's heartfelt compassion from the delusional. But this one more cigarette isn't enough. It'll inspire a quick break that leads to either a heavier drunk or a long period of unconscious drooling, and both of those'll end up back at thinking. In spite of that, I am taking this break and I am going to enjoy it like the sun. I'll avoid everything: I won't hate the world because of how much I want to embrace it. I won't dream of slipping back in. I won't twitch the leg muscles. I'll try my best to live like the rest of the world, ignore that 1/30th of a second I used to thrive in. Those were fast times for fast people, and now I've hit hard onto the laze of time's true nature. A million years, and nothing will happen. Infinite possibility. Limited probability. When anything in the world can happen, it's still the same repetitive shit getting spewed every day. Nothing new under the sun, I guess. Here's to the unlimited power of the clich? to get a point across. Three cheers. Hip hip, you know the rest. I won't toy with my instincts. I'll lap up milk from the saucer and smile at my master because it's a warm one and, regardless of if I like the fucker personally, it's been too damned cold for too damned long. Go complacent. Go dreary. Go dreamy and hopeful and pretend that if you made a call, there'd be an answer. Somewhere between defeat and angst, Jordan's built a world to thrive in victory and brilliance--because he is a bad, bad, bad, bad man. He has robbed people of their livelihoods, he has stripped bank accounts dry, he has violated contracts meant to be immutable; basically, he's been a son of a bitch to the point that he's unwelcome cancer that'd probably be behind bars for a long time if he showed his face again. I'll change my name, and everything will slip away. New life. Won't be a first--not much is. Yeah, bullshit. If there's one person in the world that wants a little closure on this mess, it's me. Might just be time for that, and I'm not sure if Dorian Howlett Gray's got quite a catchy enough ring to it. At this point, I think closure might be a good nap, a heavy smoke, and a quick thought about Trey's whereabouts. I could be happy with that, if not for her.
