Prenez Garde Au Chien

12 April 2006

Date: April 12, 2006 'There are very few monsters who warrant the fear we have of them.' -- Andre Gide ---- First Passage: Escaping Mexico ---- Poverty Incarnate. Just like she said before. As the sun beat down on the dusty road, she'd never been happier to be in the middle of nowhere. At least she knew that this forsaken road was a one way trip out of hell. At least her own personal hell. It was no wonder so many Mexicans went border jumping every year. She began to suddenly feel a little sorry for the Mexicans about the 700 mile fence bill going through congress. It was worse than jail. It was the 9th circle of hell. At least in her mind. Then again... she'd always hated extreme heat and Spanish classes when she was younger. The new truck she'd 'aquired' from down the street from Arena Mexico seemed to be serving it's purpose well. Sure it guzzled gas, but she did need lots more space for extra passengers. Especially some which are not so willing. Up ahead was a sight for sore eyes. A border checkpoint. Was it a mirage or was it the real thing? Almost screaming out in joy Vivica J. Valentine slowed as she approached the border. The lettering on the side of ger truck glistened under the pounding heat. ANIMAL CONTROL. It read almost like a flashing neon sign reading 'SEARCH ME!' as she pulled up to a stop. The border control of the United States government quickly walked up to the driver's side window. 'Hola.' One of the security people said as they looked into the window. Fucking Spanish, Vivica thought to herself. She was a little nervous about this. It wasn't a good time to go on a rant about foreign languages. She bit her tongue, this time, and continued on. 'I'm American.' She said, sweat glistened her entire body. Wearing a black tube top and a pair of really short light blue jean shorts, her outfit perfectly matched her baby blue tips. Luckily she'd gotten the change to change clothes after her little match in Total J Cup. Afterall, staying in the tournament wasn't a big deal as long as she got what she came for. 'Good. Can I see a passport and two forms of identifcation please?' The guy asked from behind a pair of 70's Police Officer sunglasses. She couldn't help but think that he looked like a guy from Super Troopers as she reached over for her paperwork and handed it to the officer. He looked it over quickly before handing it back to her. That would've been all he needed more than likely... if she wasn't driving a big truck with ANIMAL CONTROL on the side. 'Do you mind if we search your vehicle ma'am?' The officer asked her as Vivica wiped the sweat off of her forehead. Apparently Animal Control hadn't learned about the miracles of air conditioning yet. 'Well I'm sort of in a hurry... it's just a few dogs.' She said, 'I'm with the Humane Society as you can see by the paper work. I'm getting stray dogs from here in Mexico and taking them to good homes in the United States. They're already gone through a series of vaccinations. I handed you that paper work too. Is there a problem?' 'No problem ma'am...' The man said, 'We just check all vehicles this size. Could you please take your keys out of the ignition momentarily.' She took a deep breath as she took the keys out of the ignition and set them on the passenger seat next to her. Tilting her head back she made a bang against the bed area of the truck. She hoped it was enough to give proper warning. Around the back two border patrol officers opened the back of the truck with force. Looking inside the vehicle the dogs erupted into a choir of yelps. The noise rattled the officers at first, who could see everything except for the back corner of the truck, due to the angle of the sun on this day. One of the officers jumped up into the bed to take a closer look. As he walked towards the back he suddenly saw a pair of eyes glaring back at him. Big and round... they were the same size of eyes that a human would have. Yet they seemed inhuman... almost dead and sinister as they looked back at him. Every instinct in his body told him not to take a step forward. It was just dogs... all of them were dogs. Yeah, just dogs. That's what he said to himself as he turned around and jumped out of the back of the truck. 'What's back there?' His supervisor asked him as he slammed the door shut. 'Just a big dog sir.' He answered. Vivica J. Valentine sighed in relief. She had absolutely no idea how he did it... but he did it. That was all that was important. Picking the keys up off the passenger seat she was relieved to hear the next words out of Super Troopers' mouth. 'Welcome back to the United States of America ma'am. You can go now.' 'Thank you sir!' She said before putting the key back into the ignition and starting up the beast of a truck she now called her own. Driving away from the border patrol and entering the country she called her own, she looked at the rear view mirror, as what she often referred to as hell grew farther and farther away. As it disappeared over the horizon and she began her journey though the state of Texas, a voice from the back suddenly interrupted her joyous state. 'Now that he's gone, can I get out of this cage?' The voice asked. 'Yeah...' Vivica responded with a smile, 'Just don't gourge yourself. We have a long drive ahead of us. I don't want to have to babysit your ass again.' 'I have no plans of eating any dogs back here.' The voice responded as you could hear a cage on the inside become unlatched, 'No matter how annoying they are.' ---- Second Passage: Beauty And The Beast ---- The journey that started in Mexico City was beginning to come to an end. After a few stops at a motel and some quick attire changes most of the past few days had been spent on the road. At last her destination was coming into view... and what a sight it was to behold. It reminded her so much like home, the McFarlane Estate was up in front of her. Many things crossed her mind, but most of all how pissed off Shadow was going to be at her for being so late. There wasn't much she could do though. It wasn't like this was something she decided to do. Over the past few days of driving she'd gotten to know him a little bit better than she thought she would. He wasn't getting any healthier in the back of the truck. He'd stayed true to his promise of not touching the dogs... who quite honestly hadn't had much more. They wouldn't quite be such a great meal at this point anyway. With a screeching halt, mainly due to the piece of shit the truck had turned into over the past few days, she skidded into a parking space under a bright white light. 'Piece of shit.' Vivica said pulling the keys out of the ignition and practically throwing them out of the truck and onto the pavement below. Kicking the door open Vivica J. Valentine looked up at the massive Estate in front of her. A bit of a smile crossed her face before she heard a cough coming from the back of the truck. Rushing to the back, picking up her keys off the pavement she opened up the doors up to the back. 'We need to get inside.' Vivica said shaking her keys which appeared to have some sort of remote control attached to them. From inside a groan echoed through the interior of the truck. 'Is that her?' Dawn McFarlane looked over at Lia who looked back after looking outside the window from up above. She answered Dawn's question with a single nod. So many things had happened over the past couple minutes, and so much more was about to happen. Lia Vlazy was the only person in the room who knew what was about to happen. Everyone in the room waited in complete silence waiting for the final member of their group to arrive. All with their own thoughts going through their mind. All with their own problems. Yet all of them were focused on one thing... getting this thing together. They'd been successful doing their own thing thus far, but as much as some of them would hate to admit it. This wasn't going to last very long on their own. There was a hostility building up in the locker room. A few more looks were shot their way in the hall. Conversations were broken when they walked by. Each and every single one of them had seen it in action. Now it was time to do something. They were all able as singles competitors. Every single person could succeed on their own. What made this collection of talent special was that not a single one of them wanted it that way. They all wanted to be together. As much as their personalities may have clashed in the past, in the present, and will in the future... they had become like a dysfunctional family. As they looked across the room at each other in silence they could only sense what the other was thinking. Suddenly the door in the room busted open. They all glared up at The Fearless Phenom as she made her way into the room wearing a black tank top and a pair of blue jeans with rips torn in different places around her legs. With all the eyes focused on her, Vivica stopped in the middle of the room. 'So, what took so long?' Virgil Ambrosi asked, and was shortly given a look by Shadow from across the room. 'I was... picking up something.' Vivica spoke to her friends. Her words fell on mostly deaf ears until behind her a man walked into the room. His long stringy black hair covered most of his face, making the silver collar around his neck almost invisible. A long black trench coar with the collar up was worn over a tight black T-Shirt, and a pair of black jeans and combat boots. Slowly he lifted up his head and looked around the room. His eyes immediately stopped on Lia who looked back at him. 'This is what I was picking up.' Vivica stated, 'His name is Markus Bla...' 'Eaux D'Artifice.' He spoke, his voice was very deep and direct, 'Markus Eaux D'Artifice.' 'Right.' Vivica said still looking around the room a bit uneasy, 'Markus Eaux D'Artifice. I found him participating in Total J Cup in Mexico City. He's supposed to be in Chicago, but like most of us... staying in one place isn't something he's very interested in apparently. He decided to run away and go on a little road trip. That's Virgil Ambrosi and that's Dawn McFarlane. That lady over there... that's Shadow. Her real name is Lia.' Shadow looked back at Markus drumming her pen a little bit on the table. 'I guess in a way, she's your mother.' Everyone else in the room looked confused as Vivica finished her sentance. 'I don't understand.' Markus looked over at Vivica questionably, as if he didn't believe her. 'I'll explain everything later.' Shadow interrupted, 'Right now, we need Alexis to take Markus into the other room for some nourishment. We have something important to discuss. It's time for us all to get on the same page. It's time to take a step foward. It's time to do things right.' ---- Third Passage: Inevitable Conflict ---- The moon was high in the air. So tranquil, so beautiful. It was unfortunately that life was much more complex than that. Just to sit and stare at the moon sometimes all your concerns and fears can go away. Trying desperately to have this happen, Vivica J Valentine looked out the window of her guest room. Christian had called her three times since she'd called him to tell him of the news. He wanted to be the person to introduce Markus to his creator, so to speak... but this was something deep down inside Vivica felt like she had to do. It felt like this was the way it was supposed to go. Things never exactly went that easily, and after all the trouble she'd gone through over the past few days the very last thing she wanted to hear from Christian was a lecture. After almost a week of doing his dirty work for him he still had something to bitch about. So much like men, so predictable. It was probably things like this that drove all the women around her to grow attracted to other women. It was something that Vivica had silently wished so could do. The attraction just wasn't there for her, it just wasn't her. Unfortunately. It almost had to be easier than being engaged to a world famous CEO and World Champion. Suddenly a knock on the door distracted Vivica's attention away from her own throughts as she glanced up at the moon. It was probably Lia wanting to talk about Markus... or Markus wanting to talk about Lia. She didn't think twice before she pulled herself away from the window and sat down at the edge of the bed that had been made for her in the McFarlane Estate. Clearing her thoughts for a completely different topic entirely, looked up at the door before speaking. 'Come on in.' Vivica said, trying her best to put on a friendly face for whomever would be on the otherside. Sure, she hadn't been that friendly to Markus but then again, after all the trouble he put her through why should he. She was beginning to get over it though, and actually began to feel a little sorry for him. When the door opened her friendly smile turned into a look of shock. Virgil Ambrosi walked into the room and sat down in a chair next to the door before closing it. 'What brings you in my room?' Vivica asked him. The usually silent and pessimistic vampire sat across from The Black Mariah, gazing into her eyes before answering. 'You don't even realize what you've done, have you?' Virgil said, quick and to the point. Very frank. It was his style. 'Apparently not.' Vivica responded, beliving this conversation was about the hostility he'd shown her from day one. Perhaps now she was finally going to get some answers. 'You do realize having Markus with you, especially with him being the next descendant from Shadow gives you SOME sort of responsibility don't you?' Virgil asked, 'I mean, you can't just treat him like he's some sort of jackass GCW employee.' 'I was told to go and get him. This is the only way I have to make sure he doesn't run away again. I had to leave immiediately after a fight and drive all the way to Mexico to try and find someone who is supposibly genetically superior than I am.' Vivica said, 'I had to sign up for this silly little hole in the wall tournament just to get into a position where I can see him. It's not like I can just walk up to him and ask him nicely to get into the passenger seat of my Crossfire and return to a testing facility so we can lock you in a room and feed you red liquid from a tube.' 'You don't have to treat him like he is a misbehaving pet. Just because he's wearing a collar like a dog doesn't mean he's your dog.' Virgil said before standing up, 'He's a vampire of Shadow's heritage. Just because you knew him before he became that way does not give you the right to disrespect him.' 'What in the hell do you know about this Virgil?!' Vivica stood before Virgil, not backing down, 'Since when have you taken such a great interest in me. I went out and won the GCW United States Championship and you ignored me. No matter what I've done you havn't given a damn! You havn't even given me a chance. What in the fuck do you know about me?' 'That has nothing to do wi...' 'That has everything to do with this.' Vivica said, 'You just don't fucking understand do you?' Virgil's gaze told the story to Vivica as she suddenly sat back down again. Good job Viv. You've done it again. Of course Virgil understood... once upon a time he was just becoming a vampire too. He wasn't just looking for Markus, he was looking out for Vivica too. He would know what might happen if she kept this up. 'No, you don't understand, that's the problem.' Virgil Ambrosi said heading towards the door, 'At WorldWide I'm on your side Vivica. You have a lot of growing up to do little girl. I think now is the best time for you to start. For your own good.' Vivica put her head in her hands as Virgil Ambrosi closed the door behind him. She could almost hear him mutter 'Stupid mortal.' as he walked down the hallway. Nothing she could ever do would please that vampire. Perhaps everything wasn't his fault, perhaps everything wasn't her fault. Perhaps the blame could be placed squarely on both people. Maybe instead of looking for Virgil to be happy about her accomplishments, she should be concentrating on helping others with their goals. It's harder for some to realize their dreams than others. Sometimes certain people... just need a little help. ---- Fourth Passage: What Lurks In The Shadows ---- A beautiful sunset over the skyline of Miami, Florida. It looked as if it belonged on a postcard. Often times these are things that Kodak moments are made from. This time the setting of the sun signaled the beginning instead of the end. The beginning of GCW WorldWide #44. The parking lot began to fill as Toad looked around. He could already hear the music inside playing, keeping the patrons entertained until showtime which was still over an hour away. A pair of headlights caught his attention coming into talent parking. It appeared to be some sort of... Humane Society Vehicle? Quickly one of his attendants stopped them. What a good force he had, he thought to himself seconds before he saw his guard let the truck through in disbelief. If you want something done right you have to do it yourself. Some lessons you just seem to learn over and over again. Quickly Toad began to make his way towards the huge truck a few yards away, trying quickly to stop the driver before they exited the vehicle. Unfortunately for him, the door opened before his arrival... and down jumped a beautiful blonde girl dressed in all black. Her blonde hair with baby blue tipes waved in the Florida breeze when she slammed the door behind her. With a black cargo bag over her left shoulder, a pair of gorgeous teal blue eyes gazed back at him. How could something so beautiful be so annoying? 'I thought they kept the dogs in the back.' Toad said as he approached Vivica J. Valentine. Finally refreshed from her trip across the border. 'They do.' She said sarcastically with a bit of an attitude, 'I have a cage in the back ready to carry Eva away from the arena tonight after the ass kicking I didn't get to finish last week. Speaking of dogs, who in the hell asked you anything frog? If I wanted you to speak I'd wave a snausage over your nose and command you to.' 'Heh...' Toad shook his head in disbelief, she sure had a big mouth for a rookie, 'No wonder everyone confuses you for a second rate Eva VanLorne. You can't even insult people as good as she can.' GCW's Head of Security could almost see the fire ignite in Vivica J Valentine's eyes before she dropped her bag and leaped towards him. Seconds before impact suddenly she was grabbed and pulled away. Mobbed by the GCW Security Force... just in the nick of time. His force wasn't so bad afterall. At least they were good at taking out the trash, in his opinion anyway. 'Get off me!' Vivica screamed trying to squirm free, 'Do you know who in the hell I am?! I'm the GCW United States Champion!' 'Take her inside and lock her in her cage boys.' Toad said smugly, 'If you even think of laying a finger on me ever again, I'll make sure you never step foot in a WorldWide arena again. I've had about enough of you. We have a hard enough job as it is. The last think we need is to babysit you all the time.' 'Come say that to my face jobber!' Vivica screamed as one of the security guards picked her cargo bag up off the parking lot and followed his friends, carrying a kicking and screaming Black Mariah into the arena. Toad simply shook his head. He never understood rookies. She'll learn eventually. They all learn. Looking up at the Animal Control truck behind him he began to become curious. Why did she drive this piece of crap here anyway? THUNK! With that noise the truck moved back and forth. She had something in there? What could she possibly want to bring to the arena in one of those? A puppy? Toad walked around the truck slowly towards the back. Against the side of the truck the sun gleamed, making him squint as he turned the corner. On the back were two handles and heavily black tinted windows. Reaching up he grabbed the levers... it was unlocked. Pulling down on the handles he opened the back of the truck. The inside was lined with cages... all of which appeared to be empty. Yet due to the sun, he couldn't see the back right corner of the truck, concealed in darkness. There must be something back there. Listening carefully he could hear something breathing. Something was in the truck with him. Whatever it was... didn't seem very pleased about his inspection. Thump thump... Thump thump... Thump thump... It could only be a dog. Right? As he moved towards the corner, he could see a pair of eyes shining back at him. They were big and round... they almost looked, human. Either that or a really big dog. It was a dog. That's what he kept saying to himself over and over again as he reached around the ceiling of the back, looking for a lightswitch. Over in the corner he could feel something watching his every move. All he could hear was the sound of his own heart beating. Thump thump... Thump thump... Thump thump... His hand hit something on the ceiling. Hopefully a light switch. It was what he was looking for, but he hesitated. Was he ready to see what was really in that corner? Every instinct in his body told him to get the hell out of that truck. His brain had to know. What could she possibly be keeping in the back of the truck? Vivica didn't even have any pets. Vivica didn't even LIKE pets. At least, he never pegged her to. With a deep breath and shaking hands Toad ignited the back with a flip of a switch. RAWRRR!!!! Toad fell over shaking with the sound of an animal enraged. Heavily breathing he looked over in the corner and saw a Doberman barring it's teeth. It was a dog afterall. What in the world was he thinking? Of course it was a dog. What in the world did he think it was? A monster? With a cocky laugh Toad listened to the dog bark it's head off while he slammed the door shut. How immature. There are no such things as monsters. Are there?
