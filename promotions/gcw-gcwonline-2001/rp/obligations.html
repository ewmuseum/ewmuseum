Obligations

Date: Early January, 2008 "Its never gonna heal if you dont stop picking at it." She was right. He had some idea that she was right. It didn't stop him. "I think Rich Rollins hates me." A stitch tugged against his forehead; he looked to the dried black crescents under his fingernails and sucked air. "No one hates you." "Hah. Shh. Lots of people hate me. Rollins, Caldera. Others." "Well. The hell with them." "Yeah. Yeah. For all the good itll do me; the hell with em." She pressed her chest to the back of his neck; she rested her arms down his shoulders. He stared through sliding glass doors in the indirect and unreliable pulsing glow of a cigarette. "You looked good out there." He laughed. He'd laughed with every consolation she'd tried on him; all in his ribs, just a vibration by the time they'd reached his lips. "One dude aint got shit on, I aint got shit on real hatred. Fuckin. Fuck. Theyre all fake and Im all fake, and its all some insincere bullshit up until that one angry motherfucker." "Youre not fake, Jordan. Come on." He shut up. His point had not made it across. She kept leaving and leaving him alone. He soaked up the motel television and kept himself just outside of total sedation. Sage had sent him two DVDs of the show; he assumed one was meant to be broken in a rage and played his part before he settled in to watch through. He shook his head and kept mumbling to himself until his phone demanded attention. "Frank." "Sage." "Didnt know you and old Trey Tremendous were buddies." "Cause we aint." "Not known Rollins to grasp at but so short of straws. Have you talked to Trey?" "Once’other than when the three of us met up in Vegas.." "What about?" "Shit. And here I was thinkin you were gonna bitch me out for the match." "If theres time, maybe. Its a lot of disappointment to try and cover with one call." "Man. You jock the other Jordans like this?" "Just one. And I think she likes it a lot more than you do. What did you tell Trey?" "Just bullshitted him around some. He was askin about you more than me." "And, did you tell him anything? Anything, at all?" "Yeah. Just shit about you givin me advice, nothin major I mean." "Fucking asshole, Frank. Trey is, you cant trust Trey. Trey is very much of crazy. How did you get in touch with him in the first place?" "He called me up. Few weeks back." "Because?" "Said he was writin a book." "O fuck. Documentation of things you said about me and my company. Did you not consider how this could be a bad idea?" "I did." "And?" "Just thought I was bein too paranoid." "You cant be too paranoid, Frank. Paranoia keeps us safe." "I bet it does." "Okay. Look, Frank, I dont think you know enough to fuck me over’no matter how stupid of decisions you make’but, still. I want you to listen to this and remember this: keep your fucking mouth shut until you know what youre gonna say. If someone comes around asking about me or my company, I want your ass to be a press release. You hear me?" "Yeah." "They ask about income, you start talking about the new line of winter wear. They pry about my personal life, give em a history of my career. They want to know something about contracts, you tell them how were renovating inner city parks nationwide. You ignore every issue they bring up’you got that?" "I got it." "And dont, dont, dont talk to Trey or Sobias." "Sobias? Torm?" "He and Trey are, they respect each other. Sobias doesnt know much about how I run things, and I dont want him to know, and I dont like anything about this. If youve got questions on anything, dont talk to anyone other than me or Danielle." "D? I thought you two were, yknow." "She. She accepted a high ranking position within the organization." "Oh." "And dont talk about that with anyone, either." "So I just, sit and smile? Wave at anybody wants to ask questions?" "Catching on quick." "Want me to let you know if Trey tries to get up with me again?" "I want to know before hes off the phone, kid." "Word." He lit a cigarette. "Wait, Sobias? How you keep your lawyer in the dark on how the company go?" "By having other lawyers that actually do things for me. Torm being hired is, its more of a necessity for me than JXW, if you understand that. I want him where I can watch him." "Friends close, enemies closer? That shit?" "Yes. That shit." "Why not hire Trey then?" "Treys a lot more dangerous than Torm. Trey, he. He frightens me, Frank. Hes smart enough and hes got all the reason in the world to want me ruined. Sobias seems content so long as Im giving back more than Im fucking up. I fucked over Trey. I dont need him around to remind me of it, and I dont want him near enough that revenge is just a matter of reaching over." "He dont seem that, uh, that deadbent on gettin you back." "Yeah. Trey is good at hiding things." "So, dont talk to anybody about anything; cept you and your chicky." "Except me and Danielle." "That, then. Fuck you at, anyways?" "Out of the country. Doesnt matter." "What about Rollins, then?" "What about him? Hes some wrestler. Hes not going to fuck my day up." "I mean, in wrestlin. Not talkin about the company right now." "Oh, that? Yeah, look’really, I was just pretending to care. Youre still turning a profit and. Im going to be busy as fuck for the next month, so do whatever you want with that whole thing. Go wild. Show me what you can do when I take the reins off." "No suggestions?" "Same as they ever were: dont take this bullshit they put up against you personally. Its just a job. It doesnt define you." And Jordan laughed at how much advice he'd been taking from a man whose major successes were all outside of a wrestling ring. She got coffee from the lobby downstairs and brought it up to him. He was on his way out of the bathroom and had just dried off from a conversation with himself. The monodialogue had lead him nowhere. He stared at his own reflection. She crossed a knee and sat on the bed. "How you holding up?" "Eh." "Eh?" "Eh." "Go any deeper than that?" He stretched a white shirt over his head and pulled it down, straightening it out where it clung to him. "Yeah." "God, Jordan. You make me ask way too many questions before I get a decent answer." "Not, uh. Not real precise questions. You know?" "Into it." "Huh?" He turned. "Intuit. Think about it." "And guess what the question you really wanna ask is?" "Yep." He pulled his hair back from his eyes and scanned the expanse of scabs and the negative space that would be scar tissue. Given time. "That case: I feel like shit; Im not over the L; my legs are fuckin killin me. And I cant get over that, in all of this shit, I dont stand for the first damned thing. I dont mean shit. I dont inspire shit. I just. Wrestle." He breathed, something like a one note chuckle. "I mean, I mean. Rollins is an old guy that cant quit playin a teenager but, shit. At least when you see him you think of somethin bigger than any one guy. Same for Knight, same for Lia, and Terror and Viv and, Steve and everybody else there. Theyre the young fightin the old to take their spot, or theyre the system fightin chaos or, or, or whatever and, shit. End of the day, Im just some cat fightin other cats. Theyre fightin for somethin out there; Im just fightin. Fuckin. Fuckin hollow. You know?" "Some cat?" "Hah. Sorry. Sage, always calls people cats. Mustve picked it up." "No its, cute." He lifted his coffee and let the steam clear his head out before taking a swallow. "That what you wanted to hear?" She nodded her chin into her collarbones and shrank as she slid to brace her back against the headboard. "I got plenty of it. Fuck, I havent really whined in forever. I could bitch for days if someone started me and didnt finish." "Well, um. Is there anything else you wanna get off your chest?" "Huh. Hah. I was thinkin earlier and I realized, I wasnt thinkin about me. I was thinkin about Jordan White, and it was all 'him' this and 'he' that. 'He wasnt mad enough to win.' 'It doesnt look like he cares out there.' Shit like that." "Third person, huh?" "Yeah, yeah. I was lookin in on this dude that looks like me and sounds like me, but just isnt me. Shit. I dont even think this Jordan White motherfuckers real." "Oh. He seems pretty real to me." "Hah. Maybe, I dunno. Your ass wont call me Frank, so of course it aint gonna seem weird to you. But. Alright. How you think youd feel if you went on stage every week to everybody calling you Sally Struthers or some shit like that? Its just, its just weird as hell. It starts to mess with how you think about yourself, makes you start to wonder if theres a yourself in there in the first place or if. Youre just a body that people are gonna identify however they want’and to fuck with what you think of yourself. Thats all." "Okay. So wrestling fucks with you." "Not the wrestlin. I can do that’most the time. Its the everythin else that gets to me." Jordan sat at the table. Erika, opposite. "Your mom was sweet." "Thats moms for you." "Not mine." "Well. Thats my mom dukes for you." She nursed a half can of Coke. His shins banged into the edge standing up; his face twisted around some knotting focal point. Erika laughed. She sprayed a fine mist from her lips and laughed again at that. "Sorry. Hee. We going back to Charlotte anytime soon?" "How long you think youre gonna follow me around the country?" "You kicking me out?" "Not right now. Nah." "Then its gonna be awhile." To the refrigerator. He put his back to her and crouched on a deep inhale. His breath hung mist in front of him; he shook his head. "Crazy bitch." "What?" His head swiveled. "Called you a crazy bitch." Her palm twisted up, her neck leaned to the side. She shrugged. "Sometimes. You got a problem?" "Nah. Cant have a problem with every crazy bitch in the world." "What you looking for in there anyways?" "Somethin to eat." "Still some sandwich meat, isnt there?" "Too many sandwiches. Lookin for real food." "Oh. Yeah. None of that." His phone purred against the table, slid lateral. "Whos it?" "Uh," lifting it. "Ashtin?" "Fuckin. I gotta take this." "Alley oop." He popped to his feet and went sideways to snatch her throw, flipped his phone open and held it to his ear. He rode the motion to the glass against the balcony. "Whats good?" "I thought you just werent going to answer me anymore." "Nah, nothin like that. Shit just went dead on me for awhile." "Uh huh. And where have you been since, I dont know, before Christmas?" "Was gettin ready for NC-17 before, had to run New Years with the fam afterwards. I been around, I just been busy." "Too busy to call?" "Swhat it looks like, isnt it?" "Fuck you." "Hey." "Fuck hey. Fuck you, Frank. Where the fuck are you?" "In the country. Doesnt matter.” "Frank." "Texas." "Christ. You come back to town and dont even let me know before youre out again? What the fuck is your problem?" "Wasnt around long." "Whatever. Look. Just call me when you come back. Ive got something for you, and I dont want to mail it." "So. Just hit you up?" "Yes." "Aight then. Peace." She hung up. Jordan rolled his shoulders and went back to Erika. "Oh yeah. Your girl." "For now. Sorta. Shit. Just laugh about it. Best way to handle it." He found his seat again. He picked up a box of cigarettes and flipped back the lid; he grimaced and let them drop again. The clock ticked behind. He weighed his thoughts down to the gram, cut the excess until they weighed no more and no less. The clock kept ticking. "What was your major?" "Hnh?" "Your mom told me you went to UNC. Whatd you major in?" "Droppin out. Let it go." "That was. Defensive." "Because Im not gonna talk about it or pretend like Im goin to." "And thats that?" "Yeah." It was the TV that finally forced him outside’not to blame the messenger. He squinted hard against the light, tensed his body up and dug hands deep into lined jacket pockets to put a steady front against the wind. The world’washed out, faded’told him more stories than he felt like parsing so he took them in as a whole; one single, indivisible body. The gestalt of: the faint smell of sulfur caught on an errant wind; the whine and roar of a million cumulative RPM; the out of place chirp of some winter flock above; the drift of conversation; the flavor of his own mucus cracking in the back of his throat. He thought of the Youtube famous and the music industry rich and, putting himself firmly in between, staggered into the world with head high and feet dragging. Another motel. He'd gotten used to the easy access, the lack of hallways, the astroturf glued to the walkways and burned away by years of cigarette butts. He pulled his phone out on his way down the steps, took a picture of a wall of graffiti he'd mentioned or eyed with every pass; he made plans to decipher it later or, failing that, just admire the way the green and the red clashed in a war of contours against angles. He took his time getting to the car; she would still be asleep whenever he got back, a casualty to trying to follow Jordan's increasingly bizarre sleep schedule. Time wasted; his head turned to all those hours he'd burned through by staying a little too unconscious for a little too long. He consoled himself with "okay, one more cig" and one more cigarette that came to a full burn as he flipped the ignition and listened to the engine turn. "I been wastin my life." It almost rose at the end, almost became a question and unquestionably so; but he didn't have quite the pathos, quite the dedication, quite the confusion over the matter to ask anymore than say’or vice versa. He didn't fully appreciate his stance’mutable though it was, adaptive though it was, multiple choice though it was. Uncomfortable, he turned the stereo on and up. The speakers rattled in their frames, knocked the trunk until he could be certain that he wouldn't hear himself. In reverse, in drive; he snaked his way out onto the streets and set himself to idle through the concrete of myths he'd never hear. Stories about the larger than life, the eight foot tall that settled this nationstate against the tyranny of some forgotten hand’with no mind to the slavery they fought to protect against Mexico, just to the fact that they fought. If he focused, really focused, he could feel the slip of those legends, glass and sand, shifting beneath the asphalt. Just a foundation, just towering ghosts formed in the absent space in the gut of a roaming dust. Something in the orange glare against his dirtsmeared windshield. Something in his eye, maybe. He winced. "They dont mean anything." Her words, lighter than the breeze, rode intermittent gusts down to Jordan’he looked to find her frail, paling into a paler day. "Ive been thinking, about what you said and: those wrestlers, they dont mean anything. They dont stand for anything, really. Theyre all just grownups in tights, fighting over silly things." He went lax, traced shrinking circles on the arm of his chair with the filter of a cigarette and fell deeper into an allencompassing hoodie. "You said it yourself, about Rollins. Hes some old guy that wishes he was still young and wishes he was still, you know, relevant. Just because he wants to be anarchy, it doesnt mean hes not just an old creep. Its just how you look at them that make them like, mean something. They can be anything you want and youre all, building them up into big, scary bastards when theyre really not." The air rolled against his chapped lips and his drying skin, somersaulting to knock the ash off his cigarette and into a lap already smeared with streaks that fade in stripes of whiter gray and grayer white. "I dont think they are, at least. They just look like people to me. I dont know. Maybe Im wrong. Maybe they are all like, the living embodiments of a bunch of stuff bigger than us and maybe its all human drama writ large; I just, I think its like boxing and all of that is just excuses to fight each other. I think youre beating yourself more than anyone else is beating you. You said it, that youre all just empty until someone gives you some meaning that only even matters to them. And I seriously could be wrong about this but I dont think I am and that means his image kicked your ass." "Its one hell of an image, girl." Her lips cut a sharp angle up and right, a hard line smirk that drifted from frivolous to shrewd; it then drifted away. "Im just saying. You dont represent anything, you dont have to. Youre free, Jordan. Most people arent." "Freedom, uh. Freedom aint winnin matches." "No. But it can do amazing things."
