On the road again

15 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15-9-2005 A picturesque day in the “Melting Pot” of the United States of America otherwise known as New York City. Today is a day that a lot of people were waiting for because of the fact that it had been raining its ass off for the past couple of days and now they get to go out and do the errands that they put off because mother nature decided that it would be best to dump about 3 inches of rain down on them all! One of the big reasons that today was long awaited was simple: The “Army of Old School” was out and about getting ready to debut yet again in a fed that had “Global” and “Wrestling” in it but this time with “Championship” in its name! That’s right the AoOS is going to GCW in search of a title to help cement them as one of the dominant stables of the past 5 years or so. Its truly a rarity to find a collection of talent in wrestling like the unofficial leader of the “Army” ,Joshua L. Curtis has found. The 26 year old leader of the stable searched far and wide in countries that some don’t look for the talent and even tapped the “boundaries” of “sexual preference”! When some people went and got rid of talent for this reason or that reason Joshua went a picked them up promising not so much riches as exposure and some of the wrestlers that he has already teamed or just seen when he ran the “IWF” were some of the most impressive that anyone had ever laid eyes on and Joshua knew that they would make it someday either with him as a manager or in the right fed in the right situation could become all-time greats! Why is the group loved in NYC? Well think about it. New York City has a diverse population that transcends gender ,race ,religion ,creed and sexuality to the point where you can be accepted and no one judges you. If a guy is seen kissing another guy or a woman is doing likewise to another woman they would be crucified or condemned in other states but not here! Here they live ,work and play without anyone questioning what they are doing and why they are doing it. Here they help the community out as a whole and the citizens in the city and the whole state embrace the faction as a natural part of life and many people have since been heard saying that if the faction ever left New York for good life would never be the same! The impact is no less great on this day as Joshua Curtis ,Ami Mizuno ,Quatre Winner and Zack Johnson are seen taking a cab to Central Park and “Q” ,as his friends call him says something to the Cabbie in Arabic and the quartet pile into the cab with Quatre riding shotgun talking to the cab driver about how he was recruited to be the manager of “Bloodlines” and about how the ownership for the most part seems to like the duo without wrestling a match and the cabbie smiles as he turns and faces the trio in the back of the cab before speaking and this time in English! “You made a wise choice Mr. Curtis in asking Quatre to be your manager. I understand that Quatre had to hand control of his corporation over to his sister because of the fact that he was going to be handling your business affairs such as scheduling interviews and what not. What are your thoughts on entering the company with a big show coming up and all.” The cabbie asks Joshua and he looks at the cab driver with a somewhat concerned look on his face as he tries to figure out exactly what he was going to say. “The main thing Ami and myself have to do is get some momentum built up for the faction at A show. I’m not looking for this show to be “The One” but I think if you give us and the faction as a whole enough time I think we will truly dominate GCW and make our name in the federation.” Joshua said and Ami decided to jump in on the conversation. “My brother is a very selfless man. He lives to help others out and help other feds become players when they otherwise might not truly become “powers” in the industry. I’ve seen feds where champions show how much they hide behind rankings and will only wrestle the top contenders but I can tell you there were places such as Joshua’s fed where a wrestler got a chance to prove their worth and could tell when a person was giving him bull or was legit with the reason that they couldn’t show up. Joshua helped wrestlers who were struggling to get notice and made them people that fans loved to see and that is why I think his contributions were valuable to the evolution of the IWF when it was an infant.” Ami said and was followed by the angelic sound of Zack’s voice. “What Ami says is true sir. Joshua was a darling to take me under his wing when I was starting out and even when he found out about my secret he still tutored me even though at the time he was a little uncomfortable with it and it kind of showed as he hardly ever made eye contact with me back then.” Zack said and that was met with a curious glance by his slightly younger boyfriend. “I was that frightened by homosexuality back then” I could have sworn when you told me about your secret I was cool with it.” Joshua said and Ami was just as quick to speak up before anything got started. “Josh you were so scared to be near him that you told security that you didn’t want a f***ot anywhere near you or you would sue the fed for allowing him to be a student of yours.” Ami said and Joshua’s face turned ghost white when he heard the revelation but by that time they had reached Central Park where some friends were awaiting their arrival. Quatre’s boyfriend in Trowa Barton had been waiting for the arrival of his Arabian boyfriend and music partner as the crowd was getting ready for a big show that certain members of the “Army of Old School” were to be a part of and this is when Joshua REALLY shines! While Joshua’s skills in and out of the ring are certified legendary his music skills are such that people admire and respect what he does when he does covers of songs that he likes. His range goes from rock to country to pop and even recently “Goth” Style music which one of his confidants in Duo Maxwell turned him onto and believe it or not he’s better for it! He uses different songs to try and get in the head of opponents and when you hear some of the songs you can tell that the opponent gets a little nervous about whom they are facing. Are they facing a man whom is certified whacko or a man that has more common sense than a lot of guys that you meet on the street nowadays? It’s always quite the question. Today as judged by the dress that Joshua ,Zack and Ami picked out it’s a bit of a “Hodge Podge” kind of day as they have brought different attires for what is a monthly concert that they do in the park for citizens and sometimes they do it for fundraisers and in many respects despite the dress they ARE there to do their part to raise money for the Hurricane Katrina Funds and some of the songs they did helped reflect the mood but they also wanted to keep it upbeat and positive as it wasn’t too long ago that their home in New York City had suffered a catastrophe in 9-11 and they wanted their spirits to be listed as much as money raised and that is what they do for their people. Some of the songs they did included: “See Who I am” - Within Temptation (The theme song of Bloodlines btw) “Desert Rose - Sting (The song was a duet with Joshua and Quatre with Joshua actually singing the Arabic and Quat singing in English) “Wing and Epyon” “Memories” - Within Temptation “Violin Medley” - Michiru and Quatre “Sparkling Melody” - Trowa ,Quatre and Joshua ( Joshua was a request by Quatre on the drums) And many others that people loved and believe me when I say “One Day ,One Dream” got the crowd going until Joshua told the crowd to rise and the opening to “Running up that Hill” began. When that song began you could hear the cheers deep into the night and when the song and concert ended Joshua and the rest of the band stayed and signed autographs as the people’s smiles shone brighter than any shooting star in the heaven’s and Joshua knew as well as the rest of the faction that in time they would be the voice of the people and when they hit the road people would cheer them as loudly as they did that night because the “Army” wasn’t a stable that promoted gang warfare but rather a family atmosphere and that is more important to them now than any title or award could EVER be!!! Good Night and God Bless?
