The Payoff

23 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 23, 2007 They just don’t realize what they’re getting themselves into. Location: Bank Of America ? Las Vegas, Nevada Time: 8:45 AM Branson King’s long black Cadillac limousine is slowly creeping through a crowded parking lot in the outskirts of Las Vegas, Nevada. The vehicle comes to a halt in front of a bright red, white, and blue sign that reads “Bank Of America.” The driver hops out of the car and quickly walks to the back of the car to open the door for King. Branson steps out with his cellular phone pressed to his ear. He straightens his golden silk tie, and nods to the limousine driver to let him know he will be right back. “Don’t worry, I’m taking care of that right now,” he says with a smirk on his face. “Pretty soon Chris Storm and Markus Stone will realize that this isn’t one of their little games, it’s serious.” With that said Branson closes his phone and slides the device into the pocket of his sports jacket. He begins to strut up the beautiful stone steps of the bank and into the building looking cockier than he ever has. It’s obvious he has big plans for today. “Good morning Mr. King!” The bank President is locking the vault beside the offices and making sure the security alarm is set again. When he finishes he walks over to Branson with a huge smile on his face greeting him very warmly. “It’s so nice to see you on this fine day.” Branson shakes the President’s hand that he has stuck towards him and returns a smile. “So Mr. King, how can I be of service to you this morning?” he quickly asked. “Why don’t we step into your office so we can have a little privacy” You know me, I never like to air out dirty laundry? not before it’s beneficial to me anyway.” Fredrick Franklin, the President of the bank, lets out a little chuckle, but knows that Branson meant every word of what he just said. Fredrick nods and the two men begin walking past the Christmas decorations which are strewn about the building. They come to a large office with several plaques and awards stuck onto the walls. Fredrick allows Branson to walk in first before shutting the door and taking a seat behind his desk. “Now Mr. King, what do you have on your mind?” Franklin asked. “Well Fredrick, I need to make a small withdrawal out of my account.” Branson replied. The bank President leans back in his chair and smirks. “Somehow I don’t think this is going to be as small as you’re trying to make it sound.” A grin came over Branson’s face that would make the Grinch look like and angel on Christmas morning. “Let’s just get this over with, I have a lot to do today,” he replied. ---- Location: McCarran International Airport ? Las Vegas, Nevada Time: 11:30 AM Branson is walking through the long corridor at McCarran airport trying to dodge holiday travelers from ruining his white Armani suit. Besides, he despises the everyday working class of people; just a bunch of ignorant rednecks blowing his tax money. “BRANSON KING! BRANSON KING!” Branson stops and slowly turns around looking behind himself. A young pimpled face child is running up to him smiling with a pad and Sharpie pen in his hand. He stops right in front of King and looks up at him with a gaze of amazement. “Can I have your autograph?” he asked with hope. Branson looked at the kid for a moment before turning and walking away. “You suck anyway! I can’t wait to see Banned & Exiled~! beat you and the rest of the Establishment down!” the kid shouted. Branson quickly stopped and turned back to face the child. “Little kids like you shouldn’t stray from their parents in a place like this” they could get hurt.” The boy’s eyes grew very large with fear and concern when he turned and ran off through the airport screaming. Branson kept walking to the ticket counter and mumbling under his breath. “I suck” What an idiot?” As Branson made it to the ticket counter, the lady smiled at him strongly and spoke with a polite, but stern voice. “Do you have any luggage with you, sir?” she kindly asked. “No.” he replied. “I won’t be staying very long, just tending to a little business.” “Very well then, shall we be going first class?” “I shall.” Branson replied. “And where will you be flying first class to?” Branson gazed at the smiling woman and couldn’t help but grin. “Boston, Massachusetts.” ---- Location: Boston Logan International Airport ? Boston Massachusetts Time: 4:30 PM Branson struts off of the airplane only to be greeted by a limousine driver holding a sign with his name on it. He walks up to the gentleman and looks him over. “You’re the best your company has to offer?” he asked. The short heavy set man replied, “Yes sir, I’m the owner of the company.” “The owner, you say?” “Yes sir, I’m here to take care of you the best way possible.” He stated. “I guess you will do, I don’t have the time to waste waiting on someone else.” “Thank you Mr. King, follow me and I’ll escort you to the vehicle” the driver said. The two men casually walked out of the crowded airport and the stout man opened the back door to the limousine to let Branson get in. He cautiously closed it, then walked to the driver’s door and crawled in. “Where to Mr. King?” he asked. ---- Location: Somewhere in Boston Time: 5:30 PM The limousine pulls to a stop in front of a large house in a rural neighborhood. The back window begins to roll down and suddenly stops halfway. Branson’s hand reaches out, opens the mailbox and slides a very plump envelope into it. He closes the door to the mailbox and begins to roll his window back up as the limousine speeds off into the distance. On the mailbox are the letters “WINDSOR.”
