It's finals week. I'm allowed to suck.

10 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/10/06 With their hectic schedules consisting of hours of training, promotional appearances, and of course travel, it had been a while since the pair of Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor had been able to sit down and enjoy a nice, quiet dinner together. Given that nowadays, she’d much more to worry about than just a paycheck, Tessa had missed moments like this, where she and Chris could take time out of the day for each other without having to worry about their hectic lives. It certainly did not hurt any that despite being on and off again for five years now, they’d only found the time for one other vacation, and that’d been four years back. Tessa never ceased to be amazed at how much Chris had changed since he’d almost lost her heart just a year back. All of a sudden, she mattered more than anything in his life, even wrestling. As a matter of fact, it was Chris who had surprised her with the plane tickets, after she’d lost her Cruiserweight Title to Blaise McClancy. While in her mind the loss was devastating, Tessa certainly would not argue with that. Not when Chris was willing to take a vacation from his rigorous six-day-a-week training schedule. And now, there was no Blaise McClancy. There were no Ascension members summoning Tessa for advice. No fans pestering for an autograph. Or any kids for that matter, too. It was just the two, enjoying their long overdue vacation. “I can’t believe we actually managed to get here, alone at that.” Her candle lit eyes brightened as she looked across to her partner in crime, with her lips curled upright. Returning a smile, Chris couldn’t help but be amazed himself. He thought for sure she would have found a way to weasel out of this one, as she had everyone of his attempts for the past year. Lifting his glass of red wine, he proposed, “To a change in the times.” Tessa smiled back and tipped his glass with hers. She knew what he meant. For the two of them, things were changing faster than they could realize. The UWF, a place both had called home for the past year (and longer for Chris), had closed. Tessa had sought shelter in GCW a few months back when it had been announced that the UWF was financially unstable. Chris, on the other hand, had been left without a paycheck. Tilting her head up into a nod, she tapped her glass with his, and pulled away with a solitary thought regarding his toast, “And what kind of change would you be making?” His usual withdrawn self, Chris shrugged his shoulders before sipping at his wine. “Haven’t really thought about it,” His eyes moved slowly moved away from his glass back down towards her to study her reaction to his next statement, “GCW faxed me a contract.” As expected, her eyes glittered, and she’d perked up even further in her chair. “Are you going to take it?” As much success as she’d had on the road alone, she would have easily given it up for the chance to have his company on the road, “I mean, we’d never have to worry about having to make time for each other if you did.” “I’m not sure if I’m going to take it.” Chris could instantly see the disappointment in Tessa’s face. “But it’s not because of you or anything. I’m just worried that if I see you in a match with anyone and suddenly they decide to be underhanded that I might run down to the ring and punch their face in. I’m that kind of guy, you know.” Chris made sure to add in one other part, “Besides, you are doing well enough on your own. You don’t need me in there hogging your entire spotlight. You deserve a chance to shine on your own as well.” “Yeah, I guess so”.” As much as she’d heard the same kind of encouragements from others as well, the disappointment still broke though in the form of a sigh, “Plus I’m sure others feel the same way about you?” But there were still plenty of other things she could try and look forward to, such as where he’d actually end up, “So then, where are you going?” “Legacy of Champions,” Chris replied, waiting to gauge Tessa’s reaction. By the way her eyebrows wrinkled, Chris could tell the words almost seemed foreign to her. “It’s based out of Miami, which means when you come to visit me backstage it will be a great excuse to get you into a bikini.” Tessa rolled her eyes, taking a sip from her wine glass in the process, “you cad.” She gave him a playful tap on the shoulder and smiled. “Well that’s good; you won’t have much down time.” “No,” Chris said, his eyes shifting to the side, “but they stuck me on the Underground show. Not my style.” Tessa frowned, in attempts to reassure Chris that she knew what he meant, before offering her defense. “But then you could show everyone that you’re more than just a one trick pony; that you can do more than mat wrestle.” Chris purposely frowned, and offered a mocking pout, “but I like being a one-trick pony.” It sounded all too familiar to the woman beside him. After all, the words had come from her mouth many a time when she begged him to come back to her side in the ring. Then of course, as per his usual self, he returned back to his natural business demeanor the minute it seemed she were getting too into the act,” Besides, I don’t want to have to change who I am. I did that in Rush Pro, and I swore never to again.” The comment alone had cause Tessa to shrink back in her seat as well. After all, given that he had nearly had her killed off during those days, she had more than a handful of reasons to shudder towards the mention company, “I’d rather not be reminded, as I didn’t exactly make the brightest choice there myself.” “High-five to bad decisions!” Chris shouted before raising his hand over the table. Rather than reach out, she gave her eyes another roll, this time in the opposite direction. Of all the things he could make humor of, it just had to be that. “I’d rather a walk on the beach.” Chris cocked up his eyebrow, “who says I don’t have that planned later?” He could see her eyes light up, so Chris raised his hand for the check. “Because that’d be too good.” She responded, slinging her multicolored branded Coach bag over her shoulder. Looking back his way with a slight grin, she questioned his motives once more, “So what are you really up to?” “Just a little spot I noticed earlier,” he said, with his trademark grin that signified that he indeed was up to something. Before Tessa had a chance to object, the bill had arrived, and Chris in return had slapped down a few bills from his wallet. “Where about?” As if he’d answer that. Instead he hooked his arm around hers, and hauled her out of the cabana back towards the beach. Twisting and moving around the lush scenery, the two soon moved off the paved path. As they stepped over bushes and across rocks, Tessa started to get a bit worried. “Where are we going?” Still though, Chris remained silent. Further and further they moved away from the resort, which left Tessa more anxious with each step. She was about to press Chris to go back, when he finally stopped, plopping himself on boulder that easily weighed three times more than his own bulky mass. Rather than join Chris, Tessa found herself fixated by the nearby waterfall whose beauty, just as her own, was illuminated by the light of the moon. While she’d been to Hawaii a handful of times while on tour with one promotion or another, she’d never once got enjoy in the company of another (that she actually cared for). “It’s a shame that after all the time we’ve known each other; we’re finally now getting to enjoy things like this.” Chris could only nod his head in agreement. He was at a lost of words to actually speak, taken in by her beauty. His hand slipped down into his pocket and nervously fidgeted around. Sliding in beside him, disregarding any blemish on the rock that could easily ruin her cocktail dress, she plopped her head on his shoulder, and innocently wondered aloud, “Just wish we had more time for moments like this, I don’t know how it could get any better.” “Oh, I have an idea,” Chris chuckled to himself, pulling the small black box out of his pocket. Keeping it behind his back, he waited for Tessa to turn her head away before presenting it before her. “Tessa, I have a question for you.” Turning to acknowledge him, Tessa nearly bumped the box with her nose. Quite obviously by the gape in her mouth that wouldn’t close (despite numerous attempts), she knew very well what he was going to say. And she also knew very well how she’d answer, despite the fact that the words didn’t want to come out. “You can’t ask me right now!” Were the first words to escape from her mouth after her brief lack of air. Certainly not what he was expecting after near five years of perusing her, still, it seemed totally predictable given how their relationship worked. “People are going to speculate unless there are others around.” “Then let them speculate,” He mischievously grinned, as he gently pushed her head aside, so that he could proceed onward with his own mission, “because it’s none of their business to begin with. This is about me and you now.” Chris titled his head to the side, taking the ring out from the box now. With his free hand he took hold of her hand and knelt down in front of her. “So, what do you say” Will you marry me?” For once her head and her heart were in agreement, yet they still couldn’t formulate anything more than a slight pause as she looked down at the diamond, then back up at into her partner’s eyes which begged for her to make the right choice, “Of course. I’d be a fool to pass it up again.” Overcome by joy, Chris swiftly scooped her off her feet and spun her around in his arms, pausing for a long and passionate kiss. His heart wouldn’t stop beating at a million miles per hour, nor would her cheeks return from its deep red hue. They were in love and every ounce of their being showed it. Even once back at their hotel room, the two could not stop beaming with joy. Flopping down ass first onto the king sized mattress, the pint sized diva had found cloud nine, and planned to float far away from her problems back in GCW. Sure, she had five (six if you included Brack) others depending on her this week, to pull the Ascension together, but at this point in her career, she’d gotten the point where she felt she deserved this kind of reward. “I vote we fly the kids out here and never leave.” Chris chuckled at the thought, “and what” Leave the cape? Besides the flight cost would kill us, trying to go to and from Miami for Underground, plus wherever it is that GCW brings you.” Tessa’s eyebrows wrinkled as she looked at Chris, knowing what he was saying. She wasn’t disappointed in his denial to move to Hawaii, but rather that he had pointed out a rather harsh fact, the travel heavy business they took a part of meant a stable life for them would be hard. As per usual, she’d have to settle for second best, “We’ll get a condo in Miami then. If and only if, you find someone to home school the kids while I’m on the road.” Pulling him down by the pit of his elbow, she figured she might as well brush their unfortunate circumstances aside and make due with what she had right now, “It’ll be better for them and us that way. I’m tired of leaving them alone with a nanny for days on end.” “That’s fine, we can get them a home teacher, and with my loose schedule for Legacy, I will be able to spend more time with them since I’m not traveling.” Though she had started the conversation, Tessa? eyes showed she was in a different place. She was already planning out the wedding, despite the proposal coming just a few short hours ago. “Are you going to invite your parents?” While this had been a long overdue question in the five years the two had dated on and off, never once had she even hinted towards wanting to meet his parents. Needless to say, there was plenty of reason for him to be caught speechless as the back of his head nearly hit the head of the bed. “Well, I guess so; I mean I don’t know why I wouldn’t.” Tessa looked around, nervous, “I don’t know; I just wasn’t sure. I mean you never mention them.” All he had to offer was a shrug, “not much to say.” “Come on.” From one set of baby blues to another, she begged for more of a complete answer. “I don’t even get along well with my mom and you’ve still met her. I can’t even recall you going to visit them alone, since we’ve been together. Do they even know about me?” “They know; I talk on the phone with them.” Chris told her, trying to down play the entire situation. “Then how come you never visit them” How come we’ve never visited them?” Chris sighed in defeat, knowing there was no way out of this. “Fine, we can go visit them and personally invite them to the wedding if you like.” Content with the answer she’d been handed, she plopped herself up on his chest with a grin, “That’d be great.” He’d managed to shush her by teasingly sliding his hand down her bare leg. Bringing his eyes back to her level, he looked back at her and finally proceeded forth with the question he’d been dying to ask since they’d gotten back to the hotel, “Now, can we get on with enjoying the rest of our vacation and forget about everyone else but us?” Her eyes twinkled back with the most obvious answer, “Yes.” But all the while she wondered how she’d be able to manage to lead the Ascension to victory when her head was in a completely different universe. Either cloud nine would have to carry the added weight of Ascension, or Ciel and the others would have to accept the fact that she was finally moving on with life. Either way, she was ready to move on with life, with or without the squared circle. [ You can blame the boy for this sucking even harder than it already does. He contributed too.
