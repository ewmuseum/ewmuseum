Date: Now Torture: Part 2 Time: Now Place: Kawanaha Kuno’s Mansion Keiko reaches under the cushion and let’s herself free. Once out she gets up and stands in front of Christopher Clarkson with her hands on her hips. He looks up at her defiantly, but his body still shakes as he looks into her cold eyes. “Aren’t you going to free yourself,” she asks him. “You really want me to be free,” he asks. “Yes, it’s dishonorable to beat down a captive person.” Christopher sighs and finds his key to make him free. He rubs his sore wrists and looks up at Keiko again. She begins to walk up the stairs, but Christopher remains sitting on the couch. “The Mistress’s sister says you and I must spend time together. Are you going to join me?” “Whatever floats your boat, but you and I know you won’t have sex with me.” “You’re right. Now I just want to talk to you in private.” Christopher shrugs his shoulders and walks up to Keiko’s room with her. She is polite and opens the door for him. As soon as he enters Keiko slams the young man with a double axe-handle to the back. Christopher collapses onto the floor and she walks in behind him laughing. Once in she locks the door behind her. “There now no interruptions,” she smiles. She grabs him by the hair and lifts him to his feet. After a closed fisted low blow she slams his head into the dresser several times before tossing him into the wall. The blonde haired man hits the wall and slumps to the floor. “At least you are taking it easy on me,” she gasps. “I’ve only just begun,” she replies reaching into her dresser. She then pulls out a long dagger and twirls the blade onto her index finger. “So you are going to kill me?” “Yes, because a man like you is incapable of love. I will not allow you to hurt Hope any longer.” “So you are going to let your vision come true then” You’re going to become the vilest of evil all for the sake of protecting Hope!” “How the hell do you know about my vision?” Keiko grabs Christopher by the hair and tosses him onto the bed. He hits the headboard head first and is knocked out. She jumps onto the bed and tosses him onto his back slapping him awake. “Answer me!” “Hope told me about it! That’s why we didn’t tell anyone, because Hope knew all of this would happen! She knew you would never believe that I could love her. It wasn’t me who forced her. If anything she forced me. Forced me to know what love really is! That song, Is There Nothing Sacred, she was singing that to me!” “You’re lying, there is no way you could feel love.” “Oh really, how the hell would you know what love is” You did everything in your power to win Kawanaha’s love. Guess what, you have it! Hope doesn’t love, love Kawanaha anymore. She has wanted to tell you this whole time, that you won. Now you have Kawanaha all to yourself, but I warn you if you kill me, Hope dies!” For the first time during this talk, Keiko stops screaming. In fact she lets Christopher up and helps him sit up in an upright position. She begins to shake at the words he spoke to her. “You’re right,” Keiko sighs, “You’re right about me becoming evil, but it’s not just this revelation. I’ve been battling an inner war here lately with myself. I’ve been accused of acting in a dishonorable way. That isn’t your concern!” “And that is fine; I don’t want it to be. But as your manager, I have to watch out for you and Hope’s well being.” “Then why will Hope die, if you die?” “Hope hasn’t told you why Kawanaha needs her?” “No, she hasn’t,” Keiko sighs realizing everything. “Kawanaha wants me to get Hope pregnant with a girl. She wants Hope to carry her baby, so she doesn’t have to. Myself and Hope are pawns in this. She doesn’t give a damn about either one of us. You were Kawanaha’s love since the first day she laid eyes on you. Hope and I are just sex toys for her. In other words if one of us is taken out, the other one will be as well!” Keiko sits there with tears streaming down her face. Christopher places his hand on hers. “I do regret everything I’ve done,” he sighs, “Not just to her, but to you and Faith as well. The only thing is Faith can’t be saved because of Kit’s torture on her. May God, have mercy on Hope that she survives this ordeal, because she’s going to turn Faith on her.” “My God, Faith could kill her!” “That is the least of your worries right now. Hope has been acting strange anyway. She’s been acting like a vampire, and she’s having those dreams again about vampires trying to kill her. She didn’t want to tell you, but she’s afraid she’s tapped into something she knows nothing about.” “There is only one who could help her there.” “Shadow?” “Yes, but now the question is how do we help each other. I mean I’m still not convinced you love her.” “Perhaps this will convince you.” Christopher reaches into his raggedy pants pocket and tosses out a black box. Keiko’s eyes rise as she questions the contents. Finally shrugging her shoulders she opens the box and gasps. Inside the box is a sapphire ring. “I was going to propose to her in a couple of weeks. It would have been one month to the date since we’ve been together in actual love.” Keiko finally cries out her tears. She hugs Christopher and he surprisingly returns the hug. After a few minutes the two separate and look at each other. “I forgive you of what you have done,” she whispers. “And I accept your forgiveness,” he replies. “There is one last thing I need you to do. You need to take a blood oath with me. For just two reasons now. One is to signify yours and mine trust. The other is to keep a lie going to protect Hope, and show that we injured each other.” Keiko grabs her dagger and slices the pal of her hand. Christopher taking the cue does the same and they mash the bloody hands together. “You’re now bound to me by honor,” she smiles, “If you ever hurt her, I swear to God, all bets are off and I will kill you.” “I understand. I promise you Hope will be safe. I’ll die before she will.” “Okay, now for the other part. You and I are going to have to hurt the other and have sex, and since I’ve already roughed you up, hit me.” “Okay, but this is to keep us alive.” To be concluded?
