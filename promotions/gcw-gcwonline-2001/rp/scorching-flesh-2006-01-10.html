Scorching Flesh

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-10-06 He wandered for what seemed like an eternity in a place that he did not know. Even though his mind reminded him it was mid-Winter, he could feel an intense chill in the air, slashing his flesh like winter's frozen talons. He pulled his jacket tight around his body and held it closed with one hand, as he raised the other to his face; just above his brow. He squinted his eyes and tried to peer into the distance, but a gray mist obscured anything that may have met his gaze from sight. He had to face facts’he was lost. And that's when he became aware of the heavy weight around his waist. Turning his body away from the stiff, bitter wind, he opened his jacket and looked down at his waist. Fastened about it was a belt of solid gold. His trembling hands moved under his jacket to his lower back. They pulled at the heavy clasp that held the belt in place around his waist and tugged it open. Still shaking, he hoisted the huge band of metal before his face and his jaw fell open. Etched into the surface of the gold were the words: 'Jayce Bradley, GCW Cruiserweight Champion'. His fingertips caressed the lettering as though it were the most fragile of glass. He found his lips mouthing the words as he read them over and over in his mind. Instantly, the cool wind and his sense of being lost were nullified. His heart leapt and his brain buzzed with satisfaction and happiness. But, he never once asked himself how it had happened. The reason for that is simple. He never got the chance. As he gazed down at the championship in his arms like a mother with her newborn child, he then became aware of another bodily sensation. This time, however, the feeling started in the palm of his hands where the weight of the gold rested. It wasn't long before that heat escalated to a burn. Despite the pain, he would not let go of the metal strap, even when it began to blister his skin. His mind reeled and his nostrils filled with the stench of burnt flesh, but he would not relinquish his grip on his prized possession. He screamed in agony as the gold liquefied and scorched him horribly. He tried and tried to contain the flow of molten metal, but it was no use. His heart raced and his mind spun out of control, as he tried desperately to succeed but met only with futility. The championship belt slipped through his fingers, and he found himself suddenly falling and screaming, end over end into a deep black void. Then he woke up. He sat bolt upright in his bed as he woke with a furious shake. His skin was clammy and his hair was soaked with sweat. He looked around his bedroom with wild eyes, and tried to make sense of what had just happened. It was four in the morning, but he didn't sleep at all for the rest of the night. He reached over and touched the button on his remote control, the television humming to life as the soft glow illuminated a bit of the room. Kids Bop 7. Jayce hated that commercial. All of those kids with absolutely no talent whatsoever, butchering some of the years best songs. In a way, it kind of reminded him about Johnny Lexicon’or did he still go by The Marauder? Did it even matter? Absolutely not, because in the end it was still the same man. A man that may be a step in the right direction for Jayce’s career. Lexicon had connections, people knew who he was, while Jayce was still a virtual unknown. When people leave the arena, they don’t talk about the exciting match that Jayce Bradley had defending his Cruiserweight title. No, they talk about the different things that went on backstage between Michael Stevens, Eva Van Lorne, The Marauder. Even a couple of “Hey, did you see Ricky Thunder selling water during the break?” comments. Was there a lot riding on Jayce’s match against Lexicon? Not to the naked eye. But deep down below the surface, where no one can see it, there was going to be something major happening. Maybe that was what was keeping Jayce up at night. Surely he wouldn’t be afraid of a few nightmares’Right? Only time will tell.
