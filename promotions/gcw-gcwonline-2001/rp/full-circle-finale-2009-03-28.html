In the past few weeks not only had she lost to Clyde Fox, she was beginning to sink her way back down into the bottom of the giant pit of hell that was becoming her career. Things absolutely sucked for a woman who once seemed to close to the top of the world. She had said it a million times before, and if things continue to transpire the way they had in prior weeks she’d probably say a couple more million, considering that things right about now looked absolutely bleak for the one time US champion. So rather than put up the fight she would have with anyone else, Tessa sighed and gave in to his request, “No, I imagine she wouldn’t.” “Good. Now I’m going to bed. Don’t call back unless it’s urgent.” “Alright.” Before she could get another word out, Roberto had hung up, which left her alone with her thoughts again. “Maybe I ought to listen to him and actually get some sleep.” She thought, depositing her blackberry beside her on the bed.
