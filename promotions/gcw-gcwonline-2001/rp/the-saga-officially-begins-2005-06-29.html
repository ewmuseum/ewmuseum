The Saga Officially Begins

29 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-29-05 The pilot episode had debuted on the FSN a week ago. The ratings were slightly above mediocre, but slightly over mediocre makes you money in the world of television and I was content, for now. I refused to make an ass out of myself, but without an eccentric personality ala Andy Dick or that Richard goon on Beauty and the Geek the show would eventually falter. I would have to acquire some odd personalities because the chances of me acting an ass were nonexistent. The Baratta’s, while intelligent men, still were unaware of my acts of manipulation. All the pieces were beginning to come together and they would all unfold as planned in the approaching weeks and if things went as smoothly as I envisioned I would be the majority shareholder of the FS network. It hadn’t been an easy ride, and of late things had gotten a little more complicated. I was fairly certain that my ex wife might genuinely be falling for Blaise Baratta ? after all, it’d been quite some time since she had slept in my satin sheets. Then there was the little blonde college student I had enjoyed a cup of coffee with this past weekend. A stipulation I had come up with as opposed to her cleaning my suit mainly because I had it cleaned the same day she spilled coffee on it. Ah yes, and then there is the matter of the battle royal that I’m participating in this Thursday on GCW’s Dangerous Games. I had finished second in the last GCW battle royal I had participated in. Granted I had no desire to win the television title and it was my first match in over a year with the exception of my Infinite Gauntlet battle royal in which I put up a solid showing. This one might be different, and then again it might not. I hadn’t decided if I was going to expend the energy necessary to prevail in a match of this caliber. To be completely honest my mind wandered from my business ventures, my ex-wife and the little blonde, Paige was her name and for once it mattered. I had invested a bit of time and a bit of money though and so my conscious told me I should at least give it a go. I urge you to watch, keep tabs as it all unfolds. I’ll do my best to keep you in the know because I assure you some very interesting things are about to happen in the life of yours truly, Ryan Stephens. ******** Robin Boca. She was an elegant woman in her early thirties. She had soft cheeks and a hard body always adorned in expensive make up and expensive clothes. She had piercing green eyes a shoulder length brown hair with blonde highlights. Her nails were always done and she lips pursed just right. There was an aura that surrounded her. He had asked her to marry him shortly after he had turned twenty-four. With an impressive background as an athlete coupled with his intelligence, good looks, etc she knew that their collision was inevitable. He had a year of law school left and she was finishing up pharmaceutical school, both at the university of Texas at Austin. There had always been an innate chemistry between them, but he never took life as seriously as she had. She was career oriented and the oldest eighteen-year-old he’d ever met when they became friends his senior year. It was then he began envisioning them together breeding athletes and brains. Upon getting married the two were perfect for one another, but something happened when they got out of college. They were making money, and a lot of it. He yearned to have a family, she yearned to make more money and grew frustrated by his persistence to have a kid. After a while he finally stopped asking, and hoped that if they grew more wealthy, if she had the lifestyle she’d always wanted she settle down and start a family with him and it was then that he began pursuing other endeavors. Wrestling came into the mix a little later, but that’s neither here nor there right now. It’s another story altogether. A few years later they had grown extremely wealthy, but she was never satisfied and they had grown apart though on the outside, they were still perfect to all those who knew them or were around them. It was then that he devised the perfect plan to make their wealthy relatively ridiculous ? to reach heights that a lawyer/wrestler/business man and his pharmaceutical sales wife couldn’t reach. They would have to be apart for or a year or so. They longed for one another at times and at first there were occasional random visits at random hotels, nights together, but one year in they both lost themselves in their parts of the plan. She was never around and he grew lonely and bitter. He kept wrestling, he kept winning and he kept planning and they stopped sneaking around with each other. She stayed completely in her new life ? playing it to perfection. He wandered if it was too good. If they would be able to pull themselves back out once it had run its? course. Or more importantly, if they would even want to. He feared that she would not and that fear enraged him, it drove him, he lost himself in his work, in his wrestling. Then he met someone who intrigued him like he had never been before and then if it didn’t work out perfectly it didn’t seem so bad. Perhaps the requirements of working out perfectly had been altered in the last few weeks. ******** Paige Miller. She petite to say the least. Five three or four, maybe 105 pounds. She was hard because she ran collegiate track at a private institution in Austin. Her legs were trim but toned. She was a business major but wasn’t sure what she wanted to do with it. She always said her calling was simply to help people. She has baby fine blonde hair and a fair complexion, light blue eyes and thin pink lips. They had met (kinda) when she accidentally spilt her Starbucks all over his suit, but for whatever reason he wasn’t mad. He had been afraid to call her, and that too he couldn’t comprehend. He and his ex-wife (wife) had an agreement, sex with others was probably necessary, but he hadn’t even bothered. For some reason with one he would have, though he somehow knew she probably didn’t operate that way. She was silly and fun, though good hearted and serious in their recent late night introspective conversations. Their pasts didn’t matter and they didn’t mention them. He hadn’t even kissed her despite longing to since he first saw her. He couldn’t figure her out and she couldn’t understand how this man wasn’t already taken. Music was her life and she insisted on listening to it on her MP3 player when they ran early in the morning before her classes rather than talk. Her stride was carefree in the same way that she tried to portray her life to be, but he could tell there was more to her than she let on. They shared a mutual affliction for the Counting Crows and they frequented the CD player both in her apartment and his car. She had not been to his house and was beginning to wonder why. He told her he wrestled in case she happened to see him on television, but he was almost ashamed of it. She thought it was interesting. Especially when he lied and said he gave much of the money he made to charities throughout the Austin area. She didn’t take him for a wrestler, but she certainly didn’t mind because he was intelligent and seemed well-read and well-rounded. She was falling for an older man. He had fallen for a younger woman. Things started getting increasingly more complicated as they spent more and more time together. For the first time ever he was late for a meeting and suspicions were aroused. He had never been late. Never. ******** The Barrata’s. There was the elder Barrata who was approaching sixty-five with his tailored suits and his leather skin and of course, his man jewelry (who where’s that shit?) His hair was white and he combed it straight back and it fluffed atop his head. He was a broad shouldered man and quite intelligent. He was very suspicious of Stephens despite working with him for almost two years now. Instead of cutting ties; however, he enjoyed matching wits with the younger man. He thought he could manipulate Stephens, but he was unaware that Stephens was well ahead of him by now. It had taken him too long to become suspicious and it was probably too late at this point. He believed in the whole “keep your friends close and your enemies closer” and he had long since decided that he might resort to having Stephens killed if he pulled the wool over his eyes. He didn’t get where he was letting people walk over him. His life was rough and it had taken him many years to sustain the status he currently maintained which was CEO of Fox Sports Net. He was involved in various other endeavors, dirty endeavors if you will, but he left them to his nephews and eldest son. They reported back to him occasionally. He was a very powerful man ? a man who was often distracted by his youngest son. Blaise Barrata was a strikingly handsome man if you liked the type that wore expensive suits, had long black hair pulled back in a ponytail and a perfect physique. He was a spoiled, whiny brat and a sadistic one at that. He was his father’s right-hand man. It was his birth right as the youngest and thus his father’s favorite. In addition John knew his son was weak minded and soft, nothing like the other boys. He also knew that his son was smart and that he could be of some service’just not out on the streets collecting money and such. Blaise was dating Robin Boca. He had been for quite sometime now. She was his arm candy despite treating her like relative shit. She reluctantly tolerated it all and those that witnessed them claimed that she was in it for the money alone. Always anxious for the high class life’but let’s not start intertwining all these characters just yet. Blaise had just received an anonymous phone call on his mobile that someone was going around a buying up all the shares of FSN stock and that it was about time they took notice. He was walking briskly down a corridor of Baratta Inc. in search of his father. Indeed, things were about to get more interesting as all of these people and a few others get thrown together into a whirlwind of crazy antics and in-depth plots; unfolding before your eyes. Stay tuned.
