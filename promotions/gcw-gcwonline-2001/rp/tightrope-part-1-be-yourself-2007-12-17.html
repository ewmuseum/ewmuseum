Tightrope (Part 1: Be Yourself)

17 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 17, 2007 OOC NOTE: This roleplay takes place before the last episode of WorldWide. The roleplay was held off as not to have Vivica out the fact that she didn't join The Establishment before Maxwell did. So... here you go. ---- My words are weapons In which I murder you with But please don't get scared Please do not turn your head We are the future the 21st century Dyslexic glue sniffing cybersluts With homicidal minds and handguns We are the same Nothing will change We are insane Nothing will change There is a thin line between What is good and what is evil and I will tiptoe down that line but I will feel unstable My life is a circus and I'm tripping down the tightrope well There's nothing left to save me now So I will not look down It happens again and again and again And it happens again and again and again There is no beginning There is no end There is only change Progression backwards Is this where we are heading? Take back you soul Forget your emptiness There is a thin line between What is good and what is evil and I will tiptoe down that line but I will feel unstable My life is a circus and I'm tripping down the tightrope well There's nothing left to save me now I'm falling to the ground - "Tightrope" by Papa Roach ---- Introduction: Catch-22 ---- Another day, another headline: Valentine Takes V-Line To Destiny Here am I stuck in quite possibly the biggest couple of months in my entire life and this is the life I have to live with. A life where people have taken it upon themselves to make my decisions for me, and then have the nerve to criticize the decisions I never even made. I sit back at home and turn on my computer to watch people debate over I made the right decision over a decision I never made. Whenever I try to talk to anyone about it they tell me that this is what I signed up for. This is what I agreed to submit myself to when I chose to be a wrestler rather than take Christian's high-paying job to be his trophy wife. Upon hearing the news my parents suddenly halted their desperate attempts to become a part of my life again. Something they should've done a long time ago. Now they stick their nose up in the air and tell the local gossip queens they raised me better. My own friends can't look me in the eyes. I get passing stares as I walk down the street or go to the grocery store. It doesn't seem like a day goes by when there isn't some crazy fan asking me why. Why did I do it? Why did you sell your soul to Steven Caldera? Why or why Vivica? Why couldn't you just do things the right way? It was never my decision to make... I never had a choice. A Catch-22. No matter what moves I make these days I can never make people happy. They tell me they want answers and I give them. Did I join The Establishment? NO. Then I'm labeled a lair. They say my actions have obviously spoke otherwise. Like they're a part of my fucking life, and know how I've acted. If I don't answer at all then I'm obviously guilty. Every phrase the escapes my lips is met with rampant scepticism. Frustration has driven me to the brink. I wake up in the morning and look at myself in the mirror and don't even know who I am anymore. I don't know what to think, or how to act without looking suspicious and just furthering proof that I've sold my soul to the devil. The fact of the matter is I can't make you people happy. I have to admit that I've turned my back on everything I've busted my ass for over the years, or be told there is no way that I can prove otherwise. Actions speak louder than words, right? For every positive action I've made in my career someone brings up that I turned my back on my family, I've turned my back on my high school friends, I turned my back on Christian Zenith, I turned my back on Virgil Ambrosi... what makes Lia Ambrosi so much different than any of them? You can point the finger and call me a liar. It just fuels my desire to prove all of you wrong. I am who I've always been. I'm rebellion incarnate, a bird of freedom, an aerial artisan, an unyielding underdog, and most of all... I am a fighter. I am a champion. I never gave up before... and I never will. I am The Fearless Phenom. I am Vivica J. Valentine. Your next GCW World Heavyweight Champion. ---- Act One: Progression Backwards ---- A flash ignited the apathetic gray skies. From the heavens, millions of raindrops tickled the surface of the earth. Relentlessly crashing down upon the ground, the rain began to form small rivers that went down the sides of the suburban Chicago streets. Families turned off the televisions and gathered to watch the November thunderstorms finally rolling in on the drought that has affected much of the Midwest. Sheets of rain turned everything around the black Chrysler Crossfire a dreary shade of green. On the steel roof they played a serenade of thunks and clings. Inside a pair of electrifying cerulean blue eyes peered through the storm, watching as the windshield wipers vigorously tried to keep up with the pace. The effects of the piercing headlights were all but lost in the haze of downpour. After taking an extended blink the car began to slow. A blazing red lit up in the sky as the vehicle came to a stop. With a sigh from inside, Vivica J. Valentine pulled her cell phone out from inside the pocket of her tight stylishly ripped blue jeans. Flipping the phone open she navigated the menu from behind golden and red locks of hair falling in front of her face. NO MISSED CALLS. Frustrated she flipped the phone shut with a snap and tossed it down onto the passenger seat. While the car she called her baby could go 0 ro 60 in 6.5 seconds, her life seemed to go from 60 to 0 in 4.5 days. She remembered just over a week ago when she was too scared to open her phone because of what Lia might think, listening to the tune of "Misery Business" every hour or so. It'd be 3 days since the last time she'd heard that song. Either no one was interested anymore... or no one cared. Both choices were a direct result of the involvement that Steven Caldera had suddenly decided to take in her life. While she had adamantly denied the fact that she accepted an offer to join The Establishment she could hardly argue that the thought hadn't crossed her mind, or that the offer wasn't on the table. It wouldn't be the first time Caldera offered, and she was sure it wasn't the last. Standing next to the likes of LeStatt Knight, Octavian, and Rich Rollins... regardless of her personal feelings on their personalities... would be a sure fire route to success. Ever since she was a little girl she'd dreamed of two things. The day that she'd be married and the day that she'd win the World Championship. Unfortunately for her, the wedding was the more pre-planned of the two. Never had it crossed her mind how she'd get there. All she could think of was the rush of adrenaline as she held the title close to her chest, the roar of the crowd, the beating of her heart. For a good majority of her childhood it was the only thing she ever wanted out of life. With that her life would be set, she'd have nothing else to live for. Nine year-old Vivica J. Valentine wouldn't have hesitated to accept the offer but... HOONNNK! The ear-shattering sound of a car horn from behind her snapped her back into reality as she peered up through the storm at shining green light swaying back and forth in the breeze. Biting her tongue, she decided not to use the language she'd planned for the driver behind her and simply stepped on the gas. Slowly navigating through the winter rain, her tires slicked down the streets she often played on as a child. Against the better wishes of her parents, she'd one time broke her wrist playing street football in almost the exact spot she was crossing now. Moving down the street she could almost name some significant childhood event that happened at every house. In that white one she'd use to play wrestling in the backyard on a trampoline with a friend and her big brother. In the brick one next door she once watched as it was surrounded by firefighters due to a small electrical fire that happened in the kitchen. Her black Crossfire once again came to a crawl. It's neon red brake lights alerted the impatient motorist behind her as she pulled over to the side of the road. Putting the car into park, she flicked off the ignition before leaning her head back and closing her eyes. She loved thunderstorms, but this one wasn't helping her clear her head. Everything was still so confusing to her. Feeling so frustrated and helpless really took a toll on a woman as independent and headstrong as The Fearless Phenom. It was a similar feeling that made her run away from home when she was a teenager. She was exactly what she said. A bird of freedom. At this moment in her life she felt trapped and caged. Something had to give... something just wasn't right. It took everything inside of her not to just open up her door and scream at the top of her lungs out into rain. Maybe that was what she really needed to do, but with adulthood came an overwhelming sense of responsibility. Screaming and cutting yourself off to society wasn't going to cut it anymore, especially under a spotlight as big as fame. Picking up her phone she flipped it open again, listening to nothing but the sonata of raindrops beating on the roof of her Crossfire like a steel drum. NO MISSED CALLS. Looking out the passenger side of the vehicle, she watched as her mother walked across the bay window that graced the front of her childhood home. Over and over again she'd tried to explain her case... looking for any bit of guidance through hand life had dealt her. One by one, every shoulder to lean on and ear to listen had closed itself up. Now Lia Ambrosi wasn't even returning her phone calls. Her parents had tried so desperately to be a part of her life again... this was their chance. Hesitantly Vivica began to dial a number she hadn't voluntarily dialed in ages. The dull sound of a ring began to resonate through her phone as she slowly placed it against her ear... half hoping that the other side wouldn't answer. Staring inside the house she watched as her mother sat down in front of the television. RIIIINNGGG... The phone continued to ring, but her mother never budged. The channels on the television changed rapidly as another monotone ring came through her phone. Maybe her father was home... maybe she asked him to answer it. Excuse after excuse popped in her head as she aggrevatedly watched absolutely no reaction happen from inside the home. Hello! Thank you for calling the Valentine residence, I'm sorry we missed your call but if you leave your name and phone number after the beep... we'll get back to you as soon as we're available. BEEEEP! "Mom..." Vivica said reluctantly staring inside the house, "This is Vivica... I was just calling to talk." Waiting anxiously for a reaction she paused. "Pick up the phone mom, I know you're home." Vivica said, the inflection in her voice grew angrier by the second. She began tapping her foot as no response came. "I'm outside the house mom, I know you're home! Please... just pick up the phone." With that line her mother suddenly jumped up to her feet. The aggravation in her demeanor lightened as her mom walked over towards the answering machine that way laying inside the bay window. Once she reached the machine, Mrs. Valentine squinted looking outside the window for her daughter. Thinking eye contact was made, Vivica waved before her heart quickly sank when the blinds were closed. CLICK. The sound of the answering machine hanging up sealed the deal. Pissed off Vivica slammed her fist down on the dashboard and dropped her head onto the steering wheel. What did she have to do to get someone... ANYONE... to believe her. She hadn't agreed to anything, she didn't join The Establishment. It was almost like screaming into space. Her mouth was moving but no one could hear her. Looking at her phone now sitting on the floor on the passengers side buzzing for remaining open after slamming it down, Vivica wondered to herself how everything had gotten to this point. What did she do to deserve this torture? How in the world could anyone believe the words of Steven Caldera over hers? It was almost like she didn't have a choice in the matter. It was almost like... The Establishment was her destiny. "If Markus was still around..." Vivica said keeping her forehead against the top of the steering wheel, "he'd listen to me. Markus would know what to do." ---- Act Two: WWMD (What Would Markus Do?) ---- A breath of air whistled through the trees while the rain continued to pour. Soiling the soft ground with a million tears, the storm continued to roll through. Navigating through the tempest, The Fearless Phenom's cerulean blue eyes peered through the precipitation like a flashlight into the darkness. Looking into her rear view mirror she viewed the choker locket that resided around her neck. With a sigh she began to slow her vehicle. Thunder shook the ground while she shifted the black Chrysler Crossfire into park. Looking past the droplets streaming down her driver's side window, Vivica could see a newly remodeled gravestone inside her own reflection. Turning off the car with a flick of the wrist, she was left with only the sound of rain dancing on her roof as she reached over to the passenger side of the vehicle to pick up a single red rose. When she exited the car, Valentine couldn't help but notice how beautiful the new headstone looked. Almost as if it rose from the ashes of the one Bryan Mayhem destroyed, The Aerial Artisan paused for a second to marvel at its beauty, twisting the single rose in her hands as she surveyed. Water continued to fall from the heavens with a continued trickle as she made her way across the street towards the grave that read BLAIRE. Just seeing his name brought back so many memories... good and bad. Yet all of them made her smile, just seeing his face again. Vivica paused in her tracks in front of the headstone, now soaked from head to toe. None of that mattered though. The silly things she'd go through just to feel close to him again. Kneeling down to one knee Vivica gently placed the rose on the ground at the base of the gravestone. Constant rain made it hard to see, but a small tear began to roll down her face as she looked square at the proof that Markus may be gone forever... no matter what the mysterious phone call may have told her. Though in her heart she was sure that Markus was somewhere... alive or dead, watching her. He heard everything that she said and saw everything that had happened. While everyone up to now had doubted her, she had no doubt that Markus would have faith in her. Trust her judgement and be proud of what she had accomplished, living her dream and getting a shot at the one thing in life she'd wanted more than anything... the GCW World Heavyweight Championship. "I know..." she spoke outloud, no longer able to maintain her silent thoughts, "I know that no matter what other people would say, you'd have faith in me. You of all people know what it's like to be misunderstood and labeled as something that you're not. I know that if you were here you'd hold me and tell me that everyone would understand sometime. Tell me to keep my head up because my dream was just around the corner..." Vivica paused for a second, wiping the tear out of her eye. "It's so hard here without you though." Vivica continued to choke through the pain of the position she was placed in, "It's hard to do this alone. I miss you so much. The things I'd do to have you back again I don't know if I'd ever say them outloud. I'm sorry I was such a bitch and wouldn't just tell you the way I felt... I didn't know I'd lose you. I never knew how much you meant to me until... all this. You know?" Grasping the locket in her hand she couldn't talk anymore. Going from one knee to the ground, she sat in the rain drenched cemetery looking at the headstone hoping for an answer. There was no one else to turn to, nowhere else to go. She needed to just let go of all this pent up emotion, and it roared out like a Tsunami as tears poured down her face. Looking down at the rose through the tears she couldn't help but thing what do you do now? "Be strong." She could almost hear his voice resonate in her head over the thunder rumbling in the distance. "You just have to be strong." Her rain soaked blonde hair flapped in a gust of wind as she continued to clinch her locket. Strength in the face of adversity. Faith in yourself and your abilities. These were the attributes of a champion. Everyone was judging her... measuring her worth in different eyes. No matter what decisions you make in life there will always be someone there who wants to cut you down. When it comes to situations like this, there is really only one solution... "You just have to be yourself." ---- Act Three: Be Yourself ---- "You just have to be yourself." That's the phrase Vivica said out loud to herself as she pressed a button on the Pioneer CD player of her car. It has been several hours since the storm had rolled out, and with it left a burden off of the shoulders of The Fearless Phenom. Her little emotional breakdown at the cemetery had been a long time coming, it was something she'd been storing up inside for a long time. With the monkey off her back came a new sense of self-confidence and satisfaction. Someone falls to pieces Sleeping all alone, someone kills the pain Spinning in the silence She finally drifts away... A satisfaction in knowing that no matter what people thought about whatever decision she might make, that true happiness resided inside of herself. If her fans truly loved and cared about her, they'd forgive her for whatever mistakes she made along the way. After all... she was just like them. If she was to be the representation of the people... she had to be flawed, just like each and everyone one of them were. No one was perfect, as long as she never gave up on her dreams... she was sure they'd come true one day. After all, who was she to argue with destiny? Someone gets excited in the chapel yard And catches a bouquet Someone lays a dozen White roses on a grave... The car slowed down to a halt as she swerved over to a parking spot under a bright street light. Cars flew by her on the busy downtown streets of Chicago, Illinois as she pulled her cell phone out of her pocket. A familiar number was dialed on her screen before she hit the TALK button without as much as a hesitation. Listening she felt anxious as the phone rang, knowing full well that there would probably be no answer on the other end. Maybe all she really wanted to do was vent, to hear herself talk and hope that somehow... Lia heard the message. BEEP. "Lia... this is Vivica. I know I'm probably not the most popular person in your world right now and I've made a lot of mistakes up till now. I'm sorry... I've had a lot of problems handling all this. I never imagined things would end up this way but... we all make mistakes, right?" Vivica paused for an answer, knowing full well she was talking to an answering machine but continued on. "When you were training me you always told me that. I was going to make mistakes, but learning from them is where you better yourself. I've done a lot of bettering myself over the past month, and whatever Caldera has beat into your head and everyone elses for that matter... it isn't true. Joining The Establishment just isn't me, and you know that. You have to believe me... please Lia... call me back soon. I miss my best friend. I miss you." And to be yourself is all that you can do To be yourself is all that you can do Hanging up the phone Vivica realized that in two days it was all going to come to a head. Peter Maxwell, whether he lies or tells the truth, is going to finalize it in the minds of everyone with his "grand revelation." She was going to expect the worst... and she wasn't going to take it lying down. If she was going to go down in a lie, she was going to go down fighting. The truth would come out one way or another. Destiny wouldn't have it any other way. Before the big night though, there was one last thing she had to get off her mind. The phone call she'd receive about not giving up hope on Markus... it was almost like a dream but was so real. There was only one way to find out, and only one man who she knew would always know the truth. If she was going to face Peter Maxwell and whatever demons that might bring, she had to face the lingering demon from her past. She had to know if he had anything to do with Markus. Pushing the driver's side door open, Vivica stepped out into the busy Chicago nightlife. A gust of wind swept her blonde hair in front of her face as she closed the door along with her eyes. Opening them she looked into the sky at a building the rose high above her head. It was a building etched into her memories from years of trials and tribulations. One thing was for sure... this wasn't going to be pretty. After tonight, she was never going to look at the Zenith Corporation building the same ever again. TO BE CONTINUED...
