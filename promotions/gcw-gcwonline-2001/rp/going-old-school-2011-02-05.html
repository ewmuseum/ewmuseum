Going old school!!

5 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/11 Camera opens backstage at a house show somewhere in northern Delaware. An interviewer walks over and looks at Shaman and raises the mic as he starts to speak. Shaman grabs his jacket looking at the sticker name tag he then releases it in disgust. The name tag simply reads Todd. Todd: Shaman how do you feel about your upcoming match against Chris Bagwell, You both have had many interesting path crossings in the path. Shaman: Well let me stop you right there brother, Step back while the grown ups talk ok? I don't need no second bush league announcer interrupting my second rate interview in some burnt out town in northern Delaware. Rolling his shoulders and cracking his neck he looks around. Shaman: Look here Bagwell you are about as useful as tits on a bull lately. You run right up start jokes and crap. Your ass is writing checks your mouth can't cash. Look around you Bagwell there is more talent than when you came in this place all cocky and egotistical. What have you done here lately? The interviewer fidgets a bit as Shaman looks over at him. Shaman: What? What in the hell do you think you can say to …..ahh never fucking mind, tell ya what go ahead and conduct some sort of meaningful interview if you think you can succeed. Todd: Look Shaman your run as of late has been good, But only last week you lost to Bryan mayhem and lost the Hardcore title. What do you think this is going to do to your match up with Bagwell? What can you do to Bagwell? Reaching up he rubs his chin and cocks his head to the side looking upwards. Shaman: What can I do to Bagwell? Hell I can't do much that life hasn't already done really. Have you seen the pictures of his parents? They look like Ma and Pa kettle from down in the lower Kentucky Hollers. One tooth amongst them and are working welfare to welfare check. Probably kickin up some moon shine as well. Shaman tips his hand out from his head and tilts his head making the drinking motion. Shaman: Come on Bagwell you know you've been drinking the kool aid lately. Look at your antics your literally struggling to get a handle on reality or at least grasp some fame while your in ring talents just aren't cutting it. Todd the interviewer looks at Shaman confused, Shaman reaches over and smacks him upside the head. Shaman: Pay attention and conduct your interview. Damn GCW was desperate when they hire you. Are you on a Caldera budget? Todd: Excuse me? Shaman: You know, no pay, Ohio State student living off Ramon noodles doing interviews because you're a wrestling fan? Todd turns red in the face of embarrassment as he looks at Shaman. Shaman: Damn I only call em like I see em. Don't cry lets get back to the upcoming World Wide before I feel bad enough to beat ya senseless. Todd nods and looks around. Todd: So Shaman you have a chance to regain the GCW Hardcore title this week against Bryan Mayhem, what is your strategy on this. Shaman raising both hands pointing at the camera. Shaman: Don't get too comfortable holding that title Mayhem, You know I can and will come take it from you. This also goes for you Diamond, don't start to think of any ways to nab it out from under my fingers. Wait a second don't start thinking period, all you will do is strain a brain vessel or something. Turning he raises a hand with one finger pointing up as if to stop everything. Shaman: Wait a second here, just thought of something truthfully. I am stuck here in northern Deleware with you Todd the Ohio State kid. Shaman smacks his forehead. Shaman: Damn it kid were you really that gullible, I mean hell I can set ya up with some ocean front property in Arizona right next to Bagwell. Who by the way smells like Andy Murrays nut sack. Raising both hands palms towards the camera. Shaman: Just saying man, if anyone has been in the locker room around you. Just saying a little funky out of the ordinary. Kind of smells like wet sheep. Smiling a cocky confident smile. Shaman: Hell man you keep getting nervous like this you'll shit yourself a Bagwell right here on the floor and we can have a duel interview. Shaman turns and faces the camera as he looks back and forth around. He then settles down gazing at the camera. Shaman: Let me get straight to the point here. Sure Bagwell has talent, sure Bagwell has charisma, sure Bagwell has a lot of useless things in the ring, but one thing he will have when I am done is the realization that he got broke in the ring at my hands. Todd turns and looks around and nods towards the camera crew. Todd: Well folks that is all the time we have for...!?!?! Shaman yanks the mic out of Todds hand. Shaman: I will tell you when you're finished. Reaching forward he grabs Todd's face and shoves him away out of camera view. Shaman: Your stinking up the place go find a shower and clean yourself. As I was saying, only way to teach somebody a lesson is beat it into them. Mayhem keep my title warm it is going to comeback to daddy soon. Tossing the mic upwards into the air as he catches once again he speaks into it. Shaman: As for you Bagwell I have seen horses walking backwards looking like they are in a better positions. You see Bagwell no matter what you say or do I have already won. Crack your little jokes, make your little Oklahoma or Kansas jokes, in the end I win because you can never truly beat me down and keep me there. Shaman holds the mic forward for a second and then shrugs. Shaman: I am done here , none of you idiots deserve my precious time. Tossing it on the floor he exits the backstage interview area as the camera fades to static.
