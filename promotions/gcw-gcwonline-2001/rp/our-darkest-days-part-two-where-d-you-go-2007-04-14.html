Our Darkest Days, Part Two: Where'd You Go?

14 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/4/07 (written 5/2/08) April 14th, 2007, The Murraycave, Andy Murray’s Residence, Tallahassee, Florida. Having your own cave makes you incredibly cool. This is a fact. If a man owns a cave, he is automatically one level cooler than a man who does not possess his own cave. Look at it this way; Batman had a cave, Daredevil did not ? clearly, in terms of coolness, Batman is the superior superhero. Likewise, many supremely cool individuals throughout history have adopted Captain Caveman as their preferred cartoon character over cave-less alternatives such as Daffy Duck and Tom & Jerry. Therefore, on the whole, it is not hard to come to the conclusion that the majority of the Planet Earth’s most awesome individuals are proud cave owners. Andy Murray was no different. His brother younger brother stood on an elevated steel platform above the main floor of the Murraycave, clad in a pair of loose-fitting blue jeans and a white wifebeater vest and with his shoulder-length brown hair tied back into a ponytail underneath a red bandanna. He leaned forward against the handrail, squinting in the dim light as he observed the floor below him, looking for signs of life. While many had speculated, only a privileged few could truly confirm existence of the Murraycave. Aside from Andy and, of course, his brother Cayle, the only other people who had ever laid their eyes upon the visual feast that is the Murraycave were his son Marvin and wife Jane. Oh, there was Andy’s Portuguese former-butler Alvaro too, who would probably be following the Scottish King of Cool’s brother around right now if it weren’t for the fact that Andy had decided a few months ago that having a butler was a bit lame, as well as being incredibly snooty, and fired him. Boofuckinghoo. The Murraycave itself looked not completely unlike a theatrical set design from a high-budget spy movie. Nestled directly underneath Andy’s large suburban residence near the heart of America’s “Dirty South,” the Murraycave was expansive but lit up well by numerous light placements dotted around the cold, uneven stone walls. Although the Murraycave was only accessible only by a super secret elevator located behind a bush in Andy’s garden, an almost exact replica of the Batmobile sat in the centre of the caves perfectly evened-out floor. Considering that there was no visible way for this vehicle to leave the cave, the mind boggles as to why exactly it was there in the first place. Clearly, somebody had not thought things through? The walls surrounding the car at the foot of the cave were mostly lined with various monitors, flashy-lights and control panels; it is up to your own judgement to decide whether or not they served an actual purpose other than to look cool. Useful or not, the Murraycave was property of Andy Murray, and he reserved the right to fill it with whatever pap he wishes to, bitch. “Where the hell are you, you little green bastard!”” barked Cayle across the cavernous reaches of the Murraycave. “Come on out here right now or I’m-a straight-up murder your ass!” Cayle hopped down from his platform onto the main floor of the Murraycave, before taking a closer look at some of the nooks and crannies inside the cave. As he gently paced towards a gap between the rock walls, however, he heard a metallic “CLANG!” behind him. He quickly pivoted round on the heel of his foot to see a small steel pipe slowly rolling on the floor. “What the-!”! HEY! COME HERE!” As Cayle raised his vision he caught sight of a small, green blur running away from him towards the elevator which led to the outside world. On closer inspection the green creature appeared to be of humanoid form, and whatever the hell it was ran at a stupidly slow pace. In a straight-up sprinting contest it would surely have been no match for Cayle, however given the head start the creature was able to leap into the elevator and slam a button on the control panel bringing down the glass door just milliseconds before Cayle could catch up. “Grob! Come on, get out of there!” Cayle frantically yelled as he slammed a clenched fist against the glass. The creature, who was wearing little more than a pair of garish blue and red Hawaiian shorts, turned towards the younger Murray sibling to reveal the true repulsiveness of his appearance. His skin was bright green and light reflected off the thin layer of slime that coated it and his eyes resembled large balls of thick, black crude oil more than anything else. While he was humanoid without a doubt, it would be a bit of a stretch to refer to this creature as “human”. Ladies and Gentlemen, you have just met Grobschnit. Grobschnit was a prehistoric alien refugee, forced to flee his home planet of Schnitalonia when it was invaded by a technologically-superior race known as the Oogie Boogie. Well, either that or a short-ass guy from New Jersey in a kick-ass costume? depends on your open-mindedness regarding extra terrestrial life forms really. Grobschnit pressed his lips together and stuck out his tongue, blowing a raspberry at Cayle (a man, it should be noted, whom Grobschnit had very little respect for). “Fux u u bigg fagit! Izz gona hyde untill Anne D. izz bak frum Scotchlind!” he growled in one of the most incomprehensible, gravely voices possible. To the outsider it may sounds like the Notorious G-R-O-B just had a really, really sore throat, but that was not the case; he just had a voice like an un-lubricated lawnmower. “Awww come on Slimey, Andy’ll go apeshit when he finds out you’ve been let loose after dark!” Cayle pleaded, dropping the aggressive standpoint that he had adopted earlier. He knew himself that The Scottish King of Cool wouldn’t be best pleased to come home and find out that Grobschnit had been let out of the cage that he called home after the sun had went down. But it was too late; Grobber shrugged in spite of Cayle’s protests as the elevator began its descent to the exit at the top of the Murraycave. As the intergalactic super-being left his field of vision Cayle let out a sigh; it was inevitable now that Grobschnit would leave the Murraycave before him, meaning that his quest to catch him and lock him up before the imminent return of his brother was beginning to look like a hopeless cause. ---- Andy could still feel the twanging pain in the small of his back as he sat uncomfortably in the backseat of the taxicab. He was incredibly tired after a long, hard day of travelling, and if it weren’t for the ridiculously stuffy temperature in the cab and the pain in his back he would surely have fallen asleep many hours ago. At numerous points during the final leg of his journey from Glasgow to Tallahassee Andy had contemplated asking the driver to crank the air conditioning a little. He didn’t understand how the driver could sit in this environment all day; despite the fact that it was almost midnight the cab was stupidly warm and Andy had been wiping the perspiration from his brow throughout the course of the journey from Tallahassee Regional Airport to his home. Oh well, not much point making a fuss now, Andy thought, there must only be about five minutes left anyway. The Scottish King of Cool wasn’t a massive fan of travelling (well, he enjoyed visiting new places, seeing the sights and taking in new cultures; what he didn’t like was the time spent in transit) at the best of times, especially not when attempting to recuperate after a back operation that he hoped would help salvage his career. After only managing to catch a couple of hours sleep in his Glasgow hotel the night before Andy was in a foul enough mood before he’d even stepped onto the plain at Glasgow International Airport, so it’s a pretty safe bet that the two hour delay to the original takeoff time only soured his mood even more. Because of said delay Andy had missed his subsequent link flight between Orlando and Tallahassee, meaning he had to fork out yet more money on airfares to get back home. Nope, Andy Murray wasn’t exactly in the best of moods right now, and the general attitude of the cab driver certainly wasn’t helping things much either. The guy hadn’t uttered a word since they had left the airport and he’d asked where Andy was heading. The Scottish King of Cool wasn’t exactly in a particularly talkative mood, but he still expected at least a little bit of banter along the way. He wouldn’t be getting much of a tip; that was for sure. Not long now though; soon Andy would be home and normal service would resume. It was looking like his latest back operation had been a resounding success, and although he knew it would be months before his body would be ready, Andy’s thoughts were already pondering a potential return to the ring. He wouldn’t let his FUSE failure get to him; wrestling was all that Andy had ever really known, and he KNEW that somewhere there was a promotion willing to take a chance on him. Closer to hand, he couldn’t wait to get back to see his wife and his brother. While he felt it necessary to remain in Scotland after his operation in order to aid his rest and recuperation period (peace and quiet with not a sole around to bother him, just how he liked it) he had missed both terribly. He wasn’t really looking forward to seeing the state that he thought his brother might have let the house get into during his absence, but that was something he could live with. First things first though; a good sleep was in order. The Scottish King of Cool’s body was running low on gas; he knew that he’d sleep like a baby tonight. ---- Grobschnit carefully trod through the bushes behind Andy Murray’s oversized Floridian mansion, still making attempts to hide from a desperate Cayle Murray. Finding a good spot in the bushes, Grobber peered out between the leaves; from here he could clearly see the super secret entrance to the Murraycave elevator on the outside of the red-brick house. Perfect. He gently dropped down to both knees in order to make himself even less visible than he was before. It was a warm, sticky night, but thankfully the thin layer of slime coating the Notorious G-R-O-B’s skin was doing an exceptional job of keeping him cool. “Sily fagit wil nevur fynd mee heer,” Grobschnit croaked to himself as he felt an opportunistic mosquito landing on his shoulder. He slowly turned his head around so as not to disturb the hideous bloodsucking insect, before suddenly the mosquito disappeared with a quick flick of Grobby’s tongue; in a real “blink and you’ll miss it” moment Grobschnit had managed to flick out his ridiculously long, green tongue, ensnare the hapless beastie with it and drag it into his mouth. “Mmmmm, bugz,” he moaned with pleasure as he crunched the small bug between his teeth; the mosquito was a rare delicacy that Grobschnit indulged in on less occasions than he would have liked to, given the fact that he spent his evenings locked up in a cage. Out of the corner of his eye he noticed Cayle across the lawn, stumbling from the elevator into the night. He called out something that Grobber couldn’t quite decipher over the loud humming of a million crickets rubbing their legs together. He realised that he wasn’t fast enough to outrun the human in a spring, so he had pinned all his hopes on the younger Murray sibling not finding his “ingenious” hiding place. Things would be fine when Andy got back; The Scottish King of Cool treated Grobschnit with respect, like a human being, despite the fact that he kept him locked up in a cage most of the time. It was Andy who had taken Grobber in after he had been forced to evacuate his home planet, and for that he would be eternally grateful. ---- “Yo, green bitch! Come out come out wherever you air!” called Cayle Murray as he stepped out of the elevator and onto the floodlit lawn before him. He glanced across the massive area at the back of his brother’s home, scanning the lawn, bushes, and greenhouse areas for any signs of movement. Much to his dismay he couldn’t find any; in the time it had taken him to travel up from the Murraycave his little green adversary had disappeared into the night. Cayle let out an exasperated sigh and shook his head. For the first time he felt the fully extent of the clammy evening air as he strode out into the open, taking another quick look around the garden. “I know you’re out here, ya fucker!” he bellowed once again. He knew that it was exceptionally unlikely that Grobber would feel inclined to bring himself out into the open at Cayle’s request, but he couldn’t just give up the hunt; Andy would kill him. Better start looking he thought to himself as he slowly made his way across the well-kept lawn towards a cluster of bushes just a few yards away. He always had a problem with the horrible, green creature, and he never could understand why his brother insisted on keeping him around. Nonetheless his brother was a man who commanded a lot of respect, and Cayle felt he owed it to him to bring Grobschnit back inside. It was, after all, because of his doing that Grobby escaped in the first place; he’d kept his cage door open for a split-second longer than he should have during feeding time earlier in the evening, and after blindsiding Cayle with a quick kick in that sensitive male area Grobschnit had vanished. As Cayle neared the bushes, he knelt down, preparing to pull some of the branches aside and take a closer look, but he was soon interrupted as he heard a very, very faint “thud” noise coming from the house. He quickly turned around and noticed a light shining through the large French doors leading into the kitchen. A tall, black-haired figure was roaming around inside, pulling open the refrigerator and pulling out what looked like a carton of juice. Andy was back. ---- Andy closed the fridge door with his foot as he began to open up the carton of Tropicana, before pouring some of the orange juice into a glass that he’d just pulled out from the dishwasher. He soon downed the juice; the refreshing citrus burst giving his pallet a much-needed spurt of life. As he pulled up a bench at the breakfast bar he set about pouring himself another small glass of juice and took a moment to appreciate the coolness of the air-conditioned kitchen, it was a fine change from the disgustingly warm taxi he had just left. As his lips again connected with the glass, Andy’s solitude was shattered as the French doors burst open. In through them burst his brother Cayle, sweating from the unpleasant temperature outside and looking pretty tired himself. Obviously Andy’s return was enough to drive Cayle from his search for Grobschnit, even though he knew that The Scottish King of Cool probably wouldn’t react too kindly when he found out that the “Schnit was loose. “There you are, you little rat!” said Andy with a smile as his brother approached him. The brothers? hands met midair with a high five. “How the hell are you?” asked Andy. “I was pretty good before, but I’m even better now that you’re back bro,” Cayle proclaimed. “I can’t cope with that horrible green-ass bitch, man! And I’m still convinced that Jane doesn’t like me too much?” Andy soon came to the realisation that his wife wasn’t present; obviously his groggy mind wasn’t working too well. “Oh yeah, where the hell is Jane anyway” asleep?” he asked. “Nah, she went out a couple of hours ago, something business-related I think, said she’d be back before one?” Something “business-related?” That confused Andy; his wife worked in real estate, what the hell was she doing running around town at midnight? His mind was far too tired to consider the options any further, however, as he felt the dreadful shroud of jetlag coming over him. He opened his mouth to respond to his younger brother, but before he could a familiar green lump trundled into the kitchen, making a b-line for the Scottish King of Cool. Much to Cayle’s displeasure Grobschnit flung himself and Andy and embraced him with a massive hug. “ANNE D.! U IZ BAKZORZ!” he yelped with joy as Andy attempted to dislodge him; his embrace wasn’t doing his injured back any favours. “Grobby! Good to see you’re still as slimy as ever” but what in the hell are you doing out at this time?” Andy asked, raising an eyebrow. The nasty little green cretin and Cayle shared awkward glances, but neither opted to speak. “You know what” I’m far too tired? Cayle, make sure this motherhubbard gets into his cage ASAP, I’m going to catch some Z’s. Take it easy, bitches.” With that he slid from the stool he’d been sitting on and trundled out of the kitchen, leaving his suitcase and hand luggage behind. “You! Cage” NOW!” ordered Cayle, Grobschnit hung his head in dismay. ---- April 15th, 2007, Andy Murray’s Residence, Tallahassee, Florida. Andy woke the following day with the green, LED numbers “13:10” flashing at him from the alarm clock. He loved Sundays; it was the one day of the week where he didn’t feel guilty about not being woken up by the incessant drone of his alarm clock, even if he did arise at one o’clock in the afternoon. At this time of day it was bright enough for the sunlight to dimly illuminate the bedroom through the thin, wine-red coloured curtains which hung over his window. Andy stretched out under the sheets and felt incredibly relaxed as he closed his eyelids once again; sheer bliss he thought to himself. But almost immediately a thought crept into his mind that shattered his relaxed satisfaction, something was missing? “Shit, where the fuck is Jane!”” Andy sat almost bolt upright in his bed and glanced to his right. The side of the bed that she thought she would find his partner in was occupied only by a coupled of ruffled pillows. Her pulse rate began to increase. Where the hell is she?! Did she even come home last night!”! All kinds of unsavoury thoughts began to fill Andy’s head as he began to panic. He scanned his eyes around the room quickly ? other than the ruffled pillows, there was no other sign that Jane was about.. Quickly Andy slid out of his side of the bed and grabbed a discarded t-shirt from the floor, throwing it over his torso as he made for the room’s exit. He pulled open the door and hurriedly took a step forward, but in doing so he almost knocked Jane right off her feet. He breathed a sigh of relief; there she was, standing before him with a tray of toast, orange juice, cereal and various condiments. Her ruffled hair gave Andy the impression that she too had only just recently woken up and he noticed that she was clad only in his favourite navy blue dressing gown. “Hello stranger, brought you something to eat,” she smiled. Thank God for that. She must've come back after he had dozed off; she hadn't disappeared after all. "Breakfast in bed? Love it!"
