Before turning to leave he scanned her beautiful features, etching her permanently in his brain. She was the one that sacrificed her seemingly happy life to continue his life of misery. The young man slowly walked to where his master was waiting with his head hung in shame. The long delicate fingers of his maker wiped away the miniscule remnants of blood left on the fledglings bottom lip. “There my sweet Daniel don’t you feel better now?” Her voice as sultry as a blues singer. She laced her arm through his as she escorted him off into the night. Delylah didn’t wake frightened for the first time since she had been having these disturbing dreams. She actually felt relieved that young Daniel had fed. She knew that if he hadn’t fed on that buxom blonde that he would die and for some reason she actually cared if he lived or died. She had to find a way to save him, to turn all this back around on Cleopatra. She had to find a way to free Daniel from her grasp even though he couldn’t be saved from an eternity of hell. Delylah swung her legs over the side of the bed and placed her feet onto the cool floor. She slid her arms into her jade silk robe and slowly crept out of the bedroom. Before closing the bedroom door though she took a long gaze at the body of her lover. His face was so strong yet vulnerable in his sleep. His lips twitched into a slight smile as a moan of what seemed to be pleasure escaped his lips. Delylah’s eyebrows cocked with a look of bemusement as she wondered what he was dreaming of. She slowly closed the door making a mental note to ask him about his dream later. She snuck by her mother and flicked on the computer. The monitor’s soft glow eerily lit the living room. She had to find out some information on vampires if she wanted to stand a chance at defeating Cleo and possibly freeing Daniel. Delylah searched and she searched through the millions of sites that came up on her Google search. One site in particular caught her eye. It was about the differences in vampires by their regions. Each region had a special “skill” that they possessed. The Beauvais line is of French descent. Vampires of this line have the ability to enter the dreams of humans when they sleep. If they have previously enchanted the human they will be able to enter their dreams without any resistance. Beauvais vampires can create dreams or nightmares. Delylah’s eyes scanned the page on the web browser but found nothing more than this. Now she knew of what descent Cleo was and that was the biggest lead she had. The flight was booked to Paris, France and they would continue their search there. She needed to find out who Daniel was and why the dreams of him made her feel compelled to help. She didn’t know just how to help him. Maybe he was nothing more than a trap set by a devious demon from hell. Delylah didn’t care if he were a trap she would still find him and Cleopatra. She had unfinished business with her that she had to resolve. She was slowly going insane constantly feeling that someone was plotting against her. The only one she was not afraid to tell everything to was Paul. Paul, as she has told many people, was her knight in shining armor. He was her soul mate and he was the one person that she feared Cleo would take from her. She almost jumped out of her skin when she felt a hand rest upon her shoulder. She turned quickly ready to strike but it was only her mother standing there admiring her daughter. “I am sorry Del, I didn’t mean to frighten you,” Nebt-Het whispered, “I was just admiring how much of a lovely young woman you are. Have you and Paul set a date yet?” Nebt-Het asked with an air of excitement. “What?” Delylah looked at her mother with bewilderment filling her deep green eyes, “We can’t think of getting married right now mom. Not with everything that has just happened.” Delylah’s voice raised an octave implying that her mother was insane. “What do you mean” What has happened?” Nebt-Het enquired like she hadn’t been present for any of the events. “Mom” are you all right? Don’t you remember anything of what happened? Isn’t it kind of weird for you to wake up on my couch?” Delylah looked into her mother’s eyes and she could see that she had no memory of what happened. “I came here for a visit, to” celebrate your and Paul’s engagement.” Nebt-Het stumbled over her words, trying to make sense of what her daughter was talking about. “Oh” yeah?. That’s right.” Delylah nodded in agreement an unnatural smile formed upon her lips. “I’m going back to bed mom” I think you should do the same.” Nebt-Het nodded and floated back to the couch her eyes were closed within minutes of lying down. Delylah stood at the foot of the couch just looking at her mother. Secretly hoping that she was just walking in her sleep. She knew that it could be something serious though; everyone reacts different to stressful events. Maybe this was just her mother’s way of coping with her loss? no? their loss. Delylah slowly slid back between the sheets, draping an arm over the broad chest of Paul. She slowly traced her fingers up and down his abdomen and chest. She looked at his sleeping form and this made her desire peak. They hadn’t been intimate for a long time. Paul let a small moan escape his lips as he flipped to his left side rubbing his rigid, muscular body against her side. She groaned and turned to her side as well. She snuggled her back into his brawny chest and tried to cool her desires but his infernal moaning did nothing but fuel the fire burning within her. She wished she knew what he was dreaming about. Her eyes slowly fluttered closed as she drifted off. The room was very dimly lit by the small veil covered lamps in each corner of the room. The floor was covered with massive throw pillows in various shades of purple and gold. Delylah slowly walked to where she heard moans of what sounded like ecstasy. Her curiosity was perked even though her stomach became queasy. She walked toward where the sounds were coming from and encountered a set of beads that were meant to serve as a doorway. She parted the beads and the gold satin curtains that were behind them. Her heart rose up in her throat making it hard to breathe, the room began to spin. The site that she saw was something that she never hoped would happen. She saw the naked body of her beloved entangled with the venomous snake that was her twin sister. Delylah screamed and charged the pair of lovers. She leapt onto Paul’s back sinking her nails into his chest as she wrapped her arms around him. She tried to free him from her grasp. When she finally untangled the two Cleo vanished and she was left with Paul staring up at her? he stared at her blankly? like he didn’t know who she was. Delylah raised her hand and the room was filled with the sound of a sharp stinging slap to the face. Paul still continued to look at her blankly. Delylah trying the only thing that she could think of pressed her lips to his ferociously. Still no change. “I told you I would make him my sister dear,” Her sister’s voice filled with delight, “Now you have no one” no one to rely on. I will make you feel all the pain that I have felt in my life Delylah. That is a promise.” Cleo vowed. “YOU WILL NOT TAKE HIM FROM ME YOU BITCH!” Delylah screamed and beat her fists into her thighs so hard she yelped. “Del”. Del? Hey wake up?” Paul shook the thrashing body of Delylah, “Come on baby please wake up.” Delylah’s eyes slowly fluttered open, “GET AWAY FROM ME! How could you make love to her?” Delylah tried to slap Paul but he grabbed her hand in mid swing. “What are you talking about Del?” Paul looked at her quizzically. “You know what I am talking about. You slept with my” sister.” Delylah hissed as she crouched her body up and was ready to strike like a viper. “Del” I was right here in bed next to you all night. Don’t you remember us? ya know?” Paul’s face turned crimson. “I” I don’t remember Paul,” guilt filled Delylah’s voice as she lowered her guard, “It must have been a horrible dream then.” Delylah tried to dismiss the feeling that he had actually cheated on her. She knew what Cleo was capable of and she just couldn’t shake the feeling that she is whom he was dreaming of last night. She dug down deep inside of herself and covered up all of her suspicions. She couldn’t let the demon spawn drive a wedge between her and Paul. “I found out of what descent she is.” She said her voice barely above a whisper. “Is that a good thing?” Paul asked with cocked eyebrows. “Yes it is. Mom was right when she said Paris, France” she was made in France Paul.” Delylah’s voice and eyes filled with the enthusiasm of a kid in a candy store. “So when do we leave for France then?” Paul relented. “In a few days” I need someone to take care of mom. She was weird last night Paul. She acted like she didn’t even remember what had happened just a few days ago.” Delylah’s eyes turned dark with concern. Paul looked at her with befuddlement, “What?” “She thinks she is here to help me plan our wedding. I am hoping she was just sleep walking.” A spark of hope glittered behind the darkness of her eyes. “Let’s hope that’s all it is.” Paul slowly walked over toward his lover and kneeled beside the bed. He softly kissed her neck and earlobes. Delylah’s body responded with gooseflesh all over her body. Paul looked deeply into her eyes causing all suspicions to be cast away for good. “So” since were supposed to be getting married and all?” Paul slowly reached into the very back of the nightstand drawer and produced a small black box. Delylah’s heart skipped a beat and her palms became clammy. Paul slowly opened the box revealing a princess cut diamond set in a white gold band, “Delylah Nguyen, you are the only woman I ever want to be with. You make me whole and I would be honored if you would be my wife.” Tears streamed down her cheeks as she nodded voraciously, “Yes Paul, Yes!” “Now we will be forever inseparable. I will be by your side till the end of time my love.” He told her with contentment filling his voice as he slowly slid the ring on her finger. Delylah didn’t say a word as her mind began to whirl. She had wanted to marry him from the first time they kissed. She was afraid to actually go through with it though. She didn’t want their love to cloud her judgment. She needed her mind to be razor sharp when she faced off with her demonic twin sister for what she hoped would be the last time. “What’s wrong?” Paul asked as he kissed her cheek. “Nothing. I” I? Can we wait to get married until after I deal with Cleo? I mean? I just don’t want to cloud our judgment with wedding planning.” Delylah smiled trying to make light of the subject. “Sure.” Paul looked kind of disappointed but quickly hid it. “I’m going to the gym this morning. That is why I originally woke you up.” He added quickly to add to the cover up. “That sounds like a good idea” do you want me to come with?” She asked as she started to get out of bed. “No?” Delylah looked at him queerly wondering why he didn’t want her to go with him. They never went anywhere alone anymore. “You stay here and try and talk to mom. Maybe she will come around and be able to give you some more information.” Paul explained before he felt the wrath of his fianc’es temper. “Good idea.” Delylah slumped back down into the bed, “I could use a little more sleep anyway.” Paul kissed her forehead and then planted a passionate kiss on her hungry lips. He turned and exited the bedroom slowly knowing that she was looking after him with burning desire. Delylah felt like she was going to explode with all the mixed emotions that were swirling in her heart and mind. “Everything happens for a reason Delylah,” She reminded herself, “Everything happens for a reason.” She hoped that the saying was true. She hoped that she would have enough strength to defeat her evil half and save the life of the young Daniel. She needed to do these things to feel like herself, to be as free as she was before she knew of Cleo.
