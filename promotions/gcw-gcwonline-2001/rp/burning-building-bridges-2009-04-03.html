Burning & Building Bridges

3 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 3, 2009 An old saying goes: "Silence is golden." Yet the silent scene that unfolded backstage at the San Diego Sports Arena was far from golden. Ring crew members went about their jobs with their heads on a swivel, still shaken from what they'd just witnessed take place in the ring that night. No one knew the right words to say about the situation, even though they all wanted to say something. They just shut their mouths and leave well enough alone. It was a strategy that had always been taken in Global Championship Wrestling when it came to the valued Establishment. If you wish to keep your job, you keep your comments to yourself. Many didn't like it, but it comes with the territory. How many others could say they work for GCW, and get to speak with the superstars who are adored by millions everyday? Many of them could even consider many of the famous in-ring competitors friends. Opening your mouth and risking everything just wasn't worth the trouble. Especially in today's economy. There was no certain job on the other end of the tunnel. So what's a concerned employee to do? Nothing. They always did nothing. Crossing The Establishment was taboo to the highest degree. It was hard for many to live with the guilt, not even being able to make eye contact with each other... in shame that someone would witness the fact that they all stood by idly and did nothing while great people were thrown to the wolves by Steven Caldera and his favorites. The atmosphere had become a tyranny to the highest degree. There was a glimmer of hope though. One girl had stood up to The Establishment and won. Vivica J. Valentine. To them she was a hero, a savior, the messiah of the working class of Global Championship Wrestling. That's why everyone was shocked in silence this particular evening. They'd just witnessed what all of them thought would never happen. Their messiah had been fed to the wolves, and for the first time in her career... she was helpless. Battered and beaten in front of millions. Her fate was the one of everyone who'd ever stood in The Establishment's way. She was broken. Stomp... Stomp... Stomp... The sound of footsteps down the hallway stopped the entire GCW crew in their tracks. Cameramen, Sound Technicians, Ring Crew, Maintenance, Talent Scouts, Road Agents, Gophers, and Caterers alike froze and watched as she walked down the hallway. With a limp in her step and blood trickling down her face she marched through the frozen silence, accompanied only by the sounds of her own footsteps and the ringing in her ears left by a possible concussion. Her arms were covered with bruises and bits of smeared blood. Dried blood matted her golden blonde hair together, almost nullifying the bright red streak as she wiped the crimson mask away from her eyes. Her wounds went deeper than the gash on her forehead left by the GCW World Tag Team Championship. It was a betrayal at its finest. A knife in her back that would leave a scar so defined that it may never heal. Courtesy of Chris Storm and Markus Stone... newest members of The Establishment. While she never saw eye to eye with the duo known as Banned & Exiled~!... she found their insulting and annoying brand of comedy to be nothing more than immature pranks pulled by a couple with the combined mentality of a seven year old. Yet they shared the respect and love of fans all around the world. A connection that would often send most GCW fans home from the live event carrying both a Valentine and B&E~! t-shirt to add to their wardrobe. Even more so due to their relationship with Vivy's on-again-off-again best friend Tessa Windsor, who for whatever reason fell in love with Chris Storm. In her experience in dealing with the two immature frat boys, she'd learned to expect almost anything from them. But somehow she'd never expected something like this. Never in her wildest imagination did she expect to get struck down by chair shots wielded by the fan favorites now on the deep pockets of Steven Caldera. Still in a bit of shock herself, she stopped and looked around the backstage area. Everyone's eyes fixed at her as she winced in pain, leaving the employees at a loss for words. "Miss Valentine, you really need to get to the clinic." Someone finally spoke up. It was an EMT in the corner, standing next to Ayake Sonoda.... interviewer and friend of The Fearless Phenom. Ayake couldn't make eye contact with her friend. She couldn't imagine the physical and mental pain that Vivica must be pushing through right now. If she could look in her friends eyes, she'd see a spark was ignited deep with in the heart of Vivica J. Valentine. The fire burned in cerulean irises. "I'm fine." A soft spoken reply left the blood drenched lips of Valentine. "No, I insist Miss Valentine, you may have very seriou..." "I'M FUCKING FINE, GOD DAMNIT!" A furious roar left her tiny frame. "But..." "FUCKING HELL!" Valentine interrupted Ayake Sonoda as she tried to calm her down. It was useless, the ignition had been triggered inside of Valentine's psyche. Vivica picked up a steel chair in front of her and began slamming the steel into the concrete floor backstage. WHACK! WHACK! WHACK! Staff members watched uncomfortably as she relentlessly slammed the steel into the concrete. Over, and over, and over, and over again... warping the metal frame into something that became hardly recognizable. Pieces began to fly off of the steel frame as she swung harder and harder with each passing blast. With a furious grunt Valentine flung the remnants of the steel chair across the hall, watching as it hit the painted brick wall and fell into a pile of debris on the floor. "WHERE THE FUCK DID THEY GO?!" Valentine finally screamed, looking over at Ayake Sonoda... who still couldn't quite get herself to make eye contact. "I..." Ayake stuttered, nervous about being singled out and put on the spot. "WHERE THE FUCK DID THEY GO?!" Valentine screamed again. "I don't know!" Ayake answered, "They came back here with Terror and security caught up to them and they just... disappeared." "If ANY of you see any of those fucking Establishment bastards..." Valentine looked around the room in anger, taking a mental picture in her mind of everyone who was around, "...You let them know that I'm looking for them, and you let them know that I'm going to cripple every last fucking one of them. JUST LIKE I DID STEVEN CALDERA. You got me?!" "Vivica, I know you're upset but maybe you shoul..." "FUCK OFF." She interrupted a talent scout who made the mistake of trying to intervene, "You just tell them EXACTLY what I just fucking told you." "Yes, Vivica." "All. Of. Them." Looking around at everyone one last time, The Fearless Phenom limped her way forward and began to walk away. Looking at each other but never at Vivica, the assorted group were too scared to speak up against a woman most of them would consider if not a good friend then a respected member of the backstage area. Soon she disappeared around the corner and out of sight, leaving behind only a pile of steel that was once a chair... stained with the blood off of her hands. ---- There is truth beneath the floorboards There is hope in brick and stone But they tell me just to shut my mouth Leave well enough alone But I think maybe all that's needed Is a match and gasoline Because I don't think that any one of them believes that there will be a reckoning But I will see this city burn There are still good shepherds scattered But they're far between and few And the sheep's skin that the wolves all wear is so thin I see right through And I think maybe all that's need is some gas and open flame Because I don't think that any one of them believes that fire can erase their names But I will see this city burn I said I will see this city burn We will burn it down and build it again What was buried in flame Burn it down and build it again from the bricks that remain I love this city, but I've set and numbered its days I love this city, enough that I'll set it ablaze There is truth beneath the floorboards There is hope in brick and stone But they tell me just to shut my mouth Leave well enough alone But I think that maybe all that's needed is some flint and hardened steel Because I don't think that any one of them believes that the revolution's real We will burn it down and build it again What was buried in flame Burn it down and build it again from the bricks that remain I love this city, but I've set and numbered its days I love this city, enough that I'll set it ablaze It will burn. -- "The Arsonist" by Thrice ---- Chapter One: I Get By (With A Little Help From My Friends) ---- Walking down the dimly lit corridors, The Fearless Phenom was still licking her wounds from a week ago. It still felt like only yesterday that she stood in the backstage area looking like she came straight out of the movie Carrie. Small stitches still remained on her forehead, cosmetically covered by her hair. Every woman has her secrets. The slight limp was still in her step and being straight-edge had it's drawbacks. There were no pain killers to numb the pain, but over the years it was something that she'd learned to fight through. No one could deny Vivica J. Valentine was a fighter. She planned on marching into Christian Zenith's office that night and demanding Banned & Exiled~!'s heads on a platter. There was almost no shot of her getting her wish, but he hated them almost as much as she did. There was always a chance. The lone wolf approach had always worked for her in the past. Sure sometimes someone like Lia Ambrosi would come along as backup when she really needed it, but it was something she hardly ever asked for. She hardly ever asked for anything. It was a pride thing, and Vivica always had lots and lots of pride. Pulling her keycard out of her pocket she slid it in the electronic card reader before pushing open the door to her home for the day... and probably tomorrow morning. The hotel room at the Mariott Courtyard looked nice, but not much different than all the other hotels she'd stayed at in her escapades. She was usually more familiar with a hotel room she'd never stepped foot into than she did in her own house, always seeming to ask Patrick where every last thing was. Wheeling in her black suitcase, she pushed the door open with an extra shove... wincing a bit while she made her way into the room. Her back was throbbing, a nap was probably in order as she pulled up the suitcase from the floor and laid it on her bed. The life of constant travel often tended to play mind games on you. Standing in front of her suitcase she put her hands on her head, thinking about if she'd grabbed everything that she needed. Ring Gear, check. Cosmetics, check. Toiletries, check. Street clothes, check. Personal... Valentine's thought process was immediately interrupted, she felt like she was being watched. Come to think of it, she never heard the door close behind her. Reaching into her pocket she grabbed a can of pepper spray that she affectionately remembered using on Banned & Exiled~! once. Oh lord did she hope she got another chance right now. With it clinched still in her pocket she turned around and saw something rather unexpected... "I didn't want to interrupt." Tessa Windsor stood in the doorway behind her. "I almost maced you." Vivica responded, wondering why Tessa might be paying her a visit. They were once the best of friends, but lately their friendship had left much to be desired. She couldn't imagine that after one day of trying to make it work again that The Teeny Tiny Terror would already be coming to her hotel room to seek solace. Surely she had other friends who she trusted much more at this point, and rightfully so. "Did you need any help, or anything?" "With what? This tiny suitcase? I think I'm good." "No." Tessa paused for a moment, "With The Establishment." "Heh..." Vivica puffed at the suggestion, "I think they're the ones who need the help." "You're planning on taking them all on by yourself again, aren't you?" Tessa suggested. Leaving Valentine just staring back at her in silence. Wondering what it was exactly her friend was getting to. "I'll manage." Valentine answered, "I always do." Vivica went to unzip her suitcase, ignoring whatever it was Tessa might be leading towards. She didn't know what it was, but whatever she was getting at couldn't be good. A lecture surely wasn't the best approach to take at this point in time. The adjective 'irate' could still be used to describe Valentine's general demeanor. She was far from over the incident. As a matter of fact for the first time in her GCW career she decided to skip on the opportunity to swing by home and instead stay over a few days in San Diego. "You don't look like you're managing so good right now." Tessa finally spoke up. "I'm fine." Vivica said as she spun around, hoping that Tess didn't see the cringe on her face when she tweaked her back answering the statement. Tessa did see it though. She'd seen it when she was catching up to Vivica in the hallway. "Just some bruises. Nothing we all don't deal with." "But most of us have some kind of medication." "I don't need that stuff." Valentine responded almost before Tessa could finish her sentence. "I know you don't..." Tessa answered, "But you're really not planning on doing this alone, are you?" "What's your sudden interest?" Vivica snapped a bit, a sure side-effect of her attitude she'd been sporting the past week. "Have I not handled myself alright without you around?" "It isn't that at all!" Tessa exclaimed, "There's just no need to go at it alone. Why put yourself through that? You're planning on fighting what... six of the best GCW has by yourself, when they have Caldera in their pocket?" Valentine just continued to rummage through her suitcase in silence as Tessa went on with her rant. "You injured Steven Caldera." Tessa said, "You think he'll stop at anything short of crippling you? You don't even take medication Viv. Do you know what he can do to you?" "Maybe you need to have more faith in me." "Maybe you need to have more faith in me!" Tessa exclaimed, legitimately concerned for her friend who has seemed to go off the deep end. "My instead of going around like you're Wonder Woman you should accept some freaking help for once, Viv! Or is Vivica J. Valentine is too good to accept help? Always has to be stubborn and do everything alone. You know that pain you feel when you turn around? That's what happens to you when you try to be Rambo all the time. You know that beating we took last week... if you'd just accept a little bit of help every once in a while maybe we..." "Enough!" Vivica interrupted. The only think she hated more than accepting help might've been being wrong, but she was. She'd never admit it, but she was willing to create career suicide just to keep her pride intact. Her entire body hurt, it wasn't just her back. Every step came with just a bit of a wince, and even though she could never get herself to ask for any help... she'd put herself in a situation where she desperately needed it. She'd still never ask for it though. "You're never going to trust me if you don't let me help you." Tessa responded finally, after a bit of silence. "You're going to kill yourself out there, and there are going to be a lot of disappointed kids and big fans in the stands. Nobody gets a reaction like 'The Aerial Artisan'." Tessa was right. Vivica just took a deep breath, trying to push back her frustration with being wrong. It certainly wasn't one of her more endearing moments. "So you really want to help out huh?" Vivica spoke, while reaching into her suitcase. She pulled out a black bandana and wrapped it around her hand before pulling it apart and looking at it. She'd had a lot of history with bandanas. Not this specific one which she bought in a set during their last tour but it held a special place to her regardless. Revolution, rebellion, anti-establishment... that's what the bandana had always stood for. At lease, before Jay Terror bastardized it in front of millions by joining The Establishment. "I do." "Then take this." Vivica tossed Tessa the bandana, and she caught it in her hand and looked down at it. "What's this?" "It's a bandana. I bought it about a month ago." Valentine responded. "Why do you want me to have this?" "Let's just say it's a token of my friendship." The Fearless Phenom said, "If you're serious about this, hold on to that for me. It'd mean a lot to me, think of it as a keepsake. It'd let me know I could trust you." Tessa just looks down at the bandana before staring back at Valentine, still slightly confused. She held onto it anyway though. For whatever reason it meant something personal to Vivica, and she could see that in her deep cerulean blue eyes. "I just wanted to let you know, if you are really serious about this..." Vivica paused, "We're going to go through hell. This isn't going to be easy on any of us." "You always told me if you do things right, it won't be easy." Tessa remembered a conversation, a long time ago she'd had with Valentine. Before all of the drama, the falling out, and the epic battles fueled by pride, insecurity, and envy. "In this case..." Vivica closed, "it's going to be very right." ---- Chapter Two: Courting The Queen ---- "So... was it everything you'd dreamed of?" The snarky voice of Lindsay Troy bounced around the corridors in Boston, Mass. A crowd could still be heard going crazy if you listened hard enough, and for the first time in what seemed like a lifetime... a smile crossed the face of The Fearless Phenom. But it was just a taste of the revenge she wanted so badly. It wasn't the bodies of DeBough & Fisher she wanted to leave slaughtered, but the bodes of Banned & Exiled~! who's wronged her the week before. "I wouldn't quite say that." Vivica answered, "But laying into those jackoffs sure did feel great." "You looked worried, didn't think I'd make it?" Lindsay smirked as the made their way towards the locker room. "You have so little faith in me." "Well last time we spoke... I believe you were kicking me." "Yeah, I beat you good too." Troy responded immediately, "But you deserved it. GCW scum. I never know if I can quite trust anyone who accepts paychecks from Steven Caldera." "I certainly don't receive Christmas Cards." Vivica remarked, looking up at the woman who was Vivica J. Valentine before there was a Vivica J. Valentine, "But if you give me that rematch..." "You name the place, and I'll try to fit it into my schedule." Lindsay still seemed a bit out of place backstage at a Global Championship Wrestling show. A lot of weary eyes were in the crowd that night. It was her surrogate hometown, but they were still GCW fans at heart. None of them knew quite what to expect from Queen Of The Ring, resident of PRIME. "You're not dodging me are you?" Valentine hinted. "No..." Troy reassured her with a shake of the head, "I just have nothing to prove to you. I've beaten you. You have to prove to me that you deserve another shot. Last time I beat you, Stevie-poo wouldn't even air the match. It must've been pretty embarrassing." "You saw me out there, I'm just as good as you are." "I wouldn't get your hopes up... what do you say again? Sunshine?" Troy responded. "Plus I just did you a favor. Didn't I? You did say that you owed me one. I'll keep you to that. You should be thanking me right now." Vivica found herself a bit frustrated by her improtu tag team partner. On one hand Lindsay Troy was one of the most, if not THE most, respected female wrestlers this business had ever seen. Vivica J. Valentine might very well be #2. Sure Vivica won a World Championship, but Troy had done it more than once... and almost every word that came out of the mouth of The Fearless Phenom was trumped by what The Queen had to say. Valentine may be one of the best in the world, but she still had some things to learn. One of those things, is never try to win an argument with Lindsay Troy. "I did say thank you." Vivica responded rolling her eyes, "Although I could've handled those two pricks myself." "I'd have high expectations than that." Troy was quick to bring Valentine back down to Earth, "I think I've had bigger challenges from interviewers in PRIME." "Please, I think you're going to see how tough it is around here one of these days." Vivica nodded her head as she peered over at Troy. "Again, I wouldn't get your hopes up." The Queen Of The Ring retaliated, "I think my biggest challenge being here in GCW is resisting the urge to stab people because of Chris Bagwell's pedestrian trash talking." Valentine snickered at the premise, before doing an impression. "You should be in the kitchen, making pie and stuff... cause I'm a man. Men do manly things, women should be cleaning up after my lazy ass. I have a penis, and it's small stature makes me attempt to compensate by acting like a retard with a severe self-confidence problem." Valentine tried to do a Jersey accent and fails miserably. Troy doesn't seem to be as amused by it as Vivica was. "Don't quit your wrestling gig." For some reason Vivica always felt the need to try and impress Lindsay, often acting like somebody she wasn't to try and see if it works. If Vivica ever had an older sister growing up, she had a feeling that this is what it'd be like always living in someone else shadow. Lia Ambrosi was different, far different than this. Lia was always teaching her new things, she knew if she ever screwed up that Lia would be there to help her back to her feet and teach her what she did wrong. She was much more than a friend, she was more of a mentor... and kind of a mother figure for Vivica. "You should hear me do Jeff From Northern Ohi..." Vivica paused for a moment and looked around the backstage area. Everyone was staring at them. Almost in shock. Maybe it was just because Lindsay Troy was there, but Vivica had a feeling it was something more. It was almost exactly like the week before, where no one knew exactly what to say. Last time she was battered, bloody, and hurt... but this time she was triumphant. The expressions of the people had all changed. Instead of looking away, they greeted The Fearless Phenom and her partner with a smile. It felt good to be back on top, but there was still a lot of work to be done. "Northern Ohio?" Troy interrupted Valentine's thought process. "Yeah." Vivica responded, not quite remembering where she left off. Reaching into her right pocket, she clinched a cloth. She'd almost forgotten she'd left it there. Pulling it out she revealed a black bandana to everyone who was gawking, and Lindsay Troy just looked back at her awkwardly. "Do you always walk around with tacky bandanas in your pocket?" Troy questioned. "No." Vivica responded unfolding it, "It's for you." "No, I think I'm good." The Queen didn't hesitate. "Hey." Vivica insisted, "I'm sure you have your little quirks and rituals, this is one of mine. It's a keepsake. From me to you. If you take this, it'd mean a lot to me." "Why?" "Just don't question it." Vivica answered, "If you trust me, just take it. Hang onto it, do whatever you'd like but just don't lose it. If you have that, I'll trust you too." "Okay..." Lindsay Troy grabbed the bandana out of Vivica's hand, all the while staring at her like she had twelve eyes. "You think I'm weird don't you?" Vivica interrogated Lindsay Troy. "Yes." The Queen didn't hesitate at all, "I think you're very weird, Vivica. Right now I'm questioning why I cam here." "I'm not the one who invented face checking." "I had a very good reason for that." Troy defended herself, always on the ball. Always having a quick tongue and knowing exactly what to say, "You're just lucky you passed. Or else I would've beat you then. Then you would've lost twice." "I'll get you back one of these days." Vivica said with a smirk as Troy reluctantly pushed the black bandana down into her pocket, "Just you wait and see." "I think you should be more worried about getting those Tag Titles from Team Bromance." "Don't you worry about me being ready for those two assholes..." Vivica's good mood quickly turned sour at the mention of Banned & Exiled~!, "I've been waiting a long time to shut them up." It was then that Vivica realized that quite possibly for the first time in GCW, a championship didn't really mean that much to her. She was in line to hold gold once again, and it wasn't even a priority. The priority was teaching B&E~! a lesson. She wanted payback. The lesson was... hell hath no fury like a woman scorned, and Valentine was beyond scorned. She was driven. The conchairto that night ignited a flame inside of Vivica that had been dormant for a while. It was the flame that carried her to the longest World Championship reign in GCW history. It was the spark of rebellion. That wild anti-authority streak that'd led her to leave home at the age of 16 and seek new challenges. She was like a mustang untamed. She was born for this. A revolutionary born in the wrong time. Her desire for the destruction of The Establishment was rekindled. It might've been the biggest mistake Steven Caldera had ever made. He'd awoken a sleeping giant inside the tiny 5' 4" frame of The Fearless Phenom. And now... there was hell to pay. ---- Chapter Three: The Reckoning ---- I've spent three years in this company. For three years I've watched The Establishment do what they want... whenever they want to, to anyone they want to. For three years I've watched an entire locker room sit back and watch in near silence because it didn't concern them. For three years I've seen some of the greatest talent this promotion has ever seen held by because Steven Caldera wasn't their bff forever. For three years... I've watched as people have come and gone. I've seen the debut of stars. I've seen the retirement of legends. I've seen Hall Of Famers and Never-Had-Beens win, lose, bleed, and cry. I've far from seen it all... but the more things change the more they stay the same. And I'm sick and tired of what I've seen. It's time that we all take a stand for what we believe in. We got into our place in this business with hard work and dedication. We've paid for our place in this industry with our blood, sweat, and tears. Many of us have had to make sacrifices that we'd never dreamed of. We had to give up our families, our friends, our free time, our private lives, and in my case... love. All for this? All to watch Steven Caldera hand pick his champions only taking into consideration how much ass-kissing they're willing to do and not take into consideration our talent, athleticism, dedication, and the thoughts and opinions of the millions of fans who pay to let us do this for a living. We need to band together. It's time to let The Establishment know that we won't stand by in silence anymore. This is a company build on our sacrifices. No more will the accolades of the few be built on the backs of the masses. It's time to burn down this city. It's time to let them know that what we believe in is here and it's real. It's time to let them know that we're not tolerating their shit anymore. It's time to take back what's rightfully ours. What we fought for. What we've bled for. What we've dreamed of. To show the next generation of fighters that fame, fortune, and success is built on equal parts hard work and natural ability. With this black bandana I'm not the arsonist, but the arson. I am furious anger incarnate and I'm fucking pissed. I'm the flame that's going to burn all you motherfuckers down. I am the flame that will turn this boy's club to ash. I'm not going to rest until they pay with their blood, their swear, their tears. It's time they made sacrifices like the rest of us. I refuse to be your martyr anymore. Chris Storm, Jay Terror, Markus Stone... I'm going to get ahold of you you'll pay for your fucking sins. Every boot against your skull will be for all the times you've wronged me, my friends, and those fans who pay your god damn checks. I love this business and those people, and I'm not going to let you shit on them anymore. This is our home. This is the love of our lives. This is what we've earned. This industry is our life. I'm going out there and I'm going to put myself through hell for what I believe in, because I'm going to do exactly what those thousands of people tell you I'm going to do... VIVY'S GONNA KILL YOU. Then I'm going to take the only thing all of you assholes care about. Your championships. Jay Terror... Banned & Exiled~!... I'll see you in hell.
