It's Always Awkward to See Daddy...

Date: 02/03/10 [AUTHOR’S NOTE: Thanks to Joe for giving me extremely destructive, uh, ‘constructive’ feedback on this roleplay. I hope it works.] [1 Quadrillion A.D.] [Somewhere in the Midwest of ancient America. Perhaps Chicago.] The sound of an ancient drum circle echoes across the frozen tundra of a glimmering valley. A lone wolf howls in the distance as a herd of unicorns race the fleeting sun across the horizon. As the last rays of light fall behind the dark blue hills, the unmistakable hum of a Native American chant begins. Basically, think of a background appropriate for a “Pure Moods” commercial. The chant picks up in intensity as a dozen Natives dance their way around a brightly glowing bonfire. They dance in sacred formations to an array of epic and ancient tunes, but they’re not very good dancers. In fact, they dance quite like P Diddy. They slightly roll their shoulders back and bounce slightly side-to-side, confused, as if they’re afraid to bust it out. As the music comes to a sudden halt, followed by a quick John Bonham-like drum solo, the Natives begin to speak in ancient tongues. Not in any specific language, but in standard generic babble. This is then translated into English subtitles for your convenience. A husky man hobbles around the flowing embers of the fire. He is adorned in many layers of bristled mammoth skins and has blue face paint with little white Nike logos scattered about. He clears his throat to speak and the others stand at attention. “We are here in honor of the sacred earth, the water, wind, and fire,” the man says, while pointing up toward the heavens. “Except when the sacred fire burns our damn fingers and we’re forced to smoke the sacred pipe until we soar as high as brother eagle, which in turn causes us to raid our reserves for mammoth meat.” Next to the medicine man, an even more elaborately dressed figure steps forward and shakes his head. He has a full plume of peacock feathers running down his back, tye-dye snow pants, and buffalo horns attached to where his nipples presumably preside. He puts his hand on the medicine mane’s shoulders and says, “You burn yourself on purpose, don’t you?” The medicine man looks slightly offended. “I disproportionally get stuck boiling the stew water for saber tooth meat, so it’s inevitable that I get burned more often than all y’all,” he counters. The chief rolls his eyes. “You volunteer every day and then you get burned every day. And then you smoke all of the herb,” he says. Ignoring the chief, the medicine man takes a toke from his pipe. He lets out a large hit and begins to speak again. “Moving on…We are gathered here today to honor the spirits of those brave souls that we have lost in the Great War.” There are several rounds of high-pitched Taliban-esque screaming. A few natives rip off their fur clothing and begin thrusting their hips, all naked-like. Because as any good anthropologist knows, warriors love that shit. As this goes on for a moment longer than necessary, the chief steps up to the medicine man and whispers in his ear, “Maybe we should have waited to drink the sacred fermented apple cider until after the ceremony.” The medicine man shrugs, neither affirming nor challenging the chief’s wisdom. On cue, one of the Natives momentarily loses control of his over-sized saber-toothed tiger sombrero and comes dangerously close to falling into the open fire. The medicine man bangs his stick on a large drum, commanding the attention of the rowdy group. “Christ, you guys have the attention span of a…wait.” The medicine man loses his chain of thought due to the passing by a butterfly, which he imitates by lifting both arms and whimsically frolicking about. The chief grabs the medicine man by the shirt and pulls him back into the circle. The medicine man nods a thank you to the chief before continuing on. “Generations from now,” he says in a commanding tone, “legend will tell of our tribe and how we defeated the strange beings from the under earth.” As the medicine man relates the epic story, one of the Natives presses the “PLAY” button on an ancient Native MP3 player, and standard 50’s movies horror music begins to play in the background. The medicine man sways gently back in forth to the spooooooky music. “Legend will tell how these beasts came from the sky and reigned hell upon us like unrelenting herpes. We lost many warriors in the ensuing struggle.” He pauses as the tribesmen momentarily bow their heads in collective grief. “Luckily,” he says, “Horny Horse’s suggestion to rub their blankets with small pox worked like a charm” The whole tribe shouts in unison: “Blessed be!” A random warrior steps forward and gives the medicine man a quizzical glance. “Although it was a kind of underhanded and very un-warrior like thing to do…” he says, over the intermittent snickering of the rest of the tribe. The chief shrugs and says, “Eh. But we won.” “I’m not dissing it or anything,” says the random warrior, defensively. “I’m just saying it’s bad karma. You know, what comes around goes around.” There’s an awkward silence for a moment, but it’s quickly interrupted by the medicine man. “Despite Starving Bears reservation, and by ‘reservation’ I don’t mean the property that we forced him to live on, because that kind of pun is at best politically incorrect and at worst, extremely insensitive…” He stops to clear his throat. “Anyway, where were we? Ah yes. We were able to endure this Great War, despite hardships, setbacks, and less leisure time to smoke weed. And many, many moons from now our victory will be honored.” The medicine man takes a toke from the pipe again before passing it on to the right-hand side. Which just isn’t good etiquette. He then concludes, “We shall celebrate our victory by smoking sacred grass and tapping ancient ass.” The whole tribe in yells in unison, “Blessed be!” “And so to honor those brave warriors who gave their lives and their smallpox so that we could thrive as a nation…,” says the medicine man, “we now take this object that was captured from the enemy, that is suspiciously shaped like a very large egg, and dedicate it to the earth.” The whole tribe in yells in unison, “Blessed be!” With much fanfare and jubilation, several warriors unfurl a large bundle of deer skin to reveal a suspiciously large metallic egg-shaped object. They drunkenly toss it into a freshly dug hole. A couple of the natives kick dirt over the top of the suspiciously large egg-shaped object. But for the most part it’s left uncovered. “May you rest in peace, fallen brothers,” says the chief as he stumbles toward the suspiciously large egg-shaped object. “We honor you in-,” he hiccups, “death as we did in,” hiccup, “life.” He takes a swig of hard cider from a cheap plastic flask before tossing a few drops on the half-buried suspiciously large egg-shaped object. “Except for you, Faggy Deer, who we generally treated like a pile of saber tooth dung.” “And who, at times,” adds the medicine man, “who we literally fed saber tooth dung.” “And who, at times,” quips an extremely inebriated warrior, “I fucked the sister of.” He immediately receives a round of high fives from his companions. As well as one double tap on the back man-hug. “In your honor,” yells the chief in a booming voice, “we therefore curse this hallowed ground. We ask the spirits to protect our fallen brothers and to act with vengefulness on any who may trespass or otherwise disturb this sacred ground. But just in case you’re non-existent, great spirits, we will also collectively urinate on this suspiciously large egg-shaped object.” The whole tribe in yells in unison, “Blessed be!” The chief continues, “And, in the sacred manner of our tribe, we will do so after eating,” hiccup, “asparagus, so that whoever shall touch this egg shall smell of our,” hiccup, “ urine and asparagus. May our pee-pee find its mark on any souls who should disrupt this hallowed ground. Including, but not limited to, people approximately 1 quadrillion years from now.” The whole tribe in yells in unison, “Blessed be!” Collectively, the warriors whip them out and start urinating freely on the half-buried suspiciously large egg-shaped object. A round of cheers goes out as the Chief passes around another full gourd of apple cider liquor. The extremely inebriated warrior that just made the comment about Faggy Deer and his sister seems to have two distinctly separate streams of urine. At this, the others quickly take notice. The wasted warrior shrugs, and says in a sheepish tone,” I, uh…I just fucked Faggy Deer’s sister, actually…” The medicine man closes the ceremony by saying, “mother earth, we offer you our blessings. Goodbye fallen brothers. They bury the suspiciously large egg-shaped object. Kind of. They mostly just kick more dirt on top of it. “Hey,” one of the more sober warriors says, “did anyone notice how suspiciously egg-shaped and large that half-buried object was?” “Pfft. No,” says the chief. [New Scene.] [Present day America.] [In the present.] [Today, actually. At PAZUZU’S raggedy suburban apartment.] “And to think, some of them believe I’m ‘trying too hard to be funny?’ Christ, haven’t they seen Jeff Dunham?” PAZUZU laments. “Who?” asks Gnark. “Exactly,” says, PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS, as he sits comfortably on a cheap fake leather sofa, resting his legs across the top of a broken television set. Our demon and his sidekick, the enviable Gnark, are evidently making the most of their GCW sign-on bonus check. They have made their home in a dilapidated and cryptic urban apartment that reeks of rotten Salisbury steaks and gummy bear farts. Their humble abode. “Let us feast, minion,” declares PAZUZU, “for I have the munchies like a mother fucker.” Gnark reaches into a small McDonald’s “Happy Meal” box and tosses his demon master a handful of chicken nuggets. The imp then grabs the cheap plastic toy, a primate action figure with a two-toned ass resembling “Rafiki” from the “Lion King.” Gnark unflinchingly pops it into his mouth, plastic wrapper and all. “Gnark, must you be so small-minded when it comes to deciding which way to fulfill your daily caloric intake?” PAZUZU shakes his head at his sidekick. “Besides, the doctor said you must watch your sodium level.” “Yeah, I know,” says Gnark. “But how many people can brag about shitting out a plastic baboon in the morning?” “Touché” PAZUZU devours his nuggets before setting his attention back to a bong that sits awkwardly on the air hockey table in front of him. It’s covered in “New Kids on the Block” stickers. PAZUZU salivates in anticipation as he grasps the two-foot bong with crooked, yearning claws. It’s quite evident that he wants none other than to take a massive rip of that dank green buddage and fill his lungs with the sweet vapor of “where are the cookies?” “Master,” says Gnark, “I was just thinking that we haven’t done a good possession in a while. Should we indulge ourselves this fine evening?” “I don’t know. It’s a toss-up between that and masturbation followed by a Tony’s microwavable pizza,” PAZUZU responds, scratching his chin in consideration of these possibilities. “Tantalizing!” responds Gnark. PAZUZU brings the pipe up to his mouth to give it a nice, long drag. [Time Out.] No, not that. The bong, you pervert. “The last line was lame,” says PAZUZU, scolding the author. “The asides aren’t working out for you this time around, dude.” [Back to the story.] “Didn’t your mother ever tell you not to smoke and possess?” Gnark teases. “Yeah, yeah. I know.” PAZUZU pauses to take another massive rip. “Because you might get busted for double-possession: one for possessing a human body, the other for possessing the herb.” “I try to stay away from that shit altogether,” Gnark mentions in a taking-the-high-road tone, while continuing to munch down on the Rafiki action figure. “I get diarrheic as a midget in heat when I smokes thems shits." “Fuck your pedestrian ways, Gnark. I always smoke before I possess.” PAZUZU leans back with a reflective look in his eye. Or maybe it’s just the THC. “And besides…,” he coughs, “Don’t you remember last time I tried to possess someone while sober?” “Ah, yes; that slutty bitch and her stupid husband with all the filming equipment? How could I forget?” “Yes, and they never even gave me credit for the film!” PAZUZU adds. “ ‘A Pair of Normal Activities.’ Quite a fitting title, really,” says Gnark.“They were most boring-ass couple I’ve ever seen possessed. Good grief.” “I know, and all I wanted to do was chill for a while!” recounts PAZUZU. “You know? Maybe give the broad a brain tumor or something and then split.” PAZUZU clenches his fists in anger. “Instead, that cock had to go and spread baby powder all over the floor, which tickled my damn feet! Clearly, I had no recourse but to kill him.” “Pfft! And that Ouija Board?” Gnark says, shaking his head. “I mean, what: does Milton Bradley have a copyright on all séance media these days or something? Whatever happened to the good old days of straight-up withcraftery!” “Yeah, more like Ouija BORED!” says PAZUZU. “That’s why I lit it on fire. With my mind. Hiiiiigh….” Just as PAZUZU reaches a lazy arm out for the bong, it shatters from within. A rapidly growing figure begins to take form, casting off the pipe like it’s some kind of stoner egg-shell. “That shit’s bad for you, son,” a deep voice booms across the room, sending a shiver down PAZUZU’S spine. Getting to his feet to greet the unwelcome newcomer, PAZUZU’S fear quickly subsides to anger. “Oooooh, right, dad!” PAZUZU exclaims with a not-so-subtle hint of sarcasm. “And cancer isn’t?” he asks accusingly. Somewhat defensively, Satan responds, “Yeah, yeah. It wasn’t my proudest invention, I’ll admit. Whattaya expect when you get together with the likes of Hades, Dracula, and the demon dude in the painting from Ghostbusters II, and then throw a bottle of aged bourbon into the equation? Not a good day after, either.” PAZUZU shakes his head in contempt for the evil master of eternal damnation who stands in front of him. Most people call the figure “The Desolate One,” but P-ZUZ, embarrassingly enough, calls him “daddy.” “So what are you here for now, to say “I love you?” PAZUZU asks. “Huh? Feeling any guilt after 20,000 centuries of ignoring your only 6,342nd son?” Satan laughs hysterically. Regaining his composure for a moment, he replies “Ahaha! Oh, heeeell no. I just wanted to let you know that I’ve taken the keys to your hand-basket and that you’re no longer allowed to teleport.” PAZUZU begins to stomp in place like an enraged toddler. “WHAT!? You CAN’T be serious! Teleportation is how I boost up the mileage on my pedometer!” There’s a momentary flash of red on the demon’s otherwise stoic face. He shrugs. “I’m too lazy to exercise.” “I prefer wall-sits and jumping-jacks interspersed with random acts of exposing myself to unsuspecting old women…” Gnark says, much to the ire of the battling father and son. PAZUZU and Satan both shake their horned heads. “The hand-basket is my only ride to hell!” PAZUZU exclaims. “Son, you chose an earthly life of boundless pleasures over one of eternal torment and eternal damnation. Now you must deal with the consequences!” PAZUZU is not impressed, showing his displeasure by folding his arms across his chest and pouting.“That makes less sense than a signed copy of the ‘Collected Works of Nonsense-icleles: the Retarded Greek Philosopher’!” “Son, you’ve planted your bed of cornflakes,” Satan retorts, “and now you must reap the urine.” “I am so fucking confused right now…” says Gnark. Satan kicks the remaining pieces of the pot pipe aside and steps up to his son, looming over GCW’s new star by a full half a head in height. “And just to be sure that you fully enjoy the displeasure of being attached to your body of flesh, I am hereby revoking all of your otherworldly powers. No exceptions.” “None whatsoever?” PAZUZU asks, unsure how to respond. “Well… I guess levitation, invisibility, telepathy, and flight are okay. But in light of your new found hobby of pro-wrestling, however, superhuman strength is out of the question.” PAZUZU begins to stomp around the room with more gusto than a crack-head stepping on broken glass. “This is 100%, unadorned, all-natural, organic BULLSHIT! “ “I, too, will exercise my right to YELL!” says Gnark, doing his best to fake anger. “And what’s more…” Gnark pulls out a can of kerosene from under the table and douses himself. He lights a match, tosses it on his chest, and immediately bursts into flames. “Shit, that hurts,” he says as Satan looks on, clearly not amused. Before the blaze can get out of control, Satan quenches the flames by shooting fire extinguisher foam from his horns, effectively burying the imp. “Watch the carpet!” PAZUZU protests. “Quit being a whiny bitch, son. And Gnark, mind your manners, imp.” “If I had manners, I’d be somewhat respectable,” Gnark counters. “Does this look ‘respectable’ to you?” Gnark whips it out for, like, the 5th time in three roleplays , and begins to urinate. What were you complaining about anyway?” “Oh, you know, same old, same old,” says Satan, letting out a slight sigh. “Gabriel wanted a 40-hour workweek, Raphael wanted a vending machine in the break room, and I wanted God to step down as CEO of heaven for a while. And for some reason he just took it out harder on me.” “You actually asked God to step down? I know you have balls of steel, dad. Well, a uranium-dragon sperm alloy, at least. But God? We needed someone with a softer touch.” “Yep,” says Gnark, pausing to let out a belch that smells of burnt plastic and kerosene. “When I think ‘soft touch,’ I think of you, Satan. No homo.” “So just how do you plan on enforcing this little “human form” grounding of yours’ anyway old man?” asks PAZUZU, using his tiptoes to stand eye-to-eye with Satan. “I mean, you’re not fucking Santa Clause: you can’t be EVERYWHERE at once.” Satan cuts a smile. “Simple…” He reaches into his pocket and pulls out something AMAZING! So much so that PAZUZU and Gnark’s jaws drop simultaneously. Satan bellows in his loudest voice, “THE AMULET OF KROOG!” As PAZUZU does his best impression of the patented “Home Alone” shocked facial expression, Satan unceremoniously grabs his son by the throat and tosses the plastic-looking pendant around his neck. “I thought we destroyed that in the Great War!” PAZUZU stammers. “Where did you come across it?” “One of those dispensary machines in an arcade,” replies Satan, matter-of-factly. “I knew it!” says Gnark. Satan begins to pace back and forth, telling the epic tale of how he finally came across the AMULET of KROOG! “I wasted $13.75 in quarters until I finally got the fucker. That damn claw never seems to grab ancient relics very well. At first I considered trading it in for more tokens. I was only 1,700 tickets short of winning a 3-foot purple teddy-bear . But then I thought I might teach you a lesson instead.” “And what’s to keep me from taking off such a faggy device?” asks PAZUZU, his fingers already wrapped around the pendant, ready to rip it off. “Yeah!” Gnark adds. “What if we join a nudist colony where, like, even jewelry and other accessories aren’t allowed? Then he’d have to take it off. Because he wouldn’t be totally naked.” Gnark angrily pounds the air hockey table. “And then we’d be the laughing stock of the whole damn town!” “Simple,” says Satan, sporting a devilish grin. An extremely Devilish grin, in fact. “I hereby place a CURSE on your pathetic little ass, mister! And if the AMULET OF KROOG! is ever to leave your neck, you will surely burst into flames. “Pfft! I love bursting into flames. It gets me off,” PAZUZU responds, high-fiving Gnark. “To put it more precisely,” explains Satan, “I'm talking about the ‘your-human-body-will-die’ kind of flames.” Gnark tips his head in quizzically, looking like a confused dog. Satan sighs. “You’ll DIE, I mean.” “Mother fucker…” says PAZUZU. “Speaking of which,” Satan laments, “I regret sleeping with that 5,000-pound troll whose vagina you suddenly appeared from.” Satan clears his throat. “However, that’s neither here nor there.” PAZUZU is too shocked to say anything. He simply shakes his head as he looks down at the evil device that is wrapped securely around his neck. Gnark shrugs. “I think it looks snazzy, master. A real pussy-magnet!” “It’s getting late,” says Satan, taking the opportunity of the awkward silence to make his escape. “I really must be on my way. And you know what they say: when life gives you AIDS, make AIDS pie.” And with that the devil himself bows his head and bursts into flame, retreating to the depths of hell from whence he came. …That rhyme was accidental, I swear. [Meanwhile, somewhere in the Midwestern United States, below the hollow ground of an ancient Native burial ground, something evil is afoot.] A young boy is playing with his toy Tonka dump-truck, rolling it back and forth over a soft mound of sand. Then there’s a loud burp followed by the sight of the boys’ small baseball cap shooting back into the air. “When I find that faggy brother of mine…” The demon bursts out of the remains of its eggshell prison and takes its first breath of air.
