So I am off

1 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Oct 1, 2007 The bag hit the floor, the “THUD” echoed throughout the empty hall way. I had waited for this all my life, but it seems some people did not share my interest or my excitement. I turned the corner and there she was not looking as ecstatic as I had hoped. Tears were coming down her cheek, the look in her eyes were of betrayal. ? Do you have to go?” “Babe, you know this if my dream, this is my desire. Can’t you just support me and help me get thru it?” “I know but is just so hard. I love you and I love the fact you are going out and chasing your dream but at the same time it kills me that you are not going to be around.” The sounds were coming from her mouth but the tears were flowing from her heart. She had loved me for so long, and on a whim on a chance I just get up and leave. “Babe I will be back soon, I will talk to you every day and you will be coming to see me in two weeks. We will make it thru this. If we do we will be that much stronger.” With that I threw my arms around here, embraced and kissed her forehead. Both of us had known it was coming but neither one of us were looking forward to it happening. It was just a mere two weeks prior that I had received my letter. It stated that Global Championship Wrestling had decided to pick up my contract after Universal Wrestling, now defunct, failed to meet their end of the deal. I had yet to step into a professional ring, fresh out of school. I had wrestled all my life though so doing it for a profession only seemed correct. I had All American honors not only at Ohio State University but also while at High School. Where I grew up in rural Ohio, you did two things played football and wrestled. I never excelled at the pig skin so I made sure to excel on the mat so I wouldn’t be an outcast to the society in our rural town. But it happened, two weeks prior I had gotten the letter from Steven Caldera, they had watched my tapes, they had accepted my wrestling ability and were looking to find some young talent to come in and take over some of their bigger guys as they were slowly dwindling out of the federation. Sarah and I both knew that this was going to be a major strain on our relationship. I would be on the road most of the time and we were quickly approaching on our one year mark. Neither one of us wanted to leave each other; both physically or in terms of breaking the relationship work. It is going to take patience and a good heart on both of our parts. But I have that feeling deep down, that we will make it. Sarah embraced my hug; I squeezed her tight but not tight and was not expecting her to squeeze so hard. With the squeeze I could tell how much she actually was going to miss me. I wanted to bring her with me but the idea was not even fathomable. She looked up tears flowing from her hazel eyes. I pulled her in closer, hugged her tighter then I have ever done before. Looking deep into those gorgeous eyes I found some words to express my feelings. ? Babe even though I will be in different town every night, even though every night I won’t be here in your arms. My dreams will be field with the thought of you, the dreams will consist of all of our happy times and the happier times to still come.” I felt like a modern day poet, but the more I think about it I probably sounded really corney but she enjoyed it. “Christian I love you, we will only be apart shortly but our hearts will always be together.” With that we gave our final embrace. I picked the bag up watched her wipe the tears from her eyes. There was a cab sitting outside awaiting me for probably a good fifteen minutes, oh well he can wait. I shut the door behind me, it took a lot for me not to cry. Some might call it unmanly but the truth is when there is love involved it over takes everything. If I looked back I was sure I would cry so I made my way to the cab not looking back til my bag was in the car. I could see her in the window still crying, I blew a kiss and waved good bye. Ducked my head and sat in the cab. With that I was off, I was starting my journey of becoming a professional wrestler.
