The Battle

29 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-29-05 I couldn’t believe it, they should how covered. My very presence should have been enough to send the weakest among them, cowering and fleeing for the hills. I was the very image of fear, a finely crafted tapestry of terror. And yet there were no gasps, no cries of anguish from those frozen by horror. Instead they’they’they laughed. I can still hear their mocking scorn echoing in my ears. It’s horrible, and I can do nothing to silence it. My kingdom, my wealth, and all the oranges in Florida for an ice pick, so that I may gouge out my very ears. This would surely silence them. I would have to do something drastic if I could even begin to prepare myself for what would soon be to come. I’m at a cross roads, a fork in the road, to the left lies my ultimate destiny, or maybe it’s to the right. I cannot be certain any longer. When I first arrived back in the world fresh from the boat, head full Yore’s teaching I was so prepared, so focused. Yet now, I cannot be sure of anything everything is so confusing. My most valiant efforts have fallen flat, and I have not been nearly as successful in my quest as I should have. Something must be done, a change must be made. The laughter seems to bother me more and more everyday. At first I wrote it off as simply a new culture learning to adjust to the mysteries and vagaries of Fruit-Fu. Perhaps they used their laughter to hide their fear at what may lie behind my bandages. I could deal with this, it meant simply that my campaign was well on it’s way to success. Now it seems that their laughter hides nothing and is in fact mirth. Could I truly be a joke? No, of course not, that couldn’t be the truth. It must be some devious trick from one of my more clever enemies. Yes that must be it, they’re tricking people into laughing at me, knowing what effect it must have one me. I must become even more wary than before, yes on my highest alert. Wait, that could be one of them now. I crouched low to the floor, my best defensive orchard stance. I could barely make out the shadowy figure in the corner. DAMN~! Why did I have to do my thinking in the dark? In the light of a bulb I could have never been snuck up upon. Yet there it was, some vile dark lurking thing come to attack me in my moment of most terrible weakness. Must not let it know I’m aware of it’s existence. Bide my time, wait?.SPRING! I leapt up from my position and was upon him like a furious tornado of fingernails and teeth. I found quickly he must have been waiting for me, he fought back. Before I realized what was happening I was on the defensive, he was more powerful than I assumed. In those first seconds of conflicted I feared that this fiend may in fact gain the upper hand, this could be the end of RipeFist? Using all the force my years of training under Master Yore I punched forward. Odd the creature seemed soft and furry to the touch. This was not a man at all, it was a monster. Werewolf! My mind shrieked. I’d heard legends of the creatures before, but I had never believed they actually existed, I was now being proven wrong and the worst possible time. It wrapped me in warm darkness trying to smother me under it’s heavy fur. I gasp, I couldn’t breathe, there was no way out I feared. Yet, I refused to give up, this couldn’t be the end, after all if it were, I wouldn’t very well be around to tell this story, now would I? Twisting hard to the side I was able to roll out from under the creature. Thinking quickly, I came up to my feet, and dashed towards the door. Was I fleeing? Of course not, a master of Fruit-Fu never leaves a battle half done. No my goal was less the door and more what I knew to be near it. Deftly I flicked the light switch into the on position and the room filled with the piercing rays of a sixty watt bulb. Momentarily I was blinded, the light having had as ill effect on me as it must’ve had on the creature. As the stars faded from my vision I wondered at the creatures eerie silence. It hadn’t made a sound since our conflict began, that was perhaps the most unsettling thing of all. Boldly I presented my fingers crossed like well a cross. I wasn’t certain if the cross was a bane against the walking wolves, but it certainly wouldn’t hurt. Moreover I would need every advantage I could possibly get. This was one I could make for myself. Slowly the stars faded from my vision and I had the first true glimpse of my attacker. I growled preparing to leap upon the monster again, only to find it was nothing more than my Halloween costume. The giant squirrel head lay upon it’s side. Those wide white eyes staring up at me, the gleaming teeth of it’s smile mocking me. My shoulders slumped, I was dejected. This is what I had been reduced to, jumping at shadows. Bubbling up in the back of my brain I could hear the laughter once again. Surely the villains would have a good and hearty chuckle at this one, the mighty RipeFist battling a costume he occupied merely hours ago. Beating hands against my head I shrieked in inarticulate rage, and slumped to the floor, coming basically to eye level with the cheerful faux skull. It was then the idea began to form. Suddenly it all came rushing to me, and I understood. I wasn’t being set up by my rouges gallery. No there was something far different at work here. I realized without question why I had not been able to strike fear in the hearts of men. There had been something wrong with the costume, something I could fix. Yes, I had it, smiling, I set to work.
