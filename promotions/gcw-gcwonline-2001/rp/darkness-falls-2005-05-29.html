Date: 5/29 Darkness..... Darkness.....covers everything Darkness.....shadows everything around you, and nothing can be seen until its to late A night as black as outer space itself surrounds, a dank and dark place where only the scum of the eartth would dare to enter, a lonely man with no future, no past he can remember clearly, or a drunk. The clouds part in the sky's like when the red sea parted, a white light as bright as a cars headlight's gleam down from the moon itself, and illuminates the dark place, and as it does so all the vermin, and drunks run for cover in this alley like cockroaches when you open the refrigerator in the middle of the night when they think you are alseep. There is one man who is propped up against the wall, hsi head leaning against the wall like he is in a deep a very deep slumber, when all of a sudden the mans head lifts of the wall with such quickness as lighting would also appear before you With no thought at all the man walks down the alley staggering back and fourth which makes you think that hehas been drinking to much this very night, but as the man gets clsoer and closer to you, you see that it is not a stagger but actually a very bad limp, and that the man which once you thought was very short, seems to be getting bigger and bigger as he gets closer to you. Right before the man walks past, he stops, glances downwards, and utters a few words. Big Man: Its been awhile since I have visited a place like this, if my memory serves me right it has been maybe at least 4 or 5 years, and the last time i was in a place like this the voices in my head were telling me to do the wrong thing, I almost put my best friend into the hospital, and my younger brother almost six feet under, with the maggots and the worms who call that home. The big man stops talking and looks up to the moon in the sky, and as he does so the cloud seem to sense that he is staring at the sky, and move over the moon to block its light from getting down to the surface. You can hear a faint voice in the distant but it seems to be getting closer. Mysterious Voice: Darkness Mysterious Voice: Darkness which once covered the earth before the light came Mysterious Voice: This darkness that we are in now is just a way for the all the evil, and the vermin to come out of hiding to do what they do best, loot, steal, and destroy with nobody to see what they are doing, or where they are going. The voice now upon you, you get a chill down you spine as you sense something either standing next to you, or standing behind you. As you stand there, the clouds part again, and the moonlight illuminates the surface once again, and as you turn around the same man which was once off aways is now standing right behind you. Big Man: It has been two years since i have done this, it has been almost two years sinc ei almost put my best friend in the hospital, and a man that respects and idolizes me six feet under. I dropped off the face of the earth when this happened, going into suclusion, I thought long and hard about what happened, and if I can continue doing this without hurting the people I care for, and about A single tear rolls down the big man's face, as it reaches he reaches into his pocket and pulls out a hankerchief a wipes it off his face Big Man: So fianlly I have came to my final answer, this is my life, there is no way i can not say that i do not want to do this again. I live for the excitment, i live for the adventuer, and i live foir the rush of adreline when i hear my opponents head crack into the cold, and unforgiving concrete floor, this is how i got the name 'The Demented One' Dimensioner. Finally the man reveals all that was held up inside off him, and as he finishes what he is saying a devilsh grin can be seen on his face, his eyes light up with excitment. Dimensioner: Skulls hitting payment, chairs hitting skull, and my favorite a singapore cane being wrapped around my opponents head all get my adreline flowing, but the one thing that gives he the most is seeing me or my opponent bleed. Blood, our body is full with it, qand to think all the blood that i have lost, all the scars that mark my body is well worth it since your body fills back up with blood which means thats just more of it to lose when you step into the ring with me. Dimensioner: You all talk about pain? YOU ALL DON'T KNOW WHAT PAIN IS! Try having to look your little brother in the eys as he is laying in a hospital bed holding on to life my a thread, and telling him that it was you who almost killed him. Try look at your best friend, and tell him that it was you who almost took him out of the business he loved. Try loving someone with all your heart, and losing them in a heartbeat just becauseyou can not control your emper in the ring. The grin which was once on his face is now formed into a full smile, and a sinsiter laugh is coming from him Dimensioner: I bottled all this pain, anger up inside of me now, and I am ready to get back into the business that I love, the business which pay's my bills, the business which I see now is my adreniline rush, so GCW watch out 'The Demented One' Dimensioner is back, and this time around I am a force to be reckoned with, I fear that this time i will not be able to control myself in the ring, and I am scared that I will hurt someone like I did my brother As he finishes what he has to say, Dimensioner starts to walk off back down the darkness of the alley, back into the darkness in which shaodws his heart, and shadows his emotions from feeling anything at all Fade to Black Down with the Sickness
