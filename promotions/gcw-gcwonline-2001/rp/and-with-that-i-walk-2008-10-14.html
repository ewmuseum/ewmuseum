"And with that I walk..."

14 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-14-08 (A couple hours after WW100) As I sit by myself I'm sure to the outsider I currently look calm, collected, and/or stoic. If there was any way for others to listen to the inside of my head they'd be surprised how my insides are raging war on my outside. It's almost as if I'm sitting back and watching myself fight. There are too many things going on inside of my head ? too many thoughts, too many sounds, too many voices, too many recent memories. There are some things that I can't put into words as they absolutely transcend description. But I feel them all. Like hell do I ever feel them. I can't remember how long it's been since the end of WorldWide 100. Maybe an hour, maybe a few. There aren't any windows in this small room I've used to change in and keep to myself since I arrived earlier what I think was today, so there's no way of even telling if it's already tomorrow. My mind has been locked ever since the end of the show and to tell you the truth I can't remember exactly everything that has gone on upon awakening post-match with the approval of the 17,000 in attendance. ?WEL-COME HOME!! WEL-COME HOME!!? That chant ? that cry from over 17,000 people in unison. Rattled, I was shaking as I stood to my feet however long after the cameras had gone off the air; and if it was emotion or adrenaline I can't tell but at that moment I just felt like crying. Just for that moment. No one knew if I was going to stay or not, but the point was made abundantly clear that I was indeed...back home. I smiled and waved, struggling against the tears welling up in my eyes. Such a small gesture from so many meant the world at that moment. After one last thank you to everyone I left the ring and walked to the back. As soon as I was out of sight from those lights and the people something happened. Reality happened. It was an all-too familiar feeling of when my mind instantly kicks into overdrive. Reality, being the harsh bitch that she is, threw one hell of a question at me: Did I even get the job done tonight? The next few minutes were a complete blur. My mind was racing but my body was in the middle of doing everything else imaginable. I just wanted to get back to my room with my thoughts but there was so much in the way. I remember congratulations from many of the crew who were there years ago when I was the World Champ, there were several hugs and warm handshakes from the other guys in the back, but to tell you the truth even though I was talking about responding to everything happening around me, my mind just wasn't there. My right arm comes off from my leg ? it's the position I've been sitting in since I got back to this room. For some reason my face feels like the skin is peeling off. Have I gone absolutely insane? I stand from the chair I've been using, my knee greeting my with a very audible pop. I walk through the open frame to my left and look into the mirror to see what the hell is wrong. Oh. Silly me. The cut above my eyebrow is now one clumped mess of stickiness and dried blood. Dull brown streaks are still splotching my face from my match earlier tonight. What's left of the blood is crusted and cracking, some even falling to the sink under me as I try an gauge how badly I was messed up. Judging from how sticky the cut on my forehead is, I think it was reopened and made worse after Lethal's second Bandstand Bust. I take a small push on the side of the lesion with my index finger and a small amount of deep crimson blood oozes out from the middle of the cut. Looks like I'm going to need some stitches after all. I audibly chuckle to myself. It's almost like I never left when I can look into my own gaping wounds and remain completely unsurprised at the need of medical attention. ?Just like the old days? is what I utter to myself as I task myself with cleaning up. The cool water feels good on my face, as does the mask of dried blood being melted away as I splash water over it. The sink looks as though I liberally threw light red food coloring all over it by the time I'm finished washing, and the lone towel left in the room courtesy of the staff in the arena cleans up the rest of what is left on my face, arms, and hands. I try to clean off the bad cut I have but the slightest bit of pressure causes more and more blood to seep out. Realizing the futility I throw the towel aside, content in at least not looking like I've just murdered a family of four. My small task completed my mind immediately rushes back to the matters at hand. Tonight was ultimately bittersweet and I don't quite know what to take back from it. I realized from the very beginning Terror would be one hell of a threat but I almost allowed that old and cocky Jason Dread to get the front and center for the match. I'm glad that I got that taste slapped out of my mouth rather quickly as it led to my shaping up pretty damn fast. As I begin changing out of my ring attire the pros and cons of everything and fighting each other to the death. On one hand I managed for just a couple minutes to get that...indescribable rush. Despite all controversy and all formal decisions I know deep down in my heart that I was once again the GCW champion. For the second time I went out and defied all of the odds when it really mattered and came out on top. It's just a damn shame that my house of cards came tumbling down. But then again I'm torn between my obviously biased side and my overtly logical side. Part of me knows I won, I feel that; but the other part of me knows it was a bad decision. I told myself a long time ago, after the fiasco which was the crumbling of my being after the Establishment fall years ago that if I were to ever get back to the top then it would be decisive and without controversy. It would be fair, balanced, and leave no doubt as to who the better man really is. And ultimately this is where the divide in me is strongest. I lost when it really counted. There are no ifs, ands, or buts. In the straight and narrow my best just didn't happen to be good enough. I start packing the rest of the belonging scattered about the floor into my bag. A part of me is trying to be reassuring ? to rationalize that maybe it just wasn't meant for me to come and repeat history once again. Another part responds with saying I just don't have it like I used to. There are too many voices, ideas, and reasonings and frankly I'm reaching my breaking point. Sometimes I really wish I would just shut the hell up. Grabbing my bag, I head for the door. Once more for curiosity's sake I reach up and touch above my eyebrow. Nope ? still sticky. Looks like I know where I'll be for the next few hours for sure, getting myself stitch- Then it hits me. I stop dead in mid stride. Where do I go after that? There are too many options and I can't believe I haven't even considered where I want to go now. Steven has already offered me a place in GCW if I were to ever come back, and if not now then I don't know if that time could ever return...but there are far too many questions and doubts. I don't know if I have it anymore ? and not just the physical ability, but the heart. I felt like I did, but I came up decidedly short. Or did I? I thought tonight would be the answer to all my questions. I thought tonight would clearly set me down a path which I would know to be best and would be enthralled to be on. I thought tonight was going to bring back the old Jason Dread ? the former champion whose name struck fear into those not on his good side. I guess this is why I'm not getting paid to think, huh? I realize now that perhaps tonight didn't make anything crystal clear, but I have indeed left with more than I came with. The thing is I'm not leaving with gold or accolades, but with more questions than before. This isn't going to be easy. This decision I make isn't going to be able to be taken lightly. Compared to how I was merely two months ago I've come so far and proven myself to almost everyone else...but have I really proven anything to myself? I regain my composure and open the door to where I've basked in my solitary nature. Nearly everyone has cleared out that I recognize, and that's all for the better. I think I need some time. Some time alone to decide on where I go from here. There are far too many questions I can't answer myself, there are far too many doubts of where my heart truly lies. There's an old saying of ?People who can't do, teach? ; I think I may be understanding the intricacies of that statement now. If I can't do it anymore then I need to devote myself to those kids who do have the tenacity, talent, and heart to make it in my stead. On the other hand what I felt tonight...that reaction I was given...I wonder... No. I need time. Time to recoup and gain some understanding - time to figure out where I truly want to be and where the best place for me is. Tonight is momentous in that I clearly see the two paths that lie before me. Whichever one I take will completely change my life all over again. Despite anything else what I do know deep within my heart is that whatever decision I make will ultimately be for the best. I take one last look around the remains of WorldWide being packed away to move to yet another city, fully realizing this may be the last time I see something like this in person ever again. This time I know it's not adrenaline...I feel the warm tear slide down the side of my cheek and watch it fall to the ground. Even though there isn't a soul nearby to hear me I still speak as though someone has heard me all this time... ?Yeah...ultimately for the best...? And with that I walk towards those double doors in the distance which lead out to the rest of my life...
