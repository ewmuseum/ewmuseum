The Encounter: Part II

4 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/4/08 Delylah didn’t dare fall asleep even for a brief moment. She had become tired of screaming and crying for someone to let her out. Instead she looked around her parents? room as she waited to be rescued or killed. She didn’t have much of a choice but to wait. On the other side of the door was a large man whom for some reason Delylah wasn’t scared of; maybe it was the way he looked at her. She just knew that he wouldn’t be the one to harm her. She glanced at her parent’s tethered to their bed and then to the lump of a man that was Dr. Elias Nagatti in front of her only exit. There had to be a reason Cleo wanted the corpse of her adoptive father there. “It’s funny how observant you become when you have nothing to do but wait.” Delylah thought to herself as she tried to keep her mind off from the inevitable. Just as the sun started to drop Delylah found out why Dr. Nagatti was there; she had made him her child. Elias started to twist and writhe trying to release his bound feet and hands. When he looked at her his eyes were black and lifeless as his fangs sunk into his own bottom lip. Blood slowly trickled down the sides of his mouth and he let out an ear-piercing screech. Delylah didn’t know much about the undead but she did know that he was hungry. Basement of the Nguyen Home White Plains, NY 7:00 PM He just felt tired, too drained to escape the bonds that he knew he could as he watched the demon sleep. His blood was boiling at the site of her, she stirs, he fights against his bonds to try and save his beloved Delylah. Cleo sits up and immediately locks her gaze on Paul. A sinister smile twists her lips as she closes the distance between them. The demon speaks. “Paul” darling,” Cleo smiles her words dripping with false charm, “You do know what today is don’t you?” She asks as she rubs her body against his. “Don’t touch me,” his voice filled with disdain, “I do know what today is,” he smiles before spitting in her face, “it’s the day you die.” Cleopatra rears back and delivers a stinging slap to Paul’s left cheek. “You insolent prick,” she hissed, “You do not want to play games with me Paul.” Immediately her tone changes back to the syrupy one from earlier, “Why don’t you feel the right side of your neck.” Paul immediately reaches up and feels the stickiness of his own blood. “What the hell did you do to me?” He asked her hysterically, trying even harder to escape his bonds. “I just drained you a little; I was hungry,” she pouted, “Don’t worry you will be fine, I couldn’t kill you, not yet anyway.” She cackled and turned to leave but not before adding one final thought for Paul to chew on. “I want you to see what I am going to do to my darling sister.” Paul fought and fought against his bonds; with the amount of blood Cleopatra drank from him it was almost impossible to escape. “I’m so sorry Del.” Tears began to fill his eyes, “I know I made a promise to you and I don’t think I can fulfill it now,” Tears streamed down his cheeks as he choked out words meant for his lover to hear. “I love you Delylah and I hope you can forgive me.” Paul’s head slumped to the side as he sat and thought of a way to escape this place to help Delylah. Amun & Nebt- Het Nguyen’s Bedroom White Plains, NY 7:25 PM Delylah sat curled up in a ball against the foot of her parent’s bed. She jumped as soon as she heard the lock being disengaged. The door slowly crept open; her heart jumped into the back of her throat, she had hoped it was Paul. There stood her identical twin, her evil half. Her black eyes pierced holes into Delylah’s soul, her maniacal smile set goose pimples over her entire body. Delylah knew that the time was near; there was so much she hadn’t done, too much she hadn’t said to her father. She wished she could roll back the hands of time and make things right with her father. Tears streamed down her cheeks and this made Cleopatra laugh maniacally. “What’s wrong sister dear,” Cleopatra’s voice was filled with false sympathy, “are you afraid to die?” Delylah didn’t respond; she didn’t have an answer to that question. “Death isn’t really all that bad,” Cleo told Delylah as she walked closer to her, “the body only feels pain for a short time. Then you can be immortal just like me,” Her eyes filled with hope and her voice was wistful, “Maybe then we can truly be sisters.” For the first time Delylah saw remorse and longing in her sister’s eyes. She began to understand her sister a little more with just those few words. “Cleo,” Delylah stood and met her sister’s gaze, “You don’t have to make me a child of the night to be your sister.” Delylah tried to reason with her, “You can just let them all go and I will come with you. I will do whatever you want me to.” Delylah choked out trying not to show her fear. “Shut up!” Cleo screamed, slapping the right cheek of her sister; her pure half. “How do you know what I have to do?” She clenched her teeth and her fangs slowly came into view, “You have no idea what it was like for me as a child; feeling alone all the time.” Cleo’s voice was filled with emotion and Delylah thought she even seen a single tear slide down her cheek. “HA!” Delylah found that statement amusing, “You have no idea what it was like for me here.” Her voice crackled with emotion as she thought about her days at NYC College shortly after the 9/11 attacks. “Were you beat and thrown down two flights of stairs because of the color of you skin?” Her voice filling with spite, “Did you go through life with a father who regretted every decision you made?” Delylah looked to her adoptive father on the floor near Cleo’s feet, “Did he tell you whom you had to marry?” Adding extra emphasis on the word he, “I imagine he didn’t.” She spat out her last words as if they tasted like acid and turned her back on her sister. “Do NOT turn you back on me,” Cleo hissed through clenched teeth, “You may have had it rough outside of the home but at least you knew whom your real parents were. Even if they did try and run your life,” A pensive look crossed Cleo’s face, “At least they cared about you enough to try.” Before Delylah could reply Cleopatra was out the door, locking it behind her. Del knew she wasn’t far though, she could feel her. Then she heard voices just outside the door. “I have to feed before I can continue this Vassago,” Cleopatra said to Delylah’s gatekeeper, “Then we can finish all of this.” Delylah heard the clicking of Cleopatra’s heels against the hardwood floor and knew she was gone. Paul twisted and pulled at his bonds to no avail. Then it came to him, he looked down at the wooden chair, and he knew if he could break the chair he would be free. He stood up as far as he could and hobbled toward the closest wall. He took a deep breath and began ramming the chair into the wall. After numerous tries he finally heard the wood splinter and fall to the ground. He feverishly worked at the knots that bound his feet to the legs of the chair. With his legs free he began working the knots at his wrists with his teeth. Finally he was free. He stealthily moved his way up the stairs and into the pantry. He slid along the walls of the dimly lit hallway making his way to the stairs. Once he was at the bottom of the stairs he looked up, let out a heavy sigh and started to climb to the top. At the top of the stairs Paul looked left and then right to see his way clear. No one in site, he made his way to the room where Delylah and her parent’s were locked away. As soon as he put his hand on the doorknob he felt the sharp pain of being punched in the kidneys. His knees buckled a little, he spun around and met the gatekeeper face to face. Paul double up his right fist and slammed it against the left side of Vassago’s face. A sadistic smile crossed Vassago’s face as he retaliated with a right hand of his own. Paul’s head began to spin a little; they both heard a gasp come from the other side of the door. Vassago turned his head just for a moment giving Paul enough time to charge him with a shoulder to the mid-section. Vassago collapsed to the floor and Paul mounted him letting loose a flurry of right and left hands against his face. Vassago grabbed Paul by the throat and began to choke him. Slowly the lights faded and Paul was out. Vassago picked him up, unlocked the door and threw him forcefully into the far wall next to the Nguyen’s bed. The sadistic smile returned to the gatekeepers face, “Now that we have you all together again it will only be a matter of time before my mistress returns,” his maniacally laugh resonated throughout the room, “Then all the fun and games will commence.” He turned and locked the door behind him. Delylah rushed to Paul’s side and showered his face with kisses, “Paul, are you ok?” She sobbed choking on her words, “You’ve been bitten Paul.” Delylah scooted quickly away from him, wrapping her arms around her legs. “It’s ok Del,” His voice was filled with pain and sorrow, “She just drained me a little, I’ll be fine.” His eyes fell shut and Delylah was alone again. She knew her sister’s weak spot now; Cleo actually still felt emotions. She had to come up with a way to sweet talk her. A way to get her loved ones out of this unscathed. She knew she had about an hour or so left to come up with something, anything to stop Cleo. She curled up in the fetal position at Paul’s feet and sobbed uncontrollably. She was the only one who could stop this, in a way she knew it all along. She needed to get her sister emotionally worked up, to make her not think clearly. That was their only hope to survive her wrath. That is all she had left now; hope.
