True Perfection: Unwanted Territory

3 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/3/09 True Perfection: Unwanted Territory………. (The camera comes on outside the Perfection Inc building. There are a ton of reporters and camera crews outside the building trying to catch a glimpse of some Bishop Steele as he announces the grand opening of a new store in Kingston, Jamaica.) 2 hours earlier inside the Steele mansion………… Alexis Steele: “Bishop are you ready, we have to get going so that we can make it to GCW in time for the press conference.” Bishop Steele: “Here I come babe, I just have to put on my tie and then we can leave.” (2 minutes later Bishop comes walking down the stairs in his pin stripe Armani suit. He has a pink tie to match it. He looks in the mirror on the way down the stairs. Alexis looks at him and then speaks) Alexis Steele: “Damn baby you look great and I love that tie.” Bishop Steele: “Thank you mama and you look extra sexy yourself.” Alexis Steele: “Are you ready for this?” Bishop Steele: “That’s why they call me True Perfection.” (Bishop and Alexis walk out the front door and get into the Phantom. It pulls off and heads towards downtown Miami, Fl for the press conference. On the way Bishop has a glass of Crystal and relaxes listening to music.) ...................................PRESENT TIME.......................................... (About 15 minutes pass and the reporters are getting anxious. One reporter gets a glimpse of Bishop’s Phantom pulling around to the back of the building. The reporter speaks to his crew) Reporter: “Guys I think I just saw Bishop Steele’s Phantom just pull around back. Now I don’t want all these people to see us leave because then we won’t get any comments. So we have to be quick about this. Let’s just pull out like we are leaving and go around the corner to where that Starbuck’s is and then pull in and just walk across to the parking lot.” Camera Man: “Okay let’s go.” (One of the reporter crews leaves the group of reporters and drives towards the Starbuck’s. They pull into the parking lot and park. They quickly get out and head across the street. Once they get across the street, Bishop and Alexis have just gotten out of the Phantom and headed inside. The crew meets them for some comments.) Jason Long: “Hi this is Jason reporting live from KTQTV. I’m here outside the Perfection Inc building where Bishop Steele has just shown up for a big press conference taking place here today at 3:30 p.m. Mr. Steele can we get some comments from you?” Bishop Steele: “Make it quick and no dumb questions either.” Jason Long: “Last week you made some harsh statements towards Quinton Lucas. Why is there so much bad blood between you to?” Bishop Steele: “Well it’s simple Jason; I don’t like him and will never like him. He is from England and that is that." Jason Long: “Well that is pretty cut and dry, but it really doesn’t say much.” Bishop Steele: “Look ever since I have come into the GCW and met Quinton, I have had a bad taste in my mouth. He is an arrogant alcoholic that runs his mouth too much. I mean look at me and look at him, there is no comparison what so ever. I’m True Perfection, he is Just Perfection. I’m dominating and he needs AA meetings. The point is I’m simply that damn good and I have proven it in that very ring. Too bad we can’t say the same about Quinton.” Jason Long: “Okay calm down big guy, I understand where you are coming from. So the question everyone wants know is. When will this feud between you and Quinton be over?” Bishop Steele: “This feud will be over when he steps into that ring and faces me, so I can show him and the world who is truly Perfection in that ring. Until that happens, he will just have to watch what True Perfection really looks like. Jason Long: “Well this week you have another 3-way match against ‘Primetime’ Joe Milone and Maggie McVay. What can we expect in this match this week?” Bishop Steele: “What you saw last week was greatness and this week it won’t be any less that spectacular. Last week was my first match here and I wanted to make an impression and I did. Now I want to continue that impression by proving that I belong in this federation and that there is no one who can surpass my greatness.” (The camera man zooms in on Bishop’s face as he speaks.) Bishop Steele: “Now for the real interview. When you look this good and have a swagger like mine you are unstoppable. The GCW knew this when they signed me and I proved it once and I will do it again. You don’t get no better than what you see right now. In time you Jason and all of you at home getting fat and eating donuts will see what happen when I become a Maineventer. You will witness a spectacle of pure perfection. You will feel the presence of an Icon. I am the new face of the GCW. I will revolutionize the wrestling industry. As I keep adding to the list of fallen opponents to my resume. I have been in the gym for months now perfecting what is already perfect and once again Primetime will witness this and soon Maggie will witness it for the first time. Now as for this interview I think I’m done, because the more and more I stand here and spend needless time talking to you the more and more time I waste money cause everyone knows my money is a major priority in my life. I mean look at me. That why they call me True Perfection.” (Bishop and Alexis start to head into the building. Once they walk into the building flash bulbs go off all-around the room. The camera fades with Bishop’s PR standing at the podium starting off the press conference……)
