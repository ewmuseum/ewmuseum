Imprisoned in Paradise: Used

14 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept. 14th When Steven Caldera said paid vacation, he sure as hell meant it. Seymour had expected to go home for a few days, but no. Caldera had put him on an airplane, first class at that, and sent him off to the Bahamas. It was a “reward for a hell of a first few weeks”, Caldera had said. Considering everything Seymour had done, he was hard-pressed to disagree. He’d beaten two of GCW’s best in Borealis and Octavian, and done an absolute truckload of PR work. Inwardly, he wondered if he’d ever be able to sign his name again. But somehow it was all so very, very worth it lying on a sandy beach with a pina colada in his hand. Especially as he read the piece of paper letting him know what the hell he was in store for this week. “Typhoid Mary,” he read, a slight feeling of dread building in the pit of his stomach. “Wonderful. Isn’t she LeStatt’s’something?” He’d watched WorldWide upon returning to his hotel room, and was still wholly unable to determine exactly what she was. Psycho seemed to be one perfectly legitimate answer. Unbalanced another. Exactly the type of opponent that Seymour disliked fighting. He couldn’t predict such a woman. He’d simply have to step in the ring with her and see what the Hell she’d come up with to throw at him. The Final Fantasy pulled out a dossier on her that Jonathan Calver had been kind enough to put together for him. He lingered for a few moments on the image, which was far, far more attractive than he’d expected, all things considered. “Christ,” he said, shaking his head. “LeStatt’s either packing a few kilos of GHB or he’s hung like an elephant.” LeStatt Knight was the GCW World Champion. Of course Seymour would keep the champion of the entire promotion in his sights. He was able to look at the big picture very easily. It was why he kept an eye on all the champions’Savige the TV, LeStatt the World, Borealis the US, and even Summers and Stephens with the tag team titles. Seymour loved titles. But it was LeStatt that was his focus, along with Typhoid Mary. The two were obviously connected, somehow. If Knight felt strongly enough about her to interfere, he’d have one hell of a fight on his hands. A sip of his colada confirmed that was what he wanted. Borealis had proven game, as had Octavian. Heck, the latter had power bombed him through the announce table. But he had survived both contests. A battle with the GCW World Champion would prove that he was for real, that he could dominate in GCW as he did in the UWF. And he certainly wasn’t without reason to want to drive LeStatt six feet under. Really, Knight had treated both Mary and Eva VanLorne like toys. Abuse of ANYONE was not something that Seymour tolerated. Abuse of women was inexcusable. He sighed. That was why competing against women was so difficult sometimes. He didn’t want to hurt them. Hell, he didn’t want to hurt anyone. Usually he only used enough force for victory, unless he despised his opponent. Of course, that put him at a handicap against opponents who DID enjoy using lethal force, but he was Seymour. The quintessential good guy. It was all perfectly depressing. Fortunately for Seymour, he had a drink in which to drown his sorrows. He took a large sip, letting his head fall back and to the side as he looked at the group of women playing Frisbee a few feet from him. It would have been so easy, he realized. So easy to take advantage of his fame. So easy to become a LeStatt. There were fangirls everywhere. Even here on vacation, he’d signed autographs for a half dozen. He was a good enough looking man that with a kind word and a smile, he could have had any of them back in his hotel room and half out of their clothing before he even said anything. Gods, how good that would have felt. As it stood, he’d been in a sort of celibacy for the past four months or so. Even SAYING it made his desperation grow. “Come on Seymour,” he muttered, to himself. “No giving in?” Cold showers. Baseball. Steven Caldera mounting Octavian. He sighed again, clearly on the verge of defeat. In fact, he was just about to get up and go find the especially cute fan that he’d last seen in the pool, when life through a nice cold bucket of water on him in the form of the arrival of one Jonathan Calver. “Mister Almasy,” he said, formally. Seymour’s head jerked up to see what the intruder wanted, the voice by now a familiar one. “Caldera sent you too”!” he asked, in utter astonishment. “Of course he did,” came the response. “I am meant to take care of all of your needs. That means here as well. Do you need another drink?” For chrissakes? This wasn’t a vice president, Seymour had come to realize. Jonathan Calver had basically been reduced to a manservant for his sake. The thought upset Seymour greatly. “No, I’ll be fine,” he said, showing his half-full glass. “I’m on vacation. Go enjoy yourself. The Bahamas is a wonderful place.” Jonathan shook his head. “No, Mr. Almasy, I’m here to get you anything you need. Dinner” Female accompaniment?” Seymour’s eyes went wide at that last bit. “Female accompaniment?” “Of course,” came the offhanded shrug. “Prostitutes if you like, although I’m sure I could find a fan of yours who’d be more than willing?” Damn him to Hell. It was as if he knew what Seymour was wrestling with at that very moment, as if he somehow could TELL that he was teetering on the brink of temptation. The fact that Jonathan was willing to be the go-between finally sealed Seymour’s fate. “Go ahead then,” he said, utterly defeated, and feeling like a first class jack off. “Find me one.” Calver leant close to the prone Final Fantasy with a grin that was damn near conspiratorial. “I’ll find you one, alright,” he said, grinning. “Be in your room in one hour.” Slowly, Seymour Almasy gathered his towel and his tropical drink, cursing himself six ways to hell. He couldn’t believe that he was going through with this’but now that he was, he wanted to make damn sure that he did it properly. ----------- About fifty minutes later, Seymour found himself in the shower, washing his hair with the fragrant shampoo that he rather enjoyed. Wrestler or not, the Final Fantasy could at times be rather vain about his appearance. And if he WAS going to go through with this absolutely flipping ridiculous idea, he wanted to be at his best. Wordlessly, he turned the water off, reaching for a towel. He toweled himself dry somewhat aggressively, choosing to leave his hair somewhat wet. The towel was immediately wrapped around his waist as he returned to the bed, where his attire was laid out. He pondered whether or not to wear undergarments, before deciding on a pair of his favorite chocobo boxers. Over these he put leather pants that were nearly criminally tight, and a silk, button up shirt. Black socks and black shoes completed the outfit, and he stood up to look at himself in the mirror. Perfect. Crossing over to the nightstand, he sprayed a bit of cologne about his neck, making sure that the bed was made, and oh this was all so ODD. Never mind the fact that HE was the one who had asked Jonathan to procure him a willing sexual partner, he was the one who felt like a prostitute. Dolling himself up, making sure everything was perfect. He was interrupted by a sharp knock on the door. Seeing as how it was unlocked, and it was most likely Jonathan with her paramour for the evening, he announced in his sexiest voice, “Come in.” The door opened. From his vantage point, slightly hidden by one of the walls of the room, he couldn’t see who was coming in. But he COULD hear Jonathan telling someone to “have fun” before he shut the door. Seymour swallowed hard. This was the moment of truth. He could hear the pitter patter of feet coming closer and closer. Taking a deep breath, he waited. What he saw damn near made his heart stop. A lovely blonde girl stood before him, hair done up so that locks of it softly framed her face. Glasses perched unobtrusively on her nose, only adding to the beauty of her face. She also happened to wear a uniform that Seymour could have identified in his sleep. “Th-that’s a SeeD uniform,” he managed, weakly. The woman, who was probably about 18 years old, simply nodded happily. Seymour had never quite felt so weak as he did at this particular moment. A cosplayer. Jonathan hadn’t been fucking kidding when he said he’d find Seymour a good one. Apparently Mr. Calver had found out about Seymour’s love of cosplaying, or dressing up as anime and video game characters. And damned if he hadn’t found someone who was cosplaying his second favorite Final Fantasy female? “What’s your name,” he finally managed, completely stunned. “Instructor Quistis Trepe,” came the practiced reply. Gods, apparently she was serious about going through with this. She waited a few moments, before moving over to whisper in his ear. “I’m a virgin, you know.” Seymour’s breath caught in his throat, at a combination of her pronouncement and the fact that he could quite easily see what appeared to be a pink thong beneath her rather short skirt. Almost before he knew what was happening she pounced in his lap, straddling him. She cooed in his ear, and suddenly, his wonderful idea of a romp with a willing girl had been entirely turned around on him. “I have your posted in my room,” she whispered, arms wrapping around him to hold him tight as she sighed. “I’ve dreamed of this for so very long?” She nestled her head against his neck in the time it took him to draw a breath. Seymour gulped slightly, her warmth slowly melting all of his concerns. “Dreamed of what,” he managed to spit out, finally. “Of you kissing me,” she responded immediately. “Touching me. Making love to me. Oh,” she suddenly added, grinning wickedly, “and after you’re finished with all of that’holding me down and fucking me until I scream.” He almost fainted. Self control? What self control? Besides, how could he POSSIBLY resist a challenge like that? -------------------- Later, much later, when Seymour reflected on those four hours of his life, he came to a somewhat surprising conclusion. As horrible as he had felt about taking advantage of the woman, he decided that the taking advantage of was mutual. The sex had been wonderful, beyond anything his passion-starved body could have imagined. But considering how much she had claimed to love him, she had left while he was still asleep cuddling her, her undergarments draped over him as a souvenir of sorts. No goodnight kiss. Hell, no goodbye. He couldn’t be too mad, though. After all, it was just another example of how he was being used.
