out of the frying pan into the dryer

25 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25-11-05 There were camera’s lights and action and Mary did her best to shield Marianne from the horror movie that their lives had become. Well I say horror movie when horror circus is a little more accurate. News crews and screaming, neighbours and lurkers. Gaukers and detectives and cold hard steel. Yea cold hard steel. Mary smiled, everything was falling into place. She promised Marianne they would leave that hell house, they would leave and get away and never ever have to go back and she was true to her word. With her Aunty Shona screaming sporadically, a cop car took Marianne in the safe hands of Mary, her saviour. away. There was talking, lot’s of talking, lots of grown ups talking above and over her head but she didn’t mind. They threw words around like murder, life sentence, minor and psychological analysis. Mary hadn’t heard those words before, not in connection with her or Marianne. Murderer? She thought. Nah. A middle aged man in an old worn suit came to talk to her and she smiled sweetly at him. “How are you Marianne?” “NO!” “No?” She shook her head and her curls bounced. “Not Marianne. Mary.” The man raised an eyebrow. “You prefer Mary?” She shook her head again and giggled. “No silly. IS Mary now. Marianne’s tired.” He looked concerned and intrigued grabbing for a pen and note pad. “And who might you be” Are you her friend?” Mary shook her head while digging dried blood out from under her short nails. “Friend” Don’t be silly, Mary ain’t no nutty bar. Mary IS of Marianne. Marianne is of Mary.” “I don’t understand. You both coexist at the same time?” “Well duh don’t be silly. There’s no co anything, Mary and Marianne, same heart, same whole, same.” “Then why the different names?” “So ya can tell us apart Dr man.” Mary leaned back in her chair and pulled her legs up to cross them. “Mary knows who ya are, knows why ya here.” He leaned forward. “And why am I here Mari’Mary?” Mary leaned forward and adjusted his loose tie gently. “You here to put us in a jar and poke us and prod us and open us up to see why we tick.” She frowned then pouted looking sullen. “But you don’t understand that we don’t care. Anything is better than what we was going through all them years, anything is better than that and now Uncle will never do that to anyone else ever again.” The man leaned back, eyebrows raised again. “Are you saying you know what you did” You murdered your Uncle knowingly?” He crossed his legs and leaned back against the wall and Marianne mimicked him with a dangerous smile on her face. “Yes Mary knows what she did. She killed him, she saved us and she hopes he burns deeper than hell!” Things happened so fast Mary could barely keep up. More due to the fact she didn’t care much. People asked her questions finally. People were listening to her, finally she had someone to tell her horrors to. She had people at least pretending to care and not the way her Uncle pretended to care. The trial lasted so long she thought it would never end. Her Aunty Shona refused to testify at first. She had to be subpoenaed. Ain’t that some shit. She still loved her husband, dead and sick as he was and not in that order. She blamed Mary for enticing him, blamed Mary for being his whore. At least she tried to. The defence ripped her apart. Then there was more shouting, more screaming, more camera’s flashing. “On the count of Murder first. We find the defendant, not guilty. On the count of conspiracy to commit murder. We find the defendant, not guilty. On the count of Manslaughter with intent. We find the defendant, Guilty! The defendant will serve a sentence of eight years with the possibility for parole in four. Due to the defendants age, she is a minor and the prolonged severe physical and sexual and emotional abuse she has had to endure from such an early age, the time will be served at a maximum security psychiatric facility for young offenders, to be transferred when she is of age to a similar unit until her time of release.” Mary sat there and smiled and drummed her nails on the desk repeatedly. Her handcuffs chaffing her skin was a comfort. Pain that didn’t follow or lead to even more painful forced sex was a blessing in itself. The cold hard steel felt nice. She hoped they let her keep them. 3 hours later and she arrived at what was soon to be known to her and all the patients as The Institute. Her wrists still cuffed and her ankles shackled causing her to shuffle up the steps and through doorways and down the halls. Signed in and finger printed for the umpteenth time she finally had her ankle irons taken off. They took her cuffs off too and she rubbed her wrists glancing at the stark walls. “Marianne?” A middle aged woman called her. Mary shot her a warning glare. “I’m sorry, Mary is it?” Mary smiled sweetly but it soon faltered when she looked through the glass door at the zombie kid walking down the corridor. What the fuck was that about? Suddenly the life outside the hell hole of her Uncle’s house didn’t seem so rosy. The kid was drugged up to the eyeballs, shuffling through the corridors in a dressing gown two sizes too big. “Come meet the rest of the gang.” The woman took Mary’s silence for approval and she made an effort not to invade Mary’s personal space by touching her. That, Mary was grateful for. Being spoken to like she was 2, she was not. She was shown to a games room, a big games room, that was painted a warm institution grey. Nice. She was shown to a bunch of kids varying between the ages of 12 and 18 and none of them seemed to give a shit about her or’hell anything. She stayed silent. This wasn’t how it was supposed to be. It was supposed to be fun on the outside, it was supposed to be no more pain, it was supposed to be new and sparkly and people who cared. Instead it was dull and sterile and full of people who were paid to pretend to care and people who were drugged up so they didn’t care anymore. Maybe is she didn’t speak they wouldn’t notice her. She was right. Eight weeks later, Mary had no more friends than she started with. She knew their names and what they did and why they were there but she never spoke a word even when they spoke to her. Cute boys were everywhere, covered in scars and bruises. Almost everyone was covered in scars and or bruises. There was more pain in some of those rooms than there was on the streets outside. Mary’s head was jerked up from gazing down at her own scarred wrists when she heard screaming and yelling coming from down the hall. “Fresh Meat,” came Jimmy the fire starters voice from behind her. Yea a lot of them had titles, made it easier that way. You knew who to go to for what and who not to mess with for anything. Mary’s name should have been Mary the murderer in truth but for some reason there was a common bond among these kids. When they heard about what she had done, they didn’t label her a murderer. A lot of them had been through abuse too and not had the nerve to do what she did. She took charge. Not they didn’t label her Murderer. They just let her be Mary. And secretly everyone one of them admired her. The screaming got louder. They were wheeling in a new patient. Mary crept up to the glass that separated the outer shell from the inner rooms and peered through. It looked like a girl strapped to a bed with restraints at her wrists and ankles. She was bucking and spitting and swearing in English and a language Mary didn’t understand. The hall filled with staff and as they pumped a clear liquid into the girls arm, she slowly calmed down and became still. It was lights out but there were ways and means of getting out of your room if you really needed too. The doors were on auto shut in. you just had to block them for the most part. Mary did so and crept out in her bare feet avoiding the camera’s and padded down the hall to the quiet unit. There were rooms there and the doors weren’t locked. Well hell if you’re strapped to a bed, you ain’t going anywhere anytime soon. Mary had to get on tip toe to peek in and make sure she had the right room. She pushed the door open slowly and padded inside. The girl was sleeping, or that’s what it seemed like. Sleeping or dead if not for the rise and fall of her developing chest. She looked about Mary’s age. Mary stared at her face. She had caramel skin, a couple shades lighter than Mary’s like golden honey. Her hair was almost black brown. Her lashes were long and her mouth was full, wide and her lips looked so soft. Her eyes were puffy, probably from crying. Mary got so close, her breath made the hair about her brow waft. She jumped back when the two greeny brown eyes turned to her slowly. The girl stared at her a while not attempting to move any other part of her. Then she spoke. Her voice was low almost a whisper. Her eyes were questioning but hurt at the same time. “fere,” Mary’s brow pinched and she shook her head not understanding. “fere,” she said again weakly and struggled gently at her restraints. Mary’s eyes focusd on them in the dim light and realised where there should have been padding to stop the straps from chaffing, there were none. The girl had struggled so badly it had rubbed her skin raw. “fere..” She said again, then whispered. “It..hurts..” Mary didn’t move for about five minutes and the silenced thickened. The girl turned from her, eyelids fluttering closed. Mary darted forward quickly tugging the straps loose but not taking them off. She then darted out of the room and came back with some ointment to soothe the pain and sores. The girl turned to her again, ?” voc? um anjo?” Mary shook her head not understanding and still not having said a word. She finished cleaning the wounds. “Are you an angel?” The girl whispered, her accent not as thick as Mary would have thought. This girl obviously spoke English fluently. Mary shook her head. “Shhh’Mary can’t let you loose, they would come in and drug you ups again. Play nice, be good then they let you out. Then?” Mary trailed off. The girl smiled weakly again. “Um anjo. An angel.” Mary shook her head. “Nope, just Mary.” “Mary?” Mary nodded. “Skye.” She said. Mary looked outside, it was getting light, the sky outside was clearing. “Skye blue.” She smiled. Mary’s gotta go. Be good, they let you up if ya good then?” “then I’ll come find you?” She looked hesitant but Mary just smiled back and nodded. She went and eased the door open to leave when Skye whispered after her. “Anjo?” Mary looked back eyebrows raised in question. “Obrigado.” Mary smiled and nodded again. “Excitin times dingaling.!” With that she left, beaming, She had a new friend for the first time in a long time. Had a new title too and she liked it. Angel. Yea she could get used to that.
