Family

5 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 Dec 06 “You can’t just go without any explanation!” “Fuck you. You don’t need anything from me. Tell Steven WorldWide is just going to be one World Champion short this week.” “But we go?” “No! listen to me. This conversation is over. I won’t be attending tonight. If you don’t like it, fire me.” I shut my cell phone and placed it in my pocket. My nerves were at an all time high, tends to happen at moments like these. See I got a call a few hours prior telling me that I needed to come back to Florida. Mary was going into labor. Now I could have told the suits at GCW what exactly I was doing but ? well ? I didn’t want to. It’s not like they were going to fire me and what else was left? Fine me? Haha yeah because that’ll hurt my pockets so much. Besides, with the internet and cell phones is there any doubt that someone will leak this information within minutes of our baby being born? Exactly. “TAXI!” The car stopped, allowing me to get in. Immediately I remembered exactly why I never use public transportation. The whole car smelled like a mix of cheap cologne and stale French fries. Not to mention the fact that only god knows what the fuck the stains were on the seats. “St. Vincent’s ? and hurry the hell up.” Immediately the driver attempted some of that bullshit small talk that was customary of these people. Thank god he wasn’t a wrestling fan. I humored the driver with a few quick responses but I really was not in any mood to talk to him. Instead I just became lost in my thoughts. Thinking about Mary mostly, our future together, this baby. It all brings a smile to my face, each and every time. These thoughts always do one other thing to me as well. Make me afraid. I had a family once, a wife and a daughter. As the story goes however I lost them. The details ? well the hell with details. Anyone who has known me for longer than a month knows my story. I’ve grown tired of telling it. But I am always fearful that somehow or someway I could lose it all again. Life has never been generous with that little thing called happiness with me. It’s almost as if it prefers to tease me with it. Happiness has hung around for quite sometime this time around though and it’s almost as if I’m simply waiting for it to crumble. Will it crumble? I don’t really know? “We’re here.” His voice interrupted my thought process ? startled me actually. I dug into my pockets and pulled out a couple hundred dollar bills. “Keep the change.” Maybe he’d use it to get the stains out. I hustled towards the main doors of the hospital and towards the front desk. I was greeted by that fake smile they all have to give. “Mary Abitua ? having a baby.” “Hmm ? well”.” “Small girl, dressed strange, talks in third person.” “OH!” She chuckles “Second floor and to the right. There will be another desk up there that can give you the exact place.” It didn’t take long to find Mary. “HOLY BITCHFUCK AND EARL!” Just as I was ready to fly through the door where I heard these screams coming from, it swung open and out came Mary and a handful of doctors pushing her along. For a brief moment it seemed that she forgot the pain ? she saw me and I saw her. In that moment time seemed to stop and my breath seemed to escape my body. Then I felt pain run up my arm. I looked down. Mary had a hold of my forearm ? nails dug into my skin. I looked into her eyes, and they weren’t loving anymore. “MARY GONNA KILL YOU WHEN SHE OUTTA HERE YOU BIIIIIIIII-AHHHHHH!” I stood frozen for a moment until a nurse snapped me out of it. “Don’t worry they all say that.” She forced a smile. “You must be this “Statty-Bear” she keeps talking about?” “Yes,” I give out a short laugh. “Roberto.” “Nice to meet you. Here put these on and follow us ? this baby is ready to come out. I assume you want to be there when the baby is delivered?” “Yes, very much so.” So I prepared myself. I wasn’t there for my daughter’s birth, I didn’t witness that miracle. I regret that decision everyday. I have thought about this time in my life since Starr passed away. Thought about how beautiful a moment it’d be. Holding onto the woman I love’s hand, watching our child be born, knowing that I could do right by my family this time. In one moment the story of my new life flashed before my eyes. I could see the wedding day, our honeymoon, making love on the beach. I could imagine sending our child off on the always important first day of school. I see trips to Disney world or Cedar Point, Driving to school dances, High School Graduation, College ? it all just flashes by. Beautiful. Then what happens? I walk into the delivery room. Let’s just say it is not EXACTLY how I imagined it. “DEATH! DEVIL! SATAN!” Mary’s words ? that’s all that needs to be said. “Mary.” I grab her hand. “I love you.” “HUSH IT YOU BASTARD! SEE WHAT YOU’VE”.AH! Mary sorry Statty-Bear! She loves you too!” “Alright this baby is ready to come out.” The Doctor pre-warns us. I look down at Mary and smile. This was it our lives were about to change forever. We both would finally have our happily ever after. One that we both deserved after the lives we’ve been handed. Mary’s was perhaps even worse than mine. That sick and twisted freak of an Uncle tortured her. It was finally going to change. That is if she didn’t kill me first. “DIE!DIE!DIE!” “But Mar-? “MARY SAID BE QUIET!” “Well?” “NO MARY LOVES YOU!” “Alright Mary we’re going to need you to start pushing, ready” PUSH!” And she did. Now this is where things went weird. I mean I’m 27 years old and I know where babies come from. Yet for some reason I didn’t expect what came next. “Look Mr. Abitua!” What I saw can only be described as ? well I don’t think it can be described. Blood, flesh, and a whole lot of stretching in places that should never stretch that far. I lost my breath and my knees became weak. I heard screaming. It was my own. Then everything went black? I really couldn’t tell you how long I was out. Slowly my eyes began to open. It felt like a dream at first, maybe it was? Well that was my first thought. Then my surroundings began to become clearer and clearer. I was in a hospital room. I looked to my right. “Wakey-wakey sleepy head!” “Ma-Mary.” “Hi.” “What happened?” “Mary think you fainted.” She chuckles. “Don’t know why, Mary was in all the pain!” Then it hit me. “The Baby! Where’s the baby?” A voice from to our left. “Right here.” A nurse strolls into the door, almost as if on cue. She came to me and placed the baby into my arms. “Say hello to your beautiful baby boy.” I couldn’t speak, hell I could barely breath. I couldn’t take my eyes off of him. This child was the single most beautiful person I had ever seen in my life. I felt the soft touch of Mary’s hand on my cheek. My eyes move up to meet hers. “Mary loves you Statty-Bear.” “Te Quiero mucho mami,” I have to take in a deep breath. “And thank you.” “For what?” “Making my life mean something.” Then we both began to cry. Tears of joy, happiness ? maybe even relief. All I do know is that things have finally changed. Will it crumble? No Because I won’t let it.
