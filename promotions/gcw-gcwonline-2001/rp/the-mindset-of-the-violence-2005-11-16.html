The Mindset of The Violence

16 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16/2005 [The screen is pitch black and the familiar, low gruff voice of “The Violence? Vince Jones can be heard as some soft music begins to play in the background.] “For years, people have tried to figga out The Violence?” [A clip of Vince Jones at different stages of his career begins to play. First Vince is seen walking down the aisle to the ring in his Big Money Wrestling days with a cocky smirk on his face.] ? They’ve tried to figga out what makes V. Jones tick... ? [Next, Vince Jones is seen standing in the middle of a soldout Bloodbath Wrestlin Alliance ring ranting and raving with a mic in his hand and the lovely Jasmine standing behind him looking as gorgeous as ever.] “They’ve tried to figga out what goes through the head of V. Jones?” [A clip of Vince Jones sitting in the locker room with his head down after a loss is shown. A PRIME reporter approaches him and he immediately flares up in rage and runs the reporter out the locker room.] ? They’ve tried to understand The Violence. The more they try the more they off key?” [A reporter is seen standing in front of Vince Jones? apartment door. He opens it and immediately slams the door in the reporter’s face.] “People see The Violence, the thug, the man that knows no bounds, the man that’s willin? to to do whateva to get what’s his?” [A clip of Vince Jones in his 360WE days along with fellow Dynasty of Destruction members can be seen beating the hell out of a former member within an inch of his life in the middle of the ring.] “People see V. Jones, hear him speak, watch him operate and think they know him inside and out. Hell nah! V ain’t like no damn picture book or some sh*t! You don’t just flip him open, look, and everything you wanna know “bout him is right there or nothin”. Ya know? There’s more to V. Jones than that?” [Vince Jones can now be seen walking down the halls of the backstage area of Core Wrestling show alone and in silence.] “There’s a hell of a lot more to V than that. Cats see V and he be reachin” out for the money, the power, and the respect in this damn game and cats think that’s all the man’s about, gettin? his. ? [A clip of Vince Jones taping a deodorant commercial is shown. Next, Vince Jones can be seen signing autographs with a smug expression on his face. Then, a clip of Vince Jones chilling in a Jacuzzi with a variety of beautiful women can be seen.] ? Wrong! Let the people think what they wanna think “bout V. When its all said and done and V’s career is finally ova maybe, just maybe these cats’ll realize what it is that makes V. Jones who he is inside and outside the ring?” [A clip of Vince Jones can be seen as he turns his back on his agent, Reno Banks after a heated argument in cW.] ? Maybe one day they’ll realize what it is that keeps V’s fire burnin? in this game?” [A clip of Vince Jones can be seen as he is in the gym working out and completing successive bench press reps. The concentration, intensity, and determination in his eyes can be seen almost as clearly as the sweat that trickles down his face.] ? Who knows? For the time bein? the money V gets, the power V receives, and the respect V claims is like that side dish to the main course and all. You enjoy that sh*t while you can, but soona or lata you gotta move on to the real deal, the real sh*t. ? [Vince Jones can be seen sitting in a limo ride with an expression on his face that appears as though he is disinterested with all that he currently has.] “Dark Haze back in the L-O Dubb days was V’s bruh and all, but when it came down to it he had to be beaten in the middle of that damn ring.” [A clip from a Realm show from his LOW days is shown. Vince Jones can be seen dropping Dark Haze with The Silencer is shown. Vince immediately covers him for the pin and the three count.] ? Why? Cuz he was standin? in V’s way. [The face of Rei Tiu flashes up on the screen.] ? Once again we got anotha typical b*tch made that probably sees V for only one thing, a street thug, the man that’s only in it to get his money, power, and respect. Well, when its all said and done this week at Realm, V hopes to make one thing perfectly clear to Rei Tiu. There’s more to Vince Jones than all that sh*t. Deep down inside that sh*t don’t drive V to walk that aisle, step in that ring, and kick Rei Tiu's ass.from corna to corna to corna to corna. Hell nah! V can enter that damn ring, walk out on a loss and still get a damn check. So, will this Rei Tiu cat eva be able to explain to the world what makes V. Jones tick? No! Has anyone that has eva stepped in the ring eva come out explainin? what makes V tick? No! Why? Cuz the mindset of The Violence can’t be explained through simple words and expressions out the mouth.” [A clip of Vince Jones in wHw is shown as he is standing in the hallway glaring into a camera mouthing the words?] “Prepare to enter my hell, my realm, and my game” don’t tell nothin?. [A clip of Vince Jones standing across the ring from Black Widow of cW is shown as as he mouths the words?] “Prepare to Enter “The Violence”, don’t explain jack sh*t!” Even if the words? [A clip of Vince Jones in wWw mouthing the following words to long-time enemy, Ravage, right before the beginning of their match against one another.] “you “bout to get yo punk ass silenced” come out The Violence’s mouth, that still don’t mean sh*t. Nah. The mindset of The Violence can’t be explained through words. Its explained through all the punk asses that have stepped up to the plate and have actually been silenced in and out that damn ring! [Clip after clip after short clip are shown of countless opponents of Vince Jones from federations from his past are being dropped on the back of their heads by his inverted brainbuster DDT, The Silencer.] ? Its explained through all the b*tches that have walked that aisle with they full health and left the ring, half crippled on a stretcha?” [A clip is shown of Vince Jones standing in the ring of WWCW during his reign of dominance as a self-proclaimed career killer watching an opponent of his being carried out on a stretcher. A cold, hard stare is etched across his face as the EMTs carry the man out the ring.] ? Its explained through all the cats that have overlooked the real threat V. Jones posed thinkin? he was nothin? but a street thug lookin? to get paid only to end up gettin? stunned by V! [A clip of a cocky Jack “The Hellraiser” Wright is laughing at Vince’s presence before him right before their epic battle in Vince’s NYUFC days. Another clip is shown at the end of their battle as Vince Jones can be seen tossing Jack Wright’s body from off the top of a catwalk that hangs over the dungeon-like arena in slow motion.]. “Its explained through all the b*tch mades that have had to claw their way to survival in a match with The One Man Dynasty in this game!” [ A clip of Vince Jones in his one-time battle Chryste of DWO is shown. She is on the mat writihing in pain as Vince Jones has her locked in an anklelock submission hold and refuses to leg go. She can be seen clawing with all her might to reach the ring ropes.] “Its explained through all the cats that have eva had to look directly into the eyes and soul of the madman and realize that they no longa safe?” [A clip of Vince Jones and his former friend and DoD stablemate , Jay Cash from 360WE is shown as the two stand in the ring and glare into one another’s eyes with pure hatred for one another.] And its explained through the blood of his victims that have been left lyin? on they backs starin? up at the ceilin? lights and bleedin? from ear to ear?” [A clip of Vince Jones staring down at his own brother, Keshawn Jones, who turned his back on him at one point in time is shown. He is laid out on the floor bleeding after Vince Jones attacked him in his home federation, WMW. Vince looks down at him in disgust and spits on him in rage.] Add all that sh*t up and maybe you onto somethin?. Until then, the closest thing that the people’ll eva be able to use to explain the mindset of The Violence is what happens when the day comes that V. Jones is standin? toe-to-toe with the last man or woman that sits in the way of The Violence and the championship title strap. When the belt sittin? around V’s waist the only thing that the people will say is??Nuff said!” So Rei Tiu here's a quick question for ya. In fact, Ricky Thunder if you out there listenin' to this sh*t this question's for you too cuz yo punk ass could be catchin' it soon enough too. Is The Violence just a street thug? No! Is The Violence the next G-C Dubb champion to be? No! What is V? A man on a mission that’s what The Violence is come WorldWide and beyond, a man on a mission. Rei Tiu, Ricky Thunder neither one of you cats wanna be in V's way! Vachon, The Violence does this sh*t for you! Rest in peace! [The camera slowly fades to black.]
