Date: Timeless It’s late, so late the crickets have ended their sonnets of procreation and have abandoned their posts for a nights rest. The sky was clear, and it was pleasantly warm that evening. Bryan is awakened to loud thuds, drunken giggles, and moans of passion. He’s young, but grown in the head, he knows mother and father are in the other room doing the deed. He’s tired of hearing this though. His childlike mind is being exposed to this day in and day out. A third voice is heard amongst the pleasure of his mother. It was a woman’s voice. Who could this be? Bryan young and curious arises from his pillow to investigate. This was something he’s never heard before. He’s use to his mother and fathers caws of passion, but this third voice confused him. On his tip toes Bryan makes his way towards his parents room. The door is cracked, allowing him the crevice to peek through. He’s not surprised to see his father’s bare ass parked between the legs of a woman. This woman however is usually his mother. He doesn’t ever remember his mother having brown legs though. His father pumps in and out of this dark specimen. She moans with each pump, her legs squeezing his ass. Bryan understands only a little of what is actually going on, he will learn one day soon enough from an experience in the woods behind his house. Bryan’s about to walk away when he hears a choking sound. He peers back into the crack and notices his father perched up, arms down and straight. The brown legs and arms are flailing wildly, she’s gasping and choking. Bryan’s eyes light up, as he watches his father perform the final act of pressure on the brown woman. Her legs slow down, her arms stop, followed by complete paralysis of everything. Bryan just watched his father choke this brown woman to death. What confuses Bryan the most is, after the brown body grows limp, his father starts his pumping motion again. He see’s the light from his parents attached bathroom go out and his mom walks in. She drops her drink and screams. Bryan’s father continues the pumping motion, grunts, and his body shakes a little. He gts off the brown lady, and smacks his wife in the face dropping her to the ground. Bryan gasps, but quickly covers his mouth so no one hears. He runs back to his room, and lays down. He does not sleep, he keeps feeling his throat collapse, and see’s his father on top of him, crushing his windpipe. After a few hours he dozes, and will wak to a beautiful sunshine littered day. The day that turned Bryan Mayhem into raving psychopath. Bryan and Jason are on the teeter totter at the playground. It’s only 2 blocks away so Bryan and Jason are allowed to come here quite often when chores and other menial tasks are done. The vision of the horror that happened nearly 12 hours ago haunts Bryan, and Jason knows there is something wrong, he just refuses to investigate. Jason has learned if you seek into Bryan’s business, his fist will sneak into your face. Jason notices Bryan staring over at a group of black kids. This playground just 2 years ago was where they had their first encounter with black people. Jason likes them, but Bryan sill remains distant. The kids Bryan is staring at ranges in age from about 4-12. Jason thinks they are all related, because most of them look the same. What happens in the next few hours shocks the town, and makes the front page of The Belmont Herald. It read as follows. Four year old found dead in King George Park. Four year old Darla Cunningham was found strangled to death in the woods just outside King George Park. Her body was found in a basin below a tree in the woods. There are large hand prints around her neck. There is no other sign of brutality or sexual deviance to the corpse. Police have no suspects but believe it to be a suspicious man that is known to roam the parks grounds. A little over a year later an innocent man was found guilty of the murder and was executed some time afterwards. Bryan’s lust for strangulation may stem from the night he saw his father and the brown lady?.or so we think.
