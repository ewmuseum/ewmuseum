Two Lone Wolves on a Shared Path

13 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec. 13th, 2005 'And thank you for flying internationally with United Airlines. We hope you enjoyed your flight from Narita International Airport in Tokyo back into the United States of America. Those who have not currently completed your US customs forms will be given them at this time...' I awoke from what was pretty decent slumber to hear that bitch talking yet again. 13 hours on a cramped international flight wouldn't be so bad going back and forth if you didn't have to hear those damn people talking every few minutes about any minute detail that arises on the duration of the flight. Then again, it's either listen to them chatter on and on or watch Batman Begins another 3 times as one of the three in-flight movies. Flying the friendly skies my ass. With a little under an hour left they decided it'd be a great idea to wake as many people up as possible just to tell them you were so close yet so far away from being near the dull monotony of a trans-continental flight. Home seemed so far away. The instant that the rude hawk-nosed woman came by with the Customs forms for the 4th time that I was awake to notice, I could already tell I was scowling in disgust for the situation. Imagine waking up to the sound of a screeching bird and then having a form shoved in your face that you had already filled out 12 hours ago. She retracted the form away from me, obviously not liking my distaste for their service or her cucumber-douche perfume. Funny thing is that the flight over to Japan was hardly like this...it was much more amicable and far less miserable. The flight back over was a very rude awakening that I was reentering the United States. I was returning back from Japan for what will be the last time in a very long while. After Worldwide went off the air I immediately went to the airport and caught to next flight over to Tokyo to get everything squared away as far as my complete reemergence into the American wrestling scene...well, just GCW in general was concerned. They still aren't trilled about that belt of theirs I still have resting in my carry-on bag a few feet over my head...but they know there's not much that can be done about it other than to send someone over to lift it off of me. And they will...his name is Tsuru Sawamura. I've already let Caldera know in a phone call I had whilst over there that I was coming back to wrestle for the remainder of my contract, and that I was bringing the belt with me. I've not heard that man so excited in such a long time to hear that I had single-handedly arranged the first overseas title defense for New Japan Pro...and that GCW was going to be the only place that it was going to be held. Then again, Caldera is if nothing else a very wise businessman. It's one hell of a draw to hold an event like this over in the US, and he was going to be the only man to have it...that's money out every orifice of his body. How could he say no? But this isn't about money for me. I spent a good while trying to fit in there in Japan, and even longer trying to get my bearings back together and fight the way I used to before Lestatt went and fucked up my neck. Lucky for me I was able to piece everything together as I went...but most of it came back when I had a very memorable match with Sawamura, who was at that time the Middleweight Champion. Our first match was fought to a 45 minute draw, and left me the most alive I've felt in years. I felt great, and I knew that I still had everything I used to. And once I got that confidence back, all the pieces fell back into place. I picked up victories left and right on our two to three show a week schedules. I fought and fought but never felt any fatigue, and at the top of the mountain still stood Sawamura. I had my chance to take him down...and I did. We fought each other on a televised event. Sixty full minutes we fought to another straight draw. I thought I would have to wait for another chance but salvation came in the blood-hungry fans. They wanted 10 more minutes. They got it. With renewed vigor I tried to tear him apart like a rabid animal. He knew what I was out to do so he was evasive as all hell. I managed to catch up to him and lock him in the No Moment. It was the third time at that point I had done so, but his back and neck had enough. He did the most painful thing any man could ever do and willingly say 'I Quit', giving another man what you rightfully earned with your own words. I should know...I'd just done the same before surgery. So now I sit after finishing the paperwork of ending my overseas contract a bit early in a airplane on my next big assignment...the meeting with Caldera. This one isn't to celebrate my return or a good fortune. This meeting is about my future. If this doesn't go right, the New Japan Pro Middleweight title may be the only one I hold for a long, long time. I have to prove to Steven that I will still be useful in Global Championship Wrestling for years to come despite my absence in some of its' past, my abrupt silence while being champion during its' last return, and whether I can safely handle myself since surgery. Hopefully my experiences in Japan can prove myself to him, but I think there is going to be a bit more involved. Upon my conversation with Caldera while in Japan he let me on to the fact that I had been booked at Worldwide this coming week...in the main event, no less. This isn't out of the blue or a coincidence...there's so much that the guys already in GCW could gain from a main event on Worldwide...but I know Seymour Almasy was placed in front of me as a true litmus test of how I still fare...of how I still perform in the GCW environment. I may not have been wrestling here for a few months, but this place is just at home to me as ever. We are both lone wolves each on our own quests with something to prove, and it's a shame that ultimately we both fight for a noble cause. I fight for my livelihood...for my job, my home, and my sanity. Almasy fights for himself as he also wants what I do...that contract in GCW to stay firmly in my hands. He knows he has to go through Rich Rollins... I know that I have to go through him. I thought again about the situations like this that I've dealt with in the past. Back in the Establishment days I had to take on Octavian before out relationship went to hell. Recently back at ACWF Six I had to fight against Mike Hardy...and man whom was one of the most respectable and noble of any I've ever fought. As time drew closer to our match with four other men I realized that you can't have friends in a war zone. I've had to fight just about every single friend I've ever had one way or another as it has come to GCW and my many years of being here. But Almasy is far from what I would consider a friend. That term has left me feeling very hollow since I almost got set the fuck ablaze by Lestatt. 'Fuck...' I realized what I had just said aloud and so did most everyone else around me. Their stares were accusing but I was hardly in a condition at the moment to really care on way or another. In my quest to get reinstated as one of the surviving few of the old-school regime during GCW's new age, I had completely forgotten about settling the score. I tend to repress those thoughts which are hard to live by, and my situation just opened the flood gate of my mind once again. I had just realized that my goals here are not as singular as Almasy's...we aren't as alike as I thought. His is simply to keep a job by going through Rolling. I, on the other hand, have to save my livelihood. I cannot miss a single step of what I have to do. This week, it's time to prove that I still have the magic that turned me from a shameful whelp years ago into the federation-defining man I am now. This short path goes straight through Seymour Almasy. After that I have to one again take care of Tsuru Sawamura in my homeland, a task that solidifies me as well overseas as I am here. Then it's time to finish what was started years ago that never got anywhere near completed with Jason O'Neil...I've dreamt about that for what feels to be so long now. And then, only then...will Lestatt Knight pay for everything he put me through. For everything he did and was going to do, his conquering will be slow, painful, and very bloody when I finally get my time alone with the man that nearly cost me everything I'd worked my whole life to get. Khan disrespected me three years ago...he shot me down when I was at my lowest point. When I unloaded everything I had pent up on him he disappeared for two years. When he showed his face again he retreated when heard I was coming back. I hear my teeth grit. My knuckles ache due to clenching my fists so hard in my seat...I feel everything coming back that I went through...those old emotions...that old me. He dared to take me to a level not sought by very many. Before that, he feigned friendship. I want to scream out, but I stifle it. I can hold on. I've got someone on the way who I can sort some of these held back emotions on. I don't call him my friend, and he is far from my enemy. Whatever that makes him... ...God help him.
