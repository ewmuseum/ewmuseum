Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/11 (Fade from black.) (Our scene opens with the shot of a groundhog's head coming out of a hole. Taking a couple of looks around, he begins to exit the hole with curiosity, wondering whether or not his shadow will accompany him on this chilly February 2nd.) (However, Mr. Groundy is not aware that he is merely a prop for something incredibly bad ass to happen.) (Disturbed's "Down With The Sickness" plays, and anyone who linked to Youtube should automatically be disqualified because that shit wasn't around last decade.) OOOOOOOOOH WAAAAAAAAAAAAAH-AH-AH-AH! (The groundhog's hole is in the middle of the 45-yard line at John F. Kennedy Jr. High School. The groundhog is now up at full attention, only to have a cleated foot BOOT the groundhog up…) ONGH ONGH! (The gray little guy spirals perfectly through the uprights, and the camera then pans back to see the golden footed man: HOT "NICE ASS" MASK who is dressed in a pair of Airwalks, Tommy Jeans t-shirt, Tommy Jeans jeans, and Oakley sunglasses. His mask is also Tommy Jeans. Dancing the Macarena in celebration…) ONGH ONGH! (…he pulls out a can of Surge from his cargo pocket in the 7th motion and slams it down. Looking into the camera, he screams right into the lens.) Hot Mask (HM): MAXIM MAGAZINE! OOOOOOOOOH WAAAAAAAAAAAH-AH-AH-AH! (Hot Mask then runs to the sideline where there are several cheerleaders dressed in hot outfits cheering him on. One of the cheerleaders looks just like Carmen Electra, so Mask high-fives her camel toe with his face and she is cool with it, because he is a celebrity. After winning the big game, though, what celebration would be complete without the Gatorade bath? It is none other than Sugar Ray's Mark McGrath who dumps the Gatorade all over Hot Mask, leaving him soaking wet! The music stops now.) Mark McGrath (MM): Great kick, Hot Mask! HM: Thanks, Mark McGrath from Sugar Ray! I love your song, "Pretty Fly For A White Guy." MM: Um, actually, that's The Off… HM: Right. But I have to tell ya, Mark, one guy who won't be singing is Bryan Mayhem after our match on this week's WorldWide. Well, he might be singing…the BLUES. (Hot Mask laughs wickedly, like Dr. Evil. Mark McGrath joins him, then they abruptly stop after five seconds.) HM: Or I might be the one singing him…to SLEEP. (Hot Mask laughs wickedly, like Dr. Evil. Mark McGrath joins him, then they abruptly stop after five seconds.) HM: Or, he might be singing in the rain…like in Clockwork Orange! (Hot Mask laughs wickedly, like Dr. Evil. Mark McGrath joins him, then they abruptly stop after five seconds.) HM: Because I am going to forcibly rape him. (The sound of a record scratching is heard in the background. Mark McGrath shakes his head with disappointment.) HM: With a fork. And I will be able to, because our match is going to be hardcore. So Bryan Mayhem, you might be bigger than me, you might be stronger than me, and you might be able to make me pass out because you stink so bad…but I am going to put a fork in your butt-hole and stab-stab-stab. I have also written a nice poem of other things I plan on doing, too. (Suddenly, the groundhog that Hot Mask just kicked through the goalposts walks up to him and hands him a piece of paper. Mark McGrath picks up the groundhog and sticks it in his shirt. The groundhog is cool with it. Hot Mask then starts reading from the list as to what he's going to do.) HM: I will rape you with a fork. I will rape you with a spoon. I will rape you with a spork. I will rape you with the moon. I will rape you with a can of Spam. I will rape you, Hot Mask I am. (Hot Mask clears his throat out and continues.) HM: I will be the home-run hitter. I will score the touchdown run. I will violate your shitter. I will have a lot of fun! I will listen to the band Wham!. I will win, Hot Mask I am. (The groundhog gives a soft clap, and so do the cheerleaders. One will get the clap from Hot Mask later, LOL!) HM: Thank you. Now I need to go find happiness, because that is what the point of my story is. Good bye! (Fade to black.)
