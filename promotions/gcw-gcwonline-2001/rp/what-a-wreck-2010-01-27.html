What a Wreck

27 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/27/10 The mask was suffocating, it made him feel claustrophobic. It wasn't even his idea to wear the thing; it was on his boss to make that kind of command decision. Sweat had collected in every opening the leather mask made as it wrinkled away from his skin which caused his head to feel heavy and his body to become weary. On one knee, the reluctant(ly) masked "hero" stared up at his adversary. He noticed the arrogant smirk, heard the low leveled laughter and he felt the early and very real stages of embarrassment creeping in. The crowd could be heard but it was just a jumbled mess of thuds and shrieks. The kick to his ribs jolted him, sending pain surging through the right side of his abdomen. Before he could do much to fight back, his head had been pulled on and used to force him up to his feet where he was made to look eye to eye with the person who wanted nothing more than to leave him bloody, limp and perhaps even lifeless. Things felt bleak, not only for this encounter but also for the entire life force that was this certain hero's career. Something needed changed but it wasn't clear what that something needed to be. In his gut, the hero knew there was this energy, this entity that was just waiting to come forth and burst onto the scene ... it just seemed so impossible to reach. This 'Hero' is Khris Krowe ... and Khris Krowe was me. It feels like a lifetime ago now, probably because it was a lifetime ago. A choice needed to be made. In 2001 I was nothing more than a worker bee trying to make a good first impression. I toed the company line with ideas that Caldera and his group of creative thinkers knew what was best all the time. So I was trapped inside of a persona that wasn't built for me. So I stood there tired, beaten and completely at the mercy of a prick named Capo. 'Take the beating' is what they had told me, it'd assist with gaining sympathy which would set me on the path that I needed to be on. To be accepted To be loved Capo's smile grew as he wound up to finish me off. My mind raced, the feelings of another loss, more pain and punishment washed over me. It was almost unbearable. No, it WAS unbearable. When Capo swung he was met by something he hadn't encountered to that point ... resistance. Instinctively I kicked him in the stomach which probably caught him off guard more than it actually hurt him. I didn't stop though as something took me over and I kept kicking and kicking and kicking and kicking and ... Well you get the idea. I couldn't say exactly how long or how many times I drove my boot into that man's body and skull but at a point I got tired of lifting my leg. Once I finished my onslaught I took a few steps back to admire my work. Before my mind could register what was happening, my body went into the driver's seat and I was leaping outside of the ring. It was as if I had radar on and my sights were set on one thing. A steel chair. There are thousands of decisions a person makes in their life and all of them help to shape you into the person that you become. Once in awhile though you arrive at a moment to do something that can alter the journey of your life completely. I came to one of these forks in the road on that night, standing above a man I hardly knew with a weapon handy and the ability to either destroy him or snap back to my senses and follow the path laid out for me by Steven Caldera. Should I be this super hero? Can I make it by doing what I want? My decision came in a flash but that moment seemed to hang for an eternity. But I brought that chair down on Capo's head anyways, the force causing the chair to basically explode into numerous pieces. Every ounce of frustration, tension and built up anger melted away in that split second. It was like being unshackled after being locked away for twenty years. Beneath that mask, I smiled something of a wicked smile. I had never felt anything like it; freedom to do what I want. The ability to make people feel the way I wanted them to feel. It was .... amazing, even intoxicating. I brought my hands to my face and stared at them, the bright red blood glistening under the lights from above. I watched it smear as I rubbed them together, I was mesmermized. I balled them up into tight fists before lowering them to my sides. I looked to the left, beyond the ropes the crowd was in a frenzy but it was still surreal, everyone appeared to be in a fog. I looked to the right ... And there he was. Capo. Bloody, limp and lifeless. Beautiful. Slowly I pushed my body upright, shaking out the cobwebs as I rose. My eyes never left Capo's fallen body. I couldn't shake one thought no matter how hard I tried to: I caused this. The frenzied crowd, the battered body, the devastation. With one swipe of a piece of metal, I flipped thousands of people upside down. It was a power that I did not imagine I could possess, especially not the way my career had begun. I ran my right hand across the smooth surface of my mask. The leather was warm and slick. I reached to the back of my neck where the mask's opening could be grabbed and I latched on. I hesitated for only a moment just long enough to close my eyes. Then I peeled the mask off... Finally I could breath normally, I could be myself and I could let the world see what they hadn't before. In that moment I committed murder, Khris Krowe was finished. To be honest it was nothing more than a mercy killing. Krowe couldn't last because the disguise was weak and GCW didn't need a new hero without a face. What GCW needed was something much more profound, a moment that people could one day point to and realize that they had just witnessed something special. From humble beginnings arose an icon. I dropped the mask in the center of the ring. LeStatt Knight was free from his cage.
