Importance of Wrestling: Happiness

18 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18 April 2007 I had never liked professional wrestling, not even a little bit. Sure some of the guys in short, spandex shorts were incredibly attractive but other than that? I thought the sport was fairly ? well ? retarded. I was introduced to it when I was young by my father and eventually my two brothers as well. I’ve seen all the different generations from Ric Flair to Andre The Giant to Hulk Hogan to Bret Hart to Mr. Perfect to Stone Cold to The Rock to Goldberg to John Cena to Undertaker. I know what you’re thinking too ? if you hate wrestling so much then how do you know all these names? Just because you dislike something doesn’t mean you can run away from it. A lesson I’ve had to learn over and over again in my life. Anyways. I just never saw the attraction to the sport, I mean everyone KNOWS the stuff is fake, the acts are scripted and the feuds for the most part are Soap Opera-esque. I’m a grown woman and I don’t even watch soap operas. I’ve always found it funny that grown MEN would, even if they try and pretend that it isn’t just one big, testosterone filled soap opera. Ya know how guys are ? bunch of tough guys. When I finally got old enough to move out on my own it was almost a relief to be able to watch NORMAL television for once. However, things have a way of happening that you didn’t expect. Enter Glenn Ronald Crosby into my life. Have you ever met a person whom you felt was just too good to be true? Well I got that feeling about this man within a half hour of knowing him. I can’t say that it was love at first sight but it was as close as I’ll ever be in my lifetime. We hit it off so well, laughed, danced and smiled all night long. It wasn’t until the second date that I found the one thing about him that wasn’t good. His profession. No not a professional wrestler. He was a soldier in the United States Army. Many people thought I was crazy as I willingly got into a serious relationship with Glenn, hell I had a part of me that thought the same thing. Problem is that no one can control who they fall in love with, no one. So not only is Glenn a Soldier but surprise, surprise he is a fanatic of professional wrestling! YAY! So within a year Glenn and I were moved in together, 6 months after that I was Mrs. Glenn Crosby. Then came little Riley Jarod Crosby. Even on drugs like I was I remember my angel being born. Riley became our world and our light. It did not matter how angry Glenn and I would get at each other, at work, or anything else because just seeing Riley’s smile could make all of our problems melt away. We had our issues like any family does but whatever problems arose we handled them together. And of course I couldn’t escape wrestling either. From the time Riley could sit up on Glenn’s lap the two were watching wrestling together. I’m sure Riley had no idea what was really going on but every time those wrestlers came out his eyes went wide and his mouth gaped open. I’m not even sure if he would blink the whole two hours. As the years went by Riley understood more and more and began to love wrestling more and more. Father and son loved getting into their arguments about who was better than who and who made the best champion. Remember of course that Riley is but 7 years old. “Mommy?” Riley’s voice startles me a bit ? I have a habit of losing myself in thought these days. “Yes dear, what is it?” “How do you start a letter like this?” His voice is very soft but it’s still music to my ears considering he remained silent for so long. One event managed to change our lives as if we were right in the middle of it. An event in the heart of New York changed our lives even though we lived in Hawaii. 9-11-2001 Life was going so well that it never occurred to us that a war could come so fast and furious. It never occurred to us that Glenn would not always be there. Of course Glenn got deployed to Iraq as most all soldiers do. After the second tour of duty it still did not become easier but it did become expected ? we could at least prepare ourselves. All the time Glenn was home he’d spend with Riley. The two were not only father and son but best friends as well. Their common bond? Wrestling. They would go to shows, watch PPV’s, Glenn would read internet articles to Riley and of course they’d play the video games together. For hours Upon hours Upon hours. “Well sweetie this is going to be your own letter! What would you like to say to him?” He puckers his lips like a fish, puts his hand on his chin and looks upwards as if he were contemplating an incredibly tough math equation. Where do kids get these things from? “Well?” he says, obviously thinking through his words carefully. “Thank you of course!” I smile at him. “Then say thank you.” “But?” Riley frowns slightly. “It meant more to me than just that Mama.” He gives a frustrated look as he slinks further down into his chair. “Listen Babe,” I say to him as I kneel down beside him. “I think he knows exactly how you felt. I know he could see it in your eyes.” “You sure Mama?” His eyes almost plead with me to tell him the truth. “Positive.” Ever since we got the news ? It was 23 October 2006 when the letter came. Riley knew as well as I did what was happening although we never once had a talk about the possibility before that letter came. My husband ? Riley’s father, was dead. That morning was the last time I heard my son’s angelic voice. I tried everything to get him to talk ? even if it was just as he played with his wrestling action figurines. Before his father’s passing, Riley would commentate the matches he’d hold with those toys ? most of the time he would have more epic matches than anything seen on TV. Not to mention much more colorful commentary. But after that day? Nothing. I tried to do all of the things that his father used to do with him like playing video games and even watching wrestling on whatever night he so desired. He would come to the couch and sit with me as wrestling came on but for the most part his gaze remained empty. Except when one certain person showed themselves on screen. “Well Mama ? you think we’ll ever see LeStatt again?” “I cannot say yes for sure dear.” I smile and grab his hand. “But I have a good feeling you will.” And there is that smile that can melt problems away. Excitedly, Riley turns back to his desk and puts pencil to paper beginning a letter that he will pour his little heart into. “Good luck dear.” A month and a half went by with complete silence from Riley, it got to the point that I was afraid that he’d never speak again. That he’d never be the same again. My last hope was something that I never thought in a million years would work. What would happen if I sent Riley’s story to Global Championship Wrestling? What would happen if my son’s favorite wrestler of all time could actually meet him face to face? It was my last hope to get my son back. Ironic that I was counting on something that I had hated for so long to save my very world. Within three days I got a response back. Not from GCW interns or agents, but from the man himself. “Hola, is this the Crosby residence?” I remember his slight Hispanic accent cutting through. “This is, may I ask who is calling?” “Well I got a letter here that was sent to GCW headquarters, about a Riley Crosby.” “Wow you guys actually read that letter?” “I handle all of my fan mail personally ma’am.” “Wait a minute “are you?” “Yes Ma’am, LeStatt Knight is speaking.” He cleared his throat. “I’ll get to the point Ma’am, I’d really like to set up a meeting with your son.” “When!”” I answered so fast that it even startled me. “Tomorrow.” He said very matter of factly. “I wish to meet him tomorrow.” We kept it a surprise from Riley, hoping that Mr. Knight’s appearance would break him of this spell he was under. The doorbell rang that very next day at 10:30 A.M. Riley was laying on the couch watching television when it happened. My heart was beating fast, I remember that very well. I moved towards the door but realized that Riley should be the one answering the door. “Answer the door sweet pea.” I asked him. Without a word and barely even a sound he climbed off the couch and moved towards the front door. I watched as his tiny fingers grasped around the gold knob and twisted. His small stature made the pulling of the old front door a bit difficult but eventually the door opened all of the way. “Hello young man, Riley Crosby I take it?” The large Hispanic GCW superstar smiled a million dollar smile in that doorway. I made my way towards the door and knelt by my baby’s side. “Riley, aren’t you going to say anything?” Riley didn’t even acknowledge that I was standing there. In that moment I once again saw that small baby boy that sat on his father’s lap, eyes wide and mouth open staring at large men doing incredible things inside of a wrestling ring. Even if it were only going to last for that brief moment in time, having my real son back was worth it. Thankfully it wasn’t all. “Sorry.” Riley spoke softly. My neck almost snapped from twisting so fast in his direction. He knew I was staring at him because he slowly turned his head to look up at me. He smiled. “Come on in Mr. Knight.” I said with a smile. “Please,” He says as he walks into the doorway. “Call me Roberto.” “Do we hafta?” Riley asked. “Well what would you like to call me?” “LeStatt.” Riley said matter of factly. “Well then LeStatt it is.” Riley smiled again. LeStatt Knight came into our home for 2 hours, 21 minutes, and 42 seconds and I don’t think I ever looked over at Riley when he was not smiling or telling a story about him and his father’s attended house shows or long nights playing video games where Riley insists he “ALWAYS PLAYED AS KNIGHT!”. For once there were memories of his father coming to the surface that did not cause one of us to cry. On that night there was nothing but happiness. It was that night that I realized just how important having an idol can be to a young boy. Up until that night I never appreciated the power that a person that you never met in person can have. Of course I have no idea if what LeStatt did was sincere or if it was just something to get him some good publicity. I also do not know if he really enjoyed coming here ? the guy is paid to act after all. Fact is though, LeStatt Knight saved a boy’s life that day. Since then I have sat with Riley and watched every show that Global Championship Wrestling has put out. Together we have cheered and clapped for our favorite GCW superstar. The man who gave us a piece of our life back. “Mama?” Riley’s voice again interrupts my thoughts. “Yes dear?” “It doesn’t matter if LeStatt doesn’t come back to see us again.” “Oh?” “No ? I know he cares.” “And how is that” Did he tell you?” “Daddy did.” I can feel the tears coming to my eyes ? I think Riley can too. “No don’t cry mom, it’s ok!” Riley says as he gets up from his chair and hugs me tight around my waist. “Daddy said LeStatt was a very nice man.” A tear drop falls down my cheek. “D-Did he?” “Uh-huh and ya know what else?” I just shake my head. “He wanted me to tell ya that he loves you very much.” I kneel down and hold him close. A man I had never met before helped bring my baby back. A man who did not have to answer my letter back saved us. NOW I know why people love wrestling. I pull away from Riley now. “Hun ? go finish that letter, it’s very important.” “Okay Mama.” Thank you, LeStatt Knight. Wherever you are.
