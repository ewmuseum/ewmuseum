Losing Everything

undated

Date: Timeless Dorian Ryan walked up to the charred ruin of what was once the home he shared with his wife, Andrea. They had already picked through what belongings survived the fire, there wasn’t much left from their old life left. As he walks among the burnt foundation, he leans down and finds a picture. It was old, and faded. The frame was blackened from soot. The glass was broken. But the picture itself wasn’t damaged. It was a picture of a young Dorian Ryan with his brother and sister at a wrestling show in North Carolina. “I’m surprised that you survived.” Dorian muttered under his breath. He starts to search through the remains of his home to see what else he could find. There wasn’t much that was left that was salvageable, but he was hoping, against hope, that he could find more. After an hour of looking, it wasn’t to be. The cleanup crew that was hired to remove what was left of the house was arriving. He knew that he was going to have to leave soon. Head back to the place that he and Andrea had called home since the fire. He walked towards the front porch, and sees something out of the corner of his eye. The shine was unmistakable. He walked over, and pulled from the rubble a silver medallion. Something that Dorian remembered hanging near the front door as it had been passed down to Andrea from her mother. For the first time, Dorian had a smile come to his face. A half hour later, Dorian pulled up to a storage room. He opened it and noticed that some stuff was missing from it. “What the hell?” Dorian looked and found that everything that was missing belonged to Andrea. He doesn’t waste any time betting back to his car and driving off, back to his brother’s home. As he arrives, he quickly heads to the spare bedroom that he and Andrea were using. As he got there, he saw that her belongings were gone as well. There was an envelope sitting against a pillow addressed to Dorian. A few hours later, Serena Ryan arrived back at the house with her newborn daughter. As she entered the house, she saw Dorian there, with a bottle of Jack Daniels and an empty glass in front of him. “Dorian, what’s wrong?” Serena came over to see that her brother-in-law was just staring at the note that was left for him earlier in the day. Serena takes it from him and sets her daughter on the table. “My dearest Dorian, I know that by the time you get this, I won’t be anywhere near. I’m sorry that I couldn’t say this to you, but that I had to do it this way. I lack the emotional strength though, and I know that if I talked to you about it, that you would try to stop me from doing this. After the fire, I know that you lost everything in your life. You lost your past. You lost what little you had left of Keiko and Dan. For that, I’m truly, deeply sorry. I know how hard it is for you, and you’ve been acting like a rock. You’ve been a shoulder for me to cry on these last few days. But it’s time for me to find myself. When we first met, it was like a whirlwind. We never had a chance to think about what we meant to each other when we got married. This I have to do for myself. Maybe I’ll find what I’m looking for. Maybe I won’t. But believe me when I say that I am truly sorry for what I’m putting you through now, I never wanted something like this to happen. I only ever wanted you to be happy. Love, Andrea? As Serena finished reading the letter out loud, she saw Dorian fill up the glass and take a drink. It didn’t take him long to finish it, as he refilled it to drown away any of the feelings that he had about the situation. Serena could see that he was devastated by the letter, but she didn’t know the best way to consul him. “Maybe, I’ll just move back to Japan. Come to the States only for my matches. I remember being happy there.” Serena didn’t know how to respond to it. Instead, she picked her daughter up and walked out of the room. Dorian finished off another drink as Serena came back into the kitchen. “Dorian, it’s three in the afternoon. You shouldn’t be drinking alone.” Serena then took a glass out of the cupboard and sat across from Dorian. She took the bottle and filled her glass. “Do you ever have the fear that while on the road, Jason isn’t going to come back” I mean, he’s been doing this for a long time. He’s faithfulness has been bound to be tested before.” Serena takes a drink from the glass. “Dorian, if you ever?”” Before Serena could finish what she was saying, Dorian got up and left the room.
