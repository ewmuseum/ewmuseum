Abandoned and Alone

17 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/17/2005 She was trapped in a world filled with every nightmare that had plagued her since childbirth. That damaged diva lay upon a stiff hospital bed barely moving physically as her mind waged a war within threatening to steal her sanity. The struggle to wake up was taking a heavier toll on her each time she tried to force those eyes open. The medication in her IV was a heedy dose; the drip steadily stealing her will to stay in the waking world with each passing drop. That powerful will she bore deeply rooted in her soul would win out once again after a hefty struggle those dimmed blue eyes lifting to stare at a blurry ceiling. She felt numb, her limbs were heavy. It took several minutes of struggling to focus her mind before the sounds of the machines and the steady beat of her heart became apparent to her. With a slow shift that head rolled to the side to spy a sleeping Erik hunched in a chair in the corner. She frowned at the sight of him. He looked so different, broken somehow, even in his sleep. Wincing she slow moved using her sore arms to pull herself up in the bed trying to sit up. Shaky fingers ran through her platinum mane working vainly to free it of the mass of knots that had been acquired during her recent struggles. It must have been a good five minutes before it dawned on her she was being watched. Those pale colored eyes slowly turning to peer into the mirrored gaze of her twin. There was so much to say, and yet, niether of them would start what they knew would be a painful experience. The chair Erik has been dozing in creeked as he pushed himself to his feet and walked over to Eva. Leaning forward, he placed a hand on the back of her head and pressed his lips to her forehead before turning to slip out of the room. She could do nothing but watch as that door shut, the final click of it more like the sound of finality. She didn't understand it. She likely never would understand it. Why had Erik not warned her? Did he honestly think he could save her by not telling her the danger? Somehow she knew she would never know... Sleep. Eva's eyes opened once more, clearer this time as she found herself staring at a vase full of white roses. Brow furrowed as she propped herself up on elbow and took a look around. There were flowers everywhere. Orchids, Lilies, Roses, they were all here lining the walls and part of the floor. Toying with lower lip between her teeth she rolled to her back and cringed as she pulled herself to sit up just as the door opened once more to reveal the familiar sight of Steven Caldera. Slowly bruised brow perked as he offered her a small grin and said nothing as he grabbed a chair and pulled it beside the bed to take a seat. ' How are you feeling Eva?' ' I've felt better.' ' I just spoke with your doctor. He said you can leave the hospital in the morning as there is no unseen injuries.' Eva tightened her jaw at that. No unseen injuries? What of her pride? What of the mental and emotional damage that rape could cause? Doctors..what did they know? Eva merely sighed and nodded as she settled back into her pillows her mind wandering to Lestatt. ' Have you seen Lestatt?' Caldera merely shook his head. ' I only just managed to pull away to check on you personally.' How sweet, she thought with mental sarcasm. Checking on your product Caldera? Eva said nothing and merely looked at him with the eyes of a woman who was numb with anger and pain. ' Eva.... look I am sorry for what happened to you. We have increased security because of it and when you come back you will always be escorted to your car.' Eva's eyes flashed. ' You mean if I come back Mr. Caldera.' Steven leaned forward and looked her directly in the eyes. ' No, I mean when you do. Women like you are not the-' ' Women like me? So what you suddenly know me?' He merely shook his head and sighed allowing the silence to swallow them both before standing up and looking down on her. ' I know your spirit. You are not a quitter Eva. It isn't in you. When you are ready to come back to the ring..you know my number.' Eva just stared daggers at him. She wanted so bad to be angry with the man, but he wasn't the culprit here and she knew it. Steven patted her arm only to have her flinch away and turned to head out the door. He paused at the door and turned to look at her once more. ' I hope to hear from you soon.' Then he was gone. Eva was alone...again. It was a strange feeling; it was something she was unused too and something she had decidely disliked. Scowling she turned to her side, back facing the door only to stare absent mindly at the white roses on the night stand next to her. Oddly enough, this time she could read the little card included. ' Wishing you a speedy recovery...Steven Caldera.' She scowled and rolled away from the view. It seemed there was no escape from all of this, for on the other nightstand were more flowers...orchids. ' Get well soon...from everyone at Anarch Agencies.' Before she could think about what she was doing her hands were reaching out and plucking up the vase. Hauling it over head she hurled it across the room shattering the glass on the opposite wall. The anger was reaching a boiling point again, like a demon rearing its ugly head. Damn them! Damn them all to hell! Flopping back on her pillows, she crossed her arms stubbornly refusing to look at the nurse who rushed in to see what the commotion was about. As the woman made small tsking noises with her tongue Eva merely shot her a dark look. The woman shook her head and dialed for janitorial to come clean up the mess. Immediately after hanging up she leaned out the door and called for a couple of more nurses. It had been decided the flowers would all be placed out of Eva's reach, for her own safety and the safety of others apparently. Eva didn't care. She was too busy marveling over the fact there were several people in her room and still she felt that chilling feeling of lonliness. Where was Lestatt? She needed him right now and he was nowhere to be found. A single tear trailed down her face as they left and she turned to attempt to sleep once more. Alone....in the dark.
