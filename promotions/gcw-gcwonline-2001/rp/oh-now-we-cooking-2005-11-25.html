Oh Now we cooking

25 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25-11-05 Every now and then you cross paths with someone who will change your life forever. Scary thought much? True none the less. Cynics would have you believe life is only what you make it. That fate plays no part at all and maybe they’re right. Maybe. Either way, every once in a while something strange happens and you find yourself in this place, an empty void standing at a cross roads, not knowing which path to take. If you’d only think with “yourself.” Not your head and maybe not even your heart but your entire essence of self. That whole that makes you, you. That makes you see, that lets you be and do and feel all there is to taste, touch, see, hear, smell. Mary had stood at that cross road many a time since she was five but she was closed inside. She didn’t think with herself, she panicked, she froze and she waited for someone to take her away, show her the way. That’s not always a good idea because every time she did, someone came and made the choice for her. Free choice. What an odd concept. So huge a thing to be encapsulated by two small words. Hardly seems right. But that’s he great thing. There is no right, no wrong, just choice and consequence which in turn creates more choice. It’s living and breathing and all together exhilarating especially if you’re the god like entity watching the soap opera of human existence play out. Mary didn’t reckon on free choice stood at that cross road every moment of every day of her life for eight years. She gave it away each and every time instead. You see the thing about free choice, about choices in general is that they’re infinite, infinite and immortal and ever evolving. They don’t exist because of you, because you dreamt them up after a light bulb appeared over your head on the other side of that annoyingly camp talking green alien, no. They exist “in spite of,” along side, before, during and ever after you. forego your choice, and someone else gets to make it for you. Mary didn’t know or at least didn’t see and so her uncle had met her at that cross roads and dragged her down the path he wanted her to take. But see, he stole her choice. He didn’t allow her to make it herself and so tainted the choice he forced upon her. Not the best idea because taking peoples choice away never ends well. People end up being hurt on both sides. He found this out the hard way because when Mary’s eyes were open to the actuality of choice, she grabbed at it with both hands like a thirsty man at a desert spring and she drank in it as if it would end any moment and all be a mirage, an illusion, a cruel trick of the mind. It wasn’t. He was good and dead. Well not good, but dead just as dead. Or at least it was a work in progress. The nightmares had started, not frequently yet but often enough and it spooked the shit out of her. Small price to pay she guessed. So yea. Choice. A hell of a thing to have for the first time in 9 years when your only 14 years old. A hell of a thing. Mary smiled sitting in her room in three quarter length shorts and a baggy off the shoulder miffy sweater. Her striped knee high socks were odd colours and her shell toes were worn and scuffed. Well loved she called them. She stared at the squirrels fighting outside her large mesh covered window. Lunch had just finished. The place was quiet. Too quiet. The staff were busy watching the bulimics like hawks. Half the freaking ward had a habit of losing their lunch after every meal, boys and girls. The staff had to watch them for at least 3 hours post eating. Mary felt a little sorry for them. Not that she was any better off. She was labelled a self-harmer. They’d taken her shoe laces, made her cut her finger nails, taken her bobby pins, her belts, her healed shoes, her pens and pencils and anything else pointy. What a bitch. “knock knock,” the voice came from the doorway, it made Mary jump slightly but she regained her composure as the face peeked round the side. Skye was there. She sauntered in without invitation, a spring in her step. She was smiling. “Julian and Jimmy said this is where you stay at.” Mary didn’t say a word. She stared at Skye who was now standing before her in faded denim cut offs, old worn converse and a worn Rainbow Bitch T shirt. A far cry from the tired shell of a girl she saw strapped to that hospital bed not so long ago. It had been a week ago. “They let me out just like you said. I wanted to say thanks for visiting me is all.” Mary got out of her chair and climbed onto her bed at the nearest end to the wall. She hugged her headless stuffed monkey to her chest. Still she remained silent, just shrugging her shoulders in a it was nothing gesture. Skye waltzed up to her bed and perched herself on the far end picking up a near by rubick cube. “I know you were there. They drugged me up but good. I could hear you though. You came every night. I felt you check my bandages.” She smiled at Mary but Mary looked nervous and said nothing. Skye held up the cube. “Hey what’s ya record with one of these things” Takes me like months to do one, how bout you?” Mary shrugged gazing into the distance. “Three, maybe four.” “Months?” Skye asked. “Hours.” Skye’s mouth dropped open. “Woah! What are you some kinda genius or something?” Mary shrugged again and looked at Skye. “Dunno, they said gifted, don’t mean nothing no how.” Skye smiled, but Mary stared straight at her. “Your hair. Is long. Mary thought it was short, but is way long.” “Yea I know. Wanna cut it?” Skye’s greeny brown eyes lit up, her face beaming mischievously. “Can’t, they took all the sharp stuff. Definitely wont let us have scissors.” Skye rolled her eyes and giggled. “Ooh we are gonna have so much fun Mary, Mary. There are ways and means Anjo.” She hopped off the bed and darted out of the room, only to be back in minutes with a picture frame. The picture was of her and an older man, in his 50s maybe. Handsome, powerful looking, with a thick moustache, in a business suit with the shirts top two buttons undone. “This is a photo of me and mi Papa. He put me in here. Anyways that aint important. This is plastic not glass. Ya know like err’I think its called Perspex or something.” She tapped the front. “They let me keep the photo because it didn’t have glass in it.” Skye unfastened the frame from the backing and slid the hard plastic sheet out, it was about 5 by 5 inches and about a cm thick. “Check it out.” She went over to the window and slid her arms through the bars. The window was open but the bars were solid. The ledge outside the window was concrete. Skye rubbed the angled edge of the plastic back and forth until she pulled back a near knife sharp edge. She smiled excited. “You can make a blade out of anything. They cant stop you.” Mary was wide eyed. “How you?”” “My dad was inside for five years when I was younger. He used to tell me lil bits about it. Maybe that’s why he ended up having to put me in one of these shit holes.” Skye handed the shard to Mary and sat with her back to her. “My Dad’s like some kingpin underworld boss man type thing but you cant tell anyone ok?” Mary nodded, “How much you want off?” Skye shrugged, “Like I dunno, shoulder length?” Mary nodded again and Skye continued. “So I hear ya papa used to do bad things to you.” Mary grabbed a handful and had to saw her way through the strands. “Not, Daddy. Mary’s parents are gone away. Was Uncle, he did the bad things.” “Oh right, sorry. Well I also hear, you messed his ass up, like permanently snooze town.” Mary stifled a giggle and hacked some more. “Owch.” Skye let out. “Sorry.” Mary dropped went to drop the shard panic but Skye’s hand gripped hers in an instant. “No, don’t go away. Finish it’please?” Mary continued with a nod. “Mary be more gentle.” Mary hacked and hacked. Hair fluttered to the ground with a dramatic silence. Mary hacked through the last chunk of soft dark dark brown hair. She could smell it, it smelled like mangoes and coconut. She closed her eyes, sawing through the hair but her concentration slipped and she cut through the hair and into her palm and down her wrist a little. She jerked her hand away and dropped the glass but didn’t cry out. Skye turned around. “Shit Mary! Ya bleedin?” Skye grabbed her hand. “Is okies, don’t really hurt.” Skye stared at Mary. “You don’t feel it?” She wouldn’t let go of her hand. “Mary feels it. Just, she’s used to having pain and not letting it be bigger than her. With Uncle well?” She trailed off. “Just need a Kleenex, be fine so long as the Dr people don’t see it.” Mary went to pull her hand away but Skye didn’t let go, or at least wouldn’t. She stared intensely at Mary and Mary stared back. There was something in that girl, something in both of them that reached out from inside both of them and touched with the barest of fingertips. Skye didn’t take her eyes off Mary as she pulled Mary’s slowly bleeding palm to her lips. Before a drop could leak over and hit the floor, Skye licked a long trail from her palm down the wound to Mary’s wrist. She leaned back, her voice was almost a whisper. “The bleeding should stop soon. You taste like’like,” She looked as if she was lost for the right words. “You taste like burning sunshine.” She smiled. Mary shivered, she felt strange. She warmed inside. She stopped trying to pull her hand back. Skye tugged a strand of Mary’s curls out of her eye and tucked it behind her ear. “You have amazing hair. Don’t ever hack it off.” Mary blushed and tried to look away as Skye dropped her hand. But it didn’t last long. She felt like out of everyone, she could look at Skye and see nothing but the truth, get nothing but the truth. She didn’t turn away when Skye snuck into her room that night and stayed up talking. She didn’t turn away when Skye offered to take her to the pharmacy. She sure as hell didn’t look away when Skye kissed her for the first time, or when she touched her in a way she’d never really been touched before, with a tenderness and innocence she’d never known. She didn’t turn away when Skye asked if they could bleed each other, if they could taste each other, if they could inhale each other. She did turn away when she told her that she loved her, and that she would until she died. Mary had never heard or felt devotion like that since her parents had been alive. That’s why it hurt so bad when Skye was taken away. They said she was making progress, that her father wished her to be moved to a lesser secure unit nearer home. That day, they had to drag Mary and Skye apart. After they tracked them down hiding in the basement of course. Mary and Skye held on to each other so hard, with so much that they clawed each others hands raw. Skye screamed and screamed and cried. Mary yelled and raged and the words, “EU TE AMO ANJO, PARA SIEMPRE!” “I love you Angel. Forever!” The words echoed in the halls and Mary’s head for months afterwards. Months that she didn’t speak. Months that she raged against herself. Months she harmed herself and took every drug she could find. For months that she didn’t receive a single letter, not a single phone call. Skye promised to love her forever but the staff thought otherwise. They kept back her mail, had sessions to “cure” her. “Skye had to leave Marianne you know this.” “NO! MARY!” “Ok’Mary. You and Skye, you had a very special’friendship but it was’not in your best interests.” “NO! NOT FREINDSHIP! AMO’AMO!! LOVE!” “I don’t think you understand Mary.” “NO, Mary don’t think YOU understand. Skye is the first person since her parents to really love her. To be this big freaking light!” “She was..is ill Mary, just as you are and we wish to help you.” “Then bring Mary’s love back. Please..” “Love Mary” You’re only fifteen. You cant possibly understand what life has in store for you. You’ll get better and leave here, get a job and get married and be able to live a regular life like everyone else.” “Regular” REGULAR! You think Skye is just another problem to fix don’t you?” “I think your attachment to each other is some what unhealthy born of a need to belong and be loved that your Uncle did not give you. I think pouring your feelings into a person of the same sex is just a safety net for you and we can help you work that out Mary. Just let us help you.” “You don’t get it doc. You never will. It aint about Skye being a girly, hot as she is. It aint about that. Mary sees into her, she’s beautiful inside, she’s a freaking rainbow. Wouldn’t matter if she was a boy, she’d have the same heart and soul and you ripped that away from Mary. Mary’s blood is on your hands!” And that would be round about the time Mary would cut herself with a home made shank. She kept that up for three weeks before they put her in a padded room and pumped for of so many drugs she didn’t know which way was up. She almost forgot Skye when the sixth month rolled around. But almost isn’t definitely. Everybody knows, almost doesn’t count. One year, 26 suicide attempts, 39 scars and over 1500 hours of therapy later and Mary lay strapped down to a hospital bed in the “quiet” room. The same room that Skye was left in, drugged up and alone when she first arrived. Her curls lacked luster as they splayed about the off white sheets atop her mattress. Tears slowly leaked from her eyes to drain down to her ears in a constant slow flow even though she didn’t seem to be crying at all. As if the sorrow filled her so much she overflowed with it. It wasn’t sorrow at Skye being taken away anymore, not just that anyhow. It was more that she wasn’t allowed to grieve without drugs, she wasn’t allowed to write or love at all. She wasn’t allowed to express pain or any emotion that wasn’t light and fluffy. They didn’t want to help patients into society. The hospital created zombies, conformists, clones, drones. She stared unblinking at the ceiling, the straps burning at her wrists and ankles that held her down. It was dark and quiet. Well at least quieter than earlier. She wasn’t sure what day it was. She remembered there being a commotion a day or so ago, she couldn’t be accurate but it was new patient commotion. She heard the doors click open and shut towards the Brighton wing. That’s where the high risk patients were housed. Her door rattled but she didn’t move. She could hardly respond. A figure moved into the dark. Tall, slight, lithe. The figure moved to Mary’s side slowly circling the bed and she got glimpses of him. His face was narrow, high cheek bones. His eyes were narrow and dark and his hair was jet black and hung down to his shoulders like silk drapes. He moved with a silent danger and Mary would have tensed up if not for all the shit in her system forcing her moods to be neutral. She felt like a stopped up plug hole, her emotions were dampened painfully. His voice was like grey satin. “Hey momma.” Mary didn’t move, her eyes still unblinking. She needed something, but she wasn’t sure what. This man, well boy, he couldn’t be more than 18 if a day, he was new and unknown and possibly dangerous but hell if he wasn’t intriguing. “They call me Scar.” His southern accent slid over her caressing and her fingers twitched. “Am knew here.” His voice lowered to a whisper. “I saw them all haul ya in here. Brighton wing Momma. Ain’t that something. You and I done been upgraded to high risk.” He was careful not to touch her with anything other than his voice. “Don’t worry, I would never hurt you. They’ve done that enough I reckon. You aint sposed to be in here. People like me are sposed to be in here but not you. You’re pure baby. I’ve heard about you. I feel it in you. Your pure and they’re hurtin you, I can’t let them keep doing that.” He reached into his jeans and pulled a piece of tissue from his pants. He pulled it apart to reveal a crude cutting edge. Scar ran his hands down her arm then down to the hem of her shirt. He pulled it up and her fingers twitched again. “Shh’it’s so they wont see when they come checkin on you. Trust me.” He lifted the hem and drew a slow deliberate line across her side. Mary gasped as soon as her skin split. Her back bowed her limbs went rigid and then she collapsed to the bed panting. And for the first time in a week, her emotions overwhelmed her. She cried and cried. Tears pumped from her now blinking eyes, her senses were overloaded. Scar reached over with the sleeve of his shirt and wiped her tears away with a intense almost smile on his face but not quite. “You gonna be just fine Momma.” Mary fought to get the word out. “Obrigado.” He’d given her a gift. He wasn’t trying to hurt her he was trying to help her. Her brain forgot the drugs and gripped the pain, she wasn’t a zombie anymore, they didn’t have a hold of her anymore. No one had done something that amazing for her since Skye was around. Scar actually smiled this time. “No problem beautiful.” He looked like he heard something. “I gotta go before they go on they rounds. I’ll be back later. You stay strong, be good, then they’ll let you out, then?” “Then Mary come find you?” She looked hesitant but Scar nodded and smiled back. “Can’t wait. Gonna be some furniture moving Momma.” He headed for the door but Mary called out after him. “Babyboy?” “Yes Momma?” “Excitin times.” She smiled and he winked back effortlessly.
