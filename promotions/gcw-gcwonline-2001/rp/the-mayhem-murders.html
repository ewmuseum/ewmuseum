The Mayhem Murders

Date: ?? Campfire stories go back eons. They are always told in large groups of people in order to scare the living hell out of one another. These stories thousands of years ago were usually about the angry retaliating gods upon their disrespectful subjects. Nowadays most are just ghost stories, just so that one special girl that year at camp cuddled in your arms at the scary parts. Mummies, beasts, and axe carrying butchers from the bottom of a shallow lake always seem to do the trick. How many of these stories are true? Most say none, they are just stories, but there was this one time, this one story in particular I remember that stood the test of fiction and won. Before I begin I must say I do not remember all the gory details because what happened afterwards was so traumatizing to a 16 year boy that I spent years in a padded room telling tales of a fire demon killing us all, that I became addicted to so many medication that most of my memory was extinguished from my brain. My name is Charles Donoahue from London, England. I am the oldest of three children. I have 2 younger sisters, who have since than, disowned me from their lives. I came from a wealthy family. My father was a Marshall in the House of Lords, and my mother worked for a private bank establishment, the name escapes me. Every summer, ever since I was atleast 7 or 8 was spent in summer camp. I loved camp, that’s why I kept going back every year. I got my first sniff of the female paradise at camp. My man parts were also dipped into the wet ecstacy of a woman at camp, only 2 weeks prior to this story. Every summer we ended the camp with a huge backpacking and camping trip into the wilderness. It was three days long. We lived from our backpacks. It was almost like what you read in history books. No electricity, no kitchens, no running water. Us and nature, and I loved it. I was convinced back than that I lived in the wrong time period. I preferred the rugged outdoor life over the posh modern day streetlight, microwave, internet existence. This year were separated into groups of 12. Six boys and six girls, with four camp counselors. The six boys and six girls were than broken down into groups of three, 1 per camp counselor. I was lucky because my now steady fling, Margaret Bodden was in the group just across the fire. One night we snuck out into the woods and I received my first blowjob. I don’t remember much, because of medication, but I do remember how she worked it, that thought will never escape my mind. We would stay up all night around the campfire roasting weenies and marshmallows and just bullshitting. My counselor Derrek had an acoustic guitar, and knew every beatles song imagineable. So there were nights we sang about Lucy in the Sky, or about some beauty named Penny Lane. It was the most fun I’ve ever had, and unfortunately will ever have. The night in question started like this. We all finished dinner, sat around the fire and decided it was scary story time. I will be honest with you, coming from the land of Dracula and Elizabeth Bathory, nothing scares me. This was just a good chance for me to feel up on Margaret everytime something scared her. Derrek told us he had a story, but it was a true one (of course it was), and it happened right where we were camping at. Oh shit, I forgot to tell you, we decided to hike to Belmont, the home of Elizabeth Bathory. See, my mind is fried, I apologize. Now I knew the story of Elizabeth Bathory. Countess of the land in the late 15th century would kidnap virgins of the land, put them in a cage above her tub, and have her subjects poke blades into the cage allowing the blood of the virgins to drip on her while she bathed. It’s also said that she would later eat their hearts and private parts because she felt that’s where they were most pure. So I figured that was story Derrek was going to tell us. Instead he threw a wrench into my thinking and told us about The O’Neil Family. This story, another one I knew, was taboo in England. This family was so sick and twisted that all records of the family existing were wiped from history books. What makes this story so frightening, so scary is the fact that all this happened only decades ago. The mother is dead, and so is the father. The children’s whereabouts are unknown. All my thoughts of feeling up Margaret exited stage left, this I had to hear. Derrek told us about the abuse the two children went through at the hands of their father. He told us of the vicious rape of their mother by the father that the oldest Bryan witnessed. He witnessed her life being choked out, and something inside the teenager snapped and he stabbed his father in the head with a piece of wood from his bed that he, with some adrenalized strength, ripped off and used as a weapon. I vaguely remember that story from my youth, but the next part I do not recall ever hearing. Now I know how stories can be fabricated as they are told from person to person, however this one I never heard one iota. He pointed to the east, the woods where I received that blowjob, and said that a few years ago a camp group came to Belmont, just like we had, and sat around the same area we did. He said that one night while everyone was sleeping the oldest son Bryan, the tormented, giant (6’10?) one came into the campsite and wiped out the entire camp. The women were beheaded and the boys were burned alive. He also pointed towards the area where it looks like the trees were burnt and unable to bud anymore, and said the camp counselor was hung upside down, gutted, than set on fire. If you looked into the woods, the way the trees grew around the dead one, you could definitely vision this, as sick as it was. Margaret and the other campers looked at Derrek in horror. He did not smile at all, and told us that’s why he did not want to come here. He told us that Bryan is most likely miles from here to avoid the police and to go to bed, because we head back home tomorrow. I kissed Margaret and hugged her. We looked into each other’s like it was going to be the last time we ever saw each other. God’s will it wasn’t, but God had nothing to do with what happened next. I didn’t think I was going to be able to sleep that night, but for some reason I slept like a baby. I didn’t hear anything until I heard her scream. I knew it was her, even though I never heard her scream prior. I leapt up from my tent and noticed this giant beast of a man, engulfed in flames, with one foot on Margaret’s chest and the other with a handful of hair. I remember screaming her name, which got the giant’s attention. He looked at me, and in one swift motion yanked on her head with such force it pulled her head right from her body. I fell to my knees, vomited and passed out. I remember nothing else I swear. All I know is what I heard from the press. The others were all killed. Derrek’s remains were strewn about the camp. All the girls were beheaded, and the boys were burned alive, just like the story. Families wondered why my life was spared and the others were killed so brutally. I was also accused of being the murderer, but was later found innocent in court because of my story. I spent 8 years in a mental home because I told Scotland Yard what I saw. They thought I was crazy. Wouldn’t you? A giant man covered in flames killing 15 innocent teenagers. A few days after the Camp Noah Massacre I came conscious and noticed I carried a mark of the madman (whom was never found, but the story and investigation into the Mayhem Murders surfaced its ugly again) on my chest. It was a pentagram, a sign of the beast, and underneath was the word Mayhem. This beast still haunts me to this very day. The legend of Bryan Mayhem will forever cast a dark shadow upon my existence. A slip of the tongue, a slit of the throat. Words can be broken so can bones. Ashes to ashes, dust to dust. Whatever suits your darkside, the tale of Mayhem is still alive today.
