Rebirth (PT.1)

28 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/28/08 Death is a point in life where your spirit leaves the body to begin the after-life. But what does that mean to someone? Does it mean an eternity in hell, maybe it means an eternity in the eternal bliss of heaven, or maybe it just means an eternity walking the earth as a spirit? Whatever it may mean to you the fact still remains is that it's not the end but a new beginning. I go through life wondering what new beginning the people in my life are experiencing at this moment, no matter if it's the eternal bliss of heaven or the eternal damnation of hell I still wonder. But no matter what the case may be, I shall continue to live my life same way I have always lived it...in the shadows of evil that only I have created for myself, for this is my destiny...this is my Prophecy. 9:28 AM Monday, May 12, 2008 Riverside California, Galleria at Tyler Street. The mall is a breeding ground of delinquent teens shopping and hanging out sucking the life out of decent and respectable shoppers trying to acquire goods to better there lives. The different lights from the signs of the shops shine bright with alluring and colorful lights that seem to hypnotize the unexpected sheep drawing them inside to spend there hard earn money. One of the many stores in particular is much not like the others, because unlike the hypnotizing lights this store had none. In fact this store is quite dark not as alluring as the others by far. The walls around the shop had a series of cracks within the foundation to the entrance then leading up towards the ceiling of the mall. The door leading into the store is lined not of wood or metal but that of leather, at least people passing by think its leather for it more looks like human skin. A hand is seen pushing the leathery exterior of the door creeping it open. The mysterious man slowly crept inside the shop, to the left inside of the shop and sat down leaning against the wall. The dust inside is heavy and thick, the mice scavengers were getting ready to travel to the outside and raid on the nearby food court for there afternoon meal. Spider webs on each of the ceiling corner were finely spun in random designs to catch unsuspecting prey. There is even an old, black and finely aged stove in the middle of the room. To the right of the stove is a table that had an old man with long silvery hair that flowed telling the tale of his age. The man's skin has many wrinkles like a map to where he has been and where he shall go. A long slender metallic housing held a cigarette that the man held in the left corner of his mouth. The smoke from the lit tobacco piece twisted and curled in the dust filled air, while in the air it started to take shape forming images from a past clouded in darkness and despair. The dim light semi illuminating the room revealed the mysterious man to be none other then John "The Prophet" Adams. He looked upon the old man in confusion and dismay before the old man finally spoke. "Don't you know Mr. Adams that it is not nice to stare?" "Then tell me why I'm here?" Adams responded to the old man. The stove opened up by a mysterious invisible force, red fiery flames spill out of the sides dancing to the corrupt air quality that is feeding it fuel. The familiar flames of insecurity, torture, and rage can be felt to Adams as his mind is once again opened in the place of eternal damnation, and the smell of sulfur fills his lungs once more from the hellish domain. The gigantic devilish creatures are seen on the unholy grounds with demonic whips of a greenish flame tint...they use the torture weapons to punish a crowd of souls headed to their final resting place due to their past sins. The crowd of souls is a humongous group that even tops the mightiest protest rallies in the world of the living...the destination they are being led to like sheep is a dark obsidian castle that is located in the center of this realm. Tiny black demons with small bat like wings are seen running and nipping at the feet of the condemned souls. "Why are you showing me this? I have paid for my sins already!" Adams yells hoping the old man can hear. A skeletal, demonic bat like creature is seen flying through the sulfuric acidic air. The creature is also seen breathing fire every where like a dragon. Mesmerizing sounds of drums can be heard as the souls march on to the castle, being wiped from the back by the flame induced whips of torture. "He calls for you Prophet, he needs your help to avenge the hell in mans heart!" The old man started to preach back to Adams. A symphony of pain and agony is filling the air of sulfur and demonic beats of a drum. Closer to the castle we find that the structure is actually made out of blackened burnt bodies...due to a closer inspection reveals that the bodies, even though mangled and burnt through of their flesh, they still show signs of life. The blood floods like a river down the steps of flesh and filled a moat of blood...that surrounded the twisted castle. "I will never follow Devilin, never again!" Adams responded to the familiar hellish vision of his brothers' domain. Electricity in the form of lightning dances across the red and blackened skies of hell. Their destination point is the tallest tower amongst the eight in the castle. The thunder that shortly followed after is unified with the mighty roars of the demonic sheep herders leading the souls to their final destination...Food for Adams brother. "It is not your brother that needs help, but that of one that he has prisoner!" The old man responds to Adams. The scene of hell melts away to reveal the night sky, the night is very black, dark and comforting in appearance. Every type of pain deserves a relief in life doesn't it? The pain of physical harm to the pain of emotional loss...yes the night is very comforting when it's the only relief in sight. That's when an old lonely grave stone that no one has visited in many years appeared in sight. "Are you showing me my death old man?" Adams said curiously. The tombstone is on a tall dirt hill, looking out into the ocean that served as a window to the comforting star filled sky. There were a few brown and much wilted vegetation around the tombstone...almost telling the story of the once beauty this area had. The water from the ocean sang a song of how it used to be and how it is now, as it crashed against the rocks below. "No Prophet, but the answer to your life!" The old man cryptically said back. The night sky turned black as the evil in man's heart as John moved closer, even the air is as stale and smelly as the ground around the gravesite started to crack and sulfuric smoke poured out from the bowels of hell. John "The Prophet" Adams stepped foot onto the hollowed ground slowly moving closer to the tomb. "Who is buried there Old man?" Adams asked in curiosity. Black crows started to gather as a warning to the prophetic man to stay away but from what? Even a wandering owl seemed to ask who as John Adams continued going closer. A wind blew howling to speak for the lost souls of the past warn Adams also to stay away. So much pain and anguish can be sensed here, but for what reason? Isn't it supposed to be a place of final rest? "Someone from your past Adams, someone who is trapped and needs your help to be set free!" The old man said in response to Adams. The path leads Adams to the hill, on top of the hill is a large black dead willow tree...the dead branches is covering a large but lonely tomb stone. As Adams got closer to the tomb stone, dark clouds started to blanket the entire sky...closer he moves in, step by step missing the cracks in the earths crust. And with each step he takes, the sounds of thunder echoes in the sky. "No one needs my help old man, my soul is cursed and so is everyone else who ever come around me." John says in a depressed tone. A crow can be seen pecking away at the loose rock that used to be part of the tombstone starting a small pile near the corner of the tomb. As Adams continues to move closer lightning can be seen dancing wildly illuminating the way for Adams. ""Look upon the man's name Adams, he is the key!" The old man says to Adams telling him to look upon the one who needs his help. The words on the tombstone can not be seen by the black of night except from the random dancing of the lightning, "Here Lies Jack, killed from the friendship of a man that he once called friend." 10:00 AM Monday, May 12, 2008 Riverside California, Galleria at Tyler Street. Adams awakes from a slumber sitting down from a bench inside the mall. With his right hand he then proceeded to wipe the rest of the sleep from his eyes. The random people passing by the bench look upon Adams as if he is a bum asking for a hand out. "Was that all a dream?" Adams wondered to himself as he gets off the bench and stretches raising his arms up high to the heavens above. Could it be that Jack Jefferys, otherwise known as "Ace of Spades" in EPW be communicating with Adams from beyond the grave? Could be, after all his spirit is entrapped inside the demonic vessel of Devilin Rubis. Maybe it's time to finally put the wrong things right... "Maybe it's time to go and travel back to the depths of hell?" Adams thought to himself as he started to walk outside the mall doors. Good versus evil, light versus darkness...what does it mean? The eternal battle has been a war with no real winners or even real answers. I have walked the path of light with plenty of pain...I have walked the path of darkness and the pain is still there...except that the darkness seems to be more inviting, more comforting in ones hour of need. Comfort...that is one thing I have truly missed in my life, the comfort of my mother...even the comfort of my lover...now all I have is the darkness, the darkness is my own reality and there I shall stay! ? John Adams To be continued....
