For Which I Remain

16 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/16/08 For this in which I remain, I shall be thy Almighty's justice I grapple with the injustice in this world that my Lord has desperately tried to correct. I see the eyes of the innocent as they look for my help, but I cannot see myself sacrificing my body for these people that commit just as much sin as the offenders I force to repent. I try mercilessly to get rid of the urges to go after them, yet the Almighty instills a power in me that is impossible to break. A shadow appears down the sidewalk, stretching and shrinking with the exchange of light emanating from the street lamps. The shadow stops at the corner of the street. The shadow flickers with the dancing flames in a barrel in the nearby ally. "Do you have a cigarette?" Zacharia Taylor looks over at the ragged man and starts to speak in a low voice. "I shall not feed your desires, sir. You shall be..." "Ah whatever man. I just wanted a God damned cigarette." The murmurs of the man fade into the darkness. Zacharia stands, staring at the cognitive image of what remains of the guy. He blesses himself in a passionate way and continues to walk down the sidewalk. After walking down several blocks, He stops and looks across the street at the ever familiar park. The cuts on his body still ache from the events prior. The burning heat around his scars force his hands to caress the outlines of the scar. He takes a step across the street, only to be forced across by the oncoming racing car. Once across, he turns around to face the car, but the car comes to a screeching halt beside the curb. Zacharia takes a few steps back when all the doors fly open. He drops his bag on the ground and runs through the park. "There he is! Get ?em boys!" The rabid clashes of rubber on pavement resonates through the empty streets and park. Sweat escapes over Taylor's brow and falls in beads down his face. "Why run asshole!" If you only knew... A stiff tension extends from Taylor's core, down into his legs, slowing down his pace to a catchable speed. The guys that are chasing after him steadily reduce the distance between them until Taylor is within arms reach. Taylor stops and stands tall, taking off his hood and pulling off his sweatshirt. The scars glisten in the dull moonlight. The silhouette of Taylor is just as intimidating as the man in person. They circle him. "In the name of Jesus Christ, you shall be given a chance to leave, to escape an ill fate." "This asshole thinks he's got a shot. Get his necklace!" "What a sick ass cross, get that shit!" Taylor stands taller, almost arching his back. His adrenalin giving him all the strength he needs to stand his own, but a divine strength will prevent him from faltering tonight. "I will give you guys one more shot. You can either walk away, or face the consequences of sin." The men erupt in laughter. Taylor eyes down his targets, sizing them up, making decisions on who will fall first, last, and suffer the blunt blows of his fists. "This mother fucker thinks he has a chance?" "Fuck this bitch up!" All four men close in on Taylor, but the man in front of Taylor, the driver, suffers the first blow. His fists trigger a bash under his right eye, and a blood flow to start out of the corner of the other eye. The second guy to charge is the man on the right. He grabs Taylor's arm and pulls back, only to be catapulted over Taylor's shoulder and onto the asphalt of the sidewalk. His foot finds the man's nose and crushes it as Taylor lunges off his right foot and in the direction of the guy that was behind Taylor. He brings his elbow up high and nails the guy in the eye, cracking his eye socket. As the man fell to the ground, screaming in pain, the last guy kicked his back down to the ground. As he crashes against the ground, Taylor used the opportunity to catch his breath. Rising back to his feet, he straightens up to intimidate the last man standing. The last guy charges at him, arm extended but misses, tripping over his feet. As the guy crashes to the ground, Taylor walks back towards the street. Once he reaches the street, he hears footsteps behind him. Seeing the flashing blue and red lights by the abandoned car, he is not concerned. He reaches down and picks up his bag, and heads towards the car. Once the police officer comes into view... "Sir, is this your car?" The guy charged up and hit Taylor over the head with a small branch. With the momentum of the guy, and stepping forward, he falls towards the car. The police officer grabs his gun and pulls it on the guy. As the guy charges the police officer, he walks away from the scene. *BANG* Taylor keeps walking away as he hears panicking yells and gasps from the police officer... "Oh my God. Why did you... Oh my God. Why...." Taylor puts up his hood, takes out his cross and blesses himself. Walking away he starts to pray to himself.... For which I remain....
