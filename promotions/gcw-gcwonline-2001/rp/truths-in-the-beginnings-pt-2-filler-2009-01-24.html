Truths in the Beginnings PT. 2 (Filler)

24 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/24/09 Why would we suddenly go to southern California and begin again in an area where we seek truths again. A place where many people have had crossroads in their lives and found direction to their futures. Asking questions everyday and dreaming as to where they are going. One such man has returned here to continue the education and understanding of the past that had built him and eluded others. He has done it all yet there is somethign he seeks this time. Will he find it in his past? Shaman is seen sitting at a bar flipping through the channels on an old dusty tv by remote control. "Hank you know you still have not replaced that Television.?" The bartender turns around and looks as he simply laughs as he wipes a glass and places it on a shelf. "Why? If it works and isn't broken then no need to change it." Shaman taps the remote in his hand and then looks up at the tv. "That is precisely the reason Hank. Things change and with the new HDTV deal coming that television will need to change or in the very least conform to whats coming." Hank smiles as he places the glass on the shelf and reaches down into the sink and lifts another as he begins wiping it off. "Then i by a converter box and place it behind the television. Keeping the world the same and going with the flow. Change is best, but rolling in the right direction. Change is inevitable buddy. Either you embrace it or stick to the same old boring thing that has kept you going all this time. You are living proof of change, but yet you don't see it yourself. Go outback and view the change yourself have experienced. I decided to keep soemthing of your past." Shaman stands up and looks at hank as he walks out the back door. He looks around and sees a vehicle covered in a dirty tarp. He walks over and pulls it off revealing a faded white and baby blue Volkswagen Bus. Shaman laughs as he walks around it checking it out. "I thought this damn thing was in the scrap heap somewhere for sure." Hank walks up behind him wiping his hands on his apron as he smirks. "Somethings are needed to remind people who they were and where they come from. I knew eventually you'd be soul searching. Coming back to your roots is the best way." Shaman looks at Hank and nods. "Just what i needed really. I need this to continue my path down to investigating and suggesting the paths to building what and who we are." Shaman climbs into the Volkswagen bus. Grinning as he looks at the bus as Hank turns and walks back into the bar. Shaman runs his hand over the dash as he remembers certain events and times. "The great thing about remembering the past is that you can revisit it anytime you wish. The sensations, the people, the feel of the wind in your hair. These are small things that you remember before a great match or the way people react within the crowd. If you remember i left you guys last time with my first match. Perhaps i can describe it better to you in detail as i sit here and remember what it was like to be there." Shaman begins to speak about the recollections of the days up to his first match. The training the excitement, the nervousness, even the puking just before the show. He rambles on a bit with some stuff that nobody would really listen to much until he comes to a point and stops looking at the camera. "You listening?" He stares at the camera seriously. "I was young inexperienced. Broke my finger in that match on a botched call by my opponent. I learned he was trying to actually physically hurt me half way through when he actually stated so. He wanted me to know that i am new and would never go anywhere. Funny thing was i knocked his ass out later in the match with a chair when i realized i had to make him understand i was here to stay. First match was a doozy. The event coordinator stated he liked my attitude in wanting to be around the business, but would have to do as i was told. His way or the highway. I worked it for over six months and got tired of it quick. I moved on and went to Mexico and wrestled there as Snake Bite. Don't ask me why i just did it. It sounded cool and dangerous at the time." Shaman stands up and sliding out of the Volkswagen bus. He shuts the door and pulls the tarp back up over it until it reaches the front as he notices a couple of signatures on it. Guys he traveled with. He smiles and reopens the door grabbing a Sharpie off the dash and signs his name to it as well. "Leave the legacy behind for i am living it now." Shaman turns and walks back inside and walks along the wall of a lot of the wrestlers. Opponents and friends alike cover the walls here. Hank tends to be a collector of sorts. Shaman reaches up and pulls down a picture of him in the early years. It shows the first stable he was ever part of. The New Regulators in the WWA. "These were the years and Griffin was the first guy in the NCW to take me under his wing, But all the credit for me joining the NCW would have to go to Mark Harrington. He actually saw me wrestling in Florida under the CWC umbrella and brought me aboard. I took the offer and made my debut at Darkens Birthday Brawl. Went up against Steihl. I didn't do to bad considering it was my first time out." Shaman taps the picture and grins. "Anyways back to this. Griffin took me under his win in The New Regulators. Myself, Black Scorpion, Griffin, and Bob. We stood as the good guys in the NCW/WWA era. Always fighting for what was right and in the end we only had one other guy join and that was a kid named Lantern. I think Brittanica may have been a member for a short time, but that was brief. Anyways we fought for what was right. Kind of blind we were at the time." Reaching up Shaman rehangs the picture and looks at the years roll by as he walks to the last two years. "Then we get to corporate numbers and budget. The changing of all kinds of presidential guards and staff folks. new Talents come and go and at the time i was unwilling to flex and change. I Became WWA Heavyweight Champion again and get screwed because a referee cannot do anything right. So i fight with a guy and then got to fight with several others in a massive match where i take a nice little fall off a Jumbotron down onto wires and crap. was out for several weeks after that. Mal Somers gets his first WWA World title by actually earning it. Sorry Mal Somers, but recently i just saw a shadow of yourself as you had used to be. I felt you got lost in the corporations aspect of it all. Most of us at some point got lost in that corporate bubble." Shaman reaches up and grabs a picture with a magazine cover showing Shaman walking away as a WWA banner lies on the ground. "Shaman leaves the WWA the impossible has happened where will he go now. Those were a lot of the headlines rolling around magazine covers. I went elsewhere and re-established my self and goals again. I went to a place called GCW. Became Television Champion within a month or so of being there. Within a year afterwards i became US Champion there as well. Not once, but twice against several tough opponents. My partner and myself have established a lot of things as brothers in arms. Our goals were similar in nature, but i had some other unfinished business and saw my opportunity not too long before Bryan and myself became the GCW Tag Team Champions." Shaman grins as he lifts a different picture showing Shaman standing beneath a WWA banner at a show. "I returned to where i started showing myself on the worldly stage. Many friends and enemies along the way. The WWA reached out to me and i came back because i owed something to myself. Not the fans, not the staff, and not my friends who remained. I owed myself the opportunity to finish and do things i said i'd do. Most of the guys were gone whom i was looking for. Now i had realized alot. One of the first things i did was defeat Denrol. A guy who in the past had been a thorn in my side, but officially it was time to finish it for good and make sure he understood i had changed." Looking as he places the two pictures down. "The future holds a lot of things, but the biggest thing this future holds is Birthday Bash. Many more PPV's and many many more opponents. Do people actually think i forget who they are or what they have done. Am i a bit off kilter. Come step close to me and see. I am not insane i am the only normal one around. Then again what is normal, but a meaningless spread of words. You play the game with a guy who has played this game for a longtime. What game will begin next. You all shall see. It is calculated with militaristic precision." Shaman grins and looks at the pictures. "Now it is time for you to understand one thing. The past has met the present. Welcome to tomorrow and the future forever more. Once you realize these simple truths you shall know what it is like to be champion nevermore. Sleep well, but not too soundly for the sandman awaits you." Shaman leans back in the chair and looks around. "So expecting me to say GOT BLOOD? Could even say welcome to hell? Famine has spoken. Hell nothing like that, just a simple phrase." Shaman leans forward with a gleam in his eyes not seen in a longtime. "Just wait and see." Camera fades to black and goes to static as a odd laughter is heard.
