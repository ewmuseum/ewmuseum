Date: ????? -=While most of the world is experiencing intense heat, and violent weather, for the 40th conesecutive year Belmont, England is under the glow of the moon, and bitter bite of old man winter. The sun has not risen in 40 years, and the weather has not reached above freezing. No one really knows the reason for all this, but it does seem to be evident. Most of the towns folk have left Belmont seeking other areas to raise a family. Bryan Mayhem has not left. He sits on top of Old Hill in the largest and oldest house in England. The Mansion of former female serial killer, Elizabeth Bathory. Today however he's not in the dark comfines of the mansion. He's decided to take a little stroll.=- -=Belmont National Park, used to be known for it's abundance of apple tree's, is now a barren frozen tundra. Mayhem doesn't seem to mind. He slowly glides across the once crowded bike trail. Black trench coat down to his ankles, long hair blowing against his corpse painted face. His hands rest in his pockets.=- Mayhem:'The time of judgment, anarchy, and declaration of evil is creeping ever so close. For 4 years I've received letters from fans? or are they enemies? wondering where I've been. They all say they want to see the return of Mayhem, the man that has promised Oblivion. For 4 years I sat at home contemplating my next move.' -=He pulls his hands out, lights a cigarette and continues his stroll.=- Mayhem:'Rush Limbaugh once called me 'a disgrace to the world of sports entertainment' Fat boy kept telling everyone it's all acting, it's all fake, but when I enter the ring people really get hurt. -=small laugh=- That's because it's real chubby, it's all real. All the blood shed here in Belmont during the three matches sanctioned in my mansion. All three times Whether it be Caldera, Riggs, or any other fed president has had to sign waivers to air the match. Is it fake? No it's not. -=taking a hit of the cigarette=- As you see me now you are probably wondering what all this senseless Pyschobabble is all about. Well, you simple minded fools, it's me informing you all that Bryan Mayhem, the one branded The Lunatic, Satan, Cacao Daemon, has finally decided to return.' -=Mayhem stops dead in his tracks, and looks into the sky, and nods=- Mayhem:'It's time for the world to witness a new Mayhem. I'm darker, more sinister, and bent on revenge. I've been asked whether or not I'm what the sports entertainment world calls heel or face. Here's my answer to that. CALDERA....JHUB....KNIGHT....CAPO....DREAD....ROLLINS...TOXIC...TWISTED...STEVENS....but most importantly....OCTAVIAN....I have called out all of you. It's now time for me to make the largest impact ever in the GCW. Everyone watch over your back, because there's a Lunatic on the loose.' -=Mayhem's eyes almost seem to glow red, a blood red. His expression has changed from content to almost enraged, however he controls this rage from within. There is so much this world has to learn about The Lunatic.=- Mayhem:'Welcome to Oblivion...man is obsolete....erase....extinct...'
