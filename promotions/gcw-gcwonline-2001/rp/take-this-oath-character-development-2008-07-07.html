Take This Oath (Character Development)

7 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 7, 2008 A cigarette kept him company in the coffee shop, the only companion he could turn to. It had stuck by his side since day one and remained loyal through it all. No criticism from its lips, only the soothing smoke. Still, Jay sat alone. He could feel it. An emptiness filled his spine and bent him awkwardly across the table. There was no reason to be in a coffee shop. Not for him. He hated coffee. She, though, Caroline was different. She woke up to the foul smell of ground coffee beans each morning; it was her hangover remedy, morning breath relief, and overall comfort food. For her, he felt as if he could stomach the smell. As impatient as he was, waiting for her was worth it. And waiting is what he did. Fifteen minutes soon shifted to an hour. That hour transformed into an hour and a half. He opened a new pack of cigarettes to calm his needs and burned through four of them before she finally walked through the doors. There was no expression on her face, no smile for anyone inside the shop. Her eyes surveyed the scene until they found Jay packed in the corner of smoke. Her feet led her in. Still no emotion. Acting as courteous as possible, he stood from his seat and met her halfway. 'Caroline. Hey.' 'Hey.' 'Was hoping you'd make it.' 'I did.' 'Good. Good.' Fumbling for words, 'Come now, take a seat.' She did as asked and turned to the table. Elbows embraced the tabletop and supported her face. 'What's on your mind?' 'You know. I just. Well. I just wanted to apologize. I was upset. You know? Things were said and my mind was elsewhere. I messed up. I know that. I just don't want to end up loosing you or anything else.' 'You practice that?' 'Wha?' 'Jay, I'm not that easy. I gave you a warning. I thought you understood.' 'No, I do. I mean,' dragging at the filter, 'I was just out of it. Not myself.' 'No Jay, that is who you are. That's who you've become.' 'I haven't become anyone.' Exhale with no satisfaction. 'I'm still Jay Terror.' 'That's the problem.' 'How - ?' 'I want Jamison. Jamison Talley. I liked him. I liked the way he treated me. I liked the way he took me out places and didn't care how he looked or acted.' 'I don't care how I look. Come on. And I do take you out. Remember last month? We went to dinner.' 'Because there was no room service. That's hardly a romantic evening.' Another drag started to suggest he was starting a nervous habit. 'I'm just busy. You know that. I'm making us money for the time that we do get together.' 'You're not earning it the right way though.' 'Caroline. Please. I'm sorry for what I said and how I treated you. I'm sorry about how I made you feel. I'm sorry about it all. I just want to make things right. I swear.' A slight droplet of water stuck to her eyelash. A drop that she made quick work of. A secret lingered in her brain, plastered for eternity. She kept it to herself. 'Jay, I've given you chances. I've let you do what you want. How do I know that this time will be different? How can I know that you're going to love me like I love you?' 'Give me a chance at least. Let me try once more. And if that's it then that's it. I'll let you find the guy you deserve.' She considered the fact that he shot promos for his job and realized that everything he said could very well be bullshit. She understood the consequences of saying yes, yet still found a way of allowing him one last chance. Call it love or stupidity depending on your take on relationships. 'What do you have in mind?' 'Let me take you home and you can hang out for a little. I promise I'll be back with something special.' 'I can get home.' 'How did you get - ' 'Don't worry about it.' With a sigh and a last drag from his cigarette, 'Ok. I'll see you later. Yes?' 'Yes Jay. Later.' 'I love you.' 'And you.' She placed a false smile on and watched him leave the shop. Sitting for a second, she tapped her foot and shook her head at the decision. No matter how things went, a conclusion was bound to be reached. Shrugging, she stood and followed out the door. A black car rolled around to the front with a male driver behind the wheel. His hair was messy and his face unkempt. Through the window sight, Caroline watched Jay slide by in his Chrome BMW. She witnessed his eyes meeting those of Jordan White. No controversy, only the unlock of a door. She stepped inside and smiled at her driver, a man Jay surely would have something to speak about. __________ Fuck Jordan White. I swear I should devote an entire notebook to the fucker. I mean, I figured he was done fucking around. I figured he was under the impression that I didn't take keenly to him and Caroline "hanging out". Fuckin' bastard. Always playing the rat's role. Ugh. I can't tell her though. I can't show that emotion toward her. Not tonight. Not anymore. Fuck. I got the chance to redeem myself. The chance to show her that I'm not the guy she thinks I am. But fuck does this suck. That guy better hope I don't rip his head off his spine at Warpath. But shit, Caroline. How do I fix this? What do I do to save us? She likes flowers, Disney movies, recreational drugs, and the occational glass of hard liquor. I could give her alcohol and seduce her. She likes sex too. But shit. Like hell she'd drink with me. She's too smart. She's always been too smart for me. She did college. She knows the world. Me though, no way. Maybe I could get her flowers, roses even. Not enough. She needs more. Is it even possible to buy love? Do they sell it at drug stores? Maybe just drugs? No. I need to do more than that. Shit. Take her somewhere. Perfect. Yes. But where? Where in the hell? Somewhere romantic. I remember her saying that. I'll damn sure find it. __________ Romantic wasn't something that Chicago offered. Perhaps it was hidden in the shadows of the alleys but to Jay's eyes, it was invisible. A false hope. Still, it wouldn't be Jay if he came up short. Not Jay Terror one bit. Sitting on the clich? hillside overlooking the city, he waited with his long-time friend: a Marlboro 27. Thankfully, it wasn't long before the BMW rolled through the gravel. It took every bone in his body to sit tight and keep his lips shut. Somehow, he managed. Taking one of the longest drags he had ever done, he flicked the butt off the overhanging land and turned on his palms. 'Caroline. Baby. Hey!' 'Hey Jay.' 'Sit down. Over here. Please.' She took notice of the duel candles, one on Jay's side and one on hers. They lit the small portion of land leading her directly to her place. 'This looks nice.' 'Would you believe that Chicago isn't the most romantic city? I mean, it was a struggle finding this place.' 'Looks like a movie scene.' A smile encompassed her lips. A genuine smile. 'That was my aim. Looked kinda' nice, yeah?' 'Yeah. Pretty nice.' 'And the view,' he points at the city, 'look at those lights. You could touch them almost!' 'Almost.' 'You ok?' 'Yeah. Just, I don't know.' 'Did I do something wrong?' 'No,' a pause, a long pause actually, 'you're just confusing.' 'How could that be?' 'You do things like this every now and then. Not often. But every now and then you surprise me. Make me really smile and all. That's it.' 'I told you, I just needed this last chance. You thirsty?' 'A little. What's on the menu?' 'Jack Daniels? It's what we had on our first date. Remember?' 'I took you back to my place and wound up passing out before sex.' 'Something like that.' 'Pour me some. Yeah.' Jay grabbed a bottle from behind him and a glass as well. He filled it and placed it in her awaiting hands. Customary, he had a glass of his own. 'How was your day?' 'Not bad.' Prodding for details, he continued. 'Anything exciting happen?' 'Not really. No. Just met up with an old friend.' 'Yeah? Who's that?' Stumbling for a moment, a woman's name came to mind. 'Heidi. One of my college friends lives here apparently. Saw her in the mall.' 'She drive that BMW?' 'Yeah.' A silence fills the gap between the candles. Both knew what was going on, neither wanted to admit to it. Jay was first to speak again. 'You want to come to the show this week? Should be good.' 'Maybe. At ringside you mean?' 'Yeah. If you want. I mean, it's ok if - ' 'I'll go. Sure. Might as well. You wrestling?' 'Maybe. I'm not sure yet. Depends on what Steve has in his head. We haven't spoke for a few days.' 'Yeah? I didn't - ' A vibration comes from Jay's pocket; his female ringtone comes to life. Smiling, he fiddles around for it. One look at the nameplate calls for automatic ignoring. He clicks it off and shoves it back in its place. 'What were you saying?' 'Who was that?' 'No one. Just someone looking for an interview I bet.' 'A girl?' 'The ringtone?' 'Yeah. I know your phone well enough.' 'Don't worry about it. It's no one.' 'It's not the girl with the pink lace panties?' 'Wait, wha?' She pauses a second, the secret is already leaked. Sighing, she knows that the date is over. Caroline takes a deep breath and begins again. 'From the other day. You know. The one who left her underwear on the floor.' 'I don't know what you're - ' 'Jay. I saw you and her on the porch. She was as naked as I am clothed now. Don't play that game.' 'Caroline. No. It wasn't - you knew about this?' 'I knew. Yeah.' 'And you still - ' 'I figured maybe you'd be charming and make me change my mind. You were doing so well too.' 'Caroline, come on.' He stops, takes a second to gather himself, and shakes his head. 'I was drunk. Real drunk. And she wouldn't stop hitting on me. Baby, come on.' 'Drunk? Good excuse.' She begins to stand but is pulled back down by Jay's strength. 'Jay!' 'Caroline. Stay. Please. Let me explain.' 'I gave you your chance. I let you try. Maybe you'll have better luck with that new girl.' 'Caroline!' Jay stands with her but she doesn't pay attention to him. Her back turns to him and he watches her sway back and forth away from him. 'Stop this baby!' He picks up his pace and stalks her from behind. With his fingers, he spins her around to face him. 'Don't go!' She plants her palm into his cheek and sends him reeling backwards. 'Don't touch me Jay. I'm leaving.' He grabs her again, this time to keep her from walking forward. 'Caroline. I'm not letting you.' She turns and slaps him again. 'Get the fuck off Jay!' From behind them both, the BMW rolls onto the grass and the window rolls down. A pair of menacing eyes look out of the sports car and focus on Jay Terror. He releases Caroline and she quickly hops inside. Terror, making his way for the driver, winds up being splattered by dirt as the car turns around and drives off. 'Fuck you Jordan! Fuck you Caroline!' __________ Fuck the nice guy.
