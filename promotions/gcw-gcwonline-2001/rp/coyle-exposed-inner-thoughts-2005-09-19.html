Coyle, Exposed (Inner Thoughts)

19 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/19/05 Despite me being Irish, not many people know who I truly am. Fans, neighbors, acquaintances; no one has any idea of what makes me’me. Consider me “unknown” to the public, only a questionable figure amongst the rest. Does it bother me though? Nah, I can’t say that it does. In fact, it helps me- and the family. Contrary to belief, I’m not the innocent good guy that I play in the GCW. Instead, I’m the heir to a whole crime family. An Irish Mob, if you will, that’s been around for generations. Now then, I wouldn’t say I’m active within the crime family, but I am, whether I like it or not, the son of the head honcho, Patrick O’Conner. You can say this apple fell far from the tree, but then you’d only be half right. Throughout the years that I have lived, I have grew up with the sons of members of the mob. It has been around me in everyway, and my dad being the top dog pretty much makes it inevitable that’ll I’ll be in the business someday too. However, I do wrestle professionally, and this is because I want to get away from that scene before I start. It’s something that I just don’t want to do, yet, it’s something that I still want in my grasp. I could never let people know the real me; that I am the Coyle of Patrick, it would make everything a huge mess. Besides, being a professional athlete takes heat off from the family’s name, but just imagine if they knew what I was part of; then the family would blow up and be more popular than Lucky Charms. This is a secret that I have had to live with during all of my wrestling years, both amateur and professional. Not letting anyone know of who I really am actually helps my self esteem too. Think about it, I beat my opponents because I had the talent to, not because they were afraid of who I was and what could happen to them if they won. It makes me feel better in the morning. It’s basically become a habit to act as if I am so alone. Over the years, I have gotten so tricky and so sneaky; I have gotten so experienced about lying. It’s me. I think I probably deserve a pat on my back too. And for holding this secret, I have defeated X, or one of them, and Angel. And, it’ll continue on with Donny Diamond and so forth. Well, I guess it’s safe to say that I have accepted such a responsibility, but only for it to save myself and the family. Not one regret comes from it, and it makes me breath easier, though it may be a bit ironic. So as I sit here in my ol? little house, on a kitchen stool just past the stove, I smoke my cigarettes alone, and I play myself out as a loner. I could just imagine how much of a loser I look like to the neighbors; doing deeds by myself, never bringing anyone over, associating with none of them. But like I said, it doesn’t bother me. It’s me. However, I hate always thinking about this, so come on Coyle, stop, it’s time to go workout. It’s time to bring my secret-ass to the living room (weight room), start my day off with some squats, then continue on with those sneaky jogs. I have a match to get ready for; it’s time to step it up. Besides, we can’t have another one of those matches much like last week, we can’t make losing a habit. It’s time to go get better. Perhaps I should bring someone home to myself though, I must admit, it is quite lonely just living everyday without someone to cuddle with, hell, talk to, hell, even say Hi to. I mean, becoming social won’t hurt anyone, what is there to lose? Ah, who am I kidding, I’ll fold under pressure. I need to go workout, I feel the legs getting weaker by the minute. Come on you gangster motherfucker.
