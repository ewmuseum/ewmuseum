Alicia

Date: RAMPAGE~! "What the hell am I even doing here?" comes the thought. Aside from the fact that it's a Starbucks, there are other reasons why Markus is questioning his decision. All of those reasons point to one person. A woman named Alicia. Only those who've followed Markus since his DWO days will have any idea who this woman could possibly be. Even then, many would only be scratching the surface of what their relationship truly was. Cliff's notes: Markus began dating Alicia shortly after one of his many recurring knee injuries. Alicia was then employed as a pharmacist, and Markus took advantage of that to maintain his medicine cabinet. This relationship ended abruptly when Markus left DWO and moved from Cincinnati to the northwest. They have had no contact since. Until he received an e-mail from her yesterday. All it asked was that he come to this Starbucks, at this time, on this day. And now she's fucking late. Markus stares down at his coffee, deep in a world of his own. In the back of his mind, the knowledge that he is sitting here waiting on a woman he walked out on is nagging away. He's not thinking about her though, not intentionally. He's not thinking about the stares he got when he asked for a plain black coffee. Markus isn't thinking about how this Starbucks looks exactly the same as any other one with it's lightly colored chairs and tables, light floors and walls, dark counters and ceiling, with green accents every which way. He's not examining the same yuppie crowd that occupies this and every other Starbucks in existence. He's only thinking about one thing. Lindsay Troy. Vivica J. Valentine. Rampage, and the GCW Tag Team Titles. Markus is thinking about Shockwave, and the fact that himself, Terror, and Storm lost to a bunch of women. The fact that the next week on Worldwide, four members of the Establishment lost to three women. These thoughts are upsetting to say the least, but Markus takes comfort in the fact that he was able to beat Troy in a one on one match. The list of people capable of making that brag is short and dignified, and now includes the Man Formerly Known as Zero. Markus takes comfort in the fact that when it comes to title defenses, Chris and him have only lost once. And that loss was due to nefarious means, no less. Now that him and Chris are on the other side of the fence, with the bosses backing, he doesn't have to worry about "too far" any longer. Markus is so caught up thinking about Rampage, he fails to notice that Alicia is standing across the table from him. Blonde hair pulled back into a bun, bangs framing her face, she clears her throat. Markus casually looks up, noticing her. He quickly takes in the view before him. Her black polo shirt covered by a grey zip up hoodie, blue denim jeans and white tennis shoes. Casually dressed as always. Definitely one of the first things that attracted him to her. Second only to her smile. The smile that he's staring at right now. The smile that fits a face perfectly, making every other feature more attractive every time it comes out of hiding. "Take a seat," he gestures at the chair she already has a hand resting on. Alicia sets her purse on the table, followed by her coffee, probably some triple caramel macchiato or some other idiotic drink. Taking her seat, Alicia stares across the table at Markus, a pleasant smile still on her face. Just as Markus prepares to break the silence, she finally begins talking. “How’ve you been Mark?” comes the first question. Her voice, light and soft, surprises him. Markus forgot how it sounded. He forgot what it was like hearing somebody call him Mark; besides her, only his parents ever called him Mark. “Good, I’ve been really good,” he answers after a bit of thought. “What about you?” “Oh, you know. Paying the bills, right? Although with the economy these days, I’m just glad I’m able to do that. I feel pretty bad for a lot of people who can’t,” she answers back with a reply much longer than he expected. “Always the bleeding heart.” “Well, somebody has to be,” she shoots back, matching his sarcasm with a bit of venom. Markus smiles at her, and nods in acceptance. Nobody could ever say she didn’t care about others. He was living proof of that, and it was a fact they both know he took advantage of. “And you’ve certainly done your share of taking care of others,” Markus says, almost trailing the words off as he look back down at his coffee. Markus feels regret building inside of him. Strangely, he doesn’t know whether to welcome it or shove it back down. Quickly, he decides, and looks back up with the familiar cocky grin. “Long way from home, aren’t we?” Alicia certainly notices the quick change, but decides against pressing the subject so soon. Instead, she goes along with his game. “Medical conference. A group of guys think maybe they’ve made another revolutionary weight loss medicine.” “Haven’t people learned how to eat less and exercise yet?” Markus asks her, as the two share a laugh. “Apparently not. You would think with all the commercials and ads about it, somebody would get a clue, right?” she adds between laughter. “You know, you still haven’t learned how to dress up,” teases Markus. Alicia glances down at her outfit and shrugs. “Yeah well... What’s wrong with this, any way?” “Well, there’s no leg or cleavage showing, for starters,” Markus points at her chest and winks. “Oh yeah? And you haven’t learned how to get a real haircut yet,” she says pointing at his shaved head. “Point.” Markus takes a sip of his coffee, and Alicia takes the chance to do the same. Markus looks outside, to the street and sidewalk, watching the populace travel by the store as she watches him. Both of them are able to notice the growing silence, and the awkwardness that comes with it. Markus chooses to pay it no mind, and Alicia racks her mind for a new topic. Anything besides wrestling to talk about. “So, still wrestling?” she asks, failing on that goal. Markus turns and looks back at her, smiling. “What else would I do?” he answers, noticing the discomfort this is causing her. “Is your leg still...” begins the question, before Alicia lets it fade. The two of them know fully well how upset he used to become when this topic would arise. “No, not at all. The time I took off helped a lot. Feels better than ever, in fact,” Markus smiles at the surprised look on her face. “Really?” she asks, more excited than she intended. She decides to roll with it, though. “That’s great Mark!” She almost gets out of the seat to give Markus a hug, but is able to restrain herself. Instead Alicia just smiles across the table at him, genuinely happy for Markus. “That’s really good to hear.” “Thanks Ali,” Markus returns the smile. This isn’t going how I thought it would, Markus thinks to himself. He expected anger, spite, and questions. Instead he’s getting a civilized conversation from a woman that he used and left. What the fuck world am I living in? “What was that?” she asks. Markus realizes he must have said the last thought aloud. “Nothing. Nothing at all.” Another silence. Another drink of coffee. This time Markus stares back at Alicia, wondering if perhaps there’s something more to her being here. Who in this world would have a good enough heart to truly forgive and forget? To call up somebody who snubbed them, and have a casual coffee with? What angle is she really going after? These feelings are welcomed by a growing sense of anger and paranoia. “Why did you want to see me?” he asks, masking the accusation he’s trying to make with a well placed smile. “Oh, well, you know,” she begins with uncertainty. “Just seeing how you are. How you’ve been. It’s been a long time since we talked, you know.” “Yeah, I know,” Markus thinks back to the last conversation. “Last time we talked, you told me you wouldn’t be my ‘dealer’ anymore. Said that I was killing myself, and you weren’t going to help.” There’s no hiding the accusation this time. And the glare he gives Alicia doesn’t help. Instead of backing down, like she used to, she meets his gaze. “And look at you now, all better.” “And then you come calling. For coffee? Who does that?” Markus asks her, leaning forward and folding his arms across the table. “Who calls an ex years after they’ve spoken? ‘Hey, let’s catch up, I’m really interested in how life’s treated you.’” She slumps in her seat slightly, taken back the sudden shift. “I just...... God, why do you have to be like this? Maybe I thought I could forgive you for all your bullshit. Maybe I thought that I could talk to you again, and just see how the cards fell. I’m sorry for thinking that maybe things could be different this time.” “This time?” Markus lays the sarcasm on thick with that one. “Are you serious? You came all the way out here to see if I would let you back into my life? After all the shit you put ME through? All the tantrums and fits you’d always throw? Really? Wow. Just... Wow.” Shaking his head, Markus takes one last drink of his coffee and stands up to leave. As he walks past, Alicia’s hand on his forearm stops him momentarily. She doesn’t look up at him, “You mean... You don’t have any feelings for me anymore?” Markus casually moves his arm away from her hand, and waits as she gazes up at him. “Alicia, I never had any feelings for you.” With that, Markus exits the Starbucks and begins the walk to his car. Opening the door, he gets into the driver’s seat and glances back inside Starbucks. Alicia is still sitting at the table, staring down at her drink. Her yuppie triple caramel macchiato. The drive home seems to take much longer than it rightfully should, as Markus' mind travels from subject to subject. Somehow each line of thought is interrupted, and brought back to his plain black coffee. His plain black coffee, and the blonde woman with the jeans, polo, and hoodie. Of course this happens right now, Markus thinks. Two days before Rampage, and she has to come along and fuck with his head. “You mean... You don‘t have any feelings for me anymore?” “Alicia, I never had any feelings for you.” Talk about a mind fuck. The woman of his dreams, comes waltzing into a goddamned coffee shop, forgiving him for his every transgression and expects him to live with that? Of course, she never really understood that part of him. Nobody understands that part of him. Not Chris, not Tessa, and certainly not Alicia. The part of Markus haunted by his demons of the past. Nobody seems to grasp that those demons are the single most driving force behind Markus Stone. The force pushing him forward, ever onward, in an attempt to escape them. Without those demons, where the hell would he be right now? Some poor fuck working a nine to five day after day, hoping that he doesn’t get laid off by some greedy fuck worried about getting out of the red for a few more months. Well, at least he’s got one more now. One more mistake to cause regret. “You mean... You don‘t have any feelings for me anymore?” “Alicia, I never had any feelings for you.” One more instance of Markus Stone being unable to forgive himself, and digging a deeper hole of self-loathing because of it. And now, he’s got to defend his hard earned Tag Team Title with this shit going on in his mind. How the fuck is he supposed to react? Is he supposed to allow her to break past his armor and see the scared little boy he really is? Is he supposed to tell her that she was right all along. Ever since he left her he's been spiraling further downward. The only string keeping Markus up was Chris and Tessa. Now it's only Chris. How long before Markus finally falls? How long before that string snaps, and Markus plunges into the depths of himself? Into the deep, dark recesses where the monsters hide. Angel's don't trespass where there's no light. And Markus' batteries are slowly dying, light flickering more and more frequently. Maybe it won't be a flicker, maybe it'll be a supernova. One last flare, causing a sensual mixture of chaos and beauty to rain down on all those around him. Or maybe Markus will just do what he always does. Fuck everything up, and end up standing alone against forces he can't possibly overcome. “You mean... You don‘t have any feelings for me anymore?” “Alicia, I never had any feelings for you.” Of course, he has to lie to her. The only way that Markus can continue down this path of self-destruction is to do just that. "You don't have any feelings for me anymore?" "Of course Ali. I've always loved you. I'll never stop loving you." Robert Frost took the road less traveled. Markus Stone took the road most painful.
