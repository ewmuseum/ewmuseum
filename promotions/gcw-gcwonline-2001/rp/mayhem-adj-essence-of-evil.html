Date: Timeless 'Stop it Anton!' 'Shhhh. Just enjoy it. I'm almost done.' 'Goddamnit I can't breathe.' Silence...... 'Bitch! That hurt! You just bloody bit me!' WHAP WHAP WHAP! I saw it all. My mother naked and tied up. My father straddling her, swinging away with closed fists. Bones crunching with every impact. Dad see's me, stares deep into me. I knew the next step. I closed the door. I saw nothing. Nothing exactly. Never saw Ma again. 'Pissing the bed? I didn't raise a bedwetter. Damnit Jason!' The pounding was brutal. Pa was making him bleed. I kept hearing the thud of the contact. Pa's fists, Jason's face. I've seen enough of these beatings. Something finally snapped. I walked to the back of the barn, and grabbed the hayfork. I gripped it's wooden handle. I didn't rush. I knew Pa wouldn't leave a beating until it's finished. The opponent knocked out and bloody. I walked back slowly. Jason sprawled out on the ground, in a pool of his own blood. Pa's back was to me, still swinging hard with a fury. His breathing got heavy, Jason stopped moving. He was done. He stops. I clear my throat. He turns. Face to face. Father vs. Son Old vs New Blood vs Blood. 'Boy, you better put that fork down or you'll get the same as your pisspants brother just did.' Jason shovels the last pile of dirt onto the mound and spits. 'Serves you well.' Spits again. I grabbed Jason by his hair and yanked him towards me. Face to face, I smell his bad breathe.'Run.' 'What? Bryan you're scaring me.' 'Run and never come back! If you ever tell anyone what happened here, I will kill you. Good luck little brother. I love you.' I kissed my brother, and off he ran. I remember the phone call. Twenty years later. It changed my life. There he was, standing in the middle of the ring. Crowd roaring. JNightmare, the biggest name in EHWF, undefeated. 'Ladies and Gentlemen! The future of wrestling! BRYAN MAYHEM!!!' The lights. The crowd. The beginning. 'Corporate slime! That's all you are!' Jason had a way with words. There I stood in the middle of the ring, with the Agentz of Oblivion. JNightmare. Bryan Mayhem. Tobias Fang. The Almighty Kippo. The Grim Reaper. Five of the most dangerous men on earth, alligned for darkness. Fuck 'em. 'OH MY GOD!! Bryan Mayhem just snapped. He turned his back on his team, the team he formed. Look at the bodies sprawled about the ring, Such Carnage!' There I stood over a bloodied and beaten JNightmare. The man I MADE! ME! I DID IT!' Bloodied and beaten, like that very day. 'Eight months JNightmare held the EHWF title. The longest reign ever, has just come to a bloody end tonight at the hands of his very own brother.' 'The winner of this match and new EHWF Champion....Bryan Mayhem!' Only to be stripped of it the very next day by President Riggs. How's the arm? Still snapped in two? Hanging upside down, roped to the belltower, the Grim Reaper gurgles his own blood through his mask. He was just electrocuted, a door blown up into his face, and chokeslammed through a floor. The curse of Belmont has taken another victim, the infamous Grim Reaper. His last match EVER. I remember staring through the mask into his eyes. I put the man through hell..I remember saying Hanging upside down, roped to the belltower, a young and rising superstar begins to vomit blood. Syko stepped into Belmont with a lunatic, and lost his career. I remember staring into his swollen eyes. I put the man through hell...I remember saying Hanging upside down, roped to the belltower, a man they call Reed. Bleeding from his ears, he struggles to open his eyes. He never wrestled again. I put the man through hell...I remember saying 'In the mouth of madnes, the madman always conquers.' Here I am today. GCW TV Champion, ended yet another career. Ladder Match. Jason Fucking O'Neil has to ruin it. Stay away. I stand 150 yards from the House of Torture, my childhood home. The house of abuse and torture. The tombstone should say 'Here Lies Anton O'Neil.' But no one knows he's head. Right now im staring into the grave. It's dug up and empty. The empty grave my selfish brother covered with dirt many years ago. Now it's empty. I close my eyes, spread my arms, and fall into the grave. I never land, I just keep falling and falling and falling and falling..... Oblivion?
