Date: OCTABER Atken: I've been thinking about this for the longest time now and I've came to a conclusion. Dickwood: A conclusion you say? Atken: Why yes I do say! Dickwood: I really should be less shocked you reached a conclusion and more shocked you were doing any form of thinking. Atken: Most people would make a "fucking your wife" joke here, but not I. Mostly because she is a repulsive human being. Dickwood: For all the insulting you do about her, you'd have thought you at least met her once. Atken: I base all my opinions on people from a single photo. For example, I strongly believe VJ gives out quite easily. Dickwood: Didn't you already fail in that venture. Atken: She was clearly letting her river of blood flow that day, I'm sure I'd have better luck another day... unless she has some form of constant period. That'd be disturbing! I imagine your wife has that condition. Dickwood: There is no such condition. Atken: Yes there is. Dickwood: Says who? Atken: Me, a mere moment ago. Are you even paying attention to this conversation? Dickwood: I try not to, ever since that doctors visit anyway. He informed me that you are one of the few human sources of intellectual osmosis. In fact the only source. Atken: He doesn't sound like a real doctor at all. Dickwood: He isn't, it was a made up story with the purpose to insult you. Atken: No gimmick infringement please. Dickwood: I thought your gimmick was "Infuriatingly Clueless". Atken: No, that's my personality. Dickwood: So what conclusion did you reach, since I suppose you want me to ask. Atken: I want a pony! Dickwood: I was hoping you'd reach a wrestling based conclusion. Atken: I could kick everyone's ass on a pony! Dickwood: I think the pony would be classed as a foreign object. Atken: Non-sense, it would be classed as a faithful companion. Dickwood: When was the last time your researched wrestling's rules. Atken: Wrestling has rules? Dickwood: Apparantly. Atken: You know, this study needs more data and in-depth research. Also a pony. Dickwood: YOU ARE NOT GETTING A GOD DAMN PONY. Atken: You're right, I'll spent the money on whores instead. Dickwood: How about you spend the money paying your manager instead? Atken: What? Why would I do that? That's a silly idea! You're silly. Dickwood: Look, if you don't pay me, I'm walking. Atken: I'll walk my cock in your mouth. Dickwood: That was kinda gay. Atken: I'd say incredibly so. Plus if I WAS gay, it wouldn't be for you lardo. Dickwood: That's it, I'M WALKING. Atken: More like waddling, AM I RIGHT OR AM I RIGHT. Dickwood: I wish you'd die in a fire. Atken: I'd never step into a fire to die in it Dickikins. Dickwood: I was thinking less step and more push. Atken: You're a filthy communist murder, you are!
