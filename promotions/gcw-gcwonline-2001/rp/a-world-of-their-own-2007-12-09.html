A World of Their Own

9 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 9, 2007 __Start Looking at the two of them, there’s obviously a border in the middle. Two distinct categories are created and it’s easy to figure out how they weigh beside one another. On the left, there’s Jay Terror. Young, brash, and promising, he’s heard it all. Ask the fans and they’ll tell you he’s the next big thing. Ask him and he’ll tell you he’s just trying to snag some extra cash to light a couple more cigarettes. Nevertheless, he sits on the left; much like a democrat does, dictating life as it hits him in the face. He has no plan, he has no goals, everything is spur of the moment and if it doesn’t work then so what? There’s always another road to take if you fuck up. The kid is a fan favorite, hated by a few men in the royal family, and he knows it. From small town drug addict to top class wrestler, he’s on the trip of his life. Just across the way though, to the right, is a name that threatens the wellbeing of his stardom. He’s the driving force that is set on cutting off his income and leaving Jay in a wheel chair for the rest of his time alive. Evil? Perhaps. A World of Their Own Located ? The hotel room Dizzy. Happy. Lifeless. Smiling. Jay and Caroline, side by side, stared up at the typical hotel ceiling seeing things that most people would never get the chance of seeing. The ugly green paint wasn't just an outer layer of color, it was a whole other dimension with people, houses, and life crawling on every raised edge. They saw cities with their lights overlooking bays like lighthouses. They watched couples holding hands as they walked down boardwalks, married men and women going through a divorce, and senseless murder within the city walls. They saw absolutely everything on that fifteen foot by thirty foot ceiling. 'Look at that,' pointing senselessly, Caroline smiled, 'It's us. It has to be us.' 'I see it. Shit.' 'We're so small.' 'So very small.' 'And cute, we're really cute.' 'Yeah, we're adorable. Like perfect adorable too.' 'Yeah.' They continued to stare, pointing out landmarks on the plain ceiling feeling as happy as a person could humanly get. That lasted for about ten minutes though and soon after the meds kicked in. They both shut their eyes and drifted away to a world where dreams are the only reality possible. __________ The bull was charging and showed no signs off letting up making Jay readjust his stance and step to the immediate right. It snorted and growled as it missed its target by mere inches. All around them, a crowd had gathered and watched curiously. With red cape in hand, Jay turned to face the beast again. Their eyes met and for a second they stared at one another trying to calculate the other's next move. The bull slid its hoof in the dirt, aiming it horns at Jay's gut. Again, it sprinted at him and Jay waited for it to touch the swaying fabric. The last second came and Jay avoided it once more bringing a thunderous roar from the onlookers. Cockily, Jay smiled and turned again to the bull. Men in clown suits waved spears in its face and it tried to run them down too. 'Take this!' Cried a voice from behind Jay. Terror turned around and saw another clown handing him a spear of his own. Jay gripped it around the stick end and raised it into the air in triumph. The bull turned around to look at his target again but this time didn't bother waiting. His charge came full force and Jay gripped tighter around the wooden spear. 'Come on!' He yelled. The bull growled and looked to hit Jay again. A few feet away, Terror tossed the spear so it slid under the bull's rough skin creating a deep gash in its side. It moaned in pain and bucked wildly hardly missing Jay. The beast slid to a stop and breathed heavier. It took a second to run again but a look of determination was now in its eyes. Jay looked for another clown to toss him a spear but none of them offered it leaving Jay with only the cape. He turned and looked into the bulls eyes but this time found that the animal had a new move. It faked left and Jay stepped right. On the rebound, he reconfigured himself but not without getting a tear in his shirt and side. The horn ripped across the costume and dug into his hip drawing blood. Immediately, Jay grabbed at the wound and the people around them gasped at the contact. It was now that the reinforcements came in wielding spears and make-up. Three spears were sunk into its back and it shouted with every new incision. Another clown handed Jay a spear and he stood back up looking at the bull. Recomposed, Jay called him forth. The beast started his charge but was shaky on his way in. Jay waited for his moment and when it came he stabbed his weapon into its spinal column. The bull collapsed after a few steps and slid in the dirt creating a cloud of dust around it. 'You give up yet beast?' It didn't answer and instead laid there breathing heavily. The crowd was on their feet loving every second of it. As Jay celebrated in the center of the bullring, the bull itself began to find itself again. Painstakingly, it lifted its carcass from the ground and huffed loudly to silence everyone. Jay turned, ending the celebration, and looked at the rising animal again. With a smirk, he shook his head and raised his cape again. The beast turned around to face his challenger and gave one last snort. With everything it had left in it, it began its charge once more. Jay readied himself, waving the cape recklessly, and waited for the pass. The bull tried to strike but Jay was skilled enough that he misses again. On the run away, Jay grabbed the spear in its spine and rips it free from its flesh. 'Good night my friend.' As it runs away, Jay raises the weapon and aims it for a new spot. He releases the spear and it glides away digging a hole in the bull's back again. This time, the animal falls and slides with its eyes shut. Jay lifted his arms up, accepting the praise, and walked up to the bull. Checking his pulse, Jay realizes his victory is secured and kneels down beside it. He grabs the horns and lifts its face out of the dirt showing off his prize. Another loud eruption. After his smile, his costume and the bull itself fade away. Soon after, he follows until all that's left is blackness. __________ LeStatt Knight. The name alone has its own dictionary and the accomplishments won immediately come to mind. He's done it all, from world championships to tournaments and nearly every time he has come out on top. It's just how he works. The fans? Who needs them? When you have the most powerful group in wrestling at your side, you don't need a cheering section. He is a conqueror, a driving force, a mountain to be climbed, and most of all - an obstacle. The man on the right stands between the man on the left's dreams of stardom and there will be no tolerance for such a heinous act. Luckily for him, the challenge has yet to find him. There are other people standing in his way. __________ Waking up, Jay was confused. He could hear Caroline babbling about absolutely nothing whatsoever at his side and he could see that same ceiling which had held such a complex civilization. As he searched the green though, no trace of life remained. It was blank and so was his mind. 'And then I met this guy. I didn't like him that much. Not at all.' Jay couldn't say that he knew what was going on with Caroline but what he could do was make a fair guess. As he sat up, he looked across the room to the table. Two pills remained where three were a little while earlier solidifying his interpretation of the situation. Shaking his head, Jay looked over to her. She was still lying on her back and her shirt was off. 'You ok?' he asked. 'I like you better.' 'Then who?' 'The guy that I didn't like much.' 'I'd hope you would like me better.' 'Oh, I do.' There was something about her that the outlaw simply couldn't resist. Even with only three dates under their belt, the second one involving a full bottle of brandy, Jay could feel a new emotion brewing in his stomach. Perhaps he'd blocked it out for so long that he had forgotten the sensation? Maybe he didn't want to fully admit it yet? Nevertheless, he could feel a sense of longing beginning to take shape which had a warm kind of feeling to it. Lying down beside Caroline again, he wrapped his arm around her. 'Remind me not to do that again.' 'Sex?' 'We had sex?' 'It was amazing.' Jay shook his head and wondered if they'd ever have a night that at least one of them would remember the following morning. He placed his head on her shoulder and snuggled it up close to her. 'You hungry?' With a smile as wide as a cornfield, 'So hungry!' 'You trying to leave the hotel with me for a bit?' 'Not hard enough apparently.' He laughed to himself and lifted his body up from the mattress again. Grabbing her hands, he lifted Caroline from the mattress as well and watched her stagger. Barely catching her, Terror felt like rethinking his decision to go out. The thought only crossed his mind for a second though and after that second passed he turned away from her to fetch both of their jackets from the closet. 'Where are you going?' 'To get the jackets. That's all.' She smiled in a loopy sort of way and grinned a little. 'You feeling any better?' 'Wonderful. I had a dream.' 'Was it about me?' Jay shook his head and pulled the jackets free from their hangers, 'Not so much.' 'Us?' He walked over to Caroline, helped her get the sleeves on her arms, and adjusted the fit on her frame, 'I was a bull fighter.' 'What a vicious sport,' she paused, 'I love it.' 'I was good at it.' She tried to take a step but couldn't find any sort of balance. Jay leaned in, helped her stand vertically, and held her there. 'Did you kill the bull?' 'I did.' 'Very good. You're a natural.' He laughed and wrapped his arm around her shoulders. Caroline stepped at the same pace as him, minus the fact that her feet were sideways and crisscrossed. She was a riot and at that time Jay wished that he had a camera on him. 'Where to lunch?' Licking her lips, 'You can be lunch.' 'Nah, maybe another time.' 'Oh, right.' The hotel room's door shut behind them leaving an empty room and a table topped with Xanax behind. Jay and Caroline were off to lunch. __________ Tessa Windsor. Like the knot, she seems to be quite tangled in her own emotions leaving Jay with enough space to pass over her like nothing ever happened. Then again, she has history. Take a look at her credentials: cruiserweight titles, Intercontinental titles, and even television titles litter her resume leaving a shining trail of breadcrumbs. But since when have accomplishments mattered in a match with Jay Terror? The past is the past and due to the lack of perfection in the world, there is no way it has a chance of repeating itself. With that in mind, the field is level. On one side, standing at five feet and eleven inches, Jay Terror waits for his prey. On the other, Tessa stands at five feet and four inches hoping that she isn't caught. Since when do the prey get away from their predators though? __The End
