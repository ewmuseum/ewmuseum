Through Hate Filled Eyes

2 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-2-06 ?Why are we here?? Jayce asked, as he scanned the room for anyone who wasn't dangling a cigarette from their beer moistened lips. ?I asked you to help me, not to get my drunk.? Trellis shook his head as he motioned around the small hole in the wall he had found a few days before. ?This is the perfect place for you to learn tolerance, Jayce. In case you haven't noticed, there aren't that many people in here that are white.? Jayce took a quick glance, then noticed for the first time that Mario was right. Suddenly, his anger toward Mario for dragging him to some run down dump of a bar was fading, replaced by the sound of trumpets ringing in his ear; from the mariachi music blaring through the speakers of the jukebox in the far corner. ?Everything we've been working on is starting to sink in, kid.? Mario had a loose smile on his lips, it was almost a look of satisfaction. ?I doubt you would have even noticed if I hadn't pointed it out.? ?Eventually,? Jayce responded. ?Especially with this damn music. Who the hell listens to this shit?? One of the men standing not too far away happened to hear Jayce's comments, and doesn't look too happy about it. He walked up behind Jayce, then gave him a quick tap on the shoulder to garner his attention. ?Oye,? he said with a thick Mexican accent as Jayce turned his head to face him. ?What did joo said?? ?Excuse me,? came the reply from Jayce. ?Righ now,? the burly Mexican with a thick mustache that curled over his top lip. ?What did joo said about my cousin's sister's neighbor's dog's previous owner?? Jayce turned back to Mario, a puzzled look on his face. ?Did you put this beaner up to this?? ?Hey, who are joo calling a beaner, pinche gringo culero?? ?Okay, I don't even know what you said,? Jayce started, ?but it doesn't sound good at all.? ?Joo want we take this outside,? the Budweiser swigging desperado mumbled after downing another gulp of liquid courage. ?Look, I didn't come here to get into a fight,? Jayce pleaded for the man to have some common sense. ?and I doubt you came here to get your jaw broken. So let's just say that you taught me a lesson and I bought you a beer, and we'll call it even.? ?Do I look like a fool to joo?? ?I really don't want to answer that, man.? Jayce turned away from the man who was surely looking for a fight, but he knew the man was not just going to give up that easily. ?Seriously, just leave us alone.? The last thing Jayce saw, was Mario's eyes grow wide before everything went black. ~=~-~=~-~=~ The dream was always the same, and though he had no clue as to why he could not get the images out of his mind, he knew that the memory of that night would haunt him for the rest of his life. It was a surreal feeling to watch himself live through something he felt he had already made peace with and moved on from. Picture walking through a certain period of your life, staring down as you go through the same motions over and over again. It would be one thing for that to happen, but imagine seeing everything through your own eyes at that exact same moment. The idea of it all was mind boggling and ridiculous, but he couldn't get past it. He made his way through the crowd that had encircled the area, watching as a detective began to strap a bulletproof vest under his shirt. Things were getting out of hand, and he knew exactly how things would play out. With all the knowledge of the situation he possessed, he still could never figure out how to stop himself from getting involved. Maybe his reaction time was too slow, but as soon as he would see the detective he knew that he would be walking through the crowd only a few feet away. And there he was, Jayce Bradley, pushing himself through the crowd to stop the man that had taught him so much from committing suicide. ?Hey stop,? came the call of one of the officers guarding the barricade, but he did not stop. Jayce watched as he himself ran through the parking lot and grabbed hold of the older man, attempting to wrestle the gun from his hand as the little girl ran off toward the awaiting arms of her father. Then the sound of the gun going off echoed through his ears as Jayce fell to the floor with Braun slowly bleeding out in his arms. Then it was all over, except for a bright flash of blinding white light. ~=~-~=~-~=~ ?Jayce,? the voice called out to him from the darkness, a darkness caused by the fact that his eyes were still closed. ?You okay, kid?? Through blurred vision, as his eyes tried to focus in on the man inches away from his face, Jayce nodded his head. ?Yeah, what happened?? ?You got knocked the hell out, that's what happened.? ?Son of a-? ?Don't move,? Mario said, as he pushed Jayce back down on the ground. ?You got a nasty cut on your forehead, right where the bottle shattered.? ?Fucking spic,? Jayce mumbled as he pushed Trellis' arm away. ?Where the hell is he at, I'll kill the bastard.? ?Hey, calm down, kid.? ?Fuck that,? Jayce said as he staggered up to his feet, his eyes slowly adjusting to his surroundings. ?Son of a bitch doesn't know who I am.? ?Do you know who you are,? Mario asked, then watched as the question stopped Bradley in his tracks. ?What the hell is that supposed to mean?? ?Well, you're already following your gut reaction when something like this happens, instead of doing the right thing and letting it roll off your shoulder.? ?Let it roll off my shoulder,? Jayce asked as he turned back to stare at Mario with his eyes squinted slightly. ?The spic busted a beer bottle on my damn head and you want me to let it roll off my shoulder?? Blood trickled slowly down his forehead, small spider webs of the crimson syrup spreading out across his face. ?No, but I want you to look at it through untinted eyes, instead of the hate filled eyes of a racist.? The word racist was like a dagger to the heart of the one time Cruiserweight Champion. ?But I-? He then shook his head and walked back to where Mario had taken a seat. ?You're right.? ?No, I'm not.? Mario replied. ?You just don't see it yet. Now let's get out of here before the cops get here.? ?Your buddy caused quite a stir when the bouncers tried to drag him away.?
