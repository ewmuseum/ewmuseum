The Long Road: Getting Shit Straight

20 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20 Sept 08 I. Beads of sweat have formed on his forehead and almost immediately after their creation they take the short journey down his face, to his chin, then fall to the ground to end their short life span. His body is tired, weary, and his muscles ache but he refuses to give in. He can’t give in; even as the wooden staff slams into his back again and again and again. “Have you had enough?” The old man asks quietly. “You can’t break me.” Joaquin spins the weapon in his hands, showing off a bit and adjusts his body, this time driving the wood into Roberto’s chest with a loud smack. Roberto grunts as he lunges forward, gripping his bare chest. His back heaves up and down because of erratic breathing. “Look at me.” Joaquin whispers. “Now!” Slowly, Roberto brings his aching body back up, icy blues glaring from behind jet black hair greet Joaquin’s cold and emotionless gaze. The GCW superstar grinds his teeth together, trying to control his emotions ? to hide his anger. “Now do you yield?” “NO!” Joaquin swings again with all of his strength, the impact expected but never coming. Roberto extends his arms and catches the staff before it can cause even more damage. With a swift and powerful movement, Roberto snaps the staff in half and slides towards Joaquin, sweeping his legs and causing the old man to tumble backwards landing hard on his back. Although Roberto’s body is far worse for wear he manages to get to his feet before his mentor can do the same. A large hand wraps around the back of Joaquin’s head and yanks him up to his feet. “As I said ? you can’t break me.” “It’s not over.” Joaquin’s fist moves too fast, catching Roberto square on the nose causing blood to spurt out upon impact. A lightning quick kick to the stomach and a straight kick to the back of the knee later and Roberto is again on one knee at the mercy of the old man. “And you think you’re ready” You honestly believe that you have what it takes to do what needs to be done?” Joaquin says this with a snicker. Roberto fails to respond, his once lively eyes seemingly taking a far duller tone. Joaquin looks as if he expects an answer, some answer ? any answer. When he doesn’t receive anything, the old man shakes his head in disappointment. “Pathetic.” Roberto catches a roundhouse kick to the face ? All goes dark. II. Roberto’s eyes flutter open, he feels like he has slept for days. He tries to sit up in bed but his entire body is stiff with aches and pains, his muscles force him right back down in bed. “Fuck me.” “Fuck you over, maybe.” LeStatt smirks at his own comment. Roberto moves his gaze down at the foot of his hotel room bed and sees the back of Knight’s head as well as his right arm that’s handcuffed to the bed frame. “Very funny.” Roberto struggles to sit up again, this time he finds success. “That old bastard is tough eh?” “Nothing I can’t handle.” “Looks that way.” Well,” Roberto turns his body to allow his feet to slide off the bed and meet the warm carpet on the floor. “That’s why I have you.” “What?” Simple really,” Roberto stands to stretch before walking towards the bathroom, stopping to look directly down at his alter ego first. “You’re my release. Everyone needs a release.” Roberto lashes out, kicking Knight in the face. Roberto smiles. “I feel better already, thanks man.” Roberto winks down at the evil son of a bitch seated on the floor. LeStatt’s black eyes appear to glow red for a moment, anger swelling up inside of him. Roberto shakes his head with amusement before disappearing inside of the bathroom. “I’m going to really enjoy snapping you in half.” Only a few minutes pass before Roberto moves out of the bathroom. On his way back to his bed Roberto slaps LeStatt Knight across the face. “And I didn’t wash my hands.” Roberto smirks, finally getting some enjoyment out of this whole torturing a prisoner deal. Knight’s chest heaves up and down for a few moments until he can get himself composed again. “How long do you honestly believe this little game of yours is going to be played Roberto?” Knight shakes his head. “It’s getting real old, real fast.” “This will go on until I say it will ? probably until the day we both keel over and die. You’re dangerous Knight and you cannot be trusted.” “Which is both ironic and sad since you made me.” “Yeah well even the good Doctor Frankenstein accidentally made a monster.” Knight laughs out loud, probably not because he thought something was genuinely funny but more to irritate Roberto. “And you think I’m a monster” I think you’re more the monster than I am.” “And how do you come to that conclusion?” “I’ve never killed anyone.” A knot forms in Roberto’s throat, the thoughts of his late wife and daughter causing him to slide into a weakened state for an instant. Knight doesn’t wait for Roberto to counter or react, he is like a shark smelling blood. “The perfect storm of emotions created me. Anger, hatred, guilt and loneliness. I’m everything you need to be in your darkest hours, your weakest moments. I am you, only perfect. Yet you continue to lock me away like some disfigured child. I can do so much for your life. This is like finding the cure for cancer and hiding the formula.” “Not even close Knight. This is like destroying a deadly virus that could bring on the apocalypse.” Roberto swallows hard. “You’re a fucking parasite.” “Maybe I’m the answer to your prayers but you don’t want to admit that you’re pathetic.” Roberto breaths in deep. “Doesn’t matter really because I’m never letting you out.” LeStatt Knight smiles wide, a genuine but devious smile. “It’s inevitable.” “Keep believing that pal.” “You’re in control now but it doesn’t and won’t take much. I’ll find a way to open Pandora’s box then I’ll be free.” Knight’s smile fades. “It’s coming.” There’s a momentary silence in the room. “I’d worry about your headache first.” Roberto says. “Wh-? Knight knows he shouldn’t have even begun to ask the question but it’s already too late. Roberto plants his fist into the back of Knight’s head. Knock! Knock! Both of their heads turn towards the hotel room door. Another momentary silence. “Yay, guests.” Knight says sarcastically. “Be quiet.” Roberto walks to the door and looks out the peephole. “Shit.” He whispers. “Nosey reporters?” “Tessa.” “Oh shit, that piece of sweet ass” Let her in, we can have some fun with her.” “You stay quiet.” “Suuuuuure.” Roberto unlocks his door and swings it open, revealing Tessa Windsor. The petite beauty has a bewildered look on her face. “Who were you talking to?” “TV.” “Its not on.” Roberto quickly moves the topic of discussion. “You need something?” “Oh yeah.” Tessa walks into Roberto’s hotel room the same way she does his locker room ? without permission. “Wanted to talk to you about WorldWide.” “Sure.” He responds. Roberto shuts the door and quickly looks Tessa up and down. She’s wearing a short black dress that accentuates the god given curves she has and shows off her athletic looking legs. His eyes move over to Knight who smiles back knowing what’s going through Roberto’s mind. “Well you know I’m up against Murray.” “Damn, I bet she’s real fucking flexible.” LeStatt chimes in. Roberto fights back a smirk. “MmmHmm.” It all Roberto can get out. Tessa takes a seat at the edge of the bed, immediately crossing her legs with the top leg moving right in front of Knight’s face. “Well I figured we should think of something. With all that’s been going on between the two of you I know you probably want a piece of the action.” Again Roberto looks to where Knight would be if Tessa could actually see him too and catches Knight’s eyes looking Tessa’s leg up and down, even sticking his tongue out quickly as if he were ready to lick. Knight’s eyes look up to Roberto’s. “I KNOW you’ve thought about bending her over.” “Of course I have ? uh ? do.” Roberto shakes his head and moves to the bed taking a seat next to Tessa and where he cannot see Knight’s face. “Yeah I do Tess. That prick deserves what’ll come to him.” “Well good.” Tessa says. “Any ideas?” Knight shakes his handcuffed hand as if he were raising his hand in school. “I got one. Wear this muchacha out.” Roberto nods. “I’ve recently gotten a few good ideas on what to do, yeah.” “Well ?” “Well what?” “What’s the idea?” “OH!” Roberto shakes his head. Tessa chuckles. “Are you alright?” She asks. “Besides the massive erection he’s getting right now” Yeah he’s fine.” Knight yells out. “Roberto ? are you blushing?” Tessa asks, now a little concerned. “Nah, it’s just hot in here. Listen how about you let me get a shower and we’ll go somewhere to get a bite to eat and I’ll tell you everything I’ve got planned.” “Oh.Ok.” Roberto stands up and at the same time helping Tessa up to her feet and walks her towards the door. “Give me 15-20 minutes.” “Sure. Don’t keep me waiting long.” “I won’t Miss Windsor.” She smiles and walks out of the now open door. Roberto closes it behind her and turns, placing his back against the door. LeStatt claps for him. “Bravo, you had a fine lady on your bed and you rush her out the door.” He shakes his head. “God you DO need my help.” “Your talking privileges are now revoked.” Roberto moves towards the bathroom again but turns to Knight. No words come out at first. “Please, save your boring lectures.” The two look at each other. Roberto looks as if he’s going to say something else but doesn’t and just goes into the bathroom, shutting the door behind him. LeStatt puts his head back and lets out a long breath. “God I’m horny.”
