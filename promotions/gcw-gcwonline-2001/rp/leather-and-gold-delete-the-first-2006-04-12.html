Leather and Gold (Delete The First!)

12 April 2006

Date: 04/12 WarrMachine.net - 'The Emails Can Stop, I'm speaking out.' On December 17th, 2005 I returned to my place in New York city after being successfully eliminated from an HSW Tournament. Losing is a touchy subject with me, and suffice to say I was having a pretty crappy night. I'd expected to win and have to stay the night in Dallas, but my early exit allowed me to catch an earlier flight home. Upon entering the house I knew something was wrong. My long standing significant other Angela usually was at the Gym around this time, yet the shower was running. I slowly crept down the hall to the master, and only bedroom just as the water began to to stop running. I saw a man, on my bed. I recognized him at once as Angela's best friend, Nick. My worry lessened, figuring he'd just come over for a visit or something. That was until she came out of the shower in a robe and sat on his lap. She kissed him. I decided to make my presence known, but Nick decided to play the coward card. He sprung to his feet and high tailed it out the window and down the fire escape. She was shocked to see me. She apologized profusely. And then she dropped the bomb, a form of explanation I'm sure a lot of men, and perhaps women, in this business have had to suffer with. 'It's not you. It's your career'. Apparently being on the road 200 nights a year deters the common relationship. ********************* Dropping back onto the plush black leather sofa, he instantly felt all of his problems slip away. The Aches, the pains, and for a moment the crippling mental trauma he'd been grappling with the past few months. The grandfather clock in the back corner of the apartment bellowed one chime, preventing him from drifting off to sleep, and brushing the messy blue locks from his eyes, The Warr Machine sat up and gazed around the dwelling. Losing Angela had been the worst thing to happen thus far in his short existence. Worse than the time he broke his arm in the 2nd grade, worse than walking in on his father and a secretary while his mother was out of the country. After discovering the tryst one night in December he left this apartment and hadn't returned until now. The lights of the city shone through his large window, reflecting off the golden strap draped opposite Warr on a large armchair. As his eyelids ascended it was the first thing he'd seen. The object that he'd fought so hard to obtain, knowing it'd be the first hurdle on the track to recovery. The GCW Television Title. As he walked out of World Wide, for the first night in three months his thoughts weren't of her. Proving he could still handle his business in the ring had proven he wasn't worthless, an adjective he'd bestowed upon himself many times in recent months. It'd given him enough drive to pack up and leave the nearly condemned studio he'd been renting in Harlem and return to the larger and more welcoming walls that currently surrounded him. Pushing himself off the sofa he slowly paced towards the armchair where the belt sat, his tired blue eyes reflected in the light of whichever television show was on at 1 AM. Lifting the belt he returned to the couch, the spot he deemed would become his bed for the night. Draping the title over his shoulder he eyes dropped. He didn't fall asleep right away, but rather smirked as thoughts of the past consumed him. ********************* I'd never been dumped before. I mean at 23, I'd been with my fair share of women. None of them meant anything to me, until Angela. I suppose people are meant to suffer the first heartbreak while in high school, or earlier. Not when you're old enough to purchase alcohol. I, as many in the past have, formed a problem that luckily has been curbed. I had no warning that this 5 year relationship would end. There were no signs, we were happy. Seeing what I saw, gave me nightmares for nearly two months. My daily routine consisted of, Waking up. Drinking until I passed out. Waking up, Drinking until I passed out. Wash, rinse, repeat. I barely ate, slept, and my depression caused me to drop out of all wrestling commitments. I couldn't afford my apartment and was forced to move to a smaller, shabbier heap in Harlem. Thankfully I was fortunate enough to sign with GCW and return to my original place. Beforehand though, it was bad. I began waking up in the morning just to sit in my car outside of Angela's apartment. On Friday nights Nick would come over, and I'd force myself to watch as they skipped off in their blissful ignorance. One Night my good buddy Jack Daniels decided I ought to do something about it. I was drunk enough to barely clip him, as Nick crossed the street. Sprained shoulder or something like that. I ploughed into the side of an apartment building and completely damaged my car. That was the least of my problems. Being hauled off in the back of a police car was a far worse experience. ********************* Three Months Earlier.. 'Will the Defendant please rise.' The powerful baratone belonging to the Judge echoed throughout the courthouse. Jason Warr climbed to his feet, sweat forming on his forehead as he nervously tapped the wooden desk before him. 'Jury, Have you reached a verict?' A large woman in her mid 30's stood from her bench in the Jurors box. She cleared her throat loudly. 'We have, your honor.' Raising a slip of paper to her eyes she read. 'On the count of Driving under the influence of Alcohol, we the jury find the Defendant Jason Warr, Guilty'. The DUI charge wasn't what he was nervous about. It was the second that had him sweating in his seat, and constantly adjusting his tie. 'On the count of attempted manslaughter, we the jury find the Defendant Jason Warr.. Not Guilty'. Letting out a sigh of relief, Warr fell back into his seat. ********************* I'd tested the limits of the New York legal system. While I didn't stop drinking at the time, I'd altogether stopped visiting Angela's house. Instead, I'd go out with my friends every night looking for some cheap slut to help me forget. I found a bounty of them, but rather than make me feel better these women furthered me to where I am as I write this. A Distrusting Jackass. ********************* One Month Earlier.. Blonde Hair, Blue Eyes. Mammoth Rack. The Object of any straight male's affection. She sat in front of Warr, tongue somewhere near his tonesils as he attempted a near forensic investigation of her exposed cleavage. In an alcohol addled state, this scene had played out many times the past month his the bedroom of Warr. Different girl everytime of course. Lying to them about calling back, that was part of the fun. Hitting em' where they'd hit him. Where it hurt. After the deed had been done, the airheaded blonde had made the mistake of blurting out a choice piece of information that drove right into his Core. 'I hope my boyfriend doesn't find out about this. He'd totally FLIP.' She'd been given around thirty seconds to get dressed before Warr led her to the front door by her shoulder, opened it, placed his foot at the small of her back and shoved her onto the sidewalk. 'I hope he does find out, living with a whore such as yourself and not knowing? Uncool.' ********************* I sort of took it easy from that night on. ********************* Present.. For a second time the chimes of the grandfather clock pulled Warr from his slumber. Rather that one, he was roused by eight, each one louder than it's predecessor it seemed. He fought to ignore them, wanting nothing more than to get the pure sleep that he'd lost so much of lately. However, the Chimes where in cahoots with the morning sun, which shone through the window hitting his eyes. If that weren't enough the phone began to ring loudly, and giving up Warr rose to his feet and answered. “What?” he said in an annoyed tone. “Hey There Jason, this is Rebecca Gaines, call-” “Who?” he said, annoyed again by the woman's false cheeriness. “- Calling from the office of Ross Warrington.” Jason cringed at the mention of his fathers name. “Mr. Warrington requests your presence for breakfast this morning.” “Now? THIS Morning?” Jason exclaimed. Visiting his father wasn't one of his preferred hobbies. “Yes Sir! A vehicle will be along to collect you at 8:20 on the dot.” “It's 8, you're calling me to set up an appointment 20 Minutes from now?” “I Know, it was a strange request, but Mr. Warrington said you'd understand. Thank you! Goodbye.” “Bleh” was all Warr could muster as he slammed the phone down and stomped to the bathroom to get ready. ********************* The only thing that made me feel better about the pain I was feeling, wasn't the alcohol. It wasn't the slutty women I took back to my place every night. It was winning the GCW Television title. Besides the distrust, since my title Victory at World Wide I've been myself again. Winning the title proved I was still a human being, I was ALIVE. Not a fragment of a soul, withering in the corner. In boosted my spirits to the moon, and it's an experience I'd very much enjoy to relive. So it's fortunate I have that shot this week. Jayce Bradely, I respect him as a competitor. I respect what he did in GTT5, What he's done in the Total J cup. But you see, Jayce isn't in my position. Jayce's Universe isn't held together by leather and gold, as mine is. To lose at this point would halt the progress I've made. I can't let that happen. I've chosen death before dishonour, and when WorldWide rolls around, You'll see just how desperate I am to prove my worth. - Jason Warr ********************* If his father was one thing, it was punctual. Eyeing the grandather clock he waited, until it struck 8:20. He threw open the front door, just in time to see the large black limo coast up to the curb. He would have smiled, if not fighting back the urge to vomit at the sight of this man, a man he hadn't talked to in months. Stepping up to the car he opened the door, to see the smirking face of his father. 'I'm glad you could make it' he said, that ever present smile pronounced on his face. With an expression of complete disdain, Warr ducked into the car, knowing exactly what this meeting entailed. To Be Continued
