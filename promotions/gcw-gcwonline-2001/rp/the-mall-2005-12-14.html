The Mall

14 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec 14 The mall? The epicenter of capitalism. It’s the very definition of sugar coated greed that is cultivated by the media. If that wasn’t bad enough? it’s the holiday season. You see, you take that mall, that big fucking ball of commercialist joy and happiness, and you stab it in the ass with 500 CC’s of bovine testosterone. Yeah, the holidays are fucking crazy. Especially in the mall. People lower themselves to all time lows this time of the year? Do you recall frothy-mouthed mothers CRAWLING ON THE FLOOR to get the last Tickle-Me-Elmo? Serious? give the kid a fucking cardboard box, and let him suck your tits. It makes no difference to them. Anywho, we find our hero, Chris Kannon at the Madison, Wisconsin East Towne mall. There is a large display in the center of the main strip. Sherpa-loads of fake snow cover the happy-go-lucky landscape. Electronic reindeer line the outside of the little station, roped off by strings of Christmas lights. At the center of the display, sits jolly old Saint Nick. The generic mall Santa Claus is accompanied by a young blonde woman, about college-aged, dressed as an elf, clad in green. She mans the camera tripod and herds the kiddies in line to Santa. She also takes the 20 dollar bills from the parents for some cheap picture with the fat man. Chris stands in line, surrounded by whiny kids and impatient parents. Kannon: Different year? same old shit. The line slowly gets smaller, and Kannon nears the front of the line. Our hero glances at the young Ms. Elf? She’s a cute girl, not overly gorgeous? but damn it, this is Wisconsin we’re talking about! Suddenly, between pulling a screaming kid off Santy’s lap and giving some cracked out lady in tights and purple leg warmers her change, Ms. Elf makes eye contact with the gawking Chrissy-Poo. Immedaietly, Chris looks away, and at the ground. Kannon: Shit, man? awkward? A few minutes passed, and Chris made it to the front of the line. Ms. Elf smiles slightly as Chrissy steps forward. Ms. Elf: Aren’t you a little old to be seeing Santa? Kannon: You’re never too old to go see Santa? Besides there’s something I really want this year. Ms. Elf: Oh yeah? ? And what is that? Kannon: Now if I told you? I might not get it. It’s for Santa’s ears only. Ms. Elf: Well, alright? Ms. Elf sidestepped, and motioned at the newly formed lane? running straight to Santa. Chris smiled slighty at the cute Elf-schlady, and strolled on over to Mister. Claus. Santa: Well hello there, young man! Ho ho ho? Chrissy leapt into the air and plopped down on Santa’s lap. Kannon tossed his arms around Nicky-Poo. Santa: HO! Oof? Chris gives Santa puppy dog eyes and talks with an over exaggerated little id speech impediment. Kannon: Hellwo, Santa? Santa: ? and what is your name, little boy? Kannon: Cwitty-Poo? Santa: And have you been a good boy this year? Kannon: Of course, Santa? I fucked up Bwyan O’Niel and tooked his Telebision title! Santa: I see? Well? what would you like for Christmas? Kannon: Well? I was hoping I could get a present a little early this year? Santa: Oh yeah? What’s that? Chris speaks normally. Kannon: I want to win. Santa: Win what? Kannon: I want to beat LeStatt Knight. I know you don’t know who that is? but? It’s really important to me, man. I NEED this win against him? it’d elevate my career to heights I’ve never even imagined! Santa: Well? what can I do? Kannon: I don’t know? probably nothing. This is most likely the biggest match of my career. I’m going against a former World Champion, and the commercial poster boy of GCW. To tell you the truth, I’m nervous as hell? I just needed to get that off my chest. Santa: Being nervous before a big match is normal. I’ll tell you what? You have a problem when you’re NOT nervous. Kannon: Oh yeah? And how would you know that? Santa winked? Suddenly, Chris was very frightened? mostly because of the whole thing with him sitting on the lap of a winking fat guy wearing bright colors? but then Santa stealthily pulled down his white beard for just a moment. Kannon couldn’t believe his eyes. Kannon: BALLS MAHONEY?! What the hell!” XANTA CLAUS!” Balls Mahoney/Santa: Hey man? you know how it is with pro-wrestlers and cash? One Night Stand only fed me for a week. But look, Chris? I think I’ve got a little something for you? think of it as an early Christmas present. Kannon: What is it? Balls Mahoney reached around behind him and produced two large presents. He handed them to the wide eyed Chris Kannon, who quickly ripped the wrapping paper to shreads. Kannon: A? a? Folding chair! And? A can of gasoline! Oh, Xanta Claus, I WUB you! Chrissy then gave Balls a big hug. Kannon: Thanks Santa! Santa: Don’t mention it? and have a merry Christmas! Ho ho ho? Chris Kannon collected his presents , then hopped off his lap. Kannon: Oh? and I got you something too? Santa: Oh yeah? Where is it? Kannon: Just wait? ? ? ? Santa: What? Oh god? OH GOD! WHAT THE HELL HAVE YOU BEEN EATING!” DAMN! AND ON MY LAP, TOO! YOU ARE SICK! Kannon: AND TO ALL A GOOD NIGHT~! Chris Kannon dashed away into the night. Wait? Chris Kannon and Ms. Elf make out. THEN Chris Kannon dashed away into the night. The End.
