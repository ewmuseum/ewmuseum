The Adventures of Johnny Heroic Issue #423: Cold Blood

1 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/1/05 One of these days. I tell ya that one of these days, You'll come back... Memory serves to show the song just kind of trailed off into nothingness like that. And that singer is immortal. She's forever alive, though she's long since perished, because she's on something recorded. Someone knows of her, and she won't die as long as that's true. A beat up old record in my favorite bar still has her voice on it. And until not only the record is destroyed, but also anyone that has ever heard her voice is dead, and everyone that knows of them is dead, she will live on forever. Her words echo through time until they are completely and utterly severed at the source. She is an angel. Meanwhile, I'm on a mission to find some people. And no angel's going to be able to help me here. With one hand still clutching my broken side, the other one pushes open the door to Destine's manor. Yes, he owns a manor. Reason being is that Destine doesn't have a power, per se, as much as he has the power of money. If he did have some sort of supervillain power, it would be the ability to always have exact change. And I know that he and his guards, Fate and Fortune, wait inside. Most likely alone, because that's more dramatic. As for Fortune and Fate? Their powers are simple. They have the power to kill you. With guns, with knives, with explosives, or with no help at all. They make people dead. And that's something I'm going to have a problem with when I actually get to them. But we're literally going one step at a time here. 'Okay, Johnny...let's just think this through. You've gotten out of tougher situations than this. Actually...you haven't. You've never been without your powers before. You've been Johnny Heroic, superhero, your entire life. Literally, your entire life. Born from nothing into being a superhero with powers off the scale. And now you don't have your powers, and you're going up against your arch-rival and his two incredibly sexy...suptuously curvacious...so, so hot assassins. Destine's got a whole fortress to his disposal. But he's not using any of his resources, and I have no idea why. My best bet is that he and Fortune and Fate are waiting for me in his conference hall, as per the drama that Destine loves. And I'm going to walk right in there, as expected. Well...at least we'll all be expecting the same thing.' The architecture of Destine's manor is reflective of him. It's overly dramatic and superficial. He tried to design the place to look like a biblical tower. It's got spires and columns built into the wall. He's got paintings of himself, a tall and dark savior of the people, fighting off the demons of society and order. Destine with his two gorgeous platinum haired angels, one clad in black and the other in white, striving to be the Promethian figures that they have the delusion that they are. Floors are mosaics, walls are tapestries, and every room is a monument to how great Destine thinks he is. All of it is as fake is he is. Underneath the pretty colors and the intricate designs is simply rock and wood, just like every other house. Just like behind the money and the prestige, he is imperfect and insignficant, just like every other person. The superficialities only mask his imperfections, they do not fix them. And entering to his main chamber, a ridiculously tall room with a domed ceiling that I'm surprised doesn't yet show a painting of him touching the hand of Adam, Destine sits behind a throne of black marble on a chair of oak and decor. With his bodyguard/assassins on each side of him, Destine looks at me with a nearly welcoming smile and an extended hand. 'Ahh...my good friend Johnny Heroic. It has been a while since you've been in my home.' 'Still has that same smell.' 'Pachouli?' 'Jackass.' Destine nods with that same smile and Fortune, the more brash of the twins, lets out a little laugh at my insult. 'Always the candid, jovial courtier, aren't you? Even now, at the complete disadvantage, you stand before me and make jokes. That has to be my favourite part about you, Johnny.' I hate that. He actually pronounces the word so that I can hear the ou in favourite. It wouldn't bother me if he was British. But he's not. He's an American jackass that pronouces the ou in colour and favourite because he wants to sound British. Because he's pretentious and needs to die. Frankly speaking. 'And so, my lovely heiresses here have informed me about your little...predicament. I must say that when I first heard that the infamous Johnny Heroic had lost his powers, that he couldn't control electromagnetic fields and run around doing whatever he wanted with the laws of physics...I was so excited I almost cried. But then, I became rather sad. You see, I remembered your resolve. And how sad your resolve was. I suppose it's something to do with the fact that you could do whatever you wanted, and so you didn't feel a need to do anything. What was there to prove? What was there that you could...desire?' He loves when he makes a pun with his name. Jackass. 'And now that you're powerless, it's as simple as you flock to where you know the power is. Maybe you try to take some of mine. Maybe you pledge myself to me. Though I doubt both, they are possibilities. The central fact of what you are doing is that you are drawn to my power because you now have none. You couldn't have cared less about power until you no longer control anything. You had the galaxy at your fingertips, something that any other man would give anything to have, and you squandered it.' He looks at me through his perfectly well groomed hair and smiles again. He's the victor over the hero. My luck has run out, and he gets his time to gloat. 'Do you agree ladies?' Fortune and Fate have been measuring me up since I came in the room. The tension between the three of us is obvious. I want them, they want me, and they have no problem with a threesome. But our opposition comes first, and I'm not one for necrophilia. Though I don't know if the same can be said about them. 'He is...' 'Nothing. Barely even a man anymore. His drive-' '-is not commendable. It is simple better-' '-than it was earlier. He still stands there-' '-without desire, without good fortune, and without fate-' '-smiling upon him. He defies the very stars, calling them-' '-dead. He doesn't even realize that he is the one that is dead.' 'He calls himself Heroic. He calls himself a hero. He-' '-acts not the part. He is nothing more than just-' '-a man.' 'Nothing more.' There is a brief pause. Destine and I are both enveloped in watching the chorus that the girls speak. Neither one misses a beat, completing the sentences as though they truly were one person. Both eyeing me with the eyes of meat eaters eyeing a steak. Wanting to tear me limb from limb as payback for all the times I beat them in the past. They wanted to kill me earlier. Destine told them not to. Now all three will pay for that mistake. 'So let us be serious for a moment, Johnny. I truly am sorry about Larry-' 'His name was Lenny. He had a name, a family, and a life.' 'Larry, Lenny...it could Leopold Agnes III for all I care. What matters is the here and now. You are here, now, in front of me, with a couple of options. One, you can give up this ridiculous feud. You let me do as I do, and you can go off and live your life. Hell, I would even be interested in giving you employment and a purpose here at my industry. You can take this lucrative chance and wipe the slate between us clean. Two...you can continue to oppose me. And, sans powers, die.' 'Well...I don't know what I can say to that except...' Faster than they can act, I throw myself into a realm that I hadn't known before. Crime fighting? Striving for the justice? Hardly. I pull the .357 out of the holster on my back and shoot one shot at Fortune. One shot, and only one shot, and it hits her in the head. And she's dead. Just like that. Time freezes. Fortune's body sits in her chair, perfectly straight up with her head resting on the chair back. Her arms rest on the armrests, surprisingly, and she has an oddly serene look on her face. Other than the huge fucking hole in her head, in the chair behind it too, she looks perfectly fine. But you can see through her head, and through the chair, and see some of her brains on the wall behind her. And after the dozens and dozens of times that her and I have sparred, and after I threw her in jail time after time, and after she spared my life earlier today, it doesn't matter anymore. She's dead. Fate and Destine look on in horror for a second, and I do too. It takes all three of us a moment to realize what I had just done. I just pulled out a gun and shot Fortune. And now she's dead, and there's nothing else to say about it. Destine looks like he doesn't know what to do or say, but sit there. He stares at Fortune. Then he stares at me. Fate, meanwhile, has a different set of mind. 'DIE!!!' I fire the rest of the bullets in her general direction, but she's too swift now that she's alert and I hit nothing but air. And without my powers, she's on top of me in seconds and kicks the gun out of my hand. She lands a stiletto heel to my face and I go down. Hard. The barrage continues without pause as Fate goes to avenge her slain sister. 'You son of a-!!! I hate-!!! I'll rip out your-!!!' She's shouting her threats. But there's no one to finish her sentence. Fate's never been in this position before. But nonetheless she's kicking the shit out of me. Punch to the face. Kick to the crotch. Elbow to my already shattered ribs. Chop to the throat. And that's all before I hit the ground the second time. In a heartbeat she lifts me off the ground again and almost rips my head off. My head can barely tell where her fist stops and concrete begins as she punches me and knocks me to the ground over and over. The whole time, I know that Destine is just sitting there. Watching. And the whole time, Fate doesn't know what I've got in store for her until it's too late. And I pull the taser out of its back holster and hold it up to her neck. A few thousand volts course through her mid-punch as she looks at me with seering wide eyes of hatred. She drops and so do I. And it's immediately a race to see who could possibly recover. No matter which of the two of us are up first, the obvious loser is Destine, who hasn't moved from his spot. Too shocked to understand the facts of blood and death, he cannot believe what his rival just did. Fate almost gets the upper hand again. But she won't fall for the same trick twice, so I make sure she doesn't get that hand. Instead, she gets the back of my hand. I normally have problems hitting a girl. But the situation calls for it. I take the swift advantage and swipe the knife off of Fate's boot and dive at her. Her arms keep me away with all of her strength, and I feel like my ribs are going to rip out of my body. 'I'll make you pay for-....for... She deserved a better death... ...more than you can know... NEVER!' Fate's wide eye can't get away from the blade and her strength is just not enough to stop it. The razor's edge rests on her skin for a brief second and she can't help but look up at me. And my bloodshot eyes almost say the words for me. 'Should have killed me when you could. Time to die.' Shhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhpht. The noise isn't loud or powerful. But it's the only sound in the room, and could be for the rest of all eternity. The knife doesn't meet resistance, but slides into Fate's chest and stops her heart immediately. And she rests. Fate and Fortune both may rest now. Their stars are burnt out. All that remains are the feelings I can still feel quite well from their actions earlier. But they're dead. And Desire can't believe it. 'I can't believe it. You just murdered the two of them.' I give Desire the cold eye and stand up full. Then I turn around and begin to walk out. 'Hey! Where the Hell do you think you're going, murderer! MURDERER!' I stop as he shouts that word and I look back him. He wants to kill me. Not entirely. He wants me to kill him. Because he loves Fortune and Fate with all his heart, but now I've stolen them from him. And he wants there to be something right in the universe when I kill him. With all his desire, he wants me to kill him. 'I'm going home, Desire. I'm going home.' But he doesn't deserve the satisfaction. Lenny's dead. Fate and Fortune are no more. Desire loses his will to go on, and I lose my powers. Strangely, all seems right with the world.
