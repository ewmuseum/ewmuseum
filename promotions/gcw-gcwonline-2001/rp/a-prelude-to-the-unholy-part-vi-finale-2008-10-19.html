A Prelude to the Unholy- Part VI(Finale)

19 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-19-2008 I’ve often wondered what it was that made crazy people? well, crazy. Even moreso since I began to feel myself slipping farther and farther into insanity. The dictionary defines insanity as ?the condition of being insane; a derangement of the mind.? The big question is, what causes that derangement? Is it a hormonal imbalance? Is it a lack of oxygen to the brain? Or is it the effect of bad memories from the past piling on top of one another until the man they’re haunting snaps? No matter what it is, there’s one thing that I know for sure’Insanity will creep up on you before you know what has hit you. I first began seeing its signs earlier this year. It was February, in fact, while James von Doom and I were touring for an independent promotion by the name of Circle of Pride. Back then, we were known as Team AmeriPAN~!, and our gimmick was supposed to be that of a couple of American traitors, who gave up everything American in favor of the Japanese culture. As always, while I was warming up and training for our match, James was doing what he always did. He was goofing off. “James” Why can’t you be serious for even a minute?” That was probably the dumbest question in the history of mankind. Anyone who knows James von Doom knows that it’s a cold day in Hell when he’s serious about anything. He looked up from his magazine, gazing at me with his eyebrow cocked. “Why do we need to train so hard for those pushovers” After all, we came to Circle of Pride in search of stiff competition, and all we found are a couple of douchebags who are stiff for each other.” I couldn’t help but laugh at that lame insult. “Come on, James. Can’t you do better than that?”, I asked, knowing full well that it wasn’t his best work. After all, this was James von Doom I was speaking to. He was the King of Trash Talk, the Innovator of the Insult, the Progenitor of Putdowns. “After all, you’re just like me. You don’t have much of a life outside of this business.”, I said, ribbing him a little. “Alright, Team AmeriPAN~!, five minutes until you’re set to go out!”, said the stagehand who had come to alert us that our match was next. Suddenly, James started panicking and attempting to stretch and warm up. I simply shook my head in disgust, trying not to laugh at the display of complete, utter silliness he was putting on. “James, it’s a bit late for that, don’t you think” After all, I recall you saying that our opponents are nothing more than “a couple of douchebags who are stiff for each other.” Am I right?” It was probably the most pitiful thing I’d ever seen up to that point, but hey, what can you do? All I knew was that it was time for our match, and I was itching to get out there and get things going. By this point, I had already developed a rather unhealthy obsession with causing bodily pain and physical torment to others. In fact, the only reason I accepted the match was because I had intentions to maim the Heartthrobs, as they called themselves. I couldn’t have cared less that it was a #1 Contenders match for the tag team titles. The Heartthrobs, however, had no idea what they were getting into. Little did anyone know, I got barred from competing in Japan because I deliberately separated my final opponent’s shoulder from the socket. Finally, our music hit, and it was time to go out to the ring. Since we were the new team on the block, we didn’t get the warmest reception, nor did we get a standing ovation. In fact, what we did get was booed. We were nearly booed out of the building. I didn’t know why we were getting such a sour reaction from the crowd, though it was most likely because we weren’t being very nice to them. Or maybe, just MAYBE, they hated us because I was taking liberties with the crowd favorites we were facing. What can I say? The guys irked me. After the match was over, I emerged in the locker room area, covered in blood. The blood was not my own, though. Upon reaching our locker room, we were met with our pink slips. They had been tacked to the door of our locker room, and apparently, we had been fired due to my ruthless behavior. So I had practically shattered a guy’s face with my knee. What’s a bit of plastic surgery between co-workers? They were a bunch of tightwads, anyway. After being unceremoniously tossed out of Circle of Pride after our first match, we decided to head to Italy to see what we could find. What, exactly, were we looking for, you ask? It’s quite simple. We were going to try and get laid? Italian style. __________________________________________________________ Upon our arrival in Italy, I began hearing strange voices. ?Come to us?? was all I heard, and it echoed through my head like the voice of a yodeler in the Swiss Alps. I looked to James, to see if he had heard anything, but he seemed to be completely ignorant of the voices. I thought that maybe I was just hearing things, but then, it rang out again. Only this time, it was louder, and I was sure it wasn’t just inside of my head. “James, did you hear that?”, I asked him, slightly confused about what I was hearing. “Did I hear what?”, he responded, saying nothing more than that. I simply shook my head and brushed the incident off like it never happened. As the days went by, the voices grew louder and louder, and they haunted me more and more. They were especially loud when I tried to sleep. ?Come to us?! COME TO US!? It was as if they were screaming at me, and it was causing me to perspire coldly. Within my tumultuous sleep, I could see a rather ominous-looking church, the spires of which were surrounded by dark clouds. There was no doubt in my mind that this is where I was supposed to go. The problem was that I didn’t know where it was, nor did I care. I just knew I had to go. I sat up as if I’d been struck by a bolt of lightning. It took everything I had in me to breathe, because my chest felt like it was being constricted. Finally, I managed to pull myself to my feet and snatch my clothes on. Next thing I knew, I was at the door of James? hotel room, banging on it with my fist. It was 2:30 on Friday morning, and he was most likely either sleeping, or with a “lady-friend”. Well, fortunately for me, it was the former. Unfortunately, he slept in the nude, and he answered his door in his birthday suit. “Put some clothes on. We’ve got somewhere we need to go.”, I said to him, not giving him a chance to utter even the smallest word. I turned on my heels and began heading for the front door of the hotel, figuring he’d catch up once he made himself decent. Sure enough, just before I made it out the door, he was calling out to me. “Axel! Wait up!” I heard him, but my feet wouldn’t stop moving. It was almost like I was being pulled by some kind of centripetal force. By the time he caught up with me, he was out of breath and struggling to speak. He attempted to talk to me in between gasps for air, but it just wasn’t working out like he wanted it to. I had no intention of stopping. In fact, I didn’t have time to. I just couldn’t afford to do so. We had to get to that church, and we had to get there in a hurry. “They’re calling to me, James” My Mom and Dad are calling for me.” At the time, I honestly believed they were. After all, every time the voices resonated within my skull, they belonged to my parents. I still didn’t have a clue where I was headed, or how far away the church was, but my feet kept propelling me forward, and the voices spurred me onward. ?COME TO US!!!?, they cried out, almost wailing sadly. It seemed as though they weren’t just in my head anymore. It almost sounded like they were coming from right in front of me. The strange thing was that James seemed completely unphased by any of it. As we trudged along towards what I assumed was that church, I was giddier than a Catholic schoolgirl going to her first social. However, if the truth was told, I was losing my mind. Ever since I’d made my way to Japan two years ago, my mental health had been on a steady decline, and these voices I was hearing weren’t making things any better. We finally happened upon an old, seemingly abandoned church. I don’t know why, but something told me to stop out in front of it. “We’re here?”, I said, gazing up at the large structure. It was one of the old-style churches with the large spires and archways, with pillars supporting the overhang. I headed up the front steps, moving straight to the door. James was calling out to me, attempting to stop me, but it was too late for words. I pushed the doors open, and an unusually cold wind from inside washed over me like a tidal wave. As cold as it was, I was drawn inside like a moth to a flame. As I looked around, it appeared to be your normal, every day church. There was an altar up front for worship, and behind it was a section for the choir. All along the walls of the church were stained glass windows depicting images of Christ and his disciples, the Virgin Mary, and of the crucifixion. For such a holy place, there sure was quite the evil aura emanating from it. Something just didn’t feel right about it. It didn’t feel right at all. Somehow, James had worked up the nerve to come inside, and I guess he had pissed someone or something off, because the doors to the church closed themselves and locked behind us. By now, we were both scared, and were looking for somewhere to go, but there was nowhere. There was an effigy of Christ on the cross hanging on the back wall of the room, though it was inverted. It’s a well-known fact that an inverted cross was meant as a sign of disrespect to all things Holy. All of a sudden, I was drawn into the world inside of my head. It was a place where memories ran roughshod and tore my mind apart, piece by piece. Once again, I was taken back to the day that my parents died, though not at the exact time of their death like I normally was. This time, I was taken back to the point just before their deaths. I was presented with a scene that I could have sworn I’d never seen before. What I saw shook me to my very core and destroyed everything I had once believed in. I saw myself standing in front of my parents? car with the hood up. In my left hand was a pair of wire cutters and in my right was a severed brake line. I guess my mind couldn’t handle what I had seen, because after that, everything went black. ___________________________________________________________ Next thing I knew, I had awakened to a white room with padded walls and floor. I sat up and attempted to stretch, but found that I couldn’t. What was going on? I was bound in a straightjacket and was locked in a padded room. I couldn’t have been committed to an asylum, could I? I struggled to get to my feet, but found that it was near impossible to do so without the use of my arms, so I did what seemed the most natural thing to do at a time like this. ?LET ME OUT!!!!? I managed to clamber to my feet, and once I did, I went into a fit of rage. Angry over my current situation, I began throwing myself against the walls, roaring and screaming like some kind of primal beast. This went on for days on end, and by the time it was over, I was exhausted and hungry. Apparently, when you misbehave, they don’t feed you at this place. Well, they didn’t feed me, anyway. That was okay, though, because I had already eaten through my straightjacket. It didn’t taste good at all, but on the bright side, my arms were free. A month went by, and finally, someone came to my door, and it wasn’t just an orderly. It was and orderly as well as my best friend and tag team partner, James von Doom. Could they have come to let me out? When they opened my door, I smiled for the first time in three months. Was I truly happy, though? I could never again be truly happy as long as I remembered why my parents died. On our way back to America, James filled me in on what happened, and told me about the new religion he’d taken up. He told me that through Lord Tioneb, I could be absolved of all my transgressions and could rest easy without the burden of my horrid memories. It all sounded so appealing to me, so I knelt down right in front of my seat on the airplane and began to pray. It wasn’t to God, though. No, it was to Lord Tioneb. When I was finished, I felt, for the first time in a long time, real happiness. “Hail Tioneb.”, he said to me as I came up from my prayer. I looked at him and simply nodded before taking my seat once more and slipping into a peaceful slumber. ___________________________________________________________ I sit here now, just minutes before what could be considered the biggest match of my professional career, reflecting on my life and what I’ve done with it. To be quite honest, the last few months have been a complete sham, a farce, if you will. I’ve been so deluded with the falsehood of Lord Tioneb that I’ve failed to see that I never needed him in the first place. Everyone does things that they aren’t proud of, and they have to live with those things. I realize now, that Lord Tioneb was my way of passing the buck to someone else and shirking the responsibilities that come with the atrocities that I’ve committed over the years. I’m no longer blind to the truth. In fact, I embrace it wholeheartedly. That truth, my friends, is that I’m an evil, despicable son of a bitch, and I love it. I never needed Lord Tioneb’s help to become the unholy being that I am today. I never needed James von Doom to get to where I am in this business. He’s only ever served to hold me down, just like every other tag team partner and “friend” that I’ve had in this business. Simply put, I control my own destiny, and I am my own God. ??And soon, you will all bow at my feet.”
