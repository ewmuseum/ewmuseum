Date: 6 Mar 06 I. Suicide 10 November 1999 It had been a year and some change since my brother’s death and of course the fallout of it. In the year following I went into exile. Really it was the only thing that I knew how to do at that point in time. Much like the time in Spain when my wife and child were killed and I was sent away and told never to return. I couldn’t handle those words being told to me once again. My grandmother had done so much for me in the short time she had me under her care and how did I repay her? By letting her REAL grandson get murdered. I was nothing more than a consolation prize. We may have had the same blood coursing through our veins but we were not really family. She knew me about as well as you know a second or third cousin whom you see or hear about only at big family gatherings. If you would have asked me then, I would have told you that I was a curse on the family. Everyone I had ever touched had been hurt or worse. They say that everyone has a reason to live. It is said that no matter how bad it gets you can always find a ray of sunshine amongst the darkness. I have also heard that life only throws at you what it thinks you can handle. Do you know what I say to those remarks? Bull and Shit. What reason could one possibly have for living in a time when they have lost their wife, child, and brother? In a time where their parents disowned them, banishing them from even living in the same COUNTRY as they did. I wish someone could tell me how one is suppose to want to continue to fight when everything they had worth fighting for is taken from them. Well on this date in my life, these were the exact questions I had been wondering to myself. For one year I had remained stuck inside of a filthy one room apartment. Sure I had plenty of cash to go elsewhere but it was at a place like this one that no one would have thought to find me. I couldn’t stand to look any of them in the eye and see that disappointment. I didn’t even bother to go to my brother’s funeral, I just couldn’t. I saw him enough I’m afraid, hell I still do. Every night when I lay down to sleep I see his face. More importantly I get a glimpse of his eyes staring at me right before he died. They were so full of fear and helplessness that it sickened me every time. In the dreams they change. They change when that blast goes off and the bullet cuts the life from his body. No longer are they afraid. Now those once innocent eyes turned black, empty, hollow. They dig into my soul and accuse me of killing him. I cry and cry knowing no other way to release the grief. Except one. Every single day in that year that I disappeared I thought about one thing extensively. Every single day in that year, I would pull out the black pistol I had purchased and press it up against my forehead. I would close my eyes tight, place my finger on the trigger and prepare to move on. To leave this life of heartache and despair, to go to a place where I could finally be with the people who really loved me. The feeling of the cold metal on my skin felt strangely relaxing. The thoughts of being able to hold Cindy in my arms again, kiss her, and make love to her made it all seem like the right thing to do. I imagined hearing Starr crying in her crib once again and me having to rock her to sleep in the early hours of the morning. I would imagine seeing Cindy’s angelic face for the first time all over again and feeling the warmth of love for the first time. I could see Damen’s face, smiling and laughing. He was a jokester that was for damn sure. If he hadn’t loved wrestling so much then he probably would have been a comedian. I could hear his voice telling me it would be okay. I could see my Aunt and Uncle’s faces ? That’s when the tears would finally take over. No matter how hard I fought them I could not hold back the burning behind my eyes. The pain deep inside felt as if someone had drove a hot knife into my heart and began to twist. The only way to make the pain stop was to pull the trigger. Then I would slowly begin to squeeze. And just like every other day the bullet never came because my finger never quite squeezed hard enough. But unlike the rest of the days it wasn’t some fear of losing my life that stopped me. It was the knock on my door. II Angel 19 February 06 “So Dominic what is it that you have for me this time?” Dominic seemed to be a good person overall. I hired him to track down a drug lord that came from Mexico years ago and had been hiding out in Detroit. Not too long ago I had heard that the man I killed, Victor Monterro, may not have been the true mastermind that I was after in my brother’s murder. I needed someone that could get the dirt without being noticed. Sure I had a tendency to get answers but I was lacking what Eva once called, Finesse. Private investigator seemed like the logical choice. Sure Mr. Devereaux was a bit misguided but he usually had good intentions, especially when it came to the criminal persuasion. “A little asshole named Angel is being held for more observation in room 322.” Dominic smirks a bit. “He seemed to have an unfortunate run in with a stray bullet. He’ll be alright just; he may be walking with a cane from now on.” Good person ? Very misguided. “Very well but what does this have to do with what I am looking for?” “Well Mr. Knight, Angel knows the name of the one in charge of all the drugs. Sadly all he knows is a first name. David I think it was.” I nodded my head and then quickly handed Dominic his envelope of money. There was nothing else to say to him at this point and he knew that I’d keep in touch. At that point all there was to do was have a word with this Angel. It took a little time to reach the room but I got there. The room was dark and the only noise filling the room was the rhythmic beating off the heart monitor. For an instant I had a feeling of d’j? vu. Two things immediately popped into my head at that moment. First was my heart attack. A week prior to my biggest match to date, I died ? even if it were just for a few moments. The sounds and smells of the hospital were ones that I could have went the rest of my life without hearing again. Although in my profession that really wasn’t an option. The other thing was visiting Eva the night after she had been raped. Of all of the wrong I did to her and the hatred I felt for her ? in that one night I honestly felt real sympathy for her. As much as I may not have like Ms. VanLorne I would never wish that kind of abuse on anyone. Sadly sympathy has never helped anyone. Surprisingly Angel was awake, staring out of the window near his bed. He refused to acknowledge me even though I knew my shadow made it known I was there. Even when I reached the foot of his bed, his facial expression never changed. “Angel I presume?” No answer. “My name is?” “I don’t care.” “Excuse me?” “I don’t care who you are or what you want.” He swallowed hard. “Just leave me alone.” “I’m sorry but I cannot do that without asking you a couple of questions.” “Well I’m sorry but I’ve had enough cops.” “Cops?” I couldn’t help but chuckle. “Not a cop Angel. My name is Roberto Abitua and I need to ask you about David.” This caused his head to turn in my direction. “There’s nothing to tell. I have never met the guy. All I can tell you is that he is dangerous.” “Do you know where I can find him?” “Detroit.” “That’s rather vague.” “It’s where he is.” And so it was ? Detroit is where I had to go. III Train 15 November 1999 “I never wanted to come back here.” The knock on my door was an old friend, RaZiel. For anyone who doesn’t know he began as my brother’s trainer and ended up as my best friend and bodyguard. How he found me in that hell hole of an apartment I will never understand but he did. Basically he saved my life. “I know Rob but you cannot hold in all of that aggression. I know you are hurting and I understand the pain you are going through. I miss him too; he was like a little brother to me. What you really need to do though is find a way to harness that aggression. If you let it eat your insides up ? you die.” Funny thing was at that time it was all that I wanted, to die that is. I wanted to be “HOME” with my family. It was my own cowardice that kept that from happening. RaZ felt that the best way to conquer this fear was to go back to where it all started. Detroit Michigan. We found ourselves into a small beat up gym. It was where he began to mold me. In that place was where eventually a legend would be born. We found ourselves inside of the dusty ring, no pads, gloves or protective gear. This was about getting down and dirty. This was about aggression and violence. Slowly we began to circle one another. “Roberto it’s that fear that you have inside of you that cripples you. You fear being alone and you fear your destiny.” At the time my only fear was being alone. In those moments I had no idea of the man that I would become. I had no clue that I would begin to form a legacy that I never imagined. I knew nothing of destiny and fate, just carnage and bloodshed, Vengeance and lust. I Spun around and missed a roundhouse kick, RaZ swiftly hit me with a straight right hand, immediately bringing blood gushing from my nose. “You cannot be weak any longer. You do not wear your hatred and aggression out for all to see. You must hide it with a mask.” I caught an attempted right hand and drove my boot into his gut sending the 7’0? 350 lbs. man back a few steps. This was before Global Championship Wrestling, PTC, GTT5, Michael Stevens, Eva VanLorne, Jason Dread, and the Rampage tournament. Before the success and before becoming the force that I had, I was fragile and afraid. I was weak and unsure of myself. “You have so much potential Roberto. But until you look your fear directly into the eye you will never realize it. You can be the BEST if you really want it.” I swung but he had far too much strength and grabbed my fist in his huge paw. I could feel my knuckles crack under his grip. I lifted my free hand and brought it down on his wrist causing it to crack and he released his grip enough for me to spin and drive a back kick into his chest. I was young and “near sighted”. I had to move on but I had no idea how to. I wanted to make the world feel my pain and my anguish. I wanted others to fear ME the way I feared life itself. I wanted others to know that sometimes there are worst things than death. One of those things? Getting in my path. I moved in with an axe kick that RaZ pushed away and then clothes lined me. Before I knew it I was staring up at the lights on the ceiling. His boot jammed onto my throat. Again I was helpless Just like when Damen died. “I can see the fire in your eyes Roberto, that anger and that hatred. If you let me I can help you. I can build you into an unstoppable wave. I can help you use fear as a weapon.” I didn’t know it at that moment but it was then that The Devil gave birth to LeStatt Knight. What I did not know? Is that the devil was closer than I ever imagined. IV So again I sat in that chair, bloody and bruised. The tears had dried up and the fear and hurt had been replaced with anger. I wanted to kill him just like he tried to kill my life. For years I thought one thing and the reality was another. For years I was made to look like the bad guy, some sinister son of a bitch that had no heart. Yet it wasn’t me who was evil. That arrogant smirk plastered on HIS face made me want to fucking puke. He wouldn’t be enjoying life if I ever got out of these ropes. Or whatever bit of life he had left to live. When I got my revenge I would rid myself and the world of the filth that is David. His blood would be on my hands. and in this case? I never would want to wash it off.
