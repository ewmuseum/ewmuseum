A simple request

14 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/14/09 I started to believe that it would be a horrible day when I first woke up. Luckily I live fairly close to the high school in which I work, so I usually only have to drag myself out of bed around 7:10 unlike some of my colleagues who actually get up at six. But today the phone actually rang at 6:20, and for some odd reason I had placed it right next to my bed the night before. I start when I hear the excessively loud ring of the telephone. Groggy and peeved I seriously debate ignoring it, but for all I know it could be my uncle Erick. I have been hiding in this town pretending to be a gym teacher ever since someone attempted to poison me, and my uncle had promised to try and uncover who was behind the whole thing. With my eyes still closed I blindly reach out of the telephone. My hand slowly creeps along the smooth surface of the desk before finally closing around the still ringing device. “Hello,” I say with a hint of irritation in my voice as I bring the phone to my ear. “Oh hey Alex,” an unbearably cheerful voice answers back, “it’s Alicia.” Alright, I swear to God I am going to get caller ID today. You know those girls whose entire lives revolved around their high school wonder years…well Alicia is that type of girl on steroids. I honestly don’t think that she remembers she’s a teacher instead of a student half the time, and since I am the biggest thing to a jock in the faculty, it seems that she has set her sights on me. This would be all be fine if I was at all attracted to a slutty empty headed woman with bleach blond hair and an overdone spray tan. Unfortunately I prefer a slutty empty headed brunette with a natural tan. “Alicia,” I groan angrily, “it’s barely past six. Why the hell are you calling me? “Language mister,” she giggles which sets my teeth on edge. “But anyway, I was wondering if you could help me today. I have to do a presentation for my class, but I could really use some help setting everything up.” “And why,” I moan into the receiver as my eyes fight to open, “couldn’t you have asked me for help when we got to the school itself?” “Because” she responds with a tone that seems to be both mocking and annoying at the same time. “I need you to get to the school as soon as I finish telling you what to do if you’re gonna have time to finish everything“ With a grunt I hang up the phone and fall back asleep. *Later that day* “You pathetic maggots are the worst, piss poor excuse for athletes that I have ever seen!” I bellow at the group of students sitting before me. Surprisingly I’m not yelling at the wrestling team that I have been drafted into coaching. Rather, this time I’m just yelling at the regular old gym class that I teach on a daily basis. Now, I understand that I am not dealing with the with the next Michael Jordan’s of the world. The school is sixty-five percent stereotypical Asian. And not the fun stereotype where the boys all have Bruce Lee fighting skills and the girls are more perverted than Bagwell after he’s drunk and talking to a shemale. Instead I am stuck with the pencil neck geeks who have pocket protectors, and consider calculus to be slightly erotic. I wish I was exaggerating. I really do. I could have forgiven them for being unable to make a basket. Or throw a football. Or hit a baseball, even though they were clearly just trying to get through the period. But then I saw some of them were actually doing math during my class. And were they getting their homework out of the way like any respectable high-school student? No, they were doing extra work. For fun. There is clearly something horribly, horribly wrong with this school and I can’t ignore it any longer. Besides, anyone who enjoys doing math no longer qualifies as a person. At best they are a subspecies to be used for various painful experiments. “From now on,” I growl to the scared class, “You are going to put so much effort into this class it will be ridiculous. You will have daily homework, which you will do without hesitation. You shall start lifting in the weight room, and you are all going to learn how to hit a ball with a fucking bat.” The class erupted in a roar of gasps and outraged shouts. “Hey,” shouts one of the braver kids, “you can’t make us do all that!” “Oh I can,” I say with a gleeful expression on my face. “And if you don’t I’m going to fail you. Tell me, how many of you think you can get into Harvard with an F on your report card?” Suffice to say that there were no more arguments after that. Three hours later I find myself being called down to the principal’s office. Until today I’ve never even met the guy, since he was on vacation when Erick arranged the job for me, but I am pretty sure I know what his deal will be. He built a reputation for producing eggheads out of this school, and he isn’t about to ruin it because some gym teacher wants to give his kids a little exercise. Hell, I bet he is just like the students. I walk into the room, and find myself in the most luxurious place in the high school. In fact if someone told me I had been transported out of the high school I wouldn’t be surprised. Imagine what the office of the CEO of a Fortune 500 company might look like. Ok….imagine what it would look like before we found out the economy is about to collapse. On the ridiculously expensive desk is an even more expensive computer that NASA would probably like to have. There is a large fish tank in the corner of the room, in which a variety of colorful fish swim around aimlessly. My eyes travel to the person sitting behind the desk, diligently typing something into the computer. I can already see some of the traits that I expected to see. From the looks of it, the principal is intelligent, all business, and I can detect no trace of warmth emanating from the direction of the principal. However, at the same time the principal also possessed a trait that I was not expecting…she is really fucking hot. “Mr. Goldberg,” she says coldly, “thank you for coming.” Principal Wansac stares at me menacingly with light brown eyes as I slowly take a seat. Her curly hair falls around her shoulders, the brown color contrasting nicely with the white shirt that she is wearing. Even through the stern expression she is wearing, she can’t hide the natural beauty in her face that makes it seem like she should be an actress rather than a principal. I’m temporarily dazed as I find myself imagining what her body looks like underneath the clothes she is wearing. “Mr. Goldberg,” I hear her say again and there is a note of annoyance in both her voice and face. “If you are done ogling me, perhaps we can get to the reason that I called you down here?” I sternly tell myself to stop staring at the swell of her breasts and manage to look at her eyes as I lean back into the chair. “Certainly,” I say with a smile, “how can I help you?” “Perhaps you could give me a good reason not to fire you,” she snaps wiping the smile off of my face. “You’re a gym teacher Mr. Goldberg, you are not here to give our kids homework. Your job consists of making sure that they pretend to try at whatever inane activity the state is forcing them to do, and then send them back to a real class where they belong.” I feel myself scowling at the attack on my fake profession. The whole gym teacher gig may just be a part of my persona, but my persona is proud of what he does damnit! “Look lady,” I fire back angrily, “the kids in this school are the least athletic bunch that I have ever seen. Half of them are on their way to obesity, and the other half look like they’re anorexic! I’m not asking them to muscle up to my level, just to have the basic fitness that any self-respecting human being is supposed to have.” “What they need,” she hisses at me, “is an education that few students in this country receive in a public high school. It is the norm for the students in this school to get accepted to Harvard, Yale, and every other top university in the country. And its because I ensure such consistent excellence, that I get what I want which is a great deal of compensation for my hard work,” she finishes by waving a hand around the office to indicate what she means by compensation. I stare at her curiously before responding. “How does that work?” I ask her with a frown. “The state pays your salary, and I can’t imagine that it would be willing to give you enough to afford all this, no matter how good your school was.” “It doesn’t matter,” she responds but I can detect a faint blush staining her cheeks. Well isn’t this interesting, it seems that Principal Wansac is not entirely kosher somewhere in her business dealings. Still, I have no idea how she might be making money off of forcing these kids to be geeks, and I doubt its actually hurting anybody. “What does matter,” she continues interrupting my train of thought, “is that with the threats you made to those kids I have more than enough reason to get the superintendent to fire you. But, I might be willing to forgo talking to him if you were to help me with something.” I smile broadly as I realize where this is going. It’s quite clear that the ice queen is about to get herself some; courtesy of Big D (I can’t believe I just used that horrendous nickname). “Alright,” I mutter feigning nervousness in order to play out her game, “what do you want me to do?” “There’s a group of dropouts from the highschool in the town over that have decided to peddle their drugs to my students, I want you to convince them to relocate their business activities elsewhere.” Both my face and my dick fall when her request finally registers. Why is it, that no matter where I go, there is always someone who wants me to do some act of violence? It’s not like I’m a naturally violent guy…ok that’s a lie. But still…she she made it sound like she wanted me to drop by the dry cleaners. “You know, there’s these guys called the police,” I answer her dryly, “drug dealers tend to be right up there alley.” “First of all,” she responds icily, “the police in this townare the most incompetent buffoons that I have ever witnessed. Despite knowing what is going on, and where, they say that they do not have the evidence to actually do anything. Besides, the police would take quite some time regardless. I was hoping for a faster, cleaner solution.” “Right,” I drawl though I must admit she is spot on about the police force, “and what makes you think that I am the man for the job? Like you just said, I’m only a gym teacher.” She actually laughs at that, causing me to flush though I’m not sure why. “Oh come Mr. Goldberg, it is obvious to anyone with half a brain that violence is nothing new to you. I’m fairly certain that the would-be gangsters will pose no threat to you. Now, it would be preferable if the problem were to be dealt with today…” I quietly consider telling her to go screw herself. I am not anyone’s errand boy, and its not like I need this job. But, its not like she is asking me to do anything particularly difficult (well difficult for me at any rate), and I have found myself enjoying the challenge of making this school what it is supposed to be. A place where the jocks rule and the geeks sullenly study and plot for their revenge twenty years down the road. That’s the American way of running the education system. “Alright,” I answer as I get up from the chair, “but I’m not doing anything for you after this.” She nods, and silently hands me a piece of paper with nothing but an address written on it. The address leads me to a little house right on the edge of town. Despite the fact that it looks very similar to the other houses around the area, there is an ominous aura that surrounds the building. I think it has something to do with the two bald and tattooed guys standing in front of the door, with knives in their hands. Its sights like these that make me wonder what the hell the police do with all the time on their hands. As a side note, don’t get the wrong idea about the town I’m living in. It’s almost painfully safe in most areas; it’s just that that the police don’t feel like dealing with the boys who set up shop here. I think it’s because the cops in our town got so used to not having to work, that they’re unwilling to break the trend. Slowly I get out of my car and walk up to two guys. They stare at me threateningly, and one actually pulls his thumb across his neck, telling me I’m dead if I go any closer. Abruptly…my temper snaps. I’m mean come on, a few days ago Lantlas nearly took my head off with a fucking flail of all things. And these punks think that they can actually scare me off because they have a few pointy objects in their hands? Hell, Garbage Bag Johnny’s fingernails are probably sharper and more lethal than those scraps of metal that they’re carrying around. So I am not even going to try and get them to move through a peaceful negotiation. First of all, it would fails miserably. Second of all, it’s not nearly as much fun. But most of all, I need to relieve some stress. In the space of a second I have closed the gap between us, and my hand has crushed the first thug’s head into the wooden wall of the house. The second man gaps at me, unable to grasp the fact that I have managed to move so quickly. He clumsily thrusts his knife at me, but I catch his wrist before he even comes close to my skin. With a started gasp he tried to break free, but my grip is far too strong. With a laugh I drive my knee into his groin, and he falls to the floor with a sickening thud. I force the locked door open with a vicious kick, and walk into the crack house. The interior is much as I expect it to be, with a pizza boxes and coke bottles strewn all over the place. There were five other men there, all in differing levels of sobriety. They gaped at me as I strode into their abode, and with a vicious grin I taught them the true wisdom of “stay in school and don’t do drugs”. I’ll spare you the details. It mostly involves a lot of punches, kicks, and at one point I felt the desire to use one of their chairs as a blunt weapon. At the end of it, I knelt down to look into the bruised and battered face of the one who I determined was the leader of this little gang. “I think it would be a really good idea,” I say to him with a vicious grin, “if you and your boys were to move your operation to another town, don’t ya think?” He nods his head frantically, and I triumphantly head to my car. Maybe my way isn’t pretty and lacks morals and ethics. But you can’t deny it works a hell of a lot faster than trying to reason with people.
