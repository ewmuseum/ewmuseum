Vampyr: Epilogue

9 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9 May 05 The world is a place which has different sides, good and bad. Most people do not see both sides though, only a select few. Calling these select few lucky would a travesty because the other side of our world is not a good place. When the sun goes down the world shifts from a place of prosperity, love, and wonderment to a cold, cruel, and evil place. There is something about the night that changes the world, something about the shining of the moon that alters reality. For centuries there have been tales of the night, stories which have been written to warn civilization of what’s to come. Fables and fairy tales have been passed down from generation to generation, giving insight into this dark corner of the world. This Dark Half is ruled by lust and hunger, with blood being the ultimate prize. I guess the one thing that everyone must understand is that the fables, in their purest form, are true. The reality is ? Vampires do exist. In a world so enamored by scientific theories and explanations, these creatures have escaped the spotlight. They date back not only hundreds of years, BUT thousands. Forget Bram Stoker calling Dracula a Romanian Knight who damned God and Heaven, the hell with John Carpenter claiming that a vampire named Valek was the first born vampire, and you damn sure better forget the old fables about Count Dracula of Transylvania being afraid of Garlic, crucifixes, or transforming into a wolf ? these are all lies. Dracula was much more than a Count or a Knight, he wasn’t even a King or a Prince. No the one known as Dracula was far greater, he was a servant of Heaven. His father was not a noble man though, in fact his father conspired to destroy the gates to Heaven and claim the throne for himself. God found out about this ploy and damned Dracula’s father, sending him to rot in hell ? Dracula’s father was Lucipher. Fearing that the boy would become just like his father God banished Dracula to roam Earth and suffer for the sins of his creations, MAN. Dracula hated God because of this but never spoke it out loud knowing his fate would be even far worse if he did. The years passed and Dracula lived as an every day man, working, eating, living. He even got married to the beautiful Kari Sipert, and together they had a beautiful baby boy which they named LeStatt. Outside the one known as Dracula was but an ordinary man, but deep down his mind was hard at work. Dracula was there the day Jesus Christ was born, and he was there when he died. It was Dracula who captured Jesus, and it was Dracula who nailed the spikes into his body, crucifying him. He finally got his revenge, by taking God’s son who was sent there to do amazing things. It was that day that Dracula drank the blood of Christ ? and swore that from that day on he would spread his evil like a disease and never let GOD rest peacefully. Dracula did exactly as he promised, bringing death upon humanity as if it were a plague. Using the dark gifts of his father, and mixing them with the powers he obtained from the blood of Christ to destroy the very essence of life. Every chance he got he spread the blood of humanity. The hunger consumed him, a hunger that could never be satisfied. Life after life he went through and destroyed, all in spite of God and everything he stood for. With every soul that he engulfed, more power built inside of him. From the heavens they tried plotting and planning a way to destroy this spawn of Satan, but no matter what they did he would survive. Instead of taking his life, Dracula started wars, Dating back from the war of Troy to the World Wars even to such blasphemous acts such as 9/11. So not only was he destroying lives but he was finding ways to have humanity destroy themselves, and Dracula was never suspected. His influence peaked and darkness consumed much of the world. There was a problem with what was happening in his eyes though, because although the vampire race was spreading, as the years went on they were becoming diluted. More and more weaknesses were popping up, from Garlic, Silver, to holy water and even sunlight. Disgusted at what he was witnessing he felt it was time for him to rest ? to lock him self up and let his people fight for themselves, and hopefully one would come to the forefront to take charge, and rule the world as they should. In 1705 he began to sleep but said that if after three centuries the vampire race was even farther behind the humans than he would come back and unleash a fury the likes that no one had ever seen before. No matter if you were Vampire, Human, or any other creature, he would bring the Apocalypse ? the end of all things. The year is now 2005 Some will ask how I know such things, and my answer will be quite easy. I am a vampire. The world has been able to duck and dodge the wrath of the Almighty Dracula, but soon he is set to come back and reclaim his throne. It is now that the world will come to grips with reality, that their world is not all roses and candy. It is time for humanity to understand that when there is good, there is evil? and where there is life, there is death. The world beneath your world is about to erupt like a volcano, and sadly there is very little that anyone can do about it. The most anyone can do is hope that their souls are not destroyed as well as their bodies. It’s time to pray to whatever god you wish. The awakening is now.
