Tacky-Tachyons Part 5 (Transitional Dreams)

28 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/28/11 The arena roars with passion and ballyhoo as the excitement shakes the venue electrifying everyone in attendance. The crowd has clearly picked their favorite, as the young confident challenger trades blows with the villainous champion in the center of the squared circle. "Sweet Baby" Ray: "Mmy-Gawd, the champ Behr Chekov is being taken to his limits tonight! Never has he met such a barrage of maneuvers, AMAZING!" Chris N Pitts: "The Russian Behr has never faced off against the likes of the Crusher Bradley Cruz!" The Champ and the contender begin to trade blows back and forth. The grizzled champion gains the upper hand forcing Bradley into the corner with blow after blow dazing "the Crusher". Behr lifts Brad seating on the top rope. Behr climbs up setting Cruz up for a superplex. Behr lifts but Brad clings on with all his might. Cruz breaks free and retaliates with head-butt after head-butt dazing the champion. Behr is reeling until Brad helps with a shove from his boot sending "The Russian Behr" crashing to the mat with sickening crash. "The Crusher" Brad Cruz positions himself on the top rope sizing up the fallen champion. "Sweet Baby" Ray: "The champion is down and the challenger has him in his cross-hairs!" Brad poses for the crowd brushing aside his long brown hair out of his face before flinging himself into a huge shooting star-press coming down fast and hitting the champion hard. The challenger hooks leg for a clean lateral press. Chris N. Pitts: "WHAT-A-MANUEVER!" "Sweet Baby" Ray: "Shooting Star Crush!" 1!!! 2!!! 3!!! Jack Daniels: "Your winner and new Champion Brad "the Crusher" Cruz!!!" "The Crusher" by the Ramones hits over the PA and Brad is handed his title which he promptly lifts over his head as the crowd roars and suddenly falls eerily silent. Confused Brad looks around into the crowd and sees nothing but a sea of now empty seats. Cruz shrugs and looks to his newly won championship, but the title he had in his hand moments before was also gone, nowhere to be seen. Bradley is left scratching his head in confusion with a look of disbelief on his face. Brad: "What the-? I just had-!" Cruz lets out a big sigh and slumps down sitting cross legged in the middle of the ring. His head wearily slumps down. Brad: "What could possibly happen nex-" RRRRRRRRRiiiiiiip! A Large tattooed arm bursts its way through the ring canvas gripping the neck of Brad. Cruz struggles trying to break free as a large tattooed clown like figure emerges from the tattered ring mat lifting Bradley off the ground by his neck. The Brad struggles the stronger the giant clown’s grip becomes. The clown chuckles as drives Bradley to the mat completely shattering the young brazen hero from moments ago and leaving only a small scared little 13 year old boy sprawled out on the mat as the pain shoots through his little spine. He is only able to muster a few words as the killer clown. Brad: "W-who are you? W-what are you?" The dread-locked clown smiles as he lifts his boot and simply says. "MAYHEM!" His boot comes down onto of Bradley who braces for the worst! Brad: "AAHHHH!!!!" Bradley shoots up in bed yelling at the tops of his lungs. "Easy there kiddo, everything is alright." An older gentlemen wearing a white goatee, black derby, and matching Buddy Holly Glasses approaches Brad’s bed with a look of concern on his face. Bradley’s eyes slowly adjust as he takes notice of the older gentlemen. Still drowsy it takes a moment to processes the person he is seeing. Brad: "Grandpa? GRANDPA!" Bradley wraps his arms around his Grandfather and refuses to let go. Grandpa Cruz: "You feeling better, kid?" Bradley clinging to his Grandfather answers. Brad: "Yep." Grandpa Cruz: "What was all that screaming about, bad dream?" Brad: "Yeah, it started so well. I was in a wrestling match and I totally one the title and all the announcers and commentators were named after Barbeque for some reason, and then there was this scary guy… … … I don’t want to talk about it." Grandpa Cruz still concerned takes notice that Bradley is still clinging to him. Grandpa Cruz: "Are you sure? …because you haven’t eased your grip since I came in." Brad: "I’m not scared." Grandpa Cruz: "Then w-" Brad: "I just really, really missed you. I feel like I haven’t seen you for a long time." Grandpa Cruz: "But you saw he just this morning." Brad: "I know it just seemed longer for some reason." Brad lets his grandfather go. Brad: "I know it’s silly…" Grandpa Cruz chuckles to himself as places his hand over his Grandson’s forehead checking if his fever has subsided. Grandpa Cruz: "Well then kiddo, you’re my kind of silly. You’re fever is improving. Let’s get you something to eat, and we can watch a movie if you like." Brad: "Jimmy Dibbo vs. The Raging Aztec in the cage!" Grandpa Cruz: "Alright, well dinner is almost ready you should get yourself cleaned up for dinner." Grandpa Cruz is about leave when young Bradley call his attention. Brad: "Grandpa, are my mom and dad home." Grandpa Cruz: "Your mom is due back from New York tomorrow and your Old Man will probably be stumbling in late tonight, another night practice." Brad: "You’re not going anywhere are you?" Grandpa Cruz: "Don’t worry, I Promise I’m not going anywhere. Please, wash up." With a warm smile he leaves the room headed for the kitchen to serve dinner. As Bradley mulls over his grandfathers words "Don’t worry, I promise I’m not going anywhere." Something bothered Brad about that promise. Not that his Grandfather ever lied to him or gave him any reason to doubt him, but something told Brad he wouldn’t be able to keep that promise. Bradley gets to his feet and head to the bathroom to wash up.
