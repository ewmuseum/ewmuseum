Big D: Origins

Date: 11/22/2009 I find that right now, life is pretty fucking sweet. I have more money than I know what to do with, my life’s finally in order, and people just wish that they could be me. But I know that there are some people out there wondering, how Dan Black, hell how Big D, became the wrestling force of nature that he is today. Well it started in college. Long before I became Big D, hell longer before I became the Bulldozer, I was busy doing what all college students do. Drinking, fucking, and making sure do avoid doing work as much as possible. *Ten years ago* “Suddenly, I realize that the taste and smell that I am experiencing is the taste and smell of COCK!” I look up from my laptop, startled by the sudden outburst. As a general rule I tend to spend my time in my General Psychology class by doing anything that didn’t involve paying attention, but the professor gained even my attention by that statement. Usually the man is very one-track and spends the entire class talking about love, the power of love, the necessity of love, and everything else that is even slightly related to love. Can you see why the entire college calls him Doctor Love? “You see, part of me wanted to ignore the sensations that I was experiencing,” he continues as the class listens. “But I knew that I was remembering something important. So over the next few weeks I keep it all in my mind; and one day I remember that when I was kid my brother forced his cock into my mouth! But the reason I didn’t remember it was because I suppressed the memory.” My mouth, along with the mouths of almost every other student in class, falls open. I have officially become extremely uncomfortable in this class, but a quick glance at the clock reveals that I am going to have to suffer through for half an hour more. Now I understand that this is the sort of issue that a psychologist has to deal with everyday. But I’m not taking this class because I want to be a fucking psychologist. I’m taking it for the easy A! For my own sanity I quickly go back to tuning out Doctor Love and resume checking my email. Let’s see…spam, spam, pornography, some crap from Rutgers itself, and….hmm, an email from Bill. Bill is a former professional wrestler and overall badass. I met him when I tried out the professional wrestling club, where he proceeded to wipe the floor with every student there. I was the only one who even managed to get him down to the ground, at which point he decided he was going to mold me into a future wrestler. I’m still ninety-nine percent sure that I’m not going into wrestling, but his workouts are the only thing keeping me in shape. Plus, I’ve found that I enjoy the challenging of trying to kick his ass every day. Dan, the email reads, meet me in the weight room at eight am sharp tomorrow. Well fucking hell. I can’t believe the crazy bastard actually expects me to be awake, much less ready to train, at eight in the morning on a Saturday. Not to mention, in Rutgers, Friday night is drinking night (the same holds true for Thursday and Saturday night as well). I could stay in tonight. After all, it’s not like every Friday night has to be full of drinking and fucking. I could instead…read a novel, play a video game, and maybe even try my hand at writing a short story for my own amusement. Yea…that’s not going to happen. Ok, new plan, I go out, drink, try to get laid, but don’t get drunk enough for a hangover. In which case the worst I’ll have to deal with on Saturday is a lack of sleep. Ok, it’s not the best plan in the world, but it’s the best that I am going to come up with. If I could come up with something better I would probably living in the honors dorm. At long last the class finally ends and all the students start to rush out the door; no doubt to try and wipe clean the memory of today’s class. On my way out I run into a dorm mate of mine, Aman. The guy is what I like to call a white guy trapped in an Indian’s body, but he seems to be well adjusted for all that. Though it took me awhile to get used to his tendency to go out wearing his pajama pants, but hey if the guy feels comfortable in them who the hell am I to judge? “Hey man,” he says to me in greeting as I nod back at him, “what are you up to?” “I’m gonna go try and try to forget everything that my psychology professor just told me,” I answer with a pained grin, “what about you?” “Figuring out where to party tonight,” he crows and I hypocritically roll my eyes. Aman and I both like to party roughly the same amount; he just puts more effort into finding out all the available parties than I do. “Yea, and what do you have?” I ask unable to keep a slightly eager tone out of my voice. Some might think that since I live at Rutgers University I should have no trouble finding parties, but that’s not entirely true. The house parties tend to more or less suck, either because of overcrowding or a lack of supplies. Meanwhile, every frat party requires at the very least a three to one girl to guy ratio, and most go up to five or even seven to one by the time midnight rolls around. Frankly, if I can find seven girls I’m going to have a private party in my dorm room. “We’ve got 20 Huntington, 35 Richardson, but what I really want to do tonight is sneak into TDX!” I wince at that last bit. TDX is a frat notorious for throwing awesome parties, but also for requiring six to one ratios to get into those parties no matter how early it is. Its one thing to sneak into a house party, as they are relatively easy targets and getting caught just means getting thrown out. On the other hand, frats are ridiculously difficult to sneak into, and drunken frat boys have no qualms against throwing a few punches as they throw intruders out. “Dude, are you sure about this,” I ask playing the unfamiliar role of the voice of reason, “there is going to be shit to pay if we get caught. The girls at house parties are usually just as eager to sleep with us as the girls at frat parties are, and it’s a lot easier to get in.” We begin to walk back towards our dormitory while we continue to argue over the pros and cons of sneaking into the TDX party. In the end we decide that an awesome party is worth the risk of getting our asses kicked. “By the way, whatever happened with Kate?” Aman asks, getting bored of the argument. I met Kate at a party where we…how to put this delicately…eh fuck it, she gave me a lousy hand job and I fingered her. However, I figured that if she was willing to do that on day one she was a girl worth calling on again. And before I start getting criticized on judging a girl on looks and willingness to put out, let me point out that I am in my fucking freshman year of college. Looks and a willingness to put out is pretty much the only criteria I use (and looks is a far second to willingness to put out). I don’t claim to be morally decent, simply honest. “Eh, that isn’t going anywhere,” I answer with a frown. “It’s a classic case of buyer’s remorse.” “Say what?” Aman raises a skeptical eyebrow as he opens the door to the dorm and steps inside. “Buyer’s remorse,” I repeat as I follow Aman inside. “Look me and the girl spent the night making out and all that. So, when I called her a couple of days after she thought I was just calling her because I thought she was easy. Which is completely true, but I don’t want her to know that.” Aman stares at me before bursting out in a gut wrenching laugh. “Y-You’ve been reading one of those fucking pick-up artist books!” He wheezes as my cheeks go a tad red. “The Game by Neil Strauss and The Venusian Arts by Mystery,” I answer coolly despite feeling just a mite embarrassed. “Hey man, picking up girls is a skill like any other, and I aren’t ashamed to try and improve it. “It’s really a lot simpler than you are making it out to be,” he sighs as we step into the elevator. “You find a girl, you talk to her, you give her a drink. Rinse and repeat five times, and you’re in the clear.” “You’re in for an accusation of statutory rape!” I retort as the elevator door closes. “Besides, what if she throws up on your dick during the blow job?” *Hours later* “Dude, I’m officially a piss poor sorry excuse of a pickup artist!” I announce to Sam, one of the three people who has stayed in the professional wrestling club. “This has to be the worst night I’ve had…well ever. Not only am I not getting laid, I can’t even get a fucking dance! “Relax man,” Sam replies sipping what the party claimed was beer. “For one thing there are at least twice as many penises here as there are vaginas. Second, the blood that’s drying on your shirt isn’t all that attractive.” I can’t quite manage to keep a smirk from forming on my face. As it turned out, the group that I tried to sneak into TDX with got caught immediately. The brothers of the frat told us to leave which we did, but one brother drunkenly called us “party-seeking losers” as we left. I decided to reply to his joke with a good old-fashioned punch to the nose, and then quickly ran away before the brothers could all gang up on me. Now, inherently I know that everything Sam just told me is true. The party is completely overcrowded, there are two guys hitting on every girl, and there is not enough beer to lower the girls’ inhibitions. But goddamnit I am Dan Black, girls should be fighting for the privilege to sleep with me! I’ve been told that girls like really confident guys…So I’ve been experimenting with that. “So, since we’re probably not going to get laid tonight,” Sam says casually, “how about we just go get shit-faced?” I raise an eyebrow at him, then just nod in agreement. There is a sacred hierarchy of things to do on weekends here at Rutgers, and all male college students follow them to the letter (assuming they don’t have girlfriends or other extenuating circumstances). First you try to get laid, if that fails you get wasted. If you can’t get wasted you play video games. At the end three of us end up at his dorm and raid his supply of vodka. Shot after shot is handed to me, and I down it without hesitation. At one point I realize that moving my head around makes some woozy sensation that is really fun. Finally we exhausted the supply of alcohol, but I’m the one who gets to take the final shot. I eagerly raise it to my lips, gulp it down and--- Why the fuck is my bed so hard? Oh wait a minute, I’m sleeping on a floor. I sit up, wincing as the sharp swords of a hangover pierce my brain. The guys who I drank with are spread out among the floor, though the bastard Sam is actually sleeping in his bed. I look at the clock, and realize that it is officially seven fifty in the morning. Somehow, through the pain, tiredness, and general hate for the entire world, I manage to remember that I am supposed to meet Bill. Every bone in my body is telling me to just blow him off and pass out again. At worst he’ll just stop training me, and in that case I just miss out on excruciatingly difficult and rigorous workouts. But for some reason, I get up and go. I slowly stumble towards the weight room, though the two minute walk took the ten minutes that I had. It is an interesting experience seeing what my campus looks like through bleary eyes, and a hangover that makes me want to cry. But it did leave me with one important idea that I’ll take with me forever. Hangovers fucking suck! I make it to the weight room just in time. Bill is standing there right in the middle of the room. He looks at me, studying me from top to bottom. As past I can I brace myself for the reprimand that is about to flow from his mouth, for daring to come to a workout in my state. When he finally spoke, his words were brief but powerful. “You have a penis on your arm.” I look, and find that there is a rather well-drawn penis drawn in black marker on my arm. Fucking bastards, I made sure to take my shoes off before I started drinking. “I’m proud of you kid,” he says as he walks past me, “now go pass out in your own bed.” “Wait what!” I blurt out in shock, “you’re proud of me? But I showed up completely hung-over!” “Yea but you showed up. At eight o’clock in the morning, on a Saturday, completely hung-over, you still showed up. Now I know you really want this, even if you still don’t.” “So what, this was just some of test?” I ask somewhat angrily. “Hell, it wasn’t enough that I voluntarily come and get my ass kicked by you every day, you had to play some sort of fucking game with me? Do you know how badly my head hurts right now? I could be asleep!” “Passed out on the floor does not count as sleeping,” Bill corrects me. “Look kid, you’ve got passion in you, the type that can lead to greatness. But you have no idea what to do with it, hell you don’t even know that you have it. All you know is that your desperate for something, hell anything that you can grab onto with both hands and just dedicate your life to. And it hurts that you haven’t found anything worth your time.” Oh my god, I really don’t need this Mr. Miyagi bullshit. I am wrestling because it’s kind of fun, and it beats going for a run every day. “Who the hell are you to say that about me? In fact, what makes you think you even know anything about me?” “Cause I am you,” he says to me seriously, “except I didn’t start randomly punching drunk frat bros until sophomore year. Now make sure to rest up today, I am going to make sure to put you through the ringer tomorrow for showing up so drunk today.” I stare at him with my mouth wide open and watch as he exits the room. The crazy bastard has no idea what he’s talking about. The only thing i have passion for is girl's with loose morals and easy to open legs. Now I’m just going to go find some painkillers then go back to bed before this hangover kills me.
