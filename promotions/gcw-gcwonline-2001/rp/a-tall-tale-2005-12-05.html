A Tall Tale

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 Dec 05 I am here to tell a tale. A tale of fantasy, adventure, heartbreak, and disappointment. In this tale a mystical warrior faces a great evil. Now you see this Mystical Warrior was loved by all, respected by many, and feared by some. He did all that he could to make sure the world would remain a good and wholesome place to live. He fought injustice, crime, and evil. He pulled out his enchanted sword day after day and night after night to destroy the forces of darkness. He had proven time and time again to be good enough to have the love and affection of the Gods. This warrior was a warrior like no other that was for certain. It was said that he could leap tall buildings, make fire with his eyes, shoot electricity from his hands, and could defeat a thousand men with his hand to hand skills. He has been well known for his victories on the greatest stages that war had ever been fought. He had become a legend and that was something that no one could take away from him. With the power of his people at his side, he felt that he could defeat anyone that would challenge the purity of his kingdom and would dare to try and shed any sort of darkness on their lovely lives. Sir Seymour Almasy was his name. On a cold winter night he was faced with yet another challenge. His kingdom was again threatened with Darkness as a former Overlord of the Sinister world known only as “Darque” came calling. This overlord was driven from his throne by a righteous leader named only as Spitfire. This Overlord knew the only way to take back his throne was to rebuild the army that he had stolen from him, and Sir Almasy’s kingdom was where his journey led him. Almasy under estimated the Overlord at first, sending his Army to face him first. Little did they know what the Dark one was capable of. He destroyed them piece by piece, one by one. He let their begs for mercy and their cries for help ring through the night skies and float to Almasy’s ears. LeStatt Knight was his name. His magical powers were incredible, only rivaled by his physical strength. He tore through that kingdom, killing innocent men, women and children until Almasy came down to face him. Almasy had awaited a REAL challenge for quite sometime. Seymour made his way to the center of the kingdom, where Knight was awaiting him. Their eyes met for the first time. “You have come all the way to my kingdom simply to get defeated by me Dark one!”” Almasy screamed out “Never,” LeStatt hissed with a smirk on his face. “It is time to rebuild MY Kingdom. Your defeat will merely be a stepping stone to reclaim my glory.” “I have these words before from many others Knight, you will fall just like the rest of them!” Almasy howls as he pulled his Enchanted sword from it’s holster. “I will show you what true evil can do.” And they battled. The Dark Overlord did his best and fought valiantly. The Good King fought with purpose and determination. It was a classic battle of Good Vs. Evil. Blood was shed, sweat dripped, and tears fell. Pain shot through both of their bodies with each blow from the other. Almasy swung his Enchanted sword with every last bit of energy he had and smashed into LeStatt sending him crashing to the snow and dirt underneath him. LeStatt stared into the night sky, unable to move, barely able to breath. Sir Almasy fell to one knee and drove his sword into the dirt, his breath erratic. He moves his eyes towards LeStatt. “You have lost Overlord.” He said and then took a deep breath. “Go now and leave us alone before I end your life.” Silence. “Did you not hear me!”” Almasy yells as he pushes himself to his feet and pulls his sword from the dirt. Silence. “Stubbornness can kill a man.” Almasy drags the blade behind him until he is hovering over LeStatt. Silence. “Have it your way Knight, just remember that I gave you your chance for survival.” Almasy lifts the sword and drives it into LeStatt’s chest. All was silent Good conquered over evil. This is a classic story, one that everyone knows is coming. Evil shall never defeat the purity of the light. Never will forces of Darkness prevail in such a battle, it is inconceivable. But what if I told you that this is merely a fantasy world? What if I told you that in reality The Good are always the ones who are laying in the dirt, staring at the night sky for the last time? What if I said that it is really the Evil and the wicked who claim victory because they are willing to go to ANY lengths to achieve their ultimate goals. They don’t care about rules, regulations, morals or principles. Their minds are on victory and that is it. To them popularity is NOTHING. Just as Almasy is loved by all, respected by many, and feared by some. Knight is loved by none, respected by many, and feared by ALL. In the battle of Good Vs. Evil only the one who is willing to go to that extra level really come out victorious. Statements like “nice guys finish last” are definitely true. In a fantasy world Good conquers evil, love always works out, and the cat never catches tweety bird. In the real world? Darkness rules, love is nothing more than wishful thinking and that fucking bird has been dead for years. The world holds on to their dreams and fantasies like a security blanket because they fear what is really right in front of them. It is now time to finally come out of your little dreams and fantasies. It is time to step into the real world. In the real world my tale ends like this? LeStatt Knight stands over a fallen and broken King. The King can not even walk as his own sword has been driven into his leg. The low rumble of thunder in the distance can be heard. Knight kneels down and digs his knee into the arm of the King. “You come around and preach of righteousness and doing good. You claim that you can defeat anyone.” Knight smiles. “Fuck righteousness.” LeStatt stands “I win.” Knight laughs “I always win.” And The Overlord drives his boot down into the face the Great King. This is my tale and you can listen or not. This is my tale and you can believe it or not. Remember my tale because I only speak the truth An idea can be wrong A dream can be shattered I aim to do neither today. Instead I will end a man’s Final Fantasy. -The End-
