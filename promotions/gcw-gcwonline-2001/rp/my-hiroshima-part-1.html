My Hiroshima, Part 1

undated

Date: September 1st/2nd 'I hate you, but I miss you ... I miss your feel.' My god. The woman is pure gold. Having not heard her voice in so long, I bask in the warmth of it. Her words pour over me and I find myself gripping my satin sheets with the might of a barbarian. I feel my nails digging into my palm. The pain isn't even real. 'How have you been? Are you alright?' Questions abound. She always loved subjecting me to interrogations. We often joked that in another life, she was a detective, playing 'Good Cop, Bad Cop' on a daily basis. Something petty and stupid, but it attracted a smile. Not many people made her smile. I had the good luck of always righting her moods. Not luck. There is no luck. Something else, then. Words escape me. I hear her sigh on the other end. Long and drawn out, it's like a missle being dropped on a peaceful town. My Hiroshima. I'm having a hard time concentrating. I was resting after the party with Jacob and the phone rang. Once I answered it, I was caught off guard. Her sultry, raspy voice seemed to echo across canyons, stirring a mix of feelings and emotions together like a witch's brew. A dash of elation. A sprinkle of remorse. Eye of lamentable regret. Spice of sadness. It's so many all at once, I can't even comprehend. A parade of feelings, all with their own special float. Words continue to evade as we listen to each other breathe. Hers is shallow, revealing her calmness with a neon sign. I swallow a sigh. How could she be calm? Did she not share my feelings? Sh**. Let me save myself further deliberation. I already know the answer. She's always been better at holding her composure. She's always been better at smoothing out the edges. When it comes to emotion, she has as tight of a hold on those as she does on me. 'Jacob told me you were back into wrestling.' I almost respond, but I bite my tongue. I taste the rustiness of blood. Jacob. I mouth his name to myself. What a drunken, coniving f***ing liar. Of course he told her. Why wouldn't he? They were friends even before she and I. I became friends with Jacob through her. Being complete opposites, however, I thought he could escape her tractor beam of seduction. Even he succumbed. She's the poison fruit you can't help but devour. ... What the hell am I supposed to do now? What, for f*ck's sake, am I supposed to say? I can barely breathe, let alone speak. I'm thrown way off my railing and all I can manage is erratic inhalation. I feel like I'm going to suffocate. I lay back in my bed, letting my head fall gently against the pillow. I'm sweating more now than I was after my dream about being a gun for hire. I long for that, almost, as I shove the sheets away from my legs. They're beginning to stick to me. As I lay, I pray. For unconsciousness. The unexplainable. Anything. I think I'm afraid. Afraid of what? Her. Rebecca Hendley. Her smile. Her voice. I'm afraid to face her because of ... regret, probably. I made myself miserable before and I know it was pushing her limits. She never would admit that. F***. Right now, hatred consumes. I'm hating everything. Life. Wrestling. The world. I feel like I'm having a mental breakdown. My reactor core is melting. And all because of a phone call. It started with 'Hi.' I haven't even said anything, but I feel like I've said too much. Whispered too many secrets. ... I contemplate hanging up. I immediately think better of it. That'll bring about even more problems. I think it can't get any worse? Ha. If I hang up that phone, I sever one of the last remaining pieces of my life that keeps me grounded. And despite everything happening now, the confusion, sadness, hatred - despite it all, Rebecca is my refuge; I crawl into my thoughts of her like I would a cave and sleep eternities. 'Rei ...' My hair is matted to my forehead, black and red spikes now limp with sweat. I take the phone away from my ear and swallow, sharp pains of cotton mouth almost arousing a cough. I'm still dressed. My thoughts drifting somewhere else for a moment. Faded jeans with a fit for comfort and a gray t-shirt depicting a monkey pointing to his head and the word, 'Brains', centered above. I snicker, more out of desperation than anything. High above me, my ceiling sparkles from the moonlight pouring in through open blinds. Beauty that rivals the stars. Something ... 'Beauty that rivals the stars.' Rebecca says that as she replaces another ceiling tile. I admire her from below, one hand clutching a glass and the other stroking her thigh. She giggles and kicks my hand away. 'Stop! You know that distracts me!' That I do. She's adding some flair to my ceiling tiles, as she says. Pouring chunks of glitter in specific places to simulate the night sky. I have to admit, it looks nice. I smile innocently. She grins, her beauty like an angel as she looks down at me. 'You're deceitful, Rei. Ever dream of being a politician?' I watch her replace another ceiling tile, snickering as dust and insulation shower her. 'Not really. Besides, being a politican requires no conscience and a wife to secretly do all the work.' I catch her warm smile and manage as best of one as I can. Letting her think I'm hinting at marriage, I let it linger for a bit longer. Ah, sweet release. 'You're definitely not capable.' I laugh at her expression. She jumps down from the bed, trying to knock me to the floor. I nearly give in as she distorts her smile to a frown, but I'm quick to get away from her clutches. I am a wrestler, after all. The indepedent circuit may not grant me much clout, but I'm still crawling. Haven't gotten my wings. 'You think you're a hotshot, Rei? I've got a secret weapon, jackass.' We're turning circles around each other, low to the ground and slapping away grapple attempts. Amateur wrestling. The basics of the ring. I've actually taught her quite a bit. She's taken me down a few times when I was actually trying to win. Positively amazing, I think to myself. How I ever ended up with her in my life, I'll never truly know. I slap the backs of her hands as she feigns a charge. 'Whatever your secret weapon is, it is no match for my skill.' Right then, she pulls her shirt slowly up and yanks it off. No bra. Nothing. Bare, beautiful skin. I'm caught off guard, obviously. 'Girl gone wild!' Speared in the stomach and on the ground. Breath exhaled too fast for comfort. I smile, regardless. Slowly sliding her body across and on top of mine, she inches her face closer. My breathing restricts, again. She smiles seductively, running her palm up my torso. I am Rei. Getting a local television broadcasting company to film my home pornography. She tickles her fingers around my face, drawing invisible lines. Then it happens. She grabs my ear and drives a knee forcefully into my groin. 'Give up or I'll make you ashamed to ever wear spandex, again.' I smile and laugh. I am beaten. 'Fine. I submit to your will.' She laughs and helps me up. I brush the dust from her hair and face, removing a long cobweb strand from her nape. Disgusted, she knocks it away from my hand and tucks her semi-nude body into mine. I wrap my arms around her and pass away the afternoon. Not thinking. Not worrying. I'm someone's personal teddy bear. ... Driven back to the present. The phone is slipping out of my hands and I grab it tightly. Listening. Dead air. The silence is louder than San Diego, making my ears ring uncomfortably. I wonder if she hung up. I glance at the digital clock and notice it blinking 2:25 A.M. Dammit! No telling how long I was day dreaming. Reminiscing. For a while, I just lay there, phone pressed to my ear. I finally notice the pain in my palm and the sting on my tongue. How my ear hurts from pressing the phone against my skull so hard. I was trying to crawl through the telephone lines and signals to get to her. I imagine her again as I saw her that day and I can smell her. Taste her. The texture of her milky skin as my hands explore her best kept secrets. Bitter contrast. My skin rough and imperfect. The skin of a man whose daily life was reaming with violence. Hers, innocent and without scars. And when I'm about to hang up, she stirs my blood, again. 'I love you, Rei.' Click ... Five minutes pass before I decide to hang up. 'If you'd like to make a call, please ha-' Click My phone beeps. I glance down at the display. Over an hour of conversation and hardly anything was said. I shake my head. I watch as the moonlight slowly fades, obscured by forming clouds. In darkness, I let my mind wander. The night will be dreadfully long.
