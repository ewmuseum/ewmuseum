One step closer to the truth

8 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/08/06 When we last left Claudia, she was leaving the office of Mr. Dunham, the private investigator she hired to find her birth parents. She found out that her mother’s maiden name was Ryan and that she was from Ireland. She also found out that her father was from England and probably had something to do with the death of her mother, but his identity was still yet unknown. So now Claudia is in Stockholm, with Draven. They’ve got themselves a very posh hotel suite and are taking advantage of a little down time. We find them inside the suite. Draven is spread out on the bed, napping lightly. Claudia is just getting out of the shower. Her lithe body is wrapped in a towel and her long black hair is up in another towel. She knows that travel has been hard on Draven lately and decides that she’ll let him continue to nap while he has the chance. She sits down to the laptop that they always carry with them and accesses her personal email account, the private one, not the one that the GCW gives out to all the fans. She has two new messages, one of which is from Mr. Dunham. “Hot damn, I hope he’s found a lead on my father.” She double clicks the email and waits for it to open. She quickly scans the email and is satisfied with what she sees. Dear Mrs. Lox, While we are still unsure of your father’s name, we do know that he owns an establishment somewhere in London, most likely a pub that is frequented by known crime families. We are closing in on him quickly. We’ll know his name soon enough. Regards, Dunham. Claudia thought twice about waking Draven. She knew that he had recently found his father, ironically, living in London and running a pub. While she didn’t know if he was in with crime families, she knew that she never felt comfortable about Adidas Oliver, Draven’s father. “I bet that scum bag could tell me who my father is, even if he didn’t know he was my father. It seems like the type he’d rub elbows with.” If only she knew. She knew Draven had another match this week, but this time it was against some bitch dressed like Pocahontas, no offense to Dani. Dani always looks natural in her garb, however this hoe looks more like a stripper on theme night at the Bunny Ranch. She knew Draven had qualms about hitting girls, but she’d assure him that this was no lady, so there shouldn’t be anything to worry about. Draven would drop her on her head just like he had all the others.
