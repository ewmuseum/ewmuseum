The Cat and the Fiddle ( part 2)

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.26.2005 A strong but slender arm lifts to brush the sweat off her brow as Eva heaves a sigh not two hours later while digging through the attic. It is late fall and still the heat from the house rose to create a virtual sauna in the highest points of the house. Having successfully swiped away the beads of perspiration she digs back in shuffling boxes and crates around. Keepers are placed to the left and the toss away are to the right. Glancing over at J.B. she merely wrinkles her nose at the lazy bum and chuckles. ' Laying down on the job again? Lazy bum. You are supposed to be helping.' Casually he raises one lid to stare at her lazily before flopping to his side and ignoring her bitchy little pet. Shaking her head Eva goes to continue to look through things but stops herself staring at the crate her companion is lounging on. Tilting her head curiously, the diva moves to it plucking him up much to his chagrin and despositing him on the floor next to her. With the lid now free of his obstruction she undoes the latches and swings the lid upwards to stare inside. There before her eyes is something she had almost forgotten about. Reverantly hands dip inside retrieving the object that has her in awe. A gentle nostalgic smile traces her lips as she turns with the case in hand and looks at J.B. ' Well I'll be damned J.B. I haven't seen this since I was.....twenty?' In her hands is onyx case the shape of a violin or fiddle. Setting herself on the floor she places it before her. Nimble fingers flick both the locks on it and hands lift the lid to stare at the instrument within. It is still as beautiful as she remembers it being. The violin is an antique, a little over one hundred years old to be exact and a one of a kind. The shell is made of black ash, a unique would that is dark in color with paler white veins strewn through it. This particular piece was stained a deep crimson giving the vein work more a look of blood. It had been a piece her aunt-mom had given her at the age of fifteen when she announced her desire to learn to play. The German heiress reaches for it only to stall those outstretched fingers curling in hesitation as she is undecided. There is a reason she stopped playing. That reason's name is Deitri. The first man to betray her heart. With a deep breath she moves to close the lid, but instead is met with the head of J.B who is standing in her way suddenly. Pulling back she stares at him with a peculiar expression as he simply purrs and rubs his head along the prized musical instrument as if to say ' go ahead and take it. I want to hear you play.' Eva simply shakes her head. ' No. I can't.' He stares and rubs on it some more. ' I said no.' This goes on for a few minutes before once again the cat wins. Eva can not help but wonder if she has really gone as soft as some have mentioned they believe her to be. A hefty sighs escapes her lips as she reaches down and pulls it free from its home. Next the bow was taken up as she gives J.B. one last imploring look only to recieve a stern stare back. With a nod she places the piece on her shoulder, cradling it with her chin and slides the bow across the strings for a sound test. The first sound is hideous. Wincing she mumbles. ' It has been seven years. I don't know if I can even play anymore.' She could play. In fact she will play. Closing those baby blues Eva allows her heart and mind to wander back to the thing that caused her want for the instrument in the first place. Her mind fills with the haunting, yet invigorating sounds of Russian Gypsy music and before she is aware of it...Eva is playing along with the memory. The rod slides back forth as fingers dance over the strings drawing forth thoe musical notes that have the potential to lift even the most dire of spirits. At first the song is a slower tempo, soothing, uplifting for the spirt. Then as she continues to play it becomes quicker, more invigorating lighting a passion long thought lost in her being. She plays for hours, having lost herself in the music of her heart. This is the music of her soul. That thing which used to drive her and, if all indicates correctly, still does. Finally she withdraws the bow and looks at J.B who is staring at her intently...almost smuggly. The sensation rocks her soul as, again, she gets the uncanny feeling there is more to this cat than meets the eye. The violin and bow are now placed back safely with in their home as she glances at her watch and chuckles. ' Guess what time it is J.B.?' He turns his head away. ' That's right big guy, time for our little trip to town.' Before he can protest she scoops him up and heads out of the attic. Cat in one arm, violin case in the other. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ' Okay Ms. VanLorne. I need you to fill out this form as much as you can. Please make sure you indicate what you wish for your cat to recieve. ' Eva shuffles J.B. from one arm to the other as she grabs the clip board and tries to figure out how to do this without letting him go. Already she is wearing several stratch marks from the unhappy male. Typical male, someone threatens their manhood and they have to get violent. Luckily for Eva, the vet assistant takes pity on her. ' I can take him for you and put him in a carrier. You may want to purchase one. It makes traveling with him alot easier.' Eva looks up with a grin. ' You have any here for purchase?' ' Yes ma'am.' ' Fine, I will take the best you have to offer.' Within a couple of minutes J.B. is secured inside his new carrier and making quite the ruckus about it. Eva merely scowls at him as she fills out the form to the best of her knowledge and ability. ' Pipe down J.B. it isn't the end of the world.' ' YYYEEEEOOWWWWLLL' How quaint. The vet hasn't even started the prodding and probing and already J.B. is whining like a child. Eva scoffs at him making note of the similarities between he and Johnny Borealis before Johnny finally took the hint and stopped hitting on her. Placing the clipboard on the counter Eva asks the all important question. ' So how long will the procedure take?' ' They are usually an hour maybe two long. ' ' So I can pick him up tonight then?' ' No ma'am. You can pick him up tomorrow provided all goes well. We keep them for at least twelve hours after the procedure. It would be best if you came by after three p.m. tomorrow to collect him.' Eva scowls, gnawing on her lower lip slightly. That would be cutting it close...too close. She had a flight to catch tomorrow evening to make it for NC 17. Rubbing her temples Eva looks at the assistant once again. ' Alright, will he be okay by himself for a few days?' ' Possibly though I would reckomend you keep an eye on him. The stitches will itch and of course he will be aching. Animals have a nasty habit of trying to pull them out.' ' I see. Alright then..will he be okay to travel with me then? I have someplace to be tomorrow evening.' ' I don't see why not. We will provide you with everything you need to keep him as comofortable as possible.' ' Thank you.' Semi-relieved Eva leans down and places a finger inside the carrier lightly scratching behind J.B.'s ear as the girl at the desk runs the numbers for total cost. J.B. was not interested. Eva couldn't blame him. ' Three hundred fourty two dollars and seventy seven cents is the total cost. Will this be cash or charge?' ' Charge.' Eva produces her credit card passing it over. The charge is made and before she can even rethink this whole ordeal the carrier is taken back. J.B. is going to face his doom..so to speak. Anxious Eva dwells long enough for the assistant to come back. ' Take care of him.' ' We always do. We will call you when the procedure is done and let you know how things went.' ' Thank you.' ' You're welcome Ms. VanLorne.' Nodding Eva turns and heads for the door only to be stopped by the assistant one final time. ' Ms. VanLorne?' Eva turns. ' Yes?' ' Good luck in your match tomorrow night. I am keeping my fingers crossed for you.' The comment catches Eva by surprise. All this time and the woman had never given away she knew who Eva was. It was refreshing to know that she stayed professional the entire time. Eva smiles gratefully. 'Thanks.' ' You know naming the cat Johnny Borealis and then having him nuetered almost seem cruel...in a justified way.' Eva chuckles. ' Let's just say. I can't legally nuetered the real Johnny B. but I can do it symbolically.' Winking Eva turns and heads out the door leaving the assistant to her laughter. Look out Johnny, you may be next.
