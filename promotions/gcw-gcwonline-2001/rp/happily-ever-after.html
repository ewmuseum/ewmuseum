Date: Early Morning Back together again. Back together again. When we make up, You help take up That empty space in my heart when we're Back together again. We'll be here till the end. I'll sing. I'll soar. I'll ring. I'll roar. Back together again. I suppose I should be saying that Kate and I are in the process of living happily ever after. And we are, but in our own way. At times I really, honestly think that she's too damn smart for her own, and our own, good. She's too careful. Too afraid to just jump in and take a chance. But...then again...I mean, last time she took a chance, I kinda...did what I did. And wasted about six years of each of our lives. We're not yet sleeping with each other. Which is fuckin killing me. But, at the same time, I'd be kind of disappointed if we were. There'd be something about the eagerness that she took me back with...something about her just jumping right back to me like she forgives me for all the pain and all the confusion with no questions asked. That'd be insulting to both of our intelligences. ...not that it's not eating away at me not to sleep with her. Cuz it is. 'I'm getting fat, Johnny.' And comments like that make me almost spit out my first cup of coffee for the day. It's something about waking up next to the woman you love...that is, to say, after not having sex last night, and the first words you hear out of her mouth all night are about how she doesn't think she's attractive. When that right there is insulting to my...well, to my ability to tell whether or not Kate's gorgeous. And she is gorgeous. Hence me wanting to sleep with her. See? It's a pretty easy formula to follow. 'You're...getting fat?' 'I'm still not back down to where I was before I had Sarah. And that was five years ago, Johnny! This isn't just pregnancy lastings here. I'm getting a pot belly.' Easy smile. 'Pot bellies are sexy. I should punch you in your pot belly.' And she doesn't look happy in the slightest. A bit mad. A bit confused. Not a bit happy, though. 'First of all, you don't tell me that I'm getting a pot belly. That's rule number one. Secondly, they're not sexy.' 'Jesus, Kate. How long's it been since you've seen Pulp Fiction?' I knew before I said it that it was a bad idea. I'm that guy at the office that remembers abstract movie quotes, but can't remember people's birthdays or anniversaries. I'll throw out lines that have little rememberance value or addition to the plot to a movie that came out in the early 90's and I'll expect people to just be on the same page as me right away. It's a curse. It really is. 'I'm serious. I feel...gross.' Now, I know what not to do here. First rule about when a woman tells you she thinks is getting fat is by all means to never, ever, ever agree with her. I believe she's smart enough to know I was kidding about the whole pot belly thing, but this is still a tough pond to skate on. Second rule is not to get into an argument, telling them that they aren't fat. That's just asking for trouble, because then they start pulling out cards like, 'You'd love me even if I was morbidly obese!' to which you have to agree, or you've opened a whole new can of worms. Third rule is to never, ever, ever change the subject to try to bring the pity party to you. If she picks up on it, which Kate would, only causes a cataclysm of backlash as she turns evious of your metabolism and ability to have fat stay away from your thighs. And then we're back to square one. From experience, while there is not a good way out of this, I've found that constructive solutions to her problem, which you make clear you don't believe in, is the best way out. 'I think you're paranoid, Kate. But if you want to be paranoid, I'd be happy to take you to the gym with me when I work out. I usually do about six hours or so, more if I'm actually going for something. I'm sure we can get you toned up tighter than a drum.' 'I don't want to go to the gym, Johnny.' '...okay... I suppose we can start cutting back on the ready-made meals. The Boston Market and canned soup and restaurants can take a back seat to official, home-style cooking.' 'I hate cooking, Johnny. And you're terrible at it.' 'Well...you can cut back on the chocolate?' 'But I love chocolate.' '...so you think you're getting fat...but you're not gonna do anything about it?' 'Well what can I do?' 'I just listed three things! There are dozens more!' 'But I don't want to do any of them.' Then stop bitching to me about it! God! Fucking women! 'I don't get why you do this.' She turns to me, innocent as a lamb, with wide brown eyes that I could get lost in and her lips parted just enough that I can see her teeth shining behind them. Truly sexy. Incredibly sexy. Something I can barely take because it's heart-achingly gorgeous. 'What do I do?' 'You torment yourself, Kate! You're never happy with yourself in the slightest. I mean, you're the most amazing woman I've ever known, you're incredibly intelligent, and you've got a look that people would kill for! You're successful, sexy, and strong! And it's never good enough for you.' 'You think I torment myself too much? This coming from the professional wrestler!' ...fuck! See? You didn't believe me when I told you she was smart. God damn, she is. Able to turn an entire argument against someone without their even seeing it coming. Yeah...she's got that power too. And frankly, it's too early in the morning for us to be doing this. I'm still working on my first cup of coffee, and she's got her mind set that she's going to have a bad day. Which means that there's nothing I can do to make her have a good day. All I can do is hope that she runs into one of her old friends from high school or one of her gay guy friends that make her forget everything. The way I should be able to, but for some reason it never works that way with me. ...sigh... *** We've been sitting in relative silence for about five minutes. And it's eating me alive. Mainly because I know this is just causing brooding. I know this is just causing her to convince herself of her point more and more and more and in doing so, she's convincing herself that I'm wrong. Something that is never good for business for me. And so, she decides to say something. Jumping both feet into the situation. Like I said she should. 'I think you'd look good in pink.' '...excuse me.' 'A pink polo shirt. I think it'd look good on you. Besides, it's in now. There's something about a guy wearing pink that is just really...brave.' 'No. Bret Hart wearing pink is one thing. Marty McFly wearing a pink shirt is one thing. Your normal guy off the street needs to stay away from it. Because the way it's going now, it's turned into just a statement that says, 'Look at me! I'm wearing pink and I'm a guy and I've got so many balls!' The best ones, of course, are the pink T-shirts that say 'PINK SHIRT' on them. It's like wow, way to be a fucking moron and flaunt how fucking brave you think you are. Short answer, no. I'm not wearing a pink polo shirt, a pink shirt, a pink jacket, or even a pink condom! Thanks, though.' 'What do you wear?' I look down at my bathrobe and wonder what she's talking about. 'What are you talking about?' 'To the ring. What do you wear down to the ring? Like, are you one of those...lucha...lucha-somethings? The guys with the masks. Or is it the tights?' Part of me is so shocked I drop my spoon. It clangs with the audible sound of my heart dropping into my lap. Jaw gaping wide. For a moment, I don't even know where I am. '...I wear black jeans...and black boots... You've never... You've never watched WorldWide?' 'No.' A quick shake of the head to clear up the ears. Maybe the processors in my brain just aren't at full capacity. Maybe I'm dreaming still. 'In all the time you've known me...known what I do...you've never watched a single night of the action?' 'Well, I know that you're, what? Like, the State Champion. Right?' 'US Champion. Thanks, hun. I feel so fantastic right now.' 'Well, wrestling's not my thing! How can you blame me? Besides, can you even name one book that I've edited?' This is going to take a minute. ... ... Oh God. This does not bode well for our hero. '...no.' The two of us let out a collective sigh. And I look over at Kate and wonder. I just wonder what would happen if this time, I left and didn't come back. Not two weeks after rekindling, and I'm already considering saying, 'Fuck it!' But there's something keeping me here. Something keeping me with this woman. And I'm not sure if it's pride or love or the child that I promised I'd help raise... But hopefully today the dice will roll and it'll improve for me. Maybe this bus to Shitsville will veer off course. Maybe...
