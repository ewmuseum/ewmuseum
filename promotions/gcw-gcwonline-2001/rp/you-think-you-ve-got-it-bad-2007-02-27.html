You Think You've Got It Bad?

27 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27Feb2007 So, it seems that Markus Stone has been a little hectic lately. It seems that perhaps he isn't quite tuned in to what he really wants in GCW. On the one hand, he layed out Scott Nielson following said superstar's debut victory, then he layed out UNITE following their victory. Unknown to most, except those who are able to catch Markus unaware, Markus is in fact fighting constantly. Battling a Voice in his head. Sure, it's something that some people might blow that off. How often has it been said that a wrestler is crazy? And how often does that really seem to affect that wrestler? Not very often, does it? Well, truth be told, those who really due suffer from some mental disease or another really are affected. Imagine waking up every single day, and just laying in bed. No, not because you're lazy, or because you are comfortable. What if you lay in bed every single day, saving every second that you stay there, because you know that as soon as the day starts is when everything goes downhill? You lay in bed hoping that maybe today won't be as bad as yesterday was, because maybe that Voice won't be there today. As soon as you do finally get up, that weight that you carry settles itself on your shoulders, and suddenly everything seems just a little more bleak. Sunny day? Whoopdy-fuck. Rainy day? At least it's not sunny. And what about yourself? You feel like shit. Maybe you are crazy, but when will you go and get help? Never, because you are in an impossible and hopeless situation. Nobody can help you, even if you wanted it. Just imagine this for a little bit, then top it all of by constantly burying all the emotion that threatens to overwhelm you so that you can make it through another day. _____________________________________________________________ 7:46am The alarm has been beeping for the past six minutes, but Markus hasn't rolled over to shut it off yet. He just lays in his hotel bed, staring at the ceiling. How quickly the morning arrives when you don't want it to. Markus groans and stretches, then finally rolls over and shuts off the alarm. Slow to rise, and slow to get dressed, Markus drags ass all over the hotel room. It's just past 8:30 when he is finally dressed and ready to leave. But where to go? Cleveland, Ohio. Not a place that Markus has ever really cared for. He's been to this city a few times in his wrestling career. Actually closer to a few dozen. Used to be Markus would hit up a local bar and drink up until he was close to passing out. Now Markus doesn't really drink anymore. Too many demons, and too many bad memories that come back when you drink. Well, when you're done crying about how horri--- "Not today!" Markus yells at the ceiling, clutching his hands to his head. It takes a lot of effort, but Markus is able to shove the Voice down. It is a mentally draining act, and one that he is not always successful at. The reward is worth the effort. Silence. "Thank God." God? Probably not. Markus was never overly religious anyways, and the phrase holds no lingering meaning with him. Cleveland, Ohio. Two hundred and fifty miles from Markus' old home. A home he shared with a woman he loved. Two hundred and fifty miles from Cincinnati. Two hundred and fifty miles from Bethesda North Hospital, where Maria still works. Maria. "Bitch." One word says enough. What more is there to say about a woman who has another man's child, then tries to claim it as yours? And yet, feelings still linger. Feelings that are quickly shoved back down. Markus shakes his head, forcing the memories to scatter before they take form. He looks back at the clock, 8:46. One hour now, and he has accomplished nothing. "Well, let's see if Cleveland has changed any." Soon Markus is walking out the front door of the lobby, wearing a brand new GCW Banned and Exiled~! hoodie, on sale now at retailers everywhere, or order online at . Cold and cloudy, this day is perfect. Markus soon begins wandering, hood pulled over his head and eyes staring at the ground. He doesn't know where he's going, and isn't really interested in a destination, either. Funny how that goes. Of course, being back in Cleveland does bring back old memories. One in particular stands out, and before Markus realizes where his feet are leading him, he is standing in front of a craptacular house just outside of downtown Cleveland. Markus glances at his watch, 10:52. Without thinking about what he is doing, Markus walks up the porch steps and knocks on the door. Moments lady a petite forty-something lady answers the door. "Is Tony here?" The lady looks at Markus curiously, then motions for him to come in. "I didn't realize he had any friends anymore." She leaves the question hanging in the air. "Old acquaintance, I'm in town on business. Thought I'd stop by." She seems to accept this answer, and leads Markus to the living room. She motions for him to sit on the couch. "I'll go get him." Markus sits down and waits. He can hear bits and pieces of an argument, before a man in a wheelchair is rolled into the room. "Why, hello Tony." Markus stands to greet this man, this old 'friend.' Anthony Starr, formerly known in the ring as Super Starr, looks up at Markus, baffled. "Wha... What are you doing here?" What a greeting. It's been almost seven years since they've been in contact. The tone of the question would imply that it was Markus' fault, when it was in fact Anthony who severed all ties with Markus. "GCW. You still keep up on wrestling, don't you?" Anthony shakes his head. "Only thing I've ever watched was my last match." Markus sits back down on the couch, not sure what to say to that. After all, it was his best friend who paralyzed Anthony. Chris Storm executed a picture perfect piledriver, too perfect, in fact, on Super Starr. It ended his wrestling career, and the hopes of any career he could ever hope for. Besides wheelchair salesman. Markus grins at this thought. "Oh, you find it funny?" Markus quickly stops smiling. "No, not that. It's just... It's been a long time Tony." The lie slips easily through his teeth. Markus could care less about this man. So why is he here? "Well, it seems like life has been quite generous to you. I see you are still 'standing tall' in the wrestling world." Anthony nearly spits these words out. "Well, it's not as easy as you seem to think. I've got some injuries myself." Now it was Anthony's turn to smile, but not out of any amusement. "Oh, well please, tell me just how horrible your life really is Markus. How sad it must be to endure walking around in pain every single day. At least you can walk!" Well, that much was certainly true. "Actually, Tony, I didn't come here to enjoy a verbal sparring match with you. Quite frankly, you are in no position to even talk to me like that." Well, maybe the perfect position. It wouldn't be too hard to beat up a cripple. What better way to vent your frustrations at life, than by taking it out on one of life's rejects? "What do you want then?" Well, a straight question deserves a straight answer, right? "I'm not really sure. I just happened to be in the neighborhood." Maybe not. "Oh, so then you'd just drop by and see dear old crippled Johnny, huh? Thanks, but no thanks." Now would be the time for Anthony to turn and wheel his ass off, but for some reason he stays, staring at Markus. "Well, pretty much." Markus smiles at Anthony, and after a moment, he smiles back. "Julie! Get us some drinks!" So maybe today won't be so bad after all. Wait, wait a minute, Markus doesn't drink. "Wait, I quit." Anthony looks at him in shock. "Not today." Well, on second thought. Hey, you try saying no to a cripple. Not much longer, and the two men are feeling more than a little buzzed. No longer dealing with the uncomfortable walls built between them, they are talking like the old days. Before wrestling, before WIW, and before Chris Storm. "Do you remember Molly Thompson?" Markus very much did remember her. What a night. "Oh man, what a fuckin' porker!" Everybody has to lower their standards sometimes. "Yeah, but that didn't stop you!" Yep, lowered standards. "Or you!" Yep, everybody. "Yeah, but I didn't cum in two minutes." And sometimes there is a reason why you have to lower your standards, word gets around you know. "Oh shit man. I haven't thought about those days in a long time. I'm glad I walked over here Tony." As soon as the sentence leaves his mouth a silence settles over the room. "Oh shit Tony, I didn't mean---" What can you say to fix something like that? "Yeah, yeah. Why did you come here any way? It sure as fuck wasn't to share a drink with the man you left to get crippled by your new buddy." And there goes the first blow. "Well, you're right. If I were looking for that guy, I wouldn't be here. As I remember, you stopped returning my phone calls, 'Super Starr,' not the other way around." And now it's back to square one. "So what? I wouldn't have let you get maimed by some asshole with a God complex!" Now what? What do you do when you best friend is insulted by the man who turned his back on you for fame? "Yeah, maybe not. But I also didn't turn my back on you. You deserved it, and you earned it." "This? You fuck!" Well, maybe Markus was being a little harsh. "Tell me something Tony, one question." Markus tries calming things down a bit, if only to find an answer to his question. He waits for a moment, and Anthony finally nods his head. "What?" Seems like arguing tooks it toll on him. Markus quickly picks up on this quickly. "How do you deal with it? How do you make it through every day like this?" And suddenly Markus realizes why he came here. "How? Are you kidding? I don't have a choice. I can't fire this bitch, and can't stop her from shoving food down my throat. I can't even control my shit Markus! If I could, I'd have been dead six years ago. I make it through each day because I don't have a choice, not because I want to." Markus nods his head, seeming to undertand. In fact, he wasn't listening after 'I don't have a choice.' He's weak! He is a weakling, a pathetic piece of trash! Markus agrees completely. Before Anthony Starr can even blink, Markus leaps into the air and drives his knee into his former friend's face. Tony's broken body can do nothing to prevent his wheelchair from tipping over. "You piece of shit. You deserve this, and you can't even accept it. You don't deserve to have gotten off as easy as you did. Chris should have killed you when he had the chance." Markus crouches down and grabs Anthony by his hair, tilting his head back. "Still awake, I see. I'll help you with that." Without another word Markus begins slamming Anthony's face into the ground over, and over, and over again. The thuds soon give way to a wet smacking noise, complemented with a group of sickening crunching noises. Markus stands up and takes a step back. "You are weak Tony, you always were." The kick the Markus threw at Anthony's face would have put Jason Elam to shame. As Markus leaves the house, he has a smile on his face. You see, when you realize that other people are suffering, it really doesn't help. But when you see other people so overtaken by their suffering that they shrivel up, it tends to swell you ego. Markus does not question that he suffered more than Anthony Starr, and the fact that he endures every single day makes Markus a stronger person than 'Super Starr' could have ever hoped to be. On the walk back to his hotel, Markus begins thinking about GCW. How many others in this company alone feel that they suffer? How many will Markus have to destroy to prove his point? Worldwide, live in Cleveland, Ohio. Markus Stone is not scheduled to wrestle, but that doesn't mean that he won't make an impact. Or end careers. Looking at Anthony Starr... Well, maybe Banned and Exiled~! have a reputation to maintain?
