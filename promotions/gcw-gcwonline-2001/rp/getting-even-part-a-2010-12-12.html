Getting Even, Part A

12 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/12/10 The first part of an RP series I intend to wrap up at Dangerous Games. Take your time ladies, this is what I like to call... a slow burn. GETTINGEVEN "Deliberate violence is more to be quenched than a fire." Heraclitus, 483 B.C. CHAPTERI Days After Dangerous Games… Daniel Francisco sits inside an empty subway car on his way home from the gym. His head bobbles with each twist and jerk of grinding steel as the car follows the tracks toward wherever the Hell it’s heading. The squealing and piercing shriek of metal guiding metal is enough to drive someone insane, which is why Danny chooses to plug his ears with a pair of Rastafarian colored SkullCandy earbuds. He tucks his head underneath the hood of his sweatshirt and stuffs his hands into his pockets. His head leans back against the window and begins to dive into the music. He shuts his eyes as his arm clutches his gym bag to his side. El fuckin’ Moreno… we meet again amigo viejo. From the very beginning, it’s been me and you, two rookies trying to make our mark on the GCW. Looks like we’ll do the damn thing again at WorldWide 141… the first night of many on our way to the grandest stage of them all… NC-17. I’ll have to put all the past shortcomings behind me and-- Danny opens his eyes when he hears the click of the metal latch and watches the car door open, allowing three rough motherfuckers to stroll into Danny’s car. They all laugh at a joke one of them made before (likely about another one’s mother). Each of the group is tattooed, pierced, and wearing black leather jackets. Two of these frightening characters have torn their sleeves off. The patch on their back – a snake slithering around a flaming skull – is what gives Dan the idea that they might like to party. "Sup fellas?" he slaps a cunning smile on his face. "Are ya lost?" Danny offers an almost real but obviously fake smile and stands to his feet. He leans against the steel pole instead of holding onto it and seems to be staking his territory. The tall bald one looks at the short stocky one, who turns around and looks at the one with the long blond ponytail. Danny taps the back of his wrist, implying that he would like an answer sometime tonight. The bald one snaps his gaze back to Danny, "No, we ain’t fuckin’ lost. We know exactly where we are. Our car. So you got ten seconds to get your shit and beat it, bitch." Danny chuckles. The man with the ponytail shoots ahead but is wrangled by the short one, putting his thick trunk of an arm out of his friend’s chest. He watches Danny, who can’t help but rev up the blond man’s engine by flipping him the middle finger. "Fuck you, losers," Danny is blunt if nothing else. "I’ll do whatever I like. In fact, you three should probably leave if you want to keep your cute, matching leather jackets. I need to take a shit and that one looks like it might hold just the right amount of the Mexican I ate for lunch. The other look like they’ve been used for wiping before. Your patches, I mean." A fire ignites in all three of their eyes. Danny cracks his knuckles and rolls his neck till it pops. A pleased smile spreads over his face. "Was it something I said?" The bald one takes a swing just as Danny ducks ahead, punching the first contestant square in the ribs with a snap right hook. The air rushes out from the big man’s gut and buckles him over, giving the bad boy from Buffalo the perfect opportunity to realign this dumb fuck’s jaw. "Say goodnight." Danny rears his fist back and lunges forward, spinning on the front of his back foot and unleashes all of his weight with one, violent right uppercut. The big dude’s eyes light up before he gurgles briefly and then lets out a muffled, high pitch groan. He staggers back, cupping his hands over his chin and face. He pulls his hands back and every other person in the car sees what Danny knew from the second his knuckles made contact. "Frank, you’re bleeding!" the blond man quivers. "Is that…" The short man looks closer and then gasps, "You’re tongue!" The big man’s brow folds in fear, continuing to hold his mouth. The two healthy rebels look to each other and then to Danny… who thinks he’s Dynasty of the Subway right now. He opens his palms to the ceiling, frowning. "Does anybody else want me to hit their mute button?" the young GCW rebel snickers. The short man pulls out a bandana and hands it to Frank. He looks on at Danny in ultimate terror, afraid of what this dude can do if he has a few minutes, one on one. Danny winks at Frank. The blond man steps forward, removes his jacket and stretches out his shoulders. "I got no fucking clue who you are…" he pulls out a switchblade. "I really don’t give a fuck." Danny’s eyes catch the reflection of the light on the blade just as his next contestant swipes the blade at him. He swings back, shifting his hips and feet back a foot from the sharp object that would likely end this thing before it begins. Danny makes eye contact with the blond bitch, "What’s the matter? Afraid he can’t suck you off anymore?" The smart ass remark draws another swing of the blade and another skillful dodge by Danny, who crouches in mirror of the blond man. They share a standoff briefly just before GCW’s finest rookie drops back and kicks the blond man in the knee. Without affect, Danny gets to his feet just as the blond man lunges with the blade aiming at his face. With luck, Dan Francisco knows how to avoid a blade and simply drops to his back. As if shocked by everything, the blond man can’t help but pause and look down in surprise. Danny knew it would play out that way and quickly drives his foot into the left knee, driving it sideways and outward. The big man’s eyes pop, much like Frank’s did a few minutes ago, and he crumbles to the ground. Danny stands up, wrapping his arm around the man’s arm and places his hand firmly on his shoulder. He grabs him by the ponytail, pulls it back, and gets inches from his face. "You lose," Danny whispers with a smile. The blond man notices that a slight sweat has grown over Danny’s face just before he feels his elbow ligaments snap as Danny straightens his own arm out, hyperextending the blond man’s elbow beyond the point of torture. He cries out as Danny holds it for a few seconds. He smiles, snarls, and looks a little crazy all at the same time. The small man can’t help but notice he’s the last of the crew and Danny’s eyes just locked onto him. "Look, fella," he waves his hands, trying to deter an attack on Frank again or even better, himself. "We’ll leave. You made ya point… your car, your rules. We’ll leave ya be. Just let me-" Danny’s hand wraps around his neck as Frank backs away and tucks into the corner of the car, not wanting anything to do with this. Danny lifts the short man off the ground. The little man kicks out, connecting with Danny’s nuts which releases the grip and sends them both crashing onto the ground. The little man stands up and drives his foot into Danny’s face. He does it two more times before an evil glaze comes over his eyes. He walks over to the wincing blond man and grabs the blade from his side. "Ya wanna play around, motherfucker?" he shouts. "We can play. Get the fuck up!" Danny touches his face, noticing a bloody lip and brings himself to his hands and knees. He pulls himself up with the help of the seat, looking the short fucker in the eye but then is brought down as Frank drives his massive size 16 boot into Danny’s ribs. The tongue-less man begins to stomp a hole in Danny, connecting his biker boots with his head, his ribs, his face… everywhere gets some boot. "Frank…" the short man waits. "Frank! Enough. We ain’t done with this one yet. Grab JJ’s belt. String this fucker up. He took your tongue… so we’re gonna take his." Danny tries to get up again but is brought back down, this time by a small boot belonging to the short man. He chuckles and does it again, really ripping into the young man’s ribs more affectively. Danny coughs and realizes what that means. Internal bleeding… he says to himself. Great. Danny lifts his head, "What’s wrong guys? Did I reeeally piss off the… who are you, Heavens Demons?" "Ohhhh, what a fuckin’ funny guy," the short man shakes his head. "Wake the fuck up, Danny. What’re you on tonight, huh? Coke? Ex? You doin’ heroine yet, ya fuckin’ junkie? The Trick wants to see you." Danny can’t help but pause, think back, and try to remember this schmuck’s face from those days. Luckily, those days are over and Danny’s not on anything except adrenaline and the occasional Scotch overload. "The Trick?" he says, fearlessly. "Is that your gay pimp code name or somethin’?" The short man looks over his shoulder as he holds his cell phone, "My boss… the man you owe fifty-five grand to. You better hope he’s in a better mood than Frank." Danny’s focus bounces around like a hyperactive toddler. He looks to Frank with his bloody mouth and missing tongue, he looks at the blade and the light reflecting off it, he realizes that he may be facing uncertain death, and – for some reason – he realizes he’s going to go another week with missing the Office. Nonetheless, no matter which one of this dismal ideas he expores, the budding GCW star can only think one thing… What. the. fuck. CHAPTERII Detroit, Michigan… The sounds of Detroit -- loud yelling, police sirens, and taxicab horns beeping -- are enough to wake Danny Francisco up. He tries to stretch like all superstars should do after a long night’s sleep but notices his hands and ankles are bound and he is blindfolded with a ball gag in his mouth. He struggles for a few seconds before coming to grips with the situation. The Trick… the Trick… who the Hell is this guy? I owe him some heavy dough but I’m not sure what for. I wonder if I sold them autographed shit that never came to value? Either way… I need to get the Hell out of here and find somebody who TiVo’ed the Office. Plus, I do have to work this week… Danny hears something… "Wake the fuck up." The fat end of a baseball bat hits Danny in the ribs, the same ribs that have taken a licking thanks to these jerkoffs, and it sends pain all through Danny’s nerve system. The man with the ponytail removes his blindfold. Unlike last time, the man standing in front of Danny is wearing a sling on his arm and a knee brace thanks to their last encounter. Danny tries to say something but the ball gag nulls those efforts. "What?" the blond man says. Danny notices their surroundings as he muffles another few words together. He hears the city buzz up above, which likely means that they’re somewhere that noise won’t matter. He’s in a dark, loud place, with nobody around and gagged, held hostage. He sees another flash of metal from the blond man’s waistline, expecting to see the same blade, but notices the steel of a gun. This might be where things finally come to a close, Danny Boy… "Do you remember me?" the blond man bends down, looking directly into Danny’s face. Through his long bangs, the young wrestler looks into the big man’s eyes for a few seconds and then says something, but once again, inaudible. The blond man shakes his head, frustrated with not having any return conversation, and unstraps the ball gag. He steps back from the chair, "What’d you say?" Danny stretches his jaw out before looking up, "I said, yeah, I remember you… or I think I do. Aren’t you the same dumb fuck I put in a sling and knee brace on the train earlier?" The blond man whips his gun out and slams it into the dull flesh of Danny’s face. His head jerks back and slumps forward as blood trickles off his lips from his mouth. He looks back up and is met with another slam of the gun handle into his forehead this time. Danny lifts his head again, this time laughing quite loud. The blond man shakes his head and pulls out a pack of smokes. He lights one and takes a drag off of it before noticing Danny eye-balling the smoke. He holds it out and smiles. "I bet you’d like one, eh Danny?" his tone isn’t too friendly. "A final smoke before I put two fat fuckin’ bullets in your thick head… that does sound nice." "Yes it does Roach," a raspy voice echoes from behind the blond man. "I need my fuckin’ money first though." Danny’s gaze shifts to an older man with dark sunglasses and a grey goatee approaching the scene. He uses a cane to walk and wears a similar black jacket as the goons from before. Danny looks back up at the blond man who snickers at the thought of how royally fucked Danny is. Flanking the old man is the short man from the train. "It’s like a goddamn reunion in here," the short prick says. "Me, Roach, Danny… you, Boss." The old man removes his sunglasses before he and Danny lock eyes. Danny can feel that he’s come across this scourge of the underworld before but can’t recall how. He remembers the cold, icy blue eyes and thinks. Unfortunately for Danny, he uses his mouth more than his brain. "Yeah…" he cocks his head, curiously. "I think I do remember you. You are the guy who looks like the walking dead and keeps some of the shittiest company around this city, which is impressive, considering you work with people who live in the sewers." Danny gets off a brief smile before Roach slams his fist into his ribs yet again. The henchmen may go down easily to a willing fighter but they sure know how to hit their target. Danny winces as the old man chuckles. For an old man, he seems young enough to get his hands dirty. He takes a few steps to the chair and looks down at his hostage. He smiles just before slamming his gloved knuckles into Danny’s face, first with a right and then followed shortly by a thunderous left. "Guess which hand is my dominant one?" the old man jests. Danny tries to rattle the stars he’s seeing loose and he briefly makes eye contact, "I couldn’t tell. Let me see ag-" The old man rocks him again with a right. He continues with three more right hooks before pausing. He grins, "That’s my right." Danny’s eyes begin to roll around, losing focus and the strength to keep conscious. The old man shakes his head, disappointed, and then rears back with his left hand, slamming his fist into Danny’s bloody and battered face four times. He stops, steps back, and admires his own handiwork. "That’s how you do it, boys," he puffs out his aging chest. "When shit hits the fan, ya have to be willin’ to get in there and clean it up yourself." Roach nudges the small man, "Just like Boss used to do. Fuckin’ awesome." All three leather-wearing thugs turn their focus back to their hostage, who was thought to be unconscious already until he lets out a long, exhausting breath. "I don’t… suppose… you ladies wanna party, do ya?" Roach steps forward to smash him again but is stopped by the small man’s hand, once again. The old man grins, dusts off his gloves, and steps closer to Danny. He looks back to his men and nods, implying that he’s got this one. "Who am I, Danny?" the old man whispers in Danny’s ear. Struggling to keep it together, Danny’s head flops back and he looks at the old man out of the corner of a swollen right eye; "Do I give a shit?" "Danny, you really need to learn that everything doesn’t have to be so difficult," the old man steps back. Roach looks at the Trick who nods at him to do his thing. Roach steps up and punches Danny repeatedly in the ribs, both sides, until he is out of breath. The grunts and groans of the GCW rookie are silent among the sounds of the city subway system. "Now, where were we…" the old man steps back into the interrogation position. "Ahh yes, we were teaching you about life. The point I wanted you to learn here Danny is that life is really, really scary. You can live life like a good person, pay taxes, find a desk job, and have a family… or you can live life like… you, a man who spend his fortune on drugs, sex, and real-life rock n’ roll. Ain’t that right, champ?" Danny lowers his brow (as far as he can) but can’t help taking offense to that. I never pay for sex, he justifies in his head. "You may not remember this but… you owe me fifty-five thousand dollars, Mr. Francisco," the Trick smiles, patiently. "I want my money." Danny scoffs, slightly, and tries to act normal though his head is in intense pain and the sound of bass drums echo in his ears; "Fifty-five thousand? What for? I know I was really fucked up back then but, honestly? That’s a lot of-" "It is a lot of cheddar," the small man chimes in. "now fork it over… or say nitey nite, Dynamite." Roach leans in, "I thought it was Dynasty?" "Whatever it is…" the Trick silences his underlings before looking back to Danny. "It better be able to pay me my money, tonight." Danny’s eyes roll back all the way, whites of his eyes shining like pearls in a puddle of blood. The old man waits a few seconds before slapping Danny. Some reaction but not enough from the cold reality check. He nods to Roach, who uncaps a water bottle and dumps it over Danny’s head. "I don’t fuckin’ have fifty-five grand!" Danny screams, coming back to life. The Trick smiles and glances at his watch, wondering if he needs to do this much longer. Roach steps back and flashes his gun to the Trick, who silently derails that train of thought for now. The leather jackets look at the bloody face of the promising GCW grappler as he gathers himself. Danny looks out of the tops of his eyes, directly at the Trick, and begins to breath heavily. "What the fuck do you suggest we do, old man?" Danny’s tone is different, indifferent in fact. "You say I owe you fifty-five grand… I don’t got fifty-five grand to give ya. Now that we’ve got that established-" "Yet," the Trick interjects. "you don’t have fifty-five grand… yet." Danny shakes his head, "I’m not who I used to be. I don’t make millions, hundreds of thousands, or even dozens of thousands a month. I’m broke, dried up from my less-than-stellar-judgment days." The Trick straightens his cuffs and nods, "Then you’ll have to work on paying us back, won’t you Danny Boy?" "How in the Hell do you expect me to do that?" Danny becomes irritated. "I just told you I don’t-" "Are you good with your hands?" the Trick cuts him off with his serious tone. Danny still tries to fight off slipping into a coma as he rationalizes thoughts and words, "Take a good look at your boy Roach over there and you tell me." The Trick and the short man both look at Roach and laugh. The blond man doesn’t appreciate being the butt end of a joke but can’t do much since the Trick will shoot him if it costs him any gains. Danny chuckles along with them and then looks at the big blond whose passion to fight Danny Francisco grows by the second. "So here’s how this is gonna play out Danny…" the Trick pulls out a cell phone. "You’re going to take this phone and call me. We’re going to be watching but I want to give you a sense of freedom, a sense of faith… so you will contact one of my boys, either Roach or Cesar, when you are going to make a payment. I’m trusting you are as good as I’ve been reading on the internet with this wrestling adventure so I’ll give you… two months to get me my money. If you don’t get me my money… this face will be the last one you see." The Trick chuckles and Danny glares ahead stubbornly. He nods to Roach, who pulls back out the ball gag and the blindfold. Danny jerks his head around, though it doesn’t stop the blond man from wrapping the thick black cloth over Danny’s eyes. Roach looks behind him, noticing the Trick and Cesar halfway across the gravel yard, and then turns his snarling gaze back to Danny. "Fuck you, Francisco," Roach growls, shoving the ball gag back in Danny’s mouth. He picks up a box on the ground and pulls out a road flare. He rips the plastic cap off, igniting it, and then tosses it nearby to draw attention. He takes one step to leave hi but spins around, roundhouse kicking Danny in the face and knocking the chair to its back. Danny’s groan is muffled as he crashes into the ground. He closes his eyes, slipping into the warm, fluffy feeling that comes with cranial bleeding as he waits for help to arrive. The image of himself with long, grey hair pops into his head. Wearing a bright white suit with GCW World Title over his shoulder, the aged Danny Francisco stands before a spiraling, psychedelic scene and snaps his fingers. Hey! Stick with me, kid. Ya gotta keep fightin’. There’s a lot of work to do… Some voices grow closer as Danny’s heartbeat begins to slow down. A few flashlights send beams passing over his body before steadying the light on the broken young man. The first man to arrive wears a white button-down short-sleeve shirt and slides in the gravel next to Danny. He removes the hood quickly and gasps at the sight of Danny’s face. He removes the gag and listens for breathing. A second man arrives and drops to the opposite side with an orange box and white EMT uniform on. CHAPTERIII Harper University Hospital, Downtown Detroit "Hold still," Dr. Daphne Rose says, wrapping a roll of gauze around Danny Francisco’s head. "You’re going to wrinkle it." Buffalo’s Bad Boy winces as the petite brunette doctor wraps the bandages tighter and tighter. She briefly looks at Danny’s face and clicks her tongue on her teeth before she gets all high and mighty on the roughest looking stud to come rolling through the downtown her ER on this uneventful Thursday night. "You’re lucky you know?" she tries to break the painful silence. "I don’t think I’ve seen a man come in lookin’ like you did." Danny figures she’s about to sing that familiar tune he’s heard too many times about fighting and danger and blah fuckin’ blah. She realizes the same thing he does and puts on a stone face, shaking her head silently to discourage his nasty habit. Through his swollen face, Danny sees her moving through his peripheral vision and turns his head. They lock eyes just before she smiles, putting the bandaging process on hold. Dan tries to smile even though his face feels like he fell into a cinderblock, "Doctor Rose, is it?" The second-year resident twirls some strands of her silky, brunette hair with her finger, smiles and nods, "Please, call me Daphne. Doctor makes me feel so… old." Danny offers a polite smile and adjusts himself, taking a good look at the tangled mess he’s in the middle of and relishes the fact. Heart rate machine, vital monitors, morphine drip, and even the bed pan all remind him of the last place he wants to be. Fuckin’ Casualty Department of a downtown Detroit Hospital… he lets out a deep breath of unfresh air. Another day, another dilemma. "Nice to meet ya Daphne," he grimaces, leaning his head back with an exhausted sigh. "I’m Dan." She rolls her eyes slightly, "Dan Francisco, born June 21st, 1985, from Buffalo, New York… you-" Danny jerks his head toward Dr. Rose all too quick, sending a sharp, shooting pain through his entire spine on the way to his brain. He fights off the agonizing scream and opts for the silent wincing yet again. The peaceful Doctor pats his chest softly, easing him back down. Danny is stiff and sore, trying to get comfortable but seems to have trouble doing much of anything. "Relax honey," Daphne’s voice is sweet and pure, innocent and angelic. Each soft word she whispers relaxes Danny just as she suggests. "You’re pretty banged up. Two cracked ribs… a slight concussion… and a nasty lookin’ black eye. What in the Hell were you doin’ down at the old Subway Station? I know you’re from Buffalo but… don’t you know that place is dangerous? Whoever did this could have killed you." "Yeah well…" Danny rolls onto his side, finally settling in one position for more than ten seconds. "I like dangerous places. They make me feel ten times safer when I’m nowhere near danger. Anyways… I don’t even know what I was doing down there. I woke up tied to a chair in a dark room." She cocks her eyebrow, "What do you mean, you don’t know?" "Exactly what I said, Doc," he snaps. "I have no idea what I was doing down there. I’m assuming we’re in Detroit but…" Danny turns to notice the Red Wings poster on the wall of the ER ward. A shudder goes up his spine at the thought of the Red Wings winning yet another Cup and his irritation level begins to climb. Never put a boy from Buffalo in Detroit and expect him to play nice. His gaze sticks to that God-forsaken poster, Three-horns don’t play with long-necks. He turns back to Daphne, "I have no idea how I got here. I was in Chicago three days ago. I got on a train and… well, yeah. I got into a fight… a mean fuckin’ fight. I didn’t realize they knew who I was and…" Daphne waits for Dan to continue but he doesn’t. Instead his head begins to piece things together and try to solve the mystery filling his head – who were those guys? She laughs, slightly, and tries to understand what’s going on. "So you were in Chicago three days ago, got into a fight, and found yourself at the abandoned subway station?" her face grows a little pale. "Well you need to tell somebody, Dan. They could be still out there, still looking for you, and they can’t get away with this. There’s a Detroit PD booth downstairs. I’ll call them. Tell the officers what you told me and we’ll get whoever did this to you." Danny checks out the hallway and notices how empty it is. His eyes move back to Daphne’s soft, hazel eyes and puffy pink lips. He smiles and bats his eyes. She picks up Danny’s charts and walks away. She begins to pull the big, blue curtain back to conceal Danny and is almost done before she looks at her patient with a hopeful glint in her eye. "We’ll find them, Danny. Never give up," she reassures the broken brawler. He smiles, "I won’t." Daphne slides the curtain shut and spins on her heel, heading for the central ER Reception Desk. She sets the chart on the counter and watches as the clerk files it accordingly in the ‘SURVEILLANCE’ section. She picks up the Hospital phone and presses the blue button with a star on it. "Hello, Officer?" Dr. Rose’s voice is more assertive than when around Danny. "This is Doctor Daphne Rose in the ER. I’ve got a patient up here that you’ll want to talk to. He says he was abducted from Chicago and brought to Detroit, then beaten pretty badly. He’s probably going to need to be here for a few days, maybe even the weekend." She waits for a few seconds, tapping her fingernails on the counter, and then lifts her head in a positive nod, "Thank you, Officer. I’ll be here." Daphne sets the phone back on the hook and leans against the reception desk. A heavy black man strolls up to her wearing green scrubs and a name tag reading ‘WINSTON’ that dangles from a silver lanyard. He playfully jabs Dr. Rose and smiles. She doesn’t return the same emphatic display of friendship and Winston scowls. "What gives, Daph?" Winston says in a deep, thick voice. She shakes her head, "Sorry, Winston. I just have this patient…" "That guy from the abandoned subway station?" her big friend wonders. "Yeah, that’s the one," she continues to look back at her fingers instead of the resident Neurosurgeon. "He’s just… something strange about him. He doesn’t seem like he’s a bad guy but… why would somebody kidnap him and bring him to Detroit? It just doesn’t make sen--" "Excuse me, are you Doctor Rose?" Daphne turns to see a Detroit Police Officer standing behind her and Winston. "Officer John Ortega, Ma’am. Pleasure to meet you." She nods, briefly glancing at Winston, and turns toward Danny’s gurney; "Yes, yes. Please… right over here Officer." Officer Ortega’s bells and whistles jingle and jangle on their way toward Dr. Rose’s most interesting patient. He pulls out a notepad and clicks his pen to life; "You said this was an assault victim, Dr. Rose?" "Yes," she sounds distant. "Cracked ribs, mild concussion, and some major swelling around the eye." "Sounds bad…" Officer Ortega writes down some notes before lifting his head, "Did he know who did this to him, Ma’am?" "The patient didn’t mention that he knew his attackers nor did he show any signs of amnesia," Dr. Rose keeps it professional. "The only thing I can conclude is that he may have been abducted. For what reasons, I have no idea…" Dr. Rose rips the curtain back to reveal the bludgeoned Danny Francisco. Instead, she sees an empty bed with all the electronics unplugged and dangling from the machines. Winston recognizes that the bed is empty and heads for the ER reception desk. Daphne turns around, scanning the open corridor for her patient. Officer Ortega notices something resting on the pillow. He hands a small note to Daphne, "I guess he wanted to say goodbye, Doctor." Dr. Rose frantically runs a hand through her long hair and turns back to the policeman. She looks at the small piece of paper and notices it says ‘Dr. Daphne’ on the front. She smiles, finding some sort of romance in a mysterious, mangled man who runs off but won’t leave before he writes her a note first. She thinks about if she’ll see him again… and realizes the answer is likely ‘No’ as long as Dan continues to fight his way through life. She unfolds the paper. To my guardian angel, Dr. Daphne Rose… she pretends Dan is behind her, whispering her name into her ear as they stand overlooking the Mediterranean Sea in July. Thank you for saving my life. Never give up. Officer Ortega takes a stroll, clutching the radio clipped to his uniform, and talks slowly into it. A few seconds after that he folds his notepad shut and stuffs it into his pocket. He is out of sight within minutes. Winston returns to the sight of the missing patient but notices nobody is there, not even Dr. Rose. He spins around, clueless on what to do, and finds himself more irate with the situation than he should be. Dr. Rose steps out of the women’s locker room and slaps a small metal cigarette case against her opposite hand. She knows the patients who spot her passing by won’t approve but they have no idea how rapidly a doctor in the Detroit area ages. Some things were just meant to be abused and cigarettes are the vice for anyone in this up-and-down madhouse. She strolls through the automatic doors, pulling a skull cap over her head, and pulls her jacket tight. She lights her cigarette, shielding the flame from the wind, and takes a much-needed drag from her cancer stick. She looks out across the street and thinks about what she’ll deal with next and when she’ll deal with it. Traffic begins to build up as the lights change in front of the hospital entrance. She notices the backseat window roll down on a taxi nearby. A man wearing bloody gauze over his forehead leans far enough forward to show his swollen face. His free hand rises and shoots her a peace sign as the light changes. CHAPTERIV The Shadows of Motor City The Trick glances to his trusting thugs, Roach and Cesar, "Can you two work with that?" "You want me to bring him along for the job, Boss?" Cesar flicks a thumb in Danny’s direction. "How can we trust this guy? Let’s just whack him, end this bullshit charade. He’s just playin’ us for--" Danny glares at Cesar with a slightly less broken face. He shakes his head and looks right back to the Trick. He shrugs and cocks his head. "Fine, count me out…" Danny subsides. "Just remember whose idea it was to dump fifty-five large down the drain when you do the books." Cesar slams his fist on the Trick’s desk, "Look, fella! You couldn’t put fifty-five thousand together in two years let alone two months. You’d get two grand and blow it on the dirtiest, nastiest Mexican whore and three dollar bottle of Tequila you could find. Fifty-five large? Get real, Francisco." Danny grins, "So the fact that I heard Big Cecil up North wants to make a major purchase doesn’t matter?" The Trick kicks his chair back, slamming it against the wall, and leans over his desk into the small lamp. It highlights his wrinkly face, his sagging skin, and the mean look on his face. Danny shrugs again as Cesar slumps back, knowing his card was just trumped. "What do you know about Big Cecil?" the Trick whispers. "I said I don’t remember you and your goons," he replies. "I didn’t say I’m Regarding fuckin’ Henry." The Trick violently tears a stack of papers off his desk and throws them into the air. He kicks the chair out of his way and then pulls out a revolver. With a click, the Biker Boss plugs the barrel against Danny’s head, even scaring Cesar and Roach a little bit. "I’m going to ask you again…" he slowly lowers himself to eye level. "What about Big Cecil?" Danny toys at the thought of giving the Trick another sarcastic remark; "Think about it, Trick… I come to you for money. Where do ya think I spent it all? There’s only one person I used to know around Detroit who hunts big game like I do… that’s Big Cecil. I’ve never come through town without getting a hold of Big C. There is one thing I was wonderin’ though… I don’t remember you bein’ local to Motown last time we met." The Trick snickers and makes the extra effort to give his thugs a shifty look, "Yeah, well, you don’t remember a lot, kid." The Trick pulls out a stack of cash from his desk and slams it on the desk in front of Danny. He points one of his fingers at Danny and the other at Cesar. Before he speaks, a few seconds of silent fill the room. He looks to Cesar and Roach first, "Take Danny Boy here up to Windsor to see Big Cecil. Hopefully he can patch up some holes in our business relationship." "As for you," he turns his focus back to Dan. "Make sure you tell him about our new shipment coming in and the new merchandise I’ll have. Make sure he knows that I’m still in business and if he ever needs anything…" the Trick trails off, rolling his hands out to imply the rest of his directions. "Now… I’m gonna make myself very, very clear. If you even think about making a run for it, taking the cash, or fucking me over in any way… I will hunt you down. I will find you. Then… I will kill you." Danny doesn’t bat an eyelash at the threats from the aging Biker Boss. He brushes the long bangs out of his face and nods pleasingly. The Trick holds up the cash and holds it up for Danny to take. The young hands snatch it but can’t take it from the old man’s amazing grip. The two lock eyes one more time. The Trick’s face remains frozen with seriousness, "It won’t take me five years to find a celebrity this time, Danny. Think about that. If you get me Big Cecil’s business… we might be able to come to an understanding about your… unpaid tab." Danny says nothing yet instead simply jerks the stack of hundred dollar bills and shoves it into his jacket pocket. Cesar and Roach stand up to chaperone their bargaining chip. The Trick stands to show them out, wrapping his arm around Danny’s shoulder. He slowly walks with him. "Be careful Danny," the old man whispers. "Keep your head on straight. Not everything is what you think it is and the worst part… is figuring that out when you’re knee deep in it." Danny continues to limit the amount of dialogue he shares with the man who nearly wired his jaw shut. He nods and slips out the door before the Trick can follow. The old man blocks the doorway as his thugs inch closer to their Boss. When Danny is a safe distance away, he turns back to Cesar and Roach. The Trick huddles their heads together and snarls, "Make me proud boys." Cesar nods and slaps his Boss’ back before his loyal dogs head for the Canadian border. The Trick watches through the glass window of his office and finds satisfaction in the sight of the black Cadillac Escalade’s headlights come to life. He watches as the SUV leaves the parking lot. The Trick pulls out a small flask and flicks his head back, coating his throat and soul with liquid courage. A slight buzz comes from the inside of the Trick’s jacket before he heads back to his desk. He pulls it out, smiles out of appreciation, and flips the phone open. "He’s heading to you…" the Trick’s tone is cold. "Take the cash as a down payment. You’ll get the rest when I get confirmation that Danny Francisco is dead. Nice doing business with you, Big C. Show my boys a good time, too. I think they deserve it after this trip." The corner of the old man’s mouth curls up as he closes the phone shut. He remains silent, nodding his head emphatically as the sound of fresh rainfall comes down over the brisk Detroit night. The Trick turns back to his desk and picks up a smoking cigar. He takes a celebratory puff from the long tobacco log and exhales with a broad, brooding grin.
