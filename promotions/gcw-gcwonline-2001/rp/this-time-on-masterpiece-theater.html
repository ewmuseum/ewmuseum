This Time On Masterpiece Theater...

Date: over the past couple days 'Power is not all there is to this existence.' -Shadow *** 'WE INTERRUPT THIS EPISODE OF DAYS OF OUR LIVES TO BRING YOU A SPECIAL MESSAGE STRAIGHT FROM--' With an entrance like that, you damn well know you got to have something up your sleeve. Or at least think you do enough to the point where you can clutch your balls and scream superiority. See, I got this dame I'm gonna be standing across from come WorldWide and there is a little something something picking at my mind but it has nothing to do with these other failed attempts at out storying someone. Hell...when it comes to character depth, looking directly at me is about the worst thing possible to do. That's okay though. What I am trying to say is, I understand that I am not this well cultured, well rounded human being. I'm not the most eloquent talker. I'm not going to burst into foreign dialect in mid sentence or name moves names specifically to put over faux intellectual superiority on people. I don't have to create something. With you...my sweet Shadow, that is not exactly the case, is it ? Oh no...don't get me wrong...I'm sure you could read me under the table with Hume and Nietzsche. Intellect is one thing. But at the same time, I don't need to go to Stephen King for inspiration for my life story. I was caught a while ago embellishing upon my life. But I did not concoct a fallacy where I am some sort of gothic queen pushing enlightenment in a wrestling ring. That makes me more retarded just SAYING that. Best part is that no matter what I say, I'm not going to tell the style of story you will. I know this. You see, that's because I am not extraordinary. I don't slay wolves in my free time (though I have beaten Mike Randalls ass more than a couple times). I don't suck blood, I do not only go out in the night. I do not have actual meetings with Satan, and I certainly do not speak like I have a stick up my ass. I have never served in a World War. But the best part is that neither have you. The difference is, I can decipher reality from fiction. *** Cue up the hokey music, trumpets played by pimply fifteen year olds. The scene faded in upon a gray overcast sky, filled with thunder clouds. Rain spewed from the sky with relative ease, heightening a stench to unbelievable levels. The smell came directly from a mountain of black garbage bags. It was rotting foods, diapers from both the young and old. It was the smell of degradation. But on top of this mountain of garbage bags was a pair, wearing smiles. They were all dolled up for this, their shining moment. The audience could not clap, of course, as they were gagged on the stench. The pair however breathed easily. After all, when you are around the smell of bullshit your entire life, it loses its crudeness. It was a female and male, the male with a crude bowl haircut and the female having her jet black hair done up in pigtails. They looked so cute in their attire of tattered cloth, akin to that presented in retrospect of peasant clothing in the Medieval time period. They even had smatterings of mud and feces all over, to really get into their roles. Mom and Dad would be ever so proud. 'Does thou noth know--' 'WAIT!' The off scene Director screamed at the top of his lungs towards the young lady. 'She should know that her lines were to be delivered only after the crows made their return to the stage!' he thought as he played with his beret, squinting through his monocle as he sipped on brandy. This was culture, and he'd be damned if some stray dogs tried to lessen his crude arrogance. The crows did not appear on stage for a full five uncomfortable minutes. The stagehands panicked in this time, and decided it was time to do something as the parents in the crowd were starting to pass out either out of sheer boredom or from the never ending stench. From the rafters dropped a dozen or so dead pigeons, their limp bodies smacking against the skulls of those sitting on top of the garbage bags. 'BRILLIANT! I am so glad I thought of pigeons! They work so well in this scene! They are not possibly interchangeable!' The Director thought to himself as he choked on his brandy, his monocle slipping off his face due to the grease. What better way to be cultured than not bath and drench yourself in Crisco? 'NOW~!' The Director screamed, his voice breaking. He got to his knees and scurried around like a rat, grabbing on to his monocle only to have it slip out of his grasp. On the stage, the girl's void expression was replaced with a smile, with which you could see the teeth on her upper jaw were missing completely. 'Does thou noth know behest which therefore I know of?' She stammered out, trying her best to overact this scene. This was about her character developing, and she knew that there were men in the crowd from Broadway out to see this classic. The male grabbed at her, drawing her close. As he did, a garbage bag fell from the mountain and smacked right into a family that had spent fifteen thousand dollars for the seat. They could not help but smile as the bag split and covered them in decomposing Kentucky Bluegrass clippings that had turned mustard. 'I do nah not knowth whateth you speaketh of my darlin'...' he trailed off. 'NO! STAY IN CHARACTER!' the Director roared, his large buttocks seemingly clapping, which set off a legion of people in the audience clapping their hearts out. The parents seemed mortified. Apparently, they were not as enlightened when it came to art as the rest of the frauds in the audience that had paid thousands of dollars in hopes of witnessing this masterpiece theater. The young man almost could be seen crying as the crowd hooted and hollered at his perceived greatness. 'My darling...however shalt I know whath you speaketh of shant you not spoketh.' He had rid himself of that accursed southern dialect, getting more into the character than before. His name was Milford Brown, and he was a truck driver. But at night, he became a cultured thespian. Over time, he began to actually believe he was no longer an ordinary man, instead an artist who had a rich and detailed history with acting accolades that made him akin to Denzel Washington. 'My darling...I spoketh not. Leader of Dias Irae I am yet unconceivable wretched I feel.' Hers was a more pained existence. Maggie Sholtz, daughter of an mill worker and a seamstress, she so badly wanted to break through her mundane existence that she created a character that she would forever play. Becoming Frantiska, Maggie adopted a Czech accent (poorly incorporating it into her delivery) and lived a life of faux worldly experience. At the tender age of seventeen, she claimed herself to be forty two, and that her explorations were the source of her youthful buoyancy. On top of it all, she littered her resume with plays and feature films that she had not been involved in. More than half that list was dedicated to items that she could not have possibly been involved in unless she was one hundred years old. She even put that she had a speaking part in Le Voyage dans la lune (A Trip to the Moon), a 1902 French silent movie. Not only did she speak of her nonexistent experiences, but within her being, she maintained it all to be true. Those around her never questioned, considering plausibility of these things was not as important as the pedigree she represented. She was a beautiful storyteller, it did not matter that the stories she told were built on a bed of lies. 'Ye my fair damsel, for it is nominal to speak so. Whatever troubles have shaken your froth, my beautiful aristocrat?' He tried as hard as he could to maintain character, but even as he did so his own consciousness was beating him from within with a sledgehammer. He was no more adapt at playing roles than he was at brain surgery. 'Darling my inability to seeketh individual glory for all children under the divine has me precariously puzzled. Am I not a fair leader?' With her, this was not even a contest, as she would have taken that scalpel and mutilated that brain without guilt. 'Whatever possibly could that meaneth, my fair haired sequin? No harm in questioning one's abilities.' 'I trust we have crushed their spines, my darling. All is carefully and eloquently put. We have already won the battle.' Clapping once again broke out in the crowd, as the Director wept in joy of not only the performance, but also for grabbing his monocle. It was smeared in greasy fat, but that was of little concern to him. 'Their storytelling is so apt. How could this have ever been performed without them?' 'I trust in this, my fair lady, that their spines have been broken, and enlightenment dons the land we spoketh!' He exclaimed as he stood tall on the garbage bags, his fists pointing upwards. He almost slipped and fell, but he regained his balance. 'As I had no doubt of, we have conquered the unconquerable! Let us rejoice the pale moon and dance as witches in the fires of Mordor!' And there was much clapping, and much rejoicing. And then, a single ripe tomato plastered her in the face. She fell, toppling downward, hitting every piece of filth on the way down. The crowd stood, their mouths agape. The man screamed out like a little girl, screaming in his southern dialect 'DON'T HIT MAH PEEZ!' The Director gasped morbidly as the rest of the crowd turned to see where it had come from. His bald head gleamed and his eyes burned red. *** 'Oh Brandon...' she cooed. 'My sweet lil Brandon...' The docile tones of the cement office were offset by her unheralded beauty. Hips undulating, short auburn hair framed to accentuate that luscious face, pale skin dusted with light patches of freckles, she was simply the perfect woman. Past tense is the item, as was is and is currently, which means that something changed that sweet little mischievous innocence. This was all rhetorical, after all. In literary form, we sometimes embellish and come up with elaborate ways to try and captivate the audience. Drop subtle hints, give simplistic actions a flowery flair. It is all part of the Pompous Self Important Guide To Writing. And we are all richly entrenched with that fucking book. Oh...shoot, I did it again. Or should I say, I mistakenly accentuated items of vast unimportance to the overall plot development in lieu of english you would gander upon or stand witness to via the Aristocracy. The room was uneventful due to the lack of vibrant color of its surroundings. There were grays of different concrete, nothing more and nothing less. Brandon Youngblood's head rested firmly on the desk, a strain of spit hanging from his lips as he snored away. He was blissfully unaware of his ex-wife's presence, dressed in a hiked up floral print skirt and red halter top with a pair of strappy sandals. Brandon did not even have the decency to put on a shirt for the occasion, or would not have had he known he would find himself in this predicament. All he wore was a pair of dark blue Reebox shorts. Work at the Los Angeles based BloodWorks Dojo had been slow, so rather than better himself athletically he decided he would sleep, and hope a nightmare would come showing him past visages involving vampirism... Wait...fuck...did it again...sorry...sorry... Melissa Garvin (Missy for short) had thought a coo and the soothing waves of her voice would carry her little angel out of his peaceful slumber into a brutal world of financial mumbo jumbo based upon the flawed halves system in place in the United States of America. Yet, not unlike many a day during their tumultuous marriage, her expectations of being listened to and drawing a reaction from were sadly unfulfilled. 'Brandon.' She tried hard to make stern her voice. She wanted to sound appealing, yet at the same time show power and firmness. See, this is a term we call saying one thing and doing the other. The paradox of this is that you can only have it one way, not two ways. The ironic-- What...I'm being replaced?! COME ON MAN! You know this script is badly written and it's full of fucking plot holes! Look...I don't GIVE a flying batshit if it's a style that's won in the past. Look man...sometimes being creative and different works...alright. You think Angelo Deville had to put up with this shit?! 'Brandon.' Melissa tensed her words. She had taken to tapping her fingers into the desk, the strumming surely enough to awake someone via the intensity of the sound. Or at the very least there would be some element of annoyance given the constant beating. Yet still, the drool rolled from his mouth, his face still completely gone in the blissfulness of sleep. 'GODDAMMIT...WAKE THE FUCK UP BRANDON!' That got him. Brandon made a grunting noise and bolted to a sit, his eyes half closed and caked with sleep in the corners. 'huh...what...huh...shiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiit...' barely escaped his lips. Even using his mouth was a taxing operation given his overall feeling of tired. Opening his eyes fiercely to get his vision back completely rather than seeing everyone through a gloss filter, he saw a pool of his own spit on the desk, resting right on the bills for the place. He grunted in indifference as he wiped some of the drool off his face, then looking up to see his ex-wife. She smiled at him, batting her eyelashes. And Brandon laughed. 'You aren't Missy.' She couldn't stop smiling, but there was concern in her eyes. Speaking through her pearly whites, she tried to maintain the jolly appearance. 'Why whatever could you mean hon--' 'You screwed it up. Why the fuck would broken jaw be nice to me? Dude...you've been slippin'.' Brandon smirked as he looked at Melissa starting to get on pins and needles. 'Hey...I didn't’like’drool over any important papers for you...did I? I wouldn't want people getting sent here unless they deserve to be...' 'They all deserve to be here, that is the ironic thing.' Missy stated matter of factly as she turned her back to him. 'You see, I haven't been keeping an eye on you like I should. I didn't even know you cracked her jaw.' Brandon eased into his leather chair. 'I thought you beings were supposed to be all seeing and knowing and shit.' Suddenly, the room turned ice cold. Once behind a desk, Brandon now stood before the black desk, the only item it seemed that was not covered in the gloss of ice. It seemed intricate, as if someone had taken a lot of time putting it together. Melissa had morphed before his eyes into a obsidian figure, dressed in the apparel befitting the Fallen Angel. 'Doesn't help when Ginobili fouls out...' Brandon trailed off as he looked directly as the dark figure grabbed a ball of ice. 'I know, they weren't listening to me complaining last night either...I can't believe it!' 'It's okay Tim...you have a couple rings already...it's time you went fishing.' Duncan sat on his icy chair of thorns, and barbwire, tossing the ice basketball up and down to keep his mind occupied. 'You are probably right. God is having a hard on for a Mavs Pistons final. Says it will be good for ratings...' Duncan trailed off. 'So...should I be calling you by your Hebrew name?' 'PSSSSSSSSH! HAHAHAHAHAHA!' Tim Duncan snorted as he looked Brandon Youngblood directly in the eyes. 'Whenever I show up these semi-intellectuals seem to throw that scene from Devil's Advocate at me. As if I love hearing the five hundred variants of my damn name.' 'Okay Tim. I know the last person you invited was callin' you Sammael...' Tim Duncan shuddered. 'Hey, this is the same woman who honestly thinks she was involved in World War Two!' Brandon covered his chest with his arms, the cold starting to get to him. 'So why not explain to her that she's been living a lie?' Duncan sneered. 'HEY! She's a storyteller! She at least shows some level of interest in the arts and such--' Brandon Youngblood couldn't help but interrupt. 'She thinks she's a fucking vampire.' '--well...' Brandon continued. 'She's also a walking hypocrite.' '--look...' Brandon was smiling now. 'You find it funny, don't you?' 'EXACTLY!' Tim Duncan exclaimed. 'What's better than some crazy bitch who actually thinks she's a vampire?' 'She's not going to bite me...is she?' 'Not sure. Free will and stuff makes it hard to say. But I wouldn't doubt it. If she tries, just break her jaw. Apparently you are very adapt in doing that.' 'Hey...' Brandon tried to distance himself from where this conversation was going. 'I was the one who had to inform you about that. Besides, she's YOUR gothic princess.' 'OH PLEASE!' Tim Duncan brought a ice backboard from nowhere, and shot the basketball at it. It bricked hard. Duncan cranked his head to his side, looking to yell at an official that he had just been fouled, but then remember that he was in Hell rather than in SBC. 'Your dad could make that.' Brandon snickered. 'What is it with you always busting out the Ice Man references? You know they don't get them.' Duncan had taken to grabbing another ice basketball and shooting it towards the rim again, only to have it miss. Badly. 'Who is 'they'?' Brandon looked around, trying to see if someone else was in the room. Duncan rolled his eyes. 'I don't know how many people know about Gervin anymore. But getting back to Lia...fuck her! Every single Hot Topic bingeing goth who files their teeth and gets a nose piercing thinks they are my queen. You know what I call them?' 'Losers?' Brandon stated matter of factly. 'I've said this to you before...haven't I?' Brandon nodded. 'I just think it is ironic how two faced she is. I mean, at one minute she's turning down my advances, stating that power isn't the only thing of importance in the world, and then goes to GCW and starts a war for dominance and power, to rule with an iron fist.' 'You should be used to hypocrites. Just look at Bruce Bowen...' 'HEY! STOP THAT! I bring you down here out of the kindness in my heart, and there you go running down my team!' 'Look...it's cold. What do you want? Is it just you ranting about Shadow or are you here to give me something?' Brandon was visibly shivering. It did not help that the sharp jabbing pain of ice shards were sinking into his lower back. 'No...no...just here to tell you that I slipped the Italian Gentleman a little something something to keep her from bothering me--' 'But you simply couldn't resist. God...beating a dead horse for humor...you're fuckin' sick man.' Brandon interrupted. 'Agreed. But before you interrupted, I was going to tell you that he explained to her that all of her trials were over, or something. And then she started screaming about wanting to stay...oh GOD was that great. But...either way...I want you to lose.' Brandon's eyebrow arched. 'In the amount of time that we've known each other, this has to be the stupidest goddamn thing I’ve ever heard! You didn't even make sense right there...you know that right?' Youngblood walked to the left, grabbing a doorknob that seemed to blend in to the surroundings. Tim Duncan got up quickly and grabbed him by the shoulder. 'Brandon?BRANDON...she's a storyteller?SHE'S MY STORYTELLER! I'LL BE BORED IF YOU TAKE HER OUT!' Brandon dusted his hand off his shoulder. 'Vampire lady who you string along, who ACTUALLY THINKS she is a vampire and kill wolves in her spare time...you are telling me to lose to some crazy bitch who should be staying in a goddamn mental hospital to supplement your entertainment?! Sorry?TIMMY...but I'm gonna have to pass on that shit.' Duncan got to his knees this time. 'PLEASE BRANDON! THIS ONE THING! THIS ONE THING! I DON'T WANT TO BE BORED! IT'S FUNNY!' Brandon simply turned the knob. 'It's fuckin' retarded. I can deal with people thinking they are mob bosses and killing people...at least THAT'S cool...but pseudo intellectual goth fan fiction? Dude...fuck that! That ain't funny!' Tim Duncan grabbed at his leg, hugging it and not letting go. 'IT IS! YOU JUST DON'T SEE THE GREATNESS INSIDE!' 'Thank God for that...' Brandon trailed off as he opened the door to the blackness. Tim Duncan got to his feet, sneering. Getting nose to nose with Brandon Youngblood, the world seemed to shake as he quivered in anger. 'You'll pay Brandon! I'll ruin your life!' Brandon pushed Satan away, smirking as he did so. 'Didn't you get the memo that I WRESTLE?! Sure, I'm not the most interesting guy in the world...but I don't need to make up a fallacy to garner a goddamn push! And next time...do yourself a favor...remember I don't believe in this stupid bullshit. Vampires...God...the Devil...it's all retarded bullshit.' In so stating, Brandon Youngblood stepped through the door and the scene faded to black. However, the scene in question loses meaning the moment he says those final words to Satan. How is that he can have a conversation yet at the same time not believe in the entity in question? Is this not akin to insanity? How does this pass as writing? I've seen shit about vampires and werewolves and birth and Dawn McFarlane's mansion-- The scene fades to black...don't let that fucking nut job in here again. *** 'Both ways...' he trailed off. The Director had gotten from his seat in nowhere and appeared out of thin air, right before the bald headed tomato thrower. Even this seemed taxing, as he breathed in pained breaths, completely out of gas. His monocle fell from his head once again, this time the unmistakable sound of shattered glass breaking the silence. 'WHATEVER DO YOU SPEAK OF?!' the Director exclaimed as he fell to his knees, grabbing at the pieces of his monocle. His face was nearly purple, but that could have had more to do with poor circulation than in outright anger. 'Both ways...you can't have it both fuckin' ways!' The bald headed man yelled. 'I sit here, looking at this shit all the time, and it makes absolutely no sense. What is the great design? Does it get you off...is if fluff? I have no idea what purpose this serves. Hearing two pompous pieces of crap sit on top of a mountain of trash bags speaking at first about weakness then proclaiming victory to have never been in jeopardy? YOU CAN'T HAVE IT BOTH FUCKIN' WAYS! Where did you dig this shit up from, a junior high drama elective?!' The Director scrambled to find his broken pieces of monocle, the crowd practically seething towards him. Was this drama really necessary, and better yet, what great purpose did it serve to the story ahead? But those were silent thoughts. And for the two actors, their pseudo brilliance was accepted.
