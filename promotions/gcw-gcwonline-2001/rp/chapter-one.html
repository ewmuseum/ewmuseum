Date: Timeless Act One ? An Unwanted Customer Scene opens in a shop, but this wasn’t an ordinary one, this was a special kind of shop that sold magical and mystical items. Jessica, the shopkeeper, stands up on a stepladder, searching for something on the top shelf of a tall bookcase. She takes out a small, glass bottle of magic potion and reads the tag attach to it. "Yup, this is it." Jessica climbs down and walks behind the counter, where she has a customer, an elder woman, standing wait at the front of the counter. "You’ll have to warm up this potion before each serving. You won’t get the full effect of the potion if it’s not warmed up." Jessica rings up the magic potion on the cash register and puts it in a paper, brown bag. After the elder woman paid Jessica for the magic potion, she takes the bag and leaves the shop through the front door. "Please come again." However, underneath Jessica’s gentle appearance lies great concern for a certain someone. It has been eight months since she has seen him last, but she has faith that he’ll return one day. Jessica would wait for him for all eternity if she has to. Her concern thoughts are broken when she heard a chirm from the front door. She turns to the door and gives a warm smile to the person who has entered her shop. "Welcome." In disgust, she turns to a short bookcase and starts to rearrange spell books on the middle shelf. "He hasn’t returned." The person she has spoken to in such a crude manner was Megumi, a priestess who’s in charge of a secret society known as The Light Maidens which is made up of other priestesses that defend and vanquish all evil that comes to this realm. She knew it was a mistake to come to the shop since Jessica wasn’t too fond of her and her society, but she needed to come to get some vital information from her. "Has he tried to contact you?" "No." Jessica turns and gives her a mean glare. "Even if he did contact me I wouldn’t tell you what he said." Megumi keeps her composure despite the cold treatment that she was receiving. "I understand that you don’t like me and the priestesses, but you must understand that he’s our only hope for this looming evil that approaches this realm." Jessica laughs in amusement before she looks at her sarcastically. "Right? What will happen to him once he cleans up this mess? Will you treat him like the hero is, or will you banish him to an eternal sleep like your kind did to him centuries ago." "I still don’t believe our goddess would do such a thing like that." Megumi scoffs and turns back to the short bookcase, where she resumes rearranging the books. "Of course you wouldn’t. You think your goddess is right about everything especially people." Megumi wasn’t about to allow Jessica to speak ill of her goddess, she storms up to the counter and glares at her. "Watch what you say about our goddess." Jessica hears threatening tone in Megumi’s voice, but she doesn’t bother to look back at her and just continues to do what she’s doing. "Just like a dog to bark when their master is being insulted." Megumi still glares at her. "What about you." Jessica raises a brow and looks at her in some irritation. "What about me." Megumi looks at her seriously. "You defend him passionately, but that’s all what you’re doing. If you really cared for him then you wouldn’t be in this shop, instead you would be searching for him at this second." Jessica was about to say something in response to that, but she was speechless since Megumi did bring up a valid point. She turns back to the bookcase, having a timid look in her eyes while she checks the books to see if they were in their appropriate spots. "This might be the first place he comes back to. So when he does come back then I’ll be here to greet him." Megumi shakes her head in disappointment, knowing that Jessica was lying, but she does what was the reason for her not going after the man she loves and bring him back here. She decides not to ask her about this and heads to the door. "You know?" Megumi has stopped at the door. "I think he needs you more than you need him." Jessica hears the chirm from the front door, but she still stands at the short bookcase in silence, looking more timid especially after she heard Megumi’s words. "Think about that’Rukia." Megumi was walking from the shop, hoping that her words would help Jessica resolve what ever was bothering her. Unbeknownst to them, there was another strong force, searching for this special man, but not for a good, instead it was for pure evil. Act Two ? Their Speculations In the command center of the secret organization, NEST, the workers have been working tirelessly to locate the power aura belonging to the same man that everyone is looking for. "Have you located him yet?" Misaki, the person in charge of this organization, enters the command center and looks at the workers impatiently since she was growing tired of this man not wanting to be found. "We haven’t found him yet." Misaki stabs her fingers that worker in the back of the head without a second thought. The other workers have stopped what they were doing, looking at her frighteningly after they witnessed her kill one of their own in such cruelty. "If you want to don’t want to end up like him then I better see some results." Misaki approaches her throne, where she sits and watches them work at their earnest to keep their lives. "Lady Misaki..." She turns to the voice and sees Zel, her personal bodyguard, has appeared out of nowhere. "What is it?" Zel kneels before her on a knee and bows his head down in respect. "Dr. White is here to see you." Misaki looks at him seriously, licking the worker’s blood off her fingers one at a time. "Bring her in." Dr. White, the organization’s head scientist, hovers in the command centers in her wheelchair, but she didn’t come alone though. She has a tall, muscular man that has metallic parts on various places on his body, accompanying her. Misaki ignores her and looks at what appears to be a cyborg curiously. "What have you created this time, White?" Dr. White smirks confidently. "This could be the answer to your missing person’s problem." She gives her an unimpressed look then looks back at the cyborg in the same manner. "Oh? It looks like one of failures to me." Dr. White still keeps her confident smirk. "This is a scouter droid that has the ability to detect any power aura from that of a demon, an angel, or anything else that has a high power aura." Misaki rests her elbow on the throne’s arm and smirks at her in amusement. "Didn’t you create one of these before that instantly got destroyed by him?" "Yes, I did, but I assure you that this one is much tougher and more powerful than the last one I created. He won’t stand a chance against the upgraded version." Misaki sighs in boredom and looks at her in the same manner. "If you say it like that then I suppose I’ll give you another chance to redeem yourself for your many failures." Dr. White bows to her in appreciation. "You won’t be disappointed." Meanwhile, in the organization’s sleeping quarters, Misaki’s trusted group of demonic warriors were resting in their own ways. Bash and Smash, the muscles of the group, are playing a serious game of poker at their side of the quarters. "You know, I think that bastard is actually hiding from us. He finally got smart and decided to stay out of our way." Smash smirks in response to his brother’s comment. "And here I thought this was the man had a pair of balls that were the size of a grapefruits. What a disappointment is all I can say." Tsunami, the timid one of the group, was feeding her goldfishes in their glass tank. She turns and looks at the brothers in shyness. "I don’t think he’s hiding." Bash takes a swig from his flash, wiping some traces of the alcoholic beverage from his mouth and looks at her sarcastically. "You would think that since you’re in love with that coward." Tsunami blushes and looks away from the brothers in embarrassment since they spoke the truth about her. Smash laughs at her in amusement. "They deserve each other since they’re both weak and cowards." Tsunami turns back to the fish take, having a sad look in her eyes while she watches her fishes chomp at their food. Bash and Smash are laughing, but they stop at the sight of the cards in their hands being slice apart. Moon, the leader of the group, sheathes one of swords and resumes her meditation on the end of her bed. "It takes a real coward to talk about someone behind their back." Despite being blind, Moon turns and faces the brothers seriously. "Cowards are also dishonorable people. I don’t allow both in my group, understand?" Bash and Smash turn away from her, having frightened looks in their eyes not wanting to make her angrier. Tsunami glances at her and gives her a small smile being glad that she stood up for her. "Guess who’s out of the infirmary." Volt, the jokester of the group, has entered the sleeping quarters. He has been in the organization’s infirmary for quite some time due to suffering a brutal defeat by the same missing person. Volt blinks and looks at his friends curiously, wondering why he was getting a cold vibe from them. "Did I miss something?" Tsunami looks and smiles at him being glad that her friend was finally out of the infirmary, but there was some concern in her eyes. "Welcome back. But how are you feeling?" Volt holds up his hand, where he forms a strong current of electricity around it, smirking in amusement about the return of his powers. "I feel shockingly good." Both brothers and Tsunami look at him in embarrassment after they heard that terrible pun from him. Volt looks at his friends confusingly. "I thought that was good one." Moon turns and faces Volt seriously. "I suppose you’ve heard that he has disappeared." Volt nods and looks down seriously. "Yeah, I’ve heard." "I want to know what your take on this is." Volt looks at her curiously not quite understanding what she wanted. Moon still looks at him seriously. "You were the last one to have fought him before his disappearance that’s why I want to know what your take is on this. Bash and Smash think that he’s hiding like a coward. Tsunami thinks otherwise, she thinks that he’s not hiding. What do you think?" Volt looks at her seriously. "I also think that he’s not hiding. I think he’s somewhere getting stronger for the next opponent he faces on the battlefield." He clutches his hand into a tight fist and looks down at it in anticipation, having strong currents of electricity around him in response to this feeling. "I almost can’t wait to face him again." Bash and Smash shake their heads, thinking that he’s still hiding some in cave in the fetal position like a coward that was waiting for death. Tsunami still hopes that he will return and show everyone that doubt him that he’s not a coward. Moon was in deep thought after she heard her Volt’s comments. She’s not quite sure, but she thinks this missing person has a strange way of attracting people to him. Act Three ? Shopping For Supplies In an abandon armory, Morgan, a priestess whose part of the Light Maidens, was searching for clues on the whereabouts of the missing person that Megumi seeks. She stops walking when she heard beeping from a charm that hangs down from her neck. Morgan grabs her charm and pushes a button on it, activating a communicator that has been installed in this piece of religious jewelry. "This is Priestess Morgan reporting." "Have you found anything?" She was speaking to Megumi who was back at the Grand Shrine with the rest of her priestesses. Morgan resumes her search in the laboratory. "I’ve discovered that someone broke into this place recently." "It must’ve been him. He’s the only one who knows the locations of the armories of his old unit. Did he take anything?" Morgan stops at a workbench, where she takes a glove off her hand and waves her hand over it, seeing faint, brief images of someone construct some kind of weapons there. She has a unique power which is the ability to see the past of an object or person with the wave of the hand. Morgan can also see the power aura of an object or person. She has to wear gloves all the time to keep herself from looking into the past. "He stocked up on ammunition for the weapons that he created here. I’ve also noticed something else." "What?" Morgan looks concern about the discovery that she made. "He’s not giving off a strong power aura. It’s almost like he lost his powers completely." "How can that be possible. He’s a Sinner. His kind are supposed to have unlimited power." Morgan wanted to answer, but she doesn’t have an answer to her tough question. She notices something from the corner of her eye and turns fully over in that direction, seeing metal doors that have been pried open. "I think I found something." She steps through the opened doors and makes the startling discovery of a hangar, where there is a collection of advance aircrafts that have gathered dust over the years of not being used. "There’s a hangar in the armory. It looks like an emergency hangar for the soldiers to make a quick escape." "Is a plane missing?" Morgan uses her powers again to see another faint, brief image of the missing person get inside of a plane and fly out the hangar after he used the plane’s missiles to blow up the hangar doors lead to the outside. "Yes." "Where could he have gone." Morgan walks to the empty spot once the plane was, but she stops when she stepped on something. She moves her foot and sees that she has stepped on what could possibly be a clue to his location. "I think I know where." She smiles and looks in content. Act Four ? Death Mark On the other side of the world, a lone figure was walking up to a nightclub that has a large bouncer standing at the entrance. The bouncer notices the figure is heading in his direction, he gets serious not trusting this figure. The lone figure was about to enter the club, but the bouncer holds out his arm and stops him from entering. The bouncer says something in a different language which sounded like Chinese, but he realizes the lone figure didn’t understand him so he decides to speak in English. "Do you have an invitation?" The lone figure slips a chuckle after he heard the bouncer’s poor English. "Invitation, you say." He smirks in amusement in his hood. The bouncer raises a brow and looks at him curiously, but he bursts through the club’s entrance from a hard punch. The lone figure has entered the club and looks down at the unconscious bouncer. "Don’t get up. I’ll let myself in." The lone figure walks further in and sees old men are watching the perversion of young girls in school uniforms who are singing in a pop song in Chinese. He sees the men are looking at him suspiciously, but he shrugs off their stares and starts to search for a certain someone in the club. The lone figure stops walking when he sees someone leave the kitchen, it was the same person that he was searching for. The person notices the lone figure in the distant and looks at him frighteningly. "Oh shit!" He runs back in the kitchen. The lone figure runs and enters the kitchen, but he ducks down, avoiding a cleaver shot to the head. The person has started to swing the cleaver around, but the lone figure avoids the attacks until he manages to grab the person at the wrist and forced the cleaver down on a cutting board. The person tries to pull out the cleaver, but the lone figure knocks his hand away and grabs him at the hair, where he slammed him face first on the cleaver’s hard handle. The lone figure lifts the person’s head up and reveals that his nose has been broken with blood coming out. The person glares at him. "You’re not a cop. Who the hell are you?" He looks in fear when he saw a dreadful, white skull through the cloak that the lone figure was wearing from the corner of his eye. "It’s you. I thought you were dead." "As you can see I’m very much alive and still kicking people’s asses." The lone figure reaches up to his hood and pulls it down, revealing that he was a Japanese man who looks like he doesn’t take shit from anyone. This person has been given many names in the past, but he goes under one and that name was the Deathscythe Outlaw Kyo Kushinaga. "I do believe you have some information for me."
