The Skye Is Falling (Part Two): Bad Romance

Date: 07/16/11 Once upon a time there was a girl who was given the world. I watched as she dazzled millions of testosterone-inspired men, mentally wanking at her very presence. She was graceful yet clumsy, hobbled by her own inexperience as luck took her to heights most men twice her age only dreamt about. Destiny is what she claimed it to be when she beat the unbeatable, did the unthinkable, and then attained the unattainable... the GCW World Heavyweight Championship. Many said it wouldn't last as she now stood in the lion's den, yet she never wavered. Fearlessly she stood up to every challenge laid before her, making believers out of even the biggest of cynics. She had become so much more than just another pretty face, she became a legend, building a dynasty out of over-coming the odds. Then out of the blue, it was gone. Her fall was far less dramatic than her rise, a bright-light slowly fading into obscurity before smoking in the embers. They would watch her and talk about the days she used to have, knowing that her golden touch had turned to stone. Frustrated she lashed out at the world, trying desperately to gain back that which she had lost. Tunnel-vision set in and soon nothing mattered to her except having the world back in her hands. I remember her tragic tale ending inside of a bar in Las Vegas, surrounded by drunkards and burnouts. She no longer shone like the golden beacon she once was. She was just another girl with a tragic story to tell. I met her that night without a clue of who she was or where she came from. She lied to me out of fear of her story coming to light, but there was something special about her. It was an undeniable charisma that graced everyone whose hearts she touched. Even in her darkest days her true smile showed the fire not quite extinguished. This world still had a place for her, and she made sure that this world still had a place for me as well. I could relate to her story. A small brush with semi-stardom in the rock and roll scene gave me a taste of what I knew was my calling in life. She taught me not to be content with a retail job that paid the bills when I could be chasing my dreams just as she did. Together we could lift the world on our shoulders. There was nothing that could keep us down... as long as we had each other. The way she looked at me earlier tonight made me realize that this was all part of her destiny. We were supposed to be together, and even though I don't believe in God, I know someone... or something out there had a plan for us. Then she gave me the world, and when I pressed my lips against hers my heart nearly exploded from my chest. A hole was finally filled within me and for the first time in my life I felt complete. I backed away to look at her, to convince myself that what I felt was true. Instead what I saw was a girl who was terrified of her own feelings, looking back at me in silence leaving only our emotions sitting still in the night. It was love that we shared, and I tried so hard to let her know that I would take care of her. I would never let her fall from grace again. If she could only see what I saw. Experience the things that I felt. "I'm sorry, Amanda." She choked back to me, horrified that those words would pierce my heart, "I don't think I feel the same way you do." It was like a dagger to my chest. With the wind out of my sails I tried to do anything I could to make the world right again. She couldn't give me the world and just take it away... she loved me, and I loved her. "When I'm with you I just feel... alive." I convinced her, "I know you feel it too, Vivica. I feel like I know you better than you know you, and you know me better than anyone else ever could." "I didn't mean to lead you on." She backed away, with every step the dagger twisted in the wound, "I care about you a lot, Amanda. I really do, you're practically the only friend I got and I appreciate you more than you'll even know but... I'm just not that kinda girl. I hope you respect that." But I could change that, I had to make her see that she was my everything. "I'm sorry for coming on so strong..." I opened up to her, letting all these pent up emotions flow past my lips, "...but it's who I am and how I feel. I can't take this lying bullshit anymore. I just can't. I can't live that way. I can't stand this close to you and not feel this way. I want to be closer to you. I want to give you the world." "I think you just need a little time to let everything settle in." Vivica spoke cautiously, "This house thing is kind of a big deal, you need to sleep on it." I watched the expression on her face completely changed. For the first time in my life I saw fear overtake the fearless. There weren't enough hours of old DVD footage to prepare me for what I had seen. I wanted so badly to reach out and give her the strength to fight her fears, but when you're the monster that stands across from her... you feel so helpless. The best thing to do was to do what I had always done. Keep those feelings to myself and watch as everything I ever wanted inched closer to the door. "I'm going to leave." She broke the silence that had overtaken the room, "We both need some time to think." Screams of "don't leave me here alone" echoed inside my heart, but only silences made it to the surface. Hanging my head with disappointment, I shared a final glance with her before she left me to my own devices. Heartbroken and alone, that's when I realized this fairly tale wasn't going to end with a happily ever after. Reality never does. ---- The Skye Is Falling (Part Two): Bad Romance ---- "Game Over."
