Intro to a Schizo

Date: Timeless An orange glow filled the sky as the sun peaked its way above the horizon, casting looming shadows across the landscape. It was fairly quiet. A slight breeze rustled through the fallen leaves that lay across the asphalt. Other than the pattering of a runaway leaf, there was no sign of life or movement? Unless, of course, you paid close attention. Above the disturbance of the fallen autumn leaves was a continuous tapping sound. Tap. Tap. Tap. Sat in the cold temperatures of the impending winter months was a figure. A male figure sat wearing no shirt, baggy jeans and scruffy, worn shoes. He was sat in the shadows of a large green, metal dumpster. His knees were brought up and he was hugging them towards him with his left arm. His right arm was moving up and down in what appeared to be the same rhythm as the repetitive tapping sound. Tap. Tap. Tap. Upon closer inspection, the figure’s hand could be seen grasped tightly around a fallen twig and he was continuously tapping it on the floor in front of him. This was not the only strange thing about his persona. He was rocking back and forth. However, it did not appear to be due to the cold that he was moving. He seemed apparently unaffected by the cold, or at least he could tolerate it. He just sat there? rocking? and tapping? Tap. Tap. Tap. If it wasn’t for the tapping of the twig and the ritualistic rocking, na’ve passers by may believe that he was dead. His eyes were permanently fixed open, staring blankly into the space in front of him, his long, unkempt hair hanging slightly over his face in places. From what could be seen behind the hair covering his face, he seemed only young. A mere teenager sat in the cold without adequate clothing for the current climate. Tap. Tap. SNAP! The stick broke in two. The part of the stick that was now free from the tapping flicked away, but still the teenage male sat rocking and moving his hand as if the tapping still continued? even though there was now silence. He stopped and looked down at the stick. You can’t do a single thing right. You even failed at tapping a stick. TAP TAP TAP! That’s all it takes! Useless! He clasped both of his hands over his face and began to rock a little faster, shaking his head. It was at this point that he felt a hand on his shoulder. He chose to ignore it and continued with his rocking, shaking his head. “You alright there, son” It’s a little cold to be sitting with no shirt on don’t you think?” He stopped shaking his head and looked up at a man stood in front of him. He continued to rock. He felt a little comfort from the presence of the man. He was fairly old looking and wore a brown hat and a brown overcoat. He looked nice and toasty. Even his top lip was insulated by a thick, graying moustache. The man held out his right hand. He was wearing brown leather gloves. “The name’s Ernest. Pleased to meet you.” Be careful? he wants something? The rocking stopped. The shirtless teenager took Ernest’s hand and shook it cautiously. “I’m Benjamin?” He spoke with a British accent. Be careful! He’s after something? “Be quiet! He’ll hear you!” Benjamin put his finger to his lip and began to “Shush”, followed by more rocking. Ernest was taken slightly by surprise at this abnormal behavior. “Erm” How old are you, Benjamin? Shouldn’t you be at home with your family?” Benjamin began to scrape the remains of the stick back and forth along the floor. Be careful of his questions? “Shush!” I’m? I’m? I’m? Eighteen? Yeah?” “Eighteen” Why are you out in the cold with no shirt? Do your family know where you’re at?” “They live in England?” He threw the stick to one side and hugged both of his knees and continued his rocking. He is concealing things. Keeping things secret from you. Things he doesn’t want you to know. Why doesn’t he show you what’s in his pockets or under his hat? Benjamin started staring at the man’s hat, still rocking and hugging his knees. “So you’re here on your own” At 18? Where do you live?” “Out here?” replied Benjamin, still fixating his gaze on the man’s hat. “You mean you’re living on the streets?” Benjamin nodded furiously. “I bet you’re hungry then, huh” I mean, you cant have that much money if you’re out on the streets? so I’m gonna assume you’re hungry, right?” Ernest will feed you poisons. Poisons. Poisons. Poisons. Benjamin stopped his rocking and his right arm moved down and hugged his stomach. He hadn’t eaten in over 24 hours. Benjamin just nodded. His rocking slowly built up again. “Come on then.” Ernest extended his hand out and pulled Benjamin to his feet. DON’T LET HIM TOUCH YOU! Benjamin, now on his feet, snapped his hand away from Ernest and backed up towards the green dumpster he’d previously been sat against. “Hey” its alright. I was just helping you to your feet. I live a couple of blocks away from here. Come on, we’ll go get you some food, get you warmed up and I’ll see if I can find you a shirt to wear. How does that sound?” Lies. Benjamin stared at Ernest. He was still troubled by the hat. And something in his mind wondered what was in his pockets. However, he cautiously complied and slowly walked towards Ernest. Benjamin wrapped both his arms around his mid-section and walked towards Ernest with his head hung slightly. Ernest’s vision was directed at Benjamin’s chest. He had strange marks tattooed across this left pectoral. “Peculiar tattoo you have there. Does it mean anything?” Benjamin looked down at the tattoo on his chest. “Its Greek for “schizo”. It means “to shatter” in Greek.” “Oh yeah” You speak Greek?” Why ask questions? “I never tried?” Ernest turned around and began to walk and Benjamin followed, a good five paces behind. Ernest looked around and noticed that Benjamin was walking so far behind him. He stopped and turned around. But as soon as he halted, Benjamin froze and stood staring, his head still hanging slightly so his hair hung over his face slightly in a menacing manner. Ernest smiles slightly. “Come walk along side me” you don’t have to walk behind me.” He wants to hurt you! Ernest put his hands in his pockets. It happened almost as if in slow motion. His pockets! Benjamin watched as his gloved hands slowly slipped into his pockets. Benjamin’s eyes widened and he suddenly took a defensive stance. Watch him? “Come on, let’s g?” Ernest didn’t have time to finish his sentence. He pulled one of his hands out of his pocket. GO! Benjamin sprinted at him. In one swift movement, Benjamin leapt off his feet and caught Ernest in the chin with a spinning heel kick, knocking him to the floor. His hat flew backwards onto the road. Benjamin stood over Ernest as he groaned. He slowly rolled onto his stomach and managed to hoist himself to his hands and knees. He will retaliate! Benjamin stepped back slightly and then swung a kick into Ernest’s ribs. Ernest yelped and rolled over several times. Benjamin walked over to him and grabbed Ernest’s hair, pulling him back to his feet. A line of blood trickled from Ernest’s lower lip and his once grayish moustache was now a fairly grim shade of red. Benjamin pushed him against a wall and unleashed a combination of kicks and punches to the defenseless old man. You need to get out of here. Make sure he cannot follow you. “Do not tell me what to do!” You need to get out of here. Make sure he cannot follow you. He scrunched up his eyes and pulled hard on his hair. Upon opening his eyes, he swung a swift left hook to Ernest. Ernest stumbled forward slightly as Benjamin picked up one of the several trashcans stood in line along the sidewalk. With a sickening thud, he brought it down on Ernest’s back and he fell to the floor. You killed him... Benjamin stood, panting, holding the trashcan. He stood staring at Ernest’s seemingly lifeless body. He was going to kill you... Was he going to kill Benjamin? Benjamin just stood staring. He finally dropped the trashcan? and ran. Ernest groaned. -Fade to black-
