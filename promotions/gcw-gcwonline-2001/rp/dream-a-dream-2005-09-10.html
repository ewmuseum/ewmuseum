Dream a Dream...

10 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/10/05 The bright sun, with an orange hue to it, peeks slightly over the ridge on the horizon. A ray of light flickers softly through the trees, and drops heavily onto a window sill to a small broke down shack. The shack appears to be abandoned, and uninhabited for a number of years. A small rabbit hops across the front of the shack, but is hidden in the grass. He quietly sniffs the area in search of food, when the front door swings open. The rabbit startled by the sound bounds away into the safety of the trees. A man staggers from the house, looking as if he has not had human contact in years. His beard full and long, as is his hair. His hair is matted down, as though a comb has not touched it in a decade or longer. The man makes his way off the porch and walks around the side of the shack, to a small outhouse. As he heads inside, the door slams shut behind him, pulled closed by a rusted spring. The man takes a seat on the toilet and grabs a magazine stuck in a make-shift holder attached to the wall. He flips it open to a dog-eared page and stares at a picture of a wrestler diving off the top rope. The man starts rubbing his leg, and talking to himself? “Damn you are sexy?” The mans hands run up to his chest and across his stomach as he continues to talk “The things I would do to you Ricky?” Suddenly the outhouse door swings open, the man obviously startled jumps to attention. Standing in the doorway, sporting his famous smile, is Ricky Thunder. “Howdy there kind sir, I was just passing through and saw this here vintage outhouse, and had to stop in to see it. Am I interrupting?” “Not “.particularly”.Just having my mornin? shi?.” “All righty! Wouldn’t want to interrupt! Hey Sylkk, come look at this outhouse! This is super sweet!” A voice comes from the driveway where another man stands by the car. “No thanks’seen em before” “All right, but it’s your loss!” Ricky turns his attention back to the man who is still sitting on the toilet and smiles? “You build this?” “Uhh, can we talk about this when?” “HEY!! Is that July’s edition?” Ricky asks as he points to the magazine, “Actually it’is’I was just..uhh?” Ricky snatches the magazine and notices the article is opened to his page. He looks at the guy, and points to the article. “Have you read this stuff” Man I rock!” “Yes, I have actually”.I “.enjoy’that article.” “I don’t blame you at all, and I must say, I am one sexy sonofa?” The voice comes from the driveway “You ready to go yet?” “Yeah I guess so?” Ricky tosses the magazine back to the man, then peeks towards the driveway, then back at the man still sitting on the commode? “Hope I didn’t make ya uneasy by just barging in like that” “Not at all. To be honest’I kinda have a thing for you’I’m gay see”:” “Hey I don’t blame you’If I was gay, I would do me..” Ricky retorts. “Can I have a second please?” Ricky steps away from the outhouse letting the door slam shut and takes a look around the ragged property. The man emerges from the outhouse in a matter of moments and clears his throat. “Ricky I have waited for this day for many years now. Ever since you first emerged on the scene in CCW. I am definitely one of your biggest fans.” “Well it’s always nice to meet a fan!” “Would you be offended if I told you I fantasize about you?” “Not at all chum, hell I fantasize about me too!” “Well then would you be offended if I told you I wanted to have sex with you?” “Not at all”.do you?” “The man looks around sheepishly and replies “Yes” “Well hell then lets head inside bud!” Ricky leads the way to the door, and hollers at Sylkk, I’ll be out in a few “Jesus Ricky, are you”.” “Yes’I’am?” The man opens the door for Ricky smiling ear to ear. As he leads them through the broke down house to the bedroom, Ricky takes a look around. Everything is fairly dim and poorly lit, yet everything is visible. As the man opens the door, a obnoxiously loud alarm sounds. “Ugh, what the hell is that” Ricky mutters? He slowly rolls over and hits the snooze button on the alarm clock, before settling back into bed. Suddenly he jerks to an upright position and rubs his eyes. He leaps out of bed and looks around, whipping open the blinds and seeing the city streets of Orlando outside his apartment window. “Man, that was a screwed up dream! What the hell is wrong with me?” Ricky shrugs it off and cracks that award winning smile before heading into the bathroom and starting his day.
