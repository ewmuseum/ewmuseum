Passion of the Geek

11 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jun 11, 2006 A certain requisite of geekdom is being in tune with the online fanboy scene. It doesn't matter if you're a game geek, Star Wars geek, anime geek, YuGiOh geek, wrestling geek, etc... Geekdom in and of itself is intrinsically the need to know way more than you should about a hobby. In this day and age, it is essential for the geek to be wired. That's why there are two computers set up in the basement of Meatshield Trainwrecker's rural Missouri home. Together, GCW's d20 Duo, The Guild can rape the internet superhighway of any and all knowledge of things 'geek'. They can tell you the newest developments of over a dozen next generation MMORPG's. They can link you to any of a hundred WoW movies. They have six different plot outlines for the next Harry Potter book they can refer you to. They know the component costs involved in manufacturing the first run of the Playstation III's. If you need some inside knowledge to spark a conversation around the water cooler, they can provide it. However, what they can't tell you is the direction of GCW past WARPATH. Anyone who's even remotely paying attention to GCW knows that big changes are in the making. New feuds, new formats, new everything. Wrestling smark websites are loaded with rumors of mass turnover and new booking teams. Even gcwonline.net admits that notices of new instructions for all wrestlers are being distributed, effective immeadiately. Web smarks are busy predicting the eventual fallout and creating fanboy senarios that usually end up with Dawn McFarlane and Danielle Northsky dyking it out in the middle of the ring, but that's not the primary concern here. To The Rogue Paladin, the most pressing development is the continuing silence of the telephone, and the utter emptiness of the inbox of [detail withheld] . Meat continues his heavyhanded keyboard technique, searching through all the wrestling info sites he can stand to popup block against, desperately searching for any hint of The Guild in GCW's future plans. A disheartened sigh signals his growing disappointment. 'I'm not finding anything here' The Rogue Paladin nods grimly, clicking on the 'send/receive' tab for the n'th time in the last hour. Nothing... 'Same here..' Meatshield pushes himself away from the desk and rises out of his chair, pacing angrily around the basement. 'This is bullshit. They're gonna release us!' The Rogue Paladin continues typing into his blog calmly. 'What did you expect, Meat? We're not cool. We're not 'evil'. We're not Scotch-Nipponese ninja fairy-monkey vampire angels in thongs. We didn't drink the kool-aid, so we're being shown the door.' Meat stomps around the basement, looking for his 2 liter bottle of Mountain Dew. His nerves were fraying with each passing moment. 'So how are you so damn calm about this?' Only now does The Rogue Paladin stop his online diatribe, turning to look at his friend. 'Oh, I'm plenty pissed. I'm fucking irate. I'm just saving it.' Meat finishes downing the remnant of his dewski, cocking his head to the side. A few sparce dibbles that somehow made their way to his chin are wiped aside by a massive forearm. 'For what? The fix is in. It's not just the unholy order of St. Self-Absorbed, the whole fucking company is avoiding us. I wouldn't be surprised if Caldera sends his sumo assistant down to the ring as an insurace policy. There's nothing we can do.' The Rogue Paladin climbs out of his chair, a fire burning behind his eyes despite his calm demeanor. 'No. There is a way. I have a plan.' 'What?' 'We win.' Meat just stares, deadpan. 'That's it? The Al Davis methodology?' Paladin puts his hands on his hips, striking the iconic hero pose. There is no fear in his face, even on the cusp of being unceremoniously tossed from the business he's come to love. 'That's it. That's the whole plan. We win and monkey wrench everyone's plans. GCW wants us out because we don't fit in? Too bad, we've got the fucking titles. Dies Irae wants us out because we don't fit into their imaginary worldview? Tough shit, here's a couple of guys who admit that monsters and demons are geeky figments of imagination. One win, Meat. One win, and our very presence is a goddamn kick in the face for anyone who thinks we don't belong. One win.' Meatshield's head is fallen, staring at the floor. His hands shake before slowly before forming into fists. As he looks up, his face is flushed. His embarrassment at having despaired is rapidly abating. His eyes slowly focus into the middle distance, as if he were staring down some opposing army from across a field. Meatshield's momentary crisis of faith is now resolved. To punctuate this, a loud smack echoes in the basement as Meat slams a massive fist into his palm. 'One win to fuck 'em all...' 'One win to remind them...' 'One win to capture the tag team titles...' 'And in our geek-ness bind them...' And so it is decided. The Guild will not go quietly into the night, nor will they forfeit their individuality to some cult that demands their understanding but offers none. Why it has fallen to two geeks from Missouri to defend the principles that GCW was founded on, we may never know; but this burden has fallen to them, and the take it willingly. They understand the consequences that face all those in GCW. Fight and you may be fired. Join Dies Irae and you'll work -- at least a while. And dying in your beds many years from now, would you be willing to trade all the shows from this day to that for one chance, just one chance to come back here and tell our enemies that they may take our jobs, but they'll never take our geekdom!!!
