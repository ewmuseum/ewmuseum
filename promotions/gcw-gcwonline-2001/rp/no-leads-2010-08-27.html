No Leads.

27 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/27/10 Katrina Diamond: Wake up sunshine, the police are on the phone and they want to talk to you. Something about Larry I think they said. [Turning over to reach the phone from her hands I realize how much I hate the sun as it shines in my face.] Donny: Yeah? Police Officer: Hi Mr. Diamond, I'm so sorry to bother you at such early hours in the morning, but last night it seems your friend Larry Sniztz was murdered. We need you to come down to the station so we can ask you a few questions pertaining to our investigation. Donny: Larry was murdered huh? Wow that's horrible... Yeah I'll be down there in a bit. Police Officer: All right, thanks for coming down to help us sir. Donny: No problem. Anything to help catch the bastards that did this to Larry. [As I hang the phone up I notice Katrina looking at me with a nervous stare. Laying back down for a moment I pass my hands through my hair, taking in a little relaxation before getting out of bed.] Katrina: What happened with Larry? Donny: From what they said Larry was murdered last night. Katrina: Seriously? Wow. I never liked the old man, but I never wished for anything like that to happen. Donny: Well... It happens I guess. Anyway I got to get dressed, they want me to come down to the station to ask me some questions. Katrina: Need me to drive you over? Donny: Nah, I think it would be best if I went alone to get my head together before I got there and all. You understand right? Katrina: Of course I do. While you go and do all of that I'll go out shopping and later tonight we can go out for dinner. [Sliding out of bed I head towards the bathroom to dress, before heading out. A little time passes by for me to get ready, but soon I'm done as I head outside towards my car. I see her standing in the door way smiling my way and waving goodbye as I close the door and drive off.] Before I went to my room where Katrina was waiting for me, I heard the sirens and I knew exactly what they were for. The police can call me in for some questions, because I know they have no solid leads. This wasn't the first time I had to do something like this. This wasn't the first time someone promised to lead me to the top, only to have me learn it was nothing but lies. Ten years ago my dad promised me the same things and the he was forced to endure the same faith. Burning Larry in the dumpster wasn't the first time I had to do such a thing, which means I know how to cover my tracks. Let the police ask whatever questions they please, but there is no way they will get any actual information from me. [Arriving at the L.A.P.D. I walk inside and glance over at the common scum inside waiting to be booked. At the front desk I lean down on the counter as my sunglasses slide down my nose a bit while I wait for an officer.] Donny: Anyone in there? I'm here to answer some questions about the Sniztz murder. Police Officer: Sniztz? Right, the burning dumpster from last night, I believe Detective Jordon is in charge of that investigation. Donny: And where exactly is this Detective Jordon? Police Officer: Go down the right hall and take the first left. Then it'll be the fourth door on your right. [Turning around from the desk I begin the walk as I feel the eyes of the convicted staring at me. They all think I'm the same as them, but they're wrong because I would never take a life to achieve something as pitiful as the crimes they've committed. Now opening the door the detective's office, I walk inside where I see him slaving over some paperwork on his desk.] Donny: So you're the one that called me in here for some questions right? Detective Jordon: Yeah that would be me. Sorry I didn't see you walk in, I've been going over the information gathered from the crime scene. Take a seat and we can get started. Donny: I rather stand. Detective Jordon: Okay. Well you know of course we talked to Mr. Sniztz's training partner Benjamin Larther and he said the two of them have been training you for a wrestling promotion called GCW? Donny: Well seeing as I am a professional wrestler that would be correct. Detective Jordon: Could you maybe tell me a little about the relationship between the two of you? Donny: Well last time I saw Larry he was training me for an upcoming title match. He was a nice old man, I've known Larry for years. He helped me get into the business actually. Detective Jordon: And did it all work out for you? Because from what Mr. Larther has been saying you are a very violent man when it comes to what you do for work. Donny: In fact it did not work out for me, but I tell you one thing detective, I don't like what your implying here. My business is what it is and in order to build a reputation like mine, some times you got to play a little dirty. But none of that means I would ever lay a hand on Larry. Detective Jordon: You just have to see it from my point of view is all I'm saying. The man had no known enemies, he trained you to be a monster basically, and aside from Mr. Larther no one else even goes into that Gym. So maybe you got tired of things not going your way and decided to make him pay for it. Donny: Maybe you have all the answers, but the fact remains that you have no proof. So instead of wasting my time how about you go and ask Ben some of these questions. Hell... Maybe he was just a little pissed off Larry was training me instead of him. I'm done here, so until you actually find some use for my time don't bother calling my home again. [Out of the arrogant detective's office I walk and back outside I go. Once back in my car I drive around for a bit.] Flashback: [Sitting in my locker room I lace up my sparkling yellow boots as I look up to the door and see my dad walk in. I continue getting my attire ready for my upcoming match as I hear chants of "Star" from inside the arena.] Donny: Hey dad, what's up? James: Nothing much, just coming to wish my boy good luck out there tonight. Your music hasn't even started to play yet and all those people are cheering you on, they really love you son. Donny: Yeah and I love going out there and giving them a match they will never forget. Any idea who I'll be facing tonight? James: Well the tournament is still going on, but I guess you'll be facing whoever wins the next match. You just need to remember that it doesn't matter who your opponent is; stick to your game plan, believe in yourself and no one will be able to stop you. Donny: Not when I have the best trainer in the world! James: Nah, it's all you kiddo, it's all you! If you keep it up out there, no one will be able to deny your dreams of becoming a champion. Just keep working at it and one day soon, we'll be on top of the mountain. [Nodding to my dad I pass a comb through my bleach blonde hair as I hear my music starting to play. I head out the locker room door as the chants of "Star" begin getting louder.] - How dare that arrogant bastard try to push me into giving him something to go on. If he wants to squeeze some information out of me, he's going to have to put forth a much larger effort. The Los Angelas police department is filled with tired cops looking for an easy break in a case, but it just so happens that they will soon be getting one. It's just to bad that poor Ben is going to be the one that takes the fall... [Pulling into the parking lot of the restaurant I that Katrina's car is no where to be seen. Looking at the clock in my car and it reads "5:07PM", I pick up my cell phone and give Katrina a call. After a few rings the voice mail picks up.] Donny: Katrina I'm here at the restaurant, but I don't see your car. I'm going to wait around for a few minutes before heading home. [Hanging the phone up, I turn on the radio and lay back for a bit to pass some time. Thirty minutes or so pass and I drive around the parking lot, with no sight of Katrina's car before starting my way back home.] Did she know I would be manipulated so easily? Did she come back just to set this up, so she could get rid of me for good? She said she was here to help me... She said she was going to meet me for dinner... Would she really do this to me? She has done so much to me in the past, put me through so much pain... No! I was played for a fool! She used this dinner date to keep me away so she would have time to go to the police and give them the real story on what I had done... [As I drive home I notice the sun is finally starting to set, and I notice Larry's gym is coming into view. Slowing down I turn down the alley and drive around to the back. Stepping outside I notice the police tape has been cut. Curiosity gets the best of me as I kick open the already half-opened door and walk inside, as this would be the perfect time to set Ben up as the murderer. All of the lights in the gym are off, except for the one in the next room. That's where I head next and I become shocked as I see what is there to greet me.] [A sole light shines down over the wrestling ring and facing the door from whence I came is Ben. Blood covers his face and his head is lowered, his arms tied out from his side by use of the ring ropes and his feet bond together against the apron. It seems as if he was being crucified onto the outside of the ring, a sight that makes my heart flutter with joy. It's such an empowering presentation that the real reason for me coming here completely slips my mind.] [I'm addicted to the masterful work of human art before me and I can't help but to walk closer. The urge is to hard to resist as I extend my hand and pass my finger tips in the blood that drips off Ben's chin. Words could never describe how wonderful I feel from his pain and misfortune, it's to bad that he is no longer conscience. Everything at the moment seems so perfect until I snap back into reality at the sound of a door closing in the distance. Turning around I see no one in the darkness, I believe it was simply the wind, until I hear a voice.] ?: I knew this would be the perfect way to lure you here. You're caught red-handed and now there is no way for you to lie your way out of it. She set me up... - Dedicated to Jasmine. 1998-2010.
