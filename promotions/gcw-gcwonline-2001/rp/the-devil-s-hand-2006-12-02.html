The Devil's Hand

2 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/02/06 ~Princess Lilian Suite~ ~Grand H?tel Stockholm~ The hotel was the grandest in Stockholm, Sweden. It was next to the royal palace and had a view of the magnificent harbor that made the city even more of a jewel of northern Europe then it got a lot of credit for. Not many Americans ever get to come to a place like this. Not many do at all. But Blaise wasn't here for a good time. He was here to do two things. The first one was to beat the ever living hell out of a man who called himself a fallen luchadore. La Sombra. The Shade in spanish. A man who was supposed to be a shadow of his former self. Shadow he's not. But we'll get to him later. We've had his card so many times it's pretty much become a hobby of Blaise's to beat the hell out of him. But this week, a new victim steps in, a man named Aaron Rain. What has he done to earn himself a Cruiserweight Title shot? Has he even been on the winning side of any match? In all honesty, he's barely a blip on the radar when it comes to a group like the KnightMare Brigade: Dies Irae. He's not shit. But as with real people in a real world, their job can only cover so much of their life. For people like Blaise, it covers only a few scant hours a week. Mostly because he's got quite a few other things to worry about in this world. But part of what he has to worry about deals with his group, the KB:DI outside of the ring. One man controls many things with it, things that he can't control because he is but a soldier in a war that encompasses the entire world. That man is named Geoffrey Ian Childress III, and he's married to Blaise's cousin, Maeb Fey. And he's also GCW's VP of Production. Blaise was staying just down the hall from them at the top of the hotel. He and Ukyo had flown in earlier in the day, and it was not time for Blaise to check in with him. He walked up towards the door, and a man was standing there. Blaise could tell it was another of the Wild Hunt. They had taken being the official guards of Fey until her tree took full root. Her coronation and everything was slated for New Years Day back in Texas. In the meantime, no one was getting close to her unless they wanted to deal with some major shit that would probably rape their minds, let alone fuck them up on the physical level. It would make most stuff that H.P. Lovecraft ever wrote seem like a Stephen King farce. The guard knew Blaise though, or Prince Turenn as he called him. So he let Blaise pass with a wide open door. Blaise stepped in and noticed that Ian was already waiting for him, seated in one of the side meeting rooms wearing a pair of jeans and not much else. The bastard had to have known he was coming. Yeah, there was the proof. Fey had already set out another cup of tea and a small plate of finger foods on the other side of the table in front of him. Ian sat, reading a book, and eating some of the foods himself. ?How do you always know?? Ian looked up at him with his ice blue eyes. ?Remember, I am active in more then one realm at all times. There is little in this hotel that I could not know if I wanted to. Other then that, I'm mostly watching the immediate area, making sure you guys are all kept safe while you're here.? Blaise didn't answer, he just picked up a small morsel of food and ate it, so as to not be a poor guest. ?How is Ukyo doing?? Blaise expected that question. ?She's doing well. Wearing a bandaid on her forehead in public to keep the facade up. But I healed her right after it happened. I think it took a bit of her spirit though. I mean, we sit there and yes, we give them a ration of shit, and be take from them what we want. But we don't get into matches. Most of the time we wait till AFTER the match. And what happens? They think they can hold our fate in their hands. They fuck things up and people still cheer them for it.? Ian took a sip from his Tea. ?Do you think there will be a problem with Keiko and Hope taking the spot?? Blaise shook his head. ?No, they deserve it. A situation came up, they capitalized on it. Those are the rules. They get the shot. And you know the Brothers will give those girls a fight.? Ian nodded. ?That's good. But you're up here to ask me other questions.? He knew it all too well. ?Why did we do the assault on LaBatte's place? Why did we do it that way?? Ian put his cup down. ?Simple. I wanted them to know who did it. I wanted them to know that it's time that they pull themselves back a bit. They've been a bit open about some of their things as of late.? Blaise shook his head. ?Why LaBatte?? Ian answered quickly. ?Three reasons. The first was that I promised Alexis that I'd get him for her. She did a wonderful job in getting information out of him. I'll be honest, he wasn't very strong. He cracked quickly. But when it came down to it, almost everything he said was verified when we fed our backup interrogator.? That made Blaise shiver. So the illithid had some brains to go with it all after all. But that was a solid strategy too. Get them to talk and then verify it when someone gains all of their memories. ?And the other two?? Ian smiled. ?I had promised Lia that I'd take care of him. He had a long standing issue with them. She was one of her hits she never finished, and he tried to kill her and Virgil once by burning virgil's old estate down in Napa Valley.? ?Okay, so he's an asshole.? ?Yes, an asshole who was the #5 man in an organization we're at war with. And in the end, he told us the names of #4, #3 and #2, and where they are.? ?#2? We know who The Roman's Hand is?? Ian's eyes rolled. ?You've known.? Ian then glared at him across the table. ?Yes, I've known. I know that man personally. And now we know where he is also. And in that, that makes #3. It was for me, personal.? Blaise grinned. ?I's Sinclair, isn't it?.? Blaise nodded as if he'd just had a big eureka moment. Ian didn't answer. ?That's why you wanted Lia to come in. That's why you've campaigned for that. And that's why it was a bit production. You wanted Sinclair to know you're still there.? Ian didn't show any emotion. And that to Blaise was the biggest confirmation. ?And that's what Lia is giving you in payment for this time frame, isn't it?? Ian didn't answer again. Blaise grinned as if he had a eureka moment. ?You son of a bitch.? That got Ian's attention a bit. ?Excuse me?? ?You're trying to do it aren't you. You're trying to turn her into...? Ian cut him off. ?Before you say it, Blaise. IF I was a paragon, you would be my hand, and you know this.? Blaise shook his hand. ?You don't have to be a paragon. I've read the books and that. You're predecessor had...? Ian stood up. ?Colonel Southerland had a Hand, that is true. He was rather pretentious. And if he had a hand, you would have fallen into that spot. For his hand was a military leader, and that was Maury.? Blaise knew he'd touched someplace he shouldn't have, but he had to know. Michael Jefferson Preest, Maury to his friends, had been the man who had brought Ian under his wing and brought him into the Order. The seven foot monster had been killed in the ring by the very man they spoke of now, the man Ian had taken the Order from on a debt of honor over Maury's death. ?Yes, I know I'm in Maury's spot right now. And it's an honor Ian...? Ian finished his next thought a bit. ?But...?? Blaise got up and walked over to the table that had some pictures laid out upon it. Picture of family and friends of today and yesterday, including one of Blaise and Becca from right after when his daughters were born, with the two newborns right in front of them. One of the pictures was that of the six foot one inch man who everyone knew as Ian Childress, standing next to a large powerful looking man with a wild man of blonde hair and a beard. Blaise picked the picture up and looked at it. The two looked to be very close friends. Suddenly he walked over and put it down in front of Ian. ?Yes, Maury was the right hand of Colonel Southerland. But he had two hands. One, the right hand was the hand of war. But the other? His left hand was the one that would stab you in the back if he needed it. I may be in Maury's place. But, Ian, who's in your old spot?? That left Ian standing there silently. ?You'll excuse me, Mr. Childress... Colonel. But I need to get ready for my match this week. And don't worry, I can keep your secret for a while. I know she's mentioned though, that you had a personal job for her in payment for your taking her under your wings, oh Dark Seraph. She's a trained assassin, and a good one. Just do me a favor though, Colonel.? Ian looked up from the picture. ?What would that be, Captain?? Blaise turned around and started to leave. ?Don't get my friend killed for your little personal battle with the bastard.? Blaise kept going, and went right back out the door he'd come in. He had done enough for tonight, and he had more then he came with. His only worry was that this will become something bigger then they really want to be involved in at this time. ~Geoffri LaBatte's Estate~ ~Outside of Cleveland, Ohio~ ?I can't believe this. Who would do such a thing?? The man, young looking, with slick hair in a small ponytail looked upon what would have been given to him as his estate in disgust. Bodies laid about, bodies that were once family, friends, tools, allies and brethren. Not to mention some that were food as far as he was concerned. His suit was freshly cleaned and pressed, he looked the new role he had to take on. He was the new leader of the Giovanni Vampire Clan in Cleveland. After the death of the previous two leaders in the underground cavern of this very site. Luciano Giovanni looked on in disgust as the many bodies were cleaned up, and burned to remove any evidence of their kind ever being here. He shook his head as he saw the damage that had been done to the cars, the vans and the front of the estate by what survivors had called a large beast of a man swinging a tree. ?You know, I blame LaBatte for this! His dealings were never clean. I don't care if they were going to make him Pope, he was a worthless slime of a man who we should have let Shadow KILL!? He slammed his hand down onto the trunk of his expensive Lexus. Almost every vampire was dead. Including the previous two heads of the clan. The only survivors had been humans under LaBatte's or their employ, mostly ghouls or wannabes who had been bought and paid for. Luciano looked at his assistant. ?Do we know who did this?? The woman looked at him, and shook her head. ?No, we don't. But from what we understand, HE has sent his hound here to find out.? Luciano's eyes went big and his mouth went slackjawed. The hound? Here? Why? ?Why would he be coming here? This is none of his business. This is Giovanni business!? ?My prince, that is not entirely the case. The creatures described were not vampire. The weapons that have been found are not of our creation. They are something else. The Roman wants his hound to take a look. He should be here shortly. His plane landed about two hours ago.? He had only one word to say about that. ?Shit.? The woman nodded her head in the motion of the front gate. He looked over the burnt trees, burnt from the very means that brought the assault team here to destroy the good balance that the Cleveland Den and Giovanni clan had made. There a limousine had pulled up. One of the men playing security guard had walked up and was talking to the driver. A short minute later, the car was pulling in behind Luciano's own. No sooner then it stopped, did a woman step out from the driver's seat. She was dressed in a traditional chauffeur's hat and jacket but under that was nothing more then silk and lace. The color matched, but the outfit made it look like some pervert was riding in the car. And for a pious, religious man, he was. She opened the door and a man of about six feet and two inches climbed out, using his eagle headed cane for support. He walked over with a very obvious limp in his right leg. He was young looking, just breaking his thirtieth year, but he walked like an old man. ?Luciano Giovanni?? ?Yes...? Luciano played the diplomat well, extending his hand to the man. ?Yes, you can call me Sinclair. I'm Mr. Gaius' adjuster. I understand there is something here I must look at.? This guy was all business, he could tell. ?Yes. I understand that you're here to see if it was more then Vampire that did this?? ?I can already tell you that it was.? The man hobbled forward, using the cane to walk. He came up and looked at one of the bodies that was laying there. He extended his handout and a glow came out from it, enhanced by the fact that it was nighttime, of course with the vampires working there it would be. In front of Luciano's eyes, an image formed in the air above them. The man known as Sinclair looked up at it. In front of them, the man who was now laying on the ground, his entrails strewn about, was looking as he had when he was living. The image of the man pulled forth a gun from his shoulder holster and started shooting. It apparently had no effect on his attacker as then a man with a horned helmet came up with a wicked looking axe and embedded it into his abdomen. The horned man then kicked the now dead vampire off from his axe. As he did, you could almost see the very essence of the vampire be pulled forth from his falling body as the axe seemed to consume him. ?Herne...? Sinclair only said the one word, as it if was a name. ?Herne?? He stood up to the fullest he could to look like he was about the lecture the young vampire, even though this vampire was a good ten times as old as he was. ?Herne, the Celtic horned god to parts of Ireland. One of the Faery of the Unseelie Courts. The leader of one of the Wild Hunts.? ?The wild hunt?? ?Immortal creatures, similar to elves who hunt down evil and destroy it. They serve only in the old country though. Why they were here I don't know. This is interesting, as they are only aligned with one faction. Can I see the room where LaBatte was taken, and the other two were found?? Luciano motioned for his assistant to help the man. ?You'll excuse me, but I must coordinate my men out here to make sure the locals don't start making calls to the authorities and that.? Sinclair nodded. ?Of course.? Sinclair turned and motioned for his chauffeur to follow him. The scantily clad woman smiled with her eyes and her mouth as he did. Luciano's assistant walked Sinclair, slowly through the estate. They stopped in the study though, as his chauffeur suddenly motioned to the three vampires they found dead there. Sinclair looked at the on who still had a loose grip on a large pipe, and frowned. ?I would almost say that was Garou, what about you?? He looked at his chauffeur. Her eyes shifted, going to an amber color with a vertical pupil. ?Yes, Garou, but I can't tell what type. The smell of death has covered it.? Sinclair nodded and then motioned to continue on. They walked down the long hallway that lead to the auditorium where the body of the two former leaders of the Clan. Sinclair looked at the bodies and frowned deeply. He reached down and pulled the split bladed knife out of the vampire's chest. ?Yes, that is what I was expecting. The Hunt and the Order. This was a blatant act here.? ?The Order?? Sinclair looked at the young assistant and grinned. ?The Order of the Hammer, lead by the nephew of the being who created your kind.? ?Cain's nephew?? She looked surprised. Sinclair could tell that this vampire was one of them that didn't believe in the legend of Cain being the first vampire. ?Yes, Ishram. But that's getting a bit deep into it right now. In the end, the Order is powerful. Let me see here.? He held out his hand with the knife in it. The hand glowed again and the knife went straight up onto it's tip and hovered about an inch from his palm. A vision then appeared right above it, that of the man who pulled the knife from it's sheath and then tossed into the place he'd just pulled it from. Sinclair spoke calmly. ?Blaise... Blaise... Blaise... Well, that can only mean one thing if the Captain of the Brigade took part in this. Ian, my brother, you openly left your calling card here. It's war you want, it could very well be war you get.? He stopped, and the knife fell back down into his hand. He handed it to the woman in the chauffeur's costume. ~Fade Here~ ~To Be Continued~
