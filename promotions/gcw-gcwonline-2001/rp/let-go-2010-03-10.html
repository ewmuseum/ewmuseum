Let Go

10 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/10/10 NC-17 “Shiiiii…..” Clenching her pearly whites together tightly, Tessa Windsor nearly pierced a hole through the tip of her tongue as she stopped herself short of cursing right in front of her kids. Although all but the eldest were sprawled out across the various seats inside the luxury box Chris Storm had rented out for NC-17, it only took one of them to spread the news of Tessa’s cursing to cause a family wide epidemic of ‘shit’, ‘fuck’, and every other word that came out of their fathers chops anytime anything failed to go his way. Tonight, however, the only things that came from Chris Storm’s mouth were praise for the woman before him and plenty of love filled harassment. Hovering above her as she lay sprawled out stomach down across a line of barstools, a wide eyed grin crept across the face of Chris Storm as he pitifully ran his hand across her back, which had taken quite a beaten thirty minutes earlier. “Bet you wish you would have listened to me now.” Even the pain radiating throughout every inch of her back would not stop Tessa from defending her decision to square off against Markus Stone, even if her choice felt terribly reckless this moment in time, “Right. And then Markus would have let me go without so much as an incident?” “Of course. But only if you used those puppy dog eyes you’re so damn good at.” Rolling those so called puppy dogs round full circle, Tessa let loose with her own sentiments, “Someone’s been away from the business too long.” Lightly pushing down on the small of her back ever so gently with the palm of his hand to make his point, Chris lamented, “And right now someone’s wishing she would have stayed with me out of the business.” Unintentionally forming a snarl out of pain, Tessa let loose with a tear-filled whine, “I hope you know you’re a big jerk!” From the corner of his light blue eyes, Chris looked over towards their two sons who had crashed two hours earlier on the sectional sofas in the middle of the suite. Naturally Chris took the chance to shoot back ever so suavely, “and you’re a potty mouth. Guess that makes us even.” “Jerk.” “Potty mouth.” “Shut up.” Storm’s eyebrows twitched upwards in response to his long time girlfriends demand. “And let you writhe in your pain without any sort of amusement?” “Ideally.” That would never happen. Not in a million years. Besides, it is not like she actually meant a word of it. She had spent far too long pursuing the man beside her to want to give him up that easily. Running his fingertips between her battered and bruised shoulder blades, Chris did his part to ensure his presence would be the one thing that would remain intact for Tessa, who had lost far too much far too quickly over the past six months. Losing her job was one thing, losing her friendship Vivica was another, but losing the kinship she’d shared with Markus Stone damn near broke Tessa’s spirit. The worst pain of the pain had occurred far earlier in the evening. Nothing stung more than the seconds that seemed like hours when Markus approached the ring, looked Tessa in the eye, and Tessa glared back. Merely glancing over at Markus’ expressionless face almost brought Tessa to her knees. Despite the beating she had taken earlier in the evening, none of blows the Petite in Pink had taken could match up to the fact that tore her mind apart and left it for dead. The fact that Markus used to be different from all the rest. The fact that a void that used to be a mutual trust and genuine concern for a woman he looked at as a surrogate sibling was now replaced by animosity, jealously, and absolute hatred. Somewhere along the lines something went wrong, something that Tessa could not pinpoint to these days. Groaning with a giant scowl as she attempted to push herself back up into sitting position, Tessa’s feeling about the whole situation were quite obvious. If beating Markus fair and square in the middle of the ring would not win back her close friend, nothing would. Just one glance into Tessa’s glazed over eyes left Chris wondering aloud, “So let me ask you, was it worth walking out a winner?” “If he wakes up, yes.” “And when will you wake up yourself?” The blaring of “Purity” across the P.A. system provided Tessa with the perfect distraction. In an instant the Pint Sized Diva sat upright and directed her attention to the other side of the glass where her other former friend Vivica J. Valentine was due to face off against Andy Murray for the World Title. Unbeknownst to Tessa, it led Storm to the perfect seg way for the other topic she had been dodging for months, if not years. “You know, I’m kind of pulling for Viv in this one.” Tessa eyes stretched wide as if he had just taken a piss all over her favorite handbag. Considering this time last year Chris had bashed Valentine’s head in with a chair, his sentiments were big news to her. She failed to connect any of the dots within his logic. “You think it’s alright to break someone’s heart over a freaking title?” “I certainly think it helped her.” They certainly were not the words one professional wrestler wanted to hear from another, regardless of the reasoning behind it. “Hmph.” Tessa tuned out her boyfriends fairly unsubtle suggestions with the tune of “Rise” by Cult, and of course the deafening roars of the crowd shortly thereafter. Scooting closer to Tessa, Chris looked down towards the ring just in time to catch the tension filled moment between Andy and Vivica right before the bell rang. “I think you could benefit some from taking on her mentality.” “Do you have a preference on what I beat you senseless with?” If her bitter sarcasm failed to get the point across, the scornful expression he got when she turned his way would do the job. His palms turned upwards in defense. “I’m just saying…” “…that I need to be just like Vivica, Markus, and every other selfish prick in this business to be worth a damn,” Tessa spat back with a heavy sigh, “I got it.” That said Tessa’s attention returned to what was going on in the ring, her chin dropping down into her palms. Clearly she was not up for one of Storm’s so called motivation speeches tonight. Not after having to nearly kill herself out in the ring to make a point to Markus Stone. And even that seemed to fail. Chris Storm was very well aware that stubborn might as well been Tessa’s middle name. With that knowledge he took a chance and butted right back in. “Just listen to me for a second Tessa. Please?” “I’m listening.” She quietly muttered even though clearly her thoughts and focus were directed towards what was going on in the ring. Despite not having Tessa’s full attention, Chris trudged forth with his homily carefully, his ice blue eyes failing to stay focused on any one thing for too long as he worked to muster the right words. “If you want to be out there with the likes of Andy and Vivica, you need to step it up.” Just the edge of Tessa’s glare indicated if he continued any further he would likely walk out wounded. It was enough to get the self proclaimed Rock Superstar to back up a dozen mental steps or so before trying again. “You should be out there with them right now.” This time Tessa’s interest was peaked ever so slightly. Her head angled in a way which she could catch the match and Storm’s attempted proposition at the same time. “As a matter of fact I know for a fact that you would have much rather been out there with them right now then wasting your time with Markus this evening.” “What was I going to do? Cut him out of my life like you?” “Let him go Tessa.” “Would you STOP saying that?” Her French manicured tips buried themselves deep within her brunette locks as she exhaled in frustration. It agitated Tessa to no end that Chris failed to realize that things were not as simple as a mere ‘I hate you, goodbye’ type parting, at least in Tessa’s world. “Gladly. Once you stop worrying about him and make the right move.” Exasperated by this weekly battle with her other half, Tessa groaned, “I’m NOT worried about him.” “Then why won’t you finally make the move everyone’s been anticipating for god knows how many months, if not years now?” The subject lingered in the air for a moment before Tessa extended enough courtesy to at least give it a minute’s thought, quite possibly the longest she had ever given it. “Not worth it.” Chris hardly seemed bemused, “That’s it?” “Yep. That’s it.” Again Tessa’s focus drifted back to the ring. She was far more interested in what was taking place center stage than any kind of scenario her boyfriend could draft up when it came to her and the World Title. “So I had to sit up here and watch you damn near get killed this evening for the sake of something not worth a minute of your time?” As agitated as Chris was at the moment, Tessa shrugged it off and chalked it up to Chris merely missing his own chance to shine again, “Your choice.” This time it was Storm burying his fingers into his blond tinted hair. He found absolutely no amusement in worrying about Tessa’s well-being for the sake of well....nothing… “Sometimes, I don’t understand you at all Tess.” From her seat a few feet away Tessa chirped, “It’s not as complicated as you make it.” Passive aggressive as can be, Chris finally exploded with a genuine concern unlike any other he had ever thrown Tessa’s way before. “Then why are you still doing this Tess? Why are you still going out there and putting your damn livelihood at stake every freaking week? You’ve got kids! You’ve got me. And then you’re going tell me you’re passing us up for nothing? It’d be one thing if you stuck around to finally stick it to the critics and claim that belt that’s eluded you for years now. But this? This sticking around to salvage friendships crap? It’s absolutely senseless! Are you really that deluded to think I enjoy watching you get torn to shreds out there even if you do manage to win somehow?” Uncomfortable silence was an understatement for what transpired the following five minutes. In anything, the next five minutes were about as emotionally charged as they could get, without so much as peep from either of them, or even just the slightest of moves. Even a knock on the door failed to puncture a pinhole in the tension between the two. With a mute huff, Tessa crawled out of her seat and headed for the door, “I’ll get it.” And boy was Tessa ever glad she did. Never in their short friendship had Tessa ever been so glad to see Tempest. A momentary smile crept across her face as Tempest extended her arms in a congratulatory hug, “I’m so happy for you girl! You did awesome out there!” Tessa offered the same back to Tempest, who’d managed to overcome her own feat in the form of the overly clingy Cheap Labor and win the Television title, “You too! We should go celebrate.” Tempest bounced up and down in excitement, completely oblivious to any of the tension floating within the room, “Of course! You want to go now?” Tessa turned her attention back to Chris, who redirected his attention towards the ring. She then turned back to Tempest with a forced half smile. “Give me a moment to get ready and we’ll go out to celebrate properly.” “Awesome!” Tempest proclaimed with a high five to her slightly taller counterpart, “Meet me back at my locker room!” Tessa nodded in acknowledgment, “Can’t wait!” As Tempest scurried back to her room to get ready herself, Tessa’s slowly shut the door behind her. She seemed almost ashamed to look back up at Chris, who still came across as entirely disappointed in her lack of interest in the biggest prize of all. “I’m sorry.” Tessa whispered aloud as she locked eyes with Chris for the first time since their little blowout, “We’ll talk about it later, I promise.” Chris feigned neutrality with a wave, “It’s fine. Like you said, we’ll take later.” Obviously everything was not fine, but there was little Tessa could actually be able to do now that would fix it. That knowledge intact, Tessa grabbed her little black dress and makeup bag and made a beeline for the bathroom. But not before Chris probed her with one last favor, “Forget what I said for now, alright?” “Alright.” Then she disappeared behind the bathroom door. Chris plopped back down into the luxurious leather seat behind him with a heavy sigh. Clearly this was not over. Clearly his girlfriend was hesitant to claim the one thing he knew she deserved. And that troubled him to no end. “What am I going to do now?” It was the question of the evening for both Storm and Windsor.
