A vacation or melencholy?

13 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/13/10 Quietly each white flake falls upon the vast forested whiteness. Around you you see wilderness of Pines, Evergreens and the mix of Birch patches. In the distance echoing throughout the wooded snow covered area you hear chopping. Down the ridge a ways is a man in a large jacket chopping down a large pine. One heavy swing after another he cuts into the large tree. It seems to be fighting back at each chop he drives into it. Nature seemingly showing its own stubborn streak. Placing the double bladed ax down he grabs a mall and wedge. He pushes the wedge into the gap created by the ax and makes sure it is solid. He then reverses the maul to its flat side and begins driving the wedge into the tree. He smiles as he drives the wedge flush with the out bark of the tree. “This is a battle you will not win, Nobody will win the war against me ever.” He grabs the ax up and moves to the opposite side of the tree and begins chopping harder and in a faster succession of moves. Driving the ax further and further into the trunk each time. Another man walks up the hillside and is dragging a sled full of chopped and split wood. He pulls the sled up nearby and looks at the big tree. Turning he looks at the other man and speaks. “Look Shaman be it a tree or stone it doesn't respond to you talking to it, You are not Paul Bunyan so get with using the chain saw already.” Pointing to the sled with the Husqvarna chain saw in it. Shaman pulls his hood back a bit and lifts the mask he is wearing. “The tree deserves more respect than a chainsaw Jorge. Even though it would be easier I want to make the most out of this exercise and workout. Besides I have to let out some anger and frustration.” Groaning a bit he turns and begins to chop into the tree again. Samuelsson shakes his head as he turns and grabs the leather straps to the sled and hooks them over each shoulder, he turns and simply watches before he speaks again. “You better be thinking of Jimmy Thunder this week. You have faced him before and he has beaten you for the US title before if you have forgotten.” Turning his head Shaman is obviously annoyed. “I haven't forgotten who Jimmy Thunder is Jorge, but there is more at stake then this simple match. There is much more at stake.” Swinging hard he drives the ax into the tree once again. Trudging up the hill pulling the sled Samuelsson shakes his head as he mumbles. “Sometimes that guy needs to realize, there are somethings you just can't do anything about.” Trudging on through the snow he disappears into the thicker trees and snow. Shaman continued to chop at the tree as a sharp cracking sound is heard echoing throughout the area. Shaman steps back around the tree to the side and continues to swing the ax hard. Each splitting chop into the tree, another louder crack is heard as suddenly the tree gives way and falls. Stepping away into a safer area and distance Shaman watches as the felled tree hits the ground, causing snow to burst upwards into an explosion of tiny ice particles whiting out the whole surrounding. He slowly places the ax down he sits down upon the fresh stump. He stretches his arms slightly and then cracks his neck. Looking around he quietly thinks to himself. As he observes the area and listens to the silence around him. A peaceful silence he has missed since the last time he came here. Thinking to himself he ponders a few thoughts that which bothers him. Why is it everything I do comes back to me full circle. Jimmy Thunder is this example, the man leaves GCW and no word is heard from him ever again. Suddenly he returns, then he and I face off once again. This isn't just business between myself and the Aussie, it is always a test. This obviously on the heels of Diamond getting a win against me. Knowing he wasn't going to win the war he joined forces with some freaking religious fanatic. Slamming his fist down onto the hard stump he lets out a slow angered breath. Looking around as he stands up, reaching down he grabs the ax, as well as other items he was using and begins walking up the hill himself, as the snow falls harder. The flakes become a bit larger and the wind blows a bit stronger. Just fucking great this is just what I need, the weather to get worse on me. As if my days weren't bad enough dealing with all this bullshit. Guys like Tracer Dorn and Donny Diamond teaming up. Guys like Jimmy Thunder waiting in the background to just get a piece of someone to make there moves for glory. What dopes it take for me to get a damn break... He breathes heavy as he begins to grow angrier and angrier as he suddenly lashes out towards nothing and everything at the same time. “WHAT THE HELL DO I GOTTA DO, TO GET A FUCKING BREAK FOR ONCE!!” Following with a loud roar from deep within he lets out his frustrations looking skyward. Staring upwards he simply shakes his head and begins walking forward again as he lowers his gaze to the snow before him. “As expected no answer from anyone or anything.” Slowly he makes his way to the cabin, Looking over to where Samuelsson left the sled he notices the logs were tosses and stacked under the overhang. The sled pulled in neatly nearby and leaned against the support posts and the chainsaw places inside the shed. against the Cabin. He enters the shed and places the ax and other materials he carried back with him. Turning he shuts the shed door and tromps through the snow to the cabin door. He opens it and steps in removing his hood and ski mask. Turning he notices Samuelsson has the fire blazing and is cooking something on the stove. He turns and sees his own wife Suzy sitting there in a chair reading as his daughter is sleeping on the couch. From the side room out walks Valkyrie Samuelsson's longtime partner. She smirks as she looks over at Shaman. “I would almost swear you two planned to have us stuck out here in a snow storm.” watching her Shaman smiles a genuine smile for the first time in a longtime. Shaman walks over to Suzy and leans over kissing her gently as he stands up and walks into the other room. This Den area overlooks the lake below, the ridge drops lazily downwards in a slow decent. Not too steep, where in the summer time there is a beach and boat dock. He stands there envisioning this as he can only see maybe twenty yards from this window. Behind him Suzy walks in wrapping her arms around his waist as she stands beside him. “What is bothering you?” She asks quietly as she seems to already know the answer. Looking down at her he smiles at one of the few joys in his life was marrying her finally and sharing the beautiful little girl that is asleep on the couch. He stares emotionless out at the world beyond. “Why am I still not just letting it all go and let the rage work for me Suzy?” Suzy leans back a bit looking up at her much taller husband. She reaches upwards turning his head towards her. Her eyes meet his for the first time it seems in a very longtime. “You were waiting for the right time my love, The right time is now, do not worry about me or Victoria, just remember too that you have an older son out there too, but right now it is time for us to let go and allow you to run free right now.” She pulls him down and kisses his cheek, she feels the searing heat from his flesh upon her lips as she turns walking away, she smiles inwardly knowing that burning sensation is why she fell in love with this wild dangerous man. That fire seems to have reheated something deep inside him. She leaves him in the room as she closes the door returning to the couch to sit by her daughter as she gently stroke her childs hair. The young girl wakes and looks up into mothers eyes and smiles as she speaks. “Where is Daddy?” Suzy smiles as he daughter climbs up into her lap and snuggles with her. “He is preparing himself for something to be done his way for the first time in a longtime.” Taking the answer as an answer she simply yawns and lays her head down on her mothers shoulder and falls asleep once again. Later that evening..... Darkness fills the room as Shaman slowly awakens. Feeling a little chilled he stands up and walks to the fireplace and stokes the fire. Tossing a few logs into the fire he watches it begin to burn brighter once again. Looking out the window he notices that the snow is still falling. Just enough ambient light allows him to see this. He thinks it is often weird and spooky at the same time you can notice that when it should be darkest that there is still some form of light during a snow storm. Sitting down he watches the snow flash in the fire light reflecting off the window. The fire he turns his attention too. His eyes watch the flames as if he is searching for an answer within them. He places a hand on his chin as he leans against it. His eyes simply watch and stare at the flames as he thinks to himself. I wonder how they would react to me just cutting loose? They never have seen the anger filled rage driven Shaman of old. The same guy who believed in everything he has done was for the greater good. Perhaps it is time to reawaken that beast people feared so long ago rather than the man they have come to respect and expect to put up a good fight or match. Tapping his finger on his cheek he watches and waits, his mood simple and foreboding. What is he planning or perhaps what are they planning, he wants to know. Then as if he knows he shifts and sits up straight allowing himself to fall backwards and stretch. Propping his legs up his eyes simply watch the fire as he allows himself to drift off to sleep. Quietly his daughter walks into the room dragging a blanket with her. She climbs up into her Fathers lap and spreads the blanket over them both. She snuggles into his chest as he opens his eyes slightly seeing her. He places an arm around his daughter smiling. “Sleep well pumpkin, I will always protect you by any means necessary.” By any means necessary these words reverberate around in his head as they both drift off to sleep, as the fire begins to blaze brighter within and without.
