Visions of Past and Future

29 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/29/2005 Yes. I know all about nightmares. I know the feeling of cold sweat rolling over my body and waking up screaming. I am human after all, and like anyone else, I have those horrible dreams as well. My worst is the dream of my parents. Silly to you perhaps, but to me it is the most terrible thing I have suffered. Hmm? Yes, even more terrible than my attack in August. I usually avoid ever speaking of the events that led to these hellish dreams, but since you pacify me by listening to my stories about the other world, I suppose I can give you this one thing. I ask in advance for forgiveness should I break down and start to cry. I was six when the first of two tragedies struck my life. My life had been perfect until that point. I had two loving parents whom I revered and a twin brother who could do no wrong. Little did I know my parents had been keeping a dark secret from Erik and I. You see, when I was four my mother had been diagnosed with cancer of the lungs. For two years my parents would go on 'business' trips for weeks on end never taking my brother and I with them. They never told us because the treatments had apparently worked but a few weeks before my birthday my mother had a relapse. The party planned was a huge event. My parents had hired a carnival, a circus, and some terrife caterers. Erik and I had invited over a hundred people to attend the gala and were extremely excited about the upcoming party. The fact that our parents suddenly had to leave for an emergency business trip did not even scratch the surface of that childhood enthuasism. They were only gone two weeks. Back in time for the final preperations for the party. Looking back, I can not help but feel guilty for not seeing my mother's pain until it was too late. That last week she had seemed so tired all the time. Blackcircles had appeared almost permnantly beneath her eyes and when I did take notice she passed it off as merely being tired from trying to make that birthday the best one ever. As fate would have it...it would be the worst. On the morning of the event I woke up bright and early. I can still remember barging into Erik's room and bouncing on his bed exuberantly in order to rouse him. He was not to keen on the whole thing but I didn't care. I just wanted to share the whole day with him. It was our day. The morning flew by fast, and by the time one pm hit we were dressed and the guests were arriving. Our family grounds had been turned into our own personal amusement park. I remember the clothes my mother was wearing as clearly as if it were yesterday. She had on a chasmere sweater in ivory and a pair of suede tan slacks. Her hair was pulled into a loose french twist. I even remember the perfume she wore...Chanel No.5. She was beautiful despite the still evident bags beneath those eyes she endowed Erik and I with. That day she looked more tired than usual to me and , still, she kept up appearances always smiling as she mingled with the guests. I was so proud of her and I knew without a doubt, she was proud of us as well. We probably rode every ride in that place three times. I personally watched the circus for two hours. I especially enjoyed the lion tamers and the trapeze artists. We played so many games that day. I still have the four foot teddy bear my father won for me. It is one of my most valued possessions. Yes, I know it is out of character considering everything else. But it was one of the last thing I recieved from him. At midnight we gathered together in the ballroom to cut the cake and open presents. It was a tradition my parents had started from our first birthday as Erik was born before midnight and I was born after. The gifts were fantastic from a child's point of view. We got anything and everything we asked for. Erik got his pony and I got my cockatoo. I named him TuTu. It was as I was opening my final gift that things suddenly went horribly wrong. I looked up at my mother with a smile only to have it fade as I noticed how ghostly white she had suddenly become. I remember father rushing to catch her as she collapsed. My own scream still echoes in my ears when I relive the nightmare. My mother died two hours later. It was only then that Father told Erik and I what happened. I think I cried for three days. I cried at her funeral. I cried for three days after as well. My father looked like a man whose spirit was all but broken after that. It took us months to recover and for all of us to feel any sort of happiness. Father's business trips became less and less frequent. He decided that spending time at home with his children was more important than galavanting around the globe. On the rare occasion that he did have to actually leave he would leave our butler in charge of us. We didn't mind. He was a good man. When I was nine the fates decided that it was time for another cruel blow to our lives. Father recieved a phonecall about some business he had to be in attendance for and took off for Tokyo, Japan. The problem is...he never got there. His plane was caught in a sudden storm over the seas and the turbulents managed to pull the jet from the sky. No one survived. The odd thing was, I didnt cry this time. I think it is because I had cried so much when our mother died. I simply had no more tears left. I sat at his memorial numbly and staring blankly at the tombstone set up next to my mother's. I remember thinking that there was no body in that grave. It was all a sham. A cruel joke on my brother and I. I couldnt have been more wrong. To this day the memories of those days still haunt me. At times I am able to go months without ever dreaming about them. I am able to avoid the dreams where my mother's face literally melts away into a skeleton then fades into dust. I am not forced to see my father's hand reaching out for me from beneath bloody waters begging me to save him and knowing I can not reach him no matter hard I try. At other times I barely sleep. I relive those nightmares almost nightly. I am afraid to sleep. I am afraid to see the look of disappointment on my parents face when they see what I have become. So you see, I know about nightmares. I have them. But I also have my dreams..the good ones. I have a dream of wearing gold around my waist. I have a dream of beating a man in the ring fair and square and proving that I am a woman to be reckoned with. I have a dream that Erik will return to my side and walk with me through my glory days. I dream of one day falling in love with someone who will value me for who I am and cherish that rather than throw me away like a used toy. Simply put...I have a dream....and I intend to follow it.
