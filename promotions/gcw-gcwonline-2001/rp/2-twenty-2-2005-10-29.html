2 twenty 2

29 October 2005

Date: 10/29/05 The cat appeared in the hallway about three months ago. There are alot of stray cats in my neighborhood, a few of them find refuge prowling my hallway. Dixie, the scarred princess, an orange ball of fur who lost an eye somewhere, sometime. Percival, the king of his domain, a mixed tabby. And Claire. The white minx who seems too friendly and too pretty to be a stray. We named them and feed them, the hookers and I, but this cat was different than all of them. I had Savige that night. I had him beat and all I had to do was finish it. And then he showed up. I don't know who, I don't why, and I never seem to know where. He's a monster who strikes without reason or remorse. It started with the car in the parkinglot, I had Savige beat, I had that strap on my shoulder. I just didn't want to get it by scrambling his brain with a muffler. I am the better fighter and I'll win like a fighter. Too bad I haven't been doing alot of winning since then. He was black, thick and well fed, his fur glimmered with a healthy shine as he prowled like a panther. His eyes glimmered with intelligence when he looked at me, aloof and independant. The first time I found him injured, I thought it was a fight with Percival, or a scuffle with a neighbors dog. There were a few small pooches in the building. I was nursing bruised ribs for the week, so I took him in. He stayed close to the door, and never showed interest in exploring my apartment. I gave him his space, he gave me mine, together we suffered in silence. And then when I sought to address the audience, the eyes that have seen it all, he silenced me. It was genius, on his part, utter stupidity on mine. He left a tape player making noises in the broom closet, when I checked it out he jumped me from behind. For months I've toiled and worked, turned my feet to redemption and self discovery. The fans love the image, and Caldera was finally taking notice and was willing to give me air time. And he took it away from me. The first day he was in my apartment, one of the crack heads next door overdosed on heroin, my favorite hooker got beaten, and my fridge stopped running. By the third day I had two fines, a dead neighbor, a hospitalized hooker, and sour milk. The black cat looked healthier, and when he pawed at the door I obliged. The next day, my fridge started running again, and I got new neighbors. A young hispanic family, clean, just looking to get by. I gave them my fridge. By the second World Wide after FallOut I thought I was getting wise, I only showed up to try and lure him into attacking me. I got my wish, but not like I'd planned. I watched and I waited for him, and in the end he was watching and waiting for me the entire time. I played into his hands, I gave him all the information he needed to know me. And I was still no closer to the answer of who as I was two weeks before. I've been getting played in every angle. When I returned, this time nursing a dislocated shoulder, the black cat was curled up outside my door. He was so still I was afraid to touch him, he looked dead. Picking him up stained my hands red with blood, but I cradled him to my chest, I cleaned his wounds as best I could. Worse than the time before. He'd taken to a certain towel and so I put it by the door and for the next day and a half he refused to move. Sometimes watching me with those too intelligent eyes. My favorite hooker's condition worsened, and my new neighbors complained that their fridge had died. He uses every advantage I present him, and he uses them well. I prepared and I plotted, I let the facade slip long enough to gather the only ones I believe I can trust and set up a plan. I was so determined and so convinced I had outmaneuvered him. I've been waiting a long time to settle the score with Borealis and that United States title would have looked real good on my waist. But he took it a step over the line. He cost me my match, I can get another of those, but he took Eva. It took him nearly a week to recover, but on wednesday night he started pawing at my door. By this time I was convinced having him inside was the cause of my bad luck, as I'd been arrested for defending myself twice and nearly prosecuted. The neighbors fridge was still broken. He didn't look like he did the day I first saw him prowling the hallway. He was skinnier, some wounds refused to heal, but he maintained that proud; confident stride. I let him out, and before I left on thursday the neighbors fridge had started again. I was ready to kill the first person to joke about being the freak behind all of this. Still am, but in a different, more anticipating way. The only reason she was in that mess was because he wanted to get at me, and I don't even know who it is. It's maddening, to know that somebody knows you, knows how to get to you, and you don't have the first glimmer of an idea of who it is. I can ponder, but it's futile. The last assault was the worst, taser to the ribs, stopped my heart. I watched him last night, through the peep hole in my door. Hours passed and the riff raff moved into their homes. At half past one in the morning, the black cat sat straight backed in the center of the hallway, only the tip of his tail twitching randomly. My eyes felt heavy after a half an hour, and I think I dozed off twenty minutes after that. Checking the peep hole I saw movement and my heart nearly jumped, but it was just Claire, leaping from the shadows of a door way and bounding off down the stairs. And still I waited and I watched, I remember checking my watch the moment before it happened. It was two twenty two in the morning when the lights dimmed. The first thing I thought funny, they'd never dimmed before, I was about to pass it off as an electrical thing when I saw her. She glimmered like the moon on a half cloudy night, and she wavered, rippling as if the air between us was heated and churning. She came up the stairs, a dress like nothing I'd ever seen poured across her slender shoulders and over perfect breasts, opening to reveal a smooth stomach before wrapping around those hips. I squinted my eyes to make out the features of her face when she looked at me, and I felt instantly souless. The black cat suddenly lept into view and I blinked, as if waking up for the second time. But it was no dream, though I wish it had been perhaps. I stood frozen, my eyes pressed to the tiny peep hole watching as the cat I'd nursed to health was flung to the ground. On his feet with the agility of his species he lept back to the offensive, claws struck her face and the image of beauty shattered. The woman, her face ugly and hollow, eyes filled with an inky black evil, screamed a scream of silent hatred and struck the cut with her fingers turned claws. The black cat was slower to rebound, hurt and weak, he avoided a kick before leaping again, biting into the woman's leg. I flung the door open and the black cat touched down on the carpeted floor quietly, lowered himself to his belly, ears pressed flat against his head and moaned deep in his throat. I collected him in my arms, he didn't resist me though he was tense with pain and adrenaline. I fell back against the wall, hugging him to me nearly too tight and I wept for what I had seen, what she'd made me felt, and the uneasy desire to see her beautiful again. Two minutes and twenty two seconds. They say it's amazing I'm alive. It's a miracle I don't have brain damage. My heart stopped for two minutes and twenty two seconds and here I am. Still wearing the mask of lies, still eager to meet my assailant tomorrow. To make him feel everything about me he's been studying to beat. He almost killed me, I'm in no shape to go into a match tomorrow. But if I don't, then Eva will suffer, Ripe Fist or Ricky might be next. I'll let the fans down, I'll let the company down, I'll let The Marauder down. I have to know who it is, I have to protect my friends, I have to fight him... Whoever it is. She hasn't come yet tonight, it's almost three and I don't think she will. I don't what I did to deserve the black cat, I wonder if she'll come again tonight. I wonder exactly why she chose me, I wonder why he chose me, and selfishly, I wonder how much he has left to give...
