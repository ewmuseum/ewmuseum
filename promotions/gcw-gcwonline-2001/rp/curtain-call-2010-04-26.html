Date: 04/26/10 I. After WorldWide 128 March 28th 2010 10:13 P.M. He felt cold and alone; hurt and afraid. He lay, sprawled out, on the floor unable to move, hardly able to breathe. The pain was immense, traveling everywhere from his head, to his arms and all the way down to his toes. The blood covered about the same area as the pain, most of it pouring from the grotesque wound on his head. His eyes would only open halfway and the vision he did have was blurry and distorted. He tried to call out for help but nothing but a groan escaped his throat. He focused on moving his body, turning over if not standing up on his own but this would also prove to be a losing battle. He had been through tough battles, personal and bloody, many times over in his life but something about this one felt different. Something about this one made his blood boil and the hairs on the back of his neck stand up. He has never had to grow accustomed to being the victim; he’s never known true defeat before. He felt it coming for him, though. It was like cold hands slowly wrapping around his neck and squeezing the life from his body. It was suffocating, it was unimaginable … it was annoying. All around, he could make out only shadows and hear nothing but a ringing in his ears. The shadows could’ve been people, they could’ve been ring ropes, hell, they could’ve been God and an army of angels … although the last was very unlikely. The only way he knew to get out of this situation was by his own accord. It is the only person he has ever known that he could trust. Yet, no matter how hard he tried and how much energy he exerted, his battered and bruised body remained still, his vision still blurry and his blood still flowing. He felt lost and unsure of what to do next … Then those angels did appear. From the shadows, the figures formed into people. The people quickly began to probe and prod. There were there to help and suddenly he felt at ease. Until one voice cut through the darkness like a hot knife through butter. The voice made him nervous, made him cautious. “Help him.” The voice cleared his throat. “He is still needed.” And a chill runs down his spine. II. Calming my nerves hasn’t been easy, even with a stocked mini bar inside of the limo. I just can’t shake it. Caldera hasn’t asked me to make public appearances very often, something about keeping the mystery intact. I never had a problem with that either. I never learned exactly how to act at one of those conventions or autograph signings … do I act like I do when I’m outside of the ring? Do I be a prick like I’ve always done inside? Some combination of the two? There are just too many questions that I don’t like to answer for my tastes. Yet, here I am, in a limo heading to my proverbial execution. I know it is bound to happen too. These people, the fans, they aren’t stupid regardless of the amount of times that I’ve called them that over the years. They will easily see right through my façade. Sure, they’ve cheered when they have seen my image on a screen or in the ring lately but I have that figured out as well. They aren’t cheering ME; they are cheering what I’ve meant to GCW. And cheering anyone who stands opposite of that spoiled bitch, Vivica. “Fucking bitch.” The words fly from my lips before I even realize they are coming out. Not that I wouldn’t say them or that I don’t mean it, because I do. I can’t stand her, never have and I never will. Now that she’s done her best to make a name for herself at my expense, I’ll be sure to give her exactly what she wants. I intend to have a magnificent time in bloodying her up and breaking her down. That’s how I get down. It’s what I do. Which makes going out and pretending all the more difficult. I fear that they’ll expect me to be all candy canes and rainbows; they’ll want me to be Andy Murray or Clyde Fox … and fuck that. I’m no different now than I was when I went to burn Dread’s flesh from his bones. If this were Murray or even Tessa standing across from me at Shockwave? I’d unleash hell just as fiercely as I will against Valentine. Well … I can’t hide the smile from spreading across my face. “Maybe not.” Regardless though, I’m just not fit for this sort of duty. I’m not a hero, especially not the fans of GCW’s hero. I can’t be, it’s not in my DNA. “Ah, fuck it.” I reach over and pour myself another shot of tequila and quickly take it down, feeling the warm liquid flow down my throat and warm the rest of my body. I take a deep breath as I put the glass back down and then sit back in my seat, closing my eyes to attempt to calm myself again. What’s wrong with me anyways? Suddenly I give a damn about how the fans are going to react to me when I get out there? Now I sound like Vivica, that fucking bitch. “Fucking bitch.” There it goes again. I can’t help it. She does that to me. Always has and always will. “Concentrate!” Vivica will have my full attention in about two hours. I will finish plotting all the ways I will torture her inside of that ring in good time. For now, I need to focus on not getting killed in a mob of angry GCW fans who out me as a fraud … a fake hero. “I can’t fucking do this.” Maybe they ARE stupid. How else can someone explain why they cheer my name now? “I need another drink.” I pop open the tequila once more, knowing it isn’t a good idea but also not caring. I was almost there. One more for the road as they say. “Bottoms up.” III. Wylie Bick has never been anyone extraordinary, although her model-like good looks could have changed that once upon a time. Today, she’s a 28 year old woman who has only recently started figuring out exactly how to put her life together. Three years ago she met a young man that made her heart melt and that actually shared her feelings. Two years ago, he convinced Wylie to go to college. One year ago she started working as a waitress at a sports bar in California to help pay for books. School had been going well; Wylie just recently finished all of the prerequisite classes needed before beginning the two-year Registered Nursing program. Her grades were good, teachers were kind and she had her very supporting beau by her side. He was the only person around to help her along these days. Some people might find that depressing but for many years, Wylie had no one. “Is my order up, Jesse?” “Hold your horses, doll face.” “You know I hate that name.” “I know,” Jesse smirks as he slides the plates of food in front of Wylie. “Enjoy, doll face.” “You’re such a gentleman.” Wylie returns Jesse’s smirk as she takes the orders of Buffalo wings and mozzarella sticks into her possession and heads to take them to their table. The job isn’t a great one, not by a long shot, but it is easy money. Being the looker than Wylie is, she routinely gets big tips from the drunken males that came to the establishment most often and she doesn’t have to wear tiny shorts and a skin tight shirt with an owl on it to get those tips. A plus in her book! “Hey guys,” Her smile broadens as she places the food down in front of the three young men she was serving. “Can I get you anything else?” “You get X: TV?” Asks a dark, shaggy haired kid with glasses. “Um … I don’t know. Let me go check, kay?” The typical request in her place was football but seeing as how the NFL season was not in session it had moved to the NBA Playoffs. Wylie knows that the NBA isn’t played on X: TV, actually, she has no idea what X: TV is. She grabs a remote control and heads to the closest TV to where her young patrons are seated. Wylie begins going through the guide, page by page floating through it to find the channel and after a few presses of a button she finds it. The words on the screen make her freeze in place, a whirlwind of emotions ravaging her insides. Global Championship Wrestling Presents: The Countdown to Shockwave The Night The World Says Goodbye To LeStatt Knight LeStatt … … Knight Wylie immediately feels light someone punched her in the stomach. That name, that face, that man … it was part of a life that she had hoped she had left behind a long time ago. She presses the button to go to the channel and there he is, LeStatt Knight, standing in the center of a GCW ring. She has not seen the man’s face in almost seven years, not since she saw him shaking hands with Alex Grey, whom she was in a relationship with at the time. At least back then she thought it was a relationship. Now she realizes that Alex was just using her, fucking her, to piss LeStatt Knight off. Actually … to piss Roberto Abitua off. Nothing was business with those two, it was always personal. Wylie was only 21 years old when she first met Roberto. She had met him at a nightclub in Miami while celebrating her 21st birthday with a bunch of friends. Like many other young, beautiful women that have had a few drinks, she went out that night looking for a good time. Roberto was more than willing to supply her that and even sober, Wylie would freely admit that she would’ve fucked Roberto silly. What this one night of passion ended up doing was alter Wylie’s life forever. She would go on to be a pawn in Roberto’s most hated rival, Alex “Payne” Grey’s plans to destroy Roberto’s life. Alex drove a wedge through Wylie and Roberto, kept chipping away until Wylie felt Roberto did not love her and until she went crying into Alex’s arms for comfort. Alex ended up being wrong. Roberto loved Wylie dearly … but the monster that came after Wylie’s betrayal was not Roberto. He was darker, colder and much more vicious. Wylie suddenly had tunnel vision and although she is inside of a very busy sports bar, she could hear and see nothing but the television screen. She feels seven years of repressed emotions beginning to bubble up to the surface and suddenly she feels nauseous … but she continues to watch. She listens as a man named James Bryan goes on and on about all of the accomplishments that LeStatt Knight has amassed over the last five years. She watches as they show clip after clip of a man that Wylie once loved, performs unspeakable acts of violence to other human beings. Sometimes he even performs those violent acts on himself. The more she hears and the more she sees brings more and more memories to the forefront. In moment’s time, she begins to fade away from the life that she lives in. No longer is she the 28 year old waitress in a nursing program with a loving boyfriend and a side job as a waitress. Instead, Wylie is that 21 year old girl with a world full of possibilities and a thing for the dark and mysterious man in her life. From there she relives the good and the bad … Ending with Roberto cracking her skull with a steel chair because he thought she was pregnant with Alex’s baby. Wylie shakes her head, breaking the trance-like state. Her life comes back to her. She fights back the urge to cry, not wanting to make a scene on the job. She glares back up at the screen where James Bryan is sitting. “There is no denying one thing. If you are sitting out there today watching this, it means that you were affected by this man. It means that you were either in awe of his greatness, hated him for the despicable things he has done or maybe even cheered him on because of all the times he has helped carry this great organization on his back. Whatever LeStatt Knight meant to you, I believe it is safe to assume that he will be missed. Our world will never be the same. I’m James Bryan, thank you for watching.” LeStatt Knight’s face, sadistic grin and bright blue eyes and all staring back out at the world … back out at Wylie. Then he winks before the GCW logo flashes across the screen. Wylie knows that James Bryan was right, but not in exact the way he had meant. Roberto shaped Wylie into the woman she is today, for better and for worse. She learned about lust, hate and betrayal because of him. She learned about anger as well, for a long time she hated, not only herself but Roberto as well. She has always wondered what her life would’ve been like without ever meeting him. Could she have done everything she ever wanted to? But … He was also her first true love and that is a powerful thing. So, would she take it back? Would she wipe Roberto from her life if she was given a chance to do it over? Do you throw away that loving feeling? Of course she would. Fuck Roberto and LeStatt Knight. He smashed her head with a steel chair for fuck’s sake! “I hope he fucking rots in hell.” Now she smiles. Now she can move on. Now, she can live her life. LeStatt Knight Free. It’s small, bright and colorful lights flashed in the background; it’s an ambulance. He did notice the large, shadowy figure hop into the back of the ambulance with him right before the doors were slammed shut. It sat next to him and stared down at him. He knew who it was. On one hand, he was still alive and that was something that should have been celebrated … especially after taking a round from a gun straight to the head. On the other hand, it had become painfully obvious that he could not win this war that he had been waging for many years now. If he was to live, it’d have to be as Roberto’s puppet and a large part of him would have rather died than to give Roberto the satisfaction of having that kind of power at his disposal. “I’m sure you’re wondering why I’ve saved you.” Not really, LeStatt knew why he would be saved. She’s a psycho bitch and to beat a psycho bitch, you have to be a little bit crazy. That’s where you come in, LeStatt. You just have to hold on for another couple days.” LeStatt would’ve laughed and berated Roberto to no end if he could have managed the strength. The stare told Roberto the story he needed to know. It made him none too happy. “You’re going to refuse this?” The anger in Roberto’s voice quickly escalated. “Haven’t you learned anything in this whole ordeal? You cannot refuse anything to me! Although not quite as vulgar a message as he’d like to. Whatever came next could not be any worse than being a prisoner in Roberto’s head for all these years. He hoped to find out, soon. V. Steven Caldera is quite the busy man. Even without on-air GCW issues to deal with, Caldera Enterprises is always in motion. New deals being done, old ones being revised and some needing to be terminated. On this day, Mr. Caldera is working a part of the business world that nobody enjoys, paperwork. Being the CEO of such a large and prestigious corporation means that there are a lot of pages to be read and signed. Most of this type of work is boring or a chore, usually both at the same time. The bosses’ hand begins to cramp and his patience wears thin. It is days like these which really make him reconsider his position of staying out of GCW affairs and letting Johnny run things as he sees fit. Caldera was not built to simply be a man behind the scenes; he is a man of action, something he isn’t getting nearly enough of these days. After another stack of papers has his wet signature on them he places his pen down and leans back in his chair. The CEO sighs as he places his hands behind his head and stares across his desk. A certain folder catches his eye, plain manila folder with a white label taped on it. Caldera removes his hands from their place behind his head and places them on the desk before leaning over to get a look at the folder. The label reads: Roberto “LeStatt Knight” Abitua. Instantly his interest perks back up. He takes the folder in his hand and picks it up, opening it before it gets right in front of him. Steven places the folder down in front of him. The first paper inside does not bring the CEO any joy; it’s the retirement paperwork for the biggest money maker GCW has ever seen. Caldera eyes his pen again but he does not reach for it so that he can sign this document and make LeStatt’s retirement official. His first thought is of the years and money still left on LeStatt’s current GCW contract. Caldera has never been known for making things easy, especially when those things can make his products worse and/or take money out of his pockets. He considers not signing at all. He could show up at Shockwave with Knight’s contract and make him give GCW the remaining time. He’d be back in the spotlight, back in the action … back in GCW. But he can’t. He won’t. Not only because GCW is flourishing without him and that he knows he needs to give Terror room to grow without him hovering around. This is because, this is LeStatt Knight. LeStatt Knight has been the centerpiece of GCW’s greatness for a long, long time. Although the two have never had a very friendly relationship, he’s always been sure to show LeStatt Knight the proper respect. When many other icons of GCW’s past left, Knight remained. Caldera respects that more than anything. Steven Caldera is a fan of LeStatt Knight. And he isn’t ready to let that just walk away. Without saying a word, I raise my right arm into the air. Yeah, a simple gesture but it seems to get the proper reaction as the cheers and chants grow louder. LE-STATT! LE-STATT! LE-STATT! I cannot help it, no matter how hard I try to fight it, I grin. Sure, they’ve seen me smile many times before but typically those grins had a lot of sinister undertones. Yes, this one has a message to it as well, satisfaction. They’ve finally embraced what I am all about. I loved getting all of my accolades and in many cases, yes, I loved breaking many of my opponents down. There was a message to all of it though; I wanted nothing but the best for GCW. Andy Murray, Michael Stevens, Jordan White, Clyde Fox, Jay Terror … and the list goes on and on. I did not want to destroy these men’s lives. Quite the opposite in fact. I knew that this day would come eventually and when it did, I had to be sure that GCW was going to be in capable hands. I had to know that when my chapter in the book of GCW was told in its entirety that it wouldn’t be the end of the whole thing. “Thank you, GCW.” They couldn’t hear my words over them screaming but I wanted to say it anyways. I bring my arm down now and quickly move off stage. They don’t quiet down at all. The chants change however, into something else … Beat Viv! BEAT Viv! BEAT VIV! And it hits me; my story is not over, there’s still a chapter that needs finished. Yes, the fans have embraced me and it seems they understand what I stand for. There is still one more match to have, one more body to break. Fuck it, there’s REVENGE to be had. This is it, the curtain call of a legendary career. I won’t allow it to end in a whimper. I won’t allow what she did to me to go unpunished. For one more night, I have to be the monster that the entire world loved to hate. For one more night, I get to be … LESTATT KNIGHT
