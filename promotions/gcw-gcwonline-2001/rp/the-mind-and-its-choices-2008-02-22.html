The Mind and its choices!

22 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/22/08 The quiet room with flickering light gives an uncomfortable feeling across the area. The dark and damp room causes shivers for most people. Yet in the center sits a large man brooding and thinking. This man is a very familiar man to many folks in GCW as he is the former US Title champion. Losing to Tessa Windsor has caused him to rethink a lot of directions and thoughts in his life. Is this thought the changes needed for him to go all the way and defeat Vivica J Valentine. His hand raises and he stares at it. Often people wonder what is going across this mans mind as he sits in silence. Is it violent is it peaceful or is it something that nobody wishes to truly try to comprehend. The large man moves as he raises his eyes and looks straight ahead as he speaks as he raises his hands up in front of his eyes speaking softly. "Your own future is in question and you begin to question your loyalties and your capabilities. Then suddenly you lose by surprise circumstances. You don't like to be outplayed in the game. Then when you are you cannot help but question yourself. Are you good enough to survive are you a fighting source for the future did you forget who you are?" Rubbing his head the large man looks to be slightly stressed as he sweats a bit. His hands look to be a bit shaky and you begin to wonder is he afraid or is he just uncertain. Clasping his hands together he cracks the joints as he looks up cracks his neck from side to side seemingly attempting to get the tension out of his joints. "Damn it, I never thought of what was going on as something i was failing at. I am not going to pretend to be something i am not. I am going to be myself from the get go and in the end it seems people forget who i am." Standing suddenly the big man shakes his head flexing his large arms and muscles as he turns and walks around the room dodging the low hanging flickering lights. His head shadowed by the low hanging light fixtures as he growls low as he walks forward towards the wall. Reaching up he grabs the light fixture and turns the bulb towards the water stained wall with hundreds of pictures upon it. He reaches forward and plucks one off of it. Holding it up under the light he stares at it unmoving as he begins to speak softly. "This was me in the beginning. The believer of miracles and everything for the people. Many dreams and goals back then and in the end the one person who i did not put faith in was myself. I had faith in everything else, but myself. This road was to prove to everyone else i had skills and abilities." Tossing the picture to the floor he then reaches up and rips another picture off the wall as he holds that one in a similar manner. His eyes narrow as a bit of venom begins to tinge his voice. "My first title was an Extreme title in a division i helped build. 7 damn times i had held this damnable title in that place. Same goes for the Tag team, Tv Title, Us Title and low and behold the Heavyweight Championship belt. I have held many titles in my history. None of them mean anything now. I have captured several titles in GCW as well. The Tv Championship over my former partner Bryan Mayhem. Then the US Championship twice over Jordan White and Brad Jackson. Means nothing now there is a bigger target." Turning his gaze towards a poster on the wall with water stains upon it showing Vivica J Valentine holding the GCW World Heavyweight title. Reaching forward he tears it in half as it falls uselessly to the floor as the ragged remnants hang awkwardly on the wall by the little its of tape at its corners. Laughing he begins to look around and he turns walking towards the opposite end of the room as he dodges his head like a boxer around the lights as he knocks each of them with his free hand causing them to swing and cause the room to become a kaleidoscopes of rocking lights and shadows. "Goals, Achievements, and finality mean nothing unless you prove to yourself that you want something more than anything. What is it that i want?" Shaman looks at his hands looking around. He then grips them into tight fists as he turns and looks at the door. Touching the door as if it was a precious newborn child. He whispers low as he places an ear against the door. "Do you hear that? Can you here it? It is the freight train of destiny coming and it is going to want payment. Payment of blood and sweat from those who have been beaten before and from those who have yet to come. It is a cruel mistress destiny." Slowly he opens the door and shows a dark corridor as he reaches to the side and flicks on a light switch as he begins to walk down the dark corridor with very few old lights on and a lot of them obviously blown as they are in a single row on the ceiling. He stops and looks around he does move his body forward or backwards as he reaches into his pocket and pulls out a marker and rights on the wall. ----- US Title gone move forward ------ Shaman grins as he places the marker back in his pocket. He speaks not even looking at the writing afterwards. "I was beaten by Tessa Windsor with the help of Vivica J Valentine. Valentine has further earned herself a visit full of pain. She has hidden well over the course of my career and i want to hunt her down and take what she prizes and make sure she remembers the pain that follows. She has never faced anyone like me and in the end will wish she never had. I am not going to brag and promote this match as the greatest match of all time. Hell no! That isn't my style. I am going to let my actions speak for me and all in all let the match and the happenings leading to it speak for themselves." Walking his eyes forward his hands lightly tracing the walls as he walks. He leans forward and begins to run down the corridor and then stops at another door at the end as he grins. He then leans in and listens once again whispering. "Running fully into the unknown. Embracing all that i am is what i should be doing. I look to prove nothing to anyone. I open my arms wide and accept the path i have chosen. To hell with everyone else who thinks i am better. I have done the deeds and i am more than willing to accept the responsibilities of my actions. The only question that remains is whether or not anyone is willing to accept the deeds i am about to perform in full service to my own goals." Opening the door the sunlight is bright and shining in the hallway as Shaman's silhouette is outlined fully. He laughs as he speaks. "Doesn't matter if they are willing or ready. It has already begun for them to see. The new age is here and i am going to lead it places that demons fear to tread. Welcome to the nightmare that is reality. The bigger fish has come and this time things will be different." Shaman steps through as the door slams shut and the lights flicker in the hall leaving behind the dark and dank corridor that once held Shamans presence in it.
