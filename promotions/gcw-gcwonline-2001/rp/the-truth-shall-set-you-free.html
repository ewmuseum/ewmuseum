The Truth Shall Set You Free

Date: Before Warpath Looking at where we are right now in the life of Markus Stone, you would notice that he has had something of a turbulent time these past few weeks. Sure, everything is fine now, but the trip to get here... Well, that's a bit of a story in and of itself. So let's analyze these past days and see what this man has been going through. We'll start immediately after World Wide 68. For those with a short memory, you will have forgotten all about the Blue Collar Boys. If your name is Markus Stone, you don't want to remember them. It would, however, be just a bit difficult to release the memory of two men that Steven Caldera, the man who signs your paychecks, sent to cripple you. And what a night that was. ________________________________________________________ Backstage, Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor lean over the anguished body of Markus Stone. At his leg the paramedic presses and prods the knee, trying to discover the extent of the damage that was sustained at the hands of the Blue Collar Boys. "Don't worry Markus, you are going to be fine. We're going to make sure that you'll be okay, I promise." Despite the soothing words that Tessa uses to help Markus calm down, the gurgling moans that escape from his clenched teeth tell her and Chris that Markus probably won't be fine. When the paramedic looks at the two of them with a baffled look on his face, the outcome doesn't look any brighter. "The ambulance should be here shortly, but I don't know what's wrong with your friend yet," the paramedic hopelessly tells Chris. "What the hell do you mean you don't know? Isn't that your job?" Chris yells at the man. Chris quickly rises and takes a menacing steps towards the paramedic before a firm hand from Tessa stops him. "I don't think this is the right time Chris." Looking down at Markus, Chris gives a slight nod in agreement. It does seem a bit odd to see the two members of Banned and Exiled~! (copyrighted) serious, but for Tessa Windsor it is nice to see the man she loves show a bit of caring, although the gravity of the situation keeps that thought from staying for too long. "Chris," Markus strains to say without choking on his pain. "Listen for a second." Chris' attention shoots back to his friend, and Markus quickly continues. "Don't worry, I've been through worse. This knee isn't even mine, remember?" Chris Storm does remember, in fact. Chris remembers the surgery that Markus Stone had, he remembers the plastic and metal that were put in Markus' leg, and he also remembers that it is also the injury that caused Markus Stone to not wrestle for almost five years. Of course, Markus remembers, too. And those five years of memory come rushing back to him. The feeling of losing your livelihood, the daily struggle to maintain a sense of worth when his entire life was pulled out from under him. Can you imagine that feeling? Waking up everyday and realizing that you are not going back to the job that you love? That each night when you go to sleep, you will not lay down smiling with a feeling that you accomplished anything of worth? To realize that the rest of your life is going to be one big joke because you were separated from everything you love? Go with that thought for a minute, and maybe you will begin to understand just why Markus Stone always seems to be so juvenile now that he has returned to that which he loves. All that, however, might just be in jeopardy at the moment. Chris Storm realizes this, Tessa realizes this, but nobody feels that weight of this as much as Markus Stone. "Alright, we're going to lift you up now, okay?" a new face asks Markus. Lost in thought, he failed to see the paramedics arrive with a stretcher. Markus nods at the man, and chokes back a cry as they lift him. "We'll follow right behind Markus, you're gonna be okay," Chris tells his friend. Markus barely hears a word he says. Markus, Markus, are you listening? The Voice roars in his head, pulling Markus away from the doors that float past as he is wheeled to the parking area. 'Go away,' Markus thinks to the Voice. I feel the pain, too. Let me handle it for you Markus. 'Go away.' Although an escape from this pain is a good idea to Markus, giving anything to the Voice is not something Markus wants to do. Markus, I can take it away. Just give in to me for a little bit. I can handle this better than you can. Before Markus can think to say no, a wave a pain answers for him. The Voice senses the change, and springs into action, gently taking control away from Markus. ___________________________________________________________ Three days later, and Markus Stone is walking quite well. Especially for a man with a crutch tucked under each arm. Three weeks, that's what the doctor told him. Three weeks off the leg, and another appointment to analyze the knee again. Three weeks, and that's three weeks too long for Markus. Back home now, in Washington, Markus tries coping with the idea of never wrestling again, again. It would be difficult to think about it by itself, but with a match for the Tag Team Championship only days away makes it much more stressful. I could do that for you, too. Markus doesn't even think to respond. Giving up control after World Wide was bad enough, Markus refuses to release himself again. It's a surprise he even remembers anything from his stay in the hospital. You couldn't handle the pain then, you think you handle it against three other tag teams? Probably not. No, probably not. You shouldn't even be acting so afraid, I could have kept control. I could have kept all the memories from you, but I didn't. I gave everything back Markus. And I'll give it back again. All I want to do is help. Help. That's something that Markus Stone has never accepted very easily. Not when it comes to his personal problems, and his injuries have always been a personal problem. 'Pain is weakness leaving the body,' Markus always said. Another injury merely meant one more step forward. Markus, let me in. Before Markus could respond, he felt his grip being torn away. Like a balloon slipping out of a child's grasping hand, Markus felt himself slip away from reality. Flailing in vain to fight back, Markus felt himself being pulled deeper and deeper into... Into where? Looking around now, Markus is no longer in his house. The sky is no longer an overcast grey. As Markus begins trying to cope with the black mountains around him, the red sky and the barren landscape, a flicker of movement is noticed to his left. Markus tries to turn, but nearly falls over. Glancing down at his right leg, Markus sees that there is nothing but a stump below his thigh. "What the fuck?" How could these happen? This has to be a dream, just a dream. Maybe it is a dream Mark, or maybe this is the place inside your head where all the dark things hide. As the Voice appears, the movement soon materializes into a shadowed figure. Not just a shadowed figure, but a figure made out of shadows, sucking the darkness from behind everything it steps near. "I... I don't understand?" The figures shoulders softly bounce as a chuckle escapes from it. Of course not Markus, how could you? How could you ever comprehend that those doctors never told you anything was wrong with your leg? You've been hobbling around for years now, and you've never even had a sprain. Completely confused, Markus looks down at his leg. He can see it begin to materialize, only to fade back out rapidly. "Nothing wrong? You mean..." Starting to catch on Markus? "You mean all this time, I've been just fine? All this time I hobbled around like some fucking retard and nothing was wrong with me!?!" Markus tries to let this sink in, but the building rage won't let it. Aw, are you made at me? C'mon Markus, this is all for your benefit. "How, the hell, can this be for my benefit?" Through clenched teeth Markus tries listening to the Voice as he takes another steps closer to Markus. Look at you. You've always wanted something to be wrong and fucked up. You were never happy, even when you were a World Champion. You weren't happy unless you had some insurmountable issue getting in your way. Markus, you're a fucking baby, and you are only content when you have something to bitch and cry about. The Voice takes another step forward, leaning in only a few inches from Markus' face. Now, tell me I'm lying. A veritable dictionary of profanity shoots through Markus Stone's mind, but it's all cut off by on short, simple thought. "You're right." Excellent! The Voice leans back and claps its hands. So now you are beginning to see, right? To see how my crippling you was only meant to help you? How I've been right all along, and you need me, right? "No." The Voice pauses, somewhat stunned by Markus' reply. Leaning back in, the Voice glares at Markus through black pools where his eyes should be. What? "You heard me." And with that Markus spits in his face. "I don't need you." Markus, I don't really think you understand just the fuck I am. "And I don't fucking ca..." Before Markus can finish his sentence, the shadows of the Voice's face peel back, and Markus can only stare in shock at who he sees. "But... You're me..." Close. I'm better. I am whole and complete Markus. Look at this place, this wasteland. I didn't create it. This is your mind Markus. You drive every bit of happiness away and out of yourself, and in that void you created this plane, and its inhabitant. Markus, you made me. Isn't it fitting that I now take control? Isn't it right that I should be the one to carry on our name? You've had your chance brother, and you failed. You failed at life. The truth finally slams home to Markus Stone, and the veils are parted before him. In a flash Markus can remember first seeing the doctor, and hearing that he had no permanent damage to his leg. Markus can once again feel the pain that every step has caused him for the last five years, and now sees that it was all in his mind. Most of all, Markus sees every time he let the Voice control him, and the deceit that He caused. Finally, Markus Stone can see the truth. "I don't need you any more." And in a moment of willpower, Markus forces his leg to materialize. "I see it all now. And the truth is, you failed. Maybe I created you, in some moment of weakness. In the end, you also created something much more powerful. Didn't we always say pain was weakness leaving the body? Right? I've felt pain for five long years, and I've never felt stronger than I do now." Markus begins stalking towards the Voice, who hesitantly backs away. You think you can beat me? Think you can shove me back down again? "No, I'm going to forget all about you." In a flash Markus is back in his house. Looking around, Markus lets out a sigh of relief. He's gone now, the Voice is gone along with all traces he was ever present. All traces meaning the throbbing pain that Markus has endured for the past five years. Gone is the limp. The Markus Stone who found so little genuine joy in life is gone, and in his place stands the Markus Stone of old. The beginning of a new day and age, and it starts with a step five years backwards. "And now to deal with the pay per view." Reaching into his pocket, Markus grabs his cell phone and begins calling Chris Storm, a plan already forming in his mind.
