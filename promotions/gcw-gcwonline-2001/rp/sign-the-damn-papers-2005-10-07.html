Sign the damn papers....

7 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/07/05 “That’s twice Vinny boy. Two times.” Ricky reflects back to his win at NC17 over Vince Jones, and smiles to himself a bit. He grabs his jacket and heads out the door where the rain has started to fall a bit. Ricky starts towards his car and decides to just walk as he heads down the sidewalk. About a block up the road sits a small caf? where a few people sit under umbrellas drinking coffee and conversing. Ricky stares over almost as if he is looking for someone, then heads inside. “Hello Mr. Thunder, welcome to the Drinkeria.” comes an unusually chipper voice. Ricky nods in acknowledgment and grabs a bottle of ice cold water from the cooler, and proceeds to the register to pay. He notices the slick marble and can only grin to himself as he envisions someone slipping and falling. He pays for his drink as he approaches a table where a beautiful woman sits. He notices her two piece business suit and the laptop, and decides to make himself at home sitting across from her. She glances up then goes back to her laptop not saying a word. Ricky takes a sip from the bottle and sets it down on the table, and clears his throat. “You’re late.” She speaks coldly. “I’m here” Ricky retorts. She shoves papers to him “Sign here, here, and here” as she points out the three areas. Ricky reviews the paperwork and signs. “So this is finally over eh?” “It’s about time, I was thinking you would never sign these damn papers” she snaps. “Look, I’ve been busy. Let’s not make this any harder then it has to be. My schedule doesn’t allow me to just come and go. You know that” “Look Ricky, this has been going on for a month. It’s way overdue. You know it and I know it.” The woman snatches up the paperwork, and slides it into her portfolio which she then places in the briefcase next to the table. She starts typing on the laptop, as Ricky reaches over the table and pushes it closed. “I would ask you out for coffee, but since we’re already here?” “Stop. Your charm doesn’t work on me. It never has, and it never will.” “Ok, ok! I will get going then. What’s the next step?” Ricky asks “The partners will contact you once everything is in place. The equipment for the water purification facility has been ordered, as well and research analysts hired. We also have a team of scientists working with us to develop possible new flavors or other items we may?” “WHOA! Flavors” Are you insane? You can’t taint the water!” “You’re obsessed” “Maybe so, but still’water that tastes this good shouldn’t be tainted!” Ricky explodes. “Anyhow, if there is nothing else I will be going.” “When should I expect a call from the partners” I would like to tour the facilities. I would like to see the operation, once its in motion. And you said we have scientists right?” “That is correct, what do you have in mind?” “It’s top secret, but I would like to set up a meeting with them to discuss a new idea. It will triple our profits in the first quarter alone!” “Well try not to get ahead of yourself. I will let the partners know you would like to meet with the development team. Have a nice day Mr. Thunder.” The woman packs up without much more thought, and leaves the caf?, obviously not intrigued by Ricky. Ricky heads over to the counter and pays the ticket for his water, and the coffee the woman stiffed him with. He leaves the caf? and heads home, and immediately grabs the phone, punching in some new numbers. The voice on the other end is that of an answering machine. Ricky laughs a little as he leaves the message. “Hey Vince this is Ricky. Good news, the deal went through and I now own a water company. So your bottle slinging days will be few and far between. God knows you can’t have a company without lab rats. Catch up to you soon” Ricky continues to laugh as he pulls out a piece of scrap paper from his pocket with some general ideas for the meet with the scientists. He reviews the details then smiles? “Waterplement! The first muscle mass supplement based off water! I soooo rule!”
