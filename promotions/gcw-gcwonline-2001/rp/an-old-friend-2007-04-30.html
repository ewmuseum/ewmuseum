An Old Friend..

30 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/30/07 Something’s are never what they seem, at least that’s the way he saw things. Just a little less then a week ago he was sitting down with his mentor in Shaman to have a few beers and eat some good bar-b-que. Nothing seemed out of the ordinary in what they discussed and Shaman played it off as if he was just there to check up on things. Knowing damn well in his mind that Shaman had other reasons for being in Rochester that rainy night. Still a taken back by what happened on world wide, with Shaman and Mayhem coming down to the ring and making him an offer to join oblivion. Thinking the offer over as he sits on one of his patio chairs, the sun is shining and a warm spring breeze is blowing. A beer in his left hand as he sits there, looking out into his backyard that seems to stretch for an acre or two. Looking over to his right he sees his yellow lab blaze sitting next to him with his ball in his mouth. Reaching out he quickly pulls the ball from the dogs mouth and throws it off the patio and into the backyard. As the dog takes off after the ball he sinks down a little more into the chair. His hair is down and moving around in the wind as his black killer loop sunglasses shine in the reflection from the sun, not wearing shoes his feet are getting the breeze and so are his legs. His black mesh Adidas shorts stop just below his knees and his white t-shirt is wrinkled but still fits well, taking a sip of his beer he leans his head back. Choice’s, he’s had many of them before but none like the offer that Shaman laid down to him. An offer to join his mentor and the current world champion and reek havoc upon the GCW. Closing his eyes for a second he starts to think more, nothing happens out of the blue, this had to have all been set up for awhile. Back tracking he remembers seeing Shaman watching his matches from the ramp and focusing solely on him, it hadn’t surprised him as much as he thought it would. Opening his eyes quickly he stands up from his chair, hearing a noise from the roof. Backing up now he leans against the railing of the porch and looks up at the edge of the roof.. Seeing a black glove resting on the edge he quickly jumps up onto the railing of the patio to get a better view. Trying to keep his balance as he stands on the narrow railing he can now some what make out what it is, Bones. David "Bones" McCoy, a friend of his sisters and someone who has been watching over him for awhile now, it’s also his former kick boxing trainer. McCoy is a former Navy seal who when he retired picked up kick boxing and a little bit of wrestling. An older man in his mid to late forties sits up on the roof, his gray hair shining almost white in the sun light. Now leaning forward Kyle puts his hands on the edge of the roof, glancing left and right quickly he jumps from the railing and moves onto the roof. Getting his whole body on the roof now he gets to his knees, brushing him self off he quickly gets to both feet and stairs down at McCoy. Kyle: "Christ, how many times have I told you not to do this kind of shit?" Now moving the hand that was on his forehead down to the roof top he looks up at Kyle who is blocking the sun from hitting his eyes. Almost forcing a half smile across his face he shakes his head slowly. McCoy: "You’ll never get it Kyle, I do this to you in order to keep you on your toes. I’ve said it before and I’ll say it again, it’s not meant to startle you or make you angry. I just popped in to see how things were.." Before he can finish his sentence Kyle cuts his off. Kyle: "Everything is fine Bones. I don’t have a major complaint about how things are now, but like I said before.. quit this shit." Laughing at Kyle now Bones looks down, his left hand pushing hard into the roof as he pops up to his feet surprisingly quick for a man of his age. McCoy: "It keeps your mind sharp and makes you react with out thinking, a key of any sort of warfare." Kyle: "This isn’t a war bones, it’s every day life. Nothings changed since Diana died and nothing will." Glairing back at Kyle after that comment McCoy quickly looks away from him. Not wanting Kyle to see the pain in McCoy’s eyes. What no one else knows besides Kyle is that McCoy was almost as close to Diana as Kyle was. He was like her father, the only true one she had ever known but at the same time Kyle looked at him as a friend, not a father. The day she died it stopped and shocked both their worlds. Being his age, retired and with the only other thing in his life being his dog, Bones goes up to Diana’s grave often.. just to remember the good times they had as well as the troubled times they shared. Kyle: "Bones I didn’t mean it like that, she was and still is my sister. I know how it feels man." Still looking away from Kyle, he starts to walk towards the edge of the roof. McCoy: "So when are you going to see her next?" A question that caught him off guard, he hasn’t even thought about it yet. Between being back on the road again with the GCW and spending time with his wife and kid, he hasn’t been up to see his sister in almost two months. Kyle: "I? I don’t Quickly cutting Kyle off.. Bones: "You don’t know, I could have answered that question for you. I am up there at least four times a week and she was just a good friend to me. You’re her flesh and blood, make the time and go visit her." Kyle: "How do you know when I visit her anyways, it’s not like your around." Turning back to face Kyle again he has a look of anger in his eyes, a look of deception. Little does Kyle know that he sees everything that happens around him. Now walking toward him he stops just a few steps away from Kyle and looks him up and down. McCoy: "I see everything hot shot, I have eyes in the sky as well as on the ground. I could know your every move if I really wanted to, after all being a former navy seal I do have my connections still. I wasn’t there last week but I saw that you stopped by the school.. what were you looking for there?" Pushing his sun glasses up and into his hair, Kyle glares back at David who hasn’t looked away in a minute now. Kyle: "I wasn’t looking for anything to be honest, I don’t know why I stopped in. I was downtown walking around to clear my head, no one was around or out due to the weather so I figured I would walk." McCoy: "Why there and why now, years after you walked out on it and didn’t want to come back." Kyle: "I wanted to bring back a memory of me and my sister, something that I haven’t done in a long time." Nodding his head slowly he understands, he does it him self from time to time. Looking away from McCoy now, Kyle sits down on the roof and brings his shades back down over his eyes. Thinking for a minute about everything that’s been said as well as his match on world wide this week against Inaki Masata. A Japanese wrestler who has everything to prove as well as gain this week. Knowing that the Japanese wrestlers aren’t a joke nor do they have any regard for there own safety let alone others, it should be a solid match. In his mind he’ll be representing the good old U S of A against Japan. Turning to look back over at Bones he’s gone again, just as he had shown up almost silent he took off just as quietly. Shaking his head with a smile on his face now he looks back out over his backyard. As the trees sway in the slow warm breeze he looks up at the sky, knowing his sister is watching over him and that she along with his wife Faith will guide him to make the right decision. Oblivion made there offer and now it’s his time to answer up as well as show why he got this offer in the first place.
