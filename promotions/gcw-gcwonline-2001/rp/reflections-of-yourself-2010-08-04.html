Reflections of yourself

4 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/04/10 August 2nd 2010 New York City, The day is hot as the sun sets in the background. In the room a lone figure simply stares out the window in self thought. His eyes wander from street to street from this height. He simply leans against the window with one arm resting above his head against the glass. The light shines through the window casting his shadow across the floor. He breaths a deep breathe as he continues to watch. You know I begin to wonder why they all look like ants from way up here. Like little matchbox cars sliding along a road or street to nowhere. All these years always seem to be staring down at the ants. Then one day I want to step in and crush them all. Just push my thumb down hard onto each of them. It isn't as if I didn't try to please them. Screw them I should have long ago worried more about myself than any of the others. Just like the first day I came to GCW, everyone said watch out for Bryan mayhem he will crush you. I looked at them and my exact words were, Bring him on I like to teach people lessons. Next the first war between two monsters of GCW. I have faced many opponents in GCW, yet here I am facing another guy who can be classified nuts perhaps. Strumming his fingers on the window he observes the world below him. “You know sometimes I wonder if I was ever down there with them, won’t you think so?” Turning he realizes nobody is there as he sighs a bit remembering he kicked everyone out much earlier in the day. Turning he stares out at the cities skyline at a distance. Man sometimes it isn't good to have bouts of anger and kick everyone out. You always comeback and question your decisions after you make them. I am not perfect, but I will be damned if I put up with bullshit from people who think they are better than me. Thinking back here at my time in GCW it is simple really. I have held the TV Title, the US Title, almost gained the Heavyweight title in a brutal match with Valentine. After all that I feel like I slid a bit. I should have been able to beat Mayhem and crush Diamond and grab that hardcore title, but for some reason I was distracted. Walking away from the window he heads into the kitchenette. He opens the refrigerator and reaches in grabbing a beer by passing the water bottles. Turning he heads back towards the door leading to the balcony. Opening it up he walks outside, he simply takes in the evening. Drinking the beer slowly he lowers the bottle and rubs his temple. Things are different nowadays; everyone knows how dangerous I can be. Not many people recognize the dangers of it though. When do I snap, when do I happily break your pinky and watch you cry. I may be one of the most unpredictable people out there. I'd be more than happy to grab a ball peen hammer and drive it down on your toes. No the truth is there is a battle, no a war going on to prove yourselves or take your own stand against anything and everything. Look in the damn mirror people. Spencer knows I will come at him and bring the damn pain. Sometimes people need to open their damn eyes and look at who they are dealing with. Spencer is like a rabid animal to me. I need to put him down, not out of necessity, but more out of pity. The elevator doesn't go to the top floor if you ask me. Placing the beer down, he places both arms on the top railing looking out over the ocean to the right. A light out in the ocean catches his attention. He recognizes it as a lighthouse as it seems to be going around in a turning fashion. He simply just watches the light kind of mesmerized. Kind of nice to know, that a lighthouse can mean more than just a sailor’s safety. It kind of calms the raging storm inside of oneself. You always wander through the darkness thinking he where is my grounding point. What can I use to bring me back to basics? Keep me locked down to what it is I have as a center. Everyday I head to that ring there are times I want to reach into the fans and slap some idiot senseless. The people sometimes are so damned stupid ignorant backwoods hicks. They act like they are the ones who make a difference. I wonder what they would think if I reached out and snapped one of their scrawny little damn necks. Smack the piss out of them causing their eye balls to pop right out of their own head. Bah what am I thinking they are there for the show I should concentrate more on the titles that should be mine. People seem to dodge me, hasn't been many people to come straight at me. I will give Diamond and Mayhem that credit. They weren't afraid to keep coming, but low and behold these little blood wars need to be reigned in. Mayhem and I know that we will always fight. We teamed up for awhile, but he showed his true colors. Someone comes to the door behind him as she speaks. He turns and recognizes Suzy as she observes the scene. She frowns and walks over taking the empty beer bottle from him. “Yeah this will solve a lot of things won't it?” Looking at her he responds shaking his head. “It is the only one I had, just wanted to relax and enjoy the view in silence without all the jackals trying to get something from me.” Rubbing his temples he turns and watches the ocean beyond. She smiles as he looks at what he is looking at. “Nice of my Dad to be apologizing for a lot of the stuff he did.” Turning his gaze towards her he shakes his head. “Suzy he took our daughters money and used it for his own personal reasons. In fact personal reasons to try and ruin my career, to start some sort of damn reality show where he helps washed up wrestlers or famous sports figures that had fallen from grace?” He literally spits his disgust out onto the floor of the balcony. Suzy shakes her head looking down as she looks up at him. “Things will fix themselves; the people he tried to hire stated they will speak against him in court.” She smiles softly trying to calm him down as she turns him. “Doesn't make it right does it?” He speaks with venom in his voice. I'll fucking tear his intestines out through his damned mouth. Nobody and I mean nobody comes between me and my family. I hate the damned bastards that abuse my family and my kindness. Jacob stole money from my child, my own child. I can handle a person stealing from me, but my child is a death wish. Right now I need to keep my cool and concentrate on my match coming. I need to do what I need to do that is necessary for my goal in GCW. Jacob is in jail right now and he will get his in the end. Not like what I will do in the ring to those who think I am the same every time I go into the ring. I like to bleed and I will smile every time I get hit am in pain. Want to know why? It makes me feel alive when all the rest of the world just seems to stomp on you while you are down. The doctors tell me it could lead to high blood pressure. What the hell do they know? They can just as simply just stitch me up and just shut the hell up. I pay them to do their specific job, not psychiatric help. Everyone thinks it is all so simple reading me or understanding me. Placing both of his hands down onto the railing he just looks at his knuckles as he squeezes. The white stress under the skin is seen clearly. Suzy smiles and kisses his cheek as she heads back into the room through the doors. Looking through the corner of his eye his notices she leaves. Sometimes she is my foundation keeping me from snapping completely. Everyday it seems I struggle to keep the rage in check. The want and need to just lash out and severely hurt somebody. I don't know if I hate letting that side of me out. I do know I want to reach out and choke the damn life out of people who cross me. My family is important to me and I will do anything to protect them. In fact I am going to drive up to Vermont and see my grandmother. At least I know she will always shoot straight and be honest. The time I spend with her makes everything I do seem so much like fun and less like work. I hope to see her soon; even though she is old she is a great woman. Turning he walks inside the room, Turning he shuts the door walking away.
