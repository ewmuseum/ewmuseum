The Quest, Pt. 1: The Road Ahead

10 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/10/2005 [Scene fades up on security camera footage of security camera footage. We see Sam Wolack sitting at a table, hands in front of him. He's wearing the same attire that he left the Gund Arena in earlier in the evening.] Wolack: Ok, guys, where are you? I get a call at my hotel to come in for more questioning, I'm here, now where the f*** are you. [Entering the room is a detective, carrying a folder. He pulls out a chair and sits down across from Wolack.] Detective: No attorney this time? Wolack: Don't need one, I have nothing to hide or lie about. Detective: That's good. First, I want to let you know that we found evidence that proves that you weren't involved in that murder in Boston. Wolack: Good. If that's all you wanted, then why didn't you tell me that over the phone, could have saved me a few bucks from trying to find a last second flight back here. Detective: Well, that's not the only reason that we called you here. We wanted to go back over some things in your statement about the disappearance of Andrea Scabbia. Wolack: You guys just don't get it, do you? I had nothing to do with Andrea's disappearance. For all I know, you had something to do with it. After all, she wasn't found after the O'Conners were killed, so she's either been hidden by one of their associates, or she's been kidnapped again. Detective: What do you think? Wolack: What I think is that you aren't doing your job very well, detective. You have just about as many answers to what happened to her as I do. Now I've co-operated with you in trying to find her. I've told you everything that I know, and you still treat me as if I'm the scum of the earth. Detective: Well, you were the..... Wolack: Detective, I know what you're about to say, so shove it. You and every other incompetant fool that has been trying to find her has said the same god d*** thing. I was the last person to see her before she was kidnapped. Please, tell me something that I don't already know. Because either you do that, or you can stop wasting my f***ing time. Detective: Well, Mr. Wolack, that's all for now. We'll keep in touch. [Scene then fades to a gym and wrestling school in Scottsdale. Wolack is punching a heavybag. Sweat is covering his body. All of a sudden, the lights come on. Wolack grasps the bag from both sides.] Woman's voice: It's late. Wolack: Serena, sorry about how late it is, but this was the only thing that I could think of doing right now. [Into the picture walks Serena Shaw-Draven, Wolack's sister-in-law. She's wearing a robe and sweats.] Serena: Sam, you know that you are always welcome to use the facilities whenever you want, just please call first so I can tell the alarm company to expect your code to be entered. Wolack: Sorry about that, just had a very bad day. Serena: Well, Jay's not here. Wolack: I know, he told me that he had to take care of some business in Japan. Serena: Yeah, one of his students got hurt over there. Wolack: Car crash? Serena: No, broke his leg on a botched Quebrada. What's wrong? Wolack: This s*** with the police. They still haven't found Andrea and they think that I had something to do with it. Serena: Don't worry too much about it. The police know what they're doing, so I'm sure that they will find her. Wolack: Thanks, that make's one of us. It seems that they bring me in on a weekly basis to ask me the same things over and over. Serena: They just want to be sure that you didn't have anything to do with it. Wolack: Funny thing is that Andrea was my only friend in Los Angeles. She helped get me back into wrestling. Serena: Now you know what you have to do. You have to let the police do their job and find her. Don't worry about it too much. Wolack: Yeah, I'll try, but no promises. Serena: That's good. Come on up, I've got some lasagna from earlier still. Wolack: Thanks. [Scene switches to outside of the three story building that houses the wrestling school. There is a man sitting on the hood of Wolack's Jeep Wrangler. Wolack exits the building and locks up. He turns to see the man.] Wolack: You have ten seconds to get off of my hood. [The man hops off the hood and reaches into his jacket and pulls out an envelope.] Man: Everything you ever wanted to know is in this envelope. [The man thrusts the envelope into Wolack's hands. Wolack opens it up and sees pictures of Andrea Scabbia being forced into a car. He then pulls out a map of Bangkok. As Sam looks up, he sees that the man has walked away. Wolack pulls out his phone and dials a number.] Wolack: Serena, I need you do do me a favor. [Scene fades to black.]
