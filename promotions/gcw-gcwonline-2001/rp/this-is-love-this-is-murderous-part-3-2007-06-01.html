I never want to leave you and the memories for us to see I beg don't leave me (Silence you lost me, no chance for one more day) I stand here alone Falling away from you, no chance to get back home Seize The Day Avenged Sevenfold ---- Chapter Six: Playing God ---- His cold yellowish eyes grew large as he slammed the book shut and walked away from it slowly. Looking around he tried to get his mind off of what he just read. It was the worst night of his life, and some sick bastard was probably sitting in some air-conditioned room in a recliner... enjoy the show of putting him through the agony of his own past. Catching a glimpse of himself in the full length mirror he scowls. He was a monster, an abomination, a murderer. He didn't deserve life... but either did the coward watching him squirm. "You think this is funny?" He said out loud, looking around for a camera... anything where he may be getting watched from. If this vampiric curse brought him anything, it was the perception to know these type of things. He could see the bastard smiling in his mind. "I may be a killer..." Speaking out again he continued as his voice rose to a scream, "but I've paid dearly for my sins. The sins of may past may have driven away my soulmate. What price have you paid? What right do you have to look down on me for what I've done? WHAT GIVES YOU THE FUCKING RIGHT TO PLAY GOD?!" He looked down at his arms, which should be scarred from the constant IVs from the past year. Yet there was nothing. Not even a scratch. Another painful reminder of his fate. Everywhere he looked was yet another reason to die. In a fit of rage he swung towards the mirror and watched it shatter before him. Millions of glittering shards rained down onto the floor as he began to breathe in and out with sickening groans. He'd felt like this before, but never before had he wanted to kill so strongly. "Come and face me you coward." He roared, "It's my turn to play God... you sick perverted fuck." Suddenly part of the wall opened up on the side of the room and four men wearing black riot control like equipment came inside. A smirk crossed his face as they entered. Someone was going to pay. Leaping across the room he tackled the one leading the way and showing off his sharp fangs with sinful delight. The other men in panic shot their tazers, piercing into his flesh. With an electric shock he jumped back off of the man, shaking and smoking before jerking the guns out of their hands with freakish force. Ripping the hooks out of his back he fell to one knee, squinting as he felt them leave his skin. A man went to try to retain him as he began to rose. A big mistake. He grabbed the man by the throat and pinned him against the shattered mirror. His eyes lost all of their humanity as he glared into the mans soul. Slowly he opened his mouth with a sickening smile, barring his razor sharp fangs as the man squirmed in fear. Almost like a shot he dropped the man and backed away scared. Fearing what he became again. Fearing what he was turning into. Once again... his unwillingness to embrace his curse as a gift became his downfall. Quickly he felt something metal clasp around his neck from behind. Turning around quickly to see what it was he went into a panic before it felt like he was struck by lightning. Falling down to his chest he pried at the collar now around his neck, kicking and screaming as electricity shot through his body. Smoke began to rise from the collar as it singed the skin on his neck. Rolling over onto his back the pain stopped. His eyes that once were trying to roll into the back of his skull slowly started to readjust to a black blur that stood above him. As it came into focus the eyes were icy and manipulative. A shade of cadet blue that burned the soul. His face was stoic as he looked down at his prey, with a smug since of satisfaction. "Markus Blair..." The man spoke looking down at the broken vampire who laid at his feet, "I think it's time I reminded you once again what it is to be MY property." ---- Chapter Seven: Shattered Dreams ---- Walking through the desert sands, the breeze blew through her hair... just like it did on that night. Everything felt the same but so much had changed. With each passing day come more regret. Her life seemed to live in cycles since that day. Every night returning to the same place to really try and convince herself that he was gone. She didn't know if she was in a state of denial or if she really just couldn't get over the incident. It affected every facet of her life these days. A mind once focused on claiming destiny now on the fritz. A new destiny had risen over the horizons of The Fearless Phenom. A tragic destiny that left her a shell of the vibrant, charismatic self she once was. It was growing harder and harder to wake up in the morning and look at herself in the mirror without him there. She needed Markus by her side... and it didn't appear that he was coming. Walking along the rubble of what was once the guest house on the Ambrosi Estate, Vivica J. Valentine wore a black wife-beater style tank top and a pair of black and white Tripp Pants. Her cerulean blue eyes once again looking back and forth over the wreckage in disbelief. Every time she saw it, it was harder and harder to see. Sitting down outside the yellow police tape, she glared into the stars hanging high above her in the Nevada sky. Tears began to flow down her face. Just as they did the night before... and the night before that. Her heart swelled inside of her chest and her throat clinched in melancholy. Laying another red rose on the ground next to her, the collection continued to grow. Along with a small white cross and two photographs, there were now five roses and all. One for each day of her life she's had to live without him. Picking up the picture she looked at it and took a deep breath. Tomorrow she knew she'd do the same. So did Virgil Ambrosi. "Don't you have anything better to do with your life?" He spoke as he always did. True and without regret. While some people found the voice of Virgil Ambrosi to be harsh (Vivica included), you couldn't often describe his comments as false. Usually this space would be filled with a witty remark. This time Vivica replied with on silence. "You know... Warpath is coming soon. Everyone else has left. Getting ready in the arena for their matches." He spoke, knowing Vivica was listening even though her back was to him... unmoving, "One would think with all this talk about destiny, you'd get off your ass and do something about it." "You don't know what this feels like..." Vivica finally cracked a response through her tightly clinched throat. Her heart was heavy, eyes filled with tears looking down at his picture. "I don't know what it's like to watch people die?" Virgil responded cynically, "Do you realize who you're talking to? I've walked this earth longer than you can possibly imagine. I'll walk it long after you're gone, I assure you." "So..." Vivica looked back at him "Are you out here to kick my while I'm down? Because I'm not in the fucking mood Virgil. I wasn't in the mood in GCW and I'm not in the FUCKING MOOD NOW. YOU GOT THAT?!" Virgil stood unmoved as Vivica shot a look up at him. It was a look he'd seen many times before, but never did it have so much feeling behind it. Even with his experiences it struck him as making him a little bit uneasy... but found solace in the fact it was coming from a 5 foot mortal. "Look..." Virgil grumbled back, "You can sit out in my yard moping about everything you've lost for the rest of your life or you can get up off your ass and do something about it. Get back in your car, drive to the airport, get your head straight and so what I've heard you speak NOTHING ELSE about for the past year. Go out and earn your shot at the World Title." "It wasn't supposed to end this way you know..." Vivica said, "He wasn't supposed to die. He's supposed to walk this earth forever and I'm supposed to die. I was never supposed to live to see this day." "Everything happens for a reason." Virgil responded turning his back to his former teammate known to many as The Black Mariah, "If you're out here again tomorrow night, you can forget ever seeing that World Championship. If Markus was here... he'd tell you the same thing. Quit feeling sorry for yourself and live your life. He would've." Virgil Ambrosi walked away into the darkness, almost vanishing out of her sight as she watched him. Laying the picture of Markus back down next to the small white wooden cross, Vivica J. Valentine wiped the tears away from her eyes. "I miss you." She spoke down to the picture as she walked away, still shaking a little from her nightly breakdown. As she entered the Black Chrysler Crossfire, she looked into the rear view mirror and saw her reflection. In it she saw a broken shell. The same look was in her eyes that were in the eyes of Christian Zenith so many years ago when he retired from Global Wrestling to obsess over Tera. She'd turned into the very thing she ran away from. Maybe it was looking at herself in the mirror that night, or maybe it was the not so kind words of a former stablemate... but something clicked inside Vivica J. Valentine that night. She longed to move on her with life, but it was so hard to simply look away. Going this far in her GCW career was a journey she'd never want to repeat again... but nothing in her life had ever been as hard as letting go. This is her fork in the road. This is her crossroads. To the left is true love. To the right is your biggest dreams. The choice is yours.
