Duality Legacy Loyalty

1 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-01-06 There are two of me. One most of you are quite familiar with. It's the President Caldera you've seen on GCW television for the last five years. The most powerful man in professional wrestling, a self-made man who rose from obscurity to start a wildly successful wrestling promotion out of his own pocket. I collected every penny I had to begin GCW, traded in my wrestling boots for a suit and a desk, and built an empire from the ground up. I've grown and changed quite a bit in this role over the years; once I was the most hated man in the company, using my power to pull every string, to influence every outcome to satisfy my whim. Now I am an observer, stepping in only to adjudicate, but still watching every move you make with relentless vigilance. I am feared, this much is certain. But I am not feared for my physical prowess. Those who know me best know that I have no need to get my hands dirty. The stroke of a pen is all I need to dictate your fate. President Caldera answers to no one, the master of his own domain, ruling unquestioned with unbridled power. But there is another part of me most of you have never seen. It's Steven Caldera, just Steven Caldera. Steven Caldera the wrestler. Most of you are not familiar with my work in the ring, because my active career was short lived. I suffered a devastating knee injury the night I won my first world championship (the first of many). I managed to survive in the ring for a few years thereafter, but it didn't take long for someone to pick up on the weakness and take advantage of it. It was a man named Hangman that did it; he ravaged my knee, destroyed every ligament holding me together, and effectively ended my career. It's taken me four years to learn how to mask my limp. But the two aren't so different. President Caldera was the subject of a meteoric rise to prominence, quickly earning acclaim as the promoter of the most promising new wrestling company in the country. But Steven Caldera experienced a similar rise to fame; Steven Caldera, who went from wrestling in local gyms to his first major contract and his first World Title in the span of six weeks. I took the world by storm, I destroyed men with years of experience more than me. That company was the American Championship Wrestling Federation. It was short lived; most of the company, realizing how futile it would be to compete against me, deserted and fled back to the local gyms. But my knee was damaged, and I took over Nathan Krotzer's CWU, and before long President Caldera was born. But Steven Caldera lived on. After some time I realized that, much as I loved the absolute power and control that being in charge brought, I would always be a wrestler at heart. I joined the American Wrestling Alliance, a group of talented wrestlers who harbored no fear or reverence of me. Despite the damage to my knee, my star rose quickly, and two years later I was inducted into their Hall of Fame. The body of work I created there was legendary among their own fans, but is a legend most of you have never heard. I will leave it to the most curious and resourceful of you to learn the legend for yourself. But I will say that those years were among the best in my life, because it reaffirmed to me that, though my body be ravaged, there was a time when I was considered the greatest wrestler in the world. Because this legend is unknown to most of you, I have been called crazy for challenging Rich Rollins and Angelo Deville to an Asphalt, Chains, Wrenches and Forklifts match at NC-17. I have been called crazy for allying myself with a partner who is equally as fragile as I am. But what you may fail to realize is that you are witnessing the synthesis of President Caldera and Steven Caldera. President Caldera, the man who demands control and loyalty. Steven Caldera, the proud wrestler who always wants to be known as the best. President Caldera was betrayed by Rich Rollins, who defamed Global Championship Wrestling and insulted the man who had given him his career. Now Steven Caldera will avenge him. I hold no illusions that this is anything but a suicide mission. Angelo Deville and Rich Rollins are two of the most talented wrestlers in the world and are in the midst of their prime. Steven Caldera is a fragile, damaged old wrestler, and Seymour Almasy is on his retirement tour. It is likely that the damage I suffer in this match will render me unable to wrestle again. But President Caldera demands satisfaction. Global Championship Wrestling is the pride and joy, the one true love of President Caldera. Her honor has been besmirched by Rich Rollins, and he must be punished. He walked out on the only family he has ever known, on the one man who supported him unconditionally through the highs and lows of his career. I will not allow him to continue his career anywhere but Global Championship Wrestling. Rollins has forgotten that GCW is in his blood, whether he likes it or not. At NC-17, Rollins will see his GCW blood spilled. It is every artist's dream to leave behind a masterpiece before he goes. Steven Caldera is an artist, his canvas the wrestling ring. But at NC-17, the canvas will be the Asphalt, Chains, Wrenches and Forklifts of the most devastating match in professional wrestling. I do not forsee myself ever competing in the ring again. But neither shall Rollins; for my masterpiece will be the demise of Rich Rollins, the humiliation of Angelo Deville, the redemption of Seymour Almasy, and the glorification of Global Championship Wrestling, all in one magnificent match. Watch NC-17 on December 23rd. There you will witness the end of Rich Rollins, and the rebirth of Global Championship Wrestling. I will give my career, my livelihood for the good of GCW. And that, Rollins, is the definition of loyalty. At NC-17, Rollins, you will show your loyalty to GCW. I guarantee it.
