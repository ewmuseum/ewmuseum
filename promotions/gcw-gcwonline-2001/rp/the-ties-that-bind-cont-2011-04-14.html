The Ties That Bind (cont.)

14 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/14/11 Chapter III <><><><><> The sweeping wind of the Atlantic Ocean can be intoxicating when it hits you in the face. Like steam from a boiling pot, though not nearly as hot nor nearly as dangerous, the salty stink of the ocean gets in from the nostrils, through the blood capillaries in your nose, and soaks into bloodstream. Soon, you feel wet, salty, and heavy from the breeze that seems to have taken over your body, pushing and pulling you whichever direction it wants. Danny knows how these strolls go though and brought the proper gear. A thick jacket with a hard shell keeps him somehow free from the affects of the salty breeze tossing his hair about. His eyes hide behind a pair of thick sunglasses, different in make and model compared to the last time you saw him The lighting has changed, the days have too, and so has the number in his win column. Damnit, Lacy. I said stick behind, not settle down, rent a condo, and start a goddamn family. Where the fuck is this chick? I figure I'd at least hear from her by now, a text or somethin'. Having some cash to blow around this dump would be nice, though. "Got a light?" comes from somewhere behind him. He looks over his shoulder and spots a waitress standing under the overhang of a restaurant doorway. Danny grins, patting his jacket, and flashes a charming smile. She sees that he holds a Zippo in his hand and cracks an envious smile to match as he strolls over casually. "Nice lighter," she says, sticking her cigarette out into the flame. "Thanks." Danny watches on, mostly her lips, and holds the flame out in case she needs a second shot. When he can tell she’ll be fine, he pulls out a smoke of his own. When his is efficiently lit, he snaps the lighter shut with a metal CHINK, and stuffs it away in his jeans. "No sweat," he says pulling his jacket collar up. "It’s fuckin’ windy, eh?" She glances off into the distant waters and nods, "You can say that again. You should have seen it yesterday. Twice as hard, fifteen degrees colder, and they said it would be sixty-five out." "Rough," he says, exhaling into the wind and watching it zip away instantly. "I’m guessin’ you’re not from around here?" the waitress says promptly. Danny lifts his shades from his eyes to the top of his head, like a hairband, "How can ya tell?" "You weren’t here yesterday," she says as a smile grows over her face. They lock eyes for a second and break eye contact as both have a laugh. The first thing he notices is her nametag, Jane, and quickly glances at her hand. His hopes fade at the sight of a shiny diamond resting on her finger. Jane sees his reaction, glances to the source, and then hides her ring quickly. "This is just," she pulls it off, holding it up. "It’s so customers leave me alone. We get a lot of drunks so, you know, it’s rough sometimes." Danny takes the ring between his fingers and gives her a concerned look, "That’s kind of fucked up, kid." She shrugs, "Yeah, well, so is having hair like that." "This hair is prime time, babe," he runs his hand through his mane. "Don’t be jealous." "Yeah…" she waits until he looks her way and rolls her eyes. "So what brings you to town Mister…?" He extends his hand, "Danny." "Nice to meet you Danny," her voice infliction changes. "I’m Jane. What brings you to Baltimore, the scenery?" "Work," he flashes a humored smile, stepping on the butt of his smoke. "Though I can't believe how beautiful downtown gets once you get past the brown-brick buildings and hookers." "Where do you work?" Jane laughs, trying not to take offense on behalf of the city she calls home. "You look real familiar." "You’ve probably seen me on TV before," he grins. "I work for GCW." "The wrestling company?" she finishes her smoke by tossing it into the wind. "Really?" Really, sweetheart. Look at this face, do you think I flip burgers or sling dope from my Momma’s basement? "Yep," he says, fighting every urge to brag. "You're a fan?" Her eyes scan over his rippling muscles and rugged tattoos but manages to stop eyefucking him to shake her head no, "Wow. You anybody special?" The motherfuckin’ Dynasty bitch. The only anybody special. "I’m workin’ on that," he offers her a friendly smile. "I actually am fighting to be one of the guys who gets a bigger paycheck come the end of the month. Trust me, I’m not lettin’ up until I get that raise." They both laugh, her at the fact he plays the serious role so well, and him because she thinks he’s joking. The last time people thought he was fake was the last time he saw Big C's henchmen alive. Her laughter also stems from the fact that she can see him doing very well by the way he can dazzle her, a stranger, and revels in his aura. She shrugs, "So what do you have to do for that to happen?" Stomp out some rookie who never had a chance in the first place, that’s all. What we call in the biz, rape. No offense, Danny Cruz, but Brad is older, better looking, and gave me a run for my money. the truth is, now that I think about it, I'm undefeated against all 'Cruz' types and I aim to keep it that way. Danny shrugs and exhales yet again, "Win my next match." "Against who?" she asks, suddenly sounding like a wrestling fan or some bullshit. "Anybody important?" Well, why don't you just cut me off at the knee? What the fuck is that? Anybody important? I'm the important one! Danny smiles, remembering what people have said when they see his name opposite theirs' on the card lineup, "Just another rookie." I think its a cryin' shame. Throwin' this rook' to the wolves - to the WOLF - on his first day but I'm a firm believer in getting their feet wet. But soaked in blood? I guess if it's what the people want... it's what they'll get. Jane glances over her shoulder and motions toward the restaurant. Before she goes, she offers him a gentle smile, looking him up and down. Her smile grows as her eyes hit his feet and slowly make their way back to his. "Good luck then," Jane says with sincerity. "And maybe if the whole wrestling thing doesn’t work out… you can always look me up next time you're in town." Oh, it’s gonna work out, sweetheart. It’s gonna work out just fine. The wind smacks him in the face as he notices the affects of strong seaside gusts again. The conversation and her precious smile were enough to dull the wind and smell of the ocean. He feels a buzz in his pocket but ignores it. She's quick to flash an uninviting grin, "I'd be pretty upset to see you give up though." "Thanks Jane," he remembers her name the second he says it. "I needed that." She smiles even wider and dips back through the door before saying anything else worth saying. He watches her leave and the door closes fully with a boom. Away from the captivating waitress, Danny sends his hand for his pocket and pulls out his phone. His eyes light up as he flips it open and reads over the Caller ID. Finally. He clicks the send button, "Lacy. Whaddup girl?" "Danny, listen," Lacy says with a heavy tremble in her voice. "I don’t know where I am, I don’t know who they are, and whoever you sent me to ain’t who they sayin’-Ahhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh…" "Lacy…" Danny screams into the phone but stifles himself for another few seconds. "LACY!" What the fuck is going on? "…chk-chk-chk…" the sound of movement echoes from the other end. Danny waits a few more agonizing seconds, "HELLO?!?" He hears another click before nothing again, all leads to knowing whatever happened fade away with the shrill sound of a girl’s scream. He tries to steady his breath but can’t keep from emptying his chest with every exhale. He’s not sure why he’s holding the phone to his ear anymore. A few seconds of shock and his mind begins to twist and turn on itself. He suddenly thinks he hears something, hoping to hear anything, praying even – and God is no Danny Francisco fan – for her to give him something else. "…Your move, kid," a raspy voice whispers before killing the call. WHAT. THE. FUCK.
