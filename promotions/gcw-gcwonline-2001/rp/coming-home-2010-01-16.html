Coming Home

16 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/16/10 A subtly electronic chorus of voices wafted from the speakers as a laser blue hue pulsed across the screen. Flashes of lightning and quick shots of a whirling maelstrom of wind and rain illuminated the darkness of the screen accentuating the beats just as the words of the song came in. -Your construction… -Smells of corruption… -I’ll manipulate… -To recreate… -This air to ground saga… -Gotta launder… -My karma… The words echoed as the bass beat kicked in and the images flashed to that of a woman’s cobalt blue eyes flashing out of the darkness and a tumble of jet black hair, her face lit by flashes of light and a smile that bore a light all its own. These interspersed with the lightning and the storm and then… -I said hallelujah to the sixteen loyal fans A white hot light flashed from the screen illuminating the woman’s figure from behind, her arms upraised. She stepped forward in a sparkling blue bodysuit, the light reflecting off of her form in a sparkling cascade. -You’re gettin’ down on your muthafuckin' knees -And it's time for your sickness again Behind her a beam of blue laser focused light pierced the darkness just as her gaze focused front and center on the screen with a bemused smile. -Come on and tell me what you need now -Tell me what is making you bleed A scene from the past, present, maybe even the future, of the tiny Tempest in flight, seemingly crashing onto the viewer, the lights went out. -We got two more minutes and -We gonna cut to what you need She laughed. She beckoned. The screen shimmered and she skipped along while the lightning flashed and the wind whirled, but still you follow. -I'm blown to the maxim -Two hemispheres battlin' -I'm blown to the maxim -Two hemispheres battlin' -Suckin' up, one last breath -Take a drag off of death A quick flash of a long ago scene, in a fight league far far away… the man seen only from the back didn’t bend that way, but Tempest looked perfectly comfortable and her grin was one of pure glee. -Now tell me do you agree -Or tell me if I'm makin’ you bleed -I got a few more minutes and -I'm gonna cut to what you need Her hips swayed in time with the music, now in a bright red beaded hip scarf with a wine dark top and tights. She coiled her arms upward and around in a motion that seemed to invite the lightning that flashed now and again. -So one of six so tell me -One do you want to live -And one of seven tell me -Is it time for your muthafuckin' ass to give -Tell me is it time to get down on your muthafuckin' knees -Tell me is it time to get down Through the verse her arms accentuated the words and rapid fire flashes of light in time with the beat illuminated a shadowy figure behind her that seemed to have ten arms that followed her motions. Or was it the other way around? -Got a revolution behind my eyes -We got to get up and organize -Got a revolution behind my eyes -We got to get up and organize -Got a revolution behind my eyes -We got to get up and organize -You want a revolution behind your eyes -We got to get up and organize Her dance gradually shifted into a more purposeful march that interspersed with flashes of wrestling moves hit on unfortunates past and the occasional prank; ferrets leaping for the hapless James von Doom, a nearly lost clip of Meanstreak taking a swipe at Tempest dangling from a bungee cord while filming him with a camcorder. -Come on baby tell me -Yes we aim to please She winked and the lighting flashed and faded out gradually glittering its way across the screen leaving only the name: Tempest. ~ Darren Stearns stared at the screen until it went snowy before he could tear his eyes away. Then he turned to his one and only client as she sat next to him in her fuzzy black warm-ups with kitty ears on the hood and said, “Now that is a video entrance fit for a queen. Caldera’s people really do a class job with this stuff.” Tempest nodded and played with the pom poms on her hood strings, smiling happily as she said, “Yup! They didn’t even try to herd me too badly. They seemed to have this whole Goddess idea thing going, but I was like… ‘it’s Cyberpunk Goddess, you know’ It has to be punky and wild and fierce and… well, you get it.” Stearns nodded and clicked off the flat screen that sat atop the glass table inside the warehouse loft and nodded saying, “I do and they obviously did as well by the time you were done. Has Troy seen this?” Tempest sighed and stood up from the long red sofa with its dizzying array of multicolored cushions and pillows, a hodge-podge that had obviously been gathered from all over with no intention of matching or theme. “Not yet. I’m not sure he’s even watched Chaos yet,” she said quietly. “I’m not sure exactly when to offer and when to leave it alone, ya know? Sometimes it seems like I’m pushing my luck and other times, he’s ok with it.” “Well, he’s gonna love that,” Darren said, pointing emphatically at the screen. “Who wouldn’t? And how can he not watch the endless hilarity that you committed at Chaos? You were Trouble personified.” “No no, Trouble’s in the other room,” Tempest said, looking confused. As if on cue a chubby border collie trotted into the room and looked around expectantly. Tempest laughed walked over, patting him and scratching behind his ears as Darren said, “Ah yes, some things never change. Still the same old Trouble I see.” “Well, more old than the same, I’m afraid,” said Tempest with a wan smile. Trouble whined plaintively and she said, “Oh don’t take it like that, you know you’re old. And you’re grumpy too. But I still love you.” She hugged the dog, who had been with her for over ten years, appearing by her side as well. Dog years had clearly taken their toll in the graying fur around his eyes and on his face, and in his slow gait. “It’s ok, you now get to enjoy the comforts of foot warming, food and love while my new partners in crime do the hard part. Oh!” She turned to Darren excitedly and grinned as she sad, “Did I tell you? They’re finally letting me bring them home from the trainers today!” ~ Troy Rivers had seen a lot. He would never say he’d seen it all, but he’d been around the block a few times. He didn’t like to think of himself as sheltered or easily shocked. He thought he was prepared to deal with life’s little surprises. Certainly those skills had been put to the test of late. He admitted readily that he had not been prepared to learn that his girlfriend of two years, Rachel Tyrell, was in fact a retired professional wrestler named Tempest. Nor was he prepared for all that entailed as she decided to return to the ring and attempted to do it without telling him. Shocked? Yes. Angry? To a degree. Mostly disappointed. Thrown for a loop by the sudden shove into a world he never even knew existed? Most definitely. So while walking into the renovated warehouse loft they shared to find what he found might have snapped the mind of a normal man, Troy was at least very slightly more prepared. ~ “No no, like this guys!” Tempest said, standing in a pair of dark jeans and a bright pink tie dyed hoodie. Her hair was uncharacteristically tied up in a shiny pink beaded band. She pumped her fists forward at shoulder level to a beat that only she could hear, then turned to the right, raising her arms into a right angled Egyptian walk pose and took a couple of exaggerated steps, then turned and reversed her arms, and walked the other way a few steps. She stopped at the point she started and raised her right fist as she thrust her hips to the left and then did the opposite a few times rhythmically. Atop a wide wooden table, two ferrets, one dark with white facial markings and one white, stood watching attentively. “OK, now you try it with me,” she said grinning. “We get this first part down and you get Reese’s pieces, ok?” The ferrets’ heads bobbed enthusiastically and they stood at attention, raising their paws to duplicate the way Tempest was holding her fists to do the routine again. She pumped her fists. “Do do dun… do do dun…” Into the Egyptian walk, the ferrets raised their little arms and bobbed their heads forward and back with the steps. They all turned in unison and back at the starting point swung their hips, hers sleek and agile, theirs furry and round. “Uh uh uh So!... Excellent!” *Squeak* The ferrets leaned to either side and peered around Tempest’s body at Troy Rivers who had just walked in on this scene and peered back at them just as wide -eyed. Tempest turned and smiled happily, leaping once lightly and clapping her hands. “Troy!” she squealed delightedly, “Oh you’re just in time to meet my new recruits. They finally let me bring them home today. This is Emil…” She stepped to one side and motioned to the white ferret, who stepped forward and bowed before sitting back on his haunches and regarding the dark haired man curiously. “And this is Fernando…” she said. She motioned toward the darker ferret and he too stepped forward and bowed before sitting back and looking up at the new human. Troy looked between the two and then to Tempest with an expression of utter dumbfoundedness. He started to speak and then stopped, his brow furrowing just a bit. Then he pointed to the ferrets and started only to stop again. “Rachel, I’m not even sure where to start asking ‘What the Hell?’ here…” Tempest giggled, her cobalt blue eyes sparkling. “Yeah well, I suppose that’s justified,” she said. “I told you you should have watched Christmas Chaos!” Troy laid down a fairly large, heavy duty canvas bag that landed with the sound of hefty tools moving about and stepped closer as he said, “I did. I just didn’t think you’d be bringing work home with you so to speak. And for the record I liked it. You didn’t hurt anyone… much.” He looked down at the smaller woman who seemed so unassuming next to him and smirked. Tempest looked up at him innocently and then back to the ferrets for backup, but they were distracted by the new human and peered up him with great curiosity. “I thought I behaved rather well and these little guys got the first of many workouts,” she said, turning on her heel and retrieving a few of the promised Reese’s pieces from a Hello Kitty jar on a nearby counter. “They only let me have them after they had completed the entire course of their stunt training and on the condition that I continue training them. They’re super smart and very inquisitive. But their little brains are like muscles; they atrophy if you don’t work them. So I have to give them things to do and keep them stimulated.” She grinned as she tossed them the candies and each of the ferrets played catch with them a few times before sitting down and going to work on them. “Stunt ferrets?” Troy asked, his brows arching in an expression of doubt. “You went out and found trained, super smart, stunt ferrets?” “Well sure,” Tempest said. “Why not?” Troy laughed softly and shook his head. “Yeah, why not? I guess you have a point there. Normalcy is not what I’ve come to expect from you, even before I knew about the wrestling thing. In fact ‘wacky’ is one of your more endearing qualities, even if it does mean I find an ostrich feather fan in my toolbox every now and then.” He smiled and stepped closer, wrapping a black and white tattooed arm around her and pulled her into an embrace. “Listen, you’ve been watching the news. You know what’s happened, right?” he said softly. “Hmm?” Tempest tilted her head and looked up at him with some confusion. “A lot of things, what do you mean in particular?” “I mean Haiti,” he said, “I’m going to Haiti.” Tempest’s face fell and her shoulders slumped and she said, “But… but now… there’s so much going on. I’m just starting at GCW and the theater renovation is almost done and the opening is soon and…” “And an earthquake nearly wiped out Haiti, a lot of people are hurt and endangered by buildings and structures that are dangerous and I can do something about that,” he said, looking Tempest in the eye. “Habitat International’s trying to gather up some people with the skills to go with the heavy equipment they’re finally rolling in there and you know damn well that’s me. I have to go.” Tempest stared up into the dark green eyes of the man whose love used to be such a simple matter. Slowly, but surely she summoned a smile and said, “Of course you do.” She reached up and wrapped her arms around his shoulders, pulling him closer. “Of course you have to go. It’s ok. I’ll miss you terribly, but it’s ok. I love you… precisely because you have to go.” She bit her lip and squeezed Troy tightly. “Hey don’t look like that. I’m coming back, baby,” Troy said and kissed her on top of the head lightly. “Just… well, I’d say keep it real, but in your case, it’s more like ‘keep it completely off the wall.’ And it’s clear that even if I don’t understand the fighting, I do understand that the world does need a good laugh. And you’re good for that... Tempest.” It’s the first time he’s spoken that name while knowing what it means, the first time he’s acknowledged that it is who I am, just as much as Rachel, the only ‘me’ he has known until very recently. I’m so proud of him for being who and what he is, and that it is nothing at all like me or what I am. I just have to hope we can survive being nothing alike, since I didn’t give him the chance to decide at first. I have to hope that avoiding wrestlers and the clash of egos that inevitably comes with them was the right choice. I have to hope that for once, I was right about something. It will be a first. ~ Epilogue: “Look are you sure about this? It could be dangerous,”said Frank Valleta, as his dark eyes darted back and forth from the tools he was helping to load to Troy Rivers and back. “Says the man who jumps off high places and lands on people for fun and profit,” Troy laughed and heaved another box into the back of Frank’s truck. “Nah, I don’t that high flyin’ shit. That’s for girls!” said Frank, trying to sound offended and failing as an enthused smile played across his face. “Of course your girl’s an artist at it. I mostly just pick guys up and throw them around until they stop moving.” “Uh huh,” said Troy shaking his head. “And she asks me why I’m not into wrestling. “Aw c’mon, you know I’m not that bad,” said Frank in his best overgrown kid tone. “Anyway, just look out for yourself down there. I know you want to help, but don’t become a casualty yourself. And I’ll look after Tempest while you’re gone.” Troy tilted his head with a curious expression and a lock of dark hair that had fought its way free of his ponytail fell across his eyes. Frank caught the look and stammered, “Oh! No no no, I didn’t mean it like that. I meant I’d look in on her and see if she was ok, that’s all! Just…” Troy laughed and waved him off. “It’s ok man, I know what you meant,” he said. “Don’t worry about it. You’ve been trying to get your foot out of your mouth ever since you accidently outted her as being a wrestler, but really, it’s not your fault. And besides…” he took a step closer and clapped the much larger man on the shoulder. “You’re a pup, not her type.” “Oh, is that right?” Frank said, suddenly pretending to be offended. “Yeah, you heard me,” Troy laughed, “Cut a… what is it? A promo?... on that!” Frank laughed and said, “Yeah ok, I got ya. Nah, you’re right, I just admire her cuz I used to watch her on TV when I was in high school. And yeah she is a hero to me; a sparkly little pixie dust hero, but a hero still. And I will look after her… in a totally non-creepy, I’m-after-your-girl kind of way.” He raised his right and Troy nodded with satisfaction. “You do that,” he said. “I got a feeling she’s going to need a friend or two.”
