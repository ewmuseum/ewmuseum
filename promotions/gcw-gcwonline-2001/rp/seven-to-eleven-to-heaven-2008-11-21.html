Seven to Eleven...to Heaven?

21 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/21/2008 Ding-a-ling... The door to the seven-eleven opens up, letting in a warm breeze from the dark. Face draws the attention of the clerk immediately, his mask and crude attire sticking out like a giant ogre in a crowd of rabbits. The old Asian man that is at every other seven-eleven in the country reaches for his trusty red button under the counter, but Face raises his right hand in defence, his left currently twirling a silver lighter. “There’s...no need for that,” Face says with a raspy voice that doesn’t exactly help the situation. Then again, he’s dressed in completely mismatched suit pieces that aren’t really done up right and are full of holes, as well as a mask that could make Jason shit his pants. His voice could be like a choir singer’s and it wouldn’t really make an argument for him being any less of a freak in appearance. Needless to say, Face’s words aren’t the most comforting or reassuring and the clerk remains very wary of his latest customer, keeping his hand within a moment’s reach of calling the police at all times. A small, nearly inaudible chuckle can be heard from behind Face’s mask. He shakes his head and walks down the middle aisle. The clerk’s stance finally loosens a bit as he questions Face. “Why you dress up like that” You think you some kinda rebel, dressing up like tough guy?” Face continues to walk down the aisle, checking out the stock. “No...no, I’m no rebel; at least, rebelling is not my general purpose, nor is it the purpose of my attire.” Face finally stops near the back of the store, beside a stand full of insect repellent. “Then why you dress up like a freak?” Without looking away from the various brands of bug spray before him, Face quickly answers the clerk’s question in a way that is all too typical with the masked man: with another question. “Tell me...what was your first reaction to seeing me walk into your store?” After a few moments of though, the clerk replies, “Fear.” “Fear,” Face echoes. Ding-a-ling... “GIVE ME THE FUCKING MONEY NOW!” The clerk is caught completely off-guard, having been thoroughly distracted by Face. A black pistol is aimed directly at his head, making a lunge for the button underneath the counter a lunge into insanity. The ski-mask clad robber doesn’t even notice Face standing on the other side of the store as he once again barks out his orders. “Get to it already old man! I don’t got all night!” “No, you don’t.” The robber immediately points his gun in Face’s direction after Face alerts him to his presence, having now turned around to face the clerk and the robber. A look of confusion is plastered across the robber’s eyes as he takes in the masked man from across the room. “Hate to break it to you kid,” says the robber, letting out a short laugh before continuing, “but Halloween was a couple weeks ago.” Face scratches his chin with his free hand, “Ah. That would explain why no one seemed to have candy when I knocked on their doors...” The response doesn’t tickle the robber’s fancy in the slightest and he quickly adopts a more serious tone. “Look, kid. I don’t have time for your bullshit sarcasm. You just stay right there and shut up.” Turning back to the clerk, the robber adds, “And you better start putting cash in a bag before I blow you’re fucking head off!” The clerk reacts quickly, not wanting to tempt the man with the gun any further. The cash register opens with a “ching” and the clerk quickly begins dumping money into a grocery bag he grabbed from his collection under the counter. The robber now has the gun back on the clerk as he watched the money flow with anticipation. However, Face soon cuts into that anticipation. “So, were you beaten as a child, or did you just grow up in a bad neighbourhood?” “What did you just say to me?” The robber turns away from the clerk, pointing the gun back across the store at Face. “You wanna come over here and ask that question again?” Face grabs a can of bug spray and begins to slowly stroll over to the robber as if he was some four year old kid aiming a water pistol at him. He flicks his lighter on and off every few moments as he closes in on the robber, who is absolutely dumbfounded that Face is walking towards him. “Are you really looking for an early burial that bad, kid!”” the robber questions, now turning his full attention towards Face. However, Face just continues to walk methodically down the aisle towards the assailant, flicking his lighter on and off like clockwork and twirling the can of bug spray in his hand. Both the clerk and the robber are beside themselves as Face walks right up to the robber, stopping just a couple feet away, and repeats his question from earlier. “So, were you beaten as a child, or did you just grow up in a bad neighbourhood?” The robber suddenly shoves his gun in Face’s forehead, the barrel pressed right up against the mask. “You know, kid,” the robber says with a visible anger about him, “you’re really starting to PISS ME OFF! If I were you, I’d back the fuck away before I put a few bullets into that smartass brain of yours!” Face turns his head downwards and to the side, as if contemplating something. He then turns back towards the robber, still flicking his lighter on and off and spinning the can of bug spray. “Go ahead. Shoot me.” The clerk and the robber both drop their jaws for a second. After a moment, the robber begins to laugh. The laughing escalates until he’s holding his sides with his free hand. “You, you have got to be fucking kidding me! I walk into a store in the middle of nowhere and find some wannabe super man dressed up like some sort of masked hobo!” The robber’s laughing subsides and he just shakes his head and turns back to the clerk, who is still in shock. “Hey, finish it up already, will ya?” “What” You don’t have the balls to shoot me?” The robber just lowers his head, rubbing his forehead with his free hand. He turns back to Face and steps right up to him, a clear height and weight advantage being shown. When the robber looks up from his hand, his expression is one of annoyance and anger. He brings the black pistol right up to Face’s ear, clutching Face’s neck with his other hand. “Are you THIS stupid, kid!” I swear...you say one more thing and you’ll be done!” Much to the surprise of both the robber and the clerk, a faint sound of laughter can be heard from underneath the mask of Face. The laughter begins to get louder and louder, until it breaks out into a full-out, harsh, raspy cackle. The robber can’t believe his eyes as Face laughs, the barrel of a pistol pressed firmly against the side of his head. Once again, the robber just shakes his head, this time in complete disbelief. “Why the fuck are you laughing!” Do I look funny to you!” Do you think I’m fucking joking when I say I’m going to blow your damn brains out!”” The laughing continues, but begins to subside as Face brings up the lighter and the can of bug spray. His tone then becomes eerily serious. “You can’t do it, can you” You act arrogant with that gun of yours, but you don’t have the killer instinct to pull the trigger.” The robber chuckles, but Face cuts him off before he can say anything. “I’ll give you three seconds to shoot me. If you don’t, I’ll burn your face off.” The clerk and the robber are completely dumbfounded and stuck in perpetual awe. Nonetheless, Face starts his countdown. “Three...” The robber back up and points the gun back in Face’s forehead. “You’re an idiot, kid!” “Two...” The robber can’t seem to believe his eyes as he begins to squeeze at the trigger, pressing the gun firmly into Face’s mask. “You really wanna die, don’t you kid!”” “One...” Face tilts his head to the side, waiting for the robber to take the shot. However, the robber just shakes his head and backs off. “You know what, I’m not going to waste a bullet on your pathet-? Face cuts the robber off mid-sentence as he flicks his lighter on, brings the bug spray up to it and extends his arms at the robber, pressing down on the nozzle of the can. A huge fireball erupts forth from the lighter’s flame as it mixes in with the highly flammable gas. The robber’s entire head is instantly engulfed in flames and he drops his gun, shrieking horribly in pain! Face let’s up on the nozzle as the man frantically drops to the ground trying to pat his head out. The clerk is completely frozen as he watches on in horror as the flames spread to the arms and torso of the robber. Face watches on with the same fiery grin he always. The robber now begins to roll around to try and get the flames out, much to the amusement of Face, who tilts his head in curiosity. “Need some help there?” Face asks, quite obviously not with sincere care. The robber responds with more frantic screams as the flames, having easily burnt through his ski mask and shirt, are now burning away at his flesh. “What’s that” You say you want some more? Are you sure?” More yelps and screams as the flames begin to travel down towards the legs of the robber. “Fair enough!” With that, Face flicks his lighter back on and once again uses it and the bug spray as a flamethrower, raining fire down all over the robber’s body! This time, he lets the flames continue to pour out until the robber is visibly immobile. He then lets up and turns towards the clerk, casually stepping away from the burning corpse behind him. “Fear is the most powerful weapon known to mankind. If you know how to use it properly, you can do anything to anyone. But...if you can also see through it...No one can do anything to you.” The clerk doesn’t even respond. Face quickly reaches into a pocket in his pants and pulls out a pill. Before the clerk can even move, Face shoves the pill into the clerk’s mouth and makes him swallow it. The clerk coughs and gasps for air, soon dropping to a knee. His eyes then begin to flutter and roll upwards as he drops to the floor, entering into a deep sleep. Face then turns his attention to a video camera in the corner behind the clerk’s desk and quickly hops onto the counter to reach it. The only wire leading into it is that of the plug heading into a nearby socket, suggesting the tape is contained within the camera itself. Face confirms his suspicion when he pops open the camera and retrieves a tape. He places the tape in his pocket and then hops off the counter, taking one last look at the now smouldering man as he exits the store. Ding-a-ling...
