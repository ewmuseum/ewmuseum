I never pictured me losing that battle royal at Warpath. Its more of a down presser than a reality check. I'm just motionless and not focused on my next task which is climbing back up from a horrible lose. So close but so far away from gold. I guess dreaming is a perfect way to get your mind set straight and not wonder off into the darkness. For some odd reason i keep hearing a knock echoing throughout my hotel room. Actually its more of a thud sort of what you hear in a horror movie. But this isn't a horror movie its reality and in reality they're no Fred Kruger and Chuckys running around the world. So i guess that must be the door, earlier today i lost most of my endorsements so i wouldn't be surprised if they forgot to take more away from me. I rather not answer the door at all and save myself from yet another lost. ?Answer the door!? Lying down without a worry in hand and then the voice came crashing through the door at least twice. I take a deep breath and proceed towards the door. But i had to stop to take a piss which lasted about 5 minutes. With the same sad emotion i had before, i now answer the door and welcome a stranger I've never met before. ?Yeah...your going to let me in Tex?? Something told me not to let this guy in but i just had to do it. The only thing running through my mind would be the repo squad coming to take whatever i have left. ?You look pretty down kid, but thats why I'm here.? the man said with a southern accent that just made me want to run out and call for help. So what you here for? I said to him and he looked at his watch. ?I don't have that much time to be citcher chattering with you. I'm here for business and business only. You dig?? I nod my head agreeing with him. I extended my hand and he pulled out a small bag of sunflower seeds instead of a handshake. ?You only get that respect after we win our first match? i look at him as if he lost his marbles. What you mean we? I said to him. ?All I'm saying is i can rank you up there with the other beginnings.? This fool must not know about me. ?Right now going through your little knuckle head would be that you are the best in everything.? This fool must have read my book simply porter... ?Look Shawn, My name is Paul McCullen and if there's one thing i know it would be wrestling. I've wrestle pigs, bears, hell I've wrestled my third wife but thats not stopping me from making you into a star.? I look at him to see if he was bluffing but he shown no emotion and i kinda got nervous. Tell me more..i ask him with a calm manner. He laughed at me for a second. Then started talking. ?See Kids like you are very spoiled and by the way you look you, i can see you had a rough life and now you are living large and don't know if your bad or good. Kid your problem is that you think you need a gimmick to get you by in the business. Nonsense you just be yourself even if that means being a jerk to some people but the thing is people will respect you for you. Not this hip hop mojo going around the building. You believe in yourself and things will go right and correctly.? this was for the first time ever that someone has actually told me off in the most calmest manner. I felt like i owe this guy something but i know i don't. He extended his fist i guess thats better than nothing. I quickly took his offer. ?Now i got this here ranch outside off El Paso we can visit during the week for some tough training.? I smiled and agreed with him. I told him i knew this nice little restaurant called Good Tucker. He told me he drove by one called Cafe Badra and he wants to go there instead. I grab my jacket and we head out of the door. Paul isn't some small fella. He looks as if he weights about 300 pounds. He got this raggedy beard and long black hair. We two look like men from totally different planets. Me this cocky kid from Philly and Paul McCullen country boy from El Paso, Texas. I guess my career will get a shocking ending. ?A flatfoot come here.? I know he didn't just call me out of my name but i guess he did. Paul did you call me flat foot? He replied with a simple yes. ?Flatfoot means you run flatfooted nothing racial or nothing disrespectful. Just pick your feet up and I'll call you Shawny.? Well that sounds way better in the public than Flatfoot, I'm not an Indian or anything like that. We both hop in the Ford Ranger and head towards the highway. I had time to think and dream about becoming a champion. I'm hoping to get another chance at doing so. I know i have the skill to do it, its just my mouth gets me into shit. ?Uh Shawny we take Springvale, right?? I nod my head telling him yes. About 15 minutes passed and we reached the restaurant. We get in and grab a seat. I looks straight at Paul McCullen to see what he has to say next. He usually got some word of wisdom. I'm help to see that someone thinks i can climb the ranks in a fast pace. Paul looks like he has a bad habit with sunflower seeds, he done ran through two bags. ?So Shawny, whats you weak points in a match?? ?I don't know i guess the part when i hear the bell ring and i hear the opponents name as the winner.? He started to look at me really angry like. I knew the clowning around had to stop. I reached for my drink and took a sip of it. I sort of cleared my throat getting ready to talk once more. ?Um Paul, Were you at Warpath?? ?Yeah, i had season tickets, so any venue GCW appears at for Warpath it would be free for me. I was excited to see the new faces of GCW. But you stood out but didn't snatch the victory at all. I was very disappointed to see Tyson Debough win. He's good but not that good. I believe you have what it takes to take it all. But at one step at a time. Your real problem is that your an egomaniac. You need to calm down sometimes.? ?Yeah i guess..? ?I seen your promo with Terror. You bragged to the world that you were going to win the battle royal and then defeat him for the belt but guess what hot shot. You got nothing out of the deal.? ?Yeah actions speak louder than words.? ?You'll learn kid...you'll learn.?
