The truth behind the camera

20 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/20/05 '... Well when Lex first come to me with the offer, I was like, 'I dunno dawg'. See cuz me an Havoc had our own things in the work, ya know what I mean. I'm sayin we had t-shirts, market plans, a camera gimmick, we was ready to go and at the time, we were ready to sign into WWE. I'm not even lying, but back then, ya know, WWE was eating up other feds, an like, I gotta call from them saying that they din't need us anymore. Me an Havoc, that is, so like I called up Lex and said: 'Yo man, we ready to hear to what you're offering. And that's when I got on the ride.' 'All That Glitters is not Gold' 'So like, when Lexicon was pitchin his lines and all that he made it sound real pretty. Gift wit words you can call it, Anarch Agencies came with alot of dirty benefits. Now I ain't gonna mention any names, but they was a lot of big players involved, ya know what I mean. An, like, I ain't gonna say I wasn't down wit all that, right, I'm from Compton ma'fucka, but there was alotta money, alotta drugs, and alotta women. Back then, I was new to the game an all that, I din't even know what I was getting myself into. Lexicon kept tellin me he was gonna get me the big shot, me an Havoc both right. So most of the time we was just livin the life, ya know, for like two months me and Havoc was partying on Anarch's tab. An... After that I guess Lexicon figured it was time to collect on what we owed...' 'See while we was waiting, Lexicon and Lestatt was actually the ones founded the company. Now I din't know Lestatt, he din't even meet with us when we signed on, and it was only a few days before we got called into 3W that I got to meet Trey and Jordan. Trey was cool back then, kinda guy I could chill wit'. Lexicon passed up offers on a bunch of federations, and different promotions, we wanted to apply at UWF and PRIME, Lanier at Core even mentioned he was interested in our tag team. But Lex was determined to get us into 3WA, I mean, I seriously think it's because he had his contract tied up. He told me once he wanted to leave, but Victory Dragoon was at the time in charge and wouldn't let him out of his contract, so I figure he just decided if he couldn't leave, he'd wage war.' 'When Lexicon told us we were signed to 3WA, we were kinda mixed about it. The promotion was made of big money, it was big production, but it was independant. See, me and Havoc wanted to go inter-fed, but Lexicon said this was where we belonged. So we went with it, an... I din't know until that night, I think Trey and Jordan knew, but I din't know we was turnin on Lestatt. Well if you watched the show at all, you'd have seen it coming. The two were at each others throats, and it wasn't even a camera angle, they really didn't like each other. It was all Lexicon's money, and in the end he said he could do it without Lestatt. He turned on him, and I knew it wasn't gonna be the end of it, Lestatt isn't like many other people. He doesn't let a sleeping dog lie.' 'I hung out wit Jordan and Trey a couple of times, back then we was all kinda into the drugs. Lexicon practically pushed the pain killers and speed on us, by our third match I din't know what to do without em. But Jordan was really heavy into this shit, he'd dissapear and miss a show every coupla months, and nobody said anything but we all knew he was getting Trey into the habit. They got the tag belts, an me and Havoc got to do some of our tag stuff ya know, but, like, we wasn't able to actually tag ya know, cuz Lexicon didn't want any competition for Trey and Jordan. I mean they was good and all, but there was alot of dirty booking going on. Jordan getting booked into these insane matches, two, three matches in a night once. And, like, I don't know anybody can go three matches in one night without loading themselves up on drugs.' 'It was actually Lestatt that got me and Havoc one night, we was backstage and we din't get no word that there was an angle that night or nothing. And like, he got us both and we had a match that night, Lexicon was pretty mad about that on account of all the money he had bet on us... That was when the shit started to escalate right there, an... Me and Havoc was hurtin and we wasn't able to pull that win. I think it was Wylde and Raziel, that night, and Lexicon been tellin us all week we had to be on top shape. It was coupla weeks after that an Lexicon made Lestatt tap... I pause because those of you who don't know the business don't understand the levity of that statement. Lexicon made Lestatt submitte and he made himself a million easy. We were signing on for Pepsi commercials and magazine covers, turns out Lestatt had never tapped before. And you look with me, he's feuded and won angles with Alex Grey, Jeff Wylde, Jason Dread, Spitfire Stevens and none of these guys to this day have been able to make that man smack the mat.' 'Lestatt beat Lexicon 'bout a month later, but it was a long month. There was no angle anymore, I remember for weeks the camera crew was everywhere, they honestly had to hunt down the segments that ran the show. And that was the magic of that place, these guys didn't care about the drama and stories and doing the pose and lines. It was a dirty industry and there was a bunch of money, I'm saying a bunch of money going around. These guys would batter each other senseless. By the time I was dropped Lexicon was boasting a nine man count of careers he'd ended. He was ruthless, and I can say that because I was right there wit him for the highlights of his career. There was nuthin he wouldn't do to put the peices together the way he wanted, and for us, I mean, it was just easier to let him lead. He wasn't the worst leader, he ain't the best the arrogant bastard, but most of the time we was all on the winning side.' 'When Casey Torpid came back, man, he was on some freaky shit. I mean, Trey went missing for weeks, right, and everything was starting to buckle. Lexicon was focusing more on his own career and less on the company. So you know, without that leader in that role doing his job, half of us wasn't gettin booked, our feuds was moving on, to the world, ya know, it seemed like we was all starting to fall apart. Lexicon and Lestatt backed off. Behind the scenes they was basically told, look you two are one and one, you've stolen two back to back pay per views with a mid card feud that over shadows the world title. And they were told to wait, Lisa Stevens I think her name was, she took over when the bad money in that company turned up on the books. And everything was starting to look real bad an all that was when Trey and Casey showed back up and we kinda got a revival.' 'Trey was all messed up, I mean I know the dude for a few months already and he was messed up. His hair was cut up and his face scarred, the guy stopped fighting like a wrestler and more like a pit bull ya know? He was told a coupla times that he had to slow down a bit, cuz he was hurtin guys in the ring. But him and Casey took the tag belts like they was easy, ya know, and me and Havoc was just backin up the boss now. Jordan held that United States title in three dubya for like, pshh, ages man. And when it came time for him to get his shot at Zodiac for that world strap, man, he was pressured into giving it up. Lexicon threw everything he had on Zodiac, and like the good second in command, Jordan gave her the win. See this isn't fact or document or anything, this is just the truth the only way a God fearin brotha can tell it. And Lexicon wanted Zodiac for that world title, and Jordan almost got in his way and thank heaven he didn't, because that would have been the end of everything there.' 'I was dropped from the roster about two weeks before Havoc was paralyzed. Jordan, being chased by police and not cleared to wrestle was brought back by Lexicon as The Marauder. About two months after that Zodiac quit, in the ring, in the middle of her world title defense against Lexicon. Everybody lost big money on that match, and it was probably the main reason why Lisa pulled her hand out of the jar and left us high and dry. See this messed with alot of people, Lexicon especially, who had all of our contracts tied up in a company that wasn't bankrupt, it wasn't foreclosed, it just wasn't producing anymore. The equipment was hauled away and put into storage. So Lexicon picked the company up, his first big feud partner, Draven, threw a large chunk of cash into the project. Jeff Wylde sponsored the federation, we ran some closed production matches and it was looking good.' 'Lexicon was setting up television contracts with WB when Spike tv offered to run our show. I mean, we were doing this as a team, Zodiac came back, Casey, Trey, and even Jordan. We were recruitin', we set up marketing schemes, started plotting camera feuds and angles, setting up tournaments and title divisons. So Lexicon comes back and he tells us WB is gonna run the show if we can produce four pretaped episodes. So Lexicon says to them that Spike is willing to run it right away, pretaped the night before, an we get to do arena venues. Lexicon wanted WB, cuz at the time like Spike din't have no WWE, TNA, or UFC. So we came up with the plan to run six warehous venues and we set up a tournament. We ran these shows, we let in an audience and I mean the people were loving it. Lexicon like disappeared around the third taping and nobody could find the guy. Our connection in WB din't wanna deal with anybody but Lex, so we called up Spike and said: 'Yo can we still get that deal' and they was like, not a chance.' 'Lestatt was probably the biggest reason Lexicon went with GCW in the first place. It was because of Lexicon that Casey Torpid found his way into the promotion, and it was because of Casey, or rather his termination of contract, that brought Trey and Jordan White into the fed. And it was because of connections with the ever connected Jordan that Eva and Erik were signed to Anarch Agencies. So there they were, the most under estimated and over looked happy little family that ever could be. The stage was set, Lexicon predictably pulled the stringes he'd attached back with Anarch Agencies. Only this time, as messed up as the guy is he's a genius, Jordan White had other plans. This is where my details get sketched.' 'Eva signs Lexicon into a contract match with Trey, if Trey wins he gets out of his obligations to Anarch Agencies. If Lexicon wins, he doesn't. Ya know? An, like, it was Eva who prepared the contract and added a clause Lexicon didn't read. An like Lexicon was good at hiding his addiction, but, Eva found out and switched his dose. Lexicon went into a hardcore title match without his drugs and he lost, he lost everything to Jordan White. The man disappeared in Mexico. Eva and her brother take the tag gold easy, Trey trades his hardcore belt for the US title, an like, Anarch Agencies is making money again. I was wrestling HATE for awhile, and Lexicon shows up again in this mask calling himself The Marauder.' 'Everything I knew about Lexicon was different with that mask on, he acts like a different person. It's not a good or bad thing, really, cuz like he used to win and when was the last time you remember The Marauder winning a match? I mean, I spent some time wit him an he's livin in a shit hole, takin buses. I mean everything he worked two years to establish, he's let move on without him. But he's a better person on the inside. Eva, tag team champ, US Champ, GCW's top and foremost diva. Trey Tremendous, Hardcore champ, US Champ, the man to end Lestatt's US title reign. And Jordan sitting at the helm, playing the dirty books and spending the companies cash. Like I said earlier, Lexicon was an arrogant prick, but he knew how to run a business.' 'Lexicon said from day one, to all of us, he said: 'Anarch Agencies is going to get us all what we deserve.'. And I believe him. Through all the crazy highs, the money, women, and drugs. And the broken lows, the defeat, the injuries, and the come downs. It goes to show you that God has a plan for all of us. Jordan always played second fiddle to Lexicon, and in the end he was the one to finaly take what nobody else could. Trey, as messed up as the sport made him, he got his and now maybe he can spend some time to put his head back together. An Lexicon. Hopefully Lexicon finds that redemption he's been looking for. God bless ya man, it's been a crazy couple of years, we've all been through alot, and maybe now you'll realize all that glitters is not gold.' - Lupus Feral
