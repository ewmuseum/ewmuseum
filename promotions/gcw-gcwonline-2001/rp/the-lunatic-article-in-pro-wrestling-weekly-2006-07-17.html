The Lunatic :Article in Pro Wrestling Weekly

17 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-17-06 He’s vile, he’s sinister, and he’s completely insane. Upon reading the opening sentence, one might come to the conclusion that this article is about Charles Manson, or Osama bin Laden. Yes those are two are all of the above, but the description is not them. What if this was to help you. 7 Feet Tall, 300lbs, and the creator of the Belmont House of Torture Match? Would that clear it up? Yes, I’m talking about none other than “The Lunatic” Bryan Mayhem (O’Neil). Bryan has destroyed the lives of many up and coming professional athletes looking to get into this difficult and painstaking business known as professional wrestling. Four, yes I said it, four superstars careers have come to an end because of this man. For those of you that still think wrestling is fake, here’s something to prove you wrong. 2005. Jake Summers: Career ended due to massive head trauma and internal bleeding suffered from 5 Oblivion Bombs at the hands of Bryan Mayhem. 2002. Reed Standish: Career ended due to shattered shoulder, pelvis, and a broken leg suffered in Belmont at the hands of Bryan Mayhem 2002. Travis “Syko” Cross. Career ended to a broken back suffered in Belmont at the hands of Bryan Mayhem. Paralyzed from the waist down. 2001 Russel “The Grim Reaper” Tory. Largest professional athelete ever. Career ended from a 2 story fall off the roof of Belmont at the hands of Bryan Mayhem. Four years, four careers. How is this man still allowed to compete? That’s easy an easy one. Money! Karl Walnut former President of the now defunct EHFW, and former boss of Bryan Mayhem knows this beast well. “Is his style pretty” Hell no, he can’t wrestle to save his life. He lacks the skill and speed of his younger brother Jason, he lacks the charisma and love from the crowd of a Lestatt Knight, and he even lacks the dominance of one of the best ever Octavian. But what he does have is sheer anger and power. He will snap you in half and drink your blood. The fans nowadays have a gruesome bloodlust, and this man fulfills their need. Everytime he’s in the ring someone will get injured, it’s just that simple.” We asked Walnut why would Caldera keep this man (beast?) in his company. It’s the largest company in the world, do they really need this monster? “He needs him more than anyone else. Mayhem is the super villain. When Mayhem is on TV people hate him, and they want nothing else to see this man fail, grant it, doesn’t happen often, but they want it. There’s only been one person who’s been either talented enough or insane enough to deal with him. Lestatt Knight.” Lestatt is considered one of the greatest champions of all time. His feud started with Mayhem over Mayhem’s disgusting acts of violating Knight’s deceased child’s grave. Knight stepped into Belmont, and was the only one to step out. There was no winner because these two beat each other so badly from pillar to post, this 600 year old castle collapsed on them. “That match might have saved GCW. People were becoming skeptical on the future of the company and next thing you know the greatest champion ever steps into that hellhouse and survives it. Grant it he was beaten so goddamn bad, but regardless he walked out. The ratings had to be through the roof.” And Walnut was correct. 120 million people worldwide tuned into the PPV that day, but 188 million witnessed Belmont. As I’m writing this article I’m confused still. He hurts people, hurts them bad, why are people that crazy to step into the ring with him. So I decided to seek out some of his “victims”. Russel Tory was my first. I asked him what possessed him to step into Belmont with this Monster. “You’ve got to understand, no one ever heard of Bryan Mayhem at that time. Yes he was a huge powerful beast, but I’m bigger and stronger, or so I thought. I’d take it all back if I could, but I got people telling me that the reason Mayhem is so large today is because he chokeslammed a 450lb monster off the roof of a castle. I beg to differ.” And the man they called Syko, whom now lives in a wheelchair, we asked him why step into Belmont after seeing what he did to Tory a few months prior. “Walnut promised me what happened to Reaper wouldn’t happen to me. Obviously he was wrong. I agreed to the match because the man was undefeated, as was I. Just imagine what would happen if I was to beat that man in his own element. Obviously never happened.” Greed? Could it be that desire to rise up in the world that causes men to step in front of this giant? That’s my only answer. Bryan Mayhem could not be reached, and neither could his boss Steven Caldera (founder of GCW), for comments. As I’m writing this I have a picture of this man over my desk for inspiration. After a while I decided to take it down. This man is impending doom, and only God knows what is in store for us. I’m going to end this with a quote that Walnut gave us off record but allowed me to use it for this purpose. “I’m shooting for him because I discovered him, but if this man is to ever claim the title, We’ll have a front row seat to the Apocalypse.”
