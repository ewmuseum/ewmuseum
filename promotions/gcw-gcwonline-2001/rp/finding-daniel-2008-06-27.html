Finding Daniel

27 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/27/08 The bright red eyes shine like rubies as the albino boa stalks its prey. The fear in the white mouse’s eyes was evident, as it knew what was to come and curled up in the corner of the glass aquarium. In a blink of an eye the snake strikes crushing its feast before ingesting it whole. A pair of hazel eyes stare into the cage, awe struck and frightened. A smooth sultry voice brings the watchers attention to something equally as scary. The boy comes into focus; he looks sickly and weak. “Please Daniel don’t you see, you need to eat.” Cleo purred in his ear, “You don’t need to be scared of what we are. We are the most powerful beings that roam the night.” Cleo told him with pride filling her voice She ran a hand down the young boys arm trying to reassure him. A muffled cry turns both their attention to the corner of the dark, damp feeling room. In the corner a young redhead with as many curls as Shirley Temple sat on the floor bound and gagged. Tears formed in Daniel’s eyes as Cleo slowly walked him to the corner. “Daniel you have to feed or you will die, you don’t want to leave mommy all alone do you?” Cleo using her word to persuade the young boy, “It is the same thing as your snake,” Cleo turns the young boy to face her as she crouches down to his level, “I promise that everything will be just fine mon cheri, we are destined to be what we are.” Daniel looked up into the emerald green eyes of Cleo with wonder, wanting to believe everything that she had told him. He kneeled down beside the girl, looked up at Cleo, and then he leaned in to fill his need for the life force that filled the young girl. His canine’s caught the moonlight causing them to look like two pieces of ivory sharpened to a deadly point. The girl screamed. Delylah sat up in her bead covered with sweat and let out an ear-piercing scream. Paul bolted up out of the bed immediately on the defensive. Delylah’s eyes were wide with fear and Paul knew that she had another nightmare. He sat his large frame down beside her and pulled her into his arms. “I need to talk to my mother Paul. She might know who that little boy is. She could of overheard something right?” Delylah’s voice quivered as her mind whirled trying to comprehend the dream that had just transpired. “What little boy Del” You never told me about a little boy.” Paul looked at her queerly, not understanding what she was talking about. “Daniel.” Delylah uttered his name with melancholy, “She made him like her Paul, he’s still a baby.” Tears zig zagged down her cheeks as her body heaved with her sobs, “We have to save him Paul.” “We have to find her first.” Paul’s voice filled with abhorrence as he spoke of Cleopatra. “I am going to talk to my mother, you can stay here if you like or you can come with me.” Delylah’s eyes were wide with enthusiasm at the prospect of maybe finding a clue to where Cleo could be. “She is sleeping Del, that scream you let out was enough to wake the dead” Paul clamped his lips together so hard that the skin around them turned white, “I’m sorry Del I didn’t mean to say it like that, but with all that she has been through she must be exhausted.” “I don’t care Paul,” Her eyes filled with rage and determination, “She wants us to find her as bad as we want to find her.” Delylah donned her jade colored silk robe and flew out the bedroom door. Paul sighed heavily as he slipped his frame into his black silk robe. He walked out of the bedroom softly closing the door behind him. Her feet quietly patted the floor as she scurried into the living room, there sat Nebt-Het, her Egyptian features silhouetted against the soft glow of the late night infomercials. Paul and Delylah both stood there for a moment just looking at her. The events of the past few days had taken a toll on her beautiful features. The youth she once held despite her age was now gone, she looked twenty years older than her fifty years. Delylah’s heart bled for her mother. Delylah slowly walked toward her mother and sat on the couch beside her. Nebt-Het didn’t blink, the only acknowledgement she displayed was placing a hand on top of her daughter’s. “Mom, hey, I have to ask you a few questions.” Delylah said as she stroked her mother’s hair. ? Mom?” “I hear you Delylah, I just don’t know what to do.” Nebt-Het cried. “I remember so much of what has happened and no matter what I do I can’t get it out of my head.” Nebt-Het explains, her voice merely a whisper. “We want to help you mom but you need to tell us what you know.” The voice of reason chimes in, Paul slowly closes the distance between them and sits in a chair opposite them. “I” I really don’t remember much of what they said, just what they did to your poor father.” Nebt-Het began to sob uncontrollably, her body heaving spastically. “I had this strange dream about a little boy mom. I need to find him and save him. I don’t know who he is or where he comes from but he is in danger.” Delylah tells her trying to snap her back to reality, “In my first dream he was with an older woman, Cleo killed her and then made the young boy, Daniel, one of the undead.” “Don’t you see, Neb” I mean mom, we need to help this little boy. Do you know anything about Cleo’s adoptive parents?” Paul once again playing warden in an asylum for the insane. “Well” I did hear her say that she needed to visit her mother. Somewhere in France I believe,” Nebt-Het looked at them with wide eyes, “I don’t remember where though. I would suggest trying Paris though. Just a gut feeling” Nebt-Het was afraid to admit that she had a similar dream to Delylah’s. She didn’t want to be considered crazy. “Do you recall her name or anything at all about her?” Delylah insisted. “I believe her name was Oksana. I only met her briefly while I was seeing Elias during my pregnancy.” “Thank you mom, I promise we will find her and make her pay for what she did to dad. We will make them ALL pay.” Delylah’s voice filled with fortitude and desire. Finding her would be like finding a needle in a haystack, but with Delylah’s determination and need to extract revenge on her sister would drive her. She needed to save that little boy, she needed to know why she felt as though he was a part of her in some way. Tomorrow a flight to Paris will be booked. They will find her and save Daniel even with the fear for their lives.
