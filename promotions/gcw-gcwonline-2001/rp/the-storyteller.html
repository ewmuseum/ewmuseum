The Storyteller

Date: Everyday The more things change, the more they stay the same. [The camera shows pictures taped to a wall. They all show Dimensioner. Pictures from his various matches in GCW.] There is more that I know about you than you will ever know about X. You see, I know all about him. Who am I? I'm the one that discovered X. I'm the one that brought him here. In fact, you can think of me as X's voice. You see, X isn't much of a speaker. The fact that he talked as much as he has comes as a bit of a surprise. I'm no lawyer, or an agent. I'm just a man. X, now he's more than that. He's special. Let me tell you a story. This is a story that may or may not have any impact on this. About 20 years ago, there was a man. Not a very notable man, but just a man. He travelled the world. He was very fond of that lifestyle. This man had ventured into India. He found himself on the outskirts of Bombay. There was this young man playing soccer. He seemed out of place. He didn't look Indian, nor did he look like he was of British descent. It was peculiar that this young man would be here. 'Mister, you wanna play too?' asked that young man as he launched the ball at the net with a picture perfect bicycle kick. The man was taken aback by this child's agility. The man knew that he saw gold in this young man. He politely declined the child's invitation and continued to watch. Year after year, the man came back, and watched as the child grew into a lanky teenager and then into a rather strapping young man. This man would eventually find this child was prepared for anything that the outside world could give to him. Who was this young man? Who was the traveller? I don't know, the story was told to me and this story I relayed to X. You should have seen the laughter come to X's face. X told me that the story was unbelievable. That even if that story was true, that the young man that the traveller met that many years was no longer alive as he was now probably forged and scarred from what the real world has shown. You see, X has an understanding of this world that is beyond his years. It is that understanding that makes him more than a man. [Scene fades to black]
