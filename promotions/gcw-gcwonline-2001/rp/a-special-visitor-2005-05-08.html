A special visitor

8 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/8/2005 As it turned out, what I thought that doctor was going to tell me is what he did. My neck was in horrible shape after the beating I took at the hands of Octavian. In fact, he even suggested immediate surgery. I refused for the time being, and assured me one thing which will continue to loom over my head until I have everything physically corrected with me: One wrong move on mine or someone else's part and I will probably never be able to walk again. How fucking peachy. Despite just waking up for the first time in almost 24 hours, the whole hour and a half it took for that doctor to come and share with me his personal opinions and my option felt like I've been up for a full day. I was exhausted, and everything started to hurt all over again. With some of the pills they gave me in my system, I lay down to sleep once again... ~~~ The bleached white inside of a hospital are all we see. To our lefts we can hear the heavy footsteps of a man. They stop at the center of a large receptionist's counter, and out comes an all-too familiar voice: Man: I'm looking for the room number of a Mr. Cantrell. Looking to our lefts, we see a middle-aged woman with wire-frame glasses looking at a computer screen, furiously typing away. After a moment's search on the monitor, she has the answer the man is looking for. Receptionist: Room 217, anything else I can do for you? Man: No, thank you. We are looking onwards at floor level now, following the shoes of the man previously speaking. A pair of black dress shoes adorns his feet, with a matching black trench coat flowing downwards. As the man approaches an elevator, we stop following, letting the doors close as we view upwards to see who was requesting the room of Jason Dread. Unfortunately, the doors close before we can get higher that waist-level. ~~~ The pills I was given was assured to me to allow me to sleep much better by dampening the pain which can't seem to let go of me. The odd thing was, sleep never came to me as easily as I'd hoped for. In my light sleep I could swear I heard the rustling of papers next to me right after heavy footsteps. I tried to place it out of mind, but something didn't seem right. It was as if someone was constantly watching me. I was right. I woke up to see the face of what I initially believed to be Octavian. For a slight moment I panicked...that he was there to finish the underhanded job he set out to do just a few nights earlier. As my vision cleared, I relaxed. The man was not Gaius Octavian. It was my boss, Steven Caldera. For the moment he was looking through the papers that were at the foot of my bed...all of my medical records, charts, x-rays, the works. His vision casually glanced over towards me once again, and seeing me awake he laid the papers and sheets down on the table next to me. For the first few initial moments, we didn't say a thing to one another. I know that was at a complete loss of words, but as for what was racing through his mind I cannot account for. He was the first to break the silence with some idle banter: 'How are you feeling, Jason?' The question that I could either speaks volumes upon or very little. I chose the very short route through conversation. 'I've been better. There's just been a lot of things going through my mind as of lately.' 'I could probably guess...' Caldera reaches back onto the table and pulls out the x-ray. He holds it from what I can guess once again to the light and then sets it back down. With a look of concern on his face he turns back to me. 'This doesn't look good at all, Jason. A World Champion shouldn't have to function like this...are you going to?' This question didn't take me any time at all. While I may be a little disheveled about some of the current things going on with myself, this is what I'm 100% sure of. 'Absolutely, Steven. I'm not backing out just because some dickhead gets the jump he me.' 'He did a little more than just get the jump on you. He almost killed you.' 'It wouldn't be the first time I've heard that.' I forced out a weakened smile, which he returned. 'That's good to hear. Of course you know that all of this...' he motions to the room around me, obviously indicating my entire hospital experience '...will be covered as part of your GCW contract. I know about your financial situation...and coming from a friend, if there's anything that you may need I'll see to it that I can help you out until all this has settled.' 'It's a great thought of yours, Steven, but honestly I don't think I'll be needing too much. I've got a paycheck coming up, and that will get me by. All those possessions that were taken from me were nothing more than that...I can live for now without a place to live off of the road. The money I had before was just a luxury...I'll be alright.' 'Well, will you be attending Worldwide this week? Despite your current state there is nothing I can do against what that weasel Danger has gotten you booked into...but if you are in bad enough shape we could just have the match rebooked since you are unfit to compete due to an in-ring injury. If you're too hurt to compete, I understand.' 'No Steven, I'm not giving up just like that. I'm not going to just quit a match because it's unfair to my current situation. Octavian isn't going to get the better of my just like that...I know what I have to do.' For the first time in a long while Caldera smiled a genuine smile to me. I caught a hint of personal good-will in his voice as he continued in reply. 'I'm glad to hear that, Jason. If you believe you can handle everything, then I support your decision. Just understand that I know it was I who got you into this match by allowing Danger to manipulate me and get on the booking committee. Just now that when Worldwide comes, I'm going to do the right thing.' It was the best news I've heard in a long time. Caldera and I may have had out share of differences in the past, but I know that he is a man of his word, no matter what he says. In my heart, I also believe that for the first time that I may have ever seen, Caldera is sincerely out to set things right with GCW. We shared a few more moments of idle conversation before we shared our goodbyes and he left. I felt quite a bit more confident now...Caldera was firmly by my side on this matter. I lied for about an hour more, finally utilizing that television I had across from my bed. Within ten minutes of British programming I remembered why I don't have a television back home, and why I never watch it. I turn it off, disgusted. I thought about everything once again. About those on my side, about what I'm going to do with Octavian as soon as I can get my hands around his neck, and what he might have in store for me with that Coliseum Cage Match. With me giving him the clearance to make the match how he sees fit, I'm sure he going to capitalize on my current situation. I remember the doctor telling me that there was a bit of good news ? there is a good probability that my neck could heal with time...but we'd have to see. For the next year my neck is in a real danger of being permanently incapacitated. Well, alright, it wasn't ALL good news. Within the hour I'd eaten and was in the throes of sleep once again. I reached instinctively for the pills I had at the table beside me when I became consciously aware of what was going on. It seems that after Caldera has left me, the pain in my body has dulled considerably...I don't need a crutch like this medication to get me by anymore. I set the two white pills back on the table and turn the light off beside me. Tomorrow I should be cleared to leave this place. Worldwide is when I start my path to revenge.
