Date: Bathroom There was a time, and it wasn't that long ago, that I sat back and decided who I really was. But now I wonder who I really am. Inside this mask, this is the coffin of Johnny Lexicon, the container for the legacy he failed to achieve. But it's also the birth of something far greater than he ever thought he was, the phoenix within has engulfed that life in the fiery torment of it's own design. And now it has risen from the ashes of it's macabre ascention. The trials he had seen, the obstacles overcome, battles fought and wars waged. Everything before has lead to this here, and this now, what comes after is a new chapter, a new legacy. A legacy. Legacy. One word, legacy, six letters, three syllables. It's always been about the legacy, the culmination of a man's greatest achievments, and Lexi always wanted his to be shine bigger and brighter than everybody elses. But he wanted it so bad, he let himself slip. He stepped into the wrong side of the game. My legacy. Mine will be different. Win. Lose. In the end it's all about who wants it more. Things that divide those that can from those who can't. The ones willing to bleed, the ones willing to break. I don't fear breaking. I broke once. And it didn't scare me then. Nothing scares me, and sometimes I think that scares me.Then I remember nothing scares me. But always I'm remembering Lexicon, his name, his story. It was my story, but now I have no story. How am I supposed to act. How does Zeta Acosta carry himself. There's no answers to these questions, but I have the answer to one. I know how The Marauder fights. The warehouse is packed tonight. They've been chanting something lately, Sobias tells me that they're calling my name, cheering me on. I'd like to believe that, I'd like to think that they cheer me for my talent, because I earned their respect and admiration. 'Something spanish meaning 'Let's go'!' I turn my head and wince, shrugs with a chuckle and leaves again. I got to change in the bathroom, I don't recognize the mask in the mirror, it looks like something alien, something living, that was going to slowely rewire my brain, something I wanted it to do, I wish it would. Take away the questions, take away the answers I don't want, just give me peace in the ring. Give me competition, give me the rush of victory, the andrenaline pounding in my ears, heart racing, pain coursing through ever fiber of my body, feuling me to take more than him, to dish more. White knuckles wrapped tightly around the lip of the sink The Marauders eyes slowely close and after a deep breath open again. 'Fight. Win. That's all I need to worry about. Games. Dangerous. The only place I have to get to.' He says to himself. His lips a tight line of seriousness. Again his eyes closed, mind constantly running the paces of moves, suplexes to springboards and back again. He forces a finger under the mask uncomfortably, it was hot, sweat was already slicking the leather against his skin. His lips curl into a smile, Jordan's in that crowd tonight... By tomorrow, we'd be in the states, I don't know how I know, but I do. We had to be, the future of my career depends on it. They're chanting that stupid word again, I can't even pronounce it. They want to see me. They want to see the Canadian. Boy are they in for a surprise. A big surprise. Because the Canadian is dead. Lexicon is dead, everything is dead. Tonight the Marauder is born. Tonight this match, this deal with Jordan... The Marauder turns away from the mirror and stalks the door, he shoves it open wide as he hears his name being announce. 'The Marauder!' It sounded good, the fans responded. I raise my hands, adrenaline already starting to pump through my veins. My muscles twitch with anticipation, one of my favorite feelings in the world. The rings here are nothing like what I'm used too, they're old and worn, not as much give. But it's still a ring, it's still my one true love and I treat her good. The man across from me is smaller, probably a bit quicker, he had a determination that I now know very well. It's the desperation of the poor relying on their one last strength to survive. One more day, one more meal. He won't get that meal tonight. Because more than desperation and determination, there's fear.
