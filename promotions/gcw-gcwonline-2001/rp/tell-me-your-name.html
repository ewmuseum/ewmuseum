Tell me your name.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: I'll tell you. Who are you? Could you pretend for a second, to not know the answer, and just for the record, my intentions are as subtle as cancer. I go by The Marauder you see, but beneath the lie a man I be. My name I've not spoken in well over a week, Lexicon, it feels weird as I speak. A name to know, one for sure to remember. The mask it does hinder, why do you pretend? It must come off, it soon must end. Behind the lies the lights and the people in the stands, I'm not the man being cheered by those fans. I have committed great crimes, I have ventured to sin. I have broken men's dreams, I have cheated to win. Inside of this mask and behind this ideal, I cannot be broken, I refuse to kneel. A wrestler is a wrestler, you're not saving the planet. The abuse, the abuse! How can you're ego stand it? Who are you to tell me, who are you to decide. I'll not be a puppet, I'll fight or I'll die. The scientist in his lab, the baller on the court. We love what we do, to our jobs go our heart. I'll do this till I'm choking, broken or dead, I'll get up to be beaten again and again. You lose every week, get it in your head. You're not getting respected, you're not feared with dread. I remember a day, filled with mayhem and blood, you took victory or nothing, give into no one you would. You played games with the best of em, your hand never lost, no man's fool, you won no matter the cost. It was different, I was different. The game is the same no matter the name. Don't give me that shit, the entire field is new. Every way I turn I'm getting shafted by every person in view. The sinner cries for redemption. The warrior cries for vengeance. Don't give into the hype, you're better than that. The Marauder is trash, they've all kicked your ass. From Toxic to Savige, Lestatt and Trey. You no showed Borealis and got a free-bi from Rei. I'll get at Toxic again, I swear on my grave. You're in no shape, it's you I'm trying to save. If I let him win now, everything is true. I'm a loser, a loser, you can say it too. Stage two is self loathing, I'll let you have a moment. Pull it together, stop posing. Don't patronize me, I'm not in the mood. How should I say this, without being rude. Oh forget it, you're a loser, I admit it with shame. I don't know who did it, who might be to blame. It was Eva or Jordan, Trey and Lestatt. They all did it back, what you did to them, ass-hat. You're to blame, you, yourself, no other, not me. You throw it away, you're not deaf, you're not blind, yet you just refuse to see. The Marauder was never ment to help you succeed. You speak lies and translate false the facts that I give. I may lose and I lose and still I will live. The mask is not bound to any one man's head, to another it'll go, it's horror's to give. Now you're thinking, now you're planning, using your head. That bastard took Eva, I'm gonna get him, I'll see that he's dead. I'll not rest nor trangress until I succeed. Toxic has made his folly, he's shown his greed. He believes to be better, he thinks that I'm beat. Do you remember now? All the glorious heat. Do you remember the high, the sweet splendor of winning? The adrenaline pushing you harder, faster, the world spinning... Stop, you tempt me with your words, your lies. A demon this mask holds, a monster with green eyes. You can't ask this of me, I can't turn back into that man. That man is dead, he died months ago, buried under rocks and sand. Every task in life is a lesson learned hard. You can't win every hand, can't hold every card. Who are you to query, to judge, and advise. I didn't ask you to interfere, to condemn, and chastise. You did, not that long ago, I had begged you to remember. I asked you if you accepted me, I demanded you to surrender. But instead you wanted none of it, you wanted the ring. You wanted the wrestlers, the fans, and cold defeated sting. You're not supposed to be here. You called me to be here. You lie to me again. I don't do that very often. It's the honest truth that I speak, they're just the answers you didn't seek. Why did you come? You really that dumb? What are you doing out there every week? I'm gonna get Toxic, right now I'm just a little weak. You can't beat him, you never could. Not with that mask, that name, you're not that good. Shut up. Don't push me away Johnny, I just want you to win, make the big money. Shut. Up. Johnny. Shut up. Shut up. Shut up! ...
