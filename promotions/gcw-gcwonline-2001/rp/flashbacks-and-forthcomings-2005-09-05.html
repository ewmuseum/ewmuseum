Flashbacks and Forthcomings

5 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/05/05 'Mr. Summers! Mr. Summers!' [ The scenes faded in. We were in one of the suites of a downtown Seattle, WA hotel. The room's French doors leading to the balcony were wide open and welcoming the Emerald City in. Seattle was no different than any other city across the country at this time of the year. The high humidity, the hot temperatures; it may have only been 10 AM, but yet all the miserableness of summer existed. The summer effect on the surroundings was evident by the numerous hotel customers. Several were dressed in the tiniest of bathing apparrel lounging by the large hotel pool below. Hotel personnel ran to and fro bringing drinks and else to the many customers that lay in chairs near the pool. Others enjoyed themselves in the large oval pool. ] [ And we flashed back to the suite and surveyed the luxuries the room contained. Plush gray carpet aligned the floor, where many a posh leather chair set and a large parlor couch resided. A half-empty bottle of Malibu Rum set on a counter; beside it rested a few empty cans of soda. Behind the counter a large bar stood, filled to the brim with the highest priced alcoholic beverages GCW's money could affford. Leaning against the balcony railing, stood the 'Golden Boy' himself, Jake Summers, with his left hand over his face. A half-empty glass of OJ sat on railing. On the small table beside him, lay a partially eaten bagel.] 'You'd think these people had never been anywhere with a pool.' he said to himself. [A knock came from the door drawing his attention away from the hotel customers.] 'Mr. Summers... house cleaning! Mr. Summers are you there?' yelled a feminine voice from behind the door. [At that moment, the door opened revealing a petite Latino woman dressed in a tight black and white outfit. Her dress barely contained the bosom that busted at the seems.] Jake: Yes, I'm here... Out on the balcony. And the more and more I think about its too dang early to be up. [ He turned and made his way back into the room.] Latino Maid: Senor Summers, I'm here to clean your suite. My name is Maria. Pay no attention to me, I'll try not to get in your way. [ How could he not pay attention to her? She had a 36-24-32 figure, mocha skin, and a smile on her face that was as pure as snow. Need we say more? Jake flashed a smille back of his own. Perhaps he would investigate the possiblities of their chance encounter later on. ] Jake: You're ok, I'm just not feeling the best at this morning. You won't be in my way; I was just fixing to go for morning run anyhow. Figured I'd check weather before I made my way out. Maybe the fresh air will clear the many things on my mind. [She flashed a smile and acted as if she was sympathetic to his pain.] Maria: I'm sorry to hear that you're not feeling well. Would you like for me to get you some medicine? Jake: Nah I'm sure the fresh air will do me justice. And if not I'll pick up some Tylenol while I'm out. Maria: Well senor I'll make sure to leave a mint on your pillow. Jake: Maybe you can stay around and I'm sure you're the perfect relief for all my aches. [ There was a huge smile on his face. ] Maria: What's that? Jake: Nothing... [ He laughed as he made his way out the door. ] Jake: I'll be back in an hour or so.. [ Momentarily the scene faded only to repear at the entrance of the luxurious hotel where 'Golden Boy' stood. The air seem warmer than it felt on the balcony above; proof was provided by the beads of sweat that crept down his forehead. Maybe he should've took the elevator instead of the stairs. ] [ He wiped his brow and proceeded down the sidewalk leading away from the hotel. He stopped before crossing the pavement and kneeled at the street side to retie the laces of the white Adidas tennis shoes. He took the opportunity to check the time before he rose back to his feet. He pulled a set of headphones from his pocket and placed them on his head. He ran his hand across his face and felt the light fuzz that covered his cheeks. Wow shouldn't he have shaved? That was the farthest thing from his mind before he proceeded onward. 'Golden Boy' Jake Summers slowly made his way down Century Boulevard, while 'Boulevard Of Broken Dreams' began to play on his Ipod .] # I walk a lonely road # The only one that I have ever known # Don't know where it goes # But it's home to me and I walk alone [ Slowly the vibrant scene the warm morning sun provided switched to black and white images of 'Golden Boy' Jake Summers. The boiler room where he stood behind a Japanese woman gagged and bound to a chair, was filled with a cloud of steam. The scene was from his career in MSW, an organization where Jake Summers soon become a household name to many pro wrestling fans. The woman was the former fianc’e of Hiro Saotome, a former stable mate and later foe of Jake Summers. Akane, as she was known, had left Saotome, a less successful individual to manage Jake Summers, a multi-time champion at the time. She had recognized a bandwagon and decided to jump aboard. ] [ During the whole ordeal Summers and Akane played horrible mind games with her former lover along the way until Summers and Saotome met at the final MSW show in an 'I Quit' match. Near the end of the match, Jake held Hiro at bay demanding Akane hit her former lover with a steal chair. When she refused to inflict any more pain on the man she once loved, overcome with rage Jake kicked her in the gut and followed it with a corkscrew-cradle-piledriver. The distraction of Akane's mutiny which in turn led to her severely injured neck allowed Hiro Saotome just enough time to recover and deliver a destating move of his own that dislocated Jake's jaw. ] 'After all this time, I'm still troubled by the crap I put him through. Lord knows I shouldn't have played with his emotions. He and I used to ride up and down the roads together watching each others back. We were once friends. However soon after I started succeeding, he became jealous; jealous that I was not only getting title shots, but winning them. Who could blame the bitch for leaving him for the better man? And although we were accused of being more than just business partners and I'll admit she was an attractive woman, never once did we engage in anything more. However I should've known better than to even give that Asian slut the time of day. In the end, Akane not only screwed Hiro, but the coniving bitch stab me in the back as well. Furthermore she cost me what should have been a shining moment. Some learn from their mistakes and a few of us tend to repeat them.' # I walk this empty street # On the Boulevard of Broken Dreams # Where the city sleeps # and I'm the only one and I walk alone 'Maybe I was wrong to do so. There have been times when I just needed someone to talk to. Sure my wife was there, but there's some things a woman just can't understand. Any normal person in need of friendly advice would question themselves whether or not they made the right decision. However instead I wonder whether or not if I should have just forgiven them rather than choosing to disown my parents. When I stopped and asked whether I wanted to proceed on the road I was traveling; whether I wanted to go on to become the man my parents were trying to mold me into... the decision was an easy one to make. Even easier when a child I thought to have died eight years prior, starred me in the eye, as I kneeled before him and the first thing he said was, 'Daddy I love you'.' [ We see a younger black and white image of Jake Summers, as he runs to his father, Alexander. Jake is lifted into the air and as his father hugs him. We see a small wrestling trophy in Jake's hand. Abruptly the special moment, quickly switched to an older image of Jake Summers, kneeling next to a young blond hair boy. The child wrapped his arms around Jake's neck. That image is one of young Jeb Summers meeting his father, Jake, for the first time in his life. It was a special moment shared amongst father and son; the two until that day had no knowledge that they were father and son. Jake's parents had given their grandchild up for adoption, shortly after birth, a birth that caused the child's mother to die on the delivery table. Having no knowledge of his son, Jake was manipulated by his parents to forget life as he knew it in Panama City Beach and take a wrestling scholarship to Michigan. Having graduate college, married, and having another child already, eight years later Jake learned of the whole saga. Soon after being reunited with his first son, by his high school girlfriend whom died giving birth, Jake disowned his own mother and father. ] 'There's no way in hell I could ever have become the man my father grew to be. A monster so selfish and so devilishly that they'd manipulate their own son into thinking their grandson was dead. Furthermore a son of a bitch so diabolical and sinister that he agreed to give his grand kid to a couple of religious zealots that would give David Koresh a run for his money.' # I walk alone # I walk alone # I walk alone # I walk a... [ Black and white images of a black Mercedes Benz, white smoke coming from the rear tires appeared on screen. The images switched to two boys and a mother lying on cold concrete. Slowly the image switched back to the automobile where a masked man stood beside the car. ] 'And if that wasn't bad enough, how could any person pay a man to run down his own grandsons and daughter-in-law? How could anyone be that despicable, that uncaring? Furthermore could someone else, a cold hearted stranger, take blood money and fueled with their own jealousy, try to kill my wife and boys. Are there really people in this world that are so eager to get ahead that they'd resort to killing someone? Sometimes it makes me wonder what the hell this country has come to.' # My shadow's the only one that walks beside me # My shallow heart's the only thing that's beating # Sometimes I wish someone out there will find me # 'Til then I walk alone [ More black and white images began to pass by. Jake is standing next to an amateur wrestling mat, while two young boys stand in front of him. An older gentlemen is placing medals around the children's necks. Summers has a huge smile on his face, as his two sons won medals in their respective weight classes. ] # Ah-ah, Ah-ah, Ah-ah, Aaah-ah, # Ah-ah, Ah-ah, Ah-ah # I'm walking down the line # That divides me somewhere in my mind # On the border line # Of the edge and where I walk alone [ On the screen there were images of an African-American woman with a chair in hand. Other images flashed by. One of this same woman, trying to hit Jake Summers with said chair. The image was quickly replaced by one of the woman now laying on the concrete holding her stomach and forehead. The images were ones of Vanessa Chamberlain, fianc’e to Leonard Aarons, the only man that could receive the same amount of hate and contempt Jake felt for his own parents. Ms. Chamberlain's attempt to help Mr. Aarons backfired costing herself and Aarons of their own child. As she attempted to his Jake with the chair he caught it. After jerking it from her clutches, he kicked her in the stomach and delivered a DDT onto chair. Due to the injuries she endured, Ms.Chamberlain suffered a miscarriage. ] 'We all have points in our lifes that we're ashamed of. Everyone have done things we wish we could take back. No man is perfect. One of the lowest moments in my life was taking the life of someone unintentially or not. What made it even more dreadful was when the life was of an unborn child. Whether people choose to believe it or not, even today I still am haunted by the whole ordeal. Indeed I hated what happen, but in the end some may consider it an eye for an eye.' # Read between the lines # What's fucked up and everything's alright # Check my vital signs # To know I'm still alive and I walk alone [ At this moment we see an image of 'Golden Boy' Jake Summers standing in the middle of a GCW ring. ] # My shadow's the only one that walks beside me # My shallow heart's the only thing that's beating # Sometimes I wish someone out there will find me # 'Til then I walk alone [ The image is quickly replaced by an images of Jake Summers and Ryan Stephens walking away from the ring, GCW tag titles drapped their left shoulders. ] # Ah-ah, Ah-ah, Ah-ah, Aaah-ah # Ah-ah, Ah-ah # I walk alone # I walk a... [ The music suddenly ceased playing. The black and white images changed into a colored image of Jake Summers seated on a metal bench. A light drizzle began to fall. His arm was positioned in a 'V' with his chin rested in the palm of his hand. ] 'The Regulators? What the hell? Is this Yo! MTV Raps or wrestling? Ah it could've been worse. Stephens and I could have been facing Super Mario and Donkey Kong!' [Thunder sounded and lightning clashed. It begun to rain harder and the scene faded to black.] [End]
