Getting started... anew.

11 February 2006

Date: 2/11/06 Blaise's tale: The preserved forests of trees along the Onsenji Yumedono Ryokan Resort had something in them that wasn't really normal to this area. Two wolves, running side by side. One a midnight gray in color, larger and more bulky then the other. The smaller, a white colored first with red highlights able to be seen as the moonlight of the almost full, waxing gibbous moon shone down upon them. The ran, almost as if competing for a place, as if they were racing. The smaller one goes under some fallen trees, while the larger one barrels and bolts over them. It keeps going down the side of the mountain. They quickly head away from the hustle and bustle of the resort area. Running, running, they look like they're pushing each other and themselves for an all out run. Finally they approach a small shrine in a clearing. They have to be a few hundred yards, if not more from the resort and all of the extra people that have swarmed upon it for the activities of photographing a few girls. The larger one makes a final bolt for it, and looks to be about to win. The smaller one suddenly veers off, and takes track that goes up and over a partially buried bolder. He leaps from the top and slams into the side of the other canine. Both tumble down the clearing, heading towards the small Shinto shrine that had been erected so long ago. A cloud of leaves, dirt and dust come up, obscuring the final view of the observed race. When all is said and done though, it's not two wolves laying on the ground when the dust settles. Gray Brand, second in command of the KnightMare Brigade's Native American Nations component is laying face first in the dirt, his hand outstretched and touching the stone for the small shrine. Laying next to him, his foot out and also touching the stone is the half-Irishman, Blaise McClancy. Gray spits out a bunch of leaves before addressing his commander. “I take it that we will count this one as a tie and neither of us will take the wager.” Blaise chuckles and looks down to where his foot is. He had tried to put it out right in front of Gray's hand. But instead, both touched right next to each other, and it was so close, that it couldn't be told by either of their enhanced senses. Neither those they both shared based on that of their Garou mothers, nor Blaise's traits he'd taken from his Irish Seelie father. “I guess it would be. So we will try again tomorrow. That was quite exhilarating though. ? Gray nodded as he rolled onto his back and then slowly came to his feet. Blaise just kipped up from his back to a standing stance. Might as well let it all out here. These men are more then they seem in the ring. Gray Brand is a full blooded Saquamish Indian by birth. He's from the Port Madison Indian Reservation on the Kitsap peninsula in Washington State. The same tribe that had produced the great Native spokesman that the city across the sound from it was named for... Seattle. But somewhere in his blood line, a line that comes down from his Native born mother, was something that the ancient people in his area called a 'skin walker'. Normally this would put him from one of the specific blood lines of Garou, or wolf like shape shifters. However, the Puget Sound area has a history that doesn't quite match other parts of the world. Ancient viking long boats have been found buried in the silt and mud at the bottom of the sound. The specific type of Garou that Gray has the traits of resemble something along the lines that comes from the Norwegian area of the world. Yes, most mortals if they knew would call Gray a werewolf. For all intents and purposes, they'd be right. Except that he can't really transfer any 'disease' to someone he bites. It's something you're born with, not something you contract. And then you have Blaise McClancy. His father was an Irish Immigrant. But he was also a member of the Seelie Courts of Tir Na Nog. What is that? Well Tir Na Nog is what the Faery folk call their version of Ireland. This makes one half of Blaise a member of the race of beings that calls themselves the Tautha De Dannan. His father is the bearer of the spirit essence of a being known as Nauda, a former king to the Faery kind. Blaise is the bearer of the essence, the mantle as they call it, of the spiritual energies of Turren, a prince to the Tautha De Dannan. For all intents and purposes, yes, he's a Faery. To be exact, he's from the race that was called a Dryad to the Greeks and Romans, a tree spirit connected to nature. Yes, some of the old wives tales and that aren't true to a point. But some of them are. But Blaise is special in the line that his mother, a Pauite Indian from the Valley of Fire area in Nevada, was carrying a recessive gene from his grandfather, William Slyfox. This gene was similar to Gray's. Blaise is also a Garou. His race? That's the hard part. His grandfather is of one of the Native races found in the area now known as the United States. But he shows no signs of being one of them. His grandfather has told him that he may well be a throw back, a fitting one too. For there was once a race of these 'skin walkers' that was over in the British Isles, the Celtic lands of old that were wiped out. A few escaped to the new world and intermingled with the races over here. They had a connection to the Faery folk and were allied with the Seelie courts. This put Blaise into a very interesting situation. In fact his grandfather has theorized that it was his Faery blood that awoke the genetic code for these old and wiped our beings. In the end though, Blaise is a man. He's always believed that. No matter what, he was born and raised a man. In Blaise's mind, there are many things you can be. One thing that he's taken to studying in between his duties with the Order of the Hammer, and their little special forces unit he now commands, is that Man is the blank slate that all else comes from. How else could you explain the fact that everything else out there can inter-mate with them. Genetic code or not, the 'neutral stem cells' of all of the fantasy races that now hide behind the veil, is the very beings that they hide from. That aside, what is it that Gray and Blaise have been doing? Sneaking out at night and running around in their alternate forms? Well what do you think would happen when you put two wolves together? It's called a pack. The ultimate brotherhood in the world of the animals and other kinds that aren't human. If a packmate can't trust the others in his pack, then whom can he trust? Blaise looked at and studied his second in command by virtue of having been on the case before him. Gray was being silent. “What's up Gray Muzzle” You're brooding again.” Gray shook his head and looked over at Blaise as he let out a big sigh. “Just Josh. He apparently saw the tapes from last week from the garage area.” Blaise smiled. “And he gave you the rib.” Gray nodded. “Better he found out that way. If he was ever with me when I got near her, his empathy would find out in a heart beat. This way he wasn't there to fuck it up for me.” “Fuck up what?” Blaise tilted his head a bit when he asked that. He knew the answer, but the response he got showed that Gray wasn't ready to say what it was just yet. “Nothing... he's just really annoying when it comes to her and Ms. Vlassy. He's messed up more stuff then he knows. If it wasn't for the fact that he was there and helped Annie out on the night that Dani manifested, he'd have been suspended. Instead they promoted him to Corporal and said I had to keep him.” Blaise nodded as he moved to sit down on a fallen stump of a tree. Both men were wearing dark gray jumpsuits with a tactical harness on. They looked like they were dressed for a military operation instead of a night out running. “He's getting better as he works on not feeding the energy he receives right back at people. But he's got a ways to go. Although he's lucky he's not as good at scents as you and I are.” “Why do you say that?” Blaise smiles a wide, white smile. “Because the amount of pheromones you put off when she's near, it's almost sickening. If I didn't know you were a man most of the time, I'd expect you to start spraying things to mark your territory. So tell me, why did you show her your other self?” Gray looked down at the ground. “As an attempt to show her that she could trust me. If you've not noticed, her aura. She's not normal. She's intriguing. Her time with the others, Marcus, Corvus, Vlassy, even her exposure to some of the others like Ambrosi, not just her love affairs... she's drank a vampires blood before and didn't change. She's more then a regular human. I know this. Just what, I don't know.” Blaise just nodded his head. “But I do know that when I'm near her, I find that I'm attracted to her in a big way. Back at Halloween, when she wandered into our dressing room, looking for Dani to help her with her costume, I think I almost had a heart attack seeing her like that. But I kept my composure. As far as I can see, so far only a friendship could come about it. Especially when more normal fare may be available.” Blaise tossed a rock over towards him, bouncing it off a larger stone on the ground. “Why do you say that?” “Almasy... Someone I've seen her kiss, and give her a rose. Someone that she seems to have an attraction for also. Yet last week, their actions confused me. Why did they go after him” And why didn't we go help a fellow brigadier?” Blaise's face got stern on it. “Almasy was a brigadier, under Sean. He joined right before Sean's shoulder gave out and he got the deals to take over the federation he now runs. Not to mention the game was over and his now wife had won. Almasy has our support if he asks. But I'm not Sean. I'm not going to apologize to him. I'm not going to say I'm sorry. For his sake, he's not exactly been a Brigadier since Sean left the UWF. She's never flown our colors, he's never shown that he cared. I think he joined Sean just for his own sake... So, on that, I don't fully trust him. I'll carry out my orders and duties, but that's it. Unless he proves otherwise.” Gray looked worried. “That's what I'm afraid of, Lieutenant.” Blaise got the gist of that right away. Blaise got up and walked over to stand next to him. “Gray, I said I don't fully trust him. I'd serve his aid if needed out of the duties and honor of the Order and the Brigade. But I'll not be his hit man. I give you my word as a friend though, that Dawn will not be harmed by any of us. I'll see to it myself that wouldn't happen.” Gray nodded at that. “What about Shadow?” Blaise nodded. “I know that if anything happened to her, it wouldn't do Dawn well at all. They are lovers. I can not make the same promise for her, only that I will try. As far as I'm concerned, I'm not ready to rehash old squabbles from Global here. Although they wasted no time in getting their faces out there and hurting people. They let no moss grow.” Gray walked over to the small shrine and took a coin out of one of the zip up pockets in the combat gear of the Order's Hunters. He placed it in the recessed indent on the stone. “No, they're ambitious. I'm just worried about Dawn. Almost the same as you're worried about Ukyo in this week's match up.” That last bit hit Blaise like a brickbat. He didn't expect it. He wasn't trying to show it at all. “My worry is of a lover and future husband, not of a wrestler. I know she's fine. I was there when she finally subjected herself to the healing of the Order's sorcerers. She's feeling no pain in her neck, and has no issues in the workouts. It's not like her disowned-brother is here to do anything about it.” Gray nodded in agreeance. “No, he's in Global... sometimes I wonder if that was one reason you took so long to decide if you were going to negotiate a new deal and then said no.” Blaise just nodded his head in the affirmative of that. “You just keep your brother in line, so we have a real match this week, if you're still in the ring when we enter. We're former two time tag team champions in Four Winds. Don't forget that.” Gray laughed. “I won't. Just remember, me and my brother have been jerked around like a damn piece of taffy in a pulling machine. We're hungry. So don't take it easy on us.” Blaise slapped his friend's shoulder. “We won't. Now, race you to the lake?” Gray didn't answer that. He just turned and took off at a run. As he did, slowly the form of the Gray Wolf replaced him, at a run. Slowly the smaller white wolf caught up with him as they headed out from the shrine, off into the night. Ukyo's Tale: “I don't know how you talked me into this! These costumes are ridiculous!” That's not Ukyo yelling. No, it's the girlfriend of one Joshua Brand, and good friend of Ukyo Kushinaga. Danielle Lynn Northsky was the only one of the KB:NAN that had not debuted yet in action for GCW. A former CCWA North American Champion, still held the belt when they closed. Also a former Global Platinum Champion, and held it when it was merged with the Cruiser and Television Titles. She's a full blooded Lakota Sioux from the Rosebud reservation in South Dakota. A woman with a deep family history. She's also something else, as most who are in the KB are. She's a throwback to a past generation of her family. That throwback gene seems to come from a being known as the Morning Star to her people. Others have another name for him... Kokabel... ? The Star of God?, the royal astrologer of heaven. Yes, a Throne of Heaven, an Angel to the biblical scholars. This woman was a very beautiful lady, who has now graced the cover or inside of many magazines. Some of it is because she's got the looks and basically wrestles in a rawhide bikini. Some of it is because she's got the looks of a model to back it all up. And then you have Ukyo, six feet of Japanese lady from Tokyo. Not at all looking like a man though. She's by far a lady, and has graced a few magazines herself. Ukyo stepped into the room where Dani sat in front of a mirror. Ukyo, her lavender dyed hair was held back by a black headband. She was wearing a black thong that was visible with yellow vinyl hotpants and halter top. A red jacket finished it off. Yes, Ukyo was dressed as Faye Valentine from the anime Cowboy Bebop. Seeing as how her hair was normally kept the same color as Faye's and her figure accented it, it was rather fitting. Dani sat here with her long hair done up and wild, a corset with garters on, and thong panties. Behind her on the wall was hanging a pair of white wings. She looked like something else from another anime show, albeit a must less known one to the western cultures. Ukyo puts her hands on Dani's shoulders and then her head next to Dani's, smiles wide in the mirror. “Because, you said I needed to do more modeling. We come to Japan. I used to do a lot of cosplay and find a modeling job that I don't mind doing. And you said you'd join me if I got back into having my picture taken.” Dani grumbled at that. “Yeah, fine. But I'm convinced the men of your country are all sick. I mean look at your costume. You're thong is going so far up your hips, it's overly exaggerated that you've got butt floss on. I've posed in nothing but a regular lace thong before, but that's coming out of the shorts” Why?” Ukyo laughed. “Because they're all dirty young men who beat off to the TV or the magazine. Then they go to downtown Tokyo and fill these caf’s that have anime or manga based female characters serve them drinks and food. It's Seymour Almasy's paradise here. I know one of the caf’s has a Final Fantasy night.” Dani laughed. “Still, every costume for this shoot is either a French maid outfit or something with a butt floss and half a bra in it. Who but the Japanese would think an Angel would be dressed like this when they show up on the planet?” Ukyo grinned. “Well I know one who by rumor was in a blue silk thong and nothing else when she showed up. She would have been a Japanese man's dream.” Dani stomped her foot down and looked a bit dejected at that comment. “That's not exactly fair, you.” “Didn't need to be.” Dani went back to work, doing her own mascara, trying to get her native features to match the picture torn out of a magazine in front of her. The drawing on the picture was the character she was trying to emulate. Ukyo smiled as she worked on fluffing out the wings that Dani will be wearing in her first shoot. “So, Dani... next week is Valentine's Day. You and Josh have anything planned?” Dani kept working on her makeup as she answered. “Well other then locking everyone out of our little hut and having a nice candle light dinner, with some wine or champagne. Then maybe some more romantic things that I shouldn't mention that involve carnal acts that would make Jenna Jameson blush, not much, why?” Ukyo kept messing with the wings, looking over her shoulder a bit towards Dani, trying to judge reactions. “Well, I was wondering. Since Josh has been moody and you think he's been trying to ask you something since we went up to Alaska for that one show... what would you do if he finally broke down and did ask you?” ... “OW!” Dani hit herself in the eye with the mascara brush as she reacted to that one. Ukyo covered her mouth, hoping that Dani wouldn't hear her chuckle. After grabbing a tissue and dealing with her now watering eye, Dani addressed the question. “I think I'd do like you did, and say: Yes.” Ukyo smiled. “At least you have a choice.” Dani looked at her quizzically. “You didn't have a choice” C'mon, I've not seen a couple more in love in a long time. Not to mention...” “Heh, I was making a joke out of the situation. I love Blaise dearly, and if it were all normal, I'd still have said yes. But given the fact that I'm infected with the curse of loving a dryad, I'm stuck with him for our lives. And seeing as how we're both from races of beings on this planet that are long lived, that'll be a very long time.” Dani still didn't get it. “Okay, can you finally explain this 'infection' to me?” Ukyo chuckled. “Well, it seems that when someone, male or female, who makes love to a Dryad, or one of the Tautha De Danna, who has a shared feeling with that person... they become 'infected' as the other races call it. I can't live without him right now. If I ever tried to date someone else, it would fail. My only thoughts would be of him. There has been very few people break free of it, or break someone else free of it in the world. When he goes out at night for more then a few hours, I long for him to come back. More and more time, and it gets worse. There have been a few times he's come back from a two day outing and I almost jumped his bones right there in the door way when I was stuck at home with my bad neck. I think that's why I finally told Annie to arrange for one of the sorcerers to do their healing on it. I'm tired of waiting around the house for him. Now I can not only be his partner in the ring again, but also take my place at his side again on hunts. The place where I belong.” Dani nodded, finally understanding some. “Well I know he and Gray have been hanging around each other quite a bit lately.” Ukyo nodded. “Yes, that's something I expected. His grandfather told me that would happen soon enough when he encountered another Garou. Neither he nor Gray grew up around others of their kind. But it's natural for them to form a pack like mentality.” Dani smiled. “Well at least your little group has the first and second command working together.” Ukyo chuckled. “Yeah. Although I'll be honest. I'm never going to tell Gray the fact that he and I are the same rank.” Dani smiled. “What?” Ukyo smiled wide. “I was a sergeant for some time now. I'm formerly the guard of the wife of the headmaster himself. Blaise and I were the same rank until he got offered this job. But I've not problem letting Gray take all the credit and the responsibilities. He seems like the kind that needs that reinforcement.” Dani nodded at that. “He does. Thanks. You two have really turned into good friends since you came into our lives on this whole thing.” Ukyo pulls up the winged harness off it's hook while Dani hooks the garters to the thigh highs that go with the costume. She starts to help Dani get the wings on and in place. “Thank you, you have turned into a true friend for me at least. Now, let's go take up these photographer's time, before Josh kills someone out here.” At that, we fade out.
