Smile Son

10 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/10/08 The key was in the ignition. I turned it and like always, it started immediately. It never seized to amaze me, just how the mechanism that transported my very body where ever I pleased could work so well with out the single, slightest bit of a problem or error. I was a mechanic of many years and my father had taught me everything I knew, from changing the oil and cleaning the carburetor to working with hydraulic systems and heavy machinery. I was talented at what I could do, but there was one thing that I could never stop thinking about. It wasn’t just thoughts, though. I lived this dream and it was something I had grown to love and became great at, just like mechanics and how machines operated. I felt I had a knack for doing the same thing in the squared circle with my opponent eying me down with out a single idea of the thoughts I put into dismantling them just as I would a car when investigating under the hood for a specific problem or weakness. I looked over to the passenger seat and looked at my son, who in turn gave me a smile and fastened his seat belt like I had always told him to. “Good job, Dan. You’re remembering now.” “You’re not.” Dan nearly jumped and pointed to my side, where I had indeed forgotten to fasten my own seat belt. I had to set a good impression on my son and this was one of the little things that I could only hope he’d take in and one day teach his children. I looked at him as he looked forward through the windshield and out towards the door of the small house before us; I looked out and saw his mother standing at the doorway. She was wearing a pair of tight blue jeans and a white top. It was simple, but she still looked nice. She was my ex and it did no good to look at her in that way, anyway. “You ready, boy,” I asked in a playful tone. He still looked to his mother, waving as I turned back into my seat and put the car into reverse, backing away from the hell I once tolerated, but at that moment I was leaving with my favorite thing ever. The one good thing that was made in that house, the only person I trusted and loved with every ounce of me. He was my beloved son, Daniel J. Matthews. As I looked back out one last time, he walked out and stood next to her, she kissed him in front of me and of all places and times. She may not have known I was looking but I had been. It wasn’t like me to feel that way, but as I still was stuck being single I was almost jealous. I had chances but I just never could fall back onto another slut or whore, it was just difficult to find someone who really could love you and be there at the most important of times, when you really needed that special someone. “I hate him,” he stated out of the blue. I turned to him with a confused expression. “Hate who,” I asked. It was sort of dumb, who else was there to hate in his world and like his father, he used “him” to describe the person. His name was unimportant to either of us, but it wasn’t good for those emotions to be building within a young child. “Scott, I don’t like him, dad.” “What has he done for you to feel that way,” I asked in a mono tone, almost doctoring my son to explain himself in a more clear and precise manner. “He doesn’t play with me or nothing, just mommy I guess.” “I bought you that Xbox so you wouldn’t need his attention.” “I do play with it, dad, but sometimes it just gets boring.” “I can understand that.” “Mom never listens to me.” “She never listened to me, either.” We both giggled. We had a connection like non other, he was so smart for his age and communicating with him was easier then talking to my boss, but when has it ever been easy to talk with the man who keeps the money flowing for your income. “We could stop by Target and pick you up a new game, have any ones in particular that you’ve been wanting to play?” “Well, I don’t know,” he turned and looked at me innocently. Those damn blue eyes were so beautiful, they were his mother’s, however, and sometimes looking into them left me in a state of anger. But not this day, today they looked to be his own, I wasn’t going to allow myself to make any connections back to her, today Dan was mine and I wasn’t going to mess that up. “It’s my treat, Danny boy. I was thinking, I need to spoil you because I know you don’t see me as much as you’d like and I don’t see you as much as I’d like, with my job and all, so why not get you the things that can keep you occupied and out of your mothers hair.” “Well, there is a new game out called Call of Duty 4. All my friends as school say it’s awesome, if there was any that’d be the game, Dad.” He smiled a bit, looking forward out into the world, watching as cars sped by and as buildings came and went as we continued forward. It soon turned into nothing, I kept looking back at him, I wished I could understand what it was like, and it must have been hard for the little man. “I wish you’d come home,” he said, pushing it in a way that could have made me cry. I wasn’t one to be emotional, but it was my son and it was difficult watching as he grew older and I was only allowed to see glimpses of it. I never regretted choosing to be with his mother, but only because Dan wouldn’t be sitting right here next to me if I had chosen differently. “I know the situation, it sucks, I know. Be strong, it’ll get easier as time escalates and sooner than you know it you’ll be out of there and on your own, and when it’s your turn I know you’ll be the best dad. I just want you to know that I love you to death, just remember that, boy.” “I love you too, Dad. Scott will never come close to being as cool as you, don’t even worry about that. Mom doesn’t understand, though, I can feel it in me when he’s around and I just don’t think he’s a good person. Sometimes I just wish you’d get custody of me so I wouldn’t have to be afraid anymore.” I looked over at him and I wondered what there was to be afraid of. Things began to fly through my mind and I was becoming worried at a very rapid pass, and I just knew if that man ever laid a hand on him I’d break him in half. Before I could think about it any longer I found myself at the light right in front of target. But, it still ate away at me. “He doesn’t’hit you, does he?” I asked with a hint of hesitation, but I felt awful for saying it with that tone. I guess I didn’t want him to feel awkward if that wasn’t the case, but the vibe I was receiving could only lead me to the assumption. “No, he’s never laid a hand on me. He always tries to act like we’re friends and all, but I don’t buy it for a second. How can I be friends with him when he treats my mom like she’s just nothing,” he glanced over to me with a concerned face, the look a child yields when something really isn’t right. “He hits her, then?” “I don’t know, but she had a black eye the other day, I could see it right through all of the make-up she was wearing. She said she bumped her eye at night trying to find her way to the bath room at night, she said it was too dark to see and she ran into the corner of the wall.” “She’s lived there for almost ten years; I remember she’d always get up around two a.m. to use the bathroom. I guess it’s possible, are you sure it could be Scott?” He was a smart little guy, couldn’t take that away from him. He still was acting strange; with out an answer I proceed to make the left turn into Target’s parking lot. I slowly crept into a parking space not more than twenty yards away from the complex. “Dan, if anything’s ever wrong you know I’m here, you can tell me. You can talk to me whenever, wherever and even late at night when there’s no one else to turn towards. I’m going to be here for along time, I’m here for you right now and forever, I love you, son.” He nodded his head, “I know, I love you too, Dad, but I just don’t know what to think, he’s not nice when he’s got that bottle in his hands.” I nodded my head thinking about the drunk sleeping with my ex and possibly abusing her at the same time, it was a damn shame. She wasn’t always like she had been for the past few years before our separation, no, she was some what gentle but still with that little kick of sassiness. She was warm to hold and she was someone who was easy to talk with and she had potential to be a great person, I just had no idea where things went wrong, but saying something like that was ignorant, it had been done for a while now and I wasn’t in the right mind set to be thinking of anything of the sort. No, Dan was the only thing I could miss and someone who I in the end truly needed. “He’s a drunk; you stay away from that stuff, Dan. I’m talkin” about later in life, it’s no good and it only drags you down.” I looked at him and he seemed to understand with out a doubt, “C’mon, boy’let’s go.” I opened my door and locked it before slamming it into place again. My son followed my lead as we both slowly stretched onward towards the doorway. “So, how’s the business going,” He asked, a smile brewing finally to my surprise. I thought about the question for a brief moment, just to recap on who I truly was up against, a man named Andy Murray, unknown on my behalf but obviously the favorite in the eyes of the public. “Well, I got a match this week. I was meaning to ask you something, though.” “What?” “I got a seat next to me on the plane; it’s open for another passenger. Anyways, I was wondering if you knew anyone who might want to join me on the way to the event.” A smile crossed his face again, he leaped towards me and grasped onto my legs. I dragged him the remainder of the way before he leaped off with excitement. “Me! You’re the best, Dad. I just can’t believe you’re in GCW, I watch it all the time and now my Dad is actually going to be a star!” We walked into the bright store, white and red features decorated the walls while manikins stood in front of clothing selections and signs directed customers to their destinations. “I don’t know about all of that, I’m not even close to being the star. It’ll happen though, count on that my little man.” I grappled with him for a short moment before people began to stare; we laughed and proceeded towards the gaming isle. “There it is,” he ran towards Call of Duty 4, grabbing it with one hand and raising it into the air with two. “Jeez, it’s that good?” He nodded his head at me and ran out with the quickness. It was easier then expected and the smile showing was amazing. It was odd the way Dan made me feel about things. I was more appetitive towards the things I did have and even if it wasn’t for as long as I would have liked, spending time with him was essential to my own happiness and well-being. I need to be prepared for my match. My son would be present, showing any sign of weakness wasn’t an option and allowing him to see anything less then my best was out of the question. I was ready, a war had just commenced and my Son was my commander and chief, helping to lead my heart to a victory like none other. “Marvelous” Mark Matthews was ready to show his bad side, but Dan loved it more than anyone.
