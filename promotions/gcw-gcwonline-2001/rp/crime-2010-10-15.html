Crime!

15 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/15/10 INT. THE CELLAR NIGHTCLUB - NIGHT Inside the jam-packed nightclub the Cellar in downtown San Francisco, the beautiful people congregate on the dance floor, hopped up on overpriced alcohol, jumping around to the sounds of popular hip hop artists. Amongst the young ones, TOMMY, who looks good but can’t act worth a damn, drinks with his friends and looks like a goon. He soon spots AMBER, a raven-haired beauty that likely blew the casting director to get the few lines she has, who’s with her own friends. Amidst bright lights, loud popular music, and an unrealistic presentation of an average real life club scene, the two of them are making out on the dance floor. Before long... INT. AMBER’S APARTMENT BUILDING, HALLWAY - NIGHT The two slobber over each other like their plane was crashing into the ocean in the hallway. They make it to her apartment door and he presses against her. She laughs amidst his lip attack, digging into her purse for her keys. She swings open the door, and they go inside. INT. AMBER’S APARTMENT, ENTRANCE - NIGHT She kicks off her heels in the hallway and tosses her bag onto a end table. Tommy locks the door and follows her to the living room. INT. AMBER’S APARTMENT, LIVING ROOM - NIGHT Amber wanders off into the kitchen, as Tommy plops down on the leather couch. AMBER (O.S.) Make yourself at home. Tommy fumbles through the girly magazines on the coffee table in front of him. AMBER (O.S.) You want something to drink? TOMMY Beer me, baby! He’s all smiles as he leans back on the couch, kicking his feet up on the table. His head swivels around, and he takes in the place. TOMMY You got a nice place, baby. AMBER (O.S.) Thanks. After a few moments alone, a hand presses down on Tommy’s shoulder. He closes his eyes. TOMMY Aww, baby. You know what I like. But something is not right about that hand... INT. AMBER’S APARTMENT, KITCHEN - NIGHT Amber jumps around the kitchen, filling a glass with white wine. She opens up her fridge and looks for a beer, but finds none. AMBER Hey, I don’t have any beer. Is wine okay? She tilts her head for a response. Nothing comes. She shrugs. AMBER Wine it is. She pours another glass, and puts the bottle away. She grabs both glasses, and makes the short walk into the living room. INT. AMBER’S APARTMENT, LIVING ROOM - NIGHT She walks behind the couch, to behind where Tommy’s sitting. AMBER Here’s your wine. He says nothing. AMBER Hey. You awake? She smacks her elbow into the back of Tommy’s head. Nothing again. She groans, and walks around the couch to face him. AMBER You better not have passed out, you damned-- The glasses tumble out of her hand onto the carpet below her in slow motion, the droplets flying in the air in fake time. She covers her mouth. Blood stains the front of Tommy’s shirt. His open eyes stare forward. His throat, slashed. AMBER Ohmygod! At the doorway, at the entrance, the SILHOUETTE KILLER stands, dressed in black with a black hood up and a black faceless mask. In one hand is a blood-stained knife. In the other, a gas can. She keeps backing away, as he stalks forward. AMBER Please. Please! She hits the wall and slides down along it. Her eyes go wide. AMBER NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! And with a whoosh and a jump cut... INT. AMBER’S APARTMENT, LIVING ROOM - LATER Uniformed cops move about the scene of death, trying not to get in the way of DET. ALEXIS THORPE, a young blonde Crime Scene tech, taking pictures of everything. Of Tommy’s corpse. Of the magazines on the table. Of the still-smoldering body of Amber. Of the drapes on the wall. Of the television set. Of pretty much everything. Her picture taking journey takes her out of the room. Meanwhile, M.E. GINA STONE, the Medical Examiner and another beautiful young miscast blonde, examines the dead bodies thoroughly and with small flashlights and other medical examiner trinkets. In to some minor fan fare, in walks LT. TRIUMPH FROST, in a black suit and dark sunglasses. He spots DET. OSCAR DEL TORO, a lothario detective-slash-Crime Scene tech tossed in for some 18-49 female demographic flavor. LT. TRIUMPH FROST What’s the word, Del Toro? Del Toro flips through his notepad. DET. OSCAR DEL TORO Neighbor smelled something burning, called 9-1-1. Fire department called us. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Why don’t you go find someone that knows what happened. DET. OSCAR DEL TORO You got it, boss. Del Toro walks out to the hallway. Triumph sees Dr. Stone from afar, and adjusts his shades. Time seems to stop as he walks over to her, while she surveys the husk that used to be Amber. Triumph kneels down next to M.E. Stone and takes off his sunglasses. LT. TRIUMPH FROST What do you have? M.E. GINA STONE Two vics. One causasian male, one unidentified, assumed to be the female apartment owner. The male... M.E. Stone points over to Tommy. M.E. GINA STONE ...took a blade across the throat. A unnecessary zoom shot into the wound, and the shot zooms back out just as fast. M.E. GINA STONE Death was near instant. The female... A zoom in to her charred body that seems to go on forever. M.E. GINA STONE ...well, you know. The zoom flies out again. Triumph nods. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Good work, Stone. He goes to put his hand on her ass, but she turns her head and he shoves the hand in his pocket. He stands, rising very slowly, and walks into the kitchen. INT. AMBER’S APARTMENT, KITCHEN - NIGHT In the kitchen, Alexis talks to RANDOM POLICE OFFICER #6, and the officer nods and leaves. She surveys the kitchen, before turning and spotting Triumph. She half smiles. DET. ALEXIS THORPE Glad you could make it. Triumph stays stone-faced. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Sorry. Triple homicide at an orgy gone wrong. Nasty business. Triumph softens that hard exterior. But just barely. He takes off his sunglasses. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Some six-year-old won’t be playing patty-cake with mommy again. DET. ALEXIS THORPE Good thing for them you’re on the case, huh? Triumph slowly puts his sunglasses back on. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Good thing for everyone. Triumph looks around. LT. TRIUMPH FROST What do you have for me, Thorpe? Alexis flips through her notebook, pushing her hair back under her ear. DET. ALEXIS THORPE Girl’s name is Amber McGillicutty, aged 23. Wannabe model and actress. Lived alone. Receipts from a local club dated from tonight. Triumph wanders around the kitchen, opening up cabinets randomly for some reason. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Signs of forced entry? DET. ALEXIS THORPE None. Looks like an inside job. He slams a cupboard door shut. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Anything else out of the ordinary? Alexis points over to a Styrofoam food container. DET. ALEXIS THORPE Just a half-eaten turkey burger from the fridge. Triumph walks over and opens it up, taking a peek. DET. ALEXIS THORPE Cooked medium-rare. He closes the container, and reaches into his jacket pocket, grabbing his shades. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Well I guess someone... He puts his sunglasses back on. LT. TRIUMPH FROST ...Wanted her well-done. YEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHH!!!! EXT. DOWNTOWN SAN FRANCISCO - NIGHT Over shots of downtown San Francisco at night and the cast doing cop-related nonsense, FAMOUS OLD SONG by BAND THAT HASN’T BEEN RELEVANT IN AGES blasts out. The credits roll for... Crime! Starring... Triumph Frost as Lieutenant Triumph Frost (who swings around and puts on his sunglasses). Alexis Frost as Detective Alexis Thorpe (who click-clacks away at a keyboard in some lavish tech office). Juan Sanchez as Detective Oscar Del Toro (who adjusts various knobs on a microscope). Sally Ford as Medical Examiner Gina Stone (who carves into a cadaver with a scalpel). With Garbage Bag Johnny as Captain GBJ (who’s on the phone and slamming his fist down repeatedly on the desk in his office). And Andy Murray Jr. as Detective Professor Dr. Murr Jr (who messes around with a beaker filled with red liquid). The group walks down a hallway together with purpose, as CRIME! appears in large white block letters. INT. POLICE HEADQUARTERS, STRATEGY ROOM - NIGHT In an elaborate blue-hued multi-million dollar tech-filled room that no police station in the country could ever afford, Triumph, Alexis, and Del Toro sit at the roundtable, while CAPTAIN GBJ, the bearded hard-boiled veteran cop, stands in front a large plasma TV screen. GBJ holds a remote in his hand and the television cuts to three beautiful blondes, one of them Amber. CAPTAIN GBJ Amber McGillicutty. Sarah Jones. Michelle Ganz. Three girls, all blonde, all in their mid-20’s. GBJ flips through the pictures of their crime scenes. CAPTAIN GBJ All three stabbed repeatedly. All three immolated. This, guys... He ends up on all three charred bodies, side-by-side. CAPTAIN GBJ Really grinds my gears. Do we have anything? LT. TRIUMPH FROST I do. Triumph takes off his sunglasses and places them deftly on the table in front of him. All attention turns to him. LT. TRIUMPH FROST It’s the Silhouette Killer. GBJ closes his eyes and shakes his head from side to side, as Del Toro groans. Alexis sits, surprised. DET. ALEXIS THORPE The Silhouette Killer? DET. OSCAR DEL TORO Before your time, kid. GBJ holds up the remote, and flips to a sketch of a man clad all in black, much like the one seen earlier. CAPTAIN GBJ Six months before I brought you on, some creepy psycho in all black went around murdering blondes. He’d get in their houses, undetected, stab them, and set them on fire. GBJ keeps flipping through the pictures. CAPTAIN GBJ Eight girls murdered in the span of six weeks. Outside of them all being blondes, the pattern was completely random. No relations, no nothing. And then, just like that, he stopped. DET. ALEXIS THORPE So why the Silhouette Killer? GBJ flips back to the sketch. CAPTAIN GBJ There was only one witness that saw him flee a scene. That is what she told the sketch artist she saw. DET. ALEXIS THORPE No fingerprints? No hairs? No semen or blood? CAPTAIN GBJ Not a follicle of DNA. Triumph grabs his sunglasses off the table. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Well it looks like... Triumph puts his shades back on. LT. TRIUMPH FROST ...He’s back in black. Everyone pauses for a moment. No one says anything. GBJ coughs. CAPTAIN GBJ Well, if he’s out there, we better stop him this time. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Don’t worry, Cap. Triumph stands up, and adjusts his sunglasses. LT. TRIUMPH FROST I’m on the case. He turns around and pushes through the double glass doors of the strategy room. Silence ensues for a few moments, as Alexis and Del Toro share a quick, albeit confused, look. DET. ALEXIS THORPE So, uhh...should we...follow him? Del Toro shrugs and nods, as they exit. They brush past a small simian creature in a tweed jacket, a white dress shirt, a blue and white striped tie, and a pair of thick-rimmed glasses. Who happens to be DETECTIVE PROFESSOR DOCTOR MURR JR. GBJ spots him out of the corner of his eye and calls out. CAPTAIN GBJ Hey, Murr Jr! Murr Jr. stops and waddles into the room. He jumps up on one of the chairs and spins around a few times. Captain GBJ gets steamed and stops the chair. CAPTAIN GBJ Detective Professor Doctor Murr Jr! Murr Jr. tilts his head to the side. GBJ points. CAPTAIN GBJ There’s a suspect in the Presidio slayings in interrogation room two. Get over there and get a confession. Murr Jr. nods his head rapidly, and jumps out of the chair, and waddles over towards the interrogation room. GBJ strokes his chin. CAPTAIN GBJ That guy really chaps my hide. GBJ continues to stroke his chin. INT. POLICE HEADQUARTERS, TECH LAB - NIGHT Triumph stands near the exit, while Alexis and Del Toro are in front of their stations. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Well, guys...you know what to do. Triumph pulls out his sunglasses and puts them on. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Find me something good. Triumph leaves, and Alexis passes by Del Toro. DET. ALEXIS THORPE Let’s get to work. Suddenly, we get... MONTAGE OF CRIME TECH STUFF A) Alexis pours through the shots of the crime scenes, using a touch screen to send them all over. B) Del Toro looks up all of the victims credit card records and bank statements, cross-referencing them with each other. C) Alexis picks out photos of the charred bodies and calls up a mapping program. She types in “knife wounds”, and the program goes nuts, posting dots all over the place. D) Del Toro and Alexis sit at a table, conversing while they eat sandwiches. E) Alexis’ program finally finishes analyzing the body. On the screen, in bright red letters “MATCH FOUND” flashes. END MONTAGE Both Del Toro and Alexis look at the flashing words, and high-five. INT. POLICE HEADQUARTERS, INTERROGATION ROOM #2 - NIGHT Inside the interrogation room, TOAD, a large gruff brute, sits handcuffed to the table. He whistles. TOAD Someone get me a cheeseburger! The door swings open, and shuts. Toad tries to see who it is over the table, but can’t. Toad gets a bit weirded out as the chair pulls out. But when he sees Murr Jr. jump up on the chair across from him, he laughs. TOAD You guys gotta be jokin’! Murr Jr. pulls out a piece of paper and a pen and slides it across the table. It’s a confession sheet. Toad raises an eyebrow. TOAD What’s this? Toad reads it over, and shakes his head. TOAD I ain’t signin’ this. I didn’t do nothin’ wrong. Murr Jr. stares a hole through Toad. TOAD You can’t prove anything, pig. Toad leans forward, and hocks a loogie in Murr Jr.’s face. Murr Jr. pulls out a hankerchief and wipes his face down, and puts it back in his pocket. He jumps off the chair and begins to circle around Toad. He stops, holding up a finger, looking like he’s going to say something. But he says nothing, instead lowering his finger and continuing to pace around. He pauses on the other side of Toad. TOAD You cops ain’t nothin’, you know that? You’re all just a bunch of punks! Murr Jr. stops, and slowly paces towards Toad, his eyes never wavering from Toad. He gets closer and closer. TOAD W-what’re you doin’, man? Why you lookin’ at me like that? He stops in front of Toad, and merely glares at him. Sweat pours down Toad’s forehead. TOAD Stop lookin’ at me! Stop-- Without warning, Murr Jr. leaps at Toad and attacks. Toad screams, as Murr Jr. scratches away relentlessly. TOAD Aah!!! Aaah! Fine! Fine! I did it! I killed ‘em! HEEEELLLP!!! Murr Jr. has his confession, but won’t stop attacking. Suddenly, GBJ bursts into the room, pissed beyond all belief. CAPTAIN GBJ Murr Jr.! Murr Jr. stops, and looks over at his captain. CAPTAIN GBJ My office. Now!!! GBJ leaves. Murr Jr. gives Toad one last look, before leaping off of him and following his captain. INT. POLICE HEADQUARTERS, CAPTAIN GBJ’S OFFICE - NIGHT Murr Jr. jumps up on the chair, and GBJ slams his glass door shut. GBJ sits at his desk, and slams his fist on the table. CAPTAIN GBJ Damn it, Murr Jr! You can’t go around attacking suspects! Murr Jr. eeks, but GBJ slams his fist down again. CAPTAIN GBJ I don’t give a damn that you got the confession! The DA will chew my ass out raw when it gets thrown out in court! And now, I gotta let this guy go! Murr Jr. eeks some more. GBJ rubs his eyes. CAPTAIN GBJ I know he admitted to the murders. And we’ll try to nail the sonuva bitch, but you’ve gone off the rails lately! You’re a damn maverick, Murr Jr., and you’ve really been burning my toast! GBJ suddenly gets soft for a second. CAPTAIN GBJ We all know how bad you’re hurting. Losing your wife and son in that deep sea fishing accident, I sympathize, trust me. Murr Jr. suddenly flies into a rage and slaps the table repeatedly, squealing incessantly. CAPTAIN GBJ Fine, step-son, whatever. But damn it, Murr Jr, they’re dead! And they ain’t comin’ back! Murr Jr. slightly calms down. CAPTAIN GBJ Look, just go home, get some rest. Come back in a few days when you have your head on straight. GBJ picks up the phone and dials randomly. CAPTAIN GBJ Yeah, I’ll hold. Murr Jr. slinks out of the chair and opens the door, where a small crowd has gathered. GBJ spots them. CAPTAIN GBJ Get back to work, all’a ya’s! The crowd scatters, as Murr Jr. walks through the parting. INT. POLICE HEADQUARTERS, BULLPEN - NIGHT Amidst his fellow officers, Murr Jr. spots a few officers staring at him. He screeches at them and they scatter and he continues on. Watching intently are OFFICER MANDY and OFFICER SANDY, two unrealistically hot twin female cops. OFFICER MANDY He’s such a brave guy! Losing his loved ones, all after getting fired from his university teaching job. OFFICER SANDY Not to mention that he’s a doctor. OFFICERS MANDY AND SANDY What a rogue. They swoon, and he passes them by, soldiering on out into the world. INT. MURR JR’S APARTMENT - NIGHT The inside of Murr Jr.’s apartment is rather sad. Empty beer and liquor bottles, boxes of Chinese takeout and delivery pizza litter his one-room place that consists of a small bed, a ratty couch, a tiny kitchen, and a crappy TV. Murr Jr. walks around in a pair of tighty-whiteys, his hair unkempt, a bottle of Whiskey in his hand. Somber music plays in the background when he takes a sip and wipes his face. He stumbles over to a mantle where pictures are lined up of his loved ones. He picks up a picture of his wife, a big breasted beauty, and a tear streams down his cheek. He puts the picture down and picks up a picture of a young kid holding a baseball bat. He shrugs, flips it over his back, and the frame shatters on the wall. He picks back up the picture of his wife and jumps on the bed. He cradles the picture against his chest and curls up into the fetal position. INT. POLICE HEADQUARTERS, TECH LAB - NIGHT Triumph enters back into the room, and walks over to Alexis and Del Toro. Alexis pulls up the screen. DET. ALEXIS THORPE We have a match on the knife wounds. Same blade used. DET. OSCAR DEL TORO We traced the make, only one place sells the knife. A gun shop down near Fisherman's Wharf. Triumph nods and looks over at Alexis. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Let’s take a little field trip, then. Alexis heads out the door, with Triumph following. INT. GUN SHOP - NIGHT Triumph and Alexis walk into the gun shop where GARVIN, the good ol’ boy owner, complete with his plaid vest with the sleeves ripped off and sweat-stained wife-beater, stands behind the counter. They walk up to him. GARVIN What can I do for y’all? Alexis pulls out a picture of the knife and shows it to Garvin. DET. ALEXIS THORPE Do you sell this knife? GARVIN Why, that’s a mighty big knife for a mighty tiny lady. DET. ALEXIS THORPE Don’t play dumb, you smelly redneck. Do you or don’t you? GARVIN Sassy girl. Garvin gives the knife a once-over. GARVIN S’pose I mighta. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Who’d you sell it to? Garvin shows off his yellow-stained mouth with a grin. GARVIN I can’t recollect. Triumph eyes over Garvin. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Well maybe I charge you with obstruction of justice. Triumph crosses his arms, as Garvin’s grin goes wider. LT. TRIUMPH FROST I might know of a few people in prison that might like to clean up that mouth of yours. Garvin shuts his mouth quickly. GARVIN Alright, now. Don’t go gettin’ all wordy on me. Lemme see that photo again. Alexis hands him the picture, and he eyes it over. GARVIN Yeah, I recollect sellin’ this here knife to a fella. Odd guy. Wore all black. Hood, mask, all’a that. Paid in cash, though. DET. ALEXIS THORPE Did you get a name? GARVIN Heh. Never thought to ask. Triumph leans over the counter. LT. TRIUMPH FROST We need to see your security tapes. INT. GUN AND KNIFE SHOP, BACK ROOM - NIGHT In the back room, Garvin cues up the security tape, where, sure enough, it shows the Silhouette Killer buying the knife in his full-on garb. Triumph turns around to Garvin. LT. TRIUMPH FROST We need a moment. Garvin nods and leaves. Alexis and Triumph study the tape, re-winding and re-watching. LT. TRIUMPH FROST So, how’s your kid doing? DET. ALEXIS THORPE He’s alright. Hasn’t seen you in awhile. LT. TRIUMPH FROST You know me. I am the job. DET. ALEXIS THORPE You should come over for dinner sometime. He’d like that. Triumph stares intently at the screen, to the point of squinting. LT. TRIUMPH FROST We’ll...see. Triumph squints some more, and pulls back quickly. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Rewind it again. Alexis complies, and rewinds the tape. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Pause it...now. She pauses upon his command. The Silhouette Killer has the money on the counter with his hand over it, but a set of keys hangs out from his pocket, nearly dangling onto the ground. LT. TRIUMPH FROST I know those keys. Alexis cocks her head to the side. Triumph looks over at her. LT. TRIUMPH FROST I know where he lives. Triumph hurries to the exit. She examines the grainy video feed, and simply doesn’t understand. DET. ALEXIS THORPE Wait, how can you even tell? LT. TRIUMPH FROST Call it... He pauses for effect. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Intuition. And then he leaves. Suddenly, Alexis finds herself scrambling after him to keep up. INT. MURR JR’S APARTMENT - NIGHT Murr Jr. clutches the bottle, sprawled out on his ratty couch. He flips through the channels on the TV with the hand holding the remote, the picture of his well-endowed wife resting face-up on his chest. The phone rings, and Murr Jr. picks it up. CAPTAIN GBJ (O.S.) Murr Jr.! You there? Murr Jr. grumbles into the receiver. CAPTAIN GBJ (O.S.) We need you back at the precinct. Are you sober right now? You damn well better be. You better not be scratching my-- Murr Jr. whips the phone at the wall, and it shatters into a shower of plastic pieces. Murr Jr. sighs and goes back to the television. Before long, he’s on a newscast. NEWSCASTER (O.S.) Breaking news. There was another slaying in the Presidio tonight. This time, an elderly couple on a midnight stroll. Murr Jr.’s eyes slowly go wide. NEWSCASTER (O.S.) SFPD are withholding the names, but that brings the body count up to five in the past three nights. The police were said to have a suspect but released him for reasons unknown. Again, that’s five-- Murr Jr. turns the television off. There’s a new look on his face beating out the depression: passion. Murr Jr. looks at the bottle and dumps it out on the floor and springs into action. He jumps into his closet and, with right quickness, gets dressed in all black, as if going undercover. He also pulls out an almost comically massive .357 Magnum, cocking the hammer and shoves it in his shoulder holster. Murr Jr. kicks open his front door, and goes on the hunt. EXT. STARLIGHT APARTMENTS - NIGHT Alexis and Triumph stand at the entryway of the apartment building Triumph swears the Silhouette Killer lives in. Alexis rolls her head around, antsy all around. DET. ALEXIS THORPE I still don’t think this is where he lives. Triumph doesn’t say anything, as Alexis just keeps on talking. DET. ALEXIS THORPE I mean, I couldn’t see anything in that video, and it’s my job to break down video all day long. How you could ever see figure out that he lives here is completely and utterly illogical in every facet of the word. As she keeps talking, suddenly Triumph spots him. It’s the Silhouette Killer, in his black hood and mask and outfit, carrying bags of groceries. He spots Triumph, drops his groceries, and bolts the other way. Triumph swings to Alexis. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Call for backup. I’ve got him. Triumph gives chase, as Alexis pulls out her phone and dials. EXT. SAN FRANCISCO STREETS - NIGHT Through the streets of late-night San Francisco, the Silhouette Killer chugs along, shoving people out of his way as Triumph gives chase. The Silhouette Killer ducks into an alleyway and leaps over a chain link fence, and a few moments later so does Triumph, still hot on his tail. When they get out to the streets, Triumph nearly loses the Silhouette Killer before catching him duck around a corner and bolts after him. Before long, Triumph finds himself standing in front of an abandoned warehouse. INT. ABANDONED WAREHOUSE - NIGHT Triumph enters and draws his pistol. He stalks through the dank building, aiming his pistol at anything that might even flush out the slightest of movement. He ducks behind a grouping of boxes, and breathes softly before jumping out, his gun pointed upward. The Silhouette Killer holds a YOUNG GIRL in his arms, his knife pressed against her neck. LT. TRIUMPH FROST It’s over. Sirens sound outside the building, the blue and red lights flashing into the window. The Silhouette Killer’s head dances all about, and his attention jumps back to Triumph. LT. TRIUMPH FROST There’s nowhere to go. Drop the knife and end this already. SILHOUETTE KILLER Never! The Silhouette Killer presses the knife into her neck, drawing blood. Triumph holds up his gun, and slowly places it down on the ground. LT. TRIUMPH FROST I’m putting down my gun. I’m unarmed. Triumph raises back up, his arms raised. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Just let her go, and this will all be over. The Silhouette Killer shakes, his arm bouncing about. His voice trembles. SILHOUETTE KILLER You have no idea what it’s like! Nobody respects me! Nobody cares about me! LT. TRIUMPH FROST It’s alright. I understand. SILHOUETTE KILLER No! You! Don’t! He points the knife at Triumph before bringing it back to her neck. SILHOUETTE KILLER Nobody even knows who I am! Nobody! Well, now they will! Ha ha ha!!! The Silhouette Killer rips off his hood and his black mask to reveal... Barry Goldstein! Goldstein is all smiles, as he cackles like a maniac. BARRY GOLDSTEIN They’ll all know my name now! Barry Goldstein! Goldstein holds the knife straight up into the air. BARRY GOLDSTEIN No one will forget it ever again! Triumph squints his eyes. LT. TRIUMPH FROST Who are you, again? BARRY GOLDSTEIN Are...are you serious? Triumph gives him a blank stare in return. Goldstein grits his teeth, growls, and throws the girl to the ground. He points the knife at Triumph. BARRY GOLDSTEIN Darn you! I just told you, I’m Barry-- Triumph unnecessarily rolls forward, but when he comes up, he has his gun in his hand. Goldstein’s eyes go wide. BANG! BANG! BANG! BANG! BANG! BANG BANG BANG BANG-BANG-BANG-BANG-BANG-BANG-BANG-BANG! Triumph empties his clip into Goldstein, who drops to the ground, the knife tumbling out of his hand. He musters up his last words through blood. BARRY GOLDSTEIN Gold...stein... Goldstein dies. Triumph brushes himself off. He pulls out his sunglasses. LT. TRIUMPH FROST I guess... He puts on his sunglasses. LT. TRIUMPH FROST No one will forget you now. GBJ appears. He looks down at the dead Barry Goldstein. CAPTAIN GBJ So it was Goldstein all along. That guy always razzed my berries. LT. TRIUMPH FROST The Silhouette Killer has finally been put to rest. CAPTAIN GBJ Seems that way. GBJ turns to Triumph. CAPTAIN GBJ Good work, Lieutenant. LT. TRIUMPH FROST All in a day’s work, Cap. Murr Jr. appears from the shadows and sidles up next to GBJ. GBJ looks at him, seeing that he’s covered in blood. CAPTAIN GBJ You solved the Presidio murders? Murr Jr. nods. CAPTAIN GBJ You’re damn fine police, Murr Jr. Murr Jr. eeks loudly. CAPTAIN GBJ Sorry. Detective Professor Doctor Murr Jr. Murr Jr. nods his head, and all three stand stoic over hole-filled body of Barry Goldstein. INT. SPORTS BAR - NIGHT At a table, Triumph, Alexis, Del Toro, M.E. Stone, GBJ, and Murr Jr. sit around a few pitchers of beer, their glasses full, yet they all have semi-serious looks on their faces. CAPTAIN GBJ Well, we finally got the Silhouette Killer. I’m still a bit shocked it was Goldstein all along. I never thought he had it in ‘em. DET. OSCAR DEL TORO No doubt, boss. DET. ALEXIS THORPE Didn’t see it coming. M.E. GINA STONE That guy was a weirdo. Murr Jr. slams his hands on the table, and GBJ runs his hand through Murr Jr.’s hair. CAPTAIN GBJ No, we didn’t forget you solved that murder case, too!
