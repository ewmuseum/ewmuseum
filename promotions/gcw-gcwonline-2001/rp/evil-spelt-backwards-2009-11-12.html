Rays of light break through the retreating clouds, sending streaks over the trees like the rising sun on Japan's famed flag. Life... Life finally seems to be restored as if a waring battalion just left town and everyone comes out to observe the damage. But unlike the town you probably have envisioned, this sanctuary's life is only of wildlife and one lone man rowing across a lake freely. Zacharia's boat cuts through the placid water as a duck would on a pond. Each stroke of the row is nothing short of mechanic, propelling the boat forward flawlessly. The nose of the boat seems to have its own sense of direction as Zacharia pushes along a well known route. He spots out a couple landmarks that help him navigate his way around the large lake, but stops further down as he looks around for the large crablike stump that is usually protruding from the water. He turns around in the boat and begins to reverse row so he can see where he's going and notices something out of his routine. He thought he was towards the treeline of the swamp, but the heavy rains over the last week made the water rise so much that the swamp has been swallowed by the lake. Taylor checks the receding clouds before navigating through the trees. After ten minutes of maneuvering with delicate strokes on the tender path, Zacharia finds himself on the bank of a hulking mass of charred wood and tumbled stone walls. Taylor uses his oars to push himself into the wreckage that has been claimed by the high waters of the swamp. Wildlife has claimed this as their home over the dormant years. Pews lay in ruins with vines and moss growing over them, walls seem worn and torn from burrowers, and the alter is in shambles from a fallen ceiling. Taylor turns his boat into the sunken center isle of the church and plants his boat on a muddy bank. Taylor steps out onto broken floor boards and takes in everything around him. Behind the soot and wines lies the remains of a ministry that seems to be long forgotten. Taylor stands in an awestruck state as the church makes its peace with the first visitor in ages. Although extreme shadows are cast along the remaining walls, the rest of the church is oddly lit. Patchy holes in the ceiling from the trebuchets of time provide a dusty light of endless fine particles, but a very warm and welcoming light. Although mostly destroyed, it holds a sort of religious prestige in Taylor's eyes. Taylor finds himself walking forward with an objective. He makes his way towards one of the few still standing pews and takes a seat with the most gentle of motions. The pew holds up against his body weight with a few creaks but no more of a surprise than any other pew even in a perfectly good church. He moves his foot in closer and kicks a charred book, casting it across the floor, leaving the binding behind. He leans over and cautiously picks up the fragile book, but only a few pages follow his hands up into reading view. Jasmine 2:19 "...and unto the morning light, darkness shall fall. Thou shall retreat from the darkness but fear not the Man in Black for that of God shall conquer..." The rest of the passage is aged beyond recognition. Taylor is unfamiliar with the passage and even the name Jasmine from the scriptures. As he turns over to the remains of the cover to see the name someone clears their throat behind him. He quickly turns around so fast that the weathered pew loses its strength and breaks, sending Taylor into the soot ridden floor boards. By the time Taylor is able to readjust himself to look behind him, nobody is there. Taylor very slowly stands up and stares at his boat which is as still at the depths of darkness. His heart begins to beat a little faster with every second. He clenches his fists so tight they turn white. He very slowly turns around, ready for anything, but not this... Upon the broken alter is a man completely cloaked in black. His head rests on the side of the one half still in the air and his feet rest across the remaining half as if lounging the wrong way on a chair. Chills creep down his spine as he comes into full realization that the man isn't moving an inch, not even breathing.... For the first time in recent memory, Taylor is absolutely terrified. Taylor trembles in fear, slowly rocking back and forth. Out of desperation, he raises his hands to his face and begins to pray, or rather plead with God. He closes his eyes, casting all he knows in darkness and tries to separate himself from the world, but the silence finds its way into his being and begins to grow louder. The longer in this state, the more pain he finds himself in. The more pain he's in, the more he starts to rock back and forth. Please Lord, present thyself to the darkness and bring light. Bring light into this world of trickery and darkness. Please Lord, bring thy heaven's wrath to this darkness... Taylor pushes against the pain in his ears but it becomes too much and his silence is broken by the agonizing cries from deep within. He looks up at the alter, still rocking back and forth with his adrenaline spiking, and sees all but what he expected to see... ...nothing... No cloaked man, no wrath of God.... ...nothing... The church remains charred and alone, just like Zacharia. He stares up at the alter seeing the shrouded body's outline in memory with the burned up fallen cross in the background. He struggles to get himself together and struggles to lift his foot in step, but an unknown force his holding him in place. Sobs begin to develop inside him as his heart begins to slow and tears finally fall. A ripple of water in the lake behind him breaks him out of the erie silence and he successfully puts one foot in front of the other and gets to the next standing pew and takes a seat. Letting out a very slow, stuttering breath, he rubs away a tear on his cheek, smearing soot across his raw skin. God has mysterious ways to express acknowledgment or purpose...
