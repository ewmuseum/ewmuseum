Welcome To Reality

26 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/26/06 Despite walking out victorious at Devils Night after a grueling match against Dies Irae, Tessa Windsor had to stay around the Toyota Center in Houston and celebrate. After all, only one of the three people that walked into that match on her side, had managed to walk out without assistance. But even Peter Vetra, the solitary soul that made it out without a battle wound, had plenty of his own matters to worry about. While he and Tessa had scheduled to depart together aboard Delta Flight 87 bound for Boston, there was little reason for him to stick around. Just like his fellow stable mate Bradley, he’d felt like he’d been singled out of the odd equation of Sombra, Ciel, and Windsor, though he’d never uttered a word among the others about feeling this way. Thus in the end, Ascension was proving to be nothing more than a joke. A joke that centered on the equation Tessa having friends with semi successful careers in the business and the fact that they were all friends with her. Though the Ascension wasn’t her child, she’d been pinned by Lestatt Knight and Eva Van Lourne, to fly GCW’s flag next. What they saw in her, she’d never understood, and probably never would. So she turned towards the only other member of Ascension that had stuck around the locker room that evening, to provide her with those kinds of answers. He seemed to know it all when it came to peoples philosophies. “Sky, have you ever wondered why Eva and Knight pushed so hard to make me into a leader?” Collapsing back against the concrete wall behind her, if Sky’s attention hadn’t been caught by her quiet question, it certainly was caught by the hollowed sound that echoed throughout the room then. As always, Skylar Green’s radar had detected a problem bubbling inside his smaller colleagues mind, and disregarded the small goose egg surely growing on the back of her head, “Do you want the obvious answer, or the answer that’d send your self esteem on a trip to cloud nine?” Tessa always was a rather indecisive woman, so she opted for the option that had yet to be mentioned, “How about both?” “Well lets see,” Licking his index finger, the wrestler known as Ciel to GCW went to work on his imaginary glossary, shuffling his fingers through the air until his stumbled upon his answer, “Tessa is well acquainted with the four others on her side.” Led by a heavy sigh, Tessa’s shoulders fell a few inches more towards the ground as Sky continued on the parade of ideas that Tessa had heard a million times. If she’d wanted a better reason for leading Ascension, it seemed she was shit out of luck, or she’d have to find a deeper connection between her question and the answers given. “And Tessa?” “I know,” She cut off semi irritated not by her closest acquaintance, but rather that she was seemingly the only one that couldn’t add two and two together and get four, “I know that I’m the one that needs the boost to the top. But maybe I’m just cut out for that kind of crap.” Yet she received the same retort that she’d gotten every time she found mediocrity acceptable, “Self doubt’s going to get you nowhere.” Then of course the thirty second silence came before she caved in with another predictable response, “I know.” “Just embrace the opportunity and learn to accept the pressure that comes with it.” Tessa, however, chose to embrace Ciel. His words were just another thing in her life that was so much easier said by others, than actually done by her. “You’re right”.” But her thoughts were put on hold as that golden opportunity had slashed back at her, and torn her away from her comfort. There standing in the doorway looking just as susceptible as Tessa, was Tessa’s newest recruiting attempt. “Mind if we talk?” Questioned the timid Tiara Belle Russell, who poked only her head in though the doorway. Given that Tiara’s business was obviously none of his business, Ciel dismissed himself with a pat on Tessa’s back, “My phones on if you need me. I’ll see you round next week.” Lifting her hands out of her lap only slightly, the pint sized diva dismissed Ciel with an appreciative smile and wave, then directed her attention to Tiara who slowly crept her way into the room, “What brings you here?” “Aside from your offer,” easing down beside Tessa, the younger woman’s anxiety shown thought brighter than the sun, “I had a bit of a personal question for you.” Having been the new girl around many a place, she understood Tiaras tension and did her best to ease it by offering up a warm smile, “Shoot. I’d be more than willing to give you an answer.” Tiara clearly took little comfort in that as she too fell back against the concrete wall, “What keeps you around here?” It certainly was a bit left field, “Huh?” The anxiety worked it way to her fingers which tied themselves into a knot, “I mean, what keeps you from going out, getting pregnant, and focusing on the whole family bit.” While neither of Tessa’s kids of her own, she’d certainly understood the premise of Tiara’s question. It seems that her new found friend had been bitten by a nasty little bug called the “Baby Bug”. A bug that Tessa herself still had yet to recover fully from. Tiaras anxiety had become contagious, rendering Tessa speechless as she fiddled with her fingers, “I don’t really know.” “Have you ever thought about leaving to raise a family?” That answer had been predetermined ages ago, “More than a handful. The more time I spend at home with Evan and Christa, the more I want one of my own. But you know what stops me” The fact that I’d like to be living proof to my kids that you can achieve any goal you set for yourself.” If only Ciel had been here to see this kind of optimism. It seemed all too optimistic of her to be true, even from a near stranger’s perspective, “Really?” The extra glitter in Tessa’s eyes as she replied confirmed it all, 'Really. It happens to just about all of us.” Tiaras couldn’t begin to express her gratitude towards Tessa for confirming how normal this was. But this was hardly because she’d been rendered speechless as well, rather that there was now a fairly boisterous Canadian standing in the doorway. “You better start getting your act together, Ms Windsor.” It was an announcement apparently not just for her, but the entire west wing of GCW stars. Sliding her elbow gently into Tiaras side, Tessa kept her suggestions to a mere whisper, “Maybe you ought to get heading out. We’ll talk some later kiddo. I don’t want you to get caught in the middle of this.” Agreeably, Tiarra dashed for the exit leaving Tessa to deal with a rather agitated manager in Brack Conway. “I didn’t mean for Julio to get hurt Brack. Not this time. Not the time before, or any of the times before that.” It was never the fun to be the one in the hot seat, the overwhelming amount of sweat forming on Tessa’s brows displayed that quite clearly. “I don’t care what your intentions are. MY client is getting harmed. I AM GETTING HARMED, all because our associations with YOU! And you now what you’ve done about it. NOTHING!” Ah yes, the joys of being the leader of the pack. Had Tessa not wanted to cry before, she’d certainly wanted to now. Confrontations with friends were never her strong suit, “I’m sorry.” “Sorry isn’t good enough. And you of all people should know that.” His eyes narrowed as did his principle of belief that the woman before him could possibly boost the man who considered a son to the top of Global Championship Wrestling. Quite frankly he’d grown sick of her excuses. They’d changed every week, and this week was had been no exception, “It’ll change Brack. We just need time to come together.” “You’ve had time. And I’ve had enough.” Clenched fists and all, Bracks reasoning for being here were clear, and they certainly weren’t looking great on Tessa’s end. “Either you do something about that assault on Julio, or I’ll do something to see to it that your ties with him are cut.” Yet even through all the mental preparation Lesatt had given, she’d still lacked the capabilities to do anything more than drop her head in shame. “I’m sorry Brack. I’ll do what I can. I promise.” He’d nothing more to say to her. And she had absolutely nothing more that she could offer him, so the silence embraced them both as Tessa sent her head back against the wall, so she’d at least have a matching goose egg on the other side of her head to ease her troubles away. Thankfully, on cue, Cingular’s signature ring tone echoed throughout the empty hallway to break the silent tension between her and Brack, which made a nervous chuckle all the more acceptable, “It’s probably the kids. Shame I can’t even escape from them all the way out here.” Even during casual moments such as this, Brack forced himself to remain professional. Stone like in stature, not even his eyebrows lifted, “I presume you have to answer that.” “Nah, it’s just a text.” Even all the way across the country when her kids weren’t anywhere in sight, Tessa couldn’t deny that she’d been practically turned into a soccer mom as well, “I’ll call them in just a minute.” “Don’t make them wait.” Brack scornfully replied, as if she were his own child. “You don’t need to mess up the ONLY thing you’re apparently doing right.” Had he not been her elder by more than a handful of years, she would have loved to done nothing more than to swat her manicured claws in the direction of his face. But he wouldn’t be the first one to take a swing at her pride, and he certainly was far from the last. Besides, there was still plenty of self doubt there to keep her hand from going up. She’d more than a handful of reasons to believe he was right. So instead she tilted her head up and acknowledged his advice as she swung her pricy purse over her shoulder and headed for the parking lot, “I’ll be back.” Marching towards the exit doors with more burdens than the cared to deal with, she unwillingly remained blind to the fans that followed behind her with a pen in hand. Depositing them at the garage doors, her baby blues had finally opened to something that’d her caught her totally off guard, “Hey mom!” The virtually viable steam that puffed out her ears had nothing to do with the two kids that came dashing towards her. Rather the woman that stood beside them; a woman that very well could have pegged them against her, a woman that went by the name Dawn McFarlane. Physical confrontation was out of the question with the kids around. And verbally, she had to be a role model. Emotionally, there was plenty of reason why they’d flock towards her. She’d been there for them just as much as their own mother. She practically was their other mother back when she and Tessa had their short lived relationship. Yet, Tessa loathed every bit of what she was seeing now, despite knowing that the kids knew no better. They were the innocents in this war without a purpose. “Hey”.Dawn?.” The words nearly hissed through her clinched teeth, as she’d forced a half smile across cherry lips. 'Hey.' Dawn straightened from her half crouch, trademark duster falling about her calves. 'I thought I might catch you here, didn't figure I'd get a chance to see the family though.' She folded her arms just under her breasts, emerald eyes staring into Tessa's blue ones. 'We need to talk.' Naturally, her ponytail bobbed up and down in agreement, “We certainly do, about quite a few things, apparently.” Of course to the side, stood her fianc’e, who seemed to be just as displeased that he’d bumped into Dawn. At least it gave her a way to dispose of the kids. Turning her attention back to the younger ones, she eyed them in the direction of Chris, whose arms just like the others where folded across his chest. “Go see your dad.” Obediently, Evan set foot in the opposite direction as if it were a race. Christa, on the other hand, decided that at twelve, she was plenty mature enough to hang with her aunt turned mother while she conversed about “adult” matters. Knowing better than a give it a second try, given that Christa idolized everything Dawn was, Tessa waved off Chris, and pulled both Dawn and Christa along back towards the arena. “What do you want?” Given that Christa was right beside her, it wasn’t easy to keep things in a neutral tone. Tessa had wanted nothing more than to rip Dawn apart verbally for not only abusing her plenty of times in the past verbally, but for physically abusing every last one of Tessa’s closest friends in GCW more recently. Dawn's eyes weren't on Tessa however but on Storm as he walked away with Evan. Something passed across her face briefly before she closed it down again, turning to look at Tessa. 'Caved again eh?' Had she not been asked this question dozens of other times by both fans and colleagues alike, she would have been likely to snap right then and there.She’d grown tired of the pety high school style drama shit surroding the relationship between her and Chris.But rather then take upon the role of an sixteen year old drama queen, she took in a short breath, counted her blessings, and exhaled before answering, “We’ve grown up. Things are totally different now. But I’m sure that has absolutely nothing to do with the issue you feel is all so important for me to hear about. So why don’t we cut to the chase?” 'Hmph. Sure they are.' Turning away from Storm entirely she turned back to Tessa, eyes flicking only briefly to Christa. 'I want to know where you get off supporting Caldera and his little personal crusade.' “Crusade?” She hadnt spoken to Caldera in weeks, so she hadnt a clue where Dawns accusations came flying from. It wasn’t until she retraced her steps that evening that she realized that Acension, in a way, had been what fought to keep Global Wrestling clean of any inpurities such as Dies Irae, “Oh, you mean Ascension. Look, it’s got nothing to do with you personally. I just rather not have to deal with a power struggle just to get any success in life, you know what I mean?” Shaking her head, Dawn gestured impatiently with one hand, slashing it down in frustration. 'That's not what I'm talking about. Not everything here revolves around you and I.' She smirked, 'I'm talking about Caldera pushing aside everything BOTH sides are fighting for in order to further his personal vendetta.' Even Christa couldn’t help but roll her eyes at her mother. For someone that was supposed to be so sweet and caring Tessa had surely, yet unintentionally, made clear that even those of good nature, had selfish inhibitions. “Perhaps he’s doing it for our benefit.” Again Tessa’s selifsh side had shown through, causing Christa eyes to roll back in the opposite direction, “I mean, maybe he really truly believes that we’re better than our competion.” 'I'm sure he does, and we are, there's no question about that.' Dawn came back, impatience starting to show through. 'But how does his ignoring everything else in GCW prove that he has our best interests in mind here? He's more concerned with righting a percieved slight then paying attention to what's going on INSIDE GCW. Not to mention he's siding with ANOTHER traitor to do it.' She folded her arms again, glaring slit eyed at the smaller woman. 'You can't possibly be so naive as to believe his reasoning is entirely selfless.' Given that this traitor had turned his back on more than GCW, as Tessa could personally attest, she had been inclined to agree with Dawn there, “Yeah, but given that I need a paycheck, what do you want me to do about it” I mean it’s not like Caldera’s spat straight in my face.” Dawn facepalmed, shaking her head from side to side. 'Are you blind? That's EXACTLY what he did. He blew off one of the biggest matches of the year, the biggest match at Devil's Night and paid it no attention whatsoever. What else do you need him to do? Come up and personally slap you in the face?' The glare on Dawn's face wasn't hostile but it was close and it was certainly disgusted. Given that Tessa knew full out that Dawn was right, she had to contain the growl in her throat, by focusing on the fact that Dawn had pratically done the same to her, “Like you did?” But given that her daughted had jabbed her in the side to shut her up, she faced the truth full out, “But what do you want me to do about it” It’s not exactly like my names got any value. Yeah, tonights match was awesome and all, but how do I?.or should I say, “we” make them realize that?” Dawn stiffened, her eyes flashing. 'I wasn't...' Only to find herself the victim of a similar jab from Christa. She turned her emerald glare on the girl, only to find her staring fiercely back. Dawn sighed, pincing the bridge of her nose with thumb and finger. 'I haven't gotten that far. But I'm not the only one who thinks this way, and I can practically gurantee there are people in your camp who feel the same. GCW is about 'us' not about a bunch of traitors and runaways.' In typical preteen fashion, Tessa’s younger model nodded up and down as if she actually understood one hundred percent what Dawn was saying. She’d plenty of reason to agree with Dawn on that stance, given that the traitor Dawn had mentioned, and Caldera all had some kind of indirect affect on her personal life as well. There was the obvious fact that if Caldera focused on old talent, her mother would lose out on money. And then of course, her personal vendetta against the other man in the equation, Seymour Almasy, given that all along she felt he was a fake. Tessa, on the other hand, still chose to live in her fantasy world, “But if they bring in more money, wont that help us out?” The answer honestly took Dawn aback and she stared in something akin to confusion for a long moment. When she spoke her voice was heavy with dissapointment. 'You've changed. I thought it was supposed to be about more then just the money.' She jammed her hands into the pockets of her coat. 'Besides, a better GCW overall would bring in more money. And those 'men' aren't going to make for a better GCW.' She had Tessa there, and there wasn’t any way the pint sized diva was going to squirm away from this grip, “Maybe not. But neither is going to war with them. I finished arguing with Seymour months back ago incase you forgot. And I’ve got no tiffs with Rollins.” 'It's not them any of us have a problem with. They're old news, washed up and way past our concern...the problem is getting Caldera to recognize that WE are the future of GCW, and that chasing old vendetta's beside turncoats will do nothing to step us forward in the new year.' The heat of the moment carried her closer to Tessa as she was caught up in the fervour of her own words. Tessa, however, drifted back towards the thrushold of the exit, and pushed back up against it to make her escape. But not before, coming to agreement on one thing, “Then how about we start showing him why he should be investing in us.” Closing the distance between them, Dawn stepped into Tessa's personal space, her body less then an inch from the teensy tiny terrors. 'That's exactly what I'm talking about, you know which of us you should be talking to.' She held that position for a moment, looking down into Tessa's eyes before turning away. 'Good luck with him...again.” Her eyes traveled over towards the other side of the door to hint to Tessa that it wasn’t Caldera she was referring to this time. To Dawn, and millions of others, Storm was a whole new level of asshole,?You're going to need it.' Passing by Christa, Dawn ran one hand affectionately though the younger girls hair before dperating. 'You remember what I told you Christa, and don't lose that.' With nothing more to say she started away from the Windsor women, but not before slipping Christa a slip of paper. “Thanks.” Christa uttered quietly back so that it stayed between the two, but Dawns booted feet had arleady carried her away back to the arena. Instead, Christa got stuck with her aunt and grabbed her by the arm, dragging her out the door. On the other side the arms Chris came to claim the two. He could clearly could tell what kind of day this had been for his long time girlfriend, by the way her lips hung. “You don’t look too happy.What did she want?” She’d never been big on Chris worrying about her, and that wouldn’t change tonight. Her cherry lips pressed up against his cheeks that’d been turned to a similar color due to wing chil, “Don’t worry about it. I just wonder why I bother coming sometimes.” Clearly, that wasn’t any acceptable answer for her future husband, “Because, you’re?” The solution for that hadnt change any though, “Shhhhhh!” This time her lips pressed up against his as she cawled underneath the unzipped jacket that hung off his broad chest. “I’m not leaving. I just think it’s time I make a little bit of noise.” Given Chris himself had been known for being obnoxiously outspoken, he certianly had little problem with this. “It’s about damn time.” “But for now,” She’d pulled herself away from him, and drifted over towrads the kids, whome she missed all too much, “lets just get home.” Again, the unatural blonde couldn’t agree more. “Oh,” She’d started up again, looking up at her fiancee with a smile that couldve lit up the entire Toyota Center Arena and parking garage, “Thanks for suprising me by being here tonight.” For once, he’d been rendered speechless himself. Maybe there really was something as perfect bliss. Now if only it’s trap his finacee as well.
