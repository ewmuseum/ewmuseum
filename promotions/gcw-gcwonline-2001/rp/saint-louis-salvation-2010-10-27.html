Saint Louis Salvation

27 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/27/10 “CHILDREN OF THE GODDESS!!! Her Bringer of the Light is here to save YOU, Saint Louis!” Twenty-something pedestrians waiting for a crossing signal in downtown Saint Louis turn to see a massive, jet-black RV roll to a stop with the hydraulics exhaling to the max. ERRCCCHHH The Archon, Dorn Tracer, extends his arms out the window of this motorcoach of Her choosing and addresses the gathering mass. “En’Flarim has chosen to SPARE YOU…Her children…since you are all so willing to help Her escape from Her prison! Now, get on Starship Tracer and indulge in Her glory!” Many of these people give a reassuring look to one another to make sure they are awake and, yes, this is really happening. The doors pop open, cueing throaty, monk psalms that draw a few people toward the door. Wearing dark shades and his leather robe, Dorn waves them in and puts on a charming grin. “This Sunday… once again Her Champion will get in the ring and face another SCOURGE the GCW brass has chosen to sacrifice… in fear of Her wrath! She needs you all to be there, LIVE, and in person to witness Dynasty’s sacrifice to Her messenger and be there when I indulge Dynasty in The Goddess’ divine ways at your famed ScottTrade Center. This will be just another lesson taught to the vacant minds and peons that pollute the once pure waters of Global Championship Wrestling who refuse to accept HER… En’Flarim… as their SAVIOR!!” Benson’s eyes wander, noticing that a few make their retreat back to reality, meanwhile; six people buy Tracer’s pitch and board Starship Tracer. One man, wearing a business suit and carrying a briefcase, is the first to meet the Archon. He rolls his eyes. “Her child…” Dorn’s words trail off as he notices the dapper attire and overall nonchalance of this trendy businessman. “Why do you feel the needs to wear clothes given to you by the heathens that cast a foul stench upon Her beloved Earth?” The man glances to his suit, grins, and nods his head, “This is Armani, ya know? Top. Of. The. Line.” “SHE SPITES YOU WITH MY PALM!” SMACK “AND YOU SHAN’T RIDE HER SALVATION INTO INFINITE BLISS! AWAAAAAYYYYY!!” SMACK The once proud executive of some branch of GloboCorp LTD trips over his own feet at the clap of Tracer’s holy mitt colliding with his jaw. He teeters before the others waiting to get on and then goes spilling backwards, knocking all the people off the RV. Dorn looks to Lucious, who tries to hide a smile from the Archon, and then buries his face in his hand. “Maybe we should…” Lucious’ words are cut off as Tracer slowly pries his fingers open, revealing a familiar gaze that Lucious recognizes only as irritation burning brighter and brighter, “nevermind.” Dorn removes lifts his head and lets out a deep breath that makes it possible to collect his composure. He glances to the back of the RV where five people already line the leather benches of this RV; a pair of homeless midgets, a circus clown, and two teenagers with matching ‘THE ARCHON’ GCW t-shirts. Another deep sigh as he reflects on his current recruiting drive. “YOU ARE THE CHOSEN!!” Tracer shouts, bringing himself back to full effect. “Her message will come through OUR words – which will be Her words - OUR actions, and our swift kicks to the GROIN!!! Today is not simply a change for Her children but a change for the nonbelievers and the doubters as well. Follow Her Bringer of Light to GCW WorldWide One-Hundred and Forty this Sunday to WITNESS the cleansing of Dynasty and his INFECTED spirit as we SAVE him and open his eyes to En’Flarim’s GOSPEL!!!” The five people at the back become rambunctious, flailing their hands and arms above them. After waiting for them to reach a height of excitement, the Archon raises his hands again, looking into the sky. “Now… GO!! Spread Her word! Tell others, by way of kicks to the groin, that The Goddess has COME TO SAVE HER CHILDREN!!! She pities those who aren’t accepting and welcomes the believers, forever; She will remember those who do not show Her proper respect… by converting them to JOBBERS!!! Now, GO! Spread Her message!!!” Tracer nods, folds his hands together, and bows to his followers. They jump up, beyond excitement, and storm off the bus. Dorn and Lucious watch as the two teenagers’ method is to bulrush a Postal Worker and double-team nut-punt him. All the envelopes fly out of his mailbag, resembling the bursting white of his eyes as they deliver Her message. “Perfect,” Dorn whispers. He turns, giving Lucious the head nod signaling him for a word in private, and the two disembark Starship Tracer. Tracer rips off his salt and pepper wig and dark shades to show the sexually explosive gaze of Danny Francisco, known to the GCW world as Dynasty. He pulls out a roll of cash and hands it over to Lucious…or… “Good job,” Dynasty chuckles. “That should give that Jim Jones motherfucker exactly what he needs. Five more retards who go down in flames thanks to Her message.” Dynasty grins, glancing back at the bus over his shoulder, and then back to the man holding cash. The man removes his wig and glasses, flipping through his bills like a card deck. His face is dirty, his teeth are missing, and his smell… is intoxicating. Intoxicating like; ‘it will fuck you up’ kind of intoxicating. GCW’s undefeated (and defeatless) newcomer sneers at the stench as it travels up his nose and triggers a thought he’s not fond of having – a dormant memory. ”Perhaps these issues you’re having, Daniel, are because you have no purpose, no reward in your heart. You have money, fame, and women, yes. Do you have peace and salvation?” Danny’s eyes shift from the couch to Father Bryan’s soft, pale skin, and then to his tender blue eyes. The look on his face is unpleasant, unreal. Danny’s hears the words the Father speaks but doesn’t buy them, not one second, as he reads a different message on his face. “Maybe it’s time you accept the Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, Danny.” Francisco rolls his gaze back to the ceiling. His eyes flit side to side, triggering all sorts of impulsive thoughts and rationale. Danny sits up and glances to the wall where, in fact, it is a picture of his rookie year with the St. Louis Cardinals. It is one of his trademark at bats, leg up in the air just before planting and driving the ball across the Missouri River. He grins and stands up, standing in front of it before looking over his shoulder. “Do you see this, Father?” Danny asks questions he often knows answers to, so Father Bryan dare not touch that one. “This picture… it’s familiar. I think I’ve seen it somewhere before… like… a major print production. Perhaps Sports Illustrated? Or was it ESPN?” The man of the cloth sits still, composed, and waits for this to go somewhere. “I’m not sure, Dan-“ “That’s fuckin’ right, you’re not sure! Do you know why, Father? My face is all over the place, my picture’s are too. If somebody’s seen me, everybody’s seen me. You talk about Lord and Savior? I am the Lord. I am the Savior. I’m the fuckin’ second coming to America’s Pastime! Do you know how rare it is for somebody like me to come along? Don’t answer that. Take that ‘Good Book’ you like to quote so very much and find some scripture that sums this up; I’m Danny fucking Francisco!! Jesus Christ couldn’t strike me out if he wanted to.” Dynasty’s focus shifts back to the present and notices the smelly Lucious Benson actor is gone. The disciples of his Dorn Tracer impression have made their way out of sight. Dynasty turns to the driver, remembering the last part of his plan. “Alright, Hoss,” he says, again pulling out another roll of cash. “Just tell your boss I wanted to test out the bed and picked up some strippers. Here…” He pulls out a handful of tickets to WorldWide 140. Handing them over, the driver grins and they exchange pleasantries. “Thanks! Really, thanks, Mister…wait. You look reeeeally familiar. Do I know you?” Dynasty grins, nodding, and then backs out of the doorway before he gives an answer. He strolls down the sidewalk of the busy metropolis he once called Home Field. He takes in the sights, forgotten after years in exile, and thinks about what could have been and what is. This urban paradise known as Saint Louis captures the mind of the man it once-christened the 'St. Louis Slugger' before he earned the alias 'the Baseball Brawler' from media all over America. A smile draws all the what-ifs and woulda-beens out of Danny's head and he notices that, in fact, it is a beautiful day.
