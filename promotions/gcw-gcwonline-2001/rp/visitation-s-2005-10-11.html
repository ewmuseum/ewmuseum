visitation(s)

11 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/11/05 “NO you don’t understand, its not my fault that the water was tainted.” “Mr. Thunder, I am requesting on behalf of our company, that you cease trying to sell our product. You are, quite frankly, doing more damage then good.” “Well I am now on a personal mission to find out who tainted my source of water. I am no longer doing this for you. I am doing this for me. For the world, and for mom’s apple pie.” “Right. We are still asking that you keep us off of you television show.” “Nobody knew it was your water that was tainted though! I mean I never revealed the results.” “Everyone knew Ricky. Now have a good day, I have things to tend too.” Ricky sighs heavily as he hangs the phone up. He collapses in a heap on his couch, and stares at the ceiling trying to piece together what took place on WorldWide. He walks through the steps of the taste test wondering if maybe he had mis-marked the bottles. “Ricky” Comes a voice that startles him. Ricky lets out a shrill scream that would make Jamie lee Curtis jealous. Standing in front of him is his brother Shane. Shane was a former CCW Heavyweight champion, and Tag Team Champion, along with the ling time family friend The Supreme Machine. Ricky had not seen his brother for years. They lost touch when Shane died? “What the hell”! No way’This is impossible. What...or how? Is it really you?” “Yes Ricky it’s me. I have been granted a visitation. I have chosen to spend that time to come see you. I need you to listen closely. I don’t have much time. I only get 15 minutes.” “A visitation?” “Yes. In heaven, we are given visitations once a year. That’s why people always claim to see Jesus in weird places. That guy loves pretending to be a grilled cheese sandwich. He is a comedian, really makes the days go by. We all sit up there and laugh as people put sell their sandwiches on ebay.” “Well, who would’ve guessed?” “But Ricky, the reason I am here is to warn you. There is something you should know about your new friends. I speak of RipeFist,. He is not who you think he is, and should not be trusted,” “Not who I think he is” Hell I just met the guy, he was just going to help me solve the mystery of the tainted water.” “Ricky you are in a match with him this week as a partner right?” “Right. We got booked in a tag title match. I get to work on leaving the Thunder family mark in GCW now. Just like the good old days” “Ricky, there is something I want to tell you, but I’m afraid I cant as we are not allowed to disclose information that could alter the future or past in some cases. Just promise me who will keep your eye on your new friend.” “You know me Shane, I never get too close to people. Not after SuMa walked out on me in CCW.” “SuMa’Nobody has any ideas where he went. It’s not heaven, and it’s not hell. He still walks the earth somewhere. The only one who knows for sure of his whereabouts is the big man. I have asked several times, but he wont tell me. He claims SuMa has requested through prayer, that he “just wants to be forgotten”. I don’t get it.” “Prayer” SuMa? Yeah right. Well if he is out there I will find him one day and find out why he walked out on me. He made a vow, a promise to you, on your death bed, that he would be there for me. That he would watch out for me, he left me for dead in CCW and I will never forgive him for that.” “Ricky, my time is nearly up, I just want to say before I go. Your career has made me quite proud. Please keep your eye on your new “partner”. The last thing I have for you Ricky, is this question. Why water?” “Shane, it’s my own personal mission. It’s something that everyone should know about. Everyone should know how good and refreshing it is. I want to hydrate the world!” “Same old Ricky. I must now take my leave. Remember the things I have spoken for they are true. When you think you have found what you seek, the answer lies just underneath. Goodbye Ricky, remember I will always love you little brother.” “Shane wait!” and with that Shane is again gone in an instant. A tear falls from Ricky’s eye, as he realizes that for the second time, at the most opportune time, he still didn’t tell his brother how much he loved him. Ricky jerks to an upright position from where he laid slumped over on the couch. He reaches up his face still moist from the tears. He runs his hands through his hair as he leans back flush against the couch again, “Seemed so real” he thinks aloud. Ricky gets to his feet and heads out onto the balcony of his second floor apartment. He stares up at the sky which is turning a dark grey color as a storm front moves in. He pulls his cell phone from his pocket and listens as Sylkk’s answering machine picks up on the other end. He doesn’t leave a message but instead hangs up and immediately dials another number. Again he listens as an answering machine picks up. “Damnit where is everyone” he mutters. He stares off in the distance as he punches in a few, numbers, then takes a moment to reflect, then dials a few more. “Hello?” Comes the voice from the other end. “Hi. I’m sure you don’t know me, but I’m looking for a guy who used to have this number?” “Sean?” “Yes’you know him?” Ricky’s voice changes to a suddenurgency as the light at the end of the tunnel is closing in. “Who is this?” “My name is” Ricky?” “Oh my god. Little Ricky” You sound so grown up now. This is Alma.” Tears again fill Ricky’s eyes as he knows he is speaking to the woman who raised him like her very own child. She never took the time to ask how they would make it, or why she was stuck with him. She simply opened her arms and accepted him as her own following the death of Ricky’s parents. “Mom’I?” “Ricky don’t. I know why. You don’t have to try to explain anything. I have looked forward to the day I would hear from you again. I understand that for a while you lashed out at those around you. Ricky it was rough for you losing your parents and your brother so close together. I am truly sorry for your loss, I just hope you understand I did everything I could for you?” “You are the closest thing to a mom I can remember. I was 10 when it all happened. You accepted me. You could have thrown me into a boys home and that would have been the end of me. Instead you took me in’and I love you for that. I am sorry I haven’t contacted you in so long. It’s been hard. There was the incident where Sean walked out on me, and I was angry at him, not you.” “I understand Ricky. Unfortunately I haven’t seen Sean since then either. I occasionally hear his name pass me by on a breeze in a crowd, but that is the closest I have gotten to him in years and years. I am sorry for what happened and I don’t know why it happened. Maybe it was the stress Ricky. He never was good at dealing with that. You know your family was as much a part of his life s you are ours Ricky.” “I know mom” Rcky pauses as the rain starts to sprinkle down. “What time is it there?” “Roughly 2am, but you know me. I’m a night owl, never been much for sleeping, feel like it wastes too much of my day.” “Yeah I definitely know that feeling. Hey mom, I had a dream today?” “You’re still having those?” Her voice grows a bit worrisome. “Not so much anymore, but they are still around. This one seemed so real. Not really dreamlike?” “Are they still as violent?” “No not at all. I think they were fixated on my inner rage at the tome to be honest. I wish I knew why I had them, but I guess its part of living. But today’s dream, was more like a vision.” “Ricky?” “I know it sounds crazy, and we been through all this already. But hear me out please. I have no one else to talk to about this, and you would understand better then anyone’aside from Sean.” “I’m listening Ricky” the lady on the other end of the line has a certain kindness in her voice. A mothers kindness. “I saw Shane today?” Suddenly a crash is heard on the other end of the line. “MOM”! Are you there?!” The woman lies on the floor in a heap, with the phone at her side collapsing at the news she had just heard. Ricky is not the only person Shane had visited that day?
