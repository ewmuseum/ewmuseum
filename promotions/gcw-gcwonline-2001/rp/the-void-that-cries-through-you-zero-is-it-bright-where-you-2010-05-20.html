The Void That Cries Through You | Zero | Is It Bright Where You Are?

Date: 05/20/10 Iholdsecretsflamewecanwatchtheworlddevouredinitspain Beginning at the end, at the beginning. Burnt amber tongues licked at churning plumes of charcoal candy floss. Your skin prickled as the arid heat lured beads of sweat to the surface, while the tip of your nose felt the raw lash of a blistering flame. As your parched eyes were drawn up the mesmerizing smoke plumes, blotches of violent orange branded onto your retinas by the violent heart of the fire flickered around the brooding night sky. Within the veil of dense smoke, burning timbers exhaled their dying, creaking sigh as the flames overcame them. A chatter of cracking glass led the tributes, soon joined by a wheezing wail of grief from breached aerosols and canisters. While all your belongings yielded to the rampant flames before your eyes, not a single tear welled, waiting to be boiled dry in the corner of your eye. The fire baked your emotionless features solid. The hairs you’d left on your pillows, the sofa cushions engrained with your scent and the fluorescent tapestry of post-its on the refrigerator that documented your existence in scribbled notes, all reduced to an ash dusting on a torrent of molten black plastic. “It’s terrible,” they gasped from a distance, where the heat doesn’t tingle their lips with a fiery, passionate kiss like it does yours. “It’s just… terrible. Everything they own. I… I can‘t look.” Sirens wailed through the hushed chatter. “Should we say something?” trickled into your ear as a neighbor’s hissy whisper slashed through the smoky, dense night air. “Like what?” rode their slipstream through the haze. “Offer them somewhere to stay?” “Oh, God, no,” their horrified gasp let it be known what the neighbors thought of you. “They’re… well, how do I put this politely? They don’t really fit in, do they?” “May the Lord judge me for saying this,” they begged, “but this might just improve the neighborhood.” When they looked back from their poorly hushed sniping, your dead stare shamefully bowed their heads and awkwardly shuffled their feet. The choking air that antagonized and tickled the backs of their throats began to suffocate the novelty, and your fickle audience dispersed on the breeze just like the swirls of smoke. Flashes of blue blinked at you from within the churning clouds, subconsciously luring your hands deep into your jeans’ pocket, where your lighter weighed heavy. The enticing slither of the unknown between door and frame nagged and prodded his curiosity. A haunting moan hung in the dead-end corridor’s stale air, somewhere between the humming cellphone background radiation and the interwoven chitter-chatter which held the backstage together. A barely audible pitter-patter tugged at the hairs in his ears, while a sudden, tingling waft of cold air licked enticingly at his bottom lip. “We don’t need you right now,” he’d been told. “You’re just getting in the way,” they found decreasingly polite ways of saying. His body, perky with brimming eagerness, wilted with each dismissive sneer and disparaging look. He could of made more money packing bags at the supermarket or stuffing pamphlets into the hands of passers by so that they could dump them in the trash rather than him. But this was his dream, however lacking in ambition it was. To fetch, pass on notes, and be there when every menial backstage task bounced all the way down the delegation chain to the very, very bottom. Every eye in the building distracted, he shuffled unnoticed towards the door. Aborting a trembling knock, he instead prodded the door delicately with his index finger. The hinges whined. A tired eye, inked red within its shadowy sketched outline, peered through the gap. A queasy jolt tumbled down his spine, vertebrae by vertebrae, at the sight of a lifeless foot, severed above the ankle by the door’s frame. Breathing in, he slithered through narrow gap between door and frame, the hinge letting him pass without emitting another squeaky whine. On tip-toes, he crept towards the shower tray, where the flaccid body of Rasa spilled over onto the floor as the last of its vomit was driven down the drain by the icy downpour. He took a backseat to his own curiosity, a passenger watching from behind sleep-deprived eyes as the shower was turned off. With Rasa no longer imprisoned within the fine, freezing bars from the showerhead, he leant over to peer through the torn-open mouth of its ghoulish mask. Dripping wet and peeled away from its sweat-drenched face, Rasa’s mask slid off into the shower tray. Frayed nerves snapping, he shot backwards in reaction to the sudden movement, slipping on the wet tiles. His skull bounced off the floor, detaching him from his senses. His vision peeled apart, the lights above dividing outwards like a kaleidoscope. There they lay, together for the first time. He ruffled through limp, mousy hair for any sign of blood; even the slightest of tugs at the root pulling the pin on his dully throbbing skull. Crawling back to his feet, action trailing intention by a good few seconds. Hurried motions stalled and interrupted by misfiring nerves, he moved in jolts and spasms until he was hunched over Rasa’s wet, limp body. Into the shadows of its hood he stared, overlapping images swaying back and forth over one another until they finally settled. He wondered just how much damage was done when his skull cracked against the floor. “I must be dreaming…” Andiamamasterofanothingplaceofrecoilandgrace What will happen. What was happening. What would always have happened. Jellied legs bending in queasy directions, he swung limply by the tie which was wrapped around your fist. Bulging, bloodshot eyes, peeled of their lids, glared back up at you. You felt the fibers of his tie unweave, one by one, as you hammered another fist down into his mouth. Teeth uprooted, his gummy grin painted its lips with oozing, sticky crimson. “It was fate,” he gasped and gargled, mangled mouth struggling to form the words. You don’t believe in fate. His nose crumpled under your knuckles, skin and flesh splitting over jagged, splintering bone. “I was meant to find out,” bubbled out through the sickly sweet slick of blood that gushed down his gaping throat. “It w-…” Reeling him in, you wound his fraying tie again around your fist; ripped collar meaning his neck rubbed raw. Your teeth pried one of his from your knuckle, then spat it down his throat. “I lov-… I lo-…” he choked, words breaking and tumbling back down his throat with each heave. His head violently snapped back as your knee slammed under his jaw, crooked teeth stamping down into bloody gums. The blow lit a fuse which zipped along his mandible and exploded the nerve clusters, his conscience obliterated. Teary eyes fell back behind baggy lids as the putrid tang of warm urine cut through the richness of blood. The coarse fabric of his tie burned your palm as you released him from the leash. At your feet he fell, broken, bloodied, soiled; and dreaming of you. Roleplay title and chapter headings are from The Beginning is the End is the Beginning by Smashing Pumpkins.
