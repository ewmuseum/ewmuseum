Dreams Do Come True

1 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 1st, 2009 The joys of being a college graduate and words that can't describe. The feeling of accomplishment as you continue on to the next stage of your life. Some people set out to be doctors, some to be lawyers. Myself, I want to continue on this next stage of my life as a professional wrestler. I've lived a life of solitude. Everyone worrying about their own thing and not worrying about the next guy. The next guy in this case being me. And I'm okay with that. I don't need anyone to help me fulfill my hopes and dreams. Just look at my college education. I set out to accomplish that goal and I succeeded all on my own. So for me to say that the life of a professional wrestler is not easy and numerous stories and fellow wrestlers have all informed me of this. Long flights, countless hotel rooms, wrestling in cities on a constant basis, trying trying to support yourself and figure out if you can afford your next meal. So the question I get asked is "Why would I want to put myself through this lifestyle?" Simply put. Its my dream and dreams are priceless. Many men and women live there lives without even coming close to having a true shot at their dreams. I have that shot and nobody is going to keep me from it. Ring... Ring... Ring... Hesitating on whether to answer the damn phone itself is always a battle at nine o'clock in the morning. Especially on the weekends. Pulling the pillow over my head and hoping that the sound of the phone echoing in my ear will just go away. Ring... Ring... Ring... To no avail. Twisting and turning myself over to the nightstand reaching for the phone and answering it with a still weakend voice. Hello? Get up, Jeffrey and get over here. Your sister wants to see you! The annoying sound coming from the voice of my mother is hardly ever something I want to hear anymore ever since the tragic day that broke up my family two years ago. For my sister though I'd do anything. What time is it? Its time for you to get your ass up and out of bed but actually its now a little after nine. Alright give me until ten and I'll be there. Alright she's waiting for you. Hanging up the phone and springing out of bed with a bolt of energy that strangely hits through my body I wisp away to the bathroom to take care of my personal hygiene with a very thorough ten minute shower. Out of the shower and back into the bedroom with speed reaching in and out of drawers trying to find a clean enough outfit to wear. Ripped pants and this stripped shirt just do the trick. Getting up and doing all of this is even a chore anymore as a 24 year old I don't have the energy and care to really do much anymore. But I do anyways. Grabbing the keys and heading out into my all chromed out 2008 Black Ford Mustang. When I ride I ride in style. Hundreds of people stare only out of jealousy as I pull out of apartment's parking lot and onto the highway. In Kansas City all of the people are just like people from any other city. They're loud and inconsiderate of other drivers and objects around them whether it be going through a construction site or a school zone. The constant yelling at other drivers and irritating sounds of horns honking. Yes this is the world we live in. Approaching my destination I see a young girl. aged 11, in a cute little pink outfit shooting hoops on a basketball goal which appears to be lowered to 9 feet. Hey hero! Hey sidekick! Getting out of the car and grasping my sister up in my arms and swinging her around holding her tight. I love you. Love you too. I've missed you, Jenna. I haven't seen you in a while. Yeah, its kind of hard to with it being during school time. Always doing something. Yeah but that's good though. You should stay active. Enjoy your youth. How was your Halloween? Did you get any candy? It was fun. Mommy and her boyfriend took me around the block. I got a lot of candy bubba bubba. Looking around at the quality of houses and neighbors it appears that Jenna is in a safe neighborhood and even has over 100 homes. Looks like there's a lot of people around here. Have you made any new friends here yet? Yeah there's some little girl's my age across the street. They seem nice. Want to play one-on-one brother? Sure. I'll even spot you 5 points going to ten counting as all one point baskets. No I'll spot you the 5 points Jeff. Jenna says giggling. Oh so sure about yourself are you, Jenna? laughing. Well you know. Alright here we go. Rear view mirror, Jumbo dice, trunk hit hard like KIMBO SLICE. What's that, hero? That's my ringtone. Reaching down into my pocket and grabbing my phone only to see the number is unknown. Hello? Is this Jeff Davidson? Timeout Jenna. I have to take this call. Yes this is him. Well I'm a representative from Global Championship Wrestling. And I heard that you were wanting to tryout a career in this industry. Is that still true. Yes sir. It is. Well how about we schedule a meeting for 11 oclock tomorrow. I'll set up a plan for you to come to our offices and we'll discuss things more in depth of how it works out here and maybe set up a temporary contract. How does that all sound? Well I'd be looking forward to it. It sounds great. Call me back with the details. I will. And you have a great rest of your day sir. You too, sir. Upon ending the call I get ecstatic about the possibly getting the chance to be involved in one of the biggest companies in the professional wrestling industry today. What happened brother? I just got a call to possibly be a professional wrestler, Jenna. I have a meeting with a man tomorrow morning. Why? I don't want you to go? If you become a wrestler I'll never get to see you then. Yes you will. I'll always find a way too you. I'm going to have to take a rain check for this game, Jenna. I'm going to have to go home and pack.
