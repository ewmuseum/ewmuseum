Ghosts of ones past, is the road to ones future....

15 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/15/10 The lights shine down the highway as the yellow line dips and curves. At times the line seems to blur , turn to double then single solid, with broken line passing symbol. The hum of the tires rolling down the road in the silent darkness, with the only light coming from the headlight. The vibration of the engine is felt through the arms and body, as the wind flows through the hair. It is the open road a path which many men ride. Slowing down he rides into the gas station, slowing the Harley down he stops at the gas pump. He drops the kick stand and settles the machine down. Standing up he reaches down and dusts off his chaps, road dust always seems to hang onto everything out in these parts. Turning he walks into the station and looks around. He looks up as the neon light is causing the white paint to kind of look greenish in color. He looks over at the goth like chick behind the counter and nods. Turning he heads to the beer cooler, opening the cooler he pulls out two cold beers. He turns and walks by a rack and grabs a bag of beef jerky and walks to the counter and tosses them down. Looking at her he jams his thumb up over his left shoulder. “Going to fill the ride as well.” He eyes her carefully as he notices she is a bit out of place. He thinks inwardly as he eyes her. She looks up at him and smiles and then turns back to chewing her gum and reading her magazine. Freaky little bitch aintcha, kind of way out of place here in the desert. I wonder what your hiding from out here. Anybody out here has something to hide, running from something, or downright just using the open road to clear their head. Laying a twenty down he looks at her and taps it with his forefinger. “I'll be right back for the change.” Turning he quietly walks out to the Harley as he slowly removes the gas cap. He turns his head as the wind blows a bit causing a few tumbleweeds to roll by. He eyes the road and then begins to pump gas. He ponders if he should have ridden the old Indian out here instead of the Harley. Patiently he watches down the road only lit by the stars in the sky and disappearing into the darkness. He looks around as he hears a coyotes yapping, Listening closely he could tell they were a ways away. Shrugging he turns slowly and finishes filling up the tank. Placing the handle and turning the lever back down into place he turns and places the chrome gas cap with a raven symbol on it back into its place. He smiles as he did not spill a drop of gas on the custom painted tank. Turning he heads back to the station door. He steps in as the woman behind the counter gives him the change and smiles and drops a magazine down onto the counter top. On the cover shows him raising a heavyweight title and underneath shows him in several pictures with a lot of other different titles in his hands. “I knew it was you she says sharply. You're Shaman or otherwise known as Andrew Whitecloud.” He raises his gaze at her and shakes his head. Clearing his throat as he lifts the two beers with one hand and then grabs the beef jerky with the other hand. “That was ages ago, now I am just a man who has a mission to end some wrongs in this world done to me by others.” “You can't leave this place, it is always winding that road and it will always bring you back here.” She smiles and blows a bubble. He eyes her for a moment and smirks slightly, If only she knew he thought. He reaches into his pocket and pulls out an Indian head nickel and flips it up and watches it land on the desk. “I have been here before many times, I rule the open road. I have been on it long before you were born.” He exits the station walking back over to his ride and sits astride it as he places the beef jerky and extra beer between his legs. He pops the one in his hand open and drinks it down. The carbonation rough on his parched throat, but the taste and coldness of it quickly soothes. The wind picks up once again and blows right by the service station. This time with the wind there are images and spirits in the dust. Horses being ridden by Native Americans who ride hard and look at him occasionally. They are dressed in the old ways and he nods to them. He turns s he catches the girl standing beside him as she speaks. “You better leave mister the spirits are restless this evening.” She looks over at him with interest. Nearly turning his head he stares at them and then back at her. Waving his freehand towards the road and the images he speaks. “My ancestors are always restless in the wind.” He eyes her and begins to ponder her game. What is it your playing at little girl, you aren't normal. There is no way you can see what I am seeing here. Unless there is something unnatural about you. Something just doesn't feel right here, This beer is pretty good, it is as if it is messing with my mind, I feel the roar of the Harley, but I don't seem to be moving, I can even feel the bumps in the road yet why am I simply sitting here facing this woman and listening to her play games with me. She laughs a disturbing laugh as she notices his gaze narrow upon her. “I see that you have noticed that I am not what you thought I was.” She dances around him and the Harley like a little dryad or sprite in the old stories. He watches her carefully as he simply waves a hand in front of her stopping her. She grins tauntingly as she stares at him. “What are you going to do great warrior. Can you defeat me here in this land of spirits and darkness. The riders come from the east driving your ancestors to the west. My ancestors are the ones who will show you the way.” She taunts increasingly as he reaches up and pulls a medicine bag from beneath his shirt attached to a leather band which is tied behind his neck. He reaches in and pulls some sand like powder from it and whispers. “Free from the past, far from the present, bring the spirits of the animals to the field of the future. May the past come meet the present, may the spirits of my ancestors return to the hunting grounds of the forefathers.” He quietly tosses the powder up into the air as it scatters into the winds. Blue wisp like animals of beer, eagles, and wolves scatter around the area running into the dust, suddenly screams of anger are heard as Calvary soldiers appear around the woman and Andrew. His eyes watch coldly as he looks at each one. One in an officers uniform steps forward and points a time ravaged skeletal looking hand towards Andrew. “How dare you interrupt the hunt for which we have been given, These savages are to be driven until they die for we are the 13th Calvary.” The Captain eyes the him for a moment then lowers his hand. He looks to the others as he draws a gun as he points it at Shaman. Unmoving he smiles and looks around at the woman. “He is one of them and she is one our descendants. She helped continue the hunt, but he broke the chain.” Stepping forward past his motorcycle he steps onto the road as the dust seems to not swirl around him, as if sending a message, Andrew narrows his gaze at the men. He smiles as he spreads both arms and looks to the left and the right. “Come harm or stop me from doing this, It isn't like this was your only chance to stop my people. In fact you failed numerous times, hadn't one of your cavalries failed at Little Big Horn?” Almost as if he was taunting him to come forward the Captain steps forward and strikes Andrew with a backhanded fist. Andrew staggers a step to the side and stands back up defiantly looking at the Captain. The Captain angrily looks at him and then to his hand. “How...how dare you insult those fine soldiers with your savage tongue, You ….you people are nothing, but savage uneducated mongrels who need to be put down.” Andrew stares at the Captain and then to his riders nearby. He turns his gaze to the woman and looks around. “I have a solution to this problem Captain, You need to settle this by the right of combat.” The Captain turns his head and looks at his Dog soldier who steps forward. The Dog soldier whispers into his ear and then steps back removing his coat. The Captain turns and looks at him daring him to make a move as his decision is being made. He turns pondering his thoughts on this as the large Native begins taking his leather chaps off. “Easy there Captain I am going to make this easier for you, I will fight you and you alone.” The Captain makes a small hand gesture as the Dog soldier quickly raises and old Colt pistol, but just as quickly an arrow hits its target from out of nowhere and drives itself deep into the Dog soldiers chest. Andrew stops and is a bit taken aback by the arrow. A hand is placed upon his shoulder and he turns looking eye to eye with a Chief who turns his gaze back at the Captain unmoving as he speaks. “This soldier has chosen to do battle with you in an honorable way, This war will end tonight before the rising of the son.” The Captain growls as he reaches down and checks the Dog soldier. Andrew narrows his gaze at the Captain speaking clearly. Andrew steps forward reaching down grabbing the arrow and yanks it out as he shoves the Captain out of the way. The Captain quickly stands up knowing this living man is nowhere near as strong as he is. Andrew grabs the arrow and drives it through the throat of the Dog soldier cleanly through to the other side into the ground pinning the ghostly traitor to the ground. “My ancestors taught me that when one becomes a traitor to his people and ancestors, it is tradition to pin him to the ground and let the animal spirits cleanse his soul before he returns to the hunting grounds as one of them. Your taint shall no longer be afforded this one Captain.” He stands up looking at the Captain as he draws a bayonet off a rifle and looks at Andrew. Touching the tip the older military officer grins through his aged skeletal teeth. “You going to choose a weapon or will you die barehanded?” A warrior steps through the dust and throws a knife onto the ground at Andrews feet and nods to him. Andrew reaches down and lifts the knife looking at it. He turns and faces the Old Captain, the Captain laughs deeply and grins. He waves his hand at him motioning him to come. Andrew quickly charges looking to take him off balance. With a quick spin the Calvary leader cuts him just above the bicep leaving a gash. Not enough to disable, but a glancing blow almost as if he was toying with him. The Calvary Captain grins a toothy grin and points. “Did you think you a living man could face one of the immortal dead hunters of the 13th Calvary pup? You are not even strong enough to face me physically.” Realizing his handicap, he looks at the knife in his hand and tucks it down into his boot. “I guess I will have to find out the physical part, because there is no way I will lose to a blowhard like you.” As he turns he noticed both of them are encircled by hundreds of Indians and Calvary here to witness this battle. The Chief stands next to a General as neither turn to look at one another. They both observe and look on at this battle at hand. Throwing a quick right Andrew connects hitting the Captain as he head turns hard, Andrew jumps in again driving several fists into the head and then the ribs. The Captain suddenly turns and throws a punch hitting him square in the chest launching him up off his feet into the air. Coming down hard onto the ground Andrew rolls over and gets up slowly, but the Captain is on him in a quick few steps and kicks him in the side launching him once again rolling through the air sideways. Landing out into the road he feels the road scrape and tear his skin as if it wants its own penance. He stands up hurting all over this time, his head spinning as if he hit something harder than before. Almost as if his own body is fighting itself. He grabs a handful of dirt in one hand as the Horse soldier steps closer and throws the dirt into its face. “You dare use childish tricks on me pup?” He wipes his skeletal face and looks down staring in shock as the blade that was once in Andrews boot stuck straight out from his chest. Andrew stood up holding his right arm simply staring at the Captain. “So lost in your superiority that you forgot the one rule in combat. Never trust your opponent to fight like you.” Dropping to one knee Andrew leans against a rock as he watches. Collapsing down he passes out on the ground. The Chief kneels down next to him as the Captain hands the knife to one of the warriors. “The living do not realize they can never end this. The Chief nods and smiles as he stands up “Shall we return to the hunt white warriors? I hear the Coyote here are fair prey.” The Captain turns and looks at the young woman. She smiles and turns walking back towards the gas station as the ghosts and spirits simply disappear back into the dust again. Leaving Andrew lying upon the ground unmoving. Hours later in the early morning desert sun.... Shaking his head he sits up rubbing his temples he looks down at his scraped up chaps and his Harley laying on its side in the sand. He groans as he stands up feeling every ache and pain in his body. Looking around he doesn't see any gas station or anything recognizable. “Man what a messed up dream.” Turning he walks over to the Harley and lifts it up. Slowly he inspects it to make sure everything is in place and nothing is broken. Noticing the few scrapes on her there was nothing structurally wrong with her. He runs his hand along the gas tank as he sits astride her once again. “What the hell happened last night, I barely remember riding on the road and I remember a gas station, but man what a weird dream.” Reaching down he turns the key into the on position and then stands up dropping down causing the kick start to force the compression over into the engine and the roar is heard throughout the desert hills and sands. He Revs the engine a few times and roars out onto the highway and down the road. Unknown to him his future as he rides forward into the unknown. Will this be the mere beginning of his travels into the unknown or will this road find another road king in the near future and test them as they become part of its atmosphere. A Sioux Prayer... Make my hands respect the things you have made And my ears sharp to your voice. Make me wise so that I may know the things you have taught your children. The lessons you have written in every leaf and rock Make me strong--------! Not to be superior to my brothers, but to fight my greatest enemy....myself Make me ever ready to come to you with straight eyes, So that when life fades as the fading sunset, May my spirit come to you without shame.
