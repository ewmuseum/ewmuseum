Finding ones' self again
Jason Dread
Global Championship Wrestling Roleplays #16
Date: 1/2/2003
Location: Through your eyes...

For every period of recession through time, there has always been a upcoming of the situation. For the Middle Ages, there was the Renaissance, a rebirth of culture. For the Great Depression, there came the rebirth of wealth following the great war. There hasn’t been such a strong example since then.

Skip ahead 70 years, however...and here we are, faced with another Renaissance. This time it doesn’t involve a culture...or money. Those are superficial. We are now faced with the rebirth and development of a human mind...of a strained and tortured mind. The mind of a man who has been through every up and every down imaginable in just a few months of his life.

Getting it all, then losing it in a short amount of time takes its’ toll. Then, the immature taunting of those who parallel children on an elementary school playground adds fuel to the fire...this primal fire programmed into every single person who has ever lived, whether they choose to accept it or not. Some call it the side of them which they are afraid to see. Others hold it within...

Some...embrace this fire; mold their life around the hate it brings. Their pain which must be let out through the pain of someone else. They embrace, they adapt, and they live. Just like any normal member of society.

Take away the outlet, however, and you have a caged animal. We all know what happens to those wild animals that are caged. We’ve seen enough of the Discovery Channel...they become angry...listless...repeatedly thrashing out on those who may try to help. This lasts for only a period of time, however. Slowly, the fire that burned inside and led them to hate and pain slowly dies out. They become manageable.

They become...’normal’ once again.

As any fire that dies out, however, all it takes is one single spark to ignite everything to what it was once before. Suddenly, the fire roars stronger than ever, and all the old feelings return. Hate, lust, greed, pride…they are called sins…but those stronger than to rely on banter such as that calls those emotions a simple part of life.

An image comes into focus for the first time. We see a small apartment with a red interior…almost completely absent of any light whatsoever. On the far side of the room there lies a window, with the blinds shut out. Through tiny slits in the blinds, strained rays of light shine through. The next thing we see is a blinding flash of light!

Once everything is visible once again, we find that the blinds have been completely open, as a bright sun shines into the apartment, illuminating everything we could not see before…including the man standing at the window. We draw closer, noticing his eyes are staring outside…a spark of purpose illuminating those eyes which were dark or so long. The eyes that were filled with so much hate and loathing for anything that moved.

These eyes…are no more. The feelings have been internalized for the time being. A small smile creeps across the face of this man…this time without any evil notion. A smile which tells of genuine happiness.

He turns away from the window, to a small wood desk to his left. Pulling out the chair underneath, he sits down and scoots himself forward in the chair to where a large stack of papers lie in front of him. The text is small...except for the title, plainly reading “Global Championship Wrestling”. Anything else below that is too small to read for our angle of visage. He takes a take glance at the paper on top of the large stack, and reads a few lines into it. As quickly as those were read, the paper was once again on top of the stack, a look of boredom passing over the man’s face.

He scoots the large pile off to the side and picks up a piece of plain, white parchment with an ink pen on top of it. The body of the letter for which we see looks like a personal letter, which has been mostly completed. Once again he starts to write at this letter, concentration present. Nearly thirty seconds pass as we watch him scribble down word after word in a decisive fashion. With an ornate stroke of his pen, making the man’s signature, he stands up from the chair, stretching his arms over his head. He turns back to the window and stares out, the smile from earlier returning…a smile of completeness.

Slowly, we look to the letter which he was writing…except we don’t stop to read the whole thing. A small portion of what he wrote envelops the entire screen:

“…looked over the contract which you sent me. I’ve spent since the last time I actually fought debating what to do with my life, I’ve realized what I was meant to do. Steven…you can count me in this time…not as the hate-ridden man I once was. That part of me is past. I’m ready once again…I won’t disappoint.”

The signature reads:

Jason Dread
