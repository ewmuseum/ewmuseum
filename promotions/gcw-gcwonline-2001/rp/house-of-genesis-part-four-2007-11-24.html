House of Genesis: Part Four

24 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 24th, 2007 Littered across the table, various magazines spout about the best ways to do your hair for the big day, or which floral patterns look better with which dresses. Bridal this, bridal that; it was all over the kitchen table as Scott Drake walked into the room to grab a drink of water. Pausing for a moment, Scott looked at one of the magazines and flips through the pages. He looked around the room, noting he was alone and took the magazine with him into the living room where he found Tessa with their two month old, Aislinn, who was laying tummy down on one of her many “gym playground” mats. "She's trying to hold her head steady." Tessa's eyes remained locked on Aislinn, despite her fianc?'s sudden appearance. "Well, that's a tough one." Chris remarked as he plopped down beside Tessa, and cupped her chin, "Her mother hasn't even mastered that one yet." He smiled, pinching her chin in hopes that she would do the same, but Tessa hardly found that amusing. The only reason she saw herself struggling to keep her head up, was him; him and his tag titles, him and his needing to train a prot’g?, him and Markus, she thought to herself. She wanted to argue that none of those included her or their family, and yet all of those always seemed to play priority over watching his children grow up and helping Tessa out. But she bit her tongue, instead letting Scott hold the magazine out for her to see. “You’ve been ordering from these?” Tessa grabbed the extended magazine out of his hand, lifting herself from the floor and walked straight to the kitchen. Without any hesitation Tessa scoops up the rest of the magazines from the table and walked them over to the trash can, where she dumped them all. Over her shoulder she looked at Scott “they’ve been coming in regular since we announced we’re getting married.” Looking almost hurt, Scott took the magazine he had been shifting through out of the trash can, “how come you don’t want to keep them?” “I don’t need them.” Outside the kitchen window, she notices that since her and Aislinn had woken up the sky had gone from pitch black to a now light gray with an occasional orange streak, “Shouldn’t you be heading out the door” You’re going to be late for that kid you’re training.” Scott looked to his watch, “nah, I still have plenty of time” he told her as he tossed the magazine back on the table. “But Darson?” Before she could get the words out, her lips were sealed shut by his kiss. “Don’t worry about him.” He whispered in her ear, brushing back a strand of her silky brunette locks. If it were any other person, she would have jumped all over the chance of him spending some more time at home. But Jerrod Darson, known to the wrestling world as Genesis, was one not to be messed with. He was cold, he was heartless, and he certainly would not want Scott wasting time with Tessa. “No. I don’t want him pissed off, Scott.” She gently pushed away from him and made a beeline for the living room. “You’re more important,” He reached out with a hand, but her arm had drifted too far away to grab, “He can wait just a few extra minutes.” Her head peered at the window once more and noticed that the orange streaks were beginning to overtake the gray. It was beginning to become too late to save her fianc? from having to face Darson, “NO!” Her stern tone caught him off guard. It made no sense to him why she wouldn’t want him to stay home, when she was always complaining that he never spent enough time with her and children. For the millionth time this month, it seemed like something had to be bothering her again, yet Scott still had yet to figure out what it was. “Tessa”.” “Not now, Scott!” This time her voice had dropped down to a weak pitch. He had already begun to wear her defenses down.” Dropping into the plush maroon couch, Tessa tried to sink between the cushions and disappear. It didn’t work though, even when she covered her face behind her eyes. Scott sat beside her, trying to hold her hand, but Tessa kept pulling it out of his grasp. “What’s the matter?” he asked her. She shifted onto her side, and looked over at him. She looked an absolute mess, with strands of hair flying wild and tear-stained cheeks. She hardly looked like the same determined woman that walked out to GCW’s ring each week, “I can’t stand it.” Scott gave her time to catch a breath in between sobs and finish her sentence, “You had a chance to tell him no, and you didn’t. You’d rather spend time with some asshole and some rookie you don’t have faith in, than be here with your family.” “That’s not true” Scott fired back, “look, if you want; I can just cancel today’s practice. It won’t be a big deal, really. Then we can go out and spend some time together as a family.” He tried to cuddle her chin with his hand but Tessa pulled back from him. “I don’t have to train the kid today.” It was intended to solve her problems, but it only threw Tessa into further dismay, “Now you feel like you can’t do what you want to do! I’m sorry. I don’t blame you for wanting to get out of the house. You don’t do this to me. I’m sorry”.” Taken back by her outburst, Scott lifted himself from the couch and walked over to his gym bag by the door. Tessa leaned forward, still planted in the couch, and watched his every motion. But then Scott dropped the back on the floor and turned back toward the living room. Once he came back into the room, Tessa could see his cell phone in his hand. Just in case she didn’t notice it, Scott held it up for her. “I can go ahead and call Jerrod and tell him I won’t be able to come in today. It is as simple as that.” “Scott, no.” She pulled herself off the couch only so that she could pull his phone away from him, “Just go. Please” I’ll be fine.” He reached to take his phone back but Tessa kept it out of his reach from him. “Look, it’s no big deal,” he told her as he tried unsuccessfully to take the phone, “it won’t hurt anyone if I miss one day of practice with the kid.” Still, Tessa kept his cell phone clutched to her chest and out of his reach. “I’m fine.” She stated once more, stashing the phone inside the pocket of the baby blue bathrobe she was wearing, “You need to go and do your own thing. I’ll find my own thing to do. Besides, it would serve as good practice for our match this week against the Establishment. You need that extra time. Take it.” Her sudden change of heart had not gone unnoticed by Scott, who raised his eyebrow to her. “You’ve been against me training ever since I was asked,” he told her, “and yet now you are all gun-ho for me to run off and leave you to train the kid, despite me offering to skip?” He couldn’t help but ask, “what gives?” Her bathrobe fanned out like a ballerina skirt as she turned on her heel, “You aren’t skipping it because you want to. You’re skipping it because I want you to. That’s not right of me, Scott. I’m being too controlling.” “Look” he again stated, but he didn’t get any further than that. Tessa was quick to cut him off with a direct no. Scott tried to protest with her, to tell her that it was fine and he wanted to be with her, but Tessa just told him that he needed to go. It was an argument he knew she wouldn’t let him win, not if she wouldn’t even give him enough time to get a word in edgewise. Stuck to the fact that she would not take any other answer, Scott turned away from his fianc? and again grabbed his gym bag. Before he walked to the door he stopped, looking over his shoulder to Tessa, “can I have my cell phone then?” Her head nodded, and she walked over to him with the phone. Rather than place it in his hands, she opened up his bag and slipped it inside. Her attention then directed back to him. “I love you.” She whispered in his ear, as if she weren’t expecting to ever see him again. “Love you too” he replied back as he gave her a kiss on the cheek. It would have found her lips but at the last second Tessa turned her head to the side and he gave a peck to her cheek instead. “I won’t be long” he told her as he slipped out of the door. The trip to the gym was a relatively short one from previous weeks. Scott seemed to be wasting no time as he tossed his gym bag aside and rolled into the ring with his sneakers still on. His prot’g?, Cleveland Paige, wasn’t even ready as Scott rolled in behind him. Turning around, he got surprised as Drake took him down with ease with a Single Leg Takedown. He backed off for a second to let Paige get back up. The young man was obviously pissed, throwing his arms up in the air as he cursed. Arms in the air around Scott Drake screamed “take me down” and he wasn’t about to ignore the request. For the second time in thirty seconds he took him down. The third takedown came just as easy as the first two, catching Cleveland as he tried to get back up. With the third takedown, Drake rolled out of the ring and started heading toward the door. Jerrod Darson, Drake’s mentor, stepped in front to block him. “Hold up” he told his former student but Scott kept moving. “I have shit I need to handle today, I don’t have time to be playing with kids today.” The comment didn’t fly over well Cleveland Paige, who mouthed something back to Scott about not being a kid. “I could whoop your ass in a real fight” Paige told him. He didn’t say a word back, he didn’t have time. Instead Scott Drake just raised her arm over his head and let him know he was number one. Paige just chuckled; half amused and exited the ring himself. Jerrod Darson looked as if he was going to try and stop Scott, to reason with him to stay, but Cleveland headed to the showers and Darson knew at that point that it would have been useless. The practices never lasted very long, but this time it had just been ridiculously short. All Darson could do was shake his head and wonder if his plan would ever come to fruition.
