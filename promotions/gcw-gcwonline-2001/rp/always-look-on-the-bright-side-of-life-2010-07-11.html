Always Look On The Bright Side Of Life

11 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/11/10 (It would help to read Tessa's piece first, but not required.) Scott Drake sat on the steps of the hospital. He didn’t want to just sit there. He wanted to do something. But he knew he couldn’t, and it killed him. He wasn’t the kind of guy who let things play out. He was what they called a “doer”. Johnny watched him, simply feeling horrible. He walked across the parking lot and sat down next to Scott. Looking over the guy that was going to be his mentor, he saw Scott’s fist balled up so tightly they were turning very red, then white. He opened his mouth, but no words came out. What could he say? He closed his eyes, trying to think of the right thing to say. When he opened his eyes, however, Scott had already came to his feet and was walking toward the parking lot. He WAS going to do something. It may not help the problem at hand, but it certainly was going to make him feel better. The Kid hurried to catch up as Scott walked right up to a beat up and old looking car. Looking through the window, he decided to move on to the next old and beaten up car. This one seemed more to his liking as he looked through the window. “Hey man,” Johnny finally managed to get out as he hurried to keep up, “what are you doing?” Scott didn’t answer him, Johnny wasn’t even sure if he had heard him. Instead he took off his shirt and ripped it in half. Wrapping one of the halves around his fist, Scott reached back as far as he could and simply unloaded on the window. Glass shattered everywhere, sending Johnny scrambling back. “What the hell!” Still not hearing him, Scott doesn’t reply. He just moved to the back window and smashed it to pieces. His fist had to be hurting him, even the black of the shirt showed a crimson hue. If it was hurting him, Scott ignored it as much as he did Johnny. He went over to the other side and knocked out both those windows. Johnny franticly tried to get his attention, looking everywhere to make sure a police officer wasn’t nearby. Next were the headlights, both getting violently kicked in. After that, the tail lights. What was once a worn and ugly car was now a wreck. But it felt so good. It felt real good. And he wasn’t finished there. Scott hopped onto the hood of the car, stomping his feet down onto the windshield. As he cracked it, looking to shatter it to pieces, Johnny tried to talk him off the car. “Come on, man!” he cried. “You’re going to get into some serious trouble!” Scott wasn’t worried about trouble. He had enough of it to fill an ocean. What he needed some was release. With each stomp onto the hood, he got that. When the windshield finally did give in, Scott nearly collapsed with it into the car. He just barely managed to hop onto the top of the car, avoiding any further injury. With the only part still not torn up being the back windshield, Scott hopped off the car and walked over beside it. He took the other half of his shirt and wrapped it around his other fist. Despite the fact the damage was already extensive, Johnny finally stepped up and tried to hold Scott back. He wasn’t going to have any of that though, and Scott shoved him away. He raised both fist over his head and brought them down violently on the back windshield. It cracked, but refused to break on him. Scott took its refusal as a personal attack and again brought both fist down upon it. It took three shots before it finally crumbled and broke. Stopping now, his rush died down and exhaustion took over. Scott plopped himself down against the driver’s side door. Johnny stood over him, in complete and utter shock. “You finished?” Scott unwrapped both hands, gently as he does, and tried to examine the bloody messes. Thankfully there was no glass, but they were bleeding from where the skin was busted and bruised. Rather than answer the Kid, Scott only gave off a deep and needed laugh. Johnny shook his head, not sure what to do with him. Instead he sat down and joined him. They sat there in silence for a while until Scott finally broke it. He looked around, not seeming to find what he was looking for, and just allowed his head to roll back into the door. “At times like this, I wish I still drank.” He looked over at the Kid, “but don’t ever drink. Horrible habit, really messes up your body.” Johnny nodded, staring at Scott’s beaten hands that were starting to look less and less like hands each moment. Scott noticed and gave a big cheesy grin as he held them up for better view. “Yeah, we should add smashing cars to that list as well.” “You going to be able to wrestle with those?” the Kid didn’t even know if he could call them hands anymore. Scott looked them over now that they were fully uncovered. “Yeah” he answered, “I’ll just have to tape the fingers tightly, cover the hands up pretty heavy with wrap, and put on some padded gloves. Wrestlers do it all the time.” Scott stopped for a moment and looked at Johnny, “which means if you ever have an opponent who has their hands wrapped and wearing heavy gloves likely has a broken hand, so stomp the living hell out of them if you get the chance.” The Kid looked at him in shock, “so aren’t you worried someone else will do that to you in the match?” “Tessa isn’t going to try and shatter my hands, even if she’s aware they are messed up” he told his student, “and Clyde Fox isn’t smart enough to know something like that. Terror, I don’t think has enough real training to realize shit like that. He seems more like one of those guys that pick it up as he goes, so I can’t let on that my hands are fucked.” He stopped talking so he could continue rewrapping his hands with the torn remains of the shirt. “Now if it was someone like LeStatt Knight, I’d be fucked. That guy would make me regret ever entering the ring with fucked up hands. But Andy Murray, he’d know as well, but he has his little code of honor and wouldn’t try to mess up my hands. I’m make him regret something stupid like that.” With his hands rewrapped, Scott lifted himself back up to his feet and motioned for Johnny to follow suit. Once they were both on their feet Scott made an unusual request; “hey can you punch me in the face?” “What!?” “Yeah, around the eye. Something to give me a good shiner.” Johnny stood there, not sure if this was some kind of test or not. Scott looked to be serious enough and given everything that had happened since the two had met, Johnny decided just to trust him. He reached back and blasted Scott right above the eye and almost off his feet. Scott touched gently where he had been struck and felt blood slowly seeping out. “Are you alright?” It was the million dollar question these last few hours. Scott probably heard it more today than he ever had in his entire life. He had probably asked it more than he ever had before. He shrugged his shoulders, not sure how to answer that. “You didn’t hit me that hard.” “That’s not what I meant.” Scott shuffled his feet, leaning against the tail end of the car. “I know.” “Well?” Scott didn’t answer. He didn’t want to answer. He could feel the eyes of the Kid on him though. This wasn’t going to get dropped, regardless of how Scott had felt. Not after he had just gone apeshit on a stranger’s car. Neither said a word, but nothing needed to be said. The question hung above both of their heads. It couldn’t stay like that, though. One of them needed to break the standoff. Johnny would be the one to do it. “Well?” Throwing his arms up into the air, Scott looked like he was ready to bust up enough car. “What the hell do you want me to say? You going to go all Doctor Phil on me? Oprah?” His tyrant had Johnny stumbling for words, “I…I dunno man, I’m just, you know, trying to help, you’ve been really pissed off and stuff. Just angry at the world recently.” “My daughters in the fucking hospital!” he shouted as he pointed toward the building in question. “She had to get her gah-DAMN stomach pumped. My wife is a shit mess and might be addicted to pills. And if that’s not enough!” Now his hand came back at Johnny, “if that’s not enough I just got my ass handed to me by Sha-FUCKING-man right before I have to fight for the World title, which by the way also involves that same wife still a fucking mess.” He almost seemed to be counting down his list now, “my best friend turned his back on me, tried to seriously hurt my wife.” Johnny could not get a word in anywhere, Scott Drake was absolutely seething at this point. The dam had broken open. “I would absolutely love to leave this business, but I’ve got a fucking high school education and my only work experience has been beating the hell out of someone or putting my dick in a whore. I couldn’t even get a job as a consultant or staff member because I’ve got a background in porn, a known drug offense, a criminal record a mile long, and apparently I have a bad fucking TEMPER.” He looked Johnny dead in the face. “WHAT.THE.FUCK.DO.I.HAVE.TO.BE.HAPPY.FOR?” That, Johnny couldn’t answer. Everyone had been saying that Scott was too negative, that he just seemed to be an unhappy person in general. Granted, those that knew him knew Scott was never exactly a ball of sunshine, but he had so much weighing on his shoulders for so long it seemed to be making him drown in his own misery. “She’s still alive, though” Johnny told him, hoping that would at least count for something. “Aislinn will be fine.” That lightened his face some, but Scott was still wearing a heavy scowl. “And you got me, one of the best students in the whole frickin’ planet!” Johnny tried to be funny. It was a really bad attempt to be funny. But maybe because it was so bad that it actually helped to lift Scott’s spirits some. He did, after all, now have a student he felt he could pass on his knowledge to so that it didn’t die with him. To Scott, that did count for something. “WHAT THE HELL! MY CAR!” Scott and Johnny turned and saw a man frantically running toward him. It was pretty easy to deduce that he was the owner of the car. Johnny slowly backed away but Scott pushed him off the trunk and started walking toward the guy, albeit with an exaggerated limp in his left leg. “What the hell happened?” the man asked again. Making sure to hold his arms up high enough so that the man could see the blood on the shreds of the shirt, Scott stopped him. He looked back toward the car, “some crazy shit man, that your car?” The man threw his arms up into the air, “of course it’s my car! What happened!?” Johnny continued to slowly back away but Scott stayed firm. “I’m telling you, dude, some fucked up shit. I’m walking to my car with my buddy over there” he pointed to Johnny “and suddenly I see a dude with a baseball bat just going to fucking town on your car. Crazy shit.” The man’s mouth dropped, “what did he look like?” Scott acted like he never heard him and continued on with his story, “you must have some kind of enemy or something, cause that guy was going nuts all over your car. I tried to stop him but the crazy s-o-b took the bat to me instead” he again held up his hands as proof. He glanced over to Johnny, “that guy was fuckin’ nuts, right?” Johnny nodded his head in agreement. Scott tried to place a hand on the man’s shoulder but pulled it away in pain. He used the hand to point toward the now developing shiner of his eye, “guy gave me this here knock as well. Thankfully he didn’t use the bat though, then I’d be looking worse than your car.” “What did he look like?” the man repeated, not really caring how much of a beating Scott had taken. Scott looked at Johnny for a moment before turning back to the guy. “I dunno, he was short. Well…kinda short. Like I said, I dunno, I guess he would be short compared to someone like me. Though if he didn’t have that bat with him, I’m telling you, you wouldn’t be worrying about your car right now. I would have knocked the shit out of that pisser. Wouldn’t I?” “Yeah, yeah you would have” Johnny said in agreement. Giving a half laugh, Scott shook his head “would have beaten the crap out of him, if it wasn’t for that bat of course.” Seeing that he wasn’t getting any help from Scott, the man pulled him away and started to examine the degree of damage done to his car. Opening the driver’s side door, he had to push the windshield out of the way as he surveyed for anything that might have been stolen. Scott chimed in though, “ah don’t worry, my buddy and me made sure he didn’t take anything. Really it looked like he just wanted to bust your shit up. I guess you must have did something to piss him off. You fuck his wife in the ass in front of him or something?” Giving off what had to be his first laugh in a long time, Scott seemed to be enjoying himself as he jerked this guy around. The poor man seemed on the brink of tears. “Look, man, I feel real bad. I honestly do, I even told my buddy here how bad I felt.” It was the cue for Johnny to chime in, “oh yeah, said he felt just horrible.” “Simply turrible” Scott said, doing his best Charles Barkley impersonation. He reached into his pants and pulled out his wallet, “now I know it won’t help, but man I really and honestly feel for ya. You ain’t got no ride home now and I felt like I could have did something to help. But again, dude did have a bat.” “A huge bat” Johnny added, holding out his arms much longer than what any real bat would ever be. “Some A-Rod piece of wood.” Scott pulled out almost all of the cash in his wallet and tossed it on the hood of the car for the man. “Like I said, I know it doesn’t help, but hopefully this can at least get you a ride back home man, and maybe you can find the rat bastard who did this.” The man took the money and started to count through it, his eyes lighting up. “This is like five hundred dollars!” “Yeah, not much, but man hopefully it helps” Scott told him, somehow managing to keep from busting out laughing. “Look, I need to get my hands checked out” he held them up for the third time now “they look real bad. But let me give you my number so you can call me and that way I can help fill out a police report. It’s the least I could do.” The man nodded, not even taking his eyes off the money. Scott had Johnny hand him a pen from the center console of the car and Scott just scribbled [detail withheld] onto the piece of paper. He handed him the paper and quickly made an exit with Johnny at his side. Once they were out of hearing range Johnny burst into laughter. “You’re not normal.” Scott just shrugged, “can’t be normal to be in a business like this. All of us are broken in some way.” Johnny looked him over, “what about you?” “Me? I forgot how to smile.” Come Rampage, the only way Chris Storm was going to smile was when the referee wrapped that beautiful gold belt around his waist. Anything less than that? He might as well not even bother to show up. This was win or go home and he was in it to win it.
