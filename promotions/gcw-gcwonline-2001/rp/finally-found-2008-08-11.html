Finally Found

11 August 2008

Date: 8/11/08 The room was dark and damp the only thing lighting the way was a few black pillar candles scattered on the dirt floor. Two ornate sarcophagi were in the center of the room. Both looked to be made of black onyx encrusted with rubies, emeralds and diamonds. The lid of the one on the right slowly slid open enough for the form of a nimble young man to slip out. His ebony hair was tied back just enough to accent his handsome features. He slowly walks toward the other sarcophagus and slides the lid open. Inside lay Cleopatra; her eyes slowly fluttered open with a smile playing on her lips. “Good morning my love,” he cooed, “Are you hungry?” Cleo nodded her head franticly. He reached inside of his leather trench coat unsheathing a small dagger. He sliced his wrist the blood was dark and flowed freely. Cleo drank until he pushed her away. “Not too much love or you will drain me completely.” He brushed her dark hair off from her face as he looked down on her with admiration. “I am still hungry.” Cleo protested. “Then it is time for me to show you how to hunt.” He smiled as he helped her out of her resting place. “Do you think I’m ready?” Cleo asked with her eyebrows knitted together with skepticism. He closed the distance between them swiftly. He encircled his arms around her waist and looked deeply into her eyes. “The day I found you I knew you would be a wonderful companion Cleo. You had that killer instinct imbedded in your eyes love.” Paul sat on the edge of the bed watching Delylah toss and turn. She had been having a rough night. So rough that she jostled Paul out of the bed. He wished that there were something that he could do to stop these dreams. He knew that if he even tried at this point she would never forgive him. She was using these dreams to find a way to save Daniel, whom ever he was, and to get rid of her sister for good. He loved her with all his heart and he really did want to marry her. She told her mother that they didn’t have time for that right now. That they had too much happen in the past few weeks to even consider it. He reached over and unlocked the nightstand that was on his side of the bed. He pulled out a small black box that he had gotten last week from Tiffany’s. He slowly opened the lid and looked at the beautiful one carat heart shaped diamond engagement ring and sighed. He felt Delylah starting to stir and tried to put the ring back in the drawer before she was fully awake. As he turned around he realized it was too late; her eyes were wide open and watching every move he made. “What are you doing Paul?” She asked as she slid upright in the bed. “Damn,” he muttered under his breath, “Nothing babe. What are you doing up?” “I knew you weren’t here beside me. You know I can’t sleep without you beside me.” She purred as she slid closer to him, pressing her supple breasts against his back. Delylah peered over his shoulder trying to see what he was hiding. She knew that there was a reason he was awake in the middle of the night. Paul always slept straight through the night, unless she woke him with one of her nightmares. Once her eyes adjusted she saw the small black box that was mostly engulfed by his massive right meat hook. “What is that in your hand Paul?” “Nothing,” he hesitated, “It’s nothing.” “Don’t lie to me John Paul Samson. I can see the box in your hand.” Delylah raised her voice slightly and tried to rip the box from his hand. “You know I’m stronger than you so why are you even trying.” Paul chuckled at her feeble attempt. “Come on Paul. Let me see please.” Delylah pretended to pout bringing a smile to Paul’s face. Paul sighed heavily and looked down at the box. He knew this wasn’t the right time to do this but she would be persistent until she found out what was in the box. He turned on the bedside lamp and looked at her with misty eyes. “Del, we have been through a lot together over the past year. You know I would walk through fire for you, do anything in my power to protect you for always. I love you Del with all my heart,” he paused to look deeply into her eyes, searching for some kind of reaction, “You are my other half. The missing piece that everyone searches for and some don’t find.” Paul looked at her and seen a tear slowly sliding down her right cheek. He brushed it away and opened his hand revealing the black box. Delylah’s face was full of mixed emotions. He slowly opened the lid to reveal the engagement ring. Delylah’s jaw dropped. She looked at Paul then the box and then back to Paul. “Del” will you,” Paul let out a woosh of air, “marry me?” Paul closed his eyes tightly waiting for her to erupt with either joy or anger. He was hoping for the joy. “Oh Paul. I love you so much. I”,” She paused trying not to make a hasty decision. “Umm?” “It’s ok we can wait if you want. I’ll just take this back.” Paul dismissed it all as being a silly whim. “Don’t you dare do that Paul. Did I say I wasn’t going to marry you?” Delylah shot him an accusatory look. Paul just shifted his eyes to the floor. “I would be happy to be your wife Paul. Lets just wait on the actual marriage.” Delylah kissed him softly making him smile. “WOOOOOHOOOO!” Paul screamed at the top of his lungs causing Del to let out a giggle. He fumbled with the engagement ring, almost dropping it before he had it securely on her left ring finger. “You have made me the happiest man alive Delylah Nguyen.” Paul wrapped his arms tightly around her. Delylah sat there with a far off look in her eyes like something was bothering her. Paul immediately thought that she regretted her answer. “You’re not reconsidering this are you?” Paul asked her with an apprehensive tone. Delylah quickly snapped back to reality, “No Paul I..,” she paused for a moment looking for the right words, “I just hope that she don’t destroy our happiness. I wish my father were here. How the hell did my mother know what you had planned?” All of her thoughts poured out of her mouth. Paul chuckled and cuddled next to her in bed. “I didn’t tell her Del. I don’t know how she knew. Maybe with all the things that have happened to her she may have become clairvoyant. Anything is possible when someone is put under enough stress babe.” Paul reassured her. The cuddled down in bed and in minutes they were both back to sleep with wistful smiles on their faces. That smile would soon be ripped from Delylah’s face as disturbing dreams plagued her. A young couple strolled through what looked to be a park of some kind. It was filled with various basins and statues. The moonlight reflecting off the nearby river made this a very romantic spot. The couple stopped to enjoy the moonlight sky and watched a basin spray water up into the air. They kissed passionately. They didn’t realize they were being watched. “Now Daniel.” Cleo demanded in a whisper. Daniel winced but did as he was told. They pounced on the young unsuspecting lovers. Blood sprayed from their carotid arteries as Cleo and Daniel drained them of their lives. Daniel wept as he watched Cleo push their bodies into the river. Every time he took a life he wept for their souls, his soul. “Come Daniel before we are seen?” Cleo said as she grabbed his arm and drug him through the park. “Please mother” kill me. Please don’t make me do this anymore. I can’t do this.” Daniel sobbed. “You will do this. I cannot kill you Daniel. You are all I have left.” Delylah’s eyes snapped open. “He’s” he’s? my nephew.” Delylah shook Paul violently, “Wake up Paul please.” Paul’s eyes slowly flitted open. “What” What is it Del?” He asked groggily. “Daniel is’my nephew.” She stammered, “I” I? know where they are Paul. They were traveling the Seine River.” She rambled, “They are near the Louvre Museum.” Delylah laughed hysterically when Paul shot her a fretful look. “We know where they are Paul. We can stop her. Then we will be married in Les Tuileries.” Paul looked at her like she was a mad woman; he knew no good would come of this trip. He feared that she would be hurt, maimed, killed. He had a gut feeling that something horrible was going to happen. It was no use trying to talk her out of going there; she had a mission to complete. She would complete her mission if it was the last thing she ever did. It was the only thing that stopped her from being completely happy. That was the one thing Paul always wanted; her happiness. “Book the flight babe. I’ll be right by your side.” Paul whispered hesitantly. “I will Paul. When this is all over we will finally be happy. I promise we will be happy.”
