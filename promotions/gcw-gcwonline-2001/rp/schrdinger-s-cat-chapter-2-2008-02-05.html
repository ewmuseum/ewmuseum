Schr’dinger's Cat: Chapter 2

5 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 5th, 2008 Some time ago? “I love you. You know that right?” she pleaded. The tears had begun to flow once more. She had tried to explain what happened but she knew there was no explanation good enough for him. She had made a mistake and she was going to pay for it now. The hustle and bustle of the restaurant was almost like the buzz of a fly to him. Antonio had just heard what he least wanted to hear. He knew that he might not have always been the best boyfriend, but he had never cheated on her. He loved her. He had no idea how he would be able to live with what she had done. “I’m sorry. I made a mistake. I didn’t know how much you mattered to me.” As much as she tried to apologize, her transgression was something that he couldn’t forget. Antonio had given Justine his trust and she had slapped him in the face in return. Antonio got up and threw a hundred dollar bill on the table almost spitefully and walked away. Justine saw him walking away almost as if it had been in slow motion. She would love him forever and he would love her as well. --- Clouds rolled in an orange sky. The smell of sulfur made it feel as if hell had finally taken over the world. If you asked any of the inhabitants of that Earth, they would probably say that it had. There were no birds in the sky and no animals on the ground that could easily be seen to the untrained eye. Life was scarce. For years, people died. They died of everything imaginable, disease, hunger, suicide. It was as if they’d have preferred their lives to have ended with whatever had happened in that world. The more he thought about it, the more he began to believe how lucky he had been, even after all the misfortune in his life. It had been hours since he had gotten inside the dusty old sedan and taken off into what was a very familiar horizon but at the same time a different one. It was unnerving. Watching the same buildings pass by, the same infrastructure was bizarre. The only thing that was not the same was the lack of vegetation. Streets that in his universe were riddled with plant life were now barren. He imagined it was the stuffy and arid air and the fact that he hadn’t seen a rain cloud in all the time he had been there that made him think of what had happened. It made his imagination run. He sat there, looking to this strange world and imagining how it might have happened. A million of unwelcome thoughts made their way into his mind. He had been thinking of his home since he got there. He hoped that there was a way for him to go back to his life, a way to find a level of normalcy that had been lacking from his life for the past ten years. The car came to a full stop for the first time since they had left the warehouse. Antonio opened the door and got out. In front of him stood the man that had brought him here, tapping his black cowboy boots on the gravel. The man went over to Antonio and hugged him tightly, as if they had known each other for years? and they had, in a way. Burton had been his best friend since forever. They had gone through highs and lows in their relationship, but they were always there for each other. It was surprising to see him leading so many men. Burton was a desperate man trying to survive in a barren wasteland like the one they were in. “I trust the trip wasn’t too uncomfortable,” he said as he signaled two of his men to check the surrounding areas. The world had become a real challenge. It was hard to survive by oneself. “You can never be too safe,” he said when he saw Antonio looking at the men drawing their weapons and spreading around the area where they stood. “What is this place” Why did you bring me here?” Antonio asked confused. Everything was still a mess inside his head. As much as he wanted to believe that it was his long time friend in front of him, he knew that it wasn’t. He was talking to “The Raven”, that is how he was known as, and he knew that if he stepped out of line, he had the power to peck his eyes out. “This place will hold special significance to you, as you will come to find out. Walk with me.” Burton started walking with Antonio closely behind. His bodyguards had given him the signal that it was safe and he had to convince Antonio to help him. “When the stuff went down that created the world as you see it,” he waved around at their barren surroundings, “there was really only one thing you could do to survive. Do you remember one of those post apocalyptic movies way back when” It was everyone for themselves with survival the first thing in their minds.” They passed besides what had been an old tree and what was now just a skeleton of what it had been before. The two men arrived in front of what seemed like a piece of rock protruding from the ground. “Is this what you wanted to show me?” Antonio asked perplexed. He looked closely and saw the word written of the face of the stone. Most of it had been washed away with time, but he could see a name very clearly. It read “Antonio Vargas del Valle”. He staggered back. All the air in his lungs had suddenly left him and he was short of breath. It was like some invisible punch to the stomach. He saw Burton’s face and something that he had never seen before in the Burton he knew back home. There was some sort of sick pleasure in watching his face in such pain and agony. Antonio couldn’t talk. He knew he had been killed in this world, but it was still surprising to see one’s own tombstone. He realized something then that he hadn’t expected. He looked around and saw some marks that were still standing that let him know where he was. He was standing in front of what was in his world Justine’s tombstone. Too many questions raced through his mind at blinding speeds. Suddenly they both heard a loud bang and a howl. They turned around and saw Burton’s crew moving towards them with people shooting from behind them. Antonio could see as the puffs of sand were raised into the wind after a bullet had hit the ground. He looked to his side and saw Burton as he grasped a pistol from his holster and began shooting. Antonio went to his knees and took cover behind a stone tombstone. Before long, the size of Burton’s crew and the better weapons had made them able to push the attackers back to where they came from. Burton fired his last round before he stopped completely as they had already gone too far outside his reach. From the corner of Antonio’s eyes, he saw a figure walking slowly towards Burton. Antonio saw the figure holding a weapon and ran towards him connecting with a tackle that sent both men crashing down. Antonio pinned the man’s hands behind his back and was able to hold steady the struggling body of the attacker. The man was cursing Antonio until he saw the face of the man that was holding him down. “You?” he asked astounded. “Yes me!” answered Burton before any words could be exchanged between Antonio and the man. He moved towards them and picked up a cowboy hat the man had been wearing. Burton reloaded his weapon as he reached the two men. Before Antonio could say anything, Burton raised his arm and aimed the pistol at the man’s head. The shot fired was deafening. Antonio had to turn away as his eardrums felt like they were being smashed by sledgehammers. When he looked back to the man he had been holding down, he could see that the face that had been there a second before was nothing more than a mass of blood and flesh. He stood up, still transfixed with what had happened. Antonio felt the blood on his clothes and quickly tried to clean it away. The blood on his hands wouldn’t wash away without water. “Why did you do that” Who were those people?” Antonio couldn’t help but ask whatever questions popped into his head. He saw the same look in Burton’s face that he had seen earlier. “They have been trying to kill me and steal our supplies for a long time now. I don’t know who that is, but he would have just tried to destroy me again if we had let him go.” The crew was walking back towards them. “You can stay here for a little while longer, but I need to take care of some business back in the city. Stay out of trouble,” Burton said before he tipped his hat, holstered his gun, giving it a twirl in the process, and walked away. Antonio sat down, trying to understand everything that had happened. When he tried to make some sense out of everything that was happening, it just made his head hurt. There was no rhyme or reason to anything that was going on. He was stuck in a parallel universe where the world was a post apocalyptic wasteland, where his best friend was transformed into some Hollywood-like survivor, where the Antonio of this universe had been murdered for some idiotic reason or other. It was all too much. He sat there on the desolate ground expecting to wake up from this unbearable dream. The only sounds he could hear were those made by the wind and nothingness. Silence. Silence truly makes the most beautiful music. As he contemplated his life and his death, Antonio thought about a way of getting out of that place. There had to be a way. A deep audible sigh emanated from the innermost place in Antonio’s insides. So he sat there, hoping something would happen that would take him out of this place and back to his life, back to a world where he was still alive. After a few minutes of sitting in silence with his thoughts, he heard a thud. He came out of his trance and looked to his right side where Burton’s lackey had been standing keeping a vigilant eye. He was crumpled on the floor, his blood had stained an already blood soaked earth. Antonio stood up and looked all around him but couldn’t see anyone. “Who is out there” Show yourself!” He finally turned around and he saw a face, before the only thing he saw was black. His body fell limp to the ground after the butt of the rifle had hit him square on the forehead. --- The ringing of the phone woke him up from the determined trance that kept him scrutinizing his opponent and figuring out what he must do in order to come out the victor. He stood up after pressing pause and answered the phone. “Hello” Who is this?” When he heard the answer to his question he stood up. He had no idea why the police would be calling his house, but they were. He had a good friend in the force and he had met a lot of the cops in the San Juan area. “She what” You must be kidding? yes? yes? I’ll be there.” He sat down after the phone fell from his hand. It hadn’t been more than a month and a half or so since he had broken up with her and now she was gone. Deep down, he had hoped that they would get back together. He would never see her lovely eyes full of life again. Antonio grabbed the keys to his car and went to the morgue to identify the body.
