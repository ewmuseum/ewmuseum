The Machine and the terrible, horrible, no good, very bad day

12 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/12/05 Whatever can go wrong will, at the worst possible time. -Murphy's Law Ever have one of those days where life just doesn't smack you in the face, but it kicks you in the balls, and then when you're down it kicks you in the teeth? I have. It's today. Where to start? Well I missed my wakeup call this morning so I was an hour late checking out of my hotel room, and getting on the road. When I get to the airport, it turns out the Wylde Tyme Entertainment Corporate jet, which Jeff had graciously lent me was having engine problems, so I was delayed another hour while the jet was fixed. I sat in the airport bar and seriously contemplated getting drunk, but decided against it when my phone rang. 'Talk to me.' When I hung up I seriously wanted to punch something. I now had to fly out to Hollywood to the set of The Smuggler due to problems with one of the sets. So three hours later I'm looking at a set that looks like it would fall apart if a strong breeze blew through the studio. 'What the fuck is this supposed to be?' I asked, too tired at this point to be mad. 'It was the Coliseum set.' Steven Speilberg explained to me, 'But this...this...' 'Is a piece of shit.' I said, 'Where's the set designers...no, you know what? Fuck it, call ILM and see if they can whip up a CGI coliseum set, just green screen the whole fucking thing and just digitally add the rest of it later. And fire the construction crew, hire a new one.' 'That's gonna cost you, especially in the middle of a shoot.' Speilberg said as we walked out into the studio lot where the limo he had sent to pick me up sat idling. 'I don't fucking care.' I said, 'I want a movie that looks good, that's why I'm glad you signed on to do this. I want a Steven Speilberg movie, not a Charles Band movie. Just make it happen Steve, send the bill to Wylde Tyme Entertainment when you hire the new crew.' Back in the limo I again contemplated raiding the mini bar and getting drunk, but again I decided against it. Now that I was 30,000 feet up in the air and was on my way to Atlanta, I felt I could relax. Yeah right. Murphy loves me. More engine troubles. We had to land the plane somewhere in Bumfuck, Egypt. Actually it was sone little flyspeck town in Kansas. So there we were, Phil the pilot, and myself at 4am in the fucking morning, with some mechaninc who looked like his family tree didn't fork looking in the engine. Cletus mumbling 'Eeeeyp' every five minutes. Just fix the fucking plane you inbred asshole! I thought as I sat off to the side and contemplated getting drunk again. But the booze was on the plane and by the time Bubba and Phil had gotten the damn plane fixed I was too tired to do anything but fall asleep. When I woke up it was somewhere around 9am monday morning and we were just making our approach to Atlanta. Well, I'd finally made it, everything had to be okay from here on out right? That's a big negatory Ghost Rider. Shit meet fan. Fan meet shit. 'What do you mean you don't have a reservation for me?' 'Well it's not in the system sir.' 'Well check the fucking system again, lady. I need a car and I need it now.' I said, trying very hard not to raise my voice. 'I'm not getting anything.' 'Look lady, I have had a bad fucking day that started at 12 yesterday afternoon, listen to me very carefully, the name is Savitch, S-a-v-i-t-c-h. First name Michael, M-i-c-h-a-e-l. Find my fucking car or I am going to get rather upset, rather quickly, and I can promise you if that happens I will not be the only one having a bad day. Do I make myself clear?' I had leaned over the counter to deliver this point and she had invoulntarilly backed away from me, probably afraid that I was going to leap over the counter and tackle her to the floor and just take the first set of car keys I found. I was pretty damn close to that point. 'What's going on out here, Phoebe, what is...' This dickhead must be the manager. Yep, that's what his little fifty cent name tag says. 'Wait a second, aren't you Mike Savige, the wrestler?' I arched an eyebrow. Well it looks like Mr. Manager was a fan. 'Yes, I am, and if I don't get my goddam rental car, I'm going to show your reciptionist here my finishing move.' I said, backing up but not loosing any of my temper. I was already four hours behind any semblence of a schedule Caldera had lined up for me. 'Excuse me Pheobe,' he said moving past her and to the computer. He tapped some keys, frowned at the screen, typed something else in, frowned again and then typed something else in. His eyes lit up. 'There it is.' He said trimphantly, 'It turns out that there was a slight typo when your car was reserved Mr. Savige. The I and T in your name were accidentally switched.' 'Great, can I just have my car and go? I'm hours behind schedule and I just want to get to my hotel and forget about the last twenty one hours.' I said. I snatched my keys from Mr. Managers hand and left the car rental agency holding my tounge by not telling him to get a fucking receptionist who could fucking spell. As I drove to my hotel the Imperal March played on my cellphone and I snatched it up. 'WHAT?' I yelled into it. 'Bad day?' 'You could say that.' I said to Cindy. 'I'm only four hours behind the schedule I think I had when I woke up in Atlantic City yesterday afternoon.' I then proceeded to tell my wife exactly why I was four hours late. 'Fortunatley the manager was a fan and he found out that the little bubblehead....FUCK! ASSHOLE! Goddamn it! What is it with people in the south and the inability to fucking drive?' I grumbled as I narrowly avoided a collision with an SUV that decided that pulling out into traffic without checking to make sure the road was clear was a good idea. 'This has just been a shit day hon.' 'I wish I could be there to relive some of that tension.' 'Me too.' I said, 'It's been a rough day, I just wanna get to the hotel and lay down and take a decent fucking nap. I've had maybe four hours sleep, I'm tired, hungry, and I feel grimey. I need sleep, food and a shower though not in that order.' 'Call me back when you've had all three, you sound like you need it, plus, I know how you are when you're in a bad mood.' Cindy said. 'I went past bad mood around four this morning sweetie.' I said, before saying goodbye and snapping the phone shut. As I reached the hotel, I prayed to whatever higher power there was that my hotel room better be reserved under the correct spelling of my name or someone was going to get tossed around that hotel lobby until I was satisfied that I had broken every bone in their body. A few minutes later all seemed well until I stuck my keycard in the door and frowned as it didn't click open. 'Motherfucker...' I mumbled. I stuck the keycard in again thinking that maybe I didn't stick it all the way in the first time. 'Bastard...' I wiped the magnetic strip on the key on my shirt thinking maybe it was dirty, 'Open Goddamn you.' I said slipping the key in again. 'Get me a keycard that works or I swear I will kick that fucking door off it's hinges!' I growled to the concierge five minutes later as I had tossed the keycard down on the desk explaining that it didn't work. And trying (unsuccesfully) to calmly explain that I had tried wiping the magnetic strip, turned the card around and even upside down to get it to work and it didn't. 'So either get me another room or get me a working card. I've had a very long, very bad day, I've only had four hours of sleep in the last...' I checked my watch, 'Twenty two hours. I want a room, and I want one now, if I don't get one I am going to get very upset, and I'm probably going to give the lawyers at CEI fits when I get done being very upset. Do I make myself clear?' I think the poor guy wet himself. 'Yessir, Mr. Savitch. I'll fix it right away.' 'You'd better.' I said. Normally I'd be pleasant, but this was not normal. It was bordering on a full twenty four hours that I'd been awake, and all I wanted was for this day to end. I don't care what Caldera had planned for me tomorrow. I was shrugging it all off for at least twelve hours of uninterupted sleep. Five minutes later I got into my room with a newly coded keycard and put the 'Do not disturb' sign on my door, tossed my bags on the floor, kicked off my shoes and collapsed into the confort of the bed. I sat up for a moment and turned off my cell phone, and the ringer on the room phones. 'And Murphy,' I said aloud, 'Leave me the fuck alone.' It had been a terrible, horrible, no good, very bad day.
