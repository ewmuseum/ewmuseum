Date: 5/7/2009 Puzzles…I like puzzles. Just the way you tinker around a puzzle to see how it works, you desperately want to solve the puzzle but you also want to know how it works. Is that why the human race is so interesting? It is because it is one giant puzzle? No we don’t want to solve this mystery, not yet at least. We want to tinker around and figure out who we are and why we are here, that is the fun part. Our brains and bodies are the keys to a much bigger puzzle, but unlocking it is just the endgame. Of course, if we come up short, that is fine too, just making sure we are progressing is the only thing that matters, at least to me that is. * * * Lucas sat upon the roof of his house that he recently bought in Greenwich. He never needed to work a day in his life but he always sacrificed himself for the greater good. Just because he had a trust fund didn’t mean he had to take shortcuts. No, it meant he had to work extra harder just to meet the extra demand and expectations of his father. Sadly, not everyone saw the same way he did. His brother Leonard ‘Leo’ Quinton was more of a mystery, a puzzle; he would disappear for months at a time, getting jailed here and there in every other country he visited, but he would always be bailed out by his father….their father. Everything he did and didn’t do was praised by their father; he always thought the best of Leo and would tell Lucas to be more like him. Lucas could not understand this phenomenon, even his mother, who was as level headed as him bought into the fathers praises. That was not Lucas’ world, not anymore at least… “Hell” said Lucas as he reached down to put another nail in the shingles. “Maybe I should have hired that bloke anyway, would have saved me a whole mess of trouble.” He lines up another shingle and nails it with the nail compressor. He stands up and looks at what he accomplished. “Well, at least that is over with.” The shingles suddenly break down easily like bread. “Damn!” “Crap!” “Of All Bloody Well Things!” “Hey, boss, what are you doing?” says a man down below. Lucas squints down at him, he can’t hear what the man is saying. “WHAT?” “WHAT ARE YOU DOING” “WHAT??” “WHAT…..ARE…..YOU……DOING?!” yells the man even louder. Lucas wipes his forehead with a rag and descends the ladder. As he descends he comes upon a short stocky man who glares at Lucas with intense hatred. “What do you think you are doing, sir?” questions the man. “Tell the owner of this house that he can’t send out contractors at 6:30 in the morning. Now I want you to pack up your stuff and come back a later time.” “Well actually I-‘ “Well actually I nothing, sir. I can call the police on you and make them escort you off the premise, now scram.” “Heh, well actually I am the owner of this house, I am Lucas Quinton…” Lucas extends his arm for a hand shake. “Wait, wait you are Lucas Quinton? Heh, well, sorry about that mix up there, Mr. Quinton. I thought you were another one of those contractors who can’t follow simple instructions. The rules still stands though, you can’t be out here this early doing all that constructing work and whatnot.” “I’m sorry, mate, I didn’t quite exactly catch your name?” “Oh, mercy me, well I am John Irons, I live at the house right next to you with my life partner and my two ADORBALE pet poodles Billy and Grinder.” “Entirely too much information, mate; listen, the roof is leaking and I want to get this done as soon as possible, now if you will excuse me, I have work to attend to.” “No no, sir, as team captain of the homeowner’s associate, I request that you stop immediately.” “And as the guy who can beat the bloody piss out of you…sod off.” * * * It is much darker now, two men are sitting inside drinking a couple of beers. One of the men is obviously Lucas, but the other man is unknown. “Did you really tell him to ‘sod off’? “Yeah, I did actually. Do you think it was too harsh? You know me, I am generally not known as a mean spirited kind of person, but I do think I did snap at the poor bloke pretty harshly” “Well, I think you don’t want to piss off the new neighbors, I mean, you did violate their homeowners handbook or whatever.” “Really? Was I too harsh?” “A bit, yeah.” “Maybe I should go apologize…” “Forget about him, now tell me, Lucas, now that you have this fancy house, albeit the leaking roof, and now that you are living here for the time being, what are you going to do?” “A lot…” “What is that supposed to mean? A lot?” “It means I am going to do what I came here to do, Chris, a lot.” The man was Chris ‘Million’ (Brooks), he is a longtime friend and first time manager to Quinton. Chris was the type of person who didn’t take no for an answer, he always got what he wanted out of the deal and never settled for less. It was his duty to Lucas to accustom him to his new housing arrangements. Chris himself was a puzzle as well, for his outer shell, he had a tough rigid look to him that made people want to be him, but on the inside he had a hidden agenda, a different way of looking at things, things he did for his own well being, his self preservation. “So don’t worry about me, just worry about my other affairs that you are in charge of taking care of. I just finished unpacking and this place just seriously stressed me out to bloody Sunday, if you know where I am coming from. I need to get another drink, a shot or two, and fall the hell asleep. You and I both know that I really need it.” “Drinking and Sleeping doesn’t really help.” “Sure has been working for me fine, Chris, thanks for your concern.” “I can see you are really stressed out too, I mean with the situation of your family and al—“ “Honestly, mate, I really don’t want to dwell into my personal family life right now, it’s bad enough I have to think about them everyday, but I don’t need you of all people to tell me about my family affairs.” “Fine, that’s all good, now tell me what you want and I will make it happen.” “I want to sleep; really, all I want to do is fall asleep and wake up a different person.” Chris gets up from his chair; he nods to Lucas and starts walking towards the door. “Well, I guess…” “Sleep” “I will see you later, Lucas, take care, bro.” “Sleep” … “Sleep” * * * Dreaming can have a lot of effects on someone. When you lay down and go to sleep you think the day is all over with, but in reality, it just began. You sleep and you dream, you dream about things you can’t have, things that you want to grasp in your hands, the one thing in your life that makes you whole, and you can do nothing but dream. “No, no, please don’t!” yells Lucas in his sleep. He is tossing and turning around in his bed. … “Please I beg you!” … “Let me go!” … “Give me one more chance, just one more chance, I promise, I promise it won’t happen again!” Lucas shoots up from his bed, he is sweating profusely. All the pain and agony he just suffered, he soon forgot. He looked on the clock and it reads 4am. “I can’t do this anymore.” He declares as he takes a drink from his glass. “No, not anymore, this is taking a toll, listen to me, I am talking to myself for bloody well sake.” He reaches in his table and pulls out a notebook. The book seems to be entered with other material, he begins a new page. The dreams have been getting worse and worse. They are so real and vivid, like they happened to me, but why is it that I can’t remember? Do I have some sort of repressed memories? Every night this happens, on and on the same thing plays over and over again like the scene in a movie. Each day is different but each night is the same excruciating thing. What is happening? Something is not right, and I am going to figure this out if it kills me. Is my fate being tested? My heart? My soul? There are some unresolved issues at hand, but sooner or later I will come upon it and face it and be claimed victor unlike all those before me. ~ LQ * * * Words can’t begin to define us as who we are as people. We as the human race are born in a matter of hours and can die in a matter of seconds. We haven’t yet achieved our full potential as humans. No, we can’t let our grudges get the best of us, the wars, the famine, the danger of it all, it just doesn’t go away. Until we get rid of these lasting impressions we can’t move forward. Until we move forward, we have to make the best of things that we are given, no matter if its money or food or water or the gift of life, we have much to learn from one another. The people we meet every day are the ones who forge us as people, who forge our human race, their actions decide our own actions and when we finally figure this our, our lives will finally be something of worth. Once we figure out this puzzle our lives will mean something. It will all finally mean something…
