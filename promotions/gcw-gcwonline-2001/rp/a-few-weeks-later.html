A Few Weeks Later

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 252008 Flying through the sky at almost super-sonic speeds, the private concord aircraft of Gaius Octavianus on it's way back from an important business conference in Hong Kong, China. Gaius is seated in a big, throne like black leather chair in a black suit, Armani shirt, and black Gucci shoes. He is staring out the window and dwelling on this lingering problem he has with Fareed Zakaria. Sitting on the plane with him is his assistant Valeria, who is dressed as usual in a tight black business suit and her hair is up. She is typing away on a lap top. "Sir, We'll be landing in New York in about two hours." Gaius turns around in his chair, looks at his assistant, and nods. He then turns back around. Thinking to himself. 'The whole of my father's empire destroyed by a little worm with a videotape and a desire for fame. How could I have missed the videotape? How?!....I am so careful usually.' He continues to ponder his failure. In the weeks since his meeting with Zakaria, he has tried numerous ways to get him to turn over all the evidence to him. He has tried bribing him, threatening him, and even attempting to buy Newsweek Magazine! But all to no avail. 'I must think as my father would. "When you want to kill a dog, you don't cut off the tail, you cut off the head." I can not allow a global empire to fall simply because of an ambitious reporter. It is time I threatened him with more than simply a beating. ' Just then, a brilliant idea enters Gaius' mind. 'Of course.' Gaius turns his chair around to face Valeria. He speaks to her with an authoritative yet determined voice. "Valeria, get me Marco in Rome. I have need of one of his soldiers." "Whom should I request from him?" "Tell him to send 'The Turk.' And the put me on the phone with him." "Yes, Sir!" Valeria begins dialing the phone as Gaius grins menacingly. After a few moments, she gets this mercenary on the phone, who is an old childhood friend of Gaius,' and hands Gaius the phone. "Marco, Come stai?" "Gaius, It is good to hear from you." "You as well old friend. I am having trouble with a certain reporter from Newsweek magazine. He threatens to destroy me." "What can I do to help? Is it really that bad that you need 'The Turk?'" "It is. A plane is waiting at Fiumicino. I need him in Manhattan by tomorrow. I am through playing games with this nuissance." "How do you mean?" "Let's just say it may require the threat and use of Lethal force." There is a short silence on the other end of the phone. "Marco?" "I'm here, Gaius. There just may be another way." After a few inaudible sentences, Gaius has a smile on his face for the first time in a long while and only his response is clear enough to understand. "That's brilliant Marco, absolutely brilliant. When can you get it done?" "I can have everything set up by wednesday. See you in New York, old friend." "See you soon." Gaius hangs up the phone. Valeria turns to him. "What's happening, Sir?" "Shhhh" Gaius replies. "Not here." He puts his finger over his mouth. A look of pure, diabolitical evil ascends across his face. And as the plane zooms along back to New York, Gaius' thinks to himself. 'There are fates worse than death...' ....To Be Continued...
