What we long for...

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 Dec 05 There are days when I try my best just to fit in like everyone else. I try to just blend in, forget about the fact that I am over six and a half feet tall and weigh 270 lbs. It hardly ever works out the way I hope though. I try my best to do the things that people do, the things that people are suppose to like or even LOVE to do. I’ve let my mind go clear and not think about how different I really am though. People just can’t understand someone like me. No I am not claiming that I am some inhuman entity or alien. I just have a different mind set than most. I am not alone in this though, there are others who I can guarantee have the exact same mind set. It doesn’t matter where I am at or what I am doing, it never quite feels “normal” or I always feel out of place. The Mall for instance. I have to come here, the holidays are quickly approaching and gifts are customary for the ones you care about. I walk through the mall and I get approached, asked to sign autographs, and such. One set of young girls even asked me to go home with them. It is certainly always entertaining to see what they come up with indeed. Its not even the fame that makes me different either. Its something else. I walk through each different hallway the same as everyone else. The noise of other shoppers surrounds me, the different christmasy smells of pine, candy, and nuts mixed with different perfumes and colognes that are being tried on swirl through the air. I see all of the bright lights, the smiling faces, and the reds, whites, and greens. The bells jingle, the people dance, the music roars. The money flows, the stores sell, the food is eaten, children cry. I am in the middle of it all. But yet I am also not. Its almost as if I am watching a movie of someone else’s life. The sounds are there but seem muffled, the smells linger but seem artificial, the sights are right in front of me but yet seem dull and lifeless as if they were on an old television. I continue to move through the aisles and the stores though. I continue to spend my money and eat the food. I don’t let this feeling stop me from smiling and signing autographs when asked. Still something stops me from completely enjoying the events at hand. Something about who I am. What about sitting at home on a Sunday afternoon and watching the football games? Every man loves football yes? As the song says, “I Like it, I love it, I want some more of it. Something, something, something. I don’t know what it is about the pushing and shoving but I like it I love it?” well you should know the rest. Something about people just is drawn to professional football. So as I feel that I sometimes should, I buy me a case of Corona, some snacks and I sit down to enjoy that game of football. Its not what it should be sadly. The players seem to move in slow motion, the commentators speak monotone. The crowd is nonexistent; the entire image just appears black and white. I bite into the Doritos that I have open in front of me but it may as well just be decoration and a way to dirty my couch up. It crunches but I don’t taste anything. The beer goes down but I cannot taste it as it does. It might as well be water because that’s how bland it feels. I should be screaming at the screen as the Dallas Cowboys just blew another game, a team I have watched since I was a young boy. Yet I don’t. It’s just a game ? nothing more. I cannot grow excited about such a thing. My enjoyment for such trivial things died about six years ago. It was then that my life came into a very clean and clear focus. I understood what it was I was meant for and what could fill that emptiness in my life. It was six years ago that I began to understand exactly who and what I was deep down inside. I couldn’t enjoy the malls or the football games. I try to be normal believe me I do. I don’t necessarily wish for people to hate me. I do not necessarily enjoy the fact that people cheer and wish hateful things on me. Sometimes it may seem that way but it’s a fa’ade that I play very well. The only thing I even come remotely close to truly enjoying are the two people close to me. David Mary Each of them has helped bring that everyday color back into my life, in their own ways. David is the one person who has always stood by me. He is also the one who “showed me the light” for lack of a better phrase. After everything that had happened he showed me why colors were dull and how I could bring them back to life, even if only for a short chunk of time at any given moment. Then there’s Mary, the woman who showed me what emotions were again. When I am with her it’s as if she is full of vibrant colors, full of life but yet she still stands on a colorless backdrop. She is special that is for sure. Sadly not even they can bring the world to its full beauty. It takes more than that. It takes me to be in the only sanctuary that I have known for the past six years. It takes the feeling of ultimate power. Inside of my own personal Sanctuary I am given the power of GOD. I can take life and I can grant life. It is my decision who shall live and who shall die. Oh what a thrill it is. Six years ago I realized what it took to bring the color and sound back to my life. Six years ago I realized that things were never quite normal for me because I was more than a man. Six years ago I realized that while I may bleed, cry, hurt, and feel like a person I was something else. I was one of the lucky few. I was a Wrestler. The first time I heard music that belonged to me come on, causing a roar from the crowd it almost caused my ear drums to explode. I had never heard anything like it. The rush, the adrenaline, the roar of the crowd was like beautiful music. Then I stepped from behind those curtains for the first time. The flashes from the cameras are what normally blind people but not for me. For me it was the sudden flush of color and life that came to my eyes. It caused a lump to form in my throat. I had to fight back my urge to smile, to cry. It was the calling that I had always longed for. With each step I took closer to that ring, my heart began to pound even louder. When I finally climbed in I was worried that the fans might even hear that beating heart that pounded inside of my chest. Then it was time to actually get to business. I felt a sudden surge of emotion jolt through my body the first time I was hit in the face by a stiff right hand. It hurt that was for damn sure but it was a pain that I somehow invited. The pain brought a fleeting smile to my face. Then I hit back. Again And again And again And again. It was power that was absolute. In the palm of my hands was another man’s life. In the palm of my hands the future of another human being could be shattered or realized. It was intoxicating. No I would not go on to win that first match but it did not matter. I found what my life lacked. When I went back to the backstage area, back to real life the colors slowly faded, the emotions slowly died, and the sounds slowly muffled themselves again. Instantly I was hooked on it all. I needed it I wanted it I CRAVED it Six years ago my life became complete. Six years ago I became a wrestler. Six years ago I became one of the lucky ones. And Six years later? That hunger for life and that thirst for that power are as strong as ever. THIS Is LIFE
