The Killy Joke

1 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/1/07 Twizzy, stoned and exhausted, wandered into his bedroom in Brooklyn, New York. For the past twenty-four hours he had been trying to figure out a way to defeat both Jordan White and possibly Knight or Valentine at Warpath. Two competitors in one night. How could he do it? He found his recently Twistodium addicted girlfriend Janine lying on the bed where he left her, completely tranquil. “I think I’ve got it,” he said to her sleeping body. She had took to Twistodium quite easily. It was actually a simple transition. Janine had been taking Xanex for years and one day when she was going to refill a prescription she was handed a Twistodium tablet by Twizzy instead. “Mm,” she rose from slumber. “What is it, Davis?” He took a deep breath. “I think I have figured out how to beat both Jordan White and an unknown second opponent. Yes.” She stretched, “How?” Twizzy raised a finger to her and said, “A-ha!” as if a salesman trying to sell the town a pool table or a monorail or a cheese grater. “First,” he began, “I’ll take just the right amount of white and potato Twistodium to take on Jordan White. That’s first. Then, in the second round?” He waited, so excited to give away his secret. “I’m going to bash my opponent over the head with a bat!” Janine, coming off her high, looked shocked. “What?” “Not just any bat either. I’ll take a wooden bat and then I’ll wrap it in carefully frayed electrical wire and then plug it in. Then bzzzzt! all over the ring! How does that sound?” “That sounds awful,” her hand went to her mouth. Twizzy shook his head. “It’ll be perfect, I swear. Right over the head. It can’t fail!” “Won’t you get disqualified?” The thought hadn’t even occurred to Twizzy yet. “Well, maybe.” “You’re acting weird. Like The Joker or something.” “That’s perfect!” he shouted. “I’ll infect the city with my laughing gas and then no one will be able to wrestle! HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!” Janine was silent. “Well then I’ll infect the city’s water supply with my very special serum turning every fish’s face into my smiling image and then sell the patented Twizzy Fish for a huge profit! HAHAHAHAHAHA! “How is that going to help you win the Warpath tournament?” she asked. The thought hadn’t even crossed Twizzy’s mind. “Oh yeah.” A lightbulb flashed over his head. Well, metaphorically. “I’ve got it! I’ll hit whoever I face over the head with a big baseball bat. With electrical wiring around it!” Janine sighed. “Good idea.” Twizzy leaned over and kissed her on the cheek. “Thanks, Harley.” “What?” “I mean, Janine.”
