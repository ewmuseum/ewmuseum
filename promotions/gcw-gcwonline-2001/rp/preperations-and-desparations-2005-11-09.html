Preperations and Desparations

9 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/9/2005 Scene fades up as a reporter is waiting outside Cleveland's Gund Arena. A black Ford Escape pulls up behind her. The reporter tosses her cigarette away as the door of the escape opens. Reporter: It's about damn time. Out from the Escape steps Sam Wolack, in a pair of black jeans, a black bomber jacket, backwards black GCW baseball cap and dark sunglasses. Wolack: Better late than never. Reporter: You got that right. Cameraman: All right everyone, in 3..... The cameraman motions 2 and then 1. Reporter: This is Leslie Starks, outside the Gund Arena with GCW Wrestler, Sam Wolack. As the many wrestling fans in Cleveland knows, GCW is making it's return tomorrow night at this very building. Sam, what's your feelings about the match. Wolack: I feel very good about it. I'm the last participant to enter a gauntlet match against GCW's number 1 contender, 'Spitfire' Michael Stevens. This will be my first match since Devil's Night, and I anticipate to come out with a renewed vigor. Starks: Do you think that you'll have what it takes to defeat Stevens? Wolack: The only way to know for sure is if he got that far. The other two opponents in this match, Jayce Bradley and Vince Jones are out to prove that they want the GCW World Title. Me, I'm out to prove that I can run with the big boys in GCW. Maybe I'll take enough out of Stevens that he will also prove ineffective against Savige, Toxic and Borealis later in the night. Starks: As of late, you've been portrayed as a bad guy, is that how you are in real life? Wolack: Actually, I've been a shade of gray ever since ending that redundant X storyline. I'm not good, and I'm not bad. I'm somewhere in the middle. My problem is that Caldera Entertainment Inc doesn't cater to my style of wrestling. I highly doubt that we're going to see me in an Ultimate X match anytime soon. Personally, I'm starting to feel a little restrained by the limitations that are inherent in this promotion. I won't be in an ACWF match, as I'm not perceived as a top name in the promotion. That's why tomorrow night, I'm putting aside my differences with Coyle O'Conner to focus on making a name for myself. After everything is said and done, I want to be looked at as the guy that can be given the ball and can run with it. Starks: Is this you guaranteeing a win? Wolack: This is me guaranteeing that no matter what it takes, my name is going to be in lights after my match with Stevens. Win or lose, someone is going to notice me. Maybe I'll get shots at the World Title. Maybe I'll get the Main Event paydays. Starks: Thank you Sam. Tomorrow night, GCW returns to the Gund Arena with WorldWide. Tickets are still available through one of the many ticket brokers that operate here in Cleveland. For Action News, this is Leslie Starks. The light from the camera goes out as Leslie takes a cigarette from her jacket pocket. Starks: Kid, you know that you have no chance in hell of winning, right? You couldn't keep that pretty little girlfriend of yours safe. Wolack gets up in Leslie's face. He takes the unlit cigarette from her lips and breaks it in half in front of her face. Wolack: These things are bad for you. I suggest quitting. Scene fades to black.
