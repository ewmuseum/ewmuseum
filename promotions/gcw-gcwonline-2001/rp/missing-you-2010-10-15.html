Missing You

15 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/15/10 I. Dominic could feel his eyes burning a hole in him. Dominic was unable to get completely comfortable because of it. The eyes, while bright green and lively, terrified him. He never learned how to deal with such a problem. He isn’t even sure if he ever wants to learn. Dominic redirects his gaze to try and block the uneasiness in the room out but it does no good, those green eyes are still glaring at him. Trying to figure him out. Dominic doesn’t know why the eyes are fixed on him. Maybe they want something? Dominic was determined to find out. He turns and looks directly at them. A chill runs through his body. “You need something?” The mouth that rests beneath the nose which is beneath those green eyes opens. A laugh comes out. “Stop mocking me.” Another voice cuts in. “He giving you trouble?” Roberto walks into the room and immediately moves to his son, Joaquin Abitua, all of 3 years old now and the former GCW World Champion scoops the child into his arms. The laugh returns and Roberto smiles back. Dominic forces a smile as well. “Not at all.” Roberto takes a seat on a chair across the room from Dominic and places his son on his knee. A quick vision of the only man that Dominic had as something of a father figure forces Dom’s jaws to clench and makes it hard to breath. He hates that man more than anything else in his life. Roberto pretends not to notice especially when the anxiety seems to come and go like a breeze. “So how are you feeling?” Roberto questions. “You’ve got to be excited.” “I am.” Dominic clears his throat. “I guess it is a little fuc…um … it’s unbelievable.” Roberto chuckles. “You aren’t so good with words are you?” “Not always.” “Well,” Roberto adjusts Joaquin’s position. “Congratulations anyways. I have to admit that you are progressing at an even faster rate than I anticipated. Hell, you’re light years ahead of where I was at this point in my career.” “Like I said, unbelievable.” Roberto smirks and plays with his son for a few moments before continuing conversation. “Have you been paid yet?” Dominic doesn’t miss a beat. “I have.” “So?” A look of confusion takes over Dominic’s face. “So what?” “What was it?” More of the same … “Was what?” “You’re a young man. You’re becoming something of a celebrity, making more money than you ever have before. I know you bought yourself something special to mark the occasion.” Dominic hasn’t at least not yet. He shrugs his shoulders. “I haven’t actually.” Dom shakes his head. “I didn’t even think about it.” Now Joaquin’s green eyes seem to become accusing, like Dominic had failed in some monumental task. “Don’t seem so ashamed, Dom.” Roberto says with a quiet laugh. “There’s got to be something you want.” His mind races. His eyes meet Joaquin’s again. Something of an idea begins to form. II. “How did you even get this number?” Dominic doesn’t bother listening to the response and just hangs up his phone. Who knew becoming the TV Champion of GCW would bring forth so many weird people? He pockets the phone before placing his hand back on the steering wheel. A hell of a day. A hell of a week. He looks into his rearview mirror and eyes the Television Title sprawled out on his backseat. He winks to it. His eyes focus back on the road. Dominic has wanted to celebrate his victory and in a way, he has. Roberto took him out to dinner the night before. He had the biggest steak he has ever seen, side of mashed potatoes and one beer. Dominic doesn’t like to drink much. Mary and little Joaquin were there too. Joaquin was obsessed with Dominic and it freaked him out a little bit. Mary and Roberto thought it was hilarious of course. Dominic just felt queasy. Kids, not his strong suit. All in all though? It wasn’t a bad evening. Still, Dominic couldn’t help but think that something has been missing. It was Roberto who nudged his brain awake. Roberto didn’t do it intentionally but his asking what Dominic would buy with his money got him moving quickly. Dominic barely had enough time to thank them for everything before he hustled out the door and onto the open road. He would apologize later. For now he had a mission to accomplish, two in fact. He had to celebrate his new found success the proper way and he has to purchase his first item. Daunting tasks for sure, they both have to be perfect. He only has one shot at doing this the right way. He is confident that it will not be a disappointment. His phone comes alive again. “Unknown Caller.” Tentatively, he answers. “Yeah?” His face becomes overrun but the look of pissed offness “Are you fucking kidding me!? No you can not have the key to my room! I don’t care to catch something by bangin’ you lady.” He hangs up and tosses his phone in the backseat. He winks at the TV title again in the rearview mirror. III. Most guys Dominic’s age would’ve went a different route with his first purchase as a ‘well off’ person. He calls himself ‘well off’ because he knows he is nowhere near rich. The money in this pay period is definitely more than he’s seen at one time though. Dominic considered many different items to make him feel good. He thought of upgrading his car but there is so much history in the hunk of junk that he drives that he prefers to just wait until it falls apart naturally. His mind fooled around with the idea of revamping his entire wardrobe, too. He is going to be in the public spotlight from now on after all and would need to look more appropriate. Tapout shorts or sweats with a T-shirt probably won’t cut it. He reconsiders when he realizes that he will spend most of his time on a bus or in his car and inside arenas … he prefers comfort over style in that situation. Maybe a down payment on a house? One that he wouldn’t spend much time in. Take that one off the list. Season tickets to the Dallas Cowboys? The Longhorns? No and No. He doesn’t wear much jewelry and without ‘suiting up’ what would it matter even if he did buy a nice watch or two? No, none of those things were going to do for Dominic Wall. He didn’t need them. After much deliberation the answer hit him. He felt foolish for even thinking of anything else. He holds the items in his hands. Slowly he kneels down, eyeing his first purchase throughout the entire process of going to the grass on one knee, to placing it down on the ground all the way until he stands back up again. He admires it again and smiles. “I thought you’d like some flowers.” Dominic talks to no one but at the same time, the most important person in his life. The headstone in front of him reads ’Janelle Wall’. Dom stuffs his hands in his jean pockets as he looks around, admiring what a strangely beautiful day it is considering it is mid October. “I know I haven’t visited often,” His eyes fall back onto the grave. “At least not as often as I should. Sorry.” He goes silent. He doesn’t want to, in fact, he has much to tell the woman that took care of him his entire life. The one person that he absolutely knew that he could count on. He wants to tell her just how much she meant to him, how he never would’ve made it without her. He wishes he wouldn’t have been such a coward when everything happened … he wishes he could turn back time. He wants her to forgive him, to love him and to experience the life that is in front of him. Dominic wants his mother to be proud. He wants her to know this and a million other things. Things that he just cannot find the courage to say. Or has he? Dominic takes in a deep breath and smiles. Maybe he just did tell her everything he wants her to know. Maybe she understands. “I think you’d be so proud of me Ma,” He nods. “I hope you would be.” Now Dominic moves to sit down on the grass beside his adopted Mother’s headstone. He reads the name over and over again as if he is afraid of forgetting it. He can feel the tears forming inside. He licks his lips. “I didn’t want to share this with anyone but you.” Dominic suddenly sinks, the feeling of loneliness finding its way back into his heart. “You always said that I’d do something important. You said that you just knew that I had it in me.” The tears come. He doesn’t fight them. “I’m doing it Mom.” He kisses his index and middle finger of his right hand and then places them on the marker. “I really am.” He goes silent, not knowing what else to say. Maybe not wanting to say anything else. It is such a beautiful day. A light breeze makes the grass and trees sway from side to side. The sky is cloudless, bright blue with the golden sun beaming down. A perfect day. The perfect celebration. “I Love you.” Although she didn’t say it back, Dominic knew. He felt it. She loved him too. IV. The phone ringing makes Dominic groan. He ignores the sound. The calls have come at an annoyingly fast pace, at least for Dominic, since he became the GCW Television Champion days ago. The calls have been of all varieties too, from extremely flattering to wildly inappropriate. He has received calls from agents and companies that wish to have him endorse their product, those being of the flattering persuasion. Dominic has also received calls from numerous women that apparently have very low moral standards and are willing to allow him to do things to him that he hasn’t even seen in porn … inappropriate of course. Wall has also taken calls that he has been more than happy to take, such as his buddies Josh, Greg and Sam from his Army days. The annoying calls have far outweighed the wildly inappropriate or friendly calls however and Dominic shuts his phone off before placing it on its charger. Laying back into his hotel room bed, Dominic closes his eyes and tells himself that it is time for him to recharge his battery. The last week has been a whirlwind of emotion. He was angry when David Spencer once again got in the way of things, making a mess for him. He had enough pressure being Knight’s understudy. His anger spilled over into his meeting with Garbage Bag Johnny. Anger turned into excitement when he was given his opportunity. Excitement turned into nervousness. Nervousness turned into determination. Determination turned into euphoria. Euphoria into sadness. He cannot believe what he has accomplished. Sure, to some it would ‘only’ be a TV title win but It was so much more than that to Dominic. Besides the fact that he had bested a man that many other veterans of the sport couldn’t, Dominic took it as validation. Now he knows that he is truly on the right path in life and that this is what he is meant to be doing. Happiness can be his again and this sport can be his sanctuary. It was only one step but it was a large one. Yet he still finds sadness leaking into his heart. He can’t help it. Sure the calls and congratulatory e-mails, face book posts and twitter messages have abundant but there is one that he can never receive. One voice that he can never hear again. The one person who always supported and loved him, no matter how bad things got with her own life. He feels his face become warmer and the tears that have quickly formed in his eyes trickle down. He takes in deep breaths to calm himself. He wants so bad for her to see him and enjoy his success. Without her guidance he typically feels lost and afraid. Dominic tries to force the feelings out. Janelle wouldn’t want them eating away at him. She would demand that he be happy, that he enjoy this new life that he has built for himself. Dominic tries. He tries everyday to not let the sadness eat him alive. There are these quick moments when he thinks he has it beat too. Although he feels he is on the right path … the end can feel so far away sometimes. It takes hours but finally his brain slows down as much as it can and Dominic drifts to sleep. Its been a long day. Its been a long week.
