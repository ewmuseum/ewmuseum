Tightrope (Introduction: The Life Of A Hero)

16 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 16, 2007 ---- Introduction: The Life Of A Hero ---- All over the world people turn on their televisions at the chance to catch something special. A glimpse of things legends are made of. When you're on that television, you strive to make history. The pressure builds on you to give more and more. You try to go through life and the weights keep getting thrown on top of you. The more you give the more people expect in return. You want to be something special, you want to be a part of history... but in the end I'm just a human being. I can only carry so many hopes and expectations before I buckle and fall to the ground. The scary thing is... I don't know how much I can carry. This is the life of a role model. It's a life I've chosen to live, a life I'm proud of, a life that brings a smile to my face when I wake up in the morning. I remember being one of the masses. I remember turning on the television to try and be a part of history. To go around and tell everyone where I was when I saw something so spectacular happen. I had a special interest in wrestlers, I had my favorites, the ones I had admiration for reguardless of the circumstances. I remember when they could do no wrong. I remember my heart beating faster as I watched then jump over that next hurdle, scale that next mountain in their path to the things legends are made of. I remember being with them everystep of the way, only being seperated by the glow of a television screen. I also remember being there when they fell. I was there when they buckled under pressure, became a shell of what they once were. Watching them not fulfill their potential, while others lived out their dreams. Even those who accomplished everything they ever wanted slowly faded out. Embarassingly hanging onto the past trying desperately to be the champions they were decades before. Some of them died well before their prime. I know I may seem like I'm rambling but the point of this is that you never know when your number is called. On the outside it may seem like an easy decision to take the path of a hero. Fight for what's right, never sway for your beliefs and ideals. The reality of the situation is that I may not wake up in the morning. I may never have a second opportunity. I may never have my chance to fade out. In this business you can't just be gifted, you have to be very lucky. While I have full confidence in my abilities to one day achieve my destiny, I do not know what the future holds. When someone comes up to you and offers you all your hopes, dreams, and expectations of yourself since you were a little girl... it's something that's tough to turn down. Reguardless of the stigma attached to it. What if I did die tomorrow? Would anyone remember me in five years? What about ten? Or twenty? In this business it's something you have to think about everyday when you step into the arena. I suppose it's what I brought upon myself when I took the path I did a year ago by leaving Dies Irae behind. It's a dilemma I'm faced with everyday. Sometimes when you have a past such as mine, it's hard to convince people that you're capable of making the right decision. That you're trustworthy. How do you convince people that you're doing the right thing and they can trust you, when every step along the way you've proven to do just the opposite? Welcome to the life of The Fearless Phenom. ---- TO BE CONTINUED...
