Just a Smile: Part 8

12 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 12th, 2007 The plan did not go according to how I had hoped, but somehow things still worked themselves out. It was almost enough to make me laugh. I could still see Scott showing up to the bar like I had asked. Justin had been waiting for him near the corner of the building, watching his every move and waiting for the perfect moment to strike. As soon as Scott approached, Justin attempted to jump him like I had instructed. Sadly wrestling wasn’t as fake as I would have had Justin believe and Scott was easily able to throw him into the concrete wall. When his body hit the wall, I didn’t need to see the expression on Justin’s face to know how bad it hurt; I could hear it from even where I stood. It looked like Scott was even having fun as he slapped around Talos, mouthing “what the hell do you think you’re doing” Don’t you know who the fuck I am?” It must haven been too long since his last fight. Justin tried to hold his own against the man called Chris Storm, but Scott Drake countered each punch with one of his own nearly effortlessly. It made sense; after all, Scott was use to wrestling against the likes of LeStatt Knight, Vivica Valentine, and many other skilled athletes. The last thing Justin wrestled was the cap on his beer bottle. By the end of the ordeal, Justin looked like he needed an extensive stay at the nearest hospital. Not one to miss an opportunity like this, I called the police and moments later they arrived to find Justin beaten and bleeding and Scott with the blood on his hands. They didn’t need a detective to figure out what had happened. Though it certainly didn’t hurt any that when they arrived Scott was still kicking his grounded body and taunting him. “That’s all you got” Stop being a little bitch? he would cry out as he gave another hard kick to Justin’s stomach as he tried to curl into the fetal position. The cuffs were slapped on and Scott was hauled off into the cruiser. As a parting gift, I stepped out to wave good-bye to him. The look Scott gave me when the pieces finally clicked together was priceless. I wished I had brought along my sketch pad to make a record of it, but then, I believe I’ll always remember it. Along with the cops, the EMTs showed up to help Justin. As they loaded him up into the ambulance, one of them asked if I wanted to come along with Justin to the hospital. I just nodded my head, “I don’t even know the guy.” It might have been mean, it might have been cruel, but one had to look at the bigger picture. At this moment Tessa was alone. She needed someone to look after her while her fianc’e was behind bars. It only took about half an hour to get to their condo, but I decided to wait a few extra minutes. I didn’t want it to seem like I had rushed there as soon as Scott had left. I knew I had time. It’d be awhile before the booking process to be completed, and Scott would finally be allowed to make his call. Knocking on the door, Tessa seemed surprised to see me, which would make sense since I was supposed to be meeting Scott for drinks at the bar. She opened the door slightly to get a look at me, being very cautious. “Uh, Tessa, I was wondering where Scott was” I asked, looking her dead in the face as I lied, “he was supposed to meet me at the bar but never showed up.” Tessa opened the door further, a worried look spreading over her face. Given the opening, I pressed my hand against the door and pushed it open to step into their condo. There had been a bit of resistance on the door from Tessa, but she didn’t put enough pressure on it to keep me out. Nonchalantly, I continued talking as if nothing was going on. “At first I figured he was just standing me up, but after an hour I started to get worried.” Tessa seemed too shell-shocked to respond to me as I entered the living room. “I tried to call his cell phone, but never got an answer. I was hoping to find him here.” The more and more I talked, the more and more panicked Tessa became. I could almost sense it, though her facial expressions just as easily gave it away. Seemingly forgetting about my presence, she was more worried about the safety and wellbeing of Scott, than me being in her condo without him there. The only one who seemed to take notice to my presence was Tessa's niece, who Tessa had taken in after Christa's father perished in a car accident. Fortunately, as per usual for a child her age, her cell phone had been glued to her ear. "Oh yeah, that's so cool!" It had been very awkward to hear her voice echo all over the house, given that she had been quietest of the bunch. But Tessa was in no mood for words, and she likely wouldn’t be for the rest of the night. "Oh, you are?" I could only imagine who was on the other line. When Scott could be around, he'd often harass Christa about having a boyfriend. As far as I was concerned, she was far too young for this. Kids nowadays grew up way too quick. I'd only had three girlfriends in my twenty-five years on this earth, and the first didn’t come along until I was nineteen. If things went my way, Tessa would be my fourth, and final. "Hold on, let me ask my mom." Finally, Tessa would have to break her silence. "Tessa?" Christa called out, as she poked her head into the living room. Tessa lifted her head, and immediately her frown was replaced with a smile that had obviously been forced. Christa, who could easily have passed for Tessa's own biological child, differed greatly from Tessa when it came to gullibility. She couldn't be fooled by even Tessa's hardest attempts to hide her sorrows. If there was anyone aside from Scott that would figure me out, it would have been her. Naturally, I had to do what I could to try and blend in and look like I belonged there at the moment. I stashed my hand into the side pocket of my carpenter jeans, pretending to search for something vital to my research. It reality, my hands ran across the silky fabrics of the panties I hoped to see Tessa dance around in one day. Then, just as Scott had done countless times, I'd slide up behind her, wrapping my arms around her slender waist, and carry her away to bed, where she'd learn a new meaning to the word pleasure. My fantasy would have to be put on hold though, as Christa flipped her phone shut after notifying her friend that she'd call her back. "Tessa," She called once more, plopping down beside her adoptive mother, whose head lay nearly motionless on the arm of the leather couch "are you okay?" "Yeah" She answered in one short breath. Sadly Tessa wasn’t the greatest of liars, which had only added to Christa's concern, "You're not acting right." This time Tessa lifted her head off the arm, and let it fall against the back of the couch as she answered, "I just need some rest." Rather than continue to pry at Tessa's emotions, Christa attempted to find another way around to the truth, "Where's Scott?" Having studied Tessa as much as I had Scott, I knew she hated to lie to her children, but she also hated to see them worry, which is why each breath she took became shorter and shorter, as she looked straight up into her daughter’s eyes and tried her hardest to keep a calm face, "He's out with Markus doing an autograph session for World Wide since it is returning state side." "Why aren't you with them?" Clearly Christa wasn't buying it. Planting a kiss on her daughter’s forehead, Tessa desperately tried to keep her calm, "I wanted to make sure everything was packed before we headed off tomorrow. Besides, Scott has to team with Vivica, so I knew that would just mean endless hours in the gym." Christa allowed a laugh to escape her lips, knowing that it was most likely turn. The joke around the family had always been that Scott’s first home was the gym. While the two were distracted with their talk, I slipped into the kitchen to have a look around. In the background I could still faintly hear their distant chatter. Finally looking around the condo, I could see that nearly everything had been packed away now. The move was soon, so it appeared my timing could have been any better with this. The boxes were labeled different things; mostly it looked like Tessa did all the labels on the box, as I couldn’t see Scott even giving a damn. Peering my head back toward the living room, Christa and Tessa were still talking. At the moment it had looked like Christa was getting frustrated with the conversation. Even if she knew Tessa were lying, there was nothing Christa could do for her. So rather then pursue a hopeless case, she finally went back to her original mission, "Can I spend the night at Leah's?" This seemed to bring a sigh of relief to Tessa, and for the first time since I'd gotten there she seemed to have gained a bit of life back, "Of course!" Christa was wildly surprised at how quickly this decision came about, "Huh?" "Yes Christa, I'll bring you." She made sure to annunciate every last word. "Well you don’t need to," Christa hesitated, "her mom is coming to get me." Then, that life in Tessa was lost again, "Alright then, go get packed." She nodded, and went to turn, but caught herself midway. She looked back at her mother, and placed a kiss on her cheek, whispering into her ear, "He's going home soon, right?" Tessa nodded very slightly, in hopes not to offend me I guess. She must have thought that I didn’t hear or notice. "You promise?" Again she nodded, before kissing her daughters forehead, "Go get ready; you don’t want to keep her mom waiting." Christa followed suit with her mother’s words, but not before shooting me a threatening look. Scott had obviously had plenty to say about me to his children, and not a single bit of it was kind. That didn’t matter though. Scott would be out of the picture and unable to brainwash them against me. Finally, Tessa and I were alone again. It seemed to perfect time to make my move. That was, until Tessa reached over to the coffee table and grabbed her phone. From the corner of my eye I noticed the name she stopped on. She tried to call Scott, I’m assuming to see if I had lying or not. After a few moments of letting the phone ring, Tessa hung up the phone and dropped onto the couch in great despair. I was actually part surprised that the phone hadn’t been turned off at the police station, but I knew there would be no way they would answer it even if she did call. Like a knight in shining armor, I picked up on her distress and moved in. “Come here, you look like you need a hug” I said, holding my arms out for her. She didn’t move though, she just stared down at her phone as various thoughts ran through her head. So I took my opportunity and gave her a hug, one she didn’t respond to. But it was enough just to touch her like that. As soon as I released her from the hug, she dropped down on the sofa and just blankly looked forward at the television screen. Dropping myself beside her, I laid my arm down across her shoulder to try and rub her back. Instantly Tessa moved further down on the couch, which agitated me a bit. But I could understand. She was worried about Scott after all. This must have been so hard on her, and in a way I felt bad about that. I placed her in this difficult position knowingly, but I did so in hopes that she would grow and begin to see all the things I could offer her with Scott out of the picture. He would never allow me to show her just how good I could be for her, but with him out of the way, I was free to try and woo Tessa. The entire time she never put the phone away. I couldn’t tell if she held on to it in hopes that maybe Scott might call or if she thought she might need it in case I tried anything. Either way, I didn’t have any plans for anything like that at the moment. Christa yelled out to Tessa that she was leaving, cutting through the living room and giving her a kiss before she left with a bag thrown over her shoulder. Watching her go, I noticed that she never once looked to me, as if I didn’t exist. Not long after Christa left, Tessa sat in an uncomfortable silence. She kept checking over her shoulder, possibly hoping that someone else would come out. Both the boys were most likely playing together in their room (most likely wrestling, which Tessa wouldn’t be happy with). With the realization that no one would be coming, Tessa slumped down on the sofa, nearly sinking into the cushions. She looked to me as I sat in a chair quietly. “I’m really tired; I think I’m going to head off to bed.” I nodded to her, getting up from my chair. “Is there anything you need before you go to bed?” I asked, not to extend my stay, but to let her know I cared. “No,” shaking her head, “I’m fine” said Tessa as she herself lifted from the couch to head to the bedroom. Her back to me, I walked to the door and opened it, waiting until she left my view and I knew she had entered the bedroom. Closing it, I tried to step as quietly as I could. Peeking my head around each corner, I gave it a second until my eyes could adjust after Tessa had turned off the lights before she went to sleep. I knew my way around the living room fairly well now, avoiding tripping on anything or making any loud noises. Her door had been slightly jarred open, leaving a small beam of light shining into the living room. I looked in, hoping to catch her peacefully slumbering away, but was distraught to see her clinching a picture of Scott on her night stand. Holding it close to her, Tessa turned off the nightstand lamp and rolled over in the bed. I couldn’t see her anymore but I could hear her. Soft whimpers emitted from her lips, carrying into the air, and entered my ears. Each tinged my heart strings, striking me with pain. I wanted to creep into the room and wrap my loving arms around her. But not yet, I couldn’t. But soon, soon I could take away her pain. She would have to suffer for a bit longer. With her last whimper before she fell asleep, I stepped back from the doorway and headed back to my motel. Though I wanted to comfort her and slip into the bed, I had to resist. The time wasn’t right. But soon’it would.
