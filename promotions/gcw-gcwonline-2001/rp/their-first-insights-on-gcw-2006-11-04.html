Their first insights on GCW

4 November 2006

Date: 11.04.06 The rumor mills had been going crazy for weeks. Everyone in professional wrestling had heard of the thought of a current AWC team breaking their contract to join the ranks of GCW. This was almost unheard of in the sport of wrestling, it was such a slap in the face to the ones who employ you. Hell Rich Rollins did something similar to GCW just a few weeks ago with PRIME, but the only difference was he wasn’t under any agreement with GCW just on a single show appearance. Last week when Pleasure and Pain took the stage against Peter Vetra and Rufio a couple of lines were crossed. They have ruined their chances of ever getting in a federation again if they decide to leave GCW, but if you ask that doesn’t matter. All they have to say is they have found their home. The stories of Tiara Belle Russell and Wayne Russell have been told to all those fans out there who follow AWC. Now they are bringing their story to GCW showing it to a new group and different genre. They have broken the rules of the sport by switching rival factions so fast, but now they are looking to prove why they did such a thing. But the only thing that will explain why they did what they did is time. Time they look to put into at GCW?. November 3, 2006 “That’s it god damn it!” Wayne is storming around his home in Brockton. His wife was not home. She was out getting groceries or something. He didn’t know where she was and he didn’t really care as long as she was doing the things she needed to do as a wife. Wayne threw something against a wall and it made a rather large noise. He however was lucky enough for it not to break anything or his wife would have his balls. His quick temper is something that always hung over his head. It usually got worse when he was drinking but today was different, he was sober. He runs over picks it up and at the same time is dialing the Nextel phone trying to find out where his wife was at. “Yes dear?” The soft subtle voice of his wife was on the other end and he couldn’t help but no longer be angry when hearing such a tone. “We have to get going?” “What do you mean?” Tiara was confused at the actions of her husband, it was not often that he would call her in a panic but whenever he did it was usually something serious. “I just checked our email. We have a match tomorrow night in Houston, Texas versus another tag team.” Wayne was known for under exaggerating the situations he was put in but this time he wasn’t. “Ok, well book us the first flight out of Logan, I will get the rest of the things I need for us and I will be home. Start packing!” Tiara knew that this was one time in her life Wayne was being urgent about something that he needed to be urgent with. After their mediocre showing during their tenure at AWC they were not about to start their second showing in GCW with the same standards. They may have been informed a little to late for their likings but that isn’t what mattered. The only thing that mattered was that they get to the ring and perform to the best of their abilities. Wayne slammed the phone down on the counter as he started looking at Orbitz.com to see when the next flight was out. Once he confirmed their flight which would be leaving a in a mere two hours he started getting all of the other reservations that were needed done. You know the rental car, the hotel room, and any other accommodations. I mean what this isn’t PRIME they don’t take care of everything for you. Wayne stormed out of the room knowing what would have happened if they missed their flight, he had packing to do. Then he had a match to win. November 4, 2006 The lights of Houston illuminated the surrounding buildings. Houston was a city that was known for being down south, enjoyed their partying and also enjoyed their recent fame due to rap music. The area of Houston that the couple now stayed in was a little fancy compared to where they used to staying but Wayne decided on nothing but the best. “This bed is so comfortable.” Tiara who was checking the place out was seemingly jumping on the bed like a four year old. She looked over at her husband who was looking at the mini fridge for some beer. “This might be the best chance we have to start that family we were talking about.” Wayne cracks open his first beer in almost 24 hours, he thought he was having withdrawals in all actuality he was feeling something most humans live with called sobriety. He casts a glare over at his wife of only a few months. “Don’t you mean the talks you have been having” with yourself?” Wayne takes a swig of his beer, being just about six foot six a swig to him is half a beer. ? I told you I was more worried about our careers first. We are still young.” Tiara had heard him talk about this before; he wanted to be established before he brought a baby into this world. He wanted to be guaranteed a good paying job. He was able to use that excuse in AWC as they weren’t paying shit but Tiara knew the agreement with GCW, she knew they were making more money. “But now we have a contract, not a week by week thing.” “That is all well and fine but if you read that contract it insists that both of us wrestle. What happens when you get pregnant and the doctor doesn’t allow you to wrestle” No longer are we Pleasure and Pain. We are not Pain and Pregnant. Do you think GCW hired us just on our wrestling ability? No they want your good looks, and my brawn.” Wayne finished the beer. “But baby?” Tiara did that cute thing most women who have good looks do. You know she batted her eyes, looked all sad, and used that whiny voice that always got males to agree with whatever they said. “Baby” I never said we couldn’t do it and what not, I just said we shouldn’t be trying.” Wayne throws the empty beer can in the trash as he starts to walk over to his wife. He picks her up which is an easy feat for the man as she weighs a hundred pounds less then him if not more. “Plus if it just happens they can’t do anything about it really, I mean what is stop Dawn McFarlane, Shadow, Tessa Windsor from getting pregnant” Nothing but what I am saying is lets not try. Let’s just have some fun.” Tiara liked the sounds of that; she had the chance of maybe getting pregnant and having a good time while she was at it. The two started to kiss and touch. Tiara was quick to stop once they were starting to get serious. She stops him and looks up. “Wait” let me go change.” She jumps out of the bed eagerly. What a buzz kill Wayne thought, but if he knew his wife she would be worth the wait. That was one thing about their relationship that never got dull? the sex. Wayne decided to grab another beer while she did what she had to do; you know women always make men wait. Who knows maybe they think it helps the anticipation but it really doesn’t it just pisses them off. The wait of almost five minutes was well worth it when he saw his gorgeous wife walk out of the hotel’s bathroom. The thoughts of work were no longer in his head; the thoughts of her wanting a family were out the door. All that mattered was this five foot four blonde bombshell whose curves accented her body perfectly. Wayne’s jaw hit the ground, he saw the woman he married in a state he had not seen since their honey moon, and before that their first time together. There are always those couple of special times, other wise you have the ones once your married where you both roll over and see if one another wants to do it. This was going to be one of those amazing nights, Wayne had a feeling. Both were tired from the flight but had gotten a jolt of energy. Wayne felt like he drank four or five Red Bull and Vodkas but in all actuality he had just seen his wife. Her sleek and slender figure was making her way to the bed in a seductive fashion. You see it in the movies and usually laugh at he corniness but the fact of the matter is she looked sexy. She placed both arms on his chest and pushed him back started kissing his neck and slowly worked her way down. The love she had for him was about to be expressed in more ways then one. She wanted him, he wanted her. It was a perfect match which would lead to a long night for the two of them? They might regret it tomorrow during their match, but for now it was pure ecstasy.
