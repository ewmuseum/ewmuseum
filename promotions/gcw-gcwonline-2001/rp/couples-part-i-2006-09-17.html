Date: 9/17/06 What can we say? Dies Irae and the KnightMare Brigade have a plan. They have a plan of inciting a revolution. Revolution leads to change. Change is dynamic. Change is good. Change is not anarchy, but evolution. Some have tried to fight that change. They still do. Gone are the days of Dorian Ryan and his US title reign of mediocrity. Gone are the days of Jayce Bradley holding the TV and Cruiserweight Titles. Gone are the days of people like Rollins, the self hype machine and Eva Van Lorne. Gone are the days where GCW was rock bottom. ?I for one welcome our dominating overlords!? We've not taken GCW by a strangle hold. We raised the bar. We brought the standard up! There is nothing to strangle for it is ours. Yes, some of the titles have slipped from out fingers. Look at last week's loss of the Television Title... did Mr. White have to pin the champion for it? No he pinned Michael Balthazar. But in the same week that we loose something that is now below everyone in the KB and DI...we do the unthinkable. The never been done before. We almost swept not GCW, but PTC. Shadow, the new PTC Global Champion. She destroyed a man whom they said was nigh unstoppable in the Uberjudge. Vivica Valentine almost won the PTC Extreme Championship... and in this case Almost did count like it did in horse shoes, because she's being given another chance to do the same. But the crowning achievement for the Brother's Brand, the team known as Unite was defeating the Killer Pirates for what had been billed as a four way, but turned into just a tag team match up. It as the Totem Pole Suplex and Neckbreaker combination and Unite had brought the PTC Tag Team Titles home. And some could argue the validity of such, but c'mon people. Who were the last team to hold those titles over a year ago? The team that was dropped for the three count in the ring. This is more then legitamate. Unite are now not only the GCW, but the PTC Tag Team Champions. And between the two allied stables, we've proven that we can not only take GCW to a higher level, but now it's time to take PTC to a higher level. Our plans will see fruition, we're at the point that we could guarantee that. Josh was feeling actually refreshed after waking up this morning. It had been a good few days past after returning from the PTC event and now they had to all get ready for a fight that would bring the entire army, both companies into bear against everyone else in the federation. Dangerous Games. It was no secret, the KB/DI alliance was after one winner and one winner only. They had gone down and ranked everyone within their collective and if #1 was eliminated, then they needed to concentrate on protecting and supporting #2 and so on down the line. Someone from KB/DI would have to win this and go on to show that they were the most dominate force. It didn't matter who left the ring in the match before hand, either Dawn or Knight, someone from the Alliance had to step up and take the Rumble Win. And the others, they say that DI & KB can't handle facing each other in such a match? Did Team Baked leave a stash in one of the arenas or something? The KB has a strong history of it. Months ago Blaise and Ukyo joined Josh and Gray and two other teams to determine the #1 contenders for the GCW tag team titles. Both KB teams won on a double pin... Did they break up and yell and scream at each other afterwards? No... it was a fight of honor and both teams won. Then Unite got their shot first and took the belts where they belonged. And Future Shock has now sat at the side at the request of GCW... but if any of these teams can't take it, then Blaise and Ukyo will claim their spot. What about Dies Irae? Come on people! Are we that blind? Rampage Tourney earlier this year. Who were the two finalist? Dies Irae members? Yup. Friends? Yup. Lovers? Uhm, yup. Shadow and Dawn did it, they fought and the best person on that night won and was awarded the GCW World Title for it. You're all in denial. Dies Irae didn't break apart because of that. They didn't go off and have a cat fight with Shadow pulling Dawn's hair out and yelling and screaming that she should have won. What did they do? Heh, that's for your imagination. But they went home together if you sick-o pervs want to think about about. But that's really none of your business, it's there's. The key thing here is, both the KB and DI are more then just a collection of wrestlers who have teamed up to bring GCW to it's knees. In fact, GCW on it's knees is not their goal, unlike what the Ascension would like to tell you. The KB and DI are GCW. They are the new face of GCW. A GCW that can hold it's face up proud in the spotlight, and shine brighter then any of the other federations out there. You fail to see this, because you are blinded by your own lower standards. The standards have been too low for too long. Step up or step out is all we have to say. ~Brand and Northsky Residence~ ~Las Vegas, Nevada~ Josh left Dani to her journal this morning. She'd actually woken up before him, but had started writing stuff down and messing with Angel shortly after getting out o the shower. He wasn't going to bother until he actually shocked his brother completely. He was going to head down to the home's gym area without being told. Although as he approached the door that opened to the facilities, he smiled. Someone else was already using them. Apparently their house guest, Ms. Alexis Day had decided to get some time in on one of the bags while Gray was out taking care of an interview for some magazine that Josh had begged out of. He opened the door and slid inside, listening to the random sounds coming from one of the hanging bags. She was on the kick bag, snapping short side kicks, a few round houses and short elbows and jabs to it. She'd found the hand and foot pads that were kept there. While an engaged man, Josh found that he had to check out the fit of her long workout pants and halter top in matching black with silver stripes. Although all he could think about with her was the fact of things he knew from a few weeks back. She had a thing for his brother, and he knew that he was interested too. It was faint, but it was something that was blossoming and as long as his brother didn't fuck it up, it could happen. And Josh's check out made him approve of that possibility happening. ?Hey, didn't take you for a workout freak.? She stopped in mid kick and then dropped her foot back down. Stopping like that instantly appeared to make her arms increase in weight as they dropped helplessly to her side. ?It's been awhile. But it helps. Not to mention, Blaise has kinda given me the go ahead to become an armed ward of the guardians. Meaning I get to kick some ass too. I think my shooting impressed him.? Josh couldn't help but smile at her. The last few weeks since her secret had come out, attacked by a succubus and put into a dream where her dream lover and her were... doing as lovers do, Josh's empathy had seen it. That dream had her and his brother in it. ?Ah, figured you'd be working out so you could jump my brother's bones one night in his sleep.? She would have thrown something at him if she had the energy. Instead, she'd just dropped her jaw open as his crudeness. ?I would never...? He smiled wide and that caused her to turn a crimson color in her cheeks. He moved to change the subject before she decided to hit him with something, as some women were prone to do. ?So, what style is that. You're stance shows it's not just you kicking the bag and tossing random things at it.? Alexis, still red in the face started undoing the Velcro that held the hand guards on. ?Don't really know. The training we went through was classified as basically generic. Kinda like what the police forces go through. Enough to protect us and try to get the upper hand to then bring a weapon to bear against something that was usually physically superior to us. I've seen you fight, I take it you're a martial artist too?? Josh reached up onto the walls and grabbed two sticks, both white and almost an inch in diameter. She twirled them around. ?Yes, this Injun-Frenchman knows some of the Filipino Triad.? She smirked. ?That sounds like a maria based video game.? He shook his head. ?No: Arnis, Kali, and...? He hit the bag a few times with the sticks in stylized strikes. ?Escrima. The area we lived in had a large Filipino population, some of my good friends took lessons in it so I got into it.? He slapped the sticks on the bag a few more times. ?So, what's on your mind other then my brother?? She sighed. It was hard to hide things from Josh over the last few weeks It was hard hiding things from an empath. She threw the hand guards down onto the table next to her and pulled up a bottle of spring water. ?I don't know. I know so little about him. But all the same, I think that's part of why I'm attracted. But he seems to have a few hangups. His ex-wife and Dawn being two of them.? Josh snickered. ?What?? ?Call me a cynic, but I really don't see too much on Dawn anymore. He even changed his password to his computer. It used to be her middle name. Now it's something else all together. The unfortunate thing is that he's been burned too many times since he and his wife... Well, Dawn has been combative on any sort of relationship even being seeded. It's just been rough on that one.? Alexis looked down at the ground and started to mess with the draw string to her pants as she leaned against the table. ?Tell me something Josh...? He turned his head a bit and leaned against the kick bag, until it started to move and then he stood up straight, trying to play it off. ?What would that be?? ?I'm going to probably regret this. But tell me what happened with him and his wife. I want to, no I need to know. My mind is just confused right now.? Josh snickered again. ?C'mon, you should know guys and all their shit. He's going to confuse you no matter what if you ask other girls...? He looked at her, and her stare was blank. He sensed something in her that he'd never really sensed before... innocence. ?Oh shit.? She looked a bit confused herself now. ?What?? ?You don't know guys... at... all.? Again she blushed, but this time it wasn't as crimson as before. It was true. ?Other then sneaking a kiss behind the nun's backs, no.? Josh got curious. ?So how far have you been?? ?With a guy?? ?Uhm, yeah.? She couldn't believe he'd asked that. But she had nothing to hide, not anymore. ?Once let a guy put his hand up my shirt in a hall closet in school. But we got caught when we were kissing. He was moved out of that school after that. Other then that, I've seen Virgil in the nude a few times and...? ?Whoa, Whoa... Virgil?? Alexis sighed heavily. ?Back at the estate, Lia and Virgil are not always ones to keep their clothing on. They're hedonist at heart. Once Virgil came to get me to go out with them and he was naked as a jay bird.? ?Did you look?? ?Hell yes I did. And believe you me, he could give your brother a run for his money...? Josh cocked and eyebrow up. ?Shit...? He smiled wide. ?My Brother? You've seen Gray.? ?I've snuck a few peeks when he's been in the shower.? She was looking down at the ground only now. She wouldn't look up at Josh. ?Well, you're the little sneaky one. I'm going to assume you like what you see. My mom says we both take after our father, whatever that would mean. So, I take it you would like my help in landing my brother?? She smiled. ?I don't know if that would be possible. You said he kissed me back when I was under the influence of that succubus, but c'mon now. I'm just little Alexis. I'm NOT Dawn McFarlane by a long shot. Hell my both of my boobs make one of hers. I think I don't even come up to her boobs as it is.? ?Ah, vous ?tes une belle femme, bien que.? She blinked and looked up at him. ?You speak, French?? He chuckled. ?I try not to, but you've met my mother. She was born and raised in Toulon, France. I grew up speaking three languages. Other then that, as a someday to be married man I was giving my approval to your physical attractiveness.? She couldn't keep the blood from her cheeks with him as he spoke of such things. However, things were getting personal. She couldn't hide her desires from Josh. He was getting stronger. She'd only had a little to do with those who had psionic abilities before. Empathy was the most common form, but Josh's was becoming more. But there were questions she had in her head. Things she needed to know. It was getting harder for her to concentrate around him. Josh had guessed it, she was pure. She'd never had a real relationship of the type that was begging for her to get into right now. She'd seen how happy Dawn and Lia were. How happy Lia and Virgil were, Josh and Dani, Blaise and Ukyo. Having been sequestered by the Church and the group known as the Den for so long had left her feeling lacking when it came to everything else she was now exposed to in the world. Her faith had faltered in one way, but her eyes were open in others. ?Josh, can I ask you a personal question?? He nodded, she caught it barely in her peripheral vision. ?What happened between he and his ex-wife?? Josh sighed.... he'd been waiting for the day someone would ask that. ?They were high school sweethearts. He'd gotten her pregnant their senior year and they got married the day after graduation. They had a son and he was still a boy when Gray started to go through the change.? She turned to look at him, her stare actually made him a bit nervous. ?He found himself changing at night. We lived on a small ranch at the time. A few of our cattle and our neighbors were slaughtered by something big and with big teeth. I'm the one who found out later it had been him. But before that, he started acting like an asshole to her. He would go out, get drunk and then come home, do nothing. Wouldn't touch her. Basically acted like he didn't want her around him anymore. I know now that he was afraid to be around her, he feared for her safety near him. And the safety of his son. In the end, she went to another man, and left him. He didn't even try for custody or visitation.? She shook her head. ?Why not? It makes him look like the biggest asshole around.? ?That was the idea.? ?You said you found out he was the one doing it...? ?Yeah, I heard one of the cows one night. I grabbed my 30-06 and headed out to see what the hell was going on. I came down, took aim and shot it. The bullet was silver tipped, a hollow point. It did it's damage, but not enough due to the velocity of the round. He was able to jump me.... but his body shut down to fix things and shifted back. He came to suddenly in full human form, over me.? Josh pulled his shirt up to show the tattoo over his stomach of a large tribal skull that looked like spider legs were coming out of it. The tattoo was done in a white and red motif that was common to the Northwest Indian totem drawings. ?He'd opened me up pretty good too, wasn't too deep, it healed. But I got this when I turned 17 to cover it up. I lied about my age.? She could only look at it. ?What happened then?? Josh dropped his shirt down. ?I told mom. She got him in touch with the Order. They brought him in to learn to deal with it. I don't think he's ever forgiven himself for what he did to her though. She's since moved on and remarried the guy she had gone to for her support during his asshole days. Since then he's gone back to being Grayson, the same one that landed her in the first place. But I think he's afraid too.? ?Why would he be afraid? He's a rather nice looking man. And I can attest to that better then you can.? Josh chuckled a bit. ?I've seen women try to grope him at autograph signings. He's got a lot of fans. He barely surfs the Internet and does all his work on his computer. But I've been to message boards where there are girls wondering if he's available and that. He just doesn't look.? ?So, what do you think it'll take for a girl to land your brother?? Josh smiled. ?Half the time, I think it'll take a girl walking into his bed room, locking lips with him, tossing his big ass down onto the bed, climbing on and telling him that he's not leaving until their both satisfied.? ?WHAT?? She couldn't believe she'd heard that from him. ?The summary on that.. He won't make the first fucking move. Ever. Look at his thing with Dawn. He's never once asked her out. He's never once asked for a kiss or anything. If he did, he might get somewhere. But then again, he's heard that Dawn doesn't like guys who take the more direct approach. They went out to dinner and he was the perfect gentleman. And it got him no where. Hell, I don't even think she wanted to really be too close of friends after that. She didn't take to my joke with the roses too well I tried to pin on him. He's just complicated. I don't think he openly sees his need for another person as being important. But he's just going to grow old and be cantankerous if he doesn't. And from the looks of it, he might live for quite some time. He's not immortal, but there area few of his kind that have come forward that aren't spring chickens in their own rights.? She could only nod. ?And I'll tell you this, Alexis. You want him? Go for it. He could do much, much worse then having someone like you to take care of him. Hell, you've already tried to kill for him.? ?He has killed for me.? ?Yes, but at the time, that was his job. Although, I could be wrong. I've been known to be in that field before.? He started to walk out of the gym. He'd done enough for today with their talk. And now it wasn't time to be working out and have her there to ask more questions. She had to figure things out herself. He walked up the single flight of stairs that headed up to the kitchen area of the house. As soon as he stepped in, he was looking at Dani as she sat there with a cup of coffee and the Las Vegas Review Journal, her satin robe tied loosely about her. He wasn't used to her bothering with a robe, but a quick glance to the side showed him why she had decided to cover up. Blaise was there, his guitar case slung over his shoulder. ?Hey, what's up?? Blaise nodded as he'd just finished saying something to Dani. ?Not much, wanted to stop by before I headed out to the show. I'm hoping to maybe spot a potential bassist and another guitarist if I can, then we can bring them in to play and interview.? Josh headed right for the coffee pot and started to pour himself a large cup. ?Sounds good. What were you two talking about?? Dani looked up. ?Nothing.? She looked back down. Josh didn't like that. ?That doesn't sound too good.? She scowled at him. ?If you really want to know, Blaise and I were taking bets as to how long it would take for you to actually get me infront of a preacher or a justice of the peace and marry me. Took you long enough to propose as it was.? Josh looked like he'd been hit with a stun gun, and his mouth gaped open. ?For all I know, it'll take forever.? ?Well he is a procrastinator.? Blaise chimed in and showed that he was not on the man's side of this argument. ?Oh, that's precious coming from a man who proposed before I did, and has yet to marry Ukyo.? Blaise help up his bottle of sports drink to him. ?Ah, but I'm promised that the wedding there will take place in Tokyo in full Order honors for her family. That takes time and planning. We're at least looking at the dates that work with our schedules. We also were waiting on the final word that we had custody of Kait and Kaiyt. So now that's done, we've actually sat down and started figuring it out. I think you're going to procrastinate it out forever though. As it is, I don't need a new visa to get married in Japan. But Ukyo would need one to get married over here. So for us, it's logistics.? Josh just gave him a look. ?Well as you said, weddings take time and planning.? Blaise smiled as Josh continued to dig his own grave. ?But I would promise you this, my love... if I could marry you today, I would.? Blaise busted out laughing. ?What? What are you laughing about?? Blaise had to hold himself up by the table as he tried to stop himself to answer. ?Look man, I'm not dumb. What are you laughing about?? Blaise finally choked out a few small chuckles and answered. ?Dude, you're in Las Vegas, the fast wedding capital of the world... be at the court house at 1 pm, no blood test. Out by 1:30, at one of the chapels by 2 and married by 2:30... I think I'll be leaving now.? Dani had a grin that could only be coined to be the 'shit eating type'. Josh looked at her and could only give a half smile. ?So, that was a promise?? One one word could come to his mind after that... He couldn't say it out loud though. ... Shit!
