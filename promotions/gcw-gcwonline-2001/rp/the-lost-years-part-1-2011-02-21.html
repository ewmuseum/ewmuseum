The Lost Years Part 1

21 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/21/11 November 30th 2008 The headaches were so bad that I finally went to see a doctor. After they were unable to figure out whether or not I was suffering from migraines, I was sent to see a neurologist. That's where the two missing years began. "Mr. Ryan, I'm glad that you didn't get back into the ring before you came to see me." I knew that those words weren't going to be good for me. After all, they usually preceded bad news. "You have a rather serious concussion." Those were words that no professional athlete ever wants to hear. I had wrestled through a minor concussion before. But this was worse. The doctor wasn't giving me clearance to resume even training. Time where you really can't do anything makes for longer days. March 3rd 2009 "Dorian, what is this?" Andrea, my wife at the time, asked me this as she found a FedEx Envelope on our kitchen counter. It had been six weeks since I was told I couldn't wrestle. "It's my release from my contract. The office and I came to an agreement that this was the best thing for me. I can't get in the ring, so I can't earn any money. They can't leave a roster spot open for me because they have some new talent that they can bring in. This way, I can at least get my medical bills paid." Andrea returned to San Francisco a week later. I soon found myself living in my brother and sister-in-laws guest house near Detroit. 3 months after my career derailed, so had my life. At least I had Jay, Kelly, and their two kids* around. It was good for Jay and Kelly since they now had someone to look after their young ones while Jay was on the road with SCCW and Kelly conducted her business for Durant-Ryan Clothing. It was even better when they were both home, as I took up the wrestling convention circuit. Every weekend, traveling to different cities, meeting many fans, some of them thinking that there was nothing more exciting than seeing some of the old timers and younger guys coming up. GCW even provided me with a replica of the United States Title to take with me to them. Who would have known that after a few months, someone from my past would reenter my life. June 20th 2009 "So, DarkDiva.net is here at the third annual Champions and Legends Wrestling Convention in Buffalo, New York, and I have found an old friend. Dorian Ryan, what brings you here?” To say that Araceli was an old friend would be an overstatement. We met when she first came to GCW with the Cowboys From Hell. We started up a friendship that GCW officials eventually turned into New Mercy Enterprises. “Well, I’m here with one of the vendors. They seem to think that some of the fans here in Buffalo want to see me or hear some of my stories from the road.” The two of us knew that was bullshit. She knew that the real reason that I was here was the same reason that she was. As much as you want to, there is no way that you can truly escape the wrestling industry, and this was a way for me to still be involved. “So, give some of my viewers an update on what’s been going on in your world.” Even she didn’t know the real reason behind my release. I guess now was as good of a time for her to find out as any. “Well, a lot of the fans don’t know this, but I actually suffered a rather serious concussion against Rollin’ Large at Devil’s Night. At first, I thought that I was suffering from migraines, but after a while, I started to get worried and ended up seeing a couple of doctors, one of whom diagnosed the concussion and pulled me from future bookings.” Taking that last match against Tyson DeBough and the Nacho Man didn’t help as I had used a senton bomb. Then again, I didn’t know what was going on. “How long until you get back into the ring?” “Honestly, I have no clue. As of right now, I’m only cleared to return to light exercise. I can’t do anything that is too physically exerting right now. But eventually, I do plan on getting back into the ring. Whether it’s as soon as I’m cleared to return or some time after, that’s all going to depend on what I’m feeling at that time.” “Well, Dorian, I want to thank you for taking the time to talk to the fans out there on the internet. As always, I’m the Dark Diva, Araceli Negra, and this is DarkDiva.net.” Later That Night Araceli had a nicer room than I. She could afford it, as she was still involved in the business affairs of the Cowboys From Hell. They were on the road without her, as she was taking some time off from the road. I had heard the rumors, that her and Jake were no longer together, but I didn’t know the truth in that at the time. I finally got to her room. She told me just to dress comfortably, so I decided on jeans and an old NWC T-Shirt, pretty much what I had worn throughout the day anyway. I knocked on the door, and it didn’t take long for her to answer. She was dressed in a McGill University hoodie and sweats, with her dark brown hair pulled back in a pony tail. “Dorian, thanks for coming. You have no idea how tough it is to come to things like this where you really don’t know anyone. At cast you get to see some older wrestlers from the NWC at these events.” That was true. After all, at the last convention that I went to, the vendor that I was working with decided to have an NWC Feuds theme going on, and brought be in to sign with Teron Hain. “Well, not always.” OK, more often than not. This convention was a rarity in that I was the only NWC alumni there, especially since New York was such a hotbed for the National Wrestling Council. She brought me in and we sat on the couch that was in her room. She looked at me through a pair of rather nice looking glasses. She tired of wearing her contacts after the long day in the hotel’s ballroom that she switched over. I had only ever seen her wear glasses once before, but that was a while ago. “So, I heard about Andrea.” “Yeah, it hasn’t been fun. Been staying with my brother’s family lately. Couldn’t afford the apartment in Minnesota anymore.” “Well, it’s better than what I’ve gone through with Jake leaving me. I had to find a new place to live. Luckily, my parents were able to help me out with an apartment in Toronto.” “That’s always good. Whoever says you can never go home again was a lying sack of shit.” Araceli reached out and grabbed by hand. She then looked me right in the eyes. “Maybe it’s fate that brought us here tonight.” “You mean this room.” “No, I mean together, silly. After all, part of the reason that Jake left me was the fact that he thought that you and I had an affair.” I couldn’t keep the chuckle in. “The cowboy thought that I stole his girl? Sorry, but I find that funny.” I don’t know if that comment offended Araceli or not. She just looked out the window out into Buffalo. “You know, the first time I came here was with you and Wyatt to shoot those videos for GCW.” “I remember those. How many times did we have to shoot the same scene over and over again?” “Too many. I think that’s why Jake thought that you and I hooked up. After all, he knew that I would never have done anything with his best friend.” “You miss him?” “Sometimes. Then I remember how it ended, his accusations, the threats to kick your ass. After I saw him for who he really was, I don’t know why I was with him as long as I was.” It was different this time around. The last time we saw each other, Araceli didn’t tell me much about what was going on with her and Jake. Now, she was opening up in a way that it seemed like she needed to. I wasn’t going to stop her. She needed to let it out to someone, I just happened to be the person that she chose to do so with. After all, she didn’t have that many people in her life that she could trust to talk to, especially not Wyatt. “You know, Dorian, all the time that we travelled together, we never talked like this.” “I know. But then again, we never brought up our home lives with each other.” “You miss it, don’t you?” “Miss what?” “Being on the road. Wrestling for capacity crowds. I know you. You’re just like your brother, getting thrills from being in that ring.” “Sometimes. But sometimes I remember some of the harder things about the profession, like having a hard time having a personal life. I think that’s what made things hard on Andrea after it was over. She wasn’t used to having me around all the time, that she enjoyed the thrill of getting to the arena from time to time. When it was over, we were over.” Araceli reached over and grasped my hand. You could feel the pity that she felt for me. She didn’t know how much the breakup of my marriage devastated me, but she could tell that it did a number on me. She then got up and walked over to the mini bar. She poured a couple glasses of whatever was stocked by the hotel, easily adding a good chunk of change to her bill. She returned and handed one of the glasses to me. “If you don’t want to talk about it, you don’t have to talk about it.” Araceli sipped from her glass and then reached over and brushed a little bit of hair behind my ear. “If you just want to enjoy some company, you can do that too.” I know now that whatever she had planned for that night wasn’t what ended up happening. After spending the night drinking, I woke the next morning still in her room. On the pillow next to me was a note and a phone number. Little did I know what consequences the previous night were going to have. August 3rd 2009 Finally, I was able to start working out. Not just basic exercises, but a true workout. I was still getting the headaches, but my neurologist was optimistic that I was finally over the hump from the concussion that temporarily ended my career. I was making even more appearances now, refereeing at indy shows, signing autographs at conventions, even writing a column for a wrestling website. The more I worked, the better I felt. But the headaches were still there, which meant I couldn’t get back into the ring as a wrestler. It was tough, but I knew that eventually I was going to get there. The time was taking its toll on me, but I knew that eventually I would be able to get back into the ring. I was going to take my time. I was going to make sure that when I got back into the ring, that I wouldn’t put myself at risk of permanent injury. But it felt good to get a good sweat going. I was starting to get back into ring shape, for the day that I’m cleared to make my comeback. Slowly, I was shedding the pounds that I had packed on in my layoff. Every so often, Jay would come to make sure that I wasn’t overdoing it. But I was following the instructions of every doctor that I was seeing to the letter. What I wasn’t prepared for was the knock that came to the door. “Dorian?” I turned to see Kelly standing there. Her blonde hair was dyed with a pinkish hue, but pulled back in a ponytail so that her sharp features could be seen. “Yeah?” “You have a visitor.” I didn’t get many visitors, so I was surprised that someone did come. I was even more surprised as she walked into the guest house. “Araceli, how are you doing?” Araceli walks up to me, placing her hand lightly on my chest. “Dorian, we need to talk.” I wasn’t prepared for what she was about to tell me.
