Date: Yesterday into today six long grueling hours The heat in the autograph tent was stifling. His cheeks were starting to hurt from smiling so much, and his hand cramped around the sharpie pen with which he signed his name to the glossy prints of his face. The crowd had been warm and receptive to him, he’d taken the time to at least idly chat with most of them as they came forward in line. Mostly he just let his mind wander, trying to keep from thinking about the events of the last week. Today was Sheena’s first full day back with group after having been held captive by Lestatt Knight for the better part of the week. Desperately he wished he could be with her, but this fan event had been set up just after Dangerous Games, and he didn’t want to pull out at the last minute. So he’d just given her, Lance, and Alex some cash, told them to enjoy a day at the mall. She hadn’t really spoken much last night after they’d gotten back to the hotel, just gone straight to bed. Probably had a lot on her mind, and hopefully the mall trip would take her mind off it, everyone can use a break from reality sometimes. Sighing, wishing the day would end soon, he glanced up as the next photo was slid in front of him. His eyes caught shapely well tanned legs stretching out from a short skirt. If the rest of the body was anything like those legs he’d really like to get to know this woman. Arranging his face into his most charming smile he looked up, only to feel his stomach turn. “Long day bro?” Cindy, ugh he couldn’t believe he’d just been checking out his own sister. She’d always had a knack for popping up when he least expected her, and today was no different. The guy standing next to her must be her fianc?, for the life of him he couldn’t remember the guy’s name. He’d only met him once, and even then had only seem him across a crowded room. Perhaps it was time for a proper introduction. Standing he motioned for the two of them to come around the table. Cindy met him with a hug, which was almost awkward due to his earlier bad thoughts. Trying not to let his distaste show, he held her close, before letting go and pushing her back a bit. “How’ve you been sis” Seems like forever since I last saw you.” “It’s been a while, you missed the wedding.” Oh shit, he’d completely forgotten about that. He’d planned to attend but everything had been crazy. Honestly he was a little surprised it’d already happened he could’ve sworn it was supposed to be next month. But then again as busy as his schedule had been, it wasn’t too shocking that he’d gotten the dates mixed up. At least he’d already given them, their wedding gift. Inwardly he chuckled about that one, turning over ownership of a multi-million dollar company was one hell of a gift. “God I’m sorry sis, everything’s just been hectic lately. I could’ve sworn it was next month though, anything I can do to make it up to you?” She leaned in real close lips inches from his ear. “You could actually introduce yourself to my husband.” “Oh, yeah, shit, sorry.” Stevens pulled away from Cindy only to step forward with hand extended towards the husband. The guy smiled back and extended his hand as well. Firm handshake, at least the guy wasn’t as big a pussy as he looked, that was a mark in his favor. Still for the life of him, Stevens couldn’t remember the guy’s name. That would definitely be awkward, maybe if he just introduced himself, the guy would feel obliged to do the same. “Hey man, it’s nice to actually meet you, I’m Michael Stevens.” “Ha, I know, it’s not like you really need any introduction.” Shit, should’ve known that wouldn’t work. Oh well it was worth a shot, maybe he could just get by with calling him generic bro’s and dude’s until Cindy finally slipped up and called him by name. It was something to hope for anyway. Glancing back towards the crowd, he could see that many of them, were starting to get uncomfortable. They’d been standing in line all day to get an autograph, and he’d been ignoring them for a couple minutes now. He knew he’d have to get back to signing soon, or this whole event might turn ugly, especially considering he was only scheduled to be here another hour. He’d really have to blow through everyone pretty fast if he wanted to take care of everyone in line. “I hate to do this guys, but I’ve really got to get back to the crowd here. If you want, hang out, and we’ll go grab a bite to eat after I finish up here.” “Sounds like a plan, Cindy and I will do some walking around, and check out what else is going on around here.” “Yeah, Mikey, we’ll see you in an hour or so.” “See you both.” He turned back to the table and sat as the two of them walked away. He’d have to figure out that guys name if he was going to actually spend time with them. Replacing the false smile on his face he faced the next person in line, a rather large woman with a mullet, as she waddled forward she tried to catch his eye. Jesus, this should be fun. ************************************************************************************ He let out a weary sigh as the crowd finally began to disperse. He’d gone past his schedule by more than half an hour, but the fans just wouldn’t stop coming. Finally he’d simply told them they’d have to come back tomorrow. He might not actually make it back, and he felt a slight twinge of guilt for lying, but he had to go. Cindy and her beau should be around here somewhere. He wished they’d just stuck around the autograph table that certainly would have made finding them easier. Of course they were probably taking in all the sights of the fan convention. He knew Cindy was still pretty interested with wrestling as a whole, but he wasn’t sure about the husband. If he shared her passion they could be anywhere. Maybe he should just call Cindy, that should work. Before he could even reach for his phone it started to ring. For some reason, he had a really bad feeling about answering. It seemed like whenever his phone rang just as he was about to make a call it was bad news. Oh well, maybe this time would be different, one had to try and be optimistic. Pulling the phone from his pocket he glanced at the display. Sheena, ah they must be done at the mall. Well, maybe all of them could get together for a bite once he found Cindy, and what’s his name. Flipping the phone open he placed it up to his ear. “Hey babe, how was your day?” He tried to sound nonchalant, he figured she could use a complete return to normalcy. Awkward questions about what she went through could push her over the edge to a mental breakdown, and so could just acting differently around her. “Where are you?” Crap, she sounded anxious. Something was going on, and it probably wasn’t good. Was one fucking good day too much to ask for? “Still at FanFest, what’s wrong?” “Oh, nothing, you just told me when you left today you’d call me at five, as in an hour ago.” Jesus, he needed a secretary or something to keep track of all this stuff. At least she sounded less anxious and more annoyed than anything else now. He could deal with her being annoyed. “Sorry, the signing went a little long, and Cindy showed up with her new husband so that took some time away. Right now I’m trying o find the both of them, I figured we could all get dinner tonight.” “Maybe, I got to run Mike, I’ll see you tonight call me.” “Um ok. Bye?” He never even got a chance to finish his sentence she hung up so quickly. That was odd to say the least. He could’ve just chalked it up to the week she’d had but he had a nagging suspicion that there was something hiding below the surface there. He couldn’t put his finger on it, but there was definitely something there, something in her voice that spoke volumes. Oh well, there wasn’t a lot he could do about it at the moment, maybe he could have Cindy talk to her. The day’s festivities were thankfully winding down all over and the press of people at the event were starting to head towards there vehicles. As they thinned out Stevens was able to catch the barest of breezes which helped to take the edge of the omnipresent heat. Rummaging through his pockets he produced a nearly crushed pack of cigarettes and his silver zippo. Shaking one from the pack he lit it and replaced the items in his pocket. The harsh acrid smoke felt good as it burned down his throat and into his lungs, he’d been so busy all day this was the first one he’d had and it felt amazing. Spotting the taller figure of his new brother-in-law, he began heading in their direction. They stood in front of a ring that had been set up for the fans to tumble around in, watching a couple of kids trying out their skills. For a moment he stopped and watched them. Both kids had potential of course they were both at the age where you still bounced when you hit the ground, that’d all change in a couple of years. Wistfully he thought back to those days, the time before we woke up stiff and in pain every morning. Tearing his eyes away from the ring, he turned his attention to Cindy, and her man. He held her closely, and she rested her head against his shoulder. He couldn’t see their faces but just from their body language they seemed so happy, so content, not a care in the world between them. He wished he could feel like that someday, but nothing ever seemed to end up working out. He was always either messing up his love life somehow, or something else interfered. Like right now, just when he thought everything was getting on track with Sheena, Lance shows up, and then the crap with Lestatt, he knew nothing would get resolved anytime soon, c’est la vie, he could still dream. “You two ready to rock?” He must have taken them by surprise as they both jumped a bit when he spoke. Turning both had warm smiles on their face. It looked like they’d been enjoying themselves here, he was glad for that. At least they hadn’t been bored waiting for him, he wouldn’t felt like an ass if that’d been the case. Still he had an obligation to his fans, and he couldn’t have just left. Either way it didn’t seem like that was going to be an issue. Thank god for small favors. “You all done for today Mike?” “Yeah, the concerts about to start soon, and well it’s not really my type of music. Did you two want to stay and watch?” He really hoped they’d say no, he didn’t want to have to think up an excuse as to why he wouldn’t be able to attend. He just wanted to get everyone headed towards one place, so he could get Cindy to talk with Sheena. They two looked at each other and shrugged before turning back to him, he couldn’t get a read on what the answer would be from their faces, he’d just have to be in suspense a couple seconds longer, it’d feel like a lifetime. “Probably not, we were just talking a bit ago about how hungry we both were, maybe we could go get a bite to eat?” It was Cindy who spoke echoing his suggestion from earlier in the day. Perfect, now all he had to do was figure out where they were going to go and have Sheena and Alex meet them there. Shouldn’t be too hard. “So, what sounds good then” I’m up for anything honestly.” “I don’t know, Jeff what do you think?” JEFF! YES! He got the guy’s name and he didn’t even have to work for it. This was all ending up better than he could’ve possibly hoped. At least he could refer to them individually now instead of a collective. It was amazing how something as simple as not having to awkwardly ask someone their name could be so smile inducing. “Just a burger would work for me, I’m easy, you know that.” At least the guy had good taste chalk another one up for him so far. He hadn’t spent enough time with him yet to gain a good impression of the man, but so far Stevens actually kind of liked the guy. His mannerisms and the way he was dressed suggested he might be a little straight laced though. Which wasn’t exactly a bad thing, in fact it’d probably be a bonus for most people, but it wasn’t something exactly impressed Michael. Of course it was better than him being some junkie loser, that’s for damn sure. A huge smile on his face, Stevens extended his hand towards his brother-in-law, Jeff looked a little surprised but took his arm from around Cindy and caught Michael’s hand in his own. Shaking it vigorously he spoke. “I think I like you already Jeff.” “Hehe, thanks, I’m sure we’ll get along just fine. Cindy’s told me a lot about you.” “Oh has she now?” ************************************************************************************* He waited outside the restaurant, one of those chain family joints. TGIAppledays or something like that. Of course he wasn’t able to smoke inside so he’d taken Sheena’s being late as an excuse to go outside and have a puff. Not being able to have a cigarette was just a damn inconvenience, especially when you had a beer inside with your name written all over it. The government chipped away at your rights little by little, and no one bothered to say anything about it, he’d be pissed off about it, but he didn’t vote anyway so he figured he didn’t really have the right to complain’too much anyway. Flicking the cigarette onto the sidewalk, he watched as the cherry exploded upon hitting the ground. It was like a miniature fireworks display, especially for him. Putting his head against the wall and checked his watch. He hadn’t been able to get ahold of Sheena, but had talked briefly to Alex. The big man had been strangely nervous sounding while on the line. That alone would’ve been enough to convince him that something was wrong, but add that to the way Sheena had acted, and the fact that she wasn’t answering, and it all added up to trouble with a capital t. They should have been here by now. Alex had told him that he was just loading up Sheena and Lance in the car, and heading towards the restaurant. They were only a few blocks away, but that had been more than an hour ago. He cursed himself for allowing Lance to tag along on the road with them. Obviously the kid needed some kind of help, but he wasn’t getting it staying with them. He’d been getting high when Sheena was kidnapped a week ago, and he’d almost gotten Alex to fall off the sobriety wagon. The kid was a walking disaster, Stevens would’ve liked nothing more than to confront the kid and keep him to the curb, but that just wasn’t in the cards right now. If nothing else he’d have to have a talk with the kid, he hadn’t had a chance since he’d found all this out. Though he realized that soon he’d have to make time. So lost in this own thoughts he didn’t hear the door open behind him. He jumped and spun on his heel as he felt a hand on his shoulder. He felt almost foolish when he saw it was nothing more threatening than Cindy. She looked a little worried, maybe he’d let his thoughts show a little too clearly on his face. “How long have you been out here?” “Just a minute I wanted to see what was keeping you.” “Oh nothing, just waiting for Alex and Sheena. You know how reliable those two are.” He tried to make the last sound like a joke, but obviously it didn’t work too well. Cindy had always been able to tell when something was bothering him, sisterly instinct he supposed. Even though the two had never been particularly close, they were probably closer with each other, and either was with their parents. It wasn’t exactly an abusive household by any means, just cold. Their parents had just been too busy to show the children much affection, as such neither had really felt the need to show affection to the other. Where Michael had thrown himself into athletics and scholastics, Cindy had devoted more of her time to developing a rich fantasy life. Obviously she’d always been the more empathic of the two. “Mike, you know you can’t lie to me, what’s going on?” “Honestly Cin, I don’t know. Everything’s just crazy right now, Sheena’s barely said two words to me since I got her back, Alex was acting all funny on the phone, and that rat bastard Lance. Ugh I don’t even want to get into all the problems I have with that kid.” “How bad is it?” “Don’t know, and I’m almost scared to find out.” A sad little smile on her face she stood on her tip toes and planted a kiss on his cheek. He could probably count on one hand the number of times she’d done that in her life, so it was still something very special. Putting their arms around each other the stood for a moment in a quiet embrace. Two siblings united for one of the few times in their life through shared pain. Michael instantly felt a wave of guilt for dumping this at her door step, he’d never really been one to talk about his feelings. Preferring instead to keep them bottled up, he’d never wanted felt his life bad enough to really warrant complaint. Their moment of peace was shattered by his phone, blaring out the hook of The Ramones “Blitzkrieg Bop”. Pulling away from Cindy he dug into his pocket and produced the phone, Alex displayed across the front LCD screen. Flipping open the phone he took a deep breath as he answered, fully expecting the worst. “Hello?” “Mike’hi.” “What’s wrong Alex, where are you?” “We’re in the parking lot now, but Mike, I don’t think you’re going to like what you see if we come in there. Why don’t you just go inside and eat, and you can meet us back at the hotel?” He was hiding something, that was obvious. He didn’t want to talk about whatever was going on, judging by his voice he was clearly ashamed of something. Whether it was of himself or someone else remained to be seen. Sick and tired of the games, he decided then and there that he was going to find out exactly what was going on. He couldn’t take this anxious nonsense anymore. “Just stay right there, I’m already outside, I’ll come and find you.” “Mike’please, don’t.” So much agony in the big man’s voice. He was probably trying to hide something that he thought would cut Stevens to the core. Oh well, it couldn’t be helped, whatever it was, he was just going to have to face it, no matter how much Alex wanted to keep it from him. “Alex, I’ll be out there in a minute.” He snapped the phone shut, and jammed it back into his pocket. Shaking his head in mental exhaustion he feigned a smile at Cindy. “Cin, go inside and get yourself some dinner. I don’t know what’s going on but I’m going to find out. I might not be back in, if I’m not I hope we can get together tomorrow once everything has had a chance to settle. I’ve got a feel though that tonight’s going to be a very long night.” He went to give her another hug when she took a step back, placing hands on her hips. Cocking one eyebrow she gave him her best “are you crazy look”. He’d seen it more than enough times growing up to know something was coming. He didn’t relish trying to fight her on this one, so he’d pretty much given up before she even got a chance to speak. “Michael, do you really think I’m going to be able to eat when I know you’re out here dealing with god knows what?” “Ummm, yes” Look, Cindy I really don’t feel like fighting you on this. If you want to go inside and have a meal, please do with my blessing, don’t feel like you’ve got to follow me about there. However if you want to come with, you’re more than welcome to, just make sure and tell your husband where you ran off to, alright?” “I told him I’d be out here until Sheena and Alex arrived. So he’s expecting to be in there for awhile, don’t worry there was a baseball game on one of the TV’s in there, he’ll be entertained.” “Whatever.” The two began walking out into the park lot. It was a pretty big area, devoted not only to the restaurant but a small shopping center. They could be anywhere, but Stevens had faith that he’d be able to find it pretty easily. Deciding he didn’t want to think about all the bad things that he might end up running across once he got to the vehicle he decided to make a little small talk, after all there wasn’t a lot he knew about his new brother-in-law, and no time like the present to ask. “So, what does this Jeff guy do for a living anyway?” “Why do you ask?” “I dunno, just figured I should get to know a little more about the guy who married my little sister.” “He’s a doctor, psychiatrist actually.” “Great, a shrink.” “Hey, isn’t that Alex right there?” “Yeah that’s him.” The kid guy was shifting nervously beside the car. Seeing the two approach he quickly ducked down, sticking his head in the open back passenger side window. He looked to be saying something, and once he stood again he looked even more nervous than before. This didn’t bode well, but Stevens was about to find out exactly what was going on, and knowing would be half the battle. They arrived at the car without a word. Alex looked over at the two of them, meeting their gaze for only a moment he flushed a deep crimson and hang his head. Mumbling he spoke. “Hi, Mike, Cindy.” “Alex.” “Hi, Alex, long time.” “Yeah, I guess it has been.” Stevens shook his head sadly, he didn’t have the patience to deal with this right now. Exhausted from a already too long day he couldn’t deal with the waiting anymore. “Alex, just cut the crap and tell me what’s going on here. Sheena and Lance are in the back seat there I assume.” Again Alex mumbled this time he spoke so quietly it was impossible to hear him. “Speak up damn it, I’m not in the mood for games here Alex.” “Yeah, they’re back there.” “Good.” Bracing himself for the worst, whatever that may be Stevens stepped forward placing his hand on the door handle. He refused to look inside through the window, wanting to see everything fully instead of a brief glimpse before the full reveal. As he began to lift the handle, Alex spoke again once again. “Mike stop. I’m sorry man, I couldn’t stop them. They kinda got away from me at the mall, and went I caught back up, they were like that. Don’t be too mad, you know how much Sheena’s been through..” Waiting no longer he pulled open the door. Peering inside he found Lance head against the glass of the back driver’s side window. His eyes were closed, sleeves of his long shirt rolled up past his elbow. A bright red sore was visible on his inner arm, a fresh track mark. Sheena sat with her head lolling to the side cheek pressed against the headrest. Her own inner arm starting to bruise over, she must’ve been the first one to take the needle. Inarticulate rage filled his body, his cheeks flushed as his ire rose. How the hell could she be so stupid, didn’t she realize what could happen from sharing a needle with that idiot junkie brother of hers? He swore to god if that fucking prick gave her anything. He ground his teeth, as he looked at the scene before him. As he watched Sheena’s eyes fluttered open, glassy, stupid with the drugs flowing through her body, she half smiled up at him. Trying to get out of the car she fell into his arms. Holding her up was all he could do, she was nothing more than dead weight. “Hi Mikey, we missed you. Lance and I had such a good time. So much better than with that other guy.” “I’m sure you did. Can you stand up baby, I need you to put your feet on the ground and stand up.” “I’ll try.” She pushed against his chest, after a moment finally getting her feet underneath her body. He turned towards Alex and Cindy. The two stood close to each other, Alex’s head rest on her shoulder, he was wrung out, this whole thing must be tearing him up inside. “Guys can you take her for a minute, maybe walk her around some” I need a minute alone with her dear sweet brother.” He took a couple steps forward Sheena attempting to keep up. Despite everything that was happening he found himself just a little proud of her, he’d seen a lot of people like this and only the truly strong ones could actually walk, even with help. Gently he passed her off to Alex, she stumbled forward throwing her arms around his waist. “ALEX! You’re so great.” The huge man patted her on the back and started to walk her around the car, leaving Michael and Cindy alone. Glancing over his shoulder Stevens saw his sister continuing to stand there. His lips twitched into a smile and when he spoke his voice was quiet and calm. For anyone that knew him as well as she did, that was a clear indicator of trouble brewing. “Cindy, shouldn’t you go with Michael. Like I said, I need a minute alone with Lance here.” “I’m not stupid Michael, and I’ll be damned if I’m going to leave you alone with that poor screwed up kid.” “Fine, you can watch.” Reaching into the car he gripped the front of the young man’s shirt with both hands. Growling in fury he pulled Lance from the seat and onto the concrete. The violent motion brought the kid back to life as his eyes fluttered open, even more glassy than Sheena’s he’d either taken a bigger dose or fixed up in the car on the way here. “You stupid wretched little piece of SHIT! You put that junk in your own sister?” “Huh, what, Mike?” Letting go of Lance’s shirt with one hand Stevens slapped him across the face. Probably too hard, but right now he didn’t care, damn this kid and his habits. Damn him for shattering Alex’s self esteem damn him for dragging Sheena down to his own miserable level. Once the violence started he couldn’t stop backhanding the kid with the return stroke of his original slap, he let Lance crumble to the ground, blood trickling from the corners of his mouth. The kid was already starting to cry, all his bravado gone, he was nothing but a scared child being beaten by a clear headed and deadly adult. “Stop please, stop.” His cries of pain, and begging didn’t penetrate that fog that clouded Michael’s vision, all he could see was red, all he could to is deliver punishment. “You stupid sack of garbage I should kill you right here, I can’t believe you’d do something like this.” Without even realizing it he began kicking the wretched creature on the ground. His foot lashing out with careful precision striking the most painful areas possible. Desperately the kid tried to cover up but to no avail. “Michael, stop it you’re killing him.” He didn’t hear Cindy’s voice, he could only hear the pounding of the blood in his ears. He felt a hand on his shoulder, but he pulled away, lashing out with more kicks. The kid had to pay for what he’d done, and this was the best way Stevens knew. Unbeknownst to Michael, Alex had arrived back with Sheena. He deposited her on the hood of the car and wrapped his arms around Stevens’s waist dragging the smaller man off his feet kicking and screaming. After a minute he stopped struggling and just went limp. Alex dumped Stevens near another car to cool off, and as the Spitfire hit the ground all the rage seemed to drain away. He’d gotten it out, and as he looked across the parking lot his rage was replaced by horror. The kid lay in a pool of his own blood, unmoving. Stevens couldn’t believe he’d done it, but that it was his handy work was obvious. He closed his eyes thanking god Alex arrived when he had, overwise it could’ve been a lot worse. Right now the big guy was checking on Lance, while Sheena was laid out across the hood of the car, passed out again. Cindy was no where to be found, she’d probably gone inside to get Jeff, even if he was just a psychiatrist he was a doctor. Just as this thought occurred to him, the sound of flapping footsteps against the pavement, he saw the two of them arrive, Jeff immediately going to the body, while Cindy knelt down by Michael’s side. Folding her arms around him he buried his head in her shoulder. Cursing himself for a fool he began to sob quietly the full weight of what he’d done crashing down on him like a ton of bricks. He may have just killed someone. ~Fin~
