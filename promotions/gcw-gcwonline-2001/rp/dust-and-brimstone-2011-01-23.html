Dust and Brimstone

23 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/23/11 ﻿In an era where wrestlers drive the finest cars and wear the nicest clothes available... The Highway Child, Jimi Thorne, is much simplier... driving a beatup, jet black 67 Oldsmobile, his worn, psychedelic cowboy boots punches the gas as he travels to the next town. You may think he's a tramp, but it goes a little deeper than that ﻿Deep inside his rather cool demeanor, Jimi is a man of limited means, carrying his possession in a small bag. Though he has no tickets, no warrants, drug problems or gambling debts. Even his closest friends say he always seems to be running from something. Running is a very real reality for him... But not from any sort of physical reprieve... [center]*****[/center] Back behind the smoke and mirrors and quite a few years ago... Eleven years old, and in different town... Andrew Hendrix, the same circumstances every night... crying in his bed, too afraid to sleep. Too many nightmares, too many pains for his fragile age. It has been six months since his parents died, and Andy wasn't adjusting. He wasn't getting over it, each night was progressively worse and worse. Until this night when it all stopped. If you thought Andrew was falling apart... Alexandr Bapho was worse, the unwanting father, in the worst possible situation you could imagine, new kid, loss of that kids parents, and the alienation and loneliness of being in his current position. The worst possible guardian little Andrew needed... Just as the boy, Alexandr was unraveling at the seams. And tonight, something finally gave way. Alexandr Bapho: Jenny.................... I can't stand to lose another person in my life... Andrew rose up out of his bed, with a look of confusion on his face, as he heard his "Uncle Bapho" talking, even though it was a quite, somber... jaded voice... Alexandr Bapho: I already lost Maxx and Amy...... really my only fucking family in this life, and Andy, even though he's here... he's long gone as well... Rebel's too busy having fun to care about me or the boy............. But you... you... All of this has happened... Andrew slowly slides out of his bed and peaks out of his room. Bapho is standing, almost as if he's leaning, against the cheap pressboard table in his hotel room, literally drowning himself in a bottle of Jack Daniels. And next to him, clearly frazzled, listening to Bapho is the Goddess, Jenny Ignito. Alexandr Bapho: Please Jenny.... hear me out.... I need you............. I've done alot of things...... and I'm paying for those sins now........ But I need you... Alexandr loses his balance by leaning on the table and stumbles to the ground. Jenny immediately tries to help him up. Alexandr Bapho: I need you Jenny.................. I love you... Jenny sighs deeply. Jenny Ignito: I know. I've always known. Your body language, the words you used. It wasn't a secret to me, Alexandr. I love you too...and I want you in my life...but I am commited to my marriage. Gabrielle is my soulmate. Her and I are Kismet. I can't change that. Alexandr Bapho: But... I need you... Jenny puts her hands on his. Jenny Ignito: And I will always be there for you. No 'goddess' bullshit, no kayfabe. We've been friends for years...and I value your love...but...that's as far as it can go. Alexandr Bapho: But... I need you... This isn't a time when I need you as a friend... I have no-one.................. I can't just live... exist without you... Jenny Ignito: ...And you won't. I will be here for you....but I can't betray the love of my life. You can't ask me to leave my happiness...just because it would make you happy. I'm trying my damndest to make this work...can't you see that? Alexandr Bapho: But... I love you... you know me... I can count all the people I love one hand... and all of them are already gone... Without you... I have nothing... Jenny Ignito: That's not true. You have me, just not the way you want me. Gabrielle, Raquel...and aren't you forgetting Andrew? He looks up to you as a father. He loves you. Alexandr Bapho: Of course I love Andy... but I can't be the father he needs me to be... if I have no-one there for me... Jenny Ignito: ﻿Jenny: Then you're using me as a crutch. I won't accept that. You've already BEEN his father since the accident. Think about his feelings. Think about mine! We ALL lost Maxx and Amy. This is not just hard on you. Alexandr Bapho: Well, we all need a crutch... somebody to help us from falling... Maxx had Amy... Amy had Maxx... and Gabby has you... So who the fuck do I have?!? ........Who do I have? Jenny Ignito: You have all of us, you stubborn fool! Don't you get that? Alexandr Bapho: I do... but I don't... I NEED you in a way Maxx and Amy needed each other and how all our friends ALWAYS had somebody for them. Jenny........ I need you in that way... Not as a friend... or even family... I need you. Jenny stands, it being obvious to her now that Bapho can't hear her. Jenny Ignito: I can't... With tears streaming down her beautiful face, Jenny walks out of Bapho's room, as he lies in a heap next to the table, crying. Alexandr Bapho: I need you Jenny... I need you... Jenny slams the door as she leaves, gone... unattainable... After witnessing everything, Andrew Hendrix is stunned silent. But before he can say or do anything. Bapho slowly gets back to his feet, still crying, he walks for the door and exits the room himself. Not to chase Jenny, but to solve his own problems, going in the exact opposite way. Andrew Hendrix: Uncle Alex? Alone... Andrew Hendrix: Uncle Alex... [center]*****[/center] A small tear cascades down Jimi's face, from underneath his sunglasses, as he turns into the private parking area of the Wachovia Spectrum parking structure. He passes light after light in the darkened corridor, counting off every little promise he made to his Uncle Alex. Even though he had every right to hate him for overdosing on acid, and trying to forget about all his pains, and in the process, Andrew Hendrix... He always loved him and followed his every word... He knew his uncle didn't want him to follow down the same sinful path and end up with a broken life. But this was Jimi's life... Not Bapho's life... his mother or father's life... or even Andrew's life. Jimi Thorne knows that to get through hell, you have to get a little brimstone on your cowboy boots, but not let it burn you alive. That's the reality of this Highway Child, maybe one day... He won't have to run.
