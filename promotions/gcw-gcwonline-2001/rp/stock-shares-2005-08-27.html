'Stock Shares'

27 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/27/05 “Nice shot,” John Baratta said as Stephens discarded his driver back into his bag. Stephens gave a slight nod to acknowledge the elder Baratta’s comment as Blaise, his son, rolled his eyes. John and Ryan rode in one golf cart and were followed by Blaise and another little slime ball affiliated with Baratta Inc. by the name of Miles. “Ryan, we seem to have a problem,” John stated tactfully. “Well we certainly don’t need any hitches in this operation. What’s going down?” Ryan asked. “I received word less than a week ago that many of our share holders were having their stock purchased from them,” John said. Ryan smiled. “By a company, or what?” he asked. “Quite the contraire, by one man,” said John. They had reached Ryan’s shot and as he got out of the cart to choose a club he replied, “And what one man would have sufficient financial means to do such a thing?” “You would,” John said sternly, but calmly. His old wrinkled face, and the excess skin hanging down from his neck were signs that perhaps he was beginning to decline, and it was important to him that things were in order before he passed Barrata Inc. off to his youngest son, Blaise, who was a smart man, but not quite the capable brutes that his older sons were who he had running other operations, or his nephews were had ice water running through their veins. Stephens hit his ball and walked back towards the cart. “Don’t misunderstand what I’m trying to do John,” Ryan said. “I want the company to grow and monopolize wrestling television. I want to host EVERY PTC affiliated event on your network, and in order to do so” I just needed to extend my ties within the company.” John stared at Stephens, the cold black of his eyes, that tired grimace. He was calculating the threat, but he still had the advantage so for the time being he let his anger subside. “Besides John, you still control 51% of the company, and I don’t assume you’ll be selling any of those shares to me. So really now, what do you have to be worried about?” Ryan reasoned. John knew he was right, hell he was always right. But still a drop of sweat mapped it’s way down a crevice in his face, and he loosed his collar in light of a horrible drowning sensation, the world closing in around him. When the round was over, the four men sat around a table inside the clubhouse. Blaise and Miles were indulging on a round of Budweiser, while Stephens drank a water and the elder Baratta looked on. He had seen enough of for the day, but it was customary that they all act proper and behave themselves. They all conducted themselves that way, well perhaps with the exception of Blaise. “So, what are you doing tonight Ryan?” Stephens merely looked up at Blaise as if to say, why the hell are you interested? “Don’t go all hard ass on me Stephens. I only ask, because your ex-wife will be accompanying me on one of our weekend barbeques this weekend. Would you care to join us?” he sniveled. Ryan smiled, and shook his head ever so slightly. “No thanks.” His ex-wife Robin had been with Blaise for about 14 months now, and Blaise would do his best to flaunt her around if he thought Stephens would be anywhere in the vicinity. It bothered Stephens more than it had at first, which might not be what you expect, but then Stephens was more nervous than those who know him would anticipate him being. Everything had to go perfect, and there were too many additional factors that he couldn’t plan for at this point. Almost two years in the making. Finally, as the men stood to leave Ryan overheard Miles comment to Blaise, “Hey man, invite that cousin of yours out tonight.” “Who?” Blaise asked. “You know, that hot one that runs track for that little school up in Austin. The little blonde, what’s her name?” “Paige?” “Yeah, that the one. I you know I dig her. Call her up.” It hit Ryan like a ton of bricks. Another twist in the plot, one that could’ve been avoided had he just stayed on track. Had John managed to outsmart him, or did Paige know nothing of her Uncle’s intentions. She had too, you can’t have an uncle like John, and not know. He thought of calling to cancel the date, but he decided instead to feel her out. He liked her. A lot. [FAST FORWARD: BARATTA’S HOUSE] “It’s been a long time. You’ve tolerated me for this long and so I’ve decided to give you something,” Blaise said. He held Robin Boca’s hand in his own and stared into her eyes. “I don’t know if that’s such a good idea Blaise,” she said hesitantly. “Calm down my dear. It’s not a ring, not yet anyway. I just transferred two shares of Baratta Inc. stock shares into your name. If you’re going to be part of this family, you should have part of the company.” “Oh baby, you’re so thoughtful,” she said. She ducked underneath his arm, wrapped her arms around his waist and held him tight.
