The Beginning

4 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th October, 2005 The Beginning Backlog of Events What can be said about a man trying to prove himself to the world, A man known only as Macavity Frost has the world on his shoulders and he is about to prove himself to all that Is looking, Nothing can be said or predicted about Macavity Frost other than another name thrown to the lions that are known as the fans of Global championship wrestling. For those unaware of previous events leading up to the present date let me tell you a little about Macavity, He was Born and Raised by a strong beloved family in Iceland, Reykjavik to be precise. Unaware to his family at the time Macavity was one of the three children that would make names of themselves in the world, Macavity’s brother Petra was a well known scientist in Reykjavik and nearly found a cure for cancer before he was assonated. His Sister Norma was a “Miss Iceland” winner in two thousand and four; she also was assonated by the same group of rebels who ran riot in Reykjavik. The main reason Macavity wasn’t assonated was because he left Reykjavik, he travelled day and night and slummed it out on the streets trying to earn a few extra dollars so he could get his life together in another country, well years past and he grew older, wiser and a lot richer more than anything. He got so rich that one day he had enough to finally leave Reykjavik and he did just that. Though this current day Macavity is a lot different from the man who just left Reykjavik, Today Macavity can be found in a nice Studio Apartment in the south side of Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Since leaving his home-land Macavity has never looked back on it, he took up a sport of wrestling which has found him fame in Canada. Of Course you only know of Macavity because he is to debut this week for Global Championship Wrestling, it’s funny that none of the fans know Macavity just yet, oh but they soon will’They soon Will. Present Day “Drop” A splash of liquid hits a hard wooden floor; the sound is echoed around the room for a few seconds and is quickly replaced by a fresher echo of another drop. “Drop” As the second drop hits the floor we can hear some footsteps getting closer and closer, with every step we are dragged more into suspense to figure out who is actually walking and why we are sat listing to a drop. “Drop” As the third drop hits the floor an arm is reached out to catch the forth in mid-flight, the liquid hits the palm of a person who is stood in some shadows, the figure leans further into picture and looks upwards at a pipe where the drops are falling freely from. He then looks down once again at his palm where a small puddle of liquid is forming. He then waits for another drop to splash into the puddle, this causes more water to leak through the gaps in his fingers and drip down onto his clothes. After a brief pause the man throws the remaining water on the floor and walks through the puddle on the floor. He is gone for a few moments before coming back into picture with a silver pale, he bends down slowly and places the pale under the drip, he then stands back and allows the liquid to drip into the pale. “Ding” Another Drop “Ding” A loud scream is heard by the person, whom is in picture before he runs and kicks the pale to the other side of the room, Suddenly more voices are heard in the surroundings and a light suddenly flashes on, In the room stands two people and our mystery figure who kicked the pale over is recognized as Global Championship Wrestling’s own Macavity Frost. One of the un-important men walk over to the over side of the room and pick up the pale before turning to Macavity and speaking. Unknown Man ? “Jeez, was there any need for that”!” The man speaks with a middle Asian accent, from a rough guess I would go for the Philippines but I could be wrong. He stands around five foot ten inches and has a black suit on; he looks very smart and important. Macavity on the other hand is the totally opposite, he wears no top and is covered in what appears to be water that has dropped down onto him, he wears white slacks what also appear to have had water splashed on them, Macavity looks over to the Asian man and begins to talk, slowly and in a deep voice. Macavity ? “When I ask you to speak Naka I will do, but for the time being be quite please, I am trying to stop this frigid leak and its doing my head in!” Naka aka the Asian man quickly apologizes and offers a deal to Macavity Naka ? “Ok, Sorry Macavity, it was wrong of me to jump in their, I was rude. However I still think all of this is taking its toll on you, it wasn’t only two weeks ago that you was looking for work and now today you find yourself only days before the live debut of Macavity Frost in Global Championship Wrestling. May I suggest you take a few breaths in and out then focus your energy on actually beating your opponent?” Macavity looks at Naka with a smirk on his face and then nods at him indicating that he acknowledges what Naka has just put forward to him. He then raises his hand and places his finger up in the air, before nodding once more and putting it down, he then turns away from Naka walks a few steps and slumps down on an old looking couch that is made from red leather. Macavity then leans backwards and slowly begins to talk with his eyes closed, once again his mind isn’t free from thoughts so he is talking in a deep dark somewhat hard to understand European accent. Macavity ? “What must I do to prove myself once again, what can I do Naka to make myself stand out from the crowd” I need to find myself these answers Naka. And I must start finding these answers out soon. I mean once I was hailed “Macavity is one of those performers that only come around every Century”, and look at me now Naka. Nobody in the crowd knows of me, I must change their Views. Macavity makes himself more relaxed on the Couch before carrying on. Macavity ? I mean this week I really have to teach Jayce Bradley a lesson on Wednesday night Naka, I must prove to not only Jayce but to the people in the back watching and all in attendance, hell I even have to prove to the people watching at home that Macavity Frost is a force to be reckoned with in Global Championship Wrestling. I know that I am the new guy and I have to work my way up the ranks but I will not let others under-estimate me. I know because of my size and weight I may not be the biggest fighter around but I learnt wrestling fast, I am an extraordinary learner of the sport and I am ready to bag my first victory on Wednesday night. Macavity ? I am not Over-Confident that I will win, but I am confident that I will become a house hold name in Global Championship Wrestling, I am Confident that people will come and see me, They will hail me as the Greatest bright future to walk through the doors since Octavian. You see if you cheer for me, I will die for you. And that’s a motto I have lived with since I was a little boy, I am loyal to those who love me and I will prove that on Wednesday night. Macavity ? Pride and trust can go a long way in a mans life, and they are the two main factors in my Life, Jayce when you step into a ring with myself I can promise you two things, firstly I will respect you until that bell rings, because like I found out you actually have to have talent before you get signed. And secondly I will trust you to put on the best performance of your life for the fans that come to see us. Macavity ? Normally I do not care to much for the fans, they normally either cheer or boo? me during my matches, but it really annoys me if I don’t give one hundred and ten percent every match, they came to see me so I will give it my all, and Jayce I expect you to do the same, if all goes well I expect a good match on Wednesday, but please don’t feel bad when you are staring up at the lights flat on your back from a severe case of “Frost-Bite” Macavity Looks over at Naka with a refreshed look on his face, he then slowly gets up to his feet and walks over to the third man in the room who is also Asian looking, however standing nearly seven foot tall he is clearly some kind of protection for Macavity. Naka Slowly begins to talk once again hoping to get Macavity’s Attention. Naka ? Macavity? Do you feel better now? Macavity Stops suddenly and turns around looking at Naka with a red glare in his eyes, He speaks quickly and loud. Macavity ? “Yes’I feel much better now, but come with me’Matches cannot be won by sitting down on couches, we have much to plan before we make our Impact On Wednesday. Come on; let’s show the whole world what Macavity Frost is all about” Naka gets up from his seat and follows Macavity and the Protection, they walk over to the other side of the room and out of the door, A hand opens the door once again from the outside and leans over to the light switch on the inside of the room before switching it off. The Footsteps go into the distance getting quieter each time; once again we are left with the sound of a drip, “Drip?”. “Drip?”. “Drip?”. *End*
