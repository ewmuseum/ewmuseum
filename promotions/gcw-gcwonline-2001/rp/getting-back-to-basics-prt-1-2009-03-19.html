Getting back to basics prt.1

19 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/19/09 Shaman is rifling through a storage shed seemingly searching for something. Alot of things being thrown out into the driveway nearby as several passerby's walk by stop and begin talking, Shaman turns around and looks at them. He shrugs them off and begins rummaging through a box he has somewhere. Looking over his shoulder he stills sees the people gawking at him and whispering. He turns and stalks out of the storage shed looking at them. "Got something to say?" They both nod no and hurriedly get away from him. "Yeah that's what i thought!" Shaman turns back into the storage shed as he begins to rummage through stuff again. "Damn people cant even mind their own damn business. I mean if i stuck my nose in everyone elses shit. Well it'd just simply stink." Reaching in he pulls out a case of Black and White make up as he stares at it. Shrugging he throws it over his shoulder out into the driveway. "Never had a need for that shit." Stretching he cracks his neck side to side reaching back down into the boxes digging deeper. "All of this stored shit and i can never find what i am looking for. They gave me the file and told me what freaks out Garbage Bag Johnny so i am gonna use it to my advantage. I am screw with his damn head like he screwed with my damn match. Causing me to lose to some freaking rookie by getting me off my game." Stopping he looks around at all of the mess he had made. "You know what screw it." Shaman looks around and then reaches into his pocket and pulls out a cell phone and dials a number. He waits as he hears the ringing and then he stops as someone answers on the other side. "Yo hey Tyrone i need you to contact your Dad." The voice gets a little louder as Shaman speaks. "Yes yes i know he is the Beuly man. I have need of his very unique services at the next Global show. Yes yes that exactly. You got that file i sent you right? Yes i got that research directly from the sources evaluation." Stepping out from behind the edge of the storage shed stepping outside he turns and looks about. He stops looking at the phone. "A live chicken? Can't i just get him a bucket of KFC chicken or something?" Stopping he shakes his head. "Come on man a live chicken though? There has got to be some laws about this." Walking back and forth Shaman looks about. He stops and picks up a small box and tosses it back into the storage shed. He then turns frowning. "Alright i'll get it, but rest assured i am not happy about it one bit." Hanging up the phone Shaman begins tossing boxes back into the shed. He begins rambling about some different things involving his match with GBJ as he does so. "It was shitblasted hard to get any information about that guy. he just won some damned major match with Prime and my old friend Ms. Troy probably was not too happy about it. Then again she isn't happy with too much especially Caldera and his sick obsession with her. I really don't give a rats ass. I need to figure out what to do about Mr. GBJ. The asstard is in for one major lesson in pain." Walking back and forth in front of the door the site manager walks up to Shaman and says something to him. The guy is a decent sized guy and walks up poking Shaman in the chest as he speaks. "Look bub how about i make you pick this shit up by your teeth? I don't care who the hell you think you are it doesn't matter to me. I'll knock your ass around the block a couple of times." Simply looking down at the finger poking his chest Shaman raises his gaze looking at the man. "You sure have a damned death wish don't you." The man doesn't let up as Shaman reaches forward and grabs the finger snapping it backwards quickly. His eyes never wavering nor any emotion coming to his face. He forces the man to his knees as there is a loud pop and the man screams out in agony. "You should have figured out who i was by now. Then you might have understood it wasn't wise to confront me or even threaten me. Now how about you go away like a good little piss ant before i crush you beneath my foot!" Shaman shoves the guy backwards as he releases the hand. The man takes off running back towards the office. Shaman frowns and picks up his cellphone and dials a number. He waits for a second and speaks. "Hey Kawanaha call the Police over here in East Bodunk wherever the hell i am at and play damage control." Shaman sighs and looks up at the sky. "What did i do this time? The same thing i did last time. I broke the dudes finger for poking me in the chest." Shaman holds the phone away from his ear as she is yelling at him on the other end. Putting his pinky in his ear he shakes it a bit then shakes his head. "Damn that's loud." Stopping he then grins. "You know what give me his families addresses. I figured if i broke his finger maybe I'll go slap his momma for giving birth to him." Kawanaha yells something over the phone as well. "Hey good idea, Kick his grand daddies ass for even thinking of conceiving someone like his momma." Shaman smirks and laughs a bit at Kawanaha's reaction over the phone. "Look i don't give a flying fuck who he is. I am tired of every stupid jack ass looking to put a feather in his cap thinking he is a bad ass. Come at me and i am going to knock your teeth so far into the back of your head you'll have to see a god damned proctologist to get them cleaned. So don't lecture me over some little bullshit semantics." Rolling his eyes he listens. "All right all right go do what ya need to. Yes I'll pay overtime as well." Hanging up the phone he flips the top shut as he groans. "Here you'd figure she'd give me a break, but damn she is tough even charges me double time and a half while on her own personal time. I don't think i hired an assistant i think i hired a damn blood draining leech lawyer." Pulling a folding chair out he unfolds it and sits down in the entrance way of his storage unit. He turns his head and notices a police cruiser pulling up nearby his unit. The officer steps out and walks over looking at Shaman. The mess a little less and not as profound as Shaman had been throwing random items back into the shed. The officer looks at Shaman and speaks. "Sir we have heard that you recently broke the managers finger here?" Shaman looks up and looks at the officer confused. "No he got it caught in the door." The officer writes something down and then looks at the door. "In the door huh?" Shaman shrugs and rocks a bit in the chair looking about at the boxes behind him seemingly looking for something again. "Yup thats what happens when you hang onto the chain like a moron. Goofing off and keeping your fingers in the chain." The officer shrugs and walks away looking at the office as he walks up and the manager steps out yelling something at the officer pointing at Shaman with his good hand. The officer shrugs saying something as the man gets angry and slams his bad hand with the broken finger against the wall in anger. Then he buckles down clutching the hand like a precious newborn child. Shaman laughs and speaks. "He is a glutton for punishment isn't he." Turning he watches the officer speak over his radio. Turning he reaches down and begins to toss the last remnants of his rummaging fit back into the unit. "GBJ dangerous man or annoying freak. Either way i think there is something you and i need to finish isn't there. We have some very serious business. No hiding behind big upset win matches nor hiding behind your great achievements here in the GCW. Nope i think you and i are going to have to have ourselves a one on one little face to face conversation don't we." Shaman stops as he shuts the unit. He turns and walks out the gate towards his truck. Turning he notices the manager and the couple looking at him. "When do people ever learn to stay the fuck out of my business!" He begins walking towards them cracking his knuckles as the camera fades to black.
