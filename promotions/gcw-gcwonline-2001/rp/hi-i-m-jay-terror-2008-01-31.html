Hi. I'm Jay Terror.

31 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 31, 2008 Her body and his stretched naked beside one another with a narrow stream of smoke sifting out of each’s lips. The comforter did little to hide the human features they owned and, instead, acted more as an insulator for their toes and ankles. Grins on both faces, glee in their eyes, Caroline and Jay laid in silence. An empty bottle of Jack Daniels stood tall and proud on the night stand and if you squinted the right way you might be able to tell that it had a grin of its own. “That was something,” she said. “Something good?” “Something new.” “You think?” She rolled over and placed her arm on his chest in a hugging sort of way. Her fingers curled the hairs on his pecs and he cooed with delight. “Not what I’ve been used to lately. A lot more oomph than usual I suppose. Been practicing on some other girl?” “Oh yeah,” he chuckled, “been givin” it to her hard all week. Taught me a thing or two.” She punched him in the gut and placed her head over the patch of hair she had recently curled. The cigarette continued to burn in her raised hand spewing ashes every which way. “Where’d you run off to last week?” “To see an old friend.” “She help?” “What say’s she was a she?” “Unless you were wearing perfume, I’m going to assume she was a she.” Jay took in a long drag from his 27 and grinned from cheek to cheek, “You got me. Nothing to worry about. Just a friend lending an expert opinion.” “Can’t take my opinion?” “I said expert opinion. It’s what she does.” “Oh.” Silence took them over again and Jay took the time to inhale another whole cigarette. The smoke swam through his body and coursed into his lungs where the tar gathered adding an extra dose of stick to the already sticky solution. He coughed for a moment and shut his eyes. He wanted sleep. He needed sleep. If there was one thing that he was lacking since being on the road, it was sleep. Then again, he did it to himself with all the other activities him and Caroline decided to conjure while they lurked in the night hours. “You tired?” Of course he was tired, “No. You?” “Not really. Wanna” do something?” He’d given in to her pressures every time she gave him the chance and now wasn’t going to be anything different. Or was it? “Not really. I just want to lay here. Bask in the smoke and whatever’s left of this buzz I have.” “You sure?” “Yeah.” She wasn’t used to hearing Jay Terror break through in his voice. For too long, Jamison Talley was the man she knew and the sudden shift of egos was confusing yet welcoming all at once. She couldn’t figure out what to say or how to feel exactly; instead, she took his advice and lay still taking in the smog and Jack Daniels. It was actually quite enjoyable. __________ It wasn't very typical of Jay to make public appearances for the sake of promoting himself and GCW. Hell, if anything, he avoided them all together. When it came down to the raw facts though, there were some things that a man couldn't say no to. No matter how annoying or awkward they were. This was one of those situations. Terror, the up-and-comer of the year, sat behind a microphone looking over a herd of ladies and gentleman staring him down as if he was the scum of the earth. The stare reminded him of one LeStatt Knight would give him and for that reason he decided to stare back just as fierce. 'I'm going to open the floor to questions now. If you would like Jay to answer anything in particular, this is your chance,' said Kevin Klien, one of the useless pieces of dirt sent out to promote talent. The hands shot up right away as if money was being auctioned off and Kevin grinned to himself at the response. His head turned to Jay and his eyes felt out The Outlaw’s feelings instantly. Uncaring to what he wanted to say though, Klien looked over the crowd and pointed the first man out. Paper rustling ceased and random calls ended leaving one man and his words, “Pat Hilt from The Inquirer; Jay, I’m sure everyone is wondering how great of a feeling it is to top LeStatt Knight. Especially after losing to him at NC-17. How does it feel to get one back?” Klien eyeballed Terror and motioned for him to answer but Jay was calm and collective about it. The atmosphere drew him in and he paused as if to think about the question, “You know, that’s a good question. You ever smoke so many 27’s you felt like you could swim to the moon?” Pat shook his head, “I don’t smoke.” “Shame.” Jay thought about offering him a smoke to get the true feeling he meant but figured Klien wouldn’t be very happy about it. “Well it was something like that anyways. I was a plebian when the count started and a god when the referee’s hand struck that mat for the third time. I’ve been sleeping like I’m not an insomniac, which I am, and I’ve been tasting foods that I’ve never thought tasted as good as they do now. This win did something to my life that I could honestly use more of.” The hands rose again and Klien navigated the crowd picking the next man, “You, brown jacket.” He was a porky man bulging through an old-school brown leather jacket and thick box-frame glasses. As he stood he took his pad of paper in his right hand and his pen in his left, “Ken Garrity from the Chronicle; Jay, we’ve seen you win, yes, but do you think that it was a lucky break or something you can reciprocate through the round robin tournament and continue when facing names such as White, Ambrosi, and Rollins?” Unlike Hilt, this guy cut to the throat and asked the question of luck. Jay figured it was coming and was ready enough to answer. “Funny you ask about luck, seems to be a recurring thing for me in this business. I’m not going to lie to you and tell you that I’m a top athlete now but I will tell you this; I have something that can unseat others. I don’t know what it is or how to harness it exactly but as you can see, it works and will work every time.” “So wait,” Ken cut back in, “You’re saying that you have powers now” Something that is magical and makes you win matches?” “Magic is open to interpretation. What I have is a certain characteristic about me that is unmatched in the ring with others. I can’t say what that trait is or how I manage to find it but when I show it I get things done in there.” Jay looked to Klien and searched for a response through his body language. Nothing. Another man cut into the conversation with his pen and pad raised much like Ken’s, “Harry Billam, The Globe; so you’re saying that you were unable to use this trait during NC-17 but managed to harness it on Worldwide 82 somehow?” Jay knew that they were going to bury him if things continued. As much as he wanted to step away though, it didn’t seem like a viable option in a conference like this. “I suppose that’s right. Just wasn’t there.” Harry went on, “What if it doesn’t show when you have to face Rollins next week” Do you have a back-up plan in case you are left firing alone?” “I always have some kind of trick up my sleeve. Especially for Rollins.” An African American man stood next and raised his hand highest thanks to his indomitable height, “Lionel Kennert, The Union-Tribune; Speaking of Rollins, how are you feeling going into the match” Rollins was once Mr. forty-eight and oh and could easily trample over you like he has done before to just about everyone else. How do you prepare for someone knowing how dominant they truly are?” “Once.” He stopped and stared at the crowd making the mystery grow deeper. “He was once known as all of that. Thing is, I haven’t seen that Rollins now. He got lucky over White at NC-17 and figuring I’m lucky I’d say that it’s more even than you’re sloping it to be. Rollins is like anyone else I’ve faced, especially like Knight. Established, past his time, and still on his high horse. Let’s see how high the guy is about himself when he steps into the ring with someone a little different.” Klien’s voice kicked over the crowd, “Who’s next?” “No one,” Jay spoke up, “I’m done.” They hated to hear it and mocked him for the decision but Jay was over it before it mattered. He stood from his chair and left the room letting Klien settle whatever was about to happen in the room. __________ 'That went well enough, you think?' She took in a long drag from Jay's cigarette and let it cultivate in her mouth for a while as she waited for Jay to speak up and answer her. Terror watched her for a moment instead and made close observations. The way she puffed her cheeks when they filled with toxins, the way her eyes blew up wide when she was enjoying herself, the way her fly was open on her pants exposing the see-threw mesh of panties she wore underneath. He focused on everything about her for a moment and after it was gone he shook his head and leaned back in the black arm chair the hotel had provided. 'I guess. Nothing I want to do again though.' She let the smoke go free and passed the 27 across the concrete to Jay's fingers, 'Even if you keep winning?' 'If I keep winning I keep winning. They don't need to know anything more from me. I suppose.' 'I hear you. Just seems like it's good for you. A way to separate yourself.' 'A way to blend in you mean?' 'Blend how?' Jay inhaled from the filter and tapped the cherries off the end of his cigarette, 'Look at Knight and Rollins, they live off that shit. They wake up to sit on the stage and talk about themselves. I wake up to get a cigarette. I don't want to turn into one of them.' Caroline shook her head, 'I doubt that will happen. You're still Jay Terror. A couple interviews won't make you one of the established.' 'Why the push?' 'Just looking out for you.' She called for the cigarette and Jay decided it'd be easier to light her one of her own. He tossed her a fresh Marlboro and she took hold of it in her lips as she went on, 'You're getting bigger and I figured I could help.' 'You'll turn into Daniel.' 'Who's this Daniel you always talk about?' 'Old guy, not the kind of guy you want to become.' Daniel Franco, the devil himself, had luckily gone astray from Jay's career at The Outlaw's disclosure and was now god knows where tormenting god knows who. If there was one person he was afraid of Caroline changing into, Franco was that person. 'How'd you know him?' 'Look at my debut in GCW and you'll find out enough. I'd rather not talk about him.' 'Right.' Silence became the loudest chord on the balcony and both of them laid back in their arm chairs looking over the Missouri skyline. It was fading with the time and becoming a beautiful portrait created with the gentle flicks of purple, blue, and yellow. Along with the silence, Jay's mind ticked on and off passing around idea after idea creating new ones along the way. He wanted to break the silence but was unsure how. Thankfully, Caroline did it first. 'You know Jay Terror, you're really something.' 'Like what? 'Just something.' 'Oh, ok.' 'I love it.' 'Love?' 'Yes, love.' He smiled and she did the same. Reaching across from the inner arms of the chairs were their hands and their fingers interlocked as they took notice of the sun's awesome glory. There was something special about Caroline that he'd noticed since the day he asked her out in the internet cafe and even to this day Jay still felt it. Hell, he even loved it. Shit happens.
