Paris

20 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Written: 9/20/09 takes place mid August ”Please father I need you to take me to the hospital. I… I think,” Cleopatra doubled over in pain clutching her bulging stomach, “I think I am in labor.” His laugh reverberated off the hallway walls, “I will do nothing of the sort. I will deliver the baby. No one needs to know this families disgrace.” His voice filled with disdain. “YOU DID THIS TO ME! You of all people are worried about what people will think,” Cleo groaned and panted her way through a small contraction, “What will you tell people father? That the baby is yours and mother’s?” Her peal of laughter was ear piercing. “Why do you think we have kept you locked up in the house? We don’t need our disgrace of a daughter parading around the streets with her gut protruding out a mile. People would not understand.” He stated with no remorse for his actions. “You should have thought about what people would think the many times you raped me father. I will have my revenge on you. If that is the last thing I do before I die.” Cleo’s voice filled with pride and determination. “I should leave you hear to birth that child by yourself,” he said through clenched teeth, “If I didn’t care what happened to that child that is exactly what I would do. Now move away from the door.” Cleo didn’t fight she simply backed away from the door. She didn’t have the strength to fight anymore. The contractions were coming faster and they were much harder. She knew the time was close. “PUSH DAMN YOU!” Elias screamed at her. Cleo pushed with all her might and seconds later she heard the wail of her baby. “Wait where are you going? Let me see him,” She screamed after her father. “PLEASE! JUST ONCE!” Elias Nagatti stopped and turned toward her so she could briefly cast her eyes on her son. “I need to get him to your mother or our plan will not work.” He looked at Cleo with disgust, “Clean yourself up and then get the hell out of my house. You are of no use to us anymore.” Cleo laughed hysterically, “Is this some sick joke father? I already promised I wouldn’t tell him that I was his mother not his sister.” He looked at her his face distorting with anger, “I told you to get cleaned up and get the HELL OUT OF MY HOUSE!” He turned on his heels and left Cleo sitting there gaping after him. Delylah’s eyes snapped open a little more of Cleo’s life had been revealed to her. The more she saw of her sister’s life she begun not to hate her, she actually pitied her. What person in their right mind would do that to their child? They treated her like a surrogate mother instead of a daughter. Delylah looked over to Paul who was slumped down in his seat and snoring softly. She was glad she picked the window seat. She hated being on a plane and not being able to look out at the sky. She turned her attention to the scenery but the early morning sky looked like a large bruise. The clouds were angry and black, threatening to cause turbulence and make them land before they reached their destination. She prayed that the storm would hold off just a few more hours; they were almost to Paris. She just wished she knew what she would do when she found Cleo. She didn’t want to kill her sister anymore she wanted to develop a truce. She had to save Daniel and the only way she knew to save him was to kill Cleo. Delylah jumped when she felt a hand on her arm. She snapped her head around to find Paul’s sleepy eyes staring back at her. “Are you ok Del?” His face immediately taking on a concerned look. “I’m fine Paul. I was just getting ready to try and get a little more sleep before we landed.” She lied. Paul pulled her as close to him as possible so she could rest her head on his massive chest. She lay there for a little while listening to the melodic beat of his heart and to her surprise she drifted off to sleep. The door opened with the slightest amount of pressure. Daniel slid in stealthily; the medicinal smell gagged him. He held his breath and quickly made his way to a tall steel refrigerator with two doors. He slowly opened the door on the right revealing a stockpile of blood. He sifted through and pulled out four pint bags of the crimson liquid. He stole out as quickly as he had entered, shoving the bags inside of his leather coat. Daniel quickly made his way around the corner and into a darkened alleyway. He looked around making sure no one was watching him and pulled out the bags. He sunk his fangs into bag after bag until they were all empty but one. He replaced all the bags into his jacket and made his way to the busy streets. “Forgive me mother but this is the only way I can easy my conscience. If I must be what I am,” Daniel paused and sighed heavily, “Then this is what I must do. I cannot be the vicious killer that you want me to be.” Daniel continued up the street and sat on the park bench. Moments later Cleo joined him her face glowing. He knew that she had fed. “I see you are fulfilled mother.” Daniel said trying to hide his disparagement. “Yes and I see you have done the same. I am so glad that you have finally embraced what you are Daniel.” Cleo cooed her praise. Daniel nodded and they threaded arms as they walked through the streets to enjoy the nightlife. Delylah and Paul awoke to the stewardess’s voice announcing that they had arrived in Paris. Delylah was relieved that Daniel had found other means to sustain his needs. That meant that she didn’t have to kill Cleo after all. She made a vow to herself that she would not do so unless it was a life or death situation. She wanted to try and reason with Cleo and if that didn’t work, if she still insisted that she had to kill Delylah to survive then Cleo would have to be put out of her misery. They slowly made their way off the plane and into the busy terminal. They waited for their bags and got a rent-a-car. “Paul,” Delylah said softly, “I don’t want to kill her anymore.” She hung her head to her chest knowing that he wouldn’t be happy with her statement. “What? Why did we come all this way then?” Paul knitted his eyebrows together in bewilderment. “I…,” She stopped and turned to look at him, “I need to set things right with her Paul or at least try to. I have had a lot of insight to her past in my dreams lately. If I went through what she did I would probably be the same way she is.” Delylah confessed. Paul sighed heavily, “I will be by your side through all of this Del. But if she tries to hurt you I can’t promise that I wont kill her.” Paul looked at her, his eyes filled with fortitude. “I understand Paul but I have to try.” She swung her eyes away from him to the window. “I need to put an end to her misery. If killing her is the only way to do it then I will be the one to kill her.” Paul nodded, “What about Daniel?” A small smile formed on her lips, “Daniel will be fine. He has made peace with who he is.” “How do you know that?” Paul asked his voice raising an octave indicating his confusion. “I just do Paul.” They were silent for the rest of the ride to their hotel. When they entered the lobby they were shocked to see Delylah’s mother sitting there waiting for them. Delylah rushed to her, anger and puzzlement distorting her face. “What are you doing here mother?” Delylah demanded as she seized her mother’s shoulders. “I.. I couldn’t miss your wedding dear.” Nebt-Het said quickly. “I told you to stay home until I sent for you.” Delylah said as she began to rub her temples. “I just wanted to be near you. I can’t stay at your house. She is always there.” Nebt-Het’s voice quivered with fear. “She has gotten into your head too hasn’t she?” Delylah questioned. Nebt-Het was silent. Her silence made Paul and Delylah both worry more about her mental stability. “It’s ok mom,” Paul said as he helped her to her feet, “We will get adjoining rooms so you can be near us.” Nebt-Het smiled and placed a hand on Paul’s cheek, “Now I know why my daughter loves you so much.” Paul’s cheeks took on a pink hue after her comment. He smiled and turned to go check in leaving them to sort all of this out. “I guess I did need some help in planning this thing out.” Delylah looked to her mother and smiled. “Great! First things first, you need a dress. A dress so wonderful you look like the princess you are.” Nebt-Het clapped her hands together in excitement. Delylah just smiled at her and nodded, “We will go do that as soon as we are all settled in our rooms mother.” Nebt-Het nodded in agreement. Delylah stood there looking at her mother and couldn’t help but smile. Even after all that they had been through she was still a woman that was tough as the situation called for. Delylah knew it would be a losing battle if she tried to force her mother to go back home. She just hoped that Cleo didn’t find her mother before she and Paul found her.
