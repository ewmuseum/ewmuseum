The One With The Fox

6 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/6/08 START 10:05 am To kickoff his winning ways after NC-17, Clyde Fox, the Pokemon phenomenon finds himself visiting various places throughout the country to meet face-to-face with his adoring fans. Clyde is so joyful that he won at NC-17, that he wants to give back to all of you! Unfortunately Clyde was in control of booking tour stops so we catch up with the kid in an old age home. Many retired elders pass the days here with their repetitive schedules. Knowing this, Clyde has promised to brighten up their day! Yay! Follow along now! It is a bright morning at the “Fun Fun Home for Classic Oldies.” The birds are chirping, the snow is even melting. Inside the big brown building we find many elderly folks, sitting in the common area, surrounding a huge flat screen television. A man dressed in a classy black suit enters the room from beside the television. He drags a podium in from the other room and proceeds to place it in front of the TV. The elders groan as the man grasps the podium and addresses the restless crowd. Man: Hi my name is Dick Blakenburg folks. Elder Pokey: You’re blocking our view! Blackenburg: Oh am I folks? Blackenburg turns and sees the TV behind him and then proceeds to laugh sarcastically. Blackenburg: I know folks, I’m sorry. I’ll let you get back to watching Sesame Street in a minute? Elder Patches: I’M GOING TO MISS THE LETTER OF THE DAY! Blackenburg: Well it’s no big deal because I’ve seen this one already. The letter of the day is S? for sit down and shut up. The groaning temporarily stops as Blackenburg grabs everyone’s attention. Blackenburg: Ladies and gentleman, I present to you, a man who walked through Houston, Texas and resisted the fried chicken? MISTER CLYDE E. FOX! Big round of applause! No one claps as Clyde Fox enters the room with a presidential smile slapped on his face. Clyde takes the podium after a “hug-shake” with Mr. Blackenburg. Of course Clyde wouldn’t be caught dead without his companions; Squirtle and Pikachu, who he places on each side of the podium. Fox: Thank you, thank you! Take a seat everyone! They were already seated. Fox: And you too sir, you can sit down! Fox tells a wheelchair ridden man. Fox: That’s hilarious right there! Blackenburg: Bu-dum-ching! Blackenburg chuckles. Fox: This is my associate coordinator representative co-operator; Mister Dick Black-Iceburg. He’s really a homeless guy folks lol. Blackenburg: Bu-dum ching! Fox: Oh stop it Dicky! We all know you are a bum that I nabbed off the street, dressed in a suit and only promised you a Canadian Loonie! That’s like what now? One thousand dollars U.S.” So keep quiet if you want your booze! Some of the elders laugh. Fox: Alright folks. I’m Clyde and I’m here to help you and to thank you. I’m here to thank you because you all supported me through my cause at NC-17! I won-ded! I beat my brother fair and circular, one, two, three. So thank you! And now I will repay you by helping you. I put a poll up on myspace about who supports me the most and the category of elderly people won by an astounding 75%! So here I am, to thank you for all we have done. Elder Boris: What do you have for us? Fox: I have donated a portion of my earnings to the Humane Society! Waiting for a reaction, Clyde thrusts his arms out and grins embracing the upcoming cheers? Silence. Fox: Just hear me out! I like Pokemon right? So I thought if the elders support me the mostest then they must like Pokemon too! But then I thought how old people don’t have money, especially if they live in a dump like this. So I booked time here to tell you what I did! I donated to the Humane Society so they can distribute Nintendo products, like Pokemon to all of you! The disadvantaged old person can become a Pokemon Master! An elderly lady stands up from the crowd. Elder Olga: You dumb runt! The Humane Society is for animals! Fox: It’s what? Elder Olga: FOR ANIMALS YOU DUMMY! The lady collapses only to be dragged out of the room by some paramedics. Clyde turns to Dick and expresses his thoughts. Fox: Is that true? But I thought the word Humane meant for Humans! Blackenburg: Could have fooled me! I was the one who deposited the money! Come to think of it, it makes sense why the lady was looking at me funny. Fox: Well it’s not your fault little homeless man. I thought it meant Human too! Oh well, looks like many unwanted pets will pass the time with brand new Nintendo DS? and instead of Pokemon, they should get? Nintendogs! Get it! Nintendogs for dogs! L M A O! That’s right, I said laugh my ass off in acronym form! With the mob of old people angry, Elder Scottsdale and Elder Peggy start swinging their canes nearly hitting Elder Scooby. Elder Oscar: Dang-nabbit! I want my show back on right now! Oscar’s dentures slip out of his mouth as he smacks his fist against the chairs arm. Blackenburg: I think we should give them what they want Clyde! Get out of their way before they start to defecate themselves! Elder Ingrid: TOOOOOOOOO LATE! Clyde and Dick scramble to move the podium out of the way before they get sprayed with the deadly smell of old person defecation. Fox: Now I know what it feels like to be Celine Dion! She actually married one of these people! Blackenburg: Don’t think, just run Clyde! They manage to get the podium out just in time before Elder Beefy let his bowels go. And trust us, his name says it all. With Clyde and Dick out of the way, the elderly people calm down and continue to watch their show. With the credits of Sesame Street rolling, Elder Larson picks the remote up? struggles with it? still struggles with it? and then finally changes it to Friends. God can someone please teach old people how to use electronics? And while you’re at it God, could you give me a better life than subjecting me to narrate Clyde’s adventures? he’s annoying and he always leaves the lid up! Anyways? The elders shimmer down and begin watching the episode of Friends. Clyde decides to chime in and interrupt. Fox: Oh! I’ve seen this one! It’s the one with the Fox! Elder Ingrid: No you idiot! It’s the one with the Chicken Pox! Fox: Oh I knew that! Boy? you old people sure know a lot about television. Wait! Television!” Clyde flips out his Gizmondo and checks the next GCW WorldWide card. He sees that he is booked in a GCW TV Title match against Tyson DeBough. Fox: Oh my God! These old people must have psychic powers! Either that or we are linked in some sick and twisted way! Blackenburg: What’s up boss? Fox: Don’t you see? I am booked in a TV title match at the next WorldWide! I almost forgot but these balding beauties reminded me! Blackenburg: They also like TV. Fox: I know! This must mean that they want me to become the next Television Champion! Then I won’t need podiums or anything? CUZ I WILL OWN TV! Picture it Dicky? I would own every channel on every network! My ratings would be amazing because I’d own television! Furthermore, I’d own this television! Fox points to the one playing Friends. Fox: Is that what you want me to become old people? Your Television Champion? Well guess what!” I’m off to fulfill our destinies! C’mon Blacky? I sure could use a Dick by my side! Clyde grabs Dick by the wrist and pulls him away. Blackenburg: Is that a Gizmondo that you have? Fox: Yeah it’s totally cool! I can connect with THE other Gizmondo in circulation. Blackenburg: There’s only one other one? Fox: Yeah it totally choked on the market after I bought one. Blackenburg: Figures. The scene ends with the old people watching Friends. Elder Ingrid: Did that young man say he was going to win the Television! Elder Olga: I sure hope not Inny. Cuz if he does I don’t want to watch anymore television. Elder Larson: Yeah! That guy will go so drunk with power that he’ll think he owns TV. And everybody knows who owns TV! Elder Smith: Old people? Elder Larson: No? The Monopoly Man. Elder Smith: HE SCARES ME! I HATE HIS MUSTACHE! END
