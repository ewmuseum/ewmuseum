Total Eclipse of the heart

19 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19-12-05 Mary never seemed to find friends the conventional way and for the most part that was how she liked it. She never just “saw people.” She saw into them, she saw “of” them and that in turn triggered her ability to make an instinctual decision. The trouble with instinct though, is it’s just that. Instinct. Its not logical, you’re thinking with every part of you “but” your head. For the most part, its great, an advancement over the corrosion of the evolution of the human brain. On the other hand, nothing is really as it seems and what you want is not always what you should have. Mary had been at the institute for around eighteen months now. She’d made friends here and there but the one friend she truly cared about, the one she’d fallen in love with had been taken away from her just like her parents had. Well not in the exact same way. Her parents had been killed in a car crash when she was five. Skye had been relocated to a less secure unit on the request of her father and his wallet but both incidents hurt the same way. Leaving Mary screaming why? She’d withdrawn again within the 18 months that followed. Nothing really seemed to matter to her accept for drugs and razor blade edges. Mary’d had her stomach pumped six times in the last 12 months alone and the only reason it hadn’t been more was because the staff hadn’t always given a shit about her lying on her bedroom floor convulsing, foaming at the mouth and soiling herself. It was a maximum security unit, they just couldn’t figure out how Mary was constantly getting into the pharmacy. There were always ways and means especially when a third of the staff were male. By the time Mary would come to be leaving the institute, she would have ingested, inhaled and injected almost every chemical in the pharmacy and a few street ones too. “The Grand Slam.” Mary sat on her bed after finally being let out of solitary in the high risk wing. They’d kept her locked up and drugged up, tied to a bed for 6 days and she would have said it was unbearable but it wasn’t. Every night for 4 nights, her mysterious new friend, Scar, had come to visit her. He’d given her back the one thing she craved to be in control of and feel. Her ability to feel pain and, in turn, pleasure at the experience and release. It was a very intimate thing for a stranger to do for another person. But everyone in institute knew why the pain was so important, why they craved to have control over it. Because that control had always been in the hands of someone who didn’t care about them before. It was midday just before lunch and things were quiet. The staff were busy rounding up the bulimics. The sun was mild and high in the sky. Something caught Mary’s eye from her window that looked over the courtyard. She saw a leg stretched out to her far right. She couldn’t see the rest of the body as it was obscured by the trunk of a large tree. She recognized the leg though. It was covered in faded, torn dark grey denim. The foot adorned by a worn sneaker. Something inside Mary compelled her to go and at least get a decent look at him. She pulled on the nearest pair of cut offs she had, over a pair of knee high socks with holds cut into them. She adjusted the miffy slouch sweater she had on and skipped out of her room towards the yard in fuzzy bunny slippers. Getting closer, her skipping sped up until she halted beside Scar leaned against the tree. “Holaa babyboy.” She became slightly self conscious as he looked up at her squinting through his black scraggy hair with the sun in his eyes. “So they let you out then?” He didn’t seem to really care about the answer. It seemed more statement than question. He wasn’t smiling at her but he stared at her so intensely, like he had done the first night in the quiet room. It was such an intimate look as if he could either worship or murder her. Hell maybe both. You were never really sure when Scar was concerned. He gazed at her hard, taking in every inch of her from heat to toe without a single breath drawn while he did it. Mary stood fixed to the spot. He’d asked her a question, she was sure he did. “Uh, yea sure thing dingaling. Mary saw ya foot hanging out over here all by ya lonesome so she figured she’d come say hey and hey so err, yea thanks for helping her and all that jazz, like but without the flashy fingers, unless you want the fingers, Mary ain’t saying you like that she just recommends them if you are like if that’s something you wanna err explore and she well err’she’s gonna shut up now.” Mary’s caramel sun kissed cheeks were adopting a pinkish tint and she looked away for hear of looking directly at Scar. But scar didn’t laugh at her, he didn’t look at her like she was weird or strange. He looked at her like she was the most beautiful, amazing thing he had ever seen. Like she was under a microscope and he’d just discovered her. He had a blade of grass between his fingers playing with. Gently stripping it thinner and thinner until he had to pluck a new blade and start again. He looked away from Mary and back down at what he was doing as if she “d never really been there. “Dint manage to burrow their way to you then?” He said it like a passing comment more than a question again. “Say huh?” He looked up again. “The rumours, the stories, the lies!” Mary sat down cross-legged in front of Scar and looked confused. “Cant says she has. Why” Peoples been talking bouts you?” Scar pushed his sleeves up to his elbows on his tattered black sweater revealing old scars and burns. His forearms looked stronger than his seemingly wiry frame would dictate. “Seems everybody got something to say about everybody else in here.” He looked up at Mary. “They aint told you to stay away from me?” Marys brow wrinkled. “who” The staff or the kiddies?” Scar almost smiled, one eyebrow raised then fell. He plucked a new blade of grass. “Both.” Mary smiled this time. “Aww you not so bad.” Scars face went still. “Oh but I am Momma. I’m just not bad to you. Yet. I could never be bad to you. I wish I coulda seen you destroy that sick fuck!” Scars left had was balled into a fist so tight his knuckles went pale. Mary’s eyes widened in surprise and a little defensiveness. She rolled up on to her knees then settled back down. “Who told you bout that?” Scar looked away bored-like again. Expressionless. “Ah told you. People talk. Told me to stay away from you too. One of the reasons I came to see you for myself.” Mary glanced around to see if anyone was watching. She lowered her voice slightly “You did what Mary?” Scar raised his knees to rest his elbows on them. “Not entirely, no. Momma left when I was a baby. Mah pa , well he whuddent much of a pa at all. Used to rent me out to his friends when I was a kid.” “You made him go away?” “Wish I could, wish I did. Dint get the chance though. He OD’d when I was 12. S’how I ended up in care. The kids would talk in the orphanage too. Made fun of me, pushed me. So one day I pushed back. Two of them boys need 24 hour care now. Heard one died last year.” Scar shrugged nonchalantly. “Some people just don’t like life.” Mary wanted to reach out and just touch scar. To comfort him, but she stopped herself. “Mary’s sorry they hurt you.” “Me too Momma, me too.” Scar rolled onto his knees then raised so he was stooped before Mary balanced perfectly on the balls of his feet. “That’s why we oughtn’t be hanging round each other.” He draped his arms over each knee and plucked another blade of grass from between his feet. Mary couldn’t take her eyes off him once he was staring straight at her. His face was serious again. “Why not?” Her voice had lowered slightly and she coughed to clear her throat. Scar leaned in closer lowering his voice too. “Cus this whole place we livin in, itsa powder keg Momma, and we givin off sparks.” Mary smiled, “Bonnie Tyler?” Scar nodded and smiled and tiny wry half smile. “Knew you’d know that one.” “MR HUNTER!” A voice called out from across the yard but Mary barely heard it. Scar hadn’t taken his eyes off her nor she him. But she heard him curse under his breath. He rocked forward toward her slightly and used one hand to brace against the ground between them. The other hand he placed over Mary’s and lowered his voice, barely a whisper now. “Together we’re fire Momma. Together, we could burn this place down to the ground.” With that he leaned into her and kissed her softly. It was barely a kiss at all. Not probing or eager, just a gently touch of lips and when Mary opened her eyes again, he was gone. “Woah!” She managed to get out as she looked down at her hand. The one he’d held, had a blade of grass in it but he’d woven it into a small heart somehow. Mary stood up absently brushing the fallen leaves from her butt. Her face went sad and her solem for a split second before turning into a devilish smile. She looked up above her and winked. “Sorry Skye blue. Mary’ll always love you forever and ever and ever till she dies and dies but she thinks she likes the Scar too. He moves her monkey chunks directly.” She giggled and skipped off back to her room.
