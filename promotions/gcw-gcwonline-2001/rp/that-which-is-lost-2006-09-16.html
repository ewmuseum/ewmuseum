That which is lost..

16 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9.16.06 Thud! THUDTHUD Thud! Every time a foot or fist connected with the suspended Everlast bag she envisioned the face of the man who was trying to steal Theresa away. Two months. Two months of having her life torn apart by investigations, inquiries and a snarky lawyer who thought he had this case in the bag. The day she received the news still burned brightly in her mind, but not nearly as much as the possibility of losing Theresa for good tomorrow. Tomorrow.. The day of reckoning. The day that she would stand before a judge and find out her sentence. Would it be one of joyful reunion with a child she had grown to love? THUD! Or would it be the day her heart was ripped out yet again? Tears of preconceived defeat and anger stung at her icy blue eyes as she continued to abuse the bag, letting out as much of her frustration as she could. Drawing a deep breath she held back the tears and steadied the bag as her mind wandered back to that day, that dark day...the day they took Theresa away. ~*~*~*~ Two months earlier: Temporay Custody hearing ' Court is now in session. All rise for the honorable judge Lowell.' A symphony of chairs and feet could be heard as everyone in the courtroom stood and waited patiently for the judge to take his seat. Her heart was racing with anticipation and fear. Surely this judge would see through the careful facade of the man whom claimed to be Theresa's uncle in her adopted family. ' Please be seated.' Eva sat down, hands coming to rest on the desk and fingers fiddled with another in quiet anxiety while the judge looked over the papers carefully placed into a file on his elevated desk. She watched him intently, studying his facial expressions, only dropping her gaze away whenever he would lift his gaze to scan over herself or her opponent. He was an aging man. His grey streaked brown hair was receding. Age-lines were deeply embedded at the edge of his eyes and cheeks. He wore spectacles; she felt he did that for the look more than the necessity. Out of all of that, it was his eyes she studied the most. She wanted to see the man behind the eyes, the true being hidden beneath the bagging skin. What she saw put her at even more dis-ease. The minutes were crawling by like years as she kept shifting her seat, only stopping whenever her lawyer placed an encouraging hand over her own. When, at last, the judge cleared his head to speak her gaze snapped back to his face carefully masked with a calm neutrality. No matter what happened here, she would not lose her temper. ' I have studied the statements from both parties in this temporary custody case involving the child Theresa Snyder. Unlike many child custody cases, this one is unusual as it does not involve a divorce, but rather a biological parent and the relative of the child's adoptive parents. Ms. VanLorne it is my understanding that you gave Theresa up for adoption upon her birth and have had no contact, nor attempted to obtain contact since that day?' Eva swallowed heavily. ' Yes your honor, that is correct.' ' And that you only regained contact after contact due to the death of her legal adoptive parents?' The more he probed, the more her heart sank. ' Yes you honor.' ' I see...' She visibly winced glancing over to her lawyer. He merely shook his head quietly and patted her hands once again hoping to give her reassurance. Once again the judge cleared his throat and looked up to see him removing his glasses. ' After carefully considering the situation I have decided to give temporary custody to Mr. Snyder for the time during the custody hearings. During this time all funds set aside for Theresa will be frozen in accordance with court procedures.Theresa will be handed over to him no later than 8 p. m. this evening. This court is adjourned until Wednesday where the proceedings for Soul legal custody of the child Theresa Snyder will be begin.' Eva hadn't heard anything past the words Mr. Snyder. Her whole world had gone from color to black and white once again. As the judge rose to leave and everyone stood, she remained seated merely staring through the desk as she tried to contain her grief. How was she going to explain to Theresa that she had to leave yet another home? How was she going to explain to a child that none of this was her fault? She didn't know. The sound of celebrating and excited voice drew her stony gaze to the figures of Mr. Snyder and his slimy lawyer. At that moment she wanted to kill a man. Leaping out of her chair she started to step forward but found herself held back by the staying hand of her own knowing lawyer. ' Don't Eva. If you do you will ruin any chances you have of keeping Theresa.' ' What would you know? I have already lost her.' ' No, you have merely had a delay in a happy ending. This isn't over by a long shot. We can still get her back and for good.' Her chin quivered with anger and pent up tears as she looked between her lawyer and the other two actively staring at her. Finally, she brushed his hand away and rushed out the courtroom doors no longer able to stomach being in the place. She had a child at home, with whom she only had a few hours to spend before handing her over to a complete stranger whom she knew only wanted Theresa's trust fund. Thank god the judge had the foresight to freeze Theresa's trust account. It was a bitter victory, but still a victory in the face of a greater loss. ~*~*~*~ THUD THUD THUD THUD WHACK THUD The fury had surfaced again, memories serving to draw it forth as she did her damnedest to destroy that bag. It has been two months to the day that she was forced to watch as Theresa was taken away. Two months since she saw that sad, angry look in the eyes of a twelve year old girl who was ripped from yet another home. That look haunted her every night. ( TBC)
