A beginning of destiny...

10 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/10/07 The seasons slowly change from orange to brown. The fall closes slowly into its death throws. The earth begins its journey unto death before it has a rebirth. The deafening white coldness that flows till the spring birth of the new year. The sky burst through into the world below as the suns rays hit the mountain side. A heavy footfall crunches the dried leaves as the figure walks through the lands. The figure towers over most things, but this time he is the lone figure in solitude. The ancient trees creak in the wind as he is dwarfed by their ancient presence. Long black hair flows in the breeze the sun glinting off it like a raven wing. Deep brown eyes take in the view as the lone man continues walking though the woods. The bite of the wind as it flows past him from the north is nothing. The trees sigh with the wind, as though falling gently to sleep and still the man walks, alone, yet not lonely, for he is at home and relaxed in this place. A sudden sound has the man turning. From the underbrush a badger approaches. As he watches the badger looks up at him, hissing and grinning almost. Rising up on its back paws the badger grows, taller wider, claws growing longer. ?Come you and I must have a contest. If you get past me you can go on, if you do not, you must come again?, the now man sized badger hisses. The man only nods and takes his stance. The badger lunges at him. Staying calm the man retaliates grappling with the badger. Hissing and growling, the badger attempts to beat the man, but to no avail. As the man pins him the badger disappears, leaving the man bathed in a bright yellow light. The man stretches, feeling his senses opening up, energy flowing through his body. The man looks around. He is no longer alone. At his side sits a brown dog. One eye blue the other black, and one white spot on its back. The dog stands, looks deep into the man’s eyes and then turns and trots down a path. The dog stops a few steps down and waits. Without a thought the man follows the dog. The path becomes more difficult to keep, brush and vines entangling his feet as he attempts to keep the dog in sight. Each time he is ready to take the easier path, the dog stops in front of it, forcing him down the harder path. He continues on, struggling through the underbrush, the dog leading him along. At the end of the path they enter into a wide open meadow surrounded by some of the oldest trees the man has ever seen. Rich colors of fall crowing each tree in stunning glory. The meadow itself bathed in a golden light. Walking in th golden light he continues his path forward. Seemingly he sees everything and yet nothing at the same time. The world whirls around his feet he golden leaves seemingly are another magical sight in this dying world. Looking at his hands he turns and walks as the world slowly returns to normal as he walks forward. His eyes focus on the mountain ranges in the distance being shadowed by the setting sun. He begins to gather logs and branches in his arms as he walks coming to a clearing with a tent. Sitting down he slowly lays the larger branches into the fire pit. He places some smaller brush underneath in certain areas. He turns and lights a match as it burns the brush underneath. Using a smaller branch he stokes the flames to life as he sits there watching the flames become larger. He then leans back against a rock and raises a knee to place his arms on as he simply watches the flames. "Quietly and expectantly everyone waits for something. I am waiting for dangerous games. I need to do something. There is something unfinished there. I need to grasp at the tougher straws and make my own path. Destiny is a path that needs to be embraced. Oblivion has a path and it stands in front of the Establishments personal goals. What path has destiny followed." Quietly Shaman looks at his hands. "Strength means nothing if i cannot face all of the animal totems before me. Theirs is a destiny and path set by the spirits. I look and study each one and then conquer the tests they lay before me. Each test brings me closer to the over all achievement sought by myself and the peoples long before me. Why are the dog and badger the first to come before me. What tests are yet to come?" Studying the flames his mind reaches deep. He attempts to make sense of the past , present, and future. He looks out towards the setting sun to the west and turns his gaze to the east where the sky is darkening slowly. Knowing the coldness will come with the darkness as the sun slowly sets hiding its warming rays from this dying golden land for 12 hours. Watching the fire he notices nothing as the sky grows darker setting in the far distance is the last vestiges of the sun. He looks up as some of the brighter stars have begun to appear in the early evening sky. He smiles slightly as he had always enjoyed the peacefulness of the dark. The silence before the wind as the only voice and the rustling of the dead and dried earth. Silently he watches the fire which has replaced the warmth as the sun had left. The sky grows darker. A huge shadowed figure stops just outside the fire. Its large form is barely visible. It is a large bear sitting as it speaks. "I will join you by this fire. Accompany me in this conversation. You have been here before on this path. Your journey has been made clear and yet you come before all of us for these tests once again. Will you use muscle, mental capabilities? There are many abilities that man is too blind to observe. The dog led you to me through the toughest path achievable. As he blocked you you considered the alternatives, but chose the tougher path. Which means your character is strong. You defeated the badger without even flinching or batting an eye which means your will and strength is unwavering. Now what tests will be awaiting you?" Raising his eyes Shaman looks at the massive bear his eyes concentrating on the bears own eyes. He knows there is another task coming, but what could it be? What is this new task and what path will it send him down next. The bear stands and strolls off into the forest as it speaks. "Morning will come and at first suns rays hit upon that stone i will return for your next task. Rest well Shaman for you will be tested beyond your imaginations." Shaman leans back against te tree his mind going back to Dangerous Games as he waits knowing his opponents whoever they are are going to face him at his most dangerous. The US Title will be his once again. No more hiding behind the fact that he is one of Oblivion. It is time to show them that he is as dangerous as any of them. Doubters they are and this time the doubt they had will be their undoing. To be continued..
