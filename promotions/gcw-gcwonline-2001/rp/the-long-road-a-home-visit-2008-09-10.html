The Long Road: A Home Visit

10 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10 Sep 08 **sorry this is all I could get done this week, barely had time for this attempt.** “Daaaa!” “Go head ? say it!” “Daaaa!” “You almost got it!” “Daaaaa’Daaaa’Daaadee!” Roberto’s smiles grows wide and his eyes brighten, the icy blues of his seem to glow when he hears his young son speak his first word. Roberto kneels down, reaching his arms out to grab the boy by his waist and lift him up easily. Roberto tosses his son into the air, catching him on his way down causing the little one t o laugh hysterically. The child’s laughter always makes Roberto’s heart melt. “I love you” Roberto says with a hug. “What Mary miss!”” Mary’s voice causes both Roberto and his son to look over in her direction. Typhoid Mary, once one of wrestling’s most dangerous women, stands in the doorway, a towel wrapped around her head and a black robe covering herself. Of course with Mary things aren’t always “covered” because this robe isn’t tied in the front so most of what should be covered is exposed for Roberto to see. “He said it!” Roberto says with a giant grin. “Say Wha?” “Daaaadeee!” Little Javier Roberto Abitua hollers out. Roberto smiles again. “That!” Mary jumps up and down, clapping her hands like an excited child. The movement causing her robe to go open more, showing off everything that god blessed her with. “YAY! Mim-Statty can talk!” “Dad!Dad!Dad!Dad!Dad!Daaaaaad!” Like any child who figures out that adults find something they do hilarious, Javier aims to wear it out as if putting himself on repeat. Mary approaches the two most important men in her life. Her right arm moves to slide around Roberto’s waist and her other lays softly on Javier’s back, lightly running her hand up and down. Mary’s eyes shift to Roberto’s and she moves to her tip toes, puckering her lips. Roberto smirks as he leans down and plants a kiss on her lips, making Mary happy. “I love you babe.” “Mary knows it! She thinks your purrty good too!” Swiftly Mary moves her hand and pinches Roberto on the behind, causing the much bigger man to jump slightly. “Well thanks.” He says with a laugh. Mary jumps and her eyes go wide as if an idea just struck her right on the forehead. “OH! Here’s an idea! Mary thinks you should put the baby to bed for nappy time.” Her voice is very excited and she nods her head in an over exaggerated fashion. “Why is that?” She gives a devilish smile. “So Statty can put Mary to beds next!” Mary grabs Roberto’s front belt loops and pulls him close to her while biting her lower lip. “Yeah?” Roberto asks She nods. “Puh-leeeeze Daaaaady?” “Well alright, since you asked so nicely.” Again she claps and cheers. “Naz-Dee time!” “Wait for me in the bedroom.” Roberto kisses Mary again and walks away with his son, moving towards the young boy’s bedroom. Once they reach Javier’s room, Roberto lays him down in his crib and hands him his favorite Dallas Cowboys blanket and sippy cup. Surprisingly the boy doesn’t fuss because of the forced nap time, probably just as afraid of Daddy as all of his opponents are. Roberto leans down and kisses his son’s forehead and whispers to him. “Sleep tight my son.” “Very touching moment Roberto.” The voice startles Roberto, causing him to spin around quickly with his guard immediately up. Standing in the entrance to his son’s bedroom is his new mentor, Juaquin. Upon seeing the old man Roberto calms some. “How the hell did you get in here?” “Just like everyone else I suppose ? the door.” “Well what is it that you want today” To bash my ankle up again?” “No Roberto. We just need to talk.” “Look Old Man, I appreciate your help but I’ve told you before that our training sessions stay away from this house.” “Yes, yes I know Roberto. However you have much work to do and very little time to do it in. This week you have Shaman and Mayhem and next?” “Look, I have other matters to attend to right now.” “This is important. Dangerous Games is coming up fast, your chance to take over GCW again. Do not let your family get in the way of your dream again.” “Shut the fuck up.” “E-excuse me?” Joaquin looks genuinely surprised. “Don’t think what we do together or my time in the ring is more important than my son and Mary. I’ve lost-? “I know what you’ve lost Roberto.” Roberto hesitates? “Then you should understand.” “Look Roberto,” Joaquin moves into the room, coming face to face with Roberto. “You are at that point where you are right on the fence. A small push one way can lead to glory and riches but one mishap will take you back to the bottom again” this could be your last shot! Make the right damn choice.” Roberto’s eyes burn into Joaquin. “Oh please, you don’t really believe that Shaman and Mayhem are even worth worrying about do you” Even with Murray on my side.” “I believe you’ll be hurt if you aren’t ready. They have a lot to prove.” Footsteps heading towards the baby’s room stops the conversation. “Statty-Bear, Mary is huuuungry.” Her voice calls out right before she moves into the doorway, her smooth brown skin completely uncovered, her naked body looking as toned and beautiful as a goddess. For a quick moment Roberto is hypnotized by her beauty. When he realizes he isn’t alone Roberto goes forward, blocking Joaquin’s view of his Mary. “I’ll be there in a moment dear. Go wait for a little while longer, he doesn’t need to see this.” She laughs and playfully hits Roberto’s chest. “Where Statty think the baby come from” Duuuuh!” Roberto glances back at Juaquin quickly. “Very funny Mary, I’ll be there in a moment.” “Ooootay but don’t be mad if Mary starts without you!” Roberto stares down the hallway as Mary skips back down to their bedroom, stopping to give him a seductive look before disappearing. Roberto groans as he turns back to Joaquin. “Do you see what you’re making me miss?” “I do.” Joaquin smirks. “She’s not Cindy.” Roberto’s entire body tightens, his expressionless face goes pale. He takes in a few deep breaths as he tries to keep his composure. He fights to stay in control of his anger. “I know what you’re trying to do.” “I’m doing nothing.” “You’re trying to push my buttons, piss me off.” “Besides that.” “Go home Joaquin.” Roberto moves to leave “This week will not be an issue.” Joaquin stops Roberto in his tracks. “How can you be so sure?” “Because I’m the fucking greatest. I will not be denied ? fuck Mayhem, Shaman and Murray. They are not LeS ? they aren’t me.” Roberto nods. “Now I’m sure you can find your way out of here.” Roberto moves by Joaquin, leaving him alone in his son’s room. Joaquin stands there for a moment before moving towards the baby’s crib. “Your father is very foolish.” ****************** Joaquin walks slowly through the house, down the stairs and towards the front door of the “Abitua Mansion” as Mary has always called it. He reaches the large doors and pushes them open heading out into the bright sunlit sky. A cool breeze floats around, whipping his long gray hair around. A sound infiltrates, a creaking sound. Joaquin’s head slowly turns and sees a man sitting on what could be called Roberto’s porch. Slowly the man is moving back and forth on an old rocking chair ? “Hello.” The man calls out. “How are you?” “I’m strapped to a fucking rocking chair, how do you think I am?” “Very true ? LeStatt.” LeStatt Knight sits there strapped down, his black, lifeless eyes staring straight ahead. “Things seem to be coming along nicely.” LeStatt Knight says. “It would see that way.” Joaquin moves over to where Knight is seated “Why can’t I just finish this?” “Because old timer, it just isn’t the time.” “When the hell is the right time” How does one figure it’s the time to destroy someone?” “I WILL NOT DESTROY HIM!” Knight roars, his dark glare finding Joaquin. “He will not die ? he will suffer, just the way he has made me suffer.” Knight calms down again ? his eyes shifting straight ahead again before he continues. “Besides,” He smirks. “Who knows what would happen to me if he died.” “Then when do you take over?” “It’s coming ? I’ll know.” “How?” “I just will ? I always know.”
