Telling everyone the truths about Bagwell!!

26 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/26/08 The worlds end must be at its beginning. The ocean lays before you as each wave rolls slowly towards shore. Once you step foot into it you feel its cool to cold embrace. Yet there is something that needs answering. Is y6our destiny amongst the waves or was it in the past where the waves and water constantly pounded against the rocky shore that is your stone resolve. These are slight questions and yet these are question that the large man standing int he waves up to his waste are answering for himself as each day goes by. Shaman is such a man with a multitude of answers and questions within his being and he ponders whether or not that this day will be the one to set him upon his path to destiny. Whether or not to turn and simply walk away or to simply fight harder than before. He slowly places his hands down upon the rolling waves as if pushing himself up upon them. He smiles as his hands leave a trail of ripples in the rolling waves before the crash behind him. He stares out at the ocean. He speaks as he watches the waves. "The world beyond is a place of intricate mysteries. I face a new mystery within myself each and everyday i step forward. Chris Bagwell muses over what he is and what he has yet to become. I am not going to end his career i am going to make an example out of him. Like a warrior facing a mighty dragon. The sword will be raised, but if he is not prepared for the fight he will drown in the blazing fire of the fury of his opponent." Diving forward Shaman immerses himself in the water as he stands back up his wet hair cascading over his larger muscular form. The sun shimmers off the water beads as he runs his hand through his hair pulling it back behind his shoulders. "Refreshing is the ocean. Cooling the inner rage which consumes the soul when all you do is battle. Bagwell forgets that this battle isn't just merely him getting over with the group of people to fight." He gazes as a low mist is over the water. The thin misty vapors circle around the water as he watches it carefully. Eyes not squinting or ever changing just the view of the water keeps him intoxicated within natures grasp. "Under those waves and calm waters is a world where each creature fights with its own inner chaos for survival. Each fish fighting off a larger fish. Each plant surviving as an amoeba like creature eats away at it. In the end the cycle repeats over and over." He turns and walks towards the beach as he exits the water he is wearing swim trunks to his knees with tribal symbols on it. He turns and leans down and picks up a towel near a chair and begins wiping off the beads of water. His hands slowly drawing each towel stroke carefully. His eyes turn towards the sun which is slowly setting in the west. he turns and walks up the beach towards the sunset. His eyes focused on the horizon. Blurred by the brightness and the unknown of where it is taking him. "Mayhem, Bagwell, even the boss Caldera. Yet in the end i still stand strong win or lose. Firm and yet flexible when i bend so do others. I bend to break someone else. I am a fuming bottle of rage. Awaiting to be released to crush my opponent like a tin can beneath the wheels of pain. Have you ever know me to back away from a challenge?" Stopping Shaman kneels down and picks up some sand and lets it flow through his fingers. "The future and past are like grains of sand. Like AC/DC once said Are ya ready for a good time? Welcome to the night life! It is time to livin up the party and take the road less traveled one traveled by me. The Duke of pain, The kicker of puppies because they are cute, The one who will make you bleed!" Shaman laughs deeply as he brushes his hands free of the sand. "So Bagwell thinks musing on things will give him the edge. truth be told i am going to enjoy beating him around. If he bleeds that's a bonus. If he leaves on a stretcher that is an extra bonus. Is there something i must remind you of? I don't give a flying damn about you and your plans. I am the plan breaker." He turns and flips his towel over his shoulder and walks towards a Harley Davidson motorcycle sitting at the edge of the sand in a parking lot. He turns and looks back over the beach. "Bagwell when you realize there are ten people just like you waiting to fill your shoes. I can take everything away from you because i can. I have seen hundreds like you and they have all failed. In the end your failure just simply means that someone else succeeds." Sitting on the Harley he slips on a T Shirt. Looking about he smiles and grins. He leans back on his Harley and watches the waves crash as the sun slowly slips down below the horizon. He tilts his head slightly as the sun flashes a blood red color across the horizon low. He turns his gaze back towards the East as Darkness slowly creeps westward. "Much like the path someone chooses to come after me. One forgets i am not interested in mere games. I am the one who creates them. I am the one who finishes them. I am the one who breaks the game when i lose. There is one damn constant me." Shaman starts his Harley and backs it up as he turns it around looking around. "I used to like this place in the past. Filth ever changing town. They got greedy and the town is suffering from it. Follow me and i will give you comparisons." Shaman roars down the street as the camera crew hurries to follow him. They all hop into a jeep and hurry down the street to catch up with him a few blocks away as Shaman is stopped at a four way intersection with a small town square with a fountain in the center. He rolls his Harley to the square and grins as he points at the round about there. He points to the left and the right as he speaks. "On the right you see an arcade and small amusement park type of deal. An old carousel sits in the back over there. Then you turns to your right and notice that there is high rise condos. Once there stood another small amusement park deal and batting cages. There was a grand arcade there with laughing children and fun. Now it has turned to something which hopes to make money and turn the beach and its outlook to filth and grime. The rich come here now to flaunt the pier that is situated in the center here. That pier has stood for over 100 years and it may not be as long and as majestic as it once was, but it still has some of its old charm." Turning around he looks up the street a hill with a wide main street. On both sides plenty of parking and four lanes. "That is main street also known as the drag. Teenagers used to come rolling down that street with music blasting and showing off too women and girls. They just wanted to get noticed and show off. I was younger i had run down this street. It was fun. Come down that hill and let off the clutch as your Harley rumbled. To the left and right of me are East and West Grand Avenue. One road runs to Scarborough and the other runs to Camp Ellis. Now where am i going with this? It is simple really follow me." Shaman rides the Harley slowly around the square on a bricked circle and then pulls the Harley up onto the pier and kicks the stand down. Leaning the Harley over he stands up and walks out onto the pier and looks out over the ocean nearby. "GCW is like an ocean. Vast talent ever flowing in and out of her. Maybe like a bad hooker too, but that isn't my comparison today. Maybe i'll do that later for those who are less than understanding in simple terms. In Bagwells case i'll get him a helmet and a room full of bouncing balls. Truth is GCW always gets these guys who wish to rise to stardom fast an easy. Where has Bagwell once earned the run to the top rather than run his mouth? Is he working on the old nob job under Caldera's desk? Most likely i mean hell how come Caldera is looking the other way when Bagwell continues his ignorant rants and trying attitudes around the ring. He runs his mouth like man in the bathroom four hours after a Chinese restaurant visit. Just doesn't make any sense. Caldera used to be a man of power and yet he has some punk showing him up. Guess it just goes to show when you get the swallow the leader treatment anything goes." Shaman smirks and looks around. He narrows his eyes and lifts his hand and points down the beach at a barely visible rock formation. "The door to GCW is like that rock there. It is always there and yet only room enough for so many egos and personalities. Bagwell is one such personality who is fighting for acceptance like a child in a kindergarten class. He wants to stand out and he cries for attention with his little tirades and acting tough. Yet he is still below anyone's notice and mostly ignored. I have been there Bagwell and done it all. I never strive for attention and in the end i became known by hard work and my own hands. How did you get to where you are now? By being a little bitch and never knowing when to shut your mouth and do what everyone else around you has done. Work hard and earn what they deserve. No Bagwell this time you won't have a damn cake walk. You are going to learn a very valuable lesson in the ring with me." Shaman turns around and grins as he leans against the piers railing. "You have a problem Bagwell. You cannot do anything on our own. You need every Tom, Dick, and Harry to make you look good. You beg and plead with people to make a movement and then use their fame and not your own to move forward. I took Taylor from you and showed him he had a path all his own. Now look at him he has established himself well and showed he is worthy. You can't even beat your rival. Your anti thesis arch rival or what the hell ever you wanna call it." Shaman turns and begins walking down the pier towards the end where a bar stands. He smiles and walks in quietly to the bar. Sitting down at a stool he smiles and motions for a shot of tequila and a beer. "The more things get roughed up Bagwell the more people are going to understand you. You are not understanding and your fake. You hide behind others and when it comes time to stand on your own you rely on those others to boost your ego. You beat Bryan Mayhem a man who wasn't at the top of his game. He was confused and lost and you could not understand the direction he was going. Now you are going to be facing me and this time around your going to get hurt. Your forgetting one little detail Bagwell i am Bigger, Meaner, and Nastier than you are. The Native American Bad Ass is going to show you why he is known as one of the most sadistic son of a bitches in the ring. You wanted the fight and announced it round the world your going to beat me at my game." Shaman grins and smiles as he drinks the beer down and turns the bottle sideways and smashes it over his own head and grins. "How can you win a game when you don't even know what the game is? Truth is Bagwell you can't predict what i am going to do. So you better pack a lunch get a permission slip from mommy and tell them your going to be late because your going to get beat within and inch of your useless life." Shaman turns to the bar and drinks the shot of tequila down and grins as he looks towards the window ignoring the camera finally.
