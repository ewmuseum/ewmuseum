Checking In

15 December 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/15/11 <><><><><> “Another Pabst Blue Ribbon,” he stops to sip. “Miss Balls?” We find our hero, Daniel J. Francisco, assuming a familiar role; naked, wearing sunglasses, enjoying a frothy PBR, and looking dramatically through a stained-glass window somewhere in the pit of sin known simply as Las Vegas. The man best known in GCW circles as Dynasty takes this silence as ignorance and tosses her the ’Oh, hey, go ahead, have one of my mega tasty beerskis, skank!’ gesture (nudging his head, repeatedly, in the general direction of the beers while leering at her with wide eyes). “Walls,” Karen states. “Karen Walls.” “Ohhhhhh,” Dan pauses, trying to lock down somewhere in that cramped brain of his, before forgetting what it was he was doing. “Sorry, Miss Walls. I think I’m gonna call you Balls though, if you’re cool with that?- Karen shudders, “Please don’t.” Dan usually disregards most women’s comments, especially right when they are made, and affords Karen no exception to the rule this go ‘round; “You know how it is with celebrities, right, Balls!? Priorities! Yeesh. Smoke a joint?” “What? No thanks,” her brow furrows curiously, “Priorities? And you’re not a celebr–” “One, two, three! put the birdy in the tree,” Dan shouts whilst holding in a good-sized whack off this marijuana stick, and is clearly on a different page (perhaps planet!) than this fine looking piece of flesh five feet from him. “So what brings you around here? Did you hear about the pirate king thing? Pretty hot, right?” “What pirate king thing?” her look is one of cluelessness. “I’m your...” Karen stops herself, knowing damn well that if she even utters the term, Reporter, or Interviewer, or BABYSITTER, all he’ll hear, and all he’ll think is “Slut?” Now, when Danny says this, he doesn’t really say it. He more or less suggests it with his tone. That and by bending one eyebrow up and one eyebrow down while gazing toward Karen and mouthing the words to Boys II Men’s “I’ll Make Love to You.” If there’s one thing Dan Francisco knows to use when seducing a woman, it’s Boyz II Men, and the knockout punch – eyebrow dexterity. He reflects to himself, She fuckin’ loves it! “Are you okay?” Ms. Walls is once again on the brink of confusion. “Can you speak up? And put some clothes on? I think you need to go take care of that.” Danny checks out his Scene Down South with a proud grin. His response is to simply make eye contact, take a long, soothing drink from his beer, and let her soak it in. Seconds later, a knock comes to the door. In walks an old woman with a grey ponytail, wearing blue-tinted glasses, and carrying a rather large painting stencil and canvas. The painter sets up and looks at Danny with wide eyes. She turns and smiles politely at Karen, “This is going to be a great painting. I was thinking simple but… are you here to keep him hard?-“ Karen is stifled, barely capable of comprehending what she meant at first, and turns her furious glare to Danny. His braggadocios chuckle echoes through the Vegas wedding chapel chambers as he turns a hearty thumbs’ up to the elder painting his portrait for two hundred bananas an hour. With a smile, Danny sips his beer, “What is your name elder?” “Gloria,” she says, again with a warm and polite smile. “Old Gloria!” Dan declares her, instantly, before anyone else can name her. “I am King Francisco, or King Dynasty, or Your Majesty. Pretty much anything that says I’m your one and true King, is perfect. This is my slut, Ballsy. Strange combo but hey, who am I to complain? Say hi, Balls.” Karen tries to hide the embarrassment with her hands. Gloria simply smiles at her and nods politely before pulling out her paints. Danny slams the rest of his beer. He dramatically pulls the empty can from his mouth and spikes the can on the ground, stomping on it, and proceeding to do a bad rendition of the Dirty Bird touchdown dance around the can, trash-talking it all the way to Tuscaloosa. He stops and notices how empty his hands are. His attention is next brought to how thirsty his mouth is! The Buffalo Bad Ass becomes enraged, drunk step-father style, and unleashes his outdoor voice… indoors. “SLUT! BEER ME.” <><><><><> “So tell me Karen,” Danny cracks open another beer. “What’s your mission? Injure me, so I can’t compete, or sleep with me, wasting away my world-class endurance? How much did they pay you?” She rolls her eyes while mustering up enough sarcasm to laugh, mockingly, and takes a look over Gloria’s handiwork. Somehow, someway, this little old hippie lady was turning in a pretty remarkable painting so far. Karen’s eyes wander from the sweeping strokes that make up his hair to the chiseled abdomen and then slightly lower to the “Balls,” Dan snaps his fingers. “I like my questions to come with answers, comprende?” “You don’t use email, phone, or your ears much,” he tone is cold and sincere. “do you?” His blank stare answers her without words. “I told you this about an hour ago. I’m a writer with GCW,” she begs him to retain that much. “We’re doing a piece on your comeback.” “BLASPHEME!” his voice echoes off the drive-thru chapel’s walls. “Stand still, junior,” Gloria shouts, peering from around the edge of the canvas. Dan returns to form, “Apologies, old wise one. But listen, Ballsasgna (pronounced balls-on-ya), comebacks are for the weak. I was an HDTV Title contender before my rise to the pirate throne– I mean, before my unfortunate abduction, and I was destined for much, much more. If people think it’s going to take me longer than a Beijing blowjob to get back to top form, they’ve never gotten a blowjob in Beijing. Superfast. And awesome.” Karen jots down a few things on her notepad but mainly maintains eye contact. Dan flexes his bicep, mostly to reassure himself that he’s still fuckin’ smooth, bro, and gets the feeling someone is watching, and someone is. “What’s up old lady?” he blows a kiss to Gloria. “Good God,” Karen groans. “So you’ve been out of the ring for four months and you aren’t worried about how the game, and GCW, has changed in that time?” “What really could change Balls?” he slowly directs his focus to Karen. “I know Zenith is in charge, I know Renegade Rich Rollins is the champ-” “Not true,” Karen slips in. “That would be Triumph Frost. Rollins is gone.” “Frost eh?” Dan ponders the time they squared off and he got his bell rung real good’n stuff. “Pussy. Well if that’s the little kitty I have to smash with a brick, then that’s the little kitty I have to smash with a brick. Thanks for the info-“ Karen tries to end it, ”Please stop calling me” “-Balls. You’re the best,” Dan’s smile and nod are as sincere as a wrestler ten PBR’s deep can make them. “PBR?” Again, she rejects his offer, and again, he marks this down as a serious negative. What kind of broad don’t take the beer that’s given to her!? Priss. Dan peers out the window, intent on coming up with a smart, dazzling question to overcome the awkward moment caused by his nudity. It hits him, “Anybody to watch out for?” “Well, I’d say everybody, given the fact you haven’t wrestled a match in four months!,” her fight with his fantasy world continues. “Well then that’s that,” he drops down, performing picture-perfect push-ups, and maintaining ninja-like silence the whole time. “With GCW free of any potential threats, I can re-establish myself as the top player and put all those rumors to rest that I’m just another pretender. You know what I mean, Balls?” “I don’t know if you can’t hear me or…” Karen waits for some logical reason to pass through her brain. “Or you’re just… just toying with me, but… you really, reeeeally need to listen to me.” Danny looks at her with an impatient, mocking expression on his face. She figures her time is limited before his wiseass rationale railroads the conversation again. Gloria seems to be loving life, painting away a masterpiece at an impressive pace. “You’re fucked,” Karen says. ”Looooook at Balls oooover heeeeere!” Danny says, breaking his pose, but returning just before Gloria scolds him. “Watch your mouth, slut. There’s a lady present.” Karen chuckles at her word choice, “I just mean, if you’re serious about returning to the ring, you’re going to need to get ready. Physically. Mentally. You need to be ready. I know my job is just to write down what you say but, I’ve seen these guys nearly kill each other.” “Can we take five, Wise One?” he interrupts. “My neck is beginning to stiffen up. Relax, Balls, I’m not talkin’ about Little Megatron.” Danny reaches for his Marlboros as he eyes Karen; “You think I don’t know that, kid? I’m no stranger to this gig. In fact, I set the temp on this dance floor. When I say waltz, we goes slow, and when I say salsa, we get en fuego up in here!” The first drag of the smoke seems to quell the fire rising inside Danny. His eyes close, his nostrils flare, and he lets out the first plume of smoke slowly. It is at this moment he realizes that he is smoking inside the chapel. He shrugs, unsure if this can really be considered a ‘House of God,’ before flicking the ashes on the red carpet. Dan quickly takes another drag, “I think I was ready before, I’m ready now, and I’m just warming up. The last thing you want to see is GCW without Dynasty. You won’t have a job, Yale and Bryan won’t have jobs, and it’s very likely Shaman wouldn’t have a job… mainly because I heard monster actors are in surplus these days. I’ll prove it when I get to the High Spirits Casino. They call it High Stakes and think I’m NOT going to show up? Fail.” “What do you think you can expect?” Karen jots down some notes, again. Fuckin’ nerd. “Zenith will likely have you on the more dangerous side of things, considering his history with hating douchebags and all.” “I hope you know I’m just using you. The network suits wanted me to be on screen with a smaller, less-attractive person. Got that? Smaller. Faaaaarrrr less attractive.” <><><><><> Karen runs her fingers through her hair after a long day of listening to a severely deluded meathead. The simple sensation of her long nails running over her scalp soothes her to the point of normalcy. She shuts her eyes, allowing herself to be swept away to a tropical island under the ocean breeze, and calms her nerves. “Over here, Balls,” Dan says, ripping her world apart. “We need ya. How’s this look?” When Karen opens her eyes, she sees Dynasty, still very naked, standing on top of the organ, and making rock ‘n roll gestures. She fights the urge to kill him and plays the indifferent role. “Cute,” she tries to subside his need for attention for ten minutes. “But tell me, Danny, what happens at High Stakes if you win and walk into a very lucrative opportunity? There’s a lot of risk, reward scenarios out there in this one, so… what’s your take on the set up?” “First of all,” his smile broadens. “The set up is perfect. High Stakes, at Shaman’s casino, with quite possibly the most surprising card on the march toward the infamous Dangerous Games. The only question I think I have for myself is… what won’t I do on my first night back?” Karen rolls her eyes, sick of the self-promotion, and groans . She notices the end of the portrait is coming up, and it is quite possibly the best painting she has seen in a very long time. Her first reaction is to congratulate Gloria but then sees Dan, in all his glory, and wonders if she can get more out of him. “If you had a choice, what your match would be,” her eyes lock with his. “What would it be?” The last thing she should have done is provoke the now-drunk, soon-to-be wrestler into thinking he had his choice of opponents. His eyes narrow, a strong smile slowly spans from ear to ear, and he aims an index finger of certainty at the lovely blond. “You know what they say about unfinished business, right sweetheart?” he takes down the last of his current beer. “It ain’t over… ‘til it’s over. I guess you could say I was this close to winning that American Dream match at Madison Square Garden. You could also say I wouldn’t mind checkin’ that one off the list.” “Aaaand…” Gloria stands up. “Done.” “Bitchin’,” he says, shuffling across the room, and eyes his latest oversized painting of himself to the collection. “You’re a true pro, Gloria. Check it out, Balls. Pretty sweet, right?” Dan holds out the painting for Karen to see. Her eyes widen at the sight of Dan, bareback, holding a scepter with a bright yellow stone on the head. His eyes are under sunglasses, and his foot props itself upon a mountain of empty beer cans. Her initial reaction is to burn it but she has no say in the matter. <><><><><>
