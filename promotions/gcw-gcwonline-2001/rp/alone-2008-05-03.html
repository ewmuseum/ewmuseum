Alone

3 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/03/08 There is no more light in my life In the darkness is where I stay The loneliness surrounds me I am all alone It's been a few days since I left the Church of the Unholy. I've been trying to get out for months but I can't for some reason. I keep getting sucked back in. I think it may have something to do with the pills. Unfortunately I left the pills back at my pad in Rome so now I have no clue what will happen. Maybe hallucinations or illusions are going to happen. I could care less. I think the reason I've been so emotionless and feeling so empty inside is because of those pills. The past few days have been really weird. I actually feel human again. I've felt like a zombie for so long, I almost haven't felt truly human. The lack of emotion has made me feel a certain sense of loneliness for the longest time. I've spent the past few hours driving around just to clear my head and get away from the despair of my lonely abode. The gloomy, opaque sky overhead does nothing to help damper my current mood. Grayish colored clouds roll overhead from the west like lonely travelers making their way through town on a journey. Everytime I come back here, I hate it. This town is a false paradise, a dystopia in the middle of the desert so to speak. This town is all glitz and glamour on the outside but hides a darker side within. The side that no one wants to see. This town is like Basin City from the 'Sin City' graphic novels but not as bad and more glamorous. Basically a step up from Basin City to be more precise. The people are the most wretched scum seen since Mos Eisley in Star Wars Episode IV. The sky starts to turn dark as I drive down Charleston Boulevard toward my house. As I drive along in my golden chasemire 2001 Saturn SL-1, the road darkens in front of mt making it appear like my car is driving on a endless black ocean with only the streetlights and the lights on my car to guide me. The other drivers aren't helping. People who drive in this town are like maniacs. The more I drive through the darkness across town, the more I want to just hide away from it all like a coward like I've done all these years. Just hide away and let the darkness take me over instead of embracing the light. Not the church or anything like that, but the goodness in reality and in people instead of looking at the negatives like I've had for so many years. The effects of not taking my pills are starting to sink in. I'm starting to have headlikes like the ones I used to get when I was out partying and pillaging with the Killer Pirates. It feels like a migraine but is more along the lines of a sinus headache. However, there are other side effects. One is that I actually have been more in tuned with the emotions of others whereas in the past I never noticed them at all. Another is that I actually have feelings for a woman. This has never happened before. Sure, I've dated women but I only did it for the sex. I never really had feelings for them. I never had any, what I would call, human feelings for anyone before before. But I have been taking the pills since before I went to the Church full time. My doctor gave them to me to help with my emotional difficulties. Then it turned me into a callous, uncaring person. I still am today, as a matter of fact. I can't help it! It has to be those stupid pills. But I don't have to worry about taking them anymore. I don't want to take them anymore. It's nice to feel normal for once and to be treated normally because I'm not acting like an asshole to everyone I come across. I kind of feel like Melvin Udall in 'As Good as It Gets.' Actually, I have been asking myself that question all day. Is this as good as it gets? As I drive across town this question resonates through my brain as I cannot think of anything else. Everytime I try to think of something else, that same question comes up. Like earlier I went to go get some Panda Express for lunch. I ordered some Mandarin chicken with sauce on top, Barbecued pork, and half rice and half chow mein. Upon eating the food, I realized that it is best fast food place in town as far as Chinese goes and asked myself 'Is this as good as it gets?' Things like that have been happening all day. Odd coincidences that lead to me asking myself the same question over and over. Things have been weird for me here lately. Days have been meshed together, feeling the same. Days upon days, everyday feeling exactly the same as the one before it and the one after it. Everything has felt like one big blur. Even driving as I am now has not altered this fact at all. The drive seems long and endless like the lonely Spring nights I've been experiencing ever since the Church got busted by the Chinese and I returned to Vegas. Now I've experienced the weirdness of being alone for days on end and think I'm going crazy. Then I realize that I was already crazy to begin with and lose my train of thought for about the eightieth time today. That's when I see my turn as I'm driving down the street. I quickly get into the turn lane and get onto my street. The car is starting to feel like a prison and I need to get out of it. Luckily I see my house and pull into the driveway. I put the car in park and shut off the engine. Even though the walls of the car feel like they are closing in, I just sit in the driver's seat for seemingly an eternity. As much as I don't want to stay I really don't want to leave. Everytime I leave my own little world, something bad happens. But there's another reason. Besides all the bad things that keep happening, I also have this really bad gut feeling that something is wrong. That is the main reason actually. Everytime I have this same gut feeling, something really bad is going down. The last time was when GLOBAL closed, so this does not make me want to get out of the car. Eventually I relent and exit out of the car after unbuckling myself. I pull the keys out of the ignition and shut the door behind me before hitting the lock button. The car doors lock and I head to front door. The house is still as dark as it was when I left it earlier. That gives me a bit of relief considering that I was worried about someone possibly being inside. I pull out my keys and open up the front door. Once inside I close the door behind me and head toward the living room. Once there I go over to turn on the light on the coffee table next to the couch when I hear a noise down the hallway in the weight room. That gut feeling I had earlier starts to creep back into my stomach again. It feels like a small creature is inside my large intestine at this point. I'm noticeably starting to sweat as well. I walk through the darkened, empty hallway until I reach the opened door to the gym room. I start to slow down as I walk closer to the door. I wipe the sweat from my brow and go inside the room. Once in the darkened gym room, I see a familiar person standing in the darkness waiting for me. The moonlight coming in through the window reflects off him so that helps as well. I can tell by the look of him that it's my partner in crime AXEL Action. He reaches over and turns on one of the light switches. When the lights on the ceiling come on illuminating the room, I see AXEL's paitned face staring at me. On his hands are shootfighting gloves and I seem to notice that he is standing next to the punching bag. "Hail Tioneb," he mutters as I walk into the room. "Hey yo," I quietly reply back. I glance around the room looking to see if he's got weapons hiddent to try and kill me but he doesn't so I enter the room. AXEL's face is painted up more sinister than usual. He looks a bit psychotic as well. "Haven't seen you in awhile," he growls out with a hint of anger before pointing at the bench in the back of the room. "You forgot your pills at the Church in Rome." I turn to the bench and see the pill bottle laying on top of it. Deep down I know those pills are the cause of all my problems. I turn away like a person would do when they accidentally look at the sun. "I know I did," I reply back. AXEL seems a bit frustrated by something. "Are you the rat bastard whose been talking to the feds?" he asks with a snarl. "I've had the fucking FBI all over my ass for five days now and now I hear that someone from the Church spilled the beans to them. I know it wasn't me and you're about the only other one out here. You've been missing for days, James. Devil Dan and Big Tony have been with me the whole time but where have you been? Where did you go? Did you fucking rat us out James?!" I look at him a bit perplexed. Sure, Agent Harold Smith talked with me at the Church building in Rome but I didn't tell him anything. All he did was try to grill me but upon seeing I wasn't going to fall for his tricks, he gave me his card and left. That was it. Now I feel the best option is to tell him the truth so I quickly do so. "I did talk with an FBI agent in Rome, AXEL," I reply with a sigh. "But the guy told me that they already had gotten what they needed from someone else. He tried to get some information out of me but I didn't tell him anything so he gave me his card and said to call him if I 'remembered' anything before leaving the church building. Since then they've been all over me too so don't come here accusing me of jack shit because it wasn't me. I may be one evil, demented mother fucker but I'm not a liar or a stool pigeon and you know it." AXEL gazes at me with a suspicious look for a moment as he thinks about what I just said. At first he looks like he's about to blow but then he suddenly calms down. "I guess you're right," he replies after taking a deep breath. "You haven't steered me wrong in the past. You may be crazy but you're one honest bastard." I smile and nod my head upon noticing the obvious decrease in the tension that filled the room only moments earlier. "I know," I reply to him. "But we have to stick together now. These are tough times for the Church. The Church has been around for many centuries and we don't want it to end like this." Deep down I know that this statement is a lie and I desperately want to get out but I can't because of my friend. I can't escape from this Unholy nightmare. As much as I really want to, I just cant. The light at the end of the perpetual darkness just became further away than it was before I arrived back home only minutes earlier. There is no hope for me now. All there is for me is the Church. "I don't want it to end either," he replies back to me with a sinister smile on his face. "That's why I came here. I'm sorry I almost left you for dead the last time we saw each other, but I thought you attacked me. That plus the fact that after the feds started following me you disappeared, I thought you were trying to destroy the Church and escape." I try my hardest to hide the fact that he's right about me trying to escape by smiling like an evil hyena about to take a bite into a dying carcass. He is right, but may not realize. I've been trying to escape ever since we both got suckered into the church. Actually it's more of a satanic cult if anything, but that doesn't matter now. All that matters is me stringing AXEL along into believing that I'm all gung-ho for the Church while I find a means of getting out of it. "No I would never do that," I reply back with a smirk on my face to conceal my true feelings on the matter. "We need to rebuild the Church back to full strength before the government destroys it once and for all like they want to do the FLDS in Texas. That is our mission now, AXEL. We must rebuild and get vengeance for the captive and the lost." AXEL nods his head in agreement and punches the punching bag hard in excitement over this. "Hell yeah!" he exclaims with an excited grin as the punching bag sways back and forth next to him. "That's what I'm talking about! I'm all for this shit. This is the best idea you've had in ages." I smile hesitantly over hearing this response from him. I know I need to get out of this conversation and fast. However, I start getting a weird vibe coming from the shadows in the far back of the room. I get the feeling that we're not alone. It almost feels like there's some sort of vampiric, nocturnal entity watching us or something. I raise an eyebrow and stare at the darkened area at the back of the room where the storage closet is located. "What's wrong?" he asks, a bit confused by the look on my face. "I have a feeling that we're not alone," I reply, still staring at the shadowy area in the back of the room. "I feel like there's someone in the shadows watching our every move." AXEL turns and looks to the back of the room but doesn't notice anything out of the ordinary so he turns back to me. "Are you sure?" he asks as he gives me a strange look. I nod my head and look back toward the corner again. "I think we're being watched by some sort of vampiric, nocturnal entity or something," I reply back while still eyeing the back of the room. AXEL doesn't look back this time. He seems to not believe me about the prescence I'm feeling I've got about someone watching us. He could be in on it for all I know. "You're a vampiric, nocturnal entity," he replies back with a smirk. "Well yeah," I reply back quickly. AXEL gets a chuckle over this. The conversation is starting to sound like more of our normal conversations so that's a good thing in my mind, I hope. But I still think there's someone watching us and AXEL is either knows about it or is just not seeing it. I can't tell but that has a lot to do with the facepaint. It's always had to tell what he's thinking and his emotions are because of the facepaint. "You're a psychopath," he replies with a grin. "But anyway, we'd best start getting ready for this match we have. We need to be in the best frame of mind and in the best physical condition possible for this as well." I nod my head in agreement. I know he's right on this issue. "Of course," I say as I look around the gym at the training equipment I have in there. "We can train here. Just let me go get my training gear on and we can get started." "Alright," he replies with a nod as I leave the room to go get my training clothes on. Once James has left the room, AXEL turns to the shadowy corner where James was looking at only moments earlier. A shadowy figure appears out of the darkness but stays in the shadows so he is not seen. "I told you he was dangerous," the figure says to AXEL with a snake-like voice. "He knew I was hiding back there." "He's always paranoid like that," AXEL replies back. "He's not a threat to the Church." "YES HE IS!" the shadowy figure snarls with a hiss. "He's the only one who the pills didn't work on and is the only church member who didnt completely embrace Tioneb after our 'recruitment' sessions. He's a loose cannon. Stay with him. Watch him close. Anything unusual, let me know. And I will be watching." The shadowy figure walks over to the window in the room and opens it up before climbing out. He closes it again behind him and AXEL locks it back into position so James doesn't know about the person being there. AXEL turns back around and looks a bit paranoid that James knows what's really going on. Meanwhile, as I'm coming back from getting my gear on, I can hear AXEL talking to someone or too himself, I can't tell but I can clearly hear thw window open and close. I think I was right now about there being someone there. I start walking down the hallway so he can hear me coming. I clearly see the lights come on in the gym and hear him jog across the room. I walk over to the opened gym doorway and walk inside. There I see AXEL lifting weights. "Getting started a bit early, eh?" I ask with a raised eyebrow. AXEL nods his head. "Yeah," he replies back to me. "I want to be in the best shape possible for our match." I nod my head in agreement. "Good thinking," I say with a smile. "But I thought I heard you talking to someone a moment ago..." He noticeably gets a bewildered look on his face before smiling at me. "Oh, no James," he says with a hesistant smile as he continues to lift weights. "I was just talking to myself. You know, to psyche myself up. I do that all the time you know." "I do that too," I reply to him as I walk over toward the punching bag. "Now let's get ready to kick some ass at WorldWide." I know that he's lying but I don't know why and for what reason. However, I do know that I have to stick with him for as long as I can. Only time will tell if our personal agendas will tear me and AXEL apart.
