New Year's Resolutions

11 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/11/2006 Seymour Almasy’s 2006 New Year’s Resolutions for GCW. 1. Become the GCW World Champion. I mean, duh, that’s sort of obvious, but if I want to be complete, I’ve got to list it. 2. Hey, while I’m at it, become GCW United States Champion too. 3. Ooh, ooh, and Television Champion! 4. And Cruiserweight Champion! 5. ALL YOUR TITLES ARE BELONG TO ME~! 6. Okay, okay, some ones not related to titles’hmmm’no, wait, first, WIN THE TAG TITLES! 7. Done now. Alrighty then, what else’okay, it would be nice to get my likeness made into a GCW action figure. I mean, GCW marketing already has the Seymour Almasy lunchbox, and the Seymour Almasy pillowcase, but NOT the Seymour Almasy action figure. WASSUPWITDAT?! 8. Find The Guild, and play D&D with them. Hey, do you have any idea how hard it is to find people in this line of work who play Dungeons & Dragons? Most wrestlers probably think 1d20 is some sort of sexual position. 9. Beat the ever loving crap out of LeStatt Knight for being a waste of a human being and a woman-beater. Asking Eva VanLorne out on a date afterwards is purely optional, but likely would be a good idea. 10. Get into the Rampage tournament. Hey, you know how much I love tournaments. 11. Win Rampage tournament. Ditto. 12. Beat Aiden Jackson, whether it’s for the Cruiserweight Title or not, in the middle of the ring. We had a good match, Aiden. Next time, though, I’m not settling for a countout. 13. Hmm’this is tougher than I thought. Oh, wait. Laugh at the rest of the GCW roster when my friends from Global come in and start burying people. With no disrespect to the roster, these are people I either haven’t beaten or could barely beat. Quality of competition’s about to go up. Big time. 14. Cry when I still can’t beat half the people who show up. *insert frowny face* 15. Talk with Eva more. She seems like a perfectly sweet girl, and lord knows I don’t have enough friends backstage in GCW. 16. On that note, also talk to Michael Stevens more. He’s a good guy, and hey, maybe if I become buddy buddy enough with him, he’ll give me a shot at his shiny title. That reminds me? 17. Get polish to make any belts I win shinier. 18. Debut the Clearly Overdramatic Crescent Kick in GCW. Was supposed to do it in Global, but a certain female member of the roster got uppity over having to take a move called the COCK. But seriously! Imagine the fun the commentators could have. “OH MY GOD! SEYMOUR ALMASY JUST HIT EVA IN THE FACE WITH THE COCK!”, or maybe “COCK TO THE MOUTH! COCK TO THE MOUTH!” The material writes itself, does it not? 19. Dress up as Emoticon for the Valentine’s Day edition of WorldWide, and take over the show. If Steve Austin can have a beer truck, and Kurt Angle can have a milk truck, I can sure as hell have a flower truck. Besides, maybe with roses of every color, I can finally figure out what they actually do. Red is for love, yellow for’friendship? Beats the shit out of me. 20. Beat the shit out of whoever assigned meanings to the color of roses. 21. Have my rubber match with Rich Rollins. We’re 1-1-2 against each other right now. Apparently the two draws were due to the matches being put on standby or something, I don’t know. Caldera tried to explain it to me, but it makes Rollins look like a complete idiot, so it can’t be tr--no. On second thought, it probably is true. Probably won’t get my match with Rollins for a while though, as he’s too busy trying to bone Ryan-o’s mom. 22. On that note, get laid. It’s been a long fucking time, and I’m getting depressed. 23. Start bringing my sword to the ring with me. Not that I’ll ever use it, but carrying a sword everywhere you go is cool. Too bad the police don’t see it that way. But those bitches ain’t never gon? get me! I carry a weapon’s permit, AND a chocobo license! FUCK DA MIDGAR PO-LICE! 24. Three words: KINGDOM HEARTS TWO. 25. Win my first match of the new year. It’s my new buddy and I against Scott Taylor and Jonathan Borealis, who I beat in my first night with GCW. Taylor and I have some unfinished business, and I’m sure Stevens can handle Borealis. So, all things considered, it looks like 2006 is going to be a good year. P.S.: All your titles? They’re still gonna belong to me. I’m a beltwhore. *insert frowny face*.
