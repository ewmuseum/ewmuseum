When there is no more room in hell.....

undated

Date: ?? Thrashing, and sweating heavily, clenching his sheets, fearing his life. The young man can’t believe he escaped yesterday, almost losing his life, but now he’s a victim in his own dreams. The hands, those giant hands, three finger nails longer than the rest, dirt littered lifelines he can still see as vividly as the day it happened, clench tightly around his throat. Still sweating, and still dreaming his hands go to this own throat to block those giant hands from squeezing tight. His tongue begins to loll out of his mouth as those hands begin its death grip. It almost feels like those hands are popping his head off with such a tight squeeze. He is gasping for air, squeezing the dreampt hands are pulling the life out of him, his face turns blue, and his toes are pointing straight up. Than he feels the death slowly slip away and his life coming back. His eyes start to open, blur, blur, blur, and finally the picture clears up. He is staring straight up at his ceiling fan that is oscillating clockwise. The chain used for changing speeds is clanking off the light. He turns to his right and notices his clock. 7:13AM. He moves his head back and sighs a sigh of relief, what a nightmare. Sure that was a nightmare, but the ordeal was nothing more than reality. This young man begins to move from his bed. His legs hanging over the edge, T-Shirt sticking to his chest in perspiration. He looks in the mirror on his closet door for a brief second and notices something by his left mandible. It almost looks like a bruise. His eyes squint at the image, and he finally gets up and moves towards the mirror. His steps are slow and sympathetic, because even though his posture signals he knows nothing about the bruise like thing, but his brain knows exactly what it is. He stands in front of the mirror. Most would come closer and attempt to diagnose the bruise, but instead he stands still, straight as an arrow and stares at his reflection. Shirt stuck to his chest, hair a mess, and boxers bunched up near the crotch area. He closes his eyes and remembers that day, the day when his nightmares became true. “Hey, let’s go past the cemetery tonight!” He stands and looks at his friend in confusion. He has no clue why this man would want to go into the cemetery. That was something they did as children. See who can hang out in the cemetery the longest before being spooked out. “Look man, we did that shit as kids. I just want to go home, take a shower and fuck Julia. Besides, we are way to old for that spooking shit.” He laughs, “Come on man, lets just do it once, besides I wanna show you something.” Grinding his teeth, and clenching his fists, “Dude it’s fucking dead people, is it really necessary” What could possibly be in there that we haven’t seen before?” Smiling, come on, just a few minutes. And without even accepting his rebuttal he heads towards the main gates. Can’t really call them gates, because they are no gates, just a driveway through the cemetery. The big stone to your left is a map, that they both ignore. They began to run towards the very back of the cemetery. The back is dedicated to families. Husbands, wives, their children, and their children’s children were all buried together. He is following his friend, and his youth immediately begins to rear its head. He remembers running through here with spray paint and hammers, ready to destroy all that is in his path. His friend stops, puts his hands on his head and tries to catch his breathe. Both men are panting heavily like a hot dog, and than he puts his hands on his knees. Wow, he hasn’t ran that hard in years. “Ok’Check it out’Remember this name?” His friend asks, and than points at a headstone. It looks old, but the death date is relatively recent. This is a default stone that is used when the Next of Kin is no longer involved or interested. It says “Here Lies Annabelle O’Neil. 1938-2008” His jaw drops. Wow, the old lady finally bit the big one. He looks at his friend, “What happened?” His smile turns into a sullen look of gloom. “She was strangled to death. They say her throat was squeeze so hard and with so much power that everything from her esophagus to her wind pipe were crushed beyond recognition. Her neck bones were crushed into almost a dust. It was fucking crazy.” He looks back at the tombstone than back at his friend. “Did her son do it?” “Most likely not. That sick fuck loved his mother more than anything, and besides he’s too busy in the states making millions of dollars doing some stupid fake ass fucking wrestling. Fuck him and fuck her.” He spits on her grave. Jumping back so not to receive spit on his shoe. “Whoa, her sons and her husband were fucked up. She wasn’t why are you so angry with a dead woman?” He looks at him in disgust. “Do you know what this bitch did for a living man?” His silence spoke for generations. “She was a hooker man, and because of her, my parents were divorced.” She looks back at the grave and spits on it again. “Fuck you bitch.” “How is that her fault” You father could have said no!” He can’t believe what his friend just said. He looks back at his friend, and somewhere in the back of his head he knows he is right, but he also must defend his family. He looks back at his friend and a shit eating grin appears on his face. He turns slightly, and begins to urinate on the grave of Annabelle O’Neil. His friend begins to laugh as well. Sure it was wrong but you have to admit reliving your past and childhood can be entertaining. He turns around, because the sound of his friend urinating makes him wanna piss as well. When he gets his zipper down he hears a yelp and a cracking sound. He turns to investigate and standing over a the limp body of his friend is a giant man with a painted face. Fear cripples him as he looks at Belmont, England’s very own Boogeyman. He points, and screams. “BRYAN!” That was all he could let out, because the monster never let him yell O’Neil at all. The monster’s hands begin to death grip around his throat. He quickly reacts with a swift kick to the groin and is able to escape the clutches of Bryan O’Neil, although he can still feel the death grip. He keeps staring at himself in the mirror. What happened yesterday, did it even happen? He ran to the authorities, but now they must think he’s crazy, because there was no sign of violence at the cemetery, his friend’s body was not there, and there was not one scientific sign of urine on the gravesite. The authorities also said that Bryan O’Neil has an alibi. He was wrestling at a house show in New Orleans. He can’t believe it, maybe it was all a dream, but what the hell is that bruise about? He keeps staring at his bruise in the mirror, trying to decipher where and when this thing happened, and that’s when he hears a noise behind him. He looks into the mirror trying to see if he can spot the location, and than in an instinct he turns around and his eyes meet a huge chest tattoo. His eyes roll up and he see’s that famous painted face. His mouth drops, and that’s when he feels the most excruciating pain coming from his abdomen. He looks down and watches as blood begins to flow out of his stomach, around the hand of Bryan O’Neil. The monster has just put his hand into his stomach. The stream of bloody vomit begins to flow from his mouth and nose onto the tattooed body of Bryan O’Neil. The body begins to convulse, O’Neil doesn’t move his hand, or flinches as his body is being attacked by his victims blood. O’Neil looks into this young man’s eyes. “I Love my Mommy. Welcome to Oblivion.” Lights and life are extinguished.
