Shadows of Memories

17 November 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/17/11 The scene opens on a desolate street, TyDezno is visible sitting on a bench outside some shops. His hair flies through the wind as he looks at his watch, he then looks up at the camera and gives his cocky smile. He takes his sunglasses from their seated position above his nose and places them in his top left pocket on his jacket. He stands up revealing some black pants and some black shoes. As he stands a few cars pass nearby on the busy road, with newspapers flying past the camera as TyDezno dodges them. TyDezno: Oh Xander, it is you who will fall victim to the Designed Perfection, and it is you who will be beaten at Worldwide 155 because Searle, you are simply IMperfect. See when I signed that contract at the RAMPAGE PPV, I made sure that my presence was known and felt... that this "new life" included giving my all and looking past the memories. The fact is that we both a new here... but the one thing that truly separates us apart is the I'm perfected! TyDezno laughs and avoids some more paper that has been blowing in the wind. He begins slowly walking down this street as a steady pace. TyDezno: See Xander, I've heard of your past... All the accomplishments and glory, all the downfalls and failures, and all the memories. But this is a new chance, with different people. A new day as we have a chance to show the world what we are made of.... to start afresh. But I have to ask you, how are your "memories?" Are they constantly shadowing you and haunting your mind. And it seems you better get used to being in the shadows because there ain't anyway that you can win tomorrow night against TyDezno. I will be the force in this federation, I am the tornado on a destructive path that will end when I hold the GCW World Heavyweight Championship above my head. TyDezno closes his eyes for a moment, like he's getting power from somewhere, he opens them again to reveal a huge pupil in his eye, the pupil then shrinks as he continues to talk TyDezno: This is not your life... and this won't be a win in your "new chapter." You're just another win on my rankings, another step forward to the stars. My path will be tough and rough, but when you're will be merely another mark for my perfection. He catches a paper in the wind, its a Haunted Halloween article with a man in a bodybag costume. TyDezno throws the paper letting it catch the wind again, the streetlight he's under flickers as the howling wind makes it's way through the gaps in between the buildings and shops nearby, TyDezno steps a bit to the left so he's parallel with the light-post, the wind now doesn't catch him as much as before and he regains his stance. TyDezno: At the end of Worldwide 155, it will be my face on the screen, my arms will be above my head shining as photos all round the arena are took of me. Me standing over you with the flashlights in my hand. And just as this light-post behinds is flickering... so will you beginning of your career flicker. TyDezno's hair starts to regain its normal position as the wind dies down, the shops behind TyDezno becomes more clear as the camera pulls out. On the outset, we notice the KeyArena! TyDezno steps back as a huge truck almost flashes past him bringing along with it a gust of wind capable of lifting a small car off the ground. TyDezno's jacket almost rips its sleeves but the wind dies down. The camera stops shaking and pans round to reveal a huge poster above the entrance way to the arena. Poster reading, "GCW Halloween Special Show." TyDezno: There is no doubt that the arena will be filled with fans, mostly my fans I might add. The atmosphere in there will be electric. The fans are expecting a great deal out of the GCW on Monday. But mark my words... there will be some tricks on you and without any treats! TyDezno looks at the empty street, about 30 meters away, a white Jaguar headlights become visable. A few seconds later the camera turns away from the blinding light as a this Jaguar flashes its headlights. TyDezno: Our match will be epic as I debut my in-ring talents, and as soon as you enter you'll leave to a mass of booing, you'll be used as target practice for thousands upon thousands of fans packed in that arena. At my right hand will be the love of my life, my beautiful fiancee. And by your side... simply shadows... shadows of your defeats, your past and "those" memories! TyDezno closes his eyes yet again, but this time to stop the blinding headlights coming to his eyes as the white Jaguar come near. A petite lady carrying some shopping bags walks past in the background fighting against the wind. All of a sudden a huge lightning flash fills the screen and an outbreak of rain pounces down and fills the area with slight rain. The lady walking in the background turns into a jog and then a run, trying to make it to her car in the distance. TyDezno opens the door turning his back towards the camera. As the rain falls on him and with a smug smile on his face, he slowly turns back to the camera. TyDezno: 'Searle, Searle go away, come again another day.' That song, I think best fits you this Monday. Xander Searle, This is NOT YOUR LIFE! TyDezno pulls his glasses from his pocket and places his glasses back onto his nose. He enters the car and shut the door. He is seen reaching over to kiss a girl, who is his fiancee, Jasmine. She fade black as they drove off.
