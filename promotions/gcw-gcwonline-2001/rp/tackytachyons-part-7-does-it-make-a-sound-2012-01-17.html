Tacky–Tachyons Part 7 (...does it make a sound?)

17 January 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/17/12 Date: N/A Time: N/A Location: Between Dimensions The once nameless and formless entity now known as M stands in the form of a man, shoulders broad and posture proud. He stares a hole into the strung up Bradley still trapped in the memories of his past. M’s gaze for the most part is cold with slight emotion creeping into the corners of his mouth and the creases in his eyes. It’s clear he’s trying to hide his own anxiousness. He begins to mutter. M: "These nerves… I feel them. They’re familiar, yet unique. There is a tired feeling with a burden of stress and the slightest obnoxious tickle. Feels almost like a decent into madness. How do you do this? How does your kind press forward?" He gets no answer from "The SoCal Prophet" who motionlessly tied to a large cross like structure (Cruzifix). M: "These bodies are like primitive machines. Sure they have their charm and esthetic appeal, but lack all modern convenience and luxury. My previous incarnation was smooth and tranquil. This body is in constant motion pumping the blood through my veins; my eye lids constantly open and shut to keeping my eyes from drying out and worst yet I feel… I feel… everything." M rolls his shoulders and neck as a chill travels through his body like an unsettling current. M: "It’s very unsettling… oh well. I’ll have a lifetime in my new body to get used to it. I wonder how you’ll cope with becoming formless floating sentient consciousness." M creeps closer to Bradley within a few inches from Cruz’s face. M: "I suppose we’ll find out soon enough." The corner of M’s lips lift cracking an amused smile. January 5, 2003 3:00 PM, Long Beach, California The Cruz House Brad just sits at the dining room table with his mother (Maris) and his father (Martin). The room is quiet and you could cut the tension with a knife. Martin finally breaks the silence. Martin: "So, you’ve made up your mind?" Brad: "Yes." Martin: "… and it was that easy for you?" Brad: "No, it wasn’t easy at all." Martin: "Oh, it wasn’t? Taking "your future" that your mother and I sacrificed to build for you and just throwing it away wasn’t easy? … and for what!? Nothing! SO YOU CAN GO BOUNCE AROUND IN SOME RING!" Brad: "I told you, I can still go to school while I train." Bradley stands up and leans against the dining room table. Martin: I knew it; I knew this day was coming. The day you were just going to ruin everything by basically running off and joining the circus. Maris: "Bradley darling, can’t you see what your selfishness is doing this family? Sometimes you have to put your dreams aside for the benefit of the family as a whole. Please sweetie." Brad’s jaw nearly drops at her statement. He raises and eyebrow and tilts his head in confusing. Brad: "Is that what family does, mom? Is that what you do when you’re gone for weeks at a time in New York trying to keep your musical theater career alive?" Maris: "The Theater is my passion." Brad leans over the table and takes his mothers hands into his own. Brad: "…and wrestling is mine. Please understand, Mom." Maris looks conflicted, but quickly shakes it off. She pulls away from Brad. Maris: "I-I can’t, sweetie. I wish I could, but I just can’t." Bradley stands up straight and readies himself to leave. Martin: "Where are you going? We aren’t done yet." Brad shakes his head. Brad: "Well I am. I’ve already made up my mind." As Brad starts to leave his father gets to his feet grabbing his sons arm, stopping in his tracks. Martin: "If you walk out now. Don’t bother coming back." Brad looks his father in the eyes and could see he meant what he said. Without even a second thought Bradley promptly pulls away. He slowly walks to the front door. Martin: "I mean it, Bradley. I won’t waver on this. I can’t." Brad has a sad little grin. Brad: "I know, Pop… but neither can I." Bradley exits through the front door and Martin sits back down. His wife Maris wraps her arms around her husband as she begins to sob. July 4, 2004 9:00 PM, Sacramento, California MadMen of Wrestling: Rock the World Event: No Limits The arena is alive and thriving on one of MOW:RTW’s bi-monthly live events. The crowd shows their approval to the competitors in the ring. DeToX is clearly in control of the match whipping around "The Natural Disaster" Hurricane (Bradley). DeToX drives down Bradley with a hard left handed clothesline. Brad gets to his feet quickly and once again DeToX nails him with a second clothesline. DeToX sizes him up for the third. Bradley rises to his feet DeToX charges in, but Bradley ducks under. DeToX misses with his clothesline. DeToX turns to correct his trajectory but is met with a thunderous flipping dropkick from the Hurricane dropping DeToX hard to the mat. DeToX rolls to the ropes pulling himself up to his feet. Brad charges in DeToX ducks down and pitching Bradley over the top rope. Hurricane clings to the ropes. DeToX looks ready to knock him off the apron but is met with a kick to head flung over the top rope. DeToX stumbles back grasping his head. The Hurricane (Brad) springs to the third rope propelling him forward with a devastating flying spinning wheel kick nearly decapitating DeToX. Brad pulls DeToX to his feet and scoops him up into a devastating Olympic Slam!! ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! Ring Announcer: "Your winner, THE HURRICANE!!!" The Referee raises Brad’s arm in victory as DeToX slides out the ring meanders his way to the back. Brad plays to the crowd as he exits the ring and heads up the ramp and behind the curtain. 10:00 PM MOW:RTW Trainer’s Office Casanova Bradley lays in the trainer’s room with a towel draped over his face waiting the trainer to return and clear him when there’s a knock on the door. Brad: "Come in." The door slowly creeps open and a young woman enters. Brad: "Everything okay, Doc? Am I all set?" "You could be." The unfamiliar voice startles Bradley who promptly sit up stripping the towel from his face. He looks up to see a beautiful woman. She was younger but her eyes screamed old soul. Her hair was dark and cascaded down like black silk. Her eyes were green and made Bradley feel as though he were standing in the middle of an endless meadow. Brad opens his mouth to speak but his words fail him. She smiles and his heart skips a beat. Crystal: "The Hurricane aka Bradley Hegan Cruz. I enjoy your work." Brad: "Uh, thanks." Crystal: "How’s the family?" Brad: "I wouldn’t know. Do I know you? …because I think I would reme-" Crystal: "I’m Crystal Robbins: manager, agent, assistant, and legal advisor." Brad: "That’s a lot of hats." Crystal: "What can I say? I like to accessorize." Brad grins. Brad: "…so how can I help you?" Crystal: "You can help me help you." Brad: "…so you want to be the Tom Cruise to my Cuba Gooding Jr.?" Crystal: "I want to represent you." Brad raises and eyebrow. Brad: "I’ve never had an agent before." Crystal: "Then you need someone… someone to go to bat for you… someone to fight for you… someone to ensure your voice is heard." Brad can’t help to think of his Grandfather’s last words to him. "Make them hear you." Bradley simply nods. Brad: "Alright, I’ll take a chance on you." Crystal smiles and gives Brad a thankful nod. Crystal: "You won’t regret this! We can work out the details once the trainer says you can leave." Bradley lays back down the trainer’s examining table. Crystal is about to exit the office when something comes to mind. She turns looking back at Bradley. Crystal: "Mr. Cruz-" Brad: "-Brad." Crystal: "Brad, what ultimately made you decide to take on my services, if I may ask?" Brad lifts his head only slightly as he answers. Brad: "I liked what you had to say. Also you were right I probably do need someone represent me, but ultimately I would be a damn fool if I didn’t guarantee we would see each other again." Crystal can’t help but smile. Bradley lies back draping the towel over his head once more. Crystal: "I’ll wait for you outside, and by the way that towel is a good look on you." Exits the office closing the door behind her.
