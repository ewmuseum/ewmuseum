Heed My Warning, Be My Offering

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: days before dangerous games This time of year Belmont, England begins it’s new life cycle. The Corn and Maize season is all done, and this year Belmont did much better because of the nice rainy season it experienced. The stalks are now drying up, and they crunch and wither away as you pull them from the earth. The mud is starting to get harder, almost claylike. The sky is forming a heavy grey cloud look that will stay with us until early to mid march. The sun does not exist it seems during this time of year. Even though it’s been a few years, the house that use to lurk on the hill’s absence still amazes people. Belmont’s citizens spent years and years always looking over their shoulders, up at that hill. They spent years fearing that house, running for cover everytime an interior window would light up, or evaporate. Things have changed. Lestatt Knight and Bryan Mayhem beat each other from pillar to post in that house so bad that it crumbled on them. After construction crews took away the rubble the only thing that is left in a drainage pipe in the middle of the hill where the bathroom once sat. If the citizens would look up there now, they might quake in fear, and run to the hills. Bryan O’Neil has come back to his hometown once again, and like the last time he visited, the vanquished demon known to many as The Belmont House of Torture is his location of choice, well where it use to be atleast. Bryan is standing south to southwest, winding beating his leather trenchcoat, blowing his hair in his face. The wind is lashing so hard you can sometimes hear the thump of his dreadlocks pounding his coat. His coat is done all the way to the top. He’s wearing black boots, and he’s not sporting the corpse paint he’s so been famously known for. He stares at the outline of where this massive structure once stood and loomed. He stares at where all his childhood nightmares, now being put into ink in January, once frightened him. He lived here well after the death of his father and the exit of his mother. He lived in the very house where he and his brother were beaten almost daily. Like some sick Freudian moniker, he lived where his life was hell. Bryan begins to walk slowly around the hole in the earth. The dead leaves crackling lightly under his massive hoof. He circles the former foundation three times, never once blinking, never once keeping his eyes off of the hole. He stops and steps into the hole. The basement foundation has since than been filled with land, but there’s still a foot and half drop into where it once stood. Bryan looks into the sky, than back into the hole. He pulls from his pocket a tiny cross, looks at it, inverts it and slams it into the earth. “Here Lies Andy Murray, fellow European, fellow brother of the Great Empire. A myth making a name for himself. Your arrogance beseeches you and you will perish like them all.” Bryan stares back at the cross, than moves five more feet. He once again stares into the night sky, closes his eyes, exhales and smashes another upside down cross into the hard muddy land. “Chris Bagwell, truly a god to only himself. A selfish little stain on society. I grant thee a pardon into an everlasting life of fellowship and wealth. I forever condemn you to a life of burnt flesh and scarred remains. Grant thee the power to choke the life that was once a little a child, to the final breathe of a vanquished man.” Bryan this time walks towards the other end of his former residence and stops. He looks to the sky, than towards the earth. “This was where mother once cooked meals for her family. She was a loving woman, caught up in the absence of life in her soul. She took measures to make sure my life was not as destroyed as hers, and it’s in this very room where she cooked her final meal. A coagulating little gel in a spoon.” Bryan stands silent, wipes his eyes and smashes another inverted cross into the earth. “Octavian I condemn thee to a life or peril in the hands of the dark lord. A legend to some, and itch to most, I pray that your life of hell is like the shot of pain through the veins of my mother.” Almost in the same spot, only inches from Octavian’s cross Bryan smashes another into the earth. “Vivica J Valentine. There can not be enough said about you. Vivica I have beaten and left you for dead more than anyone else, and for some reason you still come back to life. Like a worm regenerating itself from it’s severed part. After I crushed and smited you not once, not twice, but three diabolical times you have returned to be the greatest thing known to man, while I slip into a desolate state of mediocrity. Like Octavian I smash this cross into the earth right where my mother took her final breathe on her own vomit. I ask a higher darker force to take your final breath and throw you out like the trash you have become.” Rain begins to pitter patter on the leather coat of Mayhem. The wind kicks up and begins to howl. The rain will only get heavier, as the sky begins to turn to black. “Zacharia Taylor, as I come to you I am given the drink of the earth in rain. How fitting, when you have become the sputum of a forgotten man. The phlegm of an addict that fills its worth in the eye of a needle.” Bryan walks due north about 15-20 feet, leaves and mud under his feet, the rain falling heavier and fatter than before. Bryan pulls out his cross and smashes it to the earth. A little mud splatters up and his Bryan in the face, but he does not even flinch. “Taylor, this cross I lay into the flesh of the earth for you, here in the bathroom where the rape of my life was taken place. My father took my mother and forced her into a reproductive acts so despicable that not even I myself have the heart to release. I rape you of your life and victory here in room of shit and filth.” Bryan moves to his left, than makes a short right as if he’s walking in the house still standing today. He stops and begins to shake a little than catches his composure. The rain is beating heavily, throwing mud into the air as it plops against the ground. Bryan’s coat is now sheen and slick with the rain, his hair getting heavy with the rain, and his boots are now resembling those of a cowboy. “This is the living room. Right where I stand was the old semen infested beat up couch that my father use to pass out and entertain his slut female friends on. I recall asking my father to show me how to throw and catch a baseball. I was young, showing an interest in sports and he did nothing. NOTHING!! I stood there for twenty minutes watching him down a bottle of Turkey, and than he looks at me and tells me to fuck off. FUCK OFF?””” Bryan pulls another cross from his coat and slams it into the earth. Each cross is becoming more and more violent. “Phil Atken. Who the hell are you” I have never paid attention to a meaningless shit stain on the earth. Like my father did to me, I will ignore you as to beg for a better life when yours is being extinguished. Fuck off, and welcome to Oblivion.” Bryan’s face is starting to show signs of anger and vengeance. His breathing is escalating, and his footsteps are becoming heavier and more distinct. He only moves a few feet when he does the cross thing agaim. “Jacob McKail, you had your chance to improve your stock, but like my brother you were used only as a step ladder for those near you. Like my brother I will stand on your skull and move on to bigger and better things. When I have my hands around your throat you too will ask for a life, a life worth living, and I will only descend upon you a life worth dying.” Bryan slows down, stops, stares, and keeps going. His gait becomes slow and methodical, his breathe becomes heavy and thick. He has toured the entire first floor of the now defunct house and he comes to the most frightening moment of his life. “I was misbehaving as usual. Typical kid and my father was very drunk, and he started to get more and more violent because by this time I was his size. Well, I’ve had enough of his abuse and instead of me, he grabbed my little brother Jason, and threw him down the steps into the basement. Jason busted his head open and broke some bones. What made it worse was my father cracked a smile and asked if I heard that. I saw read, grabbed my father and began to lay down lefts and rights. I was going off so bad he started to bleed everywhere. My mother came downstairs in a drug induced haze and just watched as I was beating my father within an inch of his life. She did nothing to stop me, she too was sick and tired of all the abuse and violence and rape. My father was lying there bleeding from his nose and mouth. I grabbed him by his head, looked at my mother and all she did was nod. It was at that point in time I snapped my father’s neck and ended it all.” Bryan stands at where the steps once were, and crouches down and gently places a cross into the earth. “Lestatt, you remember the hell we went through. You remember our final encounter ended in this very basement, the exact same place my father met his end. I place this cross here for you, as a sign of things to come. Lestatt, our war is not over, and like my father, the years of torture I have put myself through after our epic encounter has haunted me, and for that I, like the rest, will release your lungs of it’s final breathe, and separate your bones from their hinges.” Bryan stands up, and looks back to the black sky. The rain is beating down on his face. He opens his mouth to catch some drops in his mouth than closes it. “My time and destiny have come, and it’s time for me to fulfill all the voids. Welcome to Oblivion.” Bryan walks off, leaving the rain drowned mud, and a bunch of tiny upside crosses to haunt this hill of Belmont.
