Affirmative Action? You figure it out

18 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/18/2009 The setting isn’t different than any other middle class neighborhood on a Friday evening at the local McDonald’s. A group of wifeless fathers all congregate awaiting the arrival of their separated or severed “better halves”. The tone is the usual with nothing standing out of the ordinary, the milkshake machine wasn’t working and the Playland sat empty eagerly awaiting the arrival of all those young kids trying desperately to cling to some form of normalcy in their single parent homes. Just as the soccer mom mini-vans started rolling in off in the distance a steady thumping can be heard. Another van rolls in and the sound gets closer and more frequent. Finally the horns and percussion instruments become pronounced enough to distinguish them. “Bow Down” by the Westside Connection drowns out all other sounds as the murdered out Yukon Denali rolls into the parking lot. Every concerned father hit the glass trying to see through the limo tinted windows. All they can see, and for that matter smell is the chronic smoke billowing out of the windows. The Affirmative Action Mobile Unit (AAMU)?s sound system forces everyone out of the drive thru line where the Denali rolls to a stop with the sound system still blaring. “BOW DOWN” When you’re West Coast bound.” Through the speaker the white boy inside tries to talk over the radio, “Welcome to?” Even he couldn’t hear himself. His manager rushed over to see if he could save his Drive Thru times and offered his assistance, “Welcome?” “Hey yo,” a voice rang through their headsets. “Look here man; we wanna know how many African Americans yall got workin up in dere?” “BOW DOWN” When I come to your town.” Trying to respond the manager stuttered, “Umm l” let? that would be twelve.” With the music being soo loud there was no way the voice heard him, “EXACTLY AS I THOUGHT! NONE! Here in SUB-BUR-Bee-AH the black man can’t even get a job feedin you cracka ass crackas!” a loud inhaling sound is heard, “June Bug pull da whip ova” these crackas bout to hire sum brothas up in here? up in here! HA HA!” “Cause I ain’t a hater like you, BOW DOWN to a nigga that’s greater than you.” With noses pressed against the glass the father waiting for their children followed the Yukon into the nearest parking place. The whispers became voices as playtime got cut short for the kids who had already arrived. All four doors on the Yukon simultaneously pop open with June Bug emerging from the driver’s door in some baggy jeans and his black Affirmative Action poncho. From the passenger’s door Roland Washington stepped out of the vehicle, wearing his trademark black with red pinstripes pimp suit with the matching hate and feather. From the back out jumped Big O and CJ, each matching June Bug’s look. The group converged at the rear of the vehicle where they finished off the spliff and started into the McDonald’s. “BIATCHES!” Roland yells as they walked through the door. “We are here to help YOU be better people!” The fluorescent lighting casted a dim empty glare over his bloodshot eyes. Affirmative Action took notice of the five African Americans behind the counter, Roland’s lazy eyes finally caught up with his entourage. “Yo wait a minute” you went an hired sum color real quick.” He tried to spin his obvious mistake. “No if you would have turned your music down you would have heard, we employee twelve African Americans on a staff of 18 employees.” The manager angrily stated walking from around the backside of the counter. The group stood silent before eventually turning their glare to Roland. “What gives man?” inquired June Bug. CJ snapped in agreement, “Yeah man we’s posed to be sticking it to whitey!” “Yeah!” replied the ever present Big O. “Would you like to order something?” the manager asked wanting nothing more but to rush the group back out of the door. “Orda”!” cried Roland, “You think you’re in a position ta be ordain us around”!” “I mean are you hungry”!” “You’re damn right we’re hungry! We’re hungry for equality! And we’ll take it with a side order of JUSTICE!” Washington started preaching, “It’s been far too long that my people have been persecuted, and extradited against their liberties to see that sum cracka ass cracka is walking around with a Big MAC in his hand!” “So does that mean you want a Big Mac?” “Shhh,” June Bug quickly corrected the manager, “once he starts you gots ta let him finish.” “That’s right my brothas! A new day is upon us! Even under the persecution of the almighty crackas in wRAsslin have opened up their cold Caucasian hearts to the thought” no! The dream of finally seeing a black man make it in the wRAsslin biz.” Roland paused as though waiting for applause that never arrive. “That’s right my brothas let that one stew a minute.” “We don’t serve stew” maybe the Wendy’s down the street?” “So finally the time is upon us” a new day is dawning for our country with the first BLACK president ever!” Roland was abruptly cut off by CJ whispering into his ear. “Apparently we’ve missed the boat on having the first black president” someone could have told a nigga fo’real.” Washington tries to save face. “It’s common knowledge.” The manager added. “Are you tryin ta say my black knowledge isn’t as good as your COMMON knowledge”!” he barked back. “Look I’ll give you guys a friggin bag of Big Macs just to get the hell outta here!” “A bag! A BAG!” Roland was flabbergasted, “Do you think we’re back in the slaves days Mista Whitey”! You can just purchase our silence with one measly bag of Big MACs?!” “A a bag full of hot apple pies?” he inquired. Six hands slap over Roland’s mouth and Affirmative Action replied, “Done!” Big O and June Bug ushered their trusty manager out the door and back to their Yukon chuckling to themselves, “Stupid crackas” all we gots to do is sick mouth here on em and they’ll practically give us shit!” “Yeah!” Big O insightfully added. “You guys gots ta stop bustin up my flow man” dat cracka was gonna toss us sum cash if yall would’ve gave me anotha second or two!” Roland bitched as he was tossed into the passenger seat. Moments later CJ came bouncing out of the McDonald’s yelling back at the manager inside? “Yeah well if you so damn smart, tell me why it’s not the Big Mic!” “Just get out!” the voice chased CJ into the parking lot. “Damn stoopid cracka?” CJ growled tossing the bag into the car before handing in the drink carrier, “Alls I did was axe him why white people call it Mic Donald’s when it’s supposed to be MAC Donald’s.” June Bug excitedly looked back, “Wanna jack him up?” “Nah, I just wanna eat sumthin” man a nigga’s starvin like Marvin up in this bitch.” CJ replied clutching at a hot apple pie. “Man yall still shoulda gave me a few more minutes with that punk ass man” I was just about to get all oral on his ass!!!” As the words passed his lips Roland’s eyes grew twice in size. “I DIDN’T MEAN IT LIKE THAT!” “Suuuurrrrreeee!” the squad replied tearing away at the sandwich boxes. June Bug added while chomping down on a burger, “We should go be all Affirmative at Denny’s tomorrow” a nigga is craving a Grand Slam breakfast!” “And you know this?” Roland sang out and everyone replied in unison. “Maaaaannnnnn!” The AAMU was silent as each man stuffed his face like they were still in prison? err on vacation. The tunes still blared but miraculously they could still hear each other speak. Roland stuffed back a Big Mac and held his stomach, “Mm mm just like moms used ta make.” “You said it homie,” agreed June Bug, “Yo Ro-Ro who we bustin up dis week?” “Man I don’t even remember, some dudes.” He replied sinking back into the massage feature on the seat. CJ leaned forward in the gap between the seats, “Man what kinda managa is you”! Don’t even know who we beatin on dis week!” “Do you know,” he snapped jumping out of the cozy spot on his seat. “Huh, huh, HUH! I DID-ENT think so!” “Man bump you.” CJ replied backing off into the low decibels. “Do it matta nigga”!” Roland erupted looking back, “Do it really matta who them fools put out there”! It don’t to me! Cause I know dese niggas right here gon handle it straight up!” June Bug tossed out, “Yeah well that cracka White punked us last week.” “Man bump that we gon get our paper?” Ro-Ro assured. “You got a plan?” CJ inquired. “I got a plan...”
