AXEL In Time, Chapter V: A Wound That Only Time Can Heal, Part III(Finale)

16 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 16, 2009 Some people believe that time can heal all wounds, but what happens when those wounds are caused by time, itself? What happens when a painful event or hurtful moment cuts so deep that it scrapes the proverbial bone? In life, there are some wounds that never heal, no matter how much time passes or how many times you try to stitch them up. Everyone goes through life, expecting to get a fair shake at everything they do or attempt to accomplish, but they never get it; nor will they ever. To put it simply: Life is not and never will be fair. It’s a realization that doesn’t occur to most people until it’s too late, and by the time it hits, heartache and disappointment have already overcome. They say that life is ripe with disappointment. Who are “they”, though? Are “they” the same being or beings who decide what’s right, proper, fair or just? Maybe they are, maybe they aren’t. One thing is for certain- more often than not, fate rears its ugly head, and when it does, it’s usually bad news. This has quite often been the case for AXEL Action over the past few months. Life has defecated on him so many times throughout his romp through time that his aura absolutely reeks of it. Between getting practically torn to shreds by thorn bushes in ancient China, to nearly breaking his neck tumbling down a sand dune in Egypt, he’s had it quite rough. On top of that, he ended up stranded in Old England for what felt like years, got tossed into the dungeons alongside common criminals and a fellow revolutionary. Just when he thought life couldn’t get any worse for him, it did. Why those beings who decide what’s ‘fair’ chose to deal him such a poor hand in life, he’ll never know. On the day that he’s scheduled to be executed for ‘heresy’, the love of his life comes to the rescue and literally pulls his head from the chopping block. Soon after, the all-too-familiar time vortex appears and AXEL makes his escape, thinking his pregnant wife-to-be is behind him, only to find that she stayed behind. Heartbroken and alone, he’s spent the last six weeks attempting to make his way back to his dearest Alysea. There’s an old saying that says something along the lines of, “The night is darkest just before the dawn.” If that’s true, then things should start looking up for The Unholy One, right? Well, when we last left our protagonist, he had just stepped through the time vortex that was to lead him out of the hellhole that is New York City, circa 1921 and back to Old England and into the arms of his lover. Having read that with a metallic object (preferably steel) large enough, he can divert and control the flow of time, but what he failed to do is read the fine print. While the aforementioned metallic object will allow him to divert the flow of time and go somewhere else other than where he was previously destined, it doesn’t allow him to control where he will end up. Therein lies the problem that will, no doubt, plague the immediate future. While he knows he won’t end up in the original destination, he doesn’t have a clue where he will be when he exits. He simply hopes for the best and travels onward. The first thing he notices as he looks around is that the inside of the portal is pulsing, almost like a strobe light. The portal itself usually resonates in a dark blue or purple color, but contrary to the norm, it radiates a dark, almost evil red glow. As he traverses the glowing path, he can feel his molecules being shifted around, causing his legs to buckle underneath him. As a sharp, stinging sensation spreads from his midsection throughout the rest of his body, he clutches at himself and cries out in agony. “GYYYYAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHH!" His body wracked with pain, AXEL manages to stagger to his feet, making his way forward. The pain grows within him, stabbing at his insides like a thousand tiny knives, causing him to clench his teeth and fists in an attempt to ignore it. The end of the vortex, which is usually nice and wide at the end, begins growing smaller and smaller, signifying that it’s closing. The worst part for AXEL is that it’s closing at an abnormally rapid rate, and his only hope for getting out is to make a dash for it. Taking a deep breath, he quickly gathers himself and sprints toward the end of the time tunnel. The faster he moves, the sharper the pain gets. The closer he gets to the end of the vortex, the smaller the exit gets. His pace slows to a near halt as the pain becomes almost unbearable; but he knows that if he stops, he will be trapped in limbo forever. Fighting through the pain, he struggles to the exit, and just as it’s drawing to a complete close, he makes a dive for it. Shrouded in a blinding, white light, he braces himself for the worst. As the light envelops him, the searing pain his body vanishes as if it were never there. He finds himself surrounded by darkness now, his eyes unable to adjust to its pitch black recesses. Flailing his arms about, trying to find anything that might seem familiar or even a way out, he slowly trudges through the darkness. “Is this what it’s like to be blind?”, he thinks to himself as he stumbles around in the dark, seemingly trapped in wherever the vortex dumped him out at. Suddenly, the bottom drops out from under him, so to speak, and he feels as if he’s falling. Farther and farther he falls, plummeting at breakneck speed down what appears to be a bottomless pit within the darkness. He tries to scream, but not even a whimper escapes his lips. He’s soon overwhelmed with a feeling of helplessness; that same helpless feeling he felt the night his parents were pronounced dead. Giving himself over to the fear that was welling up inside of him; he accepts his fate and doesn’t bother fighting it. As he allows the darkness to swallow him up, the faint sound of a ringing phone echoes within his ears. The farther he drops, the louder it gets until finally, he awakens with a start, sitting up in a cold sweat on his kitchen floor. He shakes his head, taking deep breaths in an attempt to gather himself. The phone continues its incessant ringing, causing AXEL to take a good look at his surroundings. “No… It can’t be!” As the phone rings, the situation becomes clearer and clearer to him. Instead of dropping him off in Old England like he was hoping, the rift had brought him back to his own time. As that realization hits, AXEL breaks into tears. He finally figures out that all the hours he spent researching, and the months he spent traversing the past in an attempt to return to his one true love were all for naught. The phone eventually stops ringing and the caller on the other end of the line is soon met with the answering machine. “Hello. You’ve reached the residence of The Unholy One. Leave a message at the tone, unless your name is Big D. In which case, just hang up. Thank you.” Beeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeep! “Hey, AXEL, it’s James. Where have you been, man? I’ve been trying to get up with you for hours! Listen, I’ve scored us a date for tonight with these two smokin’ hot chicks. One of them is Asian, has big tits and says she’s a big fan of yours. Personally, I think she wants to jump your bone, if you catch my drift. Hit me up and let me know if you’re interested. I’m out.” Click! He blinks as that message rings out over the silence in the room, the sound almost ear-shatteringly loud. It’s at that point he understands the predicament he is and truly begins to realize that there is no going back. However, when denial hits, it hits hard and nothing can stop it. “I’ve GOT to find a way back to her! She can’t do this to me… It can’t end like this!” Pulling himself to his feet, he braces against the kitchen table, trying to steady himself. His legs are obviously still quite weak from his previous excursion through that swirling vortex of time. He makes his way, though rather shakily, over towards his pantry, opening the doors of it wide, only to find that there was nothing inside except the bag of chips he wanted in the first place. Making a mental note to go and stock up on food later, he begins to feel around within the cabinet, searching for anything that might resemble a way back to his precious Alysea. “Where is it? I know it was here!” His search becomes more and more desperate. He begins to go from cabinet to cabinet, snatching pots, pans, cleaning supplies and whatnot out of their proper places in hopes of finding another portal. With no sign of one in sight, he can feel his heart breaking into a million tiny pieces inside of his chest. Now knowing that there is truly no way to go back to where he was, or the way things were before, he sinks to his knees and slumps over in disappointment. Letting out a scream that echoes all through his house, he pounds his fists on the floor in anger. Nobody knows why time and fate are such cruel entities, or why they do what they do. Does time truly heal all wounds? Maybe. However, the wounds that AXEL Action now carries will remain inside of him for a long time, even if they are wounds that only time can possibly heal. Click! “James?” “Yeah, it’s me.” “About the date with those girls?” “Yeah?” “I’ll be there.” Click! It really is true what they say… Time waits for no one.
