This and That

26 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26 June 07 *Sorry it's so short but I was lacking the time this week!** "Is it really that hard to understand?" "Just a little bit." "Give me a break Alex will ya?" Of course I know better than that, Alex can be one stubborn son of a bitch. "C'mon Rob, we both know these things just happen." "They shouldn't to me." Which is true of course, LeStatt Knight should not be going into any kind of a slump ... not even a mini one like the one I've just seemed to run into. I've been doing this job for far too long and I know that I am far better than what I have been showing. Vivica Valentine and Chris fucking Storm can't even dream of achieving the kind of greatness that I stare at in the mirror every damn day. And yet I've lost to both of them. Talk about a way to get my damn blood boiling. "Look man you do this to yourself all the time, try and pick apart your game whenever you lose ONE match." "It's not just the losing." I growl. "It's more than that." "Like what?" "Well..." I allow my voice to trail off because I'm not really sure if I want to share this information. Being a professional wrestler is all about displaying an image, one that may not even completely show who you really are. You cannot show weakness of any kind because then your opponents will pick at you like some fucking vultures. Not that I was ever very good at letting someone know exactly how I was feeling ... unless it was anger. "Well what?" Alex demands "You just have to-" "Fuck that man! Stop being a goddamn pussy and tell me why you have been bitching!" Now norrmally this is where I'd do one of three things. 1) I'd verbally rip him a new asshole 2) Rip his head off with my bare hands or 3) A combination of the first two NORMALLY that is. Alex Grey had gotten a strange sort of immunity to my wrath over the past couple of years, a far cry from when we first met each other years ago. "I just get the feeling that my time may be up." "and what the hell would bring you to that conclusion?" "Look Alex, nobody stays on top forever ... eventually people grow tired of you. Fans, management, reporters, media, all of them assholes. The public loves to see someone shoot up the ladder and become famous out of nowhere but there is one things that the public probably loves even more." "Tearing them down." Alex says matter of factly, not even missing a beat. "Exactly." I say in agreement. "But how does that make you feel?" I chuckle at this statement. "What are you my therapist now Alex?" He smirks. "Nah amigo but I'm being real here with you. Are you ready to throw in the towel?" "Give me a break! You know I'm not ready to call it a career yet ... I'm only 27 years old." "Then why worry?" "If you not worry some ... then you'll always lose." "You've never struck me as a guy who worried." "Just not publicly my friend but inside I worry each and everytime I step between those ropes. I mean you never can guess when your last time will be, anything can happen in those rings." I shake my head and move to the window of the hotel room that GCW set me up in. Great view I must say. "I dunno man," I begin. "What if GCW isn't the place for me anymore?" "Nonsense." "Excuse me?" "NONSENSE! GCW is where you belong and will always belong. Who else shines in GCW like LeStatt Knight?" "Perhaps Jordan White." The words came out of my mouth faster than I anticipated. A knowing look comes to Alex's face. "So that's it, is it?" "Is what it?" "You feel like you are slowly playing second fiddle to this new flavor of the week." "Don't be rid-" "Ridiculous? Please, I think you are being ridiculous if you try and deny any of this." I hate it when he is right but I hate it more when I fuck up and say what I didn't want to say. "Fine, maybe I do wonder exactly what they see in this kid. Sure Sage knows his shit but this "Jordan"? I beat something more powerful everytime Mary leaves town." Too strong of a joke? Eh Fuck it. "Problem is I can't prove that to anyone. All the people are looking at is that Jordan won the damn contendership and I didn't ... that he beat Vivica and I..." Even thinking about saying the words make me gag. So I just won't say them. "Yeah I get it." Alex says with a sigh. "Rob when did you become such a bitch?" ......... "Excuse me?" "A bitch Roberto, when did you become one?" "Check your damn tone "amigo"." "No, since when did you decide it was the cool thing to walk around moping and feeling sorry for yourself? When the fuck did you become all those morons that you claim to hate in this business? I thought you were a take charge kind of person." "That is who I am." "Then be that man." Alex says as his voice gets louder. "Nobody is going to feel sorry for you and nobody is going to help you win again. What you have to do is focus your energy one on person and one person only." "And then?" "Seek and fucking destroy." I like the sound of that ... SEEK and DESTROY. oh wait. FUCKING DESTROY! "One person you say?" "One person." Ladies and Gentlemen we have a winner. "That asshole Twizzy." "I figured you'd say that." "LeStatt Knight vs. Twizzy." I say outloud. "Sound good?" "Sounds like a massacre." Cockiness sure ... but neccessary. You want to know what else it makes me think of? GCW. The legendary GCW. And make no mistake, that's what this shall be. GCW Legend vs. GCW Legend. Let the games begin.
