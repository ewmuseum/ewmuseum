Cursed object plays on one mans mind....

28 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/28/10 When by thy scorn, O murd'ress, I am dead, And that thou thinkst thee free From all solicitation from me, Then shall my ghost come to thy bed, And thee, feign'd vestal, in worse arms shall see : Then thy sick taper will begin to wink, And he, whose thou art then, being tired before, Will, if thou stir, or pinch to wake him, think Thou call'st for more, And, in false sleep, will from thee shrink : And then, poor aspen wretch, neglected thou Bathed in a cold quicksilver sweat wilt lie, A verier ghost than I. What I will say, I will not tell thee now, Lest that preserve thee ; and since my love is spent, I'd rather thou shouldst painfully repent, Than by my threatenings rest still innocent. By John Donne An unknown location..... A mist roll across the countryside through the hills. A river runs through the valley and a short distance northwards upon an outcropping is a blazing bonfire. The stars twinkle above in the night sky, but yet you are drawn to the fire. The fire before you blazes into the atmosphere releasing little sparks high into the night sky. Behind this huge flame sits a large man eyes narrowed down studying the flames. No emotion he just observes. Raising an arm slowly he waves his hand through the fires edges. Speaking slowly, but clearly the Native American man holds a natures talisman. “It has reawakened and found a new victim, this man cannot hide behind it. He cannot hide near it for it will always find him. He has tried and tried to get rid of it and yet fails at every turn.” His hand pulls backwards and then strikes forward opening as a sudden flash of light as the flames erupt higher and the smoke whisping into many different colors and shapes. “Chase the shadows warrior for you have become the creature of prey as the hunter continues to stalk you. You cannot run nor hide. The dream is no safe haven for it is also home to the beast.” The large man reveals himself to be Andrew Whitecloud as he raises a dead snake holding it forward for all to see. “You must flee little man for the beast has found your trail. I can only warn you as it comes and attempts to feed off your emotions ultimately swallowing you whole.” Shaman laughs a deep laughter which reverberates around the surrounding hills and valleys. Section 1......11:48 pm Salem Massachusetts..... Triumph Frost tosses and turns in his bed, he suddenly bolts upright screaming outloud. Raising his hands he rubs his face. Turning he reaches his hand over to touch where Alexis should be sleeping. Suddenly he pulls his hand back wet he observes it closely and rubs it between his fingers, but cannot tell what the liquid is. He leans over and turns on the light as he is shocked by the sight of blood upon his hands. He leaps from the bed looking all around as blood is splattered everywhere. In a panic he begins opening doors and screaming out here name. “ALEXIS WHERE ARE YOU??” In a panic he grabs the phone to find no dial tone. Looking around the room everything is covered in blood splatter, everything, but the hardcore title belt. He grabs it and slings it through the window shattering the glass outwards onto the street below. Slowly he walks towards the window and looks out. Down below he sees nothing, the title is gone. He turns and looks around the room as the blood is gone as well. Alexis sleeps in the bed peacefully undisturbed by the commotion he just caused. Turning he goes to look out the window again as the glass is fully intact and unbroken. The hardcore title still remains where he had left it before he threw it. Rubbing his eyes he looks around concerned. Shaking his head a bit he heads into the bathroom and runs some water and washes his face. Reaching tot he side he grabs a towel and pats his face dry. He raises his eyes slowly to the mirror where Andrew Whitecloud is seen looking back at him. “I told you be prepared for the beast has found you, Now run little warrior nobody is safe around you any longer.” Quickly rubbing his eyes he looks back at the mirror only showing an image of himself looking back. “I have got to be losing my damn mind.” He turns quietly and heads back to bed as a shadow passes his window and stops. The shadow looks in and simply watches and stares over at the hardcore title lying upon the chair. The shadow whispers low watching “The monster will soon reveal itself and when it does all hell will break lose, be warned little warrior.” Section 2...Within the dream again..... That was when I hit the fucker. I hit him hard and square on the bridge of the nose; with a hammer, and watched in delight as his funny, laughing face caved in and gushed red-black blood. I smiled, knowing that I had finally killed him. And that was when he started laughing at me, his wicked painted face melding back together from the blow I'd dealt him. Giggling as I let out a deep sigh and reached for the hacksaw amidst the pile of torture implements scattered on the table. I remember walking into the hardware section of Wal-Mart, sweating under those phosphorescent lights as I turned a corner and faced a thousand Mimes smiling deviantly from the windows of their bright toy boxes. Out of the corners of my eyes I even saw them turn their tiny heads and grin at me through little pairs of fangs as I walked past them. Fuck, I hate them! Ever since I was a kid, I was all too aware of the truth behind mimes.  I was five and my parents had let me stay up late to watch Halloween with Michael Myers on T.V. You know, the one with the guy and the white painted face? Yeah that one fucking Mayhem all over again now. I took the hacksaw in my hand and pondered over the little guy bound in the tiny red plastic chair where he had been drinking blood next to the Native American figure. Fucking mimes! Forget what you think you know about mimes.  Never mind that Native American, he's He is fucked up in his own way, but he doesn't matter much; but mimes .... They sleep during the day and wake up when everyone is asleep. French ones, English ones (Those really suck), Irish, African or whatever, the whole lot of them come to life at night like the little vampires they really are. Forget the hacksaw! They walk around houses and streets at night doing god knows what! I think they even have their own social system. I plug in the skill-saw, open the throttle full bore and taunt the blade at mime's face as I grin over him. "Now you're really gonna get it!" I cackle. Like a man lost at see with no other to talk with I begin to threaten the toy mime. Bringing about terror in an emotionless face. I put the saw up to mime's neck and he starts to scream as the blade rips through his chin and bright red, blood mingled, pink plastic sawdust sprays the room and I grin as mimes's head falls off the bloody pink stump of his neck onto the floor. “Just like what I did to my sisters dolls when I was a child.” I begin to laugh a bit and just simply drop the saw. "Take that, pal!" I laugh, and downstairs I go to grab a cold one after a hard day's work. I slump in my chair and click on the tube to watch baseball, but quickly change it to "In Search Of ..." hosted by "Spock" from Star Trek when a loud noise is heard. The damn mime is up and walking again. This time dragging the hardcore title with it. I turn and run, but it is as if I am running through a field of crude oil. Thick as all get out, I cannot move as I struggle to run, but the mime is right behind me. No gain for me as it comes closer and closer. Section 3....12:02 merely minutes since awakening the first time..... Sitting up in a heavy sweat he turns and gazes at the clock. He turns and looks at Alexis once again struggling to make sense of these weird nightmares and visions. Should he have actually listened to that lunatic Shaman. Spinning himself, he sits on the edge of the bed. He raises his eyes and looks at the hardcore title once again. His eyes staring at it almost as if it was telling him something. Standing suddenly he walks over to it and opens the closet door nearby. He reaches down grabbing the hardcore title and simply tosses it onto the floor shutting the door behind it. Turning he walks back over to the bed and lays back down. He stares at the ceiling for a few more minutes and drifts back off to sleep. Section 4....Morning this time perhaps?? What a lovely day! I have spent all the morning lying on the grass in front of my house, under the enormous plantain tree which covers and shades and shelters the whole of it. I like this part of the country; I am fond of living here because I am attached to it by deep roots, the profound and delicate roots which attach a man to the soil on which his ancestors were born and died, to their traditions, their usages, their food, the local expressions, the peculiar language of the peasants, the smell of the soil, the hamlets, and to the atmosphere itself. I love the house in which I grew up. From my windows I can see the bay, which flows by the side of my garden, on the other side of the road, almost through my grounds, the great and wide river of whom which flows into the bay. Staring through the window like a portal to my childhood I watch the lobster boats come in and out. On the left, down yonder, lies Gadsden the town across the river, the twin sister town, perhaps the richer and uglier twin sister who turns her blind eye to this town. What a delicious morning it was! About eleven o'clock, a long line of boats drawn by a steam-tug, as big a fly, and which scarcely puffed while emitting its thick smoke, passed my docks down on the river. After two deep sea fishing boats, whose red flags fluttered toward the sky, there came a magnificent wooden sail boat; it was perfectly white and wonderfully clean and shining. I saluted it, I hardly know why, except that the sight of the vessel gave me great pleasure. I have had a slight feverish attack for the last few hours, and I feel ill, or rather I feel low-spirited. Whence come those mysterious influences which change our happiness into discouragement, and our self-confidence into diffidence? One might almost say that the air, the invisible air, is full of unknowable Forces, whose mysterious presence we have to endure. I am broken from my trance as I am suddenly drawn to the river. I am beckoned by some unseen force. I slowly walk down there and yet once again I cannot argue nor resist the urge to follow. I am like a moth to flame and this is confusing unto me. I am stronger than this and yet I cannot seem to escape myself. As I approach the green rivers edge I see a glint of shining gold. I stare into the water and the fear of what I see causes me to take several steps back. My eyes are deceiving me, my own damned eyes, this cannot be real. I force myself to look once again and yet it is as real as anything to me. There lies my beautiful Alexis with the hardcore title around her neck holding her down underneath the flowing river. I can see her eyes open in fear, her hands outstretched straining to reach the surface. Yet she is gone all the life in her has been drained from her as if the river itself is simply taking her life force away. I reach my hand into the water to attempt to pull her out. Suddenly the hard leather strap of the title coils up my arm and pulls me into the water. The weight and strain of this title is pulling me down deeper and deeper. Beyond the realm of consciousness. This murdering intent is consuming within the cold deep darkness. I begin to struggle trying to break free. Only thing that ever holds me down is the cold glinting gold and leather belt. It has taken everything from me now as I struggle to wake up. Please somebody wake me and free me from this watery doom. I need to wake as I sink lower and lower into the darkness. Section 5.....5:03 am sun rising through the window..... Suddenly water splashes upon his face as he sits up gasping for air. Alexis stands over Frost with a glass of water in her had. He looks at her as he sits there looking at her. He rubs his face speaking. “Why did you splash water on my face?” Alexis looks at him as she points at the hardcore title. “You have been fighting that object for 15 minutes.” Triumph looks at it and then throws it across the room. He remembers specifically throwing it into the closet and shutting the door. He stands up grabbing a pair of pants and hastily gets dressed. Alexis grabs the title and throws it into a bag as they both hurry out of the building. Alexis runs across the sidewalk and opens the car door sitting down. Frost steps to the other side of the car reaching for a handle as a large hand grabs his wrist and twists it around. Andrew Whitecloud stands there looking him in the eyes smiling. “I warned you to beware the arrival of the beasts, They have come and they all want the fools reward.” Snatching his arm away Frost jumps in the car starting her and burning rubber to get out of town. He looks in the rear view mirror for Andrew Whitecloud, but sees nothing. He stops the car suddenly and gets out looking all around. Nothing and nobody is there, just the town and its citizens. He wonders if this was a dream or nightmare as he gets back into the car and drives off. In the back seat the bag slowly drips water as the gold glints through the barely zipped top. The hardcore title awaits for another opportunity to apply its curse once again. Will it get its first taste of blood or will it simply continue to follow its current master through the shadows and haunt his every waking moment.
