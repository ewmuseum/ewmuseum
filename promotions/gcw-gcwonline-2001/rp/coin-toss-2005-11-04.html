Coin Toss

4 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/04/2005 We open on the classic rental that Styles has spent on his money on for Varsity to drive around the city. Apparently, we’re in Minnesota by now, one can tell that just by the weather that’s out there for this November morning. Varsity’s in the driver’s sear, one hand on the wheel and the other hand on his cell phone. He wheels around a corner, then slides right off down the street, in the general direction of something or other. But that’s not the important part. The important part is the phone conversation... “Styles... look, I don’t care, man. So what if he’s talkin” about me. I don’t care. That’s not what I’m here for and that’s not what I’m about. You know this...” Varsity wheels around another corner, pulling up to the stoplight and relaxing for a moment. You know how red lights can be sometimes. Takes forever to get the green and then when you get the green it only lasts for like ten seconds. Don’t you just hate those lights? “Huh” Haha, no I don’t need any snaps about his mother. I’m gonna? play this straight. Words don’t win matches, Styles. Skills, determination and heart do. Just like football. All I have to do is go in, kick ass, score, and then I’m on my way. The stunt with Mike Savige set me back, yeah. That was a major reality check. But now? Now I know how this game works. I know how this game is played. And now? Now it’s time for me to get some points on the board.” The light finally changes and Varsity pulls right on through. He cruises for a good while, before flipping on his signal and pulling into the circle of his hotel. He pulls right up to the door, kicks open the door and climbs out. He immediately heads to the back, pulling open the trunk and snatching out his duffle bag. As he makes his way towards the hotel entrance, he shakes hands with the valet, slipping him a twenty, and then heads right on inside. “Alright, look. Just... okay, okay. I’ll say a few words. But I’m not gonna” blast the guy. Just set it up. I’m at the hotel now, so I’ll call you back after I get all checked in. Alright? Cool. Later.” Finally! Varsity manages to get the phone conversation ended. He makes his way up to the short line, waiting patiently for his turn at the check in counter. It takes a couple of people and some discount coupons before Varsity gets up there. He approaches with a smile, which gets significantly broader as he notices the cutie behind the counter. Her name tag says Tiffani and that’s all Varsity needed to know. “Alexander Winters. It’s probably reserved under Styles though...” Tiffani smiles, letting her fingers pass over the keyboard and she nods. “Gotcha” right here, Mr. Winters. Looks like you’ll be in one of our suites tonight. Will you be needing anything else?” Tiffani slides his keycard across the counter top. Varsity snatches up his keycard, flipping it around in his fingers and he shakes his head. “Nah, I think I’m good. Thanks.” And with that, he turns to head off towards the elevators. Tiffani, though, has other plans for the wrestling rookie. “Good luck, tomorrow.” Varsity’s foot slips as he comes to a halt in his steps. Blinking, he turns around and peers over at the girl behind the counter. “Huh?” Confusion has definitely set in. Tiffani chuckles a little bit and explains, “Your match. Tomorrow. Good luck with it.” Varsity continues to stare. Apparently, he’s not used to being recognized in such a capacity. After all, he’s only had one match and it didn’t make as many waves as he thought it would. So either Styles has been running his mouth again or... this girl’s actually a fan. Tiffani smiles and explains even further, “My little brother’s a big fan of the GCW. I watch it with him sometimes. I remember you from a couple weeks ago.” Varsity smiles some, finally, turning to head back towards the counter. “Oh. Heh, well thanks. Hopefully, I can get the job done this time. My match with The Machine” Brutal. There’s a reason he went ten in a row.” Tiffani smiles and shrugs some. “Yeah. I guess. But it’s not always about what you can do and what you can’t do. It’s more of... I think it’s more of what’s on the inside that counts. You know” It’s kind of like dating. You meet someone. You think you know what they’re all about, because they have a nice smile and the softest eyes you’ve seen in a long time...” Tiffani’s words seem to be a little distracted as she speaks, considering her gaze on Varsity. “.. but then you realize, that everything you think you know” Is probably so far from the truth... there’s no need for a second date.” Varsity can tell where she’s going with this. Why? Because he’s thinking the same thing and he’s gazing the same way. It’s not his fault. She’s pretty. He’s always been attracted to pretty. It’s like... Kryptonite. “That’s why you get to know the other person first. You can’t go into every match thinking you’re going to win. Because that’s how you lose. Confidence is one thing, cockiness is another.” Varsity shifts the weight of his bag onto his shoulder some more. “Me, personally” I’m confident. I know that when the bell rings, I’m gonna? be out there, giving two hundred percent. Why? Because that’s who I am. I always go the distance. If I don’t try my hardest, then I’ve failed, regardless of the scoreboard.” He brings the flirting back around full circle. “The same with dating. If I see someone I want... I go for it. I know that the odds are likely stacked against me. I know that she’s very likely got a boyfriend. But I don’t care. If something inside is telling me to go for it... then I go for it.” Tiffani blinks, fluttering those innocent eyelashes for a brief moment, before she realizes she’s probably making a small fool out of herself. “Heh... yeah. Go for it.” She shrugs a bit, tucking her hair behind her ear and then just standing there. “I should... probably head on up now.” Varsity utters, mostly just to break the awkward silence that happens when there’s silent flirting and gazing going on. “Big day tomorrow and all.” Tiffani just nods and offers a little finger wiggle to the wrestler. “You’ll do fine, Rookie. You’ll do fine. I’ll be at home, cheering you on.” Varsity backs his way towards the elevators, pressing the button and stepping onto them. “Oh” And what about your little brother? Who’s his favorite superstar?” This is asked with a slightly smug tone, as he’s pretty sure it’s going to be him. “LeStatt Knight, of course.” Varsity blinks, jaw dropping in slight disbelief, as the elevator doors close and the scene fades right out.
