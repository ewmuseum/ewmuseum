A day in reality Part 1 *The Beginning*

6 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/06/11 The scene before you is a somber scene. Sitting in a conference room Shaman otherwise known as Andrew Whitecloud owner of High Spirits Casino in Las Vegas, Nevada. He also is known as a professional wrestling superstar currently with GCW, But today is about something much more important to him and his businesses. The economy of the country has literally ransacked all types of business including those who are high end rollers in the money market. Sitting there you can see the serious looks upon everyone's faces at the table. Rubbing his chin, he looks around the table waiting for someone to speak. Frowning as nobody is looking up, just staring down at the papers and laptops before them. Slamming the papers down on the table suddenly standing he speaks loudly. “I did not bring everyone up here for silence and no options. Stand up come over here and look down there and tell me what you see. Be truthful and honest. I want honesty above all else.” He motions for everyone to stand up and come over to the window and look out on the casino floor. Several of the executives stand up and walk to the window. One of the younger ones speaks up looking out the window. “I see profit and margins of error at a very low percentage. I see each person giving us a piece of their pie.” looking at the young man he shakes his head. Looking to the right he notices a woman looking at the crowd of people, she is paying particular attention to how people are shaking there heads near the bars and the slot machines. She slowly brings her gaze down to the floor and observes the people coming and going and the looks on their faces. “Carey what do you see?” Looking at her expecting an answer. She turns her head up and looks at him. “I see us missing our mission objective our core values.” “Exactly Carey and now in this day and the happenings over the last two years within our economic structure we need to do something.” Clearing his throat he waves his hand towards the table. “Everyone sit back down it is time for us to begin putting our heads together to get this right.” Spinning his chair quickly he sits down resting his arms across the back rest as he leans forward. Looking at everyone on the table his eyes focus on the table itself. He imagines the situation at hand and looks up preparing to speak as the younger executive raises his voice again stepping in and speaking. “Sir I think we should downsize the staff and cut expenses in every department. Up revenue and bring about a new company wide policy of loss prevention and singular accountability.” The young man sits back and places his hands behind his head looking on with a cocky smirk on his face. Andrew reaches up and rubs his temple, slowly he turns his head and stares at the young kid. “So where did you get your degree? “he looks over at him curiously. “that is right your name is Marcus so lets get to brass tacks here, tell me about yourself Marcus.” Marcus stands up straightening his jacket out as he speaks up. “I graduated from USC and had a 4.0 GPA. I applied here and got the job of Junior Executive. Have brought about results by eliminating the security staff and bringing in a private firm to take over the the day to day security of the casino.” Flipping and envelope across the table towards Marcus, he leans back and taps the folder. “First and foremost I personally removed your security firm on grounds of false information provided and non sign off from my personal pen. Also knowingly bringing in a contractor that is related to you is a conflict of interest. The security you fired and terminated have been rehired and the salaries lost due to time off for them has been deducted from your salary.” Tapping the folder he shakes his head. “That is your compensation and your severance pay. You are fired from Shaman Works Inc. for good. You are to be relieved of duty officially and officially your position is being merged with Carey's. Oh on an official note Carey's lawyers will be contacting you with paperwork on sexual harassment. You should think before you do, you will always get caught.” Pointing to the door he looks at Marcus. “Leave before I Call Bruno and throw your ass out. I am tired of executives taking initiatives in any company and lining their own pockets with my money. If I find anyone else doing so they better just leave now, I am watching you all.” Watching Marcus leave through the door slamming it shut. His face of anger is easily shown. As the door slowly shuts the atmosphere in the room returns to the somber feeling. Sitting back he rubs his chin and stands up slowly. He reaches over and pulls down the screen which is written on. “I am going to take a drastic approach to changes that are necessary. You each know we have to make some sacrifices to make sure we reach our goals. So honestly I think we need to not cut jobs. First the bonuses must go, you each get paid well enough to not have to worry about bonuses. The way I have things calculated the bonuses will keep us from laying anyone off.” He turns and looks down at the floor below, His eyes focus on everything he can see. He sighs a bit as he looks down. “I hate doing this, but cut the costs on a lot of things as well, we don't need to charge 7 dollars for a cheeseburger and fries. Make it affordable and compare everyone's rates and beat them.” Rubbing his fingertips together he smiles slightly. “This will workout fine folks, have some faith and work hard at making things right. We will survive without losing any jobs.” Walking around the conference room Andrew looks at everyone individual. Smiling he looks about and nods his head in satisfaction. “Meeting adjourned, have a good evening and will see you Thursday afternoon.” As each person departs he sits down at his desk in the office near the conference room. Looking through the double doors he simply watches. Just another day in the life of Shaman he thinks to himself.
