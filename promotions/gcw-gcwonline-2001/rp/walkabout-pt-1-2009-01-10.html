Walkabout Pt. 1

10 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 10th 2009 Inside an old amusement park somewhere outside a small town in Pennsylvania. All of the buildings are beginning to show age and wear and the rides beginning to rust and show its decay. A lone figure walks behind the bushes and overgrown shrubbery. Weaving in and out of the world beyond, just out of eye sight. Kind of wishing to be revealed and yet not wanting to be revealed just yet. The shadows of the park seem to take a life of their own when the breeze kicks up in the silence. This place once so full of laughter and enjoyment and has fallen to corporate greed and larger money making parks and concessions. Silently the large person in the background comes walking around a building and shakes his head. He turns and looks towards a warped mirror and grins as he sees the distended version of himself. Shaman enjoys oddities in life. These mirrors were one of those things that brought him great pleasure when he was a child. Reaching out he touches the mirror. Looking at it as if it holds and answer for him somehow. "Sad when such a thing is forgotten. It is sad when a many things begin to be forgotten. Victims forget the terror of seeing their attackers for the first time and then they forget that they are always vulnerable at anytime and moment. It is that initial fear in someones eyes that makes the predator leaps and attack. It is the hesitation and fear that makes them vulnerable and incapable of reacting to defend. When they react it is too late. Who is it that i fear?" Tapping the mirror one last time he drops his hand and continues to look about the mirrors edges. Taking his hand he turns it to the side and runs it along the edge while watching the mirror. The edges metal frame is rusted and shows through the white rust stained paint. He breaks a few large chips off and holds them in his hand as he simply looks at the and then turns his hand over dropping them as the fall to the ground in an erratic pattern through the air. He turns and walks to the bumper car area. He turns and looks around as he steps over the rail. He looks about and makes his way to the center of the building. He stops and looks around looking at the scrapes and scratches of the building where the bumper cars zoomed back and forth and hit each other with force. "This place where so much controlled violence took place. The place where race car drivers and all sorts of daredevils were born. A place where folks could release road rage legally. Enjoying the changing aggressions year after year. Where adults and child were on equal terms. Much like a video game you can be the best at it, but you never enjoy it more than when you play it regardless of how powerful you are. Perhaps a lesson must be taught to those who forget who they are dealing with." Shaman turns and walks down the main pathway through the center of the park. His eyes going from right to left as he stops in front of the arcade area. He enters by pushing a broken door open. No machines are in this room. A few painted posters and advertisements upon the wall, but nothing remaining of the old arcade games. Shaman walks over and looks at a Galaga and Centipede arcade game painting on the wall. "Classics much like i have been told i am. I am 34 years old and far from over the hill. I will hurt opponents for a long time to come. Yet people forget that Mayhem and myself have become a force. Win or lose we do what is necessary and deliver the painful reminders to those who forget. We are Oblivion. Masters of our own fates and delivers of pain to those who forget. Like the old high scores of the old video games. Guys like ASS, FU2, MNM, MAS, the list can go on and on. Hard to forget those guys who had their names on top of those lists for a longtime masters of each of their games. We are the masters of our game. Nobody will come in and interfere with that game. We make sure of it." Walking to the end of the path he turns and eyes the gates as he steps between the falling fences. He then turns and walks out to a suburban nearby. He opens the door and climbs in. Looking forward at the abandoned amusement park he shrugs as his cell phone rings. He picks up the cellphone and answers it. "Yeah am heading to Centralia next. I want to see what is going on there. I had heard stories and history of the mine fire, but i want to see it first hand. I want to see why this place has turned to hell on earth. Mans mistakes had caused it and i want to go there and see why this mistake has made things rather dangerous for other and the nature around it. The time to figure out the problems are now. GCW is much like this amusement park. Each wrestler there has abandoned the base emotions and realities that is who they will always be. I am the one who will open this path again for all to see." Reaching forward he turns the key and starts the truck up. He clicks the cellphone shut as he reverses the truck. He goes to accelerate, but stops as he looks up at the Farris wheel in the amusement park. "Looking through the hour glass once again has me consuming the thoughts that are best forgotten. Oblivion has come for those forgetting who we are. Yet i have to travel in this manner as it is bringing me to the path of conclusion. It allows me to find a certain answer i have been searching for. Sooner or later those who have forgotten why i am me will soon learn the truth. There is a goal in the GCW that i will achieve regardless of the outcome. I will thrive with the challenges laid out before me. My brother in arms Bryan Mayhem and our brother in pain Kimbusa have unfinished business. Many people enjoyed getting in our way, Now that business is going to be paid for in full." The tires screech as Shaman speeds out of the old abandoned parking lot of the Amusement park as he tears out onto the road and drives over a hill disappearing.
