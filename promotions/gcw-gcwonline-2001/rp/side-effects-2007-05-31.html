Side Effects

31 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/31/07 Davis opened the pill bottle and poured four tablets into his right hand. The tablets are plain white with an inscribed T for Twistodium. Plain Twistodium. In the past Twistodium has come in many different flavors: peach, lemon, egg yolk. Each has its own distinct side effects. For example, with the cherry flavored tablet large bruises form around the eyes of the user and he then cannot feel anything in his face. The entire face, from scalp to adam’s apple becomes numb to pain or pleasure. As you can see, some side effects can be quite useful. Others can be detrimental. The egg yolk Twistodium was created because when Twistodium is mixed with eggs or egg yolk (but not egg whites alone) there is a burst of adrenaline. However, the egg yolk Twistodium became harsh and a sharp pain in the rib cage of the user would be present until the tablet and high wore off. They were taken off of the market within weeks. Plain Twistodium seems to give the user some sort of super strength and endurance. It also makes the user happy and high like marijuana, but also lucid, euphoric and crazy like LSD or the Venro Plant. It is Davis? favorite. Orange Twistodium is the closest drug, legal or otherwise, to what is described by Shakespeare as Red Death. “Plain?” she asks. Davis looked up at his girlfriend Janine. She lay on his bed, her voice lilting over everything lying on the floor. Books, clothes, wrappers. She was carried over all of them and her voice smacked him in the face and washed into his ears. He nodded. Janine held out her hand and he placed two pills in her palm. She swallowed them with one gulp, no chewing. Those were his first words to her before giving her the first of her many tablets two weeks ago. No chewing! Davis himself now popped two pills into his mouth. Delicious. The best part about Twistodium and the reason he was more prominent in taking it while wrestling is because it gives him, for lack of a better term, super powers. He should be washed up. He’s old enough to be some athlete’s father and yet it’s possible that he’ll be competing for the GCW World Title soon. The problem is finding the right method. Two lemon for speed, one plain for strength, and one raspberry for focus? Maybe for Jordan, but then Valentine or Knight could be utterly different! “Twice in one night!” he shouted without even realizing it. “Hmm?” He shook some cobwebs out of his head, but the doubled immediately. “I have to wrestle two wrestlers in one night! Who” Jordan, but then who? Jordan I can take. I will for sure if there’s no unforeseen circumstances, baby. Oh baby. But damn. Hot damn! I mean, cold damn! What the parliament fuckadelic am I going to do about Knight or VJ?” “You’ll figure something out.” She was too high to care. Davis was freaking out. Paranoid! “I shouldn’t be paranoid!” Davis knows the side effects to every Twistodium type like the back of his moustache, which doesn’t actually exist. Not anymore anyway. Now it’s a beard! Blue beard, black beard, red beard, pink beard. Oh shit? Twizzy looked into the pill bottle and saw one of his worst dreads: mixed pills. “Did you mix these pills up?” “Hmm?” said Janine. “I” yes. I took all the ones on the table and put them in one bottle. I thought we’d lose them.” Davis screamed. “What did I just take” Two pink? One purple, one orange? Shit! Shit, shit, shit! I’m freaking out, god damn, baby. God damn!” Janine was drifting so far off that she didn’t even care. Sleep was coming on. She got a white and a red. Lucid and dreamy. That was that mixture. Every Twistodium tablet has a side effect and if you mix some up they become even different. It’s an interesting concept and a fascinating formula. Like a heroin addict that needs to find the right combination of Maxwell’s Silver Hammer to get the perfect high Twizzy needs to find the perfect assortment and mixture of Twistodium tablets to defeat opponents now. Relying on his skill and muscle and experience alone won’t cut it. Not anymore. Four purple, one white, three violet, two red, six silver, five gold, nine orange, four magenta, one perrywinkle and nineteen egg yolk. Knight? how can you possibly beat Knight with that kind of combination? Twizzy’s head was moving so fast a hummingbird couldn’t keep up. “I! I! I need to sit down!” Twizzy sat in the corner, knees brought up to his chest, hugging his shins and rocking back and forth. Memories of chronicles past. At least his bedroom is warmer than an alley. Even if it is more cluttered. One red, two silver, nine lemon? Four green, one purple, ninety-two orange? Twenty gold, one bronze, half a bacon? What’s the combination to beat Both Jordan and possibly Valentine or Knight? The world may never know.
