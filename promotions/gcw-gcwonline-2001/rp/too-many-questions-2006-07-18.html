Too Many Questions

18 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/18/06 Something reached through the fuzz of sleep to tug lightly at the edges of Dawn's mind. A sound, something soft and barely noticeable, like the gentle rustling of pages in the wind. Slowly she stirred to wakefulness, the covers rustling around her as she shook off the last cobwebs of sleep. Raising her sleep touseled head slowly, she surveyed the darkness of the bedroom, trying to blink away the last sensory fuzz around her eyes. Tessa's body stirred next to her and Dawn froze, waiting for the movement to pass before sitting up slowly. The other woman stirred once more but didn't wake, her hands tucked up near her face and her curly hair spread like a curtain out on her pillow. In the soft light one bare shoulder had escaped the clinging covers and Dawn ran an appreciative finger along to curve of skin, gently enough to not risk waking her. ?Wait...? She murmured to herself, ?...where's that light coming from?? The same soft sound reached her ears once more, and Dawn slid her long legs out over the side of the bed, steadying herself against the headboard as she stood. A faint light glowed under the bedroom door and she padded slowly towards it, bare feet hissing into the thick carpet with each step. Easing the door open she slipped into the hallway, scanning the narrow space until she located the source of the light. One of the three other doors in the hallway was slightly ajar, light glowing out softly from the interior. The sound that had first woken her seemed to be coming from the same place, and so she slipped quietly down the hallway and peeked through the crack. Tessa's adopted daughter/niece was perched on a stool by her desk, seemingly oblivious to her late night visitor as she scribbled furiously away at something Dawn couldn't see. Brushing an errant lock of strawberry-blonde hair from her face, Dawn pushed the door open slightly and knocked lightly as she did so. ?Knock, knock kiddo.? She kept her voice light, still unsure as to where she stood with Tessa's eldest child. Christa raised her head from the page and looked back over her shoulder, a delicate frown creasing her youthful features. She'd turned eleven only recently, but showed all the signs of trying to act many years older. ?Oh...hello Dawn.? Dawn raised an eyebrow as Christa turned back to her page, settling one shoulder against the door frame and folding her arms across her stomach. ?You're up awfully late.? ?I couldn't sleep.? Came the short reply. It was Dawn's turn to frown, but she fought to keep it from her tone of voice. ?What's wrong? Something on your mind?? The gave the girl pause, her frantically writing hand stopping in mid-sentence. ?Yeah...kinda.? Moving slowly from the doorway, Dawn crossed the room to Christa's single bed and sat lightly down on the edge of it. Balancing on the palms of her hands she fought the urge to go peer over Christa's shoulder. ?Did you want to talk about it?? ?You wouldn't understand.? Raising an eyebrow, Dawn settled back on her elbows. ?Try me.? Placing her pencil on the desk, Christa swiveled around in her chair to fix Dawn with a hard look. She scanned the Steel Rose up and down, as though evaluating something that no one else could see. She seemed to find something she liked though and leaned one elbow against her desk, placing her chin in her hand. ?How do I know I can trust you?? The words pinky swear came to mind, but with Christa having just turned eleven, Dawn wasn't certain if the childhood gesture would be appreciated. Instead she leaned forward, placing her elbows on her knees and fixing her emerald eyes on Christa's. ?You have my word. Nothing we talk about here will go any further unless you tell me it's okay.? The girl nodded once firmly, seemingly pleased with the arrangement, and scooped the book off her desk. Moving across the small room she sat down beside Dawn, primly straightening her nightgown and holding the blue, hardcover book out. Accepting the book with what she hoped was a gracious nod, Dawn opened it to where Christa had been writing. She scanned lower and lower on the page, her movements getting smaller and quieter until she stopped entirely, looking sidelong at the eleven year old. ?These are letters to your father?? Christa nodded firmly, drawing a slight frown from the Steel Rose. ?Why don't you mail these? We could go to the post office tomorrow and...? ?Daddy can't get letters anymore.? Christa's small voice was controlled, but wavered slightly around the edges. ?He's...? ?...gone.? Dawn finished the sentence awkwardly, closing the book and letting it lie in her lap. ?So you write him letters in your journal so he can read them?? ?I guess so.? Christa shrugged, trying to look nonchalant. ?I know he's dead now and he can't read...but maybe he can still see me sometimes.? Dawn nodded slowly, resting her hands on the book's smooth cover out of lack of anything else to do with them. ?Do you miss him?? Christa favoured her with a look that clearly said 'Are you, in fact, mentally retarded?' ?Of course I miss him. But I don't like going to that place they put him, so I write letters instead.? A ghost of a smile graced Dawn's face. ?I don't like going to the cemetery either. It's not private enough for my liking.? It was Christa's turn to look at Dawn sidelong, her youthful features scanning the older woman's face. ?Why would you have to go?? ?My dad passed away too.? Years after the fact and Dawn could feel her throat constrict, heavy with emotions that seemed neither to dull nor go away as time wore on. ?He died a few years ago.? ?Do you miss him?? The question was almost teasing, but held an edge of intensity that made Dawn's answering smile a little forced. ?Yeah, I miss him a lot. My Dad was a great wrestler, everybody knew who he was. That's all I ever wanted to be, ever since I can remember, was to be as great as him.? ?Are you?? It was so painfully earnest that Dawn couldn't help but laugh. ?Not yet I'm not. I'm working on it, but I still have a long way to go before I get anywhere close to as good as my Dad was. He was one of the very best.? She smiled sadly, tracing one finger down the spine of the journal. ?Do you write your Dad letters?? A sigh worked it's way free of Dawn's throat and she shook her head. ?No. When he first...well, when it wasn't long after he had passed away I was very mad. I thought I was mad at him, mad for leaving me alone like that, for just leaving without saying good-bye. I tried really hard not to think about him at all, and tried even harder to forget that he'd ever existed.? Christa looked down at her small hands, bunching them up in her nighty. ?I get mad sometimes too. But Tessa told me that he didn't mean to go away, that he didn't want to leave me by myself.? ?Tessa's a smart gi...woman.? Dawn smiled. ?A very close friend of mine told me the same thing. Our fathers passed away at around the same time, and I didn't have anyone else but her. She helped me remember the good times, and how much I missed him even though I was mad.? ?Didn't your mom take care of you?? Christa's voice was very small, and she'd found a spot on the carpet that seemed to warrant further inspection. ?No...my mother left when I was very young.? It hurt to say, but Dawn suspected that would always be the case. ?My mom left just after Dad did. That's why I live with Aunt Tessa.? Reaching out with one arm, Dawn hugged Christa gently around her shoulders, tucking the smaller body against hers. ?I guess we're a lot alike, you and I.? ?I dunno if I want to be a wrestler though...? Christa's voice was muffled against Dawn's tank top, and drew out a deep laugh from the Steel Rose. ?Well nobodies saying you have to be one. You're plenty young yet kiddo, you've got all the time in the world to decide what you want to be. And you've got Tessa taking care of you, so you're going to be okay.? Christa raised her head, peering intently up at Dawn. ?Are you going to take care of me?? ?Me?? The question left her flat footed, scrambling to find her thoughts. ?W-what do you mean?? An impish smile slipped across Christa's face. ?I may be young but I'm not stupid you know. You sleep in the same room that Aunt Tessa does...are you getting married?? Dawn flushed a bright red, overtaken by a sudden fit of coughing as she struggled to recover from the surprise. ?Married? I ah...I don't know about that kiddo, I don't even think they allow that...down here...? Her voice trailed off at the look on Christa's face, an earnest and needy look that almost stole the Steel Roses breath away entirely. ?Even if you don't get married...are you going to take care of Evan and me?? Staring down at the intent girl in her arms, it took Dawn a long moment to find her words. Even long to decide whether or not she should even say them. ?I...I'll do my best while I'm here. I'll try hard.? ?Do I have your word?? At that she did smile, hugging the smaller girl fiercely to her. ?You have my word.? Small hands patted her shoulders and the small of her back. ?I'll take care of you too. That way you don't have to be by yourself either. I can even teach you to write letters to your dad.? It was hard not to crush the girl against her shoulder in the sudden rush of emotion, but Dawn rode it out as best she could, pulling back from Christa with a wavering smile. ?Thanks kiddo.? She sniffed, resisting the need to wipe her nose on her forearm. ?But now it's time for bed, you've got school tomorrow.? Christa nodded, taking the book from Dawn's lap and putting it onto the shelf by her headboard. She squirmed under the covers and pointed at the lamp on her desk. ?You'll have to turn that off, I can't reach.? ?No problem.? Dawn tucked the covers up to Christa's chin and then hesitated, leaning down to kiss her gently on the forehead. ?Sweet dreams kiddo.? Pushing herself off the bed she crossed to the desk and flicked the light off. For a long moment she stood in the dark, struggling against the rush of emotion the smaller girl had evoked, eventually finding enough energy to move out into the hallway and close the door behind her. Leaning back against the wall she covered her face with her hands, trying to physically hold back the tears that seemed determined to spill past mental barriers. A gentle hand touched her shoulder, making her jump and drawing a strangled yelp from her throat. ?Shhh, sorry, sorry I didn't mean to scare you.? It was Tessa's hand on her shoulder, and her clear blue eyes that stared up across the eight inches between them. ?I thought I heard a noise, and then I saw you were gone...? Remembering her promise, Dawn swiped clumsily at her eyes and tried to force a weak smile, even in the darkness. ?I was just...thinking about my Dad...? ?Oh Dawn...? Gently Tessa pried Dawn's hands from her face, standing on her tip toes to kiss at the tears that spilled down her pale cheeks. ?...you should have woken me. I don't want you to go through things like that alone...that's not what we're about remember?? The teensie tiny terror's lips hovered near Dawn's for a moment, and it was Dawn who leaned into them, kissing the smaller woman gently and wrapping her arms around her waist. Drawing a shuddering breath she rested her forehead against Tessa's. ?I'm okay...some days are just worse then others.? ?Come back to bed, everything will seem brighter in the daytime.? Tugging gentled on Dawn's hands, Tessa pulled her back towards the bedroom... ...and Dawn's eyes opened, finding themselves fixed on an unfamiliar ceiling. Her breath caught in her throat as she struggled to make sense of her surroundings, sitting up to flick on the light beside her bed...and letting out a gusty sigh of relief as her hotel room leapt into view in the dim lighting. ?Shit.? She brought her knees up, resting her elbows on them and digging the heel of her palms into her eye sockets. ?Of all the fucking times...? Tossing her long legs over the side of her bed, Dawn slipped off onto the plush carpeting and shook herself like a dog. Taking long strides across the room to the balcony, she stared out the glass doors at the lights of the city beyond. ?Why are you in my fucking head?? She growled, shaking her head sharply as if she could rid herself of those thoughts by physical force alone. ?Get a fucking hold of yourself...? But thoughts of Tessa and what she represented wouldn't go away. The big night. Warpath. Her first REAL title defense...and she blew it. Now, a week later they'd thrown her up against the only other person in GCW who had pinned her shoulders to the mat. Dawn didn't want to admit it, but the last thing she wanted to do was step into the ring with Tessa Windsor. Wounds from Warpath still stung her mind and body, and thoughts of what they had been did not seem to want to go away. ?Goddamit.? She cursed, ?Why couldn't it have been somebody...anybody else?? ?I can't escape this hell...? Dawn's head came up sharply at the unfamiliar sound, spinning her around to scan across her hotel room in search of where it was coming from. ?So many times I've tried...? ?What the fuck?? The hair on her arms and neck stood at attention, the words eerily familiar and all to disturbing in their accuracy. ?But I'm still caged inside. Somebody get me through this Nightmare, I can't control myself! So what if you can see, the darkest side of me, no one will ever change this animal I have become and we believe it's not the real me, somebody help me tame this animal...* Dawn slapped her forehead in frustration, striding across the room to her bedside table. ?That stupid phone. I can't believe I let Vivica talk me into having it.? Opening the drawer in the bedside table, Dawn snatched the singing cellular from inside and held the LCD up for inspection. ?That's the estate...? She blinked, looking at her own number on the phone. Flicking it open carefully, she held it up to one ear. ?Hello?? ?Lady McFarlane, I had hoped I got this number right.? ?Cyrus?! What time is it? Is everything alright?? Dawn sat down heavily on the side of the bed, dread gnawing at her gut. ?Yes, everything is fine. I have just awoken for the evening, and remembered a tidbit of information that might help us to understand you...condition, a little better.? Just like that the dread was gone, replaced by an almost heady sense of anticipation. ?Seriously? Did you find something in the library?? ?Not exactly. You see, I was looking over your itinerary, and realized that there is a business acquaintance of mine that lives near your most recent venue. I took the liberty of contacting her, and she's agreed to see you. She's a specialist in many of the things that are considered, not natural to this world.? ?That's fantastic! What's her number? And her address, and her...? There was a dry chuckle. ?Lady McFarlane please, try to control yourself. I'll fetch you the information right now.? Dawn waited impatiently, digging out a pad of paper that had been thoughtfully provided by the hotel staff and jotting information down as Cyrus read it off to her. ?Wow, thank you very much Cyrus. This could be the breakthrough we've been working for.? ?Indeed...although I must remind you that the closer we get to discovering the nature behind your affliction, the more I must insist that you inform Lady Vlaszy. She needs to know, and more then that, she deserves to know.? Hanging her head, Dawn nodded numbly, though he couldn't see it. ?I-I know...I'm going to tell her, really...I'm just not ready to face her on this just yet.? Her hand stole unconsciously to the collar around her neck, thumb stroking over the silver plating in a soothing motion. There was silence on the other end for a moment before Cyrus spoke. ?As you wish. I will respect your request and not pass on the information just yet, but I should tell you...should I feel that it has progressed past the point where she must know, then I will inform her myself.? ?It won't come to that, I swear. I'll tell her myself before that happens.? ?Very good. I shall allow you to return to your sleep. Good luck Lady McFarlane, I hope you find what we're searching for.? ?Thank you Cyrus...good night.? Dawn closed the phone slowly, staring at the time and address written on the hotel letterhead, and wondering where she was going to fit these new questions into the cloud already whirling chaotically about her head. * Lyrics from 'Animal I have Become' by Three Days Grace
