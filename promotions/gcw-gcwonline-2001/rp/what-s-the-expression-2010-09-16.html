What's The Expression...?

16 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/16/10 Six miles outside the Dallas Convention Center, a hush has fallen over the crowd. Twelve every-day, middle-aged, white-bread folk sit planted upon the two leather benches that lay across either side of Starship Tracer. Their eyes are wide and pleading as they gaze upon Lucius Benson, the proverbial second-in-command of the Enclave. He stands in the middle of the floor area between the benches with his face firmly planted within his suited forearm. His beige suit complements his cropped flat-top hair and his dark green eyes normally, but on this day, his eyes are clenched tightly. Those around him seem almost… worried. Their eyes travel from Mr. Benson and fall upon The Archon himself, the esteemed leader of the Enclave and En’Flarim’s champion upon this world. He stands half-in and half-out of the portal leading to his office within the enormous RV. Dorn was in mid-stride when the event happened and he came to a screeching halt when the event transpired. All small talk, debate, and meager worship came to a silent and abrupt end when the unthinkable happened. The followers of the Goddess’s message now sit silently upon the black leather benches, some clutching their chests in hope, as all eyes fall upon the Archon. Lucius Benson finally opens his eyes and lifts this towards Dorn Tracer. A look of pain and sorrow slowly melts away as he stares upon the Archon in a quizzing manner. All those aboard Starship Tracer are eagerly awaiting the words of the Archon in response to the dire event that has transpired. It’s a pivotal point in their faith; a point in which some will lose faith and others will steel themselves for the road ahead protected ONLY by faith. They look upon him as their Father; he is their GUARDIAN! His word is law, and his faith is unshakable. Yet, Lucius Benson gives the Archon a deep stare that asks questions rather than pleads fealty. But what has happened that has caused such an icy atmosphere upon the Goddess’s vessel? What is causing Lucius Benson to question Dorn Tracer himself? Lucius Benson just sneezed. Upon the completion of the rather simple act, all eyes fell upon the Archon as he stopped in mid-stride towards his office. His head slowly turns to meet their glance and the glance of Lucius himself. His eyes dart from his followers, to Lucius, and back to the followers. They seem to be EXPECTING something of him, but they look as though its arrival would shatter their entire reality. They seem afraid. After what seems like hours, Dorn’s eyes clench into a look of rage and he just blurts: ”WHAT??!” When none answer, he scoffs and steps into his office, slamming the door behind him. When the Archon has finally left the room, a sense of comfort washes over the crowd and some breath a sigh of relief. Lucius Benson removes his face from his forearm and stands tall, smoothing out his suit and straightening his tie. He clears his throat and looks about the crowd of followers for a second before striding through Dorn’s office door without a word. The followers look amongst themselves for a moment before one finally speaks out. “Why did he say, ‘Watch this’ before he sneezed?” A few shrug their shoulders and other just look at each other with puzzled glances. Why had Lucius wanted the followers to see Dorn’s response to the sneeze? They may never know… Fin.
