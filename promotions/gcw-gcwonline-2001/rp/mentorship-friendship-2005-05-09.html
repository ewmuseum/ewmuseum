Mentorship...friendship....

9 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9 May 05 It was a tough work out I put the kid through but I was pleased to see that he never once broke down because that was what this work out was for. I smiled inwardly as Darek walked from the gym, limping somewhat but yet fighting back the grimace his face was no doubt wanting to release. He tosses his gym bag in the back of my Escalade truck, then came around and got into the front seat, careful to not hit any part of his body on the way in. “Ready?” He simply nodded with a slight grin and so I pressed the gas and we began to move forward. I told him that after his first full work out that we’d go out and have a couple drinks together, just to show him that I am not a complete prick, and won’t try to just kill him out there. Plus just as RaZiel did for me when I was coming up, it’s always good to meet your trainees on a personal level as well as a professional. I wouldn’t quite call it becoming friends, but I would at least give this kid the benefit of the doubt, since he did seem to have some heart and dedication. Up until this point I had just had him doing light stuff, like running four miles a day, doing some push-ups, tumbling inside the ring, etc. I had to find the time to get with him more because of this European tour for GCW. I finally had some time to come back home for this, and so here we are. Deep down I knew that I wanted this as much as he did, because I have always felt that I had a world of knowledge to pass down, yet had no one to give it to. Not only was this kid willing to learn but he has put his entire life on hold to do it. I figured he’d crumble like I almost did in my early days but he seems to have even more desire than I did. I figured I’d just take him to a small bar down town, let him forget the aches and pains that his body is feeling by drowning them out. Hell it was the way I always preferred to do things, although not as often recently. Plus I certainly needed a drink after what happened after the show this past week, with Cindy’s sister showing up at my door. We reached the bar fairly quickly and without any problems in traffic. We walked inside and the place was dimly lit, and not very packed at all. I guess that’s what happens when you go for a drink at 6:30 P.M. A fairly young but very pretty blonde waitress seated us, and I chuckled a bit at the fact that Darek didn’t even glance over to sneak a peek. That’s how you know you’re in a great deal of pain, when a beautiful woman walks by and you don’t even glance. We got seated in a booth at the far end of the bar. The waitress who identified herself as Sharon, took our orders which were just a couple of Coronas for now. Darek let his body sink into the cushions of the booth and laid his head back, closing his eyes for a moment. He stayed in this spot until Sharon came back with our drinks and placed them in front of us. “So Darek, you going to be ready for the next session ?” His eyes reached mine and almost pleaded with me to give him some time to heal. Through all that pain though, he smiles. “Yes Roberto, if you wanted to go right now I’d do it.” “Good answer, very good answer.” We sat quietly for a few minutes now, just enjoying the silence and most of all enjoying the beer. In him I saw so much familiarity it was frightening, that look in his eye, that innocence, and that wonderment. It wasn’t me I saw in those eyes though, because by this time in my life I was too far gone. No in his eyes I saw my brother, Damen. Perhaps that’s why I’ve been pulled towards this young up and comer, and maybe not, only time will tell for sure. “Roberto I’d like to tell you how much this does mean to me. I know you are a very busy man, and for you to take the time to tutor some “young punk” like me ? well I am very grateful.” “I’ve asked myself for the past few weeks why I actually agreed to help you Darek. Don’t take that personally, because I think you have unlimited potential, it’s just that I’ve always been a man of few friends. In the past seven years I’ve only had one person I could really call a friend, and even he stabbed me in the back. I have had others come to me asking for help and would quickly shoot them down ? there was just something about you I could feel, something that made me think twice before turning you away.” “I can only hope to one day be at your level. To accomplish all that you have.” “In all reality Darek, I haven’t accomplished that much. I have been a World champion once, although it was short lived. It was the greatest feeling in the world, holding that title in my hands and having the crowd behind me 100 %. Besides that though, my wrestling career has been up and down.” “What about this run in GCW” You have gotten a great deal of respect.” “It’s strange actually, because I hear the people speak, and mostly good things are said. I have been dubbed the greatest U.S. Champion by many, the one who put GCW on his back by others, and the most dominant man in Wrestling by the rest, YET I still feel I have something to prove. I still feel that my mission won’t be complete until ?” “You are the world champ.” “Yes.” I was a bit surprised at the way he said his last line. It wasn’t a question, it was a statement, as if he were inside of my mind. He was easy to talk to, and he was very intelligent and mature for his age. He was very confident in the way he spoke, a far cry from when we first met even, when I couldn’t break him from calling me “Mr. Abitua”. “I think your time will come Roberto, I mean you have beaten everyone the bookies have put you up against. A lot of my own friends believe that you would have won that ACWF VI hands down. With the way Caldera has seemed to look at you lately you could’ve weasled your way into it.” “Probably, but I didn’t want it at the time.” “Why not?” “It wasn’t the right time nor the right situation. I know a lot of people would jump at the chance to be world champion at any cost and in any situation because, quite frankly your chance may never come again. I know I’ll get another opportunity though, there is no doubt in my mind. I don’t want to win the title by grabbing the belt off some chain. I don’t want the only reason that I am the champ to be that I dropped a steel pipe on my opponent’s face and shattered his skull. To be a TRUE champion you must defeat the champ in a one on one battle. You either make that man tap or you pin their shoulders to the mat in an epic encounter ? no interference and no cheap tricks. Eye to eye, nose to nose ? fist to fist.” “Do you think Dread is the man to beat?” “He is on his way to earning my respect.” Well World title or not, you sure have forged some incredibly huge shoes to fill for the next U.S. champion. Defending it basically every week can’t be easy.” “You have no idea Darek, ha none what so ever. My body aches every time I walk into that ring. I push it to the back because to be great you must leave no doubt. I am writing my own legacy into the GCW storybook, and any ounce of weakness that you show to THEM, is an ounce of weakness that they will use to get one over on you. I defend this title of mine willingly every week, no matter what, even after a fucking heart attack. Why you may ask ? because that’s what legends do.” “You are legend ?” “No ? not yet Darek. Just remember this old saying, because it is one that you will take with you forever. Kill one man and you are a murderer, kill a hundred, a King ? Kill them all, a GOD.” We continued to talk and drink, and the more we drank the more we talked. We spoke about wrestling, and the lessons learned from it, but most of the lessons have more meaning than just that. Life lessons are learned from what you learn in that ring. Honor, Pride, Integrity, leadership, courage ? it is all part of what it takes to be great in this business and in this life. I can only hope that Darek takes these lessons to heart, or else these efforts will be in vain. For some strange reason though I believe in this kid, or maybe it’s just me reaching out to find someone that perhaps I can one day call a friend. God knows I don’t have many of those now a days.
