Catching up

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Yesterday I glanced at my watch for the third time since I'd sat down. Eager to get to the airport and see Cindy, I'd left a little earlier than I needed to and surprisingly the traffic wasn't as bad as I thought it would be, leaving me with a half hour to kill before Cindy's plane landed. I'd wandered around the airport, hit a couple of vending machines for a Milky Way and a Code Red, and then doublechecked the arrivals board and located the gate and sat down. Finally the arrival board changed from 'On Time' to 'Arrived'. I stood back from the gate and the human corridor of people looking for loved ones. I watched as smiled bloomed on faces and people rushed into the arms of their friends and loved ones. Finally I saw her. It had only been a few weeks since I'd last seen her, but my wife's face shone like a light among all the other weary travlers. She saw me and gave me a soft smile and walked up to me, dropped her carry on bag to the ground and wrapped her arms around me and our lips met. 'I missed you.' Was all I said when we disengaged. She picked up her bag and we headed out. 'I missed you too.' She said, 'But I caught the shows. You still got it babe.' 'I never lost it.' I said smiling. 'You act like I have ring rust or something.' 'Easy dear, let some of that air out of your head.' I laughed, Cindy was one of the few people in the world who could make me laugh no matter how bad I felt. One of the many reasons I married her. 'You got any bags?' 'Nah, just this, I managed to get a weeks worth of stuff in here.' she said patting the big black and purple bag by her side. I took the bag and slung it over my shoulder, 'A weeks worth? I thought you were just staying for the weekend.' 'Trying to get rid of me already Mike?' she said fixing me with 'the look'. If you don't know what 'the look' is, then you've either never had a mother, girlfriend or wife. 'Not at all, I just didn't think you were planning on staying that long. Think I'm gonna need taking care of after the PPV?' 'Maybe.' she said with a wink. We stepped outside and the LA heat hit us like a physical thing. 'Jeez, I forgot how hot Los Angeles can get.' she said. 'Try being at a fan fest for six hours, mingling with the crowd, taking pictures and signing autographs. I may not like a lot of the guys on the roster, but at least I know they were just as miserable as I was.' I said guiding her towards my rental car. Once inside with the AC going full blast and heading back to the hotel I could see how tired she was. 'Fucking jet lag...' she mumbled, and actually nodded off for the rest of the ride back. I woke her up once we reached the hotel. 'I need a shower.' she said, back in my room, 'I feel grimy.' 'Want some help?' 'No you perv.' she said pushing me back, 'If you come in there, I'll never get washed.' Again I laughed as she made her way towards the bathroom. A few moments later I heard the shower running and flipped on the TV trying to figure out what to do. We could stay in or go out, but truth be told I wasn't in the mood to go back out. I had my wife to myself for one night and decided to make the best of it. I called room service and ordered up two steak dinners and a bottle of the hotel's best wine. It would cost me a pretty penny, but I could afford it. Cindy emerged from the bathroom a few minutes later wrapped in a towel, the food arrived moments later. I tipped the room service guy rather generously and made a grand sweeping gesture to Cindy to sit down and eat. 'In my bathrobe?' she asked, looking at me as if I had lost my mind. 'Why? Who's going to see you? We're alone, no one will know but me and I promise I won't tell.' 'You're weird, I married a weirdo.' 'And you love me.' I said, 'Now sit down and eat.' There wasn't much talking to catch up on as we ate as I had only been away from home for a few weeks and we had called each other several times in those few weeks. Once dinner was over we caught up on the one thing we had really missed, and if I have to tell you what that is, then you need to get out more.
