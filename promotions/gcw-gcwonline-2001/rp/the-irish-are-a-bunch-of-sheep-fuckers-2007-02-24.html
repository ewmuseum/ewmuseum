The Irish are a bunch of sheep-fuckers

24 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 24th, 2007 “Oh my god! Did you hear” Chris Storm is in GCW now!” “GCW” But what about Legacy of Champions?” “He’s doing both! Can you believe it” But I hear his stay in GCW is just a pay-per appearance deal since Tessa is taking a leave.” “That’s good, I would hate to have to see any more of him than I have to.” “Yeah, he’s a dick.” “Totally.” Inside Tony’s “Bar and Grill”, located on the west bank of the Mississippi river, two girls wearing Dawn McFarlane shirts were discussing the latest events of their favorite promotion. Sitting just on booth behind them, a man turned around and could almost be heard laughing. Feeling that his opinion needed to be made, he spoke up. “I don’t know; that Chris Storm guy is pretty amazing. He’s easily the most talented guy in the most talented guy in the place. I would go so far as to say he’ll most likely be World Champion in a matter of weeks.” The girl with the pink dyed hair had to turn and face the man; her jaw had nearly dropped off its socket. “Are you kidding me?” she asked, “he is like totally a loser.” “Yeah” her friend butted in, “he’s lame, is what he is. No one wants to see that tired garbage he does in the ring. Lia would mess him up. As would Dawn, she’s the best.” Jumping to his feet, the man’s hat flipped off his head. “Dawn’s just a dumb lesbo! I’d break that bitch in half if she was stupid enough to try any shit.” The two girls looked on in amazement. One of them managed to mutter “oh.my.god.” Touching his now exposed hair, Chris Storm realized he had just been ousted. Looking around to see who all had witnessed his outburst, he decided to get the final word before leaving. “And that Katie O’Clare chick needs to hop her ass on out the damn ring before I plant the bitch into the canvas like I did last World Wide!” Swiping up his baseball cap and flipping it back on, Chris nodded to the star struck girls and headed out of the bar. As he headed toward the door he flopped down a fifty to cover his tab of Cherry Cokes and exited onto the streets. A sudden urge to be vindicated overcame Chris, and the Rock Superstar had to pull out his cell phone from his pocket. Hitting a few keys, he pressed the phone up against his head while he walked down the sidewalk. “Tessa?” “Yes,” came a tired sounding reply, “Chris, what’s up?” “I’m cool, right?” “Huh?” “I said I’m cool, right” You know?” There was a brief moment of silence when Tessa was most likely wondering just what was going on. She let out a yawn, “yes, Chris, you are cool. Is that it?” Chris smiled, “damn right I’m fucking cool. I’m the shit.” No doubt Tessa was on the other side just shaking her head at her man. Sadly this was not the oddest conversation she had ever had with him, nor would it be the last. “Is that it?” she asked again. “Yeah, I’ll be home soon.” “Ok, that’s fine” she said before hanging up the phone. Chris slid the phone back into his pocket and was able to walk down the road with a broad smile across his face. He knew he was the shit, fuck what those girls thought. They were just dumb bitches like all the other chicks in GCW. They must just secretly want his cock, Chris told himself, and that is what made them so bitter told him. Lesbians get like that. Looking down to his watch, Chris made a cut across the street and headed down a back alley. As if being guided by some prior life, Chris cuts another street and down two buildings. After five minutes of planned turns and cuts he came to a dead end down a particularly dirty alleyway. The only thing occupying the dirty hole were two dumpsters, each facing the other on opposite walls. Turning to his left, Chris faced the dumpster and gave it a hard kick that rocked it back and forth. “Come on, let’s go.” There is a moment of silence. Chris looked around, seemingly annoyed by the look on his face, and gave the dumpster another solid kick that echoed through the long and deserted alley. “I said let’s go! I don’t have all damn day.” From behind Storm, the other dumpster’s lid flipped open. Rising from the filth was a monstrous man of over six and a half feet. “You looking for me?” Chris Storm turned around and looked at the hobo. A snarl on his face, he turned away and started out the alley. The large man rolled out the dumpster and dropped to his feet on the concrete. Following in tow behind Storm, it wasn’t until the two emerged out onto the streets that Chris spoke again. “Listen, Arizona, you remember that match I substituted for Tessa” The tag team one?” The Venice Beach Bum shook his head, “the one in GCW.” “Yeah,” Chris said, stopping to wait for traffic. “Look the dumb bitch Tessa was supposed to partner with apparently is pissing and moaning because I dropped her on her head after the match.” Arizona rolled his eyes, “the nerve of some people. And I bet she gave you ample reason to have to put her down. She tried a vicious assault on you?” “Even worse!” Chris cried out. “The dumb ho tried to steal my spotlight. Can you believe she had to steal MY pin” I had those two pathetic opponents of a tag team right where I wanted them. Then she had to come along and steal my win.” “What a bitch.” “Totally.” When the light finally signed that the two were able to cross the street, they hurried along toward O’Malley’s bar on the corner. “The nerve of some people, I tell you,” Chris said, “They just don’t have any sense of respect in this business anymore.” Heading into the favorite Irish bar of the locals, Chris and Arizona were engulfed in a cloud of cigarette smoke and curse words. Grizzled old men in work clothes stopped their conversation in mid sentence to take a look at the two thumb sores, who must have stumbled into the wrong establishment. Ignoring the odd silence of a typically loud and rowdy bar, the two pulled up a spot at the bar. The bartender, an aging man wearing a black shirt with green suspenders approached the two as he wiped down the inside of a glass mug with a dirty rag. “You two need some help?” he stopped his train of thought as his nose caught an odd odor. Taking in a nose full, the bartender looked directly at Arizona. “Boy, you smell right like shit.” Chris looked over at Arizona and tried to get a good smell. “Odd, I don’t smell anything” he said perplexed. Arizona just chuckled, staring down at the bar, “that’s because you spout so much shit, that you just got use to the smell.” “Funny, if you weren’t such a bad wrestler I would recommend you a career as a comedian,” Chris told him before turning back to the bartender. “Look just get me a Cherry Coke,” his eyes caught the glass mug he was cleaning with the brown looking rag, “and in a different glass.” The bartender just looked Storm dead in the eye, never bothering to stop cleaning his glass, “maybe you didn’t see the sign outside.” His thick hand left the glass and pointed toward out the door where the sign was proudly hung. “This is O’Malley’s Bar, and I’m O’Malley. And I say we don’t have coke. We have beer, whiskey, and brandy.” Arizona looked over the bar to the selection of alcohol against the wall. “I’ll have a beer, whiskey, and brandy.” His attention shifted over to Storm, “you paying right?” “Hell no,” he snapped, “get a damn job you worthless bum.” Arizona glanced back over to the bartender, “nevermind.” “You know,” Chris started, “you are only further perpetrating the idea that the Irish are just a bunch of drunken, inbred, sheep-fuckers. Now are you sure you don’t have even a single can of Cherry Coke behind the bar?” Bowing up, the bartender slammed down his fist and flung the glass mug behind him, shattering it into a thousand pieces. “No man comes into my bar and insults my wife!” Chris drew back, his eyebrows perched up. Silently he and Arizona backed out of the bar and never turned around until they felt the bright sun shining against their backs. When the two were a few paces away from the bar, only then did Chris have the nerve to turn to Arizona. “That was creepy. ? “The Irish suck” Arizona told him. “Totally.” In a moment of thought, Arizona turned to Storm, “hey isn’t your opponent this week Irish” O’Something?” “Yeah, maybe that means she’s a sheep-fucker too.” Arizona shook his head, “no dude, chicks can’t fuck a sheep.” “The hell they can’t,” Chris looked back down at his watch, “I saw it on Youtube. Now look, I told Tessa I would meet her back at the condo in a bit. But meet me at the gym in three hours. I can probably crush Katie with my massive member, but I figure a few hours working out would still be good. For the abs, you know.” “Got it” Arizona answered. The two cut their paths together, Storm heading to the west toward his condo and Arizona to the south where the gym was located. But would a few hours be enough to beat Katie O’Clare, who’s been on a roll as of late? At World Wide 62, that question is answered and the entire world finds out what Chris Storm brings to the Global Championship Wrestling.
