Fox Diaries: World of Warcraft

2 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/2/08 START 2:00 am The darkest dark of the night has surfaced. The dew begins to form on the tree leafs outside as the temperature continues its downward descent. However, with all of these elements in place, it is indeed the most enticing time to sit down in front of your trusty computer with a hot cup of java in hand and log on to enjoy the hottest online sensation known to man. It is known as The World of Warcraft. Jump into Azeroth and explore the endless dungeons, skills and quests as you try to level your character. Forget the real world details. The only thing that matters is the game. And that’s what our hero; Clyde Fox is doing on this less than enticing real world night. We find him in the basement of his parent’s house, cuddled in a corner with his new Dell XPS computer. Clyde sits comfortably in near darkness on an extra padded computer chair as he engages in the action. Both of his wrists are wrapped in hand braces to prevent any injury from happening. Yes, he’s that intense. The computer is nestled on a beaten down table. The scratches and markings of defeat can be seen in trace amounts. Fox: User name? SexiFoxi69? password? Jenny Jiggles wiggles my piggles. Hehe. The screen provides what light there is in the room as the game loads. The character select screen pops up next and shows a level 29 Hunter standing there, ready and willing. Fox: Only forty more levels. Screw level 70? it’s all about level 69? BIG TIME! Fox quickly covers his mouth as his blurts out his catch phrase. Fox: Osh Kosh-ammit Clyde! You need to be quiet if you don’t want to get caught! Clyde clicks his character the game loads the playing world. Clyde proceeds to slap on his headset for the optimum playing experience. A light jingle with a flute can be heard as the mythical world of Azeroth comes to life before Clyde’s eyes. The scene slow melts away? --Game World-- The grass textures are exuberant as the trees shimmer thanks to the powerful ATI Graphics card. It’s your typical morning in Azeroth? many Druids are collecting gold for their weapon upgrades. Over in the north sector of town is a raid group preparing for a Kara run. Just to SexiFoxi69’s right is an NPC spreading good fortune. Oh and just over yonder is a clan of warriors creating wondrous shouts and macros. Of course our hero; SexiFoxi69 has the best gear that a level 29 Hunter can. He’s got some blue level gear with some awesome accessories that enable super rare drops from all of the high leveled monsters he slays. W + Right Click. SexiFoxi69 begins to walk forward as ThundorTheCapeCastedMasta rushes up to our hero. Whipser from Thundor: ROTFL, ur geeeeer aint guud. U newbie tubie! Duual me if u dunt care 4 ur lyfe! Thundor enters /dance and begins a little hoe down. Thundor, the warrior is a decked out level 39 badass. He’s got a steepled hat that allows for half a second reduction on his mana spring but only if he uses adrenaline skills first. And if that isn’t enough, you better watch out for his purple Javelin. That thing can cause an instant ice burn during critical hits only! SexiFoxi69 stands there unimpressed. He crosses his arms as he eagerly types his reply. SexiFoxi69: You don’t want a piece of me. I’ve got the secret foam which activates when we enter battle. With that equipped, my boots gain plus five percent dexterity which means that I can parry your attacks fool! Thundor stops dancing. He just stands there. The virtual reality wind is all that blows by them. It’s either a stare down or Thundor is just taking his sweet time writing a reply. Suddenly? Notification message: Thundor is now offline. SexiFoxi69: That’s what I thought ****! Oh no! Word protection is on! B I T C H! There! Teehee! So SexiFoxi69 continues his journey as he checks his quest log. Quest Log V 2.0: Flock The Dock, Azeroth In Danger, Raid With Friends, Kill Murloc, Ysera Inquest, Beacon Callers, Return To Teldrassil, Blacksmith Skills, Mail For Yump Leate? and the list goes on. SexiFoxi69: Hmmmmmm. Let’s go kill murloc! Kill Murloc Quest accepted. Description: Head to the coast of Westfall and eventually make it through to the Black Fathom Deeps. Silent, you should creep? he is more than a fish? he is more than a man? no he isn’t Auqa Man? HE IS MURLOC! Track down this fierce beast and kill him to attain the prize! SexiFoxi69: Oh man? I remember the last time I tried to kill Murloc? --Flashback-- May 3rd, 2008. Clyde Fox sits hunched over his computer with an intense stare. We catch our hero in the middle of battle in the luscious locale of Westfall. A vast land with wonderful scenery and much more vibrant than anything in real life. Clyde’s character, then an Undead Warrior, hacks away at some random baddies. His pet, One Eyed Willy crawls alongside his master. Suddenly a huge Tauren warrior rumbles towards the Undead Fox from the far plane. SMASH! The Tauren man crushes Fox with one swing of his hammer before even getting close to Murloc’s location. The words "You are dead" appear on the screen in a bloody red color. Fox throws his headset off and starts screaming with hatred. Fox: NOOOOO! HE WAS ONLY LEVEL FIVE! WHY WOW, WHY? HE DIDN’T EVEN GET A CRAFTING SKILL! --End Flashback-- SexiFoxi69: I have to let that go. That was a long time ago. I’ve changed since then. I’m a hunter now. I don’t know what has happened to One Eyed Willy though. Maybe Murloc has him. All I know is that this is my destiny. W + Right Click. He presses on in hope. SexiFoxi69: I have to prove that I can beat Murloc. It’s the only way that I’ll be prepared for High Stakes. Fox rushes through the gates of Westfall. --End Game World-- Pee break? 5 minutes later. --Return to Game World-- SexiFoxi69 rushes past the low leveled beasts that used to give his first character trouble. Some of the boars even run at the sight of Fox. Our hero eventually arrives at the gate crossing called Black Fathom Bridge. However he stumbles upon a huge Tauren warrior! SexiFoxi69: Oh my HTML! Not this guy again! The Tauren runs towards Fox in attack mode, but just before he can swing his hammer? RWLRWLRWLRWLRWL! Murloc and One Eyed Willy come rushing in from off screen and save SexiFoxi69! Murloc quickly spits some water at the warrior as One Eyed Willy casts his skill, Unusual Howl. This skill lasts for five minutes and allows for Willy to attack without the need to wait, but it also keeps his threat to a minimum. The Tauren stands there caught in Murloc’s goop as One Eyed Willy slashes away. SexiFoxi69: Guys! OMFG! I knew you guys would come to save me! Now let me charge up my super special attack! SexiFoxi69 begins to flicker and vibrate as he casts his strongest skill? RO-SHAM-BO! SexiFoxi69 runs up and kicks the Tauren right in its virtual family jewels! Critical Hit! Instant death! The Tauren falls to his knees and disappears with a less than spectacular pyro display. SexiFoxi69: WE DID IT! He’s dead! Yay! One Eyed Willy crawls up to Fox and begins to purrrrrr in satisfaction. On the other hand, Murloc bows and begins to walk away. SexiFoxi69: Murloc! My? my quest says that I need to kill you. But you helped me? what do I do? Murloc stops and turns. Murloc: RWLRWLRWLRWL! SexiFoxi69 virtually nods his head in agreement. SexiFoxi69: NO WAY! You’re too swell Murloc! Did you hear what he said One Eyed Willy? He’s going to grant me the completed quest anyways! Quest Complete. --End Game World-- Fox takes his headset off and rests it by his computer as it shuts down. Fox: I’m like such a highly respected quester? it like totally blows my mind. The computer screen fades to black and leaves our hero in pitch black peace and quiet. The atmosphere changes with the darkness from funny to serious. Clyde sits there in his chair. Silence. Reflecting on all the good times he’s had. Suddenly he begins to lightly weep tears of joy. Fox: I have to go out there and try my best. I know that what I face in Sin City will be my biggest challenge to date? and it’s really scary. Who is he talking to? Fox: I know that games aren’t the same as the real world. No more games? there’s no more time. Las Vegas? here I come. Fox rises up from his chair and walks passionately towards the stairs. He stops right before walking up the first step. Fox: Thanks for listening? God. END
