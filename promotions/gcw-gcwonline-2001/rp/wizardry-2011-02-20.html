Wizardry

20 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/20/11 Part I – The Idiot, Grinning “Why aren’t you getting ready for your match!?” was the exasperated squeal let slip from Grady Patrick’s lips. In complete contrast to the hurry and worry that painted Grady’s face a sweaty red, Alexander Redding lounged back on a chair, grinning as he looked over his cards: All hearts. The suicide King reminded him of Grady a little. “All in,” he said, shoving to the centre of the table a small stack of toonies. He let the royal flush lay, face down of course, before bothering to respond to the bothered manager. “Relax Grady,” he assured. “Relax? Why am I supposed to relax?” the questions less actual questions than stinging barbs of frustration and hidden insults. “Look at his head,” Theo Palmer starts in. He finishes his thought not with words, but by miming an exploding head. When he picks his cards back up he sees the pair of queens joined with a triplet of sevens. He lets loose a grin of his own, confidently tossing a matching set of toonies to the pile. Although, it should be noted that Palmer has considerably more back in reserve. “Alright, I know my luck hasn’t been that great tonight,” Alex starts in reference to the cards, just as good an omen as reading chicken entrails. “But believe me when I say this, I’ve got it covered.” “Covered? I thought I was the guy coming up with the schemes around here.” “You still are,” Alex says, not doing a thing to slow the rapid pulse of Grady Patrick. “But I mean… the guy has a fucking penis for a nose on that mask. I got no worries.” “The guy with the dick on his face also came within an eyelash of being Television Champion.” It takes a moment for the ridiculousness of the previous statement to penetrate the hum of the heart beat in the ears. “Dick… face… came,” Theo says, giggling all the way. He throws down the five cards and shifts his eyes to Redding. “Full boat,” he says, already sweeping the metal pile toward him. In response, Redding holds out a hand, the clear and international sign for stop. Letting out a giant laugh, a snort, and more laughter, he lays down the royal flush. “Royale with cheese.” It is an awkward silence, less the jingle of the coins being pulled back toward Alex. It takes a while for Alex to leave behind his happy winnings and deal with the daggers currently being stared through him by Mr. Patrick. “Right, my plan. Well, we’ve talked about the phallic nose already. It’s simple, really. If I get in any trouble out there, all it takes is a headbutt, or a chance brushing of that thing anywhere near me, and I make the call. I pull the ref and yell, ‘Sexual Assault.’” Alex nods, supporting his own idea now spoken aloud. The only response Grady will give is his right hand sailing toward his own face. “Facepalm? Okay, that was harsh. But don’t you see the genius in this? There’s no way they are going to let a sexual offender win a match, right? And even if the ref doesn’t declare me a winner on the spot, I got a great distraction created. You just whack him over the head with the cane, or in the pills, whichever.” That part actually does give Grady a small smile. It is always a happy thing when the world agrees, in some small way, to the delusional idea that ‘I am the most important person in this, or any, room.’ That’s not to say that disagreements could not be turned to fit this idea, but this was just easier. “I’m out for this hand, got to go to hit the head,” Alex says standing up. He gets three steps before he wonders aloud, “Or should I hold it? You know that a little stain in the front of the tights would sell that sexual assault thing.” “But you would be pissing your pants on live television,” Palmer is so helpful to remind him. It’s a finger tapping on his lips thrice before Alex decides to walk out of the room, assumedly to ‘hit the head’. With Alex gone, Grady slides to the table. Theo begins to deal, but Grady tosses the cards back at him. “I am not here to play poker,” his voice still ringing with a tinge of that frustration. “Was that the best plan you two could muster?” “Two?” Palmer defends himself. “That bit of wizardry came from your boy. But yeah, most of the plans ended with Hot Mask taking the cane between the legs.”
