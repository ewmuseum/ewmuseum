Look Upon Me And Despair!

29 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/29/10 AAAAAHHHH YYEEAAAAAHH!! DRURY HOTEL St. Louis, Missouri 7:32 pm Mere Moon Ago The front lobby of the sad economic lodging facility known only as Drury Hotel is in total bedlam as house attendants, desk clerks, cooks, janitors, random tourists, and even a small dog scurry to and fro across the marble floor. Their voices rise over each other louder and louder, trying to shout over the chaos unfolding before them. It’s a pretty comedic scene, actually. You’ve got the Hispanic janitor screaming something in a language only his fellow custodians understand while the desk clerk has damn near transmogrified into a tomato from trying to shout over him. Women have fainted and collapsed upon the floor; They are merely victims to the awesomeness that stands before the crowd. Standing upon the front disk, wearing naught but a bathrobe and one of his own licensed GCW shirts is DYNASTY, kids! His fists are pressed firmly against his cut hips as he beams a smug grin across the hotel ANTS! His robe flaps in a breeze whose origin no one can actually pinpoint and his bright sparkling teeth are like perfect Chiclets in a row. “Everyone, please, calm yourselves! I know it’s a lot to take in, but don’t worry, the pain will pass! FEAST YOUR EYES UPON IT AND DESPAIR!” It suddenly becomes clear what he’s talking about: The dude isn’t wearing pants. But he is wearing socks. No one is sure what in God’s name Dynasty seems to be doing, but he’s enjoying himself. Some of the ladies who haven’t tumbled to the floor look to be fawning over his Adonis physique and endowment. He looks to be quite proud of himself, I cannot possibly imagine why. He booms over the crowd again, “I don’t know what your problem is! You act like you’ve never seen an albino python before! I mean really!” The desk clerk goes running up to the desk and slams his fists down upon the finished wood, almost startling Dynasty. The clerk looks disheveled and can barely catch his breath. Of course, it’s probably difficult to catch your breath when you’re damn near 360 pounds, balding, and having to chase around a small dog while calming down the restless natives of the Drury lodging facility. He looks up at Dynasty, who just grins right back at him, and grimaces. “SIR! I don’t know what you think you’re doing but you need to come down from there IMMEDIATELY! You are frightening the other guests!” Dynasty lets out a piercing laugh that rips the breath from those in attendance. “Are you kidding me? I’m the BIG TIME! They’re not frightened; they’re just looking for a pen so I can sign their various naughty bits! It’s no big deal, man. Just let it go.” The clerk’s eye start to glaze and his mouth begins to widen like a gaping anus. He looks as though he wants to weep. And really, who could blame him? I mean, here’s this superstar GCW wrestler, fresh off an almost-win, trying to bless everyone in the room, and the clerk feels compelled to stop him! Something is forcing him to try and put a stop to a good thing! Hell I’d weep too. Fucking baby. “Sir… Mr. Dan-“ “DYNASTY!” Dynasty suddenly spurts out, still flashing that toothy grin. The clerk wipes his brow before continuing, “Mr. Dynasty, this is illegal! What you are doing is illegal! Now please, come down from there!” Dynasty just sighs and rolls his eyes before hopping down off the counter. Almost immediately, the chaos ceases and everyone just stares at the half-naked wrestler as he stands before them, still firmly planted in his boisterous stance. Dynasty pulls his shoulders back and gives a hearty laugh. “Look man, you’re lucky I’m in a good mood! I’ve got a big match coming up against some crazy Buddhist pill-head and I’m gonna rock his shit. He’ll be lucky if I don’t leave THE HAMMER’S MARK on his forehead, know what I’m saying?” The clerk’s eyes begin to shift around as all attention falls to him. Does he know what Dynasty means? DOES HE TRULY KNOW?! “I… I’m afraid I don’t, sir.” Of course he fucking doesn’t. Probably has a vagina. Dynasty looks absolutely aghast!! “Come on, son! You act like I’m not standing here already beating you over the head with it! I mean, I can if you want…” Dynasty’s eyes drift down to his nether regions before snapping back up to the desk clerk and smiling. “But no, I have to save that for Darren Trotter. Or Dick Tracy. Or whatever his name is. Now, tell me something…” The GCW phenomenon grabs the clerk by the collar and pulls him ever so close. A hush falls over the crowd as Dynasty leans in close to the clerk’s face, which has begun to grow pale. “Where da bitches at?” The clerk faints from the pressure of Dynasty’s greatness before him and crumples into a heap on the floor. Dynasty rolls his eyes once again and mockingly kicks dust into the clerk’s face. He scans the room quickly and spots a particularly ravishing maid! He slides on over to her with the finesse of a young Michael Jackson and puts her arm around her. Her olive complexion darkens as she sinks a little into his arm, unsure of what to say. Dynasty pulls her in close and speaks into her ear, “Ever been with a star, baby?” The young maid shakes his head slowly, still unable to look him in the eye. And really, how could she? “It’s an amazing experience! You are beautiful, I am beautiful, we can have a beautiful time. What do you say you put down the mop and join me in my room?” The maid nods slowly, overcome with the rush of emotion running through her body. Dynasty is one of GCW’s greatest new stars, and here is her chance to leave her own mark! Here is her chance to lock his loins and his heart in her camel clutch and force him to tap! It’s perfect! Dynasty is pleased with this and gives another laugh as he suddenly sweeps the maid off his feet and makes his way down the hall. Everyone in the lobby is absolutely frozen in place as they watch this all unfold. Not a word is uttered between them; It wouldn’t do them any good anyway. Their opinions and fears don’t matter right at this moment. This moment is about Dynasty. A mustachioed janitor crosses his heart and shakes his head. “Dios mio…” FIN, KIDS
