Motorin'!

16 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 16, 2010 Y’know the old saying, “Once you get a taste of the spotlight, you never truly leave it?” That cliché holds true in more ways than one. It’s said that when it comes to professional wrestling, once you’re in, you never truly get out because it’s ‘in your blood.’ When it’s all you eat, sleep or breathe for a nearly a decade of your life, it becomes just that: A way of life. For some, it’s all they know and more often than not, it’s all they think about. Upon retirement, it gets worse, especially when one is forced to retire early because of injury. As bad as the injury may hurt, the ache of not being able to do what one loves is twice as rough. When that craving to perform strikes, the only thing that can quench it is being in front of the masses and putting on the show of a lifetime. It isn’t necessarily about the desire to compete or the will to win; it’s the addiction to the adrenaline rush that you get when the crowd is chanting your name. It’s the need to feel the electricity that surges through an arena when the crowd is on their feet and willing you to go on, even when you feel like you cannot. It’s a fate that so many former wrestlers suffer, but one that so few actually realize the seriousness of. There are times where it causes nothing but absolute grief and strife, and other times, it can keep someone awake at night. Dreams of what could have been and what actually was are all that fill one’s head when they actually manage to fall asleep. The thirst for the spotlight consumes all that it comes in contact with, and when a man whose living was made by being in the spotlight can’t have his way, it’s like a heroin junkie who can’t get his fix. Ace Action knows that feeling all too well. Breaking into professional wrestling in 1999, Ace was said to have all the potential in the world. The critics said he’d be one of the top competitors in professional wrestling by the end of the year. They were right, too. Within his first six months in the business, he won his first and only World Heavyweight Championship in a scaffold match with Eli the Eliminator. What a lot of people don’t know is that he won by default when Eli sprained his ankle and fell from the scaffold mid-match. Nonetheless, it was an upset victory that shocked the entire world of professional wrestling. After spending a few months in the singles division, he formed a tag team with his younger brother, AXEL. The two comprised the duo known simply as ‘Action Packed’. Being quite a bit bigger than his younger sibling, Ace was the “power guy” of the team. He performed feats of strength that very few had been able to accomplish before him, and AXEL was the high-flying marvel of the team; a stark contrast to his older brother’s strength. The team of Ace and AXEL skyrocketed through the ranks, plowing through team after team to earn the Omega Wrestling Alliance’s World Tag Team Championship. After holding the championships for a little over a month, they lost them to The Porkrind Kids in a rather controversial bout. Regardless, the referee’s decision was final and the two were forced to fight their way back up the ranks. Somewhere along the way, Ace suffered a broken neck that put him out of action for what he thought would be a year. About halfway through his recuperation and physical therapy, Ace’s doctors discovered a more serious problem. It seemed that the injury was much worse than previously thought, and the possibility that one misplaced bump could paralyze him had emerged. Deciding that the best course of action to take was to hang up the boots, so to speak, Ace did just that. Ever since that day, the only thing on his mind was what could have been. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Ten years later… “Hey, AXEL?” Silence. “AXEL?” Ace glances over to see what happened to AXEL, only to find his younger brother passed out on the other end of the couch where the two were watching a particularly boring movie, sleeping soundly. Narrowing his eyes for a moment, Ace lets out a flustered sigh and punches his younger brother in the arm to wake him. Grunting loudly as he’s jerked from the grasp of peaceful slumber, AXEL grasps his shoulder and looks around for the culprit. Seeing that Ace is the only other person in the room, he gives the most hateful glare. “What the fuck, dude?! That hurt!” Shaking his head and chuckling to himself, Ace takes a deep breath and looks to his little brother. To the trained eye, it would be quite obvious that he has something he wants to say. Truth is, he has a rather important announcement to make, and he is having a difficult time keeping himself from just blurting it out. “I’ve come to a decision, and I wanted to talk to you about it and see what you thought before I do anything about it.” “Alright. Let’s hear it.” Taking another deep breath, Ace braces himself for the worst as he clears his throat. He takes a moment to survey AXEL’s facial expression, only to find that it was blank and unblinking. He’d made the fellow curious, and there was no reason to keep him waiting. “I had a talk with my doctor about a week ago, and he took some x-rays and ran some tests on me. The results came in a couple of days ago and apparently, my neck has managed to heal over the last ten years or so.” “Well, that’s great to hear, bro. It must have been a HUGE load off of your shoulders, huh? I can’t imagine how happy you must have been.” “Well, here’s the thing about that. I’ve already picked up my workout routine and started my training again. I’ve decided to make a return to wrestling. It’s all I’ve been able to think about for a long time, now. Thoughts of what might have been have been haunting me for years, and now I’ve got the opportunity to reclaim my livelihood. What do you think about it?” Ace looks to AXEL once again, hopeful that his younger brother would support his decision. Tilting his head from left to right as he attempts to allow everything to sink in, a rather wide smile graces the younger Action’s lips. Reaching out to give his sibling a congratulatory slap on the back, he laughs. “That’s wonderful news, man! If you’ve been given a clean bill of health and you want to come back to the business, then go for it. You’ve got my full support, and I’ll back you up 100%. I’ll even help you along with your training, if you want.” It had been years since the two had trained together, and AXEL figured it’d be a good way to get Ace reacquainted with things. Besides, it’d be a chance for the two to face off against one another for the first time in years, even if it wouldn’t be in a sanctioned match. “Really? Thank you so much. It means a lot to me to hear you say that. I’ve also decided that I won’t be coming back simply as Ace Action.” “Is that so? Well, we’ll need to figure out a name for you to use, then, won’t we?” “No. Actually, I’ve been mulling one over for a few months, now, even before I made my decision to return. You know, kind of a ‘what if’ scenario. Anyway, I’ve decided that I’m going to use the one I came up with.” Nodding as he listens intently, AXEL strokes his chin as if doing some thinking of his own. Clearing his throat, he straightens his posture and leans against the back of the couch. “Well? What is it?” “Well, it’s…” Ace hesitates for a moment, his cheeks glowing red with a light blush, something completely uncharacteristic of a grown man. He felt that his choice of ring name might be slightly lame, maybe even borderline stupid. Deciding that his little brother wouldn’t judge him for his decision, he shakes his head and just spits it out.” “I’m going to be known simply as The Night Ranger.” AXEL’s eyes widen as his brother’s words echo within his ears, his head tilting to the left. The only thought he can seem to muster is, “Did he REALLY just say what I think he said?” Of course, AXEL isn’t just going to let this go without some kind of sarcastic response. “Really?” “Yes, really.” “REALLY?” “Um, that’s what I said.” Unable to keep himself together, AXEL falls off of the couch, laughing. As he rolls on the floor, he clutches his sides and kicks his legs, his hysterical laughter resonating off the walls. Ace looks at him, dumbfounded because he can’t seem to figure out why the younger of the two was laughing. Everyone(except for Ace, apparently) knows that Night Ranger was an American rock band from San Francisco, California that gained significant popularity during the 1980s with a series of successful albums and singles, their most well-known most likely being their power ballad, Sister Christian. “Hey, AXEL… Why are you laughing at me? Did I say something funny?” Suddenly snatched out of his fit of laughter by Ace’s question, AXEL sits up and shakes his head as he tries to calm himself. Of course, he was lying. He didn’t want Ace to think he didn’t support him in his endeavor to return to his former glory. “No, of course not. I’m just… I’m happy to see that you’re so enthusiastic about this. That’s all.” “Oh, okay. Well, what do you think of the name I picked out? Be honest with me.” Taking a moment to ponder his response, AXEL stands up and brushes himself off before retaking his seat on the opposite end of the couch from Ace. Wiggling into the cushion in an attempt to get comfortable, he leans against the back of the couch and crosses his arms over his chest. “Do you really want to know what I think?” “Yeah, of course. I wouldn’t have asked if I didn’t want to know your opinion on it. Now come on, give it to me straight-up.” “Alright, then. If you really want to know what I think, I think you’re motorin’. What’s your price for flight, in finding Mister Right?” Confused by the response he’s given, Ace blinks his eyes and shrugs his shoulders. He doesn’t have a clue what AXEL is talking about, and wonders if there’s something wrong with his brother. Afraid of looking stupid, Ace opens his mouth to speak, knowing what is most likely to come. “What are you talking about?” “Oh, nothing, really. It’s just that I’ve got a good friend that you might like to meet. She goes by the name of Sister Christian.” “Eh, okay. I’ll have to get her phone number from you, later. Anyway, what do you think of the name? Seriously.” AXEL stares, wide-eyed at his older brother, in complete disbelief that the poor fellow could be so stupid. Shaking his head and laughing quietly, he leans against the arm of the couch, propping himself up on an elbow. “Honestly, I think that if you like it that much, then you should use it. It doesn’t make a difference what I or anyone else thinks.” “I suppose you’re right. If that’s the case, then that’s what I’ll use. After all, if I don’t, then all of that thinking and planning will be for naught. Anyway, I heard you’ve got a big World Title shot coming up. You must be excited about that, huh?” AXEL gulps quietly at the mention of his match at the upcoming Worldwide, trying to swallow the nervous lump in his throat. If the truth be told, it’s the biggest match of his career so far and he’s rather nervous about it. His stomach flutters inside of him and his heart is beating ninety-to-nothing. “Well, about that… I’ve been in a few high-profile matches in my career, as you know, but this is the biggest one so far. This could be the match that makes or breaks my career in GCW and professional wrestling, in general. I’ve got to give the performance of a lifetime, or I may never see another main event in my life.” Ace nods, knowing full well what AXEL is feeling and thinking. He, himself had only been in a handful of main event matches throughout his short career, and he wanted nothing more than to see his brother succeed. “Don’t worry about a thing, AXEL. You know as well as I do that you shine brightest when under pressure. I’ve seen you perform at higher levels than anyone could ever hope to, and this time will be no different. You and I, we’re really not so different. I used to be just like you, in fact. I worried and worried, and even got cold feet when the time came to take that one, big step to the big time. You know what? Cold feet aren’t going to get you anywhere but back to the back of the line, and you don’t belong there. You belong at the very top of the ladder, and you need to make Steven Caldera and the rest of GCW take notice.” “But what if—“ “There are no ‘what ifs’ or ‘buts’. Just get out there when the time comes and show everyone what The Angel of Vengeance is made of. Do I make myself clear?” AXEL is a little taken aback by Ace’s pep talk, but nods, regardless. He knows his big brother is right, and that there is nothing to worry about. He breathes deeply to calm himself before standing up and taking a moment to stretch. Extending a hand to Ace, offering to help him up off the couch, AXEL pulls him to his feet when his hand is taken. Releasing his brother’s hand, AXEL heads to the front door, turning the knob and pulling it open. “What do you say we go and get started on your training for today? I need the extra training myself. Murray’s a beast, and well, let’s put it this way—Big D’s a pussy compared to him.” Ace laughs, shaking his head in disbelief at his younger brother as he walks out the door. “Still picking on that poor Jewish kid? I guess some things never change.” “Poor Jewish kid? The guy’s a douchebag, Ace. His lawyer’s a douche, too.” Ace erupts into another hearty laugh as the two jog off down the driveway to begin their workout and training session.
