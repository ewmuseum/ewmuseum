Date: 26 June 05 ** Like ole Renegade I decided to jump into this infinite gauntlet open invitation. This is the RP I put out over there. I just realized I didnt mention much about e-fedding which can hurt in PTC but the hell with it! It was a fun little RP for me! ** Slowly I moved the shot glass back and forth between my index finger and my thumb of my right hand. The sound of the glass grinding on the wooden bar was almost soothing in a strange way. I had been sitting at this bar for hours now, slowly drinking myself into a state of mind that I hadn’t been in for quite sometime. Things in my life seemed to be closing in around me and yet I kept a wall up to keep outsiders from knowing what was truly burning inside of me. If there was one thing I was good at, was keeping people out therefore letting people in was almost impossible for me at this point in my life. I grabbed the shot glass in my hand and brought it to my mouth with a shaky hand before closing my eyes, and downing its contents and slamming the glass on the bar when it was through. I could feel the hot liquid running down my throat, then coursing through my veins. I swallowed hard as the feeling stopped and my senses stabilized, slowly my eyes opened back up. What have I become? Over the course of my life things have gone so horribly wrong that it almost feels like I am being punished for a crime I did not commit. My life has become some sort of horrible nightmare, one that you can not simply wake up from. Sometimes the dream may play tricks on me and make me think that everything will change and that happiness is but an arm’s reach away, but that’s usually when a wall of fire engulfs the scene. Since my parents died I have been in search of a life that was never to be my own. I fooled myself to think that someone as beautiful and caring as Cynthia could have lived happily ever after with me, but God put his foot down and took that away from me in a bloody car accident. He must have felt that she could serve him better by watching by his side. GOD acts like a little boy with a magnifying glass, burning ants on the sidewalk on a hot summer day. He toys with life like his own personal game and nobody will ever be able to tell me other wise. I knocked on the bar with my glass a few times to get the bartender’s attention. He did not ask any questions, just merely filled my glass up to the brim. I pulled in the glass closer to my body and wrapped my hand around it just as I did the last one, but just as I was to pull it up to my lips something stopped me. I couldn’t quite explain it at first, but it was that feeling you get when someone is looking at you. I turned my head to the side to look around the bar, looking for someone who would be looking right back at me. My vision was a bit fuzzy, not just because of how many drinks I had put away, but because my hair was covering my face. I shook my head from side to side to get my hair somewhat away from my eyes, and what or WHO I saw made my stomach drop. As soon as our eyes connected there was a tension that slammed into my body, and if I still knew this person, the same happened to him. There are just seem to be times in your life when events happen that seem to be unbelievable, as if some work of fiction. As the old saying goes though, reality is sometimes strange than fiction. His eyes burned into mine, but not with the anger and hatred that I would have expected after all I had done to him. He seemed to be searching into my eyes, looking for the man that he knew years ago. I tried hard to just give him that emotionless stare, that stare that tells someone to stay the fuck away! Perhaps it would have worked with less alcohol and less emotion running through my body, but at this very moment perhaps I was pleading with him to speak to me. I saw his massive form make its way to a standing position. Picking up his glass, the man begins walking over my way. I stopped staring at him now, and just allowed him to sit right next to me. His glass dripped a bit as it landed on the bar and his hands nervously fiddled with it. I could hear his breathing; it was as erratic as mine was. The silence only lasted a little while, but it was an awkward silence, a silence that seemed to stop time. “Roberto ?” His voice sounded so strange to me. “It’s good to see you.” It felt as if a thousand years had passed since I heard his voice, that voice that has become my voice of reason, almost a guardian angel. I lowered my head a tad, not sure if I could look at this man directly in the eyes with us being this close’not yet anyways. “RaZ, it’s been a long time.” Indeed it had been a long time, not since I walked to his front door and punched him in the nose before walking off without a word have we actually seen each other. I figured I’d never have to look at his face again, in a way I had hoped. For all of the excuses I made for myself on why I did what I did to him, I felt bad for doing it. RaZiel was like my brother, and in many ways ? my father. There were many times in our years as compadres that he could have just left me for dead, but yet he was always there for me. I almost shattered his knee with a metal bat and yet he forgave me. I know I could never do such a thing, yet he did it without a second thought. For the longest time I laughed at this show of compassion and trust, I saw it as a weakness ? perhaps I was wrong. “Listen Roberto, I?” “You have nothing to say to me Raz, what possibly could there be?” “I miss you amigo.” I could feel all of my emotions swirling around inside of my stomach and it hurt like nothing I’ve ever felt before. I couldn’t allow him to see this though; I fought the urge to apologize to him, to say that I missed having someone to always watch my back. “You lied to me! You were fucking HER for all that time and you didn’t tell me. If we were so close why couldn’t you have told me?” He swallowed hard and looked down at his glass, obviously trying to find the words that were appropriate. “I was waiting for the right time Roberto; I didn’t know how to tell you at first. Its not like we did it to hurt you, Wylie and I are in love. After what she did to you with Alex I knew you wouldn’t approve, hell sometimes I didn’t understand it myself, but you can’t control who you fall for.” A thin smile came across my lips. “You got that right?” My words were merely whispered from my voice box but RaZiel certainly heard them loud and clear. The smile on my face faded quickly as I caught myself thinking of Mary and my love for her. I understood completely about finding love in the strangest of times and the most unique of people. It’s this reason that I knew I couldn’t hate him for what he had done to me, but something inside of me knew I couldn’t give in so quickly. I took my latest shot and slammed the glass down before looking over at RaZiel. “RaZ that girl fucked Alex Grey! That man tried taking my life?” “AND you returned the favor.” He turned his body to look at me face to face. “You two did more vicious things to each other than anyone I have ever met.” “The fact that she left me for that man should have stopped you!” “Roberto Alex acted like a completely different person with her, and YOU pushed her away when you got so caught up in DWA. She loved you and wanted nothing more than to marry you and live ?” “Happily ever after?” I pushed my hair to the side and for the first time I saw RaZiel completely. Sure my vision was still a bit blurry from the liquor but things were different. “I gave up on being happy long ago.” “Listen to yourself Roberto! For years I stood by you and listened to your problems. Do you know what your problem always has been” You feel sorry for yourself. You blame everyone for your problems, from Wylie to Alex and even God himself ? yet you don’t look inside of yourself. I know better than anyone that you have gone through hell?” “YOU DON’T KNOW SHIT!” these words came out almost as a threat. When the words I spitted out really sank in, the only thing that came to mind was that I lied to him. He knew more about me than anyone even Mary at this point. He was there with me when my brother was shot, and he was there when I won the World Title from Alex in DWA. After all of the memories of the past eight years flashed through my head I took my eyes from him and back at the empty shot glass in front of me. He waited awhile before speaking, obviously knowing that I was in deep thought. “I love you Roberto, like a father does a son. You have done wrong, but I understand that. Every time something has happened between us I have taken you back without question.” His emotions began boiling over, and it was amazing to see this 7’0? monster of a man, his eyes tearing up and his teeth grinding together. His eyes fall for a brief moment, but he quickly lifts them back to mine with a new intensity. “We have been through a lot ? I lifted you when Damen was murdered, helped you stand when you didn’t think you could.” I leaned forward and rested my elbows on the table, then buried my face into my hands. He caught me at the wrong time; I could normally have just pushed him away and left this place. Never would I have dreamed that I would let my emotions get the best of me. Maybe though, just maybe ? for once I didn’t have to. “I have been so alone for so long Raziel ?” he tried saying something but I lifted my head and cut him off. “There have been times when the demons that lurk inside of me have just become overwhelming. Every time I look at my god damn hands I can see the blood of countless numbers of people who I have hurt over the years. It can’t be scrubbed off with water and soap ? it’s too thick for that now. I have carried this ? extreme burden for so long and with no one to help.” “What about Mary?” “I love her RaZ, with everything I have but she has her own problems to deal with. Until we both air everything out, I can’t give her my burden too. Honestly I do not think she could handle my sickness as well as her own.” I swallowed hard forcing back these emotions ? but that’s when a single tear drop fell from my eye. A wobbly hand reached up and I wiped the tear away. I look at the small wet spot in my hand as if I were in a trance. “I can’t ? I can’t do this alone.” I felt ashamed in a way to cry ? here of all places. To me it felt like everyone was watching even though I KNEW no one even cared. I felt weak for giving in yet ? “You are not alone Roberto ? I am and will always be here for you.” Yet I feel stronger knowing that I have someone like David “RaZiel” Sandy to watch over me. After everything that has happened he is the one person I have always been able to trust. “Thank you David ?” It took me quite sometime but I have learned what life is really about. I am not some invincible force that will not be killed and can not be hurt. I am but a man not some fable or fairy tale. I can still do great things however. I can die just as my heart attack a few months ago proved to me, but I was given another chance. God has laughed at me for so long ? and maybe he gave me this new life because he hasn’t finished having fun with me or maybe, he is giving me back that life that I have dreamed of for so long. Perhaps those black clouds that have engulfed my life will lift ? leaving blue. “There’s one more thing Roberto?” A slight smile spreads across his lips as he drops an envelope in front of me. “Read it ? and then get back to me. I shall see you around.” He got up suddenly and walked off, not taking the time to look back even as he walked out of the now empty bar’s door. As he disappeared I opened the envelope and pulled out a card ? or more specifically a wedding invitation. Reading those names together, Wylie Bick and David Sandy strangely didn’t seem so strange anymore. A smile found its way to my face. A little note on the bottom read, “What’s a wedding without a best man?” Another tear fell from my eyes and landed on the invitation. “End of the line bud ? last call!” I looked up at the bartender as his words found me and my smile grew wider. “I hope not amigo ? I can only pray that this is just the beginning.” The beginning of that life that I could never find alone?
