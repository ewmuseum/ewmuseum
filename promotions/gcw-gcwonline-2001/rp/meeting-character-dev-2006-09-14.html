Date: 09/14/06 Disclaimer: This RP is for Character Development purposes only and not for DG. Phone Call South Dakota ? Undisclosed Location “Hello?” 'Jake? Is that you?' The female voice on the other end sounded heavy, almost out of breath. 'The one and only.' Jake made sure no one was near him either. 'Can you talk about your question?' A door shut, the volume carrying slightly onto the phone, before she spoke again. 'Yeah, I'm in my bedroom. Did you find anything out?' “I may have found out everything Dawn, but this isn’t the sort of thing you want to hear over the phone. Where are you at for the moment” We should meet.” There was a long pause and when she spoke again her voice sounded far away. 'Everything?...oh ah, I'm at the Estate right now...but so's everyone else. Where's a good place to meet?' Jake paused for a moment himself. After their last encounter, rumors had run rampant. They couldn’t meet in Dallas because everyone knew him there. They couldn’t meet in any public location, and that left little room. He did have the on place he hadn’t used in a while thanks to the fire, “You have a show coming up in Vegas, right?” 'Yeah, Deadly Games is coming up in Vegas...why?' “Well, we ever since had the fire at the Vegas arena everyone associated with Global has vacated.” Jake firmed up his idea in his head. “So, no one will be looking for me there and I own my house. You can fly in like you’re doing publicity or some shit, and then just take a detour to my house where no one will look for you. I’m a gentlemen, I’ll even cook.” Another pregnant pause, and this time she sounded a little more herself. 'You want me to come sleep over Jake?' “There’s a big difference between want and what you’re asking for. I’m only suggesting if we meet at the house no one will know about it this time, so we shouldn’t have any Internet rumors flying around. If you’re asking to stay I don’t think I would say no.” Jake gave a light laugh, enjoying the phone call more than he thought he could, and it helped keep the dark thoughts in his mind away. 'We'll see what happens, I may just grab a hotel room.' Her voice got faint, as if she was looking at something away from the phone. 'I'm pretty much good to go whenever...when should I meet you there?' “I can fly out of here in two days. Let’s meet on Monday. I’ll text you my Vegas information.” Jake realized something. “Uhm, any “special” dietary considerations? Vegan? Vegetarian? Blood sucker’just kidding.” She made a face. 'No nothing like that. You try to feed me tofu though and I can't be held responsible for my actions...' “The day a true Texan feeds a lady a tofu log, or wafer, or anything is the day my family rolls over in their graves.” Jake laughed. “I’ll have little specialty I do. I think you’ll be pleasantly surprised. See you Monday?” 'Yeah I'll see you then.' She started to fold the phone and then stopped. 'Jake...thanks.' “It’s what friends do.” Jake hung up the phone. The thoughts tortured him as he let them creep in. Even when they’re helping for personal reasons, too. I have to protect my friends and family, and that includes you Dawn. Meeting At Casa Del Perro Grande Las Vegas, Nevada Taking her eyes from text directions on her cell phone, Dawn eased her midnight blue corvette coup into an unfamiliar driveway, shifting down into park and turning the engine off. For a long moment she just looked at the house through her sunglasses, hands resting lightly on the wheel. 'God, what am I even doing here?' No answer leapt out so she unbuckled her seatbelt and slid out of the car, sneakers lightly hitting the asphalt of the driveway as she eased out. She'd dressed for the heat, black hip hugger cargo pants and a black and silver superman t-shirt that showed just the barest hint of midriff. Taking the front steps two at a time she paused at the door before knocking loudly. The door swung open, showing Jake looking casual as could be in blue jeans shorts, a black Global tee shirt and sandals. His warm smile was inviting and soothing, “Welcome to Casa Del Perro Grande.” Lowering her sunglasses, Dawn raised an eyebrow. 'Perro Grande?' “No innuendo’House of the Big Dawg.” He turned, moving his wide frame out of the way and swept his hand inward. He was looking over her shoulder to make sure no one was keeping tabs on her arrival. “Come on in.” Stepping inside she slipped out of both shoes, looking around with an appreciative eye. 'Very nice.' Jake smiled as he looked around the entryway, “Yeah, I hired a decorator to make it nice. It was supposed to be a sanctuary from everything, but in the end it didn’t turn out that way. I hardly ever stay here, but the maid keeps it clean and she and her family have a place to live in the small home out back. Maria’s a kind woman, and her family is great.” “Hey, no reason to bore you with old stories. I’ve got everything cooking in the kitchen.” He took a deep inhalation, enjoying the rich spices wafting from the right side of the house. “Can I get you a drink while we wait for the food?” Nodding, she followed him towards the dining room. 'Orange Juice if you have it...unless there's something specific I need to be drinking to fully enjoy this.' She couldn't help a slight smile, the combination of pleasant smells and the pleasing decor relaxing her. “Well, I have orange juice, but for the coconut curry chicken I’m making there’s a few rules. If you’re eating this, you don’t drink wine and liquor is often a little too heavy or mix poorly with the flavors. The orange juice can overpower the rest, but I do have a Texas specialty that pairs extremely well with the dish.” Jake explained as he led her through the sitting room with its richly appointed furniture, modern artwork and a framed Texas flag that appeared very old indeed. 'Coconut curry chicken? Sounds intriguing...so what's the Texas special then?' She took in her surroundings as they walked, eye flicking over every inch of the walls. “Not a big Thai food eater, huh?” Jake shrugged as they walked into the kitchen. The space was large, with the cooking surface in the middle where he could cook and visit with guests. A table in the connecting room had two simple place settings of bone china and silver ware. “I have Shiner Bock and Shiner Hefeweizen shipped up here to stock my fridge. A little beer from Texas that pairs exceptionally well with spicy food. Hefeweizen is a little more of a wheat brew, but the Bock is a very standard, straight forward beer above most of that micro brewery stuff out there.” 'I tend to prefer Viet Thai generally, but I'm always open to new experiences.' She paused near the door way, looking at the table. 'I don't normally drink beer, so whatever you suggest is fine.' “We’ll stick with the Bock. Can you grab a pair of bottles out of the fridge why I grab a pair of mugs?” Jake asked. 'Yeah no problem' She padded into the kitchen, white socks against the tiled floor and pulled open the door, bending down to search. 'Wow...couldn't tell you're a bachelor, I expected to find nothing other then beer and mustard in here.' “Maria takes good care of me when I’m in town, and the diet I use is super heavy in fresh vegetables, herbs and fruit along with lean proteins.” Jake opened up the small freezer down below and pulled out two frosted mugs. “I learned being a bachelor doesn’t mean not knowing how to cook, clean and do laundry. Pour those in here while I work on the meal. You’ve never had coconut curry chicken?” Pulling the beers from the fridge she straightened, sauntering to the counter and popping the caps before pouring them in. 'No, not that I remember anyway. Red and Green curry and Vindaloo, but not coconut curry.' “Well, honestly, kind of a hybrid Thai and Malaysian dish. Best of both worlds really. Throw onion, garlic, green onion, bell pepper, cilantro, and of course the chicken. Soften them down a bit after the chicken is cooked and add some chicken stock with yellow curry, paprika, pepper and a nice little liquid mix I make. Then once that boils down you add the coconut milk and let it thicken and boil for a while. All spicy and a tinge of sweet with all these flavors melting together.” Jake started laughing, “Yeah, when I cook I get a little obsessive compulsive. Sometimes it can be a pain in the ass.” She smiled, leaning back on the counter with her elbows to watch him work. 'I don't mind. I don't cook very often, but when I do I like to make sure everything's just right.' “A gastronomist after my own stomach.” Jake gave his wok one more stir, pleased with what he saw. He picked up the other pot and lifted the cover. He motioned, sending the aroma of the rice within toward Dawn across the space. “A nice little hint of jasmine in the rice adds a little something.” Leaning forward she breathed deeply, letting the smell settle into her throat and mouth. 'Mmm...very nice.' Leaning back again, watching his broad back, she couldn't help but frown a little. Was she really this cut off? Had she kept herself so isolated that she had forgotten what it was like to visit a...friend for something as simple as dinner? Her eyes clouded over as her mouth turned down slightly, the thought rolling over in her mind. Taking a large serving plate form the side counter, Jake poured the large helping of rice down over the plate. He used a plastic spoon to scoop the last of the rice out. Next he poured the coconut curry chicken over the center of the rice, allowing the sides not to be penetrated by the broth. He garnished with a handful of chopped green onions. “You ready to eat?” Starting slightly from her reverie, Dawn nodded. 'Oh yeah...did you need me to carry anything out?' “Leave the gun, take the cannoli.” “What?” “Nothing, just grab the beers.” Jake lifted the large platter and moved over to the table. The place settings were at one end so they stole a corner of the ten-person table. He set the platter down on the heat pads and pulled both chairs out. Grabbing the mugs up, Dawn followed him in, placing one mug by his seat and another by hers before plopping down and tucking her feet under. 'Smells good...you should do this for a living, less hazardous.' She grinned up at him. “The pay isn’t as good though, and how would I relieve my stress if I didn’t get thrown around every now and then?” Jake smiled at her. He took a seat along side her and motioned for her plate. He quickly served her a small amount of food, “I’m not recommending you eat only that much, but if it sucks then you aren’t feeling bad about leaving the rest.” For himself, Jake took a very healthy serving of the dish. He had not eaten quite as much as he should have, and his daily workout had drained him more than usual. “Dig in.” Dawn did so, making an appreciative noise in the back of her throat after the first mouthful. Scooping more onto her plate she chased it down with a pull of beer, then followed with another mouthful. It seemed like forever since she'd sat down to dinner with someone other then herself, and to say the food he served was well above what she was used to would have been a heavy understatement. Jake was eating too, not worrying about his appetite. He needed fuel for the rest of his training before flying back to Dallas for the next show. The spice of the curry was quick, and then a slow lingering presence cooled by the coconut milk. If he ever caught anything too hot like some of the peppers he added, the un bathed rice was an easy cool down for the heat. Finishing the first serving, he took another smaller serving, but there was still plenty left. He watched Dawn eat, and she seemed to like it. She wasn’t eating like a woman famished, but her eating habits did seem a little strange, as though she enjoyed this on many different levels. He ignored it as he finished his beer, “I’m grabbing another beer, would you like a second?” Nodding, she tossed back the rest of the first one. 'Please, if you wouldn't mind.' She helped herself to another, smaller serving, tucking in with gusto. Jake took both their glasses and put them in the sink. He took the time to rinse out the wok and the pot that had held the rice. Then he grabbed another pair of frosted mugs, and poured two fresh beers into the mugs. He returned to the table. “So, the food worked I guess?” She paused at that, a forkful halfway to her mouth as she blinked eyes gone a little wide. 'Worked? What do you mean worked?' “I mean it didn’t suck.” Jake leaned forward, staring at Dan with half confusion and half pity. “I’m not criticizing as it were, but your social skills seem to have suffered since the last time we had dinner in Japan.” She flushed at that, lowering the fork back to her plate. 'Sorry...when you said it worked I thought...I dunno, I thought maybe you'd put something in it.' She frowned, pushing the plate away. 'Sorry, I've just been kind of jumping at shadows since this all started.' “Yeah, I’m starting to wonder exactly what opinion you hold of me. I didn’t even put any MSG in this’let alone any rohypnol.” Jake leaned back in his chair. He lifted the frozen mug and took a long sip, “But you have been living with a lot on your plate here lately, and I’m not one to hold a grudge unless it’s part of the storyline or in the ring.” She shook her head slowly. 'I'm sorry. It's not that I think you're that kind of guy Jake, but since Japan it's been hard to trust anyone outside of our family. I keep seeing enemies where there aren't any, and making mountains out of mole hills.' Taking a long drink from her fresh mug, she frowned to find it already half empty. 'I just want this all to be over...I want to know me again...' “Old wise men say smart shit in smart ways, I usually just talk plainly. If I’ve learned anything in the hectic, fucked up years I’ve been on the planet it’s that you always know who you are, sometimes you just have to drop the anchor and not let the storm keep pushing you out of where you should be.” Jake sighed, knowing this was unavoidable. “And that brings us to the reason you’re here, doesn’t it?” Jake looked her in the eyes. “I have to ask one question first. Why did you come to me” Not our friend referred you, but why did you seek outside help on this?” Dawn bit her lip, hard. She'd been hoping that question wasn't going to come up, had been hoping she wasn't going to have to answer it...but the sad truth was she'd likely be answering that very same question a lot in the near future. 'I...didn't want anyone to know. If it's bad...really bad...then I'd rather that Lia doesn't know about it. That way I can take care of it on my own, and I'm not an added weight on her shoulders. This is my problem...I don't want anyone else suffering for it.' “Well, time to lay the cards on the table. I went to see my friend Gray Wolf, and he told me the most knowledgeable man would be Old Man Soaring Eagle. So I took a drive while my brothers’well, while they did something else.” Jake held those cards close. “I spent the morning when I sent that text message to you with him and his family. While we were talking, I came to learn a few things.” “His nephew is an asshole, but his nephew’s fianc” is named Terra. She has a lot of friends in common with some of my acquaintances. I believe they call themselves the Order Dawn. Perhaps you’ve heard of them.” He watched her closely, knowing this was just the first of many startling revelations. She blinked slowly at him. 'The Order...as in Blaise's Order? As in Ian Childress' Order? As in The Order I see at least once a week on a regular basis?' “It goes deeper than that.” Jake thought about how to say this to his friend. “The Order has been keeping tabs on you, and they think the person I went to get information on was you. Do you know anyone else who is in the Order?” She stared at him for a long moment, the full weight of what he was saying starting to press onto her mind. 'The Order thinks you were asking about me...' She scrubbed her face with her hands. 'I know a lot of people who're in the Order Jake. Blaise, Ian, Ukyo, Gray, Josh, Dani...it goes on.' “And on that list it includes a name very close to you Dawn.” Jake took a sip of beer before continuing. “Someone you know better than anyone else.” She shook her head fiercely, black-blonde hair flying out as she did. 'No you're wrong, Lia's not part of that, not part of them...but they do talk to her...they'd tell her for sure.' Jake could see the small cracks forming in her exterior, “This is where I deliver the bad news before the good news. According to what they told me, Lia was briefed on the Order’s observations of you. The bad news is she knew about it the whole time, whether a member or an associate. She knew you were being watched, and for some reason she didn’t share that with you.” For a long moment there was just silence. Dawn stared moodily into her beer, her hair hanging in waves around her face, hands white knuckled around the frosted mug. Her face twitched occasionally, here and there as she tried to hold in the tempest that had started swirling in her belly. 'She...knew...all along...' “Before that seal holding everything in pops loose let me tell you the’good news’I guess its good.” Jake didn’t want Dawn going crazy. “Terra told me that when Lia was told, she wasn’t concerned. She said whatever this was that is happening isn’t something to worry about.” “Having said that, you came to me outside a very close circle of people who have experience with these matters. I need to know why you did it, because unfortunately Lia is going to know I was poking around soon enough. The Order says they are the only ones who can help you with this, but I don’t think you trust them for some reason I don’t know.” 'I told you why already...' She mumbled, '...I didn't want Lia to know. I didn't want ANYONE to know.' When she looked up her eyes were blazing, caught like green fire in her sockets. 'Don't you get it?! If anything Lillith told me is true then I'm a danger to everyone around me. I could, and will, turn on everyone I love just because 'It's my nature'. If nobody knew then I could...I could...handle it. But now...' “You can still handle it.” Jake tried to rally control of the emotions back to his side. “We spoke of your mother before, and don’t mention her name no matter what happens.” She blinked. 'My mother? The woman who came to visit me? Why? I thought all speaking her name would do would let her know I was talking about her...' “Right, and allow her to find us. I don’t think we know where everyone involved stands right now. We need to figured out everyone’s motivations.” Jake was gesturing with his hands as he spoke. “Who is on your side, and who is lined up against you Dawn” That goes back to the original question of what is happening to you. That is even more complicated.” 'On my side? Lined up against me' She stared at him, openly confused. 'I don't understand what you're saying. You think Ro...my mother is against me?' “I don’t know. Dawn, I’ve been looking through old legends, having my contacts do the same and searching my own heart.” Jake felt all his own confusion bubble up. “You still don’t know what happened to me a few years ago, but after it happened I am not simply a man anymore. I am’something slightly different.” “I can sense good and evil. I just’know certain things. When I look at you, I sense both the good and the evil inside of you. The more I dig into it, the more I know your mother was a succubus, but that didn’t explain everything we found when we were looking into who you are.” That was one of the words she'd come across in Cyrus' book of demonology she'd hoped to never hear again. For a moment the world seemed to spin around her, and closing her eyes didn't help any. 'Succubus...demon...' She sucked in a deep breath, 'So if my mother was full demon, that makes me...' She looked up abruptly, holding the mug up. 'Is there anymore?' “You have something else inside of you. I couldn’t explain it on my own, so I went to Old Man Soaring Eagle. There’s the legends and mythologies the world is built on, and somewhere behind all those foggy veils is the truth. The only thing that made sense couldn’t be that we could find, but then I was corrected.” Jake finished his beer before he continued, “I need another beer to even approach this subject. You need a refresher?” She nodded. 'Yeah, that's actually what I meant when I asked if there was any more. Any more beer.' She smirked, holding her mug out to him. Jake deposited the mugs in the sink, took two more frosted mugs out and filled them once again with the Shiner Bock. He brought a pair of extras over so they wouldn’t continue to stop. “That work?” Accepting her mug, Dawn took a quick pull off of it and nodded. 'That works fine. Now what do you mean, something else?' “When I sense the divine within you, I mean it. Old Man Soaring Eagle explained in their history and beliefs, angels and devils come from the same place, just like in our own. The Devil was an angel who was cast down, and then he begins building Hell as an affront to God.” Christian history was well known. “For them, they know angels and demons too, but they are the same and make a choice. Your mother is a succubus, we know that as much as we can know anything, but that’s only half the equation.” “Physically, I believe your father is another divine being. Whether it was someone your mother seduced, someone she was with through other means or whatever it was there is the unmistakable truth that the other half of you is what I would call an angel.” The beer was helping with the courage a little, more from his reluctance to burst the bubble around her world than anything else. “You are the offspring of angel and demon, and there are quite a few people who know about this.” For a moment she said nothing, watching him through slitted green eyes and running the tip of one finger around the rim of her frosted mug. 'An angel...THAT'S RIDICULOUS!' Shoving her chair back Dawn stood sharply, hands slapping down on the table hard enough to make the surface groan in protest. 'My Father died, DIED, of cancer Jake, you of all people should know that! Angels don't die of cancer, from what I understand they don't die at all. Your information is bogus, there's no angel in here...' She tapped her right breast, high up near her collar bone. “You ever see the Exorcist?” She blinked, some of her ire fleeing as she stared. 'What?' “It’s a simple question. Nineteen seventy-three movie, Linda Blair and the pea soup. The Exorcist, did you ever watch it?” The off the wall question had served the purpose, slowing down her rage. She shrugged, 'Maybe when I was younger. What's that got to do with anything?' “A demon, possessing a little girl, and taking total control. You have her being crazy, but what if possession didn’t make the person go completely nuts and act weird, especially if instead of domination like demonic possession it is more of the temporary possession but something else.” “You know who you’re father was, and he was a good man. I know because I knew him, and I can tell you he was a good man.” Jake sipped his beer, letting the affirmation sink in. “He may not have been alone when he and your mother conceived you, and that would explain a lot of this.” “I’m not challenging your memory or love of your father, but considering who you mother is, who she would have been around and everything else this is a possibility.” He picked at the last remnants of the food. “After speaking with Old Man Soaring Eagle, I found out it isn’t crazy. They have legends of this occurring.” Dawn's hand slipped from the table to hang loosely at her sides, her expression curiously blank as she gazed impassively from behind green eyes gone suddenly clouded. 'You think an angel possessed my father when he was with my mother? That doesn't even make any sense, why would an angel kiss a devil, nevermind sleep with one.' “Uhm, they apparently get the same kind of urges that we get as humans, and are sensitive just like us to the charms of a succubus.” Jake began laughing. “Actually, at first I thought you were crazy Dawn. Probably just like you did, but that isn’t the case. There is history here, and this seems the most likely solution. So all those feelings inside are one side struggling with the other, and you’re in the middle.” She slumped back into her chair like a puppet with it's strings suddenly cut, staring moodily at a point outside the window. 'So...you're telling me I'm not my fathers daughter.' “No, I’m saying you are every bit your father’s daughter. Genetic tests even confirm you are your father’s daughter from what we can tell, and the medical records I’procured’also speak to you being your father’s offspring.” Jake stood up. He finished his beer and refilled it. He refilled Dawn’s too. “Can I show you something?” 'Yeah, sure.' She took the mug a little numbly, her eyes looking glazed over. Jake led Dawn past the living room to the back patio doors. He opened the French doors and they walked out onto the patio with the sculptured rock garden and cacti garden their view in his acre-sized backyard. Although still hot in Vegas, the outdoor air conditioners kept the patio pleasant along with the breeze coming off Jake’s pool. “I wanted to show you this.” Jake led her over to a painting sitting on an easel. “I picked this up several years ago when Xavier had some problems. The painter isn’t famous, and the work isn’t the best, but the emotion is raw and the meaning is real for anyone where you are at.” The painting showed a small figure sitting at the edge of a long wooden dock, feet in the water. To their right was a calm blue sea, serene and pretty like some unknown ideal you could never truly find in the world. To the left, the sea was stormy, with dangerous peaks ready to overpower the shoreline. The fragile figure in the middle seemed somehow even smaller caught between such forces, and she might be swept off the pier at any moment. “You’re father anchored you to yourself, but the two opposites inside will fight for control Dawn.” He spoke softly, hoping the visual might help her. 'I'm not anchored at all.' She whispered, eyes on the painting. 'That's why this is so important.' Turning to face him, she looked up through the inches that separated them. 'I don't even know who I am anymore Jake, I haven’t for a long time.' Turning back to him again she hugged her arms to her midsection, one hand touching lightly on the collar around her neck. 'The only thing I know for sure is this.' Jake reached out, softly resting his giant hand on her shoulder, “You’re more anchored than you realize. Your father gave that to you, and all you will have to do is make a choice. In the end, you are only responsible for the decisions you make. The choice is yours to do with as you will.” Leaning into his hand, she shook her head slightly. 'I don't even know what my choices are. You tell me I'm both, that I'm supposed to be somewhere in the middle...but I don't even know what that means. I didn't even know anything about this...angel when this all started, just mom.' “That leads us to what to do about it. We can work through this together, I’ll stand right next to you the whole time.” Jake felt sudden sorrow for her as he embraced her other shoulder and squeezed lightly, “Where we go from here is up to you. I can keep searching on my own, or we can approach the Order. In the end, I’ll still stand next to you wherever you go.” 'If they already know...knew all along...then there's no real choice.' She laughed bitterly. 'I went to so much trouble to keep all of this from everyone, and they all knew all along. Gods I feel so stupid.' “Maybe they’re just letting you work this out on your own. If you thought they interfered you might be bitter, and no one likes bitter beer face, right?” She laughed at that, leaning back against his hands and sighing. 'Well, whatever the reason, the cat's out of the bag now. Although I bet I have some explaining to do when I get home.' “You just might. Give me a hand cleaning up real quick?” Jake smiled at her as he turned back to the house. “You didn’t think you wouldn’t be doing any work, did you?” She smirked, following him up the steps. 'I had hoped, being your guest and all, but it was a forlorn hope.' As she raised her hand to brush aside her hair she eyed the mug in her other hand, sighing a little. 'Jake?...can I stay here tonight? I'm in no shape to drive now.' “An invitation to stay at my house not at yours” Not quite what I hoped for, but you have to start somewhere.” Jake enjoyed playing with Dawn, she was always a smart ass after his own heart. “I suppose I could let you stay in my bed.” 'Ha!' She stopped at that, one hand on her hip and the other snapping her fingers in mid-air. 'In your dreams 'Big Dawg'. Besides, you can't fool me, I saw the hallway full of doors, it's not like there isn't space.' She grinned all the same, feeling some of the tension ease out. “I meant that I owned the bed you would be sleeping in.” He shook his head while making a “tsk-tsk” sound at her. “You have such a dirty mind Dawn. A dirty, dirty mind.” Rolling her eyes, Dawn breezed past him, her long hair blowing out behind him. 'Oh of course, your intentions were wholly innocent...' She turned around once inside, pulling down one eyelid and sticking out her tongue. 'Not!' Jake Hix didn’t say anything is reply. He was still processing everything they had just discussed, but the humor had diffused the tension nicely. And at least he could watch her go.
