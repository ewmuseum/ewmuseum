Date: now 'YOU STAND NO CHANCE!' Clap Clap Clapclapclap! 'YOU STAND NO CHANCE!' Clap Clap Clapclapclap! 'YOU STAND NO CHANCE!' Clap Clap Clapclapclap! Oh lord, not the ever repetitive insert chant with clapping motion. WHAT WILL I EVER DO?! 'But didn't you hear? Bryan Mayhem is a vile evil monster!' Yep, read it. Seen it on the front page. 'What do you think? Scary end of the world kinda shit huh?!' Not really. Just because someone is a complete fucking putz in the ring doesn't get me shaking in my space boots when I got to take care of my business. 'BUT HOW?! WHY NOT?!' Simple. It's over inflated chest pounding. Lions mouth kinda shit. The story is basically from his own mouth, and all it does is go into beating his chest. Why should I be scared? In his tenure here has he ever won a title that means jack shit? Has he even really had a shot at being the guy? In MMA, this guy is comparable to Joe 'Diesel' Riggs: look tough, act tough, but when hit in the face by someone with superior skill he trembles like a little girl. 'BUT HE'S DAMN NEAR KILLED PEOPLE!' Like that fucking matters. He hasn't killed me. I ain't sitting in a damn wheel chair. 'BIG TALK!' I'm undefeated. Why exactly should I give a damn what some article says? What some people think? Hell I could point to the 2005 Mark Award for Wrestler Of The Year and say 'DUDE'S IN TROUBLE' but I'll refrain. 'BUT HE'S BIIIIIIIIIIIIG!' Yeah, and he's got KISS paint on his face. What's the point? Dude got a match with me because he's been a part of this place for a long time and simply put is going to be another one of those nice little names to put on my list of beaten opponents here. 'Whatever you say man...' Hey...it's not my windpipe that's going to be crushed by the Gridlock. I'll bitch slap that Muta paint job off his face before he can think twice about feuding with Khali here again. *** Me=Car Mayhem=Kid *** Eva VanLorne had these rocking headaches ever since Warpath, thanks in part to the concussion received from her nemesis Vivica J. Valentine. It was a cascade of emotions that throttled her that seemingly made it all the worse. Her head throbbing, the doctors saying that her career had a high probability of being over...she couldn't necessarily cope easily. She wanted vengeance, some form of validation. Vivica would be limp in her fingers at some point, and she would relish the chance to do the same damage that was done to her. Or so she hoped. And as she laid in her hospital bed, Theresa sat in the sterile waiting room with crayons. She sighed meekly as she stared out the window, bored of the dated coloring books the nice lady at the desk gave her. There was only so many times you could color the Power Rangers before you seriously felt like curling up and nodding off. She tried to watch the television, but it was all in Spanish. Walking into the waiting room, Brandon Youngblood looked over at Theresa as she stared out the window at the parking lot. He put pulled his Oakley’s up onto his shaved head, putting his hands into his khaki shorts. Having just coming from the gym, he threw on a black BloodWorks shirt, not really expecting to dress up to see one of his many benefactors with his business endeavor. 'I know what your thinkin' kid...and unless you're next to a hydrant you'd shrivel up out there from heat exhaustion.' Theresa almost instantly lit up, turning her head and running towards Youngblood, grabbing him for a big old bear hug. 'BRANDON!' 'Lookin' pretty bored there kid. How's mom's?' He said, returning the hug. 'She's fine...well...I guess so...maybe...I dunno. They say that she's gonna come out of it. Her career might be over though...' Brandon could only sneer. 'Fuckin' dumb bitch.' He seethed under his breath only to get a surprise smack right across the face from Theresa. 'What did I tell you about that language mister?!' Brandon just looked at her. 'You know if you were any other person...' Theresa batted her eyes. 'You'd just take it and like it.' Brandon could merely smirk, turning his attentions to sifting through the convoluted response Theresa gave as to how Eva was doing post concussion. 'How's she really? And none of this spinnin' in circles crap.' 'Well...' Theresa trailed off, 'I think it's upset her. I mean, she's got bad headaches is what she keeps saying. What's happened is like those bad dreams. I think she never thought she'd get hurt by those bad people.' 'Well...people sometimes don't want to think of the bad things that could happen...you know. If you worry about it too much...they can get in your head. Play with your mind. Have you questioning yourself. Your momma was brave for taking them head on...' 'But what are you going to do about it? They hurt her and nobody can stop them now.' Theresa said bluntly. Brandon stepped back, his easy going mood almost instantly dissipating. 'Look kid...I'm not in this to fight a war. I'm not a hero Theresa. I've explained this to you before. When I go out and fight them...I'm not doing it for people...I'm doin' it for myself. And I'd fight your momma the same as I would them. To me...their all the same.' 'But you're better then that!' Theresa yelled. 'I know you! You're always acting so tough but beneath that you're a nice person. I KNOW IT! I'VE SEEN IT!' She was the only one that didn't see him as a monster. Maybe it was fitting that this little girl had faith in the humanity of this callous assassin. Maybe there was something beneath the surface. Act nice to people and they make it up in their own minds about who you are and what you stand for. Santa is real, the Tooth Fairy will come, the monsters can't hurt you with the night light on, and Brandon Youngblood is going to save GCW from the plague that is Dies Irae. 'You give me way to much credit kid. When you and your mom's gonna realize that?' Don't take it completely away from them though. Let them believe in their fantasies. But sooner or later Theresa would realize that there were real monsters, and her self proclaimed hero was a ghastly one. 'Tell your mom I said hey...alright?' And with that he left, almost as if he could be burdened to continue this happy little facade of depth. He had much more important things to worry about then the problems of others. Himself. *** I segway because this is important. This is from the hip kinda shit and it needs to be said. No jokes, no plot devices, no nothing. Bryan Mayhem fashions himself a monster. This atrocity against all of mankind that should be put down for the betterment of the world. Too dangerous to even look at. And he's crippled men in his wake and has all these added elements to make this seven foot tall 'monster' look and seem badder then he already is. See...I'm going against a self proclaimed monster, but the 'monster' doesn't understand what it is getting into. I don't have a KISS paint job...I don't have to put my women in bondage to beat them up...I don't need to climb out of a pile of rubble like Super Shredder to stake claim to my inhumanity. I am supposed to be afraid of you. Your very shadow lurking over my like some vulture should make me piss myself where I stand. But it doesn't. I'm approaching you not out of fear...but willingness to prove a point. See...I didn't do anything but break my wife's jaw for giving me lip. I did drugs upon drugs upon drugs. I've faced evil men and taught them a thing or two about themselves to the tune that they say 'Goddamn...that guy is FUCKED THE FUCK UP!'. All I live for is the sport of professional wrestling and my success within it. My life is dedicated to the small portion of my time in a ring where I legally maim and hurt people. I am not a deep person. I am not even an emotional person. I understand my flaws...my lack of humanity. And I haven't just come to accept it...but to love it. Why is that? Because emotion is weakness Mayhem. I might be an asshole, I might be shallow, but at least I do not get caught up in the same stupid multifaceted relationships that make life not worth living. I am selfish...callous...and the rest of the world can kiss my ass for all I care. I am great. I've been telling myself that since day one and it's not stopped me since. All I've done is grown up and accepted my failings as to learn from them so I can stake my claim as the best wrestler to ever lace up a pair of boots. See, I might not be seven foot tall. I might not do the crazy, over the top, sports entertainment things you do. I live in the reality that I was put on this Earth to wrestle. The rest is simply semantics. And no matter what...nothing else can possibly satisfy me. I won't have a white picket fence and a huge house on the prairie. I won't hug my kids on their way to the school bus. I won't have a life after the mat because a life without wrestling for me is worse then death. But I do not fear it. I do not fear anything...not a thing. That's not just bullshit beating the chest kind of 'pull the wool over your own eyes' stuff. See...I looked into the Deville's eyes. I heard Mike Randalls snarl. I've dealt with OD's and withdrawals. I've dealt with pain. All I do is lift, train, and prepare. I've been told if I bled again I would die and did so regardless. I charge into battle with foes who greatly outnumber me just because I'm feeling frisky. So while you sit at home hallucinating blood falling from your ceiling, I will be preparing myself to beat you. See...your goddamn clumsiness is what has made you into this monster. Take away your face paint and your goddamn fake ass unreal gimmick and you're just a Joe Blow with a role to play. I don't play roles. I'm grade A, one hundred percent real and will not back down no matter what someone tries. It's why I'm undefeated Mayhem. UNDEFEATED! And I didn't just walk all over a bunch of jobbers to get to where I am. I got here by beating some of the most reputable names in this sport. And while I've been blazing a trail I've had to find myself carrying deadweight shit like you to a new era of promise. Which...I have no problem doing. But for some punk ass guy with an apparent violence fetish to think that I am some sort of stepping stone...that I'm gonna be scared? PFFT! You need to get it right dude. You're the stepping stone. You're my forth win. You'll throw some shots at me like the Undertaker and I'll grab your arm and snap it at the elbow, then shove it up your ass. And you want to know why? Because I pretty much can do whatever I want and you can not possibly stop me. As someone once said: Beat me if you can. Survive if I let you. This is Brandon Youngblood, making sure to dust my goddamn shoulder off.
