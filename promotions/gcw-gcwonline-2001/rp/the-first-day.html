The First Day

Date: Today He waited outside, a little afraid to head in. The show was supposed to be a good one, one he’d been waiting to see for quite a while. The only problem was he knew that once he entered it would all be the same thing. Liquor flowing like water, women throwing themselves around looking for someone to go home with for the night. It was the whole punk rock atmosphere hard drinking, hard fucking, total anarchy. If he were to be honest with himself it was the type of thing that had really drawn himself to the music and the culture in the first place. Well, that and his father didn’t approve, but really that was just a bonus. The thing is now that he’d given up drinking, he wasn’t sure where he fit in anymore. Without the aid of a few stiff drinks, it became a lot more difficult to have a good time. He just really wasn’t sure how those straight edge kids had always been able to do it. Oh well, just another cross to bear in his new life he supposed. He’d figure out how to have fun again, especially now that he was passed all the bad. Well he hoped he was anyway. If nothing else things were definitely starting to look up. Having finally defeated Lestatt Knight he’d resolved to put the past completely behind him. Of course that was proving to be easier said than done. The AA meetings were actually helping quite a bit, he could actually go five minutes now without having a drink. It was just in the dead of night that things really started to get bad. He missed her then, and more than anything he just wanted to call her, see how she was doing. Of course he knew that’d be a mistake, she didn’t want to hear from him, and would probably be better off not being reminded about all the bad stuff that had gone on this year. He really didn’t need to be either, but still he couldn’t forget. Lance’s face still haunted him at night, his begging for mercy would still bubble up into his ears every now and again. Yet those nights were starting to come less frequently. He knew that soon they’d fade away all together, he’d never loose the guilt, but at least he might start being able to sleep at night. Shifting uncomfortably, he head the band starting to tune up inside. The show was going to be starting sooner than he’d expected. If he wanted to get close enough to see anything he was going to head in pretty quickly. Sighing he lit a cigarette, and watched as two twenty-something girls headed in. They were both good looking and he was pretty sure one of them had glanced in his direction. Maybe he could have a little fun tonight after all. He really hadn’t been able to be with a woman since he’d last seen Sheena. It almost felt like he was cheating on her. Of course he knew it was over between them, but that didn’t make him love her any less. Yet it was just another one of those things he was going to have to put behind him. Shuffle that love off into a little secret section of his head, lock it away and never let it hurt him ever again. That was the plan anyway. Flicking the cigarette out into the street, Stevens buried his hands in his pockets, and headed inside. Looking inwardly Stevens knew this was going to be one of the most important moments of the rest of his life. Everything had just started going well in his professional life, it was time to start getting the personal life sorted out. This is the first day of the rest of his life, and it was time to start making that life a happy one.
