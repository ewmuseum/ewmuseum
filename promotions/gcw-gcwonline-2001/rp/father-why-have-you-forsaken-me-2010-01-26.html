Date: 01/26/10 "Bryan, why does daddy always play with so many different girls?" Bryan puts the shovel down and wraps his left arm around his little brother and kneels in front of him. Bryan towers over a much smaller Jason. If this was a litter there is no doubt Jason would be considered the "runt." Bryan looks his younger brother in the eyes. "Jason, please pay no attention to what father does, because he is a very bad evil person." Jason looks back at his big brother, his best friend, his idol with confusion, than tilts his head, scratches his temple with his left hand, "Evil?" Sighing deeply remembering that education is a lacking contributor in his brother's young life, Bryan ponders a way to explain to Jason just what evil is. "Jason, you don't know what evil is?" Scratching his temple again Jason shyly shakes his head no. He's 8 yrs old and his education was what Bryan has taught him and the little he absorbed in school before being pulled out by their father for reasons beyond his control. Jason was extremely little, extremely timid, and was often sought out by bullies. Jason attended "Shellrock Elementary" which is a one classroom building in the slums of Belmont. He was expelled from school for being "stupid" according to most of the other kids. Bryan was at the same school, and was also later dismissed from his education, however he was 13 at the time, and he was expelled because he broke the arm of a bully that gave little Jason a fat lip. "Jason, tell me what you think of daddy." Jason sat there and let the question absorb deeply. He put his hand on his chin, rubbed it, than his face grew stern and angry. "He's mean. He hits you, hits mommy, and hits me. Sometimes he makes me touch him when he has his pee pee out. Than there's other times Bryan where he takes all my pets and kills them. I don't like daddy Bryan, I don't." Bryan closed his eyes and listened to what his brother was saying. He can almost smell the alcohol erupting from his father's breathe as he's scolded and punched repeatedly for whatever it is he's angry at. Bryan was never molested, but lately Jason has been stand offish, almost not himself. Last week during harvest of wheat was the first time Jason mentioned the inappropriate touching by their father. "Jason. You just explained evil." Grinning slightly, voice cracked, "Really?" Rustling Jason's hair, "Yes, yes really." "But, you don't do any of that kind of stuff do you?" Bryan stares at his brother, he looks at him like Jason just slapped him across the face. "Why do you ask?" Jason scratches his temple again, and kind of squints his eyes in deep thought. "Well because Daddy told mommy that you are an evil f word that will be taught a lesson." Bryan's eyes widen as a red flush overcomes him. The rage has fueled him to a point where he may explode. He cracks his knuckles, than moves his neck to the left, a snap is heard, than back to the right followed by another snapping sound. "When did you hear that?" Jason looks into the sky, squints one eye, "I think the other day after my spanking. He hurt my little butt, but than said that. It hurts still Bryan, so you better watch out." Bryan clenches his teeth and fist in anger. The red flush is still prominent, it's almost apple red. The only thing keeping Bryan from resembling an apple is his long coarse black hair. "Don't worry about father, and don't worry about me, I have it all under control." Looking confused again, "What do you have under control. Bryan what are you talking about?" Bryan is looking at the house, staring over the head of his younger brother. He see's the silhouette of his father in the window. He's in the kitchen, which obviously means he's in and out of the refrigerator. The only thing in the fridge earlier this morning was an open can of sardines, spoiled milk, and a shit ton of beer. Bryan would bet his entire life on the fact that the beer is practically empty. A loud crash is heard from the house. Jason and Bryan both jump at the sound. Jason cowers, his face turns white. He knows what is coming, and he knows there is no place to hide. There's a loud howl, than more crashing noises from the house. The backdoor is busted open, the screen door hanging desperately on one hinge. "Run Jason!" Bryan tells his little brother. Bryan knows that one of them is in for a beating, a beating that he won't remember, but one they will never forget. Jason stares towards the house as his father comes tumbling towards them. He does not hear what Bryan is telling him, otherwise he might have ran for his life. "JASON GODDAMNIT BOY! YOUR BLOODY CAT JUST PISSED ON MY COUCH!" He's within five feet of his two children. Both are now frozen with fear. He gets within inches of Bryan's face, well his chest to be honest. Bryan was only a teenager and he stood well over 6'5". Bryan smelled the beer on his breathe. The apple red in his face turned to chalk white. "Move boy! Don't try to be a hero if you know what's good for you." He spits at Bryan's feet than attempts to move the oldest to get at the youngest, however Bryan doesn't budge. This act of insubordination sends the old man over the edge. He sends a hard jab to the gut of Bryan causing him to keel over in pain. He than does the unspeakable. The last thing Bryan heard was Jason screaming NO!, right before a full beer bottle was busted over the back of Bryan's head. The pain on the back of his head is excruciating. One side of his face is resting in a puddle of beer, blood, and broken glass. The stench of the beer awakens Bryan. He puts his hand to the pain on the back of his head and feels something wet and disgusting. He pulls his hand away and looks at blood on his hand. He pushes himself from the ground and pulls a small piece of broken glass from his cheek and slowly gets to his feet. He is trying to remember what and where he is. The world around him is spinning counterclockwise. He slouches over to alleviate the spins and realizes what just happened. In a matter of seconds the pain and the spins cease to exist. "Jason!" He yells. What the fuck did that man do? Bryan hears a sob followed by a whimper over by the barn. He gathers his bearrings and hauls ass towards the barn. When he get's there he is smashed in the face by something far more painful than the beer bottle. He is not hit literally, but what he see's hurts him more than anything he's ever been hit with. Bent over one of the pig's trough is Jason. His jeans on his rear end are stained purple. Bryan is old enough to realize it is blood stained, and that his father took another violent step. Standing just to Jason's left, his overalls still clinging to his ankle is his father. The man who's seed created both of them stares at Bryan, no remorse on his face, no sign of disgust, he's actually sport a shit eating grin. "What the fuck you looking at boy?" Bryan looks back at Jason who's body is convulsing with each deep sob. Jason does not move from the trough. He's either too scared or too embarassed or a combination of the two. Bryan than moves his glare towards his father again. The chalk white in his face goes back to its original apple red. A tear falls from Bryan's eye, something he does not ever remember doing during his entire childhood. No matter how severe the beating, he never cried, he never gave his old man the satisfaction of knowing he was hurt. Today however, today was different. "You going to cry too? Did I raise two fucking pussies or something?" He reached down to pull up his overalls, when his nose is met with a knee. Bryan ran towards him and raised his knee so hard that his father's face exploded on contact. The leg of Bryan's pants are now caked with blood, cartilidge and whatever else was looming in his father's nose. Bryan clasps his hands together and swings back down on the neck of his father. The man lands with an oomph sound, blood and snot leaking from his busted nose. Bryan acts quickly and lands a giant foot to the side of his father's head flipping the man onto his back. Jason finally turns around, face smeared with tears and dirt just watches his brother beat the holy hell out of their father. He kneels down because sitting hurts too much and watches. Jason does not attempt to stop any of the mayhem going on around him. Jason knows the pain will all be over soon. Their father's face is a bloodied disfigured mess. He spits a tooth out and attempts to get up but is met with another kick to the face. More teeth follow the first one. Lying in the dirt, naked, with his overalls still around his ankles, he makes another attempt to get up. Bryan lifts his leg and drops it with all his might right onto his private section. Their father feels a pop sound as one of his testicles explodes under the foot of his own son. The pain is so excruciating he shits himself, and that is followed by numerous squirts of blood from the top of his now deflated penis. He cowers in a little ball in torment. He begins to vomit violently all over himself. Jason once again just looks on. His face, other than smeared, is emotionless. Bryan's on the other hand is still enraged. A life of abuse, a life of watching his brother, his only friend he ever knew being beaten and raped and treated like an animal is now coming to the forefront. The stench is the barn is becoming intense. Vomit and feces, integrated with blood and the normal smell of the barn is becoming too much for anyone to handle. Their father is still in fetal position. Screams and moans curdled from blood, snot, and teeth in his throat are all that is heard. Bryan looks behind him. Shovels, hoes, and hay forks are hanging in their perspective places. Bryan looks back down at his beaten and bloodied father than back at the instruments behind him. He grabs the fork and stands over his father. "Shut up and lay on your back" Bryan demands of his father. The man that gave life to him, lies on his back. There is no fear in his eyes. His breathing is stuttering and raspy, his eyes are bloodshot, and is face is caked with blood, but there is no fear. "Kill me. Please. But remember, I did it because I loved you and your brothers." Bryan screams and drops the fork square into the chest. His father's body instinctively grabs hold of the fork and the body begins to convulse violently. Blood spews from his mouth, he twitches a few more times than stops. Lying skewered on the ground was the O'Neil Boys' very own Boogey Man. Jason limps over towards Bryan, who is still holding to the fork buried deep into their father's chest and puts his arms around his waist. "Thanks for helping me Bryan." "Or you can completely ignore me. Whatever you want." Bryan shakes away the cobwebs and realizes he's in a jailhouse. He looks at Jason who is at a desk getting his items. "Huh?" "I said thanks for helping me Bryan." Bryan looks confused, than realizes his hands are clenched so tight that his hands are beginning to throb in pain. "For bailing me out? You ok?" Bryan opens his hands, looks at his brother and smiles. "Sure, sorry I was just thinking, that's all." Jason pats him on the back, and walks ahead of him as they exit the jail. Bryan still looking down at his little brother, but smiles again. They have overcome so much.
