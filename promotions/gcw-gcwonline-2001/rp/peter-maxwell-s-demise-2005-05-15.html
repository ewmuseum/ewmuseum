Peter Maxwell's demise

15 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/15/05 I am all but determined. I might have lost a certain respect with Steven when I ran my mouth, but let’s just hope actions speak louder than words. I did the impossible tonight. The devil himself is moving to warm climates. Peter Maxwell left the building with a forever etched in ink loss to a massive underdog. I will do anything’Anything’to be number one. (My head lay resting on a pure white, feathered pillow fluffed to perfection. My nose bandaged tightly in hopes to preserve any unharmed cartilage. I begin to run out my career in front of my eyes having beaten the poison deteriorating GCW. You would think it ends in some mansion counting my endless stacked of fortune, or cruising in my Ferrari on the California coast. But nether dream is close to the sense I have in my mind. I simply see myself with lifted arms holding the only thing that has had true meaning in my life in the past few years, The World Title. My facial expression worth more than a thousand words, holds the image of tears flooding my eyes and success overwhelming my entire body. There’s not much a man could say in such a shocked situation but my excitement was running wild. I couldn’t keep my mouth shut.) “Doctors say my sense of smell might be altered and my nose will bleed from time to time. And people will ask me one simple question’Was is all worth it” And there would be only one response that could describe how proud I was of myself’I wouldn’t trade it for the world?” (I rise from the pillow as my head feels as though it were surviving a head on collision with a speeding train. I grunt in agony but manage myself into a calm manner.) “I ended the longest streak in GCW history. Over three years this man has been bullying and bossing the entire roster, claiming he was Mr. Indestructible. The man who single handedly tore apart GCW and killed it. Twice. XW barely got it’s new born feet off the ground before he came along and demolished that as well. He nearly placed my mentor and myself in the obituaries on countless occasions. This might be his final moments in GCW. His final appearance on the GCW roster. All I have to say to you Mr. Peter Maxwell is’Good Radiance . GCW can now rest easy as Peter Maxwell sits in the shadows of his own demise.” (A small smile crept up my face symbolizing how great things will be around GCW without Peter Maxwell holding it back. No smile could ever amount to the pleasure I held within this one. I may as well say I saved GCW. Maybe I am the Icon I always wanted to be. Maybe now I can say I amounted to something that would change the wrestling world forever.) “I see my true potential. It doesn’t lie beneath a shell of cockiness. It doesn’t quiver to the face of fear. And it sure as hell doesn’t give in to any amounts of excruciating pain. My potential rests within my soul, within my strive to succeed. My potential is and always will be’Me. Everything I’ve learned, everything I’ve experienced. No one can say, “He has me to thank for that” or “He learned from the best”. Frankly I am so appreciative towards Steven and I owe him more or less, my soul, but’He didn’t force me into my new ways. I adapted to them, I trained for them, I worked my damn ass off. And like I said, Cockiness isn’t what I’m aiming at. I am just so goddamn confidant and ready to take the next step. With or without a mentor, I am going to win the belt. And no one from here on out is going to stop me. They will jump in my way, they will attempt to slow me down, but nothing can stop me now. I will be Heavyweight Champion.” (The smile that once lit up my face melted to a look a pure confidence. I am unbreakable. Feeling as if nothing or no one could stop what I was about to accomplish. I walked away tonight feeling a new form of self-indulgence. This was a luxury for me to enjoy for the rest of my life. A victory, ah hell, an upset like this could only shape a man into something bigger, tougher, and stronger. And that’s exactly what this match produced. A new Michael Hardy?)
