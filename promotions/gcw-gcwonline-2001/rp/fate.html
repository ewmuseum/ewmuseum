Date: ??? …Previously… “He’s dead …” These two words, these are the words that I can’t bear to listen to. Why? Why? Why? I keep asking myself. These words put me to a complete halt, the rest of the world slowed down, every man, woman, and child is stopped in their tracks. I can hear a pin drop that it is so quiet. I don’t know what to do. I can’t speak, my mouth is moving, but my vocal chords do not want to commit. “W-what?” It was the only word I knew at the time, the only word I could think of that could sum up all my feelings into one big ball. “He’s dead Lucas, he died last night. Mom wants you back home, I want you back home, please come as soon as possible.” This was the first time that he cared about anyone but himself. Maybe he has really changed; maybe he is the brother I wanted to have. Whatever it took, I was going to be there for them, it was time to put these things behind us. * * * I was expecting a reunion when I came through the gates at the airport. That didn’t happen. Instead I was sent a car; it wasn’t good enough for them to meet me there. I know they are in grief, but so am I. No time to bust chops right now; I had to get to where I needed to go. The chauffeur pulls up and I enter the car. My head is spinning, it’s been a few months since I have been back here in the motherland, and it’s been a few years since I have actually seen my family face to face. What am I going to say to them? What are they going to say to me? “Where shall we go, sir?” He already knew the answer to that question, but he wanted to be polite as he could, he could tell I wasn’t myself, and he didn’t even know me. “Quinton Estate…” I sigh, those are the words I didn’t want to have to repeat ever again, but seeing as how these whole events involve me, I will have to get this over with. This is going to be one long ride. * * * He can’t be dead, no, he can’t…this is another sick twisted game perpetrated by my mental brother. No, no, no, no, this won’t happen at all. Where was he to get off telling me that our father is dead? He has no right, no god damn right. He can go take his perverse actions and go to hell. What am I going to do? * * * I awoke abruptly…we had arrived. I knew I had to come back, but I didn’t think it would have to be so soon. I gaze across the estate, nothing has changed all that much, on the outside at least. I want to believe it all has changed. I want to believe in something new, it was going to be hard, but I need to give it a try. I will never know otherwise. I open the car door and step outside. The lights breeze pierced my jacket, it flew in between my pores in my skin and the pockets throughout my clothes, through my burgundy tie, through my leather jacket and dress pants, I looked ridiculous but I had to throw on something quick. I wiped the cool chill from my face and began the uphill battle that I am sure I will not win. Breathe… Just Breathe… Looking out towards the yard I can see kids playing. They didn’t seem to know what was going on; they had their own little worlds in their own bubble that they had to worry about. They had only to worry about a knee scuff or a bruised elbow. I need to handle this. I straightened my tie and headed towards the front door. My reflexes tell me to open to door, but instead I knock. This wasn’t my house anymore, this wasn’t my place of solace, my safe zone, and I was a stranger here now. I was the outcast. * * * Father, what is your problem? Why did you do it? It’s all your fault, it was your fault that you left this family; you destroyed this family, why? Why would you go and die like that? Did you have a shred of dignity left in your body? Did you even care? Why would you put everyone through this? I hate you. I HATE you. I am glad you are dead; I have no more remorse for you anymore. * * * KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK I straighten my tie one last time and dust myself off. I couldn’t bear it anymore. I could hear the voices coming through the other side of door. Talking….gossiping about our family, the unvarying controversy we had. Why were they there? What correlation did they have to my father, to my family? Are they just here for the will reading? Do they think they are getting a dime of my father’s estate? It doesn’t matter… The door opens….by only a little bit. I can see the maid’s eye peering through the window. Her blue eyes….clear as the day. I smirk at her, she opens the door some more. “Can I help you?” She didn’t know who I was, perfect. Was there any pictures left of me in that house? “Quinton…” I pause for a minute. “Lucas Quinton…” A bright bulb went off in the top of her head. Her painful poignant face turned into a more animated smirk. She greeted me and apologized for her oversight and led me into the vestibule. I could tell from the outside that there was going to be an en masse of people inside this house, the unfamiliar voices, the foreign faces, and now I…I was a part of that fold. * * * God…god are you listening? Listen, I know you are busy, but…but you need to bring him back. I will be a better person; I will do anything you ask. Please… Listen to me… Give me another chance, send me back to the day I left and I will change things, I will change things and it will be better. I will be yours as I am now forever, please, I will do anything. Just help me… * * * The crowd’s whispers turned onto me. Nobody did indeed recognize me; from the conflicting looks I was given. A visitor from another planet I had become, as one might expect when arbitrarily entering a house you no longer had any ties to, it was maddening. The maid takes me to the center hall where most of the people congregated. “Presenting Master Lucas Quinton...” A sharp look emanated from each and every person in the room, their faces glowed like a fireworks display. I was suddenly surrounded by a mob of people greeting me and welcoming me back into the family, most of them I knew, from cousins to uncles to old friends, but one lit up like a tree on Christmas day. It was her, after all these years, I had re-connected with her. Everything else was just mute at this point; I had only heard her voice. Echoes… “Hello, Lucas.” She says as she puts on the most devilish grin I have ever witnessed. She takes me by the hand and we escape down the hallway, once again I had escaped from the fray once more. I look at her with intensity; she looks back at me with the same fire. It plagued us to this day, the way he hair flows evenly with the rest of her body, the way her lips smack the air in an agreeing motion. My body runs its course with the chills as I clear my throat. She leads me into one of the many rooms that made this place what it is, each room had a story to tell and it looks like history was going to be made once more. “Long time…” I snicker at her. Her faces changes from a bemused grin to an almost sad statuette. She could feel the pain as I once had. She could see through my sarcasm and wit, she knows me all too well. “You never visited me after you left; you broke your promise…” “I-I’m sorry-“ “You better will be sorry, Lucas, I have waited and waited, but you never came back. You never came back for me, like we planned.” “It’s complicated…” I couldn’t bear to see that look on her face; she was the only one I could care for in this world. My lips, her lips, would be reacquainted, sooner than later. Before our words became one, an interjector busts through the door. “Hey guys!” It was my brother, of course, he picked this of all moments to delight us with his presence, he grins, she grins, I grin, it was an old fashion grinning contest, a mark of wits. “I mean, hey Lucas, hello Lucey, delightful as ever. Listen, I need to speak with Lucas alone.” As soon as he spoke…she left, again I am away from her just another while longer. “What do you want?” “What do you mean what do I want?” “I mean, what the hell are you doing in here, I have nothing left tot talk to you about, now go away.” “Is that anyway to treat your own flesh and blood, Lucas?” “You aren’t my brother…you haven’t been for a long time” “Aw that isn’t the Lucas I used to know. You need to lighten up and forget the past, it is the past and it is dead and gone, you are looking at a new person, a new Leo, don’t forget it, either.” “You are mad…” I didn’t want to listen to him, but he was right, he looked and felt new, but there was something off putting. I couldn’t tell what it was. “You see, that is where you are wrong, I have quite proven myself lately to our mother…poor mother, I don’t know what she would have done with out me, she is a complete wreck. I don’t know how she could have gotten through this ordeal with out me, Lucas. It is simple, because I was there for my family and you WERE. NOT. EVER. I am starting to think I should have never called you since obviously you have not cared. Good bye, brother. Good riddance, you wanker.” I look at him with disbelief. I had set him off on one of his infamous rages whenever he didn’t get what he wanted. I stand up from the bed I had once sat down upon; where I was looking to reconnect with a lost one, but not being able to bear this pain anymore has taken a toll on me. I didn’t have the patience any more for the games, the lies, and the deceit. It was time to put a stop to all of this. I turn around towards the window and gaze into the wasteland I call England. It wasn’t as I once knew it, with time…comes change. “Leo, let me—“ <bWHACK! As soon as I try to get a word in edgewise my eyes begin to close, a sharp pain is entrusted at the back of my head. Blacked out once more… * * * It was a dark room, my eyes could only see shadows upon shadows, a wicked taste of solitude was salty in my mouth. I could only see the cracked edges of light illuminate from what only looks to be a door. A black mist goes back and forth between the light, trying to stomp it out, but it is unsuccessful. My head is still woozing from that blow I received earlier, I had no idea what time it was, but it seems that this was end my time…for now. It seems I have skipped the whole funeral, everyone who seemed to count on me was now lost. Lost Forever. I didn’t like what was going to happen next. The door slowly creeps open, then closes again. It was like a mind game that was going back and forth on the field, one person makes the move, and the other person hopes to counter it, if not, they could wind up injured…or worse. Dead. “YOU COWARD!” I scream to the top of my lungs, I got no response, only a slight chuckles from the other side of the door. This isn’t a game I like to place, it is disorienting and will only fuel my hate once more for the man who put me here. They will pay. The door opens up and a bright light shines through my eyes. I was blinded by darkness, and now by the light. I could hear the faint laughter get louder and louder. He was enjoying this. They will die. “Why? Why would you do this?” “Isn’t it obvious? After all this time that you have known me, brother? What did you think this was, a family reunion? No…no…this wasn’t it at all. This is my bloody stage, this is my platform to stand on, my soapbox, this is my time to be in the spot light. You say you believe in Karma? I believe in vengeance…survival…one for all and all for me. This is what I live for, and what I die for. “ “You are mad--” “No, my dear brother, I am a genius. While you were away for all those years past, it was all apart of my grand scheme, dad…or what’s left of him truly looked in me for guidance and I smashed him so. Him and mother, oh old poor mother, they didn’t realize how good their raised their perfect little son, Leonard, they didn’t know, nobody did, and now it is time to rise to my claim to fame. And with you out of the picture, I will be forever known as the sole Quinton who battled through the depression and the hate and the anger, but who finally comes out as a hero, a champion, a GOD. People will know it was you who betrayed our family, that it was you who killed father, hurt mother, and left me battered and broken. It’s all simple really, even you of all people could comprehend it. Why are you doing this, you ask? For the money of course, I forged Dad’s last will and testament making sure I get all his money…in your name of course….and leave every person nothing at all, because that is what they deserve…nothing. By the time execution of the will, I will have disappeared into the darkness with all that I deserve, and leave you to pick up all the pieces.” His ranting made me all that much angrier. “A hero? You are nothing but a bloody VILLIAN. For all the grief you have put us all through, it is time to end what is left of you. You will NEVER get away with this.” My mind and my words are enraging, hopefully they are intimidating enough for him. He laughs some more, he isn’t the person I once knew…All of this is reminiscent of a bad action movie where the villain ties up the hero and things are looking down for the hero until something finally happens to trigger the bad guy to mess it all up. That wasn’t going to happen. “Brother, the only thing left is you, you and your sick little fantasies about how you are the biggest and most worthy person around here, well I will show you that you are nothing but a sick dog. Good bye brother…” That is when I fell back into the dark slumber. It was going to be a long ride into hell. * * * Fate can be a funny thing. As one person dies, another person continues on living until they themselves perish, we all had our one moment in which we die, but it was up to us to delay the inevitable. I need to escape back into the darkness… Escape.
