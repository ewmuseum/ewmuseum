Date: 12/26/2009 Previously on Operation: FOX... Clyde assembled his rag tag group of misfits for an adventure of time traveling proportions in order to save his future self from being murdered and the entire world in the process. His team includes his good friend and fellow wrestling mentor Crayden Ragner. Then there’s the nerdy-hot girlfriend, Suzy Yates along with her talking pet owl, Clowl. Longtime friend Onjin Wiznaaski supplies the brains of the operation. Then there’s Kyle Bold. A rich reporter from Canada trying to land the big story. And last but not least, you have Cyrus The Isuzu Giga, aka the time traveling vehicle. They’ve gotten the operation together and are in possession of the all-important ‘TO DO LIST’ in order to save the world. Last time Clyde, Crayden and Cyrus attempted to time travel for the first time in human history. En route to Clyde’s initial GCW contract signing, they met up with Lord Transcepticore, the keeper of the time vortex. LT welcomed our heroes to the dimension between dimensions. Little did they know, but they were about to embark on the biggest mission since the staging of the Lunar Landing... wait, I mean, it did happen. -------Gigaboard 2010------- | Real Date: 12/26/2009 | Current Date: 9/30/2007 | Occupants: Clyde E. Fox, Kyle X. Bold | Enjoy your stay! ☺ GU-JJJJJJJUUUUUUUUUUUUUU! POOT! POOT! POOT! Smoke, steam and satisfaction. They all evaporate skyward as Cyrus finds himself deeply entrenched in a homemade crater. The whole field they collided with is nice and soft. Good thing September 30, 2007 is a fog covered, moisture filled day. It also doesn’t hurt that the business building of interest is pretty much out in the middle of nowhere. “Oh gee, oh gee,” The mechanical voice of Cyrus worries, “We’ve crash landed.” Nuts, bolts, and some wrinkled pieces of metal siding engulf the crash site. Clyde awakes from his half induced gaze and notices a tiny cut above his eyebrow. “Holy liver rats! I have a boo-boo!” Clyde cries as he licks his fingers and wipes away the smallest bit of blood. While Clyde only pays attention to himself, he neglects to look over at his human companion, Kyle Bold. He’s bloodied worse than any victim in a Uwe Boll film. Finally, Cyrus pulls himself together and notifies Clyde. “Clyde,” Cyrus starts, “I’m damaged pretty badly. You seem to be okay. Are you able to wake Kyle up?” Clyde embellishes the pain he’s in to turn and stare shockingly as Kyle, covered in red liquid. “Dear Smurfs! He’s dead!” Clyde frets. “Noooo, no,” Cyrus groans,” He’s just covered with some hot sauce that I keep in the glasses compartment. Don’t ask me why I have a bottle of hot sauce there.” Clyde flings some of the hot sauce off of Kyle’s face. He begins to stir as Clyde notices the all-important TO DO LIST sticking out of Bold’s pocket. Clyde snatches the crinkled page and wipes off some of the hot sauce splatter as he refreshes himself on their primary mission. “Mission One,” Clyde states, “The objective of this mission is to alter the original GCW contract signing of Clyde Fox. Averting this event could prevent everything in the future from happening.” Clyde folds the page down and checks The Gigaboard. “Gee whiz. Two months have lapsed in real time since we left the hangar. But it only felt like it took two minutes to get here,” Clyde mumbles. “Indeed,” Cyrus echoes, “We do not know how everything will play out. But if we are too slow in executing our missions, then there might not be a savable world to return to. And if my calculations are correct, and if we succeed in preventing your contract signing, we should simply disappear as if we never did this.” Fox rubs his chiny chin chin. “Hmmmm. So we could end this whole thing right here eh?” Fox nods. “Yeeeeah. Or we could be doomed to an endless time chase,” Cyrus downplays. A hand suddenly grabs Clyde’s shoulder. Fox’s head turns on a dime and sees Kyle pulling himself up. “I’m okay. Just a little shakin’ up. Where are we?” Bold asks. “In the land of crescent demons, just outside the seventh layer of hell,” Cyrus jokes, “No but really, the date is September 30th, 2007. It’s 1:50 pm and we are just outside The GCW Canadian Offices. My calculations tell me that Clyde will be showing up here with his mother shortly after 2 pm.” Kyle rubs his head trying to get into the game. “And what? We run up and stop him?” Bold asks. “No silly,” Clyde interjects, “Don’t forget. You have me here. I know who I am and he knows what I’d do. We gotta play it cool. Like a stealthy... you know... those planes...” “Stealth Bomber?” Cyrus posed. “No... they are stealthy and they fly. They are black and in the sky ell oh ell,” Clyde tries to figure it out. “Dude, that’s a stealth bomber,” Kyle promises. Clyde folds his arms in frustration. “No TITS isn’t!” Clyde refuses. Regardless of what he is thinking of, the team redirects their attention to come up with a plan of action. Or as they call it in those snazzy TV shows; An action plan! --5 Minutes Later-- Kyle and Clyde slip out of Cyrus. They examine him for major damages but don’t have the same brainpower as the always-analytical Onjin. “Ummm what are we looking for again?” Clyde asks. Kyle neglects to responds as he runs his hand up and down the crinkled metal. “Yoooou guys, seriously. I’ll be all right. Just leave me here. I’ll pull myself out,” Cyrus notes. The pair honors Cyrus’ request and head through the soiled field. They shift through the fog and post up behind a car in the parking lot. Clyde eagerly looks over the hood and sees an all too familiar car pull into a spot. “Hokey smoke! It’s my Mommy and I! I’m getting nervous Kyle! How do I look?” Fox worries. Kyle peers over the trunk of the car and catches a glimpse of past Clyde and his Mommy prepping for his big interview. “You look... like exactly the same,” Kyle recognizes. Past Clyde leaves his Mommy and enters the building. Just as he vanishes, Clyde and Kyle make their move. They gun it towards the door until they hear a shrieking voice... “CLYDE!?” His Mother yells, “Is that you?” The careless pair turns and see Mommy Fox bolting towards them. “Oh shit! Shit! She sees us Clyde!” Kyle notifies. But before they can do anything, Mommy Fox is on top of them. “I just saw you walk into the building. What are you doing out here?” She asks. “Ummm yeah... no... nothing. Ha. I just forgot to hug you that’s all,” Clyde covers up as he snatches his Mommy and brings her in nice and close. Kyle progressively backs away as Clyde distracts her. Bold finally gets up to the building and swings the door open. With a quick peck on the cheek, Clyde runs off and enters the building. “Wuv you Mommy! I’ll be back in a bitty bit!” Fox waves. They leave the very confused Mommy Fox in the parking lot as they nearly sprint through the secured lobby. The dynamic duo runs straight into a wall of glass, clearly not noticing the glare from the lights. SMACK! THUD! ... CLICK, CLACK, CLICK, CLACK. The sound of work boots scuffing against the tile floor starts to get louder... and louder... AND LOUDER! From his back, Clyde looks up and sees some men dressed in black tactical suits staring down at him. “Copy that. We have them. But how did you know they would be here?” One spoke into his radio. The reply was incomprehensible to Clyde. He’s clearly still dazed from running into that glass wall. “Copy that. We’ll take them to their spot,” He said as he lodges his radio back into his belt, “Hey you two. Welcome to past! Hope your visit stays with you for a very long time! Haha!” The guards laugh at the pun as they cover our heroes heads in a black stitched sack. ... Shuffle, shuffle, shuffle. CLICK! Access granted. Security personnel entrance. “Wake up! WAKE THE F*CK UP!” A grouchy man directs. Where... where am I? Clyde’s eyelids flutter to open, but when they do, he’s shocked at his surroundings. He and Kyle are laced down to what can assumed to be a torture table. White florescent lighting causes even more radiance to bounce off the already white painted room. “What’s... going on? Where am I?” Clyde drowsily murmurs. “Welcome,” A sexily soothing voice invites. All three of Clyde’s eyes widen as a lusty lady decked out in red leather slithers her way into view. “I believe we have met before Mr. Fox. But this time, the pleasure is all mine,” She sparks. That all too familiar voice was just too hard to pinpoint. In the meantime, Kyle begins to stir. “Huh? What the hell happened?” Bold interjects, “Candace? What are you doing here?” Candace Fulmont. Spy amongst spies. The spoiler of life. You know those myths you hear about life sucking carbon based femme fatales? Well this woman defines every single myth ever told. “Hmmm hi there Mr. Bold. It seems that there’s a bargain on time travelers today,” Fulmont jokes. “How in the blue heck did you know?” Clyde asks. “I have my ways. You could say that I’ve been employed by a higher power to stop this charade you call saving Earth,” Fulmont deflects, “... By any means necessary. And now may I introduce you to my squad... the team that will end such a bid of futility.” The tactical soldiers step up. Each man has a different color accenting his otherwise black garb. “Wow... I always wanted to create a rip off of The Power Rangers. Congratulations, you beat me too it,” Clyde chuckles. SLAP! Candace whacks Clyde across the face with the back of her hand. She smugly smiles much to the chagrin of our heroes. “Enough out of you,” Candace dictates, “Individually we might look good in our pretty little colors but together we form Visual Defiance. An endless amount of colors... an endless amount of soldiers. Ain’t that right boys?” “YES MA’AM!” The many members of Visual Defiance shout. “We kind of took over things here,” Fulmont informs, “If it wasn’t for you showing up, then we would have taken out your past self... but it seems you’re just like most men... you’re too fast for your own good.” Some of soldiers chuckle but are quickly put in their place with a whip of Candace’s hair. “So instead we’ll just have to let your past self sign the contract and give you your rightful end,” Fulmont concludes. Kyle starts sweating bullets, as he has no clue what to do. Clyde, on the other hand, stares confidently at his new sworn foe. “What do we do Clyde?” Kyle whispers. “Do not fret my main man,” Clyde assured, “Remember what Crayden said...” Now remember boys, if you get into any kind of trouble, remember to use the special code. It should get you out of nearly any situation “Oh yeah,” Kyle remembers, “But I have no idea what that is!” Clyde simply smiles. Candace daintily walks up to The Prince of The Playroom and seductively smiles back. “What’s so funny Clydie? Finally coming to the realization that you’re doomed?” She teases. “Heh... nopers dopers,” Clyde sniffles, “I have my secret weapon to get me out of a situation just like this. No one can resist it.” The red leather cracks as Fulmont leans back, interested in where Clyde’s going with this. “Do share,” She encourages. “Oh I will... I will,” Clyde hints, “I... Errr hmmm...” Clyde clears his throat a few times before repeating the word ‘I’ over and over again. “Otay... I’m weady,” Clyde says... “I can feeeeeel it coming in the air tonight..” Silence. Kyle looks over at Clyde with the ‘WTF are you doing?’ look on his face. “I can feeeeeeeeeeeeel it coming in the air tonight...” Suddenly... ERRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR! A random imitated guitar riff roars out from a soldier’s mouth. Candace turns to see her Grey Guard playing an air guitar. Just then, The Yellow Guard joins in, and then The Blue Guard! It was a full out rainbow of instruments! “I can feeeeeeeel it coming in the air tonight..... OH LORD! And I’ve been waiting for this moment.... ALL MY LIFE! OH LORD! OH LORD!” Clyde starts screaming. Not knowing what the hell is going on, Candace starts spinning around trying to stop her troops from jamming. Kyle is still left looking at Clyde for direction. As the rest of them are distracted, Clyde manages to inform his friend. “Just follow along! I’ll explain later,” Clyde whispers as Kyle nods. “I CAN FEEL IT COMING IN THE AIR TONIGHT! OH LORD!” Fulmont turns back to the tied up singing duo. Enraged with what they are doing, she launches herself at Clyde. “WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING!? STOP! STOP THIS NOW!” She screams. But it was too late... DUN-DUN DUN-DUN DUN-DUN DUN-DUN DUN-DUN DUNNNNN! And POP! Just like that Clyde and Kyle break out of their straps and land safely on their feet. Somehow, some way, Clyde had triggered the song that no one could resist singing. With no time to react, Candace tries to snap her troops out of the Phil Collins daze but is unsuccessful. With around 50 soldiers jamming to Phil Collins, Candace’s shrieking voice is tuned down to inaudible banter. Moving quickly, Clyde grabs Kyle by the arm and pulls him out of the cold white room. The door locks behind them as they enter an empty room. Just then an automated voice comes over the buildings speaker system. “WARNING. WARNING. EVACUATE THE BUILDING IMMEDIATELY!” “How did you do that Clyde?” Kyle inquires but Clyde’s too busy trying to hear himself think. “I’ll tell you once we get out of here. Right now we gotsta find my contract and rip it up. I think I would have signed it by now,” Fox says. The two stay together as they start opening random doors but they continually come up empty. It’s not until they come across a room labeled ‘CONTRACTS’ do they have any success. They bolt through the room as fast as they can until they find the box with the letter F on it. “Your contract should be in here,” Kyle notices as he opens the box and begins to rifle through it. Clyde gets a little preoccupied as he sees a picture of Steven Caldera pinned up on the bulletin board. Oh well isn’t that a nice picture of da boss. He looks two years younger, that’s for sure. You know... he would probably really appreciate it if I signed this picture. You know, as a keepsake. And if I sign it now, then when I go back to the present, it will have been two years! That photo could cost a fortune by that time! Clyde quickly grabs a pen and scribbles his name on the back of the photo. He then looks over and sees Kyle turning off a nearby paper shredder. “Okay man, all done,” Kyle says as he approaches Clyde. “Otay, let’s blow this stand of pop selling sugarness,” Clyde states. The two head out just after Clyde signs the photo. Once out in the hallway they run into a very pissed off Candace Fulmont. “You little pricks! You won’t get away!” She promises as she sprints towards them. The duo is quick to react as they jump a few ledges and hurdle towards the exit. Upon their grand escape Clyde has a few parting words for the deceptively beautiful brunette. “Have fun with your Power Rangers Candy! Maybe I’ll see you again sometime! MUAH!” Clyde blows a kiss. The boys leave and start their trek towards Cyrus but little did they know, Candace had other ideas. As the alarms blare, the automated voice message repeats, employees and Visual Defiance members flee alike, Candace enters the contract holding room. And what does she find? Just Clyde’s signature on the back of a certain photo. “Hmmmm,” She chuckles at Clyde stupidity, “Not today Clyde... not today.” Meanwhile Clyde and Kyle reach Cyrus, who has done what he promised to do. He dug himself out of the ground and was looking in not half bad decent shape. “Cyrus! You look useable!” Clyde points out. “Yeeeeeeeah. I know. I reversed the molecular cycle of moloton chromulites to create a healing parody throughout my body. But in layman’s terms; I healed myself,” Cyrus notes. “A truck that can time travel AND heal himself? Now that’s badass dude. The press is going to love this,” Kyle laughs. And with that, they pile in and evaporate into the thick air. BOOM! BOOM! ... POOT! -------Gigaboard 2010------- | Real Date: 12/26/2009 | Current Date: 12/26/2009 | Occupants: Clyde E. Fox, Kyle X. Bold | Welcome home! ☺ Lightning and steam accompany the gang as they land successfully back in the groups super secret hangar dock thingy. “HEY! Hey guys! They’re back!” Crayden shouts as he runs over to open the car door. The exterior of Cyrus is near freezing temperature but Crayden still manages to fling the door open. Out pops a coughing Clyde Fox and a shivering Kyle Bold. “Hey guys! It’s good to see ya. Merry freaking Christmas! We were worried where you guys went,” Ragner informs. Kyle pats Crayden on the back on a job well done. “Well we had our fair share of troubles let me tell ya. We darn near didn’t make it out of the time vortex going there... then Cyrus crash landed... then we ran into that bitch Candace Fulmont... and then we managed to shred Clyde’s contract and get out of there in time,” Bold recaps. Ragner’s eye widen at the name drop of one Candace Fulmont. “You mean that bitch daughter of that famous doctor?” Ragner inquires. Bold nods his head yesh. Clyde finally catches his breath and joins the conversation. “I had a run in with that two cent slut a long time ago. She wanted my hawt bawdy but I declined. I’m too pure for that arachnid to get her claws into me,” Clyde sparks. “Yeah and the only reason I know her was because she questioned me on why I was leaving my country. I fed her some lie that there was a story I was chasing about a rare breed of ostrich when really, I was coming here. She must have found out and wants to stop us or something,” Kyle explains. Ragner slinks down in his chair and turns to his control panel. “No shit... so how did you get away from HER?” Crayden asks. “We used the secret weapon,” Both Clyde and Kyle respond. “Oh you sang Phil Collins. Nice one boys, I like it,” Ragner grins, “But ummmm. I have bad news. According to my futurometer here... you guys didn’t change shit. You sure you shredded the contract?” They both nod like kids with something to hide. “Well why don’t you check to To Do List? It might have updated,” Ragner reminds. Clyde pulls out the crinkled sheet of paper. It reads: ‘Return to your father’s wrestling accident.’ “What?” Kyle reacts. “I get it,” Clyde’s eyes tear up, “We can’t go back to my contract signing because that hoe hoe hoe Candace and her army will be waiting for us right? Well instead we have to go back to save my Daddy from his brain accident.” Kyle looks at Crayden. Crayden looks at Kyle. “And if we can do that... I’ll be a totally different person than who I am right now... big time,” Clyde adds. Kyle and Crayden look back at Fox. “Awwww shit,” Ragner comments as he smacks the desk dramatically.
