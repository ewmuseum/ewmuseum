Date: 8/8/08 START 3:00 pm It’s another glorious Friday across the globe as many bustling business workers anxiously wait for quittin? time. Bar owners are busy making pre-night preparations, and your local unemployed money sucker is throwing away the last of his months welfare check on booze. Yep. Smell it. Take it all in. It’s the weekend. And what better way to kick it off than to have a nap? Clyde Fox thinks you can’t top that. He’s already eaten, been burped, and even went potty. Now comes the creme de la creme; the nap itself. Clyde crawls in between the race car patterned sheets of his twin mattress bed. Ahhhhhhhhhhhh. This is super duper comfy womfy! BIG TIME! The silence laces the moment like a fine mixed drink. Everything is just so calm and peaceful. The ultimate setting for an uber comfy sleep. Fox nestles the back of his head into the pure white pillow, looks at the ceiling for a brief moment and then closes his eyes and drifts off to nap world. BAM! Fox suddenly falls from a purple sky and lands in a garden patch with a thud! Thimbleberry Plant: LOOK OUT YOU CRAZY LOLLIPOP! Jumping Gobstoppers! Ye almost sat on me! The Irish accent of one of the Thimbleberry Plants rings loudly as Fox gets up without a scratch. Fox looks around in amazement as all the prettiest colors of the rainbow sparkle. Fox: Hokey smoke! Where am I? The Thimbleberry Plant turns (if such a plant can even do that) and begins to welcome Fox. Thimbleberry Plant: Ho ho ho. Ye just jumped into the magical land of lollipops and gumdrops. If the Thimbleberry could rub its stomach in a jolly manner? it would right about now. Thimbleberry Plant: The land where the impossible is possible. And the possible is impossible. My friend? you’ve stumbled across Pansy Palace. Fox’s jaw nearly smacks the ground as he is left standing in awe. Even the slight wind that blows by smells like the scent of mint. Clyde looks at the luscious purple sky. It’s accompanied by a few light blue clouds that almost look like cotton candy. Could this be real? This is like totally like paradise! Awesome! It just has to do with The Pansy Castle? Fox: The Pansy Castle? What is it? Thimbleberry Plant: I wouldn’t know. I’m just ye simply Thimbleberry Plant. I’ve heard stories though. Wondrous parades? carnivals? prizes? acrobatic shows? anything imaginable there. I’d go me self but I don’t have the legs ye possess. That’s why I would ask for you to go for me and bring me back some proof that it exists. And? you can find out why you are here too! Fox stands tall and places his hands on his hips as if he was Superman. He then puffs his chest out and looks down at the small plant. Fox: Have no fear! I shall accept your quest of destiny and find The Pansy Castle. And when I do; I’ll find out everything I can? otay? The Thimbleberry Plant bows graciously in respect as Steve, the reject Thimbleberry slowly rises up from the ground. Thimbleberry Plant: Oh thank ye young hero. Thank ye. Steve: Errrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrgh! The wretched voice of a broken down old man rings much louder than the Irish accent of the nice Thimbleberry Plant. Steve: Wow kid, lose some weight! You nearly popped my Thimbleberry. Thimbleberry Plant: Now, now Steve. You know better? no evil thoughts shall enter the pureness of Pansy Palace. Besides ye boy here is going on a quest of mine to discover The Pansy Castle! Steve stands straight up in attention at the sound of the word "Castle." Steve: You’re shittin? me!” This boy is going to find proof that the Pansy Castle is more than a myth? Fox nods his head emphatically. Steve: Alright? but you better bring me back a nice lady Thimbleberry. I am tired of playing root-sies with Chuck over there. Fox: Yes sir. Considerate it done. Chuck turns to Fox to give one last instruction. Chuck: Hero. Head down the fuchsia colored road. Rumor has it will lead you right to The Pansy Castle. Oh and one last thing? beware of? Steve covers his Thimbleberry as Chuck leans in and whispers the word of the unmentionables. Chuck: "The Floaters." Suddenly many animals including cotton tailed bunnies pop up out of nowhere and collectively "Shhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh!" Chuck at one time. Chuck then returns to his normal position. Chuck: Right. Off with ye then. March forward ye hero. And with that? Fox turns on the spot and heads toward the fuchsia colored road off in the near distance. The extra bright green grass rustles under Fox as he drags his feet across the ground. After descending from a hilltop, Fox arrives at the fuchsia brick road. And just as he steps on the colorful road of glory; a clown comes out from behind a bush. The clown is riding a unicycle and juggling all at the same time. He slowly makes his way over to Clyde before stopping. Clown: Hellllllllllloooooooo. The clown greets Fox with a very high pitched, stereotypical gay mans voice. Clown: I’m Rufus. Rufus, the clown. What’s your name scrumptious? Fox stands there and stares back at Rufus. The clown is wearing a black and white checkered tunic with all the usual garbs that a joker would wear. His exceedingly bright pink pantyhose catch Fox’s eye. Dumbfounded, Fox actually has trouble with his response. Fox: Ummmmm? heto I guess. I’m Clyde. Suddenly Rufus? voice changes into a deep and crazed killer’s voice. Rufus: Realllllllllly? You won’t make it there alive! Do you know why? Because I have some BP’s for you. As he walks through the valley of the vast land known as Pansy Palace, Fox can’t help but notice the lovely yellow bushes? the blue frogs? and even the dazzling white tree tops. This truly is an amazing place. Everything is so different here. Or maybe? just maybe? our world is really the one that is different. I mean out trees are green. Our roads are road colored. This road doesn’t even need sidewalk chalk. The only thing that’s slightly the same is the grass. I guess you can’t have all of it different. And just like that? SMACK! All one billion stories of it and all. The gray bricked building looks like a castle? smells like a castle? but as Fox just decides to randomly lick it? Fox: It tastes like sugar cubes. The dazzling tile floor bounces the light off of it as Fox shields his eyes. The dwarfs close the doors once Clyde enters. The light slowly dims as a man in a red robe and white beard floats down from the sky. Fox: Are you God? The kind looking man touches down atop the staircase and fluffs his beard during his response. ?: Oh no my little buttercup. I am King Pansy. The most pan-siest Pansy in the land. First off; welcome to Pansy Castle! The most magical place outside of the G spot! Our castle is unlike any other castle. We are not constrained to one location.
