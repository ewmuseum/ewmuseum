Pitchfest

14 March 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/14/12 INT. SCIENCE LAB – NIGHT Inside a state-of-the-art laboratory underground somewhere, DR. BLOTCH JOHNSON, a genetic scientist played with much aplomb by Brendan Fraser, works diligently at his lab station. He completes various scientific tasks, including pouring liquids into beakers, filling small vials with liquids, and using that vial-spinning device that genetic scientists use. NARRATOR In a time when mankind is looking for the next great breakthrough... Dr. Johnson finally finishes his compound, and sucks it inside a syringe. He taps the side of the cylinder, his eye gazing at the blue-tinged compound. INT. HOLDING CELLS - NIGHT Dr. Johnson passes by the other cages, all filled with anxious chimps that somehow have found the restraint not to throw their poo at their captor. NARRATOR One man will risk it all for greatness... The good doctor makes his way to the end of the corridor, where ANDY MURRAY JR. rests, casually thumbing through a Hustler magazine. Dr. Johnson opens up the cage door and sticks the needle in the unsuspecting arm of Murr Jr. He freaks out at first, but Dr. Johnson shushes him through it. DR. JOHNSON It’ll be okay. Together, we’re gonna be rich and famous. Murr Jr.’s eyes narrow, and Dr. Johnson just nods and grins like an idiot. INT. SCIENCE LAB - NIGHT NARRATOR But at what cost? Dr. Johnson continues his experiments, but when he peers into the holding cells, he notices Murr Jr. addressing everyone, very presidential-like. He pays it no mind at first. INT. SCIENCE LAB - NIGHT The alarm goes off, as Dr. Johnson wearily walks into the lab. Scientists run about like chickens with their heads cut off. Dr. Johnson puts on his glasses. DR. JOHNSON What’s the matter, Dr. Stone? DR. STONE It’s the monkeys! They’re...loose! Dr. Johnson, upon seeing the empty monkey cages, takes off his glasses. DR. JOHNSON Dear God! EXT. STREETS OF MAJOR METROPOLIS - DAY People run through the streets, with Murr Jr. his army close on their tail. After clearing out a neighborhood, Murr Jr. jumps upon a car hood and holds up his spear. ANDY MURRAY JR. For...freeeeeeeedom!!!!! The rest of his army roars, holding up their spears, swords, guns, and various weapons. They roar once more before heading towards the downtown of the city. ****** In an office space a few stories up that gazes upon where Santa Monica and Vine meet, Triumph Frost and Andy Murray Jr. sit behind a strong mahogany desk. On the other side is a disheveled-looking man, an obvious aspiring Hollywood hopeful. Triumph leans back in his leather chair, his hands clasped, his index fingers pressed against his lips. "How is this not like ‘Planet of the Apes’ again?" The disheveled screenwriter starts to come unraveled. "I told you already, it has nothing to do with that bunch of crap!" Triumph and Andy exchange quick, awkward glances. "Right." Triumph leans forward. "Tell me again why?" The disheveled screenwriter lets out an exasperated sigh. "Because." He takes in a deep breath. "This monkey…is female." Triumph’s eyebrow piques. "But Andy’s clearly a male." The disheveled screenwriter shoots out an awkward guffaw, one that sends Triumph and Murr Jr. jumping backward. "That’s the beauty of it all!" A long, protracted silence. Triumph coughs. "I think we’ll pass." The disheveled screenwriter, clearly appalled, gathers up his scattered documents and shoves them into his carry-all pack. He lets out one last huff. "You’re missing out on a unique opportunity." As the man leaves, Triumph offers his retort. "Right, unique. Like the ‘Planet of the Apes’ DVD I have in my Netflix queue, you fuckin’ twat." The door slams shut, and Triumph glances over at his chum. "Call in the next one?" Murr Jr. nods in agreement. ****** INT. FEMALE BOARDING SCHOOL, MAIN CLASSROOM – DAY In a class stocked with way-too-beautiful teenage girls, none of which have a single blemish about them, ANDY MURRAY JR. stalks about the room, ruler in hand and head-to-toe in women’s clothing. NARRATOR What happens when a gang of misfits on the run from the mob end up in a boarding school? One girl snickers, and Murr Jr. slams the ruler across her desk. He sneers. INT. FEMALE BOARDING SCHOOL, DRAMA CLASS - DAY NARRATOR Hilarity ensues at "Miss Murr Jr.’s Boutique Shop and Sasstalk Factory!" In the drama room, MISS FRANKENSLACKS hams it up, played poorly by a slurring Michael Madsen. MISS FRANKENSLACKS You goddamned women listen up or whatever. Actin’. Miss Frankenslacks drunkenly sways about, stumbling before using a chair to catch his/herself. MISS FRANKENSLACKS Err...who’s gotta bottle’o scotch for Miss Fran.. uhh…Frank...ergh...whatever. INT. FEMALE BOARDING SCHOOL, DANCE CLASS - DAY NARRATOR The fun never ends at Miss Murr Jr.’s! The dance teacher MISS PERPWALK crosses the hardwood floor, played angrily and un-thespian-like by Chris Brown, and all the girls huddle in a corner, fearful for their lives. MISS PERPWALK *mumble mumble* can’t act, beat women *mumble mumble* Miss Perpwalk then launches into a dance sequence maybe four other people in the world can emulate. The girls cry nonetheless. INT. FEMALE BOARDING SCHOOL, HISTORY CLASS - DAY NARRATOR What will they think of next? Into the classroom storms MISS HULLABALOO, played without an ounce of shame nor dignity by Eddie Murphy. He nearly screams his lines at the top of his lungs. MISS HULLABALOO History? I’ll tell y’all about history! If I keep eatin’ all them doughnuts, my waistline gonna be history! AHH-HAH-HAAAAAH!!!! His laughter would put 80’s Eddie Murphy to shame, as the girls all look on with both confusion and pity. His teacher’s aide chimes in, MISS PLUMB, played by Kevin Hart, because the budget’s running tight as it is. MISS PLUMB Oh no you di-in’t! The two crossdressers laugh, and the world dies a little bit inside. INT. FEMALE BOARDING SCHOOL, HALLWAY - DAY All five walk down the hallway with purpose, or they stumble, or they jump about like ADD kids, or they dance like idiots, depending on the actor you wish to envision. NARRATOR "Miss Murr Jr.’s Boutique Shop and Sasstalk Factory!" What will they think of next?!? All five pass by the camera, and Kevin Hart even mugs for it, because of course he would. ****** Triumph grits his teeth. His body trembles. He pulls out a drawer of the desk and reaches in, pulling out a Colt revolver. He points it at the preppy screenwriter, who just about craps himself. "Get. Out." The smarmy preppy screenwriter unwisely tries to speak. "But I--" Triumph cocks back the hammer, his teeth grinding upon one another. "Now." The preppy bolts the room like his crappy little Ford Escort is being towed, which it probably is. Goddamned parking tickets. Triumph places the revolver back in the desk and closes the drawer. He casually presses the intercom button. "Next one." ****** INT. MURR JR’S SUPER SEXY CASTLE, BABY - NIGHT Oh, yeah. In Castle Murr, overlookin’ a sea of fine-ass ho’s, SUPER-DUPER BIG FAT BACK DADDY MURR JR. just chills in his throne, ya dig? Oh yeah, there’s a buncha ho’s there, too, baby. I didn’t forget ‘bout y’all. SUPER-DUPER BIG FAT BACK DADDY MURR JR. Which one’a you bitches gonna fetch the King his juice? Hands shoot up in the air, baby, ‘cause that’s how we roll, dig? And they them sweet lovin’ ho’s, ya feel me? Them ho’s like Amanda Bynes, Rachel Bilson, and Hayden Pana..Panna...uhh, that bitch from "Heroes." Real ho’s, ya hear? HO #1 I will, daddy. HO #2 Let me get it, baby. HO #3 I got this shit, daddy. SUPER-DUPER BIG FAT BACK DADDY MURR JR. Oh yeah, baby. And the ho’s run off and fetch that royal juice, dig? And then, one of ‘em big-bootied hoes show up. Fresh off’a one’a my main man Snoop’s video shoots, dig? And they go right up to my main man, right? BIG BOOTIED HOES Oh, daddy! And they start servicing him, ya dig? And there’s scattered ass everywhere. And I’m all like ****** "Goddamnit," Triumph howls as his fist meets the wood. "I said no porn!" The sleazy-ass producer, with his greasy slicked-back hair, purple leisure suit with leopard fringe, pimp hat with an eagle feather in it, and pimp cane in hand, flashes his used car-salesman grin. "Hey, baby," the sleazy-ass producer hisses. "It’s all good." Triumph points directly at the door. "Get the fuck out of here, pimp!" The sleazy-ass producer gracefully gets up and leans over to shake Murr Jr.’s grateful hand. "We tried, baby," the sleazy-ass producer says and, with a skank on both arm, pimp-struts out. When the door slams shut, Triumph looks at his bud. He opens to mouth to say anything, but only a "whuh" emerges. He takes in a long pause. "What the hell could have possibly compelled you to…" Another pause. A thought. "You know what, forget it." He presses down upon the intercom button. "Send in the next one." ****** EXT. SOCCER FIELD - DAY ANDY MURRAY JR. is as focused as ever. He dribbles the soccer ball down the field, passing imaginary defenders, and squaring up for a perfect shot that lands past the goalkeeper’s reach. The invisible crowd cheers in unison, as the diminutive chap nods with approval. NARRATOR We all have our dreams. INT. HOSPITAL ROOM - DAY Murr Jr. sits at PA MURRAY’S side, played by a now-quite-wealthy Sean Connery. Murr Jr. grabs his pa’s hand. Pa coughs. PA MURRAY Laddie? Laddie, are yeh there? Murr Jr. eeks, and squeezes the hand. PA MURRAY Are yeh...*cough*...a soccer star yet, laddie? Murr Jr. eeks out a "no." PA MURRAY Ahh, laddie. You’ll get there soon. Pa Murray shoots up from his bed. PA MURRAY PUNCH THE KEYS, FOR GOD’S SAKE!!! Murr Jr. calms him down, running his furry paw against his pa’s forehead. PA MURRAY Och. Good boy. Murr Jr.’s eyes turn to slivers. NARRATOR But how far will we go to achieve them? EXT. OLD TRAFFORD PITCH - DAY Murr Jr. has his bag slung over his shoulder, filled with his gear. He takes a step upon the famed field of Manchester United, ready to suit up for his first practice with the big club. Players like MICHAEL OWEN and RYAN GIGGS sneer at him, because it was easier to pay for them than it was for the likes of goddamned Rooney. Murr Jr. pays them no mind, though. He knows he’s ready. INT/EXT. VARIOUS - DAY/NIGHT That’s when we get... MONTAGE OF SOCCER STUFF A) Murr Jr. dribbles past invisible defenders, scoring multiple times. B) The team wins another match, with Murr Jr. toiling on the bench. C) Murr Jr. breaks an imaginary defender’s ankle and finishes with a killer cross. D) The team goes on a five-win streak without him, as he stews and watches. E) Murr Jr. gets into a fight with a number of street toughs. He murders one of them and goes on the run. F) The team gets into the Champion’s League tournament. G) Murr Jr. gets off scot-free via a technicality. INT. HOSPITAL ROOM - NIGHT As Pa Murray hangs on for dear life, his son is at his bedside. PA MURRAY Oi. I watched your games. You didn’t play in a single one of them. Murr Jr. nods disappointedly. Pa Murray strokes his son’s head. PA MURRAY Don’t you worry, son. They’ll let you play in the championship. That’s my dying wish. Pa Murray then gurgles on his own bile and dies. Murr Jr. is now more focused than ever. EXT. FAMOUS SOCCER FIELD IN EUROPE, WHATEVER - NIGHT As the game comes down to penalty kicks, Murr Jr. grabs his coach by the throat and eeks that he’s ready to go. NARRATOR How far would you go? The coach, scared for his life, lets him play. EXT. FAMOUS SOCCER FIELD IN EUROPE, WHATEVER - LATER Murr Jr. stares down the goalie as flashbulbs go off. NARRATOR Could you pull off a... Murr Jr. gets a running start, his foot hitting the meat of the ball. NARRATOR Murracle? The flashbulbs blind the camera... ****** The Miami Heat LeBron James jersey-wearing superfan waits in anticipation. "So...?" Triumph and Murr Jr. look at one another. Triumph hems and haws. "We’ll pass. But you’re name is on file." Dejected, he gathers up his charts and his storyboards and leaves. Triumph and Murr Jr. sit at the desk, now finding themselves completely bored and anxious. Triumph turns to Murr Jr. "So, uhh…" A short pause. "Another ‘Hail to the Chimp,’ then?" Murr Jr. simply shrugs, when the intercom buzzes. "Ah, Mr. Frost?" Triumph presses down on the buzzer. "What is it, Sheila?" "There’s one more person to see you." Triumph and Murr Jr. exchange a quick glance, both rolling their eyes afterward. Triumph groans. "Send him in." The two lean back in their chair, anticipating another big ol’ fat bag of jack shit, when… KABOOOM!!! The doors fly open, a fireball erupts from the outer office, and in walks… "Michael Bay!" Both Triumph and Murr Jr. leap back, aghast with shock and sheer surprise. Michael Bay walks up to the table and slaps both palms down upon the oak, and another explosion goes off. He smiles. "Okay, guys, here’s the deal. This movie will have everything – car chases, explosions, gunfights, explosions, robots, monsters, explosions, fireballs, hot women, explosions, Martin Sheen, an underwater battle…did I mention the explosions?" Triumph and Murr Jr. sit on the edge of their seats, their eyes lighting up like monks protesting war. Michael Bay knows he has their attention, and it’s time to go in for the kill. "I won’t lie, this is going to be the greatest movie of history. Now, we’ve got a script. Well, it isn’t so much a ‘script’ as it is a bunch of notes my cousin wrote on the back of cocktail napkins that are basically ideas from movies he’s seen, but that never stopped me before. But everything will be there. I’ll get name actors willing to slum it for big paychecks, a faceless automaton…err, I mean, beautiful woman to play his charge, and there’s even gonna be a sex scene! There will be no plot to speak of because, quite frankly, I just don’t believe in the concept of continuity. Most of the budget will go to feeding drug habits and weird fetishes, and I’m going to make up a lot of shit as I go. I also have no qualms about selling final cut out to the highest bidder. Now, I just need $500 million dollars, a plot of land in a third-world country to set up the animation studio, and access to a firework’s factory." "Done!" Triumph shoots up from his chair and shakes Michael Bay’s hand. And just like that, the ritual begins. Congratulatory handshakes all around follow pleased and bemused nods, popping of champagne, and scantily-clad moneymaker-shakers dance on poles that seem to appear out of nowhere. And then a dump truck filled with about $10 million rolls into the parking lot to be set ablaze, a sacrifice to appease the god of film, Cellulo. And that’s how movies are made.
