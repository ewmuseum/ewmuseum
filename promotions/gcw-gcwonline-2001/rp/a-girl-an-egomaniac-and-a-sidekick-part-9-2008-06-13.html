A Girl, An Egomaniac, and a Sidekick (part 9)

13 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/13/2008 (takes place 9/2002) Walking through the backstage area, Chris Storm knew right where he was going. Why he was going there was a completely different story. As hard as he tried, he couldn’t figure out why he would bother to even do it. He already knew what the response would be. But something told him he needed to apologize for last week. Cutting the corner, he saw that it would be harder than he thought. Standing like a bodyguard outside her locker room, Erik Erratic took up post and eagle-eyed the surroundings. Chris still approached though, after all, Erik maybe a contracted wrestler like him but he was still only 18 years old. What could he do; be sexually awkward around Storm? “What do you want?” Erik asked as soon as he saw Storm approach. Chris bumped him on the shoulder as he reached for the doorknob to Tessa Windsor’s locker room. Erik shoved back with his shoulder, causing Storm to take a step back. He cracked a smile, “I’m here to apologize to Tessa” he regained his step on Erik, “you know, cause of last week.” “She doesn’t want to talk to you” he fired back instantly, adding “in fact she doesn’t even want to see you.” “Ah, my poor broken heart” Chris said with a phony frown. He got right in Erik’s face, “now move aside boy scout, it ain’t worth getting hurt over.” Still, Erik Erratic refused to move despite the advancements of Storm. “Look, I’m not letting you here in.” He stepped forward to further his point, “not while her kids are in there.” The answer took Storm by surprise. He silently mouthed the words “kids?” to himself as he tried to picture Tessa with kids. The idea of Tessa as the motherly type didn’t surprise him. However, she did not look like the kind of woman that had kids already. Interrupting the two was a small backstage roadie. He stepped right between the two as Erik continued to tell Storm to leave, and knocked on the door. “Your match is up next Ms. Windsor” he said after he finished knocking. From inside the locker room Tessa thanked him and he went on his way. Chris knew he didn’t have much to say what he wanted so he pushed Erik Erratic aside while he was distracted by the appearance and disappearance of the roadie. “Move aside chump” he said as he reached for the doorknob, “I said I had business to take care of with Tessa.” Reaching for the doorknob proved pointless, however, as he comes up empty-handed. Tessa opened the door, looking ready for her match, and stare at the two for a second. She turned to Erik “thanks for watching the door”. Her attention then shifted to Chris, who gave her a big grin as to say “what?” She ignored him, flipped her head to the other side and walking off toward the ring. Feeling the ice from the cold shoulder, Chris didn’t even bother to try to follow her. He just allowed her to pass without a word. He would apologize to her, whether she wanted him to or not. She’d been man handled, treated like a toy, and sexually harassed the entire match. Yet through all that, Tessa Windsor still managed to pin Golden Hammer down for the three count. She stood to her feet, immediately searching for her niece and nephew. Thankfully, they were right where she left them in the front row cheering her own as her hand was raised up high. If only their father had been around to see this as well. It would have made it all the more worth it. Instead of dwelling on her brother’s death, Tessa rolls out of the ring and makes a beeline for the kids. There was no doubt that their father was in the back of their minds as well. It’s why before she headed back to her locker room, she made sure to embrace both of them. “That was for your dad.” She whispered in their ears. The two smiled for a second before going wide eyed. Naturally, any look of fear in a kid springs alarm up in a guardian. Thus Tessa turns around quickly enough to notice a very bitter Golden Hammer coming at her with a chair raised up high. There’s absolutely nowhere for the petite in pink to go, and no way she can duck. She closes her eyes, expecting the worse, only to hear the crowd go ballistic with their cheers. She opens her eyes, and see’s the face of her hero, “Oh thank god, Erik!” Indeed it was Erik Erratic who was now trading punches with Golden Hammer outside the ring. Tessa breathed a sigh of relief for a second, until the PA system announced aloud, “So you want to be a Rock Superstar?” Surely enough as soon as she turned her head, down the ramp rushed Chris Storm. He couldn’t miss out of a free cheap shot on Erik Erratic. Not after Erik had denied him of his apology earlier. “Crap,” Tessa slipped, forgetting all about her kids behind her. Once she realized what she’d said, she turned around and covered her track by notifying the two, “I’ll be back.” Before the two could blink, Tessa was in on the action as well, going back to work on Golden Hammer. Things weren’t fairing to well for her and Erik though. Within second Chris had Erik down on the ground, which distracted her long enough for Golden Hammer to bring her down to ground as well with a devastating Powerbomb. Thankfully, she was still conscious. She breathed heavily in and out, staring up towards the lights, praying that the beating would conclude here. What fell into her vision certainly contradicted that thought. “Oh shit.” She thought as Chris Storm hovered over her body with a steel chair in hand. She closed her eyes, confident that this would be the last thing she remembered before waking up in the medics office. “How am I going to explain this to the kids?” She thought to herself, waiting for the blow, “They’re going to be terrified every time I step into the ring. While it wouldn’t do her any good to ponder right before being knocked unconscious, she tried desperately to come up with some kind of explanation that’d ease their worries. She thought?. And she thought?.. And then she opened her eyes, wondering why he hadn’t gotten through with her yet. Just as she did, the chair came swinging down. “Go figure”.” She says as her body tenses up. But instead of a chair crashing down on her, it’s the unconscious body of Golden Hammer. “Huh?” She looks up to see her kids beside her in the ring. While she certainly isn’t all too happy to see them there, she’s certainly relieved that they didn’t have to see her fall so violently. “You alright?” Her nephew Alex probes, offering her his hand. “I’ll be fine.” She somehow manages to muster out with a heavy sigh. She looks over towards Christa, who is talking to Chris. There was no doubt in Tessa’s mind that Christa was giving him a lecture for scaring her like that. “Go help her!” She heard Christa demanding as she pointed over in the direction of her fallen guardian. Surprisingly, the so called Perfect Storm, gives in. Before Tessa knows it, she’s being cradled in his arms as he carries her backstage. Of course, he makes sure to step over Erik while carrying her out. If there was one message he did care to get across to her, it was that Erik was scum. Well, at least in his eyes. “You so owe me.” He states, planting Tessa on her feet once they finally get out of the crowds view. “Not a chance.” Was all she said back before returning to her locker room with the kids. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ If there was one reason why Tessa hated to bring Alex and Christa along with her to the shows, aside from the violence, it was the fact that the two always wanted to stay around and dilly dally. Instead of being able to go straight home after the show, like Tessa preferred, she found herself lingering around the arena as the kids always found some excuse as to why they had to stay longer. This time, it was the bathroom. “Are you almost done in there Christa?” Tessa shouted to her niece, as she waited outside the bathroom inside her locker room. Even Alex seemed ready to call it a night at this point, rubbing his eyes to keep them from gluing themselves shut. “Almost.” Christa shouts as the toilet finally flushes. What could take an eight year old that long was beyond Tessa. “We need to get going Christa. We’ve got an early flight out!” “Leaving so soon?” A voice from the doorway probed. Tessa smiled, expecting to see either her good friend Markus Stone or her current fling, Erik Erratic. Instead, who she see’s turns her smile upside down. “What’s it to you?” She questions the World Champion, Chris Storm, back. Strolling right up to them, Storm held out his arms. “Forgetting something?” he asked. Tessa looked him over, hoping someone would come along and intervene between the two. But with the conclusion of the show, most of the workers and staff had already packed up and went home. Knowing no one would come out to save her, Tessa let out a sigh. “Just waiting for Christa to come out of the bathroom” she let him know. “And then you’ll thank me?” Chris asked with a grin. Instead of answering him, she cracks open the door to the bathroom, pressuring Christa to speed up, “Let’s go Christa!” Finally, Christa comes out, looking up at her aunt turned mother with a raised eyebrow, “Why are you in such a hurry?” “Early flight!” Tessa reminded, as if those two words were some secret code for let’s get out of here now. “Need a ride to the airport?” Chris questions as he follows a few feet behind, expecting one of the kids to break down and give him the answer he was looking for. Tessa pretends not to hear a thing, laying out instructions for the kids, “Now when we get in tonight, you guys have got to go straight to bed. We can’t be late tomorrow.” The kids nod obediently. Chris, however, looks to be the one to stir up trouble, “Wow. I’d hate to live under that roof. No dessert for them either I suppose?” Again, Tessa turns a deaf ear to Chris, trying to quicken her pace by three steps. The kids are unable to keep up though, which allows Chris plenty of time to catch back up to her, “You know, you’re not being a good role model when you refuse to thank a person.” Finally, Tessa caves in and gives him the kind of attention he is looking for, “Why do you have to keep playing these games with me?” “I’m simply looking for a thank you for all my hard work” he told her as he cut in front of her. Tessa shook her head, wanting all this to just end. “I’m not saying you have to make me breakfast, I’m just asking for a thank you.” “Why?” she questioned in a huff, “Next week you’ll be back to beating the crap out of me. You know it, I know it, so there’s no reason to put on some kind of show for the kids.” He asked for her to give him a bit more credit than that, “I think I deserve that” he said. Tessa looked like she wanted to give him anything more than just that, likely a shot to the jaw. Still, she somehow manages to bite her tongue, but offers him nothing more than silence. “He’s still following us.” Christa, in true eight year old fashion, whispers aloud thinking only her adoptive mother can hear. To further the point, Chris cuts in front of the pair as they are rounding the corner, leaving Tessa no choice but to look up at him or walk straight into his chest. “One date.” He frankly stated. Tessa exhales as she finally loses it, “Why can’t you just leave me alone” Why do you have to play games with my head?” She goes to cut to the right, but he is already there before she can make the slightest movement, “It’s a yes or no question.” She turns it around with a statement of her own, “How about first you decide if you’re with me or against me?” He always loved a woman with spunk. When her arms folded up across her chest it only drove him further up the wall. “Now how can I possibly do that without getting to know you more” I barely know you. It’s not fair of me.” His cockiness is apparent across his entire body, starting from his arrogant grin and ending at his feet which were firmly planted on the ground, “so let me ask you again, one date?” Not once does she bother to look him in the eye. Instead she guides Christa by hand around Chris and continues to walk toward her rental car. At twenty one years of age she was raising her deceased brother’s children, she had no time to play mind games with a semi famous wrestler, and that was the exact thing she told Chris, “No can do. I’ve got more important people to care for.” Raising her and Christa’s interlocked hands up high, she allows the Perfect Storm see clearly where her priorities lie, even if they weren’t her biological children. “Come on, can’t you get a babysitter for one night?” He heckled, this time staying put right where she left him. “I’m not leaving them with a babysitter to spend time with you.” She turned back to let him see with his own eyes how serious she was about that. Indeed her face was distorted into a nasty snarl. Her eyes narrowed to the point where you could hardly see the blue. Still, the WIW World Champion continued to pursue his prey, “Then I’ll come over to your house and we can hang out with the kids or something.” Inside his mind he was convinced that there was no way she could turn that down. He was absolutely right with that assumption. Finally the petite in pinks arms waved in surrender, “Fine.” “Score,” He muttered utter his breath, making sure to keep it to a hushed whisper. Before he could regain himself, she was already muttering out orders, “Friday night at 6. If you don’t show up, then you’re just out of luck plain and simple.” “Got it.” His head nodded up and down as he made the mental note, his mind already wandering to places only a males mind would dare to go. Before Chris could regain himself and utter another word to her, both her and the kids were strapped into the 2002 Nissan Maxima Tessa had rented pulling out of the parking lot. Still he grinned from ear to ear as he watched them pull away. “She totally loves me.” Credit to Bobby for his slight input on this
