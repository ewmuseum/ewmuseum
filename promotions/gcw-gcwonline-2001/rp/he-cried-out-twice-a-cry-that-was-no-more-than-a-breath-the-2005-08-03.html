He cried out twice, a cry that was no more than a breath: 'The horror! The horror!'

3 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 3rd, 2005 (Okay, fourth. But let's pretend for the sake of the story) She tosses a wooden cigar box to Trey and he catches it instinctively; rests it in his lap and looks at the chipped pastel logo of a woman riding a cigar Dr. Strangelove style. Alyse smiles as she takes it from him and sets it on the bed between them, opening its lid away from Trey and pulling out a zippered bag and a small pack of rolling papers. 'You mind?' 'S'your house.' 'Wanna party?' No man can say no. She pulls green and red nuggets, covered in shining white, from the bag and breaks them up onto one of the papers, rolls and seals it in one quick motion. Only her tongue is slow, a controlled slide down the adhesive. When she finishes one she hands it to Trey and quickly rolls another. 'You got a light?' She hands him a green Bic, 'what, you don't smoke?' 'Not for awhile.' 'Well, this is special--it's my best stuff.' Trey tries to pass and she waves his hand away. 'You've got your good thing, and I've got mine.' The taste is flowers in his throat and lemon on his tongue, corrosive and numbing. He keeps his lungs full of smoke even as his body swells, stressing his skin and bones to the verge of collapse. 'Damn,' stumbling through his words, 'some good shit.' 'What'd I say?' She leans back and pushes the bottom of the window up. The oily rain splatters hard on the sill, draining down the wall in slimy trails, and brings with it a fatally cold wind. The string dances, wrapped around her head and flying over the far shoulder. 'So, 'bout this art thing...' 'Honestly,' an exhale, 'I didn't think you were seriously into it. I told you you were different than other guys.' 'Not that much different; don't get ya' hopes up, kid.' 'Cute. You're not holding it in long enough.' 'Huh?' 'You're just breathing it in and out, now.' 'Shit,' Trey looks down at the joint, paper cracking and brown, then falls back on the bed to stare at the ceiling. 'Can't even feel the smoke anymore, anyway. Damn. Fuckin' chronic.' 'I'd never let a man of your stature down. It's an art, I tell you.' 'This your big artist deal?' 'Not quite. I think you'll appreciate it just as much, being an artist yourself.' Alyse leans forwards and rests her elbows on Trey's chest. 'Wouldn't say that.' 'Why not? What you do is art; it's been your life for years now. Some people just go out, sweat, and end up looking like total douche bags with no clue what they're doing. That's not you. You're a Beethoven, a Van Gogh, a man with enough fire to burn forever.' 'Shit, I'm flattered. Can't exactly agree, y'know.' 'You should. You're insane if you don't see it. You move like an animal on the hunt, aware of everything before anyone else. You stalk, you prowl, you decimate. Christ, Trey.' Her hand spreads on his ribcage, barely claiming any territory, and presses down with the full of her lithe body. Her fingers press into the scar tissue 'T' on his chest and trace the top groove from shoulder to shoulder. Her breathing is quick and humid in Trey's face. 'What you do is fucking brilliant. When you're on, Christ, no one can stop you. Even when you're off, there's no one near your level. Those fuckers, they'll try to cheat you and fuck you over anyway they can. Let 'em try, you're a monster.' She runs finger tips down the center of his chest, replaying the scar down to his navel. 'You'll make them fucking pay.' Her breathing stops and she clothes her eyes, mouth agape with her head rocking gently up and down. 'Big fan, ain't you?' 'Mmmhmm. Gimme a minute, babe.' She leaves the room, leaves Trey to muse. He sits up on the bed and throws his eyes around, Alyse's footsteps fading out further and further away. The step creaks, she's as good as gone. Trey stands and takes a long hit as he passes around, thumbing through a canvas photo album on the desk. He flips past floating traces of the past, never-ending smiles that make up a thousand uninteresting stories in Trey's head for every second that passes. Expensive SUVs and gleaming suicide machines speed through a culture he doesn't understand--pure fancy and psychosis--and can't picture realistically. Crude mockeries of conversations pass: 'Yo dude, let's go to the mall'--'Kickin', we can check out some new threads'--'Word'--'One.' He can't even convince himself, and lets his image of the pampered to the back of his head so Alyse's place in life doesn't destroy his carefully refigured mind. If one fuse shorts, then no one's really safe anymore. He takes the finishing hit on the joint and puts it out on the metal button of his jeans. He shudders. And the lights go out. He was left alone for minutes that turned into hours that felt like days, eyes hung low in his skull and body burning. It was withdrawal from an addiction he'd had shoved in his veins by a friend that shined like heaven would. His smile stretched across the room as he pried Trey's mouth open--slipping inside weaker and weaker drugs to wean Trey's decimated system away from hell. 'There you go. How do you feel?' Trey nodded and the man poured a glass of water down his throat to wash the pills back. 'He's going psychotic out there. Be glad.' 'Stop him.' 'There's no stopping him, not now. Just ignore whatever bullshit he starts spewing when I'm away. He'll get his when the time's right.' Trey leaned back in the steel chair, barely straining against his cuffs with mouth agape. 'Are you breathing?' Well? 'Jesus,' the man said. 'Fucking Jesus.' He grabbed Trey's arm, ink fresh and skin still sore for a devil's dementia, and felt for a pulse. Something. 'Breathe, you stupid motherfucker. Breathe.' He's pushed to the bed and roughly straddled. Something clicks and a dim lamp near the head of the bed comes on to cast constantly shifting shadows along the folds of their clothes, the bumps and ripples in the blanket. Trey's eyes start at the door, cracked open and fading into that nothing behind. A thin strand on the floor leads from the portal to the bed and finishes neatly in the palm of his hand. Alyse holds her arms behind her back and grins razorblades down to him; her skin hanging loose and baggy. 'I've got a surprise.' Trey blinks and it startles him. His eyes open to a clay egg pressing on his chest; elliptical and wider than his fist at the base. It still looks soft to the touch, like she'd just finished with it in a haste to prove her earlier flaunting. 'Do you like it?' Transfixed. Breathing is turmoil, but his breaks the sculpture's hypnosis and looks up to Alyse. Her eyes are soft and wide with a dreamy hope of approval, the smile on her face sending hard wrinkles and creases through her hide. It hangs looser on the edges, flapping in the window's breeze like a bat. She nods. Thunder quakes the house. Trey's chest pulsates, and his eyes go from Alyse to the egg. Another vibration rattles through his bones and a tapping sound follows it. Paralyzed. The egg begins to sway from side to side and Trey doesn't breathe anymore. Alyse is a hazy blur in the background, finally weak, and the egg begins to crack. It starts in a small point near the front and spiderwebs out for several inches. Pieces break off and fall heavily to Trey's chest, leaving strands of a thick, almost clear, ooze that pours from the hole. The tapping continues and a slimy point breaks out from the shell, twisting to clear a wider hole. It's a bird in Trey's head until more cracks on the side facing him begin to shatter. The piece shakes and coos as two more spikes pop through. The first is free; thin, gray and segmented like an ant's leg. The others soon follow, six spindly legs clawing desperately at the air above them. The egg shrieks out and rolls to its sides, further this way with one push and further that way with another, until it finally rights itself. The claws dig into Trey's abdomen as the creature tries to hold itself upright under the pressure of the shell. It spins and a fissure spreads in a circle from the smaller tip of the egg as it desperately stumbles forwards, ever-screaming and stabbing. Silence. Trey feels the end of the egg fall against his stomach. A wet gray head, arrow-shaped and armored, stretches out from the shell with its long neck. It looks to Alyse for a moment, then turns to look at Trey. Its face is soft tissue, featureless except for three black eyes at the corners and the small star for a mouth in the center. It twitches open, then closes. Small flaps, gills, on the sides of its neck follow its pattern. 'Oh my God.' Alyse leans back and her loose carapace finally falls to Trey's lap as the creature slowly sheds the rest of its shell. Trey looks up to Alyse, now nude except for the ragged, useless past wrapped around her waist. Her skin is soft and new; supple pink flesh that exists to yield and to strain. 'It's beautiful.' She scoops up her creation and brushes off loose shell before coddling it against her small breasts. Its head waves around and snaps playfully, yipping like a puppy until she screams and tosses it to the side. It hits the wall like wet meat, splatters out dark blue blood that hangs thick. The body falls to the ground with a sound only described as 'splakoosh,' leaving a few stray pieces stuck in the entrails that decorate the wall and window. Alyse has her hands tight to the side of her neck and her eyes forced shut, welling over with tears. She mutters something primal enough to be inhuman and opens her eyes to look at Trey. 'I'm sorry.' she quivers. 'You hurt?' 'It stings.' 'Lemme see.' He sits up as she moves her hands away. A circular welt leaks blood in a few spots, with three smaller circles of flayed fresh inside of it. The blood gushes for a second before beginning to dry up and scab over. He puts a finger on the outer circle and Alyse jumps. 'Don't touch it.' 'Sorry.' 'How is it?' 'Scabbing over already.' She throws her arms around Trey, gripping her fingers into his shoulder blades and gasping for air. 'Is he,' stutters, 'dead?' 'It's a smear now.' 'Art isn't eternal. A moment of glory is all it needs.' She smiles and rubs her fresh lips over Trey's neck. 'Keeping it around too long is a dangerous obsession. Makes you blind to inspiration.' Trey leans back, Alyse still wrapped on him and rocking gently.
