Date: 11-03-06 It was dark. Really dark. Almost so dark it could be compared to really, really dark. There was a slight noise in the darkness which resembled the sound of a human whimper. The cab of the truck was filling with water rapidly in the midst of the night. The whimper turned into a gurgling noise, as the water completely engulfed the man trapped in the sinking vehicle. “Gurgle, burgle, glurg” he screams?. Suddenly a hand appears from the darkness, along with an exceptionally bright light. The hand effortlessly lifts the man from the vehicle and pulls him to the surface of the water. The light so bright it can’t be made out who or what the saving grace may be. “Arise asshole.” A deep voice bellows? The man stares into the light confused “God?” There is a slight pause before an answer rings out. “Yes this is God. Now get up.” The light rapidly fades, to reveal a towering man standing over a much smaller man. The bigger man shadow engulfs the smaller one, who is lying on a hammock in a well manicured yard. The bigger man has in his hands, a 10,000 candle watt flashlight, and a garden hose in the other hand still spewing water. “Damnit Atlas!
