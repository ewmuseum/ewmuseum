The Small Things

24 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 24th 2005 The eyes of envy are often at hand, a man walks through the hallways of a wrestling arena, eyeing the superstars knowing which men he can beat, and which he can’t and which matches would come down to the wire. The man I defeated nearly 3 weeks ago to win the Cruiserweight title walked away from the match with Glory. Rei Tiu walked away with the glory, the love of the fans, everything that I desired. The scene opens in the open area of the airport as Aiden Jackson sits there, typing quickly on his laptop. People walk quickly down the center of the airport rushing towards their flights, on this very day Aiden’s flight was delayed. Several people sit around Aiden silently reading a book, or staring straight forward towards the world outside. Aiden sits there muttering to himself as he types reads an email and types another one simultaneously. To: [detail withheld] From: [detail withheld] Subject: REPLY:: whats up? “Hey Jesse I’m sitting in the airport with another delayed flight. Did you happen to catch my match with Seymour, rough loss eh? It was a well fought match I think I could take him the next time. At least I held onto the strap right? I love the smile it brings to that face knowing that I have at least one fan out there. Have you found anymore information on the book? Hopefully everything falls through as planned, and we find it all out. This time though all of the men involved will be brought to trial and jail the way it should have been done the first time, I made a huge mistake yes. Anyways, when I get back into town after these next couple of shows we’ll have to meet up for dinner again.” Aiden stared at the screen for a moment, clicked the send button then closed the laptop. He stared at it for a moment then leaned back and closed his eyes softly. Suddenly the silence was broken by a small boy walking by. “Aiden, Aiden, Can I see your title!”” He said to me in an excited manor. “Sorry Sir, he’s just a little excited about seeing a superstar. I hope that he hasn’t disturbed you in anyway.” The large parent said to me kindly “Oh No, it’s not a problem at all.” I said in reply. I pulled out the Global Championship Wrestling Cruiserweight title from my bag and showed it to the small boy standing there. He awed at the belt, and then I pulled out a picture of myself and signed it handing it to him. “Promise me that you’ll watch every match of mine boy.” I said to him with a kind smile across my face. “I’ll be sure to!” He said with a large smile. The moments like that made me enjoy the fame of being a wrestler, the fame of being someone out of the ordinary compared to the regular average joe. I enjoyed the small boy walking up to me, I enjoyed every moment of it and that was the type of feeling I wanted in the ring.
