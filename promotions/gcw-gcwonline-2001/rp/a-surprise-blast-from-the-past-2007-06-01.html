A Surprise Blast From The Past

1 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/1/07 Driving along the rural highway towards Jimmy's ranch, Emma is rather eager to get to Jimmy as quickly as humanly possible. Knowing that Jimmy recently lost his Television Title to Jay Terror and then last week, falling to Jordan White, Emma felt that it was her duty as Jimmy's girlfriend to be by her man's side. She turns into Jimmy's driveway like a rally driver and then up towards the house leaving a trail of dry dirt to fill the air along the driveway. She then climbs out to find Jimmy's sister Tia, sitting on the porch, drinking a hot cup of coffee and reading a magazine when Emma walks up to her with a smile on her face. "Hey there, Tia." Emma says in a friendly tone. "Hey Emma, how's it going?" Tia asks with a smile. "Pretty good, I thought I'd drop by and see how Jimmy was doing." Emma says, as her tone suddenly changes to one of concern. "You've actually just missed him." Tia responds quickly. "Oh?" Emma says confused, as she can see his Harley Davidson parked in the garage next to the house. "He's actually gone for a run, he's not in the best of moods this morning to be honest." Tia says as she stands up to walk back inside the house. "Even Junior noticed his mood this morning, he's actually in the gym now cleaning it up for his dad. You can go in there and say hi to him if you like." She says with a smile. Smiling at Tia, Emma says "I think I just might do that, thanks." Emma starts walking up to the gym that Jimmy had specially built for himself. Never setting foot into the gym, Emma was expecting it to be smelly and somewhat stuffy inside. Nervously opening the door, Emma braces herself for the full force of body oder stench to hit her like tonne of bricks. She slowly sticks her head inside and is pleasantly surprised to be met with the smell of disinfectant and a spotlessly clean gym inside. Feeling at ease at the cleanliness of the place, Emma walks in freely to find Jimmy Junior, shining one of his father's championship belts hanging up on a spectacular display. As well as that, also hanging are several other championship belts and a large selection of photographs of Jimmy in action in the ring and backstage with wrestlers he had worked with over the years. "This display is awesome, Junior." Emma says with obvious amazement in her voice. Without being distracted from his shining, Junior says "I made this for my dad last year for his birthday." "This display is rather breath taking Junior, I bet your dad was surprised to see it." Said Emma as she tries to reach out to Junior in hope that she can strike up a conversation with him. "Yeah, I think he liked it." Junior replied unenthusiastically. Being the type of woman that gets straight to the point, Emma takes a deep breath before asking, "You don't really approve of your father and I dating, do you Junior?" With his back still turned and shining his father's title belt, Junior just shrugs his shoulders and tries to side step Emma's direct question. Knowing that he wont open up to her, Emma realises that she is the one who has to convince Junior that she is someone that his father needs in his life. "What's the matter Junior? Have I done anything wrong?" Emma asks in a pleading tone. "You haven't done anything wrong." Junior mutters under his breath. Emma tilts her head to the side before asking with a concerned look on her face, "Then what's the wrong?" Junior turns for the first time with a tear in his eye and says, "I feel like we're betraying my mom." Emma rushes towards him and grabs a hold of his shoulders firmly and says with conviction, "I wouldn't dare replace your mother, she will always be loved by you and your dad, that's something I would never try to change. What you have to understand Junior is that I'm not the same as your mother, I'm different and I was hoping you'd see me as someone different to your mother." Junior wipes the tear away from his eye and begins to let his guard down as a smile creeps through. Feeling the self made protection walls crashing around Junior, Emma begins to smile as well when Jimmy enters the Jimmy unexpectedly. "What's going on here?" He asks with a confused look on his face. "Junior was just showing me this display he made for you." Emma quickly replied. "I didn't know he did this and it just too my breath away." She added. Emma could see in Jimmy's eyes that he really wasn't interested in what she thought of the display Junior made, or anything else for that matter. Feeling a little uncomfortable seeing a serious side to Jimmy, Emma felt that the best thing for her to do was to leave him to his own devices. Jimmy walks with purpose to his bench press and starts to add some weights to the bar mounted on two frames. It is clearly evident to Emma and Junior that Jimmy wants to be left alone as he prepares for his next match against Hydra at Warpath. "Baby, do you mind if I take Junior with me into town for some lunch?" Emma asks nervously while Jimmy strains to lift the heavy weights at a rapid pace. After completing the set Jimmy sits up and looks straight into Emma's eyes and says "That sounds like a great idea." If Junior knew his father, he could immediately tell that his dad was in no mood for socialising and quickly motioned for Emma to follow him out of the gym. As the two left, Jimmy returned to bench pressing as his fury at losing to Jordan White is slowly getting the better of him. Losing the title didn't bother Jimmy too much it was losing to Jordan White that has him agitated. In a match that would have really propelled him up the proverbial ladder, Jimmy fell short of reaching his goal and in turn, pretty much put him back at square one. After torturing his muscles during his grueling workout, Jimmy wastes no time in jumping into the shower to cool down and give his muscles a chance to relax. While soaking his head under the powerful shower head, Jimmy starts to realise that he was acting like a jackass to both Emma and Junior. Even though sometimes he gets really upset with himself, Jimmy sometimes fails to realise that he takes his frustrations out on people that are closest to him. Knowing he has to make it up to both Emma and Junior for his attitude towards them earlier, Jimmy quickly gets out of the shower, drys off and puts on some clothes before jumping on his Harley Davidson and heads into town to join Emma and Junior. Just as Emma and Junior are finishing their lunch, a male is standing across the street from the eatery that the two are seated at and is intensely staring at Emma, who is unaware of his presence. Sharing a joke with Junior, Emma is clearly enjoying herself, which starts to aggravate the mysterious male. Both Emma and Junior stand up and head out to Emma's car that is parked near the building. As she ruffles through her bag to find her keys she feels a hand on her shoulder that turns her around. When she looks into the mystery man's eyes, terror rapidly rushes through her body. "No! No!" Emma says as her panic stricken voice starts to crack from her over whelming fear of this man. "I told you I'd find you." The man says with a sadistic smile, while he tries his hardest to pierce her body with his steely gaze. Junior rushes up to the man and tries to force him away from Emma but is pushed down to the ground with the man laughing at him. The man arrogantly turns so he can face Emma again but when he does he gets nailed with a savage right hand that feels like a brick smashing his face in. The mysterious man hits the concreted car park and looks up in shock to see Jimmy Thunder hovering over his numb body. The mystery man, while in a daze from Jimmy's right hand, scrambles backwards then slowly gets to his feet as he wipes the blood from his nose and mouth. "We'll catch up soon, Emma." The mystery man says with a smile as he continues to wipe the blood from his face. He then turns to Jimmy and points to him with an evil smirk, "And you, Jimmy Thunder, or whatever you want to call yourself, you can guarantee that I'll be seeing you soon, real soon." The mystery man staggers away laughing when Jimmy turns to Emma and quickly embraces her as she bursts out crying. Jimmy then turns to his son who is seated on the ground. He looks up at his father and nods at him to let him know he's ok as the screen fades to black.
