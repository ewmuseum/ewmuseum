Date: 4/20/06 April 19 2006 Journal Entry: He was here, yes. HE was here. The Keeper of Secrets, Ian Childress. The leader of the Order of the Hammer's main operations. He had an effect on me, it was... interesting... it was all I could do not to just go full feather and manifest in front of him. Like some damn jaybird or something. His personality wasn't what I thought it would be. He was a quite man with dark hair, but it was his eyes that gave him away. Cold, deep and blue. Not sure what he could give me for information not sure if I am ready to ask him yet, but at least I know who it is. But there was something about him. My skin crawled when he came near to me, he shook my hand it was like he was looking though me, physically, to the other side. To the one side I try to hide. The side I can't control yet. Josh seems preoccupied with something. I'm not sure what, I guess I will have to wait until he is ready to talk to me, it's something that he is hiding from everyone. Although I have my suspicions. But that's all they are so far. In the meantime, he's been an utter tiger in bed. Guess that helps both of us with our stress levels? Huh? As for my research it is getting harder to get the information I need. I wish I could just tell everyone and have them help me find out the information, but I cant take the chance of putting my friends at risk. Grandpa wouldn't have spoken to me in a way they could not understand if he wasn't worried about others finding out in some way. I know it, deep down inside I know it was a test for me to see if I could find out facts about my family's past, including his part in it. It just sucks that my people didn't start to actually record any of their natural history until most of them had been slaughtered by those who had come to take over their land. Well, time to start thinking about a few other things. GCW has FINALLY booked me again. After that asshole, Jayce Bradley pulled at my bottoms to secure his win in the Rampage Tour, I'm pissed a bit that they've overlooked me. Of course, Bradley went on to be a sacrifice to Shadow on that tour, and then got a shot at the damn TV Title, and wins it. So he's walking around with Two belts while I'm overlooked for even simple shit. I think it's frustrating Blaise too. He's not been booked even with Ukyo for a tag match since Rampage... But this week, they let two pot head idiots from Seattle book the card because it's on 4/20. Oooh, they sure are aiming for the man show audience. So what do I get for a match? A fluff match, yup. Give the Indian girl with big boobs a match against some blonde skank who can barely speak English. Put them both in bikinis, on the beach, and let one of these meatheads referee the match so he can get his cheap thrills. Well, as much as I should be pissed, I'm not. That skank is the former U.S. Champion. And while she's shown she can't even hang with Vivica Valentine, she's still a big name in this federation. I'm going into this to win. I've got two major things going for me. And it's not the fact that I'm a big fake thing trying to wrestle: bleached hair, fake boobs. It's the fact that I already wrestle in a bikini. It's more comfortable, and in all honesty... do I really care if I show some of my body? I've posed topless for a men's magazine before. I've seen Eva cover up her saline or plastic boobs before, when she was on that Holiday show and Shadow attacked her. She's not comfortable with herself? Well it's obvious with all the make up and the fact that she's got a budget for her 'entourage' of make up artists and hair stylists that rivals her paycheck... she's not comfortable with who she is. Me? So maybe I'll flash some nipple, or my ass. Who cares? Oh, they'll enjoy it, that's for sure. As for the ring of action? A beach? Sand? Rocks? Water? Whens the last time this chica had sand between her ass cheeks? Or even got that bleached blonde shit in some salt water? I had twelve cousins to wrestle around at the pow wows and that, in the canyons and on the lakes of South Dakota. I've been tackled, playing football, on the plains and fields of the Rosebud Reservation. I hope she's ready for a fight, cause I know I am. GCW will know who I am after this week. That's for sure. *~*~*~* ~Jacksonville Beach, Florida~ ~The Inn on Oak Street~ ~Second Floor Suites~ The KB:NAN had decided to keep away from the hustle and bustle of the airport hotels and such, and as Blaise preferred to do, went the bed and breakfast route. They had vehicles here, and could make it to the arena and all the things they needed to do, rather easily. Between the three suites on the second floor, everyone had a bed, a TV and access to what ever else they needed. It was pretty sweet if you looked at it. Even the big man, Gray, had a king sized bed. Although he wasn't sharing it with anyone. It overlooked the St. Johns River, near where it joined the Atlantic Ocean, so the smell and just general feel of the place was rather nice, and natural. This set well with Dani. The door to the Boudoir room (the romantic room), which was given to Dani and Josh, opened and in stepped both of the girls from the KB:NAN... Dani and Ukyo. They were both giggling and had bags in their hands. Two men who were sitting on the couch, one playing his Playstation 2, the other messing on his laptop computer, looked up at them. If you can't guess which one of the two men who hung around with Dani are doing what, then we'll get you up to speed. Gray is all work, thus laptop, using the Inn's wireless internet they advertise. Josh, he's a Playstation kind of guy when he's bored. Unfortunately for him, most Inn's won't let him bring his drum kit and practice. ?Hey guys, where's Blaise?? Josh was busy playing what looked like Soul Calibur 3, using his favorite, Taki the Female Ninja. As he moved the controller in his hands to the left and right, he was doing one thing that Dani actually found to be somewhat cute, his tongue almost came out from the right side of his mouth. Gray looked up at her and answered. ?He's doing a recon for you. He found out that your match was on the beach, so he headed down to get some pictures of the area they're setting up the mobile TV Studio. That way he can bring them back and you can see what you're getting into.? Dani nodded at him. For some reason it actually made sense. And that twinged something in her gut. It scared her, yes. She was coming to understand how Blaise thought. His almost military mind as he adopted what he'd learned as a Hunter for the Order to his everyday life and the business they had gotten into. ?Well, Ukyo and I are just getting back from the mall here. I bought some new swimsuits, I'm going to try them on here, and see which one will work better for this match? Okay?? Gray gave her a stare. He didn't like it when he was cornered for such things. He was too damn old fashioned maybe? ?Uhm, is this anything like your normal sleepwear or swimwear?? Dani blushed a bit as Gray asked that question. ?Maybe?? Gray laid the screen of his laptop down and started to get up. ?In that case, I'll be asking for my leave. This is a fashion show best reserved for my little brother, not me.? ?Gray?? Gray was resolved on this, he always was. Dani knew why. He hated seeing her walking around in a thong or anything revealing actually. He saw her as a little sister, and didn't see it as proper for him to see her dressed in that manner. Sometimes it was annoying, but she'd not force the issue. Gray looked at the screen and saw Josh finish saving the game he was playing. He then reached over and hit the off button on the console. ?HEY!? Gray just looked down at him as he stood up. ?Dani would like your opinion on some things. I'd suggest waiting before he continue playing, okay?? Josh looked over at Dani and realized that he was going to be the only one. ?What's up?? He hadn't heard a thing from when they had walked in till now. Ukyo spoke up, before Dani threw something at him. ?Dani and I just got back from the store. She'd like to show you her options for the bikini for her match on Thursday.? Josh's face dropped a bit. ?Why?? ?Because she has a match? Didn't you read the announcements from the federation? Dani versus Eva VanLorne. Beach Bikini Brawl, right?? Josh's face went a little red. ?I read it. I'm asking why, as in, why bother? Why do the match? No show it.? Now Dani spoke up. ?Why? Why should I not show up for this match?? Josh tossed the controller down out of his hands. ?Because it's nothing but a ploy to get you and that other girl into skimpy clothing to parade you around. Nothing else. It's a fluff match and it's an insult to you and your talents.? Dani could almost tell what he was really thinking. ?I'm not worried about a fluff match. They happen. It's mostly grown bald men, who are still teenagers at heart who book these shows. They want some T&A, big deal. It's still a match against a former champion. The most accoladed woman from GCW's past before we came here. I win this match, I get noticed finally. Especially after the way Jayce Bradley stole my Rampage spot from me.? ?I don't want you doing this match!? Josh was pretty adamant about it. Dani suddenly stormed off to the bathroom, carrying her bag with her. ?What's that all about?? Josh just looked at the bathroom door, with a scowl on his face. ?Nothing.? Ukyo gave him a scowl back. ?Look, it's only women who should have these mood swings. What's been up your ass lately?? Josh spun around and tried to stare Ukyo down. However he wasn't getting too far with the hardened woman who stood in front of him. ?You wouldn't exactly understand.? A few tense moments later, Dani walked out in what was the most outlandish bikini that she'd ever worn. It barely covered the important parts, and what was covered, was also covered in white feathers. Josh's jaw almost dropped. What wasn't showing, looked upon closer inspection to basically be held on by what could only be fishing line. ?What the hell?? ?It's got a set of wings to it too. But I can't put them on by myself. You think this will be good for the match?? Josh's jaw could have hit the floor. ?You're NOT wrestling in that!? Dani's face contorted into what could become anger, really quickly. ?I don't remember turning complete control of my life and career over to you.? Josh appeared to take the hint pretty quicklky, and backed off some. ?You'll be an FCC disaster waiting to happen in that. One wrong move and they have more on that channel then they could face up against.? ?Cable isn't subject to FCC guidelines.? ?Yes, but you saw what happened after a boob from Janet Jackson for regular TV and radio. The FCC wants to run cable too. You slip in that, and it'll be more then a boob showing on TV during prime time. It'll be full on...? Dani blushed a bit. ?C'mon. You know you don't need this match...? Dani turned and headed back to the bathroom. Josh's body went a little limp, his shoulders falling as he looked up at the ceiling, avoiding the steel eyes of Ukyo. He huffed out a chest full of air, as he felt the tension he was building up. A few moments later, Dani came out in a black bikini that was only there a bit more then the feathered one. It looked like it was more leather, but a shiny kind. The front panels were cut out partially, only covering what needed to be for Florida decency laws. ?Dani...? Dani's glare was emotionless. ?What?? ?Don't do this... Please?? Her hands went to her hips, instinctively going onto the defensive while having her offense up. A trait only women had. ?Why not? It's Eva VanLorne. I can beat her. I'm used to fighting wearing just a bit more then this. I'm used to fighting outside. I'm a tomboy who grew up to have a natural size D rack. I can do this.? ?But....? Dani realized what it was. ?You don't want the other guys, like this Bammy idiot to look at me? Are you getting a bit jealous there Josh?? ?No... I? Dani moved forward, to a point where he couldn't advert himself away from her eyes. ?Where was this concern when I was wearing a lace thong and not much else for the FHM cameras? Where was this every time I do a photo shoot?? Josh didn't answer. ?Look at me Joshua...? He turned his head a bit and looked at her face. ?You know damn well that there are guys, quite possibly even on the Global or Core rosters who have the magazines I've been in. For all we know people like (Edited for personal names) have whacked their pud to it. I'm not ashamed of what I have. It's all natural, all native. If it shows, and they appreciate it, fine by me.? Josh didn't like that answer any. ?But... they shouldn't be seeing it!? Dani shook her head as if she was speaking to a simpleton. When it came to men, sometimes they were. ?You know Ukyo, I sometimes don't get it. I know Blaise and you are unique in a way, due to the attraction you have being on a level beyond normal earthly bounds. But men are quite strange.? She reached up and grabbed Josh by the head, and pulled his face closer to hers. ?Look, Mr. Brand... Don't worry about them. You're the one I come home to, or the one that'll be waiting for you to come home. If they look and they wolf whistle or hoot and hollar, it's because they want what you have. But they can't have it... I'm taken, okay?? He nodded his head. ?I don't get you men sometimes. Especially guys who land a woman that's considered attractive. If they're paying attention, take it as a compliment to YOU, too. You're the one who's sharing my bed. Not them. Let them applaud and cheer. Let them wish that Eva and I end up naked on national TV for them. Cause when it's all said and done, I'll pack my shit and come back here, and you'll be here. Not them.? Josh looked to be calming down a bit. ?In the meantime, you leave me no choice though, Ukyo will be my valet and backup at the Beach. Not you. I don't need a hothead attacking some fans at ringside who get rude. I can ignore them. So can Ukyo. But you? I'd question that.? Josh nodded. ?Now, you'll excuse me. While I like this one, I have a few more to try on. Stay and see them or not. But I've got to decide before tomorrow, and I'd like your input.? Josh nodded again, speechless. He then sat down in the chair and just looked up at her. Dani turned and headed back to the bathroom. Ukyo looked down at Josh and smiled. ?Oh, a side note on that little thing she pointed out...? ?Yeah?? ?Don't pull a Billy Bob Thorton either.? Josh shrugged. ?I can see you lived in a cave. He was married to Angelina Jolie, who's known for her admitted high sex drive. Guess what she warned him to do to keep her waiting for him?? ?What?? ?Not have his eye wander. Guess what he did?? ?Point taken. I'm sorry too, for this. I've just been under a lot of stress lately.? Ukyo half smiled. ?Yeah, as I asked, what bug you got up your ass? You need some midol or something?? Dani walked out again, this time in something that had a bit of flowers to it's pattern. She walked out and turned around, showing the thong back to Josh. For now, we fade out and let them continue on....
