Bedpan Blues and News (Aftermath Part 3 Conclusion)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: A few hours later It’s a dream like you’re falling. The ground rushes up to meet you, getting closer every second. You want to close your eyes but the wind batters your skin, forcing your lids open, making everything seem tight around you. You try to scream but it comes out as a choking cough, the wind rushes in every time you open your mouth and over fills your lungs. It feels like your going to drown on air. Your entire body shivers, it’s damn cold, but you’re more scared than anything else. This is it, you know you’re going to die. Inches away from the ground you’re finally able to close your eyes. You know tensing your body is the worst thing to do, if you go limp you might survive, if you’re really lucky. But you can’t help it, every muscle stiffens. You scream. And sit bolt up right in bed. The sounds of healing fill the room, the steady beep of a heart monitor, the muted drip of the IV. Looking around, you see a beautiful woman, asleep in a chair beside the bed. Everything’s still a little hazy, but you know her, Sheena, that’s her name. Lover, friend, confidant, she’s just about your entire world, and up until a couple weeks ago you weren’t even speaking to her. Amazing how things change isn’t it? Raising your hands to your face you feel your arm skin tighten around the needle. They must have given you something to sleep. A sedative, that was the word. Gently probing the bandage around your skull, you’re given a decent idea as to why you’re hear and why everything is so foggy. Must’ve taken a good shot to the head, that’d explain a lot. As for why you didn’t have a headache, the IV was probably to thank for that. It probably would have been a doozy too, considering how thickly wrapped up you are. Going to speak you realize your throat is raw. Judging from the vile taste in your mouth, you probably vomited, a lot, at least enough to give you the dry heaves. Must’ve been one hell of a night, but you still can’t remember too much. A great big shadow covering you, a sharp pain, the lights going out. Hanson, that’s right Hanson hit you with a chair, with everything he had. Nearly took your damn head off, oh yeah, that makes sense. “Mike you’re a awake.” You don’t know what startles you more, the voice or the hand on your shoulder. Snapping your head to the right, you know you’ll probably regret it once the medication wears off, but you’ve got to see who else is with you. Ah, Alex, your cousin, that fits. Same old Alex playing guard dog while the family sleeps, it wouldn’t have even occurred to him to get some rest, he’d protect you until he dropped. It’d be nice if you could believe you deserved that kind of devotion. He looks worried, understandable, you try to ease his fears with a warm smile. You’re actually feeling pretty good right now, except for the raw throat and fog. “Could I get some water?” Immediately he’s in motion grabbing a cup and pouring some water from that little Styrofoam wrapped pitcher they always have in hospital rooms. “Damn, I’m sorry man, I should’ve already thought of that.” Alex was always berating himself like that, probably didn’t help that you would usually agree with him. You tried to make a mental note to start treating him less like hired help, and more like a guy you grew up with, hopefully you’d remember in the morning. Taking the cup from him you took a sip. The water was cold enough to make your teeth ache, and it tasted like the nectar of the gods. Taking the edge off your raw throat you actually feel like talking a little more. “Don’t worry about it Alex. It’s fine.” “No, I should’ve..” “Alex, it’s Ok, don’t worry about it man. How were you supposed to know I was thirsty, it’s not like you’re a mind reader.” “Yeah, god I’ve been so worried man, are you alright?” Taking another sip of water, everything is beginning to get a little clearer. Your eyes are really starting to find their focus now. Except for the chair, everything is pretty much a big blank, but you realize that’s to expected after something like that. “I’m feeling ok I guess. I suppose I owe you and Sheena some thanks for getting me here. Normally I hate hospitals, but obviously I needed it this time.” He looks so tired, the night must have been exhausting for him. You can only imagine the wreck Sheena was, Alex must’ve had to take charge. That kind of thing is really stressful for him, he doesn’t have enough confidence in himself. That’s another thing you’re going to have to try and change about him, odd you don’t remember ever thinking like that before. “I was worried man. You seemed to go in and out there for a long time, one minute you couldn’t even walk, or talk, then you’re standing up, telling Sheena to get in the car. Then you go really quiet, it was scary.” “Alex, I don’t remember a damn thing about that. Last thing I actually remember is Hanson flying at me with a chair, next thing I know I wake up here.” That triggered something, Hanson hit you with that chair during Dangerous Games. That’s right, probably took you out of the match too, bastard you were going to have to make him pay for that one. With Steven the way he is, you know that was your best shot at gaining a place in the world title scene. Oh, well there’s always next year. “It was incredible Mike. After he hit you, everyone in the back thought you were done, but you got up and kept going.” “Hehe, I finished the match?” “Damn right you finished the match. Congratulations.” It takes you a second. The full weight of what’s just been said doesn’t sink in right away. You finished the match, congratulations. It wasn’t possible was it? There’s no chance in hell.. “You don’t mean?” “Yeah, I do. You won, you’ve got a title shot at Annihilation.” And there it was. Little bits and pieces start coming together. You remember asking who won about five times in the car. You remember them giving you the same answer five times. Then you hadn’t understood, but you did now. Must’ve been a concussion. And all of a sudden your crushed. It doesn’t matter that you won, you’re laid up in a hospital bed, no doubt concussed, you probably won’t even be able to wrestle at Annihilation. Head trauma is a really tricky thing, and doctors everywhere know it. If it was bad they wouldn’t clear you to wrestle until you had a full work up usually. That could take a lot of time, if the results where bad, well you could be out for months. “Doesn’t matter anyway, I guess.” “What, why?” Normally a question like that would’ve annoyed you, but you realize he’s been through so much he has an excuse to not be thinking. “Alex, I’ve no doubt got a concussion, I probably won’t even be cleared to wrestle by Annihilation.” That’s odd, to say the least. Alex was grinning from ear to ear, almost bouncing up and down like an over eager puppy dog. “No Mike that’s the best thing. I talked to the doctor he said you should be on your feet by tomorrow morning. The concussion wasn’t anywhere near as bad as it could’ve been. He said the main reason you were so out of it was extreme dehydration, from loss of blood, and all the fluids you puked up. They just wanted to keep you over night for observation.” Never have you been so happy laying in a hospital bed, well there was that one time, with the two nurses, but that’s beside the point. You try not to get your hopes up, Alex might not have gotten everything the doctor said right. You’d have to talk to him yourself to find out for sure. Still though Alex did have a knack for listening really closely and parroting back information. This could be the best news you’d been given all year. “Are you sure that’s what he said Alex?” “Yeah, I am. In fact he told him you’d probably be able to wrestle on Thursday. Which is good because Steven called a while ago.” That was unexpected. He probably just wanted to know why you’d left before the show ended. That was a big no-no with Steven. “And?” “Well, he wanted to check on you, and see if you’d be ready for your match next week. Said congratulations and figured you might want a little payback, so you’ve got Hanson at Worldwide.” Oh joy, Steven’s sick sense of humor at work. Put you in a match with the guy who put you in the hospital. Oh well, doesn’t matter, if Alex is right, and you can wrestle at Worldwide, you’d gladly face Hanson and twenty other guys his size. You were going to get to the top of the world damn it. Winning Dangerous Games was just the first step, and you were ready to take the next. Still one question prayed on your mind, you had to know who you were facing. Even as you think of this, and as excited as you are, you start to feel yourself drift off to sleep again. Pain medication at work. “Alex, I gotta sleep again, just one question. Dread or Knight?” You never hear the answer. ~Fin~
