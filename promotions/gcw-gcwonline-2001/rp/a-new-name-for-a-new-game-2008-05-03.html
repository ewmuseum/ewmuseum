A new name for a new game!

3 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/3/08 Sitting at a computer desk several Rock Superstar energy drinks around him is the GCW's mammoth of a man named Shaman. He seems engrossed in something detailed. The camera pans around and shows him playing the popular game World of Warcraft. He is hitting buttons randomly and smiling as he kills something he then stops and groans. "NOOOO Killed by a damn gnome." Shaman grins as he is resurrected by someone and then moves onwards and smiles as he sees a person named Clyde Fox in game and looks like an undead warrior with a pet named one eyed willy. Oh wait for the politically correct folks sleepy willy. Anyways back to it Shaman is stalking this character named Clyde Fox and suddenly hacks him down like a rabid animal. Shaman causes his Mighty Tauren to do a little dance on top of the corpse and walks away off into the terrokar forest. He suddenly hearths to somewhere. Hey i don't know every name of the place i am just the narrator of Shaman's twisted idea. "This game is sadistically fun except for gnomes. Why in the flying hell do gnomes have to be allowed to be warriors? What are they gonna do have people launch them like midget cannonballs over walls and watch them get tossed back like midget bowling balls? That is some of the stupidest crap i have seen yet." Shaman checks his mail and nods as he reads through a letter from someone wanting to invite him into a guild. "Not interested." He hits the button and deletes the mail and moves back to the game as he coems across a group of Murlocs and attacks them all. Using Thunderclap and several special attacks he downs them hard and fast as he begins to loot the corpses and finds murloc eyes and an axe. "Ok where in the hell does a murloc carry an ax?" Shaman shrugs and moves onwards as he stops and shrugs a bit. He stands up and stretches as he turns and walks towards the nearby window. "Funny thing is i tried Pokemon and i kept getting that dumb ass squirtle. I wanted a damn Charazaur he is brutal and fiery. Then again i laughed as i sent that stupid squirtle into battle and watched him get killed a dozen times over." Shaman rambles on a bit and then stops. "Enough of that lets get to the point of Clyde Fox. Ro SHAM bo, You should have done some better research. Shaman fits better with Don't squeeze the Sharmin, Quit dealing with the SHAM man, The truth is it has been over done a longtime ago. There is a world you haven't been to yet Fox and thats called reality. Put away the video games, the comic books and all of the pleasure toys you steal from your sister. Your going into the ring with me. One bad ass dangerous element you have not yet faced in the ring." Shaman shrugs. "Truth is why in the hell should i even bother addressing a person who cannot even be serious in the ring or outside. It is like having Corky as an opponent. I should toss you a rubber ball in the corner and let you be amused for a few hours." Shaman frowns and looks around the room and then sits back at his chair and plays again. "Kind of ironic i am playign this game. I am a Tauren Warrior in this game and when all is said and done i can shut it off and move on or stay in the game and crush puny little gnomes with pink hair calling themselves Clyde Fox. Worst part is every time your crush one another pops up like a damn cockroach. Go figure you can't win for trying here." Shaman moves his character over to the side of a bridge and then hearths the character to Shattrah city. He walks around checking out alliance character and laughs a bit as someone challenges him to a duel. He ignores them and mvoes on towards the target he wants. He sees a group of gnomes and charges in attacking the one who is PVP flagged and watches as the gnome dies in a quick flurry of ax blows and laughs as some of the others come to attack as he stomps and returns the flurry with vengeance upon them. "Frustrating to be crushed like little ants at a barbecue. I will enjoy this because it is relaxing at home, but when it comes to you Clyde your boss better find someone who can help you. I am going to hurt you and bring you to your knees hard and fast. Awww did i hurt your little feelings. Good because i wanted you to realize it was i who was coming after you and will bring you down off the little pedestal you put yourself on hero boy." Shaman grins as he logs out of the World of Warcraft realm and shuts down the computer. "I guess you can be the big bad mighty monster slayer, but the truth is little mighty warrior of Pokemon fame. Your not going to slay the mighty giant with a rock or jokes. Your going to have to fight for your life and fight with every little bit of energy and being you have in you. This is a war, nothing less nothing more. If you think you will stand a chance against me you have another thing coming funny man." Shaman walks out of the room and down a hall and swings two doors open into a gym as he walks over to the punching bag. He begins swing at the large one with a left then followed by a right and the combination follows a very detailed series. "One, two, three, it is every thing in your eyes Fox. A series of moves and a series of tactics foiled one by one. One after another failure will fall until in the end win or lose i still stand triumphant. The hero doesn't always win the way he wants. History is going to come and haunt you little man and when it does i will be laughing all the way to the bank. Your problem is Caldera has a sick sense of humor. He doesn't like me and you are just a tool to him. What little trick does he have? Why has he placed you against me?" Shaman slams a right hand into the bag hard as it rocks as he turns and looks at a picture on the wall of Bryan Mayhem. He leers at it as his anger deepens. "Go figure ye ol silent mime over here is running around with a chip on his shoulder thinking i forgot about him. It is the other way around Mayhem. You abandoned me to go about your own little crusade of woe is me. I lost my momma and now since i lost to Valentine and Ambrosi i am going to turn my back on the guy who i could only trust. Guess what Mayhem this is war and nothing and i mean nothing is going to stop me from coming after your painted face with clenched fists." Shaman looks at the punching bag and grins. "Wake up!" Shaman takes a punch at it. "Open your eyes!" Shaman swings another hard punch. "Take a deep breath!" Shaman unloads a series of punches into the punching bag. "Thats right bitches it is me you see!" Shaman turns and grabs a metal pipe and swings it busting the bag open as sand spills out onto the floor and he grins. "Any questions?"
