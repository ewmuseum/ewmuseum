Interview at HQ

26 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th Aug 2005 Sally Ford is walking along the GCW Headquarters corridors with her GCW mic in hand, when she spots Angel walking away in the distance with his rucksack over his right shoulder. She starts running and gestures for the camera to follow her. She manages to catch up with Angel. Sally: “Angel! Angel! Can we have a quick word with you?” Angel turns around and looks slightly annoyed at the interruption. Angel: “Have some respect. You’re not at a show, this is GCW Headquarters, you don’t run around with a camera and a mic.” Sally: “But it’s the best way to get interviews with the superstars” Angel glances at his watch in a bored manner. Angel: “Okay, you got two minutes.” (Sally turns around to the camera and straightens her top to prepare herself) Sally: “I’m here with one of GCW’s latest signings, Angel, who debuted last week on WorldWide.” (She turns around to face Angel) Sally: “Angel, you’ve been around for a little while, what are your thoughts and feelings about being on the GCW roster?” Angel: “It feels great to be here. I get to travel all over the world, doing what I love to do and that’s wrestling. Also, the entire world is watching me and witnessing my talents. I’m selling loads of merchandise, fan sites all over the Internet are popping up faster than anything, and I’m constantly being stopped in the street for autographs.” (Angel smiles to himself at the recent memories. He’s probably reminiscing about a total fantasy rather than real life.) Angel: “Guess what everyone’s talking about when they discuss GCW?” Sally: “I’m guessing the upcoming Annihilate pay-per-view” So, the excellent match for the GCW World title between LeStatt and Stevens? The dual title ladder match? Or how about the Octavian and Mike Hardy?” (Angel looks at Sally in disbelief) Angel: “No! I mean that has got some people talking, but the big news is that Angel has arrived.” (Sally looks confused in a “is-this-guy-serious?” way). Sally: “Have you changed any of your tactics since loosing your debut match?” (Angel’s smile turns into a scowl as he looks at Ford) Angel: “O’Conner got lucky. I mean, did you see how fast the referee counted?” (Sally’s face is blank) Sally: “I err ? I watched the match and thought the ref’s count was perfectly fair.” (Angel raises his flat hand with his palm up right in front of Ford’s face and slaps that hand quickly three times with his other hand. He does it again and again.) Angel: “That was the same as the ref’s count. Do you think that was fair” I certainly don’t.” (Angel’s scowl turns into a smile again as Ford looks on with a worried look on her face) Angel: “But that doesn’t matter, it’s in the past. The past doesn’t matter, it’s the future that matters. I am GCW’s future and I matter. Some people will beat me, I’ll beat some people, that’s the way it works. Only a fool goes out to win every match, and do I look like a fool” Do I?!” Sally: “Err ? no ? I don’t think so ? no.” Angel: “Well done, ten points to team “blonde and stupid”! You’re right, I’m not a fool. I will loose matches, but more importantly, I’ll win matches and I’ll be firmly on my way up the ladder of greatness. Win or loose, I’m on the road for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship.” Sally: “Is that what you're aiming for” The GCW World Championship?” (Angel looks at Ford in surprise and snatches the mic off her) Angel: “If we take a look inside your head what would we find” An “I Owe You” note? Of course I’m aiming for the World Championship! Everyone in this company is gunning for it. I not saying I’m going to get it tomorrow or next month, but somewhere down the line, that World title will be mine. I know I’m great, and having that World title will let everyone know how great I truly am. Now if you’ll excuse me, I’ve got places I have to be.” (Angel offers the mic back, but as Sally goes to take it, Angel drops it on the floor, smiles, and walks off. She picks the mic up and gives him the evil look.)
