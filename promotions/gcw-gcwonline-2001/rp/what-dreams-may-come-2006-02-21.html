What dreams may come?

21 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/21/06 Dreams are the window to our mind's desires and fears. When we dream we go into a part of our mind that we close off during our waking hours. It can be a dark place full of fears, or it can be a place full of hopes. Some times it is both. What we don't want to say or think about. For some the dreams tell a story of past, present, or future. Danielle Northsky's life changed with the coming of strange dreams of her ancestors. And now she is a throw back to those dreams. Still not fully under control of her 'other' side. To this day a few months later, she works on trying to get that control. Sitting in her room, with her journal open thumbing though some of the past few months of scribbles, she comes to a page and stops... On one side, not in her hand writing, is drawn the symbol of a man she's yet to meet. Well not really a man. It's a form of writing called Angelic Script, and it's the written name of her 6th Great Grandfather, Kokabel. Her people called him the Morning Star. Next to this sigil, for lack of a better term, are but only a few words: ?Happy Re-Birthday, Sirius Star.? Re-Birthday... She was born on August 2nd, but on that day this entry is for is for September 11, 2005. The date of her first manifestation in the desert of Nevada. The date that changed her life for ever. She date that she found out just what it is that she truly is. She sets the book down. Only one person she knows could of drawn that symbol like that. She turns and looks at the sleeping form of Joshua Brand, her lover, her protector, her confidant. She would have to speak to him in the morning about it. He'd been the one out in the desert with her, that got her through that night of her rebirth as an immortal angel, of the same 'choir' that Morning Star apparently came from; the Virtues. She wasn't going to wake him over something so simple. As she watched him, he pulled the covers a little over his face and turned his back to the light, She took that as a hint. Reaching up and turning it off, she decided that going to the living room to do her nightly journal entry would be a better idea. Grabbing her quill pen and ink bottle, she quietly makes her way out of the room, As she closes the door she realizes she has stepped out of her room and into an open field. Not just any open field, but the fields that grace her homeland, the Pine Hills Reservation in South Dakota. Home of her band of Sioux Native Americans. Turning back to her bedroom door... it's not there. All there is, are the Mountains of the Black hills behind her. Her home gone, she frantically looks around for clues as to what's going on. Grass, a breeze, the trees, snowcapped mountains... Nothing out of place. But, then, in the distance; a lone figure standing. She strains her eyes to try to make it out. However, she's compelled to move closer. She takes a few steps, but things don't seem to change, so she takes a few more. More and more, and it appears that she's not getting any closer. So she starts to run in the direction of the man. As she does, her fears, or are they hopes are realized as a pair of white wings, with ashen gray spots upon them, fold out from his back. She starts to try to hurry more, but she can't seem to get anywhere. She's not able to close the gab between them no matter how hard she tries. Then, a strange sensation, that of weightlessness. She looks down and realizes that she's no longer running. She's flying. Somehow her own wings, negative images of his with their ashen gray base and white highlights like a mountain thrush, have folded out and she's trying to fly towards him. But still she can't get any closer. Her stomach lurches, and churns and she fights back the urge to do as she normally would in this type of situation... Puke her guts out. Her great Grandfather's necklace, given to her through Joshua as a protection device, almost seems to pull her towards the figure standing there. She begins to make out more details and she can see his weathered face as he's smiling. A glow not unlike the setting sun looks to be emanating from him. Still she tries to get closer, the urge to push her body faster. The beating of her wings grows stronger, harder, faster. As she strives, her heart matches the beat, her body glistening with droplets of sweat, hair sticking to her back. As she reaches out to him she calls out ?Why are you here? What do you want?? **THUMP** It all goes black for a moment. Danielle's eyes fly open. Looking around she realizes she is on the floor of her bed room, next to her bed. She peers up and sees the face of her lover looking at her with a ?deer in a headlights? look. He'd just been awoken by the sound of her backside hitting the floor. He shows his concern by asking the obvious question. ?You Okay?? Danielle jumps up and looks around. She is in her room again. She goes and slowly opens her bed room door. She makes sure that when she looks out, she's looking at their simple living room in the Ryokan (Japanese Inn) room that they're using. Unlike Blaise and Ukyo, they don't use the traditional bamboo sleeping mats, but one of the more western beds. ?Dani?? Danielle turns with a wild eye look on her face. Her body covered with sweat... ?I'm fine, go back to sleep.? Josh looks at her with one eye brow raised doing his worst Vulcan impersonation. ?You forget what I am. You're anxious, worried and scared all at the same time.? Damnit, when will he ever learn to turn that off. Josh's empathic senses were always keeping her from hiding things from him. ?It was just a dream that's all. You forget that I have many of them.? At the same time she said that a knock came to the door. Danielle curled her nose a little. Strong cologne, Drakar Noir to be exact. Josh's brother used it to hide the scent he would normally give off. ?Yes Gray?? The door opens and in walks the person she thought it was, paper walls don't exactly hide his sometimes overpowering use of the cover scent. ?You Okay? I though I heard someone fall to the floor in here. And from how muffled the sound was, it wasn't boney butt Brand, but someone with some padding back there.? Dani frowned, but she knew why he'd taken that shot. Dani didn't sleep in much, usually a teddy or thong and a t-shirt. It all depended on her's and Josh's plans for the evening. Gray had taken to pointing out when she wasn't properly dressed to have guests a long time ago. She chose to ignore the comment. ?Yeah Old Gray Muzzle, everyone is fine. I just had a dream that's all.? He returned to her right away. ?One that knocked you on your ass, interesting.? Gray frowned at Danielle ?The last time you started to have dreams, you manifested. What type of dream?? She wasn't ready to be interrogated tonight. ?It wasn't anything big. It was more like a walk, then anything. Almost a memory but not a memory.? At that statement another voice rings out from behind Gray. ?In that case, maybe we should talk.? Danielle glared at him. It was Blaise, their glorious leader that had been assigned to this job by their sponsors. ?Maybe someone should stop sticking his nose where it doesn't belong, especially when it's not their business.? Blaise looks at her with a smile, his emerald green eyes sparkle at the challenge. In his own monotone way, he answers it. ?You are my business, or have you forgotten that part? I'm assigned to his mission, which is all about you.? Dani turns around and walks back to her bed. ?Can't any talking wait until morning? It's late, or early, depending on if you're part wolf or not.? Gray turns and looks at him and shakes his head. ?You should talk now, you could always sleep in a little later. You should always speak of these things when they're still vivid in your mind.? Dani shook her head. ?It was only a Dream Walk. That's all. A little home sickness.? Blaise hits her low with what he was holding onto. ?Then who did you yell out to?? Danielle turns around a slightly wild look in her eyes. ?Yell?? ?Yes, you yelled out to someone. Even I could hear you in over in my shack, on that one.? Dani hesitated, and that gave Blaise the hint he needed ?Well... It was no one special, just Grandpa, thats all. He's the one I miss the most. Especially with the fact that he's never taken well during the colder months of the year.? She tried to play it off by looking down at the ground. She looked towards Josh, she knew he'd sense her little bending of the truth there. He remained silent, knowing he'd only get in trouble with her if he said anything. He just looked up at his brother, showing no sign of anything. ?Okay, Blaise. Let's go take care of this meeting then. We'll leave her alone.? Blaise gives her a wink and then turns. But before he leaves, he makes a comment loud enough for her to hear. ?I swear, I'm going to see if Annie can get us an Illithid assigned for times like this. I'd just have to figure out how to hide it.? He then walked out, with Gray following shortly behind him. Dani just gave a look towards Josh with a smile on her face. Josh grinned back. ?You owe me one, you know that.? She smiled as she climbed back into bed. ?Well we are awake, aren't we?? As the two embraced though, outside Blaise looked on towards their little hut. Gray had kept going on a bit down the path. Blaise smiled as he stared at the hut though, his emerald eyes taking on a fire of their own as he brought forth a side of himself that Dani had yet to fully see. She knew of the wolf that was inside of him, but his other side, the McClancy side was something else. As he shifted into his Faery aspect, he could see the residue of a thin, silver astral cord as it broke away from where Dani's area would be, and faded into nothingness. ?I'm going to catch you, and then it's time for some answers...? ~TBC~
