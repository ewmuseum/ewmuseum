Playful Things

15 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15 December 2009 I. "Will you stop pacing around? You're making me dizzy." LeStatt has been watching his other half, Roberto, walk in circles for the better part of an hour. Up until this point he had decided to remain silent and just be an observer. Knight knew exactly what was bothering Roberto, being a figment of Roberto's imagination gave him incredible insight like that. Roberto's icy blue glare only momentarily moves from a spot three inches in front of him on the floor to meet Knight's black eyes. Those icy blues are back to the floor before he responds to LK. "I don't know what the hell to do," The tone in his voice irks Knight, like a nagging child or a bitchy wife. It makes him want to punch someone in the head. "Things are NOT going according to plan." "Well you're right about that." The two go silent again, Roberto returns to his pacing and Knight continues to be consistently annoyed. LeStatt isn't one to respond well to failure . and he especially hates it when Roberto falters, quitting before the fight has even begun. What he never understands is why Roberto has continually fought for control over his life and to be rid of him. It's not like that asshole has ever been able to do anything without him. Finally Roberto stops pacing and focuses on Knight. "Well?" "Well what?" "Are you going to fucking help me or not? I don't need you to just sit there and stare me down." Maybe this shouldn't irritate Knight so much, every issue that Roberto cannot solve himself brings Knight closer to absolute power of him. It's something he hasn't had in a long, long time. He even let Roberto lock him away before, a moment of complete embarrassment no doubt. "Of course," Knight pushes himself off the wall where he has been leaning. "I cannot let you go down in flames like this. It looks bad on our reputation." "It's MY reputation." Roberto's words ooze with anxiety, irritation and a little bit of venom. LeStatt laughs out loud. "Yeah? Which name is it that's on the programs? On the bio page of the GCW website? In every article ever written about 'YOUR' career? I'm pretty fucking sure it's LeStatt Knight. I'm not even sure anyone outside of Mary knows your real goddamn name." Roberto takes in a deep breath. Anger obviously fills him up but with no real comeback to fall back on, he buries it the best he can. Knight doesn't smile outwardly at this but it does warm the giant piece of ice that he calls a heart just a little bit. "Look," Roberto hesitates, trying to decide what to say. "What do we do about Fox?" The idea that Roberto could be having such a difficult time with this child trapped in a man's body is particularly disturbing to LeStatt. How could anyone be so weak? So inept at what they do for a living that they can continually be bested, shown up by and completely and utterly embarrassed by such a person? THIS is the man who will forever be remembered in GCW lore as the greatest to ever step into the ring? Pathetic. "Clyde Fox." The words come from Knight's mouth with as much disgust as is humanly possible. "I say we fucking choke him with a Power Ranger.” “Be serious.” “I am.” Knight folds his arms across his chest. “But it’s not a plausible idea, I know.” “Then what else is there?” “We’ve never faced an opponent like this before Roberto, not even close. Typically, our opponents have a grasp on reality and we smash that reality into little pieces for them to choke on.” “I understand this.” “But Clyde’s reality is already broken. He’s already hiding from the real world, already sees it as some dark, intimidating force that he cannot overcome.” Knight unfolds his arms and moves close to Roberto, his black eyes becoming more intense. “Neither of us can ever fully understand this person, it’s not in us.” Frustration settles in with Roberto and he throws his hands into the air. “Then what the fuck do we do?” LeStatt studies Roberto, taking in his mannerisms and his anger … his frustration and his fear. After a few long moments, Knight nods his head and puts a hand on Roberto’s shoulder. “You have to do something that you’ve never done before,” A smile forms on Knight’s face. “You have to embrace a darkness in you that has been hidden away for quite some time.” Roberto’s face distorts, his eyes narrow and he pushes Knight’s arm away from his shoulder. “Fuck you Knight, you’re not getting full control.” “That’s not what I’m talking about.” Intrigue takes over. “Really?” Knight’s grin fades, replaced by a stern look. “There’s a very primal piece of you buried deep inside. This animal side of yours will have to be unleashed for you to beat Clyde Fox.” Now it’s Roberto’s turn to study his Dark Persona standing in front of him. They have been working ‘together’ for quite some time now but Roberto knows never to fully trust him. “Why?” “You have to lose control. Clyde cannot be manipulated because he doesn’t even understand what the concept means. To battle someone who you cannot predict, you yourself has to be unpredictable. You have to be savage … you have to be a beast.” “But I thought that WAS you…” Knight pauses and clears his throat, Roberto even considers the possibility that he even recognizes a bit of fear flash through Knight’s body. “So did I.” Knight responds, disappointment evident. Roberto considers this and although the decision should not have come so quickly, he answers. “Show me.” II. Roberto would never be capable of explaining how they got to where they are. It’s as if their previous setting just melted away, exposing their true surroundings. It’s a fairly simple set-up. Concrete floors, hanging lights that just shine light on bits and pieces of the room, worn down walls with plaster and paint barely hanging on and straight ahead there is a steel door. “Are you ready?” Knight’s deep voice startles Roberto, causing his heart rate to elevate. “Are you?” Roberto merely nods his head. Knight swallows hard and locks his black eyes onto the large steel door that is but a few feet in front of him. He wasn’t adjusted to feeling things like nervousness or maybe even … fear? Knight shakes his head, trying to rid himself of the thought. His hand grasps the steel door’s handle and he slowly pulls it open. The room is suddenly full of squeaks and groans as the door seems to have been shut for a very long time. Roberto looks inside, startled at what he sees. “There’s nothing in there!” Just as he finishes the phrase a low growl escapes the darkness behind the door that sends a chill up and down Roberto’s spine. Roberto’s eyes go wide as he looks inside again and to his amazement there are a pair of eyes staring back at him. One eye, the left eye, is black with a light grey outline .. Very unique. The other is not very unique but very amazing in it’s own right: bright, bright blue … even brighter than his own. These eyes seem to belong to some sort of Beast within the darkness. “What the fuck is that?” “It’s what will set you free,” Knight’s eyes remain locked on to the eyes in the dark. “It’s what will take you over.” The Beast appears to walk on four legs, perhaps even having a tail. It’s deep, penetrating growl continues to echo through Roberto’s ears although he isn’t even sure if it is continuing to make a sound. There is nothing but eyes and shadows moving in a blur but still Roberto forms an image of this monster in his mind … and it frightens him. “I-I don’t think I-I can do this…” Roberto’s voice is more whisper than anything. This causes Knight to break from his own trance. “The fuck you can’t!” Knight roars. “You have no choice! This MUST happen!” “I CAN’T!” Roberto attempts to turn and run but Knight is too quick and too powerful for him and kicks Roberto’s legs right out from under him. LeStatt towers over his fallen self and places a boot across his chest. “It’s time to stop getting fucking embarrassed and step on Clyde’s throat! I will not stand by as you tarnish everything I’ve worked for!” He points towards the beast’s lair. “You will see what it takes to win this battle.” Knight turns his head and gazes into the blackness… “COME!” The blue eye of the beast brightens and the black eye seems to dull down. The Beast walks closer towards the light, towards freedom. Roberto’s heart pounds inside his chest, he’s sure Knight can hear it crashing into his skin. The Beast hesitates momentarily as it reaches the doorway, it’s incredible set of eyes looking all around it, perhaps not believing that it can leave. But it takes one step out …then another… Knight reaches down and pulls Roberto up by his hair but only to his knees. He then thrusts Roberto forward causing him to land on his hands and knees. The beast approaches, he’s close now and Roberto knows it. He can feel the Beast’s hot breath on his face. As it gets just a step from revealing itself, the Beast roars … the sound almost deafening. Then it steps out into the light. The roar stops. The Beast is revealed. Roberto slowly looks up to meet IT face to face… “Say hello,” Knight’s voice calls out in a very quiet tone. This Beast, this thing that had the power to even make LeStatt Knight nervous. This thing that could unleash the animal inside of him … IT was HIM … As a child. The Beast takes Roberto’s chin in his hand, cocking his head to the side as he does it. A smile spreads across the Beast’s face, a child’s laugh escaping it’s throat … but Roberto doesn’t feel warmth from hearing this child’s laugh. Fear engulfs him instead. “Hi Woberto” He laughs again, no less chilling than the first time. “I’ve been waitin’ an awful wong time for someone to play wif me.” It’s face quickly goes blank, those eyes digging into Roberto’s soul. “You’ll play wif me … WIGHT?” Roberto doesn’t answer, he doesn’t need to. Knight’s last words are barely audible. “Let’s make a play date.”
