Or so he will tell you

12 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/12/11 The life and times of a millionaire are tiresome, sometimes lonely and often fast paced, in fact, any millionaire, especially the man who calls himself ‘The Idol’ will tell you that when you have unlimited amounts of money, the whole ‘he can do anything he wants’ stereotypical view is misleading and on the most part un true. In short; the more money you have, the more pieces people want of you. Everyone wants a piece of The Idol (or so he will tell you). From his time as a fan favourite in the WWA, short stint as a professional boxer, cameo roles in movies and chat show appearances he has always loved being the centre of attention and he has always loved manipulating people in to doing what he wants, when he wants and how he wants; whether that means using money as a bribe or just plain old ‘reasonable’ force he has certainly every right to saunter around this world with the cocky, arrogant swagger that would make Kid Rock look like a scolded puppy on any given day of the week. The Idol deserves his stature as a ‘class act’, (or so he will tell you). The large, mahogany desk inside Mr Idol’s Los Angeles office is covered with various documents, hardly in any organised format as a half burnt through cigar rests on a silver plated ashtray, pushing smoke up towards the square tiled ceiling. A diamond encrusted nameplate sits at the front of the desk with the words ‘Mr Idol’ perfectly chiselled and spread evenly across the plate. As Mr Idol stands at the window staring out towards the night sky, the door to his office opens and a young, petite, brunette haired woman enters the room, wearing a skimpy, black number and carrying two glasses of Scotch whisky, looking convincingly attracted and flirtatious towards him; “This is the last of the Scotch, hope you don’t mind?” The rolling of his eyes and uninterested manner reveal that he probably does not mind in the slightest “Why would I mind? It’s not as if I’m celebrating anything.” Taken aback by his abruptness she moves in to gain his affection, puckering her lips to gently kiss the back of his neck and rubbing her hands over his shoulders; “What’s wrong baby? You seem tense tonight” “So what if I am?” He sighed, moving away and sitting down at his desk before picking up the almost finished cigar and taking a drag. “They call me arrogant; they call me deceitful and cocky, something that this GCW haven’t witnessed yet.” The woman moves in again, looking for affection she kneels in front of him, running her hands up his thighs before resting her head in his lap. “So that’s what it is? This new contract? This GCW business? You have been through all of this before, why does it still worry you?” A bellowing, if a little exaggerated laugh releases from his body as he stands up leaving the woman on the floor, on her knees, looking helpless, if a little embarrassed; she stands up and dusts herself down as he paces around the room, laughing towards the ceiling and shaking his head with disbelief. “Worried? Why the hell would I be worried? Am I ever worried?” His voice raises as the excitement wells up inside him, just the sheer mention of the GCW and his new path in the wrestling business has got his blood boiling more so than Hannibal Lector after a car crash. The woman leans back on to the desk, her skirt rides up slightly as she places her hands on the desk, trying far too hard to seduce the man before her. “I mean come on, Lizzy. What’s not to love?” “Nothing” She replied in a solid, justified tone that makes her seem even more desperate than ever. Lizzy moves over to him, placing both arms around his shoulders and leaning in to give him a gentle kiss on the lips which, for a couple of seconds at least he accepts before moving away, uninterested again. “Can you leave?” “What? Why? I thought we were going for dinner?” “Well, we’re not; I don’t want to force you to leave so can you just walk your over-sensitive, gullible piece of meat out of my office.” Her face, which was once calm, seductive and soothing, turns to rage, it is a rage that can only be explained by any man who is married or in any kind of relationship. In this instance, her rage leads to a slap across the face to the once, God like figure to her before walking out of the office, slamming the door behind her. Idol reaches for his face, smirking slightly before letting out a sigh. He loosens his peach coloured tie and undoes the top two buttons of his shirt, finally sitting down and sinking comfortably into his black leather couch. He sits there in silence for a few minutes, pondering his situation, smirking and laughing to himself before reaching towards the matching mahogany coffee table in front of him and picking up a dictating machine, he sighs once more before holding down the play and record button at the same time. “The date is, Tuesday the 12th of April, I’m in my office and yet another gold digger has been given the proverbial boot.” He relaxes back into the couch, calm and confident. “A new chapter is approaching in my already over the top life. The GCW is home to many stars from all across the globe but…….no one has ever seen a character such as me.” He leans forward, placing the machine on to the coffee table before standing up and begin to pace around the office. “You see I’ve never actually claimed to be a big shot or anything like that and the simple reason for that is the fact that I already know what I am, I know who I am and I know whole heartedly that I am better than just about everybody at everything I do. That’s just the way it is.” He takes another sip of his Scotch whilst again staring out to the bright lights of Los Angeles. “And before anyone starts piping up and saying things like ‘Oh here we go, yet another bad guy trying to get over by telling us he’s the best in the world blah blah blah’ well I got news for anyone who wants to try me and that includes Jimmy Thunder at Worldwide this week……I can talk the talk AND walk the walk and there isn’t a damn thing that anybody in the GCW or anyone in this recession ridden country can do about it.” He reaches down to press stop on the dictating machine and again sits relaxingly into his couch looking extremely pleased with himself, for this man is going to be a total revelation for the GCW, a revitalising character with wit and charm but certainly no conscience. As he presses ‘play’ on the dictation machine, settling back to listen to his voice, it is obvious that he is the best thing to happen to the GCW since it opened. (Or so he will tell you).
