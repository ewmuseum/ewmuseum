Enmity

8 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/08/10 6:54 A.M. Pacific Heights, San Francisco, California. Triumph’s House. The soothing sounds of Sean Lennon fill the main bedroom, as Triumph Frost leans on the balcony railing in only black boxer-briefs. He puffs away at a cigarette, looking over his massive well-tended private backyard, gazing out towards the sparkling Pacific Ocean. A rare moment of calmness echoes from his face. Alexis Frost, in a red silk flowing robe, wraps her arms around his stomach from behind. She kisses him on the back and moves next to him along the railing. “Morning,” she yawns, as she stretches out her arms. Triumph lets out a pleased grunt. She gives him a once-over. “You seem…” Her pause seems to hang on the air forever, as if trying to fully gauge him. “Happy this morning.” He nods his head twice, quickly, while pulling the Camel up for another drag. Alexis’ eyes rise. “Well, I’ll bite,” she remarks. She waits for something, anything. He only gives her a half-smile. He shuts his eyes and tilts his head downward. The anticipation kills. She shakes him by the shoulder. “What, damn you,” her words infused with skeptical laughter. He brushes the back of his hand along her cheek, as she purrs. He turns to his left and stubs the smoke in a nearby marble ashtray. Triumph grabs Alexis’ wrist, as she faces him. He grabs the other wrist. The half-smile shines through. He leans forward and gives her a peck on the cheek. She beams, the head cheerleader kissed by the starting quarterback. Her voice goes soft. “What is it, babe?” His smile crosses the rest of his face. “Just a good day, babe.” He winks as he lets her go, and walks into the house. She closes her eyes. “Thank God,” she mutters, relieved. 12:25 P.M. Pacific Heights, San Francisco, California. Triumph’s House. In Triumph’s own private gym that would make a 24-Hour Fitness cream in its pants, the walls are adorned with sports-related materials. The walls are littered with framed signed jerseys: Tom Brady; Kobe Bryant; Derek Jeter; Kevin Garnett; Peyton Manning. Across the west wall sprawls a painted mural, a depiction of the iconic imagery of a victorious Muhammad Ali towering over a fallen Sonny Liston, taunting his fallen opponent. From above, one could see the floor of the gym, with Merriam-Webster’s definition of “winner” ripped from the dictionary and splashed upon the ground. Triumph walks towards his bench press, a black towel over his head. Decked out in black Nike gear, he wipes the back of his neck and his face with the towel before tossing it away, and then nestles himself under the 225-pound weights. A shuffling iPod hooked up to speakers all over jumbles through an endless list of songs, finding its way to a DJ Shadow medley. Sitting off to the side on a black stool, Alexis, in black John Stockton-esque adidas shorts, black Chuck Taylor Converse sneaks, and a white baby-doll T sporting Hit Girl from “Kick-Ass”, flips through a magazine on par with Cosmo. Triumph grips on the bar, and begins his session. She reads aloud from the magazine. “Here’s a quiz to know if you’ve found your perfect mate.” She continues on, undeterred, as a look of discomfort washes over him. “Question number one: what kind of television shows does he like to watch? Romantic, comedy, action, drama, or animated?” “Are you…fucking kidding me?” He grunts as he keeps pressing on. “Well,” she queries. “Which ones?” He pushes the bar up. “Good ones.” She stares for a moment, and turns back to the magazine. “Question two: what’s his favorite musician of all time?” “Easy,” he huffs. “Madlib.” He reflects for a moment. “No, wait. DJ Shadow.” He holds his hand up. “Hold up.” He snaps his fingers and smirks. “MF Doom.” “Question three: what’s his favorite movie of all-time?” She watches for his response. “Seven Samurai,” he places the bar back on its holder, and reaches down for a water bottle. She eyes him cautiously. “You sure?” He pauses mid-sip, staring right through her. She looks back down at the magazine. “Question four--” She gets off as much before he interrupts. “How many goddamned questions does this thing have?” He sips from the water bottle and puts it back down, grabbing the towel in its place. She goes through the magazine, a soft count on her lips, and gathers her total. “Twenty-five.” He rolls his eyes back into his head, and lies down on the bench, putting the towel over his head. She gets right back into it, and peers up from the book. “Would he kill for you?” He rises off the bench slowly, the towel falling onto his stomach. He tilts his head cock-eyed towards her. “That is not in that magazine.” Her smile is perplexing. “Isn’t it?” He gets off the bench and goes after her, as she playfully keeps the magazine out of reach. “Gimme!” He tries to snatch it, but she holds it away. “It’s not yours to take,” she giggles as she keeps up the game. “Like Hell,” his own amusement leaks out. She bursts out in amusement as he tickles his way to victory. He finally wrestles it from her grasp. He looks it over as he paces around, rag in hand. He stops, and pirouettes towards her. “Oh, Christ,” he mutters, shaking his head in disbelief. “Question fourteen: does he spend more time in front of the mirror than you do?” He tosses the magazine back to her. “Who really believes in this shit?” “They put a lot of research into this,” she counters. “Doctors and experts and scientists and all those types, you know?” Triumph scoffs, taking another sip from the water bottle. “Scientologists, maybe.” Alexis rolls her eyes. “Just give it another shot.” She tosses the magazine back to him. He quickly flips through it, getting to the quiz. He clears his throat, a bit of excitement in the air. “Alright. Question number seventeen: what do…” The excitement dies. His voice grows softer. “His parents…think of you…” He lowers his head. Alexis, aghast, tries to say something, but words escape her. “I-I’m…” The two sit silently. The awkwardness builds. He laughs, breaking the tension, and tosses the magazine down. He keeps his head down. “My mom woulda liked you, I think.” Alexis goes over to the bench and rests on the bench next to him, comforting him. He turns to her. “Dad woulda thought you were a money-hungry bitch, though.” “You don’t really mention him much,” she tells him. Triumph shrugs. “Didn’t really know him enough to.” Together, they sit amongst the music. The tension snaps when she slaps her hand on his leg. “Okay,” she declares, as she rises up. “I’m going to go shopping.” She kisses him on the cheek. “Don’t pull anything.” With that, she sashays out of the room. Triumph resides with his thoughts. He reaches into his pocket, and pulls out a wrinkled folded photograph. It’s of him as a baby, being held by his mother and father. He loses himself in the image for a moment, before sighing, folding it back up, and putting it away. He lowers himself under the bar, and rattles off another set. 3:24 P.M. Pacific Heights, San Francisco, California. Triumph’s House. Alexis walks through the main white double doors, carrying bags from Cartier, Prada, and Bulgari. She tosses them down along the bottom steps of the massive staircase leading upstairs. As she maneuvers her way through the labyrinth that is Triumph’s house, she walks towards the gym. She tilts her sunglasses downward. “Hey, babe, I’m…” The gym resides, unattended. “Back.” She pushes her sunglasses back upwards. In the living room, on a long black couch, Triumph lays out, a Sierra Nevada in one hand, and the other in a bag of chips. A plasma screen television plays Pulp Fiction at a loud volume. “Does he look like a bitch?” Jules Winnfield asks from inside the screen. Alexis stands at the entryway, hands on her hips. “I thought you were going to be training all day.” Triumph fills his face with potato skins, chewing them loudly and washing it down with a sip from the beer. She waits impatiently. “Well?” “Well,” he states, as he sips from the beer again, letting out a relieved sigh afterwards. “I was looking at por---err, researching stuff online, right? For the match or fight or whatever.” Her eyes narrow. Her voice bleeds with disbelief. “And?” He grabs another handful and stuffs it in his mouth. He chews and chews and chews, finally swallowing it down. “So, this guy, uhh…I forget his name, but that’s not important, right? What is important, is that this guy is a cross-dressing Helen Keller motherfucker, so I figure that I could get a parasite, lose half my body weight, end up lookin’ like sickly Babe Ruth, and I could still get this done without really trying. So…” He finishes down the beer, and burps afterwards. “I’m done for the day.” Alexis groans. “Rasa’s just mute,” she responds. “Helen Keller was deaf and mute.” “Well, gee,” Triumph’s voice reeks of hyperbole. “Thank you so very fucking much, Professor High School.” Alexis finally has had enough and rushes forward, finger out. “You know, this is your problem, Tri!” She moves in the way of the television, as he bobs around her to keep watching. “You don’t take anyone seriously. You don’t give a shit about anything. This is how you get your ass beat.” A chill fills the room. Triumph picks up the remote and turns off the television. He rises, as she continually looks up at his growing stature. He is Godzilla, emerging from the ocean, towering over frightened Japanese scientists. She gulps as he leans in. “Get…my ass…” His pause draws out for effect. His eyes narrow, his brow furrowing. “Beat?” The word hangs in the air, as Triumph stomps to her right, as she jumps. “Nobody’s gonna beat my ass, got it?” He turns towards her, pointing right at her. “You think this motherfucker’s gonna beat me?!?” She can’t pull herself together. It’s too late, anyway. He rushes back towards her, pounding his finger into his chest. “No one’s gonna fuckin’ beat me! No one! Got it?!?” He slashes by her, nearly knocking her down, as he plops down on the couch. He grabs the remote, turning the television back on. Alexis slightly trembles. “Now go make me a sandwich, woman.” Alexis lights up like a pinball machine. The swoosh of air comes across, and then… SMACK! A long, swift cuffing of Triumph’s ear, as he falls to his side on the couch. He cups his ear, holding it, and glares at her. “Owwww!” She gets back in his face, her finger out to his eyes. “Don’t you ever talk to me like that, you immature little shit!” Triumph starts cracking up. “Take a joke, yeah?” She’s all business, but he grabs her around the waist and pulls her down to the couch with him. Face to face, he moves his mouth to her ear. “I’ve got this covered. This guy, he’s nothing. Not even a speck on the radar. I’ve got it all figured out, and I’m gonna handle it. But you gotta trust me on this. We gotta be in this together, you know that. All the way, or not at all.” “I just—“ Triumph kisses her on the mouth, and she stops. “All the way.” She nods quickly, and they passionately kiss on the couch. “We should be fuckin’ dead now, my friend!” Jules Winnfield hollers out. Triumph pulls his head up. “I love this fuckin’ part!” He springs up, enamored with the scene, as she falls off the couch and slams to the ground with an “Oof!” 7:24 P.M. Los Angeles, California. Staples Center. The Los Angeles Clippers do battle with the New York Knicks on the floor of the Staples Center. At prime courtside seats, Triumph and Alexis watch the game. Triumph, in a salmon designer dress shirt, dress jacket, faded jeans and white Nike sneakers, holds a plastic cup full of twelve-dollar piss-water beer in his hand. Alexis, clad in blue jeans, a black tank top, and black Gucci heels, fidgets in her seat. Triumph tilts his head slightly towards her. “What’s up, babe?” “I hate the Clippers.” She tenses up in her chair. “I know,” he responds. “I mean,” she blurts out, her fingernails digging into the armrests. “I really hate the Clippers!” “I know,” he returns again, this time laughing. She turns fully towards him. “Why are we watching the Clippers?” “I’m not watching the Clippers,” he tells her, his eyes following every stitch of action. “I thought you were a Celtics fan,” she huffs. “Yeah.” He takes a sip from his beer. “But I figured we’re in town, so I wanted to watch LeBron James’ team play.” Confusion washes over her face. She halts for a moment. “He doesn’t play for the Knicks.” Triumph’s brow furrows, intently watching the action. The gears turn in his head, a megalomaniac focused on world domination. “Yet,” he spits out, a sly grin crossing his face. The action gets closer and closer. Suddenly, out of nowhere, the play meets the front row, as Knick David Lee jumps between the two, crashing into the seats behind them, going for a loose ball and barely missing tossing it back onto the court. As he dusts himself off and walks back to the court, his eyes meet Alexis’, and he shoots her a smile and a wink, as she giggles and blushes. Triumph sits, dumbfounded. “What,” he struggles for a moment for words. “The fuck. Was that?” She stays fixated on the power forward, before breaking her trance. “Whuh?” He cackles noticeably. “What?” She pokes and prods as he continues his chuckle. “What’s wrong with that?” “Nothing,” he spits as he calms down. “My wife got hit on by David Lee.” She gets in a huff. “Please! You remember at the Standard? You had those three girls hanging on every word. And don’t think I didn’t see you grab that brunette bitch’s ass, ‘cause I fully well did!” Triumph begins to roar with hysterics again, as Alexis crosses her arms. As his amusement dies down, she pouts. “Let’s talk about something else, please!” “Alright,” he responds. “Whatever you want.” She stops him dead. “How about Rasa?” He sips from the beer, nearly choking on it. “Who?” She rolls her eyes. “You know, your opponent. The...” She lets out an irritated soft grunt as she speaks. “The ‘creepy tranny guy.’” Her slight distractions begin to annoy him. “Why do you care so much about that guy, anyway?” “I don't. All I was thinking was maybe we could discuss a little strategy.” She leans towards him. “I know that you said you have it covered, but I’d like to know what’s going on. I mean, you said we’re in this together.” “I did,” he quickly returns. “So,” she tells him. “I’d like to be in on the game, if you don’t mind.” Triumph grimaces. “I’d much rather watch this game.” Her head falls backwards and she moans. She looks up at the lights shining high above the area before returning upright. “Then what’re you gonna do? Just walk around the ring, gauge the guy for awhile, wait until you’ve figured him out, and finish him off?” Triumph downs the brew and ‘aaaahs’ afterwards. “Sounds about right.” She grows uncomfortable. “I just think—“ Triumph cuts her off quickly. “Honey, I love you.” He puts his hand on her shoulder. “But, please, shut the fuck up. I really wanna watch the game.” Her mouth is agape as he goes back to his focus on the court. They seem worlds apart… …Until he leans in and kisses her on the cheek. He gets closer, and whispers into her ear. “Trust.” Their eyes meet for a long moment. She nods approvingly. The game begins to pick up intensity once again.
