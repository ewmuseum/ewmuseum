Stockholm Syndrome

23 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 23, 2008 The day before GCW's Japan Tour... Barnes & Noble Bookstore Portland, Oregon A gathering of people herd like cattle towards a small display in the back of the room. The combination of small conversations upon thousands of people turn into a chaotic sound, disturbing the employees and day-to-day patrons of the Barnes & Noble Bookstore, many of whom can't help but stare at the single-file line that wraps around the massive retailer and outside the door. Together they wait patiently for an opportunity to share the same air with what many of them consider to be greatness. As the door in back of the store opens slowly and two security guard come out, the chaotic sound turns into a unified roar of approval. Following out security was a tiny woman, her flowing blonde locks and charismatic cerulean eyes capture everyone within eyesight of The Fearless Phenom. Wearing a Scarling band shirt and a pair of slightly baggy blue jeans, she holds the GCW World Heavyweight Championship in the air behind the desk as lights flicker all around her from media to fans alike. The barrage of lights flash off of the golden plate on the front of the belt as she lowers it and takes a seat behind the bench. "WE ARE THE IMAGE OF THE INVISIBLE!" The anxious crowd begins to shout in unison, forcing many employees to plug their ears in annoyance while many regular customers begin to make their way out of the store in frustration. Leaning down into her ear one of the security guards attempts to ask a question over the screaming crowd. "Are you ready to take some fans Miss Valentine?" he asks in a deep baritone voice. With a simple smile and a nod her desires are met. It's good to be the champion. Security opens the black velvet rope and allows the first person to step through. He was wearing a tattered and well worn Vivica J. Valentine shirt from GLOBAL Wrestling, the only one they ever made. A real replica GCW World Championship was over his shoulder with Vivica's nameplate on it. After all this time being champion the sight of her name on that belt still gave her a rush and sent goosebumps up and down her arms. The man stands in silence, almost as if he's starstruck and doesn't quite know what to say. With a little prodding from the security guard, Vivica finds that maybe she's better off starting the conversation on her own. "I haven't seen that shirt in forever." She said with a chuckle, "Step up, tell me your name." "My name is Devin..." He says meekly, "Devin Battaglia. Oh my God... I'm so happy to meet you! I don't know what to say." "Oh I understand Devin, you have to remember that most of us are fans ourselves." She greeted him with a smile, "Or else we wouldn't be in this business." "OH!" He almost jumps into the air as a thought crosses his mind, "The belt! I... uhh... you..." "You want me to sign your belt?" The GCW World Champion says before tapping the black sharpie on the table. She could see the awkward feeling in his brown eyes. He was so excited, he didn't know exactly how to act or what to say. He was so scared of coming off as some idiot in front of his idol. "Right?" "YES!" He almost screamed out on accident, unable to control the tone of his voice. He was doing his best not to start compulsively jumping up and down in joy. He'd been camped out in front of the bookstore since 5am. The next person didn't show up until around noon. It was a moment he'd been waiting for. Over 3 years in the making. He watched in awe as the beautiful GCW World Champion grabbed the belt from him, touching him momentarily before laying it down on the table in front of her and labeling it with her signature. As she finished it up, hesitantly he reached out and grabbed it from her hands. "Thank you for coming out here Devin," Vivica said, "I really appreciate your support." "Can I have a picture?" He blurted out. The other people in line began to grow impatient as security started to make a wrap it up motion to The Fearless Phenom. She blew it off as she got up from her chair and asked Devin to come to the other side of the table. Handing a security guard his camera he hurried to the other side. Uncomfortably he put his arm around Vivica J. Valentine, amazed to find out that he was a few inches taller than she was. Forcing a smirk on her face Valentine held out a metal sign on her right hand for the camera. Snapping a picture the security guard looked down at the digital camera to make sure he did everything alright. "Thank you so much." Devin said as Vivica nodded with appreciation. "No, thank you." Vivica said as Devin began to walk away. "Can I write or something?! I wanna keep in touch!" Devin shouted as security almost forced him away. Waving on the next fan they wanted to keep the line moving. Employees were already past the point of annoyed and many customers had walked out. "I have a P.O. Box listed on my website!" She answered greeting the next fan with a wave, "It's [detail withheld]!" "You better answer!" He raised his voice over the crowd. "I'll answer!" Vivica said chuckling under her breath as she focused her attention, "I'd be a hypocrite if I didn't right?" "See you at WorldWide 87!" Those were the final words he got to say as security forced him away and The Fearless Phenom couldn't help but laugh. It was moments like this that made everything worthwhile. She changed someone's life that day. It was a story that he'd tell his kids one day, and his grandkids. "Now where were we?" The champion said with a giggle looking over at the next fan. ---- Dear Vivica, This is the right P.O. Box I hope. I found it listed on the fansite that you suggested for me. This is Devin from the autograph signing Portland! I was the first one in line. You were more amazing in person than I'd ever imagined. So beautiful and talented. It's no wonder everyone in GCW seems to be so jealous of you. I'm your biggest fan in the world. I have replicas of every title you've ever won, including the PrimeTime Central Infinite Gauntlet Championship. I have all your shirts and action figures. All my friends make fun of me having a crush on you but they don't understand what it's like. You said you were a fan to, who are some of your favorite wrestlers? Do you have any suggestions of people I should check out? Anyway you'd make me the happiest man in the world if you wrote me back. I can't wait to hear back from you! Your Biggest Fan, Devin Battaglia ---- Two Months Later... KeyArena Seattle, Washington "WE WANT VIVICA!" A series of claps follows the near deafening chant outside of the talent exit door of the KeyArena in Seattle. The usual tranquility of night is broken by the screaming cheers of anxious GCW fans, adrenaline still pumping from the show they all just witnessed. Herded like sheep they stand around the police barricade as officers are a step back, observing the action. Together they all wait... hoping, dreaming, and chanting under the glowing white moon above. The smell of diesel is in the air, and as more trucks leave the lights of police cruisers become the sole source of illumination. Suddenly a shadowy figure begins to emerge from the exit, and then another. The crowd grows quiet and one man shoves his way to the front of the masses with a neon green sign in his hand. Pointing all the fans speculate on who it could be. Squinting he observes the figure, and doesn't even think twice about it... "VIVICA! OVER HERE!" Devin Battaglia screams before holding his sign in the air. Upon hearing her name, the rest of the crowd roars with approval. Her face becomes more visible the closer she gets. Wheeling a suitcase behind her, Devin's heart begins to race as she grows closer and closer. With the sign reading "Valentine 4 Unified Champ" the sign is sure to get noticed. Right? Faster and faster his heart beats as she approaches. He'd been waiting two months for the opportunity to talk to her again. The mere sight of her face brought a smile to his. Holding his sign high The Fearless Phenom was almost so close he could smell her. Cameras flash from all around, signaling the arrival of local and national media. The media in which a beautiful young girl being a wrestling world champion has begun a frenzy. Excitedly Devin Battaglia watches as Valentine comes right towards him, making his heart skip a beat... right before she makes a turn around the side of the car to load her luggage into the trunk with the help of good friend Lia Ambrosi. She must not have seen him. That's the only explanation right? "VIVICA!" Devin screams at the top of his lungs, shaking his neon green sign "VIIIVIIIICAAAAAA! OVER HERE!!!!! IT'S MEEEE!" But it's hopeless. The sound of his screams are drowned out by the rabid crowd and media that surrounds him. It was an accident... right? She just didn't see him, that's all. She certainly didn't purposely ignore him. She'd remember. That's what she said anyway... wasn't it? His heart sinks down into the bottom of his stomach as he watches them file into the car without saying a word to anyone. Not to the fans. Not to the media. No one. But she said she'd see him there... "WE ARE THE IMAGE OF THE INVISIBLE!" The crowd begins to chant as the red brake lights of Vivica J. Valentine's Chrysler Crossfire illuminate in the darkness. Frustrated Devin turned his back to the car and began to make his way back out of the crowd. A million thoughts raced through his mind. All of them fixated on the GCW World Heavyweight Champion. It was almost all he thought about anymore. "Will you watch where you're going buddy?!" Some guy shouted as Devin inadvertently bumped into him. Disappointed and frustrated, Devin looked up into the man's face before shoving his way past him and disappearing into the masses before the man got a chance to retaliate. The man just glares through the crowd, wondering what in the world was wrong with that guy... before shrugging it off and continuing to chant with the rest of the crowd. ---- Dear Vivica, I wrote you about two months ago and never got an answer, it must've been a busy few months for you... or I'm writing to the wrong address. This was the P.O Box on your website though. I can't imagine what must've gone wrong. I just hope to hear from you soon. I heard the good news about your PTC Unified Championship shot against Lindsay Troy next week. I've never been a big fan of hers but she's very good. I was holding a sign in the crowd after WorldWide this week trying to get your attention. You told me that you'll see me there, but you must not have. I suppose you were busy. I was just wondering how life has been and I hope to see you again very soon. Please write me back. You're my hero and my inspiration. I don't know what I'd do without you. Good luck against Lindsay Troy! Your #1 Fan, Devin Battaglia ---- Lightning flashed in the distance, illuminating the thousands... possibly millions of little raindrops pouring down from the heavens above. A wave of thunder rattled the foundation of the house as the sound of raindrops beating down on the roof above added to the atmosphere. The room was dark and dank, the lights were off and no one was home. A slight smell of musk resided in the air as another bolt of light illuminated the room to give a quick glance at what appeared to be hundreds of pictures from wall to wall. Suddenly a thumping sound stuck out over the falling rain. Growing closer and closer the steps came to a halt. A clank emitted from the doorknob as it quickly turned and with a thrust the door jarred open with a screech. The figure of a man bustles through the darkness, with something tucked under his shoulder as he looks above for something. Reaching around for a few moments he finally finds what he's looking for. "There we go..." He says out loud before pulling down on a small beaded metal chain hanging from the ceiling. As the yellow lightbulb flickers to life the room around him comes into view. Vivica J. Valentine posters old and new cover from wall to wall like wallpaper. Shelves stacked to the ceiling with Vivica J. Valentine action figures, replica belts, GCW DVDs. Pictures of The Fearless Phenom are framed in sterling silver frames on top of his television, which is also the home of more scattered GCW DVDs. Vivica J. Valentine’s bedspread, sheets, pillow cases. Everything that GCW or GLOBAL Wrestling has ever made with her name on it resided in this room. Untouched for the most part. The basement of this home was a shrine to the GCW World Heavyweight Champion. Laying the manila envelope down on the computer desk, the computer sparked to life to reveal a Vivica J. Valentine desktop. Eagerly the man sat down in his black ragged computer chair holding the envelope in front of him reading the front of it to himself. DEVIN BATTAGLIA It read in a thick point, black sharpie marker. Lightning struck behind him, firing up the sky. Thunder shook the opened blinds in his half-windows as he pulled out a pocket knife from the drawer next to him with a big smile on his face. Anxiously he flips it open and plunges it into the sealed envelope, not even being careful and cutting it right down the middle. "This better be the real deal..." He says to himself, thinking about the money he spent for this information. Thousands of dollars, the trust fund of his parents, was spent on the contents of this envelope. With a grin from ear to ear he pulled the papers out of the envelope and scattered them out on the desk in front of him. Government papers... everything that anyone could possibly know about Vivica J. Valentine laid in front of him. Birth date, home address, home phone number, cell phone number, blood type, social security number, license plate number on her Crossfire. Absolutely everything. Even the blueprint to her home now lay within his hands. He couldn't help but laugh as he sorted through the information. All of it, infinitely valuable. He'd hit the jackpot, it was the ultimate fan's dream come true. Fumbling a phone out of his pocket he flipped it open glaring down at the paper that lay in front of him. He dialed as fast as his shaking fingers would let him. It was the moment he'd dreamed about. There was no way that they'd lose his letters anymore. He had her home address, and her phone numbers. He could call her everyday, and they could talk for hours about everything. He'd get the opportunity to pick the mind of one of the world's most in-demand wrestling stars. He pressed down extra hard on the talk button, hardly able to hold back a joyous giggle from escaping between his lips. RIIINNNNGGGGGG.... He rocks back and forth in his chair, waiting for an answer. This... this was the single greatest moment in his life. Finally able to contact his hero at last. RIIINNNNGGGGGG.... "Answer the phone... please...." He said to himself as drummed his fingers on the desk impatiently. Unable to contain his excitement he was constantly fidgety, moving around to try and use the adrenaline rushing through his body. RIIIINNNNNG... "Hello?" The muffled sound of a woman answered on the other end of the phone. She sounded heavily drugged or half-awake. Neither of which seemed to bother Devin that much. "...Hey." He tried to respond in a soothing masculine tone. "Who is this?" She said before yawning into the phone, "My plane leaves this morning, why would you call me at 3am?" There was no response on Devin's end. "Hello? Are you there?" The woman's voice asked again, becoming increasingly worried. "Don't worry, it's me... Devin." He responded finally, restraining his emotions, "We met at an autograph signing in Portland." "What?" She responded now almost as if she was even more worried at this point, "Devin? Did I give you my number? I don't give this out..." "Of course you did, don't you remember me? I'm your biggest fan Viv. Devin! Devin Battaglia! You signed my belt." "I'm sorry I just don't remember..." Vivica answered from the other end, "Why would you call me at 3am?" She didn't remember. Those words cut through his heart like a sharpened knife. He could hear her breating on the other end... as he was sure she could hear his. "You.... don't..." "Where did you get this number from?" Vivica's voice turned very defensive. His throat closed up like someone clinched it with their fist. He wanted to say so many thing but couldn't as tears began to well up in his eyes. The pain inside was too much to bear. How could she do this to him? She said she'd remember! She said if she didn't she'd be a hypocrite! She said she'd pay attention to him! Heartbroken he flipped his phone shut, trying to see through the blurry tears in his eyes at all the information that laid in front of him. All of that money down the drain. They were supposed to be together. It was their destiny! How could she lie to him like that? What kind of person was she?! Those thoughts kept circulating through the head of Devin Battagalia as he threw his phone across the room, making it shattered into pieces against the wall. Right into a picture of Vivica J. Valentine holding the PTC Infinite Gauntlet Championship. Bawling his eyes out, his words turned into sobs and wheezing as his face turned a dark shade of fuchsia. Tears began to fall down his cheers like waterfalls of pure emotion as he stood up from his chair and kicked it over making it skid across the concrete floor. Lightning struck in the background as he looked around at the shrine he'd made for his now false God. "HOW COULD YOU DO THIS TO ME?!" Devin sobbed deliriously, "YOU STUPID FUCKING WHORE! HOW COULD YOU?!" With a tearful roar he grabbed handfuls of posters on the walls around him and ripped them off in anger. Screaming in a mad fit he continued to shred the posters, flailing his arms wildly making it rain ripped pieces of paper to coincide with the downpour outside. Stomping on GCW DVDs and raking action figures off of the shelves with such force they bounced off walls on the other side of the room which was now a chaotic melody of shredded paper and white paint. Finally he came to rest in the middle of the room, falling to his knees after his hysterical fit. Surrounded by dismembered DVDs, posters, and action figures, he looked around through the blurred vision caused by this earth shattering moment. Clinching a Vivica action figure in his hand tightly he looked down at it, speaking the best he could through his throat now rubbed raw from the crying and screaming... "How could you...." Devin said, "Why Viv? Why?!" ---- Dear Vivica, How could you do this to me? You said you'd do anything for your fans! I've called 127 times and still no answer on your cell phone. Not even once. I thought we were cool? I thought that we understood each other! Apparently I was wrong about you. A lot of people are wrong about you. I spent every penny I had on you. I gave up everything to be with you. I thought it was our destiny. And you just spit in my face. You stupid bitch you said that you'd remember me. But you didn't! Now I've lost all the respect I've ever had for you. I was always there along the way for you. Praying for your safety, cheering you on, doing your stupid little fucking chant! But you were never there for me! NEVER! And all I have to show for it is this belt you autographed for me... probably out of pity! Just like everyone else! You say you understand what it's like but you don't know! You don't know what it's like to be picked on! Spit on! Made fun of your entire life because you're different than everyone else! You're just another one of them... just another pretty fucking face manipulating us! Turning us into puppets like you're slaves! I was your biggest fan, and you lied to my face. You stabbed me in the back and I'll never, EVER forgive you. I hope you rot in hell you stupid fucking whore. Fuck You, Devin Battaglia ---- It'd been a rough night. Just like the two months before it were. The days certainly didn't seem to come any easier. Constant travel, interviews, events, media appearances, autograph signings... the life of the GCW World Heavyweight Champion certainly wasn't an easy one. There was much more to it than showing the belt off a few times for the fans and posing for posters. Tonight was different though. Tonight she was coming home. The thought of a nice warm bed in the comfort of her own home sounded like a utopia right now. Anything with a mattress and linens would be her own personal paradise. She had so much to catch up on. She hadn't even seen the news in a few weeks. It's hard to keep track of the world around you when everyone else is trying so hard to keep track of you. Pulling around the corner her headlights flashed off a white picket fence belonging to a house down the street from her. The sight of her street brought a smile to her face. It'd been so long she almost didn't recognize it anymore. Even at 1:00am she could see the Thompson's cut down a tree in their front yard and had a firewood sign hanging proudly in front of their home. Then there it was at last, home. Hanging a left turn The Fearless Phenom navigated the black Crossfire up the winding drive towards her home. Slowly the car came to a halt, headlights gleaming against the windows in her living room facing the street. Well... what use to be a window... "What in the..." She said aloud as she left her keys in the ignition and exited her car. What used to be her bay window was now in pieces on the porch and around the lawn. Reluctantly she took a few steps onto the porch and placed her ear up against the door. All she could hear was the sound of her home security alarm going off. It was only a matter of time until the police arrived. "H... Hello?" Valentine stuttered a little bit trying to get someone's attention inside. WHAM, thunk, thunk, thunk... Jumping back from the door quickly she did the only thing she could. In a panic she jumped into the bay window and crawled across the broken glass to get inside. Something was going on. Patrick her housekeeper should be in there. He might be in serious danger. Jumping down from the bay window she found herself in the middle of her own living room. Ransacked. "Patrick?!" She spoke loudly as if trying to get his attention. In response she heard a groan from around the corner. Walking briskly she made her way into the hallway, just in time to see Patrick laying in a heap on the floor. "OH MY GOD!" Blood trickled down his face, which was now beet red and covered with scratches and bruises. He was trying desperately to move but his body just wouldn't respond. He was too beat up. His collarbone and a few ribs were broken. Adrenaline ran through her body. She didn't know what to do. What could she do? The police were on their way but Patrick needed her help now. Something was SERIOUSLY wrong with this picture. "Patrick! Don't worry the police are o..." "DON'T YOU FUCKING MOVE!" The sound of a shaky male voice interrupted her in mid sentence. She stood perfectly still and looked in shock as her cerulean blue eyes navigated up the staircase. In front of her was a vaguely familiar face... and an even more familiar voice. His hands were shaking, much like his voice as he pointed the .45 at her head. "I was hoping you'd be here..." He said, with a quiver in his voice, his eyes raged in anger as he looked down at his fallen hero. "This is all YOUR FAULT. You lied to me!" "What are you talking about?!" "DON'T BULLSHIT ME.... SUNSHINE." The man said, it was obviously the first time he'd ever held a gun. She wasn't even sure if he knew how to load it. It was too dark in the hallway to see if it had a clip in it. "I called you 157 times! NOT ONCE did you answer. You said you'd answer! You said you wouldn't forget!" It was that voice. The voice on the phone. Devin Battaglia. It didn't take long for Vivica to realize that something wasn't right with the kid. He wasn't normal. This whole thing wasn't normal. "Devin right?" She said slowly, trying to talk the kid out of it. "I didn't forget about yo..." "IT'S TOO LATE NOW!" Devin spoke up, "You said you understood the way I felt! You said you were a fan too and YOU UNDERSTOOD. You knew how important it was to me to answer those letters! You knew how important those phone calls were to me! BUT YOU BLEW ME OFF ANYWAY! YOU LIED TO ME!" Vivica glanced down at Patrick to make sure he was okay, biting her lower lip out of worry. "LOOK AT ME!" Devin screamed again. His arms shook more and more, there was no way he'd be able to fire straight enough to kill her. The gun was almost falling out of his hands. "I haven't been home in over two months Devin..." Vivica tried to talk her way out of it, "I haven't checked my P.O. Box at all." "But you said..." Devin snarled as suddenly the sound of sirens rang through the air. Beacon lights lit up the room, sending Devin into a panic. Quickly he dropped down to one knee and put the gun against the back of Patrick's head who moaned out trying to get help. "I said I'd answer your letters." Vivica stated, "I haven't even checked my letters Devin. I haven't been home." "WE HAVE THE PLACE SURROUNDED!" The booming loud speaker said from the outside. She could see the fear in his eyes as he held that gun up against Patrick's head. He was scared. Almost like he was lost and didn't know what he was. This kid needed attention just as much as Patrick did... but of a completely different kind. "Don't do it Devin." Valentine finally said, "They'll kill you." He looked up at her, shaking so hard that the gun started to rattle. He didn't know what to do. His mind was a very dark and confused place. He didn't know right from wrong. Fact from fiction. Truth from lies. Oblivious of the consequences of his own actions, he rose the fun from Patrick right up to the face of Valentine. "I'll fucking kill you..." Devin said with a growl in his voice, "YOU'RE JUST LIKE EVERYONE ELSE! WHY IN THE HELL SHOULD I LISTEN TO YOU?!" "I'm not like everyone else!" Valentine said as she slowly backed out of the hallway and into the living room, .45 Calibur Handgun pointed at her the whole time. She just wanted to get away from Patrick. The less harm to him the better. This was her burden to bare now. "I do care." "Then where were you when I needed you?! HUH?" He screamed at her. Tears streaking down his face in the light of police beacons. "There are lots of people who need me Devin..." "NOT LIKE ME!" Devin said in a weird high pitched voice, still shaking in confusion and fear, "You told me you'd be there for me..." BANG! Vivica watched in shock as a flash ignited the room, her hands went up and covered her mouth as she jumped back and closed her eyes. Opening them slowly she realized she was still alive when the door busted open and the Chicago SWAT Team rushed inside her home. One of them grabbed her immediately and started pulling her out of her own home when she got a glance at Devin Battaglia. Screaming holding onto his arm from a gunshot wound. "He didn't know any better!" She screamed out, trying to help the young fan. Horrified she watched as she was pulled out of the door and her young fan was restrained by police. "He tried to kill you Miss Valentine!" The officer said, pulling Vivica desperately away from the scene going on inside the home. She watched helplessly as two teams of paramedics ran inside the home to tend to Patrick and Devin, "The important thing is that you're okay." "But it's not his fault..." She said with tears coming down her cheeks, "He didn't know..." ---- Dear Devin, I know that I might not be the first person you want to hear from Devin, but I told you that I'd write you back. Right now I'm backstage at WorldWide 88, about to take part in that PTC Unified Championship Match that you were so excited about. Hopefully by the time you read this I'm already the new PTC Unified Champion. It's only been a week but it seems like so much longer doesn't it? I don't think it's right what they did to you, but I don't make those sort of decisions. I'm just a wrestler after all. You asked me who some of my favorite wrestlers were didn't you? Lindsay Troy is definitely one of them. The first time I met her when she was here in GCW I was a lot like you were when you met me for the first time. I was a little star struck. I was so nervous and I didn't really know what to say, I just knew that I had to meet her. She's been around a lot longer than me and she's accomplished so much. I hope to have accolades even half as good as hers one day. I'm a big fan, it's hard to believe I'm going to face her tonight. I'm a little nervous but I'm excited. Excited in a good way though. This is what I've dreamt of my entire life. Stepping into the ring with people like Lindsay Troy. The best of the best. If I want to prove that I'm the best in this business I have to go through her. Maybe if I play my cards right in the future we'll be able to visit and I can sign a few different belts for you! Well take care of yourself. Do what the doctors tell you to do. They're just looking out for you. I hope you get to watch it. I'm expecting to win one for you and everyone else out there. The Fearless Phenom, Vivica Julian Valentine PS: I found this picture of us on my coffee table after all the trouble. I hope you want to keep it. I autographed it for you. Vivica J. Valentine looked down at the picture of Devin and herself together at her autograph signing in Portland, Oregon. Boy time flies when you're having fun. That thought gave her a little chuckle as she placed it inside the envelope and sealed it shut. The entire time a GCW Security Guard is looking over her shoulder, waiting to take the letter to drop it off into a mailbox nearby. Looking in disbelief, he couldn't help but ask... "This guy is a looney toon Viv." He said, "Why are you writing him? I'd want to keep that guy as far away from me as possible. He tried to kill you." "I know what I'm doing." She responds as she hands him the letter, "When I signed the contract to work here I knew the consequences of my actions. I knew about the media, and the fans, and everything that came with what the World Championship might bring me. I did it anyway." "But why?" He asked, sticking the letter into his pocket. "This is all I've EVER wanted to do in life." She said with a smile, "I was a fan growing up, and I know how hard love can be too. I may be young but I've gone through a lot. This is it though, I'm living the dream. How many people can say that?" The security guard thought back to his hopes and dreams when he was a kid. They certainly didn't pan out. He definitely wasn't an action hero.. and he's certainly not an astronaut.
