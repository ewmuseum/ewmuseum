Prey

10 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/10 I watched her leave the estate from my window. She didn’t care anymore, not about us, not about anything, only her selfish reasons. Her view was shielded by greed and manipulation and coercion. Her dreams of having it all was crush, destroyed, eradicated into the endless void. She was a plague, a disease that had infected me for far too long. She did not look back, she had no remorse, and not a single tear was shed. I was dead to her, I never existed. Her lust and love never gave a single chance. It was just taking and taking, all throughout the years, the hidden meanings in her words and actions, but that was a thing of the past. I was done with her, she meant nothing to me. I had broken the shell, broken free of her hold, of her chains that seemed to hold me down. No longer was I a fool, no longer was I naive. My future is being rewritten. I thought I was on a path, but recent events change the course of that. I stood there looking at the mirror and looked upon the man that stood before me. Who was he? Who was that face that looked upon me? A man? A monster? Is it all really worth it? I didn’t need comfort or solace from anyone, I needed to go solo, find myself my own way for my own reasons. I was going to show everyone who I really am. * * * “She left you?” “Yeah.” “Yeah? That’s it? What is going on man?” Complications arose when I told Chris, my manager, he didn’t seem to like the idea that she in fact did leave for no reason whatsoever, it wrapped around his brain and warped his attitude on things. I was feeling the same way, but not as much, I just didn’t have the drive or the motivation to care anymore about what people do or say, I had gotten over that phase of my life and I was ready to move on, to become a different person. “What do you mean what is going on? You know the shit I have been going through, mate. First my father dies, then now this, my head is spinning, the room is twisting and turning, I just have no direction in life anymore, mate, I just don’t. I know what I want; just I do not know how to execute it. It is one of those things where you come into a situation and you have no way of knowing what is next. My mind is blank; my thoughts are in disarray.” I sigh. I knew if things were going to be like this, I would have just ran away, I would have stuck my tail between my legs and run as fast as I could in the opposite direction, but now-now I have an obligation, a duty to uphold and I wasn’t going to run this time. “You have no idea what I have been going through; it is an absolute bloody train wreck.” I shake my head. He wanted to understand, he tried to understand, but it was not getting through to him. He purses his lips and ruffles his hair, not wanting to answer truthfully. “I don’t know what to say, I honestly have not been in the same situation you have, but what advice I can give you is that you need to move on, one day at a time, step by step, it will only get easier from here on out. For right now, you need to focus; you have a title match you need to prepare for, that is the only thing that matters at this point, not your deceased father, god rest his soul, or that little imp that suddenly decided to walk out on you. It is up to you and up to you alone.” Wisdom, truth, these are things I wanted to ask for, yet he did not give me the answer I wanted. I had enough of these bullshit games, I needed to fix this, someway, or somehow, it had to happen, and sooner rather than later. I keep talking, going on and on about things that should not matter to me, but they help me keep motivated, to keep moving. I had to realize that things are not what they used to be; I needed to get with the times or I was going to be in the past looking outward, looking with a skewed perspective, I look down upon myself day after day after and where does it get me? Nowhere; it’s a damn shame too, I have so much to offer, so much to give, my drive and motivation should have been enough, but it fluttered, floated into a puddle and step on repeatedly by the world that never stops, that never sleeps. I was alone. I could always try to keep in formation, to lie to myself and to others about what I am, I would put on a farce and fool people to think I was genuine, the real deal, to put on the act that Bruce Wayne does every day. At night, I am the dark stalker, the one who watches and waits, but unlike Batman, I am no hero, I am just a man that lurks and creeps at every opportunity, not wanting to be a hero, but a casual observer. This is who I am. Everyone can have his or her perceptions about what I do or who I am, but at the end of the day, I am just me. Lucas Quinton, I have my own ideals and my own way of doings things. No longer had I become prey to be consumed. I emerge as the hunter. I am a warrior, I know my place in life, and I will cut down anything that gets in my way. The future hasn’t come into fruition yet, but I know what the end result will be. I will emerge victorious.
