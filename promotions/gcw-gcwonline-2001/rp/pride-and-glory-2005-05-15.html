Pride and Glory

15 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/15/05 This terrain sucks. Give me an American sidewalk any day of the damn week, even if it be in some shithole of a town down in Arkansas. I should have just gone on to Athens... ...But coming here serves as a healthy reminder. And it sure as hell beats time forcibly spent with Danger and company. Nah, I'll take this reminder of how the mighty have fallen over Danger and Gaius today. Back home, I get all the reminding I need of how the underdog can step up and take control. And in GCW, we have Mike Hardy to do that. Luke Hanson is slowly walking through a nameless town in a nameless part of eastern Europe. A few ragamuffin street vendors try to sell rotting fruit for pennies. A mongrel dog is curled up next to a building chewing on a bone with what's left of its teeth. A few children aimlessly kick a flat soccer ball back and forth with noticeable effort. The street on which Hanson walks is uneven. The pavement is busted and potholes are everywhere. Rusted cars showing more than a few signs of vandalism line both sides of the road. Hanson looks around with a look that somehow blends American arrogance with human sympathy. Sweat pours down his brow as he stops to look around. His jeans have more than a few holes, and his Doc's more than a few scuff marks. His t-shirt supporting a bar in Amsterdam is plastered to his body, and he's wearing a rather lumpy backpack. He shakes his head and resumes his slow walk, now audibly speaking. 'You know...I thought after I grew up I'd be done with the meaningful walks. I figured I'd go to a bar and resolve my problems over a stiff drink, maybe crack a couple of souls. Yet here I am, reverted-or perhaps matured-to my childhood state of forcing a vision upon myself to bring about some sort of understanding. It's taken a couple of days, but I'm pretty sure I've got it now. A few of the locals here speak decent English. From what I could understand, this place, like a lot of others around these parts, used to be pretty damn profitable. Everyone had a job, drove a very practical if not luxurious car, had a family, and provided for it. And now it's like this. I mean, to cut straight through the shit, this place sucks. It's like God took a shit on the Earth and this is what came out. And so, of course, like most things over the past few weeks, this reminds me of the Jhub. You see, Jhub, this place is the metaphor for you. Once upon a time, it was good. Many would not hesitate to say great. Not necessarily the all around best, but sure as hell good enough to get the job done and be happy. But now? Now the world's 'lucky' to get your two cents once a month. It seems like since I've shown up and started closing in on 'your' territory, all the wind has left your sails. Now, let's look at the last thing you did in GCW. You went to the bossman and asked if he needed any dirty work done to keep his prized pony from getting his career ended. Who the hell are you to ask Steven Caldera what he needs? Recalling the time I spent as your 'pupil,' I seem to remember being told to do all that you could for yourself while you could or to sit back and join the rest of the GCW wallflowers. So what does this recent turn of events make you, Jhub? Glorified security with a nicer paycheck? The you I thought I knew would have been at ringside, all right. He would have been swinging a chair at anyone within ten feet, no matter who they were, forcing everyone to see that he truly was the reason people forked over the dough to be at the show. He would have made damn sure that he was in the spotlight. So yeah, I'll admit, you playing second fiddle the other night surprised the hell out of me. But be rest assured that come Warpath, there will be no surprises. I hope you beat Alex Wednesday...then you'll have something to fight for on the 29th. It's sure as hell obvious your pride's gone by now and you won't be fighting for that. But me? I might know better, but as far as everyone else is concerned, you're still some kind of a legend. And in my eyes? That makes you my first stepping stone to GCW immortality. Now speaking of stepping stones... Jonathan Borealis. Up until a few months ago, I'd never heard of you. Up until a few days ago, I hadn't remotely cared. Now? Now you have my full attention. I would say that other than a relatively similar age, you and I have nothing in common. But, due to recent events, I'd be wrong. After that little tangent I just went off on about Jhub, I can turn around and see that you can apply the same logic to me. You see, my ego aside, I am a name here in GCW. I'm big, I'm mean, and you can bet your ass that people fear me. You? You're small and you're new. I'm sure that if we had time to take a trip down memory lane you could cite multiple examples of times you played David and killed off Goliath. Allow me to go ahead and assure you that that won't be the case Wednesday. Unlike Jhub, I have no attention of serving as any man or woman's stepping stone. I promise you I won't be 'Euthenized' at WorldWide, and I also promise that if I've got enough emotion going you might find yourself lying below the canvas before the night is over. I'll go ahead and promise that none of my 'crew' will come down and screw with things. I don't need them...I wish they would understand that. So it will indeed be just you and me. Call Mom and Dad. Check in with the girlfriend or wife if you have one. Let them know you'll be concussed and possibly hospitalized for the better part of next week, so not to worry when they don't hear from you. Then lace up your boots real tight and come down to the ring to take your beating like a man. I need a warm up for Warpath, so if you could at least try to put up a fight it would be greatly appreciated. I'll see you Wednesday, Johnny. With all my sincerity, I beg you to bring your A-game. Hanson stops speaking and walking, looking around once more. He simply shakes his head once more, mutters something about 'Jhub,' lets out a half-hearted laugh, and walks on once more. End.
