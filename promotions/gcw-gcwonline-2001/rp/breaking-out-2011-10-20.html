Being a cop is all I've ever wanted to do, and there's a mountain of bullshit that's going to prevent me from ever being one again, here or anywhere else. So I'm trying to do the next best thing and become a PI, but the law says I have to shadow an established investigator, and most of then would take one look at my sheet and laugh me out of the room." "I'm sure Jonas or Alex could--" She shook her head slightly, defiantly. "I want to earn it, not get the job because some guy owes Stryker a favor or your girlfriend has something on them." "Fine, whatever. But you've gotta -- he must have some other shit you can do. Answer phones. Put on a slinky dress and get some scummy husband to want to cheat on his wife -- Shaw could give you some tips, I bet. Or... you probably still have friends on the force, right? I'm sure Jonas could use a liaison or something. But you have to... I'm telling you -- no, I'm warning you to stay the fuck away." "I can't." "Why the fuck not?" It was higher pitched, nearly a whine. Helena's hand brushed the star-shaped locket she wore. "She might have answers I've been waiting three years for." "What?" Amy shook her head, "You haven't even known her that long." "No, I know." She struggled to find her smile. "It's a personal thing, a door I thought had been closed for a while. I may be wrong. She may not have a clue what I'm talking about. But..." Another light shrug. "No harm in asking. Look, I'll try, Amy. I really will." "You'd better find a way." Maddox chuckled amicably, the way parents do when their children tell them a patently unbelievable tale. "And what does that mean?" "It means I'm capable of more than you know and, like I said, you're a good person. I don't want to hurt good people if I can help it." "Aim, please. You're a good person, too, okay? You could no more hurt me than--" Campbell closed the distance between them in a single, stabbing stride, lowering her voice. "I could. Believe me, I could. I'll do anything to keep her. Any. Fucking. Thing. Don't test me." "Don't test me. I'm a cop, Amy. Bigger and scarier than you have threatened me. I don't--it's not that I disbelieve you. It's not. It's just that I'll do anything to get my answers, too. There are things more important than your life with Alex." "Not to me." "But maybe to me." She was steadfast in her determination, arms folding. Amy was livid in hers, eyebrows shooting up. "You're gonna make me prove it, aren't you?" "If you boil my pet bunny rabbit, I'll--" It was like every single muscle in Campbell's body went taut, half of them fighting the other half's desire for confrontation. If she were a cat, she'd hiss. "Look -- you want to be useful? You want to be a cop? Then be useful. Find some way to get rid of Devonshire for good. Al's right -- I can't send anyone to that kind of end, not without them haunting me every night, and I'm not gonna give her that kind of satisfaction. So you want these oh-so-mysterious answers, you find a way to make sure she's in a locked room for the rest of her days, somewhere that Alex won't have the need to rescue her." "You want me to help you frame your girlfriend's ex?" "If it comes to that, yeah. She's a fucking whack job, though, so you'll have plenty to work with." "And if I don't? Or if I won't?" "Then you had better stay the fuck away from us, because next time I see you, I'm gonna shatter that perfect fucking smile." At some point, Alexandra had drifted away from the others, moving towards Maddox and Campbell, but not in any kind of hurry about it. "Everything going okay over here?" she asked gently. "Yeah, it's just--" Amy began. "She was telling me about your new house." Alex smiled, real joy leaking out. "I'm never going to get used to saying that. Our new house." "Yeah, it's pretty great." Amy grinned with her, tiny hand in hers. She kept close, shoulder or arm or hip touching. Like she was trying to mark her territory. "Listen--oh, crap, you've got something on your mouth." "What?" Pierce raised the heel of her hand, scrubbing at her lips. "Me." Amy rocked up, pressing a kiss to the taller woman. Unlike what usually happened, this time Amy didn't close her eyes, but cut them to the side. This is mine and you don't get any, the look said. Maddox cleared her throat. "Say, do you--I don't mean to interrupt," she said, but the only time anyone says that is when they quite clearly mean to interrupt. "But do you have time for those answers, or...?" "Come on, Helena," Amy groaned, leaning the side of her head against Pierce's shoulder. "Let's all just go home and decompress for a while, yeah? It's been a hellish forty-eight hours. I'll help you with that housewarming gift we talked about, Al and I can start unpacking, and you guys can talk all you want once it's wrapped up, alright? I think we all need to push some stuff off our plates before we pile on anything else." For a moment, it appeared the former police detective might balk, doggedly determined to pursue a lead. But before she got a chance, Campbell skimmed a hand down Alex's back, one the taller redhead had to swipe away. "Is it okay if I steal her for a moment, Lena?" Pierce asked, brows arching. "There's one last piece of business we need to go over." "I'll just bet there is." "Not like that. Think of it as clarification. Besides, I think the others are about to head out, and I'd like you to leave your keys so we have a ride." "Uhh, sure. You want me to wait till you're done?" "If you want. Shouldn't take but a minute." She smiled, but there was a brittleness to the reaction, as if the last couple days had finally begun to take their toll. Maddox joined the others -- Amy hid her grin as she watched the ex-cop introduce herself to the other redhead -- and Alex turned back, tilting her head back to peer down her nose. "And you, missy. What am I going to do with you?" "I can think of a few things..." She rested her hands on Campbell's hips, smiling sharply, a knife's edge. Her voice dropped. "I heard what you said to Helena." "Are you mad? I didn't mean--" "Why would I be mad?" Her smile softened. "I'm not so sure about the 'I will destroy you' stuff -- I've had quite enough of that from others -- I just want you to know, straight out, no lies, no fake personas. I love you, Aim. And I think I always have." "Al--" "Just... just let me get it out. I don't know if I want to have another kid. I don't know if I'd be a good enough mom. Lord knows I'm not with Quinn." Campbell brushed a hand down Pierce's arm. "Of course you are. She's a great kid, and she worships the ground you walk on." "We both know what kind of ground that is, so that's not saying much. But my point... my point is that I can't promise you I'll never hurt you, or that things will ever be easy. Bronze is still out there, and you know Randall will be looking for payback for this, and... and Shaw and Drusilla now. God knows who else. But I will never abandon you and I will never, never stop loving you. I just hope that... that until I find a way to leave all of this behind, you'll put up with me." Amy was sniffling, her eyes shiny. "Of course I will, you big goof," she said, forcing a laugh as she swatted Alex in the arm. The music didn't swell, and the movie didn't end, irising out on a kiss. Standing there in rural Georgia amidst the debris that used to be a sports car, with enemies to the left of them and troubles to the right, the two redheads simply held each other, because that was enough for now. And because neither was willing to ask how much longer "for now" would last.
