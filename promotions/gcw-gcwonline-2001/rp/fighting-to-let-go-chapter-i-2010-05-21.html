Fighting to Let Go, Chapter I

21 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/21/10 "Change is never easy. You fight to hold on. You fight to let go." Chapter I: The One That Got Away Throughout the course of a lifetime, we're all faced with that one special person. It's that one girl who turns our world upside down. When your eyes touch her's, your heart begins to melt and your hands begin to shake; you begin to perspirate, even if you're shivering from being too cold. We've all experienced the one girl whom we'd swear was our soul mate. Oddly enough, we tend to refer to her as 'the one that got away.' Despite our countless efforts in attaining her love, we always seem to come up short. It's as if some divine force was working against us the entire time, merely teasing us and shredding our heart along the way. And even long after you think you've moved on, you still find her crossing your mind from time to time. Worse, you find yourself always asking "what if" and considering the alternative reality that could've been if you'd only done one or two things differently. But in retrospect, it's not that she actually got away that hurts - it's that you never actually had her in the first place. In your mind, it was a done deal. She dug you and you dug her. You both got along great and were drawn together like gravity - it was a mutual crush. But for some reason, unbenounced to you, things could never get elevated to that 'next step.' It's not that you didn't want them to be. Hell, it's not that she didn't want them to be. It's just that they couldn't be. That's where my story begins. Unlike you, I actually had the opportunity to be with the one that stalked my dreams. For the last year and a half, I would hold her in my arms every single night. I would kiss the tip of her forehead and whisper sweet nothings in her ear. I could close my eyes every single night knowing that when I awake, I'd be looking right back into hers. Nevertheless, the keyword is had. I had that opportunity. The last seven years have been a wild adventure. Over the last seven years, I've dedicated every waking hour in search of a girl who everyone told me was dead. I traveled across the country, I spent a shitload of money, I nearly drank myself into a grave, and I stared at the moon every single night in the hopes I'd see her face glimmering back. Then I let her go. Just like that...I let her walk away. Rebecca was nothing short of perfect. She was a sweet girl. She was a beautiful girl. Her and I had gotten along so incredibly well, almost to the point where you'd think we were storybook characters. But we weren't meant for each other. It took me a long time to realize it - seven years to be exact. In all those years of searching for her, I made myself fall more and more in love with the thought of Rebecca. I fell in love with the chase and the idea that I might never see my childhood crush ever again. So much so that when I actually did find her, I didn't really know what to do. When Rebecca and I were separated in the first place, we were still young and had only been seriously dating for about a year. She had been my first real crush - the first girl I ever actually muttered the words "I Love You" to. You remember your first 'love,' don't you? At the time, you could never even picture yourself apart. You daydreamed about telling your grandchildren stories about how you and their grandmother met and how it was love at first sight - how you were each other's first kiss and will be each other's last. Once Rebecca and I had the chance to escape the spotlight and spend geniune time with one another, we realized that this was all we were. We were best friends, childhood friends. We were not in love. Seven years. Seven long, long years. Some may look back at it and call it a waste of time. But for me, there was only two words that came to mind when I looked back on it all... ...an opportunity.
