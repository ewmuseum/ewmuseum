A Girl, An Egomaniac, and a Sidekick: Part 5 (Rampage RP)

27 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/27/08 (Takes place September 2002) September 2002-Tessa's WIW Debut Right now, she could have been making a quarter short of a million dollars. Instead of dog paddling in a pond, she could be diving head first into an ocean. Right now, she could’ve been mingling with stars such as Xaiver Kannon, Jason Snow, and Skylar Green over in Primetime Championship Wrestling. Instead, she was taking baby steps. More particularly in the direction of the dark tunnel that’d lead her to the squared circle, where she’d be expected to put on the best performance of her very brief career. Why she took this offer, a lower paying, lower profile offer, was beyond even Tessa, the one who’d solidified the deal. It wasn’t exactly like she’d been sweet talked into joining the company. Hell, if anything she had been called out by their World Champion, and that was her best guess as to why she felt the urge to take the path less taken by young up and coming talent. By now, there wasn’t any doubt that Tessa was absolutely terrified. Instead of thanking the lord above for blessing her with all the capabilities that landed her here today, she was cursing him with every breath. She was not ready for this. Not at twenty one. She would have easily traded the stage she was about to walk out on, for an improvised platform intended to showcase college students transitioning into a full blown adult. Then again, she essentially faced the same fate as them, just on a different path. If this didn’t make her an adult, nothing would. “You ready?” From behind her came Markus Stone, the more levelheaded of the two that’d talked her into being here. “To puke?” She questioned back. “No, but just in case,” before she could blink, a trash can crashed into her path, courtesy of her closest friend in WIW at the moment, “you women get all sentimental about this kind of crap, so I figured you need it.” Had it been anyone else, she would have been highly offended. But Markus was impossible to get mad at. Whether it was because he seemed too dumb to grasp common sense, or he just didn’t give a damn, was still up for debate. Still, her eyes rotated a full circle, “I’m nervous, not pregnant.” “Good thing you’re not. Everyone here would want to claim that thing.” His elbow met her ribs, reminding her of what she already knew, he really was lacking in social grace. “I really hope you’re not insinuating that I’d sleep with all of them.” She remarked; taking her annoyance out on the trash can in front of her by kicking it to the side. “Of course I didn’t mean everyone,” mimicking her his own eyes rolled up, but stopped before reaching the bottom, “you’re too much like a sister for me. But you’re fair game for everyone else, sistah!” He really did not care WHAT he said. Maybe that’s why Tessa felt so comfortable around him, as awkward as that sounds. “I guess there’s no such thing as consenting around here?” She was trying far too hard to fit in with her off par, ditzy humor. Nevertheless, Markus did his best to make everyone feel like they fit in, “Nope! Especially not with my good ol” pal Chris. Just ask every woman around here.” Her eyes narrowed as the image sunk into her head, “I’d rather not. I’ll leave that for them to discuss among themselves.” “I’ve heard plenty of stories.” Markus grinned, knowing that it’d humor his best friend in the business, had he been present at the moment. Thankfully, Tessa was spared those stories by reaching her final destination: the curtain. There to greet her were many of her new colleagues. She’d never imagined her presence would be this big of a deal. Apparently, a woman in wrestling was as big of a deal as a white man rapping. All eyes remained locked on her as her cue to walk through the curtains hit. She put her best foot forward, before turning back to give Markus a thumbs up. That, was the worst possible thing she could have done. While Markus, exchanged back a smile, beside him stood the one person she did not want to see before this match. Chris Storm. Unlike Markus, his lips remained frozen in a straight line, and his eyes looked to be just as cold. If she fucked this up, he was going to be the first to let her and the rest of the world know. Her head quickly returned to a forward position, as she took a step forward into what was about to become her future. As much as the crowd tried to assure her that everything would be okay, with cheers, whistles, and cat calls, she realized that what was behind her was much more intimidating that what stood waiting for her. “Oh shit.” She tried to keep it to herself, but everyone around her could tell that this girl was way in over her head, and this was only the beginning. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “Wow.” It was a common expression circulating around the WIW locker room after Tessa’s match with fellow newcomer Vito Wynn. For one, she’d manage to pull off a win in a nail bitter of a match, proving that GTT certainly was no fluke. But the bigger news was what occurred following the match. The proverbial ass kicking she saw coming from a mile away. “Just wow,” vocalized the medic once more, “that guy certainly didn’t show you any form of mercy.” As much as Tessa would’ve loved to sputter out her lists of complaints regarding the World Champion and his kind of ethics, she kept her mouth shut. She was hardly in the position to set herself up for another battle with him, be it verbal or physical. “You’re lucky you’ll make it to the next show.” Lucky was hardly the word she’d use given that right about now it felt as though her entire body would fall apart should she move an inch. “I’m stupid is what I am.” She muttered to herself, causing the medic to look up slightly puzzled. Her hand waved to dismiss his attention. The medic had no clue just what she was in for now, but she did. She knew that this had absolutely nothing to do with her being a woman in a business full of men. This had to do with her having talent. This had to do with her being the center of attention at the moment. And most of all, this had to do with her not falling head over heels for him, like seemingly every other woman in this company had done. It wasn’t just her who had reached this realization though, “I knew by the end of the tonight, someone was going to get the brunt of his anger. I just didn’t realize it’d be you.” Despite the fact that both her head and neck were killing her, Tessa tilted her head over in the direction of the door to find another rival of Chris?, Erik Erratic. “I did.” Scooting over on the table, she made room for her newest acquaintance, who she’d known only through a very brief meeting a month or so before, “He only wanted me here for that.” “To drive you through a table?” While Erik and Chris were far from friends, even Erik knew that Chris would hardly recruit a woman for the sole purpose of being a punching bag, “He’s an ass, but even he’s not THAT bad.” But the stare that Chris had given her before the match, was more than enough to convince her that he’d hated every bit of existence in her soul, “He wants me out of here for the simple fact that I can actually wrestle.” Her reasoning broke Erik down to giggles, “No, what he wants is for you to be like all the other women around here, and bow at his feet.” “Great,” she concluded, springing down from the table, onto her feet, “so I’m going have to kiss his ass to keep from getting jumped every week?” Erik remained perched on the table, looking down at one of the few colleagues that were actually close to his age, “You could do that,” he commented while grinning ear to ear, “or you could actually try and find someone to help you out.” Glimpsing at him out of the corner of her eye, she knew exactly where he was going with this, “That being you I suppose?” “Of course!” He jumped down from the table, “What do you say we have dinner tonight and we can talk about it” No hotel rooms afterwards, I promise. Just straight up business.” “Just business?” She looked him dead in the eye, in hopes that it would provide her with some kind of sixth sense to tell if she could trust him, “Is this a promise?” “A guarantee.” His hand extended out, looking to seal the deal. She extended her own, sealing the deal with, “I’ll see you tonight then.” Now, she had to hope that his help would actually mean something.
