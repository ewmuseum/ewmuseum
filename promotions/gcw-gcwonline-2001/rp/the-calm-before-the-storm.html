The Calm Before The Storm

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Most Current The GCW train now eases into it's last stop before the tour ending Tsunami show in Sydney. The last stop being the "City Of Churches," Adelaide, a very quaint and tranquil city which is worldly famous for it's plush gardens and copious amounts of churches. Inside the peaceful surroundings within the Adelaide city, pandemonium is all but ready to break out amongst the Australian paparazzi, who have now set up a makeshift camp outside Jimmy Thunder's colonial style hotel. Looking outside his hotel window high above, Jimmy looks down at the gathering of the Australian media circus, who have inadvertently made Jimmy a virtual prisoner in his own hotel. Waiting for the hotel staff to let him know that the limo is here to pick him up, Jimmy picks up the local newspaper and flicks through the pages until he notices a full page spread on him and a mention about his up and coming match against GCW Hall of Famer, LeStatt Knight. Now this is a match that Jimmy feels that he needs to further his career within GCW. A win against someone of the calibre of LeStatt Knight would definitely sling shot him into contention. Although he's been fighting in his very own backyard, so to speak, Jimmy have been feeling rather lethargic lately. There could be a number of excuses that Jimmy could use as to why he has been feeling flat but the only one that Jimmy would openly admit to is the fact that both Emma and Jimmy Jnr are back in the United States. Jimmy promised both that once the Australian tour had finished he was going to take a mini vacation and both would head out and meet up with him but the fact that Jimmy has now been away from them for several weeks now is starting to make him feel pretty lonely right now. Jimmy hasn't spent this much time on his own and the time spent apart from his girlfriend and his son is starting to take it's toll on him. Jimmy picks up the telephone in the hotel room and dials his home phone number. He waits a few moments for the call to get connected until it rings through. "Hello" A female voice says, obviously waking up to answer the phone. "Hey Emma, how are you?" Jimmy quickly asks, making it quite obvious to Emma that he is a happy mood. "Morning baby, I'm just waking up." She replies slowly as she breathes heavily into the phone. "How'd you sleep?" Jimmy asks. "Would have slept better if you were here baby." Emma answers with a smile on her face while stretching at the same time. "Tell me about it." Jimmy responds. "Have you got any plans for today?" Jimmy asks with an obvious element of excitement in his tone. "Nothing really, why?" She answers. "How about you and Junior jump on a flight out here today." Jimmy suggests as his excitement level suddenly rises. "Emma goes quiet as Jimmy's idea catches her off guard. She then says, "I thought we were suppose to meet up with you after the Tsunami show." "Yeah, I know." Jimmy says as his enthusiasm nose dives at a rapid pace. "The thing is baby, even though I'm lucky enough to have a huge fan following here, I haven't ever felt as lonely as I am right now. I just really need you and Junior here with me, you know what I mean" "I totally know what you mean baby." Emma replies softly as she is taken aback by Jimmy's rare show of emotion. "We really miss you too. Junior especially, he's just so miserable right now." "Just get the tickets and I'll organize the rest, ok?" Jimmy asks, hoping Emma says yes. "I'll wake up now and get Junior to pack his bags, we'll leave for Australia today." She announces. "Perfect, the next time we talk it'll be here, in down under Australia." Jimmy says with relief. Jimmy ends the call and has mysteriously gained an extra spring in his step. Feeling all hyped up from his phone call, Jimmy walks over to his window and smiles as he sees the media still assembled outside his hotel, anxiously waiting to get a glimpse of the man of the moment, Jimmy Thunder. After being notified by the hotel staff that his limo is ready for him, Jimmy eagerly heads down to the car park and wastes no time climbing into the back of the limo. With the way the media has gathered around Jimmy's hotel, the smothering effect they have on him is slowly starting to get to Jimmy and being out in the open instead of being couped up inside his box sized hotel room was like breathing two lungs full of pure fresh air.
