Halloween, Halloween, Halloween, Halloween?” “The doors of the GCW tour bus fling open. All of the GCW’s ring crew and other lesser staff members look up to the front of the bus. Slowly a figure makes his way up the bus steps and into the narrow corridor. He reaches the top and turns to the passengers” The figure is dressed in black? a black hooded sweatshirt? torn off camouflage shorts? and black combat boots. His hood covers his bowed head? and his hair masks the rest of his identity? Well, most people already know its Chrissy-Poo, so screw it. Chrissy-Poo’s sweatshirt features the white horned skull logo from the band Danzig. Upon his shoulder is an old cassette player boom box. Chrissy-Poo flings back his head, cuasing the hood to unveil himself to all the bus. Chrissy-Poo: “And now everybody” this time? IN LATIN!” [Chrissy-Poo hits the music.] “Formulae ueteres exorsismorum et excommunicationum Strigas et fictos lupos credere Daemon pellem lupinam In trunco quodam cauae Arboris occultandum Halloween, halloween, halloween, halloween Halloween, halloween, halloween, halloween Halloween, halloween, halloween, halloween Metamorphoses lycanthropie Possunt inquam Metamorphoses lycanthropie Possunt inquam Halloween, halloween, halloween, halloween Halloween, halloween, halloween, Halloween? He plops down in the back seat as the music ends.] GCW Bell Ringer Dude: “You’re a freak, you know that?” Chrissy-Poo: “Yeah” I read that somewhere?” GCW Locker Room Mopper Upper Of Shit Dude: “You’re soooo going to die tonight?” Chrissy-Poo: “And what better a night to finally meet my end, dude, than the Devil’s Night AT Devil’s Night” Mike Savige may be a machine? and Bryan Mayhem a lunatic? RipeFist may be a fruit booty sack ass? and Rei Tui a poet? And Aiden Jackson might be the illegitimate son of Michael and Janet? but as you say, I, Chrissy-Poo? I am a freak? and tonight? Devil’s Night? Is MY NIGHT. Now let’s ride.” [The tour bus disappears into the night?] [Transmission Ends.]
