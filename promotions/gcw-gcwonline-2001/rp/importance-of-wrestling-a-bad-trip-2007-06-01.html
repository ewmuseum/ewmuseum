Importance of Wrestling: A Bad Trip

1 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1 June 07 Sometimes in life you find yourself in some interesting situations and by interesting I mean fucked up beyond all recognition. I know exactly how it all began but I’m not exactly sure how it came to this exactly. I’ve always thought of myself as a strong person, the kind of person who fights through adversity and remains standing. How I have ended up on my knees I will never know. “Please don’t do this!” I spit out through a mix of gritted teeth and sobs. “You are pathetic and weak ? you deserve this.” I never met the man standing above me until tonight and quite frankly I wish I never had. I’ve known of him of course, even became a fan of his over the years ? his biggest fan actually. I have cheered his name time and time again, even as other turned their backs and booed him relentlessly. “Le-LeStatt, please.” Slowly he grinds the barrel of the 357 Magnum revolver deeper into my temple and I can feel the skin break and slowly blood trickles down the side of my face. “I want you to beg for your life.” Knight says softly. Softly but with so much venom I’m surprised I’m still alive. I can’t see his face through the tears, blood, and mask of messy black hair on him but I can sense him smiling in delight. If there is one thing I’ve learned from watching Knight throughout the years in multiple federations is that he feeds off of the fear of his adversaries, it drives him, motivates him. “Haven’t I begged enough?” “If you really wished to live, there would never be enough begging.” He digs the weapon harder. “I don’t believe that you really deserve the life you have been given, you don’t appreciate it.” “But I?” “BEG!” He howls It’s something I never thought I’d do, beg, especially for my damn life. It’s amazing what a big ass revolver can do to a man though, especially when the man holding that revolver is 6’6? and 280lbs of pure muscle. “I don’t want to die!” I scream. “Give me another chance! Please! Please! Please! PLEASE!” I try to control the frantic sound of my voice but it’s no use. I am desperate, I am afraid, and I’m in pain and that sorry asshole knows it. “You know something?” He swiftly takes the gun away from my temple. “Maybe you don’t want to die.” A brief sense of relief washes over my body, cleansing me of my fear if only for a moment’s time. Perhaps he wasn’t really here to kill me; perhaps all he wanted to do was scare me. LeStatt Knight has frightened many men over the years and perhaps tonight it was just his time to play one of his mind games on some unsuspecting fool who came at the wrong place and the wrong time. Maybe “But maybe not.” The pain that slides down from my head and to my spine all the way to my toes is tremendous. The force in which the pistol collided with the back of my head causes me to begin to black out for a brief moment. I fall forward on my hands and knees from this blow only too be kicked violently in the ribs and flipped over onto my back. I can feel the blood running through my hair and I instantly have trouble breathing from what are probably broken ribs. “N-no’D-Don’t.” It’s all that comes out, the end of the road had come. Even through the darkness, black hair, tears, and half consciousness I can still see those piercing blue eyes staring down at me. That stare sends a chill down my spine and creates a lump in my throat. “Sweet Dreams Kurt ? you’ll need them.” I slowly close my eyes. BOOOOOOM! __________________________________________________ Kurt Allan Once upon a time he believed that his life was going in the right direction, he had a high paying job, a cute girlfriend, a nice home and a fly ass ride. Of course that was once upon a time. As his money grew so did his thirst to try new things, such as drinking and doing drugs. As usual he began small, smoking weed from time to time but like most people who get hooked they have to try more and more. Eventually Kurt became a user and abuser of almost any kind of drug imaginable. LSD Weed Heroin Crack Cocaine Shrooms Everything These drugs made sure of a couple of things in his life, namely the loss of his job and that had a snowball effect on everything else. No money, house taken away, girlfriend left, everything. He had but two things left in his life that he really enjoyed. Drugs and Wrestling. No one really knows where his love for wrestling came from because these days nobody really knows Kurt that well. Kurt doesn’t really know Kurt that well. Today he stands in his mirror staring at a person that he has grown to not recognize, a person he has grown to hate with a passion. “You son of a bitch.” He says to his reflection in the mirror. It’s the typical greeting to that face he once claimed as his own. The first glimpse of himself each morning, and morning to Kurt is about 8:00 PM, is actually the easiest because he gets sicker each time he has to look at it. Day in and day out this once proud man sits alone in his rundown apartment and thinks about the life that he once had and how much better things could have been. He regrets the day that drugs took over his life but does that stop him from picking up those needles and sticking them in the largest vein he can find? Or taking a number of pills that he isn’t even sure of exactly what they are? He doesn’t even think twice about it. Kurt’s life has been a drain since that first taste and it’s continued to spin out of control. Kurt walks away from the bathroom and into the room that acts as his living room, dining room, and bedroom and sits down on the dusty foldout bed that he has placed in the center. He does two things from here, he reaches over to a small counter near him and picks up his remote control to the TV and a bottle of pills. Kurt is a fan of Global Championship Wrestling and because of his “work” he was unable to catch last week’s episode. “At least I still got this.” He says glumly. With a quick strike of his index finger his 27? flat screen TV turns on along with a cheap Phillips DVD player. Without looking down Kurt flicks open his bottle of pills and forces them down his throat as if he were drinking from a soda can, taking in every available pill before tossing both the remote and empty bottle to the foot of the bed. Kurt watches his show, only half paying attention at first as some pointless banter and a lame match come and go. It isn’t until the immortal Twizzy and LeStatt Knight show up together on screen that he pays attention. LeStatt Knight is a man that he has admired for years, especially since he’s lost everything he held dear. Knight is a man who has been through it all and yet has made a name for himself. If only he could be like him, he thinks then life could go back to what it was before. Happy. The short segment on the show comes and goes but that thought remains. If only I could be LeStatt Knight. If only. Then the pills kick in. ___________________________________________________ Slowly my eyes flicker open and I’m laying in bed staring at a blue screen on the TV. I only made it through about 20 minutes of WorldWide before all those pills kicked in. “Wake the fuck up.” The voice is so low and frightening, yet oddly familiar. I normally would have shit my fucking pants but in this case and in my state of mind I can’t do anything but barely turn my head to check out the person in the room with me. “What the?” “Stand up you piece of trash.” “LeStatt Knight?” “Well you aren’t as fucked up as you look Kurt.” I try to roll my body to the side of my bed so that I can stand but it’d hard work to do so. After watching me for a few moments struggle I guess he gets annoyed because I can feel his strong grip wrap around my shirt and pull me off the bed causing my head to bounce off of the floor of my apartment hard. “Asshole!” I yell. “Stand up or I’ll throw your damn head into a window.” Even though he issued a demand he doesn’t wait for me to stand back up, grabbing my neck and pulling me to my feet. “Happy now?” I ask. “Ecstatic.” He answers back, sarcasm dripping from his voice. “What the hell are you doing?” “I was sleeping.” “Funny man.” Knight says with a smirk. “You know what I mean, what the hell have you done with your life?” “Why do you care?” “Call me a messenger.” “From who?” “That’s not important.” He sighs. “What is important is that it seems to me that you have given up.” “I have nothing to fight for anymore.” “Is that a fact?” “That’s a fact.” I’m not able to react fast enough to the vicious punch that smashes into my face, breaking my nose instantly. I fly backwards colliding with my TV, knocking it over and smashing the screen. Blood flows from my nose like a faucet and the tears come next. Knight moves over to me and kneels down. “If I had the same attitude you do then I’d have died years ago.” His hand cups my chin and brings my eyes to meet his. “You think you have it hard” Try being the cause of your own wife and daughter’s death and then tell me what hard is. You think because your ignorance got you into drugs that you deserve to feel sorry for yourself?” “Actually?” I take a swipe at him but he is too fast and too strong, he grabs my wrist and turns it causing pain to shoot up my arm. He forces me to my feet before dropping me again with a boot to my stomach, doubling me over. “I heard you say that you wish you could be like me.” Knight chuckles. “Fact is many men say the same thing, or at least think it to themselves. Nobody can be like me because I am one of a kind. You Kurt will never be remembered by anyone, anywhere. Not by that bitch of a girlfriend you used to love so much, or those co-workers that used to smile in your face but talk shit about you behind your back.” “You don’t know what you’re talking about!” “Really?” He cracks his neck. “Well let’s just say that your girlfriend has given me more than enough information about you. Hell she’s let me get inside of her ? she said she had wanted a real man for a long damn time.” “FUCK YOU!” “Is that anyway to talk to the man who has come to give you an offer you cannot refuse?” Knight stands back up and sighs. “I’m here to give you a chance to redeem yourself.” I can’t help but be weary. “How?” I ask quietly. “Murder.” “Are you kidding me?” “I don’t kid.” “Who?” “Your ex-girlfriend.” “WHAT!”” “I’m not kidding.” “I can’t.” “Would it help if I did the same?” “You’re insane!” Knight laughs out loud now. “Well isn’t that the pot calling the kettle black”!” He exclaims, his eyes growing brighter and brighter. “At least in my path that I’ve chosen I get the women, money, fame, and glory. What have you got” This shitty apartment and DVD’s of ME wrestling?” “Go to hell Knight.” “I’m sorry but I have no intentions to do any such thing anytime soon. I still have so much to do here in the next couple of days. Unlike you my life matters to people ? unlike you I have a purpose. In a matter of days I take a firm grip on destiny by ripping that bitch Vivica Valentine to pieces.” “You won’t win.” “Give me a fucking break Kurt.” He says smiling. “Those mind games won’t work with me, I know what you’re really thinking.” “No.” “Listen Kurt, there comes a time in every man’s life when he has to make a choice. A choice to be what the world wants him to be or be the person that he wants to be. A long time ago I decided that I would be remembered forever, that I would be feared and respected. GCW has given me that chance over the years and I have completed the tasks at hand.” “You chose to sell out!” I say to him, trying to get under his skin. “Call it what you will my friend but I will not end up a loser because of it. Come Warpath I will eliminate Vivica Valentine and whoever faces me in the finals. You have to reach out and take your life by the horns or you will forever be a failure.” The man confuses me, first he breaks my nose and now he gives me advice? How’d he get here anyways? And how does he know who I am!” Why does he care? “The damage has been done.” Now he erupts. “HOW PATHETIC!” His once blue eyes turn red in an instant. “YOU ARE NOT WORTHY OF EVEN THIS LIFE!” His boot comes crashing into my cheek, sending me sprawling to me hands and knees. “IF YOU DO NOT VALUE LIFE, YOU WON’T MIND IT BEING TAKEN AWAY!” “No ? please.” “Don’t worry Vivica will be joining you in a few days you little bitch.” Then the 357 Magnum Revolver makes it’s appearance. _______________________________________________ BOOOM! Kurt’s eyes shoot open but things aren’t as they once were. There is no LeStatt Knight. No bloody/broken nose But there is a pistol. And it is digging into his temple. It’s in his own hand. The drugs have taken over again and he knows it. Tears begin too stream down his face. He knows he isn’t strong anymore or brave or courageous. Could he change? Well he can and does. By pulling the trigger. BOOM!
