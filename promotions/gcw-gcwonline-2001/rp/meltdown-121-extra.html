Date: Immediately Following Meltdown The yelling attracts a Flip Minio carried by an unnamed ringside worker. The commotion is loud, even as the employee of GCW runs toward the locker room, catching a very woozy and wobbly Jason O'Neil standing, his fist repeatedly poking the chest of an EMT. "I'm not going!" O'Neil yells as two police officers move into the room. "Mister O'Neil, you've sustained two deep lacerations, and -- I suspect -- a concussion. I would not be upholding the Hippocratic Oath if I let you dictate what you do and what you don't let me do." "I'm not FUCKING going!" he screams again, this time just inches from the face of the poor EMT. For his part, he acts as though he's seen this all before and almost yawns in the face of O'Neil, infuriating him even more. "I'm not giving Caldera any justification to say he won the war, perceived or otherwise. If you take me out of here, you're going to have to do it while I'm unconscious." O'Neil looks at the second police officer to his left and notices the employee with the camera. He motions for him to come over, as the four men stand off with others around them. "Get a shot of my face, guy." The left eye of Jason O'Neil is a red in color, and will soon be puffy and black. His right eye is cut twice, the first just above the eye on the eyebrow line, the second just below the eye, his cheek likely broken somewhere. "See that, Jay Terror? See that, Caldera? A pittance compared to what either of you will look like. You wanna start a war? Fine. You've got a war. You can't break my body, because I just don't care. You can't break my mind, because I just don't care. You also can't break my body, because I don't care. And you can't break my body, because I don't care. I also don't care about my... body." O'Neil nods, his eyes distant. The lights are on, and no one is home. With a nod toward the ambulance, The EMT takes Jason by the arm. "Mister O'Neil, your limousine is here. This way." Jason looked puzzled but moved with the EMT. "It is? Good." The situation quickly diffused as the officers helped O'Neil into the Limousine with the pretty red and white lights and the big doors.
