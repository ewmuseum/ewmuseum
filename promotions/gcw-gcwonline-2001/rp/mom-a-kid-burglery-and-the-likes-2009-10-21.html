Mom, A Kid, Burglery, and the Likes

21 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 21, 2009 The Past Her name was Ellen and she was Steve's mother. Short in stature, she made up for it with her sharp tongue and shear lack of restraint. Nothing seemed wrong to say in her galaxy. In fact, her favorite gibes to use typically wound up containing something bitterly sarcastic about none other than Maggie herself. From the beginning she voiced her dislike for her. Perhaps in smaller bursts then the latter days, but still, she managed to poke small tidbits and critiques in general conversation alike. It was almost habit it seemed by the way she spoke to Steve. 'What about that young girl at the coffee shop,' she'd say. One time at brunch she even went as far as to say, 'You know, one of these days you'll realize what you've done.' Maggie wanted to hit her. She wanted to mount Ellen and pummel her face into the floor leaving a fully spackled wall beside her. In her darkest of dreams she did just that. Sometimes she even managed to use weapons and other forceful means to do the deed. In real time however, she bit her tongue harder than she ever imagined she could. Steve hated the things she said and corrected her at all times but knew that she wasn't someone he could turn his back on. She wasn't a person he could forget in the wind. As the three of them sat around her coffee table watching the news, Maggie crossed her arms counting the seconds on the Grandfather clock in the corner. With every tick she became more anxious. "You know, I'm not so sure building a gym would be best for you Steve. Gyms are a thing of the past. Just look at the population." Steve stirred his coffee, "I see them. They need a gym that they can feel good at though. I mean, I'm sure most of them want to change what they look like. I'm sure they're all unhappy with themselves deep down. I figure if I can create a gym where they're accepted and aided, I'd be doing a real service. You know?" "A service that people still won't bite on. You need a shinier worm on the hook than acceptance. That's high school talk if you ask me." Steve grinned, "Mom, I value your opinion but the fact is that I'm going to do this whether you say I can or can't. It's something I have a passion for." "That and the wrestling. That's all." Maggie bit her tongue casually looking at Steve. He shook his head, "Mom, they're far behind Maggie as you know." "Oh, I know." Only a minute had passed since Maggie had last looked at the clock. The wait was bound to kill her considering the gibes had already begun. Steve offered a comforting arm to Mags and placed his coffee back on the table. "This is going to be an exciting part of our life mom. Things are just happening so fast that it's really like an adventure from one of the stories you used to read me." "Just remember that those end happy because they're just that - stories." "Really mom, do you have to be such a pessimist?" "I'm only looking out for your best interest," she spat with a scowl. She watched as Steve ran his hand along Maggie's stomach. "Don't tell me she's part of this adventure too?" Mags couldn't refrain and spat back, "Excuse me?" "Is there a little one in the oven? A baby?" Steve raised his brow, "Why do you ask?" "There is," she concluded. "Look at you stroking her stomach like that. Like some sort of new dog." Steve looked at Maggie and then back at his mother, "As a matter of fact, you ruined that news for us. Yes, there is a child inside of here." "Well look at that. This is an adventure." "Mom..." "Look Steve, I understand the gym, I understand everything else but your decision in love. I've made it quite clear that..." Maggie cut in with a freshly sharpened tongue, "That you don't like me. That you flat out hate me. I realize that Ellen, I've realized it since the first time I came for dinner here. Guess what though, I've just won. I'm having a child with your son whether you like it or not." "Young lady." Steve holds back Maggie and calms her slightly, "Both of you, stop. I understand that you have a problem with each other..." Maggie came again, "It's not me with the problem Steven!" Steve quieted her and continued, "I don't care who has the problem. Mom, the fact is that I'm in love with Maggie McVay and I'm going to have a child with her. I've heard enough cracks from you on her already. She's an amazing girl and I wish you could realize that." "Steven, you don't talk to me like that." "Mom, I have to." "No Steven. You have to leave. I will not tolerate that from you. Go ahead and live this big adventure. Go ahead and reproduce and make your gym. Just don't come crawling back here when she leaves you and the gym falls in on itself. I won't be there to hold your hand any more." "Mom - " "Leave. Both of you." Steve looks at his mother with a look of shear rejection. He feels the cold side of her knife dig through his flesh and can't seem to grasp the fact that he's slowly dying. Maggie stands and grabs at his arm to come. Steve barely makes it to his feet. "Mom, I'm sorry." "Sorry won't fix this mess this time Steve. Not this time." Maggie holds him together as she leads him to the front door. Before they can leave however, Ellen makes her voice heard once again. "And Maggie, this is all your fault." She ignores her and they both walk out the door leaving the coffee, news, and Ellen together. Ellen offers a heavy sigh while Steve is unable to accept anything. Did he lose his mother because of Maggie? *** A Little More Future But Not Completely The emergency room seemed to shrink with every passing second squeezing Steve between the walls. He sat patiently for two hours thinking of how to breathe and what to say when it came to be his time. He wanted to be soothing, helpful even. Anything that would help Maggie focus on their love rather than the pain within. As another fifteen minutes passed though, he began to grow anxious. Standing, he found a nearby nurse. "Excuse me," he started. "Do you know if it's safe to go back to my wife yet?" She looked at him and could see the blood red lines forming in his eyes. "Is she injured?" "No, she's having our child." "Oh, of course. Would you like me to check for you?" "Could you?" "Of course." She vanished around the corner but before she could get far she returned with the doctor, "Steve, come this way." He stood without wasting a thought on it following close to the doctor's coat tails which flared in the moving breeze. He found a space beside him as they turned a corner and looked at his expression. Calm. Cool. Collective even. "Things are good?" The doctor grinned wide shaking his head slightly, "This is your first, isn't it?" "Of course it is. Everything's ok though, right?" "So far so good. Everything seems to be going as scheduled. Don't worry about anything right now. I've been doing this for a long time and the more you worry the harder it will be." "Right. I figured that but you know - it's the first one." "The best thing I can say is to walk in with a smile and offer your wife your hand. Tell her you love her and that you're there for you." Steve nodded at the instructions and they turned another corner before finally stopping at her room. Her name read across on the door outside like a locker room giving Steve a grin as he imagined stepping into the ring with Maggie. Her hand would squeeze his and he would struggle into submission. The doctor opened the door and Steve and Maggie met eyes. Hers were surrounded by red pits. "Hey Maggie." He offered his hand as he walked toward her as the doctor ordered and she smiled. "I love you," she said happily. "And I love you Mags. You doing alright?" She breathed heavy as she dilated further. "Best as I can." The doctor stepped in again after pulling on a pair of gloves. "Can you breathe for me Maggie?" She did as she was told and the doctor checked her dilation. "We're getting close, keep it up Maggie. I'm going to get the nurse. Steve, keep her company." He nodded but never took his eyes off of Maggie. He pushed a stray hair out of her eyes and knelt down beside the bed. "Have you thought of any other names?" "I think. How do you like Jason?" "I like that. Jason Alexander even?" "That flows really well," she forced out while breathing. " "Good, I'm sure he'll love it. I can see it now, the ring announcer shouting his name and all." "Maybe we could even make some money off of him," she joked trying her best to think of things other than breathing. For a moment there was silence as they held hands. Thoughts ran rampant through their heads. "You ready for this?" Steve nodded and dropped down to a knee beside her, "I've never been more ready for anything else. I just feel like it's time. You know?" "I agree. Nervous still." "It's natural. Just think of the days ahead where we'll grow old together, watch Jason grow up, and plant a garden even. There are so many positives after this initial pain." "I know. Just easier for you to say." "Naturally." The doctor returned to the room with three nurses in tow, all wore thick smiles that had obviously been worn before. Still, it was a comforting sight when you tunneled past the bull shit. "You kids ready?" As ready as ready could be - yes. *** Back to Now, Almost the Present As Steve shot his head up from his pillow he shook off yet another encounter with Jason Alexander. They were holding hands, Steve was helping him walk. All of it seemed so real that he could feel the pits of his eyes swell up and fill. Maggie remained asleep while Steve checked the bedside clock. Three thirty in the morning. He shut his eyes and fell back but every time he did Jason's fleshy face made its way into his line of sight. After wrestling with the image for another twenty minutes, Steve sat up again and pulled the covers off his body. He gathered some sweats and faltered through the lightless room out the door. The hallway lead him to the staircase which was lit slightly by the outside bulb peaking through the windows. He found his keys and grabbed a banana from the fruit basket they kept before leaving the house entirely. Their rusty pick-up truck sat obediently under the dim driveway lights and he climbed inside turning the ignition. Backing down the driveway, he was on his way. From their house to McVay's gym was a ten minute drive that he liked to make in fifteen. Tonight though, he could feel the hold the dreams had on him and the rush he would have if he caught someone inside the walls. What he'd do though was still a mystery. He liked to think that he'd come out of the shadows like Batman sweeping up the intruder with one arm tying him to the ceiling. Or that he'd slowly walk inside swinging a pair of numchucks on each side of him. Unfortunately, neither was a viable option for obvious reasons. Instead, he relied on his mindset. He'd had nights where he'd drive instead of sleep before. In fact, his therapist told him that it was common after what had happened to Alexander. "Most parents see their children, or what they believe to be thier children, when circumstances like this occur. It's only natural," he'd say at their visits. What came with the nights were the extra gears that spun wildly inside his mind. He was a different man - a thinker. Things clicked to Steve and ideas formulated through the fear and rush of blood. Still, as he rounded Henry Street, he knew that thinking wouldn't be enough against a gun. He'd have to be quick and resourceful, not smart and analytical. Coasting through Lanier Way, he felt his cell phone buzz. It was something he least expected. Maintaining his speed, he found the phone and dragged it out figuring Maggie had woken up. It was Koz. He flipped the cell open with alarm and made a turn off Lanier and onto Fenningway. "Koz, hey." "Steve, sorry to wake you. There's something going on though." "No, don't worry, I was on my way to the gym actually. What's going on? Are you ok?" "I'm fine but I just got here and the lights are on. There's a white van outside with two guys waiting by it." "Burglary you think?" "I have no idea. You're on your way though?" "Yeah, I'm rounding -" Koz cut in, "Shit someone just tripped the alarm. They're running to the van now." "Don't be seen, we'll figure it out when I get there." "How long?" "Not long." Steve pressed his foot harder and the car accelerated propelling him through another neighborhood. He turned fast onto Drayer Street but as he did the high beams of a lost car came into his line of fire. They were driving head on a few feet away before - "Steve, what happened?" The cars collided and Steve's old pick-up took the brute of it sliding backwards into a telephone pole. The car that hit him was not a car at all; it was a van rather. Three men climbed out of it, two in sweats and the other in an Armani suit. They peered through the windshield at Steve who's head was hanging through the driver's side window. The three men were gone just as fast as the accident began. "Steve, what happened? Where are you?" *** Finally to The Full Present As Maggie felt the hand on her shoulder she closed her eyes to see Steve and Alexander. Both laid side by side buried six feet under the dirt waiting for her. She wanted to drown in the Vodka with Strawberry and Lime and lay beside her men. That hand wouldn't let her though. She opened her eyes again and he offered comfort and safety. "Help me," was all she could say.
