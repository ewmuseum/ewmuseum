The Return of a Not So Infamous Beast

20 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/20/10 Some cultures would have you believe that forgiving is the best nature of life. Forgive thy neighbour or whatever it is. Love thy neighbour and all that. This is fine in context but it doesn't really seem to matter all that much to people. We turn on the news each day and we either find out that someone has been brutally murdered or else the war is still going on across the world. It is not very often that the news is presented where the contain is purely happy. We get news of political debates, who's coming to power and all that. We get news of all the top celebrities in the world, who's bonking who or spitting up with who. There's hardly any happy stuff for example two nations lay down their guns and embrace each other in a monumental hug. Forgiveness is a small part of life. You have to get down to individuals to really see what it is all about. A couple has a fight and then proceeds to forgive each other. Someone turns up late for the most important date of the year and they apologise and are forgiven. Sometimes even people tear out another person's eye and they are forgiven.... Actually scrap that last one. --------- March fifth this year. One move could have ended it all for Lori Equinox. A DDT onto glass which had rendered one of his eyes destroyed. At first the pain was striking, jarring through Equinox's body at full pace. Then the shock took over, no feeling left at all in his body as he was laid onto a stretcher. The medical team at the Cowboys Stadium that night were as quick as they could be in responding. Unfortunately it wasn't enough to save the eye of Equinox. Immediately after the event he was taken to a local medical facility where he underwent emergency surgery. The doctor had taken the decision to remove Lori's eye, with his consent of course. This could have been an adverse reaction to the drugs but apparently there was no thought in Lori's mind whether or not to take the eye out. Lori would then be able to replace it with a fake ball of his choosing. A number of hours passed during the operation, doctors working carefully to minimise the damage around the eye socket. Throughout it all Lori was knocked out, he would not only wake up without one of his eyes but with half of his sight lost. Not a beautiful thing for anybody to endure. It would be a number of weeks before Lori Equinox would actually be able to get himself out of hospital. He had to work alongside the nurses and doctors to help himself balance and become aware of his surroundings even more. At first it infuriated him, for all his life he hadn't found anything naturally difficult. This was though and the rage started to boil within his blood with each passing day. Bed pans would fly across the wards, beds would be tossed onto their sides and hospital equipment would be ripped from the walls. The true hateful side of Lori Equinox was beginning to come out once again. Fortunately for himself and the medical staff around him, Lori kept his anger to objects and not others. He decided if he harboured this hatred as pure energy then he would use it as a catalyst against his greatest enemies, whoever they may be. Then the Rampage Royal came around. As he entered the arena that night he knew that he had something to prove. He knew he had a chance to go out into the ring and first of all get retribution upon SurReal but also make a mark on the Rampage Tournament. Unfortunately it wasn't to be as he was viciously assaulted by Slash and a steel chair. Once he had mad it to the back he was pleased to have qualified. Better there than not. It would now be time to prove his worth to the GCW. --------- 3 Days ago Beep... Beep... Beep... Beep... "Do they have to keep that thing on at all times? It's driving me crazy." Lori Equinox sat beside his mother's hospital bed. The call came just hours ago and for the first time in fourteen years he faced her. "Of course they do. They need to know I'm still alive you know? Plus you were always crazy to begin with." His mother now lay in front of him, a shadow of the woman she once was. Her hair white and thinning, her skin very wrinkled and dry. Her voice very weak but sharp at the same time. "No thanks to you." A forced smile came across Lori's face. Trying to not come across to harsh. "Last time I was in hospital I never had one of these." Referring to his maiming. "Well you didn't have a stroke did you?" "Well... no, but as you can see I got my eye tore out." "You've already told me about that Lori, don't need to go repeating yourself." Lori lowered his head and sighed. He didn't really know what to say. He tried though. "I suppose. There are nearly fifteen years of catching up to do." His mother nodded. "I didn't expect to see you here son. Your uncle told me you probably wouldn't come. I got him to ask you anyway. Just in hope that you would." "Before I wouldn't have done. Before I would have shunned even the thought of it. But now..." "Now what?" "I've changed a hell of a lot in the last few years. Realised that I've been throwing my life away, one day at a time." "We all have times like that son. I'm so glad you came though, I don't have long left." "How long?" "Not long" "Well that's not very specif..." Lori's word were cut off by his mothers crying, tears streaming down her face. Her speech was sporadic. "Look... I'm sorry. I'm... just not used to this. Seeing you for a start has hit me like a brick. I fear I've left it too late." "Too late for what?" "For us to connect for once in our lives. We've come so far from when I walked out of the house that day. I swore to myself that I would never see you again. Yet here I am, looking to connect." Lori held his mother's left hand as she continues, his attention fully on her. "I think we both left it too late son. I haven't exactly been trying to contact you. I tried for the first few years after you left, but then I asked Vincent to keep an eye on you." Her tears had dried by now. "Well he still watches over me. Of that much I'm sure." "He does. I have asked him to. I cannot start now. It's too late." "Mother..." "Lori, I'm sorry, for all that has happened. Please forgive me. Please." "I do mother. I really do. But now you must rest. I'll see you tomorrow." The two embraced, which seemed like forever although it was only a few seconds. It was hard for both of them to let go. Something magically had been achieved. If your heart is in the right place you can forgive anyone. Lori headed out of the hospital in high spirits, hoping his life was finally turning around. As he arrived home he changed into some loose clothing took a seat on the floor of his living room. With his legs crossed he closed his eyes and took a deep breathe. All he thought of was the pattern of his breathing. In... Out... In... Out... As this continued for a few more minutes his body became more and more relaxed, moving in the same direction as his brain. He could just about feel his shoulders sinking down as all the tension was released. He imagined looking at himself in the third person sitting in the room. He imagined his skin was burning away into think black smoke and out the window. He then imagined his muscles melting into one another and swamping out the back door into the wilderness. Finally his organs and all his blood would explode, leaving absolutely no trace of himself. When this was complete he felt completely relaxed. In his mind he didn't exist. There was no Lori Equinox, there was no house, no girlfriend, no career. This kind of disconnection from the world would keep Equinox from going over the edge once again, or would it? You wish you were me As Lori became more and more encapsulated in his own mind it began to wonder off. He found himself in a barren landscape with blood red sand as its surface. Almost a completely red desert, only offset by a bright yellow sky. He checked his surroundings all around himself, spinning on his heel. As he got the whole way round the landscape had changed slightly, a huge wall now stood in front of him. Maybe twenty feet tall... yes twenty, I'm almost certain. He moved towards it and rubbed his hand along its rough surface. He felt a sharp pain in his right palm and turned it upwards to look at it. It has started to bleed profusely and was pouring into the ground. Before he could do any more he felt a hand on his shoulder. He turned his head quickly right and face his new friend in this world. It was him, but not him. Hello The man in front was indeed the mirror image of himself but with black hair and with both eyes still intact. Know who I am? Lori had a fair idea but couldn't answer. He brought his hands up to his mouth to find that it had been sealed over by blood. No need to speak. I shall tell you. Lori shakes his head and tries to look away as his other self walks close. Well I'm you. You're just talking to yourself. Or is it yourself is talking to you? Either way it doesn't matter. Lori slaps himself across the face to try and get himself out but it has no effect. No need to hurt us Lori. Ya know you trapped me in this hell hole. You had to be the model boyfriend. You had to get the fans to like you in your little wrestling job. You had to go and forgive the bitch who screwed us in the first place. It's plain to see that it was a bad idea to be a nice person Lori. It's a really bad idea. Look at where it has got us. We are now weak. We are now weak as everything else. We have lost an eye, we have lost any of the anger that used to make us great. As you see me now I am the image you want to see yourself as. But alas you cannot be this way any more because you went and screwed it up. If you want to succeed in life you cannot be the supporting beam of peoples lives. They have to be the steps that you use to get to the top. If you want to succeed give up the act, embrace who you truly are or you will truly fail. As the final words trailed from his own apparition it exploded and a bright light eliminated all around. At this point Lori opened his eyes back in his home. He exhaled a large breathe and spoke softly to himself. "No..." He then took a moment to get his mind active before getting up prepare to leave for Worldwide. Tempest would be facing a man conflicted by his own mentality but also one who had began healing the wounded ties with his mother.
