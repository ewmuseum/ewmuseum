Arizona Skies Pt. 4 (On His Own)

3 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Thursday, July 03, 2008 All good things come to an end. Isn’t that what they say? A more accurate description would be; all things come to an end. Good, bad, it doesn’t matter. They all stop, and this certainly one of the good things. “I can’t believe they closed!” Scott had been like this for the past few days. Shock still hadn’t worn off for him. “How does a company close while still selling out arenas?” he asked, dropping his head down onto his kitchen table. Arizona just watched him, leaning against the countertop. “Shit happens” he told Scott, enticing the kind or reaction he expected. “Shit doesn’t happen unless I make it happen!” The man also known as Chris Storm was starting to become more and more like the character he played each day. The only thing that calmed him down was the presences of his girlfriend, Tessa. They made a nice enough couple, even though he was a dick. Wiping his hair back, Scott looked over to me, “did I get many mail.” “You might already be a millionaire.” “That’s not what I mean!” he yelled back, lifting himself to his feet. “Did any new promotions send contracts, offers, even a fucking letter?” Why he worried about such things was odd. “Didn’t you already sign a contract?” Arizona asked, not being able to help the slight tint of annoyance in his voice. Scott looked over at me as if I had just said the stupidest thing in the world, which Scott was sure he had. “Oh course I already signed a contract, but my value goes up depending on how many people send me offers. I mean I’m a frickin” Hall of Fame and former World Champion caliber wrestler and the only contract I get is from Primetime?” Arizona got a chuckle out of his theatrics, muttering in a low voice, “and you only got that because Tessa asked them.” Unfortunately for him, it was still loud enough for Scott Drake to hear him. “What was that” Tessa didn’t get me any job. I don’t need anyone to get me any job.” He turned away to look at something else, debating in his head, then turned back to Arizona, “this is why I’m not bringing you along with me to Primetime.” “What!”” he yelled, approaching Scott, “what do you mean?” “I mean you’re fired.” “You can’t fire me!” “I just did” Scott told him, “I don’t need you in Primetime. I’m working with Tessa, and frankly I don’t have to worry about watching my back like I did in WIW.” Arizona shakes his head, “whatever man” and headed toward the door. “I get my ass beat week and week for you and this is the thanks I get?” “That’s the business” Drake told me, “no one is going to baby you here. You just caught a bad break.” Arizona stopped just as his hand grabbed the doorknob. He looked over his shoulder toward Scott, “yeah, I met you.” And he walked out of the apartment. Scott just shook his head, letting it go. He had other things to worry about, and a pill-popping bum wasn’t one of them. The biting wind of New York City greeted Arizona as he stepped out of the apartment building. He wasn’t trying to go to any place, rather just looking to walk off his anger. For the past year he’s had steady work with Scott, being his meat shield. No what was he supposed to do? He hadn’t saved any money (not that he got paid enough to support his habits and have extra to save), and knew of no place where he could find work like what he had. The harsh reality for him was knowing that he would have to resume the life he had before he had met Scott. Thinking to himself, Arizona wasn’t sure if he would be able to adjust to that life again, not when he had discovered better. More he walked, more he thought, “maybe I should have asked if he could have hooked me up with some small place to try and wrestle with” he thought to himself, still not sure where he was heading to. Looking down at himself, he saw the gut that stuck out just over the waist of his pants. Could he even try to wrestle with that? The other wrestlers were always so much larger and stronger. A tinge of pain shot through his knee. He certainly wasn’t sure if he could even try to wrestle with that. Taking a left to avoid any main roads, Arizona stopped as he noticed where he was. Looking up, he saw the sign for “Mark’s Gym”. It was the gym Scott had dragged him to anytime they were in New York City. Arizona decided to cut into the building to escape the howling wind that cut him deep. The owner, Mark, recognized Arizona as the man who always traveled with Scott when he visited the gym and came over to greet him, “hey, where is Mr. Drake today?” “Ah, he’s looking over some business stuff, told me to come down and work out a bit.” Arizona lied, just looking to stay out of the cold for a few hours. “That’s good, that’s good!” Mark said, “you don’t want to get all fat and cubby like me if you have to work with Mr. Drake.” He gave a hearty laugh and slapped Arizona over the shoulder, “you make yourself at home and go work out.” Arizona nodded to him, glad to be welcomed, and walked into the main area. Tossing aside his red overcoat, he looked at the different machines available to him. He made his way over to a machine to work his legs and knees, deciding maybe he should try some rehab on the knee. He figured it would be important to test the knee as much he could if he wanted to get into the business more. “I don’t need Scott” he told himself as he saddled up, “yeah, I think I can do this by myself.” Though he didn’t know how or what he would do, at that point Arizona decided he would try to make it in the business by himself. What he didn’t realize, was he was in for a very long road ahead.
