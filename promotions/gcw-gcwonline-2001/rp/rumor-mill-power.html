Date: Timeless “How could they”! HOW COULD THEY DO SUCH A THING?!” With Rampage and the GCW World Championship on the mind of Seymour Almasy, one would think that his plate was plenty full. And it was. Until, that is, Seymour bothered to check the GCW website from his wireless connection in his hotel room. At that point, he had clicked on a rumor, and damn near spat Cherry Coke out his nose at what he read. “Gamers rumble at Square Enix”!” he exclaimed, in utter shock. Line by line he read, growing more and more enraged by the word. “He called Cloud a pussy! THEY DARED INSULT THE FINAL FANTASY SERIES! IN DOING SO, THEY INSULT ME AND ALL THAT I STAND FOR! MY CAREER! MY LIVELIHOOD! MY LOVES! GODDAMN THEM, THEY HAVE GRAVELY INSULTED AERITH AND QUISTIS! THIS CANNOT STAND!” Yes, Seymour was annoyed at the Guild because they criticized his favorite video game series. This is pro wrestling. There have been FAR dumber ideas for feuds. Like a shampoo commercial, for instance. “What a bunch of’of”.NERDS!” Simultaneously, hundreds of thousands of pots worldwide turned to the nearest tea kettle. In an astonishing proclamation (especially considering inanimate objects usually can’t proclaim things), the pots stated that the tea kettles were, in fact, black. “Who CARES about the “to hit” and “critical hit” mechanisms? All you have to do is max out your Strength and Accuracy, and then you’ll always hit and hit for 99999 damage every time! Obviously, these so-called gamers have never bothered to stat max in Final Fantasy X!” Seymour was livid. Actually frothing at the mouth enraged. Of course, it was unlikely that either of his prospective partners would be as angry at this obvious slight as he was. “Bah. WHOM SHALL I PICK TO AVENGE THIS GRAVE SLIGHT AGAINST MY NAME”!” Eva VanLorne was entirely capable in the squared circle. Beautiful and deadly. Like Quistis. And she had blonde hair, so that was even better. Plus, when he wasn’t tearfully kissing her out of rampant emotion run amok, they actually got along reasonably well. Virgil Ambrosi was also deadly. Perhaps even deadlier than Eva. He was quiet. Brooding. Like Vincent. Except Virgil hated people more and had a superiority complex. Ah well, Seymour supposed that came with living for a really long time. “Who to pick, who to pick?” Seymour continued to muse, weighing out his possibilities. “Eva’s a girl, thus, it stands to reason that The Guild will be in awe of her innate hotnes--no! I cannot assume that! Perhaps they will cast a spell to make them immune to her charms, and roll a critical hit! That would be disastrous!” Yes, Seymour has managed to combine D&D with wrestling and Final Fantasy. You KNOW all the shit that’s happened to him. He’ll be institutionalized by August at this rate. “Then there’s Virgil. Strong, agile, he’s got all the tools I could want in a tag partner. Blah, but he’s a vampire! The Guild know how to deal with creatures like that! All they need is some fire or a stake and it’s game over!” Seymour was, shall we say, over thinking his situation a bit. Then again, the Guild were the only two people in GCW comparable to Seymour. While he did his quests in the ring and on various video game systems, the Guild did it “for real.” Well, as real as pencils and paper, character sheets and dice would allow for, anyway. Seymour sighed. So much was on his plate right now. And he had to balance it all out Bad enough that he was a serial multitasker. Two title matches in one night, potentially, was what awaited him at Rampage. And if he wasn’t careful, everything would topple like a house of cards. Eva and Virgil were a volatile nightmare waiting to happen, as was the prospects of the Rampage tournament itself. Surrounded on all fronts, Seymour Almasy suddenly felt what it was like to be in a war. To be trapped. To be cornered, and perhaps finally be taken down once and for all. Was it worrying? Yes. Was it frightening? Yes? Was it exhilarating? Was it a high he’d rarely experienced? Oh hell yeah. “Can’t get this from Final Fantasy,” Seymour said, grinning. Tonight was going to be one Hell of a night, for better? Or for worse.
