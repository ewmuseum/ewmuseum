The Moment

4 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/04/11 "... but I don't want to!!!" The camera flickers to life just in time to catch Toga's complaining about something. Lollie is standing in the living room of a hotel room that she probably shares with her client, the GCW Hardcore Champion, Toga. She is wearing a slinky, form-fitting, dark green sequined dress. It has spaghetti straps, but covers the twins nicely. It has a small slit up the right leg, but otherwise stops just below her knees. Her matching green pixie-hair is spiked up as usual, just as her casual green-rimmed eyeglasses are still in place. Her arms are crossed over her chest, and an impatient look covers her uncommonly make-upped face. For once, it looks like she's trying to be the woman of the relationship. Toga: "Can't I just wear a baggy purple shirt, jean shorts, and a stupid baseball hat? Isn't that what champions are supposed to wear?" The goddess in green shakes her head, as she rolls her eyes. Lollie: "No... You're better than that!" Toga: "... I know... but....." Lollie: "NO!" Silence fills the air. Lollie looks over to a clock on the wall. It's 8:39 PM. She sighs. Lollie: "Hurry up, Tommy! We're gonna be late!" Toga: "I am hurrying.... this... just really sucks." Lollie: "Well, you're a champion now! You must look the part... besides, we do we ever get to celebrate like adults?" Toga: "Adults?!" Lollie: "............ just shut up and finish!" A few moments pass before the bedroom door opens. Thomas pokes his head out. His hair is amazingly slicked back, and not sticking straight-up. He looks at Lollie who motions for him to come on out. He sighs as he reluctantly opens the door the rest of the way. He steps out. He is wearing a sharp black suit, with a white "Brave Little Toaster" themed tie... which was probably a compromise. He looks completely unlike his typical self, and does in fact look like a champion... just not what a typical Hardcore champion wears... Thomas: "I feel like a monkey." Lollie: "Honey... when don't you?" Thomas: "Touche`" The Friendly Neighborhood Sharp-Dressed Man shifts around as he tries to get comfortable in the suit. Lollie walks over to him and straightens his tie... and accidentally pulls it off. Of course... it's a clip on. She clips it back on, straight, then tugs on the shoulders of the suit to straighten that as well. She smiles sweetly. Lollie: "Thomas, you look amazing!" Thomas: "Lollie, you look like a cucumber." She glares at him, as he grins his stupid grin. Yep, Toga's still in there, ladies and gentlemen! You can take the skirt off the man, but you can't take the...... wait... what?! There's a knock at the door, and Tommy glances over to it, seemingly not expecting company. Lollie however rushes to the door, excitedly. Thomas: "... there's something you haven't told me, isn't there?" The door is obviously a thick, heavy wood to help keep sound in and out of the room, but Lollie seems to have no trouble with it whatsoever as she opens it up. There is a man and a woman on the other side. They are also dressed up nice. The woman also has short hair. But it's royal blue. It's shorter in the back than the front. The bangs are uneven. One side covers half her face, then fades up to the other side. It looks emo, but elegant on her. She is a couple inches shorter than Lollie, and noticeably more petite all over. She is also wearing a form-fitting dress, but her's is a shiny slate-blue that goes down to her thigh on her left leg, then mimics her hair and extends past her knee on her right leg. The man behind her has long dark purple hair, tied back into a ponytail. He is not wearing a suit. But he is wearing black dress pants, with a worn leather jacket over his torso. His shirt cannot be seen. He is definitely the largest person in the seen standing at about 6'5" or 6'6" and probably near 260 pounds. Lollie and the other girl eagerly hug each other, as Tommy just raises an eyebrow. The man on the other side of the door seems quite stoic. Possibly even emotionless. Lollie: "KATSY! It's great that you and Michael could make it!" Thomas: "Mickey..." The formerly skirt-clad warrior mumbles to himself. Katsy Kitsuna and Lollie look each other over, giving the other props on their wardrobe. The blue bombshell glances past Lollie to Tommy. Her jaw drops. Katsy Kitsuna: "To-.... To-.... Toga?!" His eyes shift back and forth nervously. He quickly runs back into the bedroom, looking completely embarrassed. Lollie sighs and runs in after him, before he can tear his suit off. Michael Reese: "Tell me again, why we're doing this?" Katsy: "Do you really have anything better to do?" Reese: "Well, actually..." Katsy: "Shut up. No, you don't! Milenko and Fuzzi are taking care of your training gym, so we're here to support our friends!" The large man sighs. He is either anti-social or maybe just doesn't like Lollie and Toga. It is yet unclear. Reese: "These aren't my friends." Katsy: "I know that." Reese: "So, why am I here?" Katsy: "Because you.... well, how do I put this..... you don't have any friends, Reese." Reese: "And that's a problem?" The tiny, blue pixie-like girl nods. Lollie drags Tommy out of the bedroom. The tiny champion looks defeated. Lollie: "Okay. We are ready!" Katsy: "Good! Let's get our groove-thing on, sista!" Miss Kitsuna and Lollie join arms, walk past Reese into the hall, and start skipping down the hall. Tommy slowly approaches the larger man, who just stares at him. Reese: "Don't even think about it." Thomas glances to his own arm, then Michael's, then to the girls. He sighs, then walks past the larger man. Mickey glances across the hotel room quickly, noticing the GCW Hardcore Championship sitting in a chair across the room. His green eyes stop on it. The gears inside his head are turning, for sure, but where they're going, no one can be sure. He takes in a deep breath, then closes the door. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Following the crazy dance-filled night, Lollie, Thomas, Reese, and Katsy are all walking down the street. Katsy and Lollie are walking ahead of the boys, and chatting up a storm. A few giggles can be heard, but otherwise, it's uncomprehendable. Tommy and Michael are walking about 15 steps behind the girls, but not quite side by side. Neither man really looks happy. Tommy looks kind of depressed, but Mick's emotions are once again shrouded in mystery. Ever the stoic. Thomas: "Mickey... may I tell you something?" The smaller man glances over his shoulder, to the one-step-behind larger man. He gives a quick nod back. Tommy takes a deep breath. Thomas: "I'm kind of scared." Reese isn't sure how to respond. He quickens his steps by one to catch up with his unfriend, Toga. Thomas: "I have never been successful in my career. I've barely ever even won a match. But that was never really a problem for me. I just enjoyed going out there, competing and having fun. I've never really had anything to lose..." The image of the GCW Hardcore Championship is most likely in the forefront of both men's brains now. Once again, Michael doesn't know what to say. Thomas: "But here I am. One week away from the most difficult thing I've ever had to do in my entire life... and I'm afraid I'm going to lose... her." The GCW Hardcore Championship quickly disappears from the minds of both men. Reese glances up to the gals ahead of him, still having a blast reminiscing. Tommy is watching the pavement as his dress-shoes clap down on it. Thomas: "I'm going to fight... literally fight... 4 men. All of which are larger than me. All of which want my championship. And all of which are completely psychotic..." Michael slowly nods in agreement to them being psychotic. Apparently, he's a follower of Global Championship Wrestling. Thomas: "Not very often am I considered the saner one. But I very well might be this time... But that's not even the worst part. I'm going into a fight, where I don't know the location. I don't know the rules. And all of my opponents have been beating the bananas out of each other for MY title for a long time before I was even in GCW. I took advantage of the most amazing opportunity I've ever gotten... I slipped in and I did what I do.... I stol-... er... borrowed... the belt. But even though I'm borrowing it... now I can't give it back! It's mine!" Katsy and Lollie both look back at the boys. They giggle, then go back to looking ahead of them. Thomas: "I won the title. I won the right to compete in a very special "match" at GCW's biggest event of the year. But I'm not ready... I'm going to get crushed. Maimed. Destroyed. Broken.... Hell, from what I hear, there's even a chance I could DIE in this fight....!" Mickey suddenly stops. He quickly reaches over and grabs a handful of Thomas' shirt. He throws him up against the nearest wall, and stares him straight in the eye. The girls hear the commotion and turn around. Reese: "Shut... the... hell.... up." Katsy: "MICHAEL! WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING?!" Miss Kitsuna and Lollie rush over and try to pull Reese off. Thomas' eyes are wide with shock. Reese: "You are a disgrace. You don't deserve anything that you have. And frankly, I'm more than sick of it." Lollie: "REESE! STOP IT! ARE YOU FUCKING INSANE?!" The larger man seems to completely ignore the screaming and tugging of the little ladies beside him. Tommy's face is still covered in sheer surprise. Reese: "You talk about how you stole a title that these other four men have been sweating, bleeding, and breaking each other over for months now. You talk about how scared you are. About how you might even die in this fight.... Maybe, you should've thought about that before you got your scrawny ass into this situation..." Katsy: "MICHAEL!........ MICHAEL!!! PUT HIM DOWN!!!" Mickey takes a step back, and throws Thomas over towards the curb, just before the street. Lollie rushes over to see if Tommy is alright. Katsy glares at her date. The shock is still covering Tommy's thin face, as he rolls over and stares at Reese. Katsy: "What the hell is wrong with you, Michael?!" Lollie: "Are you alright, Tommy?" Mick takes a step towards Thomas, but Katsy jumps in front of him. Katsy: "NO!" He completely ignores her, but stops just in front of her anyway. He looks over her to the fallen GCW Hardcore Champion sitting on the sidewalk. Reese: "There is no way you're going to survive the fight that you have coming. All I did was throw you up against the wall, and here you are, completely incapable of retaliating. It doesn't matter that I caught you off guard... when you fight four other men at NC-17... there's no such thing as being on your guard. You're going to get brutalized from every possible angle, in every possible way." Lollie tries to help Tommy up, but his focus is dead-locked on Reese, and his body seems to be completely frozen stiff with fear. Reese: "Like I said, you don't deserve to be the GCW Hardcore Champion. You don't even deserve to be IN GCW. Hell, I'll even go so far as to say you don't deserve Lollie managing you!" The gal in green shoots a piercing glare back to Michael. He however, keeps his eyes on Thomas. Reese: "In one week, you're going to step onto hollowed ground. You're going to stare into the eyes of four men that have made of living of literally tearing other people apart. And you will perish." Katsy glances back to Tommy for a brief second, before returning her attention to Mickey. Reese: "One week. That's all the time you have left.... So, tell me, Thomas... what are you going to do with your final days?" Tommy snaps his focus off of Reese, and slowly glances over to Lollie. She stares deep into his blue eyes, extremely concerned. Thomas: "I.... I......" The GCW Hardcore Champion glances back to Reese, who looks like he could snap at any moment. He returns his attention back to Lollie. Thomas closes his eyes.... Thomas: "I.... I....... Lollie, I..." With his eyes still closed. His heart racing a million beats per second. His new suit ruined. His GCW Hardcore Championship likely lost in a week. And Reese's words echoing through his head......... Thomas wraps his arms around Lollie and, for the first time EVER... gives her a happy-ever-after kiss on the lips! The Green Goddess is in completely awe for a few seconds, before she slowly closes her eyes and wraps her arms around him. He squeezes her tight, almost as if afraid to let her go. Katsy: "Wow...." The anger covering Michael's face suddenly disappears and an uncommon smirk stretches out to replace it. Kitsuna is gawking at Lollie and Toga embracing each other. She slowly, and reluctantly, turns around to see Reese's grin. Her face goes back to anger. She hits him in the chest. Katsy: "What the hell?! This was your plan all along?!" Mickey shakes his head. Reese: "Not exactly... It's motivation. The best motivation in life is fear.... and love." Thomas and Lollie slowly pull their lips away from one another. He opens his eyes. His heart feels like it's trying to break out of his chest. She slowly flutters her eyelashes open, still seemingly shocked. They stare into each others eyes, before a small smile appears on Lollie's face. Thomas responds with a smile of his own. She quickly pulls him close into a tight hug. Katsy: "Since when do you care about anyone else... let alone, Toga?!" Reese: "My time away from the ring has helped me see the big picture. Helped me fight my own demons." Katsy: "Are you serious?! I've been by your side for years, trying to get you to see the big picture... and it's HIM?! HE does it for you?!" Reese: "... I'm not gay, Katsy.... It just sparked something in me, seeing him on WorldWide after he won the title. How proud of himself he was. I remember that feeling." Thomas and Lollie release each other. He pulls himself up, dusts himself off, then puts his hand out to help Lollie up. She grins and obliges. The suit-clad warrior helps her to her feet. Katsy steps out from between Tommy and Mickey finally. Katsy: "You saw someone that you've never given two-shits about win a title, and it got you all misty-eyed? Awwww!" Michael shoots her a glare.... as TOGA walks up to Reese and slaps the taste out of his mouth! He hits him so hard that Reese spins around halfway!!! Both of the girls look at him, completely awestruck! Mick grabs his cheek, as he returns his angry glare to the much smaller man, who just a few minutes ago looked ready to crap himself. Now, he looks like a champion. Determined and fearless. Thomas: "You are going to train me." Reese: "Huh?....... Um.............. Okay." Thomas: "I have one week. And you are the most successful, craziest son of a bitch I know!" Not very often will swear words pass through the lips of "Your Friendly Neighborhood Klepto." But tonight, he is a new man! Thomas: "So, tomorrow, we are hopping on a plane back to Michigan. We will go back to your training gym... and you are going to make me into a man deserving of being the GCW Hardcore Champion.......... A man deserving of..." He glances back to Lollie who uncharacteristically blushes. Thomas: "Her." Tommy looks back to Reese and puts his hand out. Michael nods and shakes his hand. The girls look at each other, amazed at what they've just seen. Reese: "Kid... I'm going to break your ass in half...... Then build you back up stronger than ever! You think you can handle that?" Thomas: "Damn skippy, Mickey!" Suddenly, the seriousness is gone and the joyous, playful Toga comes out to play again. He grins his cheesy grin, forcing Mick to grin back. The larger man places his hand on the GCW Hardcore Klepto's shoulder. They start walking down the sidewalk again. Lollie quickly hooks her arm into Toga's, as Katsy walks beside Reese. Tomorrow... Toga gets broken.
