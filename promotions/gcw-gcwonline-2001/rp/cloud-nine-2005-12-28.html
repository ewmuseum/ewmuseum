Cloud Nine

28 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28-12-05 Cloud frigging nine. Mary’d been bouncing off the walls like copulating flubber for the best part of the night after worldwide 35 since LeStatt Knight, love, light and throbbing penis of her life had proposed to her. Marry me, he’d said. Was the darndest thing. Well if you consider your third strike darndest. Now she sat in the back of a private cab with Arael beside her in deathly silence. She’d been quiet ever since she and Knight had parted company that night. He had some things to take care of but promised that in a few hours they could go out to dinner before celebrating some more. Mary had some things of her own to sort out. She called her biggest fan and then new best friend Rain to tell her the news first. Rain was ecstatic for her especially when Mary asked her if Rain’s son Tai could be a page boy at the wedding. She called her new found cousin Kagerro too. Mary didn’t have many people to call straight away but there was one person who needed to be spoken to in person. The one person who had single handedly had the biggest problem with LeStatt Knight since Mary had started seeing him. Her long time best friend and ex girlfriend Skye. Mary glanced out the window and sighed before Arael forced himself to break the silence. “Chica” You seem quiet tonight.” Mary turned to him and smiled up at his dark shade adorned face the turned back to the window. “Just thinking is all.” “About Miss Skye?” Mary nodded, “Sim. Mary don’t think she’s gonna take this too well, and Mary really wants her to be a part of this.” “I’m sure she will come around to the idea in time.” Mary turned to him her face incredulous. “She put a hit on him Eddy for freaks sake!” “I didn’t say it would be a short amount of time Chica. She cares for you deeply. Many people would give much of all they have to have someone to care for them like that.” Mary gazed down at her lap. “Mary ain’t complaining Eddy is just?” she sighed again. “Just Mary knows she’s been trying hard to accept Statty and all. This is gonna be the big test and Mary don’t wanna have to choose between Skye and Statty, not ever. If she makes Mary choose then?” She trailed off again. “She wont make you choose Chica.” Mary smiled and nodded. Reaching out, she brushed her hands over Arael’s interlocked fingers before leaning back and staring out the window again while the car made another left turn. “I don’t need eyes to see there is more bothering you Mary.” She turned back to face him, her face solemn. “Three times Eddy. How much does Mary gotta take before she wises up and gives in and realises that maybe she ain’t meant to have the happy ever after” Lazlo was such a sweetypie, was like a best friend to Mary and was possibly, next to scar the only person Mary been in love with that could give her psychoseees a run for its pennys. Damien Black, he proposed too. We dint even make it down the isle Eddy. He ripped Mary’s heart out and it took Mary so long to get back and now Statty bear. He’s prolly broken Mary’s heart more times than Lazzykins or Damey, or Scar or Skye and like Mary still digs on him mucho and then there’s this. He springs this whole big thing and Mary’s not sure what to think or to feel anymore.” “You seemed happy about it at the show. Don’t you want to Marry Senor Abitua?” “Oh no no Eddy, Mary’s happy as hand up a ho’s ass, is just now shes had time to sit and think, now she’s?” “Scared?” Mary nodded. “Sim Eddy, Mary’s so scared that if this goes bad, if this falls apart, she might not be able to put herself back together again. Not again.” Arael fought to keep his tone neutral. He was teetering on the edge of that line between professionalism and inappropriateness. “You can not live your life in fear of what may be Chica. It is easier to run and fall at every hurdle than it is to sit in the stands and watch the race pass you by. Trust me Mary, I’ve spent so long in the stands now that?” This time Arael trailed off, but he quickly recovered. “You’ll do fine, Miss Skye will survive this. Fear is normal.” “Thanks Eddy.” She smiled up at him as the car stopped outside the lavish door to a modest mansion if there is such a thing. Mary got out after Arael and took his hand to lead him up the stone path to the door itself. Just as they got to the door raised voices were heard. One was Skye’s, the other was another woman Mary didn’t recognise on hearing. Arael stopped Mary from going to ring the door bell and pulled her back to his side while reaching for his gun. Mary noticed. “Is okies Eddy, Skye prolly arguing with the girlyfriend.” “How can you be sure?” “She’s speaking in English, anyone else gets her that mad and she switches to the Brazilianish in a freaking instant. Must be the g-friend. Shh, listen.” The voices got even louder and then the door flung open. A young woman with mid length wavey black hair with bluey green streaks stormed out yelling. “You never fucking get it do you Skye” I’M NOT YA PRECIOUS FUCKING PSYCHO EX GIRLFREIND! I cant be her and I sure as hell ain’t gonna stick around while you use me like a fucking effigy!” Skye ran after her in a shorty robe and knee high socks. Her hair was tussled. “That’s bullshit Cookie and you know it! You’re being over fucking sensitive you damned?” Skye stopped on seeing Mary at the door with Arael. “TyTy?” Cookie reeled around hearing the words. “Oh I nearly didn’t recognise her without that man attached her goddamn face.” Mary spun round with eyes narrowed, “Watch it ho!” Cookie smiled lividly. “Watch who you calling a ho bitch!” Mary rolled up her sleeves, “Pass Mary the Vaseline Skye!” Mary swung for Cookie but Arael caught her arm and held her back. “Let Mary go Eddy!” She struggled while Skye tried to rectify the situation. “You’re wrong for that Cookie. Mary’s done nothing to you, can we all just calm down. Ty, if Eduardo lets you go can you keep ya fists to yaself?” Mary pouted, “Sure thing dingaling, so long as the cookie keeps her ass in check.” Cookie scowled, “Check this bitch!” She flipped Mary off. Mary fought to keep her composure. Skye beckoned her over. “Can we all just go back inside. Cookie, I’ll walk you to your car.” “don’t bother Skye. You’ve made ya fucking choice, you made it a long time ago. If I’m wrong then I’m wrong but right now, right fucking now, you go take care of ya psycho bitch girlfriend ok. I hope you taste like me when you kiss her!” Mary and Arael were almost inside when cookie left them with, “Crazy fucking skank.” Which of course caused Mary to come storming out past everyone and land a fist square in cookie’s jaw laying her out cold. “And that’s how the cookie crumbles bidnite!” Mary nodded at her, and flipped off her unconscious form and headed back indoors. Skye was outside for a half hour with Cookie, waiting for her to come to, then calming her then packing her off in a cab before joining Arael and Mary in the dining room. Mary had made her and Arael a drink when Skye came into the room, her shoulders heavy and her eyes conflicted and lost. She sat down at the table and sighed, her head in her hands. Mary tried to break the tension. “Mary’s sorry she had to clean ya girlies clock but she was being mean. She deserved to get served damnit!” Skye gave a heartless chuckle followed by a smile that faded as quickly as it arrived. “It’s ok TyTy, she was angry, she flips out sometimes, she’ll be fine, she’s tough. One of the reasons I’Well it don’t really matter.” Skye looked up at Mary. “So felicita??es huh?” Mary smiled but hers wavered too. “Depends on if you really wanna congratulate Mary or not. If ya don’t wanna ya don’t gotta.” Skye exhaled. “Anjo,” She shook her head looking confused. “It’s not about wanting to congratulate you. I want to be happy for you and I’m happy that you’re happy, I really am. I’ve never seen you the way you are around Roberto. You haven’t been that taken heart and soul with someone since...” Mary cut in, “Since Mary and you.” Skye nodded and that smile played out again falling short of meeting her eyes. “Wait, you and Cookie was arguing before?” Skye cut in this time, “Yea, it was about all of us. She thinks I’m using her because I cant have you. She says we cant move on if I’m still hung up on the past.” Mary now looked a little frightened. “And is you Skye blue” Mary thought you moved way on after your big love got taken away.” Skye took Mary’s drink and sipped it. If it was booze, she would have given it the swig it deserved. “I tried to move on when they took me away from you at Institute Ty, and for a while I did. When I met Alja I thought me and her were gonna be forever. Then the cancer took her and it ripped my heart out. I never wanted to trust my heart with anyone ever again but you were always there in the back of my mind. We were always there, together and I figured it was just because it was this young intense first love ya know” You don’t forget that shit. I finally started being able to meet people, let them get closer and now with Cookie’I love her Ty. At least I feel like I could. I want to but she was right about one thing.” Skye looked Mary dead in the eye. “I’ll always love you too. If you wanted to try again, right this minute I feel like I could’Mary I need closure and I guess this is it.” Mary’s eyes widened in shock. “You leaving Mary?” Her lip trembled. Skye smiled and got up from her seat to sit in the chair beside Mary. “I’ll always be with you Anjo and you’ll always be with me but we can’t go on like this, I cant do it. It’s too hard.” Mary’s hand had circled her glass again and was squeezing so hard her knuckles were paling. “So what you saying Skye Blue?” “I’m saying Mary this has to stop. I watched you fall in love with this man and I knew he was gonna break ya fucking heart more than once if ya let him and you keep letting him. I watched him come to your bedside time and time again when you were hurt and then I had to fucking turn around and watch him put you in goddamn hospital bed himself. The man is more dangerous than Damien Black ever was and you trust him even more, you treasure him even more, you fucking hold on and break a goddamn sweat doing it Mary.” Mary’s eyes started to glaze over. Skye took her trembling hands. “So I’m saying this. If you’re gonna insist on marrying the man that I fucking nearly had assinando trying to protect you from him, then’I guess I’m gonna have to sit there and watch and be happy and try not to have him killed again.” Mary was holding her breath but she exhaled in surprise. Her face fought to smile, and then it beamed. “You means it?” Skye nodded, “sim eu significo-o” “You gonna be there?” “Front row Anjo.” Mary embraced her and squeezed her tight. “And you not gonna try an have him killed again?” Skye smiled and squeezed her back. “Baby steps Anjo, etapas do beb”.”
