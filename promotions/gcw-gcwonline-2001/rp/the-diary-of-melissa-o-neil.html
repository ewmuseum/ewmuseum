The Diary of Melissa O'Neil

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1994-1999 The following is the diary of Melissa O'Neil. The wife of Bryan O'Neil. Sister in Law to Jason. He body was found gagged and beaten ub the basement of Belmont. Her murderer was never found, but many suspect a 'Lunatic' was the aggressor. 11-13-1994: I think I just met the greatest man of my life. He's a huge monster, but has a sweetheart. He lives in the old castle on Belmont Hill. Yes I know, it's the house where all those murders took place years ago. But this guy named Bryan seems different. Sure he doesn't talk much, and people think he's weird, but I like him. 12-1-94: My 17th birthday, and Bryan brought me some old roses. Sure they were half dead but it was still sweet. We've been dating for two like 2 weeks or so now and he hasn't said much. He;s in his mid twenties that's all I know. His younger brother constantly has bruises, but Bryan says he has wooden legs or something and always falls down steps and stuff. 12-25-94 Merry Christmas!!! I wonder what Bryan got me? He can be so cute sometimes. Just yesterday he gave me this old dog, I named him Barney, Bryan had a black eye yesterday, silly guy whacked himself in the wrestling with Jason. I haven't seen Jason in a few days, I wonder where he's been? 12-27-94 Barney died. He didn't die like he was old. I found his head cut off in a box. Bryan vowed to find the killer. 1-29-95 It's been over a month since I last wrote in here. I'm scared. I haven't seen Bryan or Jason. I heard some screaming yesterday from their barn, but that wasn't unusual. Jason always screamed everytime their father slaughtered his favorite pig or chicken. 2-1-95 What have I gotten myself into? I guess for years Bryan's father was abusing them. Jason denied it, but Bryan says it happens all the time. Their father ran away because Jason ran off. Bryan asked me to move in. Should I? 4-30-95 This house scares the shit out of me. It makes wierd noises, and its always cold. I was wandering around the yard yesterday and saw what looked like shallow graves. Bryan says they are mounds left by years of erosion from the ocean. 5-15-95 Bryan just asked me to marry him. But he wants it to be quiet and in his own church. I didn't even know he was spiritual. There was over three years of silence between the last and next entries into her diary. She never wirtes about their wedding or life afterwards. Something happened during the wedding that changed her disposition forever, she herself becamse almost insane, as if she was brainwashed to do so.. 7-30-98 I can't believe I found this, yet never remember anything I've written. I was the biggest sap on earth, I'm glad my master made me realize who I was. My master just had the most intense match of his career. He ended the career of a mammoth of a man named Grim Reaper right where I complete this entry. My master and myself made love right here, where the Reaper nearly bled to death. 8-28-98 My master says his brother is becomming to big for his own good, so Bryan chokeslammed him through a plate of glass, cutting Jason deep. It was so hot, I wanted him right there. Bryan is becomming so famous, I'm so proud of my master, so proud. 9-16-98 Jason just won the world title and I'm so happy for him. Sure my master is angry, but Jason is my brother as well now, and when theres gold in the family why wouldn't I be happy. Jason will make a great world champion. 10-31-98 My master was robbed last night! He beat Jason for the title and was stripped of it because Riggs hates him and loves Jason. I love Jason, but Bryan won fair and square. 11-19-98 My master beat me good last night because he found my diary and is becomming delusional. He thinks I'm having an affair with Jason. I didn't mean to hurt my master's feelings. I did deserve the beating. 12-24-98 I was beaten bad again last night because I came to aid of Jason after he was hit in the head with a cinder block by The Almighty Kippo. He's getting to be crazy, and the beatings are getting worse. I'm getting scared, but maybe I deserve them. 1-1-99 The last year of the beginning of the end begins today. Bryan was fired from EHWF for breaking the arm of the president, and was just fired from PCW for abduction. He came home and beat me again, but we made love afterwards. 2-3-99 I brought another woman home for my master for his birthday. I just realized I must leave him, i watched him choke her until she was blue...He told me I was next That was the last entry ever written by Melissa O'Neil. Her body was found March 10,1999 in the basement of Belmont. It appeared to be a homicide. No arrests were ever made.
