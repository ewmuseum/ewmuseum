Hazy Drive (Aftermath pt 2)

Date: About half an hour later “At least since we’re leaving now we’ll beat the traffic.” The joke was an uncertain one, the chuckles that followed uneasy. Alex Morgan had made the comment and was half carrying, half dragging Michael Stevens. The big man sighed in relief as he heard his older cousin snort a bit of laughter. At least that meant Stevens was still conscious, at first he’d tried to walk but that was proven impossible. He hadn’t spoken since saying hospital while pitching forward after trying to walk, he’d been too busy trying to catch his breath, or so it seemed. Sheena stood by nervously. She’d been a wreck since she and Alex had met Stevens coming down the hallway. After he’d made it perfectly clear he needed to go to the hospital, she’d immediately run to the locker room to pick up his back, while Alex had started out towards the car. Now that they’d gotten to the parking lot, and arrived at the car, she wasn’t sure exactly what to do. The pointed look from Morgan in her direction was completely lost on her. “Sheena, I need you to unlock the door, I can’t do it while I’m holding him.” “Sorry, sorry, god sorry.” “It’s ok.” She rushed forward, fumbling to open the bag, and attempted to pull out the keys. It’s bizarre how even the simplest tasks become overwhelming when you’re in a crisis. The keys continuing to allude her, she broke down. Tears begin to flow, and she let out a screech, rage, frustration, fear, all boiled into one outburst. Collapsing against the car as the tears continued to flow, she pushed the bag away with her feet, and sobbed. “Sheena”.Sheena.” Realizing he wasn’t going to be able to break through to her with just words, Alex had a decision to face. They had to get moving quickly, he was starting to get really worried about Stevens who with the exception of the snort had been completely silent, save for his wheezing breath. That meant they had to get into the car, but he couldn’t unlock the door and hold up Stevens at the same time, so it was either try and get Sheena up, or set Michael down. Neither seemed like a great option. This was probably the worst thing he could actually think of, the two people in the world he was closest with in trouble, and he was the only one that could do anything. It’s tough realizing you’re not the smartest person in the world or even close. Alex was well aware of his own shortcomings knowing he was never the type that you’d look to in a crisis situation. What he never realized is that’s why most people did end up looking to him. He had the ability to keep a level head at all times, and when something needed to be done, he’d do it, it just sometimes took him a little longer. He wasn’t dumb, or slow in the short bus sense really, just a deliberate thinker. Not even he really got that about himself. Gently settling Stevens down by Sheena, he fished his own set of keys out of his pocket. Opening the back passenger door first, he leaned back down to pick up Stevens. Expecting to find nothing but dead weight, believing already that he must have made the wrong choice, because that’s all he ever did, he wouldn’t have been surprised to find Michael dead. What did end up surprising him was Stevens resisting. “Alex, wait..” Voice rasping and weak, Michael pushed the bigger man’s hands away. His breath had evened out quite a bit, the world no longer tilting crazily on it’s axis. Bracing himself with his hands against the pavement he struggled up to his feet. Wobbly he was able to stand, and look down at Sheena who was still a wreck. Head buried in her hands she continued to cry, completely unaware of what was happening around her. Again something stirred instead Stevens. He knew what had to be done, it just wouldn’t be easy. “Alex, you might have to make sure I don’t fall again.” Just nodding Alex wasn’t quite sure what was happening, but this was something he understood. Michael had told him what to do, it was going to be alright. Watching Stevens extend his hand towards Sheena he waited anxiously, making sure Stevens wouldn’t fall. “Sheena, you’ve got to get up sweetie, we’ve got to go, I need to get checked out.” He’d cleared his throat a bit and his voice was a little stronger. It did the trick as Sheena looked up from her hands and saw him standing over her. Letting out of little giggle of relief she bounced up to her feet, and threw her arms around Michael. Throwing him off balance he would’ve fallen in a heap with her on top of him, if not for Alex behind them both, solid as a brick wall, bracing. She never even knew. “Mikey, oh my god!” “I know, I know, look I’m still pretty woozy, but at least I can breathe. We gotta go, and quick, get in the car.” “Oh my god, ok sorry.” Immediately she released her bear hug and slid inside. Careful not to let her see him helping, Alex gently guided Stevens into the car as well. As he began to turn, so he could load up the bag, and get in the car himself, Alex felt Stevens hand grip his arm. Looking down into the face of his cousin, mentor, and friend, Alex saw Stevens smile weakly, his face still covered in blood the smile wasn’t it’s brightest, but it was warm and full of genuine affection. “Thanks bro..” Without waiting for him to respond Stevens nodded towards the bag, and sank down into the seat. Laying his head back, all he wanted was to close his eyes and sleep. However, he was regaining his senses and knew that was the worst idea possible. Wracking his brain he knew these two would have to be responsible for keeping him awake, as the ride would almost surely begin soothing him back to sleep. At the sound of the trunk slamming shut, it occurred to him what he’d asked about, what they’d speak of to keep him awake. It was a question that’d been on his mind since he stumbled to the back, but he just hadn’t felt up to asking, the pain, drowsiness, and overall crushing despair had kept him from it. Watching Alex slide into the car, and start up the engine, he asked. “Who won?” ~To Be Concluded~
