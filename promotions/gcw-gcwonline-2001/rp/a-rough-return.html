Date: Timeless -=Drip, drip, drip. Water leaks from the tiny barred window, onto its own puddle 10 feet below. The ground is uneven concrete, the walls are large brick, moldy and musty to the human eye. Sitting in the corner with his knees up against his chest, his chin resting on his knees, hair in his face, Mayhem rocks himself back and forth. The glimmer of light from the moon jumps from his forehead and left eye, to nothing, and back again. It's cold, bitterly cold. This is the cell of Belmont where Maximillian O'Neil use to put his children Bryan and Jason to sit and think about what they did wrong. It's usually preceded by a whooping. Here Bryan sits pondering, questioning himself.=- Mayhem:'My return was not quite what I anticipated.'-=he exhales deeply=- 'Am I too old? Or have I gotten weaker?' -=A famaliar voice bellows from outside the cell, near the window.=- Voice:'You disgust me. You are an embarassment, and to think I idolized you.' -=Mayhem lost in a state of confusion, he recognizes the voice, yet he doesn't, it sounds like his younger sibling, but he know's Jason doesn't have the balls to talk to his big brother that way.=- Mayhem:'Jason? If that's you Jason remember what happened the last time. You took a swan dive off the roof of a Home Depot. Don't get big on me now.' Voice:'Silence yourself you self proclaimed Lunatic. There's no need to know who I am right now.' Mayhem:'Self proclaimed?' -=starting to seeth in anger=- Voice:'Did I stutter old man?' Mayhem -=furious=- 'Old man?' -=Mayhem gets up from his corner and moves towards the window and looks out. Nothing but moonlight and frozen desert.=- Voice:'You can't find me, no use trying, Mr Lunatic.' Mayhem:'Whoever the hell you are, I never gave myself the name Lunatic.' Voice:-=Laughing=- 'Ahh yes, that's correct. President Walnut did, right before you branded his youngest son with the scar of the crucifix on his chest with a branding iron. Ahh, yes those were the days. Too bad you lost it.' Mayhem:'Lost it? What makes you think I lost it?' Voice:-=screaming=- 'You lost a no holds barred match to a fucking pip squeak half your size!!! You use to own the no holds barred match! You goddamn lost it it!' -=Mayhem begins to breath heavy, he's wearing his emotion on his sleeve.=- Voice:'Good get angry, where the hell was the Mayhem I use to know? The Mayhem that felt no pain? The Mayhem that enetered a battle royal number 4 and threw out single handedly 20 other wrestlers? The Mayhem that caused havoc and fear in the hearts of millions? Where the hell is he Bryan?' -=Bryan growls, grabs the bars and begins to shake violently, until he pulls one bar loose from its conrete foundation=- Mayhem:'Right here goddamnit!!' Voice:-=laughing=- 'Good, now prove it Thursday. I want Rollins' remains when you're done.'
