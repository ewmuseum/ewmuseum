1.4 - Family

9 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/09/11 “I am not looking for excuses War, I have no need for excuses. I wish only to see results.” – El Diablo 1 Even though it was late the lights were still on when Miguel pulled his car into the driveway. He pulled the black Ford Fusion to a halt at the end of the driveway and killed the engine. The lights of the car dimmed to black as he opened the car door and stepped out. The neighbours dogs were barking, but those yappy bastards were always barking. Miguel double-clicked the car’s alarm button and heard the car beep to tell him that it the alarm was now armed. He breathed a heavy sigh and reached into his back pocket as he made his way towards the front door. He grabbed a pack of gum and pulled a stick from the package. He quickly unwrapped the mint-flavoured gum and popped it into his mouth. Angelina never liked it when he drank after work, and being this late, he already knew that she was going to be upset. He tried the doorknob, but as he expected it was locked. He quickly fished his keys out of his jacket pocket and slid the key into the front door. With a quick twist, the tumbler aligned and the lock turned. He quietly pushed the door open and stepped into the warm light of his home. He quietly closed the door behind him, doing his best to not alert his fiancé to his presence. The smell of today’s dinner still hung in the air. Something obviously Latin was still simmering in the kitchen and it definitely smelt delicious. Angelina was from a large Mexican family and was a big fan of Mexican cuisine. She was a traditionalist and would always have dinner ready for her soon-to-be husband, although he often wasn’t around to enjoy it. Miguel followed his nose and the scent of peppered ground beef and waded his way towards the kitchen. He hadn’t even taken two steps into the kitchen before she noticed him. She had some kind of sixth sense for detecting Miguel’s whereabouts, like some kind of fiancé GPS tracking. “I don’t know how you sneak up on the bad guys like this,” she said, a tinge of playfulness in her voice. “You’re footsteps are so loud and heavy I could hear you coming from outside.” Miguel said nothing, he simply let a smile wash over his face and made his way quickly to his fiancé. She was washing dishes, and although she was dressed simply; in sweat pants and one of his old Police Academy shirts, she was as beautiful as ever. He stood directly behind her and took in everything about her. She was stunning and perfect in every way. She was his rock. No matter how shitty work could ever get, coming home to Angelina would always make Miguel feel better. Standing behind her, he smelled the soft scent of fruits in her raven black hair. He ran his hands over the soft skin of her shoulder and neck, rubbing her. He leaned down and planted a soft kiss onto the back of her neck. He continued running his fingers along the soft curves of her body, working his way down to her stomach. He ran his hands over the small lump in her belly. “How are my favourite two ladies?” Miguel asked. She turned on him quickly, a playful smile on her face, but he sensed something might be wrong. There was something amiss, she looked at him sternly for a second. She craned her neck towards the fridge and Miguel followed her gaze. There was a bright pink Post-It Note taped onto the fridge. “Someone called for you,” she said. “Someone from work I guess, says his name is Erwin.” Miguel let his guard down for a second, allowing the slightest of frowns to crawl onto his face. Angelina looked at him with curious eyes. “What’s wrong baby?” she asked, leaning in to kiss his neck. The feeling of her warm lips against his skin was enough to erase all of the thoughts about his day at work from his mind. He simply forgot everything and allowed the moment to swallow him. It was after days like this that he wished he could just quit the force, wished that he could live only for her. He wished that he could spend every minute, of every day doing nothing but loving his wife-to-be. He shook his head, and took a deep breath. She looked up at him and those dark blue eyes of hers didn’t get any warmer. An inquisitive look came over her face, as she waited for him to respond. “Baby,” she started. “Miguel, talk to me...” “It was a rough day at work, we had some problems,” Miguel began. “I don’t really want to talk about it, but there’s been some setbacks in the case that we’re working.” She seemed satisfied with that answer for a few seconds, she was never one to question him. He was always honest with her, he had no reason to lie to her. He had always told her everything that he could about his job, everything that he could without endangering her or breaking the privacy laws of the country. But, almost instantly a look of anger crossed her face. “You’re chewing gum,” she stated matter-of-factly. “So?” Miguel asked. “You want some?” “No I don’t want any gum,” Angelina said. “I want to know why you’re chewing gum, were you out drinking?” “I went for one with Tommy after work, I told you sweetie it was a rough day,” Miguel explained. He thought about going into the details of the case, but figured it wasn’t worth getting her worried about his safety by mentioning what happened to Agent Rios. “You know I hate it when you do that baby,” she said with a frown. “You were home late last night too, is that where you were? Out with Tommy drinking?!” Miguel leaned in to kiss her, but she turned away. She was always like this when it came to drinking, like a wounded animal. Her father had been a big drinker and it had cost him his life not long ago, when liver cancer took his life. Years of drinking weakened his body, and then cancer moved in and finished the job. She had never been accepting of the bottle since seeing it literally take her father away. “Baby...” he stammered, trying to reach out to her. It was too late, she spun from his grasp and stormed out of the kitchen. She made her way towards the stairs, and began stamping up them. “Dinner is on the counter,” she called. After that he heard the door to their bedroom slam shut. Miguel breathed a heavy sigh and slumped against the counter. His shoulders sagged and he almost felt like collapsing. He hated fighting with Angelina, but it would do him no good to get her worked up because of a death in the unit. And he especially wasn’t going to tell her that he was there to witness the death last night. He looked at the dinner but he wasn’t hungry. His eyes continued to make their way across the room, finally landing on the Post-It on the fridge. “Fuck...” he muttered under his breath, before reaching for his cell phone. 2 The bar was loud and crowded, as it always was. The mass of bodies were grinding and gyrating to the blasting bass of the techno-house music. The man known to most as War pushed his way through the mass of bodies. There was a mix of reactions as he stormed across the dance floor. Several of the women looked at him with horror, recoiling at the sight of his scarred and weathered face. Most of the men backed down at the mere sight of the Sergeant at Arms of Los Aztecas. Finally through the sea of tangled bodies, he made his way towards the back stairs of the nightclub. He climbed the stairs and came to the office at the top. He didn’t even bother knocking, El Diablo had summoned him here. He simply walked into the room and saw the back of a large black leather chair. War closed the door behind him, the sound of the door barely audible over the noise of the club below. The black leather chair turned quickly, and he was brought face to face with the masked leader of Los Aztecas. “El Diablo,” War said calmly. The man known as El Diablo was a mystery. He always wore a mask, the actual reason for which was widely speculated but totally unknown. He was from somewhere in Mexico, that much everyone knew for sure, but beyond that the only thing that people knew about the leader of Los Aztecas was that he was a ruthless tyrant, but a savvy business mind. “We have a problem,” El Diablo began calmly. “It seems as though our ranks are being infiltrated. The police and the FBI are clearly interested in us, but this is an important time for us. I am tasking you to build me an army, but if you cannot do this without issue, I will find someone who can.” El Guerra said nothing, he knew that there was no response needed. He watched as the leader of Los Aztecas stood and walked towards the back window of the office. He looked out the window, but continued speaking to his Sergeant, albeit much softer this time. “Build me an army, War,” he began. “But do not fail me, I don’t think I need to remind you of the consequences for failure.” Again War, knew that there was no response needed. He didn’t even bother to say anything, he simply stood idly waiting for the president of his club to release him. “How is our informant in the police department? How is it that this infiltration was allowed to sneak by our eyes?” El Diablo asked. “I am not looking for excuses War, I have no need for excuses. I wish only to see results.” “Captain Toms relayed the information along, but he is beginning to grow quite the attitude. He was also unable to take care of our problem inside the County Prison,” War answered. “Perhaps then, that Captain Toms needs to be reminded of what his duties are,” El Diablo said coldly. “Or perhaps we need to be rid of him. The decision is yours.” With that El Diablo turned to stare at his SAA, War nodded his understanding of the President’s words. And with a wave of his hand, El Diablo released him from his audience. 3 Miguel sat in silence and allowed his mind to wander. There was a lot on his mind, he still couldn’t get over his conversations with Tommy and Unit Chief Erwin earlier in the day. He also couldn’t get the haunting image of Agent Rios being gunned down in cold blood out of his mind. He couldn’t believe that Captain Toms; the man who had been his boss for nearly three years now, could be responsible for the death of another law enforcement agent. Tommy couldn’t believe it either, and was appalled that Miguel had even considered the thought. But still, Erwin had raised a good point, there clearly was some kind of leak in the unit. How else could they have found out about Agent Rios so quickly? But who could it be? The possibilities raged through his mind, conspiracy theories running rampant in his head. It was like mental torture and he could barely endure it. He shook all of the thoughts from his mind and took another sip from the Budweiser on the table in front of him. For what felt like the millionth time in the past thirty minutes, his eyes wandered over to the Post-It note on his fridge. He stood and made his way towards the note, and snatched it off. He looked at it with his mind still in a haze. He slumped back into his seat at the kitchen table and once again reached for his cell phone. He had already dialled the number several times, but closed the phone before hitting the Send button. Once again the FBI Unit Chief’s words from earlier in that day rang through his mind. ”Can I count on you?” Erazo let those words echo through his mind a few more times before dialling the number once again. This time there was no hanging up, this time he hit the Send button. He pressed the cell phone to his ear and waited as he heard the phone dialling. 4 FBI Unit Chief Erwin was sitting in his living room. The television was humming in the background, the evening news playing on the screen with the sound muted. The entire room was shrouded in darkness, the dim light of the TV the only source of light in the room. His eyes were closed, but he wasn’t sleeping, at least not yet. In his hand there was a nearly empty bottle of cheap Whiskey. He dragged the large bottle to his mouth and took down another long mouthful of the aged liquor. The liquid burned the whole way down, but Erwin revelled in the pain. Today had been the toughest day of work for him in he couldn’t remember how long. He hadn’t lost a man in his command in years, easily a decade since the last Agent was killed under his watch. He thought that he was okay, but after one day of work, he had realized the actual toll of Agent Rios’ death. There had to be a leak, of that he was sure, but who could it be? Captain Toms was the logical choice. He was a high-ranking officer, but at times he seemed to live slightly outside his means. He also had a severe power complex, and he could see the hatred in his eyes to see that he wasn’t in charge of this case. Toms hated having the FBI in his backyard running the show and everyone knew it. But was it enough for him to go rogue? To get dirty? Tommy Chase was another candidate. He was the polar opposite of his partner Erazo. Where Miguel was loyal and hardworking, Chase was mysteriously quiet and kept most of his true agenda hidden. But, that seemed to be the M.O for most of the officers under Toms’ command. With the exception of a few, most of them hated having the FBI around and wanted to be working the case themselves. The list of candidates continued to flash through his mind, but in his drunken stupor his logic was beginning to fail him. He was ripped from his thoughts by the vibrating and buzzing of his cell phone. The phone rang and buzzed on the night table and Erwin looked curiously at the phone. He reached over to the phone, his hand resting on the vibrating piece of technology, lingering for a few seconds. With his other hand he took another sip of the Whiskey and let the liquor burn his throat once again. He looked at the screen and saw the caller ID, seeing the name M. Erazo on his screen. The phone continued to buzz as he simply stared at it. Erwin shook his head and put the phone back onto the table, allowing the call to go to voicemail. He looked at the bottle in his hand and saw that it was down to its final few mouthfuls. It would be enough to end this night and put him into total blackness. He said a prayer for Agent Rios and his family, before swallowing the last remnants of the bottle. 5 Miguel listened as Erwin’s voicemail picked up on the other end of the line. “FBI Unit Chief Kevin Erwin. I’m unavailable at the moment, please leave a message.” Miguel snapped the cell phone closed and took one final sip from his beer. He left the empty bottle sitting in the middle of his kitchen table, not even bothering to clean it up. He had an upset wife that he needed to cheer up. He snuck into the bathroom and quickly brushed his teeth. He quietly snuck up the stairs and creeped towards his bedroom. He opened the door slowly, making as little noise as possible. The room was already dark and almost completely silence. Only the sound of Angelina’s soft breathing broke the silence of the night. She was wearing a long evening gown now, the red silk exposing just enough skin to remind Miguel what had originally attracted him to his fiancé. Miguel stripped out of his shirt and jeans, and tossed them silently into the laundry basket. He slinked towards the bed and carefully crawled into bed beside Angelina. The bed was warm, her body radiating heat across the entire surface of the mattress and sheets. He reached his arms out and draped his muscular arms around his wife. She didn’t make much noise, but she turned and curled into his warm embrace. He looked down at her, admiring her in all of her peaceful beauty. He kissed her forehead and brushed her hair from her face, she let out a soft sigh and snuggled herself closer to his body. Miguel wasn’t tired. Or maybe he was, but he couldn’t sleep. There were too many thoughts running wild in his mind. But he was comfortable, and he needed to enjoy this time with his family. He ran his hands over her belly, hoping to maybe feel the faintest kick or movement, but he felt nothing. Still he was comforted in the thoughts of what that small bump in Angelina’s belly would become. He let those thoughts takeover and light up his mind. His brain went from thoughts of death and conspiracies, to picnics and family vacations at Disneyland. The thoughts brought a sincere smile to his face and put him at ease. He closed his eyes, and focused all of his love and energy on his family, trying to shut out the darkness of the past few days. He felt happy, he felt safe. And on that night, that was all he needed. He would face the horrors of the world tomorrow, but he tonight he slept safely in the arms of his fiancé and in the warmth of their love.
