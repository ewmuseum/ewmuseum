A Recap of Events (Correct RP)

21 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/21/08 For months Delylah had been plagued by horrible dreams. Dreams that a twin sister she never knew existed had planted in her subconscious. On the day of her birth, Dr. Elias Nagatti and Amun Nguyen were bound together by a secret, a secret that until recently had not surfaced. Delylah was a twin; her father and the delivering doctor conspired together to allow Dr. Nagatti to take the second child as his own. As far as Nebt-Het understood the child was lost due to birth complications. Now Delylah, Paul and anyone they cared about felt the wrath of her scorned twin, Cleopatra. Delylah decided to make a trip home to confront her father and try to put an end to her sister’s torment. When she arrived at her parents? estate in White Plains, New York her mother was distraught. Delylah found out that her father was missing. Nebt-Het told her that he had left for work the morning prior and never returned home. As much animosity as Delylah had towards her father she knew that she needed to find him, before Cleo sunk her fangs into their father’s tender flesh. Delylah tried to ease her mother’s worries telling her that she would find him no matter what. She knew that she had to keep Paul away from all this but Cleopatra had another agenda. Cleo had visited Paul that night and only he knew where to find Amun Nguyen. She knew Delylah’s love for Paul would cloud her senses. Cleo wanted everyone to pay for the sins of her father. She wanted everyone to feel the pain she had felt all of her life. January 14, 2008 The Nguyen Estate White Plains, NY Delylah awoke to a light tapping on her bedroom door. She sat up slightly and bade the person on the other side to enter. When the door crept open there stood Paul. Tears filled Delylah’s eyes out of happiness and partially out of fear. She had wanted to keep Paul as far away from this situation as possible. In her heart though she knew that he couldn’t stay away. Paul crossed the lilac colored carpet to Delylah and wrapped his arms tightly around her. She knew that something was wrong. She could feel his body trembling against hers. “What’s wrong Paul?” Delylah murmured against his cheek. “Why’d you come here after I told you not to?” “I couldn’t stay away. I know where she has them.” Paul said breathlessly. “Them” What do you mean them?” Delylah asked becoming alarmed. “Your parents.” “NO!” Delylah screamed, jumped out of bed and ran down the hallway to where she had left her mother napping. Paul followed quickly behind her. Delylah flung the door open to her mother’s bedroom but the queen sized oak canopy bed was empty. She ran to the bathroom that adjoined and Nebt-Het wasn’t in there either. When Delylah exited the bedroom Paul handed her a note that he found pinned to her mother’s pillow. Now I have them both dear sister and soon I will make them my children. Hurry Delylah and suffer the fate of our parents. Cleo Tears streamed down Delylah’s cheeks as she read these words aloud. Paul rushed to her and tried to embrace her. She pushed him away. “Don’t touch me! Do you want to wind up like my parents?” Delylah screamed at him. “Del, calm down. I made a promise to you that I’d be by your side through all of this. No matter what the consequences.” Paul began, “I know where she has them Del. I am the only person that can help you now.” With that being said Paul left Delylah with her thoughts. She flopped down on the bed and began to sob uncontrollably. Her heart was being torn in two, she knew that she couldn’t do this on her own but she didn’t want to endanger another person she loved. Finally she dried her eyes, she knew she wouldn’t be able to stop Paul from coming with her so she resigned to her fate and went looking for him. They sat and plotted and planned the entire night, trying to find a way to take Cleo by surprise. The minutes ticked by like hours as they waited for dawn. They knew their best chance would be to try and sneak in while she rested. It wasn’t a guarantee but it was the only thing they could come up with. Paul refused to tell Delylah where they were headed for fear that she would leave with out him. As dawn approached they hit the road with Paul in the drivers seat. Paul urged her to take a nap to regain some of her strength for the battle they both knew lie ahead. Finally after fighting to keep her eyes open she drifted off into slumber. It was pitch black and the smell of fresh blood filled her nostrils. She heard the screams of the predators prey and her flesh crawled as if it were covered with thousands of spiders. Delylah made her way toward where the screams had originated. She followed a dark passageway that opened into a large room with concrete pillars and no windows. There she saw both of her parents bound to chairs with leather straps, their eyes covered by blindfolds. She knew she had to be quiet; she crept along the far wall hoping to come up behind them and free them. As soon as she reached their chairs she was stopped in her tracks by the all too familiar voice of her sister. “I wouldn’t do that if I were you sister dear. If you look closely at them you will see that they are very hungry.” Cleopatra said as she laughed maniacally. Delylah looked down at her parents and both of them had fang marks in their necks. “They have already begun to change sister. I do have a present for you though.” Cleo smiled, “I know how much you love surprises.” It was then that Delylah saw Paul bound to a pillar in the center of the room. His eyes looked empty, as if someone had stolen his soul. Delylah let out a scream of horror as Cleo laughed chaotically. Delylah’s eyes snapped open as the car came to a halt. They had stopped in front of what looked to be an old abandoned warehouse. Delylah knew the place; it is where she and her few childhood friends had played hide-and-seek. She stepped out of the vehicle and stood there a few moments wondering why Cleopatra would pick a place that she knew so well. “I know this place Paul,” She told him, “I used to play here as a child.” “Well, that’s a plus for us then.” Paul said a little confused. Delylah knew that her sister would know every inch of this place by now. Cleo had this planned for a long time. “We need to stop and think about our attack plan Paul. She isn’t dumb, she familiarized herself with every aspect of me. That is why she picked this place.” Delylah sat down on the ground, as did Paul. They went over and over their plans. “We will wait until night time to strike Paul. She’ll never expect us to come in while she’s awake.” With that being finalized they decided to camp out in the vehicle and wait for nightfall. A time they had been afraid until now.
