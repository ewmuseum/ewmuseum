Enjoying A Free Meal

22 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/22/11 The atmosphere is jovial as families, couples and friends enjoy each others company over dinner and for some a few too many drinks. Everyone seems to stay in their own world, paying little attention to the people not at their table in the bar...though a pair of women sitting at their own table garner increasingly longer glances from everyone. Both women are blessed with an alluring skin complexion of caramel, a gift from the older woman to her daughter. The mothers long dark brunette hair flows down her shoulders and the back of her figure hugging and shoulderless purple dress. Her jewellery consisting of her gold hoop earrings, a diamond necklace and her wedding ring all sparkle under the low lighting. Her daughter shares the same luxuriously beautiful hair colour and curvy figure which is accentuated by her white baby tee, a mini skirt and her denim jacket. They laugh together as Carmella casts a flirtatious wave at one of their many admirers. “I cant believe your still single Carm” says Gabrielle “You've always had the attention of all the boys” “None of these boys are interesting enough” Carmella responds. “Boys, men...they've always been far too easy.” “Well the easy ones do come in handy from time to time mom” Just as Carmella had said that the young man she had waved at approaches her, he takes the time to eye up Gabrielle who looks far younger than her motherhood would suggest before he smiles at Carmella. He opens with “At first I was going to come over here to make sure God knows one of his angels fell to earth...but now that I'm here I need to make sure he knows two have fallen” He stares over at Gabrielle or more precisely her 34DD breasts tightly wrapped in her dress though she just snickers at his pick up line. Carmella though smiles and playfully caresses his tie as she looks up at him. “How sweet of you...though trust me my mother would be a little too much for you” she retorts “Though you can still help us out...our drinks are almost empty and we'd love another one.” “Sure thing” he replies before grabbing both their wine glasses and proceeding to the bar. Gabrielle just giggles as she amusedly shakes her head. “Seems everything I taught you wasn't a waste after all” “Like I said the easy ones serve their purpose” “When is the last time you paid for a drink Carm?” Carmella just giggles as she glances over at the man who had approached her earlier and is now begging his friends for enough money to afford their expensive drinks. “Like you said mom, I'm just doing what you taught me” “Well it was meant to be for your wrestling career...not that I was pushing you towards one.” “No of course not mom” Carmella playfully exclaims “Dad and Jen gave you your fill of 'wrestling' once you retired I'm sure.” “Well I did still have so much energy.” While most people would be disturbed by such a conversation with their mother Carmella was in fact not. Somewhere along the way after realising her family wasn't exactly traditional, and the humming sword she found under Jenny's pillow, or the fact that she wasn't related to most of her supposed 'Aunts' she had become almost desensitised to her parents sexuality and even embraced it herself. “You're lucky though Carmella, coming into a place like GCW where you don't need to parade your looks around to get ahead. Things were very different for me” Gabrielle sighs “even with two female World Champions before me in the FWA I was still looked down upon for being a woman there...things seem to be different for you Carm. A World Championship shot in your very first match...it took me years of being ignored to get that” Carmella smiles and grasps her mothers hand reassuringly “Hey don't worry mom when me or any of the other Saints win I'll let you touch it. She giggles playfully as her mother feigns shock, already knowing all to well the sense of humour she inherited from her father. “I'm not the first Ignito or Cortez you've heard that from am I mom?” “Oh please your father doesn't simply 'let me' he still giggles like a schoolgirl when ever he comes over.” Carmella smiles for a moment before her demeanour shifts “I almost feel bad having such loving parents...sure my family's not exactly normal but Micah and his father, then Colin and Lexy's father...” “They all turned out fine, perhaps even better for it; stronger at least me and Jen gave you and your brother the best life we could...though it was after struggling that I finally got what I wanted...now you have that chance don't waste it feeling bad about your life...besides its not like I haven't sinned a few times.” “Defiantly not, most people would be counting the stitching on their straitjacket after seeing some of your sins” “Well it taught you to not go looking through our private videos didn't it Carm?” The two women giggle as their new drinks finally arrive in the hands of their very hopeful admirer, Carmella smiles at him and then slides a piece of paper into his pocket as she whispers into his ear, and he walks away happy and high fives his friends as Gabrielle shoots her a disproving glance “Don't worry mother it wasn't my number...I just suggested to him it was beneficial for him if he also pay for our meal.” Carmella giggles and takes a sip of her wine, savouring the taste and the feeling of manipulating another man to her will so easily.
