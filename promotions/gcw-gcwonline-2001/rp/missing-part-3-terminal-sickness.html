Date: After part 2 It looks as if my journey is almost over. I stand outside the airport, the little taxi driver friend groaning at my feet, blood pouring from his nose. He won't remember a thing, which will be a good thing, I've had enough adventure for one day, the last thing I need is some cops asking me why I would do such a thing to an innocent person. He's far from innocent. He beats his wife, and burns his children with cigarettes when they misbehave. How do I know? A child of abuse knows a villain better than the villain knows hes a villain. I gather up more strength and move my massive frame into the front door. I am carrying nothing of metal structure on me so the alarms do not blare, however everyone standing inside seems to have set off their internal alarm as they glance at me. Parents pull their children closer to them, and airport security quickly mumble into their walkie talkies. I know this game; all hands on deck; we might have a problem. For the first time in my life I am not a problem, I want to get on a fucking plane and head back to England, nothing more nothing less. "Excuse me, where are the International Flights?" I ask a very tiny bag clerk. He looks up at and almost scoffs and points, than scampers away. Gee thanks, that way, you are a great help. I should have snapped his neck. I probably would have grabbed him by the throat and squeeze until his head popped like a plastic ziploc bag that has been filled with air and stepped on violently. There, however, were way too many people around. "Bryan Mayhem would have done it pussy. This shadow of what use to be just let the little fuck go." "Please not here." I mumble silently. I just want to make it back home undisturbed. Little did I realize, that once I make it on that plane, my hopes of an undisturbed trip are for naught. I begin the long trek down the hall of terminals. I pass many notable names USAir, Continental, Delta to name a few. Each one I pass people continue to stare at me, as if their death is imminent. I will not harm these people. "Come on, what's wrong with a little bit of airport violence. Just imagine the headlines, 'Former Wrestling Great Snaps in Airport, hundreds dead.' Sounds good doesn't it?" Sighing, "I am going to ignore you, and when I get home, you will be banished from my head forever." "Sir, hey big man. Who are you talking to?" I neglected to realize that my last charade with the voice was a little louder than I intended it to be, and those security guards with the walkie talkies have been following me the entire time. I stop and turn around. I do not give them an answer, because if I do so, my trip to England may involve shackles. "You got a name buddy." "Bryan." "How about a last name?" "M-O'Neil." "Well Mr. O'Neil where are you heading, and most importantly where did you come from?" I really had no intention of answering these guys, but I felt if I could just tickle their fancy they might let me go. Who am I to kid though? People are staring at me because of how I look. My face feels swollen, the blood and mud is probably caked on my face like some crazy Hieroglyphics prophesizing the Apocalypse. "I was in a fight. It's ok, I am just heading home now." "Fight? Who in their right fucking mind would mess with you? Where's home fella?" "England." "Some accent you've got there. Reminds me of Cleveland, with a touch of Chicago. Last time I checked England was in Europe, and I have yet to hear you call me a chap." The other security guard lets out a little chuckle, than it quickly disperses as fast as it starts. Why is this happening now? "Look, I feel like shit, I just want to get home. I have money, my passport, and ID in my wa" Fuck my wallet! "Where buddy?" I can't believe it. I have nothing on me, my wallet is gone, for all they know my name is Abdar irk Halamahirj, and I have every intention of cutting the throats of all the stewardesses and highjacking the plane. "Bryan! What a great idea! Get rid of these two doughnut ridden hacks and let's go have fun!" I exhale slowly and close my eyes. I start to count to ten to calm myself when the other guard speaks up. "You going to answer him buddy?" "I must have lost my wallet in the fight." I felt a tremble in my voice. I am not going home, I am going to be accosted by these fucking pieces of shit." "Hmm, that's interesting. How does one plan to book a flight to England with none of the essentials? Good Looks? Looking at you I would hope you are smarter than that. Come with me my friend." They go to grab my wrists, and chances are the second they do they arms will be separated from their sockets until my savior appears. If I believed in a higher power I would have said angel, but since their is no such thing, my savior appeared. I did not realize he was my savior until I was halfway to England. "Mr. Mayhem. can you sign this?" This little boy, not even 8, was holding my GCW trading card. On the card I am kneeling in front of Belmont, staring into the camera. It's an old one, but a good one. He puts the card with a black Sharpie into my hand as the guards look at him confused. "Hey kid, what are you doing?" "Sorry, but I have met almost everyone of the GCW stars except for him, and i just want an autograph. Can I get one please?" "Wait kid. You are saying this guy is a professional wrestler?" "Yeah that's Bryan Mayhem. The oldest brother of legend Jason O'Neil. Bryan has had many good fights." He looks up at me, I almost feel a sasquatch with this child."My mom wouldn't let me watch you and Knight in Belmont, but i looked it up on Youtube." With that he smiles at me and glances back at the guards. "Holy shit! The Bryan Mayhem? Man I am so fucking sorry. I didn't realize it was you, well because you aren't wearing the face paint. But wait, you said you were in a fight, did you file a report?" I had no lie to come up with, well thats until my savior spoke up for me. "Yeah he was buried alive by Andy Murray. Bryan, GCWonline.com says Caldera fired you. Is this true?" People were looking at us, and a crowd was starting to form. To be honest I heard nothing about Caldera's firing of me, but it does not surprise me, that man is a billionaire snake and will eat his own children to succeed. I made that man so much fucking money, figures he would fire me after my most embarassing loss. But nevermind Caldera I had to act quickly, so I played the victim. I put my head down and nodded. "What? Mr Caldera fired you?" I nodded again and took his card and signed it. I gave it back to him, and he did the strangest thing anyone has ever done. He grabbed my leg and hugged it. "I am a big fan Mr. Mayhem. You are Hall of Fame in my book." That made me smile, and gave me an excruciating headache as well, because I know it pissed that voice off something furious, because I felt a boom between my ears. The security guard came up to me and said "Fuck Caldera man. Next time GCW in town I'll get his car impounded, I promise. As for you, I tell you what, sign this for me and I'll get you back on that plane to England." I looked at him, and he can tell I was in shock. People are not usually nice to me because of my in and out of ring antics. I still had the notion in my head that I wanted to smash his skull like a grape, but those thoughts never leave me. I signed his card for his son, and followed his overweight, major love handles, greying, balding fat ass down the terminal until I reach AIRTran. Not exactly First class, but I'll deal. They talk to the lady at the ticket counter. I see all three of them peering over at me periodically, than she nods as the security guards walk back towards me. "You are all set. Hey good luck man." He puts his hand out for a shake, and than thinks otherwise and pulls away. The ticket lady waves me towards her and tells me I am in C4, and to enjoy my flight. I sneer at her, and duck my head as I enter the walkway. I can't believe it. 8 more hours, only 8 more goddamn hours, and I will finally be home!! Little did I know, that I was about to embark on the longest 8 hours of my entire life.
