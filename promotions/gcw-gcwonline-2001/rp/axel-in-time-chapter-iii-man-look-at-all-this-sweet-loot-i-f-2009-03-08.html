AXEL in Time- Chapter III: Man, look at all this sweet loot I found!

8 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 8, 2009 "Death shall come on swift wings to him who disturbs the peace of the king."- Curse inscribed on the tomb of Tutankhamun. The desert; a place where cacti thrive and men die of heatstroke and dehydration. It’s a place where the heat that radiates from the sun is magnified nearly tenfold by the sand that it bares down upon. The desert sands have claimed thousands of lives, man and animal alike over the course of time. The majority of those lives are taken by sandstorms, which usually leave their victims buried underneath the sand to suffocate and mummify in the desert heat. The desert in question, though, does not have any such luxuries as cacti for wandering travelers to cut open and drink from. Oh, no. This desert is completely barren, save for sand dunes and the occasional dried-up desert plant. Through this barren wasteland trudges AXEL Action, his body still heavily bandaged from the wounds sustained in his struggle through the bamboo forest in China. Flesh chafed and blistering from the desert sun, he pushes onward, sights set ahead on what appears to be a small town in the middle of the desert. “Ah, some shelter!” AXEL picks up his pace, attempting to walk faster, but his feet sink into the sand with each step he takes. Each step buries him ankles-deep in burning hot sand, and if he wasn’t wearing shoes, the flesh would probably be baked off of his feet by now. His body perspires, practically dripping with sweat. His shirt is soaked with it, and it isn’t helping his burning flesh at all. In fact, it’s doing nothing but making it worse. The faster he tries to move, the harder the sun seems to beat down on him and the more difficult it becomes to breathe. Chest heaving, trying to breathe but only wheezing, he fights onward towards that town in the distance. “This is just my luck” First, I nearly get ripped to shreds by thorns in China, and now I’m stuck in the middle of a desert in Bumfuck, Egypt. Can my life get any worse?” He doesn’t know just how right he is. Unbeknownst to AXEL, he is trapped in the middle of Egypt, and his life is going to get much, MUCH worse by the end of the day. He finally arrives at the little village, stopping just outside of it to catch his breath. Calm and collected, though in a lot of pain, he takes a step forward and enters the village. AXEL, completely unaware that it’s a mirage, looks around, smiling and waving at the townsfolk whom reciprocate his kind gestures. Taking another step forward, he tries to enter the town but soon finds himself tumbling head over heels down a sand dune. Anyone who knows anything about anything knows that the average human body isn’t made to take large falls and rough tumbles. Down he goes; his head and neck contorted in various ugly positions as he falls. Luckily for him, his wrestling training has given him an abnormally strong neck and? “GOD DAMN IT!” He cries out in agony as he pratfalls down the sand dune, body contorting in awkward ways as he does so. Almost as suddenly as he had fallen, he crashes into something solid and sturdy, causing the last bit of oxygen that was left in his lungs to escape. He lays there on the hot desert sands, his flesh scorching and his body writhing, gasping for air. The air that he does manage to pull inside of him is hot, and it doesn’t do much to sate the pain in his chest, nor does it fix the lack of oxygen he’s been suffering for the majority of his trek through the arid locale. He forces himself to his feet, groaning and grunting in agony as the desert sands burn hot against the palms of his hands. Once he’s regained his vertical base, he leans against the wall that he crashed into while gathering his wits about him. “Why is it always me” Why can’t I just have a normal life like everyone else? Is it because I spent all that time hanging out with von Doom? I swear, if I ever make it back home, I’ll kill him!” It’s a normal occurrence for AXEL to blame his misfortunes on James von Doom. After all, he’d lived a relatively normal life until he joined von Doom’s cult, The Church of the Unholy. That’s when things started going wrong for AXEL. He lost what could have been an EXCELLENT lay to Rick Astley, then he was sucked into a time portal and dropped off in China. Now, he’s in Egypt, lost in the middle of a desert with seemingly no way out. He happens to glance up at the structure he is leaning against, only to find that it’s a pyramid, and that the front entrance is open. Stroking his chin and lost deep in thought, AXEL decides to go exploring to see what he can find inside. Stepping through the entrance, he stumbles over what appears to be a drop off, or an incline. He manages to put on the brakes and stop himself before breathing a sigh of relief. ?Alright, AXEL; You’ve got to watch your step in here. You don’t have a light or anything to help you see where you’re going.? Those are the words that echo through his mind as he steadies himself at the top of the “rampway”. Making his way down the incline, he heads further into the pyramid which, much to his surprise was semi-lit with torches placed here and there on the walls. He examines his surroundings on the his way to the center of the pyramid, looking at all the hieroglyphics and pictographs on the walls. His eyes widen in disappointment as he nears the center of the pyramid, only to find that the path branches off into three directions: Left, middle and right. This puzzles AXEL’s brain, because he remembers from all the lectures his high school History teacher gave that if he were to take the wrong path, he could end up lost inside the pyramid forever and that it would become his tomb. ?Which way?” Do I go to the left? Or do I go to the right? What if the middle path is the right one?” AXEL examines his choices as he tries to decide which way to go. Figuring that the most logical thing to do would be to use the process of elimination (read: Eenie, meenie, miney, moe), he narrows it all down to the path on the right-hand side. He takes a deep breath taking small, slow steps down the path, careful not to step on anything that might trigger a path. His body trembles with fear, which is an emotion that he doesn’t often feel or succumb to, as he embarks on what could be a treacherous journey down the stone pathway. He’s startled by a loud rumbling when his foot presses a switch in the floor. Instinctively, he drops to the floor to avoid any poison darts or deadly arrows that might fire from the holes in the walls. Waiting a few minutes to make sure he is completely safe, AXEL glances up to notice that a door had opened to reveal a fully torch lit room. This room was full of golden idols, golden coins, jewelry and all sorts of other expensive-looking trinkets. In the middle of the room sits a large, golden sarcophagus into which intricate designs are carved and painted. He stands up and makes his way into the room, wide-eyed and slack-jawed. “Whoa?” Whoa, indeed. There had to be millions’no, BILLIONS of dollars worth of valuables inside the tomb; and AXEL, being human as he is, can’t help but lust after some of it. Common sense soon stops him in his tracks, though, as he remembers that there’s always a curse on tombs such as this one. Weighing his options, he thinks for a moment, debating whether or not to snag some of the gold. He shrugs and grins, filling the pockets of his jeans with gold coins and trinkets, careful not to take anything that would weigh him down too much. His gaze happens to roll across the face carved into the upper portion of the sarcophagus, and his mind begins to wander back to the shows he’d seen on the History Channel. He instantly recognizes the face to be that of Paramessu, better known as King Ramses I. AXEL wasn’t the least bit surprised that the time rift had put him this far back in history. In fact, nothing could surprise him anymore. At least not after recent events that have left him stranded in random places in time. Coming to the conclusion that he’d taken enough loot and that there was no real curse, he began heading towards the exit of the room. As he reaches the exit, another loud rumbling ensues, and debris begins tumbling from the ceiling. “Just when I thought this day couldn’t get any worse” Why me?” The ceiling falls in on the room behind him, and he realizes that he needs to get out of there; and quickly. He makes a mad dash down the hallway, sprinting towards the exit. Somehow, he manages to get turned around and lost on one of the other paths. He takes a look around at his surroundings, trying to figure out where he is when the light from the torches dies. Letting out a disappointed sigh, almost as if he’d expected something more from his cruddy luck, he turns quickly in place to see if he can spot the sunlight coming in through the main door of the structure. Once he spots it, he heads towards it, with the words, ?Move towards the light, AXEL. Move towards the light!? ringing in his head. Bearing in mind that the pyramid is still crumbling around him, he bolts towards the door, darting up the ramp as fast as he can. “Almost there! Just a little further and I can” Gah!” He’s cut off as he trips over his own feet, falling face first into the blistering desert sands. Getting up to his knees, he spits out a mouthful of sand, wishing he had some water to wash the sand out of his mouth with. He turns to witness the pyramid practically caving in behind him, his lips curling into a big, Cheshire grin. He laughs hysterically, celebrating the fact that he escaped with his life and some of the loot, his body no longer perspiring due to the water nearly being drained from it. Weakened and in a considerable amount of pain, he forces himself to a standing position and resumes his attempt to find civilization in that wasteland of a desert. Dragging his blistered and bruised carcass across the dunes, he stops as a faint buzzing sound echoes within his ears. “What the?”” Just as quickly as he manages to get those words out, he’s swarmed by locusts. He blinks, rather bewildered by what’s going on, but he doesn’t waste much time standing around. In fact, he makes a break for it, running like a madman through the desert, arms flailing wildly in an attempt to swat the bothersome creatures away. He opens his mouth to scream, but all that escapes is the sounds of gagging and choking as locusts attempt to fly down his throat. Spitting them out, he figures it better that he keep his mouth closed and keep running; hoping that he will come across a rift that will take him somewhere away from the accursed pests. Six months ago, AXEL Action didn’t believe in mummy’s curses and the like. However, after being attacked by angry locusts which seemed to come out of nowhere after he snagged some of King Ramses? sweet loot, does he believe now? You bet your sweet ass, he does.
