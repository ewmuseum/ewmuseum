I Will Win

Date: 9/10/09 _,.-*' The music hits. The fans go BOOOOOOOO. Chris Bagwell walks to the ring wearing cool glasses and a "I'm the best" t-shirt. He smiles. A fan gives him the finger. He beats him up. He gets in the ring. He takes the mic. He beats up the ring announce. A ref tells him to stop. He beats him up. He talks and everyone shuts up.'*-.,_ CB: |[" Andy Murphy, I'm tired of you being the champion and now its my turn. At Warpath, I'm going to kick your @$$ all over the arena. I'm going to kick it in the ring and then on the outside of the ring and then back in the ring and over and over again until you finally give up and give me what's rightfully mine. I'm going to be the greatest World Champion you people have ever seen and there's not a damn thing you can do about it because I'm the best there is, the best there was, and the best there ever will be. And that's the bottom line because Pebble Cool said so. "]| _,.-*' Fans boo.'*-.,_ CB: |[" You all boo now but soon you'll have no choice but to love me when I kick Andy Murphy's @$$ all over the place. And do you know why? Because Andy Murphy's mom is so fat she has her own zip code. You're not even half as great a wrestler as me Andy Murphy "]| _,.-*' A fan yells his name is Andy MURRAY not MURPHY.'*-.,_ CB: |[" IT DOESN'T MATTER WHAT HIS NAME IS! "]| _,.-*' Chris Bagwell throws down the mic and goes and beats up that fan. He leaves the ring. He's the next World Champion. Fade to black. '*-.,_
