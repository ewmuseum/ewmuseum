Basic Training: Part 1

24 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.24.05 Fade?. “Slug bug! No slug backs.” A young boy yells as he nails the other boy sitting in the same seat with him in the arm. These boys are in the second to last seat on a bus. The last two rows are all laughing it up and having a good time. The background outside the window that passes quickly is a hilly country side. The green hills are set against a grey and cloudy sky. They aren’t major hills; rather they are gentle, rolling hills, gradually moving in a repetitive up and down motion. The favorite seat on the bus, the last seat on the left side, being only a single, is taken up by the frame of a much larger boy compared to the others. “You guys are so fucking immature.” The larger boy says. He laughs and shakes his head as the six boys around him laugh as well. The larger boy isn’t larger by strictly weight. No he isn’t simply fatter than everyone. No, he’s flat out BIGGER. Even sitting down the boy is noticeably taller than the six boys around him. Not only is he taller, but his upper body is much larger. His arms are a good two to three times the size of any other arm near him. His simple blue t-shirt is pulled tight against his chest, well-defining his pecks. Other than the t-shirt, the boy is also wearing a pair of green cargo pants. A small duffle bag sits at his feet, which apparently isn’t too out of the ordinary, because the other boys have the same. “Immature” Who was yelling “vagina” as loud as he could about an hour ago?” The boy sitting up and to the right says laughing. The larger boy chuckles as well, wiping the serious look off his face. He nods in agreement. The seven of them all begin chuckling. They seem to be the only ones having a good time on the bus. All the kids from the third to last row forward just sit staring out the windows of the bus. Some even turn occasionally to shoot dirty looks back to the other boys. “What the hell is their problem?” One of the boys in the back seat asks, pointing a finger to the people sitting ahead. Most of the “clique” of seven shrugs their shoulders in response, but one boy, sitting across the aisle from the bigger boy, pipes up. “I know why.” The boy says with a small smirk on his face. The remaining six boys all turn towards the boy, showing interest in what he has to say. He remains quiet and staring out the window at the rolling hills and darkening sky for about a half a minute, causing the larger boy to speak. “Well what the hell” I command you to speak, dammit!” He says, causing a bit of laughter from the other boys. Finally the boy turns his head back to the interior of the bus. He takes a deep breath and leans forward, lowering his voice so as not to disturb the people past their little group. “I was first on the bus. Most of the ones towards the front aren’t here by choice, like us. They’re either here because of a parental punishment or by judge’s decision. Ever heard of that” A judge sending you here?” The boy says swiftly. As he asks his question, a puzzled look comes over his face. He looks to the other boys for help. “I’ve heard of them sending you to a camp before. But not this kind of a camp.” The second boy in the back seat says. The boys around him mutter small things about the situation. Their speech soon dies down, bringing a small hush to the group. This goes on for about fifteen seconds or so. “I’ve been wondering. How old is everyone?” One of the boys in the second to last seat on the right side asks. “I’m eighteen.” “Nineteen.” “Eighteen.” “Me too.” “Make me number four in the eighteen category.” “Seventeen.” They all answer in succession. All but one. The larger boy sits content on listening to the boys. He smirks as the last boy blurts out his age. Causing the boys to focus on him. “What’s the smirk for?” One asks. “How old are you?” “Twenty-two.” He replies. The other boys are taken back by this, not really knowing what to think. They begin to talk amongst themselves, not to the bigger man. One pipes up though. “What the hell are you doing on THIS bus?” One of the boys asks curiously. “I was already in the?” The larger built man gets cut of by the sound of the bus? brakes screeching to a halt. The big man looks out the window, across the gravel road they sit on. He sees a sign set in the foreground, the grey and gloomy sky set as a backdrop. Fort Leonard Wood, MO Basic Combat Training Facility That’s what the sign reads. The group of seven in the back all turn to face the front of the bus as the driver stands and looks to them all. “All right boys. This ain’t home anymore. You’re in the ARMY now.” He says firmly. Mere seconds after he finishes, a man dressed in Camouflage fatigues steps onto the bus. He has everything. A whistle, one of those great big hats, and even the attitude that goes with it. “On your feet!” He yells. “Off the bus, on the double!” He yells again. He steps off, and the boys begin filing off the bus. The group of seven turns to look at each other, letting out their own respective deep breaths. They wait as the people in front of them file off the bus, and eventually they too walk forward down the aisle, and straight into the real world. Fade?
