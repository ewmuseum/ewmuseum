A Girl, An Egomaniac, and a Sidekick (Part 8)

16 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/16/08 (takes place 9/2002) 9/2002 "I can’t believe I fell for that!" Had she been trying to disguise her embarrassment, Tessa Windsor was doing a poor job of doing so. The only thing the five foot four diva was managing to do at this time was drawing attention to herself and her icing covered face. It’s not like it mattered anyways at this point. She’d already been embarrassed just seconds ago in front of a crowd of thousands, not including those that were watching at home. How could she possibly think that Chris Storm would call her out to the ring for anything of good nature? The only thing he’d ever done for her yet is humiliate and degrade her. Yet na’ve little Tessa, as she saw it in her mind, always had to put Chris on a damn pedestal. She hated him, but at the same time, she admired everything he was. He never put up with anyone’s bullshit, something she wished she was capable of. Instead, she found herself dealing with his bullshit day in and day out, all because she wanted him to have the same amount of respect for her. Clearly the layer of icing on her face showed how that went. Luckily, waiting for her just a few feet away from the curtain was the man who was currently winning her heart over, Erik Erratic. Down the hall he came running, like a knight in shining armor, with a hand towel in hand. "What a prick." Immediately, Erik went to work on Tessa’s face with the towel, "I can’t believe he would bury your face in a cake like that." Tessa waited until the towel was lifted away from her face to give her bitter filled response, "I can’t believe I thought he was actually trying to cheer me up with some kind of celebration. I mean honestly, why in the world would he celebrate a loss? Yet stupid little me thinks he’s actually going to go out of his way to do something for somebody else." Erik doesn’t buy her self loathing one bit. Instead, he wraps an arm around her shoulder and pulls her along down the hall, "I personally think he wanted to give you that lap dance. I mean, it’s probably the closest he’s gotten to a classy woman in awhile. As far as the cake, well, I’m surprised he even knew your favorite kind was strawberry. I wonder how he managed that." Tessa wondered that as well, for a very short bit, until an epiphany struck her, "Markus." "Markus?" "Yes, damn Markus." She sighed; obviously a tad bit annoyed that Markus had given this sort of information to Chris, "We were talking about dessert the other day. He was looking to eat somewhere nice, and the topic came up." "Oh?"Erik had no clue what to say, so instead he switched topics, "Well just try and forget about it. You’ve got better things to think about, such as being one of the best fucking stars in WIW. What do you guys say?" Pointing to the large group assembled at the catering table, they turn towards Erik to see what the commotion is about. "Don’t you guys think Tessa Windsor could kick the ass of anyone who stepped into the ring with her?" Sympathetically, they nodded; knowing that Tessa’s evening hadn’t gone very well. "See." Snatching a sandwich from the table, Erik took a bite, then smiled over at the woman he knew was falling for him, "Everyone knows you fucking rule. Why can’t you see that?" Again, they nod along with Erik. "As a matter of fact," Erik shouting rang throughout the hallway, "I personally believe she could kick the crap out of our world champion, Chris Storm, any day of the week." The small crowd cheers at the words coming out of Erik’s mouth, which catches the attention of others further down the hallway, including the aforementioned Chris Storm. Down at the other end of the hall, Chris can’t help but to grin at these words. He nudges his best friend Markus in the ribs, hoping to egg him on, "You hear this crap Markus?" "I think she’s calling you out Chris." Markus egged back as expected. "Of course she is." Chris stated, running a hand through his golden blonde locks which hung down to his shoulder, "It’s the only way she knows how to. That girl doesn’t know how to do anything on her own, and well Erik wants to get laid, so he does it for her. It’s fucking pathetic." Markus certainly wasn’t beyond teasing his own best friend, "You only say that because you want to get laid by her as well." Chris couldn’t deny that, "What kind of man wouldn’t want to get laid by a virgin?" "You think she is?" "I know she is." His grin grew wider, "Just look at the way she smiles, that says it all." "A bit obsessed?" "Not at all." Chris blew it off with a gigantic chuckle, "If I wanted, I could be in her pants right now. But that’d ruin the fun of things. It’s much more fun when you lead them on." "I think you’ve got other plans." Markus let out his own series of chuckles. "Like?" "Aren’t you the one that wanted to marry her?" Markus quickened his pace some, just incase a punch was to follow his question. "I’d do that if I meant I got to have one evening of hot sex with her." Chris said, carefully studying Tessa up and down, "Other than that, she only wishes she could walk down the aisle with me. Think of how much she’d brag to her friends? Marrying me would be better than marrying Prince Charming." Markus played along with a dose of sarcasm, "Right. She totally digs you." Chris stops in his steps, calling out to his partner who continues to walk towards the crowd,"Are you doubting my awesomeness Markus?" Markus looks his partner straight in the eye with a rare serious stare, "She’s a prude Chris. Prudes don’t sleep with men like you." Chris took this up as a challenge, "Just watch me work my magic Markus." Not moving a step, Chris called out towards the crowd in front of the two, hoping to catch Tessa’s attention, "Hey Ms. Hot Shit, I saw the way you were looking at me out there when I was giving you that lap dance." Tessa looks back up, mortified, "Look. You got one on me, great for you. Can’t we just call things even?" "I don’t quite call you getting a lap dance equal." Chris states, in his usual cocky manner. "You threw me into a damn cake!" She shouts in an unusually loud tone for her quiet self. Of course, Erik felt the need to interject, creating a barrier between her and Chris with one of his arms, "Leave her alone, you fucking prick." Surprisingly it isn’t Chris who swats his barrier away, but rather Tessa, "Don’t worry about it Erik. I’m on my way out anyways." Her body swings to face the opposite direction, immediately marching to the exit. "Tessa, wait!" Erik calls out, chasing behind her. "I’m fine Erik. I’ve got to get home, my niece is waiting for me." "Promise?" He asks, looking her in the eye. "Promise." She smiles before parting with a goodbye kiss. The minute the door shut behind Erik, Tessa sprinted towards the 2002 Nissan Altima she’d rented out for the evening. She was hoping to escape before commotion found its way back too her, but this time, she was too slow. "Leaving so quickly?" She looked over her shoulder and caught glimpse of the man she was trying to escape just minute ago. She should have known that Chris wasn’t going to let her off the hook that easy. Now she was wishing that Erik had tagged along. "Not now Chris. I’ve got a kid at home waiting for me." "A kid?" Somewhat shocked, he looks up at her with his icy blue eyes widening some, "so you aren’t a virgin?" Quickly, she mounted up a defense by raising her voice up into the squeal of a very agitated young woman, "It’s my niece!" This only fuels Chris, "You’re quite defensive. Any reason why?" "This is not the time or the place for this talk." As much as she tries to keep her voice at a stern tone, it comes off as nothing more than a meek attempt of defense, "I’ve really got to go." "Well you won’t be going too far without this." Tessa looks up in time to see her gym bag flying towards her. Quickly she takes the few necessary steps forward to keep it from smacking into the ground, "You asshole! Why were you in my locker room?" Chris shrugs, leaving her at suspense, "The minute you leave, you surrender that space. Far as I’m concerned, it was public property." "You little asshole!" Little did she know, she was setting herself up for more harassment there. "I would hardly call myself little. Just ask the other women around here." As if it were scripted, one of the makeup ladies walk by and wave to Chris, which only angers Tessa more, "Besides, you ought to be grateful I did. You’re the one dumb enough to leave her bag behind." He had her there, "Fine. Thank you for bringing me my bag. Happy?" "Not quite." He had her just where he wanted her. Alone. Why would he give that opportunity up? "Crowd seemed pretty into you tonight. Everyone seems to like you hanging around backstage, especially that pre-pubescent Erratic." Even though he seemed to want nothing more than casual conversation, Tessa still saw him as nothing more than a pig, and responded accordingly, "That’s because unlike some people, I’m not full of myself." "Or," Chris interjected, "it could have something to do with that fact that most of those drooling stage hands want to “hit that” as they say" He rolled his eyes, looking away; "I mean not that I can blame them. But I swear every time Erik gets around you; he has to spend fifteen minutes in the bathroom alone afterwards." Those were certainly things Tessa did not want to hear, "Right. So the only people reason like me is because they want to sleep with me. Is that what your problem with me is?" "You think I’m that childish? Please," he asked, "give me more credit than that." She pauses for a moment, to think of all the reasons why that wouldn’t be his problem. She comes up with nothing, "You’re jealous! That’s why you follow me around all the damn time to try and make me miserable. You’re freaking jealous!" "Jealous!" he shouted back, nearly choking on the words, "why in the world would I be jealous over that? For starters I don’t need any groupies hanging off me backstage, I have a World title. Plus there is my money, my fame, my billions of adoring fans who can’t wait just to see me." Tessa’s eyes roll the entire time he goes on. Once finished, she looks up at him with the ultimate question that would totally null and void all that, "If you’ve got all that, then why do you insist on following me around? Clearly you don’t like me and you certainly don’t seem to need my assistance any." Chris straightened up, looking directly at her now. "I’m here to offer you the opportunity of a lifetime." He allowed the words to linger in the air for a moment. Automatically, she wasn’t interested. Not after a night where he’d humiliated her by luring her down the ring the ring for a lap dance, only to smash her face into a cake afterwards. "It’s already a no." He couldn’t say he wasn’t expecting this kind of response from her. "You didn’t even let me speak. You should at least listen to my offer. Trust me; it’s no cake and lapdance." "This better be good." She stated; hands firmly placed on her hips. His naturally cocky aura shone through like a flashlight in a dark room, for lack of better "Oh it will be." "Go on then." She ordered as she juggled the keys to her rental back and forth between hands. "I have a side project, out side of wrestling, which I get some extra money from. I was wondering if you would be interested in it." Tessa looked at him with a hint of interest, so Chris continues his sell, "I know wrestling pays good, especially here, but it never hurts to have a bit on the side." Curiosity started to get the better of her at this point. Though short, her time in the company had been harder than she expected. The idea of a bit additional money had came up with her before, and there certainly wouldn’t be any way she would ever ask her parents for the money. Still, she did not want to sound too interested. "And so what is this little side project?" Extending his arms out to his side in a pose, Chris flashes her his smile. "Well you see, I just so happen to be the feature star of PPP, and I think you would be excellent for our next project." "PPP?" Tessa muttered, trying to place where she had heard that before. "Isn’t that the name of that wrestler Shorty’s company?" Shorty was a fellow wrestler with the company who went by the name of Shorty the Pimp. Out of all the people on the roster, he was without a doubt the most popular, so much so that not even Chris complained about it. Tessa had heard mentions and whispers of him having a company, but never could get anyone to expand on it. Chris, though, looked all the more willing to share with her. "Pimpin? Porn Productions. And I think you would be perfect for our next movie." She didn’t give Chris so much as another word. Instead, she marched away to the driver side door and climbed into the car, with a very angry glare emitting towards Storm from the rear view mirror. In it, she sees Chris nearly doubling over in laughter. She had half a mind to throw the car in reverse to double him over in something much more humorous to her, that being him in pain. Instead, she lets her head fall back against the seat as she lets out a heavy sigh. She really was beginning to wonder if anyone would ever take her seriously, or if for the rest of eternity, she’d be nothing more than a damn piece of eye candy.
