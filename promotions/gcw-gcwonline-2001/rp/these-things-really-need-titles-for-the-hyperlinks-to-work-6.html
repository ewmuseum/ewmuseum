Date: Timeless Welcome to Crystal Lake, GA. With its small population and near non-existent crime rate, Crystal Lake is often referred to by its residents as 'The best kept secret on earth'. Since it's founding in the early 1900's, Crystal Lake has been a haven for those who enjoy the small town feel. You know, everyone knows your name; everything operates like clockwork every day or night of the week. All the children go to school, and achieve good grades. All the houses and lawns are kept up, the attention to detail everyone in town paid was nearly surreal. Punctuality was key for the inhabitants. At 8 O'clock, The kids were sitting in class. At 9 O'clock, the parents were at work, and at 3 AM every Tuesday Morning, an armored car rolled in from the Bank of Atlanta, its engine killed in front of the Crystal Lake credit union nearly on the dot. Every week for the past two years, Gregory Cramitz and Sean Reynolds would make the trek up from Atlanta to fund the smaller credit union. Their reverent awe for the odd town never left them, even after 600 or so weeks on the job. As the secured vehicle coasted down the quiet darkened streets, Reynolds lamented this from the passenger seat. 'This heap is a Cultural Oddity if I've ever seen one,' he said. Sleep lines formed beneath is eyes, making him dread the trip back where he'd be in the driver's seat. 'Look at this, are you seeing this man? There's not a single car on the street. Nothing amiss. Something out of a horror movie!'. Clearing his throat and extending his right index finger, Cramitz replied 'It is pretty creepy, it seems as if that place is the only one in town not completely perfect.' Two pairs of eyes simultaneously drift to the house in question. It stands out, uncut grass, chipped paint. A beat up for sits idle in the driveway. The sort of place that brings down the property value of other houses in the vicinity. With the odd house out purged from their minds, the Armored Bank Car pulled to a stop in front of the darkened credit union/bank of Crystal Lake. Cutting the engine, both men removed their seat belts. As they went to exit the car, two audible thuds emanated from the rear, shaking the entire car. 'What the FUCK was that?' Reynolds exclaimed, as Cramitz reached for the Shotgun rack behind the two seats. 'I Dunno, I'll check it out.' he said, cocking the shotgun and exiting the vehicle. Fear began to creep up the spine of Sean Reynolds. At Thirty-One, he'd been driving the same truck bank to bank on the nightshift for five years, his partner Cramitz for ten. They'd had attempted robberies a couple times, but nothing the bullet proof car couldn't deter. He leaned over the driver's seat, trying to hear what was going down and if there was any scuffling or commotion. Nothing. Seconds later Cramitz returned, rifle in hand. 'Must'a been a cat or sumthin'.' he said, as Reynolds nodded nervously. 'No cat could make that sound', he thought silently to himself. Cramitz pulled his door shut, motioning to Reynolds to exit the truck. 'Go unlock the back so I can pull directly to the door and get the hell out of here, this place gives me the creeps.' he said. Throwing the passenger door open behind him, Reynolds replied 'You've got that straight, I want to get you-'. BOOM. He never finished his sentence. Before the words left his mouth, a shotgun exploded from behind. Greg Cramitz could only watch as Reynolds chest exploded, sending flesh and blood to spatter against the windshield. His torn corpse fell forward into the seat, revealing the individual in a ski mask outside the door, hunting rifle in hand. He couldn't scream, speak, or anything. The shocked Gregory Cramitz could only stare as the Murderer cocked his gun, ready to kill his second driver of the night. Throwing the drivers side door open, Cramitz stumbled to the pavement ready to stand and run as fast as he could. He never noticed the second Gunman until it was too late. As he got to his feet, two rifle shots blasted this stomach as he collapsed dead on the street. ************** 3:05 AM The eerie silence is broken as a large Greyhound bus pulls into the Crystal Lake train station. Too small for its own Bus Depot the Greyhound's that came through every other night just parked there. Normally the bus sat for 20 Minutes, no one leaving or getting on. Crystal Lake wasn't a popular vacation resort, and those who habituated the small town weren't fans of bus travel, apparently. However on this night, the mechanical doors opened. Two individuals with no luggage stepped out as the driver spoke. 'End of the line for you's two.' Both shadowy figures looked around, one of them replied 'Atlanta sure looked different the last time we saw it.' With a heart chuckle the Driver began the shut the door. 'Probably because this isn't Atlanta. This'd be.. Crystal Lake, as far as I can take ya for 42 bucks, some rolling papers and pocket lint.' he said. The door sealed itself and with a roar the large bus pulled off and into the darkness. 'Fuck.' responded one figure. His dark hoodie obscuring any facial features. The two figures sat, dumbfounded at their misfortune. One was born Theodore Moocherson, the other Vincent Bammstein. They joined as a unit to form GCW's Favorite Tag Team, Mooch and Bammy. 'What the fuck do we do now?' Shouted Bammy, the darkness his only listener. Mooch looked in each direction, before responding to his heterosexual life partner. 'Hmm, nothing we can do now. We should collect some money and try to foot it to Atlanta ourselves.' Walking around the bus station they searched for any sort of cash their stoned as could find. As they hit the street however, they saw something. A car. An empty car. An empty Armored Truck. As they approached the vehicle, they didn't see the two dead bodies lying next to it. They only saw the back, slung wide open. Stray bills decorated the street and truck, and our two heroes' quickly hit the scene. 'MONEY!' Bammy exclaimed. He picked up a few hundreds as Bammy collected bills from the street. The robbers had left quite a bit of cash in their haste to flee the murder scene. As our heroes collected the dough, a cyclist past. Shocked, she hopped off the bicycle and whipped out her cell phone. Mooch and Bammy quickly ran to a house. A particular house. One with uncut grass, chipped paint, and a rusty old ford parked in front. A safe house, for them at least. ************** 7:01 AM The two stoners sat. In the basement of their friend Tyson's house, as the local news played over the Television. 'Police have released a composite drawing of those responsible for the murder of Gregory Cramitz and Sean Reynolds.' Displayed across the screen were two familiar faces indeed. Those of Mooch and Bammy. Now Fugitives of the Law. TO BE CONTINUED.
