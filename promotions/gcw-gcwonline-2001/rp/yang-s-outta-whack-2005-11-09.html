Yang's outta whack.

9 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/9/05 'So, Kohai John...you are the bad guy. Is that right?' 'What is it that you ask, sensai?' Grand Master Chan Fo looks at me with his wise, old, tired Japanese eyes. He knows what I'm talking about. He knows what he's talking about. He just simply wants me to admit it. But guess what? I won't. 'My granddaughter tells me that you fight hard working, good people this week. She tells me that you are allied with those that do not know honor as I have tried to teach you. I wonder, Kohai John, if you have heard the words that I have spoken. If you know what you think you know. Are you, then, the bad guy?' Grand Master Fo isn't angry. Not even by a long shot. The man doesn't like to waste his time, however. And he's a big believer in the ancient ways of the 'honor' system and 'fighting nobly.' He doesn't agree with the Johnny Borealis that his granddaughter tells him about. The one that the world sees. I don't miss a beat in my exercises, however. I remain balanced on one foot on this rock in the middle of this serene pond in the middle of this gorgeous park in the middle of a tranquil world. Stand on one leg, throw a kick, jump up, land on the other leg. Lather. Rinse. Repeat. 'Define the bad guy, Master Fo.' 'It is he who oppresses the weak. He who fights without honor. He who fights needlessly.' Hold. Kick. Jump. Land. I've been getting particularly good at this exercise. It's supposed to build up balance, it's supposed to build up coordination, and it's supposed to build up a hatred for falling in the water in late Buffalo fall. It's freaking cold. I have to say...I like the feeling of the exercise itself. It's so much more interesting than pushups or chinups or any of the other stupid bullshit that I could be doing in the gym somewhere. I'm out here, in the real world, working on this. It's not some isolated, fabricated gym that I'm hiding in, hoping that the world doesn't see me. I don't hide from the world. I put all my money, all my concentration, in one place if need be. Then it's just a kick and a jump, and I'm replacing it with something else. Jump to your other foot. Land. Do it again. Story of my life. 'Well I never fight needlessly, sensai. It's my job. It's what I live by. It's what I get paid for. No different than Muy Thai fighters and no different than those who entered martial arts tournaments in years past. I ask that you do not mistake frequency with intention. I fight because it is my calling.' 'You're calling? Ahahahaha!' His hearty Japanese laugh catches me off guard. I don't see his walking stick coming at me until it whacks my leg while I'm in mid jump. There's barely any force behind it, but I still find my leg out in front of me and I'm falling backward, completely off balance. I land on the rock, which feels pretty damn good if I do say so myself, and fall down into the cold water while Master Fo laughs his head off. Freezing cold death embraces me. I push up out of the liquid fog and breathe air again and my half frozen eyes shoot over to the old bastard that knocked me in. 'Why the hell did you do that!' Grand Master Fo has always pretty much pissed me off. But, he is the greatest Kempo instructor in the states. He might be the greatest martial artist I'll ever have the pleasure of meeting. And in the last few weeks, he's completely reworked me from head to toe. Muscles that feel almost new, they've been worked so little, shrink up and cramp a bit while I try to climb out of the cold water. The breath I see might as well be fire that I exhale as he sits and smiles and looks up at the sky. 'The pond was calling you, Kohai John. Do you jump in to the ice? Do you forget who you are, what you're doing, and ignore the duty at hand to fall into the icy depths? You are cold, Kohai John. Not ice cold, as a killer. Not stone cold, as a warrior. Rather, you are shivering cold, like a child who has lost his way. He may be the greatest athlete or the greatest man or the strongest of will that you may see. He may have potential. He may have a chance. But a child he still is. And lost. You are a lost child, Kohai John. Because on one hand, you come to me to learn how to fight. And on the other hand, you already fight for a living! Out of necessity. Out of want. Out of hate.' Made my way out of the pond, I'm drying off with the towel and changing my shirt to something dry. Some girls walking by give me the eye. If I cared right now, maybe I'd give them an eye right back. Call them out. Let them know that they want Johnny Borealis more than he wants them. But right now, I need to get dry and listen to this crazy old Japanese bastard. I'm paying good money for him to throw me into cold water. It takes me a moment, as one might imagine, to state what it is that I have to state. That I need to state. I couldn't care less what any Jack Asshole in the world thinks of me. There's only a handful of people that I want to know the truth. The truth that I'm not some monster, like the fans think I am. And in two months, I may be worse in their eyes, or I may be their favorite. Their fickle nature makes it impossible to tell what status you'll hold in GCW, or any professional wrestling, a couple weeks down the road. But I guess I'll add Chan Fo to the list of people that I don't want to think I'm a monster. Because I'm not. And they deserve to know it. 'I'm not a bad guy, sensai. You know that. You wouldn't teach me if I was.' 'Wouldn't teach you...hmm...' The old man simply echos my words with a furrowed, thoughtful brow. 'It's just that I've come as far as I can get while playing 'Mr. Nice Guy' and following the bullshit stipulations that tell me what I can and can't do. The true master of his art expands beyond those stipulations, transcends those rulings until he is, indeed, greater than them.' 'Above the law...hmm...' 'No...no, not above the law, sensai. Rather, not being limited. Not allowing the signs that keep the masses in line to keep me in line. I'm not a child, no matter how cold and shivering you say I am, and thus I don't need a child's regulations. Crossing guards that help you cross the street and being told not to skip stairs while in school. Tell me why I am a bad guy, sensai. Tell me what makes Johnny Borealis different from The Marauder or Eva VanLorne or Ricky Thunder or Seymour Almasy or Chrissy-Poo or Mike Hardy or Rei Tiu or Michael Stevens in the eyes of the crowds and the masses. Is it because I realize the futility in pleasing people that will lose interest and move on to cheer on someone else? Is it because I have seen, if not first hand proved, that being anal retentive by the rulebook made by people who have forgotten what it means to be in the ring gets you no titles, no attention, and no glory?' 'Titles...attention...glory...' 'No! Not me. I won't be held down the way they are. Or maybe I'm different from them because of one simple, undeniable fact, sensai. I have the power. They don't.' The world is shaking. Wait. I am shaking. Out of rage on the inside, out of freezing on the outside, the entire world is out of equilibrium with me. Shaking. Convulsing. Crumbling around all that is. And yet, Grand Master Chan Fo seems to remain perfectly focused. Perfectly still. He radiates a sort of gravity that stabilizes everything around him. If he were to describe it, my 'chi' is raging and out of balance, while his 'chi' is balanced. He would say that I am a tempest and he is a gentle breeze. 'Power...hmm...' I guess for the first time, I have realized what I've said. But now is not the time to backpedal. Now is not the time to say something and then back down when it's thrown in your face. It's true. 'Face it, Grand Master...I speak truth. Who are those that are hated in GCW? LeStatt Knight, Global Champion. Bryan Mayhem, TV Champion. Aiden Jackson, Cruiserweight Champion. And Johnny Borealis, US Champion. How can you deny the obvious, sensai? The people hate power. The people hate whoever has the power, and they hope that those that do not have it get it. I was embraced, I was loved, by the masses when I did not have my title. And then, the moment I received it, I felt their knife in my back. And why? Because I did what I needed to do to get ahold of it. Just like every other title holder, I did what I needed to do to get ahead in life, and people can't take it. They can't take that that's the truth of the world.' 'I see...' I want him to get mad. I want him to argue with me and say that I'm wrong. I think a big part of it is because I so badly want to be wrong. I wish more than anything that everything I have ever said is bullshit. And maybe if the world wasn't the way that I say it is, a sort of faith would be reaffirmed. I wouldn't hate, I wouldn't distrust, and I wouldn't feel it to be necessary to keep parts of me from hitting the light. But he doesn't prove me wrong. And he doesn't prove me right. He's an old Japanese martial artist. He never answers anything, but finds it better for me to find the answers myself. For me to do everything myself. For me to train myself. For me to get ready myself. I always do everything myself. ...heh...I guess I do sound like a lost little child sometimes. 'The truth of the world...is that if you don't do whatever you need to do to get ahead...you won't get there. For whatever that's worth. For whatever it means to you. It means everything to me, I guess.' He nods again. Slowly. Thoughtfully. 'That which means everything is always that which will hurt you the most. You hold glory and remembrance higher than anything else, do you not? And yet, to become glorious, to become remembered, you must have those things for what are seen as the wrong reasons. You become hated. You learn to hate. And it consumes you as the snake consumes the hare. And soon, you are nothing like what you were.' Everything he says may be true. Everything I think might be wrong. But, we are so quick to pass judgement. Hate consumes, but hate also feeds. Cold freezes, but cold also preserves. Death ends life, but it also feeds new life. Who am I to say what is good and what is bad? And so who are people to point at universal truths, things that are evident across all of time and space and decree that something is evil or something is righteous, that something is just and something is unfair? Who are fans to judge the actions of someone in that ring? They are not above me to do so. 'I am beginning to think that these little talks of ours don't actually go anywhere, sensai. I feel like I'm taking lessons for a tremendous physical workout, a great using of unused muscles, and discussions that run around in circles and drive whatever sanity I once treasured out of my head. ...I'm going to get back on that rock. And this time, when your stick comes to hit me, I'll be ready.' 'That's the smartest thing you've said all day, Kohai John.'
