An Experiment, Part IV

Date: Several Days Before WW106 An Experiment Part IV: Even a Mad Scientist Miscalculates Average size for a male Face looks up from his notebook to study a man at the bar. He’s chatting with some friends and watching a hockey game on the big screen above the bartender’s trail. The man stands at about six feet tall and probably weighs somewhere between 180 and 190 pounds. “Check...” says Face, placing a checkmark beside a particular line in his notebook, tracing his pen down to the next line afterwards. In his thirties Face studies his potential mark further, noticing no real sign of grey in the man’s slicked-back black hair. The man laughs at a joke one of his friends just made, his face carrying no signs of aging past him prime. “Check...” mumbles Face once more. This time, however, he traces down the rest of the lines in his notebook, making a mental note of them. Physically strong...Straight...Doesn’t like gay men... Nodding to himself, Face puts the notebook away in his worn out, plaid suit jacket. He gets up from his booth and walks over to the bar, getting an odd look from a couple people on the way there. He steps up beside the man he was studying, waiting for him to turn around so he can start his closer studying. The man turns to look at him and immediately ? and unexpectedly ? slaps Face on the shoulder. “Physically strong...Check...” notes Face, who raises an eyebrow below his mask. The man looks rather excited to see him, as do his two friends. The reason for the excitement becomes very obvious. “Wow, this is actually happening!” exclaims the man, not taking his eyes off Face’s mask. “Face is actually at our bar!” “Well, would you look at that...” thinks Face as he realizes he’s managed to finally target someone who recognizes him. It’s a small miscalculation, but it’s one he knew would make eventually. The man directly beside Face’s potential target pipes in now. “Hey, we saw you at NC-17, man. You made that bitch tap out! She’s a tough one, too.” “I don’t know if I would make her tap out, but I wouldn’t mind pinning her, if you know what I mean!” adds in Face’s potential target, receiving a high five from his friend beside him. Face cringes at the clich?. “Straight...Check...” “You sure you wouldn’t rather pin a guy like Scott Stevens?” questions the other friend. He receives a punch to his shoulder for the comment from Face’s mark. “Don’t get starting with that fag crap again, Carl. You know how I don’t like touching that stuff with a ten foot poll,” points out the “Doesn’t like gay men...Check...” now confirmed mark. Face puts away his mental checklist and his new best friend turns his attention back to him. The man slaps his forehead, apparently forgetting something crucial. “Where are my manners” My name’s Jim and these are my two buddies, Bob and Carl,” says Jim, pointing towards his friends. “We’re big fans of yours, Mr. Face and-? Face quickly cuts Jim off before he can continue. “It’s just Face.” Jim is thrown off for a moment, partially by Face cutting him off so suddenly and partially from Face’s harsh voice. “Right. Well, we’re big fans of you, Face,” continues Jim. “The way you screw around with the minds of all of those opponents of yours before putting them to sleep and all is awesome!” “Your opinion will change very soon...” is the first response that comes to Face? mind, but he knows he’ll have to play cute for now ? or, rather, as cute as Face can be. After all, he can’t give away too much with those two friends hovering over him, especially since they know who he is. “Well, some people would call it sadistic and unnecessary punishment. Have you ever been entangled in a web that sucks the very life from you, Jim” Have you ever clung on in desperation; in a futile attempt to grasp at a last breath that will not come until you have faded to a place that, as far as you know, represents death itself?” replies Face, as cute as he can. Jim, Bob and Carl all just stare at Face for a few moments, completely caught off-guard and unable to even fathom an idea of what they might say in response to the completely casual and, thus, serious tone Face delivered such a dark message in. Even the bartender catches a bit of it and decides to head back down the side of the bar he just came from. Perhaps your opinions have already changed... is what goes through Face’s mind, an awkward silence now filling the area the four men occupy. The silence is finally broken by an unnatural, forced chuckle from Carl. This turns into a steady, equally forced stream of laughter from all three of them. Finally, it breaks out into a chorus of hysteric guffaws, the three men reassuring one another that Face was just joking and trying to scare the crap out of them. Having been convinced that Face wasn’t serious, Jim finally responds to the all-too-serious question. “Wow, you had us there for a bit, Face,” says Jim, wiping a tear from his eye. “No wonder you have all those people going through loops. You’re one creepy bastard when you want to be!” exclaims Bob, gaining the only unforced laughter thus far when Face chuckles at their attempts to rationalize. Images of Jim laughing off the fact that Face has bounded him to a chair in a concrete room in the middle of nowhere float through Face’s mind. Of course, he knows all too well that there will be no laughter at that point. Well, no laughter from Jim. Regardless, the chuckling is fleeting, as Face realizes he must remove Jim from his two buddies before he can get to that point. Complex plans begin to formulate in Face’s mind. He can’t just mysteriously disappear from the bar at the same time as Jim does. He can’t be the last one seen with him either. He’ll have to leave and then, somehow, catch Jim on his way out. Unfortunately, with the amount of beer Jim’s had thus far this evening, he won’t be driving himself anywhere anytime soon, which eliminates Face ambushing him in his car... “Whatcha thinkin” about there, Face?” inquires Jim all of a sudden. The notion of time had left Face’s mind momentarily with his engagement to deep thought. “I was thinking of how I was going to render you unconscious, before kidnapping you and leading you on to your eventual horrifying death...” “I was merely going over strategies for how I will render Mr. Woods unconscious at the next WorldWide,” says Face, a cruel, twisted grin growing beneath his mask when the men nod in acceptance. At this point, the bar tender has decided it is safe to approach the four men again. Bob is right on top of the opportunity. “Hey, can we get another round here, including one for our special guest over there?” asks Bob, pointing over to Face. Face quickly poops all over that party, though. “I won’t be drinking tonight. I’ll be heading out soon.” “Three more for you boys, coming right up!” says the bartender, nodding at Face’s objection. The objection grabs Carl’s attention, however, some electrical activity being displayed in his nicely doused brain. “So, what brings a guy like you to a bar like this, if you weren’t going to drink?” The question seems to induce a spark to both Jim and Bob’s noggins as well and now all three men anxiously await an answer. “To find someone to kill in my experiment tonight.” “I was in the area and looking for a place to think,” replies Face, the reply not peeling away the curiosity just yet, much to the masked man’s chagrin. Jim scratches his head, giving his mind some much needed power. “Is this really the best place to think” I mean, it’s kinda loud in here for that, isn’t it?” inquires Jim, now. “A bar like this is gives higher chances of finding an average size, average age, physically strong, straight person who doesn’t like members of his own gender who bat for the other team...” “I am extremely skilled at blocking out mental interferences, as you may have noticed in my short lapse earlier on...” replies Face, winning yet another round of nods from the trio. “Well, this deceit is beginning to bore me...” thinks Face, realizing it’s about time to leave so Jim can finish up his drink without more interference. “Anyways, it was a pleasure to find a person to kill tonight fans of my art, but I’m afraid I have some business to attend to now.” Jim nods, raising his hand for Face to shake it. Face accepts, taking it as a full agreement to participate in his studies. “Well, you teach that newcomer a lesson this week, Face!” bellows Jim, slapping Face on the back. Face simply nods before heading towards the door. Jim, Bob and Carl are loud enough that Face can clearly hear them discussing how “awesome” this night has been. As soon as Face leaves the bar, a quiet, but maniacal laughter escapes from beneath his mask... *Several hours later...* Loud and maniacal laughter fills the room, reverberating off of its hard walls. At Face’s feet are two men in an all-too-familiar setting. One of them is Jim, not looking quite as cheerful as he was earlier on. The other is a man not seen before, but from the tight clothing and tendency of Face to match up opposites, one can assume that the man was of average size, in his thirties, physically strong and gay. In actuality, this assumption was spot on. On top of this was the unfortunate amount of repression and prejudice the man faced for his sexual orientation. Of course, Face chose the two men for a reason. This particular scene stands apart from the ones previously seen in this room in its brutality. Unlike the other “experiments” that took place, this one seemed to last quite a long time. Signs of a hard-fought battle are splattered and strewn across the floor and walls of the room and, indeed, on the two fallen. A close comparison would be seen in the canvas of a master painter gone mad with the colour red. His laughter now slowly giving way, Face revels in the experiment’s “successful” results. “I’m guessing this isn’t quite what people envision when they say someone is “killing their fan base”...” crackles Face, twirling a bloody knife in his hand. His sense of irony is not lost in moments like this. It’s just like any other moment, really...except for the fact that there are two dead men at his feet and a murder weapon in his hand. Face doesn’t have to worry, though. The only miscalculation he made tonight was the assumption people still wouldn’t recognize him. He was able to avoid Jim’s friends, though, and pick Jim up after they had dropped him off at his house. Face begins to walk out of the room, tossing the knife off to the side, when he spots something out of the corner of his eye. Beside Jim’s limp body is a small piece of plaid fabric that matches Face’s worn-out suit jacket. Face walks over to the piece of fabric and picks it up, placing it in his pocket. “I suppose it’s true...One man’s garbage is another man’s treasure...” *The next day...* Bob and Carl sit on the edge of Jim’s driveway, not sure what to think of what has transpired. Their friend is missing, with signs of forced entry into his home. A few police cars are parked around the house, along with a lot of yellow tape and worried neighbours. Forced laugher can’t make this any less serious. There’s no way to spin a joke out of it. No, Bob and Carl are forced to go through possibly the worst side effect of Face’s research: a loss of someone that was very close to them, with no idea of how it happened. Bob looks over to his side, staring down at Jim’s lawn. Memories of Jim mowing it as he and Carl lounged around on Jim’s driveway flood Bob’s mind. He moves his head to look away, the memories quickly shifting into depressed and lonely thoughts. And it’s at this point that he sees a small piece of plaid, torn fabric. He reaches over and picks it up, poking Carl on the shoulder to grab his attention. “Would you look at that” Jim even lost that little piece of fabric he managed to rip off Face’s clothing last night.”
