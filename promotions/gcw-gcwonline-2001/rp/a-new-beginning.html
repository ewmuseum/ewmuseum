A New Beginning...

undated

Date: WW 114 Prologue "Sometimes when a hand is dealt to you, you must play it like poker. Sometimes you just need to change out a card or two, but sometimes, sometimes you need to change the whole hand." A silver New Jersey Transit bus zooms through the farm-side in New Jersey. The bus has only stopped a couple times along this vacant highway at old wooden bus terminals where a passenger would get on and only a handful would get off. It's not rocket science to realize that the further out the bus goes, the less passengers remain on the bus. A man remains on the bus from the Newark Terminal, glaringly out of place from the other passengers. "Life can be looked at as a house of cards. The species of the human being is very fragile, as I have learned. Outside forces, both physical and mental, can destroy this being. A simple cut or bruise or experience can forever change us. A house of cards is perfectly and beautifully built, but one simply change to the structure and...well, no house of cards lasts forever..." Scabbed over cuts on his face and burns on his arms draw attention from the other passengers which are usually private folk. This intruder troubles them because in every movie where someone different comes to a small town, trouble is on the prowl. This guy seems no different than those in the movies, only that this guy seems homeless. The bus slows down as it passes into town. "'Welcome to Salem'. Salem is nothing like Clifton or Newark. Salem is nothing like anyone would picture when thinking of the state of New Jersey, unless of course you live in Salem or in the surrounding areas. Something told me to get on the bus. I have no fucking idea why, but I did, and this is where the last stop is unless I want to go back up to Clifton.... There's nothing left for me there..." ******************************* The air is different. Outside of the industrial center of Jersey and away from the most densely populated areas of the country, the air sits different in your lungs. In the center of the old, quiet town, hundreds of faces turn his way as he steps off the bus. Something inside him makes him stare back instead of look away. A scowl morphs his face. He forces his feet to move. Along the main strip of the town is a variety of small town shops; hardware stores, drug stores, barber shops, etc. It is around twelve o'clock noon and the sun is bearing down on the open slits in his shirt and pants. The confrontation with Christian Thomas took a lot out of him, and the debris around the church before the fire tore into his clothing, not to mention the fire itself burning his hoodie and shirt below. All that remains from before is his gym bag and its contents, torn pants and shoes, and a tattered white tee. Maybe Christian was right, this is the start of something new, instead of the end of something old... He makes his way down the street further and sees a Salvation Army Thrift Store and he makes his way across the nearly empty street, eyes still following him. Once inside, the clerk shows no difference towards him as to any customer, service with a smile. Taylor barely looks at the guy while scanning the racks for the Men's racks. Once found, he quickly goes through for a large sweatshirt. Looney Tunes, G.I. Joe, Greenday, plain pink, plain purple...Finally, plain black. He then turns around to look at the row of jeans and picks one out at random. Further in the store is a bin of gym shorts and shoes. Taylor sizes up the shoes and picks out two pairs of shoes that would fit him for running and training and then moves onto the gym shorts. The first pair his picks up smells like cigarettes, so he puts it down and lifts up a pair of black shorts with green stripes. He holds it up closer and notices the "GCW" logo on the left corner and "Dangerous Games" written in script on the bottom. "Great. What are the chances..." The shorts don't fit so he just puts them back down and goes for the next pair, also GCW shorts, but these don't have a specific show or wrestler on them. Large. He grabs them and then another pair of plain black shorts. He takes the arm full of clothes over to the cashier and nods at him in acknowledgement. "Well, look what we got here! GCW!" Taylor's eyes tighten in annoyance of his recognition. He is about to say something when... "These were my son's. What a fan he was of GCW when he was a kid. Not anymore, no. He says they are a bunch of money hungry S-O-B's anymore. Ha, I mean, whatever that's supposed to mean. They all a bunch of entertainers! His favorite back in the day was some indian lookin feller... Oh, what's his name..." Shaman? Are you shitting me? How can that Indian bastard have any fans... "..err...OH! Knight! Lestatt Knight! That's where he got these shorts from! Knight was at this show, er, Dangerous Games or somethin. What a waste of money if you ask me, paying so much to see some guys touch each other..." Taylor grunts and attempts at a smile, but still holds his piercing gaze. The man takes a deep breath as he looks at all the prices and writes them down. "Alright, in total, it's going to be forty eight dollars. What a steal!" Taylor reaches into his pockets and feels around for some cash, but there isn't anything there. He panics and looks up at the cashier. The cashier now takes on the piercing gaze Taylor had moments ago. Right before he goes to say something Taylor unzips his gym bag and in sight is about $100k, and there is more below. The man's eyes light up, but Taylor turns around and pulls the bag away from the counter. He sees a smaller bundle that is loose and takes out a hundred. "All that money in there and all ya got is a damn hundred? Shit, I don't think I can break that...err..." The man fumbles through his pockets and register and comes up to meet Taylor with an unsure smile. "I'm sorry champ, but doesn't look like I can break that..." Taylor feels a pain in his stomach and fights an urge in his head to say..."Keep the change." Taylor grabs the bag with all the clothes in it, smiles appreciatively and walks out of the thrift store before the man can say anything. The gym bag begins to feel heavy, or more that he is aware of it more so than it has gained weight. He looks down at his hand and notices it is very tense at his side. He loosens his grip and watches his hand slowly gain its color back. He shakes his head and trudges on down the street. Nearly half an hour goes by as he reaches a house set off by itself on a large lake. There are several houses around it, but they are generously spaced out, and vacant. All three houses are up for sale with Remax signs in front of them with special inserts, "Recently Reduced". Taylor walks up to the closes house and pulls out a flyer from inside a plastic container. There is a phone number for James Calhoun, Broker on the flyer. Taylor looks around and notices a pay phone next to an old bathhouse on the edge of the lake. The phone is old and weathered, but still holds a tone. He dials the number on a quarter he found in the side pocket of the bag and a man answers. "Remax realty, Calhoun speaking. [faint] Jenny, would you knock off the vacuuming for a minute..." "I'm interested in renting the house by the lake fo...." "I'm sorry, but those houses are for sale and non-negotiable. If you would like to inquire about prices, I am more than willing to do so, but they cannot and will not be rented." The droning of vacuuming continues in the background. Taylor has an urge in his stomach and presses on. "Well, I'm interested in the house. Could you meet me here so I can go inside and take a look around?" "[faint] I'm about to make a damn sale and you're back there vacuuming a clean carpet, could you...thanks!" Mumbles too difficult to make out continue in the background and then fade. "I'm sorry about that. Yes, you've come to the right realtor, James Calhoun is your man. I can be there in about ten minute. I will bring the keys to all three houses...." "See you in ten." Taylor hangs up the phone and walks over to the bigger house in the center against the lake. The house is worn and needs work, but it will give him something to do and it would be great for privacy. The road is a dead end, capped off with the middle house and lake. A pay-phone next to the house and open land for whatever is needed. The house is a great step up from the church, but anything is better than sleeping in a pew or in the cramped quarters of Father Emanuel's room. He walks over to the curb and takes a seat. He feels a cloud of confusion in his head when he tries to think of the events of the last couple days. All he can think of is the image of Christian on the back of the cross, burning. The events leading up to it and the events after are all a mess. Every time he closes his eyes, he sees Christian leaning off the cross, staring at Taylor with bloodthirsty eyes. Other times, it is of Christian lifeless, with a smirk on his face. Others, well, there are others... He opens up the bag and sees the store of money inside. There are about fifteen bundles of hundred dollar bills. If the bundles' markings are right, there is about $300k in his bag. He pushes past a pair of shorts and sees gold at the bottom of the bag, but before further inquiry can ensue, a car pulls up. The car is a Mercedes Benz and the driver is just as flashy. The sight doesn't surprise him one bit, for he has seen many realtors that feel they are on the top of the food chain before in Clifton and surrounding areas. This James Calhoun reminds him a lot of Shane Skwalrek, until he opens his mouth. "You must be the man I spoke to on the phone. The name's James Calhoun, no relation to the president." He laughs at his own joke and then straightens up and changes his attitude when he notices Taylor doesn't find it amusing. "Yeah, anyway. Looking at, well, which house got your attention, my man? All three were owned by the same dude, but he died some years ago. Now they just sit here, vacant for years." "I was looking at the middle one, the one by the lake. How much?" "Well, I usually do a walk through first so we are..." "How much is it being sold for? It says reduced..." "Um," Calhoun picks up his folder and goes through a couple papers. "Well the middle one is... it was $200 thousand, but now... well! Looks like it is knocked down to only $100! What a steal if you take it, I mean, really, I can really hook you up with a sweet bank and get a sweet mortgage for ya..." Taylor reaches into his bag and starts to pull out some cash. Calhoun's eyes light up and his jaw drops. It looks as if his tongue would hang out if it wasn't for his incredibly large veneers. He pulls out a couple bundles which come out to around $100k. By Calhoun's greedy expression, Taylor knows he can work something more out of him. "If I were to tell you I have more of this money for you, what would you say about getting my hand into all three of these houses? You said it yourself, they are just weathering away, so I think that if the biggest house is one hundred, I think we can work out a deal for all three. I can give you cash...Say, two hundred for all three? You deal with the paperwork and make everything work where I just need to sign for it, and we have a deal." James wipes his lower lip dry and blinks heavily. "Well, yeah, yeah, yeah. I think we can make it... wow! I've never been paid in cash before. I mean, maybe a down payment, but never in this town. I think that this is...." "Mr. Calhoun, all I want is my privacy. I don't want to shoot the shit with you, I just want to get into a house, and that's it. I have the money, I think I proved that much, so if you would just help me out with this paperwork, I think we can get things rolling." Taylor sees Calhoun's eyes water up in excitement, so building onto it, he reaches into his bag and takes about a couple more bundles, equal in size, for about two hundred thousand. Calhoun reaches for the cash but Taylor pulls back. "We need to get some paperwork going, I think. Don't worry, the money is real, and it isn't going anywhere at all." James motions for his car and stutters, but no audible words come out. He walks over to the car, Taylor in tow, and goes into his trunk, but then stupidly walks over to the front and motions for Taylor to get in. "I will take you to the real-estate office and we will get everything under way. Yeah, yeah, I think we can work something out there. You're obviously not from this town, because shit, I know everyone, so I think it is safe to say, Welcome to Salem, New Jersey!" ******************************** "Salem, New Jersey. Great. It is better than the scum infested Clifton, and the area is definitely quiet and more private, so I guess it isn't that bad. Something led me hear, and I really don't know what, but I will figure it out in due time. Right now, I just bought three houses, a new set of clothes, and don't know the first thing about fixing up houses or doing my taxes. Damn, I really wish I was on better terms with Caldera, I'm sure he could really get my stuff in order, but now with Bagwell and..." The house is empty by most definitions of the word, but some of the rooms still have some old furniture and some dirty mirrors linger around the house. Over the last two days, Taylor had a cleaning service come into the house and take out all the cob webs and dust from around the house. He also ran into town to pick up a couple things like food and bathroom toiletries. The house looked like a house on the shell, but not on the inside. But coming from a church, this was still an upgrade to him. Without a match until World Wide 114, Taylor has plenty of time to get things done. Rumors have been circulating that Garbage Bag Johnny has been drinking himself into an oblivion every day, and training has been put on the back burner. Taylor is in the best shape of his life so there is no reason why he shouldn't be able to beat him at the Main Event. The only necessary training is to get in the position to be on top, but not of the US division this time, he is gunning for the World Title. Taylor wakes up on the bed roll in the center of the master bedroom at 314 Salem Road. The lightning wakes him up, and the rumbling thunder shakes the house. So far, no leaks have been found in the house, but there hasn't been a heavy storm yet. Every morning has been harder and harder to wake up. Through the night, he has stirred and jumped from his dreams, mostly of Christian, but last night was different. By the terms of the psychologists, it is a night tremor, but it feels just as real as laying on the bed roll during the thunderstorm on the lake outside. It isn't the first time that Taylor has had a dream about someone deceased, nor is it the first time to have a dream about the late Father Emanuel, but this one was different, must like the day after Father Emanuel died. Father Emanuel was angry, and maybe not so much in his tone, but his words. The words stung as they hit Taylor, and fear was instilled in him like never before. The words don't come clear to him anymore after his waking, but he remembers how Father Emanuel kept on mentioning Christian Thomas over and over. Upon waking up, he thought he saw Christian in the corner of the room, but he knows that is impossible. Yet he knows he killed Christian more than once, or was that a dream as well? The scabs over the burns and cuts are finally starting to minimize and go away, but his body is still in a lot of pain. The Rampage tournament really took a toll on him, not to mention the betrayal after the finals. They will all get their dues. Taylor is determined to train to get to the point where he can take them on, to end this bitter feud once and for all. If it has to be the end of Bagwell, so be it.... ******************* The lighting subdues, but the thunder still rumbles across the flats of the state. Taylor gets down to the kitchen and takes out a protein shaker and makes himself a drink. A book lays next to the microwave, "A More Muscular You" written by some name that is hard to pronounce but sounds like Turkish when said out loud. Next to the book is a written diet plan for himself, which has many changes on it scribbled around the lines of portions of food. Taking a final swig from the shaker, he turns on the water to clean it out and notices someone outside the window near his other house. Through the rain, it is hard to make out who it is, but Taylor wastes no time running out the back door and gives chase. Barefoot and shirtless, the former United States Champion runs off in the direction of the house. Upon reaching the house, he notices one of the windows is broken. He reaches into his pocket and unlocks the front door and makes his way through the hall and to the right where the broken window is. Several fleeting footsteps are heard upstairs, causing Taylor to quickly turn around. He notices the broken banister laying next to the door and grabs it as a baseball bat. Taylor slowly makes his way up the creaking steps and turns the corner into one of the bedrooms, but sees nobody in the vacant room. He then heads further down the hall when he hears a door slam in front of him and then the steady rumble of thunder follows. Taylor tenses up as he makes his way into the master bedroom where he heard the noise. Stepping into the room, he becomes silent and waits for movement or noise. He slowly pans the room. the thunder and waves on the lake break the silence, but Taylor is patient in waiting for a door to creak or a floorboard to break. Suddenly there is a creak beside him and a movement. He quickly turns and swings the broken banister, shattering a mirror to his left. The room falls silent except the high pitched shattering of the mirror, now on the ground. Taylor takes a deep breath and shakes his head. There is no other sound in the house except new rain falling on the roof and the thunder in the distance. Taylor opens the closet door to his left and a raccoon runs out of it and through the house. He shakes his head and looks at the broken mirror. He looks down into the shattered reflection and sees a very dark, nearly silhouetted figure where he should be. He looks away and then back, but the same image remains. He knocks the shattered mirror aside with the bannister and heads out of the house. Exiting the front door, bannister still in hand, he sees a police officer standing two steps below him. The police officer, out of cautionary measures places his hand over the gun and raises a hand at Taylor. The officer is heavier set and tired looking. Taylor lowers the bannister in his hand. "Can I help you?" "Yeah, um, I was driving down the road on a routine check of the neighborhood when I heard glass shatter, just wanted to make sure everything was alright." "Yeah, sorry." Taylor places the bannister against the door and locks up. "I saw someone near my house and then when I got out there, the window was broken, I looked through...I was jumpy and hit the mirror. Sorry, officer." "It's alright, the storms make people jumpy, I know. Did you get a good look at this guy? The intruder?" The cop reaches for a pen and paper and gets ready to write down a description, but Taylor waves him away in a forgiving manner. "I'm sorry, sir. I might have just saw something out of the corner of my eye. The window was probably broken before. No worries." "Alright, well you take care then. If you see anything, call us. We will be right over." Taylor waves him goodbye and heads for his house. "Oh, and by the way...Welcome to the neighborhood." Taylor waves him off and walks up to his house. He gets up to the bedroom again and cracks his back with the assistance of the door frame. He reaches for his bag next to the makeshift night stand out of stacked books and just reaches it by the straps and lifts it up. The way the straps wrapped around the bag, the bag spins as he lifts it into the air, and everything comes falling out. The last thing to hit the ground is a book wrapped in black velvet. A little bit of gold is showing at the top. Taylor's heart starts to pound through his chest instantly at the sight of the golden book. His breathing increases and his mind begins to race in several different directions. Sweat begins to gather at his palms while chills gallop down his spine. He slowly lowers himself to the floor and runs the tips of his fingers across the wood floor towards the book. He crawls forward when his hands can reach no further. His palms run across the floorboard once more and begins to push away the money and clothes in front of him. He crawls his fingers forward and touches the velvet. A shock shoots through his body and tears begin to wallop in his eyes. He begins to tremble as he pulls the velvet off of the book and etched in the gold it says, much to his fear, "The Modern Testament". A flash of lighting fills the room and thunder immediately follows. Taylor quickly backs away from the book, but the room feels to be getting smaller. He shakes his head repeatedly saying "No" as he presses up against the wall. The book was burned. The book burned next to Christian. The religion, the church, the antagonist...they were all supposed to burn and die last week. They were all supposed to be burned out of existence. There is no way they survived. No, he remembered throwing the book next to Christian. Taylor closes his eyes as tears try to force them open. He tries to think of the church and Christian. He pictures himself picking up the book, but instead of throwing the book, he turns towards the door and throws it down the steps and down the main isle. Another flash of lighting and thunder corrupts his memory and flashes to him leaving as the church is on fire. He picks up the book and throws it into the bag before washing his hands and face in the holy water. He had the book the whole time. He protected it. There is no way way! Taylor opens his eyes and looks straight ahead at the open bathroom door. Hung on the back is a full bodied mirror. Taylor looks at himself backed up against the wall, but his face is different; he is smiling. I'm not smiling... Taylor inches closer to see his face tearless and smiling, but when touching his own face, his hands come back wet. This can't be... Taylor stands up, and so does the reflection, still smiling. "NO, YOU'RE DEAD!" The reflection just shakes its head and continues its smile. "NO, GOD NO! I LEFT YOU TO DIE IN THE CHURCH!" "You know, Zacharia, nothing hurts more than you saying that you killed me..." The reflection laughs and Taylor reaches for his face, still feeling tears on his face. "And, if I'm not mistaken, you've said this more than once..." Zacharia struggles with himself, trying to regain composure, but it doesn't work. The reflection of himself cracks its neck, but Zacharia doesn't notice himself doing the same. "BUT YOU ARE! I KILLED YOU! NO, NO, NO...PLEASE, GOD NO!" "Oh, but Zacharia, I was never dead. As long as you breath in the name of God, by our Father, I will never die!" Zacharia takes a few steps closer to the mirror and suddenly stops as the reflections changes into Christian Thomas. His wry smile and fresh face stares back at him where he once stood. He struggles with his mind before speaking again. The tears begin to subside and he regains some strength. "I dealt with you before, Christian, I will once again. You cannot control me!" "Zacharia, you need not control me. We've been through this. You need me, you need my observations, my intuition. [laughing] You need me, Zacharia, and no matter how far you run, I will always be here." "You're not real...you're not real...YOU'RE NOT REAL!" "Oh, but I am... I am as real as you want me to be. I am as real as you need me to be. And Zacharia, you're not strong enough to get rid of me..." Zacharia takes a step closer but Christian does the unexpected and steps out of the mirror. In full body, Christian Thomas takes a few steps past Zacharia before stopping and sizing him up. Zacharia quickly looks over his shoulder and looks into the mirror, where a panicked looking Taylor stares back at him. Lighting flashes once more and casts a shadow about the room for a moment and Christian disappears until the light readjusts itself. "You look angry. Ha, but in all seriousness, I don't think I'm the one you should be so angry with. You need to really think through your life decisions, brother. I mean, everyone around you is dying and coming back to life. If it isn't me or Father Emanuel its Chris Bagwell, I mean, not everyone can fake a death in the eye of millions and then come back just to whoop your ass on life pay-per-view. That is class!" Zacharia begins to make a fist and goes to attack Christian, but his fist goes right through him. "Oh come on, please...Let me see, is this better for you?" Christian's body morphs quickly into a taller figure and more muscular. His head raises and the cynical stare of Chris Bagwell stares back at him. Zacharia nearly falls backwards in shock. After righting himself he dives forward while yelling and falls through him, stubbing his toe on the bed role and nearly goes through the wall. He gets up and turns around to see Christian standing behind him, still with his wry smile. "You know, you said I was the one with the anger issue, but I really think that you were always the one that took the cake. But then again, I am whatever you want me to be." "Get...out...of...my...HEAD!" "I don't want to play that game anymore," he turns back into Chris Bagwell, but this time, the voice changes as well. "Now listen, Taylor, I whooped your ass at Rampage, but that is nothing compared to what you're goin' to feel next week!" Bagwell breaks out laughing, but the mannerism is of Christian. Taylor looks down and sees the Modern Testament below him. He drops to his knees and then sits down. He opens the book and begins to slowly tear out pages. Bagwell looks down at him smiling and fading slightly more and more as the pages begin to tear out of the book. "I will kill you...I will kill you, Bagwell..." Taylor rocks back and forth slowly as he tears out the pages. Bagwell still staring down at him. The look in his eye is asking for more. "I sweat to God Bagwell, I will kill you... You were my friend..." Tears begin to flow from his eyes again. "You were my mentor..." The pages pile up all around Taylor. Blood is spattered on some of the pages as the stiff pages of the Bible cut into his hands. "You were like a father to me. You fake your death...Oh, no, there will be no faking this time..." Handfuls of torn pages are now being thrown around while Taylor sternly looks up at the nearly faded Bagwell. "...this time, I will kill you myself." Bagwell fades away entirely as no pages remain in the book, only the golden binding. Taylor gathers all the pages and the binding and toss them into the fireplace on the first floor. He scrambles to find a match but when he finally does, he lights the whole book at once. "...and I promise..." He throws the book of matches onto his Bible and watches the pages go up in flames. "...and I promise that your Established bastards will go down with you..." The fire burns high and bright, light reflecting off of the golden book. "I swear Chris...I swear..."
