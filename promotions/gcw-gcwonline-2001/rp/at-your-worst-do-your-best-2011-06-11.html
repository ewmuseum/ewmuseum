Date: 06/11/11 She interrupts me tying my tie, sliding one arm around my waist and pressing up against my back with a milky-smooth smile spread across her perfect lips. I can spare two minutes out of my morning to dole out some appreciation, to let her know that she will always come first in my life, and so I hold her hand in mine. Never taking my eyes off her reflection in the mirror, I plant a kiss in the middle of her forehead that conjures a warm hum from her throat. Kate is smooth, rich nourishment, and she gives me a reason to wake up and be the best I can be. This must be what happiness is like. "The kids have their breakfast?" Years ago my concern was to climb as high as I could, to be no man’s pawn, and carve for myself a legacy in this world. Descending the stairs to the kitchen, one look at my beautiful children shows me exactly why that mentality has been abandoned. Five minutes with them is worth it. Whatever "it" may be. I dove headfirst into pleasure...and it gave me the same result," a director would orgasm at the opportunity for a camera zoom, "Pain or pleasure, both are empty without you." She wants to say something. Then she doesn’t. Then she can’t. I’m out the door before she can change her mind. I’ve got to be downtown in no less than twenty minutes if I want to stop myself from ruining a life. And I don’t even know what I plan on doing once I get there. But I’m going to try.
