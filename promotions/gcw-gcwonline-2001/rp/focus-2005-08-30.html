Focus

30 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/30/2005 'Marauder stands and wastes no time to rush forward and drive a right hand into Savige’s face, Savige is caught off guard and is hit with two more right hands before stumbling backward into the ropes. Marauder goes forward for another punch but this time Savige lifts up his boot and drives it into Marauder’s gut and then quickly hits him with a right hand of his own. 'These two are starting off hot and heavy here, each wanting to prove themselves worthy tonight!' ' There she sits with remote in hand carefully watching the match between Savige and Lex. Finger idly twirled her glass of ice water as that head tilted and body came unfurled to lean forward and hit the pause button. The screen was frozen in the middle of an attack by Savige. The man was a tough customer of that she had no doubt. Play was pressed again. ' Savige tries to get the advantage back a few times but Marauder gets a good stance, Savige uses all his strength to spin all the way around, attempting a clobbering blow but Marauder ducks it and quickly gets his grip back and swiftly delivers an amazing overhead belly to belly suplex!Savige gets up holding his back and charge Marauder who leaps into the air and delivers a picture perfect drop kick which sends the big man to his back. Rather quickly Savige stands back up but is again dropped hard with a clothesline, yet again Mike picks himself up but this time is caught with a roundhouse kick that nearly decapitates Savige. Marauder backs up into the corner to catch his breath as Savige now remains on his back also regaining his composure. ' Brows creased as she hit pause once more and grabbed the pen and pad sitting next to her jotting down a few notes glancing up every now and then focusing in on the face of Savige. Okay, so the man had alot of endurance that could prove a real challenge. Scowling she tapped the pen against the paper for a moment and jotted down a couple more things. Play again. Once the video was finished as far as the match was concerned she went to hit pause but ended up pausing herself at the sight of her working on Johnny's injuries. It was odd watching herself attend to the very man she all but helped to destroy. With that odd sense came a feeling of remorse. Wincing she forced herself to continue watching. His words burned into her mind's eye. 'When a young bird is ready to fly, the mother bird will push it from the nest. And if you don't fly... ' 'It's all about the focus. Forget about what you couldn't do and forget about what you don't think you can do, do what you have to do to get that feeling, that burning in your fuckin belly. If you can focus hard enough, if you can get that, you can do anything... ' She pressed the pause button again and simply stared at the man behind the mask. He seemed different now, almost as different as she was after the....incident. Sighing she pressed stop and looked at her notes. ' Focus on keeping him down on the mat.' ' Focus on avoiding a grappling situation.' ' Focus on wearing him down.' She read the next line aloud. 'Focus on winning.' It was like a bad penny that kept popping tails up. The word was everywhere now. She flipped the channel to watch normal TV only to see the word Focus on the screen. A Ford Focus commercial was at it's end. Immediately the next commercial was rolling and talking about a medicine that focus on the digestive system lowering acid reflux. A small sigh wafted from between her lips as she lifted the remote and turned the television off. The silence that followed was deafening. Falling back on her couch she stared at the ceiling her mind going a million miles a minute. Visions of everything in the past few weeks seemed to come to a major collision in the forefront of her thoughts. Focus was definitely not there. Scowling she lurched up to her feet and pace the floor glass still in hand. She need to... To what? Focus? There is was again, that nagging word whittling away at her mind like a chiding mother to a stubborn child. Focus. Focus. Focus. Again and again it churned within that mind beginning to drive her mad until she could stand no more. Whirling she hurled her glass at the wall and stood there silently watching the clear liquid trail the floor. Focusing on it. She could hear the trickling of the water as it slide downward. Her eyes focused on how the light made it sparkle in some areas oddly reminding her of diamonds which in turn reminded her of Erik. She lost focus at that not because she was no longer staring but because the tears were clouding her vision at thoughts of her brother. ' Erik.....why?' Those proud shoulders shuddered as she felt the weight of the world coming down her once more. Mom and Dad left her and never came back. Demitri left her. Erik left her and now there was...Lestatt. She was losing him. She could feel it in her bones. Perhaps she never had him to begin with? Right now she didn't know what to think. A ragged sob shattered the quiet atmosphere of her penthouse apartment as she fell to her knees and covered her face in those trembling hands. Everyone kept leaving her. Why? Was she that horrible? What was it about her that caused them to run away and not look back? It felt like hours had passed when she finally came to a calm resolve. Those puffy eyes lifting once more to stare at the water stained wall. All emotion was gone now, having been drained along with her tears of anger and remorse. Gone was the cloudy days within her mind replaced by a sharp vision of one face. Savige. She was focused now and the cross hair of her focus was aimed on him.....
