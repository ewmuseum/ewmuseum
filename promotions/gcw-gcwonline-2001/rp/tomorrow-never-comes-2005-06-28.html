Tomorrow never comes..

28 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/28/2005 The room was draped in darkness, shadows lingering all about the four poster bed occupied by the lone figure of Eva Vanlorne. Icy blues were riddled with redness, irritated and puffy from the days of crying after the show in Washington D.C. Erik had hit her...HIT HER! She was still in shock at his outburst. In all their life he had never raised a hand to his sister. That slap to her porcelain cheek had been a revelation that things had definitely changed with her twin. A small rush of breath would pass between slightly cracked lips as she rolled her upper body to lie on her back while those supple hips remained twisted on the side. Quietly she stared at the ceiling of her own bedroom, lost in thought...lost in reality. Erik had hit her. The memory replayed in her mind over and over again like some recurring nightmare. Her departure from the building that night had been rather abrupt. No one had seen her leave that she knew of, not that it mattered...the match had not even happened that night. Something else that barbed her self conscious with a large amount of irritation. The Sick Freaks: the thought of them was enough to make her want to chew nails in powder. Dimensioner had made sure she was too busy to get to the stage...bastard would pay for that...they all would. That night Erik had come back to an empty hotel. Eva had wasted no time getting her things packed and getting the hell out of dodge, going home, seeking the solace of her sanctuary to clear her head. She needed this time to refocus and clear her clouded thoughts...thoughts riddled with the faces of Dimensioner, Erik, but most of all, Lestatt. He was like a viral infection really, something that once contracted she just couldn't seem to get rid of. Slowly that body would rise partly elbow tucking under her side as she looked across the expanse of that large room and muttered a low oath. Fingers gripped the light silk sheet draped across her waist ripping it away to float onto the bed behind her as she surged to her feet and snatched up a robe whilst stalking across the room towards her balcony. Arms hastily shoved into the sleeves just before fingers jerked the sash edges together and tied them shut. Glass doors were tugged open allowing the chilled night winds to caress pallid cheeks giving them a kiss a blush as she stepped outside and moved to the railing to rest a hip against and simply stare out over the city of Berlin in the near distance. The sight was breathtaking; it always had been to her. Lights twinkled merrily in the distance as if they mocked her angst with their own joyful glee. Scowling her gaze would drive upwards staring at the night sky, pale blues staring at the silver luminescence of the full moon as she sighed and hugged her arms close to her form warding off the chill. It did not take long for the German ice queen to lose herself in glittering jewels of the sky. At last she was relaxing, her body beginning to ease out of the constant state of tension that had clamped hold of her over these past few weeks....at last there was no thought of Lestatt and her strange draw to him. Then she heard it. From behind her there came a small sound of someone taking in a small rush of air like that of a sigh. Stiffening once again she slowly turned to face the figure, knowing before even setting eyes on him who it was. Ice met ice as she stared at her twin brother with a bitter chill. She didn't say anything, what was there to say? Erik stared at her, the sorrow evident in his eyes for what had transpired and still she just could not find it in her power to forgive him. With a small tilt of her chin upwards she merely turned her back to him once more and continued to stare out at the stars in vain effort to relax once more. 'Eva.....Are you going to stay angry with me forever?' Her gaze slid back to stare at him over her shoulder. 'What do you think Erik?' 'I think it was wrong of me to insult you as I did and even more so to have hit you....' ?Save your breath Erik. An apology isn't going to make this right.' ?Well...then what will?' Ah, the opening statement she needed. Turning around she rested her rear against the railing and crossed her arms across her chest as she stared at him long and hard. Perhaps he would figure out what it was that would make this all go away...but then again he was male afterall. An eternity seemed to pass as each stared at the other waiting on their sibling to speak up first. Stubborn German pride... Finally Eva gave in. Heaving a breath she looked away as if in shame before turning her gaze back to him and opening her mouth to speak. ?I was with Demtri that night Erik....' There she had said, now he would know why she had refused to tell him. Demtri was the one person that Erik would love to rip apart for what he had done so any years ago. Erik's face darkened with silent rage and confusion as he jerked his fingers through his hair and muttered a quiet oath. 'Demtri.....after all these years.....after what he had done to you and you meet with ~him~?' ?Erik, he tracked me down. I didn't tell you because I didn't want to be lectured on this....kind of like now. He has no chance; he ruined that along time ago. That night was the last time he would ever see me other in passing.' ?You should have told me Eva...if you had told me I would have never-' ?No but you would have done something similar Erik.? Eva slowly rose from her perch on the railing and walked towards him now, her face a stubborn mask as she peered up those two inches to look him in the eyes. ?I am a grown woman Erik. Look at me...I am not that naive young girl who was so easily duped by a gold digging bastard.' Erik stared down her hard. His eyes were filled with the warring emotions of wanting to throttle her for her own protection and simply conceding to the fact she was right. He was over protective in many ways, perhaps it was time he stop looking at her in eyes of the past and see the woman before him now...but then again, this was Eva, his younger sister. Things were never as easy as the thoughts made them out to be. Mumbling under his breath he snaked his arms out to catch the smaller frame of his twin into his embrace. Hand came to her head pressing it against his chest and he lifted his chin and rested it on her head whilst sighing heavily. The silence that followed was deafening. He needed to tell her what was going on, he had promised, but how could he place in this realm of fear and unknowing without feeling guilty for having not told her in the first place. Internally the battle waged on as he merely held her close allowing the silent healing of her heart to take place. He would tell her...just not right now, not tonight, maybe tomorrow.... Yes....tomorrow.....
