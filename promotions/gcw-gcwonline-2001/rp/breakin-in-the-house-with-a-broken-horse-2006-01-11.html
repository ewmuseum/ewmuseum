Breakin in the house with a broken horse

11 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/11-06 “I don’t want you there this week. It’s for personal reasons. You know normally I love seeing you at ringside, but if we aren’t careful, out entire personal life will disappear in the blink of an eye” Ricky says as he hangs up the phone. “I hate when she hangs up on me?” Ricky grabs his jacket and heads outside. He exhales deeply as the air is filled with a smoke from cold air meeting the air from his lungs. He steps off his back porch into a large green field, or pasture as some would call it. He walks out back to where the stable is, or will be and stares blankly across the field, when his phone suddenly vibrates in his jacket pocket, startling him. “Yes Chloe” “Don’t yes me Ricky. You treat my like a two year old sometimes and its highly annoying. Anyhow, I’m up here at that farm we passed, the one with the horses.” “I’m listening.” “Well he says that we can get them for 1500 each. Which really isn’t bad being they are pure breeds.” “How many you wanting to get babe?” “I’m thinking 3 or 4’for now.” “Why do you need 4 horses?” “Who said I needed them?” click Ricky laughs to himself a bit as he dials information. He requests a local number for a contractor, not really caring which he got. Once he gets the number he pays the additional dollar to have the call connected. “WCG Contracting. Neb speaking. How can I help ya?” “Well hello Neb. I need to see a man about a stable.” “Whatcha got in mind bud?” “A stable?” Ricky replies. “Right. How many horses you got?” “None.” “How many kids you got?” The two laugh a bit, then Ricky fills him in on the details of what he is needing. “SHE wants 4 horses to start she says. I have no idea why, I don’t even know how to ride one, how to care for one, or how to even feed one.” “That’s the way it always works. But for 4 horses, a standard stable and grazing area for hay, to keep the moisture out, will run you about 5k.” “Just for a place for horses to stand?”” “Well unfortunately you gotta have it if you want the horses. You know the whole Animal cruelty thing.” “When can you come out” We will go ahead and get this started I guess and I will call the horse guy and tell him to save me a few.” “Well we can start tomorrow, assuming you can get financing that early” “I will be paying cash, or cashiers check.” “Very well sir, supply us with the address and we will see you tomorrow. We can bring papers with us so you don’t have to come into town.” Ricky gives the guy the details on where to show up to build the stable and hangs up the phone. He calls Chloe back to let her know the company will be out tomorrow to start building on the stable. “Baby, it’s done. They will be out tomorrow to start. I didn’t think to ask how long.” “Ok sweetie. I am gonna talk to this guy about the horses. Ok?” “Sure babe, just let em know we cant get em until the stable is done.” “Will do! See you in a bit” As they hang up the phone Chloe pulls back up the driveway to the ranch with the horses for sale. She hops out of her 2006 Expedition and heads up to the house. After a few knocks on the door some elderly man answers. He is smiling as he recognizes the face from not so long ago. “Back again” he asks “Yep.” Chloe smiles innocently at the man and continues to speak “I spoke with my boyfriend and he is having a company come out tomorrow to build or start building a stable. Neither of us know how long it will take, we are new to this” “Well how many horses you want” One? Two?” “I want four?” “Four”! Surprising for someone new to the horse business. Are you having the stable built for four?” “Yes, I believe so” “It will probably take only a few days.” “Ok, can I pay you now, and leave them here until then?” “Sure, I’m ok with that” the man replies. Chloe breaks out the check book and cuts the guy a check for six thousand dollars. The man stares down at the check and starts to speak out. “I don’t know if I should accept a check for this amount’wait’is that THE Ricky Thunder?” “Yes sir, that’s him” Chloe smiles “Well I spose in the business he is in, then he can afford the horses. I will take the check this time.” “We won’t let you down” “I’m sure of it. You want to take a trip out back and see the horses” Pick which ones you want.” “Sure, let’s go!” The two head around back, and Chloe sees one she wants instantly. It is a burnt red color, with a bright red mane. She points it out and the man marks it down. She then chooses a solid dark brown one, a light tan one, then one catches her eye. He is a quiet horse in the corner of the stable. A white horse with brown spots, his mane is brown, with white streaks. His back is slightly swayed. The horse almost appears to have had his spirit broken. “I want that one?” “Miss, that horse isn’t worth 500, let alone 1500.” “I want it?” “Are you sure” I would feel really bad selling it to you.” “I’m sure, and don’t feel bad, I really like that horse. I think he just needs some time and attention.” The man marks down the last choice as he leads Chloe back to the front of the house, and to her door. She explains to him that she will return in a few days to get the horses, and gets into her car. She dials Ricky again telling him she has paid for the horses, and explaining the one choice. “I found a white broken down horse, with a slight sway back, I picked him?” “Why would you get a useless horse?” “I have you?” “Bitch.” “We’ll talk more when I get home. See ya soon!” “Sure thing babe” The two hang up the phone, leaving Chloe smiling in the car, and Ricky staring confused wondering what the hell is the point in a busted horse?
