Blue Da Ba Dee

31 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 31, 2009 A short time ago... In a wrestling ring far, far away... It was a place where there was no evil Caldera Enterprise to rule the galaxy. A land where one could glisten underneath the stage lights of the main event with worrying about having a Knightmare. A civilization where everybody was an Admiral of Awesome in their own right. Our story begins on an old Indian reservation somewhere in the mid-western United States. The air is dry and accompanied by a warm west wind. Off in the distance, among a sea of floating orbs, a bluish figure can be made out waddling along a dirt path. Not far from his side is his counterpart and best friend - his monkey. Zoey: I've walked this path a million times and every time it just seems more magical than the last. I remember walking this path the last time and being chased by the orb of Odissious. The orb of Odissious was an angry orb that day - and angry orb indeed! And if it wasn't for the orb of Omicron, I don't think the orb of Odissious would have ever stopped chasing me! Our bluish friend extends his hand to his side, reaching for the hand of his faithful counterpart and best friend - his monkey. Zoey: But today - today is a different day. Today is a wonderful day. Today is a day that the orb of Odissious is finally showing some optimism. Oh, today is a day indeed. Today is the day. Wouldn't you say that today is a lovely day, Mackenzie? Our bluish friend peers to his side with eyes of passion as he lays his sights to his trusty, faithful counterpart and best friend - his monkey. Mackenzie: Hoo-hoo. And monkey responds. Zoey: Oh you're just such a silly willy! Mackenzie: Hoo-haaa! Haaa! Hoo-haa! Monkey says with a taste of disdain. Zoey: Oh I know your name isn't Willy. Your name is Mackenzie! But you knew what I meant, Mackenzie. You're like a silly Willy. You're a silly guy who is just as silly as that guy named Willy. But your name is Mackenzie. And so a lightbulb flickers. Zoey: So maybe that makes you a Silly Mackenzie! Mackenzie: Hoo-hoo, HAA-HAA! Zoey: You like that, don't you? You silly Mackenzie, you! Mackenzie: Hoo-hoo, HAA-HAAA!! Monkey skips up and down in a serene excitment. He and his bluish friend have come up with a name and a nickname at that. It was the first sign of a day that was a day to be great. But this day in which was meant to be great would take a brash turn as the Orb of Ollection bobbled on out from Oklahoma. The orb of Ollection dropped to the path before our bluish friend and his caring, trusty, faithful counterpart and best friend - his monkey. They were startled and shocked and didn't know what to do! Zoey: Oh no, Mackenzie - this is no silly matter! The orb of Ollection is here with a ladder! And just as our bluish friend spoke, a ladder appeared in front of him. One end faced the orb and the other faced our friend. Zoey: What shall I do? Does he want me to climb? Mackenzie: Haa-hoo, haa-haa! Monkey frollicked forward and latched onto the ladder. His intentions were clear - he was to climb to the top. But our bluish friend felt scared, and wanted to hold back. The ladder looked shaky and he wanted not for Monkey to go splat. Zoey: Mackenzie, wait! You cannot go up there. You shouldn't! You mustn't! But Monkey was never an animal to let his friends dangle in the balance of danger. He swung from the bottom rungs and flipped his feet up a few rungs higher. Our bluish friend watched on in fear, but made it a point to hold the base of the ladder steady. It was moral support. Mackenzie: Hoo-hoo! Haaaaaaaaa! Zoey: Okay, Mackenzie! You got it! But the orb of Ollection grew angry. He may even have been in the mood for monkey soup. The orb of Ollection cast a ominous spell upon the ladder, causing the ground to shake underneath it. Monkey began losing his grip. Zoey: MACKENZIE, NO! Mackenzie: HAAAA! HOO-HAAA! Zoey: Stop it you angry orb! You're being a jerk! But the angry orb of Ollection would not listen to the voice of reason. The orb of Ollection continued stirring the spell and the ladder continued to sway. Our bluish friend leaped onto the bottom rungs, hoping to steady the ladder - but to no avail. Danger was imminent. Zoey: Somebody ... SOMMEEEBOOODDYYY! HEEELPPP! Mackenzie: HOO-HOO HAAAAAA HAAAAAAAA! HOO-HOO HAAAAAAA! HOO-HOO HAAAAA HAAAAAAA! Our bluish friend and his courageous, caring, trusty, faithful counterpart and best friend - his monkey, scream for assistance. They hope for a miracle upon the Indian Gods of the reservation to save them from the evil orb of Ollection. Just when all hope seems to be lost, a speckle sparks in the distant skies. The speckle grows nearer and nearer until its lengthy red cape can be seen in plain view. It was no illusion - it was clear that help had come. And who could ask for more... Zoey: Mackenize look! Mackenzie: HOO-HAAAA! HOOO-HOO-HAAAAAA! Zoey: You're right! IT'S THE MIGHTY GERBIL!!! The Might Gerbil: There's no need to fear...MIGHTY GERBIL IS HERE! The flying gerbil swoops in with his red cape swaying. He scoops our bluish friend and his brave, courageous, caring, trusty, faithful counterpart and best friend - his monkey from the ladder just as the evil orb of Ollection sends the unsturdy ladder toppling to the ground. The flying gerbil in the red cape laughs as he soars to safety. Zoey: Thanks, Gerbil! You saved us! Mackenzie: Hoo-hoo HAA-HAA! The Mighty Gerbil: There's no need to fear. The Mighty Gerbil will bring you to safety! Our three heroes continue soaring through the southern skies until they are finally able to land in a Voodoo Banana Chicken Farm. The flying gerbil safely brings our bluish friend and his strong, brave, courageous, caring, trusty, faithful counterpart and best friend - his monkey to the ground. Zoey: Where are we? The Might Gerbil: Look. Off into the distance. In the no-so-distant distance stands a wrestling ring - in the middle of the Voodoo Banana Chicken Farm. A small man hobbles out from behind it, waving his arm. Mackenzie: Hoo-hoo!? Zoey: Good question, Mackenzie! Who is that? The Mighty Gerbil: That guy? Oh that's... But the man scurries over to answer himself. Waving Man: Jeff. From Northern Ohio. FIN.
