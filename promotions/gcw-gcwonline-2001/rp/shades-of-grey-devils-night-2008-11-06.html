Shades of Grey: Devils Night

6 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/06/08 *Shades of Grey is intended to be a alternate Mid-Evil type world that my characters will inhabit, its not meant to have any impact on my GCW career , more just a fun thing to do once in awhile, but since the theme of rp’ing is slightly different for this card this seems like a good way to start things off* -- Alex Grey peered down into the village. At first glance nothing seemed out of order, plenty of lit torches fluttered in the breeze and the moon illuminated much of the surrounding area, a casual observer would be hard pressed to deny the peace or the beauty of the scene at the bottom of the hill. But Alex Grey and his companions weren’t casual observers. “You sure he’s down there?” The gruff voice of Joshua Freeborn broke the silence, glancing over at his Barbarian Companion who towered two feet over him Alex nodded “Can’t you feel it?” he inquired “The taint of black magic in the air?” “Magic?” The Barbarian snorted “Nothing but superstition and tales to frighten unruly children’I’ve yet to encounter anything that wont fall before this” the Barbarian patted the massive two handed sword slung over his shoulder. “Typical?” a third voice from the other side of Alex spoke “The Barbarian doesn’t believe it because he can’t see it” “I believe in the gold you pay me?” Joshua grumbled spitting off to the side “Are you telling me that you’ve suddenly developed a sense for the magical Draven?” Draven Masters chuckled softly, slightly shorter than Alex and considerably smaller than Josh the Mercenary was built like a solid stone foundation, a battle axe strapped to his back and a heightened sense of the area around him, Draven’s instincts were second to none. “Close your eyes and open your ears Josh” he reprimanded the Barbarian “Tell me what you hear” Joshua heaved a sigh and closed his eyes attuning his other senses to the area around him, after several long moments his eyes snapped open. ? I hear nothing?” he said looking down at the small village with renewed suspicion “Aye’not even the chirp of a cricket” Draven said, looking over at Alex “If Alex says he’s down there then I believe him” “Then what are we doing sitting up here?” Joshua growled “Let’s get on with it! If this man is as evil as you two have led me to believe then the bounty for his head will be well worth our efforts!” Alex had remained quiet throughout the banter, under any normal circumstance he would have appreciated it, but now he remained silent, his eyes taking in every detail of the scene at the bottom of the hill, he opened his mouth to say something but suddenly a shrill scream of terror filled the night. “We go’now!” Joshua cried kicking his horse into motion and barrelling down the hill “Josh! Wait for us!” Draven called out “Gods be damned that man will kill us all!” he muttered as he gave pursuit, Alex reached back to make sure his bow was secure on his back before following after them, his mind focused on the task at hand, the debt he was sworn to repay, the head of the dark sorcerer Martinicus he was going to collect. -- Josh was the first to reach the bottom of the hill, leaping off his horse as he entered the village he drew his sword and took up a battle stance as a figure raced towards him, barely visible in the flickering torchlight which suddenly seemed anything but beautiful. “HOLD!” The Barbarian commanded as the figure ran closer, the authority in his voice reached even the panicked shape in front of him, skidding to a halt in front of Joshua the figured whimpered and looked up at him “Help me!” she screamed, looking over her shoulder “They’re right behind me!” Draven jumped off his horse his battle axe in his hands, he looked down at the young woman on the ground, she could have been someone’s sister, between the age of clinging to innocence and developing into a woman, dressed in the garb of a serving woman, her dress was torn in several places and her chest torn and bloody, it looked like she’d been mauled by something with long sharp nails, and on her bare shoulder? “Tooth marks?” Draven asked “Lady who did this to you?” A low groan filled the air. Joshua and Draven exchanged a look, the moan sounded again and the young woman’s knee’s buckled, falling to the ground she began to whimper again her body shaking in fear, over her whimpers they heard the sounds of footsteps coming towards them. Three men staggered in an out of the torchlight, from a distance they looked dishevelled, their clothing and hair unkempt, but as they got closer a stench followed in their wake. “What is that?” Joshua growled putting a hand over his nose “Rotting flesh?” Draven said “Years ago I was on the wrong side of a castle under siege, they had every avenue of escape cut off, we were locked in with our dead, after a time the flesh began to rot?” he spat trying to get the taste out of his mouth “I never forgot that stench?” “Stay where you are!” Josh called out to the three approaching figures, they approached in jerking uneven movements, the man closest to them dragged a sword behind him, moving forward he stretched out his other hand reaching for Josh though he was still several feet away, he opened his mouth and a low moan escaped his lips, Josh got a good look at his face and his jaw dropped “What foul Sorcery is this!” he cried The moaning figured shambled closer and now Draven could see his face, twisted up in an expression of agony his flesh seemed to have been robbed of all color, blood spattered on his face and chunks of flesh hanging in his hair, but worse still was the gaping hole in the side of his cheek, it appeared as though someone had torn it out with their teeth, around the gaping hole the creatures tongue flapped around the edges, pulling stray chunks of flesh from the wound into it’s mouth it chewed. A second figure was missing half of its arm, several arrows filled it’s chest and one jutted from it’s throat, to Draven’s horror none of the wounds bled, the third creature seemed to be in the best shape of all, it staggered forward shoving past its two companions arms reaching out for Josh who stared in disbelief. “JOSH!” Draven cried as the creature lunged Josh was already in motion, spinning he brought his sword whistling down with considerable force into the creatures shoulder severing its arm completely, it fell to the ground still twitching its hand still opening and closing, no blood even so much as leaking from the wound the creature gave a low moan of discomfort and then lurched forward again it’s one arm reaching out for Josh. “What in the seven hells is this!” Josh cried stepping back as the three creatures staggered in. The young woman on the ground was trembling even more now, her words no longer making sense, merely a unending babble of unintelligible dialogue punctuated by low moans. Draven raised his axe intent on attacking but before he could move an arrow whistled by his head burying itself between the eyes of the one armed creature, stopping and jerking like a puppet who’s strings had been cut the creature fell to the ground, twitching a few more times it went still and didn’t move again. “The head!” Alex said stringing another arrow and letting fly “Go for the head!” the arrow struck one of the approaching creatures in the throat, Josh growled springing into action he spun and his sword lashed out decapitating the creature with the swords head from his shoulders, the body took several more steps forward and then fell to the ground as its head rolled off into the shadows. Draven brought his axe up and stepped forward burying it into the neck and shoulder of the last remaining creature, he starred in horror as the creature pushed forward trying to get it’s remaining hand on him, grasping with every fibre of it’s being, placing his boot against the creatures chest Draven gave a loud cry and kicked, pulling his axe free as the creature fell to the ground he brought the axe down in a killing blow severing the rotting things head. Looking around Josh caught Alex’s eye “Just what in the hell is this!” he demanded “Black Magic’the walking dead” Alex said notching another arrow and looking around “Thinking of backing out now Barbarian?” Draven said wiping the gore from his axe “Going to run back to mother?” Josh snorted “Your mother will have to wait?” he countered. The young woman on the ground was trembling all the harder now, Alex took a step towards her but then noticed the bite mark on her shoulder, he opened his mouth to speak but the words died on his lips as the young woman’s body suddenly went as rigid as a board, her eyes rolled up into the back of her head and she suddenly vomited up blood, her eyes closed and when she opened them again they were dead lifeless orbs, staggering to her feet she uttered a low moan reaching for the first thing she could find. Josh’s horse. The beast screamed and tried to run, the other two mounts needed no encouragement and were already fleeing, but the young woman grabbed Josh’s mount and sank her teeth into the things neck, a et gurgling cry filled the night as she began to rip chunks of flesh from the horses neck like a frenzied animal. Covered In the creatures blood the woman turned her gaze to the three and as the horse fell to the ground twitching and gurgling she advanced on Alex. Calmly raising his bow Alex put an arrow between her eyes, the woman went down and ceased moving. A low chuckle drifted in on the night breeze, looking up at the top of the hill Alex growled as he saw Martinicus, he couldn’t make out the mans features but the evil aura surrounding him could not have belonged to anyone else, Alex could feel the Dark Sorcerers eyes on him, he notched another arrow and raised his bow but the gesture was futile, Martinicus was simply too far away. A shrill scream pulled Alex’s attention from the hilltop, Josh and Draven already heading towards the sound, Alex was torn wanting to pursue Martin but knowing there was still a chance to save innocent lives, he looked back up at the hilltop only to discover Martinicus was gone. “This is far from over?” he promised before hurrying after Josh and Draven. Josh as the first to reach the doorway, raising his foot he kicked the door down and stepped inside, as he did his eyes widened in horror. The man was laying on the ground trying to fight off a small group, his only weapon was a dagger and every cut he inflicted did little damage, Josh watched stunned as the man fell to the ground and was instantly mobbed by the walking dead. Like a pack of wolves they fell on him all at once, impossibly strong fingers reaching for his stomach, tearing through the skin and reach for what lay beneath, Josh fought back the bile in his throat as the creatures pulled out long bloody ropes of intestines and feasted, oblivious to their victims screams, Josh backed out of the hut, he couldn’t go inside, his massive blade would be no use in the tight quarters of the hut, instead he grabbed a torch and lit the roof of the hut on fire, hoping to drive the creatures out into the open where his blade would claim what the fire didn’t. The dry hut went up quicker than Josh had imagined and low moans issued from within, several of the creatures staggered out burning, the smell of burning rotting flesh filled Josh’s nose as he stepped forward and took the closest creatures head off, thankfully the screams from within the hut had ceased. Draven had engaged another pair of the walking dead, as he dispatched them with relative ease he sensed a presence behind him, he turned and grunted as a blow caught his shoulder, he rolled as best he could but as he came up his arm went numb, what stood before him was a man of almost Josh’s height and no doubt Josh’s strength, a blacksmiths hammer clutched tightly in on hand, one of the creatures eyes hung by a thread from it’s gaping eye socket, it’s lips seemed to have been chewed off whether by it’s own doing or another attackers, it gave a deep low moan and advanced on Draven a permanent bloody grin on etched on its face. Draven struggled to raise his axe with one hand, around him he heard the sound’s of flickering flames, the fire Josh had set seemed to be spreading, dodging as the hammer came crashing down Draven gasped as the hammer tore a huge chunk out of the wall he’d been standing next to, the creature was slow but immensely powerful, he raised his battle axe and attacked one armed, the axe thudded into the creatures shoulder and Draven had to struggled to pull it free before the hammer nearly caved in his skull, his efforts sent fiery pain through his shoulder, he realized it was dislocated but had no time to focus on the injury, stepping backwards to avoid another blow he began to wonder just how he was going to take this creature down. His salvation came in the form of a flaming arrow thudding into the creatures chest, Draven ducked and rolled out of the way as the hammer came down again, ignoring the pain in his shoulder as he hit the ground, Alex launched another flaming arrow striking the creature in the throat, the flesh began to cook giving off a disgusting odour, the creature turned in Alex’s direction and reached for him, with a low growl Draven jerked his dislocated shoulder back into the socket and gripped his axe, with all the power he could muster he brought his axe down on the creature from behind splitting the things head right down the middle. “Burn it!” Alex cried “All of it! Everything! Only the flames will cleanse this evil!” Draven needed no encouragement, nor did Josh, grabbing torches they set fire to whatever they could and soon the whole village as ablaze, retreating towards where they’d entered the village Alex watched as half of a creature dragged it’s way out of a building covered in flames, it’s intestines dragging along behind it like ropes, it seemed to feel no pain, only an intense desire to get it’s hands on those still living, Alex notched an arrow and scored a hit on the things head. “Alex! Let’s go!” Josh growled as Alex stood transfixed by the dead thing “We’ll let the flames do the rest! Come on!” Alex shook his head, there was no use trying to gauge the depths of this evil magic, he turned and followed his two companions out of the burning town, the echoes of the dead moaning followed after him, he knew those moans would follow him even into his dreams. -- Dawn broke finding Alex standing on the top of the hill looking down into the charred remains of the village for any sign of movement, he’d been at it for over an hour when Draven’s hand fell on his shoulder “My friend we need to be far from this place” Draven said “Should the kings men find us amidst the wreckage of this place they won’t believe our stories, they’ll only see the slaughter and put the blame on us. Alex turned away from the scene below him convinced they’re ended the black magic curse that had been inflicted on the town. “Do you understand why I pursue him now?” he asked Draven and Josh. Josh grunted and nodded, his usual banter noticeably absent as the sun began to rise “I’m with you my friend” Draven said “We’ll track him down together and make sure he pays for the crimes he’s committed today.” “And more?” Alex sighed “Aye you can count me in’bastard owes me a horse” Josh said wiping the gore from his blade and slinging it back over his shoulder. “He went in this direction” Alex said “I can feel him’he wants us to follow” “We’re going to need help” Draven sighed as the three weary companions shouldered their equipment and followed the trail of the Dark Sorcerer. “The Wildman would help us” Alex said as they walked Draven glanced over at Alex “That is if he doesn’t kill us both before we set foot in his camp?” Draven added “He may?” Alex replied “But I choose to believe his hate for Martinicus is stronger than his dislike for us” “I guess we’ll find out!” Josh said cheerily as they made their ay down the road “I’ve only heard of the Wildman by reputation, but I’m certain he has no grudge with me! If he kills you two I’m sure he’ll offer me a paying position among his ranks!” “Barbarian?” Draven grumbled as they headed down the road.
