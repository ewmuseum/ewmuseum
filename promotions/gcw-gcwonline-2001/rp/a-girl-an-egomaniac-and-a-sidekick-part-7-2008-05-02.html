A Girl, An Egomaniac, and a Sidekick (Part 7)

2 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/2/08 (Takes place September 2002) She had put the rest of her career on the line for this one match. She had gone against her intuition and invested some trust into her colleague Chris Storm. Now, she was nothing more than a mess on the outside. Bruised bones, scraped skin, and plenty of stitches. She had lost. And more importantly, he had lost. She had let him down. Or at least that’s how she chose to view it. Others, such as her boyfriend and fellow wrestler, Erik Erratic, happened to see things in quite a different light. Chris Storm let himself down and just brought Tessa along for the ride. No matter how you viewed it though, it didn’t change the fact that they lost, and consequences were bound to arise from it. Tessa realized this well before she pulled the 2000 Lexus SC400 she’d rented out into the stadiums parking lot. It just hadn’t sunk in until now, when she knew there’d be no avoiding the criticism about to be tossed her way. Thankfully, Erik was well aware of how rough her evening was about to be, and decided to meet her at her car to at least get her started on a good foot. “Hello Beautiful.” He greeted as the driver side door swung open. Even knowing she was in for a rough evening, she still couldn’t help but to smile towards Erik, “Hey.” He smiled back at her. It was the best he could think to do. “Don’t worry; I’m not going to give you any crap about Sunday.” She appreciated that. But at the same time she was hoping that topic could have been avoided all together, “Thanks. But I’m sure I’m going to get it later. Deservingly.” “Deservingly?” That was certainly a new word around the WIW locker room. In a business dominated by men, the word deservingly, when it came to punishment, was rarely tossed around. “Yes, deservingly.” Her shoulders dropped some, as she popped open the trunk to fetch her gym bag, “I got too cocky, Erik. Chris warned me I was before the match even began.” Erik chuckled, obviously she must have mistaken Chris? ego for her own, “He must have been talking about himself.” “No.” She quickly defended, “I did. And it cost us the match.” Erik had been around Chris for years, and was fully aware that while it was established that Chris was a total jackass, he still managed to convince people that they were insignificant. Tessa, was his latest victim. “Let me guess. He lectured you before the match?” Tessa was hesitant to answer, “Yes.” “And he tried to convince you that he had everything covered?” Once again she hesitantly answered, “Yes.” “Well if that’s the case, you shouldn’t have lost period!” He exclaimed in a joking manner. “Chris Storm had you covered!” These words set off the radar off said person, and of course, at mention of his name, Chris came marching down the hall with a full head of steam. “What was that?” he hollered out to grab their attentions. “Ah great” Tessa groaned, not wanting to deal with him at the moment. Stomping up to the two of them, Chris looked Erik dead in the face. “You. Door. Please meet on the way out.” “What is your problem?” Squeezing in between the two, she looked up at Chris, before she could realize what she would be getting herself in to by doing so. Chris turned his head away, looking in an opposite direction from her, “I don’t recall addressing you, you half-sized Diva. This is between people who can actually WIN matches” Chris stops, looking to Erik, “ok well one of us can win matches. The other will be lucky to hit puberty this year.” His words hardly proved effective in deterring her away, “You’re pissed because WE lost.” She poked him in the chest to make sure she’d catch his attention this time. After all, she didn’t want Erik being dragged into this, “I’m sorry that WE did. But maybe you should have been less focused on the lecturing, and more focused on working”.y’know?..together?” “And what do you call the thirteen page report on the best strategies to use” I would call that team work, but clearly you didn’t study it,” he fired back on her. Her arms folded up against her body, “I’m a wrestler, not a college student.” “I hardly see what your lack of education has to do with you costing me the match.” “Ok!” She finally surrendered, dropping her hands down to her side, “I lost the match. I’m sorry. What do you want me to do about it?” Chris smirked, “Just meet me in the ring later. I’ll have a surprise for you. Something I cooked up on my own.” Before she could utter any form of a question, he was down the hall, with that grin still plastered on his face. He was up to no good, but she felt bad enough about losing that she’d set her self up just to make up for it. Even if he was a jackass, he still knew a lot more about the business than she did. For that reason alone, she was hell bent on winning over his respect, though it certainly didn’t help any that he was attractive. Attractive enough that even being a jackass, he could still manage to have a flock of girls waiting outside in the garage for him. He had her wrapped around his fingers, without even knowing it. Now she needed to figure out how to unravel herself.
