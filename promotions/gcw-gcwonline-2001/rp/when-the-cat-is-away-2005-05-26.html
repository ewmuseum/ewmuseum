When the cat is away..

26 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/26/2005 A shadow moved, bouncing from one statue to the next. High above one of the many security cameras slowly rotated on its pedestal scanning the marble floors below. The shadow did not move. As soon as the camera passed the danger zone that figure dressed in black dashed from its perch. Long limbs flitted over the floors in three long bounds taking a flying leap onto a rather sturdy statue. A moment later the eerie silence that echoed in this place would be shattered by the shaking of a can...a paint can at that. The lid would be popped off followed by the telltale sound of paint being spewed forth blacking out the camera's eye. One agile leap later, the figure landed like a cat on its feet pulling a small wallet computer from its perch of the front of the outfit. Flipping it open gloved fingers danced over the keys pulling up the Musuem Security system. A few seconds later icy eyes watched at the bypass scrambler code did its job and finally locked down. Behind that mask a smile would erupt followed by a muted purr. Three key punches later, the camera system was on auto replay. Slapping the tiny computer closed a hand would move to the back of head and pull the mask off to reveal the deceptive beauty of one Eva VanLorne. Finger pressed against a tiny button behind her left ear. ' God's eyes have been averted. Mission complete.' A dark head would lift from its perch in the lap of one rather flushed looking security guard as a husky murmur slipped past red tinted lips. ' I am sorry darling, but I did not realize what time it was. Call me when your shift is over?' Amber eyes glowed with the silent promise to finish what she started. Swallowing heavily he righted himself and glanced at the clock. ' Ahem, yeah I think that is best. I am late for checking the tapes.' She smiled as they exchanged numbers. Then promptly fixed her shirt and left. Her part was done here. Once out of ear shot she would press against one of the buttons of her shirt. ' It is all yours Eva. I will see you in Houston.' ' Thanks doll. I will have your share ready when I arrive.' With a confidant stride she moved to the room which was her goal this evening. Pale blues would immediately scan the area until they fell on her target at last. Slowly those lips would quirk, forming a sly smile as moved towards the masterpiece which would soon be hers, so to speak. It was with a slow appreciation those orbs traveled over the ancient paints taking in the textures and colors. ' The Adordation of the Shepards' was truly a masterpiece. With a slow calculation she would lift her hands to light trace around the outer edges of the frame looking for the final obstacle which still stood in her way. After a moment's breath the index finger would graze over the wire that could ruin her perfect execution. Chuckling she would reach down with the free hand and produce a pair of tiny snips. With careful execution she slipped the needle nose beneath the wire and gave a soft tug in order to bring it to sight. This was always the difficult part. With the wire exposed she would once again reach down to bring out a tiny cable with clips on either side of it and place it in mouth. Both hands began the final task of removing her inanimate opponent. Great care was taken as the pliers were slipped over that wire and clamped to make a circular motion. This action would be repeated once more just an inch past the first before finger gripped and pressed inward baring the copper beneath. That tool shoved back in pocket, she slipped the clamp bypass from between lips and placed a clamp on either side. It was done. Grinning like the cheshire cat those hands would lift and carefully pry the painting off the wall. A pocket knife would come into play as she carefuly spliced the back open and with tender care removed the painting from its nest. Rolling the prize up she would pulling the cylinder strapped to ehr back free and meticously slide it within. Snapping the lid shut Eva moved quickly now, eager to be rid of this place. Empty frame would be lifted and placed back on its perch, the clamps removed from the pressure wire trigger and finally...she turned and walked away. ' Another day, another two million.' Another burgarly gone off without a hitch ... as if there were ever a doubt.
