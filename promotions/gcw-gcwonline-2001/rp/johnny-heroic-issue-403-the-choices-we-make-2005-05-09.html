Johnny Heroic: Issue #403 - The Choices We Make

9 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/9/2005 'It's over, Heroic. You give up now, or I do this kid in right where we stand.' Ohhhhhh, this is what I've always wanted. It's bad enough to be fighting Commie-Nazi-Devil-Worshipping-Ninjas, but tonight I have the most splitting of headaches. Then, I have Dummy McDumbass, who has the superpower of being a total moron, run into the middle of ten of these bastards while I'm taking on twenty myself, and he gets caught by them. And to top it off, it's my God damn birthday. 'Look. I'm sure we can find a reasonable way out of-' Clickityclickclickkachinksnapclickkachunkclickityklakunk. That was the sound of their machine guns cocking. And they're smart. They're not aiming for me. They know bullets don't work on me. 'It's okay, Johnny! I'll be ok! Just take them out!' 'Shut up, kid. God, you're dumb.' Thirty guys. Ten of them have broken bones. Two of them are on the ground, out cold. One of them actually has powers of some sort. And they all look pretty indistinguishable. God. There's gotta be some hidden plot here. Because the one on the surface sucks. 'Tell me, Johnny Heroic...do you think yourself to be some sort of god?' 'No. But I play one on TV.' 'Insolent curr. Even now, as I stand here with the most upper of hands, you do not accept the fact, plain and simple, that the Brotherhood of Villains has you beaten. You are powerless to do anything to us without losing your precious Kid Justice! You may be fast enough to dodge a bullet, Johnny Heroic. But I wonder...are you fast enough to get from where you're standing to the bullet in this gun before it leaves the chamber and enters Kid Justice's head? You know...would a Brotherhood of Villains really call themselves a brotherhood of villains? I mean...wouldn't they see themselves as the good guys or something? That name just kinda...rubs me the wrong way. It's honestly like getting up the in the morning and saying, 'I'm going to be pure evil today.' You're not. You're your own hero no matter what you do. Whether you want to save the day, or you want to wreck havoc... ...or you want to be a nihilist... ...you really do strive for what you want. And if you strive for what you want, you are being your own hero. Any that try to stop you are the villains. So...how can you call yourself a villain, except in relation to somebody else. Maybe they're calling themselves the Brotherhood of Villains because they're saying that they're villains to my hero. In which case, they are heroes to themselves in being villains to me. Kinda like someone who succeeds at failing. And in doing so, they revolve their entire world around me. Rather flattering. All of this analyzing has taken place in a microsecond. Yeah...I have that power too. 'You've completely fucked yourself over, and now the best you can hope for is that I starve to death before you do. And I'll just slow down my metabolism so I don't.' Yeah...I have that power too. 'What are you talking about?' 'Something you should have considered. Alternatives. Choices. The reprocussions of what you've chosen to do. You chose to get up this morning and rob a bank. Why? To fund your little wannabe regime and hopefully someday purge the world of its impurities, being people and ideas that do not coincide with you and yours. The money that was stolen is covered by insurance. So, by stealing it, all you've done is raise inflation because now there is the money that you stole added to the total treasury of the country, thus depreciating the value of the dollar by a percent, and making it so that you actually made less money than if you had simply earned the amount that you stole. Something that a group with your resources could manage in no time at all. I mean seriously...how much is a couple million dollars to a terrorist group nowadays? So now you got this money, which doesn't impact the nation at all, and you are holding a gun to Kid Justice's head. You shoot him, I slaughter you all. You don't shoot him, we all wait here until I slaughter you all. Thus, by the choices you've made, you've gone from being a viable power in the world to being royally, and totally, fucked. Good job.' He looks kind of confused. Probably not the brightest terrorist out there. But then again, this is somebody that probably read 'Catcher In the Rye' and decided that centralized government is evil. He probably listened to Rammstein as a child and now thinks that killing is okay as long as the strong survive. Stupid, impressionable, mistake-making mind. And now he's probably got my words screwed up, just like all of his other influences, and he has no idea what to say. Except- 'Bullshit!' By analyzing the electro-magnetic fields around the blood vessels on his face, I can detect where his blood's flowing. How fast it's going there. How hot it is. What chemicals are in it. Basically, I can predict what he's going to say and why based purely on the resonance he gives off. Yeah...I have that power too. The best part about controlling electro-magnetism is that I can basically do whatever I want. Everything that I control has something to do with electrons and the energy they give off. Mass and energy and how they interact. In essense, I treat the physical world as one treats lovemaking, or music. It's an art. It's a mastery of simply two things: tension and relaxation. By playing with the tension and relaxation of the very electrons, the very core of any action or reaction, you can understand whatever you need to understand and do whatever you need to have done. For all intents and purposes, and to answer his question from earlier...yes. I am a sort of god. And yes. It feels good. On the other side of the story, I know the truth. I'm the luckiest fucking man on the face of the planet. Controlling electrons is something I only do because I'm lucky enough to ahve it keep working. I don't have any sort of knowledge that it's going to work. I don't even have faith in it, as some people have blind faith in everything they do. Rather, I just try to do it. And it happens. Bada-bing. And I know that one of these days, it's just not going to work. 'Enough! You don't believe I will kill the boy? He dies now!' The bullet exits the chamber and he it right. I can't get over there fast enough. But the electrons in the air and around the bullet can, and they do. And the bullet doesn't go anywhere near Kid Justice. It's a matter of asking them to do something and, by a stroke of luck, they do. I don't know what I'm going to do one of these days... One of these days when the great coin in the sky lands tails instead of heads. It's not impossible that I'll always land heads and keep doing whatever I want with time and space. In fact, each time I try, there's a fifty/fifty chance that I'll succeed. And I just keep landing on the right side of that coing. But one of these days... 'Thanks Johnny. You're definitly a hero and half, partner!' I snap back to with Kid Justice all simles and a bunch of Commie-Nazi-Devil-Worshipping-Ninjas in heaps on the ground. Most of them are dead. That's ok. Nobody cares if a Commie-Nazi-Devil-Worshipping-Ninja is dead. Because they're already dead inside. I take a second to survey some of my handiwork. For the most part, it looks like they died peacefully. A split second frying of the brain. It's quite the simple process, and it looks just like a regular punch. But on contact, I vibrate their skull to supersonic frequencies and charge all the electrons in their head. All of them. Yeah. I have that power. Too. Big shock. One thing I would be interested in knowing is what their last thoughts were before death. What last minute hallucinations did they see? What synapses went off in their brains and triggered their mind's eye to cause what vision? But it's too late. I don't have the time in the middle of a fight to follow the electrons and see what ones are shaking, and see what memories they conjur up. I figure I could find out next time. But I take the risk of getting knifed. I've been knifed. It sucks. And so, I figure their last thoughts are a private matter anyway. It's enough that I kill them. I figure I'll give them their dignity in imagining what they will before they die. And after that point, no one will know what they saw or heard in that last instant of life. And everything that they were and everything that they saw will be gone. And is gone. And nothing mroe. And no one will know what they were. And the world will go on. 'What causes people to end up like this, Johnny? To end up as murdering sons of bitches? To be the scum of the earth?' Naieve Kid Justice, with no powers of his own. Running headfirst into every battle, not caring what will happen to him. Will he ever learn? From being here at the front, or from being told by me, I think it is his lot in life to never understand. 'The choices we make, kid. The choices we make...'
