Prelude to a New Beginning.

24 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-24-06 Early October ?Nice to see you back.? Monica Trellis smiled as she walked past Richard Hartman, she had been away scouting prospects her father; Mario Trellis, was interested in. Mario Trellis was the owner of the Anywhere But Your Back Gym, and was always looking for young, ambitious, athletes he felt he could train to become the next major superstar in the wrestling industry. Monica had spent her entire life around the business, but was never really interested in the physical aspects of the sport. What she could do, was spot talent. She had an eye for things like that, noticing the diamond in the rough no one else saw. Her tawny brown hair was pulled up into a messy bun, a pencil was stuck into the center and jutted out at an awkward angle. With her hair pulled back, it was hard not to notice how attractive she was; even without make-up, not to mention the track pants and sports bra that gave her that casual athletic look. Even dressed down she carried herself with confidence, and seemed to demand attention without actually asking for it. She looked through the glass window of her father's office. She could see the entire gym through this window, but her attention seemed to be fixed on the ring ? that's where her father was. She hoped he would notice she was back from her trip, finally. This last trip took a bit longer than expected, since she had a hard time convincing some high school wrestling phenom from Minnesota that he couldn't just walk into a major promotion with his type of background. This wasn't the NBA after all. Still, after watching him perform in a competition against another school, she wasn't that impressed. The kid had some skills, but he was far too raw. Against kids his age he looked like like the next Seymour Almasy, but she would have been throwing scrap meat to the lions if she would've told him he stood a chance at joining one of Primetime Central's roster with his lackadaisical technique. ?How'd the trip go,? Richard asked her, even though he knew she was in her own little world. That was the way she was, and after watching her grow up from a little girl with pigtails in her hair to a beautiful woman, he felt more like an older brother than anything else. Hartman was one of those guys that worked his tail off in the ring, but never had the charisma to carry a match or play to the crowd. Mario always had a soft spot for Rich ? his nickname around the gym ? and let him stay on as an assistant trainer. Rich knew the ring like the back of his hand, and after a few months he really took to his new job. Now it seems this is what he had been training all of those years for, it became as natural as an old shoe. ?I convinced one kid he should come check out the gym,? she replied, her attention still directed toward the ring on the floor below. ?But he's an arrogant little prick. He asked how much we were gonna pay him.? ?You're kidding.? ?No,? she shook her head slightly, still intrigued by what was going on in the ring. ?Hey, who's that?? ?That's Shane,? Rich answered, peering over her shoulder to stare out the window toward the ring. ?That kid from Baltimore your dad went to go see personally, remember.? ?Not him,? she motioned to someone else, a man sitting in the shadows staring intently into the ring. ?Him.? ?Oh,? he nodded, ?That's Jayce.? ?Jayce?? ?I don't really know much about him,? he started, a slight shrug of his shoulders accompanied the response. ?But your father was quick to ask him to stay.? ?He any good?? Richard looked down at her, a smirk on his face. ?Depends on what you're talking about.? Monica shook her head from side to side, her cheeks tinted a hint of pink. ?In the ring, Rich.? He chuckled softly, almost a snicker. ?He is kind of cu-? out of the corner of her eye she saw a blur slide into the ring, and just as fast she saw the guy she was talking about wrap his arm around someone else's neck. ?How did he do that?? ?That was fast.? She glanced up at Hartman. ?You think?? He watched her as she sprinted out of the office, then quickly followed suit. ?Hey, wait up.? ?=?=?=?=?=? ?Just let him go, Jayce.? Mario Trellis pleaded that he let the kid go, but his grip never loosened. Monica and Richard came sprinting toward the ring, but Mario motioned for them to stay away. ?Seriously, Jayce,? he continued to plead, ?he's just a kid. I promise nothing is going to happen, but you need to let the boy go. You said you trusted me... Trust me now.? Jayce released his grip on Shane, and just as quick as he had slid into the ring and wrapped his arm around the kid's neck; he was gone. Monica climbed into the ring as Richard slid under the bottom rope, her father was already helping Shane to his feet when she approached. ?Is he okay?? Her father nodded, ?Yeah, he's just in a bit of shock.? ?I didn't-? Shane had trouble talking, his throat was sore from the pressure that had been applied. ?I didn't see him.? ?Help him to the back, Rich,? Mario told his assistant, who nodded in reply. ?Who is that, Dad?? Mario glanced toward the direction Jayce had disappeared, he wondered if he was still lurking in the shadows. ?His name's Jayce Bradley.? ?The name sounds familiar.? ?He's under contract with Global Championship Wrestling.? Her eyes lit up as she made her way toward the ropes, ?GCW signed him?? ?He's been under contract for over a year,? he said as he followed his daughter. ?Had a good run in last year's GTT and even held a few titles there.? ?So what's he doing here?? ?He needs help in other areas.? ?Since when do we teach manners,? she said with a smirk, but the joke did not sit well with her father. ?He's had some problems in his life, Monica,? her father said flatly. ?His style is wild... Uncontrollable.? ?You can say that again.? ?He has a short fuse,? he continued despite the interruption. ?And he is quick to react when he feels someone he knows is threatened.? ?He thought Shane was a threat to you,? she looked confused, knowing full well that her father could handle someone like Shane. ?It was more than that,? he replied. ?It was something else.? ?Someone needs to tell him the story about sticks and stones,? Her demeanor was upsetting her father, especially considering the circumstances. ?Sorry.? ?Look, I need to find him and talk to him.? He grabbed her shoulders and kissed her softly on the forehead. ?I'll talk to you some more during dinner, okay.? She nodded in response, then watched as he headed off in the same direction Jayce ran off to. ?=?=?=?=?=? ?Jayce.? ?I'm sorry,? Jayce replied. ?I don't know what came over me.? Mario had followed Jayce outside of the gym, and found him leaning up against one of the dumpsters in the alley out back. ?It's okay, Jayce,? Mario said softly, the emotion in his voice genuine. ?I told you I would help you work through this.? ?I didn't want to hurt him.? Mario placed a hand on Jayce's shoulder, but he quickly jerked away. ?It's okay, Jayce,? he tried to comfort him. ?You don't have to close yourself off. Just relax. You don't have to be on guard twenty four seven.? ?It's just the way I've always been.? ?Well, we're going to have to change that, aren't we?? Jayce nodded. ?Good,? Mario said as he placed his hand back on Jayce's shoulder, though; this time he didn't pull away. ?Now let's go back inside. There's someone I want you to meet.?
