Date: 11/26/06 ~The Order's Nexus~ ~Under the Four Winds Academy Barracks Building~ ~Outside of Las Vegas~ ~A bit more then a week ago~ Blaise watched as Annie rose into the air and hovered, powered by her faery wings that came from the her now bare shoulder blades. She, unlike Blaise, was one of the Faery kind that had such wings, his cousin Fey was one too, if you ever got a chance to see her manifested in her true form. There was something about seeing this type of display though, he always liked it. He sat bye the others that would be joining him, Herne, Annie's brother and leader of one squad of the Wild Hunt the old country in Tir Na Nog. They were going through, an entire hunt squad, Blaise, Ukyo and a fell troll, to be honest Blaise didn't even know what that was. He'd seen trolls before, but a fell troll? Who the hell knew. ?Alright you two, the others are in kill mode. We want some survivors, but its' a seek and destroy type order. If they get away fine, they can spread the word. You two are infiltrating, and you will bring back Geoffri LaBatte alive, or dead. If you happen to have to take him out, all we need is his head.? That made even Blaise shudder. He handed both Ukyo and Blaise micro-sd cards, modern technology to use in their combined enchanted and modern combat suits. They dropped the cards into the locking slot on one of the boxes on their belts. They knew what they were for, so they pulled the thick black goggles down over their eyes. Shadow instantly recognized the face of the man who adorned so many paintings in the house when she had been in it to kill him.
