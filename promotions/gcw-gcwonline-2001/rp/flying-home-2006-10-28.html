Flying Home

28 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/28/06 And now, now they start to look up again. A few weeks ago she lost the GCW TV title to a man who held it for one defense. Now the title is around the large man known as Bryan Mayhem, who while not being a brigadier, is considered an ally as Lia still has uses for him. One of them was seen this last week on WorldWide when Mayhem was the man who took Eva VanLorne and broke her. The bitch opened her mouth, and not only did she put her foot in it, but she was then destroyed like she had almost begged and pleaded for. But since getting her wedding over and done with to Josh, Dani has only had good fortune. Coming in fourth in Dangerous Games, second in the royal match to only Shadow in the new combined faction known as the KnightMare Brigade: Dies Irae, and she broke her GTT curse, going past SSL in a match in the Republic of Singapore. Now it was on to round two, to a woman who called herself ?The Fallen Angel?. She didn't know whom she'd be facing. She didn't know that Dani was... an angel. She just didn't advertise it. Then again, if she did, everything would be far different then it was. But that was neither here nor there as she and her husband and best friend, Joshua Brand were now approaching the flight terminal for United Airlines at Singapore's Changi Airport. Dani was in the lead, with Josh coming up behind her. They both had luggage, Dani was pulling her suitcase by it's built in wheels, and had a shoulder bag also. Josh was pulling his and that on a cart. It appeared that while here, Dani had hit the Orchard Road Shopping District. It was kinda obvious though, for she was wearing a rather sleek silk dress in the traditional Chinese cheongsam style dress. It was a sleeveless number in black, with a full length skirt and an embroidered dragon that came down the front of it. Her long hair was done up in a traditional style bun so she'd be able to play the part while here. She didn't get much out of the country other then a few tours to japan and that. So it was night to not only get out but have a day or two that was counted as hers and Josh's honeymoon also. And Josh was feeling the effects of the time they didn't spend in their hotel room acting like... newlyweds. Although he was to blame. It was hard enough to get Dani to buy lavish things, she'd never grown up wanting or needing them. She had the dress and hair done for him, the dress was custom fit by a worker here. He called it a gift for her. But for the entire total week they were here for GTT6 and their honeymoon on the overlap, every time she's said she thought something was pretty or looked nice, she'd not buy it, but he did. And now they were going back and he was lugging it all on a cart to the airplane. All had gone great for this week, putting SSL down to the mat for a three count, her honeymoon and now... this as they got to the counter with the small Asian girl behind the counter. ?Can I help you miss?? Dani looked at her and smiled wide with bright, white teeth. ?Yes, we're here to pick up our passes. Danielle and Joshua Brand. We're flying back to a destination in the US. It was done through the travel agent of employers.? The woman typed some things into the computer as Dani handed her both hers and Josh's passports. ?Oh, yes. The newlyweds. We have some messages for you too. Although your employer did register your flight under your maiden name.? Dani rolled her eyes. But she expected it after they had told the company she'd be keeping the Northsky name for her 'stage name' so that people wouldn't loose the name value attached to her face. ?That's fine, all my id's are still in the Northsky name. I've been having to use the marriage paperwork for proving who I am now outside the ring.? The lady giggled. ?We need to make sure that we're good on the extra cargo too.? The lady looked over to the cart that Josh had behind him. ?I'm sure we'll be able to handle it. The flights to the US are not always that full. Although I have some messages for you also.? The lady handed them their passes and an envelope that was reserved for Dani. Quckly the two of them moved to get all of the packages and that which they would not be carrying on, into baggage check and they ensured the money was paid that would cover the additional items would be taken care of. After that they moved past the security checkpoint and step into the area that they couldn't leave if they were going to get onto that plane. Once there, while Josh sat down and got his ipod ready for use, Dani took a look at the messages in the envelope. She opened the envelope up and first things first, was a letter congratulating her on her victory over Steven Lee from the GCW offices. It was signed by Caldera himself, and she thought that was pretty cool. Additionally there was a letter congratulating both her and Josh on their successful nuptials. It was a bit late, but it was the thought that counted. And then their was their flight itinerary in a printout showing they were going from Singapore, via Jumbo 747 to Los Angeles and then catching a connecting flight to Las Vegas, with a one day layover to get their stuff in order, and then they had their passes for Las Vegas to Dallas for this edition of World Wide. And inside the same thing of that was the booking sheet for this World Wide... ?World Title Match... Lestatt Knight defends against Danielle Northsky, 20 minute time limit for TV, show authorized to go over by X:TV for this match if it goes long.? She almost dropped the stack of papers. 2 years in many federations: CCWA, Core, Global, she now had a shot at a world title. In the CCWA she had held, in fact she was the first and only own owner it's last incarnation to hold the second highest title, the North American Championship. The belt still sat on her mantle at home over their faux fireplace in Las Vegas. Core had never given her a chance and never would have. Global, she'd taken the Platinum Championship and then before she finally gave it up, she had come through in a match that had six people, three titles and only one title was leaving. And that had been Dani. At the time the Global Cruiserweight champion was even the now former GCW World Champion, Dawn McFarlane. But no one had ever given her a shot at the big one. In wrestling it was the holy grail, no matter where you were. And while the odds would be against her, it was still a nod by the bookers that they liked what they were seeing. Her face went pale, as pale as it could go with her natural skin tone, and Josh reached over and took the papers from her hands. He read the booking committee letter and smiled a wide, almost evil smile. ?Holy crap, you mean after World Wide I could be married to the world champion?? She looked at him and stuttered. ?Y..ye... yeah right. I've got to take on Lestatt Knight. This is the guy who's taken Dawn McFarlane to the limit twice now. And he held up pretty well against Lia and Viv, last week. I'm going to get slaughtered.? Josh double took on her. He knew she still occasionally had the 'rookies' come out in her. But in the end, she has been one of the most successful rookies in the sport. ?I can't believe I just heard that. Maybe I should tell Headmaster Lox that you said that.? She gave him a look of daggers. Draven Lox. He was now there and with them. And she really didn't know why other then it appeared that the boss of the Order was up to something. But Draven Lox was one of her original teachers at the Chicago Academy for the Order's wrestling and fighting program. It had been him who taught her the sky twister press that she used as a finisher. In fact her style of fighting was very close to his. Her two teachers, Draven and Sean McClancy, her body type allowed her to settle more for the high flying and not power and tossing people around the ring. However Mr. McClancy, Blaise's cousin, had shown her quite a few counters for the big man syndrome wrestlers, like Knight. With her luck, Josh would rat her out to Draven and he'd call Sean to come and make sure that she got ready for stuff. Either that or Gray would be the one ordered to put her on suplex dummy duty like Josh usually ended up on. She could see Josh look up at the clock on the wall and verify it against his watch. ?Hey, it's almost time for the plane boarding to start up here. You might want to get yourself ready for it.? She nodded and reached down to grab her purse. Dani still had one fear, one fear that she could not shake. And it was the only way to get off this wonderful island and get back in time for her match on worldwide. She opened her purse and took out a bottle of pills. Opening it she dropped two in her hand and then put the cap back on. She grabbed the water bottle that Josh was carrying around and used it to help them go down. In an hour, she'd be almost if not fully asleep as she sat in the chair on the plane. Even before they took off. ?You did make sure they knew about this right?? Josh nodded. ?Every time. Since once you're out of it, if the plane were to be about to Crash, I'd have to go with you and break your fall on the way down.? She smiled as the hustle and bustle of the area suddenly picked up as the flight attendants and that arrived for this flight to Los Angeles. ~A few hours later~ Dani sat, ignorant to the world around her as she slept on the plane. Her seat was reclined back as far as it could go, and her own headphones for her ipod were on. She dozed as thoughts of many things, especially the news of her match came to haunt her. You could call it ?rookie who rose too fast jitters? if you wanted to. But as she laid there, incoherent of the world around her... she dreamed. She dreamed of the one thing she could not bring herself to truly do. She dreamed of having her wings extended and flying over the plains and buttes of her homeland in South Dakota. A dream she's had many times... unfortunately. For she always starts calm and peaceful. Enjoying the updrafts and the other freedom of the flight. But soon, soon it'll appear, diving out of the sun. The six winged angel of death who looks for her. He dives down and attacks her. And in the end they both crash into the canyon below. She knows it's going to happen anytime now. But she still enjoys the feelings until the final part happens. She sees the canyon coming up. She feels the need to prepare for it and rolls over onto her back, adjusting her wings to glide a bit on the currents as she looks up towards the sun for her attacker. Normally she stays face down and takes the attack from the back. And just like clockwork he comes out from the sun, the visage of the fallen angel who wants her for some reason, Astaroth. He comes right at her, his flaming sword drawn, and held in a position coming out from his chest, right towards her. His hands adjust their double grip on the blade as he dives down. She's felt it before. The blade going through her chest, to the hilt like she did the Astaroth's crony, Baraqiel. She sees him coming closer and braces for the impact that this time would drive through her heart. She wants to close her eyes, but can't. She has to watch him coming down at her. She inhales as she readies. And she doesn't notice another form, one of light and pure energy come in on an intercept course from the side. Just as she thinks in the next few moments she'll be dead though, her ears suddenly ring with a metal on metal sound. Her eyes go wide as she sees Astaroth's specter stopped in mid air, sword to sword with another sareph, another six winged warrior angel from heaven. This one's hair flows in a think black mane about his head. She can only see him from behind, as his gleaming silver armor adorns his shoulders, and other weak points, but doesn't cover him like a set of plate mail from the knight epics. It's more like something in a mix of roman, Babylonian and something else that she's seen in many of the campy sci fi or fantasy movies that Josh likes to watch. The new comer maneuvers his sword and catches the other's sword at the cross pommel and then with a flick of his wrist sends that sword plummeting towards the earth without it's owner. The one that looks like her enemy snarls as his sword disappears, but it's not going to do him any good as another few movements and an entire side of wings, 3 in all, go cascading down ahead of the rest of him. Dani watches as the form of the Archangel of Sorcery cascades down to the jagged rocks and impales on what looks to be an old tree stump. She looks up at the other one. ?This isn't how the dream usually ends up.? The newcomer turns and looks at her. He's handsome as most angels are reported to be. His eyes are a calming ice blue that don't really match his long black, unruly head o hair. He winks at her and with another flick of his wrist, his sword disappears. ?That felt good. its been a while since I've been able to even thinking of doing something like that.? ?Who are you?? Dani pulls up and assumes a position similar to his, hovering in mid air with short flaps of her wings. Although it takes surprisingly less effort then a humming bird would need, almost as if the hovering was second nature and really didn't need the wings to do it. Although with the six wings coming off his back, when they move, only in unison in pairs, it was a sight to behold. ?I am the one you seek. I am the one you desire to find. I'm the one who you wear around your neck now, hidden under a silk collar at this moment.? ?The Destroyer in the legend that Kokabel and my grandfather speak about?? ?One in the same. We are closer now, and as you always seem to have this dream when you least want to, I decided to see if I could change it for you.? She took in the scene. Here was the one that she was supposed to become according to the legend. ?Is that all?? She had to get more information. It was more then a dream now, she could feel it. ?Of course not. It is time that we meet each other. I see that you take after the woman whom Virtue Kokabel bedded. You sure don't look like him? The Seraph of Destruction smiled at her with teeth that rivaled her own in whiteness. Of course her modeling duties demanded that she take up all aspects of her hygiene and looks with concentration on outward appearance. She had to appear to be perfect. This creature just was. ?Why do you seek me out? Why am I impelled to seek you and even wear your remains as jewelry?? He laughed. ?Because soon, one day you and I will become one!? She gave a squint of her eyes and a quizzical look. ?What? I must inform you good sir, that I am now a married woman.? ?Not that way, you've been having too much time with your new mortal husband.? He flew closer to her, looking her in the eyes as he did. ?No, soon, the body I lost when Mikail, Lawrence and Jbril rended my physical form will be replaced. And its your body that will house my spirit and power.? ?But I don't want to be someone else. Why would I let you do that? I don't want to loose...? He smiled and put his hand to the side of her face. ?No, you would not loose anything with me. For I am nothing anymore. I am only spirit and power. I am only essence. But you have already claimed a mantle but you do not bond it with you. You destroyed the heart of Baraqiel. You could claim his spot now, but i would advise against it. He was only a virtue and you could not use his mantle to fight Astaroth.? She shook her head. ?But when the time comes, when I'm free of the prison I'm in now then you and I will embrace together and with the essence of Baraqiel we will become something new, something that Astaroth can not compete against.? His hand was warm, she could feel the energy coming from him as it poured into her. ?But if I'm destined to do this, why does Astaroth want me?? ?For the same reason. His body was broken, and can't be used. He was supposed to come in the body of his immortal ancestor named Jaymes. This would have made him able to find a way to repair his body for seven years.? She perked up. Jaymes had been the man who tried to take her the night she manifested. He'd mentioned that he would be taking her to his master to become his bride. She would have been married to Astaroth... ?But Jaymes... and he's around.? ?He found another ancestor. But this one is a mortal. He can only be bonded with him for a thousand and one days. A bit under three years. He's trapped unless he finds a new host. And there are no immortals who would take him that he knows of. But that would only buy him another seven years.? She nodded again. ?But me?? ?You are unique. You are an angel born on earth. You do not bear the mark of a host of angels. You do not bear the glyph on your neck. But one can be put there. When your true form, your destiny is found, you will have one. But will it be the one foretold by legend or the one that he would have you wear... his.? She pushed his hand away from her face. ?That won't happen. Its prophesied to be the other way.? ?Prophecies and soothsaying things are only recipes. If you want this to happen, these things need to. IF they don't happen, then the prophecy will be empty. Even Nostradamus was only ninety percent correct. There are reasons why.? She pushed off further as he spoke. ?Why? Why me?? ?Cause you are what you are. And in the end, you will be great. Right now you are nothing but a lesser angel. But in the end, no matter which side wins, you will be more then that, much, much more. You are destined for great things. Even the mortals you deal with in your gladiatorial combat, they know this. One day you will stand up and show them that you are more then what you appear to be. And then you will be truly great.? At that, she felt herself waking, as Josh shook her. ?Dani... Dani...? Her eyes opened, and she felt the urge to use the restroom. She'd been sleeping for quite some time. ?Hey, they're serving dinner. Its a fourteen hour flight. You'd better get up long enough to eat and take some more pills.? She looked up at him bleary eyed and smiled. He could only smile back. It looked like some of the jitters that plagued her before the pills had taken effect were gone now. One could only wonder why. ~ To Be Continued~
