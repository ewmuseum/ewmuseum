Everybody Knows This Is Nowhere

14 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/14/07 Little specks of light, white squares or silveryshimmery somethings, hang in the darkness. I'm not sure which defines which. Do I see the ocean black or the moon glinting across its surface? Neither, I mean. Just a box with a girl of shadow and substance and a cat entirely of shadow’and noise. So I can't sleep. What's new? Little rays refracting and reflecting across the ceiling until they either fade or smash into the fan, hurtle themselves back out. They don't trust me, but I don't either. I did a stupid thing; or another stupid thing, whatever. My head's just been too tight lately, my brain pulled taut over that little me at the center of my skull and now he can't see for shit; just a field of black and maybe the faint shine here and there of a membrane thin enough to let something through. And he doesn't know which defines which either. I'd listen to him then; I'd make a moral compass out of the fucker and hold him high into the sunlight until he pointed out a way with one tiny outstretched arm. Maybe I'd follow, maybe I'd just torture him in the glow that tries to make it clear that shadows are just a blotch on a world of light’he'd break down, I'd break down, if the answer was just gonna be that simple. She never snores. Sometimes it's like she never breathes. Others, like she doesn't see. Always, like she doesn't care. It’s not her job to care. She's an English major and she's my wife, not my therapist or my shrink or my guidance counselor. Or my lighthouse. Or my northern star. She's just here, knowing more about me than I do and known less by me than by her. Serious things would tear us apart, or tear me and anybody apart’I form neither the hydrogen bonds that maintain across millions of molecules nor intensely personal polar bonds, sharing my electron out or taking theirs in or both or what the fuck ever. Serious things would tear me apart. Jordan White, too; the old or the new. The old just. I don't know how he did what he did. I don't know if I can maintain a disconnect like that on some unknown promise’or maybe Jordan couldn't either and, name like some parasite, he left me and latched onto some other poor fuck waiting to have his head eaten away. Jordan White or Sage Black, I don't know which defines which, and I don't know if either define me. Fuck, I need a shower. Fuckin', cigarette's easier to come by’is cancer even the kinda thing that bugs me? I had a thought devoted entirely to it, years ago, back when Jordan White's name always started with The Hate Machine. I just don't remember it, I'm too long divorced and all that oncehate that oncegotletout has built a small city in my head that defies definition’I guess. They don't let me in. Haha, fucking, I know. I used to think I was a social cancer’clever little boy, hah. Fuck shit damn cuntass motherfucker. What the hell was my problem? I was a kid, and a pretty stupid one. That's all. Cold's the absence of heat, I think. Which is the default, though? A good jacket would keep me from worrying about that shit. Surely. There are desolate expanses outside of earth, outside of my head. Screens of stars, suspended in the aspic of some inky nothing, in black matter. I need my ego fed by more than a cat that mills at my feet and a hot bitch that sleeps quiet. My head's been black for a long time, even when I was white, and continued failure isn't going to keep it running. Christ’there's no hope in wrestling. I've got no future in it, I've got no business dicking around in some other poor fuck's career and I've got no business lingering too long in the cameras or running my fucking mouth off at the booth. Lexicon was right. I don't need these fucking vampires, or these fucking shapeshifters, or any of these fucking fakes stuck rolling around in their own shit. I've lost to Bryan Mayhem. I've lost to, to people whose names I never even knew and my name's still out there losing fights I know I could've won. Right? Maybe. Maybe I'm not cut out for this and I should just pull my head back out of whichever hole I stuck it in. Is hope the lack of despair, is despair the lack of hope? I don't fucking know, and I don't fucking know that I ever did. Questions like this just didn't used to come up. I could stand to value myself on something because it was always coming in’I thought it was money. Turns out I was wrong, turns out it was success and I'm a chump that barely knows himself’definitely not enough to betray himself, for sure. I just have problems outside the 3D linear time world I'm living in. I just have questions that stand outside the context of my reality but still have the balls to color it. World, you made me Jordan White. Fuck you. I'm Sage Black. I'm not a wrestler. I'm a loafer with wealth previously established. Jordan White? He's more cokehead than wrestler, but he's so enthusiastic about both that it's hard to hate him for 'em. He's just a kid. He'll get better, better still when I'm not holding his hand, best if I split completely and let him run on his own. The cat's trying to talk, little ripples of noise streaming out of nothing. If I open my eyes to figure out what sort of ridiculous shit he's into, then I'm burning the last thirty minutes of laying here and getting closer to passing out. Total nullification. Am I comfortable with this? Yeah. Why not? Not very sleepy anyways. He darts out of the room. Fuck this. I'm going somewhere. Or, well. I'm getting out of bed, smoking a bowl or two, watching cartoons, then going somewhere. Clowns are funny. This is my experience. Now, out into a world that's altogether not that menacing or anything. It's just confused as it is confusing, less sure what it's trying to say than we are, incapable of serving up the kind of definitions we can read’the kind that serve as answers and not questions. It's a long drive. I will slip into the morass. I will drift, unaware. And, considerable time later. Here. I park a few lanes from the door, trying to smash the ashtray with a cigarette. I picked the wrong side, but, fuck it. Dedication. Go, Sage Black. Prosper. A fat man confronts me out front, maybe my age or a little older and dressed like Fat Joe. He's got a table with a pot of money, candy, and a tall man. The fat one hands me a pamphlet. "What's up man. I used to trip a lot, didn't think nobody cared, didn't care, thought the world was black, y'know? Turns out, man, turns out there's something bigger than this. I got tired of tripping and" All I can muster are "yeah," or "I feel you." "3-6-5, drugs and sex, had to be something more to this’and there is. These people helped me out, helped me get my life straight, and could you help us out, maybe a little donation?" Dig. "Shit, thought I had more change in my pocket." "It's cool, it's all appreciated." I give up, I give him two bucks. It's all I've got greenbackwise. "This straight?" "Oh yeah, man, anything's something, y'know? Thanks brotha’I'm Brother Joe." "I'm Sage." We shake. "Help yourself to some candy, Sage, somethin' to munch on, y'know." I do; help myself and know. "And give us a call sometime. Help get your life straight." I won't. Some people say the light's there if you look for it, that shadow and grime and grunge are just built up on it to obscure it, to hide our lives away. I just. That's not the answer I wanted. Everyone gets their own reality; his doesn't jive with mine. But whatever. Two bucks to help a country of junkies? I'm fucking crazy. It's some nothing; if they weren't just bullshitting to get money for a nice ol' high’I've done it. Why wouldn't other people? The pamphlet's just a doublesided sheet of paper. The Power House Church Soul Patrol Team, Missionaries To The Ghetto. "Total Recovery is Possible," that sort of shit. Maybe it is. Fuck it, good luck to 'em. They wanna help the destitute, who am I to complain? They weren't fiends, lying to get a fix. Right? They had their own personal truth, and they wanted to share it with the world, right? It's just not enough of an argument to be the answer I need. Other hand, Famous Amos makes some decent cookies. All these cats wanna do is help and give out candy, more power to 'em. Do I really look like the kind of person that needs that speech to donate money? Do I really look like I need help that much? Whatever. Out the back of my head; there's shit to buy. Like, shouldn't this place be deserted? Whatever. Fucking weird people, Walmart people. Misshapen grotesque blobs that try not to stare. I know I need a shower. Smirk at them, dig for some hint of why I spent an hour coming here to walk around with rural midnight people, to deal with the goth stockboy wearing a Slipknot shirt and always bearing just a glimmer of recollection as to who I am’and I'm like "these are my fans?" and sighing in disgust. Or, I think it's disgust we're passing around like joints or clipped straws. Fuckin' generation of hate junkies, whine junkies, pity junkies. Lighters. I need enlightenment at the tip of my thumb for easy access. They don't get an aisle, do they? Nah, nah. They're at the register. I can't justify this one. "Sage." Who? I turn. I know this kid's face and I know this kid's name and I can't rectify either of them by the other. Frank Black or Jordan White or. I don't know. "Yo." Why is he here? "Shit, dog, I can't believe I'm runnin' into your ass up here. You live 'round here?" "Sort of." I'm scratching where my head meets my neck, wondering if I'm not somehow spotlighted by three acres of uniform fluorescent bulbs. "That's crazy, that's fuckin' crazy. I was chillin' with this chick I met in the 'Boro, rolled here to grab some more drink. Shit. Dude." "Fucking crazy, yeah." "I was wantin' to talk to you anyway, damn. I dunno, somethin's just been buggin' me." "Yeah?" "Like, like, yeah! Sage Black, I mean, I dunno. Like, why'd you take my last name?" "Huh?" "You're Sage Black, I used to be Frank Black, like. Shit just seems pretty gay, y'know?" "I had the name in mind a minute, man. Not sure what to tell you." "That's word, but, shit. It still seems pretty weird to me, that's all. Like sorta marriage or some such shit." "It's just a bad coincidence.." Like, I know I could bash his raggedy nose into pulp and blood and cartilage’but should I? It's just wrestling. It's just my name. Or, it's just what my name used to be. "Pimp. So, so like what you doin' up here, man?" "I needed. I don't fuckin' know, heh. Air." "Damn, sound like shit's hectic. Shit. This ain't gotta do with me losing that match, does it? 'Coz, I mean, I know I've got that bitch next time like it ain't even a thing, you know? I fuckin', I got that bitch." "Yeah. Fuck, it could've gone either way, couldn't it?" "It was kinda close. I think I mighta hurt her, that's somethin'." "Hah. You can't win all the time, I guess." "I can, man. I just froze up. Shiiiit, you wanna know what's up, like for real? I'm gonna be straight up sick this week, dog. Been trainin', been watchin' tapes of these kids and shit. You wouldn't believe all the shit I've found, f'real, about like just little stupid shits they've all got that's complete fuckin' candy to capitalize on. Shit'd prolly be borin' your ass though, so I ain't gonna bug you about it." He bounces on his toes, sniffles. Looks at me. Looks at me; I don't look at him. I look at millions of long sleeve sweaters with "Jordan X White" in cursive on the back right shoulder. I look at mounds of chick's jeans with "JXW" instead of an ass pocket, I see jackets and tshirts and hats and hoods all emblazoned with the idea of "get Jordan rich. Make Jordan immortal," and is it even a good idea? I'm not Jordan anymore, do I ever become Jordan again? Do I get to make use of the immortality, or does it go to that army I see in him; a Jordan White in every major fed, Jordans of both sexes and every race pushing a brand that used to be me’a me that started a town and had it abandon him, a me that left one friend in the desert to die, a me that ran both of his brothers (one blood, one through life) out of the country at threat of death, a me that already made one of them crazy and sent one of them to jail. Is it Jordan White that gets famous, or is it just his name? Fuck. Is it my name? He looks at me. I look at him. "World title shot on the line, y'know." "Shit, you ain't gotta tell me. I'm fuckin' pumped about that shit, but I ain't tryin' to get too hyped and just fuck myself over on winnin' for starin' at the gold, if that makes sense, you know?" "I know all about that." "Hah, shit. Don't be so fuckin' down on yourself, Sage. Worst comes worst? You got famous and still kept shit real. That's worth way more than some little ass gold belt on any motherfuckin' day. Y'know?" "It's worth something. Y'know, man, I'm just gonna let you get outta here, get back to your chicky before she starts to dry up." "Huhuhuhah. Oh shit, man. Oh shit, fuckin' classic. That's word though, shit. You ain't tryin' to roll back with me, was you? She's got this fly ass fuckin' roomie, like oh shit. You're down with the swirl, righ'? I mean, you never seemed racist or nothin'." "It's cool, man. I'm married." "Shit. For real? Shit. Can't believe I forgot that." "I don't think it's come up." "Offer still stands, dude." And really? I'm thinking about it. Living with Danielle's the same creeping fear that dinosaurs have when Crichton moves in, that pit in the stomach screaming out that it's all just a setup, that I'm just hanging around for inspiration. But, fuck it. I mean, things are solid. Things aren't so crazy. "Hah." I hope it sounded like a laugh. "I'm good for now." "But not forever? I feel you, I feel you. So, shit. You just gon' hit me up before it's time to head out for Ohio?" "I should." "Word. Aight then, I'll catch you later." And he's gone. What kinda bad luck shit am I on? Knew I should've found someone from across the fucking country, someone I wouldn't just bump into’not that this wasn't on the small end of the odds, I mean. Shit though, that's why it's bad luck. Good luck would've been. Many, many, many other options. All I could possibly need is a lighter. Exodus. Easy, pack of six. Easy, down the conveyer. Easy, pay. Easy, out the door’and not the one guarded by the missionaries in a land of missions. So, turn around. Fucking listen to me, feet. Better. I can take a long diagonal run across a parking lot better than I can take that confusion again. I can make it past a closed Walmart bank that only gives checking accounts to the people guaranteed to overdraw, I can stroll on by a nail salon with a sign saying it's gonna be closed for the Vietnamese New Year because they “care about their families" (goddamned condescending, innit” I’ve got nothing against mine, really), and I can turn the corner to see my good friend Jordan White talking to a cop. It doesn't look like the cool, casual style of talking’I'm doubting Duke on a 4 loss run is the subject, at least. Alright. Fuck. I'll shove my bags and receipt in the inner pocket of my jacket, and I'll walk out incredibly suspicious. Maybe even run past, screaming about how much I love stealing shit from Walmart. There's gotta be a better plan. Jordan looks pissed, backpedalling from the glow of the overhang to the shadows between a soda machine and the wall. Did he steal something? No, there's a bag sitting on the ground by 'em. Did he run out screaming how much he loves stealing shit from Walmart? I might've heard him, but. Oh. Yeah. The fact that he's incredibly drunk. New plan. Out the doors, use words to my advantage. Words like, "excuse me, officer;" words like "my friend giving you any truoble?" "Friend of yours is mighty drunk. Who's driving?" "I am." Jordan looks at me like I'm not the one with a good idea. "You tie off a few with him?" The cop’one of those snarltoothed bloated old cops, from a day before fitness was required and unevolved since before the hammerhead shark’comes up to me. His nose is wrinkling. I sure don't smell like alcohol. Heeeeeeeeeeeooeh. "No, sir. Designated driver." "Just been smokin'?" "Yeah, ran through about a pack waitin' on-" "You know that ain't what I meant." "What, what? Oh, wait. Marijuana? No sir; no, sir." He snorts again, eyes me on a slant. "Lemme see your receipt." Sure, whatever. Hurry this shit along. "I don't see those cookies on it." "Cookies? Oh yeah. Got 'em from the fundraisers over there. Brother John, or something." "Joe? Good kid." I get the receipt back’and, if I ever have to make nice with a cop again because of this fuckup Jordan White, I'm going to murder both of them. "Just get your friend the hell outta here before I have to arrest him for public drunkenness." "Will do. Have a good night." I'm waving and I'm moving and I'm dragging Jordan and he's dragging his bag of clanking glass bottles and he's mumbling about something that can't matter much. The wind's biting, or the wind bites, or whatever. I make a lot better time than I'd expected getting to my car, even for having to shove Jordan in. "Yo, Sage. Fuck was that?" "That was me saving your ass. Shit." "It was cool, dog. I had that shit under control." "Fuck did you manage that one?" "Hah, shit. He was all 'you can't be drunk in public,' and I was like 'I'm doing a good job at it, I mean.'" "Hah. Fuck. How far am I gonna have to drive you?" "My car's over by the door I was leavin'." "I mean to get to the fuckin' chick's house." "Man." "How far? You can come back for the car, fuckass. I'm not letting you burn my investment in you." "I got paid for one week, dog. One week. That ain't no investment." "I'm not letting myself have to fuckin' wrestle 'coz you've got no selfcontrol. Now, gimme directions." He starts giving directions. It's a few minutes before he bothers with anything else. "Yo." "Yeah?" "Sorry, man, shit. Sorry 'bout bein' a bitch 'bout this shit. Y'know how it is." "It's straight." "Nah, f'real. You did me a solid. I owe you. Thanks a fuckin' ton, brah. Shit, can I call you brah?" "I don't care." "Hahah, word. Shit, what you listenin' to?" "Neil Young." He turns it up. "Shit. I've heard dude before, not this song though." "It's Southern Man. Doesn't get played much in the south. No surprise, I mean." "Oh shit, man. Ain't this the song inspired Sweet Home?" "If inspired's the word you wanna use there, go for it. Sweet Home's more of a reply to it." "Shit." He falls back into silence, verbal death in the name of aural absorption. I saw cotton, and I saw black; tall white mansions and little shacks. Southern man, when will you pay them back? And Jordan's just nodding. Somehow his eyes go quiet, tighten into thin slits that end with his head rolling to the side, closer to the speaker. "Damn. Dude ain't like the south, did he?" "Dude didn't like the ingrown racist nature of the south. Black and white are just colors. South's still figurin' that out, y'know." "I hear you, I hear you. Shit's true, still though. Fuckin' harsh way dude's got a puttin' it." "Wasn't harsh enough to change anything." "Shit, it's better than it was back 'en." "Not because of some sissy Canadian songwriter." "Left up here. It's gon' be the third house past the stop sign onna left." I nod. "You sure you ain't tryna come in and chill? Ain't gotta fuck a bitch, I mean, y'know? Plenty of shit to do with a dime like her roomie, plenty of shit that ain't cheatin', y'know whaImean?" It's a good idea. "Nah, not tonight." "That's word. Shit. Aight, then. Thanks, an' shit. Hit me up?" "Yeah." "This one." I pull in. "Well shit. Guess I'm rollin' out here. Peace." And he's gone. Next: I'm gone. Next: It's gonna be a long, long drive. Under: the desolate expanses outside of earth and outside of my head; screens of stars suspended in the aspic of some inky nothing, in black matter. How much of the white is a lie? How much of the light steaming at us is from stars dead eons ago? How long until the black replaces them, replaces everything? Then, what replaces the black??or’If there is only black, then what will it hurt if we call the black white??or’Well, does it all end black no matter we call it? Fuck. Maybe they're parts of one whole. Maybe they define themselves, defining it in the process’and it isn't grey. No. Grey's too damn boring. What we really need is color. Any color, so long as it's as brilliant as the sun and as eternal as the black and the white. Course, that'd take good luck.
