Date: Timeless [Scene fades up on a television watching one of Ultimate Fighting Championship's DVDs. X is sitting with his back to the camera soaking up the brutal imagery of 'The Huntington Beach Bad Boy' Tito Ortiz and 'The Iceman' Chuck Lidell pounding each other.] Larabee: Now X, this is what you have to concentrate on doing. I know that you've been all over the world learning from the masters of puroresu and lucha libre, but this is different. X:..... Larabee: Mayhem wants your blood. That's all there is to it. He wants to taste it. He wants at the end of the night for LeStatt and Stevens to be bathed in it. X:..... Larabee: After all, he says that you have sinned. Not once, but twice. You've cost him two matches. Personally, I don't see what his problem is. You did what you were told to do. You were ordered to make an impact. You did that and now Bryan Mayhem is bitching and complaining about it. X:..... Larabee: He talks smack, that's all there is to it. He thinks that just because he's bigger, stronger, dumber and uglier than you that he's going to put you into oblivion. You broke his commandment by pissing him off. Boo hoo to him. Bryan Mayhem needs to worry more about getting his *** kicked for being a little *****. Complaining that two matches didn't go his way. Did you pin him both those times? No. Did you count the three those times? No. All you did was make it known that you were there, and he doesn't like it. He wants you to play by his rules, that's fine. He's also going to have to play by my rules. I don't care if he's heartless enough to fight his own brother in a match like this. Just proves to me that this Marilyn Manson wanna-be is a disloyal moron that deserves anything that's handed him. Whether it's a shot at a title or an *** beating. Now X, are you charged? Are you ready to take this son-of-a-motherless goat out? X:..... Larabee: Good. Because I think that it's time that Mayhem pays for what he brought on himself. You're final job with this man is to make him tap out. Don't let a knockout be good enough. I want him tapping like Gregory Hines at a softshoe convention. You hear me? [Scene fades to black.]
