Taking It Back

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: NOVUMBUR Today we find YOUR Entertainment Saviour Philip M. Atken and Dr. Dirk Dickwood PhD in some form of Micky Mouse degree sitting around the back offices of Dirk Dickwood Enterprises. Well I say back offices, I mean only office... which is at the back of Dirk's garden... next to the BBQ. Atken: You know... Dickwood: No I don't. If I did know, I wouldn't have stopped that statement. Atken: You know... Dickwood: NO I DON'T. Atken: You know! Dickwood: What? What do I know? Atken: That you can't standing living without me. Dickwood: That's merely true on a technicality, that technicality being my wife. Atken: How is Isla? Dickwood: Small and hideous. Atken: Much like the children then. Guess we know who had the dominant genes in that pool. Dickwood: Dirk Jr. only looks like that because Isla tried to bite his face off the other week. Atken: What did I tell you about letting her escape her cage? Dickwood: I believe you told me all of mankind will perish. Atken: Well, I was close. I still ponder why you are still married to a woman you find tremendously ugly with a repugnant personality. Dickwood: That's marriage for you. Atken: It's why I pay for all my relationships. STDs be damned, marriage is the worst one of all. Dickwood: How clever of you to say. Or was that cliche. One or the other. Atken: It's funny because you named your children One and The Other. Dickwood: Didn't we just establish Dirk Jr. Atken: I don't know, I rarely pay attention to anything I say. Mostly because I know it'll just be me either insult you, your family or talking about my latest hooker binge. Dickwood: And wrestling? Atken: Well of course, wrestling FUNDS the hooker binge. Dickwood: I didn't know you could binge on hookers. Atken: You're a married man, of course you wouldn't know about that. Although last time I tried it I woke up in a dumpster full of rusty needles next to an abortion clinic. It's moments like that that could possibly make you re-evaluate your life. Dickwood: The only way you would re-evaluate your life would be upon death. Atken: Don't be silly Dirk, that's not possible, when I'm dead I wouldn't be able to think. Dickwood: This being my point. Atken: ... Dickwood: That reminds me, didn't I quit this arrangement like two weeks ago? Atken: YOU HURT MY FEELINGS! TAKE IT BACK! Dickwood: No seriously, didn't I quit... Atken: TAKE IT BACK. Dickwood: But... Atken: TAKE IT BACK. Dickwood: Fine, fine. I take it back. Atken: Much better. Anyway, you didn't quit, turns out unnecessary aggression is a side effect of ether poisoning. Dickwood: How the hell was I meant to know the roofie was 99% ether? Atken: By reading the label. Dickwood: I'm a superagent, I don't have time for such trivialities as reading. Atken: Well we won the match anyway. Which reminds me, I need to send Caldera that match. Proof GCW is already one up on PRIME. Dickwood: But it didn't take place in either promotion. Atken: Well yeah, and it was also a victory of Dusk. Who hasn't won a match over that emofag. That and no one wants to see you in an Incredible Hulk costume EVER again. Heck, I had to burn my eyes after seeing that and I've seen Isla. You know what, scratch the video idea, it'll only drive Caldera to suicide. I'm still awesome though. Dickwood: So I'm informed of. Atken: By who? Dickwood: You. Atken: Oh. Anyway, I've already done my part for the Interfed show. Dickwood: No, that was your part for a completely unrelated NFW show. Atken: Can't they just air that match in the middle of the show. Dickwood: I doubt Miles would be happy with that... then you know what that whole place is capable of... Atken: Aww man, I wanted to see an entire PPV crowd attempt to stab themselves in the eyes simultaneously at the sight of you and now that dream with never be fulfilled. Just think of the lawsuits that'd cause. Dickwood: I'm now starting to realise why you used to bill yourself as "The Destroyer of Companies" on your CV. Not only did you put it down, but you stressed it as a hiring point. Atken: It showed I was all powerful and if they got in my way they would be destroyed. It's quite simple really. Dickwood: This is why you had to work for the Fox Network instead of wrestling for two years, you know that right? Atken: People still complement me on the "Peeing on the Homeless". Pity it didn't make it past pilot. Stupid police and their stupid laws. Dickwood: Regardless, I felt that in order to rise your exposure within GCW you really needed to be part of the this PRIME/GCW Supershow or whatever it is. I wasn't really paying attention either. Atken: Sounds like a great idea, so where's my title shot? Dickwood: How about you WIN a match first. Atken: I would win matches if you started doing your bloody job and interfered on my behalf, causing me a victory but the past month you've been too "busy" to accompany me to ringside. I hired you to be my manslave. Dickwood: Agent. Atken: Whatever. The point is you should be at ringside with me, we both know I'm a thoroughly incompetent ring technician, brains is where my game is at and I need a pawn. You are fat, tubby and all around a useless human being so you make the perfect pawn. It's REALLY hard to hit a guy in the face with a chair without a fat guy jumping up and down on the apron and letting his mantits distract the referee. Dickwood: I can't afford the indecent exposure charges anymore. Atken: Fine, I'll think of a new plan down the line. Being an Entertainment Saviour is hard work, let me assure you. Dickwood: Regardless, at the next show you have a three way tag match. I think you're teaming up with some French guy. Against some chick and that foreign guy and Tyson DeBough and that guy who raped all them sheep. Atken: Let me think this through... well I beat up girls all the time so that's not a problem. I don't think that foreign guy actually exists, DeBough... I've already beaten and.... raped all them sheep? Dickwood: You know, the guy. Atken: No, no I don't. Dickwood: He raped all them sheep. Atken: This is a made up story isn't it. Dickwood: Only in my head. Atken: Look, let's just get this mess over with. I need to go make complaints at the arena anyway, bloody company and it's mishandling of me. Sighhhhhhhhhhh. Dickwood: Did you just say "sigh" instead of actually sighing? Atken: I live on a much higher plane of existence that you Dirkykins. That's how we roll here. Dickwood: I roll on my wife. Atken: That's not what I've heard. And with that, the camera somehow ran out of tape. It was riveting stuff regardless.
