Discovering DeBough: Part II

13 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 13th, 2007 July 32nd, 1999 Inhale. Exhale. Tyson sat in the back of a Lincoln Towncar, with a cigarette in his hand, eyes fixed out the window. Tommy was driving, and Joey was in the front seat next to him. Neither had said anything since the warehouse, and Joey had even given Tyson a cigarette when demanded. DeBough was a bit curious as to what all this was about, since he hadn’t seen Nick in over a year and a half. Nick and Tyson were best friends from the time they were able to walk, until about eighteen months ago. It was money that split them up. Dirty money. Drug money. Nick was addicted to Cocaine, and got Tyson started on it, and they were doing deals together, which got bigger, and bigger, until they finally had one deal that was supposed to go like clockwork, but it ended sour. Nick fucked over Tyson, and his brother Chad ended up dead. Nick was responsible really, but he held it against Tyson, to cover his own guilt. This must be some attempt at revenge. .~. Present Day Number one contender to the GCW Television Championship. He’d done exactly what he’d said he’d do. Walk into Warpath with a point to prove, and walk out having proven it. Next step? Ultimately Jay Terror, but for now it would have to be Paddy O’Shea. Both O’Shea and Ramey were in the match, but basically gave Tyson the win by disqualifying themselves because of personal issues. Which, left Tyson feeling tainted. Normally, he was the type that wouldn’t care at all how he won, just so long as he won. But for some reason, this victory just didn’t seem like it was real. Which made the match with O’Shea that much sweeter. Beat O’Shea, redeem himself. “So his head got blown off?” It was the voice of Andrew, Tyson’s best friend. Tyson was back in his apartment, and the two were dueling eachother in Guitar Hero, while discussing the past weeks experiences. “Yea.” Tyson responded. “It freaked Monica out pretty bad.” Andrew just laughed. “Uh yea, dude’s head was gone.” “Heh, yea.” And then in a more somber tone. “I can almost guarantee it was Nick.” “Nick?” Andrew asked. “But why” I thought that was over like six years ago?” They finished the song, and Tyson put down his guitar. “I thought so too, but apparently Nick didn’t. He’s a very bitter guy.” “For real.” Andrew got up and went to the kitchen. “So what’s the plan?” “Nothing right now.” Tyson said, standing. “I’ll let him find me. The one thing that will piss off Nick more than anything, is me ignoring him. And then, it’s just a matter of time.” “So, GCW then?” “Basically. I’ve got a match with O’Shea this week.” Andrew laughed again. “The irish dude?” “Yea.” “Nice. Should be a nice win then eh?” Tyson shrugged. “Ramey will most likely show.” “Yea, so?” “He is my only loss so far in GCW.” Andrew walked over to him and slapped him in the back of the head. “What the FUCK are you talking about” Jesus man, is this the same Tyson DeBough from a few weeks ago?” “I..uh..” “What, did seeing a guys head blown off shake you up pookie?” Andrew continued. “Alright, enough!” Tyson said firmly. “Get the camera.” “Say what?” “We’re going old school.” .~. They arrived at a large estate about a half hour after they’d started driving. Joey and Tommy both escorted him into the estate and sat him down in what looked like a board room. A large cherry stained oak table surrounded by multiple chairs, with a large executive chair at the head occupied the room. Naturally, Tyson sat down in the executive chair. “What the hell are you doing?” Enter Nick Lawson. One of the biggest drug lords in the United States, and quite possibly the western hemisphere. His legal enterprise was a toilet paper company, so that he looked legit to the government. So far, it held pretty decently. Nick was an average sized man, with blonde hair and blue eyes. He wore dark framed glasses now, and had grown a goatee. “Making myself comfortable, what’s it look like?” Tyson responded. Nick’s eyes narrowed. “Get out of my chair.” “Oh, this is yours?”” Tyson feigned mock surprise. “Joey! Tommy!” The two goons came running. “What the hell happened” I thought I told you to torture him.” Nick said, clearly irritated. “Well, he told us that?” Joey started. “And then he said he’d?” Tommy finished. Nick gritted his teeth. “Get out of my sight.” Tommy and Joey left. Tyson chuckled. “Good guys though.” “Shut up Tyson.” “Um. Why the fuck am I here Nick?” “I need you.” “Fuck off.” “I have a job I can’t pull unless you’re involved.” Nick said. “So you had Joey and Tommy torture me?”” Pause. “Or, attempt to?” Nick scoffed. “I knew those two idiots wouldn’t be able to do jack shit to you Tyson. It got you here didn” t it?” Tyson nodded. “Fair enough. What’s the deal?” “I have an internation deal. Someone in France wants to buy a whole lot of blow.” Nick said. “And” You can’t do that on your own?” “It’s with Jenna.” Tyson almost choked. “My ex fiance” How the hell am I supposed to help there?”” Nick sat down. “You know her better than anyone. I need you to be the negotiator.” “Fuck. No.” “I can pay you.” “Fuck. No.” Nick sighed. “I’d hoped it wouldn’t have to be like this.” He snapped his fingers, and Joey and Tommy came walking out holding a woman who was bound and gagged. “Chloe!” Tyson yelled, and lunged for her, only to be met with a clothesline from Nick. “Now.” He brushed his shirt off. “What do you say Tyson?” .~. [Fade.] [Clarity.] “I’m the next big thing here in GCW.” [Tyson stands, leaning against a wall, smoking a cigarette.] “And if I need to go through O’Shea, and Ramey, and Porter’fuck, even Ambrosi or White to prove it, then so be it.” [Inhale.] “You fuckheads who were in the royal with me” You should feel like shit. It wasn’t even that hard of a fight for me.” [Exhale.] “O’Shea. While you and Ramey did your little dance together to the back room.” [Smirk.] “We know that’s where you two went.” [He takes another drag on his cigarette.] “While you two were doing your thing, I earned a shot at Terror.” [He smiles.] “While that in itself isn’t to important, it’s just another rung to climb on my ascention to the top of this god forsaken place.” [He pushes himself off the wall.] “And Paddy” You’re just the next step.” [Cheap grin.] “Soon everyone will know my name.” [Tyson DeBough.] “And you’ll just be another drunk Irishman.” [Ouch. Personal shot numero dos.] “Clich”, I know. The whole “rise to the top thing.” But hey. I never said I needed to be original to get there.” [He ashes his cigarette.] “So fuck off.” [Black.] “See you in the ring.” [Fin.]
