Decisons Part 1

13 December 2006

Date: 12/13/06 A blonde man sits in a dark room huddled in a corner. You can't see his face except for the light of a cigarette in his mouth. The camera tries to focus in on him, but there's nothing but complete darkness. “There are times in this life where, you make decisions that will affect the rest of your life,” he says coldly. “Decisions where you wish you could've changed something later on in life. Sometimes, you get the chance to change those life-altering things. Other times you don't. In the case of Fiery Passion, the two of them have made decisions that drastically changed their lives. With Keiko “The Dragon' Yoshimoto, she made a choice of love over honor, at least in her sister Umi's eyes. Two weeks ago she was banned from her own house in Seoul, Korea per her sister's order. For two weeks, Keiko tried everything she could think of to get back to see her sister, and each time Umi shot the Dragon down. Let's see what her decisions bring her this week.” Time: December 15, 206 Place: Oahu, Hawaii Today was just another day in the life for Keiko Yoshimoto right now. Everyday she would make twenty or more calls to her sister, Umi, only to receive the answering machine each time. Each time the message would tell the Oriental to stop calling. Once the phone calls stopped, Keiko resorted to emails only to have them sent back to her saying this address no longer existed. After all this, Keiko would sit in her room and cry. Family and honor meant everything in the world to Keiko, but at this time she felt she didn't have it anymore. For days, this is all the Oriental would do everyday. She made it where she didn't have time for anything else, including Kawanaha Kuno. This didn't sit well, with the Hawaiian Goddess and she was determined to find out what was wrong. During the last week, Keiko was able to talk to fellow stable mate Ukyo Kushinaga about how to restore her honor. Unfortunately, Keiko did not like the first option which was committing ritual suicide, called the seppuko. The other option would be to stand up to people who she knows to be dishonorable and atone for her actions. Again, this put Keiko between a rock and a hard place, because if she began to make her voice known, like her friend Hope has done, Keiko would be left with nothing, and she knew she would possibly be worse off than Hope. As Keiko was sitting in her room today like, she has been doing a soft rasp came upon her door. She tried to clean herself up before softly saying “Enter”, with a cracked voice. Hoping, that it was Hope Clarkson, she frowned just a little when Kawanaha entered the room. “Great, I take it, I'm being punished,” Keiko hissed removing her clothes. “What gives you that impression,” Kawanaha calmly said. The calmness alone frightened The Dragon. “I've avoided you,” she responded, “I've yet to grace your presence. Therefore, I'm to be punished. After all, you did Hope.” “Hope has done much more than that and you know it. Besides I don't punish those who need help.” Keiko actually laughed. She couldn't help but do so. “You help those who need it! Since when do you do that? I mean you've banished Hope to being your servant when you can clearly see that she is possessed! She needs your help no more than ever! We've got our title match coming up at NC-17, and now Pain and Pleasure wants a row with us, and I need her as Hope and not this demon or whatever the hell is inside her!” “It's not my concern on her present possession. It is my concern that she stole my man from me! They both lied about it, sneaking around my mansion about it. That is why they are BOTH being punished! Quite frankly, the way they dishonored me…” “What the fuck do you know about dishonor or even honor at all,” Keiko interrupted instantly, “Umi, was right, I was just too blind to see it! You're nothing more than a damn puppet master. You plotted Hope and me against each other for your affections.” “Which you won, if I'm not mistaken.” “Yes, but it wasn't until recently with the last possession scare from Hope, that I didn't have to. I had your affections from the get go. You're just using Hope and Christopher so they can bear your child!” “I didn't want that burden on you. You've seen how much I cared for you since day one. I gave you paradise, free will, anything you could ever want and more.” “I want Hope! I want her to have some of the privileges I have again! I want my house back!” Kawanaha interrupted her mid rant, “Hope will get her back when her debt to Kit is repaid! As far as your house, you signed the place over to Umi. Something I advised you against, because I had a feeling this would happen. Don't get me wrong, Umi is just like you with the looks and personality. One reason why I find her very attractive is because of this.” Keiko just sighed in disgust, “You don't get it do you. Not only do you want Umi right now, you are missing the big point! I want to go home! I want to go back to Korea so I can meditate properly and be with my family when they need me. I want one of my countries back and above all, I want my sister back!” Kawanaha stepped closer to Keiko, and Keiko flinched away from her fearful she would be cracked across the face, if not worse. Yet, the Hawaiian Goddess just gently rubbed her face. A tender moment from someone who Keiko still believed to evil. “I'll tell you what, what if I promised that after NC-17, we fly back to Korea and I straighten this mess out with you and your sister. And if this doesn't work out, I'll just buy you another damn house.” Keiko's eyes lit up at the thought of seeing her homeland again. She did have Japan, but her sister wasn't there. Her sister was in Korea, in her old house, where Keiko trained to become the person she is today. It was the only home she had ever known. She quickly threw her arms around her lover. “This is one promise you better keep,” Keiko said over Kawanaha's shoulders, “If you succeed you will make me the happiest woman on the face of this world.” Kawanaha nodded pushing her away softly, “Why wouldn't I keep this promise? You doubt my word?” “Yes I do, but it's only because I'm confused now more on what's right and what's wrong.” She had been fighting it back the whole time, but finally Keiko let a few tears fall. Kawanaha just looked at her waiting to see what her lover would do. “I feel like I'm a slave, if not something worse. My friend is being punished over something that isn't in her control. I'm at the edge Kawanaha of wondering if there's anything left in this world for me to live for. Al these decisions I've made in life just seem to hurt people!” “Search, your heart Keiko, is there anything you have done to hurt me.” It didn't take long for her to answer, “No, I've just shown you love.” “Then that's what you are truly living for. Keiko, your heart is so massive, it just reaches out to so many people. You're not evil. People around you tend to do bad things, and for that they have to be punished. What you would have done if Hope stole me away from you?” “I would probably have her killed,” Keiko giggles, “But she is my friend.” “As you know honor holds bounds even with your friends as well as your family. I'm the head of this mansion, so my word is golden here. You dishonor me you shall pay. In a way, I too live by this Bushido code of life you live by. Hope done wrong, she is being punished, along with her man in life.” “Are you happy the two found love,” Keiko couldn't help yelling it out. Now it was Kawanaha's turn to look shocked, if just for a mere moment, “I'd be lying if I said no. The two deserve love as much as anyone in this world. I don't approve of stealing another's lover from the other. I did love Christopher, I never told him. I do care about you, you're the only thing I have right now.” Keiko smiled as she heard this. Kawanaha continued, “I'll admit I did have you do things that I know you didn't want to do. It was however payment for the dishonor. Possessed or not, Hope attacked my sister and her lover, which is a crime for me! She will pay until all of Kit's medical bills are paid for, not before. All her money from wrestling and her singing comes straight to Kit! Do you understand? I will not make you do anything else that you don't want to do in injuring Christine, because I know how much you love her, or Christopher, because I can sense a bond unless it's part of payment from Hope.” “I understand,” Keiko said more calmly, “You do live by the code.” “That's your influence on me. Family is very important to me, and when they are attacked, I will turn into the vilest bitch you've ever seen. As far as your sister goes, it deeply saddens me that you two aren't getting along. I will tell you this, she has legal rights to the dojo. I may be able to convince her to let you visit, but as for your own place why would you want one?” “I need my privacy as much as you do. Besides it's a place for me to rain with out disturbing you and the house, since I have to train with Hope. Plus, if there is a time I want my toy Christine over.” “I see,” Kawanaha says smiling, “You are starting to be more like me everyday. I think I should just award you with your own house. I don't want you to be overseas though. I want you close to me, in case Hope goes crazy on us again. How would you like a place here in Oahu?” “It's not either one of my countries. It's not Japan or Korea.” “Silly girl,” Kawanaha chuckles, “With mine and Kit's money you don't think we can bring your home to us? We'll see to it that all the elements of Japan and Korea are here with you. This will be your own mansion, something you've always dreamed of, if I'm not mistaken.” Keiko smiled broadly, “Master Wong always wanted me to find my won place so I could train to be the warrior that I am.” “And as for Master Skylar, I've ‘talked' to him, he'll be seeing you shortly for another training session. Consider it a favor you will owe me later tonight, that is if you still want to sleep in my bed?” “You would have to kill me first, if I ever refused to be in your bed.” “Then it's settled, you'll live here in Hawaii, I'll help you take care of any messes you have with Umi and everything will go back to normal.” Kawanaha kisses her lover as they have many times. The Olive Skinned beauty begins to leave the room, but Keiko calls for her just as she reaches the door. “Was there something else,” she asked. Keiko sighed, “Well your only condition for me having my own place is for me to live here in Oahu correct.” “Yes.” “Then I too have two conditions for me to live in Oahu.” “I was expecting this,” Kawanaha said seductively, “What are they.” “One is Faith; I don't want her anywhere near me. You will not forget the threats her and I had with each other. I stand by my word, that one day she will fall. If you care about Kit as you say you do, you will keep her away from me.” “I think that can easily be arranged, and the other.” “The other is not quite as easy. I want Hope as my personal servant when I get my house. She will still send Kit money yes, but she will be out of your hair. You will have peace in your mansion again.” Kawanaha smiled at the thought, but she too had to show her sterness, “You can have her as long as she's your servant and nothing else! No sex, no punching bag, just your personal servant. If she gets out of line she will suffer my wrath not yours.” “Agreed,” said Keiko extending her hand. Kawanaha shook it giggling, “I knew you would turn out just like me. Even you surprised me there.” “If you think that was a surprise what until I cash in that favor.” “And when were you thinking of doing this.” “Oh right about now.” Keiko pulls Kawanaha to her as the scene fades to black. The shot then returns to the smoking narrator. Laughing, “Keiko had her honor the whole time; she was just feeling guilty for all she has done with Kawanaha that hurt her friends. That's the price you pay living by the code. Keiko's honor is restored. However, don't forget Pain and Pleasure, she will still hurt people who are dishonorable. Too bad you fall in this category. This week, you are the ones that will see a Dragon fully focused to take you out. Last week was a fluke, this week, it's personal.” “Now for the final part of this story on decisions, Hope Clarkson's” To be Continued…
