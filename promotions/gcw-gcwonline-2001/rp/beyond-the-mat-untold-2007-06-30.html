Beyond The Mat: Untold

30 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 30th, 2007 A camera opens up showing Shawn Porter sitting on a small leather chair, looks to be alone in a dark room. There is one single light shining down him as he looks into the camera. He appears in a pair of black pants and shoes with no shirt. There looks to be some sort of interviewer and you can see the five o’clock shadow from Porter's beard already growing in. He sits there, not moving an inch just looking into the camera until finally..the interviewer appears to be Barry Goldstein asks how porter feels. He nods his head saying alright. The light has increased in the room.. “My first singles match here, I made an impact in the way no one would forget. People thought I was just being another typical hot aired tough guy when I said I was in this to out-do people. No one took me serious. Kyle, you were one of those people. All week I’ve hardly heard anything of you. So here I am, no gimmick, no jokes, no script. I will tell you and the rest of the world just who I truly am. Because GCW, I’m not so different from any other man. I’m just more willing to fight for survival by any means necessary. My whole life...there’s been one thing I could do very well. Out-do people. All I ever did was win. You might say I was one of those troubled kids but truth be told, I wasn’t. I just liked it. I had a passion for it. I wanted to be like the famous athletes I saw in the ring, the field, and court on Tv. I started training to become an Wrestler fighter at the age of 16. It was all I could ever think about. All I dreamed about. I lived and breathed amateur and pro wrestling. Every free moment I had, I was training or working out. Finally years on down the road I met with some of indys best. They took me in, taught me how to become a real fighter. Do you know how that feels” To be working side by side with those who you considered to be gods? I doubt any of you do.” Shawn takes a deep breath and continues to talk more... “You'll may serve your God, but you'll yet to look him in the eye. You’ve yet to hear his criticism of you personally. I’ve worked with some of Wrestling’s legends. Rocky Johnson! Harley Race! Chris Benoit! Then just as I was about to finally make it in a business I’ve dedicated my whole life to...I lose it all. It wasn’t even my fault! I had nothing to do with anything! Just because of my name, because of my own family’s activities I was brought down. You know what they told me for a reason to release me” “We can’t have that kind of publicity Shawn.” What is it you say GCW fans? God makes us all go through rough times as trials. That was no trial folks. That was an execution of a life. I nearly lost everything, including my women. You know you’ve hit rock bottom when you’re living off fifty bucks a week and can only afford to feed your family every few days. Where was the lord in that one y'all? Where was he when I needed him? I’ll tell you. He was giving all his strength and will to pukes like y'all. People who deserve nothing. 2 weeks ago y'all underestimated me, the days before my match Kyle James you guys showed no respect. I was just the loud, annoying, cocky, Philly kid to you.” Shawn takes a near by glass of water and starts to sip on it to wet his throat... “Y'all thought you would be funny and make a mockery of me. Well then, the tables have now turned. Everyone wants to be me now! know why. It’s simple, because I'm the main event.” Shawn said it with some much emotion towards the camera he nearly broken down. “Shawn I'm guess you have some hidden skeletons in your closet. You just may want to get it all out now. Its alright to tell a story to our fans and let them know the true you.” Said Barry Goldstein. “You requested this time and the effort Mr. McCullen has put into getting you some time to speak was priceless.” “Yeah, there is a lot on my chest and I'm ready to answer all your questions.” -Porter Barry Goldstein pulls out some Q cards. “Well i would love to know how your life was while growing up in Southern Philadelphia. How you became such an athlete, and most of all, what ticks you off on your day off??- Goldstein Shawn sits up and rubs his head. “Its ok Shawn, we got time for this” said the worried Goldstein. “Well growing up in South Philly was the worst of the worst. My family barely had anything. We were on welfare, we had a little bit of clothing. I had to work two jobs to support my family. My parents were on drug and life seemed hopeless. I knew i had to do something to keep my ass out of trouble and that was sports. One thing i could say was that my grades were great enough to get me into a good high school in Philadelphia. So in that high school we had every sports from baseball to wrestling. I tried every damn sport out until i was damn near exuded. But i mastered football and both wrestling. I knew one of those were gonna take me places. By the time i reached 15 i was ready to enter the lightweight class. I was pumped but i got into some trouble and was forced to jail for 8 months. My career went to the bottomless pit. So i started training extra hard for wrestling season to come back around. My parents finally got their shit together and we moved to Detroit. In Detroit i got into some more stuff that didn't held my wrestling career back but i wanted to represent Philly so i moved back with my aunt.” “So you got in trouble and was sentenced for 8 months?” “Yeah it was Robbery” “Robbery!”?- Goldstein “Yes...I had too. My family need food so i did what i had to do to survive. It was rough down here in Philly. But i wouldn't change anything if i could because thats what made me great.” “So what do you regret..”” “I got this little story to tell you...” ___________________________________________________________ Sunday June 18th 2006 Chester Township, Chester, PA Everyone has a propose in life and i guess mine is wrestling. I've done plenty of sports in my time but there's always this empty filling i get when I'm out on the road or waiting for the show to start. Have you ever tired doing something that your not supposed too? I'm not saying rob a bank or jumping off of the statue of liberty. I'm saying ride a monster truck or even purchase a entire family of huskies. Something crazy that you would laugh your ass off about later. I'm about to tell you about what i did about 4 days ago. It was so funny that i have it on camera. The vision that goes through my head is crazy and it may be a little to graphic to certain people ears. Well not that graphic; actually not graphic at all... Its Tuesday afternoon in Chester, Pennsylvania. Most of my family live here and its that time of year we all get together and enjoy each others company. That sounds cool for a regular family but my family is a bit over the top and thats why i hate coming back to visit. But Paul had some plans he had to do so I'm off the hook for now until next Wednesday. I saw my cousins from the south again, they appear to be drinking some hard liquor and arguing over the last update on ESPN. As much as i love a good argument i rather not get into this one. “Yo Cuzo come here!” I felt like the freaking' mouth piece for the argument. The argument was basically about the NFL Draft and who got the best pick in the draft. I could careless since i wrestle but they kept bitching over the most stupidest stuff. I told them that Oakland had the best pick. Everyone started to laugh as i walked off leaving them in questioning. My closet cousin to me would be Damion. Damion is a smart bright guy that you could never play for a fool. “Shawn, How you been?” I'm surprised he remembers me...after i super kicked him at last years family reunion. I usually don't like rolling with him because he's 3 years young than me. I asked him have he ever had the urge to do something amazing or out of the box. He told me that he would love to ride a motorbike. Right then and there it hit me...thats exactly what i wanted to do. I told him the idea and he laughed for a bit then he realize it wasn't a joke. “Are you serious” You got to take classes for that bro? Little that he knew, i took 15 hours worth of training last year in Miami. I was ready to get my motorcycle and ready to ride today. He told me he's up for the run. I got in his car and he drove me to the nearest dealership. It was Yamaha, I felt the urge to jump on a super-sport. There was so many to choose from i just couldn't decide the old fashion way. So i draw straws and came up with the Ebony Yamaha. I love its detail and shit, this bike is definitely for me. The dealer name was rick fuller a dealer from Ireland. Let me tell you, he's the worst smooth talker this world ever produced. Well after we talked for a half. He finally sold me the damn bike for $2'500. “You really gonna ride this joint?” said Damian in a joking way. I told him hell yeah and sat on my ride. Feeling like a man who just lost his virginity. I rode off to the motorcycle shop to get some gear. I actually picked this nice all white set and i loved it. A year pasted and i was the stuff. Until i got hit by a F-150. That messed up my Wrestling career and i was fired from WRW Promotion. I got into a good rehab and got back in the ring for a local Philadelphia promotion and no not E.C.W. ___________________________________________________________ “That by far was the worst experience I've ever had since Philly.” Shawn laughs at the memory. “So how the GCW experience been so far?” “Well for one its been a great ride. A ride that I'm in no hurry to stop. I know that every week i put that mask on to hide my feelings about my past. I know I'm number one in mind but my heart tells me I'm born from losers. I know I'll never be a loser in any sport and I'll carry that with me for the rest of my life.” “What makes you mad on a daily basis?” “I hate to see these ungrateful people out here telling us that we suck but we put on a damn show for them every week with smiles on our faces. Its gonna stop or I'll do something crazy like hurt one of them.?- porter “Well Shawn this was an amazing experience.” “Thanks Barry. They're will be more to come believe that.”
