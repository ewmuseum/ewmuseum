What a Jackass...

8 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.08.05 Fade? “How’s your chicken?” the voice of Ehren Moore asks. He is sitting at a table inside a restaurant. He is dressed in a brown American Eagle shirt with yellow stripes and a pair of dress cargo Khakis. His blonde hair is spiked into the air and his black goatee is nicely groomed. He sits with a French fry in his right hand. Across from him sits a young woman, probably close to Ehren’s age. She has a pearly white smile, shoulder length brown hair, with layered blonde highlights and some of the most beautiful emerald green eyes in the world. She is dressed in a black miniskirt, and a white low cut top, with a pink shirt on underneath. She holds a fork in her right hand and looks across the table to Ehren. Their choice of dining is good. It’s a good sized restaurant slash truck stop. Properly named Romines, but nicknamed R-Place by its frequent visitors, it sits along the busy Interstate 80. The table isn’t really cluttered, but it has enough items on it. Ehren’s cheeseburger is almost gone and his fries are running thin. The girl has a plate with a half-eaten grilled chicken breast located towards the center. “Good. Has good flavor.” She replies as she reaches down, stabs another piece and brings it up to her mouth. She chews it up and savors every last bit before setting her fork down and reaching for her glass of Iced Tea. She takes a sip and sets it back down. The other hand isn’t far behind though, bringing a napkin up to her lips and gently wiping them off. She folds the napkin up and sets it down as the waitress walks up. She sets a small folder down in the center of the table, where it is quickly snatched up by Ehren. The waitress is younger still, probably only twenty or twenty-one. She has a fairly large bust and is wearing a semi-low cut shirt, allowing a borderline amount of cleavage to show. She also has on black dress pants, maybe just a little too small, clinging tightly to her legs, thighs and rear end. To any man, this is a good thing. This girl is hot. She smiles and turns, her long blonde hair spinning around her face, covering her baby blue eyes. As she walks away and Ehren begins to open the folder to see the bill, he continues to watch the waitress walk away. He is your typical male getting caught up in the physical beauty of a very attractive girl. “Ehren. Ehren?”.Ehren!” The girls yells. Ehren snaps out of his hypnotized look and meets the girls with a smile. “Yes Brittany?” He replies. He knows he was caught, but Brittany as we now know her has sort of brushed it off as typical male behavior. She smiles lightly at him as she looks at her cell phone. She opens the flip phone, waits a few seconds and then closes it, sliding it back into her small green purse sitting on the table. Ehren smiles as he reaches back to his wallet and brings it out of his back pocket. He ruffles through it and pulls out sixty dollars; a fifty and a ten. “You waiting for a call?” Ehren says as he points to Brittany’s purse, where her cell phone now rests. “Oh no. I was just looking for the time. It’s six twenty. We should probably go if we are going to catch the seven o’clock movie.” She says with the same small smile. Ehren nods in agreement and stands. He slips his wallet back into his pocket and picks up the folder, money inside. He slides the ten dollar bill out and sets it in the center of the table as a tip. He slides his chair back in as Brittany does the same and begins heading for the counter. Ehren walks up behind her and sets the envelope on the counter, the cashier swift to pick it up. She opens it and rings the meals up, tendering the fifty dollar bill. “How was everything tonight?” The cashier asks with a smile. She touches a few buttons on the screen in front of her as Ehren waits patiently. She touches one last button that pops the register open and catches it just before it hits her in the stomach. Brittany has come to rest in front of a large window, checking her hair from the reflection. The cashier counts the change back to herself, and then reaches towards Ehren, who holds his hand out. “Wonderful. Food was great.” He replies as the girl counts the money out loud and nods in acknowledgement. She drops two quarters, a dime, and three pennies into Ehren’s open hand, quickly followed by a five dollar bill. Ehren fold the five up and slides it into his pocket as his eye wanders over to the “loose change” bowl. Ehren stands for a few quick seconds looking at it, but eventually drops the change into his pocket. He nods towards the cashier and turns to meet Brittany who has finished checking herself out. They move forward, side by side, making their way towards the door. “Did I tell you I was a wrestler?” Ehren asks as the audio fades as the two turn a corner and walk out of the restaurant, into the parking lot. Fade?.. “After I lost the Alternative Title over in Baltimore Championship Wrestling, I got a push into the semi-main event spot.” Ehren says with a chuckle. Brittany chuckles as well, but it’s a forced chuckle. Even her smile is turning fake. The two now walk from Ehren’s car across a parking lot to a movie theatre. Ehren hops up to the door first, seemingly to open in for Brittany, but instead he opens it and walks in, just holding it open for the girl to take hold of and walk in herself. He walks inside and she follows, with the smile entirely gone now. “They gave me a shot at the World Title, but I didn’t win it. That’s about the time I got the call from GCW.” “That’s nice.” Brittany says, the enthusiasm lacking from her speech. “What are we going to see?” She asks looking up to the displayed show times. She scans the board for something good. “Oh! How about Elizabeth Town” That looks really good!” She exclaims, with actual excitement in her voice. “A chick flick” No thanks.” He says as he walks up to the ticket counter. “Two for Saw II.” Ehren requests. The older lady behind the counter looks up at Ehren and tilts her glasses to the tip of her nose. After a few seconds she pushes them back up. “Can I see you’re ID please?” The cashier asks. Ehren flashes an offended look to the lady. His wallet is already in hand. He was busy filing through for money, which he puts on pause for the time being to slam his fist down on the counter. “I am twenty five years old!” He exclaims. Some people nearby stop and turn to look, which embarrasses Brittany. She has begun to back up, but decides to step in. She takes a deep breathe and moves forward, meeting Ehren at the counter. “Ehren, take it as a compliment. Just show her the ID and let’s go.” Brittany backs up as Ehren sighs and takes out his Driver’s License, handing it to the cashier. She quickly inspects it and hands it back, also taking the twenty dollar bill from Ehren’s hand. After a few moments, Ehren has received his change and turns to Brittany. “Thank you sir. Enjoy your show.” The cashier says. “Yeah, whatever.” Is Ehren’s reply with the tone of pissed off in his voice. He walks forward a few steps and meets Brittany, who in turn meets his shoulder with a punch and a twisted face. Ehren laughs a bit, and rubs his shoulder. “Saw II” I don’t want to see a gory movie. I want a romance.” Brittany says, flashing the cutest puppy dog lip in Ehren’s direction. Ehren just shakes his head as he moves off towards the theatre hallway. “Okay then. But don’t you want popcorn or anything?” She asks as she points towards the snack counter. “Nope. I can’t be eating that kind of stuff on my diet.” Ehren replies as he stops and turns. He pats himself on the stomach and smiles in Brittany’s direction. “What if I wanted something?” Brittany asks with a rightly needed bitchy tone in her voice. Ehren kind of rolls his eyes and pulls his wallet out once again. He opens it and takes a ten dollar bill out, making his way towards Brittany. He hands her the money and her movie ticket. “There you are. I’ll meet you in the movie.” Ehren says as he turns, sliding his wallet into his back pocket. He makes his way over to the ticket taker and hands the ticket over. The teenage boy takes the ticket and rips it in half. Ehren takes a half and walks forward into the hallway, down to theatre eight. Fade? “Eww.” Is all Brittany can muster as the two walk out of the hallway. Brittany’s face is white with disgust, but Ehren’s is filled with glee. He has a wide smile on his face. Brittany looks over to him, seeing his smile. “How can you be smiling after all that blood”!” She exclaims in question. Ehren continues to smile, and even chuckles. “It was cool.” He says. Ehren reaches up and touches his still firmly spiked blonde hair. The two continue their forward motion, growing closer to the door. Just as the two of them reach the door, a little boy, aged no more than five years old comes running up. “Ehren! Ehren!” He exclaims. The young boy has a head of blonde hair himself and holds a small notebook and pen in one hand. The boy’s smile is large and one to melt your heart. Brittany’s look of disgust turns to that of adorabilty. She places a hand on her chest and lets out one of those typical girlie “Awwwwwh’s. “I see you in GCW! Can I get your autograph?” The little boy asks. Brittany’s look violently changes again though. “Scram kid. Don’t bother me.” Ehren says as he pushes the door open and walks out to the sidewalk. The little boy turns, head down and dejected, walking slowly back to his waiting mother. Brittany pushes out the same door and physically turns Ehren to face her. “What in the hell?” She says. “I’ve had enough of you!” Ehren gets a look of confusion on his face. “You have been nothing but an arrogant jerk tonight.” “What do you mean?” Ehren asks. “First off, you refused to drop sixty three cents into the community change bowl. Then you continued to talk about wrestling, knowing I had no passion to hear about those stories. Next, you didn’t even hold the door for me. You practically forced me to go see a movie I didn’t want to see. You went into the theatre without waiting for me. And you refuse to sign a piece of paper for a little boy?” Brittany says, bitchy as ever. Before Ehren can say anything, she pushes past him, reaching into her purse and taking out a set of keys. “Don’t call me again, or else I will file harassment charges.” She says as she takes the short walk to her car, looking over her shoulder. The look on Ehren’s face stays the same. Confusion. He’s so caught up in himself to realize what he’s done. How could she NOT want to sleep with him? With his body? What the hell? That’s all that is going through his mind. He stares blankly forward as Brittany’s car races by, Brittany on her cell phone. Ehren throws his arms into the air and sighs as he steps off the curb and begins the short trip to his car. Fade?
