Decisions, Decisions...

9 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/09/11 It hasn't been long since Robyn connected the dots and presented the police with the evidence that proved me guilty of three murders. I, however, cannot blame her for being so persistent to find me since I did kill husband. Her daughter will now be forced to live her life without getting to know her father. Although she only has her mother to blame, since that was the price to be paid for trying to have me arrested. I also learned that Father Tracer and The Following was the reason for me and Katrina reconnecting. Which has been a blessing since the teachings of her life have enabled Katrina and me to put our problems behind us and move forward. Becoming a member of The Following in general has been a blessing for me, everyone accepts me, they accept the monster inside of me and help my cravings to grow. After all of the problems Robyn caused me to have I had to devise a plan that would not only get the police off of my trail, but bring them to me at the same time. When Father Tracer took me under his wing as one of his pupils he told me how the roots of The Following touched inside different departments and political influences around the world. His teachings were also important as he always stressed that The Following's most important rule was sacrifice and we all need to make sacrifices in our brother's and sister's time of need. With that in mind I told Father Tracer of my troubles and then explained my plan as well. In agreement we sent Brother Issac to Robyn's home, where he was dressed as me. We arranged for him to have Katrina take Robyn's daughter for a joyride, while he kept Robyn occupied. They called for the police to have them come arrest Issac, not knowing it wasn't me the entire time. Detective Amande Jordan arrived at Robyn's home in quick fashion to take Brother Issac into custody. After rounds of questioning that was going no where fast, Brother Issac made the ultimate sacrifice. When he detonated the bomb inside of him he made the ultimate sacrifice as. The Following's roots inside the LAPD would soon be seen as Officer Myles would reveal himself as a member of The Following while playing a video tape as well. Myles instructed both Robyn and Jordan to follow the instructions on the recording while the police station remained in turmoil. - Smoke still fills the two rooms as yelling and screaming is heard nonstop from a mix of police officers and criminals. Officer Myles motions for Robyn and Jordan to head towards the door as Jordan holds on to her shivering body. “We are going to calmly walk out of this room and then head outside. If anyone asks anything, you simply say we are escorting Robyn home, to keep her away from the media. I would also advice for no interruptions in the plan, or bad things will happen to a very innocent little girl.” Myles opens the door and allows Jordan to lead Robyn forward as he trails behind. Police Officers pass them as they head towards the smoke filled room. Robyn stumbles forward after tripping over the foot of a cuffed criminal. Myles reaches at his hip for his gun as Jordan quickly waves for him to stop while helping Robyn back to her feet. They reach the door and head outside towards Jordan's car. Myles opens the back door and watches as they enter and take a seat. Before closing the door Myles reaches inside and places a black cloth bag over both of their heads and cuffs them to the protective railing that separates the front and back of the vehicle. He closes the door and walks around to the other side where he gets in the front of the car and starts it up. “Myles man you don't have to do this.” “I'm afraid I do because it is the wishes of Brother Diamond and Father Tracer.” “Please just don't hurt my little girl.” “Don't worry your little girl is safe and sound. Now it depends on the two of you if she stays that way. As for the trip, don't worry it shall be a short one.” ...Time passes by... The sound of the engine soon comes to an end and is replaced with the sound of opening car doors. Soon the cuffs come off as both Jordan and Robyn are pulled out from the car. The breeze is a refreshing feeling from being locked inside of the car. “Welcome to my home and to the home of The Following. I'm sorry we had to meet this way, but there was no other choice. Although it is very nice to finally see you Robyn.” Members of The Following pulls the bags off the heads of Robyn and Jordan. The light from the setting sun blinds them for a moment until their eyes adjust. Robyn falls backwards as she sees Donny Diamond standing before her dressed in complete black and his hair pulled back in a pony tail as he smirks at Detective Jordan. “When first confronted with The Living Nightmare everyone falls back in fear. There is no reason for you to fear me though.” “NO REASON TO FEAR YOU?!” “You just had us basically kidnapped and brought to you!” Hearing Jordan interrupt Robyn, Donny smiles and quickly strikes Jordan in the face. He falls to the ground beside Robyn as blood oozes from his lip and members of The Following circle him. “Did your mother not teach you to never interrupt a woman when she's speaking? But yes, you have nothing to fear, because I have no plans of doing harm to anyone as long as everyone acts accordingly. Although I will say someone will die here tonight.” “Oh my God please do not hurt my daughter! PLEASE!” “As I said, I will not hurt anyone. Now let's go inside shall we?” Members of The Following help Robyn to her feet while others grab Jordan around the neck and pull him up to his feet in a choke hold. They follow Brother Donny as he opens the door to the church and Brother Myles gets back into the police car. After everyone enters Donny closes the door behind him. “Downstairs.” The only light comes from candles that are lit in various places. More members of The Following head downstairs as Donny leads the way. Once down the spiral set of stairs Donny swats away a few cobwebs and opens the iron door. Everyone steps inside as Donny closes it shut and proceeds to lead the to two vertical metal stretchers. “Put them in place please my brothers.” This room is darker than the last with only two candles in the room, both on opposite sides. Robyn and Jordan are strapped down and one arm is straightened and strapped with the hand around a gun. Jordan grips the gun and laughs. “Hahaha! How stupid can you be to strap us down with a gun?” “And that comment makes for a fine example as to why the great LAPD has yet to catch me. The gun your taunting me with can not be moved and is placed directly at Robyn you moron.” 'Oh...” “Please just let us go. We don't even know where we are, just give me Katie and drive us back where you took us from. We will never speak a word of what happened I promise.” “I would like to do that Robyn I really would, but seeing as your the woman whom tried to have me taken to prison, I do not believe a single word you speak. Now please, allow me to explain what is going to happen here tonight.” Donny pulls a chair towards them and takes a seat before them. “A lot of people are afraid of the dark, but I believe that when one is placed in darkness your senses can heighten and you can focus on your objective much easier. Before we begin however, let me read her word to explain why the two of you are here.” The book of her word is handed to Donny. He nods in appreciation as he opens the book and thumbs through a few pages. “She said: “In a world where people try and silence you, you must speak louder with actions. In a world where one tries to waste your life, you must for them to take one away.” As a follower of her word, we must do as she asks of us. It is because of you two trying to lock me away, that you must decide on who is going to die tonight” “Have you gone completely insane? I'm not going to shoot Robyn, just because you don't want to go to jail!” “Please stop, please just let us go.” “You will be set free I promise, once the deed is done. You both have called me a murderer, but tonight it will be you that is seen in the same light as me. Someone will die tonight and you will have two minutes to decide which of you it will be or poor innocent Katie will suffer a horrible death. My brothers and my sisters let it all begin!” Opposite of where Donny sits flames open up as he closes the book. Inside the flames is an iron crate with chain bars, and sitting inside with some of her toys is little Katie. The flames increase in size as she begins to cry. “Mommy! Mommy!” “I'm here baby, Mommy's here! You said you wouldn't hurt her!” “What the fuck man?! Let the girl go!” “You have a little more than one minute before her flesh begins to burn.” “Just stop this dude! This is insane!” Robyn squeezes the trigger of the gun and it fires off into the chest of Detective Jordan. Blood flies from the impact as he gasps for air while Donny motions for the flames to be turned off. Getting up from his seat he walks towards Robyn and sets her free as members of The Following do the same to the bleeding lifeless body of Detective Jordan. Katrina is seen walking down the stairs towards Katie. “Katrina please get Katie ready while we finish up here.” “Sure baby!” Robyn weeps as Donny holds her, they follow the members of The Following that are carrying Jordan's body upstairs. This time they walk out the back door under the night sky and towards the river. Donny pushes Robyn forward as they toss Jordan's body into the river, and he reads from the book. “Once the choice is made your soul is free to live again. Show the survivor the truth, but never give them mercy.” ...One Day Before Robyn presented the evidence to Detective Jordan... Robyn begins walking upstairs after coming home from the police station where she was hoping they found information on where her husband was. She checks in Katie's room to see her still sleeping in her bed and proceeds to walk into her room and closes the door behind her. Taking a seat in front of her computer she begins looking through different files. “So I see you have managed to stumble across Ben's dirty little secret.” Startled Robyn turns around to see Donny standing against the door to her room. She slowly backs into a corner out of panic. “You know hiding in a corner is the worst place really. Once you're corner, there is no chance for escape, but there is no reason for you to be afraid. I'm just here to talk with you about Ben.” “You know my husband?” “Yes. He was one of my trainer's at Larry's Gym and a member of The Following.” “Those people are holding my husband hostage!” “No. I'm the one holding him hostage. I have learned that Ben had been looking for a way to leave us, but he forgot that the only way to leave our family is through death. Then he decided to use my dear Katrina's fragile state to blackmail his way out.” He takes a couple of steps forward in Robyn's direction. “So I'm going to make you a deal that I'm really sure you will not turn down. I saw your daughter sleeping when I came in. Unless you want her to grow up as an orphan you will delete all of those computer files, you will forget ever seeing me here today, you will forget what I told you, and you will accept the fact that you have no more husband.” “What? How could I possibly do that?” “I don't really care how, but if you don't, you already know the consequences.” ...Present... As Detective Jordan's body floats down the river Donny turns to face Robyn. He notices Katrina placing Katie inside of a black car with tinted windows. “I gave you this option once before and you decided to test me. I strongly advise you to not do it again, only this time I will make you suffer, should you defy me again. Katie will then suffer as well. I promised you I would not hurt her, and those flames would of never burned her.” “You... You had me kill him for no reason?” “No. He had evidence to lock me up for good, and it was all because of your crafty work. I needed to show you how serious I am when it comes to my promises and to The Following. So tonight you will be taken home and everything that happened tonight, you will forget. You will erase everything about me, and about The Following from your life and from memory. Because if you don't I promise you will regret it. Now go.” Slowly Robyn walks towards the car, wiping the tears from her face as Donny turns to the river as Katrina walks towards him. “My dear Katrina, you were so wonderful and perfect in our plan. I just need you to do one more thing for me tonight.” “Anything my love.” “Have them bag the gun she fired. We have her prints on it.”
