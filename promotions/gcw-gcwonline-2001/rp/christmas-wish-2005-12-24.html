Christmas Wish

24 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/24/05 The warm breeze caresses my face as the vents fill my house with Christmas warmth. At peace sitting on my leather recliner I think there is nowhere else on earth I would rather be. It’s Christmas time and all I can picture wanting for Christmas is to be back where I belong. I’ve felt insecure about my needs in life and I always seem to put things before others. But I have been neglecting my passion in life. I’ve been decaying away thinking to myself what could have been. But I know it’s not to late for redemption. I still have time to face my dreams and put an end to my wants and needs. I need that gold and I have to strive to make it so. The smell of freshly cooked turkey lingers to my nostrils as I crave the taste of this once wild animal. I can merely taste it but I’m still waiting for everything to be perfect, for all the food to be placed out for my appetite. A man like me needs his variety to choose from. The family gathers at the table as they all find a seat leaving the head of the table for me. They gather their food as I remain at my recliner in deep thought of something more filling than a turkey dinner at Christmas. The family finally realizes my empty seat when gathering arms to say grace. They yell for me, but I don’t answer. They try once more but I still don’t bother yelling back. If only anyone knew what I truly wanted for Christmas, they would know where I would be right now. I just want what could have been mine. I remain in my trance for a minute or two more where I come to face a decision. I can choose to sit here with my family at Christmas and open the presents they spent weeks on making and buying for me?..Or I could make things right in my life?.Finally I rise from my recliner and walk to the kitchen. The family watches my every move with all their eyes on me. I go to the turkey and rip off a leg and bite into it. Chewing and speaking?.?Merry Christmas?””..Then the door shuts behind me?..
