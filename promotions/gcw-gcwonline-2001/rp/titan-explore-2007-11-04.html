Titan Explore

4 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 4th 2007 In the middle of nowhere, GCW Tag Team Champions Banned & Exiled~! look around for a sign. Any sign really, just something to justify why they were out there in the first place. Standing with them is Chris? fianc’e Tessa Windsor, who grows increasingly annoyed with each passing moment. The three are almost ready to pack it back to Bumblebee, chalking the entire trip as a wild goose chase, when the sound of a distant rumbling halts them. Traveling along the dusty road they themselves took minutes ago, is an old beat up truck that is kicking dirt in every direction. A brown cloud hugs closely to the truck, following it along as it approaches the three. Tessa moves a bit behind Storm, though making no other indication of fear. Likewise Chris and Markus brace themselves as the rust covered pick up comes to a stop feet in front of them. Stepping out of the driver’s seat, a squat man in faded overalls greets them in a pleasing manner. He’s polite while not being too sociable about it, giving them a nod of acknowledgement and a “how do you do?” He wasn’t looking for a reply, just wanting to see polite. He didn’t care to wait too long though, walking around to the back of his pick up and reaching over the side to grab an old double barrel shotgun. The three draw back; fearing for their own safety. The man means them no hard though, tossing the gun onto his shoulder and gives them another nod to let them know it’s not meant for them. “Here ta’see tha place?” he spits out between holes in his brown teeth. Markus is the one elected to step forward to represent the group, taking one step forward. His hand shakes a bit as he holds out a sheet of paper, “from the E-Bay listing.” The man squints one eye, glaring at the paper for a moment, then spits out a bullet of black ooze. “Lemme show ya tha place” he tells them as he walks by and heads out into the deserted field. There is a small dirt path the four follow, only recognizable by the years of trampled grass under foot. The man walks for a bit, bouncing the shotgun off his shoulder and letting it playfully come back down again. After what seems like five minutes they come to a stop, pausing for a moment. The man looks up at the blazing sun above his head, reaching his hand into his back pocket, and pulls out a red handkerchief that he uses to dab away the sweat on the corners of his forehead. Banned & Exiled~! hang back a few steps, watching him cautiously. Crouching down, the man places his gun beside him on the ground and stuffs the handkerchief back into his pocket where it came from. At first none of them had noticed what he was doing until they saw him push aside a patch of tall grass, revealing a large metal lid. Three grunts later the man turns the gear and lifts the lid open to reveal a pipe line leading down deep into the ground. He steps aside, picking up his gun, “after you” he says with his arm extended out for them. Tessa looks to Markus, Markus looks to Chris, and Chris looks to Tessa; but none of them budge an inch. The old grizzly man squints his eye at them, “well?” his tone suggest a growing annoyance. The first to step forward, Chris starts down the ladder to lead the pack. Tessa goes next, not wanting to be left above ground with the strange man by herself. Not even five steps down the ladder, Tessa lets out a piercing scream. The man readies his gun, jumping right to alert. But it is merely a false alarm, as Chris? laugh could be heard even from down the pipe. “Not funny Chris! Did you have to grab my ass?” Her nose wrinkles, equal parts of anger and embarrassment. She kicks her leg, which catches Chris on his shoulder and knocks him off the ladder. He falls four feet onto his ass, the hard concrete breaking his fall. Chuckling, Markus moves past the man and starts heading down the ladder to join his friends. The man follows after Markus, closing the lid after himself. The sun gets blocked out by the heavy metallic lid, leaving only the humming seventy watt bulb hanging down below as a light source. Once all four have reached the end of the ladder and convened in the large open area by the ladder, the man returns his gun to his shoulder and looks around for a moment. Banned & Exiled~! again form their tight group opposite from the man, none of them saying a word. After another awkward moment of silence, the man motions for them to follow with a nod of his head and they start off to the left. They follow at least four steps behind the man, making sure to stay away from the barrel of the shotgun as much as possible. Passing through a large chamber, Banned & Exiled~! admire the mere scale of the place. In every direction there are tunnels, chambers, and building of all shapes and sizes. Most buildings are in fair shape, but it is obvious that they have not received an adequate level of upkeep in the past years. Their style places them back in time, the smell itself seems to linger from a place long ago. It is like a lost city, trapped deep under the ground and hidden away from the rest of the modern world. The only thing ruins the entire atmosphere is the spot graffiti found in different places. For the most part the graffiti has been covered over, but in some places it remains as a testament to American idiocy and 80’s pop culture. Most of the equipment has been removed, leaving a large and hollow feel to the place. Chris half expects a tumble weed to roll by his feet at any moment. The man stops in front of a large structure, holding out his hand to emphases the building; the man clears his throat to grab their attention. “This is one of tha silos tha use.” He looks it over, pausing himself, “stuff been taken from it bout forty years or so. But tha structure still good, mostly.” Mostly did not sit well with Tessa, who looks around the place herself. Her arms folded over her chest, she turns to Chris and stops him from following along with the tour any further. “You are not spending a million dollars on some out of shape missile base!” she yells, throwing those folded arms up into the air. Markus steps in after hearing her not-to-subtle comment, “actually it’s one and a half million.” “Even worse!” quickly follows. A pointy finger finds its way to Chris? chest, poking him into taking a step back. Looking to his partner, Chris gives him a mock grin, “thanks.” He turns toward the tour guide, who has stop to listen to their conversation. Chris pleads with him with his eyes, asking for the man to intervene and help him. But spitting out another wad of black tar from the corner of his mouth, it’s clear that Storm is on his own with this sells pitch. “Tessa” he starts off, “we could really use a place like this to help us. I mean we can’t try and fight a war against the Decepticons from our apartment!” The Pint Size Diva shakes her head, wishing her fianc’e would give up on his crusade to help the Autobots. All this, she thought, over a simply misunderstanding over the name of GCW’s large tron they used for shows. She’s tried to tell him time and time again that it’s not the MegaTron from the cartoon and that the screen really won’t change into a large tank, or jet, or gun on him and kill him. But will he listen? No. To make matters worse Chris has a friend antagonizing him. “Yeah Tess,” Markus chimes in, “don’t you know all evil geniuses need an underground lair.” At moments like this, it was good that the tour guide held onto the gun instead of Tessa. “For starts, you aren’t evil” she reminds them, as they are in fact one of the most loved tag teams in GCW in a very long time. No other superstars have their merchandise sell out faster than that of Banned & Exiled~! “And you are far, far, far, FAR from being geniuses.” Chris? shoulders slump, disappointed, but it will take more than that for Markus to get down. “Well we would be evil geniuses if we had an underground lair.” “I am not” she repeats “I am not about to let the two of you spend $1,500,000, even if it is underground.” Sliding into position beside her, Chris wraps his arms around her slender waist. “Ah come on sweetie” he purrs into her ear, “just think about it, it’s nice and quiet down here. And think of all the decorating you can do with all this open space. It’s like a Martha Steward dream down here.” He pauses, thinking about it, “Well if she was into the military and enjoyed being underground.” “I did hear she enjoys going under” Markus tosses in to try and get a laugh from Tessa to help butter her up. She doesn’t seem to budge on her opinion, however, asking them to just leave. The tour guide taps the end of the gun against the metal frame of the silo, drawing in the attention of the visitors. Once all eyes are on him again he turns the gun to its proper place on his shoulder. “Are we gonna finish tha tour or not” I got others interested in tha place. A doctor suppose to stop by after ya’ll. He’s very curious about tha place; think he said he needed it for science or sumthin?. Plus there were a rather cocky guy just hear yesterday. A rather bad chain smoker; had a cigarette going for miles and miles. But he was interested in tha place as well. So can we?” Chris gives Tessa those big blue puppy dog eyes. Dropping her arms to the side and slumping her shoulders, Tessa again caves in to him giving him a “fine”. Joyful, Chris hurries to the front of their group to hear everything the tour guide says. Continuing the tour, the guide turns and starts heading down a tunnel towards a series of buildings near a large dome. Markus points out the dome, wondering about its purpose among the different structures here. The guide keeps moving as if he hardly hears him. He just grumbles “that’s Power Dome A. We don’t go in tha Power Dome A. Mostly.” Looking up to the dome to search for any reason on why, Markus changes his attention to the guide to ask why. The guide replies without hesitation “the deep crows.” Chris? eyes shoot open, his mouth slightly ajar “the what?” He spins on his heels, looking to Tessa. She knows what he thinks he heard and instantly smacks him. “He said deep CROWS, not?” she stops herself before she accidentally finishes that sentences and smacks him again for almost making her say the word. Staring the dome up and down to get an idea of it, Markus chips in his own idea “you know, if we can fix it up, maybe we can turn it into a Thunder Doom.” “You know, I bet we could hire Whitney Houston” Chris adds. Which joke will they go with? “If Bobby Brown isn’t too busy whooping her ass.” Oh they went there. Yeah, he beat her so badly Ike would be jealous. You know it; I know it, let’s not kid ourselves here. Cutting into their little spill, the tour guide once again asserts himself by clearing his throat. The three turn back to his and hurry their step to catch up, as he has moved on to a different area in the building. He moves a bit more carefully now, his gun removed from his shoulder and cradled in his arms. Chris looks at him, head tilted to the side “problems?” “Stalagmen” he said, leaving it at that as if they knew what he was talking about. Tessa huddles up closer to Chris, worried. They pass along, unbothered, with the tour guide explaining what each building is. His gun stays close to him as they go, each step coming at a slightly quicker pace than the one before it. Moving pass a large silo, Markus stops and looks up at it. His head turns toward the tour guide “hey is there any ICBMs left in here” You know, maybe something the government may have missed.” If there was any space left between Chris and Tessa, there wasn’t any more. “There really isn’t any missiles here are there?” “That would be sweet” Storm added, “we could totally fuck up MegaTron and the rest of the Decepticons with a good ICBM.” Tessa, in a panic, tells them she wants to go but the tour guide assures her that everything was removed five years ago. But his words do little to make her feel better. She turns to Chris “we need to go. Like as in now.” Banned & Exiled~! let out a collective sigh of disappointment. There would be no underground base of awesomeness for them this day. Tessa pulls the two of them back towards the ladder as the tour guide brings up the rear with his shotgun in tow. As they climb back up the ladder, Chris keeps trying to pass the tour guide their number but Tessa cuts him off every time. Foiled, the Tag Champions head back to their vehicle. “You know,” Chris starts “I saw a listing for a whole town on E-bay. We may be able to start a base there.” “No” Tessa says “just no”.
