Christmas Knock Out?

27 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/27/09 "It's like I'm paranoid lookin' over my back It's like a whirlwind inside of my head It's like I can't stop what I'm hearing within..." "Hello?" The phone is hot against my ear. I fuckin' forgot it on the radiator in my apartment. I don't know if I pull it away because it is hot, or because of the screaming voice on the other end. "I got you a fuckin' last minute fight, you skinny fuck! It is big money, high stakes! All I have to say, you have a chance at a Main Event, but like I said, it is high stakes!" This is the first time in a long time that I get called in for a main event! Shit, I can only imagine what the high stakes are that my agent is talking about... "Jason, Jason...SLOW DOWN! Is this for..." "The big show on Christmas Day! YOU HAVE THE FUCKIN' MAIN EVENT SLOT!" Wow, tempting, but I haven't trained for this fight, I don't even know who the fuck it is against, but I know that it will be big money...money that I can use. I mean, look at this place! The apartment is nice, but it is cramped. I need to move into my father's old house, but I don't have the money to do so... This could pay off... "Alright, you have my attention Jason, who and what is he?" "He is a brawler, more of a stand up guy, very rarely takes it to the ground game. The thing you need to consider though..." There is a very long silence on the phone... "Wha..." "...He is two weight classes higher than you..." What the fuck did he just say? So he is like eighty pounds on me? over two seventy? A heavyweight? "Kris? You there? Did you hear me?" "Yeah, yeah....He's a heavyweight?" "Well, yeah. He has some experience, but you know that White isn't happy with losing someone like you to that fake shit! He wants you to choose, and to honest, he wants you to lose at the match. A big WEC match that White is overlooking, you know it will be a big pay out and all..." Money and career or money and injury to keep me away from both careers? "Well, Jay... Does this deal come with paper and film?" "No. All I know is that the guy goes by "Big Red". This is going to be a dark fight. All we know is his style, and his measurements. Look, Kris, I need to let them know by tonight... Can you fuckin' tell me if you want the paper? You've already taken on guys in your wrestling shit that were this guy's size. Just take the fight, for both of us!" Jason has three kids and I know he's taken a hit since I went to GCW. I could do this last one for him. And it really might be my last.... "Alright, look if I take it..." "THIS IS GREAT KRIS! I WILL CALL..." "WAIT! If I take this, I only have about three weeks to prepare...I want you to make the call and tell them that I will do this, knowing what the repercussions are if I lose it. I want to know that if I win, if I even lose, I want the bank to be worth it..." "Yeah, yeah... I got it Kris. Thanks man. You have no idea how much I need this! We, we need this!" "Make the call and I want you to get everything you can off of this fighter so I know what he hell I'm up against...Big Red..." "You got..." WTF man? I know the man is seriously excited, but hanging up mid sentence? Is the purse that much? Last fight in Mixed Martial Arts huh? I know that he wants me to chose, but damn. I take everything he throws at me. I've bailed him out so many times, and now he wants me gone or a full commitment? This is going to hit the press hard... ************************** 2 weeks until fight ************************** 98 99 100 "Arlgiht, jump onto the bike, and keep it moving Kris!" Spinner bike...alright... tag it... This blows. All I can think of is this monster in the ring in front of me over 200lbs and wearing all red. What else can a man named Big Red wear? Maybe he's like ten foot with red hair? I don't know... All I got is over 230lbs and he is a stand up fighter with a shitty ground game... Great.... "TIME! Go over to the jumping platform!" Fucking shit... Cardio and circuit training. Yeah Kris, we need you to get your endurance and cardio up. Beat him to every punch and get the fuck away from anything he throws! Thanks a lot Jeff... "TWENTY-FIVE, TWENTY-SIX...PICK UP THE PACE!" Fuck you Jeff. I'd like to see you do this shit... "THIRTY SECOND BREAK, GET SOME WATER!" Must he yell all the time? Alright twenty-eight seconds left. I just need to keep the heart rate up, but I don't feel anything. I'm in the best shape of my life, and he acts like I can't take the fuckin' routine anymore. Wrestling hasn't gotten me soft, it just made me lighter on the feet and heavier with a punch. Maybe I'm a little slower to the strike, but my stand up wrestling is better than ever. What does White have up his sleeves that has Jeff so worried about this fight? Jason won't tell me anything, which is odd... "Alright, go to the slider and wait for my command.......Go! One Minute..." Push Push Push Push Push Push Push....Pullllllllllllll Push Push Push Push Push Push Push....PUllllllllllllll Push Push Push Push Push Push Push....PUllllllllllllll "Time! Get the medicine ball....Go!" Big Red....Big Red... Big Red has no fucking chance... ***************** 1 week till fight ***************** I'm cut down to a solid 190 and I'm as ready as I can be for this fight. With the little info that I have on this guy, and the training I've been through with a guy that is 6'0" and a solid 250, well solid is used loosely of course. I've been trying to keep my wrestling training to a minimum as of late, but after talking to Zacharia Taylor, one of the smaller guys in GCW, he told me that I should be trying to keep my wrestling part of the training. He told me that if I am going to take on a bigger opponent, not only do I have to wind him, but I also need to be able to get back on top if I can. Wrestling is the last thing I should be shying away from. I know that my stand up is flawless right now, and I need to know that I can handle someone that much bigger than me... I'm off to a local gym to train under a technical mastermind...one that I am very fond of... "You're late, asshole. Get in your shorts and get in the ring... I don't have time for this shit...and I'm doing this as a fuckin' favor, and you're late..." Fuckin' asshole. I hit traffic and I'm five minutes late, when I'm usually early. I don't understand Bagwell sometimes. He drops from GCW, yet he still allows me to train with him as if he's trying to stay in shape to make his re-debut, the fifth or whatever it is... "You're up against some big guy huh? Well, I guess I will do for an opponent. Now, we're going to go through tosses and lifts. Nothing crazy, just the basics so you can be ready. I'm going to fight it, you aren't wrestling, remember..." I'm an MMA fighter first, and a GCW Wrestler second...you don't need to lecture me... I don't understand why wrestlers think they are better than everyone. Bagwell stands there, allowing me to toss him all over the place and he claims he's trying. Either he is going through the motions, or he's just getting old... ...I'm going with the latter... Twenty minutes in and I'm standing over him without breaking a sweat and he looks like he's about to die. I have to wait for one of his goons to make it into the ring to take Bagwell's place. Sometimes i really wonder why everyone thought he was the man in the ring. Two hours in, three partners later...I think I finally got the technique down enough to toss someone overhead that is twice my weight. A shame I don't have Shaman or Mayhem to train against. Sometimes I wish I had the audacity to call them out so I can get a couple shots in... "Alright, enough... I think you've got it down..." "No shit, Bagwell. Thanks, but I think I had it down an hour ago..." "Getting smart with me?" "Come on, you know that you don't have shit on me Bagwell..." I crack a smile so he knows that I'm just messing around. He knows it, and that is why he reacted the way that he did, but I do have to say that even though he dropped out of GCW, the man has talent, but he's losing the stamina... Bagwell comes over to shake my hand and gives me a piece of paper of tips and moves that I should try in the ring, and to try against my sparing partner... ******** I had a dream last night. Ever read "The Masque of the Red Death"? Need I say more? The idea of "Big Red" is eating at me more than the fight itself. The anticipation, the aggravation, the urgency to get to where I need to be. The idea is manifesting into something so much more than what it really is. In the dream, I was walking through rooms, just like the party, and the further I went, the darker the abyss got. I heard pounding in the distant, dark red room. I felt like it was calling my name, but all there was was this destructive sound of a pounding. Something only a fighter really knows... ...the pounding of fist on a limp body... The intoxicating sound drew me in until I realized I was no longer with a party, but by myself in a very dark red room with a spotlight on an outline of a feeble human being being punched repeatedly by this massive dark figure. His arms entering the spotlight were dark, darker than any human's skin I've ever seen, darker than anything I've ever seen; a profound darkness... There was this sound, over and over that I felt in the very center of my bones, just pulling at me with every strike. Finally, I got close enough to see a lifeless me being struck by this...monster! I raised my hands in fear, and anticipation when the monster crossed into the light. As his face broke in, I woke up... It isn't the first time I've had a dream before a fight, but it is definitely the first time I saw myself being beaten in my own dream. Beaten by something that I couldn't see. See, I always know my opponents, I always know what I'm up against. Most would think that the rush of the unknown would turn into an anticipation that would be welcomed by a fighter because it is a challenge, but that's not what I see. I see a career ending fight.... I have but days to get ready for this fight, and I feel that there is nothing left for me to do to get ready. I stay in fairly good shape all the time, a fighter's shape, but against the unknown, I feel that I'm nothing. Jason keeps telling me that I'm getting worked up for absolutely no reason at all. I'm a very well regarded fighter, and I'm getting worked up over nothing. Yeah, but I can even see it in his face, he's petrified to see December 25th to come just as much as I am... ****************** Fight Night, December 25th, 2009 ****************** I can't keep my hands from shaking... I've never been this shaky before a fight, EVER! I remember my first fight back in middle school, and I was calm and collected, what I am known for. Thank God there is no journalist or reporter here, because I would have to hide it, but my eyes show it all. Due to lack of sleep last night, my eyes are slightly bloodshot, and the RedLine and Red Bull aren't helping on that front at all. I took every supplement for energy that you can think of, trying to keep all the strength and endurance in my muscles before it walks out of the red lines in my eyes. No, I'm the Executioner, I can do this. "Five Minutes, Mr. Michaels." Five Minutes. I've been preparing for over three weeks, I can wait five minutes, but there is no way for me to keep calm. I keep telling myself that there is nothing that I can do right now, just walk tot he cage and get ready for whatever is out there for me. I think I know why I'm so shaky... Caffeine and sugar... Yeah, what was I thinking taking all that shit? I'm going to collapse on my way to the cage. I'm going to hop down to the beat of Linkin Park and then twenty feet form the cage, plow. Right into the ground face first. I should never have taken that much. The next three minutes is an absolute blur. Well, not even a blur, because even in blurs, you remember it vaguely. All I remember is hearing the middle of Papercut cut across the speakers and I am standing right in front of the referee as he gives me instructions to the Main Event's rules. Did I crash? Did I fall on my face or was it the massive amount of adrenaline and substances in my system that made me forget what just happen? Well, at least I didn't coast into the ring and come out of it as the first punch is being thrown by this Big Red... I jump into the ring with a mixed reaction. I raise my hand to the fans as my shirt is ripped over my head by Jeff. I try to focus on the tunnel so I can get a good look at my opponent. All I hear is boos and cheers, but soon enough, all I hear is nothingness, as if someone hit mute on the crowd... "And now, announcing the original fighter for the Main Event. He is coming in from the North Pole..." What the fuck did he just say? "....Coming in at Two-Hundred-Seventy pounds... Are you fucking kidding me? "...With a professional Record of 521 wins and ZERO losses!...." Who the fuck.... "...Kris "Big Red" Kringle!!!!!!!!!!!!" The crowd goes absolutely insane as a Six foot Two monster comes out of the tunnel in a red and white coat. He has a white beard and white hair, but this isn't the Santa Clause from the stories, this guy is a Santa Clause that is on STEROIDS! There is no fat on him, not that I can see at least. His veins are bulging out of his neck and exposed hands. There is no way that anyone can take on this guy! He is definitely something of myth and lore because there is no man that can possibly be this big! I turn around and look into Jeff's eyes which are twinkling with...joy? What the hell is going on here? Jeff looks like he is a five year old boy on Christmas Morning when he runs around the corner to see a million presents under the tree. Thanks a lot asshole! "Alright Gentlemen...Touch Gloves!" ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Ok, so... Needless to say, Kris Michaels doesn't remember too much of the fight, he's still groggy from the fight, so I will sum it up for you... Kris went up and touched gloves with Kringle. Ever see Rocky V? Yeah, Kringle was the Russian. He went to touch gloves and Kringles hands didn't even move. Kringle wasn't the happy go lucky guy that you see on TV, and he's not eighty years old either. This Kris Kringle was a genetic freak that not even Bryan Mayhem would be alright to face. The match started off pretty slow with Michaels circling Kringle, testing his reach and quickness. Kringle wasn't all that fast, so Michaels had the advantage there, but the fight quickly went into a brawl after the first minute passed. Kringle took two hard shots to the face, which didn't seem to phase him too much, but Michaels kept it up. He lunged at him from an awkward angle and was able to get a leg under Kringle and he tossed him to the ground. Michaels quickly made it to Kringle's back and immediately went for the rear naked choke. The fans went wild, the very short, but incredibly muscular little people in Kringle's corner didn't move an inch as they saw Kringle in what seemed to be trouble... ...guess again... Kringle turned around as if Michaels wasn't even on his back, got to his knees, and then used the cage to stand up. Michaels, still wretching on his neck for all his worth, struggled to squeeze around his waist to take away him breath. Kringle smiled, turned his back to the center of the ring and jumped backwards, crushing Michaels and forcing him to let go. The rest of the match was a series of axe handle punches, elbows, and stiff strikes... ...all coming from Kringle.... Michaels made it to the second round and his corner was too ashamed to come into the ring... Kringle walked over to his corner and the door opened with five little people with pointed ears coming out with a bucket as big as them and a water bottle that, if ingested, would cause them to explode. Kringle used the time to take advice from the little people who had to step on one another's shoulders to reach Kringle's ears. Michaels looked dazed before the second round started. The match was over with a stiff kick to the face only fifty-six seconds in the second round. Michaels became the 522nd win of Kringle's career. Ring doctors rushed to his side to wake him up. He woke up with a girl like scream and mumbles of candy canes and stockings. He clearly had the Christmas Knocked into him! Well, there you have it. Michaels faced Big Red who was Mr. Jolly himself and got his ass kicked like a penguin fucking with a polar bear. Until next time....
