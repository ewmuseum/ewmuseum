A Girl, An Egomaniac, and a Sidekick (part 10)

16 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/16/08 (takes place 9/2002) September 2002 Playing hostess was never quite up Tessa Windsor’s alley. It always felt like the most pointless of roles. Catering and pretending to be something you’re not to someone who likely knew the true you always felt kind of awkward. But even worse than catering to someone you knew, was catering to someone who wanted to play mind games with you. Why Tessa agreed to let Chris Storm over to her house was still beyond her at this very point. The only logical explanation she could think of at the moment was something along the lines of being so desperate to chase him away and his million mind fucks away. Really it didn’t matter her excuse. She could come up with a million and two excuses for why she finally caved to his plea, it still wouldn’t change the fact that he was due at the house in ten minutes, and would no doubt comment on the mess left behind by the kids. Granted it might not be a bad thing if that same mess ended up chasing him away. Indeed any person could tell that children lived at 400 Sea Crest Drive in Chatham, MA. The front yard was littered with balls for every type of sport, bicycles, and dozens of other goodies. Unfortunately for Tessa, that right there was an absolute guarantee that Chris would find the house. “Christa!” Tessa called out to her niece turned daughter. “What?” The eight year old whined as she entered into the kitchen, a Barbie in one hand, its outfit in the other. “Go clean up your mess in the living room. We’ve got company coming over.” Tessa ordered as she continued to check off completed tasks in her mental note. Intrigued by this rare occurrence Christa inquired, “Is it that guy that has a crush on you?” Before Tessa could even attempt an answer, a knock on the door seized both her and Christa’s attention. “I’ll get it!” Quite eagerly, Christa’s bare feet scampered across the cold linoleum floor towards the door. “Christa!” Tessa called out, not daring to do the same in her four inch pumps, “Don’t you answer that door! What did I tell you about that?” “But I know it’s not a stranger!” Christa hollered back with total disregard of her aunt’s warning. Tessa cringed as she heard the knob turn, followed by Chris? voice, “Hey little Tessa, where’s your mom?” Christa’s eyes heavily examined her mother’s wooer from head to toe for any imperfections before she offered up an answer, “She’s in the kitchen.” “In the kitchen?” Chris was taken aback, but not in an unpleasant manner. Unknowingly, Christa had just opened up the floodgates for her adoptive mother, “What a welcomed surprise.” Without further word or so much as an introduction, Chris barreled his way down the hall bound and determined to catch sight of Tessa in the one place he swore he’d never catch her. “You love me so much that you’d actually resume your rightful place in the kitchen?” The arrogance that shone from his face could have lit up the entire house. Just one minute of his company was enough to work Tessa’s nerves, “My kids can’t cook for themselves.” It was a very mild blow to the WIW World Champions ego. So mild that he didn’t bother with a retort. Instead he took a seat at the kitchen table and made himself at home. Christa was eager to meet a man she had heard so much about through both their mother and others in WIW. Taking a seat beside Chris, Christa is the first to prod Chris with a question, “Where’s your belt at?” “At the drycleaners” snapped back Storm who for whatever reason was offended by this question, “gotta look good for a champion of my caliber.” Of course it wasn’t at the drycleaners, even Christa at eight years of age was capable of picking up on bullshit, “Where’s it really?” She persisted, “You didn’t lose it already, did you?” Once again Chris snapped backed, sounding more like he was the child, “I did not lose it! It’s on display in my trophy case back at my house! I know you probably have no clue what a trophy case is because your mother here probably won’t even win anything she’ll want to display proudly, so please don’t ask.” This time it was Christa left feeling disgruntled, “I know what they are. My mom”.” Whether Christa was aware of it or not, Tessa was well aware that she was about to open up a can of worms thus cut her off real quick, “Don’t worry about it Christa, he’s just kidding around.” Chris looked around the place, which was in a haphazardly clean state from all of Tessa’s last minute cleaning. “Hey, isn’t there supposed to be another little midget running around here somewhere?” While Tessa wasn’t too high on the way Chris had addressed her nephew, she blocked it out and bluntly responded, “He’s at a friends,” then added in her own jab, “you know, those people you lack in your life.” Chris waved it off, “Please, I’ve got Markus. And when I don’t have Markus I’ve got scores of women waiting to hit it off with me. Unimpressed, Tessa’s eyes rolled up and around, “And that’s why you’re here.” Playing himself as some kind of suave hunk, Chris responded, “Naturally.” Of course, having to listen to all this, Christa herself was rolling her eyes. Having grown bored of all this grown up talk, she dismissed herself from her half eaten plate of spaghetti, noting, “I’m going upstairs.” Though she hated for Christa to feel singled out, this was a relief to Tessa, who far from wanted Christa to be subjected to Chris? chauvinistic ways. “I’ll be up in a little bit.” Tessa noted back but before she could blink, Christa was already on her way up the stairs. “Perfect.” Chris whispered to himself a bit too loud. Whether it was intentional or not could be heavily debated. “You bored her to tears.” Tessa said flatly as she walked over towards Christa half eaten plate. He was offended, but in order to protect his masculinity he playfully shot back, “I think it’s more that you don’t know what you are doing.” Flustered, Tessa shot back with a weak, “Whatever.” “Can’t deny that, can you?” He continued to harass. Finally at her breaking point, Tessa was lucky she had already reached the sink, otherwise she would have merely thrown the plate towards the sink in complete and utter frustration, “Is that the whole reason you came here” To critique my parenting skills?” He threw his arms up in the air in feign surrender, “Oh god, you caught on to me! I completely blew off that hot blonde babe back in NYC to play pitiful parent patrol! Man, what am I going to do now?” “Just tell me why you’re here!” She shouted back even louder in hopes that he’d finally become bored with this back and forth nonsense. Casually Chris threw out, “I’m here for some ass. Why else would I be here?” Tessa shoulders dropped as she let out a sigh, rolling her eyes all the while. In her mind she cursed herself out for taking him up on this deal, in reality all she said was, “Figures,” surprising Chris, she follows these words up with a chuckle. Her arms fold up against her chest as she flops down onto a chair at the opposite end of the table from Chris, “sadly you don’t have a chance. Looks like you wasted a good score back in NYC.” Chris bit his tongue, desperately trying to think of a quick quirky comeback. While he did so, she added in, “Besides I have standards, something most women lack nowadays.” This time it is Chris who rolls his eyes, “That’s right, you’ve got really LOW standards. That’s why you’re dating that eighteen year old chump, who is no doubt dating you because he wants a good first time.” Tessa quickly jumped to her feet to defend her boyfriend, shrieking back, “That’s not true!” Smugly, Chris responded back, “I’d need some proof to believe otherwise,” arrogantly he flashed Tessa a grin before continuing on, “tell me what else he’s done for you besides fight off anyone who dares lay a hand on you?” There was complete and utter silence. To his and her surprise, Tessa’s mind could not formulate a single piece of evidence that pleaded otherwise. The Cheshire grin on Chris? face grows wider as he notices Tessa grip her bottom lip between her teeth. “Exactly!” He shot out as he finally stood from his chair. “Me on the other hand...” “You...” He rose his voice over hers so that she would quit speaking, “I’m NOTHING like Erik. I know how to treat a woman.” For the millionth time that evening, Tessa eyes rolled, “I’ve heard it all before.” “SHHH, just listen.” He waltzed over to her side of the table, wrapping his arm around her shoulder, “I can promise you that if you stay with Erik, he’s going to break your little heart. Save yourself now while you’ve got the chance”..” Throwing his arm away Tessa looked him square in the eyes, obviously offended by his words, “Seriously, you need to get over the fact that I’m not interested in you.” This time, he looked back at her and for the very first time Tessa saw the very slightest bit of concern radiate from his eyes, “He’s not good for you. At least I’ll admit I’m a pig, him on the other hand”.” Stubbornly, Tessa refused to budge, “You know what” Forget you. You’re playing with my mind yet again!” Chris merely shrugged her off, “If that’s what you think. At least I know I’m not the one who’ll have to cry my pretty little face to sleep at night. Can’t say the same for you though.” He had finally reached the end of the rope she’d be willing to extend to him, “Well then if that’s the way you feel, maybe you ought to leave. Because your opinion means absolutely NOTHING to me. You hear me” NOTHING!” For a moment, Chris took his verbal beating like a man as he processed Tessa’s words, repeating them over and over again in his mind. Then, when it finally set in that she did not want or need his help, he lashed back albeit playfully, “Fine, suit yourself. I’ve got nothing to gain from you anyways.” “Just leave!” She commanded once more. “You know, you’re even hotter when you”.” “LEAVE!” Dismissing her anger with a wave of his hand, Chris did what he was told. If she wanted to be a prissy little bitch that was fine by him. She was the one who’d be hurt in the end. He knew it, but she’d never accept it. In the end, it’d be her loss.
