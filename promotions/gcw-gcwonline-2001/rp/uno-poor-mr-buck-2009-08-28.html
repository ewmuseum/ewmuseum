Uno - Poor Mr Buck

28 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 28th 2009 The building superintendant looked exhausted. These two guys had been living in the apartment block for around a month now and since they moved in, it had been one complaint after another. Nothing major had happened, just small things that served to irritate the rest of the residents �" pizza boxes in the trash chute, noise in the early hours, stuff like that. It was a pity, because for some strange reason, he liked the two of them. At first he’d pegged them as queers, what with the big guy wearing what looked to be a colourful gimp mask and the early reports of strange noises coming from their apartment. Not that he had anything against that, he loved Will & Grace and was very much a ‘live and let live’ guy. Turned out they weren’t fudge-packers though, at least, he was pretty sure they weren’t, judging by the number of top-shelf magazines the smaller one got delivered every week. After that initial confusion had been cleared up, Buck found himself talking to the smaller one more and more. Not deep conversations, just the exchange of pleasantries each day but still, enough to give him a sense of the decency of the guy. This was on show every time Buck had to contact them regarding a complaint, the older one, Mr Ramos, as Buck referred to him, was always super-apologetic, wanting to go and apologise face-to-face to whoever had lodged the grievance. Now they were sitting in his living room-cum-office, waiting for him to read the particulars of yet another complaint, this time from the middle-aged woman in Flat 7. Apparently Mr Ramos and his roommate, who was technically subletting from him, had a pet. Personally, Buck didn’t care if they had a pet or not, but rules were rules and he was duty bound to make them get rid of it. ‘Mr Ramos…can I call you George?’ ‘Es Hor-Gay.’ ‘Sorry, I didn’t catch that.’ ‘Es Hor-Gay, no George.’ ‘You didn’t what George?’ ‘No. My name es Hor-Gay, no George.’ ‘Oh I’m sorry, my mistake Hor-Gay.’ ‘Gracias Mr Fuck.’ ‘Buck.’ ‘No, my name Hor-Gay.’ ‘Yes I know, you called me by the wrong name just there.’ ‘You’re no Mr Fuck?’ ‘Not fuck, Buck. With a B.’ ‘Where is bee?!’ Jorge jumps up and starts swatting the air around him. ‘No, no, it’s ok! No bee.’ ‘Sure?’ ‘Yes, it was a misunderstanding, comprende?’ ‘Si, si.’ Ramos sits down again, albeit warily, his eyes scanning the air for any flying nuisances. ‘My name is Buck. B-U-C-K.’ ‘Ohhh entiendo. Mr Hor-Gay and Mr Buck. Bueno.’ The sigh that comes out of Buck’s mouth is one of relief. ‘Good. Hor-Gay, I’ve had to ask you in for a meeting because Miss Donowitz in Flat 7 has reported that you and your housemate, Mr…’ Buck pauses, waiting for Jorge to fill in the gap but the Mexican just smiles and nods as if to say… ‘Yes, she report that..?’ ‘Yes, she’s reported that you have an animal living in your apartment. As you know, this is against the rules of the building and I’m afraid you will have to get rid of the animal.’ A look of consternation passes briefly across Jorge’s face before it breaks into a wide, toothy smile. ‘I see problem. Es, what do you say, wrong identity?’ ‘Mistaken identity.’ ‘Si.’ Now it’s Buck’s turn to look confused. ‘So you don’t have a pet?’ Still smiling, Jorge shakes his head. ‘No. You come with me, I show, vale?’ ‘Uh…okay.’ Jorge rises from his seat and leads a befuddled Buck out of his apartment and up the 4 flights of stairs to the apartment he shares with his protégé, the Unknown Luchador. As they climb the stairs, Buck tries to make conversation, to find out more about Jorge; how he came to be in America, what he does for a living, who is housemate is. Unfortunately, as we saw earlier, Ramos hasn’t quite mastered the subtleties of the English language yet. A fact which makes for some interesting exchanges. ‘So Hor-Gay, when did you move to America?’ Ramos replies instantly, not answering the question but definitely giving an insight into his mindset. ‘My papers are all above the board, I can show-‘ ‘No I wasn’t che-‘ ‘So maybe a little fake. I can pay, you want how much?’ Out comes his wallet, looking remarkably well-stocked, his hand ready to pay whatever he needs to ensure Buck’s silence. Buck isn’t shocked and he sure as hell isn’t going to report Jorge, but convincing him of that is another matter altogether. ‘No, Mr Ramos, it’s fine. I don’t want to see your papers. I was just wonderin-‘ ‘I just try to make a better life, to live happily. I’m not an Alley-Cayda or terrorist, Please Mr Fuck-‘ ‘Buck’ As they reach the landing, Ramos falls to his knees and grabs the hands of Buck. ‘Please, I beg you!’ Buck doesn’t know whether to laugh or cry. In the end he goes for option one. ‘Mr Ramos, I promise you I won’t do anything to cause you trouble. I just need to sort out this pet situation.’ Just like that, everything is okay and Jorge is back to normal and on his feet. ‘You good man Mr Buck. This is my place, we go in?’ ‘Yes please.’ As Jorge unlocks the door and leads the superintendant in, Buck has various scenarios in his mind; the place could stink of dog and have a full-size Irish wolfhound living there, it could have an annoyingly aggressive cat pissing in every corner. Nothing, however prepared him for the sight that greeted him as he walked in. Where the living room should be, there was a structure, raised on concrete blocks which looked suspiciously like… ‘Is that a wrestling ring?!’ Jorge nods vigourously, pleased that his handiwork has been appreciated. ‘Si, si! Es good, no?’ Buck’s mouth was moving but no words were coming out. ‘I built myself, very hard work but es worth it now, eh?’ ‘…What? I mean…why?...You can’t…actually you probably…but how?...’ Finally composing himself, Buck asks a simple question, with slight trepidation over what the answer will be. ‘Why do you need a wrestling ring in your apartment?’ Jorge looks at him like he just escaped from a care home. ‘For practice.’ ‘…Practice?’ ‘Yes, for practice. Es very costoso to practice in heem?’ ‘In ‘heem’?’ ‘Yes, ‘heem’. como Gold’s heem, you know?’ ‘Ahh right, a gym. So what are you, a professional wrestler?’ ‘No longer, but my friend, he es spectacular. Going to be superestrella!’ Again, all Buck can do is laugh and shake his head. ‘Okay Hor-Gay, can I just ask you to try and keep the noise down when you’re practicing?’ ‘Of course, we are sorry for problem.’ ‘Alright, as long as you try to take care.’ As Buck turns to leave the apartment, he pauses, remembering the reason he had come up here in the first place. ‘Oh yes, this pet issue?’ Jorge laughs and puts his arm round the super’s shoulders. ‘Es not a pet…es Super Monito!’ As he says these words, a monkey bounds out of the bedroom. Buck’s jaw drops and only closes slightly when he realizes that it isn’t in fact a real monkey but a midget in a costume. ‘What the he-‘ ‘Super Monito es number 2 midget wrestler is Mexico! Only Mascarita Sagrada better than him!’ At this point, Super Monito is in the ring, bouncing off the ropes and doing all sorts of flips and twists. Buck is at a loss and, shaking his head as he leaves the room, he can be heard muttering under his breath… ‘I need a drink.’
