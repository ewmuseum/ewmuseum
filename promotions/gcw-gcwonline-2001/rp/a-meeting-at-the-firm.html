A meeting at the Firm

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today [Scene fades up on a long conference table. At the near end of the table, there sit two men with their backs to the camera. At the far end sits Robert Larabee, with X to his left and Ms. Scabbia to his right.] Larabee-Mr. Darcy, Mr. White, thanks for the time that you've given us. As you know, X is scheduled to face Coyle O'Conner at Fallout. Mr. Darcy-We know all about the match, Robert. You don't need to tell us about our investment that you so boldly made for us. Larabee-Well, I was wondering why there were only two rooms booked for us at the hotel that we were going to be staying at. Mr. White-Because you aren't going to be taking part in the Fallout Festivities, that's why. You are to stay in St. Louis. It's now Ms. Scabbia's job to make sure that X stays focused on his task at hand. Something that you've lost sight of, Robert. Larabee-What do you mean something that I lost sight of? Mr. White-It's very simple, Robert. Your job in GCW was to recruit more wrestlers to this firm. When you were rebuffed by Coyle O'Conner, you sent X after him. Now this has started a rather messy situation that hopefully will come to fruition at Fallout. If it doesn't, then you might just want to look for another job. Larabee-What do you mean, look for another job? I thought that my position within the firm was stable. I thought that I was doing a satisfactory job for the partners. Mr. Darcy-Well, Robert, financially, the job that you are doing is admirable. The liscensing fees that we are generating from X are amazing. So much so, that we can pay X off for the rest of the year right now and still turn a hefty profit. The problem though, is image. Particularly the negative image that X is bringing to the firm. Those who know who we are have been developing a rather concerned idea about us. We don't like that. Mr. White-What he means is that we aren't liking the direction that you've been taking X in. He hasn't won a match yet. Larabee-It's all part of the psychological warfare that we've been playing. Mr. White-Results are results, Robert. No one is going to be taking us seriously as representatives unless our only client within the pro wrestling ranks starts to show that he's more than just a sideshow gimmick. Larabee-I'm sure that you're going to see that he's much more than that soon. As soon as he takes care of Coyle O'Conner at Fallout. Mr. Darcy-Robert, what you are failing to realize is that the partners are tired of waiting on results. They saw that your ending the carefully planned and tested character of the Storyteller was, at best, a step in the wrong direction. Larabee-I did that to save the firm money. The actor hired to portray the Storyteller was going to cost the firm more money in the long run. Mr. Darcy-That was not your decision to make, Robert. That was our decision to make. If you value your position within this firm, Robert, you are going to have to find a way to respect our decision in this matter. Larabee-Yes sir, Mr. Darcy. Mr. White-Good, you are dismissed. [Larabee, Ms. Scabbia and X get up to leave the table.] Mr. Darcy-X, Andrea, we still have business to attend to. [X and Ms. Scabbia sit down at the table as Robert Larabee angrily leaves the room.] Ms. Scabbia-What else did you want to discuss, Mr. Darcy? Mr. Darcy-Nothing, other than you know that your job has been appreciated. X's focus on Coyle O'Coner should be enough to pull out a victory at Fallout? Ms. Scabbia-It should. I would also like to see some more of X's individuality shine through. There is alot that everyone can learn from him. Mr. White-We believe that he should remain the way he is. Silent. Sleek. The mystery that surrounds him should work to his advantage. We only see individuality as something that can bring about his eventual downfall. Ms. Scabbia-I have to disagree with you there, Mr. White. I think that his conformity to the rules that have been thrust onto him by Mr. Larabee and yourselves are holding X back. I think that if we allow X to show some of his true nature shine through, he will be more relaxed out there. Mr. Darcy-This is not open to debate, Andrea. He knew what he was getting into when he signed up to do this job. If you wish to make changes to that agreement, then maybe you're going to have to find yourself looking for other employment. Do you understand? Ms. Scabbia-Yes, Mr. Darcy, I understand. Mr. Darcy-Good. We can consider this discussion complete. X, your job is to defeat Coyle O'Conner at Fallout. Failure to do so can have dire consequences. You two are free to go. [Scene fades to black.]
