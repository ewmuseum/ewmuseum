Shaman stands in front of a few reporters as they seem to be thrusting mics up into his face as the cameras are swarming him. Shaman looks at them looking at each person with little emotion upon his face. His actions and demeanor are showing he is growing impatient with them. He looks at one of them and points his finger. “Johnny Smith right?” The reporter nods and quickly speaks. “Shaman it has been awhile since you have been in Australia wrestling. In fact if I remember correctly last time you were here you defeated the Australia Heavyweight and took his title. Then you simply left and relinquished the title a few months later signing with the WWA. How do you think you’ll fair this time?” Shaman looked at the man rubbed his chin. Looking upwards towards the sky in a mocked thinking fashion he suddenly grins and brings his eyes back to the reporter. “Funny you should bring that up. I am one of the most popular people to hit your country that you people embraced. I took the title from Crocodile Jones and nobody liked him at the time. Where is he now” I’ll tell ya he is retired. You see I face Thunder for my US Title belt. Either he has to put up or shut up. I am the only man he has not beaten hands down. He had to have help beating me last time.” Shaman smiles and looks over at the reporters. “I guess you can say I have Thunder by the short and curlies.” Couple of the reporters have smirks upon their faces as Shaman shakes his head looking at the reporters. He then points over at another reporter. “You off like a bride’s nighty!” Shaman grins looking at the reporter who raises an eye at him. “Aye Guvna, What does a bloke like you hope to get out of this match?” Shaman raises an eye and looks at the reporter. “You’re not from here are you” You sound a little soft in the knickers.” All the other reporters burst out laughing at the obvious Englishman in their presence. The Englishman looks flustered and stares at Shaman pointing a pen. “See here you yank!” Shaman smirks as he looks at the man who just insulted him. Shaman steps up close and then grabs the man pulling him close to his face. “I hope the hairs in your arse turn into drumsticks and beat the shit out of you.” Shaman shoves the little man out of his way and speaks. “To answer the light in the loafers” brit I will be glad to tell you. This match is a challenge to defeat one of Australia’s current heroes. Yet I am returning to the soil where I am 10 ? 0 undefeated, Including an Ayres rock match. If you remember that bloody match then you won’t ever forget it.” Shaman begins walking as he stops and looks out across the bay as he stares at the huge white structure looming there like a magnificent piece of art. The building is so brilliant in its white as it reflects off the water as he points at it. “That building is a landmark the world respects and looks to. It was constructed in 1957 and was designed by Jorn Utzon , Completed in 1973, on Bennelong Point.” Shaman grins looking at the shocked Australian reporters. “You see I like to know a little bit about the places I visit. Did you know that the band KISS used the Sydney Opera house as its cross over into using a Orchestra remixing it with there music live?” Shaman smiles as he stares at the building admiring its uniqueness and perfection in its angles and artistic lines. “Just like your advertisements state. There is no place in the world like Sydney.” Turning he walks down the walkway as he walks close to the railing near the harbors edge as he stares out over the sea of sailboats. Rubbing his hands together he admires the world before him. “You know I can see the affinity several of the people around the world see for Sydney. It was a perfect backdrop for the Olympics.” Looking down at the water he closes his eyes as the sun reflects off of it brightening his loose fitting white shirt as it flows freely in the breeze. He then turns and looks at the reporters. “You see Australia has many champions one straight to mind is Ian Thorpe. Thorpe won many Olympic medals for this country. He went out on top of the world a champion. Am I ready to retire and be a hero for the many masses around the world” Not right now so let me make Thunder the hero of his people, by making him famous and kicking his ass across the flats of Australia.” Shaman walks as a beautifully dressed woman stands just up the way as Shaman makes a beeline for her. He smiles as he wraps his arms around her and kisses her softly and turns towards the reporters. “Kawanaha the Paparazzi have found me and they have been dogging me all afternoon.” Kawanaha looks over at them her eyes narrow and shakes her head. She walks over and looks at all the cameras and speaks up. “Ok Gentlemen interviews and picture time is over. Now go away or else.” The cameras begin to get shut off one by one lens caps replaced and they begin to disperse. Kawanaha walks over to Shaman and smacks him in the arm hard. “Do you always have to attract so much damn attention?” Shaman looks at Kawanaha and leans on the railing as he places his forearms down on the metal. He then looks out at the Harbor before him. “They just want to see who is going to cave first in the media frenzy before the show. I have been here before and GCW is using it as a good way to promote its stars and show. Thunder is the hometown hero. How can he not be involved in the huge media frenzy?” Shaman cracks his neck from side to side looking out across the Harbor again, fixing his eyes on some unknown point. He suddenly stands and begins walking away once again as Kawanaha walks with him. “Why do you have to taunt them and bait them?” Kawanaha asks with a little venom in her voice. Shaman raises an eyebrow at his assistant and continues walking not answering. Kawanaha reaches up and grabs his arm. “You can answer me straight and quit getting all silent when I ask a question.” Shaman stops and looks at her hand grabbing his arm. “Most people would ask politely rather than forcefully. Yet I know it is not your style is it.” Shrugging her grip off of his arm and looks her in the eyes. “I do what I do because I can. Nobody else can say that. I do it because there is nobody else that will. I don’t mince words. I grab the bull by the horns and throw it to the ground and beat it till it is dead. Then I cut it into edible steaks and pieces to be fed to the next person in healthy doses.” Shaman is definitely annoyed at something as his mind seems clouded or in this case sharp as a tack. “He won the TV Title from me by the interference of Jayce Bradley. Then again Bradley was just seeking payback. Thunder did not beat me with Brute strength. I think it sits in his craw like flem from a cold.” Kawanaha winces at the analogy. “Could you have said something different that was a bit sick?” Shaman looks at Kawanaha and shakes his head. Turning he waves his hand forward around all the areas ahead of him. “This is Thunders backyard. He has something to prove and he wants to prove it against me. Ask me if I really care?” Shaman looks at Kawanaha very sternly. “I don’t he will fight and claw and do everything he can to beat me. In the end I am going to beat him down like a bitch in Detroit keeping money from her pimp.” Shaman looks at a brick wall and walks over to it and places a hand upon it. “Jimmy Thunder has F.I.T.H syndrome (Fucked in the head) and he doesn’t even realize it yet. I don’t give a two shits about who he thinks he is and what he is going to do. He can go pray to whatever gods or gods he believes in to help him. It won’t help him in anyway or form. The guy is so unlucky if it was raining virgins head be struck in the head by a poof.” Shaman smirks at the analogy. “You know researching Aussie insults was fun. I should use more of them. I have a few that are my favorites really. You can be in a country, but you never know the country until you understand and use their insults.” Shaman looks at his huge shadow as he narrows his gaze. “People talk about how strong he is. How powerful the huge Aussie is and how much I may try I cannot out strength the man. Truth is I am as strong as he is and can match him power for power. It has already been proven. Fact is Thunder couldn’t pull the skin off custard. Truthfully I wish his dad settled for a blow job.” Shaman began laughing at that one and Kawanaha smirked. “You should stay away from insulting the man as it could get you in trouble.” Shaman looked at Kawanaha. “Trash talking is the best way to get under your opponents skin. Nobody trash talks nowadays. It is all about the he is the hometown hero. People are depending on you. I am the champion and better than you. Forget that shit. I am taking it to the man on all fronts.” Shaman walked and looked at a GCW advertisement on a window poster. “You know Thunder is an ugly bastard. I bet is Momma was so ugly when she went to the beach sewerage got up and left. I shouldn’t even talk about the wank stain any longer.” Shaman raised his arms up and walked away from the window and advertisement. He walks and moves through and around people who look at him with awe as Kawanaha follows closely behind as she is dialing the cell phone. “Yeah I am checking in for security reports.” Kawanaha walked near Shaman as they walked down the walkway. “What do you mean you lost a car” Oh that is it when I get back I want all criminal checks on the valets.” Shaman stops and reaches over and grabs the cell phone and closes it. He looks at it and then throws it out over into the harbor looking at Kawanaha. “No more business talk. No more wrestling talk. I am going to enjoy the rest of the time I have before the damn show. This place has too many sites to just ignore.” Kawanaha looks at where the cell phone sank below the surface and huffed out a sharp breath. "I guess you will get what you want for this time." Shaman grinned as he stood victorious for this time. He walked down the walk as Kawanaha walked with him. “Did you really have to throw my cell in the harbor?” Shaman shrugged as he walked. “Couldn’t be helped.” She looks at him and frowns and they walk on down the walk way as the camera pans out over the bay fading as the sail boats sail too and fro.
