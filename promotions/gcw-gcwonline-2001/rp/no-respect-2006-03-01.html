No Respect

1 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 1, 2006 ---- Prelude: Bittersweet Victory ---- With a swagger in her step and a gleam in her eye, she marched through the backstage area. It was just like history repeating itself. The backstage area grew silent as she walked past with her covergirl smile, and her blonde hair with baby-blue tips resting gently on shiny piece of gold mounted on a black leather strap. On the gold plate it read Global Championsh Wrestling United States Championship. Talent and technicians glared into her teal blue eyes, radiating with confidence and achievement, but could do nothing but shake their head in disbelief. Veni Vidi Vici. She came, she saw, she conquered... and it felt damn good. How could this happen to GCW? How could a barely drinking age girl with a gymnastics background and a bad attitude just march in the door and take the second highest singles title in the world... in only three weeks? Most men work their entire lives to claim such a feat, but one match is all it took for The Black Mariah. Maybe Miss Valentine was right since the day she stepped foot into the door. It was much more than luck, much more than talent... it was destiny. If looks could kill, she'd be dead in her tracks. Even her arrogance couldn't shake the daggers that were being shot from the eyes of her 'peers.' With a smile she simply acted as if she didn't notice, she was quite proud of her accomplishment after all. Standing in front of her locker room, which was moved far away from the rooms of the rest of the 'talent,' she took a deep breath... completely unable to hold back a gleaming smile. Closing her eyes she turned the door knob and took a step into the room. 'Good job!' Shadow said the second The Black Mariah opened her eyes, seeming much more energetic and emotional than her normal state. Vivica took a second to appreciate the sheer happiness of a woman she holds so dear to her, 'That'll teach those idiots we mean business.' 'Yeah.' Vivica replied rubbing her forehead a bit, 'It was almost worth that pathetic attempt to capitalize on my glory from that bastard Kannon. I can't wait to step into the ring and teach him a thing or two about how to treat a lady.' 'Perhaps, you didn't need my luck.' Dawn McFarlane said extending her hand out to The Black Mariah, who shook it with a since of satisfaction. It was the first time Dawn extended anything to her since far before her falling out with Christian and The Hunting Grounds. Things were starting to come around, except for one thing. Over in the corner, completely apathetic towards his surroundings, Virgil Ambrosi watched the GCW Telecast on the less that 'high tech' television that GCW placed inside their locker room. 'Well?' Vivica asked Virgil Ambrosi, who didn't even as much as move in response. It was like she wasn't even there. What in the blue hell did she have to do to get his respect? Was winning a tag match not enough? Was winning the first singles championship for their group not enough? The biggest moment of her life, and it didn't even mean a damn thing to Ambrosi. She was sure that this would be the thing that would impress him... 'Tough fucking crowd...' She said aloud, before going back and talking about her match and gameplan with the other girls. Although still very much proud of her victory, she'd be lying if she said it was everything she expected it to be. Without his approval, as huge as it was, it still felt a bit hollow. Perhaps fame does come with a price. ---- First Passage: Anonymity ---- How could something she worked so hard to accomplish feel so... empty? It was a question that racked her brain ever since she left the arena that night in Kobe, Japan. What a better way to get her mind off of everything than to take herself out to dinner. A toast to herself, for a well-earned victory. Hell if no one else was going to toot her horn, she might as well. What a better place than outside a nearby United States Military base. At least the people there would speak English... she hoped. A couple of business partners of Christian's hooked Vivica J Valentine up with her own limo for the night. She ended up riding in the back seat solo though, as Shadow, Dawn, and Virgil all had their own plans on how to spend the evening. Pulling up in front of the stir fry restaurant... she felt like a princess joining the ball. High on accomplishment, surrounded by lowly peasants who all wanted to be just like her. Jealousy of others was something she greatly enjoyed. It made working so hard worth it all by itself. Clinching her black leather bomber style jacket, she walked towards the door while the limo pulled away behind her. Inside, a short asian man about her height collected her money before escorting her to a window seat where she could look outside at the marvelous city at night. So rich in culture, everything in Japan was beautiful to her... much more elaborate than her Chicago streets. Vivica though to herself she could get used to a place like this, maybe she'd come back with Christian some day. The first time she ever visited the country was with him in Global Wrestling, where he'd won Showdown In Tokyo on his way to his first World Championship. Just before she began to think of the ironies of herself winning a world championship in the same country, an unexpected guest arrived at the table. 'Excuse me, is this seat taken?' A young American man asked her. She looked up at him with her brilliant blue-green eyes, which were brought to life by her baby blue tank top. Without an answer she turned away from him again and looked out the window. Hoping that he had the same mentality as the species he shares the most in common with, a canine... who if you ignore them will simply walk away. Is this particular scenario, his evolution into an intelligent being was far from a blessing. 'I'm sorry, let me introduce myself. My name is James Morrison, I'm an airman in the United States Military.' He shot a cheesy smile back at Vivica, who thought she was going to throw up a little bit inside of her mouth. Being married to a man who secretly made his fortune making military technology, she probably had a higher classification as a civilian than this simpleton did. Who, by the looks of his wandering eyes, sees her as nothing more than a pair of tits with a mouth. She respected the members of the military, that is, until they tried to use it as a horrible pick-up line. 'That's nice.' She replied as nicely as she could, before once again diverting her attention away from him. It was a good night, she'd hate to ruin it by ripping some poor teenager a new asshole. A celebration shouldn't be ruined by such a thing. 'You looked lonely so I thought I'd give you a bit of company.' He said before taking a seat across from her, uninvited. The Black Mariah hoped he could see her rolls her eyes from under her long blonde hair... but he obviously didn't. 'Let me save you some time,' Vivica looked back at him flashing a ring, 'I'm engaged.' 'Whoa, I'm just looking for some company. You looked like you needed it. It's kind of boring eating dinner all by yourself don't you think?' He tried to back off a bit, hoping to calm down the firecracked who sat across from him. Leaving her vulnerable to his attack. 'Not really.' Vivica responded, 'I also don't give autographs... if that's what you're here for.' 'Autographs?' The man laughed for a second, 'Do you get asked that a lot? I'm sure a beautiful woman like yourself gets mistaken for a celebrity all the time. What name do you end up signing the most?' Vivica just glared back at the man, blinking before shooting the woman look of scorn back at him. The woman's look of scorn... the transligual symbol for even the most ignorant amoung us. Awkwardly the man looked out the window and then back at the food. He'd entered the restaurant just to try and pick her up. It wasn't going to be that easy to get rid of him. 'You really have no clue who I am do you?' Vivica snapped at him. He deserved it. He should've got up and left while he still had the chance. Now he was stuck like a fly on a spider's web, open to Miss Valentine's interrogation. 'Sorry... I don't.' The man responded, 'I thought for sure I'd recognize a beautiful lady like you. If it's any compensation though, you're already the biggest star in my heart.' 'My name is Vivica... Vivica J. Valentine. I'm a professional wrestler?' The Black Mariah said towards the dumbfounded airman. Suddenly like a light bulb turning on inside his mind, his eyes opened wide and looked back at her with excitement. 'I know you!' He said with a smile, 'You're Christian Zenith's valet in Global Wrestling! How could I forget you?!' 'How the fuck do I always get into these situations?' Vivica said to herself, shaking her head with her eyes closed. 'I havn't watched in a while... is Christian still the champion? I'm more of a Mindy Mercer fan myself.' 'Do you ever shut the fuck up?' Vivica finally said, after having enough, 'Do you think before you speak? I don't like you, I don't want to talk to you, and I'd like you to get the hell away from my table before I cut off your dick, which apparently is the only fully functioning part of your body, and shove it down your throat. Comprende?' James Morrison sat in shock, not used to the horrible sting of rejection, staring back at Vivica. 'Fuck it, I'll leave.' Vivica picked up her jacket and walked away from the table before even taking a bite. Working so hard for nothing. Trying to make a new name for herself, seperating herself from the shadow of Christian Zenith seemed fruitless. Even capaturing the GCW United States Championship did nothing for the average dumbass. Lestatt Knight was right when he said that trying to please people would do nothing but destroy you. Perhaps it wasn't the price of fame at all, because it seemed pretty apparent that fame never came. Someone out there had to be proud of her other than her teammates, someone in this huge universe had to have recognized what she had done other than herself. The only question was... where do you turn now? ---- Second Passage: Stitch In Time ---- Everything looks so different from the back of a limo. People walking the streets stop and stare at you in envy as you go by. Everyone wants to know who or what is behind the black tented windows. Could it be a famous movie star? Perhaps a famous athlete? Maybe a wealthy entrepeneur? Vivica remembers those days very well from when she was a young girl, walking the very same streets she looked at from the back of her very own limo. They weren't an unfamiliar thing to see in Highland Park, many famous people lived there while she was growing up. She remembers very vividly talking with her friends, who would occassionally make up stories about their adventures trying to go to Michael Jordan's house and meeting Air Jordan. Everything had looked the same to her, although everyone gawked as she passed. So much had changed in her life, but time seemed to stay still in the rich suburban community of Highland Park, Illinois. It was amazing to her, even the High School still looked the same. Perhaps the thing she regretted the most about leaving suburban hell was never getting her diploma. She would do it all over again if she had the chance. Everything was material, things that meant the most were what you wore, whose CD was in your CD player, and how expensive your purse was. Your value as a human being was simply placed on how much money you possessed. It made Vivica sick. Yet irony put Miss Valentine in the back of a black stretch limo, navigating the streets that she not-so-fondly refers to as her 'hometown.' A former homecoming queen, a former prom queen, toast of her high school... the most popular girl in school. A cheerleader, the captain of her gymnastics team, and even played lead in the high school musical. Missing. By now everyone had to know she was still alive and doing well, she was on television once a week. Yet the feeling of anonymity is what she seeked so badly upon her abrupt departure. It was the exact same feeling she was trying to shake on her return. Everyone here had always recognized her. As she pulled closer to her parents house high six-digit house, her nerves began to get the best of her. She wanted so badly to be recognized for her achievement. Did she really need to go this low though? Back to the very community that was ready to give her the world because her parents were such rich patrons of the community? Because she had the body and face of an angel? Perhaps the kind of recognition she'd recieve back home isn't the type of respect she wanted at all. What would her parents think seeing her again in person after all these years? Would they understand why she left? Did they still love her? Would they still accept her? She didn't know the answers, yet she was so nervous about what they may be. When it was all said and done would it really even matter what her parents think? That was the thought that crossed her mind at the very second her limo came to a slow stop in front of her parents house. It still looked the same, pardon a new garden her mother had planted in the front yard. 'We've arrived Miss Valentine...' Marques stated from the driver's cab of the limo, with the glass between the two now down, making them able to communicate with one another for the first time since their departure from Zenith Manor. The Black Mariah bit her bottom lip from the back of the limo and looked at the house she grew up in. Her father still drove the same silver BMW, parked in the driveway. They wouldn't care that she was GCW United States Champion... all they ever wanted was for her to be rich and popular. They wanted her to have the best education money can buy, and then grow up and be rich, just like them. They didn't love her because she was their own flesh and blood... they loved her because she was their ticket to more money. Their only child, the heir to their thrown. 'Marques...' Vivica said pausing before finishing her sentance, taking one last look at the place she used to call home, 'Take me home please.' 'Are you sure Miss Valentine?' 'I am sure Marques.' Vivica responded seconds before the limo began to pull away from the house. If she had stayed 30 seconds longer, she would've seen her mother and father leave the house to attend a local theatre production. Perhaps it was for the best. Maybe after everything she still wasn't ready to come back to face her past. Or maybe the saying is true, maybe you can never come home again. ---- Epilogue: Party Time ---- With a loud band, Vivica J. Valentine's arrival back to Zenith Manor North in Chicago was announced. With Marques pulling the limo around back to its parking quarters, she was sure that she'd be the only one in the house with Christian working seemingly 24 hours a day trying to restore some dignity back to Zenith Corporation. Much to her surprise, she was greeted by a familiar face... dressed in black from head to toe. His cadet blue eyes, intimidating and cold to most, was comforting to Vivica. 'Welcome home champ.' Christian Zenith said from the stairwell, walking down to greet The Black Mariah at the door, 'You looked really good out there. I see you were taught well.' 'I'm sure my natural ability had nothing to do with it Christian.' 'You have to admit you had a damn good teacher.' Sometimes it was hard to tell if Chris was joking with her or not. He wasn't one to have a sense of humor, but then again he wasn't really one to know how to make someone feel all warm and fuzzy inside either. He had his own overly serious, professional charm. She enjoyed it, and new that he was proud of her accomplishments. It was the praise she'd waited so long to hear, it was unfortunate by the time she'd arrived she no longer felt the need to hear it. The conclusion that a singles championship was a goal that she set for herself to achieve, and the most she thought about it, the more she realized that she really didn't give a damn what ignorant masses thought of her. 'Well, WorldWide is going to be something to remember next week.' Vivica said with an arrogant smile across her face. 'Why is that?' 'There is going to be a celebration for GCW's newest champion,' Vivica said while hanging up her black leather bomber jacket, 'they're never going to know what in the hell they've just gotten themselves into. It's going to be great. After all, what could be better than a party celebrating me?'
