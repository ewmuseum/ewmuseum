Date: December 11-12 *Credit goes to My Chemical Romance's Famous Last Words for song lyrics Note: There is a section of this story that does deal with extreme vampire violence. It does get a little bloody, so if this offends do not read this. Otherwise, please enjoy. The narrator, from before returns to the scene of darkness. He's lighting another cigarette and looks into the camera. This time we can see a pair of blue eyes amongst the darkness. “Let us continue this story,” he speaks in a soft voice, this time as if he was frightened, “Now we show you decisions Hope Clarkson has made. Although it is a known fact that some of these decisions Hope wasn't making on her own. She has been influenced in some unknown way to do other things. Be prepared, this isn't for the faint of heart.” Time: December 11th 2006 Place: Honolulu, Hawaii It was a rather odd night in the islands of Hawaii. There was a ferocious thunderstorm that plagued the island causing the sky to grow into a shade of blackness, that most people wouldn't be able to describe. The only light was the light of the lightning itself as most of the power was out all over the island. That was except from one house on the island. Even in the darkness you could see from the front room window a raging fire from a fireplace. This house, housed a man that was considered a personal hit man. A person who cared more about evil, than that of the good. He looked out the window and laughed at the islanders feebly working amongst the storm trying to get power restored. “Foolish people,” he said with a British accent, “It pays to have connections, and my dear Kawanaha Kuno is a very strong connection. It just pays to have money in this world.” “Money isn't everything,” a soft voice whispered in his ear. The man jumped around frightened by the sudden voice. The only thing was there was no one there. He smiled as he sipped on his glass of brandy. “You're fooling yourself, Conrad,” he laughed, “Big house like this, and your imagination is running wild. Besides, Kawanaha said she wouldn't ever be in my bed.” He throws his glass into the fire and the alcohol makes the fire rage a little more. “That woman is more concerned about that Oriental bitch that sleeps in her bed with her. Having the gall to ask me if I would rather watch them, as that's the only closeness I'll ever have to her. We shall see then won't we when I'm done with Keiko Yoshimoto.” “I don't think you'll be doing anything of that sort,” the soft voice said, with just a taste of venom. Conrad drew his gun that he had in his side holster. He fired around the room even cracking the window in a couple of places. Again, there was no one there. “How the hell can you be a hit man,” the voice now demonic like laughed, “You can't even hit a lithe woman in your own house. Then again, let's try the stun gun.” Conrad didn't have time to react as his body was jolted with a painful force of electricity. His body rocked out of his chair and he fell on the floor withering in pain. On the floor, in the glow of the firelight, he saw a pair of white high heels. Looking up, despite that his body was shaking uncontrollably she stared at a beautiful blonde haired woman who's hair hung down to her shoulders. She was wearing a white dress and had a tiara on her head. Her nails were stretched out very long as if this woman was part animal in some way. Then he saw the thing that scared him the most, fangs coming out of the mouth of this woman that formed a smile. “Hello, Conrad,” she said, “I think you know me. I told you I would return for you before you shocked me into a deep sleep.” “How the hell do you get here,” he snarled, “How did Kawanaha turn you lose.” “Kawanaha doesn't know I'm here. Plus, I think you have me a little confused with the vessel that carries my spirit. You see Hope is sleeping now. Tonight you are dealing with a Vampire Queen. Thy name is Leandra, but you shall call me your highness.” “I shall do know such thing,” he gasped, “You are Hope Clarkson, and nothing more.” “Is this how you treat your precious Queen of England,” she said with a sneer, “I met her once, when she was starting her reign. Charming woman, I couldn't bear to turn her into one like me, though. She just emitted too much power, and I couldn't have my throne in jeopardy. Yet, I'm sure to this day she still remembers me.” “Your name is Hope Maria Clarkson!” “That would be Marie, ass, you should have done your research more closely! Plus her last name is actually Lockhart! Now, you know why I'm here.” “Actually I have no idea,” he quivered. “Cowering,” she smiled, “I like that. You attempted to kill my pet. Then you attacked me, and even threatened my life. Something that I do not take lightly, the reason I'm here. You see I'm here to teach you a lesson. I would turn you like me, but I don't accept scum in my army which is the Demons of the Night.” She shuddered almost as if she was feeling pleasure of her announcement of her army. Taking advantage of that, Conrad kicked his foot out catching the woman in the chest knocking her back. He gets to his feet and tries to run but Leandra leaps to her feet smiling. “Now why did you have to do a thing like that, I was actually going to show some mercy on you.” She leapt into the air as he made his way to the door. With a quick grace she landed on his back and forced him down to the floor from the blow. Conrad tried to fight away, but Leandra was using a force that he has never dealt with before. The two thrashed on the floor trying to take control of the fight. First Conrad would have control, but it was just enough for Leandra to actually breathe, then she was back in control yet again. Finally, he rolled back into the middle of the floor with Leandra on top of the man's chest staring down into his face. He tried to throw her off, only to be cracked in the head with the handle of the gun that Conrad was using earlier. Blood came out from the wound that Leandra dabbed her fingers in and licked. “I've had better, you have a bit of a bitter taste going on there,” she cooed, “After all the more vile a human is, the more tainted his blood is.” “What the hell are you,” he cried, “I mean you are no dragon, and they shouldn't exist.” “You're right, and technically you would think I don't exist either. I'm a vampire. One of the few still left on this world.” “But you can't be a vampire, you have been born into this world, vampires are the living dead.” “There are many things about my kind you know nothing about. This vessel was born yes, but my spirit is what's immortal. I've been a host to many bodies over the years, but this one by far has been the best one. I'm not done with her yet, but I tell you that she and I are one. “So if she dies you die?” “Not exactly, usually I don't stay host to one being for a length of time. With Hope, there was a power inside her that could house my body. I think that's all you need to know about me though. Quite frankly, you know too much so…” With a quick motion and the roar of the thunder, she slashes his throat. Blood spewed from the jugular vain like a fountain. The blood splattered on her pure white dress causing her to laugh and rub the blood all over the material. She licked her hands clean as the blood drained from him. “You…,” Conrad gasped as he was beginning to leave this world, “won't…get…away…with…this.” “Oh, you don't need to worry about this. You should be lucky, I usually seduce my victims, I spared you that pleasure, for you don't deserve it.” Leandra leaned in and drank what was left of the blood spilling out of his neck. She then let out a whistle and the front window shattered around her to reveal the head of Ariel, the red dragon. “Ah my dear friend,” she smiled, “It's time for a little fiery passion, don't you think.” Ariel blew a ring of fire around Hope. The front room lit up in a blaze. “I guess it isn't true what they say. What you don't know will actually kill you,” she shouts, “See you in hell, Conrad…someday.” She leaps onto the head of Ariel and the two fly above the house. Shouting orders in German, she ordered the beast to burn down the entire house. Ariel obeyed and Leandra smiled in the firelight. She then removed her dress and held in her hands. “Let's go home,” she whispered, “Before the servant's hypnosis wears off.” “As you can see,” the narrator says coming back on the screen, “Leandra made the decisions on life based on vengeance. She was attacked and she lashed out, so you have to ask yourself the team of Pleasure and Pain, just which version of Hope Clarkson will you be fighting? The demon or this woman...” Time: December 12, 2006 Place: Oahu, Hawaii Hope Clarkson was sitting in the middle of her room in what was considered the servants house at Kawanaha Kuno's mansion. This was her and Christopher's prison because of the fact they loved each other, and the pain Hope, as her Leandra side, attacked Kit Kuno and Faith Von Almen. Unlike Keiko who was seeing the negative side of things, Hope tried to see positively despite what she didn't know when she would turn into vampire mode, and what she done while doing it, except for one time when talking to Kyo Kushinaga. When she wasn't training, doing whatever Kawanaha wanted her to do, or being with Christopher she sat in her room strumming on a guitar thinking of songs that would like to sing, or writing some for a future CD project, hoping she could raise the money for it Kit's bills even quicker. She recently purchased this guitar before she was punished and taught herself how to play it. Now she was sitting in the middle of the floor with it in her lap and singing away. “I'm not afraid to keep on living,” she sang with her eyes closed, “I'm not afraid to walk this world alone. Honey, if you stay, I'll be forgiven. Nothing you can say, will keep me from going home.” “Damn,” she said to herself, “The guitar just doesn't sound right.” “Of course it doesn't,” a heavenly voice to her said, “You're trying to sing an alternative song with just a guitar, that isn't even electric.” Hope smiled looking up at the voice that belonged to Christopher Clarkson. The only person she truly cared about when it came to love. A man she was raised to hate, and he was raised to equally hate her, found love despite the chaos in their lives. She was his angel and would always tell her that. To Hope, Chris was her protector, and he had proven to be that with the last few days when the servants would torment her from going from paradise to hell. Some of the men who tried to ridicule Hope for this and do things to her; they wound up feeling Christopher's wrath. Yet Christopher looked like he had been in another fight as his lip was swollen and bleeding. “Okay, who was it this time,” Hope said with a hint of fear. “Raul, once again,” he sighed, “Apparently he didn't learn his lesson yet about wanting to bed you.” “You didn't help by making those comments about his mother and calling him a wetback.” The two laughed and Hope got up to grab a damp towel off the dresser to clean his wounds. “Are you thinking of doing an acoustic version of Famous Last Words,” he asked her grunting with the pain. “Yes, but I was also thinking of making it my ring entrance. It's very calming and does describe me.” “You said the same thing about Baby I Don't Care. Besides you do have a dark side now.” “Chris, I don't want to talk about that with you. I don't want to turn into her around you.” “I don't even think as her, you could ever hurt me. I see the way you look at me when I've seen you as her. There's still that love in your eyes.” “Is that a risk you want to take?” “Yes, because with the love you have to give to me, you would die yourself just to be with me.” Hope smiled. He's said this to her many times, and every time it would calm her down and make her see the love he did have for her. She leaned into kiss him and he grunted a little as her lips pressed against the swelling on his lips. “No more fights,” she demanded, “This is only making you weaker and Keiko and myself needs you at ringside. Preferably in one piece. After all, I'm a trained martial artist and wrestler! I think I can handle the Hawaiian servants just as well as I can man-handle a 250 pound man. Let us not forget, I can throw your ass around.” “But I like it when you do that. Really spices things up. Come on, play so more for me.” Hope grabbed her guitar, but as she did so Christopher noticed something sticking out of the dresser. “Hope, I didn't know you had a new red dress.” “What are you talking about?” “Right there, sticking out of the top drawer,” he said walking over to it. He pulled it out, only to show that it wasn't red, a least the color of the material. It was wet in blood. “Hope…” Hope didn't answer; she couldn't as she didn't know how that got there. “I don't know how that got there,” she said her voice shaking, “I must have changed again.” Was that you with me last night or was that someone else?” “Christopher, I don't know dammit! How would I have got past all the servants?” “You had to do it somehow. I'm not upset with you. Wait there's something else here.” Christopher pulled out a wallet and opened it up. Inside was a driver's license for a Conrad Worthington. When he showed it to Hope, Hope and he both looked in fear. “Hope, do you think, you've killed someone.” “I have no control of Leandra. It's like I'm in a deep sleep, at least that's what Kyo told me would happen if I went there. She was in full control. She could've killed him. After all, he did threaten me and Ariel.” “We have to hide this. If Kawanaha finds out,” “Shh,” Hope said fearfully. She then whispers, “There are footsteps coming.” Christopher swore and threw the dress back into the dresser. He just slammed the drawer shut as Kawanaha and Keiko burst into the room. “Okay, how the hell did you do it,” Kawanaha demanded to Hope. “Do what? I can't leave this house without your servants telling you everything.” “Conrad was killed inside his house because it was sent on fire. There are no signs of his body except what's in the ash. You are the only suspect.” Christopher spoke up, “I find that very hard to believe. He could easily have had more enemies out there.” “When I want your opinion, I shall ask for it. Now then, Hope how did you do it? Burning things down and leaving no evidence. That seems to be your calling card as of late. With the Clarkson residence and your father's catfight league, you're a regular pyro maniac.” “Thanks for the compliment,” Hope smiled, “But I can show you that, I had nothing to do with it. I was here last night. Did you question the servants?” “Of course, I did,” she spat, “They all said you were here and that you never attempted to leave.” “So, then why are you questioning me? You can't even trust the words of those who serve you?” Kawanaha choked on her words. Hope didn't hide the fact she took pleasure with that last battle of words she has won. “If I find out in anyway,” Kawanaha said. “I know, I know,” Hope imitated Kawanaha, “I'll suffer your wrath and blah blah blah.” “Anyway,” the Hawaiian Goddess sighed, “Keiko has some news for you. She has requested that she tells you this news in private, so I shall respect your wishes.” Kawanaha leaves and as soon as she is gone Keiko breaks into a smile. “Kawanaha is buying me a house here in Oahu,” she says with a giggle. Hope and Christopher look at each other with disgust. They both try to speak about this, finally Chris looks at Hope. “Well you've known her longer. You might as well tell her, my love.” Hope looks at Keiko and with the plainest look she could give, “And this is supposed to make me feel better.” “This is going to benefit all of us,” Keiko whispers, “You see I convinced Kawanaha to make you two my servants.” “I'm failing to see where this is benefiting us,” Christopher groaned. Hope seeing the light bulb over her head gasps, “You would never make us work for you unless Kawanaha was there to check on us, or make a visit to you. We would have our freedom back!” Keiko nods, “As you would say in this country, Merry Christmas, early. You two would have your own room, I would see to that personally. You would have free reign, just like I do here at Kawanaha's. The only condition is you must live by the Bushido Code, until Hope's debt is repaid. Then perhaps after that, I can see to it you two get your own place.” “Well we do have our own place at the farmhouse.” “I know, but I know you two, that's really Christine's home now, and you wouldn't want to burden her.” Hope smiled and hugged her friend, “What's more important is you gave me and Christopher freedom. The two of us can keep up the façade that you are controlling us.” Christopher too looked up at Keiko, “Maybe we all can celebrate this later.” “Well I can't have sex with any of you. That was Kawanaha's condition.” “I wasn't referring to that, Keiko. I just meant a small celebration when you get your house. Besides I don't think you would want to do that with me again.” Keiko smiles, “Well it wasn't too bad, but if you don't mind I do prefer the ladies right now. As for Hope, she and I will always have that closeness, but in the end, she's all yours. I respect that. Now then, you two keep up the slave thing. We've still got two weeks at least before I can get my own place.” All three nod and the scene fads back out. All that is left standing is Christopher Clarkson and he has the cigarette in his mouth. The same cigarette as the narrator of this story. “Decisions, those for good, and those for bad,” he says, “We all have to make them in our lives. As you can see some are good with Keiko saving her friend and saving her honor. Some are for evil, as you seen with Leandra. Yet with you Pleasure and Pain, their decision is mutual. You are now just a mere statistic. You will fall to them this week as they demand vengeance for the impromptu match. There is no turning back now, can you stand to the force of two Hopes and a Dragon. Not even I could do that, and I'm their manager. Beware and if I were you, I would decide to run and not bother showing up.” With one last puff, the scene fades to a smoky black fog.
