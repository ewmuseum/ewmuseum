Senator Johnathan Richard Mabus (IG Round 42 Promo)

20 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/20/2008 The ruckus crowd in the hall is eagerly awaiting the appearance of Presidential Candidate Johnathan Richard Mabus. Mabus is appearing there to speak about his bid to become the President of the United States and about his current come from behind effort in the primaries and caucuses being held throughout the country. The crowd starts cheering wildly as Mabus enters the Hall with an entourage of his highest level advisors. The crowd cheers rabidly as a chant of "Mabus!" starts up from the crowd. Mabus is a Caucasian man in his fifties that has combed brownish graying hair, a glowing smile, and is wearing a tan suit with a red tie. He's wearing his American flag lapel to show his support for the freedom on people. He is a former pro wrestler, lawyer, and is now a Senator in the United States Senate representing the state of Wyoming. Now he is in a close second for the Nomination. The voting frenzy surrounding him has been compared to that of a cult or a congregation. It has been years since people have seen this kind of hype behind a candidate. Mabus is the man of the future and his somewhat exaggeratted tones during speeches has made him the people's darling and his oratory is some of the best the world of politics has seen in many years. Mabus walks through the crowd and climbs up the stairs onto the stage as the applauds him even more. He acknowledges the crowd with a gesture of the hands as he walks over behind the podium and stands behind it before beginning his speech. Mabus stands in front of his audience as they await his speech. His eyes gleam with pride as he begins to speak to the masses. "My fellow people of the American nation," he starts off softly. "We need a change in direction." This draws some applause from the audience as Mabus looks around with pride for a second before continuing his speech. Mabus seems very confidant at the moment and seems to also be very gifted with the art of public speaking. "The people WANT change," he continues with a little more furor than before. "They don't want the same old things going on in Washington. I am not going to come out here like some candidates and talk about false hopes. I'm not going to talk about having a dream for this country because I don't deal with dreams. I deal with reality. And the reality is that America needs someone who knows what they are doing to fix this country. We don't need ministers or women or politicians talking about false hope and unity. What we need is someone to LEAD this county. What you, the American people, are doing is electing a LEADER who will lead by example, not someone who's going to go around and blow hot air all over this country about making a difference. We need to UNITE under one strong leader who will get both our domestic and foreign policy fixed and I AM THAT LEADER!" The crowd erupts into loud cheering and gives Mabus a standing ovation. Mabus acknowledges the applause and continues with his speech. "We as Americans need to have our freedoms protected not just here but abroad," Mabus says with a stoicism not seen in many politicians as he looks around the room. "So I think we need more living space, more room to MOVE AROUND so to speak. So under my regime we will do the following. Firstly we shall remove ninety five percent of American troops out of Iraq as quickly as possible!" “Foreign policy and domestic policy in this country is going down the toilet,” Mabus continues in a sly tone of voice. “What we need in office is someone who knows how to stand up to the big corporations. Someone who can stand up to rival nations and not be bullied around. Someone who has the experience to pull off foreign policy victories and do it with the power of a pro wrestler who doesn’t take any heat from anybody without standing down. I can take a punch and keep on going. I am that candidate and I will be that president. I am the toughest president this country will ever have. No, I didn’t kill a man with a sword or survived a battle. I was a professional wrestler for three years and people laugh at me for that. But at least when I say something I can back it up by not saying “Yes, we can” over and over again or live in the past like the other candidates. I’m not here preaching to the choir. I’m here talking to Americans about American issues and I’m not going to give you people false hope. I’m here to make a change and do it without lying to the people. I will do it by doing it through the system that has been working in Washington for many years that everybody else is whining about changing. I will make the change using the defective system that we have just to prove change can happen and it can be done in the way but in a more effective way than it has EVER been done before.” The crowd starts cheering wildly again which pleases Mabus. His speech is going well and Mabus knows that he has the crowd in the palm of his hand now. "My plan will then have the following happen," he continues with a little smirk on his face. "We will then get ourselves some more living space. This country is too small. We don't have enough room to move around. So we will do the following. We will make the United States of America take control of ALL of America! What we need to do to fix the illegal immigration problem is to make Mexico a part of our country either on their own free will or BY FORCE IF NECCESARY!" "And the same goes for Canada as well!" Mabus continues to the continued ruckus applause of the people in the smallish hall in which the speech is being given. "The North and South American continents as well as the Caribbean and Central America need to be on a UNITED front not a DIVIDED one," Mabus continues as he suddenly bursts out in an emotion and movement with his arms as he speaks as he gets more motivated to give his message to the people. "We need to dissolve of the dictatorships and the fascist and communist regimes that have taken hold in the Americas! We as Americans need to be UNITED with our brothers to the North and the South before the Chinese and the Russians try to take us over! We need to unite with them and be one whole united New World instead of one divided by class, religion, race, language, culture, sex, or national border!" This draws another round of applause as Mabus becomes inspired wby this reaction and starts to continue with his rhetoric as the crowd fires him up even more. The longer the speech goes on, the more fury and passion come from Mabus' words and actions as he speaks. "WE WILL NEED NO MORE BORDERS TO KEEP OUT THE MISCREANTS, DEVIANTS, AND THE UNDESIRABLES!" he continues with a mad fury rarely seen on a political stage such as this one. "THERE WILL BE NO WAY TO KEEP OURSELVES FROM GOING WHEREVER WE PLEASE! NO ONE WILL BE HELD BACK ANYMORE BY PASSPORTS AND ALL PEOPLE SHALL BE EQUAL UNDER ONE NATION GOVERNED BY ONE FLAG AND LED BY ONE RULER! AND THAT RULER WILL BE THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA!" Mabus continues forth with his speech as the crowd grows into even more of a frenzy than they were before. He senses that he's on the verge of going over the top with the crowd and getting them buying into his ideals so he lays in with his final plans as the crowd is now in a full fledged frenzy. "AMERICA NEEDS TO UNITE AS ONE AND MAKE THESE DREAMS WE ALL HAVE BECOME A REALITY!" Mabus continues as his speech rolls on with more shouting and action. "WE DON'T NEED TO LISTEN TO WASHINGTON LOBBYISTS, HOLLYWOOD CELEBRITIES, OR BRIBED POLITICIANS ANYMORE! THAT IS WHY WHEN I GO TO WASHINGTON AS YOUR PRESIDENT ONE OF THE FIRST THINGS I SHALL DO IS BEAT DOWN CORRUPTION WITH A HAMMER UNTIL IT IS AS BLOODY AS AS THE BEACHES OF NORMANDY WHEN OUR AMERICAN TROOPS FOUGHT THE FORCES OF THE AXIS POWERS WHEN THEY SHOWED THEIR PRIDE FOR THEIR COUNTRY AND PROTECTING THEIR FREEDOMS WHEN THEY STORMED THE BEACHHEAD LIKE FURIOUS DRAGONS FROM HELL BEFORE THEY DEFEATED THE AXIS POWERS AND BROUGHT FREEDOM BACK TO ALL PEOPLE OF EUROPE!" The crowd starts going into a frenzy and is holding up “Mabus “08” signs if they have them. The rest of the crowd is cheering much to Mabus? delight which only inspires him even more. "I WILL BRING CHANGE FOR ALL PEOPLE!" Mabus shouts out with such furious passion that the people get even more revved up by his speech than they have previously. "WE NEED A UNITED FRONT WORLDWIDE! WE NEED TO UNITE ALL PEOPLE UNDER A DEMOCRACY AND BRING DEMOCRACY TO THOSE WHO HAVE NO VOICE WORLDWIDE BECAUSE WE WILL STRIKE DOWN THE DOORS OF THE ENEMY GATES AND STORM THE TERRORISTS AND ALL THOSE WHO HARBOR THEM LIKE THE MARINES STORMED IWO JIMA! AMERICA WILL BE A WORLD POWER AGAIN AND WE WILL BE STRONGER THAN WE EVER HAVE BEEN BEFORE! IF YOU WANT A UNITED AMERICA, VOTE FOR MABUS! IF YOU WANT YOUR VALUES PROTECTED, VOTE MABUS! AND IF YOU WANT A NATION THAT WANTS ONLY PEACE, VOTE MABUS! VOTE MABUS FOR A NEW AMERICA! THANK YOU!" The crowd erupts into a cheering frenzy and starts chanting for Mabus. Mabus waves to the people as he catches his breath and leaves the stage with his advisors before heading out of the Hall after shaking hands with the crowd and taking pictures with them. Once outside, Mabus' campaign manager comes over to him and smiles. "Nice work John," he says with a smile. "Next we have the wrestling match that you signed up for and then it's off to campaign in Ohio and Texas." Mabus smiles and replies, "Sounds good to me. We also have to do some more work on the unions to get endorsements otherwise we’ll lose momentum." A limousine then pulls up to the curb outside the Hall and Mabus and his advisors quickly get inside before they get mobbed. The limousine then quickly drives off and heads for the airport. However, all of his ideals and popular rhetoric hides something sinister. They hide what he has planned for the future. This man is no ordinary man. He is planning to take over the world and rule it under a regime of "false democracy" and "freedom." He is the Third Antichrist Nostradmaus predicted and all he wants to bring is death and destruction.
