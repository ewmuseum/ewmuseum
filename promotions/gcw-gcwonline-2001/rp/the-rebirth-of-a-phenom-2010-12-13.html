The Rebirth Of A Phenom

13 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/13/10 Life is a competition, and I'm a very competitive person. Beyond whatever the greater purpose may be I show up expecting to win. Anything that doesn't result in my hand raised in victory is a complete and utter failure... because in life there is no moral victories. You win or you lose. You live or you die. Life is painted in black and white, it's our own personalities that force us to see it as anything different. Our emotions, life experiences, and moral barometers warp our perception of a black and white reality... distorting it until it blends to gray. Or maybe that's just an excuse I make to justify the terrible things I've done. The blood on my hands refuses to rinse clean and even if the world chooses to forgive me, I may never forgive myself. My own competitive spirit warped me into someone I'm not and someone who I should never be. As Herman Edwards once said after a New York Jets game, "We play to win the game." I just wanted to win the game, but I lost myself. So perhaps my words will ring true to you when I tell you that losing wasn't the hardest part at Dangerous Games. Keeping my United States Championship and sticking a middle finger up to Christian Zenith would've made me the happiest girl in Chicago, but it wasn't meant to be. Winning Dangerous Games and tying together the loose ends I left last winter would've been my shot at a second chance... but I couldn't get the job done. The hardest part of Dangerous Games was looking into the eyes of the girl who inspired me to follow my dream and work my ass off to be in this business, dressed up in a monkey suit while Christian Zenith pulled the strings. I thought all she needed was a dose of reality. I thought all she needed was to know that someone would have her back... I thought wrong. You're not the Karina I fell in love with, not anymore. I'll never call you by that name ever again. From this day forward you'll always be Rasa to me, and the very taste of that word as it crosses my lips makes me want to spit in disgust. I hate you Rasa, with every fiber of my being not just for what you've done to me... but for what you've done to Karina. And I'll never forgive you Christian for being an egomaniacal, tight-assed, OCD, self-worshipping, manipulative cocksucker. I've never wished death upon any person walking this planet, Chris. I've said "I'll fucking kill you" more times in my life than I've said "I'm sorry," but I never literally meant it to a single person I've ever said it to... Except you. Fuck you, Chris. Die in a fire. Darkness and complete silence, the two things Vivica wanted the most were the only two things that seemed to go right in the past 24 hours as she slowly closed to mansion door behind her. With the slightest click she stopped holding her breath and slowly slid the lock over. A small shiver ran down her spine as the heated air swarmed around her body. "Phase One, complete." She thought to herself in a corny, action hero way as she slowly stepped backwards off of the welcome mat and onto the gray marble floors. Her tennis shoes didn't even as much as sneak while she peered down the hall, taking time to adjust the two empty cargo bags that hung down from her shoulders. Putting one foot out in front of the other, she shifted her weight, trying not to make a single sound as she made her way down the hall. Mouthing the same words over and over again... "Please don't be home. Please don't be home. I really don't want to deal with you. Please don't be home. Please don't be home..." You can't tell me to feel! "Oh shit..." The truth never set me free So, I did it myself... Squeezing her hand down into her pocket, she almost ripped her jeans straight off grabbing her cell phone. Silence. Silence. Please Christ make the phone silence. Answer it! Do anything! ... Success. Her heart fluttered as she looked down at the call, it was from Jupiter. The flutter sank into the bottomless pit of her stomach as she watched the timer on the answered call climb. 0:03 0:04 0:05 "Hello?" She spoke in a hush whisper, putting another Converse forward as if nothing happened at all. So far so good. Don't tempt fate. "It's about time I got a hold of you!" Jupiter exclaimed from the other end, "I'm not a pain in the ass or anything am I? I've been trying to call you for the better part of the last month, I'm just not the type to leave many messages." "No, no..." The fact she'd ignored her for this long made it that much harder to just snap the phone shut and continue on her way, but she knew that's exactly what she had to do. There was no telling when Christian would walk in the door behind her, or God forbid already be home. After Dangerous Games, she needed to grab her shit and run. "Just it's kind of a bad time, I have a lot of stuff going on..." "So I am a bother, is that what you're saying?" Jupiter's speech slurred through the speaker, making far more noise than Vivica's choked, raspy voice. "That's kinda fucked up." "Jupe, can I call you back in like an hour?" Viv said as she stopped outside of her bedroom door, "I just need to do something real quic..." "I was wondering when you'd crawl back." A man's voice cut through the silence like a slasher flick. It might as well have slit Vivica's throat. Words were no longer coming out. "Vee?" Jupiter's voice echoed through the ear drums of a stunned Valentine, "Hellooooooo?" "Yes 'Vee', don't you have anything to say? Don't tell me you're speechless all of a sudden. You sure seemed like you had a lot to say back in the arena." His footsteps clicked louder and louder as he approached her from behind. "Why don't you say them now while the camera isn't on, and you don't have any teenagers to impress with your angsty wit." "Vee? Is everything okay?" As everything started to come together at once, the red-handed Valentine could only think of one thing to say as she took a deep breath. "You even smell like a bastard." Turning her head to the left, she had no choice not but to look the devil into the eye... if only she was given the chance. Before her head made it to the self-proclaimed "Leader Of Men" she was popped in the mouth and forced to the ground. Her cell phone went skittering across the floor, a much needed conversation rendered meaningless by the taste of polished steel on her tongue. A sudden sensation to vomit rose up her throat. Her eyes opened and looked back at a man she'd met only a few times in passing. His name was never important enough to remember. Perhaps she should have paid more attention. "Please, Viv... give me one good reason why I shouldn't have Jonathan take care of what seems to be the source of every problem I ever have right now?" Chris spoke from a few yards down the hall, she assumed with that punkass smile he always wore when he had the upper hand. Biting down on the bitter steel, she couldn't even as much as move her tongue without her gag reflex kicking in. Lord knows a sudden movement like that was the last thing she needed. "All I have to do is say one word, and it's done." He continued on, "That's the type of influence I really have, Vivica. Why don't you understand that? This isn't something as unimportant as a booking decision, this is real power. This is what you insist on passing up time and time again. While I'm sure to you name dropping some shitty podunk town and getting a cheer feels like power, I can assure you it is nothing like the real thing. Because you see, power is better than sex... which you were always lousy at by the way." He walked in to her peripheral vision next to Jonathan, whose steady trigger hand showed the patience of a well-trained professional. Buzzcut, brown eyes, no tattoos, and a bit on the short side... he was just the type of man Christian always seemed to look to when he needed something done. If she was able to speak without the prospect of dying, she probably would've mentioned something about how he'd probably be better off with Jonathan in the bedroom than he ever was with her. "Christian, this is a really bad idea." A familiar voice now rung through her ears, "She just came to grab her things and leave." "Well she could've made this easier on herself." "Christian, this is really out of hand." "Is it, Jacques?" Christian turned the tables, "Don't you remember how she knocked you unconscious? You were like a father to her... she was your teenage princess, remember? You did her laundry, you did her shopping, you were her confidant for all those times that big, bad Chris was mean to her. Then how does she repay you for all the shit you've done for her over the years? She punches you right in the face... and over what, Rasa?" The expression of Vivica's face changed at the mention of the word. "You don't like that word, do you Viv?" Zenith kneeled down next to Valentine, and looked into her eyes before he ran his hand across her cheer, "Were you scared of Rasa? So much for being fearless. You might have those mouthbreathers buying into that bullshit but I know better. Look at the way you're trembling right now. Just like that scared, mouthy little girl I picked up from the streets and turned into a star. Don't you forget Vivica that I made you... and I can break you, any time that I want. You got that?" "Christian, just have Jonathan take that gun..." Jacques plead meekly. "Jacques, you don't get it. She doesn't live in real life." Christian interrupted with a verbal backhand, "She lives in a fairy tale where the real world doesn't exist and professional wrestling is real life. A world where she thinks she's in control because there are no guns on camera, everyone hangs on her every word, and everything revolves around little Miss Valentine. In the real world Viv, you aren't shit. Do you understand that? To 99.9% of the population you are NOTHING more than a pretty face and I can tell you right now with that pretty gash on your forehead you are a six a best. Wake up to reality and get it into your tiny little head that you never, EVER fuck with me. Do you understand me, Vivica?" She slowly nodded her head yes while tears ran down her cheeks. "GCW is a game. I am reality. Be gone by sunrise." His words struck like a speeding semi, "Jonathan..." Simply saying his name was enough to get the personality-deprived Jonathan to jerk the revolver out from between her pouty pink lips. He sheathed the revolver as Vivica rolled over to her stomach, dry heaving onto the floor, her body desperately trying to erase the taste of metal and shame. As she hacked and coughed, her vision blurred into shades of gray, turning the intricate marble patterns into an unfocused chaos. "Jacques, if she throws up make sure she cleans it up herself. I'm done taking care of her messes..." She put her forehead against the marble, tears falling into pools under her face on the floor. Her hands clinched into fists as she felt the vile filling up through her throat. "And one more thing, let her know the only reason she's alive is because watching her face when I destroy everything she cares about is the only thing in this world that would bring me more joy than having you clean her up off my expensive floors." ~*~ A part of me wishes that I'd ended up a bloodstain on Chris' floor that night... not only because I knew it would drive him crazy that it'd never come clean, but because I deserved it. I have spent my entire life trying to punish bastards like him, and I'd become one myself. Sometimes it's hilarious how the world works. If I could go back to a year ago, I'd slap the shit out of myself. But I can't. Instead I'm still arguably the most successful female wrestler in the world. I've held more championship records than most wrestlers have held titles. I'm at the peak of my career, some say a future hall of famer in her prime... yet here I am, living life out of the driver's seat of my Chrysler. This is exactly how this all began. No championship. No home. No friends. It was almost like I never did anything at all... ~*~ You can't tell me to feel! The truth never set me free So, I did it myself... Eyes slowly opened. Her new reality slowly came into view. You can't be too careful anymore When all that is waiting for you Won't come any closer You've got to reach a little more As Paramore blared through the small speaker, she reached out and grabbed the cell phone off he dash. Scars from the night before still covered the surface of the phone, cracks on the surface and scratches on the screen spoke of a night she was sure never to forget. Through the scratches and blurred vision, she read a name that was just seconds away from bringing another startling revelation to the forefront of her mind. Incoming: Jupiter Skye. "Shit..." was the first word to leave her mouth on that glorious morning. For whatever reason, she couldn't seem to motivate herself to do much more than stare blankly at the screen as the grim reality set in. Jupiter heard everything. Her bones cracked and popped as she attempted to sit straight up, but after as many consecutive days of beatings that she had taken, not much worked quite as she planned anymore. With another groan, Vivica placed the phone back down on the dash before falling back down on the reclined passenger seat of her car. Pain shot out from every joint she had as she stretched out the best she could, feeling as if her body had just been released from a vice. Letting her right arm fall next to the seat, she cranked back on the handle forcing the seat back upright again... and forcing herself to look at her reflection in the rear view mirror. Mascara laid like a dried river bed across the peaks and valleys of her face, running straight down into a pink and slightly bloodied limp. She pulled back on the bandage on her forehead to look at the stitches left behind courtesy of Rasa. Thread pulled tightly across black and blue flesh, even with how many times she'd had them in her life it never got any easier to look at... and she never had pulled the stitches out herself. She'd always made someone on the medical staff do it for her. Secretly she was a very squeamish person, not even able to watch the plastic surgery reality shows that seem to be populating cable television. When she stepped in-between those ropes, however, the thought never crosses her mind. Quickly she pressed the bandage back across the wound, taking the ostrich approach in that if she covers it up that it was never there. Yet the bandage didn't take the pain away. She looked like a train wreck, but she felt even worse. The snow all around her car caused her to grab the thick, ski jacket that hung on the steering wheel next to her. A grimace crossed her face as she fought through the pain and slid her arms into warm, comfortable bliss. With a flip of her wrist, she turned off the ignition and slid her keys into her jacket pocket. Her right hand grabbed hold of the door handle as her left snatched up her cell phone, the cold glass caused her to hesitate just for a moment before pushing her body out of the car. Wobbly legs struggled to support her lightweight when she slammed the door shut behind her. A strong, bold breeze numbed the aches and pains scattered all over her body as she looked in front of her at a sight that was long overdue. It was home. Or at least, it was home... and it soon would be again. Drywall had just been finished on the rebuilding project two days before, and she had been dying to see what it looked like. Truth be told she would've taken a glimpse at sunrise when she pulled into the driveway if the electricity was on. Her path to the front door brought back haunting memories of a past life. A life when she thought that everything had been figured out, her destiny would come true in the end, and she could do no wrong. The world had changed so much in a year. No longer was the press and paparazzi a burden on her personal life outside of GCW events. It was as if the world had moved on without her. The thought of doing something like sleeping in her car parked in the driveway would be insane, she'd never make it out alive. Now the morning was peaceful as she fiddled with the combination lock strapped onto her door. Once her frozen fingers lined up the numbers, a quick jerk unlocked the door and she pushed her way inside. Before she would be greeted with an illuminated trophy case, filled with awards, accolades, and various memories of her journey to the top. Now it was nothing more than an empty shelf, an empty shelf waiting to be filled with what the future brings. To her left used to be a leather sofa set and a big screen television, not it was nothing more than empty space and unpainted walls. A greeting from her housekeeper Patrick would never be heard again, either... he now lays dead and buried three miles down the road in a local cemetery. She had remembered to put roses on his grave every time she'd been in town, except this time. Vivica wouldn't leave town without doing it first. As her pink lips turned pale and her breath visibly left her body from between them, she couldn't help but wonder what was next. What would soon fill these walls again, what faces the house would bring, and what memories that were left in her life to be had. It was time to close the book on a bittersweet past and look towards the future. Her old trophy case being full doesn't mean anything if her new one was bare. She had a lot of obstacles to overcome and some fences that may never mend, but with a little hard work and a lot of sacrifice... she could be home again. A smile crossed her face as for the first time in what seemed like a lifetime, she was happy. You can't tell me to feel! The truth never set me free So, I did it myself... Incoming: Jupiter Skye As she felt the phone vibrate in her frozen hands, she knew it was time to stop running away from everything and start making things right again. With the click of a button, she was on the right path. "Hello?" Vivica answered, teeth chattering through the cold as she listened for an answer. "What in the blue hell happened last night?" Jupiter questioned immediately, "We were talking then all of a sudden... nothing. You were gone." "Nothing?" Viv stammered, "You heard nothing?" "Yeah, you didn't hang up on me did you?" "No, no..." Vivica ran her tongue across her frozen lips, "It's a long story." "Good, then maybe we'll have something to talk about over lunch... tomorrow?" Jupiter shocked Vivica with her sudden plans, "I just have something on my chest and I have to tell you in person. I don't feel right talking about it over the phone, that's why I've been kind of a bother lately." "Tomorrow?" Viv paused for a moment, trying to take everything in the best she could. It had been so long since she'd had a normal relationship of any kind with anyone. Not to mention a friendship. It had almost become foreign to her. "Yeah, there's this great diner right outside of town. I go there whenever I can." "Outside of Vegas, you mean?" Vivica questioned. "No, the other town we live in." Jupiter sarcastically replied, having no idea about the truth. A truth that didn't really hit Vivica until just that point in time. She had a lot of explaining to do as well, but the time for running had run out. They called her the Fearless Phenom once upon a time, perhaps now she could really live up to that moniker. Not just inside of the ring, but outside of it as well. "Right," Vivica responded with a bit of a smile, "I'll be there." But life is too short to spend the rest of it regretting the mistakes you've made. You can't go back in time to change the past, but you can change the future. Everything was all just a blessing is disguise. Rebuilding myself with sturdier walls and a fresh set of paint may be the best thing that's ever happened to me. I suppose there comes a time in every person's life a time when you stop believing in other people and you starting believing in yourself. I need to prove to myself that I wasn’t the one under Rasa’s mask. I need to prove I'm not Karina Wolfenden. I'm better.
