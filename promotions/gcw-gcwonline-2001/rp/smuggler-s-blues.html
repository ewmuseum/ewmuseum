Smuggler's blues

Date: Timeless Dammit, he's still on my tail. I whipped my ship down into a tight barrel roll to try and loose him. When I came out of the spin I checked my scopes. DAMN! He's still on my tail? What does it take to loose this guy? 'I'm starting to think you're more trouble than you're worth.' I said out loud, even though I was the only one in the ship. The medallion I'd...'acquired' not too long ago hung silently around my neck. Uh-oh...that wasn't good. That hit took out my rear deflector shield...one more hit and I'm toast. I thought they wanted me alive. I checked my scopes and made my way down toward the planet that was my destination in the first place. If they really did want me alive, maybe he'd hold off until I crashed on the planet to haul me and the medallion back. I may not have had my deflector shields, but I can still do some fancy flying. I dipped the ship down and twisted and corkscrewed around and managed to get behind him and aimed for his engines. Direct hit! I took one out and that hopefully would give me a few moments grace when I finally landed. I passed his almost crippled ship and headed towards the planet surface but the bastard fired one last blast as I passed and knocked out my navigation equipment. Great, now I'd have to land by sight and instinct and hope the ship was still spaceworthy when...if I got out of this. I initiated the landing sequence, but half those instruments were out too. Geez, what did he hit? I braced for impact and fortunatley the landing gear came down so the landing wasn't too bad. It was still enough to rattle my teeth and make my tailbone hurt but other than that I was uninjured, and my ship, for the most part was in once piece. I shut down everything except the diagnostic equipment and the homing beacon. Looks like my entire deflector array is out, and half the navigation array. I looked over the damage, I could repair it all in an hour...again if I got out of this. Great thing about StarSkimmers is they're a little too big to be fighters, and too small to be cargo ships. I rummaged around in the storage locker and pulled a few things I'd need. If they sent him after me, I'd need a hell of a lot more, but beggars can't be choosers, plus I can't take what I don't have. Last thing I did was strap on my blaster, I had no idea how much engergy it had left, hopefully enough to get me out of this alive. I disembarked and the hot muggy air hit me like a physical blow. I hated humid planets. Especially this one. I had landed just off to the side of a stand of trees. On the far side of them I could see signs of civilization. Well buildings at least, some of these planets in this sector of the galaxy have been abandoned for centuries. Not that I had much choice, I wasn't getting off this sweatbox of a planet anytime soon. 'Going somewhere?' Shit. I turned to face him. If you could call what he had a face. A black space where a normal human face would be possesed only two white eyes and a mouth. He was smiling. I hated it when he did that. All I could see were his eyes and teeth. 'You're a hard cyborg to track down.' 'There's a reason for that.' I said, 'I don't like to be caught, especially not by you.' The Marauder stared at me. His race wasn't much for talking, I hated that even more. 'We can do this the easy way or the hard way.' 'The hard way.' I said and closed my eyes and dropped the flashbang grenade. Then I turned and ran like my ass was on fire. Hopefully the flash would be enough to keep him off my tail for a few minutes and allow me to get away. I dove into the stand of trees and a second later a laser bolt bounced off a tree. Damn he recovers fast. Great thing about being cybernetically enhanced was I could run faster than the average human (and most other races with only two feet) and kept out of range, while firing back over my shoulder. What the? That shot didn't come from him! I glanced to the left and saw another form keeping pace with me. Eva? Since when did humans and Marauders work together? Well then again I did take the medallion from her, so I guess she was still kinda sore about that. I fired a few shots at her and she dropped back, then I fired at the Marauder again. I broke through the trees sooner than I thought and saw that I was on an inhabited planet after all. A lot of these speeders and land cruisers looked brand new. The warehouse was closed to the public, but not to a cyborg who can electronically pick locks. I ducked inside the dark showroom and waited. I heard the Marauder and Eva slip in and rolled under a land cruiser and watched their feet. They split up. Good, that would make this easier. This was going to be between me and him. I'd be able to take out Eva quickly. She was tough, even for a non-enhanced human, but I was part machine, and I was just that much better than her. I rolled out from under the cruiser and silently made my way after her. The showroom was a lot bigger than I had thought and it took a lot of false starts and jumping at shadows before I saw her. I raised my arm and turned on my auto targeting. The dart hit her right in the neck and she fell like a bag of wet laundry. It took some more snekaing around, but there's a reason Marauders are the best bounty hunters in the galaxy. They can move damn near silently. I almost tripped over the Marauder before I came face to no-face with him. We drew our blasters and aimed at the same time. 'Aw come on now.' I said, 'It's been fun, but I've got places to go, people to see and things to do. You don't fall into any of those categories.' He cocked his head at me and then pulled the trigger. Nothing happened except a tired whine. He was out of power. I smiled. 'Looks like you're not taking me in this time.' I said, flipping the switch on my blaster to stun. He looked over to my right where Eva was laid out. In the black leather straps that covered her more strategic areas she looked for all the world like she was sleeping. 'Don't worry about her,' I said, 'She's fine, just a sleep inducing neurotoxin. She'll be out for a few hours, but unhurt. And so will you.' I pulled the trigger. And got the same pitiful whine. He lunged and grabbed me, I rolled back and flipped him off of me and he rolled to his feet as I rolled to mine. The medallion popped out of my shirt as I sprung to my feet and I tucked it back in. His eyes lit up when he saw it, and he lunged at me again. I grabbed his wrist and twisted down and then up attempting to flip him onto his back, but on his way down he managed to kick me in the head. I let go and staggered backwards. I backed up into a speeder and dodged a kick. I caught him with a right to the side of the head and followed that up with a kick to the chest. He doubled over and the air went out of him and I kneed him in the face. He came up and rammed a fist into my face, and then leapt up catching me with a kick to the chest that sent me staggering back a few steps. We circled each other and he moved in. I grabbed his shirt, dropped back and kicked him over my head. He went flying and crashed into a speeder, cracking the front windshield. Hmm...so much for crack resistant glass. Guess they didn't count on two hundred pound beings being tossed into the vehicles. He rolled off the hood and tried to circle around me, but I was tiring of the game. As he moved in again I pulled my other ace in the hole. 'Ah, ah, ah.' I warned as the small blaster found it's way into my hand. The weapon was no bigger than my hand, and I had two, maybe three shots in it. If things wen't right, I wouldn't need any of them. 'You and I both know, it may not be the biggest gun ever made, but at this range I shoot you in the head, and you're dead, I don't care what species or race you are.' His eyes flicked to the gun and then back at me. His mouth turned down into a frown. 'You're not a killer.' 'No,' I said, 'But people change. Face it, today just wasn't your day. There will be another time.' 'I could close the space between us and take that from you before you could get a shot off you know.' he said 'Maybe, maybe not.' I pointed the gun at Eva. 'But I think it's time to make a choice. What's more important to you. Saving her life or bringing me in?' He glanced at the prone form of Eva. We had only moved about three feet away from her during our breif fight, still close enough for me to fry her brain if he pushed me. There was no stun setting on my mini blaster. I aimed it back at him. 'Or maybe I could kill you and take Eva with me. I know some slave traders on the fringes of the galaxy who pay top dollar for human women. I bet I could get some good money for Eva.' His frown deepened but he didn't say anything. 'Make your choice. Me or her.' He moved towards Eva slowly, I backed away but kept the blaster trained on him. Then I fired the second toxic dart from the wrist launcher that I'd brought with me. He never saw it coming. I walked over to their prone forms. You know, if you could ignore the darts sticking out of their necks, and the fact that they were laying on the floor of a speeder/land cruiser dealership, they could have been lovers sleeping together due to the way the Marauders arm fell over her back when he hit the floor. 'I couldn't kill you.' I said to him. 'I'd miss the thrill of the chase.' I tucked the mini blaster away and headed back outside, I hotwired a speeder and used the homing beacon on my ship to get back to it. I had the StarSkimmer spaceworthy in an hour, just like I'd predicted. The Marauder and Eva would be out for at least another four hours. As I left the planet behind me I knew he and I would cross paths again. After all, to catch the best smuggler in the galaxy, you send the best bounty hunter.
