What Dreams May Come

17 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-17-07 With no moon shining down, the Mississippi Delta was shrouded in various shades of darkness. It was exactly the way he liked it, and he seemed at peace as he squatted there knee deep in soggy grass in the middle of the swamp. He inhaled quietly through his mouth, his whole body tense as he listened carefully for the night's quarry. The sounds of a freight train could be heard in the distance, but it did not over power the deep and sporadic breathing of the desperate man that slowly made his way through the mud and tall grass. He knew all he had to do was to remain calm, as if he were back at home hunting deer. The thought alone brought a smile to his lips. His ears sprang to life as the sound of feet squishing through mud grew closer. ?Perfect,? the fairly familiar voice whispered to himself as he stood from his hideaway. It did not take him long to find the silhouette of a man as he ducked and weaved through the stalks of grass that still managed to slap him in the face. He lifted the rifle to his shoulder, his head tilted slightly to get a nice view of his prey. A quick lick of his lips preempted a gentle squeeze of the trigger. A silenced swoosh cut through the air, followed by a sudden thud as the victim fell to the ground. ?Fucking nigger,? the man said before he turned his head to the left and spit. ?You're aim is getting better son,? a soft Texas twinged voice called out from behind. ?Pretty soon you'll be able to knock those niggers down from a mile out.? ?I had a good teacher.? The man with the rifle replied, as the train slowly trudged along the tracks in the distance. ?Then again, there's nothing like grabbing a hold of that brillo pad and running a knife across their necks.? ?Now Jayce,? the man began, ?Why would we want to get that unholy blood all over our hands?? ?By our own hands shall those deemed unworthy fall at our feet.? The lights from the train grew closer, illuminating the darkness that once sheltered the two men's identities. Jayce Bradley stood there with a rifle in his arms, his head turned over his shoulder as he watched Russell Braun inch his way closer. ?That they shall, son. That they shall.? With a sudden jerk, Jayce's eyes grew wide. He peeled the sweat drenched sheets from his skin, as he breathed deeply and sporadically. His eyes darted around the room, as if in search of something. He was not in the middle of a swamp, instead he was laid out on a bed in some hotel room in Ohio. He shook his head as he rolled off of the bed, his feet slipped into a pair of black slippers with ease before he stood up and made his way out of the room. There was no one around, pretty much the way it always was when he was on the road. He had no family as far as he was concerned, and Mario Trellis hardly ever flew down before the day of the show. It was all press and media coverage to hype up WorldWide anyway, sprinkled with a few house shows to keep those unfortunate enough to live outside of the host city happy and buying pay per views. It was times like these however, that Jayce felt he needed Mario's words of wisdom the most. The nightmares of a past life still haunted him, and he fought hard to keep that past behind him. He refused to revert to a life filled with pain and prejudice, but sometimes his will was not enough... Sometimes the urge to feel the blood of one of Braun's unfortunate souls tempted him with it's sweet sense of satisfaction. It was an addiction far greater than the use of drugs could ever be. Jayce pulled the door of the refrigerator open, a few beers and a quart of juice for the vodka it's only contents. The juice would have to do, he thought as he plucked it from the shelf and twisted the top off. He brought the jug to his lips, the tangy nectar quenched his thirst as it barreled past his tonsils and raced down his throat. Then suddenly, the jug fell to the floor. Jayce's eyes widened as he stared intently at the wall over the bed in which he was once laid out on. Written in a thick red substance that slowly trickled down the white wall were a few words he had just heard in his dream. ?By our own hands shall those deemed unworthy fall at our feet.? He shook his head, as every breath that he took became labored and strenous. ?What have I done,? he asked himself out loud. ?What have I done?? Then the panic set in.
