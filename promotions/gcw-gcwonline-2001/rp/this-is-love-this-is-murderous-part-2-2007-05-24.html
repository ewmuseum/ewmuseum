Lip Gloss And Black Atreyu ---- Chapter Three: The Thing That Should Not Be ---- Through a pair of pale blue eyes he gazed around the room to still make sure he was alone. Although there was no one to be seen, he couldn't shake the feeling that he was being watched. It was almost like a thousand eyes gazed down upon him, observing his every movement. Like an animal in a cage. Then again, that wasn't much different than he felt almost everyday. If anything the journal he'd been flipping through had been a harsh reminder of a life he once had. A life lost a long time ago. He'd read about his final moments and the terrible passing that had occurred. Yet for some reason he still stood here today. Much less a man than a freak of nature. Reading her name on the paper, he still missed her so much. Vivica J. Valentine. The name still sent shivers down his spine. It was the only feeling of any kind he'd had in over a year. It felt good. Perhaps this is what love is. He missed her so much, but he'd had this feeling before. As much as he tried to forget the horrific memory still laid in the back of his mind. The dark little secret everyone had laying in the back of his closet, and Vivica was there to witness it all. It was no wonder she could never fall in love with a freak like him. After seeing what he'd gone through... it was no surprise that people wanted him to be burned alive. As much as he didn't want to relive it, he felt the urge to read on. Reading the words she had written down on paper through the whole process made him felt a little bit closer to Miss Valentine. Maybe if he through the world through her eyes, he could understand her that much better. Right now, being close to Vivica is all he wanted. FOURTEENTH ENTRY: As we made preparations early in the morning to take the corpse of the patient to the morgue, something completely scientifically baffling had occurred. We walked into the room to find the white body bag laying on the floor and the patient looking back at us, sitting on the bag. Still having no pulse, we can gather no further conclusions that the patient appears to be no longer human. FIFTEENTH ENTRY: We made a phone call early in the morning to alert the patient's fiance that we had made a crucial error, and her boyfriend appeared to be alive and well... but isolating himself from our medical team. By the end of the day, we had four people on our staff who no longer wanted to be in the same room as the patient. Saying nothing more than his very existence "freaks them out." SIXTEENTH ENTRY: The patient has become increasingly hostile once again, noticing the stares and comments made by some of the less professional staff members. We had two doctors quit the team and trying to replace them will be very difficult but necessary. SEVENTEENTH ENTRY: It appears as if his diet is no longer the same as it once was, and regardless of what we feed him (everything from ice cream to raw meat) doesn't seem to help. The regular foods have no effect, and the patient refuses to eat the uncooked meals due to "disgusting" tastes. As he continues to close himself off, we see no choice left but to bring his fianc? into the picture. Maybe she can get through to him. EIGHTEENTH ENTRY: Bringing the fianc? aboard the medical team as sort of a cheerleader has brought around fantastic results. His health and happiness seem to be regaining, as he eats the necessary foods to help him survive from the advice of the fianc?. Things appear to be going well, but at this point in time. We are nearly positive that the subject is at least half-vampiric. NINETEENTH ENTRY: Due to the overwhelming success of brining Amanda onto the team, we've allowed them to have private meetings for an hour every day. Success has continued. Health has regained ten-fold by the day. The project we once considered a failure has seemed to have become an overwhelming success. TWENTIETH ENTRY: The patient is now able to blend in with society as he once did, and we have allowed him to go into public under small supervision to spend more time with his fianc?. We think it's best at this point in time to not let the rest of his family know what has occurred. We fear the integrity of our project may come into question at this point. TWENTY-FIRST ENTRY: A hunger strike has began from the patient, it is a very stressful time for him and we expect it to pass. As of now it's having little effect on his overall well-being due to his companionship. The patient and his fianc? have begun planning for a very private wedding to be held at the Zenith Corporation building downtown in her apartment. We feel this project is closing in on completion, and should be able to start on a second prototype soon to see if we can fix some of the mistakes we made early on. ---- Chapter Four: The Kill ---- TWENTY-SECOND ENTRY: The patient met up with his fianc? over at her apartment earlier today to discuss the status and specifics of their up-coming wedding. I waited outside for a long period of time for the patient to come out at the designated time of 10:00pm. When he didn't come out the staff became increasingly worried. I decided to go up to the apartment to check on the patient when... something terrible happened... Date: March 8, 2006 Location: Downtown Chicago, Illinois A half moon hovered among the stars, spread across the top of the Chicago skyline. With the sunroof open, The Black Mariah reclined her driver's seat in the black Chrysler Crossfire she called her own and watched the stars go by above. Inside the noisy city, she managed to find tranquility within the sky. Peace of mind. Alone inside her car... Vivica was happy. Happier than she had been in a long time around Christian. Looking at her watch she saw it was nearing 10:30pm, and Markus was supposed to be back into the car a half an hour ago. Although she was a little worried, especially watching another light go out in a window of the apartment complex every time she glances, she pictured the good times they were having inside. Amanda was probably so happy that her fianc? was back, after being told he'd died just a few weeks before. The joy of being able to spend these precious moments with him were too much for Vivica to bare being able to break up. She couldn't help but think back to her own fianc?, and how much they'd grown apart since leaving Global Wrestling so long ago. Now in GCW, although she wasn't welcomed with open arms, with Lia Ambrosi she felt like she had found a good friend and more importantly... a family. It was something she never considered The Hunting Grounds to be. Always competing for authority, always striving to be the best. In Global Championship Wrestling, she'd already won the United States Championship and felt free to be herself again. It was almost the exact opposite of how life should work. At home, she had to pretend she was happy and put on that facade day after day. Acting like everything was fine in order to not upset the man of the house. A man who'd become so obsessed with besting his older sister that he'd ceased to live anymore. Now he just seemed to exist. The deep thoughts had almost lulled The Fearless Phenom to sleep before she realized where she was... and that Markus had not come back yet. Sitting up suddenly in a daze Vivica looked down at her watch through her cerulean blue eyes. It read 10:49, something had to be wrong. Looking up at the apartment complex there were significantly less lights on than before. As much as she'd hate to break the moment between the perfect couple, she couldn't help but feel something was wrong. Call it a hunch or women's intuition, The Aerial Artisan had to at least check. Holding her black leather bomber style jacket close to her body as she closed the door on the brisk March night, Vivica hit the alarm button on her car once letting the headlights flash with a beep as she marched towards the door. Opening the door she headed inside. Walking down the hallway, she still had to resist the urge to turn around and go back. What if she were to interrupt an intimate moment? After everything the poor man has gone through, especially at her hands, did he not deserve at least a few more minutes of time alone with the woman he loved? Yet she continued to move forward, her long blond hair waving in the breeze behind her as she jogged up the staircase. Her heart raced a bit, hoping for the best but expecting the worst. Little did she realize that worst case scenario couldn't even be imagined her in mind. Moving towards the door Vivica bit her lip a little and took a deep breath. Knock, knock, knock... Waiting for an answer The Black Mariah stood in silence, looking around the hallway. Trying to focus on just about anything to get her mind off of the million of possibilities swirling in her head... none of which could be good for her to walk in on. From inside the apartment she could've swore she heard the sound of a small animal yelping. There were no animals aloud the apartment, her mind must just be playing with her. Knock, knock, knock... This time, she put her ear up to the door just to be sure as no one answered. Inside the sound of crying resonated its way through the thick green-painted wooden door. Closing her eyes Vivica swallowed a little. Something was definitely wrong... hopefully Markus was still alive. Pushing the door open in a panic Vivica spoke. "Is something wrong in here?" Opening her eyes... she walked into a nightmare. Everything in the room was soaked in a dark shade of read. Hand prints soaked deep into the off-white colored carpet. Splatters were all over the walls as Vivica's mouth remained open. It was straight out of a horror movie. She could hardly even get herself to move. The sobbing grew louder and louder from the other room. Shaking she began to try and step around the blood-stained floors, her heart racing with fear as she grew closer. Entering the kitchen she saw the face of Markus Blair. His eyes were wide open as he rocked back and forth in the corner. Blood crusted in his long black hair as he looked down at his hands. Horrified, Vivica looked into the face of her patient as blood still trickled down from the corner of his lips. His hands were covered in red, as were his clothes. The sobs sounded almost like an infant. He was confused, scared, and alone. "Oh my God..." It was the only thing she could think of to say as she looked away from Markus and saw the naked body of Amanda laying on the far side of the room. Limp, cold, and covered in blood. Vivica's breath stopped as she began to back away from Markus. "Please don't go..." Markus' voice cracked through his weeps. "I'm sorry... I'm so sorry..." ---- Chapter Five: A Funeral Pyre ---- When most people think of the Nevada desert... they think of dry heat, a pounding bright sun, and an oasis nowhere in sight. After the sun goes down the rampant heat quickly cools, and chilled breezes sweep across the desert sands. This night was no different than any other. A desert enveloped in a blanket of cold with winds that feel as if they could slice the skin. Many would not dare venture out into night. Vivica J. Valentine was not many, she was one of a kind. With her 2006 black Chrysler Crossfire floored, she sped through the unsupervised desert road with speeds exceeding 100 miles per hour. Yet her mind sped even faster. A million things went through the mind of The Fearless Phenom as she watched the flame-licked skies ahead. Glowing in hues of orange, pink, and violet. It could only mean one thing: someone she was very close to was in a lot of trouble. The exclusive estate now owned by Lia and Virgil Ambrosi was the only thing even vaguely in the area. A thought of possibly never seeing her best friend again cycled through her mind over and over again as the smell of thick smoke and embers began to infiltrate her nostrils. There were so many things she needed to say that she never said. There were so many moments that she never got to thank the one formally known as Shadow for. A choir of lights came into sight as the flames towered above the stone gate that enclosed the home. Beacons of red, blue, yellow, and white together formed a symbol of distress. It could not end like this, not after everything they've gone through. The home came into sight as Vivica threw her sports car into a screeching halt that scared some of the nearby medics. The home appeared to be fine. This is when reality began to set in. "Not Markus..." Vivica said aloud before leaving her car, "Please not be Markus." Her eyes began to swell up with tears and her throat began to close and dry up like the desert she found herself surrounded by on the outskirts of Las Vegas. Sprinting as fast as she could, she made her way towards the medics and police officials. Who quickly intercepted her path and held her back. "I need to see what's going on!" Vivica tried to keep a calm demeanor about her, but it obviously wasn't working, "My best friends are in there! They need me!" As she was held back by a Las Vegas police officer, the sight of a guest house on the premises etched itself into her memories forever. Burning bright into the barren skies, it certainly appeared like there was no way in hell anyone would ever get out. She watched the roof collapse as water thundered down upon the flames from the hose of a fire engine. "You need to calm down ma'am" The officer said, "We're all trained professionals here." "But you don't understand!" She screamed out, tears now streaming down her face like a waterfall as she shoved the police officer aside and began to make a move towards the building. With her heart pounding, The Black Mariah threw her own well being by the wayside just a week away from her first round Warpath Match. Yet destiny had other plans as her attempt to save Markus was short-lived. Quickly she was tackled to the ground by another Las Vegas Police Officer. "He's not like most people! He's going to die!" Vivica tried to claw her way out unsuccessfully as she watched her hopes for the future burn in a pyre into the night. Inaudible screams and moans began to escape her lips as she gave up and buried her head into her heads. "It'll be okay..." One of the officers said putting his hand on her back as she made it to a sitting position on the dry, cracked earth. "Get the fuck away from me!" She screamed out. Lashing out at everyone around her was the only way she new how to react. Her usually energetic and lively cerulean blue eyes were haunted by grief and horror. Someone important to her was lost. Someone who she could even say that she loved. The part that hurt the most, was that she never could tell him the truth. Through the tears and the pain, it was the sobs of him saying he was sorry that still echoed through the back of her mind when she thought about him. She never quite forgave him for having to witness that horror. How selfish of her. Holding onto something that happened over a year ago robbed her of perhaps her one and only chance at true love. Vivica had changed so much as a person over the past year, yet would not give Markus the benefit of the doubt of doing the same. As the flames danced into the night, her tear stricken cerulean blue eyes watched true love burn away into the Nevada sky. It was a moment she was sure to never forget. It might be even more frightening than that night back in Chicago. Only this time... she was the monster. She let this happen. Maybe that's why it hurt so bad. Live, Love, Burn, Die... ---- Intermission: A Blaze Of Glory ---- I'm sick and tired of watching my life spiraling out of my control. I'm tired of waiting for opportunities to come to me, only to fail right before the final step. I'm tired of watching everyone else take what I feel is mine. My days of watching from the sidelines are over. I refuse to be the bridesmaid this time. Last time I was in the ring against Jayce Bradley... I guess you could call it sort of a fluke. The ball bounced in my favor and I walked out United States Champion. Lucky breaks aren't going to get the job done anymore. Jayce Bradley in the path to my destiny and just like everyone before him he will surely fall. Opportunities like this only come once in a lifetime, and you can't count on lady luck to guide you to victory. This may very well be my last chance... my only chance to be World Champion. Just a few weeks after watching that fire, I've already let one opportunity slip me by. Before my eyes I let someone I loved die without ever telling them how I feel. It was a painful yet necessary reminder of how short life is... and how quickly and unexpectedly it could end. I may not wake up tomorrow... I may die in that ring six months from now. I may live to be a crazy old cat lady with 12 grandkids. But the fact of the matter is... I'm dying a champion. If I'm going down, I'm going down in a blaze of glory. This championship was something that meant a lot to me. It meant even more to Markus to see me happy though. This time, I'm winning it for him. Markus, I'm sorry you never got the chance to see what I've become, but I promise I'll never forget about you. This isn't about pride. This isn't about destiny. This is about passion. This is love.
