Donuts and Questions in the morning

22 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/22/11 “Sometimes I don’t want to feel better. Sometimes I just want to feel like a monster because that is the only time I can feel like I am alive. You want to take that from me because you think taking this demon from my body will help me? I choose death.” KJ splashes some water on his face trying not to think of the nightmare that has been bothering him last couple nights and stares into a dirty mirror. During his early hunts for demons not for The Master personal gain, he encountered people who were over joyed to be freed to be normal again. But, there was one case that caused him to think some people deserve to be killed for being evil even before any demons taking over their souls. Every night before he goes to bed, he stares into the mirror trying to figure out if he is truly happy at the person who is staring back him. When the nights the person staring back him from the mirror he doesn’t like, he goes off into the night hunting for demons to set the balance of karma equal. When The Requite doesn’t have the demons to satisfy its hunger, it would begin to demanding any souls. During this time, Newfoundland doesn’t have any control of his own arm because the sword would take over his’ sword arm. When the sword senses even the smallest of corruption in the person’s soul, The Requite would extract that person soul to be purified and be reborn. The Requite has no sense of right or wrong when it comes to the moral reasoning behind the corruption in the soul. When it comes down to killing a person who did something wrong because they believed it was right at the time, the decision is up to KJ’s moral compass. KJ lifts The Requite off the wall next to the sink and keeps the blade below his waist as he walks out of the small room as he lifts his right leg over a large pile laying in the middle of the decaying hallway. He lifts the blade up into the air. The wood has a slight green glow in its crack that ran down the shaft to the handle. KJ looks over his right shoulder as he says with a frown, “I know you wanted to help your friend from being attacked on the street at night, but killing those kids doesn’t make you a savior: it makes you a murder.” He lowers the sword tip back down and sighs heavily as he looks ahead out into the busy streets in front of the abandon housing, “When they find this body, the will only think it was some druggy that overdosed and died in here. It is a shame that I had to clean an addict’s soul before he could find any help or probably told anyone that he was in trouble.” Newfoundland pulls his hood up over his head and slips out into the darkness of the downtown Philadelphia. BAM, BAM KJ jerks his’ sleepy head off the pillow after the sounds of someone slamming something at his’ apartment door. He reaches for the alarm clock sitting on top of the dresser next to the bed and the clocks reads, ‘7:45 A.M’. He just got back to the apartment only three hours ago and he wasn’t planning on doing anything until at least noon. He pulls himself out of his bed and leaves The Requite hidden underneath a large pile of clothes that he was going be washing sometime in the next day or two. KJ opens the door and the other side stands Kodiak with a white box. Gordert says with a cheesy grin as he opens the box to reveal a box full of donuts, “Donut?” Newfoundland asks as he rubs the sleep out of his eyes, “Do you want time it is, Gordert? Normal people don’t get out bed before nine in the morning and why are so damn happy?” Gordert ducks under KJ’s elbow and steps into the apartment with the donuts still somehow not crush by either his body or KJ. “I want to ask how you feeling after taking your ‘fall from grace’. From the looks of it, you’re taking it pretty while even thou you lost your debut match in GCW.” Gordert says as he takes a bite out of a powder donut and sits the box onto the table in the kitchen section of the apartment. He says as he swallows to clear his throat, “From what I gathered about your debut match, you did majority of offence, but was also the one who was pinned. You don’t have worry about being thrown into the rejected pile because I heard they have something plan for you.” KJ slowly rubs the back of his head remembering ‘The Fall from Grace’ from Micah. KJ says as he goes into the bedroom, “I have feeling you know what they have plan for me. So, why don’t you already tell me? I am not in the mood for playing games with you.” Gordert leans up against the door way of the bedroom with his arms crossed in front him, “You’re in a championship match up in Worldwide 144.” KJ was uncovering The Requite from underneath a pile of dirty clothes, but stop as Kodiak announced that he was in a championship match in his second match in GCW. He says as he turns his head toward Kodiak, “Let me guess for the hardcore championship?” “No” KJ pulls out The Requite and secures it onto his back, “Then what I am be going for then?” “You’re in a match for the World Championship.” Gordert says with slight grin as KJ walks pass him back into the kitchen. “But, there is a catch with your match and the catch is that it is going be an eight man scramble for the championship. Besides Jay Terror, there is going be seven recently new hires to prove to the company that they can play with the big boys here in GCW.” “I guess it doesn’t mean anything to them if I did lose my debut match, but I did something that caught the attention of someone who has the control of making matches. Am I surprise that I am in this match? Yes. I am going be doing my best to win this match or at least make Jay Terror be aware of someone who is up and coming in GCW.” Newfoundland says as he pulls a black leather jacket off the back of one of the wooden chairs in the kitchen. “I know you cannot be that happy for me getting a chance at the World Championship. So, what is making you giggle as a school girl?” Kody looks down at his feet before he answers, “I have some news that doesn’t deal with GCW, but it deals with the Project Bloody INC. It really depends on the person’s way of thought to see if it’s a good news or horrible news. The news is that Mr. Rivers is asking for ALL employees to come back to the company headquarters.” KJ begins to pull his arms through the jacket’s sleeves and The Requite disappears into the jacket. The only thing that would give someone a thought that something is slung over KJ’s back is that the very end of the handle is sticking out over his left shoulder. “I was never employed by Project Bloody INC., Kodiak. I was asked to help with a situation that needed both Creeker and Shredder in the same room. If I was not there, that room would have been a war zone of flying objects and probable some gun fire too.” KJ takes out a plan donut from the donut box says as he bites the donut in half, “It still doesn’t explain why you’re so happy about going back to work with that lunatic. He was reason why your childhood was so fucked up.” Without warning, Kodiak grabbed KJ’s throat before he could swallow the donut and yells, “He is the only father I know and I do anything that would make him proud of me! You will never talk like that again about my father!” KJ slowly let the mushed up donut slide out of his mouth onto Kodiak’s hand that was around his throat. Kodiak brief moment of my anger quickly dissolve as he begins to flip around with his hand sticking out, “EEW, EEW, YUCK!” KJ says as he wipes his mouth with his sleeve, “That will teach you for interrupt me when I am chewing food.” “Why would you do that, KJ?” Kodiak pleaded as he wipes his’ hand on his pants. “I thought you were civil compare to anyone else in GCW!” Newfoundland replies, “Those people cannot keep their private parts in their pants or cannot help themselves if the poison their bodies with either drugs or alcohol. So, I think I have some wiggle room when using mushed up food to get your hand off my throat.” KJ continues as he opens the apartment’s door, “I am going out to get more information about Jay Terror and I don’t care if you stay here or come with me. But, I suggest you not be–waves his right hand in a giant circle- your normal self. Because I don’t want to draw anymore attention to me then I have to.” “Ah KJ,” Kodiak puzzle says as he points toward the door, “you have company.” A young woman in her early twenties is standing in the doorway with her right hand up about to knock on the door. The woman has long red hair and bright green eyes looking for something that KJ has. In her left arm is brown packet. “Newfoundland?” “And you are?” asks KJ slowly reaching for The Requite’s handle “My name is Rox and I have some question about the death of your Father and the disappearance of your step-mother.” She says with a smug smile, “I know that the police detective that was in charge of it closed the case and ruled his death was a ‘hunting’ accident. But, that doesn’t explain why your step-mother’s body was never located.” Kodiak leans up behind KJ and whispers in his ear, “She doesn’t smell right, but she looks normal and asks like she has something to prove to someone.” When Kodiak smell something weird, it normally means there is something a demonic beast close. KJ says with slight grin, “Rox is it? How about you come down to the restaurant and have breakfast with us. Then I can answer your questions the best I can.” Hopefully, Newfoundland won’t need to use The Requite for next couple hours and can enjoy some eggs while being interrogated this morning.
