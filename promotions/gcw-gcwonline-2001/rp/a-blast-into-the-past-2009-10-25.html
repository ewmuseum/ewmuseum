A blast into the past

25 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/25/09 I have finally figured it all out. Every mystery that has plagued me since I entered GCW has been resolved, but the answers have hurt me more than their mystery ever could. But this…well I am hesitant to use the word conspiracy but nothing else fits, extends far past the day that Chris Bagwell first called me. In fact, it begins more than ten years ago, when I was around eighteen. Far from turning into a pro-wrestler I had my eyes set on very different priorities. Those priorities were, as anyone who has been that age can tell you, to fuck and drink my way through Rutgers, the State University of New Jersey….. *About ten years ago* “Remind me why we’re leaving so early,” I ask Steve as I inspect myself in the mirror. Like all freshman guys at Rutgers, Steve and I are continually searching for an easy way to get into a party on Thursday, Friday, and Saturday night. In Rutgers you either head to a house party or a frat party, both of which present different obstacles for entry. Most house parties will let the guys in for five bucks, but that adds up, especially for poor college students. Frats on the other hand only ask that there is a certain reasonable girl to guy ratio. But their definition of reasonable is about five girls to one guy, and frankly there aren’t enough girls to go around for that. My roommate Steve sighs as he gets up off his bed. His polish ancestry is apparent in his features, though for some reason people keep getting him and me mixed up. He’s a cool dude, though I fear that his obsession with the Yankees and baseball in general is going to drive me insane one of these days. It’s not that I mind when he decides to put a game on TV, but it’s just creepy when he starts talking to the TV like the players can hear him or something. “Because Scott says that we will only get in if we go this early. He knows some of the brothers and can get us in without the ratio, but only if we get there before everyone else,” he answers with a hint of irritation in his voice. “But why can’t Ben just let us in,” I complain, “he’s a brother.” Ben happens to be my next door neighbor and one of the more colorful characters in my dorm. A philosophy and political science major I quickly learned that not only was he one of the smartest guys that I knew; he was also a burgeoning alcoholic. On the second night of college itself he drunkenly came into my room at two in the morning and collapsed on Steve’s bed. Then Steve and I had to drag his drunk ass from our room to his while hoping to hell that he didn’t vomit on us. “He could probably get one of us in, but there’s five guys who wanna go and only two girls. He would have to be the President or something to get all those guys in when the party is actually going on.” I grunt my comprehension and continue to dress. While I understand the logic, I can’t help but think that I am probably breaking one of Shane’s cardinal rules by getting there so quickly. Shane is a friend of mine, as well as a self-styled expert on all matters relating to the seduction of women, and has had enough success that I actually take advice from him. A small treacherous voice whispers to me in that back of my mind that I have class at nine-fifty in the morning tomorrow, but I firmly banish it. First of all, it is general psychology, a class that is quite possibly the most useless class ever created by an institution of higher learning. Secondly, the massive hangover that I will most likely have in the morning can easily be combated by advil and sunglasses. Third, and most importantly, I presently care about trying to get laid a lot more than I do about my Friday morning class. A frown crosses my face as I stare at my reflection. Thankfully it is still warm enough that a t-shirt will suffice, but I can’t help wonder whether I should try for something more. I scowl as I realize that I’m probably once again over thinking everything, but I can’t quite seem to stop. Black jeans and a black American Eagle t-shirt seems like a good combination, but I’m just not sure. With a shrug I decide that I’ve done as best as I can with my limited fashion sense, and head out the door. Once outside the dorm itself, we meet up with Scott and head over to the house of Sigma Alpha Mu. Now the thing about Rutgers, is that it actually has 4 campuses. College Ave, Busch, Cook/Douglass and Livingston. Each one campus has its own set of pros and cons. Busch for instance is the finest dining hall in the entire University…which means the food it serves is actually edible and doesn’t play jump-rope with your digestive track. Cook/Douglass has the wide open spaces for anyone who is more outdoorsy, and doesn’t want the urban feel. Livingston…well…erm…well it exists. But College Ave is where everyone goes at night to party, essentially the social scene of Rutgers. Through sheer luck I was one of the relatively few freshmen who actually managed to get a dorm on College Ave, which meant that when I did get drunk I only had to stumble back to my room rather than get on a bus. I imagine that it can’t be pleasant to take a twenty minute bus ride in order to go look for a party. We finally arrive at the frat house and are allowed in without incident. The house itself seems spacious enough to avoid overcrowding, and they are two kegs stationed at convenient locations inside the basement. It’s obvious that we are the first guests to arrive, and I am beginning to feel a tad awkward just standing around waiting for the party to start. “How about we play beer pong to pass the time,” Steve suggests, and I nod grateful for something to do. There is already a table set up for the game, and two of the brothers eagerly take us up on our challenge. Now I’ve only played once before, and from what I remember my performance in that game was less than stellar, but I am willing to bet I’ll do a decent job this time. Carefully I line myself up center with the cups on the other side of table. I shoot the ping pong ball into a perfect arc and watch as it sails completely over the cups and lands harmlessly on the floor. I shoot a look at Steve, hoping that he will have better luck than I did. He does, he actually manages to get the ball to land on the table before it plunks down to the floor. Our opponents get a predatory look in their eyes as they pick the balls up and take aim. *An embarrassingly short time afterwards* We were fucking shut out in less than six minutes. The only reason that we don’t have to run a lap around the house in our underwear is that the fraternity forgot to list that in their house rules. But still, I either have to get tutoring in beer pong or just stop playing it. If the party had actually started during that game, I would’ve just accepted that I destroyed any chance I had of getting laid and gone home. Eh fuck it, beer pong is a silly game anyway. You’re throwing what always ends up being a dirty ping pong ball into a cup of beer which your opponent then gets to drink. If there is beer involved I want to be the one to drink it, and preferably without a dirty ball being dumped into it first! Luckily the party has just begun to get lively and I am casually chatting with two of the brothers while forcing down some of the beer that I recovered from the keg. I’m actually surprised at the amount of decent looking girls at the party; far better than I had originally estimated there would be. The brothers of the frat are perfectly nice guys but they are not…how to put this delicately… Well let’s just say that none of them are about to be models. But I suppose the girls are here more for the alcohol and music than they are for the frat boys who are trying to have sex with them (Yes I know I am also trying to get laid with one of the girls but I am not in a frat, it makes a difference). “Hey, I’m Kate,” a voice to my right says as the frat brothers walk off. I turn to look and see Kate smiling at me. She’s a fairly cute girl with freckles and brown eyes going well with her red hair. She’s wearing a black dress that somehow manages to be modest while at the same time promising that the girl that wears it is anything but. “Hi Kate, I’m Dan,” I respond warmly then falter. I have no doubt that if Shane was in my place his next sentence would get the girl in his room, and the one after that would get her naked. However, I do not have nearly his level of skill, so I go with the first thing that comes to mind. “So Kate,” I ask with as much confidence as I can muster, “do you dance?” I am well aware that anyone who has ever watched the Pickup Artist wishes to give me a hard slap on the back of the head. But in my defense it is hard to remember all the techniques I’ve picked up when I am full of alcohol. She grabs my hand and leads me to the dance floor where we begin to dance. Or rather we pretend to dance; in reality we dry hump each other and I enjoy the feel of her ass sliding along my dick as she takes the opportunity to measure just how big I am. “So have you hooked up with a lot of girls yet,” Kate asks me as we continue to move. Call me crazy, but I don’t think she is asking me out of pure curiosity. “A gentleman never tells,” I smirk as my arms tighten around her, “but I wouldn’t mind hooking up with a few more.” She turns and her lips meet my own. Without another word she leads me to the couch and proceeds to straddle me and we make out in view of the entire party. Yay for slutty girls! I’ll spare you the sordid details, but suffice to say that we found ourselves out of the house and in the woods sometime later. I offered to take her back to my dorm but she didn’t want to leave her friend, and thus we made do with what we had. “I’ll see you later,” she whispers to me even as I move to take off her dress. “What,” I answer in utter confusion. See, in my limited experience, I had made the assumption that a night of drunken sex would include…you know…sex. While I did appreciate the hand-job she gave me, I hadn’t planned on ending the night with that. “I don’t have sex with someone on the first night I meet them,” she continues with a grin, “don’t worry, I’ll probably have sex with you if we run into each other again.” And just like that she disappears. Only one though remains in my mind after the whole experience, one overwhelming statement that beautifully sums up all my emotions. You have GOT to be KIDDING me! Ah well, it’s not bad for my second college party ever. It’s probably too late to start working on another girl, but if I’m luck there will be enough alcohol left to make me nice and drunk before I stumble off to bed. After all, if I can’t achieve fornication I might as well go for inebriation. I didn’t make it to my morning class the next day. In fact I didn’t get out of bed until two, since I was too busy cowering from the intense hangover to actually leave the room before then. The only reason that I did leave the room was to attend the first practice of the professional wrestling club. A few years ago some students that hoped to become professional wrestlers started this club, and I joined it on a whim. I don’t actually have any plans to become a professional wrestler, but I figured it might be a fun way to fight the weight that I’ll probably gain from drinking every week. Halfheartedly I head down to the wrestling room after popping some advil. At this point I am ready to just deal with the beer belly and climb back into bed, but for some reason I force myself to get there anyway. I finally arrive and find seven others already there. They are a variety of sizes, but even as a freshman I’d say that I am bigger and stronger than most of them. They look at me challengingly, obviously sizing me up and trying to figure out how I might measure up when compared to them. I suppress the urge to smirk at them, confident that my years of amateur wrestling has left me more than capable of dealing with any one of them. “LINE UP,” some roars behind me, and his voice is loud and powerful enough that we all rush to do as he says. When we get in a line I finally have a chance to look at the source behind the voice, and I abruptly feel small for the first time in my life. The man, no, man-like creature is fucking huge. It honestly looks like he took steroids that were on steroids. He’s a black man with a shaved head, a goatee, and both of his ears are pierced. Basically, take Terry Crews, take away his sense of humor, make him even larger, meaner, and turn him into a badass. “So you little college bitches think that you have what it takes to become professional wrestlers. Well let me tell you, you are trying to enter what is undoubtedly the hardest profession that anyone could possibly have. None of you bastards look like you’re even close to having the raw athleticism and desire that you need, not to mention the mental toughness to take the raw, excruciating pain that I will be putting you through!” I really wasn’t expecting captain intensity to be so hardcore. I had honestly thought this club was supposed to be more of a hobby than anything, if not just an excuse to mess around. But by the sound of it this guy was actually planning on trying to get us into the profession itself! How in the blue fuck does he expect me to become a professional wrestler when I still have to figure out how I am going to pass fucking calculus? “To begin with, I am going to test whether it’s even worth wasting my time with you maggots. All you have to do today is knock me to the ground and hold me down for three seconds. And to sweeten the deal, you’re all allowed to work together. The only rule is that you can’t use anything other than your bodies, to do it. I’m ready when you are.” He’s just being cocky now. Sure, he may be bigger than all of us, but even he can’t hope to take on eight guys at once. I turn to them, in order to strategize a plan, but as it turns out I am dealing with a bunch of morons. They instantly rush at him, intending to take him down through the power of a mob, no doubt thinking that there were enough of us that a plan was unnecessary. The biggest student reaches our target first, and within a second he is lying down on the floor only semi conscious. It happened so fast that I could barely see it, it looked like our instructor just reached out and slapped the kid on the side of the head, which was enough to knock him out. I don’t know if the others are just too stupid to be intimidated by this, or if they just can’t stop in time but they continue their charge. One of them actually manages to grab onto the man’s arm before he casually throws the poor fool into the wall. Another student, who I assume used to play football, actually tries to tackle our teacher down. It’s easy for the man to side-step the tackle, and the kid’s body impacts quite nicely with the wall as well. The last five of them finally wise up and try to take him down together, but at that point the man decids to become aggressive. He slams two of their heads together cartoon style, and he throws two others right into the ground. But he has to turn around to deal with the last one, and that was the moment that I have been waiting for. The second his back is turned I run full speed and throw my entire body into the back of his knees. He collapses on top of me, and he is so heavy that I nearly suffocate under his sheer weight. But mindful of the requirement that I keep him down for three seconds I wrap my hands around his head and start digging my fingers into his eyes. It is easy for him to push my hands away, but he loses precious time in doing so. When he stands I am certain that I was able to keep him down for as long as he required. He stares at me for a second, and I admit that the look he gives me scares me enough that I almost piss in my pants. He looks truly angry, and I am afraid I may have crossed some sort of line even though I didn’t break any of the rules that he had set out in the beginning. But then he breaks the tension with a surprisingly warm laugh that reverberates throughout the entire room, and I breathe in a sigh of relief. “Alright kid, I admit that what you did worked. It was dirty, and if this was a real fight I would have kicked your ass right after, but it wasn’t bad for a first try. What’s your name anyway?” “Dan Black,” I say somewhat breathlessly still trying to recover from being crushed underneath the weight of his bulk. “The only rule you put down was that we couldn’t use any weapons or anything, I figured that meant the eyes were fair game.” He smirks, clearly pleased by my answers. “That is exactly the kind of attitude you’re going to need kid. My name’s Bill, and by the time I am through with you I am going to have you ready to take on anyone wrestler in the game.” “To be honest,” I say hesitantly in the face of his enthusiasm, “I figured I was going to do this as a hobby. I wasn’t really looking to make a career out of this.” Bill laughs again, “Kid, you wouldn’t believe how many wrestlers started out that way.” I fear that if I end the story now, I’ll be criticized for not showing some sort of connection between the past and present. Some might even say that this story was an excuse for me to reminisce about my college years, and holds no bearing on my current situation. But I would like to offer two interesting tidbits that could change that perception dramatically. Just a short time ago, I’ve learned that some of the enemies I’ve made since I joined GCW knew me back in these wild college years, though I wish to hell I knew who they truly were back then. Perhaps you might remember Principal Wansac, the woman responsible for drugging me, and putting me under her control. No, I did not meet her during my time in Rutgers, but as it turns out Kate was not entirely truthful with me. You see Kate’s real name was Alice, as in Wansac’s subordinate that helped set me up. Oh, and Bill, the guy who seems set to be my mentor, isn’t as innocent as you believe either. You might remember Greg, the man who I battled for months as I tried to rescue the son of the woman I loved from his custody. Well, he was given a bodyguard by his father when he turned 18, due to some dangerous business decisions his father had named. That bodyguard’s name was Bill. Now isn’t that interesting?
