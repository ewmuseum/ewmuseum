We Act Affirmatively!

29 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/29/09 “Oww!” never the first thing you want to say in the morning when rolling out of bed. Roland’s hand crept up to his face. His cheek felt? puffy. He darted across the floor still in his black with red pinstripes suit from the day before. He reached the mirror to find the entire left side of his face four times its normal size. ?HOLY SHIT!” The foundation on the house shook slightly, but everyone inside woke up. June Bug was the first to reach Roland’s room. He burst through the door turning it into splinters, “What da fuck dawg”!” He gasped wanting to laugh instead he choked it back. Roland looked his homeboy in the eyes, “Someone broke in and fucked me up! It was that hatin” ass Bagwell!” “Yo, you know you just got his name right?” June Bug questioned as the others arrived simultaneously, “Who dat?” Yo Roland someone dun fucked you up!” “I KNOW! Where da fuck was yall at! MAN!” he cried out pressing on his swollen mouth, “I can’t believe yall let em do dis to me! And in my own home!” “Yo man, technically dis my Grandmamma house dawg.” CJ added in. “I LIVE HERE TOO!?he yelled back. “Yall just let em waltz up in here, drug me up and bust all up in my shit?” “Let em?” questioned June Bug, “Nigga we don’t let anybody up in dis bitch!” “Yeah!” added Big O finally chiming in. “So if yall got drugged too, why dey didn’t fuck yall niggas up too”!” Roland angrily snapped, “See no answa, and you know why! Cause yall niggas sold me out!” “Shut dat shit up,” CJ demanded reaching for the side of Roland’s swollen face Roland dove back behind the door on the floor. “DON’T HIT ME NO MORE!” June Bug shook his head disgustedly, “Nigga if you don’t get up I’mma kick you in the head!” “Sides nigga if it was anybody fucked you up it’d be O,” CJ laughed, “an you bitchin” bout the wrong end of the body for dat nigga.” “Yea?” Big O caught himself, “Man fuck you!” “Dis shit ain’t cool man, I know who got me” it was Cracka and Uncle Cracka! Dem niggas drugged all of our Raspberry Tea?” Roland said climbing back to his feet with his back firmly against the wall, “Someone tole” dem niggas about our Raspberry Tea addiction and dey got us? err me!” “You is one of us nigga!” CJ exclaimed seeming to be getting worked up. “Bet, so dem crackas think dey can just pop up in our spot and fuck me up huh.” CJ slipped in, “Us.” “Us nigga! Is you lookin like Bagwell’s momma up in dis bitch! Huh, HUH, Huh” Is you?!?demanded Roland looking each member of Affirmative Action up and down. “I don’t think so!” “Yo homie,” June Bug tapped on Big O’s chest, “Get the Mobile Unit geared up, we gots to get dis nigga to the emergency room.” “Aww hell no!” Roland yelled trying to cut through June Bug and CJ only to be trapped. “Come on my brothas” not the emergency room! A nigga can go in there with a ingrown toenail and he ain’t neva comin? out dat bitch!” “It ain’t gon” be all like dat, we’ll be wit you.” CJ tried to assure. Roland’s eyes got big, “Yall niggas was here last night too, SOMEONE CALL MY MOMS” TELL HER I LOVE HER!” He struggled mightily against the Slaughters, to no avail. After getting him through the house they easily tossed him into the back set with each Slaughter taking the window seats forcing Washington to sit bitch. “Come on niggas! We posed to be boys?” Roland said choking back the tears, “Yall niggas playin” me dirty like dis man...” “We just gotta make sure your jaw ain’t bouts to fall off an shit.” CJ wasn’t being very helpful. “Come on nigga, yall got me all cramped up in here man” yall could at least let a nigga sit in his seat!” Roland barked, “ain’t nobody being nobody’s prison bitch ova here!” “Calm the fuck down!” June Bug assertively stated, “You know last time yo punk ass gotta damned splinter you jumped out the car door two blocks befo” we gotchure ass to da hospital so sit still an shut da fuck up!” “Yeah!” Big O topped it off. “Man fuck you niggas!” Roland huffed sinking into the bitch seat. Outside of the ever thumping stereo the rest of the ride was quiet. They pulled into the hospital emergency room entrance, June Bug popped out holding Roland’s arm firmly in his hand. CJ ran around the Yukon and snatched the other arm pulling their manager into the automatic doors of Mercy General. Big O screeched the wheels trying to make the close parking space but was cut off by a Mercedes. June Big and CJ ushered their manager up to the admittance desk where a large white woman sat. She never looked up from her computer just simply and calmly stated, “Please take a clipboard, fill out all information on all the pages and bring it back up.” “Paperwork!” CJ was offended, “do you see my homeboy’s face bitch! Fix dat nigga now!” “You have to fill out these forms before we can treat you.” She replied glancing up from her computer screen, “HOLY MOTHER OF GOD YOUR FACE!” “Is it bad”!” Roland questioned now more terrified than ever. “Nothing a Dentist can’t fix,” she said calmly returning to her computer screen. “A Dentist?” he questioned. “They’re the ones who work on abscessed teeth,” she answered then added, “That’ll be seven hundred dollars please.” “Seven hundred what”! You must be OUTSIDE yo damn mind!” June Bug snapped. “Man you besta fuck and suck him for seven hundred!” CJ added. “Nah niggas, I gots dis,” Roland assured reaching into his pocket he pulled out a card, “Just call dem cats, dey’ll get with you on payment.” The nurse took the card and looked over it. “Global Championship Wrestling, Owner Steven Caldera,***-***-****? When she looked back up Affirmative Action was already a memory. In the back of the AAMU June Bug and CJ sat with Big O driving and Roland now in the passenger seat. Roland had his i-phone out and was trying to call Global Championship Wrestling’s parent company, Caldera Enterprises. “Yes, this is Ro-land” Rah, Oh, El, Hey, En, Dee! Washington.. Dubya?” Roland paused, “Oh so you know who dis is, well look I need to talk to Cal-Der-RAH right now” dis pay per view? yeah Shockwave? he’s gonna have to postpone it!” Over the loud music banging, over CJ and June Bug wrestling in the back seat and Big O shining up his bright bald head while driving you could hear the secretary laughing. “Cancel” wait no postpone Shockwave? that’s rich!” “Listen trick, dem Holy Hell crackas busted in my house last night and abscessed my tooth!” Roland pleaded, “My shit’s all swollen up” he gonna have to give a nigga a week or so to get right ya feel me?” The boisterous, downright jovial laughter of the receptionist left Roland sitting there feeling less than happy about the current situation. He touched his face pulling back in a wince. “Listen trick unless Cal-der-RAH wants to see his little parking lot filled up with a bunch of fresh out the pen niggas just looking for a pretty white boy to call dere own HE BESTA CALL OFF SHOCKWAVE” B-I-ATCH!” “Hello?” Roland questioned, “Hello?” Realizing he’d been cut off from the lady he snatched the phone up, using his index finger he pilfered through the listing and once again tried to call Caldera Enterprises. The phone rang, then it rang again when someone finally picked up the other end Roland poked his chest out and let them have it? “I want to speak to Steven Cal-Der-RAH right now!” “Hold please,” answered one of the receptionists at the main desk. After a few moments Caldera’s personal assistant once again answered the phone. “Steven Caldera’s office.” “Hello! HELLO! Hello,” Roland’s voice cracked when he was stressed, “Now listen to me sista! This is Roland Washington” Rrah, Ohh, Elll?” “Mister Washington,” she sternly cut him off, “Listen sir as it pertains to Shockwave, the venue is booked. There have been thousand of tickets sold, and frankly even if those things weren’t all in place you’re still not important enough to stall the production of the show.” “THIS IS A VIOLATION OF MY CIVIL RIGHTS!” Roland replied flabbergasted, “I’LL GET THE NAACP! THE NCAA! I’LL GET BERMAN AND ESPN!” Again Roland came to the harsh realization he was talking to a dial-tone, “That’s right missy” you just march your fancy panties back there and tell Cal-Der-RAH that Affirmative Action will be at Shockwave since he has soo much riding on us.” “You gots the tone again huh?” questioned CJ. Roland hung his head and replied, “Yeah, stoopid biatch.” “Well dere’s only one thing to do,” June Bug observed, “We gots to get even wit dose Holy Hell crackas.” Roland started rubbing his hands over each other mimicking Dr. Evil, “Ooh we gon” get even? please believe we gonna get even!”
