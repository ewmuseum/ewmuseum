Staple by Staple

10 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 10, 2005 (Staple by staple, the pain got more intense. The feel of the staple piercing my body was really not to my liking. I couldn’t believe what he was doing to me. Why is it me he wants to see suffer so much. Does he crave my failure, or does he strive my success? As I lay face down, I knew these staples would hurt coming out as much as they felt going it. As they removed the staples, each staple removed built up more aggression and more fire burning in the pit of my well-known being for me craving my redemption. Only he would have to wait and find out down the road.) “Arrrghhhh!” (With the removal of the final flesh puncture I flipped over and sat up. The double sets of holes bled slowly as tears filled my eyes in anguish. I grinded my teeth together in utter hatred and eased the grinding teeth into a sickening smile. I knew payback would be fun, but I had a whole week or two to think of the possibilities.) “Renegade’you really do have a way about yourself. You’re predictable. I mean I knew you couldn’t let me just enjoy myself for a few minutes after my match, NO. You have to come and add insult to injury. This is the second time now you’ve tried embarrassing my name’This is the second asterisk placed next to your name on the black list. At least you had almost enough decency to await the outcome of this match.” (I hold the sign that soaks with my blood firmly in my grasp and read it out loud.) “Mike Hardy’Future World Champ!” (My face grows with gratitude for this is the first I have seen of this poster to which was attached to me moments ago.) “Rollins, don’t you see it. Read this message again. Mike Hardy’Future World Champ’if you could have only depicted the message in there maybe you would have realized that everything you have put me through wasn’t really necessary. Future is the keyword Renegade. Future means somewhere down the road. Now because of your jealousy to my success, the road grows longer and darker. Nightfall is hitting on this road I travel. Most would call it quits, most would find a place to sleep for the night. But not me, no way. I see much better in the dark. I will never call it quits on this road. I know where I am going I know where this road leads to. I say it time and time again that I will be GCW’s Heavyweight Champion, I will be a part of history in that belts name. No washed out has been is going to rid me of my dream, my FUTURE!” (I slowly place the sign down next to me as my eyes continue to scan the words. This could be the motivation I was looking for. This could be the push I’ve always needed. Maybe this is my final lesson in my teachings but to be taught from someone else’s textbook. I now know what I need to do.) “Renegade’You damn well know that this is merely the beginning. Soon enough you will feel my presence lingering nearby with every step you attempt. I will be watching you, studying you. You’ve inspired me Rich. You’ve taught me my final lesson. That lesson being, (My voice in a frantic outburst) TAKE SHIT FROM NOBODY! ROLLINS”.YOU BETTER FUCKING PREPARE FOR THE WORST!”
