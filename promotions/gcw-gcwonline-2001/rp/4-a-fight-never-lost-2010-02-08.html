4. A Fight Never Lost

8 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/08/10 The sound of deep breathing at a steady rhythm fills the room. Steady breathing and then a shudder, and then the breathing increases. It is erratic and painful to listen to.. ...good thing he is one only one in the room... Ughhhhhhhhhhhhh I can't believe that *wince* I've come down to this. Man, if I could only go back a couple months when I was on top of the *wince* world... FUCK! Little fuckin' monkey broke my ribs, and for what? To look better in front of his fucking mooch of a bitch? Half speed. Half speed and no real contact. Last time I let a fuckin' up-and-commer lay down the sparing rules. Next time, next time I'M GOING TO FUCK- *wince* FUCK! Never again, man. Never again...I never want to crawl out of my own bed on hands and knees, FUCKIN' HANDS AND KNEES, for some chump. Crawling over on my hands and knees to the bathroom because I can't even support my damn self to stand. I guess I rather crawl than lay down, at least, that's what my father would say. "Never lay down for anyone. You lay down, you give up. After that, everyone will walk all over you." I'm not so sure what's worse, waking up and not being able to look yourself in the mirror, or waking up and physically being incapable of getting to the fucking mirror. Shit. I don't see an MMA fighter in the mirror anyone. You know what I see, staring back at me in the mirror? I see a fuckin' product of selling out. Nobody wanted me to cross over, but I thought I was following my heart. Following my heart... That is a bunch of bullshit they feed you when you are younger. Your heart is a beating drum being hit by a slave of the gods for giving the human race fire or some shit. Slaves only follow one thing, commands. Commands are given by your brain and your balls. I followed my balls on this one. GCW was a product of too much blood flowing out of my brain and into my pathetic American ball sack. I wanted the fame, I wanted the attention. Your fairy tale heart would never lead you down that path... GCW is anything but a fairy tale... I came into GCW on top of my game. I came in with everything on the table. I was in the best shape of my life, I did everything right. President Caldera thought I was going to be the next big thing. I lost it, I lost it bad. I came in with several wins, then I missed out on my opportunity for the Television Belt. American Dream? Maybe if I didn't go MIA I could have made it there. I lost it all though, I lost it when my brain started to take over. I wanted to be part of one of the best WEC cards in history, but with Dangerous Games and NC-17 all around, I had to choose. What did I do? I tried to do both. I doubled on training, doubled on matches, and worst of all, I doubled up on my pain medications. I never thought that I would be standing here, looking at myself in the mirror, evaluating myself and what I'm doing with my life. The last couple months have been crazy. I tried to do it all, and it all went up in flames. I won at Christmas Chaos but then I was faced with a decision. He said to me, "Enough of this wrestling shit. I'll give you a deal that you can't pass up." 'A deal I can't pass up.' I opened my ears and evaluated my situation. I was on top of GCW, possibly able to run for a title, but then, he had to continue... "Kris, I know insider information. They aren't going to give you a title shot. No, son, they want to run this gimmick into the ground. They want to run YOU into the ground. I have a deal. You come to me and train my fighters and I will double what you are making in GCW." The Wise Man knows was strings to pull to make you go one way or another. He pulled the right string at the right time. I went back to training, but I never expected to fall away from GCW like I did. I was training chumps, and I had to bend over and let them take me out for them to get the fights they needed. I was laughed at and criticized. There wasn't anything in the world I wanted more than to be unleashed. I'M THE FUCKIN' *wince* I'm the fuckin' executioner. I wanted to do my own thing. I wanted to be able to turn around and do what I've been trained to do. No, I was told, one more fall in sparring and then I would be out of my contract again. I took that last fall last week, and my ribs are still reminding me... But that's all over. Yeah, its back in my hands. I might be fighting hurt. I might be coming back into GCW cold, but I am coming back in. There's no way that I won't succeed this time... ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ Have you ever walked down an isle or hallway to meet someone in the middle of a ring or venue with all eyes on you? Not because you stand out. Not because you are announcing. Not because you are carrying something.... Because you are there to entertain. Because you are there to give them a show. Something they can't do. Something they only dream of doing... They live through me. They live through me every time I step into that ring. Whether it be GCW, WEC, or UFC. Choose the acronym and I am their vessel, their vehicle to live the life they want to live for however many rounds it lasts. For this reason, I will never be able to leave fighting. I have a series of fights coming up in the WEC and GCW. Well, the GCW if they let have me. I have to prove myself again at the next World Wide. But tonight, I have to square of for the WEC one more time before that chance. I know what you are thinking. Why even bother doing back to back fights. If this one doesn't go the way it should, well, there is no reason to go back to GCW and fight hurt. All that said, I have one response... I'm a Mixed Martial Arts fighter. We are resilient. We are strong. And we don't tap out unless we absolutely have to... Tonight I'm up against Sean Smith from Birmingham, Alabama. There is no reason why this shouldn't be a cake walk. Then again, this is MMA and anything can happen... <DING> HERE WE GO! Ok, touch gloves and take three steps back. Yeah, I see you favoring your right foot. Ah, he caught me looking and now he's standing in good form, but the twitch of his right eye shows he's in pain. This is what a good fighter looks for right from the start; a weakness. Jab, jab, jab. Alright test the distance Sean. Your reach is a little bit better than mine. Jab, jab, jab. Throw one more quick combo and you're mine. Jab, jab.. THWAK Holy hit Batman! Sean stumbles back from a solid straight from yours truly. Sean is quick to recover, but I am still pressing. One, two, three, side step and jab. One, two, three, reverse step and jab. What many don't realize is that fighting is much like a well planned dance, but as in all dancing, there are anomalies that you must account for. one, two, jab, jab, inside kick to the right leg. Sean backs up hard against the cage. That inside kick didn't land where I wanted it to, but he knows that I know what target I should be going for. In what seems like a minute long stare down, Sean motions his eyes over to the right and I follow. Using the cage, Sean kicks off of it and comes through the air with a high flying superman punch. I put up my hands with all haste, but one of those anomalies come into play. He isn't focused on the punch, he comes in with a flying knee. Knees are so solid when bent that it can knock you out, split you open, and it won't even have the decency of paying for dinner. His knee hits my jaw and it takes all my will power to keep my eyes open and stay conscious. I hit the mat and I see a blur of the Alabama Slamma come through the air to drop down onto me. I bring my hands up to protect my face as I feel stream of hot crimson rolling down my chin and onto my chest. <bang bang bang> Ten seconds left in the round. I need to hold on, but...I...won't...stay...on...my...back... I get up to a knee with the support of the cage next to me and take the abuse that Sean delivers for the next three seconds when the ref comes running in to break Sean away from me. The crowd goes absolutely wild, but all I can hear in my head is my father... "Kris, if you lay on your back for another second without progress, I'm pulling you from the fight myself!" The worst part of it all, he said that over ten years ago in one of my first fights, and even though he's been dead for years, he is just as active of a coach as he was back then. "I'm good!" I know the ref is doing his job, but come on. Don't give me the fuckin' sympathy shit. The second round is going to be my round. I am going to knock him out or make him tap. "This guy hits hard, you need to remember your blocking patterns. Remember start high and fall low. I don't want to see another fucking knee connect, do you hear me?" "Just clean off my mouthguard..." You fucking fat Italian fuck! I wish I had my old corner, but most of my crew left when I went to GCW. A minor inconvenience. I'm still the fighter, it doesn't matter what they do... <Ding> Alright, no glove pound this time. Sean is gloating over there. Just look at him. He thinks he is on top of the world. A guy with a record of 4-0 against me. Where am I now? 25-3 professionally? Alright Sean, let's see if you can handle me... Jab, step step, Jab, step step, inside right leg kick, step step, jab... It is much like axing down a tree. If you pace yourself the same way, you won't tire before taking the tree down. Jab, Jab, Jab, inside leg kick. He's protecting himself now, but he just told me how to win. Let me get you inside a fighter's head for a minute. If you see a weakness, you can't just go for the weakness because the other fighter who is hurt with go to desperate measures to protect himself. This can either work in your favor or against you. If you let it work against you, you will find yourself kicking out his knee while he is coming down with a solid kick or punch in your jaw, knocking your ass out. If you use it to your advantage, pace yourself, and his blocking and his form will falter. I am persistent.. ...but I'm also unpredictable... That's right Sean, cover your leg, switch stances so your power hand is no longer in front. Back up, go towards the cage... Jab, Jab, step, uppercut... Damn, I missed the uppercut and...jab, jab... Sean delivers two jabs to my jab, reopening the cut on my chin. Sean isn't a bad fighter, and he's not a stupid one either... He knows when to attack, but he also knows my ground game is far better than my stand up... Back up just a little more, perfect... Jab, jab, jab, strong inside leg kick right were I wanted. Sean, as predicted turns away from the cage and gets on the other side of me, pinning me against the cage. <thirty seconds left> Plant the foot. Sean gets in closer, thinking he overtook me. I throw a single straight to back him up just enough and then... ...You tell me GCW has only destroyed my career... I kick off as hard as I can and come up high on Sean. He covers up his face and slightly ducks his body, only to make it that much easier for me. I come down and latch on my signature guillotine and hit the mat hard. Sean lets go of all of his breath which scares the shit out of him and begins to instantly tap. <Ding Ding Ding> I can't tell you what was said or done for the next ten minutes, where I stand right now, in my locker room happier than a pig in shit while a doctor tends to the slightly streaming cut on my chin. I dismissed my corner from my locker room and give myself time to relax. Relax until my door streams open with flashes and a loud moan from reporters outside, and then once again, silence. "You know, kid, If I didn't know any better, I would think you were a God damn magician." I look up in shock at the voice before me. I can't believe it, but it is my cousin, Pat Michaels, or more commonly referred to as Patillac Michaels. Pat is a bodybuilder, police officer, and personal trainer. Pat is also a prick and usually only comes around when he sees an opportunity for business, and by business I mean personal gains. "Well, if it isn't Patillac Michaels! Thought I smelt fake tan and protein." Pat smiles a crooked smile and sits down on a folding chair that groans against his mass. "I think you will want to hear me out, and I promise to make it short." Not likely... "What could you possibly want.." "I found you two new trainers to get your ass back in gear, and they are the best at what they do..." Why would he possibly want to find me...Who does he think...Why would he...How can he... "And why would I want your friends..." "I will do your weight training and diet..." Wha...wha...what? "...and Smitty will do your fight training..." His smile widens and I nearly fall back off my seat at the offer. "..S-S-S-S-Smitty wants to train me? "Yeah, and that's not all. I saw someone outside that wanted to see you..." Patillac motions to the door and he yells "COME IN". I hate how he takes over my locker room. The door opens, with the same moans and flashes of light as a beautiful blonde walks through the door in an amazing green fitted dress. "...Hi Kris..." "I..I...I thought you..." "No, I couldn't..." "But, you..." "I couldn't marry him..."
