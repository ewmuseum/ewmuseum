Date: 8/20/07 "Why aren’t you telling these cats how many times you had to suck off Satan to get that win?" Kid doesn't take it well’must not see just how much of that timeline-breaking, logic-eschewing, head-crushing magic had to've been involved for this one’and plays it down; tucks his chin to his chest and lets his eyes wander. He laughs anyways, leans into his drink and smirks. "How 'bout you ask your mom?" And the group laughs too, and I just roll with it. Fuck if it makes sense; this dipshit is alive’alive like whoa’and it's infectious. Then again, fuck disease. Been here long enough; time's come to step outside for a quiet little smoke break at the very least. Cities roar. Relatively quiet, then. Cut away from the group when no one's really looking, elbow and shoulder and wade through flesh until there's something like a cool night breeze hitting. Don't think I actually owe anyone anything. Who knows. Probably should get out of here. I’ve got better places to be than the background to someone else’s night. Door swings open: what's peace anyways? Kid's laughing and yelling at the roar behind; drops down past silence when the doors fall shut. Limps over’or, not limps, just walks with that left leg between straight and stiff like it's part of the everyday swaggerstep’shaking his head and adjusting his aviators with his drink hand. "Ol' man, got a minute?" Give the kid a nod, meet him almost halfway’not too far, though. Might think you've started trusting him, Sage. "What's up?" "Don't wanna alarm you or anythin', but this shit's about to kill my ass an', like, I guess you'd wanna know somethin' like that." "What; parties, drugs, sex and loud noise? Could, I mean’fuck knows they've killed better." "Not even; fuckin' all this press bullshit the last couple of days. Last time I slept was on an airplane, dude." "Then you avoided jet lag. What's the problem?" "All I'm askin' is just, tonight, to go crash the fuck out and get my head righ'." "What kind of asshole cuts out on his own party?" "Kinda asshole ain't slept since he got back in America." "I guess. Fuck, you know? Sounds like a good idea. You've gotta be in wardrobe at 9." "In the mornin'?" "No." Wait. "Unless the morning's the thing that comes before that commercial bit you’ve got at noon. O'clock.." "That's fucked up." "No, that's pretty much just the world's business schedule. In your position, I'd geek the fuck out and show up with a nosebleed." "You suggestin'?" "I will fucking gut you if you try that sort of shit. Already made it pretty clear that you're not the same Jordan White I was’let's try to keep you profitable, y'know?" "Talk some shit, man; talk alla that shit. I beat motherfuckin' Rollins in the Iron Man and my ass is gettin' to go to sleep. You ain't got shit on that, man." "I don't have to wrestle." "Lucky asshole. S'all good though, I am gettin' the shit outta here." "Alright then. I'll give you a call a few hours before you've gotta be ready." Something like "word." Tosses a hand up over his shoulder on the way to the limo; lets himself in and disappears with the rest of the Caddy. Kid doesn't even know why he's gotta stay focused’hell, I'm only running on the kind of vague memories that just don't sound all that believable. Shit about dark things that shouldn't, that can't exist in this world. Me thinking stuff like "he knows who he's going up against longterm; not what" without getting up the gusto to buy into it myself. Crazy fuckin' drugs. But, really? Lightyears beyond that sort of shit now; it's not about saving GCW’from those that'd use it as their own little diversionary magic hut’or the universe’from being torn in half in those cats' confusion’or even bolstering a little respect in the Establishment. Nothing personal, reality, but nothing feels very personal to me these days’least not my tired old shit about truth, or right, or even base ideas of That Which Should Not Be. And sure, I know things’or I think I know things, and that's just as good’but it's not like a sparkle of understanding means I have to all of a sudden act all responsible about the shit. I just want money. Business collides with pleasure at an intersection? Copasetic. If the kid steals a title away from something I don't like for reasons I can't really vocalize? Excellent; I'm not going out of my way for shit anymore. Good and bad get to shift into nothing but ugly little abstractions en route to that almighty one eyed dollar. It's the end of the day and I just can't say I feel bad for Twizz. Can't say I feel much of anything for any of them. They're only doing their jobs, and those jobs can't be but so important. We're, none of us, inspirational. Just junkies and madmen locked into soap operas, just musclebound geeks making white men richer; just assholes in tights and assholes in suits. Crazy fucking kid. He loves his job; you just know it. He feeds off it, thrives off it, rides it like the superneon gravy train it is. Easy to get emotional about money, yeah, and just as easy to get sucked in when it's on the line in copious, copious piles. And these days, wonder if Rollins is on the same payroll I am. Always liked the guy’but kid knowing I liked Twizz did sort of damn him to being unable to beat Twizz, so, well. Things that don't matter much’respect, loyalty’just stay quiet from now on. Yeah, yeah. That's a good call. So, well. Fuck. This is gonna keep bugging me when really what's it even matter? Renegade might've lost; Renegade might've finally accepted one of Caldera’s briefcases bursting open with hundred dollar bills. The kid's got cred; at least more of it. No reason to assume it, anyways. Just trying to justify the old heroes dying, I guess. Things that used to have a certain sacred light to them come and go three times a night (four if you keep your eyes open) and it all just fades into the same dull drone after long enough and all there is to do is to strap on the Rolex and those genuine leather loafers and motherfucking adapt. Or die. And I really don't want that. So, it's kill your idols now, is it? or stand idly by while their ghosts get thrashed and fade like smoke, at least. That's the price of surviving long enough to really make good on all of this?, then that's the price I pay. Guess it doesn't really matter; cocksucker Rollins did cause Trey and Jordan to lose that US title. Least I get a hollow victory to go alongside the truck full of cash. Yeah, that's it. Fuck him. So few real people left and I get to help demolish them all. Fuck 'em. And so hundreds of grands go to personal trainers and insurance companies and P.R. machines and lawyers and devils and all the gears keep grinding along. The price I pay to be Sage Black needs its own accounting team. "You pasty sonofabitch." Oh, Sobias found me. "Unsociable prick. What's goin' on?" "Your little cokehead left his own party early." "He's not a cokehead." "Oh. And I thought that's why he wasn't on camera more often." "Well. Alright, yeah, but. Other than that. The fuck are you doin'?" "Me? I'm just showing my allegiance to the man who defaults to the new king of festivities." "Answer the goddamn question." "Hngh. Since you apparently don't think there's time for pleasantries’a very prick move, mind you’I feel, perhaps, a little wary of letting you know about the event you're just a couple miles off from." "What, Van Halen?" "It has nothing to do with cockrock." "Hey." "Can I talk?" If I just nod, he'll get the point. And he'll appreciate me letting him talk in silence. Hm. "Yeah. Go ahead." "A." "But abbreviate it as much as possible. You're kinda, you're longwinded, Torm’nothing personal." "Be cool, Sage. Christ, just be cool, alright?" Nod. "Alright, then. One of my, ahhm, clients is a very powerful figure in publishing. His daughter, as it were, is having a very elite get together for, well, very elite people." "So I buy my way into some whore's party? Come on. I've done that enough tonight." "None of that horseshit. As a close friend of hers, I could easily stand to bring a couple of guests’were they of a certain social standing, that is." "You're sayin' you're gonna drag me into one of those coke fueled orgies for the upper class?" "You've been watching Sexy Beast too much." Shrug. He's got a point. "Okay, you're saying that you're gonna drag me into an entirely separate social circle’like, the kind whose parents run the world?" "Now you're catching on." "Mother fucker. Why didn't you just say that to begin with?" "Can't just go easy on you." "So I call the kid up and we all three head over so as to make friendly?" "It’s amazing how well you can fake thinking. I’d almost believe it came naturally." Fuck. I've gotta light this smoke before it's gonna be useful. First, first the phone. Sobias stands his ground, staring down that broad nose and waiting on something: not the kid answering, not the party (if that's even what the rich can call these things’galas? events?). Most of the time, I think he wants to crack my ribcage apart and eat the juicy inner bits. But that's just a hypothesis. "What it is, Sage?" "Where are you?" "Ridin' the limo to my crib." "You're still renting that place? Fuck it, nevermind. Look, you remember ditching all your friends at a club we rented to celebrate you, like, not sucking?" "No shit I remember five minutes ago. What up?" "Turns out that there's a legitimate, ahem, social gathering for the immensely well off, and it's not very far away." "That's, yeah; that’s coo', yeah." "So tell the chauffeur he's coming back and driving us somewhere else." "What about sleep?" "Eh, fuck it. Get blitzed with the crazy rich." "What about that commercial shit you was buggin' on?" "Thought I told you to show up with a nosebleed." "Motherfucker." "Now get to turning that limo around." Kill the call’he's bringing the limo back. What else could he do? Sobias grunts, Sobias laughs. Same thing. "So he loved the idea, I see." "Who cares. It sounds fun." What do they do, anyways, that's so different from us? Or, well. Pretty much totally divorced from that us, now; can't start denying that I'm one of them. Not that money's changed me; just my hair and my clothes and my cars and my houses and the levels of famous attached to the asses I know. Not me, though; it's only changed every single element of who I am and what I do and how I live. Fuckin' light the cigarette, Sage. Fire. Inhale. It's that simple. See? Hasn't changed me at all. Custom tailored Italian clothes have always made me itch, like I've always been a little insulted that their actual brands are all made in China now. Can't really remember what China's labor practices are like these days’quality takes a serious hit, though. Fuck, Sage. Listen to yourself. No. Oh. Then stop thinking. Alright. Lightposts. Street signs. Billboards. Taxis. Drunk drivers. A lawyer the size of a planet. Strobing background, neon sign. Trees. Alleys. Brick buildings that are just away from seeing their first century, probably. That seemed like a thought. Was it? Fuck. Just wait. Headlights. Yeah? Yeah. Pulls up too far down from the doorway, hits us in the lamps and inches on. Glacial, relatively. Sobias goes up front; says he needs to give the driver directions like a chauffeur doesn't know where the city's glitz goes. Wait, still pulling up. Must milk convenience for all it's got. Yeah, perfect. Get in the back; the kid's cracking open a bottle of champagne. "Sure this is gonna be worth fuckin' up my sleep?" "Uh. Off the fucking chain. Boyee." "Man. Ain't gotta talk down to me." "Yeah. Sorry." Nothing on TV. Infomercials and poker and is there even a point for this thing other than charging a little bit more on the rental? Need to get my own car and my own driver one of these days. Definitely more cost effective in the long run. Shit’people punching each other. Now this is good watchings. "Knew I had that shit, though." Fuck is he on about? "Fuck are you on about?" "My superkick. S'better than Rollins'. That's all." "Kid. You don’t have to fixate on the motherfucker." "Damn, sorry dog. Jus' thinkin' back was all." "If you're gonna bother with thinking, man, at least don't think about the shit you’ve already done." He crosses his arms, leans his head back and lets the bottle back to the ice bucket. "So, how's your girl?" "Dani? She's straight." "Nah, the Jordan bitch." "I told you already, she isn't a replacement. Quit worrying on that shit." "Haha, dog, f'real? Ain't no one to replace me. Just bein' friendly righ' now. She wrestlin' yet?" "Oh, well, fuck. Uh. Not since she joined up. Still looking for a fed that, you know, is suitable for a tiny chick." "Muhfuckin' midget an' shit? Smooth on, playa." "Not a midget." I know there’s an ashtray somewhere back here. But I don’t see it, so, well. Fuck the upholstery anyway. "Just pretty short and, you know, built like an actual athlete. Sorta, sorta like a gymnast, yeah." "Ah, I gotcha. Too small of titties for Caldera to even worry “bout signin' her." "Hah, right the fuck on, Frank. The very least, I know that asshole isn't going to try to make JXW a GCW exclusive thing." Fuck’this ring's getting old. Too many years, too many places; same song. Shouldn't hold that against whoever's calling, I guess. "Frank, one minute." "Word." Takes a second for him to look away; follows with sliding in closer to the TV and turns the volume up. "Yo. What's up, girl?" "I am soooooo fucked up." Slurring already. Kids and their getting wasted. "What are you doin'?" "Riding around with Jordan, uh, Frank." "Hehee. Too busy to stop by?" "Right now, yeah." "Aw. That sucksss." "You still at the hotel?" "Mmhmmm." "Shit. Should be back like, I don't know. Maybe 6?" "Why you gonna make a girl stay awake all night?" "Could go to sleep." "I don't wanna." "Then it's your fault. Can't blame me." "Oh, you fucker." "Yep. But shit, I've gotta be getting off the phone. I'll see you later." "You better." Flip it shut. Jordan turns, scratching between cornrows and tapping the bottle against the window. "Shit. Ain't realize you had Dani in town." "Huh? Oh, shit. Pfffh. Yeah, there's a lot of shit you don't realize. We're here though, so uh. Smack yourself sober and remember to just play it like you’re not a fucking tool." "And you sure this ain't gonna like, fuck up my game on Worldwide?" "Kid. What the hell could at this point?"
