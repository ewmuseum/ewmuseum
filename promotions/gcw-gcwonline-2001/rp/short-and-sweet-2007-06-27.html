Short and Sweet

27 June 2007

Date: 27/06/2007 Looking outside his hotel room window into a busy Brisbane street below him, Jimmy can't help but enjoy the fact that he is home again. Even though he appreciates the accolades he receives in his adopted hometown of Boise Idaho, the feeling he gets when he's around his own people is something unique to Jimmy. As soon as his privately charted flight landed in Melbourne, a strong sense of security overwhelmed him so much so that it nearly brought one of GCW's most powerful men to tears. Yes it is safe to say that Jimmy Thunder is happy to finally be home. Jimmy reaches out to the table next to him and puts on his necklace that has been dubbed through time as "The Demon Jewel." Jimmy came into the possession of The Demon Jewel after beating it's previous owner, "The Lord of the Depths" Chris Neptune, in a match the same night that Jimmy's wife passed away after being the victim of a drunk hit and run driver. In turn, Chris Neptune won The Demon Jewel from it's original owner, GCW newcomer Thomas Dsousa, who had The Demon Jewel made as a sign of his own personal struggles. With a twist of irony, Jimmy looks to The Demon Jewel when the cards of life are stacked against him as well, much like Thomas Dsousa did. It has come to a point where Jimmy is convinced that his deceased wife Aurora's spirit continues to live through The Demon Jewel. Every time Jimmy puts the necklace on, he has an overwhelming warmth feeling go through his body which he believes is a sign that Aurora is with him. Knowing this puts Jimmy at ease as he heads out of his hotel room and into the car park where an awaiting limo is parked with the driver standing next to an opened back door waiting for Jimmy to climb in. As a guest at an A - List celebrity party for the launch of Australia Rock Hall of Famer, Jimmy Barnes' anthology album, Jimmy is expecting a huge crowd outside who are waiting to get a glimpse of the their favourite Aussie rock musicians. With precision judgement, the limo driver manages to pull up so when Jimmy steps out his first step will be on the red carpet. Just as he climbs out of the limo and the crowd notice who he is, the entire crowd start chanting "Thunder Thunder!!!" which brings a smile to Jimmy's face. He walks over to one side and signs several autographs for some eager fans then makes his way to the other side of the red carpeted aisle and signs some more autographs. Just as he is about to leave the second group of fans, Richard Wilkins, entertainment reporter from the Today show quickly rushes up to Jimmy with his camera crew. "Here he is, the man that the whole nation is talking about, our very own Jimmy Thunder. How are you Jimmy?" Richard asks in a chirpy tone. "I feel great, It's great to be back home, I don't get much of chance to come home but when I do really make sure I make the most of it. Right now the fans are making my time back here a memorable one." Jimmy promptly replied. "The fans have really warmed to you. It can almost be said that the entire nation has gone Jimmy Thunder crazy! Can you put that down to anything in particular?" Asks Richard. "Ha ha ha I'm not sure, I guess it must be my Hollywood good looks." Jimmy says as he jokingly laughs. "It's really nice to have such warm hometown support, it really makes everything worth while for me." Jimmy finishes with an embarrassing smile. "So what can everyone expect from you this week at World Wide?" Richard curiously asks. "This week will be my first match on my home soil in three years and I'll be facing a guy by the name of Jesse Ramey. He's a guy that i don't know too much about but I'm here to tell you that I'll be putting in a great show." Jimmy answers with a huge smile on his face. Jimmy says thanks to Richard Wilkins before walking off along the red carpet signing more autographs along the way. "That was Australia's hottest property at the moment, Jimmy Thunder. You can see him and the rest of his GCW mates on World Wide, this weekend. The show is a complete sell out and the only place you can watch World Wide is on pay-per-view television. I'm Richard Wilkins for the Today show" The screen fades.
