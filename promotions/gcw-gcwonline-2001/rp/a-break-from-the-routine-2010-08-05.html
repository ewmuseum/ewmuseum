A Break from the Routine

5 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/05/10 A Break from the Routine WorldWide 133: Bryan: Shining Wizard! Yale: And toss in that knee brace to boot, it has got to hurt both of them! As expected, Tessa instantly grabs her knee in sheer agony. The crowd continues to cheer for her, each and every one of them coming to their feet. She looks up, seeing her opponent out of it and drags her body over toward him, just barely able to drape her arm over his chest. The crowd explodes. 1… 2… 3! Bryan: She's did it! Tessa Windsor has managed to pull this match out despite everything. Yale: It really looked like at times that David Spencer would be able to continue rolling, but Tessa Windsor refused to give up, even when it would have been the smart thing to do. Rampage 2010: Yale: Not even looking for the cover, JB! Tim Shipley is all about crushing his opponent right now! With Spencer up Shipley lands a quick double axe handle to the back of his head. Shipley begins to try to lock in an octopus-style armbar submission hold. But before he can finalize it, Spencer finally comes to and twists away from Shipley, firing a haymaker at Shipley. Shipley ducks the punch, though, and wraps an arm around the waist and trips out David Spencer's legs in a Russian Leg Sweep! Bryan: Every time David Spencer tries to gain some momentum, Tim Shipley executes a perfect counter to snuff it out! Gaining some momentum, Shipley runs to the ropes and bounces off as Spencer is getting up. Shipley comes in on Spencer with a huge running knee lift that sends David Spencer staggering! Tim Shipley immediately lands a side kick to the stomach that doubles David Spencer over, then runs at the bent down Spencer. Yale: Uh oh! This might be it! Shipley plants his left foot on Spencer's back, plants his right foot on the top turnbuckle, then on the way down grabs David Spencer's head and executies The Eternal Hope that rocks the ring as camera flashes fill the stadium! Shipley makes the cover as Hal Jenkins goes for the count! 1!... 2!... 3!! Amongst the boo's, Tim Shipley stands triumphant, raising his hands not in celebration but to show the crowd just how damn good he is. Andrews: Here is your winner, and Rampage Tournament Finalist...TIM SHIPLEY!!! Bryan: It was quick, all things considered, but it was vicious! WorldWide 134: Bryan: Uh-oh! This does not look good for David Spencer! He might be better off not getting off the canvas! Yale: Clyde's got him right where he wants him! The crowd continues to get louder and louder until David Spencer is finally up on his feet. He turns his groggy body around just in time to get kicked in the midsection by Clyde and pulled into a pump handle! Bryan: This is it! The fans collectively raise their arms as Clyde executes his finishing maneuver on David Spencer! INTO THE FOXHOLE! Yale: He nailed it! Bryan: That's gotta be it! ...1! ...2! ...3! RRRRRRRRRRAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHH! DING! DING! DING! Bryan: It's over! Clyde Fox is defiant over David Spencer in an energetic opening match here on WorldWide! . . . It's a few days after WorldWide 134, in the hotel room of GCW star, David Spencer. The darkness of the night plagues the room and its inhabitant. The sound of a tape rewinding can be heard. The man formerly known as Face is slumped against the wall behind the bed he is sitting on. He looks on as footage from a few nights ago shoots across the screen in a frenzied time warp. The rewinding soon stops and the familiar voices of Bryan and Yale suddenly come up, bringing Spencer through the last moments of his defeat at the hands of Clyde Fox. He repeats this process several times before turning the TV off entirely, making the room even more pitch black than before. In this heightened darkness, the thoughts of Spencer can run unhindered. Is this what I'm becoming? is the question eating away at the masked man. Am I turning into a pushover...a shadow of my former self; a pathetic stepping stone for these so-called moral people to take advantage of? Spencer drops the remote onto his bed and turns to sit on the edge. He grabs the sides of his mask tightly, searching for answers to his own pressing inquiries. Is this worth it? thinks Spencer, as a cloud moves out from in front of the moon, allowing for the lunar light to shine in through his window. The night stand beside his bed is slightly lit, about a dozen bottles of pills sitting atop of it. David picks one of the only marked bottles up and examines it. It contains the pills that had been given to him as experimental treatment for his condition. These pills attempt to curb a condition which has no cure...a condition that does not necessarily weaken me. What would it be like if I had simply been allowed to continue on my previous path...if I had not succumbed to my temptations in that alley...? The more Spencer thinks about it, the more upset he becomes. All these pills have given me is a false sense of belonging...a sense that does not even intrigue me in the first place. What else have they given me? Losses - and more than just match losses. I've lost my edge, my drive - even a portion of my free will - all for the sake of fitting in to this society I am forced to be in. Spencer puts a pause on his own thought process, simply staring at the bottle of pills in his hand. He shakes his head and grips the bottle tightly for a few moments. Then, he suddenly chucks the bottle of pills at a nearby wall, causing the plastic bottle's top to get knocked off and pills to subsequently spill onto the floor. He then proceeds to swat at the remaining bottles atop his night stand, scattering them across the floor as well. I can't afford to be handicapped with those pills any longer. thinks Spencer as he gets up from his bed and walks over to a wall. He then punches said wall, putting a small dent in it, shaking out his fist afterwards. David turns around and brushes away the curtain in front of his window, staring straight into the night sky. "I think it’s about time for the real David Spencer to make a mark on GCW, without any of those retched pills slowing me down..."
