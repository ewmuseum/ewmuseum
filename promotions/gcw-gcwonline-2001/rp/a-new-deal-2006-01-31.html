A new deal?

31 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/31/06 Shall we play catch up? A few weeks off had done them all good, right? You'd hope it would be. At the beginning of December the brothers Brand had just left Japan after the fateful night in yet another wrestling federation that had appeared to have decided that the tag division was an afterthought. After they thought about it, they decided it wasn't worth it. They had left Tokyo Japan that night after another empty promise of finally getting a chance to prove themselves in the ring as the Number One Contenders for the vacated belts, and came out with nothing. Dani, she had come out in the last few for the big tourney, but was that enough to really say that it had been a good night? But then the unbelievable happened. When they arrived back in Las Vegas, they had found out that everyone in the federation had come back to having no job. That federation, the casino and everything was closed. And then a few hours later, they all received phone calls telling them that the federation had re-opened under a new board of directors and that they were invited back to re-negotiate their contracts. Josh remembered that night all to well. Their stablemate, Blaise McClancy, went off. He'd grown up in the area and had seen actual corporations and real big casinos use this tactic to get out of union contracts and paying people's benefits. It was usually a tactic used to save money. Basically, their employer had betrayed them all, dumping all the contracts they had and restarting everything over. But, this group of people, particularly the team of Joshua and Gray Brand, were they offered less? Not really. Apparently some of the bigger Global names had decided that the betrayal had been too much, and that they were not going to renegotiate on their contracts. Everyone in this little group had been offered more, even chances, shots at all of the title belts when the fed reopened. But it wasn't enough. A federation, a home that does that type of tactic is deceitful, and is not a place that you wish to work at if you want to ensure you have a job come a few weeks or months later. In the end, a small group of ragtag wrestlers chose to take their free agency and go elsewhere. But where does that lead them? Well it's obvious that it leads them to a federation named Global Championship Wrestling. They're booked this week against Team Baked, right? Two teams from the Northwestern part of the United States. Neighbors to be exact. But by far different in their ways, in and out of the ring. Team Baked you have met, and know them somewhat. But now it's time to meet the team from Saquamish, Washington. Two half brothers with different paths to take, yet still riding the same vehicle to get there. .... Josh looked around the place. He couldn't believe his eyes. If he didn't know better he half expected Lee Van Cleef and Jack Palance to walk through the swinging shutter type door. Someone was playing an old saloon type piano over in the corner with some girl, dressed the part for the scene, sitting on the stool next to him. Others in the room showed their signs of being in the old west, they were looking more cowboy then the guys in that Deadwood series on HBO. He looked down at the table he was seated at and realized there were 5 cards laying there, face down. His head came back up to show two others sitting at the table with him, both had their cards up to their faces, doing their best to hide them from him. ?C'mon man, you going to play or what? You just looking over at the little vixen over there all night.? ?What?? ?C'mon, if you're not going to play and just look at the philly, then drop your money on the table and go. Would save us some time.? Josh shook his head and took a drink from the glass next to him... and almost spit it out. Hard whiskey, and not a good kind at all. It's at that time he realized two things. One was the woman seated next to the piano player. Her long black hair, the lines of her face, the curves of her... It was Dani. His girlfriend outside of whatever dream world he'd fallen into, his lover. He smiled towards her, and this vision of Dani waved and smiled back at him. Even in here, she was the most beautiful thing he'd ever seen. A full blooded Lakota woman from the Black Hills of South Dakota. Surprisingly enough, from the Rosebud Indian Reservation, the former location of a place named Deadwood. The second thing you may ask? That was the form that had just walked through the swinging doors in this old Hollywood stereotype set. A man named Jaymes. A man he'd seen a few months ago. A man he'd broken the knee of standing on a Las Vegas street. A man who'd tried to kidnap his girlfriend and take her away. A man who's evils were more then most on this forsaken planet. He was a 'Wicahmunga'... a witch to his people, who were also of the Sioux offshoots. Nakota to be exact, from the Missouri area. The man was now a shell of his former self, having met not only Josh's ASP batons, but also the kiss of their benefactor within the Order, an Irish woman named Annie. A Leanan Shihde, as in one of the not so goody-two-shoes faery types, she kissed him. This act ruined his mind and reduced him to a drooling shell, unable to conduct his magics again. But now Josh watched as he walked into the bar here, and walked right over to Dani. She stood up and kissed him full on the lips, a view that even here almost set Josh off completely. He forced his training on himself here. This was nothing but a dream, a vision of an over active mind. But sometimes clues could come about. He had to figure them out... He looked at the man who he saw as his vanquished enemy and noticed that things were a bit different. His face was younger. The Jaymes he had known was, while not immortal per se, was long lived. He had lived from the time he'd sold his soul to the demonic spirits, about the time Sitting Bull had become great chief. This man was younger, and had a lighter shade of color to his hair. Not to mention a slight bit of facial hair on his chin. Most natives can't grow anything there. Josh's facial hair, being half Caucasian grows about twice as fast as his brothers, but still isn't anything he has to worry too much about. He looked on while the man kissed the Dani-visions hand and then they started to chit chat. She looked over his shoulder towards Josh, with a look of distaste, but she was cordial with him. Josh decided to play the game, and finally pick up his hand of cards. ?'Bout time.? One of his fellow players snapped at him. Josh unfolded the cars into a fan and then began to take them in: five of diamonds, eight of clubs, ace of clubs, eight of spades... ace of spades. What the..? Josh looks up from his dealt hand just in time to see the man reach out and grab Dani by the arm. He starts to pull her towards the doors of the saloon. Josh comes to his feet, kicking his chair out from under him and draws the pistol at his side. The two men playing with him dive for cover. The other man spins towards Josh, a pulled pistol of his own. Two shots fire... Josh sees it happen as the bullet rips into his chest. Worse then watching the matrix series and all of the piss poor fakes since then, he lives it for a split moment in time. Seattle, Washington Uhm, not really. Close enough though. Bainbridge Island, Washington, a thirty-five minute ferry ride to Seattle or a 10 minute drive to Saquamish where the boys were born. It's a large house, but not quite as large as the one that they currently all live in Las Vegas. This one is the home of one Margariett Brand, the mother of Joshua. Josh bolts up in bed, sitting straight up. He holds his chest as he tries to catch his breath. The pain was intense. Had to be only acid reflux though, it's not like he was really shot. Before he said anything though, he stopped as the form next to him moved slightly and let out a sigh. Dani laid there, where she belonged next to him in the bed. She looked like she was having a better dream then he had. That was good, usually she was the one with the nightmares. As the reflux felt like it was getting worse, Josh decided it was time to take care of it before he got sick. Slipping out of bed he tossed a pair of loose fitting sweats on over his legs and boxer briefs and then headed out of the room towards the kitchen area. Quietly he reaches into the fridge and pulls out a half gallon carton of milk. He doesn't bother to pour a glass and breaks his mother's rules in drinking from the carton as he tries to put the burning sensation in his chest out. He stops drinking to address something in the dark. ?Yeah, yeah. She'd kill me if she saw. I'll buy a new carton tomorrow morning.? A chuckle comes from the dark. ?You've done that since you were a little pup as it is. I'd expect nothing less. Why do you think I keep a separate carton for myself back in Vegas?? Josh smiles at that comment. He and his brother knew each other too well. Out of the dark, stepped the older and much larger Brand. Gray was named for the color of his hair and eyes. A genetic defect for the full blooded Saquamish Indian man, they lived up to his name. It was a light gray at that. A sharp contrast from his darker skin tone. Josh tried to make conversation with his brother, to ward him off a bit. ?So, since when did the Gray Wolf go nocturnal?? Gray smiled a white toothed smile as he nodded to him. ?You should know by now. The night lets me think. I usually head to bed about four. Means when I wake up about your time, nine-ish, I'm rested still. My kind doesn't need as much sleep.? His kind... Yeah, we'll leave that for a later time. Let's just say that Gray is a throw back in the people of the Ft. Madison Saquamish Reservation by a few hundred years. He's not that old, but some of his unique family features passed down by his own mother, make him quite a bit different then his younger half-brother. The parts of them that are the same have to come from their late father, most of that would be their stubbornness. Gray reaches out and holds something out for his brother. ?You ready for the trip tomorrow? Back to Japan again. Two months later to end in Tokyo once more.? Josh took what looked like a cd or dvd case, one of the cheap ones, out of his brother's hand. ?Yeah, what's this?? Gray smiled again. ?Team Baked... everything I could find about them. Not much, other then they're from the other Seattle Culture then you.? ?I already guessed that. And what do you mean other one then me?? Gray chuckled lightly. ?There are two types from Seattle. Those run down low from Canadian grown herbs, or those revved up high on Colombian grown beans.? Gray motioned towards the large, commercial type, espresso machine that sat on Margariett's counter top. Josh burst out laughing as that one hit him. Yes it was true, Seattle was the place he'd grown up and the place that had gotten him hooked on Coffee. Starbucks, Seattle's Best, and many other knock offs. The whole Puget Sound Area, you couldn't make it two blocks without another espresso stand coming into view. But the other side of Seattle? Well Team Baked proved that. Where else can you hear a DJ by the name of Ricker take a toke on a bong every day at four twenty in the afternoon? ?So what can we expect? You're the training expert.? Gray nodded at him before answering. It wasn't often that Josh deffer ed to his brother's judgment willingly. ?They're mostly high fliers. Botch things on a regular basis, almost on purpose from the looks of it. Dunno really. It's more of a gimmick thing then real wrestling. So why are you up so early?? Josh took another drink, trying to come up with a reason to not answer. ?Don't try to play it off either, little brother. Your smell... it's not normal that smell comes off of you. That's nothing but fear.? Okay, we'll let you in on some of it. Gray is more then a human. He's not immortal, his kind will eventually grow old and die, and they're not invulnerable... but their senses are heightened to the point where they're almost like a wild animal's. Then again, at certain times, you can't really tell a difference between them and the animal. ?Nothing much. Just a dream. I still don't know why I have them occasionally.? Gray leaned back against the counter top to the center island in the kitchen. ?Fill me in little pup. You know what type of troubles we got into when Dani was keeping her dreams from us. Sometimes these things are big time clues.? Josh shook his head to the negative. ?Nope. This is just me still, after six months feeling the jealousy and envy for a man who proved to be the reason why Dani finally accepted me into her life. Jaymes is gone, and she's with me. Not him. But I seem to be pretty good at making him come out to be almost mine or your age. Instead of the slightly middle aged man he appeared to be. That might be what gets me the most. He was almost as good looking as me.? Gray stares at his brother. Josh grins at him. ?You're not going to let it go?? ?Nope.? ?Gah!? Josh waves his hands in the air, almost spilling the milk from the carton. ?Okay. Write it down so you don't forget it. It was an old west saloon. Straight out of Hollywood. I was playing cards and Dani was the saloon girl. He walks in, talks to her. I play my cars. He tries to take her, and I stand up. We both shoot and I get hit in the chest and die. Remember, I'm the one trained in dream interpretation. Just means I'm still feeling that he could have won her over if he'd not been the asshole he was. ? Gray only asks him one question. ?What were your cards?? ?Huh?? ?What cards did you have?? ?Uhm, five of diamonds, eight and ace of both clubs and diamonds. Why?? Gray appears to go stoic, his typical stone face when he's trying to be serious. ?Nothing. Just noting down the details. Why don't you try to get back to sleep. Dani will know you're gone soon. You know she gets cranky if you're not there while she's sleeping. She's been through a lot, and she's becoming dependent on you.? Josh just nodded and then started to walk out of the kitchen. As soon as he was back through the door of the room they were borrowing while they were visiting here. Gray looked over back into the darkness and nodded. ?Did you get that?? A man that comes to some point between the size of both Josh and Gray steps out. His hair a deep, natural red with a bit of stubble on his chin. The man was their leader, their lieutenant in the Order, Blaise McClancy. A half Paiute from the Las Vegas area, but also sharing a very distinct similarity to Gray. ?Yeah, I got it. And I don't like it.? ?I'm glad he couldn't hear you ask me what cards. Do you make something of them?? ?Yup. Very distinctive hand there.? Gray looked at him quizzically. ?I know gambling, I know cards and dice. I'm from Vegas, remember. My own moniker is the old English word for the game of Craps. That is a Deadman's Hand.? The expletive that Gray then says under his breath would not fully translate for you. Being a speaker of the Salish tongue, he says it instinctively. But the thought behind it is universal, very few words sound like that, and all bring up the thoughts of human fecal matter. To be continued...
