Enter Khaos

15 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/15/11 I have arrived, Khaos is here in GCW after two and half years off the map I’ve come back into the spotlight once again, why may someone ask is Khaos coming back to wrestling after such a lengthy time away from the ring? It’s because I wish to step out of the shadow of only one man, John “The Prophet” Adams, my eternal rival, and also my half-brother. This is the year I surpass him in power, this is the year I can finally say I am a true contender to his glory and well established history. When I decided to return to the ring wrestling critics all over said Khaos cannot do the impossible, your nothing more than John Adams’s shadow, Khaos is nothing more than a carbon copy of his half-brother he can’t prove a damn thing, the same comments again and again over and over did nothing more than fuel my desire to return to action and prove everyone wrong that I am not some fake, that I am not some carbon copy and that I can do the impossible and thus made it my mission, my soul propose to plow though the doubters, the nay sayers and the rest of the world that I can stand on my own two feet without anyone’s help. Now is the time grasp the future and cement my own legacy…the legacy of Khaos. -Tears of Blood club: Basement floor 9:45 AM- {The scene opens up on Khaos who is in front of a punching bag Khaos smacks it hard as it sways back and forth to and fro after each hit, perhaps he is envisioning it as John “The Prophet” Adams in order to push himself harder than ever before feeling that it is laughing at him.} Khaos: Huff puff, damn you to hell! Quit laughing at me! {Khaos punches the bag in front him as hard as he can sending it reeling back towards him, as it comes back Khaos then clotheslines it busting the bag on impact letting all the sand fall to the floor. Khaos looks at the pile of sand with a straight face as he walks away from it leaving it to clean up another time. He takes his towel walking towards his elevator with thoughts of what he had just done.} Khaos: Hmph, no matter how many times I try I cannot get his face out of my head, perhaps this is a punishment from God for not defeating him, either way its fine, this will only make me stronger in the long run. {The elevator door opens as Khaos steps inside pressing the second floor button which leads to his main office/room of Tears of Blood.} -Second floor 10:20 AM- {Khaos exits the elevator and heads for the shower to cleanse himself of the sweat he accumulated while working out, taking off his workout garbs he then steps inside turning on the water letting it hit him like droplets of rain.} Khaos: So I wonder who my first opponent will be in GCW. No, it doesn’t matter who it is I will crush him with my bare hands and beat him asunder and I won’t stop till he is unable to move thus making it known that anyone who crosses my path will rue the day they opposed me. {Khaos then turns off the water and steps out of shower grabbing another fresh towel from the rack drying himself before putting some black pants, shoes and a shirt on and heads for his office/room. While the club doesn’t open until six pm Khaos always likes to make sure things are kept in order, from the bartenders to bouncers Khaos leaves no stone unturned in making sure a tight ship is ran. With a touch of a button Khaos immediately calls for his loyal secretary Nightshade.} ~BUZZ~ Khaos: Nightshade, it’s me, come to my office at once. Nightshade: (Over the mic) Yes sir. {Khaos hears a knock on his steel door.} ~BAM BAM BAM~ Khaos: Enter. Nightshade: Sir you requested my presence? Khaos: Yes, any word from GCW on whom my opponent is? Even though to me it doesn’t matter I just want to know for sake of knowing so I can carve his name on a tombstone when I am though with him. Nightshade: Not yet sir, apparently that information has not been released yet at this time. Khaos: Damn it all, how long do they plan on making me wait, months, and years? What is this Garbage Bag Johnny doing? Nightshade: Garbage Bag Johnny? (She inquires) Khaos: Yes, that’s apparently GCW’s ‘commissioner’, a very interesting egg I found. I am going to keep an eye on him. Nightshade: His name leaves little to be desired, sounds like some random nuisance that just crawls under your skin like a bad odor. Khaos: Well in any case there isn’t much I can do about him for now and I don’t want to overstep my boundaries yet, besides, I am at the bottom of the ladder now so I am just going to HAVE to trust him, meanwhile I want you to sniff around GCW and find out WHO my first opponent is. Nightshade: As you wish sir, I’m off. {Nightshade walks though the steel door out of sight as Khaos has a seat at his desk until another knock is heard on his steel door.} ~BAM BAM BAM~ Khaos: Enter. {It is none other than Cory O’Brien who is part owner of Tears of Blood and Khaos’s long time friend.} Khaos: Cory, what brings you here? Wait do you have the list of bands that are playing tonight? I need them by the time we open. Cory: Isn’t that why I’m here? Of course I do. Khaos: Lay it on me. Cory: Right, Hot Pink, The Skull Kids, Phantom Riders and Deathstruck. Khaos: Interesting. Alright make sure the necessary arrangements are made with them. Screw ups will not be tolerated. Cory: Yeah, yeah I know, don’t forget we are 50/50 on this place; you don’t HAVE to remind me of what’s at stake junior. Khaos: Junior? Man why such tone? Maybe your age is starting to kick in and- Cory: (Interrupts) Don’t you even start with me pal! Start cracking old man jokes and I’ll turn you inside out! Khaos: (laughs) Right, right, anyways, what’s been up with you? Cory: Not much, but by the way you had Nightshade on her way out in a hurry. Khaos: Yes, I still do not know who my opponent will be at this coming World Wide, so I am having her dig up some info, hopefully later tonight I’ll find out just who I am facing, I hate being left in the dark. Cory: Honestly I didn’t think it mattered WHO you fight. Khaos: True and I did state to Nightshade already, that I am just going to HAVE to trust this Garbage Bag Johnny or else my ride here in GCW will be short lived one so I can’t afford ANY mistakes, I must move carefully, otherwise I’ll never be out of the shadow of one John “The Prophet” Adams. Cory: Aye this Garbage Bag Johnny sounds like a pain in the ass to me. Tread carefully; god knows what he is capable of. Anyways, is that the real reason you came back? To step out of your half-brothers shadow? {Khaos pauses briefly before answering} Khaos: …Yes, and to prove something once and for all, that I am not some god damn carbon copy of John “The Prophet” Adams and that I am my own man and not some cheap rip off, I am going to prove once and for all I can be just as great, just as glorious and just as prestigious as him I am going to silence all the wrestling critics that dare to say; “You can’t do it”, “It’s impossible” , “You’re nothing.” Cory: You have to admit it though it’s not something that can’t be done in one day, it’s going to take time for people to get to know you better. It’s like you said, you are on the bottom of the ladder now and there’s no way around that because that’s the way this business works you know that, I know that and your step-father did too so you have to take it one match at a time, period. Khaos: You are right, this will take some time, but no matter how long it takes I will achieve my goals, no matter what. It’s something that I wanted to keep telling myself over and over again, but how long will it really take to get there, how much blood, sweat and tears must be shed in order to meet such goals, how many opponents must I face in order to reach the top of the game… It doesn’t matter; bring them all on I say I’m ready for them. -First Floor 8:05 PM- {Since opening at six the club was packed full as the usual Goth/Punk community alike like moths to a flame making the hustle and bustle of many bodies more obvious as Khaos looks on enjoying the scenery from his private table where a couple of bouncers are keeping guard as he is sipping on something hard while enjoying the music from the band called Hot Pink playing their single Blood Wires. Cory walks up to the table and has seat right next to Khaos.} Cory: Well looks like another fucking awesome party again tonight eh Khaos? Khaos: Fuck yeah; we really racked it in didn’t we. Cory: Sure did, I mean look at this, people are gathered here because of us, we are like gods to these people. Khaos: Indeed, we have provided their solace away from the bullshit that’s called society Cory; these people were rejected by the outside world so we gave them a place to call their own, we gave them Tears of Blood, this is for them damnit now watch this shit. {Khaos stands up from his chair and begins to shout out the following.} Khaos: HEY YOU GUYS, YOU HAVING A FUCKING GOOD TIME TONIGHT! Crowd: YEEEAAHHH! Khaos: I CAN’T FUCKING HEAR YOU! Crowd: YEEEEEAAAAAAAHHHH! KHAOS ROCKS! Khaos: THAT’S WHAT I THOUGHT, KEEP ON PARTYING! {The crowd cheers loudly as Khaos sits back down.} Khaos: You see Cory, that’s the kind of stuff I am talking about, right there. Cory: Yeah you got them hooked all right, they love it. Khaos: Damn right they do. {As Khaos along with Cory enjoy the nightlife as the music blares on but just then Khaos gets a call on his cell phone, its none other than Nightshade who has been digging around GCW inquiring as to who Khaos will face at WorldWide 150.} Nightshade: (On the phone) Sir it’s me I finally out who you’ll be facing at WorldWide 150, I’ll be over in a few minutes to give more detail. Khaos: Understood, meet me in my office. Nightshade: (On the phone) At once sir. -click- {Khaos then hangs up his cell and gets up from his seat.} Khaos: Cory, that was Nightshade, she finally found out who my opponent is. Cory: Took her long enough, they probably held her up intentionally. Khaos: Yeah but why…guess I’ll find out soon enough, anyways, you have the floor here; I’m going back to my office. Cory: Right. {Khaos along with the two bouncers cuts though the crowd of people on the way to his private elevator he steps inside and presses the second floor button as the door closes leaving the two bouncers to stand guard at the elevator.} -Second Floor 10:15 pm- {The Khaotic One arrives on the second floor after the elevator has dinged he exits out and heads for his window where he can see the entire stage where the bands are playing from his vantage point watching the crowd from above. Just then he hears a ding from the elevator, it seems Nightshade has arrived to give Khaos and update on whom he’ll be facing at WorldWide 150.} Nightshade: I have arrived sir and I have brought you some news you requested. Khaos: My opponent? Lay it on me. Nightshade: Well sir it’s not just one person, it’s many. Take a look. {Nightshade walks over to Khaos and hands him the document pertaining information on his match which reads the following.} -Khaos we are pleased to inform you that your up and coming match at WorldWide 150 will be You vs. Daniel Cruz vs. William Curr vs. Triumph Frost vs. Ryan Gallway in a Gauntlet Match, we deeply apologize for any delay we may have caused. -GCW- {Khaos grins with delight as Nightshade breathes a sigh of relief knowing her work has paid dividends.} Nightshade: I take it you are excited about the news sir? Khaos: Excited? No, I’m thrilled, this is exactly what I expected the GCW no Garbage Bag Johnny to pull out of his hat! Nightshade: Well I have some additional information concerning him; apparently he was in the hospital due to some injury he sustained at WorldWide 148, this is probably why he was delayed the booking process. Khaos: I see. Well I am sure that someday out paths will cross, but for now I must focus on the task at hand which is WorldWide 150 which for me is a must win situation but I have go though four other men to get the victory which is fine by me because those are my kind of odds in which I relish this kind of opportunity Nightshade. Nightshade: Indeed sir, oh yes another piece of information I have found on one Triumph Frost. Khaos: Oh? Is there something that I should be aware of about him? Nightshade: Yes, apparently he believes he is the next big thing in GCW he has had a run in with the likes of Rich Rollins and the World Champion Andy Murray as well, he shouldn’t be taken lightly. {The Khaotic One folds his arms as he looks on at the crowd below.} Khaos: You know, everyone thinks they are the next big thing Nightshade, and I guess this douche is no exception because he can call himself whatever he likes to make himself THINK he is the next big thing but the fact of the matter is he is just another punk looking to make a name for himself, just like everyone else he has big ass dreams of making it big blah blah blah etc etc oh (He laughs) I guess even I have something to prove as well, but I’m not some ass clown that flaunts it like it’s a big deal, because it isn’t. It just makes me sick that’s all, I see no reason whatsoever that Triumph Frost is ANY threat to me at all. Now is there anything else before we adjourn this meeting? Nightshade: No sir, I am though with my report. Khaos: Understood, we are adjourned for the night, dismissed. Nightshade: Sir! Khaos: Worldwide 150, I will arrive, this is where it all begins… -FIN-
