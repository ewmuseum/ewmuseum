Finger Paintings of the Insane

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Timeless Old man Anton, Jason and Bryan's deranged and dead father was a horrible decorator and housekeeper. Rats and cockroaches, dead things, bad smells, nastiness littered the house. However he did keep one room spotless. He spent many hours in his own Art Gallery, just off the south end of the house. Many of England's finest would come here and marvel in the talents of Anton, and his fellow painters. Anton would spend thousands and thousands of dollars a month on fine art. He once spent $163,970 on a rare Picasso piece. Anton's art of choice however was finger painting. Bryan and Jason when they were younger would often sneak into here and just stare at all these magnificent pieces. Jason latered named these, 'Finger Paintings of the Insane' Bryan often comes in here, not for his love of art, but for his disdain of the past. He's burnt many of his abusive father's pieces as an act of revenge for the life he destroyed. 'I remember once as a child, I was sitting in my arithmetic class, and all the boys in my class wanted to meet after school and play a game of football(soccer). I'd often try to find out where the game is, but they were never interested. They would just say yer too big, or don't you have slave labor to do for nuttty father? I use to take it personal and beat the kid(s). I was later expelled for my constant fighting.' 'Fuck you father. You never gave me the life I deserved. I was a child doing man's labor. You were a sick sonofabitch. You constantly beat me until I ended it all that hot August morning. Fucking sick piece of shit.' 'Prince Phillip once honored these halls with his presence. He thought my father was the greatest thing on earth. They would drink expensive cognac, and discuss politics and they're love for hookers. How could my father discuss politics? He was a Lunatic. Prince Phillip was fond of young men, and he apparently took a liking to me. Somehow my father volunteered my services to the Prince. He took me off into the bathroom and showed me his genitals and asked me to touch them. I broke his arm and knocked his teeth out. My father never saw him again.' 'Fuck you father. You never gave me the life I deserved. I was a child doing man's labor. You were a sick sonofabitch. You constantly beat me until I ended it all that hot August morning. Fucking sick piece of shit.' 'This room has been hell to me. If I breathed the wrong way I'd receive a beating, because these paintings 'are worth more than your life, boy'. Fuck I hate those paintings, those finger paintings of the insane. Cry me a river, bleed me an ocean, i hated this room, this new death sensation. This painting right here I never threw out. I never destroyed this one bcause it reminds me of what I have become. My father calls it 'Bones of Babydolls' I call it 'Tranquilized' My broken childhood, my head full of loose screws. Now I admire pain, now I envy those bleeding, and now I only live to die. All because of this room, this fucking room, these finger paintings of the insane.' 'Fuck you father. You never gave me the life I deserved. I was a child doing man's labor. You were a sick sonofabitch. You constantly beat me until I ended it all that hot August morning. Fucking sick piece of shit.' 'It's time. It's time for me to do what needs to be done. It's time for me to leave my mark on this land.' Bryan picks up a can of gasoline and begins to douse the room. No spot left dry. Ever nook and cranny swimming in gasoline, even 'Bones of Babydolls.' Only fire. Let's start a fire, biggest one you've ever seen........ Bryan stands atop Belmont Cemetery and watches the gallery of art, his Gallery of Suicide burn to earth. Irony creeps into him, and realizes, this is not the last fire he'll see here, he can almost see it. Almost......
