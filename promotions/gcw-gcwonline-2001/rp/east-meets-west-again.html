East meets West again?

undated

Date: Unknown "Whoever wishes to foresee the future must consult the past; for human events ever resemble those of preceding times. This arises from the fact that they are produced by men who ever have been, and ever shall be, animated by the same passions, and thus they necessarily have the same results." Machiavelli ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Early in the day the clouds hang upon the rocky shores of Norway. The mist seems to cling to the rocks as if they are living entities growing one in the same with one another. The land itself seems to feed off this and puts off an invincible aura. Throughout history these shores held many battles and have born witness to the great battles of the seas just off its shores. The land itself had been frought with hardships and battles, But a hardy breed of people have come from these lands and history shows one set of the worlds greatest warriors once resided here. Today one of the powerful descendants stand upon the shore as another man of a different race of warriors has come to these lands. The unique indescribable combination of these two powers may be a foreboding warning to all that comes before them. These two men are Jorge Samuelsson and Shaman. Both have fought against each other and both have grasped championships. Now the two have come together under a common united front. A front to conquer the lands of GCW. Looking about Shaman speaks. “Look Jorge this area is kind of interesting. Is there a purpose we are here?” Samuelsson taking a deep breath looks about and grins. “There is a bit of history here for the both of us. This is where I contemplated ending your career, I nearly succeeded. It was just I never estimated the fact you’d comeback and seek me out.” Chuckling a bit Shaman looks about the landscape. “ Well I have never been one to sit down and not chase an enemy or get a bit of revenge. You see vengeance is a cruel mistress and she does not sleep nor does she wait for any being.” Shaman watches the waves crash against the rocks in the distance. His eyes focus on the coast as it disappears into the fog. Samuelsson looks about as he drags a cast net across the rocky beach. He leans forward a bit and twists himself as he spins the net upwards above his head. He then turns himself twisting as if he was an Olympic discuss thrower sling the net out into the waves quite a ways out. He hooks the nets guide lines around a stump that is buried deep into the rocky beach. Samuelsson looks over his shoulder at Shaman and grins. “So did your people fish in much like this fashion?” Shaman looks at the net and grins. “Nope we used a different type of style. We kind of hearded the fish into areas or similar to fisheries. Harvesting the fish was unique and normal.” Chuckling Samuelsson looked about. Reaching down he lifts a large branch. “Hard to think you and I would team up as warriors from different cultures and nations. Yet when two men have a large purpose and goal it is best to team up especially since these goals coincide with one another.” Leaning on the large branch Samuelsson looks over at Shaman. “You know after the WWA I went to FUSE and captured their Hardcore title their. Held it for awhile and moved on. All the while even during the WWA I was involved in the GTT tournament and beaten plenty of men who were supposed to defeat me. I still question what I could have done to become even better. Shortly after the last GTT tournament I went to Japan and wrestled there and then came back here to Europe. Yet there was a few things I needed answered.” Taking a deep breath he looks down at the ground. “You had me dead to rights Shaman why did you actually just defeat me and let me be?” Shaman looks over at Samuelsson and rubs his temple. “I was going through inner turmoils at the time. The career I had in the WWA was winding down and I had just defeated my toughest opponent since I had been in the WWA. I defeated the only guy to have ever injure me and put me out of action. I guess I wanted to make you feel that same pain and know what it was like to be injured and useless.” Clearing his throat Shaman kicks at a stone upon the beach. Samuelsson looks over at Shaman and stares at him trying to figure him out. “So you have figured out how to become what you once were? You seem more straight to the point and doing what is necessary. In fact you seem to me to be a little more dangerous than before. Would you kindly let me know how and why this can be?” Samuelsson looks at Shaman questioningly. The gaze is intense and seeks more than just a normal answer. It is as if Samuelsson is looking into the very soul of Shaman. Shaman turns himself and looks Samuelsson in the eyes a cold emotionless stare being returned. His voice low and deep. “ You seek that which you cannot find Samuelsson. It was the loss of a brother and friend. Bryan Mayhem and I had a connection that wrought destruction and oblivion to GCW. Those days have passed. Now you and I will wage a war. Against all of those who underestimated the capabilities of our skills and minds. We are Mortifera and there in lies the answer within the name. Those who will be prepared will understand what we bring to the table. The name is a mystery and an enigma all rolled into one. Solve the puzzle and you will be better prepared for the assault.” Samuelsson laughs deeply as he turns and watches the waves. “And how do you wish to get the peoples attention?” Shaman smiles his sadistic smile and looks around. “I step forward and punch them in the teeth. Plain and simple it opens some eyes.” Samuelsson looks over at Shaman eying him waiting for something. He does not know of yet. “What about Dan Black your opponent this week?” Shaman snaps his gaze over at Samuelsson. “Black, Dan Black was seriously an opponent I had to think hard about. The guy has a lot of talent and can hold his own in the ring. He earns the respect for his skills and abilities. I am not going to slam this opponent, but rest assured I am going to be ready for him.” Shaman looks at his hands. “It has been awhile since I have stepped into the ring in a singles match. Not that I haven’t wanted to. It is that I have been training and preparing my skills to do what I have to do. Bringing you to GCW. Working out contractual negotiations and my own business ventures around the world. Then I had a dream the other evening. It presented the possibilities. I stood atop a mountain staring out across an open plain.” Samuelsson nods as he watches the ocean. His hand trembling slightly as he clenches it into a fist and grins. “I tremble with anticipation. I think I understand your dream. It presents the endless possibilities we see before ourselves in GCW.” Shaman looks up at Samuelsson and smirks. He then walks over to the stump the net is tied off too. He reaches down and grabs the anchor ropes and begins to pull it in hand over hand. “This is work of men. Men will return to glory by their own strengths and wills. I have begun a path here in GCW and that path is wrought with dangers unforeseen and seen.” Shaman continues to pull the ropes as the waves crash upon the parched stones. He looks at the water drain and disappear in between the stones. “The vision also mentioned revenge. Revenge upon those who have held me down. I am going to do what I need to do. Now with you standing by my side in this battle for the ages. Native American and Viking will fight together for once rather than our ancestors legacy of fighting against one another. It is time to wake up the world and show them why we are dangerous and different.” Samuelsson grabs the ropes along with Shaman as they nod. They begin to pull the net in and they both concentrate on the sea before them. As the net comes into view you see several large fish inside the net. Shaman and Samuelsson pull them upon the rocky shore and look at them as the sun breaks through the clouds and shine down upon the wet stones glinting like thousands of little diamonds thrown upon the beach in haphazard care. Shaman looks at Samuelsson and grins. “Looks like the new day has finally arrived.” Samuelsson looks about as he reaches down and grabs a large fish under the gills and holds it aloft. “Aye that it does, May our destiny be straight and true.” Shaman reaches down and follows suit grabbing one of the fish as Samuelsson turns and drops the one he has onto several piled logs. He looks at Shaman and shakes his head. “So how are we going to salt and preserve these again?” Shaman coughs a bit and then turns. “The ocean will provide what we need and my ancestors had specific ways of preserving food, just pay attention.” The camera fades to black as the sounds of thee waves echo and leave the feeling of ease and peacefulness.
