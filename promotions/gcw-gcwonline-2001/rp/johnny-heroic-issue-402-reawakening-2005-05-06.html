Johnny Heroic: Issue #402 - Reawakening

6 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/6/05 'Back in the saddle, Johnny?' Kid Justice is perched next to me. His small, meager frame outlined against the city so vast and ominous. He just doesn't get it. He'll never get it. 'Yeah, kid. We're back in the saddle again.' And again, and again, and again. Why? Because I'll never learn. That's why. 'It's so good to have you back, Johnny! Now that we're a team again, you and I can't be stopped! We're bigger than the biggest, and we're better than the best. What can stand in the way of the Unstoppable Force, the Main Man, the one and only Johnny Heroic? And, right by his side, his ever trustworthy, ever faithful Kid Justice.' One look at him with my peripheral vision breaks my heart. He's perched, like I said, looking down at the city from these million stories up, and it's like he's trying to spot a mugger or a bank robber or some sort of villain from up here. Then he'll just hop on down to the ground with those powers that he doesn't have, and he'll go and thwart the crime. In your dreams, kid. 'Yeah. Good to be back. Keep quiet, kid. I'm trying to get a feel for the place.' 'Get a feel for what, Johnny? This city's scared shitless since your return. There isn't anybody out there with the balls or the ability to take on the Dynamic Duo, and they all know it. This town's getting cleaned up. We can save everybody.' As he says that, Sarah's words echo in my head. 'You could save everybody else, Johnny Heroic.' Her name's Sarah. That's the only part of it I was told. 'You could save everybody else, but not me.' I suppose the reason I hate doing this anymore is because it's the ultimate failure. Try to save everybody. I guarantee you can't. It's kinda like playing Tetris...every night of your life. It's entertaining for a while. Then it gets addicting. Then you find that you can't go on without it. But no matter what happens, you're always going to lose. And there's nothing you can do about it. Sarah was one of those people that I found just a little too late. She was some college girl. Probably from some suburb or some place too good to even be imaged. I remember seeing her with a long, torn brown coat pulled tight around her. She was holding it around herself to the point where it looked like she was trying to hide herself. From the world. She was walking along, in a daze but at the same time in a hurry. Like she knew she had to be someplace else than where she was, but she had no idea exactly where she had to be. And it was raining, just the slightest bit, so that there was a little bit of water dripping off of her blonde bangs. And she was gorgeous. ...and she had nice breasts. It's something about breasts that I like. I don't know exactly what. I don't know if it's their shape or their softness or their size. Because for as many as I've come across, be they comfortable or not, large or small, I haven't given a shit. It's a breast. It's gorgeous. It should be commended and given an award and celebrated before all. I even like the word. Breast. 'Is something wrong, ma'am?' Appearing before her, at the speed of light, comes Johnny Heroic: Defending of the City. That man that has every power in the book. Strength, speed, invulnerability, telepathy, energy blasts, teleportation, flight...whatever you can think of. The reason is because I have mastered the ability to control the electro-magnetic fields in and around my body, from a large scale all the way down to a cellular level. I can cause the electrons in my hands to shoot beams of condensed, powerful energy. I can change the trajectories of bullets. I can use electro-magnetic currents to, in effect, supercharge my muscles and hit harder than...than you. But that's just what everybody sees. The truth of the matter is that the only power I have is that I am the luckiest man on earth. That's the fact. I can't bend space. I can't manipulate my cells. I can't even cook a fucking bowl of maccaroni and cheese without my enhanced luck. The cosmic coin just ends up in my favor whenever I try to do anything extraordinary. Anything...except be perfect. 'You're-...too late. Johnny Heroic.' She says the name with the obvious irony. I'm no hero to her. Her name is Sarah. It's on her ID card from the hospital that she must work for. ...Sarah was my mother's name... She doesn't even have to say anything either. I know she was raped. And badly, too. Her eyes say everything for her. All excitement has left me. The cosmic coin has fallen the wrong way. 'Let me help you. Let me get you to a hospital-' But she looks at me again. She doesn't want help. She doesn't want anything except for it not to have happened. I can't turn back time. That's one of two things I've found that I'm not able to do. I mean...I've never really tried to do it. But the way I see it, if I had the power to turn back time, then nothing bad would happen in the first place. Logic dictates that had I the power to turn back time, I would do it after every event and stop bad things from happening while they were in their planning stages, and thus I would never actually have to 'save the day,' since I would have saved it in retrospect a long time ago in the future. ...or something like that. Hence, I realize I cannot turn back time. I also can't stop people from dying... Something I've tried to do too often. 'Nobody's perfect. Johnny...Heroic.' She starts walking away, and I know that she hates me. She hates me because she hates men, and she hates men because she hates the guy that raped her, and she hates herself for being prejudice because not all men are rapists, and she hates the guy that raped her for making her think that all men are rapists, and she hates all men because she has the right to. And all I can do is stand there. Stand there and watch as Sarah walks away from me. In the rain. She could get raped again. It's almost like she doesn't care. And it's not like I can stop them all. You can never stop them all. 'No matter how many murdering, raping, lying, thieving, arsoning, loitering, slandering, corrupting, scandalizing, embezzling, frauding, assaulting, speeding, drinking, fucking, violent sons of bitches you put away, you're always going to screw up and let someone else get hurt.' The kid looks over at me. Kid Justice. Bringing the evil doers to justice. That's what he does. His idol, Johnny Heroic, wanting to be a hero. Wanting so hard to be able to save the day for everybody. I even like the word. Hero. But that's a power that will always be out of reach. 'Kid...we save one person down there, we're just going to miss another. Couple million people live below us. Some of them are honest, good people. Better than you and me. And some of them are kniving lunatics or just bastards.' 'Or Baptists.' God damn Baptists... 'Fact is, kid...the best you try isn't good enough. Good enough isn't good enough. And for every baby that we save from a firefight, there's two bastard children being made not a block away. For every rape we stop, someone gets killed. It's almost like the state of this city is perpetual Hell. And in a backwards way, chaos is its own equilibrium. And it keeps on returning to it, no matter how much people like you and I try to make it go away.' Kid Justice thinks about this for a minute. The harder you push, the harder chaos pushes back. Law, order, and ethics are ideas. And ideas can get lost in a society that has their own ideas. And for a minute, he realizes that he's sitting next to a dream. Johnny Heroic, a hero, is a phantom that will never exist. Nothing more than an idea. And society has more than lost me. 'Johnny, I-' 'Shut up kid. There's a crime going on somewhere below us.' Partly to spite him. Partly to spite myself. I leap off my perch and I polarize his molecules to float down about twenty seconds behind me. After going on a ride that I suggest to any that have the means, I crash into the ground and leave an impact crater the size of a car. But I'm not hurt. ...yeah. I have that power. Kid Justice is on his way down not far behind me. I guess the lesson is to keep on existing. And I can choose to sit on top of the building and do nothing. Or I can do something and fail. But it's my failure and my choice. 'Giddyup.'
