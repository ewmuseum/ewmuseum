Danger thinks about his return to GCW

9 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-9-08 The scene opens up in Danger’s hotel room at the Marriott in Winnipeg. Danger enters the room as he drops his bags near the door. WorldWide had just finished hours ago, and Danger is just getting in from a night of celebrating his return to GCW. He walks over and sits down in a chair in his luxury suite. He takes a deep breath and smiles as he thinks to himself about the upcoming week. He has been ready for the pure sport and competition that is GCW for quite some time, and now he’s finally back. He then gets a call on his cell phone. The call is from a long time friend in the wrestling business, Big Daddy Dero. Dero was one of Danger’s biggest stars in the old ACWF days, and was a two-time ACWF World Champion. The two have remained good friends throughout the years, but haven’t spoken in quite some time. Danger: Hey Big D, what’s happening?!”! I haven’t heard from you in ages! Dero: (On the other end) Hey Danger boy! It’s been a long, LONG time indeed. I was just channel surfing earlier and caught a bit of GCW WorldWide. I almost shit my pants when you came out man! What the hell brought this on? I had no idea you were ever thinking about returning to the ring!!”” Danger: Well I hadn’t thought about it in years dude, until just recently. I made a call to an old friend in Colombus and next thing you know I was on the show tonight. Maybe in the past few weeks I’ve let my emotions get the best of me. Dero: (Laughing) Come on dude, you can’t be serious about this! Tell me this is a one time deal man. Danger: I’m as serious as a heart attack brother!! I’m gonna do this, what do you think?!”! Dero: Well honestly, I think at your age you just might actually have a heart attack if you try and step back in that ring dude!! I mean come on, let’s be realistic here man .. Danger: Come on D! We have been friends for a long time, and you should know me better than that. When I say I’m gonna do something, I do it!! And I’m gonna put 100% of everything I’ve got into it. In case you didn’t see me on television tonight, I’m in the best shape of my life man! Dero: Yeah that might be true, but you’re no spring chicken anymore man and neither am I. I mean I’d love to make a comeback too but, I’m just trying to be realistic here. Danger: You don’t think I can do it, can you? Dero: Now, I didn’t say that, all I said was ? Danger: Well D, you’re one of my best friends I’ve ever had in this business. And if you don’t believe I can do this, I guess I’ll just have to prove you wrong just like everyone else! Dero: Well, what about the knee brother? How is it gonna hold up if you get back in the ring? Have you even consulted your doctors before jumping into this? And what about your neck problems?” How can you physically be prepared for this man? Danger pauses for a moment and there is silence. Dero knows the reason Danger’s in ring career was cut short was because of a career ending neck injury, as well as 2 knee surgeries. Danger: Everything will be fine man... It will be just fine? Dero: For your health’s sake, I certainally hope you’re right. Danger: This is something that I’ve wanted to do for a long time, and before time completely passes me by, I might as well take the opportunity before its too late man. Dero: Well man I hope you’re right. I just wanted to give you a buzz and see what all that was about tonight, I mean I still can’t believe you’re doing this!!”” Danger: I’m gonna do it D. You don’t gotta worry about you’re old boss. I know how to take care of myself. Dero: Well man, good luck to you. I hope you can find the answers to the questions that you’ve been looking for man. Talk to you later bro. As Danger hung up the phone he started thinking everything over again in his head. Maybe Dero was right? How will his neck hold up if he gets back in the ring? And what about the bad knee? He thought about it some more, and came to the conclusion that this was a risk he was willing to take. He just wondered what his wife might think once he gets back home to Kansas City tomorrow, as he had told her he was just flying in to WorldWide to visit some old friends? * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * The scene opens up the next day in Danger’s hometown of Kansas City. A limo pulls up outside his mansion as he gets out of the car. He walks up to his front door, and hesitantly opens it up. He knows he’s gonna be in deep shit for this. His wife found out with the rest of the world on national television about Danger’s return to the ring. He walks into the house and sees his wife Sherry sitting in the kitchen reading a book. As he enters the room, she basically ignores his presence. Danger: Honey, I know you’re mad about all of this... But it’s just something that I’ve got to do. I need to find out if I still have what it takes honey? Sherry keeps reading her book and is still ignoring him. Danger: I mean, I’ve thought it all over long and hard, and I just don’t think my life will be complete unless I find out for sure. Sherry glances up and gives him a cold hard stare. She looks pissed, and now Danger doesn’t know what to think. Sherry: Did you ever think about what this might do to your family? Did you for one second think about us? I understand your desire to be competitive honey, but what you’ve got to understand is that you have a family now. And with your neck problems and such how can you do this to us?”” Danger: Listen to me. You knew when you married me that I will always have a love for this business. Nothing is going to take that away from me, EVER! But over the past few weeks I’ve just been thinking a lot. I need to find out if I can still have what it takes to get in there and compete! Sherry: Well, just think about this for a second will ya? You’ve got a 6 year old son to worry about, and I don’t think he wants to grow up with his father in a wheelchair, or even worse, completely paralyzed maybe!!!! Danger: That’s not going to happen honey, I will make sure of it? I promise you nothing will happen. I mean, I ? Sherry: How can you say that?!”!”! You and I both know the reason you stepped out of the ring in the first place was because of the herniated disks in your neck. Well what happens if in you’re first match.. What happens if, if ? Sherry begins to cry histarically as she runs out of the room. Danger takes in a deep breath as he walks outside on his patio. Danger: I’ve gotta do this, I know I can still do it!! Danger walks over to a chair and sits down. He thinks about how everything in his life now is near perfect. But just like he had mentioned on WorldWide, not yet complete. He knows that he only has this one last chance. One more run at greatness. And there isn’t nothing that’s going to stand in his way?. NOTHING!!!!
