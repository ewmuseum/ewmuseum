The Riddled Dream (Prt2 of Grail)

17 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/17/2005 Sleep had come back rather easily. The golden dove resting comfortably within her silken sheet as once again the irridescent fog of dreams took hold of her mind and pulled her back into that confusing and most disturbing dream. Already she could hear that male voice echoing in the canals of her brain.. “But you did”, a warm timbre of a voice replied, “but you did.' 'Answers are questions, and the grail is a composite of both question and answer without end. And you thought the grail was dead but it cannot die if it never was, only if you conceive it to be what it is not, but is.' 'Great, I am here to listen to riddles?' She startled when he seemed to answer her unspoken thoughts. 'Do not think you threw yourself away. No you brought yourself here. You carried yourself to this place and like the ghost on your back, here is where you meet yourself and perhaps from this moment forth your journey begins.' 'Meet myself? Begin my journey, but I am here,' she thought bewildered. 'Everything is contained in beginnings. When you understand beginnings you will know about endings. To understand how something will end you must understand how it began. When you know how the universe began you may then conceive how it will end and in it’s ending what will then begin. You have much to learn, one day when you are ready you will begin.' 'It never ends?, his voice trailed off, “and it always begins. You can always return but you can never go back, not once you begin”. “Riddles”, she thought helplessly, “that’s all I need more fucking riddles” He spoke again, in a choral voice that seemed to imbue her senses with emotive sound and shifting, fragmenting color. Yet for all of that, for all the beguiling harmony of the sound, the very seduction of his voice and presence, his words were plain enough. Dismissive one might say. She wondered about that, beguiled she might be but a fool she was not, at least she didn’t think so. Later when her thoughts turned to beginnings it seemed to her that she had played the part of the fool most all her life, until she sank deep and deeper in beginnings that is and opened the Fourth Gate. He spoke in that incredible choral voice of his. “What do you want” Why are you here? Who are you? How did you find this place? What?, he demanded, “do you want?” 'Well excuse me for living', she thought sourly. Aloud she said, “Is this not the Castle of the Grail” Is this not the place where I might make all my dreams come true?” Odd she should say that, considering this was a dream. His eyes clouded, he stared at her in silence for a moment and then suddenly he began to laugh. Soft, husky laughter, like that of summer crouched in his voice, rang then fell away leaving the air in chilled silence. She shivered in fear and dread the warm, safe feeling inside fading away. Acid churned in her gut. Even so she was determined to stand, when all she really wanted to do was turn and run like hell. In the midst the discordant clamor he smiled in elusive radiance revealing even white teeth that seemed to gleam like lustrous pearls against the wan, bloodless flesh of his lips. At last he answered her inquiry in a somewhat melancholy voice. “You stand before the Tomb of the Grail. The Castle of the Grail is the romance of your own heart’s longing. It is your own hearts need flaring out from the want of your desolate soul. Beginnings, return from whence you came and begin. Your world is awash with pious men. Piety is but an extension of yearning want. Piety”, his voice sank down to a whisper “will cripple the soul and blind questing spirits to what it is. It ardently will turn the questor aside that in piety’s name stumbles down an unmarked path to nowhere.' 'No?, he said, “you stand not before the Castle of the Grail but in the shadow of its tomb. There is no Baptist here” he declaimed in a rising voice, “no reliquary of saint or martyr, no chalice, no splinters of the cross, no vial of sacramental blood. There are no David’s here, no Solomon or Moses stones, no yesteryear hallelujahs, only beginnings. It is only the hint of beginnings will you find here, and you are not ready to learn of beginnings”. “Well”, she said somewhat lamely, “if I am not ready for this then tell me what I am ready for?” She had a sinking feeling inside her gut that she had spent eight years of her life in pursuit of a fading belief. “Beginning.' “Bloody beginnings means I’m going to have to fucking start all over again. Still”, she sighed ruefully, “what else is new?” His deep blue eyes sparkled as though swirling stars lurked in their depths. He said softly, “There are no curdled prayers here, no dissatisfactions, no envies or greed, no shame, no humiliation or rage.' 'Pilgrim? he said to her in a return of his ringing choral voice, “The tomb of the Grail is the Fourth Gate in your path. Though it be not the last you will find no other on all this earth. You are not yet ready but know you this, when you pass through the Fourth Gate you may never go back. Return, oh yes you can always return but you can never go back”. More of the riddles as if his spiel about beginnings wasn’t enough. What he said next froze her blood. “Your grandfather went mad before he died. Darkness and Light you can deny, but you can never run. You can never hide from what resides inside. Darkness and Light are aspects of each other. They are one and the same and it is you who determine the difference.' 'You will become what you choose to be.' 'Of course I will,' Her thoughts came bitterly,'I never lose.' 'Darkness and Light are indifferent, and like prayer it is you who accord the direction and intent. Darkness and Light are aspects of the same, neither good nor evil. Knowledge may very well be found in both, it is up to you to decide in which direction you may want to go when you reach the Fourth Gate and enter. Know that madness, death, and spiritual decay will be your reward should you in error pass through the Three Gates that come before.' 'The Fourth Gate? he said in a final chilling tone “may very well allow one who is in error to enter, but it will never allow the erroneous to pass through. Go return from whence you came and think on beginnings. One day you will awaken to find light leaking from your unseeing eyes. Go now, the Fourth Gate is not for you, not yet” He finished with a ghost of a smile. Like an obedient schoolgirl she turned and set her footsteps back along the way thinking she might spend the remaining years of her life trying to return here again. 'You can always return,' he had said ,'but you can never go back'. 'Riddles,' she thought with a rueful grin, always the riddles. She turned to catch her last glimpse of the castle and its unearthly keeper to find there was nothing there. Absolutely nothing at all remained, which struck her as frighteningly strange. It was something to do with beginnings she expected.... Icy blue eyes surged open with a purpose, head twisting on that pillow to gaze at the clock when that dream released her. 11am. Groaning in frustration she rolled to the side of her bed and sat up hands coming up to rub the lingering sleep out of her eyes. 'What a fucking dream. What the hell was that all about?' She didn't know, not yet, at least. One day very soon she would know and understand. That day would be on Thursday, the day she would face the man who has held onto his belt for some time now. It was the day she planned to take it from him at whatever cost. It was her destiny...or was it? Only time would tell..only time would.
