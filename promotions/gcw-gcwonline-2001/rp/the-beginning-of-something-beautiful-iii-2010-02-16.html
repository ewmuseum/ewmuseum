The Beginning Of Something Beautiful III

16 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/16/10 I am Lori Equinox and I am writing this note to whoever will find it first. I don't know how long I have left on this earth because I am about to travel down the most unknown path of my entire existence. All my life I have been the one that has been in utter control of all my surroundings. No one tells me what to do. No one forces me to do anything. I choose my own destiny and I reap whatever consequences that I create. I have no fear for the future. I know I will die someday but as long as I know that I did everything in my power to lead the best life I could then I will be a happy man. Back when I was younger I discovered that my father wasn't actually that. My mother had an illicit affair with the man I knew then as my uncle. No one knew about this until my own mother told me so. She told me that it was for the best that I knew. Now even when I look back it wasn't for the best. My mind is corrupted at its core and the fact that she would even dare do that to me showed her true feelings. She didn't love me, I was always the problem child. Obviously a true mistake that she tried to hide for years. I had lost the man I who I believed was my father a year before that incident, it was hard on me but it taught me to be strong in who I am. He always believed in me, no matter whether I did good or bad. He always told me that I would become something special. Those words stuck with me through all my days. Making me strive to become something more, making me strive to be the best person I could possible be. After my mother told me the truth about my father I went into a rage, turned to my darker personality to help me along. I disowned her, even till this day she is regarded as nothing more than a shadow. Do I want sympathy for this? No, but hopefully it will help you understand why I am in the state of mind that I am. When I was sixteen years old I met a girl, absolutely beautiful. I had been residing in hostels throughout numerous cities for years. I never got adopted, I thought at that time that it would be foolish of me to place my trust in a new figure head. Within these hostels I met up with a girl who would only refer to herself as Elifor. It would be a long time until I would be allowed to call her by her true name, Elle. We did everything together from drinking, smoking, wrecking sh*t. At that time she the one I could always turn to. She would keep the dark side of my mind blanked out. Let's just say she brought the best in me, and she still does. When I became a wrestler she would always be by my side, travelling from coast to coast we always had the best times. I tell you this because she is the reason for my fork in the road. She is the reason I must adhere to others wishes. Her very life depends on it, and so does mine. I was told there will be consequences for all my actions. Hopefully the next time this note sees the light of day I will be reading it back, before throwing it onto an open fire. Watching it burn alongside all my afflictions. If you find this and I am no longer with the world, find my girl. -------------------------------------------------- Date: 15th February 2010 Location: Medical City Dallas Hospital Time: 10:00 am "He's in a stable condition but he took a couple of nasty blows to the head. Lori Equinox shoke his head a little before pulling his black 1978 Pontiac Firebird into the parking lot of Moritz Body Shop just off East Lamar Boulevard in Arlington Texas. The biggest event of GCW's calendar was playing big on his mind. About ten minutes before he arrived at the body shop he had passed the venue for NC-17. The Cowboy's stadium, less than a mile away. With the car firmly in place, Lori looked up into his rear view mirror. His face was pale and sickly. Not helped but his bright red hair set in front of his left eye. His eyes bore into his reflection as he contemplated that this could very well be his last night on earth. Pulling back the hair from his face he took a deep breathe before opening his door and practically vaulting out into the cold, crisp air. Closing the door behind him he leant back, resting his back against the window before lighting up a cigarette. Lori would hardly ever smoke but every so often it would have to be done. Just to calm the nerves just this once. He let each passing breath envelope his face in smoke as he savored the flavor, calming his system ever so slightly. This would be a situation that would not be for the faint at heart. Aside from Lori and his vehicle the parking lot was completely empty. Being so early in the morning all of the surrounding area was completely deserted, with only the occasional car passing by. Lori didn't notice anyone though as his mind began to focus on what lay ahead. A number of hours beforehand he had been contacted by one of Reo's men. Stating that he was to make his way to the Moritz Body Shop before half one in the morning before calling to confirm that he had arrived. He was told to come alone and not to tell anyone of his plans. With his cigarette finished, Lori threw it to the ground with haste and flipped his phone out of his pocket. Slowly but surely he dialed the number he was given by Reo's contact and waited for the answer. "I'm here." With those words passing from his lips Lori ended the call. A feeling passed over him that he had never once felt before, it was almost confusing to him. The uncertainty of the situation that he found himself had made him afraid and he didn't like it. Within minutes of his phone call a car could be heard in close proximety. Coming closer and closer Lori felt his anxiety grow to levels never known to him in his lifetime. As the sound became closer and more clear he could see the contact. A black limousine was driving towards him at great pace and on an intercept course. Closer and closer it came until Lori had no time to move. He didn't have to though as the driver slammed on the brakes at the final second, stopping inches from Lori's legs. A huge sigh of relief was let go from Lori and his dropped down the sit on the crowd, his back still perched against his card and his knees held against his chest. Two of the back doors of the limousine opened and Lori soon found himself being pulled up by two large gentlemen. Both dressed in black suits with black shirts. The first, a chiseled face blonde man who looked like he had been through the wars all his life. His expression was stern, one that would indicate that if anyone got on the wrong side of him there would be hell to pay. The other man was expressionless, his long black hair tied back behind his head. His look was one of a man who had no soul, a man that would do anything and not care one single bit about the problems he would face. Quickly they pulled Lori down the right hand side of the Limosine and opened the back door. It would not be an easy entrance to the limo as the blonde enforcer blasted Lori's head off the top of the open door before bundling him in. Both of the enforcers followed and quickly sat at either side of him, keep his arms at bay. The back of the limousine would have been a sight to have been see. That is if you were going out partying for a night with your friends or if you were rich and could enjoy it all day long. Nearest to the door there was a wide red leather couch with drinks holders in either end and speakers lining the top of it surrounded in gold plating. At either side of the compartment bars lined with whiskey, vodka, beer and tequila could be seen in large fridges. All illuminated with a shiny green light. The floor was also a wonder to behold as it was seethrough apart from a number of glistening lights embroiled within the interior of the glass. The roof had eight circular lights, making the whole place seem like just another room in a house. A very expensive room at that. Finally at the far end there was another couch where Lori was now sat, an enforcer at each side. Unfortunately all these things wouldn't matter as Lori Equinox wasn't there to enjoy the pleasantries of alcohol or the fine classical music which filled this magical place. "Drink?" Lori by now had regained enough of his senses after his brief encounter with the car door to look up from the floor to see a familiar sight looking back. "Surprised to see me?" A white tailored suit accompanied with a black shirt and tie didn't give away who it was that looked upon him but his mask. An unmistakable sight to any man who has been tormented for weeks and weeks. Lori was actually quite surprised and his voice was one of disappointment, with a hint of sarcasm thrown in. "In fact I am Reo. I thought you would have thought that you were going to send your bitches here to mess me up, even end my life. Who am I though to interfere with your master plan?" Reo clapped his hands together louder and a sharp laugh could be heard coming from within his mask. "And you are quite right Lori. There is a master plan and you are playing along with it quite nicely. I bet it tortures you to be a lapdog for once in your life. Being told what to do, having your actions controlled. It must be ripping you apart inside no?" Lori didn't know how to answer the question. He knew the answer but didn't want to let this man know it. Before he could even come up with an answer though Reo shouted out. "One!" Within an instant the blonde enforcer delivered a swift elbow to Lori's nose, sending his head backwards against the glass which splits passengers from the driver. His noise burst wide open with blood splattering all over his face and on the surrounding area. "Are you going to answer or what?" Reo's voice was now one of purpose as if he had enough of Lori's attitude. "You will answer the question now!" Lori moved his head down to look at Reo once more. Blood now dripping from the bottom of his chin. "I can't answer." "Oh will it hurt your pride too much Lori? Will you feel weak? Because this is a situation that you should feel powerless to." "I cannot show weakness, even in times of severe distress. I have to stay strong, it is the only way I have ever lived my life." Reo then moved forward a little on his seat, placing his arms on his legs and drumming his fingers together vigorously. "I told you a number of weeks ago that if you didn't win there would be consequences but I let it slide. I let you get away with it, Mayhem was a tough opponent and I should of put so much burden on your shoulders going into that match. And then last week you didn't even finish the match so I couldn't really do anything about that either." "Who are you working for? Donny? Surreal?" "Ha, don't think such nonsense Lori. There is far more happening that you know. Donny and Surreal and nothing to do with me. I have neither contacted them or nor have they contacted me. I work for myself and have been planning this little game for quite some time. "What do you mean? "All of that will be explained in time. I'm not like the villain in the movies who tells his nemesis his plan only to be thwarted at the last moment. I have always intended to tell my plan from the beginning." "Where's Elle?" "Oh your little girlfriend. She's fine, nothing to worry about. I only used her as bait to get your undivided attention. She will be returned to you as soon as all this is over. Problem is though that it could only be the beginning." "You better not have hu..." "You are in no position Lori to be making threats." Reo clapped his hands together once more and shouted out again. "Two" Yet another rifle like blow connected to Lori's face, this time to his jaw. His head snapped back like something out of a car crash. As Lori began to regain composure he saw Reo lift off the bottom part of his mask to reveal his mouth and jaw. His voice now unmuffled was almost soft and calming. "I don't like talking through this mask Lori but it needs to be done for now. Listen to me very closely. I have been watching you for years. Ever since you were twenty years old and got yourself onto the wrestling circuit. I have seen your rises and falls throughout the years. I have watched as you have become a pretty well known person in the wrestling industry. Why that is doesn't matter for now. All I want to do is see you reach the top. You have been on the mid level of your profession for far too long. Just imagine it this way. I dangle some bait in front of you to chase, in this case it's the safety of your beloved girlfriend and the actions you must take to ensure it. And then I have a stick, if you don't uphold your part of the bargain you get hit with it. When I say hit I mean beaten, incredibly hard. Which you will now find out. Goodbye." With that the two enforcers grabbed Lori and dragged him down the limousine. As the got to the door Lori looked on in disgust at Reo who waved him goodbye with a large smile on his face. Once outside the door behind them closed and Lori was thrown to the ground with force. With his face laying against the cold hard concrete Lori heard something extend and he braced himself for impact. With absolutely no remorse both enforcers began to lay into the ribs and neck of Lori with their police batons, beating him harder and harder into the ground. Lori tried to crawl forward, the only thing he could do but was soon cut off. The next sight he would see would be on black steel toe cap boots under his eyes. "Oh sh*t" With one final heavy blow, the blonde enforcer almost took Lori's head off causing him to pass out. "HEY!" Across the other side of the street a young man caught sight of what was happened. Knowing they had been spotted, the enforcers jumped into the back of the Limo and it turned to speed off into the distance. The young man came closer. He wore a black hooded jumped and some slack denim jeans. His hair was short and spiky with a hit of blue running through the centre of it. As he reach Lori he found him lying in a pool of his own bloods. "Oh man..." Quickly he brought out his cellphone and dialed 911. "Yes. I need an ambulance please." "I'm at Moritz Bodyshop on North Collins Street. A man has just been attacked." With that the man hung up and kneeled down beside Lori until the ambulance arrived to take him to hospital. -------------------------------------------------- Date: 15th February 2010 Location: Medical City Dallas Hospital Time: 12:00 pm "Thanks for calling me John.
