How you'll learn to stop worrying and love Eddie Whisky

8 February 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/08/12 Eddie Whisky sits at the center of a ring of folding tables in a scene reminiscent of some Stanley Kubrick movie. The Shining? 2001? Doesn't matter. Anyway, Eddie has several bright red rotary-dial telephones surrounding him. Behind him on the wall is a poorly drawn (finger-painted?) map of the globe. Let's assume it's earth, since Eddie has helpfully written "Earth" in a bold red marker. Eddie Whisky shouts into one of the phones. "No, dammit! I do not have $312 for library fines! Yeah? Then I’ll just change the numbers on my apartment! Oh yeah?! See if I borrow VHS tapes from you again, jerk!” Eddie slams down the phone. “Everybody wants a piece of Eddie Whisky it seems! And I know why! No matter how many times people try to imitate, duplicate or replicate, there can be only one Eddie Whisky!” Eddie stands up. Still can’t quite master sitting still. “But on the flip side, the one-and-only Eddie Whisky that people do have is me! And I will be the first person to tell you that the genuine article Eddie Whisky is a bit of a trouble to work with.” A phone rings and Eddie answers. “Yes, I am aware that is poor grammar! How did you get this number?” The offending phone is hung up upon forcefully. “Well tough noogies, GCW. The Eddie Whisky that sits here is the Eddie Whisky you get. I am a draw. People pay good money to watch Eddie Whisky—me—in the ring. And sure, the other schlubs in the locker room don’t like me. They say I’m obnoxious, and tend to wet myself when stressed out or excited. Waaa. I don’t care that you all don’t like me. What I do care about is Eddie Whisky. Another phone rings. Do analog phones even work anymore? Are they just an extenstion of Eddie’s psychosis? Hard to say… “Yes, I am interested in Sprint! Oh hey! We talked before, you and I! It’s me, Eddie Whisky! Hello-?” Dejected, Eddie puts the phone back down. “Where was I? Oh right, the GCW out to get me. Well, I am onto you, Powers-That-Be of GCW! You took one look at the upcoming schedule and realized that I—Eddie Whisky—am just two big shows away from being your world champion! Eddie walks over to his representation of “North America” which has a criss-crossed pattern of push pins joined by red strings. “See? It took me a while to cipher the pattern, but I now know why they are doing what they’re doing. Now that I know, I can thwart them.” Eddie first points to the pin representing St. Louis, Missouri, location for Dangerous Games 2012. “As you know, Dangerous Games is set to take place in a few weeks time. And as we all also know, the winner of Dangerous Games, which will be myself, will go on to face the World Champion at NC-17. And when I go to face the champion, I refuse to lose. In fact, the only thing I would lose at is not winning.” Another phone rings. Are they even connected to a land line? “It’s been over ten years! Oh sure, but you can quote Shakespeare and be called well-read. Quote the Simpsons and you’re derivative. Well, YOU’RE derivative! Not me!” Eddie tosses the phone against the wall. He nods, satisfied. “As I was saying, BEFORE I WAS RUDELY INTERRUPTED, I will be crowned the world champion at NC-17. And then the whole GCW will never be able to get rid of me until I retire undefeated in about thirty years from now. Tough bones, GCW. You painted yourself into a corner by letting me be in Dangerous Games in the first place.” Eddie Whisky sits down. And stands up again. He now points at the push pin denoting Kansas City, Missouri; the location for the upcoming WorldWide 158. “Oh, but no! The Conspiracy Against Eddie Whisky Holding the GCW World Title, or ‘CAEWHoGWT’ for short is a crafty shadowy cabal. They intend to feed Eddie Whisky, which is myself, to a wood-chipper of a match known as the Exploding Glass Razor Wire Roped Burning Thumb Tacks Bloodtacular Hardcore DOOM~! Match. And what a coincidence, that a match designed to injure, maim, hurt, spank and disembowel was created and booked JUST BEFORE Dangerous games.” Eddie sits down again. And stands up again. He now begins to pace. You know what, let’s just presume Eddie Whisky has some issues with his attention span and spare the details. “I have never had the pleasure of fighting in such a match before. But if the CAEWHoGWT thinks I am going to go fetal and whine about this, they have another thing coming! The theme for this upcoming WorldWide is one of nostalgia. Harkening back to the glory days of the late 90s, when an online porn search could take hours, and every second bloody wrestler was goateed, bald, and asked if I could smell what their ass was cooking. And they were all pimps. Well back in the late 90s, Eddie Whisky was eating up those little HaRdKoRe wannabe wrestling companies, and each one of them would throw these idiotic matches at me.” Sit. Stand. Pace. Frankly, it just makes for a break in the paragraphs. You’re welcome. “Xander Searle and Kasidy Drake both are very unlucky individuals. Yes indeed. Have either of them had to fight Eddie Whisky before? Nosiree. So they don’t even know what they are up against. I don’t need to explode glass or thumb tacks or doom them with bloodtaculation to beat them. All I need it this!” Eddie Whisky lifts one of his big boots towards the camera. “And these!” Eddie punches his fists at the camera. “Searle, Drake, You are complicit in the CAEWHoGWT by association now. You are trying to hold the Eddie Whiskys of the world down, and being the Eddie Whisky of the world myself, it falls upon me to cast off your chains of oppression! You cannot hold me down! I will overcome! The CAEWHoGWT is only as strong as its weakest link, Which are Xander and Kasidy! Two poor individuals who now have to face the full brunt of an Eddie Whisky onslaught! If I were you, I would be very worried. Luckily, I am not you, and thus am not worried!” A knock on an unseen door is heard. So too is an unseen voice speaking: "Whitowsky! You're five months behind on the rent, AND I never leased this place to you anyway! Get out of my root cellar!" Eddie looks furtively for another exit opportunity. "Uh... nadie aquí pero nosotros pollos!" Eddie Whisky tears down the map, revealing a painted over window. He begins to pry it open. He continues... "Sun Tzu once said 'know your enemy and you'll know yourself.' Or maybe it was Cobra Commander. Well regardless, I know myself well enough to know that I am smarter, faster, stronger and, uh, smarter than either Xander or Kasidy. Oooh. He spells his name with an 'x'. Is that suppose to impress me? Two types of people spell their names with x's in my books Searle; pretentious douches, and the French. And sonny, you don't sound French to me! And Kasidy! You think you're better than me because you're taller? Well your MOM'S taller than me! Hah!" Eddie finally succeeds in prying open the window. He clamors out. "No sir, the CAEWHoGWT will not beat me! They have bitten off more than chew. They picked the wrong Eddie Whisky to mess with! I'll see you at WorldWide AND at Dangerous Games! And rest assured, that means you sure as hell will see me at NC-17!" Eddie Whisky departs, leaving whomever is running the camera to face squatting bylaw fines...
