Date: 12/10/88 START 1 Year Old Fondest Memory: The Snowbank 6:30 pm It was December of 1988. I had already been wreaking havoc on my family for a year and three months. It’s funny how in such a short span of time one person can build up so much hate for another. I was just too young and irritable to even notice right from wrong. So? how was I supposed to know? That night? the night of the 10th day in December changed my life forever. All because of a sibling rivalry. Or lack of one. It was nipped in the bud before anything serious could ever take flight. Maybe it was because he knew I would end up being the one who would beat him. Maybe he was just scared. Or maybe he was just afraid of what he’d do to me if he didn’t put an end to it that quickly. Needless to say; we avoided most of it. But it created something else? something? different. Picture a typically chilly December evening. Snow from a few days had built up in the usual places, allowing for pedestrians and cars to go about their business. It even rained the day before, dampening the snow. Our family had just left Tony Roma’s Restaurant. We had quite the family dinner. I pretty much just stuck to my Momma’s nipple. Cheap and easy? I would have made a great slut if I were a woman. I remember the feeling of suckling on her breasticle. Yummmm! Tastes so fucking good. I could care less that Daddy’s having the back rib dinner. These titties are sporting a wonderful balance of protein and carbs that can’t be beat. I could just motorboat these mofos all day long! In fact, when we get back to the house, I think I’ll be up for a dessert round? BIG TIME! I think Chris had the kids size grilled cheese meal. He was super pants excite because his birthday was coming up in nine short days. I think I remember my Mommy talking to him about having a huge birthday bath. The conversation was definitely convoluted and muffled. I only had my Mom’s bosom to thank. Those were certainly grade A cock pillows guarding me. “Are you going to invite Chloe this time Chris?” My Mother asked. Chris chomped away at a French fry. It was so annoying to watch him eat. Especially French fries. He needed to learn how to close his dang mouth. His chewing sounded just like walking through a puddle. I’ll never forget that. “Why does she have to come” She’s like two years old Mommy! I want Jayce to come instead!” Chris argued. Of course you’d pick Jayce over your own cousin! And of all people? Jayce. Now there’s a winner. Wasn’t he the kid that came over when I was six months old and nearly pulled my ears off!” And you rather invite him to your party than your own flesh and blood. You should get birthday and Christmas presents clumped together just for that. I guess that’s really the only thing I held over Chris. Clumping. I mean? it would suck. But he was so mean to me. He deserved clumping the few times he got it. When he was three he got clumped because he tried to use a Lego piece as a spoon. He ended up swallowing the Lego. Served him right. “Well we’ll see. Chloe’s going to be in town with her parents. I already have plans with Carrie, so it’d just be easier,” Mommy replied. Aunt Carrie. Now there was a quality broad. She squeezed my cheeks so hard that it felt like my lips burst open. She’s not your typical Aunt. She’s a keeper. An awesome person. The one family member that no matter what; you can always have fun with. My Uncle Clay was one lucky guy that’s for sure. “So, did you guys get what I wanted for my birthday?” Inquired Chris. My Mother laughed sarcastically. My knucklehead Father slurped his side order of spaghetti like a mutant consuming brains. I just kept on feeding. Chris sat with his pouty face on awaiting an actual reply. “Stop laughing! Stop it. Or I’ll smack that breast attachment of yours! Little shit. I hate him!” Whined Chris. I peered over at him with my soft, innocent baby eyes. He locked onto me like a missile. So much for being born as his playmate. My Mother regained her composure and put my brother in his place. “That’s quite enough young man! Watch your tongue. You have no right to threaten anyone like that! Just for that, you’ve lost one present. And you’re not allowed to know what they are until the nineteenth, do you understand me?” My Mother directed. All that did was make Chris sulk and throw a fry to the floor as he crossed his arms. The next thing I remember was getting up to leave. My Father led the way although he nearly bumped into a few couples on the way out. It wasn’t his fault that his eyes shot off in two different directions. No one knew where he was truly heading. I was done feeding by the time we got out into the cold environment. I had a cute little baby blue beanie hat on. Boy, I sure loved that hat. My mini snowsuit was even cuter. It had a picture of Glo Worm on it. I was fed, burped and tired. All I wanted to do was go home and drift off to fantasy land. But? it wasn’t as easy as that. “Here Chris, take your brother for a moment will ya?” My Mom said as she forcefully handed me over to Chris. He held me in his arms like a rookie cop with his gun. I still felt love. And a tiny bit of cuddling. I was his brother after all. I still am not too sure as to what my parents were doing though. Everything is still a little shady. “Listen. You need some help. You don’t even know where we are! You fuck around too much Casey! Every night you fucking come home drunk and smelling like weed. You have two kids and all you’ve become is some lazy fucking mute! I know happened in the ring. But you’re taking it to the extreme! If you can’t fucking get a grip, then I’m taking the kids and we’ll never see you again! Is that what you truly want to happen?” My Mother screamed. We were somewhere between the sidewalk and the parking lot when it all went down. Everything seemed so long? so far away. Whatever my Mommy blurted, my Daddy certainly didn’t reply. What are you doing Daddy? Don’t just stand there! Defend yourself! Tell her you’re not that guy. You don’t smoke up do you Daddy? I’m barely one year old? I need a Father in my life. Don’t destroy this family? please don’t! Chris drew me in closer and started to slowly back away from our parents. I knew something was up as soon as Chris whispered to me. “I’m tired of you. I don’t want a brother anymore. You’re just a retard,” Chris murmured. I looked over at Mommy. “Hold the keys Casey. I need to look for our car you lost,” Mommy stated. I reached out with both arms as Mommy walked off into the distance and left me with the two most sadistically messed up people in my life at that time. I then looked at my brother who had the biggest shit-eating grin waxed on his face. My Daddy just stood there and stared off into the distance. Chris carried me over to a snowbank. What are you going to do brother? Wait! Stop! Daddy is right there! Help me Daddy! You can’t let him do this to me! I’m the little brother! I’m supposed to be loved! Love me! LOVE ME! Chris cleared a spot in the snowbank. It almost mimicked that of a prison cell. He leaned forward and peered into my faithful eyes. His coldness transferred from his eyes to mine as if I consumed part of his evilness. “Now you can’t screw my life up. Mommy and Daddy are all mine,” Chris finalized. And then? he covered it up good. It was so dark in there. Womb-like almost. Except? colder. The pain I felt that day can never be equaled. After my burial, my brother ran over to my Fathers side. My Mother was quick to return with the car. They piled in as my Mother was still blinded by anger with my Father. Once they got home my Mother realized that I was missing. My Father had no explanations? in fact he had nothing to say at all. So she blamed him, took Chris and moved out. Ending their marriage and securing Chris? innocence. I was the reason our family went to hell. It was because I was born that I alienated my brother who in turn pushed my Mother over the edge. But she didn’t even know it. She blamed Daddy. Everyone said it was my Fathers accident that was the true catalyst to the end. But somehow I just can’t forgive myself for that day. Maybe? if I was never born? they would have never fought? and Chris would have been happy. Maybe my life shouldn’t have been. Am I going to die here? Cold, alone in a snowbank? Do I deserve this? What did I do to deserve this? I cried. Loudly. Constantly. I was in that snowbank for what seemed like forever. And as if I had a guardian angel; someone scooped me out of it and took me home. Took me to their home where I would become the Clyde Fox I am today. I was destined for that life. I wasn’t meant to be my parent’s child. And somehow? God knew that. I just wish it wasn’t at the cost of their marriage. END
