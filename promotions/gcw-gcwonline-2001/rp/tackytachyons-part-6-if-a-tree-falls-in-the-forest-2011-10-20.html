Tacky–Tachyons Part 6 (If a tree falls in the forest…)

20 October 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/20/11 October 10 1997 6:30 am The room was dimly lit as the morning sunlight peaks through the cracks of the window's curtains. 13 year old Bradley lies in bed clutching his blanket. His door slowly opens as his Grandfather quietly enters the room. He walks over to window opening the curtain to let the light in. He turns to Bradley’s bed and reaches down giving Brad’s shoulder a light nudge. Grandpa Cruz: "Bradley, It’s time to get up." Bradley rolls over covering his head with his blanket. His Grandfather gives him another nudge. Grandpa Cruz: "Come on, you’re going to be late for school." Bradley just grumbles incoherently. Grandpa: "You need to get up." Brad: "Do I have to?" Grandpa Cruz: "If you want that PPV at the end of the month, yes." Brad shoots up to a seated position with his eyes still completely shut. He rubs his eyes removing the gunk that formed around them while sleeping. He opens his eyes revealing two bright little sapphires embedded in a drowsy distressed little face. Brad: "… but you said if I kept the yard up we’d get that PPV." Grandpa Cruz: "Yes, along with your other responsibilities which includes: Manners, behavior, and anything having to do with school. You have enough tardies thanks to your mother and father, no more on my watch." Brad gives a disappointed sigh. Brad: "I’m up, but under protest." His grandfather shakes his head and chuckles. Grandpa Cruz: "Oh-me-oh-my, I was referee for over 30 years, and I’ve had my fair share of meat-heads protesting my decisions, and no offense kiddo, your no Bruiser Brody." He shoots a wink to his gloomy Grandson who couldn’t help but crack a smile. Grandpa Cruz: "Did you take a shower last night, like I told you to?" Brad nods and his Granddad smells and then kisses him on the top of his head. Brad: "Yup." Grandpa Cruz: "Alright, good boy, get ready and come down for breakfast. Oh, and your mother is home, she came in around five thirty this morning from New York. Be sure to tell her good morning and give her a hug and a kiss." Bradley: "Kay." Grandpa Cruz: "I’ll have a nice bowel of "Boo Berry" waiting for you." Grandpa Cruz walks out the room headed for downstairs. Brad jumps out of bed to throw on his school uniform (navy blue polo and kaki slacks), brushes his teeth, and spikes his hair. Bradley goes from his bathroom and quietly creeps to his parent’s room where he can see the light peaking around the door that’s only closed over. Brad peaks in and sees his mother with her back to him unpacking her suit case and dry cleaned clothing as she sings "Memory" from Cats whimsically to herself. Brad chuckles. Maris: "Memory… All alone in the moonlight… I can smile at the old days… I was beautiful then." She reaches back down into suitcase picking up a black cocktail dress that she holds against her body like a dancing partner. She sways to the left and right revealing a white long full length mirror. Bradley it catches Brad’s eye hitting him with a sense of dejavu followed by dizziness as images of a MAN standing in front of that MIRROR wearing a WHITE SUIT flash through his young mind. He shakes the dizziness off as best he can, the same dizziness he felt only two days before in class. He stumbles into the room forcing the door open. His mother is vainly staring at the mirror with her dress against her willowy frame. With her attention on the mirror Brad was able to compose himself before his mother notices his existence. Maris: "My little Darling is that you?" She keeps her eyes on the mirror. Brad answers solemnly. Brad: "Yes mother." Maries: "Mother-?" Brad remembers the small silly title he gave his mother in a fit of sarcasm. A small silly title she missed the point of entirely. A small silly title she adored and demanded. Brad: "Yes, Mother Dearest." She turns to her son with a big smile on her face. Maris: "Darling!" She wraps her arms around her son giving him big hug followed by a volley of kisses to right cheek. When she lets him go Bradley rubs his check almost immediately, not just to remove her lipstick, but to get some feeling back in his face. She turns back to the mirror this time modeling for herself a white evening gowned. Maris: "Oh how I’ve missed you, Darling. You don’t know what good it does my spirits to be home with you. Momma wishes she could have taken you to New York with her, but you had school." Brad: "I hate New York…" Maris: "I promise we’ll go this summer, but in meantime I brought you all sorts of goodies and gifts I know you’ll love." Maris: "How’s school?" She asks still giving her attention to the mirror. Brad: "All right I guess. My grades are good, and I have a science experiment due next month…" Maris: "That’s nice dear…" Bradley wonders if his mother is really listening to him. Brad: "Ms. Shultz says if I get an ‘A’ I won’t just get certificate for Mimi’s café she’ll also elope with me to a country that will over look our age difference, so we can start a family of our own." Maris: "That’s nice dear, Mommy’s auditions went well a- did you say elope?" Bradley just smiles. Brad: "I said envelope. You know, the envelope with a certificate for Mimi’s Café they give away very year for participation?" His mother raises a confused eyebrow. Maris: "Oh, right." Brad: "Well Grandpa is waiting with a bowel of "Boo Berry" cereal. Maris: "Alright Darling, come give Momma a kiss. I wish you would eat the Grape Nuts cereal I bought you." Brad gives her a kiss on the cheek. Brad: "I don’t trust cereal that doesn’t float and tastes like something on the bottom of a hamster’s cage. Bye!" Bradley rushes out the room and his mother continues her private fashion show. 7:30 am A large black Cadillac drives into the St. George Elementary parking lot pulling into the drop off zone. Bradley hugs his Grandfather good bye and jumps out of the car with his backpack slung over one shoulder. The car pulls out and drives off disappearing into residential street. Brad walks toward his class building walking through children his age waiting for the bell. 8:00 am Bradley sits the middle of a classroom full of children talking amongst themselves; Bradley couldn’t be bothered to acknowledge any of them as he was too immersed into his latest issue of Dead Pool. The teacher rises from he desk and begins the morning lesson. Bradley closes his comic and moves on to an issue of Star Trek Deep Space Nine. Ms. Shultz: "Today in the spirit of higher learning we will forgo our morning Literature lesson for something of a different pace. With principle Trahern’s permission today we will begin with our first ever class on the subject of Philosophy." Ms. Shultz begins to mark what she says on the board. Ms. Shultz: "Philosophy is the study of general and fundamental problems and basic concepts such as truth, existence, reality, causality, and freedom. People have dedicated their lives pondering existence as a whole trying to make sense of the world around them. Does anyone know why?" A young girl in class raises her hand, Mrs. Shultz calls on her. Lauren: "To help people?" Ms. Shultz: "Yes, but help them how, anyone?" William raises his hand. William: "To understand?" She begins to write what she says, on the board. Ms. Shultz: "To understand why we EXIST, what is REAL, what is CAUSES certain events, and weather we are truly in control of our own ACTIONS." She turns her attention to Brad who is still reading comic. Ms. Shultz: "… and why is it important for people to understand all these things Mr. Cruz?" Bradley replies without even looking up. Brad: "To boldly go where no man has gone before?" The children in class giggle, but Ms. Shultz doesn’t seem to be too amused. Ms. Shultz: "More to "Understand what man was unable to… before. For example Mr. Cruz, wrap your mind around this ancient riddle, ‘what is the sound of one hand clapping?’" Bradley tilts his head and raises and eyebrow. Brad: "Are you making fun of the handicapped?" Ms. Shultz is taken back. Ms. Shultz: "No of course not!" Brad: "Because that would be wrong." Ms. Shultz: "Well I assure you I’m not." Brad: "Are you sure you can assure?" Ms. Shultz: "YES, please answer the question." Brad rubs his chin as he ponders. Brad: "Can I substitute the missing hand?" Ms. Shultz: "I beg your pardon?" Brad: "Like with a thigh? Thighs make good noise, like thunder! -or maybe on your forehead, you could crack it like a snare drum!" Ms. Shultz: "I think you're missing the point…" Brad: "… or you could simply clap one hand into the other. Technically it’s still only one hand doing the actual clapping… and it would sound the exactly same." Ms. Shultz: "Is that your final answer?" Brad: "I don’t know, that forehead drum would be pretty sweet. Plus I’m not entirely convinced you aren’t making fun of the handicapped, so on second though I don’t feel comfortable answering the question, and as an added note I don’t think I’ll ever be able to look at you in the same way." Ms. Shultz: "Well Mr. Cruz, you and I can discuss this further, after class." Brad slumps in his chair as the teacher passes out Xeroxed pamphlets. Ms. Shultz: "I want you all to research and write a paper on the ‘philosophical riddle’ I chose for you. You’ll find I scribbled it on the last page of the assignment packet. Hopefully Mr. Cruz it won’ offend your sensitive sensibilities." Bradley thumbs through the packet on to the last page and sees his riddle. Bradley whispers to himself. Brad: "‘If a tree falls in the forest and no one is around to hear it, does it make a sound?’" Bradley just shakes his head and proceeds to take notes on what’s on the board. 6:45 PM Bradley sits at home watching Television in the living room. He had finished his crucial homework a few hours ago and now he was watching a rerun episode of ‘Married with Children’ when his mother calls to him for dinner. Bradley gets to his feet and walks to the dining room and takes a seat next to where his mother would sit. Grandpa Cruz sits across from Bradley and the head of the table is set for Brad’s Father. Mrs. Cruz begins to serve her son’s plate. Maris: "Your father is on the phone in the den, and told me we shouldn’t wait just for him, so here you go, my little Darling. It’s a chicken cutlet salad and your favorite pasta with alfredo sauce." Brad: "Thanks mom!" Brad smiles even though it really wasn’t his favorite. His favorite was actually ravioli, but there was no reason to correct his mother. It would just ruin her dinner and she would sulk about not really knowing her son and promise to make it up to Bradley, only to forget the next day and default back to alfredo being her son’s favorite. She strokes Brad’s hair fondly. Maris: "Please chew your food properly. It’s not a race." She then serves Granddad and her husband covering his plate with other plate to keep warm. The three enjoy their meal and are about finished when Mr. Cruz makes his way into the room taking his place at the head of the table. His wife greets him with a kiss as he sit down. He lifts the plate and looks down at his food. Martin: "Thanks, looks good." Maris: "How was your day, Dear?" Martin: "We got some really promising grapplers in the program for this season. Hopefully they keep their nose in their books and their asses out of trouble. Last thing I need is another trouble maker ruining a perfectly good season. Speaking of which Bradley how was school?" Brad promptly swallows his bite of food before answering. Brad: "Well you know, normal day… things were learned, fun was had, the usual." Maris: "Oh how nice." Martin: "You sure about that?" Brad notes his father’s tone and realizes that he may know something, but Bradley decides to call his bluff. Brad: "No." Martin: "Really? Then why did your teacher call me saying you were disruptive again?" Maris: "Is this true, Brad? Why?" Brad: "I don’t know, maybe it’s because we had a class on philosophy and I had some very controversial opinions about thunder thighs, forehead drums, and hands that could clap themselves. Besides are you really going to believe her? She hates the handicapped." Brad’s Father slams the table with his fist shaking the dinner ware resting on its surface. Martin: "Damn it Brad, what have I told you!?" Brad looks down at the table as his father shakes his head. Martin: "Brad, what have I told you. Answer me." Brad: "No body what’s to hear the nonsense that comes out of my mouth." Martin: "You’re there to learn. You think your teachers keep bread crumbs to find their way back after you completely derail things? They don’t, they shouldn’t have fight through you to teach their classes." Brad’s Mother places her hand over her son’s hand. Maris: "Dear I know this seems a little harsh, but you really must stop this. People talk and I don’t want them to think my little darling is a loud mouth. We have a reputation in this town." Martin: "Not just that, how’s being an obnoxious loud mouth going to help in the long run? It’s not like you can make living on it. What you need to do is keep your nose in those books. Speaking of which, your Teacher said she gave you and assignment something about ‘A tree falling’" Brad doesn’t look his parents in the eyes and continues to look down at the table as he answers his father. Brad: "It’s a paper of a riddle ‘If a tree falls in the forest and no one is around to hear it, does it make a sound?’, but it’s not due for a week." Martin: "Well you’re going to work on it now, and apologize to your teacher in the morning." Bradley get to his feet a walks around the table and his father grabs him by the arm. Martin: "Shape up, and stop being a disappointment." Grandpa Cruz gets to his feet and hits the table with his palms as he rises. Grandpa Cruz: "Enough! Is this how you want your son to remember you? As the man that was disappointed in him. He’s a good kid, a little long in the mouth but a good kid." Martin: "Well of course you would defend him he’s just like you." Grandpa Cruz: "I’m not surprised I send more time with him then both of you put together. You should be ashamed with yourselves how little you seem to concerned about your son’s day to day care" Martin (father) and Maris (mother) are insulted by Grandpa Cruz’s statement. Martin puts his arm over his wife as her eyes begin to tear up. Martin: "When Brad goes to bed, you and I need to talk." Grandpa Cruz guides Bradley out of the room and into the living room. Bradley pulls out assignment packet and begins to work on his paper sitting at a small desk. Grandpa Cruz looks over him as his Grandson begins to work. Brad looks down at the desk sniffling and fighting back tears. Grandpa Cruz: "Are you alright, Kiddo." Brad keeps his eyes down to his paper and nods. Grandpa Cruz: "Look at me." Brad turns his attention his Grandfather who promptly gives him a hug. Some tears stream down Bradley’s cheeks, but he quickly composes himself. Brad: "I know I say things I shouldn’t and when I shouldn’t, but I really don’t mean to. They just build in my head and when I see opening it just kind of flies out of my mouth. It’s just the way I am." Grandpa Cruz: "I know kiddo, but you need work on it, because that great way to get your eyes blacked in high school." Brad cracks a smile. Grandpa Cruz: "… and by the way your dad isn’t disappointed in you, just disappointed in the some of the choices you made." Brad: "That’s not what he said…" Grandpa Cruz: "…but that’s what he meant. He’s just too heavy handed with his words. Just like his mother. Anyways put that aside and lets work on this paper. ‘If a tree falls in the forest and no one is around to hear it, does it make a sound?’ Well does it?" Brad: "I don’t know. Mom says no one could know, because there’s no one around to witness it. What do you think?" Granddad: "I don’t know, philosophy always went over my head." Brad: "You have to have an opinion on the subject." Grandpa Cruz smiles. Grandpa Cruz: "I do, but it would be better you come to your one conclusion." 11:55 PM Bradley lies in his bed unable to sleep. His Grandfather who tucked him in at ten thirty, has been arguing with his son Martin Cruz (Brad’s father) for the past hour and twenty five minutes. It’s never been this bad before. They shout at the top of their lungs voices muzzled and distorted by the walls of a once peaceful familie's home. Brad lies on his side clutching his blanket. Brad couldn’t help but blame himself for the state of his family. Suddenly a loud slam is heard from his parent's room and then hard hitting footsteps trailing to his Grandfathers room followed by a door opening and slamming. Brad quickly jumps out of bed and tip toes to his door opening very quietly. He peeks out into the hall looking at his parent’s room door for any sign of light. It was pitch black. He looks to his Grandfathers room and sees the light on. Brad creeps across the hall like a ninja well familiar with the anatomy of the floor avoiding all the creaky spots. He makes it to the door and knocks very, very lightly followed by a slight whisper. Grandpa Cruz: "Grandpa… Grandpa… I can’t sleep." Bradley opens the door and what he sees puts him in complete shock. His Grandfather lay on the floor grasping his left arm and breathing hard. Bradley drops to his knees and proceeds to shake his Grandfather in hopes to get a response. His Grandfather eyes open and he looks straight up to his Grandson and he smiles. He reaches out asking for his hand. Brad speechless reaches out taking the hand of his Grandfather who pulls Brad in close and in whisper says. Grandpa Cruz: "The trees…" Brad doesn’t understand. Grandpa Cruz: "…of course they make a sound… just because you don’t hear it doesn’t mean it never happened. That’s the problem with the people in this world. It’s just too easy to ignore everything and anyone… as if IT or THEY never happened. Don’t be that way, see things for what they are and make some noise. Make them hear you. Whether they want to or not. Never stop being you. Do you hear me?" Tears stream down Brad’s cheeks as he nods. Brad: "Yes, I hear you." Grandpa Cruz: "I always knew if there was a chance someone could, it would be you. I love you Brad." Brad: "I love you to Grandpa." His eyes close, his grip weakens, and he’s gone. Bradley begins to sob uncontrollably and tears through the silence with a loud yell. Brad: "MOM-DAD!!!!" Date: N/A Time: N/A The grey sky swirls with billowy clouds over a large desolate open field that seemly goes on forever. In the dirt lays "the SoCal Prophet" Brad Cruz chained to the ground with a dark black cloud like halo over his head. "Oh Bradley, poor sweet innocent Bradley, how I misjudged you. I thought you where this complicated maverick marching to the beat of his drummer." The Mysterious Voice stops as a man materializes from nothingness. There stands the powerful M, the man looking to steal the body of the SoCal Prophet for himself. M: "I find you to be just young boy starved for attention. That developed a razor sharp silver tongue as defense mechanism. I have to admit, I’m disappointed." He looms over Bradley and with gesture lifts him up chained to a structure similar to a cross but in the shape of a Cruzifix, Bradley’s symbol. M: "…but don’t worry you still have your final chapter, a chance to wrap it all in neat little bow." A devious smile comes across the lips of M. M: "I assure you Mr. Cruz, you're going out BIG! Right everybody?" M glances back showing a figment of Bryan Mayhem, Slider (cousin), Eko, Crystal (fiancé), Martin Cruz (father), Maris Cruz (mother), and Sylvester Cruz (Grandfather). They all give M a nod. M: "Excellent."
