Wipeout: Part 1

8 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/08/09 (takes place after Shockwave) Shockwave (immediately following the 6 man tag match) “We did it!” ‘We’ being the trio of Tessa Windsor, Vivica J. Valentine, and Lindsay Troy, who were on their way back from the ring. It is Tessa who lead the charge, flashing her pearly white teeth at everyone who walked by. If it were not for the fact that she had just gone out there and wrestled one of her best matches, one would swear that Tessa was high, or drunk, or both. “We won!” Far too ecstatic, Tessa Windsor unexpectedly leapt towards Vivica J.Valentine leaving Vivica scrambling to catch both her balance and Tessa. Thankfully, having plenty of experience with both, Vivica was easily able to do so. It is the excessive joy pouring out of Windsor that the Fearless Phenom was not quite sure how to handle. Lightly embracing her close friend, Vivica wondered aloud, “You didn’t think we could?” Complete denial oozed out of Tessa, who was grinning from ear to ear. Her denial had little to do with doubting Troy or Valentine’s abilities in the ring; she knew the two of them along could easily dismantle all three of their opponents on their own, with it. Any bit of denial she had, stemmed much more so from herself and her ability to contain her composure against two men who admittedly knew her better than she knew herself, which no doubt left her being the weakest link of the trio, “Of course I knew we could!” After releasing her light embrace, Vivica looked back up at Tessa with her own grin that Tessa was well accustomed to, “Way to save your ass.” “I try.” Vivica casually smiled as she came to a stop beside Tessa, who was now right outside her locker room, “Too hard sometimes.” Tessa’s eyes rolled up and around in a very sarcastic but still caring manner. She had heard the words come out of Vivica’s mouth a dozen times. And if Vivica did not say it, then she heard it from Lestatt Knight, and if not him then it was her own children. There was always someone heckling Tessa about how she never slept enough, or how she was pushing herself far too hard, or that she was going to end up killing herself trying. It never ended even though year seven years into her career, Tessa Windsor was still up and moving quite fine. “Okay mom, I’ll be more careful.” Vivica’s eyes mimicked the same pattern Tessa’s had just taken. While her and Tessa were extremely close, she always hated how lightly Tessa took to her words sometimes. The two were much more like sisters than best friends. Hell, they even matched up in size. “You want to meet up back at the hotel later tonight and hang for bit?”Vivica inquired, temporarily propping herself up against the wall outside of Tessa’s locker room. “We’ll see, call me later.” Vivica tilts her head in acknowledgement before gently pushing the door shut, knowing that Tessa would in all likelihood appreciate the peace and quiet. It was not often that any of the three got a moment to themselves, especially at times like this when everyone wanted to enquire about their inner feelings coming off a huge win as if they giant smiles plastered across their faces did not tell it all. “We did it.” Tessa reassured herself once more, this time in a whisper. Quite awkwardly she glanced over at her reflection and made sure it followed her lead, as if she were expecting this to be some sort of dream where it would suddenly reach out and slap her. Then again even if her reflection did stay put, chances were it was nothing more than a daydream. It’d been so long since Tessa was on the fast track to success that it felt as if any second now her eyes would force themselves open, her head beginning to throb, after having been beaten down by every last member of The Establishment. “So glad that’s over with.” Both Valentine and Troy likely had the same sentiments. While the talent well spread amongst the three of them was highly evident, there was never a lack of criticism from some other source. If not from colleagues, than the media, and if not them, then the small group of loyal fans of Banned & now Estalishblished~!, who refused to part with the idea that perhaps someone could part them from tag team titles. Rather than take the time to shower before heading back to her makeshift home in her luxurious hotel suite, Tessa headed for the door figuring she’d catch a shower when she got in. Not only was the shower back at her suite cleaner, it was ten times less likely that someone would angrily bust in on her there. “Aloha privacy.” She amusingly whispered to herself as she headed for the door. Not that she minded the company of Vivica Valentine or Lindsay Troy, just sometimes it was nice to have a moment to herself to recollect her thoughts and analyze them aloud without someone having an opinion to chime in. Flinging her oversized gym bag over her tiny shoulder as she pulled the locker room door shut behind her, Tessa failed to see the young gentleman strolling down the hall past her locker room at that very same moment. Standing right at the six foot mark, it’d be hard for anyone to miss him. Whatever it was dancing around in Tessa’s mind that left her distracted kept her there for a good long while, and by the time she snapped out of it was already too late. “Oh shit!” Rather than turn and apologize to the young man she had just plowed straight into, she quickly scrambled to cover her gaped mouth with her hands. She was far more worried about her language than any kind of physical harm she might have caused him; after all she was barely one hundred and twenty pounds. What kind of physical harm could she do to him? Not only was he several inches taller, his chest seemed to be sculpted well enough to take harder hits than that. Just as she expected, the man hardly seemed phased. Instead of cussing her out like most in this neck of the woods would have, she closely watched as he visibly scanned her up and down to make sure she was okay. Without any further thought, Tessa blurted out the obligatory appropriate response for this type of situation, “I’m so sorry!” What she got was the usual casual response from a stranger, “Don’t worry about it. It happens.” Tessa lightly smiled, hoping this would be her chance to escape from this awkward situation. Based on the shade of red her cheeks currently were, one would have assumed that she had taken a spill on national television. Then again she had taken plenty of public spills and even then her cheeks had never managed to match the shade they were now. Just that lone factor right there made it that more imperative that she get out of there pronto. Instead, as she was famous for doing, Tessa Windsor only managed to get more herself tied further up in matters, nearly tripping on her own two feet as she attempted sly escape Just before she could reach the door, the young man she had just stumbled over called back out to her, “What about you, are you okay?” “It happens.” She opted to mimic back with a shrug and a smile. In her case she was being completely honest. Things like that always happened. They also always lead to her trouble. She kept that thought buried deep in her head before turning it back towards the exit door. She smiled once more in his direction before pushing the threshold, welcoming the tropical breeze with that same smile. Before she can completely escape, she hears him call out to her once more. “You need some help?” “I’ll be fine.” “You sure?” It was hard to convince him otherwise considering she nearly stumbled over herself once again. Before the former U.S. champion could answer his question, she found herself having to suddenly dodge a pair of press members who had planted themselves right outside the exit doors awaiting the chance for a cover page type of quote from any unsuspecting superstars. Unfortunately for Tessa, a good deal of the time it was her making those pages, and for all the wrong reasons. “Shit.” This time Tessa made sure to keep her foul language to herself, knowing anything that could be perceived as negative press could and would be used against her favor. As difficult as it was for her to pull off, the Petite in Pink somehow managed to dodge both the reporter to her left and to her right. Only to walk straight into another who seemingly came out of nowhere. “Ms. Windsor!” He delightfully proclaimed, as he’d known her for years,” I was wondering if I could get a word with you about your team’s victory over the Establishment.” What did he expect her to say? It sucked? That she totally would have rather to see the Establishment steal another win? Tessa certainly was not the brightest bulb of the bunch when it came to appropriately wording things, but even a three year old could have predicted the answer she gave. “It was great.” Why the slender man standing before her needed to jot those words down was beyond Tessa. It seemed like a simple enough answer. Then again, in all likelihood given his job description, he’d probably return back to his cubicle tonight and find some way to twist those words into something much worse, or better in his case, than the mere, ‘it was great’. Tessa sidestepped to her right, only to have that same slender reporter standing before her suddenly jump to the same side, not once looking up from his small pad of paper, “And Vivica and Troy? How are they feeling?” Her patience remained well intact despite the fact that she was being quizzed on the obvious, “Thrilled too, I imagine.” This time her feet moved to the left. Carefully eyeing the man before her for any sign of movement she slowly edged her right foot forward. Then her left. Then her right again. Her head turned to the side, half expecting the male reporter to be stuck to her side like glue. Instead, he had managed to find his way right back out in front of her again. “And what about the Establishment? Do you have any words for or about them?” This time she stood in place to contemplate her next move, “Absolutely none. I think we made a loud enough statement out there this evening.” “That’s it? What about Chris Storm?” Her eyes dropped down towards the ground some at the mention of her ex fiancé who’d long since drifted away from her. Even though she hated to talk about her obvious weakness, she still made her best attempt to answer, “What about him?” “How does it feel to be out of his shadow?” Reporters. They were always assuming the most nonsensical stuff. It’s why they were every wrestler’s biggest bane. Almost never does the press bother have their facts straight. If it was not likely that somewhere in the depths of the parking lot a camera was tucked away, Tessa would have bawled her tiny fists up and angrily stomped away. Instead, she was forced to answer the question the best she could, without being called out on a bluff. “Maybe he’s the one caught in his own shadow.” She meekly suggested with a very slight shrug of her shoulders, “After all, did that really look like the kind of performance a record holding tag champion should be putting on? Same with Markus Stone. And I have absolutely no comment on Jay Terror. I think tonight’s performance has said enough about all three of them, and the Establishment as a whole.” “Okay, but back to Storm. You have got to have some sort of opinion on him. You guys have been on and off again for years now. At least tell me something about that.” She pretended to dig deeper into her wandering mind, only to surface with another answer the reporter would fail to be happy with, “We’re off again. We’re staying off again.” “And you’re feelings about him joining the Establishment?” The man, now juggling a camera, continued to inquire as if he’d not been completely blown off, “You still haven’t broken the silence on that. Could you at least give a bit of insight? Everyone’s wondering.” This time she refused to cave, attempting to brush by him and his very small crew to the sporty Benz she had rented out for the evening. Regrettably, since his question had been ignored by Vivica and Troy earlier in the evening, there was no way he was going to let this one slip. Instead of letting the model like Windsor pass by without word, he grabbed hold of one of the straps on her gym bag, attempting to play it off as his camera getting tangled up with the straps of her gym bag. “Sorry Ms. Windsor.” Tessa failed to acknowledge him with the same polite gaze as before. This time her big blue eyes narrowed to a very small slit extremely quickly. Obviously this guy was either stupid, or dying to capture that rare angry side of Windsor in hopes that it would boil out an honest but yet exaggerated answer to his question. Tessa still refused to budge, despite wanting to give this man a first hand view of just how pissed off on the inside she was concerning the matters he refused to cease talk of. Instead, her attention shifted to the contents inside of her gym bag, more specifically the keys to the Benz. Her fingers slid their down the keys towards the battery operated key chain that manually unlocked her doors. “Please, just give me something.” “Did you not hear what she said?” The voice that boomed across the parking lot even caught Tessa off guard. Alarmingly she looked up, half expecting to see Chris, Markus, or LeStatt standing there. Instead, it was the guy she had stumbled over earlier in the evening, nearly in the reporters face. “She has no comment. Now leave the poor girl alone.” Surprisingly, it actually worked. Though the older man seemed reluctant to back down, he finally did so, retreating back to the inside where he would likely pester someone else to get the juicy gossip he was no doubt looking for. Then came the obligatory awkward silence. Sorry seemed too cliché for Tessa say, but it felt odd to just walk away. The guy on the other hand hardly wanted to come off as some kind of suck up looking to score one with a woman that was no doubt the centerpiece of many male fantasies. It looked as though Tessa would have to be the one to break the silence, if for any reason, to keep from looking like a snob. “Reporters,” she huffed with a very slight chuckle, “they never catch the hint until someone’s threatening their life.” Even the still nameless guy picked up on the highly exaggerated bit, “I would hardly say I threatened his life, unless you think I look like some kind of beast.” He totally had her there. Another light hearted chuckle tumbled out of the Pint Sized Diva’s lips as she commented, “Hardly. If anything I think he ran because he figured you’d pull some kind of crazy move on him.” This time the light hearted chuckle came from him, who easily detected the way her words could be twisted in all the wrong ways, “So I’m gay now?” At the very least it got a soft smile out of her, “Who am I to judge that? I don’t even know you’re name, let alone anything else about you.” This time he actually outstretches his hand, which Tessa shakes simply out of courtesy, “James. And you’re?” “Tessa.” She answered. While she hardly the egotistical type, even she was taken aback by the fact that he was unaware of that fact. It was not often that her name went unmentioned around the GCW backstage area, be it for good or bad reasons. “Ah yes, Tessa Windsor. I’ve heard plenty about you. Nice to finally see the face.” Well, that there just solidified that above thought. Now what the hell was she to say? “I can only imagine what you’ve heard.” The smile on his face should have been reassuring, but given who he could have very well heard from it could have been equally just as bad. Thankfully, he confirmed the former rather than the latter, “Just that you’re a sweet girl who happens to have a killer body. I hate to be a pig, but they certainly were not lying about the body. I imagine you must get some pretty mean stares, from women that is. You know, the whole jealousy thing?” Her head nods, but the answer she provides is hardly one he’d expect from someone of her stature, “Trust me, I’d trade with them in a heartbeat. They’ve got nothing to be jealous about.” “Except the whole money, fame, and looks bit.” He interjected, propping himself up against the bumper of Tessa’s rental. He looked up at her, half expecting her to chuckle again, but this time there was dead silence. “It’s not exactly hard to achieve what I have, James.” After climbing into the driver’s seat, she looked back up to her new found friend and brought the reality of it all to him as bluntly as she could, “Go out, have a one night stand, get knocked up, have a shotgun wedding, get a divorce a week later, and suck every bit of money out of your former spouse. The only thing left to achieve is the fame, and well, all you need for that is some kind of crazy out there story.” Even all that failed to deter his point of view, “Please, you can’t tell me you don’t at least moderately enjoy the kind of lifestyle you live.” Her eyebrow cocks up slightly. At this point she had to wonder if he were nothing more than a reporter in disguise, “Quit the questioning.” Any bit of suspicion got quickly extinguished after watching James head fall down towards the ground, his eyes quickly trailing away from her, his voice dropping down to a mutter, “Sorry.” God she was such a sucker when it came to anyone around her feeling the slightest bit down on themselves, “I guess it could be worse. But it’s hard to see people oogling all over people you care about, accusing you of being the bad guy. They think they know who we are. They think I’m some kind of selfish cock teasing bitch who cares about no one but myself. They couldn’t be more wrong.” Thankfully for Tessa, at least one of the two of them had some sort of sense still intact. Without James stepping in, chances are her words would have been blown even more out of proportion by anyone who might have been eavesdropping, “I’m hoping by ‘they’ you don’t mean the thousands of people who were shouting their lungs out in support of you out there earlier tonight.” “No, not them,” she casually played it off, as if James was quite stupid to even think she’d say a thing of that nature, though, “the others, the people crazy enough to think that Chris and Markus are the good guys in all this.” “So you’re getting upset over a bunch of groupies?” His inquiry managed to squeeze a slight nod out of Windsor, who was quite surprised someone actually interpreted her babble in a correct manner, “The scum of the planet? The kind of people who will turn around on those two and claim they raped them all for the sake of a couple of mil? Come on now, you can’t be serious.” While Windsor was amazed by his accuracy, she fired back like a loose cannon, “They’re making matters worse! They feed Markus and Chris all this bullshit that those two buy right into! You think I want to hear all that bullshit? About how even though I’m the only one among us three that has absolutely nothing notable about her career, somehow I was the one using them all along? Because I totally dropped my pants just to hang around those two and get harassed endlessly, NOT because I was senselessly head over heels in love with a guy who apparently had no use for me whatsoever. Do you honestly even know what any of that is like?” It only stunned her further when the only response he had for her was a flat out, “no.” It was completely far from the ‘you poor thing’, ‘they are assholes’, ‘they used you’, or ‘I would have never treated you that way’ type of response she expected. It left her with absolutely nothing to say. Complete awkwardness. “Let me ask you something,” James had to start back up, seeing as though Tessa was now sitting in the passenger seat of her rental, wondering why the hell her vehicle was still in park, “why the hell do you even care what is going on with those two? Seriously, I won’t lie, it’s a little bit strange.” Again Tessa had absolutely no response, or at least not one she wanted to share. Seeing as though Tessa had nothing she wanted to share, James took the lead again, “Maybe you just need to accept things the way they are and move on.” It humored Tessa none. She far preferred the coddling and protection she used to receive when she opened up, “Easier said than done.” Again James struck, not intending it to be as purposely as it came off. But someone had to awaken her to the reality that right now she was drowning herself in her self pity, “Are you even trying? Or are you standing around sulking hoping that the guy you refuse to get over is going to come crawling back because you shed some tears. Because if it’s the latter, you’re only hurting yourself further. You’re throwing your damn life and career away over someone who’s clearly moved on. “ More stuff the Petite in Pink clearly did not want to hear. The driver’s seat took the brunt of her anger, taking a blow from her extremely hard head, which refused to remove itself. She had absolutely nothing left to say. At this point no one was going to listen anyways. They were tired of her excuses, her reasons for being so secluded, her million and one shitty excuses for refusing to move on. From here she could either move on, or lay alone and watch The Establishment bask in luxury even after they had been defeated earlier in the evening. “Fine,” she ungratefully sighed, “where to from here?” “Maybe you need a night out.” He suggested, plopping himself on to the hood of the car. Chances were it’d be awhile before Tessa went anywhere, “I’d invite you to a luau party I’m having with some friends back at the hotel, but I’m afraid once you snap of your little funk you’d totally think I’m up to something and kick my ass all over the place. And well, what guy wants to be embarrassed like that in front of his friends?” Despite how ridiculously cheesy it came off as, it still managed to get a slight smile back on Tessa’s face, “Yeah. I wouldn’t want that for you either. I’ve already brought enough guys to tears this evening.” This time it was James who had to force a chuckle out in response to Tessa’s cheesy remark. Though he was far from the one who should have been embarrassed by that, he found himself having to quickly come up with something to counter with to combat how obviously forced that chuckle was, not that the new subject was any less awkward for either of them, “So you’re turning me down?” She blinked twice, completely and utterly lost. They were talking about kicking the Establishments ass, how did all that suddenly turn into a date proposal? Maybe it was just her taking everything completely out of context. “Not like that! I just mean hanging out. The party. Luau…..” Yeah, it was totally her taking things out of context. And now she had him completely freaking out in her presence. It’s a shame James did not realize that he was not the only one freaking out at this point. Back in the car, Tessa was already verbally lashing herself, “Smooth move chica. Way to be a completely normal girl.” Thankfully, Tessa thought of a way to save face quickly, “Actually, I have one of those cute little Hawaiian outfits back at the hotel that I’ve been dying to wear since I arrived. That would be perfect to wear it to!” Sad fact was that was actually the truth. James excitement was certainly over exaggerated when he jumped down from the hood of the car, shouting out, “Perfect!” Even Tessa in her concern about her appearance wouldn’t pretend to be amused by that, “…..uh yeah, if that’s what you think. How about you tell me about this party? ” He was more than happy to oblige to that, “Certainly, on one condition.” “What’s that?” “You give me a ride there.” Just a little bitch sketchy, at least to those with a sense of awareness. Tessa, however, trusted just about everyone far more than she should have. To her credit, at least she did a bit of inquiring before letting him in. “You didn’t drive here in your own car?” “I rode with a friend.” Was his claim, whether that was true or not could heavily be debated, just not by Tessa. “Well then, hop in.” She shrugged, tossing her oversized Coach purse from the passenger seat into the backseat of the car. Once again he followed suit with Tessa’s words. It definitely was not the wisest choice the Pint Sized diva had ever made, but hell, it was Hawaii. What was the worst that could happen? Aloha good times!
