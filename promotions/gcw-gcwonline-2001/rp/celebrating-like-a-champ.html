Celebrating Like A Champ

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May, 21 2007 __Start Someone once said that when the rain begins to fall, it is best to sit back and watch the latest flick. Who it was, no one cares...especially if you were Jamison Talley. The plate of steaming steak strips taunted his nostrils as the fumes rose into the air. The sweet blend of tomatoes and cheese joined into the zesty aroma creating a pocket of amnesia, so to speak, which caused The Outlaw to forget about how dismal of a day it was outside the Applebee’s on City Avenue. 'You really know how to get to a kid's heart.' Daniel Franco watched his colleague grab both his fork and knife in opposing hands. He piled the tortilla in the center of his plate with gobs of steak and vegetables. 'I'd say so.' Franco knifed at the top of his Lime Chicken. 'Is this what happens when I win championships?' He asks in between bites of the fajita. Daniel watches him place the quarter piece of the tortilla he has left on his plate and then sees him reaching in his pocket. 'Yeah...' He pulls lose his box of cigarettes and grabs one from inside. 'You can’t seriously be contemplating eating while smoking.' He places the lit stick in his mouth and inhales the poison graciously. 'That's impossible. You can’t fit a taco in your mouth while taking a hit on one of these. Shows what you know.' Franco drops his utensils beside his plate and retrieves his napkin from his lap. 'I need to use the restroom.' He didn’t wait for an answer. Jay laughed and took his food back up into his grip. The cigarette remained between his fingers of the other. He couldn’t help but notice the slight oddity in Daniel's demeanor. He lacked the cynical spark he was known so well to have. Being apathetic though, Jay thought little on the subject and focused his attention on the two remaining tortilla shells before him. He started to construct another fajita with the meat, cheese, guacamole, and...'Fancy seeing you here.' Slowly Jamison Talley looked up. He pursed his lips in an upset way. 'I'd say so.' Heather's face became clear through the smoke drifting off the end of her cigarette. Jay wiped his mouth with the collar of his shirt and looked perplexed. 'I'd definitely say so.' 'I was at the bar and heard that seducing voice of yours.' She tapped her ashes onto the table and leaned across getting close to the outlaw's face. 'Seducing?' He found his own cigarette and took a long drag. 'Hardly.' 'Eating alone?' 'Daniel decided he needed to play with himself in the bathroom. Knowing him, it shouldn't be long before he's done.' She laughed at the remark unlike Jay. He wore the same debonair face. It was almost as if he was a class actor when a girl like Heather spoke to him. He was the badass that tried his hardest to look tough. Apparently girls liked tough. Thankfully, his efforts weren't to waste. 'You mind if I join you two then?' 'Hardly.' He pushed his body against the stiff back of his chair and blew a puff of smoke upward into the air. She smiled politely. The problem with him was he made it far too obvious he was acting. She liked it. 'Congratulations on your win by the way.' 'My win?' 'You're the new Television champion I see. Quite a win over Thunder as well.' 'It was satisfying, yeah.' He casually looked toward the bathrooms to see if Franco was returning. Surprisingly there was no sign of him. 'It's a bit sour I have to defend it already though.' 'Anyone good?' 'Fuck knows. I never know how good the other guy is. Reputation isn't something I trust very well.' Heather ran her hand through her hair adjusting it out of her face. It was a sensuous thing to see a girl with her looks do something so subtle. 'I'm sure you'll do just fine. You're on a roll from what I've watched.' 'It's all about the finish. That's all.' 'The band breaker?' Terror flicked the cherry off the end of his cigarette and eyed Heather up good. It was a small mistake on her part. Still, it was a mistake. 'The Bandstand Bust.' 'Right,' As if to reconcile the mishap she took hold of Jay's drink. The straw poked in between her upper and lower lips and he watched her suck at the cool lager inside. 'You mind?' 'Hardly.' It was mistake number two for her. Jamison Talley wasn't just an actor. He was an avid fan of personal space and hated desecration of it. For too many reasons he kept away from sharing or anything of the like. Franco could be seen walking back toward the table with a hefty grin on his face making his situation even funnier than Jay had figured. 'Heather?' 'You remember. I was at the bar and couldn't resist saying hi.' They smiled at one another and locked eyes for a long moment. 'Feel better?' Terror laughed slightly. 'You were in there longer than I've ever seen.' It took a little but once he figured it out Franco frowned. 'Yeah yeah, have your giggles you two.' He took a sip from his birch beer and crossed his arms across his chest. 'It's almost as comical as Jay's win this week.' Both heather and Jamison stopped their chuckling and put on questioning faces. 'You can't be serious.' He barked. 'You know that the match shouldn't have been as close as it was. You have so much room to improve upon...you just need to go harder with it.' 'Excuse me sir,' it was a good thing Kait, the waitress, strolled over. It was almost too obvious that Jay was ready to stab his fork through Franco's throat if the threesome was alone for another minute. Talley and the group looked up at the redhead. 'This is the nonsmoking section.' 'Doubtful.' His gaze shifted to Daniel. 'I'm sorry but unless you'd like to move to the smoking section you're going to have to put those out or go outside and finish.' Midway through the sentence her voice faltered. It was a tense atmosphere to step into. 'We were just leaving anyways. You got the check, right Daniel?' His eyes hadn't moved from Franco's face since Kait had pleasantly appeared. He started to open his mouth but before anything came loose from inside Jay and Heather both stood. He slid the box of 27s back in his pocket and walked ahead as Heather looked back to Daniel. 'Bistro, six thirty.' He whispered. She nodded and caught up to Jay as if nothing had happened. __________ The bistro on fifth was quaint to say the least. The small brick box was tucked between the barber shop and Asian fish market. Standing just below the boldface nameplate was Daniel Franco. His eyes hid behind a pair of glasses for his mediocre vision and his hair was crumpled in a messy sort of way. Just looking at him might send the wrong impression. Once talking to the charming individual though, someone entirely different would surely emerge. 'Looking for a good time?' A woman walked in front of him showing the two teeth in the front of her mouth missing. 'Not tonight.' Her grin turned wry, 'Not any night by the looks of it.' In disgust, she walked away in search of other business. Franco's attention turned to a group of men across the street. About six or so men in their twenties stood around raising fists and shouting obscenities left and right at one another. He squinted his eyes a tad to catch the tallest man in the group spitting a juicy wad at one of the other's face. It didn't take long for the arms to start swinging. 'Boys will be boys.' The voice startled him from his blank stare at first. The sight of Heather however was a comforting one. 'How are you my lady?' Like a true gentleman he took her hand and kissed the top of it gently. 'Not terrible. Yourself?' 'Very well. And Jamison?' 'He seemed well enough when I left. I’m pretty sure he thinks I’m stupid' Franco nods, 'Maybe not stupid. He just likes to put on a show.' 'So I can tell.' She eyed up the door and Franco caught the hint easily. 'Come on,' Daniel led the way as any gentleman should. He stepped through the thick oak door and found everything in sight had an alcoholic odor attached to it. Liquor of all sorts collected as one and swarmed into heather and his nostrils luring them toward the bar. 'A Brandy, Terry.' Heather looked across the bar at the other drinks along the line. 'I'll get that,' she pointed at a tall glass with a strawberry hanging on the edge. 'A daiquiri I guess?' 'Coming right up.' Terry, the bartender, answered happily. He was a stout man with a handlebar mustache wrapping around his mouth in full. 'Do you think Jay suspects anything thus far?' Franco took a seat on one of the stools and dug his elbows into the counter. Heather followed suit. 'I doubt it. He doesn't seem like the kind of guy that recognizes much other than himself.' 'I know what you mean. Jamison is the kind of boy that wakes up knowing he's great. There is no changing his opinion on himself. With others though, it's the complete opposite. I doubt I've ever met a bigger hypocrite.' Terry returned smoothly and placed the drinks down in front of his customers with a slight nod. Before he could say hi though a woman from the other end called for him. 'Is that why we're doing this? To end his hypocrisy?' She took a sip from the daiquiri and made a sour face. 'More or less. I was hoping something inside him would spark and kick start that rage I know he has. Something needs to get him going soon before he falls on his face in front of the entire world.' 'Conniving. Very Conniving.' Daniel took a long sip from his iced glass savoring the taste on his tongue. 'Smart sounds less evil.' __End
