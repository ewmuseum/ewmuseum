Date: November 2nd 2005 The somber voice, running through my head, the night of the murder never fading through my mind. The nights walking the city side, staring at the luminescent sky, wondering when my own life will come to an end. Although, I bask in the glory of Global Championship Wrestling, I know I’d rather be staring down from heaven with the people that I love. The sun rushing through the window as though the angels had fallen victim to the large room. The figure of Aiden Jackson lies on the bed, outlined by the large plaid comforter over his body. The mind isn’t flowing, the body isn’t moving, and the slumber is deep filled with the haunting nightmares of the past. The detailed dreams of murder, maltreatment, and ultimately life. “Not another day.” Aiden muttered to himself as the alarm clock roared throughout the room, Aiden pushed down the covers, shivering then standing up in a small pair of shorts and no t-shirt. Aiden already knew this day to be unfulfilling to the soul, the mind, the body. The day had grown gloomy by noon with the clouds staring down over the Jackson Estate. The rain began falling unmercifully by 1 O’clock and Aiden grew more depressed and sad on the day of his parent’s murder. All of the money in the world, the will left behind leaving literally millions to the Jackson brothers. The wrestling career Aiden had embarked on leading to a substantially large amount of money in his name. The bonus for winning the GCW Cruiserweight title and the several defenses he had already brought. The time had come; the city was calling although the night proved to be gloomy. Aiden would return to the spot where his parent’s murder had taken place for he didn’t know how many times in the past several years. Coping with the pain of losing parents every time, knowing the exact spot in which they were murdered. It was time to have an encounter with an old friend, a wise and insightful friend that would help through the time of pain and depression. The countless times that Aiden thought of taking his life on this day. It wasn’t the way to go though; it wasn’t the way he wanted to earn his spot in heaven, because quite frankly suicide will not lead the way to heaven. The path to heaven is dealing with the curveballs that life throws at you, dealing with the stress of everyday life, dealing with the pain that is brought to your heart everyday for remembering what’s lost. The downfall of rain did not let up as Aiden exited the house quietly, not speaking words ever since this morning. Aiden got into the small SUV, turning the car on, and placing a CD into the CD player. The lyrics of The Bled scream though the booming sound system. You are the teeth and the lungs. Biting back and breathing in. Cracked like a frozen moon. The heat lamp seeps into the pores. This is your interrogation your fingerprints betray your eyes. Died on your honeymoon she made such a lovely bride. Left the door unlocked for you. The leeches took their turn. Deny. Your alibi was painted by a blind politician. Inside your swollen eyes sleeps a genius buried by miscalculations. You are the teeth and the lungs. Biting back and breathing in. Your love is homicide. The queen is beckoning, get on your knees and beg for your life. You will pay the price. Close your eyes and die. Traveling down the dark interstate, the speedometer hitting 60 MPH, the rain pouring down on the windshield as the music pumps through the vehicle. The thought of a gun, the thought of a bittersweet death was all that was on his mind. The windshield wipers bolting quickly back and forth pushing the rain aside. The music pumping, the music booming, the music screaming. The life that Aiden knew right now was simply put with this analogy, it’s like a song that you know the words to yet you don’t like the song one bit. Aiden felt as though his life was collapsing upon him but the truth was everyday that this day came about he felt this way regardless of how good his life had been going at the time. Arriving at the destination, still not another word spoken, Aiden gets out of the car and begins walking down the street. Rain pouring down around him and rolling down his long trench coat. He greets an older man at the door of the Fox Theatre, the man is graying and he looks thin as though he hasn’t eaten in many a day. “It’s been another year passed hasn’t it?” He utters to Aiden when Aiden looks up at him, the tears rolling down his face. The old man cannot tell that Aiden is crying though, it is camouflaged by the rain falling into his face. “Mabry, Is it just me or does it seem like this gets harder and harder to do every year?” Aiden spoke softly with a sense of fear in his voice. Mabry, a name introduced long ago to the avid reader’s of this cold, heartless tale of a man named Aiden Jackson. Mabry was the man that upheld the Jackson Estate as their butler. Mabry became a fatherly figure to the boys when their parents died, Aiden now looked to Mabry for advice and simply to share this day with every year. “I wish there could have been a way to stop it Aiden, but now even after 10 years you can’t stop it. There’s no going back in time and there’s no fixing it. Aiden, you will find your peace one day, and even if you don’t there’s no reason that you haven’t. It is rough dealing with the loss of your parents at such a young age.” He said to Aiden, the tears now streaming down his face’camouflaged. Aiden came closely into Mabry; Mabry pulls Aiden in hugging him tightly as Aiden sobs into his shoulder. Mabry lets the tears stream down his face knowing that the Aiden in his arms, is the same 15 year old boy who lost his parents 10 years ago. There was no fix for the man who was a broken machine in society. Aiden Jackson rules a life of fame, a life of infamy; a life of glory’Aiden Jackson never has led a life of happiness. Aiden Jackson may someday find his peace and live a life of happiness, the time shall not come soon knowing everyday that his parent’s were heartlessly murdered, the nights that he still weeps not knowing why it happened. Aiden Jackson is still that 15 year old boy who lost his parent’s. The same boy who cried himself for 2 years straight knowing that mom and dad weren’t there to tuck him in at night like they once had in his younger years. “If the people we love are stolen from us, the way to have them live on is to never stop loving them. Buildings burn. People die. But real love is forever.' Coyle O? Conner, Aiden hasn’t forgotten about you, and he knows he’s defeated you once. He knows that the time now comes again for business for another defense to be made. To hold onto the glory that motivates him to continue in the business of wrestling’Good Luck O’Conner because the third time’s a charm, not the second.
