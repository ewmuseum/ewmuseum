Family Guy

26 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26 Aug 05 “Hello?” I spoke these words into my cell phone a bit annoyed as I hated being interrupted by the phone. It seemed that every time this thing rang someone was asking me for something, if it weren’t for GCW I wouldn’t own one at all. “Mr. Abitua this is Kevin Jacobs from?” “Yeah Jacobs I know where you’re from, you people are the only people who call me on this thing anymore. What the hell do you want?” “Well as you probably know this weekend is GCW’s fanfest.” Yeah I knew about it and I dreaded getting stuck in some lame promotional ploy, here I had a feeling was when my fears came to reality. “Well Steven Caldera has chosen you for an activity to take place tonight.” Great just fucking great so I am going to probably have to sit in front of asshole fans and sign autographs and take pictures when most of them will cheap shot me by claiming I am going to get my ass kicked by Stevens at Annihilation or that “this person or that person” are better than me and should be the champ. “What the fuck do you people have me doing Kevin?” “Having dinner with a family.” I froze in my spot hoping that perhaps I just misheard exactly what the fat man just said to me. I replayed it a couple of times in my head and each time I realized that I should be happy I was drinking anything at the time because I would have spit the stuff out in that instant. “You’ve got to be kidding me?” “Afraid not, this family won a “have a GCW superstar to dinner” contest, and as their name was pulled so was yours. They live in the area and the limo will be around in about an hour to pick you up so be ready.” He quickly hung up the phone before I could even get another word in, which means he’ll get his when I see that fat slob again. He could have at least told me the name of this wretched family before sending me off to have dinner with them. I swear to .. Well if not God then somebody that Steven Caldera truly despises me and is looking to humiliate me. He is probably pissed off that I haven’t become the corporate pet that most of his other Champions have been. I’ll play his little games though, put on this damn smile but he will hate the things I do when it comes time to climb into that ring. I didn’t plan to try and get all flashy for this family so I remained in what I was already wearing, not that I ever dressed too shabby but still. I almost reached for the phone in the hotel to order room service when I remembered that I was having dinner with this family ? although that thought almost tempted me to order even more. I held the urge in and simply laid down for a quick nap before the limo got here. Almost exactly an hour later that bastard phone rings again, this time I answer it a bit groggily but simply say that I was heading downstairs. The nap was nice probably what I needed to function properly with these people. The Limo driver tipped his hat and let me inside the car before rushing to his door and preparing for take off. He didn’t say much on the drive, which was a good thing because its not like I had any desire to hear what the fuck he had to say anyways. The drive was fairly short, only about 15-20 minutes to get to our destination. We arrived in a small suburb, which actually wasn’t too bad, sure I could never see myself living here but for normal paying jobs this was probably as nice as it got. A small chuckle escaped my lips when I read the name of the street as “Spooner St.”, sure it was a tad juvenile but we all have some of that inside of us. The house we pulled up to looked almost identical to all of the other ones on the street so the driver had to point out the one I was to go into. The only way to identify it was the tan’ish mini van that rested in the driveway. I climbed out of the limo and was ready to get this night of hell over with when the drive handed me an envelope. Of course the bastard didn’t tell me what it was before taking off. I opened the envelope as I made my way up the walkway. -Knight Make sure you treat these people with the respect they deserve. I am sure you will have a GREAT day. Good luck at Annihilation. Regards, Steven Caldera I couldn’t help but roll my eyes as I balled up the paper and tossed it behind me. Steven Caldera was a prick if I’ve ever seen one and one day him and I would have words, just because he is my boss doesn’t mean I am afraid of him, I have money without this wrestling gig plus firing me would be the most ignorant thing he has ever done, next to giving a title shot to Shane Lane. I took in a deep breath and let it out slowly before lifting a hand and knocking on the door. I heard some commotion inside and knew they were coming. Soon after my knock the door swung open and like a scene from some cheesy sit com the entire family stood at the door with smiles on their face ? with the exception of the baby. For some reason he appeared pissed off. Now their smiles, while warm got a bit unnerving when they stayed plastered on their faces, they didn’t talk or move any part of their body. “Um ? Hello I am LeStatt Knight.” “We’re the Griffins.” They all said in unison. “Oh I am so sorry Mr. Knight please come in.” The mother said. This family certainly were already giving me a strange vibe. I followed the mother in and heard the door shut behind me, the rest of the family followed closely behind. The mother was a very attractive woman indeed, too bad shes married. She had red hair that fell just about her shoulders, creamy skin, and a great body, even if it was hidden underneath some clothes that looked fit for a grandmother instead. With a little work I knew she could be something special. “I’m Lois, this is my husband Peter, these are our kids, Meg, Chris, and Stewie ? and that’s our dog Brian.” “Is he drinking Bud Light?” I asked pointing to the Dog’s bowl which had a half tipped over bottle of beer next to it. Peter a rather large and round man with brown hair and thin framed glasses laughed awkwardly before rushing over and picking the bowl up in his hands. “Oh no this is uh ? my bowl ? yeah we like to role play in this house?” Everyone just looks at him as if he were retarded. “WHAT!” Watch!” I cringed as I watch him put his lips to the bowl and pours the beer from the bowl and into his mouth. I then turned my right where the oldest son, Chris was looking at me wide eyed. He had shaggy blonde hair and was very rounded just like his father. “Can I ask you a favor Mr. Knight?” “Um ? Sure.” I responded a bit reluctantly and he waves to me to come closer to him so he can whisper to me. “Can you kill the evil monkey that lives in my closet?” “CHRIS!” Lois yelps. “WHAT MOM?” “Go to your room.” “Bu’Bu?” “NOW!” Chris runs, or at least what he considers running to the far end of the room and up the stairs to where I am going to assume his bedroom is. This was probably going to be a very long day indeed. “I am sorry LeStatt we just haven’t been around a celebrity before ? except that one time when Meg here ran over William Shatner killing him instantly.” Peter said. “PETER!”” “What?” “Go to your room!” Lois yelped again, which actually made me double take, was she telling her husband to go to his room? “That’s not necessary Ma’am.” I assured her. “So whats for dinner?” Meg, the only daughter stepped forward. “Dad told mom to make tacos, he said you’re people?” Peter now rushed forward and cupped his hand over Meg’s mouth and looked around nervously. “Kids, they say the weirdest things!” Peter said this with a slight chuckle. “Indeed.” was all I could really manage. I looked to Meg and extended my hand, she took it and gave me a handshake. “Nice to meet you, my name is?” “LeStatt, I know ? I have all kinds of your posters on my wall! You have gotten me through some tough times.” “What do you mean?” “uh?” She hesitates to think about her answer. “Nevermind.” she laughs nervously. “Meg why don’t you go and make the table.” Meg runs off now and goes to make the table for dinner and all that wonderful stuff. Peter eyes me in a way that would have caused anyone else to have a fist put back in their nose. I ignore it this time though, he isn’t worth getting myself into trouble over. “Why don’t you have a seat Mr. Knight and Peter and I will get the last bit of dinner ready.” She says to me with a smile. I sit down on the couch and right as I do, Lois puts her baby down at the other end. “Alright Stewie wait here with this nice man while Mommy goes to get dinner ready.” He doesn’t say anything, just looks at her with that LOOK and yet for some reason she smiles and pulls Peter into the Kitchen, leaving me alone with her child. I notice him staring at me so I turn my head to look back at him. I give a half smile, but something about that look of his makes me uneasy ? and that’s hard to do. “I am going to give you a choice ? either help me eliminate that vile woman or I will be forced to use the DEVICE on you.” My eyes widened as his voice met my ears, did he really say what I think he said? I sat in stunned silence at first, trying to understand if that voice really came from this baby’s mouth. “Excuse me?” “You heard me ? Help me eliminate that dreadful wench Lois, and I shall spare your life when I enslave all of mankind.” “Oh you’re going to take over the world?” I asked him with a fake look of dread. “STOP MOCKING ME!” he screamed. I couldn’t help but chuckle at this kid, even though he was creepy he had a certain charm to him. Although I still think I must have drank myself stupid, that or this is one fucked up dread. “You must know little about me little man, whatever vicious things you are planning, I have done them.” “Yes mister big shot” Like what!”” “Have you ever bashed a steel chair over the head of a pregnant woman?” “You locked my brethren inside of that wretched womb!”” “Yes” I guess.” “How deliciously EVIL!” “Have you ever attempted to break the knee of your own best friend.” “Your treachery has no bounds does it!”” “Not at all nino.” “Ah wonderful ? you could help me. When I take over and enslave all of mankind you can be my right hand man. What do you say?” “Aren’t you a tad small?” “Don’t you DARE!” He said after jumping to his feet and pointing towards me. “Don’t make fun of my small stature! It’s not the size that counts buck-o it’s how you work it!” “You know that’s the excuse of small men right?” “Now you listen here you ungrateful pig, I am offering you the chance not to be destroyed by ? THE DEVICE! You would be wise to just take my offer and help rule the disgusting human race.” I figured that this dream could only get more interesting, well hopefully It was a dread because a reality like this has got to be better than anything I could have dreamed up, and this was before we even had dinner. “Fine Stewie, I will help you with your problem. I can kill them all now if you wish.” “Excellent ? Victory shall soon be mine!” “Should I use a baseball bat, pistol, or my bare hands?” “Have you ever killed a man with your bare hands!”” “Absolutely.” “I ADORE THIS MAN!” I laughed out loud this time which didn’t change the fact that Stewie was still finishing his plot to use me to take over the world. How exactly this child came out of those two parents is beyond me ? then again, you aren’t suppose to understand dreams right? “Well when shall we begin our plan?” “Immediately! Victory is within reach ? this is a day that I have dreamed of every since I escaped from that blasphemous womb!” He stops to think for a moment now. “On our way to World Domination can we stop at McDonald’s” A happy meal sounds great.” “Whatever you say little man.” “BUT NO PICKLES! God help them if I find pickles!” “Dinner!” Lois comes in and scoops up Stewie. “Lets go dear and lets put you into your high chair.” “Unhand me you vile woman! My plans are almost complete! ARGH!” He looks my way. “You have the power to end this! Do as I command!” They go out of sight and I can hear her placing him in his high chair. “BLAST!” I stood in the living room alone now just replaying the events in my mind ? all of this and I haven’t even had dinner yet. “This is going to be one long ?” I am interrupted as I see Chris running downstairs, he flies by me and into the Kitchen. “MOM THE MONKEY! THE EVIL MONKEY!” I shake my head ? A real long night, fucking Caldera will pay for this one.
