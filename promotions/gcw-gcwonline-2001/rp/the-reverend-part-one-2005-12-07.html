The Reverend (Part One)

7 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7 December 2005 OOC: I'm really disappointed with this roleplay, I couldn't find the time to get something more solid/complete out. I got loaded with homework on the night that I planned not to have it :( Sorry to disappoint guys, I guess this one will be turned into a series. proof (pr f) n. 1. The evidence or argument that compels the mind to accept an assertion as true. 2. a. The validation of a proposition by application of specified rules, as of induction or deduction, to assumptions, axioms, and sequentially derived conclusions. Often times, I don’t divert from the story, but this’this is a special occasion my friends? I, Aiden Jackson, once again proved to the world that I’m just better than measly superstars such as Rei Tiu. Not only did I take the “fans superstar” down one time, but I took him down twice. “Oh Aiden, you caught him off guard by betraying him.” You actually believe that Rei Tiu thought I was his friend, slim chance. The magazine laid there, unopened, the picture of Aiden Jackson with hands held high in glory, the caption under the picture reading “Next Big Star of GCW?”. I picked up the magazine, looking in the index, I found the article with pictures from mine and Rei’s match and I skimmed the article swiftly then set the magazine back down. A motive, I finally had one, I truly found what made me happy. What was it you ask? Winning. I walked away from the small black end table, traveling through the house and down the stairs. I greeted Mabry as he stood there preparing a meal. He acknowledged my presence in the room and went back to stirring the dish he was preparing. I opened the fridge and grabbed a Gatorade from it. “I won’t be in tonight, so why are you cooking” A lady friend coming over?” I said to him jeering. “Actually, Yes,” He said smiling softly at me. “Want me to go fill your prescription of Viagra?” I said taunting him some more. He laughed softly then turned back around throwing spices into the dish and then placing a lid on top of the boiling pot. He turned to me and stared as though he expected me to tell him something. “Yes?” “Where are you off to tonight Aiden?” “I’m going to visit the Reverend, we have issues to discuss.” “You haven’t been there in a long time Aiden. You know how he is be careful with what you say and do.” There were things that Mabry never knew about the Reverend; this man was not exactly one to model after. There are deeper ties to his story which will be reviewed in upcoming sequences. Date:: 5 December 2005 Time:: 5:35 P.M. Location:: A Small Church outside of St. Louis I walked down the center aisle, passing the large wooden pews, and the stain glass windows shed a tear of light through as the sun was just setting. I tapped lightly on the large wooden door, a murmur came from behind the door, and it was hauled open slowly. Reverend Andrew Castro stood in all black attire with his white collar sticking out in the middle of his shirt. Smoke pouring from his mouth as he took another hit on the cigarette, greeting me into the large office. “How’re you Aiden?” He questioned me as he blew the smoke from the cigarette out, then placing it into an ash tray and rubbing it back and forth to put it out. He then walked around the large wooden desk and sat into a red chair. “I’m fine, No action as of late. Only ordeals within wrestling you know?” I said to him as I watched him carefully. “That’s not what I’ve heard Aiden, my people have an eye on you. You’ve found out about the other men in your parents murder case. Don’t hide things from me Aiden,” He grinned as he uttered his words to me. He actually believed that I had fear for him; he had grown so egotistical that he had come to believe I feared for my life around him. Reverend Castro planned and helped execute the murder of Michael Mason’under the circumstance that his name was never mentioned. “Aiden?” He said sternly as I drifted into my own thought. “Yeah, I found out about them.” “Aiden, what are your plans, shall we lure them to the same fate as Michael Mason?” He said as a sick smile grew across his face. “No, they will not suffer the same fate. I’ve grown beyond that phase in my life Reverend, I’ve matured and I’ve formulated my own plan this time,” I stared him in the eyes with no expression. “Dear Aiden, where has my boy gone” You want blood; I know it’s in you. Aiden, we’ll avoid prison this time, there will be no sentence. Not for you, not for me. Aiden come back into the ranks as a Priest and I will protect you. You will be free from punishment.” Go back into the church and study? I had never thought about it before, I had never begun to believe that I would actually contemplate this, the only reason I was, was because I did want blood, but this was something Castro couldn’t know because he would exploit it and lure me back into the deceitful games. “You know where I stand Reverend. I can’t go back to prison, there is still a chance, and you know it as well as I do.” “What about X?” He said smiling once more. “How’how have you found out about X” We haven’t spoken in years Rev,” I was baffled as to who his source was in this situation. “Aiden, although you’ve been gone several years, I’ve still kept up with you. I saw Shane Carbon’s death on the news, which means there is an open position is there not?” “We’ll not go into that, you’re not worthy of the position as of now.” I said to him softly. “What did you say to me boy” I’ve built you into the monster that you are, and you deny me!”” The anger in his voice rose. “There are pre-requisites in joining X.” I lifted my shirt and showed him the scar, branded into my chest is a small X. “Something as small as this” A branding? You don’t think I can take the pain?” He laughed? I knew he could take the pain, but what I wasn’t sure of was his intentions. Castro was apparently out for the blood of another man, another murderer of the Jackson’s. The fact of the matter was, he didn’t deserve the marking. I did my time for my actions and I led my life down a more righteous road. The temptation to go back to my evil ways is still there, Castro still knows the passion drives inside of me. Castro will do anything to turn me back to my ways. “Listen, Castro, X is a non-violent organization. You may attend a meeting, tell them Prophet has sent you and you will be granted access to the building. In two days, I will text message you with the address of the building,” I rose from my chair and walked out the door, passing the pews and the pane glass windows once more. As I walked to my car, I looked over my shoulder into the window of the office, Castro stood there, smoke surrounding him, he peered out the window with a mischievous smile on his face. Castro truly had the worst intention and I knew it.
