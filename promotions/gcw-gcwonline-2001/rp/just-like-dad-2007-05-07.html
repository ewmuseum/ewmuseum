Just Like Dad

7 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 7, 2007 __Start In life there are two groups of people. Among all the labels and other titles given to members of the human race, optimists and pessimists stand out like that nail needing one last hit from the hammer. With the optimists, nothing trips them up. No matter how large the tree in the road is, they step over it with a certain confidence only others with this trait posses. They are unstoppable in their own mind and don't plan on listening to others opinions on how well they'd do at something. Instead, they KNOW how well they'll do. Just across the road are the pessimists. Everyone has surely heard of those that look at the glass half empty. With these men and women, this is their life. No matter how good things may be, it will not last. Especially when one is faced with the most wretched of situations, this person sees no escape from it. Daniel Franco was a pessimist. 'Who is Aldair though? This "so-called" wrestler has hidden his face from cameras and walked into each of his matches with his head down. No one knows who he is so why be so elated after such a meaningless win?' His voice was stern in his reasoning and told a story in the shear realism he spoke. Obviously he was in a sour mood. Jamison Talley looked across the breakfast table at his manager and friend with a perplexed look stretching the skin of his cheeks. 'I fuckin' won my first match in GCW and you still hate me? Fuck that Danny.' 'I don't hate you. I just find it trivial to gloat over such a minute win.' He persisted. 'Considering I have a television title match this week with Thunder, I'd say it was enough for that cheeseball antismoking president to see something he liked.' Daniel leaned back in his small wood swiveling chair and rotated side to side in a giddy manner. His arms stretched across the dark lines of the table. 'That is something that I can't figure out for the life of me.' 'You really have no faith in me, do you?' Jay's voice slipped out of the normal badass niche he had created and into one of a sympathetic kid looking for approval. His eyes watched Franco's fingers scrape at the glaze overtop of the wood table meticulously. 'I just think you have yet to realize the opportunities at hand. You are capable of going so far yet you continue to live in a world where Jay Terror is a deity.' 'So you have no faith in me?' His eyes wandered upward until they met Daniel's dirty gray pupils. 'I would if you tried harder.' He answered emptily. Jay stands from the table and digs into his pocket finding the box of 27s within. ?You know you’re no better than that fuck-up my mom married.? He spat. Franco watched him stand from the table and walk off to the front door. Before walking outside, a spark jumped from the mouth of his Zippo and the pungent smell of the cigarette filled the air overpowering the vanilla scent from the candles. He had had enough. __________ 'You know you're not allowed up here...' A soft voice fell from the lips of, what had to be, a woman. It pierced through the high altitude and found the ears of Jay Terror startling him to the point of nearly falling. He grabbed tight to the edge of the building and turned his neck around. He was right that it was a woman. Her body hung close to the tan threads she wore accenting her gentle curves. The badge on her chest told him that she was being honest in her statement. 'I figured since no one liked the smokers I'd stay out of their way.' A crimson shade colored her cheeks as her nose wrinkled in. 'You got another?' Jay took a heavy drag from the cigarette between his lips and offered her a quizzical look. 'I swear, if you try busting me for some bullshit I'll throw you off this roof.' She laughed playfully. 'Uh huh, I'm sure.' The funny thing about it was that he really would toss her quicker than she could stick the white paper in her mouth. 'If you're down for that, take a seat and enjoy the view.' Terror fetched the box from his pocket and tossed it her way. She grabbed it thankfully and drew it inward as she took out her own lighter. The security guard walked beside Jay and took a seat on the edge of the skyscraper to his right. He finally got a better glimpse at her facial features and just as he hoped, they were better than anything he could see below him. Her hair flowed down in a dirty blonde stream allowing her eyes to peak through her bangs and show their brilliant blue luster. She was something. 'What's got you up here alone?' He avoided the question entirely, 'Hi, I'm Jay.' She chuckled to herself and flicked the hair out of her face. 'I'm Heather. Nice to meet you.' Terror grinned and extended his hand outward. She did the same and each shook once lightly. 'So please, can you tell me how a girl like you is stuck wrangling up mutts like me on a roof?' 'Well you see, that's actually a funny story. My family is kind of big on the security business and through my lineage all of my elders have done this. Men, women, everyone...it's just tradition I guess.' 'Tradition? I hate tradition.' He sucked the end of the cigarette taking in another big breath of smoke. 'Is that what you wanted to do?' 'Yeah...I mean I didn't have much say in it.' Her voice faltered as she spoke. 'I figured it. Tell me Heather, if you could do anything what would it be?' The blue of her eyes flashed as her gaze swiveled away from Jay and off into the distance toward the sun. The tips of her fingers rolled under the concrete she sat upon and scratch it's underbelly softly. 'Don't laugh...' She begins awkwardly. Jay silently promises and urges her to go on, '...I always wanted to be a professional wrestler.' Terror lets a snicker escape his throat as he watches Heather's expression switch to an upset one. 'Is that so?' 'I thought you said you wouldn't laugh.' He flicks the butt of his cigarette downward and watches it plummet toward the pavement. 'I'm sorry, I couldn't help myself. You're serious?' 'Of course I'm serious.' He realized that when she yelled her eyebrows arched in a playful way. Somehow there was even more to Heather that he was attracted to. 'You think you're so special then what do you do? Smoke on rooftops? Get high in vans?' 'Not all the time.' He paused for a dramatic effect of some kind. 'I actually live your dream.' He reached for his wallet and opened it up to find a small picture. He handed it over to Heather and she looked it over like it was a piece of evidence to a murder case. 'You up for another cigarette?' She asked. Jay just smiles as he takes the eight by ten photo back and looks at himself raising the Pennsylvania Wrestling Academy Championship over his head. __________ 'Alright, it may not be much but we're in the process of fixing it up.' 'We?' 'Me and Danny. I'm not too fond of him but maybe you'll like the guy.' Jay pressed against the door and pushed it open to show the greeting room of the small place he liked to call home. He allowed his guest, Heather, to walk through first and while doing so she found Daniel Franco walking down the front staircase. 'Either you're the pizza person or I dialed the wrong number.' Franco's charm strikes a smile in her lips. Daniel's grin faded away at the sight of Jay stepping inside however. 'I'm heather, one of Jay's friends.' Franco brushes against her shoulders and swats under his nose. 'I can tell.' He passed by and made his way to the kitchen. 'Here to eat my food and drink my beers are you?' 'Ignore Danny; he's not one for socializing.' Jay followed him and made sure Heather did the same. 'I'm sure you'd rather see what we have here rather than hear his bitching.' 'Ah, right. I'm the one complaining.' His tone shouts sarcasm. Terror shook his head at his friend and made his way to the left to begin the impromptu tour. Heather looked at Daniel and saw his right eye open and close in a slow wink. She returned the gesture and nodded accordingly as if they had a deal. 'Over here we have our living room...' She caught up to the voice and found out what else they had in the house. __End
