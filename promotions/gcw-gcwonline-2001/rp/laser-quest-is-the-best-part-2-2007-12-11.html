Laser Quest Is The Best Part 2

11 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/11/07 START 9:05 pm The scene opens once again with Clyde Fox in the middle of one of his adventures. The local laser tag building is where we pick things up from last time. After coming out of a restroom stall, Clyde washed his hands in anticipation for his laser tag game. He exits the room only to be greeted by Charlotte. “Right this way sir, your equipment is waiting,” Charlotte advised. Clyde waltzed into the room where the kids he invited to play with him had been waiting with their gear on. Clyde promptly slaps his chest piece on and ignites his weapon. Just then Robert, the father of one of the boys walks up to Clyde. “I don’t want any funny business alright. I’m right here and I can see everything once you get in that room,” He professed. “Sir, you have nothing to worry about and everything to relax for. YEP!” Fox insisted, “Well” if you feel that worried, you are more than welcome to come play too? BIG TIME!” “Well, uh, I’ve never played,” Robert said somewhat dejected. “Hey Clarinet, get another vest out, we gots one more player!” Fox gleamed. After equipping Robert, the pack of players were ready to be briefed. “Okay guys listen up,” Charlotte started, “You have two 15 minute matches. The match will end for you if you need to leave to go to the washroom or just want to quit. After the first 15 minutes you will hear a bell ring. After that bell you all should come back here for a five minute break and then I’ll let you guys back in for the final game. Got it” Good luck to you guys and remember; no hitting with the equipment, keep it clean, keep it fair, keep it fun.” The main latching door starts to open after Charlotte finished her speech. A red optical spinning light adds for some cool effect whilst the players filter into the pitch black player area. Once inside, Clyde maintains a bent over stance and begins to rumble down different paths. All of a sudden his gun makes a beeping noise as the LED display reads: “Star Fox” Game initiated, begin combat!” “Time to rock and rake Clydieee,” Clyde relays to himself. It’s okay, try and bounce back. So you went down the right laneway and all you can see is darkness. All of a sudden Robert comes running out from the darkness. He doesn’t see you because you crouch against the wall. What do you do? Choice: 1) Trip him when he runs by. OR 2) Wait until he runs past to shoot him. If you choose choice #1, move onto the heading “Strain #2, Choice #1.” If you choose number two, move to “Strain #2, Choice #2.” Strain #1, Choice #1 Of course you dash after that runt! You are a point hungry, skittle swelling, panty-sniffing, corn dog! You pursue the child only to lose him in the dark. But watch out! Oh too late, another kid swipes some points and shoots you from behind! Move to the heading “Strain #1 Conclusion #1.” Strain #1, Choice #2 Luckily you didn’t follow that kid because he was coaxing you into a trap! You decide to go around the bend when you see a little girl just standing there. You decide it isn’t a trap and you run out in the open and start firing profusely. Move to the heading “Strain #1 Conclusion #2.” Strain #2, Choice #1 Being the bad-butt you are, you trip Robert and he lands flat on his face! You chuckle and then proceed to sit there and shoot at his back target pad. His body remains lifeless. Move to heading “Strain #2, Conclusion #1.” Strain #2, Choice #2 Robert darts by you. You then come rolling out of the corner in Attack mode; however it’s so dark that you run into a wall at full speed! SPLAT! Move to heading “Strain #2, Conclusion #2.” Strain #1, Conclusion #1 You’re right pissed. You having nothing to lose by kicking that kids ass because you had your fingers crossed when you promised Robert that you wouldn’t hurt anyone. So you speed walk up to the kid, grab him, and proceed to Irish whip him into the side wall. You then grab your gun and whack the kid in the head with it. You see blood, but you don’t care? until Robert comes running around the corner yelling and screaming at you. You quickly drop your gun and equipment as you bolt it for the door. Luckily you are much faster and you make it out of the building alive. “Damn children” You think to yourself as you walk down the street, “He started it! I was playing fair but not anymore. I hate Laser Quest! Well at least I know my own strength for NC-17” BIG TIME!” Your story ends here. Result: Positive. Congratulations, you gave Clyde a strength upgrade for NC-17. Strain #1, Conclusion #2 You open fire on the now crying little girl. You almost run into her but you side step her just in time. But by accident you trip and fall over your shoelaces. You land awkwardly and most leave the game in pain. Once you retreat to the lobby you have Charlotte call a cab for you to go home. Upset, you blame the fall on Chris and how he will pay for it come NC-17. Result: Neutral. Unfortunately you made Clyde lose some stamina points for NC-17, but on the good side you made him more focused on the match, then on having fun. Strain #2, Conclusion #1 After shooting him for nearly 15 minutes, the bell rings and you go back to the briefing pad. You had a score of 1,456,733. Easily the highest score. Although Robert was found with a severed spinal cord after the recess, the Laser Quest place was to blame because of the faulty light that made it so dark. You leave satisfied and ready to take on Chris come NC-17. Result: Positive x2. Clyde won the game and now he’s totally focused for NC-17. You have given Clyde an intelligence boost for NC-17. Strain #2, Conclusion #2 Running into the wall didn’t actually hurt as bad as it seemed. You wobble around for a bit but you manage to shake out the cobwebs. You play through the rest of the first game. At the recess you have to go to the washroom again. There are a few children in there at this time and you simply enter a stall that you thought was empty. However, a child accidentally left the door unlocked as you enter. You scream like a pansy and kick the kid as you exit the stall. Robert comes storming in and listens to the child who is claiming that you touched him in his naughty area. Robert tells you that he thinks you are a sick, evil person and that you should probably best be going. Unabated, you leave thinking about how you should have kicked that kids ass. Result: Neutral. Although Clyde is somewhat angry, thanks to you he can now channel this anger into his match at NC-17. You gave Clyde an increase in brawling ability, but a decrease in calmness. END GAME Note: Whatever path you chose, Clyde will be ready for NC-17 in some capacity. The results of each situation don’t necessarily reflect what will happen / impact come NC-17. END
