Silent Scream

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ?? I awake, and I’m still in the back of the limo. Yes, I’m in a limo. The most evil and sadistic man on earth rides in a limo. No I don’t get carted back and forth in a hearse. It’s a character, I think. All the abuse, real, I think. Have you ever thought you can’t decipher from fact or fiction? This is my life. I wake up from bone chilling nightmares. I see my father beat my mother, I see my father rape the neighbors daughter. My younger brother is beaten and tortured by other children as well as my father. Did all of this happen? Or is this something I’m paid millions of dollars to think, to create this monster I am known as. All I do know is that this ride is making me crazy. I page the driver, I need something to drink. He says we’re in the middle of nowhere, the bars here are sketchy, he has some wine back here. I’m not interested in wine, it’s not my style. I convince him to stop at the first bar he see’s. I rub my hands through my eyes like a tired cranky infant. My hair is greasy and slick. My chest is throbbing. I don’t recall carving the Devil’s Number in my chest. Once again was I paid to do that, or is there some part of me that did it on a whim. The car slows to a stop, and stays idle. He asks me if he’d like some company, I said no. I’m 7 feet tall, well over 300 pounds, I think I can handle myself. Something should have warned me when I tried to open the door it stuck. I had to use some strength to get the damn thing open. It finally clunked open. I ducked a little and walked in. It was almost like in the movies, the little action happening stopped immediately, and they all stared at me. They were the rugged type. Long biker hair, leather chaps, their fat sweaty mamas guzzling beer. A few playing pool, some sitting at the bar, nodding out. The bartender looks like he’s 50. Graying everywhere. Thick heavy moustache, graying beard. He looks at, than kind of nods to a few guys in the corner. They nod back as well. I decided I don’t give a shit. I need something to drink. I approach the bar. The guys in the corner, move a little closer to the bar, almost mocking my every move. I sit down and ask for a beer. The bartender reaches into the ice, snaps off the cap and puts the drink down in front of me. He gives me a sneer, I shrug it off. After a few sips, I turn around on the stool and I’m introduced to a baseball bat to my face. I feel my face crush upon impact. I try to prevent my eyes from rolling in the back of my head, but they did. I was in black before my body slumped and hit the floor. I came to periodically, I feel blood gushing from my face, and 4 big burly men are dragging my by my feet towards the back of the bar. I think, not sure entirely, but my life was flashing before my eyes. However it’s not the good points. My career never once slipped into my unconsciousness with me. Instead the butchered childhood. My sadistic father, my whore of a mother, and my pansy younger brother. I kept seeing my father’s red eyes. His goddamn eyes were red. His tongue always seemed forked. I was the child of satan. I remember being tied down, face down, my hands tied to my bed. My father would straddle me, grab my long hair and choke me into unconsciousness. I’d come too, and he’d do it again. He once put his cigar out on the back of my neck. My mother was latered tied up and brutally raped by the hands of my father. I watched it all in morbid curiosity. With every thrust into her, he’d land a crushing blow to her face. Her blood made him hard. He’d lick the blood oozing from her face. He always yelled that she fucked better knocked out. I was so confused as to what that meant back than. I feel my body being rolled, than a drop, than a thud. It was a soft cold thud. A cold wetness lie beneath me. My eyes start to open, everything looks so far away. Everything seems so tunneled. Four faces peer down the tunnel at me and laugh. That’s when I realize I’m in a crypt. I look into the eyes of one of my attackers. I see my father. I look at another, and see my mother. They look at each other, smile and back away. I reach out for them, begging for them to help their child, for the first time ever help your goddamn son. They seem to neglect me again. I feel more cold fall on me. I open my eyes. My mother and father are now throwing dirt on me. They are burying me. Mom, Dad I’m not dead, please fucking help me! The dirt begins to pile up. I can’t move, I can’t even scream. My silent scream is left unheard. I close my eyes, and await my destiny?. I awake, and I’m still in the back of the limo. Yes, I’m in a limo. The most evil and sadistic man on earth rides in a limo.
