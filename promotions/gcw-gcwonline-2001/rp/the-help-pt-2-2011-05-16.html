The Help Pt. 2

16 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/16/11 Previously on the new "All My Children" spin-off "All My Togas"..... "Pregnant," was the only word I heard all day. It echoed over and over, louder and louder, inside of my head all day. I couldn't focus on anything. How could this be?! How could this have happened? How could I have let this happen?! This changes everything! I think the comedy-singer Jonathan Coulton said it best, "You ruined everything... in the nicest way." But this isn't a joke! Noooo! Not this time! It's obvious who the father is... but how do I tell everyone?! How do I break the news?! I wanted everyone to know at once, so I gathered them all around me and opened the flood gate uncertainty... The room was full. Everyone who was anyone showed up. Lollie, Katsy Kitsuna, Michael Reese, Tommy, Hunter, even Francis Fuzworth and Milenko were in attendance. Everyone knew what they were there for. It was obvious. The little one had already started showing. Soon, the Battle of the Bulge would begin... Lollie: "I'm sure you can all tell why I've gathered you all here, today." Their stares were piercing. But their expressions were dumbfounded. No one could have ever seen this coming. Lollie: "I think it might be best if I let Tommy explain the situation." All eyes were already focused on him. The normally-outgoing, silly, young man was not himself today. He was reserved inside his own mind. His cheeks were red with embarrassment. He wasn't proud of the situation he had gotten himself into. All of his dreams of a successful career in the Fashion industry were over. He was not to be the next Tommy Hilfiger. Not today. Not ever. Lollie: "Come on, Tommy. I think everyone here deserves an explanation... especially me..." All in attendance nod in agreement with the slim gal in green. Tommy cradles his belly, as he stares at the floor. Tommy: "I'm so sorry, everyone!" The skirt-clad fashion entrepreneur covers his eyes as the baby kicks inside of his belly.... Lollie: "Tommy, we need to know... who's the mother?" He glances up, scans all of his friends around him, then buries his face back into his hand. He points blindly, be he knows exactly who he's pointing at... Everyone gasps! Katsy: "YOU WHORE!" Tommy continues to cry, as Hunter quickly turns and runs away, obviously embarrassed of his part in this whole matter! Tommy looks up just in time to see his goth-lover leave the room. He calls out to him. Tommy: "Hunter! No! Please come back! I need you! Our baby needs you!" But his pleas fall on deaf ears; Hunter is already gone. Now Tommy must face the biggest challenge in his young and once promising life.... Being a single Mather. Tune in next week to see Tommy's shocking life-altering decision!........................ Maybe. ~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~ Now: Global Championship Wrestling The room is quiet. Toga lies in his own bed, motionless. Lollie and Hunter stand in the hallway just outside of it, watching him. They speak in whispers. Lollie: "He doesn't remember the incident. He doesn't even remember Bryan Mayhem yet." Hunter: "Maybe that's for the best. We both saw what happened to him when he saw Mayhem on the monitor. He began to seizure!" The concerned gal in green slowly nods. She keeps her eyes on Toga, asleep, through the doorway. Lollie: "But how can Tommy be in GCW and not encounter Mayhem? Or at least lay eyes on him? It's impossible. So either he has to give up his dream or we have to find a way for him to overcome this." Hunter: "Yeah... but how? Even with therapy, how can Toga ever overcome this? He's been severely traumatized by what happened in Belmont." She sighs. Hunter: "The kid's tough. I'll give him that. But he's not built for that. He never has been and never will be. He can take a hell of a beating... but he's still got scars on his neck. He was practically tortured in that fight. His body may be small but it's one of the toughest around... but his mind... it's so extremely fragile. Even more so now. We don't know how far the trauma extends into his psyche. We know that he barely remembers anything about you, even after weeks of reminders, and that he's almost-heart-stoppingly terrified of Mayhem. But what else?" Lollie parts her lips to reply... but the words don't come. She doesn't have all of the answers. She doesn't even have all of the questions. What can she possible say to Hunter? What can she possible say to Toga? Hunter: "Beyond that, Toga has been knocked out for four weeks in a row. He should not even be standing. Let alone wrestling. I think you need to cancel his match against Aimz. In fact, I think you need to make an executive decision and retire him. If he won't, then you need to make him." Silence. The air is full of silence. Somehow... the air is full of the sound of nothingness... How you ask? I have no idea... but there's definitely a full meal of silence on the plate for supper tonight. Eat up. Lollie shakes her head. Lollie: "I can't do that. You know that. The doctors ran the tests. He's got a completely clean bill of health... somehow. The doctors are as baffled as anyone. Not even one concussion. His personality has changed. Not enough that the audience will notice. But I most certainly have noticed. He's obviously traumatized, as you said. But I cannot take GCW away from him. I can't take wrestling away from him. He doesn't even remember me... how could I possibly convince him to leave the only thing that he has any interest in anymore?!" With that, Toga's eyes flicker open. He slowly sits up. Lollie and Hunter stare straight into the room and straight into the young man's eyes. Toga: "I feel kind of funky.....?" Tommy's voice cracks a bit, as if he hasn't spoken much in the past few days. The duo walk into the room. Hunter: "That's called puberty, kid. You'll get used to it..." The gal in green rolls her eyes. Lollie: "That's uncalled for, Hunter..." Toga: "Huhza wha?" The goth points down at the sheets... Hunter: "Tell that to Tommy Lee there!" Lollie finally notices Toga's unfortunately timed blood-flowage. She quickly grabs his pillow and throws it on her client's lap. Toga: "Huhza... how did he know my middle name?!" Hunter's eyebrows raise in surprise. He turns to Lollie... Hunter: "You're kidding me?! How come I was never told that his name is Tommy Lee?! Ohhh.. I gotta know his last name now!" The well-curved, nerdy gal shakes her head. Lollie: "No...." Hunter: "Why n-" Toga: "Tommy Lee Edison." Silence has come back for seconds. This time, Awkwardness has tagged along. Hunter: "Really?! His name is Thomas Edison?! How ironic!" A huge smirk covers the goth's caked face. Lollie sighs. Lollie: "Yes..." In completely serious, obliviousness Toga sits between them. Hunter: "And his name is ALSO Tommy Lee?! Wow! That's also completely ironic because of how small his-" Lollie: "HUSH!" Toga cocks his head to the side. Not really following. The young man spins his body around to sit on the edge of the bed. He tosses the blankets and pillow aside... Hunter's eyes inadvertently wander down below the camera-line.... Hunter: "HOLY SHIT!" The larger, goth man's jaw drops in shock. Lollie blushes. Tommy Lee is a muthafuckin rockstar! Lollie: "You can joke about his names all you want..... but at least ONE of them is accurate." Hunter: "I... I.... I............" Speechless. Completely speechless. Hunter drops his head in shame as he turns and walks out of the room. Lollie smiles a bit. Toga: "......... what?" The blushing beauty hands Tommy Lee something not common seen on him, a pair of pants. Lollie: "Oh... nothing.... Mr. Long... err.. Lee..." He takes the pants, as she covers her eyes and walks out of the room as well... Tommy puts the pants on. Toga: "What?!"
