Importance of Wrestling: Killing time (and Vampires?)

2 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2 June 07 “Fuck that shit man, you ain’t killin” it.” “The hell I ain’t ? I paid for the shit.” Jeremy Sams and Tony Sellers sit together on an old couch that sits in the middle of a very plain living room which is part of a very run down apartment. Today begins like any other day that these two best friends get together and attempt to find new ways to entertain themselves without actually moving from their seats on this couch. “Fuck you, you owe me from yesterday.” Jeremy claims. “What” I paid you!” Tony answers. “Whatever.” “Whatever!” Whatever?” “You heard me.” Jeremy brings the roach to his lips and attempts to smoke it. Tony knocks it out of his hands. “What the hell did you do that for!”” “You were going to smoke it!” “You said whatever!” “Huh?” “What?” Now the two fold their arms across their chest and face forward, looking at a black TV screen. The probability of this TV not being on because these two are too lazy to actually stand up and turn it on? 99.8% likely. “Hey man,” Jeremy turns to Tony. “Why isn’t the TV on?” “Does it even work?” “I think so.” “Then turn it on.” Jeremy says. “Is there anything on?” “Has to be ? it’s TV.” “Where’s the remote?” “We have one of those?” “Shit.” And this is pretty much the typical day for these two young men. They have been friends since the first grade, meeting each other because they both were being beaten up outside of school by the same 4th grader, Tommy Jarvis ? what an asshole he was. Tony and Jeremy have argued for years on exactly whatever happened to him, Tony says suicide and Jeremy says he was raped by a goat. Since that time they have been inseparable doing everything from playing football, chasing girls, driving, drinking, and yes doing drugs together. They were around each other so often growing up it was no surprise that they ended up getting an apartment together as soon as they graduated high school. Now today may have began like any other day for these two but little did they know that they were in for quite the journey. “Dude,” Tony began. “Why exactly do you have a half naked man on your wall?” “Tony, you ask me that every time we smoke.” “Then why do you have a half naked man on your wall?” “We both watch wrestling asshole.” “But I don’t post naked men on my wall.” “That IS your wall!” Tony looks around the apartment as if seeing it for the first time. “touch” “Where’d you learn that word from?” “Well I ? HEY! Don’t change the subject!” “What!”” “Naked man! Your Wall! WHY!”” “Oh! Dude that’s LeStatt Knight!” “Holy fuck!” “Huh?” “The Vampire!”” “What?” “That fucking vampire from that movie!” “What movie?” “The written by that Rice bitch.” “OH!” Jeremy yells. “Dude I don’t think they are the same dudes.” “But how can you tell?” “Would they let a vampire wrestle with humans” Come on now! He’d eat them alive.” Both of them laugh at this unintentional joke. Eat Them Alive Har Har Har “Seriously though,” Jeremy says. “You really think they are the same person?” Tony looks from Jeremy to the poster of LeStatt and back too Jeremy. “Well they don’t look alike ? I think the guy from the movie was much shorter, whiter, and Tom Cruise-ier.” “Word.” “BUT!” Tony jumps from his seat as if he has just had some sort of epiphany. “Aren’t vampires shape shifters?” Both of their eyes go wide as they look at each other. The two have just come to the realization that LeStatt Knight may REALLY be a blood sucking vampire. Slowly Tony sits back down, his eyes never getting any smaller. “Dude?” It was the only word that Tony let escape his lips. This was some heavy stuff that these young men had just realized. At least to them. Jeremy stands up now and begins to pace around the room, trying to figure out exactly how to remedy this issue that the two boys have run into. “Well,” He says thoughtfully. “How can we really find out for sure?” Tony’s head turns and looks at the blank TV screen again. “Well it’s pretty simple.” “Is it?” “Yes.” “Well?” “Well what?” “Explain!” “OH!” Tony clears his throat. “Well I think we have to do the unthinkable.” “Get sober?” Tony, by reflex jumps to his feet and backhands Jeremy. “Never say that again!” The room remains silent for what seems to be an eternity but in all actuality it is only a few seconds. “Man did you just slap me?” “I think I did.” “What the fuck man”!” Slowly they take their seats once again but never letting their eyes off of each other. “The unthinkable is of course go and find him.” Tony says. “Find him?” “Find him.” “How?” “The smart way of course.” “Hang a virgin by a tree and wait for the scent to get to him.” Jeremy says as if it was the most important thing he had ever said before. “Well that could work.” Tony smirks. “Virgins can be fun.” “Sh-yeah!” “BUT I was thinking more along the lines of a good ole fashioned vampire hunt.” “A vampire hunt you say?” “That is exactly what I said.” “How do you even do that?” How does one go about performing a genuine vampire hunt? Of course neither Jeremy nor Tony has even considered the possibility that there is no genuine vampire hunt because there are no such things as vampires. “First of all we have to figure out all of the information that we know about our suspected blood sucking fiend.” Tony claims. “Blood sucking fiend” I like it.” “Stay on track here Jer.” “Roger that.” “How long have you been following Mr. Knight’s so-called “wrestling” career?” “Since he lost to Zippy.” “Is that a stripper?” “I think he was too young to be.” “Not in Europe.” “Indeed.” The two nod in agreement. “So,” Tony continues. “Was he the same person he is today?” “No ? he sucked back then.” Jer recalls. “Same size?” “Fuck if I remember, he may have been smaller.” “Hmmm ? maybe he was turned later on.” “Maybe.” Tony begins slowly walking around the couch with Jeremy only a few steps behind him. “Well when did Knight become a “good wrestler?”” “Couple years ago when GCW re-opened man.” “AH HA!” Jeremy jumps back because of the quick movement of Tony ? stoned people can’t deal with quick movements. “GAH!” “He must have been turned then!” Tony declares. “I’m still confused though, why would the GCW let a vampire wrestle?” “Well does he usually wrestle people much larger than him?” “Well,” Jeremy begins. “His match at Warpath is against a chick.” “A chick?” Tony chuckles. “Must be the virgin sacrifice to keep him happy.” “Shit I’d be sure to make Vivica NOT a virgin before I drained her of her blood, hot damn!” Our two “heroes” continue their walk around their beat up couch looking like a detective team in one of those old black and white films. They would almost look genuine if it wasn’t for the blood shot eyes that are only half open. “Alright,” Tony begins. “Well at least we know where we can find Knight.” “Warpath.” “Of course my trusty sidekick.” “Why do I have to be the sidekick?” “Let’s remain on topic here.” Tony continues. “We need to go to Warpath and find out if Knight really is our vampire and if he is we need to do one of two things.” “Which are?” “Kill him or make him turn us into vampires ? could you imagine being able to get stoned for the rest of eternity.” “HOLY HELL!” Jeremy hoots. “Dude let’s go right now!” Jeremy tries running towards a closet door but Tony kicks the back of his legs causing him to sprawl on the floor. “OUCH!” “Hold up speedy we need the proper tools to hunt a vampire!” “Oh,” Jeremy stands, dusting himself off. “I know we’ll need wooden stakes!” “We have toothpicks.” Tony says quickly. “I’m sure that’ll be good enough.” “I know we’ll need holy water too.” Tony says. “Well ? (Jeremy looks around the room) we have some Lemon flavored water!” “Quick thinking, I like that!” “Vampires hate the sun too right?” Jeremy asks. “True but how do we use that against him inside of a building?” “A really, really strong flashlight.” Jeremy nods triumphantly. “God Damn genius!” “Is there anything else though?” “In Blade they were allergic to garlic and silver.” “We got garlic bread from the restaurant last night!” “And a bottle of Patron Silver!” Without sharing another word the two friends scatter and begin stockpiling the items that they just went over to perform a real live vampire hunt. It doesn’t take them very long to acquire the items but longer than it would take two sober people. When they have all of their gear they meet back at the couch. “Ok looks like we have everything.” Jeremy says. “Right” OH! There is one last thing that we should do first.” The two give each other a knowing look, as if reading each other’s minds. “Smoke one.” They say in unison. Tony and Jeremy flop down on the couch in the same spots that they were in when they began this journey. Tony pulls out a joint, Jeremy pulls out a lighter and the two combine to make a masterpiece. A thin line of smoke floats into the air. Tony and Jeremy each take a hit and then lean back, sinking into the couch. A minute turns into five Five into ten Ten into twenty Twenty into ? being high as shit. Tony turns to Jeremy. “Ya know man, maybe Knight isn’t even such a bad guy ? vampire or not.” “Dude I’m a huge fan of his.” “I mean he has a big day tomorrow, going into Warpath and all.” “I know I’ll be rooting for him.” “Maybe we should give him a day off ? hunt him down in a few days instead.” “I respect him that much.” “Yeah.” The two zero in on the blank TV screen once more. “Another thing,” Tony turns to look at Jeremy. “What’s up?” “I don’t know where the fuck Warpath is being held anyways.” The two bust up laughing now. Laughter turns into crying Then their stomachs hurt from laughing so hard. And then our heroes forget about their Vampire hunt. “Shit man!” Jeremy says. “I think the Warpath pre-view shit is on now.” “Turn it on man!” “Where’s the remote?” silence. “We have one of those?” THE END
