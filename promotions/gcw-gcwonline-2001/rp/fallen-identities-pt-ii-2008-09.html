Date: Early September "Religion became a hot sport after Joan of Arc burned at the stake." ??how do you know his name?” Taylor looks over on the desk where the notebook rests, holding the picture so dearly between its pages. “It is not how I know that is the question, it is what I know. What I know is that you need to know what I know.” Taylor shakes his head in confusion and narrows his eyes to a penetrating stare. “What’s the matter Zachary, the priest is taunting your intelligence?” A very serious look comes over his face and he raises his hands into prayer. “En Nomeni Patri et Fili Spiritus Sancti. Grant my son, Zacharia Taylor the wisdom and capacity to comprehend the knowledge of his mentor, Christian Thomas.” At this prayer, Zacharia starts to build up anger. There is no fathomable way that this guy is a priest, nor trained under Father Emanuel. He is witty and smart, you have to give him that, but he is sarcastic and demeaning to the very existence of the legacy that Father Emanuel tried to establish. Taylor can no longer take his games. “What you speak of is just mockery of this church and your tactics are just a disgrace to the memory of Father Emanuel. [Christian tries to interrupt, but Taylor speaks louder, putting his hand into the air as if trying to silence him.] Much has happen to me in the last two days, I need to sit and reflect in the teachings of our mentor. In his memory, I want to find the killer, and if you have any relevant information, please don’t hesitate to share, because I am at a dead end.” Christian bows his head with a menacing grin. He does a slight curtsy and then stands tall. His figure is almost a replica of Zacharia’s, being of the same height, and similar build. The two men stare down one another, Christian still holding a very smart grin. “Jacob Fenning..” Christian belts out. “He’s dead.” “So are you.” “He was in a car accident.” “You were burnt to a crisp and fell out of the window of your house.” “Don’t believe everything you read in the newspapers.” “Glorious advice, you should save some for yourself.” The two men now stand toe to toe. Zacharia strains every muscle in his body from stopping a devastating punch or kick into his counterpart. “Zachary?” “The name is Zacharia?” “That’s right, the newspaper” Zacharia, are you familiar with William Wilson?” “Who?” ?[under a quick breath] Didn’t think so. Well, take a seat, you may find this interesting.” Zacharia complies and sits down at his desk chair as Christian starts to pace the room, as if searching for something . He comes across two pencils and picks them up. “William Wilson was a man that had a doppelganger that looked and sounded identical to himself. He went around the school, trying to master his accomplishments, but the doppelganger, with the same name, seemed to master each identically. Wilson leaves the school, only to find out that the other Wilson left on the same day as well. No matter where he went, his doppelganger went as well, but masked. Eventually they meet up at a ball and they are identically dressed and have the exact same mannerisms. Wilson kills off his doppelganger.” Zacharia stares at him in confusion. “So you are saying that Jake had a doppelganger that nobody knew about” So, he killed off this person in the faking of his own death? Or is it that the doppelganger killed Jake, and the imposter killed Father Emanuel?” “You’re not making any sense, kid!” Zacharia slumps back down in his seat in frustration. “It is a metaphor for identity. The doppelganger was himself, just his other conscious was what he saw in the other self. It is a lot more than a good verses evil story, it is identity. If we brought this into our situation, we can look back at good ol” Jakey and think of something a little more common. Such as?.” Zacharia sits in his chair waiting or the answer, not noticing that Christian was prompting him to say the answer. He starts to lip the words a little. “a ttttt”. A ttttttt? twwwwww?.Oh come on kid! Are you that thick? A BROTHER! A TWIN! BORN FROM THE SAME MOTHER, LOOKING IDENTICAL!” “I’m sorry! My mind is a little spent, cut me some slack.” “Oh, I will cut you some slack once you tighten up the slack so we no longer have any slack to cut. You need to get your head into this so you can go out there and do it is that you do.” ??and what is it that I do, exactly? You don’t know anything about me!” Christian continues with that penetrating grin. “What I am suggesting is that Jakey may not be dead after all! Did you know that good ol” Jakey from Old Ridge Road, the house you passed by yesterday, was adopted?” Zacharia stands up flabbergasted. Adopted? Jacob? He was younger than Jake, so he doesn’t remember when he was really young. It was never brought up in conversation. Wait’how di? ??d you know I went to Oak Ridge?” “Come on Zachy?” “It’s Zacharia!” “So testy! I followed you yesterday. I got to the church as you walked outside, I jumped back into my car and followed you to Oak Ridge. It was only then that I went to the Library to see what I can find about Oak Ridge. I already knew about your family and your life story. Tragic, I know. At the library I found some articles on Jakey, but it wasn’t until I searched the birth records that I found that Jakey’s last name isn’t Fenning, it was really, now here is he crazy part” Taylor.” “Bu” What?” “There you go with that articulation again. No, not your parents. I just said it was crazy, I never said anything of any relevance as he being linked to you. Like I said before, you want to know what I know.” “Why would they adopt the one brother, and not the other?” “The perfect Fenning family only wanted one child. With the tragic track record that Jerome had, mischief, destruction, and misbehavior, it was an easy choice for them. Jakey went off to the perfect family, becoming an honors student and the perfect athlete, while Jerry rotted in a group home. Tragic, so sad. Can you play violin?” “Violin?” “Never mind, kid. So, there you have it. We are not looking for Jakey, he is six feet under, we are looking for his disturbed brother, Jerry.” Zacharia sits, trying to take everything in. Is Christian really telling him the truth? “How do you know all of this” I’m certain it isn’t in the library.” “Do you think I studied under Father Emanuel for all those years for nothing” I am a priest. It is called confession. Not everyone is as disturbed as you are, but people still confess and repent.” Zacharia stands up and gets into Christian’s face. “We are both men of religion, both men of the Lord, so at least show me some damn respect.” Zacharia pushes past Christian, greatly intensified by the grin he still possesses, and starts for the door. He turns back around and looks at Christian, still staring down Zacharia. “Let me ask you something, what did you actually study under Father Emanuel, Christian?” “I stood where you stand now. “And as the heavens open up and shine new light onto a new finger of the Almighty, he that shall be named a mercenary of God shall rise into the light and push forth through the darkness.” I walked the very streets that you do, I fought for the very cause that you did. I chose knowledge over physics in the end, becoming a priest in a different district.” Satisfied with his response, Zacharia walks out the door. He wanders aimlessly down the corridor of the back of the church, leading into the basement. With the light off, it is very hard to make his way through the different boxes and clutter. He eventually just sits down randomly. Seclusion. He was used to it. He wants to be alone, away from Christian for a moment. His head hurt from everything that he was just told. The question is, is everything he said real? Does Jake really have a brother? Is he really adopted? Is this all a masquerade that Christian is planning so he can mess with his head? What is the real purpose of Christian being here? “Taylor, I’d like to talk to you about some things. I want to get to know you a little bit better. Get a feel of where you stand in your role.” Zacharia shakes his head and stands up. He slowly makes his way back to the door. He finds Christian standing there in the doorway, awaiting his arrival. He motions towards the back door, and he is reluctant to oblige. He steps down onto the bottom step, picks up a rock, and places it in the doorway so the door cannot close. Taylor looks around the green courtyard and looks over at Christian, still with his infamous stare. “I want to see what you have to offer.” “What are you talking about?” Christian unbuttons his shirt and takes it off, revealing the true nature of his build. To Zacharia’s surprise, he is much more muscular than he expected. “Are you kidding?” “I think the phrase you are looking for is, “Are you sure you want to fight?” because, Zacharia, I am not joking.” Christian pulls off his undershirt, revealing several scars across his stomach. As Zacharia takes off his shirt, his gruesome scars catch the eye of Christian. “Looks like you had a run in with some fire, huh?” “Looks like you like to swallow the knife instead or evade it, huh?” The grin intensifies. He puts up his hands and waves on Zacharia. Zacharia flexes his back and arms, getting good stretch, then goes towards Christian. He throws a quick jab at Christian’s face, but it is swatted away as quickly as it was thrown. Zacharia smiles and relaxes his body slightly. He throws a fake jab in the same manner, but quickly kicks at Christian’s left leg. To his surprise, once again, he is blocked, but countered in the same motion, throwing Zacharia to the ground. He shakes his head, looking up at Christian. “You are fast, I can give you that, kid, but you aren’t smart. You think your quickness and strength can carry you, but fighting is a lot more than that. Any man can train their muscles in the gym, but not every man has the capacity to become an intelligent fighter.” Zacharia sweep kicks Christian, but misses, and he jumps to his feet. He raises his hands once again and throws a powerful cross. He connects, but sacrifices footing. Christian nails him hard in the chest, causing Zacharia to once again fall. “You need to learn the capacity of your body, as well as acknowledge everything around you. If you could learn to focus your mind on your body and your opponent’s you would be a much more efficient fighter.” “What I do accomplishes what I need?” “Until this very moment in the courtyard where I have put you on your back twice. Fighting simple street thugs is one ting, fighting someone who knows what they are doing is another.” Christian waves for Zacharia to stand. “Now, I will go on the offensive. If I step right, you step right, unless you can find a counter on the left.” Christian comes in with a barrage of slow jabs, every punch getting slightly faster than the other. Zacharia keeps focus and evades or blocks every punch, but as the intensity increases, Christian starts moving around. He gets caught by a slick jab to the chin, but he doesn’t break concentration. Suddenly, Christian breaks formation and throws a hook. Zacharia swats it away with a strong right arm, pushing Christian’s body awkwardly to the side as he sweeps with his right foot, knocking Christian to the ground. Christian lands, still grinning. “Well done, kid. With little formal training, I think that you will be in good shape. You need to demonstrate discipline instead of going into every fight knowing that you are going to win. Your conceitedness cannot compromise your precision. You know what you can do, so focus, and do it. The Almighty punishes us for our mistakes and misguidance, as you already know.” Christian looks down at Zacharia’s chest once again. He bows his head in respect and grabs his shirts on the ground while brushing off he grass from his pants and back. Taylor picks up his shirt and heads to the church door. _______________________________________________________ Zacharia kneels down in front of the cross behind the alter and starts to pray. Everything around him looses power of any sound, not even the droning hum of the air conditioner can be heard. His body is in total tranquility. He starts to reflect on what he heard earlier about Father Emanuel’s death and the Fenning brother conspiracy. To him, it still seems impossible for this to be true, knowing the Fenning’s for the first quarter of his life. His eyes tighten so everything becomes black. Nothingness. He begins a silent prayer. While the words wiz through his head, images start to develop in his head. He is unsure what he is looking at, for the faces are blurry. There are two men talking to one another, and a newspaper is handed off to the bigger man. Suddenly the scene shifts to a masked man in some kind of store, but everything is knocked over and a mess. The image shifts down and there is a man in all black laying there, bleeding. Again, the image shifts but to a new scene. It is now of the church, a man walking towards a kneeling Zacharia at the alter. The man hastens his pace and draws a knife. The knife gleams in the sunlight, about to strike Zacharia. Zacharia swiftly turns around, throwing his hand across the air, striking an outstretched hand of Christian’s. Zacharia reacts so sudden that he does not realize that it is Christan standing in front of him. He throws another hand at Christian’s abdomen, keeling him over, until the burst of words comes into reality. “Zacharia, it’s me! It’s Christian!” Snapping back into reality, Zacharia stops mid swing and falls over. He rubs his eyes and shakes his head. Looking down at the man keeled over next to him, He begins to feel remorse yet cannot get over what he sees’a newspaper lying next to Christian. The newspaper is older, not having the crisp gray look that a recent newspaper possesses. Across the front page is says, “Car Accident Kills Local Teenager”. “There was a guy that stopped by, and he delivered this. [Christian points to the newspaper] I found it peculiar that this was the one delivered.” “Let me see that.” Zacharia starts to read though it, reliving earlier that day when he read through it at the library. It wasn’t until he saw a circle around three words that he found it most peculiar. “Look, “burnt beyond recognition” is circled. Who dropped this?” “I have no idea who he was. He just gave it to me, all nervous and ran away before I could even see what was in my hands. I was going to run after him but when I looked down at the paper and noticed what it was, the guy was already gone.” Zacharia looks at him, reading his lies. If his vision as anything true, he knows that the two men talked for a few moments, looked at him praying, and then the man walked away. True enough, Christian didn’t have a knife, but he was standing behind him. “That is strange. I wander if it was the same guy that dropped off the message and the picture.” “Message?” Zacharia looks over at Christian, forgetting that he doesn’t know about the message. Yes, he did know about he picture? “Yes. The man delivered a message with the picture. It said, “Don’t shoot the messenger”.” “Interesting. Think it has anything to do with what was instructed of the shooter” That that is what someone told Jerry, and then he killed Father Emanuel anyway because he didn’t understand the message? Do you think that it was an apology from someone, and they were sending you the same instructions that Jerry received, almost as if they were giving you proof that he was not supposed to die?” Zacharia looks at Christian. For the first time Christian looks intrigued and unknowing. Before, he had all the answers, but now, he is talking things out with Zacharia. He looks at the newspaper and then back at Christian. “Christian, do you really think that it had anything to do with more than a taunt at the fact that the messenger of God was killed” It was a taunt at me because I could not get to the church in time.” “Zacharia, you keep saying that. How do you know that you would ever been able to save him. God has a plan for everything. What if you had been there, you may have been shot as well.” “I guess you hadn’t noticed, but I was shot in the leg.” Christian looks at Zacharia’s leg and then turns around. “I meant killed Zacharia.” Christian walks away towards the back door of the church, leading into the hall where the priest’s quarters is. Zacharia grabs the hood that is next to him and puts it on. He looks behind him and sees that the sun’s going down. Perfect. He blesses himself, kisses the cross around his neck and heads for the front door of the church in an angry march. I am not dealing with this anymore by sitting in a church. I need answers? Zacharia throws open the church doors and lets them slam closed on his own. The eagle knockers bounce on the church door several times before they come to a silencing halt. He looks both ways and then decides to go left down he street, knowing that the delivery boy went left last time he saw him. He passes the park that he called home for far too long, and keeps walking. Two men stand on the corner of the street up ahead, turning towards him as he gets closer. “What’s up son” Wanna buy some shit tonight? I got shit to ice the blood or shit to turn it red fuckin hot!” The second guy smiles and slaps the dealer’s hands out of reward for saying his key line. All dealers are the same; stupid. “What I want is you to get out of my way so I can get on my way.” “Alright kid, all you had to do is say so. Get on you’re motha fuckin way then, you two cent fucking piece of shit.” “Oh baby, nice cross you got there. How much you want for it?” Zacharia looks over at the second guy. “This is not for sale.” The second guy pulls out a gun while the dealer laughs. “I’ll make it for sale, nigga, now give it up or give yours up!” Zacharia slowly takes off the chain and hands it over to the guy with the gun. He lowers the gun to take the cross, but Zacharia punches the hand holding it, causing the firearm to go off, shooting the handler in his foot. Screaming in pain, he falls over onto the curb and leaves the dealer to fend for himself. In a swift motion the dealer goes for a knife, but Zacharia nails him across the face, fist closed around the cross, and knocks two of his teeth in. Both men lay screaming on the corner for help, but nothing comes out to help them. Zacharia picks up the gun and throws it back into the park, but then picks up the knife and opens it up. Putting the knife to the dealer’s throat, he starts to talk. “A white guy walked down here about five minutes ago, where did he go?” “I hab no ibea whab oo alkin bout?” Zacharia puts the knife enough into his neck to draw blood, adding to the massive amounts of blood pouring out of his mouth. The handler, still holding his foot, speaks up. “Man, the bastard saw us and then ran into the park. The nigga never ran this way’fuck.” Zacharia takes the knife, closes it, and puts it in his pocket. “Thank you for your cooperation.” Zacharia walks into the park and follows the path straight through for the other entrance. He comes out open to a liquor store. He walks in and goes to the clerk. “Hello, sir. I was wondering if you saw anyone walk out of the park in the last ten minutes or so?” “Well there were two men, I didn’t see the one man’s face, but the other guy walked in here, grabbed a bottle of whiskey and left.” “Was he white?” “No sir, he was black.” The messenger was white, he knew that for sure. So it must have been the other guy. Where did he go? “Do you have a bathroom” I need to clean my hand.” The clerk looks down at his hand, seeing two teeth marks on his knuckle, emitting a lot of blood. The clerk nods, pointing back to the door next to the refrigerators. The clerk watches him go to the back door and closes it. The bathroom was very tight and limiting. He takes he sanitizing hand soap on the sink and covers his hand in it. Washing it out for a few moments, he takes a wad of paper towels and wraps them around the cut. He rushes water over his face and then dabs it try with another paper towel. It was time to catch him. Zacharia opens the door and starts for the front door. He looks over to the side and sees a line of bottle openers, one catching his interest; a cross. He picks it up and laughs to himself that it was such desecration for a cross to be used as a bottle opener. He lifts it a little higher to read something on it. [crashing glass] Zacharia drops the bottle opener and looks to the front of the store, there was a masked man walking through the now broken front door. Zacharia regains control of his body and starts to throw a couple jabs, pushing the masked man back into open ground in front of the clerk’s counter. He waits for he perfect moment and then throws his strong cross to the side of the guys ace, but misses, leaving himself open for a strong uppercut. The masked man waits for Zacharia to come to. “Not so tough, are you?” “Why did you kill’Father Emanuel?” “Why” For fun! To get to you!” The masked man goes for Zacharia’s feet, but Zacharia kicks him n the face. As the man stumbles backwards, he takes his time getting back to his feet. He takes off his hood and then spears the man to the ground. He tries to take off the man’s mask, but he feels a incredibly sharp pain go into his shoulder. His arm goes limp and he rolls over in pain. He fingers his shoulder, trying to find the source of the pain and finds a knife sticking out. As he pulls it out and rolls back over, he finds the masked man’s shoe heading for his face. ________________________________________ “Zacharia” Zacharia? Can you hear me? Come on, wake up!” “Chri?” “He’s awake.” His eyes start to focus on a very blurry individual in front of him, but it is the voice that gave him away. Christian was standing over him, looking like he was just in a fight. Figured I could help out, but he knocked me good in the face and was able to run out of here. He was dead as I pulled up here.” Why would they kill him? He was hiding behind the counter. He never saw the guy’s face. He was wearing a mask! “I do not want to go to the hospital. You know how to stitch right?” Christian laughs and puts Zacharia in the passenger seat of the car. When the door closes, a police officer walks towards the car. It was the same officer that came to the church earlier, to make sure everything was ok. Zacharia nods at him, and the officer closes his notebook in agreement that he will answer later. Right now, Zacharia just wants to get back to the church, get stitched up, and talk to Christian about what happen. Maybe everything was going to be like it used to be. Maybe Christian will stand in for Father Emanuel and help out Zacharia. He just wants to find the killer. Jake/Jerry, whoever it is. This is far from over?
