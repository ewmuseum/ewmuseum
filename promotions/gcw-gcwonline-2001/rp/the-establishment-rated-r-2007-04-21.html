The Establishment! (Rated R)

21 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/21/07 Davis sat at the bar of KGB and smiled at Janine. “What’ll you have, honey?” she asked. “Brooklyn Lager, sweetheart.” The two were starting to become a nice couple. Most of the waitresses that knew Janine well were giggling whenever Davis walked in. Janine used to get hit on a lot, but she never went on a date with anyone from the bar. Davis never hit on her, but kept walking her home. Every night he came to KGB he would walk Janine to her apartment in Hell’s Kitchen and the two became good friends, which then inevitably led to going up to her apartment and chatting, then spending the night, then sleeping together. They’re a good couple. Whether Davis walked her home because he liked her or because of the caffeine in a Twistodium tablet she didn’t know. But now things were going well. Janine brought Davis a pint of B. Lager and leaned over the bar. “Are you doing okay?” She looked a little worried, caring. He’d had a rough week. Not only had he been beaten and humiliated, but people had been coming up to him since High Stakes and asking him if he was going to go back and wrestle. No one had been mean about it, but just the idea that people thought he would be done was worse than the beating he took. Davis nodded. “Yeah, I’m okay.” “Are you sure?” she asked again. “What do you think about it” About them?” she added. “Let me tell you a story,” he said. Davis took a swig from his beer to clear his throat and then clapped his hands together. “All right, so Steven Caldera, Jordan White, Sage Black and LeStatt Knight walk into a promoter’s office. The office is pretty plain, there’s a tall fern in one corner, a lamp in another, a mahogany desk, a picture of the promoter’s family on the wall and an Oriental rug on the floor. Steven Caldera tells the promoter, “We’ve got a great stable for you.” The promoter says, “Okay, let’s see it.” So Steven looks at LeStatt and spits right in his face. Knight doesn’t even flinch. He’s like Miho in Sin City. Steven then knees LeStatt in the balls and Knight doubles over. This is when Sage pulls down Knight’s pants and starts spanking him on the ass, alternating cheeks.” Janine cocks one eyebrow. “I don’t know if I want to hear this.” Davis smiles a shit-eating grin. “Steven turns to Jordan and takes the US title from him. Caldera unbuttons his pants and pulls them down before taking a big steaming shit onto the title belt. And his anus lives up to his last name, let me tell you. Just blood and feces coming out. Not overdoing it, mind you, but it’s clear and noticeable. Jordan White, shirtless, takes a bottle of champagne out from behind his back, shakes it up and then pops the cork. The champagne goes all over the foursome. He places the half empty bottle on the ground then and goes looking for the cork. This is when Steven stands up, shitless, and takes a long yellow piss into the bottle of champagne.” “That better not be foreshadowing.” A giggle cracks through Davis. “So then LeStatt stands up straight, ass as red as Elmo’s fur. He lifts Sage up into a scoop slam and throws him into the lamp. The light breaks and now they’re all in darkness save for the glaring eye of moonlight that has reached into the office.” At this point some of the other patrons and waitresses are watching Davis recite this “story.” He turns to a waitress at the end of the bar. “If you missed anything I’ll start at the beginning.” Turning back to Janine, “Anyway, Steven, having finished draining the lizard drops to his knees and starts sucking LeStatt’s long, hard vampire bat. Really he’s just stalling though, waiting for Jordan to- Oh! Sure enough Jordan White has found the missing cork. He spits on Sage’s broken body and sticks the cork in Steven’s ass before kicking it further and deeper and deeper and further. Two tears squeeze out of Steven’s eyes from the pain. Finally LeStatt picks up the champagne bottle and drinks the mix of urine and alcohol until every drop is gone. He smashes the empty bottle over Jordan’s head, knocking him out and then stabs Caldera in the chest with a jagged piece. He then burps and the promoter realizes that it’s finally over.” Most of the bar is now paying close, but disgusted, attention. “The promoter asks LeStatt, the only conscious performer left, “What do you call a stable like that?” Knight smiles bright and shouts, “The Establishment!”” There’s a long silence as the vile and viscous story sits in everyone’s mind. No one quite knows what to say or do. No one is laughing. Finally Janine breaks the silence with a simple statement. “I don’t get it.” Davis shrugs and takes a long drink from his beer. “Maybe I told it wrong.”
