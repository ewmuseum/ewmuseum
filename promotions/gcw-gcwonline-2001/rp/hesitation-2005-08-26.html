Hesitation

26 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Friday, August 26th Maybe I'm jaded after living my entire life in New York City. Maybe I'm jaded after traveling all over the country and realizing that after a while every single city starts to look the same. Maybe I just haven't had enough sleep after smiling and taking fucking pictures all day long. But as I drove back to the hotel after doing my part at this FanFest shit that Caldera organized, I realized something. As much as I loved the sound of the fans booing me, and wrestling in front of them, crushing their heroes and pissing them off, I hated meeting with them and listening to them gush over us like we're goddamn superheroes. I wanted to puke everytime I heard someone say 'I love you! You're the best!' Like I needed these brain dead morons to tell me that. I know I'm great, I don't need some pimple faced kid who probably spends most of his time in front of the computer than he does with members of the opposite sex to tell me that. My face hurt from the fake smiles I plastered on my face all day everytime someone wanted to take a picture with me. Fact of the matter was, if I wasn't getting paid, I would have spent the day sleeping. I was tired as hell. My body still hurt from the beating Bryan Mayhem gave me yesterday. I hadn't gotten a decent night's sleep, and I missed the breakfast buffet this morning. I've had bad days, and this one ranked up there as one of them. I was glad I only had to do this shit for one day, I'm sleeping in tomorrow. Tomorrow. Shit. I almost forgot. Cindy's flying in tomorrow so she can be at Annihilation, and her plane lands at ten thirty tomorrow morning. Well, maybe once we get back to the hotel we can sleep in. I miss sleeping with her in my arms. And waking up with her in my arms. I got back to the hotel and got my messages, one from Cindy reminding me of what time the plane landed, one from Caldera thanking me for taking part in FanFest, and one from Jeff wishing me good luck before the PPV. Once back in my room, I grabbed a beer, kicked off my Sketchers and plopped down on the couch and turned on the TV and scanned the channels until I found a b-movie on cable. I didn't really watch it because I couldn't concentrate. I still had that image of the Marauder standing there with a chair stopped short of bashing my skull in. The dipshit made a huge mistake there. If it was me, I would have nailed his ass. But he didn't, he stopped. He let me walk out of the ring under my owm power. He had the chance to take me out, to incapacitate me before Annihlation and didn't. If it was me I would have beat him until the chair was a useless hunk of metal and he was a useless hunk of bleeding flesh at my feet. He made a huge mistake not hitting me. It reminded me of something Killer Kowalski told me back when he was training me. When I stopped short of nailing my sparring partner with a spinebuster during a practice session. He said, 'Never, ever hesitate in there. Don't ever second guess yourself, all your opponent needs is that one split second of indecision on your part and they can finish you off or flatten you like a Mac truck. They won't hesitate to hit you, so you hit them first!' Then he made me run 20 laps. Thanks Killer. It was information I took with me when I moved on. Anytime I had the upper hand I pressed my advantage and never hesistated or second guessed myself. I went in the ring, did what I needed to and left. When I got in Hacksaw's face all those years ago, it earned me the IWO World title, when I returned from my knee injury and turned heel and challenged Machine Gun Smith to a title match to win back the title I never lost, I won that match, when I attacked Mike Haggar three days before a pay-per-view title match, it helped me retain. I did what I had to do to further my career, even if it was at the expense of others. Especially if it was at the expense of others. If our positions had been reversed, the Marauder would have been lying in a pool of his own blood. I woulda ripped that mask off his face and bashed in the face underneath the mask. The rumor is it was Johnny Lexicon under the mask. I remember him from watching Jeff when he was in 3W. Lex had been the champion, and at one point was also the Hardcore champ before Jeff relived him of that title. Why the mask? I don't know. Maybe for the same reason Dusty Rhodes became the Midnight Rider, Brian Pillman became the Yellow Dog, and the late Owen Hart became the Blue Blazer. Because just like those guys, I think people weren't supposed to realize that wasn't Lexicon under there...if it was him. I never really paid that much attention. Hmm...my beer is empty and the end credits are rolling. Well fuck it, I got a long day tomorrow. I get to see my wife before I kick the shit out of the Marauder. I turned off the TV and tossed out the bottle. I headed to the bedroom and set my alarm clock before changing from my jeans into a pair of shorts. I tossed my shirt into the corner as well and slipped into bed. Tomorrow was going to be a long day and I needed....ZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ............
