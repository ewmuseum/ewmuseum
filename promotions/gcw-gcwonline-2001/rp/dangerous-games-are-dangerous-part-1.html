Dangerous Games Are Dangerous: Part 1

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October some point. Atken: So here I was the other day, screwing the brains out of Ms. Dickwood, Non-Agent and suddenly Dirk barges into the door looking all mad. No idea why, even he acknowledges she's an awful kid-eating hambeast of a woman and the only reason I even take her from behind is because she gave me Dirk's credit card. Dickwood: Why are you telling me this, I was involved in the situation. Atken: I'm not telling you the story you worthless puddle of filth, I'm telling the camera the story. Anyway, Dirk's wife then makes the suggestion... Dickwood: Sorry to cut off the story of the horrific events of last week again but why is there a camera here? Atken: GCW sent it over, apparantly I'm involved in some sort of game next week, a dangerous one I have found out on good authority. Dickwood: Wouldn't a dangerous style game not be very beneficial to your health? Atken: I dunno. Dickwood: So you're not sure if getting involved in a match labled DANGEROUS GAMES may or not be harmful to your physically. Atken: It's an elimination thingy. Dickwood: So flee like a coward the minute it gets scary? Atken: It's worked in the past. Dickwood: If by "worked" you mean relegate your standing within in company then by golly you're right! Atken: By golly? Dickwood: It's a new thing I'm trying. Atken: Don't. Dickwood: Says the master of wit. Atken: Wit and wife fuc... Dickwood: I doubt HIGHLY that this recording would be able to air if you finished that though. Atken: Alas, we must leave it like every successful idea you've ever had Dirkypoo, incomplete. Dickwood: Har de har har. Wait, did you just use "alas". Atken: We're professionals again Dirk, we need to better ourselves, so I've been reading some literature... Dickwood: Litererature with "alas" in it? Atken: Forsooth, verily. Dickwood: Would you please stop that. Atken: Hey I'm just tossing them out there, buggered if I know what they mean. Dickwood: You remain an educated man. Atken: Hot and educated, a double threat. Dickwood: If only you were talented, you'd be a triple threat. Atken: Who the heck wants to be TALENTED in this industry? OH LOOK AT ME I CAN EXECUTE A SUPLEX WITHOUT HOSPITALIZING MY OPPONENT TRALALALALALA. I CAN DELIVER A GERMAN SUPLEX WITHOUT TRIPPING OVER MYSELF AIN'T I FANCY. Where the hell is that going to get you? Dickwood: The main event? Atken: WRONG. The key audience don't care about perfectly executed maneuvers, they care about catchphrases they can yell. Dickwood: And what target audience would this be? Atken: Ten year olds suffering from ADD who go poopy during technical bouts? Dickwood: And what of your catchphrase that will be sweeping the nation? Atken: I'M BETTER THAN YOU, CUNTY. And with that the recording was cut.
