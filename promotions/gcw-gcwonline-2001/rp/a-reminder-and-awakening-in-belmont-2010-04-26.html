A reminder and awakening in Belmont!!

26 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/26/10 The darkness overwhelms you, as suddenly, lightning flashes, shining upon a darkly-painted room. There is no furniture and no window shades, but simply a dark room. A thunderstorm rages outside. Lightning continuously flashes, illuminating the room occasionally.A voice can be heard whispering from the dark. A large shadow looms forward, sitting on the floor, legs crossed. In the light, you see Shaman's face. His eyes seem to be off in a distant place. He begins to speak, displaying no emotion. "There was a time that I'd happily sit in a place like this, and search to find myself once again. I had lost who I was within the last two years." Shaman's eyes remain focused forward. "The weak and cowardice that was Bryan Mayhem bothered me. He was a brother, in the truest form, and thus, I became content." His eyes remain focused forward, still devoid of emotion. "I actually made a bond of comfort with a man I should have turned on the day he abandoned me twice." The lightning flashes, his facial features darker than usual. "Bryan, since you addressed me directly, I wish to reveal several things about myself to you." Shaman begins to twitch struggling to keep his calm and emotionless state. As the storm rages outside, Shaman's eyes seem to reveal a black coldness never before seen during his recent past. "You wish to see the beast, have me reveal things to you?" Letting out a deep breath he seethes "Fine, This wish will be granted." Shaman leans his head to the side attempting to bring himself into a relaxed state. Shaman changes postures as he stands up, he turns and looks at nothing. "Are you listening yet, child?" "You were the weak one, Mayhem. You ran away when you were defeated by Lia Ambrosi, the woman you had defeated to take away the championship. Then, when you came back, you and I battled each other. We were the Tag Team World champions. And then, surprise. We lost it and you ran away again." Shaman breaths out a breath staring at his hands as the rage builds up within himself. Outside the sky shines like a carpet bombing from World War II. "The federation I came from, Mayhem, was nowhere near as third-rate as you wished it would be. You would not have survived there as you have in GCW. Oh, I have ended many careers over the time I have been around. Much more than you profess to know." Shaman cracks his knuckles, as he reaches down and bring up an unlit candle. Using his fingernail, he sparks the match, bringing the flame to the candle. He lights the candle. "Maybe you don't know what you thought you knew about me, Mayhem. You only know what I wanted you to know." A smile, as he snuffs the match with his fingertips. "Boy." His arm extending before him, he positions the candle under his arm. Using this to bring his rage under control, he refuses to flinch from the heat. "Why do you feel that you are the only monster to walk the realm, Bryan?" "I'll tell you what Bryan. Maybe I just simply show you." Shaman simply drops the candle. He smiles sadistically, as he turns and walks to the window, reaching forward and grabbing a bottle. Opening the bottle, he pours its contents all around the room. "Ahhh. Forty-year old scotch matured." The smile takes on a life of its own. "Makes a fine excellerant." Shaman grins, as he turns and stalks out of the house and into the raging storm. He raises his hands and sneers up towards the sky. "This is my world." He looks around, an intense look of rage burning within his eyes. "I live in this fucking chaos. I thrive within it!" Closing his eyes, he simply allows the water to fall upon his face. Slowly his gaze lowers, as he peers at the people running towards the blazing house. The rain picks up in intensity. "Your suffering...Maybe I bestow it upon others. Show them what it feels like to abandon hope." Shaman waves his hand towards the burning building. The women and children cry and scream, seeming to take pleasure in their misfortune. "I leave you with one passing thought, Bryan." Shaman smiles as he stands in the streets of Belmont, England. "There is a new beast in Belmont, Bryan. And he is dealing it. In spades!" Turning around, he begins to walk down the street. Shaman refuses to look back. "I am sure they are going to blame you, Bryan. You have been the monster for years. Guess what though?" Shaman reaches up and rubs his chin, as if he had forgotten something. "I don't really care. I am showing you..." Shaman pauses, as he stops rubbing his chin. "...You?" He lowers his hand. "Are no longer relevant in the grand scheme of things." "Ah, yes. Rampage. It's not too far away, is it?" Touching his upper lip, Shaman taps it for a moment. He then turns and walks. "I can no longer be bothered with how you think you are dangerous. More important than destiny. You are merely a footnote in history, Bryan. You're a brother. A friend, perhaps. But you...are simply...in my way." "No longer will I be held back by the limitations of facing the same people. If I am to be noticed, I need to step above and beyond you." As Shaman tilts upwards at the sky, the storm continues to rage. Fire trucks go screaming by as he hears the harping sirens, a comfort to his new path he had chosen. Stopping he turns, His gaze raises as he notices a pub. He smiles and walks inside walking to the bar as he raises his hand. "Hey pub master or whatever you call yourselves here, want to give me a Scottish beer and a shot of Tequila." The barkeep walks over to him and leans on the bar. His eyes measure Shaman as he wipes a glass within his hand. "We don't serve yanks here, especially Scot swill drinkers." Tapping his fingers upon the bar Shaman looks at the barkeep and looks around. "Nice place here, would be even nicer if I didn't have to make a mess of it." The barkeep steps back recognizing Shaman. The barkeep quickly gets the pint and hands it to Shaman. He quickly turns and grabbing a bottle of Jose Cuervo and simply hands it to him as well. "Drink all you want sir." Turning Shaman looks at the patrons in the pub. His eyes narrow as he sits at an empty table. He listens to the people whisper around him. He drinks some of the pint as a woman nearby speaks. "That is the O'Neil boys wrestling partner it is!" Turning his head he looks at the woman who cowers behind her husband. He drinks a bit of the pint, he then clears his throat. "For your attention please. I am not Bryan O'Neil's partner any longer. I am much, much worse." In a rough angered voice. he turns his head back down to his pint and drinks a bit more of it. Standing up, he drinks the rest of the pint, including the swill at the bottom. "Barkeep here is some money and I am taking the bottle of tequila with me." Slamming several Euros down on the table. He heads back out into the storm raging over Belmont. "Why is it that folks in GCW won't really take me serious?" Wiping back his rain soaked hair, he observes the town around him, obviously not liking what he is seeing. Walking again he heads north through town. "You got all those people forgetting what I am capable of and what I can do. Maybe its more about what will be done." Shaking his head he notices a taxi through the corner of his eye. Raising fingers to his lips he whistles. The taxi pulls up next to him as he opens the door and gets in. "Take me to the airport. I am done with this soot covered filth town!" The man nods as he drives. The driver continues to look in the mirror back at Shaman. "Sir you know who used to live in this town?" Raising his eyes unimpressed he speaks. "Yes Bryan O'Neil and Jason O'Neil, The O'Neil brother big wrestlers. Bryan is known as the monster of Belmont. Should I elaborate more?" Staring angrily, he gazes, daring the driver to say another word. The driver quickly puts his attention back on the road. Taking a finger , he traces the fog building on the window. "GCW Rampage, a place where champions can be made." Pondering for a moment he whispers. "People can be broken there as well." The Taxi stops and Shaman steps out looking at the train station. He turns and looks back at the driver. "What is this I said airport!" The driver waves him off as he speaks. "Roads closed due to the rain. Only way out of town is the train. G'day sir." The driver quickly takes the money, leaving Shaman growing with anger. Turning he walks into the train station. Stopping as he enter shaking off the rain, he begins looks around for a ticket booth. Spotting the ticket booth, he makes his way over to it and looks outside seeing the train pulling in. "I'd like a ticket to the airport." The guy waves him off. Shaman looks on a bit pissed off now. "Look I want the fuck out of the piss hole got it!" The guy turns and looks at Shaman and smirks. "Piss off yank! I am on break." Looking around Shaman spots a manager. He walks over calmly still angered. "Hey man can I get a ticket onto the train over there? Your man behind the desk is claiming he is on break." The manager nods and walks over to the desk. Looking like he is saying something angrily. The guy behind the counter gives Shaman a dirty look and waves for Shaman to come over. Walking he observes the guy typing something up on the computer and then stamps something. As he approaches, the ticket guy holds out a ticket. "Here you are sir that will be 26 Euros." Paying the man he turns walking away. He growls a bit as he looks at the ticket and then walks back to the counter. "Hey this says it was only 11 Euros what is the deal here." The teller simply ignores Shaman. Looking around he notices a newspaper box and walks over to it. He reaches down and lifts it, slightly grinning. Hoisting the newspaper box he runs and charges at the ticket booth, tossing the newspaper box. The box flies through the side of the booth as he yells. "Did I get your fucking attention now jack ass?" The Teller stands up in the ticket booth looking at Shaman. "You are as twisted as the O'Neil family across town." Looking at the teller he speaks in a calm manner. "Good my point was made." Turning he walks to the train. Stepping aboard he walks down the corridor, entering one of the cars, sitting at a seat near the window. He reaches forward and lifts a magazine. On the cover it shows GCW. He nods a bit and opens the magazine as he looks over a few articles. He focuses on the breakdown of Rampage and then remembers the Battle Royal of sorts. "Now is the time to let the beast out. Allow the buried anger and rage to take control. Remind them that there are others to be worried about." Grinning he turns the page and smiles as he looks at the contestants. "Hope you are all ready." Looking at the list he grins sadistically. "When you dance with the devil, you better be prepared to pay the piper!" Looking at the picture, someone walks up beside him as he turns and speaks. "What the hell do you want?" The old man holds out a shaking hand. "Ticket please!" Handing the old man the ticket, he watches the man punch a whole or two into it. he lays his head back, seeking a little bit of calm, he seeks to reign in the rage and anger. "I cannot wait to get back home and prove a point before Rampage." He laughs a bit and closes his eyes listening to the train begin to move down the track. Lifting the tequila bottle he had been carrying in his pocket, he begins to take a few long drinks off it. "I am surprised i got to carry this in my pocket without breaking it." The train rolls down the track as click clack sounds are heard.
