The Bitch is Back

24 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/24-2005 Dr. Trevors stood at the bottom of her bedside going on about her orders upon release as Eva just kind of nodded pretending to listen. The truth was the only thing that hurt now was her pride. That was a dangerous thing to injure with the likes of this fierce woman.At the appearance of the dark haired beauty in her room behind the doctor who had just told her she was being released Eva's brows rose in interest. The woman entered like she belonged here. It wasn’t a typical hospital visit to say the very least, Mist allowing a moment or two for the words to sink in as the doctor entered with a smile toying about the corner of her lips. “By the way, I’m not here by some odd coincidence. I was sent to get you. You’ll be staying in the guest room of my penthouse apartment for the next few days so that?” A laugh was inserted her at the irony of it all. “you stay well outside of the reach of the press.” It was ironic, poetic justice at its best and Mist did this like she did her job with proficiency. She stepped aside as the doc turned making his exit in order to finish up Eva's release paperwork. “Just let me know when you’re ready to go kiddo, and incase you need to make sure I’m on the up and up, give Mr. White a call.” Mist had been waiting for the opportunity to speak with the GCW diva, and had wasted no time in letting her know exactly where she stood on anything. A set of keys jingled in the hand of the reporter, that knowing gleam shimmering within her eyes. Point, set, match. Eva didn’t know it yet, but this hard hitting reporter had already changed her life and now it was a matter of time. With a demure grace Eva settled the book she was reading into her lap and stared at the woman with a strain of distrust as she spoke. Mr. White? Well at least she had the name right there. Closing the book with a bit of grandeur Eva set it on the table beside her and leveled a gaze on the woman as she spoke. ' Considering Jordan's penchant for not informing us of things I have no doubt you are on the up and up. What exactly is your job outline Miss...?' Mist did not get the chance to answer immediately as a nurse came in with video tape in hand. Eva's eyes lit up with anticipation. ' About time! Put it in the VCR and hit play if you would.' It was this last week's World Wide she had been waiting for. There had been a hell of a time getting her hands on it. Apparently something had been going on she was not privy too yet. Something that was likely to set her temper off in full blown inferno. Glancing at Mist she sighed and gestured to the chair next to the bed.' Might as well sit down. Discharge takes a while.' There wasn’t anything more annoying than things falling behind schedule, unless of course it was fawning over a black cased tape of a wrestling match as if it were solid gold. Ah well, it wasn’t that it bothered her so much as had become a bit of a concern what with the hoard of reporters swarming about in the corridors on a hunt. A bag was deposited in front of Eva with a set of plains clothes. “You’re going to need to put these on, and?” the voice tapered off as the sentence was drawn out and that tape plucked out of the VCR and placed beneath her arm. “this can wait til we get back to my place. This place is swimming in press and if you want to know my angle on it, I’m your saving grace sweetheart. My gig is not just to promote you but to make sure that each situation that arises is dealt with in such a way as to bring no legal ramifications or.. scandals to the eyes of viewers.” There was clothing inside the bag, clothing that was modest and quite ordinary compared to what the starlet usually wore, but that was part of the plan. Mist didn’t think that Eva really needed to see what was on that tape quite yet anyhow, the results of the match already having been known to her. It wasn’t going to be a pleasant thing to say the very least, and the very last thing they needed was to endure the swarm of reporters in the midst of a bad situation. Eva was likely to blow her top, but that wasn’t what was shown to her in the glossed over smile of the new Public relations guru. “This isn’t negotiable, do you have any idea how many reporters are on the floor below us” Any at all? It’s a ten minute drive to my place from here?” Stephens had basically called Eva out and made a vain attempt at bullying Caldera into a fight. No, this wasn’t going to set well at all. And Mist knew it. The look in the diva's eyes upon that tape being removed was enough to make most people shrink back in fear, but not Mist. She had been in far worse situations than dealing with a moody spoiled starlet with a chip on her shoulder. The two stared at each other silently dueling in order to see who would win this little war. Eva snatched up the bag and began pulling out the offered clothing scowling at the cotton ensemble and raising her icy eyes to look at Mist with a look that spoke volumes on what she was thinking. The woman had to be daft if she thought Eva was going to wear this crap. Shoving the bag aside Eva crossed her arms and pinned Mist with a mutinous glare. ' Put the tape in and hit play.......now.' ' Eva, now is not the-' ' I said NOW. Unless of course you are up to losing your job after only just getting it?' Eva's lips twitched as she fought the smirk threatening to rise. Mist stiffened slightly only to relax and sigh as she turned to push the tape back into the machine. ' Have it your way kiddo. Excuse me while I go and try to ward the press off...' She paused at the door and looked back at the golden haired diva. ' Oh and thanks for making my job easier. I appreciate it.' Eva rolled her eyes as the pr girl waltzed out of her room to do as she was hired to do. Settling back on her pillows she crossed her legs at the ankles and picked up the remote. Pressing play she settled in to watch the show. Upon hearing about the tag match her interest was peeked. Hauling up that remote she fast forwarded through much of the show only to have it stall on the view of Stephens. Here it began to play once more and the scene that followed soon had the diva seething with rage. She hit pause on the close up of Stephen's face and simply stared at him. If looks could kill the man would have been six feet under. Her gaze only tore away from the view at the sound of Mist reentering. The woman's eyes glanced up at the frozen shot of Stephens and merely shook her head. ' I told you it could wait.' ' You knew?' ' Of course I knew. It is my business to know.' ' We aren't going to your penthouse.' Mist perked a brow and planted her hands on her hips watching as Eva snagged up that bag and yanked out the clothing offered. Quickly she stripped and pulled on the white cotton button down and loose fitting jeans. ' And just where are we going then Ms. VanLorne?' Eva stopped and looked up Mist with a dangerous gleam in her eyes. ' We are going to Oakland.' Mist scowled. Somehow she knew this was going to happen. She could read the woman like an open book. Sighing she reached into her purse and withdrew two plane tickets. ' Well I suppose it is a good thing I got these then hmm?' Eva's head snapped up eyes gluing to the tickets. Slowly she grinned at Mist. The woman had surprised her, pleasantly so. Perhaps she would turn out useful after all. Reaching out to grab her own personals she shouldered the bag and looked back to the screen again. There was a dead calm about the woman now as she looked over Stephen's face. ' They never learn..' ' Who?' ' The men of wrestling. ' Before Mist could react, Eva was gripping a vase and hurling it at the television. The glass shattered glass as the TV sparked and fizzled raining fire down to the floor. Mist ducked back covering her face only to turn and looked at Eva through the sudden outpouring of water from the ceiling. Eva looked back at her with a calm expression. ' Let's not be late. I've an appointment to keep.' Mist said nothing merely turning to walk with Eva down the hospital halls. Stephens had called down the thunder and it was time to reap what he sewed. Oakland..look out, the Bitch is back.
