Date: 04-Nov-2006 ~The Four Winds Academy~ ~Outside of Dallas Texas~ Dani had had enough of this day. First she couldn't sleep with everything sitting on her mind and now she came out to the Order's training facility, only because it was near, and she was a graduate of the Academy up in Chicago and she could use it. She'd lost her first ever world title shot and it was time to get her confidence up before going to Madrid to face this wannabe goth chick. But as soon as she got here, things had fallen apart. Who was this Semyaza or Samyaza? It really didn't matter how he said it, his accent was thick. He'd told her how long he'd been trapped in the stone that had been dug up and moved from A canyon in what is modern day Israel or Jordan, and then brought here and carved into the statue that would make the FCC flip out if it was shown on TV. A male angle copulating with a human female while their clothing was in disarray. But it wasn't the statue's subject that had her going. It was the equation of 2 plus 2. She was supposedly the staple of some prophecy from the Angels who had come over to the new world after the fallen angels had fled the middle east and Europe after the flood and other things that happened. Many things, including such as the Crusades and that were caused by the intervention by the creatures that some called the Wakan Takan, Angels, Sky People or whatever. When it came down to it, these servants of the god or gods were agents who did their bidding on earth. The first fall, led by the Seraph Sammael had been about pride. It's detailed in the old and new testaments with it's flair, but also detailed by many other religious texts as the big revolts. There are many things in all of these writings that point to them being true. That one supposedly took one third of the angels from the heavens with the Poison Angel. The second fall was two hundred angels that now were known to many occult and apochraphilic texts as the Grigori, or Watchers. They had their connection to heaven severed so they were forced to watch the skies in the hopes that they would one day be allowed to return. As far as Dani knew, only one of them had been allowed to return. There was a fallen angel, from the first fall, named Astaroth. He was the brother to Sammael, a seraph who had been in charge of the secrets of Sorcery and other magical secrets of heaven. When he'd been defeated by the most holy four archangels in Europe, he had fled to a part of the world that the god that had made him had no influence in, the Americas. A few of the ones that lost their grace in the second fall came to the America's too. But some of them weren't evil and filled with sin as some of the others. One of them, a Virtue who was in charge of mapping the movements of the stars in heaven had actually fallen in love with a woman, a Sioux woman who had been from the time when the tribes were combined into one nation. She had wed him and produced him a son. But there was evil in the area. This woman had married Kokabel, whom her people had named Morning Star. But there was his enemy, Evening Star to them. The Evening Star was Astaroth. But that's only where the legend started. For Dani's line comes from Kokabel's marriage to the Sioux woman. That allowed a woman to be born on the earth who was also an angel by blood. A genetic throwback of seven generations who shows the traits of her grandfather from that generation, Morning Star. But there was another angel. One that has only been called by names such as 'The Destroyer' and such. A single angel , the one who had carried out the punishments of Sodom and Gomorrah himself, who fell on his own doing. The reasoning given for this was because he'd been told he couldn't go after Astaroth and the evil his kind were doing in the place where their god had no influence in. But he'd gone and done it anyway. He failed, because he worked alone and had two sides after him. Heaven's own forces killed him and left his bones in a New Mexican desert. But the blow he'd delivered had allowed the only other force of good that had come from that faction to deliver what was originally thought to be a killing blow to Astaroth? a young virtue named Kokabel. And at that point, the gates of heaven had opened for the 'Star of God' again. But now prophecies, recipes for either success or disaster were running their course. And Dani was an instrument. But was she a pawn, a knight, or even a rook or bishop in this game that other powers played? An unmarked and unclaimed Angel born of the earth. Equally inclined to sin or be saintly. She knew that she was to one day be the vessel for a disembodied Angel, this destroyer as he'd been called, to once again have a physical form to use. But she couldn't find who this angel was who drew her to him. This angel that needed a host. She had known of only one like that, Suriel, who resided in the body of the man she knew as Ian Childress, the operations head of the Order of the Hammer, an immortal of his own right and thus, she knew how long Suriel would be with him. This disembodied angel had been found by her, well his skeleton, and she had destroyed all of it by the bones she wore in a native choker around her neck. But no one could say who he was. He'd not come to her at least once in a dream. But now she had found another one. The one that had led her grandfather astray and had caused him to loose his Heavenly Grace so many millenia ago. We cannot tell when morning comes Is there a choice to live another day? It’s hard to find a new direction in your fragile life The precious time of your existence is now to come Don’t throw your life away by cheating time Sugared placebos only fool your mind **BAM** The huge double doors that led to the entrance of what was called the Cathedral building at the academy slammed open. If you didn't know better, and most there didn't, they would think that they'd seen blue arcs of lightening cascade from the doors to the stone walls when they hit. Assuredly the iron on stone had caused sparks, but not that many? Right? 'Where is the Keeper of Secrets? Where is Ian Childress, I would have an audience with him! And I don't mean tomorrow.' The sun was just coming up, but things were already moving here. This was a training camp that the world did not know fully about. They knew of the other four schools being places where fighters learned to wrestle, use martial arts, or whatever caught their fancy. The best who graduated from those individual schools, and showed promise for the Order itself were allowed to come here. Here is where they started to learn the secrets of what the Order was all about, and how it handled itself around the world, in it's shadows and under the belly of the beast. Dani wasn't really a part of the Order, but her husband who trailed behind her trying to get her to stop making a scene carried the rank of Corporal in their field operations divisions. His brother was a master sergeant and the rest of the KnightMare Brigade were mostly officers. This building wasn't where the recruits were out of though. This was the operations center. This was all administrative, and it was setup to look like a religious order on the outside. Monks and that who worked with the trainees over in the Barracks. Dani wasn't paying attention to the great hall here though, as she stepped in over the mosaic of tiles that showed various things of biblical and other mythological legends on the ground. She didn't pay attention to the carved marble columns that showed reliefs of various events in history for the Order. She wanted one thing and one thing only? answers. 'Where is Colonel Childress?' She was getting perturbed as she watched underlings scamper away from her. She had not gone into full manifestation as she knew, so she didn't have a twenty foot wingspan going right now. But she wasn't hiding the fact that she was pissed off. She stopped and pointed at one of the man. He looked about to have a heart attack when she did. 'Your leader, Mr. Childress, where is he?' The man gulped down the spit in his mouth in nervousness and then answered. 'The Arboretum. There is something going on in there today that starts or started at first sun light.' She stared the man down. 'Where is that?' He gulped again. 'The mansion house behind the school. The third building. It's his and the Lady's private residence.' She nodded only an acknowledgement to him. 'If you keep walking that way, you can go out the back door to the cathedral, the pathway is laid out to go right to there. You can't miss it.' She nodded again and then motioned for him to get out of her way, and he did so without haste. Her stopping to speak to the man had allowed Josh to catch up with her. 'Dearest?' She turned her head slightly, but didn't veer from her path that had now entered a hallway towards the service rooms, and apparently the rear doors. 'What?' His answer came as a surprise. 'Turn off the fireworks.' She stopped and turned to him fully. 'What?' He pointed to a mirror that adorned this hallway's wall. She looked and saw exactly what he was speaking about. The reason why the people had been getting out of her way. Her long hair, easily going down to past the middle of her back, looked like it was a Tesla Coil at points. Blue electrical arcs were cascading down from her head to the ends of the hairs at different intervals and sizes. She'd lost more control then she actually understood in her outrage. She tried to loosen it up a bit inside her. But she didn't know if she could. She turned and kept walking towards the doors that would lead to the next building and hopefully the Keeper would not try to keep things from her this time. What is the space, the type of dance? So will I fall and not get up? I take it all in stride ~Fifteen Minutes Later~ The walk has been brisk, but the distance was not one that you would expect. In the center of the three buildings was a large courtyard area that had different plants, fountains and other things that were more aesthetic design elements then anything. They were used to keep the appearance of this being for some religious order. But she quickly approached the large manor house that was the house and home for G. Ian Childress and his wife, Fey. However, there was going to be a problem. She could see it already. Her outburst in the cathedral had set things off, silent alarms and warnings were coming down. She could see the people who weren't students, wearing their black skin tight uniforms of the hunters and other groups as they walked outside. The one in the lead was a surprise to her. She knew that Blaise and Ukyo had left early yesterday, but did not expect them to be here. Blaise was not to be found, but the unmistakable lavender hair of Ukyo Kushinaga could be seen as she stepped out of the main doors. She was wearing a dress uniform though, not like the others. Her lieutenant rank insignia on a high collar that wrapped around her throat with the flaming unicorn patch of the KnightMare Brigade adorning the right shoulder. Being in a dress uniform though meant that she was unarmed, as if she really would need them with her being a paranormal. 'Dani, we have something going on inside. This will have to wait.' She was stern with her words, but trying to be friendly too. However, that's not how Dani came off. 'I don't care about ceremonies or pomp and circumstance. I want to speak to the man who knows things that I'm not being told. I'm tired of being some subject for studying. I'm a person, and I'm more then that. I want to know some answers, and I want to know if some of the answers I was just given are true?' Ukyo's face went long. 'What happened?' 'Go ask the fucking statue.' Ukyo at first looked confused. But it went away quickly. She'd heard rumors of some form of intelligence being housed there. There were people who said that the statue talked to and taunted Ian Childress all the time. Since he'd accepted the spirit of Suriel into his body four years ago, he'd been able to speak with the 'Betrayer of God' quite a bit. Then again, there are some that said that Childress and his guest also tortured the being in it, to get answers from him when they needed them. 'I see. I'll tell you what, this is important for both Fey and Blaise. I'll escort you two in. But you have to wait till their done, alright?' Dani just wanted to get it done and over with. But she knew that if she tried getting past Ukyo, she'd be forced to take actions. And that is not something she wanted now. Ukyo was a trained hunter and guardian of the Order. She was also the bearer of paranormal abilities that Dani could not stand up against in her untrained and constantly evolving state. 'Very well.' Ukyo nodded to Josh and had him take a point. He did so out of his own training. Josh looked at Ukyo hoping he'd not be in trouble for Dani doing this. His look was almost a pleading one, trying to show Ukyo that he'd tried to stop her. Dani followed the six foot tall Japanese woman who liked to consider herself an Otaku 'nerd' and had her hair dyed to match some of the anime characters. She was almost an Almasy dream, but she was engaged to be married to Blaise McClancy in the near future. They entered the doors to the private residence and then headed towards the back of the first great room. There they exited the house major and entered into what was a large private garden with various aspects of plants that looked like they shouldn't be actually growing in the desert of Texas. Dani stopped and double took for a moment as something caught her eye and then disappeared. She stared off to one of the corners and saw nothing, but still she stared. Ukyo noticed her doing so. 'Yeah, he's cute but don't stare too much, Josh might get jealous.' Dani looked at her as Ukyo tossed a smile her way with her perfect white teeth. 'What?' Ukyo nodded in the direction Dani had been staring in. She looked again and then caught it. It moved slightly. A shape, humanoid but looking almost like a ghost there. 'They're in each corner. Not what I'd want to deal with. I know you can see into the veil when you need to. They're ghosted out for a reason. Mainly pointing them out so you don't pull a 'Josh' on us here. Cause this matter being taken care of is not one that I'd want messed up. The order is one thing. But the Wild Hunt of the Old Country is another.' Dani shuddered, but didn't know why. This was getting to be more then she really wanted. Ukyo nodded to the man in the corner, hidden from a normal person's view by magics beyond what most would be able to comprehend without becoming a raving lunatic. Dani took everything in as Ukyo walked her over to there Blaise, Ian, Fey and a few others were standing around a large tree? a very large tree. It looked like it was an old oak, a few feet in diameter at the trunk, and it's leaves were broad. It wasn't a US native tree. A crane chain fall setup had been brought in as they were apparently putting this old tree, transplanted from somewhere else It's then that Dani could see what was actually going on, she could almost see the connection this tree had to Fey McClancy-Childress, the Lady of the Order. The Dryad's tree had been shipped from Ireland to here.. but that was something Dani didn't even want to try to fathom why right now. She stood in silence as a few workers worked to remove the cover on the tree's cut roots. As soon as they had it off, they started to shovel dirt into place to cover them and get the tree growing in it's new home. 'Dani?' Dani heard her name, but did not recognize the voice. She looked around to find the person it had come from. 'Dani, it's me. I know we've na' spoken much.' She snapped her head in the direction of the voice. The thick Irish accent said exactly who it was already to her, but she had to look to confirm it. Fey McClancy-Childress. The one who started the KnightMare Brigade while managing her fraternal twin and two younger brothers. The woman who was a former women's wrestling champion, and one half of the SCWF Tag Team Champions with her trainer Draven Lox. The woman who had ended sixth in the last Chris Williams Memorial Cup. The woman who she looked up to in this sport. 'Dani, I know it's na somethin' tha' ye'd expect t' be asked. But since yer 'ere.' Fey walked over to her. Dani could see Fey's cousin, Blaise follow her path. 'I'm sure ye've guessed wha's goin' on 'ere. As ye know abou' Blaise, as 'is tree is planted a' 'is 'ome, me tree is now 'ere. This tree fer one o' me kind containes 'alf o' our soul and spirit tha' makes us one o' the Tautha De Dannan.' Dani couldn't be mad anymore for some reason. She still wanted to blow up, but Fey's presence changed that. 'Yes, M'lady.' She swore Fey smiled a bit as she was addressed that way by Dani. 'Well, again, since ye're 'ere. I would maybe ask ye a favor. This tree's plantin' is the start o' a few week long ceremony tha' is startin' a new Faery Kingdom that I will rule over, 'ere in the Americas. Could yet do me a favor thou, an' as yer the first of yer kind t' come see me? could I ask ye to offer it yer blessin's.' Dani was floored. She couldn't have expected this. Fey wanted her to bless the tree as an angel. A blessing that would grant it the protections that they could afford it. Others of her kind who were akin to her ways, even her grandfather Kokabel, would have to heed it, she knew that much after talks with him. 'I would be honored to.' Dani felt something well up that she didn't expect? a lone tear came from her right eye, and rolled down her cheek. As the two men worked at shoveling the dirt to hold the tree in place, she stepped forward. Unconsciously she wiped the tear with her right and then made the signs that her grandfather had shown her were sacred to their kind, it ended as it needed to, with her hand going to touch that which she was giving protections to. She touched it with the very hand, the very finger that she's wiped the tear away with. As she did that, she didn't see the almost shit eating grin on the face of the heavily pierced face of one G. Ian Childress III. They stood there, waiting for the workers to finish.. in silence after that. Josh was no where to be found, and had disappeared with Blaise and Ukyo as soon as they got free. Dani had been sent up here to wait for Ian. She knew why he was making her wait. She expected him to do this. The reason why? Cause it would take the bite out of her. Before she'd walked in and seen the woman she admired in this world, she had been ready to blast everything if she got the chance. Now he was in control, and she knew there was no way to control that. 'You calmed down yet?' She spun around and saw him enter. He was wearing jeans and a t-shirt now, not his 'royal regalia' as the leader of the Order. She hadn't expected that. 'Yes, but as you can see, I'm still here.' He nodded to her at that. 'I didn't expect you to back down. In fact I'd be disappointed if you did.' There was an uneasy silence for a few moments. They had been staying apart for some time. The spirit of the angel that resided inside him, sharing his body with him was a strong influence on her young and malleable spirit. Ian was the fist to break the silence. 'So, you spoke with the Betrayer, the criminal.' She snapped back at him. 'Yes, I spoke with the one that was punished for the crime of the Grigori.' 'The one who tried to throw all of the others under the bus and get his way back into Heaven? what did he tell you?' 'He told me that he was the destroyer angel, and that it was he that you are priming me to be the vessel for.' Ian coughed at her answer. 'Why would something as evil be what you have prepared me for?' Ian shook his head. 'No, Semyaza can not be presented with a vessel. He's bound by a curse pronounced by the Jewish God himself. The only one who can break it is him. Even I am not arrogant enough to try to break something as that, nor am I that stupid. Besides, you've seen the angel's spirit before? in your dreams.' She looked shocked. 'How did you know that?' He smiled wide. 'I know things, I am a man who's job is based around secrets Dani. Tell me, when you saw this angel's spirit, which can now come to you since you wear his bones? what type of angel was he?' She didn't quite get the question. 'What do you mean?' Ian walked over and poured himself a glass of ice water from a pitcher that had been provided. 'Look, Dani. We're on holy ground here, so we're safe. You've met Astaroth. You've met some of his agents. You killed one when you took the sword of Zadikiel from Blaise. How many wings does Astaroth have?' She finally got it. 'Six, he's a seraphim.' Ian took a sip from the glass and then offered to pour her one, which she accepted. 'So, this angel who came to you in your dreams, how many wings did he have?' 'Six? another Seraph.' He pointed to the bookshelf behind her. 'Third shelf down, Ginsberg is the author?' She looked over and then saw a very worn and used book laying there, almost on it's side compared to the others. 'Legends of the Jews, Third?' He stopped her. 'Yes. Open it to the entry on Ouza.' She arched an eyebrow at him, but did as he asked. 'Ouza, also known as Uzza, Azza or Semyaza? But did you read what type of angel he's supposed to be?' 'A Cherub. Funny, his picture doesn't look like some fat little baby with a bow.' Ian chuckled. 'Actually Cherubim, like the two that guard where ever Eden is, are giants with four wings and flaming swords. Nothing like fat little babies that are a take off of Cupid and Eros in Greek Mythology. They're guardians of physical things.' 'Four wings?' 'Yes, four. Not six. They're the choir of angels under the Seraphim. So you may consider anything that Ouza, Semyaza, would offer to be a flat out lie as he's used to doing.' Dani flipped through the book some? finding and stopping at a page. 'Ian. Mr. Childress. What am I? The angel that came to my dream said that I'm to be the vessel for this spirit of an angel that lost his body. I've seen his murder at the hands of the other archangels in Heaven at the Fallen Mesa. A place where you have shacked your own mother up in.' 'Actually I was born in that house. I just bought it and made sure Maria would never loose it once I had the reasources. But yes, my mother lives on and owns the land where we found the skeleton of that angel, and the skeleton I'll point out, as you saw before you destroyed it, had six wings also.' She had to remember back to that day a few months ago, when she had found herself teleported, by her own power, to the other place, the mirror image to this one in Spain, a place she's going to in just a few days to wrestle. However, it might be time for you to finally speak with others of your kind.' She kept flipping through the book, looking over it to see him. In the meantime, I'm going to also give you enhanced access to our network resources through your husband's credentials.' He stopped and just looked at Dani. It was his way of dismissing her. She didn't know if she was ready to go, but she got the hint. She'd find out what these changes in his stance were meaning soon enough. She closed the book and put it back onto the shelf and left the room. After the door clicked shut, Geoffrey Ian Childress III turned to a mirror and addressed it. 'She's getting closer. But I feel that they are too.' No one else heard nor saw the handsome, angelic face that stared back at him. It's gruff voice rang in Ian's head. 'She's wanting to find out now. The bastard in the statue actually might have done us some good.'
