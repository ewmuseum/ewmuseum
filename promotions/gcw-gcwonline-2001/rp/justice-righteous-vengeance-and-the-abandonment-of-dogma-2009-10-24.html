Justice: Righteous Vengeance and the Abandonment of Dogma

24 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 24, 2009 There comes a time in the life of every man when he must accept responsibility for his sins and misdeeds. Other times, a man decides that he needs to make things right, or make up for what he’s done wrong. There are some people who hold everything inside and let their past indiscretions eat at them and rip them apart until they’re nothing more than a hollow shell of their former selves. It drives some people to the point of insanity, and that’s something that not even years of therapy can fix. Then there are those who vow to use their past as a way to make the future better for themselves and others. These men follow the path of righteousness; of justice. On the other side of that proverbial coin, there are those that are in the final category- those who go so far that they overstep the boundaries of justice and travel into the territory of vengeance. They say that vengeance is a dish best served cold, but in reality, the only thing vengeance does is beget more vengeance. Some say that there’s a thin line between vengeance and justice, and it doesn’t take a whole lot to cross it. For a certain thirteen year old kid, all it took was an abusive father and his mother suffering one too many beatings from the patriarch of the family. Something snapped inside of his adolescent psyche, and he decided that the only way to end the abuse was to end the man behind it. If there’s anything that can drive a child to kill, it is someone harming his or her mother. Little did said child know that one severed brake line would cause an event that would send the majority of his teen and part of his adult years spiraling out of control. He spent five years in a mental institution because of what he did on the orders of a judge when the jury found him guilty by way of insanity. Insanity takes on a lot of different forms, the most common of which is the stark, raving lunatic. One of the less common forms of insanity is what people refer to as cold and calculating. The latter is what young AXEL Action grew into as time went on. Eventually, he found himself slowly becoming a part of a sacrilegious cult known as the Church of the Unholy. The basis behind the Church was that the members were to worship Lord Tioneb, the Unholiest of Unholies. Being blindly led like a lamb to the slaughter by his best friend, James von Doom, AXEL allowed himself to believe the false prophecies and worship a blatant blaspheme; even though his conscience told him it was the wrong thing to do. With each passing day, AXEL found himself getting sucked further and further into the Black Masses and dark arts that the Church of the Unholy practiced. As this continued, he grew to enjoy it more and more, even participating in virgin sacrifices to the fallen Demi-God, himself. His mind became ravaged by hatred and his heart began to grow cold. He started to despise humanity for everything people had done to him. Soon enough, worshipping Tioneb became all that mattered to him and the only thing he wanted to do was create chaos. Eventually, AXEL began to see von Doom and the Church of the Unholy for what they truly were. He began to see them for the blasphemous curs they really were, and he soon decided he wanted nothing else to do with them. After a long battle with some personal demons, both proverbial and literal, AXEL managed to break the shackles that the Church had placed on him and become his own man, no longer bound by hatred. He became a just, righteous being known as the Angel of Vengeance. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Dogma (noun) - a specific tenet or doctrine authoritatively laid down, as by a church. Religion has been a major focal point of confusion for years. Religious zealots push it as the greatest thing to ever happen to mankind and claim that if you accept their god, whichever god it happens to be, that you’ll be granted eternal life after death and a place in Heaven. On the other hand, those same religions which claim to have tolerant, loving gods, threaten an eternity in Hell if one doesn’t worship said deities. If these higher beings are so loving, why would they condemn their creations to torment? Why would they give us free will to do as we wish, only to threaten us with punishment when we exercise our free will? These are the same gods that supposedly order their followers to go to war in their name. War was waged for nearly two hundred years against the Muslim people in Jerusalem in the name of the Christian God. This war was known as The Crusade, and it lasted from 1095 to 1291. The original intent of the war was for the Christians to regain control of the Holy Land from the Muslims, but eventually, the true intentions were shown in the form of an attempted genocide. The Crusades continued on through the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, with the Latin Christians of Europe, mostly the Franks of France and the Holy Roman Empire persecuting and oftentimes killing those who didn’t believe as they do. They waged campaigns against pagan Slavs, Jews, Russian and Greek Orthodox Christians, Mongols, Cathars, Hussites, Waldensians, Old Prussians and political enemies of the popes. This was all in their God’s will, or so they claimed, but it was actually in the name of claiming what they thought was theirs and trying to force their beliefs on others. This is often the agenda of organized religions. They suck people in with their false promises of life after death in a place made of gold and force their laws down the throats of those who don’t buy into their lies. Those who do believe usually end up with their minds twisted and corrupted by the words of their leaders and their Holy Book. It is nothing more than a classic case of the blind leading the blind, and in those situations, no one ever gets anywhere. John 3:16 in a nutshell, says that Jesus Christ died on the cross so that the sinners of the world could have a way into Heaven through repentance and a belief in God. If that’s so, then why is it that people who are born a certain way are considered sinners and can’t be forgiven, simply because they lead different lifestyles than “normal” people? The dogma of the church as a whole has taught for years that the only path to righteousness is that of confession, atonement and repentance, but one man in particular has found his own path to absolution. AXEL Action has found his own higher power, his own Goddess to worship. After all, justice is blind. No one is out of her reach, and eventually, EVERYONE will have to answer to her. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Justice is the concept of moral rightness based on ethics, rationality, law, natural law, fairness and equity. The symbol of justice is that of a woman equipped with three items: a sword symbolizing the court’s coercive power; a human scale weighing competing claims in each hand; and a blindfold indicating impartiality. John Rawls once claimed, “Justice is the first virtue of social institutions, as truth is of systems of thought.” Justice is the one thing in this world that discriminates against no one. As indiscriminate as she is, justice definitely isn’t flawed, and sometimes she doesn’t get carried out the way she should. Her blindness is also her downfall, and in times like those, she needs someone to be her eyes—her enforcer. She needs someone to take up her sword and bring it down in a fit of wrath upon those who dare to go against what is right, fair and just. The one who takes up her sword must be one whose soul cannot be tainted, and whose heart cannot be twisted and blackened by the stain of evil. He or she must be steadfast in their search for truth and righteousness. One man has taken it upon himself to become that enforcer, to become the eyes of justice. He’s taken up that proverbial sword, and has brandished it in her name. He has taken it upon himself to teach right and wrong to those who bring injustice to the world, even if he has to do it by force. In fact, he prefers to do it forcefully. AXEL Action has taken the path of vengeance, but his is a righteous vengeance; vengeance in the name of his chosen goddess, Lady Justice. He has chosen to give penance for his sins and misdeeds by serving her and putting down those who oppose all that is fair and just. His wrath will be swift and deadly, and all those who follow the path of evil will learn to fear him. He is the one, incorruptible entity. He is unbreakable. He is the Angel of Vengeance. All hail Lady Justice.
