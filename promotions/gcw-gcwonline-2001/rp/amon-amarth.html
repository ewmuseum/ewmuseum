Date: Decades ago It’s getting cold, amazingly cold. This time of year in Belmont, England the winds pick up. 4-50MPH gusts. Wind chill will be in the teens, maybe poking into the low twenties. Belmont use to be beautiful in the summer, and despite the frigid winter temps people use to love to come here. That’s until the monsters moved in generations ago. That ominous house, no longer standing, their own personal cemetery. There use to be a rich and gorgeous apple orchad, but the devil’s destroyed that. Death, no apples. Rotted tree bark, no apple juice. Fallen trees, no apple pies. Everything about this place went to hell once those freaks moved in. Even as a child he remembers hearing all this. He was in school for a brief period of time, but constantly beating up the other students because they called his father Satan grew old and he was home schooled. His younger brother, so meek and fragile (now one of the most legendary names in the business) was also teased. All those popular kids, all those European Football players. Fucking bullshit royalty. Fuck them. He’s lived here his whole life up until a few months ago, but they always called him the Boogey-man. “Make sure you say your prayers or the boogey-man will get you. Make sure you jump into your bed, because the boogey-man will grab your feet and pull you under. And you’ll end up living the rest of your life in that House of Torture on Belmont Hill. You don’t want that do you?” He looks up the hill to where that house once stood. Nothing left, no rubble, not a single thing. After it collapsed the city council voted to have the rest of it destroyed, leaving the Boogey-man homeless. He can still see it’s silhouette, almost as if it’s still there, perhaps hoping in the back of his mind that it’s still there, that this is all some nightmare and tomorrow he wakes up in the house prepared to take on Lestatt Knight. That was eons ago, the house is gone. Sure the dead apple orchard is still there, as well as the family plots, but everything else is desolate. He looks away, almost in despair, his whole life went to rubble that one summer evening. He now focuses his attention to his own reflection. Ahh, yes the boogey-man. He leans over the rotted railing and stares into the rushing waters of Gagne River, half mile south of where he was born and raised. This river added to the evil of Belmont. Not only did the Lamb of Satan live just one half mile north, but no matter what time of day, week, and year, this river was a constant rush of freezing, numbing cold water. “Stay off Drowner’s Bridge, it’s not strong enough to hold your weight, you don’t want to crash in and drown do you” You know once you call into the water you are gone, goodbye, kaput.” Mayhem stands on the bridge, just like he did that one fall day, years and years and years ago?.. “O’Neil get your scrawny ass back here and give me your money before I beat the hell out of you, you little shit.” Little Jason is running down the path through the apple orchard, at that time still sprucing apples. He always ran down this path on his way home, sampling the apples before he got home and received a beating from his old man. Dominic Winslett, class bully, decided that Jason was staring at his girlfriend too much and decided to give Jason a beating. Jason, only 13 at the time, was a meek 5’3? 100lbs dripping wet. He wore thick glasses, and was constantly pale. Plus he lived in that house with them sick freaks. His father was the devil and his mother was the town whore. Jason was losing ground. The pain in his abdomen and legs began to creep onto him. His heart was pumping a million beats a minute. He can see Drowner’s Bridge and his home. He’s almost there, keep fighting. His legs must have decided the race was over because he collapsed, scraped his knee and elbow. Dominic stopped, and kicked him square in the stomach. The impact was so hard Jason began to vomit. He was coughing a lung out, he couldn’t catch his breathe. “Don’t you ever run from me again you piece of shit. When I say stop you fucking listen. Now you are going to pay, and pay big time..” Dominic grabs Jason by his hair, Jason can feel the rip and burn in his scalp. He drags Jason towards Drowner’s Bridge. Jason begins to kick and wail. He’s screaming at the top of his lungs. With every scream Dominic yanks more. They get to Drowner’s Bridge and Dominic pulls Jason to his feet, grabs his collar and leans him over the railing. Jason’s tears are coming down like a tsunami. “The more you kick and scream the more painful this will be, so shut the fuck up.” That’s when Dominic see’s him from the corner of his eye. Approaching slowly is Jason’s older brother Bryan. He lets go of Jason and walks towards Bryan, growing balls in the process. “I’ve always wanted to have a piece of you big boy.” Dominic lands a punch, Bryan stares at Dominic and smiles. Bryan grabs him by the back of his head and lands a violent punch to the face. Dominic’s blood sprays all over Bryan. Bryan drags the unconscious Dominic and picks him up. He edges towards the railing when Dominic comes to. Bryan holds him upside down the rushing current feet below. Dominic begins to scream. He literally urinates in his pants. His face is dripping blood. His nose shattered, cheekbones crushed. This 18 year old has years of work ahead of him. “My god, I’m sorry I’ll never touch him again just let me go.” Bryan looks over at Jason. Jason’s wide eyed, not sure what to expect. He simply shrugs at Bryan. Bryan looks down into the bruised eyes of Dominic, spits on him and says, “You’re right.” With that he drops Dominic Winslett into the violent current below. Never to be heard from again. Dominic’s body was found 2 years later washed ashore on a beach 60 miles north of Belmont. He was to decomposed to determine the cause of death. No one ever knew what happened to Dominic, cept for Bryan and Jason. You may remember hearing this story on the news. Rich high school jock vanished, no word ever heard from him or any eyewitnesses. Bryan looks back into that water. Something catches his eyes. Something stuck on a rock, something flapping in the current. He looks up the stream and notices a face? A human face is stuck on the rock. He begins to climb down the embankment for a closer look. He’s scaled these cliffs a million times. He knows ever foot hold, every jagged edge. When he reaches bottom, He reaches out and grabs the face. It’s Dominic’s! “Welcome to Oblivion Mayhem!” Bryan drops the face and snarls?.the time has come?”
