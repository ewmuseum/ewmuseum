A moral lesson?

26 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/26/09 It was just two weeks ago that I had been wandering aimlessly around the graveyard, in shock over Bagwell’s “death”. On one hand, I hate the fucking bastard for putting us through that shit. But on the other, his prank – if you can call it that – may have been the best thing that has every happened to me. My Uncle Erick found me and took me to his home in Ohio. Here I’ve had the opportunity to do nothing but train all day every day. For awhile it was great, as Ohio lacks all the distractions that New York City provides. All I want to think about was my match with Zacharia Taylor, and that’s all I had the opportunity to think about in the isolated area. But yesterday, when I chose to take a day off and allow my body to recuperate I realized something. For a guy who was born and raised in New York City…Ohio is fucking boring. Ohio has trees, and lakes, and a great deal of other nature that I never saw in the city, nor have any desire to see. But despite the unpleasant amount of animal life surrounding me…I feel my old vigor returning. The color has come back to my cheeks and the dark circles around my eyes disappeared. Most importantly, I no longer feel ready to climb into bed and hibernate by five pm. So that is why I am sitting in my uncle’s living room, contently watching reality television, while trying to recover from the latest torture my uncle has devised. That’s something I forgot to mention; my uncle has taken it upon himself to oversee the physical conditioning portion of my training regime. It does seem like he’s improving my overall fitness but I’ll never admit it; his workouts will always be a step away from hell as far as I’m concerned. I hear my uncle walking down the stairs but I pay him no mind. We’ve established an unspoken agreement when it comes to the living arrangements. I don’t pester him about where he goes or what he does, and he gives me the same courtesy. But today he throws a set of clothes right on top of me. Those clothes consist of a suit and tie; basically scream that “boring event! Avoid at all costs!” “Dude,” I say to him with a hint of irritation, “I’m watching Survivor.” He snorts, and I answer with a grin of my own. We’ve discovered that we share a deep disdain for those types of shows…so of course we can’t help but watch them. “It’s a rerun,” he returns after a quick glance at the screen, “besides this is more important. We’ve been invited to a dinner with the Sosa family.” “Why would we be invited,” I ask him as I sit up, “when I don’t even know them?” He gives me a nervous grin and just gestures for me to put my suit on. Instead I cross my arms and glare at him, refusing to play along until I am given an answer. We stare at each other, engaging in a test of will, before he finally admits defeat. “Alright, alright, they invited me and I asked if you could come along,” he concedes without a hint of shame. “Thing is…I plan on sleeping with the guy’s wife during that dinner thing, and I need someone to keep him distracted.” I blink at him, half expecting to be told that it’s all a big joke. As seconds pass, I realize with a sinking feeling that he is actually serious. I shrug, and promptly push the clothes off the couch. “No way,” I answer him as I fall back onto the couch, “I’ve had enough melodramatic drama in New York, and I don’t need more of it just so you can get someone to touch your dick.” “Well,” he says with an unpleasant grin, “I guess you don’t have to. But when I can’t get laid I have this tendency to start charging an exuberant amount of rent that you can’t afford.” I glare at him…then with a curse grab the suit. The last thing I need is to have to look for another place to stay on the eve of Rampage. Hours later I am sitting in the dining room of a lavish home while my uncle and his friend Lorenzo talk to each other. So far it has been a remarkably dull evening, and I am starting to wonder why my uncle actually wanted me to attend this dinner. There are a number of clues that tell me he has no attention of sleeping with Lorenzo’s wife, Lillian. The first indication is that Lillian is…well…let’s just say she lacks the physical characteristics my uncle usual goes for. When I heard her name I thought she would look somewhat like the stereotypical hot Latina. Instead she’s the very definition of plain. She has a nearly flat chest, and simply lacks the allure that attracts men to beautiful women. Moreover, she’s very meek which I know is not the type of personality my uncle goes for. “Lillian,” Lorenzo calls out with a drunken slur, “get off your ass and get us some more wine!” Lorenzo has been verbally abusing his wife all night. My uncle doesn’t seem to care, or even notice for that matter. I, on the other hand, feel distinctly uncomfortable but I know better than to get involved. It’s not like the man is physically abusing his wife, and anyway she’s a grown woman. If she doesn’t want to get yelled at, she should just go ahead and leave the guy. The bottom line is that none of this is my problem. Lillian gets up and heads toward the kitchen. Lorenzo and Erick continue to talk about various inane topics, apparently they’ve moved onto baseball. I just sit there, half-asleep and hoping that the painfully boring night will end before too long. I still can’t figure out why Erick brought me to this place, since it’s obvious that his original reason was a pile of shit. Eh, maybe he just felt like torturing me for his own sick amusement. Lillian returns with a bottle of red wine in her hands. That’s strange…her hands are shaking so badly I am almost certain that she is going to drop the bottle. Her husband doesn’t seem to notice, being too engrossed in the amazing discussion of some trade or something like that. The shaking in her hands has become ridiculous. I hadn’t planned on saying anything, but I am afraid she’s about to pass out. “Are you alright Lillian?” I ask as gently as I can. She gives a little cry and trips face first onto the floor. Goddamnit, I didn’t think I would surprise her that badly! I can see from the pool of wine coming out from underneath her that the bottle has broken, and - oh fuck there’s blood mixed in with that wine. “You stupid BITCH!” I jump at the noise and turn to see Lorenzo stomping towards me. I brace myself, certain that he’s about to hit me for startling his wife into tripping. I decide that I’ll let one punch through, just to help me get rid of these queasy feeling of guilt that has worked its way into my stomach. “YOU RUINED MY CARPET” Lorenzo roars and proceeds to drive a foot into Lillian’s ribs. Every instinct in my being is yelling at me to attack Lorenzo. In my definition of the world, men are not allowed to hit their defenseless wives. But I know better than to get involved. THUD! I doubt Bagwell would jump in. THUD! It’s not like Erick is in a hurry to get out of his chair. CRACK! Ok, he just broke her ribs. Fuck it – Lorenzo is a little guy anyway, how dangerous could he be? So before he can draw his foot back again, I slam his head into the wall with the palm of my hand. He falls to the floor completely unconscious. “Call the hospital,” I spit out through gritted teeth as I gently turn Lillian over. I breathe a sigh of relief as I see that she miraculously got away with only minor cuts as a result of the bottle. She is obviously in agony over the beating that her husband has given her, but there is nothing life threatening about it. “I’ll take care of her,” my uncle has moved right behind me, “go home.” I whirl and glare at him. The bastard had just stood there as Lillian was being beaten by her husband and now he wants me to just go home? A treacherous voice inside my head reminds me that I hadn’t instantly stopped the abuse either, but I forcefully silence it. Still, the knowledge that I allowed things to go this far makes it too difficult to confront my uncle right now – I should just let him take care of the mess. He returns home two hours after I do. For awhile neither of us say anything, we just sit there in silence. I want to tell him that he is a fucking bastard, but how can I do that without looking like the world’s biggest hypocrite? “I’m proud of you,” he says suddenly, “though I admit I was about to jump in a second before you did. But you made the right decision in the end.” “I am so glad to have your approval,” I answer sarcastically. “Now maybe you can tell me why the fuck you were friends with that piece of shit in the first place. And the real reason you wanted me to go to his house.” “I’m not friends with him,” Erick answers calmly. “It’s the area’s worst kept secret that the man beat his wife. But I figured that accepting his invitation was a good way to prove my point to you.” I look at him sharply. “Your point?” “You’re not the soulless bastard that you think you are. You don’t like to see defenseless people get hurt, you don’t like bullies, and attacking Jake Reed is doing more damage to you than it is to him!” My first slams down on the table besides me. It hurts like a bitch, but I am too busy being pissed off to care. ”Would you stop with your stupid moral lessons? Jake Reed asked for everything I did to him, and if he wants to face me at Rampage then I’ll bury him there!” Erick sighs as if the weight of the fucking world is on his shoulders. “Well for a guy that took out his mentor because he wanted to get focused you’re really letting yourself get distracted. Or did you forget that soon you have to face the guy that’s kicked your ass time and time again?” Furious I stand up and begin stalking up the stairs. ”I don’t care who the fuck I face! I’ll beat Taylor, Reed, and anyone else that stands in my fucking way!” I stop suddenly as a thought occurs to me. “And if you were as big a saint as you make out yourself out to be,” I snarl at him, “you would have called the cops on that Lorenzo guy a long time ago!” “That wouldn’t have done much good,” my uncle remarks though I notice a faint blush has stained his cheeks. “Lorenzo is a personal friend of the police chief and the mayor, they would have covered it all up for him. By the way, he’s probably going to use them to make life miserable for you if you stay here. Luckily, if he has you arrested the beating he put on his wife will come out and he doesn’t want that to happen.” I stare at him slack-jawed as I realize that once again I’ve managed to get myself embroiled in problems that I had no business sticking my nose into. “Son of a bitch!” I groan, then head to my room. But I honestly don’t know if I am going to go pack or just hide on the covers and hibernate for a few years. Both seem like good ideas right now.
