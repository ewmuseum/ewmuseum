Things Fall Apart

7 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/7/2011 January 7th, 2011 Key Biscayne, Florida. Clouds dot the lower portions of the sky, just barely covering the horizon and creates a sieve that makes it look rich violet and red. A feint breeze keeps the air from being still and stagnant, the smallest reprieve from the blanket of south Florida humidity. White plastic lounge chairs and blue and white umbrellas dot the sand, mostly vacant save a group of three chairs in a row. Lying on the third chair is a mammoth of a man with a deep brown tan, spiked fauxhawk hairstyle, and this giant is wearing a paisley patterned speedo. He is Mack Brody. However, he is wearing headphones so we won’t be hearing much from him. In the middle chair is Frank Silver. He has a towel draped around his neck and a small, disheveled man spraying him with a water bottle attached to a motorized fan. Every once in a while Frank swats at the assistant for getting water droplets on his silver reflective aviators. Frank’s body is muscular, but more ‘normal’ in comparison to Mack. He is talking up a storm on his phone, gesturing wildly with the free hand in between hitting his assistant. Walking towards the group is a man almost as small as Frank’s assistant. He is rather thin with straw brown messy hair tussled on his head. His white jeans are rolled past his ankles and he walks barefoot, carrying his blue slip-ons so no sand gets caught in his shoes. His pale blue oxford shirt was carefully semi-tucked into the jeans, making sure that one flap falls across his thigh. Frank spots him, ends his call, and shouts at the shorter man. “Ryan about time we got to see you! We’ve been in Miami since before New Years,” Frank says, “I thought you knew we were in town. Did you not get Tim’s call? I knew I should have fired him.” Frank backhands his assistant. Tim slinks away, twitching and muttering how ‘I swore I called him.’ “Nevermind that, take a seat bro. Relax. I’ve got the finest of glacier water or whatever that idiot told me he got,” Frank continues. William Ryan O’Galloway got the call just fine. Will paced his apartment and did not sleep for several days. You see, Will, or Ryan as he’s known to the wrestling community, was in the midst of a full nervous breakdown. Put simply, Will has issues discerning his life as ‘Intergalactic Space Cowboy’ Ryan Gallway and his real life outside of the wrestling business. The more entrenched he has gotten in the industry, the more Will needed to find escape. At age 19, he had the guise of backers and sponsors in Japan that would take him out to dinner, get him drunk and allow him to play on their dollar. It only got worse. Moving back to the States and in a more permissive country, Will easily escaped into the Ryan personality with other diversions. However, with Will’s entire adult life being a wrestler, the division grows more faint. Instead of a thick painted line, it is a smeared chalk scrape across the concrete. His reticence towards seeing Frank was clear as he trembled sitting down at the end of the lounge chair. Tim hands him a bottle of water and he quickly cracks it open and swallows a third of it. Frank arches his eyebrow. “The crazy life of Miami is gettin’ to you Ryno. I never thought that all the partying would be a bad thing, but you might want to give it a rest. It’s the new year, and a new year for the Heirs to reaffirm our superiority over creation,” says Frank. For the last three years, Ryan Gallway and Frank Silver formed the Heirs of Wrestling, both of them coming from superior wrestling trainers. To say they were dominant would be a gross understatement. The Heirs joined four promotions and captured each of their respective tag team titles. In the now defunct Sin City Championship Wrestling, they were the last holders of the Strength in Numbers Championship and were undefeated as a collective in their stay. The Heirs were glued to the hip. Sharing rooms on the road; nights at the bar and club enjoying the spoils. The constant presence of Frank prevented Ryan from retreating into Will. So he medicated. Ryan would stop into the bathroom to take a dropper of acid, smoking DMT instead of getting the car in a parking lot. Frank and Mack Brody treated it as ‘Ryan being wacky or living the gimmick.’ The ‘Intergalactic Space Cowboy’ was intergalactic for a reason, right? After SCCW closed, Will retreated into his Miami apartment and went through blotter sheets at an enormous pace. He tacked his blind shut and tried to fall back into himself. Now he finds himself shriveled up like a salted snail at the feet of his best friend in the industry. The words slowly escape Will’s lips, “It’s not that Frank.” Frank chuckles thinking that his partner was being sheepish. “It’s not that man? I’ve seen what you do in the clubs. We didn’t see you at all when we were in Japan last time! Or when you accidentally dosed us in Hawaii? Those were the best times, but you’re a freak man.” “I know,” Will burps out after he drinks the rest of the bottle too fast. Sweat makes his hair look matted, deceiving the fact that he has taken five showers in the last day. He thought that he kept on stepping in anthills when they were really piles of socks. “I can’t do it anymore.” Frank sits up and swings his legs over the chair. The Heirs sit side-by-side watching the barren beach. Frank puts his hand on Will’s shoulder. “I know you can’t man,” Frank says, “We should get some training time, find a new place, dominate like always, and it’ll be good. Just refocus and destroy. You know we got this.” Will shrugs off Frank’s hand. Frank is startled and almost offended by his partner’s physical rebuke. Will says “No. I can’t do this anymore. Us. Heirs. I can’t do it. I need a break.” He gets up and starts away. Frank grows furious, jumps up and grabs Will’s much smaller arm and tries to pull him back. “What do you fucking mean you can’t do us anymore! It’s you and me! It has always been this way Gallway! Do you remember the promise we made in that diner almost 3 years ago? Do you? Let me remind you, incase you are fucked up out of your gourd right now. We are the only people worth a damn in this sport, and you’re nothing with out me. We need this. Do you think that we could have done half of what we’ve done without each other? Fuck no. You are just being a fucking burnout and don’t know what you are talking about,” Frank yells, trying to berate his former tag team partner into acceptance. Will takes a step back and looks Frank dead in the eye. Even with the near ten-inch height gap, he can’t shrivel up and fall away. His face grew flush and the shirt now stuck to him from sweat. Part Magic City humidity, part frustration. “No I’m not Frank,” Will starts, “I don’t know who I am anymore. I don’t like being a prick. I don’t like flying between two or three promotions and spending the odd day by myself. I cannot exist this way anymore. Frank, I need to find this out myself. I need to be able to not take LSD or not wake up hung over as shit. I need this.” “What about me Ryan? Did you ever think about what would happen to me or Mack when widdle Wyan Gallway decided to take his ball and go home? Do you know the repercussions of this? Getting booked by myself—“ “That’s what it’s been Frank. Always about you. You came to me looking for a tag team because you were tired of being booked low on the card. Frank Pierce wanted to shake up the cards. You take your godfather’s last name cause you know it will get you more money than your own name. Listen. I don’t want to completely end the Heirs. I just want a sabbatical. I want to be able to rest, stop being fucked up all the time, and not end up like Brian.” Brian Spaes, Will’s trainer. Only 34, Spaes has several arrests on his record due to DUI and drug possession. Will’s relationship with his trainer dissolved after the final arrest that lead to Spaes closing his wrestling school. Brian was last seen in Nova Scotia, several years ago, stabbing someone in the ring with a Bowie Knife. He is textbook case of ‘the road owning you.’ Frank’s trainer however, was his godfather and ‘uncle’ Sonny Silver, PRIME Universal Champion and general Hall of Famer. Frank cut Will off and stepped to him, nearly face to face, “Brian Spaes. You don’t want to end up like Spaes? Look at yourself. You are already the 22-year-old burn out version of him. I fully expect to get a call from a bondsman needing to bail your ass out. That’s okay. Go into your gutter and shoot up whatever the fuck you use. I’ll see you soon enough; crawling back to me when you actually want to do something in this business. Say hi to Brian in the crack house for me.” “Get my shit Tim, I’m out of this shit hole. It’s hotter and sweater than my gooch, fuck this,” Silver gestures back to Tim, whom scrambles and grabs his belongings. Tears develop in the sides of Will’s eyes as Frank turns his back to his former partner and walks away. After a minute, Will O’Galloway starts back to the parking lot. By this time, the sun has dripped below the horizon. The stars aren’t out yet but the crescent of a first quarter moon provides faint light to his car. Will reaches into his pocket and clicks his unlock button on the remote and the interior of his Civic illuminates the lot. He opens the door and gets inside. Pulling the keys out of the pocket he also takes out a business card and looks at it. Global Championship Wrestling. When SCCW closed Nigel Kensington handed him this card and told him that ‘this company could use your efforts, when you are ready, of course.’
