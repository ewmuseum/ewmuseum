The SoCal Kid (Bryan Mayhem)

27 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/27/10 Sally ford makes her way through dimly lit hallway to a locker-room door. She tries to bring herself to knock but hesitates. It was the locker-room of Bradley Cruz the self proclaimed "SoCal Prophet" and last time it was a less then pleasant experience. She tries to knock again but her fortitude once again fails her, she reconsiders even going through with this latest assignment, but just as she was about to turn around and leave, the door suddenly opens as Crystal steps out into hallway. She sees Sally and greets her with a smile. Crystal: "Why hello Miss Ford, it’s good to see you again. How have you been?" *Sally is a bit shook up from her sudden appearance, but quickly shakes it off and returns the pleasantry.* Sally: "Oh, quite well thank you, and you?" Crystal replies with a warm smile. Crystal: "Well enough, thank you, Bradley once again is anxious to meet with you. He felt your last interaction went really well." Sally: "…but last time he insulted me and the company I work for." Crystal nods in agreement. Crystal: "Yeah he’ll do that…" Sally: "Then I said this company didn’t need him, called him elitist, and delusional." Crystal shrugs. Crystal: "Brad considers himself rather divisive, so he probably would have been more insulted had you not said something like that, but anyways good luck." Crystal is about to open the door to let Sally in, but as she is about to Sally interrupts Crystal with a question. Sally: "If you don’t mind me asking, what exactly is your relationship with Bradley? Are you his manager, assistant, or his girlfriend?" Crystal smiles as she replies. Crystal: "Manager and fiancee." Sally: "Oh, brave girl." Crystal: "Seriously! Well, Miss Ford... feel free to let yourself in, I have some business to take care of, excuse me." Crystal gives a polite nod as she walks down the hall. Sally reaches for the door’s handle and forces herself to open the door. What she sees is a large Romanian woman pawing and molding a SoCal prophet who lays face down on a masseuse’s table. Brad: "Oh Stephania, your hands are amazing! I take back all the things I ever said about Romania… which is surprisingly a lot… which is odd because it fits in ‘the barely a country’ category with Canada and The Netherlands." Sally: "Mr. Cruz…" Bradley pops his head up seeing Sally standing there rather befuddled. He shoos Stephania away as he quickly snaps to a seated position and just as easily to his feet. As a precaution Sally looks away. Brad: "Thank you Stephania that will be all." Stephania quickly gathers her things folding up her table and walks out the door in a matter of seconds. Brad: "Thank you for coming once again Miss Ford for my first interview as an OFFICAL member of the GCW." Sally: "Sure, it’s my job." Brad: "Oh really, I thought your job was to editorialize and attempt to completely demoralize and undercut any new talent." Sally: "You made some very bold statements insulting me and the company I’m proud to work for." Brad chuckles. Brad: "Company your proud of? If you only could see what I see." Brad walks behind a dressing partition and begins to change into some proper clothes. Sally: "Can we just get this over with instead of dragging it out like the last time." Brad: "Sure, but first things first." Bradley can be heard dialing a phone followed by the muffled sound of a phone ringing on the other end. It rings a few times before someone answers. Brad: "Sly, bring him in." A few moments pass when suddenly the door opens and enters Eko and Slider dragging in a young man who’s scared out of his mind. They force him to stand holding him in place. Bradley comes from behind the partition wearing some normal clothing holding a piece of paper in his hand. Bradley quickly sizes the kid up and gives seemingly sincere smile. Sally just stands there with confused and worries look on her face. Brad: "What’s your name kid?" Kid: "K-Keith… Keith Arbuckle. What’s this ab-" Bradley holds his index finger to his own lips letting Keith know that he wants him quiet. Brad: "What is it you do here, Keith?" Keith: "I work in the P.R. department in GCW." Brad: "Wow, the P.R. department! I’m not going to lie to you Keith I was once thinking of going into P.R. myself back in college. I have a real knack for talking things up, but I quickly realized my actual talent was talking myself up… and here we are." Bradley chuckles. Brad: "In fact Keith I’ve become quite familiar with your work as of late, which is the whole reason I have you here." Bradley holds up a piece of paper printed with text. Cruz’s face smiles. Brad: "Why don’t I read some of your latest handy work." Bradley clears his throat as he prepares to read aloud. Brad: "‘WorldWide 142 Dec 29 2010’… someplace worse than hell blah blah people I don’t care about blah blah… ah here we are and I quote: ‘Hardcore Rules Match, Hardcore Championship, Brad Cruz vs. Bryan Mayhem’ with a ‘C’ thingy indicating he’s champ. ‘Brad Cruz might not have made the wisest of decisions, calling out GCW's champions by name.’ ‘In fact, rumor has it that Bryan Mayhem personally requested this match to put the SOCAL KID in his place. … and then this tripe goes on. You know I’ve been insulted many times in my life. I mean people have been known to say some really insulting things to me, but you sir take the cake. Keith: "I don’t understand. I was just being impartial, it goes on to say that Bryan is tough, but he could very well underestimate you. I don’t see what the problem is." Brad looks annoyed. Brad: "You don’t know?" Keith: "No…" Brad: "…you don’t?" Keith: "No!" Brad: "SOCAL KID!? WHO THE HELL IS THE SOCAL KID!? Do I wear a 10 gallon hat? NO! Do I have spurs? NO, I AM A PROPHET NOT SOME SNOT NOSED KID! …and I refuse to be dubbed that way." Keith: "You mean because I botched your nickname you had your thugs snatch from the bathroom." Sally looks to Eko. Sally: "You guys caught him on a bathroom break?" Eko: "Mid shake." Slider elbows Eko. Slider: "Dude…" Bradley crumbles the paper into a wad and grabs Keith up by his collar. He pulls Keith closer to his face. Brad: "You’re on notice. Don’t let it happen again!" Bradley places the wad of paper in Keith’s mouth. Brad: "Get him out of my sight!" Eko grabs Keith and leads him out the door. Slider sits himself on the sofa. Brad: "… and make sure he holds that paper in his mouth for at least an hour. It’s for his own good." Eko and Keith exit the locker room closing the door behind them. Bradley lets out a sigh. Brad: "It’s not easy fixing this company." Sally: "You're not fixing anything. You’re just bulling people… you even tried to bully our champions and that’s why Bryan Mayhem is going to have you for dinner." Brad: "Bryan Mayhem sounds like a generic 80’s action flick. If his name were any more cookie cutter it would be ‘Oatmeal Snickerdoodle’. Seriously, have you seen this guy? He looks like a love letter to the NuMetal movement of the early 2000s. Slider speaks up. Slider: "I don’t know, Cuz. I saw that guy the other day coming out of his locker room… that dude looked like that Romulan ship from the beginning of that Star Trek movie… like a massive bouquet of blades, metal, and chaos! It was pretty wild." Sally: "He is a tough S.O.B." Brad: "Oh please, you people are so easily impressed. First off his makeup makes him look like a roadie for Kiss… in fact the only thing impressive about the makeup is the amount he wears, but then again that’s still only around 10% of what Cher uses, there’s his hair which I didn’t know Sideshow Bob was such a big fan of Korn, and then there’s his tattoos… I’m sure he can sit us down and regale us with story after story of where and when he got each one, and the deeper meanings they hold. …but at the end of the day for all the time and money he put into his personal "Flesh Louvre" he just ended up looking like Dave Navarro from the neck down. I mean I could have just as easily gotten myself all tatted up, but like respectable adult I have enough will power to pass over the ‘rub on tattoo’ vending machines as I exit the supermarket keeping my fifty cents. In fact Bryan Mayhem is the personification of GCW’s biggest problem." Sally: "Which is?" Brad: "GCW’s lack of variety." Sally: "We have a incredibly diverse roster." Brad: "I think you mean a different thing when YOU say it, then when I say it. When I think of variety I think of ‘31 flavors’ where there’s something for everyone. Whether you’re looking for a playful Strawberry, a sexy chocolate, a cool mint & chip, a fruity Sherbet, and maybe you’re a glorified Cathy comic and aiming for some random ice cream cramped together with random baked goods like cookie dough or cheesecake, the POINT is whatever you want they got it." Slider: "Amen, Cuz!" Brad: "Now if GCW ran an ice cream shop it would have 31 variations of vanilla. ‘What would you like?’ vanilla! ‘What’ll it be?’ vanilla! ‘Anything else?’ vanilla, vanilla, vanilla!" Sally: "Stop saying Vanilla!" Brad: "Your right by now Alexander Redding’s ears might not just be burning… they might be bursting into flames!" Sally: "What is your problem. I just can’t wrap my mind around you. You come out and just rail against people who barely know you’re name. People you have never had any contact or passed interactions with like Bryan Mayhem, and you just dump on them trying to make a name for yourself. What kind of person are you? Why would you demean a man who has spent his career making an impact here in GCW? Can’t you just respect what he’s done here?" Brad: "No, because I see what you and the rest of this company can’t. When you people look at Bryan Mayhem you see his size, his war paint, his hair, and his tattoos and you think ‘WOW, this dude looks tough!’ … all I see is polish… on a turd. There’s a saying ‘If you polish a turd you don’t get a diamond’… which is true, but apparently in GCW it gets you two reigns as World Champion and a stint as a Hardcore Champ." Slider chuckles a Brad continues. Brad: "Sally, you say I came here to GCW and disrespected Mayhem, but he knows what he is. For God’s sakes, he defiled his own body with the tattoo of the word ‘Oblivion’ which means to be unknown or forgotten and that’s exactly what he is, that’s exactly where he should be, and that’s where he’ll stay when I’m the new GCW Hardcore Champion, because I’m O.C. Original Cruz. We’re done here…" Sally just shakes her head and as she leaves, the scene fades.
