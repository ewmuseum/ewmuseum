Going Old School With A Cameraman And Every Word In The Title Capitalized Even When It Is Unneccesary And A Lot More

5 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/11 "The Atmospheric Phenomena" Aaron Fujita Current Fed: GCW Current Record: 5 wins and 3 losses Finisher: Quasi-Geostrophic Theory Next Match: Dynasty and Vivica J. Valentine vs. Aaron Fujita and K-Wolf Next Show: WorldWide 145 (The scene opens to a crowded place in the back of a building somewhere. Aaron Fujita stands in front of a black curtain with the GCW logo all over it. The cameraman moves closer to him and sees that there are two hot women with really big boobs standing next to him and chatting. He laughs and then reaches to the floor where a big bag of money sits. There must be hundreds, no thousands, no hundreds of thousands of dollars in this bag. He pulls out two wads of hundred dollar bills and hands them to each of the hot chicks.) Aaron Fujita Now get out of here. I’ve got some trash talking to do. (The women scatter away from Aaron, leaving him standing alone in front of the curtain.) Cameraman You ready? (Aaron nods.) Cameraman Action! (Aaron gets a serious look on his face.) Aaron Fujita What’s up everybody?! It’s everyone’s favorite weatherman, Aaron Fujita. Now I’ve been having problems with a few guys lately and I have something to say to each one of them. First off El Moreno. What the fuck kind of name is that anyway? I don’t speak Spanish but I know that stands for I’m gay. You come here into GCW and think you can just walk over everyone? Your little winning streak is bullshit. You haven’t really faced me yet. I know what you’re saying. We wrestled on WorldWide a few weeks ago. The thing was I let you win. That’s right. It’s all part of my master plan. I will be the one that ends your streak whether you like it or not. I may not hate Mexicans, but I sure as hell hate you. Cameraman How about The Magnificent Messiah? (Aaron nods.) Aaron Fujita What about him? He’s a freak. He is the reason I’m not GCW Heavyweight Champion right now. Cameraman You’ve never had a shot at that title! Aaron Fujita That doesn’t matter. Everyone knows that Dan Black was going to take us to the top of GCW and Messiah fucked it all up. Now he’s jealous that I’m in the spotlight and he’s not. It’s not my fault that I’m smart enough to put a few words on a t-shirt and sell it for twenty bucks a pop. Even though I do have an IQ of 120, no 130, no 160. That guy wouldn’t know a wristlock from a wrist watch. Maybe we should step into the ring some time and I can break him in half with my uber spectacular piledriver from hell of DOOM~! Fuck him. Cameraman Can we get your thoughts on Dynasty? (Aaron Fujita lifts his hand and flips off the camera.) Aaron Fujita That guy can suck dick. All he does is start shit. He’s so much of a punk and pussy and doesn’t want to face me in a match that he’s trying to get El Moreno, aka I’m gay, on me. I mean how much of a yellow bellied wimp do you have to be to try and get two guys to fight each other. Like I said to Moreno, I don’t hate Mexicans but I fucking hate you! You see what he wears to the ring? This yellow and black singlet thing that just yells that he’s a queer. That guy can die. Cameraman Speaking of Dynasty and facing you, you guys are in a mixed tag team match on WorldWide. What are your thoughts on that? Aaron Fujita Why in the hell do I have to wrestle a woman with a woman as my partner? Wrestling is no place for women. They belong in the kitchen or the laundry room. I’m enough of a man that those two bitches can just leave the ring and let me and Dynasty handle everything man to man. Cameraman You know one of these women is the current US Champ and the other used to hold the belt. Aaron Fujita Shut the fuck up. Who are you to say stuff like that? Shut your mouth and let me do the talking. (The camera moves up and down in a nodding motion.) Aaron Fujita I don’t care if they have both held a belt or not. They suck. Plain and simple. They haven’t faced me so they haven’t proven anything of themselves. We’ll see who the real man is come WorldWide. And the real man is going to be me. And that’s the bottom line because Aaron Fujita asked if you smelllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllllaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa what the non-Mexican is BAKING?! Cameraman Isn’t it supposed to be cooking? (Aaron gets angry. Really really really angry. He reaches forward and punches the camera as if it were the cameraman itself. The camera falls to the ground and begins shaking as Aaron kicks the cameraman repeatedly. Of course the cameraman over sells the whole thing.) Aaron Fujita That’s what you get punk. Now where are my hot bitches with huge tits?! (On the still rolling camera the women’s legs are seen walking up to Aaron.) Aaron Fujita Now get my bag of money and let’s go have sex! (FADE TO BLACK)
