Bar Talk

18 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 18, 2009 July 18th, 2009 Markus' phone rings. Glancing at it, he quickly decides not to answer it. Instead he turns back to his drink, raising the glass to his lips and swallowing a mouthful of whiskey. He's been here for a few hours now, continually drinking at the same pace, only opening his mouth to get the barkeep's attention for a refill. Single purposed in his drinking, he has sat oblivious of everything around him. A few in the bar recognize him, but the negative vibe radiating from him has been enough to keep them away. He failed to notice that his presence has slowly driven every person away from the bar, choosing instead to sit in a booth or at a table than risk his dreary atmosphere spreading to them. It does not succeed, and somehow Markus' presence alone has brought a heavy cloud into the establishment. 1 Missed Call Chris Markus turns his phone off, takes another drink. Barely 24 hours prior, he drove a wedge between him and his partner, his best friend. Banned & Exiled were no longer a cohesive unit. The pain of the decision gnaws at Markus' stomach. He hopes the alcohol can eventually start to numb it. It hasn't worked yet. A friendship thrown away, and this would only be the first step. Next week Markus has plans to bring a crippled acquaintance to WorldWide, and use him to further turn the audience against Chris Storm. An in ring accident that many have not been made aware of to be used as ammunition. Knowing Chris as well as Markus does, he knows that Chris will react out of anger. Chris is going to help Markus bring him down. All the time the two spent together, the incident in WIW was only brought up once. Markus has only seen Chris cry once. He decided never to bring the subject up again. "This seat taken?" a voice purrs from beside him. Markus lifts his head, slowly turning it while debating whether or not to give this person a hint of courtesy. "Obviously not, you really think you wanna change that?" The brunette smiles, taking his comment in stride as she sits. She's dressed well, that much Markus notices, but the details evade his vision as he turns to take another mouthful of whiskey. Knocking the glass on the counter to get the barkeep's attention, Markus holds his empty glass up between two fingers. "I'll take one of those, too" the brunette says with a smile. "Put it on my tab," Markus adds as the whiskey fills his glass. "So what's your story?" she asks, casually spinning her glass on the table. Markus smiles to himself. "Long and arduous." She lifts the glass and takes a sip, "Sound interesting. You're gonna be one of those hard ones, aren't you?" Markus cocks an eyebrow. "You know the type, I'm gonna have to pull teeth to get a good conversation out of you, aren't I?" she asks, smile still painting her face. Markus takes a drink, "Everybody else already figured that one out, too." "Well, that's the great thing about bar buds. You and I can sit here all night long and talk, and when the night ends we'll never see each other again. No need to worry about opinions or lasting impressions. It's actually quite liberating once you get yourself in the mood for it. Lucky you, I've got nowhere to go anytime soon, so I'll just sit and wait." Her logic is impressive to Markus. The two continue to make idle conversation, as she slowly eases Markus out of his shell. He learns that her name is Riley, and she takes the following joke at her expense with a soft laugh. The bar seems to come to life, slowly reviving itself from the catatonic state brought about by Markus. Eventually, and a sizable amount of whiskey later, she bring the conversation back to the start, "So what is it?" "Hrm?" Markus asks while raising his glass. "Your story? Nobody comes to a bar, and exudes so much discontent to ruin the mood of everybody around, without a story," she smiles. Somehow, Markus notices, she has never stopped smiling. How can she have so much joy in her life? "Like I said, it's long." "And I've still got all night," she leans closer to hear over the rising noise. Markus wonders how to start, surprised that he's even entertaining the notion of talking to this woman. "I had to make a choice. Probably the hardest one I've ever made. You ever run into an opportunity, the kind that comes along only once, and you realize that it'll give you everything you've ever wanted in life? Your childhood dreams come true?" She ponders this for a moment, "Can't say that I have. Sounds like you should be grateful?" Markus chuckles, "Yeah, except the only way you can take that opportunity is to sacrifice everything you have. Everything that formed you into the person you are. Every friend you have. Every remarkable thing about you. Piss it all away, and then maybe, just maybe, you'll fill that void in your life that's been gaping at you for so long." "Which is worse?" she asks him. "The void you have, or the void you're creating?" "The million dollar question," Markus raises his glass to her. "So instead of dealing with it, you come to a bar and try to drink your problems away? Doesn't sound like it's going to help any." "Not one bit," Markus agrees. "Well then, you should probably do something about that, right?" she asks him rhetorically as she stands. "As for me, I've got a flight to catch." "Thought you had all night?" he asks her, not believing her excuse to leave. "Well if I told you I had to leave soon, you'd have just waited for me to leave. Lying got you to start talking like a normal human being. Take care of yourself, and thanks for the drinks," she puts some money on the counter to split the tab with him. Leaning over, she gives him a light, friendly kiss on the cheek. "Bye." Markus watches her leave in silence. Turning around, he begins the process of settling his tab as he finishes his whiskey. Markus stands, and quickly realizes just how drunk he is. Slowly he makes his way out of the bar, unsteady feet leading the way. The cool air washes over him as Markus steps through the door. He glances side to side, noting the emptiness that permeates from the city. Pulling his phone from his pocket, Markus turns it on and waits for it to load up. 1 New Voicemail Markus deletes it without listening. Turning more swiftly than he should be capable of, he begins walking with head down and hands in his jacket pockets. To think, all this history that I've made. Now we're going to just through it away. ... Yeah. How hard is this going to be to justify to ourselves? Markus nearly stops in his tracks to contemplate the thought. "Impossible," he accidentally says aloud. Yeah, and like the lady said, which is going to be worse? Greener grass and all that. Don't know what you've got 'til it's gone? Precisely. ... Markus baffles himself. Unable to truly decide which choice is the right one to make. His best friends, or his legacy? We've always wanted a legacy. Our life's only finite, after all. Sacrifice, think anybody'll understand? Like I said, I can't even justify it to myself. Can't tell myself not to go through with it, either. So we're serious about this? ... Yeah. Yeah I guess so. Better to ask forgiveness. Let's spell this out for ourselves. Let's let this sink in for a minute. Yeah, it's pretty fucked up, isn't it? We're starting to feel time catch up with us? Or are we just spoiled after being a World Champion so early in our career? Not sure. Regardless, we want to somehow recapture that glory. We want to be a World Champion again someday soon? Hopefully. And because we nearly drowned trying to chase down a nearly empty bottle of Jack, we had a vision... Is that what we're telling ourselves it is? Unless I've got a better explanation? And because of that "vision," we are going to cut all our ties away? No more Chris, no more Tessa, no more spending time with them or their kids, no more family? Alicia's gone now, too? ... So our best friends, the very people who'd picked us back up every time we fell, who made us feel like we mattered to somebody besides a wrestling fan or promoter, who saw past all our bullshit to see some type of value in us... They're worth less than a belt around our waist? ... Are we just too scared to admit how fickle we really are? Yes. And even though we know this is going to eat us up inside, we're going to do it regardless? We're going to torment ourselves over this decision, and bury the real Markus deep down inside ourselves? Yes. Though it will burn, and tear, and bleed inside. We are going to shove our hurt down as far as I can, so that hopefully when we are too old and crippled to do this anymore, we will be remembered for our accomplishments. We are going to try as hard as we can to make my delusions of grandeur a reality. No matter the price? No matter the price. Okay. ... Look on the brightside. We'll still have Jack by our side. And I'll bury myself in his arms. Markus shakes his head at himself. Nausea wells in his stomach. Markus can't tell if it's because of how much he drank, or if the thought of the person he is turning himself into sickens him.
