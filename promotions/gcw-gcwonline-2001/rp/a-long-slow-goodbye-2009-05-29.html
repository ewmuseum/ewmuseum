A Long, Slow Goodbye

29 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-29-09 Greed’s no mystery to me. It’s a dirty word to most people, or most poor people, anyway. To them it’s too hard to understand how anyone could need so much when they thirst for so very little. But like most things in life, wealth is relative, and can never be so easily defined. I’ve been hated for most of my adult life. They used to say Watch out for that Caldera, he’s ambitious, or That Caldera just can’t get enough, he’s a greedy son of a bitch. As if it were a bad thing. They hate me because they don’t understand. They don’t understand what it is to have an insatiable thirst for wealth. They don’t understand my curse. A man trapped in the desert for a thousand years may find an oasis, but no matter how long he drinks, no amount of water can slake his thirst. I’m surrounded by riches, but they only strengthen my hunger. It has always amused me when I’m greeted with contempt by the little people who cross my path. Their contempt confirms for me the very thing they’ll never know; that they simply cannot understand why greed is good, and why the very people that are vilified on the evening news are the same ones saving this democracy so that they might continue in their self-righteous ignorance. Maybe I should slow it down for you. You need me. You hate me, you hate my guts. But you need me. Maybe you hate me all the more because you know you need me. But you don’t know why. Maybe it makes you feel better about yourself to cast aspersions upon the big, bad corporate bully. At any rate, I don’t think anyone here can deny that in the sense of moral relativity, I provide an invaluable service to society. But what, then, of greed? What right do I have to continue to take when so many have nothing at all? And how on earth can I sleep at night? I’ll try to put this in terms you can understand. The great ones measure wealth differently. To me, true wealth can’t be measured in the number of zeroes in my bank account. Once you reach a certain point, it doesn’t matter how much money one has. Billionaires don’t whip out their bank statements and compare length and girth. Much like the male sex organ, it’s not the size that counts, but what one does with it. Wealth isn’t money. The only real currency in this world is power, and though power can be purchased in cash, it can only be maintained with pure fortitude of soul. And like money, once you have a little, you immediately thirst for more. I will never stop, never tire in my relentless pursuit of more. What’s this you say? I haven’t answered your question? Quite right. In short, greed is necessary. It’s necessary because all you little people need someone with that capacity for uncompromising gain. If you don’t believe me, just take a gander into the past. No society in human history has existed without an upper class. Most developing nations in modern history have seen their wealth concentrated in less than one percent of its population, those people who cheerfully horde resources while the lesser people starve. If you think that’s evil, it’s only because you can’t see the big picture. I see the big picture because I’m not burdened by more immediate troubles like putting food on the table. That’s why I don’t blame you when you call me names. How could you know any better when the furthest ahead you can see is the first of the month when rent is due? Me, I see the endgame. And in the end, when all the nasty, brutish forces of nature come to bear on you, it will be us, the wealthy, the powerful, who will be there to shelter you. You can’t deny it. You see it every week. Hundreds of people in my employ, decrying my name to the heavens, secretly whisper a quiet prayer of thanks that I should be there to protect them. Were I not stabbing you in the back to get more for myself, you would not have the little I give you. I must knock you down that I might lift you up. Some, our President foremost among them, would say I have a responsibility to those less fortunate than myself. Redistribution of wealth as they say. Yet even in the few true socialist nations we’ve seen, an elite class has emerged. There is no stopping it, it is a force of nature. It is this force that should be the focus of your anger, but I forgive you for being unable to see it. There are forces at work in this world that would destroy you. There are people hiding in the dark reaches of the world that want nothing more than to draw your blood. Nature itself must constantly seek your destruction to ensure that only the strong survive. Without your betters, anarchy would fall from the sky like so much acid rain. We crack down on corruption, bring corporate “criminals” to “justice,” and just a few years later the economy is a disaster. This isn’t a coincidence. If you eliminate the greedy, the ruthless, the cold and calculating from a society, you’re left with a nation of equality and fairness--neither of which can exist in nature. Rail against social Darwinism all you want; it is a fact of life that cannot be altered. We are the ones that keep the horrors of the world at bay. We are the ones that trade the purity of our souls for the salvation of the innocent. We are the ones that sell our mothers so that yours might hold on for a few more undignified years in some decrepit retirement home, so you can visit her twice a month. No amount of power in the world can satisfy my lust. This equips me to be the callous son of a bitch you need to face the harsh truths of this world, so that you can maintain your innocence and assure God of your holiness when you meet him. And while you’re strumming your harp in heaven, I’ll be drinking blood with the devil, the both of us satisfied in the knowledge that we bought your ticket. That is my curse, my cross to bear. I will never be satisfied. I will never get what I am looking for. I have only one desire, more, and there is always more to be gained. So I will keep on raping and pillaging so that you can maintain your humble homes. Just think about me the next time you’re struggling to pay a bill, and be thankful that your biggest problem lies on a piece of paper and not in a hail of fire from those forces always seeking your destruction. Trouble is, I’ve reached excellence here, and now, as always, I crave more. There is something larger to be gained here, some greater achievement yet undiscovered. The time is now fast approaching when I might abandon you all in search of something more. And though you may rejoice at my departure, you will mourn for knowledge that you will soon be suffering. You will find yourself weakened without me there to guide you, and you will curse me for leaving you exposed. But you can’t see the big picture. I can see it clearly. I know that sacrifices must sometimes be made. Though I may ensure your destruction by my leaving, I ensure greater wealth for myself. And the greater I become, the more vast my power grows, the wider my umbrella becomes. This is the painful choice a truly great leader makes without hesitation. Kill a hundred to save a thousand; even as I hear your vain cries for help I do it with a smile on my face. That’s not the real reason I’m writing to you. I write to assure you that, though my justification holds true, you are special. I may cheerfully sacrifice you that my influence may expand, but I remember where my power originated. I think a little perspective is a good thing So fear not. You will not be destroyed outright. You will come along with me in my quest for something greater. If the day comes when you must be traded for something more, our time together will come to an end. Until that day, you will exist as a reminder to me of where I came from, and just how great my potential is. Maybe your scorn for me will be diminished through the course of our long, slow goodbye, though I do not hold my breath. Just know that, though you may hate my greed, it is the very thing that feeds you and your family, the thing that feeds this society of ours. God bless America.
