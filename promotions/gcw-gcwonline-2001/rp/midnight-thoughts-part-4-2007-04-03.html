Midnight Thoughts: Part 4

3 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 3rd, 2007 Vegas is not the right city for an insomniac. These bright lights, the noise, and the general knowledge that something big is happening, or could happen, at any moment make it very hard to sleep. When I pack my bags and board that plane, I know I wouldn’t be getting much sleep until I board another plane and head back home. That is why, just for this night, I am tucked away comfortably on the living room couch rather than in the bed with Tessa. After last night, I think its best if cooler heads prevail and I spend some time alone. Three nights without sleep is killing me, I don’t know how much longer I can take. It’s like the time I did three lines of blow and couldn’t sleep for an entire week. Well, except I don’t have that awesome high and I feel a bit crankier. Tessa says I need to go to the doctor, says it’s not health that I can’t sleep. I don’t need a doctor though. I know what my problem is. I have too many thoughts. They keep running wild in my head when I try to sleep. When I’m awake, like now, I can keep them bottled so that they aren’t too bothersome. But it’s when I drop my barriers and try to get some sleep that they wreck havoc. That won’t happen tonight though. I will be able to get my rest, the empty pill bottles on the coffee table will make sure of that. Anything that said drowsy on the box, I took. The way I figured it, worst that can happen is I don’t get the sniffles, coughs, or a fever on top of a good night’s rest. Or I die. Either way, I get some sleep. “Mommmmaaaammma!” I wanted to kill something at that moment. My eyes, which had just closed, shot open and twisted toward the far door that led to little Chris? bedroom. Waiting a moment, I let my head hit the pillow once again and started to close my eyes for a second time now. “Mommmmaaaammma!” I wasn’t going to get any sleep. AGAIN. I knew this, I accepted this. Though that didn’t mean I had to be happy about this. Crawling my tired and weary body off the couch, I headed in the direction of the cries. Half way there I noticed the door to the bedroom creek open. The soft brunette hair of Tessa strayed out from the doorway, but as soon as she noticed me, her head ducked back into the room and the door shut. At least I could take some comfort in knowing she wasn’t asleep either. That actually sounded rather bitter. I should clarify. I’m not upset with Tessa over our fight, but I certainly can still be mad that she had caused this sleeplessness to continue on for any longer than it had to. With a shot at the tag team titles coming up, rest would definitely be a good thing. My hand pressed up against the polished wood of the door and I opened the door slowly as to merely peer in. My eye caught glimpse of Chris sitting upright in his bed. It was too dark to see exactly what was going up, by judging by the rapid lifting and dropping of his shoulders, he was undoubtedly crying. Stepping into the room now, I sat down on the corner of the bed and looked at my son, not sure what to do. The young boy went straight for my waist, locking it in a huge hug and dug his face into my chest. “Mommy! I want my mommy!” What could I do? What could I say? Sorry kid, but your mom is a dick sucking crack whore? Ok, so I didn’t know if she was a crack whore or not. But that likelihood of that being true is still rather high. I tried wrapping my arms around him, hoping that would comfort him enough for him to go back to sleep. The sooner he went back to sleep, the sooner I could try myself. But his sobs did not lessen any with my embrace. Rather he pulled his head away from me and once again asked for his mother. She was far away, however, still living it up in New York City off the child support I had to pay her. Funny enough, she got child support yet I got the child. How the hell did that work again? Gotta love America. The child was still being loud, something that was affirmed when Tessa popped her head into the room to see what the matter was. All the annoyance and anger I had surrounding her before vanished like a cheap magician’s trick. Twisting my body, I showed her little Chris bundled up against my chest crying for his mother. I hoped Tessa would know what to do to comfort the boy, being so motherly herself. But the look I got could have frozen hell, fire, and brimstone. Needless to say it sent a chill down my spine, which is an impress feat when you consider I’ve stared down Steven Caldera over contract negations without blinking an eye. Not many wrestlers in the business can claim that. But the look I got now made me want to flee in terror like a small and scared little girl, flailing my airs in the air like some kind of crazy rat-flail. “He wants his mom,” I whispered to her, hoping that she was just giving me that look because she was just as cranky as I was for having to be up another night. Tessa shrugged her shoulders, rolling her eyes, “and?” At that moment the room got so cold that penguins were bitching that global warming needed to hurry it’s ass up. I’m not fucking joking. I know it’s not her child, that this boy is mine conceived with another woman, but did she have to be so mean about it? “Well,” I asked, “can you help?” By asking directly, I hoped that she would do something to make the situation better. Even my question did little to soften her diamond-hard exterior. “Then maybe you should call Jennifer and she can come over,” came the bitter reply of Tessa Windsor. The entire situation seemed to make Tessa incredibly agitated, more than I have seen from her in recent times. I hadn’t pegged her to be the kind of person to wrongfully place her anger on an innocent child, but I certainly hoped it wasn’t focused at me. I mean it was just a mistake I made in the past, what was the big deal? I can’t change the past, or what I did back then. She didn’t have any right to be so upset by it. Besides, I was doped up on cocaine when I did it, so I can’t be held accountable. For a moment I had gotten so worked up on Tessa that I had completely forgotten about the crying boy in my arms. I wrapped my arms tighter around him, still not sure what to do. I never had to deal with problems like this before; they weren’t my field of expertise. I could suplex a person into an accordion blindfolded. I could counter a text book list of submission holds as easily as I could walk. What I can’t do is handle a crying child in the middle of the night. I can’t deal with a pissed off woman that even after this many years, I still can’t figure out. Tessa rubbed her eyes and letting out a long yawn, “I’m going back to bed, Scott”. She turned away from us, getting ready to head back to the master bedroom. I wanted to protest, ask her to help, but the moment of hesitation I had was too long. The door shut behind her and I was left alone with Chris. Tears still rolled down his cheeks, but at least he had silenced his cries. Moving away, I gave him some room to see just what the problem was. “What’s the problem?” What? I never claimed I was father of the year. I doubt I could even win a father of the week, and there is like fifty-two of those. Chris wiped his nose, trying to hold back any further tears. “I had a nightmare!” he told me in a loud cry. Tapping my chin, it came to me. He was crying for his mother because he had a nightmare. Oddly I was kind of jealous. A nightmare meant that he was asleep. I would have liked to have had a nightmare. “So what was the nightmare?” Chris looked at me, eyes wide open. “I don’t really remember.” This entire fuse, all this commotion and all this lost sleep because of a nightmare he couldn’t even remember. Is it wrong to give a young child a sleep aid? Because right now I want to drug him and myself so I can get some sleep. One would assume that since the child could no longer remember the nightmare that would then make everything alright. That person would also have never parented a six year old child. As soon as I lifted myself off the mattress to leave, the boy let out another large cry. Like a lead weight in my pocket, I dropped back onto the bed to halt any further cries. “And the problem is?” The little boy again looked up into my face, “I had a nightmare!” I had to chuckle. It wasn’t on purpose, but it still escaped my lips. It seemed to confuse the small child that I would laugh at his nightmare. But the laugh wasn’t directed at him. “What’s so funny?” “I was thinking,” I really shouldn’t tell the child this, “I had a nightmare myself recently.” The child seemed amazed, his mouth slightly gasped open. “You had a nightmare, too?” Making myself comfortable on the bed, I shot him a look that said “yeah”. Chris moved closer to me so that he could take in every word. “I’ve had a nightmare a few nights ago.” “What happened?” The boy had seemingly forgotten all about his own nightmare now and was focused on my story. “It’s one I get often, where I’m with a woman. At least I think it is a woman. Turns out its worst, far worst. That was no woman, that’s a man! But you can’t tell that’s a man. No, he’s more of a girl than even Tessa!” By the look the child was giving me, I could tell none of that made sense. I didn’t expect it to, but it was still scary to me. Reaching told him, I patted him on the head and gave his forehead a kiss. “Look, the important thing to remember is that it’s a dream and can’t hurt us” I told him as I let loose my embrace. That, thankfully, seemed to calm the child enough so that he was willing to try to sleep again. Or he was just too confused by my story and wanted me to leave. Regardless, he was going back to sleep which meant that I could go back to sleep. Thankfully he went to sleep then, as my next story about have been about battling Asian lesbians for gold straps. I didn’t think he would get that story any better. Maybe when he’s older I can tell him the story about how his dad and his dad’s best friend bravely defeated the Dyke Brigade and freed the GCW Tag Team titles and the very division itself from their dildo-loving grasp. I’m like some kind of modern superhero, only I fight more interesting opponents.
