Enter Harrington (Paul Harrington)

29 December 2002

Enter Harrington
Paul Harrington
Global Championship Wrestling Roleplays #15
Date: 12.29.02
Location: Las Vegas, Nevada

Blackness.

It is early morning. The sun is still low in the Eastern sky. We are standing outside the Las Vegas Headquarters of TAN: The American Network. The building looks to be at least fifty to sixty stories tall, and is already very busy. People enter and exit the building at an alarming rate.

Blackness.

A large set of double doors. Wooden, with large brass handles. A desk sits off to the left of them. On one of the big doors is a goldish brass plate that reads 'Paul Harrington - President'. The doors open, and a woman walks out with a handful of papers. She sits at the desk, and picks up a phone.

'Mr. Harrington just handed me the papers, sir. Yes, I will mail them ASAP. No, thank you for your business with TAN.' She says over the phone before hanging up.

She groans a bit, then opens a drawer of her desk. She pulls a large brown envelope from the drawer, and slides the documents into it. She seals the envelope and begins writing an address on it as a large clock let's out nine low bongs, indicating the time as nine o'clock.

Blackness.

Paul Harrington sits in a large chair, talking on his phone. He is sitting behind a large Oak desk, covered in file folders. Papers hang from some of them, and some are open. At the front of his desk, a small nameplate sits, completely made of gold. Behind Paul, the back wall of his office is made entirely of glass. The curtains are open, allowing the Midmorning Vegas sunlight to shine through and into the office. Suddenly, Paul's voice is heard.

'Yeah, well, I've decided it's time I head back to wrestling.' He states over the phone. 'I'm going to GCW.' Paul says, very confidently.

Paul swivels from side to side on the phone. He has a small smirk on his face as a knock comes on the door, and the woman from earlier ducks in. She pauses, as Paul turns back to the phone.

'Yeah, that's great. Just give me a call anytime you want to, I'm almost always available. But make sure you plug their event too. Okay then, I look forward to reading it. Thanks Mr. Thorson. Talk to you later.' Paul says, the smirk still on his face.

He hangs up the phone, and turns to the woman. He begins laughing, and she becomes confused.

'Why are you so happy?' She asks.

'Victoria, 'Pro Wrestling Informer', is running a cover story on me coming back to wrestling this month!' Paul screams with joy.

'Really? That's great!' Victoria says.

'I know, this means I have to get back to business. I've gotta prove that I can still take care of business.' Paul says, getting out of his chair.

'Paul, you talk like you're forty. You're only twenty-three.' Victoria states.

'I know, but I've gone through some injuries, and I haven't been wrestling for a while.' Paul explains.

'Good point. Well, good luck with that.' Victoria says.

'Thank you. So, what did you need?' Paul asks.

'Oh, Katie called. She'll meet you up here tonight at six.' Victoria states.

'Aha. Thanks Vicky.' Paul says, a smirk returning to his face.

'No problem. I'll head back to my desk now.' She says.

Her head is removed from in between the doors, and the door closes. Paul walks over to the fireplace, and looks up onto the mantle. The logos of many wrestling federations sit in frames. Paul picks up each one, the first being hand drawn, that reads 'Backyard Wrestling Alliance'. He laughs a bit, then picks up the next one.

'Southern Illinois Wrestling.' He laughs. 'Some great times. First ever Crusierweight slash World Heavyweight champion. I was great.' He sighs to himself.

Paul cringes a bit, and a chill crosses him.

'Then came the broken leg.' He remembers. 'My career turned around that day. Took a turn for the worse.' He says.

He sets the pictures down, and walks to the back wall of his office. He looks down at the street, which is packed with the morning rush hour traffic.

'Well, it's gonna take a big u-turn now. I'm heading to GCW, and showing everyone I can still get it done.' He says confidently to himself.

Suddenly his phone starts to ring, and Paul turns his head to look at his desk.

'Nobody had better take me lightly. Or else I'm gonna wear them down. No, no one will let their guard down to me, because I will light their asses on fire if they do.' He states.

Paul turns and walks to his desk. The ringing stops, and the scene fades.

Blackness.
