The Convo

3 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 3rd, 2005 The camera opens slowly, fading in from an out of focus blur to a small apartment. Its four walls barely make it large enough to be considered a room at best. Around the baseboards, small piles of plaster dust have collected amongst the dust bunnies and rat droppings of the small tenement. Slowly, the camera glides to the left giving a better view of the small area. A small thirteen inch television; the antenna wrapped in aluminum foil, passes across the screen, followed by a small cot before coming to rest on Jayce Bradley. He’s dressed rather casually, in loose black jeans and a sleeveless t-shirt, as opposed to his normal ring attire of spandex and black tape. Casually, he runs a hand through the dirty blonde, shoulder length hair that frames his thin face, brushing a few locks away from steel blue colored eyes. His other hand holds the receiver of a cheap plastic phone to his ear, the cord snaking its way somewhere off camera. He sighs a bit, his thin chest rising beneath the worn black cotton of his shirt as he speaks in a somewhat exasperated, frustrated tone. “No, I’m not by any means taking anything away from Winters. The guy is good, no doubt about it. If he weren’t, he would not have received that tile shot in his debut match. On the other hand though, and this is the point I’m trying to make, the guy is your typical, run of the mill, blue-chipper.” He; nonchalantly, picks out something from beneath his fingernails as he speaks. “He’s cocky personified.” Shaking his head as he speaks, his hand in front of him as he searches for more grime beneath his nails. “He’s good and he knows it too well. I mean c’mon man, the guy ran a promo of himself exercising for no other reason then because he can. He acts like he’s god’s gift to the sport or something, and that will be his downfall. I honestly believe this guy thinks he is the future of Gee See Dubya, and that’s a good thing.” Jayce pauses for a brief minute as he listens, concentrating on the voice on the other end. His hand comes up briefly, rubbing the bridge of his nose between his thumb and index finger, taking in the unheard words from the opposite line before speaking again. “Yes, I know that, but understand what I’m saying here. I know full well I have the capabilities to beat him. I’ve been thrown through tables, glass windows, walls, and mirrors. God knows how many times I’ve been tossed off of scaffolds, stages and what not. Anything this guy throws at me I’m positive I can take.” Another brief pause, as someone speaks on the other end. “The only thing I have to worry about is his lame, high school throwback jersey. To be honest with you it wouldn’t surprise me if he breaks out some lame ass trophy that he won back in the day. That’s his thing man, he’s going to try and play himself up as the next coming of LeStatt Knight or something. It’s like he thinks this match is somehow similar to one of his damn High School Football games. Like he’s going to throw the wining touchdown to win the match, and get carried out of the ring on the top of the fans shoulders, or some shit.” Bradley smirks a bit, a chuckle escaping through tight lips. “To be honest with you, the punk gets on my nerves’but I’ll just leave it at that.” Bradley once again pauses as he listens to the reply from the other end of the line, before smirking a bit, a slight chuckling echoing from his thin frame. He shakes his head a bit as he pulls a tattered soft pack of cigarettes from his pocket--shaking one loose of the paper wrapper and sliding it between his lips. He pauses briefly before a silver Zippo is produced, the blue and yellow flame lighting the end as snake like tendrils of smoke coil in front of his face. “Yeah, I’m confident. I have absolutely no reason not to be. I’m by no means scared of the guy. I’m going to take it to this guy like I would any other. I’m going to take everything this guy has to throw at me, and then give it back double. Fuck the witty retorts and all that shit. I’m going to speak in the ring, granted he may not have the slightest clue in hell of who I am.” He smiles to himself. Confidently. “I promise he will when the match is over.” Something is said on the other end, causing him to shake his head. “If I have my way, he’s leaving on a stretcher. While I laugh my ass off as he passes me by. I got something to prove, and I intend on making a lasting god damn impression with this match. Unfortunately, he’s the guy that’s gonna be on the receiving end.” Bradley nods, as once again the voice on the end of the receiver speaks. Taking a long drag from his cigarette, he turns, looking out the grime stained window that rests in the wall behind the Television. Outside, the evening skyline looks ever wonderful in its blanket of smog, the stars barely able to pierce the reddish green haze. “Look man, I gotta go...Gotta hit the gym.' Jayce nods to something said on the other end, a smile splashed on his face. 'Just catch WorldWide. I intend on putting on one hell of a show.” With that, Bradley places the telephone back on the small cradle and heads toward the door. Pausing only long enough to grab a yellow and black, leather motorcycle jacket hanging next to the door on a small brass hook, as well as his gym bag throwing it over a thin muscled shoulder. With a quick twist of his hand, he sets back the latch of the deadbolt and pulls the door open. The hinges let loose an audible squeal, screaming for oil as Bradley steps into the hall. The door shuts slowly behind him, leaving only scarred, paint chipped wood in the camera’s view before the scene fades to black.
