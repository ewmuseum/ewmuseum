Date: May 23, 2006 ---- First Attempt: The Fall Of The Black Mariah I sit here clutching useless lists And keys for doors that don't exist I crack my teeth on pearls I tear into the history Just show me what it means to me in this world... ---- Burning brilliance, high above the world. Shining brightly upon ignorant millions, day in and day out sheds light upon their otherwise dark and sinister world. The sun glows for what seems to be an eternity. When does that eternity stop? Many continue throughout their days never thinking that one day the light will stop. One day, the bright and colorful world they love will come to an end. It could be long after their death... it could be tomorrow. No one knew the answers, there was no telling what secrets the sun might know. These were just a few of the things going through her mind as she looked up into the sky. She was always told as a small child that if she store at the sun... she'd go blind. With her hand over her brilliant tealish cerulean blue eyes, she couldn't help but to be fascinated. It was such a beautiful day, the sun beat down on her like an angel as she stood on the balcony high above the Hyatt private parking facility. Her golden locks shone like she was heaven sent. Several young parking tenants had been gawking ever since she stepped out of her room. Wearing a pink baby doll tee that read PRINCESS with horns over the letters and a pair of tight black jeans, she seemed like a dream come true. They certainly believed in angels. The same could not be said for Vivica J. Valentine. Then again... there were a lot of things she never believed in until now. A few years ago vampires were but horrific fairy tales told to small children to keep them in bed at night and off the streets. TICK... TICK... TICk... She was still taking this new reality one day at a time. It was hard to keep everything in perspective, it's times like these that really helped The Black Mariah keep herself grounded. Living each week in a fish bowl for the entertainment of millions seems glamorous... and it was even a dream for her. It was hard to interact with society. With even a small amount of fame came a price... 'HEY!' A scream from down below startled The Black Mariah on the balcony. With a blink it took a while for her to regain a normal sense of vision. Vivica slipped a pair of black sunglasses over her eyes to help the adjustment process. Looking down her look of deep thought quickly turned into a snarl. 'I thought they didn't allow pets at this hotel. I better go inform the managment that Eva VanLorne is keeping her with her.' Vivica retaliated with a hint of hatred in her voice. Down below Brandon Youngblood looked up at her with from behind a pair of Black Oakley's. 'Cute.' Brandon replied, 'You're forgetting I'm not the one here obcessed with Eva. That would be you... Eva happens to be obcessed with me. Not that I can blame her. Actually, I was just wondering... when I spit on you last week... did you spit or swallow? The public needs to know.' 'How about I go down there and make you swallow my fucking shoe.' Vivica responded gripping a tightly on the railing, leaning over to get a good look at him. Her blonde hair with baby blue tips hung down over her cheek, which became flustered as Youngblood took off his USC Reggie Bush jersey and laid it down on a car hood next to him to reveal a tight white tank top. 'If you want to finish this we don't have to wait till WorldWide. I saw the look in your eyes last week... you can't wait to get this over with and honestly, I'm more interested in spending my night on GCW going after something important. Why should I bother with a 2nd tier champion when the real champion is next door?' Youngblood opened up his arms as if daring Vivica to make a move, 'Or are you too scared to come alone? Do you need your big brothers and sisters to beat me up for you?' With a frusterated grunt The Black Mariah simply turned her back and began to walk away from the balcony. Down below, she could hear Brandon Youngblood to start what sounded like clucking. Vivica clinched her fist into a ball trying to restrain herself from going down there. Last time she did something like this she was fined a lot of money... but then again money isn't something hard to come by for her. Caldera wasn't in any position to start handing out fines anyway, he had his own problems. What would Dies Irae think? If she broke his legs now... there would be one less insignificant speed bump on the road to judgement day. TICK... TICK... TICK... A fire in her eyes and an attitude in her heart, Vivica opened the door in her hotel room with authority making it bounce off the wall. With a thud in the distance The Black Mariah marched through the hallway with authority, ignoring the thick air that seemed to grow around her. Down the stairwell she sprinted, her heart beating faster and faster. This couldn't wait until WorldWide, this had to be solved now. Kicking open the door on the bottom floor of the stairwell, the hotel goers looked in silence as The Black Mariah stormed past. Her eyes narrowed on the exit revolving door that lied ahead. A bellhop pulling luggage unknowingly pulls out in front of her... only to find himself on his ass and luggage scattered along the marble floors shortly thereafter. With a quick palm strike to the glass, she pushed her way to the outside where Brandon Youngblood took off his sunglasses. 'It's nice to see you could make it...' Youngblood stated sarcastically, 'What took you so long? Are you not ready for your career to end yet?' 'You know what. You may not believe in vampires, but you're sure as hell going to believe that hell hath no fury like a woman scored!' Vivica screamed as she pulled her shirt up over hear head and threw it on the ground, revealing a black sports bra. The valets who once were gawking seemed to flock to the scene in order to get a glimpse. As The Black Mariah began to rush towards Youngblood he pointed up towards her hotel room. At the last second The Black Mariah turned around just in time to see dark cloud billowing out of the room that laid next door to hers. No... it couldn't be. Stopping in disbelief she took a few steps away from Youngblood, who gave her a shove down onto the concrete parking lot. Vivica snarled back at Youngblood but couldn't help but look back up at the building once more. It was like her dream all over again... Shadow in the fire. It had to be a trick... right? 'I thought you wanted to fight?' Brandon said kicking Vivica in the stomach while she was on her hands and knees on the ground. Ignoring the pain Vivica got up to one knee and glared back at Youngblood. TICK... TICK... TICK... 'You fucker...' Those were her last words as she sprinted back inside the building. Behind her, Brandon Youngblood picked his Bush jersey up off the car and brushed off his hands. As fast as she could she sprinted up the stair well. Adrenaline pumped though her veins, making the pain of her possibly broken ribs and gravel inflicted scrapes go away. Lowering her shoulder, The Black Mariah busted through the door and quickly her eyes began to water as dark smoke congested the hallway. Coughing the entire way Vivica felt the wall grow warmer and then burning hot as she made her way towards her best friend. In front of her the door that once was the entrance to The Black Magdalene's room was ignited in flame. covering her nose and mouth with a cupped hand Vivica closed her eyes and burst into the room. 'LIA!' Vivica screamed at the top of her lungs, the air was growing too thick. The ash began to consume her lungs as the flames danced around her. Tears began to flow from her eyes as she dropped to one knee. She could feel her lungs stop and felt like she was going to explode as she fell onto the burning ground. With her last image, she swore she saw a silhouette in the corner of flame. She could've sworn she heard... 'Everything is going to be okay...' TICK... Suddenly a sound from next door grabs the attention of the Black Mariah, breaking her out of he daydream. She begins to back up slowly through the sliding glass doorway. The roar in the room next to get grows louder and louder. Vivica spins around and knocks over an inn table. A lamp shatters on the floor at her feet and tourist advertisements scatter across the carpet as The Fearless Phenom leaps over the table and sprints towards the door. With the turn of a knob and the jerk of the door, Vivica J. Valentine found herself unexpectedly face to face with Brandon Youngblood. His eyes hid behind a pair of Black Oakley's as he looked back at his WorldWide nemesis with a gallon container for gasoline in his hand. Vivica looked over at Shadow's room and felt the heat begin to rise in the room. Her eyes connected with the plastic gasoline container before looking back up at him. A piercing hatred consumed her as she cocked back her hand and slapped Youngblood across the face knocking his glasses down the hall. Slowly Brandon's head turned and looked back at The Black Mariah, before spitting directly into her face once again. You could almost see the hatred rise up through Vivica J. Valentine before she jumpes across the hallway with a scream, ripping and pulling on Brandon's USC Reggie Bush jersey. Falling down onto the hallway floor, Youngblood falls out of his pair of black flip flops before shoving Valentine away from him and into the wall. 'We don't need to wait until WorldWide, I can finish you right now and spend the rest of my time concentrating on the real champion in your little group.' He said, taking off his jersey and hanging it on a handrail that lined the hallway, 'Why should I waste my time like you shooting for second best?' TICK... TICK... TICK... 'Don't you fucking disrespect me!' Vivica screamed as she tried to kick Youngblood and it was caught before he used her leg to throw her against the wall. The Black Mariah grunts and falls the the floor. Down at the end of the hallway, dark black cloud of smoke begin to billow out of Shadow's room. Watching in disbelief, Vivica stands up and begins to walk away from Youngblood only to be caught in a KataHaJime. Vivica struggles to break free as she's being pulled farther and farther away from her best friend. The alarm squeels above them, and silver lights lining the walls begin to pulsate like a strobe light. Suddenly the doors of a majority of the rooms in the hall open at once to be greeted by panic stricken occupants. A stampede of homosapien's... men, women, black, white, hispanic, young, and old scream as they rush through the hallway. They run by Youngblood and Vivica, almost as if they were invisible on the way past. An 8-year old kids runs into Brandon, forcing him to loosed the hold just a bit as Vivica is fading. With her last bit of consciousness, Vivica springs her feet up against the closest wall and pushes back with authority. The contact of Youngblood's back against the metal door of the stairwell breaks it off the hinges. Pain shimmered through Brandon's body as the small of his back slammed into the stainless steel door knob. They both fell onto the concrete stairwell, rolling helpless down the floor below as a many watched and far fewer cared. Blood trickled out of the nose of The Black Mariah as she pulled herself to het feet at the bottom of the stairwell. Quickly she was jerked backwards by a man in a yellow coat. Kicking and screaming Vivica tried her best to escape as more men came by to help restrain her. Youngblood smiled as watched the fire department pull Vivica J. Valentine out of the building against her will. TICK... TICK... TICK... 'We'll take care of it miss. Let us handle this.' The fireman tried to calm down Vivica as he and two of his friends pulled her farther and farther away from the stairwell. Through the glass doors that entered the building the sun beat down upon Miss Valentine. Looking up as they pulled her away, she could see the clouds grow darker and thicker as they exited the building. Tears began to flow down the face of The Black Mariah. The second they put her down, she sprinted back towards the entrance... only to be tackled by several police officers a couple feet away from the barricade that had already been put up around the entrance to the Hyatt. With a face full of gravel, she couldn't believe the mistake she had made. Her own selfish plot for revenge had cost her best friend her life. How could she let something like that happen... 'We found one...' She could hear the radio of a fire chief close to her, 'It was too late, she's already gone. We lost her chief. She hardly looks human at all... God bless her soul.' TICK... From behind Youngblood tries to grab her, but The Black Mariah whips him into the door... breaking it off it's hinges. Inside the room the edge of the bed was in flames, where Shadow looked around her standing up. 'LIA!' Vivica shouted stepping over Brandon Youngblood and running into the room. As Vivica watched and racked her brain for a solution, Youngblood reached his feet and dusted himself off and walked away with a bit of an arrogant smile. 'Nazdar, Vivica.' Vivica said looking into the eyes of her mentor. Lia looks back at The Black Mariah as she closed her eyes. TICK... TICK... TICK... The dark clouds began to bring tears to the eyes of The Black Mariah as she reopened them to get a better look around. She couldn't just stand there and die. With a leap of faith, Vivica jumped off the bed towards Lia and her pink princess shirt burst into flame. In a frantic struggle Vivica managed to stumble out the door way while trying to pull the flaming shirt off of her body. Throwing the shirt back into the room, Lia could see the burns all around Vivica's body. Picking her up off the ground, Lia rushed The Black Mariah towards the exit while countless patrons tried to force their way out of the building. Eventually the duo made it to the staircase. Smoke had filled the lungs of Miss Valentine, who was slowly beginning to lose consciousness as Lia dragged her down the stairwell. Each step became more and more blurry as they made their way to the exit. The door back into the lobby of the hotel busted open and it appeared to Vivica to be the light at the end of the tunnel. Perhaps this was the end for The Black Mariah. It was certainly not what she'd planned it to be... TICK... TICK... TICK... As they approached the light at the end of the tunnel someone suddenly grabbed Vivica away from Shadow's shoulders. A blurry glance from Vivica made it out to be what appeared to be a firefighter. Shadow followed in toe as a group of EMT's surrounded The Black Mariah and shoved an oxygen mask against her face. Taking deep breaths the group made their way outside. As the sun beat down upon Vivica, she looked up at the room... where dark black smoke clouds billowed from the room. Something that would normally be a sight of horror gave Vivica a bit of satisfaction while she groggily looked back at Shadow. A smile crossed her face from under the gas mask as she looked back up at the sun. She'd often forgot Shadow's ability to brave the elements. She couldn't help but think now nice Lia looked under the sunlight. 'Thank you.' Shadow said as she followed The Black Mariah into the back of an ambulance on the scene. The looked back into each others eyes, they could almost see what the other was thinking. It was a bond far greater than that of your typical 'wrestling stable.' They were friends... almost sisters. It was amazing how far they'd come since the day they fought in the parking lot next to Zenith Corporation. Lia's gaze was soothing, it was enough alone to get her through. TICK... TICK... The sound of birds chirping quickly calmed her down as she quickly came to realize that everything was nothing more than a horrfic nightmare... she hoped. Everything felt so real, she hadn't experienced anything quite like that since the days of Hunting Grounds back in New Mexico. It was something she never wished to experience again. Getting out of bed in a dazed state, she slipped on a pair of black jeans that sat next to the bed... laid out for her the night before next to a pink baby tee that read Princess with horns over the letters. After getting into a fresh set of clothes, Vivica quickly noticed how nice of a day it had become. The sunlight burst in through the sliding glass door that lead out to the balcony. Maybe a breath of fresh air would make her feel a little better. Anything to get her mind off the horrific visions that still replayed in her mind. Burning brilliance, high above the world. Suddenly she blinked and looked around, an intense case of deja vu flickered through her mind as she backed away from the edge of the balcony and turned back to go inside her room once more. Maybe it wasn't a dream... maybe it was real. If it was real, she didn't have much time. From outside her window she suddenly heard someone shout. 'HEY!' A voice echoed through her window from the parking lot. Her heartbeat picked up as she scrambled out of the door and looked both ways down the hallway. There was no one there... not a single person. As if to convince herself, The Black Mariah turned around and looked at the door the housed her best friend and mentor Shadow. Suddenly the door pushed open making Vivica jump back a bit. 'Nazdar, Vivica.' The familiar voice of Lia rang through her ears as her frantic heartbeat turned into one of joy, and a smile quickly grew across her face. 'What was that all about?' 'It's been a long night...' Vivica ran her fingers through her hair, a little bit embarassed about believing in silly nightmares. 'Yeah, you're telling me...' Shadow responded with a bit of sarcasm, 'Vivica...' 'Yeah?' Vivica responded, finally being able to control herself after the frantic process of waking up. With the return to ground after the adrenaline high, she found herself getting a littly groggy... wiping off her eyes a little bit to get a better view of her stablemate. 'Do you think you're ready for Youngblood?' Shadow asked her, 'You saw our match, you know what he's capable of. If I could just get another shot at him, I could show you how it's done...' 'I'll be fine...' Vivica said, trying to force a smile, 'I think I'm more ready for this than I'll ever be. Maybe after I'm done with him, we can show him just how real we really are.' 'Heh.' Shadow responded with a bit of a smirk on her face. Vivica smirked a little bit as she followed Shadow. Shadow could see the intensity in her eyes as they entered the room. This time, it was just a little bit different, it was like something deep down inside The Black Mariah had changed. Vivica had a stut about her as she entered Shadow's hotel room to find Virgil Ambrosi sitting on one of the beds waiting. This would usually be the time she'd say soemthing. Alas, she didn't need to talk shit to impress people and win matches, afterall... no one likes a fuck. 'Sit down Viv, I have some... stratedgy I'd like to discuss with you. Let's make sure this is a night Mr. Youngblood won't forget for a very long time...' Vivica quickly blocked the anger she'd built up against Youngblood in her mind to pay attention to what Shadow had to say. Afterall, that is the lesson of today's story. Prioities. Something that Vivica will have to work hard to accomplish, but nothing was too hard for the cause. It had become her cause, and she was never planning on giving it up. TICK...
