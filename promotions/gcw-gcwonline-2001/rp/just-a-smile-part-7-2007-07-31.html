Just a Smile: Part 7

31 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 31st, 2007 You would think with their money, they would be able to go some place other than Target to do their personal shopping. Watch the two closely; I followed Scott and Tessa as they entered the Target shopping center that was located near their condo. Tessa headed off toward the packing section, most likely to get more stuff for the move. Scott, on the other hand, decided to hang around the Planned Parenthood aisle. Under any normal circumstances I would have followed Tessa, but the fact that Scott was hanging around the condoms struck my interest. Why would a man who had a pregnant fianc’e need condoms for? As I hung back, a woman walked past me and entered the aisle with Scott. She was attractive, looking like the typical Miami type. Passing by Scott, the man known to many as Chris Storm, grabbed a box of Magnums and turned to her. “Oh, pardon me; I was just about to buy these Magnums.” The woman just looked him over and continued walked as if she hadn’t even heard him. I mouth dropped open, surprised by his actions. That is until the next woman entered the aisle and Scott repeated his performance. “Scott!” came the shriek of his love, Tessa. The woman saw her and kept her walking as Scott turned to Tessa. “Hey, I was just about to buy these Magnums” he said with a cheesy grin slapped on his face. Tessa rolled her eyes and let out a sigh, “you don’t need condoms.” “Do you two need any help?” came an interruption from a Target worker behind them. When Tessa was about to tell him no, Scott cut her off and held up the box of Magnums. “Do you have anything larger or should I just go get a trash bag?” The young male was just stunned, at a lost of words. Tessa, however, knew just what to do and elbows Scott in the ribs. “Come on, let’s just go check out.” Embarrassed, Tessa took her stuff to the nearest register. Rather than follow them back to their condo, I decided to instead to head back to my motel. I needed to gather my things before I set the plan in motion. For a moment I feel guilty about setting Justin up like this. He and I had been friends since the first day of college when we were assigned roommates together. But this had to be done, even if it meant making sacrifices. That is, after all, what love is; making sacrifices. I needed Scott out of the way so that Tessa would be alone. He didn’t love her, he didn’t deserve her. Not with the way he was. She needed a man that would care for her, that would make her happy. I could do that. I’m sure Justin would be glad to do this for his old college mate; after all we had been through together. Despite Talos? Greek-like look, neither of us had ever been popular, I’m guessing it must have been the same way in High School for him as well. He tried so hard in college to be popular, which ended with us being hazed countless times when he would try to join fraternity houses. His favorite phrase “every Greek needs a Geek” never did him much favor, as it usually ended with them laughing and us duct taped to something. That is why Justin agreed to this. Just for once he wanted to be the one to be pranking someone else, instead of being the one pranked. For that I feel bad, having to manipulate my friend into doing what I want. But as long as it is for a greater good, I’ll persevere. Arriving at my motel, I quickly snatched up whatever I could and threw it into a file. I needed to hide whatever could be traced back to me, just in case. All my pictures of Scott and Tessa needed to be hidden in a place where no one would find them. It hadn’t occurred to me just how many photographs I had until the shoe box I had for photos overfilled as I tried to cram extras laying around into it. There was a knock on the door, startling me and causing me to drop what papers I had in my hands. Throwing everything on the far side of the bed to obscure it from view, I rose up and looked to the door. “That you Justin?” “Who else” And I brought a twelve pack!” Relieved, I let the panic look escape my face. “It’s unlocked” I cried out to him, grabbed the closed shoe box of photos to throw into the trunk of my car. Not the brightest pupil in the class, Justin could help me pack my stuff to be transported to the storage without even realizing what he was doing. Trying to move past him with the box, one of the overstuffed photos accidentally slipped on, landing right at his feet. Picking it up, Justin looked it over, “who’s she?” Snatching the photo from him and cramming it back into the shoe box, I brushed past him and toward my car. “That’s my girlfriend I told you about” I said as I popped open the trunk. Justin tried to look at it again but I already had it tossed in the trunk of my car. “Yeah, I think I remember her from the show.” Cracking open a beer, Justin looked nervous. “What’s the matter” I ask, looking for any sign that he might chicken out on me. Staring into his beer before taking another sip, his eyes turned toward me, “it’s just that, I remember the other guy. He was big. I’m not sure this is going to be so easy.” I should have known he would start to lose his nerve as he sobered up. Throwing my arm over him, I took one of the beers from the twelve pack and gave him a smile as I cracked it open. “Dude, wrestling is fake” I told him, moving away from him and leaning over the desk. “Those muscles are from steroids. There is nothing behind them, just there for looks. Trust me, I’ve met this guy. He HAS to be on the juice. He may look jacked up, but I bet he strains to pick up the Sunday paper.” Justin slowly smiled, letting me know my words were starting to reach him. I tossed him another beer as he finished his. “Look, I’m going to take some stuff out to the dumpster. You just need to go get ready for tonight. We’re going to get him so badly; even Ashton Kutcher would be shocked.” “Totally” Justin said, finally getting back on board again. Holding up the rest of the beer, he started to head out, “I’m taking the rest, cool?” I just nodded my head, letting him know it was fine. Justin smiled again before leaving out. Tossing a few more boxes into my trunk, I made sure everything looked good. The motel looked clean, nothing that could trace me toward what would happen. Picking up my cell phone, I took a deep breath as I readied to start my plan. I pressed two buttons on my cell phone (gotta love speed dialing) and heard the phone ringing. The ring quickly changed to a caller tone of Cypress Hill’s “Rock Superstar”. Somehow I wasn’t surprised by his choice of song. But the song was interrupted by a voice. “Scott?” He didn’t sound happy to hear my voice, another thing that didn’t surprise me. “Hey” I started off, trying to shake the nervousness out of my voice, “I was just wondering if you would want to head down to the bar?” I had to move the phone away from my ear as he yelled, damn near making me deaf. How was I supposed to know about some Tsunami. Though I didn’t have a clue what a flood in Australia had to do with him and getting a beer. “Ah come on man, just one” I begged, knowing that he had to go to the bar if I wanted this plan to even start. “Look, just one beer and I promise I won’t ask a single question” I told him, knowing this couldn’t fail. “Tell you what, you do this for me and I’ll leave you and Tessa alone for three days.” He argued, “five days”, he still wouldn’t back down, “fine a week.” There was a moment of silence on the line and I worried Scott had hung up on me. I could hear Tessa’s voice on the other end, wondering where he was planning to go. “Tessa doesn’t have to go; this would just be guy’s night out.” Scott didn’t seem to disagree with her not needing to go. It annoyed me how controlling he acted. But then he agreed and my whole body nearly lifted from the ground. Despite having the phone away from my ear for a second, I still heard him tell me “but none of that fag shit.” I allowed the comment to slide though, after all he agreed to head down to the bar. Hanging up with Scott, I switched the call over the Justin. As soon as he picked up the phone I just let him know “he’s on his way.” Justin sounded nervous again, knowing that Scott was going to be heading his way. “It’s simple, Justin” I told him, going over the plan again, “I’ll lead him toward you, you jump him from behind. Then when you have him down we mask him and throw him in a van I’ll have waiting.” Giving Justin a second to collect the information I continued, “The van will head back to a different motel room I have set up for us. We’ll throw him into the vacant room and prank him like we were going to shoot him, but they’ll be water guns so don’t worry.” I had to make sure I threw in the part about the water guns, as Justin started to sound even more worried at the mere mention of a gun. “Then after he pisses his pants, we’ll take a picture and send him on his way. We upload the picture on the internet of the big, bad wrestler pissing on himself and we have our ultimate prank.” Justin agreed, sounding pumped up again. I smiled, his excitement wearing off onto me. Or maybe it was a smile because I had fooled Justin into doing exactly what I wanted. “Ok, Justin, I need to go as I need to tie up a few more loose ends. We want this to go off seamless, right?” He agreed, hanging up with me. The poor sap. But sacrifices would have to be made. I was willing to make those sacrifices, as I am sure Justin was willing to make them for me. I hated to trick him, I really did. It needed to be done, however. I would hate to see the look on his face when he realized the “water gun” is actually a loaded pistol and he just killed one of the most famous wrestlers in Global Championship Wrestling. But it needed to be done. Tessa needed to be freed from Scott. I needed to free her.
