Date: 8/26/06 ~A bit more then a week ago~ If you take a piece of bacteria and place it in a petri dish and study it, it becomes more then that of which it started from. Humans are no different. The more we study them the more they change some times for the good sometimes for the bad. But then again life is a gamble isn't it. No matter what you think things do change and at times change for the better. Change comes about from stimuli from the outside. When things change around you, you have to change and adapt. In the case of some things, that change is so fast and abrupt... Dani had killed something more then an animal she was going to eat. And she'd killed him out of spite, anger, hatred. She had attacked him to save Gray. But the others in the area had not tried to fight much more after being injured. A few others had been dispatched and they had taken the high road out and left the battle for fear of loosing their life. But when Dani's hand touched the skin of the being that had been known as Baraqiel, she had known everything about it. Everything it had ever done wrong... he had been the one who had betrayed her great grandfather, the angel Kokabel. He was the one that had forced the Destroyer to be found out and allowed the others to find him. In a moments impulse, she had wished to get the vengeance, the wrath for what he'd done to her family and for the other angel who's remains she was seeking. And the sword she'd picked up, the one that Blaise had wielded since he'd taken it from an angel many months ago, responded and had ripped his heart out. She only wanted one thing at that time. To vomit. But she'd killed him. She'd done so in a way that made his immortal soul, if they had one, unable to come back to his body. She'd destroyed his very essence. And then she'd witnessed a man she had recently begun to finally call a friend, beat the ever living hell out of her boyfriend.. And after all of that? Danielle wasn't in the mood to get into Blaise's car in the least. She would have preferred to just walk back to Seattle if she could of. At this time she didn't want to ride in the same car as Josh or Blaise. She didn't even want to speak to either of them all the way home. But what she did want? She knew Blaise wouldn't allow. More then ever she wanted to go see him. It was just that strong of a pull to him, she didn't care if she exposed herself to everyone in Seattle at this point. But lucky for her the ride back was quiet. Looking at Alexis she could see that she that she and Gray had developed some sort of connection. She leaned into him on the way back to the condos. Gray sat there looking out the windows ever on guard just in case the night wasn't over. Danielle was trying to clean herself off using wet wipes that Blaise kept in the car either for himself or the kids she wasn't sure, but no matter how hard she tried the blood just didn't seem to want to come off. Black Blood. The blood of Baraqiel. The blood of “The Lightning of God”. When she got to the Condo, she stripped on her way into the bathroom. Gray went right for the medical kit to help others, and fight Alexis off from wanting to check his own wounds. Gray was a garou, he'd heal in no time. Over all it was a quiet night. Josh sat in the corner of the condo, he was looking like a scolded dog. But anyone that knew him knew what was truly bothering him is he could feel everyone's emotions around him. And at this point he was on over drive. Danielle spent an hour in the shower washing her body of the black tar that was the blood of the angel that had fallen on what was now her sword. It ran down her arms and into her jeans, which now were stained with her blood, the black blood of the Angel and dirt. The holes torn into them made them almost fashionable. But she couldn't wait to get them off. When she got out everyone was still there and she was next to be looked over. And of course all sorts of notes to be taken about what happened. She'd fallen for only the great spirit knew feet when Astaroth had let her go. And she'd landed onto the ground and took no damage that she could tell. She had seen and felt the shield encase her, the protective aura that could only be called the angel's halo. It was the only thing she'd seen everyone, including her great grandfather produce that resembled a nimbus of light that encased them. She'd also manifested the ability to control an orphaned angelic sword. It was hers now. She'd taken all the time she could to clean the very ichor of death off of her. When everyone was done looking about and making sure everyone was okay. There was only one thing left for her to do that night. Her nimbus waned quite a bit and then she spoke to the Lord of the Order. “So, did I prove myself enough to see what I've come here for?” He nodded. “Yes, come with me.” Lord Ishram had conceded the fight. And was getting ready to show her her goal. However, it looked like Lord Malik was not that satisfied. He reached behind himself and pulled forth his soul blades again. He was not a good loser, that was for sure. Josh saw him as he lunged. “Dani... Look out!” Dani spun just in time to see him in the air. She reacted on instinct, not unlike when she had been tossed down by Astaroth a few weeks ago. Her nimbus flared up again, but so did the arc of lightning that now served her. This one wasn't small though. It impaled Malik. He dropped to the ground splayed out. His daggers slid across the ground. He'd lost full control of his faculties with that hit. Dani's wings smoked from the blast that she had hit him with. Ishram laughed a hearty chuckle. “So, even when one does not play by the rules I set forth, they can not win here. Please Ms. Northsky, let us go to the museum. I have something you may wish to see.” She reduced her nimbus, but did not pull in her wings. They were almost second nature here where other things that should not be are. She came into a line behind him, Josh falling behind her. They headed out through a door to the side, and then down a spiral staircase. When they came down to the bottom, Ishram opened a door to another chamber. As they entered, Dani was in awe in what was on display. Skeletons, weapons, armor, artifacts all around of things that should not be. In the center was what Dani wanted to see. It was the piece de resistance of the entire collection. Standing on a pedestal in the middle, wired up to be as if it was standing there in some sort of stance was the skeleton that she was looking for. The humanoid part of the skeleton was about six feet tall, maybe a few inches over. From it's back though, hinged in a particular way were six full wings, not unlike her own. But six... this was a seraph like Astaroth. This was one of the highest choir of angels, more then a virtue like herself or her great grandfather. “This is the skeleton that had been in the ground at Fallen Mesa. This is the remains of the angel that had been hunting Astaroth and his followers. This is the angel who destroyed three legions of hell and killed Rashavarak before he was slain by the forces of heaven who had determined he'd gone rogue. This is the remains of the very angel who had rained hellfire and brimstone down upon both Sodom and Gomorrah.” Dani was in awe. She stepped forward and spread her own wings out to mimic what she saw with her only pair. However, Joshua seemed to have a spark at what Ishram was saying. He didn't say anything though. He just watched Dani as she examined the skeleton of a Fallen Angel. Ishram interrupted her though. “So, young one. Do you know what you need to do next?” Dani shook her head. “No, to be honest, I've been flying off the seat of my pants for about a year now.” Ishram chuckled. “Ah, you are truly worthy of all that they have given you credit for. You would make a fine member of my harem. I think when you've tired of this one, you should come back to me. My grandson may have the right idea. Taking an immortal wife may be a nice kick in the pants, especially one as fiery... nay, as electric as you.” Dani didn't laugh at his pun. “Well, if you're an asshole, I'd not even consider it. So do you know what I’m supposed to do next?” He shook his head. “Yes, everything happens in this as it's supposed to. The prophecy is going strong in you. Do what comes naturally.” Dani backed up and spread her wings out again, they almost had a mind of their own at this time. Dani's eyes flared up as the arcs began again across her wings. She put up her hands and electrical bolts came forth from all parts of her body and coursed into the skeleton. She reached and grabbed the skeleton's legs and allowed the full charge to course through her and through the metal pieces that had been used to piece and hold the display together. It didn't' take much. The bones were hundreds of years old, and had been sun bleached and dried in the New Mexican desert for just as long. The bones disintegrated, destroying them and the remaining physical links this thing had to the world. Ishram again clapped for her. “Very good. Now it can not return to this body once you find where the spirit resides.” Dani looked at him. “What?” “Oh, you didn't get that memo?” She let out a sound. It was part sigh, part temper tantrum. “What the hell type of game is this?” Ishram held out his hands. “No, the game is still on. You have done what was needed. You figured it out. Almost all of the physical remains were destroyed. When the spirit is released from it's current container, it will need a new one. That will be you.” She just looked at him as if she was shooting daggers into him. “Well that is if you have the right tools for it. I think those would make a fine, fine choker addition.” She looked down at what he gestured to. A few small bones, they looked like they were from the wings, were laying there, spared the fate of the other, larger ones. “Now, you've done your deed. I'll have Annie contact Blaise and update him to your situation. I'll summon my plane to fly you back.” Ishram was rather straight forward that he was dismissing them as Dani quickly started to gather the remaining bones. He moved towards the door. “When you're done, I'll have my servants prepare you some food and you may catch a few hours of rest. You'll need it. Then we can send you home. And don't worry, I'll make sure that you're no where near my son when heading back. He's a rather sore loser.” Ishram continued to walk away and leave them. ~To Be Continued~
