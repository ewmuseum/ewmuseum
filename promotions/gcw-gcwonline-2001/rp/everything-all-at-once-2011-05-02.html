Everything all at once.

2 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/02/11 The breeze blows in from his back as he slowly walks down Soley St towards the Bunkerhill monument. His eyes roam from left to right as he observes the history shown on the buildings on this block. Turning his gaze he looks over at his wife and he smiles. Turning his attention back towards the monument a crowd of people are standing on the corner. His wife turns and looks at him as she speaks. “Look Andrew we don't have to be here for this.” She reaches forward and grabs his hand re assuredly. He nods over at her as he turns and looks at what the distraction is. Clearing his throat he looks over at six FBI Agents running out of the corner building. Three people in tow handcuffed, not looking very happy either. One of the FBI Agents looks over at him and his wife, he then suddenly yells for three people and they run up to him. He points at Shaman as he speaks. “Mr. Whitecloud we found out you were staying in that corner block. We want you to come in for questioning about two Federal Agents and a Las Vegas police officer that is missing.” The Agent stands in front of Shaman defiant as he looks back and then turns to his wife. “Suzy go ahead and call George, I believe he has a branch here in Boston. These Agents have over stepped their bounds this time.” Shaman looks at them as his anger is growing, not just merely growing, but sky rocketing. Suzy nods as she opens her cell phone and dials a number. She waits momentarily as the phone rings and someone picks up on the other end. “George? Good good, this is Suzanne Whitecloud, yes Andrews wife.” After a brief pause she speaks again. “No that is fine, we have a serious matter involving the FBI again. Yeah they are still at it.” The Agent looks a little nervous as he looks towards the other two. Not knowing what is going on they turn and look at each other. Shaman stands arms crossed with a great bout of annoyance stretching his patience very thin. “Andrew hun, George says go with them again. He will have someone over to the Federal building to meet us when we arrive.” The FBI Agent steps forward and opens a car door. Shaman slips in quietly as he sits down patiently. Suzy goes to join him and is stopped by one of the Agents. “Ma'am you aren't included, please step back from the vehicle.” Suzy smirks as she nods her head and looks at the Agent. She picks up the phone and dials it. Moments later she grins as she speaks just out of range of the Agents who are staring at her in confusion. “Uhm who are you..?” Looking at his badge as he shows it. “ Agent Miller is it, here this is my brother he wishes to speak with you.” The Agent takes the phone and responds in a rough serious tone, suddenly he gets a look of surprise on his face. He quickly hangs up the phone and opens the door. Shaman sits inside smiling as Suzy sits down. “Called your brother didn't you?” Suzy just shrugs and waits patiently as the Agents outside the vehicle argue and then two get in. The one in the drivers seat starts the vehicle and looks back at the two in the rear view mirror. “Think your smart calling one of our superiors didn't you? Lets see how smart you are when we bust the both of you for murder and kidnapping Federal Agents. Maybe spread the word you are cop killers eh?” He smiles as he slightly gains the upper hand. “Find that funny why don't you.” Shaman raises his hand cutting off Suzy before she says something. He sits there quietly his eyes not moving from the ones watching him in the rear view mirror. Not even flinching a muscle he stares bullet holes through the man who is threatening him. After about twenty minutes of riding they pull up to a Federal building and a man stands there with a briefcase. He stands waiting patiently as they all get out of the vehicle. The Agents open the door for Shaman and his wife, they both in turn nod to the lawyer as they walk into the building. They are escorted down the hall to a room with a table and five chairs. Shaman looks at the Lawyer who cut him off as he speaks himself. “Well we all know why we are here, but since this has been rehashed over and over again, we wish to make it a point that this is the third time the FBI has deemed it necessary to question our client.” Taking some paperwork out of his briefcase he slides it across the table to the Agents. His eyes narrow as he watches them read them. “As you see Gentlemen that the investigation involving Mr. Whitecloud has been concluded and has absolved him of any wrong doing or involvement in this case.” The one cocky Agent who was driving stands up and paces back and forth. He continues to hold the paper as he tries and struggles to come up with something. Shaman leans forward on the table and his forearms holding himself up slightly as he looks at the Agents. “Look I told you guys where I was, gave you information on what my staff was doing at the time of question. I gave you my full cooperation in this matter. You have my wife in here and are keeping me from my daughter.” He lowers his voice just enough to be heard as he makes it known he is not happy. “Now if anything has gone wrong and my daughter has come to any harm hell will be the best place for you.” Standing up he places both hands on the table staring at both Agents. He turns and looks at the lawyer his face stern with no remorse. “You handle this, my wife and I are out of here.” Shaman looks over at his wife and smiles softly. “Suzy go find Kaitlyn I need to go clear my head a bit.” Kissing her softly he turns and heads for the door. Suzy smiles and nods as she walks away in another direction. Slowly opening the door stepping out into the crisp air he frowns a bit as raindrops begin to fall. Pulling his coat closer to his body he tucks his chin down closer to his chest as he begins to walk. Occasionally he turns and looks at the people passing him. Thinking to himself, he wonders if they are like ants trudging along until one or a few simply die off. Traveling one by one into the unknown, the destiny that is always at hand, yet hidden just around the next corner. After walking the length of the block he stops and looks at the intersection he has come too. His eyes watch the automobiles, buses, and people simply stop and go. As the walk signal comes up he heads across the street. Noticing a nearby book store he heads over to it and enters it shaking off the rain. Clearing his throat and pulling wet hair from his face, he looks towards the clerk as he speaks. “Where are your magazines?” “Against the back wall behind the third row.” Waving his hand to his side in the general direction as he is dealing with customers. Slowly he walks between the aisles casually looking at the different titles, he recognizes a few of them as he stops and pulls Sun Tzu's Art of War off the shelf. Side stepping a rushing child he continues his journey to the back wall. Stopping he looks at several magazines and comes looking at the wrestling ones. His eyes focus on the one with him on the cover holding the GCW Hardcore title in his hand. Reaching out he grabs the magazine and flips through the pages as he speaks to himself. “Yeah a full blown story on Shaman being investigated by the FBI, Got to love this. They don't even know why just a ton of assumptions.” The lines blur together like some sort of psychologists ink blots as he grows disturbed at some of the words that seem to portray him. Words like steroids, ageless wonder, fraud, has been. “Look at this Jimmy Thunder, back when you and I were fighting for the US Title you were the one being accused of being a steroid user, now because my business isn't publicly known they think I am using steroids, because the FBI are trying to get into my business.” Tossing the magazine back up onto the shelf, he seems to be searching for something as he turns and walks down the rows of books. Back towards the cashiers desk he lays the one book he grabbed earlier down upon the counter. “I'll take this one thanks.” Reaching into his pocket he pulls out some money and pays for the purchase. The Cashier looks at him curiously and smiles. “Say aren't you?” Shaking his head quickly he looks at the cashier. “Nope I get that a lot though.” “Well you don't look like some doped up steroid freak anyways.” Shaking his head Shaman steps out the door. He wonders why this has had to come down on him. Hasn't he always busted his ass and made his own way without asking for favors? This time this whole situation bothers him immensely. He just wished people would leave him and his family alone. To contemplate his career and activities on his own accord. No interference ever in his life, just let him run his own business and activities as he saw fit. Stopping his cell was ringing, pulling it out of his pocket he slides the little bar and answers it. “Hello? Oh hey Suzy.” For a moment he listens and then shakes his head. “Yeah I'll be back by the place in a few hours, I am heading over to Fenway to check on something.” Walking along the sidewalk he tucks the book underneath his jacket. Heading down the street he is oblivious to the man following him from a few yards distance. The man weaves and ducks through the crowd trying to stay just out of sight. He has a cell phone to his ear as a few times he can be heard. “Yeah dude it is him.” The man stops as he loses sight of him and looks around in a panic. Frustrated he stops and looks at the phone. Lifting it back to his ear he speaks again. “Sorry man I lost him.” Hanging up the phone he simply watches the street and decides to go in the general direction he had last seen him heading in. Twenty minutes pass and a Taxi pulls up near Fenway park. Out steps Shaman handing the Taxi driver some money. “Keep the change.” Walking behind the Green Monster he eyes it as if he was a kid again. Smiling he remembers his grandmother taking him here to watch such greats as Wade Boggs, Jim Rice, and Mike Greenwell play. He walks in the door nearby and slowly walks through some sort of tunnel. Green and white painted numbers sit laid on beams and shelves nearby. Slowly he comes to another door and opens it. Stepping out into left field he smiles as he takes in the sight of Fenway park in all of its grand glory. “This right here brings me back to the place I should be here.“ He pounds his fist on his chest slightly. He places a flat palm on his chest as he holds the book that was under his jacket with a free hand. He sighs a bit as he looks around. “I know reality will comeback and bother me all over again. Little doubts of whether or not I can beat Jimmy Thunder, Truth is I have done it before and will try to do so again.” Shrugging a bit he walks across the vast outfield. “Jimmy Thunder is very capable of beating me, but I am also very capable of defeating him. Question is will I be ready to do so with all this drama happening in my damn life.” Slowing his walk across the edge of center field he heads for second base. He turns around facing the wall, the Green monster, the most imposing sight in Major League Baseball. “Will I be able to conquer a wall of my own? This I need to know, Jimmy Thunder is 1-1 and I am 1-0, If I win I am pretty much on the cusp of returning to Rampage. If I lose I am on the cutting edge of possibly not getting in. Win you're in, lose you're out, or even closer to it.” Frowning he turns as a man runs across the field towards him. Behind him two more people follow including the man who was following him earlier. All three stop breathing hard looking at him as he towers over each of them. “See guys I told you it was him.” He jams a thumb over his shoulder at Shaman. “He is Shaman, not some fake imposter, but the real deal. I saw him wrestle at the Fleet Center a few years ago. He rocked man.” Smirking he looks at the kid, opens the Art of War and signs the inside cover. He in turn gives it to the kid who is staring at it in awe. “Keep it kid, nice to know there are still a few fans who support me.” The kid clears his throat and grins. “Who can't support the Native American Bad Ass?” One of the other kids raises his hand and smiles as he looks over at Shaman. “I can I support Jimmy Thunder and Bryan Mayhem.” Rubbing his forehead he turns and walks away leaving the three at second base, he steps through the door once again shutting it behind him. This time he slowly makes his way to the street. He exits as Suzy is standing outside with their daughter Kaitlyn. He reaches down and lifts his daughter kissing her on the cheek. She wriggles and looks at him and frowns. “Ewwww Daddy is wet.” Suzy tosses a towel over his head as they turn and head towards a Chevrolet Tahoe sitting with its hazard lights blinking nearby. Opening the back door he sets his daughter in and locks her into her booster seat. Shutting the door he opens the front drivers side door and climbs in taking notice that Suzy has already entered and shut the passenger door. “So how about we go to the Boston Aquarium?” He winks at Suzy as she looks over at their beautiful daughter. Who at the moment is more excited about seeing the fish than realizing how peaceful and natural this moment actually is. Suzy smiles and places her hand over her husbands. He gently grips her hand and pulls out into traffic as his mind is set at ease once again. “I think I am just going to seize the day and worry about Jimmy Thunder tomorrow and let the lawyers deal with the investigation.” “Sounds good to me darling.” Suzy responds.
