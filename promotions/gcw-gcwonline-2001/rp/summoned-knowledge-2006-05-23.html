Summoned Knowledge

23 May 2006

Date: 5/23/06 Enroute to South Carolina From Charlotte, NC The road trip was taking forever. At least that is the way it seemed for Danielle. She and Josh had exchanged words a few times on the way. Hell, the'd exchanged words since she had walked backstage after challenging McKail to the match for the TV Title. She remembered it well... Before the TV Title Match last week Dani sits in the locker room, looking up at the screen. She reaches up to turn it off when Gray speaks out to her. ?Leave it on. Gotta watch all of these guys. Need to be prepared for anything.? She shrugged and left it on. ?What's wrong Sirius Star?? Gray had always been the 'big brother' to her. But before she could answer, Josh chimed up. ?She's pissed off at all of these guys coming in and getting shots at the TV title and such before her.? Gray looked down at her, she thought she was going to get yelled at. Instead she got something she wasn't expecting. ?So challenge the winner.? ?What?? ?Challenge the winner. Do something about it.? She started to get up out of her seat again. ?Well I'll go talk to the main office right now then.? ?No.? She spun around to look at her big brother, and the man who one day could be her brother-in-law. ?Don't go through the offices and get it lost in the shuffle. Go out there and make it public. Tell him that you want him in the ring next week.? Dani stepped towards the back of the locker room, looking like she was thinking it over. She found herself looking at her bags and other gear as she had brought it to get ready for her match with Jayce Bradley later on... and then she saw the bag with the biggest title she'd ever earned to this date in her career. The CCWA North American Title. She picked it up and held it in her hands. It had been months since she'd held another title. And she was feeling the need to prove herself. She's felt as if she hadn't meant much since coming to GCW. Everyone else in the truce and alliance had been gaining their accolades. It was her turn. She stormed out of the locker room, with both Josh and Gray smiling at her as she did. Back to the Present That didn't last long though. Quickly after she had come back after laying down her challenge and her stipulation to get GCW to accept her challenge, as well as hopefully play up to McKail's manlyhood, Josh had let her have it. The only argument she kept tossing in his face was the simple one. Before they had started dating, he'd not said anything when she had taken her top off for that photo shoot with FHM. In fact he had encouraged it. She sometimes didn't understand him and his jealousy. Well, she tried to. Ukyo had told her once before that it was spawned by those around him that looked at her with lust. If he sensed any of it, he got angry. She was his girl after all. Josh was possessive, that was for sure. But now, now he now sat next to her staring out the window with the look of a scolded child on his face. He couldn't win the argument on the stipulation for this match. They had just got notice today that GCW had applied for and got the permits for Charlotte just in case she did loose. That had really set Josh off just for today. ?You're not going through with it.? She looked over at him as he had mumbled into the window. ?Why not?? ?Because you can't. You can't degrade yourself like that.? ?First of all, Mr. Brand. I won't degrade myself. I don't have any reason to be ashamed of what the Creator gave me. I have no problems with showing my body. Besides, I'm not liking your tone on this.? ?What about my tone?? ?Do you think I'm going to loose this match?? Double edged sword there. If he said no, then he had no reason to be upset with the stipulation that she'd put herself under. If he said yes, then he was going into the dog house for a while. ?I didn't say that. I'm worried about the tactics these supposed heros keep using. Look at what Bradley tried this last week with Blaise. And then you beat him, fair and square! He's going to get involved, you know it! And then you're degrading yourself in front of everyone! Due to some jackass!? She almost pulled the car over. But instead she just looked ahead and answered him. She glanced to the mirror and say Gray was just sitting there, holding the bridge of his nose between his thumb and forefinger. ?My challenge was specific Josh. If there is anything that prevents it being a fair match, even the referee doing a fast count because he wants to see some uncovered cooter, it won't happen. They can get their hopes up and hope that those two idiots who booked me into a bikini match come down to referee, or that Bradley will interfere, but if I'm not pinned or submitted in a completely fair fight, then no boobie for them, okay?? He didn't answer. ?Now, enough about me taking off my cloths unless you're wanting to pull over and kick your brother out of the car. I've got enough on my mind with this request from this chief to come down to the reservation.? Gray put his hand up and touched his brother's shoulder. Josh turned his head and saw his brother just looking at him. Josh took the hint. ?I'm sorry, Dani. I guess I'm just a bit...? ?Jealous? Envious? Possessive?? ?D... all of the above.? She smiled at that. ?Now, lets get to this Catawba Reservation, and see what they want. I don't even know who they are.? At that Gray chimed in. ?They're a nation that absorbed quite a few others. They're language is based on Sioux even though they're over here in original native Cherokee land.? ?Siouxian?? She spoke up. Of course Dani was a Rosebud Lakota Sioux from South Dakota. She spoke Lakota fluently and could dance and sing their traditions. ?Yeah, so I'm going to guess why you were called down there.? That perked Dani up a bit as Josh started to look more like he was getting more accepting of the situations. And Dani's Highlander continued down the road towards the eastern part of South Carolina and a meeting she didn't quite know about. About an hour later Catawba Reservation RockHill, South Carolina As they pulled into the parking area, Danielle's skin crawled. The reservation was nice enough and all, but something here set her senses on fire. They all climbed out and were greeted by a select few members of the small tribal nation. They had pointed them in the proper house to go ahead and change into their traditional tribal garb. Dani was wearing her fancy shawl dancing dress that was designed to specifically tell the story of her great grandfather, six generations back from her, the man the Sioux called Morning Star and the birth of his son, the half angel Fallen Star. Quickly the tribal representatives had moved her into what now served as a lodge house. Josh and Gray started to look like they were worried about something. ?I don't like this.? Josh was the first one to voice it as the tribal representatives moved away from them and headed out of the room, in fact from the door shutting it sounded like they headed out of the building. ?No shit.? Dani was agreeing. Something passed through her, cold like a ghost. She shivered violently. ?Damnit, wish they'd wait until I'm in my grave. Where ever an immortal would be buried.? Then she heard it, a voice she had only heard in passing one time before. ?Well that is a question that only a young immortal would pose. The answer to that would be, no where if you do it right.? All three saw the man walk out from behind one of the large statues that adorned one wall. Gray dropped to one knee. Josh stuttered as he saw the man and almost fell as he did so also. Dani stood there. The man was about six feet tall, maybe a bit taller. His hair down to beyond his shoulders in a soft, silky black that moved freely. His face had no less then six piercings in it and his bare arms showed signs of tattoos that went further onto his torso then could be shown. His garb was not native. He wore a sleeveless vest that tied with a leather string at the top, almost like an old yeoman's shirt. A cloak covered his shoulders partially and fall to his back. At his throat was a clasp that showed his family crest. The family of Lord Ishram, himself. This was a man that had shown his face a few weeks, maybe months ago in GCW. He had come in for a match against a man named Moraless to settle an old family score for his brother-in-law. Most knew him as G. Ian Childress III or ?The Fallen One? Nephilim But these people in the Order of the Hammer knew him as the Keeper of Secrets. Their general, their leader. But after her last meeting with her Grandfather, Morning Star, the angel known as Kokabel, she knew him now under another name too. ?Suriel? Ian stopped. He knew that she knew about his guest that shared his body with him. Years ago he had volunteered to his own grandfather many times removed to take part in an experiment. The disembodied fallen angel, Suriel, the Wrath of God, would get a new body through him. They share a form now, allowing each to carry out their tasks in this world. ?Kokabel's daughter, it's good to see you again. And this time under different pretenses.? Dani nodded. ?What?? Josh's voice was one of confusion. And then Dani realized, she's heard those words in her ears, but they hadn't been spoken in English. Ian was speaking to her in the language of her grandfather. The one she found she understood, and could speak but didn't quite know why. ?Why are you here?? Ian, or his body smiled. She could sense them both in there. Suriel looked like a dark red aura about him in her eyes. He was an angry being, bottled up and waiting for when he'll be able to get loose and fight evil once again one day. His answer wasn't what Dani was expecting. ?Because you called me.? She had no answer to that. Her grandfather had told her his angelic name and she had said it. He had already told her that when you say an angel's true name, they will know where you are and what you have spoken about them. That was why he wouldn't tell her the name of her enemy that was looking for her. For if he or she said it, then this enemy would know where she was. ?I didn't call you, but you've come. Why did you pick here?? This he said in plain language for all to hear. ?Because we're on sacred tribal ground. And just like the first time you manifested your true form and were safe from anything else, you are so here, also. This is one place we can talk and get you the information you need.? Dani almost fell down. She was about to get the information she'd been trying to come up with since they had been in Japan. ?I'm not done with my grandfather's test. I need to figure this out on my own.? Ian stepped forward towards them, Dani could see wing like extensions coming from his back in his aura. She counted them up to six... Suriel was a seraph, a warrior angel. ?Did he tell you that?? She looked down at the ground. ?No.? He smiled a toothy smile, two fangs off to the sides of his mouth on the upper part. They looked almost fake. But they weren't. They weren't as pronounced as a vampires, but close. ?So what is wrong with asking for some help. I must say, if not for your grandfather's trickery on his part I'd not have the full details I needed. We've been monitoring the searches being done from Grayson's computer for some time trying to figure out what you were after.? At that comment, Josh's face fell. He looked up, only moving his eyes to the man who he swore an oath to, and saw that he was looking at him. Gray's face was also long, cause he knew they had been using his computer. Dani tried not to look too shocked. ?Well, I see that I've underestimated the Order. Are Josh and Gray going to get punished for allowing me access to the system?? Ian smiled. ?No, they have done nothing wrong. Although some of the other activity that has been recorded is still being looked into.? ?Thank you, can I have my information now, and we be on our way?? Ian smiled a sly smile to her. ?First things first...? Dani's shield of confidence almost faltered when looking at him. ?What is it you would wish of me, Suriel?? Saying that other name in English got looks at her from both of the Brothers Brand. ?I wish to see you in your true form...? Her jaw dropped open. She didn't answer him directly. ?I wish to see you as what you truly are. It has been a while since I have seen one of the holy ones in their true forms. Years, decades, centuries.? Dani just looked at him. She could see off in the distance, a bit off to the side a head covered in long red hair. His wife, Fey Childress was here too, the Lady that had hand picked her in her school. But she knew what would be needed to manifest right now. She would not tear her ceremonial dress. ?Josh, come help me.? Josh stood up and walked over to her. ?Help you with what?? She only looked over her shoulder to him. ?I'm not going to tear the dress that Grandma made for me. I can't have anything on my shoulder blades when I manifest my wings. Help me.? Josh's face went red. ?You're not getting...? ?It's not like anyone here will get excited but you. You know the stories. The Keeper and the Lady are bonded, an immortal bond. He does not find another other woman to be for him. She is a Tautha De Dannon like Blaise, and he is a Vampyle. But inside of him is a seraph. An angel of Heaven's army. It does me no harm to show him what I am.? Gray's face also turned red, but for a different reason. He turned his head a bit, making sure Dani wasn't in his view. Dani handed the wide belt that held the robe like dress around her to Josh and then she slid the dress backwards. Josh didn't say anything. Although this left Dani is nothing more then what she originally manifested in and what she's appeared in magazines in. That can be left for many an imagination. Josh's eyes wandered towards the Keeper who stood there, emotionless. Dani took a few steps forward, pulling away from Josh so that her wings wouldn't knock him down. ?I can't always do this yet. I wasn't born knowing how to do this.? ?You'll do just fine, young one.? Dani closed her eyes and tried to concentrate, trying to push the wings from the astral to the physical realms. Nothing. She tried again. Nothing. She tried to collect her thoughts. She wasn't worried about being almost completely naked. But as usual, when she wanted to do this, she couldn't. ?I'm sorry.? Ian smiled at her. ?No, don't be. You'll get used to it after a while.? He then held up his hand to her and Dani saw a light come forth from it and hit her in the stomach. As soon as she felt the power course through her, she felt the burning in her shoulders. The burning that happens at first, and then the heat as the astral forms become solid and lift her up onto her tippy toes a bit as they catch air. The dusk gray feathers, with their white highlights were out. But it was more then that. Dani felt something coming from inside of her. Everying looked bright, and Josh had to turn his head away from her. She felt, no saw, the aura that extended out from her. Almost like a solar corona about her. It reached out and tocuhed the man who had helped her manifest and not only did he see her for what she truly was, but she could see the duality of his forms fully. Almost as if he was standing behind the man she knew as Ian Childress was a man who looked younger, clean shaven with long blond hair, naked with the six black wings that came forth from his back. She was the first person in almost six hundred years to see Suriel for his true self, with the exception of when Mr. Childress looked in the mirror. And then a few seconds later it was done and over with. Ian Childress nodded as she came down off of her power high and walked away. ?What the hell was that?? Josh held up the dress for her to slide back into. ?I don't know, but it felt great. It was...energizing.? ?Well you lit up the whole room. Now we just need to get out of here.? She looked around as she was putting the belt back around her dress. ?Now without what was promised to me.? She looked directly at Ian as she said that, and he stopped as he was coming up to where his wife stood. He nodded to someone else and they came walking out. That person was the only other person who had seen Dani manifest before. In fact, with Josh she had been there when Dani had first sprouted her wings from her back. And now, Annie Leanan, Chief Researcher of the Order of the Hammer stepped out wearing one of her sexy, feminine cut power suits. Under one arm she carried a large manila folder. ?Annie, long time no see!? ?Aye, been a long time since I've been 'ere physically. Bu' I can assure ye, I speak with Blaise at very inopportune times on a regular basis. ? ?That's good to hear. So what do you have for me?? Annie held out the manila folder. ?Well, Sirius Star, I give you... The Evening Star.? Dani took the folder in her hands and looked upon the papers inside. The first page was a symbol, a symbol that showed to her who she was facing against when he came for her. She knew the name but even under the protection of sacred ground she didn't say his name out loud. But she now knew it and had all the information in front of her. ....Astaroth, the Fallen Archangel of Wizardry. She grinned, almost from ear to ear as she read that information. She now had the upper hand.
