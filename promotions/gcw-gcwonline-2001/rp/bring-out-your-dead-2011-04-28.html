Bring Out Your Dead

28 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/28/11 ~<>~<>~<>~ March 13th, 2011 NC-17 VIII, Backstage... He could hear the roar of the crowd, could feel the energy that rolled through Madison Square Garden like a force of nature. He had been here many times before; the venue, the adrenaline, the anticipation, the insufferable anxiety. They had become a part of who he was, had nourished him throughout his career like mother's milk to a dying man. A dead man. Rich "The Renegade" Rollins smiled in the dark, appreciating the sweet irony of it all. Memories began to bubble up; old faces swam in the shadows, bringing with them a slew of names he could never forget. Names like "Blockbuster" Kurt Richards, Vincent Reed, Khan. Octavian's debonair demeanor and impossibly black hair. Rollins could remember winning his first GCW World Heavyweight Championship under the hallowed dome of the Roman Coliseum, once upon a time. Main eventing the inaugural Dangerous Games, only to lose that same belt to Jhubin. Hell, before WorldWide there was Meltdown, and on the premier episode--the very first show in GCW history--it was Rich Rollins who beat Octavian, Khan and Gigas in a Double Jeopardy main event. The Renegade remembered a time when a reference to Shane Lane was considered edgy, or at the very least relevant. He then spent the rest of his career redefining the meanings of those words. His name had become a household phenomenon, glorified and vilified by the very same tongues which now screamed for their pound of flesh on the other side of these bare concrete walls. Just listening to their overwhelming enthusiasm lit a fire in Rollins' belly, warming a part of his soul that had been cold for so very long. He absorbed it like sunlight inside this dark room, hidden away from a world that thought him no more than a ghost; a specter of the past long left behind, but never truly forgotten. Nobody had seen him enter the building, but that had more to do with luck than his makeshift "disguise." Dressed in the uniform black GCW-brand tee of a lighting technician, Rich Rollins couldn't have stood out more than if he had just worn the damn bandana. Standing a few inches taller than the other techs and bursting out of his shirt in ways that would make Richard Simmons blush, Rollins had figured the jig would be up long before now. But nobody noticed and nobody cared when he took his tote bag into an empty closet near the backstage gorilla position and locked the door behind him. This was five hours ago, before the stands had been filled with rabid wrestling fans. Now they were everywhere, shaking the very walls with their passion and provocation. When he heard the unmistakable opening to "Established," Rich Rollins knew it was time. The Renegade took a few deep breaths to gather himself, unlocked the door, and stepped out into the world of the living. A frenzy of activity usually marked the area behind the MegaTron as people ran back and forth, setting up pyrotechnics or readjusting lights. Wrestlers usually littered the hallways, rehearsing promos or stretching against walls. Tonight was no different; for the biggest show of the year, everyone knew they had job to perform and went about doing it with professional precision. Rollins walked along as if nothing were out of the ordinary, just another bee making his way through the hive, and for about a minute nobody noticed. It didn't last long. Theo Palmer almost choked on his bottled water as the Renegade walked by, eyes the size of basket balls. A technician with his arms full of cable wiring did a double-take right before crashing into the wall he forgot to avoid, dropping his payload and getting tangled in it. Heads started popping out of doors as a murmur began to build, buzzing through the air like a current of controversy that swept past Rollins and into every room within earshot. The Renegade remained calm, grinning smugly to himself as the commotion began to build. A crowd gathered in Rollins' wake. Nobody knew what to say, or how to say it. Either the ghost of GCW's past had just materialized out of thin air, or someone was playing an awful joke. From the look on every face he passed, Rich Rollins couldn't decide which he would rather they believed. He just wanted to make it to the stage without incident, but as soon as he saw LeStatt Knight standing near the prep tables behind the MegaTron, Rollins knew that just wasn't going to happen. LeStatt Knight was the picture of shock and confusion as the Renegade waltzed up to him. Shooting a sly wink, Rollins slapped him on the shoulder. Knight flinched, unable to process the things his eyes were telling his brain. LeStatt's expression forced a chuckle out of Rollins as he said, "How's it hangin' Roberto? It's been a long time, brother." What happened next rocked LeStatt Knight to his very core. Rollins wrapped his arms around the fellow Hall of Famer and Grand Slam Champion, locking the large Mexican into the most manly man-hug ever hugged since the Dawn of Man. "Thanks for keeping my spirit alive," Rollins said softly. "I appreciate that, more than you'll ever know. You're a goddamn icon, Rob." The Renegade released the thunderstruck LeStatt and brushed past him, making a beeline for the stage curtain. Knight followed him with his eyes, mouth hanging open in wordless wonder. A few moments later he blinked and turned to the nearest technician. "Did... Rich Rollins... just hug me?" ~<>~<>~<>~ Two days ago A familiar derelict warehouse, South Chicago... Inhaling the musty air with a smile on his face, Rich Rollins sank back into the years-old pile of once-clean laundry that he never got around to putting away and sighed. His old home--his haunt, Rollins thought with a snort--had been empty for a long time. The hanging lights in the main storage hanger cried with relief when he pulled their cords, tears that fell in great globs of dust that broke into powder once they hit the ground. Countless boxes full of innumerable memories were stacked at haphazard angles all over the place, some covered with dusty gray tarps that were supposed to be blue. The old hot tub he had purchased during GTT5 still sat off to one side, now more a monument to loneliness than to luxury. The damaged corner of his storage space was still there too, lurking in the back like a half-forgotten nightmare. The walls were still coated with soot, and a dark stain that the Renegade made every effort not to think about still stuck to the floor. He hadn't been back here since The Incident, and he hadn't yet summoned the strength to clean it up. Ever since his return, people were always quick with a ghost-related joke or three. They had no goddamn idea what a real ghost was, not one of them. Rollins tried not to care; for better or worse, he was home. That was all that mattered. Rich Rollins was a man of few creature comforts, but many vices. He succumbed to one now as he got up and walked across his makeshift office-bedroom to the mini fridge that sat next to his computer-less computer desk. Swinging the door open, he was pleased to find the old bottle of whiskey he had left there long ago and snatched it off its shelf. Kicking the door closed on his way back to the stacked mattresses he called a bed, Rollins unscrewed the cap and tossed it aside. His lips touched the mouth of the bottle just as his phone began buzzing in his pocket. Annoyed, the Renegade dug in his jeans until he wrangled the device into the open and looked at the screen: YOU HAVE 26 NEW MESSAGES Chuckling to himself, Rollins took a drag from the bottle and smacked his lips when the bite hit his stomach. Flopping onto the musty mattresses, the Renegade sat the whiskey bottle on his night stand and settled in. He loved sifting through hate mail. As expected, they were all from Steven Caldera. April 15th, 2011 (12:43 PM) Answer my fucking calls. April 15th, 2011 (3:27 PM) You can't ignore me forever, asshole. April 16th, 2011 (9:52 AM) I've made an appointment for you to come and record some intros for the matches on your DVD. Airline tickets have been arranged, which you'd know if you bothered to checked your goddamn email every once in a while. Flight leaves tomorrow night after WorldWide goes off the air. Be on it. April 17th, 2011 (11:38 PM) You missed your flight. Why am I not surprised. ANSWER YOUR PHONE. April 18th, 2011 (8:45 AM) Garbage Bag Johnny is in the hospital with lacerations all over because of last night's clusterfuck. He's insisting on making YOU active Commissioner for next week's show. Goddamn you. If I didn't have to be in LA for a meeting with the film branch I would do it myself. Instead, I have to explain this "Hail to the Chimp" business to our stock holders. I'm going to kill Triumph, but if he's anything like you he'd just come back and ruin my life anyway. April 19th, 2011 (2:17 PM) I hate you. The Renegade smiled. It was good to be the Chief. ~<>~<>~<>~ March 13th, 2011 NC-17 VIII, Aftermath... Excited and weary from the emotional events that had just taken place at Madison Square Garden, the Renegade slapped some water at his eyes and looked into the bathroom mirror. The face looking back was the same as ever; long greasy hair under a sweat-soaked bandana, craggy skin pockmarked from adolescent acne and years of smoking, and a prickly jaw that hadn't made a regular appointment with a razor in months. But now, after all that had just transpired, Rich Rollins noticed a new twinkle in those cold blue eyes. There was a spark of something there, something he hadn't seen in a very long time. Passion. Quite pleased with himself, Rollins wiped his face with a towel and withdrew to the main area of the private locker room. LeStatt Knight had long since departed, but he didn't mind when Rollins had asked for a place to wash up. They shared a powerful respect, and for that Rich Rollins was grateful. He was just about to get ready for a shower when the door slammed open, bouncing off the wall and rebounding into Steven Caldera's waiting palm. "You cocksucker," seethed Caldera, entering the room with a pointed slam of the door behind him. "You unimaginable cocksucker." Raising his eyebrows in innocence, the Renegade tossed the towel around his neck with a shrug. "Hi, Steven." His brow quickly fell along with his tone. "Bye, Steven." Caldera cut Rollins off before the Renegade could turn around and head for the shower. "Oh no you don't, you fucking lying piece of shit. You don't get to just come back and pretend like the last three years never happened. We thought you were dead, Richard. You owe me an explanation." Steven's eyes shone with outrage, magnified still by Rollins' indifferent attitude. The Renegade shook his head and leaned over the back of an expensive looking leather couch. "I don't owe you shit, pal. Do I look like a motherfucking information kiosk to you? 'You are here',"said Rollins, flipping a meaningful middle finger in Caldera's direction. "Now get the fuck out." "No." The word was sharp, defiant, and just a touch indignant. Caldera folded his arms and stood his ground. "I'm not leaving this room until you explain to me how I saw you in a box three years ago. I watched them close your casket and stick you in the ground, for the love of God! How is that possible, Rollins? How?" A dark cloud passed over Rollins' face. His eyes seemed to flicker back a step, gazing into the kinds of places of which Caldera could only guess. They stood there for a few moments, letting the silence and the Renegade's sudden stupor build until Caldera was fit to explode. Just as the boss was about to launch into another testy tirade, Rollins snapped back to the present and captured Caldera with the most intense glare the GCW owner had ever seen out of him, and that was saying something. "We've both had long nights, Steven. Your jaw probably hurts something awful, seeing as how it ain't been kicked properly in a good long time." Rollins' calm and deliberate tone sent a chill down Caldera's spine. "I'm gonna go inside that room over there and take a shower. When I come out, if you're still here, I'm going to punch you in the face. If you'd like to continue chewing solid food this week, I suggest you take this opportunity to kindly fuck off." And with that, Rich Rollins whipped the towel off his neck and slung it over one shoulder, shooting Steven Caldera one last dissuading scowl as he turned on his heels and disappeared into the adjourning room. Caldera let out a frustrated sigh and bowed his head with his proverbial dick in his hands. He debated on how best to proceed as the sound of running water filled the next room. He paced for a few minutes, walking literal circles around the leather couch as steam began to snake in from the showers. Fifteen minutes passed. Rich Rollins dried himself off. He took his time, wringing out wet hair that hadn't seen shampoo in weeks. He carefully reapplied his bandana, folding it lengthwise like a headband, and wrapped a towel around his waist. With an approving nod at himself in the mirror, the Renegade squared his shoulders and stepped back into the lounge area. Steven Caldera was nowhere to be seen. Rollins was more than a little disappointed. Instead, he found a note on top of the leather couch: I'm a Caldera, not a McMahon. I made you Rollins, and I'll break you just as easily. Again. The Renegade's lip slowly curled in a one-sided sneer. Just like old times. ~Fin~
