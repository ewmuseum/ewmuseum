SoCal Boo-boos

10 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/10/11 Previously on "One life to Cruz". . . At World Wide 142, Bradley battles against a badly painted behemoth gaining the upper hand. He sets up a table and a few chairs looking for a big finish, but as Cruz went for a hurricanrana he was powerbombed through the tables and chairs ending the match. This was promptly followed by a second powerbomb for good measure leaving a broken a battered Cruz lying in the ring. A few hours after GCW World Wide 142 Physically Bradley is in shambles as he struggles to walk between his two lackeys the Dogtown Saints, Eko and Slider. They each lend their mentor a shoulder supporting his weight as he struggles through the massive pain in his back. They work their way through the hall. Eko: "Don’t worry; we’re almost to your dressing room." Slider: "Man Cuz, that guy really worked you over." Brad: "Oh shut up, and don’t Cuz me." Slider: "… but we’re cousins." Brad: "Don’t remind me." Slider: "It really hurts when you say things like that." Eko: "Pretty cold, man." "Cold indeed." The trio turns to glance behind them seeing Sally Ford with a smug little smile on her lips. Seeing her Brad jumps to his feet as if he were just fine. He fakes a smile as he screams in pain on the inside. Sally: "…so what’s new, Brad?" Brad: "W-" Sally: "-I already know what happened, Brad. I came for one thing and one thing only. Can you guess what that is?" Eko: "Warm condolences?" Sally: "No." Slider: "Literary crutch? The divine writer has been afraid to take any risks." (Saints, Prophets, and Writers?) Eko elbows Slider. Eko: "Idiot." Sally: "Uh no. I am here to gloat." Eko: "Let down." Slider: "Definitely not worth a whole guessing game." Sally: "You opened your big mouth and called out the best that GCW had to offer and Bryan Mayhem was man enough to answer and creamed your corn!" Eko: "Creamed your corn?" Slider: "Folksy much?" Sally: "You know Brad there’s a lesson to be learned about picking on much larger tatted up borderline homicidal monsters in face paint. I guess you’ll think twice before crossing Bryan Mayhem again." Bradley simply shakes his head. Brad: "Oh please, screw that guy! I had him beat and he knows it, apparently the only thing greater than that joker’s worthlessness is his stupid dumb luck, so congrats to him for banana peeling himself to a successful title defense. He should celebrate by throwing on a movie like ‘The Crow’ on a big screen, while chewing glass, and cracking open the old Avon case for a makeover. Maybe lose that whole deranged mime look and try for something different like ‘the Blueman Group’ or ‘Mr. Mistoffelees’ from ‘Cats’." Sally raises an eyebrow. Sally: "You saw ‘Cats’?" Slider: "Aunty Cruz took three times when we visited New York for a summer." Brad: "Would you stop calling her Aunty? You’re twenty three years old for Christ’s sake!" Slider sheepishly diverts his line of sight to a random corner taking him out of the conversation. Brad’s attention turns back to Sally. Sally: "Well Brad it was nice seeing you again… well, whatever pieces were left of you anyways, tootles… oh, and good luck against Hot Mask and Jimmy Thunder." Bradley raises an eyebrow as his nostrils flare with intrigue and confusion. Brad: "Hot what and Jimmy who?" Bradley looks to Eko then Slider. Brad: "Double times boys." The trio quickly work their way to the dressing room. They quickly open the door as Eko detaches from the group. Brad: "Eko confirm!" Eko sprints towards the luggage and fishes out his IPad. In seconds he’s on the DOTNET! Slider: "Let's get you a chair, Cuz." Slider lugs Brad towards a chair in the corner, but Bradley throws his foot out kicking the chair away. Brad: "No more chairs! For tonight I’m done with chairs! Tables to!" Slider: "Poor Cuz, I hope you don’t develop a phobia… a chairapobia… what’s the Latin for chair?" Brad: "Will you just get me too the Sofa?" Slider lugs Brad closer to the sofa. Brad grabs on to the ottoman which he uses to pull himself into the seat. Slider: "Careful." Bradley pulls himself into the seat and he lays back giving his poor back a rest. Brad: "Don’t worry an ottoman can’t hurt me, I’m not Dick Van Dyke. What’s the word Eko?" Eko: "She was right, triple threat match at World Wide 143, Brad Cruz vs. Hot Mask vs. Jimmy Thunder." Brad: "Hot Mask and Thunder, sounds like random noun salad. Seriously how do you get a name like Hot Mask? Did he use a boggle set and just kept shaking till something completely broken came up? I like how there’s so much creativity in the world and this guy just used none of it." Eko hands him the IPad and Cruz looks through his opponent’s profiles with some intrigue. Brad: "…and this Jimmy Thunder is perfectly named, because he’s just a bunch of loud noise. First off he should have retired but instead he plans to ride what is left of his shambled career, until it needs to be shot like a lame horse pressed one mile too many, but if he really wants to give it another go… let him. He can bring the thunder, he the lightning, hell he can bring an entire hurricane. It’s still not going to make up for the fact that he just can’t hack it anymore." Eko: "so you’re not worried?" Cruz smiles. Brad: "Shouldn’t be a problem. Call Stephania, my back is killing me."
