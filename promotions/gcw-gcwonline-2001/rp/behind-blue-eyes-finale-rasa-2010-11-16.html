Behind Blue Eyes (Finale: Rasa)

16 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16/10 I always hear about how easy I had it growing up in Highland Park, Illinois. Rich suburb of Chicago. Home of the greatest basketball player that's ever lived, Michael Jordan. I grew up in wealth. I had lots of money, rich parents who'd give me well... almost everything I've ever wanted. That makes me privileged, I've been told. That because of my background I expect things like... GCW World Heavyweight Championship shots to come to me. At least that's what the wrestling media says. Do you know what it's like to grow up as the daughter of a world famous defense lawyer and one of Chicago's most public figures? Do you understand the pressure and expectations that are put on you at such a young age? Do you understand exactly what it's like to go into school at seven years old and have a teacher tell you that you'll never meet the success that your father has had? That you'll never break out of his shadow, so that you shouldn't even try? Imagine having that conversation with your mother, explaining to one the biggest charity fundraiser in the city of Chicago, that you don't want to grow up and be a lawyer... or a doctor... or a actress. Instead you want to throw it all away to follow in the glorious life of professional wrestling. Bussing tables during the day and getting my ass kicked all night long just to do it again the next day. Bleeding in a bingo hall for $200 that you might not even get. My mother was so pleased when Christian Zenith came and "rescued me" from my hopes and dreams before they ever started. Whisked away to live another life of luxury. Again I never had to lift a finger. Again living in someone else’s shadow. Again "expecting" things to be handed to me. But I've never been one to shy away from exposure. I've never been the type of girl to runaway or hide from anything at all, including my own criticisms. Headstrong is the way I've always lived my life, so why can't I just answer the phone... ------------------------------~*~------------------------------ RIIIIINNNNNNG. The electric cackle of the cordless phone was enough to make her stomach turn. As she looked down at the caller ID, it was even worse. The Las Vegas area code was the last thing she wanted to see. Taking a deep breath she knew what she had to do, she had to answer that phone. She had to make things right but... she froze. Reaching her arm out it just stopped, leaving her to watch helplessly as the voice mail picked up once again. "Hello, please leave a message after the tone..." BEEP. "Hey Vee, this is Jupiter, again..." Guilt quickly forced her eyes shut. "I don't know what's been going on lately but you have been... impossible to get a hold of. There is something I need to talk to you about, and it's not something I can do over the phone or over an answering machine. I need to do it in person. So please, when you get a chance just get a hold of me... it's not urgent but it's eating me up inside. I need to get it off my chest. I'm sure you understand." "You have no idea..." Vivica J. Valentine mumbled under her breath just before a click signified the end of the phone call. She didn't know if it was out of shame or fear of getting recognized, but every morning when she picked up her cell phone and saw a missed call from Jupiter, she wondered why she even bothered in the first place. Why did she lie? Why didn't she just tell the girl who she was or what she was doing there. As someone who once went to schools on a semi-regular basis as World Champion preaching the importance of honesty and communication, she was learning her own life lessons first hand. No matter how bad she'd wanted to, she knew she couldn't avoid the girl forever. There was just something about Jupiter that was so charismatic, so energetic, so pure. Something drew her to the rock starlet the first time she made eye contact with her. Perhaps it was because they had so much in common, or that they were into the same things but it seemed like so much more. Deep down inside, perhaps it was that Jupiter Skye had all that Vivica ever wanted in life. A small group of friends, a creative outlet, fame and fortune on a much smaller scale. Picking and choosing when to live a public life and when to blend into the shadows. The power to be a God by night and a college student by day. Just the thought of freedom brought a smile back to Vivica's guilt-ridden expression. That is until her eyes moved back to the computer screen, because in front of her laid the epitome of her feelings. A wrestling dirt sheet. No matter how bad she wanted to look away, she was an addict. Knowing that the world's opinion was at her fingertips, she just had to know what everyone thought, how everything went wrong, what kind of bullshit rumors people were trying to pin on her today. Every single bout with the internet wrestling community seemed to end in the same way. Her getting so pissed off at the comments that she's on the edge of throwing away her straight-edge lifestyle to go chug a bottle of whiskey, realizing how terrible of an idea that is, and then storming out of the room in a fit a rage to go someplace far away to get a breath of fresh air. Tonight was no different. If anything she'd become a creature of habit as she clicked the link on the forum adoringly titled "Why Vivica J. Valentine's Time Is Over." "I can't possibly be the only person sick and tired of Valentine's bitching," the post from Terrorable2471 read, "she doesn't even deserve the United States Championship, not to mention the World Title. I suppose when you grow up spoiled you may be used to crying until you get whatever you want, but in real life you have to work for rewards. Having a good performance here and there a champion does not make and if it wasn't for a fluke Dangerous Games win over LeStatt Knight, she wouldn't even have an accomplishment to hang her hat on. So from now on every time Vivica J. Valentine shows up on my television screen, I'd like to ask everyone in joining my in turning the channel. It's time we show Caldera Enterprises we want more wrestling and less hormonal bitching. If I wanted to listen to that shit, I'd watch Lifetime." The upper lip of The Bulletproof Blonde curled up as fingers furiously tapped the keyboard. "Are you fucking retarded?" She began typing out, "I'm not sure if you've noticed or not but people don't get fluke wins over LeStatt Knight, plus the girl is obviously talented. Have you ever tried to put yourself in her shoes? Imagine..." At that very point she paused and realized the absurdity of the situation she found herself in. Arguing with what was very well a fifteen year old boy over the internet about the merit and true worth of her GCW career. Holding down the backspace button she quickly wiped the type box clear. How could anyone be so blind to the blood she's spilt over the years? To the hard word and dedication she's put in, and the thousands of times she's almost killed herself to put a smile on their faces only for them to treat her like garbage. "I need to get out of here." She spoke to herself like a maniac before pushing the chair back and climbing to her feet. A deep breath filled her lungs as she closed her eyes, reaching out for a black leather bomber jacket on the door. As she slid her arm through, she felt like she could scream about how unfair it was that everyone could just sit behind their computers and take shots at her, not knowing a damn thing about what it was like for her growing up. They just acted like they knew her, because they saw her face on the television every week. It seemed like the entire population of the planet was so... ignorant. As she grabbed her purse from off the doorknob, something startled her once again. RIIIIINNNNNNG. Again it was Jupiter Skye. She flipped off the light and closed the door behind her, leaving the phone to fall upon the ears of no one... but that was really no different than usual, was it? ------------------------------~*~------------------------------ So I've dabbled in being a hypocrite, but I'm only human. I'm flawed. I've been a bit of a bitch. I'm going to make mistakes. We all do. There are times I am going to do things that I look back at and I'm not proud of but it's not that I don't care at all... ------------------------------~*~------------------------------ You treat me just like another stranger Well it's nice to meet you sir I guess I'll go, I best be on my way ou... "Yeah?" With the flip of the phone and an aggravated growl, the ringtone stopped and left her with only the sounds of the road. There was something so soothing about the sound of rubber speeding along the pavement, is almost made her forget why she was so aggravated about the phone call to begin with. "Where are you? It's six in the morning." A chilling monotone voice crackled through the receiver, there was always terrible reception up along the lake for whatever reason. At this moment it wasn't the reception that aggravated her, but the person on the other side. "What are you now, Christian... my dad?" Her wit at the crack of dawn was second to none, mainly because she was very likely the only sane person who was up at this psychotic hour. At least, that's what she'd like to tell herself. "No, I'm a concerned investor." The lack of fun just oozed through his vocal chords, "You see Vivica, you don't just live with me anymore, you and I... we have a business contract. You wanted protection from me, which is what I'm trying to provide you with right now and you..." "And I'm supposed to do your bitch work, right?" She quipped as she came to a halt at a stop light. Looking over the lake reminded her of a much happier time when she was a child visiting Navy Pier with her parents, "Be less like me, be more like Rasa?" "I certainly think you could learn a thing or two from Rasa." "Then call Rasa." CLICK. With those words she dropped her phone back down into the cupholder, just like the three times before it. Every ten minutes he had something new to say... some "enlightening" little piece of information that he was absolutely sure would make her turn around and come back to the manor, yet none of them worked. Unfortunately for Christian Zenith, taming Vivica J. Valentine was a little bit more complicated than that of Rasa... or a household pet. She was what most people would call a free spirit, marching to the beat of her own drum. She'd left the Manor at ten and had been on the road ever since. Sometimes, lately more often than normal, she just had to get away from it all. With the business she was in, a drive all night was the closest thing she'd ever had to a vacation. Plus, she had a lot on her mind, Rasa being one of the various things spinning in her head. It was fascinating to her how readily she was expected to just trust a monster that didn't even have a face. Rasa lacked a soul. Rasa lacked a conscience. Rasa lacked anything that made it well... human. How could you trust something you can't relate to? How could you ever count on something you didn't even know? She was an atheist at heart, but at least Jesus had a last name and a gender. You treat me just lik... "This isn't Rasa, I think you have the wrong number." "Can you stop being a bitch for just long enough to hear me out?" Christian's usually monotone voice this time scratched with emotion. The only emotion you could seem to muster out of The Leader Of Men. Anger. "You don't have long." She stepped down on the gad pedal, moving her forward. "Viv, will you please come back to the manor? I'm a very busy man. You know this. I have lots of work to do and I can't be up all night trying to talk you off a ledge." "You think I'm trying to kill myself?" "No, I think you're much more likely to kill me." Christian responded candidly, "You've been leaving all night and sleeping all day. You've been showing up to shows late because you can't get yourself back on schedule and honestly, I believe it's hurting your performance in the ring. Dangerous Games is this weekend. I can't have you off ruining our chances..." "OUR chances?" Vivica spoke over him to make a point, he's lucky she didn't toss the phone out of the driver's side window. She wanted to. "YOUR chances of doing something big here." Christian unwillingly corrected himself, "You're the United States Champion, you could win Dangerous Games. You could be both. You're mailing it in Vivica." "Again, Chris, I'm sorry I've never been perfect." "I've never asked you to be perfect, I'm just asking you to stop running away from your responsibility." "Perhaps you'd rather I hide from it behind a mask?" "Rasa's here. You're not. To me, that speaks volumes." "Rasa is... there? What do you mean there?" A long pause separated Valentine's question and Christian's response. "I mean here, inside the manor." "He's there?" "It's here, yes." Christian spoke, "Dangerous Games is in Chicago, I saw no reason to put it up for the night someplace else. I have plenty of facilities here." "Isn't he dangerous? I mean is it saf..." "It's fine." The forked tongue flickered behind chapped lips, "Just trust me on this one." "I don't like masks." Vivica confessed, passing a seasonal Halloween store on the corner, being prepped for the upcoming Christmas season to sell trees. "I can't trust a mask." "Well you're going to have to learn." Christian replied with a stern growl in his voice. That sat even heavier in the pit of her stomach. ------------------------------~*~------------------------------ ...it's that I care too much. I don't get a free pass like everyone else because of who I was, or am. I don't even know who in the fuck I am anymore. What am I doing? How did I ever get here? Was it the World Heavyweight Championship that did this to me? What in the hell have I done... ------------------------------~*~------------------------------ You would think with the twelve phone calls that there would be an escort, trumpets, and fanfare awaiting at the door when she arrived. Fortunately for the poor bastards who would've been stuck dealing with a very cranky Vivica J. Valentine, there was not. As a matter of fact it was calm, eerily calm as sun begin to rise behind her. Sliding her keys into her pocket she couldn't help but look all around her. Ever since Christian had told her that Rasa was in the manor, she felt on edge. Her defense mechanisms were on high alert. Above all else, she had to know the truth. Pulling the massive, cherry wood door towards her, Zenith Manor greeted her with the usual squeak followed by the sweet smell of sanitizer. Christian was so particular about every little detail, it all had to be perfect. Poor Jacques was forced measure distances picture frames were apart on the walls, and how many inches away clocks were from the edges of inn tables. She couldn't believe the circumstance in which he'd let a house pet run wild. But perhaps that's exactly the point. He wouldn't. "Welcome home, Miss Valentine." Jacques warm, glowing voice tried to soften the temperament of the Bulletproof Blonde, but it was useless. " Would you like help with anyt..." "Where is he?" Vivica asked immediately, as she tossed her purse into a nearby closet. "Christian has gone downtown for a busine..." "Rasa." She cut him off, hung a left and quickened her pace down the hallway, making a b-line for the guest quarters. Jacques dropped his tape measure and made chase after the petite blonde. Her cerulean blue eyes fixated on the door up ahead as Jacques stumbled to keep up with the much faster and younger woman. "I'm sorry, Rasa isn't available right no..." "I don't care if Rasa is available, where is he?" The frantic pace quickened, the anxiety in Jacques' voice tells Vivica everything she needed to know. She was on the right path. "By 'he' I'm guess you mean 'it'." "I don't give a damn about Chris' gender game, I just need to give 'it' a face." "Miss Valentine, now really isn't a good tim..." "What is it? Some kind of werewolf? A patchwork Frankenstein monster? Halloween is over, Jacques. What can possibly go wrong?" Jacques clinched his jaw as he began to jog, now it being his only hope to keep up. He was, after all, the last line of defense between Vivica J. Valentine and Christian Zenith's biggest secret. This was the type of thing he could lose his job over. Even if he did love Vivica like a daughter, he needed to eat. He needed a place to live. Without this job he literally had nothing. "Miss Valentine!" Jacques jumped out and shoved the door shut just as Vivica opened it. He instantly squeezed himself between Valentine and the door, cutting off any chance she had of losing him down the hallway. "You need to listen to me." "Jacques, please." Vivica tried to reason with the only friend she had in this hell hole, hoping dearly to play up his sympathies. "Absolutely not." Jacques stood his ground, "Anyone other than Christian himself is forbidden from entering the guest quarters until after Dangerous Games." "Jacques..." "Are my instructions clear, Miss Valentine?" "No, Jacques..." "Were my instructions CLEAR, Miss Valentine?" "What's so fucking special about this guy, anyway?!" She lashed out, "He wears a fucking mask, big deal! I'm sick and tired of being compared to this freak!" "Miss Valentine, I'm sure it can hear you..." "Jacques, 'it' has a mask, that means it doesn't have feelings." A mocking tone accompanied her banter, "It's supposed to be some kind of merciless, violent killing machine right? It's everything that I'm supposed to be in Chris' eyes. Rasa this. Rasa that. You know what, Jacques? FUCK RASA." "Vivica just calm down..." The professional tone of Jacques quickly went to that of a father figure as he saw the change in Valentine's eyes. The beautiful hue of cerulean blue hid the raging fire that burned behind them, but Jacques could see right through. "I'm not calming down until you let me through that door." "Vivica, I can't do that." "Let me in." "I can't." "Let me in." Vivica reached out and grabbed Jacques in an attempt to pull him out of the way, but the loyal employee stood his ground, desperately latched on to the door handle as she tried to break his grasp. "No. No. Viv..." Before he could even finish his sentence a right hook sent him dropping to the floor. The shoulders of the Bulletproof Blonde rose up and down dramatically and a snarl crossed her lips. Blood began to drip out of Jacques' nose and onto the floor as she paused, looking down at the man she'd often called the father she never had. There was no time for feelings, only time for action as she felt the cold brass handle against the palm of her hand and pulled he door open. Rasa's suit was laid out neatly on the bed as she slowly slid her way in. The door closed gently behind her, not making a peep as she couldn't help by stare into the mask at the foot of the bed. Cold, heartless, cowardly... it was no different than the masks before. A facade to clear them from the consequences of their own actions. From behind fake eyes they viewed a world that didn't view them back. An escape from reality. In a way, she was jealous. Picking up the mask, she held it in her own hands. She was at a loss for words as she felt the fabric between her fingertips. This piece of cloth was the only thing between man and beast. And after today it would be gone. Clinching it in her fist, she turned around to make her exit... only to be stopped by the masked monster themselves. What stood in front of Vivica J. Valentine wasn't a ruthless killing machine at all but a human being wearing nothing but a towel. They showed no sign of emotion, just a glare as the masked dropped down out of Vivica's fist. Her eyes grew wide. Her jaw dropped. Her throat tightened. All she could manage to do was choke out three words... "Dear God, no..." ------------------------------~*~------------------------------ Sometimes reality isn't pretty and you're forced to see things that you never wanted to see, but at the end of the day sometimes it's exactly what you need. I've seen the consequences, and the face of my decisions isn't what I want to become. Under that mask wasn't the face of a heartless monster, under that mask was... It was... It was me. The road I walk is no longer shining and paved with gold... it's a cold, dark, and shattered hell. I'm the one who has created my own nightmares. My mistakes have sealed my fate, but I've gone down the road too far to turn back now. I have to keep going, because my dreams aren't as empty as my conscience seems to be. In my dreams I'm alive again, so my dreams are all I have left. So as I look out into the Chicago sky, the only place I've ever called home, I can't help but think this year is going to be different. This year, Dangerous Games is the story of a girl with one last chance to change her destiny. One last leap of faith to make things right again. I have to win this time. I can't leave my fate up to chance. This time I have to win the World Title at NC-17, because I can't afford to fall again. God, save the souls of those who stand in my way.
