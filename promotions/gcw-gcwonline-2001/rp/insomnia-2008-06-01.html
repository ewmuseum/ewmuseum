Insomnia

1 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/01/08 Friday, May 24, 2008 Somewhere in Las Vegas, there is a normal looking house inhabited by a man. The man is sitting at his computer wearing a black “Rob Zombie’s Halloween” T-shirt, pajama pants, and socks. His name is James von Doom, the world renowned “Violator” of any show he appears on. He is me, as a matter of fact. And I’m supposed to be doing something. I know I should be getting ready for my match. I really should but instead I'm too busy playing Bloody Pingu on an online wrestling forum and watching the Lakers playing the Spurs. Yeah, I have been accused of being scatterbrained in the past but this is a bit absurd. I blame it on the fact that I am so far superior to my opponent this week that I don't even have to get ready. It's going to be an easy week for me. I can just sit and fuck around watching Kobe make Tim Duncan and Bruce Bowen look like bitches on the television instead of actually getting ready for my match. What a wonderful week this is indeed. Just then, there is a knock at the front door interrupting my peace and quiet. "What the fuck is it now?" I mumble as I get up out of my computer chair and head for the door. It’s been a hectic week, so I won’t be surprised by anything that happens now. I unlock the door lock and open the door to find a messenger with a package standing in front of me. “Package for you, sir,” the messenger says handing me a small square envelope. “Sign here please.” He then hands me a clipboard with a paper attached to it and a pen. I quickly sign the paper and hand the messenger back the clipboard. He then looks at it and turns around to head back to his truck. I go back inside and close the door locking it behind me. I then open the envelope and pull out a letter. “What the fuck is this?” I mumble to myself as I open up the letter and begin to read it over. Mr. Von Doom, We here at GCW are sad to inform you that the Church of the Unholy’s contracts have not been renewed due to lack of interest and a lame gimmick to boot. You are now relieved of your contract here and are free to explore other places to compete. We sincerely hope you have a good career and we wish you well on your future endeavors. Sincerely, GCW Management I look up from the paper and stare at the wall for the longest time. I blink my eyes repeatedly, trying to cope with everything all at once. Then a smile creeps across my face. I’m finally free. Free to be my own man again. And, most importantly, free of the Church of the Unholy trying to drag me down with them and all their stupidity. Today is a good day indeed. * * * Saturday, May 25, 2008 3 a.m. Blood... Gore... Mutilation... These things fill the nightmares that keep me awake at night. I haven't slept well since before I joined the Church of the Unholy. This is seeming more and more like a mistake everyday. And the insomnia isn't helping matter out much either. Of course this may also have to do with the fact that all I watch anymore is horror movies. Last night I watched this really insane one called Dr. Giggles. It was funny as fuck. Everything that doctor said was absolutely hilarious. It reminded me so much of me in so many ways. Except for the murderous tendencies and demented giggling, that is. I did get the thought in my brain to possibly get on a movie roleplaying forum and write Dr. Giggles fanfics but that would just be absurd. Yet, I strangely feel compelled to do so. Maybe when I have some more time and start getting some sleep at night. However, I also did happen to watch Salem's Lot and that movie always scares the shit out of me, especially when the Nosferatu appears in the jail. That part always freaked the shit out of me, even when I first saw it as a young child. But I haven’t been sleeping well at all here lately. Haven’t been able to go to sleep for hours and have gotten little rest at all. I’ve probably been driving my girlfriend nuts because of my behavior since she is more of a morning person. She probably doesn’t give a fuck anyway. She’s probably just another one of these ringrats that’s only with me because I’m a famous wrestler. But I don’t need to think about that now. I just need to get some sleep. * * * James von Doom’s Journal 25 May.- I haven’t slept for days. Everyday is feeling the same as the last. Days upon days blended together in a symphony of destruction. Maybe it’s the stress of my wrestling schedule. Maybe it has something to do with those pills I stopped taking that the Church of the Unholy handed to all of its members at Church meetings. According to what I overheard the mysterious “leader” of the group telling Axel, I was the only one who didn’t become fully “brainwashed” by their pills and religious conversion tactics. That apparently made me dangerous to them. So who knows if those idiots are going to try and kill me or something. But at least I am rid of them. I have a caring girlfriend for once. Most of my girlfriends are partially psychotic, MILFs, stalkers, strippers, drug fiends, or mildly retarded idiots, but this one seems nice. She’s a little too into animals, but that’s about the only thing I’ve found to be a little off with her. She’s a good fit for me and I hope it works out. This lack of sleep is killing me and now I have to look for a job elsewhere. I have plenty of options. There’s always SCCW I can go back to, but I need someplace new. I’ve competed just about everywhere but there is one place I have never been to and that’s PRIME. My buddies the Killer Pirates were there for a brief moment in time back in the day, but I myself have never been there. Should be a pretty exciting experience if I do say so myself. I’ll have to call my agent and see if he can make it happen. But, until then, I need to get some God damn sleep. * * * 1 p.m. I’m bored out of my mind from all this daywalking I’ve been doing. I feel like a Ginger Kid from South Park or something. This is not good. So I’ve decided to watch a horror movie. It’s a classic one. It’s called Dr. Giggles. Just watched it yesterday but I can’t stop laughing every time I think about it so I just have to see it again. All of my friends know about it because I can’t shut up about it. It’s hilarious. I’m crouched down trying to get the DVD to go in but the stupid thing won’t go in. This is the third time I’ve tried to get it to work but the stupid piece of crap DVD player I bought keeps ejecting it because I put it in the wrong way or some crap like that. “Why the hell isn’t this DVD going into the fucking player?” I growl out as I try jamming the DVD into the player and push it in so it’ll start up. My girlfriend walks into the room at this point. She is a short little thing. She’s got a pituitary gland disorder so she’s short but she puts up with me and my bullshit, so I guess she’s alright. And she’s sane too, which is a plus. “What’s going on in here?” she asks. “I’m trying to get this horror movie about a doctor to work and this DVD player won’t let me because it hates me,” I reply as I wipe some sweat from my brow. "James, what the hell're you talking about?" "Haven't you ever seen Dr. Giggles?" She shakes her head and gives me the usual “James, what the fuck are you talking about?” look that she always does. I think I’m on the verge of giving her an aneurysm after only a week of dating. "No." "You mean you haven't seen Dr. Giggles?!" I exclaim with an exasperated look on my face. I’m giving her the same look she usually gives me at this point. "Nope." "What the hell kind of universe are you living in? Dr. Giggles is classic cinema and should’ve won about ten or eleven Academy Awards for its sheer greatness. I can’t believe that you have not seen a movie of it’s caliber before." “Who’s seen Dr. Giggles?” “EVERYONE!” I exclaim, throwing my arms out to the sides to basically act out what I am saying. She rolls her eyes as I do this. “I haven’t seen it,” she replies calmly, seeing I am quickly becoming agitated. “Everyone who is cool, that is,” I say with a devious little grin which doesn’t amuse her at all. “James, you need to get back into reality with the rest of us,” she says as she walks over and puts her hand on my arm. This sends a little chill up my arm. “But I’m here to help you with that. Now put that stupid movie away and come to bed. You need to get some sleep.” She heads to the bedroom and I don’t have to be asked twice. I pull out the DVD and place it back into its case before shutting off the DVD player and television set before getting up from my crouched position. I head back to the bedroom to join her which hopefully will get me thinking about happy thoughts all the way to a mystical place called dreamland. * * * 9 p.m. I’ve finally fallen asleep. It’s taken a good thirty six to forty hours for this to happen, mind you. I’ve dozed off but am suddenly awakened by the sound of a voice saying something to me. "Hello..." I groggily wake up from my slumber on the couch and I see some guy with a doctor's outfit on standing in front of me while at the same time realizing I’m only wearing my black, skull covered boxer shorts. "You're late on your doctor's check-up," the man says to me. "Huh?" I ask, a bit perturbed by this man suddenly appearing. "You need a thorough examination." I rub my eyes and look straight in front of me to see who it is. Upon looking on in sheer terror, I realize who it is. “Doctor Giggles?” I ask in a shaky voice. “Yes, I am your doctor.” “How is all of this possible?” “I don’t know, but I think it’s time for your examination.” Dr. Giggles opens up his old school medical bag and reaches inside. He then pulls out a small surgical saw. “Don’t worry. This is just a routine operation.” I get up and start trying to hide as my girlfriend Amber walks in. “James, what are you doing?” she asks with a puzzled look on her face. I hate it when she looks at me with a little crinkle on her brow like I’m nuts. She does that a lot. Probably because I am nuts. “Some mad doctor is trying to give me a routine operation using a surgical saw!” I exclaim with a high amount of intensity in my voice. She can tell I’m scared but still has that same weird look on her face. “What do mean?” she asks. “He’s right over there!” I reply, pointing my shaking hand toward Dr. Giggles. She turns and looks at an empty space between me and the front doorway of my house. There is nothing there but a floor lamp that’s turned on and a potted plant hanging from the ceiling. “There’s no one there James,” she says, turning back to me. “There isn’t?” I ask, scratching my head. “No.” “Then who the fuck have I been talking to?” “It’s all in your head, dear,” she replies in a calm voice. She always knows how to soothe my twisted mind using only a few words. “How could it be?” “It all seems so real’I don’t know why, but it really seems real.” She walks over and plants a soft little kiss on my lips. She then brushes my hair back and says, “It’s all just in your brain. You’ll get over it.” She then strolls back to the bedroom as I get up and stretch. I look back over to where I saw the doctor before but he is nowhere to be found. I walk over and shut off the floor lamp before strolling back to the bedroom hoping the whole way there that I don’t start having hallucinations again. * * * Sunday, May 25, 2008 3 a.m. Can’t sleep again. Fifth night in a row. I think I’m starting to turn into the living dead at this rate. Stick a fork in me, I’m done. Call the Grim Reaper because it’s my time to go meet death. I then realize that I’m overreacting and starting thinking about some things. I slept on the couch for about half an hour but that was about it. And then I wake up having hallucinations about an evil doctor that I saw in a horror movie. To top that off my girlfriend thinks that I’m going crazy. This hasn’t been a good week. Now I’m sitting in my pajamas on the couch watching Grizzly Rage while she’s sleeping in the bedroom. It’s a horror movie about four college graduates who go on a trip through the woods and end up killing a bear cub. This enrages the cub’s mother who goes on a killing spree. Yeah, typical von Doom style movie. However, this one isn’t half bad. Has an old school feel about it and some nice nature shots which I like. I like being out in the woods and close to the natural environment, not in the dirty, decrepit dystopia known as Las Vegas that I am in today. Growling noises are heard emanating from the television followed by a woman’s screams. “Ooooo’another mauling,” I say with a grin. I love stupid crap like this in movies. It’s always a nice break from the normal, mundane reality of my daily life. Plus it’s fun to watch stupid people make stupid decisions in these movies. I mean seriously, you’re in a horror movie. Why are you walking around not armed and going upstairs where the only place to run is jumping out the window? This is not the way I would like to be spending my night. I’d rather be out drinking or making some sweet love with my woman but no. I’m stuck here unable to get any sleep watching a movie about a homicidal bear. Life is not supposed to suck this bad. * * * 10 a.m. Hello? Hello? “Now what the fuck is going on?” I mumble to myself as I groggily sit up out of bed. I look around the room but see just a figure standing in front of me. “Hello?” the voice asks again. “Yeah, yeah,” I grumble out before looking ahead to see it’s . “What is it?” “I don’t believe you had an appointment,” the unmistakable voice says again as I sit up out bed and stare again at Dr. Giggles visiting me again. “But in your case, I’ll make an exception.” “EGAD! It’s Dr. Giggles!” I exclaim as I jump out of bed and run down the hallway screaming. I then end up in the living room where Amber is looking at me strangely. “What are you running from sweetie?” she asks with a cute little smile. “Nothing,” I reply back, quickly getting myself collected and going over to sit with her on the couch. “What’re you doing?” “Just watching TV.” “Cool,” I say to her and sit there with her to watch some crappy daytime television shows but it doesn’t matter. At least my mind is at peace for once. And peace is exactly what I need. * * * 3:30 p.m. Starting to get nostalgic here lately. I’ve been missing the old days back when I was happy with what I was doing and not feeling so depressed all the time. I’m currently in my parlor with Amber showing her all of the memorabilia of my wrestling career. “Life didn’t suck this bad when I was in the NWC,” I say with a chuckle as I point to a picture on the wall of myself with the “Killer Klown from Outer Space.” “What do you mean?” she asks, a bit intrigued at this point. “Well I was always happy. Now I can’t sleep at night, I keep getting jerked around whenever I go somewhere, and I get stuck with these lame ass people like the Killer Pirates and other assorted garbage and misfits. My career has gone straight down the toilet and now, I am set to job. I’m gonna job and it sucks.” “What do you mean by job” You mean lose your job or get a new one.” “Lose every week,” I reply with a cocky little smirk knowing that I’m going to say something bad. “You know, like AXEL Action does.” “Who?” “Exactly,” I say, laughing hysterically. “But seriously, he’s a cool guy. I always kid around with him.” “Oh, ok.” I then let out a sigh and point to a picture on the wall of myself standing with Cave Hulk and Captain Jack Morgan on a magazine cover with the headline “Can von Doom overcome his Losing Ways?” right above the picture. “See, this is what my career has become. But that’s all going to change. My plan is simple. Get into someplace where they actually give a shit about James von Doom again and not treat me like I’m dirt. I damn near took over GLOBAL until they put me as the star of their B-show. I was never able to recover. But now it’s my time to destroy, it’s my time to maim, and it’s my time to make some people take notice that von Doom is a star in the making.” “Wow, coolness,” she says with a smile as she gives me a slight hug. “So how are you going to do all of this?” “By going to PRIME and kicking some people’s asses.” “You can actually win in PRIME?” “Probably not but at least I’ll be out of the Church for good.” “That’ll be good for you. You need to be rid of them. They are a bad influence on you from what you’ve told me. I hug her back and bend down to kiss her on the forehead. “All in due time, my dear. It’ll take some time but I’ll get back to a happier place soon. You saved me from them. It was all you?” I continue to hug and look over at a championship belt I have framed on the wall from a past federation hoping that gold is in my future yet again. * * * James von Doom’s Journal 25 May.- Another day in paradise, another nightmare. Finally slept for the first time in days but I’m still not at peace. Still having the nightmares in my tortured brain. How much longer must this go on? How many more days of this? When will this all end? But at least I have love. Real love, that is. For once I have someone who makes me happy and that I don’t drive to alcoholism or turning to lesbianism. This is what I need to overcoming the difficulties of my everyday problems that I’ve been experiencing as of late. This is what I need. She is what I need. Hopefully her love will keep me from getting sucked into eternal darkness never to escape from the insanity that has a hold of my brain? * * * May 30 8:30 p.m. I am at a local pub here in town called the Double D’s Saloon with my tag team partner AXEL Action. We are sitting at stools at the bar drinking some Coors Light. Of course we’re both wearing black. He’s got on some sort of Gothic clothing and I’m wearing jeans along with a black Critters movie poster T-shirt. I’ve brought with me the fax I received from GCW management with the card for the upcoming High Stakes pay-per-view event. I pull it out of my pocket and hand it to him. AXEL unfolds the paper and starts to look it over. “That’s what we’re going to be doing at High Stakes,” I tell him as I point to our names on the sheet of paper. “A triple threat tag team match.” “Ok, what the hell is going on?” he asks with a puzzled look. “I mean, seriously. We’re facing Samson and Delylah as well as the Cowboys from Hell. Where’d Rebel Express go?” “I have no clue. I heard they had to pull out for personal reasons or something.” “We have almost no gripe with either team. This makes no sense at all. It’s almost a contender’s match or something.” “So what the hell are we going to do now?” “I don’t know James.” “See, stupid lame ass bullshit like this is why I fucking hate wrestling.” “Wrestling is scripted.” “True, but that won’t change a thing. I am sick and tired of this tag team bullshit.” “What” Are you breaking up with me?” “Are we boyfriends?” “No.” “Are we lovers?” “No.” “Are we dating?” “No.” “Are you a homosexual?” “Hell fucking no!” “Then we’re not breaking up, stupid. We’re just not going to be playing footsies with stupid cowboys in some lame ass tag team bullshit that nobody cares about anyway.” “von Doom, what the hell are you talking about?” “I’m talking about the truth. I mean, let’s face it. GCW hates us so I say we don’t show up at the pay-per-view.” “Are you insane?” “Seriously, I want to walk out in the middle of the fucking match. I hate it! First we get made to look like punks TWICE by cowboys during one show” I can’t handle going through that fucking bullshit again. “James, is something wrong?” “Hell fucking yeah! I was fired by GCW as was the Church!” AXEL seems to have something more on his mind. I, however, continue talking regardless. “James?” “Those idiots will be wishing they had me back when I’m over in PRIME kicking some ass.” “James?” “We’ll show them just who’s the best tag team in the world. That will be our revenge. We’ll point at them and laugh when we win like eighteen tag team titles after we leave here.” “James, the letter was a prank.” I stop talking right then and turn to AXEL, raising an eyebrow and giving him a look. I’m not too happy with hearing this information. “Ok, what the fuck are you talking about?” I ask him with a snarl. “I sent you the letter,” he replies hesitantly. “It was only a joke to get you fired up for the show.” I glare at him evilly at this point. This is the last thing I wanted to hear from him at this point. “So you mean I applied to PRIME for nothing?” I respond angrily. “So I basically sold out GCW over a fucking joke?” “Well, uh, yeah,” he replies. “You are such a fucking retard!” “WHAT”! What’d I do?” “See, stupid fucking shit like this is why I go nuts on people and everybody wonders why. Jesus Christ, I just can’t wait for this team to dissolve.” “What” WHY?! I thought you liked being on the team!” “Fuck the team! After this shit, it’s fucking over.” I get up from my stool at the bar and storm out of the bar, not even looking back at AXEL. I don’t want to see that fucktard for a real long time after his lame attempt at a joke. * * * June 1 2:30 p.m. I’ve been thinking about it all day? I’ve been wanting it to end? Even before it begins. I can’t wait for this pay-per-view to be over with. I’ve been ignoring AXEL’s calls ever since his prank and am currently at my house sitting on the couch with my girlfriend Amber. I just told her what had happened with the whole GCW issue. “So you’re basically pissed with your tag team partner over a lame attempt at a prank, correct?” she asks in a caring tone. I nod my head and say, “Yes.” “So you’re just going to ignore his calls?” “For however long he keeps calling.” “Don’t you think that’s going a little overboard about this?” “Hell no! That idiot has fucked up too bad this time. Seriously, what the hell kind of shit is that” No wonder we keep losing!” “It’s not all his fault.” “Oh yeah” Then how come he did this, huh? Does he want me to leave or something? Because if he does, then he’s doing a good fucking job of it.” “James, you need to keep calm. Take some time to think about things before you make any hasty decisions.” “Like what” Breaking up the team and leaving GCW?” “Yeah, like that.” I turn to her and give her a look. “Honey, the only person who can keep me calm is you. I will think about it before doing anything hasty.” “That’s what I like to hear, honey.” She gives me an embrace and then gets up off the couch to go get something from the kitchen leaving me to ponder exactly what I am going to do. * * * 10:30 pm I’ve spent all day thinking things over and have made my decision. I pull out my cellphone and dial AXEL’s number. On the other side of the line, I hear it pick up and him cough. “Hello?” he asks groggily. “I’m in,” I say without hesitation. “Any more fuck-ups, I’m out.” “Ja-? he starts to say before I hang up. I close my phone and turn off the light in the living room before getting up and heading back to the bedroom where my sweetheart is waiting. Only time will tell if the Church implodes or if things start to improve.
