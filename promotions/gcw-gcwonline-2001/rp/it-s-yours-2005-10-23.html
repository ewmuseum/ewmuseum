It's Yours

23 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23-10-05 It’s like butterfly wings beating against your face while you’re sleeping. Ya know that soft flutter you might even be able to hear if you took enough time to stop breathing for a few minutes and just listen. It’s like gingerbread, fresh, warm, familiar and sweetly seductive, like wearing in new shoes. It’s like if youth were wine and age is time, you’d sit and sip till you died and died, like quicksand dragging you down and down. It’s all you can do to keep your head above ground. It’s that poem you didn’t realise you wrote till you read back and notice it rhymed. It’s emo, it’s coy, it’s cheesy, it’s home, it’s life, it’s breath, it’s yours. It’s yours? “And no one will tear them apart again.” Mary smiled and stretched one last time before kissing Roberto softly on the mouth while he slept. She brushed a strand of his hair from his face causing him to stir but he didn’t wake up. Leaning in clutching her shoes in her other hand she whispered, “Us and them babypie.” She tiptoed out of his room without writing a note to leave for when he woke up. After the fiasco with the last note she left him that resulted in their relationship literally nearly imploding, she’d decided to give the note thing a rest for a while. The cab ride back home was a long one, long and quiet and lost in thought. She’d been booked for a match after weeks of nothing and something about it was bothering her but she couldn’t quite figure out what or why. She gazed out the window. The sun was rising, it was nearly 6am and she was tired. Needless to say she hadn’t got much sleep while with Roberto, and that thought alone brought a fleeting smile across her face. “Woof!” she whispered under her breath and smiled again but the smile didn’t last. In fact her face stayed stoic while she paid the cab driver, had a shower and climbed into bed. She lay on her side clutching her headless stuffed monkey “Mr Farmsworth.” “So Mary gots to fight again, that’s cool but something don’t feel right Mr Farmsworth, she don’t feel right at all. See there’s this Rei person she gotta wrassle and that’s just dandy. She seen him once or twice and he’s pretty damn good. Mary reckons is gonna be a real fartknocker if nothing else. Like he a big guy and all, and fast too for someone that big. Mary’s gonna have fun, fun with that one but that’s not what’s bothering her. Well she would be lying if she said she dint care whether she lost or won, like she could do with at least one damn win but that’s not it.” She shook the stuffed animal. “You listening Farmy?” No answer. “Good. See Mary’s been thinking bout what Skye said bouts her love thrusters Statty being no different from Damien Black, being no different than he was back in the Wylie days ya know” Mary knows why he was mad at her before and he woulda never hurt her if he didn’t really believe that she left him like that but it’s like as much as Mary knows and as close as they is. There’s this lil corner of Statty that he wont let Mary near and it’s kinda scary Farmy, cus like Mary knows about having dark lil corners no one should ever see and if his is anything like hers then she’s scared to see it and scared that he dealing with it alone. Mary dunno if Skye’s right or not. Mary dunno if Eva deserved what he did to her but hell after what happened in the parking lot and all them names she called Mary well, Mary dint feel bad at all then. Hell no, Mary would pay to see that again with hot butter freaking popcorn damnit!” She rolled onto her other side and yawned. “But that’s the past Mr Farmsworth. Yep, don’t need to worry bout that at all. The past is the past and Mary’s tired of that shit ruining her day. Time to move on, up and over. Tomorrow is another day and all that o’hara crap right?” There was silence from the stuffed animal. “Damn straight.” She reached for the light switch and darkness came. It’s like the fish you caught and threw back just to catch it another day. It’s the chase, the journey, the epic hike over sand dunes in salty air. It’s the beat of the drum, the pulse of your life. It’s the candle wax and the triumph; It’s not burning when passing through the flame. Hell it’s burning all the same. It’s baking from scratch, it’s the oven timer, it’s the mess the stress and the bliss. It’s never what you expected and everything you need. It’s life, it’s breath, it’s yours. “S’gonna be alright. Everything.” And then she slept. Mary woke up to her doorbell ringing off the damn hook, “What tha fook?” It rang again and by ring, it played Out of Space, by the Prodigy. Mary’s idea of being eclectic, but hearing the chipmunk voice yelling “i’ll take ya brain to another dimension, pay close attention!” was beginning to grate even her nerves. The bell eventually stopped. Arael must have gotten the door. Mary brushed her teeth then bounded downstairs. Arael was talking to a tall blonde middle aged woman with her hair pulled into a bun so tight she could have given herself a face lift. The children were all up but still in their night clothes and watching TV, eating lucky charms out of the box. “I’m afraid Mr?” “Torrez, Eduardo Torrez.” Arael introduced himself. “I’m sorry Mr Torrez but I am on a tight schedule, I really can not wait much longer for Mary to come down.” Mary skipped down the stairs, “Hey kiddies,” They mumbled something back while glued to the TV. She padded up to the woman. “Can Mary help you?” The woman collected herself and readjusted the file under her arm. “My name is Ms Liegh. I’m from the Blackbird organistation. I believe you met Mr Lawson already when he dropped the children off with you?” Mary exhaled and smiled. “Oh sure thing lady but hey, if ya wanna drop anymore off Mary aint got much room, well maybe if she keeps a few in the attic?” Ms Leigh looked shocked. “Mary’s just yanking ya silly. What can she do you for?” Ms Leigh didn’t smile, she barely cracked any facial expressions past sourpuss. “If you could just get their things together I will take the children with me.” Mary’s face dropped. “Ya taking em back” They Momma had the baby yet?” “No, not just yet.” “You found em some family they never knew they had?” “Well, we’ve found them “a” family for the time being.” Mary’s eye’s narrowed. “Mary don’t unnerstand, what’s going on” Why you taking em, they fine here.” “Get your things children,” Ms Leigh called across the room. “I am sorry Mary but it has come to our attention that your lifestyle may not be suitable for looking after young children.” Four year old Alize came to stand beside Mary. “Get tha fluck outta here! Mary’s life is just dandy. The kiddiewinks don’t want for nothing, don’t get hurt, they just fine. Mary’s had em weeks. “Yea getta fluck outta here!” Alize repeated. Mary glared down at her. “Err, don’t help Mary just this minute huh munchkin?” Alize shrugged going back to the TV while Mary took the woman aside out of ear shot. “I am sorry Mary but I can not allow the children to stay here, exposed to?” “To what?” Mary was growing angry. “Exposed to such violence.” “Freaking violence! Them kids seen more growing up than they ever gonna see in the ring with Mary. They been watching wrestling all they lil lives. You talk to lil JD, you ask him where his Daddy is and why then you tell Mary bout her life being too freaking violent! Them kids is great kids and Mary’s doing a damn fine job of keeping em in food and fresh water damnit!” Ms Leigh opened her file. “Listen Mary I am sorry that you believe you are doing a good job but you have a psychiatric record so thick I couldn’t even bring it all with me today. These children need to be in a secure environment, not with a guardian who is prone to psychotic episodes at any given time.” Mary was taken aback by the news. She was speechless for a moment. “What the hell! How you get Mary’s files??That ain’t even impotent, Mary was ill, yes she was but she’s ok now, she’s better and she has help. She would never hurt them kids, never. The only violence is in that ring and is all supervised.” Ms Leigh sighed. “Then would you care to explain why you and a Roberto Abitua were pulled from a burning building beaten and bloodied?” Mary’s head spun, “Was a misunderstanding is all?” “And is that how all your misunderstandings are solved Mary?” Anger grew, “Woah wait! Mary would never ever!”” Ms Leigh didn’t let her finish. “Get your things children, this instant please. We have a cab waiting.” Mary’s eyes widened, The children ran to Mary clutching her legs. “Is ok kiddies, they gotta better home for ya, you go..err, you go get ya things okies?” They listened and grudgingly trotted away. They packed slowly, purposefully Mary reckoned. They gathered by the door and Mary blinked the heat away that was burning behind her eyes. Four year old Alize, three year old Kanye and 6 year old JD stood in front of her with bags by their sides. “Say goodbye to Mary children and thank her for looking after you please.” Ms Leigh sounded cold, unfeeling. The children looked up at Mary and remained silent. Kanye ran up to her and clutched her leg. Mary picked him up and he squeezed her as tightly as he did her leg. “Dun wanna TyTy.” Mary bit a quivering lip and forced a smile. She kissed his cheek. “Sorry lil man, ya gotta, but you can come visit any time k?” She put him down with some effort, peeling him off her. She glanced at Alize who had tears in her eyes but fought them all the same. Her little pale freckled face was red with the effort. “Can we wight ya?” “Sure thing dingaling and when you get them W’s sorted out, Mary take you to Richard’s for Ring dings.” “Weally?” “Damn Straight.” Alize hugged her. Jack Daniel stared at her and then turned to leave as Ms Leigh lead them away. He turned back, “Thank you.” Then he walked away again. That had been the only thing he’s said since staying with her and it was that little word that started the tears. Arael stayed distant letting her grieve. The kids had grown on her and now the place was so empty. She did all she knew to deal with pain other than causing more pain to herself. She changed into training clothes and headed to the gym. Silent in the ride over, her mind reeled with possibilities. It seemed like as she’d gained Roberto back the way she wanted him, she’d lost another part of herself in the children. “Take the pain and make it theirs.” She muttered under her breath. Then she smiled. That she knew how to do. That she could control. It’s like the gun in the hands of a child, it’s the sword in the stone. It’s like a pen in your heart from a letter they sent. It’s the sign of times if the sign had bled. It’s the light in the sun that’s overwhelmed, it’s the dark of the night. It’s comfort, it’s warm, it’s pleasure turned round. It’s like screaming no more. It’s the slap in the face, and never turning the other cheek. It’s power, it’s pain, it’s life, it’s breath, it’s yours.
