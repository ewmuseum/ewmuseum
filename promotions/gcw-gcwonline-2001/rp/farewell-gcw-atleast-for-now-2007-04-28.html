Farewell GCW atleast for now

28 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4.28.07 The buzzing of machines over rode everything else in the room. The nurses didn’t get my attention, the pain I was feeling in my leg was unbearable but to the point of no longer recognizing it and the medicine they had me made it so I didn’t know who I was anymore. It is hard to remember what had happened, the last thing I do remember however was being pulled out on a gurney after tapping out to the vice grip by Katie O’Clare. But here I was laid up in Mass General without my title, without anything except for a fractured leg. Hell I even lost my dignity to a woman. I needed something to regain my confidence. GCW officials and Steven Caldera had both told me to take sometime, hell maybe I should retire they said. “You have made enough money, you are well off. Think of your future, Peter.” I quoted Steven exactly but the fact of the matter the money wasn’t the issue, it was more the dignity and the personal accomplishments that mattered to me, if it was money I would have never joined GCW, PRIME paid me well. The noises in the room were starting to get to me thou. Then it came, I am not talking about the friends, the family, hell even Steven Caldera. I am speaking of the news from the doctor. He waited until I was in a more conscious state. There he stood my new best friend, the only guy in the world that could clear me to wrestle again, Dr. Shaun Pierce. He looked at me straight in the face, and without any grimace in his emotions said, “Me letting you wrestle is a liability, you shouldn’t wrestle until you have surgery.” As he said that my heart dropped, I wanted back in that ring and I wanted to be back in that ring right now, tonight. However he continued on, “But there are a couple of things we can do. I have a prescription I can give you it will numb the pain I don’t know how long it will work for you but it will buy you sometime. And at the same time I can prescribe you something to strengthen the bones for you. How does that sound?” There was no question in my mind this is the way I had to go. But I knew there was going to be catch, there had to be. “I am going to have you signed a waiver, this could in the long run ruin your knee. There is no doubt in my mind you need knee surgery but, with these solutions that I have suggested we can prolong it and you will be able to get back into the ring.” “Wrestling is all I know, if I have to take that chance then I will. It might cost me more in the long run but the important part is now, not later.” I had made my mind up there was no going back, I am sorry I had to make a decision like this. Should I have waited for the drugs to wear off? Probably, but I am not going to. I want this done here and now. “Alright then I will set the nurses up so we can perform this and we will have you walking out of here tonight.” So there I laid in my bed waiting for the nurses to come in and prepare me for what this doctor was about to do to me, I didn’t care what he was going to do as long as he made my knee better and I was able to step into the ring again. I sat back thinking of pinning Katie O’Clare getting the win retaining my title. I was mentally ready now the girls were going to get me ready physically. I wonder what Steven Caldera will say once I share the news with him. I guess we will wait and see. Twenty four hours later in my apartment in Boogey down Brock-ton, wait you guys know it as Brockton only. That’s a story for another time. I knew it was time; I was excited I hadn’t sleep the night before as I couldn’t wait to call Steven Caldera and let him know the news. I flip my phone up scroll down to the name boss, as it was ringing I started jogging around my apartment. Who would have thought I would be able to do it just a day or two after injuring my leg. Whatever the doctor gave me for pills and prescriptions were definitely working. Three rings then I hear his voice, “Peter my man. How is the leg today?” “Amazing, I am jogging around my apartment right now.” “What?” You could definitely hear the confusion in his voice. “You’re kidding right?” “Nope, not at all actually, I plan on hitting the gym later on.” I knew he still thought I was joking around but I figured why not play around with him a little bit. “Alright Peter, when are you expected to be back?” He was getting fed up he had enough of the bullshit he wanted the truth and he also wanted it now. “Steve, I will be wrestling at World Wide 66, I want my title back!” I think he knew I wasn’t joking after I said it like that, I was stern and angry. “Are you telling me that you actually think I am going to let you wrestle when not even three days ago you were laid up in a hospital bed and needing a surgery?” “I did, until I met good ole Doctor Shaun Pierce.” “What do you mean?” “He hooked me up with some different pain killers and then he supplied me with some steroid type of drug to increase the strength of my bones. He waived me to wrestle.” Steven was at a lost for words, he was not sure how to handle this. ? Peter I don’t know how to tell you this I can’t allow you into my ring. I can’t have you risking your knee at the expense of wrestling.” What was he saying? I mean think about it we do that every day when we wrestle. “What do you mean?” “I can’t put you in the ring as a liability.” After all I have done for this federation, I was a Triple Crown winner and now I am getting this? “Well I need to wrestle Steven, I have been cleared and I want my rematch with O’Clare for the belt this week.” “She has since left the belt vacant saying she doesn’t want it, and she already has another match. On top of that I can’t have you in a ring Peter what don’t you understand?” “I am cleared by a doctor, I don’t know how you will not allow me to wrestle in the ring.” “Peter stop being stubborn, listen to someone besides yourself for once. I need you to take some time off, I need you to rest up. I want you back but I can’t have it when you knee is on the verge of shitting the bed and I sure as hell not going to let some Steroid freak into my ring!” How the fuck could this guy do this to me? I had done so much for him; I mean yeah he helped me get back into the business once I left PRIME. But the point still was I helped put the asses in the seats. “I have been cleared by doctors, I have the right to step into the ring again, they did say I would need to have surgery. But they said with these pain medications and the steroids my leg would be fine for a couple of months. I then agreed at that point to take a break and have any surgery done.” Steven thought for a moment, I wasn’t in the same room as him but I have to assume he started to rub his chin the same way people do when they are thinking about something important. “I just can’t allow it Peter. Sorry” “Well I am going to have to elsewhere then Steve, I am sorry but I need to wrestle.” I never expected to leave GCW, but then again I never expected to leave PRIME. “Peter I hate to hear that but if that is what you need to do that is what you need to do. But once you get that surgery done I want you back into the GCW ring. Hell you are still allowed into the events but I just can’t have you wrestling.” “Maybe I will do that but for now I need to get some more wrestling under my belt I am way too young to hang it up. But I will be back don’t you worry Steven. You still might see me this week at WorldWide 66 but seeing as you have treated me well I will be still around. But for now I have to go and find a somewhere I can wrestle.” “If that is what you have to do, that is what you have to do.” “Already Steven, I will talk to you later.” “Bye Peter, Good Luck.” It killed me to hang the phone up, I wasn’t quite sure what I was going to do. I wanted to be apart of GCW but not wrestling was much worse. So it was then I decided I needed to move on, wrestle a couple of months somewhere else. Then make my return to GCW. I sat back in my chair and looked at my laptop, “Hell I mind as well check my email.” Sitting there I flipped it open and noticed I had received about twenty new emails since I had been in the hospital. Some of them trying to get me to buy something to make my member bigger while others wanted to talk about my problems, I didn’t have any of these problems where did they come up with this shit? About three emails down I actually had an offer from someone named Dean McDaniel. I guess I had gone to wrestling school with someone he knew or some shit. Or he just said that but in the end the email wanted me to join his new promotion. It was just having its first event and it wanted some star power. I sat there thinking to myself I am not going to get to wrestle in GCW for a little while so why not go over here and see what I can do there. I took a gander at their web site and was sold, I was going to reply saying I am more then happy to wrestle for his company depending on the terms. Once I had finished saying that and giving some of my credentials the email was sent. Now all I need was a reply? I couldn’t wait to see what it said. But the point was I was getting back into the ring.
