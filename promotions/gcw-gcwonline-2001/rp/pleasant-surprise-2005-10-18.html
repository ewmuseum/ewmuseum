Pleasant surprise

18 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/18/05 Damn it. My cell phone and someone knocking at the door at the same time. Which to get first? Well maybe I should wrap a towel around myself first. After all I just got out of the shower and I am soaking wet. I toweled off and grabbed the phone. 'Yo.' 'How you doing?' Ahh, my business manager, Jeff Wylde. Haven't heard from him since FallOut. 'What the hell kind of question is that?' I asked, heading for the door, 'I'm on top of the fuckin' world. I'm still Television champion, I'm still undefeated, I'm in the best shape of my life and this week I face some nobody named Varsity. I'm not just good, I'm fan-fucking-tastic.' 'Good.' Jeff said, 'Just don't get overconfident. You've got all the reason in the world to be confidnent, just don't get cocky. Don't let it go to your head.' 'Jeff, this is me you're talking to. When have you ever known me to get cocky.' If that fucker doesn't stop pounding on my goddamn door... 'Just making sure your head is on straight.' Jeff said. 'Straight as a fuckin arrow J-man. Now, someone is about to break my damn door down. I swear if there's not a beautiful woman on the other side of that door...' I yanked the door open about to yell in the face of... 'Cindy? J, I gotta go.' I heard him laughing as I snapped the phone shut. 'Well are you gonna let me in or are you just gonna stand there in a towel? Not that I'm complaining mind you.' My wife said to me with a grin on her face. 'Uh, yeah, sorry babe.' I said stepping aside, 'Come on in.' 'Surprised?' 'You could say that.' I said shutting the door behind her, 'I take it fugly is to thank for this?' 'Well sort of,' she said, 'He paid for my plane ticket. I haven't seen you in the flesh for a while and I was getting tired of sleeping alone. So you're stuck with me for a few days.' 'Oh, how horrible.' I laughed, 'Whatever shall I do? Stuck with my wife by my side, I don't what to do with myself.' She whipped the towel off of my waist. 'Really? From where I'm standing, I can think of several things to do. All of them involve the bedroom.' She sauntered into said bedroom shedding her clothes as she went. I was quick to follow. SOME TIME LATER 'Make the room stop spinning.' 'I can't see straight.' I said the former, my wife said the latter. If I need to tell you why then you need to get out more. 'Now that was a workout.' I said propping myself up on my elbow, 'I don't know about you, but I could use some food.' 'I could eat,' she muttered, 'Of course, I shouldn't have had desert first.' I chuckled as I pulled out the room service menu and we looked over it before I called down and oreded up some food. 'So how's work going? I know what I see on TV, but I'm wondering what's going on with you when the cameras are off?' 'Same shit, different day.' I said, 'People still wondering if I mowed Johnny Lexicon down with a car, Johnny accusing me of whipping his ass in his locker room the other night, gotta fight some newbie named Varsity this week. The ususal.' 'You have to defend the title again?' she said, now finding the strength to sit up. 'Babe, I'm the television champion. That means I have to defend the title every week on TV. You know me, I don't mind. I've always been a fighting champion, and even though I'm still sore from the parking lot brawl at FallOut, I keep going out there and decimating guys week after week. Last week it was Chrissy-Poo...' I started laughing, 'God that name still gets to me. Then it was Coyle O'Conner, now it's this Varsity kid. Let the GCW booking comitte keep feeding me these guys. I'll chew them up and spit them out.' 'Gonna take it to 10-0?' she grinned at me as I stood up and pulled on my sweats to go answer the knock at the door. 'You know it.' I said locating my wallet and tipping the room service guy. I rolled the tray into the room. 'You gonna come out here and join me or do you wanna eat in bed?' She shuffled into the room tossing on an oversized t-shirt that bore my lovely visage on it along with the words, 'I'm a machine, and you're spare parts!' on it. I hated that shirt, but someone at GCW merchandising thought it was a good idea. I moved the food off the tray and onto the table. I winced as I sat down. 'You okay?' 'Yeah,' I groaned as I settled my weight on the chair. 'Just the usual aches and pains that come with a full wrestling schedule and having to fight every week. I'll be fine.' We ate in silence for a while. 'What?' She had caught me looking at her. It was the same look I had in my eyes when I stopped looking at her as my manager and started looking at her as a womam. 'Just thinking about how lucky I am right now.' 'Really?' she said smirking. 'Yeah, I'm undefeated, I'm a champion, I've got a good friend managing my contract so I don't get fucked over my some unscupulous bastard, and I'm married to the best damn woman in the world.' She smirked again after taking a sip of her soda. 'You're just saying that so I'll give you some more sex later.' 'Is it working?' I asked wiggling my eyebrows suggestivley. 'Yes.'
