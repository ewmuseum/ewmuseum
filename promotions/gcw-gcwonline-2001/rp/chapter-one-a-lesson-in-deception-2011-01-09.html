Chapter One: A Lesson in Deception...

9 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/09/11 -“All warfare is based on deception…” -Sun Tzu The sky weeps tears as the subtle, gentle rolling thunder that reverberates across the sky continues its grim melody. The GCW gym is quiet, save for the low, repeating hum of a treadmill. Micah presses a few buttons on the machine and the incline goes up to a set position of 3 and a half. It is only a few days removed from the intense confrontation between his ‘brother’ Colin and his girlfriend Alexis and their father, former world champion, Ashley O’Ryan. As Micah reaches this 2 mile marker on his daily run, he can’t help but smile…He plays the scene over and over in his head as the monotone steps drift him away into deeper thought. “He really thinks I might hurt them,” he thinks to himself. “That is almost laughable…almost.” He continues to run as the scene plays out in his mind again, but he seems to focus on one thing that he didn’t realize affected him as much as it did. He can hear Ashley’s voice saying, “You don’t know what the offspring of those…monsters is capable of.” Monster. He has the audacity to call me a monster…after he threw away a marriage that he had wanted his entire life? He would call me that, even though he is so lost in his bottle that he can’t see his children have grown up without him? How could he… *THUD!* The pain was sudden, but not lasting. “Idiot! You need to pay attention!” Micah was very upset with himself. I mean…who falls off a treadmill? As he gets up and dusts himself off, he can’t help but laugh a bit. “Christ…I sound just like him.” As he puts a black towel over his head, the images come flooding back for him… _________________________________________________________________________________________ “Goddamn it, Micah! If you’re winded now, you will NEVER be at the level to attain a championship! Get up and do it again…NOW!” The sweat falls and forms a small pool under Micah, who is breathing deeply. A hand reaches down to help him up. “Come on Micah, only one more hour of training.” “Colin…I don’t know how much longer I can keep this up.” Micah accepts the hand…which is quickly knocked away by their trainer, Micah’s father, Stu St. Clair. “Just what in the hell do you think you’re doing, O’Ryan? You need to concentrate on your own training.” Colin was shocked. “Stu…he’s your son. You know how…” “Does it look like I give a religious FUCK who he is, while he is in my ring? Remember, you two came to ME to show you the real way to perform and excel in the ring. The so-called wrestling you are performing in front of me isn’t even worthy to compete in a backyard wrestling federation. I didn’t expect much out of you, Colin…considering who your father is,” Stu says, turning his glare to his son. “…But you. I had such high hopes for you, Micah. You are the fruit of my loins, and this is how you repay me? You’re worthless…” Micah stands, determination etched back on his face. “Do your worst, father…for I shall do mine.” Time passes. The sound of the old, rickety mat and bodies being slammed fill the void. Random curse mutterings are also heard as finally Stu says to his students, “That’s it for the day. Get out of my sight.” Colin and Micah limp from the ring, faces red, bodies’ barley together, suffering from a lack of oxygen and slightly dehydrated. They limp off to the locker room and Stu watches them go. As they do, Stu mutters under his breath, “Bastard kid of a red headed whore. I will destroy you…with my own hands and Jenny will have no choice but to finally submit to me. I WILL possess her.” Micah stops mid step and with a renewed sense of energy, walks slowly back over to Stu in the ring. “Whatever issues you and I have…are between you and I. Leave my mother out of this.” “Tough talk from the son of a lesbian whore.” The next few seconds are a blur. To this day, he’s still not sure how it happened. One second, he is on the apron of the ring, staring down his father and the next he is in the ring, being pulled off of Stu by Colin. His knuckles are bloody, the rage in his eyes flares wildly. “I swear to God in heaven and all the hells you’ve put me through. If you touch my mother…I swear…I swear I will kill you. DO YOU HEAR ME YOU WORTHLESS SON OF A BITCH? I… …will …kill… ...you.” __________________________________________________________________________________________ The sound of the GCW gym doors opening shakes Micah from his memories. He is still sitting on a weight bench with that black towel over his head. He looks up to see a cameraman enter the gym. Micah lowers his head and smirks. ‘Ah yes,’ he thinks to himself. ‘My first ever promotion for worldwide this week. I almost forgot that I told him to meet me here.’ The cameraman walks over and shakes Micah’s hand. “The name is Curtis. I’ll be filming your remarks for the show this week.” “Nice to meet you. Micah St…” “I know who you are, sir. I was a big fan of your father’s.” “Oh…wonderful.” “I have to ask, being that I’m such a huge fan. Did they…did they ever find out why Stu killed himself?” Micah’s eyes, underneath the towel flash a moment of happiness at the mention of the death of his father. He composes himself and feigns a sad tone. “No. You have to understand, he was a shell of his former self when that happened. I guess the light at the end of his tunnel…was a freight train.” “Forgive me. I didn’t mean to bring up such painful memories.” “Oh no. I’ve…learned to live with it.” Curtis nods his head and starts to set up his camera for the shoot. Micah smiles and says under his breath, “This…is for you…asshole.” He takes a deep breath, exhales slowly and looks up into the lens of the camera, the red light now on. The smirk on Micah’s face widens just a bit and we hear the first remarks of GCW’s newest superstar… “I will not sit here and claim greatness. I will not tell you that I will be the next world heavyweight champion. I will not insult your intelligence. What I will do, however, is expect that you will listen, comprehend and trust that these words are true. See, you all are witnessing the beginning of the end of the establishment here in GCW. You will bear witness to the end of the run of the mill stars that are being forced down your throat by television execs and writers who have no concept of…individuality or imagination. I have humbly accepted the role of your guide and sage through the chaotic times ahead. The storms will be rough and the truths…harsh, but you will emerge…enlightened. Let’s take that first step then, shall we? Tonight, Your Heartless Angel takes to the ring for the first time in a triple threat match between me, KJ Newfoundland and “Playboy” Johnny Davis.” Micah can’t help himself but laugh at the outrageous names of his opponents. He takes a quick sip of his bottled water and sighs deeply. “Forgive me gentlemen. It’s not my style to laugh at inferior individuals. It’s my calling in life to show them the error of their ways. I’m here to help you…seeing as how I deeply care about the two of you and your career choices. Oh yes…I really do care. See, after I win this war we are in…what you two do the rest of your careers will reflect on me…your better in the match and your superior intellectually.” Micah slowly cocks his head to the side, lifting an eyebrow. “You all look confused. Is it because I called this a war? Oh, gentlemen…please believe when I say…this is a war. A war for the cheers of the crowd, airtime on television, the respect of the boys and girls in the back, the favor of the management and most importantly, the attention of the current champions that sit atop the world. Yes, I prepare for war… …the two of you think this is just some bullshit wrestling match…and therein lays the difference between us. Take a minute and contemplate my words.” Micah takes another, this time deeper, drink of his water. As he is, it seems as if another thought comes to him. He quickly finishes his drink. “While you attempt to wrap your head around the first thought, think on this as well. In the very well written book, ‘The Art of War’, it is said that all warfare is based in deception. This is true. You all have deceived the public already into thinking that you’ll be worth the price they will pay for admission tonight. Now, as sins go, that’s not that hard to forgive. It will be obvious tonight, as it will be in the future, that you two are not worth the flawed genetic material some slob inserted into your unfortunate mothers. No, I think the bigger sin here…is that you two have deceived yourselves. Look at yourselves. Go on; take a good look in the mirror. Mr. Davis, your delusion is that of self importance. You think that just because you have this ‘playboy’ gimmick that seems to state that you can get any woman you want…no matter how much you pay for them…you think you’re going to get over. Let me tell you this, Mr. Davis. That gimmick was old in 1985 when Ric Flair did the EXACT SAME THING. I find it insulting that you have such a lack of forward thinking, such a miniscule amount of imagination that you would not only think that this tired gimmick will work… …you have the audacity to think that people will care.” He’s rolling now. It seems to come natural for him. He lets the towel fall to his shoulders now, revealing his distinctive eyes. They seem to lock on to the camera’s lens like a snake stalking its prey. “Now we come to Mr. Newfoundland. Your personal deception was a little harder to figure out, but sure enough, it was revealed. You think, by coming here, you can escape your past. You hope by running from your demons…that you would have the opportunity to fly with angels. Allow me to inform you that angels such as myself, heartless or not, would never allow ourselves to be in the company of someone that doesn’t care enough about themselves to own their life. How could you possibly think that you have the ability to comprehend, let alone know your enemy in the ring, when you can’t even stand to get to know your friend in the mirror? Isn’t it ironic that your last name is ‘Newfoundland’…and that is all you seem to do? Find new land to hide from the corruption and pain of the old ones you’ve destroyed?” Micah finishes off his bottle of water and tosses the empty container into the nearby garbage can. He looks back to the camera, sighing again. “Please understand, gentlemen, I don’t mean to sound condescending. That would imply that you two are my intellectual equals and that just wouldn’t be fair for any of us, now would it? No, I wish for you two to think of me as your teacher in this matter. Think of me as a sign on the road, telling you that you’re headed the wrong way in your careers…in your lives. I will do all I can as your superior to show you the way to the brass ring… …even if you lack the mental fortitude to grasp it. Remember what I said at the start of all this. These words are the truth…and tonight in that ring…I’ll make you believe them. Here ends the lesson. Class Dismissed.” Micah stands from the bench and smirks again before walking back to the locker room as the camera fades to black.
