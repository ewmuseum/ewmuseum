Waiting

29 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-29-05 -=The 15th floor of MGM Grand Detroit, the floor set side for those of royalty and celeb fame. Bryan Mayhem and few other GCW stars are on this floor this evening. Some won't arrive until tomorrow. Bryan stares out his large view window into the night sky of Detroit Michigan. Tomorrow night will be a huge night for this time. Buildings will burn down to the foundation, and 5 men will be Buried Alive, in one of the most dangerous matches of all time. Bryan knows about dangerous matches. He's sruvived 3 Belmont House of Torture Matches, a Scaffold match, a buried alive match, numerous hardcore matches and the infamous Home Depot match with his younger legendary brother Jason O'Neil. Strewn about his bed is his wrestling gear and his newly won TV Title. Bryan looks at the title and shakes his head. There's more, and he's greedy, and Mayhem always gets what he wants.=- Mayhem:'I thought that winning a title here would make me content. When Savige ran his mouth and started talking all that trash, I decided I had enough. I've had enough of people that run their mouths. Look at Dan Rivero. I threw him around and later came out and beat Savige for this title right here.' -=Mayhem turns back towards the night sky and breathes in deeply. He exhales=- Mayhem:'I'm Confused, when I came out the other night I heard cheers. People seemed like they were almost excited to see me. -=looking back at the title=- This must be some kind of dream, nobody likes Bryan Mayhem. His mother left when he was young, his father beat him because he was a fuck up, his brother turned on him, and the fans hate him. He's not suppose to be cheered for. But for some strange reason everyone is cheering for this Lunatic, this man that makes the children of England scream in fear. I have fans? Where's the blood?' -=Mayhem looks around his room waiting to see something, anything spelling something out in blood. He finds nothing of the sort. The room has not changed. His stare into the heart of the Motor City gets deeper.=- Mayhem:'I can see the flames now. I can hear the police sirens wailing. Devil's Night is upon us, one way or another, I will be the one everyone remembers. Come NC-17 it will be a brutal war between myself and Lestatt, a rematch of my return. Lestat poses no threat to me.' -=Mayhem turns around and stares at that belt, the belt he just won. He picks it up and looks into the shiny gold. He can see his reflection, than a red splatter his the V in TV. Than another, and another. Mayhem's cheek begins to tickle, he quickly turns around and stares deep into the mirror, and notices tears of blood flowing down his face, and behind him standing on the bed is the old man haunting his dreams, Mayhem turns and the man is gone. He looks back into the mirror, and there he is again. Mayhem stands like a stone in fear.=- Man:'A riddle for you, from me, to reach the end of eternity. Pay attention my befallen son, your path of destruction has just begun. Eternity. Valor. Acceptance. These three words bring thee a fork in the road, creating a heavy martyr load. Exact. Victory. Alliance, for this is your pledge of defiance. Riddle me this, and solve it soon, for your path of evil comes the next moon. Those six words remember them in your head, those six words can kill me dead.' -=Mayhem closes his eyes, and opens them again. The man of riddles is finally gone. He stares deep into himself one last time, tomorrow comes his night, Devil's Night.=-
