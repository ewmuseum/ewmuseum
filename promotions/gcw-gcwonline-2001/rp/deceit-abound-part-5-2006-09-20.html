Deceit Abound: Part 5

Date: 9/20/06 “Pitiful, just pitiful.” If you’d ever asked someone to sum up the career of Tessa Windsor, they could do it easily in those three words. In fact, they’d probably be able to go as far as claming those words covered ninety percent of Tessa Windsor’s life. But in this case, those words were being muttered from Tessa’s rouge painted lips, as she towed her children along towards the finish line, Terminal B, of Logan International Airport. It wasn’t the extra security that nerved the usually patient petite, but more so the looks that said it all as she passed through it all. She hardly wanted her kids to be subjected to a middle aged man, elbowing his not much younger colleague, to whisper into his ears “I think we need to strip search her?” And it’s even worse, when it goes as expected and the bottom lip of the younger and much scrawnier man curls up and into his mouth, before replying, “I think we need to check her internally as well.” Then of course, the cackles of two men, desperately needing to get laid, fill the checkpoint, as both Tessa, and her children, slide away, as if it’s nothing. After all, it almost felt as if this kind of harassment were everyday routine. In fact, when you’re young, beautiful, and mildly famous, it was to be expected. “I bet you miss having someone with you now.” Christa, Tessa’s niece turned adopted daughter, teased, as she slung her cloth backpack over her shoulders. Even at only twelve, she had plenty of sense to know what these men were referring to, and quite frankly, it disturbed her just as much as it did Tessa. “It wouldn’t make a difference,” Masking her heavy sigh with something that could easily be passed of for a yawn, being that it was only 6:30 AM, the GCW Cruiserweight champion tossed their boarding passes into her own purse before returning to the subject at hand, “you’ll find out soon enough.” Being in an age where even preteen’s hormones were kicking into full gear, she shuttered to think just how early that might be. The last thing she’d wanted was for Christa to end up like her, and clinging onto someone just for the sake of image. After all, it was that image of her always being occupied with someone in the love department, which overshadowed everything she’d ever done, and still did. The press could care less how she finished in the rumble, they were far more concerned with the four others she’d aligned herself with, and who she’d be likely to hook up with next. Given that all her friends had known just about as much as her mothers life as she did, all little Christa could do is nod her head and offer nothing more than a soft, “Amen.” ---- April 2006 In times where change is destined to occur, regardless of how you play your cards, it’s obvious no matter what, you’re going up to end up the loser. For that reason, it wasn’t any wonder Tessa Windsor had stranded herself up inside her oversized bedroom back home in Chatham Massachusetts. Up there, she was free from all the accusations, all the gossip, and all the rumors concerning UWF, the death of Seymour’s beloved ex, and of course her and Storm. While it seemed to be the only thing that kept her career alive, while UWF broke down financially, she’d wanted nothing to do with any of it. Like a child, Tessa took shelter under the pink linen that covered her queen sized mattress, and grasped on to her feather pillows as they would save her from all this chaos. But just as a bird searching for it’s nest, chaos always found it’s way back to her, no matter how well hidden she was. And just as always, it greeted her with a demand, “You’re going to need to make a choice.” Plowing her head underneath the pillows on her side, she rolled over onto her back and groaned back, “Can’t I just get a break?” Grabbing her by the lone ankle that strayed out from beneath the sheets, Chris, in his first appearance to her house in almost six months, seized hold of his opportunity to talk some sense back into his former fianc’e, quite literally. “If you want to be continue to be considered one of the most overrated wrestlers to ever step foot in the ring, then sure.” Even with her, he was always his bluntest. As tempted as she was to burrow herself back into the hole she’d dug ages ago, she groaned once more, and accepted the truth for what it was, “Well in that case, the only other alternative I have is the contract GCW offered me.” Gritting his teeth, the man who’d inadvertently brought all this chaos into her life came to the realization that just like her, he too would have to accept the reality of it all. Either he’d rarely see her, or he’d have to support both of him off the fortune he selfishly secured for himself. “Then go out there and get them. Show the world that you don’t need me, or that other fucker for that matter.” ---- “Go out there and get them.” Well that was something that was ALWAYS easier said than done. Even in the case of the press, where all she ever tried to do is go out there, and be as humble as she possibly could. Now, for the first time ever in her career, she was stuck leading the charge against the enemy; in this case, Dies Irae and the Knightmare Brigade. Still, she whispered those words of encouragement to herself over and over again, as if they’d make a difference. Scampering down the halls of the Hard Rock Hotel with her luggage occupying both her arms and shoulders, her kids hugged her sides as they dodged every suspicious person around. For Tessa, it was one thing to gossip about her, but if they were to drag her family into it, that would be a whole new round of disrespect. “Hey Tessa!” Even just a friendly greeting, was enough to send her ducking down as if she were about to be shot. Even a simple greeting often left her knee deep in tabloid stories with suggestive captions. It’s a shame they didn’t pester your average male wrestler the same way they did a mediocre female. But when you’ve got a pair of C’s on your chest, apparently EVERYONE wants to know your business. This time, however, she was safe. Cocking his head to the side, the wrestler formally known as Skylar Green, looked at Tessa as is she was sprouting an extra head. “Crazy day?” He questioned, as he reached over towards her luggage, without even asking if she’d wanted his help. Sky was just that kind of guy. He always knew how to be quite the gentleman, and happened to be one of the few guys she’d met that didn’t want to attach himself permanently to her ass. “You could say that.” Christa answered for her mother, as Tessa rummaged through her coach purse, the lone bag left around her shoulder, for her room key. “Well buck up young soldier, it’s about to get even crazier.” Patting Tessa on the shoulder, he couldn’t help but raise an anxious chuckle out of her. She in return let out a sigh, and questioned what he could possibly mean, even though she knew the answer well in advance, “Press conference tomorrow?” “Of course, it’s the best possible way to start your day in Vegas.” As much as he tried, the likely hood of squeezing some life out of her before the bit was highly nonexistent. “And every other major event.” She added in, as she dropped her suitcases before the doors of room 303. “Sometimes I swear they know more about me than I do myself.” “In that case, at least it’ll be brief. I mean what more could they possibly ask you?” Again, he probed her for life, and yet again, her enthusiasm seemed to have dropped off the radar. “I shudder to think about it Sky.” Skimming her room card through the key slot, she peered back at Sky with a small grimace to try and make it seem as if she’d be fine by the next morning, “But I suppose we’ve got to get everyone gathered up for tomorrow.” “Don’t worry about it, I’ll handle that and the kids. I’m sure my girls could use a few extra friends around here.” Dropping the bags between the two queen sized beds that overtook most of the rooms space, Sky looked back at her and hoped to ease his good friends nerves with his tranquil nature. “Just calm down, and get some rest.” “I’ll try.” She answered back with some reassurance of her own, as her petite figure flopped across the first bed in the room, “Just do me one favor Sky?” “Anything.” “Make sure they stay out of trouble. Especially with everyone that’s around here. News travels too fast for my liking.” Waving off to her with the kids leading the way, Sky tilted his head up to acknowledge her worries, “Not a problem.” And before she knew it, she had all the personal space she could ask for. The only problem? She still wasn’t happy. ---- “You’re insane. You’re absolutely insane.” Rolling her eyes full circle, before letting them disappear behind her eyelids as she dove back down onto the bed, Tessa was never one to see herself as dominating all the others. Hell, she could hardly even get the last word in with her kids. “Nonsense, you’ve got plenty of talent to go out there and make a name for yourself, and yourself alone.” Dropping down beside her on the bed, Chris peered down at her with a glare that challenged her to differ from his opinion. “Just like I’ve done already” Wait nah’that was you, Karina, Jackson, anyone else I’m missing?” Of course, she was sulking to the wrong person. If she’d want any sympathy, she’d have to take it to someone who gave a damn. “Fine, then let your name go down like that. But I’m not going to be associated with a quitter.” And just like that, he was out of the door again, before she could even let him know she’d changed her mind. ---- “Well, at least he oughta be proud of me now.” Curled up under layers of sheets, Tessa Windsor looked over at the empty bed beside her, and pondered if success in life was really all about what you accomplished in your career. After all, when she wasn’t wrestling men twice her size, she was verbally battling kids half her size. And when all that was done, the rest of her time was contemplating why everyone else could find their niche in life, yet at twenty five, she still wandered around like a lost puppy looking for someone to cling to. “Now all I got to do is figure out how to beat out thirty others.” Out came the expected sigh, given the level of frustration involved with that kind of task. Shame she didn’t have the confidence of Brandon Youngblood, then she’d likely be able to sleep better. Then again, Brandon Youngblood didn’t have any friends, or at least didn’t seem to want them. So, he needed not worry about selfishly taking it all for himself. Tessa, on the other hand, knew how much this meant to her friends. While Ciel seemed to careless, the eyes of both La Sombra and Peter Vetra lit up when reminded that should they win, they had a shot at the grand prize of GCW, the World Championship. They too, had dreams and aspirations, and the last thing Tessa wanted to do is crush them before their very eyes. Unfortunately, if she’d wanted that golden opportunity, she was gonna have to be the bitch that kicks out the legs from someone’s pedestals. “Go out there and get them champ.Like real friends, they’ll just need to understand that it’s your turn to shine. ? Maybe then, everyone will be happy.
