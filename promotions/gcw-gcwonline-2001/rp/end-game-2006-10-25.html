End Game

Date: 25/10/06 WFW Encore, Detroit, Michigan, May 19th 2006 Jeff being close to the ropes is able to grab a hold of the bottom one, breaking both holds, Adam and Theo both get to their feet and pick Jeff up, they whip him into the ropes, they go for a double clothesline but Jeff fires back hitting Adam with a clothesline while Theo ducks, he turns around and hits Jeff with a belly to back suplex, he then climbs the ropes but Adam has recovered and gone to the top turnbuckle, as he attempts to hit Theo with a superplex, Jeff has recovered positions himself between Adam's legs, Jeff then goes for a HardKore Bomb on Adam who at the same time hits Theo with a superplex. As the two men hit the mat hard the crowd begins cheering, Jeff gets to his feet and begins resting on the ropes, he then goes to pin Theo. Thunder: ...1! ...2 ...Kickout! Lang: Narrow escape from Theo. Jeff gets up and pins Adam instead. Thunder: ...1! ...2! ...Kickout! Michaels: Another narrow escape, Jeff is getting frustrated. Lang: Boo-hoo for him. Jeff gets up and begins yelling incoherently at Thunder who simply shrugs his shoulders, Jeff picks Theo up and hits him with a Shadow Kick, Adam slowly gets back to his feet and spins Jeff around, Jeff takes Adam down with an armbar then locks on the Chain Reaction, the fans go wild as Adam tries to make it to the ropes. Lang: There we have it! The first Chain Reaction anyone has seen from this man is a good while. Before Adam can do anything, Theo quickly breaks the hold, Jeff gets to his feet but as he does, Theo hits him with a spear then begins punching him in the face, Jimmy pulls him off of Jeff, Adam then goes over to Theo and goes to hit him with a German suplex, Theo tries to block it, Jeff gets up and hits Adam with a German suplex, lifting Theo high into the air and slamming him neck first on the mat. Michaels: Oh my God! A double German suplex. Lang: That was excellently performed. The three men lay exhausted on the mat, Adam is the first to come around and uses the ropes to help himself to his feet, he picks up Jeff and goes for the Bitter End, before he can execute it however, Theo spins him around and hits him with the Immobilizer, he then goes for the pin. Thunder: ...1! ...2! Jeff breaks the pin by kicking Theo, he picks Theo up and hits him with a standing Fate of Doom, before he can go for the pin, Adam is back up and hits Jeff in the back of the head, Jeff turns around and Adam hits him with the Bitter End, he goes to the second drop and jumps off with a Drastic Measure moonsault, he then hooks the leg. Thunder: ...1! ...2! ... Theo just breaks the three-count before the end, Jeff rolls himself over to the ropes and pulls himself up, he goes over to Theo and locks on the Chain Reaction, pulling the neck possibly harder than ever, Adam then locks on the Reckless Abandon Boston Crab as the fans jump to their feet cheering. Michaels: The Reckless Reaction! Their old tag team submission! Lang: Jimmy is confused what to do again, who the hell hired this guy? Theo begins crying out in pain and begins tapping the mat viciously, the fans cheer as Theo has just tapped out but Jimmy can't decide who wins the match, he then finally decides to just break the hold, Adam releases the hold first and kicks Jeff in the head breaking the Chain Reaction. Lang: So much for the team work. Adam picks Jeff up and goes for a Swinging DDT, Jeff reverses out of it and kicks Adam in the stomach, he then hits him with the HardKore Bomb, the fans start an AFT chant despite the fact that on this night, he is the Jazzy One, he then locks in the XTF a submission that isn't used that often, Adam can't move anywhere as the pressure is continually applied, Adam then begins tapping out and Jimmy Thunder calls for the bell, the fans give Jeff a standing ovation as “Power Struggle” by Sunna starts up. Jack O'Hara: The winner of this match, “The Jazzy One” Jeffrey James Harrison! Jimmy helps Jeff to his feet and raises his arm, Jeff then slaps his arm away, looks at Jimmy then hugs him, Theo slowly gets to his feet as Jeff then helps Adam to his, the four men stand in the ring looking at each other. Michaels: Ladies and gentlemen, the last version of Dangerous Intentions possibly put on one of the greatest matches in their careers, win or lose, this is a great match to end their careers on and I'm glad I was able to call it here at WFW Encore. Two Hours Later... The show had ended; fans began their journey home after witnessing one hell of a show. In the final segment of the night the ring had collapsed due to the immense weight from the 28 wrestlers from WFW’s past fighting each other to hopefully get the last shot. The technicians were packing up the electrical equipment and several WFW wrestlers were shaking each others hands maybe for the final time as they all go their separate ways. Jeff Harris was sat in the ring, just looking into where the crowds were once sat. Taking in everything before it all faded away forever. This was World Fantasy Wrestling, the promotion that put Jeff Harris on a global scale. All the previous promotions before were just national based but WFW went everywhere Jeff hadn’t visited. Even though he had had his ups and downs in the company, getting fired a couple of times, quitting a couple more he always respected the place and most of the people that were in it. He called a select few his friends but everyone in the company helped make it in its own unique way. One last show, not just for the promotion but for Jeff Harris before he was taken back to jail for the rest of his life. Jeff had always wanted his final match to be with someone he really cared about and to face Theodoric Solomon and Adam Masterson for the first and last time was something truly special. Despite the fact that Jeff never captured the World Championship in the promotion, many people thought he should have done at several points during his run but the opportunity never came along largely due to his backstage attitude. He caused several arguments and damn near incited riots within the upper management when he was in charge of running the place, it was something that he deeply regretted. When new management took over, people that he hated with a passion he set out to destroy the place he once loved. It was because of Jeff Harris that WFW folded. He did it not to piss off the fans or the wrestlers but he did it because the way it was being handled was wrong, way wrong. He dissected the company bit by bit and left it for dead soon the fans all flooded to Dynasty Wrestling Alliance. As Jeff sat there he couldn’t help by remember his debut match in WFW, a Canadian Championship match between himself and a legend of the business, Jay Moss. A man of higher intellect and even higher wrestling skills; when Jeff made him submit to the Chain Reaction that’s when Jeff really began to get noticed, he had beaten a former global sized World Heavyweight Champion in his first match. The similarities between himself and his step brother Neu Generation were vast but the differences were even greater. Jeff entered the promotion by attacking Neu Generation with a steel chain wrapped around his fist, people knew Neu Generation was a fantastic talent who could have reached the top but he had an obsession with wanting to take Jeff out before he stole his spotlight. Neu failed in that mission however as Jeff quickly became extremely over with the crowd. The only thing on Neu’s mind was taking Jeff out and despite their many matches he never quite got the job done, Jeff on the other hand was more bothered about rising up the ladder and he did by capturing the tag team titles with fellow Extreme Nature member “Stunning” Stan, who was then soon replaced by Adam Masterson. He then saw United States and Intercontinental Championship reigns. His track record for going into a title match as the challenger and coming out the champion is almost flawless, losing just the one title match to a guy named Maher Reims in GCW. Jeff knew he could have taken the championship from the waist of guys like Anson Drake, Revolution, Nick Kelly and Thomas Dsousa. They were nothing compared to the greatness known as The Jazzy One. All the memories of the fed that made him rushed through his mind, he hated the fact that soon the ring would be dismantled, the commentators tables would be taken away, everything would cease to exist and that he would be escorted back to his cell in Nevada. The very thought of never again being in a WFW show or any other show brought tears to his eyes. This was it; his wrestling career had finally come to an end after fighting hard through his entire life to impress the fans and to satisfy his own addiction to the sport. A true legend and former World Champion in multiple promotions turned to a convict no better than the rapists and thieves that inhabit Clark County Prison. Some of the guys that made WFW great would have been able to take something from the show home with them, a little piece to remind them what WFW was all about, not for Jeff because it wasn’t allowed inside. If there was one great thing Jeff got out of the WFW it was his best friend Adam Masterson, Jeff knew that had Adam been with him, they’d have probably been in some other promotion by now kicking ass together with the rest of their brothers Jimmy Thunder and Theodoric Solomon. Jeff stood up and looked around the arena, the stage area and then the commentators? booth where the legendary Joey Michaels and Jimmy Lang sat. Jeff had remembered many good times while being sat there insulting Joey Michaels and the wrestlers that were in the ring. Jeff got out of the ring and sat in Joey Michaels? seat, looking impressed with the comfort of the seat he begins to rock around in it then spins. Several former WFW wrestlers were walking about in the stands, they all knew what fate Jeff had and they felt terrible. Jay Moss, Anson Drake, Angel of Hate and Ravage were all walking around at different levels of the stands taking one last look at the place. Whether they loved or hated him, they all respected Jeff Harris and that’s the real thing that he’ll be taking away from the show. Jeff looked at the ring for a few moments, wiped a few tears away from his eyes then shook his head as if to snap himself out of a daydream. Jeff got to his feet and began walking around the ring he then began walking up the ramp. As he gets to the top of the stage he turns around, he imagines all the good times he had in the promotion. Images run through his head, his former girlfriend Gemma McFaden, Hunnish and Chris Lancer in their epic match, former Reckless Abandon with Adam Masterson, his matches with Ravage and his numerous title reigns. Jeff smiles but for the first time in a long time it wasn’t a sadistic smile, it was a smile of happiness and thankfulness. He taps the left side of his chest with his fist then walks backstage for the final time. As he gets to the backstage he discovers an all out party, the former presidents of the company celebrating with bottles of champagne spraying them all over the electrical equipment. They didn’t give a shit, they’re rich. Former wrestlers play fighting and drinking and generally having fun. Jeff walks into the crowd of workers and receives quite a few pats on the back, an arm then shoots out and stops Jeff in his tracks, and it was Mike Franklin the creator and founder of World Fantasy Wrestling. “Mike.” Jeff said plainly. “Jeff.” Mike replied, “Just wanted to congratulate you on winning a fantastic match tonight, never thought the WFW would take off like it did but I guess I was wrong.” “Thanks Mike. Thank you for hiring me.” Jeff responded. Coming from Mike Franklin that was almost his way of saying “can I have your babies” as the guy pretty much disliked everyone. He never gave Jeff a moment of time when he needed it the most. Whether he had earned his acceptance or not was unknown but being congratulated by a heartless bastard sort of felt good. Jeff then bumps into another former President, Damian Young and the last of the presidents before he tried giving the promotion to Jeff and Angel of Hate to run before President Humus managed to steal the place away from him. Jeff hadn’t heard much from Young since his departure from the wrestling business four years ago. “Good to see you again Jeff, it’s been too long. Just hoped we had longer to chat before” you know. Just wanted you to know that I never approved of what Humus did to the WFW and I’m glad that it died when it did before it was truly ran into the ground. Excellent match tonight Jeff, I’m proud of you.” Young said. “Thanks Damian, even though I thought you were a little biased at times you did keep this place running, you gave guys like me jobs and a good wage. Thank you for that Mr. Young.” Jeff replied, the two then shook hands and gave a nod of respect to each other before carrying along his way. Bryan Deas then turned around and saw Jeff coming at him; he plucked up the courage to hold out a hand. Jeff saw this and smiled. The amount of punishment Jeff handed out to Deas was vast and Deas being the idiot that he is took it but he took it well every time and put it behind him. Despite the fact that Deas wasn’t that much of a hard worker he sure was loyal. Jeff walked up to him and just as he was about to shake Deas? hand he changes his mind and gives him a hard bitch slap to the face before slamming it into the wall next to him. Jeff finally had broken through all the madness and got to his locker-room. He walked in and three men were sat there. His brothers, Thunder, Masterson and Solomon. Jeff smiled like a paedophile in a children’s home. He walked towards the three men and took a seat next to Solomon. “That was one hell of a fucking match.” Jimmy Thunder said, breaking the silence. “You’re damn right it was, what do you expect though it had Dangerous Intentions in it. We’re the greatest competitors on this earth you know that.” Adam Masterson replied. “That was a real great speech you gave after the match too Jeff.” Jimmy said. “Yeah man it touched the heart. It’s a fucking shame that you’re going Jeff, you are the glue that brought us together.” Theo Solomon announced. Jeff just sat there and took the tape off from around his wrists, he then took off the arm pad he had on his left shoulder. As it was said before, after tonight everyone was going their separate ways. Nick Kelly would be going off being an asshole to someone in one part of America; Wolverine would be a pain in the ass in Texas somewhere. Everyone was going for good, following their own paths for the rest of their lives. What people had never expected was that Harris, Solomon, Masterson and Thunder would be going their own ways as well. Masterson would be tending to his family in Florida, Thunder would be moving back to Australia and Theo Solomon was about to settle down with his wife and child to finally live a normal life together. Each of the three always had something outside of wrestling to fall back on, Jeff didn’t and he never did. His entire life revolved around wrestling, Jeff didn’t have family because to him wrestling came first. It’s what destroyed his marriage with Stephanie Carter the mother of Shawn Harris, it’s what destroyed his relationship with Gemma McFaden and it’s why every other girlfriend he’s had since then has left him. At the end of it all he would be a multi-millionaire but it’s not money why he wrestled. He was the passion for the business. No one could claim to be more passionate about wrestling than Jeff was and it made him a better wrestler for it, albeit a worse man. “You know, they’ll be coming soon. To take me away and it pisses me off; you’ve all asked me since I got out why I asked for the death penalty.” Jeff said quietly. “Well it’s because I didn’t want to deal with these feelings that I would never see you three; my brothers ever again. It pains me deep down inside and there is nothing I can do about it. I have to be locked away with a bunch of mad men that claim that they’re innocent and didn’t do anything wrong. Is it fair” Well maybe as I did kill a man but the law states that a man that commits a murder like I did deserves the chair and I want what I deserve! I didn’t want to be sat here having my final match tonight; I wanted to be six foot under ground. Yes tonight was glorious but that’s it, all I have left is memories.” “You think you’re the only person that has to deal with loss Jeff” You’re fuckin? wrong! How do you think we feel that we’ll never be able to sit in the sunlight together and have a beer? You sit there thinking you’re the only victim here well you’re not!” Jimmy shouted back frustrated at Jeff’s words. “This world doesn’t revolve around you Jeff and until you realize that, you can fuck.” Jeff looks up at Jimmy then stands to his feet. “Are you saying I can fuck?” “Yes you can fuck, fuck you.” Jimmy responds. Jeff looks Jimmy deeply in the eyes and says, “Fuck me”! Fuck you too! Fuck you and this whole country and everyone in it. Fuck the panhandlers grubbing for money, smiling behind my back; fuck the squeegee men dirtying up the clean windshield of my car. Get a fucking job. Fuck the Sikhs and the Pakistanis bombing down the avenues in decrepit cabs, curry steaming out their pores, stinking up my day. Terrorists in fucking training. Slow the fuck down! Fuck the Chelsea Boys with their waxed chests and pumped up biceps, going down on each other in my parks and on my piers, jiggling their dicks on my Channel 35! Overfed faces getting pulled and lifted and stretched all taut and shiny. You’re not fooling anybody, sweetheart. Fuck the uptown brothers. They never pass the ball, they don’t want to play defense, they take five steps on every layup to the hoop and then they want to turn around and blame everything on the white man. Slavery ended 140 years ago. Move the fuck on!” Jeff begins walking around the locker-room continuing to yell obscenities at his friends. “Fuck the corrupt cops with their anus-violating plungers and their 41 shots, standing behind a blue wall of silence. They betray our trust! Fuck the priests who put their hands down some innocent child’s pants. Fuck the church that protects them, delivering US into evil and while you’re at it, fuck J.C. The other three members of Dangerous Intentions look on in utter shock as Jeff then storms out of the locker-room. Outside of the locker-room stands four police officers, one of them are holding hand cuffs. Jeff walks up to the officers then turns around. They then lock on the cuffs. “Let’s go home.” One of the officers says with a grin feeling safer after having the cuffs locked on. Jeff’s look of happiness has now been fully transformed into one of hatred, not for his friends but for the entire system. He didn’t want to go back to prison but there was nothing he could do to stop it, everything that was built up in his mind over the past year has finally been released, the proverbial bottle had been smashed against the wall. Jeff was going back to prison and he wasn’t going anywhere. Fade.
