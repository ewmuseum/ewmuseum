True Love

13 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 13th, 2007 There must be something written the Human Genes, because there is something about her that could heal all pain. At least when I look at her is seems that way. Although we met briefly the first time, we spent many weeks together, infact, it feels like months. Going to New York to view the Statue of Liberty, which believe it or not, was my first time in my life, even after living in Massachusetts for my entire life. I feel as though we are true soul mates. I feel as though I am invincable when shes near. Other vacations brought us to historic landmarks such as the Grand Canyon and Mount Rushmore. She was the traveling type. Which is why I knew, with my career traveling constantly, she would understand and come along with. Although my stint as a GCW competitor has been more recently work under Dark Shows, I still feel very much alive in my wrestling capabilities and spirit. (A train come's haulting to a stop as a voice comes blaring off the overhead.) Voice - "Current Location Porter Square, next destination, Harvard Square." Sadie - "So you saw she's right off here?" Mike - "Well basically, I mean shes right across the street. She lives right on the Somerville/Cambridge line." Sadie - "Weird I had an Uncle who lived right up off Beacon street in Somerville, right behind a Star Market." Mike - "Are you Serious?! My mom use to work there for about 6 years as inventory manager." Sadie - "That's crazy! We really have way to much in common." (She let's out a soft giggle, and I can't help but admire her beauty as she smiles.) Mike - "Yes I know. But fate would have it that way. Anyway, have you ever been to his house before?" (We begin to walk as we continue our conversation while exiting the T train Red Line. Though she didn't live to far from here maybe a 3 minute walk, we still chatted on.) Sadie - "Why yes, Although I was very little and remember close to nothing, I still remember what it smelled of." Mike - "And what is that?" Sadie - "Soy milk and tabacco." Mike - "i'm sorry I asked. Haha." (We share a laugh for a moment. That is until we stand in front of her house. My grandmothers house.) Mike - "Well this is her house. Now believe me, she's a beautiful soul, kind of lost without much family left now, but still a genuine person." Sadie - "She must be if she's related to you." (I instantly light up a huge smile. She definetly brings out my caring side.) Mike - "But before we go inside. You see that old wooden bridge over there?" Sadie - "Yes why?" Mike - "Your Uncle lived right over that bridge. That's Beacon Street." Sadie - "Really?" Mike - "Yes. What a coincidence how they were basically neighbors." (She bids me a nod in agreement as I knock on the door.) After a few moments of silence, the silence is lifted with a light holler. "Who is it?" Mike - "It's Michael Grandma. I have someone I'd like you to meet." (The door creaks open a bit as my Grandmother lays her eyes upon mine.) Grandmother - "Who should I be meeting today?" Sadie - "Hello Wilhelmina. I'm Sadie." Almost as if my Grandmother had fallen for her the same way I did, she glared over at Sadie in awe of her stunning beauty. It wasn't soon after she invited us in. Grandmother - "How very lovely to meet you Sadie. Please come on in." The rest flowed very well. We talked for countless hours and viewed many baby pictures to which I were in. Talked about my parents, her parents, our cultures, our religions. The only thing we really didn't see ourselves talking much of was our differences. And that says something. For sure this would be my greatest achievement. True love.
