Worse than a hangover.

18 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/18/05 I'm not exactly sure what love is. But I think I've got it. I love getting my ass kicked. I mean I have to to do what I do, don't I? For three weeks now, every week, he gets me. He gets me. Who the fuck ever got Johnny Lexicon before? And that's not the worst, he gets me good, I don't see it coming and the next thing I know I'm ten feet past an ass whoopin and still runnin with the ball. I don't know who it is, I've been hospitalized three times by a guy I may or may not have a grudge with. I'm fighting a battle of mental assault and losing, I'm running every lap he wants me too and no matter what I do, it's what he wants. I watched that door like an animal, I was ready for him, he must have hid in the closet before I even got to the arena. He must have. How else could he have gotten past me? 'Hey.' Am I slipping that much? 'Hey, buddy.' The only time I opened that door was... 'Hey.' When Ripe Fist... 'Yo!' Showed up out of nowhe... 'Hey what is your problem buddy!' He reaches like I'd expected him too, more agressive than a tourist needing directions. I catch his hand halfway and twist it out, it's perfect how the strain on his wrist straightens his arm and makes him pull his shoulder in tight, moving his head closer to mine. Mine is harder, or so I quickly find out. He crumbles to his knees, blinded by pain, I'm still holding his hand. 'Silly me.' That looks painful. 'What the fuck!?' I spin inside, pulling him closer so that my knee hits his skull and not his temple. I don't want to kill the poor bastard. After all it's not his fault my favorite hooker got picked up by a nasty pimp. It's just unlucky that he's the doorman tonight. He's even unluckier that he left his post unlocked, I slipped into the darkness like a serpent under a rock, nice huh? I'll have to write about that later. The place is locked down like a prison, apartment after apartment, not a soul wandering the halls. She could be in any of these rooms, all I have to go by is her voice. That voice. That accent. This room. Shit. They caught me from behind, I rolled with the first punch to the back of the head, he didn't get me again. There's three of the fuckers and my ribs are killing me, if I had a couple moments to plan this out I might be doing... Something better... Than this. I catch the third punch and roll under it, my shin comes up to catch a kick, I spin friend into friend, they collide, baggy pants around their knees not aiding their cause. My head almost hits the ceiling when I drop kick the third bastard, I land on the two detangling hard and roll onto my feet. 'click' Everything stops, Marauder's arms hang frozen, the pressure of a nine mil baretta pressing into the back of his head enough to push a brick out his ass. Three thugs just got their asses handed to them in a matter of seconds by a superior contender and the most ludicrous of sounds such as 'click' can bring a frenzy to a halt. The moment hangs as tense as a criminal accountants shoulders. 'Who da fuck are you and what da fuck you doin in mah house?' Should have shot me fucker, I throw my head to the side and spin, defeaned by the crack of gun fire missing me by an inche. My elbow snaps into his wrist and his grip goes limp, I'd say he weighs about two hundred pounds of muscle. I tackle him into the room and he loses the pistol, I can't breath, I kick the door closed before the first thug can barge through. We scramble. We rise. 'Click' The pistols square past, the eyes meet, and the will is suddenly tested. Marauder's pistol already cocked, he grins, the pimp knows. Lexicon could have put a bullet between his eyes before he had the hammer of his mini magnum pulled. The door flies open and again the scene is paused, thugs watching with baited breath. 'You the one been pimping one of my girls?' 'Dis what all dat about man?' 'That's what it's all about.' 'You want we should make a deal for da girl?' 'What's your offer?' The black man smiles now, his long dreds and facial peircings only impressing his ghetto appeal. 'Ten thousand.' 'You owe me your life.' 'Five.' 'Deal.' The hammer clicks back into place and the pimp moves his barrel. I dig into my pocket and he nods to his croney, I keep the gun cocked, it makes me feel better. The wad looks good in my hand, I had to cash three paycheques to put it together. 'Bang' I didn't hear the click. I didn't see it coming. Shot from behind... Story of my life. I kick ass until the pay off.
