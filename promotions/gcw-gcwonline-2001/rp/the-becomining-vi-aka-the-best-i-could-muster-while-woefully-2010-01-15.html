THE BECOMINING. VI. AKA: "THE BEST I COULD MUSTER WHILE WOEFULLY TIRED AND AT THE TAIL-END OF A CAFFEINE BINGE." VI: PART ONE…OF REVENGE. VI

15 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/15/10 Neolithic America. Which I think is similar to “lithic” America." A long time ago. You weren’t there. Readeth now, from the book of Sargai! [Never run afoul of PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS. Not in the aisles of Wal-Mart, nor in the dark alleyways of San Francisco. Where He currently resides. Illegally. Not only because His darkness blends in with the general darkness of the aforementioned dark alleys, but because he'll flip you into a trashcan and accuse your mother of committing inappropriate acts in the underworld. PAZUZU! THE DARKNESS! Don't step on his sneakers, for they are custom made kicks from a guy in Harlem who only takes orders via text message. He is very hard to get a hold of, and PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS had to cross dimensions and change trains twice to find the guy. This shoemakers’ particular cell number is too great for your puny mortal mind to comprehend. Not in numerical length, but in evilness. And even if you were able to comprehend it, your message would not make it through. …For his inbox is full. Full of drunken moon texts from PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS. Because He drinketh the fermented blood of Scandinavian demon-whores. So don't waste your time. You’ll only burn your mortal calories and crave more Doctor Pepper and Easter Peeps. PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS does not hate Easter. It amuses Him in his demon-esque downtime. He does not carry marshmallow Peeps on his person, though, for obvious reasons. Obvious to Satan, that is. However, He does not fear them. But he does respect them. ALL HAIL, PAZUZU! THIS IS BLACK METAL BONER MUSIC! So sayeth the soothsayer… YOU WAKE HIS THUNDER AND UNVEIL HIS GLORIOUS TESTICLES! So sayeth the soothsayer… So it is written, so it shall be published. By an independent publishing firm that YOU’VE never heard of.] Shadows cascade off from a dimly lit cave wall. The roar of erupting flames inspires a chorus of jeers. In the interior of the cave, a small platoon of impish demons parade around a cauldron. In the center of the circle a heavyset imp wearing a chef’s hat stirs the murky brew. Shrieking in delight, the imps form a chain-gang -but not the type of chain-gang as prophesized in The Pretenders’ hit single, “Back on the Chain Gang.” Instead, the imps pass buckets of human remains down the line, like clockwork, as the chef imp empties them into the cauldron. The remains include the heads of all your relatives. Yes, everyone you know. All of them. Especially third cousins that you only met once at the family reunion in Maine, and felt awkwardly around. The circle breaks and the imps scramble for the cover of the cave catacombs -and large pieces of promotional Honeycomb cereal bits- as an eerie green mist forms around the edge of the cauldron. Without warning –except, perhaps, for the warning implied by the mist, which was used as a tool for foreshadowing- a dark hooded figure comes into being. The figure flips back his hood to reveal the face of the greatest second-in-command demon ever. The face of Gnark. The unflappable. (Even if he had wings, they would not flap.) Gnark has a long, stern face, dark furrowing brows, and a vicious smirk on his countenance. Which is a word pretentious writers use for “facial expression.” I think. More noticeably, he has long pointed ears that look exactly like the ears of Vulcans from Star Trek, but indeed they are not those ears, but very ORIGINAL ears! How proud he is. Gnark: Fear not my minions of evil for the master will soon be among us. And although I am famished, he is mildly hungry, perhaps just snackish. So we shall lightly feast on this demon brew. For all eternity. A round of cheers rings out as the minions leap fourth from the safety of their Honeycombs and begin to urinate in the brew. Without warning, a bolt of lightning strikes an unsuspecting imp, and PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS appears in its wake. PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS, is the most vile, disgusting looking fiend to ever roam the underearth. However, this is in the way that the GODS think of ugliness, and to you and everyone else He looks like the sexiest male figure that you can remember from your childhood, but with tighter abs. Although shirtless, He adorns a velvet black cape and sparkling black wrestling boots. A cry rings out in the night: HURRAY! MASTER RETURNS! PRAISE HIM. P-ZU: Yes, praise me indeed. Praise me or I will garnish your wages in order to pay back-owed child support. And if you unionize I will import elves that will do my bidding for significantly lower wages. “PRAISE HIM!” P-ZU: And now, I will tell you of the aboveworld and the mildly homoerotic sport of professional wrestling, which I will conquer in my spare time. Gnark: After Nip-Tuck. P-ZU: After Nip-Tuck. Gnark: Fetch Master a bowl of this blood-stained gruel! The chef imp obediently waddles over to the cauldron and places a ladle full of sauce into a Cumberland Farms’ 32 oz ChillZone cup. Gnark: And add in an ice cube in to cool it down. P-ZU: Two, please. Gnark: Master’s tongue is as sensitive as Tiger Woods’ remaining endorsement contracts. P-ZU: Nike DREAMS they could market my voluptuous demon-tongue! PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS slurps down the death-laden syrup in one swift chug. He tosses the small remainder on the ground just because he hates compost and he invented global warming. All because you didn’t say “I love you” enough to your father. P-ZU: Imps, gimps, and fellow pimps, gather round so I may tell you my plan for earthly domination. The imps nudge forward on their haunches, surrounding Him. Gnark: Gather round! Form a pyramid like the cheerleaders. Dutifully, the imps begin to form a pyramid. Gnark: You lost the state cheerleading championships last year! P-ZU: Three-dimensional triangles do not amuse me. Gnark: They do not please him! P-ZU: But championship trophies do. Gnark: So practice, you will. A quiet sigh goes over the imps as they attempt to maintain their composure in beautifully constructed pyramid. P-ZU: Now that you are gathered ‘round, but not quite in the circular meaning of ‘round, I will tell you of my future exploits and the ensuing shenanigans that will come about as a result of them! PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS levitates slightly into the air and spreads his cape, but not far enough to show his embarrassing cherry tattoo that he received on a night He’d rather not talk about, and luckily, can hardly remember anyway. P-ZU: Ten decades in the future, but 100 full years before then, I will make my presence felt on earth by bringing about the apocalypse and destroying human civilization as we know it. At the time it will be known as “apocalypse now,” but without Charlie Sheen, and in the future it will be known as “apocalypse then,” starring an up-and-coming B-actor and myself. However, before that time I will dominate the sport of professional wrestling. Gnark: Balls to the mother-fuckin’ walls, Master. P-ZU: And in my reign of terror, I will defeat any opponent who has the bad luck of being thrown to the wolves, “the wolves, of course, referring to me, and “thrown to” referring to the act of that individual being booked in a match with me. Undoubtedly on a highly respected card that is better attended than a house show. I’m thinking pay-per-view with a name less generic than “Massacre-O-Rama,” but perhaps with slightly less flavor than “I’ll-Skull-Fuck-Your-Christian-Mother’s-Soul Eve. The Third.” Gnark: Higher ratings than “Lost.” P-ZU: But with more polar bears. Gnark: But not like the Christmas polar bears who drink Coca-Colas and slide down the gentle slopes of ice-bergs, waiting for greenhouse gases to melt their homes and drown their mothers as they look on, helplessly. P-ZU: Quite helpless. Like my opponents who I will firmly pummel in my demon-grip –no homo!- and squeeze the succulent juices out of their pathetic mortal bodies so that I might feed them to, and hence appease, the one known as “Rosie O’Donnell.” And after legend of my sexy escapades make their way into popular culture, they will colonize the minds of the youth of America…throughout the world! Gnark: The tale of Master’s sexy escapades will become the crème-de-la-crème of water cooler conversations every Tuesday morning, and the sheepish office workers of middle-America will cry “Hail, PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS, let there be blood!” LET THEIR BE BLOOD, the imps cry in unison. P-ZU: Indeed, there will be. But we must stand strong and with conviction, and perhaps hand-in-hand with outright convicts –except for child molesters- in this endeavor. Because there is legend of roleplay judges that have not the foresight of the Seven-Headed-Demon-of-Foresight-and-Foreskin, and they shall cause us suffering. Gnark: But not they will not cause “suffrage,” which in general refers to the act of giving women the right to vote. Which we do not support for obvious reasons. WE DO NOT SUPPORT!, the imps respond P-ZU: Indeed, there once was a handler from Nanucket, who had so long a…idea for a roleplay, that he worked on one really hard and entered it in PTC-sponsored tournament, found here: http://community.primetimecentral.ne...ad.php’t=41057 Gnark: Indeed, the Great Handler only received 30 points for his roleplay. P-ZU: Thirty-thousand points of light, which henceforth gleamed thirty-times as bright as the mere “thousand points of light” that Ronald Reagan once spoke of. Which is why I gave him Alzheimer’s. Gnark: But also because Master was drunk and thought it was “Budweiser,” that he gave him. The deliciously intoxicating beverage of hell. P-ZU: I once funneled two-and-a-half beers. In college. Gnark: He did. I was there. PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS struts back and forth to the “oohs” and “ahhs” of his minions. Gnark: But then he puked. P-ZU: I tossed my demon-cookies. Gnark: He tossed them upon the labia of a sleeping, overweight- P-ZU: Anyway! Back to the judging… Gnark: Yes, one judge cast stones of poo at the Great Handler for the use of too many verbs. Too many succulent, glistening, desirable, and, above all else, necessary, verbs! P-ZU: And what did he say of the Great Handers’ roleplay? Gnark: He said, and I quote: “By the middle of the story, I kept playing a game trying to guess how many adjectives I would read in a sentence.” P-ZU: That game does sound fun, I’ll admit. Gnark: Like “Party-Jenga.” P-ZU: But too many verbs? That’s like saying you can have “too much fun” in the middle of tantalizing, whimsical free-spirited, epic, monstrosity of a delightfully inspired and engaging blood orgy. One that is held in the rapturous, wild-eyed, and omnipotent glow of Gary’s tent. Gnark: Gary’s our gay friend. P-ZU: Our best friend. But who would say such a thing about verbs? Gnark: “Not I.” I’ve always said. P-ZU: Indeed, you have. Gnark: Furthermore, declared the judge: “When you use them, use them well and sparingly. I believe Emerson said of adjectives, ‘When in doubt, strike them out.’ ” P-ZU: He didn’t really say that, did he Gnark? Gnark: Ney, Master, he did not. P-ZU: Tell them what Emerson really said! Gnark: He said “When in the least bit of overwhelming, consuming, barbarous, and unbearable doubt…let the verbs be.” P-ZU: “Let it Be,” said Paul McCartney. When I came to him in a nightmare. Gnark: Let the verbs stay. P-ZU: Let the verbs stay in the sentence like Brett Favre in the fourth quarter of Sunday Night Football! Gnark: Like Brett Favre. Even in December, when he sucks. Like September to January. P-ZU: December, the time of year when I come to him in the midst of night, riding in on a flaming virgin boar, and feed from the flesh of his old-man-like biceps. And pee on his Levi jeans. The ones he uses in his “pick-up” games with commoners. Gnark: Interceptions, galore! P-ZU: Verbs galore! VERBS GALORE!, echo the minions. P-ZU: But, ALAS! I am but very, very tired, and just felt, coincidentally, very much in need of saying “alas!” So now that I have relayed my epic plans to your repugnant non-understanding minds, I will return to my chambers and await the opportunity to strike the world of professional wrestling. In the face. Gnark: Master has to take a shit! P-ZU: Indeed. And with that, I bid you adieu! Gnark: And I raise you FIVE adieus! P-ZU: …I fold. With that, PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS disappears into a cloud of smoke and enters your e-fed. To haunt you in your nightmares, and make you feel awkward in your WET DREAMS! Good luck. And good night. Assholes.
