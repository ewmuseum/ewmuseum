AXEL in Time- Chapter I: The Beginning

15 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 15,2008 After my epic encounter with Dra? I mean, Rick Astley, my nerves had been worn just a tad thin. The human mind just isn’t made to cope with things like that; especially a mind as frazzled as mine already was before I lost Mindy to the darkness. As hateful and despicable a person as I had become, I would never even wish what happened to Mindy on my worst enemy. Even though it had been days since the encounter, I was still rather shaky, and I needed to unwind. I thought about trying to go to sleep, but it had been so long since I had been able to sleep without having some kind of horrible dream. Normally, I welcomed nightmares, as they were one of the few things that brought me peace and joy since my mind deteriorated. These nightmares, though, were much more vivid, and were much more frightening than anything I had witnessed as a child. Every time I’d drift off to dreamland, I’d have the most realistic visions of being tortured and killed. Drawn and quartered, disemboweled, torn to shreds by unseen beasts; It was enough to shatter a normal human being’s sanity. Fortunately, my sanity was gone long, long ago. Since sleeping was out of the question, I decided to do what anyone would do at a time like this. I dusted off the ol? video game console and plopped down onto my couch. Most people would play Gears of War, Halo 3 or something along those lines, but not me. Oh no, not the Unholy One. I played a real man’s game’Super Mario Brothers. As I booted up the game, the familiar theme from the first level began to play. I couldn’t help but smile as I heard those familiar sounds, and the longer I played, the more relaxed I became. Soon, I began to nod off slowly, my eyelids falling shut every so often, only to flutter open once more. I fought desperately to stay awake, because I knew that just as soon as I fell asleep, I’d have another one of those horrible nightmares. It got to the point where I couldn’t fight my way out of sleep’s clutches, and I drifted off as that ever-popular theme continued to loop. Suddenly, it was as if I were in the Mushroom Kingdom. I was surrounded by green pipes, question mark blocks and blocks made of bricks that were somehow magically suspended in the air and oddly shaped trees and plants. Upon taking a better look around, I saw turtles walking upright on two feet, even some with wings on their backs. Alongside the winged, upright-walking turtles that I assumed to be Koopas, there were? Goombas? Was I hallucinating? Was I going insane? I couldn’t be losing my mind. It was already gone, wasn’t it? That’s when I heard the beginning of that ever-familiar theme. ?Do the Mario!? I felt my heart drop into the pit of my stomach when I heard those words echo through the surrounding area from somewhere behind me. I remembered hearing those words every Saturday morning as a kid when I’d watch the Super Mario Brothers Super Show. No, it couldn’t be?! ?Swing your arms from side-to-side. C’mon, it’s time to go do the Mario!? I turned around, only to see something that chilled me to the bone. Right before my eyes was Captain Lou Albano in a Mario costume. What’s worse than that is that he was singing and dancing, too! I was frozen in place, unable to move or speak. All I could do was watch in horror as this fat man in a horribly designed costume sang and “busted a move”. ?Take one step, and then again. Let’s do the Mario altogether, now!? He was flailing his arms and swinging his hips, and with each move he made, he moved a few steps closer to me. My body shook with fear, my fists clenched tight. I wanted to scream, and I tried desperately, but nothing came out. ?You got it! It’s the Mario!? As I stood there, wondering just how long this could go on for, I started to feel as if I couldn’t breathe. The fear was so overwhelming that my body was forgetting its vital functions. Much to my surprise, Captain Lou started singing all over again, causing me to break into tears, sobbing loudly as he repeated those words all over again. ?Swing your arms from side-to-side. C’mon it’s time to go do the Mario! Take one step, and then again. Let’s do the Mario altogether, now!? Once he finished that line, everything went dark for a moment before bold, white text began to fall from the sky. Why there were end credits in my dream, I have no clue, but I can tell you now that these weren’t normal ending credits. These were ending credits of DOOM~!!! They were closing in fast, and I had nowhere to run to. If they managed to fall on top of me, I’d have been crushed. I awoke with a start, my body perspiring coldly, my face stained with tears. Had I really been crying over something as silly as a nightmare? I was ashamed of myself and didn’t know what to do anymore, so I picked up the controller and continued playing. “That’s right! Take that, you fuckin” mushroom motherfucker!” As I sat there enjoying my game, I felt my stomach begin to swell, and my upper lip begin to tingle. I didn’t know what was going on, but I didn’t care. I was too enthralled in the game to stop and see what was going on. I happened to glance down for a split second, only to see myself in a pair of bright red overalls with a blue, button-down shirt underneath it. Was I turning into . . . ? “Mamma Mia! That’s-a big-a Koopa!” Oh God, had I just spoken in a bad, Italian accent? I was beginning to get a little freaked out, because that wasn’t something that happened every day, you know? I felt something tickling my nose, and out of my lower peripheral vision, I noticed a large, handlebar moustache forming underneath my nose, on my upper lip. It tickled my nose, so much so that I felt like I would sneeze any moment, and sneeze, I did, and at the worst possible moment. I burst into a sneezing fit just as I was attempting to jump over a pit, and the very first sneeze caused me to drop the controller. Of course, Mario fell into the pit, and suddenly, a hole opened up in the floor below my couch, and I plummeted down, down, down, and further down. There appeared to be no end in sight. “AAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!” I awoke once more from my terrifying slumber, apparently having been trapped in a dream within a dream. I had never actually believed in those until today, as it had never happened to me before. Just to make sure I was awake, I pinched myself, and hard. Oh yeah, I was awake. Not only was I awake, but I was absolutely famished. Forcing my way up to my feet, I headed for the kitchen, opening my refrigerator door. I noticed an open can of Coca-Cola sitting on the top shelf. I removed it from the fridge and shook it lightly, to see if there was anything in it, wouldn’t you know it? It was empty. “Fuck” That was the last one, too.” I was a bit flustered upon finding the last can of soda to be empty, and to be quite honest, a bit pissed. Who would put an empty cola can back into the refrigerator instead of throwing it away? I pondered that question for a moment as I headed for the pantry, hoping to find a bag of chips that wasn’t stale or full of crumbs. I stopped dead in my tracks, my eyes widening as I came up with the answer. Of course! Why didn’t I see it before? It was so simple? Von Doom. Von Doom had to have done it the other night when we had our dates over here. After all, he was the only person I knew who had that ugly habit. “Damn it, James! You need to start telling me when you snag the last cola.” I spoke those words to myself, knowing that he couldn’t hear me. He was nowhere around, so how could he have heard? I began my very short trek back to the pantry, taking hold of the handle on the wooden door. I knew exactly what I wanted to eat. However, what I didn’t know, is that upon opening my pantry, I would be thrust into a world of adventure that no professional wrestler had ever been to. I was completely unaware of the rip in the time-space continuum that had occurred within my pantry; Upon opening the door, I was sucked in without a sound, and even though I tried to scream, nothing would come out. It’s been said that inside of a time portal, no one can hear you scream. I asked myself over and over where I would end up, but the truth was; only time would tell.
