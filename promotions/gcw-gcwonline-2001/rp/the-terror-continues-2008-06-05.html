The Terror Continues

5 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/5/08 The dimly lit loft style apartment was filled with the pungent odor of feces. The room consisted of an old wrought iron double bed and a small TV. On the bed sat a small child about ten years old curled next to a woman whom appeared to be old enough to be his grandmother. Clicking of shoes was heard crossing the distance of the room. Large emerald green eyes peered up at the dark being that reflected in his eyes. His tanned complexion turned ghostly white as his face was splattered with crimson fluid. A cackle resonated through the small room; it was a laugh that Delylah would never forget. It was Cleo torturing a small child. “Awww my poor Daniel, I had to do this. I do hope you understand.” Cleo tried to console the child, “Granny did some horrible things to me and she needed to pay for her sins.” The small boy just looked up at her and nodded. “I” I?” The boy stuttered. “It’s ok Daniel. I wont leave you here alone.” With the swiftness of a cheetah Cleo sunk her canines into Daniel’s tender young flesh. He flailed around a little and then went limp in her arms. Cleo’s lips formed a sick twisted smile as she wiped away the blood on her lips. “Now you shall never feel pain again Daniel. Now we will be together forever.” Cleo said in a singsong voice, “You Delylah will be next. I promise you no matter where you go or how hard you try to hide I will find you.” Cleo’s words rung in Delylah’s ears. Delylah screamed out in horror as she bolted up off the floor. Paul’s eyes snapped open and hoped to his feet fearing that Cleopatra had returned. His nerves eased as he saw the rising sun peering through the skylight above the Nguyen’s bed. Delylah knew that it was just a dream but to her it was reality. There was a difference between a normal nightmare and the seeds of malevolence that Cleo planted in her mind. “Del, are you ok” What did you see?” Paul asked her, his voice just above a whisper. “Nothing, just everything that happened last night plagued my dreams.” She shifted her eyes away from him. She knew if she looked at him he would know that she was lying through her teeth. “Everything will be fine as soon as we get out of this place.” Delylah paced the floor as Paul sat in the corner still in shock that he actually killed someone. The body of Elias Nagatti was sprawled out on the floor next to the lifeless Amun Nguyen. Delylah’s mother Nebt-Het still hadn’t awoken from her horror induced coma. Delylah and Paul both didn’t relish the fact of her waking up. But they both knew it was inevitable. Last night had been one of the roughest nights in all of their lives but this whole nightmare wasn’t over yet. It was far from over. They were still prisoners in a room with two corpses, a room that reeked of death and debauchery. All they could do was wait. Delylah knew that Cleo wasn’t coming back. She had other plans, plans that twisted Delylah’s stomach in knots. The young child’s horror stricken face was permanently etched on Delylah’s brain. The child’s deep green eyes shone like emeralds against his tanned complexion. The boys face looked so familiar to Delylah but she knew that it was no one important. Maybe deep down in the most secret recesses of her heart that is what she hoped her child would be that angelic looking. It didn’t matter much now; right now they needed to find a way out of this place. Her childhood home would never be the same. She would never be able to set foot in here again with out being vulnerable to Cleo’s mind manipulations. Delylah and Paul both snapped their heads toward the groans coming from the bed. Paul hopped to his feet and snatched up another piece of the broken end table. He held it high over his head and was ready to plunge it into Amun’s heart if need be. Nebt-Het’s eyes slowly started to flutter open and Delylah ran to her. Nebt-Het looked to her left side and the tears of desolation flowed freely down her cheeks. Delylah untied her mother’s hands and pulled her close to her bosom the way a mother would console a wailing child. “Don’t worry mom we will get out of here. I promise.” Delylah crooned in her mother’s ear as she smoothed her hair. “I” want? her? dead!” Nebt-Het choked out through her sobs. “Don’t worry mom, I will take care of everything.” Delylah reassured her with a kiss on the top of her head. “I will make her atone for her sins. She will rot in hell where she belongs.” Delylah’s words dripped with detestation. “Where will I go Del” I can’t stay here,” Nebt-Het looked to the dead body of her husband, “not now.” “You will come live with us.” Paul finally adding his voice to the conversation, “I will protect you as well as Del with my life Mrs. Nguyen.” Nebt-Het looked up at Paul, “Call me mom please Paul. We have been through too much together for you not to.” Paul’s eyes lit up and his jaw dropped in shock, “Uh” OK,” Paul fumbled and then finally brought the word to his lips, “Mom.” Delylah’s ears pricked at the sounds just outside the door. She pressed a finger to her lips signaling everyone to keep quiet as she crept to the door. She placed her ear against the door to see just who was out there. “She’s gone Rubis, they killed her father.” The gatekeepers voice was monotone, “I am going after her and you will clean up this mess.” Vassago enforced with his gruff tone. “They will not escape me,” Rubis grunted. The tumblers in the lock began to move and Paul bum rushed the door with one of the legs to the cherry nightstand. Delylah understood his motive and stood in the center of the room to lure Rubis inside. Nebt-Het lay back on the bed and played possum. Rubis slowly inched the door open hoping to catch them all off guard. As soon as he saw Delylah he charged after her. Paul slipped out from behind the door. Rubis grasped Delylah’s wrists and hissed showing his sharpened canines. Delylah spit in his face but Rubis just smiled evilly. Paul crept behind Rubis, which brought a smile to Delylah’s lips. Before Rubis could turn to see what she was smiling about he was cracked in the temple with the makeshift club. Rubis crumbled to the floor and that was their cue to get the hell out of there. Nebt-Het paused for a moment to place a kiss on her husband’s cold lips as Paul stripped him of the key to their prison. Delylah grabbed her mother’s arm and yanked her out of the room. Paul dashed out and locked the door behind him. “There that should hold him long enough for us to get out of here.” Paul said as they all ran down the stairs and out the front door. When they reached the car their hearts and hopes crumbled, all four tires were flat. Paul and Delylah both cursed. Nebt-Het just smiled. “Why are you smiling mom” Don’t you realize were not going to get away. He will kill us.” Delylah said as she shook her mother thinking that she was just delirious. “I have a car Del.” Nebt-Het whispered, “Not even your father knew about it. I hid it behind the garage. The keys are above the driver’s side visor Paul.” Delylah and Paul just stared at her. Paul took off toward the garage and sure enough there sat a black 2006 Mercury Grand Marquis. Paul squealed like a child in a candy store and hopped behind the wheel. By the time he had the car started Delylah and Nebt-Het were in the car and their belts fastened. Paul romped on the gas; the tires spun then caught the ground spraying dirt everywhere. Just as they rounded the corner the monster that is Devlin Rubis stood at the corner of the house. He moved to the middle of the driveway, Paul sped up. Delylah watched Devlin’s body roll up over the top of the Marquis and sprawl out in the center of the driveway. A smile of satisfaction crossed their faces as they sped down the road headed home, home to Dallas. Delylah knew that no matter where they went Cleopatra would find them. If she was going to have to do battle with her again she wanted to be on her home turf. She would be the one to seek her out; they had that link that only twins share. This time she would be prepared. “I will be prepared Cleo. You will be going to hell where you belong; you and The Gatekeeper.” Delylah’s words burnt her throat like acid. She had never said words so true in her entire life.
