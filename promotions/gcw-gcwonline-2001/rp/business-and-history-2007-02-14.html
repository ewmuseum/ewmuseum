Business and history!

14 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/14/07 Sitting in a room of pale blues and whites. A doctors desk and plenty of files and filing cabinets. Shaman sits on a chair across from a doctor who is severely balding and looks a little worse for wear. Shaman himself looks like the bastion of perfect health. Leaning forward Shaman takes one of the doctors lolli pops and grins as he opens it. The doctor barely raises his head as he speaks. ?Look Mr. Shaman i am not sure why they have sent you here, but you are fine. No health issues and no mental instability aspects.? Shaman looks at the doctor chuckling. ?You know how it goes Doc. The company wants physicals done and some sort of mental evaluation.? The Doctor simply shakes his head and motions for Shaman to move to the examination table. Shaman quietly gets up and walks over to the examination table and sits. The Doctor follows and pulls out a small flashlight like device and checks Shaman's eyes and motions for Shaman to look to his left then right. ?Ok Shaman follow my hand without moving your head.? Shaman follows the movements with ease as his eyes watch every movement with precise detail. Shaman grins looking at the Doctor. ?Why is it your always nervous when you see me?? The Doctor looks him in the eyes as his expression changes. ?Last time you cost me around 150.000 dollars in repair bills.? Shaman smirks and then leans back sucking on the lolli pop. ?Why is it everytime we meet you blame me for something?? The Doctor looks at Shaman and growls out as he looks at him. Pointing to his bald spot on his head. ?This is because of you. You are the only man who flies across the country to see me. All the other wrestlers have retired or gone to see other doctors and left me alone, But no you have to keep coming back.? Shaman looks at the doctor and smiles again as he looks at the bald spot. ?Rogain helps, I even heard they have these great new hair implants they can do now. I mean after all it is not fair blaming me for your obvious hair loss.? The Doctor is obviously frustrated as he turns and walks back to the desk. Staring at Shaman he motions for him to sit in the chair again. Shaman moves forward and sits in the chair looking at the doctor as he begins to speak again. ?When i was five i had this cute little puppy. It was a great dog..? The doctor throws his clipboard and pen up in the air and simply leans back in the chair looking at Shaman. ?Could you once not mock my profession?? Shaman rubs his chin and then leans back. ?Ok doc what do you suggest?? The Doctor looks a bit dumbfounded and leans forward on his desk. ?You serious?? Shaman frowns a bit.?Yes serious as cancer pal.? The Doctor stands up and walks back and forth behind the chair and looks at Shaman as he speaks. ?Well tell me about this new assistant you hired. Also tell me a bit about the Casino you own and how you came to be in as good a position as you are now financially.? Shaman looks at the doctor and shakes his head. ?Kawanaha is a hard worker and a great asset to the company. Most women like her have a hidden agenda and trust me i think she has her own as well. I like her hard work and that is all that matters at the moment. ? Shaman frowns and leans back. ?I think the hard part is she is very beautiful as well. Which could leads to difficult situations like the Governors ball recently in Las Vegas.? The Doctor listens intently as Shaman speaks. ?Look Doc i am not delving too much into the info there. Let me just say not one eye in the place looked away from her. She made the room beautiful.? Shaman grins as he had not usually talked highly of the women he worked with due to trying to keep it strictly a business relationship. Kawanaha was different he thought. She never tried to go beyond her means and she always stayed true to herself. Some days he wondered how hard things really had been for her and her sister. He wasn't going to talk to the doctor about this obviously since his Doctor was an idiot as far as he was concerned. Shaman turned and looked at the window across the room. ?Well Doc the Casino is a fun business venture, but it was my wrestling career that allowed me the capitol to get there. I tend to save a lot of money and not like to spend it unless it is something that is needed. If the Casino folded and went out of business I'd still be fine for the rest of my life and then some. You see the Casino business is always good and profitable. I got lucky to get into it fully? The Doctor looks at him and nods and he then sits down in his chair again and looks at Shaman. ?Well tell me about this big battle royal thing for the number one contendership for GCW? They are the ones you are working for now correct?? Shaman looks at the doctor and narrows one eye at him while raising the opposite eyebrow. ?Funny you would bring that up. Nonetheless i will tell you. It is a shot at the biggest prize in GCW. I have my allies in this match up and i have my enemies. Rest assured though this is a fight and nobody is looking past the other. Mainly because anyone can do anything here. A nobody can come along and take the title or someone who has been here before can come out of the wood work and come and take it again. Everyone in that match up are veterans.? Shaman leans back and looks about as he cracks his neck from side to side. ?Besides i got something to prove to miss Ambrosi. She beat me back in GTT4. It is time to show her that she no longer reigns supreme at the top.? Shaman stands up and looks at the doctor. ?Are we done here Doc, Because i do have better things to do than sit here and chat with you.? Shaman looks at the doctor as a level of annoyance is apparent upon his facial features. The Doctors grins and stands up. ?You are done thank you for your time. Feel free to visit again after i retire.? Shaman chuckles as he walks out the door. ?See you this time next year Doc.? The Doctor stares at him shaking with frustration. ?Not if i can help it.? Shaman walks through the lobby of the Doctors office and exits through the main door. He walks along a crowded New York City street as he observes everyone walking by him. He begins to wonder why everyone has to be so tempted to be attacked or allowed to be viewed so weak. The real world wasn't like wrestling at all. It was survive or be thrust into the bottom of the well with the rest of the refuse. He had climbed out of that well hard and tough. Fighting to get every inch of the way there. Blood sweat and tears is what he sacrificed along the way. Friendships he had a few, but none that lasted. He had respect from the wrestling community, But not from the corporate folks he had to deal with whenever his contracts were up. There was no difference that he has always been a fan favorite be it a good guy or a bad guy. Stopping at a corner he looked out across the street and saw a familiar place. A bar he used to go to often when NCW traveled this area a lot. He always wondered what happened to the var after they were changed to the WWA. Of course he purchased all the rights to the NCW. It was under his control now. The old NCW archives records and the like were all sitting in a warehouse in Vegas that he owned. Shrugging he walked across the street and entered the bar and looked about. He smiled as he recognized the bartender. ?Hey Tony been forever man.? the bartender turned around and smiled as he saw Shaman. ?Damn man it has been like four years since you walked in this place. It has kind of gone down hill since ya have gone. Not many people come anymore since the wrestlers stopped dropping by.? Shaman looks around the place as it was still the same as it had always been. He grinned and looked at Tony. ?I think i may have an idea on how to cure that situation Tony.? Pulling his cellphone out of his pocket he makes a phone call. ?Yeah is this Michael? Good good man been awhile. I want to let you know that i have a job opportunity for you. It is on an appearance basis and there will be a few others coming as well. Yeah yeah it is all good.? Shaman smiles and looks at Tony raising his finger to hold him up one more time as he dialed a new number. ?Konichiwa Inaki san. I have a job opportunity for you. Yes yes i know your on a European tour go ahead and finish it. This has to do with an American swing you take every year. Yes yes that will be fine. Call me in Vegas and i will give all details? Shaman hangs up the Phone and looks at Tony. ?Tony you have been doing this thing for many years and i am sorry that as a part owner i hadn't looked into helping to fix this stuff up and get the business aspect off the ground a little more.? Shaman looks around the place and sighs a little as Tony speaks up. ?Look man i had the bar put in your name two years ago as i ran everything as your business associate. It was your money that has kept the place running. I heard you bought a Casino and i was going to call you this week and set up a meeting and present you with the paperwork.? Shaman looked at Tony a bit of surprise on his face as he looked around at the place. ?Tony why would you sell this? This has been in your family for almost a hundred years.? Tony sat down at a bar stool near Shaman and smiled patting the big man on the back. ?Shaman old friend i have no kids and i am pushing 66. You have been the only person like a son to me and i decided that it was time to pass the bar onto someone i know who will care for it as much as i have.? Shaman just simply looks at Tony in awe as this decision he had made. Shaman stands up and walks over to a poster on the wall of himself. He runs his fingers over it admiringly. ?Those sure were the days huh Tony?? Shaman lingers on the poster remembering the past in his mind. Tony stood up smiling as he walked back behind the bar and began wiping the glasses again. ?Sure does kid. It sure does.? Shaman looks around at Tony and watches him wipe the glasses as he seems to wipe some more than twice. ?You know Tony i have always been meaning to ask you why you wipe those glasses more than once?? Tony chuckled as he looked over at Shaman. ?Well kid it is simple really. The glasses gather dust sometimes and if i keep them wiped they have a crystal like shine under the lights. Makes it seem all that more glorious to get a drink from.? Shaman looks at the glasses and nods noting they did shine brighter than other bars he had seen or the ones he owned. ?Well Tony i think i have just the job for you. You are the head guy with my Bars. With this one it makes five across the US. I have one in Maine, two in Alabama, and another in Reno, Nevada. I'd like to have you put out a memo and put these places straight with your style of bar tending.? Shaman looks at Tony and grins as he options for a beer from the tap. Tony smiles as he gets up and walks over to the glasses grabbing one and fills the beer from the tap. The beer flows out a nice golden color shining in the light. Shaman watches impressed with how the light shines through the beer and into the glass. It really did shine like crystals he thought. Taking the beer he looks at it admiring the carbon bubbles rolling up the side of the glass in a straight line. Always impressed as to the way it looked. He drank some of the beer as the foam settled on the side of the glass where the beer once sat. Looking at the large mirror across from himself he looked at himself long and hard. ?What a long hard strange road i have traveled Tony.? Tony watched Shaman and listened as Shaman drank his beer slowly. ?I have moved back into the lines of contention for World titles again. It is a strange place, but a familiar one. I can hold my own against any top competing guy in the ring. I am not just a powerhouse. I am also a technically sound wrestler. I have and will show my prowess over and over again. I don't mind showing them what i can do in the ring.? Shaman drinks some more of the beer and looks at Tony. ?I think i will get a refill after this one Tony.? Tony looks over at Tony and smiles. ?So what ya doing next kid. I think the misses would like ya to come on over for dinner this evening. I will call her. She has been wanting to cook one of her famous Italian feasts again. It gets her out of the house to shop. Promise me one thing too kid. You will come by more often.? Shaman looks over at Tony and smiles and nods. ?I will be here a few more days Tony and i'll be glad to stick around for dinner.? Tony smiles and looks at Shaman and looks up on the wall a picture of his own son who died a few years back in a car accident. ?You know Kid i wish he would have gotten to know you and you know him. I think you two would have liked one another.? Shaman looked at the picture making his mind up that that picture was going to stay. He had some friends in this world, but none like Tony. Tony was almost like a father to him. If it wasn't for his own dad dying in the Vietnam war Shaman would have never have known what it was like to have a father in Tony. Tony's wife always loved Shaman like a son. Shaman smiled a real smile for the first time in a longtime happy he was around Tony again. He had to make a decision as to what to do for Tony and his wife. He wanted them to never worry about money and that was the way he was in his heart. He drank the beer slowly as Tony continued wiping his glasses. Shaman just sat there in his familiar spot as some people looked in the window and recognized Shaman and walked on into the bar. They watched and admired him, but did not disturb him. They seemed to be content being in his presence. Tony leans over the bar slightly and whispers to Shaman. ?See kid you guys always had the magic to pull people into this place.? Shaman looked over at the people arriving and nodded to them. He turned back to his beer and looked at Tony smiling. ?I guess we do after all.? Shaman looks at Tony and raises his glass for another round.
