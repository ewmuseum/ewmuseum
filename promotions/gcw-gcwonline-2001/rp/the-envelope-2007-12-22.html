The Envelope

22 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/22/07 GCW wise, things had been going great for Tessa as of late. She’d managed to pull out a victory against GCW’s fastest rising star, Jay Terror, and then pinned one half of the longest reigning tag team Champions, Markus Stone the following week. Should she manage to beat Dorian Ryan this week in NC-17, Tessa would be able to consider herself ascending back up the treacherous mountain that was her wrestling career. Mentally and emotionally, things weren’t going as well for Tessa. She was still barely averaging four hours of sleep a night. She still had a fianc?, whom was barely around to help her with their family. And then of course there was still the thing that bothered her the most, the doubt surfacing around her love for her fianc?. Ever since she’d decided that she had wanted to make a name for herself, than live in her fianc??s shadows, rumors had flown about regarding her love for him. There were those that chose to believe Dorian Ryan’s accusations of her sleeping around with Branson King. Another batch believed she was jealous of how close Chris and Markus were. Then of course there was the main populous, which chose to believe that this was the end of her relationship before. Very seldom did she stumble across a fan who full heartedly believed that her intentions for Chris were good. The effect of it all seemed to be rubbing off even on her kids. "Mails in." Tessa’s head peered away from her three and half month old daughter to her thirteen year old niece who had more than handful of mail scattered across her arms length. "Anything for me?" GCW’s tiniest employees questioned, as she stuffed a pacifier in her babies mouth to keep her content for the second that she’d have to step away. Christa didn’t bother to respond to her mother at first. At the moment she was busy playing nosy preteen, sorting Tessa’s mail apart from all the rest. "Christa?" Tessa cued once more, tapping her niece turned daughter on the shoulder. Rather than turn and look at her guardian, Christa deposited the pile just for Tessa on the kitchen table. It wasn’t until she passed Tessa up heading back to her room that Christa finally gave her an answer, "Take a look." Automatically Tessa’s eyes darted in the direction of the larger of the two piles on the kitchen table. She knew for a fact that it wasn’t hers, but that didn’t kill her curiosity any. Like a kid on Christmas day, her hands dove for the larger pile, grabbing the first letter on top. She didn’t need x-ray vision to know what was inside of the envelope addressed to her fianc? Scott Drake, better known to the GCW world as Chris Storm. It reeked with the smell of Burberry for women, a scent usually linked to letters begging Chris to leave Tessa for random chick X. Instinctively, Tessa’s set of fresh acrylic nails dug into the envelope and tore it open without an ounce of concern about keeping it semi intact. Inside of it was nothing more than a typical fan letter, saying just about the same thing line for line that all the other ones before it had. Dear Chris, My name is Ashley. I’m an eighteen year old from St. Louis.I just want you to know that I am without a doubt your number one fan! I absolutely love watching you and Markus out there! You guys are so hot out there! Congrats so much on your title reign! I knew you guys could do it! Let me know if GCW ever comes to St. Louis. I’ll be sure to be there front row cheering you guys on! Oh and I’m sorry about the way Tessa is acting lately. She’s being a total bitch. I’d never treat you like that. She is so ungrateful. I hopes he gets what’s coming to her. If you ever go single, you know there are plenty of women like me just dying to date you. So don’t even worry about her. Good luck at NC-17 against the Establishment. Though you and Markus totally don’t need it. I love you guys! Lots of love, Ashley P.S. Merry Christmas. I hope you like my gift. Of course, nosy little Tessa wanted to find out just what the gift was. It wasn’t inside of the letter, which meant it only could have been in one other place. In less than an instant the envelope was in Tessa’s hand, upside down. "Oh my god!" Curiosity killed itself. It didn’t however kill the shriek that it emitted. In an instant, Christa was back in the kitchen to make sure that everything was fine. What she found was Tessa staring down at a black g-string which was lying on the ground. As odd as it was to see Tessa freaking out over a g-string lying on the ground, Christa thought nothing of it, "You opened Scott’s mail, didn’t you?" Tessa wouldn’t supply her with an answer, but she certainly wasn’t shy about giving her a command, "Throw that pile of mail away Christa!" "Don’t you think?" "THROW IT AWAY CHRISTA!" Tessa roared, as she tip toed her way over toward the pair of panties belonging to a total stranger. Christa was never one to be compliant when she knew Tessa was out of her mind. She reached over to grab the pile addressed to Scott and then took it one step further by grabbing Tessa’s pile as well. "Okay." She responded with a grin that only drove her aunt turned mother closer to insanity. "Not my mail!" Tessa shouted. Her attention quickly diverted away from the underwear and towards her niece, who let Tessa’s command fall on deaf ears, "Christa!" Christa turned and looked her aunt dead in the eye before slapping her with a dose of reality, "He gets mail like that all the time. Do you really think he cares about some random chick he’ll never meet?" Tessa could only try and feign her sanity at this moment, because it had long passed her by regardless of what she pleaded, "That’s not the point Christa." In terms of emotional intelligence, Christa was far beyond her thirteen years on this planet. She knew that if she toyed around enough with Tessa’s mind, she’d eventually snap her back to reality. So she gave it her first attempt, hanging the pile of mail over the trash can, "What’s the point then?" This time Tessa turned the deaf ear on Christa, "Christa, give me my mail!" "You have to give me my answer first." Christa responded back, tossing the top letter into the trash. Tessa, not knowing whether that letter belonged to her and Scott, dove towards Christa, hoping to fetch at least some of the letters out of her hand. Unfortunately for her, Christa apparently was already starting to take after her in the form of grace. Christa took a quick two steps back, leaving her aunt just short of her goal. "Christa!" Their argument, loud enough for the entire house to hear, had finally caught the attention of both Tessa’s adoptive son Evan and her step child, Chris. Both stood in the hallway outside of the kitchen with their eyes peering around the corner. "Why can’t you just tell me?" Christa herself finally reached her breaking point, shrieking in a high pitched voice. The boys, well accustomed to this drama, were more fascinated by the piece of black silk lying on the ground. "Why aren’t they picking it up?" The younger of the two boys, Chris, questioned his elder brother. "Like I know," Evan shrugged, before channeling the wit of his adoptive father, "maybe someone farted on it." Chris, only being seven, eyed the piece of fabric, trying to place what on earth it could be used for. It was an article of clothing for sure based on the tag on it. But it certainly didn’t look like it would fit anywhere on the body. Finally, he broke down and asked, "Where do you wear it?" Evan certainly wasn’t touching that one. Not with Tessa in full blown lioness mode. But he certainly wasn’t afraid to stir up some good fun up if Chris took the fall, "I think it goes on your head." "Your head?" Evan questioned his brother with a crinkled face. Either he wasn’t buying it or he was totally puzzled. "Like a mask." Evan suggested with a shrug of his shoulders. If his little brother would take the bait, it’d make for an absolutely golden moment. "Seriously?" "Go give it a shot. Those two are too busy fighting to even see you." Chris, even more na’ve than his three month old sister, tip toed his way across the kitchen and grabbed the underwear. All the while, Christa continued to dump letter after letter into the trash can as Tessa continued to fuss at her. Back behind the corner, Evan’s had to mask his mouth with his hands to stifle his laughter. If Tessa found out he instigated what was going on, he’d end up spending at least an hour or two in his room. "Fine! Throw them all away! You’re going to have to be the one that explain it to Scott." Tessa finally surrendered to Christa just as Chris was finally managing to wrap the g-string around his head. It came just in time for Tessa to turn on her heels and catch a glimpse of what was going on. "Chris!" She shrieked, her mouth falling agape, "Just what do you think you are you doing?" The look in his step mothers gave away Evan had succeeded in his gag. While Evan slipped away behind the corner, Chris looked up at Tessa wide eyed and speechless. Christa, who was thankful that she was out of the hot seat quickly found something else to occupy her time, leaving poor little Chris to deal with a highly agitated Tessa. Tessa’s eyes never left the smaller version of her fianc?. Chris shamefully removed the panties from his head, offering up a very meek, "Sorry." Just as his father, Chris had the power to make Tessa feel incredibly guilty with just one look. He dropped his lips down into a frown, let his head fall towards the ground, and put on the same pout his father would use to work his mother over. Tessa tried to block it out, but even the slightest glimpse provoked the guilt inside of her heart. She too dropped her head, "Just go find something to do." That said, Chris quickly found his way out of the kitchen, and went off to find his older brother to no doubt give him a dose of payback. "You can come home anytime now Scott." Tessa muttered to herself, obviously frustrated with all the chaos. She peered up at the clock by the stove and noticed that it was only 5:30pm. It’d still be another good hour or so before Scott got home. She sighed as she dropped back against the wall. There was no humanly way she could continue to do this job without any sort of help. Unfortunately for her, her kids had no intention of making anything easier on her. Especially not her niece, who may have been her number one fan, but was also her biggest critic. "I found this." Christa returned with an envelope in one hand, her free hand placed firmly on her hip. "What’s that?" Tessa questioned, studying it for any traces of profound evidence against her. "Let’s see." Reaching into it, Christa pulled out a small handful of one hundred dollar bills. "Oh shit." Tessa hated to curse even inside of her head, but the words nearly slipped out of her mouth as if she spoke them every day, "Where’d you find that Christa?" "Laying around inside one of the drawers in the back room a few days ago." She commented back casually, as she proceeded to count the number of one hundreds in her hand. She’d already reached five and it looked to be only halfway through. "Go put it back there." As cool, calm, and collected as Tessa tried to remain, it was obvious that something about that wad of cash was bothering her. Christa was easily able to pick up on that, "I bet Scott would be even more upset than you were about those panties if he found out about this." "It’s not what you think Christa." Hands extended out, she drew closer to her niece turned daughter hoping she’d surrender the envelope. Christa however seemed plenty content playing bad cop, "This is the money from Branson, isn’t it?" Tessa knew that lying to Christa would do little good. She played along with Christa’s game, drawing her hands back in surrender, "It is." Christa smirked, realizing just how big of deal this was, "This is all the money he gave you?" This time Tessa wasn’t so open. Hesitantly she answered, "Yes." Christa counted it to herself one last time. Once she got past ten, she stopped counting. Whatever Branson gave her was more than a thousand. It was certainly a large amount, but certainly not more than Tessa could have made on her own in just a weeks worth of time. "What are you planning on spending it on?" Christa tested, looking up at her mother, than back down at the pile of cash inside of her hand. "I don’t know Christa. I haven’t thought about it." Tessa replied with a heavy sigh. Even if she were to spend the money on the kids, everyone would still look at her as a villain. "So it’s true." As much as she wanted to tell Tessa off, all Christa could find in her heart was a very heavy disappointment. She looks up her adoptive mother, her biggest role model, the person who taught her to do what was right, and let her eyes fall right back down to the ground. Tessa tried save her eldest from the disappointment that was overwhelming her at the moment, "It isn’t true Christa. I’m not what you think I am." "You’re selling him out for money!" Christa shrieked, careless about who could hear her. "I am not Christa!" "Then why are you holding on to this?" This time Christa unleashed her anger, shoving the envelope along with the cash back into Tessa’s arms, "I’d think you’d have a bigger heart than that after all he’s done for you." "Christa?" Tessa tried to plead, but Christa’s ears were sealed shut. "When were you planning on telling everyone?" Right about now Tessa was cursing to herself for helping Christa become so mature. Oh how things were backfiring on her big time right now. "It’s not what you think." She tried to plead once more, only to be slammed back down with an even more difficult question. "Then what is it?" The second hand made one full rotation around the clock, then reached half way around the second time before any sort of noise was heard. It wasn’t Tessa’s argument or Christa laughing in Tessa’s face for proving her wrong, it was the sound of the front door opening. Things were about to get ten times more complicated. In came Scott with gym bag in hand. Clearly by the look on his face his day had been just as rough as Tessa’s. Christa needed not argue with her anymore to make a point. All she had to do is walk away, whispering one last retort in Tessa’s ears, "Good luck telling him what’s going on." She patted her mother on the back before heading into the living room, pretending as if it things were perfectly fine between the two. Instead Tessa stuffed the money into the front pocket of the hooded fleece she was wearing. She wasn’t going down like that. Not here, not now. She wasn’t the villain that everyone made her out to be. "Hey angel." Scott abruptly greeted just as she pulled her hands out of her pockets, "How was your day?" From the doorway of the living room Christa watched intently, waiting for Tessa to fess up. Rather than answer, Tessa wrapped her arms around Scott, pressing her head up against chest, "I missed you." Christa simply let out another sigh, returning back to the loveseat in the living room. There was no use in pushing the issue. Not when her mother could take a fall on an even grander stage when Branson King grew tired of her. If Tessa wouldn’t confess to Scott right now, she’d have to fall the hard way, with no one there to cushion her fall. ?Sometimes?, as Tessa once told Christa, ?people need to learn the hard way.” This time, it was Tessa’s turn.
