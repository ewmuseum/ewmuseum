The Following

30 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/30/11 Everything has finally settled down and the calming has come. Robyn and Detective Jordan were both forced to make a decision regarding their life. While neither of them wanted to do the wrong thing, I forced them to decide as, Robyn's daughter, Katie was locked inside a burning cage. After seeing what was at stake Robyn was quick to make the decision as she fired the gun and the life of the Detective was no more. As his lifeless body was dumped into the river I reminded Robyn of the problems she caused me. I made sure she understood that there was a price to be paid for the problems I had to fix. When our conversation was over she agreed to my circumstances, but before our meeting was finalized I made sure she knew what happened should she try to act against me again. Life seemed to be coming to happier times as Katrina and I were reunited with a new chance to start our life together again. All of my deepest secrets she learned about as she sliced all of the webs of my past, connecting all of the dots along the way, and she eventually learned of the monster I am. Katrina didn't run from all of the nightmarish tales I told her, she did the exact opposite and thirsted for more. Now that she knew everything it all seemed much more comfortable to be together. All of this, I eventually learned, was orchestrated by one man. Dorn Tracer is his name and he is the sole leader of a group called The Following. His coalition of followers string along based on principles written down by a woman known only as “She” and in a book she tells stories of her life. Everyone believes her words to be stronger than anything else, as it allows them to be filled with a reason to live. Dorn Tracer is an amazing man that has saved the life of many. It is through the preaching of her word that he has brought Katrina and myself together again. Not only did he save our relationship, he saved my life and showed me exactly what lies inside the souls of humanity. He also showed me how far the roots of The Following go into everyday society. The Following is an amazing underground network for those who are willing to see the truth, and we all owe our life to Father Tracer for showing us the way. Long live The Following. - The wind is whistling as the darkness closes in with the setting of the sun as a storm is nearing. In the distant horizon a black car is seen driving forward with a trail of dust waving up behind it. It comes to a stop in a front of an old church and the doors soon open. From the passenger's side Katrina steps out in a black dress with a dark veil covering her face. Opposite of her from the driver's side comes Donny, dressed a lot nicer than normal, but still wearing his traditional black trench coat. “Don't be so sad my love. Tonight is not a night for tears of sorrow, instead be happy because it is a night for celebration.” He places his arm around her and together the two of them walk towards the doors. He nods to the guards at each side as they open the doors welcoming them inside. Inside everything is lit by candlelight and across pews are several members of The Following. Down the middle Donny and Katrina walks until she takes a seat at the front while he walks up onto the stage. As he removes his sunglasses and places it on the podium his eyes survey the scene. “My brothers and my sisters I ask for you to lend me a moment of your time tonight to list to what I have to say. The Following has always been a home to anyone that is prepared to see the truth of humanity and the world. We have all made sacrifices for her word and made sacrifices to repay Father Tracer for showing us the way of righteousness.” Members of The Following can be seen all nodding in agreement. “Brother Casey, I remember when you once took a bullet in the arm to protect Father Tracer from an angry mob. Brother Jeremy, I recall the story Father Tracer told me of how you gave The Following all of your money and sold your company so that this very building could be built to give new members a home. Sister Angela, how could we forget the story of you having the courage to go on live television and preach her word to the world. Each and every one of you have made a sacrifice to pay Father Tracer back for showing you a new path in life. I, however, can hear the whispers as you all wonder what Father Tracer has sacrificed for you all.” Rain can be heard starting to hit against the windows as everyone begins to gossip over the words just spoken. “Well I can personally tell you all of the sacrifices Father Tracer has made for us all. I can also tell you of the sacrifices there were made for me to escape the problems we recently all faced. Instead I will take this time to tell you all the story of how Father Tracer saved me.” ...A little over a year ago... Doors to an elevator open and out walks Dorn Tracer dressed in a suit and tie. He walks down the bright white hallway passing a few employees on the way. At the end of the hallway he knocks on the door that reads “Jason Nickles”. He walks inside and closes the door behind him as he takes a seat in a chair in front of Jason who smiles from behind his desk. “Hello Mr. Tracer it is so nice for us to finally meet face-to-face.” “Indeed it is Mr. Nickles.” “Sorry about all the security you had to go through before you were able to get down here. It's standard protocol I assure you. With the type of work we do here, we cannot let anyone this far inside without first being cleared. I do however believe that it all will be worth it once you see what we managed to fine.” “Or instead of beating around the bush you could just tell me.” “No, Mr. Tracer this is something you need to see. Come with me sir, it's just down the hall.” Jason gets up from his seat and walks towards the door motioning for Dorn to follow him. Getting up from his chair Dorn walks past the now opened door and follows Jason down the hall. The two of them stop in front of a door and Jason inputs the key code into the number pad and it opens. As the door opens they walk inside the dark room where different personnel can be seen walking back and forth from a back room. The two of them walk towards it, and in the middle of the room is a man strapped to a bed. Dorn and Jason hover over him as several tubes can be seen connected to him. His body is covered in ash and burns. Nurses tend to his wounds and fluids are pushed into him, to make sure he remains in stable condition. “I don't know what any of this is, nor what it has to do with me. I do however know you have the wrong person, because we do not dwindle in science projects.” “Science? No. This is in fact the exact opposite. See, my organization deals in finding ancient religious artifacts. We got word a few weeks ago of a very big find in a Satanic temple deep underground in Italy. Inside there was tons of stuff we were looking for, but when we got down into the basement we found him sealed inside a marble casket.” “And what exactly does this have to do with me?” “We know of your organization and about what you guys do. After lots of reading and testing we figured out just who this man is, but some of the things we need to do in order to revitalize him will wipe his memory clean. So the offer we are providing is we will fund money to your organization if you will care for him and follow the instructions provided.” “Understood. Just tell me one thing, what are you guys planning on doing with him?” “I'm afraid we cannot mention any of that information at this time. We shall arrange everything and we will be in contact with you very soon.” ...A few weeks pass... The room is dim with light as Dorn sits in a wooden chair alongside the bed where this man is still resting. In the hands of Dorn is a small black book that he is reading until suddenly the sleeping man opens his eyes and struggles to try and sit up. Dorn quickly places his hands on his chests forcing him to remain lying down in the bed till he can regain some composure. “Relax my friend, no one here is going to hurt you.” Dorn takes a seat back into his chair and watches him closely as he gasps for air and his eyes search the room around him. “Your name is Donny Diamond. We found you in an abandoned building unconscious, so we took you back here. You've been in a coma for months.” Slowly Donny sits up on the edge of the bed as his head is still dizzy in confusion. “This place where you have been slumbering is known as the home to The Following. When the world has turned its back on its children, we take them in and show them her word. We allow them to see that while the world may have chosen to turn the other way, she hasn't and she still loves us all.” Donny tries to speak, but instead starts to cough. “Don't try and speak my brother, there will be plenty of time for you to do that. Now is the time for you rest. We will take care of you and show you the type of life she can give you, and we will also show you that we would never let you suffer like that world has made you do. “ He starts to become weak again and lays back down on the bed as Dorn helps him sip a glass of water. Dorn takes a seat back down in the chair and begins to read from the book to Donny. “She said: “On the darkest of days there will be a single ray of light. You will then follow the light to a soul that has been left for dead by the people he once trusted and loved. That soul shall be weak and seeking for nutrition, feed my word to those weakened souls so that they can be saved.”” ...Present... “My brothers and my sisters that is the story of how Father Tracer saved me. And I'm sure he has done to the same to each and every one of you! Her word is a beautiful thing isn't it?” Everyone nods in agreement to the words Donny just spoke as he raises the book of her word into the air. With his free hands he moves a lighter towards the book and sets it on fire. As he holds the flaming book in the air everyone begins to stand in anger and walk towards the stage while Katrina climbs up next to Donny. “Wait! Wait I say! I understand all of you are angered at the sight of me burning her book, but my brothers and sisters you really should be angered at Father Tracer instead! He has burned us all!” Katrina knocks the burning book to the floor and dumps a bucket of water over it as she turns her gaze back to the angry mob before them. “For The Following I lay my life on the line to bring glory to her name. Then at the Belmont, Father Tracer left me for dead. That is when I knew Dorn Tracer is nothing more than a common liar, and that her word is nothing more than a good bed time story. That is when it all started coming back to me.” Donny steps out from behind the podium and grabs the charred book. “Dorn Tracer is never coming back to us, because Dorn Tracer never was our leader. Her word was never heard of until you met him, because she has never existed. But please do not let fear enter your hearts, because The Following is not a lie! The Following is not without a leader!” With Katrina the two of them step off the stage and walk down the middle of the rows of pews. “All of the principles and fundamentals in which we have reconstructed our lives on are pure. The Following was indeed created to shine light on a higher power and to rework the world into understanding the truth. That higher power then selected a soul he deemed worthy to lead this new army and that soul was me! Someone may have managed to erase my memory, but my soul has never forgotten and together we shall make this world a better place. And to those that have may tried to disband us from the core, let's send them a message!” He raises the charred book into the air and rips open the cover where some wires and a camera lens falls out. The doors to the church open wide as the wind forces rain to come in and the candles near the doors to go out. Bolts of lightning strike down rapidly filling the room with an eerie glow. “With the strength of the Dark Lord himself we shall seek out these pawns and make them suffer. And together the world shall come to fear us all!”
