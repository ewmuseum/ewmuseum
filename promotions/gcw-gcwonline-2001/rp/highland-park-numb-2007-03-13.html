Highland Park (Numb)

13 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 13, 2007 I'm tired of being what you want me to be Feeling so faithless Lost under the surface I don't know what you're expecting of me Put under the pressure Of walking in your shoes Caught in the undertow Just caught in the undertow Every step that I take is another mistake to you I've become so numb I can't feel you there Become so tired So much more aware I'm becoming this All I want to do Is be more like me And be less like you Can't you see that you're smothering me Holding too tightly Afraid to lose control Cause everything that you thought I would be Has fallen apart right in front of you - "Numb" by Linkin Park ---- Chapter One: House Warming ---- One by one the street lights passed by during the March sunset. The pinkish sky overhead was unseasonable for the suburbs, all the small children lined the streets dressed in jeans and t-shirts. Some were bouncing on trampolines, others were passing a football back and forth, eagerly awaiting the April draft for their beloved Bears. It was amazing that no matter how much things in the life of an individual changes, life goes on. Whether you want to believe it does or not. Looking out from the passenger side of a large moving truck, she locked eyes with the children as she passed... staring at her entourage and gawking as they passed by. Remembering the days from her youth she smirked a little at the attention. She once did the same as a child, wondering if the new neighbor may be Michael Jordan or Oprah. Now that she cared much for either of them, but knowing someone famous always seemed to be a big deal in Highland Park, Illinois. A city based on reputation and wealth. For someone named The Fearless Phenom, she sure had a lot of butterflies in her stomach. She'd faced men nearly twice her size without backing down. Yet the thought of facing her past might be the biggest challenge she's ever faced. The moving truck slowed as they approached a beautiful two-story home. Modest for most people with the checkbook of Vivica J. Valentine, but it just seemed right. The moving truck came to a stop in the driveway, right in front of her new two-car garage. Which would be the new home of her Black Chrysler Crossfire, not too far behind her in the convoy. As the engine turned off, she began to have second thoughts. Why come back here? Why come back to a place she tried so desperately for years to get away from. It was her own personal hell, but would be the dream of many young girls growing up. Growing up as the daughter to two wealthy parents, getting anything you could possibly ask for (as long as it was appropriate for a female child). Leaving home and falling in love with a rich and powerful businessman. She was set for life, and threw it all away. Time and time again. Searching for answers in her life but never finding herself. It was the first time in her entire life that she was going to be alone. The thought of not having anyone to hold her hand was liberating, but frightened her at the same time. "Miss Valentine." The voice of the truck driver startled her. His name was Derek Taylor. He was a friend of a backstage employee of GCW, who just happened to grow up in the Chicago area. Vivica looked out the window at the children next door staring at her possessions, gauging her worth. Maybe they were wondering if it was Vivica J. Valentine, just like she wondered about other celebrities long ago, "We're here." "Sorry..." Vivica opened the door before stepping outside, into the full view of the kids and now their parents on the front porch. She looked back at them and waved a bit, they probably had no idea who she was but waved enthusiastically anyway. The thought of fame and being a celebrity still seemed silly to Vivica. Even though she was raised in wealth, fame was something different entirely. It was the dream of almost every little girl. Especially those raised in Highland Park. "Hey Derek?" The Fearless Phenom questioned the truck driver as he opened the back door of the vehicle in an attempt to unload everything he could before the sun finally fell over the horizon and the pink sky turned to one of darkness. "Yeah?" The truck driver stopped his task at head, albeit reluctantly. "You go around a lot, moving people to different homes and what not. Do you know if a Melissa Grishop still lives around here?" Vivica asked. "The name doesn't sound familiar, but there are a lot of people in this city... you could always look her up." Derek answered before jumping into the back of the truck. Soon a couple of his employees followed him, beginning to unstrap her belongings as others opened the door the check out the area. There were a lot of people in the city, people always got lost in the shuffle. Although she'd never forgotten her time in Highland Park, it'd forgotten about her a long time ago. ---- Chapter Two: Runaway ---- Years earlier... 2:00 AM A full moon hung high above the city of Highland Park, Illinois. Things were quiet in the high end suburb of Chicago, the sound of crickets chirping filled the air and the lightning bugs flew around in the air mimicking the stars above. Staring out of her window was a wide awake 17 year old girl. Her cerulean eyes looked back at her in the window as she waited. With her right hand against the side of her face, the fingers on her left hand tapped the window sill impatiently. A sigh escaped her lips as she watched the lightning bugs dance around her front lawn. Looking back, the teenager glanced at two pieces of luggage sitting in the corner. While most people would be scared, she was excited. Getting away from the confines of Highland Park and social hierarchy involved was like breaking out of prison. All she needed was one hundred bucks in her pocket, two suitcases full of clothes and belongings, and her own dreams. Walking across the room she glanced down an impromptu Polaroid photo taken from earlier in the evening. Prom night, when most girls get to be socialites for a day and bask in the glow of how popular they've become. It was no different for young Vivica J. Valentine, although basking was not quite the word for it. It was more like suffering. The prom queen sash she'd receive hanging from a lamp in the corner of the room was just another symbol of her mother's accolades. Living the childhood she never had through the actions of her daughter. Unlike most teenagers who are brainwashed with images of what they should be and what they should act like from the images in magazines and television shows, Vivica had picked up her mother's stage mom mentality from a young age. While she pursued gymnastics and the thrill of competition, her mother constantly pushed social etiquette down her throat. Pushing her towards more boring pursuits like ballet and piano lessons. Although eventually her mother learned to live with gymnastics, mainly due to the popularity it grew. One of the most scouted young girls in the nation, Vivica was supposed to report to USA Gymnastics tryouts in just a few months. As a pair of long awaited headlights rounded the corner, Valentine's heart skipped a beat. Highland Park High School's Prom Queen had a different idea. No more would she be forced into such puppet shows like prom night, homecoming, and beauty contests. Growing up and becoming a famous actress or singer was not on her agenda. As a matter of fact nothing was on her agenda, and that was the way she wanted it. Absolute freedom. Sneaking out the door carrying two suitcases, she knew she wasn't going to wake her parents. Her father had a big case in the morning and was already in his office preparing downtown. Being a workaholic lawyer he never really catered to Vivica's needs, or acknowledged her existence for that matter. As a matter of fact it'd be months before Jack Valentine even found out his daughter had become prom queen that night. Her mother was so proud of her own accomplishment. Shutting the door behind her, Vivica stepped forth into her new life. Leaving the suppression of the Valentine household behind her. Opening the passenger side door, she threw the luggage into the back seat. Sitting down "Jeremy" by Pearl Jam played over the radio, and the driver still had her hair up and a prom dress on. She'd left an after prom party to come pick up Vivica in the middle of the night, and although happy to help her friend, Melissa Grishop was noticeably rushed. "You're out of your prom dress already?" Melissa questioned, surprised. "Yeah, I got rid of the prison rags." Vivica answered as the car started down the street. She didn't even look at her house as it faded away in the darkness behind her. Her eyes were focused forward, towards the future. "You know, this is probably going to be the greatest time in our lives." Melissa tried to talk some last minuted sense into her friend, now realizing this might be the last time she ever sees the girl she grew up with since they were little girls, "I'd hate for something bad to happen to you Vivica, I'm really worried. You don't even have a hotel reservation." "Don't worry about me, I'm ecstatic." You could see the excitement in the eyes of the girl who would grow up to be The Black Mariah years later. Her heart beat faster and faster as each mile ticked away. She watched the street lights pass her by, one by one. "You know a lot of girls would DIE to be you tonight." Melissa stated, still holding on to some sort of hope Vivica would back out at the last minute. "That's the difference between me and most girls." Vivica responded, never taking her eyes off the passenger side window watching Highland Park go away, "I don't want to be worshiped or famous or popular... I just want to be me. I want to live my life, doing things that make me happy for the rest of my life. As long as I'm here, I can never really be myself. If I go to one more school dance and act like I give a shit about the other girls there just to make my mom happy I'd shoot myself. I'm serious." "I just don't understand you..." Melissa says, glancing over at her best friend before looking back at the road. "Chicago is dangerous you know. People get killed down here. What if you become homeless and die on the street? What if you get shot on accident? What if you starve to death Vivica?" "It's a chance I'm going to have to take." Vivica took a deep breath before going on, "I'd rather die being myself than live being just another stepford daughter." "If my parents find out I did this they'll KILL me." Melissa said going a little bit faster, not drastically more worried that her participation in this runaway girl case will be found out. "That's exactly what I'm talking about..." Vivica said, "Who gives a fuck you know? You can always come with me. It'll be fun." "I can't..." Melissa says, starting to cry about losing her friend, "I can't do it." Melissa pulls over just outside the city limits, she looks over at Vivica before talking between sobs. "Leave Vivica... before I change my mind and head back." "Thanks Melissa..." Vivica said as she opened the door and the back door behind it, grabbing her luggage out of the backseat of the four door sedan. "Maybe this is some childish rebellion thing I'm going through, or maybe it's my destiny. No matter what happens to me, I won't forget what you've done for me tonight. If I ever come to my senses and come back to Highland Park, I promise you'll be the first person I call." Slamming the door shut, the teenager who'd grow up to become The Fearless Phenom watched as the car drove away. The last bond between her and the "best years of her life" was gone. Being a rebel had never felt so good. ---- Chapter Three: Remembrance ---- Back in the present... 2:00 AM Sitting here inside my own house... here in Highland Park, Illinois. Who would've ever thought the day would come? Outside my window I still hear the crickets chirping, it's too early for fireflies still but in time they will come. Whether I'd like to admit it to myself or not, maybe deep down inside I have grown up a little and realized that I'm not quite the rebellious badass that I once thought I was a long time ago. How I'm sitting here today is a miracle in itself, but I guess along the way I've had people watching me. Just like Melissa years ago, I had people like Christian Zenith and Lia Ambrosi to hold my hand along the way. Temporarily pointing my in the direction of the light and letting me choose my own path in life, just to turn around and bitchslap them for their efforts. I hope one day Lia and I can sit down and talk about this and laugh, I hope that one day she'll understand that I have to do this for me. It's time I grow up and stop living through the lives of others. It's time I stopped being Melissa's best friend, Christian's fiance, or Lia's stablemate. I looked up to all of them. I looked up to the way Melissa turned out to be such a good person even though her dad was an abusive alcoholic. You'd never guess by talking with her that she was beaten nearly her entire life before her dad was sent to prison. The way Christian Zenith could command a crowd, and make things move out of his way with the shift of a finger. He is extraordinarily brilliant, beyond ways that I can describe... even though he's a selfish asshole. The way Lia has an infectious confidence and selflessness that radiates down to others. Somehow she's managed to bring a group of inherently selfish individuals for a cause, no matter how moot or twisted it may have become along the way. She demanded respect. I even looked up to Dawn McFarlane once upon a time. She was the pinnacle of what every woman in my line of work could be. She is athletic, beautiful, and intelligent. Everything that any teenage girl wishes she could grow up to be some day. I watched helplessly as she mowed through me on he way to become a champion. I'd be a liar if I didn't tell you even I was a little star struck at first. Unfortunately she was just as selfish as she was talented. When I was in need of a strong woman in my life she walked out on me and Lia both in the Hunting Grounds. Months later she'd be in a group that approached me to join them in GCW, I decided to tag along for the ride. At the beginning of our tenure she was better than me, and she knew it. Constantly she'd talk down to me like a child, act like she was out of my league and I'd never be as good as she was. Never given the benefit of the doubt and never given the respect I deserved. She outcasted me like all those bitches did in high school. My new life had become my prison, the one I thought I'd escaped from years ago. So I threw the towel in Virgil's face and I ran away, just like I always did. Leave my problems in the dust and never look back. Maybe if I ignore them they'll just go away. Sitting in a hotel room, and thinking about my life ate at me from the inside. I talk about achieving my destiny, fulfilling my dreams, and the hopes of the 10,000 people who pay to watch me bust my ass every night. They depended on me. Spending their hard earned dollars just to get a glimpse of watching me living my dream. One of these days when the pressure gets so much, and I get closer and closer to my dreams. Am I just going to turn my back and runaway when it gets hard, just like I always do? I guess that's why I came back here... no matter how much I deny it, every week over the intercom it's announced Highland Park is my home. It's where I came from. I can't deny it. I have to turn around and embrace it, and quit running away from my problems. I have to fight them head on like a big girl, because life isn't easy... sometimes it's going to get tough. Although I thought the first steps in my new life were leaving Highland Park a long time ago, I've found out that the real first steps in my new life were coming back home. A place I belong. Just like I've come back to face my problems from my past, I'm coming back to face my problems in the present. Starting with you Dawn McFarlane. No longer will I let you walk all over me, and treat me like a child. I'm going to prove that the sky is the limit for me, and just like how I was the first step on your road to the championship... you'll be the first step on mine. I won't run away this time, I have millions of people who depend on me. Millions of people who I'll be damned if they are let down... no matter how hard my road may be. I'm going to win this one for all the people along the way I've let down. For all the Melissa's out there, and most of all... for myself. Who I've let down the most of all.
