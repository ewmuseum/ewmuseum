Another enemy

16 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/16/2009 It’s an interesting experience to be questioned by the police. When I was suspected of murder in New York they pulled out all the stops. There was the good cop - bad cop routine that you see on TV all the time. It was only my frequent viewing of shows such as NYPD Blue, and Law and Order that allowed me to realize that the friendly police officer telling me to reveal all I know was not actually my friend. I finally realized that my best bet was to just ask for a lawyer; if I hadn’t they would have broken me before too long. But the police in Ohio are much less impressive. “So let me see if I have this straight,” says the detective. He’s a chubby man, but something about him tells me that his physique is more a result of genetics than his eating habits. “Lorenzo Vasquez was busy beating his wife when you decided to stop him by smashing his head into the ceiling.” Technically Lorenzo Vasquez was busy kicking his wife to death when I slammed his head into a wall. It would have been very difficult for me to slam his head into the ceiling…but somehow I don’t think the police care about such minor difficulties. Right now I am stuck inside the interrogation room of the police station. It’s a cozy place with some lovely chipped green paint and the obligatory two-way mirror. I have not said anything to the police, but that hasn’t stopped them from trying to get everything they can out of me. This last guy has to be the fourth guy to have a go at me, and I swear he’s copying the style of the very first one. Ah well, they probably have a bet on it or something. My uncle was the one who told them why I decided to kick Lorenzo’s ass. But since Lorenzo knows a few people, I was the one who was brought in for questioning. But I am not being bothered that much I suppose. Matter of fact, I am suffering mostly from boredom as I wait for my uncle to get me that lawyer he promised. You might ask why I haven’t just asked for a lawyer myself and brought this entire fiasco to a close. It could be because the décor in the interrogation room is better than the three gray walls I’d find in a jail cell. It could also be because I enjoy watching the interviewer’s face get red as he yells at me to answer the question. But mostly…it’s because I don’t want to be locked into a jail cell with those two guys that stared at me a little too intensely as I walked by. For the record my entire predicament can be blamed on my uncle. He was the one who arranged for me to attend a dinner with that Lorenzo bastard knowing that Lorenzo would probably beat on his wife. Now some people might say that my uncle was doing a good deed by helping Lorenzo’s wife escape an abusive husband. However, all I can focus on is the fact that my uncle arranged for me to beat up a close personal friend of both the mayor and chief of police. I give a cheeky grin to the officer, whose face gets even redder as a result. Now he’s threatening to lock me up for twenty years or something…honestly I’ve stopped caring. I feel oddly detached from my body…almost as if I am floating up and away from everything. The room has fallen oddly silent- I can see the officer’s lips moving but no sound seems to be coming out. I stand; or I think I do at any rate. The room has begun to swirl around me and it’s difficult to figure out just which way is up. Sound has returned to my senses in the form of a faint annoying buzzing that grates in my ears. Then everything begins to fade; my eyes close despite my efforts to keep them open. One day later…. My eyes open slowly, and I see the stupidly grinning face of my uncle Erik hovering above me. There is a great deal of stubble on his face, and his breath tells me that he hasn’t bothered to brush his teeth for some time. “Glad to see you’re awake,” he says with obvious false cheer. He might have pulled it off with someone who doesn’t know him better, but I can see the dark circles under his eyes telling me that he hasn’t slept in a long while. Slowly I become aware of various tubes running through me. One is attached to the crook of my elbow, one runs through my nose, and one is injected right above my wrist. Not trusting myself to speak I look at my uncle questioningly. He visibly grits his teeth but I silently demand to know what has happened to me. Like the week before he lowers his eyes first and tells me what I want to know. “I don’t even know what’s happened to you,” he admits ashamedly. “The bastards that treated you kept giving me such vague answers that I’m not sure they even know what happened to ya. From what I could gather they just pumped you with as much fluids and medication as they could safely combine and hoped to hell that it would work. Anger and a hint of fear give me the energy I need to respond. “This is exactly the shit that I had been hoping to avoid when I came here,” I gasp out. For some reason it’s harder to breathe than normal and just saying that sentence has left me huffing in air. “If I’m still like these when Rampage rolls around Jake Reed is going to tear me completely apart,” I whisper as my body starts to shake. I want to yell at him to call in a nurse, a doctor, anyone who can put me back into the shape I need to be in at Rampage. It would be bad enough to lose to Reed, but to lose to him because I was (probably) poisoned takes the cake. Erik pushes down on my chest forcing me to lie back on the bed; it’s not hard to do considering how week I am. ”Lorenzo did not do this,” he answers me quietly, noticeably making sure that no one else can hear what he says. “The man’s no killer; besides he has the pull to make your life hell for the next twenty to thirty years. Moreover, whoever did this would have had to do it during the dinner with Lorenzo according to the doctors. It’s just as likely Lorenzo was the target and you were just the only one unlucky enough to eat whatever they had poisoned. Anyway, I’ve already talked to the doctors who covered you. They say as long as you get plenty of rest you should be more or less fine for your match with Reed.” What the hell does “more or less” mean anyway. That expression has been around for years, but I still don’t understand how it’s actually supposed to be used. How could I possibly be more ready for Reed than I was before I landed in the hospital? And if I am less ready…then by how great a degree? I sigh and stop trying to force myself up and off of the bed. No matter how much I dislike it, the reality of my situation is clear. If I want any chance of being back to my old self by the time Rampage rolls around I am basically going to have to be bedridden for a good long while. “So,” I say numbly as I try to absorb it all. “I don’t suppose you have any theories on who tried to do me in since you’ve so eloquently ruled out your good friend, the wife abuser.” He answers me, but either the meds or pure fatigue is kicking in. I am too tired to bother even trying to comprehend what he’s saying, and instead I allow myself to fall back asleep. I know that when I wake up I will still face a host of problems. I have become the arch enemy of one of the best connected guys in town, and he has the officials gunning for me. It appears that once again someone may want me dead, and this time I have no idea who or why, or even if I was the actual target. Not to mention I am probably not going to be at a hundred percent at Rampage because of this entire fiasco. But none of it matters. I distinctly remember a dream I had as I slept in that hospital bed. I was lying there, and one by fucking one every failure I ever experienced came to hunt me. It started out small, nothing more than the tests I failed in High School to my failures in the ring. My repeated losses to Taylor…my lost chance at the US title, everyone who ever beat me decided to pay me a visit. Then the big ones come along. I relive my decision to get back together with a girl who was pregnant with another man’s child. My subsequent choice to break up with her about a day before the baby was due does not play too well inside my mind either. Then comes my abandonment of her child after the girl dies in labor…which come to think of it started this whole big mess in the first place. But, strangely enough the dream ends on a positive note. Sure, it does not end with a list of my accomplishments or some other positive message that would give a nice happy ending to this story. Rather it ends with an image that warms my heart. Jake Reed bloodied, bruised, and decimated lying on the floor of the ring. My arm is held high by the ref, but I am staring at Reed with unbroken concentration. In my other hand is a steel chair…ready to make sure the job is finished. Somehow, I think my body will be ready when Rampage rolls around.
