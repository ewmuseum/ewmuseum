Deliver me from EVL : Part Six- Tainted Love part one

9 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/09/2005 No tall tales time to understand A sinister clan has a master plan They're dedicated to evil intent Into vulnerable lives is where they're sent Their cause Is hate They'll prey On the weak Perverting values and God they abort Morals and order their goal to distort They pray and fast in diligent attack For the pleasure to see a righteous man turn back God's power They shun Separations Begun.... --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Everytime you come here you ask me for the same story and each time I have avoided the telling of it. Today, however, I am ready to speak on the events that unfolded between the dark knight and I. Or rather... Lleyes and Eve. What? ...... Yes yes, I know as if Lestatt wasn't enough. What can I say? I have a bad habit of falling for the bad ones. However, rest assured, after what happened with Lestatt...never again. ..... Yeah I know... Famous last words. ANYway, let me tell you the background story to Lleyes and Eva first. Then I will tell you what happened when I was Eve. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- We were on our way to Sim'sar. The trip was done by ship as the city was on an island. Actually the city was the island. For three weeks we sailed through all kinds of weather. Calm days with no wind, dangerous storms that threatened to flip the ship on its side. I had never experienced the likeness of those storms before, not literally anyway. In retrospect, I have to say the storms reminded me of the chaos in my mind over the past few months. We barely escaped a monstrous whirlpool and in doing so, we lost several good men and one of my ships. It was during these travels that Cedric thought it prudent to tell me of my past again. He spoke of the dark knight and my ellicit affair with the man who eventually betrayed me. As it turned out, Eva was as much a fool as I am. Her first encounter with him had been at the battle of Eclipsia. This was before Eva became the 'chosen one' of Calderia. She was a new addition to the global wars, a face never before seen by the veteran warriors. It had been Cedric who had brought her into the fray, hoping to use her beauty to distract the leaders of the opposing armies. His ploy had worked on everyone, well almost everyone. When the battle started, one of the armies was lead by the dreaded dark knight. He was a formiddable swordsman, known throughout the lands for his focus and his perfect technique. He was her primary target. In the midst of battle she made her way into his sights taking on the part of helpless female barely able to defend herself against the hordes who kept coming against her. She appeared weak and at the same time, courageous. When he saw her, he saw an easy target. Immediately the two paired off, swords in hand as they began a volley of blows. She attempted to use her womanly wiles to gain an advantage but failed miserably and was forced to fight. In the end he gained the upper hand and forced her to her back as he came down like a vengeful demon intent on ending her life. Luckily for her, her brother interfered having appeared out of no where and clocked him across the back of his head rendering the knight unconscious. They had tried to take him prisoner but his men were too close and as a result they merely escaped to avoid being taken themselves. A few weeks later Eva had another startling encounter with the man who she had come to know as Lleyes. On a chilled night at her ancestral home he appeared. She and her brother were hosting a masquerade and Lleyes had invited himself. In her dressing chambers Eve was preparing for the ball when she felt the presence of another and turned to find his shadowed figure lurking in the corner. When she would have called for help he stopped her. The pair had exchanged words then he vanished as quickly as he appeared. Eve was not one to let such things go, and so, she turned the tables on him not three nights later. From there it became a game. Then the game became something else. The two were attracted to each other. He was attracted to her vibrant beauty and reckless ways and she was attracted to his dark nature and wickedness. Before anyone could stop them, the two had become lovers. For the first months things seemed fine. They were inseperable and together they laid waste to many a city as they crusaded for that ultimate prize. It was when Eve's past came back to haunt her that things began to go terribly wrong for the misguided love birds. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Sent to the chapel on an early Sunday morn Just to show the arrogance and scorn Demonstrating defiance determined to mock and resist The satanic visitation is easy to be missed To see The church Deceived To fall The marriages seemed to have a chance until one arrived To offer immorality and infidelity disguised Molesters and abusers watching over our little ones But all accounts invalid because they're the young? They know The game To discredit To shame.... --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Eve nearly died in an encounter with a few of her brother's old collegues. He had stolen some precious jewels from them and turned around and sold them off on the blackmarket. Unfortunately for her, when they came to collect them back, he didn't have them and as a result she was taken captive and brutally tortured in reprise. Lleyes had found her half dead in the middle of the Tarquian Desert. He did the dutiful thing and got her to clerics..but after that he vanished. And so had her brother. Eve, much like myself, had been left to fend for herself and that was when her destiny changed. Upon recovering Eve made her way back to the capital city of Calderia and met Mystra for the first time. Mystra changed her fate. Gone was the dark Eve reborn as a new soul, with a new goal..and still her love for Lleyes was strong. These were tumultuous times for the warrioress. Her time spent with the dark knight dwindled to almost nothing and during that time his old lover had returned. It was just after this transition that I returned to Eve's form once again... --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ' So you are telling me I loved this man and he betrayed me....nearly killed me because his lost lover returned?' ' Aye, Eve, that is exactly what I am telling you.' It was like reliving the nightmare with Lestatt and Mary all over again. For me it was twice the blow to my heart and my ego. I couldn't hold onto my man in my own world and as it turned out I couldn't do it in this other world either. Go Eva go. Scowling my eyes searched the horizon as we neared the port of Sim'sar. ' We should be docking soon. Ready the ship for docking.' ' Cap'n.... you seem distracted now. Are ye okay?' I turned and looked Cedric in the eyes with a stoney silence. Finally I merely patted his shoulder and offered a tight smile. ' It is nothing I can't handle Le...Cedric. I will be fine. I swear.' He nodded and left me there to ponder on my bad fortune. I couldn't get a break in either world. In GCW anything that could go wrong had been and in that world it seemed it wasn't any better. I was in hell, I had to be. Well I wished it were that easy, but I knew better. If it had been hell things would be a lot worse. I should never have thought that..because they did just that...they got worse. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Danger lies in waiting for destruction stay Disbelief their victory as ignorance doesn't pay No fabricated story or paranoia fear Just the assassin in the midst knowing they're near Don't discount the satanic alliance sent to deceive Darkened disciples are plotting domination to achieve Wisdom and discernment is where the battle's won Allowing God's intervention let his will be done..... --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Out of no where his voice came. ' So we meet again, my sweet....sweet Eve.' Slowly I turned and faced the man whom had tried to kill me not once, not twice, but three times and thus far had failed. Until that point I had no idea what he looked like, but instinct told me the tall dark and handsome man lurking in the shadows was none other then Lleyes, the very man whom Eve had had the bad fortune of falling in love with. Without hesitation my hand found its way to the dagger slung in my belt as I regarded him through hatred's eyes. As he came into the light his face melted into that of Lestatt's and I growled. I wanted to kill him where he stood. ' Lleyes...' ' Come back to beg Eve? Perhaps you wish for more of that which only I can offer?' ' You can offer me nothing I want other than your own death you vile piece of' He laughed devilishly. ' Still the spitfire I see.' A dark grin spread across my lips. ' Somethings will never change despite what you do to change them.' ' Come now Eve, you can not honestly tell me you expected us to remain lovers when you changed.' My eyes narrowed. It was like a knife to my own heart. How similar this was to the way Lestatt behaved. His smooth words, his dark and devastating demeanor. I wanted to cut his lying tongue out. Instead I chose to cut him with sharp words. ' So where is your whore hiding? Did she decide your shadow was too long at last?' ' No, she didn't.' That second voice hissed from behind me just as a knife found it's way to my throat. Stiffening I leveled my gaze on Lleyes and sneered. ' I see you still don't fight fair Lleyes..' He chuckled. ' As you said..some things never change.' Talia pressed that blade further against my throat giggling madly as she looked to her lover. ' Let me cut her throat...no...better yet. Let me take her to my bath chambers and cut her throat there so that I may bathe in her blood. ' If that is what you want my love, then so shall it be. ' I found myself bound and dragged through the shadows of the streets. I knew I should never have left my crew's side, as a result I was going to die, unless I found some way out of it. Frantically my mind searched until at last I came upon the one thing I hoped Lestatt and Lleyes had in common. They both loved a good show. Sport so to speak. Once we reached their abode I set my hopeful plan into action and played upon his ego. ' So this is how you are going to end it once and for all? By merely allowing her to cut a helpless woman's throat? Come now Lleyes, I thought you were a sporting man...' She growled and kicked me in the lower back bringing me to my knees as she wrenched back on my hair and applied the knife to throat once again. My eyes peirced his own as he looked to be pondering. She slowly drew the knife across drawing a small amount of blood only to be stopped by his own hand. ' No.' Talia looked shocked and dismayed. ' No? What do you mean no?' ' It is too easy this way. Surely there is not that much satisfaction in it for you Talia. Perhaps she is right. We should make this more sporting...after all it always gets our blood boiling..' Talia grinned maliciously. ' What do you have in mind?' ' I don't know yet. Place her in the cage and we will come up with something together.' I hissed as she ripped into my hair again and quite literally dragged me across the room to hurl me within a cage. As the door slammed shut I pulled forth some mucus and spit it in her face. Screaming with rage she lurched back towards the cage but Lleyes caught her about the waist. ' Not yet, lover. Let's make this intersting.' Calming Talia turned into his arms and played with his hair. Her smile was dark as she purred. ' Let's go to bed. I get the best ideas in bed. ' Indeed you do.' I wanted to puke. The pair vanished from sight and left me there to think on how to get out of this. There had to be a way to escape and exact my revenge..not only for myself.... .... but for the real Eva as well.
