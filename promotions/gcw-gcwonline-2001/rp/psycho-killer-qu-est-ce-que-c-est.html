Psycho Killer--Qu'est-ce que c'est?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/01/06--let's say six hours after WW70 It's two packs a day. Right now, at least. Night's still young. Compton's Most Wanted, Hood Took Me Under. Loud; I feel my eardrums vibrating away as it falls back on that Isaac Hayes loop one last time. Jordan's no longer stopped smoking, picked up fuming too. Shadow boxing. Bullshit. Wants to know what we're doing. Didn't believe "it's a fuckin' party." Eyes wide, a little bloodshot. Rubbing his nose. Sniffling. Been there. Didn't deserve fame then, sorta got it. Stop shoving shit in my nose? Gotta step to the side and let another junkie take my place. "All 'bout this new ride, ain't you?" Grunt. Nod head offbeat; don't follow the Ice Cube or the street lamp metronome. Just drive. Kid'll talk; I'll burn gas in the name of finding the exact center of nowhere. I’m outta here tomorrow morning. The fuck do I care about this? I'm a little bitter. Can't not taste it, can't not see it. "This a six fo'?" "Five." "Hunh. Oh shit, one you took swimmin' was a six fo', wadn't it?" Nod head offbeat. He drops his arms, twists in his seat and leans against the door. "Don't know what I was thinkin' back there." "Yeah?" "Fuckin'. Big dudes." "They're not fair. You can't do anything about it. Except cheat and, well. You're not very good at that." "Man, I know I could've taken that shit. Just got caught by surprise." "Doesn't even matter." "I lost and those little shits kept their belts. And I fuckin' lost to the pig they’re stickin? me up against next. C'mon. I got pinned. Not Viv, not Lia, not Knight or any of those other big stupid fucks, me." "No, I mean. Getting snuck on. He's. What the fuck do you think people like that are hired for? Big, stupid, blunt destruction. They’re bullshit. There's a point where it's just not worth it, and, yeah. The presence of those kids always escalates that." "You sayin' I just shouldn't try 'coz a cat's bigger than me?" "I'm saying that you really shouldn't let someone bigger than you roll up without you noticing. It's, uncouth." "Man, don't get in my head like that. My gold's on the line." "You really care about that belt?" "It's my fuckin' title, dog. Course I care." "Fucking kids." Fucking kids, seriously. "Shortsighted, like. It's someone else's title you gotta care about." "Don't even get started with that, dude. One match at a time." "Think ahead. This isn't about a belt that won't even hold up a pair of fucking pants, man. This is about maximizing your value, and this is about you being a goddamned joke." "I had a bad night." "And you're fixating on that shit and that's what's making you be a joke. Are you familiar with the term, uh, choke artist? Like, I mean, this is totally just an offshot, let's say you're really one of those people that are not like, total shit at this. Okay? And it's like you still can't win and still manage to look like a fuckin' chump, in spite of this hypothetical talent level. I mean, I'm not saying that pressure's killing you’just like I'm not gonna call you a good wrestler or anything like that’but I'm gonna say that someone that got as lucky as you might wanna put forth a little more effort in his career. Get me?" The lights flicker, drop breakbeats and then drop BPM to a slow fade that's ever diminishing in scale. Low. Bottom feeding. "Not scared of Lia." "I don't believe you for a fuckin' second kid, but okay. Sure. Fuck does that matter? I'm not scared of vampires but, I mean, throw me in a room with Dracula and I'll end up just as dead as a pussy." "The fuck vampires got to do with anythin'?" "It's an analogy. Keep up. Fear is not even close to an issue is what I'm saying. Fear is like, being lost in the woods with a bullet and a slingshot. Also bears. It is not something you find under stadium spotlights’maybe some like, soap opera bullshit, but not terror. This is a job, man. This is clinical like bein' a clerk." "I still don't see what vampires matter." "Maybe you don't pay attention like I do. Whatever. That's not important. You've just, you've gotta think like a human and not fall into this wrestler bullshit. Princess fantasies and S&M and fuckin' freaks getting paid for their fuck ups. It's like a circus show man, and you taking these people's ideas seriously is a fucking bad, well, idea." "So, what?" "So you fuckin' laugh, and you wrestle, and you disconnect from whatever stigma they're tryin? to attach to themselves. Fear just isn't a word should come up, even in the negative." "Alright, then. I got Lia." "Why the fuck'd you act scared of her then?" He shuts up, crosses his arms and drops further into the seat. Orange glow. Skeleton face. A big world, made small and disingenuous by liars, frauds and fakes and people that just wanted to ruin a game everyone else was enjoying. Fuck. Someone's gotta keep their head in this kiddy shit. Clearly isn't me. "Where we goin'?" Kids never shut up. "The store. I ran out of smokes." He turns, stares at one of those "you are this far from here" signs as it drops past us. "No one ever believes me. I swear I'm getting tired of that shit." "Chill the fuck out, Sage." "That's the most chill thing I've said so far. Get your fucking head out of whatever you stuck it in, man." "Don't start some shit." This is the part I love. See, I mean, really? I can't fuckin' drive. Been lucky and been observant, but one of these days, it'll come back down to the simple little bit about me being shit at controlling a ton or so of hurtling steel. No embankment, at least. No traffic either. Turn the wheel, slam the brake. Tires squeal, squawk, roar; explode dirt and lose traction; the ass flings; Jordan smacks into the dashboard’shoulder to the window and throwing out shit and motherfucker and it's like I can finally just tune him out. Pop it in park, step away. He's crawling across the seat, falling into the dirt under highway light. Fuckin', is that Eazy? Miss all the good songs because of this punk. "Man, Sage, what the fuck was that?" Light a cigarette. Lean against the car. "You done bitching?" "The fuck's your problem? You fucking, fuckin' dumbshit motherfucker. Could've killed my ass!" Runs his hands back through his hair, keeping his elbows up and chest out. "Motherfucker!" Tosses his arms down and turns. Paces. Chump. "F'real, dude. The fuck?" Coming back in on me, in my face and looking down. Taller, on a hill. Playing the alpha game? Weird kid. "Are you done bitching?" "When the fuck was I bitching?" Something in my teeth? Steak, gristle; look to the side and spit it out. Eyes front, up. "Other than the last six hours?" "Man, gimme a fuckin' break. I fuckin', fuckin' looked like bullshit out there an' shit." "Yeah, you did." "See? Just need to get my mind off of it, that's all." "Get your mind off of it?" "Yeah, y'know. Grab some drunk ass ho, let her and whatever friends is interested get my mind offa it. Not fuckin?, have some crazy shithead try an? throw my ass out a car. Y'know?" I lose his face in the smoke and the light. He's not playing any big dog game; he's too stupid. "So, you were just gonna forget about it?" He's nodding. "I said passes and assists; not 'be a bitch.' You got that?" He's nodding. Slower. "You're not gettin' your mind off of this by moping until you can find some slut to lick your wounds. Dig?" "S'what I do." "What you do? Since fucking when? You don't even own a fucking car’all that pussy you're bragging about? That's all me, you dig? Before you had Jordan White, you were what, just out of a long relationship?" Smoke, inhale. Can't go off like I used to. "Lemme guess, first ass you got after Alyssa was ass you got as Jordan White, wasn't it?" "That ain't got shit to do with it." "Bullshit. You can fucking change, I don't care, man. You're a commodity. The moment you changing means changing for the worse, you're a commodity that I don't even give a fuck about dropping." Backpedaling, looking away. "Are you fucking listening? Not just tonight, it's you and it's this general fucking stupid bullshit malaise you're always carrying, and you can't even fuckin' stay sober long enough to think about what you've got a shot at. Man, even a fucking dog can think. Feel like I've hired a fuckin' rock." "Man. Sage." It's like he's not even breathing. "Man. Sage, what?" "Don't even start." "Hah, come on. I'm already finished. You're like a minute late." "Who the fuck you really think you are?" "I'm Sage motherfucking Black. I own you." Smile. He might; people without light on their face can't ever really smile, I guess. Tree falls in the woods. Why do people always give you this much time to think? Right now, right now you know he's weighing his options and coming up on top in every one of 'em. Like a fucking hero; like he's a bigger person than Trey or Lexicon or anyone else that didn't swing. Everyone's swung, kid. Come on. His arms go slack. He shrugs. "That's word then." "What?" "You said it. You're the boss; I don't know my place. I don't get my shit in line, I ain't got a job." "What?" He's pushing a hand out to me, open. This is how you get random animals to approach you. Take his hand, shake it. He's gray without light, gray through smoke and gray in his drooping eyes. His hand's jittery, sweaty; he won't make eye contact. "We cool?" I shrug. He lets go and starts behind the car. He didn't swing. But I can see my stocks just dropping and dropping and dropping. Turns around into it, jab at the eyes. Crack of knuckle on cheekbone, stings. He's reeling, holding his face and drawing up. So this is what it looks like when someone stalks over. Huh. Knee in the jaw. Hear his teeth collide clacking, two at a time. He grunts, pulls away’back of his wrist against his cheek, arm tight to his face with another in front of him, waving it, trying to keep me back. Grab him by the hair, catch an elbow to the ribs; nothing. Does this kid know what fire is? Hasn't had anything in his sinuses since the show, huh? Forearm to the back of his neck and he sandbags; drag him to his feet and toss. He swivels, catches the bumper against his back and cringes. "We beyond your fuckin' comfort zone now, Frank?" Faces up to me, shadows falling a deathmask across him. I think he thinks I'd kick a man while he's down. No honor code. I just think I'm cool for now. "Now, this time,” talk slow, Sage; he has to understand this one, “are you going to listen to what I say, take it to heart, and act on it” Or do you enjoy this sort of thing?" A nod and a no-shake. Respectively. "Good." Toss my Port down, fire another up. "I'm leaving tomorrow. I'm giving you a free week, alright? Do whatever the fuck you want, but be very, very aware that watching you wrestle should not mean watching my investment dry up. You're not a figurehead, you're not a spokesman, you're a fucking catchphrase or a fucking jingle or whatever you can think of more inconsequential to the actual product than that. Dig?" Another nod in this eternal series of communication through gesture. Can it talk? No one knows! "This free week of yours, this is a turn around thing. You do whatever the fuck it is you've gotta do to get your 'shit in line,' and if that means Shaman makes you look bad and me look stupid again, then, whatever, man. This is your vacation. I hope you enjoy this." Hey, a smoke ring. "Now. Call a fuckin' taxi, go get some sleep." He's pulling himself up on the car, shaking his head and staying on one knee. "And get a fucking haircut. That shit's just a liability." On his feet. I'm in the car’he’s watching, still as the dead. I'm accelerating. Sucks to be him.
