Anathema

20 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/20/10 1:25 P.M. Pacific Heights, San Francisco, CA. Triumph’s House. Silence reigns supreme in Triumph Frost’s lavish mansion. That is, until the chimes of the doorbell ring out throughout the house. Sitting in his living room, sprawled out on a couch in black Nike sweatpants and a George Gervin throwback San Antonio Spurs jersey is Triumph, a bottle of Sierra Nevada resting on his chest. He watches Tracy Morgan in the midst of standup, as the doorbell keeps ringing. He pauses the DVR and rises up slightly. "Alexis," he calls out. "Get that, will ya?" He waits for a moment, but receives no answer. Instead of action, he decides to slink back down in the chair, and hit play. "We some nasty muthafuckas," Tracy Morgan spits out. "We play with that butthole all night long, then drive home, bitin’ that same nail." Triumph guffaws, as he sips from the beer. The doorbell is relentless now. "Alexis," he screams out. "Door!" "Where this corn come from?" The doorbell starts to drown out Morgan’s standup. Triumph becomes quickly perturbed. "Goddamnit," he grunts, putting the beer down, and getting up. Making the long walk, he mumbles to himself as he reaches the lobby of his house. Reaching the oak double doors, he swings them open, only to be greeted by a young kid in gangsta garb, all the makings of a low-rent K-Fed. It’s Clinton Thorpe, Alexis’ brother. Triumph is hardly pleased at his presence. Alexis, clad in a white sundress, comes barreling down the stairs, as she spots Clinton. "Clinton!" She rushes down, blowing right past Triumph and hugging her brother, as he swings her around in the hug. "Good to see you too, lil’ sis," Clinton says, as he puts her down. He tilts his head, and takes a minute to take her in. "Damn, you lookin’ good as always." She laughs, and kisses him on the cheek. She grabs his hand. "Come in, please!" She leads him through the door, past Triumph. Triumph just rolls his eyes and shuts the door. Alexis looks over her brother with a smile. "So what’s my big bro been up to?" "Y’all know me," Clinton beams. "I’m livin’ the dream, yo." "You’re gonna be the next big rapper, huh?" Alexis queries. "Oh, hells yeah," Clinton bellows. "I ain’t ever givin’ up on that shit." She laughs, as Triumph continues to eye Clinton wearily, while Clinton can’t seem to contain his good feelings. "Yo, it’s great to see y’all!" Without warning, Clinton embraces Triumph with a hug. Clinton slaps Triumph hard on the back a few times, and Triumph manages to wriggle free. Triumph takes a moment to respond with an insincere smile. "So, Clinton, what’re you doing here?" "Y’all know me," Clinton responds. "I’m all about my family." Clinton fake-punches Triumph in the shoulder, and cracks up. "Nah, I’m just fuckin’ witch’y’all. I’m on vacation, yo! I’m all about lettin’ loose and shit. I’m all ‘bout that booty juice, knowumsayin’?" Clinton continues laughing, as Triumph sarcastically laughs. "No, really," Triumph states, a serious demeanor. "What the fuck are you doing here?" "Triumph!" Alexis elbows him in the side, but Triumph’s eyes never waver from Clinton. Clinton shrugs it off. "It’s cool, yo," he says. "On the real? Shit be hard out there, ya know?" As if expecting somber music to accompany it, Clinton launches into his dramatic sob story. "I mean, things looked honky-dory and shit, ya heard? But the road is tough. I keep tryin’, folks keep sayin’ the same thing. ‘Get out, asshole.’ ‘Go back to flipping burgers, whitey.’ ‘I’d rather hear a tape of my daughter being raped on continuous loop than listen to your godawful bullshit one second longer.’ I mean..." Clinton seems on the verge of tears. "I dunno what else to say. I tell ‘em the truth, yo. My shit makes Mickey Avalon look like Simon Rex! But...nothin’. Nothin’ at all." Triumph and Alexis watch Clinton march about, unsure of what to do. Instead, they let him continue. "I dunno, yo," Clinton muses somberly. "I figure I done it all, but…I just dunno. Ya gotta bounce back, though, right? Ya gotta bounce back." Clinton’s voice trails off, as he stares at the floor, hands in his pockets. "I gots nowhere else to go. I just thought, maybe…" The three stand there, in all of their awkward glory. Alexis breaks the tension. "Of course you can stay." Triumph nearly explodes in disbelief, as he holds up his index finger and smiles. "Give us a moment, will you?" Grabbing around Alexis’ waist, he drags her a dozen or so yards from Clinton, and huddles up with her. "Are you out of your goddamned mind?" She’s not pleased with his course of action. "He can’t fucking stay here!" "Why not?" she queries. "Because." He pauses for a moment, looking over at Clinton, and goes back into the huddle. "Did you forget what happened the last time he stayed here?" "He was only here two days," she says. "Right," Triumph responds with authority. "And in those two days, he managed to destroy a painting worth more than his good-for-nothing life, and then he stole my black card and bought a tour bus. But not any old tour bus, no," Triumph gets more and more heated. "Jay-Z’s tour bus. That he was still using. While he was on fucking tour!" Alexis rolls her eyes. "That was just a huge misunderstanding." "Yeah," Triumph retorts glibly. "Clearly it was all a mix-up, seeing as how Clinton bought it from the only Bob Smith in the history of mankind with an Indian accent. A guy he found on Craigslist. I can see how someone might get pretty fuckin’ confused in that situation." "It was a simple mistake, alright?" Alexis turns slightly away from Triumph as she crosses her arms. "It’s not like you haven’t made mistakes before." "But at least I own up to them," Triumph says. "What’d that shithead say? ‘Ain’t my fault that frog-faced bitch wouldn’t share that shit.’" Alexis goes on the warpath. "You act like you’re so damn perfect all the time." He points right at Clinton. "I’m a lot better than that degenerate." Alexis grabs Triumph’s arm and pulls it down. "Why is this such a big deal for you? I’d go to bat for your family any time." Her voice turns near-whisper. "Not like you’d ever open up about your family." "I don’t talk about my family," Triumph emphatically states. "Because ain’t no one in my family as bad as this guy." Alexis snorts. "Really. I’d beg to differ." Triumph sneers, tilting his head upward. "Who?" Alexis eyes him intently. "Your uncle. Terry." Triumph’s eyes narrow, as the wheels in his head start to spin. 3:47 PM. Avenida Revolucion. Tijuana, Mexico. Six years ago. Mariachi music fills the air, as outside of a Mexican strip club, an innocuous thirteen-year-old Triumph Frost stands next to his uncle on his mother’s side, Terry Frost. The sun beams off of Terry’s thinning blonde hair, as he sports a black tank-top, blue-blocker sunglasses, black socks with sandals, and old-man tan cargo shorts flanked by a black fanny pack. He chats with the intimidating Mexican bouncer, whose arms remain crossed. Terry beams a smile that’d put a used car salesman to shame. "He’s eighteen." The bouncer looks over the nervous Triumph thoroughly. Terry elbows him in the side. "Quit jitterin’ around, boy," he mutters. Triumph straightens himself out as best he can, as Terry awaits the verdict. However, the bouncer looks back at Terry and shakes his head "no". Terry reaches into his fanny pack, and pulls out a wad of cash. He slaps it into the bouncer’s hand, but refuses to let go. Their eyes meet. "He’s eighteen," Terry reiterates, as he loosens his grip. The money slowly goes into the bouncer’s pocket. He looks over the young Triumph again, and waves him through. Terry grabs the back of Triumph’s shoulder, and pushes him up the stairs to the wonders that wait above. 1:48 PM. Pacific Heights, San Francisco, CA. Triumph’s House. An awaiting Alexis watches Triumph entrenched in thought. He shoots his eyes back to her. "Totally out of context," he says. Alexis is up in arms, as Triumph chuckles a bit, putting his hand on her shoulder. "Alright," he finally concedes. "I get it, okay?" She regains her proper composure. "So then you’ll...?" Triumph scoffs. "Still? I mean…" "He’s family!" Alexis remains relentless. Triumph ponders for a bit, turning away from her. Meanwhile, Clinton acts uninterested as Alexis glances his way. Triumph stalks for a moment, walking back to her. "But he’s such a bastard." She glares at him, as he attempts to swing her his way. "Look, if this were anyone else--" "But it’s not," she interrupts. "Clinton’s my brother. And we can’t do him wrong." A pause. "I won’t let you do him wrong." Triumph stares into Alexis’ puppy dog eyes, and suddenly can’t resist. He presses his forehead against hers. "Fine," he sighs. Swinging back to the kicking-at-the-ground Clinton, the two emerge from their huddle. Triumph goes first. "Sorry, asshole," he says. "But you got to--" Alexis elbows him in the kidney, as he gasps for air. She remains playful. "He’s just joking," she tells her brother. "You can stay as long as you want." Clinton’s eyes light up. "Fo…fo’ real?" Alexis nods. Clinton can’t contain himself, as he embraces Alexis. "Yo, sis’, you the fuckin’ shit, yo! I can’t even…Yo, this fuckin’ rocks, yo!" Clinton steps back from his sister, and stands in front of Triumph. Triumph sheepishly tries to play it off, but Clinton engulfs him in a hug, slapping his back repeatedly. "I love you, bro," Clinton calls out. "You my mothafuckin’ fam, yo!" Clinton keeps slapping Triumph’s back, as Triumph cringes with disgust. Finally, Clinton let’s go. Clinton, all smiles, angles himself towards the front door, and cups his hand over his mouth. "Yo Josiah! Scribbles! Bugfuck! K-5! We cool, yo!" Busting through the door, an army of Clinton’s friends marches into the house, carrying boxes filled with video games, music equipment, and assorted paraphernalia. Josiah, a white kid with brownish dreads in dark sunglasses and hemp-made clothing, is first through. He stops in front of Alexis. "Little help," he sneers. She nods and guides him towards the den. Scribbles, a stout white kid Turtle doppleganger, backwards Yankees baseball cap and all, follows Josiah. Bugfuck, a slim Asian kid wearing DJ headphones, carries two large metal cases that seem to weigh more than he does. Following up is K-5, a boisterous large black man that might rival some of GCW’s genetic freaks in size. Clinton and K-5 exchange a personal and complicated handshake, the two laughing afterwards, as K-5 slaps him on the back afterwards and follows the rest of the gang. Triumph stands stunned, mouth agape, his house hijacked. Clinton sidles up next to Triumph, placing his hand on his shoulder. "Never know we here, yo," Clinton asserts. "Like ghosts." Clinton slaps down on Triumph’s shoulder hard, and follows his gang, as Triumph cringes, a rare moment. Triumph rubs the bridge of his nose, as he grunts. "Fuck." 12:35 AM. Pacific Heights, San Francisco, CA. Triumph’s House. Swimming around in Triumph’s Olympic-size indoor heated pool is Alexis, wading around in the deep end. A pool flanked by windows, wooden lounging chairs, and a cedar structuring, K-5 gets a running start from the side. "Cannonball!" He hollers as he makes his leap. The shadow slowly engulfs Alexis until… SPLASH! K-5 pops up at the same time Alexis does, water dripping off of her face. "Oh, you are so gonna get it!" She howls out as she leaps at him, giddy as a schoolgirl on "Justin Beiber Day", as she dunks K-5. They play around in the pool some more. Josiah is off to the side, his feet in the shallow end, as him and a waist-deep Scribbles enjoy a beer. Bugfuck, meanwhile, plays around with an iPod hooked up to the stereo system. Before long, he has Wax Tailor pumping out. Triumph walks up to a pleased Clinton, and sits in the chair next to him. He hands Clinton a Stella Artois bottle. "Thanks," Clinton says, as he watches the scene. "Good stuff, yo." Triumph takes a sip from his bottle and nods. "Quite a motley crew you wrangled up." Clinton laughs. "Yeah," he says. "Cats’ be straight outta a comic book and shit." "What’s up with "bumbaclat white-bwoy" over there?" Triumph points in Josiah’s direction. "That’s my manager, yo," Clinton responds. "Dude just makes shit happen, right?" "Yeah," Triumph responds incredulously. Clinton points over at Scribbles, who kicks his feet in the water. "And Scribbles? He my backup, yo," Clinton says. "Gotta have that shit. You be spittin’ game for an hour, or plus half that, yo. Gotta take a moment to breathe, knowumsayin’?" Clinton moves down the line. "And Bugfuck, he be the master turntable specialist. Kid makes DJ Premier look like a fuckin’ biatch." Triumph eyes Bugfuck from afar, working the iPod like a pro. "How do you know he’s a he?" Clinton grins, ear-to-ear. "Sheeit, boy. I--" Clinton hesitates. He ponders for a second, and taps his finger against his temple. "You know, I never been fully sure. Seen him with chicks and dudes, just figured he was open for interpretation, ya heard?" Clinton sips from his beer. "I ain’t thought it a thing, though." Clinton beams. "I’m a progressive muthafucka, right?" Triumph indulges Clinton’s statement with an insincere nod. Clinton suddenly gets on point, as he points at K-5, who carries a giggling Alexis on his back in the pool. "K-5, though? That’s my boy. Me and him been close since KC." Triumph puckers his lips. "I thought Alexis grew up in DC." "Yeah, she did," Clinton tells him. "But I ain’t get that luxury of the Cleaver upbringin’, ya know?" Triumph takes another sip. "Oh yeah?" "Hell, yeah," Clinton retorts. "Mom’s always saw me as the troublemaker." Clinton sips from his own Stella. "I get that, though." He starts to peel away at the label. "One fuck-up too many, I win me an all-expenses paid trip to boarding school." He tears off a piece, the glue sticking to his thumb, and crumples it up. "I wasn’t gonna be down with that, though." The label piece falls out of his hand, his head a bit hanging. "So I hit the streets, met my boy, and we been at this shit ever since." Triumph cradles his bottle, himself in thought. "I get that." Clinton laughs, and raises his head. "That’s family, though, right? I mean, gotta stick to whatcha know." Triumph continues to pry. "What about your other sisters?" Clinton scoffs. "Who, Kim and Sarah?" Clinton shakes his head from side to side. "They older and all that, and Sarah still pissed off I stole her ride and crashed it." Clinton holds out his arms. "But, shit, yo, that shit was a Cavalier! I did her a favor, ya heard?" The two share a laugh. A robed Alexis comes sauntering over to the pair, proud at the sight, beaming from ear to ear. "Look at you two. Getting along famously, I see." "No diggity," Clinton nearly screams, awkwardly grabbing at Triumph playfully. Triumph looks over at Alexis and simply raises his eyebrows. A thought crosses Clinton, as he stands up, screaming towards his gang. "Yo! Bugfuck! Play my shit, yo!" Bugfuck raises an arm, and gets on his equipment. "You gonna love this shit, yo," Clinton states, his words brimming with confidence. Alexis and Triumph share an odd look. A hip-hop beat, heavy with bass and synth, hits the air, as the gang bobs their head, all except Triumph and Alexis. Without warning, Clinton’s voice rips out into the air: "Y’all know me, I’m the Cali Cock Crusher! Worldwide famous, from Rhode Island to Russia! Real Straight O.G., I be straight up ballin’ thuggin’! Now I’mm’a fuck your bitch’s mouth like an Alien face hugger!" The looks on their faces are priceless. Triumph poorly fights back his stifling of laughter, while Alexis sheepishly pats her brother on the head. "That’s...well, that’s something," she finally musters. Triumph can’t help it anymore. "Cali Cock Crusher? Are you fucking serious, man?" Clinton looks confused. "That’s a hardcore name, yo! All the girls be diggin’ on my shit. My shit be stoopid, yo." Triumph can’t help himself. "Oh, it’s stupid all right." Clinton blows air out. "You just a hater, yo," he remarks dejectedly. "Whatchu know, anyhow?" "I know ten dollar hookers giving five dollar handjobs make groovier sounds than you do," Triumph states. "And I know that I’m fucking off to bed." As Triumph makes his exit, Alexis walks up to Clinton, and grabs him by the hand. She gives him the loving look of an older sibling. "It’s good to see you again." Clinton seems happy again, as he brushes her hand, and she breaks away to follow Triumph. Josiah walks up to Clinton, handing him a Stella, and sits down next to him. "Yo, the rest of the crew still comin, right?" Josiah asks. Clinton sips his beer. "They be here in a day or two." "How come you didn’t tell them ‘bout everyone else?" Josiah queries. A devilish grin crosses Clinton’s face. "‘Cause fuck them, yo." 1:38 AM. Pacific Heights, San Francisco, California. Triumph Frost’s house. In Triumph’s master bedroom, with the lights out Triumph lays in bed, his hands behind his head, eyes closed. Alexis rests next to him, her body resting across his chest. She hums to his heartbeat for a few moments. "I’m so proud of you," she softly notes. "You’re finally growing up." "I want him out of my fucking house tomorrow," Triumph mutters. Alexis pops up, turning on the light. "No more of this, Triumph," she states. "He stays as long as he needs to." Triumph slowly rises up, as she sits on the bed, facing him. "I’m sure they’re lining up around the block to give the ‘Cali Cock Crusher’ his first real job." Alexis sneaks out a chuckle, but stifles it away. "That’s not funny," she says. Triumph smells blood in the water, his grin heavy. "His skills would look great on any job application. ‘Clear communication skills, yo. World famous. Strong desire to fuck yo’ girl’s face like a movie monster. Won’t steal much. Can trust wit’ yo’ muthafuckin’ kids.’ He’s a sure bet for McDonalds drive-thru backup number three." Alexis rolls her eyes. "So he might need some...coaching," she says. "But I won’t see him thrown out onto the streets." Triumph opens his mouth, but she cuts him off right away. "I don't ask for much from you," she tells him. "I never have. But I'm asking now. Just give it a few days." Her eyes go puppy-dog once again. "Please?" Triumph can’t resist, again, and sighs. "Alright," he concedes. "But that fucker’s on a very thin leash." The two kiss quick, and then passionately, as Alexis shuts off the light in mid-kiss. 2:15 PM. Pacific Heights, San Francisco, California. Triumph Frost’s house. Two days have passed. Two days of sheer and utter horror and terror on the parts of Triumph and Alexis. Indeed, all hell has broken loose. The living room is cluttered with empty pizza boxes, beer cans and bottles, bongs and pipes, and a binge partying aftermath that’d put Delta Tau Chi to shame. New faces abound, as the crew’s membership seems to have at least doubled, with a gang that looks just as shady as the rest. They play Playstation 3, Clinton and K-5 on the controls, both resting on the couch. The television is cranked up to the maximum volume, the sounds of Modern Warfare 2 blazing about, gunshots and explosions at near-deafening levels. Scribbles lights up a pipe and passes it to Josiah, while Bugfuck jams out with DJ headphones wrapped around his dome. Triumph walks down the hall from the gym, drenched in sweat and in workout clothes. He rubs a black towel over his face, as he hears a loud bang, and makes his way to the living room, where he rolls his eyes at the sight of his unwanted guests. "Hey," Triumph calls out. They all give a half-hearted ‘wassup’ in return, Triumph in complete disbelief. Triumph singles out Clinton. "I see there’s more of you now." Clinton doesn’t break stride from the game. "S’my crew, yo," Clinton says. "You think I’m just gonna leave ‘em high and dry?" Triumph crosses his arms. "I think we need to talk." Clinton continues to ignore his presence. "Later, yo. I’m busy." Triumph isn’t amused. "Now." "Yo, where’s that chick with the food?" Clinton wonders aloud. "Yah sistah’s wicked lazy," a female’s voice rings out, drenched in so much New Englanderness, it’s sickening. Triumph’s eyes wander to the skanky broad who sits next to Josiah on the couch. With dreadlocked black hair, an ill-fitting dress, and cracking her gum, Triumph’s almost taken aback by her. "Who in the fuck are you, lady?" Triumph looks her up and down. Clinton tilts his head back slightly, grinning like a lottery-winning trailer park resident. "That’s my videe-ho, yo," he snickers, as does a number of the crew. She stands up in her high heels. "I told ya, Clinton. That ain’t my name no mores! Say that shit right!" Clinton grunts. "Fine," he concedes. "That’s my girl." His voice becomes a falsetto of a native Bostonian. "Lay-dee Light’nin’ Rahd." She cackles like an evil witch, one that causes Triumph to cringe in severe pain. Before Triumph can utter anything else, Alexis emerges from the nearby kitchen, carrying a large plate full of sandwiches. "Here’s the food," she dejectedly remarks, and the jackals go on the attack, nearly knocking her down as they grab their piece. "Don’t be so rough with my sister," Clinton states with the urgency and interest of a sloth. Alexis looks over at Triumph, and one can nearly see the steam pouring out of his ears. He walks over to her, grabs her by the arm, and pulls her into the kitchen. Behind closed doors, Triumph’s had his fill. "Enough’s enough, baby," he pleas. "You’ve proven your love for that fuckin’ piece of inhuman dogshit. But this is too much." Her voice cracks. "What can I do?" "You better figure out something," Triumph tells her. "Or I’m gonna enter the lot of us in a murder-suicide pact." She starts to cry, as she turns away from her husband. "I can’t. It hurts too much. It’d hurt way too much." She wipes the tears from her eyes, and swings around to face him. "He’s my brother. And what can I say? Family is my weakness." Triumph nods, as he pulls her in close to her. He rubs the back of her head, and whispers into her ear. "It’s okay." She lets go, tears streaming everywhere. "I just don’t have it in me." Triumph continues to hold his wife, understanding covering his face. That only gets broken up as he hears the crew laughing from the next room, and his nostrils flare. He pulls her away from him, as she brushes off her tears. "We will do this together," he asserts. "Me and you, will go in there together. You will be strong. You will stand up to him. You confront your fuckface cocksucking free-loading brother and his disease ridden crew, and you will tell them to leave." He wipes one last streaming tear away. "And I will be by your side the whole time." Alexis, in between sobs, nods her head firmly. Back in the room, Alexis has Triumph flanking her. The gang goes about their raucous endeavors, as she clears her throat. "I have something to say," she says softly. Everyone in the room ignores her, getting a tad bit louder. She clears her throat loudly this time. "I said I have something to say!" Her voice bellows, and everyone seems to notice this time. Except Clinton, who hasn’t broken stride from the game. "Alexis, you always were an annoying bitch. So why don’t you some useful shit and get me a beer? I’m thirsty, yo." Alexis freaks, and breaks away from Triumph out of the room. Everyone laughs and goes aback about their business. All but Triumph. Who’s fucking pissed. Triumph stalks over to the Playstation, and rips the cords out from the game. Clinton and gang get up in arms, as Triumph lifts the game over his head and smashes it onto the ground, electronic pieces and jagged plastic flying about. Clinton throws down his controller. "What the fuck, yo? That was a ranked online game! You know what that means?" Triumph swings around to Clinton, who leans back, startled at the fury of Mr. Frost. Clinton smirks, and shrugs it all off. "Whatever, yo," he chortles. "You just be buyin’ me a new one, anyways." With that, Triumph leaps over the table and lifts up Clinton, throwing him over the table to the ground. Josiah makes the mistake of getting in Triumph’s face. "Hey, bitch, what you think--" It takes two seconds before Triumph swats him down. In full battle mode, Triumph surveys the room. Until K-5 raises out of his seat and walks over to Triumph. The two stare at each other, nearly eye-to-eye, K-5 with the sleight height advantage. K-5 is a mountainous beast, but nary a glimmer of fear crosses Triumph’s eye. "You sure you wanna do this?" K-5 asks, his voice booming with the smoothness of Barry White. Triumph’s eyes pick up in intensity. "You have no fucking idea." As they remain engaged in their staring contest, K-5 peers into the soul of Triumph. Whatever he sees, it’s enough for him to back down first. With his victory, a grin crosses Triumph’s face, as he has the undivided attention of the room. He points to no one in particular. "Listen up, assholes," he bellows. "You have ten minutes to pack up your shit and hit the fucking road. Or I swear to the motherfucking God of Gods, I will make Vietnam look like a circle jerk." Uneasiness crosses everyone’s face but Triumph’s, who simply revels in his...triumph. 3:25 PM. Pacific Heights, San Francisco, California. Triumph Frost’s house. The gang angrily embarks on their march of shame, carrying what little stuff they brought with them. Triumph manages to provide each with a passing remark to each one of them as they pass him by. "Eat shit. Get bent. Suck cock. Die in a fire, asshole. Go fuck your mother." They give him dirty looks, but know that they are the ones without. Before long, pulling up the rear, the last one around is Clinton. Triumph waves him through, but Clinton surveys the room for his sister. Triumph, however, isn’t having it, and nearly pushes him out the door. Clinton confronts Triumph one last time. "Yo, if I could just…" "No," Triumph declares. Clinton and Triumph stare holes through one another. Triumph never budges, as Clinton turns away. "Whatever," he mutters, and walks out of the house. Triumph slams the large doors behind him, and presses his back against the wall. He closes his eyes, and lets out the biggest relieved sigh in the history of relieved sighs. Opening them, he sees the somber Alexis, sitting on the staircase, looking right at him. He walks over to her, and sits right down next to her, as she covers her face with her hands. "You were right," she says. "He was never going to change." Triumph pulls her hands off her face, and cups the one closest to him. "He’ll figure it out someday." Her eyes meet his, hers gleaming with hope, his compassion. They both break back to disappointment. "Yeah," Triumph admits. "He’ll never figure it out." She rests her head on his shoulder. "I know." The two enjoy a moment of peace, if just for a moment. "So what’s next?" she asks him. Triumph wallows in contemplation for a second. "We go to Australia." His voice firms up. "And I beat the ever-loving shit outta that Gacy-faced asshole." Alexis hugs tightly around Triumph’s waist, as he remains mired in thought.
