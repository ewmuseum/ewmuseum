Date: Timeless -=Parched upon his throne, Mayhem sits, still as the night, hands resting peacefully on his lap. He stares at the painting over his bed. Painted by a good friend from his childhood, who's life was taken by a madman during an argument in a local bar, Mayhem stares deep into it. It's an elaborate painting of Bryan and his younger brother Jason O'Neil holding the EHWF Tag Team Title's above their heads. Titles they held for nearly a year. They hold these titles high, but painted behind Bryan is a black circle and white behind Jason. The artist told his critics that the circles represented each person's aura. As he stares into the picture the image of a smiling Jason turns into a look of disgust. Bryan's eyes open wide at the image above his bed chamber. Bryan and Jason hate each other the way brother's normally hate each other but love each other just the same. Jason holding that title above his head high, grinning, looking at Bryan was Bryan's most shining moment. As children they were beaten and neglected by an abusive father and whores for mothers. Bryan was proud because they accomplished something together, something people told them would never happen in their youth. Life was good until Jason that selfish bastard became fame crazy and quickly became the Hulk Hogan of his generation. That Bastard, that son to a dead whore. Bryan's face changed into a look of rage. He stands up, walks towards the painting and throws a fit. He snatches it from the wall and begins to bash his brother on the face, putting a hole in the art. He runs to his window and throws it from the top floor and watches it land in the cold mud of Belmont, the cold mud of hell. Bryan sits back down and looks at the spot where the painting use to call home. There's a lighter shaded square on the wall from the years its stayed there, while the paint around it cracked and molded, and formed a yellowish tint.=- -=His stare is direct and evil. He is the epitome of hell on earth, he is the darkest thing to walk the soil fertilized by the blood of man. Children run from him, parents hide their children in his presence. Caldera fears him, fears what he will do next. This is not the same Mayhem that slowly crept into the hearts of fans before turning on Jason, for the second time in his life. Mayhem knows other wrestlers fear him as well. He can see it in their eyes. They ask themselves 'what will this Lunatic do if he gets his hands on me?' Mayhem knows this because the dark power told him. Mayhem closes his eyes and begins to seek advice from the dark power. When he opens them sitting cross-legged on the center of his bed is a man with shoulder length brown hair and a short well kept bed. This man is not large, he sits wearing what looks like white sheets. He's wearing a crown, but thorns protrude from this crown cutting his forehead, staining his head with his blood. His hands and feet are also caked with blood. Mayhem begins to understand who this apparition is.=- Apparition:'Im not hear to harm you giant. I am here because you seek council, but council from the wrong. You are closer to wrong and further from right my young giant.' -=Mayhem grits his teeth. This must be the work of Gabriel.=- Apparition:' Sir O'Neil, I come here not as a messianic figure but as a messenger, a prophet if you will. The dark you acquire only heeds doomsday, the armageddeon where my angels shall descend the depths and perish the souls of the lost and all shall inherit the heavens for inifinite peace. I know this because my father says so. Come with me, and end this battle of good and evil because good shall overcome in the end. Gabriel White is sent to rid this world of evil and bring peace. His first goal was you. I can save you now, save you from violence, save you from war of the heavens and bring you with the forgiven.' -=Mayhem closes his eys and opens them hoping the annoying Jesus figure leaves his head. He opens them, and nothing. It still sits on his bed. Blood still soaked into his skin.=- Apparition:'You can't rid me that easily young man. Bryan O'Neil I'm giving you the chance to free yourself of sin, to free this town or evil and walk the lighted path and seat yourself at the right hand of my father.' -=The apparition extends his hand and holds it out for Mayhem to grasp, and accept. Mayhem instead stands and stares into the eyes of the God Machine, and walks away.=- Apparition:'The Devil has stood...dark days are near, begin the end of days.' -=The minions rush from no where it seems and surround the bed with the Jesus figure. Mayhem exits, and closes his door. As he walks down the hall, a silent scream is heard from the room, along with the sound of a pack of wolves feeding.=- Mayhem:'Feed minions, for we shall keep bloody the Sabbath this Sunday at Fallout......'
