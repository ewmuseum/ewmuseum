The Encounter

24 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/24/08 The rancid smell of blood mixed with decay filled Delylah’s nostrils as she opened the front door of the warehouse. She saw a soft eerie glow from the back of the warehouse and was drawn to it like a moth to a flame. As she slowly drifted toward the light and her eyes adjusted. She saw what she was intended to see, Paul tied to a pillar with her parents on each side of him. His head was slumped to the side and she could see a slight trickle of blood from the two small puncture wounds on his neck. She let out a blood-curdling scream and she felt her body start to go limp. Her eyes moved to her mother and then her father, both of them suffering the same fate as Paul. Delylah chokes back her sobs as well as the acidic taste of vomit in the back of her throat. She hears a familiar laugh but when she looks in the direction it came from no one was there. “You were to late dear sister and everyone you have ever loved suffer the consequences.” Cleopatra’s voice told her. ? Now my dear it’s your turn to feel alone and empty for the rest of your life. The way I felt my entire life. ? She sat up into the cool air gasping for her breath. In a panic she grasped the blanket beside her to discover that Paul was not there. Delylah looked up to find the sun still perched along the horizon. It was still daylight, but that didn’t change her concerns. Frantically Delylah scrambled to her feet; despite the late day illumination she could not find Paul anywhere. With the dream still terrorizing her waking moments, her eyes rested on the steel door of the warehouse. "Del, calm down. I made a promise to you that I’d be by your side through all of this. No matter what the consequences." Paul began, "I know where she has them Del. I am the only person that can help you now."These words resonated in Delylah’s head. She had hoped to God that he didn’t go in there alone. She would never be able to forgive herself if anything happened to him. She started running toward the door of the warehouse screaming his name, tears streaming down her cheeks. She stopped dead in her tracks when she heard Paul calling out to her. “Del” hey, it’s ok. I’m right here.” Paul told her as he made his way to where she was. She wrapped her arms tightly around his waist. He pushed her thick ebony hair away from her face and placed a kiss on the top of her head. “I” I thought my dream came true and she really did take you.” Delylah moaned. “Del baby I didn’t even sleep. I stayed awake so I could keep an eye on you. There’s no way that I’d be able to sleep this close to her lair.” Paul said as he caressed her cheek. The sun slowly dropped below the horizon illuminating the sky with a fiery glow. Neither of them knew what they were going to do once they were inside of the warehouse. The only thing they could hope for is she would still be sleeping when they entered the building. Paul and Delylah laced their fingers and walked toward the metal sliding door of the warehouse. Paul slowly slid open the door and almost vomited from the stench that wafted out at him. Delylah had become accustomed to the smells; they plagued her dreams every time she closed her eyes. She slid past Paul allowing him to close the door behind them. The large room was dimly lit by four torches that were anchored to the stonewalls. Delylah gasped as she saw her parents tied back to back in the center of the room. She ran to them with Paul nipping at her heels. Both of them were bruised and bloody. Delylah placed a hand on her mother’s face and her eyes slowly fluttered open. Nebt-Het gasped and horror filled her eyes. Delylah placed a finger to her lips, “Shhh mom, It’s me.” Nebt-Het smiled weakly and nodded as she looked into her daughter’s eyes. Tears streamed down her cheeks as Delylah moved to her father whom looked the worst of the two. She could tell that he had taken the most abuse from Cleo. He was the one that she hated most. His jaw was swollen and bruised, his nose so broken that it laid almost completely flat. Delylah choked back her sobs and she caressed her father’s cheek. It was then that Amun Nguyen fluttered open his bruised eyes. “NO!” Amun thrashed about, “GET THE HELL AWAY FROM ME!” “Dad, were gonna get you out of here.” Delylah whispered to him in an attempt to calm him down. “No Delylah you’re not. She is not alone.” Amun said in wide-eyed hysteria. Glancing curiously at Paul whose expression mirrored her own bewilderment, “She’s not the only one?” Delylah asked her father. They both knew what she meant by “the only one” but even now no one wanted to discuss the taboo subject of vampires. “There is another yes,” Amun replied with a quiver in his voice. “And a man too.” “Two others?” Paul asked, interjecting himself into the conversation. “Yes.” Replied Amun, his voice sullen. “That is why you two have to leave.” “Dad I-? Delylah began until her father cut her off. “You WILL do as your told!” Her father screamed, the veins along his neck popping as he scolded her. “Take your mother and leave, this is my fault and I will not take you three down with me.” “We’re not going anywhere dad. I am not going to leave you behind.” Delylah told him stubbornly. “Cleo isn’t going to get her way this time.” The memory of Cleopatra tearing into the flesh of her adoptive father, Elias Nagatti, sent chills down Delylah’s back. She looked over at Paul and could tell he remembered that night as well. They moved toward her parents and worked feverishly on the knots that bound them to their chairs. Just as they started freeing the knots they were brought to an abrupt stop by the terror in her parent’s eyes. Delylah and Paul both spun around and saw the cause of the fear, two very large men with the devil in their eyes. That is the last thing Paul or Delylah saw before everything went pitch black. ******** Delylah slowly fluttered her eyes open and placed a hand on her head as her eyes adjusted to the dim lighting. She felt the warm stickiness of her own blood and automatically went on the defense leaping to her feet. She looked around the room feverishly for Paul. She found him bound to a wooden chair next to her parents. Delylah ran toward him but was stopped in her tracks by the sound of her sister’s voice. “Now what do you plan to do dear sister” Lover boy is tied up along side that sorry excuse of a father that you’re so fond of.” Cleo slowly stalked across the room until she was nose to nose with Delylah. “Now the playing field is even.” “Even” You call three on one even!” Delylah said her voice cracking with fear. Cleopatra laughed maniacally, “Your precious Paul wouldn’t be in this situation if he would have just kept his nose out of our business. I really don’t have a problem with him. Actually I find him quite attractive and as soon as you’re out of the way I will make him my own.” Delylah spit in Cleo’s face, “He would never be with the likes of you.” Cleo smiled and wiped the spit from her face, “Who said he would have a choice” I wouldn’t want him as a mortal.” Rage rose up in Delylah’s eyes, she doubled her fist and swung. Before it could reach its target Del was thrown against one of the stonewalls. She moaned as she slid down it and hit the floor. Cleo closed the distance between them with cat like quickness. She grabbed Delylah by the throat and raised her effortlessly above her head. “You bitch, I should break your neck now and be done with you,” Cleopatra said with disdain, “That would be against the rules though. I want you to watch the people you love suffer. I want you to suffer the way that I have my entire life.” Delylah’s thoughts raced, as did her heart. She needed to find Cleo’s weakness. Everyone had a weakness even the undead. Then it hit her it was the same weakness Del had, their parents. “You don’t know what suffering is Cleo. Did you attend college and get brutalized because you were thought to be Muslim” Did your father try and sell you off to some aristocrat when you turned seventeen?” Delylah asked with an accusatory tone. Delylah’s questions obviously irritated Cleopatra; she could see the rage glowing in her eyes. “You don’t know what I went through Delylah. You couldn’t possibly know what it was like to feel like an outcast, completely alone even amongst your family.” Cleo told her through clenched teeth. Delylah laughed in her face, “If you only knew just how alike we are in that respect sister.” Cleo’s face flushed and she tossed Delylah back against the wall. Delylah slid down the wall and her eyes slowly fluttered close. ******** Delylah awoke to the sound of Paul’s voice. She looked around the room and daylight filtered in through the grime-covered windows. She saw Paul sitting in the same spot as he was last night but her parents were not beside him anymore. “Del” what happened?” He asked. “I” I don’t know Paul. I remember waking up last night, talking to Cleo and now it’s morning.” She said, “Did you see where they took my parents?” “No Del, I am so sorry. I just woke up. I feel like I was drugged.” He told her groggily. She explained the events of the night prior to him as she freed him from his chair. “We need to find where she has them Paul. I know she is going to kill them if we don’t find her.” Her voice began to wavier and she collapsed to the dirt floor. Paul picked her up and carried her out of the warehouse placing her in the front seat of the car. She looked up at him and it hit her like a ton of bricks. She knew exactly where her parents were. “Paul we need to go back to my parents house. That is where she has them. I can feel it.” She told him, her eyes wide with terror. “We have to move fast or she is going to kill him.” Paul knew who she was talking about, the person that this whole thing stemmed from, Amun Nguyen. If he hadn’t been the greedy and insecure man that he was in his youth none of them would be going through this right now. Delylah slowly began to understand her sisters feelings the closer they drew towards the Nguyen home. She also knew why Cleopatra had brought them there of all places. That is the one thing Cleo never really had, a place to call home. It was like Delylah was linked to Cleo’s mind as she slept in their parent’s basement. Her prot’g’s were standing guard as she slept; one at the top of the basement stairs and one outside of their parent’s bedroom. “Paul pull over, we need to figure out how we are going to get into the house without them noticing us.” Delylah said as she placed a hand on his arm. “How do you know all this Del?” Paul asked looking at her confused as he pulled onto the shoulder of the road. “It’s hard to explain Paul. It’s” it’s like I am seeing everything through her eyes.” They sat for a few moments until Delylah had another vision, both men were sound asleep at their posts. “We need to move now Paul, they are all asleep.” Paul pulled back onto the road and drove like a bat out of hell toward the Nguyen home. In a matter of minutes they pulled into the end of the driveway and quickly killed the engine. They slowly stalked their way up to the front porch of the renaissance style home. Delylah slowly opened the front door and crept her way up the spiral staircase with Paul following closely behind her. “Let me go first Del.” Paul whispered. He slowly moved around her and followed the staircase up to the door of her parents? bedroom. There sat the evil twin of Bruce Willis leaned back against the wall with his hands in his lap. As soon as Paul reached for the doorknob a large hand encircled his wrist. “I don’t think so. My mistress has plans for you two.” The man said calmly. Paul tried to retaliate but was knocked unconscious and pushed back down the spiral staircase. Delylah screamed in horror and turned to run down the stairs after him. She was yanked back by her ebony hair by this beast of a man. He wrapped his arms around her and threw her into her parent’s room. She smashed her head on the footboard of the bed where her parents were tied up. The man smiled at the pain etched on her face. “You just sit tight and Cleo will be with you as soon as she wakes up. I’m sure you will just love what she has planned for tonight.” Before Delylah could respond or make a move for the door it was slammed and locked. Now all she could do was sit and wait for Cleopatra to seal their fate. She sobbed on and off the entire day as her parents slept like the dead. Finally she gave in and closed her eyes, she knew there was nothing she could do now. It was all hopeless. Her eyes snapped open as soon as she heard the doorknob begin to turn. She couldn’t believe what she saw standing in the doorway. She refused to believe it actually. It was the corpse of Dr. Elias Nagatti with his hands and feet bound together. Delylah forced back the urge to vomit. “Cleo thought he might keep you company.” The man’s maniacal laugh resonated through out the room, “She wants him here for the festivities tonight,” and then the door was slammed closed and locked once again. Delylah was more confused than ever. Why did she want her dead adoptive father here? Delylah would just have to sit and wait for either Paul to come to her rescue or Cleopatra to deliver their fate. It was only a matter of time now.
