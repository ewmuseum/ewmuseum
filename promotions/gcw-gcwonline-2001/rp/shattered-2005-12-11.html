Shattered

11 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/11/2005 The headlights cast eerie streams through the murky darkness ahead as the car continued on the upward path of the mountain roads. On the left stood formiddable walls of rock. On the right a sheer drop that should one make a false move would inevitably be the end of thier existence. Wisps of platinum blond threatened to obscure what view was there as hand swiped them away and eyes narrowed staring pointedly through the thick layer of fog. It was a dangerous trip to take, but nessecary if one wished to keep their loved one content. Fingers tightened on the steering wheel as heart bang to beat with more fervor. A dangerous path to travel... The vehicle slowly carved a path through the unrelenting miasma. A glance down read the speedometer at only twenty miles per hour on a road that was normally travelled at forty five and fifty. A sense of frustration crept into the skin prickling it with unease as those hands continued with their steady motions on the wheels. If all went well, they would be there in an hour or so. Things never went well, however, no not for this person. A large flake of icy precipitation landed on the windowshield. Then another and another. Soon they were falling steadily furthering the bleakness of the night and hieghtening the amount of anxiety already coursing deep in the bones. Muttering a curse beneath breath the figure moved a hand flicking on the wipers to help with view ahead. Another few yards taken and it was becoming damn near impossible to see the road. Only ten feet was visible at any given time. A smart person would never have taken on this journey, but a determined person would. And he was determined.... She flipped to her back within those tangled sheets. Perspiration beaded that brow as she furrowed her forehead and groaned. Rolling again she struggled with the angst she was feeling in her sleep as she helplessly watched her own hands upon a steering wheel. The heart within that chest was beginning to pound with a fury the likes she has not felt in some time. Something was horribly wrong and she knew it, yet she could not force her eyes to open and end the dark dream. Around another bend that car went a pocket of clear air revealing the forest to the left. For a moment those icy blue orbs glanced over the majestic white cropped pines. It was a moment that would forever change everything in one person's life. Life seemed to slow down as eyes turned back to the road ahead. There only a few yards ahead stood the most beautiful beast one could ever have seen. A large fourteen point white stag stood in the road like a statue. His heart crashed against his ribs even as his foot instinctively slammed on the breaks. Immediately following his action was the haunting sound of tires squealing in pain across the iced asphalt as he lost control of the car. He didnt have to see it to know what happened as the odd feeling of his rear leaving the seat gave it all away. The car was no longer on solid ground. The air in her room took on a deep chill. Breath came in plumes of mist as she moaned still struggling with the vision locked in her mind's eye. All was silent in the room for that next breath as she looked on frozen in fear and macabre awe. Immediately his spirit reached out for hers. The face of his most beloved burning brightly in his mind as his eye saw the ground coming up quickly. ' Eva,' was all he managed to say before the crunch of metal and a mighty explosion shattered the muted atmosphere all around. For that one moment the world seemed to stop. Eva lurched upwards in her bed. Her lungs felt as if someone had literally kicked them in as she sucked in shallow breaths. And then with a deeply drawn breath she released a cacaphony so shrill one could swear the heaven's answered her with the thunder and lightning that rocked the earth outside her window. The pain she felt was suffocating her. The skies outside weeped for a loss she was not certain was real or not at this time. At least she was not certain until the cell phone at her bedside shattered her uneasy silence. Trembling digits reached out for the phone next to the hotel bed. A single glance at the number and she somehow knew what was coming but clung to the idea she was wrong even as she answered the call. ' Eva?' The strained and froggy tones of Delilah was all she needed to hear as the pain renewed its strength gripping at her heart like a vice. That head began to shake back and forth in denial as she began to mumble over and over again, ' No..no no no no.' ' Eva...It's Erik...he's...' ' NO!' She didn't want to hear it. She couldn't hear. She wouldn't! The cell phone flew across the room as she struggled to breath rocking back and forth on that unfamiliar bed. No, he wasn't dead. He was alive. He was safe in their Colorado home with Delilah. She knew this. She had too, she just had too! The hotel phone rang snatching her eyes' gaze. She didn't want to pick it up. She watched that red blinking light with each ring with a dread so deep even the abyss could not fathom its depths. It was a warning. She knew it. She didn't want to face this. Not now, not after everythign was finally starting to go right! It continued to ring and seemed to get louder. The irritating tone ricocheted in her mind. Hands clamped over her ears as she curled up and screamed balefully into her pillows. This was not happening. It was all just a dream. It is just a terrible nightmare! Suddenly the room was swallowed in silence again as the phone died out. She froze, only to move a moment later staring at it skeptically. It was all okay. She would just lay down again and wake up in the morning. She would forget this vivid nightmare. KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK! She sucked in a raw breath and stared at the vibrating door as someone on the otherside pounded on it. She refused to move. ' Ms. VanLorne? Ms. VanLorne. This is the hotel manager. I need to speak with you immediately. It is of great importance.' No. No. NO! Her mind continued to scream at her even as her feet mechanically went to the floor and pumped along carrying her quaking form to the door. Numb fingers grasped the knob. The door opened and for a moment she swore she was staring straight into the face of death. ' I am so sorry to be the bearer of bad news. But the Colorado Highway Patrol asked me to give you this.' He thrust a small envelope forward with the intention for her to take it. She couldn't. She just couldn't do this. Make it all go away! Her mind continued to hammer away at the denial even as her heart began to shattered into tiny shards. She wasn't even aware of the tears that were already streaming down her face as he gripped her hand and gently placed the letter within those lifeless fingers. ' Again I am so sorry. If there is anything we can do for y-' ' What is Johnny Lexicon's room number?' ' 435.' She said nothing else to the man. That suddenly very fragile creature blindly ran through the halls in nothing more than her nightie searching frantically for the room her only friend was in. Outside his door she knocked fervently. ' Johnny!' BANG BANG BANG! ' Johnny wake up please! ' A grumble could be heard on the other side as bare feet came to the door. Suddenly it swung open and the irritated face of Johnny Lexicon was quickly melting to one of concern as she stood there. Tears were falling like a waterfall as she lifted the letter for him to see. ' He is dead Johnny.' He came forward pulling her gently inside. ' Who is?' ' Erik.'
