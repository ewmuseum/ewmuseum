Sabotage

29 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29 June 05 I finally put everything in my gym bags and quickly zipped it up. I checked my pockets for my keys, plane tickets, wallet, etc. When everything was in order I lifted the bags on my shoulder and walked out of the bedroom. I jogged down the steps and made my way to the living room where Mary was sitting on the couch. She looked up slightly when I came into the room and gave an awkward smile. I did my best to hide my concern but with Mary I wasn’t always the best at keeping what was going on a secret. I put the bags down on the floor and walked to where Mary was sitting. I bent down until I was on one knee in front of her. My hands immediately found hers and as they touched she grasped at my hands. I brought my right hand up and tilted Mary’s chin up so that she could look at me. “Mami que pasa?” Her head slightly shook from side to side obviously trying to blow off the question. “It’s nada Berto, Mary’s just feelin” a bit sick.” I could see right through her and I am pretty sure she knew. The problem with that is the fact that she’ll stay further emotionally if she KNOWS that I know she is lying. I tilted my head to the side, almost like a dog does when they are confused over something. “Well ?” I paused, not sure if I should do what I was thinking. “Do you want to come to Boston with me?” Her head jolted upwards and for a moment she had that sparkles in her eyes, that sparkle that she had been missing in the last few weeks. As quickly as it came though it disappeared. Her head fell again and a deep sigh came from her body. “No lovey-dovey, Mary stay here. Don’t want to make yous nervous on your BIIIG night.” There was no changing this woman’s mind that was for sure. I leaned in a kissed her cheek. With a smile and a slight wink I stood back up straight. “Well Mary I’ll be back as soon as the show is over. We will have plenty of celebrating to do after I beat Jason for the World Title.” “Mary be here!” We looked at each other for a moment before I turned around and lifted my bags up and walked to the door. I stopped before opening the door and looked at her. Slowly her eyes met mine. “I Love you Hermosa.” “Love you too Statty-bear.” After a quick smile I opened the door and stepped out into the sunshine. Roberto had been gone for only a few hours but Mary already felt like a piece of her had broken away from her soul. She knows she probably should just ask Roberto about Eva instead of worrying that he was fucking her on the road. Then again would he admit it even if he was? While he seemed sincere even Mary could tell he was a bit different every since War Path went off the air. She couldn’t quite put her finger on the difference but she could sense it. Scary thing is she doesn’t know if she should be nervous or not. When Damien Black turned on her she never suspected it. He hid his dark side so well that she didn’t know about it until it was too late. With Roberto his dark side is evident because he barely tries to hide it. In fact sometimes it seems that he even embraces it. Is a self aware man more dangerous than a man who hides it totally? Whatever would happen Mary knew she probably would go crazy, or at least more so than she already feels, if she stayed inside the whole time. Something inside her told her to call Skye and see if they could go out and have fun or maybe hunt Rain down. Yet she stayed put in that seat, sunken in and her mind wandering into dark places. She couldn’t shake it no matter how hard she tried. She had been let down by the opposite sex for so long that it was so hard to put her complete trust into anyone. Yet there he was, her KNIGHT in shining armor so to speak. Life was funny like that sometimes though. It seems that sometimes when you feel that everything is crashing down around you that something unexpected happens that can make you feel invincible ? like you can fly. What Roberto did for her life was a major miracle. She felt that she had a reason for living again and it was an amazing feeling. With every look, every touch, kiss, or hug her life was that much more meaningful. That is exactly why she had to have at least SOME faith that this Eva story was all fake. Just as he promised, after he won the world title he’d walk through that front door, kiss her and lift her into the air. They’d drink wine, make love, talk, laugh, make love again, and again. Then they’d put everything out in the open ? their love would be even stronger than before. Mary’s thoughts were interrupted by the sound of a phone ringing. She looked around until she found her cell phone. “Que Pasa?” “Ms. Typhoid Mary?” “Si seenyor!” “Hello this is Aaron Jones and I am a representative of HSW. I am calling in regards to your cousin Kagerro.” “Somethin wrong !”” “Well she had a bit of an accident in a match she had last night and has been injured. She is going to need to recuperate for a week or so but she has nowhere to go. We asked her if she had any family that could help and she gave us your number.” “Well Gosh lee! Wheres Kagerro at?” “Mexico City. Can you fly out tonight?” “Yessum Mary be there as soon as she can.” “Thank you Mary.” Mary hung up her cell phone and quickly rushed upstairs to the bedroom to throw a bunch of clothes into a small suitcase. It didn’t take her long to get ready, she was just ready to leave the house when she remembered to write Roberto a note. -Baby Cakes! Mary had to see Kagerro in Mexico! Call ya soon! Love, Mary She kissed the small sheet of paper and left it on the living room table where she knew he would find it. She rushed out of the door and got into the cab that she called not too long before that. The lock came apart easily and I opened the door, very careful that no one seen me. I walked through this enormous house that one Roberto Abitua lived in and must say I was quite impressed to say the least. He had done well for himself over the years, that was for certain. I had little time to waste checking out his home though as I was paid good money to do a certain job. I walked through the now dark house, most people would have slipped or stumbled to find a light switch by now but ? well that’s why I am a professional. I reached the living room and quickly found the table, where the note was waiting. I reached out and picked up the original letter and read what Mary wrote to him. “Aw isn’t that fucking precious?” With a roll of my eyes and a slight chuckle I crumbled that note up and pulled out a different one from my pocket, and placed it where the original was. I looked over and saw a picture, it was of Roberto and Mary on a beach. I picked it up and stared at it for awhile ? before realizing this needed some dramatic effect. I tossed the picture frame on the ground, letting the glass shatter near my foot. “Have a nice life Roberto.”
