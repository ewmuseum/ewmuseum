Discovering DeBough: Part I

31 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 31st, 2007 It was a cold day in New York City, and the wind just made it feel like it was in the negatives. Tyson walked down the sidewalk, a fresh White Chocolate Mocha from Starbucks in his hands, hovering close to his face as he absorbed what little steam came off the hot liquid. His A&F scarf wrapped tightly around his neck, a sweater from the gap, and designer jeans were no match for the New England cold. He checked his watch, and quickend his step, realizing he was late for his appointment with Jack Stiffland, of a local modeling agency. Though Tyson had joined up with GCW, he still kept his modeling on the side, as extra income. Many would say that modeling was for fairies, or for girls only, but you ask any straight male model, and they’ll tell you differently. Hell, why the fuck wouldn’t you want to be around a dozen scantily clad, gorgeous women for a few hours at a time? Tyson turned into a four story business complex, and made his way through the lobby, loosening his scarf as he walked towards the elevator. It was warmer inside, which was a welcome change from the outdoor nip. He pressed the button for the third floor, and waited as a few other stragglers made their way to the elevator before the doors shut. “I hate crammed elevators. Someone always smells funny, and evereyone always has that wierd “elevator silence.”” Thought DeBough to himself. The elevator stopped on the third floor, and Tyson pushed himself through the clusterfuck of people who were apparently all on their way to the fourth floor, and stepped out into the open. At the end of the hallway, was a door marked “Stiffland Modeling Agency.” Tyson shrugged, fixed his shirt, and made his way down the hall. “I swear to fuck, if this guy is gay?” He opened the door, and walked inside. The waiting area was nice enough. Sort of a retro, meets modern feel done all in black and white. He made his way to the reception desk, where a lovely young woman in her mid twenties sat. She had light brown hair, cut to her shoulders, and stunningly blue eyes. Contacts, perhaps, but Tyson guessed natural. Her nametag read “Monica.” Tyson turned on the charm. “Monica.” He said, voice oozing with flirtation. “I’m here to see Jack.” Monica giggled, as did most women when Tyson was around, and especially when he flirted with them. Tyson was not your average looking male. In fact, he put the likes of George Clooney, and Brad Pitt to shame. Dark brown hair, blue eyes, and a mediterranian tan accented his features perfectly. A well trimmed goatee added charactered to his perfectly chiseled face, and his choice in clothing was impeccable. Underneath all of it, was a well toned, athletically built man, void of body hair. (It’s amazing what laser hair removal can do nowadays). “Are you Mr. DeBough?” She said, returning the flirting. “Please dear, call me Tyson.” Tyson returned, leaning over and resting his arms on her desk. “It’s what all my friends call me.” Monica flushed. “I’ll call him right away Mr. DeB’Tyson.” She caught herself, a schoolgirl grin spreading across her face. “Thank you Monica.” Tyson pulled a piece of paper out of his pocket, and quickly wrote down his cell phone number. He rarely asked for women’s numbers anymore. It was much easier to have them call you, he had found. “Here.” He said, pushing it over in front of her. “Call me sometime.” Her eyes widened, he was sure, but didn’t actually see because he was already walking down the isle towards Jack’s office. He knocked on the door twice, and waited for a response. Pause. Pause. Pause. Nothing. He knocked again, this time a little bit harder. ??the fuck?” He muttered to himself. Just then, Monica came around the corner, a puzzled look on her face. “He’s not answering my page.” “Yea, and he’s not answering my knock.” Monica pulled her keys out, and unlocked his door, and the pair entered. Jack’s chair was turned so that the back faced Tyson and Monica. Tyson walked around the desk, a broad smile on his face. “Jacky boy! Long time no se?” His voice stopped short, and the smile vanished from his face. “Jesus fuck?” he breathed. Monica came around the desk to see what he was looking at, and screamed. Tyson shook his head. “Not again?” .~. July 31st, 1999 “Make sure he can’t get out this time.” A voice said in the darkness of what seemed to be a basement. A single light cut the dimly lit cellar, casting shadows for personalities. “I still don’t know how he got out last time.” Another voice said in response. The sound of a palm hitting a forehead. “Because you didn’t tie him tight enough, you stupid fuck.” A light flickers on in another corner of the room, shedding a bit more light on the situation. Tied to a chair, bloodied and battered sits the man we know as Tyson DeBough. He’s shirtless, and his back is split open in various places from what looks like whip marks. One man, whose quite large in his build, and has a bald head, walks over to him and grabs his face, slapping him awake. ??the fuck?” Tyson says, gaining consciousness. “Well hello there princess, how’re ya feelin?” The big man mocks. “Tommy, what the fuck?” Tyson struggles, his ribs apparently cracked, constricting his breathing. “Didja miss us while you were gone?” Tommy says. “Yea, didja miss us?” The other man says, whose about as big as Tyson. Tyson turns his head a little, and winces. “Joey?” SMACK! “Shut. Up. Tyson!” The big man yells, giving Tyson a backhand to the mouth. “What, the fuck, do you want Tommy.” Tyson hisses. Tommy chuckles, joined by Joey. “The same thing the boss always wants Tys.” He spits on the ground. “I told you, I don’t have it.” Joey bends down and cups Tyson’s chin in his hand, making him look him in the eyes. “Now c’mon Tyson, don’t give me that. You know where it is, and you’re going to find it for us.” “Or what Joey” You gonna kill me?” Tyson spits defiantely. “Then even if I did know where it was, you wouldn’t be able to get it because I’d be dead. So why don’t you back the fuck off me, and take me to see Nick.” Joey lets go, and backs into a corner. “Fucking cunt.” He shoots back at Tyson, more for his own motivation than insult. “Tommy, cut me the fuck out of this.” Tysons yells. Tommy hesitates. “NOW!” The big oaf turns into a puddle in front of DeBough as he barks the order. Tommy walks over and cuts him out. “Now, you can’t run Tyson?” He starts, only to be cut off by Tyson’s now free hand to his face. “Shut up Tommy, and take me to see Nick.” .~. Jack Stiffland had been shot in the back of the head. Normally only leaves an exit wound about the size of an acorn, if that. Unless you use a shotgun, in which case the exit wound is’well, non-existant because the face, along with half the head is gone. NYPD was now on the scene, and yellow tape was everywhere, Monica was in tears/shock over in a corner being talked to by an officer, and Tyson was on the opposite side of the room, awaiting his turn. Fucking cops. Always wanting to assume everyone at the scene of the crime knows exactly what happened, and is guilty. It was the bane of their existence. A few minutes later, one of the officers approached Tyson. “I’m Officer Carnegie, with the NYP?” “Yes, I realize who you are.” Tyson cut him off, pointing to his nametag. “Sir, I’ll have to ask you to not do that again. This is a serious situation.” Carnegie said sternly. “Right, sorry.” Tyson said. Carnegie flipped through his clipboard. “So you were here for an appointment, right?” “Yup.” “And you walked into his office’why?” “Because he wasn’t answering when I knocked.” Tyson said. “Or Monica’s pages, correct?” “That’s what Monica said.” “And then you entered the room, and saw Mr. Stiffland as he is now, correct?” “Good Lord, this guy is a fucking captain of stating the obvious.” Thought Tyson to himself. “Yes officer. I’ve told you what I saw.” Carnegie folded his arms. “Right, well, here’s my card. We’ll be in touch.” “Uh huh.” Tyson turned and walked away from the officer. He hated those sorts of things, mostly because it was exactly like it just happened. An officer, probably relatively new to the force, tries to act like he knows exactly what he’s doing, by asking a bunch of obvious questions. Fucking annoying. His phone rang, so he pulled it out and flipped it open. “Hello?” ? “Uh huh?” ? “Great, that’ll be fine.” ? Ok, later. He hung up. Since losing to Jesse Ramey, he’d been focused on a way to get back at Ramey, and boost himself up the rankings in GCW. And, they’d just dropped the solution into his lap. A fucking battle royal, with a few other people, for a chance to take on the TV Champion. “whatever the fuck that was. It was a title, and that was enough for Tyson. And apparently it was a bunch of other newbies, along with Jesse Ramey. “Fucking two birds with one stone.” Bloody good day in his book. To be continued?
