The Broken Mind of David Spencer, Part I

20 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/20/10 The Broken Mind of David Spencer Part I: A Nightmare's Dream Becomes a Dreamer's Nightmare It's two in the morning on May 1, 2010 in Honolulu, Hawaii. Many GCW wrestlers are on the mend after the night's grueling royal. Almost no one needs said mend as much as David Spencer, although that's a bit of a given, with him being driven into concrete after a fifteen foot drop...face-first. It's the kind of thing that makes a chokeslam into pavement look like a bubble bath. Alas, there will be no bubble bath for David tonight as he stumbles through his hotel room doorway, practically falling over in the process. Luckily, he manages to catch himself on the bathroom doorway. After he collects himself for a few moments and turns on the lights, he closes the door to his hotel room, locking it as he does. Before David can even release the door handle, though, an absolutely horrid sounding cough nearly doubles him over. He almost passes out, but a steady dripping sound manages to catch his attention. The sound is coming from directly below him, so he looks down. What he sees is a small puddle of blood accumulating on the tiled floor. Another drop of blood joins the pool, clueing David's very disoriented thought process in on the fact that the drops are coming from him. Wanting to learn more about this new development, David slowly hobbles into the bathroom and turns on the light, turning his attention to the mirror. He sees that his previously plain white tee has a giant streak of red down the middle of it, coming directly from the bottom of his mask. A normal man would have gone to the hospital after what David went through, but David's hatred of hospitals and general lack of caring for his body stopped him from doing that. Bunch of greedy pigs... thinks David, just before he lets out another nasty cough, causing more blood to trickle out from under his mask. I already got my medication from them... David slowly lifts up the bottom portion of his mask, allowing access to his mouth. He grabs the side of the mirror with his now bloodied hands and opens it up to reveal a medical cabinet. Numerous bottles of pills are strewn all over the various shelves. Many of the bottles don't even have labels. David rummages through the bottles, his scarred and bloody hands shaking slightly. He picks out a bottle and opens it, letting a few pills slide out into his hand. He slams them back, closing the bottle and tossing it back into the mess it came from. He then picks out another bottle and pops a pill from it, tossing it back as well. He repeats this process with two more bottles before closing the medical cabinet. Satisfied, David stumbles and hobbles his way out of the bathroom. He uses the walls to support himself, smearing blood on them as he does. He soon reaches his bed, collapsing onto it the moment he gets there. He rolls to his back and, with his mask only half off, he loses consciousness and enters a deep slumber. Soon, an old memory begins to surface within his mind. . . . It's three in the morning on July 27, 2009 in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. WorldWide 114 has ended and the fans have gone home, leaving the road crew to pack up GCW's presence at the Wachovia Center and ship it off to its next location. Yet, somewhere else in the city, a piece of GCW remains. Walking slowly through an alleyway is Face, in his typical mismatching, dilapidated suit. A waning lamp and the moon are the only two sources of light, barring the occasional flicker from Face's lighter, allowing darkness to consume the path before him. The neighbourhood Face is walking through is on the fringe of the city, where there's much less cars at this time of night, providing a near silent atmosphere. For Face, it's an environment that allows for the deepest of thoughts. He soon slips out of reality and recounts his latest dream, although most wouldn't call it that. A shriek that could pierce an elephant's hide; a blade glistening in the moonlight; a desperate cry for help; a splatter of blood across the concrete floor; an engulfing flame that rages rapidly; these are the five acts of Face's nightmare play. In fact, it's just like Romeo and Juliet, except without the love and in the present and with more emphasis on the violence and death and much less talking and, instead of Mercutio and Paris, there's just a bunch of dead bodies on fire. A much better tale... those plays always draw out the bloody ending. Why not just start there? muses Face, a hidden smile forming underneath his mask. He shakes his head, confused at the world he lives in, yet fully aware of it. They all live with their morals...their safe lives... he thinks to himself as he continues down the alley, trapped in his fantasy and blissfully unaware of what surrounds him. Face stares longingly into the flickering light being produced in his hands. He admires its power and identifies with its lack of concern for its surroundings. They can have those lives... I'll take my freedom. However, before any analysis or thoughts on freedom and the possible relationship it shares with fire can be pursued, a rustling in some nearby garbage cans behind Face steals his focus away from his dancing flame. He turns around, almost startled, to find a man putting the lid on a garbage can, having just made a deposit in it. The man stares at Face for a few moments, then opens his eyes wide. "Hey, you're that Face guy from GCW," he states, pointing towards the masked man. "Tough loss tonight, huh? Oh, by the way, my name is Fred. My friend at work said it would be better for me to develop my character if I let people know more about me right off the bat. I've always been kind of a shy guy, you know." It's a dark alleyway... There's no one around... Face thinks to himself as he scouts around, not really paying any attention to any of Fred's rambling. He gives Fred a quick look-over, noticing the man's torn jeans, stained white tee, scraggly hair and unshaven face. Likely has no one living with him... "Uh, you all there buddy?" Fred questions after receiving no response from Face. He's initially confused, but then begins to worry, remembering just exactly who he is attempting to talk to. Fred's eyes dart down the alley he just came from to his front door, then up to the sides of the buildings around him. He realizes that there is a lack of windows around him. Face realizes this as well. I can get away with this is what is going through Face's mind as he begins to walk towards his newfound friend, that cold, empty, never-changing expression looking as spooky as ever in the occasional flickering of his lighter flame. "Ummm... heyyy..." mutters Fred, trying to search for something to say as he backs up in the direction of his front door. Raising his hands defensively, he tries to defuse the situation with reassuring words and praise. "Don't worry about that loss... Losses aren't the end of the world, right? I mean, you're a really good wrestler!" Face is paying no attention to Fred's words, though. The only thing going through his mind is one all-consuming thought: It won't be just a dream anymore! Noticing that Face is still not listening to him and that the masked man known for purposely causing injuries in GCW is heading his way, Fred takes a big gulp. He quickly looks behind him to see how far his door is. Although, in his mind, even a foot away would be too far. "What's the matter?" Face inquires with his usual raspy tone as he continues to stalk forward, his head tilting to the side inquisitively. "Don't you want my autograph?" Fred constantly darts his eyes between his door and Face, giving a half-hearted, shaky thumbs up. He's swearing bullets now. His voice quivering in fear, he utters his response: "Uh, yeah! I'm just going to go get my-" And, without even bothering to think of a practical thing to get from his house, Fred turns, almost falling over in the process, and runs as fast as he can towards his doorway. Face shoots after him, pocketing his lighter. The man gets closer and closer to the door, not daring to look back in fear of it costing him a precious millisecond, a commodity he can't afford to lose at this time. Unfortunately, with just a few more feet to go, Face manages to catch up and tackle the man to the ground. It's at this point that Fred regrets deciding to break out of his cocoon of shyness at three in the morning in a dark alleyway while being alone with a crazy masked man with a fetish for fire and pain. "HEL-" is all that Fred gets out before Face punches him in the throat, causing him to choke and gasp violently. Face punches him a few times in the ribs and gut, inciting more coughing and gasping, before taking a full mount position on Fred as soon as he drops down. Then, in one swift motion, Face slips his arm behind the man's neck, shoves the man's arm against it on the opposite side and presses his own head against that of his victim. Face then grabs his remaining arm with the one around the man's neck and locks in an arm triangle. Fred struggles against it, even punching his assailant in the head a few times. But the struggle is all in vain as Face simply leaps up and over to his victim's side, stepping his way upwards, making the choke tighter and tighter with every step. Feeling his victim is about to pass out, Face whispers into the man's ear: "Dear Fred, thanks for letting me pursue my dreams...sincerely, Face." With those final words, Face really squeezes and presses in on the choke, letting out a harsh cackle he had been holding back for some time. A single tear rolls down Fred's cheek before he drops out of his conscious state. Face enters euphoria, revelling in his ability to do what he wants without any of the normal consequences. After all, he normally has to wait a week or more before getting back into the ring to do the same thing. However, before Face can do anything further, the cold nature of reality sets in. "Hey! Let that man go!" Face releases his hold and quickly gets to his feet, turning around to see a police officer running around the corner. An immense amount of adrenaline shoots through his entire body as he gets thrown out of euphoria and into panic. Could I take him out too? wonders Face. The policeman answers his thoughts by stopping a few feet away and pulling out a pistol, pointing it directly at Face, yelling: "Put your hands up!" A few moments later, a few doors in the alleyway open up, with curious eyes peering out of each and every one of them. They begin to whisper and murmur with each other. And then, from around the corner steps a man dressed in a very similar way to Face, except without a mask. This is the man who had alerted the policeman of the crime taking place; a homeless man who Face had unknowingly passed by while his dark thoughts took over his mind. Face's inevitable outburst had finally happened. Unfortunately, it wasn't as easy to elude the law as it had been in his dreams. Despite all his calculating and all his wit, he had been caught. And so, with great reluctance, Face raises his hands up in the air.
