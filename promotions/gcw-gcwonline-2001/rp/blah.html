Date: Today One of the first things you learn upon becoming a pro wrestler is to grow eyes in the back of your head because you never know who may be lurking behind you looking to drive a knife into your back. And I learned that the hard way. I got jumped many times in the early days of my career, being dumb enough to get people pissed off at me, and not smart enough to know they'd get back at me with violence. Now I knew better, sorta. After all I wasn't smart enough to realize that RipeFist was an anagram for Spitfire. And I ended up getting buried alive for my stupidity. But when Dan Rivero and his goon squad threatened to jump me at the last WW, I grew those eyes in the back of my head. And nothing happened. Nothing. Everytime I left my locker room I walked slowly looking around, making sure all my blind spots were covered at once, tensing everytime I heard footsteps behind me, coming towards me, or coming around a corner. I spent most of the night in my locker room, everytime I heard footsteps I tensed, when Vince Jones burst in to rant about some bullshit Ricky Thunder had said, I managed to keep my cool, despite the fact that I nearly shit a brick. When I headed out to the ring to have my say with Rich Rollins I thought for sure someone would jump out from behind before I got to the ring, and during the match I left quickly after Rollins got us disqualified wondering if Rivero and his buddies would show. And nothing happened. I spent all night worrying for nothing. And now I find out I have another six man tag match next week. This time I'm teamed with Johnny Borealis and Toxic against Stevens and Thunder again, and Marauder. And birthday boy LeStatt Knight is making himself special referee. I don't know if I should be worried or relived. I wonder if I should even care, I'm gonna still be looking out for Rivero and his maked cohorts. And Jeff never did manage to get to the arena. Damn rental cars. I also haven't slept well the last few nights, I've been tossing and turning for hours before sleep finally claims me, only to be rudely pulled back to the land of the living by the arrival of the morning. I've been dragging my ass through the last couple of days, but if I was gonna get anywhere in this six man match this week I was gonna have to pull my head out of my ass and get on the ball. I pulled my rental car over to the gas station I spotted on my way to the hotel and had bought seveal bottles of Vault. I grabbed another one from the mini-fridge in my room and slugged one back trying to locate my get up and go, which apparently had got up and gone. I found my bottle of asprin and tossed a couple of those back trying to kill the nagging headache which had been my constant companion since I woke up this morning. I stepped out onto the balcony of my room and watched the sun set and looked down at the traffic as the lights started lighting up the city. I trudged back inside, I was too tired to do anything else but sit down. Despite the energy drink coursing through my system, I had no energy to speak of. Just a nagging headache and the overwhelming urge to curl up into a ball and disappear. My mother once said I was prone to mood swings, and this had to be one of them. Some might say my sudden downturn in attitude had to do with the three straight losses I suffered. The first at the hands of Bryan Mayhem and the loss of the TV title, then getting buried by Aiden Jackson at Devil's Night, and then the loss in the six man match the other night. Maybe it was, maybe it wasn't. Maybe it was the mood swings, maybe it wasn't. All I know is I had a major case of the blahs. They're like the blues, you just can't sing them.
