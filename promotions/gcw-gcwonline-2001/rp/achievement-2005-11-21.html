Achievement

21 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-21-05 Chrissy-Poo plops down in his favorite easy-chair with a large leather bound book in his lap. He opens not only the book? but a doorway to another world! Chrissy-Poo: “Mayhem” mayhem? M? M.A? Chrissy-Poo quickly leafs through the pages of the book. Suddenly he stops and reads aloud? Chrissy-Poo: “Precision” noun? See Kenny Mercury? Shit, I grabbed my UWF edition dictionary? this thing is worthless!” Chrissy-Poo tosses the large dictionary over his shoulder. It crashes into the wall. The Poo then stands up and snags a different book from a coffee table. He opens this new dictionary and his eyes dart back and forth as he speeds through its contents. Chrissy-Poo: “Aha! Mayhem?” ?*Ahem*? Noun? A state of violent disorder or riotous confusion; havoc. Hmmm? Chrissy-Poo scratche his head for a moment. Chrissy-Poo: “I guess Bryan’s the one who’s “riotously confused?” Mayhem’s neither an adjective nor meaning anything about “evil”, dude?” Chrissy-Poo shuts the book and sets it down. Chrissy puts his hands on his hips and cracks a small smile. He shakes his head slowly. Chrissy-Poo: “Bryan” Bryan you really are a moron.” Poo tilts his head back and chuckles. Chrissy-Poo: “You think that you scare me” You think that you’re intimidating me? Do you think I care who the fuck your brother is?” Chrissy surveys his room, trying to get his mind off Bryan and the match at hand for a moment? just enough to settle down a bit. Chrissy-Poo: “I’m tired of it” I’m tired of being the butt of everyone’s joke. I’m tired of being looked down upon? My whole life, I’ve been brushed aside and forgotten? I’ve been labeled as mediocre at best. Well guess what, Bryan? mediocrity STINKS? It reeks as bad as your black and white painted taint, man? NC-17? I show the world what the hell I’m REALLY made of.” “Everyone wants to brush aside Chrissy-Poo. They know I’m here but *pfft*? I’m not going anywhere. Everyone knows that I’m only fucking five foot nine and a hundred sixty pounds” Everyone knows the odds are stacked against me each and every single time I step in that ring? Everyone knows I’ve got heart? I’ve got enough heart so that after I get my ass whipped like a dog, I’ll get back up and stand in line for the next show so it can happen all again.” “That’s what they know. But what they don’t know is this?” “Not only do I have enough heart to come back for more each and every day” I’ve got enough OVERCOME ANY obstacle? I’ve got enough? to win. And what people don’t know is that it takes more than heart to win a match? It takes skill. And that’s something people seemed to have not considered, man? People never stopped and thought that maybe, just maybe I have reached this level, the GCW level, because I’ve got more than just heart. They never thought that I was in GCW because of any other reason aside from I’m CRAZY and I can take a little PUNISHMENT! No one ever thought that I was here for any other reason than to be a rag doll for fat headed fucks like you? No ever thought? thought that I was in the GCW to ACHIEVE something? No one thought that I reached this level because of my talent, skill, and drive to SUCCEED.” “I am NOT a God damn, side show” I am NOT your monkey? I am a professional wrestler and I am in this federation to WIN!” “You can ignore me because of my size” you can discard me as a novelty act? believe me? I’m used to it. No one has EVER believed in me? No one but myself.” “Bryan O’Neil” At NC-17 you and the WHOLE WORLD are going to have your eyes opened? opened to ME? It’s MY time to step up on the grandest stage? and SUCCEED? to achieve my dreams? At Devil’s Night I buried you? at NC-17, not only will I bury you? but all the doubters? and all the critics? and all the smarks? and all the holier than thou “experts?” At NC-17 I bury Chrissy-Poo? and emerge? A CHAMPION.” Insights? Months ago? The GCW ring sits in a half empty arena. From the looks of the set? Tonight is Wednesday and this is GCW’s World Wide. Two wrestlers battle ferociously in the ring? though none of the those few in attendance seem to care. There’s no fancy lighting on? or play by play commentators? no entrance music or long introductions? just two guys going at it, giving the match their all. A small handful of people in suits or GCW polo shirts watch the match vigilantly. There are two men in the ring? one significantly larger than the other. The first one has shoulder length scraggily brown hair and wears blue tights and cowboy boots? the other looks like he should be working at McDonalds, not bumping it in the squared circle. He’s a small guy, and is really getting it taken to him by the larger man. The larger combatant tosses the smaller one into the ring. He hits the ropes and rockets towards his opponent at full speed. The tiny wrestler launches himself into the air, latches onto his enemy around the neck with his lightning quick legs, and turns him inside out with a flying head scissors. The larger man is tossed across the ring and the little firecracker kips up onto his feet. He dashes up the ropes and eyes his prey upon the top turnbuckle. The larger wrestler slowly staggers to his feet. That’s when he strikes? the small fighter springs off the turnbuckle and dives at his opponent? High crossbody? A move that could set up a pinning combination for the outmatched David against his Goliath? “er” No? The bigger wrestler catches him in midair and absolutely drills him into the canvass with an ultra-powerful powerslam. He hooks a leg and the referee counts to three. The bell sounds. Chrissy-Poo’s eyes roll back into his head as he stares at the arena rafters? which seem like they’re a million miles away? Holy shit? That freakin? hurt? Backstage? In the locker room, Chrissy-Poo takes off his torn and sweat dosed Pantera t-shirt. His body aches from head to toe. Poo touches his shirt to the cut above his eye? the salty sweat stings? yeah? this is pro-wrestling? Just then the larger wrestler opens up the locker next to Chrissy and tosses his boots inside. Wrestler: “So how do you think you did, kid?” Chrissy-Poo: “I dunno” How’d I do?” Wrestler: “You didn’t answer my question” How do YOU THINK you did?” Chrissy-Poo: “Alright” I guess.” Wrestler: “Heh” no confidence? You did pretty damn good out there, kid? and no matter what happens now? know that. Know that you did pretty damn good.” Chrissy-Poo: “Maybe” but you really kicked my ass out there?” Wrestler: “Heh” there’s a lot more to professional wrestling than just kicking ass? Do you know how marketable a guy my age is to the GCW?” Chrissy-Poo: “No” I, uh? no, not really.” Wrestler: “Not at all” They don’t need anyone who’s over forty and doesn’t already have a name for himself? But you on the other hand? you’re young? you’ve got a shot.” Chrissy-Poo: “Yeah, well” how marketable do you thing a guy my size is? Huh?” Wrestler: “A little shit like you” Heh? I don’t know what those GCW talent scouts think? but I’d pay good money to see you get the shit beat outta you. Like I said, there’s a lot more to pro-wrestling than kicking people’s asses.” Chrissy-Poo: “Wow” thanks? thanks a lot, that REALLY helps?” Wrestler: “Ah” I see you know a little something about sarcasm? but I know a little something about wrestling. Look, it doesn’t matter how big you are? if you use your size right. Nothing really matters as long as you use it correctly and to your advantage. You might not think you’ve got a chance in this company? but believe you me? there’s a window? it might a small one? but its there? a small window of opportunity. I see those GCW guys thinking that they might just be able to use you, if only for a few weeks? You’ve got character and you’ve got SKILLS? and they can use you. Maybe not for long? but that’s the window? that’s your chance? you gotta seize the opportunities that present themselves and run with it? Believe me? I know a little something about recognizing opportunity and grabbing it.” Chrissy-Poo: “Yeah but” What’s the point of trying my best if I only get to be used for a week or two?” ? “Hey, where’d you go?” The older wrestler is no where to be found? nor is his gear? ? and for that matter? neither is Chrissy-Poo’s. Chrissy-Poo: “WHAT THE FUCK!” MY GEAR!” ? “AND MY WALLET! SON OF A BITCH!” And on that day? a dream was achieved. Chrissy-Poo received his GCW contract? and so a new chapter began? With the end of one era? so begins another? and with his signing bonus Chrissy purchased a new jock strap. That cheeky monkey.
