The Forced Stork

12 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/12/06 Hours seemed to have passed as she mechanically rolled that card through her fingers. A far away look remained etched in her gaze as she struggled between selfishness and morality. What to do? A quick glance at the clock had the time at 3:20 A.M. More to the point it was 9:20 A.M. in Berlin. A slow rush of air fled her lungs as her gaze swung to the phone that lay only a few feet away. What should she do? Her aunt would know. Eva pulled up from her chair and crossed the distance to pick up the phone. For a moment she hesitated in dialing the number. After all, she hadn't spoken with her aunt since Erik's death. She grimmaced at the thought of her brother. If he were here, he would know just what to say to her. That thought had her grimmace again, followed by a groan. The truth was, she already knew what he would say. She released a force sigh and glanced at the card again. Thumb pressed the on button to the cordless phone and before she could stop herself...she dialed the number to the orphanage. 'Sprechen Sie Michael Waisenhaus heilig. Wie kann ich Sie helfen,' the voice on the other end answered. ' Ich rufe Beachtung zu einem Kind dort herein. Theresa Snyder. Mein Name ist Eva VanLorne. Ich wurde gebeten, zu werden gerufen.' ' OH! Eine Minute bitte. Der Direktor m’chte mit Ihnen pers’nlich sprechen. ' She said nothing in response. A slender hand rose and rubbed across her faceand through her tresses in silent agony over her torn feelings on the matter as a series of clicks sounded in her ear. In a moment she heard an aged male voice come on the line. ' Ms. VanLorne?' ' Yes.' ' My name is Michael Drescher. I have been trying to reach you in regards to a young girl by the name of --' ' Theresa. Yes, yes I know.' There was an awkward moment of silence as both of them felt at a loss for words. She heard him clear his throat a few times and cringed at her own cowardice. Erik would be ashamed at her lack of ability to simply say what she knew she would eventually say, consenquences be damned. Mr. Drescher saved her any further turmoil by speaking once more. ' I know this is not an easy decision for you Ms. VanLorne. This is not a usual happenstance, but I feel it only right to give you the chance to give Theresa a good home. It will not be easy by any means, but as her biological mother, we are willing to sign custody over to you. She is a well behaved child. Though...' Eva frowned as he trailed off. ' Though what, Mr. Drescher?' He drew a deep breath. ' She has not spoken a word since the news of her parents death.' Eva's heart dropped. In that moment she knew that, despite the fact Theresa had been raised by someone else, she was just like her. When her mother had died she refused to speak for weeks. It had been Erik who had broken her spell of silence by goading her into a fight. When their father had died, she refused to speak to anyone other than Erik. Those icy blue eyes squeezed shut then opened as she looked to the ceiling seeking some sort of divine strength. ' I see, ' she paused then rushed on before she lost her nerve,' I can not come for her until this weekend. I would like everything that is hers packed up from her home and shipped to my home in Pennsylvania. If she has an inheritance I will need all funds transferred to a new account at my bank here. All the information you need will be faxed in the morning.' She had done it. She had finally come to terms with the fact she had no choice but to take her daughter in. Though she had known she would, the doubt still lay like a fresh wound. Was she doing the right thing? Mr. Drescher's voice interrupted her train of thought. ' So you are willing to take her?' ' Yes. I can not leave her to be raised alone. My family would not have wanted that.' ' I understand. I will begin the process of custodial rights immediately. Temporary custody will be approved before you get here. But, you will have to return for the custody hearing to finalize everything in a few weeks.' ' I understand.' ' Thank you Ms. VanLorne. Are there any questions in regards to Theresa or anything else?' ' Just one, ' she asked, ' Does she speak English?' ' I wish I knew. As I said, she hasn't spoken a word.' She nodded despite the fact he could not see it. ' Thank you Mr. Drescher. I will be in contact to give you my flight details tomorrow.' The end call button was pressed before he could respond. Quietly her gaze traveled over the penthouse as another wave of doubt flooded her form. So many questions left unanswered. So much to be done. Did she have what it took to be a mother? Only time, trial and error would tell.
