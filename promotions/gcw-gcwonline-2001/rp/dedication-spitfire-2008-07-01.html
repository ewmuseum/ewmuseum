Date: 1 July 08 Jan 13th 2002 Miami, FL He was sore that there was no doubt about. He had been busting his ass for months now, every night giving it his all but yet somehow was stuck in one place. At times it felt like he was running in circles, only getting a glimpse of what life was like at a higher level. 'Another night, another win, more disrespect.' LeStatt Knight spoke these words under his breath as he walked behind the curtains after a 'thrilling' match with Capo. It was becoming a tiring scene to him because he knew what kind of fame and fortune rested inside of him, but Steven Caldera didn't see it like Knight did. He didn't know what else to do or where else to go with his problems. On one hand, it was Caldera who gave him his shot at a big time fed, prior to this he was simply 'Khris Krowe' on the Indy circuit making $100 a week, if that. Then on the other hand Caldera is the one that was keeping him from blowing up like the other stars, such as Octavian, The Jhub, Toxic, and Tristain Mayhem. Knight knew deep down that he was better than any of them, but needed the opportunity to prove it. So lost in his thoughts, Knight barely noticed as he turned a hallway corner that he ran into someone. A new face indeed, perhaps even his first show in GCW at all, house show and all. Knight smiled slightly. 'My apologies amigo.' The fresh faced newcomer took a step back. He smiled for a moment, his lip twisting into something of a smirk. 'I'm not really your buddy, but hell it's no problem.' The newcomer takes a minute to look Lestatt up and down, before speaking again. 'Listen, uh, Pedro, mind showing me where I can grab a beer before my match?' Knight chuckled and shook his head slightly. 'Yeah you're new ... we've all got something to prove with attitudes like that in the beginning. I've actually just finished my match and was going to get the hell out of here after a quick shower. Not sure how you feel about company with that cerveza though.' 'Cerveza? Isn't that goat piss? I was talking about Beer, you know, a good stout? Guinness, maybe a Porter's Oatmeal. You want to join me for a pint though, hell why not? I don't have anything better to do.' LeStatt Knight smirks again and extend his hand outwards. 'The name is Roberto, or as the people know me as, LeStatt Knight.' The youngish man raises an eyebrow at the extended hand. He lets it stay out there for a minute, before shrugging and shaking it. 'Stevens, Mike, Stevens. And that's what you can call me' The two drop their hand shake 'Now, about that pint...' The two walked on, Knight going to his locker room and got his shower, quickly dressed himself and met Stevens outside in the parking lot. Stevens was still fresh so he did not have a car of his own on the road with him. LeStatt slides his trench coat over his shoulders, as the air had a slight chill in it coming off the beach nearby. The two approach Knight's car which was a black Lamborghini Diablo. 'Ever been to Miami before?' Knight asks Stevens lights up a cigarette, and stops to take a look at the car. 'Nice, I've a red one. As for being to Miami, nah, I tend to stay as far away from the herion trade as possible.' 'Hmm well Miami isn't too bad jefe'. I have recently stepped into a management position at one of the local clubs, I am in the middle of making some drastic changes though. Are you looking for a night club tonight or just a small bar type?' 'Meh, any place I can get a good stout is fine, although I don't think my ears can handle the overproduced crap they call club music.' Knight nodded and hit the gas getting them to their destination. It was a quiet place but still had its share of beautiful women, mostly coming from the waitresses. The two men make a bee line until they are seated by a very attractive and curvaceous Hispanic woman. 'Gracias hermosa, I'll take a Corona with limon.' Knight turns to Stevens. 'What would you like?' 'Ummmm, got any Guinness Hermy?' The woman nods and goes away for their orders. It only takes a few moment for her to return with the drinks and places them on the table. Knight says a few words in spanish to her and gives her a nice tip. After a long refreshing drink, LeStatt sits back in his chair. 'So Michael, how long have you been doing this wrestling business?' Stevens lights up another cigarette and leans back in the chair, putting one foot on the up on another chair . 'Wrestled amateur in high school, and one year in college. Professionally, about three years. Met Steve when we were both working in another company. You?' 'I just fell into it about three years ago actually. I have practiced Martial Arts since I was a child though. My twin brother got me into this ...' Knight's voice kind of trails off as does his stare. He swallows hard and takes another drink of his Corona. 'It helps release alot of frustration, thats for damn sure.' 'You know what I really love? Watching people squirm when I lock in the hold. That, and the mind games, I love watching people twist in the wind trying to figure out what the hell I'm going to say or do next. Makes me feel like I know what it is to be god.' Stevens chuckles leaning back again, and finishing off his beer in another gulp, he motions for the waitress to come back over, but apparently she either doesn't see him or just ignores him. 'Bitch. What about you, besides the stress release what's your favorite part?' An eyebrow lifts on Knight's face, and it almost seems as if those icy blue eyes of his light up. Slowly a thin smile creeps onto his face. He licks his lips in preparation. 'Their pain.' Knight chuckles. 'Their screams are like music to my ears, and their blood is like a work of art. You said it yourself that there is something God-like to it, something intoxicating. I can't even say I LOVE the wrestling aspect of it all. I don't need the money either. To know that another man fears what you can do to him ... is perhaps the single greatest feeling one can have. Well next to fucking a beautiful woman.' 'Bah, sex is overrated, all women care about is money, it's easy enough to get them in the sack if you flash enough cash. But wrestling there's something beautiful about that, the sweat, the blood, the glory. Everything about it makes my blood boil. " 'Well...' Knight chuckles 'I won't stop until I have it all anyways. The women, the fame, the blood, the fear, and most importantly, the respect. I don't care if they like me or not, but they WILL respect me. I figure if you aren't out to get all of that, then you have no business getting involved in this world.' Snuffing out the cigarette in the ashtray, Stevens again tries to wave down the waitress, yet still she ignores him. 'Meh, respect is overrated methinks. Who cares if people respect you? So long as you know you could break them without a second thought, of course. I mean look at me, next month I'll be one of the biggest stars in this company. I don't care if anyone respects that or not, I know I deserve it.' 'Confidence is good Michael ...' Knight leans forward in his chair, folding his arms on the table. 'Arrogance can kill a man. Now I cannot say what you are about in that ring because I have never seen you get down inside of one, so I'll give you the benefit of the doubt about what you can accomplish. All the same things that you are claiming have been claimed by many before, including me. If you want to be big, then find your way into Steven's hip pocket, its the only way to get big, quick.' 'Haha, don't you realize I'm already there? Steve and I are tight.' Stevens continues to chuckle for a minute, when the song of a cell phone fills the bar. Reaching into his pocket he takes a look and gets a serious look on his face. 'Sorry man, it's Gaius, I should really take this. Cool chatting with you, but I'll just catch a cab. Later 'amigo'.' 'I understand ... just remember one thing Stevens. Caldera is like a parasite. From what I've seen he has done nothing but use the people he wants to gain more fame and power, when he is done with them ... he leaves them to die. Don't let it be you.' 'Hehe, whatever dude, Steve's my buddy, he'll always be at my side' Stevens walked away, leaving LeStatt Knight sitting there alone with his thoughts. The two already were so stubborn and so arrogant. Every story has a beginning ... Neither man could understand where their fates would bring them. One thing is for certain though. They both have earned respect, wanted or not.
