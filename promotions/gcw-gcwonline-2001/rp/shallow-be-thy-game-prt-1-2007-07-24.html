Shallow Be Thy Game [Prt. 1]

24 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jul. 24th, 2007 The scene before you is a grim one; the world around you is dark, the wind howls through the trees, you stand in the darkness, surrounded by a forest of darkness. Before you is a scene that could draw any man to his knees; the bodies of grown men draped atop each other, bleeding from their heads, resting on the forest floor. You begin to breath heavily, your eyes grow wide; you see a man hanging from a tree branch, a rope around his neck, runs adjacent to the tree branch. The scene before you appears to be like the climax of a horror movie? “This,” you hear a voice call out from the darkness “is what happens when people do not learn their place!” You spin around quickly, hearing the voice come from behind you. “No, try again” the voice calls out laughing at you, this time the voice sounds as though it’s coming from directly above your head. The wind grows louder, and rustles through the dead leaves atop the trees, trembling the limb body that hangs from the tree, an echoing, yet hollow laugh flows through the darkness. “The simple men before you,” the voice calls out to you again “has cursed the world, the suffering of all of humanity is now on their hands!” Lightening cracks through the sky as you hear laughing again before a rushing noise is heard, it grows louder; it’s right beyond the trees! Continuous “popping” noises can be heard as a cloud of dust rises through the trees and begins to swirl around you. Your hair begins to whip around your head, you grow dizzy as your eyes begin to grow wider and wider, the rushing noise is almost deafening, and still growing louder. You begin to scream, and realize that you cannot even hear your own screams over the noise, and suddenly’the world around you begins to spin! The laughing, your screaming, the rushing noise’it’s all become one large humming noise as the Earth begins to rotate, spinning quickly, you feel as though any second you could lose control? “Welcome” you hear an unfamiliar voice call out from what seems like thousands of miles away. Your eyes are still clinched shut, tightly, so tight that tears begin to roll down your eyes, out of fear, shock? You’re arrived’nowhere. You look around and realize; you’re somewhere, but it’s nowhere. There is nothing, just whiteness, a four sided room; painted the same, monotone color; white. “Please sit,” the same voice says as a noise is heard, like a car engine revving, and behind you slides a chair, that appears to be hovering off the ground! You sit, and the chair begins to move around as a bright light begins to shine in front of you. “My name” the same voice, this time closer says, “is Chris Cross, I am here to gain restitution for the wrongs that this world has done to me!” The chair you are sitting in begins to spin quickly, your legs fly above you as you slide to a second room, up into the air and’through the walls?! The walls begin to deteriorate in front of you as the chair quickly comes to a holt; your head snaps one way, your body the other as you “uff!” You get angry “WHAT IS GOING ON HERE!”!” you demand of the voice that seems to have disappeared. ??In due time? the voice calls out after a moment’s silence. You grow angrier, not enjoying yourself. “NO! YOU WILL EXPLAIN YOURSELF NOW!” you scream to the voice? “SIIIILENCE!” the voice calls out, booming through the room as an echoing clap of thunder is heard. “I AM IN CONTROL! YOU WILL NO DEMAND OF ME! YOU ARE A RESOURCE THAT IS RECYCLABLE, YOU ARE LUCKY TO BE HERE!” the voice screams at you. You sit in the chair quietly as the voice speaks again. “Now, again, allow me to introduce myself; I am Chris Cross; I am here to gain payment for wrongs that were done to me!” You raise an eyebrow “Look man, I don’t know any Chris Cross, and I certainly didn’t wrong you, I’m leaving” you say, heading toward the’door? You can’t seem to find a door, the room is level, you are surrounded by solid walls. “YOU WILL GO NOWHERE!” Cross calls out, throwing you back toward the chair. “SIT!” You stand, brush yourself off and mutter “make it quick” you say, sitting down. “I have come to Global Championship Wrestling to gain knowledge and to right wrongs’your are the first wrong I must right, you will pledge alliance to Chris Cross or forever be lost to time!” You laugh; “Who the hell do you think you are?” you ask before you are suddenly throw backwards. Suddenly the wall before you begins to shake and through the center of the wall walks a familiar man, newest GCW superstar; Chris Cross. “I tried to do it the easy, way, now you get what Phil Atkins and the rest of GCW will” Chris says, pulling you up by the collar of the shirt. He throws you again a wall; “GET OUT!” he demands, “walk through the walls” Cross says, turning away from you. “Phil Atkins” Cross says, pulling up the chair you have just deserted, and crossing his right leg over his left knee “listen closely, your life depends on it.” He smiles and spins around, snapping his finger, as his face appears on the wall. “Phil Atkins, I know all about you, everything there is to know, yet I have done no research, I have watched no video tapes, but I know all your moves. I know what you will say, I know how you think; you see Phil, when you get in front of that microphone, and you question the meaning of life, that was alright when we were sixteen in our backyards with a video camera, but now Phil, you’re in a different league; you’re not stepping into that ring with someone who might be impressed if you open an dictionary and incorporate uncommonly used words into your speech, Phil this isn’t just a wrestling match, this is a whole new level, this has more meaning than a win or lose?” The video image of Chris Cross fades off the wall as the real Chris Cross begins to speak; “Please understand Atkins, this week; both you and I debut, both of us will walk into that arena, but one is getting carried out in a body bag. This week is about forging a legacy as early as possible, and I will climb over anyone to forge my own legacy. You stand between myself and a new world that is Global World Championship wrestling, are you really prepared for what will happen?” Chris Cross slowly stands and looks around, before turning back toward the camera; “I’m unsure if you’re a religious man Phil, but I would begin praying right now; because you’ll need all the help you can muster!”
