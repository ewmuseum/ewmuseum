The Surgoth Story - Chapter 6 - Attack of the... Wait That's Copyrighted! (Ring B)

5 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/05/11 Here I was, what I had been waiting for for so long. I sat in a restaurant known as Leo’s, a place that had the best steak in Diamond City, with the girl I had been in love with since I first laid eyes on her. Lily sat across the table from me with a big smile on her face. She was obviously happy that we had finally decided to become a couple. There was one problem with our first date. In light of the recent attacks on me by some unknown persons, I had to have an escort. Lily said she didn’t mind, that in was to protect me and she didn’t want anything to happen to me, but I could tell she wanted it to be just her and I. “What’s wrong?” Lily asked me. “I’m glad that I’m here with you, but that guy over there, he’s ruining the whole date,” I answered her. “No, he’s not ruining it. Let’s just enjoy ourselves.” “These attacks on me, you said you were going to investigate. Did you find anything?” “Well that’s one of the things I wanted to talk to you about while we were here. I wanted to wait til after we were done eating, but since the subject has come up, might as well tell you now. That blood sample that you collected, the Chief threw it in his trash. I grabbed it out and talked to you lab friend.” “I don’t mean to interrupt you,” I began. “But one, why would the Chief throw it in his trash can, that’s not exactly smart on his part? Number two, how do you know who I talked to?” “You have friends that want answers as well and they confronted me to make sure you were alright.” “So what did you find?” “The DNA tests came back,” began Lily. “It’s coming back that it’s you.” “WHAT!?” I yelled as I stood up. The whole restaurant went quiet and I could feel their eyes on me. I sat back down. “You’re kidding me right?” “No, it’s not a joke. I also found out about the second attacker. His DNA also matches yours.” “So what, I’m a triplet?” I asked. “I don’t know. Another thing that didn’t add up about the whole thing was, before the coroner could look over the bodies, someone must of gotten into the morgue and removed the finger prints, toe prints and head,” finished Lily. “There is something fishy going on and someone doesn’t want us to know about it. It has to be someone on the inside. That’s the only explanation.” “If there is someone on the inside that’s trying to get to you then your not safe even with an escort. I think you need to lay low for a while.” “I can’t Lily. I have another job outside of Diamond City,” I said. “The wrestling thing? You can’t be doing that, not right now. It isn’t safe,” said Lily. “But I have to. I’ve been looking into the Day of Awakening like the Chief had asked me and it’s leading me to GCW. There is someone there that knows something and I think this someone might have something to do with the attacks on me. This might be my chance to find some answers, not just for me but for everyone.” “Then I’m going with you.” Just then my cell phone began to ring. On the other end of the phone was Chief Gilthor. “What is it Chief? “There is a disturbance in an apartment building a block away from Leo’s. It’s on the corner of Emerald and Sapphire. Trust me you’ll know it when you see it. It’s a light blue building. I need you to go check it out,” said the Chief on the other end of the phone. “Why not have one of the police units near by check it out? I’m kind of busy at the moment.” “You’re the closest one there and you have backup already. I really need you to go check this out.” “Alright, I’m on it Chief,” I answered him as I hung up my cell phone. “Check please. I’m really sorry Lily but the Chief needs me. There’s something going on at the apartment building a block away from here. The Chief needs me to check it out.” The waiter comes over with my check. I give him my bank card and he walks away. “It’s alright, you have a job to do. I knew before we started dating that there would be times like this. Just be careful okay?” “Don’t worry I’ll be fine. If those other two attackers couldn’t take me out, then no one else is going to get that chance.” The waiter comes back with my bank card, I give him a five dollar bill and then I walk over to Lily and give her a kiss. “Thanks for your help,” I said as I walked away. I walked up to my so called escort and said, “We got to go. We have a disturbance on the corner of Emerald and Sapphire.” * * * I had arrived at the apartment building on the corner of Emerald and Sapphire and a crowed of people were standing outside. “Out of the way people, DCPD coming through,” I said as my escort and I made our way through the crowed to the front door of the apartment building. The main lights in the hallway were out but emergency lights eerily lit the hallways of the building. I pulled my police issue baretta from its holster, put my back to the wall, and held the gun up as I slowly made my way through the hallway. My escort followed. “This is the DCPD. If there is anyone in there open up,” I yelled as I made it to the first door. There was no response. I checked the door knob and the door was unlocked. I opened the door while still beside it. I pushed it open quickly, spun to the open archway and put my gun in front of me. I checked the apartment and found no one inside. “There is no one in here,” I said to my escort. “I need you to search the second floor.” “Yes sir,” said my escort as he left me. I left the apartment and put myself against the wall on the opposite side of the hallway. I approached the door directly across from the door on the right side. “This is the DCPD. If there is anyone inside, open up,” I yelled with no response yet again. I checked the door, this one was locked. I stood in front of the door and with one, well placed kick, I kicked the door in. I searched inside this apartment as well, and it was empty just like the other one. I pulled out my cell phone and dialed the Chief. “What is it Van?” said the Chief on the other end of the phone after he answered. “Do you have any more information on that apartment building? Like what floor the disturbance was on? Maybe what apartment number? It would make this a lot easier,” I said to the Chief with a bit of annoyance in my voice. “We had more than one person in the apartment building call and no one knew where the sound was coming from or what caused it. You’re just going to have to keep looking Van.” “You’ve got to be kidding me! Alright Chief, I’ll get back to you if I find anything,” I said as I hung up my cell phone. I left the apartment back into the hallway. Down the hallway I seen a figure wearing a ski mask. “Another one of you?” I asked the figure. In the figures hand, he had a katana blade. He came rushing at me and I placed a bullet right in his skull. The figure dropped to the floor, dead. I slowly walked up to the figure, watching every door as I passed it waiting for them to open up to more attackers. Once up to the figure, I removed the mask. I couldn’t believe my eyes! Everything began to make sence now! Well sort of anyway. The reason the heads, figure and toe prints had gone missing from these bodies. Someone didn’t want this discovered, especially me. Underneath the mask was a man who looked identical to me. I removed his right glove and there was no trident scar. I pulled my cell phone out again. I was furious as I dialed the Chief. “Did you find something Van?” asked the Chief. “My attackers, you’re not going to believe this! I found out why the DNA matches mine!” “Wait,” began the Chief. “How do you know about the DNA tests?” “Don’t worry about it,” I answered the Chief. “My attackers, they are me!” I heard something behind me while I was on the phone with the Chief. I turned around to see five more men in ski masks. I dropped the cell phone and pointed the gun in their direction. I couldn’t pull the trigger! I was frozen! I couldn’t do anything at all! Panic began to set in! What was I going to do? Then, it happened! My body began to tingle all over! My body got hot! Then pain, all over my body from head to toe! It was unbearable! My scar began to glow with a bright white light! I screamed out in agony as my whole body shot an explosion of white light all threw the room sending all five of my attackers to the ground! My body had returned to normal. No light, no heat and no pain. Then, blackness! * * * I had woke up in the hospital yet again. It was becoming an all to familiar sight. Beside me was Lily, just as she had been before. She smiled at me and I smiled back. “Glad you’re okay,” she said. “Your escort wasn’t so lucky.” “You’re not going to believe what happened in there,” I replied. “Why don’t you just rest. You were in pretty bad shape when they found you.” “I was in pretty bad shape before they found me! Five guys were in that room with me! One out in the hallway I killed before the other five showed up! I couldn’t shoot my gun! I was frozen! There was pain all threw my body and I was hot all over! Then a bright light shot from my body! I don’t remember anything after that but it was crazy!” “Calm down Van,” said Lily as she placed her hand on my forehead. In walked the Chief and he said, “Glad to see you are alright Van. What happened back there? The apartment you were in was completely in shambles.” “Well I was attacked by six guys in total...” I didn’t get to say much more before the Chief interrupted me. “You were attacked? Van, no one else was in that building other than you and your partner.” “Chief I know what I saw. I was attacked and the one attacker I did get to see looked just like me. Chief I need to find some answers.” “You need to rest, you’ve been out for three days,” said the Chief. “Three days!? Shit, I need to go or I’m going to miss my flight!” “What flight?” said both the Chief and Lily. “I got a lead on the Day of Awakening and it might be tied to these attacks,” I said as I got out of bed, got my clothes out of the hospital room closet, and began to put them on. “You can’t go yourself Van,” said the Chief. “I’ll go with him,” said Lily. “It might not be safe,” I replied. “I think you need to stay here.” “No,” said Lily. “I’m going with you.” “Who’s going to protect you?” I asked. “Listen, these attacks on your life only seem to happen when you’re alone. As long as I’m with you I think you’ll be safe.” “Shes right Van, I think you should take her along. Where are you going anyway?” asked the Chief. “The United States,” I replied. “WHY THE HELL ARE YOU GOING THERE!” yelled the Chief. “If you want answers Chief then you won’t stop me from going. I’m telling you, the answers to the Day of Awakening are leading me there. Would you stop me from finding out the answers to the questions everyone in this city has?” “No,” answered the Chief. “But be careful. It’s bad enough that the U.S. don’t trust us as it is. They see one of us over there and there could be trouble.” “Can’t be that much trouble then. I’m posing as a wrestler in a place known as GCW. They hired me so I don’t think there are going to be any issues.” “Just be careful either way. You’re my best man on the force. We’d suffer without you. Also, make sure you protect her, I don’t think her grandmother would be to thrilled that I let her go with you.” “We’ll be fine Chief.” “Then good luck to you Van,” said the Chief as he shook my hand. Lily and I left the hospital and headed back to our apartments to get ready for our trip to the U.S. for my first real night at GCW!
