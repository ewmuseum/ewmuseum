Nothing Worth Fighting For?

18 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-18-06 A guitar rift breaks through the darkness, accompanied by a bass that is barely there at all. And then the song explodes. ?Your Time Has Come? by Audioslave Jayce Bradley is wearing sweat pants, pushed up to the knee, and a black mesh basketball jersey. His hair drips sweat down his cheeks and forehead. The bright blonde tuft on his head looks like it has been pushed out of his eyes constantly for the whole workout, which also seems to be an ongoing thing. He sits up, and falls back, and sits up, and falls back, grunting as he exerts himself. His hands are locked together behind his head, and he grimaces as he reaches the apex of the crunch and exhales. Sit up, fall down. Sit up, fall down. Life is full of shit we just have to keep doing. Nothing stays as it should be. ## Now one fell asleep in the street and he never woke up And now one died in pieces in his bed with a mouth full of bones And one threatened me long ago, I saw him melt in the bright light of day And one laid to rest in a field under stories and clones ## Bradley reaches the top one last time, this is number two hundred in a very small amount of time. His abs feel like they are on fire, and that fire makes him feel like he is going to vomit. He lays on his back breathing heavily, sweat pouring off of him like he just came up for air after extended sea diving. An older man enters the room, noticing that Bradley has finally exhausted himself. He helps Bradley sit up and puts a towel over the man's shoulders. ?=?=?=?=?=? ## I've been wanderin sideways I've stared straight into the sun Still I don't know why you're dying Long before your time has come Your time has come ## ?You're working hard this close to a fight.? Judging from the look on Jayce's face, The old man might have just overstepped his bounds. The old man seems to know this, but keeps his gaze on Bradley. Bradley is seated now, his legs propped up on a couch. He is in a robe, and slumped back enjoying the calming buzz of his muscles. He has a mug in his hand, probably coffee, and when he looks at the old man it is with a patronized boredom. He stands at the foot of the couch, his hands clutched in front of his waist. He looks like a servant at a five star restaurant waiting for your glass to become half empty. Or half full, whatever. When there is no response, the old man presses on. ?Surely Ambrosi doesn't have you this worked up.? The statement is just that. A statement. There is no question to it, there is no doubt. Bradley just shuts his eyes lazily. ?Virgil Ambrosi just won his title, so the last thing anyone is going to expect is for me to waltz into the ring and take it from him.? The mug sort of dangles there in his hand, its contents teasing the edge as he speaks. ?Least of all me, considering it has been ages since I've actually had to fight in a match like this.? ## Now one took some bullets in the chest in a deal gone wrong And one got a little too depressed and he went and jumped the gun One got shot right in the face and he somehow survived But he doesn't know my name or who I am and I'm not surprised ## Bradley sips his coffee, thne sets it to the side where the old man immediately snatches it up. Bradley notices this, opening his eyes long enough to watch the man for a moment. ?The media thinks he is going to be a man on a mission, determined to walk out of WorldWide with his title firmly in his grasp.? A smile tickles the corners of his lips. ?I disagree.? The old man stares at him, his attention fully focused on the former Television Champion. ?Virgil Ambrosi has tasted a bit of success here in GCW, but he is more of a lap dog to Dawn and Shadow than anyone else in Dies Irae.? He extends his legs off the edge of the couch, leaning back against the arm as he fumbles around for the remote control. ?He knows where he stands in the pecking order, taking the scraps from the table in order to keep from biting the hand that feeds him. A very intimidating habit to be sure.? ## I've been wanderin sideways I've stared straight into the sun Still I don't know why you're dying Long before your time has come ## ?This type of match suits his type well though, Jayce,? The man interrupts, trying desperately to pull Jayce back down from the clouds. ?As good as you claim to be, these types of matches are unpredictable, and Ambrosi is as unpredictable as they come.? ?Yeah,? Jayce snaps back, ?And what makes you such an expert?? ?The fact that I can see this without my ego blinding me from the truth.? Bradley shifts, and the old man puts the mug back on the coffee table. ?You're walking into this match like you can't lose,? the old man shakes his head as he speaks, knowing that what is coming next is not going to sit well with the former Cruiserweight Champion. ?When it's all you've been doing lately.? Bradley laughs. ?In case you haven't noticed,? Jayce corrects him as he leans forward and interlocks his fingers. ?I haven't lost a match in a few weeks.? ?What about Warpath, Jayce?? ## I've been wanderin sideways I've stared straight into the sun Still I don't know why you're dying Long before your time has come ## Jayce's eyes turn red with anger as he stands, ?Get the hell out of here!? The old man straightens, as if he was slapped across the face, then turns and leaves immediately. Bradley's breathing increases as he walks to the mantle and pulls a small white card off of it. He flips it around in his fingers and paces. Welcome into his head. Tessa Windsor gave him this card at Warpath, in case he needed to get in touch with her. With Eva Van Lorne gone, he and Tessa are basically the only members of The Ascension left on the roster. Right now though, she is just another face in the crowd, another brick in the wall. The Ascension is dead, so there is absolutely no reason to still have this card. ## I've seen 50,000 names all engraved on a stone Most of them men under the grave years before I was born All of them left brothers and sisters and mothers behind And most of their family and friends have all had their time ## He just couldn't convince himself to throw it away, not when he knew that The Ascension being dead wasn't going to stop Dies Irae from doing as they please. At other's expense. ## I've been wanderin sideways I've stared straight into the sun And I don't know why you're dying Long before your time has come ## She wants his help, when there are so many others she could turn to. People that would surely be more dependable. ## I've been wanderin sideways I've stared straight into the sun And I don't know why you're dying ## Why? Bradley exhales and walks towards the phone. He picks up the reciever and starts to dial the number on the card. 'Fuck...' ## Long before your time has come Long before your time has come Long before your time has come And your time has come ##
