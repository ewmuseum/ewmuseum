Diary, pt. 1

11 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11Apr2007 So, I’ve been having some bad nights lately. Yeah, yeah, I know. Everybody has bad nights, right? But, I think I’m entitled to think that mine are just a little bit worse. You see, I’ve been having some bad dreams. And I’m not talking “I’m falling down and I wake up right before I hit the ground” type of bad dream. I’m dreaming about blood, and bodies, and people being hurt. I sit here and look at the hotel room I’m staying in this week. It seems I’ll never stop staying in these. Everybody else gets to go home to something, or somebody, in between shows. Not me, I just move on to the next city and wait for my next match. Usually I’m not one for just sitting around the room. Sure it happens, but lately it’s been happening more frequently. Ever since I met with Anthony, I’ve been looking at things a little bit differently. Now, from the way that I project myself, it would seem that I’m a really bad person. Honestly, yes I am. Does that mean that I don’t have any personal feeling or emotions? Of course not. Some of the things I do, they tend to eat at me. The trick is knowing that what you did was the right thing to do. The end will justify the means. You catching on to this yet? See, no matter who you are, or what you’ve done, if you try hard enough and make the right approach you can convince anyone of anything. Even yourself. A direct result of this is that I spend a lot of time these days sitting alone, talking to myself. Not that damned Voice that I’ve been dealing with for the past few years. This is just me and me. And in the end, I seem to lose no matter what. I feel like every day I’m giving up a part of myself. Are you getting confused yet? Don’t worry, I’m just as lost as you are. How are we going to make sense of this? I guess backtracking would help. See, Anthony was a demon from the past that I had to confront. I’ve always considered myself a loyal person. Loyalty above all this. Just look at me and Chris. But Anthony had to betray my trust a long time ago. I believe in karma. I know that someday I’ll get what I deserve, but Anthony got his long ago. Chris broke his neck. It wasn’t intentional, at least that’s what me and Chris will always tell you. It’s really of no consequence whether or not any of you believe us or not. A few weeks ago, I dealt out what was justice. Anthony stabbed me in the back years ago, and I hurt him for it. I hurt him real bad. Say what you want about me attacking a cripple, he deserved it. I think, that maybe some of the karma I was talking about is starting to come back to me, though. These dreams didn’t start until after that day. Now I feel like I tapped into some part of myself much darker than I knew existed, and it feels like it’s festering. I can feel it growing inside of me. The dreams only prove it. Now, you may ask yourself just how much worse could I get. What could be worse than drugging women in a locker room? I mean, some of you consider that to be the most vile act that’s happened in GCW in a long, long time. Well, to be honest with you, I really don’t think that was all that bad. Sure, I drugged her. It’s not like Katie O’Cunt got raped that night, though, now did she? Humiliated, degraded, embarrassed? Sure, obviously. But any grown human being learns to get over that sort of thing. That is the type of thing that a high school child would cling to and cry about. If Katie were Emo enough, she’d probably use it to put more spotlight on herself like some filthy attention-whore. No, that’s not the type of thing that’s haunting my dreams. My dreams scare even me, and I’ve been through a lot of bad situations. I faced Genesis in WIW, a man who has crawled out of a grave and murdered men with his bare hands. I’ve wrestled in DWO in some of the most disgusting matches imagined. But these dreams go far beyond a wrestling ring. These dreams involved trails and pools of blood. Dismembered bodies. And a lot of familiar faces. Faces I’d sooner forget than remember. My father seems to appear rather often. Only, this time he’s not the one dishing out the beatings. In my dreams he’s not even breathing. He’s not even whole. All I ever see is his face. The sickest part? Every time I see him, it’s because his head is hanging from my belt like some kind of trophy. But that’s only the start of things. Old friends, people like Shane Slade, Jole Tempest; two of the most honest people I’ve ever known. It’s too bad that when I dream of them, they don’t have tongues. These images have to hold some sort of meaning. And I don’t fully believe they are subconscious thoughts. I think something bad is happening. Not yet, but soon it will. I don’t have the slightest clue as to what will take place, I only know I have to be prepared for it. I have to prepare myself. How do you prepare for the unknown? I don’t think I can’t do it. I feel completely vulnerable. It doesn’t help that I keep shoving this Voice down that threatens to overwhelm me every time I let my guard down. That damned Voice. I almost want to blame Him for this, but I’m sure that he isn’t a part of this. I’ve learned how to put my walls up, to put up the mental blocks to keep Him out. Those walls don’t do anything to keep this new threat out, though. Perhaps this is something of the occult nature? I doubt it. I don’t think I have any enemies who would do that to me, nor do I think any would be capable of putting some silly “voodoo” curse on me. I guess part of what really frightens me about all of this, is that I don’t know where this is coming from. I don’t know who would bring this upon me. And I certainly don’t know why this is happening to me. All I know is that I have to prepare myself for a war. A war that I don’t know how to fight, a war that I don’t know who to fight. The clock is ticking, and I have to prepare myself soon, before this darkness overwhelms me. I don’t know when these dreams will begin slipping into my reality. I have this odd feeling that I’m going to lose. My dreams even point to that conclusion. See, every time it ends the same. I’m standing over a body, knife in my hand. I know it’s the same knife that severed my father’s head. The same knife that silenced my friends. Their blood is still wet on the blade. And that body looks at me with the same helpless eyes that I see everyday when I stare at myself in the mirror. I’m standing over myself, and I realize that I’m not myself anymore. I’m something new, and much more sinister. There is a war coming, and I have to prepare myself for the fight. I’m keeping this diary, and hopefully it will help me retain some semblance of myself when everything comes crashing down. April 11th, 2007 Markus Stone
