You Lost That Hunger Feeling...

20 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-20-07 ?Hey kid, how's it going?? Jayce didn't even bother to turn around, he immediately recognized the voice of Mario Trellis. He just kept at his repetitions, lifting the weight up to his chest then lowering it back to his waist. ?Still upset, huh?? ?At myself more than anything else,? he said as he lowered the weights to the ground, a thud rang out as they hit the concrete. ?I should have been more prepared. The US title should be around my waist right now, instead Northsky and Dies Irea have it.? ?You're being too hard on yourself,? Mario tried to comfort him, almost like a Father would if his son lost a Pop Warner football game. ?You can't win all the time, and sometimes things happen that are out of your control.? Jayce just shook his head as he walked over to the ring, where a towel and bottle of water await him. ?And who's to say you won't get another crack at it.? As true as that statement was, Jayce had already been handed the opportunity to become the United States champion on two occasions, and he failed on both attempts. Maybe he was reaching too far too fast, considering the speed bumps that had shown up and slowed his progress up the ranks of GCW. Still, it was hard to change the fact that he had come so close twice, only to fall just a tad bit short. ?I don't even know if I want another shot, to be honest.? Trellis could hardly believe the words that lingered in his ears. ?Are you out of your mind,? he asked, his voice hitting a high pitch Jayce had never heard before. ?You've worked so hard for you to just give up now.? ?I'm not giving up,? Jayce laughed, as he dabbed the towel along the nape of his neck. ?I think I have to focus on something else for awhile. You know, to get my momentum going again.? ?Honestly,? Mario answered. ?I think you just need to go out there and be yourself. Stop worrying about what you think people want to see, or who you think they want you to be. You had a good thing going from what I can tell, and there was no reason to change that.? A nod was all the response Mario received, as Jayce brought the bottle of water to his lips. ?I know you're thirsty, but come on. You don't have that fire you had when you first got to GCW, anymore. It's as if you're happy being mediocre when you have so much more to show the World.? Jayce almost spit out a bit of the water, laughing uncontrollably at Mario's comment. ?You think I'm joking with you, kid,? Mario questioned, while his arms folded across his chest and his eyes narrowed in on the former Television champion. ?I've seen the footage of you when you first showed up to GCW, when you demanded the attention of the fans and the guys in the back at the same time.? Jayce could see the fire in Mario's eyes as he spoke, and it seemed to be soaking in. ?You knew what you wanted back then, Jayce. You knew it, and you took it. When Northsky was given a spot in Rampage you took it from her, same as you did with the Cruiserweight title when you took it from Varsity, and the Television title from Jason Warr.? Mario placed his hand on Jayce's shoulder, really wanting him to understand what he was saying. ?You went out there and took what you wanted, and did what you wanted. Now you're just standing there and letting stuff come to you.? ?I'm not the person I was then, and you know it.? Jayce tries to turn away from the man who is helping him change his ways, to live a better life. ?Just because you're not an asshole, doesn't mean you can't go after what you want.? Mario turns away from Jayce, shaking his head as he walks toward his office. ?Maybe that's something you need to think about. Stand up and make yourself known, no matter the risk.? As the door closed, the words seemed to slap Jayce in the face. Mario was right, he was being to laid back, too self conscious about what he could and couldn't do. This week he had Blaise McClancy for the Cruiserweight title. ?No better time to start than now.? The words were no louder than a whisper, but the only person that needed to hear them was the one that said them in the first place. And that's all that matters.
