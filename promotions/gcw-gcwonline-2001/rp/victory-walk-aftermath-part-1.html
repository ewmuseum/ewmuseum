Victory Walk (Aftermath part 1)

Date: Five minutes later Every step felt like it was a mile long. He could hear the crowd scream behind him, still engrossed in the show. Right now Jason, and Roberto would laying their bodies on the line, trying to break each other down completely. The fans soaked in and loved every minute of it, the drama was whipping them into kind of an animal frenzy. Maybe it would be doing the same thing to him if he watched, only problem was the room wouldn’t stop spinning long enough for him to even sit down. He should’ve been feeling great, excited, he’d won Dangerous Games, outlasted, outwitted, and plain beat everyone else. He should be focusing on the Dread/Knight match, scouting them, looking for weakness in both men, he’d have to face one of them next month for the World Title, the biggest prize in the game. Hell, he should be celebrating, drinking a beer with Alex, getting a victory kiss from Sheena. There were a hundred things he should be doing, and it was everything he could do just to stand. Battling Trey he remembered, coming close, just falling a little short. He’d almost blanked out when his head hit the mat, but he could still hear the ref making the three count. Feeling better before the Battle Royal he knew he still wasn’t a hundred percent, took some time, chilled out with the fans, tried to get that girls number. She slapped him. He remembered going after Borealis, taking out those guys on Steven. He remembered a chair welding flying Luke Hanson. Vaguely he wondered who won the Battle Royal. “MIKEY!” Hearing Sheena’s voice caused him to lurch forward another step. His knees stung as they dragged across the concrete, he palms burned as the flesh tore away as he tried to brace himself. Watching the blood drip from his forehead, he threw up. Everything was double, thinking about it made the world tilt in crazy directions, again his stomach heaved, nothing left he coughed on rising bile. “OH MY GOD!” Her arms were cool on his back, a stark contrast to the blistering heat of his own body. He was boiling in his own skin, he continued to choke. Her fingers in his hair, the sweet perfume in his nose she was like water to a man dying of thirst, but just out of reach. She promised salvation, but he couldn’t get it just yet. “Mike”.god.” As the shadow of the giant fell over them, Stevens felt Alex’s reassuring presence. His body thankfully blocked out the too harsh, too bright florescent lights, they were making him even sicker. He tried to focus on the blood pooling on the floor, it wasn’t pretty, but a better sight than his own reeking puke. Dropping his head to the ground, pillowed on concrete, eh closed his eyes, sweet, liberating darkness. So easy, so close, the promise of release. “No, no, no, no, NO! Alex we can’t let him go to sleep.” “I know we’ve got to get him up, help me.” The welcoming frost of the void surrounded him, everywhere was blackness, pain fading away. Silence, peace, at long last. Peace shattered. Violently hauled up to his feet, he was forced to open his eyes confront the ugly truth. She pried his eyes open, gently but urgently. Alex and Sheena almost carried him, one of his arms slung over each of their shoulders. Her cheek pressed against his, she whispered, no words, just soothing sounds. The sound of a mother with a sick child. The sound of a lover soothing a bad dream. “Come on Mike buddy. You’ve got to move man, one foot in front of the other, you can do it buddy.” The world was starting to settle, and he moved, a shuffling step on jelly legs. Quivering he nearly fell again, strong arms pulled him back up. Head lolling forward he desperately squeezed his eyes shut, away from the light, away from this angry little world of harsh light, and too loud noise. Behind him the crowd still roared. Sacrifices made to fuel the bloodlust of the masses. Dread and Knight were on their alter now, he’d had his time, been their lamb. And strangely they’d cheered, they’d made sounds for him he’d never heard before. It was incredible, filled him with a kind of energy he’d never felt before, he finally got it. Finally understand why those cheers mean so much, they pushed him to do things he never thought he could do, probably shouldn’t have done. And he was left like this. Just another broken man, too hurt to even walk. Still the noise started to bring him back, forced him to open his eyes again. Another foot step, and his stomach revolted once again. “Stop, STOP!” His voice was hoarse and raw, but they understood. Choking and gagging he was brought to a halt. It was then something strange began welling up inside him. A peculiar feeling that he hadn’t felt in a very long time. In another time, in another place, he might’ve been able to figure out what it was, but right now all he knew was it meant he had to walk on his own, he couldn’t be dragged out of the arena. Still hunched over, he attempted to straighten. As the world resumed its proper alignment, he pushed the two away. At first they wouldn’t budge, but he was insistent, he might have actually believe he pushed them way himself, he couldn’t have realized he was weak as a new born kitten. Pushing his hands through his hair, he closed his eyes, but only for a moment, a deep intake of breath. Step? “CATCH HIM!” Powerful arms wrapped around his waist before his face could smash into the floor and probably knock out a few teeth. The sudden stop pushed all the air out of him, and gasping he spoke only one word. “Hospital.” ~To Be Continued~
