No time like the present to understand.

5 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/08 22 years ago. In the distance a rumbling darkness can be seen with flashes of light. Standing in the middle of the road is a child staring forward as dust swirls across the road and at the child's feet. He watches an ambulance leave as he watches. His eyes dark and focused as they are tear stained. A loss now only hurts him more in the long run. Gripping his pant leg tighter he watches as the Ambulance becomes a mere speck on the horizon. Watching family die and disappear is a tough road for any young man. He stares down the road as suddenly three Harley Davidson's rolls thundering by as the rain slowly begins to fall as he looks at his hands and turns walking towards a building as in faded and cracked paint it reads Boxing Gym. He enters slowly looking at the man behind the desk as the man steps forward. "Looks like your needing some direction." The boy looks up and nods. "Yessir." The man pats the boy on the back and walks him forward. He then grabs a roll of tape and motions for the boy to hold his hands out. "Time to let out some of that anger and frustration of watching your mother die boy." The boy looks at the man as he is getting his hands taped. The man then grabs some gloves and puts them on the boys hands as he watches and smiles as he ties the laces completely. Taking the boy by the shoulders he brings him to the large punching bag. "Let it out boy, because there will be alot more to come." The boy begins to hit harder and harder as tears stream down his face as his eyes begin to form a concentrated look of rage and anger that will fuel him for weeks and weeks to come. 10 years ago... Wild Thing Chris Rogers circling Shaman in the ring as Shaman is staring bullet holes through him. One of the news crew writing for a wrestling magazine speaks. As the other listens as Shaman and Chris Rogers lock up and Shaman slings him across the ring and into the ropes grabbing him and executing a tilt a whirl back breaker. "Shaman shows some potential." The secondary news man shrugs. "You have seen one you have seen em all. He is new and it will wear off. He won't even be a two year hold over." The first reporter chuckles. "He had one helluva match against Steihl at Darkens event. That can't be a fluke." The second reporter shakes his head and looks at the second reporter. "Even a blind squirrel can find a nut every now and then. Besides he is new something different. When he wins a title comeback and ask me." The second reporter writes something as he looks up and watches the match continue. Chris Rogers steps in and gets underneath Shaman as Shaman simply powers out and slings Chris Rogers into the corner and charges in delivering a splash. Suddenly Shaman steps up and grabs Chris Rogers and turns hoisting him up into a power bomb position and throw him up onto his own shoulders. As he holds him Shaman steps up onto the second rope and spins executing a spinning powerbomb called the Thunderclap. Shaman rolls to the side and drops down and pins Chris Rogers as the referee makes the count and the bell rings. Shaman stands looking down at his crushed opponent and then turns and raises his arms up in the air and leaves the ring. The reporter turns and looks at the other reporter he was talking too. "Something tells me we will be talking about him for long times to come." The reporter stands and walks away leaving the other reporter to himself to contemplate the comments made by the reporter who had just left. Four years ago... Fans at a snack stand during GTT4 PTC's yearly premier event. Some fans talking to one another as they are ordering food and getting drinks. Some laughing and joking as someone hurries up and talks. He seems kind of energetic and out of breath at the same time. "Hey guys guess what?" One of the girls turns and looks at the guy. "What is it Jimmy?" The guy takes a deep breath. "Shaman defeated Reginald Atkins." The group look at each other a bit shocked. "You mean the same guy from the WWA?" Jimmy nods as they all look at one another. Another guy speaks up. "You know Jimmy he is the WWA's longest running guy on their roster. There might be something about the talent there. He did come in and say he was going to win at least one match. Against Reginald Atkins at that." Rubbing his head Jimmy looks at everybody. "He drew Shadow for the next round she was a tough competitor in her first round as well." The group hurries and gathers the food and makes their way back into the arena stands. Destiny has a path for all yet it presents itself in numerous ways before achieving the goals that you find at present. 2 years ago... Shaman looks around at the crowd for a moment- but only a moment. Resolutely, he sets Mayhem up in a front facelock, locks his hand in, and with another yell, betraying the absolutely immense strength one would need to lift up a monster like Bryan Mayhem, he holds him upside down, in the beginnings of a beautiful stalling vertical. Bryan: LOOK AT THAT POWER! BAM! The mat shakes with force as the big man goes down, right on the back of his head. Yale: DESERT SPIKE! THIS ONE IS OVER! Shaman goes for the cover, hooking Mayhem's massive leg. 1... 2... 3! Kyo makes a half-stumbling dive at the pin, but he's too late- the referee signals to the bell-keeper. Ding ding ding! Andrews: The winner of this match, and NEW GCW TV CHAMPION... SHAMAN! Shaman's arm is raised in victory, as Kyo's fist pounds on the mat, an angry defeat for the Deathscythe Outlaw, who certainly got on the wrong end of someone's personal vendetta this evening. Bryan: LOOK OUT SHAMAN! From behind, Mayhem clobbers the new champion with an axehandle smash. Shaman stumbles forward, dropping his belt, and spins around quickly to meet his hated foe once again. The two exchange rights, then it outright turns into a grapple fest as Mayhem's hands shoot forward and wrap around Shaman's neck, choking him. Yale: Hey, somebody get some security down here! Bryan: Damn straight, this is turning into chaos! Kushinaga gets out of the way, but Mayhem and Shaman continue pounding on each other- with no signs of stopping even as the security team floods the ramp. With much difficulty, they manage to get the two men separated, though it looks as though one wrong move could have them brawling again. The scene fades on the two men mouthing insults at one another that can't be heard over the roaring of the NC-17 crowd. **Source NC17 GCW.** Present Today...Osaka, Japan A small Udon shop in the Osaka District. Shaman sits at a table by himself looming large in the crowd of people around him eating. None paying him any mind as the Udon shop owner walks out bringing him some Udon with egg and pork. Shaman nods and grabs the chop sticks as he speaks. "Itadakimas!" Shaman eats the Udon as he enjoys it turning his head he notices the camera and frowns as he eats the Udon. "Take your time and relax i am eating and trust me i want to enjoy this food first." Shaman eats the Udon as he looks at the camera and frowns as he finishes it and pushes the bowl away from himself as he reaches forward and grabs a bottle of Saki and drinks a bit of it and then turns slightly and looks at the Camera. His eyes narrowing while he rubs his hands together. "I bet your here wondering what i think about my match with Vivica J Valentine. I may even deduce that your wondering why i have gone this far and haven't looked back." Shaman grins as he leans back a bit settling against a wall near him. "Truth is i fought Valentine when i first came to GCW and pinned her in the ring. In fact she hasn't even pinned me once. Yet the fact is many folks forget these facts and matters and out her ahead of me in the competition. I enjoy being the underdog because when i crush my opponent the shock is even greater." Shaman stands up towering over the Japanese people around him as he grins and exits the Udon shop and stands outside pointing up the narrow alleyway. "That way is the Tokyo Dome where i will crush Valentine win or lose. She will suffer at my hands regardless. The match is her pain and her proof she is a cancer in GCW. I am tired of all talking about her as a savior or messiah. There is one thing she will come to understand is pain at the hands of the master of pain. I make people bleed and that is enough for me." Shaman stands still as he looks to the sky as the clouds begin to darken. He tilts his head and grins as he turns and looks around. "I wonder if my fateful decision all those years ago will be a defining moment here soon. Could i hoist a third federations World Championship title on my shoulder for another time or will i have to literally go about destroying folks to gain the ground needed to get it. Are there any doubts?" Shaman stops his face becomes solemn as he looks around. "There are always doubts. I can trash talk till i am blue in the face, but what happens at the end is what the people remember. Valentine and i have been playing mind games back and forth for months now trying to gain the upper hand with one another. Secret is neither of us have gained on the other. What will truly happen in the end even i cannot answer that deep question. All i know is what i will do in the ring. I have done it for years and all have come to know pain at my hands. I have many many people bleed and myself have bled for many more." Turning Shaman begins to walk as rain drops begin to fall down as he stops again under a Sakura tree looking up at the buds as he begins to close his eyes as he whispers. "What is it that causes someone to change? Why is it that when one changes his destiny has been marred by the choices of his past?" Lowering his head and stepping forward he turns and heads for a park with sakura trees lining the paths on both sides. He walks as the rain comes down upon him as he looks back and forth as if searching for something that seems to be lost. He steps to the side of the path and reaches down and lifts a small broken branch. Holding it he stares at it. "This branch is like myself. Something is broken and yet needs to be found or is it merely a change in scenery. A new future for something thought old and thrown to the refuse." Shaman tosses the stick to the ground and walks on as he shakes a bit and clenches his hands open and closed. "I am excited first time in a longtime. It is as if i have been given extra energy to drive forward for something lost or never found in the first place. I have a chance to stand atop of the world and spit in the eye of the unknown. Do i choose to stand amongst the greats or do i stand amongst those who almost had the prize within their grasp. Valentine isn't looking past me i guarantee she is not that shallow. Yet she has made one valuable error in judgment." Shaman turns looking at the camera as he opens his hand palm up. He narrows his gaze looking at his hand. He then closes his fist. "She thinks i have shown her all of my cards and in truth she gets only the two of spades while i hold the ace and the knowledge of how to defeat her and anyone else who stands in my path. You ever wanted something so much that you can taste it. I am sure each and everyone of them have." Shaman walks as his shirt and jacket are thoroughly drenched as he walks forward his mind wandering a bit through the past. "I almost forgot what the excitement was like to be on the verge of grasping something great. Could i actually be hoisting the GCW World Heavyweight title above my head at the end of my match with Valentine? It surely is possible isn't it." Shaman looks to the sky as the clouds part a bit and sunshine blasts through like a hot knife through butter. "Anything is a possibility in this world. It is time to open the old books. Dust off the skills not used often. Time to use the old noggin and play the game only i know how. It is time to take something and make it mine once and for all. I don't think that is something too hard to achieve." Shaman laughs as he walks away down the path. His feet stepping through the slowly draining water. "Nope it is something only i can achieve for myself for nobody sleep too soundly any longer with the monster looking you in the eyes." Disappearing down the path Shaman is blurred by the rain which suddenly pours down again.
