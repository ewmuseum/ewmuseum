La Ciel Bleu: Nuage Deux

24 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/24/06 Ah, ring rust... Actually, being honest, I don't like the term ring-rust. When something's rusted, what do you think about it? Worn, old, past its prime... beyond use. I don't want to have 'rust' be associated with me at all. I'm sure Jackson Rust had to put up with this line of thinking directed at him by guys doing promos at his expense, but then again he probably just brushed it off. I'm approaching it from a different point. Fiery Passion did well, but I never said that they wouldn't. Really, thinking back about it, I've not had any kind of permanent tag team partner. The most I've tagged with anyone were those three times in PRIME with Shawn Stewart. But the majority of the teams we faced were random pairings like ourselves. We just happened to have better cohesiveness. La Sombra and I lacked preparedness as much as Stewart and I did, but I do believe that there was one thing that greatly made us work: confidence, and familiarity. We hadn't been a team, but we knew what the other could do. I was well aware of Shawn's MMA background, and he of my technical background. We just took our combat instinct to back up the other. That confidence wasn't just in ourselves, but in each other as well. And that confidence gave us an advantage in different ways. I had confidence in that our team was going to be such a powerhouse that our opponents would be intimidated by the sheer amount of damage that we could do in a short amount of time. Shawn Stewart had confidence that with a proficient technical wrestler on his side complimenting his killer instinct, there wasn't anyone we couldn't stretch into a pretzel if we so wanted. The result of matches, then, was not a guarantee... the outcome of a fight paled in comparison to the fight itself. We didn't go out there to win, we went out there to fulfill get our fill of competition. Having no pressure to win (being a replacement team simply there as a place-filler) was great... we actually won more because we weren't stressed about the match ending. Fiery Passion took their victory for granted, but they got the victory so they can do that. There wasn't a great connection between myself and La Sombra... that will handicap a team's chances of winning. I don't understand him fully, but there seems to be a lack of confidence there. He can win, but there's just something there that's not clicking. I didn't have much of a chance to speak with him after the match; he went off and did his own thing. I think he's still around, but damned if i know where he is. Not that I've time to look, my flight leaves in 2 hours and airport security takes a long time to deal with no matter the time of day. So while I'm getting ready to leave, there's an argument going on in the room. I'm not a part of it, just an observer. Not even much of an observer... just happen to be present. People wouldn't quite call me a wallflower, but at the same time I've never been one to greatly involve myself in things unless I had something to say and it directly affected me. For now, anyway, it didn't so I stood back and let the fight run its course. “You’ve got to be kidding me!” Brack’s angered growls blurt through gritted teeth; his gaze fixed on the six foot six GCW World Champion. Before this continues, there's something you have to understand about booking a professional wrestling federation on the level of Global Championship Wrestling. The shows are booked in advance. Even if it they don't fully announce until one Worldwide is over to tell the fans who's going to be booked on the next one, the management does things a week ahead at the least. They've got to; managing everyone's schedules and who's dealing with what court date or if there's an illness... it's all got to be done. Waiting until the last minute to change stuff just doesn't work. So when Brack Conway, manager of 'the Fallen Luchador' La Sombra, makes his big fuss... it's nearly a done deal. The time and setting are Worldwide 51, the locker room backstage for what can basically be counted as Ascension. It's not any time during the show... the televised program has already ended. Instead, half an hour after the show, when Dies Irae has vacated (they're quick about getting their scheisse and getting out of Dodge) the building, Conway is going back and forth with LeStatt Knight over what he saw on the booking role for the next go-around. There were two definite matches in question: Seymour Almasy vs. Ciel... and La Sombra vs. Rufio. In addition, the Jackson Brothers would get an open slot against any team that chose to face them. No big deal, but the problem for Brack is that the team LeStatt has in mind isn't exactly *choosing* this. “I don’t remember stating that this was open for discussion.” A glazed look overtook the champion’s cold blue eyes as he leans back in his chair. He raises an eyebrow to the flailing whimpers of Mr. Conway with an aura of indifference etched on his face as he continues. “As of this moment the Ascension doesn’t have a viable tag team to compete for the GCW Tag Titles” besides Sombra and Ciel that is!” A sardonic snicker escapes his lips as if the mere thought of considering La Sombra and I a viable team amuses him. “So it will be Ciel and your superstar to take on the Jackson brothers to assure we can even the playing field. I want them completely stripped of all titles. We need tag representation to do that, and because Sombra and Ciel teamed tonight, and apparently they're both in, they're it by default.” I'm hearing it, but Knight is talking as though I'm not there. For now, I'm humoring him.. He's not exactly *there* in the head, but then again I've seen enough of his type. LeStatt is an ass, plain and simple; not that I think he'd deny it. He's got an agenda, everyone else be damned. “Fine then, I take it Julio won’t be competing against Rufio?' Brack says, practically spitting out his words rather than asking a question. “Julio will be teaming with Ciel?” a smirk creeps its way to the World Champion’s face as he adds, 'in addition to taking on Rufio!” The Brack guy gets a bit animated, flailing his arms as though he were broken down at the side of the road and trying to flag down the only car for miles on a rainy day. “You’re out of your damn mind it you think I am going to set Julio up like that.' You can tell when someone's trying hard to keep their cool because their voice gets low, their words grated. LeStatt is measuring his comments. The words come out deliberately through open lips and closed teeth. “We are tasked out. Every member of Ascension is doing something to help the cause, except Sombra and him!” Goodie, Knight pointed at me. “Everyone except you!” Brack mutters, just loud enough for LeStatt to hear. But I've got good ears too, so I heard it just fine as well. 'I didn't want him joining up with you anyway since yours is a battle he doesn't need to fight and now you're trying to make him wrestle twice in one night when you haven't even done a damn thing since Warpath!” LeStatt Knight has not taken a booking since taking the world title for his own. He's been dealing with other things, trying to get Ascension going, but for two weeks he's not even entertained the possibility of having matches. That would only get in the way of his larger plans, I suppose. 'You wanna win your war, fight it yourself!' The infuriated manager exclaims, stomping is foot to the ground. Knight is starting to lose his temper. He doesn't strike me as the kind of person who would keep it for very long. It's worth getting impressed about that so far he hasn't snapped at Brack Conway, but really that wasn't going to last forever. LeStatt's mentality can't allow for dissent. He's got a plan in mind, and anyone who's not a part of it is his enemy. Though I'll protect an innocent from being attacked by a wrestler, Brack really doesn't need to push it with Knight. The bigger man will think nothing of choking him into subordination. “Perhaps,' LeStatt fumes, 'I am not making myself clear. We are at a crit... WILL YOU PUT SOME PANTS ON!” He isn't talking to Brack Conway, but to me. I'm noticed after all. And only four minutes roundabouts of the conversation going on like I'm not in the room. So there I was, dripping wet having just stepped out of the shower, and with no towel around me. I'm conservative in behavior, I suppose, but modesty hasn't really been an issue of mine. Hey, it's a locker room; you're gonna see penis at some time, so I don't really care. Besides, most people don't make a fuss about me wearing pants because they're insecure about their masculinity (a large population of male wrestlers feel that way, hence the overcompensation in testesterone-fueled acts of violence). That insecurity means that they don't even wish to admit that their eyes drifted down to my ass/member. So I, standing there naked facing away from the others, run the towel through my hair. 'Decency aside, perhaps you misunderstand that this is a locker room. If you wish not to be exposed to the hygiene side of things that goes on in here, you're free to leave. Otherwise you just have to wait until I dry off.' 'Could you at least hurry that up, Sky?' Oh, right. Tessa's here also. I've got nothing she hasn't seen, although she's also trying not to stare at me. And she knew me as Skylar Green aka Sky before I came back from hiatus with the Ciel callname. We're friends, but probably not that good friends. She's facing away from me at the same, so it's not so bad I suppose. I'll just be a distraction to Knight. I tell her, 'Sure,' a green towel over my head. Taking it down, I wipe down my legs and put on some black boxer-briefs. At least that's enough that the others won't freak. LeStatt turns his attention back to Conway. 'We are at a critical phase in the fight against Dies Irae. We have the numbers to combat them but we need to organize and go after them by delegating. Ciel and Sombra are being double-booked and they are EXPECTED to win. Then they'll have their three victories. The open challenge to teams with three wins didn't specify that they had to have three wins as a tag team, so your man is getting double-booked and so is Ciel. WHEN they when, because I won't accept any *if*, they'll be at four wins and will most certainly challenge for the tag team titles. In this war, gold is terrority... we've got to shore up as much as possible.' Tessa shakes her head. I'm looking at them now as I dry my torso. She says, 'I can't agree with that, Knight! It's not fair that you're making them go through more in order to get your way.' It goes without saying since Knight and I are so tall, but Brack is the shortest male in the room by about 8 or 9 inches of height. But he doesn't back down. Conway storms out, leaving with, 'Damn right! Sombra has his own reasons for being in GCW and being your pawn isn't one of them.' 'For his own health, he's safer with me than against me...' Knight growls, though Conway's all but left by this point. His attention going to Tessa Windsor, he chastises her for dissenting in opinion. 'And this isn't an issue of fair. If you could aspire to be the kind of leader I am, you'll see that sacrifices have to be made for the greater cause.' I can't help adding in, 'Yes, and I don't see you opting to make any of those sacrifices.' The Knight Train is now squarely set its mental tracks on me. Now boarding all passengers to Ciel, estimated time of arrival... now. 'I've given more to this company than you'll ever give in your life. I've been here eating and sleeping and breathing GCW before you heard of this company.' Which is untrue, but he's on a roll. I won't interrupt. 'In this set-up you only need speak when you're spoken to. Is that clear?' Nice, reliable Tessa backs me up this time. I don't have to say anything, although I'd normally be all over that. 'Cut it out, Knight. He's on our side, you don't have to act like that. Besides, I can tell you for a fact that intimidation doesn't work on him.' Having people to vouch for you always helps, I find. Knight changed his tone slightly. Just a quarter of a basis-point lower on the intensity and jackass meter. 'And you have no business talking anyway, you don't even have a win right now. You're lower on the totem pole than Sombra... hell, he at least managed to win one match. Frankly that I'm allowing you into this group and giving you two chances to redeem yourself, you should be thankful for it.' You know I just have to laugh at that. And I do. Not a long laugh, only a couple of seconds. But it conveys my sentiment pretty well, I feel. 'What the hell are you laughing at?' he demands. 'You forgetting your place? What even makes you an ideal member of the Ascension?' Pulling on black sweat pants, I shake my head. I have to. It's the wrong question to ask. 'I'm not, I will freely admit that. There's nothing about me that makes me *the guy* to have on your side. All that I am is a man who knows a lot about how to wrestle and I do it well. But if you're looking for obedient myrmidons to follow your every command without fail, I'm obligated to end your delusion of impressing that vision upon me. I'm not here for you, and I'm not on your side.' That cracking sound is his fist clenching. 'What?' he says louder than anything else he's done so far. I continue. 'I couldn't care less about you. I wasn't recruited by you, so I don't follow you. My only affiliation and connection here is that woman standing behind you.' I don't point at Tessa because that's not polite to do. 'You have no controlling interest over me. She asked for my help and I agreed to assist her. If anyone, I only answer to her as per the terms of our agreement.' The world champion grimaces, looking me over. 'You'll do anything for T&A, that it?' I slip on a red T-shirt, answering, 'No, but I'll do nearly anything for a friend. I wonder if you can understand that.' 'Your after-school-special lessons of loyalty don't mean a thing to me. You'll still do as I say and you're still being double-booked.' Tessa gets back into the game with, 'It's not right to try to force him into that! You don't have to do it so he shouldn't!' Her rebuke comes not from LeStatt, but from me. 'Tessa, I appreciate your concern, but if I could offer my own input, I don't mind the two matches. I like the paycheck and I like the competition. I'm guaranteed that with this plan, so while I reject Knight's order to do this, I would offer to do it of my own volition. And with that, Mr. Knight, we're done speaking' She speaks softly in aside, saying, 'I still don't like it.' Knight just pokes me in the chest. 'You just get your head straight and understand that I'm in charge here.' And thankfully, the MEMEMEMEME tension in the air all subsides as LeStatt leaves the locker room. I sit down on the bench and place socks over my feet. Windsor, mentally fatigued as she is by the events, sits down next to me and puts her head on my shoulder. 'I've never wanted to just go home and get away from a single person who's supposed to been on my side of a fight before,' she confides. I haven't known her to be dishonest, but then again she's had a lot of experience with this kind of thing. I don't know if she's forgetting something, or if LeStatt Knight is really that bad. Patting me on the knee, she adds in, 'Thanks for backing me up.' 'It's what you have me here for,' I remind her. She immediately comes back with, 'Yeah, but I have no doubt that if you weren't in the group and just happened to overhear, you'd still support me. So... thanks.' I don't undercut what she says. She needs to say it, to just get out something positive after the episode with Knight. And at any rate, Tessa's right. I ask her, 'I don't believe that after how vocal Conway was about this that he'd let Sombra be booked twice in one night. What are you going to do about it?' She shrugs, head still on my shoulder as I put my feet into my shoes. Though they're sneakers, they're tied and just tight enough to stay on my feet, but I can slip my feet in and out of them. It doesn't take long for Tessa to say, 'Knight was wrong. He didn't task everyone out. I can ask to see if Sombra just wants to wrestle Rufio. And then LeStatt totally forgot about Peter Vetra. I recruited you guys at the same time. Maybe he'll tag with you.' I just say, 'However you want to do it, I'm not picky about the partner. It's unfortunate that you have to play this game with Knight, but whatever you need of me, I will help you. I am, essentially, only here for you.' Not a declaration of love or anything, but you know sometimes you just share a moment with a person that there's... that connection. And you know that you said exactly what they needed to hear, and it's just one of those times where there aren't any other words since it'd just mar the completeness of what just transpired. This is one of those times, as Tessa doesn't say anything else to me. She just grabs my arm and holds in hers for a few moments. I can't tell you how long it takes. I don't count the seconds and I pretty much stop and just let this moment be, same as her. I guess for whatever reasons people have to be in Ascension, I've got the least team-oriented but also the least selfish. Tessa Windsor is my friend. I am her backup in this faction war that's developed. And right this moment, apparently, I am what grounds her in this hectic day.
