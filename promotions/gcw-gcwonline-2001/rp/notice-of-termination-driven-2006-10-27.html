Notice of Termination (Driven)

27 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Friday, October 27th, 2006 Only the glow of the TV set. He simply holds her in her arms, allowing her to sleep through the movie. He pays no attention to the film, instead staring down at the beauty resting against him. Has he done anything to deserve this? Truly, undoubtedly deserve the wonderful moment afforded to him? She's gorgeous. And he? He is a man that the world seems to love to hate. How is it that, with the entire world seemingly against him, he gets this peaceful moment? That rather than being out dealing with these people... The drunks that scream drunken nonsense at him, and the soccer mom's that curse him out for saying things to their children that are only half as bad as what they're yelling... He is here with her. At peace. ...Or not. Because there is always something there irritating him. Something that sits in the back of his mind... Reminding him that all moments, even this, must end. Slowly, he takes his arms away from her. He begins to slip out from under her, guiding her carefully down, lying her head to rest on a pillow. He slips away, letting her stay asleep. He moves from the TV room, away into the back room of the woman's apartment. Aaron Rain steps into the room and closes the door behind him. He takes a deep breath and lets out a sigh, adjusting the skull cap that is holding his blond hair away from his face. He reluctantly moves toward his bag, unzips it, and pulls out a small packet of white papers. He plops down onto the bed and begins to look the packet over once again. On the cover, in bold, black letters, it reads: Notice of Termination from Worldwide Entertainment Wrestling, Inc. President Benjamin Harlum WEW Board of Directors Another sigh escapes Rain's lips, desperately not wanting to continue. The last two years of his life had been spent here... Travelling all over the world. Sacrificing himself for an ungrateful employer in front of an abusive audience. Every night, win or lose, would not result in the 'high' commonly associated with participating in professional sports... But with unwanted, unneeded, and undeserved depression. Because no matter how hard one tries to convince themself that what those people think means nothing... They can't truly be ignored. Thousands of people hating you at once can have that sort of affect. But Aaron Rain lived for that... Now, however, that point in his life is over. With one meeting, it all ended. In the course of that meeting... He'd done everything he could to convince Mr. Harlum, once believed to be a friend, that he didn't deserve this. Rain had, among other things, had his hair lit on fire to put on the best show he could. But as much as he believes that the termination is unjust, there's nothing that can be done. It's time to move on. He turns the page. Dear Mr. Aaron Rain The paper begins to crumble in his fingers. Even the way that they typed his name seemed patronizing. Never once had he been called 'Mister' by anyone in that company. In all honestly, respectful titles such as that weren't commonly used when referring to him. He continues. Further to our meeting of October 11, 2006, I confirm that your employment with us is terminated with effect from October 28, 2006. Once again he stops, and this time, he crumples the notice into a ball and throws it at the garbage can by the door. He falls backwards on the bed, holding his hands over his face, and begins to mumble incoherently to himself. He sits back up and pulls the hat off of his head, allowing limp, jaw length blond hair to tumble out from beneath it. He tosses the hat to the foot of his bed. Next, he takes off the black track jacket that he's been wearing for... Well, as long as he can remember. He'd always believed that everyone, no matter what sort of person, had something that could make them feel safe... Always feel comfortable. He wears the jacket almost constantly, including on his way to the ring. But, right now, it just didn't feel appropriate. He tosses it down with his hat. Suddenly, he stops, looking over toward the camera.... And simply begins to talk. 'You know... I've been doing this for a while now. The whole... Wrestling thing. Five years now. And it seems like everywhere I've gone has been exactly the same. Everything turns out exactly the same. I come in. I do my think. I get booed. I pick up the paycheck. I move on. But I'm not going to let happen this time around.' 'This time, I won't be content to let everything just... Happen. This time I'm going to stand up and let it be known that things will be done MY way. I won't deal with the people telling me that they hate me.. With people letting me know that they want me to die. What? FUCK IT. Because it's not worth going through all of that again.' ' I love this this sport... Honestly, I do. That's why it frustrates me to know that it's filled with such pricks.' 'I'm going to make GCW my crowning achievement... My crown jewel, if you will. I will be loved, and admired, and adored, and worshipped and cherrished, AND CARED FOR.. THE LIKES OF WHICH NO ONE HAS EVER SEEN BEFORE, AND WILL NEVER SEE AGAIN.' 'This time... I don't just want to be the best. I need to be the best. I'm driven. to be the best, because in my heart I know that I'm capable of doing things right this time around. This is my chance. THIS is my moment. Remember my name. Because from the ashes of a failed opportunity will rise a new opportunity..' 'Ladies and gentlemen... Remember my name. I am the great Saint of wrestling... I am Aaron Rain.' Rain turns away, and is just about to lie down when he hears a soft knock on the door. 'Come in,' he says, just loud enough for the person outside to hear it. The doorknob turns, and in walks... A little girl. About four years of age. She's got brown hair, about down to her shoulders. The one thing most noticable, even through the sleepy expression on her face, is her eyes. They're a brilliant green... The one obvious trait that she inherited from her father, her mother's ex-boyfriend. Rain had met the man once before... An abusive alcohlic with no regard for anything but himself. That one time had ended in violence... Or, more accurately, a drunken man being pelted by knees to the head. It's not an experience that anyone wishes to relive. 'Haley.. What are you doing up so late?' He asks. 'Awen,' she begins, 'why wer yoo yellin?' He stands up and walks over to her, kneeling down so that he's eye level. 'I'm sorry, did I wake you up, honey?' 'Yeah,' she tells him, 'and you woke up Ewmo!' She shows him an old Elmo stuffed animal from behind her back. The toy obviously isn't the newest of toys; the arm that she holds it by has a partial tear in the shoulder. 'Well, I'm very sorry that I woke you two up. But you need to get back to bed! If your mom wakes up, I'm gonna be in trouble!' She smiles at him. 'Otay...' He scoops her... And Elmo... Up and stands. He gives one last look toward the camera, and walks out of the room.
