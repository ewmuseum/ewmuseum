Tell Me A Story.

13 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/13/11 The world as I knew it has been flipped upside-down. After some investigative work I learned that Dorn Tracer was not the man he led me to believe he was. Not only that, but The Following was being lied to the entire time as well. Dorn was not the leader and the preachings were all a lie as well, the real leader of The Following was me. I know that after drinking the kool aid that Dorn has fed to them for so long they will not believe me so easily. It will take a lot of work for me to convince The Following to allow me to lead them, but it is something that must be done. Unlike the sins Dorn has created, I will never run from The Following in their time of need, I will battle for our freedom. Katrina will always be by my side as I try to piece together the empty voids of my past. From everything Katrina has told me since we got back together, Dorn was the one that brought her into The Following. Something like that could be seen in a positive light, but with everything that has been going on there must be a reason for it. If Dorn had plans for something that involved me in The Following then my wife Katrina joining was not a coincidence. While I work on getting to the bottom of this little investigation of mine, I also need to get a better idea on who has been keeping an eye on The Following. Could Robyn be so dumb as to test me once again? She knows what the consequences for both her and her daughter would be if she dared. Detective Jordan is dead, I know this is certain as I watched members of The Following do as I instructed by tossing his dead body into the river. Or perhaps I'm looking past the obvious and Dorn is the one monitoring us, maybe he placed us under surveillance because he was becoming suspicious. The most important task at the moment is assuring The Following that I am more capable of leading them than Dorn ever was. In the last few months The Following as seen nothing more than failure from me while under his guidance, but it is all beginning to change. I have shown them that I am capable of leading them onward into glory and worldwide recognition by defeating Jet Mason. This next match shall prove my dominance even further as I defeat the Prophet of SoCal in one Brad Cruz. - Everyone is talking between each other as they fill the seating across the rows of pews inside the chapel. The lights are turned on tonight even though there is a row of lit candles in front of the stage. The door to the side room opens and Donny Diamond walks out with Katrina at his side. Together they step onto the stage and Donny takes his place behind the podium to speak. “My brothers and my sisters I hear the whispers between all of you. I feel the fear that nests inside and I share your confusion. Father... No, Dorn Tracer was an amazing man in all of your eyes, I know. There was a time when I shared the same feelings, believe me. Then there is some of you that see me as nothing more than a common man and wonder what I can do that he couldn't.” He speaks in a tone that demands attention from everyone before him. He walks in front of the podium in a stance of dominance as he speaks. “Since recruiting me Dorn has promised to bring us forth into the world in dominant fashion, and he has disappointed us all with failure. I on the other hand have shown my dominance! I will not allow us to be seen as weak!” As he walks back behind the podium he stops for a moment as a member of The Following yells to him. “Tell us brother Diamond; what proof do you have to such wild allegations?” Donny smiles at the question and decides to walk down in the middle of the crowd. He looks across at different members across the pews, dressed in their dark colored robes. In a curious voice he speaks. “Brother Michael, tell me what happened to you in life that caused you to walk a new path.” Being a little shy of speaking in front of so many people he whispers softly. “One night I was driving home from work after my wife came to pick me up, along with our kids. Half way there a drunk driver hit us. I was the only one that survived.” As Katrina wipes a tear from her eye, while hearing the story Donny makes a reply. “I feel your pain my brother. I was once in a similar situation a few years ago. Would anyone else like to take this time to share a story?” Donny walks around looking at different people and stops in front of Sister Trisha. She looks to him and smiles. “Why don't you share a story of your life with us sister.” She has a slight giggle in her voice as she speaks. “I like to burn things.” Members of The Following can be heard chuckling before Donny speaks. “Silence! We do not laugh at our brothers and sisters, we embrace them and help them heal!” “Continue my sister, please.” He listens intently to her story as she speaks. “Instead of getting me help for my problems when I was younger my parents kicked me to streets when I was only nine years old. I had no place to go and Father Tracer was the only person there for me.” Donny places his hand over her head and looks around for a moment before speaking. “Sister Trisha fear not. All of you fear not. Father Tracer said the pain will eventually go away, but I know its still inside all of you eating away. With me leading us however, the pain and the scars driven in our souls will all be washed away!” Sister Trisha is pleased by the words spoken by Donny, but not everyone is so easily convinced. As he takes his place behind the podium to speak the same person from earlier speaks out. “All I can see here tonight is a bunch of talking and promises we all heard from Father Tracer. I still see nothing to prove he has betrayed us all. Maybe it is you that is trying to betray him, by turning us all to your side.” Donny removes his trench coat, along with his black sunglasses and hands them to Katrina. Anger can be heard in his voice as he speaks out. “Tell me your name brother.” His eyes search around waiting for a response. “Jake Hamis.” The now known man can be smiling with the mentioning of his name. Donny smiles a moment before speaking. “Brother Jake, ever so hesitant to believe the words I speak. Do I blame you? Of course not. If I had not the respect to hear your worries, then I would be no different from the rest of the sheep in the flock. I would however like to show you a similarity in the lies you were told as a child and with the lies I say Dorn has been telling us all.” Katrina stands from her seat and hands Donny two books. He takes hold of both and raises them into the air as he speaks loudly. “In my right hand I hold the holy bible, the book that the world thrives to live their daily lives off of. On every page you will read tales of a God, his prophet's and his son Jesus. Promising love, peace and anything else that will put your mind at ease. A book of false hope and propaganda used to control mankind!” Donny places the bible down on the podium, but keeps the other raised. “In my left hand I hold the book of Her word. Dorn has stood in the very place I stand now reading from this book, and having us believe her tales. She promises us a better life and a reason to live if we do her bidding and the bidding of those she has chosen to speak for her. She tells us to believe in sacrificing for each other and for those that do, we shall live eternal.” Beside the bible he places the book of Her word. He goes to speak, but notices Katrina step in front of him on stage. Her voice is soft and calm as she speaks. “Listen to what my loving Donny is trying to tell all of you. There is no difference between these books, they were both made to brainwash you into believing something false and fake. What could my love possibly gain from any of this?” The first to answer is of course Brother Jake. “What does he have to gain? Control! Dominance! Power! Brother Donny wants to lead us, but what makes him more qualified at doing this job than any of us? I'll tell you the answer. Nothing! We are not a flock of sheep made to believe something so easily. We are strong minded and have plenty of knowledge on how the world works.” Shocked over the rant being made by Brother Jake, Katrina hammers her fists down onto the guard rails before speaking. “What is wrong with you Jake? Donny has come before all of you with nothing less of dedication to make sure all of you are treated fairly. He sees all of us as equals and all he is asking is to give him a chance to show you how Dorn has been manipulating all of us for his personal gain.” Out of worry for the woman that was once his wife Donny places his arm around Katrina and helps her back onto the stage. “Dark days are coming my brothers and sisters, dark days for us all. I remember when he told me that I would lead his new army into the war. The war between good and evil – I want to make sure everyone is protected, I just ask for you to trust me, so that...” Before Donny is able to finish his sentence the harsh and outspoken member interrupts. “Then if that's the case, all of this is quite simple. This person, or perhaps even entity, that you speak of surely has a name. If he's so all-knowing and powerful then he must have been mentioned in a book, on a scroll, or even in a tale. Just give us his name and then maybe we can understand this better.” He takes a moment to listen to the words just spoken by Brother Jake. A blank expression can be seen over his face as he speaks. “I... His name... I'm sorry, I can't remember.” Katrina notices how upset Donny has just become and takes him by the hand. The two of them walk off the stage and towards the side room as Brother Jake yells aloud. “You can't remember because he does not exist! HE'S A FAKE JUST LIKE YOU!” Members of The Following begin to gossip as Donny and Katrina enter the side room and walk down the spiral stairs lit by candles on the wall. She opens the doors to their private quarters and the two of them walk inside. Donny makes it to his bed and lays down as she takes a seat on the edge beside him, while he holds onto her hand. All of the noise upstairs can still be heard. “Katrina... Tell me the story one more time on how Dorn brought you into The Following.” His voice is calm and low as he closes his eyes to relax. “I was at the mall with my girlfriends shopping. We were on our way out and I noticed I had left my purse in the shoe store we were just at. So I told them all goodbye as I went back towards the door. On the way I bumped into this guy, I didn't really think nothing of it, till I looked down and saw he had dropped a book.” Her voice is soft to try and calm him a bit from the trauma he just endured. She lays down beside him with her head on his chest. “I couldn't find the guy so after I got my purse I went home and took the book with me. It stayed on my dresser for weeks before I even touched it again, then one day I was bored and opened it. From page to page I read on for about a week and at the end there was an address.” He turns to her and kisses her forehead before speaking. “So you got curious and came here?” Her smile is golden as she looks to him. “In a sense, yes. This place was still under construction, but curiosity did get the best of me and I paid a visit to the address. I was welcomed inside and greeted by members of The Following and eventually by Dorn himself.” The gossip upstairs hasn't calmed and Brother Jake is likely rallying them up to be even louder. Katrina tries to keep him calm by continuing the story. “We talked for a while and he noticed my finger, where my wedding ring once was and asked about my marriage. The moment I mentioned your name he became as pale as a ghost and told me how he knew where you were. He never mentioned how he found you or anything that you told us all about. He asked me if I wanted to see you again, he told me you were no longer the same person. It took me a while to decide, but I agreed and he sent me to a woman named Lola who told me where I could find you. That's when I went to Larry's Gym.” At the mentioning of her name he sits up in a state of alarm. “Lola... I remember that name. Katrina please tell me you still have that book.” She becomes a bit scared and nods to him. He replies with his voice in a panic. “You need to take me to that address.” - "It is easier to find a score of men wise enough to discover the truth than to find one intrepid enough, in the face of opposition, to stand up for it." - A. Hodge.
