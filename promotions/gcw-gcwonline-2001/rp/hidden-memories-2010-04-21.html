Date: 04/21/10 Fifteen Years Ago It was pitch-dark and the grounds were soggy and muddy and thick with puddles. It was finally quiet in the Harris home after hours of shouting and fighting. Joey ran outside and hid in his treehouse which he would commonly do when his Father started drinking. In drunken rage his father would call Joey Slash because Joey would seem like a total different person sometimes. Earlier in the evening, Joey was playing baseball and as he was rounding first base he began to hear thoughts that told him someone was coming to kill him. Instead of reaching second, Joey ran to the outfield and crouched in the corner. His father, also his Coach, was so frustrated he drug Joey by his arm to the car and screamed at him the whole way home. Joey wanted to explain that he had weird thoughts, but he did not want to seem crazy. Instead he would let his father scream at him, and when they reached home he would go to his room, down the tree outside of his window and across to the backyard. He waited for four hours watching the sun set and the lights evantually turn off in his home. "They are fighting about you", the voices made Joey believe. His mother would constantly tell his Dad to be nicer and not shout as much. His mom seemed to understand even when Joey could not explain what was going on. His mother was always like that, she was king, compassionate and always caring of her only child. "He will kill her, he will kill her and it will be your fault "Slash"!", the voices shouted inside his head. Joey inched closer and closer toward the dining-room window. He had to peak in and see if his drunken Father was still awake. For years, Joey would cry in his room alone and wish he could gain his father's approval. Not anymore though, "it's time to end their miserable lives, it's time to stop the insanity you created!" Joey snuck into the house quietly, carrying with him a can of gasoline that was used for the lawnmower. He had planned this for months, he was going to end his families misery. Misery that he was convinced he caused. Joey was so uptight that he noticed he left mud stains on the carpet from his shoes. "You idiot! The voices in his head were shouting louder than ever now. Joey could no longer escape the fear they caused. For years Joey would find an escape, or simply crawl in a ball in the corner of the room and wish it away. He could not do it any longer. It was time to stop the voices, and stop his Father at the same time. With tears in his eyes, Joey slowly crept through the hall, he would set the guest room on fire, and then wake up his Mother. Joey stood at the doorway of the guest room and stared at his Father, still in his day clothes and laying sprawled on the bed. "Daddy?" Joey whispered, watching for any response from his drunk Father. "Daddy," he said louder. Still no response, he was out for the night. "Do it, you freak!" Joey slowly poured the gasoline on the carpeted floor surronding the bed. The smell of the gasoline hurt Joey's nostrils as he made a trail of gas towards the door. Just like he had planned for months. Joey shut the door quietly, and grabbed a chair he had brought up the stairs with him. Leaning it against the door and underneath the doorknod, Joey ensured it was nice and tight. He must not escape, because if he does it would mean beatings. "Burn him! Burn the fucker!" Joey took the matchbox from his pocket. And without thinking about it any longer he lit the match and threw it to the ground. All of a sudden Joey felt extreme pain on his right shoulder and arm. He dropped to the ground and noticed his shirt was on fire. Joey screamed in pain and began to roll on the floor. Stop. Drop. Roll. Stop. Drop. Roll. The flame was still burning but Joey had no time, he had to get to his Mother. He crawled across the hall, holding his burnt arm and got to the Master bedroom. "Mommy!" Joey yelled with no response. Quickly his eyes scanned the room and noticed his Mom was not there. "Mommy!" Joey screamed. He could hear his father trying to get through the door. He was kicking the door relentlessly and Joey suddenly felt afraid if his Dad escaped. The smoke in the hall was blinding, but Joey crawled on the floor getting to the stairs. With all the strength his small body could muster, Joey stood and rushed down the stairs. "Mommy!" Joey yelled, hold his arm. He then noticed his Mother's body in the kitchen, behind the island. He hadn't noticed that before, he was too distracted. He ran over to his Mother and noticed that there was a pool of blood around her head. His dad must have hit her hard this time. Tears started flowing down Joey's cheek as he tried to wake his Mother up. "Mommy!?" Joey yelled. Smoke started filling the house and Joey's mouth started feeling so dry his tongue was sticking to the top. Joey crawled to the back door, just as he had planned it. The voices were silent now, they always stopped talking when he did something bad. Guilt started flooding Joey's mind and he could feel butterflies in his belly. He lied on the cold, wet grass in the backyard and closed his eyes. He wanted to forget this moment. "Mommy," Joey said quietly. And then he blacked out. ---- Present Day His arms were shaking so violently he could not lift the Whiskey bottle without dropping it. He stumbled around with it for a few moments before laying on the floor again. He did not know what date it was, or where he was. The bright sun beamed through the slight opening in the curtains, but Joey just wanted to sleep all day. He has been doing that lately, ever since he arrived in... "Where the fuck am I?" Joey mumbled. Joey thought for a moment, trying to clear the alchohol indused cobwebs that cluttered his thoughts. "Hawaii." Joey said... "I'm in God damn Hawaii". Pleased with himself, Joey smiled slightly before shutting his eyes again. "Hey shit-head," the woman's voice startled Joey as his eyes opened wide. Joey looked up from the ground and saw a brunette with long legs, short skirt and bra on. Her hair was done nicely, but it was hard not to notice the rings around her eyes and her countance screamed 'drug addict'. Joey could not remember who she was, instead he tried to close his eyes. Impatiently, the woman stomped her high heels on the ground beside Joey's head and the sound rung through his head at deafening decibles. "You owe me money," she shouted. Joey opened his eyes again and tried his hardest to put together a sentance, "my wal-let i-is ova-over there", he slurred. She rolled her eyes at him, "I already tried that fucker. Now find the five hundred dollars or I will call someone to find it for you," she said. Her voice felt like a sword jabbing at his cranium, and Joey had no strength to get off the ground. How much did I drink last night?, he wondered. He lifted one hand and tried to calm the prostitute down, "liset-listen I-I ummm- who are you?" Joey asked. He noticed her impatience growing as she pulled her blouse over her head and then had a cell phone. "Wai-Wait, I got fi-five hundre-hundred dollars. Che-check," Joey stopped talking for a moment and tried to figure out where his cash would be. "My pants," Joey said, "my pants ov-over there". He didn't have strength to point, so instead he moved his eyes in the direction to where his pants could be. He couldn't remember exactly where they were. She hustled to the other side of the room and pulled up a pair of jeans and pulled cash out of her pocket. Counting the money quickly, she shoved it all in her bra. "Kee- keep, the cha-change", Joey stuttered sarcasticaly. She hustled out of the hotel room with no good-bye and left Joey laying on the ground in the quiet hotel room with the whiskey bottle beside him. Joey shut his eyes again. "You killed her!" a voice inside of Joey's head shouted. Joey rolled over on his side and grabbed his head with his hands and laid there until the voices stopped talking. A quiet mind has become less and less of a common thing for Joey. In fact, he could not remember when the voices were silent last. "You are a maniac, 'Slash'!" the voice shouted. This time it eerily sounded like his father's. Joey grabbed his head righter. "It's time to show the world just how fucked up you are!" Joey gripped tighter. "It's time to show the world that you are one fucked up kid!". Joey's fingers were white from the pressure he was putting on his head with his hand. "Fuck 'em all Joey Harris! Fuck 'em all 'Slash'!". "FINE!" Joey shouted, his voice echoing off the walls of his empty hotel room. I'll do whatever you say, Joey thought. Feelings of hopelessness bombarbed Joey's thoughts. He was alone again, and he was losing control. He wished he had brought the drugs that he was suppose to take with him, but they were nowhere to be found. He had complained they caused him too tired and caused him to lose his first match. Now he wishes he didn't dump them. "Show the world how fucked up you are!"
