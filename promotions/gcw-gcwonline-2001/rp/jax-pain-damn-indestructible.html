Jackson tried to enjoy the rotting cesspool known as New York as much as he could with all the business he had at hand. He tried to ignore the fact that McLeod had been walking at his side for three blocks as he burned the soft orange tip of his deadhead Camel. Taking a brief smoke break, he looked down at the display of his cell phone, eyes scanning over the code. Coded in a mix of Russian letters and numbers, it instructed Jackson to take a trip up to a private suite in Manhattan's premiere hotel, the Ritz Carlton. Figuring it to be Lyashenko, he took his time enjoying his vice on the crowded streets of Manhattan. Taking a moment to inhale the stench the busy city gave off, his eyes narrowed, already scanning the surroundings. "Yeh're not being followed," McLeod muttered. Jackson said nothing, pushing off from the building he was leaning against, and walking across the street between cars. He didn't pause to flip off the drivers that honked and waved their fists out the windows. Fact was, he hadn't even heard them. He'd taken to not hearing much of anything lately. It made dealing with things easier. A half smoked cancer stick later and he was up in the velvety red halls, roaming for room A1, on the highest floor of the building. A note had been left on the door, neatly laced on paper with modern cursive. Like Alice, through the looking glass, it said simply to 'open the door'. He laughed under his breath, getting a little thrill out of the game. It was Halloween, after all. He squared his shoulders beneath the heavy leather duster, and reached for the handle. He pushed inside, hand already dropping towards his gun. A darkness so pure and absolute enveloped him as he stepped away from the door. It closed silently, with just a whisper of air. He took one step, then another before he heard the rapid click-click of a double barrel Mossburg pump being cocked. A defiant smirk crossed Jackson's face, as this was not the first, nor the last time a gun would have been pointed at him. "Trick or treat," he said, stifling another chuckle. "Identify yourself." The command echoed in the cavernous space, masking the location of the man with the gun. The English was flawless, with only the slightest tinge of a Russian accent. All windows were closed tight, leaving the glow of downtown Manhattan to reflect off the tinted, bulletproof glass. Only the best accommodations here at the Ritz. "And I looked, and behold a pale horse: and his name that sat on him was Death, and Hell followed with him." The sardonic smirk that curved his lips was visible in the wry twist to the words as he put his back to the door, listening to the room around him. The ticking of the clock in the kitchenette, the hum of the refrigerator, the clicking of sawed off lead against metal. They immersed his senses as a rough chuckle showed approval of Jackson's poetic entrance. "Jax? dearest of all my friends? I am so glad you could come." It was Vladimir Lemirov. He called everyone the 'dearest of all his friends'- which was the reason behind Jackson's involuntary wince. With a muted click, lights flooded the room, revealing a luxurious suite. Vlad reclined on a long leather couch, gentling stroking the shotgun while a blond with tits bigger than Texas fawned over him. He nodded at Jackson, setting aside the modified blast. "Have a seat, my friend! Kick back and-" "Vlad, if it's all the same to you," Jackson replied, "I'll stand." He kept his hands loose, making no effort to conceal it. He wanted Lemirov to know he was carrying- hell it was almost expected. A grim expression settled over his immovable features, the all too familiar Jackson scowl in place. "So, what is it this time, Vlad? You need me to break up a gang of thugs shooting up that shithole club you still haven't managed to get opened since Ragnarok closed?" Lemirov laughed, and reached to his side, plucking a remote from the table. With a click the plasma added life to their dismal conversation, bringing up what looked like surveillance footage of a stout man. Looking to be in his mid-thirties, he effortlessly dumped cellophane wrapped corpses underneath a bridge overpass. The footage was grainy, having been filmed in piss poor night vision, but that lime green glowing face made an impression on Jackson. Slightly narrowing his eyes but never taking them off the disturbing visual, Jackson said, "so who's this asshole?" "Errol Arnot. Valkyr. V- the drug that had ruined his life. "Dead?" Lemirov nodded, "Jax, you're my dearest-" "Save it, Vlad. Tell me where he is, and when you want this done." He didn't quibble over the details. Tonight he was off the clock, and his other life was forgotten. This was the real Jackson. The hunter- the killer. "Kill him with NO MERCY. And as always, I have the cash ready and up front." Folding his fingers together in his lap, Lemirov looked satisfied, knowing that he wouldn't have the killing trace back to him. Jax had the biggest mobster body count New York had ever seen. What was one more cretin in the mix? "Yeah. Fine. I have a tight schedule," Jackson remarked flatly, moving closer to the frozen face on the screen, committing it to memory. "When do you need this?" "How is tonight for you?" he replied, knowing that Jackson had nowhere to be this week. "I'm sure I can work it in," Jackson said with a wry smile, holding up his hand to the light, and picking at the blood that was still crusted beneath his nails. "You know a good exorcist?" he growled, clenching his fists tight enough to make the scars visible on his bloodless knuckles. "There's a ghost I need to put to rest." With a quirked eyebrow, intrigue set into the grooves of Lemirov's face. "Oh really?" He chuckled, figuring that Jax was just making a joke, apropos the season. His finger stabbed at the television remote and switched off the grisly footage as it sprang to life and looped again. The indifferent mask fell back into place as Jackson stared at Lemirov. He waved his hand, dismissing the topic. "Slice him up real good, Jax. I prefer machete but I'll take any sharp and heavy instrument you'd prefer." He smirked, settling back as the blonde as his side turned her attention southward. Jackson looked away, mildly disgusted. "He should be under the Brooklyn Bridge around two. Look for a windowless white van parked near the bridge's south exit. He'll be making frequent trips to it, so try to catch him as he makes one of his trips." His hands tucked comfortably behind his head as he awaited Jackson's response. Jackson nodded once, almost perfunctorily, and then grinned, flashing those unsettling white teeth at the Russian. "Consider it done, Vlad." Out the door he went and into the chilly New York night, basking in the basalt glow of the sleepless town. Black and white. Night and cold lights. The time was near enough- in two hours his objective would be at the killing grounds. Two hours would be more than enough time for him to find the perfect weapon. Lemirov wanted a powerful blade put to good use? This butcher had the perfect cleaver in mind. ---- Saturday, November 1, 2008 New York City The Viking helmet was cold against his scalp, the metal snug against his head.
