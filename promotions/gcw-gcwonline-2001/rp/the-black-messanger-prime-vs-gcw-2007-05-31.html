The Black Messanger (PRIME VS GCW)

31 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 31st "And the Black rider shall come down as a messenger, delivering the date and time of the events of judgement. And still the GCW Television Champion, Zacharia Taylor! Keegan O’Conner was no match for Zacharia. The power from his alcohol abuse and experience in the ring are never summoned together to make a good showing on the part of the abuser. Experience is nothing in the ring, not when God is on the side of the defender. His hands cut and body slightly bruised, Zacharia walks across the hall and into the parking lot of the venue. Nobody is left, only Zacharia. He walks over to the bus stop and sits down on the bench. You defied a movement, Zacharia, it is time to start our own. His thoughts slide in and out of his mind, intertwining with the sermons he is trying to bring to his lips for prayer. He is alone, nobody is in his corner, nobody wants to trust him. He is sure that if her were still with the Movement that Bagwell started upon his arrival, he would not have to sit waiting for a bus to pick him up after another winning match, successfully defending his title. Murray, Fox, Shaman. Allies? Hardly. Zacharia still sits alone, no matter where he goes. He is not understood, nor does he really care to be understood. His faith is what gets him to where he is, His faith is what made him what he is. It is God’s will to cleanse the world and right all wrongs, not to make friends and win popularity contests. Zacharia pulls out his leather wrapped book and flips to Keegan O’Conner. The small pencil falls into his lab and onto the ground. As Taylor reaches down to pick it up, the Transit bus pulls up and opens the doors. The driver is a middled aged man, black, and worn looking. Taylor throws in a couple bucks and looks up at the driver. "Where ya headin kid?" "Jersey, sir, or at least as close as possible." The driver smiles and nods, motioning his head towards the back of the bus. Zacharia readjusts the bag he has on his shoulder, with his TV Title Belt showing through the opening of the bag, and sits in the second to last row. Nobody was on the bus at this hour, only two men, sitting a couple rows apart. The ride from California to Jersey is a long one, couple days, but there is no rush, nobody is waiting up for him. Zacharia pulls out his book again, and flips to where the pencil is, Keegan O’Conner. On the second page of the information listed, he starts to write about the match. **** The clash of thunder over the plains of Texas woke up Zacharia Taylor. He rubs his eyes and stretches. The bus was still empty, all except one man, staring at Taylor from over his newspaper. At first he looks out the window as if he didn’t notice the man looking at him, but when he looks back, the man drops the paper and stares in his direction. The man is wearing an all black suit, with a matching black fedora. The strain on the still buttoned jacket shows that he is slightly over weight. The stare turns into a squinting, fierce look into Taylor’s eyes. Taylor sits up straight and stares back at the man. "If there is something that I can help you with sir, don’t be a stranger, but if there is nothing we can settle, advert your eyes." At these words the man turned away with a smirk on his face. The glistening off of his glasses at the next bolt of lightning showed small wrinkles by his eyes and a fading hair. The man pulls the newspaper back up to his face and continues to read. Taylor reaches into his bag and pulls out a copy of the Modern Testament and begins to read, keeping a sharp eye on the man. **** Taylor steps off of a bus in Mississippi and heads over to the ticket counter at the end of the bus terminal. The receptionist waves him over and presses her speaker button. "Can I help you, sir?" "I need to get to New Jersey, ma’am. I just need a ticket to the closest and fastest stop." "Well, sir, that would be the number 432, it brings you all the way up to Maryland, and then you can take one there right up through Jersey. But I am afraid that you are goin to have to wait a couple hours, I reckon it leaves at 3:30." "Thank you ma’am, but can I ask what time it is now?" "Well, it is noon on the dot, sir. If you want to take it, I would come back at around 2pm to purchas that ticket, unless you would like to do so now if you’re goin to do some exploring!" Taylor looks at her and smiles. Exploring. He just needs to get back to Jersey to prepare for his next match at High Stakes. He needs to handle a few things before heading all the way back to Nevada. Good thing he is taking the plane back across the country. "I’m in no rush ma’am, but I do have to ask, where is the closest church?" She eyes Taylor up and down before letting out a smile. Her eyes were now transfixed on his cross that is hanging around his neck. She was wearing a much smaller version of the same thing. "Well, sir, I have to say the best church in town is two blocks east from here. It is the All Saints Church. Can’t miss it, it’s a cathedral and has a huge cross on top of the tower. Might I ask what re..." "Thank you. I will make my way over there." Taylor hates the questions when it comes to what sect of faith he is. Nobody understands, nobody wants to understand, they just want to convert whomever they encounter to their own. Taylor does not convert people to his faith, he makes them feel his faith. He makes those he encounters repent with blood, repent with their souls to deter them from the evil ways of the world. They would never understand his faith, it is far too complex. Until he is back in Clifton, this church will have to do. He walks up to the cathedral, standing in the slight shadow that is cast by its tower at this hour of the day. Most walking past the church were doing just that, walking past it, but few were taking pictures as if faith was a tourist attraction, while others ventured inside. The sign on the door had a list of the time of masses, one is at noon. Stepping back a few steps to take a look at the clock on the tower, it brilliantly displays 12:30 on the gothic arms of the clock face. Without further discretion, Zacharia walks into the church. There were no more than three dozen people sitting in the pews that held at least three hundred. Taylor took up a seat in the back of the church, blessing himself with the holy water that is in a basin near the entrance. He puts his head down in prayer, taking the cross off from around his neck and putting it between his hands. "Father, please forgive me for my sins. I speak to you from a church I am unfamiliar with, but with all the intentions of talking to you in confidence. All churches are your house, but I feel distant from the one that I call my home. I sit here with the utmost intention on fulfilling the duties that you have bestowed upon me. Any that I cross, any that I meet that do not repent in your confidence will be dealt with by my hands..." Two finely polished shoes come into view at the end of the pew. Taylor can feel a man sit down next to him. There was a commotion in the front of the church where it was apparent that mass has just ended. Taylor uses the commotion ahead to raise up from his prayer and look at the man sitting next to him. The man was staring at Taylor, the same man that was on the bus the previous night with the newspaper. A sudden feeling of anger builds up inside of Taylor. He does not like to be disturbed in prayer, and he certainly does not like to be followed. The man’s smirk from ear to ear is disturbing as much as it is comforting. He looks ahead after the acknowledgment from Taylor and leans in towards Taylor’s ear and begins to whisper. The commotion of everyone walking past the end pew made it difficult for him to hear. Everyone that passes looks at the two men sitting in the last pew of the church, one man in all black, the other holding a bulging duffle bag with a hood on. The man finishes and does an awkward blessing of himself and slides a card into Taylor’s hands. The man stands up and walks out the door. Taylor looks at the front of the church and then back to the card. The card is blank. What is the purpose of a blank card? He turns it over in his hands and there is something handwritten on the back: Philips Arena Parking Lot Atlanta, GA May 31st, 12pm Taylor jumps to his feet and heads out the church door, in full sprint back to the bus terminal. When he turns the corner, there is no line at the ticket counter, so he runs up to the window. "A direct ticket to Atlanta!" **** "$15.75 sir!" Taylor hands the money over to the taxi driver and steps out of the car. He looks around the parking lot. The lot was mostly empty. A few cars here and there litter the parking lot, but there wasn’t really anyone really there. "Sir, what time is it?" "It’s 11:45 my man, 11:45." "Thanks. Take it easy." The taxi pulls away and leaves Taylor sitting there in the lot, alone. He looks over to the arena, and starts to head over. He has been all over the world with GCW, but he always likes the openness and freedom in the air in the southern states; much less pollution than New Jersey. He makes his way into the last row of cars before the stadium when a limo pulls up in front of him, blocking his way to the arena. The tinted window slowly drops and the tranquil face of Chris Bagwell pops out. "What’s up kid? So you got the message too, huh? Looks like the two stars of GCW are here. Did I see you get out of a cab?" "Yeah, I guess I’m not doing as well as you are than huh?" "Guess not. But then again, Caldera knows who brings in the ratings, brings in the fans, brings in the..." "Trouble..." "Come on Taylor, ya know, I can deal with that. Trouble... I kinda like that. What do you think this is about? Think we are fighting? Pitchin a new contract?" "I don’t know. Think we are the only ones being called up?" "The hell if I know." Two taxies pull up on either ends of the parking lot. One of the taxies hesitates, and then makes a b-line towards where Taylor and Bagwell are standing. The other taxi turns around the perimeter of the parking lot. Both taxies block in the limo on both sides, causing some concern for both wrestlers. "Please tell me this wasn’t a set up from Murray. Bastard, are you kidding me?" "Bagwell, he wouldn’t call me here if it was to jump us. Me and Murray..." "You and Murray what, Taylor?" A single man gets out of each taxi, both tower over Taylor. Taylor eyes up both men on each side of him and prepares for the worst. A monster of a man steps up to the end of the limo, brushing his hand over the hood of the limo, as if taunting its existence. The other man, a much skinnier man, but familiar, casually strolls up to Taylor with a smile on his face. Taylor shifts his feet and tenses up. Bagwell swings his head from side to side from outside the window looking at the men with a very surprising look, it was a smile. "Jackson, LeStrange, what’s up guys?" "Bagwell, is that you? Why are you still inside your damn limo? Having your mate over here doing the fighting for ya?" LeStrange smiles and puts his arm on Taylor’s shoulder. With the ease of the touch, Taylor stands down and loosens up. "I’m surprised you guys got the message too. He said there would be other people meeting up, but he didn’t exactly explain too much." Jackson looks around at his fellow GCW members and half smiles. Taylor looks over at Bagwell and shrugs his shoulders. "Hey, at least you were able to hear the guy. He followed me from California on a bus, then followed me into a church in Mississippi." "Mississippi? Why were you over there?" Taylor shoots Bagwell a fierce eye and then looks up and down the limo. "Oh, right." "Wait, he followed you on a bus after the last show?" LeStrange looked disturbed by this. "I mean, he followed me around a little too, but then just came up to me and whispered the stuff in my ear." "Sweet nothings, huh? You get down like that then..." "Can it Bagwell. Does anyone know why we are here?" Jackson stares down Bagwell, still in his limo. "Well, we are at an arena. Maybe someone saw us in GCW and thought we deserved a contract somewhere else?" LeStrange looks at the Philips Arena and then turns towards the back of the parking lot. "I don’t know my man. If this is some sick joke that got me out of bed to fly all the way down to this shit hole, I will be pretty pissed. I have a match to get ready for, and I have some, things to prepare." "Did any of you hear anything? Anybody from GCW at the last World Wide? I hopped on the bus right from the last show, and he was there soon after, so..." Taylor is cut off when he hears the sound of a door slam. Bagwell turns around in the limo and looks out the adjacent window. Jackson and LeStrange turn towards the sound of the noise. Jackson starts to make a fist and tenses up, while LeStrange just stares with a contorted face. The limo is too tall for Taylor to look over. The sound of shoes clapping against the asphalt resonate through the empty parking lot. Taylor follows the noise and the direction that the men’s heads are turning. From behind the limo, the man that gave him the card comes into view. He is wearing the same suit, glasses, and hat as he had on the bus and in the church. The man stood behind Taylor and began to speak. "Mr. Bagwell, get out of your limo, please, and stand with your fellow GCW teammates." Bagwell complies and gets out, taking his time, still with a selfish gloat about him. "Teammates?" "Good question Mr. Taylor. Welcome to Philips Arena where I am sure all your dreams and nightmares will come true. You have been brought here today by Prime to enter into our Super Show where you four will be pitted against four of our best <italic>newer</italic> members in a battle royal match. Whatever side wins, will bring back the dignity to their federation. I will show you to your locker rooms and give you the rundown of your match." The four men stand in shock. "So what you are telling me is that we have a match coming up and nobody told us?" "Mr. Bagwell, I thought you were always game for a match?" The man smiles and starts to walk away. He casually walks over to the entrance of the arena. "When is our match?" "Tonight, Mr. Bagwell. Hope you are ready..." The four men look at one another and follow in the wake of the promoter.
