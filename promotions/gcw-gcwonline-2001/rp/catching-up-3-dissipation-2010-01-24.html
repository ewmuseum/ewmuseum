Catching Up (3): Dissipation

24 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/24/10 Just an FYI, this is a continuation of my last piece (if that‘s not already obvious). August 19th, 2009 “I figured you’d show.” Markus Stone’s words would have come off much more macho if they actually meant a thing to either one of the two individuals staring him down at this moment. Instead, Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor stood opposite him with little to say. Storm with his arm folded flat across his chest took another step forward, and Tessa trying to catch her guard as her former fiancé showed up on the scene. With Storm refusing to respond to his prompt, Markus further provoked to get what he was looking for, “You can’t let her do a thing on her own, can you? You’re afraid of losing what little bragging rights you have left, afraid that she trusts me more. ” Storm still had little to offer Stone other than a slight plea that came off more like a command, “Leave her out of this Markus. She does not deserve to be dragged into your problem with me.” Markus saw things otherwise, interjecting with a hiss, “except she is the problem.” Tessa eyes suddenly burst wide open after hearing this. Markus Stone had spent the better part of an hour trying to convince her that Chris was the root of all evil, and then all of a sudden in the blink of an eye she was the epitome of their problems. She wanted answer just as badly as Chris at this point. “You’re your own damn problem!” Tessa ended up blurting, without so much as a second thought. The tension between all three of them ate away at her mind; she could hardly stand to stay quiet. Stone grinned as he turned his attention towards her, “Really? Because the last time I checked the man standing behind you couldn’t decide if it were worth us both breaking you in half for a few extra zero’s on his check. You ought to consider yourself blessed for being his favorite fuck toy, even if he’s got plenty of groupies to make up for all those times you want nothing to do with him. Regardless Tessa, I want you to walk away with the knowledge that if Caldera would have tacked on just one more number to that check, there’s nothing that would have stopped him from beating you to a bloody pulp.” Tessa turned back to face Chris, who took the slightest step back to acknowledge that some of what Markus was saying might have been true in the past, but certainly was not the case in the present. His arms slightly rose up to his hips, his palms pointed out, as if a simple hand gesture would save him from his past transgressions. “I hope he’s lying.” She mouthed to Chris, who stood there in utter disgrace before the woman he was trying to win back. There was no sense in lying about it to her, when she’d ultimately find out one way or another. But settling for the truth right then and there seemed to be ten times as painful. His head solemnly dropped as he pleaded to the woman he at one time wanted to marry, “I’ve changed Tessa.” Markus, the only one who stood there with a grin, played devil’s advocate aloud to his former best friends, “how many times has he told you that before?” Turning her head away from both men, Windsor started to march away. Even as enemies, the former duo found ways to drag Tessa into the middle of a messy situation. They absolutely loved to complicate things for anyone around them, call it their way of showing affection. “Tessa wait!” Tessa’s tiny size six feet barely made it four steps down the sidewalk before she ended up being yanked back in the opposite direction by her oversized shirts collar. Without hesitation she forced her eyes and ears shut to avoid the inevitable moment where Chris Storm would apologize and force rationality from illogical statements that only she had the capability to fall for. Surely enough it came, though at a slower pace than expected, “I’m sorry Tessa. I know I fucked up. You don’t remember me admitting that last night to you?” Rare as it was, Chris was actually speaking the truth. Twelve hours earlier, before all this chaos, he and Tessa had crashed on his couch while he relinquished every bit his pride and admitted to owning a tattered soul. Even with their shared understanding of why things played out the way they did, Tessa found it difficult to force a glance Chris’ direction to acknowledge she had heard his whispered apology. As much as she wanted to make things better, she firmly stood by her decision to keep from glancing his way. It was a petty act of protest but it had plenty of significance to her, call it her way of standing up for herself. “Hmmm, maybe she does have a spine.” Markus unnervingly chimed in. The full four steps he took to get to Tessa’s side were filled with pride and intent as he kept his eyes glued on Storm. Throwing his arm over Tessa’s shoulder to make things that much more painful, he announced with pride, “I’m glad you’ve finally developed some damn sense.” Things were not quite the way Markus Stone wanted to paint them either. Without so much as blinking, Tessa forcefully shrugged his arm away and took a step back before shouting at him, “would you lay off? Honestly, you’re just as bad as he is! ” Both members of the record breaking duo hushed as Tessa drew back another step. Her lips curled into a snarl and her brows dropped as she exchanged a brief glance with them one at a time. To the littlest one of the bunch, the timing was plenty opportunistic for her to finally put her foot down and make it clear that she followed orders from no one. However, even with opposing views of Tessa’s usefulness both Chris and Markus were well aware of how easily she could be manipulated, which is why neither bothered to back down. “Tessa wait!” Chris shouted. Zero hesitation resided inside of Tessa’s mind as she let his call fall on deaf ears. She only turned back when she had gotten about first down length away from the two without so much as another word from either of them, then she had wished she never budged. “Crap.” She whispered under her breath as she watched Chris draw closer Markus in slow motion speed, shouting every obscenity in the book loud enough for the entire block to hear. Crap was exactly what her child’s father would be in with the police if she did not go back. Of course the other little bird inside of her head reminded her of just how badly she wanted to escape these situations. Nothing else in the world made her more uncomfortable than the ruckus Chris Storm tended to create everywhere he went. Yet against her better judgment, Tessa quickly dashed in the direction of duo formerly known as B&E~! “Don’t do it Chris!” Tessa shouted out as soon as the self proclaimed Rock Superstar reared his arm back in fighting position and went straight for Markus’ face with a right. Thankfully, Markus dodged it just in time for Tessa to get back within arm’s reach. Without any thought, Tessa nabbed Chris’ arm just in time to keep him from blindly swinging again. It most certainly added to the amusement of Markus Stone, who despite being swung at just seconds ago, had a Cheshire grin plastered on his face.“Oh Tessa. You honestly cannot begin to understand how much your actions mean to me right now.” Tessa clearly did not share his amusement as she glared back at him through narrowed eyes, “you realize I just saved your ass, right?” His head nods as his arms fold across his chest ever so arrogantly, “Of course I do. But did you really have to? Or are you really that convinced that Chris is so high and mighty that none of us mere mortals can handle his strength?” “You’re drunk Markus!” She agitatedly shouted in defense, eyeing the half empty flask hanging from the pocket of his jacket, “Are you trying to get yourself killed?” “If that’s that way you want to see things.” As irritated as she was at the moment, even Tessa was willing to accept that he clearly was impaired by whatever he had chose to drink that afternoon, likely a couple of Jack’s. Even with being as pissed off as she was with him at the moment, her overwhelming soft side called for desperate measures, “go home and we’ll talk when you sober up.” “Go home?” He drunkenly chuckled, “You called me here? You don’t remember that darling?” This time it was Chris who peered over at Tessa with disappointed eyes. He’d spent the better part of the evening before talking Tessa out of any communication with Markus for reasons like this, yet Tessa, in all her stubborn glory, wanted to cling to her ideals. Even with the added pressure of Chris’ disappointment, Tessa stood her ground, “Go home Markus.” “I’d be glad to once you admit I’m right.” His composure still yet to be fully recouped, Chris chimed with his fists balled up, “I’d be glad to send you packing you piece of shit.” “Oh you scare me so much.” Markus teased, knowing full well that even if Tessa wanted to hit Markus right now herself, she would not allow Chris to swing. “Stop Markus.” Tessa pleaded this time with rounded eyes nearing tears. “Admit that I’m right then. Let Chris know how things really are. He needs to hear it.” “You son of a bitch!”Unable to control himself any longer Chris lunged forward again, his arm rapidly flailing, hoping to catch Markus this time. This time Tessa had no choice but to stand back and chance getting hit as mayhem broke out between the former duo of self proclaimed awesomeness. Thankfully, for the sake of them, Tessa, and everyone else around, Markus, albeit drunkenly was able to back Chris into a pole and keep him from swinging again. What kept him from swinging back at Chris was obvious when Markus looked back at Tessa with a haughty little smirk. “Just tell me I’m right and I’ll go home.” “Neither of you are, now please stop!” Contrary to what Tessa figured he’d do now, Markus pried for her acknowledgement one last time with a different approach, “I know you really don’t want to dip into that checking account of yours again to bail this bastard out of jail.” “You’ll go too.” “And what do I care? “ Markus kept a tight grip on Storm shoulders as he stared him in the eyes, “Him on the other hand. It’d break his heart to pieces to know you’re oh so disappointed with the way he’s acting.”His grip on Storm relinquished, hoping that’s obviously the case, but not without rubbing the salt in the open wound. “Then again, knowing Chris as well as I do, he’ll likely put his precious macho persona in front of the family again for the sake of his own pride, right Chris?” “Fuck you!” Chris spat back, not wanting to prove a single one of Stone’s twisted theories correct. “I bet you Tessa would. Wouldn’t that be fun?” Stone teased, disregarding Tessa’s presence all together. Nothing would have been greater for Stone than to walk away proving Chris and Tessa wrong. Instead of choosing to be offended, Tessa plead with Markus again, “Why are you acting like this?” “I’ve got a point to prove Tess, and Chris right here is doing it well.” Again the circle of pleas began. “Stop Markus!” “Then admit I’m right.” “You’re not!” His glare darted back to Storm, who stood ready to pounce at any given moment, “You must really be attached to this egotistical piece of shit then. To think, you’re about to have to bail his ass out of jail for the hundredth time when he’s done absolutely nothing for you.” “That’s not true Markus! You know it!” “Then tell me just one thing he’s ever done for you. One simple little thing and I’ll leave you both alone.” Tessa was ashamed of the weak answer she had come up with but voiced it nonetheless. “He loves me.” “Love?” This got a rise out of Markus, whose drunken self almost stumbled over in laughter, “He’s turned his back on you how many times? And when does he visit the kids without you having to beg? If he loved you, he’d stop getting himself into trouble. Besides, when’s the last time he’s told you he loved you back?” “You’re a fucking dead man.” Chris growled. Had Tessa not been watching him the entire time Markus rambled, he would have sprung for the kill. His frustration absolutely delighted Markus. “And that’s what I’m talking about! The minute you take your eyes off him he’s going end up in jail. And guess who is gonna have to bail him out? It’s not me anymore. Just think what you could be spending that money on Tess.” Tessa slapped back with her own logic. “If you’re that concerned about my bank account, how about you tuck in your pride, walk away, and go home? Stop trying to get yourself killed.” Witty as he was, Markus immediately had a counter set in place. “What have I got to lose by staying here? The way I see it, you need some serious help Tessa. If you don’t want it, fine. It won’t hurt me any to stick around, you on the other hand…” Noticing from the corner of her eye that Storm was ready to pounce again, Tessa wisely wrapped her petite arms around his waist the best she could to pull him towards the opposite direction, with complete disregard for the words that came out of Stone’s mouth. Immediately, Markus recognized that she had begun to tune him out, “If you leave now Tess, there’s no turning back to brother Marky for assistance. You hear me? I won’t help you again!” It was pointless for Tessa to exchange any further babble with him, so rather than exchange any further word, she waved Markus adieu without exchanging so much as a passing glance. If she were going to take control of her destiny and finally claim her prize, now was the perfect time to turn her back on the past. This time she would ensure her own survival. Markus figured otherwise and was sure to voice it loud enough for both her and Chris to hear, “Just wait. By the time you get home you’ll want my help, I promise you. He’s not going to stay Tess, he never does. All you need to do is say I’m right and surrogate big brother Marky will help you out.” Tessa turned back only to offer her last parting words, “You’re not.” She refused to turn back after that. Now, Tessa could only hope that she and Chris would prove Markus Stone and the rest of the world wrong, once and for all. Because as much as she hated to admit it, Markus was entirely correct in his theory. Without either of them, she’d drown.
