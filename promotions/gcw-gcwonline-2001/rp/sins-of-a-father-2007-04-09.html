Sins of a Father

9 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-9-07 Draven hadn’t slept in days. It would be dawn soon and he was still wide awake. He was out on the balcony with the doors shut so he wouldn’t wake Claudia. Dear sweet Claudia. Some days he wondered why she stayed. He knew why though. She was devastated at finding out who her birth father was. The news of her and Draven sharing a “sperm donor”, as Draven put it, had really fucked up her head. It had messed him up too, but he expected this sort of thing. Nothing in his life had ever been easy and now finding out that the one other human being on earth that he cared for is his own half sister was jus the icing on the cake. “I fucked my own sister.” He let his words pierce the early morning silence. Saying it out loud still didn’t make it any easier to deal with. What was more fucked up still was that Claudia’s adopted father knew about it from the beginning and set them up anyway. It was almost as if the sick fuck was getting off on knowing that his adopted daughter was fucking her own half brother. If he ever seen Ben again, he would personally separate his head from his body. It was no wonder he couldn’t sleep. He couldn’t bring himself to lay in bed with Claudia but he found himself unable to sleep in another room, away from her. What he needed was to get in the ring and make someone bleed. Lucky for him GCW’s High Stakes was coming up and he had himself a match against Kyle James. The stipulations had not been hammered out yet, but he was going to be sure to make sure that it was a violent match, regardless. Violence is what Draven thrives on. They don’t call him “The Innovator of Violence” just to sell tee shirts. Draven has been in some of the most brutal matches of all time. Again and again he has pushed the boundaries of what is acceptable and what is taboo. Surely High Stakes with Kyle James will be no different. A no DQ match inside a casino opens a plethora of possibilities for violence. The sun was starting to rise now. He could see the warm orange hue just over the horizon. The light slowly filling his backyard with it’s warmth and beauty. It was amazing how easily he could forget about everything that was going on when he was in this place. The sound of the water splashing in the big fountain was soothing, and the sounds the Koi made as they bubbled near the surface of the water in search for food. In this place the match with Kyle James at High Stakes seemed a million miles away, but he knew better. Draven knew he had to focus on this match. It was vital that he had a good showing at High Stakes. Draven decided to head downstairs and start some breakfast. Claudia always rose not too long after the sun did. He wasn’t looking forward to looking at her. A feeling he never dreamed he would have, but the situation with her was so confusing and too big of a mess for him to try to wrap his brain around this close to a match where he could very well lose his livelihood or even his life. He slowly walked down the staircase, allowing his hand to ride over the smooth wood banister. He stepped lightly so as not to make the wood steps creak too much. Not that it really mattered. Once he started the coffee, the smell would surely awaken her. He didn’t know much about this Kyle James, only that he was a newcomer to the GCW. He probably thought he would get an easy win over the old man that is Draven Lox. Not likely so, and everybody but Kyle James knows it. An old man Draven may very well be, but sure as hell not an easy win. The coffee was nearly ready now. The smell had already made its way upstairs. He could hear Claudia moving about, and soon the sound of the shower starting. She always took a hot shower and then ate breakfast in the nude. Claudia never used a towel, she would air dry her body. Not that he mind. She was the embodiment of female perfection. He would have a hard time looking at her nude now, though, knowing that she was his half sister. It was a thought that still caused him to shiver down to his very spine. He had a very new sense of aggression towards Ben but, of course, nobody knew where Ben was. So Draven would just have to take all of this aggression out on Kyle James; the very reason he requested this match to be a match with no rules. So when Kyle James is lying in a pool of his own blood, he has Draven Lox’s father in law to thank.
