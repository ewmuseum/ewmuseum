The Adventures of Johnny Heroic: Alive Again

19 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/19 Alright. I think I'm about sick of waiting. I've sat around and I've waited and I've done nothing. I've watched those that I had left past dead and behind with what feels like a hundred years of paper trails now turned to blood stand up and rise to feast on the present. My newest theory is that of the Ill-You-Mini-Nation; a thousand dreams at once in the area between tension and relaxation, the most basic of emotions. Tense, you're angry. Relaxed, you seethe. Tense, you're excited. Relaxed, you're content. Tense, you're vital. Relaxed...you're dead. Riding the wave in between is the fun part. Recently I've found that these blood trails can be traced to every question you ask, but they never answer the most important ones; who and why? Who are you? Why do you do what you do? And the reverberations of emptiness echo endlessly through these halls until my teeth rattle the rattle of the angered viper, waiting to pounce. Just waiting, waiting for the moment when it all can finally meet release. Finally, please finally, let it go into the world. Inner fangs bite, contantly bite their way toward something I fear I cannot heal. They bite with the truth; they are the truth. The truth that rests inside, the truth of myself, the monster in all of us. And its bites are the feelings of punishment for the games we play, the lie we live, thinking we're all kings or queens or bishops or knights or rooks. We. Are. All. Pawns. We keep each other in check. We go so slow. We are the undisputed shit of what we see around us. And, above all, we can only go forward. Forward through an eternity of regression and pain and being made puppets by predators that laugh and jingle our strings and watch us dance. And we dance it because we have to. I'm where the world comes crashing down. Order and Chaos, submission and loyalty, love and hate. They meet above me, us, the shitstains of life, and crash in the heavens and rain down their beautiful, godly death on all who watch. Those of us that just strive to keep on keeping on are the ones that watch in awe and cover from the shock. And I am among them. I am Johnny Heroic. Ohh, and you thought this was my monologue, didn't you? Nope. Just my comic book character. The one that doesn't symbolize anything whatsoever. A while back, I killed the Conclave. Thought so, at least. And I killed myself. Thought so. Turns out I drove Frauleine into a coma. Turns out I rattled The White Knight's brain so that he switched sides. Turns out the universe did something it hadn't done for me in a long time...it answered me. Turns out it had been answering me the whole time, I just hadn't been listening. I was convinced that I lost my powers, that I lost my infinite luck. I thought I couldn't fly and couldn't be super fast and couldn't do whatever I wanted because the universe had forsaken me. Turns out the universe just wanted me to think a little different. I still fought crime. I still didn't die, when I damn well should have. I still got the job done time and time again. And my luck was still infinite, more evident than before because with no powers whatsoever except my two guns, by all means I should have died so many more times. My powers didn't go away. They just changed. Turns out a lot of things. And now? Now everything's simply become so very clear to me. So very evident. The truth. The truth bites at me and I accept it, oh so welcomingly. Because Johnny Heroic is back, and he's back with a vengeance. 'Ladies and gentlemen of The Conclave, we are met to begin...' 'Brothers. Sisters. Allow The Circle to commence...' 'We are in the midst of a war the likes of which this city, this nation, this world has never seen. An entire force, equalling us if not surpassing us in power has risen. And they seek nothing more than our destruction as a flex of their muscle, as a furrow in their brow. They come uninvited. They stay unwanted. They insult and detest and spew their lies and try to turn our orderly, rational city into a veritable wasteland!' 'Those of The Conclave have seen their time. And what have they done with it? They claim a city that has not grown. They defend values and morals that hold no place in modern times. They hold bias against those that are only better than them. It is not our fault that we are what we are, just as it is not their fault that they are what they are. The fault lies in the hate and rage that they hold because we come to better what they cannot.' 'I urge all members of The Conclave to rise against this threat. To rise up and join as we haven't joined before. Sure, we may not trust each other as much as we should. Even I, Fraulein, look around this room and am wary of those seeking what I have and call my own. We have to put ourselves past that and unite against this common foe!' 'Our strength is as much individual as it is in our grouping. The Conclave can't withstand our wrath. They can't ready themselves enough for the tidal wave that is going to crash down upon them and leave them all broken and utterly destroyed. This is the time for The Circle! This is the time for a new order!' They both go on like this for a few more minutes. Opposite sides of the city, opposite sides of the story, same bullshit from both mouths. Fraulein speaks to The Conclave about how they need to stop scheming against each other and pull together in some sort of fake brotherhood against this new force. And Vesuvius preaches to The Circle about how those that are superior do not hold themselves over those that are inferior, but that all are equally fucked under the heel of their boot. Both stories are slavery, just in different fashions. And then there's the handful of superhumans that aren't at either conference. Maybe they're too green around the gills for either side to give a fuck about them. Maybe their minds are just too convoluted and screwed up from whatever latest craze is going around on the streets these days, streets that The Conclave hasn't bothered to try to clean since they got themselves patched back together. Or maybe, like me, they don't want to be a part of the two colliding bodies in the sky. After all, it's going to be cockroaches that survive the nuclear holocaust. Me? You'll find Johnny Heroic perched on his tower, much like I used to be back in the day when I had powers. Kinda like now, but different. I don't know if I can really explain it as well as I'd like. I don't have 'powers,' per se. I just realize what it all is. For instance, I know how both of those speeches are going, even though I'm not near them. And I know that they're happening tonight, and it might as well be mapped out right in front of me right now. Oh, sure, I've probably got a few words wrong. Hell, you can probably insert whole paragraphs that I left out or that I didn't 'know' would be there, but it's so simple to see...I know that they're being said. And they might as well be at the same time. Because I've realized that things like time or distance or senses really, in the end, don't mean anything. Don't follow? It's cool. You're human. Let me map it out to you. I see this old woman walking down the street, so far below me. The rain creates a tunnel vision as I look down and it's like I'm bullseyeing her through a reflective, moving cascade of truth. And right now she's at the bus stop. But I can already see her at the end of the street. She fixes her coat and turns the corner and I can already know it's going to happen before it happens. Not because I see into the future. Because it's already there. The fucking future is just sitting there, staring at me, because it's the present. Think of it as a giant painting. All of time is just a painting. It's all there at once, really. It's just a matter of what part of the painting you're looking at. I took a few steps back that day when the bullet hit my brain. I can see the whole fucking thing. And there's other things. Like, for instance, I don't have to redirect bullets or dodge them anymore. I don't have to stop them or will anything to happen. I know where they're going to be, and they're simply not near me. No force of the mind or quickness of the body, just the fact that I can see the truth that's lying right in front of me. Lying right in front of all of us. In front of The Conclave. In front of The Circle. But they just don't get it. '...punks...' The rain is pounding the back of me hard, very hard, as I stare down to that small speck on the street from this tower so high up. I don't even have to do anything to fall foreward, it's pounding so hard, and I'm airborne, falling toward the earth like a rock. A soft, understanding rock that effortlessly slows himself down and lands on the pavement. How? Bending physics? I don't know what you're talking about. I simply knew that that updraft was going to be there, and I simply knew that my feet would withstand the landing. Just like last week, when the ball of fire encompassed that criminal. I didn't throw anything at him or 'cast' anything near him. I simply knew that the gas main was going to blow, and sure enough it did. It's so simple. And I wish you could understand it. It's just like Tetris. All you have to do...is make the lines. There's nothing else to it. I suppose that saying my life has been kind of dream-like for the past while would be an understatement. But then again, sometimes I do go through the day and wonder what dream I am having. If perhaps any of it means anything at all. And for as much as I know, I do know one thing with clarity. 'The Circle. The Conclave.' They are dangerous. Both of them can spell disaster for everybody in the city, in the nation, in the world. Not because of either bloated beast's power, but because of the recklessness that streaks the grounds when they collide. And the people that are not on either side need to see someone stand and let it be known that that simply will not do. They won't go down today. They may not go down soon. But the force of one independant thought has long been what it takes to overthrow and topple a large, blind, rather dumb machine. I am Johnny Heroic. This is my duty. And this pawn can only go forward from here.
