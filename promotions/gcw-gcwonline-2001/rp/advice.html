Date: Timeless -=Cold, so cold. The wind cuts light a knife, an extremely sharp knife. Mid August and it's well below freezing. It's been this way ever since the O'Neils bought this land years ago. No one quite knows how long ago but its been speculated that the O'Neils have inhabited this land a few years after the end of the Seven Years War in Yorktown, Virginia USA. All of the great O' Neil's are buried on this very site, as well as others. Bryan's crypt has been spoken for, for about 30 years now. He has a nice spot picked out next to his now deceased mistress Dementia of 10 years. The wind blows through the bottom of Bryan's trenchcoat, blasting it open. He quickly snatches it and closes it, buttoning it halfway up. He has his jet black hair back in a ponytail, and you can actually see his face. It's not covered with the paint of a corpse. His eyes are dark and deep, clean shaven, sunken cheeks, thin lips. He looks like his mother. Elizabeth Walker, a hooker in her prime, met Bryan's estranged father, Anton O'Neil, a wealthy farmer and gambler. Anton had a thing for hookers, and eventually impregnanted Elizabeth, and 5 years later impregnated another hooker who's name escaped Anton up to his death 5 years ago. That seed created Jason O Neil known to the world as The Dark Messiah, whom's been missing for quite sometime. Bryan strolls passed his mother's crypt, Anton killed her when Bryan was very little over a monetary dispute. Bryan watched it happen, there was blood everywhere. Anton buried her because he later told Bryan she was a good woman, and deserved the proper death. Bryan still has nightmares about that night. Bryan kneels in front of his mother's gravesight. He misses her, he still wonders what it'd be like if she was still alive, maybe this world would make sense. Mayhem:'Mother, have I disappointed you? You told me once when I was little to use my size and my wrath to my advantage. I have tried, and tried and yet, nothing. I'm old and wearing down mother.' -=Mayhem nods, than nods again, and begins to smile. He can sense her presence, and thinks he can hear her.=- Mayhem:'Yes mother, of course I will. I will do that right now. The answer will come to me.' -=Mayhem walks over towards the site of his lovely mistress Dementia.=- Mayhem:'Mother told me the answer lies right here. So tell me about Michael Savige, tell me about him.' -=A voice booms from the distance.=- Voice:'Bryan they are toying with you. They have not given you the man you desire.' Mayhem:-=looking around=-'Time's up, tell me who you are. For two weeks you've been appearing in my dreams, voices haunting me everywhere I go. Show yourself coward.' Voice-=laughing=- 'Old man, you don't scare me, you use to, but not now. You're old, washed up, you'll never be 'The Lunatic' again Bryan.' Mayhem:'Who's paying you? Someone is paying you to come out here week in and week out and insult me. Who is it? And are they paying you enough, because if I find you, I'll snap your neck in two.' Voice:'Good get angry!! It's about damn time. Jesus Christ Bryan, what the hell happened to you? Remember when you were fired from the PCW because you kidnapped that guys girlfriend, than he tried to hunt him down and you broke both his legs ending his wrestling career? What about climbing to the top of the steal cage with Beulah and Raven's daughter only to drop her from the cage? Too bad Raven caught her, but they both got the beating they deserved. Three matches in that house behind us. Three! Everyone of your opponent's careers ended, you walked out unscathed. Remember the Agents of Oblivion? The most devastating stable of all time. They once held the World, US, Tag, and Hardcore titles all at one time. Remember your death and Resurrection in EHFW? We can go on and on, Bryan. But now, now you lose to a third rate champion, for christs sake you've wrestled bigger, better, and badder. Than you let some imbecile named X steal your title? You just let him walk right in and beat you like that. 5 Years ago you would have set him on fire or something sick and twisted like that. You are a disgrace.' -=Movement in the direction of the voice catches Mayhem's eye. He still can't tell who it is.=- Mayhem:'What do you want?' -=Quick like lightning a hand grabs the throat and comes eye to eye with Mayhem. We can't tell who it is, but Bryan knows exactly.=- Voice:'I want the fucking Lunatic.' -=Mayhem smiles, sadistically.=- Mayhem:'Savige is obsolete...erase....extinct.' Voice:'And X?' -=Mayhem grins.=-
