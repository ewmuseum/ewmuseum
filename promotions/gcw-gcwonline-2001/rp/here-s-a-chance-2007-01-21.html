Here's a chance

21 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1.21.07 [i]Hours after the posting of the World Wide 59 Preliminary card and seemingly more hours after NC-17 went off the air the gossip around the state of Massachusetts was tremendous. No they weren’t talking about the performance of the home town girl, Tessa Windsor did in the US Title Match. Nor was the buzz about another hometown favorite Peter Vetra’s retaining of the Hardcore Title. Instead it was the idea that Pleasure and Pain from Massachusetts own Newton. World Wide 59 brought the chance of gold being brought home, Pleasure and Pain would face off against Fiery Passion who had just won the belts maybe twenty-four hours prior to the releasing of the card. How would Massachusetts? own fair? We will have to wait till Sunday. [/i] Tiara was the first to awake of lovable couple known as Pleasure and Pain. Like usual she let her man sleep as she went to the kitchen to make him something to eat along with something small for herself. Walking down the stairs slowly almost in a tippy toe like manner trying not to make any noise she reached the bottom stair before there was a creek actually heard throughout the house. “Damn it” she whispered on her breath hoping that she was far enough down for her husband not to hear her. She made her way into the kitchen making the pot of coffee so she can function like the rest of the world in the morning. Squinting as her eyes had not adjusted to the sunlight yet she notices the clock on the wall reading, “7:30 am” She whips her eyes after reading it and makes her way to the refrigerator and pours herself a glass of orange juice as she waits for the coffee pot to be ready. The coffee starts to pour into the pot as she pulls out all the ingredients she needs to make breakfast, you know the eggs, sausage, bacon, toast, home fries. Tiara Belle moves around the kitchen with a certain grace making Wayne’s and hers breakfast. She did not know that Wayne had actually slipped down the stairs. He loved his wife ever so much and watching her work in such a graceful manner actually sent chills up his spine. She continued to cook the eggs and the bacon along with everything else but couldn’t help but feel that there were a set of eyes undressing her as she worked. You know how you get that feeling well that is what she was getting right now. Wayne one to pay attention to detail noticed the look in his wife’s eyes and decided to step out from around the corner, as much as he loved watching his wife at work he knew better when he saw that scared look in her eye. “Good Morning baby,” Wayne said with a deep voice. Tiara looked up from the food and smiled at her husband. She was able to put the food down as he had made his way over to her and he put his massive arms around her little fragile frame and gave her a “good morning” hug and kiss. With that she went back over to the stove and started flipping the eggs over as they were almost done. Wayne on the other hand poured them both a cup of coffee and he handed one to her and made his way over to the kitchen table where their lap top laid. Wayne turned it on as he awaited his breakfast. Tiara moved effortless in the kitchen as she fixed their breakfast, while on the other side of the room Wayne was checking the daily email. Tiara notices that he has the lap top open. “Is there anything good baby?” “Not really,” Wayne scrolls through the first page and a half of spam mail. We have everything from selling Viagra to trying to get Wayne’s penis bigger. How do they know how big it is already? Wait that’s a question for another time, he continues down the list. “Well wait a minute?” Tiara sits down at the table as Wayne continues to read the email. “At WW 59 we are facing Fiery Passion for the belts.” With that Tiara’s face started to glow as she hears the news. She places the plate down in front of her husband. “Come Monday we will have gold, come Monday it will be our chance to shine. But for now eat.” With that the pair starts to each their breakfast both equally dreaming of tag team gold..
