Realizing the Truth...

15 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/15/10 After NC-17 Backstage I. Bishop Steele: Fucking bullshit. I have giving everything I could to come out on top in my matches and every time I get let down. Alexis Steele: Sweetie, you got to take to bumps in the road and move on. You can’t let it get to you. Bishop Steele: Are you seriously trying to tell me to let this shit go? When in the hell have you ever come up against situations that you weren’t able to handle? Alexis Steele: You know what Bishop, Fuck off. I can’t believe you would say something like that to me. Bishop Steele: Baby, I didn’t mean it at all, you know that. Alexis Steele: Honestly I really don’t know anymore. Ever since you have been on this downward spiral, your attitude has changed and our marriage has changed. I’m pregnant with your baby and the last time you even told me you loved me or that I was beautiful was so long ago, that I don’t even think you care. You want hard times; try being pregnant and married to a man that doesn’t even show you affection. So you can kiss my ass and find somewhere else to stay, because I can’t do this anymore. Bishop Steele: Sweetie you don’t mean that. Alexis grabs her purse and bag and starts to head out of the locker room. Bishop grabs her by the hand and pleads his case…… Bishop Steele: Please Alexis, I love you so much and you know that and I’m sorry. This has been hard for me these past few months and the last thing I need is for you to leave me at this moment. Alexis Steele: I love you too, but I can see this is something that you need to do on your own. Bishop Steele: Where are you going to be? Alexis Steele: I’m going home to see my family. If you need me, call me. Alexis hugs Bishop and walks out, closing the door as she leaves. Bishop stands there and stares at the door and then down at his hands. All of a sudden a chair flies into the glass mirror and shatters it. Glass goes everywhere. Bishop breathing really hard and in a furious rage, walks over to the mirror or what’s left of it and stands there looking at the broken image he sees in the mirror. The camera fades away…….. Back Where It All Began…. II. The camera flickers on inside the Luxurious Jet of Bishop Steele. Bishop is looking out the window as the jet soars over Denver, Colorado, home of Alexis Steele and Worldwide 127. The city seems all too familiar for Bishop as he looks down at the city. His stewardess walks back to his seat with a bottle of Crystal. Stewardess: We are about to land, but here is your bottle of Crystal and your Maybach will be waiting for you at the hanger. Bishop Steele: Thank you and did you get a hold of my wife? Stewardess: We tried but there was no answer sorry. Bishop Steele: That’s okay; I got a match that I need to focus on anyways. The plane lands and Bishop grabs his bags. The plane door opens letting in the cold brisk air of Denver. Bishop smiles and steps out walking towards his Maybach. He gets in and picks up the phone as the driver heads towards his resort home in the mountains. He dials Alexis’s parent’s home and waits for an answer… Ring….Ring….Ring….. Answering Machine: Hi you have reached the Gunnz residence, we can’t come to the phone at this moment, but leave a message and we will get back to you, bye. Bishop Steele: Hey Mr. and Mrs. Gunnz it’s me Bishop just calling to see how my wife and future con are doing. I guess when you get this I will be at the resort home in the mountains. Talk to you later and I love you guys, bye. Bishop hangs up the phone and reclines back into his seat and relaxes on his way to his house. Falling asleep on his way to the house he abruptly wakes up when the driver comes to a sudden stop. Bishop Steele: What the fuck happened? Driver: Sorry sir they was a wreck and traffic is slow. Bishop Steele: What wreck? Driver: Look out your window sir and you can see it. Bishop looks out the widow only to see a Black Range Rover turned crushed in the front and then a Red Mercedes in the median. Bishop Steele: Stop the fucking car that is my wives Range Rover. Bishop gets out and rushes to the scene. He is stopped by the police on site, but Bishop pushes them out of the way. Once at the ambulance, he looks around and can’t find Alexis. He sees her parents and rushes to them. Bishop Steele: Oh my god what happened? Mary Gunnz: We were headed to the hospital, because Alexis’s water broke and on the way we were hit by that Mercedes going the wrong way. Bishop Steele: Are you guys alright, I mean how is Alexis? Walter Gunnz: They rushed her to the hospital, for delivery. Bishop Steele: I love you guys, but I have to go be with her. Mary Gunnz: We know Bishop and all she could do was think about you. Go be with your family, we will be okay and we will see you at the hospital. Bishop Steele: Okay love you guys. Bishop rushes back over to his Maybach and tells the driver that there is a change of plans and they are going to the hospital. On the way Bishop tries to get a hold of the hospital doctor and gets nowhere. Eventually they arrive at the hospital. The camera fades away…. Fatherhood and a Chance to Make Everything Better…. III. 18 hours later Alexis is finished with labor and giving birth to an 8lb 6oz baby boy, named Jesus Steele. Bishop holds the baby while Alexis sleeps. Bishop Steele: You are so big and beautiful. Never in my mind would I have ever thought that I would be sitting here holding my son. You know when you get older, many people are going to talk about what I have done and my profession outside of my company, but I want you to know that you will not have to suffer the pain that I did growing up. I will always be there for you, never leaving your side. I promise that I will be the best father I can be, vowing to never let you end up like me in any shape, form or fashion. I love you and always will. Alexis wakes up seeing Bishop holding his son and reaches out to hold his hand. Alexis Steele: I love you Bishop and I always will. We love you Bishop and we all know that you are great. Now you have a match to prepare for and I know you want to be here for me and your son, but I also know that business comes first. So go be the best and then come home and be the daddy I know you will be. Bishop kisses Alexis and then kisses Jesus and heads out of the hospital. The camera fades away…. Journal Entry #246 Being the Best…. Fatherhood never in this lifetime would I have ever thought that I would be a father. I guess when you absolutely think about it, millions of other men in this country probably are thinking the same thing. When I saw my son come out of his mother’s womb and into my arms of course after he was cleaned, the feeling of being someone’s inspiration hit me like the first time I stepped into that ring and the fans started cheering my name. It felt great and I wish I could go back to that very day, but I can’t, because that was then and this is now. What the future holds for me now is unclear, but with Jesus in my life now, I can’t worry about anyone else but my family and providing the best life for them. Sure I would love to be a champion one day, but when I think about it. That is not what being the best is all about. Being the best is about overcoming adversity and rising to the occasion that has been placed in front of you. In my wife and sons eyes I will always be the best and at this point in time, that is all I need to stay one step ahead of the game.
