Date: 03.22.09 Wonderful Two wins down, and several more were hopefully on the way for the little woman from Verona. She was unable to talk to her cousin immediately after the match, but young Mindy could only assume that he was pleased to see his cousin get a pinfall over one of his former rivals. If anything, at least it felt good to her to have some sort of positive streak going for herself. Back in her locker room Mindy slid off her wrestling boots to put something more comfortable on her feet. Her top and skirt didn't really bother her and she had one of the on-site trainers remove the tape from her hands and wrists before she returned to her room, leaving her little else to worry about. Her body ached and her skin was bruised in some places, but she was otherwise no worse for wear; nothing a good night's sleep wouldn't fix. Quickly she stuffed her extra belongings into her gym bag, stopping only briefly to check any messages left on her phone. She hadn't expected any, but it was more so just to get rid of any unwarranted voice mails. There had been a couple, but there was one left that stood out from the others. "Hey, Mindy, just caught you on TV again. Give me a call when you get the time, yeah?" Mindy hadn't exactly expected Markus to call her up right away, though she definitely wasn't complaining that he did. She would wait only until she left the arena and got into her plain black rented vehicle before she decided to return his call. The phone rang several times before Markus had picked up. "Heh, that was fast," he joked. Mindy grinned widely at the greeting. "If ya really watched me tonight ye'd know tha' I'm all about bein' quick an' crafty." "Yeah yeah. Michael was an exception. None of the girls out near the street had paid any attention to the fight; hell, with the odds being four on one, who would've? Naturally they were surprised to find a bloodied up American was the last man standing, and not just standing, but standing with his hands on his hips and a smirk on his bruised face. "So, you ladies still wanted to fuck, huh?" "Yay, we won!" Mindy bounced happily up and down, causing Markus to laugh and reach for his drink. Mindy eventually did the same and they tapped their glasses together. "You can be my wing man anytime," Markus said happily. In the cutest way one could possibly swear, Mindy replied, "Bullshit, you can be mine!" They both took a drink, and then gave each other a hug as the STAGE CLEAR logo popped up on the screen. That was one level down, but there were three more to go. Each one always harder than the last.
