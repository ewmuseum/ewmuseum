Feels Like the First Time All Over Again...

23 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23Feb2007 Know that sore feeling you get after a workout? The next day when it's difficult to even move? Multiply that by a hundred or so, and you'll know what Markus Stone's knee feels like. And nothing about the sunny skies of Oak Harbor can take that away. However, much like the pleasant contentment one has with sore muscles, Markus does not seem to mind terribly much. He is, in fact, attempting to enjoy a day of relaxation. Windows open, sun pouring in, and a beer in his hand. He must look almost normal. "Been a while..." Well, five years, actually, but who's counting? Okay, okay, Markus is. Every single day gets it's own special place for him to store it away in his memory. Worldwide? Another special memory. Well, three if you're counting, and we know that you are. One, Markus won his debut match in GCW. That alone would be enough to satisfy any normal person. However, Markus realizes that to springboard to the position that he wants to be in, an impact was needed. And in column number two, we have Scott Nielson. Well, when you start speaking of impacts, you only have to ask Mr. Nielson if you are curious as to what kind of impact Markus can leave. What a night for Markus. Oh, wait, three, right? Yes, one more... The reunion of Banned and Exiled. Markus Stone and Chris Storm. What a team, what a team. These two once very nearly became the PTC Tag Team champions, until personal issues got in the way of Markus' participation. Another time, and they almost ran rampant over the DWO, until Markus' knee gave out. Seems like that has been a bit of a pattern with Markus. This time, however... This time is different. This time Markus has no personal issues. This time Markus has no hope of further repairing his knee. This time Markus Stone and Chris Storm will become what they always should have been. Legendary. This brings a smile to Markus' face. Don't know why you're so happy, you would've lost if it weren't for him. And of course, the Voice quickly makes an appearance to rain on his parade. "C'mon now, it's not like it ruined anything. I think it turned out to be a pretty good thing, actually." Markus seems content with this logic, and as per the norm, has no difficulty talking to a Voice in his head. "Just enjoy the day." Markus smiles. A smile. Very rare coming from this man, and more often than not, lacking in sincerity. Oh no... No, no, no. You can't tell me that you're happy? Actually happy? "No." Markus thinks for a moment afterwards. "But I am trying." This sends the voice into a rage. Bad enough that Markus actually resorted to accepting help, but from the same crutch he has leaned on for so long? Markus understands the reasoning behind His anger before He even speaks. What the hell did you accomplish? Nothing of significance, nothing to make Markus Stone stand out taller than anyone else. Nothing, not a damn thing! The image of UNITE tumbling down the entranceway flashes in Markus' mind. "You sure about that?" Of course I'm sure! What did that accomplish for you, for Markus Fucking Stone? Nothing, nothing at all save but to make more enemies than you are prepared to deal with. This isn't a convention, you know! We can't just invite every single Johnny Come Lately that wants our counseling along for a ride. We should have dealt with Scott Nielson first, before--- "Before what?" Of course, this pesky Voice is quite adept at ruining Markus' mood, and is currently succeeding. "Oh, I get it. You don't want me to play with Chris Storm, is that it? Well, maybe if you would have had my back six years ago, I wouldn't have to. I owe this guy, I owe him everything. He's been the only person who I could ever call a friend, ever. And he comes to GCW and you expect me to turn my back on him?" Markus is quite offended right now, and if it were possible, he'd kick the Voice out of his apartment. But it isn't. Possible, that is. Then again you probably know that. Or maybe you don't. Trust me, you can't just kick a Voice out. No, you fool. I'm saying you shouldn't try and fight everybody at once. How are we going to winnow out the weak when they all band together against us? A shimmer of logic finally makes it's appearance. What could Markus really expect to accomplish when every one would be standing in his way? However, friendships are few and far between, and therefore take precedence. "You won't sway me, not on this. Just run with it." Just run with it. How many times had Markus been told to do the same? How many times had he allowed himself to be taken advantage of by others, all in the course of 'running with it?' Jason Chae, former President of WIW. His ideas included a corporate club, which he invited Markus into. This sparked the Revolution from Markus, along with a string of other bad ideas. For a time, Markus was a joke in WIW. He overcame. He went from being a main event wrestler who needed to 'pay his dues before winning a title' to a Hall of Famer. And through the entire ordeal, Jason Chae used him. Callisto Bane, former President of DWO. Her intention for signing Markus in the first place was to attempt to lure Chris Storm over. It worked, and Markus was quickly forgotten when he had to have his knee repaired. Anthony Starr, former friend, former wrestler. Used his friendship with Markus to gain entry into the WIW. Once there, he quickly forgot who his friends really were. Karma got him, in the form of a broken neck by Chris Storm. Chris Storm, friend. Always been there for Markus. Always helped. Always had a plan. What's the plan this time around? You don't even know what you're doing, do you? Does he? "Yes, I'm doing what is required of me. Loyalty may not be such a meaningful idea to you, but it means something to me." Okay then, genius, tell me something. What are we going to do with Scott Nielson? "Pain. We will hurt this man. Scotty can't possibly be prepared for what I've got in store for him, nobody would be." Markus smiles at the thought running through his head. Of course he knows what he's doing. At least, with Scott he does. Five years ago, Markus would have been disgusted with the thoughts running through his head right now. He would have gone out of his way to hurt a man planning what Markus is going to do. Now, a smile instead. "This man thinks he's untouchable...." Markus' cell phone ring, interrupting him. Yes, Markus Stone now owns a cellular phone. A perk of being back in the business. Looking at the number, Markus doesn't have a clue who is calling him. But when has that stopped anybody from answering their phone? "Hello? Yeah, that's me... Oh, I see, I see... Yeah, I think I could do that for you... Right now? Alright, shoot." An interview? It's been some time since Markus gave one of these. Well, time to brush up, right? "Where have I been? Where have you been? I've been extremely active, wrestling in Japan, Mexico, and a little bit in Canada. Oh, no, not really. I've been rehabbing my fucking leg you ignorant prick! It's because of people like you that I live in a shithole, you know that? People like you who could give two shits about us wrestlers when we aren't risking our necks for you every single night." What an imbecile. Everybody who's somebody knows by now the tragic tale of Markus Stone. This guy must be a nobody. "What? Are you kidding me? Yes, I blame everyone. It's not my fault. I wasn't the one saying 'Go for it Markus.' I wasn't the one saying 'Markus, you can do it, just keep trying.' And I sure as hell wasn't telling myself 'Get in that ring every single night and just ignore that nagging pain that all these vicadin, loritabs, and percocet don't do a damned thing for!'" Well, in truth, it was, but how can you always be right if you blame yourself? "Oh, trying to change the subject so soon? Well, to be quite honest, I'm not sure if I should tell you this or not. What the hell..." NOOOO!!!! Don't tell him a damned thing, not one word! If you do, then the whole world will know, which includes Scott Nielson you moron! "On second thought, I really can't tell you what I have planned for GCW. Just keep your eyes open. This is a new breed of Markus Stone. Stronger, smarter, and altogether superior." Well, mostly. The old Markus was faster, more wreckless, and more caring. For the most part, a poor combination. Sure, Markus can't sail through the air anymore, can't jump outside the ring, or even pull off a dropkick, but he makes up for it. Stronger and smarter. What more does he need? "Chris Storm? Of course we're still friends. No matter what happens, we retain that friendship. Yes, I know he interfered in my matchup, but that has opened the door to many more possibilities for myself. Of course I was surprised. The entire world was shocked. Who didn't have to use their hand to push their mouth closed when they saw him back in the ring? Now, where will this lead? I'm afraid I'm not too sure about that, but whatever the outcome, I shall be tall atop every victim I happen to face, including Scott Nielson." Poor, poor Scotty. Wonder what he was going to say before Markus knocked him out? Perhaps a witty comeback? Maybe he was about to flip his lid? Maybe Scotty didn't have a single word to say, because deep down he should have known that Markus was right. Markus is always right when it comes to judging people. At least, he thinks so. "Yeah, it was pretty cool to watch, wasn't it? One minute I'm talking, the next I'm knocking people's heads off. I think I actually surprised myself there, let alone poor Scotty. Do you think he's feeling any better now? It's been a few days. No, I really don't care what you think. You see, if I cared what anybody thought, I wouldn't be doing things like that to people like Scott fucking Nielson, now would I? Instead, I play the damned puppet, letting everybody else pull my strings to make some merry fucking show about how I can triumph through all my tribulations and still have a fucking smile on my face, right? Of course I'm right. However, comma, I decided some time ago that I'd start pulling the strings. I made it through my tribulations, my struggles, and instead of a smile I've grown a killer instinct. No, I'm not out to hurt anybody. I'm going to fix them. Scotty has a problem, and I will rectify said problem." Ooh, that's a good one. Markus Stone, the Rectifier? Well... Maybe not. "Well, for starters he thinks he's so fucking untouchable. The guy called himself The Un-fucking-touchable for Christ's sake! No, the 'fucking' part was my addition you nitwit. This prima donna fuck wants to step into a wrestling ring, a ring that people like myself have laid their very being on the line for a chance to succeed, and he dares to think that he is untouchable? Does he have some kind of fucking God Mode code that everybody else in the world doesn't know about? No, this guy has a fucking ego. And I'm going to take it away. I'm going to show Scotty that he is really a very vulnerable person, and then we'll just watch and see if he can survive the trials that I lay before him, or not." Everybody has a weakness, even the great Markus Stone. What makes Scott Nielson think he's any different? Well, it doesn't really matter, now does it? That thought process is about to be shaken, and drastically changed, and ultimately discarded. "No, I'm not trying to hurt him. Did you not hear what I said? I'm fixing him. I am going to help evolve poor Scotty, and when we are done, if he is strong enough to survive, he will be a better man for it. If he can't, then he isn't worthy of what he has right now anyways, now is he?" Hang up. Hang up? But this is his first interview in years. Markus is actually enjoying himself. However, he complies. "Look man, that was it. No more questions, just watch the shows. You'll see enough of me soon enough, and you'll get the answers to all of your questions." Markus hangs the phone up. Felt like the old days, back when he was still a star. Wrestling journalists used to try pretty hard to catch an interview with Markus Stone, moreso when he called himself Zero. Things are looking up indeed. But not for Scotty. "Not for Scotty." No, certainly not for Scott Nielson. Dark days are looming for that man. Dark days indeed.
