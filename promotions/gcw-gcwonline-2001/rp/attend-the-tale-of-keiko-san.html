Date: Three Days after NC-17 Attend the Tale of Keiko-San *Credit goes to the infamous musical, Sweeny Todd, The Demon Barber of Fleet Street and the following songs from it: The Ballad of Sweeny Todd, Epiphany, A Little Priest, and God, That’s Good. Lyrics were changed to fit this roleplay of course. *The following roleplay is a parody based on this musical and in no ways reflects the true natures of anyone on the GCW roster, or for that matter the Knightmare Brigade. This is intended with good clean fun. Keiko lied in her bed in Oahu, Hawaii, worried to death. She hadn’t heard from Hope since she left for Germany. So taking her mind off of things, she decided to put in a musical that Hope got her interested in called Sweeny Todd: The Demon Barber of Fleet Street. However, after the first song, Keiko was slowly drifting off into a deep slumber. ***************************************************************** In the dark void, Keiko stood there wearing a long black barber’s coat.
