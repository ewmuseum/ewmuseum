A Glimmer of Hope

7 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/7/2005 ( For Archive purposes only. This is my Regal Rumble Rp for Eva in UWF) ' Five more Eva. Do we have room?' Eva sat back on her haunches and wiped a dirty forearm across her sweaty forehead before turning to look at Mist with a grim determination. ' We will make room, Mist. We have too.' Eva had surprised Mist when she announced her intentions to go to the aid of those caught in Katrina. Everyone in the room when she announced it had thought she meant she was going to just send money. But Eva never did anything half assed. She had helped Johnny Lexicon fund the pumps to help pull water from the cities and even told him whatever he needed she would pay for. When he had simply asked her to come as well she agreed but made it known she needed supplies first. When she had arrived just west of Baton Rouge and climbed off the private plane she almost turned back. The smell; she had never caught anything like it before. No sooner had her feet touched the frail ground than she was covering her mouth fighting the urge to vomit. That had been two days ago. The small boy whose wound she had been checking and rebandaging smiled bravely at her and whispered. ' You must be an angel.' It was enough to bring tears to her eyes. Eva had been called many things..bitch, slut, whore, vixen, but never an angel. Holding back the tears she offered him a sad smile in return leaning over to kiss that brow before getting to her feet to go help Mist with the new arrivals. Not once did she think to turn any of them away despite the size and limitation of the camp she had established. She had bought the supplies from several army surplus stores to create a camp fit to hold one hundred people. They were already at one hundred and twenty, thirty of which were children waiting on lost parents or who had parents no longer living. Eva spent most of her time with them, reading stories, tending wounds and broken limbs and making sure they all were getting properly fed and clothed. They were the ones who suffered the most in all of this chaos. Looking haggard and not herself the GCW diva headed off to assist Mist in making sure the new refugees were as comfortable as they could be all things considering. As she passed the mess tent one of her volunteers called out to her in concern. ' Eva you are looking exhausted. Why don't you take a break and rest. Eating wouldn't hurt either.' She steadfastly shook her head and offered a small tired smile to the young woman. ' How can I rest? There are so many others here who need my help. I will rest once I know they are all safe.' ' At least have some water?' With a heaved breath she shook her head in agreement and made the small detour to the table taking the offer bottled water. ' Thanks.' ' No Eva..thank you.' Eva wanted to say something in return but in all honesty she just couldn't. Merely nodding quietly she lifted the bottle in gesture of thanks and headed back to her original destination. Rounding the large common tent she could already hear the quiet sobs of a child within. It was like a dagger to the heart. Composing herself for the scene she had met so many times already in these past few days she entered within and stopped dead in her tracks at the sight of those who sat huddled on the floor. In the middle of them was a young girl likely no more than six by appearances. The child was covered in blood, bruises and mud. Immediately tears welled up in the back of her icy eyes. Why was it always the children who suffered most? Mist looked up from what she was doing with an elderly lady and sighed offering her friend an encouraging smile. Eva didn't even look at her. Those eyes were set upon the little girl. Before Eva's brain could engage with what her body was doing she was striding towards the child and bending down to scoop her up into tender arms. ' Shhh, everything will be alright. I promise you that.' ' I...w..w..ant my mommy back.' Eva cradled that babe's head to her shoulder and chest and looked at Mist with the question in her eyes. Mist merely shook her head. The mother was dead. Eva's heart sank. Nodding she kissed the muddy head of her refugee. ' Mist, if you have everything under control here. I am going to take...' She whispered to the little girl. ' What is your name?' ' Hope.' How ironic her name was. Hope. The one thing many of these people had lost in the wake of Katrina's destruction. Something she had hoped to restore. ' I am going to take Hope to the children's tent and get her cleaned up fed and in bed.' Mist nodded as Eva whirled cradled her package in her arms heading back to the children's tent. Once inside she settled Hope on one of the many cots and reached for a small plastic tub of clean water and a white wash cloth. Dipping it into the tub she began to gently and diligently clean Hope's face and visible body parts. By the time she was done the water was a dark brown. Pushing it away she called for more water and one of the curtain racks for Hope's privacy as she set about the next phase of her same process over the last few days. Hope's clothes would carefully be removed and tossed in the 'burn' basket as fresh clothes that were a little too big for her were brought to replace them. Once again Eva was bathing the girl humming a soothing tone under her breath in hopes to soothing the still semi weeping babe. Two hours would go by before Eva would finally be climbing off that cot leaving a sleeping Hope to rest for the night. Pulling the blanket up to tuck her in neatly Eva found herself brushing aside a stray lock of ebony hair and smiling. Hope was a beautiful babe and she was without a parent now. Sighing with a weariness she had never known before she slips outside and stares up at the moon pensively. She was helping all these babes now, but what would happen to them once she left? Where would they go? Orphanages and shelters already being overrun with other children just like them? Turning back to peer inside the lantern lit tent she grimmaced and shook her head. No. It wasn't good enough for her. Dropping the flap she marched back through camp seeking out Mist. ' Mist.' ' Eva you should rest. You are going to make yourself sick.' Eva waved her off. ' Never mind that. What we are doing now. It just doesn't seem enough. I want to do more.' Mist nodded and sighed her own shoulders sagging. ' What more can you do Eva? You have donated a million dollars. You have bought supplies, made a refugee camp and taken in more than we can handle honestly. What else can you do?' ' I can turn my estate in Dallas into a home for orphans.' Mist just stared at her in shock. She was stunned. Finally she blink and shook her head as if to clear the cobwebs and smiled at Eva. ' That is a huge responsiblity Eva. You will need to get licensed. Make sure you pass inspections, the government's approval and lord knows what else.' ' I don't care Mist. Whatever it takes. Make it happen. That estate can house at least sixty children. And if it means I know they are safe and well cared for, then that is what I will do. I will fund it myself. Hire all the most qualified and best people to run it. No expense will be spared. Just...do it.' When Eva's words died down she looked up at Mist and arched a brow. Mist was grinning. That smile was so large it practically split her face. ' Why are you smiling at me like that?' ' There is hope yet for you Eva.' Eva smiled as her thoughts went back to the little one nestled asleep on that cot. ' Yes that is Hope and it comes in the form of a small girl.' ' So what made you decide this Eva?' ' Hope did Mist...Hope did.' Mist nodded as Eva turned away heading back to the children's tent once more. As Mist stared at her retreating friend her eyes were filled with a glimmer of something almost familiar. A glimmer of hope.
