We're Taking Over This Town

20 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-20-08 They haven’t been off the plane for more than ten minutes before the Cowboys From Hell are located by GCW cameras. This was the first time in months that they’ve set foot on American soil to wrestle. Months had gone by as the two FUSE Fatal Faction Champions waited for their contracts to expire. Now they’ve returned to the United States and are ready to take on another challenge. They are now in GCW, and they know that there is only going to be one thing that are going to make them happy. They walk through the airport, displaying on their shoulders the Tag Team Gold that they left the United States with. Eventually, they will have their chance to unify their belts with those of GCW, the only reason they’ve returned to the United States. But they knew that they couldn’t over look their first opponents. Samson and Delylah, a team formerly of Global Wrestling. Just like the Cowboys From Hell, they were making their debuts this week. Didn’t matter to the Cowboys From Hell who they were going to debut in GCW against. It was just another match in the life of Jake Ennis and Wyatt Colton. “Jake, when is Araceli going to meet up with us?” Araceli Negra didn’t travel with the team on their last trip to Japan. Instead, she stayed in the United States, finalizing the contracts to bring her charges to Global Championship Wrestling. Jake just continues to walk through the airport, without answering his partner’s question. Jake keeps his focus on what he knows is ahead for the two of them. He knows that in order to re-make their names in the United States, the Cowboys From Hell were going to have to go into the ring and beat Samson and Delylah. They finally reach the exit of the airport to see the tell tale ride. The 2007 Black GMC Topkick truck that has taken the two of them from arena to arena. “Well, boys, what do you say I give you a lift to the hotel?” Araceli was hanging out from the passenger’s side window as the one time FUSE Tag Team tosses their bags into the back and their belts into the cab of the truck. “So, baby, how was Japan?” Araceli asked as she planted a kiss on the lips of Ennis. “Now, you stayed out of trouble.” Araceli rubbed her right hand over Jake’s stubble lined jaw. “I did my job, that’s all.” Ten matches in twelve days, it was hard not to say that he didn’t have time for anything else. He didn’t even have time to shave before he hopped on the plane to return to the States. “So, no love for me?” Wyatt interrupts. “You got your girl up in Canada.” Araceli retorts. “Oh, yeah.” “Besides, Wy, you don’t want none of me.” Wyatt gets into the back of the truck and sits back, placing his hands behind his head. “Just can’t wait to get back to Calgary.” Jake crosses in front of the truck and gets in the driver’s side. The camera switches to a lipstick camera that’s mounted inside of the truck. Jake puts the truck into gear and pulls away from the curb. “Jake, honey, did you hear about your opponents this week?” “Samson and Delylah. Heard of them. Former wrestlers in Global. Not all that impressed.” Araceli looks right into the camera, shaking her head. “Jake, hasn’t your recent trip taught you not to underestimate any opponents?” Jake gets a chuckle, “Ara, I’m not underestimating them. I’m just not impressed with them yet. Did they defeat the Alliance” No. Did they defeat the Affliction? No. We did. There wasn’t a team in FUSE that we didn’t face that we didn’t have a good match with.” “Jake, we aren’t in FUSE anymore.” Wyatt interjects. “I know; we’re now in GCW. Looks like the guys that have the targets on their backs is this team called Banned & Exiled~! A couple of world class jackasses.” Jake doesn’t take his eyes from the road. “Jackasses?” “Jackasses. The both of them. Talented jackasses, but jackasses none the less.” Araceli shakes her head at the conversation. “Thinking ahead of yourselves” You two still have to defeat Samson and Delylah first.” “Do we really have to talk about them?” Wyatt asks from the backseat of the truck. “Yes, we have to talk about them. They are your next opponents, your first in the United States in how long?” “Too long.” Jake responds. “Way too fucking long. Samson and Delylah aren’t going to know what hit them when we’re done with them.” “JAKE! Stop taking the two of them too lightly.” Araceli says “Jake, I bet they’re taking us lightly.” Wyatt retorts. “I’m sure they are.” Jake responds. Araceli shakes her head at her tag team. “Sorry, but what do they know about us” Nothing really. They probably haven’t followed our careers. They don’t know nothing about us. They are going to come to the arena, and they think that we’re going to be just a pair of backwoods bumpkins that came from another organization.” “Well, aren’t we?” “Not likely. We can be sophisticated when we want to be. But GCW needs to be put on notice. We’re taking over this town.”
