Getting Even, Part C

10 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/10/11 CHAPTERXIII 2 Days before WorldWide 142 “Sleep tight,” Danny slides the Glock under the pillow. “I’ll be back before you know it.” In that minute, Lacy’s soft and somewhat pure smile melts Danny’s cool demeanor. He runs a hand through her soft brown hair very much the same way a protective brother would his sister. She looks up at him with heavy eyelids as he heads for the door. She reaches out weakly, “Be careful, okay?” “I’m just goin’ for chow, babe,” Danny shrugs it off. “You just get some rest.” He takes her hand and folds it back into her chest. She finally closes her eyes, gripping his hand tight and sends Danny’s face wrinkling and crinkling with confusion. He finally gives in and kneels down to the bedside, running his hand through her hair even more, much like scratching a dog’s ear – she’s out in less than a minute. His tongue slowly creeps out of his mouth as he slides his hand free with a surgeon’s skill, “Goodnight, angel.” Standing upright, Danny picks up his jacket and heads for the door. Just as he reaches it, he notices something inside his jacket. He fumbles around the pockets before a thick, heavy thud stops his clamoring. The cash. He turns back around, making sure Lacy’s out cold, and then scouts out the room for a safe place to keep his leverage. If nobody knows but him, the Trick might be willing to play fair for the first part of the game. With such a nasty taste for blood, the Trick will likely be on his trail soon and every wasting minute gives him better odds at finding him. Danny grips the knob of the door and turns back one more time to check on his sleeping beauty. Sleep tight, kid. He pulls the door open and slips through with ease. As he softly shuts it behind him, he slides the leather jacket on before looking up at the eyehole. He looks at it for a long time, too long in terms of getting things done. “She’ll be alright,” Danny tries to reassure himself. The words aren’t true or believable but they are words he needs to say in order to get the fuck on the road and pull his head out of the romance novel that is his life. Pulling his foot off the ground, he moves it down the hall and takes the first step in sorting out the rubble from the Windsor job. CHAPTERXIV The Trick steps out of the black Mercedes sedan. He buttons his white suit jacket and scans the landing strip outside Windsor through his thick sunglasses. He lets his cane hit the gravel as he takes confident strides toward the group of men wearing business dress across the way. Just as the Trick nears the front of the black Escalade, the back door opens and Big C steps out. The two clutch hands and embrace with a single-arm hug. “My friend,” the Trick smiles. “How’s the head?” Big C touches the swollen and obviously gaping wound on his forehead and hisses at the pain. He shakes his head, shrugging a bit and throws his hands up. “I’ll live Tricksta,” Big C waves his hand to the car behind his. “I wish I could say tha same about ya boy Roach.” The Trick glances to the car before snapping his gaze at Big C. The two share a stern exchange of silence, throwing any chance that this is a joke out the window. The Trick slowly steps to the back of the car as Big C motions to pop it open. “Jesus,” the Trick turns away, nauseously grabbing his stomach. “Who did that!?” Big C strolls to the back and looks in the trunk at the blond with a hole in his head. He looks on in silence, thoughts running through his head that seem to paralyze him with venom, the venom that comes from the notion that this could have been him. That same venom is the very reason he can’t turn away. The Trick slaps the big man’s shoulder under the shining Ontario sunlight. “Well?” the Trick grows impatient. Big C doesn’t look up, “Francisco.” “Are you fucking-THIS…” the Trick steps in front of Big C’s line of sight. “THIS was Francisco?” “It gets better…” Big C slowly turns his head toward the Trick. “He let that one live.” The Trick lowers his brow, unsure of what’s going on, ‘Where is he?” Big C breaks eye contact and nods to one of his crew. They pop open the back door of the Escalade and reveal Cesar. Bruises cover his face and eyes are swollen shut, much like his mouth. The Trick shuffles over to Cesar’s side and he rests a hand on the beaten man’s thigh. “Cesar,” the Trick whispers. “It’s me, kid.” Cesar lifts his head back and tries to smile, “Hey Boss. How ya doin?” The Trick looks up, trying to play it off and act upbeat for Cesar’s sake, but can’t. He stops before he starts, a slow growl coming out of his mouth, before he snaps his gaze back to Big C. “I thought you had this under control?” the Trick slams his flat hand on the side of the Cadillac. “My fuckin’ guy can’t even see!” Big C shakes his head and folds his arms. He looks out at the flat land all around. The luxury of remote locations is that they give you a lot to look at when you need something to occupy the eyes. Big C watches heat roll off the ground, “I think you picked tha wrong cat to clip, Trick.” “What was that, Cecil?” the Trick spins around. “Did you really just say that to me?” Big C keeps his eyes from the Trick’s. Like a little kid, angry at the world, there is no getting through to the Trick. Not since he fell flat on his back after being on top. Not after he lost everything because of Danny Francisco. “I spent years building that kid’s future,” the Trick snarls. “I paid for every single dinner, every single gift, and every single TV spot that kid ever had. I gave him the chance at a bright future, at becoming a legend. How do I get repaid?” The Trick holds up his left hand and removes his black glove to show that his index finger and ring finger are gone. He bends the remaining appendages and gives off a half-twisted smile. “I take the fall from the Firm for throwing away money…” he points back at Roach with his mangled hand. “They take two of my fingers, they stab me in the back and cut me out, and now.” The Trick looks back to Big C with teary eyes, “Now my boy is dead.” Big C can’t help but realize that Roach and the Trick look a lot alike and it finally hits him that they’re bound in blood. He shakes his head off and pulls out a smoke. He lights it, taking the edge off of the situation, and exhales. “I know where he’s goin’,” Big C says softly. The Trick refuses to look up from Roach’s body, “Cleveland.” “I figured you knew that,” Big C nods. “But I know somethin’ else.” That causes the Trick to lift his head and watery eyes, “Which is?” “Where he’ll be on December 29th.” CHAPTERXV Danny’s pocket vibrates and he pulls out the flip phone. The caller ID reads ‘Double Tree Hotel’ and is enough to warrant a smile from Buffalo’s Bad Boy. He takes a sip from his beer before flipping it open. “I was beginning to wonder when I’d hear from you, “ Danny toys. “I was just callin’ to wish ya luck hon’,” the other end comes to life with Lacy’s sweet, southern voice. “When do ya go on? I’m watchin’ right now!” Danny shrugs, glancing around the locker room inside the Quicken Loans Arena before answering. He watches things go on behind the scenes but stops looking around as Sally Ford strolls by and shoot him another dazzling smile. He purses his lips, blowing her a kiss and sends her heading toward the ladies’ room out of lust or disgust. “I go on after your bed time sweetheart,” he slides off his cowboy boots and sets them into his locker. “The big boys stay up late in this business.” She laughs, “Oh please, you’re such a punk. I did see a picture of that guy you’re fightin’ tonight, you sure you don’t need me down there?” They both laugh and Danny knows he wants her there but this is no place for a lady, unless that lady happens to be Vivica Valentine. Danny fumbles with the phone as he peels his t-shirt over his head. “I don’t think I’d be able to concentrate on the match if you were,” Danny’s common response to a woman wanting to watch him do his thing in the ring. “Speakin’ of which, how’s your head?” She pauses, “Meh, it’s okay. I got a little light headed when I was in the shower. Do you think it’s serious?” Danny slides his singlet on but lets his straps hand down around his waist, “I’m not sure. I’ve got a friend in town we can go see tomorrow. Hopefully your just slightly concussed and he can give ya somethin’ to help with the side affects.” “Oh my god! Oh my god!” Lacy becomes frantic. Danny sours his face, “What the Hell?” Nothing comes from the other end of the phone. Danny can’t help but laugh at the thought of a woman hanging up the phone on him, of all people. He laughs again. “Lacy?” he waits silently and listens to the nothing from the other end. He stands upright, “Lacy!? LACY!” The rustling of sheets on the other end is all that Danny can hear. The chill of bad things runs down his spine just as he sits back down, trying to run through what’s happening. “You’re on TV!” she screams into the phone. “you are the first person I know that’s ever been on TV! Well, except my neighbor but he was only there because he was talkin’ ‘bout how he beat up his wife ‘n kids. But you’re the first person that I know on TV because they wanna be!” Danny’s heart falls back in line and begins to beat once again. Slowly it pumps blood to the rest of his body as he leans back against the locker and begins to catch his breath. He picks up the nearly empty bottle of beer just as the locker room door opens. “Dynasty,” the techie intern barks. “Gorilla position in five.” With the bottle draining into Danny’s throat, he locks eyes with the little ginger who holds the clipboard. The kid shakes his head, sick of how these wrestlers live, and Danny does the same, sick of how these little nerds judge them with every glance. “Got it Poindexter,” Danny tosses the bottle at the door. “Now get lost!” “Who lost?” Lacy chimes in. “Nobody,” Danny stands up. “And certainly not me. But I’e got to get to the ring now, Lace. Enjoy the show.” She giggles, “Oh, I will.” “Remember…” he almost forgot. “Two knocks, one slap, three knocks.” “Got it,” she’s confident. “Two knocks. One slap. Three knocks.” Danny nods his head as if she could see it, “Good girl.” “We’re not safe yet are we?” she says in a sweet Southern melody. Danny looks into the future, “We just gotta ride it out. I’ll be back later and then we’ll figure out the next move.” She smiles and even though it’s over the phone, he can feel it warm his heart a little bit each time. Her lips part with a soft smack, “Good luck, sug’.” The sound of plastic smacking plastic ends the phone call and Danny tosses the phone into his gym bag. He slips the straps over his shoulders and slides on his traditional entrance ramp shades. Pulling the door open, Danny steps out into the hallway for a meeting with the spiritualistic Shaman. Lacy lays back against the headboard but hears her stomach rumble. With weary eyes, unsure of the next time she’d see Danny, she looks around the room for a solution to her hunger problems. Picking up a takeout booklet on the desk, she opens it. Her eyes burst when she spots the Chinese takeout menu. She waits patiently, “Hello? I’d like to make a delivery order.” CHAPTERXVI After the match, Danny comes back to find a new Phone and a letter asking for him to call. Follow: the convo between Big C and Danny, says he was lying and trying to save his own hide. Truth is, Danny owes a ton of gambling debt and Trick wants his money. Mentions of the girl send Danny to the hotel and heading for the next show Danny puts his hand flat against the locker room door as he looks down the hallway. The Clevelanders just down the way shoot looks of shock and surprise at him as he stands before them the winner against the nightmare they call Shaman. He smirks, happy with the thought of upsetting the common belief, and lifts his head toward them. “Take a good look people,” he whispers with swagger. “Check out the new guy, takin’ down the Shaman, blowin’ up the spotlight. Main Event… meet Dynasty.” He cocks his head before pressing through the door, stepping into the locker room to find that he’s the only one around, again. He grins, happy with the silence, and seemingly takes a deep breath and finally gives himself a second to relax. “Time for some good ol’ fashion celebrating,” Danny gloats aloud in a deep, confident tone. “First order of business…” His eyes light up at the thought of celebrating David Lee Roth style. He takes a few long strides spewing with determination toward his locker and begins to shuffle through his bag, toward his always present bag of goodies. Hoping to find some joints and the last of some grade A Columbian nose candy, Danny stops when he grabs something unfamiliar. He pulls out a cell phone wrapped in newspaper, “…The fuck is this?” Turning it over, he finds three words on the back in big blue ink. CALL HOME DANNY The victorious thoughts rush out of Danny’s bloodstream as curious ones replace them. He tears the paper off, brainstorming ideas of what this is, before he notices the phone buzz to life. Four letters send a slight chill down his spine. HOME He presses the green button that stops the buzzing. Slowly, Danny draws the phone to the side of his head. He remains silent for a few seconds to see if he can catch a clue as to who this might be. He takes a short breath in, “Who’s this?” “Don’t hang up,” the other end says briefly. “You’re a dead man if you do.” “Big C?” Danny cocks his brow. “Nice work tonight kid,” Big C seems somewhat friendly over the phone. “Gotta take advantage of tha mistakes y-“ “What the fuck is this?” Danny cuts him off. Big C’s end is silent for a few seconds, “Look kid, I gotta tell it like it is. You trust me?” Danny’s nose wrinkles up just before his lip curls, “Who wouldn’t trust a gun-running, drug-smuggling back-stabbing mother-“ “Just here me out mothafucka,” Big C barks. “You ain’t got tha time to be arguin’ with me, aight!?” Danny Francisco has never been the type of person to let cooler heads prevail. That is the reason he’s in this mess in the first place and it’s also the reason he’s wearing spandex in the godforsaken city of Cleveland, Ohio… in December. His silence is taken as a sign of compliance. Big C can be heard taking a puff from a cigar over the phone, “Look… I gotta tell you now… what I told yo ass was a straight up lie, kid. I was tryin’ t’save my ass.” Danny’s eyelids grow heavy as they slam shut. He runs a hand through his long, sweeping hair before letting his head fall down. His eyes remain shut as thoughts of helplessness storm around his aggressive head. “I know I said all that was bullshit but…” Big C pauses awkwardly. “I was feedin’ ya shit. Tha truth behind all this is ya fucked up, boy. Ya put money down on ya own games, ya put money down on other games, and ya did it a lot, kid. The Trick ain;t ever recovered form that. Ya owe him big, boy. Big.” Danny snarls, “fuck that, who are you to tell me what I’ve done? As far as I’m concerned, the Trick can kiss my ass. Let his boy Cesar tell him that, too. In fact-“ “He already did, Francisco,” Big C interrupts. “I found Cesar befo’ tha Trick and let me jus’ say, roach ain’t deserve that shit, kid. Ya askin’ for some bad news.” “I’m not askin’ Big C,” he snaps into the phone, grinding his teeth almost. “I’m makin’ the rules now. I’ve got the Trick’s money – no matter if I owe him or not – and I’m gone. If he doesn’t want to end up like Roach, he’ll let it go. The past is the past as far as I’m concerned.” Big C doesn’t say much for a long time and Danny can’t help but stand up and pace around the room. With the fire burning inside him over what’s going on, he’s not thinking as best as he could but there’s a lot to say for setting his foot down. “You owe him kid,” Big C mumbles. “I know ya don’t think ya do, but ya do. Whether or not you pay him is on you, I’m jus’ tryin’ to save yo ass, boy. Think it through.” Danny smirks, “Is that why ya told me all that bullshit in Windosr? Is that why ya tried to wipe me out? Save me the act, Cecil. You’re no saint, this is-“ “He’ll make you pay one way or another Danny,” Big C whispers solemnly. “Just be careful, aight?” Danny nods his head and smirks. He looks up at his street clothes and thinks about the reason he needs to get changed – Lacey. Danny smiles at the thought he might float off into the sunset with this one and hopes Cecil can find the strength to do the same someday. “What’s got you so involved Big C?” Danny wonders. Again a pause, “I’m a man of business. A deal… is a deal.” A click ends the call as Danny pulls the phone away from his ear, unclear why it was so sudden. He shakes his head and begins to change into his street gear. Within minutes, Danny is ready to hit the road and locks eyes with the cell phone sitting on the chair. He picks it up, smiles, and then sends that piece of shit Nokia to the bottom of the garbage receptacle closest to the door. CHAPTERXVII “And…” the Trick waits. “What else did he say?” Big C looks through the heavy cigar smoke, “He said to go fuck yourself.” Lacey smiles at the strong black man staring down the barrel of the Trick’s shiny, silver Desert Eagle. The Trick turns around and flashes her a sarcastic grin. Big C and the Trick lock eyes once again. “Tell him one more thing for me big man…” the Trick reaches into his pocket and pulls out a thick, round, silver pistol suppressor. “It’s easy to remember.” The Trick aims the end of his gun at Big C’s head before turning away toward Lacey. He watches the girl’s eyes burst as Big C takes two bullets head-on and his body slaps back against the hotel wall. The Trick turns around, offers a look of utter disgust, and then turns back around to Cesar, who holds Lacey by the back of the neck. “Go get the car,” the Trick commands Cesar. The Trick locks his grip firmly around her arm and pulls her in close. He forces her gaze at Big C’s body. She tries to turn away but the Trick takes his free hand and forces her focus to examine the place where Big C’s head used to be. “You see that, dolly?” the Trick says through short, adrenaline-filled breaths. “Unless you tell me where he’s goin’… that’s gonna be what I do to you… but slower and a lot more painful.”
