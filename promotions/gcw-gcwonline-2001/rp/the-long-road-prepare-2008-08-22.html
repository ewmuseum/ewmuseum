The Long Road: Prepare

22 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22 Aug 08 I. “Give me the god damn crutches, you look ridiculous.” Joaquin’s bright eyes dig a hole in me, looking for my heart is how he describes it whenever he doesn’t feel I’m giving the effort that he feels is necessary. I meet his glare with an irritated look, one he probably isn’t too happy about. “Don’t look at me like I spoke fuckin” Arabic or something. I said get rid of the crutches.” “Maybe you didn’t notice that my foot was always ripped off a few weeks ago.” “I saw it. If you weren’t ready than you would never have agreed to wrestle this week.” “Of course I would’ve.” “True.” Joaquin folds his arms and takes in a deep breath, a sigh really. “But there’s no getting out now. You will be stepping back into that ring in a matter of days. It’s time to lose the crutches.” My first reaction is to cuss him out and leave. My ankle was barely holding on after Storm got through with it and it is still hurting today. My second, and best, reaction is to do what the man says. This is what I’m paying him for. “Fine.” I spit out at him. “But if I can’t go at Worldwide because of this I’m going to snap your neck.” “No you aren’t gimpy now lose em.” I close my eyes and let out a long breath, bracing myself for the pain that is bound to shoot up my leg. I let my hands relax and release my grip on the crutches which make a metallic sound as they slap against the floor. Moment of truth. I press down on the floor with my ankle, pain fires up immediately. My body tightens and by reflex my foot snaps off the ground to relieve the pressure. “Put it back down!” “Alright.” Slowly my foot goes back down to the ground, still fucking painful but a little bit more tolerable. I open my eyes again. CRACK! The old man drives a kendo stick right against my ankle. It feels as if someone has set fire to my lower body. The surprise of the blow causes me to fall straight to the floor, my hands covering up the ankle to protect it. My breath leaves my body for a few moments as I try to regain my composure. “SON OF A BITCH!” “You weren’t ready Roberto.” “Of course I wasn’t fucking ready.” He kneels down next to me. “You have to be stronger, you have to be ready. Do you really think that whoever you’re going against isn’t going to go right after this leg” The whole world saw what happened to you and since you are stepping into the ring so soon, they know it cannot be fully healed. For god’s sake you went on-air with crutches last week. You showed your cards and now you are bound to lose.” “I won’t.” “You will Roberto, you really will. Regardless of what you may believe, there are people out there that can kick your ass. Do you think that Murray is going to give you the night off” Think he is going to put you against Rikki Roxx? I don’t think so.” He stands back up, it still amazes me how quick and agile he can be for such an old man. “Murray is going to make you prove your worth. I suggest you man up.” I nod my head, knowing that he is right ? he’s always right. I extend my right hand out but he merely glances down at it and turns to walk away. “Get up on your own.” He says after moving a few steps from my position. Although the pain is still fierce I do what he asks. It’s not an easy or quick journey back to my feet but eventually I get there and face my mentor. “Happy?” “No.” CRACK! The Kendo stick looks like its ready to snap in half when it collides with my tender ankle. I try to hold in the pain, hate giving him the satisfaction of knowing he’s got me in a tight spot but it becomes an impossible task and I crumble again, falling to one knee this time. “Come on boy, you think this is as bad as it is going to get?” Instead of answering his question I grit my teeth and push myself back up to my vertical base. His calm demeanor irritates me something fierce. “I’ve been through worse.” I whisper. “Indeed you have ? but not in awhile. You’ve been pampered since late 2005. Steven Caldera made you feel as if you were a god. Now look at you ? crippled and tears streaming down your face.” “You talk too much.” He smiles. CRACK! The stick blasts my ankle, almost buckling me but I fight it and stay standing. “Hmmm.” CRACK! CRACK! CRACK-CRACK-CRACK! My breathing is erratic, my hands quiver, my face stained with tears, my ankle on fire. “I-Is that it?” He smiles again. He brings his foot across my face in a roundhouse kick that’s so fast that I barely know what was happening even as my body turns ? then the kendo stick tears into my ankle again. The lights on the ceiling greet me as I stare at them from my back. Joaquin’s shadowy figure appears overhead, his blue eyes and wide smile look almost ghostly with all the shadow. “How do you feel?” He asks. I want to reach out and bite his ankle or something ? but then I come to a realization. “Great.” I say with a smile forming on my face. “Absolutely.” “I figured.” He extends his hand, this time willing to help me up to my feet. “Sometimes we all need our ass kicked.” I can’t put any weight on the ankle but the fire burning inside me is way more powerful then the burning sensation in that part of my body. “Am I ready?” “No.” He says flatly. “You will probably still lose. You can barely stand but I don’t expect you to just roll over. You do whatever it takes, whatever it takes.” “That’s never been a problem.” “We’ll see. For now I suggest to go sit in a tub or ice or something.” He turns to walk away. “Wait!” I hobble a step closer but I don’t dare to go further in fear of falling on my face. “That’s it?” He turns back around, his eyes focused on mine. “No.” CRACK! I fall to the floor again. “No we’re finished for the day.” II. I flip the light switch in the hotel room on and limp inside, doing my best to make it to the bed before collapsing. Taking the pressure off my injury is like a cold drink of water on a hot day in hell. Typically a gazillion things are floating through my brain when I’m alone like this ? Is Mary happy? How’s the baby? Where’s Alex? Fuck Vivica Valentine! Kiss my ass Jay Terror! Blow me Andy Murray. Ya know, important shit right? Tonight is different. Pain fills me up so much that it is tough to think about anything else but stopping it from hurting so fucking bad. “Probably need some Percocet or something.” No matter how many times I hear the voice and no matter how long I know the one it belongs to ? I cannot stop cringing when I hear it. I turn my head and look at LeStatt, hands cuffed behind him and silver tape strapping him to the chair in the room. I had originally taped his mouth shut too but obviously he chewed through that. “No I need you to shut the hell up.” “Why are you so hostile Rob” I mean really, it’s probably bad for your heart.” The joke isn’t funny to me but judging from the huge smile on Knight’s face I can see he loves making light of my heart attack in 2005. “Funny. My fist is also very bad for your ability to keep all of your teeth.” “Oh please, save the tough guy act for someone it’ll work on. I don’t need to remind you that I know everything about you ? including the fact that you are thinking about Mary giving you a blow job right this moment.” Fuck? “Shut the hell up.” “And may I say ? she is really fucking good at it.” “Quiet.” “Maybe we should give her a buzz?” “SHUT THE FUCK UP!” He smirks. “What” Are you going to hobble over here and kick my ass?” He leans his head back and lets out a loud over exaggerated laugh. “Try it bitch and see what happens.” I sit up on the bed and pull myself to the edge, my anger swelling. It’s almost impossible to get any peace and quiet these days. I remember the good old days when LeStatt would just pop up here and there, maybe take over for awhile and leave again. Now he is a constant, a nagging headache that won’t go away no matter how many pills you take. “Why are you here LeStatt?” “Don’t you like my company” Has to be better than being alone all the damn time. Besides I thought we bonded a little bit on our road trip.” “I dreamt about crashing my vehicle and killing you.” “What better way to bond” At least we’re honest.” I shake my head and push myself to my feet, ensuring that I’m steady first before making my way to the bathroom. I twist the handle that turns the cold water on inside the sink. I give it time to get as cold as it can before placing my cupped hands under the running water and splashing it on my face. It won’t get rid of Knight or the pain in my ankle ? or anything else for that matter but it’s something to do to get away for even the briefest moment. I walk out of the bathroom and sit back on the bed, my eyes lock onto LeStatt. “You do realize that this cannot go on forever.” “It’ll go on for as long as I want it to. You don’t have control Roberto, you never did.” “I’ll put an end to this eventually, I have to.” “Are you afraid of success?” He demands. “Because it is the only reason I can put together that you’d try to stop me from doing what needs to be done.” “I don’t fear anything.” “You’re lying and you know it.” “Just let me sleep LeStatt.” The room stays quiet for a total of 3.58 seconds. “One day I’ll show you the light Roberto.” He sighs and takes in a breath. “You’ll know when it is too ? because on that day we’ll rise above everyone else again.” “I don’t need you for that.” He continues, as if I didn’t say anything at all. “The day that LeStatt Knight is once again GCW World Heavyweight Champion will be the day that the world knows that I’m back in control. Destiny is inevitable Roberto and destiny says that you will lose this war with me. It’s only a matter of a time.” I don’t respond because I figure he’ll begin talking soon enough anyways. . . . . . But he doesn’t. The room is deathly quiet now, LeStatt’s head leaned back and his black eyes closed. The day’s events fly through my mind. Knight Joaquin Ankle Pain Violence Andy Murray Andy Murray Andy Murray Worldwide Andy Murray World Wide... Am I ready? Time will Tell. "Yes, yes it will." LeStatt's voice calls out the last words quietly. It's the last thing I hear before my eyes close and I fall asleep, preparing for yet another long day.
