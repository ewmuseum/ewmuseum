An Alexander Redding Moment

29 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/29/10 Sitting at a smokey bar together, Red and Grady were sinking some cold ales. Grady used the opportunity to get himself a new woman where as Red, on the other hand, reminisced on a past love. Not just an ordinary everyday love, no, this love was something special. Red was thinking about the love that he had decided to spend the of his life with. Instead of romantic thoughts of walking hand in hand down a beach with the tide coming in to splash up their ankles, all Red could think of is the day the love of his life gave back the spectacular gold and diamond ring that he had bought to profess his love for her. While Red takes another sip of his icy cold beer, Grady starts telling a story of some sorts to Red, who acts like he's listen but instead lets his train of thought head towards his match with GCW's returning past, Jimmy Thunder. Now Red vaguely remembers seeing Thunder the last time he was an employee of GCW but it was his time at Ringside Wrestling that Red really remembers. The thing that really stands out for Red is Thunder's matches with Tim Shipley and he knew that Thunder will not be a push over. Oh yes, Thunder was someone that commended respect but to hell with that, Red thought. He strongly believed he commanded his own respect. He was no push over as well and he felt that Thunder should show Red the respect he deserved. After coming oh so close to beating Dorn Tracer and Syrus, then going down to El Marico and Dynasty, the thought of going 0-3 against Thunder is a thought that is almost sending Red over the edge emotionally. How many more losses can a man take? First the love of his life, then two triple threat matches he was expecting to win. Now he has a chance to prove to not only himself but also the powers that be at GCW who book the matches that Red is without a doubt worthy enough and capable enough to earn himself some sort of title shot. This match with Thunder, according to Grady is just another opportunity for him to travel to another city at Red's expense. Grady only wrestled on the Indy circuit for about a year and never tasted the pressures of wrestling in a market leading promotion like GCW and to Red this was more than "just another match." Red sees this match as his last real attempt to gain respect from the locker room and get some sort of momentum behind him to lay a serious claim to a title shot. Although Grady had already left the bar with a female companion, Red decided to stay at the bar and have a few more beers. Even though a very attractive looking lady showed some in Red and wanted to join Grady and her friend, Red's feelings, unfortunately for her, were not reciprocated. He'd much rather spend the rest of the night engrossed in his own thoughts and preparing himself mentally for GCW's next edition of World Wide. The next day, Red woke from his drunken slumber to sounds of Grady bashing on his hotel room door. Feeling the full force of a night heavily indulged in alcoholic beverages, using instinct, Red makes his way to his door and opens it only to be blinded by the glaring hallway light. "This is going to be an awesome day Red!" Grady announced with an enthusiastic tone in his voice and an extra spring in his step. "I have organized and interview for you today with Barry Goldstein." Grady added as he started making a hot cup of coffee for himself and Red. "I thought it'd be a great way to give you some much needed exposure." he said. Internally Red was fuming at the what Grady had did without consulting him. Red had no intentions to flap his gums for the television world. He planned on doing all his talking in the ring. Deep down Red's instincts screamed at him to tell Grady to go shove his planned interview up his ass but with this hangover that has overtaken his entire being, he let it slide. After a much needed steaming hot shower and an extremely light breakfast, Red followed Grady down to the hotel lobby where Barry Goldstein and his camera crew patiently waited for the two. Barry placed the hot cup of coffee he was drinking on the glass table next to him and stood to his feet and greeted both Grady and Red. "Nice to meet you here Red, thanks for doing this." Goldstein said as he welcomed both Grady and Red who take a seat opposite Barry Goldtein. Grady and Goldstein exchange some short witted humor while Red, still nursing his hangover, waits quietly for the interview to start. Goldstein: Thanks for joining me today Grady and Red. Red: You're welcome. Grady: You're Welcome. Goldstein: Now Red, you really haven't had any luck since you've been here at GCW. What will you change to turn your luck around when you get in the ring with someone as experienced as Jimmy Thunder? Red: Nothing really, the results in those two matches were taken out of my hands. This is the first time I can really show what I've got in terms of ability. Goldstein: How will you overcome the raw power Jimmy Thunder is famous for? Let's face it, the guy is nearly seven feet tall, around the four hundred pound mark. He's not exactly the smallest guy on the planet. How do overcome all that to beat Jimmy Thunder? Red: Without giving too much away, just compare me to a tiger and Thunder as a big docile elephant. Goldstein: OK, Dangerous Games is just around the corner and the Over The Top Battle Royal is where you'll be. Your thoughts??? Red: I'm glad you asked. Listen, Dangerous Games has a history for turning people's fortunes and making them into superstars and that's what's going to happen when I come to Dangerous Games. People are going to judge me on my last two matches where the results were taken out of my hands and that's fine. But my Dangerous Games campaign starts this week at World Wide. Goldsteing: You had a promising boxing career ahead of you when you were younger and that fell to nothing when the pressure was on you. Why will things be different for you in professional wrestling? Grady: Hang on there a second Barry that's a low blow. Red: It's OK Grady, let him continue. Goldstein: I'm just asking questions using facts. I just think the people want to know why your time at GCW will be any different from your time in boxing when you were suppose to turn into a the next Muhammad Ali. Red: Here's the thing, people were saying all sorts of crazy things about me being the next Muhammad Ali and all that crap while I was in college. I'll admit I enjoyed the attention but deep down I hated boxing, I just used it as a way to keep and stay fit. Now I'm here at GCW and I'm 0-2, people are expecting or hoping for me to go 0-3. I'm not sure if the media expect me to fall flat on my face but I have a scoop for everyone in the media and the wrestling world, the difference between me as a boxing and me as a wrestler is that I hated playing boxing but I love being in the ring as a wrestler. You think Im going to fail as a wrestler? Watch me go from a zero to the top of the pile. Grady: Geez Barry, a lot of the guys said you were a pretty cool interviewer but you seem to be trying to attack Red. Red: No Grady, I'm glad he's asked the questions he has because I'm sick of hiding behind them. The only way I'll get the respect I deserve around here is to take the good with the bad. Goldstein: In all honesty I'm not trying to attack you or anything like that Red, I'm just asking the questions that the guys are asking about with you. Red: So now the guys in the locker room think I'm going to fail? OK. Let's see what they think of me after Dangerous Games. The scene fades......
