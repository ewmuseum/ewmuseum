Date: The Witching Hour Happy thoughts. That’s it, think happy thoughts, think about being back in the states. There was nothing quite like going home again to make you feel like a million bucks. Yeah sure maybe it was just Texas, but at least it wasn’t some new European shithole, at least he could get a decent meal, and people would be driving on the correct side of the road. God was it going to be good to be home. That’s right, focus on the homecoming. Don’t think about the nagging injuries, they’re healing up nicely. The pain shouldn’t prey on your mind, it’ll all be better soon. Nevermind, that your arm’s been throbbing pretty bad, change in pressure will do that sometimes, but hey it could be worse, next stop could have been another trip to London. It’ll be ok soon, you’ll be home. Forget that your new students couldn’t take out Hardy. That should be the furthest thing from your mind. It’s like the old saying goes if you want something done right you’ve got to do it yourself, and your chance would be coming up soon. Don’t think about how lucky he’s been. Don’t think about how much he’s improved, or the role he’s been on. Think about twisting his limbs, imagine his agony as you cripple the upstart bastard for trying to replace you. Yeah that’s it, let his future whimpering soothe yourself to sleep. Happy thoughts, that’s what you’ve got to focus on right now. Sure the entire trip had been a disaster but it was all over now, you’re going home, and you’re going to prove once again why you’re the greatest. Happy thoughts of cheeseburgers and fries, happy thoughts of making Hardy tap out. Happy thoughts weren’t cutting it. You’ve give your left nut for a smoke. Damn facist non-smoker bastards, won’t let you have one of life’s small pleasures even though you’re trapped in a chair for god knows how long. It’s not like you can just step outside for a quick couple puffs, if the fall didn’t kill you, the stop at the bottom sure as hell would. Everything still hurts, your new students keep failing you, you don’t know if you can beat some kid that a couple years ago wasn’t even a blip on your radar, and all you want is a smoke. So you take a drink, at least the health nazi’s hadn’t banned booze from planes yet. Maybe the answer will finally be at the bottom of your little plastic cup of scotch and melted ice. Shit, no luck. You wait for the flight attendant to come by so you can order another, yeah flight attendant, you can’t even call a sky rat a stewardess anymore. Jesus. When she finally comes by she gives you a strange look when you order your fifth double of the flight. You can tell she thinks you’ve already had too much, and should probably cut you off, maybe you were slurring, who knows. Still she brings you another anyway, cash has a way of getting you what you want, when you want it, and damn the consequences. The drink arrives and when you take a sip, you realize how stupid an idea it is to keep drinking. Every fiber of your being cries out for a cigarette. Yeah you should’ve thought about that before you started drinking, alcohol increases the craving for nicotine, it’s why a lot of people only smoke when they drink. Don’t think about it keep your mind on something else, focus on the warm fuzzies in your belly, consider how the pain melts away a little bit more. You finish your drink a little more quickly that you probably should have, and this time you decide against another. Sleeping wouldn’t be a horrible idea, and you’d love to pass out, but your mind is still racing, thinking about all the things you keep telling yourself not to think of, you’re still worried about taking on the kid. He’s gotten a lot better than you ever thought him capable of, it’s going to be tough. In spite of yourself you find you’re a little worried about your students. You did make a promise to help them become stars, and they’re getting another shot at it, when you land. They’ve got the tools to be champions, even if they couldn’t take out one little runt. Hopefully they’ll do better against that chick, you know the blonde slut with the big tits. And now you’re thinking about Sheena, and you feel yourself reacting. It’s involuntary but it’s there, and you miss her. Sure she wasn’t good for you or anyone, never knew who she’d been with lately, every time you screw her, you’re afraid your cock is going to turn black and fall off. But you still miss her touch, seems like she’s the only one that ever really understood what was going on in your head. It was kinda nice, plus she always kept herself tight, that in an of itself should make her a keeper. You know she’ll come running if you call, and you eye the little credit card pay phone in front of you. It’d be stupid to call, she’d just show up with Alex, and Bruce, and god knows you didn’t really want to see them. Or maybe you do, fighting off the wave of nostalgia is pointless. It’s coming and you know you can’t stop it. The good times, and there were plenty fill your mind, it’s easy to remember the world with rose colored glasses when you’ve got a head change. The firewater suffuses every part of your body, and you think about hanging with your old crew. You chuckle when you think of Bruce’s antics, and having a bodyguard like Alex never hut. The guy was like a big dog, stupidly loyal, and wouldn’t hesitate to destroy anyone and everyone that pissed you off. Yeah, in your mind it was settled you were going to call Sheena, maybe she could meet you in Houston, that’d be great. Alex, Bruce, maybe even Jimmie, and Sheena everyone reunited. And hell you can just imagine the looks on Kyle and Conner’s faces when you send the gay midget after em. That’d be worth a few cheap laughs, definitely. Your body’s acting before you finish your thoughts, picking up the receiver, sliding the card, dialing the number you’ll never forget. A cold swirl of fear coils in your stomach at the first ring, what if she doesn’t come running this time? You’ve fired the lot of them so many times, maybe they’ve gotten sick of your bullshit. You try not to think about that, happy thoughts remember? “Hello?” At the sound of her voice, your mouth dries up, it feels like you’ve been chewing cotton. For one of the few times in your life, you don’t know what to say. You know you shouldn’t feel like this, it’s just Sheena, but you can’t help it. You realize you should have said something by now, if you didn’t real soon she might hang up, and you don’t know if you’ll be able to bolster up the courage to call her again. You force yourself to talk, it’s now or never. “Hi babe.” You realize how no casual you sound. Suddenly you’re sweating, maybe it’s the scotch, keep telling yourself it’s the scotch that’s making you flush. “Oh, hello Michael.” There was warmth in the hello, but that was as icy as it comes. You could almost see her breath frosting as she spoke. Under other circumstances you might have been able to make with the sweet talk, but not now, you still couldn’t think of anything to say. This really shouldn’t be this damn hard. “Hey, I’m getting back into the states in a few hours, I was wondering if you wanted to meet me in Houston?” God, was that a crack in your voice when you said “meet?” What the hell is going on, this shouldn’t be difficult. It’s Sheena Ramone for god’s sake, she’ll open her legs for anyone over fifteen. “Well, I don’t know Michael, why exactly would I want to do that” You really treated me like shit last time we were together.” And you definitely deserved that. No real way you can justify it either, you were a complete and total asshole. Stupid to just take for granted the fact that she’d come running if you called, this was the stupidest idea you’ve ever had, but you continue on, because now you’re committed. It’s just then that you realize you’re in love, it sounds corny but this is the only women who’s always been there for you, the only one that ever made you feel right. You’re in deep, and it’s finally making sense why you always want to call her when you’ve been drinking. “Well I’m waiting, give me one good reason I should go all the way to Houston just to see you.” “I, well, I want to see you.” “So, I should just drop everything because you want to see me huh” Sorry Michael, I’m not that girl anymore, I finally realized you were using me, and I’m not going to take it anymore.” And just like that it’s your worst fear, right in front of your face. Sheena, your dad, Caldera, you pushed away everyone you’ve ever cared about, and maybe at one point they cared about you, but you pushed them away. Years of being an asshole are finally catching you, lie in your bed, cause you made it. “I understand.” “What” No sweet talk from Mr. Michael Stevens? No fancy words to try and lure me back? You’ve really lost your touch Michael.” She used to call you Mikey, even though you hated it, it still sounded good coming from her. Now it’s Michael, so cold, so formal. You know you deserve every bit of it, it’s no worse than what you’ve done to her, at least she’s not yelling, god knows you deserve that too. “Look, Sheena, I’m sorry I called. I know I’ve treated you like shit, and I guess I knew you wouldn’t want to talk to me even before I called. Just hoping I guess.” The silence stretched a lot longer than you would expect. Why didn’t she say something, deep down you think maybe you want her to yell at you. That’d be a fitting way to end things, her finally standing up to you, after everything you’ve put her through. “You’ve never apologized to me for anything” What the hell kind of game are you playing Michael?” God she’s pissed, but it’s not just anger, you can hear something else in the last words, maybe it’s not too late. No, don’t get your hopes up, just apologize and hang up. That’s the smart thing to do, don’t let yourself hurt anymore, just get off the phone. Whatever you do don’t tell her you love her, she’ll probably laugh at you. “No game, I’m just I’m sorry, and I wanted to say I finally realized something, I love you Sheena.” Idiot, jackass, complete fucking moron, how could you possibly tell her that? Are you retarded? Do you need a helmet? She’s going to laugh, or worse yet, think it’s some trick you’re trying to pull on her, and why not, you’re one sorry manipulative asshole, she knows that probably better than anyone. “What” Mike, are you drunk?” “Sheena, I’m sorry I didn’t mean to, shit, I’m just sorry. I made mistakes, a lot of them, I treated you like dirt, and I’m sorry. You don’t have to forgive, and if you say the word I’ll never call you again. I was just thinking about you, I’m sorry I shouldn’t have called. Are you about to cry? Seriously, what’s going on here, you’re Michael Stevens the best of the best, and you’re about to cry over Sheena Ramone. You wish you could say she’s not worth it, but you know she is, and that’s what’s killing you. Everything in you screams “be a man and stop whining”, except you can’t. You wait for her to tell you to piss off and die. That’s what you’re expecting, but she’s one of the only people in the world who can surprise you. “Listen, Mike, Houston right” I need to think about some things, but you’re going to be in Houston for a while right?” “Yeah.” Barely a whisper, you try not to get your hopes up. This can’t be what it sounds like, once she gets off the phone she’s going to realize how crazy this is, and she’ll never want to talk to you again. That’s how it’s going to end up, you’ve got to be a man now, get over it, focus on Warpath, focus on beating Hardy. “Well, I might see you there, like I said I’ve got to think. You still have the same number?” “Yeah.” What else can you say? “I’ll call you ok” One way or the other I’ll call you, but I have to figure some things out, ok? “Yeah, ok.” “Bye Mikey.” “Goodbye Sheena.” As you hang up the phone, it never occurs to you she called you Mikey.
