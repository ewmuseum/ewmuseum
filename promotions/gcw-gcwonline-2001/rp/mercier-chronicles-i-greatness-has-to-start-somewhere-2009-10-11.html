Mercier Chronicles I: Greatness Has To Start Somewhere

11 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/11/09 Success is not always defined by the championships or the amount of matches you've had. Nor is it of the amount of wins or losses. Success is defined by skill, mind games, heart, and the will to do anything for the sport. GCW wrestlers have come and gone. Legends have lived and died. Every wrestler has to start at the bottom. Every person doesn't come in as the Andy Murray or the Vivica J. Valentine. It isn't easy to come into GCW and win against the top. Just ask Aaron Nothing who thought just because he came into here, while being Just Wrestling's Heavyweight Champion, that he would be the new face here. Not every superstar is destined for greatness. Things don't aways come out as you please. But there will be times that God feels you've been destined for things. James Mercier Jones doesn't belive in half of the things that have been stated. He believes in pain, destruction, and winning. James feels that he is the greatest wrestler that has ever lived though he has only been in the business for a year. His ego overweighs the earth by a ton. But this is just hit thoughts on wrestling. Many think because people do the things they do that they act like it for eternity. This isn't the case with James Mercier. Outside the James is engaged and is expecting a child and if it wasn't for his attitude with wrestling his ego would be smaller than something microscopic. He treats his friends and family with respect and would never disrespect anyone he knows. So why is this? Is his attitude a gimmick or is it really a way of life? James never did have a great life. Was this a result of it all. Let us review it then. James was born into a not so great neighbor hood of Detroit, Michigan. His mother was a frequent drug user while his alcoholic father abused him and his mother. Even with all the drama going on in his life he never had the ability to even let a smile drop from his beaten face. When he was about a eleven years old he witnessed his mother cheating on her husband for drugs. When James father returned and found her cheating he beat her into an inch of her life and beat the man dead. James had witnessed it all. His father than dragged James into the living room and beat him violently. That night James murdered his father in his sleep. When the cops arrived it was seen as his father and the man having a violent fight with James mother agreeing with that idea. James had never been the same. From then and out when him and his mother moved to St. Louis he had never been the same. While still respecting others he knew a cocky and arrogant attitude had risen. He joined the local wrestling team and his ego continued to rise. While the rest of his life to today reamins abit of a mystery, James seems to let everyone know that it wasn't nice. But James still believes he is the best. Though he knows he is at the bottom of the ladder here, he knows that GREATNESS HAS TO START SOMEWHERE! =========================================================================================== 'How can this shit get worse!' A man sits on a bench with blood gushing from a cut on his forehead. The man looks to be very young. His hair is very short and scappy. His skin is white but tan. A small cut appears on his forehead. The air around him felt tight as he breathes. "God damn that hurts. Why the hell are we doing this?" James said wiping the blood from his forehead. Another man then appears stepping into the room. He's alittle more larger and more fit than James is. His skin was as pale as a vampires and his body was as built as Lou Ferrigno. He walks in and stands by the bench and laughs as he looks James in the eye. "James you need to be more prepared than anything for GCW." "I don't think hitting me with fucking kendo sticks is going to prepare me." James said sarcastically. "Whatever smartass!" "Don't get pissed Walter." "Screw you!" James looks down and chuckles before looking back at Walter who looks alittle pissed and sad. "Hey man don't get you panties in a bunch. Maybe you're on your period or something." Walter looks alittle heated as he furiously stares at James without saying anything. I'm just joking Big W." James said as he gives Walter alittle friendly smile. Walter's smile quickly turns into a smile as they give each other a pound. "Damn my head is pounding."James said rubbing his forehead again. "Well let's hope that it gets better before your match..." James rubs his head some more as Walter just smirks at the sight."...or we can just see Lucas stomp a mudhole in your ass." Walter laughs as James sits there in pain. "You bastard! First you kick my ass then you say Lucas Quinton is going to beat me." "Hey man I'm just saying. You've been bitching alot with the training lately. I'm just saying maybe you're losing your touch." Walter said. "ME! Oh no I'd never be lesser in any way. I am and will always be number one my friend." "Well James, you're starting off at the bottom that. Doesn't look like you're number one just yet." James smirks berfore staring at Walter,"Hey everyone hast to start off somewhere. Even if you are as great as me and should belong in the title scene." Walter smirks and takes a gander at his watch. "Looks like it's running time." "You want me to lose this damn match don't you." "If you are as great as you say you are then you won't after all this training." James laughs as he stands before wiping his forehead once again. "Maybe we should get some vasoline on that cut there." James reaches down and grabs a gym bag before looking at Walter,"You're telling me. I need something to get this shit to stop bleeding." "Well you might want to wait 'cause I'm gooing to run the shit out of you." "Bring it on!" James said as they exit the locker room. To Be Continued....
