Forced Recovery

27 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/27/05 As he hung up the phone from what was a very emotional phone call, Coyle slowly spun around in his stool as he held his back firmly. He arched low and forward because of the pain, and gently massaged his back; it seemed to be the most comfortable position for his injury. From what the hospital told him, he had a hairline fracture within his vertebrae and that it would take him 6-8 weeks to recover from this ill-fated result. Just to think, he got injured right before his big match on his first ever pay-per-view bout, FallOut; boy, did it make him depressed. However, he felt that he could wrestle through this. Yes, the pain was exceedingly getting worse and worse by the day, but he refuses to let this stop him from his spotlight. Nothing in this world would please him more than to wrestle X again, this time the real X, and to defeat him no matter what extreme measures he had to go through. Unfortunately though, every doctor he has visited since his last match had denied his request to be cleared, they claim that he simply is not ready. But, somehow, in his head, he is convinced that no doctor can stop him from his destiny. Gradually, Coyle stood himself out of the chair, only to pace himself through back stretches. When he finished stretching, he started his way over to the front door, yawning loudly every few steps. He hasn’t been getting much sleep lately because of the threat of a re-scheduled match, he didn’t want it to be this way. He wanted this match to be big so that he could embarrass X in front of a whole range of audiences, in a special manner, as X did to him. “It’s probably wasted effort,” he sighed, “The GCW won’t allow it to happen.” He opened his front door, and looked outside towards the bright sunny day. This image made him laugh because it seemed as if a thunderstorm was in his house and the nice day was just a step away. For some reason however, he couldn’t take that step. It was one of those uncomfortable laughs for him, more of a denial feeling. Nothing would be bright again until however long this injury would take to heal and bring him back to his eligibility again. So, a little ticked, he decided that slamming the door shut would be the best form of little relief for all of this tension. Shutting the door roughly barely helped after he did it though, instead, the vibrations just caused the door to lock itself on accident. Ignoring that, he turned and fell forward onto a couch. When he landed, he let his body bounce, yet he stiffened his body so that it wouldn’t hurt the back. Things like this would probably be easier to manage with if he wore his brace, but as he felt, no doctor controlled his methods of doing things. Rebellious maybe, but he had his own plans. Laying on the couch quietly, he stared out into the abyss just thinking and strategizing on what he can do. Nothing came to mind though, it was as if he had writer’s block, but instead, for thoughts on how to wrestle this week. It seemed undeniable that he won’t be making an appearance at FallOut. “This is so unfair, X loses his favored manager Robert Larabree, the only threatening inspirational force X has, and I can’t use it to my advantage. X won’t be the same with that Ms. Scabbia character, not in any way, shape, or form,” he said out loud. After sighing, he ceased thinking about it. Now, he was just staring point blankly at nothing, just easing himself down. He became so calm about the issue that he managed to fall asleep. So, there he was snoring as he laid in an entirely uncomfortable posture on the couch. He has been resting for about 3 hours now, so you can only imagine how his back might feel at this moment. Alarmingly, he woke up as he grunted in pain and braced his back with his right palm. “Ah, motherfucker?” he screamed, “God-dammit!” His eyes widened up, and his body, in an involuntary action, had Coyle jump up. “I need those Perks,” he cried, “and I need them now!” Coyle rushed to his kitchen, and scrambled through everything that stood on the counter. “Where the hell are these things?” Still rummaging through all of his possessions, he finally found the small orange bottle. With not a care in the world, he popped open the bottle, and grabbed a few Perks. With no refusal, he downed the pills without water. Not immediately after, but shortly after, he felt better. And then it hit him? “Hrm, maybe this can work. I know the match is off, but maybe I can do something about it,” he said. He looked around and grinned, now as happy as a pig in shit. “X almost got of this,” he chuckled, “but now he’ll have to lose to me if he accepts. I got an idea?”
