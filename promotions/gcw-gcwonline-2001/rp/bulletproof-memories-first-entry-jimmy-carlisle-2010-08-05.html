Bulletproof Memories (First Entry: Jimmy Carlisle)

5 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/05/10 When you're a teenager, you think you've got it all figured out. The world is your oyster. You see the strings that control the system and you can do and be whatever you want as long as you put your mind to it. Your parents are just trying to hold you down and baby you, trying to protect you from the world that you already have mastered. They're obsolete and serve no purpose, so it's your job to toss them aside so you can spread your wings and fly. Live your own life. By your own rules. Life will be amazing. It isn't until years later you see how complicated the system really is, and that everything you knew was really nothing all along. Meet Vivica Julian Valentine, seventeen years old. Known to all of her friends only as "Vivy" she was one of the most popular kids at school. The Homecoming Queen of Suburbia lived a troubled life at home, constantly coming under fire for her B+ GPA and second place at the state gymnastics championship. Verbal abuse and overprotection forces her to take drastic action. Shedding the restrictive suburban life I conspire with a friend to runaway into the big city, never to be found again. That day started my lost year. Not long into my incredible life as a homeless high school drop-out I met a man named James Carlisle, his friends called him Jimmy. He wasn't necessarily big or strong, actually he was pretty short in stature, but had a presence that could move mountains. There was an aura about Jimmy that attracted me to him immediately, perhaps it was my natural magnetism to a confident man, but there was something about Jimmy that attracted everyone. Everyone on the streets knew his name. He didn't speak often but when he did the entire world listened. He was idolized and looked up to. He was everything I'd always wanted to be. Time went by and we grew closer. Even though he was eight years older than me, people didn't seem to judge us. His friends seemed genuinely happy that he'd found somebody to fall head over heels for, and I felt the same way. We were that couple that were always together, ridiculously in love with each other to the point where it was probably detrimental to the welfare of both of us. It was my first true love, and my first lesson in how things could go terribly wrong. Love can be the most incredible feeling that you've had in your life. They say love can even save your life. I say it could just as easily end it. Bulletproof Memories First Entry: Jimmy Carlisle ---- It was exactly one year to the day since she'd left home for the ever exciting city, and what a difference a year made. A girl once looking to start a new life and make a name for herself had quickly be tossed down from the stars and hit the cold hard concrete known simply as "reality." With an apron balled into her right first, she marched along the cattle paths of downtown Chicago as just another face in the crowd. The big fish of Highland Park had finally found the ocean and was just seconds away from drowning. The only thing keeping her afloat was the power of love. Covered in grease and sweat, a day of constant belittlement somehow managed to bring a smile to her face. A flutter of butterflies in her stomach would do that to her as she saw the building come into sight. She didn't have much, but it was move-in day. The first step towards the day that every seventeen year old girl dreams of... her wedding day. A full year in Chicago and finally she had a place she could call "home." But it wasn't the bed or the mailbox that put that stupid smile on her face, it was who was waiting for her inside. His name was James Michael Carlisle and he was the man of her dreams. Five foot, eight inches of unbridled charisma... he had a smile that could make the people come in droves. They often did, giving him the nickname Saint Jimmy years before a Green Day song would immortalize it, mainly due to his boyish good looks. Even at twenty five he could've easily blended in at any high school setting. He was a child of the people, being raised by a house-ridden grandmother who didn't have the mobility to keep after such an active child, he was a product of the apartment complex in which he was raised. With his father in prison and his mother a "lady of the night" his poor grandmother was the only family he'd ever known. When she died three years ago, he'd taken over the lease with his job selling the local newspaper. Vivica Julian Valentine would become the first new person to live in that apartment in over twenty-five years, and she couldn't be happier. She'd met Jimmy at the restaurant downtown where she was waiting tables. The second they made eye contact they both knew they were meant to be together. It was love at first sight, and after passing his phone number on his receipt that night, she'd spent six hours running up a cell phone bull she'd never pay. Talking about absolutely nothing and everything at the same time. Dinner. Movies. Flowers. The formalities followed as Jimmy courted the woman of his dreams. They were nearly inseparable for the better part of a year. She was going to marry that boy one day. That was the thought going through her mind as she opened the door to an apartment she'd now refer to as "mine" for what seemed like the hundredth time, but something felt different as she checked in at the front desk. It was the first day in the rest of her life. Heading up the elevator she pulled out her new cell phone for the tenth time since this story began. She'd been waiting all day for a message from Jimmy to let her know that her few personal belongings had made it home. Even after leaving six messages, she'd just blown it off as Jimmy being a busy man. His life was full of excitement and he was constantly surrounded by friends. The life of the party, he was the central figure in several groups of friends. KNOCK. KNOCK. KNOCK. Her clinched fist banged on the door, taking a deep breath she could feel her heart flutter with excitement. Dressed in a dirty white t-shirt and a pair of black work pants, she was hardly in a condition she'd normally present herself to Jimmy in. She'd spent the time waiting sliding her cell phone into her pocket and attempting to brush out her blonde-dyed-pink hair with her fingers. But there was no answer. "Jimmy!" She spoke, putting her mouth close to the door. "You are home, aren't you?" KNOCK. KNOCK. KNOCK. Pressing her ear against the door she listening for a sound, anything at all. Nothing came, forcing her to bite her lip as she slid her free hand down into her pocket. "He probably just went to grab a bite to eat... or something." She muttered under her breath and pulled out a small golden key, "That's why I have a key." Sliding the key into the old lock, she heard a loud click as it turned to the side. With a stiff jerk she jolted it back out of the door and turned the handle, putting her shoulder into it as she shoved the heavy wooden door open. Blue eyes examined the neglected interior. Jimmy was far from a neat freak, but Vivica could hardly claim to be the organized type. She shoved the door closed behind her after sliding in, loud enough to hopefully grab the attention of anyone inside. "Jimmy?" She said out loud, "You here Jimmy?" Tossing her apron down onto the olive green couch, next to three video gaming manuals and a paper plate as she stood completely still. Listening, she heard nothing as she turned around in a circle looking for any clue to where he might have gone. A chaotic mess is what lied in front of her, but it was nothing new. Her footsteps clicked on the aging hardwood floors of the sixth floor as she made her way across the studio apartment. "Helloooooooo?" Her voice resonated throughout the hallway as she pushed a halfway opened door the rest of the way, revealed an equally messy bedroom. There we no sign of her things at all in the stay that she was so eager to call home. "Hmmmm..." That sound escaped her lips as she finished looking inside and closed the door. Taking a few steps back she looked over at a door to her right. It wasn't exactly the door that caught her attention, but the bright light beaming from underneath onto her shoes. Gripping the door knob she found it to be unlocked, and used her hip to push it open. Stepping inside she suddenly found herself in a state of horror. The sink was still running, small splatters of blood surrounded the yellowed porcelain as her eyes slowly started to take everything in. A shoebox was on top of the toilet seat, filled with needles and pill bottles. The shower curtain was shoved wide open to reveal a pair of wide bloodshot eyes staring at the wall. Eyes belonging to a skinny naked body lying in a tub of red tinted water. A work tie was ripped and bound tightly above the bicep right below the shoulder as the man's head slowly moved over to look at her. Almost an inhuman stare was fixated upon her as words were stolen from her breath just at her lips, all except one... "...Jimmy..." ---- My life style choice usually brings lots of questions. What is straight-edge exactly? What would drive a girl your age to choose a lifestyle like that? I usually just smile and say I was raised better, but to brutally honest I was hardly raised at all. I have seen things that a teenager from Highland Park was never meant to see. They are things I try not to think about, or even talk about... but love can blind you from even the biggest red flags. ---- "So what's behind the special treatment all of a sudden?" Her voice spoke behind a brilliant smile. A vibrancy radiated off her despite the fading pink hair dye, it was undeniable to anyone who'd pay the slightest bit of attention as she walked side by side next to her lover at a downtown movie theater, "Adding a little bit of romance to our lives?" "You could say that." The tone of her voice brought an arrogant smirk to his face. "I just thought that we've been through a lot, and you deserved something special." Those words brought a distinct tone of red through her already blushed cheeks. Simply grabbing his head she followed as he lead the way thought the masses. Twisting and turning through hordes of people, she couldn't help by be a bit curious. "So what are we going to see?" "Pardon?" He played dumb as she continued on. "The movie, what movie are we going to see?" "It's a surprise." He stated quickly and simply as they made it to a wall, while not the quietest place at least gave them a bit of privacy. Not like anyone was paying the slightest bit of attention anyway. Despite his outgoing and public persona, Jimmy liked to keep personal things... personal. He wasn't the type to kiss and tell or give public displays of affection. Perhaps that's why Vivica was caught so off-guard when he leaned over and kissed her on the lips. Pulling away she was on cloud nine again. Finally through all the problems they'd been through with Jimmy's now not-so-secret heroine addiction, the haze was beginning to lift on happily ever after. He grabbed onto her hands and simply looked into her beautiful eyes. He could see his reflection in the most brilliant of blues, like he belonged there. "I love y..." Vivica couldn't help but try to vocalize her emotions before Jimmy jerked his hands away. A violent coughing fit started and the smile that once dawned her beautiful face turned into a glance of worry as Jimmy turned his back. "Are you okay?" "Yeah." He stated, keeping his back to Vivica. "I'm fine." "That didn't sound fine..." Vivica walked up from behind and wrapped her arm around his waist before Jimmy pulled away again. "Was that... blood?" "No, it's fine. I just need to step into the bathroom for a second." "Jim..." "Just a second baby, I promise." In just a few steps he disappeared into the crowd of humanity that encompassed the theater. She honestly did her best to stay put like he said, but she was certain she'd seen blood on his hands. If there was something she did know about Jimmy, it was his inability to show weakness. A stubborn, macho facade he always had to have on to let other people know he was immortal... no matter how far from the truth it may be. It was then she decided to go after him. Pushing her way through the maze of impatient Chicagoans, she tried her best to see through the walls of men to keep up with him. It was of no use as people often pushed their way through the line in front of her, blocking any path that she may have had to keep up. Frustrated she lowered her shoulder and burroughed her way back to the outside where she found herself just in front of the door to the Men's Restroom. Looking up at the sign, she bit her lip as she watched men flood out in order to catch the start of their movies. "Excuse me!" She said, jumping out in front of a man before he simply stepped around her and put his arm around a woman just down the hall. Turning around quickly she tried again "Sir, could you do me a big fav..." Her cries were ignored, frustration began to boil over as her lip curled. Pushing a young teenager out of her way, Vivica forced her way into the Men's Restroom, managing to not even as much as garner a wayward stare on her trek inside. Once her heel click on tile floor, she glanced around in awkward silence. Not exactly sure what her next move should be. "Jimmy? Jimmy are you in here?" She stated, grabbing the attention of a now embarrassed older man who was using a urinal not far away from where she stood. He moved a bit to the side, hoping to better obstruct the young woman's view as she looked over towards the sink where a man was bent over and looked to be in pain. "Jimmy!" She spoke before rushing over towards the sink. Touching the man's shoulder, her eyes grew wide as he took a big snort and looked bad at her as dust mixed with blood on his upper lip. Her eyes looked down towards the sink, where a one inch line of powder laid inside of finger smeared blood. "What are you doing?" "You're not supposed to be here." "But I am here, what's going on?" "Just get out." Jimmy snapped, brushing what was left of the powder into the running sink. Looking into his other hand she spotted a small orange bottle with a white cap. "What's in there?" "I told you to leave." "Is that drugs?" Vivica snatched the bottle out of his hand, "Zanex?" "Give it back to me." "I don't think you should have tha..." "I fucking said to give it back!" He screamed before swiping his hand towards hers, forcing her to jump back to avoid contact. "I thought we'd talked about this." "This isn't the time Vivy." "Then when is the time? We've had the time before and you didn't listen to me." "We're in public..." Jimmy gritted between his teeth as he looked around the room, "You're making a scene." "I'm not the one doing drugs." "You don't understand, alright?!" As his voice boomed back and forth between bathroom acoustics, the room began to clear on its own. Whether it was by his voice or just incredible timing, one by one everyone began to leave. "You can't just quit, you have to work your way down." "No, but maybe you should go to rehab. I think you need some help." "I don't need any fucking rehab. We've gone over this!" "Obviously you do! What the fuck do you think was going on in here?" "Don't tell me how to live my fucking life!" "I'm just trying to help you! Can't you see that?!" "You can't help me! You don't get it!" "Yes I can!" Vivica screamed back before tossing the pill bottle into the trash can, "If you just give me the chance! You don't need those Jim..." Her voice is suddenly cut off by the sound of a hard right hand hitting flesh. Falling to the floor in a heap, she never saw it coming. Pushing up to her hands and knees, tears began to run down her cheeks as the taste of blood cleansed her pallet. "DON'T TELL ME WHAT TO DO!" Jimmy demanded. "I'LL FUCKING KILL YOU. YOU HEAR ME?!" "I..." "DON'T YOU EVER TRY TO RUN MY FUCKING LIFE!" "I..." "GET THE FUCK UP." His eyes were wild as he watched his fiancé get up to one knee. Blood ran out of the corner of her lip as she looked back at him, using the paper towel dispenser as a brace. Two men glanced over at the scene unfolding before turning their backs and walking out, almost as if nothing was happening at all. "We're leaving." "I..." Her throat closed before she could speak. "Get the fuck out of here Vivy." His hands were balled into fists and his arms began to shake as she quickly covered the mascara running down her cheeks from his view and left the room with her head down. Ripping the lid off the trash can, he threw paper towels all over the floor before pulling out a small orange pill bottle. Placing it into his pocket, he kicked the trash can as hard as he could, watching as trash flew everywhere and the plastic container rolled across the floor. "God DAMMIT." He grunted on his way out, leaving nothing but trash and his own blood behind as he stormed out of the room. ---- Maybe the reason I'm so reluctant to love is because of how I always end up hurt in the end. Sometimes it's just heartbreak, other times it's a broken arm and three domestic violence charges. I had to be pre-emptive Andy, no matter how badly I wanted to love you I just wasn't ready to give up that much. I'm not sure if I'll ever be ready. ---- "I'm.... I'm.... I'm...." "Just put it down, Jimmy." "...I'm sorry. I'm so sorry." "Don't do it!" BANG. Sitting straight up, her heart pounded hard deep inside her chest. Looking around the room, heavy breaths left her lungs as she glared down at the clock. 5:48am. Putting her hands over her head, the heavy breathing continued as she tried to familiarize herself with her surroundings. Everything seemed so foreign to the eighteen year old girl who'd spent her last two years hopping between run-down apartments and the back seats of cars. A nice big bed inside of a nice big room. A full body mirror next to her own set of chester drawers, one that stood even taller than she did. A closet even. How did she find herself here... KNOCK. KNOCK. KNOCK. Startled, a small squeak escaped her lips as she sped out to the other side of the bed. Her hands shook as a muffled voice spoke to her. "Hello? Miss Valentine? Hello?" An older gentlemen spoke from outside the door. Even though it wasn't familiar it certainly didn't sound threatening. "...Yes?" "Are you okay, Miss Valentine?" "I'm... fine. Why do you ask?" "I heard you scream. Do you mind if I come in?" Hesitant, she looked at her reflection in the mirror across the room. Her now completely faded pink hair dye made her blonde hair look dirty and matted in the moonlight. Wearing nothing but a sky blue cami and a pair of purple boyshorts, she was certainly not presentable. "Uhhh...." She responded without really responding. "I'm coming in." "...No!" Her request was too late as the man had already come inside. His face was that of a gentle man, not much taller than herself. The expression he wore showed great concern as he looked back at the young woman, clutching the comforter on her bed as a means of covering herself up. "Nightmares, is it?" He spoke, taking a few steps forward towards the bed that stood between himself and Miss Valentine. She wanted so badly to tell him everything was fine. To tell him that he just needed to turn around and leave. "Yes." Was the word that escaped her lips instead. "Every night since." "Not surprising. It's a lot for a girl your age to take in." He spoke, "I'm sure this place isn't very comfortable for you either. Maybe it's best that we contact your parents and you can go back with them." "No." She quickly responded, "I'm fine here." "Even so, we should probably be making arrangements for down the road, Miss Valentine." His voice was relaxing, so much so that she could feel herself stop shaking from the trauma, "Mister Zenith is a busy, busy man. He can't possibly keep you here for much longer." "I understand, it's just..." She stuttered for a moment, "... I don't even know your name." "My name is Jacques." He answered, "I'm Mister Zenith's personal assistant." "Thank you, for putting me up here. I couldn't stay there. Not in that apartment." "It's no problem." Jacques responded with a smile, "I understand. I had a similar experience when I was much younger, and so did Mister Zenith. After James didn't show up to work and we'd found out what happened, we knew how hard it must've been on you. The least we could do is give you a place to stay." "There was blood everywhere." Vivica spoke in spurts, her heart was still pounding so hard, "All over the floor. The wall. The furniture. Me." She looked down at her own hands, almost as if checking to make sure it was all gone. "I just screamed and screamed." She sputtered, "It's all I could do. I tried to stop him. I did. Why wouldn't he listen to me Jacques? Why didn't he just put down the gun?" "I don't know Miss Valentine." Jacques reassured her, "He's the only one who really knows." "I love him." Tears began to well up in her eyes, "I do. I wish it was really just a nightmare." "Awww, honey I know. I do too." Jacques walks around the bed as the tears began to roll down her cheeks. Drops turn into streams as she loses control. Jacques puts his arm around her and she buries her head into his chest. Screaming out for answers Jacques just pats her on the back and lets her get it all out, "The hardest part is all over. You're with friends now." ---- I still don't know if I could look you in the eyes, even today. Just like Jimmy you were everything I ever wanted to be. I watched you turn into everything that I thought for so long I'd already become. I looked into your eyes and I saw the same thing I saw when I was a teenager, the same feeling I got when I looked into his eyes. I was ridiculously in love with you. The feeling reminded me of the past and all the demons I'd been keeping hidden deep down inside. Those feelings were hateful, and they were stronger than ever. He took all my love away from me and treated me like shit, but you'd take far more from that away from me, Andy. Even after I struck first you still managed to take everything from me. EVERYTHING. My fans, my title, my career, my entire life has changed because of you. You fucking did this to me, and not a day goes by where I don't wonder how things would be different if I never fell for you. Every day when I walk through Zenith Manor, surrounded by everything that I came to GCW to get away from, I remember that you were the one who put me here. Every day when I'm forced to look into the eyes of the antichrist, I remember the chill that runs down my spine wouldn't be there if it wasn't for Andy Murray. Every day I look into the mirror in absolute disgust of everything that I am, and remember I didn't used to be this way... before you. The hardest part of all is how I'm the only one who feels the way I do. After everything I've lost, I'm forced to watch at WorldWide as those fans worship the fucking ground you walk on, Andy. They love you almost as much as I loved you. They love you far more than they ever loved me. That alone gives me every right in the world to step into the ring against you at WorldWide and end your career for them all to see. To take my life back and to prove that they should've given the love that they gave you. To prove to the world that I have worth. As much joy as that would bring to me... I can't do it. I can't do it because when I look into your eyes, I want to despise with you every single ounce of my being but there is one tiny thing holding me back. One single thing that hurts so much more than any other thing you've done to me... I... can't. I just can't.
