Do Not Enter: The End

29 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/29/10 The building is massive. Aron does not recall this house being this big and palatial. This could be one of the nicest houses around if someone actually gave a damn about its upkeep. Aron stood outside the Belmont House of Torture in the thick shrubs and trees surrounding the place. He looks up at the massive structure in awe. He’s never killed someone that lives in such an awesome place before. He’s also never killed someone for this large sum of people he received from the good doctor. “Kill him than burn the body,” was Landis’ request. Easy enough right? Aron begins to scope the building, each window waiting for a sign of movement anywhere. He’s heard the horror stories about Bryan O’Neil, and it’s not that he’s afraid, but he doesn’t necessarily want to get in close proximity with him either. The man is a giant with a ferocious temper and an insatiable bloodlust. The further away he was the better the odds were of him surviving and cashing in on the rest of his fortune. A light came on in the window at the very top of the house. The eaves illuminated as well as a shadow making its way across the window. Aron smiled from ear to ear. The giant was home, now it’s time to go to work. He pulls out his HK-416 with an AAC silencer and begins his wait. He knows he is home, now it’s time for him to make one mistake and he will never realize a bullet hit him between the eyes. This time tomorrow Aron will be a millionaire, Landis will be a hero, and O’Neil will be only in your nightmares. Landis takes a swig from his bottle of beer and stares back at the wall. His other hand is slowly patting the arm rest of the couch like bongo drums while his other grips the beer bottle. He looks at the painting on his wall, blinking only when necessary. The painting is a combination of swirls and wild brush strokes on a plain canvas. It was dirt cheap yet Landis loves it. He has told people at numerous gatherings at his condo that he often stares into the canvas to avoid all of life’s stress. He feels “free” in this room. So much is going through his head that it’s difficult to pick apart each thing and dissect it. He does know that he just gave Aron all of his money he had saved to take care of this problem. He knew he should have taken care of this problem when he had the chance, now it’s cost people their lives, and Landis feels he is the one to blame. If he could only go back to that one day….. The citizens of Belmont’s eyes are all at the smoke stack waiting to know for certain that their very own town ghost is now dead and will only live in their memories, not at their doorstep. When the billow of smoke erupted from chimney the crowd erupted. Fireworks were set off, cheers and chants were sang and some even started dancing. The entire crowd started to chant in unison, “The Beast Is Dead!” Meanwhile inside the death chamber Landis stares into the eyes of Bryan O’Neil. These eyes are so familiar, yet so distant. He never thought he’d ever been in such a small room with one of the most notorious killers of all time. Most would be dead and gutted by now, but not Landis. O’Neil’s eyes are large and dark. His breathing is heavy and his body is shaking. He starts to move towards Landis, who is pinned between the oven and this beast. O’Neil is blocking the exit to the front, but perhaps the exit to the back if he can just muster up the power to do so. Landis felt he was staring into death’s eyes as Bryan O’Neil, blood oozing from the wound, hair stringy and dry hanging in his face makes his way towards him. Landis put his hand out, “Stop Bryan! Your murdering spree is done here!” The shout sends a jolt through Bryan. The beast is scared of Landis, for some reason Landis has caused this sick murdering freak to fear someone/something. Bryan points towards the rear exit and nods. “Bryan, if I let you go..” Tears begin to fall from Landis’ face, “…You must never come back here.” Bryan lifts up his hands and looks at them, than back at Landis. O’Neil nods, a tear falling from his eye. “I hate you Bryan!” Bryan’s eyes close as tears begin to fall down his cheeks. He nods again, and walks slowly towards the back, slowly into oblivion. As O’Neil exit’s the small death chamber, Landis lifts his hands, locks them into one large fist and begins to pound to the stretcher, screaming at the top of his lungs. The physical exertion causes him to fall pulling the stretcher down on top of him. His eyes are welled with tears, his breathe is heavy, and his nervous system is shaking like a leaf. No one must ever know….He throws the stretcher into the fire, seconds later the crowd erupts. Staring back into the canvas , Landis remembers the sound of the stretcher burning inside the oven. The ovation of the crowd as the smoke billows into the air, and the tiny splatters of blood droplets on the ground as Bryan O’Neil exited the chamber into a life of freedom. He never ever thought this day would come. He honestly felt that Bryan O’Neil would vanish into nothingness, never to be heard from again. The murders and the tearing apart of families was never suppose to happen again. Why did O’Neil return? Was it for Landis himself? Or worst yet, was it to reveal the one secret that has haunted Dr. Landis his entire life, the one secret that would destroy not only his career and life, but destroy the very town he has committed to serving. It has been nearly two hours since the light flicked on in the room at the very top of the house. Aron has perched himself on this little mound, eye staring through the sight of his HK-416 waiting for the slightest bit of movement. Unfortunately the movement has been nonexistent. Aron begins to grow frustrated, as the clock starts to strike 1 am. Where the fuck is he is the only thing running through his brain. Two hours and nothing? Is he sleeping? Aron begins to contemplate what his next move will be? Does he stay and wait some more or does he go to the house and attempt to break in? Aron hates when shit doesn’t go as planned. The nerves begin to get to Aron as the time seems to fly on by. He stands up, puts his gun in front of him and slowly makes his way towards the house. He feels the thud against his head and feels his legs give out from underneath him. Aron falls to the cold hard ground and immediately puts his hand to his head. It’s wet and warm, someone or something busted him open. He rolls to his back and staring down on him was this ghost. White and black painted face, long black hair smiling down on him. Aron quickly catches himself and starts to search for his gun. The butt hits his head slightly, he reaches for it but it’s yanked from his grasp. Standing above him holding the weapon is this ghost. “Looking for something?” The beast booms down at him. Aron swallows hard and just stares up at O’Neil. “You can have it. I’ll just get up and leave. You’ll never see me again.” There was no sign of fear or wavering in his voice. He almost seemed calm and serene, as if he feels confident that he can talk his way out of this situation. Like a quick jab, the butt of the gun landed between Aron’s eyes busting him open. He instinctively covers his face as the blood begins to ooze between his fingers. Aron yelps in pain. “Look at me!” Aron pulls his hands from his face. His hands begin to tremble as the fear from the present situation starts to rear its ugly head. “Why are you here?” Aron is silent for a second, coming up with a lie on the spot has never been something he’s been good at. He touches the tender spot between his eyes and looks up at the monster. “To kill you.” O’Neil laughs. “Well looks like you got yourself in a predicament here don’t you? Why kill me?” “I was paid a large sum of money to come here and kill you. To rid this town of you.” Once again another laugh. “Wow it’s gotten that bad that the police have paid you to kill me?” “Not the police. Dr. Landis.” The name froze O’Neil. He was still staring down at Aron, but almost as if he was staring right through him. His eyes became dark, and his teeth started to clench in anger. He squats down, gets a little closer to Aron. “Well you failed!” Bryan lifted the gun and began to repeatedly drop the butt onto Aron’s face. The first four or five were absolutely painful. Aron felt his nose break, teeth shatter, and his cheekbone being crushed. One popped his eyeball like an egg under a hammer. He felt no more after a while as he slipped into a blackness. When a fragment of his nose and his face plate went into his brain, Aron was well unconscious by that time. For the first time in weeks Dr. Landis woke up feeling refreshed. He sat up, looked at the clock that read 530am and he smiled. The deed was obviously done and now he can go on with his life. He took a shower and was even whistling a short little unintelligible tune to himself as he rid himself of the muck from a deep night’s sleep. After his shower he stood in front of the mirror and began to brush his teeth. Whatever tune he was whistling in the shower apparently gave Landis some dancing shoes. He began to sway back and forth, followed by some awful jig as he scrubbed his teeth. He goes into his room again and opens the curtain. The sun was rising! There was not a sign of a cloud in the sky! Another sure sign that the beast is FINALLY dead. He stares at the countryside from his 5th floor condo and smiles. He has never felt so good in all of his life. The country has never looked so beautiful in all his life. Sure the tree’s were dying, and all signs of green are turning orange and brown, but nothing can ever ruin his spirits, not today, not never…. Bryan O’Neil sits at the window in his attic. He holds the skull of Aron in his left bloodied hand. He begins to toss the head back and forth from his left to his right and back to the left again, all without taking his eyes from the hillside. The O’Neil/Bathory house was on the highest hill in all of Belmont. From the attic and roof you can see the entire town and on a clear day the channel. Bryan has not been in this attic since he was a little boy. It was the same day he caught his father molesting his younger brother Jason. 5 years later Bryan would send a hay fork square through his father’s chest. So many memories, none of them good. He grits his teeth and tosses the head overhand like a pitcher right through the glass to the ground below. He stands up, looks out the broken window and mutters to himself, “This ends today.” The elevator door opens with a ding, a rather happy and sing-songy kind of ding today according to Landis. He walks out into the hallway and begins to whistle passed the nurses station, nodding and smiling at all of them on his way in. He makes his way down the hallway, comes to a door, unlocks it with his key and enters another hallway. He keeps his out of tune song going as he makes it to his office, unlocks it and steps inside. He takes a deep breathe, exhales and giggles like a little girl. He looks at his phone and pushes the message button. The machine says he has two messages. “Dr. where have you been lately. I miss you. I’ve got big plans for you tonight.” Landis laughs and realizes that he totally forgot about Ami these past few weeks. He made himself a note to give her a call later today. He could really use some touchy-touchy feely-feely tonight. The phone beeps. “New Message: Received 4:38 AM: Hey Jason….” The name and the voice stopped Landis dead in his tracks. He turns his head towards the message machine and sits down to get a closer listen. His heart begins to pound, and his throat tightens up. “…..Spending all of our inheritance to have me killed. What would father think?” Landis keels over and vomits all over the floor. Had he been standing up he might have crumbled to the ground because his legs and the rest of his body felt like jello. “…..Don’t you think it’s about time we sat down and talked. Man to man……brother to brother….” With nothing left in his system the vomiting quickly turns to dry heaving. His eyes water as his face strains with each thrust of his guts only to release spittle and perhaps a little bile. “……No weapons, no police, just us. I’m at home…waiting.” The phone clicked as Landis continued his violent dry heaving spell. When it ends he sits back in his chair staring into the ceiling trying to catch himself, trying to end the wave of dizziness and nausea that has succumbed him. He rests his head on his desk and stares at the message machine. After he regains his bearings Landis stands up, and mutters to himself, “This ends today.” The entire day Landis was at wits end. He almost forgot to remove rags from a patient during surgery, a broken leg had to be resplinted because of his own error. His entire staff knew something was wrong, but to avoid what happened last week they kept it all to themselves. Dr. Landis has not been the same since that Ellen girl died. Some doctors do have moments where losing a certain patient can affect them more than others. That girl must have been the same for him. Landis blasts open his office door, slams it shut and falls to his knees. He runs his hands through his hair and looks at the clock. 830pm. By far the longest day of his life. He could not think straight. All he kept hearing was that voice booming the name of “Jason” into his message machine. As he paces the office he contemplates his next move. Bryan O’Neil told him to come see him? Landis has been the only person thus far that has survived Bryan O’Neil, but can lightning strike the same spot twice? Landis tears open the bottom drawer of his desk, tosses papers about and finds his 9mm handgun. He checks it, still loaded. He puts it in the waist of his pants and says to himself, “Let’s see you stop a bullet motherfucker” On the way to the house Landis stops at the local gas station and fills up 2 containers. As he stands there and fills up the containers the townsfolk wave at him. He’s still revered and loved here in Belmont. He smiles as he thinks about how loved he will be in just a matter of hours as he stands outside the most sadistic structure on earth as it burns to the ground. The house seems all to familiar to him. He stands outside it staring up at the ominous structure as if he’s been here before. As if he’s survived some atrocities in this house that would make most people commit suicide at a young age. He knows this place, and as he stands there he feels an overwhelming pang that this place knows he’s here, and it knows he’s here to destroy it. Landis walks up to the front porch of this dilapidated old building and touches the door handle. He slowly turns it, and the door opens without a struggle. He steps inside, and the stench immediately gets to him. He turns his head and coughs a gagging cough, followed by spitting onto the front porch. He turns back and catches his composure and makes his way back into the house. He hears a crackle and turns to his right which is the front room. The fireplace is lit, a small fire, but enough to make this house of torture cozy if you will. Sitting in a chair is the Demon of The Fall, The Lunatic, Necrobutcher, Bryan Mayhem, and a plethora of other nicknames staring right at him. “Jason, I am so glad you came. Please sit.” The giant motions with his hands to one of the couches spread about the house. “Stop calling me Jason. And I am perfectly fine standing.” “Suit yourself Jason.” “Stop with the Jason shit!” O’Neil moves his head, a small crack is heard. “I only know you as Jason. I don’t know this new moniker you have bestowed upon yourself.” “I AM NOT JASON!!!! I have no clue what you are talking about. Why did you call me here?” Landis begins to clench his fists, as sweat begins to form on his brow. “A violent side of Jason. That’s something I never knew existed. I recall a Jason that would cry when you found an injured bird in the field. The very same boy that would never fight for himself. Always needed his big….” “I AM NOT JASON!” O’Neil stands up so quickly that his chair tips backwards. “You are Jason Francis O’Neil!! My little brother!” The full name made Landis drop to his knees. He begins to sob heavily. Tears run down his face splattering on the hardwood floors. He starts to punch the floors, moaning and crying. He looks up at his older brother who is still standing in the same spot he was. “How did you know?” Bryan turns his back and walks towards the fire. On the mantel is a picture, a picture of Jason and Bryan hugging each other at a young age. He grabs it, wipes off the frame with his thumb and walks towards Jason. He squats down, and hands it to him. “The day I was suppose to be executed, I looked into your eyes. You can do anything to change your appearance, but your eyes, the very same eyes in that picture, they can never change. Why Jason?” Landis(Jason) pulls himself to a sitting position and looks at the picture. This entire moment seems like a giant dream. His secret has been revealed. “Bryan, after you killed our mother and they found her body in the woods mangled and eviscerated, I realized I could not be your blood. I could not be the son of a satanic child molester and a whore. So I left.” Bryan points at his brother, “But why this? Why the change?” “Before I left I found out where the money was hidden. I overheard mom and dad talking about it years prior. So after you killed dad I thought things would get better. But for some sick fucking reason you decided more blood was more fun.” Bryan says nothing, he just stares at his brother as the story continues. “ You killed mother! The only person that made everything right! You killed her!” Bryan shot out, “She was a diseased whore! She brought men into our house, fucked them, took their money and left them diseased. How was everything better?” “Goddamnit Bryan! Don’t you remember the fucking life we lived? Don’t you remember waking up daily wondering if our father was going to rape us and than beat us? Wondering if his breathe would smell like beer? No matter what, I was always able to go to mother. She loved me, hugged me, and made me feel fucking important!” Tears began to fall down Landis(Jason)’s face. He turns around and punches a wall, sending his hand through the lathe. “Jason look at me!” Landis(Jason) turns back, facing his brother, almost eye to eye for the first time in over 20 years. “She never helped us Jason. She let that sick motherfucker do what he wanted with us. She was far from perfect. She was a DISEASED WHORE!!” Instinctively Landis(Jason) lashes out and slaps his brother across the face. He backs up and looks at his hand and notices the paint smear from the corpse paint all over Bryan’s face. 20 minutes ago Landis(Jason) would have never thought about slapping this beast, but now all the fear is gone. Years of pent up anger and frustration are all coming to the surface. Bryan puts his hand to his lip, touches it, pulls it away and looks at the blood. He smiles, “I am impressed with the balls you’ve grown over the years.” Still looking at the paint, “Why all this bullshit get up? Painted face, long black hair?” “That’s like the pot calling the kettle black little brother. I didn’t change my entire being. I paint my face just for a little fun. You on the other hand, you fucking coward, changed your name and identity. How’d you do it?” Landis(Jason) starts to pace. “I used the money stashed away downstairs and got, at the time, the most high tech plastic surgery on earth. Put myself through medical school, and viola here I am.” “Interesting.” Both men stand there silent. Both reluctant to move, because the tension has started to increase in the room to what could be a violent ending when Bryan puts his hands to head and starts to laugh. The laugh becomes maniacal and almost out of control. “What the fuck can be so funny?” “You let me live even after you knew I killed your daughter! Now I had no clue up until I figured out that this Landis fuck was you and that she was your daughter. Goddamn, mom, dad, and daughter. I am wiping out all of your loved ones aren’t I?” Landis(Jason) stands there, mouth agape, hands trembling. Up until now, the whole concept of his daughter being killed by his own brother was hidden away in the back of his head. He never knew why this was, because this horror all started so many years ago because of her death. “Doesn’t that make us the last two remaining members of our happy little family Jason?” Landis(Jason), head down, tears flowing, just nods. “Well..that leaves me the last living member!” It all happened so fast that it was remarkable that it even happened the way it did. Bryan reaches behind him, pulls out a knife and rushes Landis(Jason). Landis(Jason) reacts with action of his own as his lifts his shirt and pulls the gun out and fires it straight into the chest of his brother. The impact hits Bryan like a ton of bricks and sends him back a few steps. He coughs violently and drops to his knees and puts his hand to his chest. Blood seeps through his fingers as he falls to one arm and both knees. He winces in pain and looks up at his brother who is still holding the weapon, smoke billowing out of the barrel. “Bryan, I should have done this years ago.” Landis(Jason) fires one more square into the chest of his brother that drops him to the ground. The body convulses and shakes for a second than lies still in a pool of blood. Landis(Jason) drops to his knees and lets the emotions fly. His cries turn to sobs, his shaking turns to clenched fists, but most importantly, his anger turned to fury, and his fury killed the monster of Belmont. He stands up, looks at the body of his dead brother, blood still pouring from him, and walks to his car. He opens the trunk and pulls out the containers of gas. He begins to pour it all around the outside of the house, when he empties the container he just tosses it aside and heads back inside. His brother is still lying on the floor, because watching enough horror movies in his day, Landis(Jason) was a little nervous of the body no longer being there. He quickly soaks his brother and the rest of the house with gas and runs outside. He strikes a match and stares at it. “It ends today,” he muttered to himself as he throws it inside the door. The fire starts slow, but than goes up like a tinder box. Landis(Jason) backs away quickly staring at the very home he grew up in burn to the ground. Landis(Jason) lights a cigarette and continues to stare as the sirens begin to wail from town. No one will put this out, they can finally rest assure that the haunted house on the hill is destroyed, and so are all of its horrors. As he watches, a smile creeps into his face. Once again no one will ever know his secret, and once again he can go back to a normal life. Landis(Jason) walks towards his car, and sits inside, still smiling. He takes one final look at his childhood home(hell) and starts the car. He mutters to himself, “It ended today.” It certainly did because he only felt the original slice of the blade across his throat than his warm blood fall down his chest. The last thing Landis(Jason) will ever see is in the rearview mirror that awful painted face, smiling..He’s killing his brother and fucking smiling!!
