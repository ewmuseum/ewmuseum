Facing Reality

2 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/02/2007 The wheels are well and truly in motion now. In a few short days Jimmy Thunder will make his return to professional wrestling against Stephen Adams. In a time where he should be pumped for his match, Jimmy in stark contrast is feeling rather melancholy about the whole situation. Wrestling again is something he hadn’t accepted quite easily, it was something he had no choice in. His biggest fear was not facing the fans and the media but what Jimmy was terrified with the most was facing one simple question, “what happened to your money, Jimmy?” How does a man explain a failure in his life? A failure he had no control over. For a man who fiercely guarded his privacy, Jimmy’s public fall from grace was the hardest part to deal with. Apart from the untimely death of his wife, this is by far the darkest moment in his life. He took solace in the motto that he firmly lived by, “what ever doesn’t kill a man will only make him stronger.” That motto is all well and good, but one huge hurdle waited for Jimmy to climb, answering that dreaded question publicly. Jimmy walks into his dark, state of the art gym late one night during several failed attempts to go to sleep. His foot steps echoed through out the gymnasium as Jimmy walked up to the photographs hanging up on a lone lit up wall. With a smile on his face, Jimmy reminisces on the epic battles that made him a household name across America. Legendary matches with guys like Tony Aielli, Maher Reims, and his own brother-in-law, Theodoric Solomon. “We had some great times.” Jimmy instantly turned around when he heard an unexpected voice at the door way of the gym. “Who’s that?” Jimmy asked with a slight curious panic tone in his voice as the dark night disguises the mystery man from Jimmy. The mystery man steps slowly forward into the moonlight to reveal himself as Jimmy’s brother-in-law, Theodoric Solomon. ? "Theo?" Jimmy asked, just for his own confirmation. "You really have got yourself in a jam, haven't you?" Theo said with a touch of sarcasm. “I’m ok; I’ll get myself out of this mess. I’ve got a contract with Global Championship Wrestling.” Jimmy announced with conviction. “So I noticed.” Theo replied as he started walking slowly towards Jimmy. “What have you got planned?” Theo asks. “First things first, I need to make enough money to get myself out of this hole that I’m in.” Jimmy Answered. “You know you could have come to me.” Theo said with sympathy. “You didn’t have to go back to wrestling.” He added. “Thanks mate, this is my problem, and I’ll get my self out of it, my way. Always have and always will.” Jimmy said sternly. The two took a brief moment to think about what they were about to say next. Theo knew that he was walking on shaky ground by offering Jimmy a hand out. Jimmy on the other hand felt embarrassed that he had been offered a hand out by Theo. The moment had become rather awkward for both; the room had become so silent that you could hear a star shooting across the cold black sky above the gym. “What brings you here?” Jimmy asks as he tries to take the subject off himself. “Real estate, my friend, real estate.” Theo replies with a boost of enthusiasm that colours the situation somewhat. “Idaho isn’t the best place to invest money.” Jimmy informed him with a touch of curiosity behind his statement. “I’m not looking to invest, I’m looking to relocate.” Announced Theo. Now if Jimmy had the rare ability to raise one eyebrow, this would be the perfect time to do it. For the longest time, Jimmy had tried to convince Theo to leave North Carolina and settle his family in the quiet, open spaces Idaho has to offer. Jimmy had ridden through Idaho on his Harley Davidson after a DWA event in Seattle en route to Las Vegas. The large empty lands surrounded by picturesque mountains appealed to Jimmy so much so that he returned the following week and without a second thought, he bought a ranch style home that sat on one hundred acres of land for his son to enjoy. Still not convinced with Theo’s uncharacteristic motives, Jimmy asks “Why here, why now?” “You’ve been raving about this place for years.” Theo says with a smile. “If you must know, I’ll tell you, but on one condition.” Theo says as his mood suddenly turns serious. “What’s that?” Jimmy replies, giving Theo his full attention. “We go inside and talk about this over a cold beer.” He says with a smile? Jimmy smiles back and nods before the two walk back inside the house. If there was anyone that Jimmy could talk to about his financial disaster with, its Theo. The two have been best friends for several years, both been taken to hell and back with their own respective disasters. No matter what each other faced, the two had been by each other’s side. The two tossed back several cold ales before Theo retired to his bedroom. Jimmy, on the other hand, sat in his living room and watched a World Wide re-run on cable. Jimmy feels a sharp pain in his neck which wakes him from his unexpected slumber. He reaches around his stiff neck and starts to rub it slight in an attempt to soothe the pain caused by sleeping the wrong way. Sitting up right again and continuing to rub his neck, the realization sets in that he has one last day left at home before he has to fly out to Pittsburg for his debut match on World Wide 60 against Stephen Adams, who is largely unknown to him. Even though Jimmy has been in the wrestling business for several years now, he can’t help but feel like he did when he debuted for WFW in his first match ever. He hadn’t known anyone with in the company and he was very much a loner in a promotion that could eat you up, if you showed any sign of weakness. In the past, Jimmy was the face of the promotion he appeared at, and was treated as such. Not at GCW, this is a company where Jimmy has to prove to everyone he is as good his reputation makes him out to be. Here at GCW, Jimmy won’t be facing opponents who buckle under the sheer pressure he puts them under. This isn’t a haven for would be jobbers, who are all bark yet have no bite. Make no mistake, Jimmy knows too well he is in a promotion where the big boys play, and only the strong survive. Theo walked out of his room and straight for the kitchen. He wasn’t interested in dealing with anything before getting a hot cup of coffee in his hands. Meanwhile, Jimmy slowly lifted his tense body from the evil couch that was behind the temporary aches and pains anyone would get from sleeping on a couch. Jimmy slowly shuffled his feet along the cold wooden floor that lead into the kitchen where he found Theo, who was one step ahead of him and already boiling the water for their much needed coffee. “How did you sleep?” Jimmy asked. “Evidently a lot better than you.” Theo replied with a smirk. “Believe it or not but I dozed off watching a re-run of World Wide last night.” Jimmy said as he started to move his shoulder in circular motions, to try and work the tightness out. “HA HA HA!!! I could believe that. I don't watch re-runs of anything” Theo said as he laughed. “What have you got planned for today?” Theo asked. “I have to organise my flight to Pittsburg.” Jimmy answered with a serious tone. “Have you got enough to cover it?” Theo asked as he looked Jimmy in the eye. “I’m going to sell the Harley, which should keep me afloat for a while till I start getting some money in from GCW.” Jimmy answered as his embarrassment forced him to break eye contact with Theo. “How about I just lend you the money?” Theo said as he leaned back on the wooden kitchen bench and folding his arms. “Just enough to give you some breathing space.” He continued to ask. “Thanks Theo, but no thanks.” Jimmy instantly retorted. “Ok. How much then?” Theo asked with a determined tone. Caught off guard with Theo’s erratic question, Jimmy’s face contorts with his confusion and asks “How much for what?” “For the Harley.” Theo replied. “I want to buy it.” Theo declared with conviction. “But you don’t ride Theo.” Jimmy said, still with a thick sense of confusion running through his head. Scrambling for an answer, Jimmy adds “I’m not sure, I really haven’t thought about a price yet.” “Well start thinking because you don’t have much time to mess around with.” Theo informed him with some frustration kicked in, mainly because he hated Jimmy’s stubbornness. Theo could never understand why it was so hard for Jimmy to accept any help. Worried that the next thing he says might spark some unnecessary tension, Theo storms off to his room. Jimmy could tell that even though his financial status is diabolical, his private life is heading down the same path. It’s hard enough being down and out because nobody really wants to know about people down on their luck, but turning people away who care about you is just criminal. This is the time Jimmy needed Theo the most and here he is, pushing him away. This is the time where Jimmy should just swallow his pride and let Theo help him. The thing Jimmy now realizes is that he was at his strongest when he and Theo were standing by each other’s side, in and out of the ring. One thing Jimmy knows for sure, he can’t leave for Pittsburg with this happening between him and Theo. He has to make things right otherwise his mind will be anywhere but the match with Stephen Adams. With an idea popping into his head, Jimmy launches himself down his hallway, straight towards Theo’s door where he knocks on it with some authority. “Theo?” Jimmy calls out before opening his door. “Come in.” Theo replied in a dull tone. Jimmy flings the door open and walks in before declaring “I’ve got a great idea.” “What’s that?” Theo asks while looking for something in his bag. “Come with me to Pittsburg.” Jimmy asks with his enthusiasm level suddenly shooting through the roof. “I bet I could get you a contract with GCW. Imagine us reuniting on-screen, in the ring. That would really give the media something to talk about.” Jimmy ended with as he watched Theo continuing to search his bag while he eagerly waited for Theo to give him the response he was hoping for. “Thanks for the invite but there is no way in hell I’m coming back to wrestling. Just forget about it Jimmy, I’m sorry.” Theo answered sternly. Jimmy’s enthusiasm dropped like a brick from a house roof before saying “yeah, so am I.” With that, Jimmy left the room knowing that his brief dream of Theo returning to wrestling with him was over before it started. Jimmy arrives at Pittsburgh International Airport and as Jimmy leaves the baggage carousel, a reporter from an independent wrestling magazine approaches Jimmy who keeps walking. "Jimmy, I'm Murray Wilson from Wrestling Week" the young reporter says as he tries to keep up with Jimmy's long strides. Jimmy just looks at him and keeps walking without saying a word to the determined journalist. "Jimmy, people in the wrestling world are asking questions about your comeback and no-one really has a straight answer. Did you comeback for financial reasons or did you comeback to climb the mountain again? Murray asks with determination but gets no response from Jimmy who continues to look straight ahead. This is the moment that Jimmy was dreading, a reporter asking about his financial position. As much as Jimmy wants to cut loose on the young reporter, he manages to keep his cool. "Ok, who's first on the Jimmy Thunder comeback hit list?" Asks Murray as he now seems to be clutching at straws for an answer from Jimmy, who then stops and looks straight and sternly at Murray. "There's no-one I want to get more in this world than the IRS. I'm not the first person they've raped money from and I wont be the last. A man works his ass off to provide a better future for himself and his family only to have the government take it all in foul swoop. Targeting one man who wants to provide a better living for himself is nothing compared to targeting a government organisation to take back what's mine." Jimmy says with conviction and fire in his eyes before walking away and leaving Murray standing still and dumbfounded by Jimmy's outburst.
