Date: Day before tomorrow It wasn’t the greatest hotel in the world, but it was pretty good. The type of place where they’d help you with the bags up to your room, but anyone could get a room, assuming they were on a big enough expense account. Overall, it was clean, fairly comfortable, and it had nearly any luxury you could want, which is always a bonus. Right now Alex was outside with Sheena, they were talking to the bellhop about the bags, and the valet about parking the car. While he stood by the front desk, getting the rooms. It was something they never really told you about lackeys, well they never really told you anything, there wasn’t a manual for god’s sake. Still it was one of those things you just had to pick up, these people might be on your payroll, but it was in extremely poor taste to make them pay for their own rooms. Chances are on what you pay them they really can’t afford it, but they’re obligated to stay close at hand, because what’s the point of a lackey if they weren’t at your beck and call? So you pay for their rooms, or at least a room for them, if you’re really an asshole you make them all cram into one room, of course that’s only if you’ve got a wandering band of idiots following you, when you’ve just got two, a man and a woman, it should be easier. The problem he faced right now was a difficult one, two rooms should be all he needed. It was just an unspoken assumption that Sheena would be staying in Michael’s room, they were after all together, or were they? He still didn’t know really, he’d tried to bring it up a couple of times, but never ended up succeeding, it’s not that she wouldn’t listen, he just couldn’t bring himself to actually ask. It was too strange a question, too awkward. Deciding that two rooms was the only way to go, he handed over the credit card and waiting for the front desk clerk to give him the room keys. He should’ve made an appointment in advance, so he’d get the best rooms available, but he couldn’t figure out how many rooms to get, so he just put it off. Either way there was a vacancy, he just had to pay a little more, oh well, life was tough sometimes. Looking at the clerk, it was clear the man expected the card to come up fake, or stolen, he didn’t seem impressed by the way Stevens looked, in fact he barely seemed to tolerate it. You usually don’t walk into what must have been a three star hotel wearing bright orange parachute pants, and a bowling shirt that said “Bob” on the chest, it just didn’t happen normally. Still it had to be done, the most outward sign of the changing way Stevens perceived himself, going back to his punk roots so to speak. It still brought a smile to his face when he say the clerk’s face when the card went through, if nothing else that was worth the entire trip. Hearing the rumble of the luggage cart, and the sounds of multiple footsteps, Stevens turned to see Sheena, and Alex entering, followed by a bellboy with gold painted cart loaded down. He wondered and not for the first time how Sheena managed to pack so many bags, considering for the most part she’d wear whatever she came across at a thrift store that day, oh well the mysteries and vagaries of womankind he supposed. Alex looked a little anxious, and definitely grateful to be stretching his legs after driving for an extended period of time, guess the little stop at the gas station hadn’t helped him much, besides if he sat too long his knee started bothering him, old wound Stevens probably should’ve remembered, but hadn’t until now. Oh well, chalk one more up on his asshole tally, even if it was unintentional. Alex strode forward quickly, stopping just short of the desk. He looked hopeful, excited, strange, he should’ve been tired, but he looked wide awake. Maybe he was using again, heh, pipe dream, and Stevens knew it. The guy genuinely loved being clean, and having talked albeit briefly with the big man about his past problems with addiction he couldn’t see Alex backsliding. “You guys have a gym right?” The desk clerk looked over the big man, up and down. Disgust registered deep behind his eyes, barely visible, you’d only catch it if you were really looking for it. Clearly he was from a generation that didn’t approve of long hair on men, and he was probably just a little jealous of the man’s physique which thanks to an extremely tight shirt was clearly visible. “Yes sir, we do.” “Hours?” “Always open sir, for your convenience.” “Great, I’ll find it myself.” Grabbing a beaten up old gym bag off the cart, he started off to wander. That’d be fine, he’d make his way up to his room eventually, whenever he was ready. Besides with him gone, Stevens would have a chance to talk to Sheena alone, and since he’d already promised himself to speak to both privately if at all possible, this was a golden opportunity. The desk clerk handed over two plastic cards, with room numbers printed across them. The little magnetic strip worked like a credit card, all the information you could possibly need to unlock your room. Modern technology at it’s finest. He then instructed the bellboy to show the lady and gentleman to their rooms, that made him and Sheena smile, it wasn’t often they were called by such dignified names, not to mention the way the clerks mouth puckered like he’d just eaten the world’s sourest lemon, as he said the words, was classic. The two followed the bellboy, into the elevator, and to the rooms, which thankfully were adjacent. Separate spaces for separate lives, but still close enough that you could be in the other room in a matter of a few seconds, excellent. It was quickly approaching the moment of truth, and Stevens decided that it would be now or never, as they arrived at the first room. “Leave my bags and the lady’s here, and take the rest to the other room.” “Yessir.” It was all one word, quick said as a knee jerk response. “Wait, what” No. Leave his bags in this room, and take mine to the other room, I’m not sleeping with him.” Shit, he was afraid this would happen. What made it even worse was she didn’t complain about the lack of a third room, apparently she’d just naturally assumed she’d be staying with Alex, the same way he’d assumed she’d stay with him. This wasn’t going to be good, and he would be damned if the bellboy was going to get a front row seat for a very personal dispute between two people who can get very passionate while arguing. “Sheena, just let the man do his job. We’ll talk if you still want your bags in Alex’s room I’ll take them there myself. Now you, go.” By this time of course they were already in the first room, the bellhop inside unloading the bags he was told. He knew who’d be giving the tip and was smart enough to do as Stevens had instructed him. That’s one great thing about the double standard, staff always assume the man’s paying thus always listen to the man almost exclusively. Stevens slips the man a twenty under the guise of a handshake, and told him to be quick about getting back downstairs as Alex would almost invariably need help finding the gym. Stevens then not too forcefully drug Sheena into the room, and shut the door, desperately hoping he’d be able to do so without her causing too much of a scene. Thankfully she didn’t and they were secluded away in their own private little world, one of many, like the cells in a beehive. He knew he should be tactful, it was the best way to handle the situation, but quite frankly he was tired and still had to talk to Alex, or if this went the way he thought he probably wouldn’t have to, either way he wanted a drink. “So, you’re fucking Alex now.” She’d slapped him a few times before, but it was usually playful, never that damn hard. His jaw snapped shut narrowly missing his tongue, and stars exploded just behind his eyes, there was a lot of fury in that blow. It either meant he’d hit a double bullseye, or missed the board entirely, he wasn’t sure yet. “What if I am” How the fuck is that your business anymore Michael?” “Well, I thought since you found me, after the phone call and all that..” “That phone call Michael means nothing, especially now.” “So you are..” “Don’t even say it you fucking prick. But for your information, no I’m not fucking Alex, I just know that if I stay with him, he won’t try anything I wasn’t sure about you, of course now I know. God Michael, when are you going to grow up, hell when are you even going to bother to notice that I’m not the same girl you used to know?” “Well, I’m definitely seeing it now.” It probably wouldn’t be the best idea to call room service for a bottle of Scotch right now, but he could wish. That would’ve been another advantage of calling ahead, it would’ve already been in the room. Places like this there’s an honor bar, but you’re better off not even touching it, the secret is ordering room service is cheaper. “Look Mike, we’re business people, professionals. That’s all we are, maybe if you weren’t such a jackass it could be different, but you are, so it’s not.” The knot tightening his stomach had to have been that hatred he was feeling earlier. She’d changed so damn much, and he couldn’t even begin to realize the full extent until now. The fire in her eyes made them sparkle, for not the first time, he allowed himself to think just for a minute about how beautiful she was, and again he hated for her being beautiful and complete. “You know what, move to another room, that’s fine. I finally fucking figured it out, you’re better than me fine, you’ve grown, you’ve changed. I don’t even know why the hell you bothered coming back, I’m a worthless, I’m no good, I’mma goddamn sack of dog shit.” The dam broken all the pent up anger, frustration, loneliness began pouring out, he couldn’t stop it now, even if he tried, inwardly he almost chuckled, he’d always wondered what an emotional breakdown would feel like. However before it could even start his laughter was carried away on a tide of much stronger emotions. Ripping the wallet from his back pocket he hurled it to the ground at her feet. “Take it. Get another fucking room, sleep with Alex, go to a new hotel I just don’t give a fuck. You don’t want to be here, you don’t want to be with me. That’s ok I fucking get it alright, just leave me the fuck alone. I’ve tried Sheena, I’ve fucking tried, and I can’t do it anymore, you’re not my friend, or my lover, I can’t have you. I can’t have anyone I fucked it up and you all left, ok. Just go away like everyone else. I can’t keep it up anymore, there’s no reason to. I’ve never been good enough and I never will be, I can’t help it, I can’t do it.” His eyes burned, and vision blurred. Wrung out completely, he started to cry. The drain made his knees weak and he stumbled, down to one knee, supporting himself by the bed. He wanted a drink, to wash away the pain, he wanted a gun to blow away everything else. He was broken, done. A sharp pain, at his scalp, he was actually tearing the hair from his skull, he almost didn’t realize he was doing it. The world was black, he was alone with misery. He’d been depressed before, but nothing like this, it was different. Everything he’d pushed down for months, years was all coming up. Every slight he’d ever had, every harsh word he’d ever spoken, or had spoken against him came back to haunt him. Death seemed preferable to this, he was beyond saving. He never even felt her hands as they gently disentangled his own from his hair. He never felt her arms wrap around his shoulders and squeeze. She whispered in his ear, soft soothing sounds, that made worse. Showing weakness was never acceptable, weakness at this degree was a crime. And there they sat, huddled together, minutes turning into hours. They were both lost to the world, even the knock on the door, Alex seeing if they were alright, was ignored. Sometime in the night he fell into a fitful sleep, and beside him she sat, guarding his dreams. ~Yeah don’t even THINK this is done~
