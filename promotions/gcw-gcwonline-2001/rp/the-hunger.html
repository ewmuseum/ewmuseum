The Hunger

undated

Date: Around lunch The pain had faded into a dull ache, something he could easily cope with, thank god he didn’t dream about pain killers anymore. He was starting to get better, the increased workout schedule had done the trick. It really sucked though, as little pain as he was feeling, there was still a certain weakness in the limbs, a wobbliness in the knees. He should have been feeling stronger than he ever had before, with all the weight lifting and such, and the salads. Yeah that was probably the worst part, eating healthy, sure it helped him recover faster, but tell me one lion that could chase down a gazelle on sprouts and fungus? Yeah I didn’t think you could. That was probably where the weakness was coming from in fact, without a proper diet, he was becoming one of those limp-wrist, sandal wearing, tree hugging tofu jockey’s, truly a fate worse than death. Luckily, judging by the brand of gibberish everyone around him was speaking, he was in a place that had the cure for what ailed him. Sure the flight schedule said they were in Spain, but there was something familiar about the language, and he knew exactly what it was. Yeah he would find what he was looking for soon enough. The last few weeks, hell the entire trip to Europe had been nothing but one huge pain in the ass. Even England, a place that almost felt like home, well at least until he got his ass handed to him by a bunch of drunks, but hell such is life right? No, he’d come to the conclusion the whole of Europe was a sewer, and he couldn’t wait to get the hell outta here. Just one more day and the GCW crew would be packing back up and heading back to the states for Warpath, and not a moment too soon for Michael Stevens. Since the pub fiasco in London, he’d pretty much made himself a shut in, no venturing outside the hotel until the day of the show, in fact he’d basically been told that if he DID leave his hotel he’d be a moron. And of course never wanting to look like a moron, he’d followed his attorney’s advice. Besides when you’ve got the money, it’s not hard to live solely out of your hotel room. The hunger was striking him, he must eat soon, and he knew that mouthwatering bliss was only a phone call away, he was finally well enough physically to get back to his regular diet. He reached for the phone, the guy at the front desk had spoken decent English so it shouldn’t be too hard to get his order across to him. As his call was connected, he could already taste it. “Mostrador..” “Huh, didn’t you speak English last time we talked Paco?” “Oh, yes it’s you sir. How may I help you?” “You can get me some damn food.” “A room service menu should be available for your convenience in your room sir.” “Yeah well, I don’t wanna talk to someone in room service, I know you speak English, so you can just give them my order right?” “I suppose.” It’s funny, no matter how thick someone’s accent is, exasperation always sounds exactly the same. Especially when you hear it just about every time you talk to someone. It’s the little things like that, those little bits of speech pattern that never change, that tell us we’re all just human beings on this little rock. Deep down we’re all the same, of course some of us are either completely oblivious or just plain don’t care, I’m willing to bet Stevens is the latter. “Excellent. I want a couple burritos, some nachos, a cheese enchilada, and a steak chalupa.” “Uh, what sir?” “Oh I forgot English isn’t your first language, I’ll say it again paco’a’couple’burritos’some’nachos?” “I heard what you said sir, but I don’t understand. Our room service is authentic Spanish cuisine, that is NOT what we serve. And I am not dumb sir, you don’t have to speak so slowly, nor am I deaf, you don’t have to speak so loud.” “What do you mean you don’t serve nachos” What about the burritos, enchiladas and the chalupa?” “No, sir. Can I interest you in turnadors of pork in a sherry mushroom sauce” Or perhaps a nice fresh roasted duck? “What the hell” Turnadors of pork? Duck? You serve Hampton and Plucky, but you don’t have any enchiladas?” “Yes, sir, and no sir we don’t have enchiladas..” You know what I said a few minutes ago about exasperation, yeah superiority comes off the same in every accent too. That one though is a lot easier to pick up on, and far, far less likely to be ignored.” “Listen Paco, you’re trying to tell me everyone in the whole country speaks Mexican but I can’t get a damn chalupa” What the hell is going on around here?” “Mr. Stevens, I must insist you not only lower your voice when speaking to me, but you refrain from calling me Paco. As you should well know the fine people of Spain, speak Spanish, and NOT Mexican as you say. We’re a proud people with a long standing tradition of fine cuisine, and if it’s not to your liking perhaps you should leave. If there’s nothing else, I’ll take my leave now, good bye.” The force with which the phone slammed down was enough to set his ears ringing. It had to be a trick, it was all a conspiracy, how the hell could you not get decent Mexican food in a place where everyone spoke Mexican? Oh well, he’d just call back later and order the duck, that didn’t sound too bad really, actually. Still it burned him just a little. “No wonder Columbus left this shit hole, can’t even get a burrito.”
