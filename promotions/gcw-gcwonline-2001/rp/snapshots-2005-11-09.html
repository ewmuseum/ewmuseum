Date: 11-9-05 The list of good things that happened to Rich Rollins was fairly short. After all, a man who maims other men for a living isn’t exactly the kind of guy who gets invited to your mother’s Christmas dinner. Fate had a funny way of serving Rollins? pride on a plate, garnished with the remains of whatever hope he had dared to hold on to. He had always figured that he’d pissed off the Man Upstairs one too many times, and it was far too late for an apology. Guess he was wrong. If the events of the last week were any indication, Rollins would say that his immediate future was looking good. Very, very good. ~<>~<>~<>~ “Holy shit, you pulled it off!” Standing outside the remains of a lobby at Harvard’s University Hall, one of the cigarette-puffing party animals who had been following the Renegade around campus raised one fist triumphantly in the air. “You won the fuckin” title!” Rollins eyes stung as he staggered through a now-trampled rose hedge, his newly won Global title swinging loosely at his side. A slowly gathering mob of students and other onlookers began to voice either their support or their scorn for the man who had just fought his way to the top of the wrestling world, but he’d be damned if he let his exhaustion get the best of him now. Even with the onset of a concussion pounding nails into his brain, the Renegade’s lips peeled back in a satisfied smile. A new era had officially begun. The Era of Anarchy. ~<>~<>~<>~ Many a concussed and nonsensical thought ran through Rollins? head as the cell phone in his pocket began to vibrate, breaking a trance he hadn’t known he had been dwelling in. One quick glance at the outdated T-Mobile, however, made him forget all about his pounding head. 1 New Text Message It was from Steven Caldera. Congrats? you’re now one of the most wanted men in sports entertainment history. While you’re dining on your new spoils, just be sure to remove the chicken bone from your throat. I wouldn’t want to see you, you know, CHOKE. -Caldera The Renegade couldn’t help himself. He smiled all the same. ~<>~<>~<>~ He wasn’t sure what he had expected when he arrived at the arena for WorldWide, but Rich Rollins knew that this was most certainly not it. There, mauling the security fences like an army of rabid wolverines were somewhere in the ballpark of a hundred loyal GCW fans. And they were trying to crucify him. “You screwed Michael Stevens!” a woman shouted, using her bare fists to slam the chain link without restraint. “Seymour’s gonna whoop your ass, bitch!” A small child. How tragically amusing. At least one fan didn’t have a death threat waiting on the tip of his tongue: “Was it really you driving that bus” How the hell did you get into that building?” Ducking out of an empty soda can’s flight path, the Renegade offered the mob a wry grin as he hefted the PTC Global title over one shoulder. “I’m a magic man,” he offered just before slipping away into the relative security of the loading dock. ~<>~<>~<>~ Seymour Almasy The name on the door stared right back at the Renegade, silent and foreboding. It was the only barrier between himself and that which he hated most; the man on the other side was a walking, talking representation of everything Rollins hated about the business he had grown to love. But tonight he had to work with this man. A morbid image of himself dancing at the whims and strings of some omnipotent puppeteer suddenly swam throughhis mind, and try as he might he couldn’t blink it away. Sometimes he wondered if it wasn’t himself he really hated the most. Subconsciously locking his jaw, Rollins reached out to knock on Almasy’s door. His knuckles came within a centimeter of the smooth wooden surface? “Seymour’s not in yet,” a voice piped in from down the hall. A young ring tech gave Rollins a helpless shrug. “Besides, you wouldn’t want to kill yourselves before the match tonight, would you?” Rollins snorted. Who said anything about starting a fight? Not yet, anyway. ~Fin~
