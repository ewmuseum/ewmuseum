Hate...the purest emotion

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: During Annihilation 'FUCK!' The chair made a sastifying noise after it sailed across the room and collided with the wall and fell to the floor. 'SHIT!' So did that one. I ran out of chairs to throw, so I stalked across the locker room and tossed the two chairs back across the length of my locker room with wordless cries of anger. A knock pulled me from my chair abuse. 'WHAT?!' I hollerd hoping it wasn't that little fat bastard Kevin Jacobs, I was not in the mood to give another interview. Cindy stuck her head inside the door, and All Access pass dangling from her neck. 'It is safe?' 'Sure babe.' I said to my wife. I stood in the middle of the room and regained control of myself. 'You sure? Or you wanna throw some more chairs around?' I looked at the battered chairs, after just two trips across the room neither one of them would be good for sitting. 'Yeah, I'm sure. I just need some new chairs.' Cindy came into the room and sat on the bench instead, and looked at me with the same concern she had used when she was my manager. 'Mike...' 'I had him! I fucking had him! I coulda taken him!' I yelled, stomping around the room, I'm pretty sure my face was red and if I was any angrier steam would've come from my ears. I had just gotten back from the EMT station after getting stiched up, and the cuts on my forehead itched like crazy. 'I coulda taken that masked freak out! Dammit! Why did they have to stop the match? I was that close!' I held my fingers an inch apart to indicate just how close I was. 'You didn't loose.' she said soothingly. 'I didn't win either!' I spat. 'I drew, a fucking No Contest! That's like going to a restaraunt and ordering a steak well done, and getting a piece of hamburger meat cooked rare!' 'These things happen, Mike.' 'Not to me they don't! I'm the Machine dammit!' I said turning around and kicking an empty water bottle that had fallen down during my ranting. I turned around and was met by a slap to the face from my wife. 'Get over it!' she yelled in my face. Anyone else would be picking up their teeth right now after I punched them across the room, but not my wife. I would never hit Cindy, not in a million years. 'So what? You fought to a no-contest! Did you have any idea what you two looked like out there? Neither one of you could stand! You were bleeding buckets out there! I haven't seen you loose that much blood in a match since you won the IWO World title back from Roadkill in that cage match!' 'But it's a pay-per-view. Pay-per-view matches aren't supposed to end in draws. They're supposed to have winners, definitive winners, and I go to a no-contest with this fucker. I shoulda made it a hardcore match. I woulda taken his fuckin' arm home with me! Next time I'm gonna break his fuckin' arm!' She slapped me again. 'Michael Anthony Savitch get over yourself! I know you're the Machine! I made you the fuckin' Machine. I know what you can do! I stood there by your side all these years. I helped turn you into a multiple time champion! I was there through the highs and lows with you. I fell in love with you, and I married you! I didn't do all that becasue I had nothing better to do. I did that because I love you and nothing can ever change that. No number of losses, no contests or double countouts or DQ's. I don't give a shit about Mike 'The Machine' Savige. I give a shit about Michael Savitch, the man I married and the man I love.' Before I could say another word her lips met mine and everything else became inconsequential. I blocked out the sounds from the monitor in my locker room. The bustle outside my locker room door of techs running this way and that who were keeping the show running smoothly. At that moment nothing mattered except the woman in my arms, her body pressed into mine and the searing kiss that she was planting on my lips. When she finally let me go and disengaged she looked up at me, I could see the love in her eyes, and the fact that she was right. It didn't matter that I hadn't won. She didn't care if I'd lost to the Marauder. She only cared that the man behind the Machine was alright. And for all intents and purposes I was. I was a little sore of course, but what wrestler isn't? Especially after that match I had. I was a bit ligtheaded after they got me backstage, but that was because I'd lost a lot of blood from the two cuts on my forehead. Other than that I was alright. I could still see straight, all my limbs worked, nothing was broken and when I looked at people there was only one of them standing before me instead of three or four. 'Dammit woman, why do you have to be right all the time?' I asked pulling her close. 'Because I'm a woman...and you wife.' she said smiling up at me. 'Now why don't you go shower? You smell awful. I'll keep you up to date on what happens.' I kissed her again and grabbed some clean clothes, a towel and headed to the shower in my locker room. After washing away the sweat and blood that the EMT's hadn't got off, I felt much better. After I got out of the shower I dressed in a pair of jeans and an old Danger Zone t-shirt. 'Better?' 'Much. I feel like a new man.' 'Not the ranting asshole I saw earlier?' 'No, that guy's gone...for now.' She raised an eyebrow at me, I hated it when she did that. 'For now?' 'Yeah, I'm not done with the Marauder just yet, but I'll save his beating for later. I'm gonna beat that fucker, but for right now I'd rather head back to the hotel and get some rest. And a rubdown, I could use a good rubdown.' 'But what about the rest of the show?' she asked pointing at the monitor as I pulled on my socks and sneakers. 'You forget one of the perks of working for a wrestling promotion my dear.' I said standing and grabbing my equipment bag and stuffing my tights and boots in, before zipping it shut and slinging it over my shoulder. 'Free tapes of all the shows delivered right to your hotel room. Besides if you really want to see the show, if we leave right now, we can still get back to the hotel in time to order the replay.' 'You just want a rubdown.' I waggled my eyebrows at her, 'You say that like it's a bad thing.' She grabbed me by the front of my shirt and leaned up to kiss me again. 'Let's get outta here.'
