Pain in its many forms!!

25 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/25/10 Journal of Pain 1st warning. Here i am pondering ways to make someone hurt further. The surprise to me is the varied simplicities of it. Those like Bryan Mayhem and Tessa Windsor have met several degrees of defeat by others. Bryan Mayhem had finally defeated me and a partner. Yet i am curious if my partner made me weak. Rely too much on another, then your goals become skewed and blind. Twice Tessa Windsor has faced me and twice she has defeated me. Are my own skills diminished or am i missing something from my past that i had lost. Could it be something that has been elusive for a few years. Have i ever been myself? Time to answer these question. Anyways back to studying. I need to get back to basics. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Shaman closes a small book as he looks around. He reaches over and grabs a cup of coffee and drinks it slowly. Sliding the pen into the small book, He then grabs another book from his right. This book is the infamous anatomy book he had been well known for recently. Lifting a piece of paper he reads something on it. Opening the book he begins thumbing through the pages as he stops and begins to read. "Serious dislocations of the Humerus, ulner, and radius int eh arm can be some of the most painful dislocations. The join is hard to put back in place without proper medication to loosen the muscles. The muscles in such an area can be traumatically be affected." Shaman turns the page as he looks closely at the picture. Taking a moment to look at his own arm he follows the books diagram. Nodding his head in understanding, he then reaches for and grabs the cup of coffee. Drinking a bit of it, he then places it back down as he begins to read again. "Three bones come together to make up the elbow joint. The humerus is the bone in the upper arm. Two bones from the forearm." Checking the diagram he speaks " The radius and the ulna, form the lower part of the elbow." Touching his elbow he feels for the bones and then looks back to the book reading aloud. " Each of these bones has a very distinct shape. Ligaments connected to the bones keep all of these bones in proper alignment." Tapping his fingers on the edge of the book he thinks for a few moments. His mind racing through he possibilities of what this type of injury could do. Thinking to himself this injury could cause the momentum of a match to sway drastically. "In the most severe dislocations, the blood vessels and nerves that travel across the elbow may be injured. If this happens, there is a risk of losing the arm." Suddenly smiling sadistically he leans back and grins. "Wow this can be fun, fun to the point of getting a little bit of vengeance." Shaman drops the book down upon the desk. and drums his fingers across the desk. Suddenly the doorbell rings and Shaman looks up from the desk. He frowns a bit and then walks out of the office area and down the hall. He comes to the door and opens it. "Yeah what the hell do you want?" Caught off guard the delivery man looks at him. "Sir package for uhm....Mr. Whitecloud?" The man holds a clipboard out to Shaman. Shaman looks at the clipboard and looks up at the man. "Yeah that's me." Shaman signs the clipboard and looks out the door at a decent sized crate. Pointing to the garage door he speaks up. "Shove it in there i will get to it in a few minutes." Shaman turns to the wall and presses a button as the garage door raises revealing a 1957 Chevrolet his dad had owned. The delivery men pull the crate in and hurriedly get out of the garage. Shaman presses the garage door button and flings the delivery me the clipboard as he speaks. "Keep the change!" Kind of disgusted with being disturbed Shaman heads back to his office. "What the hell could be in that crate? Oh hell back to studying anyhow." Sitting down Shaman eyes the book, but in the back of his mind that delivery is nagging him. Eying the book he remembers that he had studied everything this hard at the early part of his career. Knowing that this was the way to get back what he felt he had lost. Looking at the book he becomes a bit frustrated with the studying. Easily distracted by the crate that was delivered to him. He turns and looks towards the garage door. "That damned delivery is causing a problem." Getting up slowly Shaman carries the Anatomy book with him. He opens the garage door and flips on the garage light. He walks out and runs his fingers across the fins of the 57 Chevy. He slowly admires the car for a moment as he passes by it slowly. He stops and gazes at the crate. He looks over tot he work bench and grabs a crow bar. he slowly walks over to the crate. On the crate there sprayed in black spray paint is the two words Black Betty. Shaman brings a finger to his lips and smiles. "Never thought i'd see this crate again." Taking the crowbar tightly in his hands, He reaches down and slips the crow bar in between the cracks and applies leverage to pop the lid. He grabs the lid lifting it off the top. He turns and drops it on the garage floor. He turns himself back his eyes wide at what is in there. His gaze is fixed upon a few of the items as he reaches down into the crate. Slowly he pulls out a pair of cowboy boots, he smiles a bit as he looks at them and pats the side gently as some dust floats through the air. "Still dusty." He smiles a genuine smile not often seen on his face. He reaches in and pulls out a small teddy bear. Looking at it and then around the room, he silently kisses the teddy bear softly and smiles smelling it. Reaching in he pulls a picture out of the box a picture of a little girl. He touches it softly and then places it aside. "Parts of my past better left buried and unknown. She was my world, my little angelic gift, nothing prepared me for the changes to come. That pain is the most horrible pain one man can ever feel. The people i wrestle week in and week out complain about physical pain, but i deal with the physical pain of losing my daughter and i dedicate every damned day to achieve something. Why am i such a bad ass in the ring?" He holds the picture up to the camera. "She is why i do this and i will beat every damn person until i can justify what happened. I am anti establishment because the business cost me my wife and kid. Can you say that your pain is greater than my own? No you can't because these damned pains hit us differently." Shaman gently puts down the teddy bear and picture on top of it. Reaching into the crate he pulls out a baseball bat with barbed wire wrapped around it. There is dried blood still staining the bat. He simply looks at the bat and places it down. "When i was sixteen years old i stood over my mothers grave and made a promise. A promise i'd do whatever i could to survive. That bat signifies my first championship title." cracking his neck side to side. He stops for a moment and begins to remember something. Nodding softly he then looks down into the crate. The camera follows and shows things from his early career. The first feathered headdress he wore when he entered the ring, His very first pair of wrestling boots, and of course the very first championship title he had won. Slowly he pulls that out and stares at it. The gold color kind of tarnished and the leather cracked. "My own pains are the reason i have become weak. I forgot what it was to hurt inside and use that to drive me." Standing up and walking away from the crate Shaman picks up the anatomy book and heads back into the office carrying the Anatomy book. Shaman slams the door shut behind himself. Jorge Samuelsson walks in from another door in the garage. He smirks and walks over looking down into the crate lifting a knee brace that Shaman had worn after Samuelsson took him out in a match. "Interesting he forgot his pains. Now maybe he will quit being so damn weak and be a man about his defeats and rise from the ashes." Samuelsson chuckles as he walks across the garage. "I am glad Madcoil thought of sending me this crate. I never thought that idiot Mongol would be useful." Turning he looks around the garage. Back in the office Shaman is studying the anatomy book once again. His mind seeking ways to cause more pain in the elbow area, than a normal person could withstand. He seems more driven as he reads on. Never wavering from the pages, he breaths slow deep breaths, as if he was expelling a bit of himself with each breath. "There comes a time when the past and present collide. This is a dangerous time for anyone. It is a dangerous time to be meeting me, no matter who the hell you are." Taking his gaze he stares back down into the book as the camera fades to black.
