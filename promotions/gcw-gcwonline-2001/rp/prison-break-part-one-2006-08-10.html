Prison Break - Part: One

10 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/10/06 written for archive - Bloodbox, October 2005 - Jeff bangs the mat, the referee decides whether to call for the bell or not, Jeff begins shouting no however, he then drags his way to the ropes, but instead of grabbing the bottom rope he pulls himself out of the ring, Caine still on his arm refuses to let go, Jeff picks up the sledgehammer and throws it at Caine with his free arm, the hammer hits Caine in the stomach, Caine lets go of the hold, Jeff quickly picks the hammer back up and slams it into Caine’s chest, he hits him a few more times in the chest and Caine begins coughing up blood. Magnus: That was the close one for Jeff, now Caine is bleeding at the mouth. Goliath: He could have some serious internal injuries right now. Jeff raises the sledgehammer up and slams it into Caine’s throat, the barbed wire tearing his flesh, Caine rolls around grabbing his throat in pain trying to breath, Jeff picks him up and hits him with the HardKore Bomb, he holds his shoulder in pain for a moment, the crowd goes wild as he then locks on the Chain Reaction, Caine screams out in pain as he tries to get to the ropes, he fails however as Jeff pulls back harder on his neck, moments go by but Caine begins hammering the mat with his fist, the referee calls for the bell as the crowd goes wild, Jeff keeps the hold on, the referee tries to pull him off but fails, Caine’s screams begin to fade as he loses consciousness, Jeff let’s go of him as Power Struggle by Sunna starts up, Jeff raises his right arm in victory, the referee then hands him his championship belt along with the deed to the 50% ownership to Bloodbox. Lovelace: Your winner by submission, and still World Heavyweight Champion and new Bloodbox Co-Owner, “All Fucking That” Jeff Harris! Magnus: Jeff Harris has won, all the odds were stacked against him but he overcame and he is now co-owner of Bloodbox! Goliath: Not to mention the World Champion still, no one has been able to beat him, it makes me wonder if someone will. The crowd starts an AFT chant as a load of paramedics come racing out of the back with a number of stretchers in tow, they load Errol, Tyrone and Wrecker onto a stretcher and begin wheeling them to the back, Caine is then dragged out of the ring and placed on a stretcher also, as they begin wheeling Caine up the ramp Rachel Ryan walks out from the back, she looks down at Caine and grins, she continues her walk to the ring, she walks up the ring steps and steps through the ropes, she walks over to Jeff and hugs him, she then raises Jeff’s hand then hands Jeff a pen, Jeff then signs the piece of paper which now makes Jeff’s ownership official, Neu Generation is helped up by referee’s and is then helped to the back, as Jeff watches Caine disappear behind the curtain he drops the title and the deed in the ring then leaves, he runs up the ramp clothes lining Neu Generation on his way up then disappears behind the curtain also. Magnus: Where the hell is Jeff going? Goliath: I don’t know but I kind of feel sorry for Neu. Magnus: Is Jeff going after Caine? Goliath: I just said I didn’t know, fucking hell. Moments go by we are shown the parking lot where an ambulance is waiting for the arrival of Caine, Caine is wheeled to the back with no sign of Jeff anywhere, he is loaded into the ambulance and the drivers close the doors, Jimmy Thunder then bursts onto the scene and slams the ambulance drivers heads into the side of the ambulance, he then beats the shit out of the EMT’s then pulls them out of the way, a few seconds later a huge Bloodbox 18 wheeler slams into the side of the ambulance, the crowd look on with shock as gasps can be heard, the 18 wheeler them slams the ambulance against the wall. The 18 wheeler reverses off scene, the cameraman tries to get a peek into the ambulance but before he can the 18 wheeler slams into the side of the ambulance again crushing it, the door to the 18 wheeler then opens and Jeff Harris gets out with a sadistic look on his face, he walks over to the ambulance, foaming at the mouth and his eyes wide open, even Thunder has a worried look on his face, Jeff continues to stare at the ambulance, breathing heavily as the show goes off the air. - Clark County, Nevada, April 2006 - Possibly the greatest moment in a wrestlers career will be something that they did at the end of it or during their prime. This is no different for Jeff Harris who, just six months ago murdered another human being, not for the love of a woman or the love of money but because of the love he had for the wrestling business, the one thing that gave him a reason to be, Jeff returned to wrestling in February 2005 after faking his own death several months earlier. After he was rejected by his son Shawn he was asked to stay around by CEO Rachel Ryan due to the rising problem that was Simon Caine, repeating Jeff’s history in the business would be pointless, as it’s already been said time and time again, if anyone would be able to take out Simon Caine after taking the company he spent millions on. There aren’t many men that would be capable of murder and the moment that really defines you is the moment right before you have the choice to end another’s life or not. Had Jeff killed before? No, but did he have the desire to? You’re damn right he did. Simon Caine was a cancer in the wrestling business, only in it for the money and not giving a damn about the wrestlers safety, one thing he didn’t think about when Jeff Harris was hired is that Jeff didn’t care about his own personal safety either. He was loyal to Rachel Ryan as it was her that gave Jeff another chance to succeed and succeed he did, most wrestlers in this day and age that are long time veterans of the ring do not go out the way they wanted to, they either retire due to age, career ending injury or just plain embarrassing themselves at wrestling events, Jeff was set apart from the rest as he went out a World Heavyweight Champion, the one thing he wanted to do if his career ever did come to a close. After signing over the papers for Bloodbox to Rachel Ryan, Jeff made his leave some say it was because he had nothing left to accomplish in the ring, others day it was because he burned out but the truth is, despite Jeff’s attempts at running, the past finally caught up with him and the law took him down on a brutal murder charge, he was charged with first degree murder and just scraped past the death penalty and given a life sentence with no chance of parole. Jeff was disheartened, he was devastated, many believed what Jeff did was noble and to be locked up with a bunch of rapists and thieves was a terrible thought. His cell stood at just ten feet tall and ten feet wide, his cell mate was in prison for a series of rape charges and was the kind of guy that had many bitches at his disposal, despite the man being quite a bit smaller than most people in the prison he did have a lot of money and promised to pay a number of random thugs when they eventually got out. This man tried to get his men to intimidate Jeff, when did didn’t work he ordered them to attack him, but it resulted in three broken noses, three broken arms, a broken leg, two broken collar bones, 23 broken ribs and a broken neck, none of these bones however, belonged to Jeff. It wasn’t long before Jeff was known as the type of guy you don’t want to even give a snide remark to. Jeff knew he wasn’t going anywhere any time soon and that the only way out of the prison was by death. The thought of never again stepping inside of a wrestling ring tore at Jeff like a flesh eating virus, wrestling was his life and as far as Jeff was concerned, his life was over. As the scene opens up inside the High Desert State Prison, Jeff is seen wearing an orange jumpsuit, common amongst most prisons in America, the cell was cramped compared to what Jeff was used to living in, his roommate stood at around 5’7 and probably weighed about 150 pounds, a white man with greasy black hair, the type of hair you’d have if you were a porn star in the 1980s, he wore the same kind of jumpsuit as Jeff and had a very cheesy grin on his face. The man originated from Texas, a redneck for sure, the worst thing since hillbillies. The man had been living with Jeff for the past five months and paid a lot more men for protection from him outside of the cell, which is ridiculous considering he was unprotected while being in the same cell as Jeff. The corrupt guards however that took bribes gave Jeff’s cellmate the option of stopping in another mans cell and by another man I mean one of his bitches, but usually the guards that don’t take bribes would often put a stop to this, Jeff’s cellmate, Billy “Six Inch” Shaw had just returned from one of his bitches cells, wiping the sweat off of his face and the screaming still going on outside, Jeff looked away in disgust, he just sat there looking at his picture of his son. Jeff rarely spoke since the moment he went into prison and when he did it was when being spoken to by the guards. Despite Billy’s attempts to get him to talk all that resulted from it was his allies in hospital. Jeff didn’t care who he hurt now that he knew he’d never be leaving the prison system. Jeff continued to look at his son and his ex-wife Stephanie, Billy peered over Jeff’s shoulder looking at the photo also, his hot breath landing on Jeff’s short black hair. Jeff turned his head revealing the scarring he suffered at the hands of his step brother still fresh on his face and growled. “Hell boy, that’s a mighty fine looking” boy you have there, shame he ain’t in here, I’d give him something to cry about, heh hell by the time I was done by?” Billy was saying before being cut off as Jeff’s hand was firmly locked around his throat, Jeff stood up and lifted him up with one arm then slammed him against the wall. “If you ever, ever mention anything to do with my son again, I will personally tear out your larynx and crush it under my finger nails, I’m sick of you, you’re walking a fine line you do not want to cross, you know what happened the last time you did, your “homeboys” or whatever you redneck fucks call them ended up in hospital, what happened to me? A black eye and a busted lip, truly pathetic. Do not test my might little man or you’ll be next.” Jeff verbalized, he then dropped Billy who landed on the concrete hard. He composed himself however and stood up, “Hell boy! Five months and this is the first time you speak” I knew there was a way to get you to speak and looks like I found it. Your son a sore subject with you then?” Jeff looked at him sadistically when he mentioned his son yet again, Billy instantly edged his way closer to the door. Since ending the life of Simon Caine, Jeff had remained the evil bastard he was forced to be. The All Fucking That in him still marched on. “So uh yeah man, what are you in for” I’m in for?” Billy said before being cut off again. “I know what you’re in for, 27 rapes and grievous bodily harm, you sicken me, you’re the type of man that should have been executed and the fact that you’re gay?” Jeff said before Billy intervened. “I’m no faggot!” Billy exclaimed. “Where are you from?” Jeff asked. “Muleshoe, Texas.” Replied Billy. “Damn, there’s only two things that come from Texas, steers and queers and you haven’t got any antlers so you must be a queer.” Jeff said with a smile. This angered Billy but decided against lashing out at Jeff, “Me” I’m in for murder, I seriously injured a man then once he was placed into an ambulance I crushed it and him with an 18 wheel truck, I then got out and watched him die as I grinned.” This information made Billy stop talking and politely nodded before leaving the cell. Jeff finally had some more peace, but was short lived as male screams could be heard from down the corridor. “No! Not again!” A voice shouted, the man whose voice it belonged to sounded quite young, Jeff shook his head, he put the picture back under his pillow and stood up, he cranked his neck as an audible cracking sound is heard, he then turns to the door and exits his cell. The view outside of his cell is pretty much repetitive as along the corridors are just cells all in a row in a square shape, you are then able to see the other floors by looking over the railings, Jeff heads towards the cell where the screams are coming from, as he gets there Billy Shaw is attempting to give his six inches to a young man who couldn’t be much older than 23, usually Jeff kept out of other peoples business but was beginning to get pissed off at the constant screams. He steps inside of the mans cell and grabs Billy, he throws him to the floor and boots him hard in the chest, the two men holding the young man down let go of him and head towards Jeff, Jeff boots one in the face then hit’s the other with an elbow smash knocking him back slightly, he then hits him with a roundhouse kick to the temple knocking him unconscious, Billy goes on the offensive and hits Jeff in the back of the head, he turns around and grabs Billy, he lifts him up over his head, the footsteps of guards come running towards the scene, Jeff then throws Billy over the railing, luckily for Billy there is a safety net to prevent death. Billy lands on the safety net. The young man pulls his pants up and breathes a sigh of relief, “Thank you! Thank you so much!” He said with obvious gratitude. “Oh shut up you whiney little bitch.” Jeff responded before punching him hard in the jaw knocking him down. As the guards arrive at the scene, Jeff shakes the pain off his hand before walking back towards his cell. “Billy’s alive” I think.” As Jeff gets back to his cell, his door closes automatically locking him in to prevent further incident, Jeff sighed and laid on his bed, it wasn’t too long before he fell asleep. What seemed like only a matter of minutes, he was being shaken awake by a guard, two other guards were stood there with nightsticks in hand, Jeff found it all rather humerous as he knew he could take the nightsticks off of them at any given moment and do something rather uncouth with them. “Jeff, wake up, you have a visitor.” The guard who did the shaking said, it took a couple of moments for Jeff to come around but he eventually got off of his bed and stood up straight, towering over the fat, balding guard. Jeff was actually surprised that he wasn’t punished for throwing Billy Shaw over the rail, had the security net not been there he surely would have plummeted to his death, the other two men however were escorted to the infirmary. “Quite the brawl earlier on eh Jeff?” The guard said. “I’m sick of hearing groaning noises from Billy from other cells and I’m tired of little bitch boys screaming, all I want to be in this place is left alone, I at least earned that much but you have to put me in with that joke. If I am to spend the rest of my life in this place then I want to be put in solitary or at the very least, in a different cell.” Jeff responded. “Yeah we’ll see. First time I’ve seen you actually do something in this place since you’ve been here, I’m not too bothered about the way you treated Shaw anyway, there are certain guards in this place that let him do whatever he wants.” The guard exclaims, the two guards behind him and Jeff look at each other and grin. As the four men finally reach the visiting room door the guard puts handcuffs on Jeff, he then shackles his ankles together while saying, “So are all your friends so big?” Jeff didn’t know how to take that, whether his visitor was tall or the guard had him in the conjugal room beforehand. Jeff shrugged his shoulders regardless, the guard then opened the door and followed Jeff into the visiting room, the door shut behind him, Jeff scanned around the room and saw the one man he could have gone the rest of his life without seeing, Jeff walked over to the table and took a seat opposite his visitor. “Hello Jeff.” His visitor said smiling. ??Hello Peter.” Jeff replied. The scene fades as we see Jeff sat about five feet away from his greatest foe, “Neu Generation” Peter Howorth.
