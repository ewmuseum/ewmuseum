The Thrillion Man March With Zoey Preparing for Wolfy

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Undesired The Thrillion Man March is totally kickin’ it straight street backstage, yo…. Or something… Beef Supreme: Dude, and that’s why I think that it would be a totally obvious mismatch. Quesa Dilla: You’re a fool, it would go fifteen rounds and the refs would have to call for a decision. Beef Supreme: No way, you wrapped piece of cheese and stuff silly goose! Quesa stops for a minute. Quesa Dilla: Did you seriously just call me a silly goose? Beef Supreme: Would you prefer queer duck? Nacho Grande interrupts the two’s argument. Nacho Grande: What the hell are you arguing about now? Beef Supreme: Who would win in a fight. Nacho Grande: Between…? Quesa Dilla: Riddick from Pitch Black and Dom from the Fast and the Furious. Beef Supreme: What about Dom from Fast and Furious? Quesa Dilla: What about shutting the hell up? Nacho Grande: Of all the things that are wrong with this circumstance, THAT’s what bothers you? Beef Supreme: Dude, he’s got silver eyes… You don’t mess with a guy with silver eyes. Quesa Dilla: I’ll… mess with you when you have silver eyes. Beef Supreme: Yeah, I’ll… gold your silver eyes! Quesa Dilla: I’ll platinum your goldness, bitch. Nacho Grande: RIDDICK AND DOM ARE THE SAME PERSON! Beef and Quesa stop and ponder. Quesa Dilla: It’s a conspiracy, man! Like Nantucket! Beef Supreme: Nantucket? Quesa Dilla: Think about it, man. There once was a man from Nantucket? Doesn’t make sense, cause it doesn’t exist! Zoey's mug becomes familiar as he nears the Thrillion, wearing his blue facepaint in a battle sequence. His wrestling tights are complimented with a kilt and a worn out fanny pack. Zoey: SONS OF SCOT-STEVENS-LAND! I AM ZOEYILLIAM WALLACE! Nacho Grande: Zoeyilliam Wallace is twelve feet tall. For the visually impaired, the Zoey before us is not twelve feet tall, but rather a measily five feet and ten inches. Zoeyilliam Wallace: Aye! Yes, I've heard. Kills men by the hundreds and if he were here right now, he'd consume the GCW with fire orbs from his eyes and freshly baked crumpets from his arse. I AM ZOEYILLIAM WALLACE! And I see a whole army of my countrymen here in defiance of tyranny. You have come to fight as FREE MEN and FREE MEN YOU ARE! What would you do without freedom? WILL YOU FIGHT? And from behind a bookshelf down the hall, a familiar voice... "Jeff".. .."Jeff, from Northern Ohio. Jeff." Zoeyilliam Wallace: Aye! Fight and you may die. Run and you shall live - at least for a while. And dying in your beds many years from now, would you be willing to trade all the days from this day to that for once chance, just one chance to come back here and tell our enemies that they cannot hurt our mother, the trees and our father the sun and our brother the wolf and our sisters the sacred water. Tell your enemies that they may take our Ben Tea, TERifics but they may NEVER...TAKE...OUR.. Beef Supreme: FREEDOM! Nacho Grande: FREEDOM! Quesa Dilla: FREEDOM! Zoeyilliam Wallace: TEATERTOTS! Beef Supreme: Haha, he’s like the New Guy. Quesa and Nacho: … What? Beef Supreme: He’s doing the joke from that movie “The New Guy”. Nacho shakes his head. Nacho Grande: Beef, that is officially the stupidest thing you’ve ever said… this week. Instead of replying with words, Zoey snaps his head and widens his eyes to the point where they look like they're going to pop out of his head. The sound of a whip can coincidentally be heard at the exact same moment. Everyone jumps back. Zoey: CRAZY EYES! Quesa Dilla: What the… Where the hell did that sound come from? Beef Supreme: It’s the CRAZY EYES! Watch! Beef tries to do the crazy eyes, and instead a galloping horse is heard. Zoey claps the broomstick behind his head. Zoey: Who’s the Biotch NOW?! Nacho Grande: I’ll vote on crazy psycho Progressive lady for that one! Beef Supreme: …::cough:: Yeah…. Quesa Dilla: I’ll get all amped up and crazzzzzzy about my car insurance shit! Nacho Grande: Don’t forget to get male customers that sound like they just broke into puberty! Quesa Dilla: HI! WELCOME TO PROGRESSIVE.COM! CAN I SHARE MY LINE OF COCAINE WITH YOU TO MAKE THIS INTERESTING TOO! Jeff: I’m Jeff. Quesa Dilla: HI JEFF! VROOM VROOM! I’M GONNA PLAY A KAZOO AND PRETEND I WROTE IT. DISCOUNT!!!!! Jeff: From Northern Ohio. Quesa Dilla: WOW YOU’RE FROM NORTHERN OHIO?! THAT’S SO COOL I’M GONNA NEARLY HAVE AN ORGASM BY SELLING YOU PROGRESSIVE! SO YOU SAY YOU NEED A POLICY? CAN I POSSIBLY MAKE THIS ANY MORE ANNOYING? Jeff: Jeff. Quesa Dilla: THAT HAPPENS TO ME ALL THE TIME! Jeff: From Northern Ohio. Zoey: DISCOUNT! Nacho Grande: Enough! I can’t stand that crack lady! Beef Supreme: … Discount? Nacho Grande: I have something to show you! Nacho leads the crew towards a door at the end of the hall. As he opens the door, it’s revealed to be a walk-in closet… filled with TACOS! The three friends start jumping around and screaming like idiots, reveling in the Taco goodness. Quesa Dilla: This is the greatest thing ever in the history of anything! Zoey: Hey, is that Bobby back there?
