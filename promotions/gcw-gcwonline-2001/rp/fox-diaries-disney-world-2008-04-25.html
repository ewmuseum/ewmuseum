Fox Diaries: Disney World

25 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/25/08 START 9:30 am The morning sun rises up from the horizon, slowly forcing the dew all around to fade away. It’s another beautiful day, dawning before us in the lovely state of sunshine; Florida. This is the place where snowbirds flock to avoid winters harshness, where the sun is brighter, hotter and bigger. The whole state smells like sun screen soaked old people? what’s not to love about that? And your hero, Clyde Fox, is on his way to Disney World. The asphalt in the parking lot begins to heat up with the glaze of the sun as the crackle of car tires turning on the asphalt comes closer. A bright yellow cab volleys its way up to the drop section like a bumble bee to a flower. The brakes screech ever so slightly as our hero, Clyde Fox, literally kicks the back door wide open and exits. His shirt fizzles in the winds as he shuts the taxi door with a smile. He then skips off to the ticket booth like a fairy eager for teeth collecting. Fox bellies right up to a bulletproof ticket booth window and stands impatiently for the next cashier. The only problem is that one cannot see if there is a person on the other side of the window due to glare and the thickness of the window. Clyde stands there, waddling his feet as the few scattered tourists that were there initially get served by a different window. Looking like he has to pee, Clyde begins to panic. Fox: OH EM GEE! DIZNEY! LEMME IN LEMME IN! Like a bird scouring the sky, a Disney employee rushes up to Clyde to inform him that the window is vacant. The man is a shriveling old man, probably in his 60s. The man has a well kept mustache but no hair on his head to show for it. Employee: Sir? sir. This line is closed. The lady over at number ten will serve you. As Fox turns, he jolts backwards in shock as he was repulsed by the mans appearance. Clyde scuffs his sneakers on the sidewalk as he bends slightly and pears toward the man by squinting. Fox: You’re a puny little guy aren’t you? You’re kind of cute. I like you? BIG TIME! Now where is Walt Dizney? The employee moves back to avoid any possible kissing from what he believes is a retarded gentleman. Employee: Just go to number ten sir and sorry? I’m not gay. Clyde rubs the back of his neck as the clouds temporarily cover up parts of the sun. Fox: I’m totally gay! Gay as in happy! I’M AT DIZNEY WORLD! Fox smacks the man on the shoulder (probably separating it) and heads off to booth ten which happens to be vacant. Fox orders up his tickets in typical fashion as he grasps the platform that juts out from the booth. After gaining entry and bypassing all the boring parts, it was finally time. Clyde Fox had entered Disney World. What to do first? What to save for last? What to simply do? Like a child confronted with 50 million different toy options, Clyde did what any other kid would do? Fox: MICKEY!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! About thirty feet away from him stood THE Mickey Mouse. No jokes. IT WAS HIM! No doubt about it. Like your favorite scene from the motion picture “The Matrix” on DVD NOW, time seemed to slow down. Fox examined his route to Mickey. There weren’t too many people around. Just enough to snake his way through, but which way was the best? One falter and he might miss getting to meet THE Mickey Mouse, but the right route might get him extended time for a possible hug! To his left there were three families, all with strollers. “That’s no good” he thought as at least ten to fifteen children were frolicking around there. It’s a range of ten to fifteen because he doesn’t want to waste time counting? MICKEY MOUSE IS RIGHT THERE! To his right there are some more spaces, but also more people continually shifting and scattering throughout. He then turns his attention to the center. Some old people with walkers and electronic powered wheelies consume the void as they huddle up and chat about who has hemorrhoids. Clyde takes the right side of the yard just as Mickey Mouse becomes consumed a pack of kids that "attacked" him from behind. “Oh shoot!” Clyde thought. He better hurry before the pack of kids destroy his hero! But suddenly the crowd begins to shift. Like an unpredictable volcano eruption, the old people start to move and begin to cut off Clyde’s route! Fox: FACKING OLD PEOPLE! DON’T YOU HAVE GRANDKIDS TO SPOIL? Clyde makes a Reggie Bush like cut and spins fiercely to avoid the dastardly old people. Mickey is just up ahead? about fifteen feet away! Mickey is seen shaking off the children from hell as Clyde races to him. Like shields protecting their warriors, Disney security storms in and takes Mickey away from the rush just as Clyde pulls up. Fox: NOOOOOOO! FACKING KIDS! DON’T YOU HAVE GRANDPARENTS TO ANNOY? Mickey is seen slipping inside a nearby merchandise shop. Luckily our hero saw his hero so everything is good. Clyde steamrolls past the kids, leaving a few battered and bruised in his wake. Upon entering the shop, the door clangs shut behind him and complete silence overtakes everything. Clyde creeps along the store room as he shifts his head around in search for Mickey. With no one in sight, Clyde turns and sees a bunch of white Mickey Mouse hoodies on display. Suddenly? AN IDEA! He lessens his stance and begins to move around the shop normally. He grabs a disposable camera and a hoodie. Folks this is what A.D.D. can do to our young kids. Even an irresistible force, like Mickey Mouse, can be forgotten by a child with A.D.D. So please, act now? donate today, operators are standing by. For Clyde’s sake. Anyways? Clyde grabs his things, makes an evil looking face and well, this is how his escapade turned out. Fox: I LOOK LIKE DR. Z Z Z! I’m funny! BIG TIME! Just then, Mickey Mouse emerges from the back and tries to make it out the door, but Clyde is just too quick! Fox hurdles past some bins and tackles the mascot to the ground. A grunt erodes from the being as they both stumble to their feet. Fox: MICKEY MOUSE! I WUV YOU SO MUCH! GIMMIE A HUG! Mickey compiles himself, turns and blatantly pushes Clyde backwards. And starts out talking in a grizzled old man voice, but out of habit, it turns into the Mouse voice. Mickey Mouse: FUCK YOU MAN! WHO THE FUCK DO YOU THINK YOU ARE? Freaking psychopath, how old are you? Fox places his fingers on his lips as he looks to the ceiling to ponder. Fox: I think I’m this many? He flashes his ten fingers twice to indicate 20 years young. Mickey Mouse: SECURITY! The security guards march in and stand in front of Mickey Mouse as if he were a rock star. Mickey Mouse: Get this guy out of here? he’s not magical at all! And with that, the guards latched onto Clyde and dragged him out of the store and towards the parking lot. Successful trip to Disney Clyde? “that’s what we thought. At least he got to meet his hero Mickey Mouse. On the count of three, lets all say it together now” 1 2 3?. THAT’S OUR CLYDE! END
