A walk to remember.....

31 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-31-07 (There was alot of tension between me and my grandmother for I had not been home in over three years. Although I always made sure to call on the holidays and wish her well, I wasn't there for her as she was for me. I had lost my parents at an early age, but she had lost her husband, my grandfather, far to early into their undying love. She was 45. Theodore was 47. I wish I were only there these past few years to be there for her in her time of need.) (I had just celebrated my 26th birthday and I was home to celebrate so I took it upon myself to throw my grandmother a surprise. I figured a good way to ease the tension between myself and her would be to catch up on things face to face. Plus I wasn't exactly against being a grandmomma's boy.) (The rain was heavy as usual, of course due to these April showers. Although there was a delightful warm air temperature thanks to spring creeping out from the depths of a hellacious winter. It was a rather perfect day to visit her. The timing was great for I needed to relax from my twelve hour days of conditioning.) (I planted my damp feet in front of the once welcoming victorian house. Now weather beaten, color faded, and mold eroding, I stood in front of my once beautiful and cherished home. I was afraid to knock on the door.) (I took a deep breath and let out a light sigh...) "Here goes nothing." :::Knock, knock, knock::: (Footsteps towards the door seemed to increase in volume until finally that familiar face peeks through a slightly open door.) Grandmother: "Oh it's you." (She turns and walks away from a half opened door. I almost feel rather put off for it wasn't exactly the welcome home I had in mind. I stand there in confusion until finally I hear...) Grandmother: "Well are you coming in or what? And make sure you shut that door behind you. I have the air conditioner on and I'm surely not paying to keep the neightborhood cool." (It almost feels like I didn't know my Grandmother anymore. She sounds so....alone. I decide to enter anyway shutting the solid oak door behind me. Although it seemed as thought nothing had changed interior wise, I could sense the drastic change in the way she was living. For now she owned three all black cats. A big sign in aging alone.) Grandmother: "Would you like anything to eat or drink?" "I'm alright Nana. Thank you." Grandmother: "Are you cetain, I have cookies baking in the oven." "I'm positive, really, but thanks anyway." (A few akward seconds pass until her stern voice breaks the silence.) Grandmother: "So what brings you home stranger?" "Well my birthday just passed...." Grandmother: "Yes, I know. Are you trying to hint to me something?" "No Nana nothing like that at all....." Grandmother: "You thought I might have forgotten your birthday?" "No, I wasn't hinting anythi..." Grandmother: "What kind of Grandmother would I be to forget my only Grandson's birthday!" "Nana, I'm not trying to hint anything of any nature. I'm just saying that I'm in town because I celebrated my birthday at home. So I could be with friends and visit my parents graves....(I pause)....And to see you Nana." Grandmother: "Well it's been what now, three years since you've been back to visit. I was almost certain you were dead, if not paralyzed from that boxing you were doing before." "I wasn't boxing I was wrestling." Grandmother: "Boxing, wrestling! It's all the same anyway. One man showing another that he can beat him up and hurt him. Like some high school bully! That's all you guys were. Nothing but bullies." "Are." Grandmother: "Huh. What are you talking about?!" "Well Nana that's what I also wanted to tell you. I resigned with GCW." Grandmother: "What you're boxing again?" "Wrestling Nana, it's wrestling." Grandmother: "Get out! Leave this very instant. You've made it perfectly clear what your choices are in life! And it's going to get you killed someday! And quite frankly I don't want anything to do with another death in this family. We've both lost so many to this family already." "Nana, I'm not gonna get hurt....I know what I'm doing. You know that I am strong....." Grandmother: "Strong? You've been through so much MICHAEL! Numerous concussions, countless surgeries, and nights of endless agonizing pain. I told you, if this is what you choose now, then I ask you kindly to leave." "But Nana..." Grandmother: "But nothing. Make sure you shut the door upon your exit." (I stand in disbelief as I slowly work my way to the front door once again. This is who I am and this is what I love. And nothing and no one will tell me otherwise.) "I Love you Nana. See you in three years." (I walk out the door making sure to lightly shut it behind me. I feel as though walking out that door meant I was walking away from my life. Or maybe I'm just heading towards my new one....AGAIN.)
