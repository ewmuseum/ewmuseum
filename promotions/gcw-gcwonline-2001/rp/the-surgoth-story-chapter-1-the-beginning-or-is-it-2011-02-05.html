The Surgoth Story - Chapter 1 - The Beginning... Or Is It

5 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/11 Prolog: Out in the middle of the Atlantic Ocean a fishing boat was the scene of an epic battle between a Father and Son. With them stood a hooded figure wearing white. "You guys must stop this!" yelled the hooded figure which had a female voice. "Not until my father is dead," yelled a man who stood in a black trench coat, his hair cut short and spiked. "Hounisty son, do you really think you can defeat me?" began the man who wore a erd trench coat, his hair jet black and reaching the middle of his back. "You should join the Hored my son, that is where true power lies!" "All you do is spread lies father! The Seriph Force has shown me the path of light. They have shown me what you have done to them and they want you dead!" "That's not true," said the hooded figure. "We need you both with the Seriph Force. The Hored, no Hell needs to be stopped!" "No, it is Heaven that must fall! And it shall with the death of you and my son." A ball of dark light, purple in color, begins to glow above the raised hand of the man in the red trench coat. "You must not do this! There is to much power in that energy ball! You will kill us all!" said the one in the white hood. "Then so be it, and with our deaths, something new and old will rise!" The man in the red trench coat threw the ball of light down at the ship, causing it to explode and sink into the ocean. After the ship completly sank, the whole earth began to shake and the sea began to rise up. Something was raising up out of the Atlantic Ocean. Chapter 1 I had been on the job for 6 months now, or atleast I believed it to be, and already I had alot of questions. I wasn't the only one I'm sure. It seems that people can only remember their life up until about a 8 months ago, and then everything is just blank. The only thing I do know is that my name is Van Jahan Surgoth and I work for the DCPD (diamond City Police Department) as a Paranormal CSI Agent. But I've learned that it goes a bit further than that. A couple of days ago people started dying of an unknown virus. Most of us put it off as nothing more than that, a virus is the only known cause. Myself, however, believe different. Where exactly did this virus come from? If it is unknown then I feel that someone has to be responsible. I tracked the virus without anyone knowing and it led me to a lab located in a building just outside the city. The name, Glidus Labs. At Gildus labs is a man known as Doctor Tilius Argath. He has been doing a study on a new bio-weapon that uses a similar virus known as the Delta Virus, that has been killing the city locals. Today I plan on running it past my Police Chief, Rutunda Gilthor. I only hope he is in a good mood because when he says a case is closed, it usually means don't dig or you might find something you don't want to. The hour is ten-hundred hours. I had already been in the office for four long hours tying up loose ends to my research on this Doctor Argath. I knew that if I was going to convince the Chief, that I was going to have to do my homework thoroughly. As I got up from my desk to go talk to the Chief, she walked in. Long blonde hair, bright blue eyes, if only I knew her name. I only see her twice a day, delivering lunch to those who ordered it from our cafeteria. Twice in one day? Well as much as I work, two lunch times usually go by. Every day I order both times in hopes to get up the courage to talk to her when she brings me my food. She always stops and talks to the same girl and you never hear a word they say but when they start laughing, it echos through the place. Her laughter haunts me every time I try to sleep. I can never get her off my mind. It was weird seeng her up here at this time, and with out her cart no less. I knew it wasn't lunch time but she was up here anyway. I had no excuse to get her to come over to me. I knew I didn't have the time anyway, I needed to get over to the Chief. I grabbed the folders I planed to present the Chief and headed over to his office door which was only six feet away. I knocked on the door and looked in the window. He say there on the phone and waved for me to come in. Once in he motioned for me to sit down. I sat there and listened to one side of the conversation. "I don't care what you have to do, I want that report on my desk in an hour," said the Chief in a surprisingly mild tone. Normally when things aren't going his way, he begins to get very loud. "I want no more excuses is that understood?" There was a pause for a moment. He was calm now but I was half afraid to see how he was going to be once I presented these files. I knew that I could quite possibly disappear today and know one would know where I went, just like my partner did about three months ago trying to do the same thing I'm doing now. "Thank you," said Chief Gilthor as he hung up the phone. "What can I do for your today Van." I threw the folders up onto his desk and replied to him, "It's the case on the unknown virus, I really think there is more to it than meets the eye. There is a lab known as Gildus Labs and a scientist known as Doctor Tilius Argath who has been doing research on a bio-weapon that uses a virus very close to this unknown virus that has been killing people." Chielf Gilthor looks through the files, occasionally looking up at me. I felt a chill go down my spine thinking of the last guy that did this. I was in the room when my partner presented his case to the Chief and he gave him the same treatment, the stare over the folders. The Chief placed the folders on the desk, folded his hands and placed them on the desk. "Van, you've been working here for around 6 months now and I thought for sure you would know how things work around here. When I say a case is closed, there is a damn good reason for it. I don't need you digging into old cases like this or you are going to end up finding something you don't want to," said the Chief. "Aren't you the least bit curious?" I asked "This is our job, to protect when we can." "No, our job is to find killers for people who are already dead. If you want to save people, go be a super hero or a crime fighter. This case is closed Van, drop it." "But sir," I had begun but was quickly interrupted. "I like you Van, that is why I'm going to keep you around. Normally I would have your ass thrown our in a heart beat. I have something else I need you to look into and seeing as your so good at research you should be able to find something and get some answers. I want you to find out what happened before the Day of Awakening." The Day of Awakening, that is what it has been known as. The day when we all seem to just wake up from a long deep slumber not remembering anything about our selves but having such intelligence that would take years to acquire. I just stared at the Chief like he was insane. How was I to find out about something that no one knows anything about. "Well, what are you waiting for. Get to work," said the Chief as he shooed me from his office. Maybe this was a big mistake taking them files in for the Chief to see. I lost my research and not only that, I have been given an impossible task. A task that I think is only a diversion to keep me away from the truth of what is going on at Gildus Labs. I will idulge the Chief though and try to look into the origin of our awakening, but I'm not going to quit researching this Gildus Labs or Doctor Argath. He must be stopped before anyone else is killed. One thing I don't understand, how can the Chief be so heartless? People are dying and he says our job is only to find out what killed the person, not save everyone else. Another thing that don't make sense is, aren't I doing my job? This virus is killing people, people I have seen laying there dead. The keep putting it off on an unkwnon virus, but I think they know exactly what it is. It was time to pay Gildus Labs a visit and ask about this 'Delta' virus. * * * I walk into GIldus Labs building a few hours later and find myself in a large lobby with black marble floors. The walls are made of the same marble along with a column that stood on either side of the reception desk. More black marble made up the desk. I walked up to it and behind it sat a rather attractive red head. Freckles covered her face a good bit, and usually I'm not one to find this kind of woman attractive, but her, it worked for her. She was doing something on the computer in front of her and she didn't seem to notice me standing there, or perhaps didn't want to notice me. I made a little coughing sound to get her attention. She looked at me with a rather annoyed look and said, "Can I help you sir?" "Yes you can when you get that bug out of your ass," I said to her rather annoyed by her annoyance. She might be beautiful but that doesn't prevent her from being a complete bitch. "I don't care for your tone sir." I whip out my badge showing the DCPD symbol to show her that I am somewhat of importance and that I shoudl be shown a certain amout of respect. :I need for you to tell me where I can find Doctor Tilius Argath. I have a few questions about one of his experiments." She then gives me this look that I probably won't soon forget. She looked at me shocked as if I should know exsactly what is going on in the lab. She then hits a button on an intercom box and from the other side of the intercom comes a male voice. "What is it Angela?" "There is a police officer here to see you sir," said the girl who's name was obviously Angela. "I'll be right out." "He'll..." "I know. I heard him," I said in a kind of rude way to make her feel the same way she was making me feel. A few seconds later a man in a long white coat comes from the corridor on the right side of the reception desk. "Well hello Van," said the Doctor as if he knew who I was. "You seem confused?" "Guess I shouldn't be to surprised. You were still pretty out of it the last time you saw me." "Could you please explain to me what you mean?" "During the Day of Awakening, there were a few people found stil asleep. Some of them seem to be taking seizures. You were one of the ones that took a seizure. You were in luck though that such a smart Doctor was asked to help these people out. Most ended up dying unfortunately. I was actually quite surprised you lived seeing as you were one of the worst of the bunch. "Your boss was also one of the ones that I had to take care of. He was much easier though, but his life was still threatened by it. I think if it wasn't for saving him I think I would have been in trouble already." So that is why the Chief wanted me to quit looking into the case, this guy actually saved him and I both. I guess I owe this man my life but I still can't turn a blind eye on experiments that are killing people. I had to tell him what I found regardless of what he has done for me. "Thanks," I began. "I guess I owe you my life. I'm not here to discusss that though I'm afraid. There has been a few deaths latly, at least that is what everyone else is calling them. I call them murders because I believe they were murdered." "What do you believe the cause is?" asked the Doctor. "You, or should I say something you created." "I don't understand?" "I've done a little research on this unknown virus that has killed these people. It turns out that the virus that is killing people is the same one that you have been using to create a new bio-weapon. Care to explain this to me?" "I feared this was going to happen," said Doctor Argath in a rather sad and upset voice. "I had a couple vials of the Delta Virus go missing a couple of weeks ago. What your research has yet to find is that I did have a partner. He took off a couple of weeks ago, the same day the vials went missing, and I haven't heard from him since." "Well this would co-inside with the start of these deaths cause by your virus." "I do still feel responsible. I should have kept my partner in check. So what are you going to do now?" I stood there thinking, what could I really do? For all I knew this man was telling the truth and I could be arresting an innocent man. I had to bring him in though, not as guilty man but so that he could at least give his statment. I know that the boss will be a little pissed about this because he asked me to drop the case, but he thinks he is protecting a friend that is in trouble when in reality, by doing so, he is helping the guilty man get away. "Know that what I'm about to do is to help prove your innocence," I began. "I need to ask you to come down to the station with me and give a statment. If my boss is trying to protect you, he is helping the actual guilty party get away. Will I have your cooperation?" "Absolutly," answered the Doctor. "I'm not going to cuff you but I will have to put you in the back of the car. I'm not allowed passengers in the front." Both Doctor Tilius Argath and myself left the Gildus Labs building and headed for the station where he would give his statment to prove even to my Chief that he really is an innocent man from the beginning. * * * Doctor Tilius Argath sat behind the one sided mirror. Another man was inside asking him questions about the virus and his partner who took off with a couple of the vials. Chief Gilthor walks into the room I'm in and stares at me with intense anger. "What the hell are you doing!?" he yelled at me. "I thought I told you to drop this case!?" "Sir, the man you've been trying to protect is innocent. He told me all about the Day of Awakening and what he did for the both of us. That is why you wanted me to drop the case wasn't it? I asked. "You don't seem to understand that what the Doctor is trying to do is protect this cith from outside influence! That is why he created this virus! A potential illegal practice that could land him in jail for all of those deaths regardless if it was him or his partner! Your lucky I don't put you out on the streets for this!" "Then why don't you!?" I yelled back at the Chief. I had finally enough for being yelled at for doing my job and it was time to defend myself. "All I am trying to do is my job! I'm trying to be great at the only thing I know! You want to put me on bullshit cases that have to do with the Day of Awakening when it has nothing to do with my job! I investigate murders, nothing else! You want me to look into the Day of Awakening, that's fine, but don't think that is going to keep me away from my real job! You say about protecting this city from corruption and the like? Don't you think maybe you are part of the reason by not letting me do my job?" I walked out of the room and headed for my cubical where I do most of my work. It felt good to tell the Chief how I actually felt about the things he's doing. I might lose my job because of this, but in the end, it might actually be worth it.
