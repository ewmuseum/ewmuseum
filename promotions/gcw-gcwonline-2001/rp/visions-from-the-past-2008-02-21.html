Visions From The Past

21 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/21/08 What have I done? I asked myself that question a lot during the time of my separation. My wife was a real pain but that didn’t exemplify the love that I had for her. It was her other side, when she had those eyes, brilliant in every way and almost orgasmic. __________ “Mark,” she shouts, surprised and also angered. He looks at her up from between her legs. “What,” he simply replies. “What are you doing down there” I’m not in the mood, baby.” “You always say that,” he exclaims. She shuts the book and eyeballs Mark. She sets the novel down on the bed-stand and closes her legs, curling them up beside her and underneath the warm comforter. “It’s all you think about, let’s just cuddle for once.” “Are you kidding me?” The conversation seems to be going in the wrong direction if Mark is trying to score with his woman. He slowly positions his body next to her and tries to put his arm around her. The attempt fails, miserably. “No.” “Alright, you’re acting like a child,” Mark argues, backing away from her and crossing his arms, displaying a tantrum of his own. “Please, Mark. Don’t kid yourself on that one, look at you.” “What?” “It’s all you want and I can’t right now.” “Why” Is there someone else?” He asks another grueling question. “No, don’t be stupid.” “Then what is it?” There’s a slight pause in between each answer. “I’m.” There’s another pause in her speech. “Pregnant.” There’s another stretch of silence, but it doesn’t pass. __________ “How is your sex life?” They both look at each other and then back towards the doctor. He can sense a bit of distance between them and he speaks, “I see that you’re pregnant, so there is something there.” “Yes, but she didn’t even want to tell me about it,” Mark erupts. The statement seems to make “her” spring as she’s fast to defend herself. “That’s a lie,” a simple tone in her words. She gazes back at Mark, a smile etched across her face. He doesn’t seem too pleased about the grin, but he doesn’t make it a problem. “Well, you seemed timid to tell me about it.” “I’ve been with a great deal of couples,” the doctor acknowledges. “But, you two have something I’ve rarely seen in my practice.” This remark silences the room, which had sounded like children at recess previously. “I don’t believe you two have ever sought treatment before. It’s apparent to me that this void between you two has been built up for over a period of time, causing a lot of stress for both of you. Have you ever just wanted to be alone and away from the other” I’m not saying to be with someone different but just a break, a time to breathe on your own and to relax with out any worries of a break-up or fight?” The question still has the two in silence among the other words the doctor has spilled. __________ “Push, you can do it.” The room is filled with nurses as they all rush around the small hospital room. A doctor stands in front of her and examines the baby as its being partially shown. “Can you push any harder, dear?” The doctor asks. Mark just stands alone, looking a bit out of it as all of the drama commences right before his eyes. “It’s a boy,” the Doc exclaims, taking the baby over to another table to have it looked at, cleaned and secured in a blanket before the mother is able to hold him. “Come over here, Mark. What’s your problem; I could use your support.” “I just can’t believe it’s really happening.” “Well, aren’t you happy?” “Yes I’m happy, I mean?” “Whatever.” __________ I know what I have done. It’s all over now. I have come to realize that all things were not meant to be. The world wasn’t ready for a couple like my ex and me and it won’t be for as long as I live. I dread what my son might be going through, but the hell I endured is beyond the suffering he’s undergone. He’s my boy but I can’t care for him one hundred percent if I have to do it under her rules and within her house. I love my son, but she’s like a cancer to me. __________ “Catch,” Mark yells as he throws the football towards Dan. Dan jumps into the air in a diving motion and pulls the ball into his chest. “Damn, that was awesome.” Mark tells himself, excited to see his son performing very well in the hard hitting sport of football. “How’d you like that, Dad?” “It was sweet. I can’t even do that!” They both laugh and Mark begins to run towards Dan, tackling him to the ground and wrestling around with him in a playful form. They stop and Mark gets serious, “Do you want to play football, or wrestle.” “I don’t know, I haven’t really thought about it.” “I’m just asking because I was talking to the middle school coach and he thinks you’d be a fine wrestler. He’d teach you a lot and you’d be following in my foot steps, but you also have the soft hands to play football, pulling in a few TD’s as wide receiver. It’s your call, Dan.” He shrugs a bit a then gets up. He picks up the ball and tosses it into the air, watching it as it falls back into his arms. “I’d like to do both, I could, you know.” “Yeah, but isn’t that a bit much for you, with homework and everything.” “I’m doing fine in school, Dad. I haven’t gotten a B yet, A’s straight across.” “You’re so smart, did you know that. I love you too.” “I know.” __________ “Do you know what he means to me?” “Yes, Mark, I realize he’s your son but let’s face it, you’re never around for him.” “That’s a lie!” He slams his fist down onto the counter, enraged as he eyeballs his wife. “We need this divorce and I’m just letting you know that I’m getting custody of him. You’ll have weekends, it’ll work out Mark and everything will be better, trust me.” He shakes his head, “No, I can’t agree with you on that.” “Why do you always have to be so unreasonable?” “I’m not being unreasonable. We’re talking about our son and who gets him. We shouldn’t have to share something we both brought into this world.” She begins to break down. He slams his hand against the wall and turns away. “I just love that kid,” he weeps. His wife slowly slides down the wall, sitting with her head between her legs. __________ Things never will be the same. It’s a battle that I lost from the get-go, but now it’s just a matter of surviving in any possible way. That’s my only option.
