Stale Cell and Flickering Light

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Past Sweat plummets down off of Zacharia's brow and lands on the dirty cement floor below him. The environment isn't so kind; baring harsh colors on the cement walls in a five by nine area, open on two sides to a small corridor. A single light twitches in and out of life as its lifelines choke against the howling wind only faintly heard from the inside. No fan, no food, and no water. Only heat. A scorching heat that the streets of Miami or LA would feel, but this is a dry heat indoors only to irritate those held within. Nobody said that a holding cell was supposed to be comfortable. "Not what ya expected?" A sore, raspy voice encourages the dry heat to cool off for a moment as he is distracted out of his meditation. Zacharia looks up from his hands and looks into the opposite corner he is sitting in. A wretched shadow stands out in the dimmed corner. The unrecognizable figure takes on something from a nightmare with the raspy voice. "Huh?" Zacharia beads his eyes to try to figure out the form. "The holdin cell. Not what ya would expect? Well, Hollywood jazzes it up a lil but this is the real deal." The shadow takes form as the figure leans forward. A boy no older than Zacharia stares back at him. His cheeks are raggedy and splotched with the sixteen year old's attempt at a beard and his eyes are deep and weathered. Zacharia stares into his eyes as if looking into a mirror. "Well, I did not know what to expect, to be honest, but I guess I understand the necessity. Sure is uncomfortably hot though..." "Whoa, Poe, slow it down wit the high society lingo. I knew ya new blood, but damn." The figure readjusts and smiles. A distinct half crescent line distorts the wrinkles of his smile. "I've seen my fair share. I don't think this is high class, but its no bottom grade bared paradise. What ya in here for anyways?" "I guess you can say that I was in the wrong place at the wrong time, but to get technical, assault." Zacharia uses the back of his hand to wipe away the newly formed spheres on his forehead. "I was walking home from..." "No need to explain, kid. I don't want ya whole life story..." "What are you in..." "I'm here on a bogus fuckin' pick up. Holdin' me here till they figure me out, but hell, nobody can fuckin' do it if I can't..." He throws his hand over his shoulder as if to say "no big deal" but his eyes show a different story. He coughs a fake cough, straining his neck, also revealing some spotted scars. "Pigs don't know when to give up on a lost battle..." Zacharia tries to study his cellmate but draws too much attention. "Why you eyein me like some kinda project or some shit?" "Your scars. I just...curious I guess." "N' you wanna hear what I'm all about, I get you. I respect that. Most people wanna know who they spittin with before they get into some shit..." "What is that one on the side of your face." He points over in the direction of the half crescent. "What is that one all about?" His face drops into a chilled fright and he rubs the scar with his hand as if to try and make it disappear. "Yeah, jump right into the fuckin' shit... This one is, well, its got some fuckin' history, that one. My fuckin' mom's boyfriend, man. He got me good one night when I was comin home from a party up on Broad. Drunk fuck was outside with a Hein' in his hand yellin about some shit or another. I walk past his drunk ass n he comes through with some shit about how I don't help out my mom's. I say this, that, n the third...next thing you know, he comes across with the fuckin bottle and cracks me in the face. Believe that shit?" Zacharia slightly regrets asking about the scar and shakes his head, squeezing up against the bars of the cell like some retreating alley cat. "Mother fucker got his dues though..." "What do you mean? What...?" "Nah man, you don't wanna hear my fuckin sob story. What about you, bro?" "I..." How do you tell someone the truth? "Well, I do some training at the gym, study..." "What kinda shit you tryin to pull? Come on, man!" The ominous teen smiles his wretched look, but narrows his eyes to figure out Zacharia. "What's your fuckin' story, Poe?" Zacharia studies him for a second, staring at the teens scarred face. "Well, I punish those that defy the rules of God. I live at a church down...." "Wait, what? You some kind of Jesus mother fucker aren't you? Shit, I shoulda known." "Its not like that, it is more..." "Hey, more or less of whatever the fuck ever you believe in man, that's your business, but tell me, Pope, what kinda Roman crucifixion punishment shit you doin?" Zacharia tightens up, pressing his eyes into each of the teens eyes. "I, well, under the rules of my priest, I find those that deliberately defy God and punish them. I let God's power posses me and I unleash the pain as payment for their sin..." A stale silence falls between them. Zacharia begins to feel embarrassed but annoyed by the judgment of the man in front of him. The dry air pains his cliff hung lips, wanting to say more, but afraid at the same moment. Finally, the teen speaks up. "That's some deep shit bro. So, you let loose on 'em. I hear you, every man deserves what he dishes out. Karma is a bitch, and you are the fuckin deliverer. tight man. I get it, but don't get carried away bro, I know too much about that shit. Let me tell ya. I'd be on your fuckin' list, if you even have one." Zacharia takes a deep breath and is surprised by the openness of this guy. He loosens his back's grip on the bars behind him. The kid stands up and takes a few steps into the center of the holding cell, standing under the dancing light. "Ya know how I said that mother fucker got his due? He used to fuckin walk into my room and just start hittin me. Just, layin into me like I was a slab of fuckin meat that he could ground and pound into a fuckin sirloin. One day, I came home from my boy's house, and I find the drunk mother fucking piece of shit on top of my mom's slappin her around. Belive that shit?" Zacharia tightens up, picturing the girl in the park being raped from weeks prior. A cold chill of malice overpowers the control of his back. The teen's eyes falls deeper with pain and anguish, and his posture is that of an animal ready to pounce. "I just, I fuckin let loose. I ran up to the mother fucker and kneed him in the side." His eyes enlarge and his hands raise like claws. "It made him roll onto the floor. I didn't even look down at my mom's, I couldn't. I saw out of the corner of my eye that she was all bloodied and fucked up. I just let loose. I couldn't even... I just fuckin went off. My hands were like fuckin sledge hammers, ya know?" The teen no longer sees Zacharia in front of him, but his eyes play as white screens to a graphic movie that hits home. He is reliving the moment, as Zacharia stands witness to the horrid act. "I was yellin, but I don't know what I was sayin. I was prolly going through every fuckin word in the book. I just laid into him, let him have every fuckin thing he deserved. I just fuckin let him have it. My mom tried to pull me off, I remember that, but she stopped. I didn't know why at the time, but she fuckin let me go. I just kept hittin and hittin. I didn't see my fuckin step father anymore, I saw a fuckin scumbag in front of me." His eyes begin to drain of emotion, letting out streams of steaming hot tears. His face became white along with his clenched hands. Zacharia struggles to move, but he can't. "I kept hittin, just kept it up, until I couldn't raise my fuckin hands anymore. I just... I couldn't... The fucker got what he deserved. Fuckin asshole ruined every aspect of my fuckin pathetic life." He was so close now that Zacharia's clothed knees were now soaking in the fallen tears and sweat from the reminiscent teen, burning the cuts below. "Anyway..." The teen clears his strained throat and uses the back of his hand to wipe away the salty mixture on his cheeks. "These fuckin marks," pointing to his neck, "were from his nails digging into my neck as I was beatin on him. I was so caught up in the fuckin moment that I didn't know that he was tearin at my fuckin neck. All I remember was silence after the whole thing. Fuckin pigs and doctors were in my fuckin apartment tryin to revive him, but I beat his fuckin skull in, fuckin bastard got what he deserved. My mom's testified to the whole situation and shit, kept me outa jail, but they sent me to see some fuckin docs and shit. Bullshit bro..." Zacharia just realizes that the teen's eyes are welling up with tears again. "I fucked up,I know that. I shoulda just let the fucker have a couple pumps from my fuckin hands and then let the fuckin guy go. I just... you understand right? Gettin all lost in emotion and shit..." Again, a stale silence devastates the cell. Zacharia finally musters the strength to talk up. "Look, I understand what you did... I know how you feel. When you get so infatuated with everything, so invested into the situation, you just lose yourself and become an act of God. I'm not saying anyone deserves the loss of life unless directly warranted by God..." The teen just stares into Zacharia's stern eyes and nods, reassuring what he thought to be right. Zacharia stops and looks down at his own hands, resembling similar scars across his knuckles. "I'm not fuckin' sayin I'm a saint, but I know what I did was right. I really believe that shit. But look, my time is up..." A guard comes into view in the hallway, walking with a clipboard towards the holding cell. "I never told nobody that shit, so take that as a fuckin confession, Pope. Maybe I'll see you around the streets. Ya never know who you'll run into these days..." "Uh, Judas Smith, we need you to talk to the detectives about the information you have them. We got his story, now we need yours. You turned him in after all kid..." Zacharia turns around and stares at the once again ominous teen standing in front of him. He gives Zacharia a wry smile, wretched by the crescent scar on his face. "Irony is a bitch aint it? Fuckin name served me right, huh?" Zacharia stands up next to the opening cell door. Judas leans in before walking up to the cop, "Not my real name though...But its all about the name..." He smiles again and begins to laugh. He continues to laugh down the hall with the cop, letting it echo through the holding cell. Zacharia looks down at his scarred hands again in the twitching light.
