The Encounter: Part 3

18 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/18/2008 Paul and Delylah sat huddled together in the corner on the Master Bedroom of the Nguyen estate. Trying to find a way to cheat fate. The only advantage they had was their torturer had shown a weakness, she had shown human emotion. From all the things Delylah had learned of her type is they were not likely to show any emotion but hate, greed, anything ill of heart. Delylah had a plan, no more of a hope then a plan, to use Cleopatra’s emotional instability against her. They knew that it was a long shot but it was their only chance at survival. The sat and waited for the demon to return from feeding. Enriching her energy was her hope of succeeding in making Delylah and everyone she loved suffer a fate she thought they deserved. “I’m weak Del but I will be right behind you.” Paul whispered against her ear, “I will protect to.” He had to force these words to even form on his lips. He knew that they were a lie. Cleo had sampled just a little too much of his blood, he knew he was too weak to put up a decent fight. He would have to rely on brains and not brawn in order to survive the ordeal that lie ahead of him tonight. Delylah nodded and looked to her parents still bound to the head and footboard of the bed. Delylah had tried to pick the locks of their chains but it was a vain attempt. Now all they had to do was wait, something that their lives had been full of in the past 11 months. Paul laid his head against her shoulder and she wrapped her arms tightly around her lover. She knew that this could very well be the last time he would hold her. The last time she would smell him, a smell only a woman in love could describe. She pressed her lips to his enjoying the feel of his, etching them permanently in her mind and heart. They both snapped back to reality as the door slowly squeaked open. Cleopatra’s frame filled the crack in the doorway. Her body was fluid as she slid around the door and into the room like the snake she was. Her face filled with the glow of the first feed of the night. Delylah’s fury filled eyes met her sister’s satisfied eyes, a small smile played around Cleo’s lips before she spoke. “Now that I have all of my “favorite” people in the same room,” adding a sarcastic tone to the word favorite, “I can finally end all my pain and misery.” Delylah couldn’t suppress a hysterical laugh from escaping her lips, “End all your pain and misery,” using the word your as an instrument of torture, “I guess the whole world has just treated you so badly hasn’t it?” Paul grabbed Delylah’s arm trying to silence her, the anger and frustration filled her evil half’s eyes. Delylah’s lips twisted in a tortured smile, knowing that she was getting under Cleopatra’s skin. “You’re the daughter of a once very respectable doctor. The best in the Gynecological field of medicine, and being the best won him a daughter that he couldn’t produce because of his inadequacies.” Every word dripped with venom as she closed the distance between her and Cleo. Paul jumped to his feet and rushed after her but was stopped dead in his tracks when Delylah spun to meet him eye to eye. He slowly walked backward, he had never seen her eyes filled with such determination, and such hatred in all the time he had known her. “I have to do this myself Paul. I should have never drug you into this.” Her eyes flickered, a small amount of compassion slipped through, just enough to show him she still had control of the situation. Delylah spun around and met her sister’s gaze matching its intensity. She knew that she had to appear unafraid, that she had to maintain control of Cleopatra’s emotions. It was the only way to survive and end the torment she and her loved ones had endured for nearly a year now. “I know what you have done to him,” Delylah hissed as her gaze locked on the body of Elias Nagatti, “If you hated him so much why did you make him that way?” “Shut up, you have no clue,” Cleopatra clamped her mouth shut in an effort not to feed her sister ammunition, “My childhood wasn’t what it should have been.” Cleo looked down at her adoptive father and mumbled low enough that she thought no one would hear, “No clue what it was like to not exist.” An abrupt end came to the battle for control when a shriek pierced all of their eardrums. Nebt-Het had woke up, her eyes filled with horror. She looked at Cleopatra and she began to tremble she searched the bed beside her with her eyes making sure her husband was still alive. Her frightened eyes shot to Delylah as she fumbled on her words, “I” I know what she has planned Del.” Nebt-Het finally spat out the words and then went into a trance trying to suppress the vivid memory of her dream. “You only think you know what I have planned mother. I only let you see the half of it, I wanted you to see your precious daughter die.” Cleo spoke each word deliberately filled with hate and envy. “I know even if you would have kept me she would still have been your favorite.” These words made Nebt-Het flinch with pain. “I” I would never?.” “Shut up!” Cleo shouted making her mother retreat back into her shell of timidity. “I don’t need to hear your words to know the difference between truth and fiction.” Amun let out a slight moan that caused Cleopatra and Delylah to snap their heads in his direction. Cleo was the first one to regain sight of her mission and took Delylah out with a chop to the throat. Delylah dropped to the floor grasping her throat. Cleopatra cackled and met Paul with a boot to the groin as he charged her. As soon as he dropped to the ground she assaulted his temple with a vicious kick that sent a shut off notice to the light that lit his eyes. She dropped to her knees beside her gasping sister. “You never stood a chance in outwitting me sister. Now it is time for you to sit and watch as my plan unfolds.” Cleo whispered softly in her ear, “It is time for our parent’s to repent for their sins.” Cleo moved toward the body of Elias Nagatti and slowly undid his bonds. He crawled to his knees, and slowly climbed to his feet. His eyes rested on Delylah’s face, showing that her life force was a drink that he would savor. Cleo pointed toward their father and Nagatti was on the move. Delylah tried to get to her feet, she had to try and stop him. Cleopatra kicked her in her ribcage, a loud crack resonated through the room and Delylah was back on the ground. She gasped for air and held her ribs knowing that most of them were broken. A searing pain enveloped her entire right side as she tried to make it to her feet again but failed. Cleo’s face filled with satisfaction and she stomped the stiletto heel of her boot into her sister’s hand. She had to ensure that he sister was not a factor in their father’s demise. Cleo yanked Delylah up by her hair, locking her arm around her throat so she could see Elias standing over Amun’s body. He watched his prey with intensity, his eyes only flickering away to see the warm body of the now unconscious Nebt-Het. “Do what I have brought you here to do father. You show your forgiveness for the sins you committed against me, your maker.” Cleo emphasized the word me making it clear that she was the victim. Delylah tried to yank her head away, not wanting to see Amun’s canines pierce the tender flesh of her father’s neck. Cleo held Del completely still, “You will watch this,” Her tone filled with a regal tone, “I command you.” With those three words Amun Nguyen’s fate was sealed. Elias? fangs pierced through the skin and into the carotid artery. The blood sprayed into his mouth as he sucked every last ounce of the vital sustenance that kept him roaming this world. Delylah choked on the vomit that rose into the back of her throat as she saw the blood covered face of Nagatti emerge from her father’s neck. He stood next to the bed looking at Nebt-Het with hunger in his eyes. There was a loud thud that turned both Nagatti’s attention to the far corner of the room. In a split seconds time Delylah freed herself from Cleo’s clutches with an elbow to the bridge of the nose. Paul flew through the air hitting Elias with a shoulder in the side. Elias landed on the cherry night table shattering it. Paul acting on instinct picked up a shard of the wood and plunged it into his black heart. Fear, sadness and shock flooded Cleopatra’s eyes as she bum rushed the door, Paul lunged barley missing her as she escaped the room with her life. The familiar sound of the tumblers in the lock shattered Paul and Delylah’s hopes. Delylah placed her ear against the door in an attempt to hear if The Gatekeeper was still on the other side of the door. “I need to sleep Vassago, we have to leave this house now.” Cleo told him rashly, “I need to get away from them” now.” The word now almost sounded full of fear. “I will have Rubis clean up the mess. You get yourself as far from here as possible and I will catch up.” Vassago told her coolly, his voice always exhibiting complete control over any situation. They were once again trapped in a room with only one exit. They had made it this far; only a little patience would be required of them. They would escape Cleo this time, they would make sure of it.
