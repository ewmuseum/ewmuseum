Date: 12/15/2008 Chapter 1: My Apartment 4:30 pm In the time it took for me and Max to get to the apartment, I realized that if I ever lacked money I could write a story about my life and make a movie out of it. Here I was an average hardworking professional wrestler, who was trying to get Ben, the baby boy of my dead girlfriend, out of the clutches of Greg, a high-powered corporate executive who wanted to increase his wealth through Ben’s trust fund. In addition, the only person who was helping me prove that Greg was not the father was Max, the detective I hired. Luckily, despite his sexual identity issues, Max turned to be arguably good at his job. At the very least he managed to get a bunch of flash-drives, and if we were lucky one of them would show that Greg was really a pimp. Unfortunately things were not going quite as well as I would have hoped. “Fuck,” Max snarled as yet another message popped up saying that the flash drive could not be opened. That had been the tenth flash drive he tried, and we were able to open none of them. “I could understand why he would encrypt these two,” he said, pointing to the two that Greg’s partner had bothered to hide, “but making all of them inaccessible just doesn’t make sense. In fact I am not even sure what he did to them, it’s not like they ask for a password. It’s almost like the flash drives know I’m not Greg, and then tell me to fuck myself.” I ignored his rant and calmly indicated for him to try another drive. Inside I was seething with frustration, but I was determined not to show it until the very last drive had been tried. But my hope grew dimmer and dimmer with every obscenity that Max yelled at. Desperate for a distraction, I took to examining one of the two hidden flash drives-which basically meant staring at it aimlessly. I closed my hand around it, as if I could squeeze out whatever secrets it hid. I felt its smoothness and ? I frowned as I felt a slight scratch on the side of the drive. I looked at it again closer, but while I could make out that there was something embedded in the drive I could not it well enough. “Max,” I said as he cursed his latest failed attempt. “You stupid cunt,” “Max.” “I hope you’re raped by a baboon!” “MAX!” He cut off his latest rant and turned to look at me. “What the fuck do you want?” “Do you have a magnifying glass?” “Why in the hell would I have a magnifying glass?” “Well, you’re a detective.” He gave me a look of contempt before responding. “See that is exactly the stereotypical bullshit I always have to deal with. Just because you see one cartoon that shows the private investigator, which by the way is the proper term, looking at something through one of those useless things, everyone assumes I have one.” “So do you have one?” “Well of course I do, fuck man how could I not” In case you haven’t noticed Americans love to judge on stereotypes, if I have a magnifying glass I am a detective, if I don’t I’m Dog the bounty hunter. Now what do you want me to look at?” I tossed him the flash drive, and watched as he meticulously studied it through the grass. His face took on an unusually serious tone as he concentrated, particularly as he examined the side in which I felt the scratch. “There’s a switch” he muttered, and flicked it with his fingernail. Without prelude Max popped the flash drive back into the computer, and this time it opened instantly. “Nice!” Max shouted gleefully, “and people said I would be bad at this job.” “You are,” I whispered, looking innocent when he turned towards me. He shrugged, and we began to explore the contents on the drive. The first folder was full of nude pictures of females. Max decided it would be prudent to examine each and every picture, and I admit I had to agree with that. But eventually we opened the folder named “Johns”. “Dude, I know these guys.” Max whispered. “Judges, lawyers, cops, can’t any of these fuckers get laid without paying for it?” It was an impressive list of names. Each file had a picture and of the client, along with preferred hookers, dates and times, the client’s occupation, marital status, and whether they owed Greg any money. My teeth grinded as I realized just how hard it would be to bring Greg down. So many of his clients were powerful, and they would probably spare no expense to ensure that he would remain unexposed. Otherwise their source of easy girls would dry up, and Greg might decide to name names. As I was thinking about the breadth of the problem, Max opened another file, named “Sam Sherban”. The first thing we saw when we looked at his file, were the words at the top of the page which said “To be terminated”. It did not take us that long to figure out the meaning of those words. “Why the hell,” I asked Max, “would Greg want to kill him. I admit, I’ve never been a good businessman, but killing off your customers seems like a bad idea. “Who knows, or cares?” Max replied snidely. “the important thing is that we’ve got enough to blackmail Greg with.” “Blackmailed?” I questioned, “wouldn’t it be easier to go to the police, and let them arrest him.” “Oh sure,” he sneered, “we’ll just go and say, yo copper, we have proof that this rich guy, is a pimp. Plus, his clients are all powerful men, some of could very well be your bosses. Not to mention, the evidence is flash-drive that two guys stole from a strip-club that is connected to Greg, so go ahead and arrest him. While you’re at it, get some of your female coworkers to suck our dicks please.” “Alright, alright,” I interrupted grumpily, “so what do we do” Call him and ask him when would be a good time to issue threats?” “A definite possibility or we could just wait near his car, and threaten him when he tries to go home. This way there is less of a chance he could record us, and then twist our words to get us arrested for something or other.” “Well do we know what time Greg gets out of his office” I don’t feel like waiting around all day.” “It’s not that hard to figure out. Let’s say he spends the morning on his actual company. Eats lunch at twelve, and does one of his hookers at 12:30. At 12:35-? “Five minutes?” “It’s a hooker; he’s not going to bother with foreplay. But fine, we’ll pretend he does. At 12:37 he checks the status of the rest of his girls. Spends an hour calling clients, getting requests, and demanding payment. Then he watches some TV, does other leisure activities, and is done with the day by five. Might do his hooker again so he’s out the door ten minutes later.” “How do you know he’d do all that?” “Isn’t that what’d you would do?” I nodded and looked at the clock. “Alright, if we hurry we can make it a few minutes before he leaves for his car. Let’s go.” Chapter 2: Parking Lot 5:15 pm We arrived later than we had hoped, around five fifteen. I ran into the parking lot hoping that Greg would be held up, and then realized I had no idea what kind of car he drove. Considering the huge size of the lot, and the fact that most of the spaces were still occupied, I held little hope that I would be able to figure out which car was Greg’s. “We’re looking for a blue Bentley Continental GTC,” Max remarked behind me. At my look he sighed and elaborated, “It’s standard procedure for a private investigator of my immense caliber to find out what car the target drives. Luckily, rich bastards like Greg have a tendency to pose with their cars on the company website. All it took was a quick google search of his company. “Impressive,” I said admiringly, “now, what does a Bentley look like?” His face took on a tone of disbelief and contempt. “What does-, oh my God are you really a man” Just, look for a blue car that looks expensive as hell alright?” “Dude,” I pointed out as I looked out across the lot, “a lot of them are blue, and they all look expensive as hell.” “Alright new plan,” Max decided through clenched teeth. “I’ll go look for the car, you stay here and keep an eye on the door. If you see Greg, just yell his name out loud enough for me to hear and stall him. Do not attempt to blackmail him without me, you’d mess it up beyond repair.” He did not wait for me to respond but instead set off to look for the car. I sighed, wondering what we would do if Greg had already left, and began to watch the door. I must have spent at least ten minutes just staring at the damn door, just wishing it would open and Greg would come out. To my surprise it actually did. Out came Greg, in an expensive Armani suit looking very disgruntled for some reason. I began to open my mouth to yell as Max had instructed. “GREG! You bastard!” Someone had beaten me to the punch. I looked around, wondering who had called out before looking back to see someone standing toe to toe with Greg. Greg stood at six feet, and was broad shouldered enough that he would not be messed with at a bar. In fact, in terms of body type he kind of looked like Dana White. But his face looked just like the picture Max showed me. Basically, Greg resembled a weasel in human form. I could not see the other man’s face, but he was a diminutive figure when compared to Greg. He was also wearing a suit, thought it did not seem to be as expensive as Greg’s, but as far as I could tell he had the body type of one of those geeky kids in high school that get their heads flushed down a toilet. The two men began to converse in low tones, too low for me to overhear what was being said. Within seconds the discussion between them became incredibly heated, until Greg finally had enough. From my view point I saw Greg’s hands clench, and he threw a powerful punch at the man’s face. The force was enough to spin the man 180 degrees, enough for me to see his face. To my shock and horror I recognized the man to be Sam Sherban, the man whose file said he was marked for termination. Even worse as I looked at him I saw him reach deep into his suit for something, and I could easily what it was. I set off at a run, but I was to far away to do anything. Before I was even halfway there Sam brought the gun around and fired three shots into Greg’s chest. Sam stared at Greg’s body in disbelief, as if he could not believe what he had just done. I did not blame him, I hated Greg, but the shock of seeing someone killed in front of my eyes temporarily muddled my wits. Sam turned and saw me run towards him. His face paled, and he abruptly fled in the opposite direction, going so fast that I knew there was no chance that I would be able to catch up to him. I didn’t even try; instead I dropped to my knees to check Greg’s pulse, and sadly realized he was dead. Don’t ask me why I felt sad at the knowledge that Greg was no longer a problem. Maybe it was a gut reaction to seeing a human life extinguished. Maybe it was because I realized that would Greg’s death there might be another vulture coming around to pick off Brian, and the game would start all over again. Maybe it was because I just realized I got my finger prints on a dead corpse. By the time I stood up Max had arrived, alerted by Sam’s shout. He stopped dead in his tracks when he saw Greg’s body, looking at me disbelievingly. “What’what did you do?” I shook my head and dully replied, “Nothing. Sam Sherban got to him, and, oh god I checked his pulse. But Sam never touched Greg!” Max’s eyes widened as he understood the implications of what I was saying. Without a word he pulled a knife out of his pocket, and rammed it into the side of Greg’s neck. With swift and vicious cutting motions, he cut off the part of Greg’s neck that I had touched. “Let’s go,” he said coolly holding the piece of flesh in his hands. “We can flush this at your apartment, and then figure out what the hell we are going to do.” I didn’t say a word as we ran for the car. Chapter 3: My Apartment 7 pm I waited anxiously as Max flushed the evidence down my toilet. “Alright,” he said as he stepped out of the bathroom and sat down on my couch. “I don’t think that there is anything connecting us to the murder anymore, but there is no reason to take chances. We’re going to look closer at the files we found on the flash drive, and then we’ll go hide it in case we need it.” I nodded in affirmation, still took shaken up to verbalize. We headed to the computer, where the folder was still open from when we first looked at the files. “Let’s look at Greg’s clients a bit more in depth,” Max continued seriously. In the back of my mind I realized he had lost much of his jovial and vulgar nature, but I figured it was a normal reaction to the circumstances. “Two Senators, “Max read off, “five Representatives, three Federal Judges, I’m kinda surprised Bill Clinton isn’t on here.” “I am sure Bill Clinton has his own private supply,” I managed to joke. “You mind if I turn on the news” Greg’s death might have been reported by now.” Max nodded and I turned on a local news station. Sure enough, the newscaster was reporting on Greg’s death. “His body was earlier today. Greg Bifaro was known to have had a number of enemies, but at this time it is unknown whether the police have any leads. Stay tuned as we bring you more on this gruesome murder.” Max and I looked at each other in relief. Obviously the police had not found anything, which meant that we could finally relax. Or so we thought. But mere seconds after I turned off the TV, there was a knock on the door. “NYPD,” a loud voice shouted, “open up.” Quick as a flash Max took the flash drive out of the computer, and shut the machine down. “Coming,” I yelled as Max stuffed his pockets with all our other flash drives. “I’ll hide in the bathroom,” he whispered. “If they want you to go downtown go, it may give us a chance to find out what they know. If they have anything on you, say they’ll have to talk to your lawyer. If it turns out they have enough to arrest you, I’ll get you a lawyer. Do you have a preference?” “My boss Steve Caldera,” I whispered back. “I am valuable enough to him, for this week at least, that he’ll probably want to get me out. Tell him what happened, and he might send his lawyer to defend me.” Max nodded and quietly headed towards the bathroom. Once the door was closed, I opened my front door to reveal two annoyed looking cops. “Mr. Black I presume?” said one of them. I nodded through a suddenly dry throat. “Sir, we have some questions for you regarding the Greg Bifaro murder. We would appreciate it if you would come to the station with us” It shouldn’t take very long.” “Alright,” I forced out, “but I have to be back in a few hours.” “We’ll do our best to end things quickly,” he said with a smile as they led me to the car. Chapter 4: Police Station 8:00 pm It was a strange feeling sitting in the back of a cop car, knowing that I was about to be interrogated in a police station. This sort of thing was something that was supposed to happen to the other guy, the guy on tv, or the neighbor, or the friend of a friend. Having it actually happen to you was a very disconcerting experience. The interrogation began as soon as I entered the room. The interrogation area looked eerily similar to the one you see on Law and Order. “Sir, can you tell me where you were today around six pm.” Wait, why were they asking me about six pm, instead of five thirty when Greg had died? I watched enough shows to know that the labs could easily tell when a person’s time of death was. Then it hit me, they hadn’t received the results from the lab yet, so they were just guessing when Greg was killed. It seemed strange, but Greg’s body must have only been found (or reported) about a half hour after he had been killed. I didn’t know why it took so long, but I would worry about that later. “At six pm I was at home,” I answered truthfully. “Watching the news.” “I see,” the detective said nodding his head. “Tell me, did you know Greg Bifaro before today?” I nodded my head. “I used to date Greg’s girlfriend. She was pregnant with his kid, but he had dumped her. She died in childbirth, and Greg took her son Brian in. I called him a couple times, cause I figured he didn’t really want the kid. He was a busy corporate executive, and I was more than happy to take care of Erika’s kid. But Greg said he was connected to the kid, so I back off.” The officer nodded, but there was a subtle shift in his demeanor. I could not put my finger on it, but I guessed that my answer had not served me as well as I might have hoped. “We actually searched the home, and found the kid. He’s with social services right now. Have you had any contact with Greg since then?” I shook my head in the negative. “Alright Mr. Black I appreciate your-? He was cut off as the door opened, and an underling handed him a piece of paper. The officer read it slowly, and then looked me up and down judgingly. “Mr. Black, why were your hair particles on Greg’s suit at the time of his death.” I licked my lips nervously, and cursed whatever strand of hair had fallen out. “Well,” I said haltingly, “I could not being to tell you. But if you have any other questions, I think you could ask my lawyer.” The detective nodded, and took out his handcuffs. “Dan Black, you are under arrest for the murder of Greg Bifaro. Anything you do or say?” I paced nervously in the cell, glad to have it to myself. It was a dark and grey place, exactly the way it was described in every book and movie. My mind was a mess with a thousand thoughts flying through my mind. I truly hoped that my lawyer was going to arrive soon, and hopefully find a way to have a bail set that I could actually pay. Spending the next month in jail would not be good for my health, or my career.
