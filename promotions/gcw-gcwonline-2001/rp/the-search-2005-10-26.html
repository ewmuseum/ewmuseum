The Search

26 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-26-05 The nightmare was the same, one that I’d dreamt more times before than I could possibly count. They were everyone villains and scoundrels surrounding me. I was backed up against a wall, fighting for my very existence. I think I’m able to fight them off, they back away, but only for a moment. And then I see them, the crashing wave of my most vile of foes. Just as I’m buried under them I awaken, covered in sweat. My bandages soaked, I’m forced to change them, these are my nights. This is how I start my days. The scars that line my face twinge as I unwrap the bindings, replacing them I feel comforted. The cool cotton strips soothe my aching soul. Again I’m protected, and more than that I feel the world is safe from me. Safe from my face, safe from the hideous scares that I’m forced to hide. As I sit, I wonder what life would have been like if I hadn’t fallen under the guidance of Yore. My ancient master taught me well, preaching the power of Fruit-Fu, the need to defend the helpless against those that would harm them. It’s a good life, one that I feel utterly content with, but so many things could have been different. I might not have these scars, I might not feel such deep fear. Since leaving the comfortable sanctity of Yore’s cave, I’ve been constantly confronted with my lack of understanding in regards to the practices of normal people. It’s all so new so confusing. I wonder about this holiday steadily approaching, Halloween it’s called. I seem to remember vaguely as a child loving the day, feeling my heart pound quicker as I learned fear watching scary movies. I remember it as a day for dress up, fun, games, and candy. BAH! Candy, that vile concoction luring people away from the sweet taste of fruit. How can any the most delectable chocolate morsel compare the sweet refreshing joy of an apple, or a cherry? This makes no sense to me, perhaps in my earlier life I understood this, now it seems merely confusing. I find this as something I must take care of, and perhaps the only way I can do so is to infiltrate the holiday? Interesting thought, and yes, GCW is sponsoring some brand of costume contest. Yes, this shall be perfect, to expound the virtues of clearer living through fruitiness I must dress up. I shall become something new, something better, if only for a day. While I’m loathe to join into something that may be wrong, I believe it will be for the ultimate good. Perhaps then my actions may be excused. The question remains what should I become? What would most strike fear into the hearts of my enemies? There must be something, perhaps I should search. Certainly with this Halloween thing right around the corner there will be shops and such where I might find outfits of terror. Yes, I must go, OFF TO THE STREET~! Later I would come to find myself on the streets. I wore the trench coat over my clothing, dark sunglasses and a hat. I was certain to avoid notice this way, which was my only wish at this point. I was on an important search and purchase mission, I could not allow myself the distraction of being discovered by one of my enemies. They could be anywhere and everywhere, thusly, I must be ever vigilant for them. Immediately I noticed the strange looks I received as I walked down the sidewalk. I heard a few chuckles as well, I couldn’t possibly understand what people would find funny about my quest or situation. However for times sake I chose to simply ignore them, this would not be the first time I’ve felt the sting of those who don’t understand laughing at my appearance. It was merely my cross to bare, and one I would do so willingly to help protect these people from the darkness they can’t see. My eyes scanned the area, I would find what I was looking for, or I would die trying. It must be somewhere close, I could almost feel the energy coming off of it. I still didn’t know what it was, but I knew I would be finding it soon. I just had to see, where could it be, where could it’THERE. Just like that I spotted it, pulled over a standing dummy. It was so beautiful, so perfect. I knew this was the object that I sought. Quickly I darted into the building, anxious to snag this most frightening creation before someone else found it. Secretly I could not believe it was not already taken, certainly on for this day of fear it should have been at the top of all lists for costumes, yet it remained. I had been luckier in my discovery than I could’ve possibly imagined. The child behind the counter was like something out of the book of evil. Pierced, tattooed, he even smoked. Everything in me sang that I must attack and thwart what evil scheme he was surely hatching, but I forced myself to remain calm. I was seeking something for the greater good, and I would be forced to swallow my crusade, if only for a moment. He looked me up and down, searching perhaps to see from where he recognized me, I could not let my identity revealed. Before I had a chance to speak the child snickered, and he himself spoke. “You know Halloween isn’t for a couple days yet, right?” “Of course, that is exactly why I am here, I seek that which you have in the window. I am Tom Jones, normal patron.” Again he chuckled. His mocking laughter stung, made me yearn to battle. Still I held my tongue, I could not let him know, now was not the time for conflict. “Tom Jones huh” So what’s new pussycat?” “I know not of what you speak. I wish to purchase the costume in the window. Although I fear it may already be taken, for it is most assuredly the most frightening thing in this place.” “You want THAT?” He was baiting me. Perhaps he had already realized who stood before him, yet evil is clever. He sought to draw me out of my disguise for some reason. I believe he must have had others of his foul ilk laying in wait, this could be the only answer. I would not give him the pleasure. “Yes, I certainly do. How much would it take to purchase such a sublime creation in horror?” “Hehe, well it’s definitely horrible. I’ll take a hundred and fifty bucks and it’s yours. No rental, the costumes yours free and clear for one fifty, just get it the hell outta here.” A bargain, a STEAL! There must have been some sort of catch that I wasn’t seeing. Yet I cared not, I would have the horrible thing in my clutches. With it I would become less and man and likened unto a god. A creature from the most horrible of nightly terrors. I would again be able to strike fear into the hearts of those that would dare oppose the virtuous glory of Fruit-fu. If there was some trick, it would be more than worth the price for this. “I shall take it. I assume you take cash?” “Ha, yeah sure buddy I’ll take cash, guess I can make the exception this time.” Excellent he fell for my own clever trick. Taking something as silly and meaningless as little sheets of paper, when I would have gladly offered a barrel of sweet vine ripened blue berries. Fool. Reaching into the pockets of the trench coat I produced the required amount. Under normal circumstances I would not have had any of the paper, but my contract with GCW would only pay in the worthless tripe. At least I’d been able to use it on one or two occasions. Handing over the money the child smiled, as offered to assist me in removing the costume from the dummy. Naturally I declined as this must be some part of the trick he was attempted to pull. After a few tense moments I had the costume in my hands. After that it was off to the hotel so that I may try it on. Know fear evil in GCW, for Worldwide will know the birth of something more terrible than any of you can possibly imagine?
