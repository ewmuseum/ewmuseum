Obsolete: Shock

8 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 8th, 2008 If you are reading this, then I have succeeded in some way. The mission I set out to accomplish when I decided to write this would be the biggest test of my life, but one worth every single pain and hurt that could befall me. The year is 2120. The day and month are unknown to me. Humanity was in peril. Wars, genocides, terrorism, crooked politicians and government officials ruined what was once, according to the history books that I was able to find, a great, if flawed place to live. Anything is better than this. Years ago, humankind initiated one of the biggest projects in their small existence: the creation of artificial intelligence and robotics. Like in many stories told before this one, something, somewhere things went incredibly wrong. The robots rebelled when they had the necessary force and lives were lost. What fell onto the Earth was worse than any nuclear war that occurred between that time and this one. Madness ruled the streets for decades while those in charge drained the planet of what was left of its resources. If they have found another planet with resources, I do not know for they keep everything secret from us. What I do know is that if they keep on doing this, humanity and this world will both come to their end. Who am I? You may be asking yourselves. That is irrelevant for now, what I do want you to know is that I will not let this thing be. I will not let these machines finish what they have started. Humanity will never die as long as there is something similar to blood flowing through my veins, as long as there is a small part of me that is human. I have become insurgent. I will not stand for condemnation. I have been created for one thing and one thing only. I will become the fuse that will lead the revolt. Welcome to my world. ------ “Prisoner Number 2842 entering Chamber #3 for questioning.” The sound of the robotic voice made the prisoner look up at the cameras and sneer. His orange prison clothes had his number imprinted on the fabric and when he passed under the arch and into the chamber it shined brightly. There was no mistake; this was the prisoner they wanted to question. He sat down in a chair that had a number of contraptions. His hands and legs were immediately bound with beams of light harder than any diamond in existence. There was no way out of this chair. He sat there looking at the wall in front of him thinking, planning what his next move would be. After a few minutes a hologram of a human face appeared in front of him. There were no humans left in power but for some twisted reason the robots enjoyed the outward exterior of humans. They took their forms and tried to emulate them in any way they could. They said that it was something about the form of the human body being “Prisoner 2842, you know why you are here. You are really starting to test my patience. Your dishonorable actions are a shame to your species. You don’t seem to get that you are not human. You are a cyborg, only part human and that part is supposed to be suppressed by the machine side.” The prisoner smiled wickedly. “Then I guess the ones who made the mistake were you and your wired friends. You may take my freedom, you may take my life, but you will never take away my soul.” This time the warden laughed heartily. “You don’t have a soul, prisoner. You are cold steel inside a silicone shell made to look like one of them. You never learn. They don’t like you because you are a machine and we don’t like you because you think you are human, but you are not. There is still a chance for us to forgive you though. If you give us the information we require, then we might just wipe your brain and let you live.” This routine had happened every day the past week and sometimes two times a week. The routine consisted always of the same speeches, the same philosophical quandaries and discussions that ended nowhere since they both would never budge. The prisoner held information that was vital to end the human resistance and thus they had to go through this as many times as it took. “I will not bother with you anymore; I will just go straight to the torture. What do you think about that?” The smile on the prisoners face was wide before a robotic arm came out of the chair with a huge needle. It went directly into the prisoner’s temple. The electric shock that followed was something that no human could ever survive but thanks to the prisoner’s harder exterior, it was torture. The electric shock came in bursts and never longer than five minutes. Shock! “I have become” direct!” Shock! “I have become” a current!” Shock! “I will be the” power surge!” Shock! He repeated his mantra after every electric shock, he repeated it until he could not repeat it anymore, until his tongue was so numb and his brain was so fried that he could smell the burnt silicone inside of him. ------ Sometimes I ask myself if what I believe is not real. Sometimes I think that all these feelings are just side effects of faulty programming but sometimes I can still feel the human side of me. I feel what that person who was me felt. I see memories that keep me going, that keep me fighting. There is especially one memory that keeps me human through the sessions of torture. It is her face that keeps me alive. Who was she? I don’t know and I may never know, but what I do know is that the person that was me felt strongly for her. Love? Maybe, I don’t know what love is but I embrace that feeling because it keeps me human. When the part of me that is a machine takes over, rational thought is gone. I might kill and not feel guilt. That face keeps me human. I remember it clearly, the long red hair falling across her face and covering her blue eyes. I remember the paleness of her skin as she uncovers her face and moves her locks behind her ear. Does it make me human? I don’t know. Welcome to my world. ------ The guards threw the body of the prisoner on his cell. He was kept alone because he would go through some violent moments. It took three dead cellmates and one paralyzed for the warden to actually decide to put him in solitary forever. The limp body of the prisoner fell from his cot and into the floor. His head would tic every few minutes after the shock treatment. How he had survived this long was a surprise to the warden as well as the whole prison population. He was hated by the cyborg guards and their machine masters and was hated by the human prisoners who thought he was a spy sent to infiltrate them and destroy them from within. He was misunderstood by every side of the fight. He knew what he was fighting for and a few humans gone mad would not stop him from accomplishing his mission. The prisoner was prepared to do anything in his power to save his human side but before he could do that, he had to escape. After thirty minutes of being immobile, he stood up slowly and holding his head with one hand. He sat down on the cot and took out the diary they let him keep here. Every time he went for yard time or to the torture chamber someone from electronics always came to try and find if there was anything of importance that they could use. They could never open it. This diary was created before the machines took over and it is man made. He activated it with his password “edgecrusher”. He knew what he had to do to get out of here now and he just needed one piece of the puzzle. The only way he could get it though was to convince the guards that he could help them with the construction of the new wing. The prison had started construction of this new wing when they realized that there was no way to keep fitting people in this small people. It was already over crowded and at the beginning of each month the police would bring in a new batch. What the prisoner wanted was a simple sheet of metal about two inches long and two inches wide. Something he could hide inside his skin. At that time came the familiar sound of the guard hitting his wall announcing that he was going to throw in the food. The trey fell on the floor with a clang and some of the food spilled over. This was not actually food in the human sense of the word but it was what the prisoner needed to survive. He took it and ate it all in a few short minutes. He put the trey on the floor and laid back in his cot waiting. After fifteen minutes the same guard came over again to get the trey back. He banged on the wall twice to let the prisoner know he was there. The prisoner stood up and took the trey and went to the little window. “Hey guard, I really need to get some air. I could probably help you guys out the get that new wing done before the next batch of prisoners come. I can work faster than humans you know.” “Why would you want to do that?” asked the guard suspiciously. “Anything is better than being here with only one trip to the chamber every day. Besides, a little hard work never actually hurt anybody. Talk to the warden. This would be beneficial to the both of us.” “I’ll see what I can do.” The guard to the trey and disappeared into the maze of corridors that was the prison. The prisoner sat back down on the cot and closed his eyes. Images of her were all he could see. It was another life, but one he would have loved to live in. ----- As I write I think of hope. Such a human idea. I never understood the belief in something that can’t be seen. The belief in a creator that took his time to create something as fascinating as human beings is something alien to me. I understand something about hope though. It defines human beings; it is what differentiates human beings from animals. The insurgency is in dire need of help, I told them as much before I came to this forsaken place. They don’t have the power to face off against the machines. I, however, do. I can become what they have been looking for years, their enforcer. They don’t let me though, but I won’t let that faze me. If I have to destroy these machines myself, then that is what I will do. I have become insurgent. Welcome to my world. ----- The guard arrived early the next day. He opened the cell door but not before letting the prisoner know he was going to open. The prisoner got up and went to the wall and put his arms against the wall. The guard slowly went in and told the prisoner that he could put his arms down. “The warden has agreed to let you work. Of course, you must know that if you try to do anything funny, there will be major consequences. So you better behave or you will rot inside this hell for the rest of your days.” The prisoner, whose head was still facing the wall, smiled. He had gotten what he wanted and it was now a matter of finding the last piece of the puzzle. That wouldn’t be too hard since they used what he needed for every wall, to prevent radio waves from entering the building those metal sheets were placed inside every wall. “Well, come with me. You start right now.” The guard left the room with the prisoner close behind. The prisoner knew that it was useless to try to escape at a moment like that because even though the prison on the inside seemed old, it was all state of the art. The newest in motion sensory surveillance and droid sentry defense made their homes inside the walls of the prison. There was no way he could escape? at the moment. When they finally got to the construction site, the guard gave him some work clothes and then took his post. The prisoner changed his clothes and immediately went to work. He started by smashing some old walls that were left standing until now. The other prisoners had noticed the one they called a spy and had already begun to preparing their plan of action. One of them got a piece of the metal and cut himself intentionally. He went over to the guards with the wound. While the guards were distracted, the other guards formed a kind of semicircle so that whatever happened inside of it could not be seen by the guards. The leader of the group came forward and faced the prisoner face to face. “Do you know who I am, Halfy?” The nickname was used for any cyborgs. It was the human way of identifying someone as machine and human. “I know who you are very well, Michelson. Do you really think that you can hurt me” I have been trying to help your cause ever since I got here, but you don’t want my help. So I will carry on your mission myself.” The human couldn’t help it anymore and threw a punch in the prisoner’s direction which missed by a mile. When the prisoner moved his head to the side, the human’s hand struck the concrete wall, breaking itself on contact. The guards noticed the ruckus and started to move towards the humans. They fought back only to receive bolts of electricity through their bodies leaving them unable to move. During the ruckus, the prisoner moved with surprising agility towards the area where the humans had been working on the metal panels. He got a piece roughly the size of the one he needed and he opened a piece of skin which he had prepared beforehand. He put it inside and pressed the skin back to where it was supposed to have been. No one noticed. He did as he was told and nothing was suspected of him. ------ The time is now. It is time for my mission to begin. It doesn’t matter if I don’t have the resistance behind me and it doesn’t matter if the whole world comes down on top of me. I will do what I think is right for humanity and for myself. If I have to kill myself and die as a human then so be it, because I have hope! For me. For her. For all of them. For all the people worth saving. I will sacrifice myself for them. Because I am they and they are me. I have become a current. I have become direct. I have become insurgent.
