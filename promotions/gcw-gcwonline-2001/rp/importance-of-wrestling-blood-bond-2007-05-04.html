Importance of Wrestling: Blood Bond

4 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4 May 07 LeStatt Knight Just the thought of that name brings up so many memories it’s almost enough to make my brain overload. I have been around the man known by that name since 2001 and to be quite honest, for the majority of it I wanted nothing more than to rip the flesh off his bones. The hatred was warranted and he would have done the same to me in the blink of an eye. As a matter of fact we came close a number of times ? in and outside of the wrestling ring. Once upon a time I was one of the gladiators that put their lives on the line inside of the squared circle. Once upon a time I was looked at as an even greater prospect than the “mighty” LeStatt Knight. It was in that lifetime that my greatest enemy was the enormous Mexican with Icy blue eyes. However in that lifetime he was not the most dominant GCW World Champion ever or one of the best United States Champions ever ? Hell he hadn’t even won the Television Title. He was just a young man, hungry to be taken seriously. Hungry for the chance to shine. I still remember the first time we stood toe to toe, almost as if it were yesterday. I remember the look on Kevin Jacobs face when LeStatt pushed him out of the way and got right up into my face. I remember the blood trickling slowly down Knight’s face and pooling up right above his upper lip. I remember the sounds he made as his chest heaved up and down as he continued to catch his breath from being in the match he had just lost. But what I remember most of all? The determination in his eyes. I remember that better than the pain that I felt when his right hand collided with my temple and knocked my over. His eyes told the story of 1,000 lifetimes, the kind that I could relate to. Thus LeStatt Knight vs. Payne was born. This is a man that I have seen in every possible emotional state known to man. During our brutal war we put each other through hell and back and back again. Our hatred and the lengths that we were willing to go to had no boundaries. If you were close to one of us in any way then you were a target. Global Championship Wrestling only saw the beginning of our WAR. Our battles had everything Me stealing away his fianc’e, getting her pregnant then him smashing a steel chair over her head when he found out? CHECK! Him kidnapping my manager, Martin Smalls and having the letters “LK” carved into his chest? CHECK! Me defeating him the first two times and then having GCW close? CHECK! Us reuniting in another fed and being forced to be a tag team? CHECK! Our team demolishing everyone in our paths? CHECK! Me winning a World Title before he could get the chance? CHECK! Him damn near breaking my fucking back while taking that World Title? FUCKING CHECK! Throughout the entire time of knowing Knight I have understood one thing above all else, that being that he thrives on victory. I have never met another person that lived off of victory like him. LeStatt Knight knows only one speed and that is full throttle. If you are in a battle with him then you had better buckle your safety belt because the ride is going to be bumpy. Even in the darkest of times he is confident that he will succeed. I tried everything I could possibly think of to make him kneel before me and quit but he refused to do so. He never even had to speak and I knew he would never quit. Not unless he died first and for some reason I think he may have came back and haunted me until I had died. Persistent bastard. I never have and probably never will tell him that at times he would scare me. Something in his eyes, that fire, that hunger frightened me. It was as if he wasn’t meant to fail, he was far too gifted for that to happen. Worse part is, is that he finally has always known that too. I bet if a survey was taken from everyone that he has ever stepped into the ring with, 90% would tell you that there was something about him that exceeded expectations. If you ask the many who have lost to him? After digging through the bullshit you’d see, sense and probably taste fear. My days of fearing this man have been over for quite some time now. No matter the living hell that we put each other through and the fear that we sent crawling up and down each other’s spines ? there was a strong respect. I believe it the same with any epic battle. You may despise your enemy but you are forced to respect them because of what they have helped bring out of you. Your best It was this bond that brought us together years later as friends and allies. That’s a night I will never forget. As clich? as it may seem, the setting was a cemetery. At night. With rain. Told ya, clich?. Clich? or not though the two of us made an unspoken pact that night. We decided that killing each other was never going to solve the issues between us ? because deep down we didn’t want to hate each other. “Funny thing is, Alex ? you’re the only person I feel that I can trust anymore.” Those were the words he said to me as the rain drops pounded down around and on us. What I remember most about that time? Through all of the dark and rain and cold? The fire in his eyes, those icy blues penetrated the night and burned into my soul. His eyes told his story as they always had previously. “Son of a bitch!” Knight’s growl brought me back to today. “What?” “That was just pathetic.” On the television in front of us ? WorldWide 65, LeStatt Knight vs. Kyle James. He has been watching this match over and over again for a few hours now, picking apart each bit trying to figure out “what went wrong” as he calls it. “Roberto, you won the damn match.” “That punk kid had no business keeping that match as close as it was. PERIOD!” Always the perfectionist and he always will be. “Look, why worry about Kyle James now” The match is over; I mean I believe it is fairly apparent that on his best day he could never beat you on your worst.” This seems to calm him slightly, but not enough to make him forget that he isn’t happy with his performance. “You can never overlook a bad performance Alex, you know that.” He sighs. “It only takes one wrong move to end up with a loss and I refuse to lose this tournament.” “Who is your next opponent?” “Katie O’Clare,” Knight smirks. “Some bitch that I plan to break in half.” It’s amazing how much Knight has transformed over the years, from some rich snob wanna-be Triple H to “The Official DWA Jobber” to The Greatest GCW World Champion. If there was ever a man who has evolved, started from the very bottom and crawled his way to the top it is LeStatt Knight. What’s most impressive is that he has done it all on his own. Well mostly. “Listen amigo, I don’t normally ask these questions because you do what you want to.” He lifts an eyebrow. “Go on Alex.” “The Establishment” You’re better than that.” Now I’ve seen the way he reacts to this question to reporters, interviewers and fans ? normally he just sneers at them and walks away but this time he folds his arms over his chest and contemplates the question. After a bit of time he speaks up. “There are two main reasons Alex and neither of them have anything to do with wanting to be pals with Steven Caldera, if that is what you are thinking.” It pretty much was exactly what I was thinking. For years now he has despised The president of GCW because he felt that he held him down for years prior to Knight’s first World Title reign. “Then why?” “To save GCW.” He says matter of factly. “This fucking company has been going downhill since NC-17 2005. It should come as no surprise that this also coincides with the end of my first World Title reign. The place has become too mixed up and watered down with lackluster talent. GCW needs someone to take control and Bryan Mayhem is NOT that fucking person.” “So for GCW you would risk your reputation?” “My reputation?” His blue eyes narrow. “You really think that my reputation is going to fall because of this” Can anyone take away the things I’ve accomplished in a GCW ring? Hell has ANY GCW Champion before or after me done the things that I have completely ALONE? No they have not Alex. I have prided myself for years on the fact that I need no one else to succeed, I have proven it time and time again.” “I understand that but why change it now?” “Because I refuse to go to the bottom of the god damn ladder.” Knight’s lips curl into a semi snarl. “Why should I have to wait in line with the likes of Jay Terror and Kyle James” Explain to me why I should be looked down upon just because I took some time off to do personal things? The Establishment is not your daddy’s Establishment. We have formed to make sure GCW is cleansed.” “And if you win the World Title on the way then who is to argue.” “Fucking right!” His eyes begin to get brighter and brighter. “I have been the main superstar in the construction of GCW! It is time that the lead wolf takes his place at the front of the pack. All great leaders have led from the front and I will be no different.” “Then handle it.” I say with a smirk. “Do what it is in your nature to do.” “I plan to.” He does not smile ? his intensity remains. “The GCW is my Kingdom and it’s time to rule with an iron fist.” THIS is the man known as LeStatt Knight. Once my greatest enemy Now my most reliable friend Although he has gone by many names throughout his lifetime I can safely assume that one more is going to be coming very soon. THREE time GCW World Heavyweight Champion. Don’t believe me? Then look into his eyes ? You’ll be a believer too.
