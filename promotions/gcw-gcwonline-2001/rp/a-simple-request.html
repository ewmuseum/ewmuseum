Date: During World Wide To say I was pissed would be a royal understatement. I was fucking livid. I entered into my locker room and started throwing stuff around to ease some of the tension I was feeling. Who in the fuck did this Marauder bastard think he was interfereing in my match? Who was he to ruin my debut? For that matter who was he? My debut match ruined by a guy who didn't even have the balls to show his face! And I had Hardy! I fucking had him! I had the little shit right were I wanted him and this idiot has to come in and rain on my parade! Hardy would have to get his another time, if there was anything left of him. After all the little idiot picked a fight with Octavian, and from what I'd seen in the tapes Jeff lent me, the guy was a monster in the ring. Almost as good as me. But back the business at hand. The Marauder. And how far up his ass I should insert my foot. Well I wasn't gonna take this lying down. I'm the Machine dammit! Alright Mike, calm down. Count to ten, and then plan the bastard's demise. Light bulb moment! Annihilation! The pay-per-view is right around the corner! What a better way than to make my pay-per-view than to kick the crap out of the Maraduer! I'd rip that damn hood off his head revealing his ugly face to the crowd before shoving the damn thing up his ass! I grabbed a bottle of water and took a healthy swig and started to think like a rational human being. Or as rational as I could get. Which wasn't very. I was still mad as hell but at least I wasn't showing it anymore. Not that it mattered, there was no one to see my face anyway. I composed myself, or at least made a good facsimilie of getting composed and stood up. I half glanced at the monitor in my locker room and saw Ryan Stephens mouthing off about something or other. I had other things to take care of. I pulled on one of my t-shirts and jerked my locker room door open. I grabbed a random crew member as he rushed by. 'Hey.' 'Please don't hurt me.' 'Geez, what is it with you crew guys? Always thinking we're gonna hurt you. If I wanted to beat up on someone defensless, I'd pick on that tub of lard Pete at ringside.' I said rolling my eyes. 'I ain't gonna hurt you junior, just point me in the direction of Caldera's office.' He pointed with a shaky finger. 'Down the hall, fourth door on the right.' 'See? Now that wasn't painful was it?' I said spreading my arms. 'Thanks.' I hurried off towards Caldera's office. I kicked the door open and stormed inside all set to throw something around. To my dismay the file cabinet had already been assaulted. Bryan Mayhem's work no doubt, as the big bastard was already in the room. I had nothing else to overturn so I kicked the downed file cabinet. Then I stomped over to Caldera's desk, standing next to the rather imposing form of Mayhem. 'Pardon me, you two, but I’ve got a demand to make. I want that masked mother at Annihilation!' I almost dropped an f-bomb, but I saw the cameraman there and decided not to make the censor's job any harder. ' Back off, Savige, X is mine!' Mayhem roared at me. So that's what it sounds like when you eat a pile of rocks, brush with steel wool and gargle with acid. Interesting. 'Not that masked bastard, the other masked bastard.' I said more to Mayhem than Caldy. But then I turned to our Esteemed President. 'Marauder! Give him to me at Annihilation, Caldera, and make the match NOW.' Hey, he said he was granting PPV matches to anyone who wanted one so I figured why not get one while the getting was good. After all, I had my good name to uphold. 'Fine.' Caldy said after shrugging and giving that cat-that-just-ate-the-canary smile. I hated that kind of smile. 'Tell you what, you can both have exactly what you want. At Annihilation, we’ll see Mike Savige taking on The Marauder, and Bryan Mayhem versus X!' Now this was more like it. Results. Maybe Steven wasn't as bad as Jeff made him out to be. 'Oh, but before you go,' Shit. I knew it. There had to be a catch. 'it looks like you two could stand to blow off some steam. So next week, before Annihilation, you two...will be facing each other. Enjoy.' I take back everything nice I just said about Caldera. I glared at Mayhem. Sure he was big, but I could take him. I've faced guys his size before. Hell, I've faced guys bigger than him before. I headed out of Caldera's office and back to my locker room. I glanced at the monitor again. Where the fuck was RaZiel going? Ahh who cares? Show's almost over. I think I'll head back to the hotel, call Cindy and get some sleep. After all I've got Bryan Mayhem to prepare for. And then the Marauder. Well actually, they had to prepare for me.
