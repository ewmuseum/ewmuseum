Date: 31 August 2005 Mary didn’t know what she was thinking pulling that stunt with the prisoner swap. She didn’t know what she was doing or thinking or feeling but she did it anyway because he asked her to. It was 3WA all over again. Roberto and Mary had gotten close in DWA but never really made anything official, not really. They held themselves back from each other, sniffing at each other like two predators in no mans land. They’d both been hurt, badly by former lovers at the time and this new feeling growing between them scared them both as much as it thrilled them. They took things slow but then at the last minute Roberto was called away on business back to Spain. He left with little word and stayed away with no contact at all. Then came a random meeting in a mall a year later and Mary was left speechless for all of five seconds. This before the tirade of accusations, insults, voicing heartache and confusion and pain. None more so than when she found out he had found himself a girlfriend in the time he had been away. That news hit her harder than the news he had left without a word. So what does this have to do with now? There is a point I promise, because you see he turned up in her life again and asked for her help in 3W. Just like with the prisoner swap, Mary agreed to help him, just like that. He’d hurt her in 3W and he’d hurt her a few weeks ago but Mary seems to have this uncanny ability to simultaneously extract her private and personal life from each other while at the exact same time have them inseparable. It really is a mindfuck for want of a better word but hey, Typhoid Mary is no stranger to the mindfuck or any other, well’that’s going off subject. Mary sat in the waiting room of a doctors surgery worrying her hands like she was trying to squeeze water from them. It was one of the few things in her life she could rely on never to let her down, never to betray her, never to leave her. Sad for most people but for Mary, sometimes it’s all you have and you hold on to it like a freaking lifeline otherwise you drown in nothingness and that is ten times worse. A lot of people don’t seem to realise how easy it is to be literally so numb it slowly kills you. Skye understood. She’d been there. Her and Mary were at the psychiatric institute together for almost 3 years until Skye had to be transferred. Her relationship with Mary was considered dangerously destructive. Mary and Skye felt the exact opposite. The separation was devastating to them both but they survived. Now they were no longer lovers but still best friends. Skye sat beside Mary in silence but it didn’t last. “How could you Anjo” I still don’t understand why?” Mary lifted her head from gazing at the floor between her feet. Her raven curls bounced in front of her eyes and she didn’t shake them away like usual. She needed them now, to hide behind. “Why did Mary help Statty?” Skye looked over at Mary, her hazel almond shaped eyes meeting Mary’s huge brown doe eyes and just didn’t understand. It was taking all she had not to confess that she had ordered Arael to kill Roberto. Arael couldn’t take an order like that unless it was from the top, her father. Unfortunately for some and lucky for Roberto, or LeStatt, Skye’s father had been out of town and still was. He would be out of contact until further notice which wasn’t unusual for him to do or to be but it meant that Arael couldn’t take LeStatt out. Not yet anyhow. Skye folded her legs up underneath her on the waiting room chair. “sim. por que voc” ajudou a esse diabo?” She spoke in her native Brazilian when she was nervous or anxious. “He’s not a devil Skye blue, he’s just. Mary looked over at her. “Mary know’s you don’t unnerstan bebe, but she has to do this. She knows something ain’t at all right with her statty. She would say it ain’t like him but it is. Mary seen him like this before and she ain’t gonna leave him to drown in it. She loves him too much, she can’t just give up, not just like that. She took Skye’s hands in her own and looked at her. “If you know Mary then please, just trust Mary. She can do this, she’s gonna be okies eventually. She just needs you to trust her, let her do this cause she can see Statty splashing about in that deep water Skye, and she won’t watch him drown.” Skye exhaled again. She remained silent. She hadn’t changed her mind about wanting LeStatt dead. But Mary didn’t ever need to know that. Instead she changed the subject. “Do you know what you’ll do” If?” Mary cut her off by shaking her head. Then there was silence again. Skye got up. “I’m gonna go get a drink. voc” quer?” Mary shook her head again and Skye didn’t press for any verbilisation. She knew when Mary needed to be alone with her thoughts, so she would give her that. For now. Mary leaned her head back against the wall and stared up at the ceiling. She was still in her gym attire which consisted of a slashed up a fishnet body stocking and a pair of tiny booty shorts and matching “lil miss naughty” crop top. Her feet were adorned with a new purchase of late. Sneaker soled satin ballet shoes that tied up with ribbon to her knees. Custom made no less. Mary closed her eyes and tried to focus on something other than the smell of antiseptics and the sound of a whirring water cooler and AC system. Her mind flicked to the contract that had been mailed to her not so long ago and then to the confirmation that she would be taking part in her first ever match at GCW against another new signee. Zodiac. She was less than happy but at the same time at least she had someone and somewhere to focus her energies other than at Eva. She was still hearing the rumours about her and Roberto and did her best to ignore them, but she couldn’t ignore all of them all of the time and for that she probably hated herself a lil more than she did Eva for being anywhere within said rumours. Mary’s mind fleeted over the thoughts of her match against Zodiac and settled somewhere far less savoury.
