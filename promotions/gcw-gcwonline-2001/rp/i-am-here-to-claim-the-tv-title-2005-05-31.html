I am here to claim the TV Title.

31 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/31/05 [ The scene opens up to a large building. It appears to be a hospital of some sort. White walls, people in white lab coats, everything is white. Plain and sickening, enough to make any man or woman go crazy. The camera zooms in on a room that reads “321” the door slowly opens and sitting in the corner with a straight jacket on is Bryan Marshall. The GCW’S newest recruit.] Bryan Marshall: I am trapped! I am being held back! For my own safety! I don’t care about my own health. Nor do I care about anyone else’s I should be released immediately. But they said no. They said they are going to try and keep me here until Thursday’s show. That’s when I am going to be making my debut. That’s when this thing will be taken off me because they know I won’t harm myself. Because I will have so many other people to harm. Nine others to hurt and make them feel pain. Pain that I have felt all throughout my career! [ The door slowly opens and a man in a white lab coat steps into the padded room.] Bryan Marshall: What’s up doc? Doctor: Bryan, it turns out they want you out early. They want to make sure you don’t have any problems getting to San Antonio. So where going to get you out of that jacket and have you sign some papers and you will be on your way. Bryan Marshall: Wonderful. Just wonderful. [ A few large men dressed in white scrubs enter the room and pick Marshall up off the ground. The untie his straight jacket and Marshall is free. Marshall stretches out his arms and cracks his neck. He follows the doctor out of the room and signs the papers he was told to sign. Marshall is handed a box of some of his personal items and walks down the halls and out of the hospital. There is a long black limousine waiting for him. Marshall doesn’t realize this is for him. The chauffer has to chase him down and get him into the limo. Marshall overjoyed hops into the back of the limo with the GCW camera.] Bryan Marshall: This is the first time the GCW is laying eyes on me but I can promise you it won’t be the last. You see what I have done before the GCW I know does not matter. Which is why I plan on doing things that matter in the GCW and what better way to start then by winning this battle royal and becoming the GCW Television champion! I know your all doubting me. But I like it that way. Because you don’t know what to expect from a mad man. I will stop at nothing to become a legend here in the GCW. But not just because of titles that I plan on holding here in the GCW. I want you all to know me for the crazy stunts that I plan on pulling and the unbelievable matches that I will put on. The GCW has not seen anything like this man. I care more about inflicting pain on my opponents then I care about winning the match. That may be my biggest flaw. It’s a shame really. I have lost so many matches just because I refuse to let go of a move or two. I don’t understand the big deal. I consider it to be tough love. [ Marshall pushes the button that lowers the middle window separating the chauffer and Marshall.] Bryan Marshall: Hey Buddy, where the hell are you taking me? Chauffer: I have instructions to take you right to the airport. Your boarding a flight to San Antonio in about two hours. [ Marshall pushes the button and raises the window once again.] Bryan Marshall: I do not like being told what to do. This isn’t going to last long. I make the rules! I live by my own rules! In my world I am the king and everyone else just lives in it. They do what I say when I say to do it. I say jump you all say how high. If you don’t like it then do me a favor and stay out of my damn way. I didn’t come to the GCW to make friends. I came here to get a job done. Many may say I am to old to compete with the younger guys today but thirty years old used to be considers a man’s prime. That’s exactly what I am considering it. I am thirty years old and I have never felt better! No body knows what goes on inside my head but me. I feel that I have gotten my emotions together. For now anyways. But One slip up and I hate to be the person standing in a radius of five feet of me because they will feel my wrath! I have no mercy for anyone. No one had mercy for me ten years ago when I almost broke my neck. When I was put on the shelf for a year and a half. You see I made the most remarkable come back anyone has ever bared witness to and what did I get for it, I got a big slap in the face. So seeing as I can’t return that slap in the face to the man that has caused me so much pain, I have decided to just cause pain on each and every superstar here in the GCW. Starting with the nine that will be in the ring with me in Texas in just two short days. [ The window lowers again and the chauffer tells Marshall that there are drinks to his left and sandwiches to his right. Marshall nods and the window goes back up.] Bryan Marshall: I could get used to this. I signed with the GCW just yesterday and I was told that I would be in this battle royal. Which doesn’t give me much time to prepare but I don’t really need it. I never go in with aa game plan. The only plan I have is to hurt the other people in the ring. Nine others. I don’t know anything about them and they do not know anything about me. But we all have one thing in common and that is trying to take the TV title away from Capo and go on in the GCW to become known as some of the top superstars ever to wrestle inside the ring. I have accomplished many things but what I want most is to top those accomplishments here in the GCW. Everyone here can talk a good game, as can I. But talking is not even half the battle. The old saying goes you can talk the talk but can you walk the walk? Well I can. Not only can I walk it, I can run it and I can run circles around anyone here I the GCW. If don’t believe me then just wait until Thursday when I prove it. When I prove that I am the man to beat! The GCW will never be the same now that Bryan Marshall is here. [The limo stops and the chauffer gets out. Bryan Marshall’s door opens and the chauffer waits for Marshall to step out. Marshall steps out and sees? the bright sky, Marshall hears the airplanes leaving and landing and smiles.] Bryan Marshall: I love this. Most guys don’t like the traveling but I do. It gives some peaceful time to think about all the pain that I can inflict on my opponents once the bell rings. Since once the bell rings I turn into a different man. I become more focused and more intense then I am outside of the ring. From bell to bell I have no control over what I do. I can’t say I mind it either. I prefer it that way. The GCW KNOWS THIS. That is why they hired me. This is why they brought me to the GCW because they want to see me not only push myself but everyone else around here to heights they never knew they could reach! World Wide is going to be a day no one forgets. Right the date down. June 2nd, 2005. Bryan Marshall enters the GCW with an accomplished past but a nobody to the GCW. But when he leaves the arena that night he will be leaving with gold. The Television title will be around my waist and I will not stop at that! Nobody is safe. I’m not safe. The fans aren’t safe. The owners of the GCW are not safe. NO ONE IS SAFE AND NEVER AGAIN WILL THEY BE FEEL COMFORT. That level is gone. Is is now non existent. If anyone has a problem with the words I am saying then I suggest they step up and confront me. Because I am here to stay. Now if you will excuse me I have a plan to board. Camera man why don’t you come with me, I have a feeling I am going to have a lot to say on my way to Texas. [ Marshall grabs the camera man by the arm as the camera drops to the mans side and the scene fades out.]
