House of Genesis: Part Three

22 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 22nd, 2007 Every single day Scott Drake laced up the same pair of boots. One lace crossed the other, creating a pattern up the entire length of the boot until they came to a knot at the very top. Each day was a photocopy of the last. He only wrestled twice for the entire month of October, but he’s laced up nineteen different times. Tomorrow he would lace them up again, be it for a match or not. The day after that, he would again lace the boots up as he had yesterday and the day before. It wasn’t a habit or a routine. It wasn’t an obsession or disorder. Rather, it was the slow and planned transformation he went through each day. Each day Scott Drake put on a pair of wrestling boots and started to lace them up. As the final knot was firmly tied, he stood up as Chris Storm. The children never bothered him, Tessa never interrupted him. That was his moment here he peeling away his outer shell to reveal something completely different. It wouldn’t be a stretch to say Tessa didn’t like it. She fell in love with Scott Drake, not Chris Storm. If anything, she and Chris Storm have been at odds for just as long as her and Drake have been in love. But it was a required step that needed to be taken. Scott Drake simply could not do the things Chris Storm could do. Scott Drake would never be a former Global Champion. Scott Drake would never be considering one of the greatest tag teams ever. Scott Drake was simply too nice. That is why Scott needed Chris. At the same time, Chris needed Scott. Despite the passion for competition and the pride of glory, all of it was shallow if there was no one to share it with. But Chris Storm has never been a man for feelings. He would say that he feels feelings are for women, but that just wouldn’t make sense. Hence, Scott does the feelings and family. Chris takes care of business when shit needs to be handled. The majority of the time that relationship worked in perfect harmony. It can get complicated though when the two worlds are forced to mix. This is one of those times. At the usual gym, Scott headed straight to the back where Jerrod Darson had reserved the ring for them once again. By now Scott was used to the events and just threw his bag down beside the door. This wouldn’t take long. It never did. In the ring, still warming up, stood what was supposed to be his apprentice, Cleveland Paige. Cleveland looked at Scott, Scott in turn never bothered to even glance at him or the ring. He just tossed off his jacket, cracked his neck side to side and got to business. Darson was sitting near the ring, looking over various wrestling magazines when he finally looked over to notice Scott had arrived. “Late again” he grumbled just loud enough for Drake to hear as he passed by. Scott laughed; something that caused Darson to look up. “If you brought me someone worthy of training, I might actually bother to show up.” The comments stung at Paige, who did his best to ignore them and just continue on with his training. Scott hopped onto the apron, finally turning his attention to Paige, “let’s get this shit over with, I actually have important business to attend to.” Insult after insult just chipped away at Cleveland, who felt what patience he had slip away. Deciding that today would be different, Paige went right for Drake. It didn’t matter though. A post to the elbows, dropping down to his knees, and Scott had a huge opening on Paige and took him down with a Double Leg Takedown. Doing what he could to scramble, Paige got away from Scott and managed back to his feet. “You aren’t ready to be trained” he was told again. Paige kicked the bottom rope, but just felt himself open for a Single Leg Takedown this time. Before his leg could return to the mat from kicking the rope, the other leg had been pulled from under him and he went tumbling back first to the mat. He stared up at the lights, wondering what the hell just happened. That was two. This time he resolved himself to taking his time. He would try to scout each motion slowly, as to build a solid game plan. His best attempt was to try and circle Scott, looking for an opening. He went left but Drake went right. His lead leg planted on the mat and before he could bring it back, Scott had the ankle in his hand. One upward motion of his wrist and Scott tend Paige right back onto the mat for the third time. Scott released the ankle, clapped his hands, and exited out of the ring. “You still aren’t ready, kid.” Practice was over. Quick to move, Darson headed Scott off before he could reach his bag. “Ah come on” he told him, “you aren’t even training the guy.” “These are my rules” came the tort reply. “You promised me a guy worth training and I won’t start the training until I see someone worth of my time.” The look in Scott’s eyes showed that this wasn’t just trash talk meant to belittle anyone. This was a fact he believed. Darson shook his head though, refusing to give Scott the opening to grab his bag, “train him seriously.” His hand planted onto Darson’s chest. With just enough force to let him know he’s serious, Scott moved Darson out of the way and grabbed his bag and jacket. He stepped back but halted mid way to the door, his head turning to face toward Jerrod. “What do you want me to do?” he asked, “break his nose?” “I can still hear you!” came the shout from the ring, Cleveland Paige standing with both hands planted firmly on his hips. Snapping his head around, Scott hollered back, “I know; if I didn’t want you to hear me I would have whispered.” Taking his bag and jacket in hand, Scott pushed past Darson and left the two alone. Most likely Darson would just have the kid do a few work outs, maybe talk to him about what happened, but until Paige could figure out why Scott was able to take him down so easily, Drake would never train him. Back at the apartment, Scott found Tessa yelling at the children. He stopped dead in his tracks, dropping his gym bag on the ground beside the door, and listened as she yelled for them to get to their rooms. “What’s the problem?” Tessa spun on her heels, realizing that Scott had finally gotten back home. “And just where in the hell have you been?” Scott nearly fell back into the door, almost afraid that Tessa would literally bite his head off. His lips parted to answer her but she stormed off into the bedroom and left him with his jaw still hanging a foot from his mouth. His eyes wandered to the only person who could possibly explain what was going on. Their teenage daughter, Christa, just looked back at Scott with as much confusion on her face as he had. “What in the world was all that?” he asked, to which she could only shrug her shoulders. “I don’t know; she just suddenly got in a bad mood. We weren’t even being loud or anything.” She looked over to the bedroom where Tessa had marched herself to, “she was just fine a minute ago.” Sighing, Scott placed his jacket on the coat hanger, “I’ll go see what’s crawled up her ass and died.” Neither laughed. In the bedroom, Tessa was face down on the bed crying her eyes out. Scott could tell by the distant sound of her sobbing between gasp for air, even if it was muffled through the pillows. Plopping down beside her, Scott rubbed her back until she rolled over to look at him. Her eyes were soaked with tears, made into a dark red. “What’s wrong” he asked, pushing away the hair that was plastered to her forehead. Forcing back another sob, Tessa looked him in the face, “do you think I’m a good mom?” The poor display causes Scott to lean forward, embracing her in a hug. “Oh course! Why would you ever ask such a thing?” Tessa fought back another sob as Scott went back to rubbing her back, “if you weren’t a good mom you wouldn’t be crying right now.” Tears continued to flow though, despite his reassurance. Chris held her close, allowing her to lean her head on his shoulder. “Things are just stressful right now,” he told her, “while you are trying to get back in the ring.” “If you want” he said, “you can take a bit more time off from the ring until you are fully ready.” Tessa screeched at the very idea, “no!” Sitting up right now, she looked to him, “if I don’t get in the ring now, I’ll miss my chance.” It was an argument Scott couldn’t disagree with. It didn’t sound logical by any normal person’s standards, but for Scott, the call of the ring was a strong thing. “Ok,” he told her, “if you want, I’ll call Vivica for you and the two of you can go out and train or just shopping if you want. I’ll stay home and watch the kids.” Looking at him, thankfully no longer looking like she was about to break into tears again, Tessa asked “don’t you still have to do the training thing?” Scott just smiled and shook his head, telling her know he had finished her already. Tessa finally started to cheer up upon learning that she could leave the house with the children being watched. “I’ll go make the call” Scott told her as he got up from the bed and headed out of the room. Once outside, Christa was there waiting for him. She had seen Tessa a mess before, but even that seemed odd for her. “Well?” “She’s fine, albeit a bit stressed out” Scott told his adopted daughter, “she’s just having a hard time adjusting to getting back to work and being a mom.” Christa frowned though, “she’s always been a mom though.” Scott wanted to kick himself, realizing what he had just implied. He had to quickly correct himself, letting her know that “yes, I’m sorry, I just meant with the baby. It’s been kind of crazy.” Crazy was Rob. Christa knew all about Rob. So, she decided, she would let him off the hook this time. After all, if Scott was brave enough to go in there when Tessa was like that, than he deserved some slack. Still, even as he picked up the phone to call Vivica Valentine, Scott couldn’t help but look back at the bedroom door and wonder just what had happened. It wasn’t like Tessa, and something just didn’t seem right.
