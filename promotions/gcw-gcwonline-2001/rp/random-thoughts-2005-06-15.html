15 June 2005

Date: 15 June 05 The dream world is a very fascinating thing is it not ? I mean for those few hours as you sleep, anything can and will more than likely happen. All of your worries, all of your problems, and all of your misfortunes all disappear for those hours and perhaps you can be exactly where you always wanted to be. If you have always wanted to be on some kind of tropical island drinking cocktails, eating fresh fruit, and making love with that beautiful crush that you’ve had for years, then in your dreams you could go there and have the time of your life. If you have always loved your birthday, or a particular birthday where maybe you got your first kiss, or just had such an incredible time that you never wanted it to end, then that’s where you would be. Maybe you watch a very enjoyable movie, and just completely envy the main character, well your dream would make you just that. The list goes on and on from Super Heroes to Fire Fighters to police officers to Amusement parks to tropical islands, your dreams can be your sanctuary. Of course where there is light, there is dark, and where there is good there is always evil. If you cannot control your dreams then the very darkest pieces of your mind can seep in and ruin it all. A black sky can quickly invade your blue and white skies, blood shed can quickly transform your family outing into devastation, and your peace can be known as war almost with the snap of a finger. For better or for worse though, eventually your dreams all come to an end. You awaken to face the reality, and sometimes the harsh one at that, that is your life. To some your life merely passes by, as if you wish you were in the sanctuary of your dreams. To others life is a dream, because they go out and do everything they want, to fill their lives the way they wish. Then to some, the cruelty that is their lives is even worse than even their most feared nightmares. I would just so happen to fall into the latter. Every time I close my eyes I do not see the life of luxury or happiness, I see blood and war. The thing is when I open my eyes I see much of the same. No matter where I turn, there is death and destruction, broken bones and broken lives. Some of it is my own doing, but some is the doing of others. I am man enough to step up and handle my own responsibilities. I have the blood of my family on my hands, be it my Wife and my daughter or my brother Damen. It is because of me that their lives were ended years before they should have been. Perhaps that is why my mind has been over run with hatred. Am I being punished for my sins ? Probably am, and I wouldn’t blame god for doing so. It’s funny to me when fans come up to me and ask me if I am some sort of Devil worshipper, or cult follower simply because of my dark persona. They love to ask me ? “Do you believe in God?”. Ah indeed I believe in God, Jesus Christ, the Virgin Mary as well as the dark half of the story as well. Does it mean that I trust in God anymore? No I do not, but then again why should I? Sure punishment may be in order after all of the years of f*cked up things I have done ? but he brought the punishment my way far before I committed any acts of sin. Yet I should pray and worship THAT? No, I am sorry but I refuse to follow someone who has the audacity to forgive us for something that no one wanted or needed forgiveness for. Things are no different now than before, if someone now claimed to be the SON of God ? say if “Jarred Christ” came out of the woodwork from a virgin martini, what would we do to him? People would cast him off as insane, and have him locked up. Would he forgive us ? Would we ask for forgiveness? The hell we would, and if he tried we’d laugh in his face for being so ignorant. So if not religion, what else could possibly save my life from being worse than my nightmares? Well there are only a few things left in my life worth living for. The one I have laying here next to me is one, the amazingly beautiful and unique Typhoid Mary. What a pair her and I make indeed. Both of us have gone through incredible amounts of pain, physically and mentally, both at times wish we could change the past ? and both of us find comfort in each other. In many ways, she is my greatest strength. I feel alive when I am with her, and I must fight a smile every time I think about her. I feel that with her love, that perhaps even with a life as screwed up as mine has a chance at salvation. That is, if I even am deemed deserving of salvation. Now Mary could also be considered my greatest weakness as well. If I weren’t in the business that I am in, she would not be, but as things are it’s completely different. I know that in this business most will do anything to one up the competition, hell I know I certainly would. So when they learn of a passion in your life or a certain person, the real good ones will figure out ways to warp your mindset, to make you stumble ? to make you lose your focus. I already know that if THEY find out about Mary, and someone sick and perhaps foolish enough goes after her, I would stop at nothing to make sure their lives, and the lives of all around them suffered as well. I would be blind with rage ? which would make my love for Mary a weakness as well as a strength. Of course this all brings me to the other thing that my life is worth living for ? Destruction After seeing lives shattered, blood shed, and lies constantly surrounding my being, I had to find a way to harness my aggression. Enter my way into professional wrestling. It seems so long ago now, Global Championship Wrestling ? LeStatt Knight’s debut into the wrestling world. I was younger, and much more wreckless, much more. Over the years a few choices and a few people have altered my life, and my way of looking at the world. I have grown to trust almost no one, and to like even less. I do give my hard earned respect every once in awhile, and its usually to those who make my life the worst. There are only two men in this business that I respect ? although one more than the other. I have made my living through altering lives and destroying even more. I have tried to get even with god with every ounce of blood I spill, and with ever tear I make fall from someone’s eye. I figured that with every scream that I create, God cringes and wishes he never would have taken my family away from me. Getting even with God is no easy task ? he has seemed to find ways to make my out look even bleaker than before. So here I am ? laying in bed with the love in my life. Wide awake, as I would rather not fall into my dream world of blood and death. Only because reality is so much more fun to me now. Once I had run from what I was destined to become ? but now I have embraced that dark corner of my mind and sadly for Jason Dread and GCW they are my new feeding ground. Jason will stand across from a man who has no regard for himself, or for the well being of whom he steps into the ring with. For years I have claimed that I would create a Legacy, that I would create a persona that no one would soon forget. I still intend to do just that, to make the name LeStatt Knight, a name that they will talk about in folk lore for thousands of years. Like the most evil of villains and the most loved heroes, I will be adored and hated all the same. And no, this will not just be in a dream world ? My Legacy will be everyone’s reality. Now I lay me down to sleep I pray my lord my soul to keep If I should die before I wake ? Then maybe God has told me I have what it takes ? Amar es odiar, ser bueno est? al malo, y ser YO debe estar POR SIEMPRE.
