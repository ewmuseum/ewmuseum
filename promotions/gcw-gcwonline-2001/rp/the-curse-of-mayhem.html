The Curse of Mayhem

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Devil's Night and Beyond Suffocating. Smothering. Claustrophobia. Paralysis. Lungs heavy with the final breathe of life. Blackness. No scent, just a bland smell of dirt. I'm starting to lose consciousness. The walls around me are starting to cave in. I'm buried alive, but will I still be alive in the end? CRUNCH, CRUNCH, CRUNCH. What's that noise? Is this the end? Am I wormfood? I must be hallucinating, or is that a sliver of light in my eye? It is! It's a light! It's a goddamn light! My lungs have started to inflate, deflate, inflate, deflate. There is no more heaviness. Shit, my eyelids are getting heavy. What is going on? Stay open eyes! What the hell is going on? STAY AWAKE! STAY AWAKE GODDAMNIT! Someone is digging me out. But Who? He looks... He looks like... He looks like...NO! This can't be another nightmare! Blackness.... Voice:'Come with me son. Come with.......... Different Voice'...me. Hey you listening to me?' A jolt runs through Bryan and he comes back to reality. People, smoke, loud music, the stench of beer. He looks around and there is little light and some really bad rock music blaring. In front of him is an empty tall glass. Foam coats the bottom of the glass. Bartender:'Hey, big guy, you want another?' 'Another? Another what?' The man behind the counter gives a little laugh. 'Either you're stupid, but with your size, I don't have the balls to say it directly to you, or you can't hold your beer.' 'Beer?' 'Yes Beer! It's the shit you've been drinking since you got here. You've been off in La-La Land, but here nonetheless.' Another man cuts in front of Bryan and asks for a seven and seven. This does not anger Bryan because he knows not where he is, and wouldn't mind focusing on that for a split second. He rubs his face and yawns. He doesn't remember why he's so tired. Has he slept? Bryan looks at his hands and see's what looks like white paint. He looks up and stares into the mirror wall behind the couner he;s sitting at. Normal face. Same old Bryan O'Neil. Where the hell did that white shit come from? 'Looks like you've seen a ghost there man?' Bryan jolts again. 'Huh?' 'Wow calm down there man. Lay off the drugs brother.' 'Jason?' 'No, I'm Bert, the barternder, see.' He points to his nametag. 'See Bert. No Jason.' 'You called me brother. Jason is my brother.' 'look man, you've got some serious issues, so serious that I don't have time to handle them. Can I get you another beer?' 'How did I get here?' In obvious annoyance. ' I dont know, ask the guy you walked in with.' Bryan stares at Bert dumbfounded. Pointing to a single table with one elderly man with a blue hue in his complexion. 'Him.' Bryan looks and recognizes the face, but at the same time does not. He ignores the bartender, stands up and makes his way towards the old man. Patrons of the bar simply part like the Red Sea as this unfamaliar giant walks amongst them. Bryan arrives at the table, beads of sweat make his face their habitat. Bryan stands tall, looking down at the bluish man. He stares deep into his eyes. 'I know I know you old man, now give me your name.' 'Bryan how can you say that to me? You owe your life and career to me Bryan Mayhem.' The name sends a shiver through his spine. He knows but, but from where. Bryan Mayhem? Why does that sound so famaliar? 'Who are you?' 'I'm the one that dug up your lifeless corpse as you lost a match that was in your hands. Maybe I should have hit you harder, it certainly helped your brother out, atleast he's successful and made us proud.' Bryan's eyes widen with shock. He begins to to shake with anger. 'That's righr, come on Mayhem, give daddy a hug.' Bryan clenches his fists harder and puts his hand over his chest where the name O'Neil is tattooed, and drives his fist square into... his mirror. Some of it shatters, and some of it remains in tact. Growling with anger because of another nightmare, Bryan breaks out in a cold sweat and rips his shirt off. Branded on his upper stomach is CHRISSY. Bryan stares deep into what's left of his mirror when the bizarre happens. Letters carve themself into his lower stomach, just below the Chrissy brand. Not feeling any of the pain involved he watches as the spelling ends. Carved into his stomach is MAYHEM. Waking up screaming, immediately checking out his abdomen, Bryan sighs with relief. Just a dream. But who or what is Mayhem?
