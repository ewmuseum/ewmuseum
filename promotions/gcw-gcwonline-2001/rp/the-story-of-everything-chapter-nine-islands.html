I slowly released my hand, hoping that no screams would be released, and Marsha obliged. She was still breathing heavily, but she tried to talk through it. "Who are you?" she said through separate breaths. "Johnny," I said, crawling out from under the bed. I helped her to her feet. "Johnny Don't." I breathed in the air in the room, and Curtis' pungent stench was all over, and I recognized it instantaneously. It was Desert Stretch. The hues and embers of boutique Jenkem danced around in my nostrils, and I knew I could track him down. I ran off for the corridors, and I saw his robe flourish down the alleyway. He was apt at evasion, but I was hot on his scent. Another flash of the robe, and I could smell him even stronger. "Westenbrook!" I yelled, and I ran, sensing his odor weaken. He was headed back out towards the chaos, and as soon as I exited the corridors of private rooms, I saw him, heading towards the central mass. I gave chase, and almost stumbled over a fallen body who had apparently stuck himself with a dagger in the fervor of all the surrounding rage. This was perhaps the first convenient thing to happen to me all day. What luck it was to find a dagger unclaimed by any of the raucously flailing competitors around me! I pulled the dagger out of the new corpse and raised it triumphantly in the air, charging after Curtis Westonbrook as he rushed towards the amorphous mess of bodies blobbed together. I was gaining on him, but he was too fast for me to catch up, but perhaps the unusual nature of running with his new anatomical schematic was what did him in. He ended up losing footing, tripping on his robe and falling just short of the mass. I stood over him and held the dagger to his throat. "Johnny Don't!" he begged. "You didn't really think I was going to kill you, did you?" The worm writhed. "Sorry, boss." I said flatly. "I got a better offer." "Whatever he's paying you, I'll double it!" his desperation was testing my patience. "There's only one thing I want from you, Curtis." "No! Johnny Don't! Johnny! Don't! Don't, Johnny!" And thus, I retrieved the collateral from the chaos and left Curtis Westonbrook there, writhing and newly wounded in a new place to wound.
