Que Pasa?

21 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/21/08 START 4:48 pm It’s a normal day in the wonderful city of Oklahoma. All is quite? well at least for the time being. Clyde Fox strolls down the street with all three of his companions: Squirtle, Pikachu, and his TV Title. Showing little respect for others on the sidewalk, Clyde hogs the path all to himself, brushing shoulders with many strangers. Clyde continues down the street until he bumps a man much bigger than him. The man stops and looks puzzled at Clyde. Man: Excuse me man. Watch where you’re going! You just can’t take the whole damn sidewalk up! Fox: Que Pasa? Man: Don’t give me that B.S. that you can’t speak English. You can’t hide your American looks. Fox: Que Pasa? American? Canadian? it’s all the same right? Hey, at least the Canadians mimic the looks of Americans. God bless Ca-merica. Man: Are you asking for a whopping? The tall, pleather jacket wearing man starts to become agitated. Fox: Que Pasa? Man: Dude, take your f**king meds or something! The man carries on with his business and continues to walk down the street shaking his head. Clyde turns and yells. Fox: Que Pasa?! The man promptly flips off Clyde while not bothering to look back. Clyde accepts the wonderful gesture, turns, and continues onward. To where might you ask? To the only thing that’s been on his mind since he first had an erotic encounter with a Mexican stripper? Taco Bell. He always wanted to know how she got such a fit figure? all 500 pounds of her. Anyways, back to creating the plot? Clyde continues down the street until he arrives at his destination. There it is. In all its glory. Taco Bell. Not particularity busy considering it was in the gully between the lunch and dinner rushes, Clyde lulls towards the entrance. He enters the store with a gleaming smile on his face. He is about to get him some, that’s for sure! With no one in line, Clyde moves with haste to the counter. A teen girl walks up to the till to serve him better. Girl: Welcome to Taco Bell, I’m Candace. We tend to think outside the bun, so why not warm up your winter with a cheesy gordita crunch combo? whad-da-ya want? Fox: Que Pasa! Candace: Excuse me? Fox: Que Pasa? Candace: W-what’s that? I don’t think we have that here. Clyde just stands there, not even looking at her. Heck, he’s not even blinking. He’s just staring of into space wanting some fricken? “Que Pasa?” Candace: Okay then? I’ll check for you. Candace runs out of sight while slowly, the line grows with people coming in. Being the ignorant Lego lover Clyde is, he turns to the middle aged woman behind him and starts talking. Fox: Que Pasa? Woman: Okay? weirdo. Fox: Que Pasa! Woman: Yes? very nice. Clyde starts to drool a tad just as he turns to see Candace back at her station. Candace: Sorry sir, we don’t have any of that, would you like something else? Fox: Que Pasa. Candace: Yeah? no? we don’t have that sir, sorry. If you could just pick something else please. Fox: QUE PASA!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Man form the end of the line: Hurry up man, we got hungry people here. Candace: Okay so I’ll get you some que pasa then okay. It’ll be eight dollars and ninety cents okay. Candace rings in a meal for a Quesadilla combo and tries to pass it by, but Clyde is quick to read the monitor behind her. Monitor: Quesadilla Meal. Fox: QUE PASAAAAAA!!!!!! Almost turning into the Incredible Hulk, Fox seethes at the teeth and grabs a bunch of napkins and throws them around. He then turns his attention to the crowd behind him. Some scramble out of the way while others are consumed in his wake of napkin abuse. Clyde thumps his way out of the restaurant without a confrontation? we’re guessing it’s because of the gross amount of nose buddies pulsating out from his muzzle. Anyways, Clyde stands just yards from the building and lets out a brutal battle cry. Fox: QQQUUUEEE PPPAAASSSAAA!!!!!!!!!!! Just then his Gizmondo vibrates. Gizmondo Voice: You’ve got mail. The monster-like Fox pulls his Gizmondo out from his pocket and reads the text. Gizmondo Text: Sorry baby. The thing that we had was actually a quesadilla. Sorry for the confusion! Love, Mommy and Daddy. Fox: F**K! END No napkins were hurt in the making of this adventure.
