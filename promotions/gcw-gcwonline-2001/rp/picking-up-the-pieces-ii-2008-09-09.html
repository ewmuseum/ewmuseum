Picking Up The Pieces, II

9 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Written: September 9, 2008 Flashback. Six Years Ago September 9th, 2002 Odessa, Texas "I miss him. I really miss him a lot." Rebecca Paris sat staring blankly on the edge of her bed, peering out the window at an otherwise spectacular sunset. The sun was just touching down along the horizon, emitting an ominous orange glow. It was surely a picture perfect moment. But not for Rebecca Paris. It'd had been over two months since she'd last seen the love of her life. She still had so many questions she wanted to ask, but just knew it wasn't the right time. She'd have done anything to talk with him again, even if only for a few minutes. She wanted to let him know that everything was okay. That she was okay. But she couldn't. It was imperitive to Abigail's master plan that Rebecca stay far away from Chris' life as she possibly could. If only Abigail would just open up and clue Rebecca in, perhaps everything would seem a lot easier. But of course, that was only a distant hope. Rebecca sat on the bed, gripping tight a photograph. She pressed it to her chest as tears drizzled down her face, one by one.. by one. "Abby can't be right. She has to be wrong. I know we'll both be better off together. We could run away somewhere and at least be happy with one another..." Rebecca hadn't given up hope that Abigail might change her mind. Although she certainly should've. As time raced by and the sun dropped below the horizon, making it almost invisible except for its powerful glow, Rebecca tried to compose herself. She slide towards her nightstand and opened the top drawer. She slowly lifted the photograph from her heart to her lips... And kissed it. One final tear drizzled down from her eye and along her cheek as she placed the photograph inside the drawer and shut it. The creaking noise the drawer made told wonders of its age. It also had some to say of the loneliness within that very room... ..within her very heart. Rebecca glanced at the digital clock that sat atop the nightstand. "8:28," she whispered to herself. "It seems to be setting two to three minutes earlier every day." She'd been keeping track of the sunset's time since almost the time she had arrived in Odessa. It gave her something to look forward to every night. More importantly, it gave her a chance to spend some alone time with Chris. She knew that somewhere, somehow, Chris doing the exact same thing as she. She knew that he'd watched the sunset every single day since the moment she lived, still holding hope. "Good night, Chris.." Rebecca climbed into her bed and snuggled up with her fluffy comforter just as the moon began to appear, lighting up the room. She stared out the window into the darkness.. "I love you." -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Present Day. September 7th, 2008 Union, New Jersey It'd been just another week for me. Another show, another day on the road, and another night I wasn't booked. I don't know what Caldera has had brewing in the back of his mind for my future, but I can't say I was too thrilled about it. I'd went from headlining events every week against a top dog like Andy Murray to being downgraded in a match against a former Television Champion. Then, to boot, I win my match and don't get anything for it the week after. That would be last week. And Caldera goes and gives the guy I beat up a shot at the United States Championship. Now, by all means, thats not the direction I want to be moving. I don't want some cheesy US Title belt. I'm bigger than that. But what the hell is the deal, here? Why are the guys I beat getting pushed and I'm getting drug down? If he wants to tie an anchor to my foot, he better be prepared when I whip it right back in his face. But then I have to think -- could it really just be me? I know I've been all meddled up with this whole Rebecca thing. But I'd like to think that it really isn't holding me back THAT much. In my personal life, yeah, I'm an absolute wreck. But has my performance really suffered in the ring? I'd like to think not, but it seems as though everyone and their mother is turning their back against me. First the bullshit with Caldera and then, to top off an eventful night, Dan Black approaches me in my locker room to tell me he, Axel, and Kincaid are overthrowing me. Since when was this a government body? There isn't any voting or overthrowing. I founded The Movement. Its MY creation. You don't like it, then you get the fuck out of it. I'm happy with calling myself the sole member of The Movement because I'm still standing up for my very principals. THE very principals that I founded the damn group on. I'm just too tired to argue about it, though. Let Black think he's in control of The Movement. I've got too much other garbage already filling up on my plate. I'll deal with him and those other two cronies later. I really hope they bask in their glory now as it'll most definitely be short-lived. And boy oh boy will they be in for a shocking surprise when I set my sights back on them. They thought I was ruthless before... HA! RRRIIIINNNNGGGGG. RRRRIIINNNGGGGG. Ugh, this Goddamn phone. I better just answer it. "Hello?" I tried to make my voice sound as uninviting as I possibly could. But, of course, you've got an optimist on the other end trying to make light of my misery. "Chris! Hey! It's me." And, nevertheless, talking to me like I'm supposed to know exactly who ME is. "And me would be.." I wasn't even trying to be funny. I hadn't a clue. "I spoke to you on the phone a few weeks ago. I have some information regarding the whereabouts of Rebecca." And we've got a winner! Way to take my already shitty day and dump a couple more piles of wet, week old dog manure on top of it. That sonuvabitch. "Oh is that who it is?" I'm hoping he could tell at this point that my patience was wearing thin. "Well let me tell you something Mr. Brittanica. You tried to pull this shit with me the other week and, I'll admit, you got my hopes high. But I'm done hearing your bullshit. You strung me along with your idiotic postcards and your little mind games. So you're either going to tell me what you know or I'm going to have this call traced and then take that phone you're talking on right now and ram it up your ass." You think that would've set him straight. Nothing sets a person straight like telling them you've going to shove something up their ass. But then, of course, you have to worry about the creepy type who are actually into that kind of stuff. And my God was I praying that he wasn't one of those. "You have an interesting approach to negotiating, Chris." He apparently wasn't buying into my threats. "The fact is that you're NOT going to trace this call because if you were, or even had the connections to do so, it would have been done already. You're not the type of guy who is going to sit around and waste time chatting if you know there's an easier way to get your answers." And he had me pinned. Shoulders to the mat for the three count. Whoever this guy was, he definitely knew me and he knew me well. I wouldn't even know where to start if I wanted to get a phonecall traced. I know for damn sure that the police department wouldn't have been of any help. I'd be surprised if they knew how to tie their own shoes! "Who is this?" I asked sternly, hoping for some sort of indication. "Lets just say I'm an old friend." An old friend, huh? Some friend he must have been. "An old friend that knows where my girlfriend is and refuses to tell me. An old friend that insists on playing games just to see me get all pissed off." The sonuvabitch had to have known me well if he was going to toy me around like this. He HAD to have. Otherwise he'd be in for one hell of a beating. It just killed me because not too many people knew the details of Rebecca. I just had to figure out who knew what and who would know WHERE. "Rebecca is alive and she's well. It was, in fact, her that you saw in your hotel before Warpath." He's telling me things I knew. Well, I guess I more or less speculated these things. I wouldn't have put it past someone to hire a Rebecca look-alike. So I suppose this was somewhat reassuring. Still, I wasn't getting answers. "So then where the hell is she, dear old friend?" He chuckled at my attitude. His laugh sounded so distinguished. Where the hell did I know him from? "She's been living in Texas for the last six years." Bingo. I got an answer. I got a state. Granted, I was dealing with the biggest possible state in the country, but it was a start. There were only a couple hundred cities in Texas. I could start searching them one by one and, in hopes that she doesn't ever move, find her in six more years. "Can you give me a little more to work with than Texas?" I'd changed my tone to something I considered more negotiary... if that's even a real word. "Are you getting soft on me?" he asked. He had to call me on it. That sonuvabitch. "Don't go getting cute on me now. What more information can you give me or are you just going to continue on with your bullshit?" His antics made me question if he was just a pranker. Anyone could've said Texas. The hell would Rebecca be doing there, anyway? She'd never been there nor would she know where to go down there. She'd be totally lost. She definitely couldn't be in Texas. "Oh knock it off, chief. Meet me at the Newark International Airport at a quarter to six outside Terminal C and I'll fill you in." Normally, I'd have told the guy to fuck off. But the airport was only about twenty to thirty minutes from my place. The drive wasn't bad at all and I wouldn't get that pissed off if he totally stood me up. Besides, I had to go that way anyway to board a flight for Worldwide. "Alright. I'll be there." I confirmed. But something was fogging my mind. He called me chief. I've only know a handful of people that have ever used that word in context like that. Most people were to know that kind of stuff doesn't fold well with me. Chief. He hung up the phone and then the alarm went off in my head. My flight for Canada leaves at 1:15. There's no way I'd be able to make his rendezvoux. I'm at a crossroads. Do I blow off a show in hopes to finally be reunited with Rebecca, yet risk the fact that he may be full of shit. Or do I call his bluff and blow him off, risking the chance he might actually know bout Rebecca's whereabouts. .. ... .... ..... .... ... .. The fuck with Steve. He should've booked me last week instead of this week, that sonuvabitch! This was my one chance to mend six years of a broken life. It was worth the risk of losing my job. Besides, I'm sure I could find work elsewhere. I'm always one for stirring up trouble. A bottom of the barrell feeding organization like PRIME would scoop me right up if it were to spit in GCW's face. They're scumbags like that. With the moon rising and the sun setting, I can help myself but to stare out the window, mesmorized at the orange glow. It seems brighter tonight than it normally does. Perhaps its the calm before the storm. Or perhaps its just a beautiful night. "Goodnight, Rebecca..." Either way, I can't help myself. I'm a hopeless romantic at heart (as hard to believe as it sounds) and I've kept my promise for well over six years. "I love you.." -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Two Days Later. September 9th, 2008 Newark International Airport 5:39 PM Tick, tock. Tick, tock. Tick, tock. Boy oh boy am I ready to explode. Pacing back and forth in front of Terminal C, looking for someone I might recognize. He's late. Well, he's not actually late .. I'm just early. But only six minutes early. I guess I just figured he'd be here already, waiting. But I really should've known better. He's got the information for me. I've got nothing for him. He doesn't have to be on time. Sadly, I do. He's dancing me around like his little puppet right now. Or at least that's how I feel. I feel like he's got control over me to do whatever he wants. I'm actually pretty much admitting that he does. And I absolutely hate that I'm doing that. I hate it because I love control. I love being in control. I love controlling other people. I know how to manipulate people and this bastard, in some way, knows how to manipulate me. That's some scary shit right there. I'd like to consider myself a master of manipulation... and he's better than me! Now I'm not talking this Clyde Fox manipulation shit. My work with Clyde is elementary. I wouldn't even call that manipulation, but instead just capitalization. I'm merely cashing in on an opportunity to make some shmuck my slave. I'm talking like Kiljaden Kincaid manipulation. Taking an unstable monster of a man who could rip your head off and making him your pet dog. It's like trying to dangle your fingers in front of a wild ratweiler and praying to God he doesn't bite your hand off. I'm talking like James von Doom manipulation. Taking a guy with more split personalities than an enite psych ward of schizophreniacs and controlling all of them to believe that you're their friend and that doing your dirty work will actually be beneficial to them. Or even like Dan Black manipulation. Taking a champion from a never-before-heard-of organization and leading him to believe he's going to be the next big thing if only he can take a few beatings in your honor, first. Then letting him believe he's ready to run with the big dogs by allowing him to take over the little stable you worked up. That's some real mind manipulation. It's like Dan Black came from being a gold medalist in the Special Olympics and expects to do well in Beijing. Worlds apart. But thats some serious manipulation. And I mean, I've definitely mastered that. But this sonuvabitch. Chief. He called me chief. He tried to belittle my mind by calling me chief. It's such an elementary step. Make the person you're manipulating feel small about themselves and you can capitalize from there. And he knew how to do it and he did it. And I let him. That sonuvabitch. It's 5:44. Where is he? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Meanwhile September 9th, 2008 Dallas, Texas Dallas-Fort Worth International Airport 5:37 PM EST "Come on, Abby, we're going to miss the flight!" Rebecca Paris wore a brilliant smile on her face as she nearly sprinted towards the doors to the airport terminal. Abigail Williams followed in the short distance behind her, trying to keep up with the vibrant Rebecca with a steady power walk. "Jesus, Rebecca, slow down!" But Abigail's demands were simply ignored. There wasn't much that Abigial could do to curbside Rebecca's enthusiasm. She'd been waiting well over six years for this moment. The moment where she'd finally be reunited with her boyfriend, Chris, and not have to leave within minutes of seeing him. w She'd finally be able to fill him in on where's she's been, why she left, and how much she'd been thinking about him. Once reaching the gates, Rebecca came to an abrupt stop as she peered up at the boarding screens. "We're not even set to board yet," she said with a slither of sadness in her voice. Abigail finally catches up with only a slight panting. "Yeah. I figured," she responds. "What do you mean," Rebecca exclaims. Abigail places her bags down and whipes away a bit of sweat from her forehead. She tries desperately to keep her lady-like composure. "These things are always late. The time on your boarding pass is just an over-estimate to make sure you're here." Rebecca throws herself into a near-by chair, wearing a puss on her face. "This sucks. How much longer are we going to have to wait." Abigail shrugs her shoulders. In her mind, she knows it could take anywhere from a half hour to two hours just to get the plane ready. She glances up at the terminal boards before responding to Rebecca. "Oh man.." Rebecca rolls her eyes. "Don't say TTHHAAT! How long?" Abigail smirks. But not one of those happy smirks, rather one of those smirks when you know you're about to tell someone bad news and you're merely just trying to soften the blow. But Rebecca picks up on it. "Oh no. What's that look for?" Rebecca turns and glances at the boards herself. "FLIGHT DELAYED?! WHAT??" Rebecca was obviously not too thrilled. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Back in New Jersey. September 9th, 2008 Newark International Airport 5:47 PM I couldn't believe it. I was played like an absolute fool. Our meeting time had come and gone and I was standing here, staring down the street, looking like a complete moron. I toyed with the idea that he's probably standing off in the distance watching me and laughing at how naive I was. But the more I considered that, the more pissed I made myself. It wasn't so much that he stood me up that bothered me. I actually didn't care so much about that. Alright, well maybe I cared about it a bit. But alone, it wasn't THAT much to really get me fired up. What really got my blood boiling was that I blew off my flight earlier to meet with this bastard. Now my chances of getting up to Worldwide on time were really running thin. I've began coming to terms that I'll have to forfeit to Phil Atken. The sonuvabitch does nothing in his entire GCW career and makes some grand re-debut, only to get a W because I missed my flight. Well... skipped my flight. What a welcoming gift for Phil, huh? And here, all along, I'd planned on just beating his face in and trying to get him to quit again. So much for that idea. And so much for my damned career. Caldera didn't book me when I won matches. I can't imagine what he'd do when I no-show events. That sonuvabitch had to play me like this. I swear if I find out who it is, I'm going to kill them. VROOOM. VRRRRROOOOMMM. VRRRRRRRROOOOOOOMMM. And to top it all off, some punk kid has to roll around here revving his engine. But as I looked off in the distance, I saw a whole lot more than just some punk kid rounding the corner. Oh yeah. The black paint shined like it had just came from the detailing shop with a fresh coat of polish. The sun glimmered off of every angle on that gorgeous car. It was a car I'd seen before. A car I knew VERY well. And then it all hit me. The laugh. Chief. The black Dodge Viper. Windows tinted and exhaust roaring. As the car drew closer, I could soon read the license plate. And it was at that point I knew EXACTLY who was coming to meet me. He may have been late, but he's always fashionably late. I should've known that. He had the attention of every on-going outside the terminal. They all stood and stared at his gorgeous car. He loved the attention. As the car grew closer, the plate became clear. The plate read "SS". It was at that moment I knew that I'd be making it to Worldwide. I may have been a little late, but this guy was sure to get me there before my match with Atken. His car pulled up and his window went down. I bent forward and looked inside. Our eyes met and he greeted me with a smile. "Chris." I couldn't help but smirk and shake my head. He was the only person who could ever help me pick up the pieces and put them back together. And so I greeted him and said, "Shane." Fade.
