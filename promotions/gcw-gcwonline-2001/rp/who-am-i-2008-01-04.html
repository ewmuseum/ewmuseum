Who am I?

4 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-04-08 Sitting in his empty home, Joshua A. Schuler squats in the middle of the floor with a single picture of him and his daughter from a few years back. It has been a long three months since he has seen her and been out of prison, he hasn't been the happiest of men. In fact he has been kind of in a coma of depression, not really doing much of anything, but drinking his life away. Can you really blame the man, his daughter Candace was his world, he loved his little girl more than anything. Hell when he first got out and came home to an empty house he didn't even know what to think, until he found that dreaded letter. The fucking letter that explained it all to him in heart piercing words that would soak into Joshua's head and leave him stained in darkness. A few tears begin to stream down his cheeks as he remembers his daughter, the tears drip down splashing into a silhouette on the picture. He then wipes his eyes and cheeks with his bare arm and tucks the picture back into his black leather wallet. Joshua then stands up and stares out the window with no curtains or anything to block the view; it is a cold snowy day in Kent, Ohio, about five inches of snow on the fourth day of January. Joshua sniffles and walks over to the only table in the house where the letter lays on the table. He grasps the paper in his hand and begins reading the heart piercing words once again. ~Dear Joshua, I am sorry to inform you this, but I have just come to recent conclusion that I do not want my daughter being raised by a monster. I mean for Christ sake Joshua, you got discharged from the Marines for killing a man with your bare hands. You spent three years in prison and your daughter has been lonely all that time. Now that your release date is coming up I have decided to take Candace and leave this country, we are going to start over and I suggest you don't try and find us and make this hard on yourself. You haven't been around and we don't need an ex-convict, even if the man you killed was trying to harm me. You should have just stopped once he was unconscious and none of this would be happening... You would still be a Marine and have your life, you would still have your daughter, but no you had to continue to beat him. I am very sorry Joshua and I believe you will understand that I had to do this. I really did love you, but I lost that love a long time ago when I watched them take you away in the back of that police car. I know this kind of seems selfish, but Candace doesn't need a monster in her life like you. You use to be such a brave and strong man and now you’re just a sad pathetic waste of life, I hate you Joshua, and I hope you rot from misery for what you put me through! You put me through hell, I lived three long years alone, with no one to protect me, or fill me with warmth, while you sat in the prison... You never did feel bad, did you, you didn't know what the hell it was like knowing your husband isn't lying here with you in bed, but instead is rooming with a man in his all orange jumpsuit. People made fun of us, said I was married to a monster and how it felt to be in love with a monster, it wasn't pleasant. You are the one that caused all of this on yourself and us, it wasn't easy writing this letter, but I knew it had to be done. I will send divorce papers in the mail, in the up coming weeks or so, so please don't be surprised when they come. Well I wish it could have been some other way Joshua, but it isn't, so please don't come trying to find us, because Candace won't want to leave with a monster... Sincerely, Kendra~ Joshua begins to weep once again, those words hurt, he wasn't a monster, he just lost control when someone he loved so much was in harms way. He really wasn't a bad man, a brave soul he was, filled with courage and pride. His daughter Candace was much like him, she took a great fancy to the Marine Core and what it represented she loved going outside with her father when he was on leave and playing special ops games. They would all get decked out in black Camouflage and paint their faces to sneak around at night. She was also good with a rifle herself, being raised around guns since she first turned three when he bought her a 22 rifle, bolt action, a nice small gun to start off with. She instantly picked up like her father, just like a natural sniper would. He taught her everything he new from shooting to martial arts. The real Tom Boy, if I might add. Joshua crumbles up the letter and tosses it back on the table as he walks out of the kitchen and down the hall to the bedroom. Walking into the bedroom sends chills down his spine; he hasn't really been able to sleep in his own bed yet, always sleeping on the living room floor. Looking around the room, he stares blankly at the bed, the only piece of furniture his wife Kendra left. She never was too fond of the bed, she actually wanted to get a new one, but it was Joshua's Grandfathers bed and he would never get rid of it. Everything else was gone, all the stuff he bought for their home, gone. Not even his gun box and rifles were still there, she took everything from him, but all the materialistic stuff could be replaced. Not the part of his heart she stole. Not the sadness of not being able to see the one you helped create, no none of that could be replaced, just lost... Walking over to his closet, he opens the door and pulls out a hanger with a shirt on it, he takes the shirt off the hanger and pulls it over his head. His hand then goes over his mouth not even knowing what to think, besides the images of his daughter spiraling through his head, his brain was in a coma. No state to be doing much of anything, besides drink, which he had been doing plenty of since he was released. Joshua tosses on his coat that was lying on his bed; he then walks out of the bedroom and into the kitchen once again. Once in the kitchen, he manages to find a bottle of Jack Daniels but only a swig is left. Downing the little bit of alcohol left in the bottle, he grows angry thinking about the letter and throws the bottle at the wall across the room. The glass shattered into shards and pieces, much like how his life had just shattered in a mere instant. Joshua then wipes his mouth from the wet roughness of Jack Daniels. Walking to the front door, he grasps the door knob, twisting as he pushes walking through the open doorway, slamming the door behind him. He walks down his icy porch and down his walkway to the main side walk as he begins walking towards downtown, passing houses and people as he walks. Kind of stumbling here and there from not having slept much at all and having all the alcohol in his system from the night before and the little he had just drank. Walking the icy sidewalks he ponders on life and how it just isn't fair... Stopping for a second, he keels over holding his stomach his head almost out in the road, as vomit begins to burst from his mouth. Joshua continues to puke out into the road off from the sidewalk with a little projectile vomit, of almost pure alcohol, since he hasn't been able to eat, sleep, or do anything besides drink for the last three months. He was a complete mess, but he didn't care, how could he? He lost everything that actually meant something to him, he lost his love, his daughter, the two people he cared so much about, he lost his life. Things that would make me feel as if I had no soul, which Joshua did feel as well. Finishing up puking he wipes his mouth and spits in disgust as he continues on his way to the liquor store. Making it to the bridge downtown, he stops and looks down at the river and the trail where he use to take his daughter fishing or just for walks when she wanted too. He loved those days, he loved the outdoors, and just like him so did she. Candace was always a real daddy's girl which was one reason how he couldn't believe she wanted to leave with her mother. He believed his lost love stole her from him and she would pay, whenever he would be able to find the two. His hands grasp the rail as he spits off from the bridge, watching the wet accumulation hover to the water. Joshua then lets go of the bridge and memories of the days on the river with his little girl. He continues on his way to the liquor store, making it down past the wreck center he realizes he was heading the wrong way, until he remembers the El Dorado, mainly an all black bar underneath some apartment. Knowing he would be fine going in there being a white man, most wouldn't but people around Kent, Ohio knew Joshua A. Schuler and know he isn't one to be fucked with. So he takes a right on Elm Street and heads down the sidewalk, passing a few brotha's sitting out on their porch staring at him as if he doesn't belong. Not paying any attention to them, he continues down the middle of the street coming to the outside of the El Dorado. He staggers up the sidewalk and into the front door of the bar, as he catches awkward attention for a second, before everyone returns to their business. Joshua walks straight to the bar and takes a seat in-between to bigger brotha's who turn and look at him glaring, he just ignores them and continues to face forward. As his eyes go across the back shelf behind the bar, it has a wide variety of alcohol that could really get Joshua A. Schuler wasted to a point of no return. After looking at all the different bottles, he begins tapping his hand on the counter top waiting for the bar tender to offer him some service. After a couple minutes Joshua gets the hint that the bar tender doesn't want to serve him, so he finds it in his best interest to just speak up for himself. He doesn't speak in just a regular tone, but more in a demanding kind of way which really catches the attention of the bar tender. "Hey you fucking bastard, I need a fucking drink and if you think just because I am a little different that you aren't going to serve me, then you my friend must be fucking stupid! Now get me a shot of Jack and a beer..." The Bartender doesn't budge or say anything, which begins to agitate Joshua, who recently developed a temper and short fuse. Joshua smirks a little and looks at the two bigger guys sitting next to him, he reaches his hands up behind their heads and begins to smash their faces into the bar counter repeatedly until they fall over out of their stools. Joshua then stands up and stares into the eyes of the bar tender as he speaks again. "Now this time I am going to ask politely, could you please serve me with a shot of Jack and a brew, damn it?" The bar tender nods his head and turns grabbing the bottle of Jack Daniels, pouring a shot. Afterwards he goes over to the fridge and pulls out a nice and cold, fresh 22oz of Colt 45. The only beer that is drank around the neck of the woods Joshua was in at this very moment in time. The bar tender hands him the drinks and begins to speak in a settle tone for what just happened to two of his customers. "You know I am not mad atcha for bashing those two fuckers skulls in, but next time please just ask nicely the first time and I would have served yah. I am not racist if that is what you where getting just believe I deserve respect around here from blacks, whites, yellows, greens, or purples; you know what it is like around here. Let someone punk on yah and dey all will, this place is like a pack of wolves hungry for a bitch." Joshua just shrugs his shoulders and laughs a little bit as he begins to speak himself. "Yeah, yeah, whatever, just keep the drinks uh coming and leave your tongue off of me, I don't need to hear that shit, I have problems of my own..." The bar tender nods and continues to pour another shot of Jack and grab Joshua another beer, this continues through out the day, while others come and go as they please. Joshua sits there just drinking, drinking, and drinking some more, he doesn't really have anything to do or need a job since he saved up plenty of money being an ex-Marine. Before he had his daughter and got married he started his own bank account which he has kept even after he started a family, just for himself the bank account was for. Thank god that he ended up doing that because it sure has shown to take care of him since he was released, being able to afford all the alcohol in the world seemed to be the only thing in life Joshua could be happy about. After getting pretty drunk Joshua was feeling good, he was still angry with the world and nothing would stop that, but now he felt the need to go talk to god. Joshua taps the counter calling over the bar tender, he hands the bar tender his credit card and smiles, but before the bar tender takes it, Joshua speaks to him this time a little more friendly. "Hey brotha, could you do me a favor and sell me a flask of the Jack, so I can be on my way? I need to go talk to God about my pathetic life and I ain't quite done drinking yet, so if you could do me that huge favor that would be great!" The bar tender shakes his head and smiles. "Sure why not, but you just promise you won't leave here and go do something stupid, you are a good man and you just need to realize that." Joshua smirks and chuckles as he watches the bar tender walk over to the bottle of Jack with a flask he pulled out from under the bar counter. He fills up the flask, twisting the cap back on he hands it to Joshua and then swipes his card in his machine before giving back his credit card too, Joshua then nods his head and thanks the man as he begins to stumble out of the bar, out into the snow he walks with his flask tucked in his jacket pocket. He lets out an extremely loud burp before tumbling over, falling face first in the cold white powder. He just rolls over on his back and begins screaming out loud as people watch and stare for a bit before just ignoring his belligerent ass. "Fucking aaaah! God damn snow is all fuckin slippery and shit, no one even loves me anymore, I feel so weak and pathetic anymore, fucking aaaah! Fuck love, fuck women, and most importantly fuck the CORE!" Joshua then begins to throw up with his head tilted to the side; he then wipes his mouth with white snow and climbs to his feet. Once on his feet he looks at some of the people who stopped and where staring at him, he once again begins to yell out in belligerence. "WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU LOOKING AT?!”!" The words scare those staring as they scamper off, Joshua just throws his hands and begins to walk down the sidewalks again, heading towards the towns church. Every once and awhile he takes out his flask and takes a few sips on it. Before making it to the church, Joshua takes out a pack of cigarettes from his pocket and pulls one out, which slips out of his hand. The cigarette falls to the snow and Joshua just spits at it in rage and stupidity, Joshua then pulls another one out and places it between his dry parched lips. He then pulls out his zippo and lights the end of the cigarette with tobacco almost hanging out, he inhales once and exhales the smoke, as he begins to feel a little calmer. Joshua continues on his path, finally making it to the church two cigarettes and half a flask latter. Standing outside of the church he looks up to the heavens as he begins asking himself who he is. 'Who am I? A question I had been asking myself for many months now, ever since my wife left and took my daughter, I have hated myself... Every time I look in the mirror from that date September 15, 2007 all I can see is someone who I had never known existed. Was I completely lost for ever, which it had felt like so far, could I never be Joshua A. Schuler again? Who was this new man I had became, I didn't know, but I kind of liked him, he was less merciful and didn't really have too many cares in the world... Plus his only weakness is my daughter, not being able to see her drives him even more insane than I could have ever imagined. Depraved is his name, not only because he is morally corrupt, but because he doesn't fear people or anything, even if they were depraved. He isn't Joshua A. Schuler but he certainly does know what to do with my body, I could not yet get him under control, for he had only been around for a few months, since I lost my soul... So God, do you have those answers, why is this being taking over me, what the hell did I do to deserve this?' Joshua's thick fingerless gloved fist closes as he stares up into the heavens wanting answers, his eyes glossed over with hate as his muscles tense. Filled with rage and emotion as he walks into the church before his eyes no one empty seats. For a few weeks he had been no where but home since he had lost everything from binge drinking on a regular schedule. What else was he really to do, he felt so dead inside, like he was worthless. The Marines have no more use for him and his wife couldn't handle him anymore, where was he to turn, he had no clue, but he figured he would try a fighting route again. Walking into the church, he felt the presence of God, but he does not allow him to be blessed for he reeks of terrible horrors and sins... He is not nor never was the holiest of men but for some reason, he had been finding himself kneeling before GOD looking for guidance. In need of something, he wouldn't give up; he would keep calling for a sign. Passing row after row of pew’s he stops at the seventh row and walks into the aisle, taking his seat in the same place he had been going to for a while now. Still smelling of Jack Daniels and beer, wearing an all black leather jacket, blue jeans and a thermal shirt, and a pair of work boots. He was a little scuffing and trashy looking from the cold harsh night before, he still smelt like brew and booze. It wasn't his fault he had nothing to change into, so he had to stay in his alcohol soaked clothes or freeze to death. To be honest smelling like booze was better to him than freezing and not living to see what he was meant for in life... Feeling the rage building up even more as he takes a seat before the fatherly one, with a pastur standing at the back of the church eyeballing his every movement. For Depraved had tryed out for a wrestling promotion who honored the ring, something that was quite MMA but close enough. He showed him another way, now hopefully he would make enough money doing the whole wrestling career to support his booze and provide him with some dignity, because the winter was just starting to freeze over hell. He falls down to his knee's before the creator of the heavenly gates as he folds his hands, bows his head, and close his eyes, as he begins to speak. "GOD please I really need you in this moment in time, I have finally chosen a path to follow thanks to the guardian you have sent me... Depraved has been showing me paths of my choosing and allowing me to bring some fortitude my way, which I am very thankful for. You have given me this angel, who has allowed me to see through all the darkness that has been cast down upon me and now it is my turn to pay thanks..." A dark wind blows through the small church blowing out the holy light as I fall into darkness, as if I was in some sort of coma as DEPRAVED, enters my body... +++Damn. How it felt to be so damn alive, it is about time someone can put Joshua A. Schuler to use. He is such a powerful being and he would never even know if it wasn't for me. I begin to chuckle to myself a little, in a satanic hiss. My eyes slowly open from when Joshua was in control, I raise my head, and climb to my feet. Spitting at the floor, I look into the heavens out a high window on the church and begin to speak a little, with anger and pain in my voice. "I bow to no man or GOD, not even the holiest of men, I am no sinner nor saint, I am my own. I fight for no one besides myself. I kill for pleasure and hunt for glory, no man or beast shall stand in my way. Nothing I will fear. The fearless shall strive!" Looking from the heavens into the eyes of the pastor I begin to smile maniacally as I lick my lower lip in excitement. As my adrenaline begins to pump, I walk out from between the benches and back into the main aisle. I start walking towards the pastor passing a row of benches that held the people who looked for some high being to save them from themselves. I stop and take out my flask taking a drink, not caring I am in the house of god. Not a single ounce of happiness was pumping through my veins just hate, hate for the Marines, hate towards my wife, and most importantly hate towards myself. How could I not hate me for not letting myself go, for some reason I kept the urge to live, but not yet LIVE... Why? Instead of dwelling in thought for as long as I would have enjoyed, I stare into the eyes of the pastor with a demon's pierce. "Why the hell do people come and look up to your god and the man's god that was just here kneeling like some smut? I honestly don't get it, he doesn't do shit for anyone except tare them down, he makes humans believe they are no good and get down on themselves. Religion is a fucking waste if you ask me and your ass is just apart of the brainwashing all you holy men do to us, and you call yourselves God's decedents... Hah, your a fucking joke if you ask me and deserve to be punished, you pathetic piece of shit!" I smirk and pull out a flask of Jack Daniels from inside my leather jacket, twisting the top off; I toss it to the floor. Boy was I fucked up, if there was a heaven I definitely wouldn't be going. Taking a huge swig from the bottle as I walk closer to the pastor. My other fist is quenched in a tight ball of fury, wanting to harm anyone and anything. Knowing I must wait for the right time and place to do so, I calmly take another swig from the bottle. It sent a fiery river down my throat like rampaging waters; I could almost feel my chest grow hair. I loved whiskey, one of the best drinks on the damned earth. Looking into the pastor’s eyes looking a complete mess in front of God himself, I begin to speak again. "Don't be bothered by my appearance either, it isn't what you will have to worry about, now what I want to do inside this very church or where ever is... Just because one associates with alcohol doesn't mean they can't do what all the people can, does it? Because I guarantee that I can do what every single last one of you so called holy men can do... You people aren't so damn special, you don't even know the meaning of life, and you sit here in your robes behind your church walls and do what? Preach to people on how they should live their lives, well guess what, if there is a God don't you think he would have equipped us with utensils to live on our own. Yeah sure some fucks like violence, death, and destruction, but who is to say they are fucking wrong? Violence is something I breathe for, so fucking what; does that make me a bad guy? No it fucking doesn't! It makes me a monster ready to be unleashed and do what monsters do best, DESTROY! Oh yeah and don't feel the need to try and change me, because I don't give a shit about life or death, only PAIN!" I then go completely insane as I smash the candle stick next to me. The pastor runs out the church as I take a swig from the Jack and hold it in my mouth. Pulling a lighter from my pocket I flick the flint and as the flame ignites, I think about spitting the jack from my mouth: Directly into the flame it travels, spreading the fire like a flamethrower all over the church's wooden benches. I laugh as the flames begin to feed on other wood and books, many bibles begin to burn. Standing in the flames before I come back to reality. Standing in the church I scream out to god, "Fuck off!" I then begin to loose control as I charge through the room, flipping shit, smashing shit, and just throwing stuff all over. As I black out, only remembering red...+++ Joshua wakes up somewhere, not having a clue as to what had happened or where he was. It was cold and dark; he could feel concrete beneath his back and ass. Sitting up he realizes he is under the bridge downtown; he looks at his hands as they are crimson and fleshy from the trashed church he had ripped apart. Instead of remembering what had happened earlier that day, he could just picture red, what the hell did the color red have to do with anything? He had no clue what it meant or who he was... "Who am I?" The perfect question to ask yourself, because most people really have no clue who they are. He knows for sure he is neither sinner nor a saint, he is just here, and he has no true purpose, besides to figure out the ultimate meaning of whom we are and why we seek... Who am I? Is all he seeks...
