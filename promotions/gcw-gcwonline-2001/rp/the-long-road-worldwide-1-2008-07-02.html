The Long Road: WorldWide 1

2 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2 July 08 I. The old man’s eyes stay fixed on me, challenging me, the old bastard was always trying to test me. He’s damn near seventy years old but he could probably pass as a 50 year old stud. His hair is full, long and thick, even if it’s grayed out. The wrinkles in his face don’t completely tell the story of his life; it’s his eyes that allow a person to understand. They are blue like mine but not as bright, not as lively. Instead those eyes are a faded and dull blue but still that stare is as intimidating as ever. Joaquin doesn’t walk with a hunch or a limp like many others his age would do; he keeps himself in too good of condition for something like that. His shoulder length hair is kept in place by a black tie, only a few loose strands are allowed to roam free. If he ever did smile I guess the best way to describe that smile would be crooked, awkward. He isn’t used to smiling much I’d guess. “Worldwide 1,” I reveal very quickly. “My first match was actually at that show. I didn’t waste anytime getting back into things.” “Then tell me,” His voice is deep, thick with the Spanish accent. “Everything you remember.” “I remember everything.” II. “The following hardcore contest is set for one fall, and is for the GCW Hardcore Championship! Making his way to the ring being accompanied by his manager Troy Newman weighing in at 318 pounds from Werneuchen, Germany’Niklas Bauer!” Joey Andrews words lit a fire under the crowd. Immediately the “USA” chants filled the arena. I looked up at my opponent just as he was prepared to walk through the curtains and he glared back at me briefly, just enough time to give me an arrogant wink and he walked through the curtains and out in front of the crowd. I remember envying him in just that one moment; he was out in front of the capacity crowd. A loyal and dedicated bunch. The adrenaline flowing, the passion swelling up inside, the anxiety of wanting to do well. I was determined not to make a fucking fool of myself this time around, this time I would make Steven Caldera understand that I was the only man capable of carrying GCW. I also found it humorous. The German getting booed, chants of USA and all right? Yet a few moments later the fans would see a Mexican, raised in Spain come down to fight him. There’s nothing American about that is there? I can’t recall all of the bullshit that Bauer’s manager spewed out that night, most of it didn’t matter. I just remember getting more and more nervous as the moments rolled on by. Steven Caldera sitting front row in the announcer’s chair, The GCW Hardcore Title on the line, making a good impression being of the utmost importance. Then I heard it. “AND HIS OPPONENT?” I felt a knot tie up in my throat, I couldn’t breathe. O’Fortuna hits? “From Mexico City, Mexico. Weighing in at 266 pounds”.” I closed my eyes, taking it all in. Trying to stop my hands from shaking and my knees from trembling. “LESTATT KNIIIIIIIIGHT!” My eyes opened up. “Let’s fucking go.” I didn’t figure to get a great reception but on that night it felt amazing. I don’t know if they cheered because I was facing some arrogant German fuck or because it was the first match the new GCW would see, it didn’t matter. It was my time, it was my night. The walk to the ring is a blur; nerves got the better of me but fuck if I wasn’t ready to throw down. “DING-DING” Like a swimmer diving as soon as that gun sounds I rushed Niklas Bauer as if I had been fired out from inside a cannon. His look of surprise would have made me laugh out loud if I hadn’t been trying to rip his god damn head off. He was huge and hard to move but I could still use my strength and I remember tossing him to the ropes. I ran after him and launched my body through the air. BAM!! I collided with him, landing a nasty clothesline. The kind of clothesline that could take a man’s body apart. Yet Bauer barely moved backwards, the fucking hulk of mass that he was. I could only think to myself; Do it again! So I did. WHAM!! I landed another clothesline, this one sending that German freak back some more but again he did not fall, like some giant stubborn Red Wood tree. I was feeling frustrated already, I just imagined Caldera and the rest of those assholes that ran the place laughing at me. So I rushed at Bauer again ? the fuck hit me with a spine buster. Now understand that this was the first match I’d had in months. I hadn’t felt so much as a body slam let alone a spine buster from a 300lbs. behemoth. Needless to say, it hurt ? bad. Quite frankly I thought that was it, all the air was forced from my lungs with that one blow. I couldn’t breath, I could barely fucking see. It didn’t help matters that that big bastard laid all his body weight on top of me to get the cover. ONE! I felt the vibrations from the referee’s hand slamming against the mat. TWO! I closed my eyes, already thinking of where the hell else I was going to work after losing such a sad and short match. The three never came though. I managed to sneak off my back, not sure how even to this day. Bauer couldn’t believe it either but what he couldn’t believe more was that by the time he got to his feet, I was there waiting for him. Normally the element of surprise is a good thing in these situations right? Catch your opponent off guard, hit em? with a quick one and gain the upper hand. Niklas Bauer had other plans. As in lifting me over his head with a guerilla press. It hurts like hell being dropped from that high. I did what most young ones do in this situation, get the hell out of there. I rolled right as fast as I could and spilled out onto the floor. It was really the only thing I could do to even attempt to catch my breath, regroup. It’s your typical idiot wrestler move ? which really says it all about this match. I just don’t do typical, except that night. I swear this is when, if I were in a cartoon, that a light bulb would have turned on right over my head. We were in a Hard Core title match, key words being Hard and Core. I remember thinking; it’s time to take advantage of that. I took off straight for the time keeper’s table ? and by straight I mean not straight at all, wobbly really. Now with a steel chair in hand I climb back into the ring and stand across from Bauer. At this point I had no real clue what would come next. Well yeah I’d try to bash his brains in but it’s not that simple with a guy that big, Godzilla size is what that fuck was. I didn’t know it at the time but I was scared, intimidated by Bauer in that moment. I looked into his eyes; there was a fire in those eyes. A fire that I had been missing since I beat Payne down a few years before that. “HIT ME!” Bauer screamed those two words at me over and over, probably the only two damn words in the whole English language that he could actually speak. Well that was until he gave me a third word. “COWARD!” Fear turned into embarrassment and that turned into anger. I swung as hard as I could, grunting as the chair came flying over my head. The sound of metal and skull colliding filled my ears and my hands hurt from the impact and the chair falls from my hands. I was on my back before I knew what was going on. That chair shot didn’t do shit and I was back to square one. Reacting wasn’t an option as I felt the first of five crushing chair shots slam into my body, again ripping the air from my lungs. It felt like my insides had been scrambled, everything was upside down. Then that 300lbs. monster fell on top of me again for a pin fall. 1! 2! NO! I couldn’t let it end that way! My body was weak and broken and getting up probably would never have happened if it wouldn’t have been for Bauer’s assistance. Not that him grabbing me by my hair and then tossing me back outside the ring was the kind of help I exactly needed at the time. It was embarrassing, me on my hands and knees, crawling around the ring trying to get away from my opponent. Funny when I think back on it now, it’s something I’d never do today. After half a lap around the ring with Bauer on my tail something clicked. I dunno, I can’t really explain it. All of the things that I didn’t recognize in myself melted away, embarrassment, fear, being timid. None of it mattered anymore; none of it would define my career in GCW. My fists clenched up into fists, my teeth grinded together. I could hear my heart pounding in my chest as if it were ready to explode. The laughter that I imagined before from Caldera was even louder now but this time it was different ? this time I was going to make it fucking stop. I got to one knee in time to see Bauer charging forward so I worked him, tripping him up with a drop toe hold, causing his face to slam into the steel steps. The sight of that man’s blood splattering out of his mouth and onto those steps made me grin that sadistic grin that has become one of my trademarks. I was blood drunk, that feeling washed over my entire body. Move after move I continued my mission to dominate. I smashed his head with those same steel steps again. I pick the chair back up and crack is against Bauer’s skull And back And chest And ribs And face And shoulder Again and again and again and again. It was glorious and invigorating! I throw the chair down and growl into the air! Bauer rolls off of those steel steps and lands on his hands and knees on the floor, this time it’s HIM crawling for his life. This time it’s him that’s afraid and embarrassed. THIS TIME I muscle the steps up over my head and with a running start I tossed the steps and watching and enjoying the sound they make as they crush Niklas? cranium. I closed my eyes and took it all in. The crowd, the lights, the intensity and I loved it all, I cherished it all. In that one moment I had the world in my palm and it was my fucking time! I was going to be the god damn hard core champion of GCW! I was going to start my run to the top on that night! NO ONE WAS TAKING IT AWAY FROM ME! My eyes opened ? Niklas Bauer got to one knee. I could smell his blood and his fear as if they were one and the same. The giant pushed himself up to his feet, no matter how wobbly those legs were. I locked in as if I was carrying guided missiles. He looked at me. I charged with a roar! IT WAS MY FUCKING TIME! . .. ? “. But it really wasn’t. I had to watch the video tape over and over again to get what happened. All I remember from the moment itself is the whole world spinning and then it all got blurry and I heard the bell. “LADIES AND GENTLEMAN! THE WINNER ? AND NEEEEEEEEEW GCW HARDCORE CHAMPION! NIKLAS BAUER!” I didn’t see Bauer get his title or leave the ring. I forced myself up to my feet as quickly as I possibly could. Trying to save some face you know? I smiled at Bauer as he disappeared to the back, the title belt draped over his shoulder. So I stood alone in the center of the GCW ring, a loser. It was an eerily familiar feeling. The difference? I wouldn’t let it break me down this time. No fucking way.
