AXEL in TIME- Chapter V: A Wound That Only Time Can Heal, Part 1

1 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 1, 2009 “When the clash of swords echo, the journey chasing the past ends and the journey to know today begins. One soul seeks another and pledges their sworn friendship to another. One mystery begets the next and the doors of fate are opened.” -- An excerpt from The Legend of Dragoon Fate, as defined by the Dictionary, is the universal principle or ultimate agency by which the order of things is presumably prescribed; the decreed cause of events. It is believed that every event that occurs is already predetermined by fate, and that one’s life is planned out by some higher power even before said being is brought into the world. For some, fate has nothing but good in store; but for others, it’s a cruel, cruel mistress. For those who are unfortunate enough to somehow incur fate’s wrath, they are forced to lead lives of heartache and misery, never really knowing what true happiness is. Those select few unfortunate souls are dealt what is known as a shitty hand in the Poker game of life. They focus all their anxieties and hatred of their misfortunes on other, more fortunate people, leading to nothing but more hate. Some people look at fate as being that inherently evil child who would always sit over an anthill with a magnifying glass, seeing how many of the poor insects he can set on fire with it -- a torturous, psychotic child who has nothing better to do with its life than to make others miserable. In reality, though, fate is nothing more than life’s way of kicking someone in the teeth when they’re down. Fate has a funny way of putting a lot of things into perspective, especially when it comes to people who take their lot in life for granted. AXEL Action has always been one of those people who takes everything for granted and assumes that everything will be the same tomorrow as it is today. For years, he’s prided himself on being the best all-around wrestler to come down the pike and has touted himself as the most underrated talent to ever sign a contract for a major wrestling promotion. For all his pride and ego, he failed to see that he was taken his God-given talents for granted, and it’s like they say: “The Good Lord giveth and the Good Lord taketh away.” When it comes to talent, AXEL isn’t any worse for wear, nor has he lost any of the strides he’s made. Fate has its own, special way of dealing with brash, arrogant people like The Unholy One, and he was unfortunate enough to bring its wrath down upon himself like the mighty hammer of Thor. Over the last few months, he’s been tossed around the timeline; bounced from Ancient China to Elizabethan England. He’s been beaten, cut, jailed, nearly executed and had his heart broken all for what -- a lesson in humility? He had finally found his happy place, the one thing he’d been lacking all of his life. One woman had turned his entire life around for the better and had given him a reason to go on living, a reason to look forward to each passing day. Having finally found someone he could love and call his own, he thought life had finally given thrown him a bone, so to speak. Boy, was he EVER wrong! Almost as soon as it had given him his moment of happiness, that cold, calculating hand of fate took it all away from him in one, fell swoop. “Why?” That is a question that we, as human beings often find ourselves asking when something bad happens to us. It’s the most natural reaction to things when a loved one dies or a lover leaves us. It’s funny how the human psyche fills with all sorts of questions in times of turmoil or tribulation, almost to the point of overload. In these times of pain and agony, some people even question their faith in their God of choice, even going so far as to swear them off completely. One man in particular has made it his quest to fix the wrong that the fates have done him. He’s had his fill of asking why and blaming other people for his misfortunes, and he’s decided that he’s going to take matters into his own hands. He’s decided that he’s going to find a way to go back for his beloved Alysea, and that she’s going home with him this time; even if he has to drag her. After all, what does he have to lose? He knows that his chances of actually getting back to that period of time are practically zero, zilch, nada, none. He also knows that there’s no guarantee that she’ll even still be alive upon his return, with the Queen’s bloodlust the way it is. Even so, his mind and heart are set on regaining the one thing in the world that means more to him than any silly wrestling championship ever could -- his family. He was finally going to be what every man aspires to become: a husband and father. He had plans to teach their child right from wrong, and to give him or her the life that his own father couldn’t give him when he was a child. Fate always has a way of throwing the proverbial monkey wrench into one’s plans and ruining everything. Even if all seems lost, when one door closes, another opens, and the door of opportunity has opened once again. In his effort to find a way to return to a previously visited era of time, he managed to find a way to control the portals ever so slightly. Upon doing some trial-by-error research, he found that items made from steel can slightly bend the course of a temporal rift and change the direction in which it goes. In order to even have a slight chance of changing the era in which he gets sent to next, he will need a piece of steel large enough to divert the energies of the rift into a different direction. Stroking his chin, AXEL paces across the floor of his hotel room in New York City, circa 1921, the era of prohibition. As he moves back and forth across the room, his eyes scan his surroundings for anything that might help him to achieve his goal. After searching the room for what seems like an eternity, he spots the only remotely metal object in the room that’s large enough to suit his needs: the bed. “Hmm… Could I use this?” He decides to take a closer look at the rather fancy, elegant bed and give it a thorough examination. Upon close inspection, he finds that it is, indeed, made of steel and that it can be used. Figuring it will probably be easier to take a piece of the bed with him instead of the entire thing, he searches the room over for something to take the hunk of metal apart with. Finding nothing but a letter opener, he decides that for the time being, it will have to do the trick. Dismantling the bed piece by piece, which was a rather slow process with the letter opener, he finally managed to pull the headboard loose from the rest of the bed. Gazing around the room, his eyes lock onto the wall where the rift had appeared when he came through to Prohibition City, as he likes to call it. Sitting down in the lone chair in the room, he decides that the best course of action at this time is to wait. Without a clue as to when the rift will appear again, there’s really no telling how long it will take, and AXEL realizes that. His wait is made easier knowing that he’s one step closer to reuniting with his dearest Alysea, but the question is, will fate allow him to? He’d just have to wait and see.
