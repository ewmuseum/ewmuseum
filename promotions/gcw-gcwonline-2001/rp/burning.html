Burning

undated

Date: Timeless Canada?” It’s a calm night. A green and black motorcycle pulls up outside of a gate. Stepping off of the motorcycle is a man dressed in a black and green leather motorcycle outfit and helmet. He removes the helmet, letting his black hair fall to his shoulders. His face is just outside of the light, making his features almost impossible to determine. “So, this is where the wicked play” I’m impressed. I knew that pure evil could afford a lot, but I never thought that it could afford this.” The camera pans around to find the man standing outside of the McFarlane Estate in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. The camera pans back around to find the man stepping into the light. His green eyes pick up the illumination very well. Dorian Ryan was staring right into the darkness. “Eventually, those that take this residence as it’s own is going to find that it’s going to be difficult to hold onto what they cherish most dearly. They are going to lose the power that they have garnered in GCW.” The winds have changed. Dorian Ryan was within a handful of feet of being able to end it all with one violent act. Instead, he put on his helmet and pulled away. The next day again found Ryan on his motorcycle. This time, instead of being just outside of the McFarlane estate, he is making his way to the Detroit-Windsor tunnel. As he’s riding through Windsor, he notices that his gas is running low, so he stops at the nearest gas station. As he dismounts the bike and removes his helmet, he notices a kid staring at him. Instead of talking to the kid, he just pulls a pair of sunglasses from out of his riding leathers and puts them on. He walks into the gas station and grabs a bottle of Cherry Coke. “That be all for you, sir?” A young college student asked from behind the counter. “See the bike over there?” “Yes sir.” “Fill it.” “Yes sir.” Dorian pulls out a credit card to pay for his purchases and heads back out to his bike. He opens the gas tank. Dorian then takes the nozzle from the pump and inserts it into the bike. It only takes a minute to fill up the bike. During that time, Dorian drank down the bottle of soda. “Hey, mister.” The little boy that saw Dorian pull up is now standing next to him. “Yes.” “My dad used to ride a bike like that, until he got hurt.” “Is that so?” “Yeah, he doesn’t ride anymore. He just sits on the couch and complain about the Canadiens.” “Well, kid. That’s great. Personally, I think all the problems with the Canadiens started when they traded Roy to the Avalanche, but that’s just me.” “I recognize you from somewhere, don’t I?” “I don’t know, kid.” Ryan takes off his sunglasses and puts his helmet back on. He then peels out and heads even closer to the border. Eventually, he finds himself back in the States. Portland? The city of Portland near the end of the summer seems alive. Maybe it’s the fact that GCW is rolling into town. Maybe it’s just the way the city seems during the summer. Either way, Dorian Ryan finds it rather unbearable as he tries to sleep. Then, as he’s just about to finally get to sleep, his phone rings. He quickly rolls over and answers it. “Do you know what time it is?” Dorian sits up and turns on X:TV. He is horrified at the images that he sees. His home, the home that he and his wife shared, ablaze. “Authorities aren’t sure how the fire started. What we do know is that Andrea Ryan was able to get out of the home safely. Her husband, GCW Wrestler Dorian Ryan, we are told is currently in Portland, Oregon for an upcoming GCW WorldWide event.” A tear starts to form in Ryan’s eye. A sense of sadness fills Ryan. He looks in horror as the fire consumes everything that he’s ever saved in his life. His memories of his first wife and child. Sonograms, pictures, memories have all gone up in flames. Dorian hangs the phone up. He then brings the handset back to his ear and dials a number. “Andrea, are you ok?” On the other end of the line, Andrea responds that she’s OK. She then starts to cry as everything that they have is destroyed by the fire. “The important part is that you’re OK. All that other stuff, it’s meaningless compared to you.” The next day, Andrea arrives in Portland. She walks into the hotel wearing a Minnesota State Wrestling T-Shirt and a pair of sweats. Serena Ryan follows behind her. Andrea sees Dorian and they hug. Andrea nearly collapses as she starts to cry in Dorian’s chest. “Dorian, I’m so sorry. I was scared. I couldn’t save any of it.” “Andrea, you don’t have to apologize, you’re OK. That’s all that matters right now.” Dorian motions to one of the bellhops. “Can you please show my wife to our room.” “No problem, Mr. Ryan. Please follow me, ma’am.” As Andrea and the bellhop head towards the elevator, Dorian and Serena head towards the hotel bar. “It’s a good thing that you and Andrea stored stuff in our garage.” “Yeah. How much was lost?” Again, a tear forms in Dorian’s eye, as he already knows the answer to the question. “Just about everything. They were able to recover your safe, so anything that was in it was spared.” “You know, Serena, no matter how hard I try, I can never get a break in life. First it’s me and Jay losing our sister, then it’s my nearly dying. Then it was Keiko and Dan. Now this.” “I know, Dorian, I know. It’s hard.” “You know what hurts about all of this. The fact that Andrea feels guilty about all the stuff that we lost.” “She doesn’t care about the stuff that she lost. It hurts her all of the stuff that you lost from Keiko.” “Accidents happen, and Andrea blames herself for the loss. You don’t know how much that hurts.” “Tell me about it.” “I can’t do this right now. I’ve got a match to worry about, and right now, I can’t even think about that. I have a wife who’s hurt, and I need to take care of her. Make sure that she’s alright. Thanks for coming.” “Don’t mention it. Listen, you and Andrea can stay with Jason and myself until you get back on your feet.” “Thanks.”
