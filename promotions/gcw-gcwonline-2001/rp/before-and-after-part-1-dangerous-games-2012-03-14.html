Before and after Part 1 **Dangerous Games**

14 March 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/14/12 Monday 5:30 am. Diary Entry Sitting here quietly I begin to ponder the course of my day. Things have slowly begun falling apart. My life at home is rough. My wife moved out leaving me with everything but herself and my daughter. She tells me when I find myself I can come find her. In her on way this is her being right, yet it is the wrong thing to do. So what do I do? I let her go of course, It is the only logical thing to do. My heads pounding from the damn migraines I get from thinking about everything too much. What have I got left to prove? Am I even a value to everything I do anymore? No there is something I need to do and that's redefine my place, I have everything I could want monetarily. I own businesses, I am a world renown wrestler. What could I really want in the very end of all of this? For years I have always had people tell me I am not good enough. Once had a few jack asses tell me that the only reason I had won titles is because people felt bad for me and gifted them. Where are these fucking idiots now? I'll tell you where these fat out of a job slobs are, Home sitting on their asses wishing they could keep it up as much as I have. Always being told you can't do this or you can't do that, every time I do it and they shut up. Then out of the blue they try again and fail. Hey here is a hint morons, you are not going to defeat me or are you going to keep me kicked down. I will scratch and claw my way back up, when I get there kick your damn teeth down your throat. I made the big time in 1997 after working small for awhile, then bam I grew and now have watched places crumble and fail. Here I am in GCW watching everyone work to get where they wanna be and what has happened to me? I'll tell you I fell behind thinking inside the box rather than outside of it, shits gonna change now. I am done writing for now I am tired of this bullshit, time for something new... 9:15 am Office meeting with Agent and Movie producers. Watching the men carefully he looks over at his Agent and speaks to him softly. His eyes not leaving the two men as he does not trust them in the slightest bit. "Can you trust these guys in the slightest? Are they on the up and up?" He slowly turns his head towards his agent waiting for a simple response as he only nods. A sudden interruption turns his attention back to the two in business suits and rolling out movie flyers. Pointing at three posters put up on tripods the men speak clearly. "Shaman or should we call you Mr. WhiteCloud?" He looks at them kind of annoyed and simply motions them to continue. "Ahh uhm ok anyways here is what we have for you. Imagine would you." Spinning he points a laser pointer almost as deftly as a swordsman in a contest in medieval times. The poster reveals Shaman standing in torn and burnt clothing, One of his eyes glowing red with torn body parts of Terminators around him, nearby on the poster is Arnold Schwarzenegger holding up a beaten and battered Christian Bale, Arnold looks worn and torn revealing his own Terminator parts. "You a secret experiment on a Terminator thought failed and simply dumped away in a secret bunker by Cyberdine, Your failure is a plus to the resistance. They find you and bring you back to operational status only to find that you have your own sub routine not connected to the machines, in fact you are your own being something different and an individual. You decide to fight the machines rather than join them as you are your own being. You take up Native American philosophies of war and combat which confuse the machines and allows you to wage a guerrilla war.." Holding up your hand you cut him off slightly, Thinking for a moment you speak up. "Wouldn't it be easier to keep the experiments secret even to some of those in the resistance? Allowing the mystery to roll in a finely tuned detail, revealing that perhaps the other main players know, but kept it to themselves?" He then points to the other two movie posters. "Conan really? Who am I supposed to be?" Waiting for an answer he stares carefully at the poster. Knowing he has read most of the books written of Conan. The gentleman look at each other and then look back at Shaman. "Well we were hoping you'd play a new Barbarian King seeking revenge on Conan for killing your brother. Only to find Conan did not kill your brother. After a massive battle you and Conan come to a grudging respect to kill a sorcerer who in the end you discover killed your brother." Standing proud the man explaining looks over at Shaman who is motionless, actually kind of in shock and awe at what they suggested. He looks at the last one that says Little Big Horn. He stands up and walks over to the poster and lifts it off the tripod. He looks at it carefully and begins tearing it in half. "Sorry Gentlemen, but this was no before it even got explained. I won't condone slaughter of my ancestors by playing a part that degrades myself and who I am with my heritage." Turning as he heads to the door shaking his head as he waves them off. "My agent will contact you, Thank you for your time Gentlemen." Shutting the door behind himself he walks down the hall. He opens a door and enters revealing a hidden suite in his Casino for himself. Walking casually to a chair he sits down staring out the window at the desert beyond. Narrowing his gaze he simply ponders what is to come today, it is going to be a busy one he knows. 12:33 PM Las Vegas McCarran International Airport Carrying a travel bag with all he needs, he heads to a private jet. Boarding the jet he ducks his head as he enters, Slowly he sits down feeling the texture of the leather seats as he bought this plane from a Government surplus company. This used to be a private jet for Generals and Officers in the Air Force. Not a bad price for one such as this and owning a hangar at the airport isn't bad either he thinks to himself. Watching as the flight crew closes the door, he taps his fingers enjoying the privacy of this specific flight. Not knowing whats going on he waits patiently for his day to unfold, what will unfold before himself. He turns and looks at the waitress aka air stewardess hired for this job. He motions for her to come forward. "Get me a beer please, Once the pilot informs us we are air bound." He leans back as the stewardess nods and seats herself buckling up. Looking out the window he watches the runway race by disappearing into the distance as the rise above the earth. In a way he begins to compare what is happening and how his career in wrestling can be compared to a take off and landing of an air craft. Tired of traveling, tired of the road, just tired lately. Watching his own career wash by the window, Hardly remembering half the times who is coming and who is going. Falling asleep slowly he closes his eyes as he drifts away to dream, the dream is a sweet medley of memories long gone, once again he is some place unfamiliar to him or is it. The land shimmers with waves of heat blowing across the great deserts, looking up you see the double suns as they scorch the land below. In the distance you see a shimmering aqua green sea, In the foreground a village lies before you. Slowly you begin to march down the path. As you walk many people walk past you, several very familiar faces. You stop as you recognize several of them, old opponents you think. Following them as you proceed you seem to be slowed as another passes you. Your steps seem shorter and your stride slows. You are no longer walking as fast as you suddenly stop and wipe your brow from the scorching heat. Then suddenly a hand grasps you on the shoulder. A smile on this young man brings you back to a bit of a focus. You see yourself from when you first began, he smiles emitting radiant confidence. He speaks catching you a bit off guard with his words. "Come on old man or you'll be left behind." he turns and jogs down the path ahead of you, but your feet feel like lead weights as you begin to run slowly. Each steps seems to distance you from them further and further and before you know it you are on the trail by yourself. You stop and realize the trail has changed, you are no longer familiar with your surroundings and then with a sharp slaps to the face you seem you focus once again as a woman stands before you. It is your mother she is angered. She turns and heads down a different path as you realize it isn't the struggle that is bothering you, it is moving on from the past that is taking you down that longer spiral. Stopping you feel cold rain splashing against your face, you close your eyes for a moment and then you open them again as you awake on the private jet. You see the stewardess holding a cold face cloth smiling at you. "You were sweating sir so I wiped your face to try and cool you down a bit." You nod to her respectfully as you sit up taking the cold be she has offered you. You look out the window again as you wonder where your going to be in your future before you know it. 6:17 Pm St. Louis Missouri... Standing near the Mississippi River looking up at the arch his eyes focus at the top and slowly down towards the Courthouse in the distance. "This is called the gateway to the west, Perhaps it is also the gateway to my future." He simply brings his gaze back up to the arch as he waits for his gateway to appear before him, In the end it comes down to what awaits him at the Dangerous Games Rumble and will he do well enough to take down his opponents with prejudiced aggression. Motto: Why wait till tomorrow when you can use a hand grenade today to take care of the problem before it reaches tomorrow.
