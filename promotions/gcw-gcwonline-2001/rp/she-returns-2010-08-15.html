She Returns...

15 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/15/10 It had been several years since I have heard anything regarding Katrina, and it was even longer since I've seen here. She was nothing more than a double edged sword in my eyes. She was the mother to my daughter, and the reason my life had turned into a complete Hell. Although at the same time she was a delicious poison I could not get rid of, and here she was again. Home: [Sitting on the edge of the sofa my mind is a little baffled as I wonder why Katrina is here in my life again. She sits across from me with her legs crossed, and the sun is flowing through the window, reflecting off her raven locks. I notice her looking towards me, with a wise smile across her face as she knows I'm completely confused as to why she's here.] Donny: Why exactly are you here Katrina? Katrina: Do you really need to be so hostile, is it so wrong for me to miss you? It took me years to build up the courage to come here and admit my faults to you in hopes of us making things right again. Donny: Make things right again? Do you not remember all the things you put me through, all the pain you caused me to live with? Nothing good ever happens when you're around Katrina. I have something good going for me now, and I don't need you back in my life making sure all those good things go bad. Katrina: My dear, dear, Donny that's exactly what I'm talking about. I remember all of the trouble we had and I want to make it all better again. I've seen you on television... Donny: So that's it right? You saw that I was wrestling again and you decided now was the right time for you to come squeeze some more cash out of me! [She sits up from her relaxed position on the couch across from me, making her way towards me. Leaning in I can feel the heat from her breath against my face, and softly she kisses me. For a moment I let her continue, but then I force myself back into reality. Grabbing her by the arms I push her back, but she slaps my hands down and takes a seat on my lap.] Donny: No... You will not do this to me again. Katrina: Do what my love? All I was going to say is; I saw you on television and wanted to help you get back on your game. Help take you to the top again, unlike what that old man has done for you. Remember when we first got together? I made you unstoppable, back when AXEL was nothing more than a lower card jobber. Now AXEL is main event material and you're barely making waves in a hardcore division, we can reverse those roles again Donny. Donny: No... It wasn't Larry's fault I didn't win the gold, it was mine, because I was to focused on Shaman. Katrina: And Larry would let you believe that, but I know better, and I know you can do better. But I'm not going to force you baby. Go take a shower, a walk, or sleep on it if that's what you need to do, but I just want to make things better again. I'll go and put my stuff up in your room, you don't mind right? Donny: Um... Sure. I think I'm going to go and take that walk then... Katrina: Okay baby, enjoy yourself and I'll be right here waiting for you. [As she gets up from my lap and goes to grab her stuff, I can see right through all her lies, but the temptation is killing me. I start making my way towards the door, but before I walk out I turn to look towards her one more time, and she knows just what to do with that addictive smile. Outside I start my walk towards Larry's Gym, the embracing breeze is nice, as the sun sets in the distance and the overlapping darkness starts to comfort my soul.] She said so many things back there and it all made feel so confused. How could she just expect me to forget the years of her blaming me for our daughter's death. All the years I had to live on the streets, because she took away from me everything I earned. But we sure were an unstoppable team... The disappointment from not winning the Hardcore title really has been burning inside of me. It was all because of me though, I'm the one that let Shaman become the main focus, and if I asked Larry he would tell me the same thing. Then again that is what Katrina said he would tell me... Has Larry been lying to me all of this time? Could he be using this excuse of training me as an easy way to make some quick cash at my expensive? He did promise me gold, and instead I'm left with disappointment and defeat. I deserve greatness, Triumph Frost doesn't! I want to win my upcoming match against Spencer and Bhava, I want to see some wins on my record! I was doing fine all by myself against Bishop Steele, and I earned those title shots alone as well. Larry didn't help me do any of that, ever since I met Larry nothing that he has promised has happened. Larry has been using me this entire time... [All these thoughts are flowing inside my head as I lean against the wall waiting for Larry to come outside. Finally he walks outside where he notices me there, with my head down, the wind blowing my hair in my face.] Larry: Whoa! Donny man, you scared me. What are you doing out here so late? Donny: I'm tired of losing Larry... I miss the feeling of having some gold around my waist. You promised the gold would return to me Larry... Larry: Come on man, don't let it get to you. You just need to keep practicing and training and soon no one will be able to stop you. We talked about this Donny, it's not something that will happen over night, it's going to take some time. Donny: I'm really tired of talking Larry, I really am... Larry: What? [Before Larry can get a grasp of the conversation I grab him around the back of his neck and slam his head into the concrete wall. Blood begins dripping down his face as he starts to fall limp from the blow, but it doesn't stop me from doing it one more time. As Larry falls back I catch him under the arms and drag his barely breathing corpse towards the end of the alley behind the gym. Lifting him up I push his body into the opened dumpster.] Donny: She did say you would lie to me Larry. Well I am tired of the lies, and I'm tired of the games. I deserve so much better! [Listening for Larry to say something is pointless, because he is barely conscience from the attack. Looking at my hands I notice some of his blood has seeped onto my gloves, so I begin sliding them off and toss them into the dumpster. I reach into my pockets and pull out some matches and flick one across the box to get the flames going. As the match burns I watch the flame dance for a moment, before I drop it into Larry's final resting place. Noticing the trash inside starting to burn I close it shut as the gargled screams from Larry brings a smile over my face. Smoke feels the air as I turn to begin my walk home.] I'm sorry Larry I guess you just reminded me to much of my father for your own good. This really was the best thing for the both of us; your life had no real purpose and I deserved much more than you could ever give me. Katrina knows exactly what I need, and she knows exactly what I deserve. She wants to see me as a Champion again and she will lead me onto victory. I'm sure the two of us can work out the problems we had in the past, and move on from there. When I get back home should I tell her what I did? Would it make her pleased to know that I took care of my problems? I think for now I should keep this to myself. [Walking into my home I let my trench coat fall to the floor and I make my way up the stairs. Making my way towards my room I cannot wait for some much deserved rest, after such an active evening. Opening the door I notice my room is lit by candle light, and to my surprise I see Katrina. Laying across my bed on her side, with her head propped up on her hand wearing a gorgeous black silk gown. With her finger she motions me to come towards her and a smile shows on my face as I close the door behind me with the sounds of siren's in the distance.] She really did know what was best for me after all...
