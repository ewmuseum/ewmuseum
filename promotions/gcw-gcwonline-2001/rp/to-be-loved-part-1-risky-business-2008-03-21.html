Date: March 21, 2008 ---- Introduction: The Death Of A Renegade ---- I don't like funerals. They just strike me as such a big show, they're almost kind of creepy you know? Everyone dresses in black and comes together to look at a dead guy. I'm always surprised at some of the people who end up in the same room together. Watching Lia Ambrosi and Seymour Almost practically standing next to each other was surreal. There is Steven Caldera in proximity of Jay Terror on the other side of the room. This place is like the twilight zone. I guess I shouldn't really be surprised, that asshole Shaman is practically behind me in line and I've hardly noticed until now. Rich Rollins never really struck me as the type of guy who would want a funeral. He always really just wanted people to leave him alone. Always the loner type, didn't really talk to a lot of people. Yet the kind of respect he garnered in the ring was amazing for a guy who probably didn't even know the name of half the people on the GCW roster. Everyone is here, EVERYONE is here. Approaching the corpse I can almost see him inside. This is crazy! I just saw this guy walking around backstage about a week ago! I watched him wrestle, just like me. What if this were to happen to me? Would all these people still be here? Would anyone even give a damn? Sure the fans would but... would Chris Storm be here? What about Kimbusa? Would Steven Caldera even cared if people came? At least Rich is leaving behind a legacy. A former GTT Champion, his victories outside of GCW can't be denied on top of the impact he's had here. What about me? A GCW United States and World Champion. Outside of GCW do they even know who in the hell I am? Maybe they know me as that "Karina Wolfenden ripoff" that Brandon Youngblood refers to me as. Or that little blonde girl who for some reason couldn't ever beat Youngblood. Perhaps my "fluke win" over Tony Gamble will come to mind for a few. Is that really the legacy I'd leave behind? Some crazy girl who killed herself probably doing something stupid off the top rope. No husband, no kids, no one to carry on my memory. My legacy would be over as soon as it got started. In this business it can happen literally any day now. I have to get out of here, this place is really starting to creep me out. I've never really been into looking at dead guys anyway. ---- Chapter One: A Cold Spring Night ---- 3:40am The neon glow of the alarm clock was the single source of brightness in the otherwise dark room. Outside the snow continued to fall down on Highland Park, Illinois leaving a hint of fog on the two windows. Heat blasted into the room from the two vents, trying desperately to counter to effects of mother nature. Inside the bed Vivica J. Valentine laid perfectly still with a light blue wrapped around her shoulders as she slept. Her usually lively cerulean blue eyes were closed and her blonde hair laid gently against her face, wavering as she breathed in and out. She looked almost divine as she laid there, fast asleep. Sleeping in the silence with a content smirk. Happy, Peaceful, Tranquil... a life as the champion almost brought a calmness to her otherwise hectic life. It was all she'd ever wanted ever since she was a little girl. Living the dream everyday, what more could she ask for? Suddenly her eyes burst open. In a frenzy she shoves off the covers revealing a black tanktop before looking over next to her. No one was there, the bed was empty. It takes a moment for her eyes to adjust and realize that everything that had happened just moments before was nothing more than a dream. Pounding her mattress the once peacful look of the The Fearless Phenom quickly turned into a look of anger and disappointment. "Fuck." She said aloud to herself as she pushed the comforter off of her like it was a drunk college guy at a concert. Standing up she revealed a pair of black panties before walking around the room, obviously distraught. Running her hands through her hair she looked back at the clock contemplating something. Her dream it seemed to real. How could something feel so real and feel so right, and then never happen? Walking over to the nightstand she opens up a small drawer. Pushing and shoving things around finally she pulls out a wrinkled up old Polaroid photo. The photo looked worn and very shown as she walked over to the window and held it under the moonlight. In the reflection of the moon she could see Markus Blaire, her boyfriend who died in the blaze at Lia Ambrosi's Las Vegas Estate last year. Closing her eyes she laid the picture down and clinched the locket around her neck with a deep breath. It seemed like an eternity since Markus had died. So much had happened, he'd be so proud of her. Going from wasted talent to comeback of the year in 2007 was something he would've loved to see happen. It wasn't meant to be though. Someone had other plans. Without him through she never really felt as happy. Deep down inside there was always this feeling of emptiness. A feeling that something was missing from her life. It was a void that she thought finally achieving her dreams would fill. The void still lived on though. Manifesting inside of her now with the death of Rich Rollins. Thoughts of dying alone began to rise to the forefront of The Fearless Phenom's psyche. Leaning her head against the cold glass she began to think about what his kiss felt like. To feel his touch again, run her hands through his hair. Feel his heart beating along with hers. She missed him so much. A single tear began to run down her cheek as fog from her breath began to expand on the cold window. KNOCK KNOCK! "Miss Valentine?" A voice could be heard from the other side of her door. It was Patrick her housekeeper. Quickly Vivica wiped the tear away and crawled back into bed, trying not to be so suspicious, treating Patrick almost like he was her dad. "Yeah?" Vivica answered. Slowly Patrick pushed the door open slowly and peeked inside, seeing Vivica sitting up in her bed wrapped in covers. Sliding into the room Patrick walked over towards Vivica's bed. As she glanced over at him he could see the look in her beautiful blue eyes. Something was wrong, but she was never the type to openly share what was bothering her. Always feeling the need to put on a facade of being invincible. "Is everything alright Miss Valentine?" He questioned, figuring to give it a try anyway. "Yeah... I'm fine." Vivica said with a deep breath. "Try to get some sleep Viv." He said, "You have to get back on a plane tomorrow to get back on tour with GCW. I bet having the belt with you will make everything better." "I sure hope so." Vivica said under her breath feeling the cold slap of loneliness across her face. Patrick awkwardly took a few steps backwards, hoping for Vivica to confide in him before giving up and making his way out of the room. With a click the door slowly closed behind him. "If the title was supposed to make everything better... how come I can't sleep at night anymore?" ---- Chapter Two: Paralyzer ---- Taking a step inside was almost like an adventure to another world. Her cerulean blue eyes navigated around the club, looking for a place to settle down. The music was so loud that her heart was beating along with the bass of the song. Nervously she bit her lip as she slowly took her first step over towards the bar. She'd been sixteen years old the last time she went to a place like this. It was completely out of her element and it showed as she slyly met the bartender with a smile as she sat down. Blue, violet, and red lasers danced along the walls while she nervous twiddled her fingers. Tapping them on the bar, she kept looking around her... almost half expecting someone to come and hit her from behind with a steel chair. You never know where those crazies will come from. "Welcome!" the bartender shouted to The Fearless Phenom, who was only half paying attention at the time. A bit startled she turned around and greeted him with a half-smile. Her blonde hair with one red streak laid over her face as looked up at him. "What can I get for the lovely lady?" "I'll just have a diet soda..." She spoke loudly, "I'm not a drinker!" "One Diet Coke for the pretty lady!" He said as he pulled out a glass and a drink maker. As he squirted coke down into the glass a young man came and sat down next to The Fearless Phenom, making her shy away just a little bit. Pulling his ID out of his wallet he watched as the bartender slides a glass over to Vivica. "That'll be a dollar fifty miss!" The bartender spoke. Valentine reached around for her wallet before... "I'll pay for it." The man said with a smile sliding a buck-fifty across the table. "You didn't have to do that... I have plenty of..." Vivica began to speak. "No, no it's okay." The man said with a smile. He had emerald green eyes that almost had a glow under the lights of the club. His dark brown hair was shorter, but spiked up in the front. He had a lean but muscular build, wearing a tight fitting black shirt and a cross necklace around his neck. "My name is Mark, and yours is?" "Vivica." She said, almost afraid to respond because he might know who she is. God she felt so embarrassed being in a place like this. "It's very nice to meet you Vivica, you're a very pretty lady..." Mark smiled, "Girls like you don't usually end up in a dive like this." "Well..." She nervously smiled again, "I don't usually go to places like this. I don't even know why I'm here right now... this is all a little much for me! You know? The lights and the music, I'm not really that much into dancing!" Mark began to laugh realizing that it was very possible that Vivica had NEVER been to a place like this before. "I've never been here myself..." Mark answered, "I just tagged along with some friends and they ditched me for some slutty girls they met on the other side of the club. This place, this place isn't really for me. I can't even think in here!" "Then why are you here?" Vivica responded, lightening up to the mood a little bit. "I don't know!" He laughed not really having a great answer, "I'd leave but now I know you're here and I'd feel like an asshole leaving you here alone. Why don't we go somewhere more quiet? We can actually hear each other talk. I think that would be nice." "Well..." Vivica hesitated. Deep down inside she realized that it wasn't fair that she wasn't even giving this guy a chance. He was cute, and he seemed like he had a good head on his shoulders plus he doesn't even know who she is. He's obviously not a wrestling fan. Markus was gone, and he'll never come back. She needed to move on with her life... maybe give someone else a chance to fill that void that kept her up at night. "Sure." she responded with a smirk, "That sounds like fun." "Thank God!" he said getting up from the bar and throwing a jacket over his shoulders, "Let's get the hell out of here." ---- Chapter Three: Last Chance To Lose Control ---- After leaving the club the two made their way to a nice little coffee shop in downtown Chicago. They spent all night talking about music, hopes, and dreams. Childhood stories of growing up going to rival high schools and homecoming dances. Fights with parents and shattered expectations. On the way out of the coffee shop Vivica began to feel something deep down inside, something that she felt had been missing for a very long time. The feeling of being loved. It was something that she only felt the days she stepped foot inside of a wrestling ring, in front of all the fans. The sensation was often overwhelming, and the rest of the night she never really stopped smiling. Since Mark was brought by his friends he traveled in the passenger seat of Vivica's black Chrysler Crossfire, spending most of the car ride oblivious to the scenery around him and staring into her beautiful eyes while she tried to focus on the road. Learning that he was just in town for a week visiting his family, Vivica offered to drop Mark off at his hotel room. It was a Holiday Inn on the east side of town by the lake. As she pulled into the parking lot she could feel the butterflies whirling around in her stomach. The feeling slowly rose from the pit of her stomach to her throat as she shifted the car into park and took a gulp to try and loosen the feeling. It helped, but that was only temporary. "I had a great time tonight." Mark said, his voice only accompanied by the stir of the motor, "I wish I could talk to you some more. I really have nothing better to do tonight." "I was supposed to catch a plane tonight myself," Vivica responded with a chuckle, "I suppose as long as I make it to the show I'll be okay." "Show?" Mark asked, "What kind of show like a concert or something?" "Yeah something like that," she responded with a smirk, "Well goodnight." "Why don't you come up with me?" Mark asked abruptly, "Nothing you know... like that. I'm not expecting anything just you know... just to talk to you a little bit longer." "Uhhh..." there it was again, the lump in her throat rose right back up to the occasion. She didn't know how she should answer, but she probably couldn't make a noise even if she wanted to. Staring blankly back at Mark he started to laugh. "Come on, let's go." Mark said, "Look at me, I'm harmless. I just don't want to look back at tonight and be thinking about missing an opportunity to really get to know you better." "Okay." Vivica finally spoke, "I think I'd like that." Leaving the car they slammed the door shut as tiny flakes of snow continued to fall gracefully from the sky. They landed in Vivica's hair as she walked towards the Holiday Inn with Mark, holding his arm around her to keep her warm as they braved the weird spring snow showers. Mark pulled the door open for them as they walked inside, awkwardly in silence as they made their way towards the elevator up to his room. Once the elevator opened Mark grabbed Vivica by the hand and slowly escorted him to their room. She could feel her stomach turning and her hands begin to shake a little as they approached. Pausing at the door Mark turned around and greeted her with a smile as he slide the key lock and pushed the door open. Inside the room was dimly lit, with one lamp on a nightstand emitting light for the entire room. "Sorry this isn't the nicest place ever..." Mark said under his breath as he closed the door behind Vivica. "It's okay," Vivica said nervously before sitting on the bed, "Do you have a remote for the television?" "I haven't really looked for it really, I haven't been here much." Mark said before sitting on the bed next to Vivica, "Remember?" "Right you just got in last night and checked in." Vivica remembered, "You said that you were worried that your family checked you into the Bates Motel." Mark and Vivica share a laugh as she makes a stabbing motion. As she does Mark grabs her hand and she looks up at him a bit surprised. He stares into her eyes, gripping her hand. "It's been a long time since I've felt the way about someone that I feel about you." Mark talks slowly, "You sure are a special girl Vivica. I think I've fallen for you and I don't even know your last name." "Valentine." She says with a bit of a smirk. "Like the wrestler? My little sister loves that stuff." Vivica feels a little awkward as Mark puts two and two together, "You ARE the wrestler?" "I could be." She smiled in response. "Well I didn't know I had a celebrity on my hands. Maybe I should ask for an autograph. I never expected a kick ass girl like THE Vivica J. Valentine to be so..." "Normal?" Vivica responded. "Not normal," Mark retorted, "Amazing." Slowly he moves forward and kisses Vivica on the lips. Reluctantly at first Vivica doesn't respond. Pulling away he looks into her brilliant cerulean blue eyes again before gently kissing her on the lips. The second time Vivica succumbs to temptation and wraps her arms around Mark kissing him back. Now she was really shaking. What in the hell was she doing? She wasn't some horny high school girl... she was a professional. A champion. A role model for little girls. She had to be better than this. No matter how scared or lonely she was. How could everything get so out of control so quickly? "I need to get away from all of this..." Vivica said to herself out loud as the elevator doors closed, "I just need to get away." ---- Chapter Four: Spring Break ---- * Rumor has it that Vivica J. Valentine might not be as much of a good girl as GCW press may want to lead their audience to believe. A chance to be in the ring with a couple people who she always wanted to test her skills against and get in front of those fans? She couldn't ask for anything more. With a carry-on messenger bag over her shoulder she made her way through the Mexican airport crowds over towards luggage claim. Impatiently she tapped her foot, waiting for her two black rolling suitcases to make their way to her. Closing her eyes and taking a deep breath she tried not to think about the wrestling article, even though it was eating away at her. What a fucking liar. That creep would've stuck it in anything with legs, and she was such a fool to fall for his charm. If only Markus was still around... she wouldn't have to deal with assholes like that. Clinching her locket around her neck she slowed her breathing trying to calm down and not rip the nearest frequent flyer in two. She could almost hear his voice, and it brought a calming sensation. Over her. Just calm down Vivica, calm down. It'll all be okay Vivica, this stuff always passes. "Vivica." Slowly opening her eyes she thought to herself for a minute. Was that one... "Viv?" Her heart skipped a beat and her eyes grew really wide. The sound made her jump so hard she almost ripped the locket off from around her neck. Quickly spinning around The Fearless Phenom looked almost like she saw a ghost. Standing in front of her was the thing of her dreams. It was Markus Blaire, wearing a pair of baggy khaki cargos, black sunglasses, a black ELEMENT T-Shirt... ...and a copy of The Grappling Gossip in his right hand.
