Rebirth (PT.2)

28 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/28/08 Location: Riverside California Time: 11:00 pm Date: 6/15/08 ? Fathers Day A neighborhood is usually a community of people that live in peace and harmony in there perfect little lives. The whole block is aligned with houses, abandoned houses...but for what reason? Is it the recession that this country is facing that all the houses on this block now resemble a graveyard? No, in-fact the reason is much darker then any government crisis this country may be facing. Ghostly images of Dads long ago are seen outside of there houses sipping there coffee with there random color bathrobe they just got for Fathers day. Home is a place where the heart is right? Not if your heart is as black as the night sky, the stars up above twinkle with a soft glow shining on the half dead vegetation on the ground inside the neighborhood. This block has many empty shells of houses that hold many stories, but one at the end of the block has the worst. The half destroyed empty shell of a house still had a lot of evil energy from the previous battle John Adams had inside so long ago against his father. The ghost of the people who died that day still screamed to Adams as his foot took its first step on the unholy ground. Thoughts of the old prophetic man still were coursing through Adams mind. Hell's forces were calling to Adams still after a year and Adams had to deal with it. The stone brick that once was a completed wall housing the structure of the famous Adams mansion is now mere stone pillars that served as markers of the area the house once stood. Companies have tried to come in since the devastation to demolish the rest of the rubble, but there attempts were all but successful since the rest of the structure could not be destroyed as if it is stuck in there by some unnatural force. Trees on the plantation were now but skeletal figures of there former beauty. The ground is quite fresh with earths smallest scavengers like worms, maggots and bigger like roaches. As Adams moved in closer an unnatural cloud formation started to form over the once proud house. The dark with a slight gray tint clouds is swirling in an oval type shape right over the shell of a house. Electrical energies started to crackle from the swirling vortex. The wind started to pick up as Adams long black hair blew to the front of his face. The sound of thunder filled the corrupted night air with each step Adams took closer to what seemed to be his destiny. Dead vegetation is flying through the air being carried by the wind, it is clear to Adams that the spirit that lived inside this house is alive and well...and it is aware of Adams presence. Adams went to where the back yard is located, ghostly images of him as a boy is still fresh inside his mind. Every part of this house held a part of Adams past, the thoughts of his mothers death is playing over and over in his head...he could still smell the gun powder from the hit men's guns filled his nose and the sounds of the gun shot still rang inside of his head even louder then the mighty thunder that filled the night sky with it's echoing roar. His thoughts changed to a more recent event that happened last Fathers day when his father Frank Adams breathed his last breath yet again. Adams could still see the glimmer from the knife Frank used when he slit what he found out to be his daughters throat. The bright rosy red blood that dripped from her opened wound still is coursing through Adams head as if it is happening all over again. "I do this for you son, my one true son Devilin!" Franks last words, before he fell to his death combining with the outside thunder still pierced Adams ear. He went to the location of what was the garage; each step he took is difficult for he had to push aside with his feet the white adobe brick rubble that once formed the structure of the house. Each piece of rubble sheltered earth worms and maggots that had dubbed this as there home. Rain started it's downfall as if the heavens up above were crying at what Adams had to do. Each drop of water that dropped onto Adams turned into a red silky color of blood as it slowly traveled off of Adams massive body. As he entered the once garage he saw a peculiar stone tablet behind a half rotted out wall at the left side of the old garage structure. Adams put out his hand to grab the tablet, the thick dust that settled on the tablet got disturbed and floated all over the air by Adams nose causing it to twitch and contort. "ACHOOO!" Adams had to sneeze, a series of centipedes and worms that lived in the back of the tablet started to crawl over Adams massive hands in an attempt to find who had disturbed there once peaceful home. Adams grabbed the tablet and pulled it out of its resting place...silky thin silver spider webs also connected to the back of the tablet then released it, the spiders from the web scattered at the floor right next to Adams feet. Adams dusted the rest of the dust off as he tried to get more of a clear picture of what the tablet had in stored for him. The language inscribed in the tablet is in Latin, but luckily for Adams he knew Latin. "For whoever hold's this tablet and yet recites these words of Evil, shall open the door way to the twelfth level of hell to find the power of darkness they shall seek." That's what the words on the tablet roughly translated into...Adams spoke the words out loud but as he did the environment around him started to shake violently as Adams did everything to keep his balance. Loud moans started to fill the garage area as Adams nose that once was filled with dust of old, now is filled with a putrid smell of sulfur. The earth beneath him started to crack revealing deep lines in the ground, sulfuric smoke started to pour out of it filling the air. Adams eyes started to fill up with teary salt water as John's eyes tried everything to adjust. Bony structure of a hand escaped from the grave of the earth, rotten flesh that used to be the form of once is started to hang off the skeletal hand. The mighty bone demonic hand pushed itself and the rest of the body that the hand connects to. The head of the skeleton demon is half exposed to reveal a fleshy half decomposed brain with maggots crawling in and out of the brain eating away the outer fleshy exterior of thought. The eyes of the demon are hanging out of the sockets half way down by the nose cavity of the head. Green worms aligned with maggots are seen crawling out of the nose and up the fleshy part that connected to the eye of the demon creature. The half eaten internal organs of the demon started to pour out of the open crevasse and hit the ground looking like cracked open rotten eggs. "Surprised to see me son?" The demon revealed himself to be John Adams father Frank Adams who died last year. No matter what life brings Adams there is no escaping the terror that is Frank. "I have to go, I have to save him." John said still trying to get the smoke and sulfur out of his senses. "Stop your sniveling you little bitch! You think the dark lord even wants to see you? You're a pathetic little wretch that's not even worth his time." Frank spouted back to Adams revealing his old yellow and green teeth that were blackened with rot by the gum line. "I must find Jack!!" "Oh you will find him John, and a lot more...you see because you spotted those words on the tablet now I'm required to take your dumb ass to the depths of hell...so believe me you will get your chance!" In that moment in time that seemed to stand still for what seemed to be an eternity, they stared at each other. Frank held out his right arm murmuring Latin of old conjuring some dark arts... "AHHHH!!" Adams screamed out in pain that even the thunder that is still going strong outside of the garage complex could not even mask. His soul is in the process of being ripped out of his fleshy vessel by his father. "Isn't this what you want son? Don't you want to go to hell you pathetic excuse of life..." Everything around Adams started to get dark as Adams head felt lighter then air...he felt sick as he turned around to see his body on the ground. "My God..." Adams says in amazement as he realizes his soul has left his body. As the environment around Adams goes completely dark his last thoughts are that of Jack Jefferys and why he must do what he must do so he keeps his faith in God almighty close to his heart and his prayers as his weapons of choice as he embarks his unholy trip to find the one thing in this world that is hard to come by....his friend. Flames of insecurity, hatred and torture in the place of eternal damnation, the smell of sulfur is heavy in the atmosphere of this hellish domain as Adams opens his eyes as if for the first time. Gigantic devilish creatures can be seen on these unholy grounds with familiar demonic whips of a greenish flame tint that danced devilishly around...they use the torture weapons to punish a crowd of souls headed to their final resting place due to their past sins. Adams finds himself amongst a crowd of souls in a humongous group that even tops the mightiest protest rallies in the world of the living...the destination they all are being led to like sheep is a familiar dark obsidian castle that is located in the center of this realm. Tiny black demons with small bat like wings are seen running and nipping at the feet of the condemned souls that are straying from the pact. A skeletal, demonic bat like creature is seen flying through the sulfuric acidic air. The creature is also seen breathing fire every where like a mythical dragon. Mesmerizing and hypnotic sounds of drums can be heard as the souls march on to the castle, being wiped from the back by the flame induced whips of torture. A symphony of pain and agony is mixing in the air with the sulfur combined also with the demonic beats of the drum. As the souls get closer to the castle we find that the structure is actually made not of a obsidian stone but out of blackened burnt bodies...due to a closer inspection reveals that the bodies, even though mangled and there flesh burnt clean off there body, they still show signs of life. The blood floods like a river down the steps of flesh and filled a moat of blood...that surrounded the twisted castle. A Bridge of sorts connects one end of the moat to the castle end...the bridge is made of many fused bones penetrating the flesh of souls who were misled a long time ago. The blood that spilled from their bodies helped also to fill the moat. Adams being here once before knew exactly where to step as he kept up with the heard of the condemned. Electricity in the form of lightning dances across the red and blackened skies of hell, pointing to their destination point witch is the tallest tower amongst the eight in the castle. The thunder that shortly followed after is unified with the mighty roars of the demonic sheep herders leading the souls to their final destination. Inside of the tower it is very dark and musky, the stench of death fills the room...a loud moaning sound can be heard slightly off to the right of this room. A dim light can be seen illuminating from a candle on a dusty wooden table. A picture of tormented souls burning in a lake of fire can be seen on the wall across the table but closer look reveals it to be really a window of souls being tortured. A head is seen decapitated next to the candle. The blood mixes in with the melting wax as they drip to the floor forming three sixes on the ground before traveling away. Deeper into the room a door is seen, very archaic in design with a skull engraved in the middle of the design. The jaw of the skull slightly opens ajar letting out a scream of torturing souls as the door opens outward. The next room is quite large in its grand design with a pit in the middle of the room. Flames generated from the evil of hell itself rage out of it like a furnace. Also in the room is a throne, made of gothic style metal...a obsidian black with real human skulls engraved all through it. There were words described into the throne... "He who sits upon the throne of hellish torture shall rule all of Hell itself!" The souls are led to this room to wait for the dark lord himself, Adams brother Devilin Rubis. Adams is not prepared to wait for his brother...when one of the demonic guards attention is averted Adams decided to make his break for it. He traveled in search of a long hallway fifty yards to the left hand side. Along the sides of the walls were actual human faces of beings that were once also in the land of the living. "GO BACK!" They sang in unity, but Adams came so far already and is not about to go back no matter what. He entered into the hallway with his mighty weapon... "The Lords Prayer." "PATER noster, qui es in c’lis; sanctificatur nomen tuum: Adventiat regnum tuum; fiat voluntas tua, sicut in c’lo, et in terra. Panem nostrum quotidianum da nobis hodie: Et dimitte nobis debita nostra, sicut et nos dimittimus debitoribus nostris: et ne nos inducas in tentationem: sed libera nos a malo. Quia tuum est regnum, et potentia, et Gloria, in secula. Amen." He said the prayer in Latin before he then proceeded to say it in English: "Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name. Your Kingdom come, your will be done, on earth as in heaven Give us today our daily bread. Forgive us our sins, as we forgive those who sin against us. Lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. For the kingdom, the power and the glory are yours. Now and for ever. Amen" A great light started to pour away from Adams like the suns own rays causing the faces of lost souls in the walls to close there eyes and then rest there eternal rest of damnation. After he gets out of the hallway he then proceeds to a large room filled to the brim of rotten death and decay. In the center of the room is a fiery pit of hell, the flames danced a hypnotic dance of seduction to try and seduce the good that is in Adams. Adams is shocked and thinks his eyes are deceiving him, inside the flames he sees the body of Jack Jefferys other wise known as Ace of Spades. Could this be the hellish containment of his brothers captured souls? Either way it is a risk he had to take... "The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want. He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters. He restoreth my soul: he leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for his name's sake. Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff they comfort me. Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies: thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over. Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life: and I will dwell in the house of the Lord for ever." He said Psalm Twenty Three as he entered the flames of hell to save his friend, to save a man that never deserved to die...to save Ace. (To Be continued...)
