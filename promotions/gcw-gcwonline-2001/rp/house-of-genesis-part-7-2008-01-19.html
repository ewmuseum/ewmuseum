House of Genesis: Part 7

19 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 19th, 2008 Every that could go for his career went the right way. His personal life proved to be a different matter. No matter what he seemed to do, he could not please Tessa Windsor. He had tried; no one could deny him that. He had tried everything he could think of. That is why when it came time to head toward the gym, he dropped his head down silently and made his way without saying so much as a word to anyone. Once he finally got to the gym, he could again lift his head up proudly. He headed to the back of the gym where a ring was set up inside a large and mostly empty room. A usual sight was there to greet him; two men waiting for him, one in the ring and the other out the ring. Drake nodded to Darson, who was leaning against the wall. He dropped his bag and started to lace up his boots. In the ring, Cleveland continued to practice his foot work, making sure to keep his eyes on Storm. Within minutes Scott has his wrestling boots on and rolls into the ring. He tossed his shirt aside and rolled the shoulders to get loose for a minute. Paige just waited as he always did, leaning against the turnbuckle with his arms crossed. Feeling he is ready, Scott signals for Cleveland to approach him. He does, making sure his pads were in place. Scott circled him for a moment, getting a feel for Paige’s movements. Cleveland had to be sweating. Scott had did this every time they trained and each time he took him down with little resistance. There was that gun-slinger silence in the gym, where no one made a sound and time seemed to stand still. Then, drawing his gun, Scott shot in on him for a Double Leg. They probably should have had a celebration. Maybe Darson had cake in the back, because in a matter of only the smallest of moments, Drake had once again taken Paige down near effortlessly for the fifth time. Scott only needed to take him down two more times and he could leave and actually get on with his own training. The eyes of Jerrod Darson bore into the back of his head, Scott could feel it. He could still hear the words of Darson, still fresh from yesterday when he overheard him berating Paige. Scott Drake wanted to forget about them, those words didn’t concern him. But regardless of what he did, they kept creeping back in the more he felt those icy cold eyes staring into him. "Come on? kid" he muttered under his breath as he backed away to allow Cleveland Paige time to get back to his feet. Paige stayed on his guard, even as he got back up, for he had knew that Scott had no problem taking him down before he could even regain his bearings. Even though Scott never budged an inch, Cleveland kept his eyes on him the entire time. Once he was back on his feet, he moved away from Scott to try and give himself some time to regroup for the next attack. Scott doesn’t come at him. Paige didn’t flinch though. This would just be another one of his tricks. He would wait until Paige let down his guard, then he would attack. But Cleveland was going to be ready for that. Scott waited to see when Paige would be ready. He didn’t want to say he was giving him an advantage, because he was going to go all out at him, but he partly wanted to give Paige his best chance. He waited until the perfect moment. And he struck. Make that fifty-one straight takedowns. Scott wanted to punch the canvas, but resisted the urges. He gathered himself up and got back up. He was just going to take him down before Paige could get back standing. His eyes darted over toward Darson, checking to see what his expressions were. Soon as he turned away, Scott put his attention back onto his student for the third take down. Number fifty-three? Not so fast. Scott Drake shot in, looking for that Double Leg he often used, but his head didn’t turn around fast enough and Cleveland Paige was able to get his hands in place and hook them in just the right spots. With a simple flip of the wrist, he flipped Scott. Even Cleveland looked surprised by it. He just stood there, staring down at Scott, wondering if it all actually happened. Scott, laying flat on his back, was proof that it had. The eerie silence again filled the room when no one had any idea of what to say. Finally, Cleveland extended his arm down for Scott and helped him back up to his feet. Back on his feet, Scott patted him on the shoulder and gave him a smile, "you did good." His smile turned to a devilish grin, "and now we can start with the real training. Meet me back here tomorrow. At 4:30 A.M. sharp." He rolled out of the ring, grabbing his stuff as Cleveland smiled proudly. "We’ll start with an hour of warm ups and then move on to two hours of practice, followed by another two hours of technique clinics." Cleveland dropped into the turnbuckle. Five hours? Each day? Just a few minutes with the man made his entire body ache. He almost had to wonder what he got him into. But even with the idea of torture training, Paige couldn’t wipe the smile off his face. This was one more step closer toward his dream. To be the greatest professional wrestler ever. He would be the greatest. Cleveland Paige, remember the name, you’ll hear it when they introduce the winner. "Yeah" he said, "that sounds cool".
