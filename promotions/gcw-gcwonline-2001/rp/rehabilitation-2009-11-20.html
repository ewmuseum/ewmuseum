Rehabilitation

20 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20 November 2009 I. He doesn't wake up where he figures he would. He's engulfed in darkness, unable to even see a few feet in front of him. When he went to bed the night before he remembers things being quite different. His mind thinks back to that time, no more than 7 hours ago probably, when he was wrapped inside of his comforter. Such a warm blanket. He thinks about the silk sheets and how, and he'd never admit this openly, he loves the way they feel on his skin . Especially after a night of love making with Mary. Now he thinks about Mary. Her soft, smooth skin, her perky breasts and perfect ass. He thinks about the way the hair on the back of his neck stands up when she whispers into his ears when they are making love, asking, begging, and pleading for more. Yes, 7 hours ago things were going swimmingly indeed. But . Instead of a mattress that your body can sink into, he now rests his body on something made of wood, he can also feel dirt grinding into his back, replacing those silk sheets. A blanket? He doesn't find one here, not at all. He reaches his hands up and it only takes one quick movement for his hands to collide with more wood. He kicks his feet and the same happens. His breathing becomes more erratic now, sweat forms almost instantly along his brow. Panic sets in. He thrashes wildly, howling out a mixture of cries for help and terrible obscenities as he does. Of course this does nothing useful. The only thing accomplished is getting dirt in his mouth and eyes and causing his knuckles to bleed from the splinters that are now imbedded into his hands. It doesn't take long for him to cease his thrashing, although his body continues to quiver. Reality sets in and reality can be a cold bitch. And today's reality? Roberto Abitua is in for one fucked up day. II. FIVE months ago … A battle within one of the greatest wars the world has ever known raged. It was, in fact, the FINAL battle that was supposed to settle the great conflict once and for all. It was Good vs. Evil, Heaven vs. Hell . if you can dream up an analogy similar then that is what you'd label it as. Fire burned bright and scorched the earth, lighting up the area for all to see. The warriors were incredible, fast, strong and unwilling to give in to the other. It would take nothing short of total destruction to be declared the victor. Each man looked inside themselves and had to ask the question, 'are you willing to face death itself? They were both up for the challenge. Still, only one could be victorious. There were no draws or ties when it came to absolute glory. One way or another, someone was probably not going to walk off this battlefield under his own power. In his mind, LeStatt Knight never figured he'd fall to his nemesis. Sure, Andy Murray had put up a fight but this was HIS game, a war zone full of bloodshed, chaos and yes, hell's fire. As things came closer to the climax, Knight could feel and taste victory. He smiled inwardly at the prospect of besting Murray for the third straight time. No matter that mere mortal men wouldn't have been capable of even walking after all of the punishment Knight's body took that night, LeStatt was standing tall . his icy blue eyes gazed into the pit of fire that he had brought with him. He was moments away from his destiny. The funny thing about destiny however, is that you can never be sure what yours is. A person can claim what they wish but REAL destiny cannot be predicted and it cannot be altered. The next few moments were nothing more than a blur, small snapshots of what really transpired. He stared into the fire. He attempted to throw Murray into hell. The World spun. Pain. That mandatory ten count. His eyes opening to stare at Murray at the last moment. It was this moment that he could finally understand Andy Murray and he also got the feeling that maybe, just maybe . Murray finally understood him as well. It was a sobering moment for LeStatt Knight, coming face to face with his true destiny . with his own mortality. His world was upside down and turned inside out. He knew he wouldn't be the same, he couldn't be. He hid it from the world that night but as he heard that final count and Murray's victory music exploded throughout the arena . A single tear slid down his cheek. III. He can't believe it and he refuses to accept it. No way does he die like this, buried in a fucking box, clawing away for his life. Somewhere inside of him, the answer was there just waiting to be ripped out. He also knows that time is precious and very much his enemy. Soon he'd lose his ability to breathe which would lead to even more fear and panic which leads to death. And death isn't something he is very interested in at the moment. So he shuts his eyes to think, a ridiculous gesture because of how dark it is but it helps him calm himself and get back into something resembling a decent state of mind. He focuses on his breathing. In and out, long heavy breaths. He places a hand over his chest to feel the rise and fall, this helps. This continues for a few minutes before his eyes open again. The blackness greets him with a wicked smile and even in his predicament there's this dark voice in the back of his mind that yells back something simple: FUCK YOU. Roberto smiles at the soft voice. Now he's ready to do what needs to be done to escape. First he places his hands on the wooden lid to his prison and pushes with all of the strength that he can muster. The wood groans under the pressure but it hardly does anything you could consider movement. "Fuck. Fuck. Fuck. Fuck!" Not the most productive phrases in the English language but Roberto could come up with anything more fitting. He closes his eyes again to think. But there isn't much to think about. If he is going to survive then it'll be the same way he has defeated every challenge he has faced in his adult life … with brute fucking force. Short, precise strikes begin landing on the box's wooden seal causing the dirt lining the box to jump around and find new places to position itself. Anger trickles in, anger directed towards the person who could have locked him away and hidden him like trash. He's angry that he could possibly never step foot inside of a GCW ring again. His anger builds when he thinks about Andy Murray and Rampage. He can feel his face getting warmer as he considers the possibility of Vivica fucking Valentine taking his mantle as greatest GCW superstar. But then he gets pissed off at himself. Why is GCW the first thing on his mind as his potential final moments creep up on him? Shouldn't it be his family? Sweet, sweet Mary and precious Joaquin? Maybe Starr and Cindy? Damien? David? Anyone who has truly cared about him throughout his life? As the anger builds, so does the force of his blows and he can feel the warmth of fresh blood dripping down his fists towards his wrists, pooling up there. He imagines the blood and what it must look like and again his mind wanders. Blood, like the kind that he's felt and tasted from his very body. The kind he has brought out of others. Then there's that voice again, once burrowed deep in the back of mind but now snaking its way up to the front. It tells him to hit harder, to escape, to get the fuck out of here and get the fuck back to doing what he is born to fucking do. Roberto obliges over and over again. This wouldn't be the end. It couldn't! It shouldn't! And with one last blasting effort, the seal breaks and the lid snaps. Dirt comes crashing down on his face. IV. 6 weeks ago… The world continued to spin, even months after that Highland Hangover that Roberto took at the hands of Andy Murray. No, he didn't have a concussion or any type of head injury for that matter. On a daily basis, Roberto's world spun because he was drunk out of his mind. He had never felt like he did when he lost the title to Andy Murray, not even when he lost it in a match to two 120 lbs. females. Against Murray, he gave everything he had and he was so close to winning that it was devastating once it was all over. Depression sank in, something he never figured that he'd be ill-equipped to deal, factoring in everything he has seen in his life. But this was different, this was something MORE. It wasn't something that could be fixed with just an apology or a thank you. He couldn't even load himself up at work to get away from his problems . that'd just remind him of his failures. So he drank. He sat at the edge of his bed, drink in hand, staring out at the setting sun. Mary had taken their son out for some ice cream, leaving Roberto alone for a few hours. He liked it better this way. Although he replicated happiness and affection towards Mary and Joaquin when they were around, his acting wore on him. It became a tiresome task, one that would eventually cause him to lose his mind if he couldn't right the ship that was his life. He took a gulp, closing his eyes to let the burn of the straight liquor travel down his throat and settle into his stomach. He was confused, agitated and worried about what was next for him. Could he continue to mask his true feelings AND stay sane? Would Mary ever see the mask slipping and decide that she was better off without him? Could he resurrect his now fading career? All good questions: All impossible to answer. He takes another drink, unable to get any real answers from the bottom of the glass either. He frowns. So the world continued to spin and his questions remained unanswered. Frustrated, he tossed the glass against the wall and watched as it shattered into a number of individual pieces. He stares at the pieces for longer than he should have before turning his glare back up to the window, catching the last sliver of the son before it completed its descent behind the horizon. Roberto lets out a weak laugh. 'Broken ' he sighed. 'Setting' The best ways to describe his career. His life. He closes his eyes again and allows himself to fall backwards into his bed before drifting to sleep. V. The thought came suddenly, frightening Roberto as he continued to dig and fight his way from his muddy grave. It’s not as if he hadn’t thought about it before, just he wasn’t in any situation where his actions could directly lead to the desired results. Would the world be better if he just gave in? If he closed his eyes and drifted back to sleep, letting his oxygen run out and letting the weight of all the earth just flatten him … would anyone care? “You could be legendary. A martyr perhaps.” There is that voice again, nagging at him. It started as a whisper but was getting louder every time he heard it. But he can’t give in, its not in his nature. Quitting wasn’t something that he took lightly. It may sound cliché but to Roberto, once you start quitting in life, its hard to break such a habit. Roberto manages to shake the notion so he continues to fight and dig, to attempt to reach the surface where freedom could be had. He has no idea if he’ll make it up to the surface again because he has no real idea how far down he really is. Then dirt fills his mouth, making it impossible to breath. Panic begins to drive its way into his mind again, taking over in just a matter of seconds. Although his thoughts are broken and unclear, he still has the basic of survival skills, the same as everyone else. He attempts to muscle his body upwards … all the strength in his body being exhausted. Then he sees a shimmer of light glimmer down from a split in the ground above. His heart begins to beat faster as hope takes over for fear. He claws and claws above, releasing more and more sunlight. And suddenly? Something or someone grasps his hand! The force of the pull catches Roberto off guard and almost makes him fight it off. The alternative however made him think twice and he just goes for the ride. Like a rocket his body shoots from the dirt and comes barreling into the sunlight. Roberto lands face first on the surface, his hands breaking his fall enough that he doesn’t loose his front teeth from the impact. He’s turned over, forced to look up at his savior. It takes a few moments but once his eyes refocus and his breathing becomes less erratic, things become much more clear. “Glad to have you back.” Roberto says nothing, mostly because he just can’t and some because he has no idea what to say. LeStatt Knight on the other hand grins a wide grin and takes a knee by Roberto’s head. Knight places a hand over his more refined halves’ forehead. “You’ve been gone a long time.” He nods. “I was beginning to think that you had decided to stay down there.” “Wh-Why?” It was a simple question and good thing it was, Roberto couldn’t have asked anything else. LeStatt’s black eyes seem to burn red from the light of the sun and they stare at Roberto in something almost resembling empathy. “I had to test you,” He chuckles. “I had to test you, FOR you.” LeStatt takes his black eyes away from Roberto now and looks up at the yellow sun. “It’s a new day Roberto. As long as I’m around you’ll always have a purpose, a reason to continue doing the things that you do. You cannot hide anymore, it’s time to come back to the world … even if they THINK they are better off now than with you.” Roberto doesn’t respond. Knight turns back to face him. “It’s time for us to go back home.” Now Roberto smiles. “Home.” And Roberto closes his eyes again, realizing that the darkness is where he feels the most safe. VI. 3 weeks ago … Roberto shot up in bed. He’d been having his buried alive nightmare for almost two months straight. Just like every other night he woke up sweating profusely and waking Mary up with his sudden jerk up from his laying position. She sat up with him and put an arm over his shoulder. “You okay baby?” He didn’t respond. He knew he wasn’t okay, at least not yet. He had something that needed to be done. Something that he wanted to do but had just been too afraid and too embarrassed to do. “I don’t know.” She kissed his shoulder and then his cheek. “Need me to stay up with you?” He shook his head. “No, actually I won’t be up long. I just have something I need to do quickly. I’ll be right back.” Roberto gets up out of bed and heads for the door, grabbing his cell phone from the dresser as he leaves. The entire walk, all 10 feet of it, down the hall and to the bathroom, he stared at the phone and in particular, a certain number that he had highlighted already. He takes in a deep breath … and presses SEND. After one ring, he almost hangs up as nerves almost get the best of him. After two rings he feels a knot form in his throat. After three rings he prepares himself for the voice at the other end After four rings he just gets annoyed. After five rings he is about to hang up! … But he gets an answer. “This better be fucking good, I’ve got a big meeting in the morning.” Roberto hesitates only for a moment. “It’s me jefe.” A pause from the other line. “Knight. What is it?” “I’m back.” Before another word can be said Roberto hangs up his phone, staring at it for but a moment longer before looking up at the bathroom mirror and is greeted by his own reflection. And that dark voice, now whispering … but somehow loud enough to ring in Roberto’s ears. “Oh this will be fun. Oh how we’ve missed it so.” Roberto nods, a smile creeping over his face. HE’S BACK…
