Full Circle

19 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/19/11 I often get questioned what I do while on my occasionally often hiatuses from the wrestling ring. The truth is I used to do I lot of work at Dirk’s office for B and C-List clients but since our amicable split months ago I’ve had to find a different path to make some of that delicious cash-money. Now, some can easily fall back on their natural stardom but when you’ve never truly been a star in this little industry of ours, you can scratch that off the list. Others fall back to the simple con-men acts of Real Estate and Sales, after all those are just a natural extension of a wrestler’s abilities. To me that kind of job screams end of the line, not “vibrant young sexy professional”. No, I needed something suited to my swarthy and obnoxious ways. I needed something that really showed my lack of empathy for others. I needed to work in politics. I got work here and there in October, American campaigns wanting advice on how to make a straight laced father of two look like a kid-fucker – easy one, inferred never direct – or making people fear that a woman in charge could lead to her perioding all over important legislation. Turns out that is a very deep concern to many - that one turned into a landslide. It saddened me for a moment to see people so easily swayed but any more than that would require that empathy thing previously mentioned. The important thing is context, or rather how to frame context. When speaking for a prolonged period of time, you’re bound to slip up once or twice. After that, all you need is someone to find a way to either bury that slip up or… well, y’know. All it comes down to is media management and I learned it from the best. I mean how else did Dirk get me all those title shots? On the grounds of my natural ability? That’s funny, you’re funny! I actually managed to wrangle a job on a national Senate campaign for a while until I was politely asked to leave by the McGarrity campaign. Turns out they disagreed that they had to either photoshop pictures of his opponent marrying a body pillow or they’d lose it big time. I mean who really cares about the legality of such matters? Losers, that’s who. Sadly November comes around and all that delicious consultancy work all dries up as all the cattle turns up to put a cross in a box and you take your pay packet and a little vacation to boot. It’s great work if you can find it but you really need to have complete contempt for man, unadvisable otherwise. There were days I even found that I hated myself for what I was doing. Still, that sweet, sweet money does mop up them tears with ease. Which I suppose brings us to the second question I’ve been asked as of late. “Phil”, Johnny Q. Public will ask say. “Phil, why on earth did you come back to wrestling if you were making money underestimating the American public’s intelligence” and as I admonish them for being so familiar with me I give them a wry smile and say but only two words: Eddie Fowler. You see, my friends, as November turned to December I decided to listen, almost by chance, to GCW Radio and was delighted to find out that I was nominated for an award. It almost felt like up until now my legacy in this industry and GCW in particular was lacking. I’d never held a title in GCW, in fact I hadn’t won a title belt in over seven years and even then I was handed it. The “Most Improved” award nomination was just a little thing that I could hang my hat on. I had finally achieved something, no matter how small, in GCW. Sadly, instead of acknowledging what I had achieved over admittedly a long period of time, Eddie Fowler decided to shit all over everything I had ever done. He then proceeded attempt to rub that shit all over my face (in a metaphorical sense, obviously, I wasn’t in the studio that day so there’s no way he could have actually smeared shit into my face). He smeared that shit but promising to be my manservant should I actually win the award. Basically he outright said to the thousands upon thousand of people listening of the world wide internet webs that he didn’t think I had a chance of winning. Eddie Fowler tried to destroy my legacy and sweep it under the carpet. So why did I return? To take him up on that very challenge. To put the very skills I had be honing for months prior to use in the wrestling industry. To take every little piece of Eddie Fowler audio I could and use it to paint him as the worst human being imaginable. I wanted to make sure that win or lose; Eddie Fowler was done in GCW. I wanted to ensure that he was aware that calling me out was a career fatal mistake. For hours I went through Fowler audio and painted him in the light that I felt he should be seen - as a misogynist, as a racist and as a wreckage zone of a human being and if I may say so myself, I did done good. The commercials were ready to go but GBJ refused to air them through GCW. The world needed to see my work and a GBJ wasn’t going to stop me. So with a little funding from an old friend, I became a sponsor of GCW. Fowler has yet to make a public comment since I aired those commercials, with a little funding from an old friend. I had Fowler on the ropes and I was ready to go in for the kill. There would have been no sweeter revenge than to take his job. Destroying his reputation is child’s play but taking his job, to become to new voice of GCW Live while he stewed at home, it would have been a glorious day. Sadly, Commissioner of GCW and general drug fiend Garbage Bag Johnny did not agree. Clearly under the influence of some substance or another he stone walled me. I laid out my case as clear as day, it was irresponsible to have Eddie Fowler broadcasting in light of recent revelations, it was dangerous to keep Eddie Fowler employed and I got nothing but silence in response. I do not and will not stand for complete and utter disrespect. Last week Johnny tried to drive me out of the company through combustion as if setting me alight would quell my campaign. However, this week I appear to have been bought off with a title shot. Turns out my media management skills ain’t too bad either.
