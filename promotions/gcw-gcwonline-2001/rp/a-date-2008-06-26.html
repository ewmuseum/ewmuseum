A date

26 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/26 I winced as another screech ripped its way from Erika’s throat and made its way to my poor ears. She had been giving me a hard time ever since the last show, the one in which I somehow ended up hitting on Daria of all people. I still had no idea why I decided to ask her out on national television, but my temporary lack of common sense led to a very unhappy pregnant girlfriend. “What am I supposed to tell all my friends when they tell me that they saw you asking out some whore in front of the entire world?” I briefly considered protesting that it was doubtful that Daria was a whore, and that I was fairly sure that GCW did not yet broadcast to the entire world, but I could sense that it would not be the wisest course of action. Luckily I had managed to think up a defense that I hoped would calm her, or at the very least direct her rage at someone else. “Bagwell made me do it,” I replied as soon as I could get a word in. I know it seems like a bit of a weak defense, but Erika already knew that I was basically under Bagwell’s thumb because it was his influence that got me into the company. He was high enough on the roster, and I was low enough, that he would not have a problem raising a big enough fuss to get Caldera to kick me out. Besides Erika already hated Bagwell because’well its kind of redundant to say that the average viewer of GCW hates Bagwell. To my utter surprise and relief my ploy worked. Or rather it bought me a momentary reprieve from Erika’s attempts to render me deaf. I dared to glance at her and saw that she was working her jaw furiously, though no sound was coming out. If I had to guess, I would say that she desperately wanted to berate me for listening to Bagwell in the first place, but she knew as well as I did why I had no other choice. Right when I thought I was off the hook she asked, with a hint of triumph in her voice, “Why the hell would Bagwell want you to ask that tramp out in the first place?” The smile that had begun to grow on my face froze while my mind furiously churned. To me the question was incredibly unfair, mostly because I did not believe that Bagwell even had a reason for doing most of the things he did, much less the things that I just pretended that he did. Still, if I didn’t want to start sleeping with a knife under my pillow I had to figure out some sort of logical explanation. “Because” I began and stalled by taking a drink of water, “because” I said again and cleared my throat. I could see Erika tapping her foot impatiently and I knew I probably only had one more “because” to go before she started screeching again. “Because he thinks she’s going to get another shot at Taylor since she was still tired from the Battle Royal the last time they wrestled. He figures that if I get close to her, we might find away to make her shot my own, and you know how gung ho Bagwell is about getting some gold into his precious Movement.” She stared at me piercingly for a second before giving me a sharp nod. Then with a sniff she picked up the phone and began to furiously dial a number. “Ah honey,” I said using the nickname that I only use in bad situations, “who exactly are you calling?” “Who do you think?” she snapped as if it were obvious. “I am going to call Bagwell and give him a peace of my mind. Who does he think he is embarrassing me, by having you do something like that?” Well this was certainly becoming a rather interesting confrontation. On one hand there was Bagwell. A first rate speaker, a rising star in GCW, and someone who could undoubtedly be characterized as ruthless, mean, and cocky. On the other hand there was an angry pregnant woman who though Bagwell had caused her to be embarrassed in front of the entire world. Bagwell was about to verbally get his ass handed to him. But knowing that the process would probably include Erika raising her voice to deafening levels, I decided to leave while she was distracted and went to visit my brother Sean. Sean’s House “So let me get this straight,” Sean said in a carefully controlled tone, “your girlfriend who’s pregnant with a kid whose not yours is yelling at Bagwell. She’s yelling at him because she thinks he made you hit on another girl, when really you did it on the spur of the moment. Meanwhile, while Bagwell is being yelled at, you’re getting ready for a date with the girl you hit on, and you’re doing it at my house in New Jersey even though your date is in New York.” “Well, when you put it that way,” I said defensively, “you make it sound like I am the party at fault. Its not like I want to go on this date, but if I don’t then Daria will be pissed and the movement will gain an enemy.” “Sure,” Sean said dryly raising an eyebrow, “and that’s why you’ve been glued to the mirror for the past hour trying on every piece of clothing that I have.” I ignored him and continued to observe my image in the mirror. The suit was a tad small, but I didn’t have a suit at all so I was forced to make do. I suppose that I should have considered wardrobe when deciding on the location of the’stipulation, but it was a hasty decision. “Of course that isn’t even taking into account the bigger issue,” Sean continued as I tweaked my hair. “You know, the fact that you tend to be horrible on dates. Before you deny that, let me point on you’ve been on about six, and none of them ended well.” I turned to him with a hot retort that died on my lips. “So, so what do I do?” I asked helplessly. Sean sighed than comforted me with a pat on the back. “Look, if you are determined to go on this date you stick to the basics. Open doors for her, stick to pleasant small talk, and kiss on the cheek at the end of the date. If you somehow manage to keep from messing all that up, she might invite you in and you’ll have to decide how far you’re willing to go.” “I’ll keep that in mind” I said quietly before looking at the clock and realizing it was time for me to go. Outside the restaurant. I sat nervously in my car in the restaurant’s parking lot. I had one minute to decide whether I really wanted to go on this date. I knew that I would be betraying my girlfriend and probably digging myself a nice deep grave. But I didn’t care, all I knew was that I would rather be in that restaurant with Daria than at home with my girlfriend. Without any hesitation I got out of my car.
