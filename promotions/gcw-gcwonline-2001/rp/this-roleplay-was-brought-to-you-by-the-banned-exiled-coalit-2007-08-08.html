This roleplay was brought to you by the Banned & Exiled~! Coalition to get Steven Caldera to change the name of the Tron to Optimus Prime.

8 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 08, 2007 "We are the Champions, my friend!" Fans all over the arena stop what they are doing and turn toward the entrance ramp. As per the cue of the music, the most beloved tag team in GCW steps out from behind the curtain to greet them. Thrusting their arms up high, the duo of Chris Storm and Markus Stone hold their championship belts in the air for all to see. Andrews is on the microphone, ready to introduce the two, when he is once again stop from being able to do so. Storm: Hold it buddy, we don't need a hack trying to introduce the greatest thing since baby jesus. Cheers come raining down hard and fast. Andrews lowers his microphone, grumbling to himself as he exits out of the ring. Markus Stone pulls out his microphone from the back of his trunks and brings it slowly to his mouth. Stone: Ladies and gentlemen’boys and girls’children of all ages. Prepare your eyes, check your pulse, and grab onto the edge of your seats. Tonight, in that very ring, the greatest tag team ever, weighing in at a combined weigh of four-hundred and eighty pounds, we are BANNED & EXILED~! The fans once again cheer for everyone's favorite Tag Team Champions as the two toss their microphones aside and get ready. As they get half way down the ramp something on the GCW MegaTron stops them. The screen flickers to life, flashing different images of a deserted metal wasteland. The Tag Team Champions tilt their head sideways to try and get a better look at what is going on when the screen just goes blank. Figuring just a technical malfunction, or one of the techies backstage was high, Banned & Exiled~! turn back to head down the ring. But from the blackness of the screen fades in an image. Feeling something is wrong, the two turn around very slowly to see the GCW MegaTron transforming before their very eyes. How could they not know that there was more than meets the eyes with it? Dropping down from where the screen hung, the large robot falls onto the top of the entrance ramp and effectively cuts off Banned & Exiled~! escape route. With nowhere else to go, the two run and slide into the ring. Stone: Dude, I feel this is a good time to mention I hate robots. Storm: And I think this is a good time to mention I hate jumbo screens. B&E~!: CRAP! Lifting its large metallic body up, the evil purple and grey robot makes its way toward the ring methodically, heavy metal feet crushing the steel ramp below it like it was a cheap foam cup. Chris and Markus back up ever more until they finally hit the ring ropes. Pausing, the two stare as the jagged and sharp face of the robot leans in towards them. MegaTron: Are you username: Ilikethickchicks? Storm: Dude! Stone: Uh’no? The robot slams its humongous fist down into the ring and nearly collapses the enter ring around the two Tag Team Champions. MegaTron: Where is username: ilikethickchicks? Stone: I think you should probably go talk to Bryan Mayhem. Storm: Yeah, he seems to be into thunder thighs. I mean he does hang around Shaman. Stone: Hey, did Caldera hire Mike Awesome? That might explain this. Storm: I don’t know, I don’t think so. I mean why would a giant robot be after Mike Awesome? Stone: I think the better question here is why wouldn’t a giant robot be after Mike Awesome? Luckily in the time Banned & Exiled~! spends cutting jokes is more than enough for the panicking crowd to make an escape, though they did trample over poor Johnny Stevens in accounting. All that was left in the arena was Chris, Markus, the MegaTron, and the other robot that was flying from the backstage area. Wait’the what? Sure enough, just when everyone’s favorite duo was about to meet their doom at the hands of an angry Jumbo Screen, another robot of red and blue comes barreling at them and slams into the MegaTron. The evil Decepticon leader is sent crashing into the mass of seats in the upper deck. Robot #2: Hello, I am Optimus Prime, leader of the Autobots. I am looking for username: ilikethickchicks. Stone: Dude, what is up with all the robots looking for a fat chick lover? Storm: Maybe they know some lonely and overweight robot chicks? Perhaps now wasn’t the best time for the two to be making jokes as the MegaTron launches itself from the upper deck and decks Optimus Prime. The Convoy is sent into the set from which the MegaTron once rested. The blow, though shakes Prime, is not enough to keep him down and the leader of the Autobots charges back to exchange blows with MegaTron. After knocking MegaTron back for a bit of breathing room, Optimus Prime looks down to notice Chris Storm and Markus Stone still watching the fight, though now from the comfort of the front row. Somehow they had managed to get popcorn and material to make signs that read "I <3 ilikethickchicks" and "Optimus > PRIME". Optimus: You two should get to safety. Stone: And miss this fight? How often do we get to watch two robots battle it out? Storm: Does last the Forth of July count? The distractions from the fight causes Prime to suffer a shot right to the face that knocks him back a step. Showing great skill and reflexes, he still manages to count MegaTron’s next move and send him flying into the only seats in the right side of the arena that wasn’t destroyed yet. Wreckage fills the arena, the ring is broken to pieces, things are on fire and yet among all of it, the two seats Chris and Markus sit in remain perfectly fine. Optimus: Can you two fight? Storm: Can we fight? Markus, tell this fool who we are. Stone: We are the greatest tag team alive, the greatest GCW Tag Team Champions of the World! We are Banned & Exiled~! Storm: So of course we can fight! We even have the tilde and the exclamation point after our name. Accepting that as a "yes", Optimus Prime tosses the two down rifles to use in adding him in battle. Markus studies his gun. Stone: Sweeeeet. I now love robots. Storm: At least ones that arm us with deadly weapons. It seems it was just in time too, as MegaTron unleashes a vicious blast attack on Optimus Prime that knocks him off his feet and onto his back. MegaTron pounces and Banned & Exiled~! let loose on his metal hide with their rifles. Though the guns don’t prove to be very effective against his armor, they do distract MegaTron enough to let Optimus Prime flip him over and send the two crashing through the wall. With the structure compromised, the entire arena starts to fall down around Chris and Markus? ears. Stone: Dude, I so told Caldera he needs to change the MegaTron. Storm: Totally. Markus notices the two still have their guns. Stone: So, what do you want to do now? Storm: I dunno, want to go all vigilante Punisher style on some bitches? Stone: You know it. B&E~!: Sweet!
