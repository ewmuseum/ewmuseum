Who Will Be First? Oh, It Looks Like Poor Kyle...

9 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09Feb2007 So it's really happening. Markus Stone is really coming to GCW. To be technical, Markus is already a part of GCW, although most casual fans it is still unknown. A majority of the wrestlers know of his arrival, yet most of them do not care. Why should they? Who would worry about a wrestler who has been out of action for five years? A wrestler who has a well known knee injury? A wrestler who has, when you look at the broad picture, been on a downhill slope ever since his first World title reign in a federation that closed it's doors years ago? Most people wouldn't even care about a man like that. However, Markus Stone is intent on proving them all wrong. Not for any kind of glory, not for another title reign, not even to be considered a 'top dog' in GCW. Markus wants to prove them all wrong so that he can show everybody the truth. What is the truth? You are strong, and they are weak. "You're right," Markus replies to the Voice. Markus Stone, the wrestler long ago known as Zero, is at the beach. Contrary to popular belief, not all beaches are sandy and sunny. On this little island just off the coast of Washington, the beach is covered with rocks and trees that have been blown over from the strong winds. The sky is covered in clouds, and there is a soft drizzle. Such is the way of the state of Washington. Markus, however, is as unperturbed by the whether as most Washington residents are. Most people stay indoors for a good portion of the year, slowly become more bored and depressed as the years wear by. If there is any doubt about weather's effect on people's moods, then look to the suicide rate in Washington. Number one in the nation. Markus, however, has always enjoyed a gloomy day to a warm, sunny lie. You always have been quick to see the truth to the world. "I'm not the only one, you know. Lot's of people realize that life sucks," Markus informs the Voice. "However, I'm strong enough to deal with it and face it," he adds. That you are Markus. A true warrior in every sense of the word. "Warrior.... Yes... An emotional gladiator..." Markus hazily agrees. Emotional gladiator? Don't get corny Mark, our mission won't allow for that sort of thing. The Voice has certainly been more serious as of late. Used to be, the two of them would jab back and forth. Nowadays, the Voice spends much more time keeping Markus focused. "No, I wasn't trying to be," comes the reply. "But I AM a gladiator. A survivor." Yes, yes, now would you quit beating your drum and focus. The Voice was right. Markus will debut shortly. Against a man named Kyle James. This should be great news, and in fact it is. However, when dealing with Markus Stone one must realize that he does not see positives. The only side of things is the worst case negatives. What does Markus know about this man? Nothing. Although Markus does have the ability to beat this man, it's a matter of whether or not he will be willing to push himself far enough to do so. Will Markus push through the pain that his knee gives him? Will Markus push through his own insecurities to display enough confidence to be victorious? "I'm worried," Markus states, seeking a little reassurance from the Voice. You are a fool for being so. So what if you know nothing of this James fellow? What does he know about you? Do you think he even remembers who you used to be? Let alone the man you've now become? "True, but..." Markus begins, but stops himself. Looking out at the cold ocean water, Markus stands from the log he has been sitting on. He walks forward, stepping into the water. The water sends chills up his legs, and soaks his jeans. Markus doesn't back away, however, instead walking further into the water. Markus stops when he is about waist deep in the water, the light waves lapping at his torso. He is shivering almost violently, but Markus stays where he is. Pain is weakness leaving the body. "Exactly," Markus manages to say. The cold isn't so much painful as a major discomfort. However, Markus realizes that his body will adjust. Another victory for Markus, however slight. "Kyle James has no idea what is in store for him," he states. Markus waits for a reply, but receives none. Perhaps the Voice has gone away for now? Markus honestly relishes the times when he is truly alone. It gives him time to reflect without having to hear nay-saying and bitter comments from some one who never experienced what he has. Why? If not for me, you'd still be rotting away in your apartment. At least I'm making you do something. Nevermind. "I c-c-could have done it on my own," comes his reply. I'm glad you like to say so, but you can't fool me Markus. Even I know you don't mean that. "Aren't you the one who wanted me to focus?" Markus asks, changing the subject. The Voice's laughter echoes in Markus' head. Well, you are right in that respect. Think about it Mark, you have the opportunity of a lifetime. This could be the start of it all. "My first victim?" Markus empashizes 'victim,' still not one hundred percent behind the ideas the Voice has posed. Victim? Oh no, not in the least. We are not hitmen, or murderers. You and I, Markus, we are saviors. We are liberators, freeing these poor souls from the lies they have built around themselves. Is it not better to fall to pieces in the truth than to sustain yourself with deceit? Markus thinks on this. Had he himself not tried to make his own life bearable by doing the same? Had he not told all those close to him that everything was fine, hoping to convince himself it was true? Always quick to crack a joke, to defer any dark thoughts from entering his own mind? Is he better off now? Surely so. "Liberators... That, my friend, sounds corny." Old habits die hard. Now is not the time. "You're right," Markus agrees as he turns and walks back to dry land. "Now is a time to look forward. Kyle James, this man stands in my way." Yes. "He will try to stop me, to keep me from accomplishing our goal." Yes. Markus continues, gaining intensity as he does so. "This simple man expects to keep me from freeing every one else." Yes, yes! "This foolhardy man thinks to stand across the ring from me and end our mission, our Quest! Before it ever even begins!" That's right Markus, and what are we going to do? "What are we going to do? We are going to tear this man down! We are going to dig deep inside of him, and rip out the happiness from his very core!" Markus shouts these thoughts to the Voice. The two of them are feeding off of each other's energy now. Yes! YES! This fool thinks to stand before the despair that is Markus Stone? We will make him suffer for this transgression! This poor sheep that was directed to the wolf shall be consumed! "And we shall gnaw the bones, savoring in the meal as we devour his joy." Markus is overcome by these thoughts. Why did he never think of this before? What better way to make people suffer than to put them through his own pain? The question of how, however, enters Markus' mind briefly. After all, Markus doesn't even know anything about his opponent. How can he find a way to scar him with no knowledge? The thought, however, does not last long. Dark thoughts force them away. "He will suffer...." Markus says softly, though no less intense than he was moments ago. And if he survives, he will be stronger for it. "And if he does not, then we have winnowed another weakling from the pack," Markus adds. The two of them stand there in silence, satisfied with the accord they have come to. Kyle James, Markus Stone, GCW World Wide... This is the return of Markus Stone to the ring, and the beginning of a new crusade. With every campaign, there must be a start. Will Kyle James be the first to fall? Or will he prove to be strong enough, will he prove to be one of the fit? We shall see...
