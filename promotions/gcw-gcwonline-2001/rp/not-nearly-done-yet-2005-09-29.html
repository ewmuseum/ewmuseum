Date: 29-09-05 It was never supposed to be this way. It was never supposed to get so bad that she couldn’t stand to even watch any of the broadcasts that GCW put out whether she was on card to wrestle or not. It was supposed to help her, it was supposed to be cleansing, a kind of closure on the past and an opening of the door to the future but as it stood, in contradiction with where Mary sat; It wasn’t anything like she had hoped. Life has a habit of shitting on you when you’ve run outta wetnaps but some times all you have to do is go to KFC, order a two piece and get some more. Or some analogy that means when life gives you lemons, squeeze lemon juice in the bitch’s eyes. Mary tried to keep up this kind of positive thinking as best she could but with a mind more battle scarred than Beirut, it wasn’t an easy task. Then there were the children she had seemed to acquire over the last fortnight. They were both a hindrance and a help. They both took her mind off her problems and at the same time took up time that she would have used to sit and sort her thoughts, her feelings, her situation. She didn’t complain, the kids were growing on her. Her dream of being a mother lay like a feather on the edge of a cliff. She had no idea if it would ever be a realisation. The fact that she hadn’t conceived when the man by the name of Shina kidnapped and inseminated her was both a blessing and a worry. Had it just not been the right time to conceive or had her miscarriage while carrying Damien Black’s child years go left her infertile for good this time? She had no idea and part of her wanted to keep it that way. Somehow knowing the truth, knowing for certain, taking away all doubt seemed more devastating than the speculation. Mary sat on the swing in her yard in her work out clothes. She’d been to the gym every other day since her last match even though she hadn’t been booked to wrestle in a fortnight. It helped to keep her focused on things that didn’t directly involve LeStatt. When she was working out it was all about the ring, all about wrestling, no one else existed, nothing else mattered. Right now she needed that, she needed a focal point that wouldn’t waver if she stared at it too long because everything else seemed to be wavering like, well, a motherfucker, for want of better phrasing. She swung back and forth in the cool fall air. It wasn’t freezing but it was cold enough for a sweater, one she wasn’t wearing. She shivered with a passing wind. Staring off into the distance, a small voice startled her while tugging at her bracelets. “TyTy” You missing da westlin. Da lady and ya fwend Statt, they was?” “Woah,” Mary cut Alize off before she could finish, her freckled and rosy cheeked face glancing up at Mary with scrunched up eyes against the gentle wind. “Is ok, Mary don’t really wanna know ya know.” Alize climbed onto the swing beside Mary and straddled it like a horse. “Why comes?” Mary sighed. “It’s kinda hard to explain. Ya know kinda like when ya dint want ya momma to tell you Santy clause wuddent real.” Alize’s eyes sprung wide in shock. “Sanny Clause ain weal?” Her lip started to tremble. Mary had to think fast. “Er..er’sure he is, Mary dint mean he ain’t real. Sure thing he’s real, how else would ya get ya presents” Silly,” She tussled Alize’s fiery red hair. “Mary means like ya know like the easter bun?” She stared into Alize’s eyes seeing her waiting with impending doom. “Err, shitburgers,” “Ooh you saida bad word. Momma says whenever we says that word we getsa spankin.” Mary stared at the child. “And ya momma would be right. Mary should get a spankin.” “Ok.” Alize hopped off the swing and marched across the garden towards a tree. “What ya ding there girlie?” “Gettin a switch.” Mary’s eyes widened, “Woah there horsee, how bouts you just spank the back of Mary’s hand with yours and we save the switch for the bigger badder words?” Alize turned back with a, “Ok.” And hopped back onto the swing. “So?” she said staring back up at Mary. “So what?” “So why?” Mary looked confused, “So what why what?” Alize looked more confused. “So why comes?” “So why comes what?” Alize was growing angry. “SO WHY COMES!” Mary raised her eyebrows. “Don’t you be yelling at Mary halfpint.” “I just wanna know why dangit!” Mary raised her eyebrows again, “How bouts Mary tells you why she’s gonna go back for that switch, how bout that?” Alize shrunk down on the swing. “Nooo, pwease, I’ll be good.” “Good. Now what the heck you talking bout?” Alize thought a moment and then shrugged. “Don’t member now.” Mary sighed and smiled, “Yep Mary gets that a lot too.” Alize was staring at her again. She had reached over and started playing with Mary’s bracelets again when her fingers brushed over Mary’s wrist. The mottled skin there was bumpy to the touch. Alize turned her hand palm up and rubbed her hands over the old scars. She looked up at Mary. “Does these hurt?” Mary looked down into her innocent eyes and smiled, “Not a bit dingaling. Thems is old scars.” “Howd ya hurt yaself?” Mary gently pulled her hand back from the inquisitive four year old. “Mary’Mary was a very unhappy bunny one time and she figured the only way to?” She glanced over at Alize who was looking up at her intently. She couldn’t fill the child’s head with her own horrors. She couldn’t steal her innocence like that like hers had been taken from her when she was 5. Mary smiled and rubbed at her arms. “Mary fell over is all. No big deal. Was a long, long time ago.” Alize smilled, “When I hurt maself, Momma gets me da bunnywabbit bandaids. “Did your Momma do that too?” Mary stroked her hair, “No, she wasn’t abouts.” “How comes?” “Well, Mary’s momma is?” A voice interrupted, “If you wish to play twister Miss Alize, your brothers are waiting for you to join them.” “Oh shit yea!” Alize yelped and jumped off the swing but got her foot caught and tumbled to the ground. She sprung back to her feet instantly though. Mary shot her a stern glance. “What did we say about that word missy?” Alize looked scolded and began to walk over to the tree again, her head hung low. “Hey now, we don’t need no switches today ok, you go play just watch ya frea”.err, watch ya mouth okies?” She nodded and skipped indoors. Arael stood beside Mary peering down at her from behind his trademark dark glasses. “She is a spirited one Chica, I’ll bet you were just like her at her age.” Mary shook her head, “Nuh uh, Mary dint get the sailor mouth till way into highschool. That lil rugrat got the jump on her.” Arael smiled and pulled up a chair beside her. “Shouldn’t you be watching the children?” Mary kicked at the ground to swing herself gently, “Nah they be ok, so long as we can hear em arguing. When it goes quiet then we gets worried.” “I use the very same theory with you Chica. Which is why I am out here.” “You worrying bout Mary again?” “Sim, I am cursed to do so. You’ve been’distant these past weeks. You won’t let the children watch any GCW broadcasts while you’re in the room. I thought you were glad to have signed your contract there.” Mary exhaled and kicked at a fallen leaf by her foot. “Mary was, she is, is just a lot harder than she thought it would be. She didn’t think being around Statty again, like this, would hurt so much. But is not just Statty, it’s Mary too. She’s a two time loser now and ya gotta wonder sometimes is she losing cus the games changed so much since she was away or is it cus she just cant cut the cheese no more ya know?” “Self doubt comes to everyone Chica.” “Even you Eddy?” She looked over at him. “Sim, Even me. When I was told that I might never see again I thought my life was over but Levi, he moulded me and trained me to be even more efficient than I ever was with my sight. Unfortunately this was all just training and when it came to me handling my first job alone, I feared I would fail, and fail badly. In my line of work that is not an option you can choose more than once, if you choose it at all.” “So did you get the job done?” “I did in the end. The unknown can be a very frightening thing whether you can see or not.” Mary sighed and gazed over at the trees to her right. “You ain’t wrong Eddy. This federation, Statty, Mary dunno where any of it is going and they both scare her. She’s done these movies and these comic book deal things but in the end when it all comes down to the riceroni of it all, Mary ain’t got much if she ain’t got wrestling. She loves it like she loves Statty. And both of them cause Mary the most trouble.” Arael eased back in his seat but not so much so that he wasn’t alert to all his surroundings. “Love is like a baby Chica. It is selfish, and it gives back to you pleasure only when it has what it wants. It wants your attention, it wants your time.” Mary turned back to him, “But Mary gives love and wrestling and Statty her time, she gives em her attention she gives em all she got.” Arael looked at her this time, “Nobody said all babies are good babies. Some are no trouble and some are trouble until the grow up and move away. In the end your child is still your child and you care for it no less.” Mary looked quizzical, “Not sure what ya trying to say Eddy,” He smiled, “Me either Chica. You must be rubbing off on me.” He got up, “I will go check on the children. Just think on this Chica. It is all about you, not Senor Knight, nor the federation, just you. What was it that your cousin Ashura used to say?” “If you want it, try and take it?” He nodded, “Sim, perhaps you should take her advice this once no?” Mary didn’t reply as Arael walked back into the house. Ashura was ruthless, sick, twisted, heartless but one thing she wasn’t was a quitter or afraid of any challenge thrown her way. She made the moves, she made and broke the deals, she took what she wanted from the world and left it crying and feeling violated. Was that what Mary needed to do she thought? Was that who she needed to be? A clown can only entertain, a warrior commands everything they own and everything they seek to own. Was it time to put the clown to bed and unleash the real Typhoid Mary? She’d almost let that happen in her fight with LeStatt back at his mansion and it had nearly cost them both their lives. “Mary don’t wanna kill no one, she just wanna hurt em a lil.” She sighed and her mind fleeted to an image of LeStatt Knight kissing Eva. She shut her eyes tight as if to erase the memory but it was branded into her mind. Could she do this? Could she stand by and watch this? Was it worth the pain? “Thems is all gonna pay. Mary wants her Statty back, she wants her career back damnit! Eddy’s right, its about Mary not them and if they wanna battle, they all fucking got one!” She smiled. “Now watch this swing.” She giggled to herself and shoved off with her feet.
