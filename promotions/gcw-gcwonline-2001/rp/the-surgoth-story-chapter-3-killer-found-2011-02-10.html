The Surgoth Story - Chapter 3 - Killer Found

10 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/10/11 It was now going on twelve-hundred hours and lunch was to be served shortly. I couldn't wait for my turkey on wheat to show up, not because I was hungry, but because she would be the one delievering my lunch. Oh how I love her, and I have no idea who she is. I knew that I should be more focused on the case I was working on currently, but I just couldn't get her off my mind. Lately I haven't been in the office during lunch hours due to this case, but finally I was here and this time I promised myself I would talk to her. "Ding!" There was the elevator bell! She would be stepping through those sliding doors any second! Out came the car first, then... disappointment. She wasn't the one delivering today. It was some older lady I had never seen before. I figured she must be one of the cooks from down in the cafeteria. It figures, I finally get up the courage to talk to such a lovely woman and she doesn't even show her face today. Just as well, I had a lot of work to be done today. I turned to look at my computer screen to do a little bit of research in hopes to find out what could have possibly killed some of the members of the crime syndicate known as The Triabls. The police were alerady tracking down the syndicate but I had to find whatever was killing them because whatever it was might end up killing an innocent person. I just couldn't have that on my conscience. A lot of the pages I found on the net had to do with vampires. This didn't surprise me much due to the fact that all of the victims were drained of blood. THere was a few problems with this theory though. Number one, a vampire can't drain its victim completely of their blood or it would kill the vampire. The bodies that were found had no blood left at all. Number two, vampires mostly go for the neck and there are two puncture wounds left behind. The one body I did get the chance to look at had one puncture wound and was on the right side of the victim. Number three, there was a slimy substance around the wound. Vampires don't leave behind slimy substances. Another problem with this scenerio is the fact of the victim's skin turning purple and becoming purnce like. I had tried so many different keywords though the Google Search Engine. No matter what I put in, none of the pages could possibly be a connection to what was killing these people. I even looked up the Day of Awakening in hope to find some sort of clue and kill two birds with one stone since the Chief wanted me researching it anyway. Still, I found nothing. Finally, my sandwich and mountain dew were set in front of me at my desk. I handed the lady four, one dollar bills and had a quarter returned to me. She was the typical old woman, white hair up in curls that were moer than likely from using curlers at night while she slept. She had a hair net over her hair but you could still see the curls clear as day. Her skin was wrinkled, she looked to be in about her seventies, but these days you never really can tell. I decided I would ask her where 'she' was at today. "What happened to the usual girl that delivers lunch.?" I asked the woman. "She called off sick today," she replied. "I heared she might be out for a couple of days. I will let her know you asked though." "Oh please, don't trouble yourself." "It won't be a trouble at all, she is my granddaughter. Just so you know, she talks about you all the time. She sees how you stare at her while she is up here. Why don't you actually get up the courage to ask her out. I know that's what you want to do, and just so you know, she's waiting for you to do it," the wold woman said as she winked at me then walked away. I couldn't hide my excitement at this bit of news. A wide grin spreak across my face. Maybe this was the best thing to actually happen today. I felt like an idiot though after the old woman had walked away. I forgot to find out what her granddaughter's name was. I guess I will just have to ask her the next time I see her. I figured it was time to get back to work while I eat my lunch. decided to check my work e-mail to see if my contact got a hold of me with any information about what he thought was killing these men. When I opened my e-mail, I had two new messages, one from my contact at the mortuary and the other had a name and subject that said the same thing. That e-mail was simply this '???'. I decided to check the e-mail message from Rohan Talis, my contact at the mortuary. The e-mail subject was, "Some Information' and simply just that. I opened the message and this is what it had to say: "Van, "All the lab tests came back and it's rather strange. What I thought was possibly a parasite that was killing these men, isn't. Both tests came back saying that the slime around the wound site is, in fact, saliva. It's not human saliva nor is it of animal origin. We aren't sure what the saliva has come from only that it does share some of the smae characteristics as human saliva. I will continue to look into this and as sson as I have more information I will be sure to pass it along to you. "Your good friend, "Rohan Talis" Not human nor animal. So what could it be then? The slime isn't slime at all, it's saliva. I had one other e-mail that needed to be checked, the one that was all question marks. Normall I wouldn't open an e-mail like that due to the fact of a possible virus, but my insticts told me that I needed to open it. I had a feeling that this would give me more unformation about this deadly, unknown thing. I opened the e-mail and it had a video attachment. I hit the downlaod button and waited for a few minutes. Once the download completed I opened the video. The first thing I heard as the video came on was screaming and funfire. A ski masked face appears on the camera and begins to talk in the same raspy voice as the man that tipped me off about these killings. "It's here, whatever it is! We can't kill it! It is to fast for our gun shots! How can it dodge machine gun fire? I hope you get this video Van! I think this maybe it for myself and the rest of our men! Please find and kill this thing before it kills any innocent people! You have to... AAAAAHHHHHHH!!!!!!!" The camera seems to fall to the ground and is laying on its side. I see a slight bit of blood splatter onto the lense and then shortly after an eye stares into the camera as well. The eye is bloodshot and has no pupil. Only a white film covers the eye. A loud scream is heard as the camera is tossed to the side and the video stops. I picked up the phone at my desk and pushed the button that rings into the Chief's office. "What is it Van?" asked the Chief on the other end. "I think there is something you need to see," I began. "I think it might have to do with the killings of The Tribals." "I'll be right out." A few seconds later, Chief Gilthor was standing at the desk and I replayed the video for him. "What is that thing" asked the Chief after the video was over. "I'm not real sure," I answered. "But we need to figure out where this is taking place." "Take the video back to where it is just laying on the floor before the blood splatter. Maybe whatever surrondings it took a picture of can give us an idea of where this is at." I did as the Chief asked and we both took a good look at the scene that it was paused at. A couple of crates stood off in the distance and something was written on them. The only problem was that they were to far away to make out. "Look at those crates Chief," I began. "Something is written on them." "Send the video to our video annalist Calby," suggested the Chief. "Maybe he can do something with the video to help us out." "Right away." "I e-mailed the video to Calby and waited. I finished eating my lunch and about five minutes later my phone started to ring. I looked at the flashing light on the phone and it was Calby getting back to me already. I picked up the phone and said, "What did you find out Calby?" A males voice came from the other end of the phone, "I got a good visual on three of the crates off in the distance. All three of them say 'Warehouse Thirty'. What is going on in that video Van?" "I'm not real sure but I'm going to find out." "Be careful Van, those guys had machine guns. I don't think you are going to be any match for this thing." "I'm not going to fight it, I'm going to talk to it." "I don't think that thing is in the mood for talking," said Calby with some concern. "And I'm not in the mood to put up with any bullshit from anyone or anything," I respended "Good luck Van." "I hung up the phone, grabbed my gun from my desk drawer and went up to the Cheif's door. I knocked on it and he motiioned for me to come in. "What did you find out Van?" asked the Chief. "Whatever this thing is, it's at warehouse thirty," I responded. "I'm on my way there now to find out what this thing is and what it wants." "I'm sending backup with you." "No Chief." I replied. "Let me go alone. Maybe this thing can be resoned with." "That thing didn't look like it was in the mood to be reasoned with! You are one crazy son of a bitch, good, but crazy. If anything goes wrong, don't get brave, radio in." "I will Chief." "Good luck Van." "Thanks Chief," I said as I left his office and headed for the double doors of the elevator. I had to admit I was scared shitless, but this was my job and one one else was going to do it but me. * * * I had arrived at Warehouse Thirty at fifteen-hundred hours. Black cars were parked everywhere but the area was quiet, except for the squwaks of seafulls. The warehouse has many broken windows and the building was blue in color. In the front were two large garage type doors. To the left of the furthest on the left was a ergular door. I approached it and turned the knob to find it unlocked. Inside was somewhat dark, the only light came though the windows from the sunlight. It was enough light to reveal the carnage that I had seen in the video I had received in my e-mail. At least forty men lay on the ground dead. I approach one of the bodies and remove the ski mask he is wearing. His skin is purple and cold. Whatever it was killing these men was the same thing that had killed the man I had already examined. "Where are you!?" I yelled out. "What are you!?" In the back I heard a somewhat snake like voice reply, "I am just like you. I want to bring the criminal society toa stand still. I want them gone just as much as you. Why do you try and prevent me from doing my work?" What you are doing is wrong," I answered him. "Yes, they maybe criminals, but they shouldn't be slaughtered like this. They have the right to a fair trial in court. You are robbing them of that right and there for, you are a criminal yourself." "Wrong! I am a hero to all of those that are innocent! If it wasn't for me, these men would push their drugs and weapons unto innocent people. They would pass on their whores to desperate men. Whores that are full of disease and disgust. I couldn't let them live and continue their work. How does this make me a criminal?" "Killing these men has made you a murderer. Show yourself to me." "If you want to see me then it will be your fault for the madness that will br brought unto you," said the voice and shortly after I heard something running across the walls of the steel building. Seconds later the noise stops until a thud is heard in front of me. There, before me, stood somethign I had never seen before. Its skin was a light purple color and its tung kept exiting and entering is mouth after licking its lips.On the end of its tung was a small hole. From it dripped a little bit of blood. It had no teeth and its face was almost scale like. It stood only a little shorter than myself, I guessed five foor, ten inches. "What are you?" I asked it. "A Vamlisk," it answered me in its snake like voice. "What the hell is a Vamlisk!?" "It seems as though I am a mix between a Vampire and a Basilisk. I'm a freak created by some sick scientist. I was his final project before he ended up landing himself in jail due to releasing a virus that began killing people. I had broke out of the cell he kept me in for the purpse of killing him. Unfortunately he was put away. So I thought I would do the next best thing, kill these other criminals before they could kill any innocents." "And now you are no better than the rest of them," I said as I pulled out my Police Issue Beretta and pointed it at the monster's head. "You can either come quietly with me or I can splatter your brians all over the place." Just as I had done that, the Vamlisk quickly ran. I took a shot but I knew I had missed as it ricocheted off one of the walls. "Sorry, but you aren't getting me that easy," laughed the Vamlisk. "I'll see you another time and next time I won't allow you to leave." I knew now that the Vamlisk was gone. I had found the killer, but he had gotten away. Where he was headed, I wasn't sure, but I knew that I had to get back to the office and let the Chief know what I had found. I wasn't sure if he, or anyone else, would believe me. Hell, I had a hard time believing it myself. I will find the Vamlisk again, and when I do, he isn't going to have a chanceat a fair trial.
