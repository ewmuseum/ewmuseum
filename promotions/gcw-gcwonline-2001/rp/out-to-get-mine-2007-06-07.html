Out to get Mine

7 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 7th 2007 (The day only felt minutes long. With Warpath behind me, I feel as if even though I came out with the loss, I had achieved so much. Whether it be that I had proved myself worthy as an active pro athlete once again, or maybe it was the push I needed to excel myself more. Although some would say Thunder had a huge advantage with height, I had the advantage with HEART. I came out of Warpath a fairly changed man, for now I knew that GCW wasn't exactly the "cake walk" it had once been. I needed to practice if I was to get better. Twelve hours a day at a local gym wasn't going to cut it. I was going to need that extra boost.) (I weighed my options and was able to cut them down. I figured steriods was just an easy way out of hard work. It was going to effect more than just my muscles, so that was an easy dropper. Plus, if I wanted roids I would have just went and talked to Vince McMahon at WWE. I'd rather die poor in the gutter than to go to that hunk of crap. I also thought of gaining weight and drinking supplimentary drinks, but that would more or less lower my stamina and my flexibility. I have charisma, so why throw it away. I also thought of more hours of training. But that was far from what I needed, for after some sessions I would be drained, emotionally and physically. It was was like I was lifeless.) (That's when it hit me. Maybe I was doing everything wrong. Maybe I was only hurting myself more by conditioning more. The more hours spent on a treadmill or doing countless curls and squats was making me worse. So it came easy to me what was needed to be done in order to fix this. I needed a personal trainer.) (I had prior recommendations from previous partners and friends who I had worked Indy shows with before making it big. I figured I could stick my nose into the Yellow Pages and maybe just hunt me down one. I didn't want me just your average Joe trainer. I needed someone who could push me to success. Or at least strive me to want more.) (After running the list of prior recommendations through my head, I felt that if I were to go out and find one it would be easier. Just find a gym or yoga class, anything, and find someone who showed heart. I will settle only for the best.) (I walked towards the door of my dazzling hotel suite in hot pursuit. I kept a steady pace towards the elevators only to find myself jogging down eight flights of stairs instead. If I see an alternate from an easy road, i'll just about take it.) (I finally reached the bottom landing, little out of breath but adrenaline and determination kept me marching forward. I walked out the door out to the main corridor which lead to the front lobby. I made my way to the glamourous thickly paned glass mohogany revolving doors and exited through them. There I stood on the hard cement of the earth with a decision that stood ahead of me.) "Left or right? Hmmm.....(Pausing for thought)......Left." (Oddly enough I only made that option for I was left handed so I figured why not. I kept a steady jog down the fairly busy sidewalk street. Though it was an inconsistant irregular breathing pattern, each breath of fresh air oddly seemed more refreshing today. Sweat was beginning to work its way down my face, and my pace had slowed down a bit but I pushed forth the courage to continue on until I would stumble across the first fitness class or program my eyes set their sight on.) (It felt as though I had been jogging for hours until I reached my first fitness gym on my left. I peered in through the glass only to see elderly people doing standard routine stretches with a Richard Simmons-esc trainer. There wasn't much else more to that place so i continued on my way.) (It was merely minutes later when I found myself standing outside another fitness center, JR's Fitness. I snuck a peek through the windows only to see the place being used as a Child's Karate class. It seemed full of life but once again, it wasn't exactly the professional ranking I fell into. I turned to continue on when there I saw her.....) (She was standing at the crosswalk waiting for the light to change so she could walk right into my arms, or so I dreamt within those seconds I stared. They say staring is impolite, but I think it is only if it's misused for harassment reasons rather than the reason I had. Standing there with her long brown hair in a ponytail down to her midback in length, with the athletic body of a Greek god, The face of an inviting angel, she continued to weight as I nervously pondered what to say to catch her attention to me as she approached my side of the busy street. Should I go with the Weather angle, or maybe Got the Time. Or maybe just tell her she just so happened to be the most beautiful thing my eyes ever laid eyes upon.....Then it hit me.....Butterfiles.....I felt as if it were a must to talk to her, but I was afraid of chickening out. Then I acted "Out of love at first sight" some would say and I walked off the sidewalk onto the busy street in a mysterious love trance the led me into on coming traffic.) (It was the haulting sound of screeching breaks broke the trance but it was too late. I stood there almost welcoming death into my life. It's funny how they say your whole life flashes infront of your eyes, because if that be the case.....I must have lived a short life. I closed my eyes fearing for the worse and then I felt it.) Voice 1 - "Somebody call an ambulance!!!!" ........... ................. ....................... ............................ ................................ ..................................... (It was faintly heard in the backround the voice of an angel approaching me. I figured I was dead but opened my eyes to a world much larger now. Much more lively and beautiful......Not because I survived....possibly....but because there she was. Standing above me taking holding my head up as if I was just given the world in one shot. Yes I was in pain, but at this point the pain was well worth it.) (I completely focused in again on my surroundings. When she spoke to me...) Angel Voice - "Oh my god. He's alive. Are you okay?" "Yeah I'm fine. What happened?" Angel Voice - "Well one minute you were staring at me, the next minute you're in the middle of a busy street holding up traffic laying injured on the ground." "Oh jeez...." Angel Voice - "What, what's wrong?" "You caught me staring at you. That's embarassing." Angel Voice - "(Giggling flirtatiously) I don't think that should be your biggest concern right now. Are you okay to stand, are you hurt at all?" "Not really, my chest hurts a bit but I think I'm okay to make it to my feet." Angel Voice - "Good..." "(Trying to flirt) With your help." (She smiles at me, cute as could be, as if to let me know everything was perfect. She helped me to my feet where we would make our way slowly to the sidewalk back to safety once again.) Voice 1 - "An ambulance is on the way. "No, no.....No need thank you I'm sure I'll be just fine." Angel Voice - "Don't be silly, you need to spend the night in a hospital, just to be checked out and cleared of all or any injuries. "No really I'm fine, I really don't need it. I will just walk it off." Angel Voice - "Walk it off. Sorry to say but if you walk it off like you say you will, It's called leaving the scene of an accident, a federal offense." (She throughs a cute grin at me as if though she just won this dispute. But little did she know what I had in store for her. The ultimate question of entrapment.) "Will you bring me there? I just think it would be fair since your beauty is what got me into this mess." (She looks up at me with eyes that speak a million words. Although this time they spoke out with the only answer I expected.) Angel Voice - "Of course I will." "Thank you so much. That's truly kind of you." (Thankful wasn't even the word to describe this feeling. It was much more. But I tried to make small talk to lighten the mood.) "The name's Mike. Michael actually." Angel voice - "Sadie. Sadie Hawkins." "Cute. Like the dance?" Sadie - "Yes like the dance. I think my mother only named me Sadie for that reason." "It's beautiful. It's a graceful, and beautiful name." Sadie - "Thank you Mike. So if you don't mind me asking, What is it you do for a living?" "Well this might sound weird but I am a professional wrestler for Global Championship Wrestling." Sadie - "Wow. Sounds like an exciting life." "It is. I love it. I couldn't picture myself doing anything else." Sadie - "Thats awesome! Then you've found your true passion in life." "Yes. I have. So if you don't mind me asking Sadie, (I throw a grin at her) what is it you do for a living?" Sadie - "Well actually, I work right here at JR's Fitness. I'm a personal trainer." (As if the world ironically fell into place within this last hour, the day turned out to be picure perfect. I found my trainer, my love, and my way to a night of pain in the hospital with bruised ribs. But this time I wasn't alone through the pain, and I felt as if though I never would be alone again from this day on...)
