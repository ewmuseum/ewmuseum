Islands

12 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 12, 2008 Crying baby. Snoring gentleman. Cell phone call. Mile high club. It was all going on at the same time making some sort of hellacious racket that clamored together in a steady pulse. A single beat formed from so many diverse sounds that it made you wonder just how things worked and why they worked in those ways. As for Jay Terror, he couldn't hear the noise. His ears were being teased by the sweet harmonies of Slightly Stoopid and a cool reggae breeze creating a barrier between himself and reality - a barrier that he didn't mind sinking behind. To his left, Caroline shut her eyes and drifted away to the island of "Z's". Jay took one look over her and with a smile plastered on his lips he shut his own eyes and allowed Miles Doughty to guide him to the same island his lady was headed to. 'Drink sir?' And like that, he sunk into the depths of the ocean and began to choke on the salty taste entering through the gaps in his teeth. Jay opened his eyes sternly and turned toward the hand that had just tapped him. Pulling his headphone free from his ear, 'You need something?' She was about the normal height of any regular woman and wore an obnoxious blue jumpsuit that was cut off around her thighs. On her right breast she was labeled as Rebecca. 'A drink. Would you like a soda or water?' 'Jack Daniels?' 'We don't serve liquor in the air sir.' 'Pfft.' He took the headphone off the front of his chest and placed it back inside his eardrum before giving her a suitable answer. 'Nothing.' She jogged through her emotions bank and found a false smile to flash for him before pressing on the back end of her cart and moving forward in the narrow hallway. Jay shut his eyes again and began to book his next quest to the island with Caroline. If he had any luck, he'd make it there before the plane touched down in Yokohama. __________ The smoke rising from the tip of his cigarette swirled around Jay's head creating a toxic bubble. Each breath he took brought in the poison making him subsequently breathe more often than he needed to. His eyes watered as the gas seeped into his tear ducts but he wiped them off easily enough with the triangle end of his bandana. Making himself comfortable against the back of the booth, Jay grabbed at his drink and swirled the contents around to clear the murky build-up. 'In the words of LeStatt Knight, JB, this one is going to be brutal.' The TV in the background echoed David Yale's voice straight to Jay's ears causing him to shy away from the current match and take a long sip of Jack Daniels. The liquid singed his vocal chords and puckered his lips as it went down but he loved every second of it. Actually trying to avoid watching the TV, Jay glanced across the room toward a set of women. There were three packed into the far corner and all of them had that slutty characteristic all men dream of. Short skirts, rising shirt bottoms, and birthday cake icing for make-up seemed to be the keys to instant success later in the night. Luckily for them, they already had their first fish. Like sharks, they smelled the blood and gently lured him in. As great as a threesome looked, the man was going to learn what too much really meant. He found his cigarette from the ashtray and pulled it to his lips again. 'You know, you're actually one of those guys that looks the same inside and outside of the television.' It was a stray voice that he was unfamiliar with but due to the close proximity of it he knew that it was meant for him. Turning his shoulder with cigarette in hand he nodded, 'I try.' 'Trying is good. I like people that try.' He was an American man placed in the Japanese scene looking to strike up a conversation with Jay Terror himself. Hair trimmed neatly, shirt tucked into a pair of black trousers, he topped it off with a brilliant golden tie dripping down to his leather belt. 'Yeah.' 'Sorry to intrude, just figured that you'd like something better to stare at instead of the porno in progress.' 'S'wrong with porn?' 'Nothing wrong with it. Just been around this Asian scene long enough to know what happens when you watch too closely.' Jay dropped his cigarette off on the ashtray and took another swig from his Jack. The booth did well to support his back as he inched along it trying to find the most comfortable space. 'I hear ya'. Do I look gay on the TV?” “Not that I’ve seen.” “Why the interest then?” Laughing it off, “Oh. Yeah. Sorry about that. Just making conversation.' “Why?” “You’re tougher to crack than I figured.” “Guilty.” Both men stared at one another for a long minute in silence. As loud as the bar was, no noise stood between them creating a hidden tension. This man wasn’t about to run just yet though, his lips cracked open and his words spilled freely again, “The name’s Conrad.” “Jay.” “Mind if I sit?” Looking at the three empty spaces at the four person table Jay points, “Pick your favorite.” He finds that the chair across and diagonal from Jay is his best bet and sits on the cushion. After straightening the golden threads of his tie he places his hands together on the table and looks at Jay solemnly. “It’s weird that I ran into you here.” “You talk like I know you. I’m lost.” He picks up the 27 and sips off the end of it again creating a cherry encrusted cap. “Jay, I’ve been watching you for a while. Nothing creepy, just TV stuff. Hell, everyone’s watching you these days. Thing is, you got a talent that’s running wild.” “Good note.” “As good as wild is though, I’m afraid it may get too wild. Look at the scene. You’re stuck in match-ups against Caldera’s army and you know damn well how he treats those guys. They’re royal to him and if they want to win the round robin then he’ll do all he can to make it happen.” Jay took the final bit of Jack Daniels in and opened his eyes wide, “What about Knight?” “You won, yeah. It’s all part of a bigger plan though.” “How big?” “Big enough to screw you and the others. You think he wants someone like you heading GCW and wearing his belt” Hell, he might as well give you the pants so you have something to hold up while he’s at it.” “No chance for me then?” “None at all.” Smirking, “What do you offer?” Conrad taps his fingers on the table and sits up straight as if he’s been waiting for the question the whole while. He mirrors the smile Jay wears and tightens his eyes, “I offer you a chance. I’m sure you’ve heard the saying “If you can’t beat them join them”. Why not do just that?” “So you’re with him?” “Maybe. Maybe not. All I’m saying is that I can find you a place that’ll ensure you keep this ball rolling. Not even a three foot tall speed bump will be able to stop you.” Thinking it over, Jay takes one last drag on his cigarette before smashing its face into the ashtray. The proposition is tempting but lacks the proper facts to back-up the legitimacy. Terror shakes his head and grabs another 27 from the box on the table, “Not so sure bout that.” “You don’t trust me?” “I don’t know you.” “So it goes.” Conrad reaches under the table and comes up with a white card. “If you feel like playing the game like it’s supposed to, give me a call. If not, best of luck in the United States title bracket.” He slides the card across the table to Jay and stands from his seat. “Nice meeting you.” “Yeah. Something like that.” Jay watches him disappear into a crowd of people and lights the end of his cigarette. It burns freely and Jay shuts his eyes on the first drag. He sees paradise island in the distance calling his name ? begging him to come close. Terror sits back and listens to its sweet voice allowing it to sway his decision. __________ “So he offered you a place in the Establishment.” “I guess. Not sure really.” “You going for it?” “Doubt it.” Caroline nodded and took in the response. She sat upright in the hotel bed and looked off to the corner where Jay tinkered with his thumbs. His back was plastered against the back of a tall chair and his hair was slicked back in a Rich Rollins-esque bandana set-up. “Because of Caldera?” “Nah. Just not me.” “Yeah. Real true. You’re better off anyways. Who knows who the guy actually was.” “Yeah.” “How about Rollins” You think the guy’s serious about Caldera playing the game his way?” “I can trust that. Seems legit by the way he tends to run his business. The rest - who knows. Rollins could beat me on his own if he has the drive. Not sure he has that left though.” He stands from the chair and walks across the rug to the bed. His body stretches and comes up beside Caroline’s with his head taking homage on the pillow. “Seems like he’s lost his cause.” “You think?” “Yeah.” Her hands crawled on his forehead like spiders as they massaged his temples lightly. Jay shut his eyes with euphoria and found himself dreaming of the island again. So tempting, so open ? he wanted to walk on the sandy shores and sleep under the coconut trees but something held him back. Someone denied him the chance and made sure that he’d never make it there on his own. “What are you thinking about?” “Stuff.” “Anything good?” “Not really. Just stuff. Shit’s been digging at me for a bit.” “Maybe you’re thinking too hard.” “Maybe.” “Maybe just the tournament?” “I guess. S’all kind of stuff packed together. S’hard to figure out.” “Need help?” “Nah, I’ll live.” She nodded and took his response to heart. If Jay Terror didn’t want help he wasn’t going to get help. He was the kind of guy that you didn’t do things for unless he asked. Unfortunately with Jay, he never asked anyone for anything. He was an outlaw living the land like he figured he should. There were no helping hands reaching out to him, there were no words of wisdom unless he wanted them. The only thing he had was his own mind and what he saw inside it. That’s it. Within ten minutes, Caroline’s eyes shut as well. She watched quietly as Jay tried to swim to the island with her. As long as she watched, he never made it. Every time his fingers touched the shore, a wave came down on his back and the riptide dragged him back further. She wondered if he’d ever make it to the island.
