All That Came Before was Forgotten

27 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/27/11 Christopher Declan Sheffield, your one true friend, stood beside Jack Carpenter, your one true love. No two different people did you know. One, a hulking mass of scars, tattoos and bandages, had just fought his last war by your side. The other, the personification of the gentler soul encased in Sheffield's body, had to decide whether he wanted to spend the rest of his life by your side. Christopher would say the words he spoke that day were his wedding gift. "Don't think there's been anyone born into this world quite like her. You knew her folks... there ain't a single thing they have in common that you don't need a microscope to see. She grew up bein' told everything she ever done was wrong and everything she ever wanted, she shouldn't. She spent the best part of 18 years apologising when she didn't mean it, and playing at being their daughter until she nearly damn near forgot who she was. She didn't exactly grow up surrounded by love so it ain't a word she throws about easy. She ever says it to you, she means it, 'cause she knows what it's like not to hear it said. She was broken and has spent every day since tryin' to put herself back together again, and she ain't done too bad a job." "She's vibrant, she's wild and she's unpredictable. She's brave, fierce, and when you need her to, she'll fight 'til she drops. She's... spectacular, really. She's also stubborn as a damn mule and frustrating. Is she ever frustrating. She's made herself few friends and a fair few enemies... but the friends she has, well, we're enough to keep those enemies at bay. There'll be times when you think you'll never be able to figure her out and days when you'll think it's you and her against the world. You'll never be able to tame her, but eventually you'll come to realise that you'd never want to. So don't give up on her. It won't be easy, but she's here, and that means more than you know right now. She's seen most of this world, but right now she's looking out across that ocean down there waiting to see if you'll leave your footprints in the sand beside hers. She wants to know that you haven't given up on her. Now, I'm not sayin' to walk down there and all's gonna be happy ever after. You'll wish you'd left her down there more than once, but the feelin' won't last. She's got the passion of that sun up there goin' supernova and the temper of those hurricanes that pass over here. She's everything that life is... but I don't see you givin' up on that, Jack." The End. Forever.
