Change for the Better

8 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 8th, 2005 “Changed for the better?” (Upon my face lays a look of sure coolness. My hair drapes below my shoulders catching up in length to the way it use to look before I trimmed it all away. It remains damp from the recent shower I just evacuated from minutes ago. I relax in my sweatpants with my bare chest revealing no clothing. I am just about ready to relax and call it quits for the day and hit the sack but its obvious I have some unfinished business to attend to.) “Why is it people claim that I have changed for the better, seen the light in the business now” Why can’t anyone just understand it? I haven’t made changes, I haven’t seen the light. The light has always been on but I thought before the light was slowly dying out. Until one day Steven Caldera showed me that the light was still very much shining bright. This whole time I haven’t changed who I am. I am improving what I use to be. Changes come within minutes, hell, seconds of ones life. I mean hell one minute you could be lighting a firecracker with a friend and the next your hand could me dismembered from early explosion. A change is something a person is willing to deal with. Changes are something you have to learn to live with because there happens to be no alternatives. You have nothing to run back to. Changes are life altering. Just because I happen to have made a name for myself world wide now, that means I’ve changed for the better?” (My eyes grow heavy with fatigue as I begin to stir a little bit with drowsiness.) “I haven’t changed who I am one bit. I am physically training and mentally preparing myself in every possible way. That’s determination my friend. What I have done with myself was determined when I want to be bigger. better and stronger. And the only way I could enforce that was by taking the step forward and trying much harder. I’m eating fresh, I’m exercising daily, I remain free of drugs and alcohol. What I’m trying to say, Ricky, is that I am putting my dreams first, and my past behind me. I’m expanding my name because one day, someday, it will be the name no one will ever forget.” (With that I slowly lift myself back upon my feet and take off towards the rest that lays just minutes ahead.) OOC: sorry it was so short. I’m wrecked! Long day?.LOOONG DAY!
