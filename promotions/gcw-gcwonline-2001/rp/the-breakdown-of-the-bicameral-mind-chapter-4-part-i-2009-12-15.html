The Breakdown of the Bicameral Mind: Chapter 4 Part I

15 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 15th, 2009 Excerpt taken from Dr. Lawrence A. Matheson’s private journal… As the days pass, I get closer and closer to my goal. Dancing on the razor’s edge has finally started to pay off. All those years of being ostracized and marginalized will finally come to fruition. Everything is ready. Once I am done with my experiment, a new era of humanity will have begun. --- The sun was shining and the birds were singing their sweet songs. Green and blue covered most of the horizon. The weird swirls in the clouds made it seem like Van Gogh’s brush strokes had left their impression over the sky. The leaves rustled the wind with tremendously gentle force. It was a wonder to behold, nature was. And wonder they did, every day at a specific hour they were left to roam the outsides of the hospital. Most of them didn’t understand the marvel of freedom. They were too busy being crazy to comprehend even the smallest of things. Their brains were damaged. But none was more injured than the head of one Antonio Vargas del Valle. He had spent his life on a quest to become the best at what he did only to end up with his own brain being his demise. He sat there, looking at the horizon but not really seeing it. His body slumped into a wheel chair and his mouth drooling on himself as the clouds passed him by. They weaved into different forms in the sky as if trying to convey some kind of cosmic message. There was someone in that place who could read the future in the clouds; he imagined that they waved at her just as they were waving at him at that moment. There had been many times in his life where he had felt helpless and powerless. It was probably one of the worst feelings ever. The dread of not being able to do anything at all to help your situation is intense and frustrating. He felt like that now. Or rather, what little of him was able to comprehend what was going on felt like that. The drugs had invaded all parts of his body and everything seemed impossible. Just lifting his head upright was a chore that was better left undone. This was the end of him. That much he understood. The first few days he struggled against everything. There was still a fight left deep inside that battled as hard as it could to survive. That side of him had been defeated long ago. With the edge of his vision, he saw the woman. His Eve. --- Excerpt taken from Dr. Lawrence A. Matheson’s private journal… Both subjects are showing great results. Every test we have administered seems to be having the desired effect when it comes to brainwaves. We will soon be starting to test the practical side of the experiment and I am very hopeful that it will be a success. If it works on both test subjects, we will see a change to our very humanity. I can’t seem to contain my excitement. Soon. Very soon. --- You could see yourself in the mirror. Completely naked except for the cables that ran all around his body, taking all manner of information straight from the source. You couldn’t help but look up at the man who was doing all this to you. The man that had slowly turned you into his very own guinea pig stood in front of you and you couldn’t even muster enough energy to hate him for it. He sucked the will out of you. The will to think for yourself, the will to live and most importantly the will to fight. He sucked you dry. There was nothing left to do but to let him. To let him rape your mind and twist your body into some foul abomination. To let him turn you into someone else’s idea of the perfect being. If there could ever be such a thing. You no longer endured. --- Antonio was no longer the same. The last treatment had gone by and it was a new day that dawned. Dr. Matheson stretched his arms and back as much as he could and then cracked his neck. The final arrangements were done and it was time to see what had happened to his first subject. Dr. Matheson stood slowly from his chair and threw on his white coat. He grabbed his chart and went off to finally see the new beginning of the human race. The doctor walked the corridors of the hospital with his head held high. He could envision the future that would come after he was finished. It would be a world full of accolades for him and a brighter future for the whole of human kind. He finally reached the door that led him to subject zero. Inside, Bill the orderly was monitoring Antonio’s vital signs. “Is everything okay?” Dr. Matheson asked the orderly. “Yes, sir. Everything seems to be fine. He should be waking up very soon,” Bill answered. “Good. Now, when he comes to, he will be confused and hopefully with little to no memory of the good times in his life. There will be no memory of what we did to him but there will be the vague recollection on the back of his mind of everything that ever hurt him. It is perfect. We will be able to tap into those pseudo-memories and have him do my bidding. “I think you might be rich when this is all done. I can’t even imagine how much the militaries of the world will pay you for created what is basically a super soldier,” Bill said with giddy excitement on his voice. “Indeed. But we are not yet ready to go that big. We have to make sure that Subject Zero is a success and if he is and Subject Eve works fine, we will be able to broaden our horizons and go for the big payday,” Dr. Matheson rubbed his hands together. He checked the chart for the fifth time and nodded towards Bill. “It is time.”
