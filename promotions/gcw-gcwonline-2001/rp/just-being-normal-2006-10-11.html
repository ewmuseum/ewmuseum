Just being normal

11 October 2006

Date: 11 Oct 06 “Well I have good news and I have bad news.” “Please don’t tell me that you are saving a bunch of money on your car insurance.” He laughed out loud. “No Mr. Abitua that’s not it. The good news is that you do not have any fractures. You did twist it up pretty good though.” “So what’s the bad news?” “I really don’t think you should be wrestling this week.” Now this was not going to happen. I knew it. The good Doctor knew it. The fans knew it. No way was I going to sit out after what had happened at Dangerous Games. Not a chance. “Sorry Doc. I’m going for it. I have some things to prove.” “Is it worth permanently injuring yourself” Look we’ve had this conversation before. Ankle breaks are very unpredictable. Most of them never completely heal, you could have problems with that ankle for the rest of your life.” “I’ve been good these past couple years.” “No you are just much stronger and have one hell of a pain threshold. Just take a week off.” The thought crossed my mind for a total of about 3.76 seconds. After that I tossed it to the side like day old garbage. “Sorry, but this week is important to me.” “Fine,” he sighed. “But at least take this prescription.” I took the bottle from his hand and gave a slight nod. “Alright, gracias.” I got off of the hospital bed and onto my feet. “Good day.” What I was feeling could only be understood by a small amount of the population. Mostly professional athletes. Sometimes you put your own body on the lines for the good of your name or your legacy. My Legacy is more important to me than most anything else. I have fought hard to earn the respect of my peers as well as the fans around the world. I won’t give that up. ___________________________________________________ It was good to be home for a couple of days. For the past few weeks Mary had been out and about with her cousin so I saw no real reason to venture back. Two days ago I got a call from Mary however letting me know that she was feeling ill. Pregnancy will do that to a woman. I walked through the front door. “Hunny I’m home!” Yes the line is corny and clich?” but I am pretty sure that’s the whole point. Mary doesn’t immediately appear but after a few moments of scanning the area I got a glimpse of part of her face sneaking out from behind a door. The sight makes me chuckle while shaking my head slightly. This causes her to open the door completely. Her body looked awkward seeing as how she is so tiny yet sporting a 7 month pregnant type belly. I have never seen anything so beautiful. I approach her with a slight rub of her belly and a soft kiss to her lips. These were the only times in my life when I get to feel normal again. With GCW life can be so hectic. Not that I usually mind though. It’s what I fight for, it’s what I live for. Like with all things, however you need time to recharge. “Statty-bear, Mary missed you somethin” horrible.” “I missed you too mami. Hows our baby doing?” “Makes Mary wonder why in the name of yellow monkey arse she ever left wrestling!” The look I give Mary tells it all. Confusion. “What!”” “Lil guy kicks Mary something fierce!” I can’t help but laugh out loud at this. Mary had this certain charm about her unique to only her. She was a grown woman physically that was for sure but she had this innocence about her at the same time. Even more confusing? When you look into her eyes you can see the story of her life. A long road full of pain and hatred. We understand each other. Which is why we love each other. “Well Mary you won’t have to worry about that much longer.” I led her to the living room, helping her sit down when we reach the couch. I take my seat at her feet, grasping one in my hand and begin to gently massage. “How’d things go with Kagerro?” Mary shrugged. “She Otay.” She gives a half smirk. “Mary missed those Games of Dangerousness! Heard my Statty messed him ankle up.” “Just a slight tweak.” A look of seriousness came over Mary. “Want Mary to kill that skank-ho-biatch?” ??No?” Although the thought was tempting. “I can handle this.” “OH You no fun.” Mary pouts, sticking out her bottom lip slightly. “But tell Mary if ya ever changes your mind!” She always made me smile. I’m not even sure if she always means to, could just be the way she is. It’s always impressed me that she could stay happy even though everything in her life gave her the excuse to fold. She is strong, stronger than I ever could dream to be. I crawled my way up the couch to lay right by her side. Immediately her small but strong arms wrap around my shoulders. I lay my head gently on her stomach and give it a soft kiss. “The time is coming soon babe.” I twist my neck to look up at her. “Our baby is coming.” “Well This Baby might be coming but um ? Mary needs to cum too see?” She gives a devilish grin. I smirk. She licks her lips. I ? Well? What happens next is none of your business. __________________________________________________________ Good friends are hard to come by. This is a sad lesson that I have learned over the years. Not that I have ever had too many friends though, on the contrary I have but had a few. David Sandy, AKA, RaZiel was that man for a long time. At least I thought he was my friend. Friends don’t feed your brother, whom you thought dead for years, lies and then at the right time send him to kill you. At least last time I checked. I had Wylie long ago. But that slut went and fucked around with my most hated rival. THEN RaZ. What.THE.Fuck? Then WAY before that there was Cindy. And I just don’t have it in me to get into that story again. Which leads me to the now. 11 October 2006. Who is it that I consider a friend? Alex ? Grey? ALEX fucking GREY. Now most people wouldn’t know this but this man once tried to kill me once or twice. I also gladly returned the favor. We have battled to the brink of death, to the gates of hell and back. Now that it’s all done all that was accomplished is that we have a great respect for one another. For some odd reason I feel like I can fully trust him, that he is one of the few people who understand me. Yeah I lead a strange life. “Fuck that amigo, Wylie was not THAT good.” I said with a chuckle. “C’mon Rob, we both know she had talents. Then again it could have just been because I knew it’d kill you to see us together.” “At the time I wouldn’t doubt it.” I take a swig of my beer ? I don’t even know what kind it is. Might as well be adventurous once in awhile yes? “So what’s next?” Alex asked. “What do you mean?” “With GCW. Ascension, Dies Irae, NC-17. You’ve got a lot on your plate at the moment.” “Well,” I put my mug down. “It’s simple really. I go on and win.” “What about Eva?” I snicker. “Give me a break.” “What?” “What about her” She’s stuck in what happened in the past. What should I feel about her? Should I be afraid of what she might do? Fuck that. I will play nice but it will only last so long if she doesn’t keep her bitchiness in check. I’d have little problem straightening her out again if need be.” “Shouldn’t you want to work together, at least this week?” “I guess,” I shrug. “I have a feeling that’ll be harder for her than me. When I get into that ring it’s all business. Eva is a woman, therefore she wears her emotions on her sleeve. We’ll see what happens.” “And Shadow and Vivica?” “Just another match. I have nothing to prove to them or anyone else. I am the World Champion.” But I did have something to prove. That I was better than what was being shown lately. That I was worthy of the World Title that I held. That I wasn’t about to just lay down and roll over at the sight of the big bad Dies Irae. I have to prove that I am still the BEST the fucking World had to offer. And hopefully bust a few skulls in the process. HA. I’ll drink to THAT.
