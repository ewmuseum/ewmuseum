Date: July 31 and August 1(Broadcast August 3) “He is hurt badly, Keiko. We don’t know if he’s going to make it through the night.” “Doctor, please do anything you can to bring him back to us.” I could hear Keiko and the doctor. I didn’t feel anything. I couldn’t open my eyes. I was starting to wonder, was I dead. That’s when I saw it. That light. I knew what that meant. Years of my sister bringing me up as a Christian taught me that when I saw it, that it was the end. I could see a hand coming down and grasping mine. I was then told one thing. “Kid, you better fucking wake up. It isn’t your time yet.” “God?” “No, but when you need me the most, I’m going to be there for you.” I needed him years later. Whoever this guardian angel was that was watching over me. My wife was dead. My son died with her. But that guardian angel never came. I was bitter. I hate you God. You spared me, but you took Keiko and Dan away from me. “Forgive me father, for I am about to sin.” The window in the confessional opens. A little bit of light hits the eyes of the man kneeling inside. A priest can be seen though the screen. “Son, if you know that you are about to sin, why don’t you follow a path to keep from doing so?” “It isn’t that easy father. I am about to go to war. I believe that when my time in that war is over, that I will have committed atrocities that are too heinous to describe. I do this voluntarily father.” “Son, you go to fight against those that wish to harm us.” “Father, I don’t fight for the country. My war isn’t spiritual or philosophical. My war is personal.” “My son, many of our soldiers find that fighting against those that attempt to destroy us and our freedoms is a personal battle.” “Father, you do not understand my personal war. You will never understand why I do the things that I do. I don’t fight for a nation that has been hurting. I fight for my own honor. I wasn’t trained to be a killer, I just find myself being pulled towards being just that.” “My son, you can choose a different path.” “No, I can’t. My path was decided for me already. That is why I’ve come here, to see if I can get the forgiveness from your god for what I do. Because my god is a vengeful god. My god took from me what was most precious. “My god has forsaken me and my own.” The priest can visibly be seen doing the sign of the cross. “My son, you have not been forsaken. You are one of God’s children.” “Then I must be god’s red headed stepchild.” “My son, why do you feel that way?” “Because, father, I’ve been beaten in life. I’m spiritually and emotionally drained. All that’s left, father, is vengeance. I believe in your god, and I absolutely fucking hate him.” “Wake up.” “What?” “Wake up. I’ve come to help, you little fucker.” “Who are you?” “You remember me, Dorian.” “How do you know my name?” “Because, ever since you were in that coma, I’ve been watching you. I told you that I would be there for you when you needed me the most. That time is coming.” “I know that voice.” “Yes, it’s your voice. Haven’t you realized it yet?” “Why now” Why didn’t you show up when I needed you the most?” “You see, those events, they were just preparing you. You see, you’re going to battle Dorian. You’re going to battle against forces that are nearly unstoppable.” “What is this, a battle for god” Why would I do that? He fucking took everything away from me.” “This isn’t for God. This is for yourself. You’ve become hardened since we last talked. There is nothing stopping you now.” “Fuck you. I don’t care who you are. I got this far without you, and I can get even farther. You stay out of my fucking head. You hear me?” “Yes, you are ready.” I watch. To me, Danielle Northsky is just another wrestler that will fall. Her allegiance has already dictated that she will be one of many to lose it all. She will be the first. She definitely won’t be the last. Eventually, they will all fall under the path that I cut. The atrocities that I’m prepared to bring to the arena night after night is going to show just that. I listen for the blood curdling screams that they are going to let out when the massacre begins. My soul has grown cold. There are few that are close to me. They want to save me from becoming a darker, more sinister version of myself. Andrea, I feel that I’ve betrayed you. You’ve stood by my side as I’ve worked hard to straighten myself out, and I’m turning into him again. You’ve been there as I worked through my loss. I owe it to you not to become him again. But there isn’t anything that I can do to keep me from becoming him again. I need the strength to go into a war that is going to cost me everything. My blood will be spilled. My soul will be lost. I will be lost. No. It’s not time to lose my focus. Destroying those that have tried to take away what I’ve become should be my focus. Danielle Northsky should be my focus this week. Her pain. Her suffering. Her loss. Her blood will be on the hands of those that stand before me. Her soul will be saved by her destruction. After Northsky, it will be another. And then another. Until all of those that are associated with Dies Irae are gone. “Dorian.” The blaring of the alarm clock would awaken anyone. “Dorian.” The shaking of a man’s wife would do that as well. “DORIAN!” The elbow to the ribs finally does it. “Andrea, what the fuck?” “You’re going to be late for your flight.” Dorian Ryan rolls out of his bed. He puts on a pair of jeans that are hanging over the arm of a chair. He then pulls on a black tank top. “You’re going to call me when you land, right?” Dorian says nothing as he goes into the closet of his and Andrea’s bedroom and pulls out a suitcase. He opens it up and tosses a few t-shirts and some slacks in it. He then grabs his equipment bag and heads to the door. “I’ll call you as soon as I land at Sea-Tac. I love you.” The radio car radio is going in a cab waiting for it’s fare. It’s tuned to the cab driver’s favorite channel, AM 950. “We’re back, and now on the phone with us, promoting the upcoming Global Championship Wrestling WorldWide event from the Tacoma Dome is GCW Superstar and Challenger for the GCW Television Title, Dorian Ryan.” “Thanks for having me.” “Not a problem. This week, you are going to be taking on Danielle Northsky in your first title match since losing the GCW United States Championship back in February.” “Yeah, yeah, yeah. You know what” This time, it’s going to end differently. It’s going to end with Danielle Northsky injured, and out of action for more than four months.” “There are many within the IWC that talk about the controversy that you have sparked since you re-signed with GCW back shortly before Warpath.” “You know what” That’s good. Because, the more controversy that surrounds me, the more that my words and actions are going to stick around. Look at Dies Irae. They were controversial when they first got to GCW. And in the half a year that they’ve been here, they’ve become the establishment.” “How do you feel about that?” “How the F*** do you think I feel” When I came to GCW, I didn’t go around beating the S*** out of other wrestlers. I came, I did my job, and I went home. I didn’t try to change the face of GCW. They did. They ran roughshod over GCW. Many of the stars that were around when I first got here are gone. You look at the current champions, LeStatt Knight was the only one on the roster when I first joined.” “Now, there are three major groups currently in GCW. The Ascension, Dies Irae and the Knightmare Brigade: Native American Nations. What are your thoughts on these three groups.” “I think that Dies Irae and Knightmare Brigade are pieces of filth that need to be flushed. As far as the Ascension goes, they are starting to get in over their head. I told one of their members at the last show that they had to be ready to do whatever it takes, no matter how cruel or heinous that might be. I don’t see them having what it takes to do that yet.” “Is there anything else that you wanted to tell our listeners as we are running out of time.” “Yeah. Shadow, last week, you said some things about me that I didn’t like. At WorldWide, I want you to listen closely at what I say. Then I want you to watch what I do to Danielle Northsky as I intend on using her to send a message to the rest of Dies Irae and the Knightmare Brigade. The war has started. I intend on winning it.” “Thank you very much, Dorian Ryan. WorldWide will be coming live from the Tacoma Dome, very few tickets are still available for the show, they won’t last very much longer.”
