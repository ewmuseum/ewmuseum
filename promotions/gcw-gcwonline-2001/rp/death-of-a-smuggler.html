Death of a smuggler

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Many light years ago So this is what it feels like to die. Not too bad. It doesn't hurt and I think I've finally got rid of that nagging pain in my knee. Yeah, I may never be able to eat steak again or see a sunrise, or know the touch of a woman again, but it could be worse. I could be scattered atoms. Funny, I thought there'd be more to it. Maybe a white light or something, pearly gates, old guy with a book. Something. I do feel like I'm floating. And that smell. Smells like a wet dog. Death smells like a wet dog. Wait. Can you smell when you're dead? Open an eye. Good, now the other one. Death looks like the inside of a ship. And my guide to the otherside looks like... 'Wild?' A hairy face turned to look at me. 'Good, you're up.' 'Up?' I asked, 'I coulda sworn I was dead when the guy with the fire on his head tossed me in the ground.' 'Close.' 'Okay, tell me the whole story.' I said sitting up. Whoah. Bad idea. I laid back down. Wild hit the autopilot on his ship and came over to where I lay. He pulled up a chair next to me and I slowly ventured sitting back up again. This time the pain wasn't as intense. I felt pretty good, despite the fact that I still thought I was dead. 'Lemme guess,' Wild said, 'You got an invitation by a Dark One to a tournament for the ultimate prize.' 'How'd you know?' 'Every now and then they do that. Tease beings with a chance at the ultimate prize, and then when you get to the cordinates you find yourself with five other beings in a fight to the death.' Wild said, I nodded, and it felt like rocks were being smashed around in my head. 'Ow.' 'How much do you remember?' 'Bits and pieces.' I said as Wylde handed me a pill and a glass of water. After swallowing I felt somewhat better. 'I remember doing a lot of fighting, and burying at least one guy. Then the guy with the fire on his head tossing me in a hole.' 'That would be Spitfire.' Wild said, 'I had my run ins with him, and last I heard he was dead. Guess I shouldn't believe everything I hear. Anyway, you're not the first one to be suckered into that. The Dark Ones are real bastards like that, I'd wager there's hundreds of beings buried on that planet, probably even thousands.' 'Wait a sec...' I said, 'How did I...' 'Get out?' I nodded, no more rocks, just pebbles rattling around in my head this time. Wild bared his claws at me, still caked with some dirt. 'Wait another sec...' I said, 'How did you...' 'Find you?' I nodded yet again. 'You think I'm gonna let the best smuggler in the galaxy run around free?' 'You've been tracking me?!' 'I saved your life.' 'Thanks.' I said, 'But you've been tracking me?!' 'Well yeah,' Wild said, 'You've been a real help to me over the years, and I know I'll have need for your services again. So as soon as the Dark Ones were gone, I dug you up.' 'Not that I don't appreciate it, but why didn't you help me in the first place?' I asked. 'Some kind of barrier around the area where the fight was taking place. I couldn't get in. I could only watch. Once the barrier was down and the Dark One had gone, I dug you up hoping I wasn't too late.' 'So what happened to my ship?' 'Probably scrapped by now.' 'Damn, I loved that little scrap heap.' 'I see you lost your medallion.' 'I was getting tired of it anyway.' I said. 'So what is the ultimate prize, that sounds like something I'd like.' 'I doubt it exists. No one's ever seen it.' 'Well maybe it's time for someone to find out if it's real.' I said grinning. 'You know for a dead guy, you're pretty livley right now.' Wild said. 'Yeah, because now I have something to live for.'
