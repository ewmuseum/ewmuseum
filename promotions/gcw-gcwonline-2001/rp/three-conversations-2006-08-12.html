Date: 8/12/06 This counts for absolutely nothing, just to clarify, other than a sign of late night boredom. ------- Something's a killer. I don't really wanna meet it. Not now. Now here. It's too dark to be around killers. It's too loud to hear their shifting. It's too rancid to smell their creep. It perches on trees, staring down with coal eyes. It drags along the moss, clicking its sharpened teeth. It leaves no traces. It takes its time. It just does as nature might intend. Violent, blind, stupid thrashing. Something proud. Something vain. Something cold. Something dead. I let it in my head a long time ago. The mistake wasn't there; mistake waited for me to let that big bad something out of my head. Boogeymen are meant to be kept in the minds of sleepdeprived children, noit just casually tossed into the world without a good backup plan other than feet in motion. But one was. A silent killer, there like mist or lightning or smoke is there. Tangled, erratic, fleeting. In deep blue swamps of fog or forests dead at night, green sheen shimmering in the pitch. Or. Or on a quiet island, safe from unwanted interference. Selfamused, or jestered or concubined up or anything, just amused. Or. In a world of rising rebar and plexiglass. People huddled shoulder to shoulder. Ribcages colliding in the shadows of titans. In the heart of the human machine. The island's a much better place for it, yeah. Still, I can taste it in the air. Under the saltwater wash, beyond all the caked sand and sodium in my sinuses, there's a great and quiet rot. It won't speak unless spoken to. Then, it can see only master or prey. A dumb, mewling animal that sees only motion, that will never let thoughts betray the name of action. Sweat doesn't escape here. It sticks to the skin in beads that drip, or in swathes that suffocate. The air's too humid to take any more water. That makes it so much easier to bear, this extra thirty pounds of shit to carry soaked into everything that'll absorb and pooled in everything that won't. Because I, former mogul and current wrestling junkie Jordan White, have good business wandering around in an island jungle. Why, I'm only hunting down the most dangerous man I've ever known to ask him a few questions. And, besides that shit, he's only scary physically. Mentally, I mean, he's not that bright as a raving--sometimes bloodthirsty--lunatic. Can't just steal all my money, either. Not that bad. Or, could be worse. Still breathing. Still smoking. Still kicking. So far. A forest, or a jungle? I don't know. Vines, luminescent aquamarine, spiderweb erratic/mechanical through it; whatever the hell it is. Semantics. Not my problem. Here there be mosters, child; here there be ghouls and krakens and dragons. It is a dark place. It is a wicked place. It is a damp place. It is home to a tiger. This is my problem, or a problem in my great scheme of problems. There was once this country, England, and this land, America. England tried to live in America. At first, it failed. It erected a site and returned to find it vanished. It found Croatoan, Croa carved around the remains.. And that became a plot point in a comic book. And leaked over into this poor kid turning into a monster whenever it showed up. Croatoa. A neat little word. Worthless, now. Three syllables, a funny story about a small colony getting assimilated into native culture. I changed the word. It changes nothing. Not what the monster is, not where it lurks, not what it can do. Not that the monster is. Another silly accident on a normal kid. Not some trivial anecdote about history. Not some metaphor about the nature of brutality, about the nature of assumptions. Not some comic book reference. But; a dangerous, dangerous, dangerous thing that hides in a human skin. That is tucked away inside of something as fragile as a human mind. That is kept at bay or at large by something as simple as a human word. I can't believe I trusted myself that much with something that ungood to the rest of us human things. I mean, it wouldn't be much of a stretch to call this entire event a bad thing, if not sorta terrible. Almost even a minor fuck up. But we will persevere, and we probably won't be very scared until it's too late. Only the scared and the delusional use the royal we, Jordan. Which one am I? Both, maybe. If he'd been at his house, things would've been different. He's on this little roll of hills jutting from the ocean. Just not the part that he lives in, and does human things in. There's a chance--just a tiny nothing chance that totally is not a plague that wreaks havoc on my head--that he's out here, somehow driven above or below humanity, and he's hunting me for the sport of it. I mean, I am the most dangerous game. Next to, well, smarter people, or armed people. It's some nagging humanity in most that makes it so hard to kill us people, though. Stripped of that prestigious nada, there's not much left to keep a hungry predator from leaping madly out from behind a tree and beating me to death with my own arm. Before ripping it off. I mean, big ifs here. Kid's had an average life; he's gotten enough pussy of enough quality to not just be some glaring freak of nature that wishes to rend my flesh from my pale bones. So he's not in his first class days anymore; his high profile, rounds on the house years died. No clue where it left the bastard. I just know that I left him here, and, well, fuck. Here he is not. Could just head back to his house, sprawl out on that leather sofa--big like the world is big--and smoke like I still own the place until he shows up. Not like the neighbors five miles off are going to call the Sea Police out after me. I hope. Hey, Jordan. Quick question: what has bones exposed under a loose layer of frayed gray flesh, matted fur, and one beady eye that stares blindly off at The Big Dipper? Something dead, something rotten. Only aware of one predator native to this island. Also, I'm sort of loaded, I guess. Irrational claiims aren't hard to come by or anything. So, I'll just let it cloud my mind until I'm wondering if I took the right turn at the tree with three vines instead of five, or maybe confused the moss covered stone with some other moss covered stone; and I'll let it be all I see until I turn around and notice the patch of dead bushes with a single thriving tree at the center; and I'll obsess its minutia on the way through branches that snag until I'm back where I started. Why the fuck did I give this place away? The landing strip, the docks, this fucking minimansion overlooking an eternal Pacific Ocean--all very, very nice things. It's just, shit. Didn't earn 'em myself. Shit, don't earn or deserve them regardless of how they got to me in the first place. Only real things matter; it would've been a more personal gift if I'd have baked the bastard gingerbread cookies or had his name stitched into a jersey. I like what he's done with this shit. Assuming it's even a print. God damn. No offense to all the gaudy shit covering this room, like the rest, but I've got a date with the first bottle of rum I see.
