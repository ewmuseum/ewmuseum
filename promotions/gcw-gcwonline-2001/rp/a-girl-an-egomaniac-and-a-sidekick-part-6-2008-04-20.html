A Girl, An Egomaniac, and a Sidekick (Part 6)

20 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/20/08 (Takes place September 2002) September 2002 It’s remarkable how much three weeks can change the smallest of things. In just three weeks, Tessa Windsor had gone from being a fairly unknown rookie to one of WIW’s hottest stars, both literally and figuratively. She’d developed quite the following both backstage, and at every venue they’d flown to in the past three weeks. She’d yet to lose, from any kind of standpoint. She was at the very top of her game, and looked to be losing zero momentum. Coming off another victory against WIW hall of famer, ETO, Tessa was floating on cloud nine. She disregarded the sweat drenched mop atop her head, pulling it back into a ponytail, and tossed both her coach purse and gym bag over her shoulder. She’d have plenty of time to fix it back at the hotel, where she’d no doubt celebrate this big victory. “Great job out there girl!” Tessa needed not turn back her head, as she knew the voice belonged to none other than Markus Stone. “Think I’ve proved enough in my three weeks here?” She commented, half kidding, half hoping he’d critique her. His unexpected answer caught her completely off guard, “I’ll leave that for the boss to answer.” “Chae?” She asked, as if she’d never heard of the guy before. Markus leaned over to whisper, “He wants you in his office.” He might as well have said it aloud, given the oval shape that Tessa’s mouth formed into after hearing this gave it all away, “He’s not mad, is he?” “Heck if I know.” “Great.” She huffed aloud. It wasn’t lady like, but after all she’d done to prove herself, if she was about to be in trouble who cared what was lady like and what wasn’t? She hadn’t gotten more than five steps away, when Markus? shouting had caught her attention once more, “Oh, and Chris is there too, so you must be in real big trouble.” “Great.” She huffed once more, turning the corner and walking into the first room on the right, Jason Chae’s makeshift office. Just as warned, inside was not only Jason Chae, but the man who’d relentlessly harassed her the past few weeks, Chris Storm. “Bout time you showed up.” Griped Chris, who’d kept his self occupied in the meantime brushing back his shoulder lengths locks into the perfect ponytail. She turned her attention over to Chae, silently mouthing the words, “Why is he here?” He failed to acknowledge her question, directing his attention to the empty seat next to Chris, which of course was being held for Tessa. It made it all the less comfortable that Chris was never one to be a gentleman in front of a fellow man. So rather than scoot his seat back to allow Tessa moving space, he scooted equidistance between the wall and Chae’s desk, making it impossible for Tessa to scoot by him without bumping into him. Chris grinned as he moved his knee forward to bump her as she passed. Tessa shot him a look to let him know she was fully aware that he did that on purpose, but Chris remained tight lipped, knowing better than to say a word in front of the boss. Her eyes kept glued to Chris until a commanding voice broke the silence, “Ms. Windsor.” “Yes?” Obviously it was best for her to shift her attention to her boss, but all the while the eye closest to Chris was desperately trying to find any type of suspicious behavior that’d lead to him being kicked out of this meeting. “I’d like to have a word with you regarding you and Mr. Storm.” Just the mention of his name, brought a smirk back to the lips of WIW’s current World Champion. While it was disgusting to know that one man could be that self absorbed to assume that his name always corresponded with great things, that smirk had a vice grip on Tessa. She always wondered how Storm managed to remain so confident, despite the slander of many both inside and out of the wrestling world. However, when he caught her peering out of the corner of her eye she always had to find a quick way to recover. This time it was by quickly jumping into verbal action, “What about?” “Well, at the moment both you and Chris are two of WIW’s hottest stars,” To this, Chris slowly ran his hand through his golden locks, to emphasize that Chae surely meant physically as well, “and as such the two of you are seen as ambassadors of the promotion. Now at the moment we are trying to build a working relationship with a fellow Primetime Central promotion, DWO, and I think it would be a great idea to have you and Storm team up to take on two of their stars.” The moment he had the chance to, Storm stole the spotlight, “I’m in,” he turned to his colleague, immediately picking up that she wasn’t all too thrilled about being asked to team with him. He had plenty of strong feeling about this as well, “but do I really need her to come along” I mean this is the DWO we’re talking about. I could take down their whole damn roster by myself.” While Chae took comfort in the talent level of his employees, even he wasn’t foolish enough to buy into Storm’s self promotion, “Chris, we are looking to promote more than just one person. While you may like to believe you are the heart of WIW, we certainly have plenty of other talent I’d like to promote. Like Ms. Windsor here.” The two turned to see what her reaction to all of this would be. Naturally, Tessa would have loved to take the spot. It provided exactly the chance she needed to promote herself to new fans. On the other hand, Chris would do anything he could to make sure she knew that this was all about him. “I’d love to?” In kicked her nerves, “but I think Markus would be a much more suitable choice.” Immediately Chris had to add in his two cents, “You damn right he would.” Markus had crossed Chae’s mind when formulating the possibilities, but there was one thing both Chris and Tessa were forgetting about, “He has a bummed knee. Do you really want to chance his ending his career?” “Of course!” Chris blurted out, sarcastically slapping his forehead, “Why else would they want me to team with her?” Her eyes rolled to this remark. It would not be worth chiming in her opinion on the matter. Not when the man you’re supposed to team with can’t see past his own damn shadow, “I’m still not the right candidate.” It was time for Chae to play another card. “I suppose you haven’t heard the things they’ve been saying about you Ms. Windsor.” New to the business, she had not yet figured out that this was nothing more than a ploy to get her right where he wanted her, “What’s that?” His hands jumped into the air, to count in front of her the reasons why she should take his offer, “That you are nothing more than a fraud. Your matches are rigged. You are nothing more than eye candy. I could go on, but I’m sure you don’t want to hear the rest of them in front of Mr. Storm.” Her eyes widened some, but she still wasn’t fully convinced. Not until her colleague felt the need to push her over the edge, “Well, I’m glad someone agrees with me.” This is what brought her to the edge. Now, she was more than ready to jump off without a parachute. The last thing she wanted is for him to be right. While he was nothing more than a self absorbed asshole, his opinion mattered to her more than any others. Not because he was the world champion, not because he admittedly had a thing with women, but because his desire for the business told her that if she could win over his approval, she could soar in the business. “If that’s the case, count me in.” “Oh great.” Chris groaned, “So what am I supposed to do, hold her purse while she wrestles?” He looked to his boss, “Please, Chae, tell me this is some kind of pathetic joke.” Chae would not answer him though, instead he shook his head. Chris slumped back in his chair, folding his arms over his chest like a brat. “I just know I’m going to end up regretting this.” And that he still swore by it at this very time. He’d been given a weeks notice, but that made it no easier on the World Champ. Having to accept the help of another, particularly that of a female, was beyond him. Even the hefty chunk of money he was being paid for this match could not convince him to allow Tessa to help him out. Rather than make good use of the time the two had to prepare for this match beforehand, Chris paced methodically back and forth in the locker room the two were given to share, running down his lists of expectations for her. “You should stay out of my way” he told her, “you should try to avoid interrupting my flow of the match. I work a very planned pace during the matches and all your retarded flipping and flopping can throw that timing off.” Glancing over at her, he threw in “you might want to be writing this stuff down. I may quiz you later.” Her head peaked up from the table her knees had formed, “You’re not done yet?” “Done?” he scoffed, “why I’ve just begun.” Tessa dropped her head back down to her knees as his mouth opened again, “Now I trust you got my thirteen page fax about the best strategies we should use to ensure my victory.” Tessa groaned loudly. “Good, I hope you studied up on that before the match. I would absolutely hate for you to cost me a match of such importance.” At this point, she had enough, “I don’t think you have to worry about that. I haven’t lost a match since I” uhhhh’begun here?” “Don’t get cocky,” Chris told her, failing to make any sexual reference that Tessa was expecting, “no one likes people who are too cocky.” “You would know that better than anyone, wouldn’t you?” She scoffed, not bothering to look up at him. Completely missing her point, Chris nodded his head in agreement, “I sure do, I’ve had plenty of would-be partners get cocky because they were tagging with the all-time great and lose it for me. That’s why I want you to stay humble and let me do all the work.” “Well unfortunately for you, I believe in earning my pay.” Her attention shifted down to her boots, which had yet to be tied, “So you’re going to have to adapt to flips and flops. You just handle up on Kristi Kiss. I’ve got Terry Woods, because incase you forgot, I’ve BEATEN him before. ? “Yeah, and so did the ugly stick, but unfortunately Chae tagged me with you instead.” Rolling his shoulders to warm up, Chris turned toward Tessa; “why don’t you take skankvilla” I haven’t been vaccinated for any serious STDs as of late. I would hate to get Gonorrhea just from smacking the ho around. Can you get Gonorrhea that way?” As much as she wanted to laugh at that one, the last thing she wanted is for him to assume she was going to take backseat to him, “I didn’t exactly ask to be thrown into this mix with you either. Just let me get Woods. What have you got left to prove?” For once, it was her getting on his nerves, “Plenty. Don’t question me, chick.” “You’ve got nothing.” Chris folded his arms, “I shouldn’t have even asked for this match. You obviously aren’t going to take this seriously.” This lifted her eyebrow some, “Excuse me.” “You heard me.” “Ask for the match?” She chuckled in total disbelief, “You’re right. You shouldn’t have if you knew Markus had a bummed knee.” Rolling his eyes, Chris grabbed his bag and headed toward the door, “whatever” he uttered before slamming the door behind himself. Tessa blinked once at the door, then twice after processing what he had just said. He had asked for the match? He must have been planning on working with Markus. Must have. It would not have made sense any other way. Unless, he had something up his sleeve.
